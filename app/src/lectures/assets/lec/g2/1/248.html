RecapDevelopment of Telugu Literature After 1956 – Digambara, Viplava, Dalit, Feminist and Muslim Minority Streams

Recap

  • Dalit Literature – Gurram Jashuva and Gabbilam — First modern Telugu Dalit poet · Works · Gabbilam (1941) · Firdausi · Kandiseekudu (The Refugee) · Kreesthu Charitra · Gabbilam author
  • Telugu Jnanpith Awardees and Classic Works — First Telugu Jnanpith · Viswanatha Satyanarayana · Ramayana Kalpavrukshamu · "Kavi Samrat" · over 100 works · Vishwambhara · Dr C. Narayana Reddy
  • Dalit Anthologies and Dalit Women's Writing — Nallapoddu · Syamala / Shyamala · Dalit surge after Karamchedu (1985)
  • Novels and Fiction – Work and Author — Jakkadona · R.C. Krishnaswamy Raju
  • Viplava Sahityam – Virasam and the Digambara Kavulu — classical/romantic themes · anti-establishment · Cherabandaraju, Jwalamukhi, Nikhileswar · Virasam in 1970 · Srikakulam peasant struggle (1969) · Vempatapu Satyam · Visakhapatnam
  • Feminist and Muslim Minority Literature — Neeli Meghalu (1993) · Volga · Feminist poets · Jayaprabha, Vimala, Kondepudi Nirmala · Bhumika · Kondaveeti Satyavathi · Jaljala
