Recap

  • Gandhi's Journals, Books and Writings — Collected Works of Mahatma Gandhi · 100 volumes · Publications Division · 1933 English weekly · 1903
  • Return to India (1915) and First Steps — 19 July 1914 · Pravasi Bharatiya Divas (NRI Day) · 1915 · at the age of 46 · Gopal Krishna Gokhale · political guru · one year touring and observing India
  • Ashrams and Settlements — Order · Yerawada Jail (Pune) · "Mandir" · 'Phoenix' · Gandhiji · Potti Sriramulu · Sabarmati Ashram
  • Early Life and the South African Phase (1893-1914) — 2 October 1869, Porbandar · Karamchand · Putlibai · Rajkot · Kasturba at 13 · Samaldas College, Bhavnagar · Inner Temple, London (1888-91)
  • Titles, Honours and Associates — Saralaben = Catherine Mary Heilman · Mirabehn = Madeleine Slade · not Irwin · Last words · "He Ram" (Hey Ram)
  • Hind Swaraj (1909) — Gandhi · 1909 · Gujarati · Kildonan Castle · Banned · English version by Gandhi himself · self-rule
  • Influences – Book and Author — Henry Polak (1904) · Raychandbhai · Tolstoy · Ruskin · Ruskin, Thoreau and Tolstoy – all · Parshvanatha · brahmacharya added by Mahavira
