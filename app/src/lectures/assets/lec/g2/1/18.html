RecapVedic Literature – Vedas, Brahmanas, Aranyakas, Upanishads, Vedangas, Sutras

Recap

  • Epics, Puranas and Smritis — Vyasa · Jaya / Jayasamhita · Shatasahasri Samhita · 18 books (parvas) · Harivamsa · Sanskrit · Vaisampayana
  • Four Vedas – Content, Priests, Trayi — Samaveda · compiled from the Rigveda · Atharvaveda · kshetra-patni ("mistress of the field") · Prajapati · Paippalada, Shaunaka · Trayi / Vedatrayi
  • Upanishads and Vedanta — 108 · 13 principal · anti-ritualistic · Atman = Brahman · Vedanta · Prasthanatrayi · Upanishads, Brahma Sutra
  • Rigveda – Mandalas, Hymns, Gayatri — 10 mandalas, 1,028 hymns (suktas) · collection of hymns · Avesta · gotra · Rigveda · Memory of the World (2007) · 30 Rigveda manuscripts
  • Mahabharata – Characters, Epithets and Episodes — Ganesha · Vyasa · Sanjaya · Dhritarashtra · Vishnu Sahasranama · Bhishma · Dushta chatushtaya
  • Yajurveda Branches and the Brahmanas — Shatapatha Brahmana · Great Flood · first reference to money-lending · upanayana · Purushamedha · Aitareya Brahmana · Andhras
  • Bhagavata Purana, Sages and Puranic Stories — Shuka · Bhagavata Purana · Parikshit · Ekavimsati · 21 avataras · Durvasa · Atri
  • Vedangas, Sutras and Grammarians — not · Baudhayana, Apastamba, Katyayana · Baudhayana theorem · Pythagoras theorem · Vaisheshika – Kanada · Gayatri chandas · 24 syllables
