RecapBuddhism – Life of the Buddha, Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path, Pratityasamutpada, Sangha

Recap

  • Places of the Buddha's Life – Lumbini, Bodh Gaya, Sarnath, Kushinagar — Lumbini · Bodh Gaya · Sarnath · Kushinagar · Malla republic · Mahaparinirvana Temple, Kushinagar · Chunar sandstone
  • Core Teachings – Nirvana, Karma, Anatta, Middle Path — Three marks · God neither affirmed nor denied · Rejected Vedic authority · purity of conduct / a moral life · did not abolish caste and class · challenged the Brahmins' top rank · jatis and crafts (hina-sippa) low
  • Life Story – Dates, Renunciation, Epithets and Texts — Born 563 BC · died 483 BCE · Four sights · Mahabhinishkramana · departure from home · Channa · Kanthaka
  • Family, Teachers and Disciples — Goshala Maskariputra (Makkhali Gosala) · Ajivika · No successor · nominated nobody · Dhamma and Vinaya · Current (May–June 2026) · Sariputta and Mahamoggallana
  • Sangha, Triratna and Monastic Terms — Triratna · Buddha, Dhamma, Sangha · Ashtanga Margam · Linga · Nature worship · above 15 · caste no bar
  • First Sermon – Dhammachakkappavattana — Name · Wheel of Dharma · first sermon at Sarnath · Theme · Four Noble Truths · Eightfold Path · Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta
  • Other Sites – Shravasti, Vaishali, Rajgir and the Kingdoms — Council venues · Rajgir, Vaishali, Pataliputra, Kashmir · Rajgir – Lion Capital ✗ · Sarnath · Magadha · Bimbisara, Ajatashatru · Kosala
  • Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path — Order · A-D-B-C / I-IV-II-III · Buddha and Mirabai · world full of sorrow · Middle Path
