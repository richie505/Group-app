RecapBhakti Saints and Their Teachings – Ramanuja, Madhva, Nimbarka, Vallabha, Ramananda

Recap

  • Acharyas → Philosophy (Vedanta Schools) — Pushtimarga = Vallabhacharya · Shuddhadvaita = Vallabhacharya · Vishnuswami · Madhva did NOT preach Nirguna bhakti · Bijak is Kabir's · Pushtimarg is Vallabha's · Abhinavagupta
  • Ramananda – Hindi, Caste and Disciples — Born at Prayag · Kanyakubja Brahmin · Varanasi · Ramanuja's · Rama (and Sita) · First Bhakti saint to use Hindi · Let no man ask a man's sect or caste
  • Ramanuja – Teaching, Andhra and Recent Links — Sriperumbudur · Srirangam · Ilaya Azhwar · Adisesha / Lakshmana · First true exponent of Bhakti · Sri Bhashya · Teaching
  • Shankaracharya – Advaita, the Forerunner — Kaladi, Kerala · founder of Advaita · Guru = Govinda Bhagavatpada · Padmapada · Saundarya Lahari · Goddess · Forerunner / pioneer of the Bhakti cult
  • Chronology, Birthplaces and Contemporaries — Order · Contemporaries · Krishnadevaraya
  • Vijayanagara Court and Dvaita in the Deccan — Purandara Dasa · Sri Krishnadevaraya · Vallabha · Vyasaraya (Vyasa Tirtha) · Rajaguru · Narahari Tirtha · Dvaita
