Recap

  • Dynasty, Capital and Mother Goddess — Badami (Vatapi) Chalukyas · c. 543–753 CE · Deccan and the South · NOT North India · Capital = Vatapi · Badami, Bagalkot district, Karnataka · Kanchi
  • Pulakesin II – the Greatest Chalukya — Pulakesin II (610–642) · Chalukyas of Vatapi · Hiuen Tsang · Eastern campaign · Kosala & Kalinga · Durjayas · Mahendravarman I
  • Aihole Inscription of Ravikirti — Kalidasa is mentioned in · Aihole inscription of Pulakesin II · Victories · Harsha · Kadambas · Kosala, Kalinga, Pishtapura · Pithapuram
  • Andhra Inscriptions and Place Names — Mangalapura = Marturu · Dandin · Andhra Nagaram
  • Rulers and Succession — Vajapeya, Hiranyagarbha · Kshatriya · Succession war · Mangalesha · regent · Elapattu-Simbige
