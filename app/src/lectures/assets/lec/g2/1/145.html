RecapChamparan, Ahmedabad Mill and Kheda Satyagrahas; Rowlatt Satyagraha and Jallianwala Bagh

Recap

  • Champaran Satyagraha (1917) – Gandhi's First Satyagraha in India — Champaran (1917), Bihar · first satyagraha in India · first successful · 2017 was its centenary · First civil disobedience · defied the order to leave the district · first peasant (farmers') movement
  • Chronology of Gandhi's Early Movements and the Freedom Struggle — Order to remember · Rowlatt Satyagraha · first venture in all-India politics · Non-Cooperation Movement · pan-India mass movement · 1917–1919 · national leader
  • Leaders and Persons of Champaran — 10 April 1917 · Not connected · Jai Prakash Narayan, Ram Manohar Lohia · Dr. Rajendra Prasad · "Mahatma" · Tagore · before 1917
  • Tinkathia and the Indigo Problem — Champaran – indigo (neel) · Bengal Indigo Revolt (1859–60) · German synthetic dyes · unprofitable in the world market · raised rents and demanded illegal dues · tenants (ryots)
  • Ahmedabad Mill Strike (1918) — cotton-mill owners and workers · industrial workers · withdrew the "plague bonus" · Ambalal Sarabhai · Anusuya Sarabhai
  • Champaran Agrarian Committee and Its Results — Gandhi as a member · Chairman: F.G. Sly · Anugrah Narayan Sinha was not a member · abolition of Tinkathia · 25% refund · Champaran Agrarian Act, 1918
  • Kheda Satyagraha (1918) — Kheda district, Gujarat · first non-cooperation · crop failure (drought) · did not suspend revenue collection · Gujarat Sabha · Vallabhbhai Patel · Gandhi–Patel alliance
  • Rowlatt Satyagraha and Jallianwala Bagh (1919) — Rowlatt Act · without trial up to two years · Satyagraha Sabha, Bombay, 24 Feb 1919 · 6 April 1919 · Kitchlew and Satyapal · Dyer · unarmed
