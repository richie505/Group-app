RecapDelhi Sultanate – the Five Dynasties in Outline

Recap

  • Slave Dynasty – Aibak, Iltutmish, Razia, Balban — Aibak · Turkistan · Malik / Sipahsalar · Quran Khwan · Iltutmish's rivals · Tajuddin Yalduz · Tarain 1215-16
  • The Five Dynasties – Order, Periods, Founders — Delhi Sultanate · 1206-1526 · Second Tarain (1192) · First Panipat (1526) · Order · 4, 1, 3, 2 · Longest dynasty
  • Tughlaqs – Ghiyasuddin, Muhammad bin Tughlaq, Firoz Shah — MBT · Jinaprabha Suri · Badauni · Ibn Battuta · cousin Firoz
  • Khaljis – Jalaluddin and Alauddin — Jalaluddin Khalji · benevolent to Hindus · Kara (1296) · Alauddin Khalji · market control · prohibited liquor · largest standing army paid in cash
  • Timur, Sayyids and Lodis — Timur invaded in 1398 · Nasiruddin Mahmud Shah Tughlaq · not MBT, Firoz or Bahlul Lodi · Khizr Khan · Sayyid · Bahlol Lodi · Jaunpur (Sharqi kingdom, 1476)
  • Titles, Coins and Cities — Cities of the Sultans · Chronology of monuments · Qutub Minar · Tughlaqabad fort · Lodi Garden · Fatehpur Sikri · Tanka (silver) and jital (copper)
  • Breakaway Kingdoms and the Andhra Angle — Bengal · Tughril Khan · Haji Iliyas · Firoz failed · Vijayanagara (1336) · Vidyaranya · Anegondi
