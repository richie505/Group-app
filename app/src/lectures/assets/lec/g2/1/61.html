Recap

  • Revenue Systems – Todar Mal's Bandobast, Dahsala, Zabti — Karori experiment (1574) · Dahsala (1580) · Raja Todar Mal · one crore dams · karoris · salaried, transferable nobility · predictable cash revenue
  • Revenue Officials and Revenue Terms — APPSC match · a-II, b-IV, c-I, d-III · Deshmukh · collected land revenue · Qutb Shahi Golconda · one-third
  • Mansabdari System — Introduced by Akbar · Central Asian / Mongol · combined civil-military, non-hereditary · jagir · 10 to 5,000 · 7,000 · royal princes
  • Land Classification — four · three
  • Jagirs, Central Ministers and Provincial Units — Jagirdari crisis · Diwan
