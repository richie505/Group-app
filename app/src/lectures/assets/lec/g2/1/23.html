Recap

  • Buddhist Learning Centres and Monasteries — Dharmapala · Param Saugat · Haribhadra · Palas · Not a Buddhist learning centre · Nagpur · Ikshvakus
  • Buddhist Sites and Sect Centres of Andhra Pradesh — Vijayapuri · 3rd–4th c. CE · submerged under Nagarjuna Sagar · Sect centres · Purvasaila – Dhanyakataka (Amaravati) · Rajagirika – Guntupalli · Siddharthaka Chaitya – Gudivada
  • Major Stupas – Largest, Oldest, Location — Largest stupa · Borobudur · Kesariya · Sanchi · Amaravati · Event sites · Sanchi – none
  • Stupa, Chaitya and Vihara – Concept and Parts — Stupa · concept is pre-Buddhist · Buddhist stupas · relics · objects of devotion · commemorate events · only two
  • Rock-cut Caves – Buddhist and Jain — Besnagar · Bhagavata Heliodorus (Garuda) pillar · only two pairs · Western India rock-cut caves · Nasik, Ellora and Ajanta · Cave chronology
  • Spread, Patrons and Decline of Buddhism — Causes of spread · missionary · language · Mauryan followers · Ashoka · Dasharatha · Sites (IAS 1998)
