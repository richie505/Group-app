RecapGovernment of India Act 1935 – Provisions, Provincial Autonomy, 1937 Elections and Congress Ministries

Recap

  • Provisions of the Government of India Act, 1935 — Abolished provincial dyarchy · provincial autonomy · responsible governments · 11 provinces · 1 April 1937 · Section 63 · Article 175
  • 1937 Elections and Congress Ministries — February 1937 · 11 provinces · 716 of 1,161 · except Punjab and Sindh · B.G. Kher · G.B. Pant · Rajaji
  • Madras Ministry, 1937 – Andhra Angle — 15-20 Feb 1937 · People's Party · 14 July 1937 · C. Rajagopalachari · Hindi · Periyar's · Sri Bagh Pact, 16 Nov 1937
  • Congress Reaction to the Act — Lucknow (April 1936, Nehru) · rejected the Act · resolved to contest · Faizpur (Dec 1936, Nehru) · M.N. Roy first (1934)
  • Resignation of the Ministries, 1939 — 3 Sept 1939 · Lord Linlithgow · Germany · resigned Oct–Nov 1939 · Day of Deliverance – 22 December 1939 · 23 March 1940
