RecapRevolt of 1857 – Political Causes

Recap

  • Doctrine of Lapse – Dalhousie's Annexations (1848-56) — Rule · without a natural male heir · adopted son · private property only · Lord Dalhousie · not its originator · 1848
  • Annexation of Awadh (1856) – the "Misgovernment" Plea — Awadh annexed Feb 1856 · "misgovernment" (maladministration) · not · Wajid Ali Shah · dethroned and exiled to Calcutta · Summary Revenue Settlement 1856 · village proprietors
  • Mughal Emperor, Nana Saheb and Other Princely Grievances — began at Meerut · 1857 = 100 years after Plassey (1757)
  • Subsidiary Alliance – Background (Wellesley, 1798) — British subsidiary force at his cost · Resident · independent foreign policy · AP angle · Rayalaseema · "Ceded Districts"
