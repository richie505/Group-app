Recap

  • Central Departments – Kandachara, Athavane and the Council — Rayavachakam · nine departments · Samprati · Athavana · Dandaniti · Mansabdari · President of the imperial council
  • Amara-Nayankara System and the Palegars — Nayakas were · Amaranayakas were NOT · military commanders · collected taxes · contingents · Administrative system · law and order
  • Revenue and Taxes — Chief feature of the financial system · land revenue · 1/6 · 1/4 or 1/3 · Adhuvanatantram · Neerarambamu / Magani · grain
  • Justice and Police — Pradvivaka · judiciary · Authoritative law book · Parasara Madhaviyam · Himsodbhava · Dhanodbhava · Raya
  • Provincial and Village Administration – the Ayagars — Biggest unit · Mandalam · Parupatyagar · collected revenue · repaired and maintained forts · 17 rajyams · Achyutaraya
  • Army, Forts and the Nature of the State — Siddha (standing) army · Dalavayi · 4 months · Amaranayaka · Bukka I · cannon · Saluva Narasimha
