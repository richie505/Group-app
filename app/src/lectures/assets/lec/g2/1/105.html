RecapGovernors-general of Bengal and of India – Warren Hastings to Dalhousie: Key Measures

Recap

  • William Bentinck (1828-35) – Sati, Thugs and Reforms — Sati declared illegal · Regulation XVII of 4 Dec 1829 · Bengal Sati Regulation · William Bentinck · Raja Rammohan Roy · Thuggee suppressed (from 1830) · Captain William Henry Sleeman
  • Warren Hastings (1772-85) — Governor of Bengal from 1772 · first Governor-General of Bengal · Regulating Act, 1773 · Dual Government · introduced the District Collector · District Diwani and Fauzdari Adalats · Wars
  • Cornwallis (1786-93) – Permanent Settlement and Civil Service — Cornwallis Code (1793) · revenue administration from justice · District Judge · Civil service · Covenanted Civil Service · ICS · Father of Civil Services in India
  • Governors-General in Order and the Office — Titles · Clive, 1758 · Viceroy (GoI Act 1858: Canning) · Robert Clive · Dual Government (1765) · Order asked · Not matched trap
  • Wellesley, Metcalfe and Ellenborough – Other Measures — Wellesley (1798-1805) · "Bengal Tiger" · Ceded Districts (1800) · not · Charles Metcalfe (1835-36) · Press Act of 1835 (Metcalfe Act) · liberator of the Indian press
  • Dalhousie (1848-56) – the Maker of Modern India — Railways · Railway Minute (1853) · Bombay-Thane (16 April 1853) · Telegraph · Post Office Act (1854) · Public Works Department · Ganges Canal
