RecapLater Vedic Polity – Territorial Kingship, Royal Sacrifices, Ratnins

Recap

  • Kingdoms and Philosopher-Kings — Kosala, Kashi and Videha
  • Royal Sacrifices – Rajasuya, Ashvamedha, Vajapeya — Muvendavelan · not · Shrauta · dakshina · Brahmanas · AP angle · Ikshvakus
  • Assemblies and Taxes — Vidatha disappeared · Sabha and Samiti continued · women no longer sat · Vaishyas · tribute-payers
  • From Tribe to Territory – Kingship — Jana · janapada / rashtra · hereditary territorial monarchy · Mahajanapadas · eldest son · Ekarat · sole / paramount ruler
  • Ratnins and Officials — Ratnins ("jewels") · Ratnahavimshi · no standing army
