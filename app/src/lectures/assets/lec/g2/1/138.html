Recap

  • Anushilan Samiti and Yugantar – Bengal — Founded 1902, Calcutta · Pramathanath (P.) Mitra · Satish Chandra Basu · secret revolutionary society · Barindra Kumar Ghosh · Dhaka Anushilan (1906) · Pulin Behari Das
  • Abhinav Bharat, Mitra Mela and V.D. Savarkar — Abhinav Bharat · A.M.T. Jackson · Nasik conspiracy case · Andamans · Savarkar abroad · London in 1906 · All four true (RAS 2013)
  • Early Actions – Phadke, Chapekars, Muzaffarpur — Vasudev Balwant Phadke (1879) · Ramosi peasants · Ramosi Krishak Jatha · armed uprising in the 1870s · Chapekar brothers · Plague Commissioner W.C. Rand · Poona, 1897
  • Alipore and Delhi Conspiracy Cases — Aurobindo and Barindra Ghosh · Chittaranjan Das defended Aurobindo · 23 December 1912 · Viceroy Hardinge · Chandni Chowk · Mastermind · Rash Behari Bose
  • Revolutionary Press and Organisations — Sandhya · Yugantar · Kal · all three · Bandi Jivan · Sachindranath Sanyal · Revolutionary bodies
