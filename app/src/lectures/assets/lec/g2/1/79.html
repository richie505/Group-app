Recap

  • Ashtapradhan and Shivaji's Officials — Shivaji's council of eight (by 1674) · Advisory only · directly responsible to the Chhatrapati · independent · not hereditary · Panditrao and Nyayadhish · Chitnis
  • Shivaji's Career – Birth, Guru, Forts, Bijapur and the Mughals — Born at Shivneri fort · 19 February 1630 · 1627 · 1627, 1674 · Shahaji Bhonsle and Jijabai · Dadaji Kondadev · Pune jagir
  • Successors of Shivaji – Sambhaji, Rajaram, Tarabai, Shahu — Order (UPPSC 2005) · Treaty of Warna (1731) · Kolhapur · Current linkage · Attock to Cuttack · Baji Rao I
  • Revenue System – Chauth, Sardeshmukhi and Land Terms — Chauth and Sardeshmukhi · without the cost of direct governance · Mughals (1719) · Nizam at Palkhed (1728) · Jagirs abolished · cash · deshmukhs and deshpandes
  • Military, Navy, Coins and Records — Guerrilla warfare (Ganimi Kava) · hill forts on the table mountains · Over 300 forts · Havaldar · Sabnis · Sarnaubat · Bargirs
