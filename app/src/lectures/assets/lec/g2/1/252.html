Recap

  • Gana-sanghas – Vajji, Malla, Shakya — Vajji confederacy · Lichchhavis · Vaishali · world's first republic · Chetaka · Mahavira · Malla
  • Sources Listing the Mahajanapadas — Anguttara Nikaya + Bhagavati Sutra · Panini · Pushkalavati · 8 chapters
  • Kingdoms and Rulers of the Buddha's Time — Four great powers · Magadha, Kosala, Vatsa, Avanti · Kashi · Pradyota · Bimbisara and Udayana · Vasavadatta · Bhasa's Svapnavasavadatta
  • Rivers and the South – Assaka on the Godavari — Assaka (Ashmaka) · only Mahajanapada south of the Vindhyas · Godavari · Dakshinapatha · Paithan (ancient Pratishthana) · Suttanipata · Bavari's
