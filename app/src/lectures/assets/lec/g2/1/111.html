RecapEconomic Impact of Colonial Rule – Drain of Wealth, Deindustrialisation, Commercialisation of Agriculture

Recap

  • Drain of Wealth and the Economic Nationalists — Drain of wealth · Britain · adequate economic or material return · British · Began · Diwani of Bengal (1765) · favourable balance of trade
  • Stages of Colonialism; One-way Free Trade and Deindustrialisation — leading exporter of manufactures · supplier of raw materials · Charter Act of 1813 · one-way free trade · duty-free / nominal duty · heavy duties in Britain · Imperial preference
  • Modern Industry, Currency and the Economy at Independence — "Indian domination of Indian markets" · Indian capitalists · Hilton Young Commission (1926) · 1s 6d · RBI 1935 · chawls · strong pre-British industrial base
  • Andhra Crafts and Industries Under British Rule — Decline · lost foreign and local markets · Eluru carpets · Kurnool rugs · Persian immigrants during Muslim rule · London Exhibition, 1851 · Kamsalis
  • Commercialisation of Agriculture; Opium and Peasant Laws — Commercialisation of Indian agriculture · cash revenue · famines · exploited by merchants and middlemen · Ganjam / Deccan · Triangular trade · opium
  • Social Changes under British Rule – Laws, New Classes, Caste and Women — Rule of law · Indian Penal Code (1860) · Macaulay · one law to all castes · Social legislation in sequence · New social classes · middle class
