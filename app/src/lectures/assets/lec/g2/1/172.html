RecapLater Reorganisation of States – 1960s to 2000; Creation of Telangana 2014

Recap

  • AP Reorganisation Act 2014 – Parts and Sections — 12 Parts, 108 Sections, 13 Schedules · 58.32 : 41.68 · without the AP Assembly's consent · Sec 50 and Sec 51 · life, liberty and property · not employment · A.P.V.N. Sarma
  • Bifurcation – Dates, Districts, Seats and Apportionment — 10 districts · 13 districts · Seemandhra · AP was not formed on 2 June 2014 · not Madhya Pradesh · Amaravati, 1 Jan 2019 · lost Hyderabad
  • Jai Andhra 1972–73, Six-Point Formula and 32nd Amendment — 1972–73 · against the Mulki Rules · separate Andhra State · Main / immediate cause · Supreme Court judgment of October 1972 · Kakani Venkataratnam · B.V. Subba Reddy
  • New States After 1956 and Article 3 — President's recommendation · 5th Amendment, 1955 · not binding · simple majority · Articles 3 and 4 · Puducherry · Union Territory
  • Capital, Districts and Polavaram after 2014 — Amaravati · 3 Mar 2022 · June 2024 · AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Act, 2026 · Amaravati sole, permanent capital · 2 June 2024 · 28 Mar 2026
  • Separate Telangana Movement 2001–2014 — Srikrishna Committee · Justice B.N. Srikrishna · Member-Secretary V.K. Duggal · smaller State · Samaikyandhra · united · Hyderabad – the bone of contention
  • Mulki Rules and Jai Telangana 1969 — 1888 · Prime Minister's (Diwan's) · 1919 Nizam's Farman · Nizam's personal permission · Mulki · 15 years · 12 years
  • Presidential Order 1975, GO 610 and Girglani — 6 zones · I–III Coastal Andhra · IV Rayalaseema · V–VI Telangana · Hyderabad in Zone VI · GO 610 · 30 Dec 1985
