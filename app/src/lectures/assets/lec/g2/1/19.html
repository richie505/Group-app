Recap

  • Six Orthodox Schools – Founders, Texts and Pairs — Three pairs · oldest darshana = Samkhya · earliest exponent · Uttara Mimamsa = Badarayana = Vyasa · Outside the six · Smriti · Lokayata
  • Rigvedic Gods – Indra, Agni, Varuna, Soma — War-god · Agni · Priest of the gods · Brihaspati · Later Vedic · Prajapati · Vishnu
  • Vedanta Schools – Shankara, Ramanuja, Madhva — Shankara · four mathas · Sringeri · Puri · Dwarka · Badrinath / Jyotirmath (Joshimath) · crypto-Buddhist
  • Mimamsa – Jaimini, Kumarila Bhatta, Karma and Apurva — Founder · Jaimini · Kumarila Bhatta · Prabhakara · Purva Mimamsa · Slokavartika · defended Vedic ritual (karma-kanda)
  • Doctrines of the Schools – Samkhya, Nyaya, Vaisheshika — Samkhya · accepts rebirth · self-knowledge · forces of nature, not a divine agency · Nyaya · 16 padarthas · Vaisheshika
  • Astika vs Nastika; Charvaka (Lokayata) — Test · belief in the authority of the Vedas · Charvaka = Lokayata · Brihaspati · materialism · perception · Ajita Kesakambali
  • Nature of Vedic Religion – Rita, Dharma, Sacrifice — Nature worship · sacrifice (yajna) central · no temples, no idols · Henotheism · Max Müller · Rita · Varuna
  • Upanishadic Philosophy — Main theme = philosophy · Atman is Brahman · mechanical sacrifice · ignorance · Katha Upanishad · Nachiketa · Yama
