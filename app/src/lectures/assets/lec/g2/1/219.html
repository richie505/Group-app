  • Hampi Temples – Virupaksha, Vitthala, Hazara Rama — Musical pillars · Vittala temple, Hampi · Virupaksha is at Hampi · wrong pair · Kings ruled on behalf of Virupaksha · Vidyaranya's portrait · Ugra Narasimha

  • Tadipatri and Other Andhra Temples — Tadipatri (Anantapur) · Chintala Venkataramana · Bugga Ramalingeswara · Pemmasani Nayakas · Ubattu Shilpalu · Vatapatra Shayi · Ekambaranatha, Kanchipuram
  • The Vijayanagara Style – Raya Gopurams, Kalyana Mandapas, Secular Buildings — Raya gopurams · Krishnadevaraya · Kalyana mandapas · Vijayanagara · Percy Brown · secular buildings in temple premises · Indo-Saracenic / Indo-Islamic building
  • Forts, Cities and Later Capitals — Gooty (Gutti) fort · 7th century · Bukka I · Penukonda fort · Vijayanagara · Aravidu capital (1570) · Chandragiri
