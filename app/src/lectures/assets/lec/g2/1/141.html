RecapRevolutionary Nationalism – Second Phase: Hsra, Bhagat Singh, Chandrashekhar Azad, Surya Sen, Kakori and Lahore Cases

Recap

  • Lahore Conspiracy Case (1929-31), the Executions and Jatin Das — Trial · Sukhdev · Rajguru · Saunders murder · Lahore Conspiracy Case · death sentence came in October 1930 · Assembly Bomb Case
  • HRA (1924) and HSRA (1928) — Azad · renamed · HSRA · founder (leader) of the HSRA · HRA founders – dispute · Sanyal, Bismil, Chatterjee · Shiv Verma
  • Saunders, the Assembly Bomb and Other Assassinations — Lala Lajpat Rai · anti-Simon · Bhagat Singh, Azad and Rajguru · ASP J.P. Saunders · SP James A. Scott · Rajguru fired first · Maulana Hasrat Mohani
  • Kakori Train Action (9 August 1925) — HRA · 8-Down train at Kakori near Lucknow · finance the organisation · 43 arrested · Jagat Narain Mulla · Govind Ballabh Pant · Chandra Shekhar Azad
  • Writings, Ideology and the Naujawan Bharat Sabha — Bismil Azimabadi · Ram Prasad Bismil · criticism and independent thinking · by the masses, for the masses · Naujawan Bharat Sabha · Bhagat Singh · Lahore, March 1926
  • Chittagong Armoury Raid (18 April 1930) and Bengal — Surya Sen ("Masterda") · April 1930 · cut telephone and telegraph lines · Provisional Revolutionary Government · Jalalabad Hills · 16 February 1933 · hanged 12 January 1934
  • Revolutionaries – Birth and Death — APPSC (2024) · not hanged
