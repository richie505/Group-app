Recap

  • Protests: Tagore, Sankaran Nair, Gandhi, Montagu — in protest at the Punjab atrocities · Malaviya, Jinnah, Mazhar-ul-Haq · Imperial Legislative Council · Rowlatt Act · Michael O'Dwyer's libel suit · only Sankaran Nair · Viceroy's Executive Council
  • Jallianwala Bagh Massacre – 13 April 1919 — Why the crowd gathered · 9 April 1919 · Dr Saifuddin Kitchlew and Dr Satyapal · Baisakhi · Marcella Sherwood · blocked the exit · no warning
  • Rowlatt Act, 1919 — Aim · revolutionary nationalists · Provisions · arrest without warrant · three High Court judges · no appeal · habeas corpus
  • Chronology – Swadeshi to Quit India — Bardoli · Tebhaga · Pochampalli · Chauri Chaura
  • Rowlatt Satyagraha — first all-India struggle · used the Home Rule League · urban · Sedition Committee · Simon Commission (1928) · not · enactment of the Rowlatt Act
  • Hunter Committee and the Congress Inquiry — Disorders Inquiry Committee · 14 October 1919 · Lord William Hunter · Jallianwala Bagh massacre · Bombay, Delhi and Punjab · Chimanlal H. Setalvad · Pandit Jagat Narayan
  • Udham Singh — Sir Michael O'Dwyer · 13 March 1940 · Caxton Hall, London · not General Dyer · Ram Mohammad Singh Azad
