RecapGandhi-irwin Pact, Round Table Conferences, Communal Award, Poona Pact; Resumption and Withdrawal

Recap

  • Gandhi-Irwin Pact (Delhi Pact), 1931 — Signed 5 March 1931 · Lord Irwin · Main purpose · suspended the CDM · Second RTC · Not in the Pact · 18% reservation for depressed classes
  • Chronology, 1920-1935 — More events asked in orders
  • Karachi Session, March 1931 – Fundamental Rights Resolution — 29-31 March 1931 · endorse the Gandhi-Irwin Pact · President: Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel · black-flag demonstrations · admired their bravery · disapproved of political violence · reiterated Purna Swaraj
  • Resumption and Withdrawal of the CDM, 1932-34 — constructive programmes · service to Harijans · eradication of untouchability · preaching ahimsa
  • Round Table Conferences, 1930-32 — Second RTC attendees · Sarojini Naidu · Madan Mohan Malaviya · Dr Rajendra Prasad did NOT attend · V.V. Jogayya Pantulu · separate-Andhra resolution · all three
  • Communal Award and Poona Pact, 1932 — Gandhi's fast unto death · Yerwada Jail, Poona · 20 September 1932 · Poona Pact: 24 September 1932 · Ambedkar · Madan Mohan Malaviya · Harijan Sevak Sangh
