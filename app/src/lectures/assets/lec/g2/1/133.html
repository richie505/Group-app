RecapGrowth of Nationalism – Factors; Pre-congress Political Associations

Recap

  • Pre-Congress Political Associations – Bengal, Bombay and London — All the above were pre-Congress bodies · Poona Sarvajanik Sabha · 1875 petition to the House of Commons · Debendranath Tagore · British Indian Association · G.V. Joshi at the Delhi Durbar (1877) · Dec 1883
  • Nationalist Literature – Anandamath, Vande Mataram, Nil Darpan — Vande Mataram · Bankim Chandra Chatterjee · National Song · Telugu translation · Y. Doraiswamayya and M. Ramarao · The Cry of Mother India · 15 Dec 1905
  • Madras and Andhra Associations — Founder and first president · Gazula (Gajula) Lakshminarasu Chetty · The Crescent · Somasundaram Chettiyar · First political body of South India · Petitions · Charter renewal of 1853
  • Factors of Nationalism; Lytton and the Ilbert Bill — K.M. Panikkar · unification of India · Western education · English as a link language · educated middle class · Press and literature · rediscovery of India's past
  • Surendranath Banerjee — ICS · 1869 · dismissed in 1874 · Indian Association (1876) · 21 → 19 · Indian National Conference (1883, 1885) · merged with the INC in 1886
  • Nationalist and Telugu Newspapers — Swarajya · Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao · First Telugu journal = Satyadoota · Kesari · Mahratta · Poona, 4 January 1881 · Tilak and G.G. Agarkar
