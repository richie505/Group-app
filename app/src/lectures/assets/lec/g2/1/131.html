RecapMuslim, Sikh and Parsi Reform – Aligarh, Deoband, Ahmadiya, Wahabi, Faraizi, Singh Sabha

Recap

  • Sir Syed Ahmad Khan and the Aligarh Movement — judicial service · loyalist · never connected with the Congress · English / Western scientific education · rational reading of the Quran · Urdu · her two eyes
  • Faraizi, Taiyuni and Ahmadiyya Movements — Faraizi (Fara'idi) Movement · 1818 · Haji Shariatullah · East Bengal (Faridpur) · followers of Haji Shariatullah · Dudu (Dadu) Miyan · 1840
  • Wahabi Movement – Barelvi, Patna, Anti-British; Hyderabad Angle — Abdul Wahab of Arabia · Shah Waliullah · Syed Ahmad Barelvi · Dar-ul-Harb into Dar-ul-Islam · Sikhs · British · anti-British
  • Deoband School (Darul Uloom) and Nadwatul Ulama — Darul Uloom, Deoband, 1866 · Aims · Quran and Hadis · keep alive jihad against foreign rulers · train religious leaders · welcomed the Congress · fatwa (1888)
