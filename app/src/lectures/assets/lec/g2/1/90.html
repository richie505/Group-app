RecapRevolt of 1857 – Economic, Socio-religious, Administrative and Military Causes

Recap

  • Immediate Cause – Enfield Rifle and Greased Cartridges — Immediate cause · Enfield rifle · cow and pig fat · bitten off · interfering in religion · replaced the Brown Bess · December 1856
  • Military Grievances – the Sepoys Start the Revolt — began as a mutiny of the sepoys · discontent of soldiers · "peasants in uniform" · Crimean and Persian wars · Not a cause · inefficiency of the officers · Earlier mutinies
  • Socio-religious Causes — Christian missionaries · police protection · railways and the telegraph · religious neutrality
  • Books on 1857 and Other Books — Sir Syed Ahmed Khan · Asbab-e-Baghawat-e-Hind · first Indian-language work · no Indian in the Legislative Council · P.E. Roberts · sepoy mutiny
  • Economic, Political and Administrative Causes — "Absentee sovereignty" · "drain of wealth"
