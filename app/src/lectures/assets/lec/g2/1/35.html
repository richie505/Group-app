Recap

  • Chandragupta II Vikramaditya – Saka Conquest, Silver Coins, Titles and Fa-hien — Killed his elder brother · Chandragupta II · Ramagupta · Dhruvadevi (Dhruvaswamini) · Vishakhadatta's Devichandraguptam · Order · 380–415 CE
  • The Hunas – Skandagupta, Toramana, Mihirakula and Yashodharman — Hunas = Hephthalites / White Huns · Oxus · Gandhara · Toramana · Gupta ruler who defeated the Hunas · Skandagupta · Reign of the Huna invasion
  • Gupta Chronology, Kumaragupta I and Nalanda — Order asked (APPSC) · "Gupta dynasty ruled" (CGPSC 2003) · 319–500 AD · Distractors · Bindusara · Titles · Shakraditya, Mahendraditya
  • Gupta Decline and the Successor States — NOT a cause · Arab invasion · Signs of collapse · less gold, more alloy · payments in land · western Malwa and Saurashtra · UPSC 2021
