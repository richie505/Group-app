RecapMughal Administration – Mansabdari, Jagirdari and the Jagirdari Crisis

Recap

  • Mansabdari System – Origin, Purpose and Nature — Akbar · Arabic · rank/position · borrowed from Mongolia/Central Asia · One grading for army and civil service · military system · Purpose (UPSC 1996 key)
  • Zat, Sawar, Classes and Later Changes — Three classes · sawar could NOT exceed zat · Chehra · not a rank · umara · 1,000 zat or above · Horse rule
  • Army Terms and Central Officials — revived · waqia-navis · khufia-navis · harkaras
  • Jagirs and the Jagirdari Crisis — Jagir · in lieu of salary · Mauza = village · Madad-i-maash · 3-4 years · bejagiri · Aurangzeb
