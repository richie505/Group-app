Recap

  • Folk Dances and Theatre of Other States — Bahurupiya · Jankilal · Tamasha · Gopiji Bhatt · Gavri (Gowari) · Bhils · Bhavai
  • Handicrafts, Textiles and GI Tags of Andhra Pradesh — Panchanam varu · Andhra · Lepakshi · 2017 · Tapeswaram Kaja · bamboo work · 2014
  • Fairs, Jataras and Temple Towns — Kumbh Mela 2019 · Prayagraj · Nasik · Godavari · Medaram Jathara (Sammakka–Saralamma) · Mulugu district, Telangana · Kurnool tourist pair
  • Classical Dances – Kuchipudi and the Eight Forms — Kuchipudi · Andhra Pradesh · Siddhendra Yogi · Abul Hasan Tana Shah · Kuchipudi vs Bharatanatyam (IAS 2012) · speak dialogue · Tarangam
  • Festivals of India and the World — Onam · Chingam = August–September
  • Crafts, Textiles and Ornaments of Other States — NE handicraft centres (IAS 2006) · Mon – Nagaland · Nalbari – Assam · Pasighat – Arunachal Pradesh · Tura – Meghalaya · 4-2-1-3 · Rajasthan costume and ornaments (RAS)
  • Festivals of Andhra Pradesh — Ugadi · Telugu New Year · Sankranti · Kanuma · thanksgiving to cattle and birds · Kolla Pandalu · Vinayaka Chavithi
  • Folk Arts, Songs and Sports of Andhra — Folk arts (Janapada Kalalu) · Janapadam · Biruduraju Ramaraju · World Folk Day: 22 August · Gathasaptasati · couple and choir dances · Folk songs
