Recap

  • rDNA Technology – Tools, Vectors and Steps — chimaeric (chimeric) DNA · Transgenic organism · foreign gene from another species · desired characteristic or product · GMO · in ways that would not occur naturally · Across species
  • Bt Technology and Bt Cotton — Bt = *Bacillus thuringiensis · soil bacterium · Cry (delta-endo) toxins · protoxin · alkaline gut · resistance to insects · both true, R explains A
  • Golden Rice and Other GE Crop Traits — pro-vitamin A · vitamin A deficiency · Genes · daffodil · Ingo Potrykus and Peter Beyer · Beta-carotene · vitamin A precursor
  • Firsts – Insulin, Cloning and Transgenic Animals — Mon 863 · GM pest-resistant maize
  • Genome Editing – CRISPR-Cas9 and Genome-edited Rice — CRISPR · defence system of bacteria and archaea · Cas9 = the molecular scissors · guide RNA · gene (genome) editing · not · Gene editing
  • Environmental Impact of Transgenics and Terminator Seeds — pollen · superweeds · build-up of insect resistance · harm to non-target organisms · genetic homogenisation · centre of diversity · brinjal
  • Gene Therapy and Genetic Medicine — Gene therapy · Genetic medicines · correct or compensate for faulty genes · NOT alter the entire DNA sequence · Vectors · Somatic · germline
