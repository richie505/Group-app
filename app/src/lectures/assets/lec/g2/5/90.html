  • World Ozone Day — 16 September · UNGA on 19 December 1994 · 10 years of the Kigali Amendment

  • Chemicals and Waste Conventions – Basel, Rotterdam, Stockholm, Minamata — 1992 · Ban Amendment · OECD/EU + Liechtenstein · 5 December 2019 · PIC · importing country must agree · acceded 2005
