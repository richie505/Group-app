RecapBiodiversity – Levels, Values, Measurement; India As A Megadiverse Country

Recap

  • Values of Biodiversity and Ecosystem Services — Most significant aspect · maintenance of the ecosystem · Butterfly decline (UPSC 2017) · wasps, spiders and birds · I and III · Himalayan nettle · textile fibre
  • Meaning, Definition and Levels of Biodiversity — "biodiversity" · Walter G. Rosen (1985) · popularised by E.O. Wilson · different species · genetic diversity · species and ecosystems · CBD (1992) definition
  • Measuring Biodiversity – Alpha, Beta, Gamma — R.H. Whittaker · Shannon-Wiener · Simpson · same richness, A more even · Functional diversity · ecological roles · mist nets
  • Richness Patterns – Latitude, Altitude, Himalaya — Latitudinal gradient · increases towards the equator · lower latitudes · lower altitudes · tropical rainforests · coral reefs · Butterflies
  • Biogeographic Zones and Natural Range of Indian Fauna — Rodgers and Panwar (WII, 1988) · 2 realms · Palearctic + Indo-Malayan · 5 biomes · 10 zones · 25 provinces · Deccan provinces (APPSC FRO)
  • India as a Megadiverse Country — 17 megadiverse countries · ≥5,000 endemic plants · India · not Thailand · LMMC · Cancun Declaration 2002 · 2025 additions
  • Agro-biodiversity and Biopiracy — N.I. Vavilov · 8 centres · Hindustan (Indo-Burma) · Nokrek · Citrus Gene Sanctuary · Borjuli · rice
  • Andhra Pradesh Biodiversity — Eastern Ghats · Mahanadi to the Nilgiris · Arma Konda (~1,680 m, ASR district) · Mahendra Girulu · Srikakulam · not a global hotspot · Seshachalam BR (2010)
