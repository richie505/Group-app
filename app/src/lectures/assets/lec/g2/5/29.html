RecapSatellites for Defence and Academic Purposes – GSAT-7 Series, Edusat, Risat

Recap

  • Student and Academic Satellites; ISRO Student Programmes — GSAT-1 · first GSLV payload (2001)
  • RISAT Series – Radar Imaging Satellites — RISAT = Radar Imaging Satellite · Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) · day and night · through clouds · before · 12 Jan 2026 · PSLV-C62/EOS-N1
  • PSLV-C37 Record and Foreign Co-passengers — 104 satellites · 714 kg Cartosat-2 series · 2 ISRO nanosats · 101 foreign · 6 countries · Over 400 foreign satellites · PSLV-C62
  • EDUSAT (GSAT-3) – Education Satellite — GSAT-3 = EDUSAT · 20 September 2004 · GSLV-F01 · Sriharikota · Two-way satellite communication
  • GSAT-7 Series and Defence Space — TSAT-1A · Tata Advanced Systems · Satellogic · Vemagal (Karnataka) · SpaceX Falcon 9
