RecapOther Renewables – Biomass, Biofuels, Small Hydro, Geothermal, Tidal, Waste-to-energy

Recap

  • Biogas and Biomass Energy — Biomass energy · photosynthesis · Gobar gas = biomass energy · Composting · aerobic · "Gramalakshmi" · Jashbhai Patel (1950s)
  • Bioethanol – Feedstocks, Gasohol, Power Alcohol and Global Producers — Bioethanol · fermentation · Saccharomyces cerevisiae · USA · largest producer, consumer and exporter · Brazil · mandatory ethanol blending since 1976
  • Geothermal Energy — Source · Earth's interior · decay of radioactive · hot springs, geysers, volcanoes · geothermal and nuclear · 24x7 baseload · India's potential
  • Biodiesel – Jatropha, Pongamia and RUCO — Biodiesel · transesterification · Palm stearin oil · 94% · RUCO · FSSAI · used cooking oil into biodiesel
  • Tidal, Wave, OTEC and Desalination — 5 m or more · Gulf of Mannar · Ocean energy potential (MNRE) · 12,455 MW · 40,000 MW · Sindhuja-I · IIT Madras
  • Aviation Biofuels and Sustainable Aviation Fuel — First biofuel flight in India · SpiceJet · Dehradun–Delhi, 27 August 2018 · CSIR-IIP, Dehradun · SAF · drop-in · 50%
  • Biofuel Generations and Algal Fuels — Biobutanol · Algal biofuel · and on land · Limitations · high expertise/technology · large-scale facilities
  • National Policy on Biofuels and Ethanol Blending — NPB 2018 · E20 + B5 by 2030 · basic · advanced · bio-CNG · not groundnut seeds or horse gram · Surplus food grains
