Recap

  • Observed Impacts – Ice Sheets, Sea Level, Tipping Points and Species — significant reduction in ice-sheet mass · quadrupled since the 1990s · shrinking mountain glaciers · Arctic and Greenland ice sheet · about four times · WMO (2025 report) · over 90% of excess heat
  • India's Temperature Record and Warmest Years — Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES) · first national climate assessment · about 0.7°C over 1901-2018 · IMD · 1875 · New Delhi · 2016
  • Climate Risk Index and Vulnerability — Global Climate Risk Index (CRI) · Germanwatch · Bonn · Children's Climate Risk Index · UNICEF (2021) · exposure · vulnerability
  • Agriculture, Monsoon and Adaptation — Climate-Smart Village · CCAFS · CGIAR · France · ICRISAT (Patancheru, Hyderabad) · reducing wheat · wheat/maize lose 4-10% yield per 1°C
  • Heat Waves, IMD Alerts and Wet-bulb Temperature — Heat wave (IMD) · 40°C in plains · 30°C in hilly regions · ≥45°C regardless of normal · neither · Causes · Rossby waves → heat domes
  • Coasts and Cyclones – India and Andhra Pradesh — Cyclone-prone area · 8% of India's land area · more frequent in the Bay of Bengal · 52% · Marine heatwaves · winds up to 20% · rain up to 12%
  • Himalayan Glaciers and GLOFs — Himansh · NCPOR (Goa) · Lahaul-Spiti · 2016 · GSI · Gangotri · 1,727 m since 1935
