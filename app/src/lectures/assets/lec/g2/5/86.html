  • Water-borne Pollutant Diseases — Mercury → Minamata; nitrate → blue baby · not · methaemoglobin · Metal-toxicity diseases · dermatitis · DDT · calcium metabolism in birds
  • Measuring Water Pollution – DO, BOD, Coliform, Solids — dissolved oxygen · less DO · water · Coliform count · faecal contamination by human wastes · Ganga · effluents

  • River and Lake Conservation Plans — Swaroop Sagar · Rally for Rivers · Isha Foundation · AP under NRCP
  • Groundwater Contamination — Arsenic · non-biotic · arsenic, fluoride, uranium · Nalgonda technique · alum + lime + bleaching powder · does penalise
  • Drinking-water Standards — CPCB designated best-use classes · 2,500 MPN/100 ml
