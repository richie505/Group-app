RecapRenewable Energy in Andhra Pradesh – Potential, Installed Capacity and State Policy

Recap

  • Solar Parks – Concepts and the Largest Parks — Solar Park Scheme · 12 December 2014 · ₹25 lakh per park · ₹20 lakh/MW or 30% · Ultra Mega Solar Park = 500 MW or more · Largest solar park of India (2023) · Bhadla Solar Park, Rajasthan
  • AP Solar, Hybrid and Floating Projects — Match (APPSC G2 Mains 2025)
  • Installed Capacity, Potential and Ranking — 10,639.75 MW · 3,865.63 MW · State ranking (31 Mar 2026) · 15,980 MW · 5.8% · 6th · Gujarat
  • State Policies and New Investments — Land policy (Apr 2026) · 50,000 acres of barren land · avoid diversion of farmland · SAEL 600 MW · Kadapa · Tata Renewable 800 MW hybrid · Tata Power + Suzlon (Aug 2025)
  • Power Plants, Pumped Storage and Agencies — ~267 GW · ~7 GW · 42,020 MW
  • Rooftop and Farm Solar in AP — PM Surya Ghar in AP · 20.5 lakh SC/ST households · Virtual Net Metering · PM-KUSUM in AP · 2.93 lakh farm pumps
