Recap

  • INSAT System — INSAT = Indian National Satellite · multipurpose · meteorology · not astronomy · Meteorological Satellite · DoS, DoT, IMD, AIR, Doordarshan · ICC
  • GSAT Series – Key Satellites — Other astronomy facts · ASTROSAT · Kepler · Hughes Communications India · China · Shijian-20 · Long March 5
  • Geostationary Orbit – Physics of a Communication Satellite — Geostationary · fixed height · not depend on its mass · What keeps it in orbit · centripetal force · No energy needed · Global TV
  • Strategic and Regional Satellites — 2,000 km · South Asia Satellite = GSAT-9 · 5 May 2017 · Pakistan opted out
  • Transponder Bands and Applications — MF band is NOT used · BharatNet · DTH · VSAT · DSNG · MSS · Swayam Prabha
