RecapEnvironmental Legislation in India – Water Act 1974, Air Act 1981, Environment (protection) Act 1986

Recap

  • Chronology of Environmental Acts — 1978, 1988, 2024 · 1987 · Older / other Acts · 1927 · Indian Fisheries Act 1897 · 1995 · Other milestones
  • Environment (Protection) Act 1986 — Bhopal Gas Tragedy (December 1984) · Stockholm Conference, 1972 · Article 253 · umbrella legislation · Central Government · Public Account · 75% to States/UTs, 25% to Centre
  • CPCB, SPCBs and MoEFCC — Statutory body · September 1974 · Water Act · MoEFCC · coordinates SPCBs · NAMP · National AQI 2015
  • Air Act 1981 — air quality standards · 29 March 1981 · 16 May 1981 · 7 chapters, 54 sections · CPCB got air functions · Noise · 1987 amendment
  • Constitutional Provisions — 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976
  • Consent Mechanism and Industry Categories — SPCB · Consent to Establish (CTE) · Consent to Operate (CTO) · 29 Jan 2026 reform · CTO valid until cancelled
  • Penalties and Jan Vishwas Act 2023 — 5 years or Rs 1 lakh · Rs 5,000 per day · decriminalised · adjudicating officer · NGT · PLI Act 1991 · Rs 25,000 to Rs 5 lakh
  • Water Act 1974 and Water Cess Act 1977 — prevent and control water pollution · Article 252 · CPCB · 1988 amendment · 8 chapters, 64 sections · discharge of effluents · Section 33A
