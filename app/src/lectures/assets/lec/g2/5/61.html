RecapCoal Sector – Reserves, Production, Washing, Commercial Mining, Gasification

Recap

  • Coal Types, Carbon Content and Fuel Quality — 25–35% · Calorific value · carbon content · high · Coal is a natural (fossil) fuel · Lignite · Gondwana
  • Coalfields, Reserves and Producing States — Mosabani · copper · Damodar Valley = "Ruhr of India" · one-fourth · Reserves · 400.715 bn t (1.4.2025) · 389.421 bn t (1.4.2024)
  • Indian Coal Quality, Imports, Washing and Health — Indian coal · high ash (35–45%) · low calorific value · low sulphur (~0.5%) · high ash-fusion temperature (~1500°C) · arsenic, lead and mercury · SO₂ and NOx
  • Production and Coal Companies — 2024-25: about 1,047.5 MT · 1 billion tonnes · 55% · Coal India Ltd · Kolkata · Maharatna · largest coal producer
  • Commercial Mining and Laws — 204 · MMDR · sale of coal from captive mines · SHAKTI (2017) · New Coal Distribution Policy 2007 · auction or tariff-based bidding · Revenue-sharing commercial auctions
  • Gasification, CBM and UCG — Gasification · syngas (CO + H₂ + CH₄ + CO₂) · IGCC · ethanol · urea · not nitroglycerine · CBM
  • Andhra Pradesh – Coal Plants — APGENCO · MCL's IB Valley Washery
