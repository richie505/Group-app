RecapBiodiversity Hotspots in India, Endemism and Biological Diversity Act 2002

Recap

  • India's Four Hotspots – Which Region Is, Which Is Not — not · 2004 reappraisal · Himalaya · Old framing (25 hotspots, 1999–2000) · two · North-East / Eastern Himalaya · Western Ghats
  • Endemic and Signature Species of India's Hotspots — Endemism · one region only · Birds of the hotspots (UPSC 2020) · Not a bird (UPSC 2022) · Golden Mahseer · fish · UPSC 2023 (nocturnal)
  • Hotspot Concept, Criteria and Count — Definition · threatened with destruction · Norman Myers (British ecologist), 1988 · Conservation International (CI) in 1989 · reassessed · Not · richness, endemism and threat
  • Western Ghats – India's Richest Biodiversity Region — Sahyadri · 1,600 km · 6 States · Water tower of peninsular India · 8 hottest hotspots · Silent Valley NP · 5,000
  • Biological Diversity Act 2002 and 2023 Amendment — CBD (1992) · December 2002 · 5 February 2003 · Section 37 · Section 38 · Amendment Act 2023 · 1 April 2024
  • BMCs, PBRs and Biodiversity Heritage Sites — PBRs are prepared by BMCs · 2.62 lakh BMCs · 2.72 lakh PBRs · Nagoya Protocol · BHS · State Government
  • Access and Benefit Sharing — excludes human genetic material · Nagoya Protocol 2010 · Kani model · Arogyapacha · TBGRI · Jeevani · ₹266 crore
