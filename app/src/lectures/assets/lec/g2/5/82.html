RecapForest Rights Act 2006, Campa, Compensatory Afforestation, Jfm, Agroforestry

Recap

  • Afforestation, Forestry Terms and Their Benefits — Benefits · afforestation controls floods · checks soil erosion by running water · Floods worsened by · NOT reforestation · Criticism of social forestry · commercial monocultures
  • Joint Forest Management and People's Forest Movements — JFM · Arabari, West Bengal (1971-72) · NFP 1988 · MoEF guidelines 1 June 1990 · Vana Samrakshana Samithis (VSS) · government-initiated · 1,669 VSS
  • Afforestation Schemes – NAP, NAEB, GIM, Nagar Van — Green India Mission goals · 5 million ha · another 5 million ha · 3 million households · 2 million ha of non-forest land · GIM revised 2021-30 · CAG
  • Agroforestry — National Agroforestry Policy, Feb 2014 · first country · GROW report (NITI Aayog, Feb 2024) · ~8.65% · ICAR-CAFRI · Jhansi · Sub-Mission on Agroforestry (SMAF)
  • Forest Rights Act 2006 — Nodal ministry · Tribal Affairs · 13 December 2005 · Eligible · STs · OTFDs · 3 generations (75 years)
  • Compensatory Afforestation and CAMPA — equal non-forest land · twice the degraded forest land · CA cost + NPV · CAMPA · non-lapsable, interest-bearing · 90% States : 10% National · National CAMPA
