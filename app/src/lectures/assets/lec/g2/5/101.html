  • Three Pillars and Types of Sustainability — Triple bottom line · People, Planet, Profit · NOT a pillar · Sustainability · Strategy → example · solar power · crop rotation
  • Precursors and Thinkers — Green Development · W.M. (Bill) Adams · When did the term come in? · 1980 · 1987
