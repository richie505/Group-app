RecapSpace Policy and Commercialisation – Indian Space Policy 2023, In-space, Nsil, Private Launch Sector

Recap

  • Private Launch, Propulsion and Satellite Firms — Green propulsion (MoD, Feb 2024) · environment-friendly · low orbit · 3rd country (after US and China) · VIT-AP University's VISWA-M · POEM = PSLV Orbital Experimental Module · PS4
  • NSIL and Demand-driven Missions — Demand-driven model · builds, owns and operates · assured customer demand · 141 satellites (138 customer)
  • Indian Space Policy 2023 and Space Legislation — Indian Space Policy 2023 · released by DoS 20 April 2023 · will not be privatised or reduced · no Space Activities Act
  • Organisations – Antrix, NSIL, IN-SPACe — Chairman Dr Pawan Kumar Goenka · Pixxel · SSLV · Technology Adoption Fund · Space City
  • Space Reforms 2020, FDI and Space Economy — June 2020 reforms · Non-Governmental Entities (NGEs) · 100%
  • Andhra Pradesh – Space Policy 4.0 and Space Cities — AP Space Policy 4.0 · space cities · ₹25,000 crore · Tirupati Space City · 3,000 acres near Sriharikota · Lepakshi Space City · R&D
