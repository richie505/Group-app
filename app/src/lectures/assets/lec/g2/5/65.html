Recap

  • Photovoltaic vs Solar Thermal; Solar Cells — Photovoltaic cells = solar cells · Material · silicon · 30.2% · Artificial satellites · Solar water pumps · surface and submersible
  • Solar Firsts — Jamnagar · Seshachalam · Hailey/Corbett (1936) · 1979 · Rio 1992
  • International Solar Alliance (ISA) — 112 · $1 trillion · Partners · ADB · ITEC
  • Installed Solar Capacity and the Leading States — FY 2025-26 · 44.61 GW · China · 3rd · Gujarat · total RE · NEDCAP
  • Solar Manufacturing – DCR, ALMM, PLI, Duties — Domestic Content Requirement (DCR) · National Solar Mission (2010) · developing solar power production · US challenged India's DCR at the WTO · CPSU, PM-KUSUM and rooftop-subsidy · June 2026 · 190 GW
  • Jawaharlal Nehru National Solar Mission — January 2010 · 8 missions of NAPCC (2008) · global leader in solar energy
  • Solar Potential of India — 250-300 clear sunny days · 4-7 kWh/m²/day · 5,000 trillion kWh · NISE estimate: 748 GW · 3% of wasteland · Rajasthan 23.70% · AP 9.10%
  • Solar Schemes – Purpose and Chronology — "33 Stack Defensive Scheme" · Green Energy Open Access Rules, 2022 · 1 MW → 100 kW · GTAM · IEX · CERC · Order
