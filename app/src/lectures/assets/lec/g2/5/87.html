Recap

  • Noise Pollution – Units, Sound Levels and Sources — Noise pollution is measured in decibels · Every 10 dB rise ≈ doubles the loudness · below 30 dB · jet flight · maximum noise · completely man-made · fire-crackers
  • Noise Rules 2000, Fire-cracker Standards and Noise Control — Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 · Art. 21 · Day = 6 am–10 pm; night = 10 pm–6 am · Noise Rules · Silence zone · 100 m · NANMN
  • Marine Pollution – Microplastics, Oil Spills and GloLitter — ultimate sink · radioactive wastes · oil spills · humans too · plastic is not its only form · Ennore oil spill (28 Jan 2017) · BW Maple
  • Radioactive Pollution – Sources, Effects and Units — Radon · noble gas · cancer treatment · Chernobyl · radioactive · half-life · genetic mutations (hereditary changes)
  • Radiological Emergencies – NDMA, MRDS, AERB and Tummalapalle — unclaimed (orphan) radioactive material · Mayapuri, Delhi (2010) · cobalt-60 · NDMA guidelines · 2009 · District Collector · AERB
  • Soil Pollution – Causes, Heavy Metals and Control — Definition · radioactive materials · Natural causes · acid rain · Man-made · pesticides, fertilisers · leachate
  • Bioremediation and Remediation of Contaminated Soil — Bioremediation · genetically engineered · Oil Zapper (and Oilivorous-S) · TERI · Heavy-metal removal from soil · immobilisation · soil washing
  • Thermal Pollution — Definition · thermal pollution · Main source · thermal and nuclear power plants · coolant · Effects · warm water holds less dissolved oxygen
