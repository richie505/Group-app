RecapAndhra Pradesh – Coastline, Deltas, Hill Ranges (eastern Ghats, Nallamala, Seshachalam)

Recap

  • Eastern Ghats Ranges in AP – Seshachalam, Nallamala, Velikonda, Mahendragiri — Tirupati / the Venkateswara temple · Seshachalam · Seshachalam = Eastern Ghats · Long mountain pass (>300 km) · Ganjikunta pass · Horsley Hills · Javadi, Sirumalai, Karanthamalai
  • Coastline – Length, Rank and End Points — Old figure · 974 km, 2nd longest · on the east coast · eastern boundary · Ichchapuram (Srikakulam) · Tada · Srikakulam to Nellore
  • Eastern Ghats – Character, Sections, Rocks and Limits — western side of the Coastal Plain · eastern side of the Deccan Plateau · major hill system of AP · Eastern Ghats · Discontinuous, eroded, residual hills · Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri · older and lower
  • Pulicat, Kolleru and Sriharikota — Sriharikota · near Pulicat Lake · Andhra Pradesh · Sullurpeta · Tada · separates Pulicat from the Bay · Uppalapadu
  • Highest Peaks of AP and the Eastern Ghats — Arma Konda · Jindhagada (1,690 m) · Chintapalli (Paderu) · Eastern Ghats · Visakhapatnam · Alluri Sitharama Raju
  • Coastal Plains and Deltas — Coastal plain · emergent Eastern Coastal Plains · Eastern Ghats and Coastal region · Deltas · Godavari · Krishna · "Rice Bowl"
  • Waterfalls, Caves and Hill Stations — Hill stations · Araku · Lambasingi · Horsley Hills · Maredumilli
