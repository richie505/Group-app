Recap

  • Coral Reef Places – World and India — World's largest coral reef system · Queensland, north-eastern Australia · Coral Sea (Pacific Ocean) · 2,300 km · false · 2,900 reefs · UNESCO World Heritage Site 1981
  • Marine Life, Ocean Zones and Productivity — 71% · hydrothermal vents · chemosynthetic · freshwater · Phytoplankton · main primary producers · about 50%
  • Coral Reefs – Nature, Conditions and Types — calcium carbonate · coral polyps · Cnidaria · aragonite · Oyster shells · Mutualism with zooxanthellae · food and colour
  • Coral Bleaching and Ocean Acidification — Meaning · expel (eject) their zooxanthellae · white · stressed, not necessarily dead · Most significant cause · global warming · marine heatwaves
