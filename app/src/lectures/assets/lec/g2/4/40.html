RecapAndhra Pradesh – Soil Types and Their Distribution

Recap

  • AP Soils by Area – Red First, Black Second — wrong · Red soils dominate the rainfed areas · are found in AP · granitoid-gneiss · alluvium · 45.6% · red dominates
  • Black Soils (Regur) – Names, Properties, Cotton — Vertisol · Very fine, clayey · very high water (moisture) retention · best soil for retaining moisture · needs little irrigation · self-ploughing · Rich
  • Deltaic Alluvium – the Most Fertile Soil — river-borne silt · river deposition · Godavari and Krishna · renewed with silt in each flood · Deltaic alluvial soils · most fertile · Konaseema
  • Red Soils of Andhra Pradesh — iron oxides (ferric oxide) · rich in iron · Poor · nitrogen · organic matter · potash · poor moisture-holding capacity
  • Laterite and Skeletal Soils — high temperature and heavy rainfall · intense leaching · hydrated iron and aluminium oxides · incorrect · ~1% of AP · acidic · poor fertility
  • Soil and Crop Suitability; Problem Soils and Soil Care — sand and loamy soils suit onion · Acid soils → lime · alkaline soils → gypsum or sulphur · decrease alkalinity · saline soils → drainage and leaching · unfit for cropping · Salinity
