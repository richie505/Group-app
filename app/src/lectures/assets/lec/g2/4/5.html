RecapInterior of the Earth – Crust, Mantle, Core; Discontinuities and Seismic Evidence

Recap

  • Layers of the Earth and Their Composition — Mantle = 83% of volume, 68% of mass · largest · core = 16% of volume, 32% of mass · Core is mainly iron · silicon · Mantle = "mixed metals and silicates" · basaltic
  • Seismic Waves, Shadow Zone and the Seismograph — P, S and L (surface) waves · body waves · P-waves are sound (acoustic) waves · reflection and refraction · S-wave · liquid outer core · Shadow zone
  • Earth's Magnetism, Heat and Pressure — Cause · currents in the liquid outer core · geodynamo · solar wind and cosmic radiation · reversals · Magnetic axis ~11.5° · Magnetic equator
  • Discontinuities, Lithosphere and Asthenosphere — phase changes · barysphere · delaminating · 100 km
  • Sources of Information about the Interior — indirect
