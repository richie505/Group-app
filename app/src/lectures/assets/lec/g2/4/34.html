Recap

  • Wettest and Driest Places, Rainfall Zones — funnel · stalagmites and stalactites · Highest rainfall regions · Kerala · Ajmer (dry) vs Shillong (very wet) · two-thirds · SW monsoon
  • Seasonal Share and Regional Gradients of Rainfall — Northern plains · decreases from east to west · Ganga basin · away from the Himalayan foothills · north to south · Peninsula · decreases from west to east
  • Drought – Types, Extent and Management — IMD · all-India drought year · more than 10% · West Bengal plateau · lateritic · Manual for Drought Management 2016 · Disaster Management Act = 2005
  • Rainfall Variability, El Nino and IMD Rainfall Categories — CV = (standard deviation / mean) × 100 · 2015 · Rainy day · 2.5 mm or more · 2026 · AP −42%
  • Flood-prone Areas — Ganga and Brahmaputra · 40 million ha ≈ 12% · floods and river erosion
