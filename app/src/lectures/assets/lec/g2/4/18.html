RecapAtmospheric Pressure – Pressure Belts; Planetary, Periodic and Local Winds; Jet Streams

Recap

  • Local Winds of the World and India — Cold · Mistral
  • Pressure Belts – Doldrums, Horse Latitudes, ITCZ — ITCZ · at / near the Equator · Doldrums · equatorial low-pressure belt · not · neither · Horse latitudes
  • Atmospheric Pressure – Causes, Gradient and Instruments — weight of the air column · gravitational pull · expands → density falls → low pressure · falls with altitude · nose bleeding · isobars · Pressure gradient
  • Planetary Winds and Wind Controls — Trade winds = planetary winds · each · western disturbances · NW European ports · North Atlantic Drift · west onto east coasts · Coriolis force
  • Jet Streams and the Polar Vortex — high-velocity westerly winds · upper troposphere near the tropopause · 10–12 km · both hemispheres · Sub-tropical jet · 10–16 km · Polar front jet is strongest in winter
  • Roaring Forties, Furious Fifties, Shrieking Sixties — westerlies of the Southern Hemisphere · less landmass · great strength and constancy · from north-west to east (west to east) · overcast skies, rain and raw weather · 2, 3 and 4 · not
  • Monsoon and Periodic Winds — "mausim" = season · Seasonal reversal of winds · differential heating of land and sea · high temperature and low pressure · south-west monsoon · from south-west to north-east · 1957
