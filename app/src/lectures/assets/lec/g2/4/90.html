Recap

  • Eighth Schedule – Scheduled Languages — 22 languages are officially recognised · Bodo · Bhojpuri was never in the list · Not included · English (never) · Rajasthani · Angika
  • Language Families of India — Four families · Dardic · Indo-Aryan · South-Central – Telugu · central and eastern India are Austric · Khasi (Mon-Khmer) · Romani
  • Official Language – Articles 343-351 and the Commission — No national language · none · official · Official Language Commission · 1955 · B.G. Kher · 1956
  • Speakers, Counts and Language Institutions — Marathi passed Telugu · Sanskrit, Bodo, Manipuri · Mandarin · English · Hindi 3rd · Bengali 7th · World language families
  • Andhra Pradesh – Telugu and Urdu — AP Official Language Act, 1966 · Telugu · 2022 Amendment · Urdu second official language statewide · Telugu and Urdu · classical since 2008 · Italian of the East
  • Classical Languages — Government of India · Pali and Prakrit · Gujarati, Konkani, Bhojpuri · not · 2024 revision · dropped "original, not borrowed"
  • States, Tribal Languages and Official Status —
  • Bilingualism, Endangered Languages and Language Policy — ~26% bilingual, ~7% trilingual · UNESCO Atlas: ~197 · lack of scripts · Odisha · Devanagari · Three-language formula · Kothari Commission (1964-66)
