RecapNatural Vegetation of India and Forest Cover (india State of Forest Report)

Recap

  • ISFR – State Rankings — Arunachal Pradesh · ~80% forest · Nagaland is not · Recorded forest area · Madhya Pradesh · West (Paschim) Champaran · Jodhpur
  • Deciduous, Thorn and Dry Evergreen Forests – Key Trees — Bhabar–Terai/Siwalik strip · Teak is the dominant species · sal · tropical deciduous · sandalwood (south) · tendu, palas, khair · forest fires
  • Mangrove, Littoral and Tidal Forests — tidal / littoral · stilt and knee roots · Sundari · Andaman & Nicobar Islands · every coastal State · Assam · Sundarbans
  • Tropical Evergreen (Rain) Forests and Biodiversity — > 45 m · multi-layered continuous canopy · lianas, epiphytes · leaf litter decomposes fastest · Maximum plant diversity and biomass · tropical moist (rain) forests · Western Ghats
  • ISFR – National Figures, Canopy Classes and Global Ranking — Forest Survey of India · Dehradun · biennial ISFR · Forest cover · > 1 ha · ≥ 10% · tree cover
  • Himalayan, Montane and Temperate Forests; Sholas — Deodar · highest elevation · Western Himalaya · 1,800–2,600 m · 1,500–3,000 m · oak–rhododendron · temperate
  • Forestry Practice – Seeds, Logging, Timber and Forest Fires — Types · ground · surface · crown · coniferous · Counter fire (back-firing) · traditional
  • Forest Policy, Law, Silviculture, Social Forestry and Grasslands — ecological balance · one-third (33%) · two-thirds in the hills · National Forestry Action Programme · Silviculture · Coppice · high forest
