Recap

  • Solstices, Equinoxes and Day Length — 22 Dec · 21 June · 21 March · 23 Sept · Equinox · overhead at the Equator · day = night everywhere
  • Overhead Sun, Shadows and Insolation — between the Tropics · twice a year · zero-shadow days · Delhi, Srinagar · never · Hyderabad, Mumbai, Kolkata · Andhra Pradesh
  • Why Seasons Occur – Tilt, Revolution, Perihelion — day-length variation · revolution on a tilted axis · 23½° (23.5°) from the perpendicular · 66½° to the orbital plane · Parallelism · Pole Star · angular momentum
