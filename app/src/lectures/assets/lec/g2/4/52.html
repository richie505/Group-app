Recap

  • Leading States – Commercial Crops (as of 2024-25) — Bt cotton · over 90% · Gujarat · Karnataka · Andhra Pradesh · chillies 1st · not
  • Oilseeds – Seasons, Varieties, Production — Rabi-only oilseed = mustard · Varieties · Varuna, Pusa Bold, Pitambari · Kaushal · Groundnut · pegging · gypsum
  • Cash, Fibre, Food and Plantation Crops — Cash crops · Food crops · jowar, millets · Soybean · Fibre crops · cotton, jute, sunhemp · Plantation crops
  • Growth Conditions – Climate and Soil — Cotton · kharif · not · black pepper · Lower Gangetic plain · paddy and jute · tea
  • World Producers and India's Rank — Cotton yield · China · Long-staple cotton · Egypt · Giza · Pima · India leads ELS + LS output
  • Spices – States, Varieties and Nicknames — Kerala = "Spice Garden of India" · black pepper and cardamom · Odisha · over 200 cm · 1,100 m · Lakadong turmeric · Meghalaya
  • Commodity Boards and Research Institutes — Five statutory boards · Two IISRs
  • Tea and Coffee – Regions, History and Araku — First tea plantations · Brahmaputra valley, Assam · Barak Valley · Tea-producing States · Tripura, Himachal Pradesh (Kangra) · Araku · all four
