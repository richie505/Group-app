RecapPeninsular Drainage – East-flowing and West-flowing Rivers, River Basins, Watershed

Recap

  • Krishna System — Mahabaleshwar · Sangli · Hamsaladeevi · ~1,400 km · second-longest Peninsular river · Puligadda · 64 km below Vijayawada
  • East-flowing vs West-flowing Rivers – Deltas, Estuaries and Direction — Western Ghats · higher in the west and slopes east · tilted north-west → south-east · Narmada and Tapi · trough faults (rift valleys) · Why no deltas in the west · short courses
  • Waterfalls on Peninsular Rivers — Nohkalikai · Barehipani · Chitrakote · 'Niagara of India' · widest · Gokak falls · Ghataprabha
  • Mahanadi, Kaveri, Pennar and Other East-flowing Rivers — Sihawa · Odisha · not · 851 km · 1,41,589 sq km · Cuttack · delta in Odisha near Paradeep
  • Narmada, Tapi and the West-coast Rivers — Gulf of Khambhat · 1,312 km · longest west-flowing · Jabalpur, Bhedaghat · Dhuandhar · Tawa · Banjar
  • Sources of Peninsular Rivers – Amarkantak, Western and Eastern Ghats — Western Ghats · Water Tower of Peninsular India · Godavari, Krishna and Kaveri · Amarkantak · Son · north · Narmada
  • Godavari System — Dakshin Ganga / Vriddha Ganga · length and the area it covers · longest Peninsular river · largest Peninsular basin · Trimbakeshwar (Nashik) · Widest/extensive delta · Konaseema
  • Lakes, Lagoons and River Islands — Srirangam · Kollidam · Aliabet · Majuli · Centripetal drainage · Loktak · Rajasthan saline lakes

Recap (cont.)

  • Structure and Sequence – Older Drainage, Rift Valleys, Consequent and Superimposed Rivers — older · broad, graded, shallow valleys · fixed courses · non-perennial · Consequent rivers · most Peninsular rivers · Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri
  • River Linking, Water Disputes and Watershed — Telangana and AP · Krishna and Godavari · River linking · Sir Arthur Cotton · National Perspective Plan 1980 · 30 links = 14 Himalayan + 16 Peninsular · Godavari–Krishna (Pattiseema), 2015
  • Length and Basin Ranking — Godavari > Narmada > Mahanadi > Tapi · within India · Sabarmati
  • Luni and Inland Drainage — Luni · Ajmer · Sagarmati · Telwara · lost in the marshy Rann of Kutch · endorheic / land-bound · Jawai, Sukri
