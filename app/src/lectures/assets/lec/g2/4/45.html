Recap

  • Disaster Management Cycle – Phases and Measures — Four phases · Eradication · Stages · during = response · sequential and sometimes simultaneous · continuous · proactive strategy
  • Nodal Ministries, Early Warning Agencies and Relief Responsibility — Ministry of Home Affairs · 2002 · Primary responsibility · State Government · Central Relief Commissioner · State Relief Manuals / Codes · not for nuclear hazards
  • Disaster Management Act, 2005 and the Authorities — Act No. 53 of 2005 · Presidential assent 23 December 2005 · Gazette of India on 26 December 2005 · 11 Chapters, 79 Sections · 2004 Indian Ocean tsunami · disaster · reduce the risk, impact or effects
  • NIDM and Training Institutions — New Delhi · NCDM · IIPA in 1995 · NIDM in 2003 · statutory · DM Act 2005 (s.42) · President of NIDM = Union Home Minister
  • Evolution, Policy, Plans and International Frameworks — August 1999 · J.C. Pant · 2001 · "Building a Culture of Prevention" · 31 types of disasters · five broad categories · separate Department of DM
  • Key Concepts – Hazard, Disaster, Vulnerability, Types — Least · physical · social · economic · environmental · systemic/institutional · high crop-insurance coverage reduces
  • Andhra Pradesh – APSDMA, Cyclones and Awards — APSDMA · chaired by the Chief Minister · 2007 · AP DM Rules 2007 · State Emergency Operations Centre · Vajrapath, Varuna, Red Message · relief-centric to mitigation-centric
  • Technology – GIS and Remote Sensing — GIS · spatial data · analyses flood patterns and topography · Landslide Hazard Zonation · GIS = Geographic Information System · GPS · cannot solve all natural disasters
