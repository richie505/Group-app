Recap

  • Sericulture, Lac and Apiculture — only country producing all five · China 1st · India 2nd · Karnataka · Karnataka + AP · three-fourths · not
  • Horticulture – Fruits, Propagation and States — 2nd in fruits and vegetables · Amrapali · Dasheri × Neelam · alternate (irregular) bearers · stock · scion · Sweet orange – grafting/budding
  • Livestock – Breeds and Animal Husbandry — Holstein-Friesian · highest milk yield · Jersey · buffalo · goat about 150 days · lambing season = winter · *Trifolium alexandrinum* = Berseem
  • Fisheries and the Blue Revolution — Blue Revolution · fish / pisciculture · 2nd · aquaculture · West Bengal · inland · Now Andhra Pradesh
  • Research Institutes — Tiruchirappalli (Trichy), Tamil Nadu · Lucknow · Solan (Nauni), Himachal Pradesh
  • Dairying and the White Revolution — world's largest milk producer · 247.87 MT · 485 g/day · butter and ghee · Uttar Pradesh · Operation Flood · 1970
  • Veterinary Practice – Blood Sampling, Pregnancy Diagnosis, Breeds — Fremitus · 120 days · Nellore sheep · weaning weight of lambs about 12 kg · Camel · water management · Alsatian
