RecapRight to Constitutional Remedies – Article 32; the Five Writs and Their Scope

Recap

  • The Five Writs – Literal Meaning and Purpose — Article 32(2) · five writs · Supreme Court (Art. 32) · High Courts (Art. 226) · all five · prerogative writs of English common law · Calcutta, Bombay and Madras
  • Heart and Soul – Article 32 as a Fundamental Right — move the Supreme Court directly · itself a Fundamental Right · Dr. B.R. Ambedkar · nullity · Why · worthless declaration · effective remedy
  • Writ Jurisdiction – Article 32 vs Article 226; Guardian of Fundamental Rights — Supreme Court (Art. 32) · High Court (Art. 226) · Arts. 32 and 226 · Only the SC and HCs · Article 226 · High Courts · not the President
  • Scope and Limits of Each Writ — all persons · lawful · competent court · not dismissed for imperfect pleadings · Wrong statement · private individual or body · public duty
  • Clauses of Article 32 – What It Enforces — Only Fundamental Rights · refuse relief · no Fundamental Right has been infringed · Right to property · 44th Amendment, 1978 · Art. 300A · cannot
  • Suspension during Emergency; Articles 33-35 — Who suspends FRs? The President · Art. 359 · Art. 358 · Arts. 20 and 21 cannot be suspended · 44th Amendment (1978) · ADM Jabalpur v. Shivkant Shukla (1976) · Parliament
  • Public Interest Litigation — No Article mentions PIL · USA · Arts. 32 and 226 · not itself an FR · locus standi · public-spirited person · poor and weak access to justice
