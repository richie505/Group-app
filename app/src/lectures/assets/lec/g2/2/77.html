RecapFundamental Duties – Article 51A, Swaran Singh Committee, Enforceability

Recap

  • Basics – Part IVA, Article 51A, 42nd and 86th Amendments — Fundamental Duties = Part IVA · only one Article – Article 51A · every citizen of India · separate Part · not part of the Fundamental Rights · 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976 · Swaran Singh Committee
  • What Is Not a Fundamental Duty — not · elderly, differently abled and children · respect parents and teachers · 48A vs 51A(g) · DPSP · Art. 48A · citizen's duty
  • Swaran Singh Committee (1976) — Swaran Singh Committee · Recommendations NOT accepted · penalty/punishment · not to be questioned in any court · duty to pay taxes · strive towards excellence · modified form
  • The Eleven Duties – Clause-wise — Moral duties: (b), (f), (h), (j) · first duty · fundamental duty · 51A(f) – composite culture · Protection · wildlife · respected
  • Nature, Enforceability, Source and Criticism — Non-justiciable · cannot be enforced by writs · no legal sanction · only FRs are justiciable · interpret ambiguous laws · constitutionality · reasonableness
  • Rights and Duties – Correlation and Cases — Rights are correlative with duties · Holland · Art. 19(1)(a) · 51A(c) · Ramlila Maidan case (2012) · Subhash Kumar (1991) · Art. 21
