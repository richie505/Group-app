Recap

  • Legislative Assembly – Strength, Term, Qualifications, Quorum — Article 170 · not more than 500, not less than 60 · directly elected · Exceptions to the 60 minimum · Sikkim (32, Art. 371F) · Goa (40) · Mizoram (40)
  • Legislative Procedure – Money Bills, Ordinary Bills and Deadlock — only in the Legislative Assembly · Minister · Governor's recommendation · Art. 207(1) · Art. 199 · Speaker certifies · Art. 198
  • Legislative Council – Composition, Size and Tenure — Industrialists · not · wrong · Muthulakshmi Reddy · Article 171 · Vidhan Parishad · Maximum one-third
  • Article 169 – Creation and Abolition of Legislative Councils — Article 169 · Parliament · abolish · create · total membership · Step 2 – Parliament · simple majority
  • States with Legislative Councils — Six States · 2019 (before 31 October) · seven · J&K Reorganisation Act, 2019 · No Council · Rajasthan · Kerala
  • Presiding Officers – Speaker and Chairman — Officer of the Assembly · Speaker · Art. 178 · Resigns · Deputy Speaker · Art. 179 · Vacates office
  • Framework, Articles and Proceedings (Part VI) — Art. 208(2) · pre-1950 provincial rules · not · Order of proceedings (MP Assembly) · Art. 210 · Hindi or English · 24 days
  • Disqualification of Legislators — other than the Tenth Schedule · Governor · Art. 190 – vacation of seats · both Houses · Speaker's / Chairman's acceptance · 60 days' absence · vacated
