RecapRight to Information Act 2005 – Structure, Exemptions, Amendments and Issues

Recap

  • Background, Chronology and Enactment — Sweden → UN → FOI Act → RTI Act · CIC · 12 October 2005 · Wajahat Habibullah · 15 June 2005
  • Preamble, Objectives and Nature of the Right — transparency of information · revelation of information · informed citizenry · paramountcy of the democratic ideal · answerability · containing corruption · NOT
  • Key Sections – Who May Ask, What Is Covered — not · Central Information Commission
  • Time Limits —
  • Information Commissions and Penalties — No Chief Justice · journalism · civil-court powers · prescribed by the Centre (3 years) · Section 20 penalty · CIC or SIC · ₹250 per day, max ₹25,000
  • Exemptions – Sections 8, 9 and 24 — Procedure followed in decision-making · not exempt · 8(2) · 8(3) · 20 years · Section 9 · Second ARC
  • Public Authority, Judgements and Issues — pendency · vacancies · Whistle Blowers Protection Act 2014 · corruption, crony capitalism · foreign contributions · lapsed · eSAKSHI
