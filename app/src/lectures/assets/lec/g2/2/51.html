Recap

  • Arms-control Treaties, JCPOA and ICAN — START · Strategic Arms Reduction Treaty · US + Russia · 90% · Iran · June 2025 · NPT exit
  • NPT, CTBT and the Nuclear-weapon States — Signed 1968 · in force 1970 · extended indefinitely in 1995 · 5 years · five Nuclear Weapon States · tested before 1 January 1967 · USA, Russia, UK, France, China
  • NSG and the Export-control Regimes — NSG · 1974 Pokhran-I · voluntary, consensus-based · consensus rule · not · China and Pakistan · Wassenaar and MTCR
  • FATF — 1989 by the G7 · Paris (OECD) · money laundering and terror financing · Grey list · increased monitoring · black list · Myanmar
  • IAEA, OPCW, CWC/BWC and Headquarters — IAEA · Vienna · 1957 · founding member · Rafael Grossi · OPCW · The Hague
