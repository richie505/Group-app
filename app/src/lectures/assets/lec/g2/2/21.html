RecapLaws for Women – Dowry, Domestic Violence, Posh, Maternity Benefit and Trafficking

Recap

  • Sexual Harassment at Workplace – Vishaka and POSH Act 2013 — Vishaka v. State of Rajasthan (1997) · working women at the workplace · POSH Act, 2013 · Vishaka guidelines · Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal · in force 9 December 2013 · Time limits
  • Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961 — Enacted 1961; in force 1 July 1961 · 1st joint sitting · Section 2 – Dowry · any property or valuable security · directly or indirectly · Not dowry · dower/mahr
  • Domestic Violence Act 2005 and Section 498A — Enacted 2005; in force 26 October 2006 · civil law · Section 3 · verbal/emotional · economic · Protection Officers · State Government
  • Other Acts – Sati, Indecent Representation, ITPA and Dates in Force — Sati Act · glorification · 1 to 7 years · District Magistrate
  • Maternity Benefit and Equal Pay — Trap (UPSC 2019) · Equal Remuneration Act 1976 · Code on Wages 2019 · not · OSH Code 2020 · before 6 a.m. / after 7 p.m
  • Trafficking – Constitution, Laws and Reports — Article 23 · traffic in human beings, begar · Article 24 · ITPA 1956 · trafficked women · not · CLA 2013
  • BNS 2023 – Offences Against Women; Acid Attacks — 1 July 2024 · Laxmi v. UoI (2013) · photo ID · ₹3 lakh · RPwD Act 2016
  • Support Institutions and Schemes — 26 Shakti Sadans
