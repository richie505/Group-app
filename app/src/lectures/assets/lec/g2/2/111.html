RecapCentre-state Financial Relations – Articles 264-293; Taxes, Grants-in-aid, Borrowing

Recap

  • Distribution of Tax Revenues – Articles 268 to 271 and State Taxes — Income tax · levies, collects and distributes · surcharge · Before 2000 · assigned to the States · railway fares and freights · 80th Amendment
  • Finance Commission and Devolution – Article 280 — Art. 280 · quasi-judicial · President · fifth year · Art. 281 · "balancing wheel of fiscal federalism" · not binding
  • Part XII Framework – Key Articles 264 to 293 — Part XII · 264-300A · 268-293 · distribution of revenues = Arts 268-281 · Part V
  • GST and the GST Council — 101st Amendment Act, 2016 · 122nd Amendment Bill · 1 July 2017 · destination-based · Arts 246A, 269A, 279A · Subsumed · State VAT/sales tax
  • Grants-in-aid, Plan Transfers and Special Category Status — Finance Commission's · not NITI Aayog's · Gadgil formula · Central Plan assistance · devolution of funds · Planning Commission (2015) · 90% grant : 10% loan
  • Borrowing – Articles 292 and 293 — Net Borrowing Ceiling · 293(3) · Kerala v Union of India (2024) · five-judge bench · 2024-25: ₹60,485.40 crore
  • Amendments, Emergencies and Fiscal Disputes — not · Art. 354 · modify revenue distribution · financial year · Art. 360 · financial propriety · Money/Financial Bills reserved
