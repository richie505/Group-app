RecapNhrc and SHRCs – Composition, Powers, Functions and Limitations

Recap

  • State Human Rights Commissions — Optional · may · Jurisdiction · State List and Concurrent List · UTs · except Delhi, J&K and Ladakh · NHRC
  • Status and Establishment of the NHRC — NHRC = statutory body · not constitutional · not by an executive order · both false · NCBC · 102nd Amendment, 2018 · life, liberty, equality and dignity
  • NHRC Composition — four · Deemed members · NCM, NCSC, NCST, NCW · NCBC, NCPCR · all of NCSC, NCM, NCW and NCBC · not equal in status · CCPD
  • Appointment, Term and Removal — Appointed by the President · six-member committee · Chairman of the Rajya Sabha · CJI · NOT · after consulting the CJI · Term (since 2019)
  • Functions and Powers — Inquire · negligence by a public servant · suo motu · Intervene · with the court's approval · Visit jails · including terrorism
  • Amendments 2006 and 2019; Human Rights Courts; GANHRI — true · Human Rights Courts (Sec. 30) · Court of Session · HC Chief Justice's concurrence · Special Public Prosecutor (Sec. 31) · 7 years · NHRC annual report
  • Limitations of the Commissions — Constraints (NHRC itself) · manpower · 30 Apr 2026
