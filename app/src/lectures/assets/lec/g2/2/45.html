RecapUnited Nations – Principal Organs, Unga, Unsc and Reform; India's Candidature

Recap

  • Security Council — 15 members · 10 non-permanent · UNGA · 2 years · no immediate re-election (Art. 23) · Veto (Art. 27) · substantive
  • Charter, Founding, Languages and UN Days — Atlantic Charter (1941) · Franklin D. Roosevelt · 50 nations · San Francisco Conference · 26 June 1945 · Poland · 51st original member
  • International Court of Justice — Principal judicial organ · The Hague · 15 judges · 9-year · UNGA and UNSC voting separately · B.N. Rau · Nagendra Singh
  • Principal Organs, General Assembly and ECOSOC — Six principal organs · UDHR (10 Dec 1948) · UN General Assembly resolution (217 A) · Credentials Committee · Uniting for Peace (Res. 377), 1950 · 1956 Suez Crisis · Non-member observer States
  • Secretaries-General — Guterres
  • India at the UN — Founding member · British India · Eight UNSC terms · 2028-29 · First Hindi speech · Atal Bihari Vajpayee · 4 October 1977
  • UNSC Reform — IGN · UNGA · 2008 · China
