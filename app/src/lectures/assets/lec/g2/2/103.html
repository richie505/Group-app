RecapSupreme Court – Composition, Appointment, Collegium, Njac and Jurisdictions

Recap

  • Chief Justices of India and Firsts — M. Fathima Beevi · first woman SC judge · Governor of Tamil Nadu · 23 Nov 2023 · Gyan Sudha Misra (4th, April 2010) · Indu Malhotra · R. Banumathi
  • Jurisdiction – Original (Art. 131), Appellate (Arts. 132-136), Writ (Art. 32) — original, appellate and advisory · SC as a federal court · Centre-State and inter-State · legal right · exclusive · fiscal · West Bengal v. Union (1961)
  • Special Powers – Court of Record, Review, Arts. 141, 142, 144, 145; Guardian of the Constitution — final interpreter · Fundamental Rights · federal court · highest court of appeal · Supreme Court · Not powers of the SC · ordinances
  • Composition, Appointment, Qualifications, Tenure; Acting, Ad Hoc and Retired Judges — President · not by the Governor · CJI · senior-most judge · Other judges · CJI is obligatory · may
  • Collegium, Judges Cases and NJAC — CJI + four senior-most SC judges · Not in the Constitution or any Act · Supreme Court decisions · 1993 · 1998 · President · must
  • Advisory Jurisdiction – Article 143 — Art. 143(1) · President · question of law or fact · public importance · may · refuse · Art. 143(2)
  • Removal of Judges, Independence Safeguards; Supreme Court v. High Court — order of the President · address by each House · same session · special majority · total membership · two-thirds present and voting · cannot remove
  • Establishment, Seat and Strength — Part V, Chapter IV – Articles 124-147 · Art. 124 · Federal Court · 1937 · 1949 · inaugurated on 28 January 1950 · Tilak Marg in 1958
