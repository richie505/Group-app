RecapAttorney General and Advocate General; Solicitor General

Recap

  • Attorney General – Highest Law Officer, Appointment and Qualification (Art. 76) — Attorney General (AG) of India · highest law officer · first law officer / chief legal adviser · Government of India · Part V · Union Executive · not a member of the Union Cabinet
  • Attorney General – Rights in Parliament and in the Courts — speak and otherwise take part · both Houses · joint sitting · without being an MP · cannot vote · Art. 105(4) · privileges and immunities of an MP
  • Advocate General of the State – Article 165 — official legal adviser · Part VI · Governor · not the President · qualified to be a High Court judge · 10 years in a judicial office · 10 years as a High Court advocate
  • Attorney General – Term, Remuneration, Duties and Limitations — No fixed term · no grounds or procedure for removal · during the pleasure of the President · wrong · Resigns · by convention · Remuneration
  • Solicitor General and the Office-holders — SG = second law officer · assists the AG · Additional Solicitors General · legal adviser · Statutory, not constitutional · Appointments Committee of the Cabinet · 3 years
