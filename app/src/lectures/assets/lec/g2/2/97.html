Recap

  • Attorney General of India – Article 76 and Article 88 — Article 76 · Attorney General (AG) for India · highest (first) law officer · President · Qualification · Term · not fixed
  • Collective Responsibility – Article 75(3) and the Parliamentary Principle — Article 75(3) · Art. 164(2) · Collective responsibility · nominal and real executives · majority party rule · double membership · PM's leadership
  • Cabinet Secretariat, Cabinet Secretary and Chief Secretary — Cabinet Secretariat · directly under the PM · 1 and 2 only · Cabinet Secretary · 11th · Civil Services Board · Appointments Committee of the Cabinet
  • Cabinet and Council of Ministers; Individual Responsibility – Article 75(2) — "Cabinet" · only once · Article 352(3) · written advice of the Union Cabinet · 44th Amendment Act, 1978 · not include the Cabinet Secretary · Article 75(2)
  • Cabinet Committees, Business Rules and Reports on the Machinery of Government — Cabinet Committees · extra-constitutional · Transaction of Business Rules, 1961 · Art. 77(3) · Eight · Parliamentary Affairs · Accommodation
  • Cabinet in Practice – Kitchen Cabinet, Shadow Cabinet, Coalitions — Shadow cabinet · main opposition party · British (UK) · Kitchen cabinet · informal inner circle · extra-constitutional power centre · Single-party majority
