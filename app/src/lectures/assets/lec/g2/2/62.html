Recap

  • Articles 23-24 – Right Against Exploitation — 23(1) · traffic in human beings, begar · forced labour · citizens and non-citizens · State and private persons · without payment · 23(2)
  • Article 25 – Freedom of Conscience and Religion — 25(1) · freedom of conscience · profess, practise and propagate · public order, morality and health · other provisions of Part III · Art. 25(1) · Grounds of restriction
  • Secularism – Meaning and the Indian Model — Secularism · no official (state) religion · equal status/respect for all religions · not · Applies to all · irreligious persons · Modern Indian concept
  • Articles 26-28 and the Group at a Glance — Right against Exploitation · 23–24 · Right to Freedom of Religion · 25–28 · four · State-neutrality provisions · collective
  • Religion Cases – ERP Test, Sabarimala, Anthem, Conversion Laws — Essential Religious Practice (ERP) · Shirur Mutt (1954) · Sabarimala · Pathanamthitta · Jan 2019 · Bindu Ammini and Kanaka Durga · 2026 review
