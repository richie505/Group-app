Recap

  • Office-bearers, Drafting Committee and the Constitutional Adviser — "First President" of the Assembly · Sachchidananda Sinha · Rajendra Prasad · Not the presiding officer · Nehru · Ambedkar · Sir B.N. (Benegal Narsing) Rau
  • Chronology – First Meeting to Commencement — Adopted, enacted and given to ourselves · Constituent Assembly · 26 November 1949 · twenty-sixth day of November, 1949 · In force on 26 Nov 1949 · citizenship · elections
  • Composition and Method of Election — Provincial seats · Muslims, Sikhs and General · Indirectly elected · Provincial Legislative Assemblies · NOT · 1935 Act, Sixth Schedule · partly elected, partly nominated
  • National Symbols Adopted by the Assembly — National Flag · 22 July 1947 · Ashoka Chakra (Dharmachakra) · 24 spokes · wheel of law · justice · Flag Code of India, 2002
  • Demand for a Constituent Assembly and the Cabinet Mission — not · Cabinet Mission · 24 March 1946 · Pethick-Lawrence · Stafford Cripps · A.V. Alexander · NOT William Wood
  • Interim Government (1946) and the First Cabinet — Announced 24 August 1946 · took charge 2 September 1946 · Viceroy · President · Nehru · Vice-President · Muslim League
  • Time, Sessions and Size of the Text — Copies signed on 24 Jan 1950 · three · hand-written in English · printed in English · hand-written in Hindi
  • Committees and Their Chairmen — Rule of thumb · Fundamental Rights · Patel · sub-committee · Kripalani · Flag committees · "Steering Committee – Nehru"
