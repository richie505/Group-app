Recap

  • Finance Commission – Article 280, Composition and Qualifications — Article 280 · XII · quasi-judicial constitutional body · 280 and 281 · 290 · President · every fifth year or earlier
  • 14th Finance Commission (2015–20) — 2 January 2013 · 1 April 2015 – 31 March 2020 · 24 February 2015 · 32% → 42% · largest increase ever · Tax devolution = the primary route · sector-specific grants dropped
  • Finance Commissions – Chairmen, Award Periods and States' Share — 16 Finance Commissions · Never FC chairmen · Dr. Manmohan Singh · Ashok Mehta · M.V. Mathur
  • Functions – Article 280(3) and the Divisible Pool — vertical · allocation among the States · horizontal · Principles governing grants-in-aid · Consolidated Fund of India · 275 · Panchayats and Municipalities
  • Grants, Disaster Funds, Special Category Status and Andhra Pradesh — Art. 275 · Plan · Assam, Jammu & Kashmir, Nagaland · hilly terrain · tribal · strategic border · backwardness
  • 15th and 16th Finance Commissions – Devolution Formula — 27 November 2017 · N.K. Singh · first FC to cut · 42% → 41% · J&K's status · 2011 population · ₹4.36 lakh crore
