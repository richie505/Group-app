Recap

  • Articles 29 and 30 and the Linguistic Minority Provisions — Art. 30 = only minorities · Art. 16(2) · public employment · old Art. 31(1) · property · not a "corrupt practice" · 350A and 350B
  • Meaning of Minority and the Notified Communities — "Minority" is not defined · Articles 29, 30, 350A and 350B · religious and linguistic minorities · NCM Act 1992, Section 2(c) · The State is the unit · T.M.A. Pai, 2002 · 11 States
  • National Commission for Minorities and State Minorities Commissions — five incl. the Chairperson · 3 years · Statutory, not constitutional · civil court · July 2026 · Harjit Singh Grewal · State Governments
  • Minority Educational Institutions – NCMEI and Court Cases — Chairperson + 3 Members · Central Government · minority community · 5-year term · no Parliamentary approval · minority status · Section 10 – No Objection Certificate
  • Committees on Minorities – Sachar, Ranganath Misra, Kundu — Equal Opportunity Commission · Communal Award 1932 · National Integration Council 1961
  • Andhra Pradesh – Minority and BC Welfare — Urdu · second official language · AP State Minorities Commission · Roshini · minority · Tatkal · Rs 20,000
  • PM Vishwakarma, BC and DNT Schemes, Finance Corporations — 17 September 2023 · artisans and craftspeople in 18 trades · not fishermen · Vishwakarma · Ministry of MSME · PM-YASASVI · Rs 2.5 lakh
  • Minority Welfare Schemes and the 15 Point Programme — 15-Point Programme 1983 · PM's New 15 Point Programme 2006 · 15% · PM VIKAS · Virasat Ka Samvardhan · 2022 · Central Sector
