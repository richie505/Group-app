  • Beti Bachao Beti Padhao and Gender Mainstreaming — Objectives · Child Sex Ratio · female infanticide/foeticide · education · not legal aid · PM flagship · Make in India

  • Girl Child and Adolescent Girls — 1990 · SAARC Year of the Girl Child · CBSE Udaan · engineering · not medical colleges · Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana · Bihar
