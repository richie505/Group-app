Recap

  • Suicide – Durkheim's Types and NCRB Data — Sati = altruistic suicide · Durkheim's *Le Suicide* · 1897 · ADSI 2015 – farmer suicides · bankruptcy/indebtedness · 38.7% · farming-related issues
  • Human Trafficking — Meaning · modern slavery · NOT a Directive Principle · Immoral Traffic (Prevention) Act, 1956 · BNS Sec. 143 · Palermo Protocol · Most cases – West Bengal
  • Jogini (Devadasi) System — Jogini/Devadasi/Basavi · Law · Most prevalent (Telangana) · Mahbubnagar · 2,000 Joginis · Age of dedication · 8-12 years
  • Attempt to Suicide in Law — Suicide · attempt · completion · Tele-MANAS · 10 Oct 2022 · 14416
  • Beggary — West Bengal · 81,224 · Bihar · 4,13,670 · Bombay Prevention of Begging Act, 1959 · Delhi HC (2018, Harsh Mander) · decriminalised begging
  • Alcoholism — Art. 47 (DPSP) · intoxicating drinks · except for medicinal purposes · Health effect · liver cirrhosis · nervous-system damage · Anti-Arrack Movement
  • Drug Abuse — Golden Crescent · Golden Triangle · alcohol 14.6% · cannabis 2.8% · Tolerance · larger doses
  • Social Defence and the AP Angle — Scope · MoSJE · punishment · health and rehabilitation
