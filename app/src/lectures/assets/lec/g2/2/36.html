RecapSc/st (prevention of Atrocities) Act 1989 and Protection of Civil Rights Act 1955

Recap

  • Protection of Civil Rights Act 1955 — Enacted 1955 · Untouchability (Offences) Act · in force 1 June 1955 · Art. 17 · Protection of Civil Rights Act · 1976 · Rules 1977
  • PoA Act 1989 – Enactment, Scope and Offences — Act 33 of 1989 · 11 Sept 1989 · in force 30 January 1990 · 23 sections · 1995 · 2015 · 2018
  • PoA Act – Special Courts, Powers and Procedure — cannot impose a collective fine · No power to dissolve local authorities · s.14 · s.16
  • PoA Rules 1995 – Investigation and Monitoring — Rule 7(1) · Rule 7(2) · 30 days · charge-sheet within 60 days · Relief · ₹85,000 to ₹8.25 lakh · National Helpline 14566
  • PoA Amendment Act 2015 (in force 26 January 2016) — 31 Dec 2015 · in force 26 January 2016 · Five broad areas · appeals (new s.14A) · New atrocities · garlanding with footwear · abusing by caste name
  • Dilution and Restoration – the 2018 Episode — Section 18A
  • Enforcement Data and Andhra Pradesh — NCRB 2024 · 55,698 · -23.1% · 32-33% · 14 Special Sessions Courts · East Godavari 47 · Jogini
