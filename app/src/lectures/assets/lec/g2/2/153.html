RecapNiti Aayog – Structure, Functions, Initiatives; Contrast with the Planning Commission

Recap

  • Planning Commission and the National Development Council — 15 March 1950 · non-constitutional, non-statutory · not · First Chairman: Jawaharlal Nehru · Gulzarilal Nanda · Cabinet Minister · market economy at bay
  • NITI Aayog – Status, Structure and Hubs — 1 January 2015 · Union Cabinet resolution · neither constitutional nor statutory · think-tank · cooperative federalism · Chairperson: the Prime Minister · Vice-Chairperson
  • NITI Aayog vs the Planning Commission — empowered States as equal partners · think-tank · catalyst
  • NITI Aayog Indices and Reports — "Strategy for New India @ 75" · NITI Aayog
  • NITI Aayog Initiatives – Aspirational Districts and Others — Health and Nutrition 30% · Convergence, Collaboration, Competition · Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam, YSR Kadapa · 4 Rayalaseema
