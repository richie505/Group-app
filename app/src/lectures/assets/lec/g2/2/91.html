RecapParliamentary Committees – Pac, Estimates, Public Undertakings, DRSCs, Ad Hoc

Recap

  • Financial Committees – Composition (Estimates, PAC, COPU) — The three financial committees · PAC, Estimates, COPU · not · Only committee with no RS member · Estimates Committee · Election · every year
  • Public Accounts Committee – Functions, Chairman, Reports — Oldest parliamentary committee · 1921 · Government of India Act, 1919 · Function · CAG's audit reports · within the scope of the demand · "Watchdog"
  • Other Standing Committees – Petitions, Subordinate Legislation, Assurances, Offices of Profit — Financial · 24 DRSCs · to inquire · to scrutinise and control · Welfare of SCs and STs · day-to-day business · house-keeping
  • Estimates Committee and Committee on Public Undertakings — Origin · 1950 · John Mathai · 30 in 1956 · Function (Rule 310) · alternative policies · "continuous economy committee"
  • Departmentally Related Standing Committees (DRSCs) — Set up 1993 · Rules Committee · 24 since 2004 · Each: 31 members = 21 LS + 10 RS · 2 : 1 · quorum 11 · Ministers ineligible
  • Ad Hoc Committees, JPCs and the Role of Committees — Role · administration · through parliamentary committees · Ad hoc committees · Inquiry committees · Advisory committees · Select / Joint Committees on Bills
