RecapBudget in Parliament – Stages, Cut Motions, Appropriation and Finance Bills, Funds

Recap

  • Annual Financial Statement (Art. 112) – Budget Basics, Presentation and Firsts — Constitutional name · Annual Financial Statement (AFS) · Article 112 · "Budget" not in the Constitution · "bougette" · Laid by · President
  • Consolidated Fund, Public Account and Contingency Fund (Arts. 266-267) — All revenues · Consolidated Fund of India · Withdrawal from the CFI · Parliament · no "comptroller" power · Art. 266(3) · Art. 114(3)
  • Charged Expenditure on the Consolidated Fund (Art. 112(3)) — Charged expenditure · non-votable · not submitted to the vote · can be discussed · President · Chairman and Deputy Chairman · Speaker and Deputy Speaker
  • Stages of the Budget, Demands for Grants, Guillotine and Cut Motions — 1. Presentation · 2. General discussion · 4. Voting on Demands for Grants · Lok Sabha only · reduce · 5. Passing of the Appropriation Bill · 6. Passing of the Finance Bill
  • Appropriation Bill, Finance Bill and Parliamentary Control over Finance — Appropriation Bill · voted grants + charged expenditure · passed by both Houses · Art. 114(3) · No amendment · Automatic repeal clause · Appropriation Act, 2016
  • Vote on Account and Other Special Grants (Arts. 115-116) — Vote on account · withdraw money from the CFI · Since the 1 February Budget (2017) · election years · AP angle · February 2019 AP Budget · vote-on-account budget
