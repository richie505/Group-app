RecapHuman Rights – Concept, Generations, Udhr, Iccpr, Icescr and Un Machinery

Recap

  • Concept of Human Rights – Meaning, Thinkers and Milestones — Human rights · all human beings by nature · inherent, inalienable, universal · Core value · dignity of man as a human being · indivisible and interdependent · Vienna Declaration, 1993
  • UDHR 1948 and Human Rights Day — Covers · guides the SDGs Agenda · Included · life, liberty, security (Art. 3) · privacy (Art. 12) · work (Art. 23) · adequate standard of living (Art. 25)
  • NGOs and Human Rights Prizes — Amnesty is NOT a UN organ · Viskuli · Belgrade Convention · Paris · UN Human Rights Prize 2018 · Rebeca Gyumi · women and girls
  • UN Machinery – Human Rights Council, OHCHR, Paris Principles — 2006 · UN Commission on Human Rights · Geneva · 47 members · 3-year terms · two consecutive · two-thirds
  • Generations of Human Rights — Karel Vasak (1977) · liberty, equality, fraternity · Right to Education · Right to Work · second-generation
  • International Bill of Human Rights and Core Treaties — ICESCR entered into force first · India · 10 April 1979 · Child rights · ICCPR Art. 24
