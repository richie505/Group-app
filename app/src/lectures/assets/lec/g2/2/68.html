RecapArticles 20, 21, 21A and 22 – Protection in Conviction, Life and Liberty, Education, Preventive Detention

Recap

  • Article 21A – Right to Education, 86th Amendment and RTE Act — Text · free and compulsory education · 6 to 14 years · by law · elementary · Inserted by · 86th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2002
  • Article 20 – Protection in Respect of Conviction for Offences — Available to · citizens, foreigners and legal persons · Ex post facto law · retrospective · Parliament cannot · criminal law with retrospective effect · Double jeopardy
  • Rights Read into Article 21 and the Right to Privacy — Right to life · personal liberty · citizens and non-citizens · food · shelter · livelihood · no right to permanent housing
  • Article 22 – Arrest and Preventive Detention — told the grounds · Art. 22(2) · 22(3) exclusions · enemy aliens · preventive detention · Also outside · court order
  • Article 21 – Procedure Established by Law, Due Process, Natural Justice — Text · procedure established by law · Japanese · due process of law · principle of natural justice · Article 21 · audi alteram partem
  • Right to Die and Euthanasia — Mental Healthcare Act, 2017 · BNS, 2023 · 1 July 2024
  • Articles 20 and 21 During an Emergency — 44th Amendment (1978) · Articles 20 and 21 cannot be suspended
