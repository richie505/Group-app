RecapSectoral Regulators – Trai, Cci, Fssai, Cerc/sercs, Pngrb, Aera, Data Protection Board

Recap

  • Competition Commission of India — Competition Act, 2002 · Competition (Amendment) Act, 2007 · Ministry of Corporate Affairs · replaced the MRTP Act, 1969 · MRTP Commission · promoting competition · Established
  • TRAI and TDSAT — TRAI · Telecom Regulatory Authority of India · 20 February 1997 · TRAI Act, 1997 · Anil Kumar Lahoti · TDSAT · TRAI (Amendment) Act, 2000
  • FSSAI – Food Regulator — FSSAI · Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 · 2008 · Ministry of Health and Family Welfare · eight · 1 July 2019 · no recycled plastics or newspaper
  • Energy and Airport Regulators – CERC, SERCs, PNGRB, AERA — CERC · Carbon Credit Trading Scheme · SERCs · APTEL · APERC · 1999 · AP Electricity Reform Act, 1998
  • Data Protection Board of India — DPDP Act, 2023 · DPBI · 4 members · ₹250 crore · no imprisonment · TDSAT within 60 days · RTI Sec. 8(1)(j)
