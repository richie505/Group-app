RecapAccountability and Transparency Mechanisms – Whistle Blowers Protection Act, Prevention of Corruption Act, Citizens' Oversight

Recap

  • Money Laundering, Benami Property and Offshore Leaks — in force 1 July 2005 · Section 3 · "proceeds of crime" · 3 to 7 years · Director of the Enforcement Directorate · attach property · Adjudicating Authority
  • UN Conventions against Corruption and Organised Crime — UNODC · both UNCAC and UNTOC · B and D only
  • Corruption Perceptions Index — Transparency International (Berlin) · 1995 · perceived public-sector corruption · 0 (highly corrupt) to 100 (very clean)
  • Prevention of Corruption Act and Sanction for Prosecution — PCA 1988 · Santhanam Committee · Public servant (Sec 2(c)) · local-authority · Judge · public duty · Vineet Narain (1997)
  • Whistle Blowers Protection Act 2014 — Whistle-blower · public interest disclosure · Competent Authority · Identity · confidential · victimisation · no anonymous
  • Agencies – CBI, CVC, ED — Santhanam Committee (1962-64) · Prevention of Corruption · CBI 1963 · CVC 1964 · CBI data (CVC report, Sep 2026) · 71.71% · 7,229
  • Lokpal and Lokayuktas — Lokpal (2013 Act) · Chairperson + up to 8 members · 50% judicial · SC, ST, OBC, minorities, women · Selection committee · Speaker · LoP
  • Citizens' Oversight Tools —
