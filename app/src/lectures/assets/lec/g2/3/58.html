Recap

  • Financial Inclusion – Meaning, Objectives and Committees — Financial inclusion · all of the above · Rangarajan definition · access to financial services · timely and adequate credit · vulnerable groups · affordable cost
  • Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) — 15 August 2014 · 28 August 2014 · financial inclusion · a bank account for every household · Mera Khata – Bhagya Vidhata · Guinness World Records · SI 2016 statements
  • NPCI, Cards, ATMs and RTGS-NEFT — National Payments Corporation of India · December 2008 · RBI and Indian Banks' Association (IBA) · not-for-profit · Section 25 company · Section 8 · promotes financial inclusion
  • MUDRA – Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana — PMMY · 8 April 2015 · Ministry of Finance (DFS) · 18 March 2015 · SIDBI subsidiary · refinances · collateral-free
  • Microfinance and SHG-Bank Linkage — Microfinance · 1, 2, 3 and 4 · Grameen Bank, Bangladesh (1976) · Muhammad Yunus · International Year of Microcredit: 2005 · AP microfinance crisis (2010) · SKS Microfinance
  • Digital Payments Policy, Schemes and RBI Indices — Lucky Grahak Yojana · Digi-Dhan Vyapar Yojana · merchants get prizes · MDR (Merchant Discount Rate) · charge a bank levies on a merchant · Zero MDR · RuPay debit and BHIM-UPI
  • UPI – Features, New Products and UPI Abroad — UPI = Unified Payments Interface · April 2016 · 21 banks · IMPS · mobile wallets are not necessary · BHIM · Only I
  • Cryptocurrency, CBDC and the Digital Rupee — RBI liability · CBDC · without the US dollar or SWIFT · programmed · Both · e-rupee pilots · 1 Nov 2022
