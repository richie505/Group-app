Recap

  • EPZs and Free Trade Zones – Kandla to Surat — SEZ = Special Economic Zone · Kandla · 1965 · Surat · 1997 · first private-sector EPZ · 1 November 2000
  • SEZ Policy and SEZ Act – Dates — 2006 · both 2000 and 2006 · SEZ India mobile app · Ministry of Commerce and Industry
  • SEZ Act 2005 – Objectives and Features — additional economic activity · exports of goods and services · employment · infrastructure · 1 and 2 only · services only · discouraging
  • SEZ Performance and Criticism — 31 Dec 2011 · ₹2,49,630 crore · ₹14.64 lakh crore · 21% · less than 3% of FDI · Nandigram · 2007
  • Andhra Pradesh SEZs — Operational SEZs in AP: 19 · now 25 operational, 30 notified · AP's largest SEZ: Atchutapuram · Sri City · Satyavedu · USSR · Maki
  • Industrial Corridors and NICDC — NICDC · DMIC Development Corporation · 11 industrial corridors · Kakinada · Coastal Economic Zones · Sagarmala · 2016
