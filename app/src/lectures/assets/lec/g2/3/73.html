Recap

  • Meaning and Purpose of Deficit Financing — Deficit financing · deficit budget · spending in excess of revenue · RBI · Fiscal deficit · spends more than it receives · deficit unit
  • Methods and Their Inflationary Effect — cash balances · issue of new currency · borrowing from the RBI · Monetised deficit · high-powered money · inflationary in character · increases the money supply
  • Consequences – Inflation, Debt Trap, Crowding Out — 20 paise · 40% of revenue receipts · ₹5.23 trillion · 62% · subsidies to capital expenditure · 5.05% · market loans ≈ 75% of AP's debt
