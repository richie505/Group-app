Recap

  • Insurance Schemes – PMSBY, PMJJBY, AABY, RSBY and Others — Jan Suraksha schemes · PMSBY, PMJJBY · no suicide cover
  • Pension Schemes – APY, NPS, PM-SYM, PM-KMY, PMVVY — PFRDA · PMJJBY, PMSBY and APY
  • Social Security Laws and Bodies – ESI, EPF, NSSN — EPF interest 2016-17 · 8.65% · Central Board of Trustees · Labour Ministry (EPFO) · UAN (2014) · eShram · 64.3%
  • DBT, PAHAL and Ujjwala — DBT · 1 Jan 2013 · ₹6.9 lakh crore, 327 schemes · DBT Mission · Cabinet Secretariat · State DBT nodal officer / coordinator · MGNREGS
  • NSAP and Social Security Concepts — NSAP · 15 Aug 1995 · Ministry of Rural Development · Article 41 · old-age pension to the very poor · NOT in NSAP / not social security · Mahila Kisan Sashaktikaran Pariyojana
  • PM Jan Dhan Yojana, JAM and Aadhaar — PMJDY · 28 Aug 2014 · Department of Financial Services · zero-balance · RuPay · Guinness World Record · Phase II (2015-18)
  • AP Social Security – NTR Bharosa, Chandranna Bima — NTR Bharosa (2014) · ₹1,500 for persons with 80%+ disability · YSR Pension Kanuka · NTR Bharosa Pension · ₹4,000 · ₹6,000 · ₹15,000
