RecapAgricultural Marketing – Apmc, E-nam, Contract Farming, Model Acts, Fpos, Warehousing

Recap

  • Grading and Standardisation – AGMARK — AGMARK · quality · agricultural goods · not FAO, not FCI, not BIS · AGMARKNET · One Nation, One Regulator · BIS and AGMARK
  • e-NAM – National Agriculture Market — April 2016 · One Nation, One Market · linking APMC mandis and other yards · SFAC · Agri-Tech Infrastructure Fund · Rs 75 lakh per mandi · Reforms required of States
  • APMC Acts, Model Acts and the Farm Laws 2020 — State List · APMC Acts enacted by States · area notified · 2,477 · cartels of licensed traders · price spread · consumer price − farmer's price
  • Contract Farming, e-Choupal and Small Farmer Large Field — Contract farming · no land transfer · Punjab (PepsiCo, late 1980s) · e-Choupal · ITC · Small Farmer Large Field (UPSC 2023) · PepsiCo tomato
  • Cooperative Marketing, NAFED and FPOs — NAFED · pulses, oilseeds, copra · TRIFED · AP MARKFED · Rs 3,000 crore Price Stabilisation Fund · 10,000 FPO scheme (2020) · Rs 6,865 crore
  • Operation Greens, Export Schemes and Agri Infrastructure — Operation Greens · Union Budget 2018-19 · TOP (tomato, onion, potato) · on the lines of Operation Flood · Food Processing Industries · TOP to TOTAL · 2021-22
  • Farmers' Markets and Marketing Institutions — Jaipur · GrAMs · 2018-19 · 22,000 · Krishi UDAN
  • Warehousing — e-NWRs · e-NAM · Cooperation · PACS
