Recap

  • NABARD – Establishment, Role, Refinance and RIDF — Merged/replaced at birth · Agricultural Credit Department (ACD) · Rural Planning and Credit Cell (RPCC) · Apex development finance institution · agriculture AND rural development · Primary role = refinance · not EXIM Bank
  • Cooperative Credit Structure and Land Development Banks — StCB → DCCB → PACS · LDBs / CARDBs · Cooperative Credit Structure · "No Profit No Loss" · NABARD is not a cooperative bank · Direct credit to rural households · RRBs and Land Development Banks
  • Regional Rural Banks — supplement Scheduled Commercial Banks · not take over agricultural refinance · Bhandari Committee (1994) · RRB restructuring · One State, One RRB · 1 May 2025 · 28 RRBs
  • Kisan Credit Card — August 1998 (1998-99) · NABARD model scheme · R.V. Gupta Committee · adequate and timely credit · Issuers · post-harvest expenses · consumption requirements
  • Sources and Shares of Agricultural Credit — Largest source · commercial banks · three-fourths · Agency order · least: foreign private banks · 1991 reforms · agriculture
  • Types of Credit and Rural Credit Terms — Taccavi (taqavi) · agricultural loans · Bank-linked SHG · modern · entry of commercial banks · JLG = Joint Liability Group · without collateral
  • Interest Subvention and Kisan Rin Portal — Central Sector · RBI and NABARD · Rs 5 lakh (Aug 2026) · 6 months · WDRA negotiable warehouse receipts · Kisan Rin Portal (September 2023) · manually with RBI and NABARD
