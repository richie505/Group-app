RecapProjects of External Assistance in Andhra Pradesh – World Bank, Adb, Jica and Other Funded Projects

Recap

  • Project → Funding Agency — Externally Aided Project (EAP) · HUDCO and NABARD are domestic · JICA · KfW · UNDP · APIIATP – World Bank · ADB
  • World Bank Projects in AP — first Indian State · independent World Bank loan · APRIGP · 19 December 2014 · $75 million · five components · value chain development
  • EAP Repayment under Special Assistance — Special Assistance Measure · 15 March 2017 · 2015-16 to 2019-20 · 90% · ₹7,798 crore · 17 EAPs · VCIC
  • Amaravati, ADB, NDB, KfW, AIIB — Japanese yen · IBRD · VCIC · East Coast Economic Corridor · APCRDA · not VCIC · NDB roads
