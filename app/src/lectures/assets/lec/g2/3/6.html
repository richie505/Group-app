RecapIndia and the Wto - Agreements, Ministerial Conferences, Public Stockholding, Fisheries Subsidies, Appellate Body Crisis

Recap

  • WTO Basics – Establishment, HQ, Members, DG, Principles — Nature · international organisation · broader multilateral framework · services, IPR and investment measures · "Third pillar" of the world economy · IMF and World Bank · multilateral trade
  • TRIPS, TRIMS, SPS and India's Compliance Laws — TRIMS (UPSC 2020) · goods, not services · 1 and 3 only · WTO · SPS · Codex Alimentarius Commission · FAO and WHO, 1963
  • GATT, Uruguay Round and the Dunkel Draft — 30 October 1947 · Geneva · 1948 · Geneva, 1948 · India · 8 July 1948 · tariffs and quantitative restrictions
  • Agreement on Agriculture – Pillars and Subsidy Boxes — 1 January 1995 · Peace Clause · WTO · amber, blue and green boxes · No Red Box · White, Pink, Yellow · S&D box
  • Ministerial Conferences, Doha Round and Country Groups — Highest decision-making body · at least once every two years · MC14 · India blocked · Annex 4 · Doha Round · central issue = agriculture
  • Public Stockholding and the Peace Clause — Peace Clause (Bali MC9, 2013) · food-security public stockholding (PSH) · breaches de minimis or AMS limits · notification · not made permanent · Agreement on Agriculture · India first to invoke it
  • Dispute Settlement and the Appellate Body Crisis — Appellate Body · December 2019 · USA blocked appointments · MPIA · EU-led, 2020 · India has not joined · restoration of the WTO Appellate Body
  • Fisheries Subsidies and E-commerce — MC12 (Geneva, June 2022) · in force September 2025 · IUU fishing · unregulated high seas · overfished stocks · marine wild capture · Fish Fund
