RecapMinimum Support Price – Mechanism, Cacp, Crops, Procurement, Criticism; Pm-aasha

Recap

  • MSP – Meaning, CACP and Who Fixes It — Minimum Support Price (MSP) · market intervention · sharp fall in farm prices · at the beginning of the sowing season · Purpose · distress sales · ensures a minimum fixed price
  • Crops Covered and MSP Figures — 22 mandated crops · FRP for sugarcane · 14 kharif + 6 rabi + 2 commercial · No MSP · areca nut, coffee, turmeric · Niger (Guizotia abyssinica, ramtil) · tribal
  • PM-AASHA, PSF, MIS and Other Price Support — PM-AASHA · September 2018 · farmers · Outlay ₹35,000 crore · up to 2025-26 · Pulses mission · tur, urad, masoor
  • Sugarcane – FRP, SMP and SAP — Legal basis · Sugarcane (Control) Order, 1966 · Essential Commodities Act, 1955 · recommends · not approve
  • Procurement at MSP and Its Price Effects — Neither · not unlimited · Rice prices · all four · Decentralised Procurement (DCP), 1997 · expedite kharif procurement · DCMS
  • Price Floors, Ceilings, Phases and WTO — WTO Agreement on Agriculture (1995) · Aggregate Measure of Support (AMS) · Amber box · de minimis 10% · green box · blue box · Peace clause (Bali, 2013)
  • CACP Factors, Cost Concepts and the MSP Formula — Factors CACP considers · cost of production · inter-crop price parity · effect on consumers / the economy · Not a factor · cost of living index · Present formula (since 2018-19)
  • Criticism and the Legal-Guarantee Debate — Ineffective MSP · Legal MSP · PDPS-type deficiency payments · Economic Survey 2025-26 · voluntary crop diversification
