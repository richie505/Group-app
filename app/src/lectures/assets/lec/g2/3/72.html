Recap

  • Union Deficit Trends and Targets — not risen steadily · non-tax revenue grew strongly · did not · three rating upgrades in 2025 · End-July 2026 · 26.8% · long history of fiscal deficits
  • Deficit Definitions and Formulas — excluding borrowings · fiscal deficit · PD = FD − interest · gross primary deficit · PD + interest = FD · ERD · Union Budget 2011-12
  • Objectives and Instruments; Fiscal vs Monetary — full employment · balanced regional development · Regulation of inter-state trade · monetary policy · 58.8% · taxation (tax policy) · public expenditure
  • Fiscal Policy – Meaning, Makers and Persons — Fiscal policy · taxation, expenditure and public debt · fiscus · Ministry of Finance · Department of Economic Affairs (DEA) · Article 112 · "Annual Financial Statement"
  • Relationships Between Deficits — FD > PD · FD > RD · ERD ≤ RD · PD is never higher than FD · FD > budgetary deficit · fiscal deficit · fiscal > revenue > primary
  • Fiscal Stance, Recession and Crowding Out — Recession · increase expenditure on public projects · Counter-cyclical · Atma Nirbhar Bharat, 2020 · Double-dip recession · another recession · Quality of deficit
  • Reducing the Deficit; Subsidies — downsizing bureaucracy · selling/offloading PSU shares · not · Reducing revenue expenditure · rationalising subsidies · rationalising subsidies through DBT · Financial subsidy
  • Financing the Fiscal Deficit — market borrowings (G-Secs) · small savings/NSSF · State Provident Funds · draw-down of cash · Domestic borrowings · 98-99% · ₹11.73 lakh crore
