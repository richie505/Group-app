RecapBasic Characteristics of the Indian Economy – Features of A Developing Economy

Recap

  • Features of a Developing (Underdeveloped) Economy — developing economy · underdeveloped · low per capita income · Low per capita income and unemployment · massive unemployment · Predominance of agriculture · primarily an agricultural economy
  • Economic Systems – India's Mixed Economy — Meaning · public and private sectors · How the State regulates · taxes, subsidies and credit · Socialistic pattern of society · through the mixed economy · both State and Person
  • Basic Concepts of Economics and Economists — "Economics" · Greek · Fixed cost · expenditure on machines · Perfect substitutes · Giffen good · income effect is stronger
  • Micro Basics – Production, Costs and Factor Pricing — Law of diminishing returns · marginal product eventually diminishes · Constant returns to scale · all inputs · labour alone by 10% · less than 10% · CES production function
  • Micro Basics – Market Structures, Oligopoly and Game Theory — Perfect market · large number of buyers and sellers · Monopolistic competition and monopoly · downward-sloping demand curve · never · pure profits in the long run · blocked entry
  • Micro Basics – Welfare Economics, General Equilibrium and Linear Programming — Pareto's welfare analysis · utility · MRT(xy) = MRS(xy) of A = MRS(xy) of B · Consumption (exchange) contract curve · Grand utility-possibility curve · both production and consumption · Social welfare function
  • India at Independence and the Colonial Legacy — Colonial rule · Plassey, 1757 · NOT a supplier of manufactured goods · stagnated · Paul Bairoch · 1.7% by 1900 · Angus Maddison
  • Ownership, Institutional Sectors and Savings — Largest share of national income · private sector · four institutional sectors · cooperatives are not a separate one · Gross domestic savings · household sector · SI 2017 match
