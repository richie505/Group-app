RecapAllied Sectors – Horticulture, Animal Husbandry, Dairy, Fisheries (pmmsy), Agro-processing

Recap

  • Animal Husbandry Practice – Breeding, Health, Poultry and Meat — Artificial insemination (AI) · superior bulls can reach every village · avoids contagious (venereal) diseases · economical · Small ruminants = sheep and goats · Ranikhet (Newcastle) disease · Small dog breed: pug
  • Veterinary Practice – Vaccination, Feeding and Livestock Management — Purpose of vaccinating animals · prevention of infectious diseases · Brucellosis · 4-6 (up to 8) months · Foot-and-mouth disease · inactivated (killed) · Broilers
  • Horticulture – Fruits, Mango, Spices and GI — Fruit ranks · mango, banana, guava, papaya · Top fruit by output in India: banana · national fruit: mango · Mango exports · Alphonso · Banganapalle
  • Food Processing – SAMPADA, Mega Food Parks, Operation Greens — PM Kisan SAMPADA Yojana (2017) · 32 projects sanctioned (2020) · Mega Food Parks · Operation Greens (2018) · Tomato-Onion-Potato (TOP) · MoFPI · PMFME
  • Dairy – Operation Flood, Cooperatives and Milk Science — Operation Flood · milk production · Operation Flood (1970-1996) · Anand pattern cooperatives (Amul) · NDDB · 1965 at Anand, Gujarat · per cow is low in India
  • Livestock – Population, Census and Schemes — 20th Livestock Census (2019) · 535.78 million · 21st Livestock Census · 16 species · 219 · Output ranks · 2nd
  • Fisheries – Blue Revolution, Production and PMMSY — Blue Revolution = fish production · NFDB · HQ Hyderabad · China is the largest fish producer · 36% · India 2nd · leads the world in inland capture
  • Sericulture and Apiculture — Mulberry silk · 90-95% · 'Kausheya' · Honey bees · Apis indica (Apis cerana indica) · Sweet Revolution · National Bee Board
