  • Wage and Self-employment Programmes – Lineage and Chronology — Order · 1970s motto · JRY objectives · and · JGSY · SGRY · JGSY and EAS
  • MGNREGA – Funds, Works, Monitoring and Social Audit — Women · one-third · about 56% of person-days (2016-17) · Work within 15 days · unemployment allowance · transfer payment · Funds
  • MGNREGA – Entitlement, Eligibility and Objectives — Objective · enhancing livelihood security · household · Unit = household · 150 days · Eligible · any rural household
  • MGNREGA 2005 – Enactment, Phases and Name — Enacted 2005, scheme launched 2006 · REGA / NREGA · Largest rural employment programme · Manmohan Singh · RTI 2005 · NFSA 2013
  • Other Employment and Skilling Schemes — DDU-GKY · not exclusively for rural girls · dividend · 10% · 100 days · 125 days from April 2023 · 15 days
  • Food for Work and NFFWP — Food for Work Programme · April 1977 · Janata government · food grains · NREP in October 1980 · First wage programme among exam options · 14 November 2004
