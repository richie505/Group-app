RecapStart-up India, Stand-up India, Innovation and Entrepreneurship Ecosystem

Recap

  • Start-up India – Launch, Bodies and Programmes — 16 January 2016 · job creators instead of job seekers · 16 January = National Startup Day · DPIIT · nodal agency for start-up recognition · Start-up and Stand-up India · enterprising youth
  • Stand-Up India Scheme (2016) — Non-individual enterprises · 51% shareholding and controlling stake · 85% · 7 years · 18 months · Working capital up to ₹10 lakh · overdraft
  • Entrepreneurship Programmes and Theory — 17 September 2023 · 5 years, FY 2023-24 to FY 2027-28 · Guru-Shishya parampara · 18 traditional trades · ₹1 lakh (1st tranche) + ₹2 lakh (2nd) · ₹3 lakh · 1961
  • Funds and Financing Sources for Start-ups — all the above · Big deal · Walmart · Flipkart · Microsoft · 100X100X100
  • Andhra Pradesh and Other State Start-up Ecosystems — AP Innovation Society (APIS) · 2015 · Best Performer · 9th · Ratan Tata Innovation Hub · Amaravati (Mangalagiri) · 20,000 start-ups and 1 lakh jobs
  • Start-up Definition and Benefits — registered partnership firm · 3 consecutive years out of 10 · 80-IAC · self-certification · angel tax abolished · 2024-25
  • Ecosystem Data and Unicorns — 3rd-largest start-up ecosystem · 2.47 lakh+ · Unicorn · US$1 billion+ · Hurun Global Unicorn Index 2026 · 4th with 61 · Bengaluru
