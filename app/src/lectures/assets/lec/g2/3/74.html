Recap

  • FRBM Act, 2003 – Origin, Objectives and Targets — 2000 · Yashwant Sinha · 26 August 2003 · 5 July 2004 · fiscal discipline · reducing revenue and fiscal deficits · both FD and RD
  • What It Prescribes and Does Not; Statements Laid — March 2009 · Annual Financial Statement · Art. 112 · suspended since 2021-22 · fiscal discipline a legal duty
  • N.K. Singh Review Committee and the 2018 Amendment — 2016-17 · May 2016 · five members · January 2017 · 2017-18 · N.K. Singh · Sumit Bose, Urjit Patel, Rathin Roy
  • State Fiscal Laws and Andhra Pradesh — 12th FC · Debt Consolidation and Relief Facility · their own · AP FRBM Act, 2005 · eliminate RD · FD 3% of GSDP · 2020
  • Kelkar Task Force, Amendments and the Consolidation Path — Vijay Kelkar · tax reform · comprehensive GST · 13th Finance Commission · 3% FD · 40% Central debt · Halving commitment
