Recap

  • State Bank of India – Origin, Size and Milestones — SBI "nationalised" in 1955 · Largest commercial bank · largest public sector bank · Headquarters: Mumbai · only Indian bank in the global top 100 · 47th April 2024 · odd one out
  • Banks – Founders, Taglines, Awards and News — Pattabhi Sitaramayya · Andhra Insurance Co. (1925) · Bharat Lakshmi Bank · Krishna District Co-operative Bank · INC President 1948-49 · Private · South Indian Bank
  • Bank Nationalisation – 1969 and 1980 — Total nationalised: 14 + 6 = 20 · 85% of bank deposits · Stray Thoughts on Bank Nationalisation · 1969 – 14 · Dena (now BoB) · Syndicate (now Canara) · 1980 – 6
  • Banking Operations, Instruments and Customer Rules — Deposits are NOT an asset · 14 June 1995 · Section 35A, BR Act 1949 · appointed by the RBI · not final · appeal · Green banking
  • Lead Bank Scheme and Service Area Approach — D. R. Gadgil Study Group · October 1969 · Nariman Committee · November 1969 · Lead Bank · Introduced by the RBI in December 1969 · bank-nationalisation
  • Bank Mergers and Banking Law Milestones — BR Act · C. D. Deshmukh · Canara + Syndicate · Indian Bank · 12 PSBs
  • Banking Firsts and Early History — Oudh Commercial Bank · Yes Bank · greenfield bank licence
  • Regional Rural Banks — regional · loss-making · One State-One RRB · 1 May 2025 · 43 → 28 · Andhra Pradesh Grameena Bank · four
