RecapRural Development – Issues, Programmes, Rural Infrastructure (pmgsy, Pmay-g, Jal Jeevan Mission)

Recap

  • Rural Institutions, Missions and Other Schemes — Budget · ₹1 lakh crore · 58,000 · MGNREGA · NRLM · Development communication · B2B media
  • Community Development and Early Rural Programmes (1948-1980) — Etawah Pilot Project · September 1948 · Lt. Col. Albert Mayer · first rural-development pilot project · Community Development Programme · 2 October 1952 · first rural development programme
  • PMGSY – Rural Roads — Launched 25 December 2000 · Objective · single all-weather road · develop the road network to villages · Target year · 1,000+ by 2003 · 500+ by 2007
  • Rurbanisation – PURA and SPMRM — PURA · Dr A.P.J. Abdul Kalam · 54th Republic Day, 2003 · *Target 3 Billion · Srijan Pal Singh · bridging the rural-urban divide · Rurban
  • Bharat Nirman (2005-2009) — Launched December 2005 · rural infrastructure · 2009 · Six components · Irrigation component · inter-linking of river projects · Drinking water
  • Rural Water and Sanitation – JJM and SBM-G — ODF sustainability convergence · RuRBAN · PMAY-G convergence · Ujjwala · 2011 · Ministry of Jal Shakti · June 2019
  • Housing – PMAY-G and PMAY-U — Indira Awaas Yojana · sub-scheme of Jawahar Rozgar Yojana · 1 Jan 1996 · PMAY-G (2016) · ₹1.20 lakh (plains) · ₹1.30 lakh (hilly/difficult) · SECC 2011
  • Rural Infrastructure Gaps and Andhra Pradesh — not high-speed rail · 16th Finance Commission · NTR Sujala · safe purified drinking water · 2007-08: 63.9% · JJM in AP · 73%
