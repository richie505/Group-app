  • Extension Services – KVK, Kisan Call Centre, ATMA — Extension methods · "seeing is believing" · field demonstrations · Meghdoot app (2019) · agro-meteorological advisories · Extension education · rural people
  • Mechanisation and Pesticides — labour-saving mechanisation · 40-45% · Zero Till Seed-cum-Fertiliser Drill · Cage wheel · wetland (paddy) · SMAM · custom hiring centres
  • Soil Health Card, Manures and Organic Farming — Soil Health Card Scheme · 19 February 2015, Suratgarh (Rajasthan) · Interval · once in every 3 years · free every two years · 8-10% · 25 crore
  • Andhra Pradesh – Inputs and Extension — Tenant farmers' cards · Crop Cultivator Rights Cards (CCRCs) · AP Crop Cultivators Rights Act 2019 · 9,13,283 · D-Krishi · seed · ATMA in AP
  • Input Subsidies vs Public Investment — MSP · 2, 3 and 6 only
