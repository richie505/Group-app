Recap

  • Disguised Unemployment – Meaning, Test and Where Found — Meaning · Test · marginal productivity of labour is zero · need not reduce production · Where · agriculture / the primary sector · rural areas
  • Types of Unemployment – Cause and Where Predominant — urban · all · At full employment · frictional · employs more hands · low-skilled, routine jobs · Structural unemployment
  • Nature and Causes of Unemployment in India — structural · cyclical · Cause in a developing country · labour-surplus economy · land-scarce and labour-surplus · 245.3 million by 2030 · Causes
  • Theories – Keynes, Classical, Phillips, Friedman, Okun — Natural rate = frictional + structural · Stagflation
  • Educated Unemployment and Youth — Urban · curriculum mismatch · Kerala · K.P. Kannan · 40.1% · NEET · 17.5%
  • Underemployment and Other Classifications — Disguised ≠ underemployment
