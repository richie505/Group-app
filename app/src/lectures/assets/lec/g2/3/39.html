  • Poverty Committees – Chronology, Chairmen and Firsts — Chronological order · Not poverty-line committees · Lakdawala and Tendulkar

  • Calorie Norms and the Method of Each Committee — Why State poverty lines differ · price levels vary from State to State · 2,400 kcal rural, 2,100 kcal urban · CPI-IW · CPI-AL
  • Recall Periods, NSS Data and Price Indices — incidence of poverty · 61st NSS round (2004-05) · both URP and MRP · household consumer expenditure · HCES 2022-23 and 2023-24
  • BPL Identification Committees — Automatic exclusion (Saxena) · Automatic inclusion · PVTGs · Hashim = urban BPL
  • NITI Aayog National Multidimensional Poverty Index — Body · NITI Aayog · OPHI and UNDP · Alkire-Foster · 3 equally weighted dimensions · 12 indicators · maternal health and bank account
  • Early Estimates and Poverty-Line Debates — Dadabhai Naoroji · ₹16-35 per capita per year · National Planning Committee (1938) · Bombay Plan (1944) · since the Sixth Plan · BPL Census · Debate
