Recap

  • Composition of Exports — engineering goods $116.5 bn · Petroleum products · crude petroleum · Basmati rice · 78.7% · Electronics · $38.56 bn
  • Direction of Trade — Total trade 2024-25 · FY 2025-26: China · $151.1 bn · $112 bn · export · Surplus · USA
  • Composition of Imports — US$ 185.8 bn, 25.8% · gold · one-third of imports · Two largest import groups · petroleum products and capital goods · Edible oil · agricultural
  • Trade Terms and Trade Theory — India · most anti-dumping measures in 1995-2005 · Singer-Prebisch thesis · secular decline in their terms of trade · welfare loss · Direction · Devaluation/depreciation
  • India's World Share and Services Trade — Ireland · $387.5 bn · record surplus $188.8 bn · 49% · 3rd in the Global South · USA · 2011: about 1.66-1.7%
  • Trade Deficit and Balance — Trade deficit = imports − exports · oil and gold · 2012-13 (US$ 195.66 bn) · 2016-17 (US$ 112.44 bn) · both POL and non-POL deficits fell · 2020-21 vs 2019-20 · declined
  • Trade Agreements and WTO — CEPA · CECA · Cooperation · 14 FTAs and 6 PTAs · ASEAN, Japan, Korea · SAPTA · SAFTA
  • AP Angle – Exports and Ports — marine products · Visakhapatnam Port · 50.87% · Kandla (first, 1965) · US$ 20.78 bn
