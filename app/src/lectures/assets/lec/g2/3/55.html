Recap

  • NITI Aayog – Basics, Date and Status — think tank, not a fund allocator · NITI Aayog · constitutional · Zonal Councils · statutory · Union Home Minister
  • Vice-Chairpersons, CEOs and Other Office-holders — VC and CEO are separate posts · Rajiv Kumar · Suman Bery · Ashok Lahiri · 149 state-owned oil and gas fields
  • Approach, Objectives and Functions — Cooperative federalism · "marble-cake" federalism · Bottom-up · top-down, one-size-fits-all · information processing · competitive federalism · competitive sub-federalism
  • Composition, Governing Council and Regional Councils — 5 full-time + 2 part-time · R. Balasubramaniam · Joram Aniya · Members · Chief Ministers of all States · CMs of Delhi and Puducherry · Lieutenant Governors of other UTs
  • Attached Offices, Hubs and Transformation Units — Aspirational Districts Programme · SDG India Index
  • Planning Commission vs NITI Aayog — 1950 · NDC · constitutional · not statutory · RBI nationalised 1 January 1949
  • Andhra Pradesh and NITI Aayog — State Institution for Transformation · Swarna Andhra @2047 · N. Chandrababu Naidu · 11th Governing Council · Visakhapatnam Economic Region · City and Regional Growth Hubs (G-HUB) · four
