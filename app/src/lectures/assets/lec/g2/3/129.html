Recap

  • AP Industrial Base, Investments and Rankings — Endowments · 1,053.07 km – 3rd longest · six operational ports · not · Manufacturing ≈ 11% of GSDP · 22.11% in 1987-88 · 18.83%
  • Single Desk Portal, APIIC, APEDB and Other Promotion Bodies — SDP · 93+ clearances · 19 departments · no physical visits · National · e-Biz · 28 Jan 2013
  • Industrial Development Policies – Editions, Targets and Incentives — IDP 2020-23 incentives · 16.2% · Rs 20 lakh · IDP 2023-27 aims · minimum 30% · 21 days · YSR AP One
  • MSME Policy 2015-20, Sectoral Policies and MSME (4.0) — at least 3.5 lakh people · at least Rs 15,000 crore by 2020 · Rs 100 crore · Rs 126 crore · M-Parks Policy · 200 MSME parks by 2023 · 30,000 MSMEs
  • Traditional Industries and Crafts — GI tags · Pochampally Ikat · Hand-crocheted lace · Narsapur and Palakollu · Narsapur crochet lace
  • Industrial Nodes, Corridors, Parks and SEZs — DIPP (1 March 2016) · Hindupur · Bobbili · AMTZ · Visakhapatnam · MAS · Nellore
