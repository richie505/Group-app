RecapFdi and Fpi Policy in India - Routes, Sectoral Caps, Trends; Ftas and Cepas Signed by India

Recap

  • Routes, Sectoral Caps and Prohibited Sectors — Negative-list approach · over 90% · Not a post-2010 change · automotive · Defence production · fully to private (Indian) investment · Lottery
  • FDI, FPI and FII – Meaning and What Counts — FDI · lasting interest and management control · largely non-debt-creating · stable · unlisted · 10% or more · Arvind Mayaram Committee
  • FDI Trends – Sources, Sectors, States — 2008-12 and last decade · telecommunications · services · Services = 80.2% · information and communication 25.8% · Weak net FDI · World Investment Report 2026
  • FTAs, CEPAs and RCEP — ASEAN's FTA partners · Australia, China, India, Japan · not Canada or USA · RCEP · 15 Nov 2020 · 1 January 2022 · India is not a member
  • Forms of Foreign Capital – ADR/GDR, P-Notes, ECB, NRI Deposits — P-Notes · registered FIIs/FPIs · without registering with SEBI · FCNR(B) · 15 May 1993 · ECBs · RBI under FEMA
  • FPI Limits and Flows — G-sec limits (General Route) · unchanged · Corporate bonds · 15% (from 9%) · Union Budget 2020-21 · 2024-25 (APPSC Group 2 Mains 2025) · narrow range
  • Press Note 3 – Land-border Countries — April 2020 · land border · Government route · Sri Lanka not covered · 10% · automatic route · PML Rules, 2005
