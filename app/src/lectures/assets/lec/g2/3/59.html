RecapReserve Bank of India – Establishment, Organisation and Functions

Recap

  • Currency Management – Note Issue, Reserve System and Demonetisation — all · wrong · Governor's · ₹10,000 · Minimum Reserve System · to any extent · Sec 26(2) of the RBI Act
  • Central Banking Functions – Banker to Government and Banker's Bank — Central Bank · public debt of Centre and States · Public Debt Office · all State Governments except Sikkim · Fiscal agent and adviser · represents India at the IMF · UPSC 2001
  • Monetary Authority, Credit Control and What RBI Does Not Do — Price stability / controlling inflation · RBI · controls "money and credit" · A-R (APPSC 2024) · because · R explains A · Regulating and controlling banks
  • Establishment, Nationalisation and Headquarters — Hilton Young Commission · on its recommendation · B.R. Ambedkar's 1925 · Austen Chamberlain · 1934 · 1935 · 1949
  • Organisation and Governors — Central Board of Directors · 4 years · Governor and Deputy Governors · Central Government · not more than 5 years · RBI Act · Four Deputy Governors
  • Note Presses and Mints — Dewas · printing currency notes · no mint at Delhi, Bangalore or Nashik
  • Regulatory Role, Committees and Publications — Banking Regulation Act, 1949 · RBI · UPSC 2013 · all four · RBI licence · mergers approved by the RBI · winding up
  • RBI Autonomy, Income and Developmental Role — UPSC 2021 · RBI Act · 1 and 3 only · Finance Commission · Article 280 · Not income · cost
