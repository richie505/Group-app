  • GM Crops in India – Approvals, Bt Brinjal and GM Mustard — insect/pest resistance · DMH-11 = Dhara Mustard Hybrid-11 · "2 (B) only" · *Bacillus thuringiensis · Defiance · Maharashtra and Haryana · 7-10 years

  • IPR, Biopiracy and Farmers' Rights — Intellectual property · Biopiracy · TKDL (2001) · CSIR and the Ministry of AYUSH · prior-art · loss of seed-saving
  • The GM Debate – Biodiversity, Health and GM Food Law — fungus · bacterium · 3 and 4 only · Genetic modification · biodiversity · 1 March 2021 · "non-GM cum GM-free" certificate
  • Biosafety, Cartagena Protocol and Bioethics — Cartagena Protocol on Biosafety · CBD · living modified organisms (LMOs) · January 2000 in Montreal · 2003 · Cartagena, Colombia · USA
