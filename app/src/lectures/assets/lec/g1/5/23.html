Recap

  • IT Milestones, Institutions and Companies — IT companies · Infosys · N.R. Narayana Murthy · TCS · Raymond · Technopark, Thiruvananthapuram (1990) · first technology park
  • NPIT 2012 – Goals, Context and Targets — Goals · Context (2011-12) · USD 100 bn · ~80% · exports · six hubs · 90%
  • National Policy on Electronics 2019 and Schemes — NPE 2019 · 20 February 2019 · ESDM · including chipsets · Schemes (2020) · PLI · SPECS
  • India Semiconductor Mission — Semicon 2.0 pillars · C2S (Chips to Start-up, 2022) · C-DAC (not NIELIT) · 85,000
  • Outcomes and AP IT Policy — Digital economy · ~12-14% of GDP · ~20% by 2029-30 · Electronics · ₹13.11 lakh crore · 3rd-largest export · GCCs
