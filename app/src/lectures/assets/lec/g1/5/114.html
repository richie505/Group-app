RecapHealth and Environment – Vector-borne and Water-borne Diseases, Air Pollution, Amr, One Health

Recap

  • Bacterial and Viral Diseases – Quick Match — Meningitis · meninges · Pneumonia · alveoli · Triple antigen (DPT) · Haemophilus influenzae (Hib) · Chickenpox
  • Water-borne and Faeco-oral Diseases — Unsafe water + poor sanitation · 5,000 children die daily · Kwashiorkor · Floods · leptospirosis · Shallow hand-pump · NOT fluorosis
  • Mosquito-borne Diseases – Vectors and Pathogens — Vector · vectors · Only female mosquitoes suck blood · bigger wings · males feed on plant juices/nectar · Culex, Anopheles and Aedes · NOT mosquito-borne
  • Zoonotic and Animal Diseases — Rabies, Ebola, Nipah = zoonotic viral · malaria = vector-borne, not zoonotic · Fever types · remittent · more than 2°F · Anthrax · biological weapon
  • Antibiotics and Antimicrobial Resistance — Fleming: penicillin, 1928 · Waksman · streptomycin · natural or semi-synthetic · Prontosil · Griseofulvin · Acetobacter
  • Worm (Helminth) Diseases — Tapeworm = hermaphrodite · roundworm = separate sexes · filaria is caused by a nematode · Livestock worms · deworming (anthelmintics) · Amphistomiasis · snail control
  • Infection, Immunity and Vaccination — Disease vs symptom · cholera · fever · Contagious · yawning · not spread by casual contact · Measles
  • Malaria and Plasmodium — Pathogen · *Plasmodium · liver · red blood cells · spleen · Five · several species

Recap (cont.)

  • Fungal Diseases, Mycotoxins and Plant Diseases — Baldness · colour blindness = genetic · Epidemic dropsy
  • Air Pollution and Health — respiratory system · PM2.5 raises heart disease · natural sources too · planting trees reduces · WHO · 4.2 million · ambient
  • Sandfly, Tsetse Fly, Kissing Bug and Other Vectors — not kala-azar
  • Vector Control, Insecticide Resistance and Elimination — DDT resistance · natural selection · 50-60% · 'Debug' · NCVBDC · Elimination · 2027
  • One Health, Surveillance and Programmes — One Health · One Health Summit 2026 · National One Health Mission · PM-STIAC · BCG · at birth · WHO definition of health (1948)
  • Climate-sensitive Diseases — 3.6 billion+ · WHO · 2.5 lakh extra deaths a year · child undernutrition 95,000 · 58% (218 of 375) · 'Under the Weather' · 40% of districts
  • Andhra Pradesh – Disease Burden and Response — ASR, Polavaram, Vizianagaram · 941 · Agency (tribal) districts · IRS, LLINs, source reduction · Adult BCG drive · 16 May 2024 · 70.3
