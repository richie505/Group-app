RecapNuclear Energy – Three-stage Programme, Reactor Types, Phwr/fbr/ahwr, Thorium Strategy

Recap

  • Fission vs Fusion – Chain Reaction, Reactor vs Bomb, Energy of the Sun — Breaking apart of a nucleus = fission · more neutrons · chain reaction · Critical mass · criticality · Reactor vs atom bomb · reactor it is controlled
  • Atomic Structure – Nucleus, Isotopes, Isobars and Isotones — Nucleus = protons + neutrons · almost all the mass · gold-foil (α-scattering) · positively charged nucleus · electric (electrostatic) · valence electrons · James Chadwick (1932)
  • Nuclear Fuels – Fissile vs Fertile, Enrichment, What Is Not a Fuel — Uranium · thorium · natural uranium · Enrichment · U-235 · isotope separation · gas centrifuge
  • Radioactivity – Alpha, Beta, Gamma and Half-life — Henri Becquerel (1896) · nuclear · alpha, beta and gamma · Geiger (Geiger–Müller) counter · α < β < γ · β⁻ emission · one proton
  • Reactor Components and Reactor Types — out of control · Heavy water · moderator · both moderator and coolant · Ordinary water · Light-water reactors · over 85%
  • Research Reactors, BARC and DAE Institutions — power · Dhruva · Chicago Pile-1, 1942 · Enrico Fermi · Italian · Atomic Energy Act, 1948 · peaceful uses
  • Three-stage Programme, Thorium and the AHWR — Homi J. Bhabha · abundant thorium · scarce uranium · closed fuel cycle · 25% · large reserves in India · Thorium over uranium
  • Fast Breeder Reactors – FBTR, PFBR, IGCAR and BHAVINI — Breeder reactor · more fissile material than it burns · fission · U-238 blanket · Pu-239 · Pu-239 fissions · No moderator; liquid sodium coolant
