Recap

  • Human Spaceflight Firsts and Records — Own crewed launch so far · USSR/Russia, USA, China · 4th · Axiom-4 · 25 Jun 2025 · SpaceX Dragon on Falcon 9 · 18 days
  • Space Stations – ISS, Tiangong and BAS — BAS (Bharatiya Antariksh Station) · Sep 2024 · five modules · BAS-1 by 2028 · 2035 · 2040 · SpaDeX
  • Test Missions, Vyommitra and Gaganyatris — Vyommitra · female · uncrewed G1 and G2 · IAF test pilots · Pankaj M. Sinha is NOT · Astronaut Training Facility, Bengaluru · parabolic flights
  • Microgravity and the Human Body — Weightlessness · normal force is zero · zero gravity · 1/6 · Earth's centre · Charlie Brown effect · puffy face and thin legs
  • Infrastructure, Institutions and Analogue Missions — ISRO Human Spaceflight Expo · Jawaharlal Nehru Planetarium, Bengaluru · 22-24 Jul 2022 · India's first analogue space mission · Leh, Ladakh · Nov 2024 · AAKA
  • Gaganyaan – Programme, Spacecraft and Flights — first human spaceflight programme · 2018 · crew of 3 · ~400 km LEO · 3 days · Indian Navy · HLVM3
