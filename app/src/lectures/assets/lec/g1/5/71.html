Recap

  • PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana and Net Metering — Launched Feb 2024; MNRE · ₹75,021 crore · March 2027 · Up to 300 units/month free · prosumers · 100% online · Capacity building
  • UDAY and RDSS – Discom Finances — 75% · 50% in 2015-16 + 25% in 2016-17 · UDAY bonds · 97% · Rajasthan · largest discom debt exposure
  • Saubhagya, DDUGJY and IPDS – Electrification — Saubhagya · Sept/Oct 2017 · universal household electrification · rural and urban · DDUGJY (Dec 2014) · feeder separation · RGGVY
  • PM-KUSUM – Farm Solar — PM-KUSUM (2019, MNRE) · subsidies for solar irrigation pumps · 30% Centre, 30% State, 40% farmer
  • Andhra Pradesh – Free Power and Rooftop Solar — Free power for SC/ST households · 75 units · 100 units · 200 units · 20.26 lakh · Virtual Net Metering · 2.93 lakh
  • Clean Cooking – PMUY — PMUY (2016, MoPNG) · 8 crore · 2019 · 9.5 crore+ · Ujjwala 2.0 · self-declaration · ₹1,600
