RecapIndia's Climate Commitments – NDCs, Panchamrit, Net-zero 2070, LiFE, Isa, Cdri, Carbon Market

Recap

  • Climate Indices and Coalitions – CCPI, C40, Biofuels Alliance — since 2005 · empty · in 2019 · released in 2019 · CCPI 2020 → 9th · 11th · 83%
  • International Solar Alliance and CDRI — ISA Assembly · Malta = 119th member (Feb 2024) · One Sun One World One Grid · IRIS · CDRI · COP26 (2021) · Gurugram
  • Panchamrit and Net Zero 2070 — COP26, Glasgow, 1 Nov 2021 · 500-50-1-45-2070 · II, III and IV · Net zero · balanced by removals · not · CBDR-RC
  • India's NDCs – INDC 2015 to NDC 3.0 (base year 2005) — INDC · climate-change action plan · Base year 2005 · 45% · MoEFCC · Union Cabinet · not remove adaptation
  • Energy Transition and Carbon Sink — positive measure · 51.5% · generation share only ~25% · 3rd · Green Hydrogen Mission · ₹19,744 crore · 5 MMT/yr by 2030
  • Mission LiFE — LiFE = Lifestyle for Environment · India-led global mass movement · individual and community action · COP26 (2021) · 5 June 2022 · 20 Oct 2022 · Kevadia (Ekta Nagar), Gujarat
  • Carbon Markets – CCTS and Green Credit Programme — compliance · Carbon Credit Certificates · offset · Intensity-based, not cap-based · 45% · 282 obligated entities · UK CBAM
