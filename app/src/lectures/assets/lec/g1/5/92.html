Recap

  • Greenhouse Gases – Which Are and Which Are NOT — Natural GHGs · Synthetic · Most important set · Most responsible pair for warming · CO2 and methane · never oxygen · Statement sets
  • The Greenhouse Effect and Heating of the Atmosphere — Greenhouse effect · lower atmosphere · absorption and re-radiation of heat · naturally occurring · Joseph Fourier · 1820s · −18°C
  • Sources – Fossil Fuels, Deforestation, Paddy and Livestock — Largest source of GHGs · burning fossil fuels · deforestation · Primary cause of present global warming · increase in GHG concentrations · Deforestation increases CO2 · loss of biodiversity
  • Contribution Order and Global Warming Potential — N2O < CH4 < CO2 · Second most abundant anthropogenic GHG · methane · Water vapour · indirectly · GWP · 100 years
  • Observed Impacts and Evidence — Matching (APPSC) · patches of cooling · Floods · indirect · Anthropocene · Indicators of past climate change · Keeling curve
  • Carbon Footprint, Carbon Pricing and Removal — Singapore · from 2019 · industry · $86/t CO2 · EU CBAM · 1 Jan 2026
  • Radiative Forcing, Feedbacks, Tipping Points and Warming Limits — WMO Bulletin (Oct 2025) · 16% · CO2 · 54% rise · 2°C · Amazon · savanna
  • Short-lived Climate Pollutants and Black Carbon — SLCPs · Black carbon · Climate and Clean Air Coalition (CCAC) · methane, black carbon and HFCs · not a G20 initiative · Tropospheric ozone · secondary pollutant
