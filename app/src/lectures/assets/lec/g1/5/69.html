RecapEnergy Storage – Battery Technologies, Pli for Acc, Grid Storage

Recap

  • Cells and Batteries – Basics and Redox — redox · Battery · two or more cells · Oxidation = loss of electrons · anode · reduction = gain of electrons · cathode
  • Common Batteries – Dry Cell, Lead-acid, Ni-Cd — Dry cell A–R (UP 2004) · I and IV · sulphuric acid · ammonium chloride + zinc chloride
  • Lithium-ion and New Battery Chemistries — Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2019 · development of lithium-ion batteries · Li-ion · anode → cathode on discharge · anode = graphite · LiCoO2, LiFePO4/LFP, NMC · Mobile phone batteries
  • EVs, Fuel Cells and Alternative Powertrains — all three · water vapour · Battery swapping policy · 2022-23 · PM E-DRIVE · FAME-II · 8.5%
  • Battery Minerals and Lithium Finds — Lithium in J&K · 5.9 million tonnes · import dependent · lithium, nickel, cobalt · >70% · Marlagalla, Mandya · KABIL
  • Andhra Pradesh – Storage Firsts — World's first thermal battery plant · Bharat Energy Storage Technology (BEST) · High Energy Density Storage (HEDS) · Amaravati · Pinnapuram · pumped storage · 3,931 MW battery
  • PLI for ACC Batteries — 12 May 2021 · 50 GWh · ₹18,100 crore · Ministry of Heavy Industries · Ola Electric 20 GWh · Krishnagiri, Tamil Nadu · Reliance 15 GWh
  • Grid Storage – BESS, Pumped, Mechanical and Thermal — bottleneck · 24x7 RE · 336 GWh by 2029-30 · 500 MWh · Energy Storage Obligation · Ministry of Power · 1% (2023-24) → 4% (2029-30)
