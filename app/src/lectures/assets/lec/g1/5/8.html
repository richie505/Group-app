Recap

  • Indian Supercomputers and Computing Firsts — PARAM · C-DAC, Pune · PARAM 8000 (1991) · first · Vijay Bhatkar · father of Indian supercomputing · Computing firsts
  • National Supercomputing Mission (NSM, 2015) – Systems and Hosts — DST and MeitY · C-DAC Pune + IISc Bengaluru · National Supercomputing Grid · National Knowledge Network · 40 systems · Rudra · Trinetra
  • National Quantum Mission (NQM) — Qubit · quantum information · superposition · 50–1000 physical qubits · 2000 km · QNu Labs · 14 April = World Quantum Day
  • Deep Ocean Mission — Ministry of Earth Sciences · June 2021 · ₹4,077 crore · Samudrayaan · Matsya 6000 · NIOT Chennai · 3 people to 6,000 m
  • BIO-E3, ANRF and Other Missions — 24 Aug 2024 · DBT · smart proteins · climate-resilient agriculture · marine and space research · over US$165 billion · Biopharma SHAKTI
  • Andhra Pradesh Quantum Push – Amaravati Quantum Valley — Andhra Pradesh · first State · quantum computing policy · Uddandarayunipalem · 14 April 2026 · Quantum Reference Facility · 1S at SRM University-AP
