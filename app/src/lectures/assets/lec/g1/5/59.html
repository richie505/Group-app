RecapElectricity Sector – Capacity Mix, Generation, Transmission, Discom Finances, Electricity Act 2003

Recap

  • Electricity Basics – Units, Frequency, Transformers — kWh ("units") · 50 Hz · Step-up transformer · voltage · not · transformer works only on AC · capacitor stores electric charge/energy
  • Andhra Pradesh Power Scenario — Central Generating Stations · no nuclear · No coal of its own · Singareni and MCL · 21,297 MW · 9.25% · APEPDCL Visakhapatnam
  • Discom Finances – Why the SEBs Went Sick — below-cost sale · poor cost recovery · rising commercial losses · delays · Not a cause · autonomy of States · profit ₹2,701 crore
  • UDAY, RDSS and Smart Meters — UDAY · Nov 2015 · 75% · RDSS (2021) · ₹3.03 lakh crore · REC and PFC · TOTEX (PPP)
  • DDUGJY and Saubhagya – Rural Electrification — 28 April 2018 · 18,374 · Leisang (Manipur) · solarised under PM-KUSUM
  • Thermal Power – Plants, UMPPs and Reforms — UMPP · 4,000 MW supercritical coal · Sasan · Krishnapatnam · Reliance Power · Mundra UMPP – Tata Power · FGD
  • Transmission – One Nation One Grid — One Nation-One Grid-One Frequency · largest synchronous grid · Five regional grids · Southern · December 2013 · POWERGRID (PGCIL) · Green Energy Corridor
  • Installed Capacity Mix — coal > solar > wind > large hydro · 44.6 GW · 552 GW · over 54% · Gujarat · Maharashtra · CEA National Generation Adequacy Plan
