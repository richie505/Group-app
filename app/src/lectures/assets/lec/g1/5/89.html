RecapSpecies-specific Conservation Projects – Tiger, Elephant, Dolphin, Gib, Vulture, Cheetah

Recap

  • Project Tiger – Launch, First Reserves and Tiger Reserves — Project Tiger · 1973 · task force set up by PM Indira Gandhi · save the tiger from extinction · First reserve · Jim Corbett NP (Uttarakhand) · 1 April 1973
  • Vultures and Diclofenac — Cause · diclofenac · painkiller (NSAID) · cattle · kidney failure (visceral gout) · Peregrine Fund · Decline first noticed
  • Ganges River Dolphin and Project Dolphin — National Aquatic Animal · Ganges River Dolphin · 5 October 2009 · 5 October = National Dolphin Day · Dolphins are mammals · Status · Endangered
  • Great Indian Bustard, CMS and Other Species — Critically Endangered · Thar (Rajasthan) · Rollapadu · Threat · overhead power lines · Arts 14 and 21 · Sam and Ramdevra, Jaisalmer
  • Project Elephant — Launched February 1992 · Centrally Sponsored Scheme · Objectives · corridors · man-animal conflict · 33 Elephant Reserves in 14 States · MIKE
  • Project Cheetah, IBCA and Project Lion — Cheetah · extinct in India (declared 1952) · Iran · African · Project Cheetah · open forest and grassland · Kuno NP (Sheopur, MP)
  • Tiger Estimation, NTCA and Global Tiger Efforts — Pugmark technique · estimate the population · Now · camera trapping · ExtractCompare · DNA from scats · double sampling
  • Species Recovery Programme and Conservation Organisations — Species Recovery Programme · gharial and sloth bear · National CAMPA Advisory Council · 5 years · Project HANUMAN (AP, 3 March 2026) · BNHS · NGO
