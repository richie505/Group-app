RecapDigital Identity and Payments – Aadhaar, Upi, Dbt, India Stack, Ondc, Account Aggregator

Recap

  • Aadhaar and UIDAI — UIDAI · 28 January 2009 · Planning Commission · Aadhaar Act, 2016 · MeitY · 12-digit random number · free and lifelong
  • UPI, NPCI Products and Payment Systems — April 2016 · NPCI · VPA · UPI 2.0 · Rs 2 lakh · UPI Lite · without a UPI PIN
  • Nodal Bodies, Committees and Digitisation Drive — After demonetisation (Nov 2016) · NITI Aayog · N. Chandrababu Naidu · MeitY · February 2017 · 17 July 2023 · Nilekani Committee
  • India Stack and Open Digital Platforms — online, paperless, cashless · identity layer (Aadhaar e-KYC) · Open-source platforms · Aarogya Setu, CoWIN, DigiLocker, DIKSHA · ONDC (2022) · breaks platform monopolies · Account Aggregator
  • DBT – JAM and Delivery in Andhra Pradesh — JAM · CPSMS (now PFMS) · Aadhaar Payment Bridge · First JAM application · PAHAL · DBT Mission · Cabinet Secretariat
  • Chronology and Data — UPI FY 2025-26 · 85% · 49% · 41% CAGR
