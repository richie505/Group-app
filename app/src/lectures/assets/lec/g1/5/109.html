RecapEco-friendly Technologies in Agriculture – Ipm, Biofertilisers, Biopesticides, Organic and Natural Farming

Recap

  • Fertilisers, Plant Nutrients, Manures and Soil Health — Magnesium is a macronutrient · manganese and zinc are micronutrients · 4:2:1 · Khaira · Excess nitrogen delays maturity · hastens · wheat
  • Biofertilisers — Biofertilisers · fix nitrogen, solubilise phosphorus · applied to seed/soil · do not alter soil chemistry · can be used with organic manures · absorptive area / drought resistance · tolerate extreme pH
  • Sustainable Agriculture and Environmental Impact of Modern Farming — Sustainable agriculture · judiciously · pollute soil and water · does · harms · Rachel Carson, *Silent Spring* (1962) · environmental protection
  • Organic Farming – Sikkim, Schemes and Certification — Organic farming · world's first 100% organic State · FAO Future Policy (Gold) Award 2018 · Sikkim, Meghalaya, Nagaland · Assam is not one of them · Padma Shri 2019 · Venkateswara Rao Yadlapalli
  • Pesticides, Fumigants and Mosquito Repellents — superphosphate
  • Natural Farming – ZBNF, APCNF and NMNF — ZBNF · Subhash Palekar · Karnataka · Chemical fertilisers are not used · cow dung, urine, jaggery · opposes vermicompost · farmer collectives / organic clusters
  • Integrated Pest Management, Pests and Plant Diseases — IPM (FAO) · all suitable techniques · below the economic injury level · optimise, not maximise · cultural control · birds, dust, rain and wind
  • Biopesticides – Neem, Bt and Microbial Agents — Biopesticides · not correct
