RecapForest and Wildlife Legal Framework – Ifa 1927, Fca 1980, Wpa 1972, Bd Act 2002

Recap

  • Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972 – Year, Scope and Key Sections — Enacted by Parliament in 1972 · Protects wild animals, birds and plants · not animals only · Poaching · 66 sections, 7 chapters · 2022 · Gharial, Indian wild ass, wild buffalo
  • WPA Schedules and the 2022 Amendment — Number of schedules · Schedule I effect · the same protection as the tiger · Schedule VI (old) · licence is required to cultivate · Flying fox · fruit bat
  • Indian Forest Act 1927 and Colonial Forest Laws — Degree of protection · Reserved > Protected > Village · village forest · reserved forest · Reserved forest (Ch II) · Sec 26 · trespass
  • Forest (Conservation) Act 1980 / Van Adhiniyam — Enacted 1980 · 25 Oct 1980 · Sec 2 · non-forest purposes · regulates forest-land diversion · 2023 amendment · Exemptions
  • Authorities – WCCB, NBWL, NTCA, AWBI — WCCB · 2007 · New Delhi · five regional offices · AWBI · 1962 · Chennai
  • National Forest Policy 1952 and 1988 — NFP 1988 includes · wood substitutes and alternative fuels · insecticides and pesticides · Ecological balance · Draft NFP 2018 · NAPCC launched 2008 · Order
  • Biological Diversity Act 2002 and Which Act Protects What — "Nature (Protection) Act, 2000" · not exist · NGT · WPA 1972 · BD Act 2002 · NBA (Chennai, 2003) · Biodiversity Management Committees
  • Protected Areas and Community Reserves under WPA — Five kinds · Community Reserve (Sec 36C) · State Government · hunting is not allowed · collect non-timber forest produce · NP vs sanctuary · India's PAs
