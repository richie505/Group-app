Recap

  • Right to Privacy – Article 21 and Puttaswamy — Decided 24 Aug 2017 · 9-judge · unanimous · Part III · Fundamental Right · Article 21 · Justice K.S. Puttaswamy (Retd)
  • GDPR and the Right to Be Forgotten — GDPR · European Union · April 2016 · 25 May 2018 · 1995 · financial penalties only · Right to Be Forgotten
  • DPDP Rules 2025, Localisation and Surveillance — DPDP Rules · 3 Jan 2025 · 14 Nov 2025 · Consent Manager Nov 2026 · May 2027 · Localisation · RBI (April 2018)
  • Aadhaar Judgment (26 Sep 2018) — 5 judges, 4:1 · Justice D.Y. Chandrachud
  • Road to the DPDP Act — Kris Gopalakrishnan · non-personal data · A.K. Bhargava · net neutrality
  • DPDP Act 2023 – Scope and Terms — digital · non-personal · foreign entities
  • Rights, Duties and Children — Rights · erasure (Sec. 12) · nomination · Duties · frivolous complaints · Children · verifiable parental consent below 18
  • Exemptions and RTI — Sec. 7 · legitimate uses · Sec. 17 · State agencies · journalism – no exemption · Sec. 44(3) · RTI Sec. 8(1)(j)
