Recap

  • Bretton Woods Conference and the IMF — Bretton Woods Conference · July 1944 · Bretton Woods, New Hampshire, USA · 44 allied nations · Harry Dexter White · John Maynard Keynes · IMF + IBRD (World Bank)
  • World Bank Group — IBRD · World Bank · Washington, D.C · medium- and long-term · India a founding member · Match (UPSC 1999) · IDA – soft loans
  • Heads of the Bretton Woods Institutions and Persons in News — Jim Yong Kim · 2012-2019 · Ajay Banga · Krishna Srinivasan · IMF Asia and Pacific Department
  • NDB, ADB and AIIB — 4th BRICS Summit, New Delhi, 2012 · Fortaleza Declaration · Shanghai, China · GIFT City · K.V. Kamath · equal votes; no veto · Egypt
  • Reports – Which Institution Publishes What — 2.5%
  • India, AP and Externally Aided Projects — 1991 · $7 bn · $1.5 bn · National Green Hydrogen Mission · AIIB + World Bank · ₹7,500 crore · Mission Schools of Excellence, Gujarat
