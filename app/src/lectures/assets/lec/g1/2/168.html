RecapAndhra Pradesh Welfare and Social Security Programmes

Recap

  • Farmer and Occupational Support Schemes — Launched · October 2019 · Navaratnalu · Amount · land-owning · includes · Promise vs payment
  • Women, SHG, Marriage and Girl-Child Schemes — SERP · leverages community-based organisations · 84.47 lakh rural women · MEPMA · 2.74 lakh urban SHGs · Streenidhi · Anna Sanjeevani
  • Education Welfare — Jagananna Amma Vodi · Rs 15,000 a year · mother/recognised guardian · enrols and attends · school-enrolment support · Talliki Vandanam · Rs 15,000 per child
  • Health Welfare — YSR Aarogyasri (2019-24) · below Rs 5 lakh · Rs 1,000 or more · Hyderabad, Chennai, Bengaluru · Rs 10,000 a month pension · Rs 25 lakh per family a year · 1.65 crore poor and middle-class
  • Delivery Machinery, Women's Safety and Campaigns — Family is the unit · Nava Nirmana Deeksha · 2 June 2015 · Chandranna Bata · internal CC roads in villages · 30 March 2025 (Ugadi) · top 10% support the bottom 20%
  • Navaratnalu, Super Six and Renamed Schemes — nine · revenue expenditure · Rythu Bharosa · Aarogyasri · Amma Vodi · Pedalandariki Illu · Pension Kanuka
  • Social Security Pensions — NTR Bharosa · social security pension scheme · 3.5 lakh new pensions · January 2017 · 61.92 lakh · Rs 27,719 crore · Aadhaar biometric
  • Food, Housing and Sanitation — Door delivery of ration · 2021 · 5 kg rice per family member · Anna Canteens · Rs 5 · urban poor · Dharanikota (Palnadu)
