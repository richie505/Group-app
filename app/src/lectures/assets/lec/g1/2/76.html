RecapCentral Information Commission and State Information Commissions; Cbi and Its Legal Basis

Recap

  • CBI – Legal Basis, Director and Units — Special Police Establishment (1941) · CBI · CBI set up 1 April 1963 · Ministry of Home Affairs resolution · CVC (1964) · Neither constitutional nor statutory · Ministry of Personnel (DoPT)
  • Status of the Bodies and Who Appoints Whom — either House of Parliament · Public Information Officers · each public authority
  • Other Enforcement Agencies and the Corruption Perceptions Index — Vohra Committee (1993) · PMLA · Director · Corruption Perceptions Index · Transparency International · 0–100 · CPI 2025
  • Information Commissions Under the RTI Act — CIC · 2005 · Section 12 · public authority · No appeal lies against the CIC's order · binding · writ
  • Andhra Pradesh – ACB and Vigilance Commission — AP Anti-Corruption Bureau · PC Act · State Vigilance Commission (1964)
