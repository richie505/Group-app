RecapParliament – Composition of Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha, Qualifications, Terms, Special Powers of Rajya Sabha

Recap

  • Seats by State in Both Houses – the AP Angle — Rajya Sabha · Fourth Schedule · 4 and 80(2) · by population · 1971 Census · One LS seat · Chandigarh
  • Rajya Sabha – Composition, Election and Nominated Members (Article 80) — Maximum 250 · 238 · States and UTs · 12 nominated · Present 245 · 8 UTs · Delhi (3), Puducherry (1), J&K (4)
  • Terms of the Houses; Lok Sabhas and General Elections (Article 83) — Rajya Sabha – Art. 83(1) · permanent House · never dissolved · 6-year · RPA, 1951 · lottery · Lok Sabha – Art. 83(2)
  • Lok Sabha – Composition and Strength (Articles 81, 330, 331) — Art. 81 · 530 · States · UTs · maximum 550 · 552 before 2020, 550 now · Art. 331
  • Parliament – Article 79 and the President's Place — Article 79 · President · Council of States (Rajya Sabha) · House of the People (Lok Sabha) · President + RS + LS · not · Union Legislature
  • Rajya Sabha vs Lok Sabha – Equal Powers and Special Powers — No joint sitting for a Money Bill · Lok Sabha has overriding powers · Financial Bill (I) · No tax without LS approval · President gives assent · not · LS dissolved
  • Qualifications and Minimum Ages (Article 84) — Article 84 · EC-authorised person · 25 years (LS) · 30 years (RS) · RPA, 1951 · elector in a parliamentary constituency · No educational qualification
  • Disqualification and Vacation of Seats — Art. 102 · office of profit · law of Parliament · undischarged insolvent · not lack of a degree · Art. 103 · President

Recap (cont.)

  • Expansion and Delimitation Debate (2026) — ~815 · 2.2:1 → 3.3:1 · 81 → 122 · 550 → 850 · defeated · Tamil Nadu Assembly · 543
