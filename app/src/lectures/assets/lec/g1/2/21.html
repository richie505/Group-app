RecapBasic Structure Doctrine – Kesavananda Bharati and After

Recap

  • Kesavananda Bharati (1973) – Where the Doctrine Was Propounded — basic structure doctrine · first propounded · None of the above · 24 April 1973 · 13 judges · 7:6 · CJI S.M. Sikri
  • What the Basic Structure Means — core features · Art. 368 · cannot be abrogated · judicial interpretation · judge-made · Act of Parliament · Not exhaustive
  • Evolution – Shankari Prasad to the NJAC Case — 1967 · Golak Nath v. State of Punjab · Fundamental Rights cannot be amended · Kesavananda Bharati · Judicial review · L. Chandra Kumar (1997) · nowhere defined or used
  • Elements Declared Basic Structure → Case — S.R. Bommai · secularism · even before the 42nd Amendment (1976) · not amendable · Judicial activism · not a basic feature
  • Ninth Schedule, Articles 31A–31C and the Coelho Case — 1st Amendment (1951) · Art. 31B · Art. 15(4) · Art. 19(2) · land-reform · Central and State · 76th Amendment, 1994
  • Supreme Court vs Parliament – Doctrines, Supremacy, Criticism — not sovereign in the British sense · limited · synthesis · 43rd Amendment (1977) · 2026 · voting · 129th Amendment Bill, 2024
