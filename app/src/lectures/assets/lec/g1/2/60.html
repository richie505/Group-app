Recap

  • State Finance Commission – Article 243I — Governor · Finance Commission · every five years · constitutional body · 243Y · same SFC · Municipalities
  • Fourteenth Finance Commission (2015-20) – Grants to Local Bodies — 14th FC (Y.V. Reddy) · ₹2,87,436 crore · Panchayats ₹2,00,292 crore · duly constituted · only to Gram Panchayats · directly to the GPs · Inter-State distribution of the grant
  • Panchayat Revenue – Own, Assigned and Shared (Andhra Pradesh) — Property (house) tax · local tax · major own-source revenue · AP Panchayat Raj Act, 1994 · surcharge on the seigniorage fee · quarried in the village · seigniorage fees
  • Union Finance Commission and Local Bodies – Article 280(3)(bb) and (c) — Art. 280(3)(bb) · (c) · 1992 · does have a role · false · Local government · State subject
  • Vertical and Horizontal Devolution – Finance Commission Criteria — 41% is the States' share · only three · Income distance · highest weight · 16th FC changes · average of the three largest States · population growth 1971-2011
  • Audit of Local Bodies — Art. 243J / 243Z · maintenance and audit of accounts · AP State Audit Act, 1989 · Local Fund Audit Department · State Audit Department · Director of State Audit · primary (statutory) auditor
  • Decentralisation Terms and Early Committees on Local Finance — reservation · SEC (243K) · SFC (243I) · 29 Eleventh Schedule subjects are not
  • Scheme Grants and Municipal Finance — RBI Report on Municipal Finances · 0.6% of GDP · GIS mapping · AP municipal bonds · GVMC · ₹80 crore · 10%
