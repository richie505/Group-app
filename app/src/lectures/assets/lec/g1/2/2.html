RecapSources of the Indian Constitution – Borrowed Features and Their Origin Countries

Recap

  • Ireland and South Africa – Directive Principles, President's Election, Rajya Sabha, Amendment — Irish Constitution · Spain · Method of election of the President · Trap · not · "novel feature" · Election of members of the Rajya Sabha
  • USA – Fundamental Rights, Judicial Review, Preamble and the Judiciary — Fundamental Rights = USA · Judicial review originated in the USA · neither · Britain · no judicial review · Preamble idea = USA · language
  • Britain – Parliamentary Features, Rule of Law, Single Citizenship — cabinet system · single citizenship · bicameralism · writs · Independence of judiciary · 1, 2, 3 · concept of constitutional government
  • Federal Features – Canada and Australia — Federation with a strong Centre · Canadian · "Union of States" (Art. 1) · not the result of an agreement · no right to secede · declared will of the people · Residuary powers with the Centre
  • Government of India Act 1935 – the Largest Source, and the Length of the Constitution — Single largest source · office of Governor · judiciary · Public Service Commissions · emergency provisions · Greatest / most profound influence · GoI Act 1935
  • Emergency, USSR, France, Japan — Emergency · not · except Arts. 20 and 21 · Art. 19 · Trap · Justice · Election Commission (Art. 324)
  • Country → Features Master List and Match Questions — A-3, B-5, C-1, D-2 · GoI Act 1935 · Germany · Australia · a-iii, b-iv, c-ii, d-i · USA, France, Ireland, South Africa
