Recap

  • Backward Classes Commissions – Kalelkar, Mandal, Rohini — First Backward Classes Commission · Kaka Kalelkar · Second Backward Classes Commission · B.P. Mandal · Bindheshwari Prasad Mandal · Janata · Morarji Desai
  • Constitutional Amendments on Reservation — 77th / 81st / 82nd · public employment · admissions · 93rd · 85th
  • Constitutional Basis – Articles 15, 16, 335, 338, 340 — "Backward Classes" is NOT defined · Art. 16(4) · mainly social · caste and social backwardness · Art. 338(10) · President · Central Government
  • Landmark Cases – Indra Sawhney to Janhit Abhiyan — Caste · upheld 27% · not above 50% · extraordinary cases · year by year · carry-forward · Creamy layer
  • Andhra Pradesh – BC Groups and Muslim Quota — 29% · Muslim quota · proposed/ordered Muslim reservation · 2007 Act – 4% · Supreme Court's interim order · Sukhdev case · 112 classes
  • Creamy Layer and EWS Reservation — Status-based exclusions · Class-I officers · both Class-II · not all OBC children · SC/ST creamy layer debate · Arts. 341 and 342 · Up to 10%
