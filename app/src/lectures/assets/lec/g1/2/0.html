Recap

  • Government of India Act, 1935 – Federation, Provincial Autonomy and the Constitution's Blueprint — Based on · Simon Commission · Round Table Conferences · White Paper (1933) · Size · 321 sections, 10 schedules · longest Act
  • Charter Acts 1793–1853 – From Trading Company to Administrator — Ended the trade monopoly · tea and China · Christian missionary activity · English education chain · 1813 · Orientalist–Anglicist controversy · Bentinck's English Education Act (1835)
  • Government of India Act, 1919 – Montagu-Chelmsford Reforms — Montagu's declaration, 20 August 1917 · responsible government · Montagu-Chelmsford Report 1918 · Edwin Montagu · Lord Chelmsford · Dyarchy in the provinces · reserved
  • Crown Rule – Government of India Act, 1858 — Revolt of 1857 · direct Crown rule from 1858 · "Act for the Good Government of India" · not · Governor-General also styled Viceroy · Lord Canning · Mountbatten
  • Regulating Act 1773, Pitt's India Act 1784 and the Act of 1786 — Act of 1767 · £400,000 a year · 1772 crisis · Lord North · Before 1773 · Governor of Bengal · First
  • Indian Councils Acts 1861 and 1892 – Ordinances, Portfolios, Indirect Election — Portfolio system · Ordinance power · six months · Indian High Courts Act 1861 · Calcutta, Bombay, Madras (1862) · Indirect election · Budget discussion
  • Indian Councils Act, 1909 – Morley-Minto Reforms — John Morley · Lord Minto · "Father of Communal Electorate" · Separate electorate for Muslims · communal representation · Elected representation · 1909
  • Allied Facts Asked with This Topic — Local self-government · Lord Ripon · 1882 · Fundamental rights demand · Constitution of India Bill, 1895 · Congress (Bombay special session, 1918) · Declaration of Rights
