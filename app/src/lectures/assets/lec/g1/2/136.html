Recap

  • Forest Rights Act, 2006 — redressal of the historical injustice · Parliament passed it in December 2006 · 15 Dec · 18 Dec 2006 · 29 Dec 2006 · 31 Dec 2007 · 1 Jan 2008
  • Scheduled Tribe Identification and Committees — Art. 366(25) · Art. 342(1) · President · consultation with the Governor · State/UT-wise · Art. 342(2) · only by Parliament by law
  • Tribal Sub-Plan, ITDA and Institutions — TSP · 1972 · Fifth Plan (1974) · DAPST · in proportion to the ST population · ITDP/ITDA · bottom-up
  • Education and SC/ST Empowerment – EMRS, Sports School, DACE — ST students · NESTS · Navodaya · >50% ST and ≥20,000 · 740 · Odisha · APTWR Sports School, Araku Valley
  • Tribal Schemes – PVTGs, PM-JANMAN, Dharti Aaba, Van Dhan — PVTGs: 75 · AP – 12
  • Tribal Problems, Approaches and Revolts — indebtedness, land alienation · common factor · Diku · Marang Buru · Panchsheel · A Philosophy for NEFA · own genius
  • PESA and AP Scheduled Area Laws — PESA 1996 · Fifth Schedule · 50% · Odisha · 2,038 · Polavaram · Koya and Kondh
