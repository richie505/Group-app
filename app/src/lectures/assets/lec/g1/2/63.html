Recap

  • Part XV and the Right to Vote – Articles 324-329, Adult Suffrage — Part XV · ECI – Art. 324 · Art. 171 · 25 January 1950 · IIIDEM · Mohinder Singh Gill v CEC (1978) · reservoir of power
  • General Elections – Dates, Firsts and Records — 18 general elections · Congress · 1984 (8th, the last) · Longest poll · Shyam Saran Negi · first voter · every general election
  • Reservation of Seats – Articles 330-334 and Anglo-Indians — ceased in 2020 · common electorate · Madhya Pradesh · Municipalities (Art. 243T) · one-third · women
  • Delimitation – Commission, Freeze and Amendments — Delimitation · number and boundaries · SC/ST reserved seats · census · one person, one vote, one value · Delimitation Commission · constituency
  • Electoral Systems and Types of Elections — direct election on adult suffrage · PR by single transferable vote · list system · cumulative vote · FPTP · more votes than any other · Karnataka 1994
  • Model Code of Conduct, Polls and Voter Facilitation — ECI guidelines · consensus of parties · not enacted by Parliament · Art. 324 · announcement of the election schedule · Harbans Singh Jalal v UoI, 1997 · warn / censure
  • EVM-VVPAT, NOTA and Indelible Ink — First used: Parur (Kerala), 1982 · 3,840 · 2,000 · N. Chandrababu Naidu v UoI (2019) · 5 polling stations per Assembly segment · ADR v ECI (2024) · 100% VVPAT
  • Candidates, RPA Acts, Machinery and Election Petitions — 25+ · Sec. 8 RPA 1951 · Parliament · 2+ years · 6 years · qualified for membership · Sec. 14 / 15
