Recap

  • State Lokayuktas – Firsts, Appointment, Reports and Jurisdiction — Trap · Maharashtra · Act · 21 States and 1 UT (Delhi) · Section 63 · Lokayukta · own State law
  • Lokpal Bill History and the 2013 Act – Dates — First Lokpal Bill: May 1968 · 9 May 1968 · passed by the Lok Sabha in 1969 · lapsed · 2005 and 2008 · Trap · 16 January 2014 = in force
  • Ombudsman Idea and Recommending Bodies — Ombudsman · Sweden in 1809 · New Zealand 1962 · Indian versions: Lokpal · Lokayukta · Administrative Courts · Procurator
  • Andhra Pradesh Lokayukta — 2024 · Lokayukta · Upa-Lokayukta · Chief Minister is outside its purview · other than the Chief Minister · Ministers (other than the CM), MLAs · Time bar (Section 8(2))
  • Central Vigilance Commission and Whistle Blowers Act — CVCs · Praveen Kumar Srivastava · Whistle Blowers Protection Act, 2014 · 9 May 2014 · public interest disclosure · competent authority · Trap
  • Lokpal – Composition, Selection, Jurisdiction and People — Chairperson + not more than 8 members · 50% judicial · SC, ST, OBC, minorities and women · CJI · 25 years' · 5 years or age 70 · no reappointment
  • Civil Society Movement (2011) — India Against Corruption (IAC) · Anna Hazare · Jantar Mantar (April 2011) · Ramlila Maidan (August 2011) · Jan Lokpal Bill · Joint Drafting Committee (April 2011) · Pranab Mukherjee
