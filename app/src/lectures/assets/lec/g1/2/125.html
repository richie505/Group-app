RecapHuman Rights in the Indian Constitution; Protection of Human Rights Act 1993

Recap

  • Fundamental Rights as Human Rights – Key Articles — Right to Equality · Freedom of expression · Fundamental Right · Right to Information · right to housing, work, to die · Others · Art. 13
  • Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993 – Key Sections — Ordinance · Art. 123 · 28 Sept 1993 · deemed in force from 28 Sept 1993 · Jan 1994 · NHRC constituted 12 Oct 1993 · SHRC
  • Article 21 Expanded by the Courts — A.K. Gopalan (1950) · executive · Maneka Gandhi (1978) · fair, just and reasonable · living with human dignity · citizens and aliens · health
  • UDHR in the Preamble, DPSPs and Duties; Treaties in Indian Law — UDHR is reflected in the Preamble · DPSPs · Fundamental Duties · all three · 42nd Amendment, 1976 · in consonance with the UDHR · Vishaka (1997)
  • Related Acts and Laws — "Draconian laws" · preventive detention
