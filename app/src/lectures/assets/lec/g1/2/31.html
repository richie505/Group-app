Recap

  • Speakers of the Lok Sabha – Firsts and Office Holders — Never Speaker · K.V.K. Sundaram · Vithalbhai Patel · Central Legislative Assembly · First Indian women · M. Fathima Beevi · Rajkumari Amrit Kaur
  • Powers of the Speaker – Money Bill, Casting Vote, Joint Sitting — President · Rajya Sabha · Finance Minister · Supreme Court · Casting vote · RS Chairman · only in a tie
  • Speaker of the Lok Sabha – Election, Oath, Resignation, Removal (Articles 93-97) — Article 93 · chooses (elects) · its own members · all members of the Lok Sabha · Arts. 93-96 · 100 · 110
  • Sessions, Adjournment, Prorogation, Dissolution; President's Address — Art. 85(1) · President summons · six months shall not intervene · last sitting of one session · first sitting of the next · at least twice a year · Three sessions
  • Chairman and Deputy Chairman of the Rajya Sabha — Vice-President · ex officio Chairman · Arts. 64, 89(1) · not a member · Rajya Sabha · non-member · casting vote only in a tie
  • Parliament Secretariat – Article 98 — Art. 98(1) · each House · separate secretarial staff · posts common to both Houses · Art. 98(2) · Parliament · Art. 98(3)
  • Deputy Speaker and Pro-Tem Speaker — Deputy Speaker · Secretary-General · from the Opposition · convention · plurality · Art. 95 · vacant
