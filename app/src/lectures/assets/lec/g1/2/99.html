Recap

  • IRDAI – Insurance Regulator and Insurance Reforms — IRDAI · IRDA Act, 1999 · April 2000 · insurance and reinsurance · Hyderabad · Malhotra Committee · 1993
  • SEBI and Market Regulation — SEBI · 12 April 1988 · statutory on 12 April 1992 · Controller of Capital Issues · mutual funds · Quasi-judicial · Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT)
  • IBBI, IBC, PFRDA and the Companies Act 2013 — Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 · 28 May 2016 · IBBI · 1 Oct 2016 · IBBI Board (Sec. 189) · 3 ex officio · 1 RBI nominee
  • Regulators at a Glance – Act, Year, HQ, Ministry — Fiscal policy · Finance Ministry · monetary policy · RBI · Ministry of Finance · sandboxes · IBBI
  • RBI, the MPC and Payment Codes — monetary authority · banks and NBFCs · forex manager · FEMA 1999 · except the ₹1 note and coins · Ministry of Finance · Public Debt Management Cell
  • FSDC and FSLRC — FSDC · 2010 · non-statutory · not an organ of NITI Aayog · Union Finance Minister · RBI Governor · Raghuram Rajan Committee (2008)
  • Who Regulates What; Investor and Depositor Protection — NHB · 1988 · DICGC (1978) · RBI subsidiary · IEPF Authority · Corporate Affairs · NFRA
