RecapFlagship Social Sector Programmes and Social Security – Health, Education, Nutrition, Housing, Pensions, Pds

Recap

  • Education Programmes – School, Literacy and Higher Education — PM SHRI trap · not · Open Educational Resources (OER) · openly licensed · MOOC · eGyanKosh · Gyandarshan / Gyanvani
  • Housing – PMAY-Gramin and PMAY-Urban — Indira Awaas Yojana · 1985 · 1996 · address the gaps · 1 crore houses in 3 years · 2 crore more · 2028-29
  • Pensions, Insurance and Social Assistance — OROP = One Rank One Pension · NPS · 1 January 2004 · armed forces excluded · State employees · PFRDA · Tier I mandatory, Tier II voluntary
  • Health – Immunisation, Ayushman Bharat and Digital Health — NHM · NRHM + NUHM · ASHAs and ANMs · Right to Health · Rajasthan · Right to Health Bill on 21 March 2023 · ₹5 lakh per family per year
  • Labour Social Security – ESI, EPF, Code and e-Shram — EPF Scheme, 2026 · Aadhaar + PAN + UAN · 9 laws · First legal recognition · gig and platform workers · 1-2% of turnover · 5% of payouts
  • Nutrition, Sanitation and Drinking Water — POSHAN Abhiyaan · 8 March 2018, Jhunjhunu (Rajasthan) · Ministry of Women and Child Development · ICDS, NHM and Swachh Bharat · Mission Poshan 2.0 · Poshan Maah = September · Poshan Pakhwada = March
  • Welfare Campaigns and Social Security Concepts — SEED · MoSJE · not entertainment · ILO Convention No. 102 · nine branches · India has not ratified it
  • Food Security and PDS — Antyodaya Anna Yojana · 35 kg per household · Annapurna · 10 kg free grain · eligible for IGNOAPS but not drawing it · PMGKAY · 5 years from 1 January 2024
