Recap

  • Appointment, Composition and Term – UPSC, SPSC, JSPSC — not fix · at least half · 10 years · Acting Chairman · President · Conditions of service · 41st Amendment (1976)
  • Constitutional Status – Part XIV, Articles 315-323 — independent constitutional body · central recruiting agency · UPSC has nothing to do with the SPSCs · constitutional status · Part XIV, Articles 315-323 · Sources of UPSC functions · Parliamentary laws
  • Removal and Suspension – Article 317 — insolvent · Paid employment · Infirmity of mind or body · President's opinion · Misbehaviour · Supreme Court · advice is binding
  • Joint State PSC and UPSC Serving a State — JSPSC (Art. 315(2)) · agree · Legislatures pass resolutions · Parliament creates it by law · statutory · Punjab and Haryana · President
  • Annual Report and Nature of Advice – Article 323 — Non-acceptance · Appointments Committee of the Cabinet · advisory, not binding · directory, not mandatory · not invalid · selection confers no right to the post · Art. 321
  • Origin, Chairpersons and Current Linkage — Dholpur House, New Delhi · Roze Millian Bethew · MPPSC · 1 Nov 1956
  • Functions and Limitations – Article 320 — examinations · All-India, Central and Group-A services · Methods of recruitment · recruitment rules · Disciplinary matters · legal expenses · pension
  • Andhra Pradesh Public Service Commission — APPSC · 1 November 1956 · Andhra PSC (1953) · Hyderabad PSC · AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 (s.83) · PSC of AP · Telangana
