  • Eastward Expansion – Kuru-Panchala, Videgha Madhava, Agastya — Course · Punjab → Ganga-Yamuna Doab · Kosala · Kashi · Videha · Kurus · Bharatas and Purus
  • Pottery Sequence and Painted Grey Ware — OCP · ochre colour on the fingers · Red Ware · Black-slipped Ware · Black-and-Red Ware · Painted Grey Ware · NBPW is NOT

  • Agriculture, Settlements and Crafts — Agriculture · 6, 8, 12 and even 24 oxen · Settlements · villages · burnt bricks not common · true towns · NBPW phase
  • Later Vedic Religion – Gods and Priests — sacrifices · ritualism
