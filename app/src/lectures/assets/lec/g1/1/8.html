Recap

  • Metals and Raw Materials → Source — Copper = the first metal used by man · copper, bronze, gold, silver, lead · iron NOT found · Bronze = copper + tin · Dancing Girl · pioneered silver
  • Earliest Agriculture – Mehrgarh and the First Cereal —
  • Agriculture, Crops and Cotton — Main occupation = agriculture · sesame · mustard · Cotton · first to grow cotton · Sindon · Maize
  • Animals – Known and Unknown — not known · Horse · Lion
  • Crafts, Industries and Standardisation — Chanhudaro · Spindle-whorls · no spinning wheel · Boat models · Kenoyer · standardisation · state control
  • External Trade – Meluhha and Mesopotamia — Closest external contact = Mesopotamia · Susa, Ur, Nippur · Sargon of Akkad · Exports · Imports · Nov 2025 · Dilmun temple
  • Internal Trade and Transport — barter · bullock carts · boats · Harappa · urbanisation
