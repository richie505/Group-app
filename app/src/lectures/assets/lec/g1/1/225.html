Recap

  • Simon Commission – Appointment, Boycott and Visits — Boycott decision · Dec 1927, M.A. Ansari · Jinnah's · not · Justice Party · accentuated discontent · "Simon Go Back"
  • Round Table Conferences (1930-32) – Dates, Aim and Outcome — London · constitutional reforms · discuss the Simon Commission report · dominion status · one conference in three sessions · Gandhi-Irwin Pact, 5 March 1931 · Second RTC
  • Nehru Report (1928) — Dominion status · Joint electorates with reserved seats · no separate electorates · Linguistic provinces · 19 Fundamental Rights · bicameral · Supreme Court
  • Who Attended Which Round Table Conference —
  • Protests – Lala Lajpat Rai and the Andhra Boycott — Lahore, 30 October 1928 · Lala Lajpat Rai · lathi charge · 17 Nov 1928 · both true, R explains A · Saunders · Lucknow
  • Simon Commission Report (1930) — May 1930 · Abolition of dyarchy · responsible (representative) government · autonomy in the provinces · Separate communal electorates retained · "Scheduled Castes" · Sindh
  • Butler Committee and Deepavali Declaration —
  • Jinnah's Fourteen Points (1929) — M.A. Jinnah · Nehru Report · Fourteen · Delhi Proposals (March 1927)
