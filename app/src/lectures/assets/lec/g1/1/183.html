RecapRevolt of 1857 – Outbreak, Spread, Centres and Leaders

Recap

  • Centres and Leaders of the Revolt — Kunwar Singh · Jagdishpur (Shahabad, Bihar) · Not 1857 leaders · Bhagat Singh · Uddham Singh · Amir Chand · Kashmir's Gulab Singh
  • Tantia Tope, Azimullah, Ahmadullah and Other Associates — Lakshmibai · Kashi (Varanasi) · Sir Hugh Rose · Gwalior · 17 June 1858 · samadhi at Gwalior
  • Outbreak – Barrackpore, Meerut and Delhi — First martyr · Mangal Pandey · Commanding officer at Barrackpore · Lt Gen Sir John Bennet Hearsey · straight to Delhi · march to Delhi's Red Fort · Governor-General
  • Suppression and Fate of the Leaders — Sir Henry Lawrence · Havelock · Neill · Nicholson · Delhi · Tantia Tope
  • Affected, Unaffected and Loyal Areas — Main zone · North and Central India · Largely unaffected · Chittor · Jaipur · Bihar · Munger
  • Bahadur Shah Zafar – Symbolic Leader — Last Mughal emperor · Shahenshah-e-Hindustan · vacillated but agreed · Hindu-Muslim unity · Badshahi · Rs 1 lakh per month · emperor without an empire
  • Andhra and Hyderabad in 1857 — Kadiri
  • Significance and Consequences — weaknesses · coordination and central leadership · Government of India Act 1858 · Crown · Secretary of State for India · Viceroy
