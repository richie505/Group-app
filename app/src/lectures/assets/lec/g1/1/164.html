Recap

  • Treaty of Allahabad (August 1765) and the Diwani — Clive · May 1765 · second · August 1765 · Grantor of the Diwani · Mughal emperor Shah Alam II · legal foothold
  • Battle of Buxar (22 October 1764) – the Most Decisive Battle — Rohillas and Marathas · Buxar = the most decisive battle · open military victory · confirmed Plassey · Diwani of Bengal, Bihar and Orissa · Pairs · Order
  • Mir Qasim (1760-63) — Son-in-law of Mir Jafar · Vansittart (1760) · Faujdar of Purnia and Rangpur · Treaty of 1760 · Burdwan, Midnapur and Chittagong · Army on European lines · Gurgin Khan
  • Mir Jafar, Najm-ud-daula and the Plassey Settlement — Rs 1.77 crore · zamindari of the 24 Parganas · Dutch · Chinsura/Bidara (1759) · Removed 1760 · Najm-ud-daula · Muhammad Reza Khan
  • Key Terms – Farman, Dastak, Diwani, Nizamat — Dual Government (1765-72) · Diwani · Reza Khan · Raja Shitab Rai · Bengal famine of 1770 · John Cartier
