  • Literary Sources for Andhra — Gathasaptasati · Andhakaratta · Buddhist · Chilukuri Veerabhadra Rao
  • Coins and Trade Evidence — Ship coins · Yajnasri Satakarni · maritime (naval) trade · Vasishthiputra Pulumavi · lead, potin, copper, bronze · Roman coins · Satavahana
  • Copper-plate Grants and Land Grants — Donor · donee · village/land · tax exemptions · Satavahanas · Vishnukundin · nivartanas
  • Archaeological Sites and Foreign Accounts on Andhra — Puranas · Prakrit inscriptions · coins
