Recap

  • Formation of Andhra Pradesh, 1 November 1956 – First Holders — States Reorganisation Act, 1956 · linguistic basis · Andhra State · Kurnool · Tanguturi Prakasam · prohibition · Bezawada Gopala Reddy
  • Gentlemen's Agreement, 20 February 1956 — Signed 20 February 1956 · Hyderabad House, New Delhi · Sribagh Pact (1937) · Not signatories · Tanguturi Prakasam · Komarraju Lakshmana Rao · Cabinet ratio = 60:40
  • Visalandhra Movement and the Visalandhra Mahasabha — Visalandhra · Greater Andhra · one state for all Telugu people · 1950 · Nyapati Subbarao · Madapati Hanumantha Rao · 1946
  • SRC, Merger Debate and the Leaders' Stands — SRC · 30 September 1955 · 8 July 1954 · acknowledged Telangana's special claims · para 386 · 1961 · two-thirds
  • Hyderabad State Background – Mahasabha, Congress, Burgula — Chanda Railway Scheme agitation (1883) · Andhra Jana Sangham · Nizam Andhra Mahasabha · Jogipet, 1930 · Suravaram Pratapa Reddy · Hyderabad State Congress · Swami Ramananda Tirtha
