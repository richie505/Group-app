  • Mayo, Lawrence and Dufferin – Assassination, Census, Shimla, Masterly Inactivity, INC — Only Viceroy assassinated in office · Lord Mayo · Sher Ali Afridi · Andamans · 1872 · First census · 1881 (Ripon)

  • Viceroys 1916–1947 – Rowlatt Act, Irwin, Order of Tenure — Rowlatt Act · Chelmsford · Match · Order · Hardinge II (1910-16) · Chelmsford (1916-21) · Full order
