Recap

  • P. Anandacharlu and Andhra at the Early Congress — 7th session, Nagpur, 1891 · first INC session (1885) · Trap · Bombay 1885 (72 delegates) · P. Anandacharlu · P. Rangaiah Naidu · Nyapathi Subbarao (Rajahmundry)
  • Associations – Madras Sabhas and the Krishna District Association — Formed in 1892 · Organiser: Ramaswamy Gupta · second: Machilipatnam, 1893 · Key fixed · Guntur · 1894 · Eluru
  • Early Telugu Press, 1835-1905 — Role · criticised administrative failures · nationalism and democracy · Satyadoota · Andhra Prakasika
  • Andhra Moderate Leaders and Local Politics to 1905 — repeated the INC's economic critique · After 1908 · Konda Venkatappayya and B.N. Sarma · 1852 · 1884 · 1885 · 1891
