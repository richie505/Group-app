RecapMauryan Economy, Society, Urban Life and Art – Pillars, Sarnath Capital, Barabar Caves, Didarganj Yakshi

Recap

  • Stupas – Sanchi, Bharhut and the Stupa Cult — Stupa cult · Ashoka popularised it · 84,000 · near trade routes · not in rural areas · Sanchi Stupa was built by Ashoka · Chronological order (UPPCS Mains 2014)
  • Society – Megasthenes' Seven Classes, Slavery and Women — Megasthenes · Seleucus · Indica · seven classes · Slavery · no slaves · nine
  • Popular Art – Yakshas, Dhauli Elephant, Didarganj Yakshi — Yakshas · Jainism, Buddhism and Hinduism · All four Mauryan (APPSC G1) · Kanaganahalli · Satavahana-era
  • Economy – Coins, Trade and Ports — Coinage metals: silver and copper · no portraits · Tamralipti · main port of the Ganga basin · Land grants · not · State control
  • Pillars, Capitals and Court Art — Ashokan pillars · monolithic · highly polished · tapering · Chunar sandstone · no base · not parts of architectural structures
  • Barabar and Nagarjuni Caves — Barabar caves · granite · Ashoka · Dasaratha · Ajivikas · Oldest surviving rock-cut caves · not Badami
  • Pataliputra – Palace and City — wooden wall · D.B. Spooner · Susa and Ecbatana · Fa-Hien
  • Religious Conditions under the Mauryas — Policy · News (2026) · Balirajgarh · Vaishya Tekri
