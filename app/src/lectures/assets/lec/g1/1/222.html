Recap

  • Andhra Jana Sangham and the Nizam's Andhra Mahasabha — Andhra Jana Sangham · "Andhra Pitamaha" · Andhra Mahasabha · Madapati · political awareness · opposed the Nizam's autocracy · Suravaram Pratap Reddy

  • Leaders, the 1964 Split, Journals and Culture — 1964 · CPI(M) · Sundarayya, Basavapunnaiah · Praja Natya Mandali (1943) · IPTA · Burrakatha · Kothapatnam school (1937)
  • Peasant Struggles and the Armed Struggles — village soviets · Jalantra Kota · People's War Group (1980) · Kondapalli Seetharamaiah · Komaram Bheem · Gonds
  • Visalandhra and Electoral Politics — won 41 of 145 · 1952 · first to demand Visalandhra (1946) · supported · 1945-46 elections · 15 Nov 1954 · 15 of 196
