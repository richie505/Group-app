  • Humayun – Battles, Chronology and Exile — Accession · 29 December 1530 · Rival brothers · Kamran, Askari, Hindal · Usman · Rao Jaitsi of Bikaner · Kamran Mirza

  • Sher Shah – Rise, Titles, Rajput Wars and Death — Farid (Farid Khan) · Hasan Sur · jagirdar of Sasaram · studied Arabic and Persian at Jaunpur · Siraj-i-Hind · 1511 · Bahar Khan
  • Sur Administration and Land Revenue — 47 Sarkars · Parganas · frequently transferred · Army · Dagh · chehra / huliya · Police and justice
  • Sur Successors, Hemu and the End of Afghan Rule — Panipat II – 5 November 1556 · eye by an arrow · Surs = last Afghan dynasty
