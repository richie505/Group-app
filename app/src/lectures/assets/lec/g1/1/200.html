Recap

  • G.K. Gokhale and the Servants of India Society — Born · Ratnagiri · 1884 · Fergusson College · Career (UPSC 1997 biography) · Sudharak · Secretary of the Poona Sarvajanik Sabha
  • Dadabhai Naoroji – the Grand Old Man of India — Title · Grand Old Man of India · first economic thinker · Professor · December 1855 · first Indian to become a full professor · Elphinstone College
  • Economic Critique of Colonialism – Drain of Wealth — Greatest contribution of the Moderates · broke the myth of British benevolence · Drain theory · Dadabhai Naoroji · Calcutta, 1896 · Channels of the drain · Home Charges
  • Moderate Phase – Leaders, Methods and Demands — Moderate (liberal) phase = 1885-1905 · liberalism · urban · Bombay Trimurti · Founders of the INC · Naoroji and Dinshaw Wacha · Constitutional agitation
  • Achievements and the Official Attitude — Pan-Indian national identity · economic critique · democratic politics · not complete independence · strongly criticised the economic side · Lansdowne · More non-official members
  • Bodies in Britain – British Committee and Parliamentary Committee — Wedderburn · Bombay 1889 · 1893
