  • Music – Sitar, Tabla, Qawwali — Sitar · Amir Khusrau · veena · tambura · Hindu-Muslim harmony · Tabla · Origin
  • Amir Khusrau's Works — Uttarakhand 2025 · all four
  • Ibn Battuta and Other Travellers — Ibn Battuta · Moroccan · Tangiers · 1333 · Qazi of Delhi · Muhammad bin Tughlaq · envoy to China
  • Languages and Translations — Persian · official language · Hindavi → Urdu · Dakhni · Apabhramsa · early forms of modern Indian languages · Trap
  • Nizamuddin Auliya and Sufi Literature — Nizamuddin Auliya · never visited the court · Alauddin Khalji · two doors · Jalaluddin · Ajodhan · *Fawaid-ul-Fuad
  • Telugu and Dakhni Under the Sultans (AP Angle) — First Telugu farmans · Abdullah Qutb Shah · Abul Hasan Tana Shah · Addanki Gangadhara · Ibrahim Qutb Shah
