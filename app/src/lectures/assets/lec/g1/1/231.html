RecapGandhi on Caste, Untouchability, Religion, Women and Education (nai Talim)

Recap

  • Harijan Sevak Sangh and the Harijan Campaign (1932-34) — wells, schools, temples · inter-dining, inter-caste marriage · Harijan · 11 February 1933, Poona · Harijan tour: November 1933 – July 1934 · 20,000 km · Wardha
  • Nai Talim – Basic Education (Wardha Scheme, 1937) — Basic education · Mahatma Gandhi · craft-centred · self-supporting · Zakir Hussain Committee · Haripura (Feb 1938) · free and compulsory
  • Women and Religion — purdah, child marriage, dowry · supported remarriage of child widows · picketing liquor shops · Sarva dharma samabhava · ashram prayers · Hindu-Muslim unity · Khilafat
  • Temple Entry Satyagrahas — Calcutta and Noakhali
  • Gandhi in Andhra — Khadi tour (April 1929) · Bezawada · Saraswata Niketanam · Vetapalem · Harijan tour (December 1933) · Mogalrajpuram, Vijayawada (16 Dec 1933) · Desasevikalu
