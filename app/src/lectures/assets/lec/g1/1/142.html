RecapBhakti Movement – Origins, Alvars and Nayanars, Nirguna and Saguna Streams, Social Base

Recap

  • Alvars and Nayanars – the Tamil Beginning — 6th-9th c · Pasurams (pasuras) · in praise of Vishnu · Nayanars = Shaivites · all castes · Periya Puranam · Sekkizhar
  • Regional Bhakti Poets – Shankaradeva, Jayadeva, Raskhan and Others — Raskhan – Rasikpriya is a wrong pair · Keshavdas's · Prem Vatika
  • Origins, Roots and Common Features — Bhakti · "bhaj" · Roots · Chapter 12 = Bhakti Yoga · Bhagavata Purana · Navadha (ninefold, 9) bhakti · Spread
  • Women Saints, Guru Lines and the Social Base — Daya Bai = disciple of Saint Charandas · Buddha and Mirabai – common view · the world is full of sorrow · Ramananda · Hindus and Muslims · Palnati Brahmanayudu · Chapakudu
  • Nirguna and Saguna Streams — Advaita · Dadu Panth · Dadu Dayal · Raskhan · Krishna
  • Virashaiva Movement and the Vachanas — Basavanna (Basaveshwara) · Bagewadi · Virashaiva / Lingayat · Karnataka · Kalyana · Bijjala · Anubhava Mantapa
  • Nathpanthis, Siddhas and Yogis — Northern India · Matsyendranath · Gorakhnath · hatha yoga · renunciation · Charyapada · Siddhacharyas
