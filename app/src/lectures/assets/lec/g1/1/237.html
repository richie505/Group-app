RecapCivil Disobedience Movement – Lahore Session and Purna Swaraj, Dandi March, Spread and Phases

Recap

  • Dandi March and the Salt Satyagraha, 1930 — 31 Jan 1930 · eleven demands · Irwin · 2 March 1930 · Feb 1930 · daily necessity of rich and poor alike · state monopoly
  • Lahore Session, 1929 – Purna Swaraj — one-year ultimatum · 31 December 1929 · complete independence · Delhi Manifesto · Purna Swaraj (complete independence) · Nehru Report / Dominion Status · Round Table Conference to be boycotted
  • Spread of the Movement – Regions and Leaders — non-payment of revenue · no-chaukidari tax · forest-law violation · Charan Paduka massacre
  • Khudai Khidmatgars, Peshawar and the Garhwal Rifles — Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan · Frontier Gandhi · Pathans of the NWFP · non-violence · 23 April 1930 · Peshawar · Peshawar incident
  • Women, Martyrs and Notable Participants — Salt Satyagraha · Kamaladevi Chattopadhyay · Babu Genu · Bombay, 12 Dec 1930 · Khudiram Bose, Prafulla Chaki · Chapekars · Vinoba Bhave
  • Chronology and Phases of the CDM — Phases · 1930 – March 1931 · 1932-34 · repression · Significance · break laws openly on a mass scale · NWFP
