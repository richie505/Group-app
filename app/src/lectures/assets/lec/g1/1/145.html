Recap

  • Telugu Bhakti Composers – Bhakta Ramadasu, Tyagaraja, Kshetrayya and Others — Kancherla Gopanna = Bhakta Ramadasu · Bhadrachalam · Bhadrachalam Rama temple · 12 years in Golconda fort · Abul Hasan Tana Shah · Dasarathi Satakam · Dasarathi Satakam = Gopanna
  • Tallapaka Annamacharya and the Tallapaka Family — Tallapaka · Cuddapah (Kadapa) district · Annamayya district · Saluva Narasimha Raya · Purandara Dasa · 32,000 sankirtanas · Lord Venkateswara of Tirumala
  • Bhakti Shrines of Andhra – Kotappakonda, Srisailam, Pancharamas, Tirumala — Kotappakonda = Shiva temple · Not in AP after reorganisation · Akka Mahadevi caves · Nagarkurnool district, Telangana
  • Virashaivism and Saiva Bhakti in Andhra — Basavanna (Basaveshwara) · Brahmana · Kalyana · Akka Mahadevi · Shiva · Ishtalinga · Shakti Vishishtadvaita
  • Bammera Potana — Bammera · Vontimitta (Ontimitta), Kadapa · Sahaja Panditudu · Srinatha · Veerabhadra Vijayam = Potana · Refused to dedicate · Sarvajna Singabhupala of Rachakonda
  • Telugu Women Poets — Madhura Vijayam = Gangadevi · Amuktamalyada = Krishnadevaraya
  • Vemana, Veerabrahmam and the Social Message of Andhra Bhakti — Kalagnanam = Veerabrahmam · C.P. Brown · Verses of Vemana (1829) · Social impact · satakam and dwipada
