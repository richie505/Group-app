Recap

  • The Iqta System — Iqta · assigned · in lieu of salary · iqtadar / muqti · Origin · Arabic / Caliphate · Siyasatnama
  • Army – Cash Pay, Dagh and Huliya, Ranks — Alauddin Khalji · largest standing army · paid in cash · first Sultan to pay soldiers in cash · Mongols · Dagh · branding of horses
  • Provincial and Local Units — Amil · revenue collection · Muqaddam or Chaudhari · village headman · Khuts, muqaddams, chaudharis · Alauddin
  • Land Assignments, Post and Intelligence — Khalisa and Iqta · chauth = one-fourth · sardeshmukhi = one-tenth · silver coin · Post (Ibn Battuta) · ulaq · horse post
