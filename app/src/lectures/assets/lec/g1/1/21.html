Recap

  • Epics, Puranas and Smritis — Vyasa · Jaya / Jayasamhita · Shatasahasri Samhita · 18 books (parvas) · Harivamsa · Sanskrit · 18 Mahapuranas
  • Four Vedas – Content, Priests, Trayi — Samaveda · compiled from the Rigveda · Atharvaveda · kshetra-patni ("mistress of the field") · Prajapati · Paippalada, Shaunaka · Trayi / Vedatrayi
  • Upanishads and Vedanta — 108 · 13 principal · anti-ritualistic · Atman = Brahman · Vedanta · Prasthanatrayi · Upanishads, Brahma Sutra
  • Rigveda – Mandalas, Hymns, Gayatri — 10 mandalas, 1,028 hymns (suktas) · collection of hymns · Avesta
  • Mahabharata – Characters, Epithets and Episodes — Dushta chatushtaya · Duryodhana, Dussasana, Karna, Shakuni
  • Yajurveda Branches and the Brahmanas — Shatapatha Brahmana · Great Flood · first reference to money-lending · upanayana · Purushamedha · Aitareya Brahmana · Andhras
  • Bhagavata Purana, Sages and Puranic Stories —
  • Vedangas, Sutras and Grammarians — Gayatri chandas · 24 syllables · Baudhayana, Apastamba, Katyayana · Baudhayana theorem · Pythagoras theorem · Vaisheshika – Kanada
