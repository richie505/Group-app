RecapConsequences of 1857 – Queen's Proclamation, Government of India Act 1858, Army Reorganisation

Recap

  • Queen's Proclamation, 1 November 1858 – Allahabad, Objects, Promises — 1 and 2 only · 2 January 1859 · Kimberley · shall always be Europeans · 1858–1885 = "Era of Broken Promises" · Madras Native Association (1852) · Gazula Lakshmi Narasu Chetty
  • Lord Canning – Governor-General in 1857 and First Viceroy — First Viceroy of India = Canning · Allahabad · emergency headquarters · White Mutiny (1859-60) under Canning · European troops · batta · portfolio system (1859)
  • Government of India Act, 1858 – Transfer to the Crown — 2 August 1858 · Act for the Better Government of India · Year of transfer = 1858 · Secretary of State for India · Council of India of 15 members · de facto ruler · two kinds of territory: British India
  • Empress of India (1876-77) and the Delhi Durbars — Royal Titles Act, 1876 · Lytton · 1877 · Delhi Durbars · 1877, 1903, 1911
  • Army Reorganisation and Divide and Rule — Army Amalgamation Scheme 1861 · Eden Commission (1879) · Indian Army (1895) · four commands
  • Indian Councils Act, 1861 – Portfolio System — Portfolio system · Canning in 1859 · Indian Councils Act, 1861 · ordinances valid six months · 1862 · three Indians
