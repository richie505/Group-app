Recap

  • Congress Socialist Party, 1934 — dictatorship of the proletariat · separate electorates · Nehru never joined · Nehru and socialism · 1933 · Bombay Manifesto, 1936 · openly opposed Nehru's socialism
  • Early Labour Organisations and the First Trade Unions — Bombay workers' housing · chawls · Sasipada Banerjee · 1870 · N.M. Lokhande · Bombay Millhands' Association (1890) · first labour organisation
  • Communist Party of India – Founding, Line and Bans — M.N. Roy · Vanguard · Kanpur 1925 · Tashkent 1920 · CPI and RSS both 1925 · K.B. Hedgewar · Ahmedabad 1921
  • AITUC and the Later Central Trade Unions — Order · 1920 · INTUC 1947 · HMS 1948 · CITU 1970 · Hind Mazdoor Sabha · December 1948
  • Labour Laws and the Whitley Commission — not · Motilal Nehru · "the Slavery of India Bill" · 2026 · 100 years
  • Conspiracy Cases Against the Communists — M.A. Ansari
  • Workers' and Peasants' Parties and Left Journals — Kirti Kisan · Labour Swaraj · communist · After 1928 · bourgeois · R. Palme Dutt
