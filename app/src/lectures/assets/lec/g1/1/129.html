Recap

  • Religious Policy – Ibadat Khana, Mahzar, Din-i-Ilahi, Sulh-i-Kul — Chronology · built by Akbar (1575) · Sulh-i-Kul · peace with all · promulgated by Akbar · Rama and Sita · "Rama-Siya"
  • Accession, Bairam Khan's Regency and Conquests — 15 October 1542 · Amarkot · 14 February 1556 · Kalanaur (Gurdaspur, Punjab) · Bairam Khan · Age at coronation · fourteen
  • Court, Navratnas and Translations – Abul Fazl, Faizi, Tansen — Ata Ali Khan · Abul Faizi · Abul Fazl's death · Prince Salim · Bir Singh Deo Bundela · refers to the discovery of America · adviser and spokesperson
  • Rajput Policy, Mewar and Haldighati — conquest and conciliation · autonomy and honour · First marriage alliance – 1562 · Ajmer · Man Singh, Bhagwan Das · Contemporaries of Akbar · Jaswant Singh
  • Social and Educational Reforms — Sati · Akbar · Monogamy order · only one wife · barren · Marriage age fixed · consent of bride and groom
