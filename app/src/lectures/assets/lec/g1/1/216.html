RecapPeasant Movements – Indigo, Deccan Riots, Moplah, Bardoli, Tebhaga, Telangana Armed Struggle

Recap

  • Indigo Revolt, Nil Darpan, Pabna and the Deccan Riots — advances and fraudulent contracts · Digambar and Bishnu Biswas · Nadia · Supported by the Bengali intelligentsia · Indigo Commission (March 1860) · fulfil existing contracts · could refuse future ones
  • UP Kisan Sabha, Awadh and the Eka Movement — Hardoi, Bahraich, Sitapur · conversion of produce rent into cash · Eka vs Bardoli (UPSC 2026) · 30% hike in land revenue · high rents
  • Kisan Sabhas, Swami Sahajanand and the AIKS — Bakasht movement (Bihar) · return bakasht land · Karyanand Sharma · Barahiya Tal (Munger) · peasant (Kisan Sabha) movement in Bihar
  • Telangana Armed Struggle (1946-51) — Nizam's feudal order · vetti · CPI · Andhra Mahasabha · village soviets · called off October 1951 · Doddi Komaraiah
  • Moplah Revolt (1921) and Bardoli (1928) — Moplah (Mappila) · Muslim tenants of Malabar · Hindu landlords (jenmis) · Khilafat · Ali Musliyar · Variyankunnath Kunjahammad Haji · Wagon Tragedy, November 1921
  • Tebhaga Movement (1946-47) — Floud Commission's · two-thirds of the crop to the bargadar · from one-half to one-third · Bargadars / adhiars · jotedars · Bargadari Bill / Act (1950)
  • Peasant Movements – Chronology and Regions — After 1857 · not · Match (UPSC 1997) · Phases · Gandhian satyagraha · Kisan Sabhas · Permanent Settlement (1793)
  • N.G. Ranga and the Kisan Movement in Andhra —
