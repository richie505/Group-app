Recap

  • Gunaga Vijayaditya III (848–892) – Greatest King, Titles, Pandaranga, Kandukuru — Gunaga Vijayaditya = Vijayaditya III · greatest king · 43 years · Military · Rashtrakuta Krishna II · Kalinga king Devendravarma · copper-plate
  • Nannaya and Telugu – Nandampudi, Narayana Bhattu and the First Telugu Inscriptions — Nannaya Bhattu · court poet of Rajaraja Narendra · patronised · Andhra Mahabharatamu · Adi, Sabha · Aranya · earliest surviving Telugu literary work
  • Capitals and Cities – Rajamahendravaram, Vengi, Pithapuram, Bezawada — Rajamahendra · cultural capital of the Andhras · Dynasty · Eastern Chalukyas · Statement (VRO) · Ammaraja Vishnuvardhana I · Rajahmundry district
  • Founder, Dynasty Outline and the Chola Connection — Kubja Vishnuvardhana · brother of Pulakeshin II · Chalukyas of Vengi · Vishnukundins · viceroy 617 · independent 624 · 631
  • Temples, Religion and Jain Patronage — Vengi school · local sandstone (not granite) · Chalukya-Chola · Match (APPSC JA) · Draksharama · Pancharama kshetras · Chalukya-Chola architecture
  • Vijayaditya II (Narendra Mrigaraja) – the 108 Shiva Temples — 108 battles in 12 years · as penance · Sataluru · Bhima Salki
