Recap

  • Sharia Taxes and Cesses — Taxes on land produce · not khams · Alauddin and MBT took 4/5 · Firoz restored the sharia 1/5 · Firoz Tughlaq · kharaj, jizya, zakat, khams · haq-i-sharb
  • Coins of the Sultanate — Iltutmish · regular standard currency · Delhi the capital · iqtadari · two basic Sultanate coins · silver tanka and copper jital · rupiya and dam
  • Agriculture and Irrigation — Araghatta · water-wheel for irrigation · animal-driven · open wells · Panchatantra · Rajatarangini · Persian wheel (saqiya)
  • Land Revenue Demand — one-third · Alauddin Khalji raised it to one-half · MBT · Doab · false · Acharya Mahasena · NDA 2002
  • Towns, Crafts and Trade — Towns · 1,300 Sanskrit manuscripts · Nagarkot · Royal karkhanas · Crafts · leather, metal-work, carpet-weaving · paper
