Recap

  • Sri Krishnadevaraya Andhra Bhasha Nilayam and the Hyderabad Libraries — 1901 · Hyderabad · Komarraju (Venkata) Lakshmana Rao · Ravichettu Ranga Rao · first Telugu library · Kovvur 1916 · Komarraju Lakshmana Rao
  • Vignana Chandrika Mandali and Andhrula Charitramu — 1906 · Hyderabad · Komarraju (K.V.) Lakshmana Rao · Munagala Zamindar · Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao · Ayyadevara Kaleswara Rao · 1910
  • Early Libraries and Institutions – Place and Year — G2 Mains 2025 match · a-II, b-III, c-I, d-IV
  • Andhra Desa Library Association, Conferences, Laws and Innovations — By 1913 · 125 libraries · Ayyanki · Suri Venkata Narasimha · First Andhra library conference · Vijayawada, 10 April 1913 · 200 delegates
