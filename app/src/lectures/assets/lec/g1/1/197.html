Recap

  • Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar and Widow Remarriage — Widow remarriage · legalise widow remarriage · cited the ancient (Vedic) texts · Dalhousie · passed under Lord Canning · First lawful Hindu widow remarriage · Calcutta, 7 December 1856
  • Social-Reform Acts – Year, Governor-General, Provision — Order · Ages ladder · 12 (1891) · 18/21 · Prohibition of Child Marriage Act, 2006 · Thuggee · Colonel William Sleeman
  • Western India Women Reformers – Phule, Ramabai, Karve — Karve = widow remarriage
  • Age of Consent Act 1891 – Malabari, Tilak, Rakhmabai — Behramji M. Malabari · Parsi · Indian Spectator · Voice of India · Tilak opposed · Rakhmabai case (1884-88) · restitution of conjugal rights
  • Reform Institutions and Women's Organisations — Radhakant Deb · Dharma Sabha (1830)
  • Andhra – Veeresalingam and Women's Reform — Kandukuri Veeresalingam · Sitamma/Gauramma · Gogulapati Sriramulu · Girls' schools · Dowlaiswaram, 1874 · Innispeta, Rajahmundry, 1881 · 1897
