Recap

  • Price Terms, Economic Cost and Food Subsidy — two-thirds · FCI subsidy + subsidy to DCP States · NSSF · Budget 2021-22 · (c) · uniform CIPs · open-ended
  • Procurement, Buffer Stocks and Storage — Buffer stocks · check price rise · Buffer norms · CCEA · 1 April, 1 July, 1 October, 1 January · 210.40 LMT · Strategic (food security) reserve
  • PDS Evolution – RPDS, TPDS, AAY, NFSA, PMGKAY — PDS → RPDS → TPDS → AAY · One Nation-One Price-One Ration · Fortified rice · vitamin B12, folic acid · NFSA · rice (and wheat) at subsidised prices · 6.52 crore BPL families
  • PDS Reforms – ONORC, Leakage and the Shanta Kumar Committee — ONORC · away from where the card was issued · Mera Ration · 1 June 2020 · all 36 States/UTs · DBT in food · Puducherry
  • Andhra Pradesh PDS — 450 ration cards · one FPS per 1,376 persons · ePoS · GPS-tracked transport vehicles · FPS portability · not · 1 June 2025
  • Food Management and the FCI — Objectives of PDS · control of price fluctuations · free grain to the weak under PMGKAY · Not objectives · export of foodgrains · FCI missions · State APMC Acts
