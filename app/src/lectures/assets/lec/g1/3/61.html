RecapIndian Industry – Structure, Growth, Iip and Public Sector

Recap

  • Early Industry, Textiles and Major Industry Facts — cotton mills · Indian-owned · jute mills · foreign (British)-owned · Textile firms by founding year · largest textile centre = Mumbai · Textiles = largest agro-based industry
  • CPSE Profiles, Ownership and Performance — CPSE · 51%+ · Public Enterprises Survey · Estimates Committee's · over 50% GoI equity · Number of CPSEs · 244 by 1990
  • CPSE Categories – Maharatna, Navratna, Miniratna — turnover, net worth, net profit · autonomy to compete globally · Department of Public Enterprises (DPE) · 5,000 – 1,000 – 500 – 300 · Navratna (27, Apr 2026) · BEL · June 2007
  • Steel Plants and Their Collaborations — Third Plan · Vizag Steel · PM Indira Gandhi, 20 Jan 1971 · RINL · 17 Nov 2010 · Maharashtra Elektrosmelt · UPSC 2005 (not correct)
  • Index of Industrial Production and Core Industries — short-term changes in the volume · monthly · NSO (MoSPI) · mining, manufacturing, electricity · NOT included: construction · Base 2011-12 → 2022-23 · 1 June 2026
  • Growth Phases, Structure and Problems — Second Plan (1956-61) · basic and heavy industries · IPR 1956 · 1991 · Commanding heights · capital-heavy, long-gestation · Services, not industry
  • Andhra Pradesh Industrial Profile — 21.16% (2024-25) · 21.57% (2025-26) · 11% of GSDP · $20 bn → $40 bn · ₹5,001 cr · Visakhapatnam · State PSUs
  • Public Sector Administration and Reform – DPE, MoU, PSE Policy 2021 — Department of Public Enterprises (DPE) · four schedules · MoUs · Ratna status · Public Enterprises Survey · Ministry of Heavy Industries · PESB
