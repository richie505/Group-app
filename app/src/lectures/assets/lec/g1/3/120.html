RecapApproaches and Initiatives in Ap Agriculture – Apcnf/zbnf, Horticulture, Aquaculture, Allied Sectors

Recap

  • Farmer Schemes, Extension and Institutions — RBK = Rythu Bharosa Kendram · 30 May 2020 · 10,778 · Rythu Seva Kendras (RSKs) · 10,606 · 8,489 · not industrial licences
  • Land Use, Holdings and Crops — 60% of population · 33% of GVA · 162.97 lakh ha · 49.55 lakh ha = 30.40% · 59.14 lakh ha = 36.29% · Older land-use keys · Vizianagaram
  • Fisheries and Aquaculture — Coastline 1,053.07 km – 3rd in India · 974 km, "2nd longest" · 30% of India's fish · 31% of shrimp exports · Shrimp / cultured prawn · India's largest producer · 70%
  • Livestock and Dairy — 1st in 2018-19 · White Paper on Agriculture (Dec 2018) · livestock and horticulture · 9.41% · crossbred cattle · graded Murrah buffaloes · Older district keys
  • Natural Farming – ZBNF to APCNF — ZBNF · mid-1990s · Subhash Palekar · no purchased fertilisers or pesticides · 2016 · 2018 · RySS = Rythu Sadhikara Samstha
  • Horticulture and Oil Palm — AP = largest fruit producer in India · 1st · 2nd · 1st in productivity · mango, sweet orange, turmeric · Rayalaseema · 11 crops in 24 clusters
  • Research Institutes and Varieties — ICRISAT · Patancheru · Dr YSR Horticultural University · 2007 · Venkataramannagudem · none of the listed places · Coffee
