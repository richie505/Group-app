RecapPolavaram Project – Status, Funding, National Project Status, Submergence and R&r

Recap

  • National Project Status – APRA Section 90 — National Project · in consultation with AP and Telangana · NITI Aayog (2016) · the State Government (AP) executes · Centre funds · GoAP executes it on behalf of GoI · Section 90
  • Project Features — Polavaram-Nallamala Sagar link · Banakacherla link · APRA 2014 · 1980 GWDT award · 45 TMC to AP · 35 TMC · Karnataka 21, Maharashtra 14
  • Polavaram Ordinance and the Seven Mandals — Polavaram Ordinance · resettlement of those displaced · G.O.Ms. No. 111 (27 June 2005) · Lok Sabha on 11 July 2014 · Seven mandals · Bhadrachalam (except the temple town) · Pinapaka, Aswaraopeta and Bhadrachalam
  • Funding and Cost — revised cost estimate · August 2024 · ₹12,157.53 crore
  • Submergence, Inter-State Cases and R&R — Koya and Kondh tribals · RFCTLARR Act, 2013 · ₹1,943 crore · ₹1 lakh → ₹2 lakh
  • Construction Milestones and Status — 8 June 2017 · cofferdam · 1941 · "Ramapada Sagaram" · GWDT set up 1969 · 1980 award · commenced 2005
  • Polavaram Project Authority (PPA) — Union · AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 · funding · inter-State coordination
