Recap

  • Green Revolution – Meaning, Package and Nature — Green Revolution · wheat and rice · High Yielding Variety (HYV) seeds · increasing output through HYVs · Scientific core / focus: HYV seeds · Seed-Fertiliser-Water technology · assured irrigation
  • Second Green Revolution, Evergreen Revolution and BGREI — Second Green Revolution · PM Manmohan Singh · biotechnology · May 2006 programme · seven-point strategy · Dry zone · Evergreen Revolution
  • Yellow Revolution – Oilseeds — Yellow Revolution = oilseeds · 1986 · Technology Mission on Oilseeds · Sam Pitroda · rapeseed-mustard · National Mission on Edible Oils · NMEO-Oilseeds
  • Persons – Borlaug, Swaminathan, Subramaniam — Not associated · Sardar Swaran Singh · Rafi Ahmed Kidwai · Mexico · from the USA · wheat
  • White Revolution – Operation Flood — Operation Flood (1970) · NDDB · milk production · Anand (Amul) three-tier cooperative · started in Gujarat · GCMMF (1946) · NDDB set up 1965
  • Background and Timeline — PL-480 · 1965-66 and 1966-67 · started from Pantnagar · 18,000 t
  • Colour Revolutions and Rainbow Revolution — Prawns - Blue · Fish - Pink
  • Outcomes and Criticism — Gains · 72 MT (1965-66) → 357.73 MT (2024-25) · Inequality · more than 10 ha · Crop bias · pulses · restricted to small pockets
