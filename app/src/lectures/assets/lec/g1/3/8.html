RecapObjectives of Indian Planning; Types of Planning (imperative, Indicative, Perspective, Rolling)

Recap

  • Rolling Plan (1978-80) — current year · 3, 4 or 5 · revised every year · perspective plan · 10, 15 or 20 years · weakened commitment
  • Objectives of Planning — Four long-term objectives · all · Economic growth = principal objective · removal of poverty · To achieve social equality · self-reliance · standard of living
  • Perspective Planning and Plan Horizons — Planning Commission function · Long-term perspective plan 1985-2000 · Seventh Plan · AP now · Swarna Andhra Vision 2047 · Rs 308.11 lakh crore by 2047-48 · Padi Sutralu
  • Plans – Thrust, Models and Financing — Trickle-down · Wage-goods model · C.N. Vakil and P.R. Brahmananda · Plan financing · market borrowing · Eighth Plan (1992-97) · government borrowings
  • Imperative vs Indicative Planning — Five-Year Plan · indicative plan of action · Kolkhoz · Regional planning
  • Other Types of Planning — open process
