Recap

  • National Income – Meaning, NNP at Factor Cost and Formulas — National income (NI) · normal residents · final · Income view · sum of factor incomes · Source · productive activity
  • Nominal vs Real Income; Measuring Growth — Real GDP · prices of a single year · constant prices · Real national income · GNP at constant prices · Rs 8,000 crore · Prices and money income both doubled
  • Income Determination – Consumption, Saving, Investment, Multiplier — Match (JL) · A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4 · Mobilisation of domestic savings · propensity to save · tax policy · zero · Autonomous consumption
  • Personal, Disposable and Per Capita Income — PCI = average income · standard of living · regional imbalances · PCI falls while NI rises · population grows faster than NI · Statements (Sachivalayam) · I and III
  • GDP and GNP – Definitions and NFIA — GDP (Gross Domestic Product) · all final goods and services · within the domestic territory · GNP (Gross National Product) · normal residents · inside or outside · Gross National Income
  • Gross to Net – Depreciation, NDP and NNP — Depreciation · machinery · not new output · Incorrect combination (NDA 2015)
  • PPP and India's World Rank — PPP · prices of the same basket · International Comparison Program (ICP) · World Bank · NSO · prices are lower in India · Older facts
  • Factor Cost vs Market Price – Indirect Taxes and Subsidies — MP = FC + indirect taxes − subsidies · FC = MP − indirect taxes + subsidies · GDP at MP vs GDP at FC · indirect taxes and subsidies · not direct taxes · Subsidy · added
