Recap

  • Economists and Books on Wealth, Poverty and Inequality — market economy · Adam Smith, Hayek, Friedman
  • Lorenz Curve and Gini Coefficient — Lorenz curve · 45° line = perfect equality · greater the inequality · Gini coefficient · derived from the Lorenz curve · income inequality · higher Gini = more inequality
  • Measuring Inequality in India – Consumption, Income and Wealth — MPCE · richest 1% hold 58% · 65-70% of households · Sen's poverty index · income inequality · Bihar · Goa
  • Measures to Reduce Inequality — K.N. Raj Committee (1972) · Agricultural Holdings Tax · Wealth tax · 1 April 2016 · estate duty · 1985 · Art. 38(2)
  • Kuznets Curve and Environmental Kuznets Curve — (Environmental) Kuznets curve · economic growth
