Recap

  • Sectoral Shares in AP's GVA — Largest sector at reorganisation (2014) · services · 2011-12 to 2016-17 · 2015-16 at constant 2011-12 prices · 23.33% · NITI Aayog · more than one-third
  • GSDP Size, Growth Rate and AP's Rank — 2011-12 · 3 sectors, 17 sub-sectors · construction · Estimate cycle · India's new GDP series · 2022-23 · slower than India in 2023-24
  • Vision 2029, Swarna Andhra @2047 and Growth Strategy — Sunrise AP Vision 2029 · 2016 · growth target 12% · ₹9.61 lakh · HDI 0.9 by 2029 · saturation approach · UN Sustainable Development Goals
  • Sector-wise Growth Rates and Agriculture — SES 2016-17 · agriculture · short-run engine of double-digit growth · Q2 2025-26 · Industry 2024-25 · construction fastest (9.55%) · manufacturing ≈ half of industry GVA
  • Bifurcation's Impact and AP in India — 2 June 2014 · 13 districts · Hyderabad alone ≈ 48% · ₹70,548 crore (2012-13) · chronic revenue-deficit State · Amaravati · agriculture's share rose
  • Districts and Service Infrastructure — Annamayya district · YSR Kadapa and Chittoor · not comparable · 8,555 · CD ratio 157.4% · Palnadu · RMZ GCC Park
