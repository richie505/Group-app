Recap

  • Origin, Timeline and Ratification — France · Canadian dual · Amitabh Bachchan · Revenue Neutral Rate · Arvind Subramanian · 15-15.5% · 17-18%
  • GST – Nature and Objectives — GST (Goods and Services Tax) · indirect tax · every value addition · value-added consumption tax · not a gross value tax · Taxable event: supply · Destination principle
  • GST Council — Constitutional body · Article 279A · never statutory · Chairperson · Union Finance Minister · Members · MoS Revenue/Finance
  • Rates and Rate-change Procedure — four slabs 5, 12, 18, 28% · GST 2.0 · 22 Sep 2025 · 5% (merit) · 18% (standard) · 40% (sin / ultra-luxury) · statutory ceilings
  • Taxes Subsumed vs Not Subsumed — indirect · 8 Central + 9 State · Central as well as State · not · Not subsumed · Basic Customs Duty · stamp duty
  • Items Outside GST and Exemptions — not · "All of the above" · Exempt · GST 2.0
  • GST Mechanisms – ITC, Anti-profiteering, Composition — Anti-profiteering chain
  • GSTN, Saksham, E-way Bill and E-invoicing — GSTN · not-for-profit, non-government company · 51% · fully government-owned · 2018 · GSTIN · Project Saksham
