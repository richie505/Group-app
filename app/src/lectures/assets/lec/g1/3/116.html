Recap

  • Relocation – Capital, Secretariat and APRA Provisions — 2017 · AP's Secretariat buildings in Hyderabad · June 2019 · handed over to the Telangana Government
  • Industrial Base and GSDP Share — 5th · 60% agriculture-dependent · manufacturing about 10.5% of GSDP · 43% · 33% · 24% · 10.84%
  • What Residual AP Lost — 58% of population and debt · 46% of revenue
  • Section 94 Incentives — 94(1) · 94(2) · 7 backward districts · 35% additional depreciation · 15% investment allowance · 32AD
  • New Industrial Clusters — 4.0 (2024-29) · 87 proposals, Rs 34,000 crore
  • Commerce, Investment and Ease of Doing Business — commercial nerve centre · Asia Competitiveness Institute (2019) · 1st
  • Thirteenth Schedule Promises, Corridors, Ports and Unfinished Projects — Section 93, APRA 2014 · Thirteenth Schedule · within ten years · 13th Schedule – infrastructure for AP · SAIL · major port at Dugarajapatnam · IOC / HPCL-GAIL
