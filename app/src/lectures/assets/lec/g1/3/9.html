Recap

  • Plan → Model It Was Based On — Order 1-2-3-4-5 · Mahalanobis model launched in 1956 · architect of the Second Plan · Harrod-Domar · Solow (Solow-Swan) · Cobb-Douglas · no Indian plan was based on it
  • Pre-independence Plans – Author and Year — Wrong pairs asked · Rajaji · Dhirubhai Ambani · All correct (APPSC Group 1) · John Mathai · Shriman Narayan · Chronology
  • Mahalanobis Model — Heavy sector strategy · unbalanced growth · heavy capital goods industries · G.A. Feldman · 1928 · 1953 · Feldman-Mahalanobis
  • Harrod-Domar Model and Capital-Output Ratio — g = s / k · savings · capital-output ratio · rises with savings · falls with a higher COR · along with savings · one unit
  • P.C. Mahalanobis – ISI and Statistics Day — ISI Bill, 2026 · 2 Aug 2026 · Standing Committee on Finance
  • Other Growth Theories — Cobb-Douglas · α + β = 1 → constant · commodity balances
  • Gandhian Economics and the Janata Strategy — Trusteeship · Charan Singh · Gandhian Constitution for Free India · Shriman Narayan Agarwal · Ram Rajya · not a Hindu state · Janata alternative strategy (1977-80)
  • Wage-goods Model — C.N. Vakil and P.R. Brahmananda · Planning for an Expanding Economy · 1956 · wage goods · not capital goods · disguised unemployed · employment
