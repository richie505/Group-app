RecapIndian Agriculture – Importance, Land Use Classification, Cropping Seasons and Cropping Pattern

Recap

  • Cropping Seasons – Kharif, Rabi and Zaid — Crop seasons · third season is Zaid · Rabi · western disturbances · between rabi and kharif · vegetables and fruits · Sugarcane
  • Operational Holdings – Agriculture Census — Operational holding · one technical unit · regardless of title, size or location · five size classes · 2010-11 average holding · 1.15 ha · SCs 0.80 ha
  • Land Use – Terms, Nine-fold Classification and AP Data — GCA · Net irrigated area is NOT · Land use pattern · NSA · Forests · Lateritic soils · UP, Punjab, Haryana
  • Importance of Agriculture and Growth — raw material · food security · improved methods and technology · Rain-fed farming · frequency and adequacy of rainfall · 1991 · decline in public investment
  • Cropping Pattern – India and Andhra Pradesh — Factors of cropping pattern · government policy · Rice: largest cropped area · MSP procurement · ~20% of rice area to pulses · Soyabean · Madhya Pradesh
  • Crop Classification – Food, Cash and Mixed Farming — Cash crop · cotton · Jowar · soyabean · food crops · groundnut, cotton, tobacco, sugarcane · Commercialisation of agriculture
  • Irrigated Area — net irrigated area 77.9 Mha · gross irrigated area 120.3 Mha · rainfed · 52% of GCA · Canals in the north
  • Agro-climatic Zones — Agro-climatic zones · AP: six zones · 22 soil categories · 88%
