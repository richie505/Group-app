Recap

  • Make in India – Launch, Pillars and Sectors — Launched · 25 September 2014 · Aim · global manufacturing hub · foster innovation · 25 key sectors · Logo
  • National Manufacturing Policy 2011 and NIMZs — Objectives (RAS 2013) · 1, 2 and 3 · Present share · 16.3-17.5% · 25% target missed · 143 million jobs by 2035 · NIMZ
  • Ease of Doing Business – State Rankings (BRAP, ACI) and AP — BRAP = Business Reform Action Plan · DPIIT · 2015 · World Bank Group · AP's rank in State EoDB · 1st · DBRAP
  • Electronics and Semiconductors — after China · ₹5.45 lakh crore (2024-25) · 99.2% · India Semiconductor Mission · Digital India Corporation (MeitY) · ₹76,000 crore · up to 50%
  • Ease of Doing Business – World Bank Report and Reforms — Doing Business report · World Bank Group · IFC · guides foreign investors · 10 parameters · Discontinued 2021 · B-READY
  • Flagship Missions – Chronology — Atmanirbhar Bharat (May 2020) · ₹20 lakh crore · five pillars · not privatisation · "China+1" · Logistics cost · 13-14% of GDP
  • PLI and Sectoral Schemes — PLI (2020) · 14 sectors · ₹1.97 lakh crore · incremental sales · NOT coal mining equipment · 12.60 lakh jobs · PLI-Auto
  • SEZ Basics for this Row — SEZ Act, 2005 · 10 February 2006 · Central Govt, State Govt or any person · Sec 10AA · 100% · 50% · sunset
