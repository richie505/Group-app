RecapInfrastructure Initiatives – Power Sector Position, Transport (ports, Airports, Highways, Railways)

Recap

  • Power Reforms, DISCOMs and Energy Efficiency — AP Electricity Reform Act 1998 · 2000 unbundling · Hyderabad · After 2014 (APRA) · Anantapur and Kurnool · APSPDCL · carved out of APSPDCL in 2020
  • Installed Power Capacity and Generation Mix — thermal · 7,522 MW · solar 5,614.52 MW (largest) · Simhadri · 2,000 MW · Pranahita-Chevella · 3,466 MW
  • Ports and Maritime Policy — 14 non-major ports · 16 notified ports · Coastline: 1,053.07 km – 3rd in India · Duggirajupatnam · Visakhapatnam port · Dolphin's Nose · Bailadila
  • Industrial Infrastructure Bodies and Communications — 41,998 acres · 50 acres without Cabinet · March 2016 · bank branches Guntur
  • Airports, Roads, Railways and Corridors — South Coast Railway · Visakhapatnam · Thirteenth Schedule · 1 June 2026 · East Coast DFC · North-South DFC · Raipur-Visakhapatnam
  • Solar, Pumped Storage and Clean Energy Policy — AP Integrated Clean Energy Policy 2024 · 160 GW+ · Rs 10 lakh crore · 7.5 lakh jobs · Net Zero by 2047 · Green Hydrogen & Ammonia Policy 2023 · 0.5 MTPA
  • Oil, Gas and Refineries — KG basin (Kakinada, Konaseema) · oil and gas hub · APGIC · 2009 · APIIC 51% : APGENCO 49% · BPCL refinery-cum-petrochemical complex · Ramayapatnam
