Recap

  • CRR and SLR – Reserve Ratios — CRR = Cash Reserve Ratio · Net Demand and Time Liabilities (NDTL) · cash balances with the RBI · "Required reserve ratio" · fixed by the RBI (the central bank) · Section 42 of the RBI Act, 1934 · no floor or ceiling
  • Policy Rates – Repo, Reverse Repo, Bank Rate, MSF and LAF — Repo · not the cost of collateral · monetary policy · to regulate liquidity · Bank Rate · LAF (June 2000) · borrow via repos
  • OMO, Sterilisation and Liquidity Management — OMO · both sale and purchase · Sell G-secs → absorbs liquidity · buy G-secs → injects liquidity · interest rates fall · quantitative · a part of credit policy
  • Quantitative vs Qualitative Instruments — Not selective / not qualitative · variable (cash) reserve ratios · Bank Rate policy · Not quantitative · rationing of credit · OMO · Selective controls of RBI
  • Tool → Effect: Dear vs Cheap Money — 'Dear money' · high rate of interest · Inflation rising steadily · increases the repo rate · Anti-inflation set · raise Bank Rate, CRR and SLR · buying
  • Objectives, Authority and Post-1991 Shifts — Monetary policy · money supply · framed by the RBI · RBI Act, 1934 · MPC · Primary objective · Not an objective
  • Lending-rate Benchmarks and Transmission — RBI does NOT fix the PLR · Base rate of interest · minimum rate for all loans · transparency · and · fair to borrowers and banks · 125 bps
