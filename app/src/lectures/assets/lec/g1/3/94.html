Recap

  • Direct and Indirect Taxes – Classification and Taxable Events — Not direct · Indirect pair · customs and excise · Income tax · GST · destination-based · Service tax
  • Union and State Taxes – Who Levies, Who Shares — Union alone (Union List) · customs duties, corporation tax · imposed by the Central Government · not shared · not Union taxes · tolls and tax on mineral rights · Income tax on salary (TDS)
  • VAT, MODVAT, CENVAT, Sales Tax and Service Tax — VAT · value added at each stage · multi-point · destination-based · non-cascading · self-enforcing · harmonised
  • Composition of Central Tax Revenue – Then and Now — Union excise · 2005-06 · corporation tax · 2006-07 · income tax · Two largest Central taxes in the 1990s · Union excise and customs
  • Progressive, Regressive and Degressive Taxes; Laffer Effect — Indirect taxes are regressive · same rate for all income groups · Income tax in India · progressive and direct · GST, sales tax, motor vehicles tax · India's tax system · Redistribution
  • Tax Reform Committees — Kelkar final report (27 Dec 2002) · scaled down · ₹50,000 rebate on housing-loan interest · Global Trust Bank · Oriental Bank of Commerce · Simpler tax system · productivity gains
  • Tax, Fee, Cess and Surcharge — Ad valorem tax · % of the value · specific tax · Clean Energy Cess · Clean Environment Cess · 2016-17 · surcharge 15%
  • Tax-GDP Ratio and Tax Buoyancy — Low ratio · informal sector · evasion · falling ratio · slower than GDP · not · 2026-27 BE
