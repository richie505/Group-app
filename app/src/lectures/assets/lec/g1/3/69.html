Recap

  • Bharatmala, Sagarmala, Railways and Logistics — Sagarmala pillars · Railway electrification · 50.91% · March 2019 · 99.1% · 100% electrification by 2023 · net-zero carbon by 2030
  • National Infrastructure Pipeline (NIP) and Infrastructure Types — India Investment Grid (IIG) · Invest India · only III · Economic infrastructure · social infrastructure · coal mines · 2.5-3.5
  • Infrastructure Financing and PPP — PPP = Public Private Partnership · IIFCL · InvIT · NIIF · NaBFID Act 2021 · Deepak Parekh Committee · Vijay Kelkar Committee (2015)
  • PM GatiShakti National Master Plan — 13 October 2021 · whole-of-government, GIS-based · multimodal connectivity · 16 ministries · BISAG-N, Gandhinagar · Empowered Group of Secretaries · Cabinet Secretary
  • National Monetisation Pipeline (NMP) — Union Budget 2021-22 · brownfield core infrastructure assets · ownership retained by Government · Strategic objective
  • Andhra Pradesh Infrastructure — ~1,053 km · 3rd · AP Maritime Board · Ramayapatnam · Mulapeta · Machilipatnam · MEIL
