RecapOverview and data: National - committees, commissions and their reports

Recap

  • Pay Commissions, Inquiry Commissions and the PM's Economic Advisory Council (PYQ) — non-statutory · Justice K. Venkataswami · November 2002 · Justice S.N. Phukan · 2004 · 2009–2014 (UPA-II): five members · C. Rangarajan
  • Committees – Chairperson and Subject (PYQ) — eight-member · constitutionally and legally justified · in kind · Dinanath Batra · Bharatiya Education Service · Uday Kotak Committee (2017) · SEBI
  • NCRB Reports – Crime in India 2024, ADSI 2024 — MHA · 1986 · Tandon Committee · Crime in India 2024 · Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 · +17.9% · 53.3%
  • Nature of Bodies – Constitutional, Statutory, Executive — not under the NCM
  • Law Commission of India — Macaulay's · IPC (1860) · 1955 · M.C. Setalvad · neither constitutional nor statutory · executive, advisory · fixed 3-year term
  • New Panels and Expert Groups 2025-26 — Nandan Nilekani task force · technology-driven exams · NEET-UG 2026 leak · K. Radhakrishnan Committee · NTA reform · society · Societies Registration Act, 1860
  • Commissions Proposed or Constituted — 543 → 850 · 815 States + 35 UTs · Defeated · 17 April 2026 · two-thirds majority
  • Higher-Education Regulator and Parliamentary Committee Reports — NCHER · Yashpal Committee (2009) · Kapil Sibal · never enacted · 15 December 2025 · single apex body · UGC, AICTE, NCTE
