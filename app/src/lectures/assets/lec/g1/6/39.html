RecapSep–4 Nov 2026: National - new central schemes, missions and their features

Recap

  • Agriculture, Food, Nutrition and Fisheries — Announced · Budget 2025-26 · Cabinet approval 16 July 2025 · 11 October 2025 · Period · 2025-26 to 2030-31 · Coverage
  • Health Missions — Launched 23 September 2018 · ₹5 lakh per family per year · SECC · 48.54 crore Ayushman Cards · Ministry of Health and Family Welfare · National Health Authority · JOSH = Joint Squad for Health
  • Sanitation and Clean Energy — Launched 2 October 2014 · Gandhi's spectacles · 2 October 2019 · Now · SBM-G Phase II · SBM-U 2.0 · ODF Plus stages
  • Literacy, Schools and Higher Education — ULLAS · 2022 · 2030 · 15 years and above · Class 3 · FLNAT · Jan Jan Sakshar
  • Infrastructure, Industry and Trade Schemes — Ude Desh ka Aam Nagrik (RCS-UDAN) · 21 October 2016 · Shimla-Delhi, 27 April 2017 · ₹2,500 · 687 routes · 74 (2014) → 165 (July 2026) · Modified UDAN
  • Border Villages, Panchayats and Social Security — Launched 2023 · "first villages" · Ministry of Home Affairs · VVP-II · only blocks already under VVP-I · Viksit Vibrant Village Programme · MY Bharat
  • Skilling, MSME and Artisans — Launched 17 September 2023 · Ministry of MSME · ₹13,000 crore · 18 traditional trades · Pillars · Samman, Samarthya, Samriddhi · National Exhibition-cum-Trade Fair
  • Social Welfare and State Initiatives — Bhiksha Vritti Mukt Bharat · SMILE · Article 21 and Article 41 · UNICEF child marriage data · ~9.05 crore before 15 · "Eat Right Place of Worship" · 2 years
