RecapMajor Landforms – Fluvial Landforms and River Profiles

Recap

  • Deltas and Estuaries — Conditions for a delta · weak tides and currents · West-flowing Deccan rivers · estuaries, not deltas · steep gradient · ecotone · World's largest (greatest) delta
  • Plains and River-plain Terms – Doab, Prayag, Bhabar, Terai, Lacustrine Plains — Panch Prayag · Vishnuprayag · Nandprayag · Karnaprayag · Rudraprayag · Devprayag · Bhagirathi + Alaknanda → Ganga
  • River Course, Erosional Landforms, Gorge and Canyon — II, I, IV, III · V-shaped valleys · wrong · Grand Canyon · Colorado · Arizona, USA · Gandikota canyon
  • Drainage Patterns — dendritic · Antecedent drainage · most Himalayan rivers · insequent · no apparent control
  • Depositional Landforms, Ravines and Badlands — outer bend concave, erosional · river terraces · Gully erosion → ravines · badland topography · Chambal ravines · Chos
