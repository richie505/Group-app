RecapHydrological Disasters – Floods, Flash Floods, Cloudbursts, Droughts, Tsunamis, Storm Surges

Recap

  • Tsunamis – Meaning, Causes, Behaviour and the 2004 Tsunami — Tsunami · "tsu" = harbour + "nami" = wave · not Latin, Arabic or Hebrew · series of waves · not a single wave · first wave is often not the largest · natural disaster
  • Floods – Extent, Causes and Management — rapid-onset · mitigated through early warning · 1976 · 1980 · 329 mha · more than 40 mha · 12%
  • Disasters – Meaning, Classification and Onset — Disaster = Hazard × Vulnerability · developing countries · Bhopal gas tragedy · man-made · India is not disaster-free · mangroves reduce cyclone impact · 1, 2 and 4
  • Flash Floods, Cloudbursts and GLOFs — local precipitation of high intensity · debris · IMD · Bangladesh, Bhutan, Nepal · thunder and lightning · mostly in hilly areas · 250–300 mm in a couple of hours
  • Droughts — slow-onset · coterminous with the monsoon · irregular or delayed monsoons · IMD · 26–50% · moderate · over 50%
  • India's Vulnerability Profile — incorrect · only D · 27 of 35 · disaster-prone · 13 coastal States/UTs · 84 coastal districts · 1980–2010
  • Urban Floods and Major Floods — encroached drains and lakes · wetland destruction · floodplain construction · high tides · Wetland restoration · Housing and Urban Affairs · Vijayawada – Budameru
  • Storm Surges and the Coastline — Storm surge · cyclone winds and low pressure · high tide · sea-level rise · salinity · East coast · Coastline remeasured (2023–24)
