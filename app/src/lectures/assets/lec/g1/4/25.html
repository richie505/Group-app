RecapOcean-atmosphere Interaction – El Nino, La Nina, Enso, Iod, Walker Circulation

Recap

  • El Nino – Meaning, Mechanism and Effects — El Nino · abnormally warm ocean current · Peru and Ecuador · eastern (and central) tropical Pacific · Pacific Ocean · Name · "the little boy / Christ child"
  • ENSO, La Nina and the Indian Monsoon — Southern Oscillation · Sir Gilbert Walker (1920s) · SOI · Positive SOI · La Nina · negative SOI = El Nino · ENSO
  • Indian Ocean Dipole (IOD) and Madden-Julian Oscillation (MJO) — IOD · sea-surface temperature · two poles of the tropical Indian Ocean · western pole (Arabian Sea / off Africa) · eastern pole (south of Indonesia) · not the eastern Pacific · 1999
  • Walker Circulation, El Nino Types and ENSO Monitoring — sinks over the cool eastern Pacific · easterly trade winds · weakens · Bjerknes feedback · late or weak Indian monsoon · Nino 3.4 region · 5°N–5°S, 120°W–170°W
