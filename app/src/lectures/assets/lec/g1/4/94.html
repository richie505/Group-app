RecapCommunication Networks in India – Postal, Telecom and Digital Connectivity

Recap

  • Postal Network – PIN, Speed Post and India Post — PIN code · 6 digits · 15 Aug 1972 · 9 PIN zones · 8 regional · 1 functional · 9 = Army Postal Service
  • Mass Media – Radio, Television and Development Communication — Prasar Bharati · Yojana · Information & Broadcasting · Anna FM · 2004 · Edgar Dale's Cone of Experience (1946) · radio, recordings, still pictures
  • Telecom – Regulators, Milestones and Market — Jyotiraditya Scindia · Pemmasani Chandra Sekhar · Sanchar Saathi + ASTR · Fastest indigenous IP/MPLS router · Bengaluru · Teledensity 86.76% · 99.9%
  • Digital Connectivity – BharatNet, Digital India, PM-WANI — Digital India: 1 Jul 2015 · 9 pillars · BharatNet · NOFN (2011) · Gram Panchayats · USOF (Digital Bharat Nidhi) · BBNL
  • Transmission Media and Submarine Cables — core index higher than cladding · endoscope · sensors
  • Satellite Communication — INSAT-1B (1983) · GSAT-N2 (GSAT-20) · SpaceX
  • AP Digital Connectivity — APSFL · IP-MPLS ring · triple play · AP FiberNet Phase 3 · 4,000+ tribal hamlets · Amended BharatNet MoC (Feb 2026) · 3,942 villages
