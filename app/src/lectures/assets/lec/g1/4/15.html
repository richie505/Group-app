Recap

  • Heating of the Atmosphere – Radiation, Conduction, Convection, Advection — heated from below · heated by the Earth's surface · not · short-wave · long-wave infra-red · neither · carbon dioxide and water vapour
  • Albedo, Heat Budget and Greenhouse Effect — Albedo = reflecting power · fresh snow · 75–95% · water (lowest) · Low, thick clouds reflect · high, thin clouds trap heat · Ice-albedo feedback
  • Cloudy and Clear Nights; Range of Temperature — Cloudy nights are warmer · hinder terrestrial radiation · Clear nights are colder · (terrestrial) radiation · Interior / continental · coasts · NW India
  • Insolation – Meaning, Factors and Distribution — Insolation = incoming solar radiation · length of the day · transparency of the atmosphere · latitude · longitude · Perihelion ~3 January, ~147 million km · aphelion ~4 July, ~152 million km
  • Heat Waves and Temperature Extremes — 4.5–6.4°C · severe above 6.4°C · severe 6°C or more · 45°C = heat wave · 47°C = severe · NDMA · 2016
  • Distribution of Temperature – Isotherms, Lapse Rate, Inversion — Isotherms · Southern Hemisphere · Highest temperatures · 20° N · Thermal equator · north of the geographical equator · January 18°C isotherm
