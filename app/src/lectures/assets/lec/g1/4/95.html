RecapTransport in Andhra Pradesh – Highways, Railways, Ports, Airports and Waterways

Recap

  • Ports of AP – Framework, Districts and Models — Coastline 1,053.07 km · third-longest · 974 km = second · 1 major port · 16-17 notified non-major ports · AP Maritime Board (APMB) · Union List Entry 27
  • Airports of AP — Six · three international · Puttaparthi · not among the six · Vizianagaram · GMR (GVIAL) · PPP-DBFOT
  • Roads, National Highways and APSRTC — more than 80% · 17.64 km per lakh population · SH-31 · PMGSY · 99.09% · APRDC · APSRTC
  • Visakhapatnam Port — October 1933 · SS Jaladurga · Deepest land-locked and protected port · "Brightest Jewel" · Dolphin's Nose · Outer harbour · iron ore exports
  • Railways – South Coast Railway, Lines and Tunnels — 100% electrified · South Coast Railway · AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 · SCR and ECoR · India's 18th zone · HQ Visakhapatnam · 1 Jun 2026
  • Inland Waterways and Canals — NW-4 · Godavari and Krishna · Canals · not Krishna Canal · Buckingham Canal · longest canal of commercial importance · Pulicat Lake
  • Greenfield and Proposed Ports — Four greenfield ports · ₹17,067 crore · 108 MMTPA · 30-50% · Dec 2026 · 145 MTPA by 2030 · 300 MTPA by 2047
  • Logistics, Sagarmala and Metro — Nexus Integrated Logistics Park · APMB = AP Maritime Board · APLINC · AP Logistics Fund · Sagarmala pillars · DFCs not a pillar · Visakhapatnam Metro
