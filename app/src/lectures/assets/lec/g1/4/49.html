Recap

  • Cyclone Vulnerability and Major Cyclones of AP — Coastline · 1,053.07 km · 3rd longest · Gujarat (2,340.62 km) · Tamil Nadu (1,068.69 km) · 974 km ("2nd") · Flood Hazard Atlas of AP (2021, NRSC)
  • Cyclone Mitigation – NCRMP and Shelters — World Bank · NDMA (MHA) · New Delhi · 2011 · forecasting, tracking and warning · multi-hazard risk management · multipurpose cyclone shelters
  • Climate and Rainfall – Two Monsoons — hot and humid · March to June · moisture level is quite high · semi-arid · Coldest place · Lambasingi · Visakhapatnam
  • Drought-prone Areas and Drought Management — rain shadow · NE monsoon · chronic drought · Prakasam, Anantapur, Kurnool, Kadapa · rain-shadow · 750 mm · chronically drought prone
  • Floods and Disaster Management Institutions — Flood season (APSDMA) · 1 June–31 October · 1976 · 1980 · 40 million ha · CWC · 1958
