RecapAtmosphere – Structure (layers) and Composition

Recap

  • Ozone Layer – Location, Formation and UV Shield — Ozone (O₃) = three oxygen atoms · stratosphere · Lower stratosphere · ~15–30 km · ~15–35 km · 15–20 km above the surface · 90%
  • Ozone Depletion, the Ozone Hole and Treaties — stratosphere · Main cause: halocarbons · CFCs · chlorine and bromine · Carbon dioxide has no direct role · HFCs · Antarctica
  • Composition – Gases of the Atmosphere — N₂ > O₂ > Ar > CO₂ · Nitrogen + oxygen = 99% · Permanent gases · variable · Chlorine · not · Xenon = "Stranger Gas"
  • Layers – Basis, Sequence and Temperature Trend — vertical profile of air temperature · smallest height · atmosphere · no definite upper limit · thins gradually · falls, rises, falls, rises · stratosphere and thermosphere
  • Troposphere and Tropopause — Average height 13 km · 18 km at the equator · Thickest at the equator · high insolation and strong convection · Almost all weather · cloud mass of a cyclone · water vapour and dust
  • Stratosphere, Mesosphere, Ionosphere and Exosphere — 80–400 km · electrically charged ions
  • Carbon Dioxide, Water Vapour, Dust and the Greenhouse Property — absorbs infrared · Greenhouse effect · CO₂ · Water vapour · under 1% · blanket · Dust
  • Homosphere, Heterosphere and Ionospheric Layers — ionosphere is not in the homosphere · GNSS
