Recap

  • Alluvial Soil, Khadar–Bhangar and the Soil-Type Ranking — Eight · Residual (in situ) · Peninsular India · drifted (ex situ) · North India · Deposited by rivers · deltas
  • Soil Conservation – Methods and Practices — Soil conservation · soil erosion · Best single method to prevent erosion · afforestation · contour bunding and afforestation · Soil fertility is improved by · seed modification does not
  • Black (Regur) Soil — Deccan Trap basalt · fissure volcanic rock · regur · black cotton soil · Clay above 62% · highest moisture retention · needs little irrigation
  • Laterite and Red Soils — "later" = brick · intense leaching · iron and aluminium oxides · Red · acidic · low fertility · excess iron
  • Problem Soils – Salinity, Alkalinity, Acidity and Degradation — reh, kallar, usar · Salinisation · make some soils impermeable · Largest saline area: Gujarat · maximum alkali area: Uttar Pradesh · Soil impoverishment · Green Revolution reduced soil fertility
  • Soil Erosion – Types, Agents and Causes — Increasing soil loss · splash → sheet → rill → gully · Ravines · periphery of the Gangetic plain · Deforestation · Terrace cultivation is a remedy · all
  • Other Soils, Rain-Forest Soils and the ICAR/USDA Orders — Arid / desert · phosphate normal/high · calcium increases downward · Peaty / marshy · Forest / mountain · acidic with low humus · Podzol
  • Soil Science Terms – Pedology, Eluviation, pH, Water and Soil Biology — Pedology · edaphology · soil geography · Edaphic · Eluviation · illuviation · Capillary rise
