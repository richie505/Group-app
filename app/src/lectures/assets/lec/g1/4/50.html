RecapAndhra Pradesh – Natural Vegetation and Forests; Forest Cover and Types

Recap

  • Forest Cover and Recorded Forest Area – ISFR Data — 8th in India · 85.8% · 13.6% · 0.6% · 36.88 lakh ha (22.63%) · ISFR 2023 · 5,340.02 sq km
  • Tree Species, Forest Produce and Plantations — Red sanders (*Pterocarpus santalinus*) · endemic · Seshachalam, Palakonda and Veligonda · dry deciduous · not · greatest economic value · Endangered
  • Forest Types and Natural Vegetation — dry deciduous · Seshachalam hills = dry deciduous · Papikonda NP = moist deciduous · ISFR 2019 forest types · Southern Dry Mixed Deciduous · Coringa · Godavari
