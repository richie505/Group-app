RecapIndia – the Northern Plains, the Peninsular Plateau, the Coastal Plains and the Islands

Recap

  • Central Ranges – Vindhya, Satpura, Maikal, Aravalli and Peaks — West → east · Barwani – Mahadeo – Maikal · Mahadeo – Maikal – Chota Nagpur – Khasi · North → south · Narmada · Vindhya (north) and the Satpura (south) · UPSC 2014 pairs
  • Islands — Salsette · Divi · Piram · Diu · Mount Koyob · South Andaman · Rameswaram (Pamban) island
  • Western and Eastern Ghats and the Southern Hills — "Ghat" · river-landing steps (stairs) · mountain pass · "gap or pass" · Local names, north → south · Sahyadri · Nilgiri
  • Coastal Plains — Cape Comorin = Kanyakumari · West coast north → south · submergent · East coast · emergent · more rice · less
  • Indian Desert and the Rann of Kutch — north-west / west of the Aravallis · barchans and longitudinal (seif) dunes · below 150 mm · Marusthali · Bagar · Rann of Kutch · Luni
  • Northern Plains — Aggradational · Himalayan Frontal Fault · youngest · about 200 m drop over ~1,600 km · Kosi · Belts from the hills southward · Bhabar
  • Physiographic Divisions of India — Six divisions · Plains · 43% · Oldest · youngest · Sahara · not
  • Peninsular Plateau – Structure, Plateaus and the Deccan Traps — Oldest and largest · Precambrian shield · Gondwana land · stable · not · metallic minerals · 16 lakh sq km
