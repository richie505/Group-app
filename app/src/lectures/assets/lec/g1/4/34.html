  • Dimensions, Origin, Structure and Significance — northern boundary · Indian Himalayan Region · not Uttar Pradesh · Young fold mountains · Tethys (geosynclinal) · Tertiary · Indian plate
  • Glaciers — Largest among options · Siachen · Himalayan glaciers · highest rate in the world · Longest (decreasing) · Kumaon glaciers · Pindari, Milam and Kafni
  • Regional Divisions — Four · five · Eastern hills and mountains · Arunachal Himalaya · Kangtu and Namcha Barwa · not · Nokrek
