RecapHimalayan Drainage System – Indus, Ganga and Brahmaputra Basins

Recap

  • Ganga Tributaries – Yamuna, Son, Damodar, Kosi and the Left Bank — Yamuna · westernmost and longest · Son · Mahananda · last, easternmost · West Bengal · flows northwards
  • Indus System — Singge Khabab · northern slopes of the Kailash range · Singi Khamban · Ladakh and Zaskar · Leh · right bank · Zanskar
  • Dams, Canals and Projects on the Himalayan Rivers — Multi-state projects · Chambal Valley – MP and Rajasthan · Kosi – Bihar and Nepal · Canals · Sirhind – Sutlej · Upper Bari Doab – Ravi · Peninsular projects (for contrast)
  • Ganga – Headwaters, Panchprayag, Course and Delta — Bhagirathi · Gangotri glacier at Gomukh · Uttarkashi is on the Bhagirathi · Alaknanda · Satopanth glacier · second glacial source · Badrinath
  • Brahmaputra System — Chemayungdung glacier · U-turn at Namcha Barwa · Dihang gorge · born before the Himalayas · 2,900 km · 885 km · 1,280 km up to Dibrugarh
  • Character of Himalayan Rivers – Antecedent, Perennial, Inland Drainage — Inland drainage · Ghaggar · River Khari (Rajasthan) · Banas tributary · Bay of Bengal · Definition · existed before the Himalayas rose
  • Himalayan Lakes and the Terai Arc — Wular lake · Pir Panjal · Zaskar · Kolleru · Budameru and Tammileru · Kanwar lake (Kabar Tal, Bihar) · Burhi Gandak
  • Sources – the Mansarovar Group and Others — Brahmaputra and Indus · Rivers not rising in Indian territory · Sutlej · Ghaghara
