RecapEarth Movements – Continental Drift, Sea-floor Spreading, Plate Tectonics and Plate Boundaries

Recap

  • Continental Drift – Pangaea and Gondwanaland — Continental drift · Alfred Wegener · 1912 · pole-fleeing force · tidal force · too weak · Pangaea
  • Plate Tectonics and the Plates — Plate tectonics · asthenosphere · 1967 by McKenzie and Parker · Morgan · based on sea-floor spreading · not given by H.H. Hess in 1967 · Arthur Holmes
  • The Indian Plate and the Himalayas — Palaeomagnetic · northward · Cretaceous · Deccan Traps · Réunion hotspot · Eurasia · ~40–50 million years ago
  • Sea-floor Spreading and Palaeomagnetism — Sea-floor spreading · new crust is continually added · subduction · age, density and thickness · increase · 200 million years · palaeomagnetism
  • Plate Boundaries and Earth Movements — Owen Fracture Zone · Ladakh Magmatic Arc · Neo-Tethys · Sudden movements · volcanoes
