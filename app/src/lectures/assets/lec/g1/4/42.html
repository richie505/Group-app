RecapIndia – Tiger Reserves, Elephant Reserves, Ramsar Sites, Important Bird Areas

Recap

  • Tiger Reserves – Project Tiger, NTCA and Reserve → State — Project Tiger · 1 April 1973 · 9 reserves · Corbett · India's first tiger reserve · NTCA · statutory body
  • Ramsar Sites – Counts, States and Firsts — Most Ramsar sites · Tamil Nadu · Largest site · Sunderbans · Loktak · Manipur · Keibul Lamjao NP
  • Ramsar Convention, Montreux Record and Wetland Programmes — 2 Feb 1971 · Ramsar, Iran · India joined 1 Feb 1982 · Gland, Switzerland · conservation and wise use of wetlands · World Wetlands Day: 2 February · 22 March World Water Day
  • Migratory Birds and Flyways — Chilika Lake · Central Asian Flyway · CMS COP13 · Gandhinagar (2020) · National bird of India · Indian peafowl (peacock) · Nagaland
  • Bird Sanctuaries and Important Bird Areas — IBAs · BirdLife International · BNHS · painted storks · spot-billed pelicans · Sambhar Lake, Nov 2019 · avian botulism
  • Species Sanctuaries – Turtles, Gharial, Lion — Olive ridleys · Odisha and Andhra coasts · mechanised trawler nets
  • Elephant Reserves and Elephant Census — Project Elephant · February 1992 · 33 elephant reserves in 14 States · overlap · Most ERs: Assam and Tamil Nadu (5 each) · Largest elephant population: Karnataka · 6,013
