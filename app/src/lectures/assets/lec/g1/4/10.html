  • Focus, Epicentre and Basic Terms — John Milne · Father of Modern Seismology · below · Depth of focus · 0–70 km · Shallow-focus · most damaging
  • Earthquake as a Disaster – Classification and Prediction — natural · geological · not · Sea surge / storm surge · Prediction · cannot yet be predicted accurately · probabilities
  • Major Earthquakes – India and World — weak, non-engineered houses · shallow focus · Worst disasters (FRO 2020) · Bhola cyclone – Bangladesh · Tohoku earthquake – Japan · 365 AD Crete tsunami – Europe · Yellow River floods – China
  • Effects – Tsunami, Landslides, Liquefaction — Tsunami · displacement of the sea floor · undersea (sea) earthquakes · not · shallow focus · vertical · Indian coast
  • Causes and Types of Earthquakes — near faults · all four · Koyna · old fault plane · Latur 1993 · stable areas along hidden faults · not
  • Seismic Monitoring, Early Warning and Management — Not · heavy materials · 38 cities · half a million+
