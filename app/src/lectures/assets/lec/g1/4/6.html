RecapRocks and Minerals – Igneous, Sedimentary, Metamorphic; the Rock Cycle

Recap

  • Igneous Rocks — primary rocks · no layers, no fossils · crystalline and non-crystalline · By silica · diorite = intermediate intrusive · Felsic · Mafic (basic)
  • Composition of the Crust and Minerals — oxygen and magnesium · silicon · Feldspar · one-half · wrong · Statement I only · Quartz
  • Sedimentary Rocks, Fossils and Geological Time — hydrological system · weathering of pre-existing rocks · lithified · layers · contain fossils · 75% of the Earth's surface · coal, petroleum, natural gas
  • Metamorphic Rocks — heat and pressure · both · garnet · halite is not · gneiss · granite · coal → diamond
  • Petrology and the Rock Cycle — Petrology · Rock cycle · lithification · any rock type can become another · CO₂ · carbonic acid · Enhanced rock weathering
