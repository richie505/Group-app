## P 4:17:0 | Humidity, evaporation, condensation, clouds and forms of precipitation | Humidity terms

L4:17:0:11 Steam burns more severely than boiling water — **latent heat released on condensing**
L4:17:0:12 **Refrigerator** refrigerant — **absorbs heat on evaporation** (inside), **releases it on condensation** (at the back)
L4:17:0:13 Higher pressure → boiling point **increases** (pressure cooker cooks faster); lower pressure at altitude lowers it
