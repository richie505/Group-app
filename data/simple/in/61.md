L2:116:1:1 Only about **5%** of Indian marriages are inter-caste
L2:116:1:2 Electoral politics — caste-based **vote banks**; caste associations as pressure groups
L2:116:1:3 Urban migrants carry caste prejudice — caste networks, associations, caste stickers on vehicles; identity does **not disappear**

L2:116:2:0 **Kaka Kalelkar** — casteism "an overriding, **blind and supreme group loyalty**"

L2:116:2:2 Ambedkar — *Annihilation of Caste* (**1936**)

## P 2:116:3 | Casteism - meaning, causes, manifestations and remedial measures | Caste in politics
L2:116:3:0 Casteist element in electoral politics — traced to **Minto-Morley Reforms, 1909** (separate electorates)
L2:116:3:1 **Samajwadi Party** and **Rashtriya Janata Dal** — from backward-caste mobilisation
L2:116:3:2 **Justice Party** (Andhra) — mobilised **Reddys, Kammas, Kapus, Velamas** against Brahmin dominance; won the **Communal G.O.** in **1921**; ground for their post-independence dominance
L2:116:3:3 Post-**Karamchedu** Dalit mobilisation — through independent **Dalit Mahasabhas** (not the Justice Party)
L2:116:3:4 Caste census debate — **ossify** caste identities or **expose** existing inequity

## P 2:117:1 | Communalism - meaning, causes, forms and growth in India | Scholars & leaders
L2:117:1:0 **Ram Ahuja** — communalism an **ideology**: religious communities with differing, often **opposing interests**; communal persons practise politics through religion
L2:117:1:1 **Robert Melson and Howard Wolpe** — "the **political assertiveness** of a community to maintain its identity in a plural society undergoing modernisation"
L2:117:1:2 **Bipan Chandra** — followers of one religion share **common social, political and economic interests**
L2:117:1:3 **Lord Minto** — "**Father of the Communal Electorate**" (separate electorates for Muslims)
## P 2:117:2 | Communalism - meaning, causes, forms and growth in India | Bipan Chandra's three stages
L2:117:2:0 **Bipan Chandra** — communalism: one religion = common social, political, economic interests
L2:117:2:1 Stages — **communal nationalism** → **liberal communalism** → **extreme communalism**
L2:117:2:2 **Liberal** — interests different but **reconcilable** within one nation
L2:117:2:3 **Extreme** — interests **mutually antagonistic**
L2:117:2:4 After **1937** elections — Muslim League to extreme communalism based on **fear, hatred and violence**; echoed by Hindu Mahasabha and RSS
## P 2:117:3 | Communalism - meaning, causes, forms and growth in India | Oommen's six dimensions
L2:117:3:0 **Secessionist** — independent state outside India, e.g. **Khalistan**
L2:117:3:1 **Separatist** — territorial state **within India**, e.g. **Gorkhaland**, **Bodoland**
L2:117:3:2 **Retaliatory** — attempts to **harm** members of other communities (riots, revenge cycles)
L2:117:3:3 **Welfarist** — works for one community's welfare
L2:117:3:4 **Political** — uses religion for votes
