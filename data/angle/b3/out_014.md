## Growth phases, policies and economists → since 1951 | from: S1
- **Early Plans:**
  - First Plan (1951-56): agriculture focus; industrial production rose about **40%**
  - Second Plan (1956-61): heavy + basic industries on the **Nehru-Mahalanobis model**
- **Framework:** **IPR 1956** reserved **17** industries for public sector → **1991 policy** cut this to **8**

| **Phase** | **Character (approximate growth) **[GK] |
|---|---|
| 1951-65 | Rapid build-up: public-sector-led heavy industrialisation (about 7-8%) |
| 1965-80 | Deceleration (wars, droughts, infrastructure bottlenecks, licensing) and licence-raj stagnation (about 4%) |
| 1980s | Recovery with partial liberalisation (about 7-8%) |
| 1991 onward | Liberalisation; services outgrow industry |

| **Economist** | **Concept** |
|---|---|
| **Dani Rodrik** | **Premature deindustrialisation**: manufacturing share peaks at lower income than in early industrialisers |

- After 1991, cheap imports shut many small units
- ⚠ IPR 1956 = **17** reserved industries; 1991 = **8**

## IIP → meaning, compiler, base year and weights | from: S5
# 1. Definition & compilation
- **Definition:** Composite indicator of short-term changes in **volume of industrial production** vs a base period
- **Compiled by:** **NSO (MoSPI)**, monthly (formerly the CSO; older notes say CSO)
- **Base year (10th revision):** Revised from **2011-12 to 2022-23**, effective **1 June 2026** (new series released Jul 2026); WPI base revised to 2022-23 at the same time
  - ⚠ **Trap:** Any IIP reading from before June 2026 is on the old base
  - New basket: **1,042 products** in 463 item groups (earlier: base 2011-12, 407 items)
  - Old basket: 809 manufacturing items in 405 groups
# 2. Coverage
- **Sectors:** **Mining, manufacturing, electricity**
  - Use-based groups: primary, capital, intermediate, infrastructure/construction goods, consumer durables, consumer non-durables
  - Outside IIP: services, agriculture, construction activity
- **New series additions:**
  - **Gas supply** + **water supply, sewerage and waste management**
  - Minor and rare-earth minerals
  - **Renewable electricity** shown separately
# 3. Weights
- **Basis:** Each sector's share of **GVA in 2022-23**

| **Series** | **Weights** |
|---|---|
| New series | Manufacturing 76.1%, mining 11.1%, electricity and gas 10.9%, water supply 2.02% |
| 2011-12 series | Primary goods highest (**34%**); capital goods lowest (**8.22%**) |

# 4. Trends & services index
- **Trends:**
  - FY 2023: **capital goods and infrastructure/construction goods** indices grew most (BPSC 2024)
  - Feb 2026: non-core manufacturing + capital goods outpaced core sectors
- **Index of Services Production:** New services counterpart of IIP (base **2024-25**)
  - First release **14 July 2026**; **60-day** lag
- ⚠ IIP = **NSO (MoSPI)**; ICI = **DPIIT** (Office of the Economic Adviser)

## CPSEs → definition and data | from: S8
# 1. Definition & numbers
- **CPSE:** Company where Central Government or other CPSEs directly hold **51% or more**
  - Company/statutory body with **not less than 51%** Central Government share capital (ES 2025-26)
- **Number of CPSEs:** **5** at start of First Plan → **244** by 1990 (APPSC-G1 2004) → **250+ operating CPSEs** now
- **Scale:** Combined net profit above **₹3.43 lakh crore**
  - Employ about **9-10 lakh** people
  - R&D spend over ₹10,800 crore; CSR about ₹5,000 crore a year
# 2. Profit & loss makers

## Acts → year and provisions (MSMED Act, 2006) | from: S0, S2, S11
- **MSMED Act, 2006:** Introduced as Small and Medium Enterprise (SME) Development Bill on **12 May 2005** → Act, in force from **2 October 2006**
  - PYQ: passed in **2006**
- **Chapter V (delayed payments):**
  - Buyers must pay within **45 days**
  - After that: compound interest at **3 times the RBI bank rate** (Sec. 16)
  - Disputes → **MSE Facilitation Council**
- **MSMED (Amendment) Bill 2026:** Passed by Rajya Sabha (Aug 2026)
  - National Digital Platform for registration
  - Additional State MSEFCs
- **Public Procurement Policy for MSEs Order, 2012:**
  - Central ministries + CPSEs must buy **25%** of annual procurement from MSEs (raised from 20% in 2018)
  - Sub-targets: **4%** SC/ST-owned MSEs; **3%** women-owned MSEs
  - **Achieved (FY26, to 1 Dec 2025):** **44.76%**, above the mandate
- **GeM:** **Rs 18.4 lakh crore** GMV; **11 lakh+** MSEs (Aug 2026)
- ⚠ Bill introduced **12 May 2005**; Act in force **2 October 2006**
- ⚠ Procurement mandate **25%** (from 20%): **4%** SC/ST + **3%** women
