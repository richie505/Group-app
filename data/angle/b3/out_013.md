## Food management → objectives, missions and aims | from: S0, S1
# 1. Department of Food & PD
- **Works via:** **procurement, storage and movement, public distribution, and buffer stocks**
# 2. Objectives
| **Arm** | **Objective / aim** |
|---|---|
| **PDS** | Guaranteed minimum grain at affordable prices for low-income groups + equitable distribution + control of price swings |
| **Procurement** | Assure MSP to farmers + **free grain** for the weak under **PMGKAY** + market intervention to check prices |

- ⚠ **Trap:** **Export of foodgrains is NOT an objective of food management** (BPSC 2020)
# 3. FCI missions
- (1) **efficient procurement at MSP**
- (2) **storage and distribution** (storage, movement and distribution) of foodgrains
- (3) **operational and buffer stocks** for food security
- (4) Foodgrains **accessible at reasonable prices** to weaker sections through **TPDS**
- ⚠ **Trap:** **Not** an FCI mission = **regulating markets in agricultural products** → job of State APMC laws (APPSC-GS 2018)

## Price terms → meaning | from: S4, S5
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **MSP** | Floor price announced before sowing (CACP → CCEA) |
| **Procurement price** | Price at which the government actually buys grain for PDS and buffer stock (earlier announced separately at harvest; later merged into one price) (APP) |
| **Central Issue Price (CIP)** | Price at which the Centre issues grain to States and beneficiaries for PDS; states fix the FPS retail price (UPSC notes) |
| **Ceiling price** | Maximum price that may be charged, to protect consumers |
| **Economic cost** | **MSP + bonus** + **procurement incidentals** + **distribution cost** (UPSC 2019) |
| **Food subsidy** | **Economic cost minus CIP**, reimbursed to FCI and to DCP States (IYB 2026) |
| **Explicit subsidy** | Shown in budget |
| **Hidden subsidy** | Cost not shown as subsidy, e.g., FCI's carrying cost financed by borrowing instead of budget releases |

- **Table note:** p.192 (procurement price, CIP, ceiling price)
- **Economic cost of FCI:** **MSP + bonus (if any) + procurement incidentals + distribution costs**
  - Pooled grain cost (MSP + State bonuses) ≈ **two-thirds** of economic cost
- **Retail price:** **States fix the retail price** at FPS; State's role in PDS is operational
- ⚠ **MSP** = floor, before sowing; **ceiling price** = maximum, for consumers; **CIP** = Centre's issue price, not FPS retail

## Causes of malnutrition → factors | from: S1
- **Biggest factor:** **Poor dietary habits and food insecurity** (APPSC-G2 2025 key)
- **Other causes:**
  - **Cereal-heavy diets** short on protein, iron, micronutrients
  - **Open defecation and unsafe water** → disease-linked nutrient loss
  - **Early (adolescent) pregnancy**
  - Poverty and gender gaps
  - Weak counselling and follow-up
- **Approach:** Malnutrition is **multi-causal** (sanitation, maternal education, poverty) → combine nutrition with **WASH** (2026)
