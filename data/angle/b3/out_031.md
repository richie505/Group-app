## Pattiseema and Polavaram — features and status | from: S2, S3, S5
# 1. Pattiseema
- **Type:** **Lift irrigation scheme** (PYQ) on Godavari at Pattiseema
- **Route:** Lifts water into **Polavaram Right Main Canal** → **Krishna at Prakasam Barrage** = first Godavari-Krishna link
- **Beneficiary:** **Krishna delta** farmers (PYQ 2018, 2017)
- **Features:** **24 pumps**; built 2014-19; commissioned **2015**
- **Nature:** **Temporary** until Polavaram's gravity flow starts
  - ⚠ **Trap:** "canal runs 100 km through West Godavari" = the false statement in the PYQ
- **Performance:** ~**450 TMC** transferred; kept Krishna delta supplied during 2026 El Nino deficit
> Exam angle: "Pattiseema is a lift irrigation project" and "not true" statement sets on its canal and purpose.
# 2. Polavaram
- **Status:** **National Project** on **Godavari**
- **GWDT award (1980):** Permitted Polavaram diversion of **80 tmc** Godavari water to Krishna
- **Polavaram status:** **88% civil works, 93% land acquisition, 45% R&R** done; left-canal water via Purushothapatnam lift from July (May 2026)
- **Jalayagnam:** Polavaram (started 2005) still a Jalayagnam project
