=== ROW 39 | Food security - NFSA 2013, PDS reform, buffer stocks, One Nation One Ration Card, PMGKAY

## Food security — definitions, dimensions and FIRSTs | from: S0, S1, S2
# 1. Definition
- **FAO definition (World Food Summit 1996):** Food security exists when **all people, at all times**, have **physical and economic access** to sufficient, safe and nutritious food that meets their dietary needs and food preferences for an **active and healthy life**
  - ⚠ **Trap:** Food security does **not** imply "access by all people at all times to the food **of their choice**" (APPSC G2 2017)
# 2. Dimensions
- **Four dimensions:** **availability, access (affordability), utilisation/absorption, stability**

| **Dimension** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Availability** | Production + imports + previous stocks |
| **Accessibility** | Food within reach of every person |
| **Affordability** | Money enough for sufficient, safe, nutritious food |
| **Absorption/utilisation** | The body's ability to use the food (the fourth) |

- **Not dimensions:** Price, quantity of exports etc.
  - ⚠ **Trap:** **"Accountability" is NOT a dimension** (UPPSC 2025)
# 3. Levels
| **Level** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **National** | Adequate aggregate production/availability + buffer stock |
| **Household** | Each household can actually access and afford food |

- **India's record:** Achieved national (buffer stocks) without fully assuring household → hence **PDS**, **NFSA**
# 4. Famine, vulnerable groups and FIRSTs
- **Bengal Famine of 1943:** Killed about **30 lakh** people
- **PDS origin:** Began as a **World War II rationing** measure in the 1940s (Bengal famine backdrop)
- **Buffer stock origin:** Formally introduced in the **Fourth Five-Year Plan (1969-74)**
- **Most food-insecure:** SCs, STs and landless/poor OBC sections

## Economists and indices → views on hunger | from: S0, S7
| **Person / index** | **View / finding** |
|---|---|
| **Amartya Sen** (Poverty and Famines, 1981) | Introduced **entitlements** in food security (UPPSC 2025, "Only 4") |
| Entitlement core idea | Famines arise from collapse of people's **entitlements** (command over food through income, exchange, transfers), not merely a fall in food availability/supply |
| **ICMR-NIN** recommended diet | Households unable to afford it fell from about **52% (2011-12)** → about **25% (2023-24)** (25% rural, 21% urban) |

  - Three of the four indicators concern children only
  - Undernourishment figure comes from a small opinion-poll-type sample

## Buffer stocks and OMSS — mechanism, norms and stock data | from: S1
# 1. Buffer stock
- **Mechanism:**
  - Wheat and rice procured by **FCI** at pre-announced **MSP** (on **CACP** advice) from surplus states
  - Sold at the lower **issue price** in deficit areas and in calamities
- **Norm-setting:** Minimum buffer levels fixed by the **CCEA**, set for each quarter (**1 April, 1 July, 1 October, 1 January**)
- **Strategic reserve:** **5 million tonnes** above operational stocks
- **Stock levels:**
  - Latest: about **602 LMT**, around **3 times** the buffer norms
  - Earlier: **702.03 LT** on 1 Sep 2025 (rice 369.06 + wheat 332.97)
- !! Norms include a strategic reserve of wheat and rice held for emergencies [GK]. Not in your sources: the current quarter-wise norm figures.
- !! Not in your sources: the actual quarterly tonnage norms for rice and wheat, and the size of the strategic reserve.
- ⚠ Norms fixed by **CCEA**; MSP advised by **CACP**; procurement by **FCI**
# 2. Open Market Sale Scheme (OMSS)
- **Mechanism:** **FCI** sells surplus central-pool grain in the open market at predetermined prices (**OMSS (Domestic)** sales to cool prices)
- **Purposes:** Raises supply in lean seasons and deficit regions + moderates prices + offloads excess stock + cuts carrying cost

# NFSA 2013 — nature and coverage
- **Legal status:** **Legal, justiciable right** to subsidised foodgrains; commenced **July 2013**
- **Objective:** Food and nutritional security at affordable prices so people live with dignity (APPSC 2025)
  - ⚠ **Trap:** Distractors were a "PDS Act", the "FCI Act" and "NFSA 2014"
- **Ministry:** Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution (**DFPD**)
- **Coverage:** Up to **75% of rural** + **50% of urban** = about **two-thirds (≈67%)** of India (2025-26) (asked APPSC G2 2017: 75%)
  - ~**80-81 crore **beneficiaries; about **80 crore **people across all States/UTs
> Exam angle: "what % of rural population" - answer **75%**; urban **50%**.

# PMGKAY

- **Extension:** **5 years: 1 Jan 2024 – 31 Dec 2028**; cost about **Rs 11.80 lakh crore** borne by the Centre
- **Food subsidy (Union Budget 2026-27):** **Rs 2,27,629 crore**

## Causes and challenges — push-pull, slums, housing, transport, waste | from: S2, S3
