## Principles → meaning | from: S1, S3
| **Principle** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Precautionary principle** | Act to prevent damage (stop the activity) even **without full scientific certainty**; **burden of proof shifts to the actor** to show the activity is benign |
| **Polluter Pays** | The **person or company responsible** bears the cost of **repairing** the damage (remediation + compensation) (APPSC 2025; PYQ) |
| **Public trust doctrine** | The State holds natural resources in trust for the public |

=== ROW 22 | National income concepts - GDP, GNP, NDP, NNP, factor cost vs market price, GVA, deflator

## Terms → meaning (GDP, GNP, national income, SDP) | from: S0, S2, S3
| **Stem** | **Term** |
|---|---|
| Value of all **final** goods and services produced **within the country** in a year | **GDP** |
| Final output of the country's **nationals/normal residents**, wherever located | **GNP** |
| Wages, rent, interest, profit earned by the factors of production | Factor income |

# 1. GDP
- **GDP definition:** **GDP = market value of all final goods and services produced within the geographical boundaries of a country during a year**, whoever produces them (GS 2025 key)
- **GDP formula:** **GDP = Q x P**; counts production by both residents and non-residents inside the territory
# 2. National income
- **Definition:** **NNP at factor cost = National Income**
  - Total factor income (wages, rent, interest, profits) of a country's normal residents in a year
  - = Net domestic factor income + NFIA
- **Flow vs stock:** National income = **flow** (per year); national wealth = **stock**
# 3. State Domestic Product
- **State Domestic Product:** Aggregate economic value of all goods and services produced within the **State's **boundaries, **counted without duplication**, during a specified period, usually a year (APSES wording; APPSC 2025; APSES 2024-25; GS 2025)
# 4. Other terms
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Depreciation (consumption of fixed capital)** | Deducted because output that only replaces worn-out capital adds nothing new |
| **NFIA (Net Factor Income from Abroad)** | Factor income received from abroad - factor income paid abroad |
| **Production taxes** | Levied irrespective of output (e.g. land revenue, stamp duty, property tax) |
| **Product taxes** | Levied per unit of product (e.g. **excise, sales tax/GST**) |
| **Transfer earnings** | **Not payments for factor services** → **not included** in national income |

- **Transfer earnings:** APPSC-G2 2025 A-R: both true, R explains A
- **Comparative note:** GDP = territorial; GNP = nationality/residence

## Aggregates → formula | from: S1, S2, S5
| **Link** | **Formula** |
|---|---|
| Domestic to National | **GNP = GDP + NFIA** (NFIA = factor income received from abroad - factor income paid abroad) |
| Gross to Net | **NDP = GDP - Depreciation**; **NNP = GNP - Depreciation** |
| Market price to Factor cost | **FC = MP - Net Indirect Taxes** (indirect taxes - subsidies) |
| National Income | **NNP at FC = NDP at FC + NFIA** |

| **Aggregate** | **Derivation** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|---|
| GDP (MP) | Value of final output within domestic territory | Includes output by residents and non-residents |
| NDP | **GDP - Depreciation** | What remains after replacing worn-out capital |
| GNP | **GDP + NFIA** | Output of **nationals/normal residents** wherever located |
| NNP (MP) | **GNP - Depreciation** | How much a country can consume in a period |
| NNP (FC) | NNP (MP) - net indirect taxes | **National Income** |

- **NFIA form:** GNP = GDP + X - M (X = income of nationals abroad; M = income of foreigners in India)
# 1. Market price vs factor cost
- **Difference:** GDP at MP vs FC differ by **indirect taxes and subsidies** - not direct taxes (APPSC-G2 2025)
- **Market price = factor cost + indirect taxes - subsidies**
- **GDPfc = GDPmp - Indirect taxes + Subsidies** = **GDP (FC) = GDP (MP) - indirect taxes + subsidies**
  - ⚠ **Trap:** Some notes print "- subsidies": sign is wrong - subsidies are added back
# 2. Three price levels
- **GVA at factor cost + net production taxes = GVA at basic prices**
- **GVA at basic prices + net product taxes = GVA at market prices (GDP)**
- **GVA vs NVA:** **GVA includes depreciation, NVA excludes it**
# 3. Personal, disposable and per capita income
- **Personal Income:** **Personal Income = NI - undistributed corporate profits - social security contributions - corporate tax + government and business transfer payments + net interest paid by government.**
- **Personal Disposable Income:** **Personal Disposable Income = Personal Income - personal direct taxes - non-tax payments (fines, fees)** = what individuals can spend or save
- **National savings:** National disposable income - consumption = **national savings**
- **Per Capita Income:** **Per Capita Income = National Income / Population** (APPSC 2025; GS 2025 key)
  - PCI rises only when NI grows faster than population

## GNP vs GDP — rule and worked examples | from: S1
# 1. When one exceeds the other
- **Rule:** **GNP > GDP when NFIA is positive** (residents earn more abroad than foreigners earn at home); GNP < GDP when NFIA is negative
- **Indians abroad:** Remittance-type factor income earned (e.g. Indians earning in Saudi Arabia) is **excluded from GDP but included in GNP**
- **India:** NFIA usually **negative** → GNP lower than GDP
# 2. Worked examples
- **Example 1:** GDPmp **500**, depreciation **50**, NFIA **-10**, NIT **40**
  - GNPmp = **490**; NNPmp = **440**; **NNPfc (NI) = 400**; NDPfc = **410**
- **Example 2:** GDP (MP) Rs **110** crore
  - Indians earn Rs **20** crore abroad; foreigners earn Rs **10** crore in India → NFIA = +10 → **GNP = 120**
  - Depreciation **15** → **NNP (MP) = 105**
  - Net indirect taxes **5** → **NI = NNP (FC) = 100**
> Exam angle: numerical questions give GDP, NFIA, depreciation and net indirect taxes and ask for NI.

## Included vs excluded in national income | from: S3
| **Included** | **Excluded** |
|---|---|
| Wages and salaries, rent, profits, dividends, interest on productive loans | **Transfer payments** - pensions, scholarships, unemployment allowance, prizes (no productive service rendered in return); added back when moving from NI to **personal income** |
| Value added at each stage | **Intermediate goods** (double counting) |
| Commission on resale [GK] | **Second-hand sales** (already counted when produced) [GK] |
| - | **Interest on public debt**, **tax revenue** (transfers, not factor payments) |

- ⚠ **Transfer earnings are not included** in national income - not payments for factor services

## Nominal vs real GDP and the GDP deflator | from: S4
# 1. Nominal vs real GDP
- **Nominal GDP:** At current prices (includes price change)
- **Real GDP:** At constant prices; values output at **base-year prices**; strips out inflation
  - Base year now **2022-23** (earlier **2011-12**)
  - Example: nominal GDP **1,000 → 1,200** while price index **100 → 120** → real GDP unchanged
- **Growth gap:** Nominal growth exceeds real by roughly the inflation rate
  - 2025-26: nominal **8.0%** vs real **7.4%** (estimates, old series)
# 2. GDP deflator
- **Formula:** **GDP deflator = Nominal GDP (current prices) / Real GDP (constant prices) x 100** = **GDP at current prices / GDP at constant prices x 100** (APPSC-G2 2025)
- **Comprehensiveness:** Most comprehensive measure of inflation (G2 2025 key)
  - Covers **all** goods and services in GDP (consumption, investment, government, exports), not a fixed basket like CPI/WPI
- **Purchasing power:** Rising deflator = higher price level → **purchasing power of money falls** / **lower purchasing power of money**
- **GNP deflator:** Same ratio for GNP
- **Double deflation (2022-23 series):** Deflates both output and input prices for manufacturing and agriculture, replacing single deflation
- **Sectoral deflators (2025-26):** **Agricultural GDP deflator grew fastest** → services → industry → manufacturing
  - Hence manufacturing's nominal share fell while real share stayed about **17-18%**

## Data — GVA headline, PPP and India's rank | from: S2, S5, S6
# 1. Headline measure
- **GVA at basic prices:** Headline sectoral measure since the **2011-12** series; still used in the **2022-23** series
  - CSO/NSO headline growth is on GVA at basic prices
- **Q1 2026-27:** Real GDP **Rs 81.36 lakh crore** vs real GVA **Rs 73.82 lakh crore** (difference = net product taxes) (Sep **2026**)

# 2. PPP exchange rates
- **PPP:** Compares GDP by pricing a **common basket** at each country's own prices → removes price-level differences
- **Estimation:** Under the **International Comparison Program (ICP)** of the UNSD
  - **World Bank** = global implementing agency
  - **NSO** = India's nodal agency
- **Reason:** Lower prices in India → PPP GDP much larger than market-rate GDP
# 3. India's rank
| **Measure** | **India's rank** |
|---|---|
| Nominal (market exchange rate) GDP | **4th** (overtook Japan in **2025**, about **USD 4.18 trillion**) |
| PPP GDP | **3rd**, after USA and China (ICP **2021**) |
