## Green Revolution technology — origins, timeline and phases | from: S0, S1
# 1. Scientific core and origins
- **Scientific core:** Use of **HYV seeds** (APPSC 2024)
- **Wheat:** From Mexican dwarf wheats (**Kalyan Sona**, **Sonalika**)
  - **Mexican wheat:** In **1966**, **18,000 t of Mexican wheat** imported + crossed with local varieties in Punjab
  - Wheat production rose from **5** to **17 MT**
- **Rice:** **IR-8** from **IRRI, Philippines**
- **Pioneer state:** **Punjab** = "Granary of India"
# 2. Timeline
- Hybrid seeds from **1960**, widely adopted by **1963**
- **Start:** Mid-1960s; HYV programme full-fledged from **1966**, after the **1965-66 and 1966-67 droughts** (foodgrains fell to **72.3** and **74.2 MT**) + **PL-480** dependence
- Launched during the **Annual Plans (1966-69)** → agricultural growth **4.16%**
- **Chronology (APPSC 2023):** **Intensive Agriculture District Programme (IADP, 1960-61)** → first Green Revolution period ended → **M.S. Swaminathan Research Foundation (1988)**
  - Answer order: **II, I, III**

| **Phase** | **Years** |
|---|---|
| Pre-Green Revolution | 1951-68 |
| Early Green Revolution | 1968-81 |
| Later Green Revolution | 1987-92 |

# 3. Second Green Revolution focus areas
- Pulses, oilseeds, millets (**Shree Anna**)
- **Precision farming** with drones and AI
- **Micro-irrigation**
- Integrated nutrient and pest management
- Climate-resilient varieties
- (UPSC Env p.256)

## Causes of yield gaps and pathways | from: S7
# 1. Causes and determinants

| **Factor** | **Effect / figure** |
|---|---|
| **Holdings** | **86%** small or marginal; average **1.08 ha** |
| **Rainfed** | About **50-51%** of net sown area |
| **Irrigation gap** | Rice ~**67%** irrigated vs pulses ~**26%** |
| **Inputs** | Skewed **NPK ratio**, low **SRR** / HYV seed replacement, lack of balanced fertiliser, mechanisation |
| **Post-harvest** | **30%** cold-chain losses |
| **Soybean** | Below **2 t/ha** in many districts (rainfed) |

- **Climate:**
  - Yields fall about **8% per 1°C**; a **20% rainfall deficit or 1 deg C rise cuts yields ~8%**
  - Wheat falls **6-10%**
  - **1 deg C** wipes ~**4 years** of rice/wheat productivity gains
- **El Niño years (pulses):**
  - Area / acreage = **-2 to -9%** (falls **2-9%**)
  - Yield falls **5-25%**; output **-6 to -30%**
# 2. Pathways
- **Seed:** Better seed through **SRR/VRR** and **genome editing**
- **Water:** Micro-irrigation, **DSR**, **SRI**
- **Nutrition:** Balanced nutrition via **Soil Health Card**
- **Mechanisation:** Via **CHCs**
