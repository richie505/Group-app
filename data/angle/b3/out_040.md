=== ROW 143 | Special Category Status - criteria, the 14th Finance Commission position and the controversy

## SCS — definition, origin and FIRSTs | from: S0
- **Origin:** Introduced **1969** on recommendation of **5th Finance Commission** (APPSC key, GS 2023; APPSC-GS 2023)
- **Legal status:** **Not** in the Constitution; executive/Planning Commission-era classification
| **FIRST** | **FACT** |
|---|---|
| First year of SCS | **1969** |
| Recommended by | **5th Finance Commission** |
| First three SCS states | **Jammu & Kashmir, Assam, Nagaland** |
| First Plan-transfer formula | **Gadgil formula** (1969) |
- **Gadgil Formula (1968-69):** Endorsed by **National Development Council (NDC)** for distributing Plan funds → SCS states got preferential central assistance
  - Plan transfers: **Gadgil formula** (1969) → later **Gadgil-Mukherjee formula** (1991)
  - SCS states got reserved first share of Normal Central Assistance
> Exam angle: "SCS was first introduced in 1969 on the recommendation of which FC?" - 5th.

## SCS criteria and benefits — features | from: S1, S2
# Criteria (features weighed)
- (1) **Hilly and difficult terrain**
- (2) **Low population density and/or sizeable tribal population**
- (3) **Strategic location along international borders**
- (4) **Economic and infrastructural backwardness**
- (5) **Non-viable state finances**
- **Summary:** Disadvantage from geography + economic and infrastructural backwardness
- **Rationale:** Larger tax share meant to cover the gap
# Benefits
| **Benefit** | **SCS state** | **General state** |
|---|---|---|
| Central loans | Concessional interest | Normal |
| Industry | Tax breaks and incentives (income tax/excise type) | None special |
| Unspent funds | Do not lapse; carried forward [GK] | Lapse |
- **Other examples:**
  - Consumer Welfare Fund seed money: **90:10** for SCS vs 75:25 (2026)
  - PMGSY: habitations of **250+** in SCS vs 500+ in plains (2026)
  - GST exemption threshold lower (₹10 lakh) for SCS
- ⚠ **90:10** = SCS vs 75:25 general (Consumer Welfare Fund); PMGSY **250+** SCS vs 500+ plains

|---|---|---|

=== ROW 144 | Special Assistance Package vs Special Category Status - Government's stand and measures taken

## Special Assistance Package — definition and Act provisions | from: S0
- **Announcement:** **September 2016** by Union FM **Arun Jaitley** in place of SCS
  - Also called **Special Assistance Measure (SAM)** or "Special Package"
- **Period:** Five years **2015-16 to 2019-20** = matching promised SCS period
| **Component** | **Content** |
|---|---|
| Special assistance | Money equal to the **extra central share** AP would have got as an SCS state (90% grant), routed through **Externally Aided Projects** (CDI) |
| Polavaram | National project (**Sec 90**); Centre bears **100% of the remaining irrigation-component cost from 1 Apr 2014 **(CDI) |
| Tax incentives | Industrial incentives under **Sec 94(1)** - promised 10-year tax holiday was delayed, diluted or subsumed in GST (CDI) |
| Backward areas | Special development package for **Rayalaseema and North Coastal** districts - **Sec 46(2)** (APP) |
| Revenue deficit | Centre to bridge the 2014-15 resource gap - **Sec 46** (CDI) |
- ⚠ **Sec 90** = Polavaram; **Sec 94(1)** = tax incentives; **Sec 46(2)** = backward areas; **Sec 46** = revenue deficit

## Externally Aided Projects and SASCI — how the schemes work | from: S1, S4
# Externally Aided Projects route
- **Mechanism:** State borrows from multilateral agencies (**World Bank, ADB**, etc.) → **Centre repays the loan and interest** up to SCS-equivalent amount (no open grant)
  - Share = SCS-equivalent 90%
  - ⚠ **Conflict:** some notes say "Centre to bear 40% cost for EAPs"; fuller account + package design give 90%
- **Problems:** Long approval cycles + heavy compliance → AP says red tape and delays
- **Current use:** **₹15,000 crore for Amaravati** raised via multilateral lenders
  - State wants grants vs Centre prefers loans and guarantees
# SASCI
- **SASCI:** Run by **Ministry of Finance**; began **Oct 2020**
  - **50-year interest-free** loans, over and above net borrowing ceiling
  - Outside 16th FC's **3% of GSDP** deficit ceiling
  - Capital works only; line ministries run sub-schemes
- !! Not in your sources: the SPV route proposed for special-assistance funds and NITI Aayog's role in the package.
