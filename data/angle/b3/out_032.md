## Network backbone — AP State FiberNet, BharatNet, APSWAN | from: S2
# 1. AP State FiberNet Ltd (APSFL)
- **Incorporation:** **October 2015**; launched **27 Dec 2017**
- **Licence:** Licensed as ISP + telecom + IPTV provider
- **AP Fiber Grid Phase-I:** **24,000 km** on an **IP-MPLS ring** (not GPON); NOC at **Visakhapatnam**
- **BharatNet Phase-II:** APSFL = state agency
  - **54,502 km**, **11,254 GPs**
  - ₹645.30 crore extra state investment
  - Cabinet cleared the **Amended BharatNet Programme** roadmap
- **Totals:** OFC **78,502 km**; 22,000+ government connections
- **Last mile:** 6,300+ local cable operators; triple play (internet, IPTV, voice)
- ⚠ **Trap:** Phase-I **24,000 km** + BharatNet **54,502 km** = OFC **78,502 km**
# 2. Government backbone
- **APSWAN (Nov 2010) → APSDWAN:** operational **1 May 2021** (some notes say introduced 2020)
- **SHQ:** Moved Hyderabad → **Velagapudi** in **2017**

## RTGS — command centre, data lake, Mana Mitra and tools | from: S4
# 1. RTGS and command centre
- **RTGS (from 2017):** Nodal agency for real-time monitoring, data analytics, AI, early warning
- **Command & Control Centre:** At the **State Secretariat**; AI, big data, GIS, IoT, dashboards
- **AI dashboard:** Unified AI governance dashboard for all department KPIs announced/planned
- **Review:** Singapore delegation reviewed RTGS (Jul 2026)
- **Budget 2026-27:** ~**₹100 crore** for RTGS
# 2. State Data Lake
- **Location:** **AP State Data Centre**; with Data Lens
- **Scope:** "single source of truth" for **40+ departments, 335+ HoDs and ~400 applications**
- **Use case:** Found **2,51,868 children** (**2.51 lakh**) missing from rice cards

## Universal angles | from: S15 | universal
- **Current relevance:**
  - Google 1 GW Vizag AI hub/data centre foundation (Apr 2026)
  - Aug 2026 APCRDA call for a 9 GW data-centre ecosystem; April 2026 quantum test beds
  - Quantum Valley launch due Jan 2027
  - Mana Mitra in state Budget (₹100 crore, 2026-27); handles ~30% of temple bookings
- **AP angle:**
  - Naidu era (1995-2004; 1990s IT push): HITEC City, eSeva, SMART governance model
  - After 2014: IT base rebuilt around Visakhapatnam
- **FIRST/LAST:**
  - eSeva (2001) = India's first integrated citizen service centre network
  - APSFL = first state-owned ISP
- **Criticism & debates:** India has no binding data-centre sustainability policy; Data Lake privacy + algorithmic-bias concerns
- **Comparative note:** Karnataka also runs GCC and data centre policies
