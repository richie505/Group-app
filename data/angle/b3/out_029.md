=== ROW 109 | Basic features of the Andhra Pradesh economy after bifurcation - GSDP, growth rate, sectoral shares

## Bifurcation (AP Reorganisation Act, 2014) → effects on the economy | from: S0
- **Bifurcation:** **2 June 2014** under the **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014**
  - Centre accepted the residuary State would be adversely affected; PM gave assurances in the **Rajya Sabha**
  - State says these are not fulfilled in full
| **Factor** | **Effect** |
|---|---|
| **Revenue loss** | **Hyderabad alone** ≈ **48%** of undivided State's total revenue of **₹70,548 crore** (2012-13 estimate) |
| Asymmetric division - AP got | Bulk of agrarian population, irrigation-project liabilities, power-sector deficits |
| Asymmetric division - Telangana kept | IT, commercial and industrial assets + the capital |
| Result | AP = **chronic revenue-deficit State** |
| **Ongoing strains** | Asset-liability allocation, institutional division, river waters → Centre-State and inter-State friction |
| **Capital city** | New capital (**Amaravati**) + many institutions built from scratch |
- **Area:** AP left with **1,60,205 sq km** + **7 Khammam mandals** transferred for **Polavaram**; undivided AP ≈ 2,75,000 sq km

## GSDP terms → meaning; compilation and base year | from: S2, S5
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **SDP** | Value of all goods and services produced within the State's boundaries in a year, **counted without duplication** |
| **GSDP** | GVA + product taxes - product subsidies |
| PCI | Computed on **NSDP** |
| **"Missing middle"** | Manufacturing only ≈ **11% of GSDP**; AP moving agriculture → services without a manufacturing-led stage |
- **Compiled by:** **Directorate of Economics & Statistics (DES), AP**, at current prices + constant **2011-12** prices
- **3 broad sectors** + **17** sub-sectors:
  - Agriculture (**5**): agriculture, horticulture, livestock, forestry and logging, fishing and aquaculture
  - Industry (**4**): mining, manufacturing, electricity-gas-water supply, construction
- **AP series base:** Still on **2011-12** - SES 2024-25 and SES 2025-26 both use it
  - Some notes say AP uses **2019-20** as base → an error; Survey tables are on 2011-12
- **India's new GDP series:** Base **2022-23**, released by MoSPI on **27 Feb 2026** (20 Mar 2026)
  - Revised **2023-24** growth to **7.2%** (from **9.2%**); **2024-25** to **7.1%** (from **6.5%**)
  - GDP's absolute size shrank by about **3-4%** vs old series (28 Feb and 12 Mar 2026)
- ⚠ Base year: AP = **2011-12**; India new series = **2022-23**; **2019-20** = wrong

## Per capita income — terms, formula and limits | from: S0, S5
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **PCI** | **Net State Domestic Product (NSDP) ÷ population** (net measure, after depreciation) |
| Per capita **GSDP** | Gross measure; current or constant prices |
| Current prices | Include inflation |
| Constant (2011-12) prices | Show real growth |
| **District Domestic Product (DDP)** | District GDP, compiled by State **Directorate of Economics and Statistics (DES)**; DES = also nodal agency for GSDP |
- ⚠ **Trap:** "GSDP ÷ population" is loose; the Survey's PCI is NSDP-based
- ⚠ **Trap:** never compare AP's per capita GSDP with India's per capita income - match gross with gross, net with net (per capita **income** = NSDP/NNI; per capita **GSDP** = gross)
- **PCI rises only when:** national income grows faster than population
- **Slow growth causes:** population growth, low productivity, low-income/low-saving "vicious circle"
- **Limits:** average hides inequality; ignores cost-of-living differences
