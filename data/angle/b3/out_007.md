=== ROW 34 | Measurement of unemployment - UPS, UPSS, CWS, CDS; PLFS methodology and recent findings

## Unemployment approaches — definitions and types (UPS, UPSS, CWS, CDS) | from: S1
| **Approach** | **Reference period** | **Employed if** | **Captures** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Usual Principal Status (UPS) | **365 days** | Major time (183+ days) spent working | **Chronic** (long-term) unemployment |
| Usual Principal + Subsidiary Status (UPSS/ps+ss) | 365 days | Principal status, or subsidiary work for at least **30 days** | Chronic; broadest count of workers |
| Current Weekly Status (CWS) | **7 days** | Worked at least **1 hour on any 1 day** of the week | Chronic + **seasonal** unemployment |
| Current Daily Status (CDS) | Each day of the 7 days | Unit = **half day**; 4+ hours = employed for the day | **Daily/intermittent**; most inclusive |

- **CDS:** Gives highest rate - counts days of unemployment even of persons "employed" under CWS
- **Census (not an NSS approach):**
  - **Main worker** = **183+** days a year
  - **Marginal worker** = < **183** days
- ⚠ **365 days – 7 days – half day** = **UPS – CWS – CDS**; UPSS adds subsidiary work of **30 days**
- ⚠ **183** days = UPS "major time" and also the Census main/marginal worker line - but Census is **not** an NSS approach
> Exam angle: odd-one-out lists slip in a non-method such as "Current Monthly Status" or "Rangarajan formula" among UPS/CWS/CDS.

## Terms → meaning: labour indicators (LFPR, WPR, UR) | from: S2
| **Indicator** | **Formula** |
|---|---|
| Labour force | Employed + unemployed (seeking/available for work) |
| UR | Unemployed / labour force x 100 |
| Proportion Unemployed (PU) | Unemployed / population |

- **Identity:** **WPR = LFPR x (1 - UR)**
  - UR measures only the unutilised part of the labour force
  - Not seeking work (students, homemakers) = outside labour force
- **Recessions:** LFPR usually falls (discouraged workers)
- ⚠ UR divides by **labour force**; PU divides by **population**
> Exam angle: "percentage of employed persons in the population" = **Worker Population Ratio** (APPSC 2023).

## PLFS — agency, launch, design and 2025 revamp | from: S4
- **Agency:** **National Statistical Office (NSO)**, **MoSPI**
- **Design:** **Standing Committee on Labour Force Statistics (SCLFS)**, set up on National Statistical Commission's advice
  - Aim: more frequent data than 5-yearly NSS rounds
- **Launch:** **April 2017**; first annual report (**2017-18**) released **May 2019**

| **Feature** | **Original design** | **Revamp from January 2025** |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | - | **Monthly** all-India CWS estimates (LFPR, WPR, UR) |
| Quarterly | **Quarterly** estimates: **urban** only (CWS) | Quarterly estimates extended to **rural** areas |
| Annual | **Annual** estimates: rural + urban, UPSS and CWS | Annual report on **calendar year** (Jan-Dec) |
| Sample | - | Sample raised to about **2,72,304 households** (**2.65 times**) |

- **Status:** Now standard NSO mechanism for India's unemployment rate (APPSC G2 2025)
- ⚠ Launched **April 2017**, but first report (**2017-18**) only in **May 2019**

## Terms → meaning (jobless growth, elasticity, gig work, feminisation) | from: S0, S3, S4

- **Scale:** GDP grew **6-7%** but formal job creation lagged
  - About **12 million** persons enter the job market each year; some notes say **7-10 million** young Indians a year (15 May 2026)

## MGNREGA, 2005 — Act, objective, entitlement and wages | from: S1
| **Item** | **Fact** |
|---|---|
| Act | National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, **2005**; passed **September 2005** (first proposed in 1991 by P.V. Narasimha Rao) |
| Launch | **2 February 2006** in **200** districts [GK] (APPSC: launched **2006**) |
| Phases | 130 more districts in 2007-08 [GK]; **all districts from 1 April 2008** |
| Renaming | "Mahatma Gandhi" added on **2 October 2009**[GK] (MGNREGA) |
| Guarantee | At least **100 days** of wage employment per **financial year** |
| Unit of entitlement | **Household** (not individual) |
| Eligibility | **Rural** households whose **adult** members volunteer for **unskilled manual** work (not urban, not skilled) |
| Wages | State-specific notified wage; paid within **15 days of the work done**[GK]; delay attracts compensation; DBT to Aadhaar-linked accounts |
| Ministry | **Ministry of Rural Development** |
| Constitutional anchor | Rights-based, **demand-driven**, anchored in **Article 41** (right to work, DPSP) |

- **Name and coverage:** Was **NREGA**; began in **200** districts
  - ⚠ **Trap:** APP notes say "Mahatma Gandhi Employment Guarantee Act" and 625 districts - both wrong; **625** = all-district coverage of **2008**
- **Objective:** "Enhancing **livelihood security** of rural households" via at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment (APPSC 2023 answer)
- **Other aims:** Durable asset creation; natural resource management (drought, deforestation, soil erosion); reducing distress migration
- **World Development Report 2014 (World Bank):** Called it a "stellar example of rural development"
- ⚠ **2005** Act - **2006** launch - **2008** all districts - **2009** "Mahatma Gandhi" added
> Exam angle: APPSC 2017 - mandate = "100 days to every **household** whose adult members volunteer", not "100 days to every adult".

## MGNREGA — key features and monitoring | from: S2
# 1. Key features
- **Women:** At least **one-third** of beneficiaries; actual share consistently **above 50%** of person-days
- **Unemployment allowance:** If work not given within **15 days** of application
  - At least **1/4** of wage for first **30** days
  - **1/2** thereafter
- **Permissible works:** Water conservation, land development, rural connectivity, durable community assets
- **Convergence:** **90/95 person-days** to **PMAY-G** beneficiaries via **AwaasSoft-NREGASoft** link
- **Scale:** **5-6 crore** households + **200-250 crore** person-days a year
# 2. Monitoring
| **Tool** | **Use** |
|---|---|
| **Bhuvan** mobile platform (**ISRO**) | Geo-tagging - "GeoMGNREGA" (APPSC 2018) |
| **NREGASoft** | MIS |
| **Gram Sabha** | Mandatory social audit; Gram Sabhas plan and monitor works |

- ⚠ **15 days** twice: wages within 15 days of work; allowance if no work within 15 days of application

## Acts compared — MGNREGA 2005 vs VB-G RAM G Act 2025 | from: S3
- **Status (2026): MGNREGA has been replaced** by **Viksit Bharat - Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025 (VB-G RAM G)**
  - Passed by Parliament **18 Dec 2025**
  - Effective + MGNREGA repealed from **1 July 2026** (13 May 2026; 2 Sep 2026)

| **Aspect** | **MGNREGA 2005** | **VB-G RAM G Act 2025** |
|---|---|---|
| Guarantee | 100 days/household | **125 days**/household (operated over 305 days) |
| Seasonal pause | None | States may pause works up to **60 days** in sowing/harvest |
| Funding | Mostly Central, demand-driven | **60:40** (90:10 NE/Himalayan States, UTs); normative State-wise allocation |
| Wage payment | Within 15 days | **Weekly** or within a fortnight |
| Works | Many scattered categories | **4 priority areas**: water security, core rural infrastructure, livelihood infrastructure, extreme-weather/disaster works |
| Admin cost ceiling | 6% | **9%** |

- **Other VB-G RAM G points:**
  - Statutory wage floor **₹300/day**
  - Job cards → **Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Cards**
  - No contractors; assets on **VB-NRIS** stack
  - Central and State **Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Councils**
  - Unemployment allowance retained
- ⚠ **125 – 60 – 9%** = guarantee days – seasonal pause – admin ceiling (VB-G RAM G)
