=== ROW 107 | Union Budget - structure, process, key documents; highlights of the latest Union Budget

## Union Budget — FIRSTs, timeline and records | from: S0
| **FIRST / event** | **Fact** |
|---|---|
| First budget of India | **James Wilson**, **7 April 1860** |
| 1947-48 budget | Liaquat Ali Khan (interim Government) |
| First budget of independent India | **R.K. Shanmukham Chetty**, **26 November 1947** |
| Separate Railway Budget | From **1924** (Acworth Committee, 1921) |
| Railway Budget merged | **2017** (Budget 2017-18) - 92-year practice ended |
| Date moved to **1 February** | **2017** (from last working day of February) |
| Plan/non-plan distinction removed | **2017-18** (Expenditure Management Commission) - same year as the Railway merger and the 1 February date |
| Outcome Budget / Gender Budget | **2005-06** |
| Paperless (digital) Budget | Since **2021** - budget presented digitally |
- **Named budgets:** **1973-74** "Black Budget"; **1997-98** "Dream Budget"
- **Etymology:** French bougette ("little bag"); word not in the Constitution
- ⚠ **2017** = three changes together: **Railway merger** + **1 February** date + **plan/non-plan** ended (2017-18)

## Budget types and fiscal terms → meaning | from: S1, S8
| **Type** | **Meaning / key point** |
|---|---|
| Balanced | Receipts = expenditure (zero revenue deficit) |
| Surplus | Receipts > expenditure - curbs inflation, may slow growth |
| Deficit | Expenditure > receipts (India's normal case) - counter-cyclical tool, risks inflation and debt |
| **Zero-based** | Every activity justified afresh from zero each period - **Peter Pyhrr**; first used in government by **Jimmy Carter** |
| **Performance** | Links spending to programmes, activities/work done and results - **First Hoover Commission** (USA); ARC recommended it for India; India from the late 1960s on ARC advice |
| **Outcome** | Tracks outputs and outcomes, not outlays (Output-Outcome Framework; now a budget document) - introduced **2005**, from **2005-06** |
| **Gender** | Allocations by gender where disparities are chronic; tracks women-specific (Part A, 100%), 30-99% (Part B) and below 30% (Part C) allocations; Statement 13, since **2005-06** |
| Line-item | Traditional; control over items of expenditure |
| Green | Tracks environment-related spending |
> Exam angle: APPSC-G2 2025 - justifying the entire budget from the beginning = **zero-base budgeting**.
- **Output-based spending:** **16th Finance Commission** ties Centrally Sponsored Schemes to measurable outcomes
# 1. Deficit formulas
- **FD** = total expenditure - receipts excluding borrowings
- **PD = FD - interest payments**
- **RD** = revenue expenditure - revenue receipts

## Constitutional Articles and key budget documents | from: S2, S5, S7
| **Article** | **Provision (BUD 2026-27)** |
|---|---|
| **112** | **Annual Financial Statement** (the Budget) |
| **113** | Procedure on estimates - **demands for grants** |
| **115** | Supplementary, additional or excess grants |
| **116** | **Vote on account**, vote of credit, exceptional grants |
| **117** | Special provisions as to financial bills |

- **Other Articles:** **Art. 112(3)** charged expenditure; **Art. 114(3)** Appropriation Act; **Art. 110(1)(a)** Finance Bill; **Art. 266** Consolidated Fund; **Art. 266(2)** Public Account; **Art. 267** Contingency Fund
| **Document** | **Content / basis** |
|---|---|
| **Annual Financial Statement** | **Art. 112**; estimated receipts and expenditure in the **Consolidated Fund, Contingency Fund and Public Account**; "Budget" is not a word used in the Constitution |
| **Demands for Grants** | **Art. 113**; usually one per ministry/department, voted by Lok Sabha; revenue + capital + loans; charged items (like interest) shown separately, not voted |
| **Appropriation Bill** | **Art. 114(3)**; authorises withdrawal from CFI |
| **Finance Bill** + Memorandum | **Art. 110(1)(a)**; tax proposals and explanations |
| **Receipt Budget** | Detailed estimates and trends of tax, non-tax and capital receipts [GK] |
| **Expenditure Budget / Expenditure Profile** | Scheme- and ministry-wise spending |
| **Outcome Budget** | Output-outcome framework for schemes |
| FRBM statements | MEFS; MTFPS cum FPSS (Macro-Economic Framework, Fiscal Policy Strategy, Medium-Term Fiscal Policy) |
- **Other documents:** Budget at a Glance
- **Date:** AFS on the "first working day of February"; in practice **1 February** even on a Sunday (**2026**)
- **Prepared by:** **Budget Division, Department of Economic Affairs**, Ministry of Finance, with NITI Aayog + spending ministries
  - DEA also prepares budgets of States under President's Rule and UTs (IYB 2026)
- **Revenue (tax) estimates:** Department of Revenue (**CBDT/CBIC**)
# 1. Funds
- **Public Account (Art. 266(2)):** Provident funds, small savings, deposits → government acts as banker; **no parliamentary appropriation** needed for withdrawals
- **Contingency Fund of India (Art. 267):** Corpus raised to **₹30,000 crore** from FY **2021-22**

## Charged vs voted expenditure — items | from: S5
- **Basis:** **Art. 112(3)**
- **Charged expenditure:** **Not voted** by Lok Sabha (non-votable), only **discussable**/can be **discussed** (**Art. 113(1)**); comes straight from the Consolidated Fund; shown separately within Demands
- **Voted:** The rest - needs LS vote
# 1. Charged items
- Emoluments + office expenses of the **President**
- Salaries of **Chairman/Deputy Chairman (RS)** and **Speaker/Deputy Speaker (LS)**
- **Debt charges** (interest, redemption)
- Salaries, allowances, pensions of **Supreme Court judges** (**SC judges**); **pensions of HC judges**
- Salary etc. of the **CAG** + administrative expenses of his office
- UPSC chairman/members + office expenses
- Sums to satisfy court decrees/judgments/awards
- Anything Parliament declares charged by law
- ⚠ **SC judges** = salaries + pensions charged; **HC judges** = only **pensions** charged

## Receipts and expenditure → classification | from: S3, S4
| **Receipt** | **Nature** | **Examples** |
|---|---|---|
| **Tax revenue** (revenue receipt) | No liability, no asset reduction; recurring | Direct (income tax, corporation tax), indirect (GST, customs, excise) |
| **Non-tax revenue** (revenue receipt) | No liability, no asset reduction | Interest receipts (interest on loans given), dividends and profits (RBI surplus, PSU dividends), spectrum, fees, fines, licence fees, grants |
| **Debt** capital receipts | Create liability | Market borrowings / market loans, T-Bills, external loans, small savings/PF, borrowing from RBI |
| **Non-debt** capital receipts | Reduce assets | **Recovery of loans**, **disinvestment**, other receipts |
- **Correct sequence:** **Revenue Receipts → Tax Revenue → Non-tax Revenue → Capital Receipts** (APPSC-GS 2025 key)
- **PSU stake sale:** **Not** a revenue receipt → reduces government assets
- **"Paper taxes":** Wealth tax, gift tax, estate duty → never large revenue, now abolished
| **Pair** | **Distinction** |
|---|---|
| **Revenue vs capital** | Revenue creates no asset, maintains assets (salaries, pensions, interest, subsidies, grants to states); capital creates assets or reduces liabilities (capex, roads, defence equipment, loans given incl. to States, debt repayment) |
| Plan vs non-plan | Old split tied to Five-Year Plans. **Scrapped from Budget 2017-18**[GK] |
| Developmental vs non-developmental | Economic and social services vs interest, defence, administration, pensions [GK] |
- **Revenue budget:** Revenue receipts + revenue expenditure
- **Capital budget:** Capital receipts + capital expenditure (borrowings, disinvestment, loans, asset creation)
- **Accounts:** **Revenue account** = current year transactions; **Capital account** = assets and liabilities
  - Mnemonic: "Revenue = Running the government; Capital = Creating assets"
- **Guiding rule:** Borrow only to invest, not to fund current spending
# 1. Plan vs non-plan expenditure
- **Plan expenditure:** On Central Plans (Five Year Plans) + central assistance for State/UT Plans
- **Non-plan expenditure:** The larger part → **interest payments, defence, subsidies, salaries, pensions** + maintenance of assets created in earlier Plans
  - **Interest payments** = classic example (APPSC-G2 2025 key)
- **End of distinction:** **Plan/non-plan distinction removed** (**Expenditure Management Commission**) → replaced by revenue/capital classification from **2017-18**
> Exam angle: APPSC-G2 2025 - non-plan expenditure = **interest payments**.
# 2. Development and committed expenditure
- **Development expenditure:** Education, health, employment, industry
- **Defence:** "Committed" spending → little scope for cuts

## Budget process — stages and parliamentary control terms | from: S6, S7
# 1. Preparation and presentation
- **Budget circular:** Issued in **September**
- **Halwa ceremony:** Marks printing; printing begins at **North Block**
- **Houses:** Presented in Lok Sabha; Rajya Sabha can discuss but **cannot vote on demands**
- **Budget vs Finance Bill:** Budget = estimate statement; Finance Bill = legal instrument for taxes
# 2. Six stages
- **Six stages:** Presentation → general discussion → **Departmental standing committee** scrutiny → voting on demands → **Appropriation Bill** → **Finance Bill**
| **Stage** | **Key facts** |
|---|---|
| 1. Presentation | FM in LS; **1 February** since 2017 (earlier last working day of Feb; Budget 2026-27 on 1 Feb 2026); no discussion that day; Economic Survey a day or more earlier |
| 2. General discussion | Both Houses, **3-4 days**; no cut motions or voting |
| 3. Departmental committee scrutiny | DRSCs examine demands of their ministries during recess [GK] |
| 4. Voting on demands (**Art. 113**) | **Only Lok Sabha**; demands need President's recommendation; RS cannot vote |
| 5. Appropriation Bill | Art. 114 - see below |
| 6. Finance Bill | Gives effect to tax proposals; a **Money Bill** under Art. 110(1)(a) |
# 3. Control terms → meaning
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Guillotine** | On the last allotted day, all remaining/outstanding demands are **put to vote without discussion** (PYQ APPSC-GS 2025) |
| **Policy cut** | Reduce to **₹1** |
| **Economy cut** | By a stated amount |
| **Token cut** | By **₹100** |
| **Vote on account (Art. 116)** | Advance grant for part of the year, about **two months** (one-sixth); **3-5 months** in election years; covers only expenditure, until the budget is passed |
| **Interim budget** | Full statement of receipts and expenditure (accounts) by an outgoing government in an election year |
- ⚠ **Policy cut** = **₹1**; **Token cut** = **₹100**
- **Vote on account:** Rarely needed since the 1 February shift, except in **interim budget** years
  - ⚠ **Trap:** Still used in election years → has **not** become ineffectual after the move to 1 February
# 4. Appropriation and Finance Bills
- **Appropriation Bill (Art. 114):** No amendment may vary a voted grant, change its destination or vary charged expenditure
  - **Art. 114(3):** No withdrawal from the Consolidated Fund without an Appropriation Act; **Art. 266** Consolidated Fund
- **Finance Bill:** Must be enacted within **75 days** (**Provisional Collection of Taxes Act, 1931**)
- **Supplementary demands (Art. 115):** Lok Sabha approved the **second batch for 2025-26** with an **Economic Stabilisation Fund of Rs 1 lakh crore**

=== ROW 108 | Economic Survey - structure and key themes of the latest Survey

## Economic Survey — preparer, FIRSTs and format | from: S0
- **Prepared by:** **Economic Division, Department of Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance**, under the CEA
- **FIRSTs:** First Survey **1950-51**; presented separately from the Budget since **1964**
- **Presentation:** In **both Houses** during the Budget Session, **before the Union Budget**
  - A convention, **not a constitutional obligation** (unlike the Annual Financial Statement, **Art. 112**) (12 Apr 2026)
- **Format:**
  - Two volumes in the Subramanian years; single volume + statistical appendix since **2021-22**
  - ES 2025-26 reconfigured to **17 chapters** (16 + Chapter 16 in two parts); added AI and urbanisation chapters
- ⚠ Survey = convention; Budget (AFS) = **Art. 112** obligation
