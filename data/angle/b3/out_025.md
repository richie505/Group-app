# 2. Minimum Alternate Tax
- **MAT:** Taxes "zero-tax companies" on book profits (15%)

## GST Council — composition, voting and functions | from: S2
# 1. Nature
- **Status:** **Constitutional body** under **Art. 279A**
  - Created by the Constitution; never a statutory body (APPSC G1 2024)
  - Formed by the **President** in **September 2016**
> Exam angle: APPSC-G2 2025 and G1 2024 tested "269A vs 279A" and "statutory then constitutional" - both statements are false.
# 2. Composition and working
- **Chair:** Union Finance Minister
- **Members:** Union MoS (Revenue/Finance) + Finance/Taxation Minister of each State and UT with legislature (UPSC)
- **Vice-Chairperson:** Chosen by State members from among themselves

| **Voting rule** | **Value** |
|---|---|
| Quorum | **quorum = one-half** of total members [GK] |
| Centre's weight | **1/3** of votes cast |
| All States together | **2/3** of votes cast |
| Decision | **3/4 majority** of weighted votes of members present and voting |

- **Meetings:** **55th** (21 Dec 2024, Jaisalmer)
# 3. Functions and binding nature
- **Recommends:** Rates, exemptions, thresholds, model laws, revenue sharing, dispute resolution
- **Character:** Institution of **cooperative federalism**
- **Union of India v. Mohit Minerals (2022)**: Recommendations **persuasive, not binding** on Union and States
  - Parliament and State legislatures have simultaneous power to legislate on GST

## Nature and objectives; taxes subsumed vs outside GST | from: S3, S4
# 1. Objectives and nature
- **Objectives:**
  - Replace complex, fragmented structure with a **single destination-based tax on value addition** = "one nation, one tax" (APPSC 2024)
  - End **cascading** (tax on tax)
  - One nation-one market; transparency and compliance (APPSC-GS 2024 answer)
- **Levels:** Applies at both central and state levels (APPSC 2023)
- **Nature:** **Multi-stage, destination-based consumption tax** on every value addition
- **Taxable event:** **Supply**
# 2. Subsumed vs retained
| **Subsumed - Central** | **Subsumed - State** (**State taxes subsumed**) |
|---|---|
| Central Excise Duty, Additional Excise duties | State VAT / Sales Tax, Central Sales Tax |
| Service Tax | Purchase Tax, Luxury Tax, Entry Tax (all forms), Octroi |
| Additional Customs Duty (**CVD**), Special Additional Duty (**SAD**) on imports | Entertainment Tax (except by local bodies) |
| Central cesses and surcharges on supply (e.g., KKC, SBC) | Taxes on advertisements, lotteries, betting, gambling |

- **Subsumed:** **8 Central + 9 State taxes** (UPSC)
  - "Declared goods of special importance" concept dropped
- **Not subsumed:** **Basic Customs Duty**, stamp duty, electricity duty, motor vehicle tax, property tax, alcoholic liquor (State excise), petroleum products
  - ⚠ **Trap:** Some notes list stamp duty as "merged" - it was not
# 3. Items outside GST
- **Outside GST:**
  - **Alcoholic liquor for human consumption** (**alcohol for human consumption**)
  - Five petroleum products: **crude, petrol (motor spirit), high-speed diesel, natural gas, ATF** (UPSC) = **crude, petrol, diesel, ATF, natural gas**
  - **Electricity** supply
  - Real estate (stamp duty)
- **Petroleum:** Constitutionally within GST; levied only from a date the **GST Council recommends**
- **Alcohol:** Excluded by the Constitution itself; stays with States
- ⚠ Petroleum = in the Constitution, waiting for Council date; alcohol = excluded by the Constitution
- **PYQ:** APPSC-G2 2025 - no GST on petrol/diesel, liquor, electricity to consumers, services of charitable entities → "**All of the above**"

## Structure and slabs — CGST, SGST, IGST, GST 2.0 | from: S5
# 1. Components

# 3. Rate-change procedure
- **Procedure:** Rates changed by **government notification on GST Council recommendation**, not by amending the Acts
- **Ceilings in the Acts:** **CGST 20%**; **IGST 40%**

## Rate structure — multiple rates and GST 2.0 | from: S3
# 1. Drawbacks of multiple rates
- **Biggest drawback:** **Misclassification of goods and services** + classification disputes (APPSC-G1 2017 key; APPSC-G1 2017 answer; Aug 2025)
  - Near-identical products in different slabs → disputes + litigation
  - Four-slab structure was meant to be transitional
  - Pending reform (Aug 2025): "standardise product classification"
> Exam angle: "most important disadvantage of multiple rates" and IDS definition; the current slab set (5/18/40) vs the old four-slab set.
# 2. Rate structure - GST 2.0 rationalisation
- **Original structure (2017):** Slabs **5%, 12%, 18%, 28%** + exempt goods
  - **Compensation cess** over 28% on luxury/sin goods (luxury cars, aerated drinks, pan masala, tobacco)
- **GST 2.0 (NextGen GST reforms):** Approved at **56th GST Council meeting, 3-4 September 2025**; effective **22 September 2025** (also noted 10 Sep 2026)

| **Item** | **Before 22 Sep 2025** | **From 22 Sep 2025** |
|---|---|---|
| Slabs | 0, 5, 12, 18, 28% (+ cess) | **0, 5% (merit), 18% (standard), 40% (demerit)** |
| Old 12% slab | - | ~**99%** of items moved to 5% |
| Old 28% slab | - | ~**90%** moved to 18%; rest to 40% |
| 40% demerit goods | - | ultra-luxury cars/SUVs, yachts, private aircraft, tobacco, pan masala, online gaming |

- **Special rates:** **0.25%** rough diamonds; **3%** gold/silver/jewellery
- **Examples (ES 2025-26):**
  - Tractors 12%→5%
  - Small cars, bikes ≤350cc, ACs 28%→18%
  - Bikes >350cc + big cars → 40%
- ⚠ Old **12%** → **99%** to 5%; old **28%** → **90%** to 18%

## Terms → meaning: inverted duty, ITC fraud, destination principle | from: S4, S6
# 1. Inverted Duty Structure (IDS)
- **Inverted Duty Structure (IDS):** Input tax rate > output tax rate → blocked credit; ITC piles up, working capital blocked
  - Law allows **refund of unutilised ITC**
  - Domestic refunds surged **72.6%** in Aug 2026 due to inverted duties
# 2. ITC fraud
- **ITC fraud:** Bogus entities issue invoices without supply
  - AP-Telangana-Karnataka network used fake registrations + scrap bills to claim ITC (3 Jan 2026)
  - Countered by e-invoicing, UPI/Aadhaar tracking, AI scrutiny
# 3. Destination principle — consuming vs producing States
- **Principle:** GST = **destination-based consumption tax** → revenue to State where goods/services are **consumed**, not produced
- **IGST:** On inter-state supply (Art. 269A); levied by Centre and apportioned; destination state gets state share
- **Effect:** Consuming states gain; manufacturing states (e.g., Maharashtra, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka) feared loss
  - Key reason for 5-year compensation guarantee
  - Destination principle favours **consuming States**; AP (a consuming State) benefits via IGST settlement
# 4. Other asks
- **Coverage gap:** **Petroleum and electricity** still outside GST
- **Other asks:** **GST 3.0** simplification (CII AP, 1 Feb 2026); sector pleas e.g. notebook makers hit by Sep 2025 rate revision (9 Sep 2026)
