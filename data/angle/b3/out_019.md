=== ROW 77 | India and the WTO - agreements, ministerial conferences, public stockholding, fisheries subsidies, appellate body crisis

## GATT, Uruguay Round and WTO — origin, basics and FIRSTs | from: S0, S1, S5
# 1. From GATT to WTO

| **Stage** | **Year** | **Fact** |
|---|---|---|
| **GATT** | Signed **1947**, in force **1948** | **23 countries**; **8 rounds** of multilateral trade negotiations |
| **Uruguay Round** | **September 1986**-**1993** | **8th round**; **Punta del Este** launch; **123** contracting parties |
| **Dunkel Draft** | Tabled **December 1991** (some notes say **20 October 1991**) | Proposals by **Arthur Dunkel**, GATT DG + chair of the **Trade Negotiations Committee**, for the Uruguay Round multilateral trade agreements |
| **Marrakesh Agreement** | **April 1994** | Created the **WTO on 1 January 1995**, replacing the GATT system |

- ⚠ **Trap:** "**110** countries" is the later membership, not the founding number
- **Uruguay Round aims:** Cut farm subsidies, open services, cover investment and **IPR**
- **GATT now:** Survives as part of WTO agreements
# 2. WTO basics
- **Basics:**
  - Est. **1 Jan 1995**; HQ **Geneva**
  - **166 members** (Apr 2026)
  - DG **Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala** (Mar 2026)
- **Third pillar:** WTO = **third pillar** of the world economy, with the **IMF** and **World Bank**
- **India's rank:** **18th** in merchandise exports, **1.8%** share (**WTO Global Trade Outlook, April 2025**)
# 3. FIRSTs

| **FIRST** | **FACT** |
|---|---|
| India | Founding member of both **GATT** and **WTO** |
| AFS | WTO's **first agreement with an environmental-sustainability objective** |
| New agreements | Only two in **30 years** - **TFA** and **AFS** |

## Terms → meaning: MFN, AoA boxes, de minimis, tariffication, Peace Clause | from: S1, S3, S4
# 1. Trade principles

| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **MFN** | No discrimination among trading partners |
| **National treatment** | Imports treated like domestic goods once inside |

- US unilateral tariffs (**2025-26**) violate MFN and bound-rate obligations
# 2. Agreement on Agriculture boxes
- **AoA (1995):** Three pillars = **domestic support, market access, export competition**

| **Box** | **What it covers** | **Treatment** |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Research, disease control, infrastructure, food security, direct income support not linked to production | Not trade-distorting; no limits |
| Blue | Direct payments that **limit** production (production-limiting programmes) | Trade-distorting but exempt from cuts |
| Amber | All other support tied to prices/quantities - **MSP**, power, fertiliser, seed, irrigation subsidies (measured as AMS) | **Trade-distorting**; subject to reduction |

> Exam angle: "Amber box subsidies refer to" - trade-distorting support.
- ⚠ **Distractors:** "Red box", "Orange box", "Yellow box" are not AoA boxes
  - A "red light" category exists only for prohibited subsidies under the **SCM Agreement**
# 3. Other terms

| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **De minimis** | Amber support allowed without penalty - developed = **5%** of value of agricultural output; developing = **10%** |
| **Tariffication** | Non-tariff barriers converted to tariffs and cut - developed **36%** over **6 years** vs developing **24%** over **10 years** |
| **SCM Agreement subsidies** | Prohibited, actionable (countervailing duty), non-actionable (R&D, regional, environmental) |
| **Peace Clause (Bali MC9, 2013)** | Shields developing-country public stockholding from disputes until a permanent solution; interim protection - no legal challenge even if food-security procurement breaches AoA limits |
| **PSH** | Public stockholding for food security |

=== ROW 78 | FDI and FPI policy in India - routes, sectoral caps, trends; FTAs and CEPAs signed by India

## FDI vs FPI — definitions, types and classification of foreign capital | from: S0, S1
# 1. Distinction

| **Basis** | **FDI** | **FPI/FII** |
|---|---|---|
| Threshold | **>=10%** equity in a listed company, or any stake in an unlisted one | **<10%** in a listed company |
| Nature | Long-term, management control, technology | Short-term, liquid, "hot money" |
| Route/regulator | Automatic/Government route; DPIIT + RBI | SEBI-registered FPI; SEBI + RBI |

- **FII vs FPI:** Essentially the same; FPI opened in India in **1992**
# 2. Classification of foreign capital
- **Foreign capital:** Includes **FDI, trade credit, NRI deposits and ECBs** - all four (APPSC-GS 2019 key)

| **Form** | **Classification** |
|---|---|
| FDI | non-debt, long-term, management control (>=10%) |
| FPI/FII | non-debt, portfolio (<10%), liquid; FII and FPI are essentially the same |
| ECB, trade credit, FCCB | debt |
| NRI deposits (FCNR(B), NRE) | banking capital (debt) |
| ADR/GDR | equity raised abroad through depository receipts, counted as FPI [GK]; counted as portfolio investment in BoP data |
| Participatory notes | offshore derivative instruments issued by registered FPIs to overseas investors [GK]; issued by registered FIIs/FPIs to overseas investors not registered with SEBI → portfolio channel |

# 3. Types of FDI

| **Type** | **Meaning** | **Example** |
|---|---|---|
| Greenfield | New facility | Hyundai, Sriperumbudur |
| Brownfield | Acquisition | Vodafone-Hutchison Essar |

- ⚠ **10%** line: **>=10%** = FDI; **<10%** = FPI

## NBFCs — registration, categories and features vs banks | from: S5, S6
# 1. Registration and categories
- **Registration:** Under the **Companies Act**

| **Category** | **Minimum Net Owned Fund** |
|---|---|

# 2. NBFC vs bank - restrictions on NBFCs

| **Basis** | **Bank** | **NBFC** |
|---|---|---|
| Demand deposits / cheques | Yes | **No demand deposits; cannot issue cheques on itself** |
| Payment and settlement system | Part of it | **Not part of it** (LENSD, Aug 2026) |
| Deposit insurance (DICGC) | Covered | Not covered (no deposit-insurance cover) |
| CRR / SLR | Must keep both | No CRR; deposit-taking NBFCs keep liquid assets instead [GK] |
| RBI LAF (repo) access | Yes | No [GK] |

# 3. Scale-Based Regulation (SBR)
- **Four layers (rising regulation):** **Base (BL) → Middle (ML) → Upper (UL) → Top (TL)**

| **Layer** | **Who / what applies** |
|---|---|
| Base | Small non-deposit NBFCs [GK] |
| Middle | All deposit-taking NBFCs; HFCs, IFCs, CICs [GK] |
| Upper | Identified by RBI; enhanced, bank-like norms for at least **5 years**; **mandatory listing within 3 years** |
| Top | Kept empty; only for a sharp rise in systemic risk [GK] |

- **NBFC-UL list 2026-27:** **17 companies**, including **Tata Sons** (earlier 15 as of Apr 2026)
  - RBI **rejected Tata Sons' bid to surrender its NBFC licence** and asked it to prepare to list (TH, 13 Sep 2026)
- **Proposed reform:** **Single asset threshold** (simple **₹1 lakh crore** asset-size test for UL) in place of the parametric scoring model + bringing government NBFCs in
