=== ROW 128 | Skill development and employment in AP - AP State Skill Development Corporation, employment schemes

## Skilling institutions → functions and data (APSSDC, Naipunyam, ITIs) | from: S0
# 1. APSSDC
- **APSSDC:** AP State Skill Development Corporation = nodal agency for placement-linked training
  - Via **Skill Hubs, Skill Spokes**, colleges, Sector Skill Councils
  - Partnered with tech companies for **50 skill hubs** in rural colleges (Jan 2026)
- **Restructuring:** All skill departments merging into one **Commissionerate of Skill, Employment and Training** under "One District - One Skill" (24 Apr 2026)
- **Placement rule:** **70% placement** threshold for all State schemes

## Government recruitment and overseas employment | from: S5, S6
# 1. Government recruitment

- !! Not in your sources: the current staff strength of the secretariats.
# 3. Overseas employment — OMCAP
- **OMCAP (Overseas Manpower Company AP Ltd):** Nodal agency for overseas recruitment under the **Emigration Act, 1983**
  - Places workers in Germany, Japan, GCC, Europe
  - Drives: UAE taxi drivers (Jul 2026), Saudi Arabia (L&T)
# 4. Gulf migration → issues
- **Gulf migration:** Heavy among women from **Konaseema** (20 Mar 2026)
  - Housemaids must be recruited via OMCAP, not private agents
  - Over 90% of abuse complaints from workers not recruited via OMCAP
