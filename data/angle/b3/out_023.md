## Terms → meaning: stress indicators and causes of NPAs | from: S0, S1
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Interest Coverage Ratio** | **EBIT** / interest payable |
| **Twin Balance Sheet problem** | Over-leveraged corporates + capital-starved banks → stalls private capex, squeezes **MSME** credit |
| **Evergreening** | Fresh loans to repay old ones → NPA stays hidden; the **AQR** exposed this |
| **PCR** | Provisions / gross NPA; higher PCR → more resilient bank |
- **Interest Coverage Ratio:** Below **1** → firm cannot service its interest
  - Lenders use it to judge a borrower's present and emerging risk
- **Other causes:** Loan frauds and diversion of funds, weak credit appraisal, slow recovery machinery, corporate over-leverage

## Recovery forums — Lok Adalats and Debt Recovery Tribunals | from: S2
# 1. Lok Adalats for small NPAs
- **Status:** **Statutory forums** under the **Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987**
- **Feature:** Quick, fee-free settlement of small loans by compromise [GK]
- **National Lok Adalat (13 Sep 2026):** **DRT Visakhapatnam bench** settled **13** cases worth **₹46.11 crore**
# 2. Debt Recovery Tribunals
- **Law:** Recovery tribunals under the **Recovery of Debts Due to Banks and FIs Act, 1993 (RDDBFI)**[GK]; also used under **SARFAESI**
- **Structure:** **39 DRTs** and **5 DRATs** (Debt Recovery Appellate Tribunals)
- **Threshold:** Debts **above ₹20 lakh**
- **e-DRT:** Launched **1 Oct 2018**; e-filing mandatory from **1 Feb 2023**
- ⚠ **1987** = Legal Services Authorities Act (Lok Adalats); **1993** = RDDBFI (DRTs); **2002** = SARFAESI

## SARFAESI Act 2002 — sections → provisions, ARCs | from: S3
- **Full name:** **Securitisation and Reconstruction of Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest Act, 2002**
- **Basis:** **Narasimham Committee**
- **Core feature:** Banks enforce security on NPAs **without court intervention** (recovery **without court**)
| **Section** | **Provision** |
|---|---|
| **Sec 13(2)** | Lenders holding **60%** of dues issue a **60-day** demand notice |
| **Sec 13(4)** | Then take possession, take over management or sell |
| **Sec 13(8)** | Borrower can redeem by paying full dues only until the auction notice is published |
| **Sec 17** | Appeal to the **DRT** within **45 days** |
| **Sec 18** | Bars civil courts |
- **ARCs:** Buy NPAs/bad loans from banks at a discount against **security receipts**; registered with and regulated by **RBI** under SARFAESI [GK]
- **AP angle:** Division bench of the **AP High Court** clarified **Sec 13(8)** redemption, auction-stay rules and auction protocols (May 2026)
- ⚠ **60%** of lenders + **60-day** notice; appeal within **45 days**

## IBC 2016 — definitions, enactment and FIRSTs | from: S5
# 1. Definitions
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Insolvency** | The **state** of being unable to pay debts as they fall due (financial state) |
| **Bankruptcy** | The **legal** declaration/process for an insolvent individual or firm |
- ⚠ **Trap:** BPSC 2023 swapped the two definitions → those statements are wrong/false
# 2. Enactment - **IBC 2016 as structural reform**
- **Enactment:** Assent **May 2016**; enacted **28 May 2016** on the **T.K. Viswanathan Committee** (**Bankruptcy Law Reforms Committee, 2015**)

## Institutions under IBC — NCLT, NCLAT, DRT, IBBI | from: S5
# **NCLT/NCLAT - parent Act, adjudicating authority under IBC, appeal route**
| **Institution** | **Role** |
|---|---|
| **NCLT** (Sec 408, Companies Act 2013) | Adjudicating authority for **companies and LLPs** (APPSC GS 2019 key) and personal guarantors of corporate debtors |
| **NCLAT** (Sec 410) | Appeals from NCLT (from 1 Jun 2016), IBBI and CCI orders |
| **DRT** | Adjudicating authority for **individuals and partnership firms** (LENS Jun 2026); appeals to DRAT [GK] |

  - NCLAT: Principal Bench **New Delhi** + a bench at **Chennai**

  - **Related:** IBBI (**Secs 202 and 211**); the **CCI from 26 May 2017**; the **NFRA from 7 May 2018**
- ⚠ NCLT = **companies and LLPs**; DRT = **individuals and partnership firms**

## IBC provisions — CIRP, timelines, liquidation, pre-packs and the 2026 amendment | from: S6, S7
# 1. CIRP
- **Trigger:** Financial or operational creditors, on a default of at least **₹1 crore** (Jun 2026)
- **Committee of Creditors:** Financial creditors/lenders vote on resolution plans
- **Timeline:**
  - **180 days** (+ **90 days** extension); outer limit **330 days** incl. litigation (**2019** amendment)
  - In practice: **713 days** on average; **853 days** for cases closed in **FY25** (ES 2025-26)
- **Bidding bar:** **Wilful defaulters**/NPA promoters **cannot bid** for their own firms (**Sec. 29A**) — **Barred from bidding under IBC** (May 2026)
- **Liquidation waterfall:** Resolution costs, workmen's dues, secured creditors rank **above government dues** (29 May 2026)
- **Pre-Packaged Insolvency Resolution Process (PPIRP):** For **MSMEs**, **2021**, **120-day** limit; only **14 admissions in 4 years** (ES 2025-26)
# 2. IBC (Amendment) Act, 2026
- **Status:** **IBC Amendment Act 2026** Passed by Lok Sabha (31 Mar 2026; 15 Jun 2026)
- **Key changes:**
  - NCLT must **admit or reject a CIRP plea within 14 days**; rule on plans within **30 days**
  - Liquidation cut to **180 + 90 days**
  - New **Creditor-Initiated Insolvency Resolution Process (CIIRP)**: **150 days**, needs **51%** by value of notified financial creditors (**Secs 54C-54P**); hybrid of debtor-in-possession + creditor-in-control
  - Out-of-court route
  - "may" → "**shall**" in **Sec 7(5)(a)**, overturning **Vidarbha Industries**
  - Clean-slate protection + cross-border insolvency framework
- **Acts in news - provisions, amending Acts:** **Waqf (Amendment) Act, 2025:** See judgments
- ⚠ Timelines: CIRP **180 + 90** (outer **330**); PPIRP **120**; CIIRP **150**; admission **14** days

## Prompt Corrective Action framework — full form, triggers, restrictions | from: S9
# 1. Basics
- **Full form:** **PCA = Prompt Corrective Action** (APPSC-GS 2019 key)
- **Introduced:** By RBI, **introduced 2002**, to intervene in banks whose financial health is failing; weak banks placed under watch + restricted until capital and asset quality recover
> Exam angle: APPSC GS 2019 asked the expansion "PCA = Prompt Corrective Action".
# 2. Triggers
- **Current triggers (revised Nov 2021, effective 1 Jan 2022):** capital, asset quality, leverage (table below)
  - Earlier **profitability (RoA)** trigger dropped
| **Trigger parameter** | **Indicator** |
|---|---|
| Capital | CRAR / Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) [GK] |
| Asset quality | **Net NPA ratio**[GK] |
| Leverage | Tier 1 leverage ratio [GK] |
- **2017 framework (older notes):**
  - Threshold 1: CRAR between **7.75%** and **10.25%**
  - Threshold 2: CRAR between **6.25%** and **7.75%**
  - Threshold 3: CET1 below **3.625%**
  - Net NPA **6-9%** = threshold 1; **12%** or more = threshold 3
# 3. Restrictions
- **Curbs:** **Lending, branch expansion**, risky exposures, growth, **dividends**, management pay — until capital and asset quality improve
- **RBI powers:** Order a capital infusion, change of management, or a merger

## Gilt-edged market and government securities — types, Acts, participants | from: S1
# 1. Gilt-edged market and G-Secs
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
