=== ROW 122 | Rural credit cooperatives in Andhra Pradesh - structure, performance and problems

## Acts, amendment and committees on cooperatives | from: S0
# 1. Acts → year
| **Act / policy** | **Year** | **Provision** |
|---|---|---|
| **Cooperative Credit Societies Act** | **1904** | Started the movement |
| Cooperative Societies Act | **1912** | Allowed non-credit societies |
| **AP Cooperative Societies Act** | **1964** | Main cooperative law; the **AP Cooperative Tribunal** is constituted under **Section 75**; APHOUSEFED was registered under it in 1966 |
| **AP Mutually Aided Cooperative Societies (MACS) Act** (PYQ 2008) | **1995** | Self-reliant societies without government share capital, run by members with minimal state control [GK] |
| National Cooperative Policy | **2002** | — |
| **97th Amendment** | **2011** | Added **Art. 19(1)(c)** (right to form cooperatives), **Art. 43B** (DPSP), **Part IXB** |

- ⚠ **97th Amendment:** Supreme Court struck Part IXB down for State cooperatives; kept it for multi-State societies
- ⚠ **1964 Act** = main law, Tribunal under **Section 75**; **MACS Act 1995** = no government share capital
# 2. AP Cooperative Tribunal → features
- Chaired by a **judicial officer**
- **Appellate forum under the 1964 Act**
- **Original jurisdiction over disputes under the MACS Act 1995**
# 3. Committees on cooperative credit → chairman
| **Committee** | **Year** | **Chairman **[GK] |
|---|---|---|
| Task Force on revival of cooperative credit | 2004 | A. Vaidyanathan |
| Expert Committee on short-term cooperative credit structure | 2013 | Prakash Bakshi |

=== ROW 123 | Institutional credit to agriculture in Andhra Pradesh - banks, NABARD and SHGs

## NABARD and Andhra Bank → establishment | from: S0, S5
# 1. NABARD
- **Set up:** **12 July 1982** under the **NABARD Act 1981**
- **Recommendation:** **B. Sivaraman Committee (CRAFICARD)**
- **HQ:** **Mumbai**; initial corpus **Rs 100 crore**
- **Took over:** RBI's **ACD** and **RPCC** + the **ARDC**
- **Ownership:** **Fully Government-owned since 2019**
- ⚠ **Act 1981**, set up **1982**
# 2. Andhra Bank
- Founded by **Bhogaraju Pattabhi Sitaramayya** at **Machilipatnam** in **1923**
- Merged into **Union Bank of India** on **1 Apr 2020**

## Sources of institutional finance → who lends what | from: S1
- **AP farmers borrow from:** **Commercial banks, RRBs and PACS, i.e. all of them** (PYQ 2008)
  - Other formal sources: SHGs/JLGs, microfinance, KCC
| **Source (national pattern, Apr 2026)** | **Role** |
|---|---|
| **Scheduled commercial banks** | **over 70%** of formal farm credit |
| Cooperatives (**PACS-DCCB-SCB**) | Short-term credit |
| **ARDBs** | Long-term credit |

- **Priority Sector Lending:** Applies to commercial banks, RRBs, SFBs, LABs, primary UCBs; mandated agriculture share
- **Exclusion:** Formal credit excludes many **tenant, tribal, women and oral-lessee farmers** (needs land records)
- **NABARD Guntur study:** Formal interest rates about **2.5-10%**

=== ROW 124 | Agricultural marketing in Andhra Pradesh - market yards, Rythu Bazaars and e-marketing

## Acts and rules on agricultural marketing | from: S0
| **Act / rule** | **Year** | **Provision** |
|---|---|---|
| **AP (Agricultural Produce and Livestock) Markets Act** | **1966** | Act No. **16 of 1966**; covers crops + **livestock and livestock products** |
| **AP (AP&L) Markets Rules** | **1969** | Under **Section 33**; govern licensing (issue, fees, turnover, period) |
| Three central farm laws | **2020** | **Repealed in November 2021** → 1966 AMC framework remains operative |
| **Model APMC Act** | — | Single market fee, registration instead of licensing, farmers'/consumers' markets |

- **Mechanisms of the 1966 Act:** Open auction, standard weights, recorded prices, licensed buyers → correct farmer-trader bargaining imbalance
- ⚠ Act **1966** (No. **16**); Rules **1969** under **Section 33**

## Market institutions → functions and features | from: S0, S1
# 1. Agricultural Market Committees and market yards
- **Process:** Govt declares **notified area** → **Market Committee** constituted → committee establishes **notified market**
- **Supervision:** **District Agricultural Trade and Marketing Officer (DATMO)**
- !! Not in your sources: the current number of AMCs and market yards in AP.
# 2. Rythu Bazaars
- **Concept:** **Farmer sells directly to the consumer**, no middlemen
- **Purpose (always a pair):** **A better price for the farmer** + **a fair price for the consumer**
- **Start:** AP government, **1999**; **functioning from January 1999** (PYQ 2018)
- **Management:** **Managed by an Estate Officer**; under the **Revenue Department**; outside the purview of **Agricultural Market Committees**
  - ⚠ **Trap:** "**District Collector is the coordinating officer**" = **wrong** (PYQ 2025 key); the **Joint Collector** coordinates
- **Pricing (PYQ 2025 key):** Prices "fixed aiming to be around **25%**" (relative to open-market prices) = correct
- **Number:** **124 Rythu Bazars** functioning (2024-25); Survey 2025-26 analysis: **129 functioning**
# 3. Market yard vs Rythu Bazar
|  | **Regulated market yard** | **Rythu Bazar** |
|---|---|---|
| Basis | AP (AP&L) Markets Act, 1966 | Executive scheme, 1999 |
| Managed by | Agricultural Market Committee | **Estate Officer** |
| Department | Agricultural Marketing | **Revenue** |
| Seller | Farmers sell to licensed traders | **Farmer sells directly** |
| Fee | **Market fee 1%** | No middleman margin |
