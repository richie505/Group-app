## Standard incentives → rate (tax, land, power, employment, interest) | from: S5, S6
# 1. Standard incentives (all categories)
- **De-carbonisation subsidy up to 6%** of FCI (capped at **6% of FCI**)
- **Revival support** for sick units
- **PLI top-up** = **10%** of GoI PLI incentive (cap **5% of FCI**)
- **Local procurement subsidy** **1% of export turnover** for **3** years
# 2. Tax incentives
- **SGST (IDP 4.0, APSES 2024-25):** **100% of net SGST for 5 years** from commercial production
  - On final products made, sold and registered in AP
  - Some notes give a sliding scale of 100% / 50% / 25% for micro / small / medium-large; flat 100% net SGST followed here
- **SGST (IDP 2020-23):** Reimbursement for medium, large, mega units tied to **employment creation**, for **5 years** (APPSC 2023)
- **Stamp duty:** **100% stamp and transfer duty** reimbursement
  - On land purchase, lease of land/shed/buildings, mortgages, hypothecations
  - Paid **once only** on a given plot
# 3. Land incentives
- **Land-conversion fee (IDP 4.0):** **100%** reimbursement (older summary: 25%)
- **SC reservation (IDP 2020-23):** **16.2%** of plots in APIIC parks for **SC** entrepreneurs (APPSC 2023)
  - !! Not in your sources: the ST plot share.
- **APIIC parks:** **25%** rebate on land cost, up to **₹20 lakh** per enterprise
# 4. Power incentives
- **Fixed power cost:** Reimbursed at **₹1 per unit for 2 years** from DCP
- !! Not in your sources: electricity duty exemption under 4.0.
# 5. Employment

  - Paid over 5 years
- **Local hiring:** **75%** reserved for locals under the 2019 Act

- !! Not in your sources: EPF support or a skilling subsidy under IDP 4.0.

## Single window → Act, portal and timelines | from: S13
# 1. Act and portal → year
| **Item** | **Year** | **Provision** |
|---|---|---|
| **AP Industrial Single Window Clearance Act** | **2002** | Gives the single window statutory force, incl. **deemed approval** if a department misses its deadline |
| **AP Omnibus (Speed of Doing Business) Bill** | **2026** | Amended the 2002 Act; passed Aug 2026 (20 Aug 2026) |
| **AP Single Desk Portal** | **launched in April 2015** | Under the **Single Desk Policy 2015-20** |

- ⚠ **2015** = launch year of the **Single Desk Portal (April 2015)**, not the statute year (**2002**)
- !! Not in your sources: the Act's penalty for delay.
# 2. Purpose
- **Purpose:** One application point for every clearance **investment proposals** need (APPSC 2018: policy was for clearing **investment proposals**)
- **G2B services:** Single G2B point for all industrial clearances: online application + document upload + payment + status tracking + certificate download
- **Benefit:** Removes **physical interaction** with regulators; no physical visits (APSES 2024-25)
# 3. Clearances and timelines
- **Scope:** End-to-end processing of **93+ clearances across 19 departments** within **21 days** (under IDP 4.0, still **21-day** target)
- **Approvals:** **Pre-establishment and pre-operation** approvals within **21 days** (APPSC 2021, 2023)
- **Approval categories:**
  - Self-certification
  - In-principle approval → inspection
  - **Deemed approval if the SLA is breached**
- **Tourism investors:** Same portal for **13 pre-establishment** + **11 pre-operation** clearances
# 4. Performance
| **Performance (APSES 2024-25)** | **Figure** |
|---|---|
| Applications since inception | **1,20,634** |
| Approved (as of 1 Jan 2025) | **1,17,954** |
| Received 1 Apr 2024 - 1 Jan 2025 | **16,783** (**16,458** within SLA) |
| Approval within SLA | **99.20%** |

- **SDP 2.0:** Business-specific services + better service categories + stepwise application timelines with approval hierarchy + centralised dashboard
- **Deemed approval in current practice:** **City Gas Distribution policy (G.O. Ms. No. 8, Mar 2026)**
  - Single window + **deemed clearances**
  - State Level Utilities Coordination Committee under the **Chief Secretary**
  - Committees headed by District Collectors
