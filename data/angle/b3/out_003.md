=== ROW 14 | Economic growth vs economic development; measures of development

## Growth vs development — definitions and indicators | from: S0, S1, S2
# 1. Growth vs development
| **Basis** | **Economic growth** | **Economic development** |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning | Sustained rise in **real output** | Growth **plus** better living standards, capabilities and distribution |
| Nature | **Quantitative** | **Qualitative and quantitative** |
| Scope | Narrow | Broad; includes growth |
| Measure | Real GDP/GNP, per capita income | HDI, poverty ratio, literacy, life expectancy, inequality |
| Distribution | Not considered; not a concern | Considered; central concern |
- **Relationship:** growth is **necessary but not sufficient**; development cannot occur without some growth; growth = means, development = end
- **One-liner:** growth = increase in output; development = increase in wellbeing
- **Growth without development:** via **jobless growth** or unequal gains
# 2. Models of development
- **Western model of development:** key factor = **economic growth** (rising GDP, industrialisation) (APPSC 2018)
- **"New view" of development (1970s):** development = reduction of **poverty, unemployment and inequality** within a growing economy
  - Does **not** rest on simple growth rate alone - **simple growth rate of the economy** not stressed (APPSC-G2 2017)
  - **Implication / PCI caveat:** rising per-capita GNP or per capita real income is **not** development if poverty and unemployment also rise
> Exam angle: "which is NOT emphasised by the new view" - answer is the simple growth rate only.
- **Welfare-centred alternatives:** **Gandhian** model (decentralisation, village industry); capability approach
- **Directive Principles:** envisage adequate livelihood + reduced inequality, not just higher national income
# 3. Indicators of development
- **Valid indicators:** **institutional change**, **structural transformation** (agrarian → industrial and service economies), **inclusive growth**
  - Structural and institutional change: falling share of agriculture, rising industry/services, modern institutions
- **Not an indicator:** **larger share of GDP from the primary sector** (APPSC-G2 2008 framing)
- **Other signs:**
  - Workers moving out of agriculture → higher labour productivity
  - Rising capital formation, social overhead capital, human resource development

## Curves and inequality measures → economist | from: S7
| **Curve** | **Economist** | **Relationship** |
|---|---|---|
| **Lorenz curve** | **Max Lorenz, 1906** | Distribution of income - cumulative % of population vs % of income; farther from 45-degree line = more inequality |
| **Kuznets curve** | **Simon Kuznets** | **Inverted-U**: inequality first rises, then falls as income grows |

=== ROW 16 | Human Development Index - components, methodology, India's rank and trend

## Human development — concept, persons and origin | from: S0
# 1. Persons → contribution
| **Person** | **Contribution** |
|---|---|
| **Amartya Sen** | **Capabilities approach** (Development as Freedom) - development = expanding real freedoms; co-conceived the HDI |
| **Martha Nussbaum** | extended it with a list of **central human capabilities** (Women and Human Development) |
| **Mahbub ul Haq** | **Conceived** the HDI with **Amartya Sen** (team also incl. Inge Kaul) |
# 2. Concept
- **Core idea:** people and their capabilities, not growth alone, are the ultimate criteria of development
- **GNI vs HDI:** GNI cannot replace HDI - income is a means, not the end
- **Same GNI, different HDI:** shows role of **national policy choices** (APPSC-G1 2024)
  - Example: **Malaysia** has higher GNI per capita than **Chile**, but Chile has ~7 years more life expectancy + 3 more years of expected schooling → much higher HDI
# 3. Origin - FIRST
- **Publisher:** **UNDP**, in the **Human Development Report**; first in **1990**
- **HDR compares:** **education, health and per-capita income** (APPSC-GS 2025 key: "Human Development Report")

## HDI methodology — components, goalposts, formula | from: S1
| **Dimension** | **Indicator** | **Minimum** | **Maximum** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long and healthy life | Life expectancy at birth (years) | 20 | 85 |
| Education | Expected years of schooling | 0 | 18 |
| Education | Mean years of schooling | 0 | 15 |
| Decent standard of living | **GNI per capita (PPP $)** (2011 PPP $), log of income | $100 | $75,000 [GK] |
- **Dimension index:** (actual - min)/(max - min)
- **Education index:** **arithmetic mean** of its two sub-indices
- **Income:** uses **logarithm** of GNI → diminishing importance of income (diminishing returns)
- **Income indicator:** HDI uses **GNI**, not GDP, per capita (some notes say GDP per capita)
  - GNI + the two schooling indicators came in with 2010 revision
- **Formula:** HDI = **geometric mean** of three normalised dimension indices; introduced **2010** - weak dimension not offset by a strong one
  - Worked example: Georgia 0.769 = High
- **PPP dollars:** fair cross-country comparison across price levels (conversion from **2011 ICP** surveys); goalposts first used 2011 ICP PPP, later Reports moved on
- ⚠ Education index = **arithmetic** mean; HDI = **geometric** mean
- ⚠ Expected schooling max **18**; mean schooling max **15**

## Related indices → year introduced and what they measure | from: S2
- **Limits:** only part of human development - no inequality, poverty, human security, empowerment, environment
  - **Not included (odd-one-out):** MPI, inequality, poverty, gender gaps, human security, empowerment, participation, environment
  - Criticism: HDI ignores inequality, sustainability, gender → hence IHDI, GDI, GII, MPI

| **Index** | **Introduced (HDR)** | **Measures** |
|---|---|---|
| Capability Poverty Measure | 1996 [GK] | Lack of basic capabilities |
| **Human Poverty Index** (HPI-1 developing, HPI-2 developed) | 1997 [GK] | Deprivation in HDI dimensions; replaced by MPI in 2010 [GK] |
| **GDI** | 1995 [GK] | **Ratio of female to male HDI** (health, education, earned income) |
| **IHDI** | 2010 [GK] | HDI discounted for inequality; **IHDI < HDI** whenever inequality exists; gap = loss |
| **GII** | 2010 [GK] | Reproductive health, empowerment, labour market |
| MPI | 2010 [GK] | Overlapping deprivations |
| **Planetary pressures-adjusted HDI (PHDI)** | 2020 [GK] | HDI adjusted for CO2 emissions and material footprint [GK] |
- **IHDI vs HDI:** no inequality → HDI = IHDI; HDI = "potential", IHDI = "actual" human development
- ⚠ **GDI** = ratio (1995); **GII** = inequality (2010)

## Terms → meaning: inclusive growth and human capital | from: S6, S7
# 1. Inclusive growth
- **Three pillars:** **Market economics, Empowerment and Pragmatism** (APPSC 2025)
- **Eleventh Plan (2007-12) theme:** "**Towards Faster and More Inclusive Growth**"
- **Instruments:**
  - **SHGs** - women's credit and livelihood
  - **MSMEs** - labour-intensive jobs
  - Financial inclusion - RBI FI-Index **67.0** in 2025, up from 53.9 in 2021
  - Welfare DBT
- **Measures:**
  - India's Gini **36.8 (2004-05) to 32.1 (2019-20)**
  - WEF Inclusive Development Index: India **62nd of 74** emerging economies (older notes)
# 2. Human capital formation
- **Meaning:** investment in **education, health, skills (on-the-job training), migration and information** that raises productive capacity of people
- **Returns:** higher productivity and earnings, faster growth, lower poverty
  - Poverty falls with education: **35.1%** of those with no formal education poor vs **14.9%** with post-secondary education (Jun 2025)
