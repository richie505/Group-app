## Manufacturing policies → year, targets and design | from: S0, S1, S4
# 1. Manufacturing share targets - National Manufacturing Policy, Make in India

| **Policy / mission** | **Year** | **Target** |
|---|---|---|
| **National Manufacturing Policy** | **2011** (notified **November 2011**) | Manufacturing share of GDP → **25%**; **100 million jobs** within a decade = **100 million additional jobs** by **2022** |
| **Make in India** | 2014 | Make India a manufacturing, design and innovation hub; 25% of GDP by 2022 (later 2025) [GK] |
| **National Manufacturing Mission (Budget 2025-26)** | 2025-26 | **25%** share + **143 million** jobs by **2035** |

- ⚠ **100 million** jobs = National Manufacturing Policy and Make in India; **143 million** jobs = National Manufacturing Mission
# 2. National Manufacturing Policy 2011 — design
- **Design:** **Sector-neutral, location-neutral, technology-neutral** (**sector-, location- and technology-neutral**) - except incentives to green technology
- **Infrastructure:** **PPP** basis
- **Skilling:** Rural youth
# 3. Make in India — targets and status
- **Targets:**
  - Manufacturing growth **12-14%** a year
  - Manufacturing share of GDP **16% → 25%** (**25% of GDP by 2025**)
  - **100 million** additional manufacturing jobs
- **Status:** Manufacturing still only **~16.3-17.5%** of GVA/GDP → 25%-by-2025 target missed
  - Strategy shifted to global-scale manufacturing, design capability, integration with global value chains (Jun 2026)
# 4. Atmanirbhar Bharat and logistics
- **Atmanirbhar Bharat - five pillars:** **Economy, Infrastructure, System, Vibrant Demography, Demand**
- **May 2020 package:** ~**Rs 20 lakh crore** (~10% of GDP)
- **Logistics:**
  - **National Logistics Policy** (September 2022)
  - **PM GatiShakti** (October 2021)
  - Target: logistics costs, historically **13-14% of GDP**

## Zones and Acts → NIMZs and SEZ Act, 2005 | from: S0, S5
# 1. NIMZs
- **NIMZs (National Investment and Manufacturing Zones):** Large integrated industrial townships - zoned land use, clean technology, social infrastructure
  - Differ from SEZs in size, infrastructure planning, governance, exit policy, fiscal incentives
- **AP NIMZs:** Proposed in **Chittoor** and **Prakasam** - in-principle approval; TEFS report submitted (2025-26)
- !! Not in your sources: the first state in which an NIMZ was proposed.
- !! Not in your sources: the first NIMZ location. Industrial corridors: DMIC, CBIC, BMEC, AKIC.
# 2. SEZ Act, 2005
- **SEZ Act, 2005:** With SEZ Rules, in force **10 February 2006**
  - Single-window, export-oriented enclave treated as foreign territory for trade and duties
- **Who can set up:** **Central Government, a State Government, or any person** (private, joint or foreign developer)
- **Tax benefits (Sec 10AA):**
  - **100%** income-tax exemption on export income for 5 years
  - 50% for next 5 years
  - 50% of ploughed-back profit for a further 5 years
- **Sunset clause:** Only units operational by **30 June 2020** get 10AA benefit (extended from 31 March 2020)
- **Proposed reform - DESH framework (Development of Enterprise & Service Hubs) (Jun 2026):**
  - Drop compulsory **positive NFE**
  - Allow sales to **Domestic Tariff Area**
  - **WTO-compliant**

## Start-up — definition, criteria and benefits | from: S0
# 1. Start-up India → launch
- **Launched:** **16 January 2016** (**National Startup Day** = 16 Jan)
- **Nodal:** **DPIIT**; Action Plan of **19** points
# 2. Definition → criteria

| **Criterion** | **Start-up** | **Deep-tech start-up** |
|---|---|---|
| Legal form | Pvt Ltd, partnership, LLP, co-operative | Same |
| Age | Up to **10 years** | Up to **20 years** |
| Turnover | Not above **₹200 crore** | Not above **₹300 crore** |

- **Turnover:** Older notes give **₹100 crore** (revised)
- ⚠ **Trap:** Entity formed by splitting/reconstructing a business is not a start-up
- ⚠ **10 years / ₹200 crore** = start-up; **20 years / ₹300 crore** = deep-tech start-up
# 3. Benefits
- **Benefits:**
  - Income-tax deduction for **3 consecutive years out of 10** (s.80-IAC; incorporated before 1 Apr 2030)
  - Self-certification under labour/environment laws
  - Fast-track patents; winding up under IBC

## Schemes and funds → launch, ministry, amount and target | from: S1, S4
# 1. Stand-Up India, 2016
- **Launched:** **5 April 2016**; **Ministry of Finance**; via scheduled commercial bank branches
- **Loans:** **₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore** to **SC/ST and women** entrepreneurs (18+)
  - For **greenfield** (first-time) enterprises - manufacturing, services, trading, agri-allied
  - ⚠ **Trap:** **Not brownfield**
- **Norm:** At least **one SC/ST and one woman borrower per bank branch**
- **Loan terms:**
  - Covers up to 85% of project cost; borrower contribution min 10%
  - Repayment up to 7 years (18-month moratorium)
  - **SIDBI** ₹10,000 crore refinance; portal "Standupmitra"
- **Status:** Extended for 15th FC period; operational till **31 March 2025** (IYB 2026)
  - 2.75 lakh loans, **₹62,790 crore** (to 31 Mar 2025)
# 2. Government funds for start-ups
- **Seed Fund (SISFS):** **₹945 crore**; launched **19 April 2021**
  - Up to ₹20 lakh grant (proof of concept) + ₹50 lakh investment
  - Via incubators (up to ₹5 crore each)
- **Fund of Funds (FFS):** **₹10,000 crore**; managed by **SIDBI**; invests through SEBI-registered AIFs (not directly)
  - **FFS 2.0:** Another ₹10,000 crore for deep tech, space, AI
- ⚠ **SIDBI** = manages Fund of Funds **and** gives Stand-Up India refinance (both **₹10,000 crore**)
# 3. Financing sources → feature

| **Source** | **Feature** |
|---|---|
| Angel investors | Individuals investing own money at idea/seed stage [GK] |
| Venture capital | Early-stage **equity** with active guidance for high-growth firms (VIS) |

| **CGSS** (Oct 2022) | Collateral-free loans; guarantee through **NCGTC**; max **₹20 crore** per borrower; fee 1% for champion sectors |

- **Credit guarantee:** Earlier Credit Guarantee Fund for Startups

## SEZ features → developers, approval, FDI, tax | from: S1
# 1. Setting up SEZs
- **Developers:** Set up **jointly or severally** by Central Government, State Government or **any person** (public, private, joint sector)
- **Purpose:** Manufacturing, services, or as a **Free Trade Warehousing Zone**
- **Approval route:** Proposals via State → **Board of Approval**
- **Ownership:** SEZs under the Act mainly private
- ⚠ **Trap:** "SEZ is set up **primarily** to generate employment" is NOT true – primary aim is exports (APPSC-G2 2017 key)
# 2. Investment and tax provisions

| **Provision** | **Content** |
|---|---|
| **FDI** | **100% FDI** under automatic route for most manufacturing in SEZs |
| **IFSC** (**s.18**) | Allows an **IFSC** in an SEZ – only one IFSC per SEZ |
| **Tax (s.10AA)** | **100%** income-tax exemption for **5** years; **50%** for next **5**; **50%** of reinvested profit for **5** more |

- **Tax holidays phased out 2017-2020** (sunset clause)
- ⚠ **s.18** = IFSC; **s.10AA** = tax holiday (**100-50-50**, **5+5+5**)
