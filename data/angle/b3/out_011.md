## Agricultural strategy — phases, definitions and goals | from: S0, S5
# 1. Phases of agricultural development

| **Phase** | **Period** | **Defining feature** |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-Green Revolution | **1951-68** (foundations 1950-67) | Land reforms, irrigation, Community Development; PL-480 imports; droughts 1965-67 |
| Early Green Revolution | **1968-81** | HYV seeds (wheat first), fertiliser, irrigation - New Agricultural Strategy |
| Later Green Revolution | **1987-92** | Spread to rice and other regions |

- **Foodgrains:** **72.3 MT (1965-66)** → record **357.73 MT (2024-25)**
  - Horticulture (**362.08 MT**) larger
# 2. Sustainable agriculture
- **Meaning:** Using land so its **quality remains intact**, meeting present needs without compromising future generations (APPSC 2019 key)
- **Ultimate goal:** **All three pillars** (APPSC 2018 key)
  - Environmental health + economic profitability + social and economic equity
# 3. Rainfed areas
- **Rainfed farming:** About **50-60% of net sown area** (some notes say **50%**) + ~**40% of food production**

=== ROW 52 | Agrarian crisis - causes, farmer distress and suicides, loan waivers, small-holding viability

## Causes of agrarian crisis → latest data point | from: S0

| **Cause** | **Latest data point** |
|---|---|
| Small holdings | **86%** of holdings are small and marginal (under 2 ha); average foodgrain yield 2,929 kg/ha against a 4,000 kg world average (LENSD, 8 Jun 2026) |
| Rainfall dependence | Rain-fed farming covers about **50%** of net sown area; **64%** of irrigation relies on groundwater; 17% of groundwater blocks are over-exploited (LENSD, 8 Jun 2026) |
| Price volatility and middlemen | Farmers get only about **35% of the retail price**; a 30% cold-chain deficit causes up to 40% post-harvest loss in fruits and vegetables (LENSD) |
| Input costs | Fertiliser, seed and pesticide costs rose **300%+** since the early 2000s (LENS Jan 2026) |
| Soil degradation | NPK ratio **10.9:4.1:1** (Economic Survey, 2023-24; kharif 2023 alone: **10.9:4.9:1**) against the recommended (ideal) **4:2:1** (LENSD) |
| Climate | Yields fall about **8% per 1°C** of warming (LENSD) |

- **Structure:** Tenancy (rent due even when crops fail), moneylender debt, fragmentation
- **Causes of distress (SAS view):** Crop failure, high input costs, unstable prices, rainfed dependence (failed borewells), small holdings
- **UPPSC 2016 key:** **Rural poverty** = effective reason for slow agricultural development
- **Debt cycle:** High input cost → borrowing → crop failure or low price → inability to repay → fresh borrowing → asset sale → **debt trap**

## Crop insurance — PMFBY, RWBCIS and AP | from: S7, S11
# 1. PMFBY
- **PMFBY (2016):** Replaced **NAIS (1999)** and **MNAIS (2010)**; "**One Nation, One Crop, One Premium**"
- **Type:** **Central Sector Scheme** - Centre's premium subsidy goes straight to insurers

| **Season / crop** | **Farmer's premium (max)** |
|---|---|
| Kharif food and oilseeds | **2%** |
| Rabi food and oilseeds | **1.5%** |
| Commercial / horticultural (annual) | **5%** |

- **Premium sharing:** Rest of the premium shared by Centre and states
- **Voluntary:** **Voluntary for all farmers since 2020** (**Kharif 2020**); made **voluntary in 2020**
  - Some notes say compulsory for loanee farmers
- **Coverage:** Pre-sowing to post-harvest, localised calamities/risks
  - From **Kharif 2026**: also **wild-animal attack and paddy inundation** losses
- **Scale:**
  - **4.19 crore** farmers insured in **2024-25** (FY25), **32%** more than **2022-23**
  - **9** years: **78.62 crore** applications; claims **₹1,82,136 crore** (**₹1.82 lakh crore**) vs farmers' premium **₹35,864 crore** (≈ **₹508 per ₹100**)
- **Tech:** **YES-TECH** (remote-sensing yield estimation), **WINDS** (weather stations), **DigiClaim**
- **Helpline:** **Krishi Rakshak** - **14447**
- ⚠ **2%** kharif, **1.5%** rabi, **5%** commercial
# 2. RWBCIS
- **RWBCIS:** Weather-index insurance - pays when weather deviates from set triggers; AP runs it alongside PMFBY
# 3. Crop insurance in AP
- **Free crop insurance (2019-24):** State paid the whole premium (farmer's share); **CCRC** holders covered
- **Switch:** **Reintroduced voluntary enrolment from Rabi 2024-25**
  - Farmer pays small/nominal share of actuarial premium; rest split **equally by the State and Centre**
- **Coverage:**
  - **2024:** **34.93 lakh** farmers on **28.67 lakh ha**
  - Then **10.63 lakh farmers, 11.81 lakh ha** (AP SES 2025-26)

=== ROW 53 | Land reforms - abolition of intermediaries, tenancy reform, ceiling laws, consolidation; outcomes

## Land reforms — goals, types and FIRSTs | from: S0, S2, S3, S5
# 1. Tenancy reform — three goals
- **Regulation of rent** (capped at **1/4 to 1/6** of produce)
- **Security of tenure**
- **Ownership rights** to tenants
- **Failure:** "Voluntary surrenders" + evictions
# 2. Joint cooperative farming features
- Land pooled **voluntarily**
- Members **retain ownership**
- Management **elected by all members**
- Produce shared per land/labour contributed
- ⚠ **Not a feature:** compulsory membership (APPSC G2 2017 key)
# 3. FIRSTs

| **FIRST** | **FACT** |
|---|---|
| **First State to implement** the Model Agricultural Land Leasing Act, 2016 | **Uttarakhand** (APPSC 2018 key) |
| **Bhoodan (1951)** began | **Pochampally** (Nalgonda, Telangana) on **18 April 1951** |

## Programmes → region and outcome — Operation Barga, consolidation, Bhoodan, cooperative farming | from: S0, S2, S3
- **Operation Barga (West Bengal, Left Front):** Recording names of sharecroppers (**bargadars**)
  - Heritable rights + **75%** share of produce
  - Launched **1978** (some notes say 1977, the year the Left Front came to power)
  - APPSC 2019 key: land reform for recording names of sharecroppers
- **Consolidation:** Succeeded in **Punjab, Haryana and western UP**; little progress in southern and eastern States
- **Bhoodan:**
  - Amid the Telangana armed struggle
  - Appeal to donate **1/6th** of land; over **40 lakh acres** pledged, much barren
- **Cooperative farming:** **Kumarappa Committee (1949)** → **Nagpur Resolution (1959)** made it a party goal
  - Failed: captured by rich peasants to evade ceilings
- ⚠ Rent cap **1/4 to 1/6** of produce; Bhoodan appeal = **1/6th** of land; bargadar share = **75%**

## Land ceiling laws — unit, limits and outcome | from: S1

| **Feature** | **Pre-1972 laws** | **1972 national guidelines** |
|---|---|---|
| Unit | **Individual** (encouraged benami transfers to relatives) | **Family** (effective 1972) |
| Ceiling | High, varied by State | **10-18 acres** two-crop irrigated; **27 acres** single-crop; **54 acres** dry [GK for 54] |

- **Two rounds:** **1950s and early 1970s**; about **21 States** had ceiling laws as of 2020
- **Outcome:** Less than **2%** of cultivable area redistributed
  - Exemptions: plantations, orchards, "mechanised" farms

## Constitutional amendments, Articles and cases on land reform | from: S4

| **Amendment** | **Land-reform change** |
|---|---|
| **1st (1951)** | Inserted **Arts. 31A, 31B** and **Ninth Schedule** (after Kameshwar Singh v. Bihar) |
| **4th (1955)** | Restated Art. 31(2); widened 31A to zamindari abolition, planning, mineral control |
| **17th (1964)** | Land under personal cultivation within ceiling acquired only at **market value**; widened "estate"; challenged in **Golaknath (1967)** |
| 25th (1971) | Inserted **Art. 31C**[GK] |
| 34th (1974) / 66th (1990) | 20 more land laws; **55 State Acts incl. AP** placed in Ninth Schedule |

- **Art. 323B:** Clause (h) "rent and tenancy rights" (tribunals) added by **75th Amendment (1993)**
- **I.R. Coelho (2007):** Laws added to Ninth Schedule after **24 April 1973** (Kesavananda) open to Basic Structure review
- ⚠ **1st** = 31A, 31B, Ninth Schedule; **25th** = 31C; **75th** = 323B(h)

## Recent reform initiatives and committees | from: S5, S8
# 1. National
- **National Council for Land Reforms:** Constituted **2008** under the **Prime Minister**
  - **Committee:** "State Agrarian Relations and Unfinished Task in Land Reforms"
- **Model Agricultural Land Leasing Act, 2016:** By **NITI Aayog** (T. Haque committee)
  - Legalises leasing; tenants get credit/insurance; protects owner's title
  - Preferred to acquisition
- **AP routes:** CCRC Act 2019 (11-month cards); proposed **AP New Tenancy Act 2024** → tenant entitlement cards
# 2. Koneru Ranga Rao Committee (2006) — AP Land Committee
- **Nature:** AP government **Land Committee** headed by **Koneru Ranga Rao**
- **Year:** Constituted **2006** (Y.S. Rajasekhara Reddy government); AP Land Committee, 2004-06
- **Subject:** Resolve **land-related problems** in AP (APPSC 2021 key)
- **Focus areas:** **Assigned lands, confirmation of land rights, tenancy regulation and land records**
  - ⚠ **Trap:** **NOT** **corporate farming** - the "not the focus" option (APPSC G2 2017 key)
> Exam angle: "which was not a focus area" - eliminate corporate farming.

## AP land laws → year and provisions | from: S6, S7
# 1. Abolition of intermediaries and tenancy, 1948-1956

| **Law** | **Key provisions** |
|---|---|
| Madras Estates (Abolition and Conversion into Ryotwari) Act | Abolished zamindari and inamdari in Andhra area; recommended by **Prakasam Committee (1938)**; APP gives passage 1949, in force **1950 **(Act dated **1948**[GK]) |
| Hyderabad Jagir Abolition Regulation, **1949** | Jagirs and Sarf-e-khas lands became Diwani (ryotwari) after Police Action |
| Hyderabad (Telangana Area) Tenancy Act, **1950** | Passed amid Telangana armed struggle; **protected tenants** (6 years' cultivation) got heritable rights; ownership on paying 60% of market price in 20 instalments |
| AP (Andhra Area) Tenancy Act, **1956** | On **Subba Rao Committee** advice; written leases; max rent **30% wet / 25% dry**; minimum 6-year lease; 1974 amendment gave permanent rights to pre-1974 tenants |

# 2. Ceiling and land protection

| **Law** | **Key provisions** |
|---|---|
| Ceiling Act, **1961** (common law) | **Individual** as unit; ceiling 27-324 acres; surplus under 15,000 acres |
| **AP Land Reforms (Ceiling on Agricultural Holdings) Act, 1973** | Bill under CM **P.V. Narasimha Rao**; in Ninth Schedule; in force **1 January 1975** |
| AP Assigned Lands (Prohibition of Transfers) Act, **1977** | Land assigned to landless poor cannot be sold or transferred |
| AP Land Grabbing (Prohibition) Act, **1982** | Punishes grabbers of government and weaker-section lands |

- **1973 Act - unit:** **Family of 5**
  - Husband's and wife's land clubbed
  - Each major son = separate unit
  - +1/5 ceiling per extra member
- **Standard ceiling:** **10-27 acres wet**, **35-54 acres dry** (some notes say 30-54)
- **Benami ban:** Transfers after **30 Jan 1970**
- **Land Tribunal:** Headed by **RDO**
- **Exemptions:** Plantations, orchards, religious/charitable trusts, cooperatives, **Bhoodan** lands
- **AP phase II:** **20 lakh acres** surplus estimated vs only **5 lakh acres** taken over
- ⚠ **1961** Act = individual unit; **1973** Act = family of 5

=== ROW 54 | Land records and titling - Digital India Land Records Modernisation, SVAMITVA

## Terms → meaning in land records and titling | from: S1, S3

| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Unique Land Parcel Identification Number = "Bhu-Aadhaar"** | **14-digit alphanumeric** ID from geo-coordinates of a parcel's vertices; standards: ECCMA + OGC |
| **National Generic Document Registration System (NGDRS)** | **"One Nation One Registration Software"** under DILRMP - common, configurable registration application for sub-registrars and citizens |
| **NAKSHA** | Urban pilot under DILRMP, Survey of India; issues "Ur Pro Card" |
| **N-LRSI** | NCAER's Land Records and Services Index; grades States on digitisation and quality of land records |

# Presumptive vs conclusive titling

| **Presumptive (present system)** | **Conclusive** |
|---|---|
| Registration records a transaction; title can be challenged with earlier deeds, mutation, possession | State **guarantees** the title; once issued it cannot be disputed |
| Registration, survey and revenue records kept separately | Single authoritative title register |

- ⚠ **ULPIN** = **14-digit** parcel ID; **NGDRS** = registration software; **N-LRSI** = NCAER index

## Schemes → origin, funding and components — DILRMP and SVAMITVA | from: S0, S2
# 1. DILRMP origin and funding
- **Predecessor:** **National Land Records Modernisation Programme (NLRMP) in 2008** (UPPSC 2016 key)
  - Merged **CLR** (Computerisation of Land Records) + **SRA&ULR** (Strengthening of Revenue Administration and Updating of Land Records)
- **Revamp:** **DILRMP** = **Central Sector Scheme with 100% central funding from 1 April 2016** (earlier centrally sponsored)
  - UPSC 2024 key "1, 2 and 3": **100% central funding** + **digitisation of cadastral maps** both correct
- **Tenure:** Extended **2021-22 to 2025-26**
# 2. DILRMP components
- **Core components:** Modern record rooms (tehsil), survey/resurvey, data entry, digitisation of cadastral maps/FMBs, State data centre, computerised registration, core GIS
- **New components:** **Consent-based Aadhaar seeding** of land records + **computerisation of revenue courts**
- **Gender:** Mandatory **gender column** in the Record of Rights
# 3. DILRMP phases

| **Phase (DILRMP 3.0 guidelines, Sep 2026)** | **Focus** |
|---|---|
| Phase 1 (**2008-16**) | Digitisation, resurvey, cadastral maps, registration-mutation link |
| Phase 2 (**2016-26**) | ULPIN, NGDRS, multilingual records, e-Court integration |
| Phase 3 (**2026-31**) | GIS-based **Land Stack** for rural, semi-urban and urban parcels |

# 4. SVAMITVA

| **Item** | **Fact** |
|---|---|
| Full form | Survey of Villages and Mapping with Improvised Technology in Village Areas |
| Launch | **24 April 2020** (National Panchayati Raj Day); pilot 2020, nationwide **2021** |
| Ministry / type | **Ministry of Panchayati Raj** (not Mines); **Central Sector** |
| Agencies | **Survey of India** (implementing); **NICSI** (technology) |
| Coverage | Rural **inhabited (abadi)** areas only - **agricultural land not covered** |
| Output | **Property Cards** (Record of Rights); DigiLocker access |
| Pilot States | Haryana, Karnataka, MP, Maharashtra, UP, Uttarakhand, Punjab, Rajasthan, **Andhra Pradesh** |

- **Geo-referencing:** **CORS** (Continuously Operating Reference Stations); State governments prepare + distribute cards
- ⚠ SVAMITVA = **Panchayati Raj**, abadi only; DILRMP = **100% central funding from 1 April 2016**

## Acts and rulings on conclusive titling | from: S3, S4

| **Act / ruling** | **Year** | **Provision** |
|---|---|---|
| **NITI Aayog Model Conclusive Land Titling Act** | **2020** | A model; adoption = States' choice; aims: fewer disputes, easier credit, lower transaction costs |

## AP land records — Bhu Bharathi to Mee Bhoomi and resurvey | from: S4
# 1. Digitisation platforms

| **Platform** | **Feature** |
|---|---|
| **Bhu Bharathi** | Computerisation of land records in united AP from **1992**; piloted in **Nizamabad district** (some notes say completed in Ranga Reddy district) |
| **Webland** | AP's digitised land-record platform; PACS software integrates with it |
| **CARD (registration)** | Anywhere registration, auto-mutation, registration at Grama Sachivalayams |
| **Mee Bhoomi-Blockchain** | Pilot launched **24 July 2026** in 7 mandals of 7 districts; statewide from November; unique digital ID per parcel |

- **Mee Bhoomi pilot districts:** Kurnool, Chittoor, Bapatla, Krishna, East Godavari, Parvathipuram Manyam, Vizianagaram
# 2. Resurvey and passbooks
- **Resurvey (Jagananna Saswatha Bhu Hakku and Bhu Raksha, from Dec 2020):** Drones, CORS, GIS
  - **6,901 villages, 81 lakh parcels**; 86,000 boundary disputes resolved
  - ~₹6,000 crore; Centre gave **₹400 crore** incentive (Jan 2026)
- **QR-coded pattadar passbooks with the State emblem (from Jan 2026):**
  - **36 lakh** issued; **56 lakh** more by **March 2027** covering **8,232 villages**
  - **16 lakh acres** to leave Section 22-A prohibited list (Sep 2026)

## Universal angles | from: S5 | universal
- **Significance:** Clear titles → land as collateral, less litigation (~80% of AP public petitions are land disputes, 7 Feb 2026)
- **Comparative note:** Karnataka's **Bhoomi** (2000) - pioneer of computerised RTCs; 25 years in 2026
- **Current relevance:** **National Digital Land Initiative (Land Stack)** - cloud GIS with Bhu-Aadhaar for every parcel (Jun 2026)
