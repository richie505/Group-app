## Expenditure terms → meaning — developmental, committed, welfare | from: S10, S11, S12
# 1. Developmental vs non-developmental
| **Developmental** | **Non-developmental** |
|---|---|
| Promotes growth and welfare; productive | Routine running of government; consumptive |
| **Social services**: education, health, water supply, housing, welfare of SC/ST/BC, nutrition | **General services**: organs of State, police, **defence**, tax collection, administration |
| **Economic services**: agriculture, rural development, irrigation, energy, industry, transport | **Interest payments**, **pensions**, administrative expenditure |

- **Classification (RBI State Finances):**
  - Developmental = social & economic services
  - Non-developmental = general services (incl. interest and pensions)
- **Nature:** Developmental spending stimulates growth, employment, quality of life; non-developmental = consumptive, routine operational spending
- **Cross-cutting:** Development expenditure can be revenue (salaries of teachers) or capital (irrigation dams) → dev/non-dev split cuts across revenue/capital split
- **PYQ (APPSC-GS 2019):** **Defence** and **maintenance of law and order** = non-developmental
> Exam angle: "identify the non-developmental item" - pick defence, police, interest, tax-collection charges, administration.
> Exam angle: APPSC 2019 - development expenditure does **not** include **defence**.
# 2. Committed expenditure — AP 2026-27
- **Interest:** **interest ₹37,281 cr (16% of revenue receipts)** (year-wise trend under Interest payments)
- **Pensions:** **Pensions Rs. 23,713 Cr = 10%** of revenue receipts
- **Rupee share:** Interest + principal repayment = **18 paise** of every rupee, level with welfare (17 paise)
- **Fiscal rigidity:** High interest + administrative costs + welfare commitments → crowds out capital outlay
