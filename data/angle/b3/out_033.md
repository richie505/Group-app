## APCNF practices — terms → meaning | from: S10
- **Principles (ZBNF, Subhash Palekar):**
  - No chemicals; seed treatment with local inputs
  - **Mulching**; microbial cultures
  - Crop diversity/intercropping; minimum tillage; livestock integration

| **Practice** | **What it is** |
|---|---|
| Beejamrutham | Seed treatment with cow dung, cow urine and lime [GK] |
| **Jeevamrutham** | Bio-fertiliser from **cow dung, cow urine, jaggery, pulse flour, soil** |
| Ghanajeevamrutham | Solid form of jeevamrutham |
| Mulching (Acchadana) | Crop-residue/straw cover to keep moisture and suppress weeds |
| **Pre-Monsoon Dry Sowing (PMDS)** | Sowing before the rains on residual moisture; millets, pulses, oilseeds |
| 365-day green cover | Cover cropping and polyculture keep the soil covered all year |

- **Other practices:** Native seeds, no/minimal tillage, livestock integration, trap crops (marigold, castor)
- **PMDS in Kurnool:** **92,000 acres**, due to **El Nino** rain deficit (Jul 2026)
- ⚠ **Trap:** Beejamrutham = seed treatment; Jeevamrutham = bio-fertiliser; Ghanajeevamrutham = solid form
