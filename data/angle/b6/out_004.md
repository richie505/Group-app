## IMF, World Bank group and multilateral banks → terms and members | from: S11, S12
# 1. IMF and World Bank group
- **IMF:**
  - Formal existence **27 Dec 1945** (29 members); operations **1 Mar 1947**
  - **France** = first borrower
  - Lends only to members for BoP problems
  - **ESAF** had the softest terms
  - **Rapid Financing Instrument:** quick liquidity without a full programme (disasters, pandemics, post-conflict)
- **IFC:** private-sector arm (184 members)
- **SAPs (1980s-90s):** austerity, privatisation, devaluation; studies link them to rising poverty and inequality in the Global South
# 2. SDR, quota and voting share
- **SDR:** IMF reserve asset ("paper gold"); basket = **USD, euro, yuan, yen, pound**
- **India's forex:** foreign currency assets + gold + SDRs + **reserve tranche**
  - Reserve position rose to **$4.7 bn** (2025-26)
- **BRICS vote share:** **20% of world GDP but 11% of IMF votes** in 2011; now ~40% of GDP with barely higher vote share
  - New Delhi Declaration seeks quota and voting reform
- **SHANTI Act 2025:** caps nuclear liability at **300 million SDRs** (~₹3,900 crore)
