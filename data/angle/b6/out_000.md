## Capital, districts and places in news | from: S1, S2
# 1. Amaravati — capital
- **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Act, 2026:** Parliament made **Amaravati** the **sole, permanent capital**
  - Retrospective, with effect from **2 June 2024**; ends the three-capitals plan
  - Earlier: Section 5 of the 2014 Act made **Hyderabad** common capital for 10 years (to Jun 2024)
- **Governance framework:** APCRDA hired a consultant (Mar-Apr 2026), in line with the 73rd/74th Amendments
- **Planned in Amaravati:** **National Judicial Academy** campus + a Quantum Valley
# 2. New districts → headquarters
- **Reorganisation (31 Dec 2025):** Districts **26 → 28** (new districts **Polavaram** and **Markapuram**); boundaries of 17 districts changed

| **New district** | **Headquarters** | **Carved from** |
|---|---|---|
| **Polavaram** | **Rampachodavaram** (not Polavaram) | ASR district: Rampachodavaram and Chinturu divisions; 11 mandals, ~3.49 lakh people, mostly tribal |
| **Markapuram** | Markapuram | Western Prakasam: Markapuram and Kanigiri divisions; 21 mandals, 11.42 lakh people |

- **Annamayya district:**
  - HQ moved from **Rayachoti to Madanapalle**
  - Divisions: Madanapalle, Pileru, Rayachoti; 25 mandals
- **Revenue divisions:** Five new ones notified (2 Jan 2026)
- ⚠ **Polavaram** district HQ = **Rampachodavaram**, not Polavaram; **Annamayya** HQ = **Madanapalle**, not Rayachoti

## P4 → objective, model and scale | from: S6
- **P4 = Public-Private-People Partnership:** Launched **Ugadi, 30 Mar 2025**; Planning Dept.
- **Goal:** **complete eradication of poverty** (Zero Poverty sutram)
- **Model:** Well-off **Margadarsis** (mentors) adopt poor **Bangaru Kutumbam** families; government only facilitates
- **Swarna Andhra P4 Foundation:** Poverty eradication, under the Planning Dept.
- **Scale:**
  - Phase 1: ~**21.8 lakh** families
  - About **9,000** Kuppam families adopted by Adani, TVS Motor and MEIL (TH, 6 Jul 2026)
- ⚠ **Margadarsi** = mentor (adopts); **Bangaru Kutumbam** = poor family (adopted)
> Exam angle: "primary goal of P4" = poverty eradication; match scheme → department/benefit.

## Health schemes → coverage, limits and new initiatives | from: S8
# 1. Dr NTR Vaidya Seva — coverage
- **Dr. NTR Vaidya Seva:** Renamed from Rajiv Aarogyasri; run by the Dr NTR Vaidya Seva Trust

  - 5 more cardiac procedures (Mar 2026)
- **Other Trust schemes:** Employees Health Scheme, Working Journalists Health Scheme, Aarogya Raksha (APL), Amrutha (orphans)
# 2. Hybrid model and limits
| **Layer / limit** | **Fact** |
|---|---|
| Insurance layer (announced Jan 2025) | **₹2.5 lakh per person** for all residents under the **Universal Health Policy** |
| Trust layer (announced Jan 2025) | Trust-funded treatment up to **₹25 lakh** per family for the poor (APPSC 2025 key); PYQ answer: **₹25 lakh** |
| Procedures (2024-25) | Up to **₹5 lakh per family/yr**; total up to **₹25 lakh** |
| Cancer | **no limit for cancer** |
| Eligibility | Annual family income up to **₹5 lakh** |
| Parallel scheme | Runs alongside **Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY** (₹5 lakh/family) |

- ⚠ **₹2.5 lakh** = per person (insurance); **₹25 lakh** = per family (Trust); **₹5 lakh** = per family a year
# 3. New initiatives (2025-26)
- **SHRESTH** = **State Health Regulatory Excellence Index** (Aug 2025): strengthen the **health regulatory system** - benchmarks State drug regulators; MoHFW, run by **CDSCO**
- **C-sections:** Audit in network hospitals (Jul 2026)
- **Sanjeevani:** Digital health profile + ABHA ID

  - Statewide from **1 Jan 2027** after the Kuppam pilot (TH, 27 Aug 2026)
