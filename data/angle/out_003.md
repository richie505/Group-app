=== ROW 123 | Poverty, unemployment and social exclusion as social problems

## Poverty and exclusion — definitions, types and FIRSTs | from: S0, S3, S6
# 1. Definitions
- **Absolute poverty:** Below the minimum needed to survive
- **Social exclusion:** Process that shuts people out of facilities, benefits and opportunities others enjoy
- **Bases of exclusion:** Caste, gender, disability, religion, region
- **Practices of exclusion:** Untouchability, segregation, denied access to land, water and jobs (UPSC)
# 2. Social indicators of poverty
- Illiteracy, malnutrition, poor health care, lack of safe water and sanitation
  - ⚠ **Trap:** UPPSC 2025 keyed "**size of the house**" (Only 4) as NOT a social indicator

## Committees on the poverty line → method and estimate | from: S1
| **Committee** | **Year** | **Method** | **Line / estimate** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Y.K. Alagh** | **1979** | Calories: **2,400 rural, 2,100 urban** | Spending needed for those calories |
| **Lakdawala** | report **1993** | Calorie base; **state-specific** lines; **CPI-AL (rural), CPI-IW (urban)** | 2004-05: 27.5% |
| **Tendulkar** | set up **2005**, report 2009 | Dropped the calorie anchor; **MRP**; urban basket for both; health and education included | 2004-05: 37.2%; 2011-12: **21.9%** |
| **Rangarajan** | report **2014** | Food plus non-food (housing, clothing, education, health, transport) | 2011-12: **₹32 rural / ₹47 urban a day** (₹972/₹1,407 a month) |

- **Cut-off confusion:**
  - ⚠ **Trap:** APSES and CDI wrongly give ₹972/₹1,407 as Tendulkar's; those are **Rangarajan's** (APPSC-GS 2025 key confirms ₹32)
- **2025 update:** RBI updated the **Rangarajan** line with **HCES 2022-23** + a poverty-line-basket price index (Oct 2025)
- ⚠ **Alagh** = calories only; **Tendulkar** = dropped the calorie anchor; **Rangarajan** = food plus non-food

|---|---|

| In force | **2 Feb 2006** |
| Renamed **MGNREGA** | **2 Oct 2009** |
| **VB-G RAM G Bill, 2025** passed | **18 Dec 2025** |

# 3. MGNREGA — provisions
- **Core guarantee:** **Legal right** to **100 days** of unskilled wage work per rural household a year
  - No work within 15 days → unemployment allowance due
  - One-third of workers must be women
  - Gram Sabha holds **social audits**
- **VB-G RAM G Bill, 2025:** Raises guarantee to **125 days** + changes funding pattern
- ⚠ **100 days** = MGNREGA; **125 days** = VB-G RAM G
