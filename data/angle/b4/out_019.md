=== ROW 70 | Urbanisation in India - trends, class-size of towns, million-plus cities, urban agglomerations

## Urbanisation trend 1901-2011 — data, phases and projections | from: S0
# 1. Census → urban share
| **Census** | **Urban %** | **Note** |
|---|---|---|
| 1901 | **10.84 (10.8%, ~11%)** | - |
| 1931 | **11.99** | 1901-31 = slow phase |
| 1951 | 17.29 | - |
| 1961 | **17.97** | 1931-61 = medium phase |
| 1971 | 19.91 | - |
| 1981 | 23.34 | - |
| 1991 | 25.72 | - |
| 2001 | **27.81** | 28.6 crore urban |
| 2011 | **31.16** | **37.71 crore (377.1 million)** urban; 1961 onward = rapid phase |

- **Data note:** Figures for 1931, 1961, 2001 and 2011 from one source; other years from general notes
# 2. Three phases
| **Phase** | **Years** | **Features** |
|---|---|---|
| **Slow** | 1901-31 | Urban growth about 0.35% a year; 33.46 million people in 2,049 towns in 1931 |
| **Medium** | 1931-61 | Towns 2,049 → 2,330; 78.94 million urban in 1961 |
| **Rapid** | 1961 onwards | Urban population 78.94 million → 377.11 million (**+262%**) |

- **Number of towns:** about **1,827 in 1901** → **7,935 in 2011** (UPSC)
- **Other indicators of urbanisation:** number of towns, urban share, urban growth rate, average size of an urban centre, urban density
# 3. Projections and global picture
- **Projections:**
  - Urban population **about 60 crore by 2031**
  - More than **50%** of Indians urban by **2050**
- **World Cities Report 2026** (UN-Habitat, released at World Urban Forum, **Baku**):
  - About **40% (~3.4 billion)** people face housing shortages
  - **1.1 billion** live in slums or informal settlements

## Census 2011 urban jump — data, FIRST, causes and components | from: S1, S2
# 1. Size of the 2001-11 jump
| **Indicator** | **Figure** |
|---|---|
| **2011 split** | Rural **68.84%** (833.1 million) vs urban **31.16%** (377.1 million) |
| **Urban population** | 286 million → **377.1 million** (**+31.8%**) |
| **Urban share** | **27.81%** (2001) → **31.16%** |

- ⚠ **Trap:** "31.80% of total population" = **decadal growth**; urban share = **31.16%** (Census figure)
- **Milestone (FIRST):** **First time since Independence** absolute urban increase (about 91 million: urban **91.0 million** vs rural **90.4 million**) > rural increase
# 2. Components
- **Main reason:** **Reclassification of rural areas as urban**
  - Census towns: **1,362 → 3,894** (APPSC 2017 PYQ)
  - ⚠ **Key:** "categorisation of erstwhile rural areas as urban" (APPSC-G1 2017)
- **Components of urban growth:** **natural increase** + **rural-urban migration** + **new towns / reclassification**
> Exam angle: the reason for the 2001-11 urban jump (reclassification), and ordering the census years by urban share.
# 3. Causes of urbanisation
- **Causes (APPSC 2008, "all of the above"):**
  - Growth of industry and trade
  - Pressure of population on rural land
  - Low farm productivity
  - Services and transport

| **Factor** | **Effect** |
|---|---|
| **Push factors** | Monsoon failure, rural unemployment |
| **Pull factors** | Jobs, health and education facilities, transport links |
| **Industrialisation** | Industry and trade create urban jobs |
| **Migration** | Main reason urban and rural growth differ |

## Urban terms and class-size of towns | from: S3
# 1. Terms → meaning
| **Term** | **Meaning** |

=== ROW 72 | Human development - concept, HDI components and India's position

## Human development — persons, reports and concept | from: S0
# 1. Persons → contribution
| **Person / body** | **Contribution** |
|---|---|
| **UNDP** | Publishes the Human Development Report; **first HDR in 1990** |
| **Mahbub ul Haq** | Pakistani economist; conceived the HDR |
| **Amartya Sen** | Co-conceived the HDR (Nobel in Economics **1998**); capabilities = **substantive freedoms** to lead lives people value; avoided prescribing a fixed universal list (10 Mar 2026) |

- **Book → author:** "Development as Freedom" (1999) = Amartya Sen
# 2. Concept
- **Core idea:** Development = **enlargement of human choices**, not just rise in national income
- **Purpose:**
  - Make **people and their capabilities** the test of development
  - Show how two countries with same GNI per capita reach different outcomes

| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Growth** | Quantitative (income) |
| **Development** | Quantitative + qualitative |
| **Constituents** of development | Literacy, health, non-discrimination — not only instruments |

## HDI — dimensions, indicators and calculation | from: S1
# 1. Dimensions → indicators
| **Dimension** | **Indicator** | **Goalposts (min-max)** |
|---|---|---|
| **A long and healthy life** | **Life expectancy at birth** | 20-85 years [GK] |
| **Knowledge** | **Mean years of schooling** (adults 25+) and **expected years of schooling** (school-entry age) | MYS 0-15; EYS 0-18 [GK] |

- **Income:** Enters as a **logarithm** (diminishing importance as income rises); **$75,000 cap** stops very high incomes dominating
- **Aggregation:** **HDI = geometric mean** of the three indices
  - Replaced arithmetic mean in 2010, when literacy and gross enrolment gave way to MYS and EYS
  - **Literacy:** No longer used in HDI; replaced by years of schooling in 2010
# 2. Categories
| **Category** | **HDI value** |
|---|---|
| Very High | 0.800+ |
| **High** | **0.700-0.799** |
| **Medium** | **0.550-0.699** |
| Low | below 0.550 |

# 3. Not in the HDI
- **Excluded:** Inequality, poverty, gender gaps, human security, empowerment, environment, military expenditure (APPSC-GS key)
  - Inequality → "loss in potential development due to inequality" = **IHDI** (APPSC-G2 2017)
> Exam angle: "which is NOT an HDI component" (inequality, military spending, MPI).

## Related indices → what they measure | from: S4
| **Index** | **What it measures** |
|---|---|
| **IHDI** | HDI discounted for inequality in each dimension. IHDI = HDI only if there is no inequality; otherwise **IHDI < HDI**; the gap is the "loss" due to inequality |
| **GDI** | **Ratio of female to male HDI** (health, education, earned income); close to **1 = parity**[GK] |
| **GII** | Reproductive health, empowerment, labour market. **0 = equality, 1 = worst**[GK] |
| **MPI** | Deprivations in health, education and living standards (UNDP-OPHI) |
| PQLI | Pre-HDI index: literacy, infant mortality, life expectancy |
| **HPI (Human Poverty Index)** | Forerunner of the MPI |

- ⚠ **Trap:** "0 = equal, 1 = women worst" range belongs to the **GII**, not GDI; GDI = female/male ratio
