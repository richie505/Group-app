=== ROW 30 | Continental resources - land, forest, water, mineral and energy resources; conservation

## Resources — types, depletion and global milestones | from: S0, S2

| **Basis** | **Types** |
|---|---|
| Origin | **Biotic** (plants, animals, micro-organisms: APPSC 2025 key) vs **Abiotic** (non-living: land, water, minerals, rocks, metals) |
| Exhaustibility | **Renewable** (forests, fish, water, wildlife) vs **Non-renewable** (fossil fuels, minerals; exhausted with use) |
| Status of development | Potential; Developed; **Stock** (exists / available but technology to use it is lacking, e.g. hydrogen from water); **Reserve** (subset / part of stock usable with present technology, kept for future) |
| Ownership | Individual, community, national, international |

- ⚠ **Stock** = technology lacking; **Reserve** = usable with present technology (a part of stock)
- **Depletion:** **Overuse** of natural resources → **resource depletion** (APPSC 2025 key)
- **Resource → cause of depletion (APPSC 2025 key):**
  - **Groundwater → deep bore tubewells for irrigation**
  - **Topsoil → brick production**
  - **Biodiversity habitats → expansion of agriculture**

| **Year** | **Milestone** |
|---|---|
| 1968 / 1972 | Club of Rome; report **"Limits to Growth"** (1972) |
| 1987 | **Brundtland Commission** report "Our Common Future" - defined sustainable development |
| 1994 | **Global response** to land degradation: **UNCCD 1994** |

## Terms → meaning: land, forest and water | from: S2, S4, S5

| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Nine-fold land-use classification** | Forests; land put to non-agricultural uses; barren and unculturable land; permanent pastures; miscellaneous tree crops; culturable wasteland; current fallow; other fallow; net sown area |
| **Culturable wasteland** | Uncultivated for **more than 5 years** |
| **Current fallow** | **Up to 1 year** = cropland left unsown in the current year |
| **Other fallow** | **1-5 years** |
| **Wasteland** | Degraded land that can be brought under vegetative cover with reasonable effort |
| **Silviculture** | Raising and tending forest trees/crops (regeneration, thinning, rotation) |
| **Agroforestry** | Trees grown with crops/livestock on the same land |
| **Shifting cultivation (jhum)** | Practised on NE hill slopes (e.g. Arunachal); degrades forests + soil erosion when fallow cycles shorten |
| **CGWB categories** | **safe, semi-critical, critical, over-exploited** (plus saline) |

- ⚠ Fallow ladder: current fallow **up to 1 year** → other fallow **1-5 years** → culturable wasteland **more than 5 years**
- **Wasteland mapping:** satellite-based under **National Wastelands Monitoring** using IRS remote-sensing data (IRS commissioned **1988**)
- **Degradation causes:** soil erosion, waterlogging, **salinisation**, nutrient depletion, falling water tables, soil pollution → mostly human-induced (UPSC)

## Mineral Acts and amendments → year → provision | from: S10

| **Act / amendment** | **Provision** |
|---|---|
| **MMDR Act 1957** | **Parent law** |
| **2015 amendment** | **Auction** mandatory for mineral concessions; created **District Mineral Foundation** (DMF) → in mining-affected districts, funded by miners' contributions linked to royalty; created National Mineral Exploration Trust |
| **2023 amendment** | Critical and strategic minerals (lithium etc.) auctioned by the Centre; exploration licence introduced |
| **MMDR Amendment Act 2026** | Passed Aug 2026 (14 Aug 2026); see below |

- **MMDR Amendment Act 2026 provisions:**
  - Bars States from taxing **mineral rights or mineral-bearing lands** except within Central limits
  - Union regulates mineral-bearing land by mineral density
  - **Retrospective** clause voids unrecovered State cesses
- **Criticism:** **Section 9D** undercuts the **2024 nine-judge SC verdict** (Mineral Area Development Authority vs SAIL)
  - Verdict: **royalty is not a tax**; States can tax mineral rights (**Entry 50**) and mineral land (**Entry 49**, State List)
- ⚠ **Entry 50** = mineral rights; **Entry 49** = mineral land

=== ROW 34 | India - physiographic division: the Himalayas (ranges, regional divisions, passes)

## Himalayas — dimensions, origin, structure and classification counts | from: S0

| **Dimension** | **Figure** |
|---|---|
| **Length** | **2,414 km** from **Nanga Parbat to Namcha Barwa** (IYB 2026: "about 2,400 km", depth 240-320 km) |

| **Area** | About **5 lakh sq km** |

- **Origin:**
  - Tethys sediments; uplift through the Tertiary (**Eocene to Pliocene, ~60-7 million years ago**)

  - Wider in **Kashmir**, narrower in the **North-East**

- **Slopes:** steep slope faces the plains vs gentle slope towards Tibet

| **Classification** | **Count and names** |

| Longitudinal parallel ranges | **3**: Himadri, Himachal, Shiwalik (IYB 2026) |

| Structural zones | **4** (APP) |
