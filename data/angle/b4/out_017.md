## Articles, Schedules and ST Orders → provisions | from: S2, S3
# 1. Articles → provision
| **Provision** | **Content** |
|---|---|
| **Art. 342** | President notifies STs for each state/UT **after consulting the Governor**. Only **Parliament** can change the list (ST Order, 1950). Art. 366(25) defines STs by reference to it |
| **Art. 244(1) - Fifth Schedule** | Scheduled Areas; Governor's special powers; Tribes Advisory Council |
| **Art. 244(2) - Sixth Schedule** | Autonomous districts in **Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram** |
| Art. 338A | National Commission for STs |

- **Art. 342(2):** Only an **Act of Parliament** can change the list; lists are state-specific
# 2. Fifth vs Sixth Schedule
| **Feature** | **Fifth Schedule** | **Sixth Schedule** |
|---|---|---|
| Area type | **Scheduled Areas** | **Tribal Areas** (autonomous districts/regions) |
| States | All states except Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram. Scheduled Areas are in **10**: AP, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, HP, Jharkhand, MP, Maharashtra, Odisha, Rajasthan, Telangana (VIS) | **Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram** (IYB) |
| Machinery | Governor's special powers; **Tribes Advisory Council** (TAC) of up to **20 members**, about 3/4 of them ST MLAs (IYB) | Autonomous District and Regional Councils (IYB) |

# 3. Tribal welfare minister - Article 164(1) states
- **Art. 164(1)** proviso: States must have a **Minister in charge of tribal welfare** (may also hold **SC and backward-class welfare**)
  - States: **Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh and Odisha** (**Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, MP, Odisha**)
- **Bihar:** Dropped by the **94th Amendment** (2006) [GK] → no Scheduled Areas now; Chhattisgarh + Jharkhand added
# 4. ST Orders and amendments → year
| **Order / Act** | **Year** | **Change** |
|---|---|---|
| **Base:** Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order | **1950** | Each change needs an amending Act |
| J&K inclusion | 1991 | Gujjars, Bakarwals, Gaddis, Sippis added |
| ST Order (Amendment) Act (J&K) | **2024** | Added **Pahari ethnic group, Paddari tribe, Koli and Gadda Brahmin** |

- **Origin:** **Dhebar Commission** → PTG category (**1973**) → renamed **PVTGs in 2006**
- **Four criteria:**
  - Pre-agricultural technology
  - Low literacy
  - Economic backwardness
  - Declining/stagnant population

=== ROW 66 | Racial groups of India - Risley and Guha classifications

## Basis of racial classification — terms and indices | from: S0
- **Traditional criteria:** Skin, eye and hair colour and form; cephalic, nasal and stature indices; blood groups

| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Cephalic index** | (maximum breadth / maximum length) x 100 |
| **Dolichocephalic** (long) | Living head CI 76.99 and below; dry skull CI 74.99 and below |
| Mesocephalic (medium) | Living head CI 77-81.99; dry skull CI 75-79.99 |
| **Brachycephalic** (round) | Living head CI **82 and above**; dry skull CI 80 and above |
| Leptorrhine (narrow nose) | Nasal index under 70 |
| Mesorrhine | Nasal index 70-84.9 |
| Platyrrhine (broad nose) | Nasal index 85 and above |
| **Stature** (Topinard) | Tall = 170 cm or more; small = under 160 cm |
| **leiotrichi** | Straight, coarse hair (Mongoloid) |
| **cymotrichi** | Straight-wavy hair (Caucasoid) |
| **ulotrichi** | Woolly/frizzly hair (Negroid) |

- ⚠ **leio – cymo – ulo** = **straight – wavy – woolly**

## Racial types → diagnostic traits | from: S0
| **Type** | **Diagnostic traits** |
|---|---|
| **Mongoloid** | **Yellowish** complexion, **oblique eyes** (epicanthic fold), **high cheekbones**, **sparse hair**, medium height, broad head, flat face (UPSC 1997 key; CDX; APP) |
| Negrito | Dark skin, woolly/frizzy hair, short stature, broad flat nose [GK] |
| Proto-Australoid | Dark skin, wavy hair, long head, depressed nasal root, broad flat nose, short-to-medium stature [GK] |
| Dravidian (Risley) | Long head, **broad nose**, dark complexion (CDX) |
| Nordic | Tall, fair, long-headed, light eyes [GK] |

| **Trait** | **Caucasoid** | **Mongoloid** | **Negroid** |
|---|---|---|---|

| Eye | Occasional lateral fold | **Medial epicanthic fold** common | Vertical fold |

| Nose | High bridge, narrow | Low-medium bridge | Low bridge, broad |
| Blood group | More A than B | High B | Both A and B |

## Scholars → classifications and statements | from: S1, S2, S3
| **Person** | **Statement / contribution** |
|---|---|
| **H.H. Risley** | **Census Commissioner for the 1901 Census**; organised its **ethnographic survey**; 7 types; exponent of the **racial theory of caste** → caste as a by-product of racial difference |
| **Haddon (1924)** | **Hair form** (leiotrichi, cymotrichi, ulotrichi) as main basis for world populations |
| **Eickstedt** (1930s) | Four main groups (Weddid, Melanid, Indid, Mongolid) on physical + regional criteria |
| **J.H. Hutton** (Census Commissioner, **1931**) | India peopled by **successive waves of migration** |
| **S.S. Sarkar** | **Six ethnic elements** based on the **cephalic index** (head shape; dolichocephals and others) |
| **Griffith Taylor** | Migration-zone theory → primitive races pushed to the periphery (e.g. Andaman Islands) |
| **B.S. Guha** | Based on **1931 Census** data → **6 main racial groups** (6 main races) with 9 sub-types |

- **Risley's method:** Used anthropometry, mainly the nasal index, to rank castes (**Risley** relied on the nasal index)
- **Risley's dictum:** "The social status of a caste varies inversely with its nasal index"
  - Now rejected as colonial race science
- **Book → author:** **The People of India** (1908) = Risley
- ⚠ **Risley** = nasal index; **Sarkar** = cephalic index; **Haddon** = hair form
- ⚠ **Risley** = 1901 Census; **Hutton** and **Guha** = 1931 Census

## Guha's six racial groups → region and peoples | from: S3
- **Six groups:**
  - (1) **Negrito**
  - (2) **Proto-Australoid**
  - (3) **Mongoloid** = Palaeo-Mongoloid (long-headed + broad-headed; north-east and sub-Himalaya) + **Tibeto-Mongoloid** (Sikkim, Bhutan) [GK]
  - (4) **Mediterranean** = Palaeo-Mediterranean (south) + Mediterranean (north) + Oriental (Punjab, Rajasthan) [GK]
  - (5) **Western Brachycephals** = Alpinoid + Dinaric + Armenoid
  - (6) **Nordic**

| **Racial type (Guha)** | **Region** | **Representative peoples / community** |
|---|---|---|
| **Negrito** | Andaman Islands; traces in the hills of the far south | Andamanese: **Onge, Jarawa, Great Andamanese, Sentinelese**; Kadar(s), Irula(s) traces [GK] |
| **Proto-Australoid** | Central and southern India (tribes) | **Santhal**, Munda, Oraon, Ho, Bhil, **Chenchu** |
| **Mongoloid** | Himalayan and **north-eastern** tribes (**NE hills and forests**) | **Palaeo-Mongoloid**: Sema Nagas, Lepchas; **Tibeto-Mongoloid**: Tibetans of Sikkim (massive head, tall, light-brown skin); **Shompen** (Great Nicobar) |
| **Mediterranean** | Palaeo-Mediterranean: Dravidian-speaking south and much of the peninsula; Mediterranean: Punjab, Rajasthan, Ganga plain | Dravidian-speaking peoples |
| **Western Brachycephals** | Broad-headed: Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka (Coorg), Bengal-Odisha | Alpinoid, Dinaric, Armenoid (Parsis) sub-types |
| **Nordic** | North-West India: Punjab, Rajasthan, NW Frontier [GK] | Tall, fair, long-headed |

- **Significance:** Most widely cited classification of the Indian population

## Criticism of race classification and genetic studies | from: S6
| **Factor** | **Effect** |
|---|---|
| **Shared genome** | All humans share **over 99%** of genetic material → racial division subjective; one species, Homo sapiens |
| **Modern science** | Rejects ranking by race |
| **Colonial basis** | Old schemes rest on visible traits + colonial ethnography; Risley's race-caste theory rejected |
| **Genetics (2009)** | Most Indians descend from a mix of **Ancestral North Indians (ANI)** + **Ancestral South Indians (ASI)** |
| **Gut microbiome study (2026)** | Tribal groups vs genome-verified relatives who moved to cities → differences point to **environment, not genes** |
