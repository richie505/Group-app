=== ROW 61 | India - literacy: levels, gender and regional gaps, trends

## Literacy — Census definition and FIRSTs | from: S0, S5, S6
# 1. Definition of literate
- **Literate:** Person aged **7 and above** who can **both read and write with understanding** in any language
  - One who can only read = not literate
  - Censuses 1951-1971 counted age 5+; age 7+ from 1981
- **Full literacy (ULLAS):** = **95%+** among those aged 15+ (10 Jul 2026)
# 2. FIRSTs
| **FIRST** | **FACT** |
|---|---|
| **FIRST district: Ernakulam (Kerala)** | First successful **Total Literacy Campaign**; India's first totally literate district in **1990** |
| First fully literate town (1989) | Kottayam |
| First fully literate UT (ULLAS) | **Ladakh** (2024) |
| First fully literate state (ULLAS) | **Mizoram** (2025) |
- **Fully literate sequence (ULLAS):** **Ladakh** → **Mizoram** → Goa, Tripura, Himachal Pradesh (5th State/UT), **Sikkim (5th state)** → **Uttarakhand (6th, Jul 2026, 98%+)**
> Exam angle: "first" facts (district, UT, state) and scheme age group (15+).
- ⚠ First UT **Ladakh (2024)**; first state **Mizoram (2025)**; first TLC district **Ernakulam**

## National data — trend, gender gap and rural-urban gap | from: S0, S1, S3

=== ROW 62 | India - occupational structure: main and marginal workers, work participation rate, cultivators and agricultural labourers

## Terms → meaning — worker categories, Census Town, PLFS indicators | from: S0, S4, S6, S7
# 1. Census worker categories
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Main worker** | Worked at least **183 days (6 months)** in reference year |
| **Marginal worker** | Worked less than 183 days |
| Cultivator | Cultivates own or leased land |
| Agricultural labourer | Works on another person's land for wages |
| Household industry worker | Production within the household (e.g., handloom) |
| Other workers | All other work - factory, trade, transport, services, government |
- **Four categories:** **Cultivators**, **agricultural labourers**, **household industry workers**, **other workers** (services, trade, industry)
- **Census non-worker groups:** Students, household duties, dependants, pensioners, rentiers, beggars/vagrants, others
- **Census Town:**
  - Population **5,000+**
  - **75%** of male workers non-agricultural
  - Density **400/sq km**
  - Unchanged since 1981; retained for **Census 2027** (17 Sep 2026)
# 2. PLFS indicators
| **Indicator** | **Definition** |
|---|---|
| **LFPR** | Persons in the labour force (employed + unemployed) per 100 persons = (employed + unemployed) / population x 100 |
| **WPR** | Employed persons as % of population = employed persons / **total population** x 100 (RPSC 2024 key) |
| Unemployment Rate (UR) | Unemployed as % of the **labour force** |
| Proportion Unemployed (PU) | Unemployed as % of population |
- **Agency:** PLFS run by the **NSO (MoSPI)**
- **Age group:** Headline rates for age 15+
  - "Working age" defined variously as 15-59, 15-64 or 16-64 → check the stem
> Exam angle: WPR's denominator is total population; LFPR's includes the unemployed.
# 3. Activity status
| **Status** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Usual status** | **365-day** reference; unemployment under it = **chronic** |
| **UPSS** | Employed if worked **30+ days** in the year |
| **Current Weekly Status (CWS)** | 7-day week; employed if worked **1 hour on any day** (at least **1 hour** on any one day); captures short-term/seasonal unemployment |
| **CDS** | Records activity for each day of the week |
- **U-curve:** India followed the first half of **Claudia Goldin's U-curve** till 2018-19
- ⚠ UR denominator = **labour force**; PU and WPR denominator = **population**

# 2. No notified SC population
- **No SC notified:** **Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Andaman & Nicobar Islands and Lakshadweep**
- **Mizoram:** Negligible SC share (≈ 0.1%)
  - ⚠ **Trap:** APPSC 2022 key grouped it with the zero-SC list → pick the option with Nagaland, Lakshadweep, A&N and Arunachal
  - Single "zero SC" state = **Nagaland** (UPPSC 2016)
- **Reason:** Populations overwhelmingly tribal; SC status comes from untouchability in the Hindu caste order vs STs identified by tribal traits
# 3. District-level concentration
- **Highest SC share district:** **Koch Bihar (West Bengal), about 50%**
  - Next: **Shahid Bhagat Singh Nagar (Punjab)**, above 40%
- **Pattern:**
  - Densest in fertile alluvial plains (Punjab, Ganga plain, West Bengal, coastal deltas) → settled farming used landless labour
  - Sparse in hill, forest and north-eastern tribal districts
