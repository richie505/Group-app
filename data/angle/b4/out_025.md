## Postal network → PIN code, Digi PIN, APT and services | from: S1
# 1. PIN code
- **PIN code:** 6 digits; introduced **15 August 1972**

| **Digit(s)** | **Indicates** |
|---|---|
| 1st | **Postal zone/region** (1-8 geographical zones; 9 = Army Postal Service) [GK] |
| 2nd | Sub-zone (postal circle) [GK] |
| 3rd | Sorting district [GK] |
| Last 3 | Delivery post office [GK] |

# 2. Digi PIN and APT platform
- **Digi PIN:** **10-digit alphanumeric** code for precise delivery
  - Part of the Advanced Postal Technology (APT) platform
  - India's first **Digital Address DPI** - Digital Address Code (DAC) project
- **APT built by:** **CEPT** (Centre for Excellence in Postal Technology)
- **Hosting:** **MeghRaj 2.0** cloud
- **Rollout:**
  - Pilot: Karnataka circle (May-Jun 2025)
  - National rollout across 23 postal circles completed **4 August 2025**; **1.64 lakh offices** migrated
- **Daily load:** ~32 lakh bookings + 37 lakh deliveries
- ⚠ PIN = **6 digits**; Digi PIN = **10-digit alphanumeric**
# 3. Services and network
| **Service** | **Year / feature** |
|---|---|
| **India Post Payments Bank (IPPB)** | Payments bank of the Department of Posts; launched **1 September 2018** |
| **Speed Post** | Express mail service; launched **1986** |
| First-class mail | Letters, postcards |
| Second-class mail | Registered newspapers, books, parcels |

- **Network:** One of the largest postal networks in the world; often the only formal link in interior areas
- **AP:** **10,660 post offices** (2025-26)

## Transmission media and submarine cables → features and links | from: S3
# 1. Medium → signal → capacity
| **Medium** | **Signal** | **Speed/capacity** |
|---|---|---|
| **Optical fibre** | Light pulses by total internal reflection | **Fastest, highest bandwidth**, low loss, immune to electromagnetic interference [GK] |

| Radio waves | Wireless, terrestrial towers | Mobility; capacity limited by spectrum [GK] |
| Satellite | Microwave via satellite | Reaches remote areas; high **latency** for geostationary orbit (~36,000 km) [GK] |

- **OFC:** Backbone of internet and data connectivity
