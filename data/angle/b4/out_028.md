## Logistics cost, policy and indices → ranks | from: S3
# 1. Logistics cost
- **Official estimate:** **7.97% of GDP (2023-24)**
  - Replaces older **13-14%** figure in older notes
# 2. National Logistics Policy (NLP 2022)
- **Role:** Complements **GatiShakti** → improves processes, regulation, skills, technology
- **Targets (by 2030):**
  - **Single-digit logistics cost**
  - **Top 25** of the **LPI**
- **World Bank LPI:** India rank **38th (2023)**
