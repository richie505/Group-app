=== ROW 96 | International trade - patterns, trade blocs, chokepoints; India's export and import composition

## Trade policy phases, Acts and missions → year | from: S0
# 1. Phase / Act → year and key features
| **Phase** | **Key features** |
|---|---|
| 1950s-1990 | **Import substitution**, an inward-looking policy of four decades; QRs, high tariffs |
| 1965 | **Kandla EPZ**, Asia's first |
| **1991** New Trade Policy | two-step **rupee devaluation (July 1991)**; move from **quantitative restrictions** to a price-based mechanism; import licensing eased |
| 1992 | **partial current-account convertibility**: 40% at the official rate, 60% at the market rate |
| 1993 / 1999 | FERA liberalised in 1993, then replaced by **FEMA, 1999** |
| 2000 / 2005 | SEZ policy; **SEZ Act 2005** (in force **10 Feb 2006**) |
| FTP 2015-20 | MEIS incentives for merchandise exports |
| **FTP 2023** | moved from incentives to **remission** of duties; ease of exports, e-initiatives, **Districts as Export Hubs **(APPCA, Jan 2026); remission and district focus. In force from **1 Apr 2023** with no end date [GK] |

- ⚠ **FERA (1993 liberalised) → FEMA (1999)**; **SEZ Act 2005** → in force **2006**
# 2. Terms → meaning
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Import substitution** | inward-looking policy (1950s-1990): QRs, high tariffs |
| **Export promotion ("outward-looking")** | Pushes manufactured exports; East Asian economies adopted it in the 1970s and moved ahead of India |
| **Special Rupee Vostro Accounts (SRVA)** | accounts through which exports + imports are invoiced and settled in **INR** (RBI rupee settlement, July 2022) |

# 3. Missions and targets
- **Export Promotion Mission (2025-26 to 2030-31):** Run by DGFT through Niryat Protsahan + Niryat Disha
- **Target:** **US$ 2 trillion exports by 2030** (goods + services)
- **NITI's EPI:** Aligned to **$1 trillion merchandise exports by 2030**
  - ⚠ **$2 trillion** = goods + services; **$1 trillion** = merchandise only
# 4. RBI rupee settlement (July 2022)
- Purpose: less dependence on the dollar; INR internationalisation; trade with sanctioned/embargoed partners
- Nov 2025: surplus SRVA balances may be invested in **corporate bonds and commercial papers**
- India-Tanzania trade settled through SRVA

## Causes and issues — east vs west coast ports, FTA criticism | from: S11, S4
# 1. Why the east coast has fewer ports
| **Factor** | **Effect** |
|---|---|
| **East coast:** wide, **shallow continental shelf** (deltas of the Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna and Kaveri) | fewer natural harbours |

| **West coast:** submerged, indented coastline + deep water close to shore; estuaries rather than deltas | natural harbours |
