## Soils → parent rock, formation and properties | from: S1, S2, S3, S4
# 1. Red soils
- **Parent rock:** Ancient weathered **granite, gneiss, Dharwar schists**, greenstones
- **Colour:** **Ferric oxides**
- **Properties:** Well drained; **poor in N, P, organic matter** (**nitrogen, phosphorus**); low moisture retention
# 2. Black soils
- **Parent rock:** **Deccan Trap basalt** in Prakasam, Palnadu, Guntur, East Godavari + crystalline schists and basic gneiss
- **Texture:** Fine, clayey; neutral to strongly alkaline
- **Nutrients:** Rich in **calcium, potash, magnesium** (Ca, K, Mg); poor in nitrogen, phosphorus, humus (N, P)
- **Water:** **High water retention**; deep cracks when dry → **"self-ploughing"**
# 3. Deltaic alluvium
- **Origin:** Laid down as **river-borne silt** by the **Godavari and Krishna**; renewed with silt each flood
- **Texture:** Porous + loamy; high moisture retention
- **Nutrients:** Rich in **potash and lime**
# 4. Laterite soils
- **Formation:** **High rainfall and high temperature** (**high temperature and heavy rainfall**) → intense **leaching** of silica and lime
  - ⚠ **Trap:** "Formed in semi-arid low-rainfall regions" is the **incorrect** statement - **not** in semi-arid areas (APPSC 2023/2024 key; APPSC-G2 2023 and 2024 key)
- **Composition:** Rich in hydrated **iron and aluminium oxides**
- **Nature:** **Acidic (high acidity)**, poor fertility, red to yellowish-red, well drained
- **Nitrogen:** Some notes say plentiful; standard fact = poor in nitrogen
- ⚠ Rich in **Ca, K, Mg** = black; rich in **potash and lime** = alluvium; rich in **iron and aluminium oxides** = laterite

## Problem soils, erosion and degradation → areas and causes | from: S5, S6
# 1. Nature of the problem
- **Salinity:** Lowers yields + breaks down soil structure
- **Alkalinity:** High pH from limestone/basalt parent rock or alkaline irrigation water → cuts nutrient availability
- **Acidic soils:** Laterite soils
- **Alkaline tendency:** Black soils (neutral to strongly alkaline)
- **Rayalaseema red soils:** Shallow; low moisture retention + low organic matter
- **Soil organic carbon:** Sandy soils + steep slopes lose SOC (leaching, erosion); clay soils hold more
# 2. Area → problem
| **Area** | **Problem** |
|---|---|
| **Anantapur, Sri Sathya Sai** | Drought-induced degradation, **desertification**, wind erosion of topsoil |
| **Uppada (Kakinada)** | Coastal soil erosion from sea-level rise and waves |
| Delta regions | Salinity, waterlogging |
| ASR, Parvathipuram Manyam | **Podu** (shifting) cultivation strips topsoil |
- **Delta regions:** **salinity and waterlogging** from poor drainage + faulty irrigation
- **Coast:** **saline water intrusion**
- **Rayalaseema plateau:** Saline soils high in lime and salt → unfit for cropping
- **Groundwater:** **39 watersheds (896 villages)** classed "saline" (2025-26)
- !! Not in your sources: AP districts affected by salinity, alkalinity or waterlogging, and their reclamation area.
# 3. Causes
- Heavy chemical use (Guntur chilli + Krishna paddy belts)
- Overgrazing, deforestation, erratic rain

## Climatologists → classification schemes and regions | from: S0, S1

- **Moisture types:** **perhumid/wet (A)** → humid (B) → sub-humid (C) → semi-arid (D) → **arid (E)**
| **Thornthwaite type (India)** | **Places** |
|---|---|
| Wettest (perhumid) | **Western Ghats-west coast and the North-East** |
| Arid | **western Rajasthan (Thar)** + Kutch |
| Semi-arid | rain-shadow interior Deccan |

  - Up = **natality + immigration**
