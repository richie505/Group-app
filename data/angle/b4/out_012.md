## Eastern Ghats — features, rocks and limits | from: S6
- **Character:** **Discontinuous, rugged, highly eroded** range, not a continuous chain — **discontinuous and eroded hills**, cut by the Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri (PYQ, APPSC-GS 2025 key; May 2026)
- **Vs Western Ghats:** **Older and lower**
- **Height:** Average 400-600 m (some notes say 600-900 m)
  - North: 60-70 km wide, ~1,200 m high
- **Rocks:** Mainly **charnockites and khondalites**; Archaean, over 2.5 billion years old
- **Division:** **Godavari and Krishna** split the AP Ghats into northern and southern parts
  - Northern = "Eastern Ranges"; southern = "Kadapa Ranges"
- **Limits:** Begin with Mahendragiri in the north (in AP); beyond AP run through Javadi and Shevaroy hills → join the **Western Ghats near the Nilgiris**
  - Meeting point at the **Nilgiris** (Gudalur) via Biligirirangan + Male Mahadeshwara hills
- **Rivers rising here:** **Vamsadhara, Nagavali and Sabari**

## Polavaram, Pattiseema and canals → purpose and beneficiaries | from: S5, S6
# 1. Polavaram
- **Status:** Multipurpose dam on the Godavari; **National Project under Sec. 90, APRA 2014**
  - ⚠ **Trap:** Some notes (Apr 2026) wrongly cite Sec. 89
- **Design:** **194.6 TMC** storage, **7.20 lakh acres**, **960 MW**, **80 TMC** diverted to the Krishna
- **Progress:** **89% complete**; to be dedicated by **March 2027**, before the Pushkarams (14 Jul 2026)
  - Earlier figure: 78.64% (Dec 2024)
  - CM target: complete before the **2027 Godavari Pushkaralu** (7 Apr 2026)
- **Left Main Canal (LMC):** Godavari water to North Coastal Andhra and Visakhapatnam
  - Revised Administrative Sanction issued for the LMC to supply Visakhapatnam (Jun 2026)
  - Godavari water set to reach **North Coastal Andhra on 2 Sep 2026** using pumps (dam not finished)
  - **27 TMC** planned for the Visakhapatnam region, incl. 5 TMC to the Kanithi reservoir at the steel plant (1 Sep 2026)
# 2. Pattiseema
- **Type:** **Lift irrigation** scheme (APPSC-GS 2018)
- **Function:** Lifts Godavari water into the Polavaram Right Main Canal → Krishna at Prakasam Barrage
- **2026 data:** Diverted **17.52 tmc ft** this season by 10 Aug 2026 (TH, 10 Aug 2026), while ~521 tmc ft of Godavari water flowed to the sea
# 3. Telugu Ganga project
- **Source → beneficiary:** **Krishna** surplus water from **Srisailam** → drinking water to **Chennai (Tamil Nadu)**
  - Beneficiary State: **Tamil Nadu** (APHQ 2025)
- **Route:** Srisailam → Penna basin (Somasila) → Kandaleru → **Poondi** (TN)
- **Irrigation:** Also irrigates Rayalaseema and Nellore; **2.75 lakh acres** in Kurnool and Kadapa (2026)
- **Conceived by:** **N.T. Rama Rao**; inaugurated in the 7th Plan (1985-90)
- **Water shares:** **Maharashtra, Karnataka and AP each gave up 5 tmc** = 15 tmc for Chennai
- **2026:** **10 tmc ft** to be released for Chennai's drinking water
- **Industry:** Bulk water to the **Naidupeta** industrial cluster (2025-26)
