## Terms → meaning — density, Census Town, "Great Divide" | from: S2, S6, S9
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Density** | Persons **per unit area** (per sq km); crude measure of human-land relationship |

## Decadal growth 1901-2011 — phases and data | from: S2
# 1. Phases
| **Phase** | **Period** | **Features** |
|---|---|---|
| I - Stagnant/stationary | **1901-1921** | High birth and death rates; growth **negative in 1911-21** (**-0.31%**) |
| II - Steady growth | **1921-1951** | Mortality fell (health, sanitation, transport) while birth rate stayed high |
| III - Population explosion | **1951-1981** | Rapid fall in mortality, high fertility; average annual growth about **2.2%**; migration from Tibet, Bangladesh, Nepal, Pakistan |
| IV - High but declining / slowdown | **Post-1981** | Crude birth rate falls: later marriage, female education |
- **Phases (sub-dates):**
  - Explosion = **1961-1971** (some notes give 1951-81)
  - Peak decadal growth **24.80% (1961-71)**
  - High-but-declining = **1981-2011**

| 1951 | 36.11 crore | 13.31% |

| 2001 | 102.87 crore | 21.54% |

=== ROW 59 | Demographic dividend, ageing and population policy

## Thinkers and books → population theories | from: S0
| **Theory / work** | **Author / propounder** | **Core idea** |
|---|---|---|
| **Principle of Population** / Essay on the Principle of Population (1798) | **Thomas Malthus** (**T.R. Malthus**) | Population grows in **geometric**, food in **arithmetic** progression; preventive and positive checks (APPSC-GS key: overpopulation theory = Malthus) |
| Demographic transition (1929) | **Warren Thompson** | Based on demographic history |
| Formal 3-stage transition theory (1940s-50s) | **Frank Notestein** | Birth/death rates change with economic development |
| Optimum population | **Edwin Cannan** | Population size at which per capita income is maximum |
| **The Population Bomb** (1968) | **Paul Ehrlich** | Warned of mass famine from overpopulation |
- **Malthus (1798):** Population grows **geometrically**, food **arithmetically** (UPPSC 2024 key)
  - **Positive checks:** Famine, disease, war → raise death rate
  - **Preventive checks:** Moral restraint, late marriage → lower birth rate
- **Marxian view:** "Overpopulation" = product of capitalism (relative surplus population/unemployment), not of nature
> Exam angle: UPPSC 2024 asked the Malthusian blanks (geometric, arithmetic).
- ⚠ Transition theory: **Thompson (1929)** first; **Notestein** formal 3-stage version (1940s-50s)

## Terms → meaning — transition, dividend, TFR | from: S1, S3, S5, S6, S10
# 1. Demographic transition stages
| **Stage** | **Name** | **Birth / death** | **Growth** | **Example** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | High stationary | High / high (high BR, high DR) | Low | Pre-industrial |
| **2** | Early expanding | High / falling (high BR, falling DR) | Explosion | India 1951-81 |
| **3** | Late expanding | Falling / low (falling BR, low DR) | Slowing | India overall now |
| **4** | Low stationary | Low / low (low BR, low DR) | Stable | **AP** |
| **5** | Declining | Very low / low (BR below DR) | Declining | Japan, Germany |
# 2. Terms
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **"Demographic winter"** | Persistent structural decline - fertility below 2.1, inverted pyramid |
| Demographic dividend (**UNFPA**) | Economic growth potential from a shift in age structure when the **working-age share (15-64)** exceeds the non-working share (**0-14 and 65+**) |
| Demographic dividend (alternative) | "A rise in economic growth due to a rise in the **share of working people** in the population" (APPSC-G2 2017 key) |
| **"Demographic nightmare"** | ES 2018-19 warning - dividend lost without health, education, skills and jobs |
| **TFR** | Average number of children a woman is expected to have over ages 15-49 (reproductive years) |
| **Replacement level 2.1** | Population stays stable across generations (**Total fertility rate and replacement level**) |
| **"shrinconomics"** | AP Health Minister's warning (1 Apr 2026) on the "alarming" fall in fertility |
- **Dividend age group:** asked by APPSC/ILO = **15-64 years** (APPSC-G1 2022, APPSC-GS 2019 keys)
  - **Indian projections:** Often use **15-59**
- **Dividend channels:**
  - More labour supply
  - **Higher savings and investment**
  - Lower **dependency ratio**
  - Freed human capital
> Exam angle: the age group (15-64) and the "rise in share of working people" definition are the most-asked points.

## Data — demographic history and India's dividend window | from: S2, S4
# 1. Demographic history

## Acts, schemes and cases — BBBP, PCPNDT Act, Cradle Baby Scheme | from: S7
| **Act / scheme** | **Year / place** | **Provision / aim** |
|---|---|---|
| Beti Bachao Beti Padhao | **2015** - **22 Jan 2015 at Panipat, Haryana** | Reverse falling CSR; improve CSR and SRB, survival, protection and education of the girl child |
| **PCPNDT Act, 1994** | 1994 | Bans sex selection + prenatal sex determination |
| Tamil Nadu **Cradle Baby Scheme (1992)** | 1992 | Against female infanticide |
# 1. Beti Bachao Beti Padhao
- **Approach:** Multi-sectoral action in gender-critical districts
- **Targets:** Institutional deliveries + girls' toilets in schools
- **10th anniversary:** 22 Jan - 8 Mar 2025
# 2. PCPNDT Act
- Tests only for genetic/chromosomal abnormalities
- Penalties for advertising and disclosing the sex of the foetus
- Machines registered; records kept for every scan
- **Unintended effect (birth vs survival):** After curbs on prenatal sex selection, **firstborn girls** → **25% higher child mortality** than firstborn boys (discrimination shifts from birth to survival)
# 3. Supreme Court
- **Supreme Court (Jun 2026):** Justices **Sanjay Karol and P.K. Mishra** - CSR shows "clear signs of improvement" but sex selection reflects deep-seated patriarchy (bias "deep-seated" despite improvement)
- ⚠ **PCPNDT 1994** (Act) vs **Cradle Baby 1992** (TN) vs **BBBP 2015** (Panipat)
