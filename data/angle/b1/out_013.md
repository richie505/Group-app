=== ROW 66 | Gupta art, literature, science and technology - Kalidasa, Aryabhata, Varahamihira, Ajanta, Mehrauli pillar

## Gupta age — FIRSTs, earliest and the classical-age label | from: S3, S4, S7, S8, S9, S12
| **FIRST / earliest** | **FACT** |
|---|---|
| First to treat **Mathematics as a distinct subject** (APPSC 2012) | **Aryabhata** - Aryabhatiya has a separate **Ganitapada** |
| Earliest epigraphic evidence of the place-value system in India | **Mankani plates, Gujarat (AD 595-596)** (UPSC 2026) |
| Earliest dated place-value numeral (some notes) | **Sankheda** copper plate, **594 CE** |
| Earliest zero written as a circle | **Gwalior inscription, 876 CE** |
| First images of Hindu gods | **Vishnu, Shiva** and other Hindu gods |
| First structural temples | Shift: Rock-cut → **free-standing structural temples** |
| First "completely evolved painting" | **Shwetagaja / Chhaddanta Jataka in Cave 10**, Ajanta (APPSC G2 2012 answer: Cave 10) |
| Among India's first **UNESCO World Heritage Sites** | Ajanta (**1983**, with Ellora) |
- **Gupta legacy:** **Decimal system** and **concept of zero** = lasting gift to world mathematics
- **Transmission:** Arab contact carried decimal place-value notation → Islamic world → Europe
- ⚠ **Mankani (595-596)** vs **Sankheda (594)** = two versions of the earliest place-value record; zero as a circle comes much later (**Gwalior, 876**)
# 1. Classical age label - for and against
- **For:** Peak of Sanskrit literature, mathematics and astronomy, Ajanta painting, Sarnath sculpture, first structural temples
- **Against:**
  - Land grants and feudatories
  - **vishti** (forced labour)
  - Decline of trade and towns; rigid caste; falling status of women
  - → "golden" mainly for the elite

- **Romaka Siddhanta:** Deals with **astronomy** (APPSC G2 2025); Roman/Greek influence, like Paulisa [GK]
- **Brihat Samhita:** Encyclopaedia - astronomy, astrology, geography, botany, architecture
- **Brihat Jataka:** Standard work on astrology (some notes misprint it "Brihatkatha Jataka")
- Work "possibly influenced by Greek astronomy"

## Mehrauli pillar and sculpture → ruler, site, feature | from: S6, S7
# 1. Mehrauli iron pillar
- **Ruler:** **Chandragupta II** (inscription names king "**Chandra**")
- **Claim:** Authority over **North-West India and Bengal**
- **Location:** Now at **Mehrauli, Delhi**; originally at **Udayagiri** near **Vidisha** (MP)
- **Metallurgy:** Has **not rusted for over fifteen centuries** → proof of Gupta ironwork (high-phosphorus iron forms a protective layer)
# 2. Sculpture schools and images
- **Origin:** Gupta stone sculpture grew out of the **Mathura school**
- **Sarnath school:**
  - **Chunar (buff) sandstone**, polished, little ornament
  - Seated Buddha in **Dharmachakra mudra**
  - Thin clinging robe covering both shoulders; halo with floral carving
  - Model for Buddhist art across Asia

| **Image** | **Place** | **Feature** |
|---|---|---|
| **Sultanganj Buddha** | **Bhagalpur, Bihar** | Over **2 m high bronze** = finest Gupta metal image |
| **Bamiyan (Afghanistan) Buddhas** | Afghanistan | Of the Gupta period |
| **Eran Varaha** | Eran | Shows Vaishnava presence |

- ⚠ **Sarnath** = Chunar sandstone; **Sultanganj** = bronze; **Mehrauli** = iron

## Ajanta → location, phases, patrons, panels, technique | from: S9
# 1. Ajanta - basics
- **Religion:** **Buddhist** (APPSC 2025; APPSC GS 2025)
- **Location:** **Aurangabad district, Maharashtra**; horseshoe-shaped gorge of the **Waghora** river
- **Discovery:** **1819 by John Smith** (British officer) while hunting tigers
- **Caves:** About **30 caves** (some notes count **25 viharas + 4 chaityas**); **5 Hinayana**, rest **Mahayana**
- **UNESCO:** Among India's first **UNESCO World Heritage Sites** (**1983**, with Ellora)
# 2. Phases and patrons
- **Patrons:** Satavahanas (early), then the **Vakatakas** (Harishena)

| **Phase** | **Caves** | **Fact** |
|---|---|---|
| Early (Satavahana) | **Caves 9 and 10** | Earliest paintings are **Satavahana-era**; **Shwetagaja / Chhaddanta Jataka in Cave 10** (**Svetagaja/Chhaddanta Jataka**) = first "completely evolved painting" (APPSC G2 2012 answer: Cave 10) |
| Later (Vakataka king Harisena / Harishena, 5th c.) | **Cave 16** | Excavated by his minister **Varahadeva** |
| Later | **Cave 17** | Brahmi inscription + **Bhava-chakra** (wheel of life) |
| Later | **Cave 19** | Chaitya with standing Buddha and two Yakshas |

- **Date:** Some notes say construction "started by Mauryas"; standard view = c. **2nd century BCE**, Satavahana era
# 3. Themes, panels and technique
- **Themes:** **Jatakas** / **Jataka tales** + secular life; Bodhi-tree worship and group dances

| **Panel** | **Theme** |
|---|---|
| Padmapani, Vajrapani | Bodhisattvas (Padmapani in Cave 1 [GK]); **Bodhisattva Padmapani** (and Vajrapani) (BPSC 2023) |
| Dying Princess | Secular theme (Cave 16 [GK]) |
| Mother and Child | Before the Buddha (Cave 17 [GK]) |
| Mahaparinirvana | Sculpture of the dying Buddha (Cave 26 [GK]) - a sculpture, not a painting |

- **Technique:** Tempera on dry lime plaster (**fresco secco**), not true fresco; outlines drawn in red, then filled with colour
  - ⚠ **Trap:** some notes say blue is absent at Ajanta; **lapis-lazuli** blue is in fact used

## Universal angles | from: S13 | universal
- **Significance:**
  - Gupta age = "classical age" of Sanskrit literature, maths (zero, decimal) and art
  - Fixed Sanskrit as language of inscriptions and courts
  - **Nalanda** drew students from China, Tibet, Korea and South-East Asia
- **AP angle:**
  - Ajanta's earliest paintings (Caves **9-10**) = Satavahana work; some notes link them to **Amaravati** style
  - Varahamihira also listed in AP-history notes
  - **4th-5th c.**: Vishnukundina adaptation of the Gupta rock-cut style
  - Regional cave temples: **Mogalrajapuram** (**5** caves, Nataraja), **Bhairavakonda** (Kapalika, **8** caves)
  - Satavahana-era Sanskrit works: **Katantra** (Sharvavarman), **Kamasutra** (Vatsyayana), Aryadeva's Chittashuddhi Prakaranam
- **Comparative note:** Charaka (Kushana) and Bhaskara II (**12th c.**) = frequent "not Gupta" distractors
- **Current relevance:**
  - Ashwin Prabhu's book on Ajanta's murals (3 Apr 2026)
  - PM gifted a **Sarnath Buddha in Dhyan mudra** to Thailand's King (Apr 2025)
- **Criticism & debates:** Varahamihira's Romaka/Paulisa siddhantas show Greek input → "Indian science" not isolated

## Harsha's religion and the Kannauj and Prayag assemblies | from: S5
# 1. Religion - stages and sects patronised
- **Stages:** Devout **Saiva** → **Hinayana** Buddhism → **Mahayana** (converted by Hiuen Tsang)
  - Influence of sister **Rajyashri** and Xuanzang
- **Pro-Buddhist measures:**
  - Banned animal food; punished killing of living beings
  - Thousands of **stupas**, rest houses, monasteries at Buddhist sacred sites
- **Tolerance:** Kept patronising **Brahmanical scholars**
# 2. Kannauj vs Prayag assembly
| **Feature / point** | **Kannauj assembly** | **Prayag assembly (Moksha Parishad)** |
|---|---|---|
| Year / frequency | **643 CE**, one-time | **Every five years** (every 5 years) |
| Place | Kannauj | Confluence of Ganga-Yamuna at **Prayag (Allahabad)** |

## Hiuen Tsang → profile and observations | from: S8
# 1. Profile
- **Identity:** Chinese Buddhist monk, **"prince of pilgrims"** (some notes: "**King of Travelers**")

- **Duration:** Varies in notes (**8 years** at court; **9 years** at Nalanda)

- **Praised:** **Harsha**, **Pulakesin II** (Chalukya), **Narasimhavarman** (Pallava)

## Universal angles | from: S10 | universal
- **Significance:**

  - Last great empire / unified north India of ancient times; court at **Kannauj** = intellectual centre
  - Ended with his death in **647 CE / 647 AD** (no successor)
- **AP angle:**
