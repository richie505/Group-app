=== ROW 78 | Eastern Chalukyas of Vengi - Kubja Vishnuvardhana, dynastic history, patronage of Telugu (Nannaya)

## Eastern Chalukyas — origin, period, identity and FIRSTs | from: S0, S1
# 1. Origin and founding
- **Branch:** Of the **Badami Chalukyas** (also Purva / Vengi Chalukyas)
- **Founder:** **Kubja Vishnuvardhana** - **Brother of Pulakesin II**
  - Pulakeshin II took Vengi (Krishna-Godavari) from the **Durjayas**; some notes say from the Vishnukundins, 616 CE
  - Made viceroy in **617 CE** → declared independence in **624 CE**
- **Rule:** **624-1075 CE**
# 2. FIRST, greatest and LAST
| **Angle** | **Fact** |
|---|---|
| Founder | **Kubja Vishnuvardhana** |
| Greatest ruler | **Gunaga Vijayaditya III** (APHQ 2011) |
| Last ruler | **Vijayaditya VII** (1070-1075); died in **1075** = **End of the line** |

# 3. Dynastic identity
- **Emblem:** **Boar (Varaha)**
- **Official language:** **Sanskrit** - about 100 donation inscriptions, mostly Sanskrit; Telugu rose later as literary language
- **Matronymic:** **"Hariti Putra"** in inscriptions (like Satavahanas, Ikshvakus, Kadambas) - read as a sign of southern origin

## Wars, defeats and marriage alliances | from: S1, S2
# 1. Rashtrakuta wars → ruler
| **Ruler** | **Rival / event** |
|---|---|
| **Vijayaditya I** | Conflict begins with **Dantidurga** (founder of Rashtrakutas, contemporary), who ended the Badami line (Vengi had helped Badami) |
| **Vishnuvardhana IV** | Backed Govinda III in the Rashtrakuta succession war; defeated by **Dhruva**, who married his daughter **Sheela Mahadevi** - marriage alliance saved his kingdom |
| **Vijayaditya II** | Fought the Rashtrakutas (Amoghavarsha); traditionally **108 battles in 12 years**; 108 temples commemorate his victories |
| **Gunaga Vijayaditya** | Defeated **Krishna II** + Kalinga king Devendravarma |
| **Chalukya Bhima I** | Repelled Krishna II's forces at **Niravadyapura and Peruvanguru** |

- Some notes merge Vijayaditya II's records with Gunaga Vijayaditya's under one heading
# 2. Later rivals - Telugu Chodas and Kalyani Chalukyas
- **Jatachoda Bhima** (Telugu Choda of Pedakallu): Killed **Danarnava**, occupied Vengi
  - Danarnava's sons **Saktivarma I** and **Vimaladitya** fled to **Rajaraja Chola**
  - **Interlude:** He held Vengi until Danarnava's sons (Saktivarman, Vimaladitya) recovered it with Chola help
- **After 973 CE:** Rival = **Kalyani Chalukyas** (Satyasraya 1006; Someshvara I)
- **1006 CE:** Satyasraya of Kalyani sent Bayyalanambi against Vengi → **Saktivarma I** beat him with Chola help
  - Saktivarma's coins found in **Arakan (Burma)**
# 3. Marriages and succession war
| **Marriage / war** | **Detail** |
|---|---|
| **Vimaladitya** (crowned May 1011) | Married **Kundavva** (Kundavai), daughter of **Rajaraja Chola I**, Chola princess and mother of Rajaraja Narendra; also married Melama, daughter of Jatachoda Bhima |
| Succession war | **Rajaraja Narendra** (son of Kundavva), backed by **Rajendra I**, fought **Vijayaditya VII** (son of Melama), backed by **Kalyani Jayasimha II** |
| Coronation | Rajaraja Narendra, **16 August 1022**; married **Ammanga Devi**, daughter of Rajendra Chola |
| Dharanikota, then **Koppam (1053)** | Rajadhiraja I won at Dharanikota and burnt Kollipaka, but was killed at Koppam by **Ahavamalla Someshwara**, so Rajaraja Narendra had to accept Kalyani suzerainty |

- ⚠ **Kundavva** (Chola) = mother of **Rajaraja Narendra**; **Melama** (Jatachoda's daughter) = mother of **Vijayaditya VII**

## Society and economy — agraharas, guilds and overseas trade | from: S1, S2
# 1. Society
- **Social order:** Varna-based; Brahmin privilege
- **Agraharas:** **Tax-free villages (agraharas)** given to Brahmins - performed Hiranyagarbha + Bahusuvarna rituals
- **Tribal assimilation:**
  - Under **Jayasimha II**: Koya Kottalu etc. drawn into the Brahmanical fold
  - Panduranga subdued the **Boyas**
# 2. Economy
- **Land grants:** Brahmadeya + agrahara grants came from the Badami model
- **Merchant guild HQ (medieval Andhra):** **Penugonda**
- **Overseas trade:**
  - Saktivarma's coins in **Arakan (Burma)**
  - Trade with the eastern islands under Rajaraja Narendra
  - Kulothunga I sent **72 merchants to China**

## Religious sects and centres → features | from: S3, S4
# 1. Vedic Hinduism and Shaivism
- **Vedic Hinduism:**
  - Royal prashastis boast of many Ashvamedhas
  - Titles: Parama Maheshwara, Parama Brahmanya, Parama Bhagavata
- **Shaivism (dominant):**
  - Sects: **Kapalika, Kalamukha and Pasupata**
  - Custom: a temple for each battle fought
# 2. Vaishnava shrines
- **Other shrines:** Andhra Mahavishnu (Srikakulam), Ahobilam, Bhavanarayana (Narsavaram)
# 3. Jainism and Buddhism
- **Potlacheruvu:** Famous / the leading Jain centre of the period (G2 2017 key)
- **Migration:** Brahmanical pressure → many Jains migrated to Karnataka, e.g. family of **Basaveshwara**
- **Buddhism:** Declining but surviving at Amaravati and Nagarjunakonda

## Temple arts — music, dance and sculpture | from: S5

- **Popular dance:** **Hallisaka (Kolatam)**
- **Instrument sculptures:** Veena, mridangam, cymbals at **Bhairavakona** (Nellore)

=== ROW 80 | Eastern Chalukya art and architecture - Biccavolu, Draksharama and Samalkot temples

# 1. Draksharama vs Samalkot
- **Common:** Both **two-storeyed** Bhimeswara shrines of **Chalukya Bhima I**, closely similar plan; both later renovated by the Reddis
- **Draksharama:** Massive lingam, huge Nandi mandapa, intricate carving; one of the Trilinga kshetras
- **Samalkot (Bheemarama/Kumararama):** Core sanctum + black-granite lingam = Chalukya Bhima; towering gopuram added later
