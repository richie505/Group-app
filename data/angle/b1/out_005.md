## Digambara vs Svetambara → leader, dress, beliefs; sub-sects | from: S2
- **Origin of split:** **12-year famine** under Chandragupta Maurya (Parishishtaparvan)
  - Formal split dated to around the **3rd century CE**; another view links it to the first council (c. 300 BCE)

| **Aspect** | **Digambara** | **Svetambara** |
|---|---|---|
| Leader | **Bhadrabahu**'s group that went south | **Sthulabhadra**'s group that stayed in Magadha |
| Dress | Sky-clad (nude monks) | White-clad (white robes) |
| Women | Deny salvation to women directly - must be reborn as men for moksha | Can attain liberation (jina-hood) |
| Mahavira | Never married | Married Yashoda |
| Monk's possessions | Broom and water pot only | 14 items incl. clothes |

| **Sub-sect** | **Leader / feature** |
|---|---|
| Yapaniya | Tried to reconcile (bridged) the two sects - active in Andhra, the **Yapanacharyas** |
| **Sthanakvasi** | Svetambara reform against idol worship - Lonka Shah [GK] |
| **Terapanthi** | Svetambara sub-sect founded by Acharya Bhikshu [GK], 18th c. |

- ⚠ **Bhadrabahu** → went south → **Digambara**; **Sthulabhadra** → stayed in Magadha → **Svetambara**
- ⚠ **Sthanakvasi** and **Terapanthi** = Svetambara offshoots; **Yapaniya** = bridge sect (Andhra)

## Jain canon — terms → meaning | from: S3
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Agamas / Siddhanta** | Sacred texts, in eastern Prakrit **Ardhamagadhi** |
| **14 Purvas** | Earliest texts, now mostly lost |
| **12 Angas** | Mahavira's Ganadharas compiled his teaching into these |
| **Svetambara canon** | **12 Angas, 12 Upangas, 10 Prakirnas, 6 Cheda Sutras, 4 Mula Sutras** + others |

- **Key Angas:** Acharanga, Bhagavati; early texts also **Sutrakritanga**, **Kalpasutra**
> Exam angle: odd-one-out - Jain texts (Acharanga, Bhagavati, Kalpasutra) vs Buddhist (Dipavamsa, Mahavamsa, Divyavadana, Milindapanho).

## Contributions of Jainism — language, literature, social base | from: S5
# 1. Language and literature
- **Language:** Early Jains **discarded Sanskrit**, preached in popular **Prakrit** - key to spread; canon in **Ardhamagadhi**
- **Apabhramsa:** **Earliest important works in Apabhramsa** + its first grammar
- **Later literature:** Rich Prakrit, Sanskrit and regional (Kannada, Tamil, Telugu) - grammar, poetry, mathematics
# 2. Social base
- **Base:** **traders and commercial communities** / **traders and merchants**; banking; urban growth
  - Agriculture avoided - harms life (strict ahimsa)
  - Jain merchant guilds moved on Satavahana trade routes
- **Social reform:** Challenged caste hierarchy; action over birth; promoted vegetarianism
- **Andhra:** Under Eastern Chalukyas, **Shudras** adopted Jainism for social advancement

## Universal angles | from: S9 | universal

## Core teachings — terms → meaning | from: S3
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Nirvana** | The **main goal** (APPSC GS 2012) - extinction of desire/craving; liberation from suffering + freedom from the cycle of rebirth |
| **Middle Path (Madhyama Marga)** | Rejects both **extreme asceticism** and **sensual indulgence** |

| **Anatta** | No permanent, unchanging self/soul (rejects atman) |

## Doctrines asked as lists — Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path, twelve nidanas | from: S4, S5
# 1. Four Noble Truths (Arya Satya)
| **Noble Truth** | **Content** |
|---|---|
| 1. **Dukkha** | Life involves suffering - life is suffering ("sabbam dukkham") |
| 2. **Samudaya** | Cause = craving (**tanha/trishna**), ignorance, attachment |
| 3. **Nirodha** | Cessation of suffering = **Nirvana** (suffering can end) |
| 4. **Magga** | Path to cessation = Noble Eightfold Path |

# 2. Eightfold Path (Ashtangika Marga)
- **Eight steps:** Right **View** (samma-ditthi), **Intention** (sankappa), **Speech** (vaca), **Action** (kammanta), **Livelihood** (ajiva), **Effort** (vayama), **Mindfulness** (sati), **Concentration** (samadhi)
- **Grouping:** In three groups

| **Group** | **Components** |
|---|---|
| Prajna (wisdom) | Right View, Right Intention |
| Sila (conduct / ethics) | Right Speech, Right Action, Right Livelihood |
| Samadhi (mental discipline) | Right Effort, Right Mindfulness, Right Concentration |

# 3. Pratityasamutpada
- **Doctrine of dependent origination:** Every phenomenon arises from a cause; chain of 12 nidanas (12-link causal chain)
  - Begins **avidya (ignorance) → samskara (formations) → vijnana (consciousness)** ... ends in old age and death

| **Order** | **Link** |
|---|---|
| 1-4 | Avidya (ignorance) → Samskara → Vijnana (consciousness) → Namarupa |
| 5-8 | Shadayatana (six senses) → Sparsha (contact) → Vedana (feeling) → Trishna (craving) |
| 9-12 | Upadana (clinging) → Bhava (becoming) → Jati (birth) → Jara-marana (old age-death) |

- ⚠ **Samudaya** = cause (craving); **Nirodha** = cessation; **Magga** = path

## Triratna and the Sangha — rules and monastic terms | from: S6
# 1. Triratna (three jewels / refuges)
- **Three pillars/refuges:**
  - **Buddha** = teacher
  - **Dhamma** = teaching
  - **Sangha** = order of monks and nuns, torch-bearers of Dhamma
# 2. The Sangha
- **Membership:** Open to all **above 15**, regardless of caste
  - Excluded: criminals, lepers, those with infectious diseases
- **Women:** Admitted by Buddha after requests of **Ananda** and **Mahapajapati Gotami** (Mahaprajapati Gautami = first nun)
- **Monastic life:**
  - Shaved heads, yellow/saffron robes, daily alms
  - **Vassa** = rainy-season retreat of 3 months
  - Democratic functioning with power to punish
- **Vinaya:** Monastic code
  - **Bhikkhuni Sangha** taken to Sri Lanka by **Sanghamitta**; thrived 1,000+ years until the Chola invasion of **1017 AD**
# 3. Ascetic and Sangha terms
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| Parivrajaka | Wandering ascetic [GK] |
| Shramana | Striver/ascetic outside the Vedic fold [GK] |
| Bhikkhu / Bhikkhuni | Monk / nun living on alms |
| Upasaka | Lay follower |
| **Pravrajya** | Going forth - leaving lay life (head-shaving, robes) |
| **Upasampada** | Full ordination |
| **Uposatha** | Fortnightly (full/new moon) confession and rule-recitation |
| **Parajika** | Grave offence - expulsion (expulsion offences) |

- ⚠ **Pravrajya** = going forth; **Upasampada** = full ordination; **Uposatha** = fortnightly confession

## Universal angles | from: S7 | universal
- **Current relevance:**
  - Buddha Purnima (Vesak): greetings by President and PM on **1 May 2026**; stressed India-Nepal Buddhist diplomacy

|---|---|---|---|

| **Mahayana** (after 4th council) | **Bodhisattva** ideal - postpones nirvana for all beings; Sunyata | **Idol worship** | Sanskrit - Prajnaparamita, Lotus Sutra; China, Japan, Korea, Tibet |
| **Vajrayana** (c. 8th c.) | Salvation through magical power (vajra); mantras, mudras, mandalas | Taras, wrathful deities | Tantras, mantras, mandalas; Bengal-Bihar, Tibet, Nepal, Bhutan; Vikramashila |

- **Vajrayana:**
  - Developed **c. 8th c. CE**
  - Founder/link: **Padmasambhava**; some notes credit **Siddha Nagarjuna** of Sriparvata
  - Centres: **Vikramashila**, later Nalanda
- **Bodhisattva's paramitas (perfections):** dana, sila, kshanti, virya, dhyana, prajna
- ⚠ **Arhat** = Hinayana; **Bodhisattva** = Mahayana; **vajra** = Vajrayana

## Philosophical schools → founder and doctrine | from: S4

|---|---|---|
