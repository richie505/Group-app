=== ROW 263 | Later reorganisation of states - 1960s to 2000; creation of Telangana 2014

## Articles 2, 3, 4 and 368 → provisions on new states | from: S0
# 1. Articles → scope

| **Article** | **Provision** |
|---|---|
| **Article 3** | Parliament may **by law** form a new state, increase/diminish area, alter boundaries or name of a state |
| **Article 2** | Admission of new states/territory from outside (e.g., **Sikkim**, **36th Amendment**) |
| **Art. 4** / **Article 4** | Such a law is not an amendment under **Article 368** |

# 2. Article 3 procedure - recommendation, reference, majority
- **Introduction:** only on the **President's recommendation** (**President's prior recommendation**)
- **Reference:** President first **refers it to the state legislature** (**affected state legislature**) for views within a set period
  - Time limit added by **5th Amendment 1955**
  - Views **not binding**; no fresh reference for each amendment to the bill; **no reference for a UT**
- **Majority:** **simple majority**
# 3. Basis of the AP Reorganisation Act 2014
- **Constitutional basis:** **Article 3** (Parliament's power to form a new State out of an existing one); with **Arts. 3 and 4** - **no Art. 368 amendment** needed
  - ⚠ **Trap:** some notes say **Article 2** - wrong; **Art. 2** = admitting new states/territory from outside

## Mulki rules — origin, definition and chronology 1888-2014 | from: S3, S22
# 1. Origin and FIRST
- **Origin:** **1888**, **Hyderabad State**
  - Local residents to get State posts; outsider appointed only with the **Prime Minister's (Diwan's)** permission
  - Came out of the 1880s **Mulki vs non-Mulki** rivalry in the Nizam's bureaucracy (**Salar Jung II** era)
- **1919 Nizam's Farman:** Made rules stricter - outsiders needed the **Nizam's personal permission**, to curb unemployment among locals
# 2. Term → meaning
- **Mulki:** Person with a fixed period of continuous residence in the State
  - Nizam's rules = **15 years**
  - **Gentlemen's Agreement (1956)** = domicile rules such as **12 years' residence** (some notes give 15 years for the Agreement)
  - ⚠ **15 years** = Nizam's rules; **12 years** = Gentlemen's Agreement (some notes give 15)
# 3. Chronology → event → stance

| **Year** | **Event** | **Region / stance** |
|---|---|---|
| 1919 | Nizam's **Mulki rules** - jobs reserved for locals | Hyderabad State |
| 1956 (20 Feb) | Gentlemen's Agreement: domicile safeguards for Telangana | Both regions |
| 1969 | **Jai Telangana** agitation (Telangana Praja Samithi, Marri Chenna Reddy): implement Mulki safeguards / separate State | Telangana, **for** Mulki rules |
| 1972 | **Jai Andhra** agitation after the SC upheld the Mulki Rules | Coastal Andhra and Rayalaseema, **against** Mulki rules |
| 1973 | Six-Point Formula; **32nd Amendment** - **Art. 371D** (local cadres, zones, Administrative Tribunal), 371E (central university) | Compromise |
| 1975 | Presidential Order on local cadres | Implementation |
| **1985** | **GO 610** to correct violations of the Presidential Order [GK] | Telangana grievance |
| **2001-04** | **Girglani Commission** on violations of GO 610 and the Presidential Order [GK] | Telangana grievance |
| 2010 | **Srikrishna Committee** (Justice B.N. Srikrishna) report, **30 Dec 2010** - six options | - |
| 2014 | Telangana formed, 2 June | - |

- **Chronological order (PYQ):** Jai Telangana → Jai Andhra → 32nd Amendment → GO 610 → Girglani Report
- ⚠ **1969** = Telangana **for** Mulki rules; **1972** = Andhra **against** Mulki rules

## Court verdicts on Mulki rules → case, year, effect | from: S7
# 1. 1969 - A.V.S. Narasimha Rao v. State of AP
- **Ruling:** Supreme Court struck down the **Telangana residence requirement** in the Public Employment (Requirement as to Residence) Act, **1957**
- **Reason:** Under **Art. 16(3)**, Parliament cannot prescribe a residence condition for only part of a State
# 2. October 1972 - Director of Industries v. V. Venkata Reddy
- **Case:** **Director of Industries v. V. Venkata Reddy**, decided **October 1972**
- **Outcome:** Supreme Court **upheld the Mulki Rules** - held **valid and still in force**
  - Non-locals could be legally barred from public posts in **Hyderabad/Telangana**
- **Impact:** Immediate cause / trigger of **Jai Andhra**
- ⚠ **1969 vs 1972:** 1969 ruling struck down the **1957** residence law; 1972 ruling concerned the older **Mulki Rules**
# 3. Law / verdict → year → effect

| **Year** | **Law / verdict** | **Effect** |
|---|---|---|
| **1957** | Public Employment (Requirement as to Residence) Act, 1957 (Parliament) [GK] | Residence condition for posts in Telangana |
| **1969** | SC, **A.V.S. Narasimha Rao v. State of AP**[GK] | Struck down the Telangana residence clause of the 1957 Act (Art. 16(3)) |
| **Oct 1972** | SC upheld the **Mulki Rules** (CDI) | Triggered Jai Andhra |
| **1973** | Mulki Rules repealed under the Six-Point Formula; TRC dissolved 1 Jan 1974 | Replaced by the zonal system |

## Jai Andhra 1972-73 → cause, escalation, President's Rule, end | from: S9, S10
# 1. Cause and demand
- **Jai Andhra (1972)**: **Year:** **1972** (ran into **1973**); counter-agitation in **Coastal Andhra and Rayalaseema** - the movement **against the Mulki Rules**
- **Immediate cause:** **Supreme Court judgment of October 1972 upholding the Mulki Rules**; Coastal and Rayalaseema people protested
- **Main issue:** Andhra-region people treated as "outsiders" in Hyderabad, a capital built with revenue of whole State
  - **Demand:** **repeal of the Mulki Rules or a separate Andhra State**
# 2. Escalation and President's Rule (FIRST)
- **Flag:** protesters hoisted a **separate Andhra State flag on 10 Dec 1972**
- **CM:** **P.V. Narasimha Rao** (from **30 Sep 1971**); resigned **18 Jan 1973**
- **Cause of President's Rule:** Breakdown of law-and-order machinery
- **President's Rule:** **18 Jan 1973 to 10 Dec 1973** (about **11 months**)
  - **Significance:** **First President's Rule** in AP since the State was formed
# 3. End
- **Ended by:** **Six-Point Formula** proposed by the Central Government
  - ⚠ **Trap:** Neither repression nor abolition of the Mulki Rules ended it; PYQ answer = **Six-Point Formula**
- **Agitation called off:** **Jai Andhra 1972-73** called off on **1 Oct 1973**
- ⚠ **10 Dec** twice: Andhra flag hoisted **10 Dec 1972**; President's Rule ended **10 Dec 1973**

=== ROW 264 | Naxalite movement in Andhra Pradesh - Srikakulam origins, course and impact

## Uprisings → place, period, leaders, causes (Naxalbari 1967, Srikakulam 1968-70) | from: S0, S1
# 1. Uprising → place → period

| **Uprising** | **Place** | **Period** | **Leaders** |
|---|---|---|---|

# 2. Naxalbari - slogans and causes
- **Slogan:** "**Land to the tiller**"
- "**Ballot to Bullet**" line rejected the CPI(M)'s parliamentary path
- **Causes:**
  - Land concentration
  - Failed land reforms (zamindari abolition, ceilings, tenancy)
  - Exploitation of tribals
# 3. Srikakulam - nature, tribes, party, outcome
- **Nature:** Tribal-peasant uprising in the **Srikakulam Agency** vs landlords, moneylenders and forest officials
- **Tribes:** **Savaras (Soras) and Jatapus**
- **Party:**
  - Started under the **CPI(M)** (PYQ key for the "**1967** Srikakulam uprising")
  - Shifted to the **CPI(ML)** in **1969**
- **Outcome:** Suppressed by the State, but seeded the **Naxalite movement** and revolutionary literature in Andhra
- ⚠ **Srikakulam** began under the **CPI(M)**, shifted to the **CPI(ML)** in **1969**
