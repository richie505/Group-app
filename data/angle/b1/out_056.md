## Visalandhra — meaning, roots and FIRSTs | from: S1, S3
# 1. Meaning
- **Visalandhra ("Greater Andhra"):** one state for all Telugu people split between **Madras** and **Nizam's Hyderabad**
  - APPSC-GS 2024: united-Telugu-state movement = **Visalandhra Movement**

| **FIRST** | **FACT** |
|---|---|
| First to use the word | **Puchalapalli Sundarayya** |
| First demand by a party | **Communist Party** first demanded it in **1946** |

# 2. Roots — year → event
| **Year** | **Event** |
|---|---|
| **1912** | **Unnava Lakshminarayana** + **Jonnavittula Gurunatham** sketched united Andhra (Telangana + Circars + Ceded districts) |
| **1914** | Second Andhra Mahasabha - **Nyapati Subbarao** proposed one rule for all Telugu areas |
| **1936** | **Andhra Congress Swaraj Party** manifesto |
| **1937** | Some notes date the concept in the Circar districts to this year |
| **1954** | **Madapati Hanumantha Rao** (**1954** Mahasabha): idea **50 years** old, as old as the Andhra Movement |

# 3. Order of events
- **APHQ 2025 order:** **Dhar Commission (1948) → Visalandhra Mahasabha (1949) → Swami Sitaram's fast (1951) → SRC (1953)**
- ⚠ **1912** = Unnava + Jonnavittula sketch; **1914** = Nyapati Subbarao; **1946** = Communist demand; **1949** = Mahasabha founded

## Telangana background — Mahasabha, Hyderabad State and Mulki rules | from: S0
# 1. Nizam Andhra Mahasabha
- **Origin:** grew out of the **Andhra Jana Sangham (1921)**
- **First Andhra Mahasabha:** **Jogipet (1930)**, presided by **Suravaram Pratapa Reddy**
- **Role:** vehicle for Telugu cultural and political awakening in the Nizam's state
- **Later:** split along moderate and **Communist** lines
# 2. Hyderabad State — data
- **Status:** **Part B** state from **1950 to 31 Oct 1956**
- **Population:** **18.7 million**

| **Language** | **Share** |
|---|---|
| Telugu | **47.8%** |
| Marathi | **24.3%** |
| Urdu | **11.6%** |
| Kannada | **10.5%** |

# 3. Mulki rules and the 1952 agitation
- **Origin:** from **1888**; tightened by Nizam's farman of **1919** (**Nizam's era (1919)**)
- **Rule:** only residents of about **15 years** could get local jobs
- **Mulki agitation (1952):** under **Burgula** ministry
  - Police firing near **City College, Hyderabad** (**September 1952**) killed students
- **Outcome:** domicile protection → central Telangana demand → Mulki/domicile clause of **Gentlemen's Agreement**

## Arguments for and against Visalandhra | from: S5, S7
# 1. Arguments for
| **Argument** | **Content** |
|---|---|
| Linguistic-cultural | One language, one culture; Satavahana-Kakatiya legacy (Andhra Mahasabha literature) |
| River waters | Integrated planning of the **Krishna and Godavari** basins, upper Telangana to coastal deltas |
| Economic | Andhra's agriculture and coastline + Telangana's minerals and industry = balanced growth |
| Capital | **Hyderabad** as capital (APCC 1950, 1953; Mahasabha 1950) |
| Telangana's development | Telangana backward under the Nizam, Telugu undeveloped there; Ramananda Tirtha: Telangana would **develop** in Visalandhra, and a separate Telangana would be weak |
| Agrarian (Communists) | Unification to push land reform and weaken feudal structures |

# 2. Arguments against
- **Nehru:** called it **"Andhra imperialism"** (**Oct 1953**) → strengthened (boosted) separate-Telangana camp
- **Rajagopalachari (1949):** preferred keeping Hyderabad a **multilingual state**
- **Argument used for separate Telangana:** Telangana had higher **per capita income** and **revenue** than Andhra State → viable on its own
- **Telangana fears:** loss of jobs + seats in education to better-educated Andhras; earlier protected by **Mulki (domicile) rules**

| **Area** | **Apprehension of Telangana leaders** |
|---|---|
| Employment | **Mulki (domicile) Rules** protecting local jobs would be weakened |
| Education and administration | Coastal Andhra's more advanced educational and administrative position would dominate public services |
| Revenue | Telangana's revenue surplus would not be spent on Telangana |
| Development | Regional economic disparities would widen without institutional safeguards |

- ⚠ "**Andhra imperialism**" = **Nehru** (**Oct 1953**); "**multilingual state**" = **Rajagopalachari** (**1949**)

## Gentlemen's Agreement → safeguards for Telangana | from: S10
| **Subject** | **Safeguard / clause (as finally agreed, Feb 1956)** |
|---|---|
| Finance / revenue | Administrative expenditure (common costs) borne proportionately; Telangana's **surplus spent only in Telangana**; reviewed after **5 years** |
| Education | Existing facilities protected; Telangana students to get technical-institution admission priority or at least **1/3** (**one-third of seats**), whichever more favourable |
| Services / retrenchment | Recruitment by population ratio; proportionate retrenchment from both regions |
| Domicile (Mulki) | Mulki/domicile rule for recruitment in Telangana - recruitment reserved by residence of **12 years** (some notes say 15 years) |
| Land | Sale of agricultural land in Telangana controlled by the Regional Council |
| Urdu | Kept in Telangana administration and courts for **5 years**, then reviewed by the Regional Council |
| Prohibition | Left to Telangana legislators |
| Cabinet / political posts | **60:40** (Andhra:Telangana); at least **one Muslim** minister from Telangana |
| CM / Deputy CM | From different regions: if CM from Andhra, Deputy CM from Telangana, and vice versa |
| Portfolios | Key portfolios to Telangana: at least **two** of Home, Finance, Revenue, Planning & Development, Commerce & Industry |
| Regional body | Statutory **Regional Council/Committee** for Telangana |
| Party | Separate Telangana PCC till **1962** (Andhra PCC did not object) |

- **Cabinet share:** **60:40 (Andhra:Telangana)** also put as "Telangana at least **40%**"
  - ⚠ **Trap:** Older notes print **3:1**; keyed answer = **60:40** (APPSC G2 2025)
- **Provision count:** Ten provisions vs fourteen points in different notes — same content, different grouping
- **Earlier Andhra Assembly resolution:** **CM Bezawada Gopala Reddy** - unanimous resolution: Telangana priority + population-based allocations in jobs, education, irrigation, development
  - Some notes date it **25 Nov 1953**; it belongs to **1955**, after the SRC report
  - !! Not in your sources: year of the Andhra Legislative Assembly resolution.
- ⚠ Domicile: Mulki rules = about **15 years**; Agreement = **12 years** (some notes say 15)

## Regional Committee, Deputy CM post and violations | from: S11
# 1. Regional Council/Committee
- **Basis:** Provided by the **Gentlemen's Agreement** (APPSC GS 2019)
- **Statutory:** appointed by the President under **Article 371(1)** (**Art. 371**); constituted in **1958**
- **Composition:**
  - MLAs/MPs from Telangana elected by Telangana legislators; all Telangana ministers (except CM) **ex officio**
  - Some notes: all elected Telangana MLAs
- **Powers:** **development planning, irrigation, agriculture, industry, education, public health, recruitment, prohibition**
  - Disputes with state government → **Centre** for final decision
  - Review after **10 years**
- **Fate:**
  - **1968:** extended for five years
  - Wound up after **32nd Amendment (1973)** removed AP from **Art. 371(1)**
- !! Not in your sources: the name of its first chairman.
# 2. Deputy Chief Minister post
- **What happened:** **Sanjiva Reddy did not appoint a Deputy CM**; dismissed the post as a "**sixth finger**"
# 3. Violations alleged later
| **Clause** | **Alleged violation** |
|---|---|
| Domicile / employment | Large-scale recruitment of non-locals into Telangana posts in the late 1950s-60s |
| Mulki rules | Bypassed by reclassifying local posts as "state-cadre" or "unreserved" |
| Revenue surplus | Promise to spend Telangana surplus only on Telangana ignored |
| Deputy CM | Post not filled |

- **Root flaw:** political assurances, not constitutional mandates
- **Result:** grievance (including the Deputy CM breach) produced the **1969 Jai Telangana** agitation

## Formation of Andhra Pradesh, 1 November 1956 — basis, name, capital | from: S13
# 1. Formation
- **Date & law:** **1 November 1956** under the **States Reorganisation Act, 1956**
- **Basis:** **Linguistic reorganisation** (APPSC GS 2023)
- **Merged:**
  - **Andhra State** (Coastal Andhra + Rayalaseema) = **11 districts of Andhra State**
  - **Telangana (Telugu) districts of Hyderabad State** = **9 Telugu-speaking Telangana districts**
  - Kannada areas of Hyderabad → Mysore; Marathi areas → Bombay
- **Preceded by:** **Gentlemen's Agreement** (**20 Feb 1956**)
- **Andhra Legislature (Kurnool):** Passed the merger unanimously; both ministries resigned in 1956 for the unified state
# 2. Name — proposal → source
| **Name** | **Proposed by** |
|---|---|
| "Andhra Telangana" | Draft bill |
| "Visalandhra" | Communists |
| **"Andhra Pradesh"** | Final: 7th Amendment Bill (Lok Sabha, **10 Aug 1956**) |

# 3. Capital
- **Capital:** **Hyderabad**, replacing **Kurnool** (Andhra State capital **1953-56**, chosen in spirit of **Sri Bagh Pact**)
- **Reasons:**
  - SRC: Hyderabad would solve Andhra's permanent-capital problem
  - Largest city with ready administrative infrastructure
  - Visalandhra Mahasabhas had demanded Hyderabad as capital
# 4. Inauguration
- **Inauguration:** **Jawaharlal Nehru**, **1 Nov 1956**, **Hyderabad** as capital
- !! Not in your sources: the text of Nehru's inaugural speech.
- ⚠ **11** Andhra districts + **9** Telangana districts; **Kurnool** (1953-56) → **Hyderabad** (1956)

## Universal angles | from: S20 | universal
# 1. Visalandhra Mahasabha and merger
- **Significance:**
  - Mahasabha turned a Communist slogan into an all-party demand that shaped the **1956** merger
  - Produced **Andhra Pradesh on 1 November 1956** - first major linguistic state, merging **Andhra State (1953)** with **Telangana**
  - First state formed under **SR Act 1956**; united Telugu-speaking areas for the first time
- **FIRST/LAST:** first Mahasabha session - **Warangal, 1950**; second - **Hyderabad, 1954**
- **Criticism & debates:** "**Andhra imperialism**" fear + SRC's wait-till-**1961** formula foreshadowed later separate-Telangana demands
# 2. Telangana apprehensions and Gentlemen's Agreement
- **Significance:**
  - Apprehensions of **1953-56** → merger came with written safeguards, not unconditional union
  - Gentlemen's Agreement repeated **Sribagh Pact** model - settling regional fears by a moral pact
  - Written basis of the 1956 merger; later settlements (**1969 Eight-Point**, **1973 Six-Point**, **371D**) build on its safeguards
  - Violations of safeguards → **1969 Jai Telangana** agitation
- **Criticism & debates:**
  - Safeguards (**Deputy CM post**, **surplus funds**, **Mulki rules**) weakly enforced; **Telangana was carved out in 2014**
  - Political assurances without constitutional teeth; diluted in the **1950s-60s** → **1969** agitation; seen in Telangana as systematically diluted
- **AP angle:**
  - Hyderabad capital from **1956** until **2014** bifurcation
  - AP's local-cadre + zonal recruitment under **Art. 371D** descends from the Agreement's domicile clause
- **Current relevance:**
  - **BRS** leader **K.T. Rama Rao** (**Jan 2026**) blamed Congress for merging Telangana with AP despite **Fazal Ali Commission**'s recommendation
  - **BJP** MP **Tejasvi Surya**'s **April 2026** remark in Parliament comparing **2014** division with Partition → protests in Telangana (**5 Jan** and **17 Apr 2026**)
  - AP Public Employment Order **zones and multi-zones** being re-mapped (**May 2026**)
# 3. Capital and first government
- **Current relevance:**
  - AP Assembly resolved **28 Mar 2026** asking Centre to name **Amaravati** in **Section 5(2)** of **APRA 2014** (**29 Mar 2026**)
  - **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Bill, 2026** (Amaravati sole capital) passed by **Lok Sabha** (**Jun 2026**)
- **AP angle (2026):** capital **Amaravati**, Governor **S. Abdul Nazeer**, CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu**
- **FIRST/LAST:**
  - Last CM of Andhra State - **Bezawada Gopala Reddy**
  - First CM of AP - **Neelam Sanjiva Reddy**
  - First Governor of both - **C.M. Trivedi**
# 4. 1983 and 1984 crises
- **Significance:** **1983** ended over **26 years** of Congress rule → regional-party politics in AP
- **Criticism & debates:** **1984** dismissal = textbook case of Governor's discretion misused; shaped later **Sarkaria Commission** debates
- **Current relevance:**
  - **Nadendla Bhaskara Rao** died in Hyderabad, **April 2026**, aged **90** (**23 Apr 2026**)
  - **N. Chandrababu Naidu** (TDP) again AP CM, heading **NDA** coalition; **K. Pawan Kalyan** Deputy CM (**Jun 2026**)
