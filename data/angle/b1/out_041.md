## Jyotiba Phule and the Satyashodhak Samaj → life, aims, symbol | from: S0
# 1. Jyotiba Phule
- **Founder:** **Jyotirao (Jyotiba) Phule** (**1827-1890**), Mali community, Maharashtra
- **1848:** Girls' school in **Pune**
- **1854:** Home for upper-caste widows
- !! Not in your sources: Phule's own quoted sayings on caste (add from a Phule reader).
# 2. Satyashodhak Samaj
- **Founded:** At **Pune in 1873**
- **Satyashodhak objectives:**
  - End caste and untouchability; break **Brahmanical** dominance and priestly monopoly over ritual
  - Free lower castes from Brahmin priests and religious exploitation
  - Education for women, Shudras, Ati-Shudras; social service
  - Satyashodhak marriages without priests
# 3. Symbol
- **Symbol:** **King Bali** against the **Brahmanical** symbol of Rama (UPSC)
- **Gulamgiri** (Slavery, **1873**): compared lower castes' condition to **slavery**
- ⚠ **Phule symbol** = **King Bali**; **Justice Party symbol** = **Scales**

## Justice Party → background, aim and social base | from: S1
- **Background:**
  - Brahmins = only about **3%** of Madras Presidency population but held **over 90%** (90%+) of government jobs
  - Non-Brahmins met at Madras in **1916** to organise
- **Aim:** Protect interests of **non-Brahmins** (PYQ) (APPSC 2008)
  - End Brahmin dominance in jobs, education, politics and religion
  - Reservation and representation in jobs, education and legislatures
- **Social base:** Non-Brahmin landed gentry, the intermediate landed castes (Andhra: **Reddys, Kammas, Kapus**) → secured the **Communal G.O. (1921)**
- **Dalits:** Built a parallel "Adi" movement
- **Andhra Mahasabha (Vijayawada, 1917):** Sought reserved seats for non-Brahmins
  - Echoed by **Ponaka Pattabhirama Reddy** + **Tripuraneni Ramaswamy Chowdary**
- **British support:** Backed it partly to weaken Home Rule; party used against **Home Rule** and Gandhi's movements

## Justice Party decline → causes and effects | from: S5
- **Causes:**

## Periyar → Self-Respect Movement, journals, DK and DMK | from: S6
# 1. Periyar's Congress years
- **Posts:** President and secretary of the provincial Congress; even then supported the **Hindu Religious Endowments Act**
- **Broke with the Congress in 1925:**
  - Over non-Brahmin communal representation
  - Caste segregation at the **Cheranmadevi Gurukulam**
# 2. Self-Respect Movement
- **Self-Respect Movement (1925)**
- **Founder:** **E.V. Ramasamy Naicker (Periyar)**
- **Launched:** **1925** (mid-**1920s**)
- **Aims:**
  - Attack caste hierarchy, Brahmin priests and religion; reject Brahmanical religion and caste
  - Self-respect marriages without priests
  - Women's equality and property rights
- **Journals:** **Kudi Arasu** + **Viduthalai**; Revolt (English)
- **In Andhra:**
  - Inspired **Bhagya Reddy Varma** and **Jala Rangaswamy** to adopt the "Adi" identity and demand temple entry
  - Fed the Justice Party's non-Brahmin politics
# 3. Later phase - DK and DMK
| **Event** | **Year** |
|---|---|
| **Anti-Hindi agitation** | **1937** |
| **Dravidistan demand** | Erode **1940** |
| **Dravidar Kazhagam (DK)** formed from the Justice Party | **1944** |
| DK split → **C.N. Annadurai** formed the **DMK** | **1949** (**17 Sept 1949**); in power from **1967** |

- **When/where:** **1924-25**, Vaikom (Travancore, Kerala)

- **Outcome:** Led on to **Travancore Temple Entry Proclamation (1936)**

## Universal angles | from: S17 | universal
- **Current relevance:**
  - Phule: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment began a two-year nationwide celebration of Phule's **200th birth anniversary**, marked on **11 April 2026**
  - Sree Narayana Guru's **172nd birth anniversary** (**28 Aug 2026**) - "One Caste, One Religion, One God for Man"; **Aruvippuram** Shiva consecration (**1888**)
  - The Hindu (**16 Jun 2026**): Justice Party formed government **four times** from **1920**; declined by **1937** as Congress rose
  - Non-Brahmin reservations began in **1920** → shaped Tamil politics; Self-Respect/DK stream separated religion and caste from politics
- **Significance:**
  - Communal G.O. **1921** = root/forerunner of South Indian reservation policy
  - "Adi Andhra" name (**1917**) = first Dalit identity assertion in Telugu country
- **Criticism & debates:** Pro-British, landlord-led, weak among Andhra masses
- **AP angle:**
  - Periyar's movement inspired the **Adi-Andhra** leaders
  - Non-Brahmin politics helped Reddys, Kammas and Kapus rise
  - Justice Party gave Andhra its university → Andhra University **1926** = its lasting Andhra legacy
  - Vijayawada (**1917**) and Machilipatnam (**1919**) sessions → Krishna district was the hub
- **Comparative note:**
  - Gandhi: varna = division of labour; untouchability = corruption; used "Harijan"
  - vs Ambedkar: caste's **annihilation**, legal rights, separate electorates
  - Periyar's movement anti-religious vs Andhra's mostly reformist and education-led
