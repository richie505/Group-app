=== ROW 234 | Khilafat and Non-Cooperation Movement - programme, spread, Chauri Chaura and withdrawal
## Khilafat and NCM — aims, background, launch and FIRSTs | from: S0, S1, S9
# 1. Khilafat — aim and trigger
- **Objective:** Protect/restore the **Ottoman Sultan/Khalifa** (Caliph, Sultan of Turkey) and the territorial integrity of the **Ottoman Empire** after its defeat in WWI
- **Trigger:** Treaty of **Sevres, May 1920**; British PM had promised Turkey would not be humiliated
- **Organisation:** **Khilafat Committee (1919)**
- **End:** Lost relevance when Turkey abolished the Caliphate (**1924**)
# 2. NCM — background
- **Rowlatt Act** + **Jallianwala Bagh (1919)**
- **Khilafat** wrong
- "Inadequate" **Montagu-Chelmsford reforms** (Amritsar session **1919**, president **Motilal Nehru**, called them inadequate and disappointing)
- **Viceroys:** Chelmsford, then **Lord Reading**
# 3. Launch
- **Launch:** **1 August 1920** (the day **Tilak died**); Gandhi returned his **Kaiser-i-Hind** medal
  - Khilafat Committee started non-cooperation on **31 August 1920**
- **Year of launch = 1920:**
  - Approved at the **Calcutta special session (Sept 1920)**
  - Formally adopted at **Nagpur (Dec 1920)**
# 4. FIRSTs
| **FIRST** | **FACT** |
|---|---|
| First proposal of non-violent non-cooperation | Gandhi at the **All-India Khilafat Conference (Delhi, Nov 1919)** |
| **First nationwide mass movement** | NCM - Swaraj in one year not achieved, but made Congress a mass force |
- ⚠ Launched **1 Aug 1920** (Gandhi) vs **31 Aug 1920** (Khilafat Committee); approved **Calcutta (Sept)**, adopted **Nagpur (Dec)**

## Chauri Chaura and the withdrawal — date, place, reactions | from: S9
# 1. Chauri Chaura
- **Date:** **4 February 1922** (many notes say 5 Feb 1922; a "2022" print is a misprint for 1922)
- **Place:** **Gorakhpur district, UP**
- **Events:** Police fired on/lathi-charged a procession (**Bhagwan Ahir** among those beaten) → crowd burnt the police station
  - Killed **22 policemen** (some notes say 21)
- **Defence:** **Madan Mohan Malaviya** defended the accused; of **172** sentenced to death, **19** finally hanged
# 2. Withdrawal
- **Withdrawn Feb 1922:** After Chauri Chaura; Gandhi fasted **3 days** and called off the movement
  - **Bardoli resolution (11-12 Feb 1922):** Congress turned to constructive work
- **Reactions:** **Subhas Bose** - "sprinkling cold water", "national calamity"; **M.N. Roy** - weak leadership
- **Result:** Khilafat issue died; Hindu-Muslim unity broke; communal riots **1921-27**
> Exam angle: statement pairs - NCM made nationalism a mass movement (true); withdrawn after Jallianwala (false - after Chauri Chaura).

## Gandhi's arrest and the Great Trial — date, charge, judge | from: S10
# 1. Arrest and Great Trial (1922)
- **Arrest:** **10 March 1922** at **Sabarmati, Ahmedabad**
- **Charge:** **Sedition (Sec. 124A IPC)** for three **Young India** articles (incl. "Tampering with Loyalty", "Shaking the Manes")
- **"Great Trial":** At **Ahmedabad**, **18 March 1922**, before Judge **C.N. Broomfield**
  - Gandhi pleaded guilty, asked for the highest penalty
  - Said "affection cannot be manufactured or regulated by law"
- **Sentence:** **6 years**; released **1924** on health grounds
- **Gandhi's papers:** **Young India**, Navajivan, Indian Opinion, Harijan
# 2. First arrest and sedition trial
- **April 1919:** Stopped and detained while going to Punjab (at **Palwal**) → sparked riots

## Palnad forest (Pullari) satyagraha, 1921–22 — place, grievance, action | from: S2
# 1. Place and year
- **Place & year:** **Palnadu, Guntur district**; **1921-22** (APPSC 2012 key)
  - The **first forest satyagraha** in Andhra (APPSC 2023 key)
  - Some notes date the Pullari rebellion from **Sept 1921**
# 2. Grievance
- **Forest Act (1878)** reserved forests
- Heavy **Pullari (grazing) tax** = **12 annas per cow, Rs 1.50 per buffalo, goats banned**
- Bribe-taking forest staff
- Failed **1921 rains** → fodder shortage
# 3. Action
- Refusal of Pullari
- **Social boycott** of officials at **Macherla** (even the Collector and SP)
- **300 cattle** driven into the forest untaxed → impounded at **Minchalapadu**
- **Kanneganti Hanumanthu:** Shot dead in police firing during the cattle-rescue at Minchalapadu, **26 Feb 1922** (a "2022" print is a misprint)
# 4. Repression
- **28 men and 8 women** arrested
- Normalcy by **March 1922**
- ⚠ **Macherla** = social boycott; **Minchalapadu** = cattle impounded, Hanumanthu shot

## Dandi March, 1930 — demands, date, route, marchers, remarks | from: S1
# 1. Prelude
- **Ultimatum:** Gandhi's **11 demands / 11-point ultimatum** to Viceroy **Irwin** (letter of **30 Jan 1930**; sent/ignored **2 March 1930**)
  - Abolition of salt tax was central; Irwin refused
- **Why salt:** Universal necessity of rich and poor; salt tax/monopoly = most visible face of colonial exploitation/taxation
# 2. The march
- **Start:** **12 March 1930** from **Sabarmati Ashram** (Ahmedabad) with **78** followers
  - APPSC 2025: start date = **12 March**
- **Route:** About **240 miles (385 km)** through Gujarat to **Dandi** (Navsari coast)
- **Salt law broken:** **6 April 1930**
- **Only Telugu marcher:** **Yerneni (Erneni) Subrahmanyam** of Krishna district; later founded an ashram at **Komaravolu**
- **Successors after Gandhi's arrest:** **Abbas Tyabji** first, **Sarojini Naidu** second
# 3. Persons → remarks
| **Person** | **Statement** |
