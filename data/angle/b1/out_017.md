=== ROW 89 | Chola art - Brihadeshwara temple, Dravida style elements, Chola bronzes (Nataraja), Tamil literature

## Dravida style — temple terms, Nagara vs Dravida vs Vesara and the Chola shift | from: S0
# 1. Chola shift to structural temples
- **Shift:** Rock-cut caves → massive **free-standing structural temples**
  - "The Pallavas laid the foundation, the Cholas built the cathedral"
- **Zenith of Dravida style:** Massive vimanas, Nandi mandapas, enclosure walls
- **Legacy:** Chola-style building continued under **Kalyani Chalukyas** and **Hoysalas**
# 2. Temple terms → meaning
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Garbhagriha** | Womb-house housing the main icon |
| **Vimana** | Multi-storied **pyramidal tower over the garbhagriha** - dominant in Chola temples |
| **Shikhara** | Topmost, **dome-like** crowning part of the vimana (monolithic at Thanjavur) |
| **Antarala** | Vestibule between the garbhagriha and the mandapa |
| **Mandapa** | **Pillared hall**/portico in front of the sanctum for gatherings, rituals and dance |
| **Jagati** | Raised platform |
| **Vahana + dhvaja** pillar | Placed axially before the sanctum |
| **Gopuram** | Mighty, elaborate **gateway tower** at the entrance of South Indian temples (BPSC 2017 key) |
| Nandi mandapa, **prakara** | Pavilion for Nandi; **enclosure walls** |
# 3. Temple styles - Nagara, Dravida, Vesara
| **Style** | **Tower** | **Other features** | **Region** | **Examples** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nagara** (north) | curvilinear **shikhara** over the garbhagriha | built on a stone platform; usually no elaborate boundary walls or gateways | North India (from 5th c.) | Kandariya Mahadeva, Lakshmana (Khajuraho), Kedarnath |
| **Dravida** (south) | **Vimana** - pyramidal, multi-storeyed | Prakara walls with **gopurams** | South India | Kailasanatha (Kanchipuram), Brihadeeswara |
| **Vesara** | stepped shikhara | hybrid of Nagara and Dravida | Deccan: Badami & Kalyani Chalukyas, Rashtrakutas, Hoysalas | - |

- **Vesara** (also "Chalukyan" / "Karnata Dravida"): **Dravida vimana tiers** + **Nagara** curvilinear profile
  - Pioneered by Badami Chalukyas
- **Teli temple, Gwalior:** Pinnacle in Dravida style (UPPSC 2016 key)
  - Bhitargaon, Kandariya Mahadeo, Osian = Nagara
- **Brihadeeswara:** Classic Dravida temple
  - Some notes call it a Nagara-Dravida fusion → follow "Dravida" for exams
- ⚠ **Vimana** = Dravida tower; **shikhara** = Nagara tower (in Dravida, only the crowning part of the vimana)

## Chola temples → builder, site, features, chronology and UNESCO | from: S1
| **Order** | **Temple** | **Builder** | **Site** | **Key features** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| - | **Koranganatha** | Early Chola | Srinivasanallur | Early Chola structural temple |
| 1 | **Brihadeeswara / Rajarajeshwara, Thanjavur** ("**Dakshina Meru**") | **Rajaraja I**, completed **1010 CE** | Thanjavur | Vimana **216 ft**; **monolithic capstone** (about **80 tonnes**) on a granite vimana; frescoes; huge Nandi (great Nandi **added later by the Nayakas**) |
| 2 | **Gangaikondacholapuram** | **Rajendra I** - marks the **Ganga expedition** | Gangaikondacholapuram (his new capital) | Vimana **180 ft**, **concave curvilinear** profile ("feminine" grace vs Thanjavur's "masculine" bulk); bronze well, giant Nandi |
| - | Airavatesvara | **Rajaraja II** | Darasuram | Miniature masterpiece; stone chariot drawn by horses; **"Musical Steps"** |
| 4 | Kampaharesvara | **Kulottunga III** | Tribhuvanam | Late (later) Chola |

- **Builders (APPSC-GS 2018 key):** Chidambaram, Thanjavur and Gangaikondacholapuram = **Chola rulers** / **Cholas**
- **Thillai Nataraja temple, Chidambaram:** Shrine of **Shiva as Nataraja**, enlarged by Chola kings
  - **Chit Sabha** (Hall of Consciousness) with **108 classical dance poses** carved
- **Chronology (UPPSC 2018):** **Seven Pagodas → Shore Temple (both Pallava, Mahabalipuram) → Brihadeeswara → Gangaikondacholapuram**
  - Seven Pagodas = "Sapta pagoda" = **Pallava** Mahabalipuram rathas
- **UNESCO "Great Living Chola Temples"** - three components:
  - **Brihadeeswara (Thanjavur)**
  - **Gangaikondacholapuram**
  - **Airavatesvara (Darasuram)**
  - **Listing:** Thanjavur listed **1987** → extended to the other two in **2004**
  - ⚠ **Trap:** Chidambaram is **not** in the group
- ⚠ **216 ft – 180 ft** = **Thanjavur – Gangaikondacholapuram** vimanas
> Exam angle: builder-temple matching and 4-temple chronological order mixing Pallava and Chola monuments.

## Chola bronzes, frescoes and sculpture — Nataraja iconography | from: S2, S3
# 1. Bronzes - lost-wax casting
- **Technique:** Cholas perfected **cire perdue (lost-wax)**
- **Masterpiece:** **Nataraja**
- **Features:** Worshipped icons; fine craftsmanship, intricate jewellery, expressive faces
# 2. Nataraja element → meaning
| **Nataraja element** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Four hands** | Standard form: dancing Shiva is **four-armed** (UPPSC 2021 key) |
| Upper right hand | Holds the **damaru** (drum) - creation |
| Upper left hand | Holds **agni** (fire) - destruction |
| Lower right hand | **Abhaya mudra** (fear not) - **Protection** |
| Lower left hand | Points to the raised left foot (gaja-hasta) - release, **Liberation / refuge** |
| Right foot | Tramples the dwarf **Apasmara** - conquest of **ignorance** |
| Ears | **Man's earring in the right ear, woman's in the left** = fusion of male and female principles (UPPSC 2021 key) |
| Circle / ring of flames | Prabhamandala - cosmic cycle |

> Exam angle: UPPSC 2021 - "four hands" and "man's earring right, woman's left" are **both correct**.
# 3. Frescoes and stone sculpture
| **Site** | **Art** |
|---|---|
| Brihadeeswara, Thanjavur | **Thanjavur frescoes** on walls of the **dark ambulatory passage**: **Shiva as Tripurantaka**; portraits of **Rajaraja I** with his guru **Karuvur Thevar** |
| Gangaikondacholapuram | **Other murals** |
| **Narthamalai** | Cave paintings of Shiva-Parvati |
| Thanjavur and Gangaikondacholapuram | **Stone sculpture** in deep relief; **Dakshinamurthy** panels rated finest carving in South India |

## Hoysala style — features, terms and sculptors | from: S5

# Ganapatideva - release and accession
- **Early life:** Held captive as a boy by Yadava **Jaitugi**; general **Recherla Rudra** crushed revolts + installed him
- **Release:** From Yadava captivity in **1199**; first inscription **Manthena, 26 Dec 1199**
- **Coastal Andhra:** Still held firmly; title **'Andhra Deshadhiswara'**; unified "Telugudesam"
- **Coastal marriages:** Married Pinnachodi's daughters **Narama and Perama**; made their brother **Jayapa** 'Gajasahani' (elephant-force chief)
# Succession after Ganapatideva - daughters and marriages
- **Daughters (no son):**
  - **Rudramamba** → married Nidadavolu Eastern Chalukya prince **Veerabhadra**
  - **Ganapamba** → married the **Kota** chief
- **Successor:** **Rudrama Devi**, raised as a son (**putrika**); ruled **1262-1289**; mother Kupama Devi guided her training

=== ROW 93 | Rani Rudrama Devi and Prataparudra; Marco Polo's account; fall to the Delhi Sultanate

## Rudrama Devi → accession, reign, family and FIRSTs | from: S0, S1
- **Parentage:** Kakatiya; daughter of **Ganapatideva**; mother Kupama Devi
- **Training:** Groomed from childhood as a son (**putrika**) in war, statecraft and administration
- **Reign:**
  - Ruled as **'Rudradeva Maharaja'** from **1258** (co-regency); Tripurantakam inscription (1266) still calls her Ganapati's vassal
  - Full coronation after Ganapati's death; ruled **1262-1289**, about **26 years**
  - **4th sovereign Kakatiya** (after Rudradeva, Mahadeva, Ganapatideva)
- **Marriage:** Chalukya prince **Veerabhadra**
- **Status:** Second great woman ruler of medieval India after Razia Sultana
- **Royal guru:** Visveshwara Shivacharya (Mandaram agrahara)
- **Orugallu fort:** Repaired + strengthened (abutments, bastions)
- **Nayankara system:** Some notes say she introduced it in her father's time

|---|---|

| Rudramma | m. Yadava prince Ellanadeva |

## Prataparudra II → succession, campaigns and the Nayankara system | from: S3
# 1. Succession
- **Relationship:** **Prataparudra II** = Rudrama's **grandson through her daughter Mummadamma** (son of Mummadamma and Mahadeva)
- **Accession:** Trained by her; **crowned in 1289**; title **'Kumara Rudradeva'**; ruled **1289-1323**
- **Early revolts:** Kayastha, Velama, Reddy factions; Ambadeva's son **Tripurari II**
- **Prime minister:** Muppidi Nayudu
# 2. Campaigns
| **Campaign** | **Year** | **Result** |
|---|---|---|
| Against **Ambadeva** at **Tripurantakam** | **1291** | Commanders Kolanu Munumagammayya, Induluri Annayya won; his principality annexed |
| Against **Ranganatha of Nellore** (Pandya ally) | 1290s | Replaced by Manuma Gandapalu |
| Against the **Yadava** southern borders | 1290s | Commander Gonavithala pushed control toward Devagiri |

# 3. Nayankara system and economy
- **Reform - Nayankara system:** see A-78
  - Reorganised into **72 Nayankaras**; other notes: **75 (or 77)** nayakships/principalities held by military officers
  - Each Nayaka held a **fort**; **one-fourth of the land** under Nayakas
  - Nayakas kept troops + paid a fixed tribute
  - Later continued by Vijayanagara as the **Amara Nayankara** system
- **Economy:** Forests → farmland; tanks/ponds + wells dug; new villages founded

## Khalji and Tughlaq invasions of Warangal, 1303 to 1323 — year, commander, outcome | from: S4, S5
| **Invasion** | **Year** | **Sultan / commander** | **Outcome** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | **1303** | Alauddin Khalji / **Malik Fakhruddin Juna** | **Defeated (repulsed) at Upparapalli** by Pothuganti Aili and Recherla Venna - first attack on Orugallu (APPSC G2 2012 key) |
| 2nd | **1309-10** | Alauddin / **Malik Kafur** | Fort besieged; Prataparudra defeated, submits, pays a large tribute and accepts Delhi overlordship (Amir Khusrau accompanied) |
| 3rd | **1318** (tables: 1317; table in APP: 1317) | Mubarak Shah (Mubarak Khalji) / **Khusrau Khan** (Khusru Khan) | Defeated again; fresh treaty and tribute; renewed vassalage |
| 4th | **1321-22** | Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq / **Ulugh Khan / Juna Khan** (later Muhammad bin Tughlaq) | False rumour of the Sultan's death; Kakatiyas chased the army back to Devagiri |
| 5th | **1323** | **Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq** / **Juna Khan (Ulugh Khan)** (future **Muhammad bin Tughlaq**) with reinforcements | 5-month siege; **Bobbareddi's** desertion (withdrew his forces mid-battle); Prataparudra surrenders and is **captured**; **Warangal fell**; end of the Kakatiyas |

- **1294/1295:** Alauddin's first Deccan raid hit the **Yadavas of Devagiri**, not Andhra
- **1311:** Prataparudra's forces joined Malik Kafur's march on **Madurai** (commander Muppidi Nayaka)
- **1316:** Alauddin's death → Prataparudra stopped tribute
- **1323 siege:** Returned from Devagiri + besieged Orugallu; Prataparudra fought **5 months** → surrendered
- **End:** Held captive by **Ulugh Khan** (APPSC G2 2019 key)
  - Drowned himself / died on the way to Delhi in the **Narmada** (**Vilasa Tamra Sasanam** / Vilasa copper plate / Vilasa grant; Musunuri Prolaya Nayaka's record)
  - One account (some accounts) says the Kaveri
- **Renamed:** Orugallu/Warangal → **Sultanpur** (**'Sultanpur' (Sultanpurti)**)
- **Administration after the fall:** Andhra placed under **Malik Burhanuddin**, wazir/vizier of Devagiri
  - **Malik Maqbul** stationed at Warangal = converted captured Brahmin **Jagannatha Pandit**
- **Causes of the fall:** Disunity of southern kingdoms, weak cavalry, betrayal by chiefs (internal betrayal - Bobbareddi), exhaustion from repeated raids
- ⚠ **1303 – 1310 – 1318 – 1323** = **Fakhruddin Juna – Malik Kafur – Khusrau Khan – Ulugh Khan**
> Exam angle: "first Muslim attack on Orugallu" → **1303** (APPSC 2012).
> Exam angle: captor = **Ulugh Khan** (not Malik Kafur, not Alauddin).
