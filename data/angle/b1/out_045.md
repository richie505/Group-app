## Impact events → date (Lucknow, internment, Montagu Declaration) | from: S3, S4
# 1. Unity - reunion at Lucknow (1916)
- **Reunion:** Surat split (1907) healed at **Lucknow 1916** - Moderates and Extremists reunited + Congress-League unity cemented
- **Eased by:** **Tilak and Besant's** efforts + deaths of Gokhale and Pherozeshah Mehta (1915)
# 2. Besant's internment
- **Besant interned 15 June 1917:** At **Ootacamund** with **B.P. Wadia and G.S. Arundale**
- **Montagu's remark:** On the folly of the internment (quote in the quotations table)
- **Effect:** Outrage boosted the movement + drew in Moderates
- **Released:** **17 September 1917** under INC-League pressure
# 3. Montagu (August) Declaration
- **Montagu (August) Declaration, 20 August 1917:** By **Edwin Montagu, Secretary of State**
  - ⚠ **Trap:** Some notes loosely credit Chelmsford, who was Viceroy
- **Goal:** Increasing Indian association in administration + **gradual development of self-governing institutions / responsible government** → "progressive realisation of responsible government"
- **Effect:** British answer to Home Rule agitation; blunted and effectively ended the agitation → **1919 Act**
- **Split over Mont-Ford proposals:** Congress special session, **Bombay (Aug 1918) under Hasan Imam**
