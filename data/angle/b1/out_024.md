=== ROW 119 | Foundation of Vijayanagara - Harihara and Bukka, Vidyaranya; the Sangama dynasty

## Founding of Vijayanagara — year, coronation, emblem and FIRSTs | from: S0, S1, S2
# 1. Founding
- **Founders:** Brothers **Harihara I and Bukka Raya I**, sons of **Sangama** → **Sangama dynasty**
- **Year & site:** Founded **1336** on the **southern bank of the Tungabhadra**
- **Coronation:** Harihara I crowned **18 April 1336** at the Virupaksha temple
  - After **Vidyaranya** of Sringeri reconverted the brothers to Hinduism
- **Family origin:** Sangama came from "Mangalanilaya"; placed in the **Kuruba** clan; other stock given: Kuruba/Yadava
- **Founding legend:** A hare turned on the royal hunting dogs → site chosen as a place of valour
- **Emblem:** **Varaha** (boar)
- **Religion:** Sangamas = **Shaiva**; later three dynasties = **Vaishnava**
# 2. FIRSTs
| **FIRST** | **FACT** |
|---|---|
| First record to call Harihara I king | **Nellore inscription** (**1336-1355**) |
| First seat of the kingdom | **Anegondi** |
| First city completed | Vijayanagara completed by **1343**; some notes say entered in **1344** |
- ⚠ **1336** = founding + coronation (**18 April**); **1343** = city completed; **1346** = Sringeri visit after the Hoysala victory

## Origin theories → what they say and who backs them | from: S0
# 1. Number of theories
- **Number of theories:** **7 theories**; Robert Sewell records **seven** founding (local) traditions
# 2. Theory → claim → source
| **Theory / version** | **What it says** | **Source** |
|---|---|---|
| **Kakatiya service** | **Sangama** was a **Kakatiya chief**; Harihara and Bukka served **Prataparudra of Warangal** (the **Kakatiyas of Warangal**); after 1323 taken to Delhi and converted | **APPSC G2 2005 key: "a Kakatiya chief"**; UPSC, APHQ; PYQ 2005 |
| Kampili | Governed **Kampili**; captured by Muhammad bin Tughlaq, converted in Delhi, sent back as Sultanate governors | APP |
| Delhi service | Tughlaq sent them to crush Deccan rebels (Musunuri Prolaya Nayaka, Prolaya Vema); defeated, they met Vidyaranya | CDX |
| **Kannada / Hoysala theory** | Officers (Kannadigas) of **Veera Ballala III**; backed by **Father Heras, Rice, S.K. Ayyangar** (Iyengar), **H. Krishna Sastri** (Krishna Shastri), **P.B. Desai** | CDX, APP |
| **Andhra theory** | Andhras; backed by **N. Venkataramanaiah, Sewell, Nilakanta Sastri** | CDX |
# 3. Delhi legend
- **Delhi legend:**
  - After Warangal fell → taken to **Delhi** and converted
  - Muhammad bin Tughlaq sent them to crush the Deccan rebels
  - After defeat met **Vidyaranya** (head of the Sringeri math) near Pampa → returned to Hinduism
  - Founded **Anegondi** in the name of **Virupaksha**
- ⚠ **Sewell** appears twice: records the **seven** traditions + backs the **Andhra theory**; **Heras** backs the **Kannada** theory
> Exam angle: APPSC treats the Kakatiya link as the correct answer.

## Places of the founding → feature — Anegondi, Vijayanagara, Hampi, Sringeri | from: S1
| **Place** | **Feature** |
|---|---|

| **Penukonda**, **Chandragiri** | **Later capitals:** After Talikota → **Penukonda** → **Chandragiri** (both in AP) |
# 1. Vijayanagara ("City of Victory")
- Built on the **south bank of the Tungabhadra** as a Giri Durga on Ramadurga hill; ruins at **Hampi**
- **7 stone ramparts**; 14 x 10 miles
- Completed by **1343**; some notes say entered in **1344**
- **Irrigation:** **Devaraya I** built a dam across the Tungabhadra to carry water to the city

## Sangama rulers → contributions — Harihara I to Devaraya I | from: S3, S6
| **Ruler** | **Achievements** |
|---|---|
| **Harihara I** (1336-56) | Capital from Anegondi to Vijayanagara. Annexed the **Hoysala** kingdom (1346) and the Kadambas (1347); Bukka took **Udayagiri (1343)**. Title **Purva-Paschima Samudradhipati**. **Ibn Battuta** visited (CDX) |
| **Bukka I** (1356-77; APP 1351/52) | Completed the city; **Madurai** conquest; **reconciled Jains and Vaishnavas** (Shravanabelagola inscription; APPSC G2 2003). Took Srisailam and Dupadu; Bitragunta inscription. **Embassy to Ming China (1374)**. Titles **Vedamarga Pratishthapaka**; patron of **Nachana Somana** |
| **Harihara II** (1377-1404) | **Longest-reigning** Sangama king; titles **Rajavyasa, Rajavalmiki**; minister Sayana; **first to attack Sri Lanka**. Minister **Irugappa** built the Ganigitti Jain temple and wrote Navaratnamala. Famine "Durgadevi Karamu"; gave a daughter to Kataya Vema's son |
| **Devaraya I** (1406-22) | **Dam on the Tungabhadra** and canals to the city; stronger walls. Mudgal war with Firuz Shah; gave his daughter to Firoz Shah Bahmani (Bankapur as dowry). **Nicolo Conti** visited. CDX: first to recruit Muslims |
| **Devaraya II** (1422-46) | Greatest Sangama ruler (see Devaraya II page) |
# 1. Bukka I — southern conquest and foreign embassy
- **Southern conquest:** Son **Kumara Kampana** overthrew **Sambhuvaraya** (1366) + destroyed the **Madurai Sultanate**
  - General **Saluva Mangu** killed the Sultan → title "Parapakshi Saluva"
- **Literary record:** **Madhura Vijayam** (Sanskrit) by **Gangadevi**, Kampana's wife
- **Foreign embassy:** **Embassy to the Ming emperor of China, 1374**
- **Patronage:** Telugu poet **Nachana Somana** (Uttara Harivamsam) granted the agrahara **Penchikaladinne**
# 2. Campaigns in Andhra
- **Bukka I:** Captured **Srisailam and Dupadu** (near Markapur)
- **Harihara II:** Pushed towards the Krishna in alliance with the Reddis
- **Devaraya I:**
  - Fought the Kandukuru Reddis → made them feudatories
  - Allied with the Velamas + Kataya Vema
  - Took southern Kondaveedu incl. **Motupalli** → recorded in his **Motupalli Abhaya Sasanam**
- ⚠ **Harihara II** = longest-reigning Sangama king; **Sangama dynasty** = longest-ruling dynasty; **Devaraya II** = greatest

## Devaraya II → army reforms, titles, deeds and patronage | from: S5, S6
# 1. Army reforms
- **Muslim soldiers:** Enrolled **10,000 Muslim soldiers** (APPSC G2 2011 key; PYQ 2011)
  - Muslims got **jagirs** + a mosque; kept a **Quran** near his throne
- **Cavalry & archery:** Improved
# 2. Titles
- **Titles:** **Gajabetekara (elephant hunter)**, Praudha Devaraya, Ilanti Raikonda
# 3. Deeds
- Annexed **Kondaveedu (1424)**
- Tribute from **Sri Lanka** via Lakkanna Dandanayaka ("Dakshina Samudradhipati")
- Repelled the Gajapatis (Draksharama inscription, 1444)
  - Sent **Mallappa Odayar** to help Rajahmundry (**Draksharama inscription, 1444**)
# 4. Culture
- **Srinatha**'s kanakabhishekam after he beat **Gauda Dindima**; patron of Srinatha; honoured **Srinatha**
- Own work: **Mahanataka Sudhanidhi**
- **Vinukonda Vallabharaya:** Author of **Kridabhiramam**; governor of **Vinukonda** under Devaraya II
- ⚠ **Gajabetekara** = Devaraya II; **Purva-Paschima Samudradhipati** = Harihara I; **Dakshina Samudradhipati** = Lakkanna Dandanayaka

## Scholars and poets → contributions — Sayana, Madhava, Vidyaranya, Gangadevi | from: S4
# 1. Vedic commentaries
- **Sayana:** Brother of Vidyaranya; **minister of Harihara II**
  - Wrote the Vedic commentary **Vedartha Prakasha**
- **Joint work:** **Sayana** + **Madhava** → **commentaries on the Vedas** **under Bukka Raya I** (APPSC G2 2008 key)
  - Continued under **Harihara II**
# 2. Two Madhavas
- Madhava-Vidyaranya = Advaita head of Sringeri
- **Madhava the minister/general** conquered **Goa**; title **"Bhuvanaikavira"**
- Some notes merge the two
- **Vidyaranya:** **Vidyaranya** inspired the founding; wrote the **Vidyaranya Kalajnana**
# 3. Early Sangama poets
- **Gangadevi:** Wrote **Madhura Vijayam** (Sanskrit) (PYQ 2012)
  - Wife of **Kumara Kampana**; daughter-in-law of **Bukka I**; **disciple of Agastya**
  - Theme: Kampana's southern campaign → defeat of **Champaraya of Tondaimandalam** → advance to **Kanchi** → conquest of Madurai
- **Nachana Somana:** **Nachana Somana** (Uttara Harivamsam) patronised by the first two Sangama kings
- ⚠ **Sayana** = minister of Harihara II; Vedic commentaries with Madhava began **under Bukka Raya I** (the 2008 key)

=== ROW 120 | Saluva and Tuluva dynasties; Krishnadevaraya - conquests, Amuktamalyada, the Ashtadiggajas

## Saluva Narasimha → first usurpation (1485) and rule | from: S0
# 1. Reasons for the usurpation
- **Reasons:**
  - Weak rulers after Devaraya II
  - **Virupaksha II** a drunkard (Nuniz); **Praudha Raya** worthless
  - Gajapati + Bahmani invasions ate into the kingdom
- **Usurpation (1485):** Saluva Narasimha, lord of **Chandragiri**, sent general **Tuluva Narasa Nayaka** → took the throne
  - The "**first usurpation**"; he was called **Raya Maharasu**
# 2. Saluva rule (1485-91)
| **Angle** | **Fact** |
|---|---|
| Capital | Kalyanapuram → **Chandragiri** |
| Conquered | Took **Honnavar, Bhatkal, Mangalore** |
| Army | Brought **Arab horses** (Ormuz) + Arab cavalry; introduced **Samugaris (gunmen)** |
| Lost | Lost Udayagiri to Gajapati Purushottama |
| Court poet | **Rajanatha Dindima** (**Saluvabhyudayam**) |
- **Annamacharya:** His contemporary (APPSC G2 2011); **32,000 kirtanas** to Venkateswara
  - Tirupati became the leading Vaishnava centre

## Tuluva dynasty → origin, second usurpation (1505) and succession | from: S1
- **Name:** From **Tulunadu** (Mysore region), conquered by Harihara in 1342
- **Progenitor:** **Timmaraju**; mother tongue Kannada, but Telugu favoured
# 1. Second usurpation
- **Tuluva Narasa Nayaka**: Saluva general + real ruler under Immadi Narasimha; died 1503
- His son **Vira Narasimha** killed Immadi Narasimha at Penugonda in **1505**
- Founder = **Vira Narasimha** (APPSC G2 2002)
- ⚠ **Tuluva Narasa Nayaka** helped both usurpations (1485 general; 1505 father of the usurper) but the founder in the key = **Vira Narasimha**
# 2. Succession
- **Succession:** **Vira Narasimha (1505-09) → Krishnadevaraya (1509-29) → Achyutaraya (1529-42) → Venkata I (1542) → Sadasiva (1543-70/76)**

## Krishnadevaraya's works → language — Amuktamalyada and the Sanskrit works | from: S5
# 1. Amuktamalyada
- **Amuktamalyada:** Telugu; also called **Vishnuchittiyam**
- **Meaning:** "**one who offers the garland she has worn**" ("one who offers the garland she wore")
- **Circumstance (legend):** **Andhra Mahavishnu of Srikakulam** (Krishna district) commanded the king in a dream to write it; written during the Kalinga campaign
- **Dedication:** **Dedicated to Lord Venkateswara**
- **Subject:**
  - **Goda Devi (Andal)** + her marriage to **Sri Ranganatha**
  - Sub-stories: **Yamunacharya Charitra** + **Maladasari Katha**
  - Describes **Villiputtur** + Madhura
  - Statecraft, social life + the **genealogy from Bukka to Krishnadevaraya**
- **Political maxims:** Practical advice on **statecraft, kingship and administration**
  - Records the king's political ideas + social, religious and economic life of the period
- **Preface:** "**Desa Bhashalandu Telugu Lessa**" ("Telugu is the best of the regional languages")
- **Status:** One of the **Telugu Pancha Kavyas**; first Telugu work named after its heroine
- **PYQ (APPSC G2 2003):** It does **not** describe his invasions
  - ⚠ **Trap:** option not in the poem = "Describes the invasions of Krishnadevaraya"; it does contain Godadevi's marriage, political matters + genealogy from Bukka to Krishnadevaraya
# 2. Other works
- **Jambavati Kalyanam:** Drama in **Sanskrit** (APPSC G2 2019)
  - Mentions **Virupaksha** + the Vasantotsavams
- **Other Sanskrit works:** Some notes add **Usha Parinayam**

## Central administration → officers and departments (Kandachara, Athavane) | from: S0
# 1. King & council
- **King:** Supreme head, commander + fountain of justice; bound by Dharma + the ministers' counsel
  - Burton Stein: a **"segmentary state"**
# 2. Officer → role

|---|---|

| **Karanikas** | Scribes |
# 3. Departments

  - Office = the **Rayasam**, with Karanalu (accountants)
| **Department / official** | **Function** |
|---|---|
| **Athavane / Attavana** | **Revenue / finance** (APP, CDX) |

## Amuktamalyada → Krishnadevaraya's political ideas | from: S1
# 1. Nature of the work
- **Approach:** Devotion + **practical advice on kingship and administration**; "political matters are discussed" (APPSC G2 2003)
- **Genealogy:** Records the **genealogy of the dynasty** but not his campaigns (APPSC G2 2003)
# 2. Political maxims
| **Area** | **Maxim** |
|---|---|
| Revenue | **Bulk of revenue should go to the army** |
| Diplomacy | A **spy system** + **ambassadors (sthanapatis)** are essential |
| Forts | **Brahmins** should command forts |
| Dharma | King should rule with an eye on **Dharma** + protect his subjects |
| Trade | Improve **harbours**; encourage import of **horses, elephants and gems**; treat foreign merchants generously |
| Welfare | Build **tanks and irrigation**; win over **forest tribes** rather than only crush them |

## Provincial administration → units, heads and governors' powers | from: S2
# 1. Unit → head
| **Unit** | **Head** |
|---|---|
| **Rajya / Mandala / Rashtra** (province) | Prince (Kumara; Sangama princes titled **Odeyar/Vodeyar**) or **Durgadhipati / Durga Dannayaka**; office called Chavadi |
| **Nadu / Seema** (district) | **Nadagauda**; Parupatyagar revenue officials |
| **Sthala / Kampana** (sub-district) | Reddi, Gauda, Sthalakaranam; Kavalgar for order |
| **Grama** | Ayagars |
- **Levels:** **Rajyam/Rashtram** (province) > **Seema** (Nadu in Telugu; headed by Parupatyandaru) > **Sthalam** (Reddi, Gouda or Sthalakaranam) > **Gramam**
- **Number:** About **17 rajyams** under Achyutaraya (listed in his inscriptions)
  - Prominent: Udayagiri, Chandragiri, Kondaveedu
- **Vanitas:** Aravidu inscriptions divide the **Udayagiri, Penukonda** and Hampi Rajyams into **Vanitas**
# 2. Governors
- **Head:** Provinces built around forts → **Durgadhipati/Durgadannayaka**; office = **Chavadi**
  - Often princes, styled **Odeyar**
- **Powers:** **Mint coins, remit old taxes and levy new ones**; grant tax exemptions; own armies
- **Control:** Often transferred; Brahmins deliberately posted as dannayakas in border provinces
- !! Not in your sources: the individual governors of Udayagiri, Penukonda, Chandragiri and Gooty.

## Nayankara system → amaram terms, obligations and scale | from: S3
# 1. Terms → meaning
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Amaram** | Land the king granted to military chiefs instead of cash salaries |
| **Amaranayakas / Nayakas / Poligars** | The chiefs holding amarams (also Nayaks, Palaiyagars) |
| **Mahamandaleshwara** | Their title |
| **Amara gramalu** | Collected **Durgadayam** to pay for the army |
| **kist / kappam** | Fixed **annual tribute** |
- Their soldiers were paid in cash
- **Status:** More autonomy than the Durgadannayakas
  - King could punish or remove them for failure
- **Powers within amarams:** Collected taxes, kept law and order, administered justice
# 2. Obligations
- **Tribute:** Fixed **annual tribute (kist / kappam)**
- **Troops:** **Maintain a quota of troops** (infantry, cavalry, horses, elephants) ready for the king in war
- **Court attendance:** **Attend court at Mahanavami** festival with gifts
- **Local duties:** Keep order, maintain forts, fund temples + irrigation
# 3. Scale (data)
| **Reign** | **Figure** |
|---|---|
| **Krishnadevaraya** | About **200 nayankaras** (Nuniz: **200 Nayankaras**) |
| **Achyutaraya** | **6,00,000** amara troops (Nuniz) |

# 1. Tax terms

## Army → wings, firearms, forts and cavalry | from: S8
# 1. Army wings & composition
- **Two wings:**
  - **Siddha / standing army**: under the king via the **Kandachara**, headed by the **Dalavayi**; paid every 4 months; Nuniz: about 50,000
  - **Amara-nayaka levies**: Nuniz counted **600,000** under Achyutaraya
- **Krishnadevaraya's strength:** 24,000 horses + 1,200 elephants + 5 lakh infantry
- **Composition:** All castes incl. **Chenchus, Koyas, Boyas** + **Muslim** troops (Devaraya II, Rama Raya)
# 2. Firearms & forts
- **Firearms:** Came with Turkish + Portuguese experts
  - Bukka I = first Vijayanagara king to use cannon
  - Saluva Narasimha introduced **Samugaris** (gunmen)
- **Forts:** **Giri, Sthala, Jala, Vana** (Rayavachakam), under **Durga Dannayakas**
  - Krishnadevaraya advised making Brahmins fort commanders
# 3. Cavalry & horse imports
- **Cavalry:** Decisive
- **Horse imports:** **Arabian/Persian horses** imported via **Goa, Honnavar, Bhatkal**
  - Trade run by Portuguese + Arab merchants → **bullion drain**
- ⚠ **FIRST:** cannon = **Bukka I**; **Samugaris** = **Saluva Narasimha**

## Nayankara compared → Kakatiya, Vijayanagara and Mughal mansabdari | from: S10
