=== ROW 143 | Bhakti saints and their teachings - Ramanuja, Madhva, Nimbarka, Vallabha, Ramananda

## Vedantic acharyas — chronology, contemporaries and FIRSTs | from: S0, S1, S3
# 1. Chronological order
- **Order:** **Shankara (788-820) → Ramanuja (1017-1137) → Nimbarka → Madhva (13th c.) → Ramananda (1400-1470) → Vallabha (1479-1531) → Chaitanya (1485-1533)**
  - Nimbarka usually placed in the 12th-13th c.
  - ⚠ **Trap:** Nimbarka was NOT a contemporary of Akbar (UPSC 2019)
- **Contemporaries:** Vallabha and Chaitanya
# 2. FIRST / pioneer
| **FIRST** | **FACT** |
|---|---|
| Forerunner / pioneer of the Bhakti cult | **Shankaracharya** (Advaita) |
| **First true exponent** of the Bhakti movement | **Ramanuja** |
| **First Bhakti saint to preach in Hindi** | **Ramananda** |
| First medieval reformer to denounce caste outright | **Ramananda** |

- ⚠ Shankara = forerunner (Advaita, knowledge); Ramanuja = first true exponent (devotion)

## Acharyas → school, teaching and deity | from: S1, S3
# 1. Philosophical schools → founder
| **Acharya** | **School** | **Teaching emphasis** | **Deity** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shankaracharya (788-820) | **Advaita** (non-dualism) | Monism; knowledge | - |
| Ramanuja (1017-1137) | **Vishishtadvaita** (qualified non-dualism) | Salvation through intense devotion (prapatti) to Vishnu; souls and universe are dependent parts of the Supreme; a reaction against Advaita | Vishnu / Narayana with Lakshmi (Sri Vaishnava) |
| Madhvacharya | **Dvaita** (dualism) | God (Vishnu) and souls eternally distinct; Saguna devotion [GK] | Vishnu (Krishna at Udupi) [GK] |
| Nimbarka | **Dvaitadvaita** (dualistic non-dualism) | Devotion to Radha-Krishna [GK] | Radha-Krishna [GK] |
| Vallabhacharya (1479-1531) | **Shuddhadvaita** (pure non-dualism) | Pushtimarg, the "path of grace"; Vatsalya bhakti to child Krishna; householder life for devotees | Child Krishna; Radha-Krishna |
| Ramananda | - | - | **Rama** |
| Chaitanya | - | - | Krishna |

# 2. Teachings
- **Ramanuja:**
  - Best means of salvation = **devotion** (not Vedas, logic or meditation) (UPSC 2022)
  - Devoted worship of **Narayana and Lakshmi** to escape cycle of birth and death
- **Vallabha:** Worship of child Krishna (**vatsalya** bhakti)
- **Ramananda:** Replaced Vishnu worship with devotion to **Rama**
- ⚠ Advaita = Shankara; Vishishtadvaita = Ramanuja; Dvaita = Madhva; Dvaitadvaita = Nimbarka; Shuddhadvaita = Vallabha
> Exam angle: "which statement is NOT correct" - e.g. Madhva propounded Nirguna bhakti is wrong (RPSC 2024); Shankara = Advaita (APPSC-G1 2012).

## Acharyas → contributions and disciples | from: S0, S3
# 1. Person → contribution

|---|---|

| **Ramananda** | Preached Vaishnavism in North India; **first Bhakti saint to preach in Hindi** |

# 2. Ramananda — Hindi, caste dictum, lineage

- **Caste:** Rejected caste and Brahmin supremacy
  - Saying: **"Let no man ask a man's sect or caste."**
- **Lineage:** Called a "disciple of Ramanuja"; since Ramanuja died in 1137, read as **follower of Ramanuja's school**

## Places → acharyas; Ramanuja in Andhra | from: S0, S4
# 1. Birthplace and main centre
| **Acharya** | **Birthplace** | **Main centre** |
|---|---|---|
| Shankaracharya | **Kaladi** (Malabar, Kerala) | Mathas; Sringeri head Vidyaranya was the Vijayanagara-era proponent |
| Ramanuja | **Sriperumbudur** (Tamil Nadu) [GK] | **Srirangam** monastery |

# 2. Sri Vaishnavism in Andhra
- **Theological base:** His Vishishtadvaita (11th-12th c.) grounded Bhakti in Andhra
- **Spread:** **Sri Vaishnavism** = most widely spread sect of the Vijayanagara era
  - Centres: **Tirupati**, Kanchi, Mangalagiri, Pushpagiri
- **Patrons:** **Velanati Cholas** + **Palnati Haihayas**
- **Influence:** Inspired **Brahmanayudu's Veera Vaishnava** movement in Palnadu (aimed at casteless society)
- **Inclusion:** Gave **Panchamas temple entry**
  - Prime example: Krishnadevaraya's Amuktamalyada ("**Mala Dasari Katha**")
- **Tirumala:** Credited with ordering temple worship + installing **Govindaraja** at Tirupati
- **PYQ:** Movement Ramanuja led in Andhra (devotion to Vishnu + social equality) = **Bhakti movement** (APPSC-GS 2022)
# 3. Commemoration
- **Statue of Equality:** Hyderabad; world's second-tallest sitting statue, inaugurated by the PM (UPSC 2022)

## Universal angles | from: S5 | universal
- **Current relevance (2026 discourse pieces):**
  - Ramanuja as "Ilaya Azhwar"; held to be incarnation of Adisesha/Lakshmana
  - Title **Koil Annan**: earned by fulfilling Andal's vow (18 Sep 2026)
  - Cousin **Embar** (Govinda of Maduramangalam, near Sriperumbudur) saved him from teacher **Yadava Prakasa's** plot (22 Apr 2026)
  - Taught any eager student without the customary long wait (23 Feb 2026)
- **AP angle:**
  - Narahari Tirtha (Madhva's disciple) spread Dvaita in Andhra-Kalinga from **Srikurmam**
  - Vedanta Desika spread Vadakalai in Andhra (14th c.)
- **Significance:** Acharyas gave Bhakti a philosophical base against Advaita; opened temple worship to lower groups

## Saints → teachings and contributions | from: S1, S2, S3
# 1. Kabir
- **Birth legend:** Born to a Brahmin widow; raised by Muslim weaver **Niru** and wife **Nima**
- **Stream:** **Nirguna Bhakti** (APPSC-GS 2024)
  - One God: Ram, Rahim, Allah, Khuda, Hari or Govinda
- **Attack on ritual:** Condemned rituals + idol worship of **both** Hindus and Muslims; opposed caste
- **Mission:** Hindu-Muslim unity; regarded as greatest saint of the Bhakti cult
  - ⚠ **Trap:** Kabir was **not** influenced by Shaikh Ahmad Sirhindi, who lived later (1564-1624) (UPSC 2019)
# 2. Chaitanya
- **Turn to sainthood:** After his wife's death; passionate devotion to Krishna
- **Sect:** Founded **Gaudiya Vaishnavism**
- **Sankirtan:** Popularised collective devotional singing; later written up as Padavali kirtans
- **Spread:** **Bengal and Odisha**
- **Followers' view:** Incarnation of Vishnu
# 3. Maharashtra saints → contribution
| **Saint** | **Key fact** |
|---|---|
| Namdev | Tailor family; abhangs in Marathi and Hindi; hymns in the **Guru Granth Sahib**; had the greatest influence on the people |

| Ramdas | Stressed strength, discipline and devotion to Rama; role in Maratha political awakening; Shivaji's guru |
| **Janabai** | Namdev's disciple; low caste; servant in his home; 300+ poems |
| **Bahinabai** | Abhangas on women's daily struggles |

=== ROW 145 | Bhakti in Andhra - Annamacharya, Bammera Potana, Vemana, the Tallapaka poets

## Alvars and Nayanars → deity, number, saints, canon | from: S1
# 1. Alvars vs Nayanars
|  | **Alvars** | **Nayanars (Nayanmars)** |
|---|---|---|
| Sect / deity | **Vaishnava** (**Vishnu**) | **Shaiva** (**Shiva**) (UPPSC 2014) |
| Traditional number | **12** | **63** (MPPSC 2022) |
| Women saints | **Andal** - only female Alvar (MPPSC 2025) | **Karaikkal Ammaiyar** (Tamil Shaiva; extreme asceticism) |
| Key saints | **Nammalvar, Tirumangai Alvar, Andal, Periyalvar** | **Appar, Sundarar, Tirugnana Sambandar, Manikkavachakar** |
| Canon | **Nalayira Divya Prabandham** (Sri Vaishnava scripture) | **Tevaram** - earliest, most sacred part of the **Tirumurai** |

- ⚠ Alvars = **12** = Vishnu; Nayanars = **63** = Shiva
# 2. Period, hymns and canon
- **Period:** c. **6th-9th centuries**; hymns in Tamil
- **Sacred geography:** Sang **Tamil hymns**; identified sacred sites that grew into temple and pilgrimage centres; hymns became part of temple ritual
- **Social base:** Challenged caste and Brahmanical dominance; Nayanmars included Dalits, hunters and women
- **Tirumurai:** **12-volume** Tamil Shaiva canon, compiled 7th-12th centuries; the "**Fifth Veda**" (JPSC 2021)
- **Siddhas (Sittars):** Tamil region; monotheistic; condemned idolatry (UPSC 2016)
# 3. Chola ruler → patronage
| **Ruler** | **Patronage** |
|---|---|
| **Parantaka I** | Installed statues of Appar, Sambandar, Sundarar |
| **Rajaraja I** | Consecrated bronzes of all **63 Nayanmars** at **Chidambaram** |

- **Chola temples:** Thanjavur, Gangaikondacholapuram, Chidambaram

- **Wife of Annamayya:** **Timmakka** - Subhadra Kalyanam [GK]
# 2. Sankirtanas
- **Output:** About **32,000 sankirtanas** to **Lord Venkateswara** of Tirumala; only about **12,000** survive
- **Preservation:** On **copper plates at Tirumala**
- **Veturi Prabhakara Sastry:** Recovered/excavated + edited the copper-plate songs; said Annamacharya created the science of sankirtana
- **Titles:** **Padakavita Pitamaha**, Sankirtanacharya, Harikirtanacharya
  - **Father of the Telugu keertana**; pioneer of padakavita / the **sankirtana / pada** form
# 3. Annamacharya — other works
- **Telugu:** Sringara Manjari (258 dvipadas); **Sri Venkateswara Satakam**; Dvipada Ramayanam; Alivelumangamma Satakam
- **Sanskrit:** Sankirtana Lakshanam, Venkatachala Mahatyam

## Bammera Potana → life, works, patronage debate | from: S8
# 1. Life and titles
- **Dates:** c. **1450-1510**
- **Native:** **Bammera** (Janagama district, Telangana)
- **Srinatha link:** Contemporary; some notes even make him Srinatha's brother-in-law
- **Titles:** **Sahaja Panditudu** / **Sahaja Panditya**, Nigarva Chudamani
# 2. Works

| **Bhogini Dandakam** | About Sarvajna Singabhupala and Bhogini |
| **Narayana Satakam** | Lost |

# 3. Literature - Bammera Potana patronage debate

## Vemana and Potuluri Veerabrahmam → anti-caste voices | from: S9
# 1. Vemana
- **Identity:** Yogi-philosopher poet; **satakam-style verses in colloquial Telugu** for common people (**Vemana** padyalu) [GK]
- **Themes:** Satire on **empty ritual, caste discrimination by birth, idol worship, superstition and hypocrisy**; strongly condemned caste
  - Stood for humanism, inner wisdom (Jnana), equal treatment of all castes; plain moral and practical wisdom
- **Verse form:** Mostly the **Ataveladi** metre; short verses with refrain **"Vishwadabhirama Vinura Vema"** ("Viswadabhirama Vinura Vema")
- **C.P. Brown:** Collected verses from manuscripts, edited + translated into English → Verses of Vemana (**1829**)
- **Dating disputed:** **17th c.** vs **14th-15th c.** - learn both
# 2. Veerabrahmendra Swamy
- **Potuluri Veerabrahmam:** Prophecies in **Kalagnanam**
- **Social message:** Strongly condemned caste differences
- **Disciples:** Across castes and faiths, e.g. **Siddaiah**, a Muslim
- ⚠ C.P. Brown: Verses of Vemana **1829**; Andhra Maha Bhagavatamu **1840**
> Exam angle: Vemana is paired with Potuluri Veerabrahmam (Kaalagnanam) as Andhra's anti-caste voices.

## Later Andhra composers → deity and works | from: S10
# 1. Bhakta Ramadasu (Kancherla Gopanna)
- **Name:** Bhakta Ramadasu (**Kancharla Gopanna**); deity **Rama**
- **Birth:** **1620** at **Nelakondapalli** (Khammam)

## Universal angles | from: S14 | universal

- **Significance:** **Telugu Bhakti** Carried through padams, keertanas, satakas, dvipada → base of Carnatic music's Telugu repertoire

  - Potana = Sahaja Panditya
