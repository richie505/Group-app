## Sources — earliest references to Andhras and FIRSTs | from: S0, S3, S5
# 1. Earliest references to Andhras
| **FIRST / earliest** | **Source** |
|---|---|
| **Earliest literary reference** to Andhras | **Aitareya Brahmana** (Sunahshepa story - Vishwamitra's disowned sons) |
| Earliest Buddhist reference to "**Andhrapatha**" | **Vinayavatthu** (PYQ 2019) |
| "Andhrapatha" first in an inscription | **Maidavolu copper plates** of Pallava **Sivaskandavarma** (Prakrit, c. 300 CE) |
| **First decipherable inscriptions** | Ashoka's edicts - **Prakrit** in **Brahmi** = the earliest inscriptions of India (APPSC 2017) |
| First Telugu word | "**Nagabu**" (Satavahana period) |
| **First Telugu inscription** | **Kalamalla** (Renati Choda) |
| First definite references to coins | **Pali texts** - **kahapana, nikkha, kamsa, kakanika** |

- **Mahabharata:** Andhras fought for the **Kauravas**
- **Buddhist name for region:** "**Andhakaratta**"
- **Pali coin references:** Corroborated archaeologically by **punch-marked coins** (UPSC 2026: both statements correct)
- ⚠ "Andhrapatha": **Vinayavatthu** = first in Buddhist text; **Maidavolu** = first in an inscription

## Dating methods → principle | from: S7
| **Method** | **Principle** |
|---|---|
| **Stratigraphy** | Layers in sequence: lower layer is older (superposition) [GK] |
| **Radiocarbon (C-14)** | Decay of C-14 in organic remains; half-life about 5,730 years [GK] |
| **Dendrochronology** | Counting annual tree rings [GK] |
| **Thermoluminescence** | Light released on heating pottery/burnt material measures radiation absorbed since last firing [GK] |

=== ROW 2 | Stone Age cultures - Palaeolithic, Mesolithic, Neolithic, Chalcolithic sites of India

## Stone Age phases → technique, tools and sites | from: S0, S1
| **Stage** | **Technique / tools** | **Sites** |
|---|---|---|
| Lower | **Handaxe-cleaver** (Acheulian); chopper-chopping (**Soanian**) [GK] | **Attirampakkam** (TN), **Hunsgi** (Karnataka), **Didwana **(Rajasthan), **Sohan valley** (Pakistan Punjab) [GK] |

- ⚠ **Acheulian** = handaxe-cleaver; **Soanian** = chopper-chopping

## Megaliths → types, terms and AP districts | from: S5
# 1. Nature of megaliths
- **Definition:** Graves with weapons, tools, pottery, surrounded by big stones (PYQ 2018)
- **Age:** Iron Age, with **black-and-red ware**; c. 1500/1000 BCE to 300 CE
- **AP names:** "**Rakshasa Gullu**" (also Pandava Gullu, Kurambara Gullu, Morivara Gullu)
- **Social ranks:** Burial goods show ranks - dolmens for elites, pits for commoners
# 2. Types → form and AP distribution
| **Type** | **Form** | **AP distribution** |
|---|---|---|
| **Cist** | Stone-slab box burial (chamber) | Common in AP; **not** numerous in Visakhapatnam (Visakhapatnam claim keyed wrong - see below) |
| **Dolmen** | Stone table-like chamber | Krishna, Chittoor, East Godavari |
| **Sarcophagus** | Terracotta coffin, often with legs | More in **Chittoor** and **Anantapur**; one found near Nallavaram |
| **Menhir** | Upright memorial stone, 2-6 m | Krishna district |
| **Anthropomorphic** | Human-shaped stone | **Tirupati** region: largest collection |

- **AP megalithic sites:** **Nagarjunakonda** (centre), **Brahmagiri**, Gudivada, **Jonnawada** (Nellore), Boodigapalli, Peddaveedu
- **Types:** About **12 types** in all; others - cairn circle, urn burial
# 3. PYQ key
- **APPSC G2 2025 key:** The **incorrect** statement = "many cist burials in Visakhapatnam"

=== ROW 4 | Pre-Harappan and Early Harappan cultures - Mehrgarh, Amri, Kot Diji, Kalibangan-I

## Early farming sites → region and FIRSTs | from: S0
| **Site** | **Region** | **Note** |
|---|---|---|
| **Mehrgarh** | Baluchistan, near Bolan Pass [GK] | c. **7000 BCE**; earliest agriculture, domestication and pottery in the subcontinent; **earliest settled life** (PYQ UPPSC 2008); remains from **Stone Age to Harappan** times (PYQ UPPSC 2009) |
| **Kili Gul Muhammad** | Quetta valley [GK] | Early farming village |

- **Neolithic phase (Baluchistan, Indus plains):** Pastoralism + limited cultivation → permanent villages
  - Crops: wheat, barley, dates, cotton
  - Animals: sheep, goats
  - Mud houses + crafts

## Mehrgarh periods → crops, animals, crafts and burials | from: S0
| **Period** | **Evidence** |
|---|---|
| I (aceramic Neolithic, c. 7000 BCE) | **Barley, wheat**; sheep, goat, cattle; mud-brick houses [GK] |
| II | Pottery begins; **cotton**[GK] |
| III and later | Copper working, wheel-made painted pottery, beads [GK] |

- **Burials:** Dead under or near houses, often flexed, with **grave goods** (ornaments, tools, sometimes goats)
- ⚠ Period I = aceramic (no pottery); pottery + **cotton** begin in Period II

## Early Harappan cultures → type site and pottery | from: S1
# 1. Phase and cultures
- **Early/Pre-Harappan phase:** **3300-2600 BCE**
  - Older notes: 2900-2500 BCE or 3200-2600 BCE
- **Names:** **Regionalisation Era**; corresponds to the **Hakra phase**

| **Culture** | **Type site / area** |
|---|---|
| **Amri** | Amri (Sindh): transitional pre- to Mature Harappan; rhinoceros remains; later **Jhangar** traces |
| **Kot Diji** | Kot Diji (Sindh): **fortified** pre-Harappan settlement, destroyed by fire; many stone implements |
| **Sothi-Siswal** | Sothi, Siswal (Rajasthan-Haryana) |
| **Hakra** | Ghaggar-Hakra (Cholistan) |
| **Damb Sadaat** | Quetta |

- **Other Early Harappan sites:** Padri (Gujarat), Kalibangan, **Dholavira**, Harappa, Balakot, **Bhirrana**, Gumla
  - **Banawali:** Pre- + Mature Harappan
  - **Mitathal:** Pre-, Mature + Late phases
# 2. Pottery traditions
- **Regional traditions:** Amri ware, Kot Diji ware, Sothi-Siswal ware, Hakra ware
  - Mature Harappan pottery became uniform
- **Technique:** Early pots hand-made → later wheel-made; Early Harappan used the potter's wheel

## Kalibangan-I → features | from: S2
- **Location:** Rajasthan, on the Ghaggar; name = "**black bangles**"
- **Pre-Harappan level:** **Earliest ploughed field**, criss-cross furrows → **mixed cropping**
- **Settlement:** Enclosed by a **mud-brick fortification**
- **Pottery:** **Six fabrics** ("6 types of pottery")
- **Excavation:** **1961-69** by **B. B. Lal and B. K. Thapar**
  - Identified earlier by A. Ghosh (APP says "K. Ghosh")
- **Later phase:** Mature-phase fire altars come later
- ⚠ Ploughed field = pre-Harappan level; fire altars = Mature phase

## Early to Mature Harappan transition → features and debates | from: S3
- **Proto-urban growth:** Fortified towns, craft production, trade contacts
- **Continuity:** Kalibangan + Kot Diji pottery and buildings anticipate Mature Harappan → urbanisation **gradual and internal**
- **Early-phase features:**
  - Copper, the wheel, the plough; granaries + defence walls
  - Motifs: **pipal, humped bull, cobra, horned deity**
- **Open problem:** The **gap** between Neolithic Mehrgarh and the urban phase

## Universal angles | from: S5 | universal
- **Rakhigarhi:** ~**550 ha**; continuous occupation **Early to Mature Harappan**; Mound 7 yielded **56 skeletons** (23 Jun 2026)
- **Current relevance:** Haryana protected **Tighrana** and **Mitathal** (Bhiwani)
- **Significance:** Mehrgarh + Early Harappan phase → civilisation grew locally, not from Mesopotamia
