- **Patronage:**
  - Buddhist caves = earliest (c. 600-730 CE, before the Rashtrakutas); some notes list them as Rashtrakuta

  - Jain caves = 9th-10th century; some notes credit the **Yadavas** (later additions)
> Exam angle: cave count, the 12-17-5 split, and ordering of named caves.
# 4. Elephanta - patron debate, Trimurti
- **Location:** Island cave complex near **Mumbai**
- **Patron:** **Rashtrakutas** per some notes
  - MPPSC 2021 key: Ellora and Elephanta = **Rashtrakuta**
  - **Debate:** many historians date the main cave to the **6th century Kalachuris**
- **Masterpiece:** **Trimurti** (Maheshamurti) - three-headed Shiva
  - "Trimurthi (Elephanta)" + Kailasa = Rashtrakuta zenith of art

## Kailasa temple (Ellora Cave 16) → ruler, technique, features | from: S4
- **Ruler:** **Krishna I (r. 756-773 CE)**, Rashtrakuta
- **Identity:** **Cave 16** of Ellora (APPSC-GS 2025)
- **Technique:**
  - **monolithic**, carved **top-down** from a single **basalt** cliff
  - Excavated downward and inward, not built stone upon stone
  - **largest monolithic rock excavation in the world**; surface area > **Parthenon**
- **Deity:** **Shiva** as lord of **Mount Kailasa** (early Rashtrakuta rulers = staunch Shaivas)

| **Feature** | **Detail** |
|---|---|
| Size | c. **200 ft long, 100 ft wide, 100 ft high** |
| Plinth | **25-ft** plinth with elephants and lions - temple seems to rest on their backs |
| Tower | **three-tiered Dravida vimana** although in Maharashtra (Nagara territory) |
| Parts | main shrine + **Nandi mandapa** |
| Panels | **Ravana shaking Mount Kailasa**; Mahishasuramardini; Shiva Nataraja; Ramayana, Mahabharata, Purana scenes |

- **Style:** Rashtrakutas shaped the **Vesara** synthesis (Nagara + Dravida) → Deccan style
> Exam angle: "carved during the reign of" - Krishna I, not Dantidurga or Amoghavarsha.

## Universal angles | from: S6 | universal
- **Current relevance:**

- **Significance:** Rashtrakuta era raised Kannada to a classical medium + gave Ellora its Hindu peak

# 4. Alampur Navabrahma temples

  - Alampur = the place in the Telugu region with Badami Chalukyan temples
- **Style:** **Nagara** (curvilinear rekha shikhara)
- **District:** **Jogulamba Gadwal, Telangana** (earlier Mahabubnagar, undivided AP)

=== ROW 76 | Pallavas - Simhavishnu, Mahendravarman I, Narasimhavarman I Mamalla, Nandivarman II

## Pallavas → origin, territory, period and capital | from: S0
- **Territory:** **Tondaimandalam** (APPSC-GS 2023); capital **Kanchipuram**
- **Period:** c. **275-897 CE**
- **Origin theories:**
  - **Parthian (Pahlava)** foreign origin
  - **Andhra origin** - earliest charters (Prakrit) from Krishna-Guntur region; early Pallavas replaced Ikshvakus there
  - **Local Tamil** origin in Tondaimandalam

## Inscriptions and charters → ruler and point | from: S1, S3

| **Charter** | **Ruler** | **Point** |
|---|---|---|

- **PYQs:**
  - APPSC-G2 2025: Manchikallu and Maidavolu inscriptions = **Pallavas**
  - APPSC-GS 2018: **Manchikallu inscription of Simhavarman** proves Pallavas replaced Ikshvakus of Vijayapuri
- **Loss recorded:** Krishna-Godavari lands of Mahendravarman I to **Pulakesin II** (Maruturu, Kopparam inscriptions)

  - ⚠ **Trap:** Some notes place Simhavishnu in the 3rd century - he belongs to the late 6th
# 2. Key reigns
- **Mahendravarman I:** **c. 600-630 CE**; son of Simhavishnu; many-sided - painting, music, literature
- **Narasimhavarman I - Chalukya war:** Defeated and killed **Pulakesin II** (Manimangala, 642); sacked **Vatapi** via general Paranjoti
- **Narasimhavarman I - Sri Lanka:** **Two naval expeditions** to restore ally prince **Manavarma**
- **Nandivarman II - accession:** **Nandivarman II Pallavamalla** - throne as a boy of **12**; chosen by ministers + nobles from a collateral line after Paramesvaravarman II died (c. 731)
- **Nandivarman II - setback:** Lost Kanchi temporarily to **Vikramaditya II** (Chalukya)
# 3. LAST - later Pallavas and end
- **Later rulers:** Dantivarman, Nandivarman III, Nripatunga, **Aparajita**
- **End:** **Aditya I Chola** killed Aparajita (c. **897-898 CE**; dates given as 897 or 898)

=== ROW 77 | Pallava art and architecture - Mandagapattu inscription, Mahabalipuram rathas, Shore Temple, Kailasanatha

## Phases of Pallava architecture → ruler → example | from: S0

| **Phase** | **Ruler** | **Type** | **Examples** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mahendra** | Mahendravarman I (600-630) | rock-cut pillared mandapas | Mandagapattu, Mahendravadi, Dalavanur, Mamandur |
| **Mamalla** | Narasimhavarman I (630-668) | monolithic rathas and mandapas | Pancha Rathas, Mahabalipuram |
| **Rajasimha** | Narasimhavarman II | first **structural** temples | Shore Temple; Kailasanatha, Kanchi |
| **Nandivarman / Aparajita** | Nandivarman II and later | smaller structural temples [GK] | Vaikuntha Perumal, Kanchi [GK] |

- **Evolution:** Rock-cut → monolithic rathas → structural temples

## FIRSTs, largest, smallest, oldest | from: S1, S2, S4
- **Mandagapattu inscription:** **Mahendravarman I**
  - **Claim:** Temple for **Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva** "**without brick, timber, metal or mortar**" = beginning of rock-cut cave temples in South India
  - **Dvarapalakas:** At the entrance - later common in South Indian temples
- **Arjuna's Penance / Descent of the Ganga:** **largest open-air rock relief** in India
- **Ratha sizes:**
  - **Dharmaraja ratha is the largest** - precursor of the Dravida vimana
  - **Draupadi ratha the smallest** (hut-shaped single cell)
  - Nakula-Sahadeva ratha has an **apsidal** end
- **Kailasanatha, Kanchi:** oldest surviving Pallava structural temple
