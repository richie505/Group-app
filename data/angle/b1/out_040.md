## Aims, beliefs and principles of the movements | from: S2, S3, S4, S7, S8, S9
# 1. Vivekananda's ideas
- Strength, self-confidence, youth for national regeneration
- Condemned caste and empty ritual
# 2. Ramakrishna Mission - aims and work
- (1) Monastic order devoted to renunciation + practical spirituality
- (2) Lay disciples doing preaching, philanthropy, humanitarian work
- **Work:** Schools, colleges, hospitals, orphanages; famine and disaster relief
# 3. Theosophical Society - aims and beliefs
- **universal brotherhood**; comparative religion / study of ancient religions
- Karma and reincarnation (rebirth)
- Drew on the Upanishads, Samkhya, Yoga, Vedanta
- **Social stand:** Condemned child marriage; upliftment of outcastes and widows
# 4. Arya Samaj - principles
- One God; **idol worship useless**; all may read the Vedas
- Against caste by birth + **child marriage**
- For widow remarriage + women's education
# 5. Shuddhi Movement - purpose
- **reconversion** to Hinduism of those who had turned to **Islam or Christianity** + admission of outsiders
# 6. Common features of the reform movements
- Rationalism against **superstition and ritual**
- Promotion of **education**
- **women's emancipation**
- Attack on caste
- **Political role:** **cultural confidence** against the colonial "inferiority" narrative → ground for the **national movement**
  - Theosophy led straight into **Home Rule**
