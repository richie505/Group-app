=== ROW 31 | Other heterodox sects - Ajivikas (Makkhali Gosala), Charvaka/Lokayata materialism

## Heterodox teachers and founders → doctrines | from: S0, S2, S4
| **Teacher** | **Doctrine** |
|---|---|
| **Makkhali Gosala** | **Niyativada** - fatalism (Ajivika) |
| **Purana Kassapa** | **Akriyavada** - actions bring neither merit nor demerit; even killing or charity has no effect |
| **Ajita Kesakambalin** | Materialism - body of four elements dissolves at death; nothing survives [GK] |
| Pakudha Kaccayana | Seven eternal, uncreated elements; no killer or killed [GK] |
| Sanjaya Belatthiputta | Scepticism/agnosticism - refused to affirm or deny [GK] |
| Nigantha Nataputta | = Mahavira (Jain) |

- **Ajivika founder:** **Makkhali (Makkali) Gosala** = most important leader; usual exam answer for "founder" (UKPSC 2024)
  - Gosala was the third chief who popularised it
- **Charvaka founder:** Legendary founder **Brihaspati** → name **Barhaspatya**
- ⚠ **Niyativada** = Gosala (fate); **Akriyavada** = Purana Kassapa (no merit/demerit); materialism = Ajita Kesakambalin / Charvaka

## Ajivikas → doctrine and features | from: S2, S3
# 1. Doctrine
- **Core doctrine:** **Niyati (fate/determinism)**
  - All thoughts and actions fixed before birth; no free will
  - **Karma rejected** as a means to change destiny
  - All beings powerless before fate
- **Atomic theory:** Of matter; influenced later Vaisheshika
# 2. Lifestyle and social base
- **Lifestyle:** Extreme asceticism
  - Nakedness, lying on nails, walking through fire, sitting in large earthen pots
  - Organised ascetic groups; often called Sudra sannyasins
- **Social base:** **No caste discrimination**; appealed to warrior, industrial and mercantile classes
# 3. Relations with Mahavira and the Buddha
- **Gosala and Mahavira:** Gosala spent some years (about six) as Mahavira's companion before breaking away
  - Some notes call Gosala Mahavira's "guru"; Jain texts make him Mahavira's disciple
- **Buddha:** **Majjhima Nikaya** records debates with Jains and Ajivikas
> Exam angle: statement-sets - determinism **and** austerity true; "order of rebirth can be changed" false (RPSC 2024); caste discrimination false (BPSC 2024).

## Rulers → patronage, Barabar caves, survival and decline | from: S3
| **Ruler / factor** | **Ajivika link** |
|---|---|
| **Bindusara** | Popular under **Bindusara** |
| **Ashoka** | **Ashoka donated the Barabar caves** (Bihar) - among the **earliest rock-cut caves** in India; great respect in the Maurya era (UPPSC 2023) |
| **7th Pillar Edict (Delhi-Topra)** | Names the **Ajivikas** along with Brahmanas, Sangha and **Nirgranthas** |

- ⚠ **Trap:** "Ashoka was an Ajivika for most of his life" is incorrect - Ashoka was a Buddhist who also gave caves to the Ajivikas
- ⚠ **Trap:** Barabar caves made by **Ashoka**, not Chandragupta Maurya (UPSC 2013 statement is false)
- **Ajivika survival:** Nearly **2,000 years**, until the **14th century CE**, in **Karnataka and Tamil Nadu**, then disappeared
- **Causes of decline:** Puranic-Bhakti revival (e.g. under the Pallavas) + loss of royal patronage

## Charvaka/Lokayata → names, tenets, aphorism | from: S4
- **Names:** **Lokayata** (APPSC GS 2025); also **Barhaspatya**, after legendary founder **Brihaspati**
- **Complete materialism:**
  - Body formed of matter, ends in matter
  - Aim of life = **enjoy material pleasure** (kama = highest value)
- **Body-soul view:** "**The conscious body is the soul**" - no separate soul survives death (CGPSC 2024 answer: Charvaka)
- **Means of knowledge:** Only **perception (pratyaksha)**; inference + testimony (Vedas) rejected
- **Famous aphorism:** "Live happily as long as you live; borrow and drink ghee - once the body is ash, how can it return?"
> Exam angle: name-matching (Lokayata, Barhaspatya) and "which school accepts only perception".

## Texts and inscriptions → sources on the sects | from: S1, S4
| **Source** | **Type** | **What it gives** |
|---|---|---|
| **Samannaphala Sutta** | Digha Nikaya (Buddhist) | **Ajatashatru** reports the views of the six teachers to the Buddha |
| **Bhagavati Sutra** | Jain Anga | Fullest account of Gosala and his quarrel with Mahavira |
| Ashokan edicts (7th Pillar Edict) + Barabar cave inscriptions | Inscriptions | Ajivikas named / caves gifted |
| Brihaspati Sutra | Charvaka original | Lost - no surviving texts |
| **Sarvadarshanasangraha** | Madhava (opponent) | Charvaka known mainly via opponents' refutations |

## Heterodox schools compared → karma, soul, fate, Vedas | from: S5
| **School** | **Karma** | **Soul** | **Fate** | **Vedas** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jainism | Material bondage; removable by penance | Eternal jiva in all things | Effort decides | Rejected |
| Buddhism | Intention-based; accepted | **Anatta** - no permanent self | Effort decides | Rejected |
| Ajivika | Cannot alter destiny | Accepted [GK] | **Niyati** - all predetermined | Rejected |
| Charvaka | Denied | Conscious body = soul | - | Rejected |

- **Common ground:** Rejection of Vedic authority and ritualism
  - Shramana reaction of the 6th c. BCE amid urbanisation and Magadhan patronage

## Universal angles | from: S6 | universal
- **Significance:** Intellectual ferment of the 6th c. BCE + Mauryan religious pluralism
- **Comparative note:** Charvaka's perception-only view vs Nyaya's four pramanas (perception, inference, comparison, testimony)

## Political situation on the eve of invasion → causes of Alexander's success | from: S2

|---|---|

- **Nandas:** Huge army deterred Alexander's eastward march; unpopular for heavy taxation
- **Causes of success (UPPSC 2003 key "1, 2 and 3"):**
  - **No central power**
  - **Superior army**
  - Help from **traitorous Indian rulers** (not his administration)

## Alexander and Indian rulers → submitted or resisted | from: S3, S4
- **Background:** Son of **Philip of Macedonia** (born **356 BCE**); first defeated Persian king **Darius III**, then turned to India
  - Also wanted to solve the "limits of the ocean" puzzle of Greek geographers

| **Ruler / people** | **Response** |
|---|---|
| **Ambhi** (Omphis), king of **Taxila** | **First to surrender**; helped Alexander |
| **Porus** (Puru), kingdom between Jhelum and Chenab [GK] | Resisted at the Hydaspes |
| **Assakenoi** (Ashvakas), capital **Massaga** (Swat valley) [GK] | Fierce resistance; **women took up arms** after heavy losses (UPPSC 2013) |
| Abhisara (Kashmir hills) [GK] | Submitted |
| Malloi (**Malavas**) and other gana-sanghas | Fought him during the retreat |

- **Porus outcome:** Porus defeated, but Alexander, impressed by his bravery, **restored his kingdom** (later enlarged)
  - UPPSC 2013: king defeated on the Jhelum = Porus
> Exam angle: "which people's women fought Alexander" = Massaga; "first to submit" = Ambhi.

## Effects of the invasion → satrapies, contact, politics | from: S6
| **Effect** | **Detail** |
|---|---|
| **Satrapies** | Conquered area split into **three parts** under **Greek governors**; most states returned to rulers who submitted |
| **Contact** | **Direct contact** Greece-India + new land and sea routes; Greek settlements (Alexandria, Bucephala) |

## Writers → accounts of Alexander's campaign | from: S7

## Universal angles | from: S8 | universal

- **Samprati:** Ruled c. **230-215 BCE**
- **AP angle:** Megasthenes on Andhra strength; Ashoka's Yerragudi edicts; Samprati spread Jainism in Andhra

=== ROW 35 | Ashoka - accession, Kalinga War, Dhamma and its content, Dhamma Mahamatras

## Ashoka → titles, early career, accession and sources | from: S0
# 1. Titles and names
| **Title / name** | **Meaning / where found** |
|---|---|
| **Devanampiya** | Beloved of the gods |
| **Piyadassi / Piyadasi** | Of pleasing appearance/looks |
| **Name "Ashoka"** | In **Maski** (**"Buddhashakya Ashoka"**) and Gujarra minor rock edicts |
| **"Dharmasoka"** | Sarnath inscription |

# 2. Early career
- **Parents:** Son of Bindusara and **Subhadrangi (Dharma)**
- **Viceroyalties:**
  - **Taxila** - **Governor of Taxila**, sent to quell/crush a revolt (Divyavadana)
  - **Ujjain/Avanti** - **Viceroy of Ujjain**; MPPSC 2020: Ashoka won Avanti in Bindusara's time - source **Samantapasadika of Buddhaghosa**
# 3. Accession
- **Succession war:** With brother **Susima**
  - Mahavamsa: killed **99 brothers** (exaggeration)
  - RE V itself mentions/refers to his brothers' families
- **Dates:**
  - Accession c. **272 BCE**; consolidation/coronation c. **269 BCE**
  - Reign **273-232 BC** or **268-232 BCE** (UPSC) - gap = four-year succession struggle
# 4. Later Buddhist legends → text
| **Text** | **Legends** |
|---|---|
| **Ashokavadana / Divyavadana** (Sanskrit) | Upagupta as guide, Taxila campaign, cruel "Chandashoka" turned Dharmashoka |
| **Mahavamsa / Dipavamsa** (Sri Lankan Pali) | Nigrodha, 99 brothers, mission of **Mahinda**; Mahavamsa paints early Ashoka as cruel |

## Kalinga War → motive, year, edict, policy shift | from: S1
- **Motive:** **Kalinga (Odisha)** broke free in Bindusara's last years; independence threatened Mauryan unity (and the east-coast route south)
- **Date:** **c. 261 BC** (c. 261 BCE), **8th regnal year**; turning point of the reign
- **Major Rock Edict XIII:** Remorse; **1,50,000 deported, 1,00,000 killed**
- **Policy shift:** **Bherighosha** (war drum) → **Dhammaghosha**; **Dhammavijaya** replaces conquest
- **Kalinga sites:** **RE XIII absent at Dhauli and Jaugada**
  - Carry **Separate Edicts** instead - to officials at **Tosali** and **Samapa** on just treatment of the people
- **Extent:** **Hindukush to Bengal**, incl. Afghanistan and Baluchistan
  - All India except the far south (Chola, Pandya, Chera, Satyaputra, Kerala lands outside)
- ⚠ **1,50,000** = deported; **1,00,000** = killed

## Dhamma → nature, virtues, source and debate | from: S3
# 1. Nature and definition
- **Nature:** A **moral code / social ethic**, not a religion or piety
  - Aims: moral discipline, **harmony among sects**, social welfare, loyalty to the state

| **Virtues promoted** | **Vices to avoid** |
|---|---|
| Kindness, charity, truthfulness, gratitude, purity | Violence, cruelty, anger, pride, jealousy |
| Respect for elders, parents, teachers; courtesy to slaves and servants; regard for Brahmanas and Shramanas | Killing of animals, wasteful festive gatherings (samajas) [GK] |

| **Edict** | **Dhamma teaching** |
|---|---|
| **Pillar Edict II** | Defines Dhamma = **few faults, many good deeds** (mercy, charity, truthfulness, purity) |
| **RE VII and XII** | Preach respect for all sects |
| **Bhabru** | Ashoka's devotion to Buddha, Dhamma, Sangha |

# 2. Source and the Buddhism debate
- **Source text:** Definition taken from the **Rahulovada Sutta** (UPPSC 2024 key); rests on Buddhist **upasaka (lay) dhamma**
- **Debate:**
  - Ashoka personally Buddhist (Bhabru edict)
  - But Dhamma drew on ideas shared by all sects → view that it was a **state ethic, not Buddhism**
- **Central point:** **Non-violence and religious toleration together** (BPSC 2017 key: "more than one")
- **UPPSC 2023:** Line on people doing good deeds, "minds unpolluted with jealousy" = **Ashoka**

## Dhamma officials and welfare measures → edict | from: S4, S5
# 1. Officials → function
| **Official** | **Function** |
|---|---|
| **Dhamma Mahamatras** | Worked among all sects, women, prisoners and frontier peoples |
| **Pativedakas** (reporters) | Could report to the king at any time (RE VI) |
| Rajukas, pradeshikas, yuktas | Quinquennial tours (RE III) |
# 2. Welfare measures
- **RE II:**
  - **Medical treatment for men and animals**
  - Planting of **medicinal herbs**, roots and fruits
  - **Wells** and **trees along roads**
  - Also in the Chola, Chera, Pandya, Satyaputra lands and the Greek king's realm
  - UPPSC 2015: India's first hospitals and herbal gardens = **Ashoka**
- **PE V** (26th year): Ban on killing specified animals and burning forests; no branding of cattle; prisoner releases
- **PE IV:** **3-day respite** for condemned prisoners

## Universal angles | from: S6 | universal
- **AP angle:** **RE XIII names the Andhras**; Mahadeva went to Mahishamandala after the Third Council
- **Comparative note:** Hellenistic contemporaries in RE XIII - Antiochus II, Ptolemy II, Magas, Antigonus Gonatas, Alexander of Epirus
- **FIRST/LAST:** First Indian king to address his people directly through inscriptions (Prakrit)

## Kalinga War and Dhamma → as told in the edicts | from: S4
# 1. Kalinga War
- **Date:** **261 BC**, Ashoka's 8th regnal year
- **Cause:** Kalinga controlled the east-coast land and sea routes
- **RE XIII:**
  - About **1,00,000 killed**, **1,50,000 taken captive**
  - Many more dead of famine and disease
- **Result:** Remorse, conversion to Buddhism, no more wars of conquest, **Dhammavijaya** replaces military conquest
  - **Policy shift:** **Bherighosha** (war drum) → **Dhammaghosha** (call of Dhamma)
- **Separate Kalinga edicts:** At **Dhauli and Jaugada**, take the place of RE XI-XIII (RE XIII omitted there)
  - Addressed to officers of **Tosali and Samapa** against unjust imprisonment and torture; "all men are my children" [GK]
# 2. Ashoka and Dhamma
- **Reign:** **273-232 BC**; earlier governor of Taxila and Ujjain
- **Nature:** Moral code for subjects; steeped in Buddhist ethics yet not sectarian
  - Some ideas from other traditions (UPSC notes mention Zoroastrian strands)
- **Virtues:** Kindness, charity, truthfulness, gratitude, **respect for elders**, compassion to humans and animals
  - PE II: "few faults, many good deeds"
- **Vices:** Violence, cruelty, anger, pride, jealousy

# 2. Buddhism in Andhra
- **Amaravati:** Ashoka enlarged the stupa that Andhra tradition says Mahadeva first built and enshrined relics; plaque bearing his name found
  - Dharanikota sculptures older than Sanchi and Bharhut
- **RE XIII** (c. **256 BCE**): Lists the **Andhras** among peoples following Dhamma
  - Some notes read it as placing the Andhras on or beyond the Mauryan frontier
  - Same notes (p.23) link them with the Satavahanas as "Andhra-bhrityas" - read as Satavahanas being Mauryan feudatories and Buddhism in Andhra before Ashoka ('Andhra-bhrityas' itself is a Puranic term)
- **Mission:** After the Third Council, to **Mahisha Mandala**
