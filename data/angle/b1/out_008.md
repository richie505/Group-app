=== ROW 42 | Indo-Greeks - Menander and Milindapanho, Indo-Greek coinage and its significance

## Bactrian Greeks — origin, entry and places raided | from: S0, S1
- **Origin:** after Alexander, Greeks settled in **Bactria** (north Afghanistan)
- **Independence:** **Diodotus I** revolted against the **Seleucids** in the 3rd c. BCE → independent Bactrian Greek kingdom (UPSC)
- **Entry into India:**
  - Pressed by the **Scythians (Sakas)** → moved south; helped by weak successors of Ashoka
  - Raids reached **Saketa, Madhyamika**, towards **Pataliputra** (UPSC; Mahabhashya)
- **Successors in the north-west:** **Sakas** → **Parthians (Pahlavas)** → **Kushanas** (UPSC)

## Indo-Greek rulers → contributions | from: S1, S2

| **Ruler** | **Contribution / coin feature** |
|---|---|
| **Demetrius** | First Bactrian Greek to invade India (c. 180 BC) [GK]; defeated by Pushyamitra, and by Kharavela per the Hathigumpha (UPSC; APP) |
| **Menander I (Milinda)** | Greatest Indo-Greek; spread Hellenistic culture; issued coins with **Indian symbols** (APPSC key); reign **165-145 BCE**; capital **Sakala** (modern **Sialkot**) (UPSC) |
| **Antialcidas** | Sent **Heliodorus** to the Sunga court at Vidisha (CDI) |
| **Agathocles** | Coins showing **Sankarshana (Balarama) and Vasudeva (Krishna)** (UPPSC 2017 key) |
| **Eucratides** | Rival dynasty to Demetrius; issued the largest known ancient gold coin (20 staters) [GK] |
| **Strato** | Menander's son; ruled after the regency of Agathocleia [GK] |
| **Hermaius** | Last ruler of the line; his defeat ended Greek supremacy south of the Hindu Kush (UPSC says by the **Parthians**) |

> Exam angle: "Sankarshana + Vasudeva on coins" = Agathocles, not Kushana or Gupta rulers.
- ⚠ FIRST to invade = **Demetrius**; greatest = **Menander**; LAST = **Hermaius**

## Menander → Milindapanho, evidence and matches | from: S2

| **Milindapanho ("Questions of Milinda")** | Dialogue with Buddhist monk **Nagasena**, in **Pali** (UKPSC 2024; UPPSC 2023); Menander converted to Buddhism |

=== ROW 45 | Schools of art - Gandhara, Mathura and Amaravati compared

## Buddha image — FIRSTs and the origin debate | from: S0

## Gandhara and Mathura schools → region, patron, features | from: S1, S2
# 1. Gandhara
- **Region:** **North-West frontier** - Peshawar, Taxila, Swat Valley (modern Pakistan/Afghanistan)
- **Influence:** **Hellenistic (Greek), Roman and Persian**
  - Indian craftsmen + Greeks and Romans → **Indo-Greek / Greco-Buddhist art**
- **Patron dynasty:** **Kushanas** (introduced by them; flourished under Kanishka's line)
- **Period:** c. **1st-5th century CE**
- **Theme:** predominantly **Mahayana Buddhist** - Buddha with Bodhisattvas (Avalokiteshvara, Maitreya)
> Exam angle: "Indian + Greek features = ?" → Gandhara; "flourished under which dynasty?" → Kushanas.
# 2. Mathura
- **Nature:** indigenous school of the Ganga plains; **not restricted to Buddhism**
- **Earliest images:** **earliest surviving iconic images of Hindu deities** (Shiva, Vishnu, Surya) and of **Jain Tirthankaras** + Yaksha-Yakshi figures
- **Kushana royal portraits:** red sandstone - e.g. **headless standing statue of Kanishka** from Mat near Mathura
- **Gupta continuity:** continued into the Gupta period with greater elegance and softness

=== ROW 46 | Satavahanas - origin debate, Simuka, Gautamiputra Satakarni, Nasik Prasasti, Vasishthiputra Pulumavi

## Satavahana names, FIRSTs and dynastic sequence | from: S0, S3
- **Puranic names:** **Andhras / Andhrabhrityas**
- **Andhrabhritya:** = "servants of the Andhras", or Andhras who had been feudatories
- **Origin:** began as **feudatories of the Mauryas**; independent in the Deccan after Mauryan decline
- **Progenitor:** Satavahana/Shalivahana (legendary)
- **Status:** **first historical ruling dynasty of the Deccan**; one account makes them **Brahmins** by caste
- **First in Andhra:** Satavahanas = first kings to issue inscriptions **in Andhra itself** (Ashoka's were earlier)
- **Sequence:** **Mauryas → Sungas → Kanvas** (Magadha) → **Satavahanas**
  - Simuka overthrew the Kanvas
  - A Satavahana king defeated Kanva Susarma and held Magadha (Matsya/Vayu Puranas)
- **Successors:**
  - **Ikshvakus** (Andhra), **Abhiras** (Maharashtra), **Vakatakas** (Vidarbha), Chutus, **Pallavas**
  - Later Chalukyas, then Rashtrakutas

## Scholars → theories on origin | from: S0

| **View** | **Scholar** |
|---|---|

## Gautamiputra Satakarni and the Nasik Prasasti | from: S6, S7
- **Reign:** **GPS (c. 106-130 CE)**
- **Victory:** Defeated **Nahapana** (Western Kshatrapa, **Kshaharata** branch) → **regained lost territory** (G2 2000)
  - Recovered **Malwa, Saurashtra, Konkan, Nasik** (APPSC 2000 key)
- **Coin proof:** **Restruck Nahapana's coins** → **Jogalthambi hoard** (Nasik district): Nahapana's silver coins **restruck by Gautamiputra**
- **Periplus:** notes Saka-Satavahana conflict
- **Nasik Prasasti — language & form:** **Prakrit prose**, Brahmi script
- **Content:** Expeditions, victories, empire and titles
  - Praises him as "Destroyer of **Sakas, Yavanas and Pahlavas**"
- **Gifts:** With Balasri to monks on the **Trirashmi** hill; Balasri called wife of a Rajarshi
- **King's number:** Gautamiputra = **23rd** king
  - ⚠ **Trap:** "28th ruler" in some notes is an error
- **Varna:** Classes Balasri's line as **Brahmana varna**
  - Fostered Brahmin families, upheld varnashrama, **stopped varna-sankara**
- **Status debate:** "Crusher of Kshatriya pride" claim sits beside his Brahmin status → Satavahanas were Brahmins, not Kshatriyas
- **Buddhist patronage:**
  - Village near Trirashmi gifted to monks
  - 100 nivartanas of his own land donated
  - Sanchi toranas: some notes credit him, but the south torana inscription names Siri Satakarni (Satakarni I/II), not Gautamiputra
