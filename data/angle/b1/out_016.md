## Village autonomy — dynasty identification and comparison | from: S0

## Assemblies → village type and composition | from: S1
| **Assembly** | **Where** | **Composition** |

- **Nagaram:** Of the nadu's market town; worked alongside it

## Uttaramerur inscriptions → ruler, year, qualifications and election | from: S2, S3
- **Ruler:** **Two inscriptions** of **Parantaka I**, dated **919 and 921 CE**
- **Location:** Uttaramerur, **Kanchipuram district**
- **Importance:** Source for Chola village government (APPSC G2 2000, 2003); most detailed record of village administration in ancient India
  - ⚠ **Trap:** Not Kudimiamalai, Gangaikondacholapuram, Silappadikaram, Tolkappiyam or Periya Puranam
| **Qualifications** | **Disqualifications** |
|---|---|
| Own at least **1/4 veli** of land | Failure to submit accounts |
| **Own house** (residence) | Committed **sins** |
| Age **35-70** | **Stolen** property |
| Knowledge of the Vedas | Served on a committee in the **past three years** |
- **Election procedure - Kudavolai:**
  - Village divided into **30 wards (kudumbus)**
  - Eligible names written on palm leaves → put in a **pot (kudam)** → a **young boy/girl** drew the names
- **Term:** Members held office for **three-year** terms; chosen by lot or rotation
- **Bar:** Those who had served in the last three years were barred
- **Oversight:** Sabha resolutions passed **in the presence of a king-appointed official**

=== ROW 87 | Chola central and provincial administration, land classification, revenue and irrigation

## Chola kingship → succession, titles and image | from: S0
# 1. Succession
- **Yuvaraja practice:** King **named his successor (yuvaraja) in his own lifetime** (**named their successor in their own lifetime**) + made him co-regent (UPPSC 2001 - Chola)
  - e.g. **Rajendra I** co-regent in **1012**, king in **1014**
# 2. Titles and image of the king
- **Inscriptional titles:** **ko, perumal, raja-rajadhiraja, ko-konmai kondan**; also **perumal adigal** ("the great one")
- **Rajaraja I:** **"Ulakalanada Perumal" / Ulakalanda Perumal** = one who measured the earth (like Vishnu's three strides)
- **Divine status:** King supreme, compared to gods; images of kings and queens installed beside deities ("Rajaraja temple")
- **Ideal king:** Warrior, patron of arts, giver to Brahmanas, protector of **varnashrama dharma**
- **Security:** **Administrative tours**; **hereditary bodyguards** paid in land revenue
  - **Marco Polo** noted bodyguards ready to die for the king
> Exam angle: "Which dynasty's king declared his successor during his own tenure?" - **Chola**.

- **Pay:** Revenue-bearing land assignments
- **Bureaucracy:** Grew from **Rajaraja I's** reign → weakened after **Kulottunga I** as local chieftains gained power
- **Bilingual orders:** **Sanskrit preamble** (genealogy, conquests) + **Tamil** operative part
- ⚠ **Trap:** **Perundaram** = highest, **Sirudaram** = lower; **Tirumandira-olai** writes, **Olai-nayagam** countersigns

## Administrative divisions and provincial government → units | from: S2, S3
| **Level** | **Unit** |
|---|---|
| 4 (highest) - Province (8) | **Mandalam** - province (**eight** in the empire), under viceroys/governors, often princes or nobles |
| 3 - Division | **Kottam / Valanadu** - group of nadus |
| 2 - District | **Nadu** - district (assembly: nattar) |
| Village group | Kurram [GK] |
| 1 (lowest) - Village | **Ur** / sabha / nagaram - village (largely autonomous) |
- **Provinces:** Some = annexed principalities; others = vassal (samanta) territories paying tribute
| **Mandalam** | **Region** |
|---|---|
| **Cholamandalam** | Chola core - the **Kaveri delta** (Thanjavur area) [GK] |
| **Jayankondacholamandalam** | **Tondaimandalam** - the former Pallava country around Kanchi [GK] |
| **Mummudicholamandalam** | **Northern Sri Lanka**, named by **Rajaraja I** |
- **Mandalam governance:** **Viceroy/governor**, often a **royal prince** or noble; paid by land assignments
- **Vengi example:** **Kulottunga I's sons** as viceroys (**Rajapratinidhi**)
  - **Rajaraja (Mummadi Chola)** from Jagannathapuram
  - Then **Vira Chola** (twice)
  - **Rajaraja Chola Gangu** (up to Mahendragiri)
- **Samanta:** Defeated king restored on accepting overlordship + paying tribute + giving military help
- **Historiography:** **Burton Stein** → Chola state **"segmentary"**
  - Royal authority radiated from Kaveri core; peripheral local autonomy intact
  - vs K.A. Nilakanta Sastri's centralised picture

## Army and navy → terms and rulers | from: S4
- **Army:** Elephants, cavalry, spear-armed infantry
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Kaikkolar** | Royal **infantry regiments** ("strong-armed") [GK] |
| **Velaikkarar** | **Loyal bodyguard troops** sworn to die with the king [GK]; Marco Polo noted such loyalty |
| **Kadagams** | **Military cantonments**[GK] |

| **Ruler** | **Naval role** |
|---|---|
| **Rajaraja I** | Credited with building the Chola **navy** (CGPSC key); destroyed the **Chera navy at Trivandrum**; took **northern Sri Lanka** and the **Maldives** |
| **Rajendra I** | Carried it furthest - **All of Sri Lanka**; **1025 CE** expedition against **Srivijaya** → took **Kadaram (Kedah)** in Malaya + parts of Sumatra |
| **Kulottunga I** | Conquered **Kalinga** "with great naval power" (Kalingattuparani) |
- **Navy:** Strong → Maldives, Sri Lanka, Srivijaya campaigns
- **Overseas footholds:** **Sri Lanka**, **Maldives**, **Kadaram/Srivijaya**
  - Navy made the **Bay of Bengal a "Chola Lake"** (K.A. Nilakanta Sastri)
> Exam angle: "Which South Indian state was famous for naval power?" - **Chola**; "Which king started the navy?" - **Rajaraja I**.

## Land survey, revenue and tax terms → meaning | from: S5
# 1. Survey and assessment
- **Land survey:** **Rajaraja I** → detailed survey and assessment + revenue reorganisation → earned **"Ulakalanda Perumal"**
  - **Periodic land surveys** repeated; recorded holdings and transfers
- **Main income:** **Land revenue**, normally **1/6th of produce**
  - Paid in **cash, kind or both**; collected through **village assemblies**
- **Revenue department** = **Puravu-varitinaikkalam / Shrikaranam**
# 2. Taxes and dues
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Kadamai** | **Land revenue collected in kind** |
| **Antarayam** | Rural tax in **cash** |
| **Eccoru** | Obligation / duty to feed visiting state officials |
| **Vetti** (also muttaiyal, kudimai) | **Forced labour** owed by villagers to the state |
| **Nettal** | Compulsory labour |
| **Ayam** | **Royal tax / dues**[GK] |
| **Irai** | **Tax / levy** on land [GK] |
| **Kasu** | Gold coin |
- ⚠ **Trap:** **Kadamai** = in kind; **Antarayam** = in cash; **Vetti** / **Nettal** = labour, not money
# 3. Remission
- **Tax exemptions / remission:** Land exemptions handled and granted through the **ur** (village assembly)
- **Kulottunga I:** Abolished tolls → **Sungam Tavirtta Chola** (abolisher of tolls)

## Land classification and land rights → terms | from: S6
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Eripatti** | Land whose revenue was set apart to maintain the **village tank** (UPSC 2016 - correct pair) |
| **Vellanvagai** | Land of non-Brahmin peasant proprietors; non-brahmadeya villages governed by the **ur** |
| **Brahmadeya** | **Rent-free** land/villages granted to **Brahmins**; agrahara villages with a **sabha** |
| **Devadana** | Land gifted to temples [GK] |
| Salabhoga / **Shalabhoga** | Land for the maintenance of a **school**[GK] |
| Pallichchandam | Land donated to Jaina institutions [GK] |
| **Taniyur** | Large village (often brahmadeya) made a **separate administrative unit** - not "a village donated to a single Brahmin" [GK] |
| **Ghatikas** | Colleges attached to temples |
| **Kani** | Possession with rights and duties |
| **Karanmai** | Right to cultivate |
| **Mitachi** | Superior possessory/possessive right |
| Kaniyudaiyar | Land-owning farmers |
| Ulukudi | Tenants |
- **UPSC 2016 pair-matching:**
  - Correct: pair 1 **Eripatti** (tank land) + pair 3 **Ghatikas** (colleges generally attached to temples)
  - ⚠ **Trap:** Taniyur pair ("villages donated to a single Brahmin") is WRONG
> Exam angle: UPSC 2016 pair-matching - Eripatti (tank land) and Ghatikas (colleges) correct; Taniyurs described as "villages donated to a single Brahmin" is wrong.

## Irrigation and the temple → functions | from: S7, S8
# 1. Irrigation
- **Tanks (eri):** Every village tank run by the **eri variyam** (tank committee) → distributed water to fields
- **Upkeep:** **Eripatti** land financed repairs/maintenance
- **Cauvery delta channels:** Built on Sangam-era **Grand Anicut (Kallanai)** on the Kaveri by Sangam Chola **Karikala**; kept up by later Cholas
# 2. Temple as administrative-economic centre
- **Grants:** Large **devadana** lands
- **Royal direction:** **Rajaraja I directed the Sabhas of Cholamandalam** to perform services for the **Brihadeeswara** temple (Thanjavur inscriptions)
- **Roles:** Biggest landholder, employer and lender of the locality; venue for village assembly meetings

## Inscriptions and copper plates → ruler and content | from: S9
- **Chola inscriptions:** Most voluminous and detailed in South India; **Tamil** = language of administration
- **Records:** Royal land grants recorded on **copper plates**
| **Record** | **Point** |
|---|---|
| **Anbil plates** | Sanskrit genealogy, cited with the Leiden grant as royal prashasti; issued under **Sundara Chola**[GK] |
| **Tiruvalangadu plates** | Copper plates of **Rajendra I** giving Chola genealogy and conquests [GK] |
| **Larger Leiden (Anaimangalam) plates** | Ordered by **Rajaraja I**, engraved under **Rajendra I**; grant of **Anaimangalam** village (near **Nagapattinam**) to the **Chulamanivarma-vihara** built by Srivijaya king **Sri Mara Vijayotunga Varman** |
- **Leiden plates:**
  - **21 large + 3 small** sheets
  - Ring seal: **Chola tiger** + **Pandya fish** + **Chera bow**
  - Languages: **Sanskrit** (genealogy) + **Tamil** (grant)
- **Smaller plates (3 sheets) = Kulottunga I's:** Extended grant after two Javanese envoys appealed
  - **4,500 kalam** of paddy plus land, on top of Rajaraja I's **8,943 kalam** (granted by Rajaraja I in the Leiden plates)
  - Grant to the Chudamani Vihara at Nagapattinam (Srivijaya king, Chola grants)
