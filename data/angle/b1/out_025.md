=== ROW 123 | Vijayanagara art and architecture - Hampi, gopurams, kalyana mandapa, Vitthala temple, painting

## Vijayanagara style — features, terms and scholars | from: S0, S2
- **Percy Brown:** Vijayanagara architecture = "matured state of the Dravidian tradition"
  - Blends **Chola, Hoysala, Pandya and Chalukya** styles
  - **Models:** **Chalukya and Chola**

| **Feature** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Raya gopuram** | Towering gateway that dwarfs the central shrine; set in massive enclosures |
| **Kalyana mandapa** | Large pillared hall for the divine marriage |
| Amman shrine | Separate shrine for the goddess (e.g. Hazara Rama) |
| Composite pillars | **Rearing yalis** and charging horses with riders, 7-8 ft tall |
| **Musical pillars** | **Vittala** Ranga mandapa - **56 pillars** that give notes when tapped |
| **Thousand-pillared halls (Sahasra Stambha Mandapa)** | Halls with elaborately carved composite pillars |
| **Hoysala-type pillar (Ekapaadam)** | Single pillar carved into 3-4 sections on one base |

- **Other distinctive additions:** Pillared corridors added to older temples
- **New feature:** **Secular buildings inside temple premises**
- **Spread in Andhra:** Through courts at Kondavidu, Udayagiri, Chandragiri, Vinukonda

## Lepakshi — location, builders and features | from: S3
- **Temple:** **Veerabhadra Swamy (Papanaseswara / Papanaseshwara) temple**, Lepakshi village
  - **Sri Sathya Sai district** (formerly Anantapur); near **Hindupur** (2026)
  - Stands on a hill called **Kurma Shailam**
- **Builders:** Nayaka brothers **Virupanna and Veeranna** of Penukonda (Penugonda Virupanna, a governor/palegar)
  - c. **1530s** = Achyutaraya's reign (the c. 1530s date of the murals falls in Achyutaraya's reign [GK]); some notes (CDX) say "under Sri Krishnadevaraya"
- **Shrines:** **Shiva, Veerabhadra and Vishnu**, each with a mukha mandapa
- **Hanging pillar** + richly carved ceilings
- **Monolithic Nandi:** The famous "large stone bull of the Vijayanagara period" (APPSC 2017); some notes call it the largest monolithic Nandi in India
- **Naga linga:** Linga sheltered by a seven-hooded serpent, carved from a single boulder
> Exam angle: "stone bull / hanging pillar / frescoes" → Lepakshi.

## Painting — centres, themes, technique and source | from: S4
- **Lepakshi murals:** Most celebrated Vijayanagara frescoes in Andhra; peak of the **Vijayanagara school of painting**
  - APPSC 2019: Lepakshi famous for **"pictorial inscriptions"** in Vijayanagara style
  - Ceilings: Ramayana and Mahabharata
  - Walls: Shiva stories (Shivapurana) → **Parvati Parinayamu** (divine wedding of Shiva-Parvati), Tripurasura Samharam, Shiva Tandavam, Bhikshatana, Dakshinamurti

| **Centre** | **Murals** |
|---|---|
| **Hampi Virupaksha** | **Dashavatara**, **Girijakalyana** (Girija-kalyana) |
| Hazara Rama | — |
| **Kanchi** | Kamakshi and Varadaraja temples |

- **Technique:** **Mineral pigments** (ochre, green, black, white) on lime plaster; bold dark outlines, flat colours, elongated figures, energetic movement
- **Standard survey / key source:** Stella Kramrisch, **A Survey of Painting in the Deccan**

## Harikatha and Burrakatha → form, region, instruments and use | from: S1, S2
# 1. Harikatha
- **Form:** Also Harikathalu; a **single artist** sings, acts, dances (about 3 hours), with cymbals (**Chidatalu**)
  - Fused music, story-telling, dance, philosophy and literature into one performance
- **Roots:** Harikatha deeply rooted in **Vizianagaram** (Jun 2025)
# 2. Burrakatha
- **Home:** Birthplace **Guntur**; folk form of **Andhra Pradesh** (APPSC-GS 2019 key) and Telangana
- **Roles:** Group of **three** - main narrator (stands in middle, plays the **burra**) + **two Vantalu** (respond and comment)
  - Evokes heroic and tragic rasas
- **Instruments / format:** **Gummata, Burra, Tambura, Ukki**; chorus "Thandana Taana"
- **Political use:** Narrators carried news, reform and nationalist messages to unlettered villages
- **Praja Natya Mandali:** Folk plays for socialist/communist ideals
- **Historical ballads:** Draw on wars like the **Bobbili War (1757)**
## Tolu Bommalata, Kathas and Bhagavata Mela → performers, region and format | from: S3, S4, S5
# 1. Tolu Bommalata
- **Material:** Thin, translucent **leather** figures painted on both sides
  - Moved behind a **white screen** lit by an **oil lamp**; puppeteer sings verses + dialogue from behind
- **Unique feature:** Moving the **heads** of the puppets
- **Themes:** **Ramayana, Mahabharata, Puranas**, with classical music
- **Shadow puppetry elsewhere:** Odisha, Kerala, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu
- **Surabhi:** Venue: wedding in **Sorugu village near Jammalamadugu (Kadapa)** → Sorugu came to be called **Surabhi**
  - Family theatre ~**140 years** old
# 2. Other narrative forms
| **Form** | **Performers** | **Instruments / format** |
|---|---|---|
| Bhagavatul u | Mostly **Yadavas** (Krishna Leelas); famous in Rayalaseema | Large bells (Jeganta), ankle bells, cymbals; dance round a pillar with Sita-Rama-Lakshmana idols |
| Asadi Katha | **Asadi** sub-caste of Madigas (story of Ellamma) | Javiki, Chedika |
| Pichukuntla Katha | **Pichukuntla** community, troupe of 3 | Sings Srinatha's **Palnati Veeracharitra** for 15 nights; turban, sword, shield |
| **Oggu Katha** | Golla/Kuruma (shepherd) community; ballad form of **Telangana**; voiced rural life and struggle | Small **oggu** drum |
| **Chindu Bhagavatam** | **Chindu** (Madiga sub-group) performers of Telangana | Dance-drama |
| **Veeranatyam** | **Veeramushti** community | Shaiva ritual "dance of Veerabhadra" in Shiva temples |

# 3. Yakshaganam and Kuchipudi
- **Yakshaganam of Andhra:** Performed in Telugu; elaborate costumes + epic/puranic stories (Jun 2025)
- **Kuchipudi — name:** From **Kuchipudi village, Krishna district**
- **Origin:** Grew out of **Bhagavata Mela** dance-drama of hereditary **Brahmin families** → village-theatre origin for a classical form
> Exam angle: Kuchipudi <- Bhagavata Mela <- Siddhendra Yogi; Tana Shah grant.
- ⚠ **Oggu** = shepherds (Telangana); **Asadi** = Madigas; **Bhagavatulu** = Yadavas
