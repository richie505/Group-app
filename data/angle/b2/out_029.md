## Civil society and pressure groups — definitions, classification and status | from: S0, S5, S6
# 1. Civil society
- **Civil society:** Pressure groups + trade unions + NGOs
- **"Third sector":** Alongside State (first) + market (second)
- **World Bank definition:** NGOs, unions, faith-based groups, professional associations, foundations
- **Numbers:** Estimated **1.5-3.3 million** NGOs in India
- **Status:** CSOs = **development partners and democratic watchdogs**
- **APPSC 2019 key:** NGOs as **"observationalists in development"**
  - ⚠ **Trap:** Standard texts call them **partners** of government
# 2. Pressure groups
- **Definition:** Organised group influencing public policy without contesting elections or seeking office
- **Represents:** Interests of a **particular section** (farmers, industrial workers); presses government, legislators, bureaucracy for favourable laws
- **PYQ:** **Trade union** members = pressure group; MPs, ministers, panchayat members are not (MPPSC 2017)
- **Almond and Powell classification:** Institutional, associational, non-associational, anomic
# 3. Pressure groups vs political parties

|  | **Political party** | **Pressure group** |
|---|---|---|
| Aim | **Capture power** and govern | **Influence** those in power |
| Elections | Contests; answerable to voters | Does not contest; answerable to members |
| Scope | Whole range of policy | One section's interest |

- **Test:** Does **not seek office** + lobbies on a **specific interest** → pressure group

## FCRA — Act timeline, amendments, data and 2026 changes | from: S2
# 1. Timeline and administration

| **Act / change** | **Year** | **Provision** |
|---|---|---|
| FCRA first enacted | **1976** | - |
| Replaced by **FCRA 2010** | 2010 | - |
| **2016 amendment (retrospective)** | 2016 | Indian subsidiaries of foreign firms no longer "foreign sources"; followed **Delhi HC 2014** Vedanta-donations ruling against BJP and Congress (UPSC) |
| Amended | **2020** | Designated account; admin cap; sub-granting ban |

- **Administration:** FCRA = **MHA** vs **FEMA 1999** = RBI + ED
# 2. 2020 amendment
- **Designated account:** All foreign contributions first into **FCRA account at SBI, New Delhi**
- **Admin expenses cap:** **20%** (earlier 50%)
- **Sub-granting:** To other NGOs **banned**
# 3. Registration, cancellation and data
- **Validity:** **5 years**
- **Data:**
  - **18,000+** NGOs' registration cancelled since 2015
  - **14,538** FCRA NGOs active as on **15 Apr 2026**
  - 2024-25: **16,200** associations received **₹22,963 crore**
- **Grounds for cancellation:** Breach of Act/rules/registration conditions + non-functioning
  - Many administrative, e.g. returns not filed, renewal not sought on time
- **PYQ:** **Public Health Foundation of India** placed under FCRA prior permission (2017) for **lobbying with parliamentarians** (APPSC 2017)
# 4. 2026 changes
- **FCRA Amendment Bill, 2026:** Introduced in Lok Sabha **25 March 2026**
  - Authority to take over assets of NGOs with cancelled/suspended registration
  - Passage deferred after protests
- **FCRA Amendment Rules, 2026:** Notified **22 June 2026**
  - Registration under **five categories**: social, economic, educational, cultural, religious
  - Separate fees per category + per State/UT of work
  - Disclose websites + social-media handles; no "political content"
  - Ineligible: NGO with foreign citizens (other than PIOs) as key functionaries
  - Renewal: at least **₹10 lakh** foreign funds spent over two years
  - 75% of an instalment used before next is sought
  - No foreign funding for proselytisation
- ⚠ **Bill** = **25 March 2026** (Lok Sabha, deferred); **Rules** = **22 June 2026** (notified)

## Globalisation — meaning and cultural-change terms → meaning | from: S0
- **Globalisation:** Free flow of goods, services, capital, technology, ideas, people

| **Term** | **Meaning **[GK] |
|---|---|
| Diffusion | Spread of cultural traits from one society to another |
| Acculturation | Two cultures in contact exchange traits; both change, but each stays distinct |
| Assimilation | A minority culture is absorbed into the dominant one and loses its distinct identity |
| Glocalisation | Global products and ideas adapted to local tastes, e.g. local menus of global food chains |
| Homogenisation | Cultures converge towards one global (often Western) pattern |
| Hybridisation | Global and local elements mix to form new, blended forms |
| McDonaldisation | Principles of the fast-food restaurant (efficiency, calculability, predictability, control) spreading to society |

- ⚠ **Acculturation** = both change, stay distinct; **Assimilation** = minority loses identity

=== ROW 124 | Human rights - concept, generations of rights, UDHR, ICCPR and ICESCR; UN human rights machinery

## Human rights — concept and Karel Vasak's three generations | from: S0, S1
# 1. Concept
- **Meaning:** Inherent, inalienable rights held simply because one is human
- **Basis:** Rest on **human dignity**; **universal** across cultures
- **Indivisible and interdependent:** Civil-political + socio-economic rights stand together (affirmed by **Vienna Declaration, 1993**)
- **FR vs human right:** Every Fundamental Right is a human right, but not every human right is an FR
  - Right to vote = only a **statutory right** (**Sec 62, RP Act**)
# 2. Generations
- **Proponent:** **Karel Vasak (1977)** → three generations mapped to liberty, equality and fraternity

| **Generation** | **Rights** |
|---|---|
| First (liberty) | Civil and political rights: life, speech, fair trial, vote |
| Second (equality) | Economic, social and cultural rights: work, education, health, **eradication of social discrimination **(JPSC 2016) |
| Third (fraternity/solidarity) | Collective rights: **healthy environment, development, peace** (UPSC) |

- **Overlap:** Generations overlap, e.g. education is both a second- and a third-generation right (UPSC)
- ⚠ **1st – 2nd – 3rd** = **liberty – equality – fraternity**

## UDHR, 1948 — adoption, features, rights included and not included | from: S2
# 1. Adoption and structure
- **Adopted:** By the **UNGA** on **10 December 1948** (JPSC 2021)
- **Human Rights Day:** **10 December**
- **Drafting chair:** **Eleanor Roosevelt**

## Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993 — timeline, objective, definitions | from: S3
