## Acts → provisions: MGNREGA 2005 vs VB-G RAM G | from: S2
| **Feature** | **MGNREGA 2005** | **VB-G RAM G (replaces it)** |
|---|---|---|
| Guarantee | **100 days** per household per year, unskilled manual work (IYB) | **125 days** (CDI) |
| Status | **Repealed** | Presidential assent **20 Dec 2025**; notified **11 May 2026**; in force **1 July 2026** (CDI) |
| Implementing body | Gram Panchayats [GK] | **Panchayati Raj Institutions** (CDI) |

  - Rules still pending as of **Mar 2026** (9 Mar 2026)
