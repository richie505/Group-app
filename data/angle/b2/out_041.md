## EPF and ESI → coverage, wage ceiling, contribution rates | from: S10
# 1. EPF
- **Contribution:** **12%** of wages from employee **and** employer (**10%** for notified establishments) (4 Jul 2026)
- **Coverage:** Establishments with **20+** employees
- **EPF Scheme, 2026:** Replaced **EPF Scheme 1952** under Code on Social Security
  - Members carry over automatically
  - Voluntary contributions above ceiling allowed
  - Principal employer liable for contract workers' PF
  - Aadhaar + PAN + UAN linking mandatory
- **Wage ceiling:** Raised from **₹15,000 to ₹25,000 a month** (Cabinet, 17 Sep 2026)
  - ₹15,000 ceiling dated from **Sep 2014**
  - Expected to add **51 lakh+** employees
- **Benefits:** PF + **EPS** pension + **EDLI** insurance
# 2. ESI
- **ESI (ESI Act 1948):** Ceiling **₹21,000** a month
  - Employer **3.25%**, employee **0.75%**
  - Benefits: medical, sickness, maternity, disablement, dependants'
- **Under SS Code:** ESIC coverage **pan-India**
- **SPREE (Jul 2025-Jan 2026):** Added **1.03 crore** employees (17 Jan 2026)
- ⚠ EPF ceiling **₹25,000** (was ₹15,000); ESI ceiling **₹21,000**

## Code on Social Security 2020 → gig workers, e-Shram | from: S11
# 1. The Code
- **Merger:** **9 laws** (EPF, ESI, Maternity Benefit, Gratuity, Unorganised Workers' Social Security Act, etc.)
- **In force:** **Four Labour Codes** from **21 Nov 2025**, with **2026** Central Rules
- **Gig & platform workers:** First legal recognition of **gig workers, platform workers and aggregators**
  - Aggregators pay **1-2% of annual turnover** (capped at **5%** of payouts) into social security fund
  - **National Social Security Board** recommends schemes (Jul 2025)
- **Maternity & creche:** Maternity leave stays **26** weeks; creche required at **50+** employees
# 2. e-Shram
- **Launch:** **26 Aug 2021**, MoLE
- **Database:** Aadhaar-verified National Database of Unorganised Workers; **12-digit UAN**
- **Eligibility:** Age **16-59**; excludes income-tax payers + EPFO/ESIC members
- **One-Stop Solution (2024):** Joins up **15 schemes**
- **Registrations:** **31.87 crore** (26 Aug and 16 Aug 2026)
