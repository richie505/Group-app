## Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961 → sections and penalties | from: S1
# 1. Commencement and definition
- **In force:** **1 July 1961** (APPSC 2025)
- **Dowry s.2** (Section 2): Property or valuable security given or agreed to be given, directly or indirectly, **before, at or any time after marriage** in connection with it
  - Excludes **dower/mahr** (Muslim personal law)
- **Amendments:** **Amendments of 1984 and 1986**
  - 1986 brought in **dowry death (Sec. 304B IPC)** + presumption in **Sec. 113B** of the Evidence Act
  - **Presumption of dowry death** now in **BSA s.118** (Evidence Act **113B**)
- **Cruelty:** **Cruelty s.85/86** (BNS; IPC **498A**)
# 2. Section → offence / penalty
- **Scope:** Penalises **giving, taking or abetting** dowry (2026)

| **Section** | **Offence / provision** |
|---|---|
| s.3 | Giving, taking or abetting dowry: imprisonment of **not less than 5 years** + fine of **not less than ₹15,000** (Rs 15,000) or the value of the dowry, whichever is more [GK] |
| s.4 | **Demanding** dowry: **6 months to 2 years** + fine up to Rs 10,000 [GK] |
| s.8A | **Burden of proof on the accused**[GK] |
| s.8B | **Dowry Prohibition Officers** appointed by the State Govt |

- ⚠ **Trap:** Some notes say "up to five years" → the Act fixes five years as the **minimum**
- **Nature:** **Cognisable, non-bailable and non-compoundable**
- **Cognisance:** Courts may act on their own knowledge or on complaints by recognised welfare bodies
> Exam angle: APPSC-G2 2025 - the Act came into force on **1 July 1961**.

## POSH Act, 2013 → committees and time limits | from: S3
# 1. Scope
- **Addresses:** **Sexual harassment of women at the workplace** (APPSC 2018)
- **Definition:** Unwelcome physical contact/advances, demands for sexual favours, sexually coloured remarks, showing pornography
- **In force:** Dec 2013
# 2. Committee → where → composition

| **Committee** | **Where** | **Composition / key rule** |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal Committee (IC/ICC)** | Every employer/workplace with **10 or more employees** | Senior woman employee as **Presiding Officer**; 2+ employees; **1 external NGO member**; half the members women |
| **Local Committee (LC/LCC)** | **District level**; set up by the **District Officer** | For workplaces with fewer than 10 employees, or complaints against the employer |

- ⚠ **IC** = workplace with **10 or more**; **LC** = **District Officer**, fewer than 10
# 3. Time limits → days

| **Step** | **Time limit** |
|---|---|
| Complaint | Within **3 months** (extendable by 3) |
| Inquiry | Within **90 days** |
| Employer acts | Within **60 days** |

# 4. Employer duties
- Set up the IC + display the policy + run awareness programmes + act on reports
  - Penalty for not doing so: up to **₹50,000**
> Exam angle: APPSC-GS 2018 - the "Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal Act 2013" deals with **sexual harassment of women at the workplace**.

## Criminal Law Amendments 2013 and 2018, BNS 2023 → provisions | from: S7, S8, S9
# 1. Criminal Law (Amendment) Act, 2013 ("Nirbhaya Act")
- **Scope:** Amended IPC, CrPC and Evidence Act

| **New offence (IPC 2013)** | **Now BNS** [GK] |
|---|---|
| **Acid attack** **326A** (min 10 yrs-life); attempt **326B** (5-7 yrs) | 124(1), 124(2) |
| Sexual harassment **354A**; disrobing 354B | 75, 76 |

- **Context:** **Kathua and Unnao** cases (2018) → led to FTSC scheme

| **Victim** | **Punishment** |
|---|---|
| Rape of a girl **under 16** | minimum **20 years** |
| Rape of a girl **under 12** | 20 years to life or **death** |
| **Gang rape of a girl under 12** | life or **death** |

- **Timelines:** Investigation **2 months** + trial **2 months** + appeals **6 months**
- **Bail:** **No anticipatory bail** for rape of a girl under 16
- ⚠ Minimum for rape: **7 years** (2013) → **10 years** (2018)

# 1. AP Disha Bill
- **Passed:** **AP Disha Bill** by AP Assembly in **December 2019**, after the 2019 Hyderabad veterinarian rape-murder (APPSC 2018)
- **Punishment:** **Death sentence** for rape and gang rape (death penalty in serious cases)
- **Timeline:** Verdict within **21 days** = investigation **7 days** + trial **14 days**
- **Provisions:**
  - **Special courts mandatory in every district**
  - Forensic evidence mandatory
  - Penalties for cyber-stalking and deepfake misuse
- **Status:** Needs President's assent (criminal law in Concurrent List); not in force as passed

# 2. Initiative → purpose

| **Initiative** | **Purpose** |
|---|---|
| **Disha App** | **SOS app** for women in distress or danger; instant SOS alerts |
| **Disha police stations** | **18** dedicated women's police stations set up by the previous government (TH, 1 Jan 2026) |
| **Abhayam** | Chittoor police awareness campaign for the safety of women and children (Dec **2019**) |
| **Abhay** pilot | Prakasam (Ongole) police: free pick-up and drop for women stranded between 9 pm and 5 am (Dec 2019) |
| **Mahila Samrakshana Karyadarsi** | Women-protection functionaries in village/ward secretariats [GK] |

- ⚠ **Abhayam** = Chittoor awareness campaign; **Abhay** = Ongole pick-up scheme
> Exam angle: APPSC-G1 2022 - the SOS app is **Disha**. APPSC-GS 2018 - Chittoor's campaign is **Abhayam**; the Ongole pick-up scheme is **Abhay**.

# 2. Newer measures
- **Trend:** Crimes against women **fell 4.4%** — a further **4.4% decline** (2026), credited to:
  - **Shakti Teams** (APPCA, Mar 2026)
  - Women's Safety Week rallies
  - **Sexual Offender Sheets (SOS)** of repeat offenders
- **Trishul** committees: State and district panels for "zero drugs, zero tolerance to atrocities against women, zero road accidents" (TH, 12 Sep 2026)

## Universal angles | from: S16 | universal
- **Current relevance:**
  - **NCRB Crime in India 2024:** **6%** fall in total crime; crime rate **418.9** per lakh (lowest since 2019); cybercrime rose 17.9%
