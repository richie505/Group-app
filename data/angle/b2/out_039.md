## UN Charter → dates, Articles and number game | from: S0, S2, S3, S5
- **Signing:** **50 nations at San Francisco, 26 June 1945**
- **In force:** **24 October 1945** (UN Day); **81st anniversary** in 2026
- **Six official languages:** Arabic, Chinese, English, French, Russian, Spanish
- **Members:** **193** member states

| **Article** | **Provision** |
|---|---|
| **Art. 1** | Four purposes - peace and security, friendly relations, cooperation, harmonising action |
| **Art. 2** | Sovereign equality |
| Art. 18 | UNGA "important questions" = **two-thirds** majority |
| **Art. 23** | No immediate re-election of non-permanent UNSC members |
| **Article 27** | Veto of the P5 |

- **Charter amendment:** Needs **two-thirds** of members, incl. all P5
- ⚠ Art. **18** = two-thirds in UNGA; Art. **23** = no re-election; Art. **27** = veto

## Principal organs → composition and mandate | from: S1, S2, S3, S7
| **Organ** | **Composition / mandate** |
|---|---|
| General Assembly | All members, one vote each |
| Security Council | **15** members; the only body whose decisions **bind** members |
| ECOSOC | **54** members, 3-year terms [GK]; economic, social, SDG coordination |
| Trusteeship Council | Inactive since **1994** (Palau) [GK] |
| ICJ | Principal judicial organ; The Hague [GK] |
| Secretariat | Headed by the Secretary-General |

# 1. General Assembly
- **Voting:** One member, one vote; "important questions" = **two-thirds** majority (Art. 18)
- **Non-member observer states:** **Holy See, Palestine**
- **Credentials Committee:** 9 members appointed each session
- **Presidency** rotates among 5 regional groups; President **does not vote**
# 2. Security Council
- **P5:** China, France, Russia, UK, USA - veto under **Article 27**
- **Non-permanent:** **10** members
  - Elected by UNGA by **two-thirds** secret ballot for **2 years**
  - No immediate re-election (**Art. 23**)
# 3. ICJ
- **Composition:** **15 judges**, **9-year** terms; elected by **UNGA and UNSC** voting separately
- ⚠ **15** = UNSC members and ICJ judges; **54** = ECOSOC
# 4. ECOSOC functional commissions
- **Commission on the Status of Women (1946):** CSW-70 held 2026
- **Commission on Sustainable Development:** Reviewed **Agenda 21** + Rio Declaration (APPSC 2018); replaced by High-Level Political Forum in **2013**
