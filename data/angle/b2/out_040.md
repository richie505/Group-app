=== ROW 157 | Bretton Woods institutions - IMF (quota, SDR), World Bank Group; ADB, AIIB, NDB

## Founding timeline and FIRSTs — Bretton Woods to NDB | from: S0, S3, S4, S5
- **Conference:** UN Monetary and Financial Conference, **Bretton Woods, New Hampshire, USA, July 1944**

| **Event** | **Date** |
|---|---|
| IMF and IBRD (World Bank) founded | **1944** |
| IMF formal existence (29 members) | **27 December 1945** |
| IMF began financial operations | **1 March 1947** |
| First IMF borrower | France, 8 May 1947 |
| SDR created ("paper gold") | 1967-69 |
| ADB established, at UN-ESCAP's initiative | **1966** |
| AIIB Articles signed | 2015 |
| AIIB operations | **January 2016** |
| NDB idea | **4th BRICS Summit, New Delhi (2012)** |
| NDB agreement | **6th Summit, Fortaleza, Brazil (2014)** (Fortaleza Declaration) |
| NDB operations began | **2015** |

- **Created at Bretton Woods:** IMF + IBRD
- **Not created:** Proposed **International Trade Organization (ITO)** → GATT (1947) filled the gap until WTO (1995)
- **System:** Fixed exchange rates; currencies pegged to dollar, dollar pegged to gold
  - Later Smithsonian Agreement (1971) widened band to 2.5%; lasted only 14 months
- **India FIRSTs:** **Founding member** of IMF + IBRD; **founding member** of ADB
> Exam angle: 1944 (WB founding), 27 Dec 1945 (IMF established) and 1 March 1947 (IMF operations) are all asked.
- ⚠ **1944** = founded; **27 Dec 1945** = formal existence; **1 March 1947** = operations

=== ROW 158 | World Trade Organization - structure, agreements (AoA, TRIPS, TRIMS, GATS), dispute settlement, India's issues

## WTO — establishment, predecessor and FIRSTs | from: S0, S1
- **Established:** **1 January 1995** under **Marrakesh Agreement**, after **Uruguay Round**
- **Predecessor:** Replaced **GATT (1947/48)** system; GATT survives as integral WTO agreement
- **GATT rounds:** **8 rounds**
- **HQ:** **Geneva**
- **Decision-making:** **One-country-one-vote**, consensus-based
- **Membership:** **166** (Mar 2026) (older notes say 164)
  - Latest members: **Comoros and Timor-Leste (2024)**
- **Wider scope:** Beyond goods to **services (GATS)** + **intellectual property (TRIPS)** + rules-based dispute mechanism
- **FTAs:** Allowed as MFN exceptions under **GATT Article XXIV** if they cover "substantially all trade"
- ⚠ **Marrakesh** = WTO agreement; **Uruguay** = round that created it; **Geneva** = HQ

## Nuclear treaties → year → India's position | from: S0, S3

- **FMCT conditions:** Universal, verifiable, non-discriminatory, not cap existing stocks
