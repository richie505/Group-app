=== ROW 94 | Impact of LPG on the role of the State - from provider to regulator and enabler

## Role of the State — the 1991 shift, models and meaning | from: S0, S5
# 1. The 1991 shift
- **Trigger:** **1991** crisis + LPG reforms (PM **P.V. Narasimha Rao**, FM **Dr. Manmohan Singh**)
- **Direction:** "state-controlled" → "market-oriented" economy
- **From Government to Governance:** State moved from direct provider/controller of all services → **facilitator, regulator and catalyst** for private + civil-society participation
# 2. Models of the State
| **Model** | **Role of the State** | **Indian marker** |
|---|---|---|
| Welfare / provider state | Owns and runs production; "commanding heights" | Industrial Policy **1956**: the size of the public sector measured how "socialised" the economy was |
| Regulatory state | Sets rules; independent regulators police markets | SEBI (**1992**), TRAI, CERC, IRDA |
| Facilitator / enabler state | Creates conditions for private and civil-society delivery | PPPs, DBT, GeM, UMANG; private sector becomes the "engine of growth" |

# 3. Sector → role shift
| **Sector** | **Shift** |
|---|---|
| Financial sector | **RBI** shifted from **regulator to facilitator**; banks decide without consulting the RBI → entry of private banks (Indian + foreign) |
| Housing | State moved from direct provider → **enabler of private real estate** (TH, 22 Apr 2026) |

# 4. Minimum Government, Maximum Governance
- **Meaning:** leaner State that does less itself but governs better via rules, technology, accountability
- **Origin:** slogan dates from **2014**
- **Instruments:**
  - **RTI Act 2005:** ended colonial culture of official secrecy
  - **E-governance:** reduces discretion + middlemen → **Aadhaar, DBT, GeM, UMANG**
  - **Capacity building and right-sizing** of the bureaucracy
- ⚠ **Trap:** **SEBI** (**1992**) = regulatory state; **PPPs, DBT, GeM, UMANG** = facilitator / enabler state
> Exam angle: match the role (provider/regulator/enabler) to the reform or institution.

## Terms → meaning (market failure to embedded autonomy) | from: S1, S2, S6
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Market failure** | Public goods, externalities, monopoly, information asymmetry → justified the planning-era State |
| **Government failure** | Justified 1991: PSU problems = low efficiency + profitability, mounting losses, political interference, no autonomy, labour problems, project delays |
| **Public choice theory** | **James Buchanan**; intellectual basis of the government-failure case |
| Washington Consensus | Term for the IMF + World Bank + US Treasury package (**John Williamson**, **1989**) |
| Flailing state | The "head" (elite institutions) works but is not connected to its "limbs" (field delivery) |
| Developmental state | A strong, autonomous bureaucracy steers industrialisation (East Asia) |
| Embedded autonomy | A bureaucracy insulated from capture yet tied to business |
| **Hybrid equilibrium** | Economic Survey 2025-26: Indian corporates are neither East Asian "developmental partners" nor purely Western "rules-bound" firms → political mediation substitutes for market discipline |
| **Capable State** | Economic Survey 2025-26: reduces uncertainty → economic coordination scales beyond kinship and caste networks (e.g. auto, pharma sectors) |

=== ROW 97 | Public-private partnerships - models (BOT, BOOT, HAM), issues; disinvestment and strategic sale policy

## PPP models → expansion and risk allocation | from: S0
| **Model** | **Expansion** | **Who finances / bears the risk** |
|---|---|---|
| BOT (Toll) | Build-Operate-Transfer | Private player finances, builds, operates and collects toll; **bears revenue risk** |
| BOT (Annuity) | Build-Operate-Transfer | Government pays a fixed annuity; **government bears revenue risk** |
| BOOT | Build-Own-Operate-Transfer | Private player owns the asset during the concession, then transfers it [GK] |
| BOO | Build-Own-Operate | No transfer back to government [GK] |
| BOLT | Build-Own-Lease-Transfer | Private player builds and leases the asset to government, then transfers it [GK] |
| DBFO(T) | Design-Build-Finance-Operate-(Transfer) | Private player also designs and finances [GK] |
| EPC | Engineering, Procurement, Construction | **Government funds entirely**; private player only constructs |
| HAM | Hybrid Annuity Model | Government pays **40%** during construction; developer's **60%** repaid as **semi-annual annuities** over about **15 years**; government collects tolls and **bears traffic risk** |
| TOT | Toll-Operate-Transfer | Toll rights on already-built highways transferred to private players |

- **HAM:** blends EPC + BOT-Annuity
  - ⚠ **Trap:** HAM **40 – 60** = government – developer
  - **FIRST outside roads:** **Namami Gange** STPs pioneered HAM outside roads → performance-linked payments under One City One Operator model
- **MoRTH changes in 2026:**
  - **Revised BOT guidelines (May 2026):** **sovereign wealth funds, pension and infrastructure funds and private equity** may bid for BOT (earlier only TOT)
  - **Stricter HAM norms:** developer net worth = **20%** of project cost (up from **15%**); disqualification for major structural collapse within **2 years** (LENS, May 2026)

## Disinvestment — DIPAM, types and methods | from: S4
# 1. DIPAM
- **Role:** under Ministry of Finance; manages Centre's equity in CPSEs; runs disinvestment via strategic sale, **OFS** and other methods
- **Evolution:**
  - Began as **Department of Disinvestment** in **1999** (APP says 2000)
  - Later a Ministry
  - Renamed DIPAM in **2016**
# 2. Types of disinvestment
| **Type** | **Government's position** |
|---|---|
| Minority disinvestment | Keeps **>51%** and management control |
| Majority disinvestment | Hands over control, keeps a minority stake |
| Strategic sale | Transfers a block of shares **plus management control** to a strategic partner |
| Complete privatisation | Sells **100%** ownership and control |

# 3. Methods - OFS, ETFs, IPO, strategic sale
| **Method** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| IPO | First-time public issue |
| FPO | Further issue by an already listed PSE |
| OFS | Auction of shares on stock exchange; used widely since **2012** |
| IPP | Placement to Qualified Institutional Buyers only |
| ETFs | **CPSE ETF** + **Bharat-22 ETF** |
| Buyback | PSE buys back shares, government tenders its stake [GK] |

- ⚠ **Trap:** **IPO** = first-time issue; **FPO** = already listed PSE

## PPP issues — stalled projects, renegotiation, Swiss challenge | from: S2
- **Stalled projects:** **aggressive bidding** (winner's curse) + traffic/revenue shortfalls + land/clearance delays → projects **stalled**
- **Renegotiation:** contracts then need **renegotiation** → Kelkar Committee wanted a built-in mechanism
- **Swiss challenge:** criticised for information asymmetry; Kelkar Committee (2015) discouraged it
- **Proposed bodies:** **IPAT** (adjudication tribunal) + **IPRC** (project review committee)
