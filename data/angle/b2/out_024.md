=== ROW 83 | PESA Act 1996; Fifth and Sixth Schedule areas and tribal self-governance

## Scheduled Tribes and Scheduled Areas — definitions, criteria and data | from: S0, S1
# 1. Who is an ST - Article 342
- **Notification:** **President**, in consultation with Governor, notifies STs for a State/UT → **Constitution (ST) Order, 1950**
  - Later inclusion/exclusion: only **Parliament by law**
- **Religion:** ST status is **religion-neutral** (2026)
  - Kalelkar Commission: tribes may belong to any religion
  - vs SC status = Hindu/Sikh/Buddhist only
- **Identification traits (Lokur Committee, 1965):** **primitive traits, distinctive culture, geographical isolation, shyness of contact, backwardness**
# 2. What is a Scheduled Area
- **Declared by:** **President**, **in consultation with the Governor**
- **President's powers:** Increase/decrease, alter boundaries, rescind or re-declare
- **Criteria (IYB 2026):**
  - **preponderance of tribal population**
  - Compactness and reasonable size
  - Viable administrative unit (district/block/taluk)
  - Economic backwardness
- **Purpose of both Schedules:** Area-based protection of tribal land and culture, since STs live in contiguous areas
# 3. ST data and FIRSTs

| **Item** | **Fact** |
|---|---|
| STs notified | **705** ethnic groups |
| Largest tribal population | **MP** |
| Largest tribe | **Bhil** |
| No ST notified | Delhi, Punjab, Haryana, Puducherry, Chandigarh |

- ⚠ ST = any religion; SC = Hindu/Sikh/Buddhist only

## Articles → provisions (342, 338A, 244, 275, 243M) | from: S0, S1, S2, S3

| **Article / Amendment** | **Provision** |
|---|---|

- **Amendment of Schedules:** Parliament can amend the Fifth and Sixth Schedules by **ordinary law** (not Art. 368)
# 1. NCST (Art. 338A) - extra focus
- **Ownership of minor forest produce**
- Safeguarding **mineral and water rights**
- **Rehabilitation** of displaced tribals
- **Powers used:** Requisitioned FRA implementation reports from MoEFCC

## Fifth Schedule — Governor's powers, regulations and TAC | from: S1
# 1. Governor's role
- **Executive power:** State executive power extends to Scheduled Areas; the **Union** can give directions on their administration
- **Report:** **Governor** reports to the President **annually or when required**
- **Application of laws (Para 5):** Governor may direct that **any Act of Parliament or the State legislature** **does not apply**, or applies with modifications, to a Scheduled Area
- **Regulations (Para 5):** **regulations for peace and good government** (G2 2017 key)
  - May **prohibit/restrict land transfer** by or among STs
  - Regulate **allotment of land** and **money-lending**
  - Made after consulting the TAC; may repeal Acts; need the **President's assent**
> Exam angle: "Governor is NOT authorised to direct that an Act does not apply" is the false statement (APPSC-G2 2017). Regulations are issued by the **Governor**. TAC size is **up to 20**.
# 2. Tribes Advisory Council (TAC)
- **Tribes Advisory Council:** Mandatory in every State with Scheduled Areas; if the President directs, also in a State with STs but no Scheduled Areas
- **Size:** **not more than 20 members** (**up to 20**), about **three-fourths ST MLAs**
- **Role:** Advises only on ST welfare matters **referred by the Governor**
- !! Not in your sources: the current composition of AP's TAC.
> Exam angle: TAC size "up to 20" (G2 2017 - answer key was left blank; the Constitution says not more than 20).

## Sixth Schedule — autonomous districts, regions and councils | from: S2
# 1. Terms → meaning

| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **"Tribal Areas"** | Areas specified in **Parts I, II, IIA and III** of the table to **para 20** |
| **Autonomous districts** | Tribal areas constituted as **autonomous districts**; the **Governor** can organise/re-organise them and alter their areas, boundaries and names |
| **Autonomous regions** | Where a district has different tribes, the Governor may divide it into **autonomous regions** |
| **Autonomous region** | A unit **within** an autonomous district, with its own Regional Council |
| **Regional Council** | Each autonomous region has one |

- ⚠ **Key:** **autonomous region is a unit within an autonomous district** (APPSC-G2 2017)
# 2. Councils - numbers
- **ADCs:** 10 ADCs - Assam 3, Meghalaya 3, Tripura 1, Mizoram 3
- **Autonomous District Council:**
  - Up to **30 members - 4 nominated by the Governor, 26 elected** by adult franchise
  - Elected members serve **5 years**
# 3. Powers and application of laws

| **Power** | **Content** |
|---|---|
| Legislative | Land, forests, canal water, shifting cultivation, village administration, inheritance, marriage, social customs - needs Governor's assent [GK] |
| Judicial | Village councils/courts for suits between tribals; High Court jurisdiction as the Governor specifies [GK] |
| Executive/financial | Wide executive powers; assess land revenue and levy specified taxes [GK] |

- **Financial:** Land revenue and certain taxes; grants-in-aid under Art. 275
- **Application of Acts:** Acts of Parliament/State legislature apply only as directed, with exceptions:
  - Assam → Governor
  - Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram → President (some texts: President/Governor)

## States → coverage under the Fifth Schedule, Sixth Schedule and PESA | from: S1, S2, S5
# 1. Fifth Schedule and PESA States
- **Coverage:** Scheduled Areas and STs in any State **other than** the four Sixth Schedule States (Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram)
- **States now:** **10 States** - **Andhra Pradesh, Telangana**, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Rajasthan
- **PESA covered:** All **10 Fifth Schedule States** (listed above), incl. **Andhra Pradesh, Telangana**
  - **Odisha:** Only **draft rules** (Apr 2026)
# 2. Sixth Schedule States
- **Coverage:** Tribal areas of **Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram** only
- **Not covered: Nagaland** (Art. 371A), Manipur, Arunachal Pradesh and Sikkim - NOT under it
- **Not population-based:** Coverage not tied to any population share
  - ⚠ **Trap:** "All states with ST above 40%" is WRONG → answer: **none** of the statements correct (APPSC-G2 2025)
> Exam angle: G2 2025 statement set - the Sixth Schedule is NOT applied "wherever STs exceed 40%"; the key was "None" correct. GS 2019: Nagaland is not in the Sixth Schedule.
# 3. PESA not covered
- Sixth Schedule States: **Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram**
- States without Scheduled Areas, e.g. **Tamil Nadu**
- ⚠ **Key:** Assam-Meghalaya-Tamil Nadu (RPSC 2016)

## PESA Act 1996 — year, committee and objectives | from: S3
# 1. Act → year → committee

| **Item** | **Fact** |
|---|---|
| Full name | Provisions of the Panchayats (Extension to the Scheduled Areas) Act |
| Committee | **Bhuria Committee** (**Dilip Singh Bhuria Committee**), report **1995** (some notes say 1991) |
| Enacted | **1996** (passed by Parliament), **24 Dec 1996** |
| Need | Art. 243M(1) bars Part IX in Scheduled and Tribal Areas |
| Enabling clause | Art. 243M(4)(b) |

- **Operationalises:** Extends **Part IX (Panchayats)** to **Fifth Schedule Areas** with exceptions; recognises governance through **customary law**
- **Applies to:** **Fifth Schedule** (Scheduled) Areas only
# 2. Objectives
- Extend Part IX with modifications
- **self-rule** for the tribal population
- Gram Sabha as the **nucleus** of village governance
- Administrative framework consistent with traditional practice
- Safeguard **customs and traditions**
- Stop higher-tier panchayats from taking over lower-tier powers
- **Not an objective:** Creating **autonomous regions** (that is the Sixth Schedule) (UPSC 2013)

## PESA provisions — Gram Sabha powers, reservation and higher tiers | from: S4
# 1. Gram Sabha
- **Level:** Habitation level - **para, majra, tola**
- **Functions:** Protects traditions and community resources; manages common property; settles local disputes
- **Presiding officer:** An **ST member elected by the Gram Sabha**, not the Sarpanch (CGPSC 2015)
  - Fixed by each state's PESA rules; check AP PESA Rules 2011 for the exact provision
# 2. Powers of Gram Sabha and Panchayats

| **Subject** | **Gram Sabha / panchayat power** |
|---|---|
| Minor forest produce | **Ownership** of minor forest produce |
| Land acquisition and resettlement | Must be **consulted** before acquisition for projects (consultation, not consent) |
| Minor minerals | Recommendation mandatory before prospecting licences or mining leases for **minor minerals** [GK] |
| Intoxicants / liquor | Enforce prohibition or regulate sale and consumption |
| Money lending | Control **money-lending to STs** |
| Land alienation | Prevent alienation; restore unlawfully alienated land [GK] |
| Village markets, social-sector institutions, local plans incl. Tribal Sub-Plan | Management and control |

# 3. Reservation and higher tiers
- **Seats:** Reserved in proportion to population; STs get at least **50%**
- **Chairpersons:** **All Chairperson posts at every level go to STs.**
- **Nomination:** State may nominate unrepresented tribes to block/district bodies
- **Higher-level panchayats:** Must not usurp Gram Sabha powers
- **District level:** States should follow the **Sixth Schedule pattern** for Zila Panchayat arrangements
- **Zila Panchayat** in Scheduled Areas: Plans minor water bodies; controls social-sector institutions and Tribal Sub-Plans; other functions conferred by the State (CGPSC 2016 - "all of the above")
- **Inconsistent state laws:** Lapse **one year** after assent

|---|---|---|
| **Samatha v. State of AP** | SC, 1997, an AP case | Scheduled Area land cannot be leased to **non-tribals or private companies**, incl. for mining |

## Fifth vs Sixth Schedule — comparison | from: S9

| **Point** | **Fifth Schedule** | **Sixth Schedule** |
|---|---|---|
| Article | 244(1) | 244(2), 275(1) |
| Name of areas | **Scheduled Areas** | **Tribal Areas** |
| Where | 10 States (not the NE four) | Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram |
| Who declares / key authority | President declares; Governor's regulations | Specified in the Schedule; Governor organises districts; councils make laws |
| Executive power / Union role | Union's executive power extends: Union can give directions | Administration within the state's executive authority |
| Institution / body | **Tribes Advisory Council** (advisory) | **District/Regional Councils** with legislative, executive and judicial powers |
| Application of laws | Governor may exclude/modify | Governor (Assam) / President (other three) |
| Local self-rule law | **PESA 1996** applies | PESA does not apply (2026) |

- ⚠ **Scheduled Areas** = Fifth = **TAC** = PESA; **Tribal Areas** = Sixth = **District/Regional Councils**
