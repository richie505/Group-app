=== ROW 52 | Emergency provisions - National Emergency (352), President's Rule (356), Financial Emergency (360); S.R. Bommai case

## Emergency types → source, grounds, approval, duration | from: S0, S1, S3, S6
- **Source:** **Government of India Act, 1935** - emergency provisions
- **Ordinance power (Arts 123/213):** also a 1935 Act relic

| **Feature** | **National (Art. 352)** | **President's Rule (Art. 356)** | **Financial (Art. 360)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground | "**internal disturbance**" → "armed rebellion" (**44th CAA, 1978**); can be declared on **imminent danger**; whole or a part of India | President satisfied, on the **Governor's report or otherwise**, that the state's government cannot be carried on per the Constitution | **financial stability or credit** of India or any part is threatened |
| Approval | Both Houses within **1 month**, by **special majority** | **6 months** per approval | within **2 months**, **simple majority** |
| Duration | Continues **6 months**, extendable indefinitely with approval every 6 months | Maximum **3 years** | **no maximum period** - continues till revoked |
| Revocation | President any time; must revoke if **Lok Sabha** disapproves by simple majority | By the President at any time; no parliamentary approval needed | - |

- **Art. 352 - Cabinet advice:** **Written advice of the Union Cabinet** mandatory (44th CAA) → inserted as **Art. 352(3)**
  - ⚠ **Trap:** UPSC notes wrongly say "new Article 352A"
- **Art. 352 - special sitting:** Notice by **1/10 of Lok Sabha members** → special sitting within **14 days**
- **Art. 356 - also deemed** under **Art. 365** (non-compliance with Union directions)
- **Art. 355:** Union's duty to protect states from external aggression + internal disturbance and ensure constitutional governance → basis for Art. 356
- **Art. 356 beyond 1 year:** only if **a National Emergency is in operation AND the Election Commission certifies** that elections cannot be held (2026)
  - ⚠ **Trap:** both conditions needed - some notes say "or"
- ⚠ **1 month – 2 months – 6 months** = **352 – 360 – 356** approval windows

## Amendments → changes to emergency provisions (38th, 39th, 42nd, 44th) | from: S1, S2
| **Amendment** | **Change** |
|---|---|
| **38th CAA (1975)** | Made satisfaction non-justiciable → later reversed |
| **39th CAA** | Put the PM's and Speaker's election disputes beyond courts |
| **42nd CAA (1976)** | "mini-constitution"; raised Lok Sabha/Assembly terms to **6 years** |
| **44th CAA, 1978** | Ground "**internal disturbance**" → "armed rebellion"; written Cabinet advice (Art. 352(3)); Art. 358 **not** for **armed rebellion**; Art. 359 cannot touch **Arts. 20 and 21** |

- **44th Amendment 1978 on Art. 358:**
  - **Not** when the ground is **armed rebellion**
  - Only laws related to the Emergency are protected

## Effects of National Emergency — Centre-state relations, terms, Articles 358 and 359 | from: S2
| **Area** | **Effect** |
|---|---|
| Executive | Centre can direct states on **any matter** |
| Financial | President can **modify distribution of revenues**, till the end of the financial year in which the emergency ends |
| Terms | Lok Sabha and Assemblies extendable by law **1 year at a time**, not beyond **6 months** after it ends |
| Art. 358 | **Art. 19 suspended automatically** - only for **war/external aggression**, not armed rebellion |
| Art. 359 | Presidential order suspends enforcement of specified FRs - **except Arts. 20 and 21** (44th CAA) |

- **Lasting protection (Art. 358):** acts done during the emergency cannot be challenged even afterwards
- **Art. 359 (other rights):** needs a Presidential order; can **never** suspend **Arts. 20 and 21**
> Exam angle: LENS Jul 2026 MCQ - the statement "Arts 20 and 21 can be suspended under Art. 359" is **wrong**.
- ⚠ **358** = Art. 19, automatic; **359** = Presidential order, never Arts. 20 and 21

# 1. S.R. Bommai (1994) - principles
- **Judicial review:** Proclamation under 356 is **subject to judicial review** (mala fide or irrelevant grounds)
- **Floor test:** in the Assembly = only test of majority, not the Governor's opinion
- **Assembly:** may only be **suspended** until Parliament approves; no arbitrary dissolution; court can **restore** a dismissed government
- **Basic features:** **Secularism** + **federalism**; a state government acting against secularism can be dismissed
