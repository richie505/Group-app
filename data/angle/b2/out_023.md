## State Finance Commission and Union Finance Commission — Articles 243I, 243Y, 280 | from: S10
# 1. State Finance Commission - Articles 243I and 243Y, constitution, functions
- **Constituted by:** **Governor**, every **five years** (**once in five years**); first within one year of 24 April 1993
- **Composition:** State Legislature decides composition and members' qualifications
- **Report:** Goes to the **Governor**, who lays it before the Legislature with an **action taken report** (memorandum)
- **Nature:** **Advisory**, not binding
- **Reviews:** **Financial position of Panchayats** (243I) + of **Municipalities** (243Y - the same Commission)
- **Recommends:**
  - Distribution of net proceeds of State taxes, duties, tolls and fees between State and local bodies
  - Taxes **assigned** to them
  - **Grants-in-aid** from the State Consolidated Fund
  - (d) Measures to improve local finances
  - Any matter the Governor refers
- **PYQ facts:**
  - Examines financial position of local governments **and** reviews distribution of revenue between state and local bodies (APPSC-G2 2025)
  - Set up **once in five years**; recommends taxes, duties, tolls and fees assigned to local bodies (APPSC-G1 2022)
# 2. Article 280(3)(bb) and (c) - Union Finance Commission and local bodies
- **Role:** **Central Finance Commission** recommends measures to augment a **State's Consolidated Fund**, **on the basis of the SFC's recommendations**, to supplement resources of:
  - **panchayats (clause bb, 73rd Amendment)**
  - **municipalities (clause c, 74th Amendment)**
- **Flow of funds:** Central tax devolution goes to states; panchayats get Central money only as FC grants routed through the state
  - ⚠ **Trap:** "Union FC has no role in LB assistance" is **false** (UPPSC 2011)

## 74th Amendment Act, 1992 — dates, Part IXA, compulsory vs voluntary | from: S1
# 1. Enactment and commencement
- **Name:** **74th Amendment Act, 1992** - the **Nagarpalika Act** (**Municipalities**)
- **Timeline:**
  - Passed by both Houses: **December 1992**
  - Presidential assent: **April 1993**
  - In force: **1 June 1993**
  - ⚠ **Trap:** 73rd came into force on 24 April 1993 - the classic distractor
# 2. Provisions
- **Part inserted:** **Part IXA "The Municipalities"** / **Part IX-A** (**Art. 243P to 243ZG**, 18 Articles)
- **Schedule:** **Twelfth Schedule** (18 items, linked to **Art. 243W**)
- **Effect:** ULBs got constitutional status; uniform municipal framework obligatory for states
- **Conformity:** States to bring municipal laws into conformity within **one year** (243ZF)
- **Subject:** 'Local Government' remains a **State List** subject (Seventh Schedule)
# 3. Compulsory vs voluntary

| **Compulsory (must be in state law)** | **Voluntary (left to state legislature)** |
|---|---|
| Three types of municipalities (243Q) | Representation of MPs, MLAs, MLCs in municipalities |
| Direct election of all members from wards (243R) | Manner of electing the Chairperson (Mayor) |
| Ward Committees where population is **3 lakh or more** (243S) | Reservation for backward classes |
| SC/ST reservation in proportion to population; not less than one-third for women (243T) | Powers, functions and Twelfth Schedule subjects actually devolved (243W) |
| 5-year term; election within 6 months of dissolution (243U) | Taxing powers and grants to municipalities (243X) |
| State Finance Commission review (243Y); State Election Commission (243ZA) | Other committees besides ward committees |

## Composition, age, term and elections of municipalities — Articles 243R to 243ZA | from: S4
# 1. Composition (Art. 243R) and reservation
- **Members:** **Directly elected** from **wards**
- **Additions by State law:**
  - Persons with municipal-administration experience (**no vote**)
  - Lok Sabha/Assembly members for the area
  - Rajya Sabha/Council members **registered as electors** there
  - Chairpersons of committees other than wards committees
- **Reservation (243T):** Mirrors 243D
- **Chairperson:** Election left to the State
# 2. Qualifications to contest (Art. 243V)
- **Minimum age:** Eligible for state legislature elections, but minimum age = **21 years** (not 25)
- **Disqualification:** As under the law for State Legislature elections or a State law - disqualifications follow state law
# 3. Term (Art. 243U)
- **Five years** from first meeting
- Election before expiry, or **within six months** of dissolution
- Municipality is heard before dissolution
# 4. State Election Commission and State Finance Commission
- **Elections (Art. 243ZA):** Electoral rolls + all municipal polls under the **State Election Commission** (same SEC as Art. 243K)
- **243Y:** The **243I Finance Commission** also reviews municipal finances
- ⚠ **243K / 243ZA** = SEC; **243I / 243Y** = SFC (one body each for panchayats and municipalities)

## DPC and MPC — Articles 243ZD and 243ZE | from: S7

| **Feature** | **DPC** | **MPC** |
|---|---|---|
| Article | **243ZD** | **243ZE** |
| Where / area | Every district | Every metropolitan area (population **10 lakh or more**, Art. 243P) |
| Elected share | Not less than **four-fifths** elected by and from elected members of the district panchayat and municipalities, in proportion to rural:urban population | Not less than **two-thirds** elected by and from elected members of municipalities and chairpersons of panchayats in the area |
| Electors | District panchayat and municipal members | Municipal members and panchayat chairpersons |
| Plan | Consolidates plans of panchayats and municipalities into a **draft district development plan** | Draft development plan for the whole metropolitan area |
| Chairperson | Manner of choosing fixed by state law; chairperson forwards the plan to the State Government | Same |

# 1. District Planning Committee (DPC)
- **Source:** In Part IXA - inserted by the **74th** (not the 73rd) Amendment
- **Purpose:** Every State constitutes one at district level to **consolidate the plans of Panchayats and Municipalities** + prepare a **draft development plan** for the whole district
- **Composition:**
  - **Four-fifths** of members **elected** by and from among elected members of the **district panchayat and municipalities**, in proportion to the district's **rural-urban population** ratio
  - Rest per State law (ex-officio/nominated) → **not** fully elected
  - ⚠ **Trap:** "All members elected" is false
- **Draft plan considers:** Common-interest matters (spatial planning, water and resource sharing, integrated infrastructure, environment) + available resources
- **Forwarding:** The **chairperson forwards** the plan to the **State Government**; State law settles composition and chairperson election
> Exam angle: APPSC-GS 2018 - "DPC provided by 74th Act; consolidates plans; all members elected" - only the first two ("provided by the 74th" and "consolidates panchayat and municipal plans") are correct.
# 2. Metropolitan Planning Committee (MPC)
- **Mandatory for:** Every **metropolitan area** = population of **10 lakh or more** (may span districts)
- **Composition:** **Two-thirds** of members elected by elected members of municipalities + **chairpersons of panchayats** in the area, in proportion to urban-rural population
- **Plan considers:** Local plans, common-interest matters, Union/State objectives and investments
- **Forwarding:** Chairperson forwards the **draft plan** to the State Government
- ⚠ **DPC four-fifths** vs **MPC two-thirds**

## Universal angles | from: S11 | universal
# 1. Current relevance: AP Cabinet, funds, polls
- **AP Cabinet (18 Aug 2026):**
  - **33 1/3% BC quota** (**33.33% BC**, G.O. Rt. 1065) in ULBs; **34%** in rural bodies
  - **Direct election of Mayors** and municipal/nagar panchayat chairpersons, replacing the indirect system
- **Funds at risk:** ULBs may lose **Rs 12,150 crore** of Central funds if polls slip past October
  - 16th Finance Commission guidelines: only a duly constituted ULB gets FC grants
- **Telangana SEC:** After 36 hung ULBs, SEC clarified only **RS members and MLCs registered as voters in the ULB** can vote as ex-officio members in Mayor/Chairperson polls (15 Feb 2026)
# 2. Criticism & debates: finances and parallel bodies
- **Fiscal weakness:** ULBs spend **less than 1% of GDP**; employ only **10%** of public workforce - the "stepchild" of federalism
- **Property tax:** Key own revenue; chronically under-collected
- **District Development Councils:** A State's proposal criticised as a parallel authority bypassing Zila Panchayats and ULBs - unlike the DPC, which aggregates plans from below
# 3. Significance: urbanisation, DPC/MPC, firsts
- **Urbanisation:** Cities house **~36%** of India's population
- **DPC/MPC:** Only constitutional bodies joining rural and urban planning
- **FIRST/LAST:**
  - Hanumantha Rao Committee (1984): Collector central to district planning
  - Balwantrai Mehta, ARC-I, Ashok Mehta and G.V.K. Rao: Collector's role reduced
