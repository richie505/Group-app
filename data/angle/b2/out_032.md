## 106th Amendment and 131st Amendment Bill → Articles → provisions | from: S2, S3
# 1. Constitution (106th Amendment) Act, 2023 — Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam
| **Article inserted** | **Covers** |
|---|---|
| **330A** | Lok Sabha |
| **332A** | State Assemblies |
| **239AA(2)(ba)** | Delhi Assembly |
| **334A** | Commencement and duration |

- **Quota:** **One-third** (**1/3**) of seats in **Lok Sabha, State Assemblies and the Delhi Assembly**, incl. one-third of SC/ST reserved seats
- **Commencement:** Only after the **first Census after the Act** + the delimitation that follows (**next Census and delimitation**)
  - Linked to the **2027 Census** → not enforceable before **2034** under current law
- **Duration:** **15 years**, extendable by Parliament
- **Rotation:** Seats **rotate** after each delimitation (**Art. 334A**)
- **Exclusions:**
  - No reservation in **Rajya Sabha** or **State Legislative Councils**
  - **No OBC/BC sub-quota**
- **In force for:** **J&K and Puducherry** Assemblies from **17 April 2026**
# 2. Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill, 2026 — defeated attempt to fast-track the quota
- **Aim:** Implement/fast-track quota from 2029 by raising Lok Sabha maximum from **550 to 850** (815 States, 35 UTs), delimitation on the **2011 Census**
- **Result:** **Defeated in Lok Sabha** / **negatived** (**17 Apr 2026**, special session of 16-18 April; **defeated on 17 April 2026**)
  - **298 for, 230 against** of **528** present and voting (298-230)
  - Short of the **Art. 368** special majority = **352** needed
- **Later:** LENS (28 Aug 2026) still discusses the expansion proposal; see Universal
- ⚠ **Art. 334A** = rotation + duration of the 106th quota; **Art. 368** = special majority the 131st Bill missed

## 73rd/74th Amendments → Articles → women's reservation | from: S4
| **Provision** | **Panchayats (73rd)** | **Municipalities (74th)** |
|---|---|---|
| Women's seats, not less than **1/3 of directly elected seats**, at least **one-third** (incl. SC/ST women); same one-third rule for municipalities | **Art. 243D(3)** | **Art. 243T(3)** |
| 1/3 of seats reserved for SCs/STs go to SC/ST women | **243D(2)** | - |
| Women chairpersons, not less than **1/3 of chairperson offices** at each level | **Art. 243D(4)** | **Art. 243T(4)** |
| Rotation of reserved seats | Allowed | Allowed |

- **Women's one-third:** **Compulsory**; constitution sets a **floor, not a ceiling**
- **Backward classes:** **Voluntary**/optional; States may reserve seats for BCs
- **SC/ST seats:** At every level **in proportion to population**
- **Rotation:** Reserved seats + chairperson offices (as State law provides) allotted **by rotation**
- **ST seats in municipalities:** **Art. 243T(1)** (MPPSC 2020)
- **73rd in force:** **24 April 1993**
- **Exception:** Not applicable to **Arunachal Pradesh** (no SC population)
- **Sunset clause:**
  - **SC/ST reservation** (seats + chairpersons): ends with **Art. 334** period, now **2030** under the **104th Amendment** (Art. 243D(5))
  - **Women's reservation has no such sunset**
- ⚠ **Trap:** Statement 2 in UPPSC 2017 is false
- ⚠ **243D(3)** = women's seats; **243D(4)** = women chairpersons; **243T** = the same for municipalities

## Gender indices → publisher → dimensions; India's rank | from: S8
| **Index** | **Publisher** | **What it measures** |
|---|---|---|
| **Gender Development Index (GDI)** | **UNDP** (Human Development Report) | **Ratio of female to male HDI** across health (life expectancy), education (expected and mean years of schooling) and command over economic resources (earned income) |
| **Gender Inequality Index (GII)** | **UNDP** (introduced 2010 [GK]) | **Reproductive health** (MMR, adolescent birth rate), **empowerment** (share of parliamentary seats, secondary education) and **labour market** (female LFPR) |
| **Global Gender Gap Index (GGGI)** | **World Economic Forum** (since 2006 [GK]) | Four sub-indices: **Economic Participation & Opportunity, Educational Attainment, Health & Survival, Political Empowerment**[GK] |
| **Women, Peace and Security (WPS) Index** | Georgetown Institute for Women, Peace and Security with PRIO [GK] | Inclusion, justice and security of women [GK] |

- **GDI scale:** Parity near **1**
