=== ROW 66 | Party system in India - one-party dominance, multi-party and coalition eras

## Party system — definitions, types and FIRSTs | from: S0, S2
# 1. Nature and types
- **Multi-party system:** **India has a multi-party system** as society divides along **region, language, caste, religion and class**
  - Two-party system (UK, USA) presumes one dominant axis
- **Constitution:** as adopted **said nothing about political parties**
- **Types:** **cadre vs mass** parties; **national, state, registered-unrecognised** by ECI status
- **RUPPs:** over **2,800 registered unrecognised parties**, only about **750** contested the 2024 general election (11 Sep 2026)
# 2. FIRST non-Congress government at the Centre, 1977
- **Year:** **1977**, after the Emergency
- **Party:** **Janata Party** defeated INC → first time Congress lost a general election since independence
- **PM:** **Morarji Desai**
- **Nature:** also **first coalition-type government** at the Centre (merger of parties with different ideologies), but stable with a Lok Sabha majority
- **End:** Congress returned in **1980**
> Exam angle: "first non-Congress government" = 1977 (not 1989, which was the first non-Congress coalition of the multi-party era).

## Appointment, term and post-retirement bar — UPSC vs SPSC vs JSPSC (Art. 316, 319) | from: S2
# 1. Appointment and composition
| **Body** | **Appointed by** | **Strength** | **Term / age limit** | **Resigns to** | **Removed by** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| UPSC | **President** | President decides (usually **9-11**) | **6 years or age 65** | President | **President** |
| SPSC | **Governor** | Governor decides | **6 years or age 62** | Governor | **President** (not the Governor) |
| JSPSC | **President** | - | Age **62** | President | **President** |

- **Strength:** Not fixed by the Constitution
  - UPSC: left to the President (usually **9-11** members incl. Chairman)
  - SPSC: left to the **Governor**
- **Qualification:** **Half** the members must have held office under GoI/State Govt for at least **10 years**
- **Acting Chairman:** Appointed by the President during vacancy/absence of the Chairman
# 2. Term
- **Term:** **6 years** or age **65** (UPSC) / **62** (SPSC, JSPSC), whichever earlier (SPSC: **6 years or 62**)
  - **No second term** in the same office
# 3. Post-retirement (Art. 319)
| **Office** | **May later hold** |
|---|---|
| **UPSC Chairman** | No further employment under GOI or any State |
| **UPSC member** | May become UPSC Chairman or SPSC Chairman, nothing else |
| **SPSC member** (or chairman) | May become **chairman/member of UPSC** or **chairman of that/another SPSC**; no other government employment |

## Removal and suspension of members — Art. 317 | from: S3
- **Grounds (both):**
  - **Insolvency**
  - **Paid employment outside duties**
  - **Infirmity of mind or body**
  - **Misbehaviour** → only after reference to the **Supreme Court**
- **SC reference:** Removal only if SC upholds the cause; its advice is **binding**
- **Suspension:** President (Governor for SPSC) may **suspend** the member during the SC inquiry
- **Misbehaviour includes:** Being interested in a government contract or sharing in its profit (Art. 317(4))
> Exam angle: APPSC 2019 - SPSC chairman **appointed by Governor, removed by President**.
> Exam angle: "Governor appoints, President removes" for SPSC chairman is the favourite trap.

## Functions — consulted and not consulted (Art. 320, 321) | from: S4
# 1. Functions (Art. 320)
- **Examinations:** For appointment to Union (UPSC) / State (SPSC) services - All-India, Central and Group-A services
- **Consulted on:**
  - Methods of recruitment
  - Principles of appointment, **promotion and transfer**
  - Suitability of candidates
  - **Disciplinary matters** (censure, withholding increments, demotion, compulsory retirement, removal)
  - Claims for pension for service injuries + legal expenses
  - Temporary appointments of **more than one year**
  - Extension of service/re-employment
# 2. Outside UPSC's purview
- **Excluded (Outside UPSC's purview Reservation):**
  - **Reservations** of posts for backward classes / SCs-STs
  - Claims of SCs/STs to services/posts
  - Temporary appointments of **less than a year**
  - Group B/C posts
  - Highest diplomatic posts
  - ⚠ **Trap:** **Manner of reservation is NOT a UPSC function** (APPSC-G1 2022)
- **Exclusion by regulations:** President (Governor for SPSC) may exclude posts; regulations laid before Parliament/Legislature for **14 days**
- **Not concerned with:** Classification, pay or training
# 3. Extension and nature of advice
- **Art. 321:** Functions **extendable** by an **Act** of Parliament/State Legislature (e.g. personnel system of local bodies, corporate bodies, corporations, public institutions)
- **Nature:** Only **advisory**; consultation **directory, not mandatory**
  - Decision taken without consultation is still valid
  - Selection confers **no right to the post** (SC)
- **CVC (1964, statutory 2003):** Also consulted on disciplinary matters; UPSC has the edge
- **SPSC:** "Watchdog of the merit system"
> Exam angle: APPSC-G1 2022 - "manner of reservation" is **not** a UPSC function.

## Annual report and non-acceptance of advice (Art. 323) | from: S5
| **Body** | **Report to** | **Laid before** |
|---|---|---|
| **UPSC** | the **President** | **both Houses** with a **memorandum on non-accepted advice** (cases where advice was **not accepted**) |
| **SPSC** | the **Governor** | the **State Legislature** with a similar memorandum |
| **JSPSC** | **each concerned Governor** | his Legislature |

- **Non-acceptance:** Every such case must be approved by the **Appointments Committee of the Cabinet**; a ministry cannot reject UPSC advice on its own

## JSPSC and APPSC — bodies → features | from: S6, S7
# 1. Joint State Public Service Commission
- **Creation:** **Act of Parliament** on request of **two or more state legislatures**
  - **Statutory**, not constitutional, body
- **Appointment & removal:** Chairman + members appointed, suspended and removed by the **President**; resign to the President
- **Term:** **6 years or 62**
- **Service conditions:** Number + service conditions fixed by the President
- **Annual report:** Goes to **each concerned Governor** → placed before the state legislature
- **Example:** **Punjab and Haryana** had a JSPSC briefly after 1966
# 2. APPSC — establishment
- **Origin:** **Andhra Pradesh Public Service Commission** came into existence on **1 November 1956**, with formation of Andhra Pradesh (APPSC 2023 key)
  - Merged the **Andhra PSC (1953)** and the **Hyderabad PSC**
- **After bifurcation (2014):** APPSC continues for residuary AP; Telangana got **TSPSC**
# 3. APPSC — composition and functions
- **Nature:** SPSC under Art. 315
- **Appointment:** Chairman + members by the **Governor of AP**; strength fixed by the Governor
- **Term:** 6 years / age limit **62**
- **Removal:** By the **President**
- **Functions:** State service recruitment + lower judicial service examinations (Art. 234)

## Universal angles | from: S8 | universal
- **Current relevance:** Crisis of trust in State PSCs (Dec 2025)
  - Paper leaks, flawed evaluation, delays, litigation (e.g. Telangana 2023, Bihar 2024 exams)
  - Reform needed to protect merit
- **Significance:**
  - Called the **"watchdog of the merit system"**
  - SC in **TNPSC v A. Balasubramaniam (1994)**: PSC independence essential to fair administration
- **AP angle:** APPSC = Governor appoints, President removes; established 1956 (PYQ APPSC-GS 2023)
- **FIRST/LAST:**
  - Satyendranath Tagore = first Indian to clear the ICS (1864)
  - Simultaneous ICS exams in India from 1922 (Allahabad)
