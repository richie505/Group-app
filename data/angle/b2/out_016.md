## Federalism — definitions, types and FIRSTs | from: S0, S7

| **Type** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Cooperative federalism** | Centre and States work together on a common platform |
| Cooperative federalism (NITI Aayog's version) | brings states together on a single platform through a **bottom-up** approach; also called **"marble-cake federalism"** |
| **Competitive federalism** | Competition **among States** (and between States and the Centre in policy) for investment and citizens |
| **Competitive sub-federalism** | **Competitive sub-federalism = local bodies competing with local bodies**, i.e. competition **among local bodies** (APPSC-G1 2017 key) |

| FIRST | FACT |
|---|---|
| Cooperative federalism — **First recommended by** | the **Sarkaria Commission** (APPSC-G1 2017 key) |

- **NITI planning under cooperative federalism:** credible **village-level plans aggregated upwards**
- **Competitive federalism:**
  - Tool: better policies and services → attract investment, improve outcomes
  - Driver: NITI's **performance rankings**
- **Competitive sub-federalism example:** district rankings (ADP **"Champions of Change"** dashboard)
- **Usual pairing:** **GST Council = cooperative federalism** vs NITI Aayog rankings = competitive federalism
- ⚠ Competitive federalism = States compete; competitive sub-federalism = local bodies compete

=== ROW 60 | Devolution of powers and finances to local levels - Finance Commission and State Finance Commission transfers

## Decentralisation and devolution — terms → meaning | from: S0, S2

| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Democratic decentralisation** (Balwant Rai Mehta's term) | Power moves to **elected** local bodies |
| **Administrative delegation or deconcentration** | Work moves to field officers who stay inside the bureaucracy |
| **Vertical** devolution | Share of the **divisible pool** (Art. 270 taxes, **excluding cesses and surcharges**) going to states |
| **Horizontal** devolution | Allocation of that share **among states** by a formula |
| **Vertical fiscal imbalance** | Centre has the more buoyant taxes; states carry heavier spending duties (health, education, agriculture, police) |

- **Order of depth:** Deconcentration → delegation → **devolution**
- **Three dimensions:**
  - **Political** (decide)
  - **Administrative** (implement)
  - **Fiscal** (raise and spend)
- ⚠ Divisible pool **excludes cesses and surcharges**

## Articles → local body powers and finances | from: S0, S1, S6

| **Article** | **Provision** |
|---|---|
| **243G** | **Eleventh Schedule (29 subjects) is only a menu:** Art. 243G says "may" |
| **243H** | Own taxes, duties, tolls, fees only if the State Legislature authorises (**Art. 243H**, "may") |
| **243I** | **SFC (243I)**; grants-in-aid from the State Consolidated Fund on SFC advice |
| **243J** / **243Z** | State legislature provides for maintenance and audit of LB accounts (Art. **243J** / **243Z**) |
| **243K** | **State Election Commission (243K)** |
| **243X** | **Art. 243X** = municipal equivalent of 243H |
| **270** | Taxes forming the divisible pool |

## 3Fs and key terms → meaning | from: S0, S2, S5

| **F** | **Meaning** | **Reality** |
|---|---|---|
| Functions | Subjects actually transferred: **Eleventh Schedule 29** (panchayats), **Twelfth Schedule 18** (municipalities) | Devolved on paper |

| Functionaries | Staff answerable to the local body, not a line department | Report to State line departments |

## Articles → devolution provisions | from: S0, S2, S4, S5

## ECI — status, composition, FIRSTs and landmark dates | from: S0, S6
# Status
- **Basis:** **Art. 324** in **Part XV** (Arts. 324-329)

## Appointment, tenure, salary and removal | from: S1, S2

| **Stage** | **Composition / rule** |
|---|---|
| Search Committee | Headed by the **Law Minister** + 2 Secretary-level members (Secretary rank or above); shortlists **5** names |
| Selection Committee | **PM (chair)**, **Leader of Opposition** (or leader of the largest opposition party) in the Lok Sabha, a **Union Cabinet Minister** nominated by the PM (CJI replaced); may pick names outside the shortlist |
| Appointment | By the **President** on the Selection Committee's recommendation |
| Eligibility | Holds/has held post equal to **Secretary to GoI**, with election-management experience |
| Term | 6 years or 65; **no reappointment** |

- **Criticism:** Gives the government a **2-1 majority**
# Tenure, salary, removal
- **Tenure:** **6 years or age 65**, whichever is earlier; 2023 Act bars **reappointment**
- **Salary:** **Salary equal to a Supreme Court judge**; CEC and ECs equal; service conditions cannot be varied to their disadvantage
- **Removal of CEC:** Only like a **Supreme Court judge**
  - Grounds: proven misbehaviour or incapacity; special majority in both Houses
  - Motion needs **100 Lok Sabha or 50 Rajya Sabha** signatures
- **Removal of ECs and Regional Commissioners:** Only **on the CEC's recommendation**; Section 11 of the 2023 Act gives the procedure
- ⚠ CEC removed like an **SC judge**; ECs only on the **CEC's recommendation**; SEC like an **HC judge**
# Constitutional gaps (listed flaws)
- **Flaws:** Constitution prescribes **no qualifications** and **no term** for members
- **Post-retirement:** **The Constitution does not bar** / **Constitution does not debar** retiring ECs from further government appointment/office → a listed flaw
  - ⚠ **Trap:** APPSC-G1 2024 statement saying otherwise (Statement I) is false
  - ⚠ **Trap:** Some notes say the CEC cannot hold any office of profit after retirement; the Constitution has no such bar
- **Contrast:** CAG (Art. 148(4)) and UPSC Chairman (Art. 319) have constitutional bars

## Powers and functions — administrative, quasi-judicial, advisory | from: S3, S4
- **Types of function:** **Administrative, advisory, quasi-judicial**
- **Range:** Decides every step from announcement to results (APPSC-G1 2022)
# Administrative
- **Rolls:** Prepares and revises electoral rolls
  - **Special Intensive Revision (SIR)** ordered under Art. 324 + **Section 21, RPA 1950**
- **Process:** Notifies schedules; scrutinises nominations; regulates expenses
- **Constituencies:** Decides territorial constituencies under the Delimitation Commission Act
- **Model Code of Conduct:** Enforces it
  - **MCC is not a statute**; rests on Art. 324; applies from the day the schedule is announced (Harbans Singh Jalal, 1997)
- **Cancellation:** Can cancel polls for rigging or booth capturing
- **Delimitation examples:**
  - J&K: Commission under **Justice Ranjana Desai** (2022)
  - **Assam**: delimited by the **ECI itself** (effective Aug 2023)
# Quasi-judicial and advisory
- **Parties & symbols:** **Recognises parties** and **allots symbols** under the **Election Symbols (Reservation and Allotment) Order, 1968** (issued under RPA 1951 + Art. 324)
  - Settles symbol and recognition disputes like a court
- **Disqualification advice:**
  - **Advises the President on MPs' disqualification** (Art. 103)
  - **Governor on MLAs'** (Art. 192)
  - Anti-defection (Tenth Schedule) cases go to the Speaker/Chairman instead
# Election disputes — limits
- **Art. 329(b) petitions:** Tried by the **High Court**, appeal to the **Supreme Court**
- **Convention:** Courts do not interfere once the poll process begins (UPSC)
- **ECI's limits:** **ECI cannot review a result**; **does not try election petitions**
  - It decides party and symbol disputes and advises on disqualification
- ⚠ Disqualification (Art. 103/192) → ECI advises; anti-defection (Tenth Schedule) → Speaker/Chairman
