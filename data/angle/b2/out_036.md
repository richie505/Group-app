## Nutrition missions → launch, funding and targets | from: S1, S2

- **Basics:** **2021**; MWCD; CSS; period **2021-22 to 2025-26**

# 3. POSHAN Abhiyaan

- **Launch:** **2018** - on **8 March 2018** at **Jhunjhunu, Rajasthan**
- **Goal:** **Suposhit Bharat**, a **Jan Andolan**
- **Targets:** Reduce **stunting, undernutrition, anaemia and low birth weight** (also underweight)
  - Mission goal: cut stunting to **25%** by **2022**
- ⚠ POSHAN Abhiyaan = **Jhunjhunu 2018**; BBBP = **Panipat 2015**

## Mid-day meal to PM POSHAN → origin, norms and features | from: S4
# 1. Origin and renaming
- **Mid-Day Meal Scheme:** Introduced **1995** - on **15 Aug 1995** as **NP-NSPE** (APPSC 2025; G2-GS 2025 key)
  - "First started in **Tamil Nadu**"
- **Renamed:** **PM POSHAN (Pradhan Mantri Poshan Shakti Nirman)** in **2021**
# 2. Scheme design and financing
| **Item** | **Fact** |
|---|---|
| **Nature** | Rights-based **centrally sponsored scheme**, implemented under **NFSA 2013** |
| **Nodal ministry** | **Ministry of Education** (Dept of School Education and Literacy) |
| **Tenure** | **2021-22 to 2025-26**; outlay **₹1,30,794.90 crore** |
| **Centre-State sharing** | **60:40** |
- ⚠ PM POSHAN = **Ministry of Education**; ICDS/Poshan 2.0 = **MWCD**
# 3. Coverage and norms
- **Coverage:** **Bal Vatika** (pre-Class I / pre-school) + **Classes I-VIII** in government and government-aided schools
  - One hot cooked meal to over **11.8 crore** children (29 Jun 2026); **over 11 crore** students (VIS 2026)

| **Norm** | **Primary** | **Upper primary** |
|---|---|---|
| Nutrition | **450 kcal + 12 g protein** | **700 kcal + 20 g protein** |
| Cooking cost (per child per day) | **₹5.45** | **₹8.17** |
# 4. Aims and features
- **Aims:** Better nutrition; address **classroom hunger**; raise **enrolment, attendance, retention** and concentration of disadvantaged children
- **Features:**
  - One hot cooked meal
  - **Tithi Bhojan:** Community contributes meals on special days; first introduced by **Gujarat**
  - School nutrition gardens
  - **Mandatory social audit** in every district
  - Extra nutrition in aspirational and high-anaemia districts
  - FPO/SHG participation
# 5. Partners and AP
- **NGO partner:** **Akshaya Patra Foundation**, Bengaluru, **2000** (Madhu Pandit Dasa) - largest NGO mid-day meal provider
- **AP - Dokka Seethamma Mid-Day Meal:** Extended to **Intermediate (Classes 11-12)** in **2024-25**
  - **27.97 lakh** students
  - 5 eggs + 3 chikkis + ragi java a week
- !! Not in your sources: current per-child cooking-cost norms and partner organisations.
- ⚠ Mid-day meal **first** = **Tamil Nadu**; Tithi Bhojan **first** = **Gujarat**

## Child marriage → Act, data and models | from: S7

## RPwD Act 2016 → provisions and number game | from: S1
# 1. Enactment and scope
- **Enacted:** **2016**; in force **19 April 2017**
  - ⚠ **Trap:** "Passed in 2018" is WRONG (UPSC 2026)
- **Section 2(s):** PwD = long-term physical, mental, intellectual or sensory impairment that, with barriers, hinders full participation
# 2. Provisions → value
| **Provision / entitlement** | **Value / quantum** |
|---|---|
| Replaced | **PwD (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995** (welfare-based to **rights-based**) |
| Specified disabilities | **21** (from **7**), e.g. autism, **acid attack victims**, sickle cell disease; Centre may add more |
| Benchmark disability | At least **40%** of a specified disability (Dec 2025) |
| Reservation in government jobs | **4%** |
| Reservation in higher education | **5%** of seats |
| Allotment in land, housing, poverty-alleviation schemes | 5% |
| Free education | Children with benchmark disabilities aged **6-18** |
| Monitoring | **Chief Commissioner for PwDs**, Sec. 75(1) |
- ⚠ **4%** jobs vs **5%** higher education; **21** disabilities vs **7** in 1995; benchmark **40%**

## Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act 2007 → provisions and Sections | from: S1
# 1. Obligations and penalties
- **Obligation:** **Children (biological, adopted, step) and legal heirs** must maintain parents and senior citizens who cannot maintain themselves (Apr 2025)
- **Neglect:** The Act **punishes neglect by children**; invites **punishment for children neglecting old parents** (APPSC 2018)
- **Abandonment:** Up to **3 months** and/or **Rs 5,000** fine
- **Administration:** **MoSJE**
# 2. Tribunals and provisions
| **Feature** | **Provision / detail** |
|---|---|
| Maintenance Tribunal | Sub-division level; in AP headed by **RDO / Sub-Collector** (2024-25) |
| Appellate Tribunal | District level; in AP headed by the **District Collector** |
| Maximum maintenance | **Rs 10,000 per month** |
| Time limit | Case to be disposed within **90 days** |
| Property (Section 23) | Transfer/gift made on condition of care can be declared **void** / **revoked** (cancelled by the Tribunal) if the transferee neglects the senior citizen/parent (Apr 2025) |
| Medical care | **Section 20** - medical care of senior citizens (basis of NPHCE) |
- **Old age homes:** At least one per district
- ⚠ **Sec 23** = property; **Sec 20** = medical care; cap **Rs 10,000**; **90 days**
# 3. Amendment bill, earlier State law, AP cases
- **2019 Amendment Bill:** Lapsed
  - Remove the **Rs 10,000** cap
  - Widen "children" to sons/daughters-in-law and grandchildren
- **Earlier State law:** **Himachal Pradesh Maintenance of Parents and Dependents Act, 2001**
- **AP cases:** **2,549** filed, **2,236** resolved
