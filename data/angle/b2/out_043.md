## Agriculture schemes → year → benefit | from: S1
| **Programme** | **Purpose / benefit** |
|---|---|
| **YSR Rythu Bharosa-PM KISAN** (2019) | **₹13,500** a year per farmer family, including PM-KISAN ₹6,000 [GK]; succeeded by **Annadata Sukhibhava** (₹1,000 crore in Budget 2024-25 BE, APP) |
| **YSR Zero Interest Crop Loans** (launched **20 April 2021**) | Crop loans up to **₹1 lakh** repaid on time become interest-free (APPSC 2023) |
| **YSR Jala Kala** | Free borewells for small and marginal farmers [GK] |
| Tenant farmer support | **Crop Cultivator Rights Card (CCRC)** under the AP Crop Cultivators Rights Act, 2019 - loans, insurance and input subsidy for tenants without touching the owner's title [GK] |
| **Rythu Bharosa Kendras (RBKs)** (2020) | Village one-stop centres for seeds, fertilisers, advisory and procurement [GK] |
| **APCNF** | Community-managed natural farming run by **Rythu Sadhikara Samstha (RySS)**, since 2016 [GK] |
| **e-Crop** | Digital booking of the crop on every field each season; basis for insurance, input subsidy and procurement [GK] |

- **PMFBY in AP:**
  - Coverage risen because extreme-weather crop losses rose - A and R both true, R explains A (APPSC 2023)
  - Farmer premium = **2% kharif, 1.5% rabi, 5% commercial/horticulture**
  - ₹1,023 crore in Budget 2024-25 BE
  - **Voluntary for all farmers since Kharif 2020**
  - ⚠ **Trap:** CDX notes still say "compulsory for loanee farmers"
- ⚠ Years: **RBKs 2020**; **Rythu Bharosa 2019**; **APCNF since 2016**; **Zero Interest loans 20 April 2021**

  - **Mega** = **2,000+ direct jobs**

- ⚠ **Large** is defined by capital; **Mega** by jobs
