## Reservation — definitions, Articles and FIRSTs | from: S0, S4, S7, S8
# 1. Definition
- **Definition:** "Backward Classes" **not defined** in the Constitution (APPSC G2 2017 key: the "defined" statement is the wrong one)
  - Art. **16(4)** backward classes mainly identified by caste/social backwardness (**Indra Sawhney**)
- **Terminology:** Correct term = **Socially and Educationally** Backward Classes
  - ⚠ **Trap:** IYB writes "Socially and Economically" - WRONG
- **OBC (CEI Act):** classes "so determined by the **Central Government**"
# 2. Articles → content
| **Article** | **Content** |
|---|---|
| **Art. 340** | **President** appoints a commission to investigate conditions of **SEBCs** + recommend remedies; **Kalelkar** and **Mandal** were appointed under it |
| **Art. 338(10)** | **Before 2018:** treated references to SCs as including backward classes specified by the President → BC complaints went to the SC commission |
| **Art. 338B** | **National Commission for Backward Classes** (constitutional, 102nd) |
| **Art. 342A** | SEBC lists (102nd) |
| **Art. 366(26C)** | Defines SEBCs by reference to **Art. 342A** (102nd) |
| **Art. 15(5)** | Special provisions for **SEBCs, SCs, STs** in **admission to educational institutions, including private aided or unaided**; **Excluding minority institutions** (93rd) |
| **Art. 16(4A)** | Reservation in **promotion** for SCs/STs (77th) |
| **Art. 16(4B)** | Unfilled **backlog vacancies** carried forward (81st) |
| **Art. 15(6) and 16(6)** | **10% reservation** for EWS (103rd) |
| **Arts. 341/342** | SC/ST status flows from them (Union's argument against SC/ST creamy layer) |

## Cases → rulings | from: S2, S4, S5, S7, S8
# 1. Indra Sawhney
- **Caste criterion:** Caste **can** be the main criterion of backwardness; overruled **Balaji** on this
- **Ceiling:** Total reservation **not above 50%** except in extraordinary cases
  - Applied year by year; **carry-forward** allowed within 50%
- **Creamy layer:** Excluded from OBCs (**Origin:** introduced by **Indra Sawhney**; excludes socially advanced OBCs)
- **Promotions:** Reservation confined to **initial appointment**, not promotion → answered by the **77th**
- **Permanent body:** Directed a **permanent statutory body** for OBC lists → became the **NCBC**
# 2. Janhit Abhiyan v. Union of India (2022)
- **Janhit Abhiyan (7 Nov 2022):** Upheld **3:2** by a **five-judge** bench
- **Dissent:** CJI **U.U. Lalit** + Justice **S. Ravindra Bhat**
- **50% cap:** Applies to backward-class quotas
# 3. Other cases
| **Case** | **Ruling / answer** |
|---|---|
| **P.A. Inamdar (2005)** | Overcome by the **93rd Amendment** (Art. 15(5)) |
| Jaishri Patil | Answered by the **105th (2021)**, which **restored States' power** to identify and list SEBCs |
| **Union of India v Rohith Nathan (11 March 2026)** | **Parental salary alone** cannot place a candidate in the creamy layer; Court ordered **supernumerary posts** |
- **Rohith Nathan follow-up:** **Aug 2026:** Centre sought clarification on **958** CSE-**2025** candidates; SC likely to set up a special Bench

## Creamy layer and EWS — criteria and income limits | from: S5, S8
# 1. Creamy layer - status-based exclusions
- **Status-based exclusions:** Children of **constitutional post-holders**, **Class-I officers**, parents both **Class-II**, senior government and **armed-forces/military officers**, property owners/large landowners, high earners
# 2. Creamy layer - income limit
- **Revisions:** ₹**1 lakh (1993)** → **2.5 lakh (2004)** → **4.5 lakh (2008)** → **6 lakh (2013)** → **₹8 lakh (2017)** (**₹8 lakh a year**), still current
- **AP:** Aligned its State/public-sector definitions with the central threshold
# 3. EWS - extent and criteria
- **Extent:** **10% reservation** (**up to 10%**) for **economically weaker sections** not covered by SC/ST/OBC quotas
  - **Over and above** / **in addition** to existing reservation
  - In jobs and education; central institutions (incl. **private**) and posts
- **States:** Enabling, **not mandatory**
- **Criteria:** Family income **below ₹8 lakh** a year + asset limits (**5 acres** farm land, **1,000 sq ft** flat)
- ⚠ **₹8 lakh** = OBC creamy-layer limit (2017) = EWS income ceiling

## Nehru's Panchsheel for tribal development — five principles | from: S3
# 1. Five principles
- (1) develop **along their own genius**
- (2) respect tribal **rights in land and forests**
- (3) rely on and train **tribal people for their own administration**
- (4) **avoid over-administration**; work through their own institutions
- (5) judge results by **quality of human character** evolved, not money spent
# 2. Sources differ
- **Tribal languages:** CDI lists "encourage tribal languages" as a separate principle; standard wording keeps it inside principle 1
- **Source:** Nehru's foreword to Elwin's **A Philosophy for NEFA**

| **Sec. 6** - Gram Sabha | **Gram Sabha initiates:** determination of nature and extent of individual/community rights + passes resolutions on claims (UPSC 2013 key) |
| **Sec. 5** | **Duties:** protect forests and wildlife |
| **Committees** | **six-member** **Sub-Divisional and District Level Committees** (Revenue, Tribal, Forest officials + elected panchayat members) examine claims → issue titles |
# 3. Eligibility - cut-off 13 December 2005
| **Category** | **Eligibility** |
|---|---|
| Forest-dwelling STs (FDST) | Living in, dependent on and occupying forest land **before 13 December 2005** (APSES, TH) |
| Other Traditional Forest Dwellers (OTFD) | Resided in/depended on forest for **3 generations (75 years)** before 2005 (LENS) |
# 4. Rights and ceiling
- **Individual rights:** individual land, habitation, self-cultivation
- **Community rights:** **nistar**, **grazing**, fishing, CFR management
- **Rights list:**
  - **ownership of minor forest produce** (**MFP**)
  - **habitat rights for PVTGs**
  - **community forest resources** rights to protect and manage
  - biodiversity/traditional knowledge
- **Ceiling:** up to **4 hectares per family** (**4 ha**)
- **Public utilities:** forest land diversion for schools, dispensaries etc. on **Gram Sabha recommendation**; up to 1 ha per project
# 5. Habitat rights for PVTGs
- **Habitat rights:**
  - Follow **2014 MoTA guidelines**
  - Four State departments (Forest, Revenue, Tribal, Panchayati Raj) coordinate, with UNDP help
  - First: Bharia (MP); then Kamar and Baiga (see FIRSTs page)
