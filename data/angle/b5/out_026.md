=== ROW 87 | Conservation - in-situ (protected areas, biosphere reserves) and ex-situ (zoos, gene banks, seed banks)

## Conservation — definitions, types and FIRSTs | from: S0, S2, S5, S6, S7, S9
# In-situ vs ex-situ
| **In-situ (on-site)** | **Ex-situ (off-site)** |
|---|---|
| National park, wildlife sanctuary, biosphere reserve, tiger reserve | Zoo, botanical garden |
| Sacred grove, sacred lake, community reserve, gene sanctuary | Seed bank, gene bank, field gene bank |
| OECM, conservation reserve | Cryopreservation, tissue culture, captive breeding |

- **In-situ effectiveness:** In-situ is the **most effective** method - keeps species interacting with their environment
- **PYQ:** "not an in-situ site" = **Botanical Garden**
- **Ex-situ definition:** **off-site** conservation away from natural habitat
- **Ex-situ methods:**
  - Seed and gene banks, **pollen banks**
  - Long-term **captive breeding**
  - **animal translocation**
  - **tissue culture banks** (meristem culture = virus-free propagation)
  - Cryopreservation of gametes and embryos; in-vitro fertilisation
  - Botanical gardens, zoos
- **Captive breeding:** For species whose habitat is permanently lost; bred animals released back
  - e.g. **Gangetic gharial** reintroduced in several states
- **Ex-situ value:** Education, research, reintroduction
- **Ex-situ limits:** Needs space + money; cannot save the ecosystem

## Protected area categories under the Act and India's PA network | from: S1, S16
- **Four PA types:** **sanctuary, national park, conservation reserve, community reserve** (last two added in **2002**)
- **Tiger reserves:** Notified under the NTCA chapter

| **Category** | **Legal basis / feature** |
|---|---|
| National Park | WPA 1972; highest protection, no rights allowed, no grazing [GK]; boundary alteration only on NBWL recommendation |
| Wildlife Sanctuary | WPA 1972; human activity regulated but permitted to an extent (UPSC) |
| Conservation Reserve | WPA (2002 amendment) [GK]; State declares on **government land**, usually adjoining/linking PAs, after consulting locals (APP) |
| Community Reserve | WPA (2002 amendment) [GK]; State declares on **private/community land** when a community volunteers (APP) |

- ⚠ Conservation reserve = **government land**; community reserve = **private/community land**
# BR vs NP vs sanctuary
| **Feature** | **Biosphere reserve** | **National park** | **Wildlife sanctuary** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legal basis | UNESCO MAB recognition; no change in land's legal status or ownership | WPA 1972, state notification | WPA 1972, state notification |
| Human rights | Settlements allowed in transition zone | **No rights**, no grazing | Some rights; grazing regulated by Chief Wildlife Warden |
| Boundaries | Zoned core-buffer-transition | Clearly demarcated | Not strictly specified |

# Terms → OECM
- **OECM (Other Effective Area-based Conservation Measure):** CBD-defined area **outside the formal PA network** delivering long-term in-situ conservation
  - Recognised for its **outcomes**; **confers no legal, financial or management authority** → land ownership does not change
  - **4 criteria:** Outside PA network, demarcated, inclusive governance, lasting benefits

## Acts and court orders → eco-sensitive zones | from: S3
- **Definition:** ESZ = **"shock absorber"** / transition zone around a National Park or Sanctuary - from high protection to areas where development may be permitted (2026)
- **Legal basis:** ESZs are **notified by MoEF&CC under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986** (Sec. 3 with Rule 5, EP Rules 1986)
  - **not under the Wildlife (Protection) Act** (WPA 1972)
  - ⚠ **Trap:** APPSC 2019 statement saying ESZs come under the WPA was wrong
- **Width:** MoEF's 2011 guidelines allow ESZ **up to 10 km** around a PA

| **Prohibited** | **Regulated** | **Permitted** |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial mining, quarrying, polluting industries, major hydro projects [GK] | Tree felling, hotels and resorts, road widening [GK] | Ongoing agriculture, organic farming, rainwater harvesting [GK] |

# Supreme Court orders on mining
- **banned inside NPs and WLS** (barred by WPA 1972)
- Impermissible **within 1 km** of every NP/WLS boundary
- Larger ESZ notified → larger buffer prevails
- Mining **within 10 km of wetland conservation reserves** also needs wildlife clearance (7 Aug 2026)
- **Clearance:** Projects affecting wildlife vetted by the **Standing Committee of the NBWL**
- ⚠ **10 km** = guideline width; **1 km** = SC minimum

## Biosphere reserves — MAB programme, criteria, zonation and funding | from: S4
# Count and designation
- **Count:** India has **18 BRs**, about **91,425.11 sq km**
- **Designation:** UNESCO's **Man and the Biosphere (MAB) Programme** - intergovernmental, launched **1971**
  - Some keys/notes say 1972 (RPSC 2013 key) or 1973-74
  - BRs started by **UNESCO, not IUCN**
  - Nominated by national governments
- **India:** **IN-MAB Committee** (MoEF&CC) recommends sites (nomination route); States prepare Management Action Plans
- **Land status:** A BR does **not change legal status or ownership** of land
# UNESCO criteria
- Effectively protected, minimally disturbed **core** with viable populations of all trophic levels
- A **management authority** involving local (tribal) communities
- **Seville Strategy (1995):** Added social, cultural and economic needs + community participation
# Zonation
| **Zone** | **Status** | **Rules / activities** |
|---|---|---|
| Core | Legally protected, usually an NP or sanctuary under WPA 1972 | Strictly protected, no human activity; conservation and monitoring only |
| **Buffer** | Surrounds the core | regulated activities: research, education, training, eco-restoration, sustainable use, limited recreation |
| **Transition** | Outer interface of people and nature | Settlements, farming/agriculture, forestry, tourism and sustainable livelihoods |

# Funding scheme
- **CSS "Biodiversity Conservation"** (umbrella: Conservation of Natural Resources and Ecosystems):
  - **60:40** Centre:State for others
  - **90:10** for North-Eastern and (three) Himalayan States
  - Implemented by the State Forest Department
- **Integrated Management Plans:** Five years, in four BRs, since FY 2022-23

# Gene banks
- **Gene bank:** Cold store keeping germplasm (seeds, pollen, sperm, tissue cultures) under controlled temperature + humidity; each stored sample = an **accession**

| **Bureau (ICAR)** | **Genetic resources** | **Location** |
|---|---|---|
| **NBPGR** - runs the **National Gene Bank** | Plants / crops | **New Delhi** (PYQ) |
| NBAGR | Animals (livestock) | Karnal, Haryana [GK] |
| NBFGR | Fish | Lucknow, UP [GK] |
| NBAIM | Agriculturally important microorganisms | Mau, UP [GK] |

- **National Gene Bank, ICAR-NBPGR (New Delhi, set up 1996):**
  - About **4.7 lakh accessions**; 12 regional stations (CDI)
  - One of the world's largest plant gene banks
- **2nd National Gene Bank:** Announced in **Union Budget 2025-26**
  - Capacity **10 lakh germplasm lines**
  - Safety duplicate; also supports SAARC/BRICS nations
  - Budget presented by FM Nirmala Sitharaman (some notes wrongly credit PM Modi)
- **Field gene banks:** Living collections of vegetatively propagated or recalcitrant-seed crops (mango, coconut, banana) grown in orchards
- **International Rice Genebank:** IRRI, Los Banos, **Philippines**[GK]
- **Linked treaties:** **ITPGRFA** (farmers' rights), Nagoya Protocol

## Cryopreservation and the LaCONES frozen zoo, Hyderabad | from: S12
# Terms → cryopreservation
- **Medium:** Gametes + embryos stored in **liquid nitrogen at -196°C** (APPSC 2021 key)
  - Halts all metabolic activity → material viable for decades or centuries
- **Materials:** **gametes, embryos**, seeds, pollen, disease-free **meristems**, tissues, DNA
- **Significance:** "Newest application of technology" for preserving biotic parts; allows later IVF + research
# Institutions
| **Institution** | **Place** | **Role** |
|---|---|---|
| **LaCONES (Laboratory for the Conservation of Endangered Species)** | **CSIR-CCMB, Hyderabad** | India's frozen zoo; assisted reproduction + DNA banking |
| **National Wildlife Genetic Resource Bank** | At Nehru Zoological Park | Stores **DNA, tissues, gametes and embryos** of endangered and threatened wild animals |
