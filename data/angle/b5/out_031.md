=== ROW 99 | Air pollution - sources, pollutants, NAAQS, AQI, National Clean Air Programme, stubble burning

## Air pollution sources — types, terms and smog | from: S0
# 1. Natural vs anthropogenic
| **Basis** | **Types / examples** |
|---|---|
| **Natural sources** | Forest fires, volcanic eruptions, dust storms (APPSC 2025 answer: forest fires) |
| **Anthropogenic sources** | Vehicles, power plants, industry, biomass burning, agriculture (ammonia, methane), waste burning |

- **Main source in Indian cities: automobile emissions** (APPSC 2025)
  - Also asked as **Primary source in Indian cities: automobile emissions**
  - Delhi = city most often in the news for bad air
  - NCR: transport = largest primary source → **23% of winter load** (CAQM synthesis report, Jan 2026)
- **Vehicle/fuel combustion products:** CO, NOx, VOCs, PM, unburnt hydrocarbons, lead
# 2. Primary vs secondary
| **Type** | **Examples** |
|---|---|
| Primary pollutants (emitted directly) | CO, SO2, NOx (NO), PM, lead |
| Secondary pollutants (formed in air) | **O3, PAN** (**ozone**), photochemical smog, ammonium sulphate, ammonium nitrate |

# 3. Smog types and the Asian Brown Cloud
| **Smog** | **Formed from** | **Conditions** |
|---|---|---|
| Classical / industrial (London) | SO2, smoke, particulates + fog | Cold, humid |
| Photochemical (Los Angeles) | NOx + VOCs + sunlight → ozone, PAN | Warm, sunny |

- **Asian Brown Cloud:** Soot + black carbon + aerosols over South Asia and Indian Ocean in dry season; from biomass and fossil-fuel burning
- ⚠ **London** smog = cold + SO2; **Los Angeles** smog = warm + sunlight

## Pollutants → source and health effect; indoor pollution; bioindicators | from: S1, S2
# 1. Pollutant → health effect
| **Pollutant** | **Main source** | **Health effect** |
|---|---|---|
| **CO** | Vehicle exhaust, incomplete combustion, smoking | **Reduces oxygen-carrying capacity of blood**; giddiness, death |
| SO2 | Burning coal/oil, smelters, furnaces, volcanoes | Eye irritation, breathing problems; acid rain; secondary PM2.5 |
| NO2 / NOx | Vehicles, power plants, combustion | Respiratory illness; acid rain, smog |
| PM (PM2.5 / PM10) | Combustion, dust, vehicles | Respiratory and cardiovascular disease; deep lung penetration; PM2.5 crosses placenta - pre-term birth, low birth weight (AIIMS study, 3 Jun 2026) |
| O3 (ground-level ozone) | NOx + VOCs in sunlight | Asthma, eye irritation; crop damage |
| **Lead** | Motor vehicles, smelters | **Brain damage** |
| H2S | Sewage, refineries, volcanoes | Rotten-egg smell; eye, respiratory irritation |
| Ammonia | **Fertilisers and livestock (77-80%)** | Drives secondary particle formation |
| Magnetite particles | Combustion, vehicle brake wear | Found in human brain tissue; possible link to Alzheimer's |

> Exam angle: "pollutant from vehicles that reduces O2 carrying capacity of blood" = carbon monoxide.
# 2. SO2 and secondary particulates
- **SO2 sources:** Coal and oil burning (esp. **coal-fired power plants**), metal-ore smelting, vehicles, volcanoes
  - IIT Delhi study: curbing power-plant SO2 could prevent **~1.24 lakh deaths a year**
- **Secondary PM2.5:**
  - Ammonium sulphate (SO2 + NH3) → nearly **1/3 of India's PM2.5**
  - Ammonium nitrate → up to **50%**
# 3. Indoor air pollution
- **Sources:** Dust, smoke from solid fuels, mould, pollen, pesticides, gases (**radon**, CO), biomass and coal for cooking/heating, mosquito coils (smoke, PM)
- **Health:** COPD, lung cancer, heart disease → **women and children** suffer most
- **Intercity buses:** Cabin air 2-3 times WHO limits (IntrCity study, 15 Mar 2026)
# 4. Bioindicators
| **Bioindicator** | **Indicates** |
|---|---|
| **Lichens** | **SO2** pollution (APP PYQ) → absent/disappear where SO2 is high |
| **Mosses** | Accumulate heavy metals → indicate their deposition |

## Acid rain — definition, FIRST, causes and chemistry | from: S3
# 1. Definition, pH and FIRST
- **Acid rain:** Any precipitation (rain, snow, fog, mist, hail) with **pH below 5.6**
  - Typical acid rain **pH 4.2-4.4**
  - pH 7 = neutral; each pH step = tenfold change in acidity
- **Normal rain:** Slightly acidic (~5.6) → CO2 dissolves to weak carbonic acid
  - ⚠ **Trap:** CO2 alone does not make "acid rain" in the exam sense
- **Term coined:** **1872** by **Robert Angus Smith** (coal burning in Manchester)
# 2. Causes
- **Main culprits:** **SO2 and NOx** (APPSC 2018 answer: "SO2 and NO2"); main single factor = **SO2**
  - ⚠ **Trap:** CO, O3, CH4, CFCs are NOT causes

| **Gas** | **Anthropogenic sources** | **Natural sources** |
|---|---|---|
| SOx (SO2, SO3) | Coal-fired thermal power plants, smelting of metal sulphide ores, sulphuric acid plants, oil refineries | Volcanoes, seas/oceans, decomposition of organic matter |
| NOx (NO, NO2, N2O) | Vehicles, power plants, fossil-fuel burning, biomass burning | Lightning, forest fires, oceans |

> Exam angle: "which gases cause acid rain" and "excess of which causes acid rain" (RPSC: NOx + SO2 only).
# 3. Chemistry
- **Reaction:** SO2 + NOx + oxygen + water + photo-oxidants (sunlight-formed ozone) → **sulphuric acid (H2SO4)** + **nitric acid (HNO3)**
- **Relative share:** Sulphuric acid ~**two-thirds**; nitric acid ~**one-third** of acidity

| **Wet deposition** | **Dry deposition** |
|---|---|
| Acids fall with rain, snow, fog as sulphate, nitrate, ammonium, H+ ions | Acidic gases and particles stick to dust/smoke and settle on surfaces in dry areas |
| Occurs in humid regions | About **half** of atmospheric acidity returns this way (APP) |

## Acid rain — effects, Taj Mahal case and regions affected | from: S4
# 1. Effects by target
| **Target** | **Effect** |
|---|---|
| Lakes and fish | Acidification lowers pH; water becomes inhospitable to fish, frogs, insects |
| Forests and soil | Leaches calcium and magnesium; releases toxic aluminium that damages roots; trees become susceptible to cold, pests, drought |
| Buildings and monuments | Corrodes limestone and marble, metals, paint |
| Human health | Indirect: releases heavy metals from soil into drinking water |

# 2. Taj Mahal — term, zone and case
- **Marble cancer:** Acid rain decays **limestone and marble** (CaCO3) buildings, e.g. Taj Mahal → yellow/lustreless = "**marble cancer**"
- **Taj threat:** Mainly **SO2** (APPSC 2018) from industries around Agra, notably **Mathura refinery**
- **Taj Trapezium Zone (TTZ):** ~**10,400 sq km** trapezium around the Taj → protects Taj, Agra Fort and Fatehpur Sikri (2026)
  - Agra, Firozabad, Mathura, Hathras, Etah (UP) + Bharatpur (Rajasthan)
- **M.C. Mehta v. Union of India (1996):** SC barred coal/coke use by TTZ industries → shift to natural gas or relocation
# 3. Regions most affected
- **Nature:** Pollutants carried hundreds of km by wind → **transboundary** problem

| **Region** | **Feature** |
|---|---|
| **Scandinavia** | Sweden/Norway lakes ← UK and central European emissions |
| **NE USA-Canada** | Great Lakes |
| Industrial **China** | Classic case |
| **India** | **largest SO2 emitter globally (2022)** → over **20%** of anthropogenic emissions (CERA) |

## Air quality standards — NAAQS and WHO AQG 2021 data | from: S6
# 1. NAAQS
- **NAAQS:** Notified 1982; revised 1994 and **2009**
  - **12 pollutants** = AQI eight (PM10, PM2.5, SO2, NO2, CO, O3, NH3, Pb) + benzene, benzo(a)pyrene, arsenic, nickel
  - Ozone included for the first time in the 2009 revision
- **SO2 limits (NAAQS, Air Act, 1981):**

| **Averaging period** | **SO2 limit** |
|---|---|
| 24-hour | **80 ug/m3 (24-hour)** |
| Annual, urban | **50 ug/m3** |
| Annual, ecologically sensitive | **20 ug/m3** |

# 2. WHO AQG 2021 vs NAAQS
- **PM2.5:** WHO annual **5 µg/m³**; 24-hour **15 µg/m³** (NAAQS 24-hour PM2.5 = 60 µg/m³)
- **PM10:** WHO 24-h 45
- **NO2:** WHO annual 10 µg/m³

| **Pollutant** | **WHO AQG 2021 (annual)** | **India NAAQS (annual)** |
|---|---|---|
| PM2.5 | **5 µg/m³** | 40 µg/m³ [GK] |
| PM10 | **15 µg/m³** [GK] | **60 µg/m³** |

# 3. Health burden

- **Fossil-fuel air pollution:** ~**1.72 million premature deaths in India in 2022**; worldwide ~7 million deaths a year (WHO)

## BS-V skipped — Supreme Court deadline and why | from: S2
- **Leapfrog:** **BS-V skipped**; decided in **2016** → BS-VI for all new vehicles from **1 April 2020**
- **Why:**
  - Urgent urban air pollution (Delhi-NCR)
  - Close the gap with Europe in one step instead of two changeovers

=== ROW 101 | Water pollution - sources, eutrophication, Namami Gange, sewage treatment, groundwater contamination (arsenic, fluoride)

## Water pollution — sources and terms → meaning (DO, BOD, COD, solids) | from: S0, S1
# 1. Sources
| **Type** | **Examples** |
|---|---|
| **Point** | sewage outfalls, industrial effluent pipes |
| **Non-point** | agricultural runoff, urban runoff |
| **Sand mining** | raises turbidity + degrades river beds |

# 2. Oxygen demand and pollution level
| **Parameter** | **Measures** | **Clean-water (unpolluted) value** |
|---|---|---|
| DO | Oxygen dissolved in water | > 8.0 mg/L |
| **BOD** | Oxygen (O2) bacteria need to decompose organic matter | < 4.0 mg/L; **high BOD = very polluted** (a **heavily polluted water body**) |
| COD | Oxygen needed to oxidise all organic matter (biodegradable + non-biodegradable) | < 5.0 mg/L |
| NTU | Turbidity | - |

- **Measure of river pollution:** its **dissolved oxygen**
# 3. Other terms
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Coliform count** | (MPN/100 ml) shows **faecal contamination** |
| **Suspended solids** | retained on a filter; examples: **silt**, clay |
| **Dissolved solids** | salts that pass through the filter (e.g. **sodium**) |
| **Turbidity** | measured in **NTU** |
| **TDS** | = dissolved salts |

- ⚠ **Key:** silt = suspended solid (APPSC 2023)

## Eutrophication — causes, process and stages | from: S3
- **Cause:** Nutrient (**nitrogen, phosphorus**) enrichment
- **Triggers:** fertiliser runoff, sewage, detergents, aquaculture effluent, airborne nitrogen
- **Process:** enrichment → **algal blooms** → algae decay → decomposition uses up DO (**dissolved oxygen falls**) → hypoxia, fish kills
- **Stages:** oligotrophic → mesotrophic → eutrophic (→ hypereutrophic)
- **Ritual offerings:** e.g. dairy effluent can have BOD far above sewage → deplete DO fast (15 Apr 2026)
