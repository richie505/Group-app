=== ROW 70 | Energy efficiency - Bureau of Energy Efficiency, Energy Conservation Act, PAT scheme, star labelling, UJALA

## Acts and amendments → provisions | from: S0, S1
# 1. Energy Conservation Act 2001
- **Institution:** Set up **BEE**
- **Designated consumers:** Mandatory energy audits + norms for **designated consumers**
- **EE norms:** For appliances, equipment and buildings with connected load >**100 kW** or contract demand >**120 kVA**
- **Enforcement:** Penalties for non-compliance
# 2. Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act 2022
- **Passage:** **Energy Conservation (Amendment) Bill passed in December 2022** (PYQ 2021; APPSC 2021 PYQ)
  - Lok Sabha Aug 2022 → Rajya Sabha Dec 2022

| **2022 amendment** | **Provision** |
|---|---|
| **Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS)** | Legal basis to a **carbon market** - **Section 14AA** |
| Non-fossil obligation | Designated consumers must meet a minimum share of energy from **non-fossil sources **[GK] |
| Building codes | Extended to large **residential** buildings - Energy Conservation and Sustainable Building Code [GK] |
| Vehicles and vessels | Efficiency norms can be set [GK] |

- ⚠ EC Act = **2001**; BEE operational **1 March 2002**; amendment passed **December 2022**

## Organisations → status and role | from: S0, S6, S7, S8
| **Body** | **Status / role** |
|---|---|
| **BEE** | **Statutory body under the EC Act 2001**; **Ministry of Power**; operational from **1 March 2002**; aim: cut the energy intensity of the economy |
| **EESL** | Energy Efficiency Services Ltd, JV of Ministry of Power PSUs - implements UJALA, SLNP, National Efficient Cooking Programme |
| PCRA | Driving advice [GK]; launched **SAKSHAM** |

- **BEE programmes:** SEEI, PAT, Standards & Labelling, ECBC; runs CAFE and NECA
- **Mission link:** **NMEEE** is one of the 8 missions of **NAPCC (2008)**
- ⚠ NECA given by **BEE under the Ministry of Power** (not MNRE)

## Schemes and programmes → year and feature | from: S0, S3, S6, S7
# 1. PAT scheme - mechanism
- **Nature:** Market mechanism under **NMEEE** (NAPCC)
- **Targets:** energy-reduction targets for **Designated Consumers**
- **Trading:**
  - Over-achievers earn **ESCerts**; under-achievers (laggards) buy them
  - ESCerts traded on **power exchanges** (IEX)
- **Launch:** **2012** (PAT-I 2012-15)
- **Cycle I (2012-15):** **478** units in **8** sectors; now rolling cycles
- **Original sectors:** thermal power, iron & steel, cement, aluminium, fertilisers, petrochemicals, chlor-alkali, pulp & paper, textiles
- **Later added:** railways, DISCOMs, refineries, commercial buildings
- **Shift after CCTS:** as CCTS covers 9 heavy industries, **PAT will keep only 4 sectors - thermal power, railways, DISCOMs, commercial buildings** (LENS, Jun 2026)
# 2. Other schemes
| **Scheme** | **Year / body** | **Feature** |
|---|---|---|
| **UJALA** (Unnat Jyoti by Affordable LEDs for All) | **January 2015**; **EESL** | LED distribution |
| **Street Lighting National Programme (SLNP)** | **January 2015**; **EESL** | LED street lights replace sodium vapour |
| **ADEETIE (Jul 2025, ₹1,000 crore)** | Jul 2025 | MSME efficiency - audits + interest subvention: **5%** micro/small, **3%** medium; **60 clusters, 14 sectors** |
| **CAFE norms** | First notified **2017**, enforced by BEE | Average fuel-efficiency targets for M1 cars |
| **National Efficient Cooking Programme** | EESL | **20 lakh** induction stoves, **25-30% **cost advantage over traditional cooking, with MECS (APP) |
| **SAKSHAM (Sanrakshan Kshamata Mahotsav) 2026** | Launched by **PCRA at Chandigarh** (Jun 2026) | Fuel conservation and awareness |

## Carbon Credit Trading Scheme — design and coverage | from: S2
| **CCTS feature** | **Fact** |
|---|---|
| Notified | 2023 - India's **first mandatory compliance carbon market** (LENSD, 9 Sep 2026) |
| Administrator / Regulator | **BEE** / **CERC** |
| Mechanisms | Compliance (obligated industries earn or buy Carbon Credit Certificates) + Voluntary Offset |
| Coverage | **9 heavy industries**; GHG intensity targets for **208 units** |
| Goal / aim | **45% cut in GDP emission intensity by 2030** (vs 2005) |

- **Sep 2026: UK recognised India's CCTS** as qualifying carbon price under its **CBAM**
  - CBAM from **1 Jan 2027**; covers aluminium, cement, fertiliser, hydrogen, iron and steel
- ⚠ CCTS administrator = **BEE**; regulator = **CERC**; legal basis = **Section 14AA**

## Standards and labelling — star label, mandatory vs voluntary | from: S4
# 1. Programme basics
- **Run by:** **BEE** under the **Ministry of Power**
- **Launched:** 2006
- **Coverage:** **40+ equipment** (>40)
- **Rating scale:** **1-5 star**; each extra star saves about **10-15%** energy

| **Mandatory (examples)** | **Voluntary (examples)** |
|---|---|
| Refrigerators, deep freezers, ACs, fans, TVs, washing machines, geysers, LED lamps, TFLs, induction hob, solar inverter, **domestic LPG stove**, distribution transformers, chillers | General-purpose industrial motors, computers, microwave ovens |

# 2. Mandatory labelling from 2026
- **From 1 Jan 2026:** star labelling became **mandatory** (earlier voluntary) for:
  - Refrigerators (fridges), TVs, LPG stoves, cooling towers, chillers, deep freezers
  - Distribution transformers, grid-connected solar inverters
- **Penalty:** manufacturers now penalised if actual consumption deviates from the label

## Lighting and everyday conservation — lamp types and practices | from: S6, S7
# 1. Lamp types
| **Lamp** | **Key features** |
|---|---|
| **LED** | Highest efficacy, lowest energy use, longest life, no mercury, little heat - the energy-saving choice (PYQ 2025) |
| CFL | Better than incandescent but contains **mercury**; shorter life than LED |
| Incandescent ("bright bulb") | Most energy lost as heat |
| Sodium vapour | High efficacy, yellow light, poor colour rendering - older street lighting, replaced by LEDs under SLNP |

- **PYQ 2021 (A-R; APPSC 2021):** Govt urges replacing bright and CFL bulbs with LEDs because LEDs use less energy - **A and R true, R explains A**
- **Using LED:** Saves energy (APPSC 2025)
# 2. Everyday practices
- **Sustainability link:** Energy efficiency **reduces the need for new energy sources** (PYQ 2025; APPSC 2025)

| **Area** | **Practice** |
|---|---|
| Lighting / appliances | LEDs, star-rated appliances, switch off idle loads, avoid AC overuse (PYQ 2025) |
| Cooking | Induction cookstoves - National Efficient Cooking Programme (EESL); lids and pressure cookers [GK] |
| Driving | Correct tyre pressure, steady speed, engine off at signals - PCRA advice [GK] |
