## Birds → Great Indian Bustard, vultures, Amur falcon | from: S8, S10, S12
# 1. Great Indian Bustard
- **Identity:** **Great Indian Bustard (Ardeotis nigriceps)** = **Godawan**; Rajasthan's **state bird**
- **Status:** **Critically Endangered (CR)**; **fewer than 150** birds left
- **Range:** Mainly the **Thar (Rajasthan)**; also **Gujarat**, **Maharashtra**, **Karnataka**
  - AP: **Rollapadu WLS** (**Kurnool**; now **Nandyal**)
- **Main threat:** Collisions with **overhead power lines** in solar and wind zones
- **Conservation breeding:** **Jaisalmer, Rajasthan**
- **Programmes:** One of MoEFCC's **five new national species projects** + on the **Species Recovery Programme** (2026)
- !! Not in your sources: GIB captive-breeding centre names.
# 2. Vultures
- **Cause of decline:** Vultures ate cattle carcasses with veterinary painkiller **diclofenac** → **kidney failure (visceral gout)**
  - Decline noticed at **Keoladeo**, **1998**
  - Safe alternative: **meloxicam**
- **Breeding centre:** **Vulture Conservation and Breeding Centre, Pinjore (Haryana)**; **Vulture Safe Zones**
# 3. Amur falcon
- **Route:** Breeds in **SE Russia** and **northern China** → flies to **southern Africa**
- **Stopover:** Huge numbers in **Nagaland** - **Doyang reservoir**; **Pangti** = "Falcon Capital of the World"
- **Feature:** One of the longest raptor migrations; non-stop crossing of the **Arabian Sea**
- ⚠ **Diclofenac** = killer drug; **meloxicam** = safe alternative

## National Forest Policy 1952 vs 1988 → targets and objectives | from: S3
- **NFP 1952:** One-third of land under forest - **60% in hills**, **25% in plains**
- **NFP 1988 target:** **33%** of geographical area under forest/tree cover; **two-thirds (66%)** in hilly and mountainous regions (Jul 2026)
# 1. NFP 1988 objectives
- Ecological stability; conserving natural heritage
- Checking soil erosion and desert spread
- Massive afforestation and social forestry
- Fuelwood, fodder and minor forest produce needs of tribal and rural people
- **Massive people's movement involving women**
- Corridors between protected areas; fuel-efficient chulhas
> Exam angle: the statement "at least two-thirds of the country's land should be under forest" is **NOT true** (APPSC 2024). Two-thirds applies only to the hills.
- ⚠ **1952** hills = **60%**; **1988** hills = **two-thirds (66%)**; national target both ≈ one-third (**33%**)

## WPA amendments 2002, 2006 and 2022 → changes | from: S5, S6
# 1. 2002 amendment
- **Conservation Reserves:** Government-owned buffers and corridors (e.g. **Tsarap Chu**, HP; **Hesaraghatta**, Karnataka)
- **Community Reserves:** Private or community land, run by a **CRMC**
- Replaced **Indian Board for Wildlife** with **National Board for Wildlife**
# 2. 2022 amendment (Wildlife (Protection) Amendment Act 2022)
- **In force:** **April 2023**
- **Schedules:** Cut **6 to 4**; separate **vermin schedule deleted**; rationalised schedules + added **invasive alien species**
- **CITES:** Provisions brought into the Act (**management and scientific authorities**)

## Forest (Conservation) Act 1980 / Van Adhiniyam → provisions and 2023 amendment | from: S8
# 1. Original Act
- **Sec. 2:** **Prior Central approval** needed to de-reserve a forest, use forest land for non-forest purpose, or clear trees
# 2. 2023 amendment
- **Renamed:** **Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam, 1980** (w.e.f. **29 Dec 2023**)
  - Not repealed → both names appear in questions
- **Coverage - only two kinds of land:**
  - Land notified as forest under **IFA 1927** or other law
  - Land recorded as forest in government records **on or after 25 Oct 1980**
  - Excluded: land converted to non-forest use before **12 Dec 1996**
- **Exemptions from Central approval:**
  - Strategic linear projects within **100 km** of the **IB, LoC or LAC**
  - Eco-tourism facilities; zoos and safaris under WPA; minor infrastructure
- **Compensatory afforestation (CA):** **Non-forest land of equal area**; else **twice the area of degraded forest land** + **Net Present Value (NPV)** paid
- **Van (S.E.S.) Amendment Rules 2025:** Assisted natural regeneration + plantation on leased forest land = **"forestry activities"**; CA and NPV do not apply (Apr 2026)
# 3. Effect on tribal rights
- **FRA:** Amendment **silent on FRA**; no **Gram Sabha** consent mandated for new exemptions
  - Critics fear loss of tribal land in border States
- **FCR 2022:** Dropped the consent clause → **NCST-MoEFCC** dispute
- ⚠ In force **25 Oct 1980**; renamed w.e.f. **29 Dec 2023**; cut-off for converted land **12 Dec 1996**

## CAMPA and compensatory afforestation → provisions | from: S2
# 1. Compensatory afforestation (CA) basics
- **Principle:** CA replaces "land by land" and "trees by trees" on **non-forest land**
- **Exception:** Central PSUs, or projects with no non-forest land available → may use **twice the area** of degraded forest land
- **Payment:** Agency diverting forest land pays CA + **NPV** of lost forest ecosystem **before** the project starts
# 2. CAMPA under the CAF Act 2016
- **Law:** **Compensatory Afforestation Fund Act, 2016** - set up **National and State Compensatory Afforestation Funds**
  - **Non-lapsable**, interest-bearing public accounts (Jul 2026)
- **Two tiers:**
  - **National CAMPA:** Headed by **Union Environment Minister**; sets policy
  - **State CAMPAs:** Carry out the work
- **Split:** **90%** to State funds + **10%** to National fund (Feb 2026)
- **Uses:** Artificial and natural regeneration; wildlife management; catchment treatment; **relocating villages** out of PAs; human-wildlife conflict; forest-fire prevention
- **Tracking:** **e-Green Watch** portal
- **Utilisation:** Fund held **over ₹66,000 crore** by **2024**, but only **30-35%** used
> Exam angle: CAMPA exists at **both national and state levels**, which is correct. "People's participation is mandatory" under the 2016 Act is **wrong** (UPSC 2019).
- ⚠ **90 : 10** = State : National; non-forest land else **twice the area** of degraded forest

## Compensatory afforestation rules → land banks, Green Credit, consent | from: S3
# 1. Land banks and Green Credit link
- **Forest (Conservation) Rules 2022:** Introduced **Accredited Compensatory Afforestation**
  - Developers use plantations raised in advance (effectively **land banks**) to meet CA obligations
- **Green Credit Programme:** Afforestation raised under it can be exchanged for CA
  - Allowed under the **Van (S.E.S.) Rules 2023**
  - Guidelines issued: **16 Sep 2025**
# 2. Consent controversy
- **Dropped clause:** FC Rules 2022 removed the requirement of forest-dwellers' consent before diversion
- **Dispute:** **NCST** disputed this with MoEFCC
- ⚠ **FC Rules 2022** = land banks + dropped consent; **Van (S.E.S.) Rules 2023** = Green Credit swap
