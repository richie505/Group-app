=== ROW 54 | India's energy resources and import dependence - crude oil, coal, gas import share; energy security

## Issues → oil challenges, 2026 Hormuz shock and import bill | from: S2, S3
# 1. Core challenge & India's position

- **India's rank:** **3rd largest energy consumer** + **3rd largest oil consumer** (2026)

| **Angle** | **Issue** |
|---|---|

| Fiscal | Subsidy and excise pressure when prices spike |

# 2. 2026 supply shock & prices
- **Strait of Hormuz (between Iran and Oman):** Effectively closed by **Iran** in **early March 2026** (6 May 2026)
  - Carries ~**15 million bbl/day (~34% of world crude trade)** + a fifth of global **LNG** trade
- **Prices:** India's crude basket **$82.04/bbl in July 2026** (vs **$70.95** a year earlier); **Brent** ~**$91.84** in Aug 2026 (19 Aug 2026)
- **Macro impact:** Wider **trade deficit** + **inflation** (Jul 2026); **LPG** shortage + volatile **LNG** prices in 2026 (22 Apr 2026)
- **Refinery response:** Ordered under **LPG control order** to maximise yields → LPG output **35 to 54 TMT/day in five days** (2 Jul 2026)
# 3. Energy import bill
- **Crude import bill (July 2026):** **$13.7 billion**, up **41%** YoY (**PPAC**, 19 Aug 2026)
  - Volume **21.4 MMT** (**+13.3%**)
- **Product exports:** Petroleum product exports up **10%** to **5.5 MMT** in same month

## Import-substitution fuels → terms and benefits | from: S7
- **NITI Aayog's Methanol Economy aims:**
  - (i) Cut oil import bill
  - (ii) Cut **GHG** emissions
  - (iii) Turn coal reserves + municipal solid waste into methanol
  - APPSC-G1 2024 key: **all three**

| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Methanol** | Low-carbon **hydrogen-carrier fuel** |
| **DME (Di-Methyl Ether)** | **Two methanol molecules** minus one water molecule |

- **Methanol sources:** biomass, **high-ash coal**, **agri-residue**, **CO2 from thermal plants**, **natural gas**
- **Methanol uses:** blended transport fuel; replaces petrol/diesel in transport, **DG sets**, boilers; partly **LPG**, **kerosene** in cooking
- **DME use:** Can **substitute propane in LPG** + diesel in slow-RPM engines

| **Blend** | **Benefit (IYB)** |
|---|---|
| **M15** (15% methanol in gasoline) | At least **15%** cut in gasoline/crude imports; **20%** cut in PM, NOx, SOx |
| **20% DME in LPG** | Saves **₹6,000 crore/yr**; **₹50-100 per cylinder** |

## Coal gasification, CBM and UCG → terms and products | from: S4, S5
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Coal gasification** | Coal partly oxidised with oxygen + steam at high temperature and pressure → **syngas** (CO + H2 + CH4 + CO2) |
| **CBM** | Methane held in coal seams; partly free in joints, partly adsorbed in micropores; released by drilling wells + pumping out water |
| **"Sweet gas"** | CBM; few heavier hydrocarbons |
| **UCG** | **In-situ** process; air/oxygen + steam injected into coal seams → syngas underground |

- **Gasification products:**
  - Synthetic natural gas (SNG), methanol, **ethanol**
  - **Ammonia for fertilisers (urea)**, hydrogen, DME, ammonium nitrate, petrochemicals
  - Syngas can also run IGCC power plants
  - ⚠ **Trap (UPSC 2025 key):** Of ethanol, nitroglycerine and urea, **only two** (ethanol + urea) can be made this way
- **UCG:** Uses deep, thin or unmineable coal
  - Risk: groundwater contamination
  - MoEFCC expert panel **refused to waive the 300 m minimum depth** for UCG pilot projects
