## Acts, rulings and rules — spectrum, satcom, EMF | from: S0, S4, S5
# 1. Spectrum auctions and Supreme Court
- **TRAI (Feb 2026) recommendations:**
  - Auction **all available spectrum across nine bands**
  - Uniform **35% spectrum cap**
  - New-entrant net worth halved: **Rs 100 cr → Rs 50 cr** per service area
  - At reserve price ≈ **Rs 81,000 cr** (25 Feb 2026)
- **Supreme Court (Feb 2026):** Telecom companies **do not own spectrum**; IBC cannot restructure spectrum rights (Aircel case) (14 Feb 2026)
# 2. Telecommunications Act 2023
- **Telecommunications Act 2023:** Licensing → **authorisation** (light-touch)
  - DoT's July 2026 rule for infrastructure providers: all telecom data and logs **stored in India** (25 Jul 2026)
- **Satellite spectrum:** **Assigned administratively** (not auctioned) under the Telecommunications Act 2023's First Schedule
- ⚠ Terrestrial spectrum = **auctioned**; satellite spectrum = **assigned administratively**
# 3. Satcom licence and ITU rules
- **GMPCS conditions:** **geofencing**, no cross-border spillover, lawful surveillance, **terminals made in India within 5 years**
- **ITU rules:** Orbital slots first-come, first-served; de-orbit within **25 years** of mission end
# 4. EMF limits for towers
- **EMF limits for towers (India, since 2012):** **one-tenth** of ICNIRP limits
  - e.g. **0.92 W/m² at 1800 MHz** (ICNIRP: 9.2 W/m²)
  - ⚠ **Trap:** APPSC 2021 key gives 9.2 W/m², which is the ICNIRP value

## Satellite communication — GEO vs LEO and operators | from: S5

## Optical fibre — features, undersea cables and national fibre data | from: S6
# 1. Features
- **Speed:** **Fastest** data-transmission medium vs copper cable, radio waves, satellite signals (APPSC 2025 asked)
- **Main use:** **Communication**
  - Single-mode fibre = long distances
  - Multi-mode = short runs
> Exam angle: statement sets on TIR, light signals and single-mode fibre ("All of the above").
# 2. Undersea cables
- **Global network:** Fibre-optic lines on the seabed, ~**1.5 million km** (Sep 2025)
  - Optical repeaters every **60-80 km**
  - Carry nearly **99%** of international internet and telecom traffic
  - Cheaper + lower-latency than satellites
- **CANI:** Chennai-Andaman & Nicobar Islands submarine OFC, ~**2,300 km**, laid by BSNL, inaugurated **August 2020**
# 3. National fibre
- **NOFN:** Planned to link all **2,50,000 Gram Panchayats** using existing PSU fibre (BSNL, RailTel, Power Grid)
  - Funded by **USOF**; uses **GPON** technology
  - Est. cost **Rs 20,000 cr**
  - Later renamed BharatNet
- **PM Gati Shakti NMP assets (2026):**
  - ~**13.5 lakh route km** of PSU OFC
  - 43,000 route km state/private OFC
  - **8.26 lakh towers**
  - **30.15 lakh BTSs**

## Policies → vision, objectives and targets (NPIT 2012, NPE 2019) | from: S1, S3
# 1. NPIT 2012 - vision and objectives
- **NPIT 2012:** ICT as a driver of growth, jobs, governance reform and inclusion
- **Twin goals:**
  - Full power of ICT **within reach of the whole of India**
  - India as **Global Hub and Destination for IT and ITES Services by 2020**
