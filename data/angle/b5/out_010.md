=== ROW 45 | Applications of space technology - agriculture, disaster management, telemedicine, tele-education, navigation

## Remote sensing — definitions, types and what it measures | from: S0
# 1. Definition and types
- **Remote sensing:** Information about Earth's surface **without physical contact**, from reflected/emitted radiation
| **Basis** | **Types** |
|---|---|
| Source of energy | **Passive** (solar radiation - Resourcesat, Oceansat) vs **active** (own signal - **RISAT** radar) |

- ⚠ **Active** = own signal (**RISAT**); **passive** = sunlight (Resourcesat, Oceansat)
# 2. Microwave / SAR and InSAR
- **Microwave/SAR:** **Microwave (radar/SAR)** bands see through cloud and darkness - used for **cloud-covered areas**
  - E.g., Assam's planned flood satellites may carry SAR (State cloudy half the year) (25 Mar 2026)
- **InSAR:** (Interferometric SAR) compares phase of two or more radar images of the same place to **map ground deformation**
  - Uses: earthquakes, land subsidence, landslides, volcanoes, glacier movement
  - APPSC 2024 / PYQ: statement A correct
  - **NISAR** detects ground movement to the **centimetre** - land subsidence, earthquakes, glaciers
# 3. What can be measured
- Land cover, vegetation, **chlorophyll**, forest cover, surface and groundwater prospects
- Snow and glaciers, soil, wetlands, mineral/hydrocarbon zones
- Crop area and **yield** (YES-TECH)

## Terms → meaning: spectral indices and GIS | from: S1, S4
# 1. Spectral indices
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **NDVI** | **Normalized Difference Vegetation Index** = (NIR - Red) / (NIR + Red); basis: healthy vegetation reflects strongly in NIR; range -1 to +1; high values = healthy green vegetation |
| **Vegetation Condition Index (VCI)** | Derived from NDVI; used for drought declaration (2024-25) - mandal-level in AP |
| **NH Green Cover Index 2025-26** | (NHAI + NRSC) India's first satellite-based green-cover assessment of highways; uses **chlorophyll detection** over about 30,000 km in 24 States |

- **NDVI:** Most widely used index for **operational drought assessment** (PYQ)
# 2. GIS components
| **Component** | **Examples **[GK] |
|---|---|
| **Hardware** | Computer, **digitizer**, scanner, GPS receiver, plotter |
| **Software** | GIS packages (e.g., QGIS - used in APSAC workshops) |
| **Data** | **Spatial** (vector: points, lines, polygons; raster: satellite images) + **attribute** tables |
| **People and methods** | Analysts, procedures |

- **Data layers:** Each theme (roads, soils, land use, water) = a layer; **overlay** analysis combines them
- **Situational awareness:** Real-time layers for disaster response, flood alerts, shoreline mapping

=== ROW 46 | Space debris, space situational awareness and international space law

## Space debris — definition, sources, terms and FIRSTs | from: S0
# 1. Definition, sources, scale
- **Definition:** All **non-functional man-made objects**, incl. fragments, in orbit or re-entering
- **Sources:** (UPSC)
  - Defunct satellites, **spent rocket stages**
  - **Fragmentation** from collisions and explosions (main source)
  - **ASAT tests**, tools lost on spacewalks
- **Scale:** **Over 1.2 million** objects larger than 1 cm (**ESA Space Environment Report 2025**); a 10-cm object can shatter a satellite
# 2. Terms → meaning
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Kessler Syndrome** | Debris density so high that collisions create more debris in a self-sustaining **cascade** → orbits unusable; named after NASA scientist **Donald Kessler (1978)** |
| **MMOD** | (micrometeoroids and orbital debris) top risk in **LEO** |

# 3. India's ASAT test
- **Mission Shakti (27 March 2019):** DRDO **kinetic-kill** ASAT test destroying Indian satellite **Microsat-R** at about **300 km** - raised debris concerns (UPSC)

## Rules and mitigation → re-entry rules, DFSM 2030, POEM, ADR | from: S2, S3
# 1. India's new re-entry rules (2026)
- **India's new re-entry rules (2026):** (24 Aug 2026)
  - Warn aircraft and ships in re-entry area **at least 45 days** before re-entry
  - Clearance from any foreign State whose territory/**EEZ** holds the re-entry site
  - Turns IADC + UNCOPUOS soft law into domestic rules
- **Gap:** No international duty-of-care standard for orbits; licensing conditions differ → operators pick permissive regimes (1 Apr 2026)
# 2. Debris mitigation measures
| **Measure** | **Fact** |
|---|---|
| **Debris Free Space Missions (DFSM) by 2030** | India committed at the **2024 IADC meeting** to make all its missions debris-free |
| **POEM** | ISRO tests **drag-augmentation devices** (inflatable sails) so it re-enters within months, not decades |
| **POEM-4** | (SpaDeX/PSLV-C60 mission) **re-entered and fell into the Indian Ocean on 4 April 2025** (PYQ, UKPSC 2025) |
| **PS4 de-boost** | PSLV-C62 (Jan 2026) planned to **restart PS4** to de-boost it into re-entry trajectory after payload injection (12 Jan 2026) |
| **Active Debris Removal (ADR)** | Servicer craft with robotic arm or net to capture a defunct Indian rocket body; in testing at **URSC** |

## Treaties → year, core rule and India's status | from: S4
| **Instrument** | **Core rule** | **India's status** [GK] |
|---|---|---|
| **Outer Space Treaty 1967** | Space free for all; **no national sovereignty/appropriation**; Art. VI national responsibility incl. private actors; **Art. VII liability**; **Art. IX** due regard/environment | **Party** (ratified 1982) |
| Rescue Agreement 1968 | Return of astronauts and objects | Party |
| **Liability Convention 1972** | Absolute liability for damage on Earth; fault liability in space | **Party** |
| **Registration Convention 1975** | Register space objects with UN | **Party** |
| **Moon Agreement 1979** | Moon as common heritage of mankind | **Signed, not ratified** |

- ⚠ **Liability Convention**: **absolute** liability on Earth, **fault** liability in space
- ⚠ **Moon Agreement** = only one India **signed but not ratified**
- **Sovereignty:** "The Outer Space Treaty does not recognise sovereignty in outer space" - landing at a lunar site gives no ownership (5 Aug 2026)
- **ATLAC:** UN working group building consensus on lunar landing-site coordination + debris rules
