# 1. S. Chandrasekhar
- **Chandrasekhar limit:** Maximum mass of a stable **white dwarf** (~**1.4 solar masses**)
  - Beyond it → star collapses into a neutron star or **black hole**
  - Hence "principle of black hole" answers point to him (UPPSC 2011)
- **Famous work:** Radiation of energy from stars, esp. **white dwarfs**
- **Books:** Ten books incl. The Mathematical Theory of Black Holes
- **Profile:** **Nephew of C.V. Raman**; US citizen from **1953**; guided 50+ PhDs
- **Named after him:** NASA's **Chandra X-ray Observatory** (1999)

- Firm linking LANs in Mumbai, Bengaluru and New Delhi uses a **WAN** (APPSC 2019)

| **Topology** | **Layout** | **Resilience** |
|---|---|---|
| Bus | All nodes on one backbone cable | Backbone break stops the whole network [GK] |
| Star | All nodes to a central hub/switch | One node failing is isolated; hub failure stops all [GK] |
| Ring | Each node linked to two neighbours | One break can disrupt the ring [GK] |
| Mesh | Every node linked to every other | Most resilient; costliest (n(n-1)/2 links) [GK] |
| Tree | Hierarchy of star networks | Branch fails if its parent fails [GK] |
