=== ROW 58 | Oil and natural gas - basins, refineries, strategic petroleum reserves, gas grid, city gas distribution

## Petroleum geology → terms and meaning | from: S0, S2
- **Origin:** Oil formed in **Tertiary** period

| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Source rock** | Organic-rich shale where oil forms |
| **Reservoir rock** | Porous, permeable sandstone or fissured limestone |
| **Cap rock** | Impervious bed that seals the trap |
| **Traps** | Anticlines, fault traps, salt domes; most reserves in anticlines + fault traps; commercial oil at **crests of anticlines** |
| "Wet gas" | Associated gas |
| "Dry gas" | Non-associated gas |
| "Sour gas" | H2S-rich gas |
| **Conventional gas** | In permeable sandstone |
| **Unconventional gas** | In impermeable sandstone, shale or coal seams; needs costly technology |

- **Layering by density (top down):** **gas, then oil, then water**
- **Composition:** Petroleum = **90-95% hydrocarbons**
- ⚠ **Wet** = associated; **dry** = non-associated; **sour** = H2S-rich

## Electricity Act 2003 and regulators → year and role | from: S3

  - Set up **CEA**, Regulatory Commissions, **APTEL** (Appellate Tribunal)

|---|---|

| SERCs | members appointed by State Govt; fix retail tariffs for all consumer classes, license, enforce standards, settle disputes |
