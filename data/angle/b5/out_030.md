## NDCs and Panchamrit → targets, years and progress | from: S0, S1
# 1. NDC targets by version
| **Commitment (base year 2005)** | **INDC 2015 (for 2030)** | **Updated NDC, Aug 2022 (for 2030)** = **NDC 2.0 (2030)** | **NDC 3.0 (for 2035)** | **Progress** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cut in emissions intensity of GDP | **33-35%** below **2005** levels by 2030 | **45%** | **47%** | **36% achieved by 2020** |
| Non-fossil share of **installed** electric capacity | about 40% of cumulative electric power **installed capacity** by 2030 | **50%** | **60%** | **crossed 50% in June 2025**, five years early |
| Additional carbon sink from extra forest and tree cover | **2.5-3 bn t CO2e** (**2.5-3 billion tonnes CO2e**) by 2030 | 2.5-3 bn t CO2e (unchanged) | **3.5-4.0 bn t CO2e** | **2.29 bn t added** so far |

- **Base year:** **2005** (APPSC 2019); target year **2030**
- **Aug 2022 intensity target:** **45%** (APPSC 2022)
- **Process:** MoEFCC prepares NDCs; **Union Cabinet** gives final approval
# 2. NDC versions → date and feature
| **Version** | **Date** | **Feature** |
|---|---|---|
| **INDC** | Submitted to UNFCCC in **October 2015**, before COP21 | **Four of the eight NAPCC missions** focus on adaptation |
| **Updated NDC** | Communicated **August 2022** | Formalised the Panchamrit; added **Mission LiFE** (sustainable lifestyles) as qualitative element |
| **NDC 3.0 (2031-35)** | Approved by **Union Cabinet in March 2026** | Embedding **LiFE**; scaling up adaptation (coasts, agriculture, Himalayan states); new, low-cost climate finance |

- ⚠ **Trap:** Some notes write "30-35%" and "40% of power generation"; correct = **33-35%** and **installed capacity**
> Exam angle: the target is non-fossil **installed capacity**, not generation. UPSC notes say "40% of power generation", which is wrong.
- **Global status (31 Dec 2025):** India and Argentina = only G20 members without a 2035 NDC; **128 Parties** (~78% of emissions) had filed
# 3. Panchamrit — the five COP26 pledges
- **Announced:** By the PM at **COP26, Glasgow, 1 Nov 2021**

| **#** | **Pledge (announced at Glasgow, 1 Nov 2021)** | **By** | **Status** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **500 GW** non-fossil capacity | 2030 | in progress |
| 2 | **50%** of energy needs/requirement from renewables (NDC: met as 50% of installed capacity) | 2030 | Achieved **June 2025**; **~51% achieved (Sept 2025)** |
| 3 | Cut projected emissions by **1 billion tonnes** | 2030 | ongoing |
| 4 | Cut emissions intensity **45%** from 2005 | 2030 | 36% achieved by 2020 |
| 5 | **Net zero** | **2070** | long-term |

- **Memory hook:** **500, 50, 1, 45, 2070**
- **NDC link:** Two pledges (45% and 50%) entered the Aug 2022 NDC
> Exam angle: memory hook 500-50-1-45-2070. The trap is capacity versus generation.
# 4. Progress data
- **Emissions intensity:** Fell **36%** below 2005 levels by 2020
- **Non-fossil capacity:** **51.5%** (262.74 of 509.64 GW) in Nov 2025; ~**52%** in 2026
- **Generation gap:** Only ~**25% of power generated** is non-fossil, due to storage shortage (26-27 Mar 2026)
  - ⚠ **Capacity** crossed **50%**; **generation** only ~**25%**

## Net zero 2070 — terms, LT-LEDS transitions and Net Zero Portal | from: S2
# 1. Terms → meaning
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Net zero** | Remaining GHG emissions balanced by removals (forests, carbon capture) → net additions zero |
| **CCS/CCUS** | **Carbon Capture (Utilisation) and Storage** captures CO2 from industrial processes |
| **LT-LEDS** | Long-term low-emission strategy; submitted to UNFCCC at **COP27 (Nov 2022)** |

# 2. Net-zero target
- **India's target year:** **2070**, announced at **COP26 (2021)** (APPSC 2024; key left blank)
  - Later than most developed countries' 2050 → defended on **CBDR** + development grounds
- **Pathway:** Relies on forest and tree cover + newly announced **CCUS technology pathways** (27 Mar 2026)
- **Supporting missions:** National Green Hydrogen Mission; Nuclear Mission
# 3. LT-LEDS — seven transitions
- low-carbon electricity systems
- low-carbon transport
- urban adaptation and efficient buildings
- decoupling growth from emissions in industry
- CO2 removal
- forest and vegetation cover
- low-carbon finance
# 4. Net Zero Portal
- **Net Zero Portal (Sept 2026):** Voluntary disclosure of corporate net-zero targets
  - Scope 1 and 2 reporting encouraged; Scope 3 voluntary
  - Launched with a **NAPCC Dashboard** tracking the national missions

## Carbon market — CCTS and Green Credit Programme: Acts, sections, administering bodies | from: S6
# 1. Act / scheme → year → provision
| **Scheme** | **Legal basis / launch** | **Administrator** |
|---|---|---|
| **Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS)** | Notified **June 2023** under **Section 14AA** of the **Energy Conservation Act, 2001** (amended **2022**) | Bureau of Energy Efficiency, under the Ministry of Power |
| **Green Credit Programme (GCP)** | Launched **Oct 2023**, under **EPA 1986** | **Indian Council of Forestry Research and Education (ICFRE)** = **ICFRE, Dehradun** |

# 2. CCTS
- **Status:** India's **first mandatory compliance carbon market** (Indian Carbon Market); designed to meet the **45%** intensity target

| **Mechanism** | **Design** |
|---|---|
| **Compliance** | Compliance mechanism for energy-intensive sectors: obligated entities get **GHG Emission Intensity (GEI) targets**; those beating them earn tradeable **Carbon Credit Certificates**, those missing them must buy |
| **Offset** | **Offset mechanism for voluntary projects in 10 sectors** (e.g. agriculture, waste); approved methodologies (**nine** published) |

- **Coverage:**
  - GEI targets notified **Oct 2025** for **aluminium, cement, chlor-alkali, and pulp and paper** → **282 obligated entities**
  - Now **7 sectors**, **~490 industries**, **~477 Mt CO2e**; only China, EU and South Korea cover more
- **UK recognition:** CCTS = qualifying carbon price under UK **CBAM (from 1 Jan 2027)** → avoids double charging of Indian exports
# 3. Green Credit Programme
- **Green Credit Programme - launch, activities, administering body:** see table above
- **Nature:** **Market-based incentive for voluntary environmental actions**
- **Activities:** Tree planting, water management, sustainable agriculture, waste management, air-pollution reduction, mangroves, ecomark, sustainable building
- **Compensatory afforestation:** GCP afforestation exchangeable for CA obligations; guidelines **16 Sep 2025**
- **Listing:** Among MoEFCC initiatives (Environment chapter, IYB 2026)
- !! Not in your sources: current GCP methodology/credits issued.
- ⚠ CCTS = **Energy Conservation Act** / **BEE**; GCP = **EPA 1986** / **ICFRE**

=== ROW 98 | Environmental pollution - types, sources and impacts (overview)

## Pollution — types, classification and terms | from: S0
# 1. Pollution type → source → impact
| **Pollution type** | **Major primary source (APPSC 2025 key)** | **Key impacts** |
|---|---|---|
| Air | **Industrial emissions** (SO2, NOx, PM); vehicle exhaust | Respiratory and heart disease; acid rain, poor visibility |
| Water | **Sewage discharge**; industrial effluents | Water-borne disease, eutrophication, fish kills |
| Soil | **Chemical fertilisers and pesticides**; industrial waste | Loss of fertility, toxic crops, soil fauna imbalance |
| Noise | **Construction**, traffic, loudspeakers | Hearing loss, stress, sleep loss |

- **Air pollution deaths:** ~**7 million** a year (WHO); household air pollution ~3.8 million
# 2. Pollutant classification
| **Basis** | **Types** | **Examples** |
|---|---|---|
| Form / persistence | Primary (as emitted) vs **secondary** (formed by reaction) | Plastics vs ozone, peroxyacyl nitrate |
| Existence in nature | Quantitative vs qualitative | CO2 vs insecticides, herbicides |
| Natural disposal | Biodegradable vs non-biodegradable | Sewage, heat vs plastics, DDT [GK] |
| Source geometry | Point (chimney) / line (vehicles) / area / diffuse (non-point) | Thermal plant; highway; mining area; farm runoff |

# 3. Terms → meaning / matched pairs
| **Term** | **Meaning / pair** |
|---|---|
| **Xenobiotics** | Synthetic chemicals foreign to living systems (pesticides, PCBs, plastics); resist degradation |
| **Marble cancer** | **Marble cancer - acid rain (SO2)** (APPSC 2023) |
| **Biomagnification** | **biomagnification - DDT** (APPSC 2023) |
| **Greenhouse effect** | **greenhouse effect - CO2** (APPSC 2023) |
| Lung cancer | Lung cancer - asbestos/particulates (APPSC 2023) |
