=== ROW 0 | Nature, scope and characteristics of science and technology; science vs technology

## Science and technology — definitions, types and FIRSTs | from: S0, S1, S2
# 1. Science vs technology

| **Basis** | **Science** | **Technology** |
|---|---|---|
| Aim | Understanding - "know-why" [GK] | Application - "know-how" [GK] |
| Output | Laws, theories [GK] | Products, processes [GK] |

# 2. Research types and invention vs innovation

| **Type** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Basic research** | curiosity-driven |
| **Applied** | solves a set problem |
| **Translational** | takes lab results to use |
| **Invention** | first creation of a product/process |
| **Innovation** | turning it into value in market or society |

- **Future:** lies in **interdisciplinary and translational research** (2026)
# 3. Science vs pseudoscience
- **Science:** **empirical, testable/falsifiable, reproducible, systematic, self-correcting**
- **Pseudoscience:** claims scientific status but cannot be tested
- **PYQ fact (UPPSC 2018):** per astrophysicist **J.V. Narlikar**, **astrology** has **not** been considered a science
  - Considered sciences: astronomy (celestial bodies), cosmology (origin and evolution of the universe), nanotechnology (1-100 nm)
> Exam angle: "which has NOT been considered a science" - astrology.
# 4. Branches of science and interdisciplinary fields

| **Basis** | **Types** |
|---|---|
| **Natural** | physics, chemistry, biology, earth, space |
| **Formal** | mathematics, logic, statistics |
| **Social** | - |
| **Applied** | engineering, medicine, agriculture |

| **Field** | **Combines** |
|---|---|
| Biotechnology | Biology + engineering [GK] |
| Geoinformatics | Geography + IT (GIS, RS, GPS) [GK] |
| Data science | Statistics + computing [GK] |
| Cyber-physical systems | Computing + physical systems via sensors (LENSD, 1 Sep 2026) |
| **ESSO (2007, MoES)** | ocean science and technology + atmospheric and climate science + geoscience + polar science and cryosphere |

## Scientific method and Technology Readiness Levels → stages | from: S1, S3
# 1. Scientific method - steps from observation to theory and law
- **Steps:** **Observation → question → hypothesis → experiment → analysis → conclusion → replication/peer review**

| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Theory** | well-tested explanation of why |
| **Law** | description (often mathematical) of what always happens, e.g. Newton's laws |

- ⚠ **Trap:** a theory does not "become" a law
# 2. Technology Readiness Levels - lab to market
- **TRL 1-9 (NASA origin):**

| **TRL** | **Stage** |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | basic principles → lab proof of concept |
| 4-6 | validation in lab and relevant environment, prototype |
| 7-9 | operational demonstration → **proven in actual use** |

- **ANRF Governing Board:** finances projects at **TRL 4 and above**, strategic technologies, a **Deep-Tech Fund of Funds**
- **Deep-tech start-up eligibility:** notified Feb 2026 (Inter-Ministerial Board, convener JS DPIIT)

=== ROW 1 | Relevance of science and technology to day-to-day life - health, food, transport, communication, household

## Food and health terms — definitions and types | from: S0, S2, S7

| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Dialysis** | recommended when the **kidneys fail** (APPSC-GS 2019); removes wastes and excess water from blood |
| **haemodialysis** | machine filter |
| **peritoneal dialysis** | peritoneum as membrane |
| **Food additives** | chemicals added **intentionally** during production, processing or storage to improve the qualities of food (APPSC-GS 2019) |
| **Adulteration** | intentionally adding inferior/harmful substances |
| **Irradiation** | ionising radiation destroys harmful organisms **without altering nutrition** |
| **Fortification** | adding micronutrients |

- **Additive types:** preservatives, colours, emulsifiers, sweeteners, flavour enhancers (MSG)
- **Adulteration effects:** acute poisoning, allergies, nutritional deficiency
- ⚠ Additives = added **intentionally** to improve food; adulterants = inferior/harmful substances
- **Fortification examples:** Iodised salt; rice with iron, folic acid and B12; oil and milk with vitamins A and D; FSSAI "+F" logo
- **Irradiation features:**
  - Cuts need for chemical additives and refrigeration
  - Radiolytic products cause negligible chemical change
  - DAE: **25 food irradiators** installed in PPP mode
  - Gamma rays extend shelf life of **potatoes and onions to 8 months** without cold storage
- **General science scope - PYQ practice only:** syllabus has no separate general-science section, but APPSC and other exams keep asking basic physics, chemistry, biology and human biology → these questions are filed here
  - Physics: motion, light, sound, heat, electricity, magnetism, units
  - Chemistry: elements, compounds, acids and bases, metals, everyday chemicals
  - Biology: cells, plants, animals, genetics, evolution
  - Human body: organs, blood, nutrition, vitamins, hormones

=== ROW 2 | Science, Technology and Innovation Policy - 1958 Resolution, 1983, 2003, 2013 policies and the STIP draft

## S&T policies → year, Prime Minister, core focus; FIRSTs | from: S0

| **Policy** | **PM** | **Core focus / core thrust** |
|---|---|---|
| **Scientific Policy Resolution, 1958** | Jawaharlal Nehru | India's **first** comprehensive S&T policy; "foster, promote and sustain" cultivation of science "pure, applied and educational"; national labs and institutions |
| **Technology Policy Statement, 1983** | Indira Gandhi | **Technological competence and self-reliance**; development, absorption and adaptation of technology; less import dependence (cut import dependence) |

| **Draft STIP, 2020** | Narendra Modi | India's **5th** national S&T policy; decentralised, **bottom-up** consultation; self-reliance (**Atmanirbhar Bharat**), open science, gender equity |

- **SPR 1958:** came during the **Second Plan (1956-61)**
  - Same year **DRDO** formed by merging TDE, DTDP and the Defence Science Organisation
- ⚠ **1958 – 1983 – 2003 – 2013 – 2020** = **Nehru – Indira Gandhi – Vajpayee – Manmohan Singh – Modi**

## Policy objectives and provisions — STP 2003, Decade of Innovation, STI 2013, STIP 2020 | from: S1, S2
# 1. S&T Policy 2003
- **STP 2003 objectives:**
  - Sustainable development, poverty alleviation, quality of life
  - Promotion of research, innovation and **technology transfer**
  - **Optimal utilisation (use) of existing physical and knowledge resources** also a stated objective
  - ⚠ **Key:** all four listed objectives were correct (UPPSC 2008)
# 2. Decade of Innovation (2010-2020)
- **Declaration:** India declared **2010-2020** the **"Decade of Innovation"**
- **Vehicle:** **National Innovation Council (2010)** under **Sam Pitroda**
- **Goal:** inclusive innovation; state and sectoral innovation councils
# 3. STI Policy 2013
- **STI 2013 targets:** (STI Policy 2013)
  - Raise India's share of global publications from **3.5% to over 7%**
  - **Quadruple** papers in top 1% journals
  - Enhance private R&D; new PPP structures; high-risk innovation
- **STI Policy 2013 (new):**
  - "**small idea-small money**" and "**Risky Idea Fund**"
  - **Fund for Innovations for Social Inclusion**
  - ~**10 high-impact sectors** for direct STI intervention
  - Traditional knowledge through modern science
  - Agricultural R&D policy left to **ICAR**
# 4. Draft STIP 2020
- **Draft STIP 2020 (new):**
  - **Vision:** **Atmanirbhar Bharat** - India among the **top three scientific superpowers** in the coming decade; people-centric STI ecosystem
  - **Double FTE researchers, GERD and private share of GERD every 5 years**
- **Open Science Framework:**
  - "**One Nation, One Subscription**" for pan-India journal access
  - Publicly funded data under **FAIR** terms
  - Portal **INDSTA** (Indian Science and Technology Archive of Research)
- **Funding:** National Research Foundation; hybrid funding with private capital and CSR; **STI Development Bank** (corpus fund for long-term strategic areas)
- **Institutions and research quality:**
  - **Research and Innovation Excellence Frameworks (RIEF)** (BPSC 2025)
  - **STI Observatory**; **STI Policy Institute**
  - **Strategic Technology Board** linking strategic departments
  - Traditional Knowledge Systems and grassroots innovators
  - Diaspora portal; "Diplomacy for S&T"
- **Equity:** women, marginalised and LGBTQ+ inclusion; day-care at funded labs
- **Governance:** periodic review and **exit strategies**
- **Outcomes:** ONOS and RIEF were outcomes
  - ⚠ **Trap (BPSC 2025 PYQ):** **Atal Innovation Mission was NOT an outcome** of STIP 2020 - AIM set up by **NITI Aayog in 2016** as a separate initiative promoting innovation among students
- ⚠ **top five** by 2020 = STI 2013; **top three** in the coming decade = STIP 2020
> Exam angle: "which is NOT an outcome/objective" of a given policy.
