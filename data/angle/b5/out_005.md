## NeGP 2006, e-Kranti and core infrastructure | from: S1, S2
# 1. National e-Governance Plan 2006
- **Formulated by:** **DeitY + DARPG**
- **Approved:** **18 May 2006**
- **Structure:** **27 Mission Mode Projects + 10 components** (MMPs)
  - The 27 split = **9 Central, 11 State, 7 Integrated**
  - Later expanded to **31 MMPs**
- **Vision:** make all government services accessible to the common man **in his locality** through common service delivery outlets
  - Transparency, reliability and efficiency at affordable cost
- **Integrated MMPs:** **e-Courts**, **e-Procurement**, **National Portal** (india.gov.in)
- **Other MMPs:**
  - **CCTNS** (MHA, **2009**)
  - **e-Panchayat**
  - **IVFRT** (MHA, **2010**); extended till **March 2031** as IVFRT 3.0 (Apr 2026)
> Exam angle: "NeGP 2006 identified how many MMPs?" - **27** (APPSC 2025); e-Kranti = 44.
# 2. e-Kranti
- **MMPs:** **44 MMPs**; essential pillar of Digital India
- **Features:** integrated (cloud, mobile, geospatial), outcome-oriented, fast and agile

| **Item** | **NeGP (1.0)** | **e-Kranti (NeGP 2.0)** |
|---|---|---|
| Place | Stand-alone national plan | A pillar of Digital India |

- ⚠ **27** MMPs (9 Central, 11 State, 7 Integrated) → **31** → **44** under e-Kranti
# 3. Core infrastructure
- **Core infrastructure:** **SDC** (State Data Centres), **SWAN**, village-level **CSCs**

| **Pillar** | **Role** |
|---|---|
| SWAN | Converged backbone network for data, voice, video across the state |
| SDC | Hosts state e-gov applications and data on common secure IT infrastructure |
| CSC | Village-level front-end delivery outlets |

- **Outcome:** together enable seamless **G2G, G2C, G2B** services
- **MeghRaj (GI Cloud):** now provides government cloud hosting

## DigiLocker, e-Sign and secure messaging — features and legal backing | from: S4, S5
# 1. DigiLocker
- **DigiLocker (MeitY):** cloud locker for documents (Feb 2015); **56.06 crore users**, **970.70 crore documents** (IYB 2026)
- **Account:** cloud account per citizen **linked to Aadhaar**
- **Documents:**
  - **Driving licence and vehicle RC** (linked with MoRTH in **Sept 2016**)
  - **CBSE mark sheets** (from **2015**)
- **Legal validity:** DigiLocker-issued documents = equal to originals under **IT Act 2000** + **2016** Digital Locker Rules
# 2. e-Sign
- **e-Sign (MeitY; CDAC's e-Hastakshar among issuers):** Aadhaar-based signing of e-documents; **110.26 crore** e-Signs issued, **29.07 crore** by CDAC (IYB 2026)
- **Technology:** **PKI-based**; gives **non-repudiation** in a legally acceptable form
- **Authentication:** Aadhaar eKYC/OTP of the holder
- **Legal backing:** IT Act 2000
# 3. Secure messaging and e-mail
- **GIMS:** Government Instant Messaging System = India's in-house alternative to WhatsApp/Telegram for secure government use (APPSC 2018)
  - Released as the **Sandes** app, built by **NIC**
- **NIC Mail:** official government e-mail
  - Integrated with CollabFiles office suite + e-Sign + Bhashini + Parichay single sign-on
- !! Not in your sources: regional-language (IDN) government e-mail services and which states launched them.
- ⚠ **GIMS** = **Sandes** app (NIC)

=== ROW 28 | Netiquettes - digital etiquette, digital literacy, misinformation and fake news

## Terms → meaning (online behaviour and information disorder) | from: S1, S2, S3, S5
# 1. Online behaviour terms
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **Cyber venting** | Using websites to pour out one's grievances (JPSC PYQ) |
| **Doxxing** | Publicly sharing someone's private information (real name, location, workplace) online without consent to harm or shame (LENSD, 1 Aug 2026) |
| Cyberbullying | Repeated online harassment/humiliation, esp. of children (IYB 2026 lists it among online risks) |
| Trolling | Deliberately provocative or abusive posts to upset or derail discussion [GK] |
| Cyberstalking | Repeatedly monitoring or contacting a person online against their will [GK] |
| **Nomophobia** | Irrational fear of being without one's mobile phone (no signal, battery, balance) (APPSC PYQ) |
| **Digital footprint** | Trail of data a person leaves online; keeping it low reduces exposure to fraud |

# 2. Information disorder
| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| Misinformation | False content spread without intent to deceive (innocent mistakes, rumours) (UPSC notes) |
| Disinformation | False content deliberately created to achieve political/social/economic goals (UPSC notes) |
| Malinformation | Genuine information shared to cause harm (e.g., leaked private data) [GK] |
| **Filter bubbles/echo chambers** | Algorithms prioritise content by user preference → isolate users from other viewpoints |

- ⚠ **Mis**information = no intent; **Dis**information = deliberate; **Mal**information = genuine info shared to harm
- **Fake news:**
  - Causes: **profit motive** (clicks, ad revenue)
  - Effects: **polarisation** + **misinformation cascades**

## Netiquettes and digital citizenship — dos and don'ts | from: S0, S1
- **Core rule:** engage in **constructive online dialogue**, respect privacy
  - "digital freedom should be accompanied by digital responsibility"
- **Nature:** the table below lists general netiquette conventions

| **Medium** | **Do** | **Don't** |
|---|---|---|
| Email | Clear subject line, polite greeting, check recipients before sending | Type in ALL CAPS (reads as shouting), forward chain mails |
| Social media | Verify before sharing, respect others' privacy, credit sources | Post others' photos/data without consent, troll or abuse |
| Online meetings | Join on time, mute when not speaking, use chat/raise-hand | Talk over others, record without permission |
| Forums | Read rules/FAQs, stay on topic, disagree civilly | Spam, flame, cross-post repeatedly |

- **Responsible digital citizen:** identifies misinformation, **verifies sources**, protects personal data, respects privacy, engages in civil discourse
  - One misleading forward → panic, violence or reputational damage
- **Rights side:** online grievance redressal, e-consultations, open government data, e.g. AP **Grama and Ward Sachivalayam** system
