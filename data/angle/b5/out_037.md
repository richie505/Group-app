=== ROW 114 | Health and environment - vector-borne and water-borne diseases, air pollution and health, antimicrobial resistance, One Health

## Vector-borne diseases — disease → vector → pathogen | from: S0
- **Definition:** Vector-borne needs an intermediate vector (mosquito, flea, sandfly, mite)

| **Disease** | **Vector** | **Pathogen (type)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Malaria** | **Female Anopheles** | ***Plasmodium*** spp. (protozoan) - falciparum (deadliest), vivax, malariae, ovale; destroys **RBCs** |
| **Dengue** | **Aedes aegypti** (also albopictus) | Virus (flavivirus); mainly hits **platelets** (APPSC 2023) |
| Chikungunya | Aedes (incl. **Aedes albopictus** / aegypti) | Chikungunya virus (alphavirus) |
| Zika | Aedes | Virus |
| Yellow fever | Aedes; **Haemagogus** (e.g. ***Haemagogus leucocelaenus***) in forests | Yellow fever virus |
| Japanese Encephalitis | **Culex** | JE virus; pigs amplify it |
| **Kala-azar** (visceral leishmaniasis) | **Sandfly** - NOT a mosquito (APPSC-G2 2025) | ***Leishmania donovani*** (protozoan) |
| Lymphatic filariasis | **Culex quinquefasciatus** | Wuchereria bancrofti (nematode) |
| Sleeping sickness | **Tsetse fly** | Trypanosoma brucei (protozoan) |
| Scrub typhus | Chigger **mite** | Orientia tsutsugamushi |
| Plague | Rat flea | Yersinia pestis |

- **Human Plasmodium species:**
  - P. falciparum - most lethal, cerebral malaria
  - P. vivax, P. malariae, P. ovale, P. knowlesi
- **Odd-one-out:** **Leishmaniasis** spread by sandfly, not mosquito (APPSC 2025 PYQ)
> Exam angle: odd-one-out "not spread by mosquito" - kala-azar, sleeping sickness, scrub typhus.
- **2024 PYQ key:**
  - Zika - Aedes
  - Yellow fever - Haemagogus
  - Chikungunya - Aedes albopictus
  - Malaria - Anopheles

## Antimicrobial resistance — causes, terms, new antibiotic, policy | from: S6
- **Causes:** **Overuse/misuse** of antibiotics in **humans and animals**/livestock/farms
  - Self-medication, unregulated veterinary drugs
  - Poor sanitation, poor waste control, **pharma effluents**
  - → a One Health problem; complicates India's infectious-disease burden
- **Biofilms:** Bacterial communities in a self-made slimy matrix → shield from antibiotics and immunity
- **WHO Bacterial Priority Pathogens List (2024):** Critical = carbapenem-resistant Acinetobacter baumannii, Enterobacterales, rifampicin-resistant TB
- **Nafithromycin ("Miqnaf"):** India's first indigenous **macrolide** antibiotic, for community-acquired bacterial pneumonia
  - Launched **20 Nov 2024** by **Dr. Jitendra Singh**
  - Developed by **Wockhardt** with **BIRAC** support
  - ⚠ **Trap:** **NOT Sun Pharma** (PYQ key: statements 1 and 3)
