## Climate treaties → adoption, entry into force, parties and FIRSTs | from: S0, S2, S4
# 1. Treaty → dates

| **Treaty** | **Adopted** | **In force** | **Parties / note** |
|---|---|---|---|

- **UNFCCC:** Opened for signature at the **Rio Earth Summit (June 1992)** (UNCED)
  - Some notes say "adopted at Rio"
- **Kyoto status:** **First legally binding climate agreement**
- **Kyoto non-parties:** USA never ratified; Canada withdrew in 2011 (some notes say 2012)
# 2. Objective and goal
- **UNFCCC objective:** Stabilise GHG concentrations at a safe level → prevent dangerous anthropogenic interference
- **Paris temperature goal:** Hold warming **well below 2°C**, pursue **1.5°C** above pre-industrial (APPSC 2025)
- ⚠ UNFCCC adopted in **New York** (**May 1992**), opened for signature at **Rio** (**June 1992**)
- ⚠ Secretariat **Bonn** (UNFCCC) vs **Geneva** (IPCC)

## UNFCCC → Annex groups, CBDR-RC and bodies | from: S0
# 1. Annex parties

| **Group** | **Members** | **Obligation** |
|---|---|---|
| Annex I | Industrialised countries + economies in transition | Lead emission cuts |
| Annex II | OECD members of Annex I [GK] | Provide finance and technology to developing countries [GK] |
| Non-Annex I | Developing countries incl. India [GK] | No binding cuts; special care for LDCs, SIDS |

# 2. CBDR-RC
- **CBDR-RC (Common But Differentiated Responsibilities and Respective Capabilities):** Foundational principle of the **UNFCCC**
  - Developed countries (**Annex I**) caused most historical emissions → must **take the lead**
  - Special consideration for **LDCs and SIDS**

| **Text** | **Reference** |
|---|---|
| Rio Declaration, 1992 | **Principle 7**[GK] |
| UNFCCC | **Article 3.1**[GK] |
| Paris Agreement | **Article 2.2**, "in the light of different national circumstances" [GK] |

# 3. Bodies
- **COP:** Supreme body, meets annually
  - Two instruments: Kyoto Protocol (CMP) + Paris Agreement (CMA)
- **SBI and SBSTA:** Meet mid-year in Bonn
- ⚠ CBDR-RC = **Principle 7** (Rio) = **Article 3.1** (UNFCCC) = **Article 2.2** (Paris)

## Kyoto Protocol → commitment periods, mechanisms and gases | from: S2
# 1. Commitment periods

| **Period** | **Commitment** |
|---|---|
| **1st (2008-2012)** | 37 countries; average cut of about **5% below 1990** levels (some notes: **5.2%**) |
| **2nd (2013-2020)**, via **Doha Amendment** (2012) | **18% below 1990** by 37 countries; Doha Amendment entered into force 31 Dec 2020 |

# 2. Binding targets
- **Annex I only:** Binding targets for industrialised + transition economies
  - Called on industrialised nations to cut emissions significantly (APPSC 2019)
# 3. Flexible mechanisms

| **Flexible mechanism** | **How it works (between)** | **Unit earned** |
|---|---|---|
| **International Emissions Trading** | trading among Annex I countries (Annex I and Annex I) | Traded allowances |
| **Clean Development Mechanism (CDM)** | Annex I invests in projects in **developing** countries | **Certified Emission Reductions (CERs)** |
| **Joint Implementation (JI)** | Annex I earns from projects in **other Annex I** countries (Annex I and Annex I) | Emission Reduction Units (**ERUs**) |

# 4. Gases covered
- **Six gases:** CO2, CH4, N2O, **HFCs, PFCs, SF6**
  - NF3 added for the 2nd period
- ⚠ **CDM** → CERs (developing country); **JI** → ERUs (other Annex I)

## Carbon markets → CDM to Paris Article 6 | from: S3
# 1. Terms

| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| Carbon credit | **1 credit = 1 tonne CO2-equivalent** reduced, avoided or removed |
| CERs | CDM units (**Kyoto Protocol** market mechanisms) |
| **Article 6.2** | Bilateral cooperative approaches (e.g. India-Japan JCM) |
| **Article 6.4** | UN-run **Paris Agreement Crediting Mechanism (PACM)**; **Replaces the Kyoto CDM**; credits called **A6.4ERs** |
| **Corresponding adjustments** | Prevent double counting |

- **CDM:** Earned CERs for projects in developing countries such as India → being replaced by **Article 6.4 PACM**
# 2. Operationalisation and firsts
- **Operationalisation:** Standards finalised at **COP29** → **Article 6.2 and 6.4 (PACM)** rules finalised → UN-supervised carbon market replacing CDM; Article 6 fully operational
- **First Article 6.4 credits:** Issued by UN to a clean-cooking project in **Myanmar** (with South Korea)
  - Under scrutiny: credits possibly overstated **7-14 times**
- **India-Japan:** **Joint Crediting Mechanism** signed **Aug 2025** (Art. 6.2) + Rules of Implementation adopted (Jan 2026)
- ⚠ **6.2** = bilateral (JCM); **6.4** = UN-run PACM

## Paris Agreement → Articles, NDCs and withdrawals | from: S4, S5
# 1. Articles

| **Article** | **Provision** |
|---|---|
| **Art. 13** | **Enhanced Transparency Framework:** Common reporting via Biennial Transparency Reports; first due end-2024 |
| **Art. 14** | **Global Stocktake:** Every **5 years**; **First concluded at COP28 (2023)** → world "severely off-track" → UAE Consensus |
| **Art. 28** | Party may withdraw 3 years after entry into force; takes effect 1 year after notice |

# 2. NDCs and legal nature
- **Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs):** Each country sets its own targets, submitted/updated every **5 years**
  - "NDC 3.0" due for COP30 (2025)
- **Legal nature:** Legally binding treaty, but **binding elements are procedural** (prepare, communicate, report NDCs)
  - **NDC targets are nationally determined and not legally binding** → **no country** (USA, China, India or EU) legally bound to its emission targets
  - ⚠ **Trap:** "legally binding for all" is true only of the procedural duty; answer = "None of these" (APPSC-G2 2025 key)
- **Ratchet mechanism:** Each new NDC must be a progression beyond the previous one, informed by the Global Stocktake
# 3. Data - India's NDC

| **NDC** | **Target** |
|---|---|
| 2015 target | 33-35% emissions-intensity cut (2005 base) by 2030 |
| Updated (2022) | **45%** by 2030 |
| New NDC for 2031-35 | **47% by 2035** |
| Achieved | **36%** reduction by 2020 |

- **Rejoined 19 Feb 2021**
- USA **left the Paris Agreement a second time in January 2026**
- Presidential memorandum ends US participation in **UNFCCC, IPCC, ISA and IRENA**

## IPCC reports → key findings (SR1.5, SRCCL, SROCC, AR6) | from: S16, S17
# 1. SR1.5 (2018)
- **Released:** **October 2018** (SR1.5)
- **Warming so far:** Human activity already caused ~**1.0°C** above pre-industrial
- **1.5°C pathways:**
  - Net human-caused CO2 to fall ~**45% from 2010 levels by 2030**
  - **Net zero around 2050**
  - Cuts must be **deep, rapid and sustained**; gradual or limited cuts not enough

| **Ocean and Cryosphere (SROCC)** | **September 2019** | Oceans absorbed **>90%** of excess heat; marine heatwaves more frequent (coral bleaching, fisheries); sea level rising from thermal expansion and ice melt |

# 3. AR6 Synthesis Report - global findings
- **Climate Change 2023: Synthesis Report:** Released **20 March 2023**; closed the AR6 cycle
- **Key findings:**
  - Human influence has **unequivocally** warmed the climate
  - Surface temperature already **1.1°C** above pre-industrial (2011-2020)
  - GHG emissions at their **highest in human history**
  - **1.5°C threshold is likely to be crossed in the early 2030s** without immediate, deep cuts
- **Mitigation pathways:**
  - Emissions must **peak before 2025**
  - **Net zero by mid-century**
  - **70-85%** of global electricity from renewables by **2050**
- **Carbon budget:** ~**500 GtCO2** from 2020 for a 50% chance of 1.5°C (AR6 WG I)
# 4. AR6 - India-relevant findings
- **Heat:** More frequent and intense **heatwaves**; **India is warming faster than the global average** (IPCC AR6, cited Apr 2026 on heat stress in textile hubs)
- **Monsoon & sea level:** **Erratic monsoon** → severe floods; **sea-level rise** threatens **Mumbai and Chennai**
- **Himalayas:**
  - High mountains warming at **twice the global average**
  - **Gangotri Glacier** retreated **1,700 m since 1935**
  - Black carbon on snow lowers albedo → faster melting
  - Up to **25% of mountain species** may face extinction by 2100
- **Oceans:** Marine heatwave frequency **doubled (1982-2016)**
- ⚠ SR1.5: ~**1.0°C** so far; AR6 SYR: **1.1°C** (2011-2020)
