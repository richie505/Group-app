h: Total internal reflection (TIR)
b: Conditions: light goes from **denser → rarer** medium **and** angle of incidence **> critical angle**; light is reflected **with no loss of intensity**.
b: Refractive index: **Diamond (~2.42) > sapphire (~1.77) > dense flint glass > glass (~1.5) > fused quartz > water > air** – so TIR is possible **diamond → glass**, **diamond → air**, never air → water/glass.
b: Higher refractive index = **slower light** (among **glass**, water, air, vacuum: slowest in glass).
t: Phenomenon / device | Principle
   row: **Optical fibre** | **TIR** (successive TIRs let light follow a bent, zig-zag path)

   row: **Mirage** (desert) | **TIR** in hot, rarer air near the ground
   row: Glass beads on traffic signs | **TIR** (A and R both true, R explains)
   row: Crack in glass seen obliquely | Looks **silvery** (TIR)
   row: **Rainbow** | **Dispersion + (total) internal reflection** + refraction
   row: Stick bent in water | **Refraction** (not scattering)
   row: Sea looks blue | **Reflection of sky + scattering** of light by water
h: Optical fibre communication
b: Thin strand of **glass (silica)** or plastic; **core (higher n) + cladding (lower n)**; signal travels as **pulses of light (light waves)**.
b: **Fastest** medium vs copper, radio, satellite; main use **communication** (telecom, internet); very **low power**, **free from radio-frequency interference**.

h: What a cryogenic engine is
b: **Cryogenics:** Greek **'Kryos'** (frost) + 'genic' (to produce); temperatures below about **-150°C**; liquid nitrogen, hydrogen and oxygen are the usual cryogenic liquids – the technology of obtaining/using liquid O2 or H2 at industrial level is **cryogenics**.
b: **Propellants:** **liquid hydrogen (LH2, -253°C) as fuel** and **liquid oxygen (LOX, -183°C) as oxidiser** – not chlorine, fluorine, sulphur, helium or nitrogen; exhaust is only **water vapour**; specific impulse ~**450 s** (highest).

b: **Other cryogenic use:** **liquid nitrogen (-196°C)** stores **bull semen** for artificial insemination.
