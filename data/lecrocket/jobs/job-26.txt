h: Meaning
b: **Democratic decentralisation:** transfer of power to **elected local bodies** so that people take part in the decisions that affect them – the scheme that "ultimately came to be known as **Panchayati Raj**".
b: **Term coined by:** the **Balwant Rai Mehta Committee** (report November 1957), not the Ashok Mehta Committee.
b: **Local self-government / a constitutional provision for local government** = an exercise in **democratic decentralisation** – not delegation, de-concentration, federalism or a confederation.
t: Term | Meaning
   row: **Democratic decentralisation** | Power passed to **elected** local bodies
   row: De-concentration | Field offices of the same government get work; no transfer to elected bodies

   row: Confederation | Union of sovereign States
   row: Direct democracy | Citizens decide **without representatives**

b: **Fundamental objects:** **people's participation in development** and **democratic decentralisation** – code "**1 and 3 only**".
b: **Political accountability** and **financial mobilisation** are not its fundamental objects (they may follow from it).
b: **Main purpose:** to enable people to **participate in developmental administration** – not merely to raise farm output, jobs or political awareness.
b: **Why adopted:** **to decentralise the power of democracy**.
b: **What PRIs brought:** **formal representation of the weaker sections** in village governance (SCs, STs, women through reservation) – not eradication of untouchability, land to depressed classes or spread of education.
b: **Democratic governance:** **participation of the public in power** – power rests with the people and is exercised by them or their representatives.

x: Code trap: a single-answer version of the item has two right options (participation + democratic decentralisation) – the full answer is both.
b: **Critique of Indian democracy since 1947:** for most people **sovereignty is reduced to the mere exercise of the franchise** (voting once in five years) – the case for decentralised, participatory democracy.
