h: Heat and states of matter
b: **Boiling points:** mercury ~357 °C > water 100 °C > benzene 80 °C > ethanol 78 °C (mercury boils last).

b: **Changes of state:** **sublimation** = solid → gas; **deposition** = gas → solid.
b: **Convection** – air-conditioners and heaters circulate air (cool air sinks, warm air rises).
b: **Specific heat** = heat to raise **1 g by 1 °C**.
b: **Water expands ~9% on freezing** – a corked glass bottle full of water **breaks** in the freezer.
b: **Supercooling** = cooling a liquid **below its freezing point** without it solidifying.
b: **Water has a high specific heat** – a watermelon stays cool longer than an apple; kerosene ≈ **0.5 cal/g °C** (water 1); **1 calorie ≈ 4.2 J** (4.186).
b: **Dissolved salt raises the boiling point and lowers the freezing point** (colligative): salt in boiling water stops the boil until it reaches the new, higher boiling point; **rock salt melts ice** on roads.
b: **Pressure lowers the melting point of ice** (regelation) – ice skating; **melting ice stays at 0 °C** (latent heat of fusion); **triple point of water = 0.01 °C** (273.16 K, ~611.657 Pa).
b: **Mothballs (naphthalene)** work by **sublimation**.
b: **Anomalous expansion:** water contracts from 0 °C to **4 °C (maximum density)** and expands above it.
b: **Sound-proofing:** rockwool, wood, laminated glass – **not iron sheets**; **copper-bottomed pans** – copper conducts heat better than steel; a **small LPG burner** saves gas.
h: Motion, force and gravity
b: **Free fall:** without air resistance all bodies fall with the same g – brick and feather land **together** (Galileo; Apollo 15).
b: **Friction** acts in brakes, striking a match, walking – **not** in planets' revolution (gravity in near-vacuum).
b: A ball released from an orbiting satellite keeps its orbital speed and **revolves in the same orbit**.
b: **Motion** = change of position with time (strictly, change of position = displacement); **Newton's 1st law** (inertia) – rest or uniform motion unless an unbalanced force acts; 2nd F = ma; 3rd action–reaction.
b: **g decreases with depth** below the surface (and with height) – weight **decreases slightly** in a deep mine.
b: **Displacement** = shortest path between initial and final position (distance = actual path); at **maximum height** the velocity of a body thrown up is 0.
b: **Friction** slows a moving object; **writing with a pencil** = friction (not gravity); gravity – planets, rolling rock, tides.
b: **Levers** are of **3 classes** (fulcrum, load or effort in the middle).
b: **Conservation of momentum:** no external force → momentum cannot change (a man on frictionless ice can move by whistling – printed key: both true, R not the explanation).
h: Pressure, fluids and gases
b: **Boyle's law:** V ∝ 1/P at constant T – an air bubble **grows** as it rises in a lake; **Charles's law:** V ∝ T at constant P.
b: **Bernoulli:** fast air blown between two sheets lowers the pressure there → the papers **come closer**.
b: **Surface tension** lets insects (water striders) walk on water.
b: **Floating:** stones in a boat displace their weight of water; thrown in, only their volume – the **lake level falls**.
b: **Density of water** ≈ **1 g/cm³** (1000 kg/m³), highest at 4 °C.
b: **Charles's law in daily life:** a balloon in **hot water swells** (air expands on heating).
n: NCERT Class 9 Physics
b: **Otto von Guericke (17th century):** pumped the air out of **two hollow metal hemispheres** – they could not be pulled apart, showing the force of **atmospheric pressure**.

h: Light and colour
b: **Visible spectrum (VIBGYOR):** wavelength rises from violet to **red** (orange > yellow > indigo > violet); an opaque object shows the colour it **reflects**.
b: **TV remote** = **infrared** LED.
b: **Refraction** = change of direction (bending) of light passing into another medium: tank looks **shallower**; **prism dispersion** (each colour refracted differently); **twinkling of stars** and **Sun seen ~2 min before sunrise/after sunset** = **atmospheric refraction**.
b: **Scattering:** **blue sky**, **red Sun at sunrise/sunset**; the **rainbow** is **dispersion**, not scattering; a **pinhole camera's** smallest aperture is limited by **diffraction**.
b: **Grass looks green** – it **reflects green**; **red** has the longest visible wavelength (~620–750 nm).
b: **Dispersion** = splitting of white light into seven colours by a prism.
b: **Primary colours of light:** red, green, blue – **yellow is not** one.
h: Electricity
b: **Rectifier:** AC → DC (inverter: DC → AC).
b: **Indian supply: 230 V, 50 Hz** – a **110 V heater** draws ~4× its power and **burns out** within seconds/minutes.
b: **Resistivity** depends on the material and temperature, **not on shape** (resistance does); insulators – practically **infinite** resistance; a **semiconductor's resistance decreases** on heating (metal's increases).
b: **Short circuit:** resistance ≈ 0 → **current increases heavily**; a **fuse** melts on heavy current.
b: **Galvanometer:** detects current and its **direction**; null detector in bridges; more sensitive, not more accurate, than an ammeter.
b: **Transformer** works on **mutual induction**.
h: Sound
b: **Sound** is a **longitudinal** mechanical wave: **speed rises with temperature** (~0.6 m/s per °C in air); **solids** carry both longitudinal and transverse waves; **cannot travel in vacuum** (ultrasound too).
b: **Loudness** depends on **amplitude**; **pitch** on frequency.
b: **Echo:** reflected sound must arrive ≥ 0.1 s later → reflector at least **17.2 m** away (344 m/s); **women's voices** have **higher pitch** (shorter, thinner cords).
b: **Frequency** depends only on the **source** (same in every medium); speed and wavelength change with the medium.
h: Magnetism
b: **Magnetic shielding:** put the instrument inside **soft iron of high permeability**.
