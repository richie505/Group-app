b: **N₂ > O₂ > Ar > CO₂**. **Nitrogen + oxygen = 99%** – the air near the surface is "mainly nitrogen and oxygen".
b: **Permanent gases** keep a constant proportion: nitrogen, oxygen, argon (and the noble gases); **variable:** CO₂, water vapour, ozone → answer **nitrogen**.
b: **Chlorine** is **not** a normal constituent of air; neon, helium and CO₂ are.
b: **Xenon = "Stranger Gas"** (Greek *xenos*), found by **Ramsay and Travers, 1898**.
b: Weather balloons are filled with **helium** (light, inert).

h: Change with height and with time
b: 99% Of the mass lies within 32 km. Moving up, **CO₂ and water vapour vanish first (~90 km)**, then **oxygen (negligible by ~120 km)**, while nitrogen continues → order of disappearance **CO₂ → O₂ → N₂**.
b: Light gases rise highest: the **exosphere** is mostly **hydrogen** (with helium).
b: **Early atmosphere:** **reducing** – methane, ammonia, hydrocarbons, little free oxygen; methane and hydrocarbons were oxidised and replaced by **carbon dioxide**; photosynthesis later made it oxygen-rich.

b: **Oxygen in the atmosphere:** free oxygen did not exist until about **2.4 billion years ago** (Proterozoic Eon, 2,500–541 mya, cyanobacteria); the **Phanerozoic Eon (541 mya – present)** has the present atmosphere.

b: **Maximum biodiversity** of all biomes (over half of known species) –.
b: Luxuriant growth = **hot, wet climate all year + intense sunlight**; the soils are **not fertile** (leached) and there are **no seasons**.
b: **Leaching** is the main soil problem; leaf litter **decomposes faster than in any other biome**, so the forest floor is often bare; plants reach the canopy by climbing (lianas) or as **epiphytes**.
b: **Amazon** = "**lungs of the planet**"; **Amazon fires, 2019** (Brazil; INPE recorded a sharp rise in fires). **Congo Basin** = 2nd-largest rainforest.
b: Tropical rainforest has the **highest share of the world's forest area** among the forest types (APSC 2017; FAO: tropical forests ≈ 45% of all forest).
b: Equatorial region = **megathermal** (hot all year) with **aerial streamlets** (climbers/epiphytes, water dripping from the canopy); it is **not sclerophyll** and not a region of high development.
h: Taiga and tundra
b: **Taiga = largest land biome**; it has a **very wide** (not narrow) annual temperature range – "incorrect statement"; moderate precipitation, snow in winter; acidic **podzol**; lowest productivity of forests; northernmost temperate biome.
b: **Tundra:** mosses and lichens, no trees, permafrost; it is wet because **low temperatures make evaporation slow** and permafrost blocks drainage. **Alpine tundra** lies above the tree line.
b: **Tree line:** the altitude/latitude beyond which trees cannot grow. Himalaya: **lower in the west** (about 3,300–3,600 m) than in the east (3,600–3,800 m), because the Eastern Himalaya is **nearer the Equator and the sea** (Bay of Bengal moisture) –.
h: Adaptations
b: Dry regions: smaller leaves **reduce transpiration**. Deciduous trees shed leaves in winter/dry season **to conserve water**. Desert animals = **xerocoles** (fossorial = burrowing; arboreal = tree-dwelling).

b: **Common names:** **alum** = potassium aluminium sulphate dodecahydrate; **aspirin** = acetylsalicylic acid; chalk = CaCO3; **barium nitrate** – oxidiser giving **green** flame in fireworks (strontium red, copper blue).
h: Acids and bases
b: **All acids contain hydrogen** (H⁺ in water); **pH = "potential of hydrogen"**; **NaOH + HCl → NaCl + H2O** = neutralisation, a **double displacement**.
b: **Acids** conduct electricity in water, taste sour, change indicators and **react with active metals** (H2); **litmus:** **purple** in neutral, **blue → red** in acid; **ammonia** turns red litmus blue (**weak base**).
h: Carbon and non-metals
b: **Graphite** = **inorganic** solid lubricant (lanolin, mineral oil, PTFE are organic).
b: **Sulphur** = common **fungicide**; **bromine** = only **non-metal liquid** at room temperature (mercury is a metal).
b: **Noble gases in air:** argon, neon, helium… – **radon** is the one usually said to be absent (strictly, traces near the ground); **dry ice** = **solid CO2** (sublimes at −78.5 °C) – vaccine transport.
h: Mixtures and colloids
b: **Aerosol** = tiny liquid droplets (or solid particles) dispersed in **air/gas** – fog, mist, sprays; **sea water** = a mixture (table salt, sugar, lime are compounds).
b: **Solubility in water (25 °C):** **glucose ~90 g/100 mL** > ammonium chloride ~38 > table salt ~36 > alum ~14.
b: **Butter** = water-in-fat **emulsion** (NCERT lists butter as a gel – liquid in solid); vinegar, sugar solution and honey are solutions.
h: Laboratory
b: **Titration** uses a **burette**; pipette transfers a fixed volume; **dry litmus + dry HCl gas → no change** (no ionisation without water); **turmeric** is a natural acid–base indicator (red-brown in base).
h: Industrial processes
b: **Haber process:** N2 + H2 → **ammonia** (iron catalyst, high T and P); **Contact process:** SO2 → SO3 over **V2O5** → **sulphuric acid**.
n: NCERT Class 10 Chemistry ch. 1-4
b: **Cations:** **positively charged ions** formed when an atom **loses electrons**, typically from **metals** (Na⁺, Ca²⁺); anions are negative ions formed by gaining electrons, typically from non-metals (Cl⁻).
b: **Actinides:** **radioactive** metallic elements – e.g. **uranium, thorium, plutonium** – used in **nuclear energy and weapons**.
b: **Sulphur (brimstone):** allotropes – **rhombic** (yellow crystals, stable at room temperature), **monoclinic** (needle-like, stable at higher temperatures) and amorphous **plastic** sulphur; used for **sulphuric acid** and **vulcanisation of rubber**.
b: **Anodising:** forming a **thick oxide layer on aluminium** – the clean aluminium article is made the **anode** and electrolysed with **dilute sulphuric acid**.
b: **Pyrometallurgy:** one of the **three main methods** of extracting metals from ores – **pyrometallurgy, hydrometallurgy and electrometallurgy**; its last step is **refining** (e.g. electrolytic refining of copper).
b: **Hydrometallurgy:** extracting metals from ores using **aqueous solutions** – **leaching** (dissolving the metal with acid, base or water, e.g. **gold leached with cyanide**), then **solvent extraction**.

h: Medicine and first aid
b: **Diuretics:** furosemide (loop), mannitol (osmotic), acetazolamide (carbonic-anhydrase inhibitor) – all increase urine.
b: **Catgut** (collagen from animal gut) = classic **absorbable suture**; silk and nylon are non-absorbable.
b: **Burns:** **1st degree** – epidermis only, redness and pain; 2nd – partial thickness, blisters; 3rd – full thickness.

b: **Wounds:** **abscess** = cavity with **pus**; hematoma = collected blood; **contusion (bruise)** = classic **closed wound**; **root canal therapy** saves an abscessed tooth.
b: **Dosage forms:** syrup = liquid; tablets, capsules, boluses = solid; suppositories and enemas are rectal – **ecbolics** (oxytocics) are injected.
b: **Burn first aid:** cool the part in **cold running water**; do not pull off stuck cloth or apply butter.
b: **Capsule shells** = **gelatin**; **Dettol** = **chloroxylenol** + α-terpineol; **KOH mount** clears skin scrapings for fungi/mites; **sulphur ointment** controls **mange**.
b: **Antipyretics** (paracetamol) lower fever; analgesics relieve pain; **bronchoscopy** examines airways – not a nervous-system test (EEG, myelography are).
b: **Anorexia nervosa, bulimia** = **eating disorders**.
h: Digestion
b: **Digestion:** pancreas – digestive enzymes **and** insulin; **small intestine** – most absorption; **gall bladder stores bile** (made by the liver).
b: **Enzymes:** **lipase** – fat; **amylase** – starch; **trypsin** – protein; **lactase** – lactose; **oesophagus** links pharynx to stomach.
b: **Digestive system** = breaks food down and **absorbs nutrients**.

h: Heart and blood
b: **Resting heart rate** of an adult = **60–100 beats/min** (~72).
b: **Sphygmomanometer** measures **blood pressure**.
b: **WBCs** (with platelets) are **~1%** of blood volume (buffy coat).

b: **Vessels:** **capillaries** = narrowest (5–10 µm, one cell thick); **aorta** = largest artery; **arteries** carry blood from the heart to the body; **pulmonary artery** carries CO2-rich blood to the lungs; **renal** – kidney, **hepatic** – liver, **coronary** – heart.
b: **Blood cells:** **erythrocytes = RBC** (formed in **red bone marrow**), **leucocytes = WBC** (colourless, no haemoglobin), **thrombocytes = platelets**.
h: Brain and nerves
b: **Right cerebral hemisphere** – **spatial abilities**, face recognition; left – language.
b: **Adult brain** ≈ 1.3–1.4 kg (~**3 pounds**); **axon** = the long fibre carrying impulses away from the cell body (dendrites short, branched); **Nissl's granules** are in the neuron cell body (not excretory); **trigeminal (V)** = largest cranial nerve, sensory + motor.
b: **Synapse** = junction (gap) between two neurons.
b: **Neurons** send and receive information; **medulla oblongata** – involuntary actions (BP, salivation, vomiting, **breathing**).
h: Skeleton and muscles
b: **Axial skeleton** = skull, vertebral column, ribs, sternum; **femur** = longest and strongest bone (tibia = lower leg).
b: **Ossification** = deposition of calcium and minerals on cartilage → bone.
b: **Tooth enamel** = hardest substance in the body – **calcium phosphate** (hydroxyapatite); **calcium + phosphorus** give bones their strength; **milk teeth** fall from **6–8 years** (most keys; strictly ~6 to 12).

h: Teeth, eye and senses
b: **Adult dentition (32):** **8 incisors, 4 canines, 8 premolars, 12 molars**.

h: Respiration
