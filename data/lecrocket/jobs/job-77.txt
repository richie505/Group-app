page title: Everyday Physics – Light, Sound, Electricity and Magnetism
h: Light and colour
b: **Visible spectrum (VIBGYOR):** wavelength rises from violet to **red** (orange > yellow > indigo > violet); an opaque object shows the colour it **reflects**.
b: **TV remote** = **infrared** LED.
b: **Refraction** = change of direction (bending) of light passing into another medium: tank looks **shallower**; **prism dispersion** (each colour refracted differently); **twinkling of stars** and **Sun seen ~2 min before sunrise/after sunset** = **atmospheric refraction**.
b: **Scattering:** **blue sky**, **red Sun at sunrise/sunset**; the **rainbow** is **dispersion**, not scattering; a **pinhole camera's** smallest aperture is limited by **diffraction**.
b: **Grass looks green** – it **reflects green**; **red** has the longest visible wavelength (~620–750 nm).
b: **Dispersion** = splitting of white light into seven colours by a prism.
b: **Primary colours of light:** red, green, blue – **yellow is not** one.
h: Electricity
b: **Rectifier:** AC → DC (inverter: DC → AC).
b: **Indian supply: 230 V, 50 Hz** – a **110 V heater** draws ~4× its power and **burns out** within seconds/minutes.
b: **Resistivity** depends on the material and temperature, **not on shape** (resistance does); insulators – practically **infinite** resistance; a **semiconductor's resistance decreases** on heating (metal's increases).
b: **Short circuit:** resistance ≈ 0 → **current increases heavily**; a **fuse** melts on heavy current.
b: **Galvanometer:** detects current and its **direction**; null detector in bridges; more sensitive, not more accurate, than an ammeter.
b: **Transformer** works on **mutual induction**.
h: Sound
b: **Sound** is a **longitudinal** mechanical wave: **speed rises with temperature** (~0.6 m/s per °C in air); **solids** carry both longitudinal and transverse waves; **cannot travel in vacuum** (ultrasound too).
b: **Loudness** depends on **amplitude**; **pitch** on frequency.
b: **Echo:** reflected sound must arrive ≥ 0.1 s later → reflector at least **17.2 m** away (344 m/s); **women's voices** have **higher pitch** (shorter, thinner cords).
b: **Frequency** depends only on the **source** (same in every medium); speed and wavelength change with the medium.
h: Magnetism
b: **Magnetic shielding:** put the instrument inside **soft iron of high permeability**.
n: NCERT Class 10 Physics ch. 10-12
