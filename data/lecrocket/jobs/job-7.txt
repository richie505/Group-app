b: **Viceroy:** **Ripon**; drafted by **Law Member C.P. Ilbert**.
b: **Aim:** remove the **disqualification of Indian magistrates** – let Indian **district magistrates and sessions judges** try **European British subjects** in **criminal cases**; Indians and Europeans **on par in criminal jurisdiction**.

h: Meaning

h: Reserved vs transferred
t: Subjects | Examples | Run by | Answerable to
   row: **Reserved** | **Administration of justice, police, land revenue**, finance, prisons, irrigation, control of the press | **Governor + Executive Council** | Not the legislature
   row: **Transferred** | **Local self-government**, education, public health, agriculture | **Governor + Ministers** | **Provincial legislative council**

b: **Dyarchy at the Centre** = the **1935 Act** (never worked); dyarchy in the **provinces** = **1919**; the 1935 Act abolished provincial dyarchy.
