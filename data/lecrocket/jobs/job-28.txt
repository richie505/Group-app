page title: Agreement on Agriculture – Pillars and Subsidy Boxes
h: Pillars
b: AoA negotiated in the Uruguay Round, in force **1 January 1995**. **Three pillars: market access, domestic support, export competition** – **anti-dumping and countervailing duties are NOT a pillar**.
b: "Agreement on Agriculture", "SPS Agreement" and "**Peace Clause**" = **WTO** terms, as are **amber, blue and green boxes**.
h: Subsidy boxes
t: Box | Covers | Treatment
   row: **Green** | Research, pest/disease control, infrastructure, public stockholding for food security, decoupled income support, environment, regional development | Minimal or no trade distortion; **no limits**
   row: **Blue** | "Amber box with conditions" – **direct payments under production-limiting programmes** | Trade-distorting but **exempt from reduction**; no spending limit
   row: **Amber** | Price- or quantity-linked support: **MSP**, power, fertiliser, seed, irrigation (measured as **AMS**) | Trade-distorting; **to be reduced** to de minimis
b: **Blue Box = "production-limiting subsidies that distort trade"**.
b: **No Red Box** in the AoA (traffic-light "red = forbidden" belongs to the SCM Agreement); **White, Pink, Yellow** are not boxes either.
b: **S&D box**: special and differential support for developing countries.
b: **India + China proposal (July 2017):** eliminate developed countries' **Amber Box (AMS)** support beyond de minimis first.
h: Mechanics
t: Term | Rule
   row: De minimis | Amber support allowed up to **5%** of production value (developed), **10%** (developing)
   row: Total AMS cut | **20% over 6 years** (developed); **13.3% over 10 years** (developing)
   row: Blue Box conditions | Payments on fixed areas and yields, or on **85% or less** of base production, or a fixed number of livestock
   row: Tariffication | Non-tariff barriers converted into tariffs; cuts **36% over 6 years** (developed), **24% over 10 years** (developing)
   row: Export subsidy cut | Developed: **36% by value or 21% by volume** over 6 years; developing 24% / 14% over 10 years
b: A-R: "India need not reduce subsidies under WTO" is **false** although India is a developing country (R true) – developing countries also had reduction commitments.
b: **Price-gap payment (MSP minus market price) paid directly to farmers:** keyed **Blue Box** by (direct payment); strictly, such deficiency payments are Amber unless tied to production limits.
n: De minimis 5%/10%); Ramesh Singh Indian Economy 10th ed. part 04 p.186-188 (traffic lights, "nothing like red box", blue = amber with conditions, S&D box)
