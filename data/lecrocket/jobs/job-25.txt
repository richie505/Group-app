h: First past the post vs proportional representation
b: **FPTP** (plurality): the candidate with **more votes than any other** wins – no 50% needed. Vote-seat distortion, e.g. **Karnataka 1994**: Janata Dal 116 seats on 36% votes.
b: **PR** gives seats in proportion to votes → **representation of minorities**; not needed where a **two-party system** has developed (UK). Rejected for the Lok Sabha: complex, fragmentation, **unstable governments**, no by-elections.
h: Types of elections and special constituencies
t: Type | Meaning
   row: General | All seats, at normal end of term
   row: **Mid-term** | After the House is **dissolved before its term** ends
   row: By-election | A single vacancy; within 6 months (Sec. 151A) – not if the remaining term is **under one year**
b: **Floating constituency**: no fixed geographical boundary – **Sangha (Sikkim)**: only registered **Buddhist monks** vote and contest (both statements correct).
b: **Security deposit** is forfeited if a candidate does not get **more than one-sixth** of the valid votes (Sec. 158).
b: **Minimum age to contest:** Lok Sabha/Assembly **25**, Panchayats and Municipalities **21**.

h: EVM and VVPAT
b: EVM = electronic vote-recording and counting device (not an electoral system). **First used: Parur (Kerala), 1982**; older EVMs recorded up to **3,840** votes – current ECI EVMs up to **2,000**.

h: NOTA
b: **PUCL v Union of India, 27 September 2013** – SC ordered a NOTA button on all EVMs (secret negative vote, **Art. 19(1)(a)**). NOTA introduced in **2013** – first used in the **Nov-Dec 2013 Assembly polls**; first Lok Sabha use **2014 = 16th Lok Sabha** (1.1% votes). The 12th-15th Lok Sabha polls had no NOTA, and it was not used in 2009.
b: Symbol: ballot paper with a **black cross**, designed by **NID Ahmedabad**. NOTA votes are **not counted as valid votes** for result/deposit; even if NOTA tops, the leading candidate **wins** – no fresh poll; using NOTA is **voting, not abstention**.
b: **Not** for **Rajya Sabha / Legislative Councils** (SC struck it down, **2018**) – only direct elections.
b: Old **Rule 49-O**: voter refused to vote before the Presiding Officer, noted in **Form 17-A** – broke secrecy.

h: Candidates and the RPA

t: Basis | RPA 1950 | RPA 1951
   row: Deals with | **Allocation of seats**, delimitation framework, voter qualifications, **electoral rolls** | Conduct of elections, disqualifications, **party registration (Sec. 29A)**, **corrupt practices**, election petitions
   row: Linked Articles | 81, 82, 170, 171, 325, 326 | 84, 102, 173, 191, 324, 329
b: **Sec. 14 / 15**: **President** (Lok Sabha) / **Governor** (Assembly) issue the formal notification on ECI's recommendation; the ECI notifies the schedule, scrutinises nominations, allots symbols. **Validity of an election** is settled only by the **courts**.

h: Rolls, machinery and parties
b: Rolls: four **qualifying dates** – 1 Jan, 1 Apr, 1 Jul, 1 Oct (not 1 December); **Special Intensive Revision** rebuilds the roll (**Bihar** SIR removed about 70 lakh names).
b: Officers: **CEO** (State), **DEO** = usually the **District Collector**, **Returning Officer** conducts the poll and declares the result; observers (Sec. 20B) – general, police, **expenditure**. The ECI controls officers on poll duty.

National party: State party in 4 States, or 6% votes in 4 States + 4 LS seats, or 2% LS seats from 3 States; State party: 3% of Assembly seats or 3 seats. **Six** national parties (2025): BJP, INC, BSP, CPI(M), AAP, NPP (not TMC). ECI delisted **808** registered unrecognised parties.

t: Section (RPA 1951) | Content
   row: **80** | No election can be questioned except by an **election petition** (Art. 329(b))

   row: **100** | **Grounds** for declaring an election void (disqualification, corrupt practice, improper rejection of nomination – not a small margin)
