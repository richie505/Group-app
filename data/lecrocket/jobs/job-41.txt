h: Laterite
b: Latin **"later" = brick**; formed by **intense leaching** under **high temperature and heavy rainfall (> 200 cm)** with alternate wet and dry seasons; lime and silica leached, **iron and aluminium oxides** left.

b: **Not** moisture-retentive with a large clay factor – it is porous and well drained (that describes black soil).
b: **Building material:** cut into bricks that harden in air.
b: Crops with manure: **tea, coffee, rubber, cashew, tapioca**, coconut, arecanut.
b: **Where:** summits of Western and Eastern Ghats, **Malabar coast (Kerala)**, Vindhya–Satpura, Rajmahal hills, Malwa, south **Maharashtra**, Karnataka, Odisha, Assam, Meghalaya; **Gangani (West Medinipur, West Bengal)**; **not** Rajasthan, UP, HP or Punjab.
h: Red (and yellow) soil
b: From **ancient crystalline granite, gneiss and schist** under low rainfall; red colour from **ferric oxide** (wide diffusion of iron), **yellow when hydrated**.
b: Porous, light, **low moisture retention**; poor in lime, humus, nitrogen, phosphates; fairly rich in potash.
b: **Where:** Peninsular India – almost all of **Tamil Nadu**, **Andhra Pradesh**, Karnataka, Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Chota Nagpur; Kanyakumari–Jhansi and Kutch–Rajmahal; surrounds the black soil on the south, east and north.
b: Crops: ragi, groundnut, tobacco, millets, pulses.
h: Andhra Pradesh
b: Cultivated area: **red ~60%**, black ~25%, deltaic alluvium 5–6%, laterite ~1%; 22 soil categories; **six agro-climatic zones**.
b: Red soils (Rayalaseema): red gram, green gram, horse gram, groundnut, castor; AP laterite is poor in nitrogen.
b: **Coffee** is grown on the red and laterite soils of the Western Ghats, not on alluvium.
b: **Tea** needs slightly acidic soil without calcium and cannot bear standing water – so it is grown on hill slopes.

h: Causes and rate
b: **Deforestation** is the main human cause (removes root binding and humus); also overgrazing, shifting cultivation, faulty farming. **Terrace cultivation is a remedy**, and tropical climate is not counted a cause – "only deforestation".
b: **Deforestation generally decreases rainfall** (and increases erosion, drought, warming).
b: Erosion rate depends on **all** of: rainfall intensity and duration, slope, vegetation cover, soil texture, land use.
b: **Rainfall intensity matters more than total annual rainfall**; higher intensity → more runoff → **effective rainfall reduces**.

b: **RUSLE** model predicts long-term water erosion on slopes (ICAR C- and P-factor maps).

h: Meaning (asked 17 times)
b: **Tsunami** = Japanese word: **"tsu" = harbour + "nami" = wave** ("harbour wave") – **not Latin, Arabic or Hebrew**.
b: A **series of waves** of very long wavelength (over 500 km) and long period (10 minutes to 2 hours) – **not a single wave**; the **first wave is often not the largest**, later waves can be bigger.
b: A **natural disaster**; can strike at **any time of day or night**. **Not a "tidal wave"** – tides (gravity of Moon and Sun) play **no role** in generating it.
h: Causes
t: Cause | Note
   row: **Undersea earthquakes** | **Main / most common cause** – sudden vertical displacement of the sea floor at **subduction zones** (shocks above about 7.5)
   row: Submarine or coastal **landslides** | Displace water
   row: **Volcanic eruptions** | E.g. Krakatoa 1883 (about 40 m wave), Anak Krakatau 2018
   row: Explosions, meteorite (bolide) impacts | Rare
b: **Not caused by hurricanes / cyclones** (they raise **storm surges**) or deserts.
b: Most tsunamis occur in the **Pacific Ocean** (Ring of Fire – Alaska, Japan, Philippines, Indonesia).
b: **Coastal States/UTs** of India are vulnerable to **tsunamis** (not volcanic eruptions).
h: Behaviour
t: Deep ocean | Near the coast
   row: Very long wavelength; height under 1 m ("slight swell" about 30 cm); speed **hundreds of km/h** (about **700–850 km/h** at 4,000–6,000 m depth) | **Slows down** (speed depends on depth); wavelength shortens, **height rises sharply** (shoaling) – 15 m or more; on coastal plains it moves at roughly **50 km/h**

b: **Fukushima (Japan), 11 March 2011**: Mw 9.0 Tohoku earthquake + tsunami → nuclear radiation.

b: Sequences: during – early warning → evacuation → rescue → shelters → emergency medical / water; after – **relief → restoration → reconstruction → resilient rebuilding**.
