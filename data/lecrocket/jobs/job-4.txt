b: **Bhagavata Purana** (Tamil land, 9th-10th c.) put bhakti at the centre; **Navadha (ninefold, 9) bhakti** – shravana, kirtana, smarana, padasevana, archana, vandana, dasya, sakhya, atmanivedana (UP UDA 2010).

b: **Why it arose:** reaction against ritual, priestly dominance and caste; **jnana and karma margas too hard** for ordinary people.
b: **Common feature of all Bhakti saints:** they **composed in the language of the people** (vernacular) (State PSC) – not all rejected priests or idols (Saguna saints kept idols).
b: **Ramanuja's movement in Andhra** (Vishnu devotion + social equality) = the **Bhakti movement** – not Arya Samaj or Lingayatism.
b: Core beliefs: **monotheism**, **guru** essential, **prapatti** (self-surrender); devotion above birth and status.
b: Modern scholars trace its roots to the **Alvars and Nayanars**, not to Islam; Islam shaped later egalitarian ideas.
