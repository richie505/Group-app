b: **254(1):** **Union law prevails**; the State law is **void to the extent of repugnancy** – the **doctrine of paramountcy** (reconciles conflicting laws where both levels can legislate on the same matter).
b: **254(2):** a State law **reserved for and assented to by the President** prevails **only in that State**.
b: **Proviso:** Parliament can later **override** it by a fresh law.
b: **Art. 251** (not 254) covers conflict with laws made under **Arts 249/250**; Art. 250 is **not** the repugnancy article.
h: Central control over State legislation
b: **Governor may reserve** State Bills for the President (Art. 200/201); Art. 254(2) reservation is an exception to aid and advice.
b: **State Bill** with **reasonable restrictions on inter-State trade** in the public interest needs the **President's previous sanction** (Part XIII, Art. 304(b)).
b: **Arts 301-303:** Parliament cannot **prefer or discriminate** between States in trade laws, except in **scarcity**.
b: **Nov 2025 SC advisory opinion (Presidential Reference):** **rejected "deemed assent"** and **judicially fixed timelines** for Governors and the President.

h: Article 53
b: **Art. 52:** there shall be a President of India.
b: **Art. 53(1):** the **executive power of the Union is vested in the President** and is exercised by him **either directly or through officers subordinate to him** (Ministers included) in accordance with the Constitution – "only through officers" is wrong.
b: **Art. 53(2):** **Supreme Command** of the Defence Forces vests in the President, its exercise **regulated by law**.
b: **Art. 53(3)(b):** nothing in Art. 53 **prevents Parliament from conferring by law functions on authorities other than the President**.
b: **Appointing the PM** (Art. 75) is an **executive power** of the President.
h: Nominal vs real executive
b: President = **nominal / constitutional head**; **Head of State**, not Head of Government; **First Citizen**; **Rank 1** in the Order of Precedence; part of Parliament (**Art. 79**).
b: **Real executive:** the **Council of Ministers** headed by the **PM** (**Art. 74(1)**), collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha – the feature that shows this is **parliamentary democracy** (not federalism or adult franchise).
b: **Art. 77:** all executive action is taken **in the President's name**.
b: **Art. 299:** all **contracts** made in exercise of the Union's executive power are **expressed to be made by the President** (for a State, by the Governor).
x: A-R angle: "President is the executive power of the Union" (A) is explained by Art. 53(1) (R) – both true, R explains A.
