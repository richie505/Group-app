b: **Post-Gupta caste (AP recruitment):** the **Abhira** tribe split into Abhira Brahmanas, Kshatriyas and Shudras; **peasants** were placed in the **Shudra** varna; **Kayasthas** worked as record-keepers and scribes – all true; but Brahmanas **were** divided into many sub-castes, so (b) is false → **a, c, d**.
b: **Kayastha = scribes**: a new caste of scribes and accountants from the land-grant bureaucracy; **prathama kayastha** sat on town boards.
b: **Jajmani system:** hereditary service castes serving landowning patrons (**jajmans**) for a share of produce – became prevalent in the **post-Gupta** period.
b: **Medhatithi:** the post-Gupta commentator on Manu who declared that **Shudras were not slaves by nature** – not Vijnaneshvara, Narada or Jimutavahana.
b: **Shudras and untouchables:** Shudras' position improved (seen as agriculturists by the 7th c.; could hear the epics), but **untouchability** grew – **chandalas** lived outside villages; **Narada Smriti** lists **15 types of slaves**.
h: Women
b: **Marriage:** pre-puberty marriage advised; **svayamvara** gave way to marriages arranged by parents; widows to stay celibate.
b: **Property:** no general property rights, but control over **stridhana** (marriage gifts); Katyayana let women sell their stridhana.
b: **Earliest sati record:** **Eran, 510 CE** – wife of **Goparaja**, a general/friend of Bhanugupta; sati was still rare.
b: **Land:** Amarasimha's **Amarakosha** lists **12 types of land** (urvara = fertile, usara = barren, maru = desert).
b: **Equality with men:** women enjoyed it in **no** age (Mauryan, Gupta or Chola) – in the Gupta age their status fell (early marriage, no property rights).

h: Origin

b: **Originator:** **Krishna (Vasudeva)** of the **Vrishni (Satvata)** clan, son of Devaki, pupil of Ghora Angirasa (Chhandogya Upanishad) – not Janaka, Yajnavalkya or Surdas.

h: Gupta patronage
b: **Greatest contribution to the Bhagavata sect:** the **Guptas** (State PSC) – virtually the state religion; rulers styled **Parama Bhagavata** (first: **Samudragupta**); dynastic emblem **Garuda**.

h: Avatars and attributes

b: **Dashavatara:** Matsya, Kurma, Varaha, Narasimha, Vamana, Parashurama, Rama, Krishna, Buddha, Kalki.
b: **Kaustubha (Kausthubham):** the gem on **Vishnu's chest**, from the churning of the ocean (APPSC Endowment 2022).
b: **Vaishnava symbols:** chakra, shankha, Garuda; **AP angle:** Salankayanas were Vaishnavas (title Parama Mahesvara); Pedavegi inscription of Nandivarman II – first AP record of Vishnu temples.
b: **Avatar by yuga:** Satya – Matsya, Kurma, Varaha, Narasimha; Treta – Vamana, Parashurama, Rama; **Dvapara – Krishna** (and Balarama); Kali – Kalki.
