h: Meaning
b: **Dyarchy (diarchy):** "double rule" (Greek *di-arche*) – the **subjects delegated to the provinces** were divided into two categories, **reserved** and **transferred**.
b: **Introduced by:** the **Government of India Act, 1919** = **Montagu-Chelmsford (Mont-Ford) Reforms** – in the **provinces** (not 1861, 1892, 1909 or 1935; not the Morley-Minto Reforms or the Simon plan).
b: **Aim:** partial **responsible government** in the provinces – the transferred half was run by ministers answerable to the legislature (so A-R "dyarchy = division of administration in two sections; introduced to bring responsible government in provinces": both true, R explains A).
b: **Provinces gained power:** the 1919 Act, not the 1892/1909 Acts or the Gandhi-Irwin Pact, gave provincial governments more powers under dyarchy.
h: Reserved vs transferred
t: Subjects | Examples | Run by | Answerable to
   row: **Reserved** | **Administration of justice, police, land revenue**, finance, prisons, irrigation, control of the press | **Governor + Executive Council** | Not the legislature
   row: **Transferred** | **Local self-government**, education, public health, agriculture | **Governor + Ministers** | **Provincial legislative council**
b: Reserved set = **justice, land revenue, police (1, 3, 4)**; **local self-government was transferred**.
b: Flaw: ministers held transferred subjects but **finance stayed reserved**; the experiment was "largely unsuccessful".
b: **Dyarchy at the Centre** = the **1935 Act** (never worked); dyarchy in the **provinces** = **1919**; the 1935 Act abolished provincial dyarchy.

b: **Source of the Constitution / of political power:** the **people of India** – the Preamble opens "**We, the People of India**", showing **people's sovereignty**; the Constitution derives its authority from the people (HWO 2017).
b: **⚠ Trap:** not the Constitution, Parliament, "Parliament and State Legislatures", the President or the Supreme Court.

b: **Enacting clause:** "We, the people of India … **in our Constituent Assembly** … do hereby **adopt, enact and give to ourselves** this Constitution" – the Constitution is declared adopted and enacted by the **people of India**, acting through the **Constituent Assembly** (when only the CA is offered, it is the answer for "adopted by").
h: The date
