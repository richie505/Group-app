topic: Chief Minister, State Council of Ministers and Advocate General
page title: Appointment of the CM – Article 164 and the Six-Month Rule
h: Who appoints – Article 164(1)
b: **Appointed by:** the **Governor** under **Article 164(1)** (not Art. 163, 165 or 166; not the President, a High Court judge or the Attorney General). Other Ministers are appointed by the Governor **on the CM's advice**.
b: **Constitutional (nominal) executive head** of the State = the **Governor**; the **real executive** = the CM and the Council of Ministers (not the Chief Secretary or a Deputy CM).
b: **Hung Assembly:** the Constitution lays down **no criteria**; the Governor uses discretion. The principal consideration = the person **most likely to command a stable majority** (not simply the largest party), followed by an early **floor test**.
h: Article 164 – clause by clause
t: Clause | Provision
   row: **164(1)** | Governor appoints the CM; other Ministers on the CM's advice; Ministers hold office **during the pleasure of the Governor**; Tribal Welfare Minister proviso
   row: **164(1A)** | Size: maximum **15%** of the Assembly, minimum **12** (91st Amendment, 2003)
   row: **164(2)** | Council **collectively responsible to the Legislative Assembly** (not the Council or the Governor)
   row: **164(3)** | Oath of office and secrecy administered by the **Governor**
   row: **164(4)** | A non-member may be a Minister for **six consecutive months**; then he **ceases** to be a Minister
   row: **164(5)** | Salaries and allowances = as the **State Legislature by law** determines (until then, Second Schedule) – not the CM, Governor or Chief Secretary
b: **Six months, not 3 months, 1 year or 3 years:** a Minister (incl. the CM) can continue without being elected to the State Legislature for **six months** (Art. 164(4)).
b: **Case – P. Shanmugam (Puducherry):** a non-member CM, resigned in **October 2001** after failing to get elected within six months; N. Rangasamy succeeded him (27 Oct 2001). Jayalalithaa (Sept 2001) went after the Supreme Court annulled her appointment – a different ground.
b: **Term:** **not fixed** by the Constitution; the CM stays while he has the **Assembly's confidence** (on losing it – resign or be dismissed). Resignation is addressed to the **Governor**.
b: **Chosen by the majority party:** the CM is the leader of the **majority party / coalition in the Assembly**, only **formally appointed** by the Governor – the Governor does not 'select' him.
