b: **Gains:** wheat (most), then rice; Punjab, Haryana, western UP; end of PL-480 dependence; foodgrains **72 MT (1965-66) → 357.73 MT (2024-25)**.

b: **Minimum Support Price (MSP):** a form of **market intervention** by the Government of India to insure farmers against a **sharp fall in farm prices** – a guarantee (floor) price announced **at the beginning of the sowing season**, before the crop reaches the market.

b: **Regional imbalance:** industry concentrated in **Maharashtra, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka**; backward regions lag.
b: **Public sector issues:** loss-making CPSEs, political pricing → disinvestment and **New PSE Policy 2021**.
b: **Jobless growth:** capital-intensive growth; contract workers about **42%** of factory workers (ASI).
b: **Industrial sickness** and delayed payments to MSMEs (MSME Samadhaan, TReDS).
h: Andhra Pradesh angle
