## page 058d270bbd73
topic: India – Physiographic Division: the Himalayas (ranges, Regional Divisions, Passes)
page title: Dimensions, Origin, Structure and Significance
h: Dimensions
t: Dimension | Figure
   row: Length | **~2,400 km** (2,414 km) – **Nanga Parbat** (west) to **Namcha Barwa** (east)
   row: Width | 500 Km (Kashmir) → 200 km (Arunachal)
   row: Area | About **5 lakh sq km** (not 15 lakh)
   row: Eastern limit | The **Brahmaputra** (Dihang gorge) marks the eastern boundary
b: The Himalayas form the **northern boundary**, separating the subcontinent from the rest of Asia (not the Aravalli or the Ghats).
b: **Indian Himalayan Region:** HP, Uttarakhand, Sikkim, Arunachal, NE States, J&K, Ladakh and hill WB – **not Uttar Pradesh**.
h: Origin and structure
b: **Young fold mountains** of tectonic origin, raised from **Tethys (geosynclinal)** sediments in the **Tertiary** (Eocene–Pliocene) as the **Indian plate** pushed into Eurasia (~70 million years ago, still rising).
b: **Geologically young and unstable** landmass of India.
b: **Trans-Himalayas formed before** the Himalayas.
b: Folds of the **Himadri are asymmetrical** with a **granite** core; **steep slope faces the plains**, gentle slope faces Tibet.
b: **Structural zones (N → S):** Indus–Tsangpo Suture Zone (separates Himalaya from **Tibet**) → Main Central Thrust → Main Boundary Thrust → **Himalayan Frontal Fault** (separates Himalaya from the **plains**, not from Tibet).
b: **Antecedent drainage:** the Indus and Sutlej are older than the Himalaya and cut gorges through it.
b: Fossils: Greater Himalaya sedimentary rocks fossil-less; marine fossils in the Lesser Himalaya; remains of human civilisation in the Shiwaliks.
h: Significance
b: Blocks **Siberian cold waves**; shapes the **monsoon** pattern; rivers and Shiwalik debris built the **Indo-Gangetic Plain's alluvium**.
b: **Plains** are the largest relief division of India (~43% of area).
b: **East v. west:** in the east the Himalaya rises **almost abruptly from the plains** over a narrow belt, so **Everest and Kanchenjunga** stand not far from the plains; in the west the ranges rise gradually across a wide belt.

h: Polavaram
b: **Site:** multipurpose **earth-cum-rockfill** dam on the **Godavari** at **Polavaram** village (erstwhile **West Godavari**, now Eluru), **42 km upstream of the Cotton Barrage**; contemplated 1941, begun 2005 (Jalayagnam).

b: **Design:** gross storage **194.6 TMC** (FRL 45.72 m); **live storage ~75.2 TMC ≈ 2,130 Mm³**; **7.20 lakh acres**; **960 MW**; **80 TMC Godavari → Krishna** (the Godavari–Krishna link of the ILR, not Krishna → Godavari); 23.44 TMC for Visakhapatnam; reservoir spreads into **Odisha and Chhattisgarh**.
b: **Left Main Canal** → **North Coastal Andhra and Visakhapatnam** (5 TMC to Kanithi, Sep 2026); **Right Main Canal** → Krishna at Vijayawada.
