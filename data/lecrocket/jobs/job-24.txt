## page ee6a4a09db5b
topic: Human Rights – Concept, Generations, Udhr, Iccpr, Icescr and Un Machinery
page title: Concept of Human Rights – Meaning, Thinkers and Milestones
h: Meaning
b: **Human rights:** rights given to **all human beings by nature**, simply because they are human – **inherent, inalienable, universal**; essential conditions of social life without which no one can lead a normal life.
b: **Core value:** **dignity of man as a human being** (UDHR Preamble: inherent dignity and equal rights); **indivisible and interdependent** – civil-political and socio-economic rights stand together (**Vienna Declaration, 1993**).
b: **Rights vs the State:** rights are **claims of the citizens against the State** – limits on State power, not claims of the State on citizens or privileges of a few.
b: Every **Fundamental Right** is a human right, but **not every human right is a Fundamental Right**.
b: **Kinds of rights:** **economic rights** give a **reasonable and legal source of livelihood** (work, wages, leisure); **political rights** = vote, contest, hold office (the **right to vote is a political right**; in India only a **statutory right** – **Sec. 62, RP Act 1951**, not an FR); civil rights = life, liberty, religion.
