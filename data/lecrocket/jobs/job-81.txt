   row: **157** | **Qualifications** – **citizen of India** (not necessarily by birth) and **completed 35 years**
   row: **158** | **Conditions** – not a member of Parliament or a State Legislature (seat vacated on appointment), no **office of profit**; emoluments fixed by **Parliament by law**, not reduced during the term
   row: **159** | **Oath** before the **Chief Justice of the High Court** (in his absence, the senior-most judge)

b: **Nominal / constitutional head** of the State and its **executive head**; the **Chief Minister and Council of Ministers** are the **real executive**.

b: Emoluments **charged on the Consolidated Fund of the State** (Art. 202(3)); a Governor of two or more States has them shared in the **proportion the President fixes** (Art. 158(3A)).
b: **Conventions:** usually an **outsider** to the State; the State **CM is consulted** (not compulsory).
h: Term and removal
b: Normal term **5 years**, but he **continues until his successor enters office** (Art. 156(3)) – "quits immediately after his term expires" is **wrong**.
b: The five years are **not secure**: he serves at the President's pleasure (= Union Council of Ministers) and can be **removed or transferred at any time**, and reappointed.
b: **No procedure or grounds for removal** are laid down in the Constitution; he is **not impeached** and **the State Legislature cannot remove** him (President, Election Commissioner and HC judges are removed by Parliament; the Governor is not).
b: He is **responsible to the President**; **not answerable** to either House of Parliament, either House of the State Legislature or the State Council of Ministers.
b: **B.P. Singhal v. Union of India (2010)**: the pleasure doctrine is **not unfettered** – removal must not be **arbitrary** or merely because the **Government at the Centre changed**; limited judicial review.
h: Immunity – Article 361
b: **361(1)**: not answerable to **any court** for acts done in the exercise of his powers and duties.
b: **361(2)**: **no criminal proceedings** instituted or continued during the term – not even with a court's permission; **361(3)**: no arrest or imprisonment.
b: **361(4)**: **civil proceedings** for **personal acts** only after **two months' written notice** – so "a Governor cannot be sued for any act" is **false**.
b: Members of a State Legislature are separately immune for anything said or any vote in the House (**Art. 194(2)**).
x: "No procedure has been laid down in the Constitution for the removal of a Governor" = correct.: Art. 361(1), 361(2) and 194(2) all correct.
b: **Reappointment:** no limit in the Constitution – a person may be Governor for **any number of terms**.
b: **Hargovind Pant v. Raghukul Tilak (1979):** the Supreme Court held the office of Governor is an **independent constitutional office**, neither subordinate to nor under the control of the **Union Government**.
