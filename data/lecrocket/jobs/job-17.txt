b: **Sec. 2(s)**: PwD = long-term physical, mental, intellectual or sensory impairment which, with barriers, blocks full participation.

page title: Types of Disability – Learning Disability and Blindness
b: **Learning disability** = difficulty in a specific academic skill despite normal intelligence: **dyslexia** (reading), dysgraphia (writing), dyscalculia (maths).
b: **Dyslexia** is the example specific to learning disability; **autism spectrum disorder**, **ADHD** and **mental retardation (intellectual disability)** are separate developmental conditions, not specific learning disabilities.
b: RPwD Act 2016 lists **specific learning disabilities** among its 21 disabilities, separately from autism and intellectual disability.
b: **New definition of blindness (April 2017, MoHFW, WHO criteria):** a person is blind if unable to **count fingers from 3 metres** (the 1976 definition used **6 metres**); programme renamed National Programme for Control of Blindness and Visual Impairment.
x: Trap: dyslexia = learning disability; ADHD and autism are not "specific learning disabilities"; blindness now 3 m, not 6 m.
