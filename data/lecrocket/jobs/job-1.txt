b: **Kushinagar:** the **Malla republic**, not the Lichchhavis or Sakyas; the Buddha died there at **80** under sal trees.

b: **"Birthplace of Buddhism":** calls **Sarnath** the "birthplace of Buddhism" – the **Dharma** and **Sangha** began there with the first sermon; Lumbini is only the Buddha's birthplace.

h: Bodh Gaya facts
b: **Mahabodhi Temple:** UNESCO World Heritage Site since **2002**.
b: Original Bodhi tree destroyed by **Shashanka** (7th c., per **Hiuen Tsang**; earlier Pushyamitra Sunga by legend); the present tree was planted under **Alexander Cunningham** (1881) and is called the **fifth generation** (Bihar Tourism) – some count it the sixth, so BPSC dropped the question.
b: **Maha Bodhi Society (1891):** **Anagarika Dharmapala**, to restore Bodh Gaya to Buddhists.

page title: Core Teachings – Nirvana, Karma, Anatta, Middle Path
t: Term | Meaning
   row: **Nirvana (nibbana)** | **Main goal**; "**extinction of the flame of desire**"; end of suffering and of rebirth
   row: **Middle Path (Madhyama Marga)** | Avoids both **self-indulgence and self-mortification** – the path to nirvana, not the goal
   row: **Anicca** | **Impermanence** – "the world is unstable and transient"
   row: **Anatta / Anatmavada** | **No permanent self (soul)** – a Buddhist doctrine, not Sankhya, Vedanta or Jain
   row: **Dukkha** | Suffering
   row: **Karma** | Intention-based morality; present state from past actions; **rebirth** till nirvana
   row: Raft simile | Teaching as a raft – upaya (skilful means), not dogma
b: **Three marks:** Anicca, Anatta, Dukkha.
b: Beliefs: karma ✓, rebirth ✓, nirvana ✓; **God neither affirmed nor denied** – belief in God ✗; belief in God not needed for the Sangha.
b: **Rejected Vedic authority**, animal sacrifice and ritual; stressed **purity of conduct / a moral life** over philosophical problems.
b: Caste: Buddhism **did not abolish caste and class** as social facts, **challenged the Brahmins' top rank** (Kshatriyas listed first), and texts call some **jatis and crafts (hina-sippa) low** – exams: all three true.
b: **Statements on the Buddha:** born in (the Sakya state of) **Kapilavastu** – strictly at Lumbini near it; enlightened at **Bodh Gaya**; **discarded the Vedic religion**; preached the **noble truths** – key: all four.
x: Nirvana = goal; Middle Path = means; Anatta = no soul; God neither affirmed nor denied.

b: **Last words:** **"appamadena sampadetha"** – "all composite things decay; **strive diligently**".
