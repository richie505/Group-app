h: Citizens' Charter

b: **First ARC** (1966-70, **Morarji Desai**): recommended a **Lokpal at the Union** and **Lokayuktas in the States**.
b: **Filtering** (communication barrier): the sender **manipulates information so that the receiver sees it more favourably**.
