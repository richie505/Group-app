h: Genetic and blood disorders
b: **Thalassemia:** autosomal recessive; **reduced rate of synthesis of one of the globin chains** (α or β) of haemoglobin → anaemia (not X-linked, not a clotting defect, not phenylalanine build-up = PKU).
b: **Haemophilia:** **X-linked recessive** defect of a **clotting factor** – a cut keeps bleeding.
b: **Viruses** contain **nucleic acid** (DNA or RNA) in a protein coat; no enzymes for metabolism of their own.

h: Kidney and dialysis
b: Urine ~1.5 L/day, **pH ~6.0**; normal BP **120/80 mmHg**.
b: **Dialysis** removes wastes and excess water, but **cannot reabsorb nutrients** (glucose, amino acids) as the kidney tubules do; haemodialysis (machine) vs **peritoneal** dialysis (peritoneum as filter).
