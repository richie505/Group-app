page title: Afforestation, Forestry Terms and Their Benefits
t: Term | Meaning
   row: Afforestation | New forest on denuded, degraded or unproductive land
   row: Reforestation | Re-establishing forest cover in existing / depleted forest areas
   row: Social forestry | Trees for community needs; term used by the **National Commission on Agriculture (1976)**
   row: Extension forestry | Trees along **roadsides, canals and railway strips**
   row: Agroforestry | **Trees + crops + livestock** on the same land
b: **Benefits:** **afforestation controls floods** and **checks soil erosion by running water** (roots bind soil, slow run-off) – deforestation, overgrazing, urbanisation, tilling or removing topsoil worsen them.
b: **Floods worsened by:** unscientific agriculture, disturbing natural drainage channels, colonising flood-plains and river-beds – **NOT reforestation**.
b: **Criticism of social forestry:** drifted into **commercial monocultures** (eucalyptus); fix = mixed native species.
b: **Miyawaki method:** dense, multi-layered **mini-forests of native species in urban areas**; Japanese botanist **Akira Miyawaki**, **1970s**.
b: **Seed bomb (seed ball):** a ball of **mud/clay, dung and seeds** thrown on land to grow trees.
b: **Craib's thinning:** heavy early (advance) thinning of plantations, done in a regular crop **in anticipation of suppression** (I.J. Craib, South Africa).
b: **Wild animals** help natural regeneration through **seed dispersal** (e.g. elephants, hornbills).
b: **Vanamahotsav:** **K.M. Munshi, 1950**; first week of July.

b: **Eutrophication = nutrient (N, P) enrichment of a water body** – the **natural ageing of a lake by nutrient (biological) enrichment** of its water; human-accelerated = **cultural eutrophication**.

page title: Ozone Depletion – CFCs, Chlorine and Bromine
h: Cause
b: **CFCs (chlorofluorocarbons)** are the **main cause** of ozone depletion and of the "ozone hole" – not CO2, CO, methane, N2 or SO2.
b: **Full form:** CFC = **Chlorofluorocarbon** – carbon + chlorine + fluorine; entirely **man-made**, non-toxic, non-flammable.
b: **Mechanism:** under UV in the stratosphere CFCs release **chlorine**; **chlorine and bromine atoms destroy ozone catalytically** (one Cl atom breaks thousands of O3 molecules before it is locked up again) → more UV reaches the Earth.
b: **Bromine** comes from **halons** (fire extinguishers) – about **100 times more potent** than CFCs; **carbon tetrachloride** and **methyl chloroform** also release chlorine.
b: Depletion happens in the **stratosphere** (not in the lower atmosphere where humans live, not in the mesosphere).
b: **CFCs are also greenhouse gases** – they cause both warming and ozone loss (A-R: both true, but "CFCs are used in fridges, ACs, aerosols" is **not the explanation** of that).
b: A–R: "ozone depletion = reduction of stratospheric ozone" ✓; "caused by high chlorine and bromine from CFCs and halons" ✓ → **R explains A**.
b: **Wrong statements trap (TSPSC JL):** ozone is in the **stratosphere, not mesosphere**; it is the **chlorine**, not the carbon, of CFCs that splits O3.

   row: **Methane** | **Bacterial decomposition** (wetlands, paddy, livestock)

## page 2b7428f761b5
topic: International Treaties on Chemicals and Waste – Basel, Rotterdam, Stockholm POPs, Minamata; Montreal Protocol and Kigali Amendment
page title: Ozone Layer, Dobson Unit and the Ozone Hole
h: Ozone layer
b: **Ozone = O3** (three oxygen atoms); formed when **UV radiation splits O2** (UV + O2 → O3).
b: About **90%** of ozone is in the **stratosphere** (**10–50 km**; main layer ~15–30 km) – **protective**, absorbs harmful **UV (UV-B)**; ~10% is in the troposphere, where it is a **pollutant**.
b: Ozone protects the biosphere from **ultraviolet rays** – not infra-red, X-rays or gamma rays.
b: **Natural regulation:** stratospheric ozone is naturally regulated by **nitrogen oxides – "nitrogen dioxide"** in books and keys (the NO/NO2 cycle; N2O from soils is the source gas).
h: Dobson Unit
b: **Dobson Unit (DU)** measures the **thickness/concentration of ozone in a column of air from the ground to the top of the atmosphere** – not decibel (sound), angstrom (length), knot, poise or Maxwell.
b: **1 DU = 0.01 mm** of pure ozone at **0°C and 1 atm**; an **"ozone hole"** = total column ozone **below 220 DU**.
h: Ozone hole
b: Most affected region: **Antarctica**; seasonal thinning in the **Southern Hemisphere spring (Aug–Nov)**.
b: **Polar stratospheric clouds** in extreme cold turn stable chlorine compounds into **active chlorine**, which destroys ozone when sunlight returns.
b: Ozone began to **drop sharply in the 1980s**; the Antarctic hole was **reported in 1985 by the British Antarctic Survey**.
b: **Tibetan ozone low ("halo") 2005:** discovered by **G.W. Kent Moore**, University of Toronto.
b: **Recovery:** NASA/WMO report healing of the Antarctic hole, mainly due to the **ban on CFCs** under Montreal.
t: Region | Back to 1980 levels
   row: Most of the world | **2040**
   row: Arctic | **2045**
   row: Antarctic | **2066**
