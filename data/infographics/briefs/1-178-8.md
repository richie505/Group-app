Title: Satavahanas – Sources: Puranas, Literature, Foreign Accounts

1. Puranas
- Matsya Purana: main source; genealogy and reign of each king.
- Matsya: 30 kings, 456 years (271 BC – 174 AD).
- Kings 1-23 = early Satavahanas; 24-30 = later Satavahanas.
- Matsya Purana compiled under Yajnasri Satakarni.
- Vayu / Vishnu Puranas: 17 kings, 272 years – not treated as standard.
- Vishnudharmottara Purana (Chitrasutra): sourcebook of Indian painting, not a Satavahana source.

2. Literature
- Early history told in Kathasaritsagara, Pratishthanapura-kalpa, Dvatrimsatputtalika.
- Gathasaptasati does not narrate early Satavahana history.

3. Kharavela
- Kharavela of Kalinga: contemporary of Satakarni I.
- Contact recorded in the Hathigumpha inscription and the Chulakalinga Jataka.
- Hathigumpha line 4: sent an army west "disregarding Satakarni", to the Kanhabenna; no defeat recorded.
- Kanhabenna: Hathigumpha inscription's name for the Krishna river.

4. Foreign Accounts (table)
| Writer | Work / fact |
| Megasthenes | Indica; first foreign source on the Andhras |
| Pliny | Natural History (Latin); Roman gold drained to India |
| Periplus (anonymous Greek) | Saka–Satavahana conflict; Koddura the only east-coast port; Arikamedu |
| Ptolemy | Geography; Pulumavi vs Chashtana; Maisolia a great market |

5. Andhra Army (Pliny quoting Megasthenes)
- 30 walled towns, 1,00,000 foot, 2,000 horse, 1,000 elephants.
