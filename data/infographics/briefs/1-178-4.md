Title: Satavahanas – Capitals

1. Principal Capital
- Pratishthana (Paithan) on the Godavari, Aurangabad district, Maharashtra – principal capital and main town.

2. Andhra Capital
- Dhanyakataka (Dharanikota, near Amaravati, on the Krishna).
- Vasishthiputra Pulumavi moved the capital east to Amaravati (Dhanyakataka) after Rudradaman's attacks.
- Amaravati, designated capital of Andhra Pradesh today, was the historical Satavahana capital.

3. First Capital – Debated (table)
| Proposed first capital | Scholars |
| Srikakulam, Krishna district | Barnett, V.A. Smith, Burgess |
| Pratishthana | Mirashi, Raychaudhuri |

4. Other Centres
- Kotilingala (Karimnagar): claimed early centre.
- Mahishmati: capital of Anupa.

5. Capitals of Other Dynasties
- Vengi: Eastern Chalukyas; Warangal: Kakatiyas.
- Nagarjunakonda (Vijayapuri): Ikshvaku capital.
