2. Dakshinapathapati
- Satakarni I (Sri Satakarni): earliest Satavahana king to win wide recognition.
- Named Dakshinapathapati in Naganika's Naneghat inscription.

3. Kavivatsala
- Hala, the lover of poets.

4. Gautamiputra's Three Oceans
- Trisamudra-toya-pita-vahana: "whose horses drank the waters of the three oceans" (Nasik).

5. Trisamudradhipati
- Bana's Harshacharita calls Nagarjuna's friend "Satavahana, the lord of the three oceans" = Yajnasri.
- Some accounts give Trisamudradhipati to Gautamiputra.
