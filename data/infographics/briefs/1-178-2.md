Title: Satavahanas – Nasik Prasasti and Gautamiputra's Conquests

1. Nasik Prasasti – The Record (table)
| Particular | Fact |
| Issuer | Gautami Balasri, mother of Gautamiputra Satakarni |
| Date | 19th regnal year of her grandson Vasishthiputra Pulumavi |
| Place | Nasik, Pandavleni Cave 3 (Maharashtra) |
| Language, script | Prakrit prose, Brahmi |
| Content | Gautamiputra's military victories, empire and titles |
- The inscription describing Gautamiputra's achievements = Nasik.
- Gift: a village and 100 nivartanas of land to monks on Trirashmi hill.
- Other records: Naneghat = Naganika (Satakarni I); Kodavali = Chanda Satakarni; Hathigumpha = Kharavela.

2. Conquests
- Gautamiputra defeated the Kshaharata Saka king Nahapana.
- Regained Malwa, Saurashtra, Konkan and Nasik from Nahapana.
- Main enemy: the Sakas; praised as "destroyer of Sakas, Yavanas and Pahlavas".
- Jogalthambi hoard (Nasik district): 13,270 silver coins of Nahapana restruck by Gautamiputra.

3. Regions Named in the Prasasti (table)
| Region | Area |
| Anupa | Narmada valley, capital Mahishmati |
| Akara / Avanti | East Malwa (Vidisha) / West Malwa (Ujjain) |
| Asika, Asaka (Asmaka), Mulaka | Godavari valley, Paithan area |
| Suratha, Kukura | Saurashtra; western Malwa–Rajasthan |
| Aparanta / Vidarbha | North Konkan / Berar |

4. Social Claims
- Varnasankarya-nirodhaka: stopped the mixing of the four varnas.
- Brahmanical titles: Ekabrahmana, Kshatriya-darpa-mana-mardana ("crusher of Kshatriya pride"), Agama-nilaya.

5. Era and Dates
- 78 CE begins the Shaka era, tied to Chashtana's accession.
- "Salivahana era" linked to Gautamiputra is a later legend.
- Reign traditionally c. 106-130 CE; 23rd king, ruled 34 years.
- Some scholars now prefer c. 60-85 CE.

6. Nahapana's Circle
- Ayama: minister of Nahapana; linked with the Junnar inscription (a Kshaharata record).
- Ushavadatta: Nahapana's son-in-law.
