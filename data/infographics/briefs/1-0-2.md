Title: Pre-Harappan Cultures – Early Harappan: Amri, Kot Diji, Sothi, Kalibangan-I

1. AT A GLANCE
• Early (Pre-) Harappan phase: 3300–2600 BCE = Regionalisation Era (Hakra phase).
• Copper, the wheel and the plough were already known.
• Amalananda Ghosh (1953): first to see the likeness between a pre-Harappan culture (Sothi) and the Mature Harappan.
• Pottery was regional (Amri, Kot Diji, Sothi-Siswal, Hakra wares), not uniform; Early Harappans used the potter's wheel.

2. SITES (table: Site | Area | Feature | Excavator)
• Amri | Sindh | Transitional pre- to Mature culture; rhinoceros remains; later Jhangar | N. G. Majumdar (1929), later J.-M. Casal
• Kot Diji | Sindh | Fortified, destroyed by fire; many stone implements | Ghurye; F. A. Khan (1955)
• Sothi-Siswal | Rajasthan–Haryana (Ghaggar) | Pre-Harappan pottery | A. Ghosh identified Sothi
• Hakra | Cholistan (Ghaggar-Hakra) | – | –
• Banawali | Fatehabad, Haryana | Pre- and Mature phases | R. S. Bisht (1974)
• Mitathal | Bhiwani, Haryana | Pre-, Mature and Late phases | Suraj Bhan (1968)

3. KALIBANGAN-I
• Earliest ploughed field with criss-cross furrows = mixed cropping.
• Mud-brick fortification; pottery in six fabrics.
• Excavated 1961–69 by B. B. Lal and B. K. Thapar.

4. EXAM TRAPS
• Hakra is in Cholistan – not Gujarat.
• Kalibangan: the ploughed field is pre-Harappan; the fire altars belong to the Mature phase.
