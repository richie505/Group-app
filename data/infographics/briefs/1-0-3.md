Title: Pre-Harappan Cultures – Origin Debate and Excavators

1. VIEWS ON HARAPPAN ORIGIN (table: View | Scholar)
• Indigenous growth | John Marshall
• Migration from Sumer | E. J. H. Mackay
• Diffusion of ideas from West Asia | Mortimer Wheeler
• Local evolution from the Mehrgarh Neolithic | Current consensus

2. KEY POINTS
• W. A. Fairservis wrote "The Roots of Ancient India" (1971) on the prehistoric village cultures of Baluchistan.
• Kalibangan and Kot Diji foreshadow Mature buildings and pottery.
• So urbanisation was gradual and local, not imported from Mesopotamia.
• Unsolved: the gap between Neolithic Mehrgarh and the urban phase.

3. RECENT – JUNE 2026
• 23 Jun 2026: Rakhigarhi (~550 ha) shows unbroken Early to Mature occupation.
• Haryana protected Tighrana and Mitathal (Bhiwani).

4. EXAM TRAPS
• Harappan urbanisation was not imported from Mesopotamia – it grew locally.
• Mackay = Sumer migration; Wheeler = West Asian diffusion; Marshall = indigenous.
