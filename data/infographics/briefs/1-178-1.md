Title: Satavahanas – Founder, Firsts and Independence from the Mauryas

1. The Firsts
- First historical ruling dynasty of the Deccan: the Satavahanas (c. 200 BC – 200 AD), the oldest Deccan dynasty.
- First dynasty to rule Andhra: the Satavahanas.
- Puranas call them Andhras or Andhrabhrityas ("servants of the Andhras", or Andhras who had been feudatories).
- Caste: Brahmins; they claimed Brahmana status, not Buddhist descent.
- Nasik inscription calls Gautamiputra "Ekabrahmana".

2. Founder – Simuka
- Founder: Simuka = Simukha = Srimukha = Shimukha (Puranic "Sinthaka").
- Also founder of the administration; united several principalities into one kingdom.
- Killed the Kanva king Susarma and ended the Kanvas.
- Ruled from Pratishthana; said to have ruled 23 years.
- Progenitor: per the Puranas, Srimukha; per inscriptions, coins and Kathasaritsagara, Satavahana (Srimukha's father).

3. Successors
- 2nd king: Kanha (Krishna), brother of Simuka.
- 3rd king: Satakarni I, son of Simuka.

4. Independence and Span
- Began as Mauryan feudatories; the Andhras won freedom from Mauryan rule under them.
- Ruled the Deccan for 456 years after Mauryan decline (Matsya Purana) – about 450 years.
- Ashoka's 13th Rock Edict lists the Andhras among his vassal peoples.
- Long conflict with the Sakas over the premier western ports (Periplus).

5. Timeline – Sequence in Magadha
- Mauryas → Sungas → Kanvas → Satavahanas

6. Successor States
- Ikshvakus (Andhra), Abhiras (Maharashtra), Chutus (Karnataka), Pallavas (Tamil Nadu), Vakatakas (Vidarbha).
