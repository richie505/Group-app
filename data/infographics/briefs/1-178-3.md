Title: Satavahanas – Queens and Metronymics

1. Queens (table)
| Queen | Role |
| Devi Naganika | Wife of Satakarni I; daughter of Maharathi Tranakayiro (Amgiya family); regent for her minor son; issued Naneghat |
| Gautami Balasri | Mother of Gautamiputra; issued the Nasik Prasasti; gave a Nasik cave vihara to Bhadrayaniya monks |
| Lilavati | Sri Lankan princess, queen of Hala |

2. Devi Naganika
- Ruled as regent (guardian) during her son's minority.
- Queen of Satakarni I.
- Her father Maharathi Tranakayiro: the marriage allied the Satavahanas with the Maharathis.

3. Metronymics – What and Who
- Kings put their mother's name before their own: Gautamiputra (son of Gautami), Vasishthiputra.
- Began with Gautamiputra Satakarni.
- Prefix = mother's gotra name: Gotami < Gautama, Vasithi < Vasishtha.
- Dynasty known for metronymics: the Satavahanas.
- Gautamiputra took pride in calling himself son of Gautami.

4. Meaning
- Usually explained by polygamy.
- Shows respect for mothers; succession stayed patriarchal.
- Metronymy under the Satavahanas and Ikshvakus: sign of high social status of women.
