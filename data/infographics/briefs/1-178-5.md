Title: Satavahanas – Key Kings in the Matsya List

1. Key Kings (table)
| No. | King | Key fact |
| 1 | Simuka | Founder; ended the Kanvas |
| 2 | Kanha (Krishna) | First Satavahana inscription (Nasik); expanded to Nasik |
| 3 | Satakarni I | Greatest early king; 2 Ashvamedhas; Dakshinapathapati; Kharavela's contemporary; first silver coins |
| 6 | Satakarni II | Longest reign, 56 years; southern torana of Sanchi |
| 13 | Kuntala Satakarni | Made Sanskrit the royal language |
| 17 | Hala | Gathasaptasati; Kavivatsala; ruled 5 years |
| 23 | Gautamiputra Satakarni | Greatest of all; last of the early Satavahanas |
| 24 | Vasishthiputra Pulumavi (Pulomavi II) | Nasik Prasasti issued; capital to Amaravati; Navanagaraswami |
| – | Vasishthiputra (Sivasri) Satakarni | Married Rudradaman's daughter |
| 27 | Yajnasri Satakarni | Greatest of the later kings; Nagarjuna's patron; ship coins |
| – | Chanda Sri (Chandasri) | Kodavali inscription |
| 30 | Pulomavi III | Last king; Myakadoni inscription |

2. Notable Facts
- Gautamiputra Satakarni was succeeded by Vasishthiputra Pulumavi.
- Pulomavi II built Navanagara (new city); title Navanagaraswami.
- Rudradaman twice defeated his son-in-law (Sivasri) Satakarni but spared him as a near relative (Junagadh).
- Nagarjuna lived at the court of Yajnasri Satakarni.
- Yajnasri won back territory lost to the Western Kshatrapas.
- Last king (Puranas): Pulomavi III, the 30th; also numbered "Pulumavi IV".

3. End of the Dynasty
- Ikshvaku Santamula (Srisantamula) overthrew Pulomavi III (c. AD 225-230).
- Santamula took Dharanikota and Nagarjunakonda.
- Pulomavi III spent his last days at Myakadoni (Bellary).
- Dynasty ended in the mid-3rd century CE; its break-up paved the way for the Ikshvakus.
- Upasika Bodhisri's gifts at Nagarjunakonda: reign of Ikshvaku Virapurushadatta, golden age of Buddhism there.
