Title: Pre-Harappan and Early Harappan Cultures

1. AT A GLANCE – MEHRGARH
- Mehrgarh: Kachhi plain, Baluchistan, near the Bolan Pass; c. 7000 BCE.
- Earliest agriculture (wheat, barley), domestication and settled life in the subcontinent.
- Excavated by J.-F. Jarrige (1974).
- Seven periods (I–VII): from the Neolithic to the Harappan period.
- Burials under or near houses, flexed, with grave goods, sometimes with goats.

2. TIMELINE – MEHRGARH PERIODS
- Period I (c. 7000 BCE): aceramic Neolithic; mud-brick houses; barley, wheat; sheep, goat, cattle.
- Period II: pottery and cotton begin (c. 5000 BCE, oldest cotton).
- Period III onward: copper working, wheel-made painted pottery, beads.

3. EARLY VILLAGES OF BALUCHISTAN
- Kili Gul Muhammad – Quetta valley – W. A. Fairservis.
- Damb Sadaat – Quetta valley – W. A. Fairservis.
- Rana Ghundai – Zhob-Loralai valley – E. J. Ross.
- Crops: wheat, barley, dates, cotton; main animals sheep and goats.

4. EARLY HARAPPAN PHASE (3300–2600 BCE)
- Regionalisation Era (Hakra phase); copper, the wheel and the plough already known.
- Amri (Sindh): transitional culture; rhinoceros remains; N. G. Majumdar (1929), later J.-M. Casal.
- Kot Diji (Sindh): fortified, destroyed by fire; F. A. Khan (1955).
- Sothi-Siswal (Rajasthan–Haryana): pre-Harappan pottery; A. Ghosh identified Sothi.
- Banawali (Fatehabad, Haryana): R. S. Bisht (1974). Mitathal (Bhiwani): Suraj Bhan (1968).
- Kalibangan-I: earliest ploughed field, criss-cross furrows = mixed cropping; B. B. Lal and B. K. Thapar (1961–69).
- Pottery was regional, not uniform; potter's wheel was used.

5. ORIGIN DEBATE
- John Marshall: indigenous growth.
- E. J. H. Mackay: migration from Sumer.
- Mortimer Wheeler: diffusion of ideas from West Asia.
- Current consensus: local evolution from the Mehrgarh Neolithic; urbanisation gradual, not imported.
- Rakhigarhi (~550 ha): unbroken Early to Mature occupation.

6. EXAM TRAPS
- Mehrgarh is a Neolithic site, not a Harappan city.
- Earliest settled life and agriculture = Mehrgarh, not Kili Gul Muhammad.
- Kalibangan: ploughed field is pre-Harappan; fire altars are Mature phase.
- Hakra is in Cholistan, not Gujarat.
