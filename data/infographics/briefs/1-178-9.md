2. Karle Grant
- King's name is damaged; Senart reads Vasishthiputra Pulumavi.
- A separate Karajaka record names Gautamiputra.

3. Language and Location
- All in Prakrit, Brahmi script.
- Earliest inscriptions of the early kings are in Maharashtra (Naneghat, Kanha's Nasik).

4. Related Records
- Patagandigudem copper plates: Ikshvaku king Ehuvala Chamtamula (3rd c. CE).
- Earliest known copper-plate land grant of south India; grant to a Buddhist monastery.
- Bhairavakonda: Saiva centre with eight rock-cut caves, Nellore district.
