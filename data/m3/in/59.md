2. [FALSE, use the wrong answer: stabilises] Q: Deforestation generally ____ the amount of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere. => ANSWER: raises | WHY: Deforestation reduces carbon uptake and may release stored carbon, raising atmospheric CO2.
3. [TRUE] Q: Which of the following greenhouse gases does not occur naturally and is entirely man-made? => ANSWER: CFCs | WHY: CFCs are synthetic chemicals made for refrigerants and aerosols, so they are entirely man-made greenhouse gases. Water vapour, methane and nitrous oxide all occur naturally, though human activity adds to them.

## M mbbd6b7af5c4b | Climate terms
1. [TRUE] Q: Which one of the following correctly describes Global Warming Potential (GWP)? => ANSWER: The heat absorbed by a gas over a specified period, usually 100 years, relative to the same mass of carbon dioxide | WHY: GWP compares the heat absorbed over a specified period, generally 100 years, by a given mass of a greenhouse gas with that absorbed by the same mass of CO2.
2. [FALSE, use the wrong answer: Linear changes that can always be reversed immediately] Q: Tipping points in the climate system are best understood as: => ANSWER: Thresholds beyond which small changes may trigger self-perpetuating shifts that cannot be reversed on human timescales | WHY: Tipping points are thresholds at which small changes can initiate self-perpetuating, non-linear shifts that are not reversible on human timescales.
3. [TRUE] Q: The Keeling curve refers to the continuous record of atmospheric carbon dioxide measured at: => ANSWER: Mauna Loa, Hawaii, maintained since 1958 by Charles David Keeling | WHY: The Keeling curve is the continuous atmospheric CO2 record from Mauna Loa, Hawaii, begun in 1958 by Charles David Keeling.
