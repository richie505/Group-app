# APPSC Group 2 (3.13-g2): the Group 2 app

Built from APPSC Complete by `tools/group2_build.py` (then `tools/build_plan_mcq.py app/src/group2/assets`).
Installs beside APPSC Complete as its own app (com.appsc.prep.group2), with fresh progress.

|---|---|---|---|
| Indian History | Screening | 89 | 83 |

471 sections (of APPSC Complete's 927), 22,911 MCQs, 6,730 MCQ-notes pages; MCQ notes and full notes.

Nothing of Group 1: Group 1-only sections left out; each section shows only its Group 2 syllabus codes and
"Group-II"; past-paper counts are Group 2's; Group 1 source lists and "see A-30" cross-references removed; the 583
Group 1 PYQs removed; citations of Group 1 papers removed from the notes and explanations (the facts stay).
The notes are arranged by the Group 2 syllabus: each book's topics are its Group 2 subjects, sections in syllabus order;
the Notes tab tracks each subject's syllabus items.

## The plan (5 Nov 2026 - 21 Jan 2027, exam 24 Jan)

35 first-pass days (HIGH, then MED, then LIGHT topics; one subject a day), 14 days R2, 7 days R3, 17 MCQ days,
5 mocks (Screening, Mains Paper I, Paper II, Screening, Mains I + II). Same daily targets and sessions as
APPSC Complete.
