  (2) Korba
  (3) Raipur
  (4) Durg
explanation (OCR): Government of India in 1972 approved setting up of a coal- based fertilizer plant by the Fertilizer Corporation of India Ltd. (FCIL) at Korba district of Chhattisgarh with Urea Production capacity of 4.95 lakh MTPA, though its operation and revival plans have faced significant challenges. Erection of plant and machinery was kept on hold at Korba to see the performance of other two Coal-based plants at Talcher and Ramagundam. After a series of CCEA decisions regarding revival of FCIL, Korba in August, 2011 CCEA decided to revive Korba Project through 'bidding route'. However, it is yet to be taken off.

## gc-econ-245bd4a6 | U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) (Re.Exam) 2015 | key: (4)
Which amongst the following now decide the oil-prices in India?
  (1) Ministry of Finance
  (2) Respective State Governments
  (3) Ministry of Petroleum
  (4) Oil companies
explanation (OCR): Prices of petrol and diesel have been made market-deter- mined by the Government with effect from 26 June, 2010 and 19 October, 2014 respectively. Since then, the Public Sector Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) take appropriate decision on pricing of petrol and diesel in line with their in- ternational product prices, exchange rate, tax structure, inland freight and other cost elements. Since 2017, the OMCs have been following daily price revision mechanism under which the prices of petrol and diesel are revised based on the 15-day average of the product prices every morning. However, due to political reasons the Government still interfering through backdoor to keep petrol and diesel prices stable in the country.
