Main Plant Diseases Notes • Plant Pathology is the study of the diseases and disorders of plants. • Diseases can be defined as a harmful deviation from the normal functioning of the physiological processes caused by an infectious agent. • In the case of plant diseases, the causal agent may be virus, bacteria, fungus or a parasitic agent or lack of nutrients. • Plant pathology is also known as phytopathology.
