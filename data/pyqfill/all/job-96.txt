It is much stiffer and much less flexible than muscle. The matrix of cartilage is made up of glycosaminoglycans, proteoglycans, collagen fibres and, sometimes, elastin. Cartilage does not contain blood vessels (avascular) or nerves (aneural). Nutrition is supplied to the chondrocytes (the only cells found in healthy cartilage) by diffusion.
