explanation (OCR): The term eurodollar refers to US dollar-denominated deposits at banks outside the United States (especially in Europe), and thus are not under the jurisdiction of the Federal Reserve of US. explanation (OCR): Overseas transactions such as purchase-selling and transfer of foreign currency should be done through authorized dealers but transfer of black money generally made through using informal networks.
