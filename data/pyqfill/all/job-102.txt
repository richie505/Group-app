## gc-gene-51a7e3d8 | state PSC / UPSC (Ghatna Chakra) | key: (1)
Buds are typically found :
  (1) Both at the base of leaves and tip of branches
  (2) At the tip of branches
  (3) At the base of leaves
  (4) None of the above
explanation (OCR): A plant bud is an undeveloped or embryonic shoot. Buds are typically found in the axil of a leaf (base of the leaf where leaf joins the stem) or at the tip of a stem or branch. These are known as axillary or terminal buds, respectively. Axillary buds are located at the angle between a leaf and the stem. They can develop into branches, flowers, or new stems. Terminal buds are located at the tip of a stem or branch and are responsible for the vertical growth of the plant. While less common, adventitious buds can also arise in unexpected places like on roots or leaves. These are referred to as radical buds (on roots) or epiphyllous buds (on leaves), respectively.
