- Q: The Earth's orbital speed is greatest at: => ANSWER: Perihelion | WHY: Earth moves fastest at perihelion (early January) and slowest at aphelion (early July).
- Q: The Earth's aphelion distance from the Sun is approximately: => ANSWER: 152 million km | WHY: At aphelion Earth is farthest from the Sun, about 152 million km; perihelion is about 147 million km.
- Q: Which law explains why the Earth moves fastest at perihelion and slowest at aphelion? => ANSWER: Kepler's Second Law | WHY: Kepler's second law: equal areas are swept in equal times, so Earth moves faster near the Sun.

## SEC 4:2:0 | Rotation vs revolution
- Q: Which two planets rotate in the direction opposite to Earth's west-to-east rotation? => ANSWER: Venus and Uranus | WHY: Earth rotates west to east, whereas Venus and Uranus spin in the opposite direction.
- Q: Approximately 12 hours of daylight throughout the year is experienced at the: => ANSWER: Equator | WHY: The Equator has about 12-hour days all year; variation in day length increases with latitude.
- Q: The Earth rotates on its axis from: => ANSWER: West to east | WHY: Earth spins west to east once in 24 hours, turning each place toward and away from the Sun to give day and night.
- Q: Which of the following is NOT primarily associated with the Earth's rotation? => ANSWER: Perihelion and aphelion | WHY: Perihelion and aphelion belong to revolution; day and night, the Coriolis effect and time zones come from rotation.
- Q: The spinning of the Earth on its own axis is called: => ANSWER: Rotation | WHY: Rotation is Earth's spin on its axis; revolution is its elliptical path around the Sun.
- Q: The linear speed of the Earth's rotation is maximum at the: => ANSWER: Equator | WHY: Linear rotational speed is greatest at the Equator and zero at the poles.
- Q: Assertion (A): The Coriolis effect applies to cyclones. Reason (R): The Coriolis effect is caused by Earth’s revolution around the Sun. => ANSWER: A is true, but R is false | WHY: A is true: the Coriolis effect shapes cyclones. R is false because it comes from Earth's rotation, not revolution.
- Q: The Earth takes about how many days to complete one revolution around the Sun? => ANSWER: 365¼ | WHY: Earth takes about 365¼ days to complete one revolution on its elliptical orbit.
## SEC 4:2:1 | Rotation facts
- Q: The rotation of the Earth is generally not felt by people on its surface because: => ANSWER: The atmosphere and objects on Earth move along with it at a nearly uniform speed | WHY: Earth's motion is nearly uniform with no perceptible change in velocity, and the atmosphere and objects on it move along with it.
