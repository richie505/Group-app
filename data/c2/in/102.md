## SEC 5:24:1 | Core infrastructure
- Q: Which of the following was NOT identified as core infrastructure of the National e-Governance Plan? => ANSWER: National Investment and Infrastructure Fund | WHY: State Data Centres, SWAN and village-level Common Service Centres formed the core infrastructure of NeGP. - Q: Under NeGP infrastructure, the village-level front-end delivery outlet is the: => ANSWER: Common Service Centre (CSC) | WHY: CSCs are the village-level outlets; SWAN is the backbone network and SDC hosts State applications and data.

SWAN is the state backbone network, e-Taal gives e-transaction statistics, and CSCs are village-level front-end outlets.
- Q: Which of the following portals is wrongly matched with its function? => ANSWER: e-NAM – Department of Agriculture's e-governance network | WHY: e-NAM is for online agricultural commodity trading; the Agriculture Department's network is DACNET.

- Q: Which one of the following pairs is correctly matched? => ANSWER: SDC — State Data Centre | WHY: SDC stands for State Data Centre. SWAN means State Wide Area Network, MMP means Mission Mode Project and DACNET means Department of Agriculture and Cooperation Network.
## SEC 5:24:2 | Central platforms
- Q: e-Pramaan is India's: => ANSWER: National e-Authentication framework | WHY: e-Pramaan is the e-Authentication framework; SARATHI handles driving licences, VAHAN vehicle registration and Bhavishya pensioners.
- Q: Which of the following pairs is correctly matched? => ANSWER: GeM – Ministry of Commerce and Industry | WHY: GeM belongs to the Ministry of Commerce and Industry. UMANG is under MeitY, e-Office is a MeitY-NIC platform and CPGRAMS is associated with DARPG.
- Q: Jeevan Pramaan is associated with: => ANSWER: Aadhaar-based biometric Digital Life Certificates for pensioners | WHY: Jeevan Pramaan is a MeitY initiative providing Aadhaar-based biometric Digital Life Certificates for pensioners.
- Q: A citizen who wants to apply online for a new driving licence would use which portal of the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways? => ANSWER: SARATHI | WHY: SARATHI handles driving licence applications. VAHAN is for vehicle registration (the classic trap), Bhavishya is the pensioners' portal, and e-Pramaan is the national e-authentication framework.
- Q: Which of the following is NOT correct about DigiLocker? => ANSWER: Its documents are treated as equal to originals under the RTI Act, 2005 | WHY: DigiLocker documents get legal validity from the IT Act, 2000 and the Digital Locker Rules, 2016, not the RTI Act.
- Q: Which of the following is NOT correct about e-Sign? => ANSWER: It is based on blockchain rather than Public Key Infrastructure | WHY: e-Sign is based on Public Key Infrastructure (PKI), not blockchain.
- Q: GIMS, released as the Sandes app for secure government communication, stands for: => ANSWER: Government Instant Messaging System | WHY: GIMS is the Government Instant Messaging System, India's in-house secure messaging alternative built by NIC and released as the Sandes app.
