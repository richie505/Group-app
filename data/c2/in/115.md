## SEC 5:102:1 | Soil pollution
- Q: The build-up of toxic chemicals or radioactive material in soil is called: => ANSWER: Soil pollution | WHY: Soil pollution is the build-up of toxic chemicals or radioactive material in soil; it causes toxic crops and reduces vegetation.
- Q: Mercury pollution is mainly associated with: => ANSWER: Chlor-alkali industry effluent and fungicides | WHY: Mercury is associated with chlor-alkali effluent and fungicides; chromates with tanneries, cadmium with phosphate fertilisers and batteries.

- Q: DDT, a persistent organic pollutant, is listed under which convention? => ANSWER: Stockholm Convention | WHY: DDT is a persistent organic pollutant listed under the Stockholm Convention.
- Q: Under the Contaminated Sites Rules, sites are tested against how many hazardous chemicals? => ANSWER: 189 | WHY: The Rules, made under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986, test sites against 189 hazardous chemicals.
- Q: Which one of the following is NOT a measure for controlling soil pollution? => ANSWER: Watersheds | WHY: Crop rotation, biofertilisers and integrated pest management reduce chemical inputs and are soil-pollution controls. Watersheds are not a soil-pollution control measure (APPSC 2019 trap).
- Q: Fly ash from thermal power plants must be used in bricks, cement and roads within what distance of the plant? => ANSWER: 300 km | WHY: Fly ash must be used in bricks, cement and roads within 300 km of thermal plants; 50 km is the limit for using treated STP wastewater.
- Q: Adding nutrients to boost native microbes at a contaminated site is called: => ANSWER: Biostimulation | WHY: Biostimulation adds nutrients to boost native microbes; bioaugmentation adds pollutant-degrading microbes.
- Q: Oil Zapper, a bacterial consortium from TERI, is used to clean up: => ANSWER: Oily sludge and crude oil from oil spills | WHY: Oil Zapper is a consortium of bacteria from TERI that consumes oily sludge and crude oil from oil spills.
- Q: Which one of the following is NOT an alternative to conventional pollution-control methods? => ANSWER: Incineration | WHY: Green technology, bioremediation and carbon capture and storage are cited alternatives. Incineration is itself a conventional method, so it is not an alternative (APPSC-G2 2025 trap).
- Q: Oil Zapper, used against oil spills, is a: => ANSWER: Bacterial consortium developed by TERI | WHY: Oil Zapper is a bioremediation agent, a TERI bacterial consortium, not a chemical dispersant.
- Q: Consider the following statements: 1. DDT biomagnifies in marine food chains. 2. Mercury biomagnifies in marine food chains. 3. Dissolved oxygen biomagnifies in marine food chains. Which of the above statements is/are correct? => ANSWER: 1 and 2 only | WHY: DDT and mercury biomagnify through marine food chains; dissolved oxygen is not a pollutant and does not biomagnify.
- Q: The IMO Ballast Water Management Convention came into force in: => ANSWER: 2017 | WHY: The Convention, which addresses the spread of invasive species through ballast water, entered into force in 2017.
- Q: The Andhra Pradesh Pollution Control Board was set up in 1976 under which law? => ANSWER: Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974 | WHY: APPCB was set up in 1976 under the Water Act, 1974. - Q: The night-time ambient noise limit for a residential area in India is: => ANSWER: 45 dB(A) | WHY: The residential night limit is 45 dB(A); the silence-zone night limit is 40 dB(A) and the industrial night limit 70 dB(A).

- Q: A rise of about how many decibels roughly doubles the perceived loudness of a sound? => ANSWER: 10 dB | WHY: Every rise of about 10 dB roughly doubles perceived loudness.

- Q: 'Green mufflers' along roads refer to: => ANSWER: Rows of trees and shrubs that absorb noise | WHY: Green mufflers are rows of trees and shrubs along roads that help absorb noise.
- Q: The intensity of noise is measured in => ANSWER: Decibels | WHY: Noise is measured in decibels (dB) (APPSC 2025); dB(A) is weighted to human hearing. Hertz measures frequency, sievert measures biological radiation dose and becquerel measures radioactivity.
