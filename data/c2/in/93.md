- Q: For Census language classification, a language generally needs at least how many speakers? => ANSWER: 10,000 | WHY: The Census counts a language when it has 10,000 or more speakers.

- Q: The Sitakant Mohapatra Committee on inclusion of languages in the Eighth Schedule was constituted in: => ANSWER: 2003 | WHY: The Sitakant Mohapatra Committee was constituted in 2003 and the Ashok Pahwa Committee in 1996; there are no fixed criteria for inclusion.
- Q: Which language was the first to be recognised as a classical language of India? => ANSWER: Tamil | WHY: Tamil was recognised in 2004, Sanskrit in 2005, Kannada in 2008 and Odia in 2014.

- Q: The Scheme for Protection and Preservation of Endangered Languages (SPPEL) is run by: => ANSWER: Central Institute of Indian Languages, Mysuru | WHY: SPPEL, a Union Education Ministry scheme of 2013, is run by CIIL, Mysuru and covers languages with fewer than 10,000 speakers.
