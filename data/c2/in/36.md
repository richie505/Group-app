- Q: The three Lists under the Government of India Act, 1935 became which of the following in the Constitution of India? => ANSWER: Union List, State List and Concurrent List | WHY: The Federal, Provincial and Concurrent Lists of the 1935 Act developed into the Union, State and Concurrent Lists of the Constitution.

- Q: Which of the following is regarded as the single largest source of the provisions of the Indian Constitution? => ANSWER: The Government of India Act, 1935 | WHY: The Government of India Act, 1935 is regarded as the single largest source, particularly for the federal scheme, Governor's office, judiciary, Public Service Commissions, emergency provisions and administrative details.
- Q: The Indian Constitution is criticised as a 'bag of borrowings' mainly because it: => ANSWER: Drew many provisions from foreign constitutions | WHY: The criticism refers to the large number of provisions drawn from foreign constitutions and earlier laws; length is the 'elephantine' criticism and legal detail the 'lawyers' paradise' one.
