## SEC 2:63:0 | Electoral systems
- Q: Under first-past-the-post in Lok Sabha elections, a candidate wins by securing: => ANSWER: The most votes (plurality) | WHY: FPTP needs only a plurality in a single-member constituency, not a majority.

- Q: Which type of revision rebuilds the electoral roll comprehensively? => ANSWER: Special Intensive Revision | WHY: A Special Intensive Revision rebuilds the electoral roll, whereas summary revision is conducted annually.
- Q: The State Legislative Assembly is elected from which type of constituencies? => ANSWER: Single-member territorial constituencies | WHY: Assembly members are directly elected from single-member territorial constituencies, by first-past-the-post.
