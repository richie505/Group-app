- Q: How many secondary kin does a person have? => ANSWER: 33 | WHY: There are 8 primary, 33 secondary and 151 tertiary kin.
- Q: Which of the following is NOT an example of ritual or fictive kinship? => ANSWER: A father-son relationship by birth | WHY: A biological father-son tie is consanguineal; the others are ritual or fictive kinship.
## SEC 2:108:1 | Descent rules
- Q: Which of the following communities are associated with patrilineal descent? => ANSWER: Kondhs, Santhals and Bhils | WHY: Patrilineal descent is found among the Kondhs, Santhals and Bhils, while Khasi, Garo and Nair are examples of matrilineal communities.
- Q: Transmitting movable property through the mother and immovable property through the father is called: => ANSWER: Double (bilineal) descent | WHY: Different attributes through each parent is double descent; in bilateral descent both sides are treated equally.
- Q: Which one of the following correctly describes parallel and cross descent? => ANSWER: Parallel descent traces women to daughters and men to sons; cross descent traces the mother to the son and the father to the daughter. | WHY: Parallel descent follows women to daughters and men to sons. Cross descent follows the mother to the son and the father to the daughter.
## SEC 2:108:2 | Descent groups
- Q: A unilineal group descended from a known ancestor and generally practising exogamy is known as a => ANSWER: lineage | WHY: A lineage is a unilineal descent group whose members trace descent from a known ancestor and which is exogamous.
- Q: Which one of the following is correctly matched? => ANSWER: Phratry—two or more related clans | WHY: A phratry consists of two or more related clans. A clan believes in a common ancestor without a demonstrated link, gotra is an exogamous Hindu clan, and a moiety divides society into two halves.
## SEC 2:108:3 | Key terms
- Q: The same-gotra marriage prohibition is associated with => ANSWER: the Later Vedic period | WHY: Gotra is an exogamous Hindu clan, and the same-gotra bar dates from the Later Vedic period.
- Q: A residence arrangement in which the husband lives with his wife's family is called: => ANSWER: Matrilocal | WHY: In matrilocal residence the husband lives with the wife's family; in patrilocal residence the wife lives with the husband's.
- Q: Which of the following pairs is correctly matched? => ANSWER: Joint family – Multiple generations together | WHY: A joint family is several generations living together; avunculocal residence is with the maternal uncle, a nuclear family is parents and children, and consanguineal kin are blood relatives.
