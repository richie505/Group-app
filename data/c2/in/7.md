## SEC 1:61:15 | Gupta titles and epithets → ruler
- Q: Which one of the following Gupta rulers is referred to as ‘Lichchhavi-Dauhitra’ (Son of the daughter of the Lichchhavis)? => ANSWER: Samudragupta | WHY: The Gupta ruler Samudragupta was referred to as ‘Lichchhavi- Dauhitra’ (Grandson of the Lichchhavis). Samudragupta's mother, Kumaradevi, was a princess of the Lichchhavi dynasty, which is why he was given this title. Samudragupta's father was Chandragupta I. | PYQ: Chhattisgarh P.C.S. (Pre) 2024
- Q: The first Gupta ruler to assume the title of “Param Bhagavata” was => ANSWER: Samudragupta | WHY: The copper plate inscription of Samudragupta from Nalanda and Gaya describes param Bhagavata title of Samudragupta. Although UPPSC has given option (c) as correct answer while UGC has accepted Samudragupta as right answer. | PYQ: U.P.P.C.S. (Pre) 2009
