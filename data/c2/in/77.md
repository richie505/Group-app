- Q: Which of the following is NOT a feature of the AP (Agricultural Produce and Livestock) Markets Act, 1966? => ANSWER: Abolition of all licensed buyers from market transactions | WHY: The Act provides for licensed buyers; it does not abolish them. - Q: Each Rythu Bazar is managed by an Estate Officer and comes under which department? => ANSWER: Revenue Department | WHY: A Rythu Bazar is managed by an Estate Officer and functions under the Revenue Department.
- Q: In a Rythu Bazar, farmers sell their produce directly to: => ANSWER: Consumers | WHY: Farmers sell directly to consumers in Rythu Bazars, whereas in regulated market yards they sell to licensed traders.

- Q: Rythu Bazars in Andhra Pradesh have been functioning since: => ANSWER: January 1999 | WHY: Rythu Bazars were started by the AP government in 1999 and have functioned from January 1999 (PYQ 2018). The other dates do not relate to their start.
