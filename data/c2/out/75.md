# ROW 3:108 | Economic Survey - structure and key themes of the latest Survey
## SEC 3:108:0 | Preparer & format
### Who prepares it
- ★ Published every year by the **Ministry of Finance**, Govt. of India — flagship annual document of the Ministry
- Prepared by the **Economic Division**, **Department of Economic Affairs**, Ministry of Finance
- Under the guidance of the **Chief Economic Adviser**
- Presented in Parliament (both Houses) **before the Union Budget**
### Format and history
- First Survey was for **1950-51**
- Presented **separately from the Budget** only since **1964**
- Presenting it before the Budget is only a **convention** (Article 112 concerns the Annual Financial Statement)
### ES 2025-26
- New chapters added: **Artificial intelligence** and **urbanisation** (reconfigured Survey)
- Reforms of 2025 listed: **GST overhaul**, **100% FDI in insurance**, nuclear power opened to private sector, four **Labour Codes** notified, **QCOs** placed on hold
  - **Complete abolition of GST** was not among them

## SEC 3:108:2 | ES 2025-26 data
### Growth
- FY26 real GDP growth (First Advance Estimates) — **7.4%**
- FY27 projected — **6.8%-7.2%**; potential growth raised to **7.0%** from 6.5%
- FY26 sector growth: **Agriculture 3.1%** < **Manufacturing 7.0%** < **Services 9.1%**
- **Services** (9.1%) — the "**stabilising anchor**"
### Money, banks, external
- Repo rate cut by **125 bps** since February 2025
- Banks' gross NPAs at multi-decade low of **2.2%**
- High forex cover — treated as an **external strength**
  - Survey's concerns: **State revenue deficits** and **unconditional cash transfers**
- Public capex proposed for FY 2026-27 (Viksit Bharat @2047) — **₹12.2 lakh crore**; infrastructure as growth pillar
### Climate and earlier Surveys
- Climate chapter (scarce fiscal resources) favoured **adaptation over mitigation**; against diverting resources from health, agriculture, poverty reduction; called civilisational collapse unlikely
- **Deregulation** — central theme of **ES 2024-25**; ES 2023-24 spoke of "letting go" and lighter MSME inspections
- **Twin Balance Sheet** problem (ES 2016-17) — over-leveraged corporates + stressed banks choking private capex
  - Hidden NPAs exposed by the RBI **Asset Quality Review** of **2015** (not 2019); response: IBC and bank recapitalisation

## SEC 3:108:3 | ES 2025-26 themes

- ES 2022-23, response to supply disruptions — **cut fuel excise** and **increased food and fertiliser subsidy** support (not raising fuel taxes or cutting subsidies)

# ROW 3:109 | Basic features of the Andhra Pradesh economy after bifurcation - GSDP, growth rate, sectoral shares
## SEC 3:109:0 | Bifurcation & its effects
### Bifurcation and Hyderabad
- Bifurcated on **2 June 2014** under the **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014**
- **Hyderabad** — about **48%** of undivided AP's total revenue (₹70,548 crore) in 2012-13
- Chronic **revenue-deficit** State — loss of **Hyderabad's revenue base**, large agrarian population, irrigation and power liabilities
- New capital needed — the **capital** and major institutions stayed with **Telangana**; AP develops **Amaravati**
- Hyderabad-centred services and industry went to Telangana — **agriculture's share** in AP rose, industry's fell
- New **IT parks** — **Visakhapatnam**, **Tirupati**, **Vijayawada**
### AP at a glance
- Area — **4.96%** of India (about **1,62,970 sq km**); population **4.09%** (10th)
- GSDP rank — **8th** among States
- Coastline — **3rd** longest (**1,053.07 km**)
- 2025-26: **1st** in **SHG-bank linkage**; 3rd in MGNREGS employment
- Casual labour — **33.8%** of rural workers (India 22.5%); regular wage/salaried 13.7%
### Regions, districts, policy
- Three economic regions — **Visakhapatnam**, **Amaravati**, **Tirupati**
- **Aspirational Districts Programme** — **112** districts, incl. **Vizianagaram**; Backward Regions Grant Fund now defunct
- District GDDP/PCI pre- and post-2022 not comparable — district **boundaries and composition changed**
- Debt-GSDP 2024-25 RE — **35.15%**; capital outlay up from ₹16,141 crore to ₹33,135 crore; 370 loans restructured
- Draft **Population Management Policy 2026** — **₹25,000** for 2nd/3rd child; two-child norm for local polls removed; TFR **1.5**
- **SES 2025-26** (not tabled in Budget Session) — released **12 April 2026**; FAE growth **9.88%** vs India 7.4%
### India
- ★ Horticulture production outpaced food grains since **2012-13**

## SEC 3:109:5 | Records & sub-sectors
### Compilation and classification
- AP's GSDP is compiled by the **State Directorate of Economics & Statistics** (not NSO)

# ROW 3:110 | AP per capita income, district-level disparities and the state's position among Indian states
## SEC 3:110:0 | PCI terms
- Per capita income is based on **Net State Domestic Product** (net of depreciation), at current or constant prices
- Per capita GSDP (gross) and PCI (net NSDP) are **not interchangeable**

## SEC 3:110:1 | AP vs India PCI (current, ₹)
- 2025-26 AP PCI growth — **10.43%** vs India 6.9%; AP PCI above national average throughout
- Fastest PCI growth — **15.26%** in **2021-22**; slowest 4.10% in 2019-20
- Telangana PCI = **1.35 times** AP's in 2024-25 (1.3 times in 2013-14; 6% higher in 2004-05)
- **Rayalaseema** — 43% of AP's horticulture area, **52%** of its output
- **District Domestic Product** — compiled by the **State Directorate of Economics and Statistics** (not NSO)

## SEC 3:110:2 | Key PCI facts
- PCI = income / population — rises only if income grows **faster than population**
- PCI is an **average** — hides inequality and cost-of-living differences
- India's PCI fastest growth — **2021-22**, **18.6%** (post-COVID rebound)
- Swarna Andhra PCI target 2047 — above **$42,000**, about **₹55 lakh**; ₹5.43 lakh is the 2028-29 per capita GSDP target
- AP PCI above India's every year since **2014-15**; 2025-26 AP PCI about **1.34 times** the national figure
- **P4** scheme — targets the gap between high average PCI and persisting **poverty and inequality**

## SEC 3:110:3 | Historical baselines
- **National Income Committee (1951)** — India's PCI for 1948-49 at **₹225**
- AP's PCI — **₹7,781** in 1960 to about **₹2.07 lakh** in 2021-22

## SEC 3:110:4 | AP among States

# ROW 3:111 | Impact of bifurcation on the endowment of natural resources - minerals, forests, water, coastline
## SEC 3:111:0 | What each State got
### Land, people, coast
- Residual AP area — **1,60,205 sq km**, later shown as **1,62,970 sq km**; Telangana **1,12,077 sq km**
- Population (2011) — AP **4.96 crore**, Telangana 3.52 crore; APRA ratio **58.32 : 41.68**
- **Residual AP** kept the entire **coastline** and all ports (Telangana is landlocked)
### Minerals and energy
- **Coal** went mainly to **Telangana**; AP kept coastal and basin minerals
- **Mangampet** (YSR Kadapa) — India's largest **barytes** deposit
- **Tummalapalle** (YSR Kadapa) — **uranium**; bauxite in **Araku, Chintapalli, Paderu**
- Onshore oil and gas royalties — to the **producing State**; gas allocation per GoI policy
- **Twelfth Schedule** — existing **SCCL** coal linkages for AP plants **kept unchanged**; new ones under GoI coal policy
- AP got about **46%** of Central Generating Station power despite about **58%** population — CGS power split by **last five years' consumption**
### Forest frameworks
- **Green India Mission** — **5 mha** new forest cover + **5 mha** quality improvement (not 10 mha new)
- **CAMPA** (MoEFCC) — spends **compensatory levies** for forest land diverted to non-forest use on **afforestation**
- **FRA 2006** — Gram Sabha recommends diversion for **public utilities** (not commercial mining); recognises PVTG habitat rights, MFP ownership, nistar rights

## SEC 3:111:1 | Coastline & ports
- Coastline (2023-24 remeasurement) — **1,053.07 km**; **3rd** after Gujarat and Tamil Nadu; older figure 974 km
- **Visakhapatnam** — AP's one **major port**; **17** notified non-major ports; four greenfield ports under construction
- **Krishnapatnam** — most cargo among notified non-major ports

## SEC 3:111:2 | Minerals: where in AP
- **Limestone** — AP **3rd** largest producer after Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh; Kurnool, Kadapa, Guntur, Palnadu, NTR
- Beach-sand minerals (ilmenite, rutile, zircon, garnet, monazite) — **Srikakulam, Visakhapatnam, Kakinada** coast
- Old districts — highest forest cover **Visakhapatnam**; largest forest area **YSR Kadapa**; least **Krishna**

## SEC 3:111:3 | Water (Schedule XI, APRA)
### Schedule XI and Krishna
- Priority — **drinking water** > irrigation > power; tribunal allocations already made unchanged
- KWDT-I 811 TMC (2014 ad hoc) — **AP 512 TMC**, **Telangana 299 TMC** (**66 : 34**); 58:42 population ratio not used
- Srisailam and Nagarjuna Sagar — **left-bank** power houses in **Telangana**, right-bank in AP
### Polavaram
- National project under **Section 90**; Telangana's consent deemed given
- **960 MW**; irrigates **7.20 lakh acres**; transfers **80 TMC** to the Krishna basin
### Regions
- **Rayalaseema** — rain-shadow, drought-prone; Godavari, Krishna, Penna deltas — fertile coastal alluvium
- Agro-climatic zones — **6**
- AP kept the coast, deltas, Krishna-Godavari tail-end; **lost** coal and the **Hyderabad industrial base**

## SEC 3:111:4 | Forests (ISFR 2023)
- **ISFR** — published **every two years** by the **Forest Survey of India**, **Dehradun**
- Forest + tree cover — **21.74%** of AP's geographical area
- Recorded Forest Area (ISFR 2021) — **37,258 sq km**, ranked **eighth**
- Forest cover — tree canopy density at least **10%**, regardless of ownership or legal status
