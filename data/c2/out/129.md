## SEC 6:49:0 | OPS vs NPS vs UPS
### The three schemes
| Scheme | Nature | Pension |
|---|---|---|
| OPS | — | **50% of last basic + DA** |
| NPS | **Defined contribution** | **Market annuity** |
| UPS | **Hybrid** (assured payout) | **50% of average basic of last 12 months** for 25 years' service |
- **NPS** for government employees began in **2004** (not 2005)
- NPS: **60%** of corpus withdrawn tax-free, **40%** to annuity
- **UPS** effective from **1 April 2025**; **employee contribution** required
- UPS employee share is **10%** (not 14%)
- Choice of UPS is **irreversible**
- Minimum pension of ₹10,000 under OPS — marked **incorrect**
### Other facts on this page
- India's **tiger** population: about **1,411** (2006) → over **3,000** (2023) — cited in Tribal Affairs Ministry's conservation policy
- SEBI's proposed MII reforms: **Critical Operations** vertical must be headed by an **Executive Director** (Commercial Interests and Business Development — ED only at MII's discretion)

## SEC 6:49:2 | Draft Seeds Bill 2025
### Draft Seeds Bill, 2025
- Prepared by the **Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare**
- Replaces the **Seeds Act, 1966** and **Seeds (Control) Order, 1983**
- Traceability: onboarding on the **SATHI Portal** + mandatory **QR code** labelling
- Varieties notified under the 1966 Act — **deemed registered**
- Market seeds need **VCU**-based registration; VCU = **Value for Cultivation and Use**
- Existing varieties — **three-year provisional** registration
- Multi-state seed firms deemed registered under the **Central Accreditation System**
- Unregistered imported varieties — only for **research or trials**
- Concern: **foreign entities** can conduct VCU testing (GM seed risk); seed banks, FPOs, women collectives may be treated as commercial units
