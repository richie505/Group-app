# ROW 1:210 | Andhra in the freedom movement - Alluri Sitarama Raju and the Rampa Rebellion, Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya
## SEC 1:210:0 | Agency grievances
- ★ **1879** — Rampa rebellion spread to **Rekapalli** (acquired by the British from the Nizam) against forest restrictions on **podu** cultivation
- **Madras Forest Act, 1882** — reserved forests, restricted **podu** and minor forest produce, curbed grazing
- **Begar** — **forced unpaid labour** (not voluntary paid labour)
- Rampa / Manyam Rebellion based in the **Gudem hills**, Agency area of **East Godavari**

## SEC 1:210:1 | Rampa Rebellion 1922-24
### Earlier rebellions (1879-80)
- **Rekapalle rebellion** — caused by an increase in the **axe tax** during a famine
- **Rampa rebellion 1879-80** — arose from **Madhava Bhupati**'s exploitation
- **Sullivan** removed Madhava Bhupati; rebellion crushed in **November 1880**
### Nature and causes (1922-24)
- Lasted **January 1922 to May 1924** (about 2 years 5 months), Agency tracts of **East Godavari** and **Visakhapatnam**
- Armed tribal rebellion in the **Agency area**; not affiliated with the Congress; troubled the British more than the Non-Cooperation Movement
- Causes: restrictions on **forest and podu** rights, **begar** for road building, oppressive **Muttadari** system, actions of Tahsildar **Bastian**
  - **Permanent settlement** was NOT a cause
### Events
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| August 1922 | Police stations raided: **Chintapalli** (first), **Krishnadevipeta** |
| September 1922 | Direct battle with British forces at **Panjerighat** (Damanghat) (APPSC 2012 key) |
| 24 September 1922 | Officers **Scott Coward** and **Heiter** killed at **Damanapalli ghat** |
| 6 December 1922 | **Peddagaddapalem** — first defeat of Raju's forces by the **Malabar Special Police** |

- Raju was wounded at **Lingapuram**
- ★ Tomb (samadhi) / last rites of Alluri — **Krishnadevipeta**
### Chronology of revolts
- **Gulugonda** tribal revolt (1845-48) → **Phadke**'s revolt (1879) → **Khudiram Bose**, Muzaffarpur (1908) → **Alluri**'s Rampa rebellion (1922-24)

## SEC 1:211:1 | Three poets
### Rayaprolu Subba Rao
- **Janmabhoomi** ("Ye desamegina ... pogadara nee talli bhoomi Bharatini") — **Rayaprolu Subbarao**
- **Father of Telugu Romanticism** — pioneered **Bhava Kavitvam**, drawing on **Shelley and Keats**
- Moved away from **translation of Sanskrit epics**; introduced **original, emotive verse**; helped free Telugu poetry from rigid prosody
- **Bhavakavitvam** — **romantic and personal emotion**, nature and love; freedom from classical prosody
### Garimella Satyanarayana
- **First people's poet** of the freedom struggle
- Song cyclostyled **a thousand times**; praised by **Mahatma Gandhi**
- Jailed **1922** for **Maakoddee Tella Doratanamu**; later served **2.5 years RI**
### Gurajada and others
- Gurajada — made **vyavaharika bhasha** (spoken Telugu) a normal literary medium
