# ROW 3:124 | Agricultural marketing in Andhra Pradesh - market yards, Rythu Bazaars and e-marketing
## SEC 3:124:0 | Acts & rules
- **AP (AP&L) Markets Rules, 1969** — framed under **Section 33** of the 1966 Act
- **1966 AMC framework** — **remained operative** after the 2020 farm laws were repealed (**November 2021**)
- **Model APMC Act** — **single market fee**; registration in place of licensing; farmers' and consumers' markets
## SEC 3:124:1 | How a regulated market is set up
- Step 1: Government declares a **notified area**
- Step 2: constitutes a **Market Committee**
- Step 3: Market Committee establishes the **notified market**
## SEC 3:124:2 | Market yard vs Rythu Bazar
- **AP (Agricultural Produce and Livestock) Markets Act, 1966** — provides for **licensed buyers** (does not abolish them)
  - covers crops, livestock and livestock products; promotes open auctions, standard weights, recorded prices
- Rythu Bazar — managed by an **Estate Officer**, under the **Revenue Department**
- Rythu Bazar: farmers sell directly to **consumers**; regulated market yard: farmers sell to **licensed traders**
## SEC 3:124:3 | Rythu Bazaars
- **Joint Collector** — coordinating officer for Rythu Bazars at district level (not the District Collector)
- Prices fixed at about **25%** in relation to open-market prices (above wholesale, below local retail)
- Started by AP government in **1999**; functioning from **January 1999**
- For perishables: Government **procures through Rythu Bazars** to stabilise prices (quickest market-intervention channel)
## SEC 3:124:4 | Market fee & storage
- AP warehouse godowns (2025-26): **1,052** godowns (9.65 lakh MT); **643** operational (5.92 lakh MT)
- Main post-harvest gap: **limited operational storage** and need for more **cold storage**
## SEC 3:124:5 | Schemes
- **Agriculture Infrastructure Fund** — farm-gate infrastructure at **Rythu Bharosa Kendras**; conversion of PACS into multipurpose facility centres
- **Raitu Bandhu** (AMCs) — **pledge loan**: store produce in market-yard godowns and borrow against it; avoids distress sales
## SEC 3:124:6 | Exam corner
- **e-NAM** — mainly helped in **price discovery**; not yet fully operational for payments or inter-mandi trade
## SEC 3:124:8 | Market institutions → functions and features
- ★ Rytu Bazaars — farmer-to-consumer direct marketing yards, established by AP Government in **1999**
- ★ Day-to-day management by an **Estate Officer**; coordination by **Joint Collector** — **not** the District Collector
- ★ Rythu Bazars functioning from **January 1999**
## SEC 3:124:10 | Schemes → purpose (storage, price intervention, FPOs)
- ★ Ryot Bazar scheme (direct farmer-to-consumer sale, no middlemen) — first introduced in **Punjab**; AP **second** State
