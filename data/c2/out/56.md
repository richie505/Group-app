# ROW 2:140 | Rights of persons with disabilities - RPwD Act 2016, UNCRPD, accessibility (Sugamya Bharat)
## SEC 2:140:0 | RPwD Act 2016: number game
### Definition & numbers
- **Section 2(s)** — person with disability: long-term **physical, mental, intellectual or sensory** impairment which, in interaction with **barriers**, hinders full and effective participation
- Specified disabilities raised from 7 to **21** (incl. autism, acid attack victims, sickle cell disease); Centre can add more
- Reservation: **4%** in government jobs, **5%** in higher education seats
- Passed 2016; came into force **19 April 2017**
- Survivors of forcible **acid ingestion** recognised as acid attack victims after a **Supreme Court** interpretation in **2026**
### Bodies & institutes
- **Chief Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities** — statutory authority under the Act; monitors implementation, takes up complaints
- **SVNIRTAR** — **Cuttack**; visual-disability institute at **Dehradun**; **NIEPID** at **Secunderabad**
### Courts & education
- Safe **footpaths** — part of right to life under **Article 21**; minimum width **1.8 metres** (Supreme Court)
- Supreme Court: **prison manuals** to be revised for accessibility of prisoners with disabilities, in line with the **RPwD Act, 2016**
- **Samagra Shiksha** inclusive education: assessment, aids, assistive technology, therapy for children with disabilities — **not** exclusive education of gifted children

# ROW 2:141 | Rights of the elderly - Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act 2007, schemes
## SEC 2:141:0 | Senior Citizens Act 2007
### The Act
- Maintenance duty on **biological, adopted and step-children**, and **legal heirs** (as relevant)
- **Section 20** — **medical care** of senior citizens; basis of the National Programme for Health Care of the Elderly
- **Section 23** — Tribunal may declare a **property transfer void** if transferee fails to give promised care
- Brought into force **state by state** from **29 December 2007** (not one date for all)
- **2019 Amendment Bill** (remove maintenance ceiling) — **not passed**
- **Himachal Pradesh** — first State law: Maintenance of Parents and Dependents Act, **2001**
### Schemes
- **Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana** (BPL seniors) — funded from **Senior Citizens' Welfare Fund**; at least **30%** women beneficiaries per district
- **IPOP** (AVYAY component) — up to **90%** of project cost for NGOs (old-age homes, day-care centres, mobile medicare units)

## SEC 2:141:1 | Tribunals (AP)
- AP Maintenance Tribunal — sub-division level, headed by **Revenue Divisional Officer or Sub-Collector**
- Maximum maintenance **Rs 10,000** per month; case to be decided within **90 days**; appeal allowed

# ROW 2:142 | Population problem, ageing and problems of the elderly
## SEC 2:142:0 | Demographic transition
- Stages: high birth and death rates → **death rate falls** → **birth rate falls** → both low
- India now — **youth-bulge** stage

## SEC 2:142:1 | Growth phases
- **1921** — **Great Divide** (after decline 1911–1921); explosion 1961–1971; high but slowing 1981–2011
- **Integrated Programme for Older Persons** — NGOs get up to **90%** of cost of old age homes

## SEC 2:142:2 | Population data
### Figures
- Census 2011: decadal growth 2001-11 **17.7%**; population about **121.09 crore**; **68.9%** rural
- NFHS-5 TFR: India **2.0**, Andhra Pradesh **1.7**
- SRS 2024 TFR: India **1.9**; rural **2.1**; urban **1.5**
- India's TFR below replacement (2.1) for the **fifth year**; lowest **Delhi (1.2)**; **Bihar** highest-fertility State
### Debates
- Population weight in **Finance Commission** formulas — **15–25%**
- Draft **Population Management Policy** — **Andhra Pradesh** (Kerala: first with senior-citizens framework)

## SEC 2:142:3 | Effects & debates
- About **9.7 million** new working-age people each year; demographic dividend only if employed
- **Javed v. State of Haryana (2003)** — SC **upheld** Haryana's **two-child norm**
- AP draft **Population Management Policy** (March 2026) — control → management; **Rs 25,000** for a second or third child; **removes two-child norm** for local elections

## SEC 2:142:4 | Problems of the elderly
- Ageing does **not** eliminate health expenditure: 80+ face chronic disease; insurance age caps; elderly about **30–35%** of hospitalisation spending
- About **78%** of elderly have **no pension**; about **70%** depend on others
- **NPSC 2011** — **ageing in place**; highlighted **destitution among rural elderly**

## SEC 2:142:5 | Exam corner
- **Dependency ratio** = (0–14 + **65+**) ÷ (**15–64**)
- **Kerala** — first State with Senior Citizens' Welfare Department, Commission and **Elderly Budget**
- **NPOP** — **1999** (NPSC 2011; Senior Citizens Act 2007)

# ROW 2:143 | Transgender persons - NALSA judgment, Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act 2019
## SEC 2:143:0 | NALSA v Union of India
- **NALSA v. Union of India** — **15 April 2014**; Justices **K.S. Radhakrishnan** and **A.K. Sikri**; recognised transgender gender identity
- Gender identity is **self-determined** — not by biological examination or State verification
- **2026 amendment** criticised for **reversing self-identification** (medical certification)

## SEC 2:143:1 | Legal journey
- **Rights of Transgender Persons Bill, 2014** — private member's Bill by **Tiruchi Siva** (DMK)
- Passed Rajya Sabha **April 2015** — first private member's Bill there in over four decades; not passed by Lok Sabha
- **2019 Act** criticisms: **no reservation**; lighter penalties than similar crimes against women; District Magistrate gatekeeping of identity
- **National Council for Transgender Persons** — statutory body under the **2019 Act**

## SEC 2:143:2 | 2019 Act vs 2026 Amendment
- 2019 Act: certificate of identity issued by **District Magistrate** (revised one after surgery); National Council only advises and monitors
- 2026 amendment: **excludes self-perceived identity**; omits **Section 4(2)**

## SEC 2:143:3 | Bodies & schemes

- **SMILE** (MoSJE, **2022**, umbrella Central Sector scheme) — **Support for Marginalized Individuals for Livelihood and Enterprise**

# ROW 2:144 | Minority rights - Articles 29 and 30, National Commission for Minorities, Sachar Committee, schemes
## SEC 2:144:0 | Meaning & notified minorities
### Who is a minority
- **NCM Act** does not define 'minority'; **Section 2(c)** — any community **notified by the Central Government**
- **States** can designate their own minorities (for State purposes); **11 States** had declared Jains a minority before 2014
- After **T.M.A. Pai Foundation (2002)** — minority status decided by population in the **State concerned**, not all India
### Notified communities
- **1993**: Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Parsis; **2014**: **Jains** — so six notified communities (**Jews** not included)
- ★ Jains — Cabinet decision **20 January 2014**; notified as **Sixth Minority Community** on 27 January 2014
### Related bodies
- **NCMEI** — determines minority status, decides affiliation disputes, issues minority certificates; civil court powers; **cannot amend the Constitution**
- **Criminal Tribes Act, 1871** (branded Denotified Tribes) — **repealed in 1952**
- **SEED** portal — DNT online applications (health insurance, coaching, housing); run by **Department of Social Justice**, not NTA

## SEC 2:144:1 | Constitutional articles
### Articles
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **29(1)** | protects **language, script or culture** of any distinct section of citizens |
| **29(2)** | no denial of admission to State-maintained/aided institutions on grounds only of **religion, race, caste or language** (sex and place of birth not listed) |
| **30** | State cannot discriminate in **aid** to minority institutions; may regulate standards without destroying minority character |
| **30(2)** | no discrimination in aid on ground of minority management (**religious or linguistic**) |
| **30(1A)** | protects minority institutions on compulsory acquisition of property — **44th Amendment** |
| **350A** | **mother-tongue** instruction at primary stage |
| **350B** | **Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities** — appointed by and reports to the **President** |
- ★ Special Officer for linguistic minorities — **Article 350B**
- 'Minority' used in **Articles 29, 30, 350A and 350B**, never defined
- **350A and 350B** inserted by the **7th Amendment, 1956** (States reorganisation)
- Religious minorities — **NCM** and Ministry of Minority Affairs; linguistic minorities — identified **State-wise**, Special Officer under 350B
### Cases & bodies
- **T.M.A. Pai Foundation (2002)** — **11-judge** Bench; State as unit; regulation but not control of minority institutions
- **NCM** functions: evaluate development, monitor safeguards, examine complaints, recommend measures (not conducting elections to minority institutions); **civil court**-like powers (summon witnesses, call records)
- **NCMEI** — **2004** Act; statutory State Minority Commissions in **19** States/UTs incl. Andhra Pradesh
- Section 2(c) criticised for giving Centre **'unbridled power'** to notify minorities
- **Justice Mohammed Sardar Ali Khan** — first NCM chairman; **Ranganath Misra Commission** studied religious and linguistic minorities
### Committees & schemes
- **Sachar Committee** — set up **2005** by PM, Justice **Rajinder Sachar**; report **November 2006**
- **PM's New 15 Point Programme** — **2006**; about **15%** of targets/outlays earmarked; education, jobs, living conditions, prevention of **communal riots**; review committees
- **PM-YASASVI** — **30%** slots for girls; **60:40** cost sharing
- **PM Vishwakarma** — **Rs 1 lakh** then **Rs 2 lakh** collateral-free at **5%**; nodal **Ministry of MSME** (MSDE and DFS also involved)
- **Communal Award** — **1932**; National Integration Council **1961**; National Foundation for Communal Harmony **1992**
### Finance corporations & DNT/BC

|---|---|---|

- NBCFDC **New Swarnima** — **women**; **Saksham** — young professionals; **Virasat** artisan loans — NMDFC
- **Idate Commission** — about **267-268** DNTs never classified; about 1,200 already in SC/ST/OBC lists
- AP **G.O. 1065** (August 2026) — **33.33%** BC reservation in ULB polls; AP's **138** BC communities in Groups A-E
