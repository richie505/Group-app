# ROW 4:99 | Energy resources - coal, petroleum, natural gas, hydel, nuclear and renewable; distribution in India and AP
## SEC 4:99:0 | Capacity & targets
### Energy mix and targets
- **Solar and wind** — 'energy-transition sources' (shift to cleaner, non-fossil energy)
- Solar plants use **solar radiation**, not coal; thermal plants burn fossil fuels; gasoline and many plastics come from petroleum
- **Coal** — about **55%** of India's primary energy; over **70%** of electricity
- **Solar** — largest RE component (about **164.59 GW**); wind about 58.14 GW
- Wind potential at **120 m** — about **695.5 GW**
- India's global rank — **3rd** in RE, **3rd** in solar, **4th** in wind capacity
- Natural gas share target — from about 6.7% to **15%** by **2030**
- About **10,600 MW** — India's **geothermal potential** (not installed capacity)
- **Net-zero** target — **2070** (2050 for many developed countries)
- **Revised SHAKTI** policy (May 2025) — coal allocated through **two windows**
- **SHANTI Act, 2025** — repeals **Atomic Energy Act, 1962** and **CLNDA, 2010**; admits **private companies** to nuclear power
### Hydel
- Most hydel potential — **Himalayan basins** (Brahmaputra, Indus, Ganga): perennial snow-fed flow, steep gradients; Peninsular rivers seasonal
- Highest hydel potential State — **Arunachal Pradesh** (Brahmaputra basin)
- Hydel capacity — about **48 GW** (June 2025)
- Off-stream closed-loop **pumped storage** — no longer needs **Central Electricity Authority** concurrence
- **Srisailam** hydel project — on the **Krishna**, AP–Telangana border; construction began **1968**
- **Baira Siul** — **Chamba**, Himachal Pradesh
### Renewables and grid
- **Bhadla Solar Park** — near **Jodhpur, Rajasthan**; **2,245 MW**; world's largest solar park
- **Green Energy Corridor** — dedicated transmission carrying RE power to the grid
- **PM Surya Ghar** — 1–3 kW rooftop system gives up to **300 free units/month**
- Wind-energy States listed — **Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Andhra Pradesh** (not Odisha)
- About **150 GW** of solar goes offline after sunset — night-time grid stress
- Battery storage target — about **47 GW** by **2032**
### Oil, thermal and world hydro
- India imports about **88%** of its crude oil
- **Strait of Hormuz** closure (March 2026) — India fell back on **coal and gas**, imported **LNG and LPG** from other suppliers
