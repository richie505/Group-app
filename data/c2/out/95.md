# ROW 4:82 | India - agriculture: commercial crops (cotton, sugarcane, oilseeds, jute, tea, coffee, rubber, spices) and their regions
## SEC 4:82:0 | Crop → conditions → states
### Fibre crops
- **Cotton** — about **21–37°C**, **50–70 cm** rainfall, **180–200 frost-free days**; **black (regur) soil**
- Major cotton States — **Gujarat**, Maharashtra, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh (black-soil regions)
- Black soils suit **cotton**, chillies and tobacco
- **Jute** — hot humid climate, heavy rainfall, **alluvial flood plains**; leads in the **Ganga-Brahmaputra delta**
- Indian jute goods face export competition mainly from **Bangladesh**; cotton competes with **synthetic fibres**
### Sugarcane
- **Uttar Pradesh** leads in both sugarcane **area and production**; Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu follow (tropical belt: higher yields, smaller area)
- Long-duration crop — **10–15 months**; hot humid conditions; alluvial or black soil
- Sugar mills sit near cane fields — cane is a **weight-losing** raw material (loses weight and sugar after cutting)
- Sugarcane suffers **red rot**
### Plantation crops
- Plantation crops (tea, coffee, rubber, coconut) — **labour-intensive and capital-intensive**; colonial plantations European-dominated, export-oriented
- **Rubber** — hot humid climate (**25–35°C**), heavy rainfall, **equatorial climate with over 200 cm rain**; **laterite soil** of the Western Ghats; not a crop of the delta flood plains
- **Tea** — moderate temperature, high rainfall, **well-drained slopes**
- **Araku** and ASR district — AP's **coffee** clusters; **Kodagu** in Karnataka, **Nilgiris** in Tamil Nadu
### Spices and other crops
- ★ **Kerala** — black pepper and cardamom; leading State for spices and rubber
- **Saffron** — **Jammu and Kashmir** (not Kerala)
- AP ranks **first in chilli**, **second in turmeric**
- **Red chilli** — warm climate; major crop of **Andhra Pradesh**
- **Lakadong** (high-curcumin turmeric) — **Meghalaya**; Meghalaya also leads in **pineapple**; Arunachal grows over half of India's **kiwi**
- **Groundnut** came to India from **Brazil**
- **Soybean** — needs a warm growing season; is a major oilseed (true, but being an oilseed does not explain the warm season)
- Water-efficient crops — **millets**, pulses, oilseeds; paddy in Punjab and sugarcane in Maharashtra reflect subsidy distortions
- **MIDH** funding — **60:40**; **90:10** for NE and Himalayan States; sub-schemes NHM, HMNEH, National Bamboo Mission

## SEC 4:86:0 | Industry orientation
### Raw material and market
- **Sugar** — raw-material-based (cane weight-losing, perishable)
- **Iron and steel** — raw-material-based (about 4 t input per tonne); **cement** — raw-material-based on limestone
- **Bakery** — market-based; market-based products are **weight-gaining or fragile** (bakeries, soft drinks)

- **Aluminium** — power-oriented
### Footloose and Weber
- ★ **Software** — footloose (can be located anywhere, not tied to resources or transport)
- Footloose — **light inputs**, **negligible transport costs**
- Footloose examples — **diamond polishing, electronics assembly, software**
- **Material Index above 1** (weight-losing: iron and steel, sugar) — pulls to **raw material**, not market
