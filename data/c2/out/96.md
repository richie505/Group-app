# ROW 4:87 | India - iron and steel, aluminium and other metallurgical industries: location and plants
## SEC 4:87:0 | Crude steel output (MT)
- India's crude steel production 2025–26: **168.4 MT**
  - **10.7%** increase; about **9% CAGR** since 2021–22

## SEC 4:87:1 | Steel at a glance
### Raw materials
- ★ India to remain self-sufficient for the next three decades in **coking coal** (IAS 1993 framing)
  - Energy Policy report (Planning Commission): petroleum reserves to deplete in **22 years**, gas **30 years**, coal **80 years**
- Domestic coking coal is **high in ash** → large coking coal imports
### Process and products
| Process | Share |
|---|---|
| Blast furnace–basic oxygen furnace (BF–BOF) | **43%** |
| Electric arc furnace (EAF) | **22%** |
| Induction furnace | **35%** |
- India is the leading producer of **sponge iron** (direct reduced iron, **DRI**)
### Plants and places
- **Dahej** (Gujarat) — **HINDALCO** copper plant using imported concentrate
  - Zawar — Hindustan Zinc; Visakhapatnam — zinc smelter; Thoothukudi — Sterlite copper
- Vizag steel plant: alternatives considered were **Bailadila, Goa, Neyveli, Salem, Hospet** (not **Durgapur**)

## SEC 4:87:2 | National Steel Policy 2017 (by 2030-31)
- NSP 2017 target by 2030-31: **300 MT** capacity, **255 MT** production
- National Green Hydrogen Mission: Ministry of Steel has **Rs 455 crore** for green-hydrogen pilots in steel till **2029-30**
  - Rs 25,938 crore = PLI-Auto; Rs 33,660 crore = BHAVYA; Rs 3,000 crore = NICDIT (BE 2026-27)

## SEC 4:87:5 | Copper, zinc, lead

## SEC 4:88:2 | Location factors
### Sugar
- ★ **Ukraine** — produces sugar almost entirely from **sugar beet**
- Sugar — India's **second-largest** agro-based industry, after **cotton textiles**
- Mills within about **20 km** of cane: cane is **perishable**, yields only **9–12%** sugar
- **Meerut–Saharanpur–Muzaffarnagar** — western UP belt; Gorakhpur, Deoria, Basti — eastern UP
- AP sugar belts: **West Godavari, East Godavari, Visakhapatnam, Chittoor** (not Nandyal — a cement cluster)
- **UP, Maharashtra, Karnataka** — leading sugar centres
### Jute
- About **90%** of jute mill capacity along the **Hooghly**
- **JUTE-ICARE** — certified seed and microbial retting with **SONA**
- **Bhatpara** — jute centre (not paper)
### Cement
- Inputs: **limestone, silica, alumina, gypsum**; also fly ash and slag
- **Satna, Katni** — Madhya Pradesh clusters
- **Yerraguntla** — **YSR Kadapa**; Jaggayyapeta — NTR district; Kadapa–Kurnool limestone makes **Rayalaseema** a cement hub
- Cement leads in AP, Rajasthan, MP, Karnataka; **Lakheri** — cement
### Paper
- Inputs: bamboo, bagasse, poplar, eucalyptus, softwood, rags (not rock phosphate)
- **Jagiroad** — **Assam**; Sirpur — Telangana; Rajahmundry — AP; Ballarpur — Maharashtra; **Titagarh** — paper
### Fertiliser
- Inputs: **naphtha, natural gas, coal, rock phosphate, sulphur, gypsum**; steel slag and coke as by-products
- Rock phosphate — fertiliser input
- Urea — **ammonia** (Haber process) + **carbon dioxide**; rock phosphate + sulphuric acid → superphosphate
- Sulphur for sulphuric acid — mainly from **oil refineries**; natural gas (methane) is ammonia feedstock
- Fertiliser centres: Sindri and Chota Nagpur steel towns
### Steel and others
- Chota Nagpur steel cluster: steelmaking is **weight-losing** (about **4 tonnes** inputs per tonne steel); region gives iron ore, coking coal, limestone, dolomite
- **IISCO, Burnpur** — pre-1947 private; taken over by government **1976**; associated with SAIL
- PAT scheme moving to Carbon Credit Trading Scheme for **9** sectors (cement, fertilisers, paper, textiles, petrochemicals, refining...)
- **Kota** — synthetic silk; Ludhiana — hosiery; Indore — cotton textiles; Sakkar Nagar — sugar
- **Cottage industry** — first industry to develop in India
