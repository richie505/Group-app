# ROW 1:45 | Schools of art - Gandhara, Mathura and Amaravati compared
## SEC 1:45:0 | Three schools compared
### At a glance
| School | Region | Material | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gandhara | **north-west** | **grey schist**, later **stucco** | foreign-influenced, Buddhist (**Mahayana**) |
| Mathura | **Ganga valley** | **spotted red sandstone** | **indigenous** and **multi-religious** |
| Amaravati | **Andhra** | **white Palnadu limestone** | indigenous and **narrative** |
### Gandhara
- Mainly **Mahayana** Buddhism; Buddha shown in human form with **Bodhisattvas** (**Avalokiteshvara**, **Maitreya**), not only through symbols
- **Realistic, Apollo-like faces** and **spiritual calm**
- Heavy **toga-like robes**, **wavy hair** with a top-knot
### Mathura
- **Jnanachakra** halo behind the Buddha's head — Mathura feature
- **Standing Buddha** images prominent in the **Gupta** phase
- **Gupta stone sculpture** — continuation of the **Mathura** school (greater elegance and softness)
### Amaravati
- **Slender, dynamic, emotional** figures with intense movement
- **Crowded narrative** compositions; **Yakshas and Gandharvas** dancing around the Buddha
- Mature phase linked with the **Ikshvakus** and Mahayana themes (not the Kushanas)
### Periods
- **Gandhara** and **Mathura** — **Kushana**-period schools
- **Sarnath** style — **Gupta** period
- **Pala** school — **8th–12th centuries**

## SEC 1:45:1 | Buddha image firsts
- Buddha first widely shown in **human form** in the **Kushana** period (**1st c. CE**), by both **Gandhara** and **Mathura**; which came first is **debated**; Amaravati was not the first
- Before human images: **aniconic** worship through **throne, footprints, wheel, tree and stupa**
- **Chaitika Vada** (symbol worship) in Andhra — introduced by **Mahadeva**
- **Amaravati Ayakapatas** show the Buddha through **symbols**, not in human form
- Evidence for Kushana-period ports and trade — **Periplus of the Erythraean Sea** and **Arikamedu** excavation

## SEC 1:45:2 | Gandhara & Mathura
- **Gandhara** — **Hellenistic Greek, Roman and Persian** influences combined with Indian traditions
- **Mathura** — early iconic images of **Shiva, Vishnu and Surya**; also **Jain Tirthankaras** and **Yaksha-Yakshi** figures

## SEC 1:45:3 | Amaravati school (AP)
### Praise and influence
- **James Fergusson** — publicised Amaravati art in **Tree and Serpent Worship**; called it the **'Epitome of Indian architecture'**
- **Percy Brown** — called it the **pinnacle of Indian art**
- Influence spread to **Sri Lanka** (esp. **Anuradhapura**), **South-East Asia** and later **Gupta** art
- **Purna-Kumbha** motif — emblem of the **Andhra Pradesh State Government**
### Sites and makers
- Oldest stupa in AP — **Bhattiprolu**
- **Nagarjunakonda** — main centre under the **Ikshvakus**; **Ghantasala** another main centre
- Sculptors of the Amaravati school — **Selavardhakulu**
- First depiction of **Sati** in Indian art — Amaravati-school sculpture of the **Ikshvaku** period

## SEC 1:45:5 | Exam corner
- **Hinayana** — **symbolic worship** of the Buddha
- **Mahayana** — encouraged worship of Buddha **images**

## SEC 1:45:6 | Buddha image — FIRSTs and the origin debate
- ★ Oldest sculpture of the Buddha — **Mathura** style
- ★ First human statues worshipped in India — of the **Buddha** (idol worship by the **Mahayana** sect)
- ★ **Sarnath** Buddha image in **Bhumisparsha** mudra — **Gupta** period

## SEC 1:45:7 | Gandhara and Mathura schools → region, patron, features
- ★ Indian craftsmen's interaction with **Greeks and Romans** — **Gandhara** art
- ★ Gandhara — synthesis of **Indian and Greek** art; also called **Greco-Buddhist / Indo-Greek / Greco-Roman**
  - ★ developed from merger of **Greek, Syrian, Persian and Indian** influences; flourished in the **Saka and Kushan** era
- ★ Gandhara bears **Hellenistic** influence (realistic sculptures, drapery), but is linked with **Mahayana**, not Hinayana
- ★ Gandhara school flourished under the **Kushans**; patron **Kanishka**
- ★ **Standing statue** of the Buddha — **Kushana** period (both sitting and standing postures)
- ★ Future Buddha, yet to come — **Maitreya**
  - ★ **Bodhisattva** (one with **Bodhicitta**) — concept of **Mahayana** Buddhism

## SEC 1:45:10 | Mudras of the Buddha → event depicted
- ★ **Bhumisparsha** mudra — Buddha calling the **Earth to witness** his purity despite **Mara**'s temptations; moment of **enlightenment** under the Bodhi tree; right hand touches the earth
- ★ Gandharan Buddha image of the **Sarnath first sermon** — **Dharmachakra** mudra
  - ★ Gandharan postures: Dharmachakra, Meditation, **Abhaya**, **Varada**
- ★ **Sarnath** Buddha in **Bhumisparsha** mudra — **Gupta** period; mudra represents **steadfastness**
  - ★ **Dhyani Buddha Akshobhya** — also linked with the earth-witness mudra

## SEC 1:45:11 | Gandhara, Mathura and Amaravati compared — patron, material, theme, depiction
- ★ **Amaravati** — carvings of plants, creepers and especially **lotus flowers**

## SEC 1:45:12 | Universal angles
- ★ **Gandhara** sculptures — invariably used **green schist**; influenced by **Greek** sculpture

# ROW 1:46 | Satavahanas - origin debate, Simuka, Gautamiputra Satakarni, Nasik Prasasti, Vasishthiputra Pulumavi
## SEC 1:46:0 | Key rulers
### Sequence
- Magadha succession — **Mauryas → Sungas → Kanvas → Satavahanas**; **Simuka** linked with overthrow of the **Kanvas**
- Puranic numbering — **Hala** the **17th**, **Gautamiputra Satakarni** the **23rd** (last of the early Satavahanas, rulers 1–23; "28th" is an error), **Pulomavi III** the **30th and last**
### Rulers and facts
| Ruler | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Krishna / Kanha** | **first Satavahana inscription**, at **Nasik** |
| **Satakarni I** (3rd king) | performed the **Ashvamedha** |
| **Satakarni II** (6th king) | **longest reign — 56 years** |
| **Kundala Satakarni** | **Katantra** — **Sharvavarman** |
| **Hala** | ★ wrote **Gatha Saptasati** (Prakrit, 700 verses on rural life and love); title **Kavivatsala** |
| **Gautamiputra Satakarni** | titles **Ekabrahmana, Ekashura, Shakasanhari**; began **metronymics** (Gautamiputra, Vasishthiputra) |
| **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi** | shifted centre east to **Dhanyakataka (Amaravati)** after **Rudradaman**'s attacks; **ship-motif coins**; extended rule into **coastal Andhra** |
| **Yajna Sri Satakarni** | greatest of the later Satavahanas; **ship coins** — revival of **maritime power**; title **Trisamudradhipati** |
| **Pulomavi III** | last king; defeated by **Ikshvaku Srisantamula**; last days at **Myakadoni (Bellary)** |
- **Ptolemy** records Vasishthiputra Pulumavi's clashes with **Chashtana**
- **Hala** called **Kavivatsala** from his **love of poetry**, not from his dynasty
### Related works and dynasties
- **Leelavati Parinayam** — **Kutuhala**; **Brihatkatha** — **Gunadhya**
- **Virapurushadatta** — **Ikshvaku** king; his reign the **golden age of Buddhism** (Buddhism flourished at **Nagarjunakonda**)
  - Devinaganika, Hala and Gautamiputra Satakarni — Satavahana
- **Guntupalli** — centre of the **Rajagirika** school
- **Ajanta** — **Swetagaja Jataka** painting; **Nasik** — cave inscription; **Bhairavakonda** — **eight Saiva caves**

## SEC 1:46:1 | Origin theories
- ★ **V.S. Sukthankar** — Satavahanas **were not Andhras** ("Andhras and Satavahanas are not the same"); original home in the **Kannada (Bellary)** region
- **Bhandarkar** — keyed to the **Bellary** original-home view
- **D.C. Sircar** — Satavahanas were **Andhras, not Andhrabhrityas**
- **Nilakanta Sastri** — distinguished **tribal, dynastic and family** names (Andhra a tribal name)
- **R.S. Sharma** — linked them with the **Dravidians**
- **B.S.L. Hanumantha Rao** — belonged to the original **Andhra janapada**
- Puranas: **Andhras** and **Andhrabhrityas** (= servants / former **feudatories** of the Andhras)
- **Periplus** — notes the **Saka–Satavahana** conflict
- Successors: **Vakatakas** in **Vidarbha**, **Ikshvakus** in **Andhra**, **Abhiras** in **Maharashtra**

## SEC 1:46:2 | Gautamiputra & Nasik Prasasti
### Wars with the Western Kshatrapas
- Defeated **Nahapana** (of the **Kshaharata** branch) and recovered **Malwa, Saurashtra, Konkan and Nasik**
- **Jogalthambi hoard** — **Nahapana**'s silver coins **restruck** by Gautamiputra
- **Ayama**, Kshaharata minister — **Junnar** inscription; **Ushavadata** — Nahapana's **son-in-law**
- **Junagadh** inscription — **Rudradaman** defeated Satakarni **twice** but spared him as a **near relative**
### Nasik Prasasti
- Main source for Gautamiputra's reign; **Prakrit prose**, **Brahmi** script
- Praises him as destroyer of **Sakas, Yavanas and Pahlavas**; records his expeditions, empire and titles
- Gift to monks — a village near **Trirashmi** and **100 nivartanas** of his own land
### Regions named
| Region | Modern area |
|---|---|
| **Anupa** | **Narmada** valley; capital **Mahishmati** |
| **Akara** | **East Malwa** |
| **Avanti** | West Malwa |
| **Aparanta** | North Konkan |
| **Vidarbha** | Berar |

## SEC 1:46:3 | Inscriptions
- **Kodavali** (East Godavari) — **Chanda Satakarni**
- **Naneghat** — **Satakarni I** and **Naganika** (issued by **Devi Naganika**)
- **Amaravati** inscription — first Telugu word **'Nagabu'**

## SEC 1:46:4 | Royal titles
- **Trisamudra-toya-pita-vahana** (Gautamiputra) — whose **horses drank the waters of three seas**
- **Varnasankarya-nirodhaka** — stopper of the **mixing of varnas**
- **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi** — **Dakshinapatheshwara** and **Navanagaraswami**
- **Metronymics** (e.g. Gautamiputra) — **high status of royal women**, respect for the mother; succession stayed **patriarchal**

## SEC 1:46:5 | Capitals & queens
- ★ Main town / principal western capital — **Paithan (Pratishthana)**, Maharashtra, on the **Godavari**
- Nasik Prasasti presents Gautamiputra's line as **Brahmana**; crusher of **Kshatriya pride**
- **Devi Naganika** — wife of **Satakarni I**; **regent** during her son's minority
- **Lilavati** — queen of **Hala**
- **Gautami Balasri** — mother of Gautamiputra; built a **Nasik cave** for **Bhadrayaniya** monks; her **Nasik Prasasti** glorified her son

## SEC 1:46:6 | Sources
- Rise: began as **Mauryan feudatories**; independent in the Deccan after Mauryan decline
- **Matsya Purana** — **30 kings**, **456 years**
- **Vayu** and **Vishnu** Puranas — **17 kings**, **272 years**; not treated as standard
- First kings to issue inscriptions **in Andhra itself** (Ashoka's were earlier in time)
- Earliest inscriptions — **Maharashtra** (**Naneghat**, **Kanha's Nasik** record)
  - later: coastal Andhra (Amaravati), Karnataka (**Myakadoni, Bellary**), Kodavali-type records
  - all in **Prakrit**, **Brahmi** script

## SEC 1:46:7 | Exam corner
- Works on early Satavahana history — **Kathasaritsagara, Pratishthanapura-kalpa, Dvatrimsatputtalika**; **not Gathasaptasati**
- **Megasthenes** — Andhras lived in **towns**
- **Pliny** — Natural History; **Ptolemy** — Geography
- **Kotilingala** (Karimnagar, Telangana) — claimed early Satavahana political centre

## SEC 1:46:8 | Satavahana names, FIRSTs and dynastic sequence
- ★ Andhras gained independence from the Mauryas under the **Satavahanas**; first king **Srimukha**
- ★ Satavahanas regarded as **Brahmins** — Nasik inscription calls Gautamiputra **'Eka Brahmana'**
- ★ Oldest Deccan dynasty — **Satavahanas** (c. **200 BC – 200 AD**)
- ★ Early Satavahana inscriptions found in **Maharashtra** — **Naneghat** (Queen Naganika) and **Nashik** caves; centre later shifted east to Amaravati, Dharanikota

## SEC 1:46:9 | Scholars → theories on origin
- ★ **V.S. Sukthankar** — 'Andhras and Satavahanas are **not the same**'; **Kannada (Bellary)** origin
- ★ Capital — initial capital believed to be **Amaravati**; actual capital **Paithan (Pratisthan)**
- ★ Founder per the Puranas — **Simuka**

## SEC 1:46:10 | Puranas, books and foreign accounts → authors and content
- ★ **Matsya Purana** — compiled in **Yajnasri Satakarni**'s reign; **longest list** of Satavahana kings (**30**)
- ★ Early Satavahana history not in **Gathasaptasati** (700 rural romantic verses)
- ★ First to make **tax-free land grants** to Buddhists and Brahmanas — **Satavahana** kings
- **Brihatkatha** — **Gunadhya**, in **Paisachi**

## SEC 1:46:11 | Inscriptions → issuer, ruler and findspot
- ★ **Nashik Prasasti** — records **Gautamiputra**'s victories; issued by mother **Gautami Balasri** in the **19th regnal year of Pulumavi**
- ★ **Trisamudradhipati** — **Gautamiputra Satakarni** (horses drank the waters of three seas)
- ★ Village donated for **Mahasanghika** bhikshus at **Karle** — **Yajnasri Satakarni**

## SEC 1:46:12 | Caves → location and features
- ★ **Nasik** prashasti of Gautami Balashri — achievements of **Gautamiputra Satakarni**
- **Guntupalli** (Rajagirika centre) — near **Kamavarapukota**, **Eluru** district

## SEC 1:46:13 | Capitals, AP places and regions conquered
- ★ First (major) dynasty to rule Andhra — **Satavahana**

## SEC 1:46:14 | Rulers → period → contribution
- ★ Founder — **Simukha (Srimukha)**; greatest — **Gautamiputra Satakarni**; last — **Pulumavi III**
- ★ Gautamiputra faced the **Sakas** — defeated **Nahapana** (Kshaharata); "destroyer of the Sakas"
- ★ **Kavi Vatsala** — **Hala**, compiler of the Gathasaptasati
- ★ Enlarged the **Amaravati stupa** and built a stone railing around the **Mahachaitya** — **Srimukha**
- ★ Married **Rudradaman's daughter** — **Vasishthiputra (Sivasri) Satakarni** (Junagadh/Kanheri records)
- ★ Gautami Balasri's **Nasik** inscription — engraved in the **19th regnal year** of her grandson **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi**

## SEC 1:46:15 | Gautamiputra Satakarni and the Nasik Prasasti
- ★ Protector of the **Varna system** — **Gautamiputra Satakarni**; patron of Brahmanism; barred **intercaste marriage** among the four varnas (Nashik inscription)

## SEC 1:46:16 | Royal titles → ruler and meaning
- ★ **Dakshinapathapati** — **Satakarni I** (Naneghat inscription of Naganika)

## SEC 1:46:17 | Queens and metronymics
- ★ **Devi Naganika** — wife of **Satakarni I**; ruled as **regent** for her minor sons; issued the **Nanaghat** inscription
- ★ Naganika — daughter of **Maharathi Tranakayiro**
- ★ **Metronymics** — Satavahana kings prefixing their personal names with their **mothers' names**

## SEC 1:46:18 | Universal angles
- ★ Founder — **Simukha (Srimukha)**
- First historical dynasty of the **Deccan** — **Satavahanas**; link between northern and southern India

# ROW 1:47 | Satavahana administration, economy, coinage and Prakrit literature (Hala's Gathasaptasati)
## SEC 1:47:0 | Administrative units
- Hierarchy, larger to smaller — **Rashtra** (province) → **Ahara** (district/province) → **Vishaya** (sub-division) → **Grama** (village)
- **Gulma** — cluster of villages under a **Gulmika** (law and order; post later **hereditary**)
- Most prominent Ahara in the Nasik inscription — **Govardhana Ahara**
- Satavahana towns — **Barukaccha, Sopara, Kalyan, Govardhana**

## SEC 1:47:1 | Officers & bodies
### Officers
| Officer / body | Role |
|---|---|
| **Mahasenapati** | day-to-day **military** administration of regions (king the supreme commander) |
| **Rajjugahaka** | **land surveyor** |
| **Heranika** | treasurer |
| **Akshapatakas** | kept the king's orders |
| **Pattikapala** | drafted **land-grant** inscriptions |
| **Visvamaatyulu** | consulted for **important decisions** |
| **Mahaamatyas** | special officers |
| **Rajaamatyas** | advisors |
| **Amatyas** | ran the **Aharas** |
| **Mahasenadhipathulu** | ran **distant Aharas** |
| **Gaaha Pati** | representative of **farmers** |
| **Gramani** | headed **five or ten villages** |
### Nigama Sabha
- **Urban council** that administered **towns** (not villages); members **Gahapatis**
- Referred to in the **Bhattiprolu** inscription
- King's council — **Mantri Parishad**; guilds headed by **Shreshthis**
- Allahabad pillar — Samudragupta; **Hathigumpha** — Kharavela; **Junagadh** — Rudradaman
### Camps
- **Skandhavara** — **temporary** campaign camp; **Kataka** — **permanent** camp / fortified centre
- Both served as **administrative centres** when the king was present
### Other
- **Roman coins** — found at **Bhattiprolu** and **Nagarjunakonda**; **Constantine** gold coin at **Kudali Sangameswaram** is later
- **Gathasaptasati** social picture — **sati**, **no child marriage**, plight of wives of **seafaring merchants**
- Ancient names: **Vijayapuri** — Nagarjunakonda; **Vengipuram** — **Pedavegi** (near Eluru); **Koduru** — **Gudur**; **Bhattiprolu** — **Pratipalapuram**

## SEC 1:47:2 | Polity & feudatories
### State and army
- Power **more decentralised** than the Mauryas; kingship **hereditary and patriarchal**; king upheld **dharma**
- Mauryan-style officers **Amatya** and **Mahamatra** alongside local feudatories
- **Gramani** — five or ten villages; distant Aharas under **military officers**
- **Pliny** — army of **1,00,000 infantry, 2,000 cavalry, 1,000 elephants**
- **Amaravati sculptures** — infantry in **front**, cavalry and elephants on the **flanks**, archers at the **rear**
### Feudatories
- **Raja** — highest feudatory, could **issue coins**
- **Mahabhoja** — powerful local chief
- **Maharathis** — hereditary **tax-collecting** feudatories of the **western Deccan**
### Society, women, coins
- **Shreni** — guild of traders/artisans of one craft; fixed work rules, quality, prices, wages
- **Ujjain symbol** — **cross with four circles**, on coin reverse
- **Gautami Balasri** — issued the Nasik inscription in reign of grandson **Pulumavi**; donated a **Buddhist vihara**
- **Naganika** — **Naneghat** inscription
- Queens and women donors patronised **Buddhism**; kings followed **Vedic** religion
- Women's status shown by **matronymics** and inscriptions of Naganika and Gautami Balasri
### Literature
- **Brihatkatha** (Gunadhya) — basis of **Kathasaritsagara** (**Somadeva Suri**) and **Brihatkatha Manjari** (**Kshemendra**)
- Women contributors to Gathasaptasati — **Anulakshmi, Anupalabdha, Reva (Revati), Madhavi**
- Gathasaptasati mentions **Brundagana** (group dances, choral singing), sugar and jaggery, house-wall paintings, hanging of criminals
- **Harshacharita** (Banabhatta) — **Yajna Sri** called **Trisamudradhipati** (lord of three seas)

## SEC 1:47:3 | Taxes & land grants
### Taxes
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Bhaga** | king's share of harvest — **one-sixth** |
| **Kara** | general levy |
| **Deya-meya / Bhodga / Deeva Meeya** | king's share of the peasant's produce (in kind) |
| **Karukara** | tax on **artisans and professionals** |
| **Moturpha** | tax on trades |
- Chief source of income — **land revenue**
### Grants and labour
- First **tax-free land grants** (**agraharas**) to Brahmanas and monks — **Satavahanas**; a root of the early-medieval **feudal** order
- **Naneghat** — **Satakarni I** gave land, cows (**godhana**) and gold to **Brahmanas**
- Gautamiputra gave **Tirasmri** to monks
- Hereditary village headships → **feudalism**; **vetti** → **forced labour**
- **Kamasutra** — vetti performed by **women**

## SEC 1:47:4 | Guilds & trade
### Agriculture and production
- Cultivation expanded in the **Krishna-Godavari** basin; **paddy transplantation**; main crops **rice and cotton**
- **Guntupalli** — **copper and iron** (not gold); **Guduru** — fine cotton; **Vinukonda** — metal; **Palnadu** — diamonds
### Guilds
| Guild | Members |
|---|---|
| **Gandhika** | **perfume** |
| **Tailika** | oil |
| **Tilapishaka** | **oil-pressers** |
| **Kolika** | **weavers** |
| **Kulara** | potters |
| **Padhika** | butchers |
| **Udayantrika** | makers of water-machines |
- **Sarthavaha** — long-distance / foreign traders
- Guild head — **Sethi / Shreshthi**; **Gahapati** — urban administration
- Guilds existed **across India**, not only the north; regulated members; acted as **banks**, paid **interest** on deposits (incl. to monasteries)
- Guild (**Srenulu**) interest — **12%**
- **Ushavadata**'s Nashik inscription — Govardhana weavers' guild: **2,000 karshapanas at 1%/month**, **1,000 at ¾%/month**
- **Chitrameli** — medieval agriculturists' guild; **Nigamalu** — Ikshvaku trade guilds
### Ports and inland trade
| Ancient name | Modern / note |
|---|---|
| **Barygaza / Bharukachha** | **Bharuch** (Gujarat); Satavahana after Nahapana's defeat |
| **Kantakossyla** | **Ghantasala** |
| **Maisolia** | **Machilipatnam** |
| **Koddura** | **Gudur** area (Krishna); Periplus's **only east-coast port** |
| **Dhanyakataka** | Amaravati / Dharanikota |
- **Kalyan** — destroyed by the **Sakas** (Periplus); **Sopara** and Kalyan — west-coast ports in Maharashtra
- **Motupalli** — later, Eastern Chalukya context
- **Vengi** — trade centre; **Tagara** — emporium linked to coastal ports; **Pratishthana** — capital and inland trade centre
- Satavahanas — road builders, **'Mahapatha nirmatalu'**
### Roman trade
- **Pliny** (Natural History, Latin) — **drain of Roman gold**, fine cloth
- **Periplus** (anonymous Greek) — **Arikamedu** the **"market street of the Romans"**; Arikamedu on the **east coast**
- Import — **camphor from China**; exports — spices, ivory, muslin, pearls, diamonds
- Roman trade **declined** after the Satavahanas
- **Pulomavi** and **Yagnasri** coins — flourishing sea trade
- Land grants, guild-banking, Roman trade — base of **Deccan urbanisation**
### Telugu
- **"Italian of the East"** — named by the **British** (words end in vowels); phrase from **Niccolo de Conti**

## SEC 1:47:5 | Coinage
- **Gautamiputra Satakarni** — **restruck Nahapana's silver coins**; began **portrait coins** (indigenous innovation)
- **Kondapur** mint (Medak) — legend **'Siri Chimuka Sata'**
- Later bilingual silver portrait coins — **Prakrit** and a **Dravidian** language
- **Ship-motif coins** — **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi** and **Yajna Sri Satakarni**; no later ruler issued them
- Silver coin — **karshapana**; **1 suvarna = 35 karshapanas**
- Gautamiputra's self-description — **Ekabrahmana** and **Varnasankarya nirodhaka**
- **Desi Bhaasha** (language of common people) — **Dravidian** family
