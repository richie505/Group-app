# ROW 1:154 | English East India Company - Charter of 1600, Surat, Madras, Bombay, Calcutta; Farman of 1717
## SEC 1:154:0 | Charter of 1600
### The charter
- **31 December 1600** — charter granted by **Elizabeth I**; Company established **1600**
- Formed during the reign of **Akbar** (1556–1605)
- Monopoly of East Indies trade for **fifteen years**, later made perpetual
- Granted **trading rights**, not territorial power
### Organisation and powers
- Run by a **Governor** and a **24-member committee** (later the **Court of Directors**)
- Shareholders formed the **General Court** (Court of Proprietors)
- **Charles II** and **James II** delegated powers: coining money, jurisdiction, **making war and peace with non-Christians**
- **Cromwell** — **1657** charter for a permanent joint stock
- **James I** sent **Hawkins** and **Roe** to Jahangir; **Charles II** received Bombay as dowry
### Early trade
- Indian goods paid for mainly with **imported bullion**
- **Ralph Fitch** — in India **1583–1586**; visited **Agra** and **Fatehpur Sikri**; first English merchant at **Akbar's** court
- **Farman of 1717** ("Magna Carta" of Company trade) — issued by **Farrukhsiyar**; enhanced trading privileges
- Other dates: **1757** Plassey, **1764** Buxar, **1773** Regulating Act

## SEC 1:154:1 | Factories & presidencies
### Factories
- **Globe** reached **Machilipatnam** in **1611** under **Captain Anthony Hippon** — first English factory in Andhra (first temporary factory)
- **Battle of Swally (1612)** — **Captain Thomas Best** defeated the Portuguese near Surat, winning the English a factory at Surat
- **Surat (1613)** — first **permanent** English factory

|---|---|---|

### Presidencies
- **Madras** (with Fort St. George) — founded **1639** by **Francis Day**
  - grant from Chandragiri ruler **Venkatapati Raya III**, with efforts of the **Damerla brothers** (Venkatadri and Ayyappa)
- **Fort St. George** — seat of the **Madras** Presidency; **Fort William** — Calcutta (Bengal)
- **Bombay** — leased to the Company by **Charles II** in **1668** for an annual rent of **£10**; replaced Surat in the west
- **1698** — Company bought zamindari of **Sutanuti, Gobindapur and Kalikata** — basis of Calcutta
- **Sutanuti** — an **English** trading centre and Calcutta base; **Batavia** was the Dutch eastern centre

## SEC 1:154:3 | Farman of 1717 (Farrukhsiyar)
- Bengal trade **duty-free** for **Rs 3,000** a year, with the right to issue **dastaks**
- **Surat** duties waived for **Rs 10,000**
- **Bengal** — Company's first major territorial base, after **Plassey (1757)** and **Diwani (1765)**; Madras and Bombay were trading settlements

## SEC 1:154:4 | Dastak misuse to rule
- **John Surman** — led the mission to Farrukhsiyar that secured the Farman of 1717
- **Dastak misuse** by Company servants for private trade — **drained the Nawab of Bengal's revenue**; an underlying cause of **Plassey**
- **Battle of Buxar** — **1764** (after Plassey 1757; Company rule ended 1858)

## SEC 1:154:5 | Other milestones
- **Early objective** — eastern trade, especially **spices and textiles**; territorial rule over Bengal only after **1757**
- **Child's War (1686–1690)** — Company crushed by **Aurangzeb**, pardoned after paying a fine
- **Golden Farman** (Sultan of Golconda) — free trade in Golconda ports for **500 pagodas** a year
- **Madras** — India's first municipality, **1687**; **Bhimili** — Andhra's first, **1861**

## SEC 1:154:7 | Foundation and the Charter of 1600

# ROW 1:155 | French in India - Colbert, Compagnie des Indes, Pondicherry, Dupleix and his policy
## SEC 1:155:0 | Compagnie des Indes
- **1664** — founded by **Jean-Baptiste Colbert**, minister of **Louis XIV**
- French — **last** European company to arrive
- Charter: **50-year monopoly** in the Indian and Pacific Oceans + rights over **Madagascar**
- Weakness — **state-owned**, dependent on **subsidies**
- Lost mainly for lack of **naval supremacy** and steady **state support**

## SEC 1:155:1 | Settlements
### Founding
- **Surat (1668)** — first French factory, under **François Caron**
- **Machilipatnam** — permission from **Abdullah Qutb Shah**
- **Sher Khan Lodi** (Bijapur governor of Valikondapuram) — gave land to **François Martin** in **1673**; Pondicherry founded **1674**
- **Fort St. Louis** — built by François Martin at Pondicherry; French headquarters in India
- ★ Chronology: **Surat → Pondicherry → Mahe → Karaikal → Yanam**
### Locations
- ★ **Yanam** (Godavari delta) — the French settlement in Andhra; only French territory in Andhra
  - under French control until **1954**; now part of UT of **Puducherry**
- **Mahe** — French settlement on the **Malabar** coast; **Chandernagore** was not in Malabar

## SEC 1:155:2 | Governors in order
- **François Martin** — first French governor; builder of Pondicherry; died **31 December 1706**
- **Count de Lally** — lost at **Wandiwash** to **Eyre Coote**

## SEC 1:155:3 | Dupleix's intervention policy
- After **Nizam-ul-Mulk's** death (**1748**), Dupleix exploited two successions:
  - **Hyderabad** — **Nasir Jang** vs **Muzaffar Jang**
  - **Carnatic** — **Anwaruddin** vs **Chanda Sahib**
- **Battle of Ambur (1749)** — Anwaruddin killed; **Muzaffar Jang** Subedar; **Chanda Sahib** Nawab
- **Muzaffar Jang** murdered at **Rayachoti**; **Bussy** set up **Salabat Jang** as Subedar of the Deccan
- **Treaty of Pondicherry (1754)** — no meddling in Indian princes' quarrels; **Dupleix recalled**

## SEC 1:155:4 | French in Andhra
### Northern Circars
- **1753** — granted by **Salabat Jang** to pay for **Bussy's troops**; high point of French influence
- Circars granted: **Mustafanagar (Kondapalli), Eluru, Rajahmundry, Chicacole** — **Nellore** not included
- Other Circars British in **1768**; **Guntur** in **1788**
### Bobbili
- **Battle of Bobbili** — **24 January 1757**
  - **Bussy** aided **Vijayaramaraju** of Vizianagaram; **Ranga Rao** of Bobbili killed
  - **Tandra Papayya** later killed Vijayaramaraju
### After Wandiwash
- **Treaty of Paris (1763)** — French kept only trading enclaves: **Pondicherry, Karaikal, Mahe, Yanam, Chandernagore**
- **Chandernagore** — joined India by plebiscite, **1950**
- Other four enclaves — de facto **1954**, de jure **1962**

## SEC 1:155:5 | Exam corner
- ★ First French trade centre in Andhra — **Machilipatnam**, **1669**, with permission of **Abdullah Qutb Shah**
| Pondicherry event | Year |
|---|---|
| Captured by the **Dutch** | **1693** |
| Restored by **Treaty of Ryswick** | **1697** |
| Taken by the British after Wandiwash | **1761** |
| Restored by **Treaty of Paris** | **1763** |

## SEC 1:155:6 | French East India Company — founder, nature, FIRST and LAST
- ★ **Colbert** — founder of the French Company in India; established on his recommendation in **1664**
- ★ Formed in the reign of **Louis XIV** (Colbert was his Finance Minister)
- ★ Name: "**Compagnie Française pour le Commerce des Indes Orientales**"
- ★ The **French state** was the largest shareholder and bore most of its expenses

## SEC 1:155:7 | French settlements → year → founder; enclaves and merger
- ★ **Pondicherry** — French control from **1674**, under **François Martin**; became the most important French settlement
- ★ Earliest French factory in India — **Surat**

## SEC 1:155:9 | Battles and treaties → year → outcome (Ambur to Treaty of Paris)
- ★ **Dupleix** (Governor-General of French India, 1742–1754) — first European to intervene in Indian princes' quarrels to gain territory
- ★ Pioneered use of **trained Indian sepoys** and strategic alliances
