# ROW 1:256 | Wanchoo Committee and the problems of the new Andhra State
## SEC 1:256:0 | From announcement to state
- **Partition Committee** (1949–50) on administrative arrangements for a new Andhra State — chaired by **Kumaraswamy Raja**
- **Wanchoo Committee** appointed **7 February 1953**; reported **23 March 1953**
- Wanchoo Committee's terms — **boundaries**, **finances**, **assets** and **administration** of the new Andhra State (not the capital of India)
- **25 March 1953** — Nehru announced the capital decision

## SEC 1:256:1 | Committees and officers
- **Bellary taluk** assigned to **Mysore** on the basis of **Justice Mishra**'s report
  - Justice Mishra — **Chief Justice of the Hyderabad High Court**

## SEC 1:256:2 | Stand on Madras city
- Wanchoo recommended the Andhra Government work temporarily from **Madras city** for **three to five years** (Andhra lacked capital infrastructure)
- Nehru's announcement of **25 March 1953**:
  - **Madras ruled out** as capital of Andhra State
  - Capital to be located **within Andhra**
  - **Some offices** could continue in Madras for a period

## SEC 1:256:3 | Bellary district split
| Area | Went to |
|---|---|
| **Rayadurg** taluk | Andhra — **Anantapur** district |
| **Adoni**, **Alur** | Andhra — **Kurnool** district |
| **Bellary**, **Hospet** and others | **Mysore** |

## SEC 1:256:4 | Problems of the new state
- New Andhra State — "**administratively and economically orphaned**": no developed **capital** and no **commercial centre**
- **Andhra High Court** set up at **Guntur** in **1954**; Madras High Court retained jurisdiction until then
- **Tungabhadra Board** (works shared by Andhra and Mysore after Bellary's division) — set up under the **Andhra State Act, 1953**

## SEC 1:256:5 | Exam corner
- **Sreebagh Pact** (1937) — irrigation priority for **Rayalaseema** and **Nellore** on the **Tungabhadra**, **Krishna** and **Penna** (for ten years or as needed)
- 2014 bifurcation committees:
  - **Kamalanathan Committee** — allocation of **employees**
  - **Sheela Bhide Committee** — **Schedule IX** institutions
  - **Section 6** committee — the **capital**

## SEC 1:261:2 | SRC: set-up and principles
### Andhra State
- Arguments for linguistic states — government in the people's **own language**, cultural identity, easier administration
- **Andhra State** — first linguistic state, carved out of **Madras State**; capital **Kurnool**
- First CM of Andhra State (1953) — **Tanguturi Prakasam**; first CM of Andhra Pradesh (1956) — **N. Sanjiva Reddy**
### The SRC
- Prompted by the creation of **Andhra State** (1953) leading to similar demands elsewhere
- Set up **1953** — announced **22 December**, constituted by Home Ministry Resolution **29 December 1953**; reported **1955**
- **Kailashnath Wanchoo** — not a member
- Language — **important but not the only factor**; rejected "one language, one state"
  - could divide same-language areas into **more than one State**
- **Bombay** — retain as **bilingual** state (Gujarati and Marathi areas)
- Report (1955) — **16 states and 3 Union Territories**; abolish Part A/B/C classification
- **Belgaum** dispute — **Maharashtra** and **Karnataka**, continued after reorganisation

## SEC 1:261:3 | SRC on Hyderabad / Telangana
### Background
- **Visalandhra** idea began in **1937** in the **Circar districts**
- **Sri Bagh Pact** — irrigation priority, university centres, equal legislative representation for Rayalaseema; **not** immediate merger of Telangana
### SRC recommendations
| Area | Assigned to |
|---|---|
| **Marathwada** | **Bombay** |
| **Raichur**, **Gulbarga** | **Mysore** |
| Telugu districts | separate **Hyderabad State** |

- **Paragraph 386** — Telangana to function initially as a separate state called **Hyderabad State**
  - unification after **1961** only with **two-thirds** of elected members of the Telangana Assembly
- Hyderabad in Visalandhra — a common capital solving **Andhra's capital problem**
- Objection to immediate merger — threat to **Mulki rules** (local jobs) and diversion of revenue surplus
### Centre and after
- Centre **overrode** the wait-till-1961 formula; settled it by **political agreement** → 1956 merger
- **Nehru** (1956) — merger a "**matrimonial alliance**" with a provision for **divorce**
  - later cited by **separate-Telangana advocates** (merger conditional)
- Departure from **paragraph 386** — core grievance of Telangana movements of **1969** and **2001–14**
