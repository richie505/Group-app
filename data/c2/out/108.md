# ROW 5:63 | Applications of radioisotopes in agriculture, medicine and industry
## SEC 5:63:0 | Medical isotope → use
### Isotope → medical use

| Isotope | Use | Note |
|---|---|---|
| ★ **Phosphorus-32** | treats **blood cancer (leukaemia)** and polycythaemia | beta emitter; **Cobalt-60** also accepted for leukaemia |
| ★ **Arsenic-74** | detects / locates **tumours** (brain tumours) | emits positrons |
| **Iodine-131** | diagnosis and treatment of **thyroid** disorders | beta + gamma; supplied by **BRIT** as **sodium iodide** |
| **Sodium-24** | **blood-flow** speed and circulation blocks | gamma emitter; also detects **pipeline leaks** |
| **Technetium-99m** | **most widely used diagnostic** isotope | images heart, brain, bones, lungs; used in **SPECT** |
| **Iron-59** | **iron metabolism** | |
| **Carbon-14** | dating organic material | |

### Techniques
- **Radioimmunoassay** — measures minute quantities of **hormones, enzymes and drugs** in blood
- **SPECT** — single gamma emitters, chiefly **Technetium-99m**; **PET** uses positron emitters such as **F-18 FDG**
- **Food irradiation** works by **radiolysis of water** in the food
  - sources: **gamma rays (Co-60), X-rays or electron beams** (not Co-60 only)

## SEC 5:63:2 | Therapy & imaging

## SEC 5:63:3 | Radiometric dating
- **Carbon-14** — half-life about **5,730 years**; dates **once-living organic remains** up to about **50,000 years** (not ancient volcanic rocks)

## SEC 5:63:4 | Agriculture & food
### Mutation breeding & BARC varieties
- **L. J. Stadler** — first showed **induced mutation** in crop plants (**barley**) using **X-rays** (**1930**)
  - gamma and neutron radiation used later for inducing mutations
- **72** crop varieties developed with **Trombay (BARC)** technology
- **TBM-9** — **banana** variety of BARC Trombay with **NRCB, Trichy**; **RTS-43** is a **sorghum** variety
### Pest control & food irradiation
- **Sterile insect technique** — gamma-sterilised males mate with wild females, **no viable offspring**, pest numbers decline
- Food irradiation **stops sprouting, delays ripening, kills insects, parasites, pathogens**
  - increasing genetic diversity of field crops is **mutation breeding**, not food irradiation
- **Radura** — international green symbol for **irradiated food**
### Cancer care
- **Tata Memorial Centre** — Anchor Centre for the IAEA's **'Rays of Hope'** initiative

### Facts & uses
- ★ **Hydrogen** — **most common element in the Universe** (about 3/4 of matter; helium most of the rest)
- Storage — compressed gas, cryogenic liquid, solid **metal hydrides**; carried as **ammonia** or **methanol** (not CO₂)
- **Green ammonia** — **nitrogen + green hydrogen**; fertiliser, fuel, marine fuel
- Hydrogen suits **steel, shipping, fertilisers** — **direct electrification is difficult** (hard-to-abate)

## SEC 5:68:4 | PEM fuel cell
- **Electrolyser** — uses electricity to **split water into hydrogen and oxygen**
- **PEM fuel cell** — hydrogen split into protons and electrons at the **anode**; protons cross membrane to cathode, electrons via external circuit
- **Fuel cell** — hydrogen + oxygen → **electricity and water**; zero emissions at point of use
