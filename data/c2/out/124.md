## SEC 6:28:0 | SHANTI Act 2025
### What it replaces and opens up
- SHANTI Bill, 2025 replaces the **Atomic Energy Act, 1962** and the **Civil Liability for Nuclear Damage Act, 2010**
- Ends the **NPCIL** monopoly: first-ever **private and foreign** participation in nuclear power generation
- Private entities may **operate plants**, **manufacture equipment**, fabricate fuel and enrich **U-235** up to the prescribed limit
- Reserved for the **Centre**: enrichment beyond the limit and **spent fuel management**
### Liability and regulation
- Liability cap: **300 million SDR**
- **Supplier liability eliminated** (not retained); liability above the cap covered by a **Nuclear Liability Fund**
- **AERB** (Atomic Energy Regulatory Board, set up **1983**) given **statutory status**, accountable to **Parliament**
- **Section 39**: Centre can declare nuclear information **restricted**; overrides the **RTI Act** for it
- **IAEA safeguards** apply to reactors using **imported uranium**
### Nuclear plants
| Plant | State |
|---|---|
| **Kakrapar** | Gujarat |
| Tarapur | Maharashtra |
| Rawatbhata | Rajasthan |
| Kudankulam | Tamil Nadu |

## SEC 6:29:0 | Four Labour Codes
### The four Codes
- Four Labour Codes in force from **21 November 2025**; replaced **29** Central labour laws
| Code | Laws merged |
|---|---|
| Code on Wages, 2019 | **4** |
| Code on Social Security, 2020 | **9** |
| Industrial Relations Code, 2020 | **3** |
| **OSH Code, 2020** | **13** |
- **Code on Social Security, 2020** (not the Code on Wages) — first legal definition of **gig and platform workers**; **Social Security Fund**
- Code on Wages: overtime at least **twice the normal rate**; deductions capped at **50%**
- Fixed-term employees: gratuity after **1 year** (5 years applied to permanent employees)
- **IR Code** absorbed the **Industrial Disputes Act, 1947** and **Trade Unions Act, 1926**
- IR Code: layoff/retrenchment/closure permission threshold raised from **100 to 300** workers (not 500)
- OSH Code: women may work before 6 am / after 7 pm **with consent and safety measures**; **appointment letters** and **annual free health check** mandatory
### Ladakh, LWE and States
- September 2025 Ladakh protests: four demands incl. **Statehood**; by **Leh Apex Body** and **Kargil Democratic Alliance**

- LWE districts: from 90+ a decade ago down to **11**; core in **Bijapur, Narayanpur, Sukma** (Chhattisgarh)
- **Goa** acquired **1961**, **Sikkim** **1975**; both later made States
- Kolhan **Manki-Munda** system: **Manki** heads **8–15 villages**, appellate authority; **Munda** heads one village
- '**Provincial citizenship**' — coined by **Alok Ranjan** (JNU); Myron Weiner — 'Sons of the Soil'
- Examples: **Jharkhand**'s domicile policy (State formed 2000) and **J&K**'s post-**2019** domicile rules
