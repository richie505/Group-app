## SEC 2:150:1 | LAC sectors (3,488 km)
- India-China border — about **3,488 km**
- China **occupies** about **38,000 sq km** in **Aksai Chin**
- China **claims** about **90,000 sq km** in the north-east, incl. most of **Arunachal Pradesh**
- **Middle sector** — the **least contested** sector of the LAC

## SEC 2:150:2 | 1962 war
- War ran **20 October – 21 November 1962**
- Ended in China's **unilateral ceasefire** (not a negotiated one)
- Causes: **unsettled boundary**, the **Tibet question**, India's **Forward Policy**
- **Henderson Brooks-Bhagat Report (1963)** — reviewed India's military failure in 1962
- War was a **blow to NAM idealism**; India **sought Western military aid**
- **Shaksgam Valley** (Trans-Karakoram Tract) — ceded by Pakistan to China under the **1963 Sino-Pakistan Boundary Agreement** (after the war, not a cause); India regards it as integral to India

# ROW 2:158 | World Trade Organization - structure, agreements (AoA, TRIPS, TRIMS, GATS), dispute settlement, India's issues
