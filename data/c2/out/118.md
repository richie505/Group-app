# ROW 5:115 | Recent trends in India's disease burden - communicable and non-communicable diseases
## SEC 5:115:0 | Burden terms
- **DALY** = **YLL** (years of life lost) + **YLD** (years lived with disability); does not add years gained through treatment
- **India State-Level Disease Burden Initiative** — **ICMR, PHFI and IHME**; part of the **Global Burden of Disease** study
- **Dual burden of disease** — persistent communicable diseases (TB, malaria, dengue) coexisting with rising **NCDs**
  - undernutrition with obesity = dual burden of **malnutrition**; adding injuries = **triple burden**

## SEC 5:115:1 | Epidemiological transition (Omran)
- **Omran's** stages: **Age of Pestilence and Famine** (first) → Receding Pandemics → Degenerative and Man-Made Diseases

## SEC 5:115:2 | Key data
### Mortality and transition
- **Cardiovascular disease** — leading cause of death in **India**
- **Ischaemic heart disease** — world's single biggest killer (WHO)
- **NCDs** — **57%** of all deaths in India
- **Under-five mortality** — fallen by **78%** since 1990
### Vectors
- **Japanese encephalitis** — **Culex** mosquito
- Dengue, chikungunya, Zika — **Aedes**
### NCD risks and cancer
- **FSSAI** caps trans fat in fats and oils at **2%** (trans fats common in bakery products made with hydrogenated fats)
- Main NCD risk factors — **unhealthy diet**, **physical inactivity**, **tobacco**, **alcohol** (also urbanisation, ageing)
- Cancer not nationally notifiable; **17 States** have notified it, **Telangana** the most recent

## SEC 5:115:3 | Communicable vs lifestyle
- ★ **Coronary artery disease** — result of plaque buildup in the arteries
- **Malaria** — communicable (Plasmodium, spread by mosquitoes), **not** a lifestyle disease
- Lifestyle NCDs — **diabetes**, **hypertension**, **obesity**

## SEC 5:115:4 | Programmes & targets
### Fats
- ★ **Trans fat** is harmful because it lowers **HDL** ("good cholesterol"); high HDL reduces heart-disease risk
- **FSSAI** caps trans fat in fats and oils at **2%**
- Excess **saturated fat** — raises **blood lipids and cholesterol**
### Programmes and targets
- **UNGA declaration on NCDs and mental health** — calls for **stronger** regulation of tobacco, trans fats and **e-cigarettes**
- **NP-NCD** (formerly NPCDCS) — screens everyone above **30 years**; NCD clinics at district hospitals and CHCs
- **SDG 3** — reduce premature NCD deaths by **one-third**; promotes mental health; **halve** road-traffic deaths
- **Tele-MANAS** — **24x7 tele-mental-health** support in all **36** States/UTs
  - vaccination registration is **U-WIN**

## SEC 5:115:5 | AP angle
### Uddanam CKDu
- **Uddanam** (incl. **Kaviti**) — **Srikakulam** district; chronic kidney disease of unknown cause (**CKDu**)
- Samples (soil, water, blood, urine) tested for **pesticides and heavy metals**, with **CSIR-IICT Hyderabad**
- **AIIMS Mangalagiri** — conducts CKDu screening camps in Uddanam
- **Kidney Research Centre and Super Specialty Hospital** — **Palasa**, Srikakulam
### NCD care
- **Vizianagaram** — **diabetic-foot clinic**
- Growing NCD burden needs a shift from **curative care** to **screening and prevention**

## SEC 5:115:6 | Exam corner
- ★ Cook in **oil** rather than vanaspati ghee — oil contains **unsaturated fats** (polyunsaturated, monounsaturated), which lower blood cholesterol and CVD risk
- Four major NCD groups — **cardiovascular diseases**, **cancers**, **chronic respiratory diseases**, **diabetes**
- **Meningococcal meningitis** — spreads person to person by **respiratory droplets**; symptoms include vomiting, neck stiffness

## SEC 5:115:7 | Disease burden — terms → meaning and transition stages
- ★ **Dual burden** — persistence of infectious diseases (TB, malaria, diarrhoeal diseases) alongside rising **non-communicable** / lifestyle diseases (diabetes, cardiovascular disease)

## SEC 5:115:10 | Fats and lifestyle disease risk factors — fatty acids, PYQ statements and data
- ★ **Eskimos** (Inuit) — relatively free from heart disease despite high meat/fat diet; rich in **omega-3** from fish and marine mammals
- ★ **Linseed** — rich source of **omega-3 fatty acid**; prevents fat deposition in blood vessels
- ★ **Flaxseed** — richest source of **alpha-linolenic acid** (also canola, soyabean, walnut oil)
- ★ **Sunflower oil** — suitable for heart patients; mono- and polyunsaturated fats, low saturated fat, more **vitamin E** than any other vegetable oil
- ★ Blood pressure generally **increases** with age

# ROW 5:116 | Epidemic and pandemic challenges in India - major outbreaks, lessons from COVID-19
## SEC 5:116:0 | Outbreak terms (by spread)
- **Epidemic** — sudden, severe outbreak in a region at a particular time
- **Pandemic** — epidemic spread across **countries and continents**
- **Endemic** — constantly present at a baseline level
- **Epizootic** — epidemic among **animals**
- First Indian **avian influenza** outbreak — **Navapur**, **Maharashtra**, **2006**
- **Nipah** outbreaks in India — **Kerala**
- **Kala-azar** (visceral leishmaniasis) — spread by **sandfly**; **Anopheles** carries malaria

## SEC 5:116:7 | Outbreak terms → patterns of occurrence
