# ROW 2:135 | Reservation - constitutional basis, Indra Sawhney, creamy layer, EWS reservation, promotion in reservation
## SEC 2:135:0 | Backward Classes Commissions
### First Commission (Kalelkar)
- ★ **Kaka Saheb Kalelkar** — chairman of the First (All India) Backward Classes Commission
- Appointed by the President on **29 January 1953**; inaugurated **18 March 1953**
- Appointed **1953**, report submitted **1955**; report **not implemented**
- **First** commission to use **social and educational** criteria to identify backward classes
- Recommended a **caste-based census** and reservations
### Second Commission (Mandal)
- **Article 340** — President appoints a commission on conditions of SEBCs (Mandal appointed under it)
- **B.P. Mandal** chaired the Second Commission (**1979**), set up by the Janata Government of **Morarji Desai**
- Concerned with reservation in **government employment**; recommended **27%** for OBCs (11 indicators)
- Identified **3,743** backward castes
- Implemented by the **V.P. Singh** Government in **1990**; 27% of Central civil posts reserved for OBCs in **1993**
### Later bodies
- **National Commission for Backward Classes** — permanent statutory body directed in Indra Sawhney for backward-class lists
- NCBC examines requests for **inclusion in or exclusion from** backward-class lists and advises
- **M. Veerappa Moily** Oversight Committee (**2006**) — OBC reservation through **expansion of seats**, so general-category seats did not shrink
- **Justice G. Rohini Commission** (**2017**) — **sub-categorisation** of OBCs in the Central List, for equitable distribution of benefits to under-represented groups
  - Finding: about **97%** of benefits taken by **25%** of sub-castes; about **1,000** communities had zero representation
  - Proposal: divide Central-list OBCs into **three or four** sub-groups
- **R.N. Prasad** — framed creamy-layer criteria; **Ranganath Misra** — 15% for minorities; **Bhuria** — basis for PESA
### Andhra Pradesh BC groups
| Group | Comprises | Share |
|---|---|---|
| BC-A | — | **7%** |
| BC-B | vocational / occupational groups | **10%** |
| BC-C | SC converts to Christianity | **1%** |
| BC-D | **other classes** | **7%** |
| BC-E | socially and educationally backward Muslims | **4%** |

- Combined BC-A to BC-E quota — **29%**
- AP High Court struck down the **2007 Act** in **2010**; Supreme Court **interim order** let the **4%** Muslim quota continue pending a **Constitution Bench**

## SEC 2:135:2 | Indra Sawhney
- Backward classes under **Article 16(4)** — identified mainly by **caste and social backwardness**, not economic criteria alone
- Reservation confined to **initial appointments**, **not promotions**
- Creamy layer — specified socially advanced categories, e.g. children of **Class-I officers**, and of parents who are **both Class-II** officers; not all OBC children
- **Sukhdev case** (AP High Court) — **caste alone** could not be the basis; **112 classes** listed using **poverty, residence and occupation**

## SEC 2:135:3 | Creamy layer income limit
- Creamy-layer criteria — **DoPT Office Memorandum, September 1993**, based on the **Justice R.N. Prasad Expert Committee**
- Income ceiling — **₹8 lakh per annum** (revised **2017**, still current)
- **Kapu** reservation — Centre **never approved** the 2017 **BC-F** proposal
- Mandal Commission set up through the **Ministry of Home Affairs** (not Social Justice); **3,743** castes; **27%** for OBCs

## SEC 2:135:4 | EWS and key cases
- **Union of India v. Rohith Nathan** — **parental salary alone** cannot place a candidate in the creamy layer
- **103rd Amendment** — introduced as the **124th Amendment Bill**; inserted Articles **15(6)** and **16(6)**
- **10%** EWS reservation, outside SC/ST/OBC quotas — **enabling, not mandatory** for States
- EWS criteria — family income below **₹8 lakh**, agricultural land below **5 acres**, flat below **1,000 sq ft**
- **Janhit Abhiyan v. Union of India** — 103rd Amendment upheld **3:2**; dissent by CJI **U.U. Lalit** and Justice **S. Ravindra Bhat**
- AP local bodies — **N.T. Rama Rao** introduced **20%** BC quota in **1984**; later reduced from 34% to **24.15%** under GOs **176, 559 and 560**

## SEC 2:135:5 | AP BC groups
- **CEI (Reservation in Admission) Act, 2006** — 27% OBC reservation; came into force in **2007**
- **BC-E** — added by the **Andhra Pradesh Act of 2007**; earlier lists had only BC-A to BC-D

## SEC 2:135:6 | AP angle
- **Anantharaman Commission** — first AP Backward Classes Commission; report **1970**; framed **A-D grouping** and **25%** quota
- Supreme Court **triple test** for OBC reservation in local bodies:
  - a **dedicated commission** to examine backwardness
  - reservation proportion fixed **local-body-wise**
  - total within the **50% ceiling**

# ROW 2:136 | Scheduled Tribes - Forest Rights Act 2006, Fifth and Sixth Schedules, TSP, Eklavya schools
## SEC 2:136:0 | Forest Rights Act 2006
### Tribal concepts and policy
- **Diku** — outsider-exploiter: moneylender, trader, landlord, contractor
- **T. K. Oommen** — modern tribal movements seek political autonomy, identity recognition, control over resources
- Nehru's **Panchsheel** — first principle: develop tribals along the lines of **their own genius**
  - Results judged by **quality of human character** evolved, not money spent
### FRA 2006 — basics
- Nodal ministry — **Ministry of Tribal Affairs**
- Aim — undo historical injustice to **forest dwellers**
- Recognises **individual and community** forest rights of forest-dwelling STs and **Other Traditional Forest Dwellers**
- Passed **2006**; in force **31 December 2007**; rules notified **1 January 2008**
- STs — occupation of forest land before **13 December 2005**
- OTFDs — residence/dependence for **three generations (75 years)** before 2005
- Ceiling — **4 hectares** per family
- **Overrides** inconsistent laws
- Sub-Divisional and District Level Committees — **six-member** bodies
- Rights include minor forest produce, CFR management, **PVTG habitat rights**; **no** automatic ownership of minerals
### Rights under FRA
| Right | Type |
|---|---|
| Habitation and self-cultivation | **individual** |
| **Nistar** | community |
| Grazing, fishing | community |
| Ownership and disposal of MFP | community |
| Community forest resource management | community |

- **Bamboo** — a **minor forest produce**; ownership vested in forest dwellers
- **Indian Forest (Amendment) Act, 2017** — removed bamboo grown **outside forest areas** from the definition of "tree" (no right to fell bamboo in forests)
- First PVTG with habitat rights — **Bharia** (Madhya Pradesh); then Kamar and Baiga (Chhattisgarh)
- **Critical Wildlife Habitat** — areas of national parks/sanctuaries kept **inviolate**, notified after an open process with communities
### Forest diversion and land acquisition
- MoEFCC guidelines — **no diversion** under FCA until FRA processes (esp. **Gram Sabha consent**) complete; upheld by the Supreme Court
- **Forest Conservation Rules, 2022** — dropped forest-dwellers' **consent clause**; dispute between **NCST** and Environment Ministry
- **August 2026** — **MoTA** said FRA has no provision for Gram Sabha consent for **Stage-II** clearance
- Implementation gap — **weak** MoTA capacity for CFR
- MoTA sought MoEFCC funds for **CFR** areas; **Agasthyamalai** eviction orders criticised for ignoring FRA (separate issues)
- **RFCTLARR Act, 2013** — **Social Impact Assessment mandatory**
- Scheduled Areas acquisition — **last resort**; consent of **Gram Sabha, Panchayat or Autonomous Council**
### Institutions
| Body | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| NSFDC | **1989** | — |
| **TRIFED** | **1987** | tribal marketing, MSP for MFP |
| **Ministry of Tribal Affairs** | **1999** | separate Union Ministry |
| **NSTFDC** | **2001** | lends to STs with income up to **double** the poverty line |
| **NCST** (Art. 338A) | **89th Amendment, 2003** | functions from **19 February 2004**; civil-court powers |

- **Girijan Cooperative Corporation** (AP) — buys MFP at fair prices, runs PDS depots, credit to tribal farmers
### Schemes
- **TADP/ITDA** — improved seeds/fertilisers, fruit-bearing trees, income-raising activities
- TSP monitoring moved to **MoTA** in **January 2017**; online monitoring of ST component of **41 ministries**
- **PM Janjatiya Vikas Mission** (2021-22, TRIFED) — MFP at MSP, value addition, GI tags
- **Roshni** (MoRD, 2013) — skills and jobs for tribal youth in **LWE districts**
- **EMRS** — Central Sector scheme under **Ministry of Tribal Affairs**
- **Dharti Aaba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyan** — nodal **Ministry of Tribal Affairs**, converging line ministries
- **National Sickle Cell Elimination Mission** — Health & Family Welfare with Tribal Affairs; **screening** tribal populations
- **PM-AJAY** (2021-22) — **100%** centrally funded, **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment**; plans, pools funds for and monitors works in **SC-majority Adarsh Grams**
- Tribal artisans' marketplace — **TRIFED / Tribes India**
- SC **Post-Matric Scholarship** — cost shared **60:40**
- **Stand-Up India** — for **greenfield** enterprises
- **Dr Ambedkar Centres of Excellence** — free Civil Services coaching for **SC students in Central Universities**

## SEC 2:136:2 | AP land laws & cases
### Colonial background
- **Colonial moneylenders and revenue farmers** — tribal indebtedness and land alienation; forest laws cut tribals off from habitats
- **Birsa Munda's Ulgulan** — against **British land-grabbing** and the **dikus**
### AP land laws and cases
- **AP Scheduled Areas Land Transfer Regulation, 1959** (Regulation 1 of 1959) — bars land transfer in Scheduled Areas to **non-tribals**
- **1970 amendment** — **presumption** that land belongs to tribals; bars transfers even **between non-tribals**
- **Samatha v. State of AP (1997)** — lease of Scheduled Area land to non-tribals / **private mining companies** is **void**
  - Mining left to **State undertakings or tribal cooperatives**; Gram Sabha consent does not validate a private lease
- **G.O. No. 3** (2000) — 100% Scheduled Area teacher posts to local STs; struck down by Supreme Court in **2020**
- **Alluri Sitarama Raju district** bandh — enforce **1/70** law, replacement for **G.O. 3**
- **Polavaram project** — opposed by **Koya and Kondh** (land rights, compensation, displacement)
### Other
- **Niyamgiri** (2013) — **Dongria Kondh** Gram Sabhas rejected bauxite mining under FRA; project cancelled
- **RFCTLARR Act, 2013** — consent of **80%** (private) and **70%** (PPP) of affected families
- **NAMASTE** — implemented by **NSKFDC** (MoSJE and MoHUA); Rs **349.70 crore**
- **SHRESHTA** portal (with NTA) — CBT registration, merit and seat allotment for SC students in **private residential schools**

# ROW 2:137 | Child rights - Articles 21A, 24, 39(e)(f); UNCRC; Right to Education Act 2009
## SEC 2:137:0 | Articles on children
| Provision | Content |
|---|---|

| **Article 24** | no child below **14** in factories, mines, hazardous employment |

- UNCRC four core principles — **non-discrimination**, **best interests** of the child, **life-survival-development**, **respect for the child's views** (not free secondary education)

## SEC 2:137:1 | UNCRC 1989
- **UNCRC** — adopted by UNGA on **20 November 1989**; **54 articles**
- India acceded on **11 December 1992**
- Four categories of rights — **survival, development, protection, participation** (education not a separate category)
- India ratified the two **2000 Optional Protocols** in **2005**; not a party to the **2011** Communications Procedure Protocol
- Universal Children's Day — **20 November**; India's Children's Day — **14 November**
- **NCPCR** — set up in **2007** under the **Commissions for Protection of Child Rights Act, 2005**
  - Monitors the JJ, RTE and POCSO Acts (not its parent law)

## SEC 2:137:2 | RTE Act: key sections
- **Section 12(1)(c)** — **25%** entry-level seats in **unaided private schools** (excluding minority institutions) for weaker sections and disadvantaged groups
- **Society for Unaided Private Schools of Rajasthan v. Union of India (2012)** — upheld the 25% quota; not for unaided minority schools
- Pupil-teacher ratio — primary **30:1**; upper primary **35:1**
- **2019 amendment** — detention allowed at **Classes V and VIII** after exams and re-exam (original Act barred detention up to Class VIII)
- **School Management Committees** (**Section 21**) — in every government and aided school; **three-fourths** members parents/guardians
- Not covered — **pre-school** (below six) and **secondary/higher** education
- Out-of-school child — admitted to an **age-appropriate class**; no screening, no capitation fee
- **Samagra Shiksha** cost-sharing — **60:40**; **90:10** for North-Eastern and hill States
- Monitoring and grievance redressal — **NCPCR** and **SCPCRs** (SMCs are school-level bodies)

## SEC 2:137:3 | Road to RTE

- **86th Amendment, 2002** — first made education a **Fundamental Right**
- **RTE Act, 2009** — gives effect to **Article 21A**; children **6–14** (Classes 1–8, neighbourhood school); in force **1 April 2010**

# ROW 2:138 | Juvenile Justice Act 2015, POCSO Act 2012, Child Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Amendment 2016
## SEC 2:138:0 | Three child laws
### Forms of abuse
- Physical abuse by **omission** — **medical or nutritional neglect**; by commission — beating, burning
- Emotional abuse — verbal battering, educational neglect, fostering delinquency; **child prostitution** is **social** abuse
- **Draft National Policy on Child Protection** — **zero-tolerance code of conduct** in all organisations
### POCSO and trafficking
- **POCSO Act, 2012** — drafted by the **Ministry of Women and Child Development**
- Child — below **18**, **gender-neutral**
- Child-friendly procedure — **no** night at police station; statement by a **woman** officer (at least **Sub-Inspector**), **not in uniform**, at child's residence or chosen place
- Trafficking — now **Section 143, BNS 2023** (widened by Criminal Law Amendment Act 2013); IPC replaced by BNS from **1 July 2024**
- **NCPCR and SCPCRs** — set up as statutory bodies under the **CPCR Act**, for child-welfare monitoring (not POCSO)
### Child labour
- **Gurupadswamy Committee** → **National Policy on Child Labour, 1987**
- **PENCIL** — **Platform for Effective Enforcement for No Child Labour**; **Ministry of Labour and Employment**; enforces the amended **Child and Adolescent Labour (P&R) Act, 1986**
- **National Child Labour Project** — **Ministry of Labour and Employment**
### Juvenile justice
- **JJ Act, 2015** — **16–18**-year-olds may be tried as adults only for **heinous offences**

## SEC 2:138:1 | JJ Act 2015 machinery
### JJ Act 2015 — features
- **Study on Child Abuse: India 2007** — **two out of three** children faced physical abuse
- Compulsory registration of **all Child Care Institutions**
- Replaced "juvenile" with "**child**"
- Heinous offence — minimum punishment **seven years or more**
- Followed the punitive response to the **Delhi gang rape case of 2012**
- Guiding principle — **best interests of the child**; child-friendly, rehabilitation-oriented
- **CARA** — given **statutory** status by the JJ Act 2015
- Working children — **children in need of care and protection**
- **JJ Act, 2000** — uniform age of **18**; amended **2006**
### Boards and courts
| Category / body | Dealt with by / composition |
|---|---|
| Child in Need of Care and Protection | **Child Welfare Committee** |
| Child in Conflict with Law | **Juvenile Justice Board** |
| JJB | **Principal Magistrate** (MM or JMFC) + **two social workers**, at least one woman |
| **Children's Court** | a Sessions Court; tries cases transferred by the JJB |

## SEC 2:138:3 | Child labour: 1986 vs 2016
