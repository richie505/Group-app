# ROW 1:145 | Bhakti in Andhra - Annamacharya, Bammera Potana, Vemana, the Tallapaka poets
## SEC 1:145:0 | Annamacharya & Tallapaka
### Annamacharya
- **Annamacharya** (1408-1503) — contemporary of **Saluva Narasimha** (APPSC-G2 2011)
- Titles: **Padakavita Pitamaha**, **Sankirtanacharya**, **Harikirtanacharya**
- **Father of the Telugu keertana**; pioneer of **padakavita** (pada form)
- **Veturi Prabhakara Sastry** — recovered and edited Annamacharya's sankirtanas on **copper plates** at Tirumala
- **Tirumala** — associated with Annamacharya and the **Tallapaka** family
  - Bhadrachalam — Ramadasu; Muvva — Kshetrayya; Srikurmam — Narahari Tirtha
### The Tallapaka family
| Person | Relation to Annamacharya | Work / title |
|---|---|---|
| ★ **Tallapaka Timmakka** | wife | **Subhadra Kalyanam** (Telugu); early Telugu poetess |
| **Peda Tirumalacharya** | **son** | Vairagya Vachanamalika, Sudarshana Ragada |
| **China Tirumalacharya** | grandson | title **Ashtabhasha Kavi Chakravarti**; Ashtabhasha Dandakam |
### Tamil background and music
- Traditional number: **12 Alvars** (Vaishnava, Vishnu) and **63 Nayanars** (Shaiva, Shiva)
- **Tirumurai** — **12-volume** Tamil Shaiva canon, compiled 7th-12th c.; called the **Fifth Veda**
- Alvar hymns were in **Tamil**
- Telugu Bhakti poets' gift to Carnatic music: **padams, keertanas, satakas and dvipada** compositions

## SEC 1:145:1 | Bammera Potana
### Potana
- ★ **Potana** — **Veerabhadra Vijayam**, **Bhogini Dandakam**, **Andhra Mahabhagavatam**
- **Veerabhadra Vijayam** — Potana's **first work**, at the request of his guru **Ivaturi Somanaradhya**
- **Bhogini Dandakam** — the **first Telugu Dandakam**
- Titles: **Sahaja Panditudu** and **Nigarva Chudamani**
- **Andhra Maha Bhagavatamu** (Telugu Bhagavata Purana) — dedicated to **Lord Rama**; Potana wrote **8 of its 12 skandhas**
### Srinatha
- After **Kondaveedu** fell (**1424**), Srinatha went to **Rajahmundry** under **Veerabhadra Reddy**, through **Bendapudi Anantamatya**
- Srinatha — pioneer of the **Prabandha** style; his age is the **Kavya Yuga**

## SEC 1:145:2 | Srinatha (c. 1385-1475)
- **Vidyadhikari** (education minister) at the **Kondaveedu** court of **Pedakomati Vema Reddy**; wrote the king's Sanskrit inscriptions
- Under **Veerabhadra Reddy** at Rajahmundry — **Bhimakhandam** and **Kasikhandam**
- At **Devaraya II**'s court — title **Kavi Sarvabhauma**
| Work | Dedicated to |
|---|---|
| **Sringara Naishadhamu** | minister **Mamidi (Madiki) Singana** |
| **Haravilasamu** | **Avachi Tippaya Setti** |
| **Sivaratri Mahatmyam** | **Mummadi Santayya** |
- **Haravilasamu** — source for **sea trade** (imports and exports) in the Reddi period

## SEC 1:145:3 | Vemana & Veerabrahmam
### Vemana
- **Vemana** — criticised **caste, superstition, empty ritual**, idol worship and hypocrisy; advocated humanism and equality
- Metre: **Ataveladi**; refrain: **"Vishwadabhirama Vinura Vema"**
- **C.P. Brown** — collected, edited and translated **'Verses of Vemana' (1829)**
### Veerabrahmendra Swamy
- Condemned **caste differences**; disciples across castes and faiths, incl. **Siddaiah**, a Muslim
- Alvars, Nayanars, Basava, Vemana and Potuluri Veerabrahmam **criticised caste**; some promoted **common dining** — Bhakti did **not uniformly uphold Brahmanical dominance**
### Related traditions
- ★ **Gangadevi** (author of Madhura Vijayam) — disciple of **Agastya**
- **Ramanuja** — **Vishishtadvaita**; worship of **Narayana and Lakshmi**
- **Ishtalinga** — worn on the body; personal worship of **Shiva outside temples**
  - **Kayaka** — honest labour; **Dasoha** — sharing surplus; **Shunya Sampadane** — later compilation of discourses
- **Virashaivas** — rejected caste and untouchability, condemned child marriage, **promoted widow remarriage**, questioned rebirth

## SEC 1:145:4 | Veerashaivism in Andhra
### Basavanna and the Virashaiva movement
- **Basavanna** (Basaveshwara) — founded the **Virashaiva / Lingayat** movement (**12th c.**)
- Minister of **Bijjala II** of the **Kalachuri** dynasty
- Theology: **Shakti Vishishtadvaita** — soul and Shiva fundamentally identical
- **Anubhava Mantapa** ("religious parliament") — at **Kalyana**; discourses compiled in the **Shunya Sampadane**
### Virashaivism in Andhra
- Introduced into Andhra by **Mallikarjuna Panditaradhya**; Pandita reformers spread it
- **Aradhya Saivism** — moderate Andhra Shaiva sect (Panditaradhya); kept **Vedic learning and varna** while preaching Shiva bhakti
- **Sivatattvasaram** — by **Mallikarjuna Panditaradhya** (not Palkuriki Somanatha)
- **Palkuriki Somanatha** — **Basava Puranam** (Basava's teachings in simple Telugu), **Panditaradhya Charitra**, **Basavana Ragada**
- **Vrushadhipa Satakam** (Somanatha) — **first Telugu satakam** with all satakam features
### Other
- **Dhurjati** — **Srikalahasteeswara Satakam**, a foremost Shaiva satakam
- **Satakam** and **dwipada** — forms that carried Bhakti to common people
- After **Rajahmundry** fell (**1448**), Srinatha's **chatu** verses — hard life and coarse food of poor **Palnadu**

# ROW 1:146 | Sufism - doctrines, khanqah, sama; silsilas - Chishti, Suhrawardi, Qadiri, Naqshbandi
## SEC 1:146:0 | Silsilas compared
### Chishti
- **Muinuddin Chishti** — brought the **Chishti** silsilah to India, set up at **Ajmer**
- ★ Urs of **Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti** — celebrated yearly at his dargah in **Ajmer**
- Early Chishtis avoided court patronage and **state grants**; later **Deccan** Chishtis accepted grants
- Chishtis — relatively **aloof** from the court; encouraged **sama** and **qawwali**
### Other orders
| Order | Leader / founder | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Suhrawardi** | **Bahauddin Zakariya** (Multan, Sindh); Shahabuddin Suhrawardi | close to the court; accepted royal service; sama and raqs |
| **Firdausi** | **Sharfuddin Yahya Maneri** | flourished in **Bihar** |
| **Qadiri** | **Abdul Qadir Jilani** | not a Chishti |
| **Naqshbandi** | **Bahauddin Naqshband** (Central Asia) | orthodox; opposed **Sulh-i-Kul** and **sama** |
| **Shattari** | Shah Abdullah Shattari, Muhammad Ghaus | absorbed yogic practices |
- **Bahauddin Zakariya** — title **Shaikh-ul-Islam** for backing **Iltutmish** against **Qabacha**
- Naqshband did not come to India (revered by **Babur**); **Khwaja Baqi Billah** brought the order to India in the **late 16th c.**; also **Shaikh Ahmad Sirhindi**
### General
- **Be-shara**: **Qalandars** (wandering) and Madaris; **Ba-shara**: Naqshbandi, Firdausi
- Sufism absorbed **Vedanta, Yoga** and **Neo-Platonism**, accepting the Quran and the Prophet
- Significance: gave Islam a **tolerant, vernacular** face; khanqahs as **welfare centres**

## SEC 1:146:1 | Sufi terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Khanqah** | Sufi **hospice**: disciples lived and trained; food and shelter to the poor |
| **Sama** | devotional music to produce **ecstasy** |
| **Raqs** | ritual dance with sama |
| **Piri-Muridi** | bond of **pir** (murshid, master) and **murid** (disciple) |
| **Fana** | annihilation of the self in God |
| **Baqa** | subsistence in God |
| **Tazkiras** | **biographies of saints** (not anti-Shia tracts) |
- **Maqamat** (10 stages) — first **Tauba** (repentance), last **Riza** (submission)
  - in between: Wara, Zuhd, Faqr, Sabr, Shukr, Khauf, Raja, Tawakkul
- Anti-Shia tract: **Radd-i-Khawafiq**
- **Alberuni** compared Sufi ideas with the **Gita** and **Patanjali's Yoga Sutra**
- ★ **SufiKalam** (devotional music) — characteristic of **Kashmir**

## SEC 1:146:2 | Two doctrines
| Doctrine | Meaning | Associated with |

| **Wahdat-ush-Shuhud** | Unity of Appearance / Witness; creation reflects God, not identical | **Sirhindi** |

## SEC 1:146:7 | Sufism — meaning, origin, FIRSTs and Sufis vs Ulema
- ★ **Naqshbandiya** — order **against music**; founded by **Bahauddin Naqshband** (14th c.)
  - ★ **Khwaja Khawand** — Naqshbandi saint who made **Kashmir** his centre

# ROW 1:148 | Interaction and impact of the Bhakti and Sufi movements on society and language
## SEC 1:148:0 | Common principles
- **Bhakti** — devotion over **Vedic ritual** and priestly mediation; **Sufism** — inner experience over formal **Shariat**
- Shared: **guide-disciple bond**, devotion over ritual, **vernacular** preaching, monotheism, asceticism, religious tolerance
- **Not** shared: **idol worship** (rejected by Sufis and Nirguna saints)
- Impact: weakened **priestly and Brahmanical dominance**; khanqahs welcomed different groups; caste **not** completely abolished

## SEC 1:148:1 | Persons of interaction
### Bhakti-Sufi meeting points
- **Kabir** and **Nanak** — criticised orthodoxy of **both Hinduism and Islam**; spiritual equality
- **Sant** tradition (Kabirpanth) — **Kabir, Nanak, Ravidas, Dadu**; blended Hindu and Islamic ideas
- **Nath yogis-Sufis** — dialogues, meditation, **breath control** (not confined to temple rituals)
- **Hamiduddin Nagori** — equated Nath **'Alakh Niranjan'** with **'Khuda'** in Hindi verse
- **Dara Shikoh** — carried forward Akbar's **Sulh-i-kul** / **Din-i-Ilahi** syncretism; **Ahmad Sirhindi** attacked these
- **Naqshbandi** — opposed **sama** and musical practices (unlike Chishti, Suhrawardi)
### Rishi movement (Kashmir)
- **Universal love**, service to **all castes**, **vegetarianism** (though Shariat permitted meat)
- **Lal Ded** (Lalla) — **Kashmir**; inspired the Rishi movement
- **Sheikh Nuruddin** — **Rishi movement**; Basaveswara — Lingayat / Veerasaiva

## SEC 1:148:2 | Yoga & music
- **Gorakhnath** — Nathpanthi **Hath Yoga**; followed by the **Kanphata** Yogis
- **Amir Khusrau** — introduced the **Qaul** (early qawwali)
- **Carnatic** — remained tied to **temple Bhakti**; **Hindustani** absorbed Persian, Bhakti and Sufi influences

## SEC 1:148:3 | Vernacular literature
- **Jnaneshwari** — **Jnaneshwar**, **Marathi** (not Bengali)
- **Bengali** — **Padavali kirtans**
- **Kaalagnanam** — **Potuluri Veerabrahmam**
- **Vemana** (17th c.) — **satire** in common Telugu against **birth-based inequality**
- Saints used **the people's speech**, not Sanskrit alone — regional languages grew
| Saint | Background |
|---|---|
| **Namdev** | **tailor** |
| Kabir | weaver |
| Ravidas | leather tanner / shoemaker |
| Sena | barber |
| Dhanna | Jat cultivator |
- Marathas' early national awakening — **Tukaram, Ramdas and Shivaji**

## SEC 1:148:4 | Caste reformers (Andhra focus)
- **Basava's Veerashaivism** — **caste opposition**, **widow remarriage**, **gender equality**; condemned child marriage
