# ROW 3:45 | Indian agriculture - importance, land use classification, cropping seasons and cropping pattern
## SEC 3:45:0 | Importance
- Agriculture employs **46.1%** of workforce (PLFS 2023-24); nearly one-fifth of national income
- Average agri growth FY16-FY25: **4.4%** (highest decadal)
- Agri exports record: **$51.91 billion** (about 11.86% of merchandise exports)
- **Horticulture**: about **one-third** of agri GVA; 2024-25 output 367.72 MT overtook foodgrains 357.73 MT
## SEC 3:45:1 | Land use: India 2021-22
- **Cropping intensity** = GCA ÷ NSA × 100 = 219.16 ÷ 141 × 100 ≈ **155.4%** (2021-22)
- **GCA > NSA**: GCA counts a plot once for each crop; NSA only once
- Net irrigated area sources: **tubewells 47.3%**, canals 24.7%, other wells 13.2%, other sources 12%, tanks 2.8%
- **Nine-fold** land-use classification includes current fallows, culturable waste, misc. tree crops, net sown area; **net irrigated area** not a category
## SEC 3:45:2 | Landholdings
### Holdings
- **Operational holding**: land used for agricultural production, operated as **one technical unit**, irrespective of title, size or location
- Average holding: **1.08 ha** (2015-16), down from 2.28 ha (1971)
- 2010-11: small and marginal = about **85%** of holdings, **44%** of area
| Class | Size |
|---|---|
| Small | 1–2 ha |
| Semi-medium | **2–4 ha** |
| Medium | 4–10 ha |
| Large | 10 ha and above |
### Andhra Pradesh
- AP average holding (11th Census 2021-22, provisional): **0.86 ha** (from 0.94 ha)
- Largest average holding: **Ananthapuram** (1.62 ha); smallest Visakhapatnam (0.40 ha)
- AP agro-climatic zones defined by **climate, soil, topography** and factors determining suitable cropping system
- AP has **22** soil categories; 10 cover over 88% of the State
## SEC 3:45:3 | Cropping seasons
- **Kharif**: sown **June–July** (south-west monsoon), harvested September–October or October–November
- **Rabi**: depends on **western disturbances and irrigation**
- **Zaid**: short, irrigation-dependent summer window, **March to June**
- **Cotton** = Kharif; wheat = Rabi; watermelon = Zaid
## SEC 3:45:4 | AP land use & crops
### AP land use
- AP forests **22.63%**; net area sown 36.29%, current fallows 8.96%, barren land 8.19%
### Crop classes
- Food crops: **cereals, millets, pulses**; cotton, jute, sugarcane, tobacco, several oilseeds are cash crops
- AP traditional commercial crops: groundnut, cotton, tobacco, sugarcane; **paddy** is a food crop
- **Rice**: largest cropped area in India (about **51-52 million ha**)
- Shift of about 20% of rice area to pulses and legumes: **reduce urea use and conserve groundwater**
### AP crop trends
- **Paddy**: largest AP foodgrain crop, **20.97** of 37.51 lakh ha (2024-25, 2nd AE); pulses 11.66, maize 3.20 lakh ha
- AP foodgrain area 2021-22: about **41.3 lakh ha** (earlier key: 41.44 lakh ha)
- **Cotton** = "**white gold**" of AP; expanding crop
- **Groundnut** area fell from **11.36 to 3.46 lakh ha**; jowar and ragi also shrank
- Kharif 2026 deficient rainfall: planned shift to **pulses and drought-resilient crops**
## SEC 3:45:5 | Agro-climatic zones
- Long-term trend: shift from food crops to **commercial and high-value crops** (irrigation, prices, technology)
- Maize expansion in Godavari–Krishna belt: **poultry feed and starch** demand
- Planning Commission regions include Western Himalayan, Trans-Gangetic Plains, Southern Plateau and Hills; **Central Coastal Delta** is not one
- AP: coastal districts in **East Coast Plains and Hills**; Rayalaseema in **Southern Plateau and Hills**
- **NARP**: **127** zones; Planning Commission: **15** regions, 72 sub-zones
- **APSES 2024-25**: **six** AP zones (13 pre-2022 districts); older material and 2017 Group-II key: seven
| Zone (pre-2022) | Districts |
|---|---|
| North Coastal | **Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam** |
| Godavari | East and West Godavari |
| Krishna | Krishna, Guntur, Prakasam |

| Scarce Rainfall | **Kurnool, Anantapuramu** (HQ **Nandyal**) |
## SEC 3:45:6 | Exam corner
- **Jute**: kharif, not rabi; rabi crops: wheat, barley, gram, mustard, peas, linseed, potato
- Post-1991 liberalisation: decline in **public investment** in agriculture
- **WTO Agreement on Agriculture**: **1995**; covers market access, domestic support, export subsidies (not land ceilings)

## SEC 3:45:14 | Crop classification — food vs cash crops, both-season crops
- ★ **Cotton**: cash crop (textiles); rice, maize, wheat are food crops
- ★ Kharif: **cotton, groundnut, maize**; **mustard** is Rabi (sown October–November, harvested February–March)
