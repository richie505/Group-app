# ROW 5:2 | Science, Technology and Innovation Policy - 1958 Resolution, 1983, 2003, 2013 policies and the STIP draft
## SEC 5:2:0 | Policies: year, PM, focus
### The policy sequence
| Policy | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Scientific Policy Resolution (SPR) | **1958** | first national S&T policy |
| Technology Policy Statement (TPS) | **1983** | |
| S&T Policy (STP) | **2003** | under **A.B. Vajpayee** |
| STI Policy | **2013** | under **Manmohan Singh** |
| STIP | **2020** | the **fifth** national S&T policy |
### Draft STIP 2020
- Research governance — **decentralised, bottom-up**; self-reliance, open science, equity
- **ONOS** and **RIEF** — associated with the draft's proposals
- **Atal Innovation Mission** — NOT a STIP 2020 outcome; set up by **NITI Aayog** in **2016** (innovation among students)
### Bodies
- **ANRF** — President: **Prime Minister**; Vice-Presidents: Ministers of S&T and Education
- State S&T councils (e.g. **APCOST**) — frame state S&T policy, give research grants, popularise science; not the national statutory functions of ANRF or NITI Aayog
- **APCOST** — under AP's **Environment, Forests, Science and Technology Department**

## SEC 5:2:1 | STI Policy 2013
- Innovation made central; India among the **top five** scientific powers by **2020**
- Share of global publications — from **3.5%** to over **7%**; quadruple papers in top **1%** journals
- STI intervention in about **ten** high-impact sectors

## SEC 5:2:2 | Draft STIP 2020
- Vision — India among the **top three** scientific superpowers in the coming decade; people-centric STI ecosystem
- **National Research Foundation** — hybrid funding from private capital and **CSR**
- **STI Development Bank** — corpus fund for long-term strategic areas
- Double FTE researchers, GERD and private share of GERD every **five years**
- **One Nation, One Subscription** — from the Draft STIP 2020 (not STI Policy 2013); approved **2024**
- **DRDO** — formed by merging **TDE**, **DTDP** and the **Defence Science Organisation**
- **DST-STI Hub, SPMVV Tirupati** — **sericulture**, agriculture and livelihoods for **SC/ST women** (three-year project)

## SEC 5:2:3 | GERD targets vs reality
- **SPR 1958** — during the **Second Five Year Plan** (1956–61)
- **STP 2003** — GERD to **2% of GDP** by end of the **Tenth Plan**
- **STI Policy 2013** — GERD **2% of GDP**, private spending matching public
- **2%** — national target every policy has missed
- GERD **0.84%** of GDP in **FY 2023-24** — a **15-year high** (earlier about 0.64–0.65%)

- **ANRF** funding — **₹50,000 crore** for 2023–28; about **₹36,000 crore** expected from non-government sources (not the entire amount)

# ROW 5:3 | Research and development spending in India; GERD, innovation indices, patents ecosystem
## SEC 5:3:0 | GERD (% of GDP)
- India's GERD — **0.84%** of GDP in **FY 2023–24**; a **15-year high**, reversing a decade-long decline

## SEC 5:3:1 | India's R&D data
- Private industry share of R&D (FY 2023–24) — **51.8%**; overtook public funding for the first time
- Private share of GERD (2020–21) — about **36%** (USA ~70%, South Korea ~76%)
- FTE researchers — about **262** per million people
- Scientific publications — India **third** globally; nanoscience also third (after China, USA)
- Patent filing (WIPO 2023) — India **6th**

## SEC 5:3:4 | IP law
- **Patents Act, 1970** — protection for **20 years**; invention must be novel with commercial application
- **National IPR Policy, 2016** — slogan **"Creative India; Innovative India"**
- **IIITDM Kurnool** — **Institute of National Importance** (not CSIR lab, DBT institute or state council)

## SEC 5:3:5 | Funding & start-up push
- Private R&D gap reasons — **risk aversion**, weak **lab–industry linkages**, shortage of **deep-tech capital** (not excessive private basic research)
- **RDI Scheme fund** (approved **1 July 2025**, **Rs 1 lakh crore**) — custodian **ANRF**
- **ANRF Act, 2023** — funds **basic as well as applied** research
- **Startup India** — launched **16 January 2016**
- Startup India features — winding up in **90 days** (not 30), **Fund of Funds**, **three-year** tax exemption, self-certification

## SEC 5:3:6 | Ecosystem
- **WIPO** — established **1967**, HQ **Geneva**, UN specialised agency; 1893 refers to its predecessor union bureau
- India — **third**-largest start-up hub; **1.59 lakh** DPIIT-recognised start-ups
- **KIND-X** — India–**South Korea** innovation initiative

## SEC 5:9:0 | Six great scientists
