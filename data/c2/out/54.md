### ITPA

## SEC 2:129:1 | Dowry Prohibition Act
- **Dowry** (Section 2) — property or valuable security given or agreed to be given, directly or indirectly, before, at or any time after marriage, **in connection with the marriage**
  - **Dower / mahr** under Muslim personal law is excluded

| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| **3** | giving or taking dowry — minimum **five years** |
| **4** | demanding dowry — **six months to two years** |
| **8A** | burden of proof on the **accused** |
| **8B** | **Dowry Prohibition Officers** appointed by the **State Government** |

- Offences are **cognisable, non-bailable, non-compoundable**
- As of 2025, monitoring under the **Indecent Representation** law extends to **digital and AI-generated content**

## SEC 2:129:3 | POSH Act 2013

## SEC 2:129:5 | Cases & trafficking
### Trafficking
- **CLA 2013** trafficking definition — sex trafficking, forced/bonded labour, child trafficking, slavery, **organ removal**; covers all forms **irrespective of consent**
- **US TIP Report** — only **Tier 1** fully meets the minimum standards; **Tier 2 Watch List** is a watch-list category linked to Tier 2
- **Ujjawala** (**2007**) — prevention, rescue, rehabilitation and reintegration of trafficking victims
  - now part of **Shakti Sadan** under the **Samarthya** component of Mission Shakti
### Acid attack: Laxmi v. Union of India
- Minimum compensation **₹3 lakh** for victims
- Sale of acid: purchaser's **purpose recorded**, **photo ID**, a **register**, no sale to persons **below 18**
### Other
- **POSH**: showing **pornography** is itself sexual harassment (with unwelcome contact, demands for sexual favours)
- Anti-dowry laws ineffective due to **poor enforcement**, faulty FIRs, deaths disguised as **accidents or suicides**

# ROW 2:130 | Crimes against women - trafficking, honour crimes, acid attacks; Criminal Law Amendments 2013 and 2018
## SEC 2:130:0 | NCRB data
### NCRB crime heads
- 2023 share: **cruelty by husband or relatives 29.8%** > kidnapping and abduction 19.8% > assault on modesty 18.7%
- NCRB **2024**: cruelty by husband or relatives dominant at about **42%**
- **SLL heads**: **indecent representation**, domestic violence (PWDVA); IPC/BNS heads: cruelty by husband, kidnapping/abduction
- 2023: highest crime **rate** — **Telangana**; highest **number** of cases — **Uttar Pradesh**
- Rape conviction rate 2023 — **22.7%**
- Cybercrime (NCRB 2024) crossed 1 lakh (**1,01,928**) for the first time; **fraud 72.6%**, sexual exploitation 3.1%
- Most dowry-death cases — **Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan**
- Causes: **dowry demands** may escalate into **bride burning**
- Under-reporting: stigma, fear, lack of awareness, economic dependence; **conviction rates are low**
### NFHS and Census
- NFHS-5: **23.3%** women married before 18 (NFHS-4: 26.8%)
- NFHS-5: **32%** of ever-married women faced spousal violence
- NFHS-4: **5.4%** reported marital rape; marital rape exception remains; **Delhi High Court** split verdict
- NFHS-5: sex ratio at birth **929**; overall sex ratio **1,020** (NFHS-4: 991)

|---|---|---|

- **Beti Bachao Beti Padhao** (**2015**) — declining **Child Sex Ratio**; now covers all districts
### Law and schemes
- **Honour killings** violate Articles **14, 15, 19, 21**
- **Palermo Protocol** — supplements **UNTOC**; trafficking in persons, especially women and children; **ratified by India**
- **Article 23(1)** — prohibits traffic in human beings and forced labour
  - 21A right to education; 24 child labour in hazardous employment; 39(f) DPSP on protecting children
- **Lillu v State** (2013) — struck down the **two-finger test**
- **Nirbhaya Fund** funds **One Stop Centres**, **Women Helpline 181**, **ERSS 112**
- **Safe City Projects** — **CCTV and AI**-based safety for women in **eight** major cities
### Andhra Pradesh
- **Trishul committees** (State and district) — **zero drugs**, zero tolerance to atrocities against women, **zero road accidents**
- Further **4.4%** decline in crimes against women credited to **Shakti Teams**, Women's Safety Week rallies, **Sexual Offender Sheets**

## SEC 2:130:1 | Criminal Law Amendments
### 2013 amendment (IPC)
| IPC section | Offence |
|---|---|
| 326A | Acid attack |
| 326B | Attempt to acid attack |
| 354A | Sexual harassment |
| **354B** | **Disrobing** |
| **354C** | **Voyeurism** |
| 354D | Stalking |

- Rape definition widened: penetration by **objects or body parts** and **oral acts**; minimum **seven years**
- **IPC 376E** (death for repeat rapists) — now **BNS 71**
### 2018 amendment
- Enacted after the **Kathua and Unnao** cases; linked to the fast-track court response
- Minimum for rape raised from **seven to ten years**
- Rape of girl **below 16** — minimum **20 years**; below 12 — 20 years to life or death
### BNS
- **Honour killings** — prosecuted as murder under **BNS 103**
- **BNS 69** — sexual intercourse by **deceitful means** (false promise of marriage or job, identity fraud)
- Gang-rape provision age raised from 16 to **18 years**; IPC provisions on rape, stalking, voyeurism retained
### Other
- **PCPNDT** law bans **advertising sex determination**; permits diagnosis of genetic abnormalities
- **Disha Act** needs **Presidential assent** — criminal law is in the **Concurrent List**; not in force as passed

## SEC 2:130:2 | Nirbhaya case → outcomes

- **SHe-Box** — **Sexual Harassment electronic Box**; online POSH complaints portal, speeds redressal

# ROW 2:131 | Women's political participation - 73rd/74th Amendment reservation, 106th Amendment; gender indices (GII, GGGI)
## SEC 2:131:0 | Where women's quota applies
### Constitutional provisions
| Body | Article on women's reservation |
|---|---|
| Lok Sabha (House of the People) | **330A** |
| State Assemblies | **332A** |
| Delhi Assembly | **239AA(2)(ba)** |
| Panchayats | **243D** |
| Municipalities | **243T** |

- **Article 334A** — commencement, duration, **rotation after delimitation**
- **Articles 325 and 326** — equal electoral franchise
- **106th Amendment** — no reservation in the **Rajya Sabha** or **Legislative Councils**; covers Lok Sabha, State Assemblies, Delhi Assembly
- **Delhi Assembly** — women's quota covers directly elected and **SC-reserved** seats (no ST reservation)
### Local bodies
- **Article 243D(3)** — not less than **one-third** of total Panchayat seats, incl. one-third of SC/ST seats, for women
- **Arunachal Pradesh** — exempt from SC reservation in Panchayats (no SC population)
- Women's reservation in local bodies has **no sunset**; SC/ST reservation tied to **Article 334** (to **2030** under the **104th Amendment**)
### Panchayat initiatives
- **Bokkasam Palem** (Srikalahasti mandal, Tirupati) — ranked **first nationally** among women-friendly Panchayats (Deendayal Upadhyay Panchayat Satat Vikas Awards)
- **Mahila Sabha** — women-only meeting **before the Gram Sabha** (e.g. Maharashtra)
- **Sashakt Panchayat Netri Abhiyan** — leadership, legal-awareness, gender-justice and **gender-based violence** training for Women Elected Representatives
- **Model Women-Friendly Gram Panchayats** — at least one per **district**

## SEC 2:131:2 | 106th Amendment: Art. 334A
### Article 334A
- Commences after delimitation based on the **2027 Census** (first Census after the Act)
- Reserved seats **rotated after each delimitation**
- Duration **15 years**, extendable by Parliament
- No **OBC/BC sub-quota**
- Within SC/ST reserved seats, **one-third** for SC/ST women (not 50%)
- Women's groups demanded removal of the **Census-delimitation condition** (incl. Monsoon Session)
- 106th Amendment Act, 2023 = **Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam**
### Other
- **131st Amendment Bill, 2026** — proposed Lok Sabha from **550 to 850** seats on the **2011 Census**
- Legal shields for Dalits and tribals — **SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989** and **Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955**

## SEC 2:131:3 | 73rd/74th: women in local bodies
- Reserving seats for women in **local bodies** (73rd/74th) came first in political empowerment (APPSC 2007 key)
- **73rd Amendment** in force **24 April 1993** — National Panchayati Raj Day
- **One-third** is a constitutional **floor**; States may give more (e.g. one-half)
- **Bihar** — first State with **50%** for women in PRIs (**2006**); **19 States/UTs** now give 50%
- **Andhra Pradesh** — **50 per cent** for women in local bodies

## SEC 2:131:5 | Gender indices
- **GDI** — female-to-male ratio in **health, education, command over economic resources**; value near 1 = parity
- **GII** — 0-to-1 scale, **1 = worst** for women
  - reproductive health: **maternal mortality ratio**, **adolescent birth rate**
  - India's GII pulled down by low **female labour-force participation** and low **political representation**
- **GGGI** — published by the **World Economic Forum**
  - four sub-indices: **Economic Participation and Opportunity**, **Educational Attainment**, **Health and Survival**, **Political Empowerment** (Reproductive Health belongs to GII)
- India GGGI 2023: economic participation parity **36.7%**; enrolment parity at all levels

# ROW 2:134 | SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act 1989 and amendments; Civil Rights Protection Act 1955
## SEC 2:134:0 | PCR 1955 vs PoA 1989
