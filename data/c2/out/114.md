# ROW 5:96 | India's climate commitments - NDCs, Panchamrit, net-zero 2070, LiFE mission, ISA, CDRI, carbon market
## SEC 5:96:0 | NDC targets (base year 2005)
### Targets by version
- Base year for emissions-intensity targets — **2005**
- 2015 INDC: **40%** non-fossil target was for **installed capacity**, not actual electricity generation
- Updated NDC communicated **August 2022** — formalised Panchamrit, added **Mission LiFE**
  - Panchamrit pledges in it: **45%** emissions-intensity cut by 2030 and **50%** energy from renewables (2030)
- **NDC 3.0** — **60%** non-fossil installed-capacity share (45% is the 2022 intensity target)
- **Mission LiFE** (Lifestyle for Environment) written into **NDC 3.0**; shifts focus from state action to **individual behaviour** and mindful consumption
### Progress and debates
- Emissions intensity of GDP fell **36%** below 2005 level by **2020**
- Non-fossil **generation** share need not equal **installed-capacity** share — different measures
  - Criticism: capacity alone does not show **actual electricity generated**; may overstate the transition
- **Carbon Credit Trading Scheme** — designed to meet the **45%** intensity target
- **Net zero** ≠ zero gross emissions: **residual emissions** balanced by **removals** (forests, carbon capture)
- India **supports CBDR-RC** (Common but Differentiated Responsibilities); does not reject it

# ROW 5:99 | Air pollution - sources, pollutants, NAAQS, AQI, National Clean Air Programme, stubble burning
## SEC 5:99:0 | Sources & smog
### Sources
- Main source of air pollution in Indian cities — **automobile emissions**
- **Natural** sources — **forest fires**, volcanic eruptions, dust storms (APPSC 2025: forest fires)
- Natural source of **SOx** — **volcanoes**; of **NOx** — **lightning**
- **Cadmium** — metal production and **smelting**; PAHs — incomplete combustion; ozone — photochemical reactions
- **Hydrogen sulphide** — **rotten-egg** smell (sewage, refineries, volcanoes); a toxic pollutant
- **Methane** — potent greenhouse gas
- **Lichens** disappear where **SO2** is high; **mosses** indicate heavy-metal deposition
### Smog and haze
- **Photochemical smog** — **Los Angeles**, warm sunny weather; **classical smog** — **London**, cold humid weather
  - Sunlight makes vehicle **NOx and hydrocarbons** react into **ozone and PAN** → forms on sunny days in traffic-heavy cities
- **Asian Brown Cloud** — dry-season layer of **soot, black carbon, aerosols** over South Asia and Indian Ocean; from biomass and fossil-fuel burning
### Cleaner fuels
- **CNG** — mainly **methane**; **SATAT** — compressed biogas
- **Ethanol Blending Programme** — E20-compatible vehicles (not compressed biogas)
- **Fuel injection** — promotes **complete combustion**
- **Hydrogen** — cleanest fuel; gives only **water** (no carbon, CO, SO2, particles)
- **CNG and LPG** — less CO, hydrocarbons, PM than petrol/diesel, but still emit **CO2**
## SEC 5:99:1 | Pollutant → health effect
- **Carbon monoxide** — reduces blood's **oxygen-carrying capacity**; lead — brain damage; SO2 — irritates eyes, airways
- **Ground-level ozone** — formed from **NOx and VOCs** in sunlight (not emitted directly); harms lungs and plants
  - **Stratospheric** ozone shields from UV; CFCs deplete it
- **Ammonia** (fertilisers, livestock) — drives **secondary particle** formation (ammonium salts)
## SEC 5:99:2 | Acid rain
### Pollutant facts
- ★ Steel industry pollutants — **SOx, NOx, CO and CO2** (all four; coke making, blast furnace, fuel combustion)
- ★ Gases used in AQI calculation (of CO2, CO, NO2, SO2, methane) — **CO, NO2, SO2**; AQI launched **April 2015** under **Swachh Bharat**; six categories (Good to Severe); **8** pollutants
- ★ Acid present in maximum amount in acid rain — **H2SO4** (sulphuric acid; also nitric acid)
- **Primary** pollutant — **lead** from vehicles; ozone, PAN, **ammonium sulphate** form in the atmosphere (vehicles do not emit it directly)
- Vehicles emit CO, NOx, VOCs, PM, unburnt hydrocarbons
- **PM2.5** — crosses the **placenta**; linked to pre-term birth, low birth weight (AIIMS study, 3 June 2026)
- Indoor pollution — not only biological agents: dust, smoke, pesticides, gases, mosquito coils
### Acid rain
- Acid rain — pH below **5.6**; typical **4.2–4.4**
- Main single factor — **SO2** (SO2 and NOx principal gases)
- **Sulphuric acid** — about **two-thirds** of acidity; nitric acid — one-third
- About half returns by **dry deposition**
- **Transboundary** problem — Scandinavian lakes acidified by UK and central European emissions
- Forest soils: leaches calcium, magnesium; releases toxic **aluminium**; lowers lake pH
- **Marble cancer** — yellowing and loss of lustre of marble (Taj Mahal)
- **Taj Trapezium Zone** — protects Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Fatehpur Sikri (not **Sanchi Stupa**)
- **Liming** — remedial (neutralises acidity), not preventive
### Control
- **NCAP** — CPCB coordinates with **SPCBs** (not CPCB alone)
- **Catalytic converters** — **platinum, palladium, rhodium**
- **Cloud seeding** — silver iodide, potassium iodide, **dry ice**, common salt
- **LPG** (like biogas) — burns without smoke; wood, cow-dung give smoke, CO, fine particles
## SEC 5:99:3 | Acid rain facts

## SEC 5:99:10 | Air pollution sources — natural, anthropogenic and fuel emissions
- ★ Volcanic eruptions emit **all four**: pyroclastic debris, ash and dust, nitrogen compounds, sulphur compounds
- ★ CO2, SO2, NO2 dissolve in rainwater → acid rain; high gas concentrations pollute air — both true, but not cause-and-effect
- ★ Petrol combustion in automobiles — releases **lead** (high blood pressure, kidney damage)
- ★ Tree for preventing dust pollution — **Sita Ashok**
## SEC 5:99:11 | Primary vs secondary pollutants and smog
- ★ **Photochemical smog** (summer / Los Angeles smog) — **NO2, O3 and peroxyacetyl nitrate** in sunlight; always contains **ozone**
- ★ **Asian Brown Cloud** (2002) — over **South Asia**; January–March; aerosols, sulphate, black carbon
## SEC 5:99:12 | Gases → environmental effect
### Acid rain
- ★ Acid rain caused by **nitrous oxide and sulphur dioxide** (NOx and SO2; not CO or ozone)
- ★ Components — sulphuric and nitric acids (both)
- ★ **Sulphur dioxide** — air pollutant released by burning **fossil fuel**
### Other gases
- ★ Photochemical smog produces **ozone**
- ★ **CO** — far higher **affinity for haemoglobin** than oxygen (about **200–250** times) → tissue hypoxia
- ★ Inorganic gaseous air pollutants — **hydrogen cyanide**, hydrogen sulphide
- ★ **Hydrogen sulphide** — chief source **decaying vegetation and animal matter**; also sewage plants, landfills
- ★ Harmful to sleep under a tree at night — releases **carbon dioxide**
## SEC 5:99:13 | Pollutants → source and health effect
### Carbon monoxide
- ★ **CO** — vehicle pollutant cutting blood's **oxygen-carrying capacity**; main gas in **vehicle exhaust**
- ★ Colourless gas from vehicles and cigarettes by **incomplete combustion** — **CO**; incomplete burning of petrol/diesel produces CO
- ★ CO — dissolves in haemoglobin faster than oxygen (forms **COHb**); can kill; called the most dangerous air pollutant
- ★ **Cigarette smoke** — **carbon monoxide and benzene** (also hydrogen cyanide, arsenic, formaldehyde)
### Acid rain and smog
- ★ Coal thermal stations contribute to acid rain; they also emit oxides of carbon — but acid rain is due to **SO2 and NOx** (R not the explanation)

- ★ **Smog** — a **secondary** pollutant; caused by **oxides of nitrogen and sulphur**
- ★ Produced during photochemical smog formation — **nitrogen oxides** (key)

# ROW 5:100 | Bharat Stage emission norms - evolution from BS-I to BS-VI and their implications
## SEC 5:100:0 | Norms in order
| Stage | Milestone |
|---|---|
| First norms | **1991** petrol, **1992** diesel |
| Bharat Stage (Euro-based) | began **2000** |
| BS-II | **Delhi**, **1 April 2000**; nationwide **2005** |
| BS-III | nationwide **2010** |
| BS-IV | nationwide **April 2017** |
| Leapfrog decision (skip BS-V) | **2016** |
| BS-VI | all new vehicles from **1 April 2020**; = **Euro 6** (Euro 5 = skipped BS-V) |
| BS-VI Phase 2 | **April 2023** — **Real Driving Emissions** testing on actual roads |
| BS-VII | planned **2026–27**; = **Euro 7** |
- Euro norms cover **CO**, **hydrocarbons** and **lead**
- **Benzene** — carcinogen, **leukaemia**; **NOx** forms ground-level ozone and acid rain
- **Chernobyl** — **nuclear (radioactive)** pollution; photochemical smog — NOx and hydrocarbons in sunlight
## SEC 5:100:1 | Origins & authority
- India lagged Europe by about **five years** in equivalent standards
- BS norms notified under **Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989** by **MoRTH** (advice of CPCB, MoEFCC)
- **PUC certificate** — mandatory under **CMV Rules, 1989**
- **National Auto Fuel Policy 2003** — **Mashelkar Committee** report
## SEC 5:100:2 | Why BS-V was skipped
- BS-IV → BS-VI leapfrog: tackle urgent **urban air pollution** (esp. **Delhi-NCR**) and close the gap with **Europe** in one step (no two changeovers)
## SEC 5:100:3 | Fuel sulphur by stage
| Stage | Fuel sulphur |
|---|---|
| BS-II | **500 ppm** |
| BS-III | **350 ppm** |
| BS-IV | **50 ppm** |
| BS-VI | **10 ppm** |
- BS-VI implication: refineries upgraded to supply **10 ppm** sulphur fuel nationwide
## SEC 5:100:4 | Emission limits
- **CAFE III** third draft (**16 July 2026**) — targets about **21% more relaxed** (not stricter) than the September 2025 proposal
## SEC 5:100:5 | Control technologies
- **SCR** — turns NOx into nitrogen and water using **AdBlue** (urea)
- **DPF** — traps **soot**; **OBD-II** — monitors emissions, alerts driver
- **BS-I** era — catalytic converters turned **CO into CO2**; **BS-II** — shift from carburettors to **multi-point fuel injection**
- **Nanoparticles** — pollute **water, soil** and the **food chain**
## SEC 5:100:6 | Allied measures
- **E20** — sold in all States/UTs from **1 April 2026** with minimum **RON 95** (not 91)
## SEC 5:100:7 | Exam corner
- **Euro II** ultra-low sulphur diesel — **0.05%** sulphur or less = **500 ppm**
- **Bioremediation** — uses **microorganisms**; heavy metals (cadmium, lead) limit it by poisoning microbes
## SEC 5:100:10 | Vehicular pollutants → health effect; what Euro norms limit
- **Photochemical smog** needs **sunlight** — forms when **NOx and hydrocarbons** react under sunlight

# ROW 5:101 | Water pollution - sources, eutrophication, Namami Gange, sewage treatment, groundwater contamination (arsenic, fluoride)
## SEC 5:101:0 | Key parameters
- High **BOD** — heavy organic pollution; BOD = oxygen **bacteria** need to decompose organic matter
  - Oxygen to oxidise all organic matter (biodegradable and non-biodegradable) — **COD**
- **NTU** — turbidity; **TDS** — dissolved salts; **coliform count** — faecal contamination
- Ganga bathing standard, faecal coliform — **2,500 MPN/100 ml**
- CPCB minimum DO: Class **A 6**, **B 5**, **C 4**, D 4 mg/L
- CPCB **Class A** (drinking source, disinfection only) — total coliform ≤ **50 MPN/100 ml**
- Eutrophication chain: **nutrient enrichment → algal bloom → decomposition → DO depletion** (hypoxia, fish kills)
## SEC 5:101:1 | Eutrophication
- ★ Harmful algal blooms in Indian seawater — **nutrient discharge from estuaries**, **monsoon land run-off**, **upwelling** (all three)
- ★ Blooms of pigmented **dinoflagellates** in estuaries — **red tides**
- Triggers: fertiliser runoff, sewage, detergents, aquaculture effluent, airborne nitrogen (not a **reduction** of atmospheric nitrogen)
- Groundwater **nitrate** — mainly **fertiliser use**; **56%** of districts reportedly exceed **45 mg/L**
## SEC 5:101:2 | Groundwater contaminants
- **Fluoride** (BIS **IS 10500**) — **1.0 mg/L** acceptable, **1.5 mg/L** permissible
- CGWB Ground Water Quality Report **2024**: fluoride above 1.5 mg/L at **9.04%** sites — **Rajasthan** highest, then Haryana, Karnataka
- Fluorosis: fluoride replaces hydroxyl in hydroxyapatite → **fluorapatite**
- **Nalgonda technique** (fluoride removal) — **alum, lime, bleaching powder**
- **Arsenic** belt — **Ganga-Brahmaputra** plains: **West Bengal, Bihar, Assam**; extends into Bangladesh
- **Uranium** — Punjab's **Malwa**
- Nitrate → **methaemoglobinaemia** (**blue baby syndrome**) in infants
## SEC 5:101:3 | Pollution diseases
| Disease | Cause |
|---|---|
| **Minamata** | **methyl mercury** biomagnified through fish; neurological damage |
| **Itai-itai** ('ouch-ouch') | **cadmium** from mining wastewater used for irrigation; bone, joint pain (APPSC 2024) |
| Blue baby syndrome | **nitrate** |
| Arsenicosis (blackfoot disease) | **arsenic** |
- BOD discharge limit relaxed **10 → 30 mg/L** — criticised: allows more organic pollution in effluent
## SEC 5:101:4 | Sewage treatment
- **Sequencing batch reactor (SBR)** — treats sewage in **batches in one tank**; **MBR** — biological treatment + membrane filtration
- **AMRUT 2.0** — mandates **20%** reuse of treated wastewater (not 50%); **National Framework on Safe Reuse of Treated Water** — **2022**
- **CETP** — shared plant for clusters of **small-scale industries**
- **Tiruppur** textile cluster (Zero Liquid Discharge example) — **Tamil Nadu**
- AP: **9 CETPs**; directed ZLD; banned non-biodegradable effluent discharge
- AP main water polluters — **Pharma City (Vizag)**, **tanneries**, **paper mills**, **aquaculture** (not coal mines, cement)
