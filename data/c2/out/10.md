# ROW 1:79 | Eastern Chalukya administration, society and religious conditions
## SEC 1:79:0 | Administrative terms
- Kingdom followed the **Saptanga** idea
- **Ashtadasa Tirthas** — **eighteen** administrative offices
- Council of ministers — **Tirthulu**; **Samaharta** its chief member
- **Vishaya** or **Mandala** — under a **Vishayapati** or **Mandaleswara**

## SEC 1:79:2 | Nadus → region
| Nadu | Region |
|---|---|
| **Pakanadu** | **Nellore** |
| Venginadu | parts of Krishna and Godavari |
| Kammanadu | Bapatla, Ongole, Narasaraopet |

## SEC 1:79:3 | Society & economy
### Agraharas and trade
- Brahmins receiving tax-free **agraharas** performed **Hiranyagarbha** and **Bahusuvarna** rituals
- **72 merchants** sent to **China** — **Kulothunga I**
- **Saktivarma**'s coins found in **Arakan** (Burma)
- **Penugonda** — headquarters of the **merchant guilds**
- **Pithapuram** and **Vengi** — Eastern Chalukya capitals
### Culture and learning
- **Hallisaka** (= **Kolatam**) — a popular **dance**
- **Ghatikas** — advanced **Sanskrit schools** attached to agraharas or temples
- Ghatika subjects: Vedas, Vedangas, Dharmashastras, Tarka, Vyakarana, Arthashastra, Dhanurveda, Ayurveda (Hallisaka was **not** a subject)

## SEC 1:79:4 | Religious patronage
- **Jayasimha I** — performed the **Ashvamedha**; royal prashastis boast of several Ashvamedhas
- **Vimaladitya** — built **Ramakonda** cave temple (**Ramatirtham**) for his Jain guru **Trikala Yogi Siddhanta Deva** (Ramateertham inscription)
- **Kaluchumbarru grant** — **Ammaraja II**, at the request of **Chamekamba**
- **Mushikonda** village granted to Jains — **Vishnuvardhana III**
- **Nedumba Vasati** (Jain), Vijayawada — Queen **Ayyana Mahadevi** (wife of **Kubja Vishnuvardhana** per the key)
  - Kundavva — Vimaladitya's Chola queen; Chellavva — court singer
- Jain persecution at **Potlacheruvu** — **Kakatiya age** (Eastern Chalukyas patronised Jainism, Buddhism and Hinduism)

## SEC 1:79:5 | Sects & education
- Shaiva sects — **Kapalika**, **Kalamukha**, **Pasupata**
  - Pancharatra and Vaikhanasa — Vaishnava traditions
  - Virashaiva persecution — later, Kakatiya age
- **Potlacheruvu** — a famous **Jain** centre (not Buddhist)
- Heads of Ghatikas — **Ghatika Samanyulu**

## SEC 1:79:6 | Scholars & artists
- **Chellavva** — court-linked **singer and dancer** (musicians and dancers attached to temples and courts)
  - Patronised by **Chalukya Bhima I** — granted **Attili** village and two kandikas of land
- **Kalyanakaraka** (medical treatise) — **Ugradityacharya**
- **Bhattavamana** — **Kavyalankara Sutras**; title **Kavi Vrishabha**

|---|---|
| **Ammaraja II** | **Kavigayaka Kalpataru** |
| Vishnuvardhana III | Kavi Pandita Kamadhenu |

- **Panchadarla** (Anakapalli) — **Uma Dharmalingeswara** Temple; Eastern Chalukya and **fifteenth-century** inscriptions

## SEC 1:79:9 | Society and economy — agraharas, guilds and overseas trade
- **Jayasimha II** — **Koya Kottalu** assimilated into the Brahmanical fold
- **Panduranga** — subdued the **Boyas**

## SEC 1:79:13 | Education — Ghatikas, subjects and scholars
- ★ **Ghatika** — settlement/institution of **Brahmin scholars**, often linked to temples; study of scriptures, philosophy and Vedic learning

## SEC 1:79:14 | Court scholars and artists → title, work and patron
- ★ **Kalyanakaraka** — **Ugradityacharya**, Jain scholar of **Ramatirtha**

# ROW 1:80 | Eastern Chalukya art and architecture - Biccavolu, Draksharama and Samalkot temples
## SEC 1:80:0 | Vengi school: style
- Eastern Chalukyas mainly followed the **Dravida** style — blend of Pallava and Badami Chalukya influences with local Andhra features = **Vengi School**
- Temple building concentrated in the **Krishna-Godavari delta**
- **Draksharama Bhimeswara** temple — model of the **Chalukya-Chola** style
  - Brihadeeswara — Imperial Chola; Padmakshi — Kakatiya; Lepakshi — Vijayanagara
- **Badami Chalukyas** developed **Vesara** in Karnataka; Vengi line carried a Dravida-based style into the Chalukya-Chola phase
- **Navabrahma** temples (nine), **Alampur** — **Badami Chalukyas**, 7th–8th c. CE

## SEC 1:80:1 | Temples → builder
- **108 Shiva temples** — source is the **Sataluru** inscription (Kopparam inscription is Pulakesin II's record)
- **Mandavya Narayana** (Raja Narayana) temple, **Samalkot** — **Danarnava**
- **Parvateswara** temple, Indrakeeladri, Bezawada — **Bhattupu**, commander of Chalukya Bhima I
- **Rajaraja Narendra** — Rajahmundry temples (**Vishnu**, Kotilingala, Markandeya)
- Rajahmundry **Vishnu** temple — later the **Pedda Masjid** under the **Tughlaqs**
- **Rajahmundry** — **cultural capital** of the Andhras
- Twin **Bhimeswara** temples (Draksharama, Kumararama Bhimeswara at Samalkot) — **Chalukya Bhima I**

## SEC 1:80:2 | Pancharamas (Chalukya Bhima I)
- **Chalukya Bhima I** — built the **Pancharamas** / made them Shaiva kshetras
| Pancharama | Place |
|---|---|
| Draksharama (**Bhimeswara**) | **Draksharama** |
| Kumararama / Bheemarama | **Samalkot** (Chalukya Bhimavaram) |
| **Ksheerarama** | **Palakollu** (West Godavari) |
| **Somarama** (Someswara) | **Gunupudi** near Bhimavaram |
| Amararama | Amaravati |
- Kumaraswamy — at Chebrolu

## SEC 1:80:3 | Key temple groups
- Two-storeyed **Bhimeswara** shrines, Draksharama and Samalkot — **Chalukya Bhima I**; later renovated by the **Reddis**; Samalkot gopuram a later addition
- **Bikkavolu** (Biccavolu) group — **Gunaga Vijayaditya**'s time (**9th c.**)

## SEC 1:80:4 | Facts to recall
- All Pancharamas two-storeyed except **Ksheerarama**
- Literary sources on Pancharamas — **Skanda Purana**, Srinatha's **Bhimeswara Puranam**
- **Vijayaditya II** (Narendra Mrigaraja) — **108 battles** over **12 years** against the Rashtrakutas (Govinda III, Amoghavarsha I) and Gangavadi allies

## SEC 1:80:10 | Universal angles
- ★ **Ammaraja I** (921–927) — title **Rajamahendra**; **Rajamahendravaram** (**Rajahmundry**) named after him, the cultural capital of the Andhras

# ROW 1:81 | Andhra Cholas / Telugu Cholas - Renati Cholas and their inscriptions
## SEC 1:81:0 | Renati Cholas: basics
- Claimed **Suryavamsa Kshatriya** descent from **Karikala Chola**; culture and administration Telugu
- Principal capital — **Peddachapalli**, Kamalapuram taluk, **Kadapa**
- **Dhanunjaya Varma** — **founder**, son of Nandivarma; capital **Chimbapuram**
- Their Telugu epigraphic practice later taken up by the **Eastern Chalukyas**

## SEC 1:81:1 | Rulers in order
- **Nandivarma** — **progenitor**
- **Dhanunjaya Varma** — founder
- **Punyakumara** (625–650 CE) — **greatest** ruler; titles **Pormukharama**, **Prithvivallabha**
- **Elachola Maharaja** — last ruler

## SEC 1:81:2 | Inscriptions
- **Kalamalla** inscription — **Dhanunjaya Varma** (Erikal Mutturaju Dhananjaya), **575 CE**

## SEC 1:81:3 | Telugu in records
- **33** Renati Chola Telugu inscriptions, mostly donation records (**dana sasanalu**)
- Made Telugu a royal and legislative language, replacing Prakrit and Sanskrit
- Before 575 CE — Telugu mainly in **individual names**
- **575–848 CE** — Telugu in **full prose sentences** (Renati Cholas)

## SEC 1:81:4 | Telugu Choda families
| Family | Seat |
|---|---|
| **Nellore** Chodas | **Vikrama Simhapuri** |
| Konidena Chodas | Kammanadu |
| Pottapi Chodas | Pottapi (Kadapa) |
| Renati Chodas | Renadu |
- **Velanati Chodas** ended when Kakatiya **Ganapatideva** killed **Mummadi Rajendra Chola**

## SEC 1:81:5 | Nellore Chodas
- Punyakumara's titles — **Pormukharama**, **Purushashardula**, **Uttamottama**
- **Tikkana Choda** (Tikkabhupati) — son of **Manumasiddhi I**; helped **Rajendra Chola** ascend the throne; title **Chola Sthapanacharya**
- **Kavi-Brahma Tikkana** — minister and court poet of **Manumasiddhi II**; sought Kakatiya Ganapati's help against **Vijayaganda Gopala**

## SEC 1:81:6 | Exam corner
- ★ First Telugu inscription — **Renati Cholas** (**Kalamalla** inscription of **Dhananjaya Varma**)
- **Vaidumbas** — Telugu **hero-stone** (**veerakal**) inscriptions
- Renati Cholas gave Telugu royal dignity about **five centuries** before **Nannaya**

## SEC 1:81:9 | Inscriptions → ruler and the FIRST of Telugu
- ★ Credit for establishing Telugu in Andhradesa — **Renati Cholas** (Kadapa region); earliest inscriptions entirely in Telugu, making it a language of administration

# ROW 1:84 | Kalyani (Western) Chalukyas - Tailapa II, Vikramaditya VI; Bilhana's Vikramankadevacharita, Vijnaneswara's Mitakshara
## SEC 1:84:0 | Rulers & events
- Kalyani Chalukyas — c. **973–1195 CE** (543–753 CE is Badami)
- **Tailapa II** — overthrew last Rashtrakuta **Karka II** in **973 CE**; founded the Kalyani line
- Order — **Tailapa II**, Satyasraya, Someshvara I, Vikramaditya VI
- **Someshvara I** — granted **Hanamkonda** to Kakatiya **Prola I**; killed **Rajadhiraja I** at **Koppam**; title **Vengi Purveshwara**
- **Vikramaditya VI** — greatest ruler; **Tribhuvanamalla**; ruled **50 years** (**1076–1126 CE**); controlled the Vengi feudatories
- **Chalukya-Vikrama era** — founded by Vikramaditya VI in **1076 CE** (accession), replacing the **Saka** era in royal records
- Architecture — **richly decorated doorways**, ornate pillars, intricate wall motifs, stepped superstructures

# ROW 1:85 | Imperial Cholas - Vijayalaya, Rajaraja I, Rajendra I; Gangaikonda title and the naval expeditions
## SEC 1:85:0 | Succession
### Rulers in order
- **Vijayalaya** — founded the Imperial Chola line, mid-9th c., taking **Thanjavur** from the **Muttaraiyars**
- Succession — **Vijayalaya**, **Aditya I**, **Rajaraja I**, **Rajendra I**
- **Rajaraja I** (985–1014 CE) — aka **Arumolivarman**; made the Chola state the largest in South India
- **Rajadhiraja I** — killed at **Koppam** on an elephant; title **Yanai-mel-thunjina Devar**
- **Kulottunga I** — first ruler of the **Chalukya-Chola** line; ruled from **1070** (to 1120 CE; another MCQ gives 1070–1122)

| Ruler | Title |
|---|---|

### Rajendra I and Rajaraja I
- ★ **Gangaikonda Cholapuram** — founded by **Rajendra I** (built c. **1035 CE**)
- **Kadaram** conquest, **1025 CE** — **Rajendra I** (naval expedition against Kadaram and **Srivijaya**)
- Rajendra I completed the conquest of **Ceylon**, capturing **Mahinda V**; Rajaraja I had conquered the north
- Rajaraja I — **Maldives**; attack on the Chera fleet at **Kandalur Salai**
- **Brihadeeswara** — built by Rajaraja I, completed **1010 CE**
### Chalukya-Cholas
- **Madhurantaki** — married **Rajendra Chalukya** (Kulottunga I)
- Kundavai — married Vimaladitya; Ammanga Devi — married Rajaraja Narendra
- **Saktivarman I** — Vimaladitya's elder brother; ruled Vengi with Rajaraja I's help
- **Kulottunga I** — united the Tamil (Chola) and Andhra (Vengi) kingdoms; **Mandalika Yugam** followed (1076 to c. 1210)
- Kulottunga I — abolished **tolls**; married his daughter to a Sinhala prince; campaigned in Kalinga
- Last Chalukya-Chola ruler in Vengi — **Parantaka Chola**; Vijayaditya VII — last Eastern Chalukya
- Decline after Kulottunga I — weakening **central bureaucracy**, growth of **local chieftains**; then the rise of the **Pandyas**
### Other
