# ROW 3:131 | Central assistance to Andhra Pradesh - Finance Commission transfers and centrally sponsored schemes
## SEC 3:131:0 | Central Sector vs CSS
- Plan era: State Plans financed by own resources + **Normal Central Assistance** (Gadgil / Gadgil-Mukherjee formula)
- **Centrally Sponsored Schemes** — State or Concurrent subjects; **jointly funded** by Centre and States
- **Central Sector Schemes** — **100%** Centre-funded, run by Central Ministries
| Scheme | Type |
|---|---|
| **PM-KISAN** | Central Sector |
| **Ayushman Bharat** | Central Sector |
| **NHM** | Centrally Sponsored |
| **ICDS** | Centrally Sponsored |
- APSES 2024-25 fiscal stress: **lesser than anticipated flow of funds** from the Government of India
- **Sixteenth Finance Commission** urges **outcome-linked** CSS

## SEC 3:131:1 | Constitutional channels
- **Article 275** — statutory grants: **revenue-deficit**, local-body and disaster-related grants
- **Article 282** — **discretionary** grants
- Article **280(3)(bb),(c)** — local bodies

## SEC 3:131:2 | Devolution & SCS
- States' share in divisible pool: **32% → 42%** (14th FC) → **41%** (15th FC) → **41%** (16th FC retained)
- 15th FC's cut to 41% — due to the change relating to **Jammu and Kashmir**
- **Special Category Status** began in **1969** on advice of the **Fifth Finance Commission**
- **14th FC (2015)** stopped distinguishing special and general category States

## SEC 3:131:3 | Gadgil formula & SNA-SPARSH
### Normal Central Assistance and sharing patterns
- Gadgil-Mukherjee NCA loan:grant — general category States **70:30**; Special Category States **10:90**
- CSS sharing: Special Category and Himalayan States **90:10**; general States **60:40**
- **Fourteenth** FC did not distinguish Special and General Category States in its devolution formula
- Criticism of CSS: **matching shares** may squeeze the **fiscal space** of States
### Budget and fund flow
- From **2017-18**: **Plan and Non-Plan distinction dropped** (Outcome Budget was earlier, **2005**)
- **SNA-SPARSH** — **just-in-time release** of CSS funds via State IFMIS, RBI e-Kuber and PFMS
- **VB-G RAM G Act** replaced **MGNREGA**; **60:40** Centre-State pattern for most States (MGNREGA: Centre bore 100% of unskilled wages)

## SEC 3:131:4 | Package in lieu of SCS
- SCS not granted; **Special Assistance Measure (SAM)** of **Rs 24,350 crore** instead
- RD grant within SAM: **Rs 3,979 crore**
- Centre bears **90%** of cost of **externally aided projects** under the package
- **Polavaram** — Centre to fund as national project; older estimate **Rs 55,548 crore**; releases delayed

## SEC 3:131:6 | Exam corner
- 2024-25 RE: grants fell short of BE, but total central resources rose due to **EAP loans**

## SEC 3:131:8 | Central assistance terms → meaning — Gadgil formula, CS vs CSS, SNA-SPARSH — CS vs CSS

# ROW 3:143 | Special Category Status - criteria, the 14th Finance Commission position and the controversy
## SEC 3:143:0 | Origin & FIRSTs
- SCS introduced **1969** on recommendation of the **Fifth Finance Commission**
- **NDC** endorsed the **Gadgil Formula** (1968-69) for Plan funds
- **Gadgil-Mukherjee Formula** — **1991**
- SCS **not in the Constitution** — executive, **Planning Commission-era** classification
- First three SCS States: **Jammu & Kashmir, Assam, Nagaland**
- Later: Manipur, Tripura, Himachal Pradesh, Sikkim, Mizoram, Meghalaya, Uttarakhand; **Uttarakhand** last of the **11**

## SEC 3:143:1 | Criteria
- Criteria: difficult terrain; low population density or sizeable tribal population; border location; economic and infrastructural backwardness; non-viable finances
- Not a criterion: **high population density and advanced infrastructure**

## SEC 3:143:2 | Benefits: SCS vs general
- Rationale: **larger share of central taxes** to cover gap from geographic disadvantage and backwardness
- Central loans at **concessional** interest
- Unspent funds **do not lapse**, carried forward (lapse in general States)
- **PMGSY**: habitations of **250+** (SCS) vs **500+** (plains)
- Consumer Welfare Fund seed money: **90:10** SCS vs 75:25

## SEC 3:143:3 | Finance Commissions
| FC | Chair | Period | States' share |
|---|---|---|---|
| 14th | **Y.V. Reddy** | **2015-20** | 42% |
| 15th | N.K. Singh | 2021-26 | 41% |
| 16th | **Arvind Panagariya** | **1 Apr 2026 – 31 Mar 2031** | 41% |
- 14th FC — **no distinction** between general and SCS States; Centre argued higher devolution **subsumed SCS benefits**
- AP: 42% applied to all States; did not address AP's **asymmetric loss of Hyderabad**
- **16th FC** added **Contribution to GDP** criterion, weight **10%**

## SEC 3:143:4 | AP and SCS
- SCS assurance — PM **Manmohan Singh**, **20 February 2014**, in the **Rajya Sabha** (not Lok Sabha); for **five years**
- **Special Assistance Package** — **September 2016** (after 2015 14th FC award)
- **2018** — **TDP** left NDA; **no-confidence motion** in Lok Sabha (July), defeated
- **SCS** — automatic **90:10** funding pattern; special package = project-linked assistance (no automatic 90:10)

## SEC 3:143:5 | Exam corner
- **Uttarakhand** — SCS State; Bihar, Odisha, AP never granted
- Among 11 SCS States: Mizoram, Arunachal Pradesh, Tripura; **Telangana** never granted
- Centre treats AP SCS assurance as non-binding — **not written into the AP Reorganisation Act**
