# ROW 1:119 | Foundation of Vijayanagara - Harihara and Bukka, Vidyaranya; the Sangama dynasty
## SEC 1:119:0 | Founding
### Founding legend
- Sequence: fall of **Warangal** → conversion in **Delhi** → sent against Deccan rebels → meeting with **Vidyaranya** → return to Hinduism
- **Vidyaranya** — head of the **Sringeri Shankara Math**; linked with the brothers' return to Hinduism
### Place, name, emblem
- Founded **1336** on the **southern bank of the Tungabhadra**
- **Vijayanagara** = "**City of Victory**"; built by Harihara and Bukka; ruins at **Hampi**
- **Anegondi** — **first seat** of the kingdom, set up in the name of **Sri Virupaksha**
- **Harihara I** shifted the capital from Anegondi to Vijayanagara
- Royal emblem — **Varaha** (boar)
- Sangamas **Shaiva**; the later three dynasties **Vaishnava**
### Harihara I
- **Nellore inscription** — first inscription to call Harihara I a **king**; dates his reign **1336–1355**

## SEC 1:119:1 | Origin theories
### Theories on Harihara and Bukka
- **Kakatiya-service theory** — they served **Prataparudra of Warangal**; taken to Delhi after **1323**
- **Kampili version** — captured by **Muhammad bin Tughlaq**, converted in Delhi, sent back as Sultanate governors
- **Kannada / Hoysala theory** — they were officers of **Veera Ballala III**; supported by **Father Heras**, Rice, S. K. Ayyangar, H. Krishna Sastri, P. B. Desai
- **Robert Sewell** — recorded **seven** local founding traditions
- **Kampili and Vijayanagara** — by **Nelaturi Venkataramanaiah** (not Sewell)
### Early expansion
- **Bukka I** — captured **Srisailam** and **Dupadu** (near Markapur)
- **Devaraya I** — fought the **Kandukuru Reddis**; allied with the **Velamas**
- **Harihara II** — extended rule over Kanara, Mysore, Kanchi and **Trichinopoly**
### Other city foundations
- **Nauraspur** — founded by **Ibrahim Adil Shah II**
- **Ajatashatru** — fortified **Pataliputra**; his successor **Udayin** shifted the capital from Rajagriha to Pataliputra
- **Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq** — built **Tughlaqabad** in Delhi

## SEC 1:119:2 | Four dynasties
### Order and origin
- Order: **Sangama → Saluva → Tuluva → Aravidu**
- **Andhra in origin**: only **Sangama** and **Aravidu**
- **Sangama** — longest-ruling dynasty; greatest ruler **Devaraya II**; last ruler **Praudha Raya**
### Founders

| Dynasty | Founder | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Sangama | **Harihara I** and **Bukka I** | — |
| Saluva | **Narasimha Saluva** | **First usurpation**, 1485 |
| Tuluva | **Vira Narasimha** | crowned **1505** after executing **Immadi Narasimha** |
| Aravidu | **Tirumala Raya** (Tirumala Deva Raya) | capitals **Penukonda, Chandragiri, Vellore** |

- First usurpation (**1485**): Saluva Narasimha, lord of **Chandragiri**, expelled **Praudha Raya** with help of **Tuluva Narasa Nayaka**; Narasimha Saluva immediately succeeded Prauda Deva Raya
- **Tuluva Narasa Nayaka** — father of Vira Narasimha; also called Tuluva founder in another usage
### Capitals and other facts
- After **Talikota**: **Hampi → Penukonda → Chandragiri** (both later capitals in present-day Andhra Pradesh)
- ★ **Amuktamalyada** — written by **Sri Krishnadevaraya**
- **Devaraya I's coronation** copper plates — **Vamana** seal (not the usual Varaha)
- **Nagalapuram** (near Hampi) — founded by Krishnadeva Raya for his mother **Nagala Devi**; **Tirumala Devi** and **Chinna Devi** were his queens

## SEC 1:119:3 | Sangama rulers
### Harihara I
- Annexed the **Hoysala** kingdom **1346**; the **Kadambas** 1347
- Title **Purva-Paschima Samudradhipati**
- **Ibn Battuta** — first foreign traveller, in Harihara I's reign
### Bukka I
- Took **Udayagiri** in **1343**
- Title **Vedamarga Pratishthapaka**; completed the city; reconciled Jains and Vaishnavas
- Embassy to the **Ming** emperor of China — **1374**
### Harihara II
- Longest-reigning Sangama king; titles **Rajavyasa** and **Rajavalmiki**
- First Vijayanagara ruler to attack **Sri Lanka**
- His minister **Irugappa** — built the **Ganigitti Jain temple**; wrote **Navaratnamala**
### Devaraya I
- **Motupalli Abhaya Sasanam** — records his capture of southern **Kondaveedu**, incl. **Motupalli**
### Devaraya II
- ★ Title **Gajabetekara** (elephant hunter); issued **Gajabetekara coins**
- Other titles: **Praudha Devaraya**, **Ilanti Raikonda**
- Enrolled **10,000 Muslim** soldiers, gave them jagirs and a mosque; kept a **Quran** near his throne; improved cavalry and archery
- Annexed **Kondaveedu** — **1424**; **Draksharama inscription** (1444) records his repulse of the Gajapatis
- Wrote **Mahanataka Sudhanidhi**
### Travellers by reign
- **Nicolo Conti** — Devaraya I/II; **Abdur Razzaq** — Devaraya II (**1443**); **Domingo Paes** — Krishnadevaraya
- ★ **Purandara Dasa** (1484–1564) — contemporary of **Sri Krishnadevaraya**

## SEC 1:119:6 | Bahmani wars

- **Krishnadevaraya** retook **Raichur** in **1520** (Gobburi war) with Portuguese help; took **Gulbarga** in **1523**
- Sequence: Mudgal (**1358**) → loss of **Goa** (**1472**) → Gulbarga (**1523**) → **Talikota** (**1565**)

## SEC 1:119:7 | Exam corner

## SEC 1:119:8 | Founding of Vijayanagara — year, coronation, emblem and FIRSTs
- ★ Founded **1336** by **Harihara I** and his brother **Bukka I**
- ★ Founding dynasty — **Sangama** (1336–1485); followed by Saluva, Tuluva, Aravidu
- ★ The dynasty is named after **Sangama**, father of the brothers; the family claimed **Yadava** (lunar) descent
- ★ Harihara and Bukka — feudatories of the **Kakatiyas of Warangal** (treasurers of Warangal); established **Vijayanagara**
- ★ **Harihara I** — founded the city on the south bank of the **Tungabhadra** (tributary of the **Krishna**); ruled as **agent of a deity** owning all land south of the Krishna
- ★ **First (Saluva) usurpation**, **1485** — **Saluva Narasimha** (Mahanayakacharya) overthrew the weak **Praudha Raya** to save the empire from degeneration and disintegration

# ROW 1:120 | Saluva and Tuluva dynasties; Krishnadevaraya - conquests, Amuktamalyada, the Ashtadiggajas
## SEC 1:120:0 | Usurpations & succession
### Before and after the Saluvas
- **Nuniz** — called **Virupaksha II** a drunkard and **Praudha Raya** worthless
- Usurpations: **Saluva Narasimha** (**1485**) and **Vira Narasimha** (**1505**)
- **Tuluva Narasa Nayaka** (a Saluva general) — real ruler under **Immadi Narasimha**
- **Vira Narasimha** — began Tuluva rule, replacing the Saluvas
- (Other transitions: **Tirumala Deva Raya** began Aravidu rule; **Pushyamitra Shunga** replaced the Mauryas)
### Tuluvas
- Tuluva mother tongue — **Kannada**; progenitor **Timmaraju**; **Telugu** favoured at court
- **1509** — Krishnadeva Raya succeeded his half-brother **Vira Narasimha**
- ★ **Saluva Timmarusu** (**Appaji**) — Prime Minister / chief minister of **Krishnadevaraya**
- **Achyutaraya** — only king crowned at **Tirupati** (and Srikalahasti); **Nuniz** visited in his reign
- **Aliya Rama Raya** — Krishnadevaraya's **son-in-law**, of the **Aravidu** family
- Sadasiva and Rama Raya defeated Governor **Martin de Souza** (damaged temples); **João de Castro** made peace
- After **Rama Raya** was beheaded at **Talikota (1565)**, Sadasiva and Tirumala fled to **Penukonda**
- Last Tuluva ruler — **Sadasiva**

## SEC 1:120:1 | Krishnadevaraya's campaigns
### Campaigns

| Campaign | Year | Result |
|---|---|---|

- **Balakrishna idol** — taken from Udayagiri to **Hampi**; Krishna temple built for it
- **Kondaveedu** conquest — **Talapurusha Danam** at **Amaravati**
### Portuguese and titles
- For helping **Albuquerque** take **Goa** — Portuguese allowed a fort at **Bhatkal**; he received horses and military training
- Krishnadevaraya's titles: **Andhra Bhoja**, **Sahiti Samarangana Sarvabhauma**, **Kannada Rajya Rama Ramana** (not Gajabetekara — Devaraya II)
- **Amuktamalyada** = "**one who offers the garland she has worn**" — story of **Goda Devi / Andal**

## SEC 1:120:2 | Kalinga & Portuguese

## SEC 1:120:3 | Amuktamalyada
### The work
- Written during the **Kalinga campaign**, after **Andhra Mahavishnu of Srikakulam** commanded him in a dream; dedicated to **Lord Venkateswara**
- One of the Telugu **Pancha Kavyas**; first Telugu work named after its **heroine** (Goda Devi)
- Contents: Goda Devi's marriage, **Yamunacharya Charitra**, **Maladasari Katha**, political advice; does **not** describe his invasions
- "**Desa Bhashalandu Telugu Lessa**" — in the preface to Amuktamalyada
### Language of his works

| Work | Language |
|---|---|
| **Amuktamalyada** | **Telugu** (his only Telugu work) |
| **Usha Parinayam** | Sanskrit |
| **Jambavati Kalyanam** (drama; mentions Virupaksha, Vasantotsavams) | **Sanskrit** |
| Rasamanjari, Madalasa Charita, Jnana Chintamani, Satyavadhu Parinayam | Sanskrit |

## SEC 1:121:2 | Nayankara (amaram)
- **Amaram** — land to military chiefs in lieu of cash salary; chiefs called **Amaranayakas**, Nayaks, Palaiyagars, Poligars
- **Palegars / Poligars** — grew out of the **Amara-nayakas** when central authority weakened after **Talikota**
- After **1565** — semi-independent, later independent **Nayaka states**: **Madurai**, **Tanjore**, **Keladi**
- Land classes: **Bagayati** — garden; **Nanja** — wet; **Punja** — dry; **Banjara** — waste
