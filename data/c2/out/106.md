# ROW 5:53 | India's energy needs and deficit - primary energy demand, per-capita consumption, peak deficit
## SEC 5:53:0 | Key terms

# ROW 5:54 | India's energy resources and import dependence - crude oil, coal, gas import share; energy security
## SEC 5:54:0 | Import dependence
- India — world's **fifth-largest** coal reserves (older estimate: fourth)

## SEC 5:54:2 | 2026 Hormuz shock
- India's **26** sedimentary basins — only **7** producing
- **Strait of Hormuz** — about 15 million bbl/day, roughly **one-third** (34%) of world crude trade; about a fifth of global LNG trade
- Higher oil prices — **wider trade deficit** and **higher inflation**
- After Hormuz closure (**March 2026**) — 41% jump in July crude bill, LPG crunch, proposed **41 MT** strategic fuel system
## SEC 5:54:3 | Strategic Petroleum Reserves
### India's position
- India — ~17% of world population, only **0.4%** of oil reserves; **0.6%** of gas, **7%** of coal reserves
- Largest crude supplier in recent years — **Russia** (Iraq, Saudi Arabia also major)
- **OPEC** — about **40%** of global oil; its share of India's crude imports below 50%
- **Krishna-Godavari Basin** — **offshore East Coast**; AP produces gas and crude there
### Reserves and policy
- **IEA norm** — **90 days** of cover (not 60)
- **Visakhapatnam** SPR — **1.33 MMT**; **Padur** (Karnataka) — 2.5 MMT
- **HELP** — **single licence** for all hydrocarbons; OALP concerns upstream exploration and production
