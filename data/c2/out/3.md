# ROW 1:25 | Rise of Magadha - Haryankas (Bimbisara, Ajatashatru), Shishunagas, Nandas; causes of supremacy
## SEC 1:25:0 | Causes of supremacy
- **Elephants** from the east strengthened Magadha's military power
- Nearby **iron ore** and **Ganga-Son** river transport also aided its rise
- Geography: **alluvial soil**, **ample rainfall** and **navigable rivers** (agriculture, transport, trade)
- Society relatively **unorthodox**, with little **Vedic** hold
- **Pataliputra** at the confluence of the **Ganga and Son** (Gandak nearby): a **water-fort**, central in the Ganga plain
- Defensible capitals also supported the rise

## SEC 1:25:1 | Rulers in order
### Haryanka succession
- ★ **Ajatashatru** killed his father (Bimbisara): patricide prince
- Buddhist texts: patricide chain **Bimbisara → Ajatashatru → Udayin**
- **Udayin** probably killed at the instigation of **Palaka of Avanti**
### What each ruler did
| Ruler | Associated with |
|---|---|
| Bimbisara | conquered **Anga** |
| Ajatashatru | killed Bimbisara; defeated and annexed the **Vajji** confederacy; patron of **First Buddhist Council** at **Rajgriha** |
| Udayin | shifted the capital |
| Kalashoka | **Second Buddhist Council** |

### Places
- Buddhist Councils: First **Rajgriha**, Second **Vaishali**, Third **Pataliputra**
- **Ujjayini** — capital of Avanti
- **Rajgriha** (Girivraj) = modern **Rajgir**; Pataliputra = modern **Patna**
- ★ **Bulandibagh** — ancient site of **Pataliputra**
- ★ **Kumrahar** — remains of **Pataliputra**

| **Khema** | **Madra** | |

### Vaishali
- ★ **Vaishali** — first republic of the world (6th c. BC)
- ★ Established by the **Lichchhavis**
- Named after King **Vishal** (Mahabharata era); Buddhist pilgrimage site; birthplace of **Mahavira**

## SEC 1:25:3 | Capitals
- **Rajagriha** — protected by hills; Bimbisara ruled from it initially
- **Ajatashatru** built a fort at **Pataligrama**
- **Udayin** shifted the capital to **Pataliputra**
- **Shishunaga — Vaishali**
- **Kalashoka** — associated with Pataliputra again

## SEC 1:25:4 | Nandas
- Nandas: first **non-Kshatriya** dynasty; among India's first **empire-builders**
- **Mahapadma Nanda** — extensive imperial expansion
- **Sarvakshatrantaka** (Mahapadma) — destroyer of Kshatriyas; **Ekarat** — sole sovereign
- **Dhana Nanda** — Greek names **Agrammes** / **Xandrames**; ruled at the time of **Alexander's** invasion
- **Hathigumpha inscription** (Kharavela): a **Nandaraja** and an **irrigation canal** in **Kalinga**; Nanda power towards Kalinga and Deccan
- Magadha's rise became the base of the **Mauryan Empire**

## SEC 1:25:5 | Persons
### Jivaka
- ★ **Jivaka** — physician, contemporary of Buddha, at the court of **Bimbisara**
- Sent by Bimbisara to treat **Pradyota (Chandapradyota) of Avanti** for **jaundice**
### Rulers
- **Bimbisara** — real founder of the Magadha Empire; succeeded by Ajatashatru
- ★ First Buddhist Council held in the reign of **Ajatashatru**
- ★ **Saptaparni Cave** — at **Rajgriha**
- **Jarasandha** (Brihadratha dynasty) ruled from **Girivraj** (Sabha Parva, Mahabharata)

## SEC 1:25:6 | Exam corner
- ★ Not a contemporary of Bimbisara, Prasenjit and Buddha: **Milinda** (Menander)
  - **Bimbisara** (544-492 BC), Haryanka, actual founder of Magadha empire
  - **Prasenjit** — ruler of **Kosala**, contemporary of Buddha
- ★ First capital of Magadha: **Rajgriha / Girivraj**, not Vaishali
  - Puranas: first dynasty of Magadha **Brihadratha**; **Jarasandha** made Girivraja (Rajgir) capital
  - Rajgriha: First Buddhist Council; Mahavira's first sermon
- **Vaishali** — Vajji capital; Second Buddhist Council venue
- **Champa** — capital of **Anga**; Bimbisara annexed Anga
- **Bimbisara** — conquest of **Anga**, matrimonial alliances (Kashi marriage settlement), contemporary of **Buddha** and **Mahavira**
- **Ajatashatru** fortified **Pataligrama** on the Ganga during the war with the **Vajjis**; son **Udayin** made Pataliputra the capital

## SEC 1:25:8 | Haryanka rulers and persons — queens, succession, physician
### Bimbisara
- ★ **Bimbisara** — founder of the **Haryanka** dynasty (Haryanka Vansh)
- ★ **Jeevaka** — physician of Bimbisara
### Ajatashatru
- ★ **Ajatashatru** (492-460 BC) killed his father **Bimbisara**; was himself killed by his son **Udayin** (not Udayan of Vatsa)
- **Kunika** — other name of Ajatashatru
- ★ **Ajatashatru** founded **Pataligrama** on the Ganga → grew into **Pataliputra**
- ★ Ajatashatru defeated and assimilated the **Vajjis**
  - Vajji capital **Vaishali**, ruled by the **Lichchhavis**; estrangement from Bimbisara's time
### Pataliputra and capitals
- ★ Pataliputra (modern **Patna**): **Ganga** to the north, **Son** to the west
- ★ Magadha capitals: **Girivraja/Rajgriha**, then **Pataliputra** (shifted by **Udayin**)
- ★ **Kaushambi** was not a Magadha capital — ruled by **Vatsa**
### Avanti
- ★ **Chand-pradyota** — ruler of **Avanti**; son of **Pulika** (killed his master at Ujjain); contemporary of Buddha and Bimbisara; named in the **Mahavagga** and Puranas

## SEC 1:25:9 | Capitals and places → who and why
- ★ Initial capital of Magadha: **Girivraj (Rajgriha)**
- ★ First Buddhist Council: **Rajgriha (Rajgir)**, **Saptaparni Cave**, just after Buddha's Mahaparinirvana, under **Ajatashatru**
- ★ Second Buddhist Council: **Vaishali**
- ★ Third Buddhist Council: **Pataliputra**
- ★ **Champa** — capital of **Anga** (modern Munger and Bhagalpur); annexed by **Bimbisara**; Magadha lay to its west

## SEC 1:25:10 | Rulers → contributions, titles and FIRSTs
- ★ **Udayin** — first chose **Pataliputra** as capital

## SEC 1:25:11 | Universal angles
- ★ **Magadha** — initially the most powerful state in 6th c. BC; richest Mahajanapada
- Long rivalry with **Kosala**, **Anga** and **Kashi**

# ROW 1:26 | Jainism - Tirthankaras, life of Mahavira, Triratna, Panchamahavratas, anekantavada and syadvada
## SEC 1:26:0 | Tirthankaras
### Historicity and symbols
- Historicity established only for **Parshvanatha** (23rd) and **Mahavira** (24th); first 22 uncertain
- **Munisuvrata** (Munisuvrat Swami) — **20th** Tirthankara

| Tirthankara | Symbol | Place |
|---|---|---|
| Rishabhadeva | **Bull** | Mount **Kailash** |
| Munisuvrata | **Tortoise** | **Sammed Shikhar** |
| Parshvanatha | **Serpent** | |
| Mahavira | **Lion** | |
| Neminatha | | **Girnar** |
| Vasupujya | | **Champapuri** (not Pavapuri) |

### Mahavira and images
- ★ **Jamali** — first follower of Mahavira
- Mahavira's titles: **Jina**, **Kevalin**, **Nirgrantha**, Mahavira, **Arhat**; **Tathagata** is the Buddha's
- Tirthankara images: **Padmasana** or standing **Kayotsarga**

## SEC 1:26:1 | Life of Mahavira
- **Parshvanatha** — symbol **serpent**; renounced at **30**; followers wore **white**; Nirvana at **Sammed Shikhar**
- Mahavira born at **Kundagrama** near **Vaishali**; son of **Siddhartha** of the **Jnatrika** clan
- Mother **Trishala** — sister of **Chetaka**, chief of the **Lichchhavis**
- Renounced at **30**, **12** years of asceticism, **Kevala Jnana** at **42**
- Kaivalya at **Jrimbhik**; died (Nirvana) at **Pavapuri**, aged **72**

## SEC 1:26:2 | Doctrines
- **Anekantavada** — reality is many-sided (manifold nature of reality)
  - basis of **tolerance** and **pluralism**, acceptance of multiple viewpoints
- **Syadvada** — conditioned predication
- **Nayavada** — theory of partial standpoints
- **Digambara** — **sky-clad**; monks remain unclothed

## SEC 1:26:3 | Path to liberation
- **Jiva** — conscious soul; **Ajiva** — **non-conscious matter**
- **Siddha** — fully liberated soul; **Jina** — conqueror of passions
- **Samvara** — stoppage of karmic inflow; **Nirjara** — elimination of existing karma
- Syadvada — relativity of knowledge; **Sallekhana** — gradual fasting
- Liberation: Jainism **kevala jnana**, Buddhism **nirvana**; Buddhism spread widely **outside India**

## SEC 1:26:4 | Five Mahavratas
### The vows
- Parshvanatha's four: **Ahimsa**, **Satya**, **Asteya**, **Aparigraha**; Mahavira added **Brahmacharya**

| Vow | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ahimsa | non-violence |
| Satya | truth |
| Asteya | **non-stealing** |
| Aparigraha | **non-possession** |
| Brahmacharya | **chastity** (celibacy) |

- **Anuvratas** — milder vows of householders (non-injury, non-stealing, non-adultery, truth, non-possession)
- Jain **ahimsa** stricter, extends even to **thought**; Buddhism follows the **Middle Way**
### Sallekhana and the south
- **Sallekhana** — ritual fast unto death
  - **Paryushana** festival; **Samvatsari** day of forgiveness; **Pratikramana** introspection
- **Chandragupta Maurya** — Sallekhana at **Shravanabelagola**
- **Rayalaseema** as Jain corridor: **Bhadrabahu–Chandragupta** route to Shravanabelagola passed through **Kurnool** and **Anantapur**

## SEC 1:26:5 | Jainism vs Buddhism
- ★ **Anuvrata** — Jainism; small vows for householders (**śrāvakas**), unlike Mahavratas for ascetics
- ★ **Tripitaka** — Buddhism (in **Pali**)
- Jain **Agamas** — **Ardhamagadhi**
- Jainism **rejects** Vedic authority; non-theistic; souls even in natural objects
- Jain **Ratnatraya**: Right Faith, Right Knowledge, Right Conduct (path to moksha)
- Buddhist **Triratna**: Buddha, Dhamma, Sangha (three refuges)
- Jainism: eternal soul vs Buddhism: **anatta** (no-self); both accept karma and rebirth
- Decline / limited appeal of Jainism: **severe austerities**, loss of **royal patronage**, **Digambara–Svetambara split**

## SEC 1:26:6 | Persons
### Teachers
- ★ **Kundakundacharya** (Digambara philosopher) — preached **Syadvada**
  - **Somadeva Suri** only had the title **Syadvadachala Simha**; wrote **Yasastilaka Champu** and **Nitivakyamrita**
- Kundakundacharya wrote **Samayasara**, **Pravachanasara**, **Panchastikaya**
- **Bhadrabahu** — linked with taking Jainism south
- **Makkhali Gosala** — companion of Mahavira; founded the **Ajivika** sect (**niyati**)
- Mahavira had **eleven Ganadharas**; **Arya Sudharma**, the only survivor, became the first **Thera**
### Rulers
- ★ **Chetak — Lichchhavi**; daughter **Chelana** married **Bimbisara**; Lichchhavi a clan of the **Vajji** union
- **Lichchhavi** republic — first to recognise Jainism
- Mahavira's contemporaries: **Bimbisara**, **Ajatashatru** (Magadha), **Chetaka** (Lichchhavi), **Pradyota** (Avanti)
- **Prasenjit** — king of **Kosala** in Buddha's time

## SEC 1:26:7 | Jainism in Andhra
- **Ajivikas** — doctrine of **niyati** (fate); Charvaka — materialism
- **Vaddamanu** basadi (Guntur) — built by **Samprati**, Ashoka's grandson
  - **Kharavela** built a vihara near it; **Indra III** built **Danavulapadu**
- **Mahapadma Nanda** — conquered Andhra and propagated Jainism
- Early Kakatiyas were Jains: **Prolaraju's Hanamkonda** inscription begins with a Jain prayer
- **Rishabhdev** first, **Parshvanath** 23rd, **Mahavira** 24th and last

## SEC 1:26:8 | Exam corner
- ★ **Tirthankara** — Jain term; **24** Tirthankaras (1 Rishabhnath, 2 Ajitnath, 3 Sambhavnath...)
- Four forms of existence: **Deva**, **Manushya**, **Tiryancha**, **Naraki**
- **Santhara** (Sallekhana) — **Jain** fast unto death to purify karma

## SEC 1:26:9 | Tirthankaras → symbol, nirvana place; FIRSTs
### Mahavira
- ★ Mahavira — **Kshatriya**, royal family; father **Siddhartha** of the Lichchhavi confederacy / head of the **Gyatrik** Kshatriya Sangh
- ★ Born at **Kundagram (Kundalpur)** near **Vaishali**, c. **599 BC**
- ★ Mother **Trishala**, sister of **Chetak** (Lichchhavi head); elder brother **Nandivardhan**
- ★ He **did marry**: wife **Yashoda**
- ★ Enlightenment on the **Rijupalika** river at **Jrimbhik**
- ★ Died at **Pavapuri** (near Rajgir), c. **72**, **527 BC**
- ★ **Mahavira** — last (24th) Tirthankara
### Others
- ★ **Nathamuni** — not a Jain Tirthankara
- ★ **Sammed Shikhar** — pilgrimage linked with **Parshvanath**
  - Parshvanatha (23rd): born **850 BC** at **Varanasi** to **Aswasena** and **Vama**; **Ikshvaku** dynasty; renounced at **30**

## SEC 1:26:11 | Persons → association with Mahavira and Jain thought
- ★ **Yapaniya** — Jain sect; from Digambaras, followed some Svetambara views; worshipped nude Tirthankara images
- ★ **Makkhali Gosala** — founded the **Ajivika** sect; early disciple of Mahavira, later fell out

## SEC 1:26:12 | Jain philosophy — terms and doctrines → meaning
### Doctrines
- ★ **Anekantavada** — core philosophy of **Jainism**: multiple viewpoints
- ★ Two main doctrines: **Anekantavada** and **Syadvada**; every being has numerous properties (permanent one = attribute)
- ★ **Anekantavada** and **Syadvada** — Jain; **Sunyavada** and **Sarvastivada** — Buddhist
- ★ **Triratna** (Right faith, Right knowledge, Right action) — **Jainism**, means to Moksha
- ★ Souls even in **rocks** and **running water** — Jainism
  - universe = living souls + five non-living categories (e.g. **akasa**)
- ★ Four forms of existence do not include **Yaksha** (demi-gods)
- ★ **Samadhi Maran** (Sallekhana, Sanyasna Maran) — **Jain** last vow, gradually reducing food and liquid
- ★ No **final annihilation** of the world in **Jainism**: universe eternal
- ★ Jainism and Buddhism both: **karma** and **transmigration** of soul
- ★ **Rishabhanath** — first Tirthankara; **Parshvanath** born at **Varanasi**
- ★ **Ajivika**: leader **Makkhali Gosala**, central idea **Niyati** (fate), regular **sabhas**
- Five **Mahavratas** of monks: Ahimsa, Satya, Brahmacharya, Asteya, Aparigraha
### Religion of Andhra dynasties
- ★ **Ikshvaku** royal women (**Chantisiri**, **Bodhisiri**) patronised **Buddhism** (Nagarjunakonda)
- ★ **Satavahana** royal religion: **Vedic**, while patronising Buddhism
- ★ Early **Kakatiyas**: **Jainism** (as Western Chalukya feudatories); later **Shaivism**

## SEC 1:26:13 | Panchamahavratas, Anuvratas and Jain practices — vows, rituals, Sallekhana
- ★ **Gomateshwara** statue at Shravanabelagola = **Bahubali**, not a Tirthankara
  - Bahubali — son of the first Tirthankara **Rishabhanatha** (Adinatha)

## SEC 1:26:14 | Jainism and Buddhism compared
- ★ Common to both: **indifference to Vedas**, **denial of rituals**, **non-injury** to animals (non-Vedic śramaṇa traditions)
  - Not common: avoidance of extremes of penance and enjoyment
- ★ **Self-mortification** — not common between Buddhism and Jainism
- ★ **Anatmavada** (no-self) — **Buddhism**; from **Pratityasamutpada**

# ROW 1:27 | Jain councils, Digambara-Svetambara split, Jain literature (Angas), spread and royal patrons
## SEC 1:27:0 | Jain councils

| Council | Place | Year / patron | President | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First | **Pataliputra** | reign of **Chandragupta Maurya** | **Sthulabhadra** | canon arranged into **12 Angas**; Svetambara Agama edited; first split |

- ★ First Jain Council at **Pataliputra**; Svetambara Agama edited there
- ★ **Sthulabhadra** — first founder of the **Svetambara** creed at the first partition
- Satavahana-era Jain conference at **Venkatathatipuri** — presided by **Arhabali**
- Jain texts: **Agamas** or **Siddhanta**, in **Ardhamagadhi** (eastern Prakrit)
- **Mathura** and **Ujjain** — Jain centres in the early Christian era
- **Samrat Samprati Museum** (April 2026) — **Koba**, **Gujarat**

## SEC 1:27:1 | Digambara vs Svetambara
- **Digambaras** — sky-clad; women must be reborn as men for moksha
- **Svetambaras** — white robes; accept liberation for women
- **Terapanthi** — founded by **Acharya Bhikshu**
- **Sthanakvasis** — oppose idol worship
- **Sthulabhadra** led the group that stayed in **Magadha**; **Bhadrabahu** led the group that went south
- **Acharanga Sutra** — code of conduct for monks; **Bhagavati Sutra** — doctrines

## SEC 1:27:2 | Split & sub-sects
- **12-year famine** in Magadha (Chandragupta Maurya) → **Bhadrabahu** (sixth Thera) and Chandragupta went to **Shravanabelagola** (Karnataka)

## SEC 1:27:3 | Jain canon
- ★ **Fourteen Purvas** — earliest Jain scripture; now mostly lost
  - last to know them: **Sambhutavijay** and **Bhadrabahu** (time of the last Nanda)
- Ganadharas compiled Mahavira's teaching into **12 Angas** (not 14)
- Digambaras: all original Angas **lost**; Svetambara canon has the 12 Angas

|---|---|
| Angas | 12 |
| Upangas | 12 |
| **Prakirnas** | **10** |
| Cheda Sutras | 6 |
| Mula Sutras | 4 |

- Jains wrote the earliest **Apabhramsa** works and its first grammar
- Jain literature enriched **Prakrit**, **Apabhramsa**, **Kannada** and **Telugu**

# ROW 1:28 | Buddhism - life of the Buddha, Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path, Pratityasamutpada, Sangha
## SEC 1:28:0 | Family
- Mother **Maya** — **Koliya** princess; father **Suddhodana** — chief of the **Sakyas**
- Raised by **Mahapajapati Gautami**
