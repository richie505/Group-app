# ROW 1:188 | Raja Rammohan Roy and the Brahmo Samaj; abolition of Sati; Debendranath and Keshab Chandra Sen
## SEC 1:188:0 | Raja Rammohan Roy (1772-1833)
### Titles and life
- ★ **Father of Indian Renaissance** — Raja Rammohan Roy; also Father of Modern India, first Modern Man of India
- **Maharishi** — title of Debendranath Tagore, not of Rammohan Roy
- Title **'Raja'** given by Mughal emperor **Akbar II**, who sent him to England as his envoy
- Knew Persian, Arabic, Sanskrit and English
- ★ Died at **Bristol** (England), 1833; his **Samadhi** is at Bristol
- **Bengal** — first Indian region to experience a renaissance; in Andhra, **Rajahmundry** linked with the first renaissance
### Institutions and campaigns
| Body | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Atmiya Sabha | **1815** | grew into the Brahmo Sabha |
| Hindu College, Calcutta | **1817** | ★ Rammohan Roy helped **David Hare** (with Alexander Duff); India's first Western-style secular college, later **Presidency College** |
| English school | **1822** | started by Rammohan Roy |
| Vedanta College | **1825** | blended Indian and Western learning |
| Brahmo Sabha, Calcutta | **August 1828** | founded with **Dwarkanath Tagore**; later renamed Brahmo Samaj |
- Protested the **Press Ordinance of 1823**; demanded fixed maximum rents for peasants
- Respected Eastern philosophies (Upanishads, Vedanta) but wanted a **rational and scientific approach**
- **Brahmo Samaj** opposed **polygamy** and child marriage; supported widow remarriage, women's education and property rights
### Related bodies
- **Prarthana Samaj** — worked through **persuasion**, not violent confrontation (widow remarriage, women's education, inter-caste dining)
- **National Social Conference** (1887) — **M.G. Ranade** with **Raghunath Rao**; social wing of the Congress sessions
- **Ramakrishna Mission** — **non-political, non-sectarian and humanitarian**

## SEC 1:188:2 | Brahmo journey
### Splits and bodies
- ★ **Atmiya Sabha** (1815) — Rammohan Roy, not Debendranath Tagore
- **Brahmo Sabha** (1828) — Rammohan Roy with Dwarkanath Tagore; renamed **Brahmo Samaj** by **Debendranath Tagore**
- **Tattvabodhini Sabha** (1839) — led by **Debendranath Tagore**
- 1866 split — Debendranath's body became the **Adi Brahmo Samaj**; Keshab Chandra Sen led the **Brahmo Samaj of India**
- ★ **Prarthana Samaj** (1867, Bombay) — Atmaram Pandurang, inspired by Keshab Chandra Sen
- **Sadharan Brahmo Samaj** (**1878**) — split after Keshab married his **under-age daughter** to the **Cooch Behar** prince; Ananda Mohan Bose and Shivnath Shastri
- **Nava Vidhan** (New Dispensation, 1880–81) — **Keshab Chandra Sen**, not Debendranath
- **Paramahansa Mandali** — 1849
### Order and pairs
- Brahmo Sabha (1828) → Tattvabodhini Sabha (1839) → Arya Samaj (1875) → Ramakrishna Mission (1897)
- **Native Marriage Act** (Act III of 1872) = **Brahmo Marriage Act**; legalised inter-caste marriage, fixed minimum marriage ages
- **Jyotiba Phule** — Satyashodhak Samaj; Dayanand — Arya Samaj; Vivekananda — Ramakrishna Mission
## SEC 1:188:3 | Abolition of Sati
- **Bengal Sati Regulation**, **4 December 1829** = **Regulation XVII** of the Bengal Code; Sati illegal and punishable
- Passed under Governor-General **Lord William Bentinck**; extended to Madras and Bombay in **1830**
- **Dharma Sabha** (1830, orthodox, against the Sati ban) — **Raja Radhakant Deb**

## SEC 1:188:4 | Brahmo leaders
- **Keshab Chandra Sen** — Acharya 1862; Brahmo Samaj of India 1866; Indian Reform Association 1870; **Nava Vidhan 1880–81**
- **Mannava Buchayya Pantulu** — first Andhra associated with the Brahmo Samaj (not one of the Trinity)

## SEC 1:188:5 | Brahmo tenets
- Brahmo Samaj worshipped **one formless, eternal God**; rejected avatars; no scripture as final authority
- **Idols and animal sacrifice** banned in Brahmo halls; rejected priestly ritualism and caste; collective prayer
- **Ramakrishna Paramahamsa** — Hari, Ram, Allah, Christ = **different names for the same God** (unity of all religions)

## SEC 1:189:2 | Mandali vs Prarthana Samaj
### Paramahansa Mandali
- **Secret society** believing in **one God**; broke caste rules; for women's education and widow remarriage
- Founded by **Dadoba Pandurang** with **Durgaram Mancharam** (Manav Dharma Sabha, Surat)
- Not an open public organisation; the Arya Samaj was not secret
### Prarthana Samaj
- Bombay **1867**, inspired by the visit of **Keshab Chandra Sen**
- Leaders — **M.G. Ranade** and **R.G. Bhandarkar**
- Worked by **persuasion and social work**; raising marriage age, uplift of depressed classes
- Unlike Brahmos, did **not forbid idol worship** or abandon caste practices
### Others
- **Sharada Sadan** (Pandita Ramabai) — **home for widows**
- **Arya Samaj** — rejected idol worship and ritualism

# ROW 1:190 | Arya Samaj and Dayananda Saraswati - Satyarth Prakash, shuddhi, Dayanand Anglo-Vedic schools
## SEC 1:190:0 | Dayananda Saraswati
- ★ **Suddhi Movement** — **Dayananda Saraswati**; reconverting people back into the Hindu fold
- ★ **"India for the Indians"** — **Dayananda Saraswati**
- Born **Mool Shankar Tiwari**
- Died **1883** at **Ajmer**; guru **Virajananda** of Mathura; Lahore was later Arya Samaj HQ
- **'Martin Luther of India'** — title given by **Lala Lajpat Rai**; Dayananda first used the term **Swaraj**
- **'Back to the Vedas'** — four Vedas **infallible**, primordial word of God
## SEC 1:190:1 | Satyarth Prakash
- **Satyarth Prakash** = **'The Light of Truth'** (also 'The True Exposition')
## SEC 1:190:2 | Arya Samaj (1875)
- Founded at **Bombay**, **1875**, by Dayananda; HQ later shifted to **Lahore**
- Name means **'Society of Nobles'**; strongest base **Punjab**
- **Ten Principles** — truth, rational thought, welfare of all; rejected **caste by birth**
- **'Nursery of militant nationalists'** — influenced Lala Lajpat Rai, Swami Shraddhanand, Bhagat Singh's family
## SEC 1:190:3 | Shuddhi movement
- ★ **Arya Samaj** — 1875; ★ **Al Hilal** (Urdu weekly, 1912) — **Maulana Abul Kalam Azad**; ★ **Presidency College** (formerly Hindu College), Calcutta — Rammohan Roy; ★ Lala Lajpat Rai was associated with **'The People'**
- ★ **Swami Shraddhanand** — speech on Hindu-Muslim unity from the pulpit of **Jama Masjid, Delhi**, **4 April 1919**
- **Satyarth Prakash** — criticised orthodoxy, idolatry, caste by birth, child marriage (did not advocate orthodoxy)
- **Shuddhi** — started by the **Arya Samaj** to reconvert converts to Islam/Christianity
- 1920s Shuddhi campaign — **Swami Shraddhanand**, **Bharatiya Hindu Shuddhi Sabha** (1923)
## SEC 1:190:4 | Hyderabad & Andhra
- Arya Samaj **satyagraha in Hyderabad**, **1938–39** — against the **Nizam's curbs** on religious and civil rights
## SEC 1:190:5 | Exam corner
- Dayananda wrote his main work in **Hindi**; promoted Hindi as a **link language**
- **Shuddhi** and aggressive attacks on other faiths — criticised for sharpening communal divisions
## SEC 1:190:7 | Satyarth Prakash and "Back to the Vedas" → author, language, ideas

- ★ Dayanand — born **1824**, **Morvi** (Gujarat); original name Mool Shankar Tiwari

# ROW 1:191 | Ramakrishna Paramahansa and Swami Vivekananda; Ramakrishna Mission; Theosophical Society

### Ramakrishna
- Mystic and priest at the **Kali Temple, Dakshineswar** (near Calcutta)
- Paths to salvation — **devotion**, meditation and renunciation (devotion not excluded)
- Birth name **Gadadhar Chattopadhyaya**

- ★ **'Practical Vedanta'** — **Vivekananda**; first speech on it **10 November 1896**, London

- **Vedanta Societies** — in the **USA and Britain**
- **Sister Nivedita** — **Margaret Noble**
### Ramakrishna Mission
- **Monastic order** (renunciation, practical spirituality) **plus lay disciples** (preaching, humanitarian work)
- **'Daridra narayana'** — **God manifest in the poor**; service to the poor is service to God
