# ROW 5:90 | Forest and wildlife legal framework - Indian Forest Act 1927, Forest (Conservation) Act 1980, Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972
## SEC 5:90:0 | Laws & policies in order
### Chronology
| Law / policy | Year |
|---|---|
| Indian Forest Act | **1927** |
| National Forest Policy | **1952** |
| Wildlife (Protection) Act | **1972** |
| Forest (Conservation) Act | **1980** |
- Order of enactment: **IFA 1927 → NFP 1952 → WPA 1972 → FCA 1980**
- **Indian Forest Act 1878** — introduced **reserved, protected and village** forests; first Act drafted **1865**; **1927** Act still in force
- ★ IFA 1927 degree of protection: **Reserved Forest > Protected Forest > Village Forest**
### What each law does
- **Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972** — protects wild animals, birds and plants; manages their habitats (APPSC 2025 key)
- **EPA 1986** — umbrella law for environmental protection and pollution control
- **Forest (Conservation) Act 1980** — regulates **diversion of forest land**, checks deforestation; IFA 1927 sets the forest legal framework
- **2023 amendment** renamed FCA 1980 as **Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam, 1980**
- FCA 2023 amendment exemptions include strategic projects, eco-tourism, zoos; criticised: silent on FRA, **no mandatory Gram Sabha consent** for new exemptions
- **Biological Diversity (Amendment) Act 2023** — **decriminalised** offences, introduced **civil penalties**; did not abolish benefit-sharing or exempt foreign entities from NBA approval
- **NGT** hears cases under seven laws (FCA, EPA, BD Act…) but **not WPA 1972** or FRA 2006
### Constitution
- **42nd Amendment (1976)** — forests to Concurrent List as **Entry 17A**; protection of wild animals and birds **Entry 17B**
- 42nd Amendment inserted **Article 48A** (DPSP) and **Article 51A(g)** (Fundamental Duty)
- State Bill amending WPA (e.g. Maharashtra, Section 12) needs **President's assent** — it amends a **central law**
### WPA amendments
- **2002** — **National Board for Wildlife** replaced Indian Board for Wildlife
- **2022** — brought in **CITES** provisions (management and scientific authorities)
### Award
- ★ **Amrita Devi Smriti Award** — protection of forests and **wildlife**; given by forest department of **Rajasthan**; cash award to individuals/institutions
## SEC 5:90:1 | IFA 1927 forest categories
- **Village forest** — a **reserved forest** (not protected) assigned to a village community
- **Section 26** — acts prohibited in a reserved forest; **trespass** punishable
- **Unclassed forests** — outside reserved and protected classes; **least regulated**
- **2017 amendment** — **bamboo** grown outside forests removed from definition of 'tree' (**Section 2(7)**); can be felled and transported **without transit permit**
- SC: **mining barred within 1 km** of every national park and sanctuary, unless larger eco-sensitive zone notified
## SEC 5:90:2 | Forest Policy 1952 vs 1988
### NFP 1952
- Target: **one-third** of land under forest — **60%** in hills, **25%** in plains
- Classes: **national, protected, village forests** + **tree-lands** (national parks not a class)
### NFP 1988
- Aims: **ecological balance/stability**, biodiversity conservation, social forestry, reforestation
- Promoted **wood substitutes** and **alternative fuels**
- Never promoted **pesticide** use
### Related years
- **NAPCC** launched **2008**; **2018** = Draft National Forest Policy

## SEC 5:90:5 | Van Adhiniyam (2023)
### FCA 1980 / Van Adhiniyam
- **Godavarman case** — 'forest' covers every area recorded as forest **irrespective of ownership**

- 2023: excludes land converted to non-forest use before **12 December 1996**
- Compensatory afforestation: equal-area non-forest land preferred; else **twice** the area of degraded forest land + NPV
### Other

- WPA **Section 2(37)** defines **wildlife**; Section 2(1) defines animal
- BD (Amendment) Act 2023 — benefit-sharing exemption for cultivated medicinal plants certified by **Biodiversity Management Committees**, and registered AYUSH practitioners
- Protected areas (2026): **1,75,169.42 sq km** ≈ **5.32%** of India's land area

# ROW 5:92 | Climate change - greenhouse effect, greenhouse gases, radiative forcing, evidence and observed impacts
## SEC 5:92:0 | How the greenhouse effect works
- Atmosphere — **transparent to incoming short-wave** radiation, relatively **opaque to outgoing long-wave** (infrared) radiation
- Without greenhouse effect Earth averages about **−18°C** (instead of about +15°C)
- **Argon** — inert gas, **not** a greenhouse gas
- ★ **CFCs** (carbon, fluorine, chlorine) — **are** responsible for the greenhouse effect; their **chlorine** destroys ozone
## SEC 5:92:1 | Natural vs enhanced
- Greenhouse effect first described by **Joseph Fourier**, **1820s**
- **WMO** — modern body reporting greenhouse gas levels
## SEC 5:92:2 | Gases: GWP, lifetime, source
### GWP and lifetime
- **CO2** — reference gas, 100-year **GWP = 1**
- **SF6** — highest GWP (about **23,900**)
- **Methane** — 100-year GWP about **28–36**; lifetime about **12 years**
### Sources
| Gas | Main anthropogenic source |
|---|---|
| **N2O** | **Fertilisers and biomass burning** |
| CO2 | Fossil fuels, deforestation, cement |
| Methane | Paddies, livestock, landfills |
| SF6 | Dielectric fluid |
- Largest GHG source — **fossil-fuel combustion** (coal, oil, gas) for energy, transport, industry; others: deforestation, industrial processes, agriculture, waste
### Not GHGs / traps
- **N2, O2, Ar** — not greenhouse gases; **carbon monoxide** — only **indirect** GHG
- **SO2** — causes **acid rain**, not a GHG; **CFCs** — both GHG and ozone-depleting
- **Stratospheric ozone** — **UV protection**; methane = marsh gas; N2O from nitrogen fertilisers; tropospheric ozone = smog pollutant
### Recent
- UN SG's **Call to Action on Methane** — **London Climate Action Week**, **24 June 2026** (waste, agriculture, fossil-fuel operations)
- EU **CBAM** — fully enforced from **1 January 2026**
## SEC 5:92:3 | GHG facts & traps
- Textbook shares of enhanced greenhouse effect: CO2 about **60%**, **methane 20%**, CFCs **15%**, N2O **5%**
- **Deforestation raises** atmospheric CO2 (less uptake, releases stored carbon)
- **CFCs** — entirely **man-made** (refrigerants, aerosols); water vapour, methane, N2O occur naturally
## SEC 5:92:4 | Climate terms
- **GWP** — heat absorbed by a gas over a period (usually **100 years**) relative to the **same mass of CO2**
- **Tipping points** — thresholds where small changes trigger self-perpetuating shifts **irreversible on human timescales**
- **Keeling curve** — continuous CO2 record at **Mauna Loa, Hawaii**, since **1958**, by **Charles David Keeling**
## SEC 5:92:5 | Data & impacts
### Concepts
- **Natural** greenhouse-gas levels stable for thousands of years; **fossil fuels** drive the **enhanced** effect
- **Water vapour** — most important GHG; humans raise it **indirectly** by warming air
- **Climate sensitivity** — warming after a **doubling** of CO2 over pre-industrial
- **Ice melt** — **positive feedback**: lower **albedo** → more heat absorbed → more melting
- **Tropospheric ozone** (NOx + VOCs in sunlight) — **secondary pollutant**; short-lived GHG; cuts crop yields
- **Black carbon** — incomplete combustion of **both biomass and diesel**; darkens snow and ice
- Deforestation removes **carbon sinks**; fertilisers add **N2O** (separate facts)
- **Ocean acidification** — oceans absorb CO2 → **carbonic acid** → lower pH
- **CCUS** — captures CO2 from **point sources** (flue gases); from ambient air = **direct air capture**
- **Carbon bomb** — fossil-fuel project able to emit over **1 Gt CO2**; **LINGO** identified **425**
### Data
| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| CO2 concentration 2024 | **423.9 ppm** (highest in about 2 million years; pre-industrial about 280 ppm) |
| CO2 rise in 2024 | **3.5 ppm** — largest since **1957** |
| India's CO2 emissions 2024 | about **3.2 billion t/year** (China 12, USA 4.9) |
| Ocean share of extra energy, 1971–2010 | **over 90%** |
| Arctic sea ice, 1979–2012 | **−3.5–4.1% per decade**; both ice sheets losing mass |
- **WMO GHG Bulletin (Oct 2025)** — **CO2** (not methane) drove about **81%** of the **54%** rise in warming effect, 1990–2024
- **2024** — first year global mean temperature exceeded **1.5°C** above pre-industrial
## SEC 5:92:6 | Pledges & projections
- **Global Methane Pledge** (COP26, Glasgow) — **30%** cut from 2020 levels by **2030**; **India not signed**
- **UNEP Emissions Gap Report 2025** — updated NDCs → about **2.3–2.5°C**
- **UNEP Limiting Overshoot** — current policies **2.6°C**; all pledges delivered 1.8°C
- India's **CCUS road map** — covers **industry**; agricultural removal via soil carbon, biochar, agroforestry
- India's country-level **social cost of carbon** — **$86 per tonne CO2**, highest globally
## SEC 5:92:7 | Exam corner
- Primary cause of present global warming — **rising greenhouse gas concentrations** (APPSC 2025 key); ozone depletion a separate problem
- **Short-lived climate pollutants** — black carbon, methane, HFCs, tropospheric ozone; **not water vapour**
- **Climate and Clean Air Coalition** — action against **short-lived climate pollutants**
- Black and brown carbon **warm**; sulphate droplets **cool**
- Global warming — **rising sea levels**; air pollution — respiratory illness; sub-regional variation — patches of cooling
- **Oil spillage** — **oxygen depletion** in water bodies; burning plastic reduces (not enhances) seed germination
## SEC 5:92:8 | Greenhouse effect → mechanism, natural vs enhanced
### Gases
- ★ **Greenhouse gases** trap the sun's heat and raise Earth's temperature; greenhouse effect = **increase in global temperature**
- ★ Main GHGs: CO2, methane, N2O, water vapour; **oxygen (O2)** — not one of the four principal human-driven GHGs
- ★ **Water vapour** — most important GHG; humans raise it **indirectly through temperature increases**
- ★ Warmer air holds **more water vapour** (humidity rises with temperature)
- ★ **Soot (black carbon)**, **sulphur hexafluoride**, **water vapour** — all warm the atmosphere; soot absorbs solar and infrared radiation
- ★ **UNFCCC** — **greenhouse gas emissions mitigation**; aim to stabilise GHG concentrations
### Heating of the atmosphere
- ★ Atmosphere mainly heated by **long-wave terrestrial radiation**
- ★ Insolation: about 35% reflected, 14% absorbed by atmosphere, **51%** absorbed by surface
- ★ Clouds and gases reflect about **one-fourth** of incoming solar radiation; Earth's surface does **not** re-emit heat as ultraviolet
- ★ **Cloudy nights warmer** — clouds prevent radiation of heat from the ground and reflect it back
- ★ Heat absorbed and trapped by the atmosphere **maintains average temperature**; 'below freezing everywhere without atmosphere' judged incorrect (average would be about −18°C)
## SEC 5:92:9 | Greenhouse gases → types, largest contributor and contribution order
- ★ Primary GHGs: **water vapour, CO2, methane, N2O, CFCs, ozone**
- ★ Most important set: **CO2, methane, nitrous oxide, water vapour**
- ★ Most responsible for global warming: **CO2 and methane**
- ★ Main component: **CO2** (about **66%**, WMO GHG Bulletin)
- ★ Contribution order: **CO2 > CH4 > N2O > CFCs**; water vapour about 60%, CO2 about 26%, CH4 4–9%, O3 3–7%, N2O about 1.5%
- ★ CO2, N2O and methane — all GHGs
- ★ Not GHGs: **nitrogen**, **propane** (propane use reduces GHG emissions)
- ★ **Oxides of sulphur and nitrogen** — do not contribute to warming directly
- ★ **Carbon monoxide** — the answer to "does not cause tropospheric ozone depletion"
### Methane sources
- ★ **Paddy fields** — methane, about **50–100 million t/year**
- ★ **Cud-chewing animals** — methane (**marsh gas**; also biogas, bacterial decomposition)
## SEC 5:92:10 | Greenhouse gases → GWP, lifetime, source
- ★ **Rice** — most important anthropogenic source of both **methane and N2O** (flooded paddies; burning straw and stubble)
## SEC 5:92:11 | Greenhouse gases → distractors and exam traps
- ★ **Deforestation increases** CO2 (releases stored carbon, fewer trees absorb CO2)
- ★ Not GHGs: **argon**, **hydrogen**, **oxygen**
- ★ **Nitrogen oxide** — the answer to "not a GHG found naturally"; naturally found: CO2, methane, ozone, water vapour
- ★ **Water vapour** — highest share, about **half** of Earth's greenhouse effect (NASA); most abundant GHG
- ★ Atmosphere heated more by **terrestrial** than by incoming solar radiation; **CO2 and GHGs** — good absorbers of **long-wave** radiation
## SEC 5:92:13 | Short-lived climate pollutants → source and lifetime
- ★ **Fresh snow** reflects the most sunlight — about **80–85%**
## SEC 5:92:14 | Carbon accounting and removal → terms
- ★ **Social cost of carbon** — monetary value of **long-term damage** from one tonne of CO2 emitted in a given year
- ★ **Carbon credit** — tradable permit to emit **one metric tonne** of CO2 or equivalent GHG
- ★ **Direct Air Capture (DAC)** — removes CO2 **directly from ambient air**; stored underground or converted into products
## SEC 5:92:15 | Emission sources → sector and top emitters
- ★ **Low-land rice** — main GHG **methane** (anaerobic conditions)
- ★ Methane sources: **rice fields, coal mining, domestic animals, wetlands** (all four)
- ★ Human sources: rice, coal mines, domestic animals; natural: **wetlands** (largest natural), sea hydrates
- ★ **Singapore** — **carbon tax** from **2019** (announced Feb 2018, under Paris commitments)
## SEC 5:92:17 | Observed impacts → effect
- ★ Global warming — **rising sea levels**; air pollution — **respiratory illnesses**; sub-regional temperature — **patches of cooling**
- ★ Beyond **3°C** — terrestrial biosphere may turn from carbon **sink to source**
- ★ Causes: **fossil-fuel burning**, oil-driven **automobiles**, **deforestation**; **solar flares** not a cause
- ★ Impacts: **melting glaciers, sea-level rise, weather changes, rising temperature** (all four)
## SEC 5:92:18 | Pledges, reports and projections → body and finding
- ★ **Hydrogen** — not a greenhouse gas

# ROW 5:93 | Ocean acidification - causes, effects on marine ecosystems and coral reefs
## SEC 5:93:0 | Chemistry of acidification
- Hydrogen ions bind carbonate ions → **less carbonate** left for shell formation
## SEC 5:93:1 | Key facts
- Ocean acidification — **'evil twin' of global warming** (another result of anthropogenic CO2)
- pH **8.2 → 8.1** ≈ **30%** rise in acidity; ocean still alkaline
- Worse in **cold/Arctic** waters — CO2 **more soluble in cold water**; melting sea ice exposes more water
## SEC 5:93:2 | Who gets hit
- **Pteropod** — '**sea butterfly**'; shells dissolve; food for fish
## SEC 5:93:3 | Related stressors
- **Marine heatwaves** — **doubled** since **1982**; cyclones over them up to **20%** stronger winds
- Marine heatwaves — **strongest global driver** of coral-cover decline
- **Dead zones** — oxygen used up by **decay of algal blooms** (from nutrient runoff)
## SEC 5:93:4 | Coral restoration
- **NGT (Feb 2026)**, Great Nicobar — MoEFCC to ensure **coral protection and regeneration** by a proved scientific method
- **Gulf of Mannar** — India's first **marine** biosphere reserve; 3D-printed reef modules nearby under **PMMSY**
## SEC 5:93:5 | Monitoring & missions
- **SAMUDRA app** coral bleaching alerts — **INCOIS** (not NCCR)
- **NCCR, Chennai** — reef health monitoring via **D-COIN**
- **Varaha** — deep-sea **mining system**, trialled at **5,270 m**
- **Matsya 6000** — Samudrayaan submersible
## SEC 5:93:6 | Exam corner
- ★ **Biorock technology** — **restoration of damaged coral reefs**; invented **1976** by **Wolf Hilbertz**
- Biorock = **mineral accretion**; moving colonies away = coral **translocation**, a different method
- **Lakshadweep** — entirely **atoll reefs**; giant **Pavona clavus** (potato patch coral) at **Kadmat**
## SEC 5:93:10 | Monitoring bodies, missions and targets → role
- ★ **Samudrayaan** — India's first **manned deep ocean mission**; **6,000 m**; submersible **MATSYA 6000**
## SEC 5:93:-1 | (whole topic)
