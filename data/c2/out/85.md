## SEC 4:31:0 | Continents & countries
### Continents by area
- ★ **Asia** — largest continent (44,568,500 sq km)
- Descending order: **Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe, Australia**

- ★ **Australia** — maximum per capita land (World Bank)
- ★ **Europe** — highest percentage of plains in its total area
### Countries
- Largest by area: **Russia, Canada, USA, China, Brazil, Australia, India** — **USA** is third

### Mountains, plains, Antarctica
- ★ **Andes** — longest mountain range (7,000–8,900 km), western (Pacific) coast of South America
  - Rockies (North America) — 4,800 km; **Alps** — highest and most extensive range of Europe
- **Great Plains** — east of the **Rockies**, in the USA and Canada
- ★ **Tungurahua Volcano** — **Ecuador** (Andes, near Baños); UNESCO Global Geopark on 10 April **2025**, with Napo Sumaco
- ★ **Canada**'s **RADARSAT-1** — first detailed, complete map of Antarctica (**1997**)
- ★ **Taylor Valley** — **Antarctica**; discovered by **Robert Falcon Scott** (1903), "Valley of the Dead"
## SEC 4:31:1 | Longest ranges

- **Drakensberg** — shared by South Africa and **Lesotho**

- **Gurushikhar** (1,722 m) — highest point of the **Aravallis**, India's oldest mountain system
- **Vinson Massif** — highest peak of **Antarctica**; Denali (North America), Elbrus (Europe), Kilimanjaro (Africa)
### Plateaus, plains, deserts

- **Thar** extends through India and Pakistan; **Great Victoria** is Australia's largest desert (unrelated facts)

# ROW 4:32 | World - major rivers, lakes, seas, straits, canals and islands
## SEC 4:32:0 | River records
### Asia and Africa
- ★ **Yangtze** — longest river of Asia (about **6,300 km**), Tibetan Plateau to the **East China Sea**; **Three Gorges Dam** (largest hydroelectric project)

- **Niger** — forms an **inland delta** in **Mali**

### Europe
- **Volga** (about 3,530 km) — longest river of Europe; Danube second

- ★ Danube capitals: **Vienna, Bratislava, Budapest, Belgrade**; **Rome** is on the **Tiber**
- **Rhine** (not the Danube) — Europe's busiest commercial inland waterway; Rhine and Elbe reach the **North Sea**
### Americas and others
- **Amazon** — rises in the Andes of Peru; through Peru, Colombia, Brazil into the **Atlantic**
- **Niagara Falls** — between Lakes **Erie** and **Ontario**
- **About 75%** of Andhra Pradesh lies in the Godavari, Krishna and Pennar basins
### Seas and straits
- Strait of **Hormuz**: Iran not party to UNCLOS, but transit passage binds it as **customary international law**
- **Red Sea** — between Africa and West Asia (not Europe); **Mediterranean** touches Europe, Africa and Asia
- **Black Sea** — between south-eastern Europe, the **Caucasus** and **Anatolia**
- **Shikoku** — smallest of Japan's four main islands; Hokkaido northernmost, Kyushu southernmost
