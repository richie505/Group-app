# ROW 1:195 | Social and religious reform in Andhra - Kandukuri Veeresalingam, Gurajada Apparao, Raghupathi Venkataratnam Naidu
## SEC 1:195:0 | Veeresalingam's milestones
### Titles
- ★ **M.G. Ranade** called Veeresalingam the "**Vidyasagar of South India**" (**1898**, when Veeresalingam presided over the Indian Social Reform Congress at Madras)
- **Gadya Tikkana** and **Andhra Vaitalikudu** — titles of Veeresalingam
- **Andhra Rammohan Roy** — Veeresalingam; father of the Andhra renaissance
- Other titles: **Andhra Kesari** — Tanguturi Prakasam; **Andhra Ratna** — Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya; **Kavi Raju** — Tripuraneni Ramaswamy Chowdary
- **Father of Andhra Drama** — Dharmavaram Krishnamacharyulu
### Schools, widow homes, institutions
- First girls' school in Andhra (**1874**) — **Dowlaiswaram**, near Rajahmundry (some keys: Rajahmundry); second girls' school at **Innispeta**, Rajahmundry (1881)
- Widow homes — **Madras (1897)** and **Rajahmundry (1905)**; Rajahmundry home called **Vitantu Saranalayam**
- Entire property (**Rs. 50,000**) donated to **Hitakarini Samajam**, which managed all his institutions
### Widow remarriage
- **3 August 1879**, Rajahmundry lecture — widow remarriage **valid according to Hindu scripture**; attacked, protected by his students
- First widow remarriage in Andhra — **11 December 1881**, **Ananda Udyana Mandapam**, Veeresalingam's house; groom **Gogulapati Sriramulu**, bride **Seethamma (Gauramma)**
- **Stree Punarvivaha Durvada Nirvaranam** (**1883**) — **Vedam Venkataraya Sastri**; first major anti-remarriage book
- **Paidah Ramakrishnayya** — supported remarriages at Kakinada; **Kasibhatta Brahmayya Sastri** — Aryananda Brundavani Sabha

## SEC 1:195:1 | Firsts of Andhra reform

- Reform began in **Rajahmundry**, led by the **English-educated middle class**

## SEC 1:195:2 | Reformers
### Raghupathi Venkataratnam Naidu
- ★ Title **Brahmarshi** (from the Brahmo Samaj)
- Joined Brahmo Samaj inspired by **Pandit Sivanath Sastri**
- **Samajika Shuddhi Samajam** — **1891**; led the **anti-nautch** campaign (Madras)
- Knighthood **1924** — first **non-official educator** knighted
- Brahmo movement declined after splits when he joined the **Justice Party**
### Brahmo Trinity and Bapayya
- Brahmo Trinity — Veeresalingam, Raghupathi Venkataratnam Naidu, **Desiraju Peda Bapaiah**
- **Desiraju Peda Bapayya** (Bapatla) — first practising (**Anushtanika**) Brahmo; discarded sacred thread; founded **Temperance and Social Purity Sangham**, Rajahmundry (not Veeresalingam)
- **Bhogam** = **nautch**
- Brahma Naidu — Dasa Samaj; Veeresalingam — first widow remarriage (1881)
### Gurajada Appa Rao
- **Mutyala Saralu** — introduced a **new metre**; **Kanyasulkam** — attacked bride-price; **Desamunu Preminchumanna** — patriotic song
- Kanyasulkam — written **1890**, first staged **13 August 1892** by **Jagannatha Vilasa Nataka Sangham, Vizianagaram**, published **1897** (Vavilla)
- **Diddubatu** — first modern **Telugu short story**
### Gidugu Venkata Ramamurthy
- With Gurajada, championed **vyavaharika** (spoken) Telugu
- Born **29 August 1863**, **Ponduru, Srikakulam**; 29 August = **Telugu Language Day**
- Savara studies: created a script, bilingual dictionaries, *A Manual of Savara Language* (not the Andhra Sahitya Parishad — classical side)
- **A Memorandum of Modern Telugu Prose** — Gidugu
- Cradle of the language movement — **North Coastal Andhra** (Vizianagaram, Srikakulam, Parlakimidi)
- Telugu classical language status — **2008**

# ROW 1:197 | Women's question in the 19th century - widow remarriage, age of consent, education, women's organisations
## SEC 1:197:0 | Social-reform Acts
### Early laws
- Bengal Regulations XXI of **1795** and III of **1804** — **infanticide** (incl. female) declared murder
- **Sati Regulation** (Regulation XVII of **1829**, Bentinck) — participation treated as **culpable homicide**
- **Lex Loci Act** = **Caste Disabilities Removal Act, 1850** — a convert could still **inherit ancestral property**
- **Hindu Widows' Remarriage Act** — bill prepared under Dalhousie; enacted **July 1856** under **Lord Canning**; legalised remarriage, legitimised children
- **Vidyasagar** campaigned against child marriage and **polygamy**
- **Female Infanticide Prevention Act** — **1870**, **Lord Mayo** (1802 = ban on child sacrifice at Sagar Island, Wellesley)
### Marriage and consent
- ★ **Native Marriage Act (1872)** — **Keshab Chandra Sen**; also called **Brahmo Marriage Act**; minimum age **14** girls, **18** boys; barred polygamy, allowed inter-caste marriage
- **1878** Brahmo Samaj split — Keshab married his minor daughter to the **Cooch Behar** prince, breaking the 1872 Act
- ★ **Age of Consent Act** — **1891**, by efforts of **Behramji M. Malabari** (Parsi, born 1853, Baroda); Lansdowne
- **Sarda Act** (Child Marriage Restraint) — moved by **Har Bilas Sarda**, under **Lord Irwin**
- Order: Caste Disabilities Removal (**1850**) → Widows' Remarriage (**1856**) → Age of Consent (**1891**) → Child Marriage Restraint (**1929**)
### Andhra
- **Kandukuri Rajyalakshmamma** (Veeresalingam's wife) — hosted widow and inter-caste marriages; sheltered widows and destitute women

## SEC 1:197:1 | Ages: consent & marriage
| Law | Age fixed |
|---|---|
