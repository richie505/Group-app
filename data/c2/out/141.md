## SEC 6:108:3 | AP inscriptions & festivals
### Sri Modakondamma Jatara
- Held every **May** at Modakondamma Temple, **Paderu** (Alluri Sitharama Raju district, Eastern Ghats)
- Grama Devatha revered by **Kondhs and Koyas** — a form of **Goddess Durga**
### Srisailam copper plates
- First use of **'Swarajya'** in a Telugu inscription ('Swarajya Punarudhharana'); three copper leaves
- Bilingual — **Sanskrit and Telugu**
- **Recharla Lingama Nayaka** (Recharla dynasty) — reclaimed areas from the Turakas, restored **eight agraharas**
### Vankeswara temple inscription (Durgi)
- **22 November 1518 CE**, reign of Sri Krishnadevaraya; sinking inscription at Durgi mandal, **Palnadu** district
- Commissioned by **Timmapa Nayanim Garu** (a doorkeeper in the royal court)
- Inspected by **E. Sivanagireddy**; tip given by Ch. Srinivasachary

## SEC 6:108:4 | Swadeshi idea

- **R.C. Dutt** — 'The Economic History of India' (deindustrialisation); **Naoroji** — Drain of Wealth
