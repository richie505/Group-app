## SEC 6:36:0 | GST 2.0 (56th Council)
### Council and structure
- **56th GST Council** (3 September 2025) — chaired by **Nirmala Sitharaman**; Revenue Secretary Arvind Shrivastava; Samrat Choudhary (Bihar), Suresh Khanna (UP)
- GST Council decides by **consensus**; voting rare
- Final decision on rate rationalisation rests with the **GST Council** (GoM accepted Centre's proposal)
- Slabs: **0%, 5%, 18%** plus **40%** demerit rate; **12% and 28% dropped**
- **40%** slab — sin goods: tobacco, cigarettes, online real-money gaming
- 28% → 40% shift reflects merger of **compensation cess**; not a real hike in every case
- Cigarettes — **28% GST plus cess**, effective burden **52–88%**
- Average effective GST rate expected to **fall** from ~11.5% to ~**10%**
- Economic implication — correction of **inverted duty structure**
### Rates from 22 September 2025
- **Nil GST** — individual health and life insurance, **reinsurance**, life-saving cancer drugs
- Non-ICU hospital rooms above ₹5,000/day — **5%** without ITC
- Revenue foregone (with income-tax reforms) — about **₹1 lakh crore**; PM called it "**GST Bachat Utsav**"
- States more affected — **Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu** (earlier rate cut → 3–4% monthly dip); Bihar, UP, North-East less affected
### Other
- "**Your Capital, Your Right**" — launched by **Nirmala Sitharaman**, 4 October 2025, Gandhinagar; partners RBI, SEBI, PFRDA
- "**Digital arrest**" frauds — impersonating **police or customs** officials
- Sitharaman on stablecoins — India must be **ready to engage** with them
- Draft Quality Control Orders — on WTO **TBT** (Technical Barriers to Trade) website; usually six months for compliance
- SEBI (August 2025) — **regulated venue** for pre-listing trading to replace the grey market
- **11% cotton import duty** — withdrawn until **30 September 2025**; introduced in Budget **2021** to protect farmers
- Cotton — about **six million** farmers; 2024-25 production 294, requirement 318 lakh bales; 2021: 350 and 335 lakh bales
- South Asian integration triggers 2025 — **Pahalgam** terror attack and US reciprocal tariffs; suggestions: revive SAARC summits, BBIN Motor Vehicle Agreement
