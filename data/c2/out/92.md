# ROW 4:60 | India - sex ratio and child sex ratio: trends, regional pattern, causes
## SEC 4:60:0 | Terms
- **Sex ratio** (India) — **females per 1,000 males**; UN convention: males per 100 females
- **Child sex ratio** — girls per 1,000 boys in the **0–6 years** age group
- **Sex ratio at birth** — girls born per 1,000 boys born; biological norm about **950** (more boys normally born)
- **'Missing women'** — concept by **Amartya Sen** (1990); demographic deficit of women, esp. in Asia

## SEC 4:60:6 | Causes
- Accepted causes of adverse sex ratio: high **maternal mortality**, high **female child mortality**, **female foeticide**; more male births
- **MMR** — **254** (2004–06) → **88** (2021–23)
- Supreme Court: gender bias **deep-seated** despite improvements; sustained by **son preference** and patriarchal norms

## SEC 4:60:7 | Acts & schemes
- **Beti Bachao Beti Padhao** — launched **22 January 2015** at **Panipat**, Haryana; to reverse falling child sex ratio
- BBBP improves status and protection of girls; does **not** permit prenatal sex determination
- **PCPNDT Act, 1994** — prenatal tests only for **genetic or chromosomal abnormalities**; bans sex selection and prenatal sex determination

# ROW 4:62 | India - occupational structure: main and marginal workers, work participation rate, cultivators and agricultural labourers
## SEC 4:62:0 | Census worker terms
- **Main worker** — worked **183 days** (six months) or more in the reference year
- **Census Town** — **5,000+** population, **75%+** of male main workers in non-agricultural work, density **400+** per sq km
- Feminisation of farm work — driven by **men's out-migration**; women hold only **12.8%** of operational holdings
- Non-worker categories: students, household duties, dependants, pensioners, rentiers, beggars/vagrants, others (no "permanent industrial employers")

## SEC 4:62:1 | PLFS indicators
- **PLFS** — conducted by **National Statistical Office**, **MoSPI**
- **UPSS** — employed if worked **30 days+** in the 365-day reference year
- **CWS** — employed if worked **one hour** on any day of the reference week
- **WPR** = employed persons ÷ total population × 100
- **U-curve** of female labour force participation — **Claudia Goldin**; falls then rises with development; India on declining half till 2018-19

## SEC 4:62:2 | India, Census 2011
- **Household industry worker** — production within the household (e.g. handloom)
- **Cultivator** — own or leased land; **agricultural labourer** — another's land for wages

| Category (India 2011) | Share of workers |
|---|---|
| **Other workers** | **41.6%** (largest) |
| Agricultural labourers | 30.0% |
| Cultivators | 24.6% |
| Household industry | 3.8% |

- 2011: **agricultural labourers exceeded cultivators** for the first time
- AP 2011: largest share **agricultural labourers 47.9%**; other workers 34.8%

## SEC 4:62:3 | Census 2011 facts
- Total workers **4,818.89 lakh**: main **75.2%**, marginal **24.8%**
- Agriculture's share of workers: **58.2%** (2001) → **54.6%** (2011); secondary and tertiary rose
- 2011–12: agriculture about **48.9%**, services about 27% of workers
- Rural **Scheduled Castes** — mostly **marginal farmers and landless labourers**
- WPR 2011: **39.8%** (male **53.3%**, female **25.5%**); about 60% non-workers
- WPR higher in less-developed, hill and tribal states — subsistence work needs more **manual labour**

## SEC 4:62:4 | State patterns
- High cultivator share: **Himachal Pradesh**, **Nagaland**
- High "other workers" share: Delhi, Chandigarh, Puducherry

## SEC 4:62:5 | PLFS & women
### Women in agriculture (Census 2011)
- Women — **55%** of agricultural labourers; **24%** of cultivators; **12.8%** of operational holdings
### PLFS figures
- Q2 FY 2025–26 (Jul–Sep 2025): agriculture **42.4%** of employment; self-employment 55.8%; casual labour 18.9%
- Annual PLFS 2025: **LFPR 59%**, **WPR 57%**, **UR about 3%**
- Jan–Mar 2026, CWS, 15+: **LFPR 55.5%**, **WPR 52.8%**, **UR 5.0%**
- Female LFPR (15+): **23.3%** (2017–18) → 30% (2019–20) → 37% (2022–23) → about 40% (2025)
### Reading the trend
- Reasons for rise in female work: subsistence farming, unpaid family work, economic distress, return migration, broader survey coverage
- Falling UR may reflect **distress entry into low-productivity work**, not decent jobs

## SEC 4:62:6 | Andhra Pradesh
- AP WPR 2011 ≈ **46.6%** (230.81 lakh workers ÷ 495.77 lakh population); above all-India
- Main agricultural labourers **86.12 lakh** of 193.17 lakh main workers — about **45%**
- PLFS 2023–24 (usual status, all ages): AP **LFPR 47.5%**, **WPR 45.6%**; India 45.1% and 43.7%
- AP female LFPR **35.8%** (India 31.7%); urban young women (15–29) WPR only **15.9%**
- Higher rural female WPR in AP — one reason **MGNREGS**
- Low female work participation in India: **mechanisation**, more education of girls, no flexible hours, distance, safety, family duties

## SEC 4:62:7 | Exam corner
- **PMEGP** — nodal agency **KVIC** (not NABARD)

# ROW 4:66 | Racial groups of India - Risley and Guha classifications
## SEC 4:66:0 | Indices & terms
### Cephalic index
- **Cephalic index** = maximum **breadth** ÷ maximum **length** × 100

| Cephalic index (living) | Head type |
|---|---|
| **82+** | **Brachycephalic** (round-headed) |
| 77–81.99 | Mesocephalic |
| 76.99 and below | Dolichocephalic (long-headed) |

| Nasal index | Nose type |
|---|---|
| **85+** | **Platyrrhine** (broad) |
| 70–84.9 | Mesorrhine |
| below 70 | Leptorrhine |

## SEC 4:66:1 | Risley's seven types

| Risley type | Region |
|---|---|

## SEC 4:66:2 | Guha's six groups
### Count
- **Risley** — **seven** types; **Guha** — **six** main groups (nine sub-types)
### Guha's groups in India

| Group | Associated with |
|---|---|
| **Negrito** | **Andaman** tribes; traces in southern hills; Kadar, Irula |
| **Proto-Australoid** | **Santhal** (standard example, Central India), Bhil, **Chenchu** (Nallamala), Munda, Ho |

| **Western Brachycephal** | **Parsis** (**Armenoid** sub-type) |
| Nordic | north-west frontier, Punjab |

- Mediterranean sub-types: **Palaeo-Mediterranean, Mediterranean, Oriental**; Oriental — Punjab, Rajasthan
- Andaman Negritos — sub-race of the Negroid group
- AP plains — mainly Mediterranean, predominantly **Dravidian-speaking**
### Hair and world groups
- **Leiotrichi** (straight, coarse hair) — **Mongoloid**; **ulotrichi** (woolly/frizzy) — Negroid
- **Mongoloid**: Chinese, Japanese, Koreans, Mongols, American Indians
- **Caucasoid**: Europe, North Africa, Middle East, parts of Central and South Asia; Europeans, Arabs, Persians, many North Indians
