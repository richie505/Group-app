# ROW 5:45 | Applications of space technology - agriculture, disaster management, telemedicine, tele-education, navigation
## SEC 5:45:0 | Remote sensing tools
### Sensing types
- **Active** sensing — transmits its own signal, e.g. **RISAT** radar; **passive** — natural radiation, e.g. **Resourcesat**, **Oceansat**
- **SAR / microwave** — sees **through cloud cover and in darkness**, hence **flood mapping**; **RISAT (SAR)** maps floods through clouds
- **InSAR** — compares the **phase** of two or more radar images of the same area → ground deformation
- **Soil moisture** — best measured by **microwave / SAR** sensors; **NISAR** uses **L-band** and **S-band** radar
### Indices
- **NDVI** = **(NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red)**; healthy vegetation reflects strongly in **near-infrared**
- **NDVI** — most widely used index for operational **drought** assessment
- **NH Green Cover Index 2025-26** — India's first satellite-based green-cover assessment of **national highways**; chlorophyll detection
### Satellites and uses
| Satellite / system | Use |
|---|---|
| **INSAT-3DS** | tracks **cyclones** |
| **Oceansat** | ocean-state data |
| **Cartosat** | damage mapping |
| **Resourcesat** | **crop acreage**, **watershed management**, **precision farming** |
- **Bhuvan** — combines RISAT/SAR and INSAT-3DS data for **flood-inundation maps** and **cyclone warnings**
- **GEMINI** — sends PFZ, Ocean State Forecast and disaster alerts to **fishermen**; soil-resource mapping is the **Soil and Land Use Survey of India**'s
- ★ **Assessing soil nutrient levels through chemical testing** — NOT a remote-sensing application in agriculture

# ROW 5:46 | Space debris, space situational awareness and international space law
## SEC 5:46:0 | Space debris
### Basics
- **Space debris** — all **non-functional man-made objects**, including fragments, in orbit or re-entering
- Main source — **fragmentation from collisions and explosions**
- **Mission Shakti** (**27 March 2019**) — DRDO **kinetic-kill ASAT** test; destroyed **Microsat-R** at about **300 km**
### India's response
- **Debris Free Space Missions (DFSM)** — all missions debris-free by **2030**; pledged at the **2024 IADC** meeting
- **POEM-4** — part of **SpaDeX/PSLV-C60**; fell into the Indian Ocean on **4 April 2025**; **inflatable sails** → re-entry within months, not decades
### Way forward and gaps
- Proposed — **more SSA data-sharing** (not less); confidence-building via **advance notices** and **hotlines**
- Space treaties (1960s–70s) do **not** adequately address **cumulative debris** harm or **mega-constellations**

## SEC 5:46:1 | Kessler Syndrome
- **MMOD** (micrometeoroids and orbital debris) — top risk in **low Earth orbit (LEO)**, not GEO

## SEC 5:46:2 | Indian SSA systems
- **Project NETRA** — indigenous space situational awareness; **safeguards Indian space assets** from debris and collisions
- **Digantara** (Bengaluru) — **space situational awareness** and **debris tracking**; builds space-surveillance satellites
| Outer Space Treaty Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| **Article VI** | **national responsibility**, incl. private actors |
| Article VII | liability |
| Article IX | due regard, environmental considerations |

## SEC 5:46:3 | ISSAR 2025
- **NETRA** — run by **ISRO**; **24–72 hours'** warning of close approaches
- **IS4OM** — compiles the annual **ISSAR**
- India's collision avoidance manoeuvres in **2025** — **18** (Cartosat, Resourcesat, GSAT)

## SEC 5:46:4 | Mitigation
- India's **2026 re-entry rules** — aircraft and ships in the re-entry area warned at least **45 days** before re-entry
- ISRO's planned **Active Debris Removal** mission — servicer craft with a **robotic arm or net** to capture a defunct Indian rocket body; tested at **URSC**
- **United States** — moratorium on destructive **direct-ascent ASAT** tests, **April 2022**; then taken up in a UNGA resolution

## SEC 5:46:5 | Space treaties
- **Outer Space Treaty (1967)** — bars national appropriation of space; India ratified in **1982**
- **Moon Agreement (1979)** — the only one India has **signed but not ratified**
- **Liability Convention (1972)** — launching State **absolutely** liable for damage on the **Earth's surface**; **fault-based** for damage in **outer space**; India is a party
- Legal gap — **no Space Activities Act** in India addressing **liability**

## SEC 5:46:6 | Bodies & accords
| Body / accord | Year | India |
|---|---|---|
| **UNCOPUOS** | **1959** | **founding member** |
| **IADC** | **1993** | ISRO a member; DFSM-2030 pledge at 2024 meeting |
| **Artemis Accords** | US launched **2020** (non-binding; 60+ signatories) | signed in **2023** |
- **PAROS** — long-running agenda item of the **UN Conference on Disarmament**; backed by UNGA resolutions
- International Space Summit, **Paris**, **10 September 2026** — PM Modi advocated **"cooperation over confrontation"**

## SEC 5:46:11 | Rules and mitigation → re-entry rules, DFSM 2030, POEM, ADR
- ★ **POEM-4** (PSLV Orbital Experimental Module) — re-entered and impacted the **Indian Ocean** on **4 April 2025**; controlled re-entry; from **PSLV-C60/SpaDeX**
- Governance gap — no international **duty-of-care standard** for orbits; differing licensing conditions push operators to permissive regimes
