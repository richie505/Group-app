# ROW 1:18 | Later Vedic period - eastward expansion, iron (krishna ayas), Painted Grey Ware culture
## SEC 1:18:0 | Eastward expansion
- Sequence: **Punjab → Ganga-Yamuna Doab** (Kurukshetra, Delhi-upper Doab) **→ Kosala, Kashi and Videha**
- Reached Kosala, Kashi, Videha by about **600 BCE**

## SEC 1:18:1 | Videgha Madhava legend
- Legend of **Videgha Madhava** — narrated in the **Shatapatha Brahmana**
  - Videgha Madhava and **Gautama Rahugana** followed **Agni Vaishvanara** eastward from the **Sarasvati**
- **Kurus** = union of the **Bharatas** and **Purus**
- **Kuru-Panchala** (capital **Hastinapur**) — centre of Aryan culture

| Upanishadic king | Kingdom |
|---|---|
| **Ashvapati** | **Kekaya** |
| Janaka | Videha |
| Ajatashatru | Kashi |
| Pravahana Jaivali | Panchala |

## SEC 1:18:3 | Iron: dates & sites
### North India
- Iron technology in North India — about **1000-800 BCE**; common at most **Painted Grey Ware** sites
- **Videgha Madhava** legend — clearing **Ganga-region forests** with **iron axes and fire**; shows iron's role in eastward expansion
### Tamil Nadu dates
- **Mayiladumparai** (**Krishnagiri** district) — iron AMS-dated to **2172 BCE**; earliest iron-use evidence in India
- **Sivagalai** (Thoothukudi) — iron dated to about **2953 BCE**
- Thirumalapuram (Tenkasi) — Iron Age urn chamber
- These dates push Indian iron use **far earlier than about 1000 BCE**; they say **nothing** about the origin of Painted Grey Ware

## SEC 1:18:4 | Pottery sequence
- Order (earliest first): **Ochre Coloured Pottery → Black and Red Ware → (Black Slipped Ware) → Painted Grey Ware → (Red Ware) → Northern Black Polished Ware**
- **NBPW** begins around **600 BCE**
- **Ochre Coloured Pottery** — post-Harappan, **second millennium BCE**
- Hallmark Later Vedic pottery — **Painted Grey Ware**
- **Second urbanisation** (true towns, after the Vedic period) — **NBPW** phase; **Shravasti, Kausambi, Hastinapur**
- **Iron Age in Andhra Pradesh** — linked with **Megalithic culture** and **Black and Red Ware** (e.g. **Nagarjunakonda**)

## SEC 1:18:5 | Painted Grey Ware
- Four Later Vedic wares: **Black and Red Ware, Black Slipped Ware, Painted Grey Ware, Red Ware**
  - **NBPW** is not in this group — belongs to the Mahajanapada / second-urbanisation phase
- **PGW** — **grey** pottery with **linear painted designs**; mostly **bowls and dishes**; c. **1000-600 BCE**
  - Highly polished black pottery = **NBPW**, not PGW
- PGW sites mainly in the **upper Ganga basin** (not the lower Indus basin)
- PGW sites yielded **iron weapons**, earthen bowls and dishes, copper objects
- PGW sites: **Atranjikhera, Hastinapur, Bhagwanpura**
- **Adichanallur** — **urn burials**; not a PGW site

## SEC 1:18:6 | Ratnins & taxes
### Vedas and priests
- **Atharvaveda** — spells and charms against disease
- **Samaveda** — Rigvedic hymns set to music
- **Yajurveda** — ritual procedures in prose; priest **Adhvaryu**; **Krishna** and **Shukla** branches
- **Adhvaryu** — **practical, manual work** of the sacrifice
- **Hotri** — recited hymns; **Upadhyaya** — taught; **Suta** — charioteer
### Crops
- Rice = **Vrihi** — chief crop with ritual importance; barley still grown
### Officials
| Official | Role |
|---|---|
| **Bhagadugha** | Revenue / tax collector (king's share) |
| **Sangrahitri** | **Treasurer** |
| Akshavapa | Accountant; superintendent of dice |
| Palagala | Messenger |
| Senani | Army chief |
| Purohita | Chief priest |
| Suta | Charioteer and herald |
### Taxes
- Compulsory taxes: **Bali** (tribute), **Bhaga** (king's share), **Shulka** (customs / tolls)
- **Bhaga** fixed at **one-sixth** of the produce
- **Vaishyas** — the tribute-payers

## SEC 1:18:7 | Farming, trade & gods
- Ploughs yoked with 6, 8, 12 and even **24 oxen**
- Settlements — mainly **villages** with **mud and wattle** houses; burnt bricks not prevalent
- True towns came **after** the Vedic period (**NBPW** phase)
- **Shresthins** — groups of **merchants**
- **Nishka** — unit of value; **barter** still the main form of exchange
- **Prajapati** became supreme; **Indra** and **Agni** lost prominence
- Worship centred on **elaborate rituals** — criticised by the **Upanishadic** thinkers

## SEC 1:18:8 | Exam corner
- **Ratnins** ("jewels") — king's council of **twelve** functionaries
- Ratnins honoured in the **Ratnahavimshi** rite of the **Rajasuya** (offerings at the Ratnins' houses)
- Early Vedic = tribe, cattle, **voluntary bali**; Later Vedic = **territory, agriculture, compulsory taxes**

## SEC 1:18:9 | Later Vedic texts → content
- **Aranyakas** — "forest texts"; **symbolic readings of ritual**
- **Upanishads** — Atman, Brahman, Karma, Moksha

## SEC 1:18:10 | Eastward expansion — legend, places and kings
- ★ **Kuru, Panchala** — axis of Aryan culture in the post-Vedic period (achievements in science, mathematics, astronomy, religion, philosophy)
- ★ King **Asvapati** — ruler of **Kekaya**
  - Kshatriya scholar-kings who taught Brahmins: **Janak** (Videha), **Pravahanjabali** (Panchal), **Asvapati** (Kekaya), **Ajatshatru** (Kashi)

## SEC 1:18:14 | Pottery — PGW sites and the protohistoric sequence — Later Vedic pottery

## SEC 1:18:15 | Pottery — PGW sites and the protohistoric sequence — PGW sites → region

# ROW 1:19 | Later Vedic polity - rise of territorial kingship; rajasuya, ashvamedha, vajapeya; the ratnins
## SEC 1:19:0 | From tribe to territory
- Shift to **hereditary territorial monarchy** prepared the way for the **Mahajanapadas** (**6th century BCE**)

## SEC 1:19:1 | Key terms
- **Rashtra** (territory) — appears for the first time in the Later Vedic political context
- **Aitareya Brahmana** state forms: **Rajya, Bhojya, Swarajya, Vairajya, Samrajya**
  - **Ganatantra** is not in the list

## SEC 1:19:2 | Early vs Later Vedic polity
- Kingship generally **hereditary**; succession usually to the **eldest son** (traces of election remained)
- **Vidatha** disappeared; **Sabha** and **Samiti** continued under chiefs and wealthy nobles
- **Ratnins** — Later Vedic polity
- Early Vedic polity: elective chief, voluntary bali, strong assemblies

## SEC 1:19:3 | Titles by region (Aitareya Br.)
| Region | Ruler's title |
|---|---|
| East | **Samrat** |
| South | Bhoja |
| West | Svarat |
| North | Virat |

## SEC 1:19:4 | Royal sacrifices
- **Vajapeya** — **chariot race** in which the king defeats his kinsmen
- **Rajasuya** — consecration
- **Ashvamedha** — horse sacrifice asserting supremacy

## SEC 1:19:5 | Divine origin & effect
- **Aitareya Brahmana** — gods, defeated by the **Asuras**, chose a **king** as war leader (military leadership + divine sanction)
- **Aitareya Brahmana** — king as **protector of Brahmins**
- **Shatapatha Brahmana** — linked the king with **Prajapati**; **Rajasuya** and **Vajapeya** gave the king **divine qualities**
  - Did not trace kingship to village election
- Large **Shrauta** sacrifices — needed **many priests** and heavy **dakshina** → strengthened Brahmanas

## SEC 1:19:6 | Ratnins, taxes & assemblies
- **Shulka** — customs duty or toll
- **Bali** — regular tribute; **Bhaga** — king's share of produce
- **Bhagadugha** — collected revenue; **Sangrahitri** — treasurer who managed it
- **Vaishyas** — main tribute-payers

## SEC 1:19:7 | Kingdoms
| Kingdom | Associated with |
|---|---|
| **Kuru** | Delhi–Meerut / upper Doab; **Hastinapur** and **Kurukshetra** |
| Panchala | Ahichchhatra |
| Kashi | Varanasi |
| **Videha** | **North Bihar**, beyond the **Sadanira** (Gandak) |
- King **Janaka** — **Videha**
- **Kuru–Panchala** dominated the upper Ganga valley; **Kosala, Kashi, Videha** rose in the east → emergence of the **Mahajanapadas**

## SEC 1:19:8 | Exam corner
- **Ashvamedha altar** found at **Nagarjunakonda**; early Andhra dynasties performed such rituals to proclaim sovereignty

# ROW 1:20 | Later Vedic society - four varnas, ashrama system, gotra, decline in the status of women
## SEC 1:20:0 | Four varnas
- **Aitareya Brahmana** — Shudra = "**a servant of another**"; could be expelled or struck at will
- **Vaishyas** — **farmers, traders, artisans**; paid tribute
- **Udgatri** — chanted the **Samaveda**
- **Hotri** — recited the Rigveda; **Adhvaryu** — performed sacrificial acts

## SEC 1:20:1 | Varna & caste
### Varna
- ★ First reference to the four varnas — **Purushasukta**, **Rigveda, 10th Mandala**
- Varna increasingly based on **birth**; **gotra exogamy** became important
- **Dvija** (twice-born) — **Brahmanas, Kshatriyas, Vaishyas**; entitled to **Upanayana**
- **Upanayana** — sacred-thread initiation into Vedic study for the upper three varnas; **Shudras and women excluded**
- **Vratyas** and **Nishadas** — outside the formal four-varna order
- Proliferation of **jatis** — **post-Vedic** (incl. Gupta-era diversification)
- Formal **untouchability** — **post-Vedic Dharmasutras** (Later Vedic society discriminated against Shudras and Chandalas)
- First reference to **money-lending** — **Shatapatha Brahmana**
### Ashramas and women
- Later Vedic ashramas: **Brahmacharya, Grihastha, Vanaprastha**; **Sannyasa** added later
- First three ashramas first referred to in the **Chhandogya Upanishad**; all four first listed in the **Jabala Upanishad**
- Women lost the right of **Upanayana**; restricted education; excluded from religious and public activities
### Satavahana Andhra
- **Anuloma** offspring — **Ugra**; **pratiloma** offspring — **Chandala**
- **Gautamiputra Satakarni** — claimed to protect **varnashrama dharma**

## SEC 1:20:2 | Four ashramas
| Ashrama | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Brahmacharya | Vedic study |
| **Grihastha** | Household life; daily **Pancha Mahayajnas** |
| **Vanaprastha** | **Gradual withdrawal** from worldly life |
| Sannyasa | Complete detachment |
- **Varnashrama Dharma** = varna + **ashrama** system

## SEC 1:20:3 | Gotra, samskaras, purusharthas
- **Gotra** literally = **cow-pen**; as descent from a common male ancestor, established in Later Vedic times
- **Irawati Karve** — *Kinship Organization in India* (**1953**); sapinda and gotra rules; **four kinship zones**
- **Trivarga** — **Dharma, Artha, Kama** (Later Vedic-Sutra phase); **Moksha** added later (Upanishadic influence)
- **Artha** — wealth; Dharma — duty; Kama — desire; Moksha — liberation
- Samskaras (shodasha) — **16**
- Declining status of women — references to **child marriage** and **sati**

## SEC 1:20:4 | Upanayana metres
- ★ **Sarthavaha** — **Vaishya**; head of trade guilds (also **Srenipramukh**); rules called Samay and Srenidharma

| Varna | Upanayana metre |
|---|---|
| Brahmana | Gayatri |
| **Kshatriya** | **Trishtubh** |
| Vaishya | Jagati |

## SEC 1:20:5 | Marriage forms (8)
- **Arsha** — groom gives bride's father a **token cow and bull**
- **Asura** — **bride price** / marriage by purchase
- **Rakshasa** — **capture or abduction**
- **Gandharva** — **mutual consent**
- **Paishacha** — seduction of a sleeping or intoxicated girl; the **lowest** form

## SEC 1:20:6 | Women: texts
- Elaborate description of **Upanayana** — **Shatapatha Brahmana**
- **Gargi**'s debate with **Yajnavalkya** — **Brihadaranyaka Upanishad**

## SEC 1:20:7 | Exam corner
- **Kula, Vamsha, Gotra** — kinship terms; **Kosa** = treasury (odd one out)
- **Gotra exogamy** — no marriage **within the same gotra**; alongside caste endogamy and sapinda rules
- **Bhuta-yajna** — towards **living beings**; hospitality to guests = **Nri-yajna / Manushya-yajna**
- **Acharya** — initiated the pupil (upanayana); taught the whole Veda **free**
- **Upadhyaya** — taught the Veda or Vedangas **for a livelihood**

## SEC 1:20:8 | Varnas → role; groups outside the varna order — The fourfold order
- ★ Four varnas: **Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya, Shudra**
- ★ **Grihastha** Ashram — **Pancha Mahayajnas**: **Brahma, Deva, Pitra, Bhuta, Atithi** Yajna
- **Brahmanas** — priestly duties; Kshatriyas — warriors and rulers; Vaishyas — farming and trade; Shudras — manual work

## SEC 1:20:9 | Varnas → role; groups outside the varna order — Rigidity and emergence of caste
- ★ Untouchability took definite shape in the **Dharmashastra** phase (early Christian era)

## SEC 1:20:10 | Gotra and kinship terms → meaning
- ★ **Kosa** — not a kinship term; relates to the **treasury**
- ★ **Kula** — family; **Vamsha** — lineage / descent; **Gotra** — clan from a common ancestor, linked with exogamy

## SEC 1:20:12 | Samskaras and upanayana → purpose
- ★ Kshatriya **Upanayana** — **Trishtubh Mantra** (martial and heroic qualities)

## SEC 1:20:14 | Teachers and priests → terms
- **Gurukula** — pupil **lived with the guru**
- Education largely confined to the **Dvija** communities

# ROW 1:21 | Vedic literature - four Vedas, Brahmanas, Aranyakas, Upanishads, Vedangas, Sutras
## SEC 1:21:0 | Four Vedas
### Veda, priest, content
| Veda | Priest | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Rigveda | Hotri | Hymns |
| Yajurveda | Adhvaryu | Sacrificial formulas |
| **Samaveda** | **Udgatri** | Verses chanted at ceremonies |
| **Atharvaveda** | Brahman | **Spells and charms** |
### Samaveda
- ★ **Samaveda** — compiled on the basis of the **Rigveda**; shortest Veda; all verses meant to be chanted
- Borrows almost all verses from the Rigveda — about **1,549** verses, all but about **75** from the Rigveda
### Atharvaveda
- ★ **Ayurveda** ("Science of Life") — first in the **Atharvaveda**; an Upaveda of the Atharvaveda
- **Kshetra-patni** ("mistress of the field") — **Atharvaveda**
- **Atharvaveda** — the **latest** of the four Vedas; **Rigveda** the oldest
- Atharvaveda recensions — **Paippalada** and **Shaunaka**
### Yajurveda
- **Shukla (White)** Yajurveda — **mantras only**; **Krishna (Black)** — mantras mixed with prose
- Recensions: **Madhyandina, Kanva** — Shukla Yajurveda; **Taittiriya, Kathaka** — Krishna Yajurveda
### Other
- **Shilpa Veda** (Upaveda) — **architecture**, not astronomy
- **Trayi** — first three Vedas (**Rigveda, Samaveda, Yajurveda**); Atharvaveda excluded
- **Veda Vyasa** (**Krishna Dvaipayana**) — **classified / compiled the Vedas**
- **Vedanta** — "**end of the Vedas**" (Veda + anta); the Upanishads close Vedic literature

## SEC 1:21:1 | Rigveda: mandalas
- ★ **Ninth Mandala** — wholly **Soma** (114 hymns); Rigveda has **10 Mandals**
- Mandala VI — **Bharadvaja**; VII — **Vasishtha**; X — **Purusha Sukta**
- **Gayatri Mantra** — sage **Vishvamitra**, deity **Savitri**; in the **Third Mandala**
- Seventh Mandala — **Battle of Ten Kings**; Ninth — Soma Pavamana hymns
- **Vaisheshika** — **Kanada**; Nyaya — Gautama; Sankhya — Kapila; Purva Mimamsa — Jaimini
- **Mahabharata** — also **Jaya Samhita** and **Satasahasri Samhita**; **Harivamsa** appended
- **Prasthanatrayi** — Upanishads, Brahma Sutra, **Bhagavad Gita**; foundation of Vedanta

## SEC 1:21:2 | Layers of Vedic texts
- ★ Order: **Samhitas → Brahmanas → Aranyakas → Upanishads**
  - ★ Samhitas + Brahmanas = **Karma-Kanda**; Aranyakas + Upanishads = **Gyan-Kanda**
- All Vedic texts — **Shruti** ("heard"); **apaurusheya**
- **Brahmanas** — **prose ritual commentaries**; **karma-marga**
- **Aranyakas** — **forest texts**, concluding parts of the Brahmanas; bridge **karmamarga → jnanamarga**
  - Not domestic law books

## SEC 1:21:3 | Brahmanas & Upanishads
- **Gopatha** Brahmana — **Atharvaveda**
- **Jaiminiya** Brahmana — **Samaveda**
- **Mundaka** Upanishad — **Atharvaveda**

## SEC 1:21:4 | Vedangas & Sutras
- **Upanishads** = **Vedanta**; tradition counts **108**, about **13** principal
- Central teaching — **identity of Atman and Brahman**; also karma, samsara, moksha; anti-ritualistic

- **Ramayana** — about **24,000 verses**; composed in **five stages** from the **5th century BCE**

# ROW 1:22 | Vedic religion, ritual and philosophy; the six orthodox schools (shad-darshanas)
## SEC 1:22:0 | Rigvedic gods
