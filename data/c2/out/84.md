# ROW 4:28 | Disaster management framework - hazard vs disaster, DM Act 2005, NDMA, early warning systems
## SEC 4:28:0 | Key terms
### Hazard, disaster, mitigation
- **Hazard** — a potentially damaging event (e.g. an earthquake in an uninhabited desert with no damage)
  - becomes a **disaster** when it interacts with a vulnerable population or exposed assets
- **Section 2(d)**, DM Act 2005 — disaster causes loss beyond the coping capacity of the **affected community** (not the State Government)
- **Mitigation** (DM Act) — measures to reduce the **risk, impact or effects** of a disaster or threatening disaster situation
- **Mitigation Funds** (National and State) — for **long-term reduction** of disaster risk, not immediate relief alone
### Vulnerability types
| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| **Environmental** | **mangrove loss** (raises cyclone impact), deforestation |
| Physical | weak houses |
| Social | caste and gender discrimination |
| Economic | poverty |
| **Systemic / institutional** | weak governance, poor coordination, lack of preparedness |
### Phases of the cycle
- **Prevention** — proactive, long-term; e.g. **regulating deforestation**
- **CAP-SACHET** — **early warning dissemination**; a preparedness measure (like mock drills, stockpiling)
- **Response** — **time-critical**; includes restoring critical services (long-term activity = recovery)
- **Recovery and reconstruction** — **compensation** and **psychosocial care**
### Laws, policy and plans
- **2009 National Policy on DM** — prevention, mitigation, community participation, mainstreaming DRR (not exclusive reliance on post-disaster relief)
- **Section 35** — hazard-specific plans of Union ministries; **Section 37** — Ministry DM Plans; **Section 25** — DDMA
- **DM (Amendment) Act, 2025** — Urban DM Authority (UDMA) headed by the **Municipal Commissioner**
  - UDMAs cover Municipal Corporation cities except **Delhi and Chandigarh**
- **Landslide Hazard Zonation** — homogeneous zones by **degree of susceptibility, hazard or risk**; maps are probabilistic, not certain predictions
### Andhra Pradesh
- **State Emergency Operations Centre** — run by **APSDMA** (coordinates districts, early warnings, restoration)
- Cyclone **Michaung** (December 2023) — landfall near **Bapatla**
- Cyclone **Hudhud** — **Visakhapatnam**, October 2014

- Chaired by **J. C. Pant** (its first chairman)

- Original mandate — **natural disasters only**; later widened to man-made disasters

- **Sendai Framework** — **2015–2030**; succeeded the **Hyogo Framework** (2005–2015)
- **Uttarakhand** — first State with a separate **Ministry of Disaster Management**
### Cabinet committees
- **Cabinet Committee on Security** — **nuclear, biological and chemical** emergencies
- **Cabinet Committee on Natural Calamities** — takes **policy decisions**; expert advice comes from the National Advisory Committee
### Agencies and tools
| Agency | HQ / role |
|---|---|
| **Central Water Commission** | flood forecasting; HQ **New Delhi** (not Hyderabad) |
| IMD | New Delhi |
| NRSC | Hyderabad |

- **GIS** in floods — overlays spatial data to analyse **flood patterns with topography** for proactive action (an analysis tool: needs rainfall data, builds nothing, pays nothing)
- Community vulnerability factors — kutcha housing, weak health infrastructure, weak early warning; **high crop-insurance coverage** reduces vulnerability

## SEC 4:28:3 | DM Act 2005 sections
### The Act
- Enacted after gaps exposed by the **2004 Indian Ocean Tsunami**; extends to the whole of India
- **11 Chapters** and **79 Sections**
- **Primary responsibility** for DM — **State Governments**; Centre (MHA) supports and coordinates
### Sections
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| 14 | SDMA |
| 25 | DDMA |
| **42** | **NIDM** (statutory institute) |
| **44** | **National Disaster Response Force** |
| **46** | **National Disaster Response Fund** |
| **47** | **National Disaster Mitigation Fund** |
| 48 | State Disaster Response Fund |
### Funds
- **National Disaster Response Fund** — Central fund that **tops up the SDRF** in severe calamities; not the first fund for local disasters
- **Mitigation Funds** — for mitigation, not post-disaster relief (relief = response funds)
  - operationalised after the **15th Finance Commission**
- **State-specific disasters** — up to **10%** of the SDRF (not 25%)
### Forces and institutions
- **NDRF** — raised in **2006** under Section 44 (2005 = year of the Act)
- **SDRFs** not yet set up in all states — strains NDRF in simultaneous disasters
- **SDRF** personnel — State Police, Armed Police, Fire Services, Home Guards, Civil Defence; NDRF draws on central forces (BSF, CRPF)
- SDRF, like NDRF, on a **battalion** model with multi-disciplinary teams
- **NIDM** — began as **NCDM** in 1995; **Southern Campus** near **Vijayawada** (not Visakhapatnam)
- **IDRN** (online inventory of response resources) — under **NIDM/MHA**
- **National Advisory Committee** — expert advice, works under the **NDMA**
### Mitigation and AP
- **Retrofitting** vulnerable buildings — **pre-disaster** mitigation (damage assessment, compensation, psychosocial care are post-disaster)
- **Coastal tree planting** — mitigation against **cyclones**; awareness and education = non-structural mitigation
- Urban response — shared by civic bodies, **fire brigade** and police, district administration in overall charge (not the fire brigade alone)
- AP shift — from **relief-centric** to **mitigation-centric**; a national leader in DRR
- **Vajrapath, Varuna, Red Message** — alert systems run by **APSDMA**

## SEC 4:28:4 | NDRF force
- Specialist force drawn from **central paramilitary forces**; search-and-rescue teams, engineers, **dog squads**; under NDMA superintendence
- Parent forces — BSF, CRPF, CISF, **ITBP**, SSB, Assam Rifles (on deputation)
- **16 battalions**, expanded from an initial **8**
- Regional HQ for AP, Telangana, Karnataka — **Vijayawada** (**10th Battalion**); academy **Nagpur**; HQ New Delhi
- **Raising Day** — **19 January**; motto "**Aapada Seva Sadaiv Sarvatra**" (Disaster Service Always Everywhere)
- Units placed by India's **vulnerability profile**, with regional response centres
- **NCMC** — chaired by the **Cabinet Secretary**
- **NDMA** formally constituted **27 September 2006**

## SEC 4:28:6 | DM cycle
### Stages
- **Pre-disaster** — prevention, **mitigation**, preparedness; **response** during; **recovery** post-disaster
- Cycle is **continuous** — lessons from recovery feed better prevention
- Post-2005 **3 Ps** — **Prevention, Preparedness, Proofing (mitigation)**; old **3 Rs** — rescue, relief, rehabilitation
- **NPDM** — framed under the **DM Act, 2005**; moved from relief to prevention and preparedness
### Phase by measure
| Phase | Measures |
|---|---|
| **Prevention** | **land-use zoning**, control of **industrial siting**; GIS hazard zonation |
| **Mitigation** | quake-proof buildings, hazard maps; **most cost-effective** phase |
| **Preparedness** | **Emergency Operation Centres**, **contingency plans**, GIS **evacuation route planning**; called the **first critical step** |
| Response | immediate action; GIS real-time damage assessment |
| **Recovery** | restore normal life, reduce future vulnerability; **building back better** |
- Post-disaster sequence — **relief → restoration → reconstruction → resilient rebuilding**
- Preparedness tools — mock drills, **Aapda Mitra** volunteers, tsunami escape-route signage
- AP cyclone risk reduction — IMD warnings, cyclone shelters, evacuation, **shelter belts**, **mangroves**

# ROW 4:30 | Continental resources - land, forest, water, mineral and energy resources; conservation
## SEC 4:30:0 | Resource classification
- **Minerals** — **abiotic** resources; fossil fuels non-renewable; forests and fish renewable
- **Stock** — exists but no suitable technology to use it yet (e.g. **hydrogen from water**)
- **Nine-fold land-use classification** — forests, non-agricultural uses, barren and unculturable land, permanent pastures, miscellaneous tree crops, culturable wasteland, current fallow, other fallow, net sown area
  - "**Seasonal plantation reserve**" is not a category
- **Wasteland** — degraded land restorable to vegetative cover with reasonable effort; mapped by satellite-based **National Wastelands Monitoring**
- Land degradation causes — soil erosion, waterlogging, salinisation, nutrient depletion, falling water tables, soil pollution (not natural nutrient increase)
- **Neeranchal** — **World Bank**-assisted national watershed project; **Watershed Development Fund** — set up by **NABARD**
- India — first country to sign an ISA contract for **polymetallic sulphides**, on the **Carlsberg Ridge**, Arabian Sea

| Category | Uncultivated for |
|---|---|
| Current fallow | up to **1 year** |
