# ROW 4:80 | World agriculture - types of farming, major crop belts and agricultural regions
## SEC 4:80:0 | Subsistence types
### Nomadic herding and ranching
- ★ **Nomadic herding** — moving with animals for pasture; mainly in **dry (arid) areas**, e.g. Eurasian steppes
- Found in dry or cold lands; **not** in the **Amazon and Congo** basins (those have shifting cultivation)
- **Livestock ranching** — **large-scale commercial** rearing (nomadic herding = seasonal migration for fodder and water)
### Intensive and shifting farming
- **Intensive farming** — high labour and capital per unit land; **extensive** — large areas, low input
- **Oriental intensive farming** — **Monsoon Asia**; wet paddy; high output per hectare, low output per worker
- Intensive subsistence farming — **family labour**, small holdings, high yield per hectare
  - **Rice** dominates in wet areas; wheat in drier parts
- **Shifting cultivation** — primitive **subsistence** farming (not capital-intensive or export-oriented)
### Plantation
- **Cocoa** — **West Africa** (Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana), not South Asia

## SEC 4:80:1 | Commercial types
### Plantation
- ★ Plantation crops: **cocoa — Ghana**, **coffee — Brazil**, **tea — Sri Lanka**, **rubber — Malaysia** (not sugarcane)
- Plantation agriculture — export-oriented monoculture, a product of **colonialism**
### Grain, mixed and dairy
- **Commercial grain** regions: **Prairies, Pampas, Steppes** (not the Mediterranean Basin)
  - High commodity (marketed) rate — due to **large mechanised farms** and low local demand; wheat dominance true but not the reason
- **Mixed farming** — **crops and livestock on the same farm**; highly mechanised
  - Regions: **north-west Europe**, **eastern North America**/eastern USA, Argentina (not Monsoon Asia)
- **Dairy farming** — needs **refrigeration and transport**
  - Regions: NW Europe, **Denmark**, **Netherlands**, **New Zealand**, US Midwest, SE Australia
### Mediterranean
- **Mediterranean** regions — **winter rain**; viticulture, citrus, olives
- Also in **south-western Australia**
### Other
- **Mocha** coffee — Arabica, named after port of **Mocha, Yemen**; Arabica originated in **Ethiopia**
- India's Green Revolution core: **Punjab, Haryana, western UP**; key figures **M.S. Swaminathan**, **C. Subramaniam**

# ROW 4:81 | India - agriculture: major food crops (rice, wheat, maize, pulses) and their regions
## SEC 4:81:0 | Cropping seasons
### Seasons
- **Zaid** — hot summer between rabi and kharif: **watermelon**, **bitter gourd**, **cucumber** (not rabi)
- **Kharif**: rice, soybean; **moong** (kharif pulse), cotton (kharif fibre)
- **Rabi**: **gram** (rabi pulse), peas, linseed, mustard, lathyrus
- **PMFBY** from Kharif 2026 — covers **wild-animal attacks** and **paddy inundation**
### Cropping systems and regions
- Indo-Gangetic Plains — dominant system **rice–wheat** (paddy then wheat; double cropping)
- **Parallel cropping** — two crops of different habit/duration without competition, e.g. **maize + green gram**
- **Trans-Gangetic Plains** (Punjab, Haryana, Delhi) — wheat, rice; **highest productivity**
  - Upper Gangetic Plains — core Green Revolution belt; Lower Gangetic — rice and jute
- ★ **Rice** — **transplanted** crop (less seed, more labour; weed control in puddled fields)
### Millets
- **Major millets**: bajra, jowar, **ragi** (finger millet)
- **Minor millets**: foxtail, little, **kodo** (kodon), proso
### Varieties and hybrids

## SEC 4:81:1 | Crops: conditions & states
### Crop types
- ★ **Stimulant crops**: **tea**, coffee, tobacco, opium poppy, datura, hemp, betel nut
- ★ **Cash crops**: sugarcane, cotton, jute, tobacco, oilseeds, groundnut — **not** jowar or **wheat** (food crops)
- **Pulses** used in rotation — **fix atmospheric nitrogen** (leguminous)
### Growing conditions
- ★ Sugarcane: 21–27°C, 75–100 cm rain; **frost during ripening** lowers **sucrose**
- **Wheat** — rabi; cool growing season, **50–75 cm** rain, warm sunny ripening; not the hot zaid summer
- **Rice** — **~25°C**, rain above **100 cm**, high humidity; **clayey/loamy** deltaic and coastal alluvium
- **Bajra** — hot, dry conditions, **sandy soils**; led by **Rajasthan**
### Leading States
- Wheat production order: **UP > MP > Punjab > Haryana**; chief wheat States UP, Punjab, Haryana, MP
- **Rice Bowl of India**: **West Bengal** — largest rice producer; also UP, Punjab, AP
- Maize: **Karnataka** and **Madhya Pradesh**; rising in AP's Godavari–Krishna districts
- **Ragi** — Karnataka (peninsular India); **jowar** — Maharashtra and Karnataka
### Policy
- First phase of Green Revolution (mid-1960s–1980s) centred on **wheat**; rice in later phase
- **MSP and free power** favour rice–wheat, including paddy in Punjab

## SEC 4:81:3 | Green Revolution & missions
### Green Revolution
- ★ Started **1966-67**; first stage: **Haryana, Punjab, western UP**
- ★ Greatest impact — **wheat** (production and productivity), then rice
- **New Agricultural Strategy** — **1965**; HYV seeds, fertilisers, irrigation, MSP; aim foodgrain self-sufficiency
- **Third phase** of agricultural development — price policy on **MSP and procurement**
- Limitations: **unequal gains** (big landowners benefited most), groundwater depletion, salinity, crop and regional imbalance
- Core Green Revolution belt: **Upper Gangetic Plains** (esp. western UP)
### Missions
- **BGREI** — **2010-11** under **RKVY**; rice-based cropping system of eastern States
- **NFSM** — **2007-08** (rice, wheat, pulses); nutri-cereals added **2018-19**
- Renamed **NFSNM** in **2024-25**
### Millets and dryland
- **Bajra** (pearl millet) — ~**60%** of India's millet output; India **42.75%** of world millets
- **Dryland farming** — rainfall below **750 mm**, no assured irrigation
