# ROW 2:88 | E-governance - models, types of interaction, National e-Governance Plan, Digital India and governance outcomes
## SEC 2:88:0 | Halachmi's five models
- **Arie Halachmi** — five models of e-governance: broadcasting, critical flow, comparative analysis, e-advocacy, interactive service
- **Critical flow** — critical information channelled to a targeted audience (e.g. corruption data to an **ombudsman**)
- **Comparative analysis** — learning from best practices elsewhere
- **E-advocacy** — online civil-society networks influencing decisions
## SEC 2:88:1 | Interaction types
| Type | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **G2C** | Government to Citizen | online birth, **income**, caste certificates (UMANG, MeeSeva) |
| **G2B** | Government to Business | MCA portal, **GSTN**, **e-Procurement** |
| **G2E** | Government to its **employees** | services to employees |
| **G2G** | between **governments** | inter-government interaction |
## SEC 2:88:2 | National platforms
### Central platforms
- **UMANG** — Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance
- **UMANG** — Central and State services (tax, EPF, gas, passport) via web, **SMS** and **IVR**
- **MyGov** — citizen-engagement platform for **participative governance**
- **ERSS** (Emergency Response Support System) — linked to single emergency number **112**
- **CSCs** (Common Service Centres) — village-level service outlets (unrelated to ERSS–112 link)
- **Aadhaar** — launched **2009**; Aadhaar Act **2016** (amended 2019)
- **NAeG** (National Awards for e-Governance) — began **2003**, instituted by **DARPG**; presented at National Conference on e-Governance
### AP platforms
- **RTGS** (Real Time Governance Society) — set up **2017**; Data Lake and real-time dashboards
- **e-Pragati** — unified state service portal and **state enterprise architecture**; faceless, paperless, cashless governance
## SEC 2:88:3 | AP initiatives
| Initiative | Year | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **eSeva** | **2001** | integrated citizen service centres (bills, certificates) |
| **e-Procurement** | **2003** | online tendering; stop **collusion** |

## SEC 2:88:4 | e-Governance conferences
- **28th NCeG** — **Visakhapatnam Declaration**: extend **NeSDA** framework to low-connectivity areas (Ladakh, North-East)
- **29th NCeG** — **Jaipur Declaration**: **DPI**, AI-enabled governance, cybersecurity, data governance
## SEC 2:88:5 | National Awards (NAeG)
- **2025 NAeG** (with Ministry of Panchayati Raj) — first-ever dedicated category for **Gram Panchayats**
## SEC 2:88:6 | Exam corner
- Significance of e-governance — citizen engagement, better service delivery, fewer face-to-face contacts, **audit trails**
- E-governance does **not** remove audit trails; digital systems can strengthen them

# ROW 2:89 | Citizen-centric administration - Citizens' Charter, Sevottam, grievance redress (CPGRAMS), social audit
## SEC 2:89:0 | Origins & firsts
### First laws
- **Madhya Pradesh** — first Right to Services law: Lok Sewa Guarantee Act, **2010**; Bihar followed in 2011
- **Meghalaya** — first state law making **social audit** mandatory (**2017**)
- **Second ARC** — social audits to be **institutionalised in all major welfare schemes** (beyond MGNREGA)
### Ombudsman
- Ombudsman originated in **Sweden**, **1809**
- **L.M. Singhvi** coined the term "Lokayukta" (not Morarji Desai)
- **Maharashtra** — first Lokayukta, **1971**
- **First ARC** (headed by **Morarji Desai**) — recommended **Lokpal for the Union**, **Lokayuktas for the States**
## SEC 2:89:1 | Citizens' Charter
- Origin — **UK**, **1991**, under **John Major**; India adopted it in **1997**
- Six principles — **quality, choice, standards, value, accountability, transparency** (participation is **not** one)
- Charter = written commitment on **service standards**, **time-bound delivery**, **grievance redress**
- Not legally enforceable / **non-justiciable** in present form
- **Second ARC** — recommended **statutory status** for Citizens' Charters
- **DARPG** coordinates charters (goicharters.nic.in)
- **Citizens Charter Bill, 2011** — never became law; **lapsed** with dissolution of Lok Sabha in **2014**
## SEC 2:89:2 | Sevottam: three modules
- **Sevottam** = **Seva** + **Uttam** (excellence in service)
| Module | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Citizens' Charter** | publish **service standards** |
| **Grievance Redress** | handle complaints |
| **Service Delivery Capability** | build organisational capacity |
- Sevottam-compliant certification — **IS 15700:2005**
## SEC 2:89:3 | 2nd ARC 12th report (2009)
- **Sevottam** — suggested in Second ARC's **12th Report**, promoted by DARPG; implemented from **2009** (Second ARC set up in 2005)
- Right to Services enactment order — **Punjab** (earliest), Assam, Gujarat, Maharashtra
## SEC 2:89:4 | CPGRAMS
- **CPGRAMS** — **Centralised Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System**
- Launched **2007** by **DARPG**; 24x7 via web, app and **UMANG**
- **DARPG** — under **Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions** (not Home Affairs)
- Pensions — separate platform **CPENGRAMS**
- DARPG circular of **23 August 2024** — max disposal time cut from **30 days to 21 days**
- **GRAI** (Grievance Redressal Assessment & Index) — 4 dimensions, 11 indicators: **efficiency, feedback, domain, organisational commitment** (transparency is **not** one)
## SEC 2:89:5 | AP grievance platforms
- **Prajavani** — web-based online receipt, reply and monitoring of grievances at **District Collectorates**
- **Meekosam** — **single-window** portal to lodge grievances and suggestions and track status
- **Spandana** — launched **2019** under the **YSRCP** Government; one-stop grievance redressal platform
## SEC 2:89:6 | Mechanisms compared
- **Right to Services** laws — notified services in fixed time, appeals against delay, penalties on officials; do **not** abolish other grievance mechanisms
- **MGNREGA Section 17** — social audit of all works by the **Gram Sabha**
- **SSAAT** (AP Society for Social Audit, Accountability and Transparency) — set up **2009**; AP pioneered state-wide MGNREGA social audits
- **CPGRAMS** / State portals — service-delivery grievances
- **Ombudsman** — independent officer of the **legislature**
## SEC 2:89:7 | Exam corner
- ★ **Social Audit** — process used to enhance **accountability** in **MGNREGA**; communities verify funds and works
## SEC 2:89:8 | Citizen-centric administration — origins and FIRSTs
- ★ **ARC 1967** — headed by **Morarji Desai**; first recommended **Lokpal and Lokayukta**
## SEC 2:89:9 | Citizens' Charter — principles, elements and adoption in India
- ★ Citizen's Charter purpose — **time-bound service delivery** and **grievance redress**
- ★ **Right to Service** concept — originated in **Great Britain** (UK); laws guaranteeing time-bound delivery of public services
## SEC 2:89:12 | Grievance redress — CPGRAMS, GRAI and AP platforms
- ★ **Prajavani** — AP e-governance **public grievance redressal** platform; register and track grievances online

# ROW 2:90 | Right to Information Act 2005 - structure, exemptions, amendments and issues
## SEC 2:90:0 | Landmarks
### Origins
- **Sweden** — Freedom of the Press Act, **1766**: world's first freedom-of-information law
- **UDHR Article 19** (1948) — recognised right to seek information
- **MKSS** — **jan sunwais** in **Rajasthan** demanding muster rolls and bills; started the RTI movement
- MKSS leaders — **Aruna Roy**, **Nikhil Dey**, **Shankar Singh** (not Medha Patkar)
- **Tamil Nadu** — first Indian State RTI law (**1997**)
- **Wajahat Habibullah** — first CIC
- RTI Act 2005 — assent **15 June 2005**; fully in force **12 October 2005**
### Provisions
- **BPL** applicants — information **free** of fee
- **Section 2(f)** "information" — material in **any form**: records, documents, e-mails, opinions, advice, contracts, samples, models, electronic data
- **Section 24 / Second Schedule** — intelligence and security bodies (IB, R&AW, CRPF) exempt; **CIC** is **not** exempt (it is the appellate body)
- State Information Commissioner removal grounds — insolvency, conviction involving **moral turpitude**, proved incapacity; **not** disagreement with State Government
### Issues
- Delays — chronic **vacancies** in CIC and SICs plus large appeal backlog
- Concerns — appeal backlogs, vacancies, misuse of **Section 8**, threats to activists (excessive speed is **not** a criticism)
- **Second ARC** — link RTI firmly with **whistle-blower protection** law
## SEC 2:90:1 | Key sections
### Constitutional basis and Preamble
- Right to know read into **Article 19(1)(a)** (also Article 21) — State of U.P. v. **Raj Narain**, **S.P. Gupta**
- Preamble — informed citizenry and transparency to **contain corruption** and hold governments **accountable**
### Sections
| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| **2(f)** | definition of information |
| **2(h)** | **public authority** — bodies set up by law/notification, and bodies **substantially financed or controlled** by government |
| **2(j)** | right to information: inspection, notes, extracts, certified copies/samples, electronic info (not creating new opinions) |
| **4** | **suo motu** / proactive disclosure |
| **4(1)(b)** | proactive disclosure (violations found in **AP Information Commission** statewide inspection) |
| **5** | PIOs |
| **6(2)** | **no reason** needed for request |
| **8(2)** | public-interest override |
| **8(3)** | exempt info disclosable after **20 years** (exceptions e.g. cabinet deliberations) |
| **9** | copyright-based rejection |
| **11** | **third-party** information |
| **12** | constitutes the **CIC** |
| **15** | constitutes the **SIC** |
| **18** | **complaints and inquiry** by the Commission |
| **19** | appeals |
| **19(8)(b)** | Commission may require authority to **compensate** applicant for loss/detriment |
| **20** | penalty; **burden of proof on the PIO** |
| **24** | listed intelligence/security bodies excluded, except info on **corruption** and **human-rights violations** |
- Section 6(2) — applications by post, in person or e-mail; in Hindi, English or local official language
- 2014 **Madras High Court** observation requiring reasons — withdrawn
- Second appeal — within **90 days**; first appeal within **30 days**
- **Second ARC** — Section 8 exemptions to be read **narrowly**; maximum information in public domain
### Public authorities
- **BCCI** — **not** a public authority under 2(h) (CIC ruling); CJI's office and substantially financed NGOs **are**
- CIC declared **six** political parties public authorities; they did not comply; 2005 Act does not name parties
- Demand for parties under RTI — check **corruption** and **crony capitalism**, scrutinise illegal **foreign contributions**; parties get state privileges (free Doordarshan airtime)
### Commissions
- CIC and SICs — **statutory bodies** under RTI Act; civil-court powers; hear second appeals and complaints
- **Sitting MP** cannot be a State Information Commissioner; must be eminent in public life, no office of profit
- Pre-2019 salary — State IC linked to **Chief Secretary**; CIC equal to **Chief Election Commissioner**
- **CBSE v. Aditya Bandopadhyay** (2011) — evaluated **answer sheets** are information examinees can inspect; not held in fiduciary capacity
## SEC 2:90:2 | Time limits

## SEC 2:90:3 | Exemptions 8(1)
| Clause | Exemption |

- **DPDP Act 2023, Section 44(3)** — rewrote 8(1)(j): personal information a **blanket exemption**; dropped public-interest override (and public-activity / privacy-invasion test)
- DPDP amendment notified **November 2025**; challenge referred to Constitution Bench **February 2026**; on **7 August 2026** Court agreed to examine repugnancy
## SEC 2:90:4 | Information Commissions vs CVC
- RTI — a **statutory right** (right to know separately read into Article 19(1)(a))
- State IC removal for proved misbehaviour/incapacity — by **Governor** after **Supreme Court** inquiry
- Each Commission — Chief IC + up to **ten** ICs
- Original IC term — **five years or age 65**, no reappointment; since 2019 Centre notifies; 2019 Rules — **three years**

## SEC 2:90:6 | Cases
