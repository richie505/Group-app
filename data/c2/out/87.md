# ROW 4:36 | India - Himalayan drainage system: Indus, Ganga and Brahmaputra basins
## SEC 4:36:0 | Indus rivers: Greek & Vedic names
### Names and formation
- **Ravi** — Greek name **Hydraotes**; **Beas** — Greek **Hyphasis**
- **Chenab** — formed by **Chandra + Bhaga** at **Tandi** (Himachal Pradesh)
- **Kishanganga** — called **Neelum** in Pakistan; **Jhelum** rises at **Verinag**
- **Ravi** joins the **Chenab** (which also takes the Jhelum) → combined flow joins the **Sutlej** → Indus
- ★ Tributaries of Sutlej: **Baspa, Spiti, Beas**; **Ravi** is a tributary of the Chenab (only a sub-tributary of the Sutlej)
- **Beas** originates near **Rohtang**, not in the Mansarovar–Kailash area; **Karnali, Indus, Brahmaputra** rise in/near that area
### Projects
- ★ **Tulbul** (navigation) project — **Jhelum**, at mouth of **Wular Lake**; proposed **1984**, Pakistan protested **1987** (Indus Waters Treaty 1960)
- ★ **Dulhasti** (390 MW) — **Chenab**, **Kishtwar** district, J&K

## SEC 4:36:1 | Indus system
- ★ **Brahmaputra and Indus** — almost the same point of origin (Tibetan Plateau, "Roof of the World")
- **Indus** — rises at **Singge Khabab**; **Sutlej** near **Rakas Tal**; **Brahmaputra** at **Chemayungdung Glacier**; **Karnali** in Mansarovar–Kailash area
- **Indus** — flows between **Ladakh and Zaskar**, turns south at **Nanga Parbat**, enters Arabian Sea near **Karachi**
- **Kishanganga (Neelum)** — tributary of the **Jhelum** (not Sutlej)
- **Tso Moriri** — brackish lake, **Ladakh**, **Ramsar** site
- ★ **Ram Ganga** (rises **Dudhatoli** ranges, Pauri Garhwal) — tributary of **Ganga**, not Yamuna; **Chambal, Betwa, Ken** — Yamuna tributaries
- **Telugu Ganga Project** — carries **Krishna** water to **Chennai**

## SEC 4:36:2 | Course of the Ganga
### Headwaters
- ★ **Bhagirathi** — rises at **Gangotri glacier** near **Gaumukh/Gomukh**, **Uttarkashi** district; cuts through Central and Lesser Himalayas
- **Alaknanda** rises at **Satopanth**; **Yamuna** at **Yamunotri**
- ★ **Devprayag** — Bhagirathi + Alaknanda unite; river called **Ganga** downstream
- ★ **Panchprayag** (on Alaknanda): **Vishnuprayag, Nandaprayag, Karnaprayag, Rudraprayag, Devprayag**
### Lower course
- **Farakka**: left arm **Bhagirathi-Hooghly**; right arm **Padma** joins Brahmaputra and Meghna
- **Barak** (rises Manipur hills) — headstream of the **Meghna**
### Related
- ★ **Indira Gandhi Canal** — water from **Satluj, Beas, Ravi**
- ★ North→south: **Kishanganga** (Jhelum tributary, J&K) → **Ganga** → **Wainganga** (Godavari tributary) → **Penganga** (Wardha tributary)

## SEC 4:36:3 | Ganga tributaries
### Classification
- India's four drainage groups: **Himalayan, Deccan, Coastal, Inland** (western Rajasthan)
- **Yamuna** — **westernmost and longest** Ganga tributary; rises Yamunotri, joins at **Prayagraj**
- **Son** — **second-largest right-bank** tributary after Yamuna; joins near **Maner** (near **Patna**)
- Right bank: **Damodar, Son**; left bank: **Gomti, Kosi**, Ghaghara, Ramganga (Himalayan)
- **Mahananda** — **last left-bank** tributary, joins in **West Bengal**
- Below Prayagraj (west→east): **Ghaghara, Son, Gandak, Kosi**
### Confluences and sources
| River | Fact |
|---|---|
| Ramganga | joins Ganga near **Farrukhabad** |
| Ghaghra | rises **Mapchachungo Glacier**; meets Ganga at **Revilganj** (Saran, Bihar) |
| Gandak | enters India at **Valmikinagar** (not Raxaul) |
| Betwa | joins Yamuna at **Hamirpur** |
| Chambal | joins Yamuna in **Etawah** |
| Hindan | joins Yamuna from the **left**; Chambal, Sind, Betwa, Ken from the right |
### Gandak
- ★ **Gandak** — called **Narayani** (and Gandaki) in **Nepal**; **Sadanira** in Shatapatha Brahmana
- ★ Two streams **Kaligandak, Trishulganga**; rises in Nepal Himalayas between **Dhaulagiri** and **Everest**
### Damodar
- ★ **Damodar** — famous for **fault valley** drainage; "**Sorrow of Bengal**" (floods); Jharkhand & West Bengal
- **Damodar** — right-bank Ganga tributary; flows into the **Hugli**
- ★ **Rajarappa** — confluence of **Damodar and Bhera (Bhairavi)**; Chinnamastika temple, near Ramgarh
- ★ **Damodar Valley Corporation** — **1948** (DVC Act, Act XIV of 1948)
### Projects and water-sharing
- **2026 Bihar-Jharkhand MoU** (Son, ended 25-year dispute) — splits **7.75 MAF** of the **Bansagar Accord, 1973**: Bihar **5.75**, Jharkhand **2** MAF
- **Kosi Project** — **Bihar and Nepal**
- **Chambal Valley Project** — **MP and Rajasthan**; **Tungabhadra** — **AP and Karnataka**
- **Jayakwadi** — **Godavari** (not Cauvery)

## SEC 4:36:5 | Brahmaputra facts
- Rises at **Chemayungdung Glacier**, Kailash range, east of Lake Mansarovar
- Right-bank (northern, Himalayan) tributary: **Subansiri**; left-bank: **Burhi Dihing, Dhansiri, Dikhu**
- "**Sorrow of Assam**" — due to **frequent floods**
- **Sankosh** — marks **Assam–West Bengal** boundary; rises in **northern Bhutan**, joins Brahmaputra (the source does not explain the boundary)

## SEC 4:36:6 | Lengths

- **Ganga** — navigable up to **Prayagraj**

- **Maharashtra** — most polluted river stretches (BOD above **3 mg/L**)

## SEC 4:36:8 | Exam corner
- ★ **Yamuna** — westernmost and longest tributary of the Ganga
- **Yamuna** — rises at **Yamunotri**, western slopes of **Banderpunch**
- ★ **Kishan Ganga** — main tributary of **Jhelum**; Kishan Ganga project; **Tulbul** also on Jhelum
- Himalayan rivers perennial — fed by **rainfall and snowmelt**
- **Indus Waters Treaty, 1960**: eastern **Ravi, Beas, Sutlej** → India; western **Indus, Jhelum, Chenab** → mainly Pakistan
- **Salal** — **690 MW** NHPC project on **Chenab**; India's first project in the region under the treaty
- **Namami Gange** — implemented by **National Mission for Clean Ganga**; launched **2014**

## SEC 4:36:9 | Himalayan drainage — types, character and terms
- ★ Himalayan rivers in plains — **perennial**; form **ox-bow lakes** and **floodplains**
- ★ **Inland drainage** — river does not reach the ocean, ends in inland lake: **Ghaggar, Luni, Rupen**
- ★ River **Khari** — part of **indeterminate drainage**

## SEC 4:36:10 | Rivers → source (Mansarovar or not)
- ★ Near **Mansarovar** (Tibet): sources of **Brahmaputra, Indus, Sutlej**
- ★ **Sutlej** — does not originate in India; rises at **Rakas Tal (Rakshastal)**/Manasarovar–Rakas lakes near Mt Kailash; enters India via **Shipki La** (HP)
- **Beas** — originates at **Beas Kund** near Rohtang Pass
- ★ **Teesta** — in **Brahmaputra** basin
- ★ **Kalagarh Dam** (= Ramganga Dam) — **Ramganga**, Uttarakhand; **715 m** long, **128 m** high
- ★ **Kaimur Range** — water divide between **Yamuna and Son**; NW boundary of Son
- ★ Catchment areas: **Son 70,055** > Gandak 45,731 > Ramganga 30,811 > Mahananda 23,700 sq km

## SEC 4:36:11 | Indus system — tributaries, course and Greek/Vedic names
### Jhelum
- ★ **Jhelum** — rises in a spring at **Verinag**, south-east Kashmir, foot of **Pir Panjal**
- ★ Names: **Vyeth** (Kashmiri), **Vetesta** (Sanskrit), **Hydaspes** (Greek); flows through **Srinagar**
- ★ **Jhelum passes through Wular Lake** — the only correct statement of a set also claiming "Krishna directly feeds Kolleru" and "Gandak meanders formed Kanwar Lake"
- ★ **Kishanganga** — tributary of the **Jhelum**
### Sutlej
- ★ **Sutlej** — the only one that joins the Indus directly: **Ravi and Jhelum → Chenab → Sutlej → Indus**
- ★ Rises at **Manasarovar–Rakas lakes**, **4,570 m**, within **80 km** of the Indus source; NW course to **Shipki La**
- ★ **Sutlej** — **antecedent** river, flows through **three countries**, rises on Tibetan Plateau, important for irrigation
### Others
- ★ **Beas** — does not originate from Mansarovar
- ★ **Indus** — flows through north-western India and Pakistan
- ★ **Brahmaputra** — called **Tsang Po** in Tibet
- ★ Rivers of **Himachal Pradesh**: **Beas, Chenab, Ravi, Sutlej, Yamuna**
- ★ **Chandra and Bhaga** — flow in **Lahaul** valley; Chandra from glacier near **Chandra Tal**, Bhaga near **Bara Lacha La**; meet near **Tandi** → Chandrabhaga
- ★ **Leh** — **right bank of Indus**; in UT of Ladakh since **31 Oct 2019**

## SEC 4:36:12 | Ganga — course and tributaries by bank
### Course
- ★ **Devprayag** — Alaknanda + Bhagirathi form the Ganga
- ★ **Uttarkashi** — not on the Ganga (on Bhagirathi); **Fatehpur, Bhagalpur, Kanpur** are on the Ganga
- ★ In Bangladesh the Ganga is the **Padma**; joined by Brahmaputra (**Jamuna**), then **Meghna** → Bay of Bengal
- ★ Ganga — total **2525 km**; flows past **Rajmahal hills**, enters Bangladesh via **Farakka**
- ★ **Yamuna** — rises at **Yamunotri Glacier** near **Bandar Poonch** peak
### Tributaries
- ★ Downstream of Prayagraj (west→east): **Gomati** (Ghazipur) – **Ghaghara** (near Chapra) – **Gandak** (near Patna) – **Kosi** (Katihar)
- ★ **Gomti** — the **only Ganga tributary rising in the plains**; **Gomat Taal (Fulhar lake)**, Pilibhit
- ★ **Kosi** — **maximum shifting of course**; dangerous floods; 7 tributaries, **Arun** most important
- ★ **Son** — **flows northwards**; rises near **Amarkantak**; main right-bank tributary, joins near **Patna**
- ★ **Chambal** — tributary of **Yamuna** (not Narmada), joins near **Etawah**; rises **Janapav Hills** near Mhow (Dr Ambedkar Nagar)
- ★ **Manas** — tributary of **Brahmaputra**
- ★ **Betwa** — rises in **Vindhya Range**, joins **Yamuna** near **Hamirpur**

## SEC 4:36:13 | Ganga — left-bank tributaries, Son and Damodar
- ★ **Yamuna** — credited (APPSC key) as an **antecedent** river older than the Himalayas
- ★ **Mahanadi** — not a tributary of the Indus
- ★ **Mahakali Treaty** — **India–Nepal**, **Feb 1996**; river Mahakali (**Sharda** in India); Sharda Barrage, **Tanakpur** Barrage
- ★ **Satluj** — cuts across **all the ranges** of the Himalayas; rises **Rakas Lake**, enters HP near **Shipki La**; **Trans-Himalayan** river; Beas joins near **Harike**, Punjab

## SEC 4:36:15 | Brahmaputra — names by country and tributaries
- ★ Brahmaputra — **Yarlung Tsangpo / Tsangpo / Sanpu** in Tibet; **Dihang** in Arunachal; Brahmaputra in Assam; **Jamuna** in Bangladesh
- ★ **Yarlung Zangbo** — Brahmaputra; originates at **Angsi glacier**, western Tibet
- ★ Catchment: **Tibet, Bangladesh, India** (and Bhutan)

# ROW 4:37 | India - Peninsular drainage: east-flowing and west-flowing rivers, river basins, watershed
## SEC 4:37:0 | East- vs west-flowing
### Direction
- ★ **Narmada** — flows **west** (rises Amarkantak peak, Maikal ranges; rift valley); Tungabhadra, Penganga, Mahanadi flow east to Bay of Bengal
- West-flowing group: **Narmada, Tapi, Sabarmati, Mahi** (Arabian Sea)
- East-flowing rivers form **large deltas**; west-flowing more commonly **estuaries**
- **Narmada** — rises at **Amarkantak** (Maikal Range); valley between **Vindhya and Satpura**; flows into **Arabian Sea** (not Bay of Bengal)
- **Mahanadi** right-bank: **Ong, Tel, Jonk**; left-bank: **Seonath, Hasdeo, Mand, Ib**
### Doabs
- **Bist Doab** — Beas–Sutlej; **Chaj Doab** — Chenab–Jhelum; **Rechna** — Ravi–Chenab
### Cities

|---|---|
| Ahmedabad | **Sabarmati** |
| Surat | **Tapi** |
| Mumbai | Mithi |
| Pune | Mula-Mutha |
| Hyderabad | Musi |
| Kota | Chambal |
| Gorakhpur | Rapti |

### Andhra Pradesh
- **Pennar** — "lifeline of **Rayalaseema**"

- **Tammileru** — drains into **Kolleru Lake**; dam built on it; separates **Krishna and West Godavari** districts
- Indus Waters Treaty: **Sutlej** (with Ravi, Beas) allocated to India; Indus, Jhelum, Chenab mainly Pakistan

## SEC 4:37:2 | Godavari
### Godavari
- **Dakshina Ganga** — Godavari
- Right-bank: **Pravara, Manjira, Manair**; left-bank: **Purna, Pranahita, Indravati, Sabari**
- **Indravati and Sabari** — Godavari tributaries; Ghataprabha, Malaprabha — Krishna; Tel, Hasdeo — Mahanadi
- **Pranhita** — largest tributary; **Wardha + Wainganga**; joins near **Kaleshwaram**
- **Sabari** joins near **Kunavaram** (AP); **Sangameswaram** — Krishna–Tungabhadra confluence
- Delta — **Konaseema**; seven distributaries incl. **Gautami, Vasishta, Vainateya**
- Mouths: **Vasishta** — **Antarvedi**; **Gautami** — **Yanam**; **Vainateya** — **Komaragiripatnam**
- Cotton's anicut — **Dowleswaram**; river **2.7 km** wide at **Rajahmundry**
- **Nanded** — on Godavari (Nashik → Maharashtra, Telangana, AP)
### Others
- ★ **Purna** — tributary of **Tapi**, not Narmada; **Dudhi, Banjar, Hiran** — Narmada tributaries
- **Gandak** — second-largest **gharial** habitat after **Chambal**
- **Sambhar** — largest salt lake; **Wular** largest freshwater; **Chilika** largest brackish lagoon
- **Majuli** (Assam) — largest river island
- **Prayagraj** — Ganga–**Yamuna** confluence; Varanasi, Patna, Bhagalpur on Ganga
