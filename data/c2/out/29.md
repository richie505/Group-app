# ROW 1:224 | Government of India Act 1919 - dyarchy, Montagu-Chelmsford Reforms; Rowlatt Act
## SEC 1:224:0 | Road to the Act
### Montagu Declaration and reactions
- **Montagu Declaration** (**20 August 1917**) — pressure chiefly from the **Home Rule Leagues**
  - "progressive realisation of **responsible government** in India as an integral part of the British Empire" — first official recognition of self-government as an aim
- **Moderates** (Surendranath Banerjee) accepted the reforms, left Congress, formed the **Indian National Liberal Federation** (**1918**)
- **Amritsar Congress (1919)** — reforms "**inadequate, unsatisfactory and disappointing**"
- **GoI Act 1919** — first **direct elections**; 1909 Act had indirect, separate electorates
- **1919** — GoI Act and **Rowlatt Act**
### Later milestones

| Event | Year |
|---|---|
| Partition of Bengal | 1905 |
| Morley-Minto Reforms | 1909 |
| Nehru Report | 1928 |
| **Jinnah's Fourteen Points** — League's answer to the **Nehru Report** | **1929** |

- **Congress ministries resigned** (Oct–Nov **1939**) — India dragged into the **Second World War** without Indian consent

## SEC 1:224:1 | Provincial dyarchy
- Dyarchy in the provinces — subjects divided into **reserved** and **transferred**
- **Local self-government** — a **transferred** subject (Governor with ministers)
- **Finance** — a **reserved** subject, so ministers lacked financial control
- **Section 63**, GoI Act 1935 — Governor's **address to the provincial legislature**

## SEC 1:224:2 | Centre & franchise
### 1919 Act at the Centre
- Bicameral centre: upper chamber **Council of State**, lower **Legislative Assembly**
- Direct elections, franchise limited by **property, tax and educational** qualifications
- Viceroy's Executive Council — **six** members besides the Commander-in-Chief, **three Indians**
- Statutory review commission after **ten years** — Simon Commission appointed two years early
### Simon Commission
- Seven British members, chaired by **Sir John Simon**; included **Clement Attlee**
- Arrived at Bombay **3 February 1928**
- **'Scheduled Castes'** — first used in the **Simon Commission Report**
- Did not join the boycott: **Justice Party** (and Shafi's League faction); Jinnah's faction and the Hindu Mahasabha joined
- **'Simon Go Back'** — coined by **Yusuf Meherally** (APPSC GS 2018)
- Lajpat Rai — Lahore protest; **S. Satyamurti** — Andhra anti-Simon committee
- **Tanguturi Prakasam** — **'Andhra Kesari'**, bared his chest before the police at the **Madras** anti-Simon protest
- **Nehru Report** — reply to **Lord Birkenhead's** challenge to frame a constitution
### 1935 Act
- Federation with **dyarchy at the Centre**; provincial autonomy in the provinces
- **321 sections** and **10 schedules** — the longest British Act on India

## SEC 1:224:3 | Firsts & bodies
- **Public Service Commission** (Section 96(C), 1919 Act) — set up **1 October 1926**, after the Lee Commission
- **Chamber of Princes (Narendra Mandal)** — **1921**, royal proclamation under the 1919 reforms

## SEC 1:224:8 | Terms → meaning: dyarchy, reserved and transferred subjects
