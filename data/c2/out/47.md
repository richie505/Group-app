# ROW 2:68 | Recognition of national and state parties - criteria and the Symbols Order
## SEC 2:68:0 | Three states of a party
- Three statuses: **unregistered**; **registered but unrecognised (RUPP)**; **recognised**
- ★ **2016** (2 Sept): **Trinamool Congress** got National Party status (no longer a National Party now)
- ★ **Aam Aadmi Party** — a **National Party**
## SEC 2:68:1 | Who decides
- **Election Commission** does both: registration (**Section 29A**, RPA 1951) and recognition (**Election Symbols Order, 1968**)
- Registration is under Sec 29A, NOT under the Symbols Order (which governs **allotment of symbols**)
- **Paragraph 15**: split disputes over name/symbol — **Sadiq Ali test** (majority support of factions)
- **Paragraph 16A**: suspend/withdraw recognition for defying the **Model Code of Conduct**
## SEC 2:68:2 | National party: any one
### Registration
- ECI's power to **deregister** is limited — arises e.g. for **fraud** or ceasing allegiance
- Sec 29A: party constitution must pledge allegiance to the Constitution and to **socialism, secularism, democracy** (and national integrity)
- **Reserved symbols** only for recognised parties; **free symbols** for RUPPs and independents
### National party routes
- **2%** of Lok Sabha seats (**11**) from at least **three** States
- **6%** votes in **four** States **and** **four** Lok Sabha seats (both mandatory)
## SEC 2:68:3 | State party: any one
- **6%** of valid votes in Assembly election + **2** Assembly seats
- or **6%** in Lok Sabha poll + **1** Lok Sabha seat
- Seat route: **1 Lok Sabha seat per 25** seats allotted to the State
- **8%** valid-vote route: **no seat** needed
- Any one route suffices
## SEC 2:68:4 | National parties
- **April 2023** review: **CPI, NCP, AITC** derecognised; **AAP** recognised
- **NPP** (Meghalaya) — first **North-Eastern** party to get National Party status
- Not National Parties: **DMK, RSP, All India Forward Bloc, Peasants and Workers Party**
## SEC 2:68:5 | Recognised vs RUPP
| Benefit | Recognised party | RUPP |
|---|---|---|
| Proposers for nomination | **1** | 10 |
| Star campaigners | **40** | 20 |
- Recognised parties also get **two free sets of electoral rolls**, broadcast/telecast time on **Akashvani and Doordarshan**, consultation by ECI
- RUPP must contest at least once in **six years** or face delisting
## SEC 2:68:6 | Exam corner
- **Symbols Order, 1968**: a **statutory order** of the ECI under the **RPA, 1951** and **Article 324** (not an Act, not a constitutional provision)
- Not a National Party criterion: **15% votes in two States** without a seat
- Valid: recognition in **four States**; 6% + four seats; 2% Lok Sabha seats
- RUPP donations: donor disclosure above **Rs 20,000**; above **Rs 2,000** only by **cheque/bank transfer**
- AP: **TDP** and **YSRCP** — **recognised State Parties** (neither national)
## SEC 2:68:7 | Laws, paragraphs and cases on registration and recognition
- ★ **Election Commission of India** recognises political parties — under the **Election Symbols (Reservation and Allotment) Order, 1968**
## SEC 2:68:9 | National party criteria — paragraph 6B of the Symbols Order
- ★ Each is a valid, independently sufficient criterion (need only **one**):
  - **2%** Lok Sabha seats from **3** States
  - **6%** valid votes in **4** States + **4** Lok Sabha seats
  - recognised as **State party in 4 States**

## SEC 2:69:3 | Composition & removal
- At least **half** the members must have held government office (GoI or State) for **10 years**

- Misbehaviour (Art. **317(4)**) includes being interested in a **government contract** or sharing its profit
- UPSC advice is **advisory**; action without consulting it is **not invalid** for that reason alone
- **JSPSC** member resigns to the **President** (not the Governor)
- **Finance Commission**: constituted by the **President** every **five years** or earlier (Art. 280); qualifications prescribed by **Parliament** (FC Act, 1951)
## SEC 2:69:4 | Post-retirement (Art. 319)

- Former SPSC member may become **UPSC Chairman/member** or Chairman of the same or another SPSC
- But **no other government employment** (e.g. under the Government of India)
## SEC 2:69:5 | Functions (Art. 320)
- Ordinarily consulted: **temporary appointments for more than one year**
- SPSC not concerned with **classification, pay and training** of employees
- SPSC principal concerns: recruitment, promotion, transfer, candidate suitability
## SEC 2:69:6 | Reports, JSPSC & APPSC

- Regulations excluding posts from consultation (President / Governor): laid before legislature for **14 days**
- SPSC — watchdog of the **merit system**
- Non-acceptance of UPSC advice: approval of the **Appointments Committee of the Cabinet**
- Annual report: SPSC → **Governor** (laid before State Legislature); UPSC → **President**
- **JSPSC**: created by an **Act of Parliament** on request of **two or more State Legislatures**
- **APPSC**: **1 November 1956** — merger of **Andhra PSC (1953)** + **Hyderabad PSC**; after 2014 for residuary AP, Telangana got **TSPSC**

## SEC 2:71:4 | Audits & limits
### Name, appointment, term
- Official name: **Comptroller and Auditor-General of India**

### Jurisdiction
- Audits both **receipts and expenditure**
- Audits **government companies** and bodies **substantially financed** by government; **local bodies** only on request
- **RBI** — audited wholly by **private auditors**; no CAG supplementary role
- **Secret service** expenditure — accepts a **certificate** from the competent authority
- Three Union reports: **Appropriation Accounts**, **Finance Accounts**, **Public Undertakings** (not Estimates)
- Sequence: **CAG → President → both Houses → PAC**
- Uses **AI/ML-based audits** to detect welfare fraud; expanding remote audits

- Functions: **performance audits**; NOT day-to-day **treasury** control, NOT prosecution
- Cannot **disallow** expenditure, **enforce recovery** or **punish** — only reports to the legislature
