# ROW 5:19 | Blockchain and distributed ledger technology; cryptocurrency and CBDC (e-rupee)
## SEC 5:19:0 | Definition & Bitcoin
- ★ **Cryptocurrency** — encrypted (cryptographic) peer-to-peer fund transfers without a **central bank**
- **Blockchain** — time-stamped series of **immutable** records managed by a cluster of computers not owned by one entity
- Records protected because stakeholders **hold copies** and changes need **consensus**
- **Bitcoin** — introduced **2009** by **Satoshi Nakamoto** (pseudonymous)
## SEC 5:19:1 | How a chain links
- **Distributed ledger** — shared ledger replicated across many nodes; single source of truth; no central validator
- A block holds transaction details, a timestamp and the **hash of the previous block** (not private keys)
- **Immutability** — data nearly impossible to alter without consensus; no central authority modifies it
## SEC 5:19:2 | Key terms
- **Stablecoins** — pegged to fiat currencies, commodities or crypto-assets
- **Tokenisation** — converts ownership rights in an asset into a blockchain token; **mining** validates transactions
- **NFT** — **Non-Fungible Token**; unique, not exchangeable at equivalency; can represent physical assets
- **Smart contract** — self-executing code; **DeFi** uses them for lending/trading without banks
## SEC 5:19:3 | Types & consensus
- **The Merge** — Ethereum's switch from Proof of Work to **Proof of Stake** (**September 2022**); Bitcoin still uses PoW
