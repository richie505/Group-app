## SEC 1:0:1 | Religious texts
### Earliest references to Andhra
- **Aitareya Brahmana** — earliest literary reference to the **Andhras** (story of **Sunahshepa** and Vishwamitra's disowned sons)
- **Vinayavatthu** — earliest Buddhist reference to Andhra as **"Andhrapatha"**
- **Andhranagari** (Bhimasena and Serivanija Jatakas) — identified with **Dhanyakataka**; **P. C. Ray** identified its river as the **Krishna**
- Ashoka's **13th Major Rock Edict** — lists the Andhras within the **Mauryan Empire**
### Other texts
- **Dipavamsa** and **Mahavamsa** — **Sri Lankan Pali chronicles**; illuminate Mauryan history and Andhra Buddhism
- **Puranas** — name **Pulomavi III** (not Gautamiputra Satakarni) as the last Satavahana
- **Sangam texts** — about **3rd c. BCE – 3rd c. CE**; describe Chera, Chola and Pandya kingdoms
- **Periplus of the Erythraean Sea** — records the **Saka–Satavahana conflict**
- **V. S. Sukthankar** — Andhras and Satavahanas **not the same** people; linked to the **Karnataka theory**

## SEC 1:0:2 | Key inscriptions
### Kharavela — Hathigumpha
- ★ **Hathigumpha inscription** — **Kharavela** of Kalinga (present-day Odisha); records his campaigns, including against **Satakarni**, and mentions **Tamil kingdoms**
- Kharavela — **Chedi (Mahameghavahana)** dynasty
- In the **Udayagiri caves** near **Bhubaneswar**; undated and partially damaged
- Language **Prakrit**, script **Brahmi**
- Opens with a salute to the **Jinas** — Kharavela was a **Jain**
### Rudradaman — Junagadh
- ★ **Junagadh (Girnar)** inscription of Saka ruler **Rudradaman I** — first long **Sanskrit** inscription in India; early **Kavya**-style work
- Records repair of the **Sudarshana lake** **without forced labour**
### Satavahana records
- **Nasik Prashasti** — composed by **Gautami Balashri**, mother of Gautamiputra Satakarni; hails him as destroyer of Shakas, Yavanas and Pahlavas
- Nashik inscription calls **Gautamiputra Satakarni** **"Ekabrahmana"** (unrivalled Brahmana)
- **Naneghat** inscription — Queen **Naganika**; key source for early Satavahana history
- **Myakadoni** inscription of **Pulumavi III** — basis of the **Karnataka-homeland** theory
- **Alluru** inscription — records **multiple donations** to various communities and institutions
### Other inscriptions
| Inscription | Ruler / content |
|---|---|
| **Allahabad Pillar** (Prayaga Prashasti) | **Samudragupta**'s conquests; composed by **Harisena** |
| **Aihole** | **Pulakeshin II** stopped Harsha at the **Narmada**; by **Ravikirti**; **Sanskrit**, Southern Brahmi |
| **Bhitari** | **Skandagupta** repulsed the **Hunas** |
| **Ayodhya** (of Dhanadeva) | **Pushyamitra Shunga** — performed **two Ashvamedha** sacrifices |
| **Sarnath** Buddhist image | **Kanishka**, his 3rd year (**81 AD**) |
| **Rabatak** (found 1993) | Bactrian language in **Greek** script |
| **Mahasthan** (Bengal) | **famine relief** |
| **Sohgaura** plate | earliest known **copper plate**; earliest royal famine-related order |
- **Nayadhammakahao** (Jain text) — Andhra traditions on Ikshvaku ruler **Yashodhara** and son **Dhanada**
### Miscellaneous
- **Annamacharya** — **"Padakavita Pitamaha"**, earliest known Telugu song-composer
- **Ibn Battuta** — came to Delhi **1333**; **qazi** under **Muhammad bin Tughlaq**; his **Rihla** records the Sultanate
- **Kallanai** (Grand Anicut) across the Kaveri — **Karikala Chola**

## SEC 1:0:3 | Foreign accounts in order
### Order of visitors
- Chronological order: **Megasthenes → Fa-Hien → Hiuen Tsang → Al-Biruni** (Mauryan → Gupta → Harsha → 11th c.)
### Greek and Roman writers
- **Megasthenes** — envoy of **Seleucus Nikator** to **Chandragupta Maurya**; lived at **Pataliputra**
- **Indica** (Megasthenes) — first foreign work to mention the **Andhras**; not by Aryabhata (who wrote the **Aryabhatiya**)
- Andhras per Megasthenes — **30 fortified towns**, **1,00,000 infantry** (80 forts is only a textual variant)
- Megasthenes wrote of **Brahmins and Shramanas**
- **Pliny** — **Natural History** in **Latin**; complained of the drain of **Roman gold** to India (about **50 million sesterces** a year) for spices, pearls, muslin
- **Ptolemy** — Geography; **Masulia/Maisolia/Masolia** = **Machilipatnam**, a great trading centre (for **textiles**), not a Roman military headquarters
- Ptolemy's **Ben Ra** (Vengi) — a trade centre
- **Periplus** — by an anonymous Greek sailor
### Chinese pilgrims
- **Fa-Hien** — during **Chandragupta II**; wrote of Buddhist pilgrimage
- ★ **Si-Yu-Ki** (Buddhist Records) — **Hiuen Tsang** (Xuanzang), 7th c. CE
- Si-Yu-Ki: the **Sriparvata vihara** lacked patronage
- Hiuen Tsang — studied at **Nalanda**; wrote of Mahayana
- **I-Tsing** — **671–695 CE**; landed at **Tamralipti**; studied at **Nalanda**, wrote on Buddhist monastic life and relics
### Arab writer
- **Al-Biruni** — **Kitab-ul-Hind** in **Arabic** (not Persian); wrote on Hindu religion, Puranas, Sanskrit, Yoga
### Related points
- **Inscriptions** — most reliable source; foreign accounts only supplement
- **"Andhra Shivaji"** — **Parvataneni Veeraiah Chaudhary**

## SEC 1:0:4 | Scripts & decipherment
- **Ashokan edicts** — first decipherable inscriptions; **Prakrit** in **Brahmi** script
- **Shahbazgarhi** and **Mansehra** edicts — **Kharosthi**, a right-to-left script of north-west India
- **Mangulam, Sittanavasal, Arittapatti** — Tamil-Brahmi sites in the **Pandya** region
- **James Prinsep** deciphered Brahmi in **1837**

## SEC 1:0:5 | Coins
### Coin types
- **Punch-marked** coins — earliest Indian coins
- **Kahapana, nikkha, kamsa, kakanika** — first definite references in **Pali texts**
- **Indo-Greek** coins — carried **names and portraits** of rulers
- Kushanas and Guptas — issued **gold** coins extensively
### Satavahana and later coins
- **Gautamiputra Satakarni** — **re-struck Nahapana's** coins
- **Ship-motif** coins — **Yajna Sri Satakarni** (and Vasishthiputra Pulumavi); evidence of sea trade
- Satavahanas also issued **lead** coins
- **Karpanamulu** — Satavahana **copper** coins
- **Mada** — **Kakatiya gold** coin (not Satavahana)
- Roman coins found at **Bhattiprolu** and **Nagarjunakonda**
- **Santamula I** (Ikshvaku) — gave away **crores of gold coins**
- **Bhattiprolu** inscription — refers to a **Nigama-sabha** (guild assembly)

## SEC 1:0:7 | Dating methods
- **Thermoluminescence** — light released on **heating** pottery or burnt material
- **Radiocarbon** — measures **C-14 decay**; half-life about **5,730 years**
- **Dendrochronology** — counts annual **tree rings**
- **Superposition** (stratigraphy) — lower layers are **older** than those above
