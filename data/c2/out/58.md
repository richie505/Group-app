### NSG
- NSG formed **1975**, popularly the **London Club**, in response to India's **1974 Pokhran-I** test
- NSG is **voluntary** and **consensus-based**; **48 members**
- NSG has **no permanent secretariat**

## SEC 2:161:1 | Nuclear treaties & India
### NPT and CTBT
- **NPT**: signed 1968, in force **1970**, extended indefinitely in **1995**
- India rejects the NPT as **discriminatory** — "**nuclear apartheid**"; seeks a **time-bound disarmament** plan from nuclear powers before signing
- **CTBT** (adopted **1996**) bans all nuclear explosions
- India has not signed the CTBT: lacks a **time-bound disarmament** commitment and permits **subcritical tests**
### Other treaties
- **TPNW** (binding ban on nuclear weapons) entered into force **2021**
- India did not sign the TPNW: says it **adds nothing to customary international law**
- India supports **FMCT** negotiations at the **Conference on Disarmament** (treaty not capping existing stocks)
- **CCW** (inhumane conventional weapons): India is party to **all five** protocols

## SEC 2:161:2 | NPT & arms control
- **North Korea** left the NPT in **2003**
- **JCPOA** dates from **2015** (not 2010)
- US withdrew from JCPOA in **2018**; Iran dropped all limits on **5 January 2020**
- **New START** (2010) — **USA and Russia** (not China); Russia suspended it in **2023**
- **INF Treaty** (1987) — **USA and USSR**; US withdrew in **2019**

## SEC 2:161:3 | Organisations
### IAEA
- **IAEA** HQ **Vienna**; established **1957**
- India joined the IAEA in **1957** as a **founding member**
### Chemical and biological weapons
- **CWC** in force **1997**; India completed destruction of its declared stockpile in **2009**
- **OPCW** is **not an EU body**; implements the CWC, monitors the chemical industry
- OPCW won the **2013 Nobel Peace Prize**
- **BWC** adopted **1972**, in force **1975**; India is a party; **no verification organisation**
### FATF
- FATF secretariat at **Paris** (OECD)
- **Grey list** = increased monitoring; does not by itself mean a call for action
- **Call for action** = the **black list** of high-risk jurisdictions

## SEC 2:161:4 | India's nuclear deals
- India's **Additional Protocol** to its IAEA safeguards agreement in force **2014**
- **2008 NSG waiver** (India-US Civil Nuclear Deal): permits **civil nuclear trade**, import of **uranium and reactors**
- The waiver does **not** make India an NPT member
