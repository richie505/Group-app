### Coastline
- Recalculated AP coastline — **1,053.07 km**; **third** after **Gujarat** and **Tamil Nadu** (older figure 974 km)

## SEC 4:47:2 | Coastal lakes
### Pulicat
- Separated from the Bay of Bengal by **Sriharikota Island**
- India's **second-largest brackish lagoon**, shared by **AP** and **Tamil Nadu**
- **Greater flamingos** — Pulicat (not Kolleru)

- Glacial erosion by Himalayan ice fields — not a threat to AP coastal lakes
### Other
- **Rollapadu** — **dry grassland** (rangeland) for the Great Indian Bustard, not a lagoon

# ROW 4:49 | Andhra Pradesh - climate, rainfall regions, cyclone vulnerability, drought-prone areas
## SEC 4:49:0 | Seasons of AP
| Season | Period |
|---|---|
| **Winter** | **January–February**; mostly dry, **13 C to 30 C** |
| **Summer** | **March** to mid-June |
| **South-west monsoon** | mid-June to September; peaks in **July** |
| **Retreating monsoon** | **October–December** |
- Chronological order — **Winter, Summer, SW monsoon, Retreating monsoon**

## SEC 4:49:1 | Two monsoons compared
### Rainfall
- **South-West Monsoon** — about **two-thirds** (65–70%) of AP's annual rainfall
- Wettest month — **July**
- **River floods** — mainly in the **SW monsoon**, not post-monsoon
### Disaster framework
- NDMA Cyclone Guidelines 2008 — **storm-surge barriers** are **structural** (with shelters, raised platforms, evacuation roads)
- **NCRMP Phase I** — **World Bank**-aided; covered **AP** and **Odisha**
- Flood management — **State** subject; Centre gives technical guidance and funds through **FMBAP**
- **AP Disaster Management Rules** — **2007** (with DM Act 2005)

## SEC 4:49:2 | Rainfall & extremes
- **Lambasingi** — coldest place, 'Kashmir of AP' (about 2 deg C); **Alluri Sitharama Raju** district
- **Mango showers** — **pre-monsoon** showers of **April–May** that help mangoes ripen
- Summer — **March to June**; climate generally hot and humid
- Cyclone-resistant roof — **hipped roof** firmly anchored to the walls (better than gable)
- 2026 monsoon deficiency — **El Niño**

## SEC 4:49:3 | Why Rayalaseema is drought-prone
- **Rain shadow** of the Western Ghats and Karnataka–Telangana highlands → little SW monsoon rain in **Rayalaseema**
- Interior Maharashtra, interior Karnataka, Telangana, AP, Tamil Nadu — **rain-shadow areas**
- Chronic droughts in Rayalaseema and southern Prakasam — failure of the **north-east monsoon**; groundwater depletion aggravates
- Greatest rural distress — **Prakasam, Anantapur, Kurnool, Kadapa**
- Watershed raises groundwater but yields flat — **neglect of crop diversification and soil management**
- **Gandikota** reservoir (Telugu Ganga) — **Pennar**, **YSR Kadapa**
- **Crop Weather Watch Group** — **Ministry of Agriculture**; meets weekly in the monsoon

## SEC 4:49:4 | Cyclone vulnerability
### Exposure
- ★ Most cyclone-vulnerable stretch — **Ongole to Machilipatnam** (APPSC 2018)
- Peak cyclone season — **October to December**; about **one-third** of AP rainfall falls then
- Most vulnerable coastal districts — **Nellore** and **Srikakulam**
- Storm surge can inundate villages up to **10 km** inland
- Coastline (MHA remeasurement, Dec 2024) — **1,053.07 km**; third after Gujarat and Tamil Nadu
### Hazards and tools
- Inland plains — **heat waves**; Coastal Andhra — cyclones and storm surge
- **1990** cyclone — about **1,000** deaths; turning point for disaster management
- **SCATSAT-1** — ISRO scatterometer satellite for cyclone prediction and tracking
