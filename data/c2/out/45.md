# ROW 2:52 | Emergency provisions - National Emergency (352), President's Rule (356), Financial Emergency (360); S.R. Bommai case
## SEC 2:52:0 | Three emergencies compared
### Parliamentary approval
| Emergency | Approval within | Majority |
|---|---|---|
| National (352) | **one month** | **special majority** in each House |
| President's Rule (356) | **two months** | — |

### National Emergency
- Proclamations: first **1962**, second **1971**, third **25 June 1975**
- **Minerva Mills** — Art. 352 proclamation **reviewable**; reversed the **38th Amendment**'s bar on review

### Financial Emergency
- **No maximum period**; continues **until revoked**; **never declared** in India
### Related rules
- Presidential vacancy (death, resignation, removal) — **Vice-President** acts; filled within **six months** (Art. 62(2))
- Maximum gap between sessions — Article **85**

- **44th Amendment** — Art. 352(3): **written advice** of the Union Cabinet mandatory

- Chairman — Justice **Madan Mohan Punchhi**, former **Chief Justice of India**
- Formally the **Commission on Centre-State Relations**; appointed by the **Government of India** (UPA), **April 2007**
- Report — **2010**

## SEC 2:57:1 | Governor
- **Fixed five-year** tenure, insulated from Central pressure
- Governors **not** to be **Chancellors** or hold statutory university roles
- Constitutional amendment — **six months** for Governor to decide on Bills
- Governor — eminent person **not in active politics** (even locally) for a couple of years

## SEC 2:57:2 | Proposed amendments
- Delete "**during the pleasure of the President**" from **Article 156**
- Removal only by **impeachment** by the **State Legislature**, like the President (not rotation of Governors)
- Amend **Articles 355 and 356**; Art. 356 **sparingly**, **last resort**, never partisan

## SEC 2:57:3 | Security & forums
- **National Integration Council** — meet at least **once a year**
- **PM and Chief Ministers** — meet regularly
- **Inter-State Council** — at least **thrice a year**
- **Localised emergency** — Centre takes over a **district or part of a district** for a limited period
- Communal violence — central forces deployed **suo motu**, without prior State consent, short period; consent **afterwards**

## SEC 2:57:4 | Lists & finance
- Concurrent List laws — **broad agreement** with States through the **Inter-State Council**
- New Union List entry — **environment, ecology and climate change**
- **Cesses and surcharges** — to be **limited**

## SEC 2:57:5 | Status & cases
- **8 April 2025** Tamil Nadu case — fixed **timelines** for Governors on Bills
- **20 November 2025** Presidential Reference — courts cannot impose fixed timelines or **deemed assent**
- **Article 156** unchanged — Governors still hold office **during the pleasure of the President**

## SEC 2:57:6 | Exam corner
- **16th Finance Commission (2026-31)** — chaired by **Arvind Panagariya**; revised formula raised the share of five southern States incl. **Andhra Pradesh**

# ROW 2:59 | Cooperative and competitive federalism - NITI Aayog, GST Council, state rankings
## SEC 2:59:0 | Types of federalism
- **Cooperative federalism** — first recommended by the **Sarkaria Commission**; put into practice by **NITI Aayog (2015)**
- "**Marble-cake federalism**" (NITI Aayog) — Centre and States on a common platform, **bottom-up**
- **GST Council** — cooperative; **NITI Aayog State rankings** — competitive
- **Competitive federalism** — States compete with each other and with the Centre to attract **investment** and improve outcomes
- **Competitive sub-federalism** — competition among **local bodies / districts**; e.g. **Aspirational Districts Programme** rankings

## SEC 2:59:1 | Kinds of body
- ★ **Extra-constitutional** — power/rights not found in the Constitution; e.g. **Planning Commission** (Cabinet resolution)
- **Constitutional body** — set up by the **Constitution** itself (ECI, CAG)
- **Statutory body** — created by an Act of Parliament / State Legislature; e.g. **NHRC**, **Lokpal**, **NGT**

| Body | Created by | Year |
|---|---|---|
| Planning Commission | **Cabinet resolution** (non-statutory) | **1950** |
| NDC | executive resolution | **1952** |

# ROW 2:60 | Devolution of powers and finances to local levels - Finance Commission and State Finance Commission transfers
## SEC 2:60:0 | Key terms
- **Deconcentration** — work to field officers in the bureaucracy, not to elected bodies
- Increasing depth: **deconcentration → delegation → devolution** (devolution transfers most authority)
- **Vertical devolution** — Union and States; **horizontal** — among States
- Vertical fiscal imbalance — Union has **buoyant taxes**; States carry heavier spending (health, education, agriculture, police)
- **Cesses and surcharges** — excluded from the divisible pool; pool ≈ **81%** of gross tax revenue (**2025-26**)

## SEC 2:60:1 | Articles for LB finances
- **Eleventh Schedule** — menu of **29** subjects that **may** be entrusted under Article **243G** (no automatic transfer)
- Article **243I** — **State Finance Commission**, constituted by the **Governor**
- Article **243K** — **State Election Commission**
- Article **243H** — Panchayat taxing powers; grants from the **State Consolidated Fund**
- Article **280** — Union Finance Commission, constituted by the **President**
- **Eleventh Finance Commission** — CAG **Technical Guidance and Support** over local-body audits

## SEC 2:60:2 | Panchayat revenue
- Article **243H** — Panchayat taxes, duties, tolls, fees only when **State Legislature** authorises
- **Property tax** — major own-source revenue

## SEC 2:60:3 | FC vertical share
- **16th FC** — **Dr Arvind Panagariya**; 14th — **Y.V. Reddy**; 15th — **N.K. Singh**

## SEC 2:60:4 | Horizontal criteria (%)
- **73rd Amendment** — mandatory **reservation** in PRIs; also **State Finance Commissions**

|---|---|---|
| 13th | **47.5%** ("fiscal capacity distance") | population 1971 **25%**, area **10%**, fiscal discipline **17.5%** |

- **16th FC** — new **contribution to GDP** criterion (**10%**), helped AP; dropped **tax and fiscal effort** (2.5%)

- Income distance — from average of the **three largest States**

# ROW 2:61 | Challenges in local devolution - funds, functions, functionaries; parallel bodies and capacity gaps
## SEC 2:61:0 | The 3Fs: paper vs reality
- **Eleventh Schedule** — **29** subjects (Panchayats); **Twelfth** — **18** (municipalities)
- 3F reality — functions on **paper**, funds **tied to schemes**, staff report to **State line departments**
- **Roadmap for Panchayati Raj** — weak 3F devolution, bureaucratic control, tied funds, grant dependence, reluctance to tax

## SEC 2:61:1 | Articles
- **DPC** — Article **243ZD**; **MPC** — Article **243ZE**
- **Five-year term**; elections before expiry or within **six months** of dissolution, by the **SEC** — local government permanent after **1993**
- Article **243D** — at least **one-third** seats for women; AP reserves **50%**

## SEC 2:61:2 | Funds gap
- Schedules don't transfer automatically — Arts **243G** and **243W** use "**may**"
- **Property tax** — main own revenue of ULBs (under-assessed, under-collected)
- ULB own-source revenue ≈ **0.3% of GDP**; cities produce ≈ **67%** of GDP but get **less than 1%** of tax revenue
- **16th FC** urban share **36% → 45%**; split **80% basic**, **20% performance**
- **State Election Commissioner** (Art. 243K) — appointed by **Governor**, removed like a **High Court Judge**; CEC like a Supreme Court Judge
- **Janmabhoomi committees** and secretariats — **parallel bodies** weakening PRIs

## SEC 2:61:3 | Parallel bodies
- Bypassing elected bodies — **DRDAs**, user groups, SHGs, **MPLADS**/MLA LADS (DPCs, MPCs are constitutional)
- **MPLADS** — MP **recommends**, **Implementing District Authority** executes; **RTI** applies; portal **eSAKSHI**
- AP **village and ward secretariats** and volunteers — **2019** (after Janmabhoomi committees 2014-19)
- 16th FC local-body grant conditions — properly constituted bodies, **audited accounts**, timely **SFCs**
- **RGSA** (2018-19) — strengthens PRIs to achieve the **SDGs**

## SEC 2:61:4 | Elections, SFCs & AP
- AP Cabinet, **18 August 2026** — **34%** BC reservation in rural local bodies, **33⅓%** urban; direct election of Mayors and chairpersons
- Delayed local elections risk Central grants — 16th FC links grants to constituted bodies, audited accounts, timely SFCs

## SEC 2:61:5 | Women representatives
- Proxy behaviour **falls** in a woman's **second term**
- Responses — **capacity building**, **exemplary penalties** in proven proxy cases
- **Sashakt Panchayat Netri Abhiyan**, Model Women-Friendly Gram Panchayats — elected women's participation
- **BRGF** merged into **RGSA**; RGSA converges with **Mission Antyodaya**

## SEC 2:61:6 | Capacity building
- **RGSA** funding — **60:40**; **90:10** NE and hilly States; **100%** Central for UTs
- Revamped RGSA — **1 April 2022 to 31 March 2026**, matching 15th FC period
- **eGramSwaraj** — Panchayat **planning, accounting, reporting**; **AuditOnline** — audit; **SVAMITVA** — property records

## SEC 2:61:7 | Committees
- **State Finance Commission** reports — often **ignored**
- **Local Body Ombudsman** — **Second ARC**, **Sixth Report** (Local Governance, **2007**)
- "**Status of Devolution to Panchayats in States**" — **Ministry of Panchayati Raj** with **IIPA**
