## SEC 4:94:4 | Transmission media
- **5G Standalone** — pure 5G, **independent of 4G core**; **lower latency**, **network slicing**
- **Optical fibre** — light pulses; **fastest**, highest bandwidth
- **Copper** (twisted pair, coaxial) — **electrical signals**; lower bandwidth, signal loss over distance, interference
- **Geostationary** satellites — ~**36,000 km** → high latency
- Fibre core refractive index **higher** than cladding; TIR needs angle of incidence > **critical angle**
- **Optical-fibre sensors** — measure **temperature** and **strain**; decorative illumination is **fibre lamps**
- **Endoscope** — **TIR** in fibre bundles; one bundle illuminates, another carries the image back
