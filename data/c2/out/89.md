# ROW 4:43 | India - soil types, characteristics and distribution; soil erosion and conservation
## SEC 4:43:0 | Major soil types
### Soil basics
- Soil horizons, surface downward — **O → A → B → C → R** (organic litter, topsoil, subsoil, weathered parent material, bedrock)
- **B horizon** — subsoil where minerals washed down from upper layers **accumulate**
- Good soil by volume — **mineral 45%, organic 5%, water 25%, air 25%**
- **Soil organic carbon** — binds particles into **aggregates**, raises infiltration, feeds microbes
- **Residual (in situ)** soils dominate **Peninsular India**; **drifted (ex situ)** dominate North India
- **Eight** major soil types: alluvial, black, red and yellow, laterite, arid, saline and alkaline, peaty and marshy, forest and mountain
### Soil types
| Soil | Key features | Crops / regions |
|---|---|---|
| **Black (regur)** | from **Deccan Trap basalt**; neutral to alkaline; rich in **calcium, potassium, magnesium**; poor in N, P, humus | **cotton, jowar, chillies**, tobacco; **Deccan lava plateau** |
| **Red** | red from **iron oxide**, yellow when hydrated; from granite and gneiss | ragi, groundnut |
| **Laterite** | **heavy leaching, rainfall > 200 cm, high temperature**; rich in **iron and aluminium oxides**; **acidic, infertile**; poor in N, phosphate, calcium; excess iron oxide and potash | tea, coffee, rubber (manured); summits of **Western and Eastern Ghats** |
| **Arid (desert)** | sandy; poor in **nitrogen and humus**; **kankar** at lower depths; improves with irrigation | — |
| **Forest and mountain** | **humus-rich**, on slopes; poor in **potash and phosphorus**; mountain meadow soils above tree line | — |
| **Alluvial** | — | rice, jute, wheat, sugarcane; **Northern Plains** |
- ★ **Tea** — best on **slightly acidic** soil without calcium; intolerant of stagnant water, so grown on hill slopes
- **Laterite** as building material — cut into **bricks that harden on exposure to air**
- **Coffee** — red and laterite soils of the Western Ghats (not alluvial)
### Problems and nutrients
- Salinity, alkalinity, waterlogging — caused by **unscientific irrigation and poor drainage**
- Salinity/alkalinity-affected states — **Rajasthan, Haryana, Punjab, UP, Bihar**; canal-irrigated **Punjab** after the Green Revolution
- **Wind erosion** worst on **bare, dry, fine-textured** soil with **strong, frequent winds**
- Soil Health Card micronutrients — **zinc, iron, copper, manganese, boron** (N, P, K, S tested separately)
- Most Indian soils deficient in **nitrogen, phosphorus and humus** — a reason for Soil Health Cards
- **Kurnool** — **Pre-Monsoon Dry Sowing** (target **92,000 acres**) with **Jeevamrutham** to conserve soil moisture

## SEC 4:43:1 | Alluvial terms
- **Karewa** (Kashmir Valley; old lake-bed deposits) — famous for **saffron**, esp. around **Pampore**
- **Khadar** — **new alluvium** deposited by floods; renewed by fresh flood silt every year, so **more fertile** than Bhangar
- **Bhangar** — **old alluvium** on higher ground beyond normal flood reach; with **kankar**
- **Bhabar** — **coarse, pebbly foothill belt** where **streams disappear** (sink)
- **Tarai** — **marshy** land lying **south of Bhabar**

## SEC 4:43:2 | ICAR soil orders
- Soil orders by share of India's area — **Inceptisols 39.7%** (largest) > Entisols 28.1% > Alfisols 13.6% > Vertisols 8.5%
- **Laterite**, when adequately manured — **tea, coffee, rubber, cashew**
- **Black soil** — over **62% clay**, high moisture retention, **deep summer cracks**, **self-ploughing**
- Very deep black soils — **north-western Deccan Plateau**
- **Peaty and marshy** soils (**Alappuzha–Kottayam**, **Sundarbans**) — from heavy rain and waterlogging; **black, heavy, highly acidic**
- Soil erosion — rainfall **intensity** generally matters more than total annual rainfall
- **Conservation agriculture** — **retains crop residues** (does not remove them)

## SEC 4:43:3 | Water erosion sequence
- Water erosion stages — **splash → sheet → rill → gully**; **gully** = final, most advanced stage
- **Sheet erosion** — removes a **thin, uniform layer** of topsoil by **overland flow**; widespread, least noticeable
- **Tunnel erosion** — parts of **Australia and Africa**; subsurface removal may create **sinkholes**
- **FAO Status of the World's Soil Resources 2026** — soil-biodiversity loss ranks **4th of 7** threats, after **erosion, SOC loss and nutrient mismanagement**

## SEC 4:43:4 | Problem soils & remedies
- **Reh, kallar, usar** — local names for **saline and alkaline** soils (from over-irrigation, poor drainage)
- **Gypsum** — reclaims **alkaline/sodic** soils; **lime** — for **acidic** soils
  - Drainage, leaching and salt-tolerant crops also help reclamation

## SEC 4:43:5 | Conservation methods
- **RUSLE** — predicts **long-term water erosion on slopes**; ICAR prepared national **C- and P-factor maps** for it
- **Check dams** — trap sediment, check gully growth
- **Shelter belts** — tree rows that cut wind speed
- **Strip cropping** — alternating strips of erosion-resistant crops (e.g. **grass**) with **row crops**
- **Crop rotation** — improves **fertility**, reduces **pests**, helps prevent **erosion**
- Remedies — deforestation → **afforestation**; soil erosion → soil stabilisation; habitat destruction → restoration; biodiversity loss → conservation

## SEC 4:43:6 | Schemes
- **FAO 2026** — sustainable soil-management practices worsening in **58%** of assessments, improving in only about **10%**
- **IWMP** — began **2009-10**; merged into the Watershed Development Component of **PMKSY** in **2015-16**
- **Soil Health Card** — launched **19 February 2015** at **Suratgarh, Rajasthan**; cards every **two years**; slogan **'Swasth Dharaa, Khet Haraa'**
- Tests **12** parameters — N, P, K, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, B, pH, EC, organic carbon

## SEC 4:43:7 | Exam corner
- Generally **acidic** soils — **laterite, peaty, forest**; black and saline-alkaline soils are neutral to alkaline
- Very intense rain bursts can dry soil — they **exceed infiltration capacity**, so water runs off or evaporates instead of recharging
- **Andhra Pradesh** cultivated area — **red soils ~60%**, black ~25%, deltaic alluvium 5–6%, laterite ~1%

## SEC 4:43:8 | Soil — composition, classification and types
- ★ ICAR (USDA classification), decreasing area — **Entisols > Alfisols > Aridisols > Ultisols**
- **Soil order** — most general level of the **USDA** Soil Taxonomy
- **Entisols** — about **16%** of the world's ice-free land; on recently deposited parent materials or eroded areas

## SEC 4:43:9 | Soil terms → meaning: Khadar, Bhangar, Bhabar, Tarai and horizons
- ★ **Khadar** — **fertile**, ideal for intensive agriculture (the claim that khadar holds calcareous deposits is false)
- ★ **Bhabar** — a **piedmont** situation, south of the **Shiwalik**; narrow belt **8–16 km** at the break of slope where streams drop rock and boulders
  - **Tarai** belt lies south of Bhabar

## SEC 4:43:10 | Soil types → distribution, regions and crops: alluvial, black, red, laterite
- ★ **Lime** — makes **acidic** soil cultivable
- ★ **Bhangar** — the **old Kachhari clay** of the Gangetic plain; older alluvium above flood level; **calcareous concretions**; pale reddish brown
  - **Khadar** — newer, younger alluvium

## SEC 4:43:12 | Black, laterite, peaty and Karewa soils → properties and places
- ★ **Peaty soils** — areas of **heavy rainfall, high humidity and good vegetation**; organic matter builds faster than it decomposes; marshy, humid regions

## SEC 4:43:13 | Problems and remedies; salinity and waterlogging
- ★ Soil erosion prevented/checked by **afforestation**; also contour tillage, banning shifting cultivation

## SEC 4:43:14 | Problem soils — reclamation, land degradation and global status
- ★ **Contour bunding, relay cropping and zero tillage** — all help **carbon sequestration** in soil (keep more organic matter)
  - Contour bunding — widely used soil and water conservation technique in rainfed areas
- ★ Soil salinity and alkalinity — solved by **gypsum**; needs about **one foot of water** for leaching

## SEC 4:43:15 | Soil erosion — types and factors — Types of erosion
- ★ Increasing soil loss — **splash → sheet → rill → gully**
- ★ **Chambal Ravines** — formed by **gully** erosion
- ★ **Soil conservation** — protecting soil from harm; not only controlling erosion but keeping land fertility high

## SEC 4:43:16 | Soil erosion — types and factors — Factors controlling erosion rate
- ★ Long-term solution to soil salinity and alkalinity — **gypsum**

## SEC 4:43:17 | Soil conservation methods → how they work
- ★ **Contour bunding** — used in **hilly and sloping terrain**; small embankments along contour lines slow runoff
- ★ **Crop rotation** — essential for **increasing soil fertility**; different crops in different seasons so the same nutrients are not drained
- ★ **Seed modification** — does **not** improve soil fertility (crop rotation, mixed cultivation, multiple cropping do)
- **China's straw checkerboards** — **stabilise dunes**, control movement of desert sand

## SEC 4:44:1 | Ores → metal

## SEC 4:44:4 | Mica & deposit types
### Deposit types
- **Placer** deposits (alluvial sands) — **gold, tin, platinum**
- Sedimentary beds — coal, gypsum, salt; **veins/lodes** — tin, copper, zinc, lead
- **Bauxite** — by **residual weathering** (laterite-linked)
### Mica
- **Mica** — an **insulator**; in **pegmatite veins** in granite, gneiss, schist
- Major mica belts — **Nellore** (AP), **Koderma–Gaya–Hazaribagh**, **Ajmer–Bhilwara**
### Native state
- **Aluminium** — not found free (reactive; mainly as bauxite)
- **K, Na, Ca, Mg, Al** — top of reactivity series, never free; **Au, Ag, Pt** occur native
### Rare earths
- ★ Rare earth metals — group of **17** elements; all the world's **heavy rare earth** comes from **China**
- ★ **Mexico – Silver** — the pair keyed as not correctly matched in the PYQ

### Critical minerals
