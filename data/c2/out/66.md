# ROW 3:51 | Agricultural strategy and policy - National Agriculture Policy, doubling farmers' income, National Missions
## SEC 3:51:0 | Phases & policy milestones
### Phases and commissions
- Early Green Revolution (1968-81): HYV seeds first in **wheat**; Later phase (1987-92) spread it to rice and other regions
- **National Commission on Agriculture** (1970–76, report **1976**) — chaired by **Nathu Ram Mirdha**
  - recommended HYVs, irrigation, credit reforms; coined **social forestry**
- **National Commission on Farmers** (M.S. Swaminathan) — MSP at **C2 + 50%**
  - Government's present MSP policy: **1.5 times A2+FL**
### National Agriculture Policy, 2000
- Growth target: **more than 4%** per annum
- Broad-based **Rainbow Revolution** across farm sub-sectors; private participation, price protection, insurance
- **Silver Revolution** — eggs; **Grey Revolution** — fertilisers
### Doubling income, rainfed areas, missions
- **Ashok Dalwai Committee** (Doubling Farmers' Income) — base year **2015-16**, target year **2022**
- Rainfed farming — **50–60%** of net sown area, about **40%** of food production
- **NRAA** (2006) — expert body: knowledge inputs, coordinates upgrading of dryland/rainfed areas through a **watershed approach**
- **NMSA** — Krishonnati sub-mission from **2018-19**; under **PM-RKVY** from 2022-23
  - components: RAD, Soil Health Management, Per Drop More Crop; **National Agricultural Market** is not one
- **PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana** (2025-26) — Central Sector scheme for **100 low-performing districts**, **six years**

## SEC 3:51:1 | Doubling Farmers' Income
- Seven sources of income growth: crop productivity, livestock productivity, resource-use efficiency, cropping intensity, diversification to **high-value crops**, better price realisation, movement to **non-farm jobs**
  - exclusive dependence on traditional low-value crops is not a source
- Shift from **production** to **income**: value addition, e-NAM and APMC reform, post-harvest infrastructure, allied integration

## SEC 3:51:2 | WTO subsidy boxes
- India's WTO entry (**1995**) — increased **import competition**; subsidies declined as a share of farm income
| Box | Nature |
|---|---|
| **Amber** | trade/production-distorting; **reduction commitments**; de minimis **5%** developed, **10%** developing |
| **Blue** | Amber-type support with **production-limiting** conditions |
| **Green** | minimal distortion |
- Amber Box: **price support**, production-linked subsidies
- Green Box: decoupled income support, environmental programmes, farmer training

# ROW 3:52 | Agrarian crisis - causes, farmer distress and suicides, loan waivers, small-holding viability
## SEC 3:52:0 | Causes of agrarian crisis
- Post-harvest loss of fruits and vegetables — up to **40%**; cold-chain deficit about 30%
- Ideal N:P:K ratio — **4:2:1**
- Over-exploited groundwater blocks — about **17%**; groundwater supplies about 64% of irrigation
- Small-holding viability: **Custom Hiring Centres**, **cluster farming**, cooperative or joint farming
- Actual cultivators missing from **e-crop data** can lose insurance and relief

## SEC 3:52:1 | Debt cycle
- Debt cycle: high **input cost** → borrowing → crop failure or low price → inability to repay → fresh borrowing → **asset sale** → **debt trap**

## SEC 3:52:2 | Holdings & debt data
### Holdings
- Agriculture Census **2015-16** — semi-medium and medium holdings: **13.2%** of holdings, **43.6%** of operated area
- Agricultural Census **2010-11** — small holdings about **85%** of holdings, about **44%** of operated area
### NSSO 77th Round SAS
- Relates to **2019**; about half of agricultural households indebted; average debt about **₹74,121**
- Indebted households — **AP 93.2%**, **Telangana 91.7%**
- Largest income source — **wages**, then crop cultivation, then livestock
### Guntur study and costs
- Average debt — tenants **₹2,07,444**, owners ₹76,306
- Informal sources — **51.5%** of loans; about 60% of tenants' loans informal
- NABARD Guntur study: **tenants** carry higher debt than owners; about **50%** of AP farmers rely on non-institutional credit (not the explanation for tenants' higher debt)
- **C2** = A2 + imputed family labour + **imputed rent and interest** on owned land and capital
### Schemes and cards
- **PM-KISAN** — Central Sector scheme since February 2019; **three instalments of ₹2,000** into Aadhaar-seeded accounts
- **CCR cards** (AP, from 2 October 2019) — valid **11 months**; need owner-cultivator agreement; **do not transfer ownership**

## SEC 3:52:3 | Farmer suicides (NCRB ADSI)
### Suicide data
- Official source — NCRB's **Accidental Deaths and Suicides in India** (ADSI)
- Farmer suicides concentrated mainly in **rain-fed belts**, not irrigated ones
- ADSI 2015 — biggest cause: **bankruptcy or indebtedness** (crop failure, family problems, illness ranked lower)
- AP **Microfinance Institutions (Regulation of Money Lending) Act** — **2011**, after the 2010 microfinance crisis
### Tenant distress in AP
- Major obstacle for tenants — requirement of the **landowner's signature**
- AP pioneer in tenant finance: **RMGs** (Rythu Mitra Groups), **JLGs**, **CCRCs**
- Tenants pay rent and carry **production risk**, often without passbook, **KCC** or insurance

# ROW 3:53 | Land reforms - abolition of intermediaries, tenancy reform, ceiling laws, consolidation; outcomes
## SEC 3:53:0 | Four measures
### Ceiling
- ★ **Land ceiling** — legal **maximum** land an individual/family may hold; surplus redistributed to the landless
- Ceiling laws (1950s, early 1970s) redistributed **less than 2%** of cultivable area
- Reason: exemptions for **plantations, orchards, mechanised farms**
- Land reforms reduce **inequality** because ceilings free **surplus land** for the landless
- AP Ceiling Act, 1973 — husband's and wife's lands **clubbed together**
### Tenancy and consolidation
- Rent ceiling — **one-fourth to one-sixth** of produce
- **Operation Barga** (West Bengal, **Left Front**) — recorded bargadars got **heritable rights** and **75%** of produce
- Consolidation succeeded in **Punjab, Haryana, western UP**; little progress in southern and eastern States

## SEC 3:53:1 | Ceiling: before vs after 1972
- Pre-1972 unit: **individual** (encouraged benami transfers); **1972** guidelines: **family**

# ROW 3:54 | Land records and titling - Digital India Land Records Modernisation, SVAMITVA
## SEC 3:54:0 | Key terms
- **ULPIN** (Bhu-Aadhaar) — **14-digit alphanumeric** ID from geo-coordinates of a parcel's vertices
- **N-LRSI** (NCAER) — grades States on **digitisation and quality** of land records
- Clear titles — land as **collateral**, less **litigation**
- **Land Stack** (National Digital Land Initiative) — cloud-based GIS; a **Bhu-Aadhaar** for every parcel

## SEC 3:54:1 | Presumptive vs conclusive titling
- **Conclusive** system — State **guarantees** title
- **Presumptive** system — registration only records a transaction; title can be challenged

## SEC 3:54:2 | DILRMP phases
### Origin and status
- Began as **NLRMP** in **2008** — merged **CLR** and **SRA&ULR**
- **Central Sector Scheme** since **1 April 2016** (centrally sponsored only before)
### Phases (DILRMP 3.0)
| Phase | Content |
|---|---|
| Phase 1 (2008-16) | digitisation and resurvey |
| Phase 2 (2016-26) | **ULPIN**, **NGDRS**, multilingual records, **e-Court** integration |
| Phase 3 | GIS-based **Land Stack** |
### Related tools and AP
- Bhoomi (Karnataka), Bhu Bharathi (AP) — State projects; SVAMITVA (2020), NAKSHA — later schemes
- AP resurvey (Jagananna Saswatha Bhu Hakku and Bhu Raksha) — from **December 2020**; drones, CORS, GIS; **6,901 villages**, 81 lakh parcels

## SEC 3:54:3 | DILRMP components & progress
- Extended from 2021-22 to **2025-26**; new components: consent-based **Aadhaar seeding** of land records, computerisation of **revenue courts**
- RoR digitised **99.8%**; cadastral maps 97.24%; SROs computerised 95.73%

## SEC 3:54:4 | SVAMITVA
- **SVAMITVA** — Survey of Villages and Mapping with Improvised Technology in Village Areas
- Launched **24 April 2020**, **National Panchayati Raj Day**; pilot 2020, nationwide 2021
- **Ministry of Panchayati Raj**; **Central Sector** scheme
- Implementing agency **Survey of India**; technology support **NICSI**
- Covers rural **abadi** areas, not agricultural land
- Target **6.62 lakh villages**, Rs 566.23 crore; over 2.76 crore property cards

## SEC 3:54:5 | Titling acts & ruling
- **AP Land Titling Act** — State as **guarantor** of titles; shift from **presumptive to conclusive** titling
- NITI Aayog **Model Conclusive Land Titling Act, 2020** — adoption is **each State's choice**; reduces disputes, eases credit, lowers transaction costs
- Supreme Court (**1 January 2026**) — registration of a transfer deed is **distinct from title**; Bihar's mandatory mutation-proof rule not upheld

## SEC 3:54:6 | AP land records
- **Bhu Bharathi** — land-record computerisation, united AP, **1992**
- **CARD** — **anywhere registration**, **auto-mutation**, registration at Grama Sachivalayams; not a court

## SEC 3:54:9 | Schemes → origin, funding and components — DILRMP and SVAMITVA — DILRMP origin and funding

# ROW 3:57 | Minimum Support Price - mechanism, CACP, crops covered, procurement, criticism; PM-AASHA
## SEC 3:57:0 | How MSP is fixed
- **MSP** — Government-announced **minimum price** for selected crops
- Market price below MSP — **government agencies purchase** the stock offered; guards against distress sales
- **CACP recommends**; **Government of India** (CCEA) fixes MSP
- MSP protects farmers against a sharp **fall in market prices**
- CACP considers **domestic and international** prices
- **CCEA** (not CACP) approves sugarcane **FRP**
- Coarse grains for the Central Pool — **State agencies** with prior GoI approval
- MSP procurement builds **PDS** stocks — linked to **food security**
| PM-AASHA component | Nature |
|---|---|
| **PSS** | physical procurement |
| **PDPS** | pays price deficiency; no procurement |
| **PPSS** | 2018 pilot; private agencies procured oilseeds at MSP |

## SEC 3:57:1 | CACP
- **APC** set up **1965**; renamed **CACP** in **1985**
- CACP — **attached office** of the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare; **not statutory**
- MSP — **administrative decision**, announced before sowing; not backed by statute
- **Market Intervention Scheme** — triggered by price fall of **10%** or more, or production rise of 10% or more

## SEC 3:57:2 | Cost concepts
- **A2+FL** — paid-out costs + imputed **family labour**; excludes rent on owned land and interest on fixed capital
- **C2** — A2 + FL + **land rent** + interest on fixed capital and other costs (fullest cost)
- Present MSP — at least **1.5 times A2+FL**; **C2 + 50%** was the Swaminathan Commission's recommendation

## SEC 3:57:3 | 22 mandated crops
### Coverage
- **22** mandated MSP crops + sugarcane **FRP** = 23
- Split: **14 kharif**, **6 rabi**, **2 commercial**
- Also stated: **7 cereals**, **5 pulses**, **7 oilseeds**, **3 commercial** (raw jute, cotton, copra)
- **Gram** — pulse; paddy — cereal; groundnut — oilseed; copra — commercial
- Open-ended procurement — **wheat** and paddy (FCI and State agencies); pulses and oilseeds limited PSS
- Pulses mission (PM-AASHA) — 100% MSP procurement for **four years** of **tur, urad, masoor** via NAFED and NCCF
### Context
- **Tobacco** — cash crop (like cotton, sugarcane, oilseeds); rice, millets, pulses subsistence crops
- **Bt cotton** approved **2002**
- **WTO membership** — post-1991 phase, not Pre-Green Revolution
