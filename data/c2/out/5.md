# ROW 1:37 | Mauryan administration - central, provincial and local; espionage, judiciary, revenue system
## SEC 1:37:0 | Saptanga (Kautilya's order)
### Saptanga and the king
- Standard order — **Swami**, Amatya, Janapada, Durga, Kosha, Danda/Bala, Mitra; **Swami** comes first
- **Shatru** — NOT one of the standard seven; some lists add it only as an eighth item
- **Mantriparishad** — council of ministers that checked the king's authority, with paura-janapada assemblies and public opinion ("a chariot cannot move on one wheel")
### Provinces and districts
| Province | Capital |
|---|---|
| Dakshinapatha | **Suvarnagiri** |
| Magadha | Pataliputra |
| Uttarapatha | Taxila |
| Avantirashtra | Ujjain |

- Chandragupta's four provinces — ruled by **Kumaras** or governors
- Districts — called **Vish** or **Ahara**
### Officials and law
- **Gramika** — village head (village level)
- **Rajukas**, Pradeshikas, Yuktas — district-level officials
- **Rajukas** — land survey and assessment, with **judicial powers**
- **Yuktas** — subordinate revenue or secretarial officers
- **Megasthenes** — Mauryan law had **no written code**
- Punishments — fines, mutilation and death; severity varied by **varna**

  - ★ Agronomoi — district officers; **Shulkadhyaksha** — trade and service taxes; Akradhyaksha — mines

## SEC 1:37:12 | Local, city, army and spy officers → roles

- ★ **Registration of births and deaths** — municipal function continuous from Mauryan times (Megasthenes' **third committee**)
- ★ City administration — municipalities under a **Nagrak** or **Purmukhya**; Megasthenes: **6 committees** of **5 members** each

# ROW 1:38 | Mauryan economy, society and urban life; Mauryan art and architecture (pillars, Sarnath capital, Barabar caves, Didarganj Yakshi)
## SEC 1:38:0 | Economy
### Agriculture and state control
- **Sita land** — crown land cultivated directly for the state under the **Sitadhyaksha**, using slaves and hired labourers too (not only free peasants)
- Famine/drought relief — state gave **seed, cattle and tools**
- **State monopoly or control** — mining, forestry, salt, liquor, arms and metallurgy
- **Rainfall records** — predate Korea's **Cheugugi** rain gauge (**1441 CE**)
### Trade, credit, pottery
- **NBPW** (Northern Black Polished Ware) — typical Mauryan pottery
- Arthashastra interest rate — **15 percent** a year
- **Sarthavaha** — leader of trade caravans; state took **road tax** for protecting them
### Labour and slavery
- **Megasthenes** — no **slavery** in India; described hereditary occupations and endogamy
- Arthashastra slavery — by **birth, sale, capture in war, judicial punishment**
- **Karmakara** — hired wage labourer; **Vishti** — unpaid forced labour for the state; **shreni** — guild
### Society and religion
- **Moksha** — divorce for a wife deserted by her husband (Arthashastra); anuloma/pratiloma — inter-varna marriage types
- Religious policy — **liberal and tolerant**, freedom of faith, while protecting **varna and ashrama**
- **Kautilya** — Brahmana called **dvijarshabh**
- **Chandalas and Shvapakas** — untouchable groups that grew in number
### Sites
- Pataliputra palace — Megasthenes compared it to **Susa and Ecbatana**; Fa-Hien admired it in the 5th c. CE
- **Balirajgarh** fort — **Madhubani** district, Bihar; ASI excavation, punch-marked coins; Mauryan to Pala habitation

## SEC 1:38:1 | Trade routes & ports
- Good roads and state incentives — encouraged **crafts**
- Merchants, artisans and **shrenis** — financed early Buddhist centres, including in **Andhra**
- **Uttarapatha** — northern highway, **Taxila** through **Pataliputra** to **Tamralipti**
- **Dakshinapatha** — great southern trade route; the **Krishna–Godavari delta** was a node
- **Tamralipti** — **east-coast** port, principal port of the Ganga basin
- **Sopara** and **Bharukachchha** — west-coast ports
- **Berenice** (Bernis) — Red Sea port of trade with **Egypt**

## SEC 1:38:2 | Society
### Megasthenes' classes
- ★ **Seven** classes (Indica) — philosophers, farmers, herdsmen/hunters, artisans and traders, soldiers, overseers/spies, councillors and assessors
### Arthashastra on society
- **Antyavasayins** — five mixed castes outside Aryan society; **Sishtas** — learned men
- **Nine** categories of slaves (dasas) in the Arthashastra
- Son of a female slave by her master — entitled to the legal status of the **master's son**
- **Stridhana** — a woman's own property, including jewellery and maintenance
### Learning and coins
- **Taxila** — most famous centre of learning, ahead of Ujjain and Varanasi
- Mauryan currency — mainly **punch-marked silver** coins with symbols (peacocks, hills), **no portraits**

## SEC 1:38:3 | Popular art: yakshas
- **Manibhadra Yaksha** (inscribed) — **Parkham** near Mathura
- Chauri-bearer Yakshi — **Didarganj**; a Yakshi — **Besnagar**; a Yaksha — **Noh**
- Yaksha worship — shared by **Jainism, Buddhism and Hinduism**
- Chronology — **Dhauli elephant** (3rd c. BCE), Udayagiri Varaha (Gupta), Mahabalipuram (Pallava), Lingaraja Temple

## SEC 1:38:4 | Pillar & capital
- Mauryan pillars — **monolithic**, free-standing, circular shafts of **Chunar sandstone** with the lustrous **Mauryan polish**
- Parts, bottom to top — **shaft**, bell-shaped inverted lotus, abacus, animal capital

### Stupas
- ★ **Sanchi Stupa** — built by **Ashoka**
- **Sanchi Stupa** — oldest stone structure vs Ajanta (Satavahana–Vakataka), Brihadeeswara (Chola, 11th c.), Konark (13th c.)

### Barabar and Nagarjuni caves
- ★ **Ajivikas** — used the Barabar caves; **Ashoka** and **Dasaratha** donated Barabar and Nagarjuni caves to them

- **Satghar** ("Seven Houses") — the seven caves together
- **Barabar** — Lomas Rishi, Karna Chopar; **Nagarjuni** — Gopika, Vadathika
- Inscriptions (Brahmi) — call Ashoka **King Piyadasi**; Mauryan, 3rd c. BCE

# ROW 1:39 | Decline of the Mauryan Empire - competing explanations
## SEC 1:39:0 | Theories of decline
| Theory | Historian |
|---|---|
| Financial crisis | **D. D. Kosambi** |
| Brahmanical reaction | **H. P. Shastri** |
| Pacifism | **Raychaudhuri** |
| Over-centralisation | **Romila Thapar** |

## SEC 1:39:1 | Evidence & counter-points
- **Great Wall of China** (c. **220 BC**) — diverted **Scythian** tribes west and south towards India
- Financial crisis evidence — **debasement** of later punch-marked silver coins (more alloy)
- Treasury strain — Ashoka's **Dhamma missions** and donations to the **Sangha**
- **Divyavadana** — calls **Pushyamitra** a persecutor of Buddhists; growth of **Sanchi and Bharhut** under Sunga rule points the other way

## SEC 1:39:2 | Later Mauryas: divided empire
- Western part — **Kunala**, later **Samprati**; eastern part — **Dasharatha** through **Brihadratha**
- Western later Mauryas supplanted by the **Indo-Greeks** by about **180 BC**
- Indo-Greek raids/pressure — a cause; a permanent alliance with them was NOT

## SEC 1:39:3 | End of the dynasty
- **Pushyamitra Sunga** — founded the Sunga dynasty in Magadha
- Mauryan commander-in-chief; killed **Brihadratha** during an **army review** (military coup)
- Sunga dynasty — **187 BC** (some say about 185 BCE)

## SEC 1:39:4 | Invaders & breakaway regions
- **Demetrius** and **Menander** — Bactrian (Indo-Greek) rulers who threatened the north-west after Ashoka
- **Antiochus III** — crossed the Hindu Kush about **206 BC**; tribute from **Subhagasena**
- Andhra/Deccan — broke away under the **Satavahanas**, earlier Mauryan feudatories
- Puranas call the Satavahanas **Andhra-bhrityas**
- **Yajnasena** (Malavikagnimitram) — **Vidarbha**

## SEC 1:39:5 | Successor dynasties
### Sungas and Kanvas
- Magadha order — **Mauryas – Sungas – Kanvas**
- **Pushyamitra Sunga** — Brahmana of the **Bharadvaja** gotra; a **senapati**
- Two **Ashvamedhas** of Pushyamitra — **Ayodhya inscription of Dhanadeva**
- **Vasumitra** (grandson of Pushyamitra) — defeated a **Yavana** army on the **Sindhu** (Malavikagnimitram)
- **Vasudeva Kanva** (Brahmana minister) — killed **Devabhuti**, last Sunga, and founded the Kanva dynasty
- **Susharman** — last Kanva; ended by the **Andhras (Satavahanas)**, not Menander
### Kharavela and the Satavahanas
- **Hathigumpha** inscription — year-by-year record of **Kharavela**'s reign; Udayagiri-Khandagiri caves, Odisha; 2nd c. BC
- Kharavela raided **Magadha** and recovered the **Jina image** taken by the Nandas
- Kharavela sent an army west **ignoring Satakarni** (no alliance)
- **Nanaghat** inscription — Satavahana queen **Naganika**
- **Simuka** (Srimukha) — founder of the Satavahana dynasty

## SEC 1:39:9 | Foreign invaders and breakaway regions → powers
- ★ **Malavikagnimitra** — love story of the son of the Sunga founder
- ★ **Kharavela** — leaned heavily towards **Jainism**

## SEC 1:39:12 | Kharavela of Kalinga — Hathigumpha inscription
- ★ **Udayagiri and Khandagiri** (Bhubaneswar, Odisha) — both **natural and artificial** caves
- ★ **Udayagiri** — **18** caves; **Khandagiri** — **15** caves
- ★ **Hathigumpha** inscription of Kharavela — from **Udayagiri**, not Khandagiri
