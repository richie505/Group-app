 MECHANICS

 • Friction opposes (retards) motion
    Friction acts opposite to relative motion; static friction is greater than kinetic friction, and rolling friction is least.
    Lubricants and ball bearings reduce it.

 • Inertia is a property of mass
    Mass is a measure of inertia; Newton's first law is the law of inertia. Heavier bodies resist changes in motion more.

 • With no external force, the centre-of-mass velocity stays constant
    Total momentum is conserved, so the centre of mass moves uniformly (or stays at rest if it was at rest).

 • Doubling momentum raises kinetic energy by 300%
