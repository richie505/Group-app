    Digambara Jain teacher, wrote Samayasara in Prakrit. Kutuhala's Prakrit Lilavati describes Hala's marriage with
    Lilavati; Sarvavarma wrote the simple Sanskrit grammar Katantra for a Satavahana king.
    Group-1 Prelims 08.01.2023 (B2 p.140 Q4)

 • Ketana translated Mitakshara into Telugu as Vijnaneswariyamu
    Ketana, a disciple of Tikkana, rendered Vijnaneswara's Mitakshara into Telugu, the first Telugu work on
    dharmasastra. He also wrote Dasakumara Charitra (title Abhinava Dandi) and Andhra Bhasha Bhushanamu, an early
    Telugu grammar.
    Trap: Baddena wrote Sumati Satakam; Madiki Singana wrote Sakala Neeti Sammatamu.
    Group-1 Re-exam 2010 (B2 p.142 Q14)

 • Kridabhiramamu gives a detailed description of Warangal
    Vinukonda Vallabharaya wrote this Telugu adaptation of Ravipati Tripurantaka's Sanskrit play Premabhiramam. Two
    friends tour Orugallu, so it describes the city's streets, temples and social life.
    Group-1 2004 (Backlog) (B2 p.144 Q32)

 • Machaldevi, a court dancer, appears in Kridabhiramamu
    Kridabhiramamu mentions the courtesan-dancer Machaladevi of Prataparudra's time in Orugallu.
    Group-1 2004 (Backlog) (B2 p.144 Q34)
