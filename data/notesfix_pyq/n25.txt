 • Mapping the sea floor (measuring depth) is called bathymetry
    It uses echo sounders and multibeam sonar. Oceanography is the wider study of oceans.
    Group-2 Prelims 2019 (B1 p.71 Q41)

 • A guyot is a flat-topped seamount showing gradual subsidence
    Seamount: pointed peak not reaching surface; submarine canyon: deep valley across shelf; atoll: coral ring around
    lagoon.
    Group-1 Prelims 2024 Paper-I (GS) (B3 p.17 Q99)

 • Terrigenous deposits come from land, settle on continental shelves and get finer away from the shore
    Muds are the finest terrigenous deposits; their colour depends on chemical content.
    Trap: Coarseness decreases, not increases, from shore.
    Group-1 Prelims 2024 Paper-I (GS) (B3 p.17 Q100)

 • Ocean floor relief is shown by a hypsometric (hypsographic) curve
    It shows the proportion of earth's surface at various elevations and depths.
    Group-1 Screening Test 2018 Paper-I (GS) (B3 p.76 Q93)

 • Humboldt (Peru) current is cold; Gulf Stream, Kuroshio, Agulhas are warm
    Cold currents flow along western coasts of continents in tropics.
    Group-1 Screening Test 2018 Paper-I (GS) (B3 p.77 Q103)

 OCEANS

 • The Pacific is the largest and deepest ocean
    It covers about one-third of the Earth's surface and contains the Mariana Trench (Challenger Deep, about 11 km).
    The Atlantic is second largest.
    Group-1 Prelims 2010 (Sep) (B3 p.149 Q6)

 • Offshore oil deposits are mostly on the continental shelf
    Shallow shelves accumulate thick marine sediments. Bombay High and the KG basin are shelf fields.
    Group-1 Prelims 2008 (B3 p.168 Q26)

 • The Sargasso Sea (not the Yellow Sea) is the coastless sea
    The Sargasso Sea in the North Atlantic is bounded by currents (Gulf Stream, Canary, North Equatorial) and noted for
    sargassum weed. The other statements given (January hottest in the southern hemisphere; stratosphere as
    ozonosphere; Equator a great circle) are correct.
    Group-1 Prelims 2008 (B3 p.170 Q65)

 • The Bermuda Triangle in the western Atlantic is linked to vanished planes and ships
    It lies between Miami, Bermuda and Puerto Rico; scientific studies attribute losses to storms, currents and human
    error.
    Group-1 Prelims 2004 (Mar) (B3 p.207 Q84)

 • Among Dead Sea, Caspian Sea, Utah Lake and Red Sea, the highest salinity is in the Red Sea (book answer)
    Salinity is high where evaporation is strong and fresh-water inflow is low. The Red Sea, enclosed and lying in a hot
    dry belt, is the saltiest of the open seas; the book's key marks it.
    Trap: Distractors: Dead Sea, Caspian Sea and Utah Lake. Note: some standard sources give the Dead Sea (about 340 parts
    per thousand).
    Group-1 Prelims 2002 (B3 p.224 Q96)

 • Major fishing grounds lie where warm and cold currents meet
    Mixing brings nutrients and plankton; examples are the Grand Banks (Gulf Stream and Labrador current) and waters
    off Japan (Kuroshio and Oyashio).
    Group-1 Prelims 2002 (B3 p.224 Q102)

 TIDES

 • High tide recurs about 52 minutes later each day (8:00 am to about 8:52 am)
    The Moon returns overhead every 24 h 52 min, so tides shift later by roughly 50-52 minutes daily; there are two
    high tides about 12 h 26 min apart.
    Group-1 Prelims 2008 (B3 p.168 Q30)
