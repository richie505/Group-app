 Population & Census (16)
 CENSUS & DEMOGRAPHY

 • As per Census 2011, the child sex ratio (0-6 years) of Andhra Pradesh is 929
                       The child sex ratio is the number of girls per 1,000 boys aged 0-6. The book records AP's 2011 figure as 929,
                       compared with the national figure of 918.
                       Trap: 890, 940 and 849 are the distractors; 940 is close to AP's overall sex ratio figure and can confuse.
                       Group-2 Prelims 2019 (B1 p.75 Q113)

 • India's population explosion phase was 1951-81
                       Falling death rates with high birth rates gave over 2% annual growth. 1921 is the 'year of great divide' and 1921-51
                       was a period of steady growth.
                       Group-2 Prelims 2019 (B1 p.77 Q142)

 • Himachal Pradesh has the highest share of Hindus (about 95%) by Census 2011
                       Odisha and Chhattisgarh also have very high shares. Nationally, Hindus were 79.8% in 2011.
                       Group-2 Prelims 2019 (B1 p.77 Q143)

 • Demographic dividend refers to growth in the working-age population (15-59 or 15-64)
                       It boosts growth when the working-age share rises and the dependency ratio falls. India's window is expected to last
                       until about 2055.
                       Group-2 Prelims 2019 (B1 p.77 Q144)

 CENSUS & POPULATION

 • India launched the Census 2011 on 1 March 2010 (book answer)
                       Census 2011 was India's 15th census and the 7th after Independence, with the slogan 'Our Census, Our Future'. The
                       book gives 1 March 2010 as its launch date; population enumeration followed in February 2011.
                       Trap: Distractors are the 1st of January, February and April 2010; the houselisting phase start date is the usual confusion.
                       Group-1 Prelims 2010 (Sep) (B3 p.154 Q124)

 • About 62.9% of India's population was in the 15-64 working age group (then)
                       This large share is the demographic dividend. The share has since risen above 65%.
                       Group-1 Prelims 2008 (B3 p.171 Q80)

 • Census 2001: UP, then Maharashtra, then Bihar were most populous
                       The same top three hold in Census 2011, followed by West Bengal and undivided AP.
                       Group-1 Prelims 2004 (Oct) (B3 p.194 Q149)

 • In 1991-2001 Nagaland had the highest and Kerala the lowest population growth
                       Nagaland's decadal growth was about 64% and Kerala's about 9.4%. In 2001-2011 Nagaland recorded negative
                       growth and Meghalaya was highest.
                       Group-1 Prelims 2004 (Mar) (B3 p.204 Q32)

 CENSUS & WORKFORCE

 • Census 2011: total workforce about 482 million, male workforce about 332 million
                       Workers include main and marginal workers. The work participation rate was about 39.8%. Female participation
                       stayed low at about 25.5%.
                       Group-2 Prelims 2019 (B1 p.70 Q21)

 Family, Marriage & Kinship (9)
 CHILD MARRIAGE

 • ALIVA, a data-driven approach against child marriage, was adopted by Nayagarh district of Odisha
    Nayagarh used village-level data on adolescent girls to track and prevent child marriages, and it was recognised as a
    good governance practice. Odisha aims to become child-marriage free under its state strategy.
    Group-2 Prelims 2024 (B1 p.7 Q61)

 DOWRY

 • The Dowry Prohibition Act 1961 came into force on 1 July 1961
    It prohibits giving or taking dowry; later amendments (1984, 1986) made it stricter. Dowry death is a separate
    offence under Section 304B IPC (now in the BNS).
    Group-2 Prelims 2024 (B1 p.8 Q77)

 HINDU MARRIAGE LAW

 • Section 5(v) of the Hindu Marriage Act bars marriage between sapindas unless custom permits
    The Delhi High Court upheld its validity in 2024. Section 5(iv) bars marriage within degrees of prohibited
    relationship. Sapinda relation extends three generations on the mother's side and five on the father's.
    Group-2 Prelims 2024 (B1 p.9 Q86)

 KINSHIP

 • Consanguineous kinship is blood relation, e.g., between Krishna and his niece Jyothi
    Kinship is of two kinds: affinal (through marriage, e.g., husband-wife, in-laws) and consanguineous (through
    blood/descent). Krishna and Jyothi share common ancestors (Ravindra-Seema), so their bond is consanguineous.
    Trap: Ravindra-Seema and Madhavi-Harish are affinal; Krishna-Harish are in-laws.
    Group-2 Prelims 2024 (B1 p.7 Q66)

 MARRIAGE & FAMILY

 • Divorce facts: W.P. Scott's definition, Parsi Marriage and Divorce Act, desertion as 'poor man's divorce'
    Desertion by the husband is popularly called the poor man's divorce since it avoids legal costs. The book treats all
    three statements as correct. Note that the Indian Divorce Act of 1869 (for Christians) is also an early divorce law; the
    Parsi Marriage and Divorce Act dates from 1865 (re-enacted 1936).
    Trap: Statement II's year (1869) actually fits the Indian Divorce Act; check the official key.
    Group-2 Prelims 2024 (B1 p.7 Q65)

 • Sambandham was the informal consensual union under Kerala's matrilineal system among Nairs and
   Namboothiris
    Under Marumakkathayam, children belonged to the mother's tharavad. Younger Namboothiri sons often entered
    sambandham with Nair women. It was regulated by laws like the Malabar Marriage Act and declined in the 20th
    century.
    Group-2 Prelims 2024 (B1 p.9 Q85)

 • Niyoga was the ancient custom allowing a childless widow or wife of an impotent man to conceive through
   a designated male
    It is mentioned in the Dharmashastras and epics; the Mahabharata births of Dhritarashtra and Pandu are classic
    examples.
    Group-2 Prelims 2024 (B1 p.9 Q88)

 MUSLIM PERSONAL LAW

 • After the 2017 triple talaq verdict, Talaq-e-Hasan was challenged in the Supreme Court
    In Shayara Bano (2017) instant triple talaq (talaq-e-biddat) was struck down; the 2019 Act made it a crime.
    Talaq-e-Hasan involves pronouncing talaq once a month over three months, and petitions argue it is discriminatory.
    Trap: Talaq-e-Ahsan is a single pronouncement followed by iddat.
    Group-2 Prelims 2024 (B1 p.8 Q74)

 TRIBAL MARRIAGE CUSTOMS

 • 'Diku Andi' refers to marriage by negotiation among the Ho tribe
    Among the Ho of Jharkhand, 'Andi' is the formally arranged marriage involving bride price, regarded as the
    prestigious form. Other forms include marriage by capture and by elopement.
    Group-2 Prelims 2024 (B1 p.7 Q63)


 Women & Child Issues (19)
 CHILD LABOUR

 • PENCIL portal enforces the Child and Adolescent Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Act
    PENCIL (Platform for Effective Enforcement for No Child Labour) was launched by the Ministry of Labour in 2017. It
    links central, state and district authorities, and supports complaint handling and rescue under the 2016 amended
    Act.
    Group-2 Prelims 2024 (B1 p.7 Q64)

 CHILD RIGHTS

 • POCSO = Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012
    2019 amendment added death penalty for aggravated penetrative sexual assault.
    Group-1 Screening Test 2018 Paper-II (GS & Aptitude) (B3 p.91 Q109)

 CHILD WELFARE

 • Creches for children of working mothers were started by the Central Social Welfare Board.
    They provide day care for children of 0-6 years; later continued as the Rajiv Gandhi National Creche Scheme and
    now Palna under Mission Shakti.
    Group-2 Mains 2019 Paper-1 (B1 p.89 Q36; answer p.97)

 CHILD WELFARE SCHEMES

 • PM CARES for Children was launched on 29 May 2021 for children orphaned by COVID-19
    It covers children who lost both parents, a surviving parent, a legal guardian or adoptive parents from 11 March
    2020. It gives a monthly stipend from age 18 and Rs 10 lakh at age 23, plus education and health insurance support.
    Trap: Statements I and II are incorrect; III is correct.
    Group-2 Prelims 2024 (B1 p.9 Q81)

 • Mission Vatsalya is the centrally sponsored scheme for child protection aligned with SDGs
    It subsumed the earlier Integrated Child Protection Scheme (ICPS) and supports child care institutions, foster care,
    adoption and Childline.
    Group-2 Prelims 2024 (B1 p.9 Q87)

 ICDS

 • NIPCCD trains Child Development Project Officers (CDPOs).

 WOMEN & CHILD SAFETY

 • Cri-MAC (Crime Multi Agency Centre) of MHA shares information on serious crimes in real time

 SC/ST, Tribes & Minorities (11)
 DISABILITY

 • The Inclusive India Initiative (2016) aims to bring people with intellectual and developmental disabilities
   into the mainstream.
    It is run by the National Trust under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.
    Mains 2017 Paper-I (second set labelled 'held 15-07-2017' in book) (B1 p.165 Q118)

 DISABILITY LAWS

 • The Rehabilitation Council of India Act, 1992 regulates training of rehabilitation professionals.
    RCI maintains a Central Rehabilitation Register. Other disability laws: PwD Act 1995 replaced by RPwD Act 2016,
    National Trust Act 1999.
    Group-2 Mains 2019 Paper-2 (held 19-07-2019) (B1 p.107 Q79)

 MANUAL SCAVENGING

 • Bandicoot is the robot used to replace manual scavenging in manholes
    Developed by Kerala start-up Genrobotics, Bandicoot cleans sewer manholes. Manual scavenging is banned under
    the 2013 Act. Vyommitra is ISRO's humanoid for Gaganyaan.
    Group-2 Prelims 2024 (B1 p.7 Q68)

 MINORITIES

 • Sachar Committee said Ajlaf Muslims need attention similar to Hindu OBCs
    The Sachar Committee (2006) on Muslim socio-economic status classified Muslims as Ashraf (upper), Ajlaf
    (converted occupational/backward groups, like OBCs) and Arzal (converts from lowest castes, like SCs). Arzal were
    suggested to be treated like SCs.
    Trap: Arzal correspond to SCs, not OBCs.
    Group-2 Prelims 2024 (B1 p.7 Q67)

 SC/ST ISSUES

 • SCs were about 16.2% of India's population in Census 2001 (key option 17.5%)
    STs were about 8.2% in 2001. In Census 2011 SCs were 16.6% and STs 8.6%.
    Trap: Exact 2001 figure is 16.2%; 8.6% is the 2011 ST share.
    Group-1 Prelims 2008 (B3 p.171 Q73)

 SC/ST PROTECTION

 • Subhash Kashinath Mahajan vs State of Maharashtra (2018) diluted the SC/ST Atrocities Act
    The Supreme Court allowed anticipatory bail and preliminary inquiry before arrest. After protests, Parliament
    passed the SC/ST (PoA) Amendment Act 2018 to restore strict provisions, which the Court upheld in 2020.
    Group-2 Prelims 2024 (B1 p.7 Q69)
