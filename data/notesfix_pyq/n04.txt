 • As per the book's answer, Sri Krishnadevaraya erected a pillar of victory at Kondaveedu to commemorate
   his conquest of coastal Andhra
    During his campaign against the Gajapatis, Krishnadevaraya captured the coastal Andhra forts of Kondaveedu,
    Kondapalli and Vinukonda. The book takes Kondaveedu as the place where he raised the jayasthambha marking this
    victory. The campaign gave Vijayanagara control over the rich coastal delta and its trade routes.
    Trap: Distractors: Udayagiri (another fort he captured), Potnuru and Nagarjunakonda. Note: some standard sources give
    Potnuru (near Simhachalam) as the site of the victory pillar.
    Group-2 Mains 2025 Paper-1 (B1 p.22 Q10)

 • Besides Yakshagana, shadow puppetry (Tholu Bommalata) entertained rural Andhra under Vijayanagara
    Tholu Bommalata uses leather puppets and lamplight to tell epic stories. It is a living Andhra folk art (Nimmalakunta
    puppets have a GI tag).
    Group-2 Prelims 2019 (B1 p.72 Q67)

 • Krishnadevaraya wrote the play Jambavati Kalyanam in Sanskrit.
    His Telugu masterpiece is Amuktamalyada; Jambavati Kalyanam and Madalasa Charita are Sanskrit works. His court
    had the Ashtadiggajas.
    Group-2 Mains 2019 Paper-2 (held 19-07-2019) (B1 p.105 Q40)

 • Abdur Razzak reported 300 ports in the Vijayanagara empire.
    Abdur Razzak, Persian envoy, visited Devaraya II's court in 1443. Other travellers: Nicolo Conti (1420), Domingo Paes
    and Nuniz (Portuguese, 16th century).
    Group-2 Mains 2019 Paper-2 (held 19-07-2019) (B1 p.105 Q41)

 • The Kandachara department of Vijayanagara dealt with military affairs.
    The Dalavayi was the commander-in-chief who headed it. The Athavane department dealt with revenue.
    Group-2 Mains 2019 Paper-2 (held 19-07-2019) (B1 p.105 Q42)

 • The Porumamilla inscription lays down 12 rules for constructing tanks.
    It is from the time of Bhaskara Bhavadura (son of Bukka I), 1369, in Kadapa district, and is a rare technical record of
    irrigation engineering.
    Group-2 Mains 2019 Paper-2 (held 19-07-2019) (B1 p.105 Q43)

 • Domingo Paes vividly described Krishnadevaraya's House of Victory (Mahanavami Dibba).
    Paes, a Portuguese traveller (c. 1520), described the Mahanavami festival, the city and the king.
    Group-2 Mains 2019 Paper-2 (held 19-07-2019) (B1 p.105 Q45)

 • Vipra Vinodins were Brahmin jugglers who entertained only Brahmins.
    Vijayanagara inscriptions and literature mention many entertainer groups with caste-based patrons.
    Group-2 Mains 2019 Paper-2 (held 19-07-2019) (B1 p.105 Q46)

 • 'A Forgotten Empire' (1900) on Vijayanagara was written by Robert Sewell.

    Trap: See also 2019 Paper-2 Q46.
