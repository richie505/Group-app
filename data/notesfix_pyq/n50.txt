 ECOLOGY BASICS

 • Population density is the number of individuals per unit area or volume
                     Natality, mortality, immigration and emigration change density. Dense populations face more competition and
                     disease.
                     Group-2 Mains 2025 Paper-2 (B1 p.53 Q84)

 • Biotic factors: humans-top heterotrophs, saprotrophs-recycle nutrients, reptiles-stenothermal,
   camel-passes little urine
                     Saprotrophs (fungi, bacteria) decompose dead matter. Stenothermal animals tolerate only a narrow temperature
                     range. Camels conserve water by producing concentrated urine.
                     Trap: Code a-II, b-I, c-IV, d-III.
                     Group-2 Mains 2025 Paper-2 (B1 p.53 Q86)

 • The grazing food chain starts with living green plants, not dead leaves (that is the detritus chain)
                     In the grazing chain, autotrophs capture solar energy, then herbivores and carnivores follow. In forests, the detritus
                     food chain carries a large share of energy flow.
                     Trap: A false, R true.
                     Group-2 Mains 2025 Paper-2 (B1 p.54 Q102)

 • Statement A is true (natality refers to births in a population) and statement B is false (natality rate is
   expressed per unit population, not per female)
                     In population ecology, natality is the production of new individuals by birth, hatching or division. The natality rate is
                     usually expressed relative to the whole population, for example births per 1,000 individuals per unit time. Natality,
                     mortality and migration together change population size.
                     Trap: Statement B uses a 'per female' wording that sounds technical, which makes 'Both true' tempting.
                     Group-2 Mains 2025 Paper-2 (B1 p.54 Q107)

 • Producers in aquatic ecosystems are green plants (phytoplankton, algae)
                     Consumers are heterotrophs that cannot make their own food. Some archaea are chemosynthetic, but the option
                     framing makes producer-plants the best answer.
                     Group-2 Mains 2025 Paper-2 (B1 p.56 Q129)

 • Organisms tolerating only a narrow temperature range are stenothermal
                     Eurythermal organisms tolerate a wide range. Similarly stenohaline/euryhaline refer to salinity tolerance.
                     Group-1 Prelims 2010 (Oct) (B3 p.136 Q21)

 • Plants growing on salty seashores are halophytes
                     Mangroves are halophytes with pneumatophores. Xerophytes live in dry areas, hydrophytes in water and
                     psammophytes on sand.
                     Group-1 Prelims 2010 (Sep) (B3 p.151 Q50)

 • The food chain is the dynamic (functional) part of an ecosystem
                     Energy flows one way through food chains and webs while nutrients cycle; producers, consumers and decomposers
                     form the structure.
                     Group-1 Prelims 2008 (B3 p.174 Q141)
