    NTR Health University Junior Assistants 2012 (B2 p.163 Q71)

 • The Malkapuram inscription gives information on the Pasupata Saiva sect
    Kakatiya Saivism had Pasupata, Kalamukha and Kapalika sects. The Malkapuram inscription describes the Golaki
    matha lineage of Visvesvara Sambhu.
    NTR Health University Junior Assistants 2012 (B2 p.163 Q74)

 • Hospital with a maternity ward at Mandadam was built in Rudramadevi's reign
    The Malkapuram inscription records that Visvesvara Sambhu set up a hospital, a maternity home, a college and a
    feeding house at Mandadam on land given by Rudramadevi.
    Panchayat Raj Lab Junior Assistants 2012 (B2 p.168 Q142)

 • Nritta Ratnavali was written by Jayapa Senani
    Jayapa, commander of Ganapatideva's elephant corps, wrote this Sanskrit treatise on dance.
    Panchayat Raj Lab Junior Assistants 2012 (B2 p.169 Q153)

 • The Magallu inscription (Eastern Chalukya Danarnava) mentions the Kakatiya progenitor
    The Magallu grant of Danarnava (956 CE) mentions a Kakatiya chief, the earliest reference to the family. The
    Bayyaram tank inscription of Mailamba gives the family's genealogy.
    SI Prelims 2023 (B2 p.203 Q1)

 • Sakala Neeti Sammatamu was compiled by Madiki Singana
    Madiki Singana, a 15th-century poet, compiled Sakala Neeti Sammatamu, an anthology of verses on polity. It is a
    useful source on Kakatiya-era administration.
    Police Constable Prelims 22.01.2023 (B2 p.204 Q20)
