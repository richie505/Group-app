 • The Battle of Khanwa (1527) truly established Mughal power
    Babur defeated Rana Sanga of Mewar at Khanwa after winning Panipat (1526). He then beat the Afghans at
    Chanderi (1528) and Ghaghra (1529).
    Group-1 Prelims 2008 (B3 p.167 Q8)

 • Babur founded the Mughal Empire (1526)
    He defeated Ibrahim Lodi at the First Battle of Panipat using artillery and the Tulughma tactic. Akbar later
    consolidated the empire.
    Group-1 Prelims 2008 (B3 p.167 Q11)

 • Under Akbar the Mughal empire became a lasting political fact
    Akbar built the mansabdari system, zabti/dahsala revenue system, Rajput alliances and Sulh-i-kul policy.
    Group-1 Prelims 2004 (Oct) (B3 p.187 Q41)

 • Dara Shikoh translated the Bhagavad Gita and Upanishads into Persian
    His Persian Upanishads were titled 'Sirr-i-Akbar' and he wrote 'Majma-ul-Bahrain' (Mingling of Two Oceans). Akbar's
    court had translated the Mahabharata as 'Razmnama'.
    Group-1 Prelims 2004 (Oct) (B3 p.187 Q42)
