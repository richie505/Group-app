 AGRICULTURAL GEOGRAPHY

 • Crop combination-Weaver, Doi; diversification-Bhatia, Jasbir Singh; concentration-Chisholm/Bhatia;
   productivity-Kendall/Majid Husain
    J.C. Weaver (1954) introduced the crop combination method; Kendall's ranking coefficient measures productivity.
    Group-1 Prelims 2022 Paper-I (GS) (B3 p.49 Q97)

 • Sengupta and Sdasyuk first divided Indian agriculture into 4 macro regions
    Other regionalisations were by Randhawa and Jasbir Singh.
    Group-1 Prelims 2022 Paper-I (GS) (B3 p.49 Q104)

 AGRICULTURE

 • Mustard (rapeseed) is grown only as a rabi crop
    Mustard is sown in October-November and harvested in spring, mainly in Rajasthan, Haryana and UP. Groundnut,
    sesame and sunflower can be grown in more than one season.
    Group-2 Prelims 2024 (B1 p.6 Q53)

 • Tea plantations in India began in the Brahmaputra valley (Assam) in the 1830s
    The British found wild tea in Assam and set up the first estate in the 1830s-40s. Darjeeling and the Nilgiris came
    later. Assam remains the largest tea-producing state.
    Group-2 Prelims 2024 (B1 p.6 Q54)

 AGRICULTURE (INDIA)

 • Wheat is the second most important cereal; black soil Deccan also grows wheat
    It is a rabi crop needing a cool growing season and bright ripening sunshine; chief areas are Ganga-Satluj plains and
    black soil Deccan (MP).
    Group-1 Prelims 2024 Paper-I (GS) (B3 p.19 Q117)

 • Tropical south India has higher sugarcane yields due to latitude, long sunshine and cool clear nights
    Sub-tropical north India has extreme temperatures and frost that reduce yield and sugar recovery.
    Group-1 Prelims 2022 Paper-I (GS) (B3 p.49 Q103)

 • Chief producers: Barley-UP; Groundnut-Gujarat; Ragi-Karnataka; Rice-West Bengal
    These were leading states at the time of the exam.
    Group-1 Screening Test 2018 Paper-I (GS) (B3 p.77 Q106)
