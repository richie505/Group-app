 • Buckingham Canal is the longest commercially important canal in Andhra
    The Buckingham Canal is a saltwater navigation canal running parallel to the Coromandel coast from Krishna district

 PREHISTORIC ANDHRA

 • Cuddapah basin is the oldest basin in Andhra Pradesh
    The Cuddapah basin is a Proterozoic crescent-shaped sedimentary basin resting on older Dharwar/Archaean rocks.
    It contains quartzites, shales and limestones and is rich in minerals such as barytes and asbestos.
    Trap: The Godavari basin is a much younger Gondwana basin.
