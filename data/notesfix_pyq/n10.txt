 • The first Telugu inscription was issued by the Renati Cholas
    The Kalamalla inscription (Kadapa district, c.575 CE) of the Renati Chola king Erikal Mutturaju Dhananjaya is
    regarded as the earliest wholly Telugu inscription.
    Group-1 2004 (Backlog) (B2 p.144 Q36)

 • Rajasekhara Charitramu was written by Kandukuri Veeresalingam
    It is regarded as the first Telugu novel (1878), inspired by Goldsmith's The Vicar of Wakefield. An English version was
    published as Fortune's Wheel.
    Trap: The question calls it a musical treatise, but it is a novel.
    Group-1 2004 (Backlog) (B2 p.144 Q40)

 • Simhasana Dwatrimsika was written by Koravi Goparaju
    Koravi Goparaju, a 15th-century poet patronised by the Recherla Velamas, rendered the 32 throne stories of
    Vikramaditya into Telugu.
    Group-1 1994 (B2 p.144 Q42)

 • Nannaya was the first poet to translate the Mahabharata into Telugu
    Nannaya, court poet of Rajaraja Narendra, began the Andhra Mahabharatam in the 11th century and completed the
    Adi, Sabha and part of Aranya parvas. Tikkana and Errana completed it.
    Group-1 1994 (B2 p.145 Q54)

 • Subhadra Parinayam was written by Tallapaka Timmakka
    Timmakka, wife of Annamacharya, wrote Subhadra Kalyanam and is regarded as the first Telugu woman poet.
    Group-2 Limited Recruitment 2012 (B2 p.162 Q54)

 • Abhinava Dandi: Ketana REPEATED
    Ketana translated Dandi's Dasakumara Charitra into Telugu, dedicated it to Tikkana, and earned the title Abhinava
    Dandi.
    Group-2 Limited Recruitment 2012 (B2 p.162 Q57); Asst. BC Welfare / Social Welfare Officer (B2 p.253 Q85)

 • Ranganatha Ramayanam is the greatest Telugu dvipada kavya
    Gona Budda Reddy wrote Ranganatha Ramayanam in the 13th century in dvipada (couplet) metre. It is still used in
    tolu bommalata puppet shows.
    NTR Health University Junior Assistants 2012 (B2 p.163 Q73)

 • Pothana refused to dedicate his Bhagavatam to Sarvajna Singabhupala (Singama Nayaka II)
    According to tradition, Pothana dedicated Andhra Maha Bhagavatam to Lord Rama rather than the Recherla Velama
    ruler Singabhupala II. His other works include Bhogini Dandakam and Virabhadra Vijayam.
    NTR Health University Junior Assistants 2012 (B2 p.163 Q77)

 • Kavitva Vicharam was written by C.R. Reddy
    Kattamanchi Ramalinga Reddy wrote Kavitva Vicharamu (1914), a pioneering work of modern Telugu literary
    criticism. He was also the first Vice-Chancellor of Andhra University (1926).
    NTR Health University Junior Assistants 2012 (B2 p.164 Q88)

 • Panduranga Mahatmyam was written by Tenali Ramakrishna
    Tenali Ramakrishna, one of the Ashtadiggajas, wrote Panduranga Mahatmyamu and Udbhataradhya Charitramu.
    NTR Health University Junior Assistants 2012 (B2 p.164 Q90)

 • The first Telugu magazine Satyadoota was published by the Bellary Christian community
    Satyadoota (1835) was brought out by Christian missionaries at Bellary for religious propaganda. It is regarded as
    the first Telugu periodical.
    NTR Health University Junior Assistants 2012 (B2 p.165 Q109)

 • Andhra Nataka Pitamaha: Dharmavaram Ramakrishnamacharyulu
    Dharmavaram Ramakrishnamacharyulu of Bellary wrote many Telugu plays (like Chitranaleeyam) and founded the
    Sarasa Vinodini Sabha theatre group.
    Trap: Kolachalam Srinivasa Rao is also called a father of Telugu drama in some texts.
    NTR Health University Junior Assistants 2012 (B2 p.167 Q128)

 • Asamardhuni Jeevayatra was written by Tripuraneni Gopichand
    Gopichand's 1947 novel is regarded as the first psychological novel in Telugu, about a middle-class man's inner life.
    NTR Health University Junior Assistants 2012 (B2 p.167 Q129)
