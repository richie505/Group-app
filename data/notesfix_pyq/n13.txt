    ended with Alluri's death in May 1924. Grievances included forest laws and forced labour.
    Trap: The earlier Rampa rebellion of 1879-80 was led by Chandraiah.
    Group-1 2008 (B2 p.143 Q21)

 • Andhra branch of the Home Rule movement: Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao
    Annie Besant started the Home Rule League in Madras in September 1916. Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao served
    as secretary of the Andhra branch.
    Group-1 2004 (Backlog) (B2 p.143 Q27)
