  1. Article 131
  2. Article 132
  3. Article 132 read with Article 134 A
  4. Article 133 read with Article 134 A
KEY: 3
EXPLANATION: Article 131 of the Constitution of India deals with the Original Jurisdiction of the Supreme Court. Article 132 deals with the Appellate Jurisdiction of the Supreme Court in appeals from High Courts in the Following cases - any Judgement, decree or Final order of a High Court in the territory of India, whether in a civil, criminal or other proceeding. Thus, during constitutional disputes reading Article 134A together with Article 132 creates appellate jurisdiction of the Supreme Court.
