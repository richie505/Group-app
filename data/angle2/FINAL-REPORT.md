# APPSC Complete v3.31: angle, wording, notes-gap and web pass

## What changed
- MCQs in APPSC Complete: 78,504 in v3.30, 93,917 in v3.31. Group 2 went from 50,589 to 60,209.
- Angle pass: all 158 packets. Own MCQs were written for each blueprint keyword and APPSC PYQ angle that had none, and 29,288 stems were reworded into APPSC framing (options and keys unchanged; old stems are in reworded.jsonl).
- Notes-gap pass: all 157 packets. One MCQ was written for each askable fact in the notes that no MCQ tested.
- Web pass: where the notes lacked a fact for a keyword or PYQ angle, it was searched on the web. 316 facts were appended to the notes (nothing existing was changed). The MCQs on them carry a `web` source URL; about 600 such MCQs were logged.
- No MCQ or PYQ was removed. Repeats were refused by the duplicate check.

## Notes that are probably wrong
- **Gondwana coal:** the notes say Carboniferous; it is Permian.
- **Valley of Flowers:** the notes say "Himachal range"; it is usually placed in the Greater Himalaya.
- **Shaktiman-I/II:** the notes call them GM maize; they are quality-protein maize hybrids.
- **First US rover on Mars:** the notes say Spirit (2004); it was Sojourner (1997).
- **Kulasekarapattinam:** the notes give the foundation date as 27 Aug 2025; the stone was probably laid in Feb 2024.
- **Pantanal:** the notes call it the "world's largest Ramsar site"; this is doubtful.
- **Yana Caves:** the notes give 13 Apr 2026 as the geo-heritage date; a web report says 8 March.
- **"First co-hosted T20 WC" (2026):** the 2021 and 2024 events were also co-hosted.
- **Local-body quotas (6:0):** the notes say 34% rural and 33.33% urban; a UNI report gives the reverse.
- **AP coastline "Srikakulam to Nellore":** the southern end is now Tirupati district.
- **Navy chief:** the notes say Krishna Swaminathan; as far as the agent knew, it is Adm Dinesh Tripathi.
- **Kondaveedu:** the notes put it in Guntur; it may now be Palnadu.
- **AP per-capita income:** the notes say 1.34 times the national figure; the web suggests about 1.19.
- **India as the "4th largest economy":** IMF Oct 2026 projections put it 4th only in 2027.
- **SAM total of ₹24,350 cr:** Parliament replies call this AP's proposal. MCQ nd8eab016b8c asks it.
- **Mayawati:** the notes say 3 terms; it was 4. Carried over from earlier.
