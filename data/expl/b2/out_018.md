Q n7b07a2f1562
- **Vihaan Kumar v State of Haryana (2025)** held that **grounds of arrest must be meaningfully communicated to every arrestee** — option **(1)** (reported **14 Aug 2026**).
- It builds on **Art. 22(1)** — the right to be **informed of grounds** of arrest.
- Advisory Boards concern **preventive detention** (beyond **3 months**), not ordinary arrest; preventive detention is **without trial**, not after conviction.
+ An arrestee must be produced before the **nearest magistrate within 24 hours** (excluding journey time).
+ New code: **Secs. 47 and 58 BNSS** (earlier **CrPC 50, 57**).
+ ⚠ These rights are **not available** to **enemy aliens** or **preventive detainees** (**Art. 22(3)**).

Q n885e822d7a9
- **Unnikrishnan v State of AP (1993)** shaped the right to education — the topic's **AP angle** — option **(4)**.
- It held free education only **up to 14 years**; beyond that it depends on the State's **economic capacity**.
- **Vihaan Kumar** is on grounds of arrest; **K.A. Najeeb** on speedy trial; **Harish Rana** on withdrawal of life support.
+ **Mohini Jain (1992)** earlier read education into **Art. 21**.
+ The **86th Amendment, 2002** inserted **Art. 21A** (**6 to 14 years**).
+ The **RTE Act, 2009** and Art. 21A came into force on **1 April 2010**.

Q ne2ce58e148a
- **Harish Rana** (**PVS for 13 years**): his parents sought **withdrawal of life support** in the **SC** (**16 Mar 2026**) — option **(1)**.
- It relates to the **right to die with dignity** read into Art. 21.

Q n784054184cd
- **Art. 20** gives **protection in respect of conviction for offences** — option **(1)**.
- **Art. 21** is **life and personal liberty**; **Art. 21A** is **education** (6 to 14 years); **Art. 22** covers **arrest and preventive detention**.
- So **ex post facto** protection is in **Art. 20(1)**, not 21A, and education is in 21A, not 22.
+ Art. 20's three protections: **no ex post facto law**, **no double jeopardy**, **no self-incrimination**.
+ Art. 20 is available to **citizens, foreigners and companies**; **non-suspendable** in emergency.
+ Art. 22(4)-(7) are the **preventive detention** safeguards.
