## topic group2 2-5 | Kinship – Descent, Lineage, Clan, Kinship Terminology; North-south Indian Kinship Contrast
### part 0: Kinship – Meaning, Consanguineal, Affinal and Ritual Kin
[0] h: Meaning
[1] table: Scholar / view | Definition
  [1.0] **Kinship** | A system of social relationships based on **blood (consanguineal) and marriage (affinal)** – the set of relationships among **consanguineous and marital relatives**
  [1.1] **Radcliffe-Brown** | A system of **dynamic relations** between persons, regulated by **social usage**; a field of rights and obligations
  [1.2] **Murdock** | A structured system of relationships binding individuals by complex interlocking ties
  [1.3] Short definition | Kinship = **social identity expressing genealogical relationship**
  [1.4] **Marriage creates kinship** | When two people marry they become kin to one another
[2] h: Types
[3] table: Type | Basis | Example
  [3.0] **Consanguineal** (blood) | Birth / descent | Parent–child, siblings, uncle–nephew; an **adopted son** is treated as consanguineous kin
  [3.1] **Affinal** ("relatives by marriage", **affines**; "matrimonial kinship") | Marriage | **Husband–wife** (ego's wife), son-in-law–father-in-law, brothers-in-law
  [3.2] **Ritual / fictive** | Functional social relationship, not biology | Godparenthood, dharam bhai/behen, rakhi ties
[4] table: Fact | Key detail
  [4.0] **Genetic traits** | Pass only through **blood relatives**, never through affines
  [4.1] Father–son by birth | Consanguineal; husband–wife is the classic **non-consanguineous** pair
  [4.2] **Inheritance** | Transfer of property (immovable) from a dead person to heirs
  [4.3] **Cognates** | All **consanguineous (blood) relatives** together form a **cognate group**; **agnates** = kin related through males only
