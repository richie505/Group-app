### part 3: SRC Report 1955 – Principles and Hyderabad/Visalandhra
[0] b: **Report:** submitted **30 Sep 1955**, made public **9 Oct 1955**; **1,52,250** memorandums; **four parts** – I Conditions of the Problem, II Factors Bearing on Reorganisation, III Proposals for Reorganisation, IV Administrative and Allied Matters (safeguards for linguistic groups).
[1] b: **Principles:** language **important but not the only factor**; rejected "**one language, one state**" – same-language areas could form more than one state; other factors: **unity and security of India**, **administrative efficiency**, **financial and economic viability**, national plans.
[2] b: **Recommended 16 states + 3 centrally administered territories**; abolish the **Part A/B/C** classification.

[4] h: Hyderabad (Part III ch. V) and Andhra (ch. VI)
[5] b: **Division of Hyderabad State:** most regions (Telugu, Kannada, Marathi) wanted it divided; **Kannada areas (Raichur, Gulbarga) → Mysore**; **Marathwada → Bombay** State.
