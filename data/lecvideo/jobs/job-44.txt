### part 3: Agreement on Agriculture – Pillars and Subsidy Boxes
[0] h: Pillars
[1] table: Fact | Key detail
  [1.0] Negotiated in | **Uruguay Round**; in force **1 January 1995**
  [1.1] Three pillars | **Market access**, **domestic support**, **export competition**
  [1.2] Outside the pillars | **Anti-dumping and countervailing duties**
  [1.3] WTO terms | "**Agreement on Agriculture**", "**SPS Agreement**", "**Peace Clause**"; **amber, blue and green boxes**
[2] h: Subsidy boxes
[3] table: Box | Covers | Treatment
  [3.0] **Green** | Research, pest/disease control, infrastructure, public stockholding for food security, decoupled income support, environment, regional development | Minimal or no trade distortion; **no limits**
  [3.1] **Blue** | "Amber box with conditions" – **direct payments under production-limiting programmes** ("production-limiting subsidies that distort trade") | Trade-distorting but **exempt from reduction**; no spending limit
  [3.2] **Amber** | Price- or quantity-linked support: **MSP**, power, fertiliser, seed, irrigation (measured as **AMS**) | Trade-distorting; **to be reduced** to de minimis
  [3.3] **S&D box** | Special and differential support for developing countries | –
[4] b: The AoA has **no Red Box** – the traffic-light "red = forbidden" belongs to the **SCM Agreement**; there are no White, Pink or Yellow boxes either.
[5] b: **India + China proposal (July 2017):** eliminate developed countries' **Amber Box (AMS)** support beyond de minimis first.
[6] h: Mechanics
[7] table: Term | Rule
  [7.0] De minimis | Amber support allowed up to **5%** of production value (developed), **10%** (developing)
  [7.1] Total AMS cut | **20% over 6 years** (developed); **13.3% over 10 years** (developing)
  [7.2] Blue Box conditions | Payments on fixed areas and yields, or on **85% or less** of base production, or a fixed number of livestock
  [7.3] Tariffication | Non-tariff barriers converted into tariffs; cuts **36% over 6 years** (developed), **24% over 10 years** (developing)
  [7.4] Export subsidy cut | Developed: **36% by value or 21% by volume** over 6 years; developing 24% / 14% over 10 years
  [7.5] Developing countries | Also had **reduction commitments** – India too must reduce subsidies
  [7.6] **Price-gap payment (MSP minus market price) paid directly to farmers** | Treated as **Blue Box** (direct payment); strictly, such deficiency payments are Amber unless tied to production limits

### part 5: IHDI, PHDI and Beyond-HDI Statements
[0] table: Index | Key detail
  [0.0] **IHDI** | Discounts each HDI dimension for inequality: **no inequality → IHDI = HDI**; otherwise IHDI < HDI
