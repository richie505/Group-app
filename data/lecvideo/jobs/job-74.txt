## topic group2 5-56 | Oil and Natural Gas – Basins, Refineries, Spr, Gas Grid, City Gas Distribution
### part 0: Fuel Gases – LPG, Natural Gas, CNG, LNG and Their Chemistry
[0] h: What each gas is made of
[1] table: Fuel | Main constituents | Key detail
  [1.0] **Natural gas** | Mostly **methane (CH₄)**, with some ethane, propane, CO₂, N₂ | **Cleanest fossil fuel**; burnt directly or piped
  [1.1] **CNG** – **Compressed Natural Gas** | **Methane** (**80–90%**) | Natural gas compressed to **200–250 kg/cm²**; vehicles
  [1.2] **PNG** – Piped Natural Gas | **85–95%** methane | Supplied to homes through city gas networks
  [1.3] **LNG** – Liquefied Natural Gas | Methane | Cooled to about **−160°C**; volume shrinks about **600 times**; carried in cryogenic tankers; **lighter than air**
  [1.4] **LPG** – Liquefied Petroleum Gas | **Propane + butane** (roughly **65%** butane, **35%** propane; a little ethane) | Refinery / gas by-product; liquefied under pressure; **heavier than air**
[2] h: LPG and its gases
[3] table: Fact | Key detail
  [3.0] Chief constituent of LPG | **Butane**; the three gases are **ethane, propane and butane** (LPG is not mainly methane)
  [3.1] Smell | **LPG is odourless**; **ethyl mercaptan (ethanethiol)** is added so that a **leak can be smelt** (the "rotten" stench)
  [3.2] **Butane (C₄H₁₀)** | Gas in **cigarette lighters**; a **Natural Gas Liquid (NGL)**
  [3.3] **Propane** | Clean-burning LPG gas (coke, petrol and wax are not clean energy)
  [3.4] **Methane** | **Greenhouse gas** (ethane, propane, butane are not counted as GHGs); in the air it oxidises to CO₂ in about a decade
  [3.5] **Methane + air** (firedamp, **5–15%** methane) | Causes most **explosions in coal mines**
[4] h: Other fuel facts
[5] table: Fuel / term | Key detail
  [5.0] **Water gas** | **CO + H₂** (producer gas = CO + H₂ + N₂)
  [5.1] **Acetylene (ethyne)** | **Gas welding** (oxy-acetylene flame)
  [5.2] **Kerosene** | Calorific value about the **same as petrol** (~**45,000 kJ/kg**); LPG ~**55,000**, methane/CNG ~**50,000**
  [5.3] Hydrocarbons by molecular weight | **Methane (16) < ethane (30) < propane (44) < butane (58)**
  [5.4] **Petroleum** | A mixture of many hydrocarbons of different densities; its **grade** depends on their relative proportion
  [5.5] LNG | Liquefied under extreme cold for storage and transport
  [5.6] India's first LNG terminal | **Dahej** (Gujarat, Petronet LNG, **2004**)
  [5.7] **NGLs** (ethane, propane, butane, **natural gas**oline) | Separated from natural gas
