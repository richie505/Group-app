[0] h: Guilds (shreni)
[1] b: **Shrenis:** associations of artisans, merchants and moneylenders under elected or hereditary heads (**jetthaka / pamukha**). Craft shrenis trained workers, procured raw materials and sold products; merchant shrenis organised trade.
[2] table: Fact | Key detail

  [2.1] Autonomy | **Autonomous – the king did not interfere**
  [2.2] **Guilds as banks** | Deposits invested at interest – the **Nashik inscription** records guild deposits at fixed interest
  [2.3] **Satapatha Brahmana** | The first reference to **money-lending**; usurer = **kusidin**

### part 1: Core Teachings – Nirvana, Karma, Anatta, Middle Path
[0] h: Core teachings
[1] table: Term | Meaning
  [1.0] **Nirvana (nibbana)** | The **main goal**; the "**extinction of the flame of desire**"; the end of suffering and of rebirth
  [1.1] **Middle Path (Madhyama Marga)** | Avoids both **self-indulgence and self-mortification** – the path to nirvana, not the goal
  [1.2] **Anicca** | **Impermanence** – "the world is unstable and transient"
  [1.3] **Anatta / Anatmavada** | **No permanent self (soul)** – a Buddhist doctrine (not Sankhya, Vedanta or Jain)
  [1.4] **Dukkha** | Suffering
  [1.5] **Karma** | Intention-based morality; the present state comes from past actions; **rebirth** till nirvana
  [1.6] Raft simile | Teaching as a raft – upaya (skilful means), not dogma
[2] b: **Three marks:** Anicca, Anatta, Dukkha.
[3] b: **Beliefs:** karma, rebirth and nirvana; **God neither affirmed nor denied** – belief in God was not needed for the Sangha.
[4] b: The Buddha **rejected Vedic authority**, animal sacrifice and ritual, and stressed **purity of conduct / a moral life** over philosophical problems.
[5] b: **Caste:** Buddhism **did not abolish caste and class** as social facts; it **challenged the Brahmins' top rank** (Kshatriyas listed first); its texts call some **jatis and crafts (hina-sippa) low**.
[6] b: **The Buddha:** born in (the Sakya state of) **Kapilavastu** – strictly at Lumbini near it; enlightened at **Bodh Gaya**; **discarded the Vedic religion**; preached the **noble truths**.
[7] x: Nirvana = goal; Middle Path = means; Anatta = no soul; God neither affirmed nor denied.
