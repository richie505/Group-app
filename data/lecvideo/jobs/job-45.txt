### part 4: JNNURM and Earlier Urban Schemes
[0] h: JNNURM
[1] table: Fact | Key detail
  [1.0] Launch | Announced in Budget 2005-06; launched **3 December 2005**; a **7-year** **urban renewal programme**

  [1.2] Objectives | Urban infrastructure (water supply, sewerage, solid waste, roads, **urban transport**, renewal of old city/**heritage areas**), houses and basic services for the poor (not urban electrification, rural housing or employment)
