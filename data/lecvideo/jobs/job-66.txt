### part 2: Concepts and the National Trend, 1901-2011
[0] h: Definitions
[1] table: Term | Meaning
  [1.0] **Sex ratio (India)** | **Females per 1,000 males** – the social indicator of **equality between males and females**
  [1.1] Sex ratio (UN / international) | **Males per 100 females**
  [1.2] Child sex ratio (CSR) | Girls per 1,000 boys aged **0-6**
  [1.3] Sex ratio at birth (SRB) | Girls born per 1,000 boys born
  [1.4] Biological norm | About **105 boys per 100 girls** = roughly **950** girls per 1,000 boys
  [1.5] **Missing women** | Shortfall of women, especially in Asia; coined by **Amartya Sen** (1990)
[2] b: Sex ratio covers the **whole population**, not only adults; first Census **1872**, then every **10 years**; density is **lower** in mountains.
