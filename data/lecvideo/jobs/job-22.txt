### part 3: The Saluva Dynasty and the Two Usurpations
[0] h: Decline of the Sangamas
[1] b: **Nuniz** called **Virupaksha II** a drunkard and **Praudha Raya** worthless; Gajapati and Bahmani invasions ate into the kingdom.
[2] h: The two usurpations
[3] table: Year | Event
  [3.0] **1485** | **First usurpation:** **Saluva Narasimha** of Chandragiri expels **Praudha Raya**; ends the **Sangama** dynasty, begins the **Saluva** dynasty
  [3.1] **1505** | **Second usurpation:** **Vira Narasimha** (son of Tuluva Narasa Nayaka) kills **Immadi Narasimha**, the last Saluva, at **Penugonda**; begins the **Tuluva** dynasty

  [3.4] 1543 | Sadasiva made a figurehead under Aliya Rama Raya

[5] b: **Founder of the Saluva dynasty:** **Saluva Narasimha (Narasimharaya)** – he ended the old dynasty and took the royal title **to save the kingdom from further degeneration and disintegration**.
[6] b: **Chronology:** Deva Raya II (Sangama) → Saluva Narasimha → Achyuta Deva Raya (Tuluva) → Venkatapati II (Aravidu).

### part 1: Amara-Nayankara System and the Palegars
[0] h: Amaram and Amaranayakas
[1] table: Term | Meaning
  [1.0] **Amaram** | Land granted to **military chiefs** instead of cash salary; the Rayas owned all land
  [1.1] **Amaranayakas** | Holders; also Nayaks, **Palaiyagars, Poligars**
  [1.2] **Mahamandaleshwara** | Their title; more autonomy than Durgadannayakas
  [1.3] **Amaranayaka** (Amara-Nayankara) system | A military governor (Nayaka) given amarams for salary and army expenses
[2] table: Fact | Key detail
  [2.0] Nature | **Military commanders** controlling particular territories (**palaiyam**), given by the Rayas
  [2.1] Within the amaram | **Collected taxes**, kept **law and order**, administered their territory
  [2.2] Obligations | Tribute (**Kappam**); a quota of troops (**contingents**), horses, elephants; attend court at **Mahanavami** with gifts; maintain forts, temples, irrigation
[3] h: Kakatiya nayankara vs Vijayanagara amara-nayankara
[4] table: Point | Kakatiya nayankara | Vijayanagara amara-nayankara
  [4.0] Origin | **Prataparudra II** | Grew out of the Kakatiya system
  [4.1] Scale | **75 or 77** nayakas | **About 200** nayakas
  [4.2] Outcome | Discontent among older feudatories | Semi-independent, then independent **Nayaka states** after 1565 (**Madurai, Tanjore, Keladi**)
[5] h: Palegars (Poligars)
[6] table: Fact | Key detail
  [6.0] Origin | **Amara-nayakas** became Palegars when the centre weakened after **Talikota (1565)**; hereditary; **forerunners of the zamindars**
  [6.1] Nature | **Territorial administrative and military governors**
  [6.2] Duties under the Nayakas | Administer palaiyams, collect taxes, maintain law and order
  [6.3] End | **Thomas Munro**, with **Campbell**, crushed the Rayalaseema Palegars, then introduced the **Ryotwari** system
[7] b: Vijayanagara markets dealt in **spices, textiles and precious stones**; **Krishnadevaraya's** reign was the empire's peak, free of strain within the imperial structure.
