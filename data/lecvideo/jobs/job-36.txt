[1] table: Basis | Adjournment Motion | Calling Attention Motion

### part 3: Stages of the Budget, Demands for Grants, Guillotine and Cut Motions
[0] h: Six stages
[1] table: Stage | Key detail
  [1.0] **1. Presentation** | No discussion that day
  [1.1] **2. General discussion** | Both Houses, 3-4 days; no cut motion, no vote
  [1.2] **3. Scrutiny by the Departmentally Related Standing Committees** | House adjourned 3-4 weeks

  [1.5] **6. Passing of the Finance Bill** | Last stage; gives effect to the tax proposals

### part 4: Appropriation Bill, Finance Bill and Parliamentary Control over Finance
[0] h: Appropriation and Finance Bills
[1] table: Fact | Key detail
  [1.0] **Appropriation Bill** | Authorises withdrawal from the CFI for **voted grants + charged expenditure**; **passed by both Houses** (as a Money Bill); **Art. 114(3)**: no withdrawal except under it – charged expenditure included
  [1.1] Amendments | **No amendment** may vary the amount or alter the destination of a voted grant, or vary charged expenditure
  [1.2] **Automatic repeal clause** | Since the **Appropriation Act, 2016** (after the Appropriation Acts (Repeal) Act, 2016 repealed 758 old Acts) – an **automatic repeal (sunset) clause**

[0] h: Electoral college – Arts. 54, 55
[1] table: Votes in the Presidential election | Excluded

[2] table: Fact | Key detail
  [2.0] Articles | **Art. 54** = electoral college; **Art. 55** = manner of election – together the **election of the President**
  [2.1] Only **"elected" members** | **Elected MPs + elected MLAs** (States, Delhi, Puducherry); nominated MPs are out
  [2.2] **Indirect election** | Chosen by the elected representatives – not directly by the people, nor by Parliament alone

  [4.0] MLA | **Population of the State ÷ elected MLAs × 1/1000** | **Varies from State to State** (uniformity among States, Art. 55(1))
  [4.1] MP | **Total value of all MLAs' votes ÷ total elected MPs** | **Same for every MP**, Lok Sabha or Rajya Sabha (parity between the Union and the States)

[7] h: Nomination, counting, disputes
[8] table: Fact | Key detail
  [8.0] **Nomination** | At least **50 electors as proposers and 50 as seconders** (since 1997; **10 + 10** from 1974 to 1997); deposit ₹15,000; Vice-President: **20 + 20**
  [8.1] **Quota** | More than half of valid vote value; lowest candidate eliminated and votes transferred (V.V. Giri, 1969, won on **second-preference** votes)

[0] h: Articles 56–60

### part 3: Impeachment (Art. 61)
[0] h: Impeachment – Article 61
[1] table: Fact | Key detail
  [1.0] **Art. 61** | Procedure for **impeachment** (removal) of the President; Art. 56(1)(b) refers to it
  [1.1] **Only ground** | **"Violation of the Constitution"** – the term is undefined in the Constitution
