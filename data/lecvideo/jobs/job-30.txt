## topic group2 2-27 | Poverty, Unemployment and Social Exclusion As Social Problems
### part 0: Social Exclusion
[0] table: Fact | Key detail
  [0.0] **Meaning** | A **process** that shuts people out of **facilities, benefits and opportunities** that others enjoy; **involuntary and systematic** (NCERT)
  [0.1] Term coined | **René Lenoir** (France, **1974**)
  [0.2] Bases | Caste, gender, race and tribe – all are bases of exclusion
  [0.3] **Practices leading to exclusion** | **Untouchability**, **segregation** (e.g. a **special school for the blind** instead of inclusive schooling), denied access to **land, water and jobs**; MGNREGA wage work is a remedy
  [0.4] **Results** | Hurts **human-capital development**, **widens inequalities**, drives **social unrest and crime**; it does not bridge historical divisions
  [0.5] **Stereotypes** | **Rigid and generalised views about a group**; prejudices often stem from them – misleading in diverse countries like India, where **colonial history** left certain characterisations of communities
[1] table: Basis | Example
  [1.0] Caste | Untouchability against SCs
  [1.1] Gender | Denial of property, work, mobility
  [1.2] Race / tribe | Adivasis in remote areas, racial discrimination
  [1.3] Disability | Lack of access to schools, jobs; **segregated special schools**
  [1.4] Religion / region | Minorities; backward, remote areas
[2] h: Constitutional remedies
[3] table: Remedy | Key detail
  [3.0] **Reservations** | For SCs, STs, OBCs – **social justice and equal opportunity**
  [3.1] **Art. 46 (DPSP)** | Promotion of **educational and economic interests** of SCs, STs and other weaker sections
  [3.2] **10 Aug 2026** | The Union **opposes a creamy layer for SCs/STs**, since income alone does not end caste-based exclusion
### part 1: MGNREGA and the VB-G RAM G Act
[0] h: Timeline
[1] table: Event | Date
  [1.0] NREGA passed by Parliament | **August 2005** (assent September 2005)
  [1.1] In force | **2 February 2006**, launched in **Anantapur** district (AP); all districts from 1 April 2008
  [1.2] Renamed MGNREGA | **2 October 2009**
  [1.3] VB-G RAM G Act, 2025 | Passed 18 Dec 2025; **in force 1 July 2026, replacing MGNREGA**
[2] h: Provisions (MGNREGA)
[3] table: Fact | Key detail
  [3.0] Right | **Legal right** to at least **100 days** of unskilled wage work in a **financial year** per **rural household**
  [3.1] Eligible | **Adult members of any rural household** willing to do unskilled manual work (not only SC/ST/BPL)
  [3.2] Aim | Guarantee the **"Right to Work"**; livelihood security, durable assets, **strengthening the livelihood resource base of the poor**, **proactive social inclusion**, **strengthening PRIs**
  [3.3] Distance | Work within **5 km**
  [3.4] **Unemployment allowance** | If no work is given within **15 days** of demand
  [3.5] Women | At least **one-third** of workers
  [3.6] **Gram Sabha** | Recommends works, monitors them, holds **social audits**
  [3.7] **Gram Panchayat** | Registers households, **receives work applications and issues dated receipts**
  [3.8] Constitutional basis | **Directive Principles** (Art. 41); the right to work is outside Part III (Fundamental Rights)
[4] h: VB-G RAM G Act, 2025 (now)
[5] table: Fact | Key detail
  [5.0] Full name | **Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin)**
  [5.1] Ministry | Rural Development
  [5.2] Guarantee | **125 days** per rural household per year
  [5.3] Wage floor | **₹300/day** (2026-27)
  [5.4] Funding | **60:40** (NE/Himalayan 90:10)
  [5.5] **AP ranked first** | In implementation – 2.10 crore person-days in the first 45 days

### part 3: Poverty – Concepts, Causes and Indicators
[0] h: Concepts of poverty
[1] table: Concept | Meaning | Measured by
  [1.0] Absolute poverty | Spending below the minimum for basic needs | A fixed poverty line (MPCE cut-off)
  [1.1] Relative poverty | Being poor **compared with others in the same society** | Inequality / share of income
  [1.2] Multidimensional poverty | Deprivation in health, education, living standards | National MPI (NITI Aayog)
[2] h: Indicators and causes
[3] table: Fact | Key detail
  [3.0] Official poverty line | **Consumption-based (MPCE)**, not income-based
  [3.1] **MPCE 2011-12 (NSS 68th round, MMRP)** | About **₹1,430 rural** and **₹2,630 urban** – a gap of about **₹1,200** (urban ~84% higher)
  [3.2] Causes | **Illiteracy and lack of quality education** (main cause for rural and tribal poor), unemployment, disguised unemployment in agriculture, discrimination, health costs, inequality
  [3.3] **Social indicators** | Illiteracy, malnutrition, poor health care, lack of safe drinking water and sanitation (the size of the house is not one)
  [3.4] **Culture of poverty** | **Oscar Lewis (1959)** – fatalism and poverty passed down generations
