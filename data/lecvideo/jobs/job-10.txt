### part 0: Why the Revolt Failed
[0] h: Why the revolt failed
[1] table: Cause | Key detail
  [1.0] **Main cause:** | Lack of a **common strategy, coordination and central leadership/organisation**; no unified **programme or ideology**, no vision of the future

  [1.2] **Limited territorial and social base** | No **all-India character**; British power intact in the **East, West and South (Madras Presidency peaceful)**, from where troops came

  [1.4] Weak leadership | The old **Bahadur Shah**, whose leadership lacked wide support; quarrels such as the **Begum of Awadh vs Maulvi Ahmadullah**
