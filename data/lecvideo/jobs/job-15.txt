### part 0: Indigo Revolt, Nil Darpan, Pabna and the Deccan Riots
[0] h: Indigo Revolt (1859-60), Bengal
[1] table: Fact | Key detail
  [1.0] Cause | European planters forced ryots to grow **indigo instead of paying crops like rice**, through **advances and fraudulent contracts**, at prices that did not pay
  [1.1] Oppression | Intimidation, flogging, seizure of cattle
  [1.2] Leaders | **Digambar and Bishnu Biswas** of **Nadia**
  [1.3] Methods | Refused to sow; rent strike; used the courts
  [1.4] **Bengali intelligentsia** | Supported it – press (**Harish Chandra Mukherjee's *Hindoo Patriot***), meetings, legal aid; missionaries too
  [1.5] **Indigo Commission (March 1860)** | Found planters coercive; ryots must **fulfil existing contracts** but **could refuse future ones**; it did not find indigo profitable to ryots
  [1.6] Nov 1860 notification | No compulsion
  [1.7] Timing | **Immediately after 1857** (Sanyasi **1763-1800**, Santhal **1855-56** earlier; Pabna **1873-76** later)
  [1.8] **Ryoti system** | Contract farming – the ryot took an advance and signed a contract to grow indigo on **his own land**
  [1.9] **Nij** | The planter grew indigo on his own land with hired labour

[1] table: Fact | Key detail
