### part 0: Padma Awards and Bharat Ratna
[0] h: The ladder
[1] table: Award | Rank | Given for
  [1.0] Bharat Ratna | 1st – highest civilian award | Exceptional service of the highest order
  [1.1] Padma Vibhushan | 2nd | Exceptional and distinguished service
  [1.2] Padma Bhushan | 3rd | Distinguished service of a high order
  [1.3] Padma Shri | 4th | Distinguished service in any field
[2] table: Fact | Key detail
  [2.0] Instituted | **1954**
  [2.1] Announced / conferred | Padma lists announced on **Republic Day**; conferred by the **President** at **Rashtrapati Bhavan** (March–April)
  [2.2] Nature | Bharat Ratna is a civilian award (the **Param Vir Chakra** is the military gallantry award); the Nobel is not Indian
  [2.3] **Article 18(1)** | No titles except **military and academic distinctions**
  [2.4] **Balaji Raghavan case (1996)** | Padma awards are **not titles under Article 18**; not to be used as prefix or suffix
  [2.5] Count | **2009 – 93 Padma Shri**
