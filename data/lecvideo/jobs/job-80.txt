### part 6: Computer Types and Supercomputers
[0] h: Computer types
[1] table: Type | Feature / example
  [1.0] Analog | Continuous quantities – **car speedometer**, thermometer
  [1.1] Digital | Discrete (binary) data – PCs; works on the **logical** principle (Boolean 0/1)
  [1.2] Hybrid | Analog + digital features; **converts analog output into digital form** – ICU monitors, ECG
  [1.3] Micro / personal | **Most widely used** – desktop, laptop, tablet, smartphone, wearables
  [1.4] Embedded | Dedicated function inside a larger system – **digital watch**
  [1.5] Supercomputer | **Largest, fastest and costliest**; speed in FLOPS
  [1.6] Quantum computer | Uses **qubits** (superposition, entanglement); the "smallest and fastest computer imitating brain working"
  [1.7] **Teacube** | A **2-inch cube-shaped computer**
[2] b: **Q-Day:** when quantum computers may break **RSA-2048** encryption with **Shor's algorithm (1994)**. Oxford researchers made **10 billion quantum entanglements** in silicon.
