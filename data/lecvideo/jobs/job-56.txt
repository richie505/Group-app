### part 4: Groundwater Regulation and Water Policy
[0] table: Fact | Key detail
  [0.0] **APWALTA, 2002** (AP Water, Land and Trees Act) | Checks random borewell digging; the authority (Panchayat Secretary in villages) may require **rainwater harvesting structures on premises of 200 sq m or more**
  [0.1] **National Water Policy 2012** | **Water Users Associations** to be given **statutory powers** to collect and retain water charges
  [0.2] **Maharashtra (April 2016)** | Banned digging bore wells **deeper than 200 feet** during drought

  [0.4] Bore well growth | 40,000-50,000 new bore wells a year: area irrigated per well falling
  [0.5] **National Hydrology Project** (2016-17, 100% grant) | Set up AP's real-time groundwater monitoring
