## topic group2 1-160 | Gandhian Philosophy – Truth, Ahimsa, Satyagraha, Means and Ends
### part 0: Satyagraha – Meaning, Rules and Weapons
[0] h: Meaning
[1] table: Fact | Key detail
  [1.0] Word | **Satya + agraha** = insistence on (holding to) truth; "**soul force**" / "truth force" in place of physical force
  [1.1] Origin | Born **1906 in the Transvaal**
  [1.2] Passive resistance | Its early English name – but passive resistance = weapon of the weak, **satyagraha = weapon of the strongest**
  [1.3] Gandhi's fame | **Using passive resistance (satyagraha) to win Indian independence**
  [1.4] Aim | **Not to defeat the enemy** but to **change his heart**
  [1.5] Driving force | **Suffering**
  [1.6] The satyagrahi | **No ill feeling** towards the opponent; **firm in his belief**
  [1.7] **Weapon of satyagraha = ahimsa** | Two-fold method for conflicts = **non-violence and satyagraha**
[2] h: Rules and forms
[3] b: Rules: **never insult the opponent**; bear assaults **without retaliation**; **submit to arrest**; obey jail discipline.
[4] b: Forms: non-cooperation, boycott, picketing, hartal, strike, civil disobedience, **fasting**.
[5] b: **Fasting = the last weapon**: "Fasting unto death is the **last and the most potent weapon** in the armoury of Satyagraha" (Harijan, 18 Aug 1946) – used only when all other avenues have failed.
[6] table: Struggle | Year | Form / "first"
  [6.0] Champaran | 1917 | First civil disobedience
  [6.1] Ahmedabad | 1918 | First hunger strike
  [6.2] Kheda | 1918 | First non-cooperation
  [6.3] **Rowlatt Satyagraha** | **1919** | **Hartal**; first **all-India mass** movement
[7] x: In Gandhi's words **fasting is the last resort** of satyagraha.
### part 1: Truth and Ahimsa
[0] h: Truth and Ahimsa
[1] table: Fact | Key detail
  [1.0] Relation | **Truth is the end; non-violence (ahimsa) is the means** – two sides of one coin; non-violence is **a way to attain truth**
  [1.1] Truth | Reality – **existence, consciousness and bliss** (sat-chit-ananda)
  [1.2] Formulations | **"God is Truth"** = earlier; **"Truth is God"** = later
  [1.3] Ahimsa | In **thought, word and deed**; a quality of the **strong (brave)**, not the weak
  [1.4] Cowardice or violence | Between **cowardice and violence**, Gandhi would choose **violence**
  [1.5] **Cruellest form of violence** | **Persistence of poverty**
  [1.6] **Family planning** | Through **self-control** (not sterilisation or the loop)
  [1.7] **2 October** (birth anniversary) | **International Day of Non-Violence** (UN General Assembly, 2007)
### part 2: Means, Ends, Politics and Swaraj
[0] h: Gandhi's sayings
[1] table: Quotation | Point
  [1.0] "**Wrong means never take us to right ends**" | Purity of means and ends
  [1.1] "Means are after all everything. **As the means so the end**" | Means decide the end
  [1.2] "What is morally wrong can never be politically right" | Ethics in politics
  [1.3] "Politics bereft of religion is a **death-trap**" | Religion = morality of truth and non-violence
  [1.4] "Real swaraj … the **capacity of all to resist authority when abused**" | Swaraj and authority
  [1.5] "Swaraj will not be a **free gift of the British Parliament**; it will be a declaration of India's full self-expression" | **Young India, 1922**
  [1.6] **Gandhi's Talisman** | Recall the face of the **poorest and weakest**
[2] h: Politics and Swaraj
[3] table: Fact | Key detail
  [3.0] Politics | **Activity for public welfare**, built on **ethics, religion and humanity** – **no place for authority**
  [3.1] Main suggestion | **Decentralisation through village panchayats**
  [3.2] **Swaraj** | More than political freedom: **self-rule, self-control** and the capacity of the masses to govern themselves
  [3.3] Linked principles | **Swadeshi, ahimsa, satyagraha, decentralisation, education for all** (**class dictatorship** has no place)

## topic group2 1-161 | Gandhian Social and Economic Thought – Trusteeship, Sarvodaya, Swadeshi, Village Republic, Bread Labour
### part 0: Sarvodaya and the Bhoodan Movement
[0] h: Sarvodaya
[1] table: Fact | Key detail
  [1.0] **Sarvodaya** | Welfare / uplift of all, reaching the last person
  [1.1] Term | Used by **M.K. Gandhi** – title of his **1908** Gujarati paraphrase of Ruskin's Unto This Last
  [1.2] **Sarvodaya Day** | **30 January**
  [1.3] **Partyless democracy** | First advocated by **Jayaprakash Narayan** (*A Plea for Reconstruction of Indian Polity*, **1959**) – community-based politics without parties
[2] h: People and contributions
[3] table: Person | Contribution
  [3.0] **Gandhi** | Coined / used "Sarvodaya"; charkha and khadi
  [3.1] **Vinoba Bhave** | Made Sarvodaya the cornerstone; organised the **Sarvodaya Samaj**; started the **Bhoodan** (land-gift) movement
  [3.2] **Jayaprakash Narayan** | Drafted the **Sarvodaya Plan (1950)**
  [3.3] **Shriman Narayan Aggarwal** | **Gandhian Plan (1944)**; Gandhian Constitution for Free India (1946)
  [3.4] Tummala Sitaramamurthy | Wrote **Sarvodaya Ganam**; translated Gandhi's autobiography into Telugu
  [3.5] Ambedkar | Dissent: the village = seat of **caste domination**, "a sink of localism, a den of ignorance"
[4] x: **Bhoodan:** started by **Vinoba Bhave** on **18 April 1951** at **Pochampalli** (Nalgonda, now Telangana); landlords asked to gift at least **one-sixth** of their land.
### part 1: Gandhian Economy and Trusteeship
[0] h: Gandhian economy
[1] b: **Non-violent economy:** **centralisation leads to exploitation and inequality** → decentralise; **production for need (consumption), not profit**; self-reliance.
[2] b: Priority to **agriculture, village and cottage industries**; against mechanisation that displaces labour in labour-surplus India.
[3] b: A country's economy should suit its **climate, land and the nature of its citizens** → Gandhi **did not oppose mechanisation in the USA**.
[4] table: Fact | Key detail
  [4.0] Modern industry | Not opposed as such; close to **G.D. (Ghanshyam Das) Birla** (healthy capitalism working with Gandhi for a common object) and Ambalal Sarabhai
  [4.1] **Gandhian Innovation** | **Produce more from less input for more people** – coined by **C.K. Prahalad and R.A. Mashelkar**
  [4.2] **Ram Rajya** | **Ram Rajya** = sovereignty of the people based on **pure moral authority** (Harijan, **1937**) – not Hindu Raj
[5] h: Trusteeship
[6] table: Fact | Key detail
  [6.0] Idea | The rich hold wealth **in trust for society**; **class harmony**, not class struggle; capitalists as trustees of workers
  [6.1] Origin | **South Africa, 1903** (law office at Johannesburg); roots in the Gita's non-possession and the English law of trusts
  [6.2] Applied to | **Capitalists and zamindars** (e.g. Ahmedabad mill owners)
  [6.3] **Ahmedabad Mill Strike 1918** | **35% wage rise**; **Textile Labour Association (1920)** – arbitration
[7] b: **Practical Trusteeship Formula:** drafted by **Narhari Parikh and Kishorelal Mashruwala** (Aga Khan Palace), refined by M.L. Dantwala – **minimum living wage and a ceiling on income**.
### part 2: Swadeshi, Khadi and Rural Reconstruction
[0] h: Swadeshi and khadi
[1] table: Fact | Key detail
  [1.0] **Swadeshi + khadi** | Rural employment, **self-reliance**; bridge between towns and villages
  [1.1] **All India Spinners' Association** | **1925**
  [1.2] **All India Village Industries Association** | **1934**
  [1.3] **Ponduru (Srikakulam)** | Finest khadi

  [3.1] Rural development approach | **Samagra Gramseva** (comprehensive village service)
  [3.2] **"The future of India lies in its villages"** | **Mahatma Gandhi** – village republics, rural reconstruction
[4] h: Gandhian principles in the DPSP
[5] table: Article | Gandhian principle
  [5.0] **40** | Organise **village panchayats**
  [5.1] **43** | **Cottage industries** in rural areas (individual / co-operative)

### part 3: Books Behind the Ideas; Bread Labour
[0] h: Books behind the ideas

  [1.0] **Unto This Last** | **Ruskin** | Gujarati retelling "**Sarvodaya**" (**1908**); dignity of labour; a source of bread labour
