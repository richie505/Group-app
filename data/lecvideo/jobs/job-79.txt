### part 1: Dietary Fats, Cholesterol and Heart Health
[0] h: Good and bad fats
[1] table: Fat | Key detail
  [1.0] **Trans fat** | Raises **LDL** and **lowers HDL** ("good cholesterol"); excess raises cardiovascular risk
  [1.1] **Bakery products** | Made with **hydrogenated fats** → contain trans fats; excess trans fats raise the risk of NCDs
  [1.2] **WHO REPLACE plan** | Eliminate industrially produced trans fats from the global food supply **by 2023** (many countries still pending)
  [1.3] **FSSAI** cap | Trans fat at most **2%** in fats and oils
  [1.4] **Saturated fat** | Excess raises blood lipids and cholesterol
  [1.5] **HDL** | Removes bad cholesterol – high HDL lowers heart-disease risk
  [1.6] **Triglyceride** | Ester of glycerol + three **fat**ty acids = a fat; main constituent of body fat
  [1.7] **Cholesterol** (LDL) | Elevated in **most coronary heart disease patients**
[2] h: Oils and fatty acids
[3] table: Fact | Key detail
  [3.0] Oil vs **vanaspati ghee** | Cook in oil: oils hold **unsaturated fats** (mono- and polyunsaturated) that lower cholesterol
  [3.1] **Sunflower oil** | Suits **heart patients** because it is rich in unsaturated fatty acids; more vitamin E than other vegetable oils
  [3.2] **Highest PUFA** | **Safflower oil** (about 75% linoleic acid) > sunflower > soybean; **coconut oil** mostly saturated
  [3.3] **Fish** | Healthier than other meat – **polyunsaturated fatty acids** (omega-3) cut LDL and heart risk
  [3.4] **Eskimos (Inuit)** | Little heart disease despite a high meat/fat diet – **omega-3** from fish and marine mammals
  [3.5] **Linseed = flaxseed** | Richest source of **alpha-linolenic acid (18-carbon omega-3)**
  [3.6] **Essential fatty acids** | **Cannot** be made by the body – must be in the diet; absence causes deficiency disease; body cells make most other fatty acids
[4] table: Fatty acid | Source
  [4.0] **α-Linolenic (omega-3)** | **Linseed oil**
  [4.1] Linoleic | Peanut oil, chicken fat, olive oil
  [4.2] Arachidonic | Liver, brain, glandular organs
  [4.3] Oleic | Olive oil, canola oil
  [4.4] Lauric | Coconut / palm-kernel oil, breast milk
  [4.5] Palmitic | Butter, palm oil, meat
  [4.6] Stearic | Meat, cocoa butter
  [4.7] **Butyric** | **Butter**
[5] b: **Obesity:** excess energy intake + low physical activity → obesity and heart disease; NFHS-5 overweight/obese: **24% women, 23% men**.
[6] x: **Trans fat** lowers HDL; **oil** beats vanaspati; **safflower** has the most PUFA; **omega-3** – linseed, fish.

[10] h: Blood and circulation
[11] table: Fact | Key detail
  [11.0] **Blood** | A fluid **connective tissue** (plasma + formed elements); ~**5–6 L** in an adult; takes **oxygen** to all parts of the body
  [11.1] Blood pH | **7.35–7.45** (~**7.4**, slightly basic); held by buffers – **bicarbonate**, **phosphate** and proteins – plus lungs and kidneys

  [11.6] Bone marrow | RBCs, neutrophils and lymphocytes form in the **bone marrow** (of long bones)
  [11.7] **Thrombin** | Turns fibrinogen into fibrin (clotting)

  [11.10] Distribution | Veins hold ~70% of blood; cells ~45% of volume

  [48.5] **Giant panda** | Bear family (**Ursidae**)
  [48.6] **Mammoth** | Extinct relative of the **elephant** (Proboscidea)
  [48.7] **Peacock** | ***Pavo cristatus*** (national bird)
  [48.8] **Smallest bird** | **Bee hummingbird** (Cuba, ~5–6 cm); largest – ostrich
  [48.9] **Largest brain relative to body size** | **Ant**; largest absolute brain – sperm whale
  [48.10] **Monogamous** | **Wolf** (pair-bonds for life); walrus, seal, deer are polygynous
  [48.11] **Male frogs** | Croak louder using **vocal sacs** (resonating sound box); females lack them
  [48.12] **Wall lizard (gecko)** | Climbs walls and ceilings with **adhesive pads (setae) on its digits**
  [48.13] Fish ear | Internal ear chiefly an organ of **balance (equilibrium)**; the lateral line senses water movement
  [48.14] **Grasshopper 'ears'** | Tympanal organs on the **first abdominal segment** ('stomach' = abdomen)
  [48.15] **Honey bees** | Trained to sniff out **landmines** (explosive vapours)
  [48.16] **Pearl** | **Calcium carbonate (nacre)** made by **molluscs** (oysters); **lac** = secretion of the lac insect
  [48.17] **Hibernation** | Winter dormancy with **reduced metabolism**; aestivation = summer dormancy
  [48.18] **Analogous organs** | Different structure and origin, **same function** (bird and insect wings); homologous – same structure, different function

[50] table: Fact | Key detail
  [50.0] **Darwin's natural selection** | Main mechanism – **overproduction, struggle for existence, variation, survival of the fittest**; mutation supplies variation; acquired characters are not inherited

  [50.2] **Archaeopteryx** | Oldest known **bird** (**Jurassic**); **reptile–bird connecting link** (teeth, bony tail, claws + feathers)

  [50.4] **Dryopithecus and Ramapithecus** | Primates of ~**15 million years ago**; Dryopithecus more ape-like, **Ramapithecus more human-like** (first fossils in the **Siwalik hills**)
  [50.5] **Australopithecines** | Early hominids of **Africa, ~3–4 million years ago** – **bipedal**, small brains, stone tools, mainly fruits and vegetables
  [50.6] **Human ancestors** (old → recent) | Java man, Peking man (*H. erectus*), Neanderthal, **Cro-Magnon** (early modern *H. sapiens*)

[3] h: Storage, spoilage and ripening
[4] table: Fact | Key detail
  [4.0] **Cold storage** | Low temperature **lowers the rate of respiration** of fruits
  [4.1] **Frozen storage** | Meat such as pork at **−18 °C** or below; 3–5 °C is only chilling
  [4.2] **IQF** | **Individually Quick Frozen** – each piece frozen separately and fast
  [4.3] **Drying (sun drying, dehydration)** | Removes the moisture microbes need
  [4.4] **Silica gel** | Common **desiccant** (absorbs moisture in packets)
  [4.5] **Spoilage** | **Exposure** to air, moisture, microbes causes it; prevented by refrigeration, drying, salting, canning, preservatives
  [4.6] **Rancidity** | **Oxidation** of fats and oils (check the manufacture date of fried foods); nitrogen-flushed chip packets prevent it
  [4.7] **Retrogradation** of starch | Recrystallisation – main cause of **staleness** of bread
  [4.8] **Cut apple turns brown** | **Polyphenol oxidase** oxidises phenols in air (enzymatic browning)
  [4.9] **Blackheart of potato** | **Oxygen deficiency** in storage (an abiotic disorder)
  [4.10] **Ethylene** | Gaseous plant hormone for **fruit ripening** (and the gas used for artificial ripening)
  [4.11] **Calcium carbide** | Releases **acetylene**, which acts like ethylene – (illegally) used to ripen mangoes
