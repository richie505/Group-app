### part 1: Stupa, Chaitya, Vihara – Terms
[0] table: Fact | Key detail
  [0.0] **Chaitya** | **Prayer hall / place of worship** (Chaitya-shala, Chaitya-griha); rectangular, ending in an **apse (horseshoe plan)** with the stupa; horseshoe **chaitya window** on the facade
  [0.1] **Vihara** | **Monastery – dwelling of the monks**; square, cells round a central hall (**Madhyashala**)
  [0.2] **Stupa** | A **mound**, generally a **repository of relics**, and a **votive and commemorative** structure; the concept predates Buddhism
  [0.3] **Stupa, chaitya and vihara** | All **Buddhist** structures
  [0.4] **Arama** | Stupa + vihara + chaitya together (e.g. Guntupalli)
  [0.5] **Stupa types** | Dhatugarbha (bodily relics), Paribhogika (objects used by the Buddha), Uddeshika (symbolic)
  [0.6] **Uposatha** | Monks' meeting in the vihara's central hall every **15 days**
[1] table: Term | Meaning
  [1.0] Stupa | Mound
  [1.1] Garbhagriha | Place where the deity's image is installed
  [1.2] Mandapa | Place in temples where people assemble
  [1.3] Pradakshina patha | Circular path around the stupa
[2] x: **Chaitya** = place of worship; **Vihara** = dwelling of monks.

[0] table: Fact | Key detail
  [0.0] **Who** | A **Buddhist philosopher**; spread **Mahayana** Buddhism in Andhra
  [0.1] **Titles** | **"Second Buddha"**, Indian Martin Luther, Indian Einstein
  [0.2] **Philosophy** | Founder of the **Madhyamika** school; propounded **Sunyavada** (emptiness)

  [0.4] Other schools | **Syadvada** = Kundakundacharya (Jain)

  [0.7] **Death legend** | The Kathasaritsagara says a Satavahana prince had him killed

  [2.3] **Yogasara** | Credited to **Nagarjuna**
