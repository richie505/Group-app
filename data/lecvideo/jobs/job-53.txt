## topic group2 3-97 | Minimum Support Price – Mechanism, Cacp, Crops, Procurement, Criticism; Pm-aasha
### part 0: MSP – Meaning, CACP and Who Fixes It
[0] h: Meaning
[1] table: Fact | Key detail
  [1.0] **Minimum Support Price (MSP)** | A form of **market intervention** by the Government of India to insure farmers against a **sharp fall in farm prices**
  [1.1] Timing | Guarantee (floor) price announced **at the beginning of the sowing season**, before the crop reaches the market
  [1.2] **Purpose** | Save farmers from **distress sales**; **procure foodgrains for public distribution** (PDS buffer stocks → food security); **ensures a minimum fixed price** (not a promise of profit)
  [1.3] **Mechanism** | When the **market price falls below MSP** (bumper crop, glut), **government agencies purchase the stock offered** by farmers at MSP
  [1.4] **Who** | **CACP recommends → Government of India (Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs, CCEA) approves/fixes**
  [1.5] **Legal status** | An **administrative decision**, not backed by a statute; not a legal right
  [1.6] Government tools for agricultural marketing | **PDS, MSP and buffer stocks**
[2] h: CACP
[3] table: CACP fact | Key detail
  [3.0] Origin | **Agricultural Prices Commission (APC), January 1965** (same year as FCI)
  [3.1] Renamed | **CACP in 1985**
  [3.2] Status | **Attached office** of the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare; a decentralised agency, **not statutory**
  [3.3] Composition | Chairman + **Member Secretary + 1 official member + 2 non-official members**
  [3.4] Main objective | Recommend MSPs to **incentivise farmers to adopt modern technology**, raise productivity and output
  [3.5] Mandated objectives | **Stabilise agricultural prices; ensure meaningful real income to farmers; protect consumers via PDS**
[4] b: **Ashok Gulati** (CACP Chairman 2011-14): long-term food security needs agricultural growth of **more than 4% a year**.

### part 6: CACP Factors, Cost Concepts and the MSP Formula
[0] h: Factors CACP considers
[1] b: **Cost of production**, demand and supply, **price trends in domestic and international markets**, **inter-crop price parity**, terms of trade between agriculture and non-agriculture, a minimum 50% margin over cost, and the likely **effect on consumers / the economy** – including the effect of MSP on the cost of living (a **cost of living index** itself is not a factor).
[2] table: Cost concept | Meaning
  [2.0] **A2** | **Actual paid-out costs** (seed, fertiliser, hired labour, fuel, irrigation)
  [2.1] FL | Imputed value of **family labour**
  [2.2] **A2+FL** | Paid-out cost + family labour (leaves out rent on owned land and interest on fixed capital)
  [2.3] **C2** | **A2 + FL + land rent + interest on fixed capital** – the fullest cost
[3] table: Fact | Key detail
  [3.0] **Present formula (since 2018-19)** | MSP at least **1.5 × (A2+FL)** – a 50% margin over A2+FL; the support price is **"cost plus"** a margin
  [3.1] **Swaminathan Commission** (National Commission on Farmers, 2004-06) | MSP = **C2 + 50%**
  [3.2] Farmer unions (SKM) | Paddy at C2+50% should be ₹3,243/q for 2026-27 vs ₹2,441 fixed
  [3.3] **Farm running (working) capital** | Seed, fertiliser, irrigation water, hired labour; **land revenue** is a fixed charge
