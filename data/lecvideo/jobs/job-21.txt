### part 3: Telugu Language, Earliest Inscriptions and Court Languages
[0] h: The language

  [1.0] **Family** | **Dravidian** (South-Central branch, with **Gondi and Kui**)

  [1.2] **"Italian of the East"** | **Telugu** – coined by the Venetian **Niccolo de' Conti** (15th c.) because its words **end in vowels**
  [1.3] **"Desa bhashalandu Telugu lessa"** | **Krishnadevaraya**, in Amuktamalyada
  [1.4] **Official languages of AP** | **Telugu** (since 1966); **Urdu** as second official language in **all districts** since the 2022 amendment
  [1.5] **Classical language** | Telugu and **Kannada**, **31 October 2008**
  [1.6] **Telugu Language Day** | **29 August**, birth anniversary of **Gidugu Venkata Ramamurthy** (not Gurajada)
  [1.7] **Trilinga** theory | Srisailam, Kaleshwaram, Draksharama
  [1.8] **Origin debate** | **Chilukuri Narayana Rao** (Andhra Bhasha Charitramu): born of **Sanskrit** and the Prakrits; **Bishop Caldwell**: Dravidian
[2] h: Classical languages in order
[3] b: Tamil (2004), **Sanskrit (2005)**, Telugu and Kannada (2008), Malayalam (2013), Odia (2014); Marathi, Pali, Prakrit, Assamese and Bengali (**3 October 2024**) → **11** classical languages.
[4] h: Before written Telugu literature
[5] table: Fact | Key detail

### part 6: Nannaya – Adikavi and Andhra Shabda Chintamani
[0] table: Fact | Key detail
  [0.0] Role | **Court poet, teacher and adviser** of **Rajaraja Narendra**
  [0.1] Task | The Chalukyas, claiming lunar descent like the Pandavas, encouraged him to "Andhraise" the Mahabharata (c. 1045–65) as the Andhra Mahabharatam, helped by **Narayana Bhattu**
