# Find mental ability / aptitude questions among the apps' previous-year questions

The apps teach General Studies only (history, polity, economy, geography, science, environment, current affairs,
AP-specific topics). Mental ability / aptitude / reasoning questions must not be shown. Example that must go:
"During the last month a company organized three training programs ... 150 participants ... 100 attended both ...
Statement I: the number who attended only Technical is less than ... Statement II: more than 250 ..."

Aptitude = any question answered by calculation or logic on data given in the question rather than by GS knowledge:
sets / Venn counting, puzzles, seating, directions, blood relations, coding, series, analogies, odd one out,
syllogisms, statement-conclusion / assumption / course of action, data interpretation (tables, graphs, numbers
given), arithmetic (percentages, ratios, averages, interest, profit, time-work, speed, ages, mixtures), clocks,
calendars, dice, figures, English grammar / vocabulary / comprehension.

NOT aptitude (keep): GS facts that contain numbers (census figures, budget shares, years, article numbers, GDP
growth, percentages of area, scheme amounts), "consider the following statements" about GS facts, assertion-reason
on GS facts, science numericals that test a concept (e.g. "what is the escape velocity of Earth"). If unsure, keep.

Read your packet in full. Write data/pyq/apt/out-<N>.json: a JSON list of the ids (the [y...] codes) that are
aptitude. Validate it. Touch nothing else, do not commit. Reply with only: <N>: <count> aptitude of <total>.
