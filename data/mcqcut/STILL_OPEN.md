# Still open after source checks (v2.49)

Items the source check could not settle (sources disagree or no confirmation). Everything else on the review list was fixed or confirmed; see data/mcqcut/fixes_b*.md.

## History

- Q38.3 / Q48.24: left. The "most ancient" and "largest" stupa claims are source-specific (Sanchi, Bharhut, Piprahwa and Kesariya each get superlatives), and sources disagree.
- Q65.16: left. Chandragupta II as the first "Parama Bhagavata" (on his coins) is the standard view, and the key already follows it.
- Q108.39: left. Ibn Battuta's contact under Harihara I (coastal Honavar, 1340s) is real, and the stem is hedged ("is described as"). No source gives a clean alternative.
- Q11.9: left. This follows NCERT *An Introduction to Indian Art* ("more realistic in Gujarat sites and Kalibangan"), and it is not clearly wrong.
- Q15.2: left. "Ganga once, in Mandala 10 (Nadistuti)" is the standard exam fact. RV 6.45.31 ("gangya") is a scholarly quibble.
- Q117.19: left. Most sources give Ashrafi Mahal to Mahmud Khalji (1436-69), and one gives it to Hoshang Shah. "Late Malwa" is loose, and the notes cite UPSC, so sources disagree.
- Q118.36: left. The Kohinoor's origin is uncertain, but the statement is hedged and the 1656 Mir Jumla/Kollur tradition is standard in exam texts.
- Q91.2: left. Singhana II's reign is given as 1200-1247 and as c.1210-1246 in different sources.
- Q78.23: left. There is no factual error: Kalyanakaraka is by Ugradityacharya, and the WHY's Kavijanasrayam line is hedged ("associated with").
- Q48.15: left. "Mahishasaka the only Hinayana sect" looks doubtful, since Bahusrutiya and the Theravada Mahaviharavasins are also non-Mahayana, but I could not confirm which inscription list the notes mean.
- Q52.13: left. The APPSC 2012 key gives Rajagirika at Sriparvatam, and notes rows 29 and 52 give Guntupalli. No reliable source settles it.
- Q138.3: left. This is placement only and the content is correct. MCQs may not be moved.
- Q195.48 notes: left. No source found that confirms 1905 or 1906 for the Hitakarini Samajam.
- Siddhantam district (flag 039, Q250.34, Q194.123, notes rows 194 and 250): left. A Sidhantham exists in Penugonda mandal, West Godavari (Census 2011). But the GandhiServe 1933 chronology puts the 17 Dec 1933 Siddhantam temple opening between Gudivada, Gudlavalleru and Masulipatam, all in Krishna district, so "Krishna district" may well be right. Not changed.

## Polity

- Q24.36 nb4585b05ff6: left. 1830 Commons evidence (british-history.ac.uk) and BL catalogue say Munro's 1816 regulations moved the magistracy from the Judge TO the Collector, so "separated in 1816" looks backwards; 1802 (Cornwallis system in Madras) is likely but sources are weak/conflicting, so the correct year is not confirmed.
- Q35.29 n756a468156f: left, confirmed. The first PAC meeting for 2026-27 was held (22 May 2026, chaired by K.C. Venugopal; dynamitenews.com, punjabkesari.com), so statement 3 is rightly false.
- Q80.110 n352d0e016b3: left. Sources disagree: IASbaba and most prep material credit Ashok Mehta (1978) as first to seek constitutional status; APPSC 2018 key and others give L.M. Singhvi (1986).

## Economy

- Q1.40 / Q1.46 + notes: left. MoSPI's Provisional Estimates press note (Jun 2026, static.pib.gov.in) gives agriculture 4.2% (FY25) -> 3.0% (FY26), so Q1.46's key and R1 S9 / R50 S1 are right; the SAE figures (2.5%, FY25 4.3%) in R1 S6 / Q1.40 are labelled SAE and I could not find the Feb 2026 SAE press note to confirm them.
- Q63 notes (joint sector 1970 vs 1973): left. The 1970 licensing policy (after the Dutt Committee) and the 1973 Industrial Policy Statement are both cited for the joint sector; I found no source that settles one convention.
- Q132.48: left. R132 S2's "Survey overview" (fishing 16.0%, communications 15.28%) is a different estimate vintage from the 15.13% / 17.69% used everywhere else, and I could not find the AP Socio-Economic Survey 2025-26 online to confirm which one is current. The MCQ follows the majority figures.
- Q109.96: left. This is an APPSC 2019 PYQ (key "All the above", as in notes R109 S17). I could not find the Vision 2020 first-rank product list to rewrite the options.
- Q144.23: left. I could not confirm what the "~₹4,000 crore from UIDF" in R144 S4 funds. The MCQ uses it only in a wrong option, so the key is unaffected. Press reports (Deccan Chronicle, KNN, Oct 2026) put the Centre's share of the Rayalaseema hub at ~₹40,000 crore under Purvodaya, which does not match R144 S4's "Centre ₹10,000 crore + State ₹30,000 crore"; that needs a separate check.
