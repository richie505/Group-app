Cellular Jail); Nicobarese Cultural Continuity in the Central Nicobar Islands; Shekhawati Painted Haveli Towns, Rajasthan (merchants' havelis with murals); ICOMOS advises UNESCO on cultural sites and IUCN on natural sites; UNESCO HQ Paris; India ratified the Convention in 1977

- [23 Oct 2026] India approves accession to Southern Indian Ocean Fisheries Agreement (SIOFA): aims at long-term conservation and sustainable use of fishery resources in the Southern Indian Ocean; signed in Rome on 7 July 2006, in force June 2012; lets Indian vessels fish there under international rules; covers fish, molluscs, crustaceans but excludes highly migratory species in Annex I of UNCLOS; bodies: Meeting of the Parties (governing, at least annually), Scientific Committee, Secretariat
