### Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar → contributions and the Widow Remarriage Act, 1856
## 1. Profile
- Life: 1820-1891; Bengali Sanskrit scholar
- Title: "Vidyasagar" (Ocean of Learning) for his Sanskrit learning
- Posts:
- Principal of Sanskrit College, Calcutta → opened it to non-Brahmin students
## 2. Widow remarriage
- Campaign: Petitions + writings → Hindu Widows' Remarriage Act (Act XV of 1856) - widow remarriage made legal
- Governor-General:
- Drafted under Dalhousie (some tables list it under him); enacted July 1856 under Canning

- Provision: Also legitimised children of such marriages

- Maharashtra: Vishnu Shastri Pandit led the same campaign
## 3. Other women's causes

- Women's education:

### Marriage and consent Acts → ages (1872, 1891, 1929 and later) — Native Marriage Act (Civil Marriage Act), 1872
## 1. Native Marriage Act (Civil Marriage Act), 1872
- Act III of 1872: Passed at Keshab Chandra Sen's urging for Brahmo marriages - product of his campaign
- Minimum age 14 for the girl, 18 for the boy (APPSC-GS 2025 answer)
- Inter-caste marriage allowed
- Polygamy barred
- Other names: Native (Civil) Marriage Act, "Brahmo Marriage Act"; listed as "Brahma Act (Anti-Child Marriage Act)"; some notes say it "prohibited child marriage"
- 1878 Brahmo split: Keshab broke this Act by marrying his minor daughter to the Cooch Behar prince
## 2. Age of Consent Act, 1891 (Lansdowne)
- 1891 Act (Lansdowne): Age of Consent Act, 1891 raised the age of consent for girls 10 to 12 - from 10 (IPC 1860) to 12 (p.102)
- Regulated consummation, not marriage (some notes say it "prohibited marriage below 12")
- Later steps: Age of consent 14 (IPC amendment 1925) → 16 (1949) → 18 (POCSO 2012)
- BNS 2023: Section 63 keeps 18 (Jan 2026)
### Marriage and consent Acts → ages (1872, 1891, 1929 and later) — Child Marriage Restraint Act (Sarda Act), 1929
## 1. Child Marriage Restraint Act (Sarda Act), 1929
- Sarda Act:
- Passed 1929; in force from 1 April 1930 → some notes date it 1930
- Minimum marriage age girls 14, boys 18
- Moved by Harbilas Sarda, under Irwin
- Amended 1978: 18 (girls) / 21 (boys)
- Today: Prohibition of Child Marriage Act, 2006 = 18 for women, 21 for men
- A 2021 Union Cabinet proposal to raise women's age to 21 has not become law - still 18 (Jan 2026)
- ⚠ 14/18 = both the 1872 Act and the 1929 Sarda Act; 1891 = consent 12
