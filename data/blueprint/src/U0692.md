### S&T policies → year, Prime Minister, core focus; FIRSTs
| Policy | PM | Core focus / core thrust |
| Scientific Policy Resolution, 1958 | Jawaharlal Nehru | India's first comprehensive S&T policy; "foster, promote and sustain" cultivation of science "pure, applied and educational"; national labs and institutions |
| Technology Policy Statement, 1983 | Indira Gandhi | Technological competence and self-reliance; development, absorption and adaptation of technology; less import dependence (cut import dependence) |
| Science and Technology Policy, 2003 | A.B. Vajpayee | Brought science and technology together; stressed investment in R&D; S&T for sustainable development, poverty alleviation, quality of life |
| STI Policy, 2013 | Manmohan Singh | Added Innovation (made innovation central); theme "Science, Technology and Innovation for the People"; aim: India among top five global scientific powers by 2020 |
| Draft STIP, 2020 | Narendra Modi | India's 5th national S&T policy; decentralised, bottom-up consultation; self-reliance (Atmanirbhar Bharat), open science, gender equity |
- SPR 1958: came during the Second Plan (1956-61)
- Same year DRDO formed by merging TDE, DTDP and the Defence Science Organisation
- ⚠ 1958 – 1983 – 2003 – 2013 – 2020 = Nehru – Indira Gandhi – Vajpayee – Manmohan Singh – Modi
### Policy objectives and provisions — STP 2003, Decade of Innovation, STI 2013, STIP 2020 — S&T Policy 2003
## 1. S&T Policy 2003
- STP 2003 objectives:
- Promotion of research, innovation and technology transfer
- Optimal utilisation (use) of existing physical and knowledge resources also a stated objective
- ⚠ Key: all four listed objectives were correct (UPPSC 2008)
## 2. Decade of Innovation (2010-2020)
- Declaration: India declared 2010-2020 the "Decade of Innovation"
- Vehicle: National Innovation Council (2010) under Sam Pitroda
- Goal: inclusive innovation; state and sectoral innovation councils
## 3. STI Policy 2013
- STI 2013 targets: (STI Policy 2013)
- Raise India's share of global publications from 3.5% to over 7%
- Quadruple papers in top 1% journals
- Enhance private R&D; new PPP structures; high-risk innovation
- STI Policy 2013 (new):
- "small idea-small money" and "Risky Idea Fund"
- Fund for Innovations for Social Inclusion
- ~10 high-impact sectors for direct STI intervention
- Traditional knowledge through modern science
- Agricultural R&D policy left to ICAR
### Policy objectives and provisions — STP 2003, Decade of Innovation, STI 2013, STIP 2020 — Draft STIP 2020
## 1. Draft STIP 2020
- Draft STIP 2020 (new):
- Vision: Atmanirbhar Bharat - India among the top three scientific superpowers in the coming decade; people-centric STI ecosystem
- Double FTE researchers, GERD and private share of GERD every 5 years
- Open Science Framework:
- "One Nation, One Subscription" for pan-India journal access
- Portal INDSTA (Indian Science and Technology Archive of Research)
- Funding: National Research Foundation; hybrid funding with private capital and CSR; STI Development Bank (corpus fund for long-term strategic areas)
- Institutions and research quality:
- Research and Innovation Excellence Frameworks (RIEF) (BPSC 2025)
- Strategic Technology Board linking strategic departments
- Traditional Knowledge Systems and grassroots innovators
- Outcomes: ONOS and RIEF were outcomes
- ⚠ Trap (BPSC 2025 PYQ): Atal Innovation Mission was NOT an outcome of STIP 2020 - AIM set up by NITI Aayog in 2016 as a separate initiative promoting innovation among students
- ⚠ top five by 2020 = STI 2013; top three in the coming decade = STIP 2020
- Exam angle: "which is NOT an outcome/objective" of a given policy.
### GERD targets and data — policy comparison and reality
| Policy | GERD target |
| SPR 1958 | No numeric target [GK] |

### Bodies → role in S&T policy — ANRF
## 1. ANRF
- Status: statutory body under the ANRF Act, 2023
- Role: strategic direction for research and innovation; pools public and private R&D money
- Structure:
- PM = President
- S&T and Education ministers = Vice-Presidents
- Funding: ₹50,000 crore over 2023-28; about ₹36,000 crore expected from non-government sources
- Ease of Doing R&D in India report (Aug 2026): ~80% of ANRF funds go to IITs; slow approvals
## 2. NITI Aayog
- NITI Aayog:
- Nodal agency for improving India's GII rank
- Publishes India Innovation Index (3rd edition, 2021, released 21 Jul 2022)
- Hosts Atal Innovation Mission
