### Scientists → contributions and titles — Vikram Sarabhai
## 1. Vikram Sarabhai
- Title: Father of the Indian space programme
- INCOSPAR → ISRO: Headed INCOSPAR (1962) → became ISRO (1969)
- ISRO role: First Chairman until his death in 1971
- Other role: Chairman, Atomic Energy Commission (1966)
## 2. Har Gobind Khorana and Venkatraman Ramakrishnan - protein synthesis
| Scientist | Contribution |
| Har Gobind Khorana | interpreting the genetic code and its role in protein synthesis - hence "synthesis of protein" (BPSC 1994); first to chemically synthesise oligonucleotides → first synthetic gene (1970s); Profile: Indian-origin American molecular biologist |
| Venkatraman Ramakrishnan | Birthplace: Chidambaram, Tamil Nadu (APPSC-GS 2012); Indian-born American structural biologist, MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology, Cambridge; Discovery: Atomic structure of the 30S ribosomal subunit → insight into protein synthesis |
### Scientists → contributions and titles — M.S. Swaminathan
## 1. M.S. Swaminathan
- Green Revolution: Architect - high-yielding dwarf wheat and rice varieties (with Norman Borlaug)
- Title: "Father of Economic Ecology" by UNEP (UPPSC 2007)
- Commission: Chaired National Commission on Farmers, set up 18 November 2004
- On farmer suicides; reports = "Swaminathan report"
