### 106th Amendment (Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam) → where reservation applies
## 1. Baseline
- First constitutionally institutionalized through the 73rd and 74th Constitutional Amendment Acts, 1992 — not less than one-third (33.33%) of directly elected seats and Chairperson offices in rural and urban local bodies
- 106th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2023 — Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam — extends not less than one-third reservation to the Lok Sabha, State Legislative Assemblies and the Legislative Assembly of the National Capital Territory of Delhi
## 2. Where reservation applies
| Body | Article | Enabled by | Covers — not less than one-third |
| Panchayati Raj Institutions | 243D | 73rd Amendment, 1992 | Total seats and offices — directly elected seats (243D(3)); Chairperson posts at all three tiers (243D(4)) |
| Municipalities | 243T | 74th Amendment, 1992 | Total seats and offices — directly elected seats (243T(3)); Chairperson posts (243T(4)) |
| House of the People (Lok Sabha) | 330A | 106th Amendment, 2023 | Total seats — general seats and seats reserved for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes |
| State Legislative Assemblies | 332A | 106th Amendment, 2023 | Total seats — general seats and seats reserved for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes |
| Delhi Legislative Assembly (NCT) | 239AA(2)(ba) | 106th Amendment, 2023 | Total seats — directly elected seats and Scheduled Caste-reserved seats |
| Rajya Sabha and State Legislative Councils | — | No provision | Nil — strictly excluded from the scope of the 106th Amendment |
| Commencement and duration | 334A | 106th Amendment, 2023 | — (next page) |
- ⚠ Trap: The 106th Amendment applies to the Lok Sabha, State Legislative Assemblies and the Delhi Legislative Assembly — it does not provide 33% reservation in the Rajya Sabha or State Legislative Councils
- Article inserted by the 106th: 330A, 332A, 239AA(2)(ba), 334A
- Quota: One-third (1/3) of seats in Lok Sabha, State Assemblies and the Delhi Assembly, incl. one-third of SC/ST reserved seats
- Exclusions: No OBC/BC sub-quota (besides no reservation in Rajya Sabha / Councils)
- In force for: J&K and Puducherry Assemblies from 17 April 2026
### 106th Amendment → commencement, duration, rotation (Article 334A)
## 1. Commencement — Article 334A
- The pre-condition for the 106th Amendment reservation — does not come into force immediately
- Takes effect only after a delimitation exercise, following publication of the relevant figures of the first decennial Census conducted after the Act (first Census after the Act + the delimitation that follows: next Census and delimitation)
- Linked to the 2027 Census → not enforceable before 2034 under current law
- Duration: An initial 15 years from commencement — Parliament may extend it by law
- Rotation: Reserved seats rotated after every subsequent delimitation — as determined by parliamentary law (seats rotate, Art. 334A)

### 73rd/74th Amendments → Articles → women's reservation
## 1. Panchayats in detail — Article 243D
- 243D(2): Within seats reserved for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes, not less than one-third are reserved for SC and ST women — the mandatory sub-quota is one-third, ⚠ not 50%
- 243D(3): Not less than one-third of the total directly elected seats in every Panchayat — including SC and ST women
- 243D(4): Not less than one-third of Chairperson offices — at the village, intermediate and district levels
- Baseline: One-third (33.33%) — States have statutory discretion to increase it
- Bihar — the first State to grant 50% reservation for women in Panchayats (2006); followed by Madhya Pradesh, Uttarakhand and Himachal Pradesh
- Minimum age to contest Panchayat and Municipality elections: 21 years — Articles 243F and 243V — ⚠ not 25 years
## 2. Panchayats vs municipalities
| Provision | Panchayats (73rd) | Municipalities (74th) |
| Women's seats, not less than 1/3 of directly elected seats, at least one-third (incl. SC/ST women); same one-third rule for municipalities | Art. 243D(3) | Art. 243T(3) |
| 1/3 of seats reserved for SCs/STs go to SC/ST women | 243D(2) | - |
| Women chairpersons, not less than 1/3 of chairperson offices at each level | Art. 243D(4) | Art. 243T(4) |
| Rotation of reserved seats | Allowed | Allowed |
- Women's one-third: Compulsory; constitution sets a floor, not a ceiling
- Backward classes: Voluntary/optional; States may reserve seats for BCs
- SC/ST seats: At every level in proportion to population
- Rotation: Reserved seats + chairperson offices (as State law provides) allotted by rotation
- ST seats in municipalities: Art. 243T(1) (MPPSC 2020)
- 73rd in force: 24 April 1993; Exception: Not applicable to Arunachal Pradesh (no SC population)
- Sunset clause:
- SC/ST reservation (seats + chairpersons): ends with Art. 334 period, now 2030 under the 104th Amendment (Art. 243D(5))
- Women's reservation has no such sunset
- ⚠ Trap: Statement 2 in UPPSC 2017 is false
- ⚠ 243D(3) = women's seats; 243D(4) = women chairpersons; 243T = the same for municipalities
