# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:33
### Arthashastra
- Arthashastra (Kautilya) — rediscovered at Mysore in 1905 by R. Shamasastry; published 1909
- 15 books called adhikaranas
- Subject — administration, economy, espionage and taxation
### Indica of Megasthenes
## Indica
- ★ Indica — written by Megasthenes
- Pataliputra called Palibothra (also Polibrotha)
- Society in seven classes; endogamy and hereditary occupations; not the fourfold varna scheme
- Claimed no slavery in India — contradicted by the Arthashastra and Ashokan edicts (which mention dasas)
- First foreign work to mention the Andhras — 30 fortified towns, 1,00,000 infantry, besides cavalry and elephants
- Strabo and Arrian only kept parts; Deimachus was Antiochus I's envoy to Bindusara
## Kautilya
- Other names — Chanakya and Vishnugupta (not Pushyagupta)
- Pushyagupta — governor linked with the Sudarshana lake
### Literary works
- ★ Number of Puranas — 18
- Vishnu Purana — usually used for Mauryan king lists and genealogies
- Mudrarakshasa (Sanskrit drama) — Vishakhadatta; overthrow of the Nandas, rise of Chandragupta Maurya
- Mamulanar (Sangam) — refers to the Mauryas' southward push
### Buddhist & Jain texts
| Source | Subject |
| Parishishtaparvan | Chandragupta's Jain end |
| Puranas | genealogies |
| Divyavadana | Ashokan legends |
| Mahavamsa / Dipavamsa | Buddhism in Lanka |
### Inscriptions & archaeology
- James Prinsep — deciphered the Ashokan edicts in 1837
- Greek-Aramaic edict — Shar-i-Kuna (Kandahar); Girnar/Junagadh is linked with Rudradaman's inscription
- Junagadh inscription of Rudradaman I (c. 150 CE) — governor Pushyagupta built the Sudarshana lake under Chandragupta Maurya
- 80-pillared hall of Pataliputra — Kumrahar near Patna
### Exam corner
- Ashoka called his edicts Dhammalipi
### Indica of Megasthenes → observations and errors
- ★ First to mention forts in Andhra — Megasthenes (Indika, 4th c. BC): 30 fortified towns, a lakh of infantry, 2,000 cavalry, 1,000 elephants
- ★ Polibrotha / Palibothra — Pataliputra (Patna)
- ★ Indica — states no slavery in India; gives city administration of the Mauryas; mentions society in seven classes
- ★ Pataliputra administration — six committees of five members each; town officials called Astynomoi
- ★ Author of Indica — Megasthenes
