### AP first in supporting women entrepreneurs
## 1. What happened
- Union Ministry of Rural Development report — ⚠ AP ranks first in promoting women entrepreneurs
- 2025–26: individual business loans to 1.47 lakh DWCRA women — 99% of target

- AP also leads in PMFME use — 35,934 beneficiaries; DWCRA loans ₹33,614 crore vs target ₹32,000 crore; CM target 5 lakh women entrepreneurs this year

- Chetana (Anantapur district police) — ⚠ first such programme in AP for transgender persons: free training in computers, tailoring, handicrafts; self-employment and private jobs; slogan "Steps towards the right path... foundations for a proud life"

### Mango exports — AP varieties in demand
## 1. What happened
- Top importer 2025–26: UAE — ₹144.41 crore; shipments up 11,622 MT
- AP varieties in demand: Banganapalli, Benishan, small Rasalu
- AP share: about 8–10% of India's mango exports
- Markets: 52 countries (DGCIS, APEDA data)
| Market | Trend 2025–26 |
| UAE | Top importer, ₹144.41 crore |
- Dominant varieties: Alphonso, Kesar, Khader
## 2. Background
- APEDA: statutory, APEDA Act 1985; Ministry of Commerce and Industry; HQ ⚠ New Delhi (not Mumbai)
### EESL MSME sustainability plan for AP
## 1. What
- Agency: Energy Efficiency Services Limited (EESL) — PSU under Union Ministry of Power
- Aim: energy efficiency and water efficiency in AP MSME sector — called one of India's largest MSME efficiency programmes
- Implemented under: Centre's RAMP Programme + AP MSME & Entrepreneur Development Policy 4.0
- Work order: handed to EESL at the AP MSME Growth Summit
## 3. Background
- RAMP: Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance — World Bank-supported Central Sector Scheme, launched 2022, by Ministry of MSME, period 2022-23 to 2026-27
- EESL: known for LED lighting, e-mobility, smart meters programmes
### 16th Finance Commission grants to AP local bodies
## 1. Facts
- Total: ₹16,627 crore for 2026–27 to 2030–31 — GPs, Mandal and Zilla Parishads
- Split: ₹13,302 crore Basic + ₹3,325 crore Performance; ⚠ ₹3,827 crore more than 15th FC
- Share: Gram Panchayats 70%, Zilla Parishads 15%, Mandal Parishads 15%
- Conditions: local body elections held, e-Gram Swaraj records, State Finance Commission constituted
- Background: FC under Article 280; 16th FC chair Dr. Arvind Panagariya
- 16th FC local-body grants: ₹16,627 cr (rural) + ₹12,158 cr (urban)
### VB-GRAM in AP from 1 July 2026
## 1. Facts
- Scheme: Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Aajeevika Mission (Grameen) [VB-GRAM] — implemented in AP from 1 July 2026
- AP allocation: ₹12,845.35 crore — Centre ₹7,707.21 crore (60%), State ₹5,138.14 crore (40%)
- Components: wages ₹7,071 crore, materials ₹4,714 crore, admin ~₹1,060 crore; 13.90 crore person-days
- Start date: was due 1 April 2026, deferred to 1 July 2026
### AP tops India in pulse production growth
## 1. Facts
- ⚠ AP 1st in pulse production growth in 2025–26 — +57.73%; UP 2nd (+17.7%)
- Production: 9.96 → 15.71 lakh tonnes (+5.75 lakh t); area 28.45 → 34.70 lakh acres; green gram the largest share
- Reasons: self-sufficiency drive, quality seeds, pest management, value addition, higher MSP
- AP also: among top 5 States in number of establishments (6th Economic Census); 3rd in ongoing Central projects (142)
