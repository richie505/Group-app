### Science communication and educational channels → run by
## 1. Vigyan Prasar and its platforms
- Vigyan Prasar: autonomous organisation of the Department of Science & Technology (DST), Ministry of Science & Technology; set up 1989, New Delhi
- ⚠ Trap: NOT under the Ministry of HRD/Education (UPSC 2005)
| Platform | What it is |
| DD Science | One-hour daily science slot on DD National (Doordarshan) [GK] |
| India Science | Internet-based (OTT) science channel, run by Vigyan Prasar [GK] |
| Vigyan Dhara | DST umbrella scheme merging 3 DST schemes: S&T institutional and human capacity building; research & development; innovation, technology development and deployment (Cabinet, Aug 2024) [GK] |
- Launch: DD Science and India Science launched together on 15 January 2019 by the Union Minister for Science & Technology
- Exam angle: "two new science communication platforms" = DD Science and India Science (APPSC 2019), not Gyan Darshan/Gyan Vani or DD Kisan.
