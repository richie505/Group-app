### Art. 275 vs Art. 282 grants
- Article 282 — grants for any public purpose chosen by the executive
- Article 275 — grants for States in need, ST welfare, Scheduled Areas
- Plan grants ended when the Planning Commission was abolished in 2015
### Cesses & surcharges
- 16th FC "grand bargain": States accept a smaller share of a larger pool if most cesses merged into regular taxes
- Cesses and surcharges per ₹100 of Central tax: decade high ₹13.5 in 2021–22; ₹11 estimated for 2025–26
- Headline devolution 41% of divisible pool; effective devolution only 30–32% of gross tax revenue (cesses/surcharges outside pool)
### Vertical fiscal imbalance
- Basic cause: Union holds larger, faster-growing tax bases (corporation tax, income tax, most of GST) while States carry heavy spending duties
- Worsened by cesses and surcharges outside the divisible pool, loss of indirect taxes to GST, CSS conditions, weak State revenue
