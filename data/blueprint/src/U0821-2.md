### Russia imports gasoline from India
## 1. News
- ⚠ Shift in energy trade: India usually imports Russian crude; now Russia buys India-refined gasoline (refinery disruptions at home; also from Belarus)
- Volume: ~42,000 tonnes, refined at Nayara Energy's Vadinar Refinery (Gujarat), transshipped via Egypt's Damietta port — Russia's first gasoline import from India
- Loaded: 18 June 2026; first cargo reached Russia August 2026; Nayara has ownership links with Rosneft
## 2. India's Russian crude
- Russia >51% of India's oil imports in July 2026 = all-time high
- Had fallen below 20% in Jan 2026 after U.S. 50% tariffs
- Asked before: China's seaborne Russian crude 1.82 mn bpd vs India's 1.36 mn (Mar 2024)

## 1. What happened
- India–UK Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA) in force on July 15, 2026
- ⚠ Sixth FTA implemented by India — after Mauritius, UAE, Australia, EFTA, Oman

### India–Oman CEPA comes into force
## 1. Key facts
- In force: 1 June 2026
- Duty-free access: 99.38% of India's exports by value; covers 98.08% of Oman's tariff lines (earlier only 15.33% zero duty under MFN)
- Other notes round it: 99% of India's exports duty-free; India's 2nd GCC CEPA (LENSD 2 Jun 2026)
- Pharma: medicines approved by USFDA, EMA, UK MHRA, TGA get Omani authorisation within 90 days
- Recognises: NPOP organic certification, Export Inspection Council certificates, halal certification

- Bilateral trade: USD 11.18 billion (FY 2025–26)
- Oman: capital Muscat, Sultan Haitham bin Tarik; GCC est. 1981, HQ Riyadh — Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE
