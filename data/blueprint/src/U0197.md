### Colonial scholars of Telugu → contributions
| Scholar | Contribution |
| Benjamin Schultze | German Lutheran (Danish-Halle Mission); came 1719; Telugu grammar; returned 1743, printed 4 Telugu books at Halle, 1747 |
| Colin Mackenzie (1754-1821) | First Surveyor General (1815); collected Kaifiyats (manuscripts) with the Kavali brothers (Borraiah, Venkataramaiah, Lakshmaiah); reached Amaravati Stupa in 1797 |
| William Carey | Serampore Mission; complete Telugu Bible - NT 1812, full 1818-19; Telugu typefaces |
| A.D. Campbell | First systematic Telugu Grammar (1816) + Telugu-English dictionary - tools for missionaries and Telugu scholars |
| Thomas Munro | Kept Telugu as an administrative language |
| C.P. Brown (1798-1884) | Collector of Cuddapah; learnt Telugu from Kodandarama Pantulu; Telugu-English & English-Telugu dictionaries (1853), Grammar (1840); collected ~2,000 Vemana poems, Verses of Vemana (1829); called Telugu "Italian of the East" (phrase first from Nicolo de Conti) |
