## 1. Patrons
| Patron | Contribution |
| Ashoka's missions | Mahinda + Sanghamitta → Sri Lanka; Sona + Uttara → Suvarnabhumi |
| Siri Satakarni (Satakarni I/II) | Sanchi south torana |

- Upasika Bodhisri: Some notes list her with Veerapurushadatta's officials

## 2. Decline
- Decline: Loss of patronage; Brahmanical revival (Bhakti); Vajrayana ritualism
- Hostile rulers: Pushyamitra Shunga, Shashanka, Mihirakula; Turkish raids on Nalanda
- ⚠ Bodhisri = Ehuvala Chantamula's reign; Vishnukundins = LAST Andhra patrons
