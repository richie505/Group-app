### G20 → structure, India's 2023 Presidency and Johannesburg 2025
## 1. Structure
- G20 structure:
- Finance track + Sherpa track

## 2. India's 2023 Presidency
- Summit: 18th G20 Summit, New Delhi, 9-10 Sep 2023, at Bharat Mandapam
- Theme: "One Earth, One Family, One Future" (Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam)
- Headline outcome: African Union admitted as permanent member
- Presidency term: 1 Dec 2022 – 30 Nov 2023; meetings across about 60 cities in all States/UTs
- Launched under India's G20 Presidency (2023): Global Biofuels Alliance (GBA); MAHARISHI (millets research initiative); Global Initiative on Digital Health; "G20 satellite" for climate monitoring
- ⚠ 18th G20 = New Delhi 2023; 18th BRICS = New Delhi 2026 — both at Bharat Mandapam
## 3. 20th G20, Johannesburg (Nov 2025)
- Theme: "Solidarity, Equality, and Sustainability"; spirit of Ubuntu philosophy ("I am because we are"); host South Africa
- Johannesburg Leaders' Declaration (adopted unusually early): critical-minerals value-addition framework; MDB reform; first standalone debt declaration since COVID; Cost of Capital Commission on borrowing costs for developing economies
- African Union: permanent member (2023); fully operationalised at Johannesburg 2025
- Digital Innovation Alliance: G20 2025, by India, Brazil, South Africa — DPI as a Global South alternative
- India's proposals at G20 2025:
- Africa Skills Multiplier (1 million trainers in a decade)
- Open Satellite Data Partnership
