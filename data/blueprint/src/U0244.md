### Congress sessions and the Tripuri crisis
## 1. National Planning Committee — 1938
- Set up under Subhas Chandra Bose — Chairman Jawaharlal Nehru; Secretary K.T. Shah
- Forerunner of planning; APPSC 2022: National Planning Committee: 1938
## 2. Sessions
| Session | Year | Note |
| Haripura (Gujarat) | 1938 | 51st session; Congress president; National Planning Committee set up |
| Tripuri (Madhya Pradesh) | 1939 | Re-elected, then resigned |
- Tripuri, 1939: Moderates vs radicals for the presidency
- Bose defeated Gandhi-backed Dr. Pattabhi Sitaramayya (1,580 to 1,377)
- Gandhi's reaction: "Pattabhi's defeat is my defeat."
- Dispute: Over forming the Working Committee; old guard refused to cooperate
- Pant Resolution: Asked Bose to name the Working Committee in line with Gandhi's wishes
- Outcome: Working Committee deadlock → Bose resigned (April 1939) → Dr. Rajendra Prasad became President
- Exam angle: APPSC 2018 asked whom Bose defeated at Tripuri - Pattabhi Sitaramayya.
- ⚠ National Planning Committee set up under Bose, but its chairman was Nehru

### Indian National Army — formation and phases
## 1. Formation
- INA stands for Indian National Army — also known as Azad Hind Fauj
- Brainchild of Giani Pritam Singh Dhillon and Major Iwaichi Fujiwara
- Captain Mohan Singh: The first operational commander — Mohan Singh's INA was formed under his leadership in July 1942 (the INA was first formed in 1942)
- December 1942: Dissolved or reorganised
## 2. Under Subhas Chandra Bose
- Rash Behari Bose: A key organiser — handed over INA command to Subhas Chandra Bose
- July 1943: Subhas Chandra Bose took command — the Supreme Commander of the INA
- ⚠ Sources differ: other notes say Bose took over the IIL and INA in July 1943 but was Supreme Commander of the INA from 25 Aug 1943; HQ Singapore, later Rangoon
## 3. Phases (Prep notes)
- First INA place: South-East Asia (Singapore/Malaya); some notes say "in Japan"
- June 1942: IIL leaders met the Japanese to set up the INA
