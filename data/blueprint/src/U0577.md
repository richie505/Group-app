### Sections of APRA 2014 → provisions — Key sections
## 1. Key sections
| Section | Provision |
| 3 / 4 | Telangana formed from 10 districts [GK]; the Khammam villages in the Polavaram submergence area stayed with AP (the Polavaram Ordinance amended Sec 3) / residual AP |
| 5 | Hyderabad common capital for up to 10 years; afterwards Telangana's capital, with a new capital for AP |
| 7 / 8 | Common Governor / Governor's special responsibility in the common capital |
| 10 | Amends the First Schedule of the Constitution; Telangana becomes the 29th State |
| 46 | 13th FC award split by population ratio and other parameters; grants and a special development package for backward areas, especially Rayalaseema and North Coastal AP |
| 84 | Apex Council: Union Water Resources Minister (chair) and both CMs, 3 members |
| 85 | GRMB (HQ Telangana) and KRMB (HQ AP) within 60 days; security by CISF |
| 89 | KWDT term extended for project-wise allocation |
| 90 | Polavaram declared a national project; Telangana's consent deemed given |
| 93 | Centre to take the Thirteenth Schedule measures within 10 years |
| 94 | (1) tax incentives; (2) backward-area programmes; (3) special financial support for the capital: Raj Bhavan, High Court, Secretariat, Assembly, Council; (4) denotify degraded forest land for the capital |
- ⚠ GRMB HQ = Telangana; KRMB HQ = AP
- ⚠ 89 = KWDT; 90 = Polavaram
