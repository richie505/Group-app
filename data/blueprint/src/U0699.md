### Scientists → contributions
| Scientist | Contribution |
| J.C. Bose | Showed plants respond to stimuli like animals - "plants have life" (MPPSC 2015); pioneer of microwave/millimetre-wave radio - coherer + galena (semiconductor) detector; worked at IACS |
| Srinivasa Ramanujan | Origin: born 22 December 1887, Erode (TN); collaborator G.H. Hardy at Cambridge; number theory, partitions, infinite series, continued fractions, mock theta functions; died 1920 |
| C.V. Raman | Discovery: Raman Effect; Background: worked at IACS outside office hours while in the Accountant General's Office, Calcutta (from 1907), on the invitation of Amrit Lal Sircar; Other work: first to study the harmonic nature of tabla and mridangam; first Indian director of IISc |
| Meghnad Saha | Origin: Dacca district (now Bangladesh); Saha (ionisation) equation from his theory of thermal ionisation of elements (UPPSC 2014); instrument measures the weight and pressure of solar rays; Chief architect of river planning in India - prepared the original plan for the Damodar Valley Project; Politics: elected to Lok Sabha (1952) |
| S.N. Bose | Key paper: "Planck's Law and the Hypothesis of Light Quanta" → Bose-Einstein Statistics |
| Homi Bhabha | Title: "Father of India's nuclear programme"; AEC: first chairman of the Atomic Energy Commission (1948); conceived India's three-stage nuclear power programme (thorium-based); UN role: presided over the first UN Conference on Peaceful Uses of Atomic Energy, Geneva 1955; died in Air India crash on Mont Blanc, 1966 |

### Six great scientists
## J.C. Bose
- Microwave and millimetre-wave radio pioneer — coherer and galena detector
- Books — The Nervous Mechanism of Plants, Response in the Living and Non-Living, Niruddesher Kahini (Bengali sci-fi)

- Ramanujan — worked with G.H. Hardy at Cambridge; 1729 — Hardy-Ramanujan number

- IACS — J.C. Bose, Meghnad Saha, S.N. Bose and C.V. Raman all worked there
