## Visakhapatnam
- Eastern Naval Command HQ Visakhapatnam, faces the Bay of Bengal; India's largest naval command
- Visakhapatnam holds one of India's three Strategic Petroleum Reserves

### Energy security
## India's energy profile
- India imports about 88% of crude oil and about 50% of natural gas (older figures 80% and 25%)
- India is the third-largest energy consumer (not second)
- Four 'A's of energy policy: availability, accessibility, affordability, acceptability (not autonomy)
## Energy bodies
- ISA launched 30 November 2015 at COP21, Paris; treaty-based; HQ Gurugram
- Global Biofuels Alliance launched 2023 at the G20 Summit in India
- India — Association country of the IEA since 2017; not a full member
- IEF secretariat at Riyadh; India hosted the 16th IEF ministerial in 2018 (not 2023)
