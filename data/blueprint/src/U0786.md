### Carbon market — CCTS and Green Credit Programme: Acts, sections, administering bodies — CCTS
## 1. CCTS
- Status: India's first mandatory compliance carbon market (Indian Carbon Market); designed to meet the 45% intensity target
| Mechanism | Design |
| Compliance | Compliance mechanism for energy-intensive sectors: obligated entities get GHG Emission Intensity (GEI) targets; those beating them earn tradeable Carbon Credit Certificates, those missing them must buy |
| Offset | Offset mechanism for voluntary projects in 10 sectors (e.g. agriculture, waste); approved methodologies (nine published) |
- Coverage:
- GEI targets notified Oct 2025 for aluminium, cement, chlor-alkali, and pulp and paper → 282 obligated entities
- Now 7 sectors, ~490 industries, ~477 Mt CO2e; only China, EU and South Korea cover more
- UK recognition: CCTS = qualifying carbon price under UK CBAM (from 1 Jan 2027) → avoids double charging of Indian exports

### NDC targets (base year 2005)
## Targets by version
- Base year for emissions-intensity targets — 2005
- 2015 INDC: 40% non-fossil target was for installed capacity, not actual electricity generation
- Updated NDC sent August 2022 — formalised Panchamrit, added Mission LiFE
- Panchamrit pledges in it: 45% emissions-intensity cut by 2030 and 50% energy from renewables (2030)
- NDC 3.0 — 60% non-fossil installed-capacity share (45% is the 2022 intensity target)
- Mission LiFE (Lifestyle for Environment) written into NDC 3.0; shifts focus from state action to individual behaviour and careful consumption
## Progress and debates
- Emissions intensity of GDP fell 36% below 2005 level by 2020
- Non-fossil generation share need not equal installed-capacity share — different measures
- Criticism: capacity alone does not show actual electricity generated; may overstate the shift
- Carbon Credit Trading Scheme — designed to meet the 45% intensity target
- Net zero ≠ zero gross emissions: residual emissions balanced by removals (forests, carbon capture)
- India supports CBDR-RC (Common but Differentiated Responsibilities); does not reject it
