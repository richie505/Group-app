### Census — FIRSTs, legal basis, Acts and Articles
## 1. FIRSTs
| FIRST | FACT |
| First census 1872 | non-synchronous, Lord Mayo |
| First complete/synchronous census 1881 | Lord Ripon; decennial since |
| Census 2011 = 15th census since 1872 | 7th after Independence; motto "Our Census, Our Future"; reference date 1 March |
| Census 2027 | first digital census (digital, mobile-app-based; self-enumeration) |
| Census 2027 | first caste count in Independent India beyond SC/ST; includes caste enumeration |
- ⚠ 1872 = first (Mayo, non-synchronous); 1881 = first synchronous (Ripon)
## 2. Legal basis and custodian
- Legal basis: Census Act, 1948; Union List Entry 69 (Art. 246)
- Conducted by / custodian: Registrar General and Census Commissioner of India under Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA)
## 3. Amendments tied to the census
| Act / Article | Provision |
| 42nd and 84th Amendments | delimitation - seat freeze on 1971 census, till 2026; freeze till first census after 2026 |
| 106th Amendment, 2023; Art. 334A | women's reservation |
### Census 2027 — delay, questions and timeline
## 1. Delay and features
- Delay: Census 2021 postponed (COVID-19) → Census 2027, 16 years after 2011
- Announcement: 4 June 2025 → census with caste enumeration, to finish by 28 Feb 2027
- Questions: 40 questions notified on 14 Aug 2026
- 13 of them new (e.g. digital literacy, bank accounts); 2011 had 29
- open-ended caste column
## 2. Timeline
| Census 2027 phase | Timing |
| I - House Listing and Housing Census (HLO) | April-September 2026 (1 April - 30 September 2026) |
| II - Population Enumeration (PE) | February 2027; reference date 00:00 hrs, 1 March 2027 |
- Snow-bound areas: Ladakh, J&K, HP, Uttarakhand → 17 Aug - 30 Sep 2026 (Sep 2026; reference 1 Oct 2026)
### Terms → meaning — density, Census Town, "Great Divide"
| Term | Meaning |
| Density | Persons per unit area (per sq km); crude measure of human-land relationship |
| Physiological density | Total population / net cultivated area |
| Agricultural density | Agricultural population / net cultivable area |
| 1921 = "Year of the Great Divide" | Only census with a decline (influenza pandemic, famines); population has grown every decade since |
| Census Town | Village-status settlement with population >= 5,000, >= 75% of male workers outside agriculture, density >= 400/sq km |
- Census Town: Unchanged since 1981; RGI will retain it for Census 2027 though MoHUA calls it outdated (male bias, uniform thresholds)
### Decadal growth 1901-2011 — phases and data
## 1. Phases
| Phase | Period | Features |
| I - Stagnant/stationary | 1901-1921 | High birth and death rates; growth negative in 1911-21 (-0.31%) |
| II - Steady growth | 1921-1951 | Mortality fell (health, sanitation, transport) while birth rate stayed high |
| III - Population explosion | 1951-1981 | Rapid fall in mortality, high fertility; average annual growth about 2.2%; migration from Tibet, Bangladesh, Nepal, Pakistan |
| IV - High but declining / slowdown | Post-1981 | Crude birth rate falls: later marriage, female education |
- Phases (sub-dates):
- Explosion = 1961-1971 (some notes give 1951-81)
- Peak decadal growth 24.80% (1961-71)
- High-but-declining = 1981-2011

- ⚠ Only decline = 1911-21; peak = 1961-71 (24.80%)
