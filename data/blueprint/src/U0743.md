### Energy and electricity terms → meaning
## 1. Electrical energy
- One kilowatt-hour: The energy consumed by a 1 kW device in 1 hour — 3.6 × 10⁶ joules
- One unit of electricity = one kilowatt-hour — the board definition: energy equivalent to 1 kWh; electricity cost is charged per unit in kWh
- Energy shortage is counted in MU (million units)
## 2. Energy terms

### India's energy demand → global rank and fuel mix
## 1. Global rank and outlook
- Global rank: Third-largest energy consumer (2026)
- Coal: World's second-largest coal consumer (2025-26)
- Outlook: Energy demand expected to triple by 2047 (22 Apr 2026)
## 2. Fuel-wise share
- Coal: 55% of the energy mix + over 74% of power generation (2025-26)
- Installed power capacity (2026) ranking:
- Coal (230 GW) → solar (157 GW, ~29%) → wind (56.8 GW) → large hydro (52 GW) → oil & gas (20.7 GW) → bio-power (11.75 GW) → nuclear (8.78 GW) → small hydro (5.18 GW)
- Second source after fossil fuels: Solar energy (UPPSC 2021 key)
- Energy crisis: Danger that fossil fuels like coal and petroleum will run out (UKPSC 2006 key)
