### Ganga — course and tributaries by bank
## 1. Course of the Ganga
- Bhagirathi: Originates at Gomukh in Uttarkashi district, Uttarakhand — Uttarkashi is situated on it
- Alaknanda: Originates at Satopanth Glacier above Badrinath
- Alaknanda and Bhagirathi meet at Devprayag, Uttarakhand, to form the Ganga (called Ganga from here)
- Enters the plains at Haridwar; flows through Farakka in West Bengal before entering Bangladesh — known as Padma in Bangladesh
- Lower course: swings south at Farakka barrage (WB); left arm: Bhagirathi-Hooghly; right arm: Padma into Bangladesh → joins Brahmaputra and Meghna → enters the Bay of Bengal as the Meghna
- Navigation: navigable up to Prayagraj
## 2. Tributaries of the Ganga
| Bank | Tributaries |
| Left-bank (Himalayan) | Ramganga, Gomati, Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi, Mahananda (easternmost) |
| Right-bank (Peninsular) | Yamuna — the longest tributary of the Ganga (Yamunotri; joins at Prayagraj); Son; Tons, Punpun, Damodar |
- Order of confluences below Prayagraj: Tons (R) → Gomti (L) → Ghaghara (L) → Son (R) → Gandak (L) → Kosi (L) → Mahananda (L)
- Exam angle: "south-based tributary" = Betwa/Son/Yamuna group; Burhi Dihing and Dhansiri are Brahmaputra tributaries, not the Ganga's (PYQ 2024: neither statement correct).
### Ganga — left-bank tributaries, Son and Damodar
## 1. Left-bank tributaries in detail
- Ramganga: Joins Ganga near Kannauj, Uttar Pradesh
- ⚠ Sources differ: other notes say it joins at Farrukhabad
- Ghaghra (Ghaghara): Originates at Mapchachungo Glacier; meets Ganga at Revilganj in Saran district, Bihar
- Sharda — also called Kali — joins the Ghaghra in the plains, before the Ghaghara meets the Ganga
- Gandak: Originates in the Nepal Himalayas; joins Ganga at Sonpur near Patna, Bihar
- = Narayani in Nepal; enters India at Valmikinagar; Sadanira of the Shatapatha Brahmana (28 Aug 2026)
- Kosi: Tributaries — Sun Kosi, Arun Kosi, Tamur Kosi
- Mahananda: The last left-bank tributary of Ganga — joins Ganga in West Bengal
## 2. Son and Damodar
- Son: Originates at Amarkantak Hill; joins Ganga near Patna, Bihar — near Maner, Patna (1 Sep 2026)
- Tributaries: Johilla, Mahanadi, Banas, Gopad, Rihand, Ghogher, Kanhar, North Koel
- Damodar: Originates at Khamarpat Hill in the Chota Nagpur Plateau; flows through a rift valley; joins — flows into — the Hugli
- Tributaries: Barakar — the main tributary; Konar, Jamunia, Barki
- Chain: Barakar flows into the Damodar → Damodar flows into the Hugli — the Barakar–Damodar–Hugli chain

### Indus system — tributaries, course and Greek/Vedic names
## Jhelum
- ★ Jhelum — rises in a spring at Verinag, south-east Kashmir, foot of Pir Panjal
- ★ Names: Vyeth (Kashmiri), Vetesta (Sanskrit), Hydaspes (Greek); flows through Srinagar
- ★ Jhelum passes through Wular Lake — the only correct statement of a set also claiming "Krishna directly feeds Kolleru" and "Gandak meanders formed Kanwar Lake"
- ★ Kishanganga — tributary of the Jhelum
## Sutlej
- ★ Sutlej — the only one that joins the Indus directly: Ravi and Jhelum → Chenab → Sutlej → Indus
- ★ Rises at Manasarovar–Rakas lakes, 4,570 m, within 80 km of the Indus source; NW course to Shipki La
- ★ Sutlej — antecedent river, flows through three countries, rises on Tibetan Plateau, important for irrigation
## Others
- ★ Beas — does not originate from Mansarovar
- ★ Indus — flows through north-western India and Pakistan
- ★ Brahmaputra — called Tsang Po in Tibet
- ★ Rivers of Himachal Pradesh: Beas, Chenab, Ravi, Sutlej, Yamuna
- ★ Chandra and Bhaga — flow in Lahaul valley; Chandra from glacier near Chandra Tal, Bhaga near Bara Lacha La; meet near Tandi → Chandrabhaga
- ★ Leh — right bank of Indus; in UT of Ladakh since 31 Oct 2019
### Ganga — course and tributaries by bank
## Course
- ★ Devprayag — Alaknanda + Bhagirathi form the Ganga
- ★ Uttarkashi — not on the Ganga (on Bhagirathi); Fatehpur, Bhagalpur, Kanpur are on the Ganga
- ★ In Bangladesh the Ganga is the Padma; joined by Brahmaputra (Jamuna), then Meghna → Bay of Bengal
- ★ Ganga — total 2525 km; flows past Rajmahal hills, enters Bangladesh via Farakka
- ★ Yamuna — rises at Yamunotri Glacier near Bandar Poonch peak
## Tributaries
- ★ Downstream of Prayagraj (west→east): Gomati (Ghazipur) – Ghaghara (near Chapra) – Gandak (near Patna) – Kosi (Katihar)
- ★ Gomti — the only Ganga tributary rising in the plains; Gomat Taal (Fulhar lake), Pilibhit
- ★ Kosi — maximum shifting of course; dangerous floods; 7 tributaries, Arun most important
- ★ Son — flows northwards; rises near Amarkantak; main right-bank tributary, joins near Patna
- ★ Chambal — tributary of Yamuna (not Narmada), joins near Etawah; rises Janapav Hills near Mhow (Dr Ambedkar Nagar)
- ★ Manas — tributary of Brahmaputra
- ★ Betwa — rises in Vindhya Range, joins Yamuna near Hamirpur
### Ganga — left-bank tributaries, Son and Damodar
- ★ Yamuna — taken (APPSC key) as an antecedent river older than the Himalayas
- ★ Mahanadi — not a tributary of the Indus
- ★ Mahakali Treaty — India–Nepal, Feb 1996; river Mahakali (Sharda in India); Sharda Barrage, Tanakpur Barrage
- ★ Satluj — cuts across all the ranges of the Himalayas; rises Rakas Lake, enters HP near Shipki La; Trans-Himalayan river; Beas joins near Harike, Punjab
### Brahmaputra — names by country and tributaries
- ★ Brahmaputra — Yarlung Tsangpo / Tsangpo / Sanpu in Tibet; Dihang in Arunachal; Brahmaputra in Assam; Jamuna in Bangladesh
- ★ Yarlung Zangbo — Brahmaputra; originates at Angsi glacier, western Tibet
- ★ Catchment: Tibet, Bangladesh, India (and Bhutan)
- ★ Tributaries: Manas, Kopili, Tista, Kameng, Subansiri, Dhansari, Burhi Dihing, Dibang
- ★ Sankosh — boundary of Assam and West Bengal; rises northern Bhutan (Puna Tsang Chhu)
- ★ Devprayag — Alaknanda + Bhagirathi; Tehri Garhwal district; "holy confluence"; on Rishikesh–Badrinath route
- ★ Son — does not join the Ganga from the left (right-bank)
- ★ Betwa — originates in Vindhyan Hills
### Lengths, basins, FIRSTs and superlatives
- ★ Majuli (Assam) — largest riverine island; good paddy crops after Brahmaputra floods
- ★ Baglihar — on Chenab, Ramban district; run-of-the-river; Pakistan took it to the World Bank
### Lakes of the Himalaya and north-east → state and water type
- ★ Yaya Tso — Ladakh; Ladakh's first heritage site under the Biological Diversity Act; "bird's paradise"; about 4,820 m
