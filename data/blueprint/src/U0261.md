### Leaders → life, institutions and contributions — Ayyanki Venkata Ramanayya
## 1. Ayyanki Venkata Ramanayya
| Aspect | Detail |
| Associations | Secretary, Ramamohan Library (1911); ADLA (1914); helped found the Bengal (1925), Madras (1928) and Punjab (1929) associations and the All India Public Library Association (1933) |
| Journal | *Grandhalaya Sarvasvam* (1915) |
| Work | Training camps (1920, 1934); respected by S.R. Ranganathan |
- Slogan: "Public libraries are temples of knowledge."
- Memorial: Birth anniversary marked yearly in Vijayawada
- Not in your sources: the venue and year of the first All India Public Library Conference.
## 2. Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao
- President of the library association in 1946
- Building bought via Komma Sitaramayya's gift, through the Andhra Granthalaya Dharma Samstha (1946) → named Sarvottama Bhavan, opened 1949
## 3. Paturi Nagabhushanam
- Bala Saraswati Bhandaramu: Village library started as a schoolboy; later served 40 villages
- Secretary of the Guntur Library Association: Libraries in about 160 villages; new buildings for 28

### Library innovations and laws → pioneer (PYQ)
- Library pilgrimage: Conceived by Ayyanki Venkata Ramanayya (APPSC GS 2019)
- Boat library: Paturi, on passenger boats on the Bank Canal for 7 years (1935-42) (APPSC GS 2019: boat library service)
- Legislation: Paturi was a member of the committee that drafted the AP Public Libraries Act
### Gadicherla → newspapers, jail, "Rayalaseema", Sreebagh
- Papers: Swarajya (1907), Navayugam, Andhra Patrika, Matru Seva, Panchayati Rajyam
- Imprisoned: For Swarajya editorials such as "Veerabali"
- "Rayalaseema": Renamed the Dattamandalam (Ceded Districts) "Rayalaseema" in 1928 (APPSC G2 2011, 2012)
- Other roles: Signed the Sreebagh Pact for Rayalaseema; linked the movement to the Home Rule League
- ⚠ 1928 = "Rayalaseema" name; 1946 = library association president; 1949 = Sarvottama Bhavan opened
### Universal angles
- Current relevance: Ayyanki's 136th birth anniversary at the Tagore Library, Bandar Road, Vijayawada (25 Jul 2026)
- Grandson Ayyanki Murali Krishna recalled the slogan "Public libraries are temples of knowledge"
- Current relevance: Film Viswadata on Kasinathuni Nageswara Rao planned for his 160th birth anniversary (14 May 2026)
- Significance – three leaders:
- Ayyanki = built institutions
- Gadicherla = mobilised people
- Nageswara Rao = money + political backing

## Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao

- Conceived the library pilgrimage
- Grandhalaya Sarvasvam — his journal
- Associations in order: ADLA (1914), Bengal (1925), Madras (1928), All India Public Library Association (1933)
## Paturi Nagabhushanam
- Started Bala Saraswati Bhandaramu library as a schoolboy in his village
- Boat library on passenger boats on the Bank Canal, 1935–1942 (seven years)
- Member of the committee that drafted the AP Public Libraries Act
## Komarraju Venkata Lakshmana Rao
- Founded SKDABN (Sri Krishnadevaraya Andhra Bhasha Nilayam) in 1901
