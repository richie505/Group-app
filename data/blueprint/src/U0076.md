### Three phases of Chalukya art
- Badami (rock-cut) → Aihole (experimental) → Pattadakal (mature) = birth of Vesara
- 1. Badami: rock-cut caves ↓
- 2. Aihole: experimental / early temples - "Cradle of Indian temple architecture" (K.A. Nilakanta Sastri) ↓
- 3. Pattadakal: mature - Nagara and Dravida side by side ↓
- 4. Vesara = hybrid of Nagara + Dravida (Chalukya style)

### Epithets, FIRSTs and UNESCO status
- Badami: Rock-cut caves; Aihole: Experimental or early temples; Pattadakal: A Chalukya architectural centre — a synthesis of Nagara and Dravida styles
- Vesara: A hybrid of Nagara and Dravida styles — associated with the Chalukya style
- Associations: Kanchi with the Pallavas; Tanjore with the Cholas
- Aihole epithet: "Cradle of Indian temple architecture" (K.A. Nilakanta Sastri) - experimental, structural phase
- Percy Brown: Chalukyas first broke the monopoly of the rock-cut tradition in the Deccan
- Earliest caves (Badami): by Mangalesha
- Lad Khan: earliest massive bracket capital; RPSC 2018: Lad Khan = early Chalukya temple
- Pattadakal: Royal coronation site ("coronation stone")

### Sites → features: Badami, Aihole, Pattadakal, Alampur
## 1. Badami cave temples
- Badami: Rock-cut caves — four rock-cut temples; Caves 1–3 are Hindu temples; Cave 4 a Jain temple
- Caves: Four main caves in sandstone around Agastya lake, 6th-7th century
| Cave | Deity |
| 1 | Shiva - 18-armed Nataraja |
| 2 | Vishnu (Varaha, Trivikrama) [GK] |
| 3 | Vishnu; largest, Mangalesha's inscription of 578 CE [GK] |
| 4 | Jain |
## 2. Aihole - early freestanding temples
- Lad Khan:
- Rectangular, flat roof, stone grilles for light, pillared porch
- Durga temple: Apsidal (gajaprishtha) plan + pillared gallery round the shrine
## 3. Pattadakal - mature phase
- Phase: Mature - Nagara and Dravida side by side
- Innovations: Nandi mandapa; narrative friezes (Ramayana, Mahabharata, Panchatantra); polished pillars
## 4. Alampur Navabrahma temples
- Navabrahma (Temple Complex): A nine-temple complex at Alampur (Alampuram) — built by the Badami Chalukyas
- Complex of 9 temples at Alampur, on the Tungabhadra; 7th-8th century CE
- Alampur = the place in the Telugu region with Badami Chalukyan temples
- Style: Nagara (curvilinear rekha shikhara)
- District: Jogulamba Gadwal, Telangana (earlier Mahabubnagar, undivided AP)
- Early temples in Andhra: Temple architecture: Initiated during the Ikshvaku period; Parasurameswara Temple: Gudimallam — the oldest Saivite temple
