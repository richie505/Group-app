### Resources — types, depletion and global milestones
| Basis | Types |
| Origin | Biotic (plants, animals, micro-organisms: APPSC 2025 key) vs Abiotic (non-living: land, water, minerals, rocks, metals) |
| Exhaustibility | Renewable (forests, fish, water, wildlife) vs Non-renewable (fossil fuels, minerals; exhausted with use) |
| Status of development | Potential; Developed; Stock (exists / available but technology to use it is lacking, e.g. hydrogen from water); Reserve (subset / part of stock usable with present technology, kept for future) |
- ⚠ Stock = technology lacking; Reserve = usable with present technology (a part of stock)
- Depletion: Overuse of natural resources → resource depletion (APPSC 2025 key)
- Resource → cause of depletion (APPSC 2025 key):
- Groundwater → deep bore tubewells for irrigation
- Topsoil → brick production
- Biodiversity habitats → expansion of agriculture
| Year | Milestone |
| 1968 / 1972 | Club of Rome; report "Limits to Growth" (1972) |

### World forest types; resource regions → resource (subcontinent and world)
## 1. World forest types — key facts
- Amazon Basin: The largest, densest tropical rainforest — the world's largest continuous rainforest
- Congo Basin: The second-largest tropical rainforest
- Dense forests: Mostly found near the equator
- Tropical rainforest after forest clearance: Soil is nutrient-poor; regeneration is slow
- Taiga / temperate coniferous forests: The world's largest terrestrial biome — the highest percentage of the world's forest area by type; occur in temperate and sub-Arctic regions — ⚠ not in Amazonia
- Bhutan: A constitutional provision to maintain at least 60% of its geographical area under forest
- Canada: The largest producer and exporter of softwood timber and wood pulp

| Act / amendment | Provision |

### Resource classification
- Minerals — abiotic resources; fossil fuels non-renewable; forests and fish renewable
