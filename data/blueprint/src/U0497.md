### Land reforms — measures, aims, goals and FIRSTs
## 1. Land reform measures
- Land consolidation: Associated with land reform
- Abolition of intermediaries: Removing middlemen — the actual cultivator has direct contact with the State
- Tenancy reform: Rent, tenure and ownership
- Regulation of rent (capped at 1/4 to 1/6 of produce)
- Security of tenure
- Ownership rights to tenants
- Failure: "Voluntary surrenders" + evictions
- Land ceiling: The maximum legal holding; surplus land is associated with redistribution
- ⚠ Multiple cropping is not land reform
## 2. Aims
- Land reforms reduce inequality, involve agrarian restructuring and promote agricultural growth
## 3. Joint cooperative farming features
- Land pooled voluntarily
- Produce shared per land/labour contributed
- ⚠ Not a feature: compulsory membership (APPSC G2 2017 key)

### Programmes → region and outcome — Operation Barga, consolidation, Bhoodan, cooperative farming
- Operation Barga (West Bengal, Left Front): Recording names of sharecroppers (bargadars)
- Heritable rights + 75% share of produce
- Launched 1978 (some notes say 1977, the year the Left Front came to power)
- APPSC 2019 key: land reform for recording names of sharecroppers
- Consolidation: Succeeded in Punjab, Haryana and western UP; little progress in southern and eastern States
- Bhoodan:
- Amid the Telangana armed struggle
- Appeal to donate 1/6th of land; over 40 lakh acres pledged, much barren
- Cooperative farming: Kumarappa Committee (1949) → Nagpur Resolution (1959) made it a party goal
- Failed: captured by rich peasants to evade ceilings
- ⚠ Rent cap 1/4 to 1/6 of produce; Bhoodan appeal = 1/6th of land; bargadar share = 75%
### Land ceiling laws — unit, limits and outcome
| Feature | Pre-1972 laws | 1972 national guidelines |
| Unit | Individual (encouraged benami transfers to relatives) | Family (effective 1972) |
| Ceiling | High, varied by State | 10-18 acres two-crop irrigated; 27 acres single-crop; 54 acres dry [GK for 54] |
- Two rounds: 1950s and early 1970s; about 21 States had ceiling laws as of 2020
- Outcome: Less than 2% of cultivable area redistributed
- Exemptions: plantations, orchards, "mechanised" farms
### Constitutional amendments, Articles and cases on land reform
| Amendment | Land-reform change |
| 1st (1951) | Inserted Arts. 31A, 31B and Ninth Schedule (after Kameshwar Singh v. Bihar) |
| 4th (1955) | Restated Art. 31(2); widened 31A to zamindari abolition, planning, mineral control |
| 17th (1964) | Land under personal cultivation within ceiling acquired only at market value; widened "estate"; challenged in Golaknath (1967) |
| 25th (1971) | Inserted Art. 31C[GK] |
| 34th (1974) / 66th (1990) | 20 more land laws; 55 State Acts incl. AP placed in Ninth Schedule |
- Art. 323B: Clause (h) "rent and tenancy rights" (tribunals) added by 75th Amendment (1993)
- I.R. Coelho (2007): Laws added to Ninth Schedule after 24 April 1973 (Kesavananda) open to Basic Structure review
- ⚠ 1st = 31A, 31B, Ninth Schedule; 25th = 31C; 75th = 323B(h)
### Recent reform initiatives and committees
## 1. National
- National Council for Land Reforms: Constituted 2008 under the Prime Minister
- Committee: "State Agrarian Relations and Unfinished Task in Land Reforms"
- Model Agricultural Land Leasing Act, 2016: By NITI Aayog (T. Haque committee)
- Legalises leasing; tenants get credit/insurance; protects owner's title
- AP routes: CCRC Act 2019 (11-month cards); proposed AP New Tenancy Act 2024 → tenant entitlement cards
## 2. Koneru Ranga Rao Committee (2006) — AP Land Committee
- Nature: AP government Land Committee headed by Koneru Ranga Rao
- Year: Constituted 2006 (Y.S. Rajasekhara Reddy government); AP Land Committee, 2004-06
- Subject: Resolve land-related problems in AP (APPSC 2021 key)
- Focus areas: Assigned lands, confirmation of land rights, tenancy regulation and land records
- ⚠ Trap: NOT corporate farming - the "not the focus" option (APPSC G2 2017 key)
- Exam angle: "which was not a focus area" - eliminate corporate farming.

### AP land laws → year and provisions — Ceiling and land protection
## 1. Ceiling and land protection
| Law | Key provisions |
| Ceiling Act, 1961 (common law) | Individual as unit; ceiling 27-324 acres; surplus under 15,000 acres |
| AP Land Reforms (Ceiling on Agricultural Holdings) Act, 1973 | Bill under CM P.V. Narasimha Rao; in Ninth Schedule; in force 1 January 1975 |

- 1973 Act - unit: Family of 5
- Husband's and wife's land clubbed
- Each major son = separate unit
- +1/5 ceiling per extra member
- Standard ceiling: 10-27 acres wet, 35-54 acres dry (some notes say 30-54)
- Benami ban: Transfers after 30 Jan 1970
- Land Tribunal: Headed by RDO
- Exemptions: Plantations, orchards, religious/charitable trusts, cooperatives, Bhoodan lands
- AP phase II: 20 lakh acres surplus estimated vs only 5 lakh acres taken over
- ⚠ 1961 Act = individual unit; 1973 Act = family of 5
