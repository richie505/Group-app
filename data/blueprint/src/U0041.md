### Persons → contributions in early Andhra
| Person | Contribution |
| Bavari | Brahmin teacher from Kosala; settled on the Godavari in Assaka territory; sent 16 disciples to the Buddha |
| Megasthenes | Greek ambassador of Seleucus at the Mauryan court; First to mention forts in Andhra (G2 2002) - 30 forts (walled towns); Andhras lived in towns |
| Ashoka | Andhra came under Ashoka after the Kalinga War; edicts at Erragudi, Rajula Mandagiri, Amaravati |
| Mahadeva Bhikshu | By Andhra tradition sent by Ashoka to Amaravati - first Buddhist teacher in Andhra; enshrined Buddha's bone relics there |
| P.C. Ray | Identified the river at Amaravati-Dharanikota as the Krishna |
- Bavari significance: Buddhist ideas reached the Godavari region before the Mauryan expansion
