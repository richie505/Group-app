### Transport — railway zone, corridors and highways
## 1. Corridors and zones — concepts
- Industrial corridor: Combines industrial development with connectivity
- Economic corridor: Connectivity that facilitates the movement of goods, services and economic activity
- Dedicated Freight Corridor: Dedicated railway freight transportation infrastructure
- National Investment and Manufacturing Zone (NIMZ): A large developed area with an ecosystem for world-class manufacturing
- Instruments of the National Manufacturing Policy, 2011 — envisaged as large areas of developed land with the required ecosystem
## 2. South Coast Railway (SCoR)
- Origin: Promised in APRA 2014 (13th Schedule); carved from SCR + ECoR
- Status: India's 18th zone; HQ Visakhapatnam; operational 1 June 2026
- Divisions: Guntakal, Guntur, Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam
## 3. Freight and industrial corridors in AP
