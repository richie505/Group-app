### Plateaus → types and notable plateaus
## 1. Types of plateau
| Type | Nature | Examples |
| Intermontane | Enclosed or surrounded by mountains | The Tibetan, Pamir, Bolivia, Peru and Colombia Plateaus |
| Piedmont | At foothills or mountain margins | The Piedmont Plateau of the USA; the Patagonia Plateau |
