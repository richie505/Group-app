# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 3:17 - Related UNDP indices - IHDI, Gender Inequality Index, Gender Development Index, Multidimensional Poverty Index
### Year introduced
- MPI = headcount ratio x intensity of poverty
- 1995 — GDI & GEM
- 1996 — Capability Poverty Measure
- 1997 — Human Poverty Index (HPI)
- 2010 — MPI, GII, IHDI; MPI replaced HPI
### Index → meaning
| Index | Reading |
| IHDI | HDI discounted for inequality; HDI - IHDI = loss |
| GDI | 1 = gender parity; 5 groups |
| GII | Higher GII = greater inequality |
| MPI | Headcount x intensity; poor if deprived in one-third |
| HPI | Indicates standard of living (APPSC-GS 2018) |
### Dimensions & indicators
- GII
- Reproductive health: MMR, adolescent birth rate
- Empowerment: women's seats, secondary education
- Labour market: labour force participation
- Capability Poverty Measure
- Births unattended
- Female illiteracy
- Global MPI
- Education, health: 1/6 each
- Standard of living: 1/18 each (6)
### Global vs National MPI
| Feature | Global | National |
| Publisher | UNDP + OPHI, since 2010 | NITI Aayog (with UNDP, OPHI) |
| Method | Alkire-Foster | Same |
| Indicators | 10 | 12: + maternal health, bank account |

### Indices → dimensions and indicators
## 1. Capability Poverty Measure — three indicators
- Children under 5 who are underweight (health and nutrition)
- Births unattended by trained health personnel
- Female illiteracy
- BPSC 2025 key: child malnutrition + female illiteracy both included
## 2. Human Poverty Index
- Dimensions: deprivation in longevity, knowledge, decent standard of living
- Indicates: standard of living (APPSC-GS 2018 key)
## 3. Gender Inequality Index
| Dimension | Indicators |
| Reproductive health | Maternal mortality ratio, adolescent birth rate |
| Empowerment | Share of parliamentary seats held by women; adults 25+ with some secondary education |
| Labour market | Female and male labour force participation (15+) |
## 4. Global MPI — 3 dimensions, 10 indicators
| Dimension | Indicator | Weight |
| Education | Years of schooling; child school attendance | 1/6 each |
| Health | Child mortality; nutrition | 1/6 each |
| Standard of living | Electricity, drinking water, sanitation, housing, cooking fuel, assets | 1/18 each |
## 5. National MPI — 3 dimensions, 12 indicators
- Design: 3 equally weighted dimensions, 12 SDG-aligned indicators
- Health 3, education 2, standard of living 7
- National additions: India adds maternal health + bank accounts
| Dimension | Indicators |
| Health | Nutrition, child and adolescent mortality, maternal health |
| Education | Years of schooling, school attendance |
- ⚠ Global 10 indicators vs National 12
### Terms → meaning (IHDI loss, parity, headcount x intensity)
| Term | Meaning |
| IHDI | Discounts each HDI dimension for its inequality |
| HDI - IHDI = loss | Loss from inequality, in % |
| GDI | 1 = gender parity; countries put into 5 groups by distance from parity |
| GII reading | Higher GII = greater inequality; built on IHDI framework |
| MPI | Headcount ratio x intensity of poverty (UPSC) |
| MPI-poor | Deprived in at least one-third of weighted indicators (UPSC) |
- IHDI: IHDI = HDI when no inequality; otherwise always lower
- MPI data: household-survey micro-data; multiple deprivations at the individual level
- ⚠ Trap: some notes describe GDI as 0 = equal to 1 = worst - that describes the GII
- ⚠ Trap (UPPSC 2025): "measures extreme poverty below $1 a day" = wrong (key: only 1)
- Exam angle: 0.069 vs 0.690 vs 0.096 style options; the value is small because MPI = headcount x intensity.

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 3:17
### Year introduced
| Index | Year |
| GDI / GEM | 1995 |
| ★ HPI | 1997 |
| MPI / GII / IHDI | 2010 |
- HPI (HDR 1997) — deprivation in longevity, knowledge, decent standard of living; supported the HDI
### Index → meaning
- IHDI — cuts HDI for unequal distribution; lower than HDI whenever inequality exists
- GDI = 1 — gender parity (health, education, earned income)
- GII — brought in HDR 2010 to fix GDI's weaknesses; higher GII = more inequality
- National MPI: MPI-poor if deprived in at least one-third of weighted indicators
- Alkire-Foster method: MPI = headcount ratio (H) × intensity (A) — hence small values (e.g. India 0.066)
- not a sum; not a geometric mean (that is HDI); not a $1-a-day income measure
### Dimensions & indicators
- Capability Poverty Measure indicators — child underweight, unattended births, female illiteracy (not male labour-force participation)
- GII reproductive health — maternal mortality ratio and adolescent birth rate
- Global MPI dimensions:
- health — nutrition, child mortality
- education — years of schooling, school attendance (not living standards)
- National MPI — 12 indicators (Global MPI 10); same three dimensions and Alkire-Foster method
- health 3, education 2, standard of living 7
### Global vs National MPI
- Global MPI — UNDP and OPHI (Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative)
- National MPI — NITI Aayog with UNDP and OPHI
- National MPI standard-of-living indicators: cooking fuel, sanitation, drinking water, electricity, housing, assets, bank accounts

- ★ Social indicators of poverty — illiteracy, safe drinking water, job opportunities; size of the house is not one
- ★ Capability Poverty Measure — UNDP, HDR 1996; lack of three basic capabilities (healthy and well-fed, healthy reproduction, …)
- includes child malnutrition (health and nourishment) and female illiteracy
