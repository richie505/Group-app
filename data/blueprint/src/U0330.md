### Implementation → Inter-State Council (Article 263)
## 1. Inter-State Council — Article 263
- Set up on 28 May 1990, following the Sarkaria Commission
- Implemented: of the 247 recommendations, the Inter-State Council (1990) is the main one
- Not in your sources: the exact number of Sarkaria recommendations the Centre accepted or implemented (check the Inter-State Council Secretariat's status report).
### Governor → Sarkaria's recommendations
- Selection: an eminent person in some walk of life (APPSC 2017 key)
- From outside the state
- Not closely involved in local politics; not an active politician in the recent past
- Consultation: the Chief Minister must be effectively consulted
- Tenure: secure 5-year term, not cut short except for extremely compelling reasons
- Appointments: strictly non-partisan
- Bills: decided within a specified period
- ⚠ Trap: NOT recommended - Governor "should have travelled extensively in the state" (APPSC-G2 2017 key)
### Articles and Lists → Art 356, floor test, Concurrent List, residuary powers
## 1. Art 356
- Majority test: floor test in the Assembly
- Hung Assembly: invite parties in a set order of preference → later endorsed by the Punchhi Commission (2010)
- Bommai (1994): Court drew on Sarkaria to separate proper from improper uses
## 2. Legislative relations
- Concurrent List: Centre should consult states before legislating
- Parliament should avoid legislating without an institutionalised process of prior consultation
- Residuary powers (Art 248): taxation stays with Parliament
### Administrative and financial relations → AIS, central forces, cesses
## 1. Administrative relations
- All-India Services: strengthen them + create more such services
- ⚠ Opposite of Rajamannar (abolish)
- Central armed forces: Centre may deploy in a state even without the state's request; consulting the state first is desirable
## 2. Financial relations
- Cesses and surcharges: kept to a minimum and time-bound, then merged into the shareable tax pool
### Universal angles
- Significance: standard framework for the Governor, Art 356 and cooperative federalism
- Relied on in Bommai (1994) and Rameshwar Prasad (2006)
- Court quoted Sarkaria: Governors recommend President's Rule to "promote the political interests of the party in power at the Centre" (May 2026)
- Current relevance: its report widely debated; Tamil Nadu's Kurian Joseph Committee (2025) got little attention (Sep 2026)
- Comparative note:
- Sarkaria (Centre, 1983): within the Constitution; strengthen AIS; strong Centre
- Rajamannar (Tamil Nadu, 1969): autonomy; abolish AIS and Art 356
- Punchhi (Centre, 2007): localised emergency + fixed tenure for Governors
