### Literacy — Census definition and FIRSTs
## 1. Definition of literate
- Literate: Person aged 7 and above who can both read and write with understanding in any language
- One who can only read = not literate
- Censuses 1951-1971 counted age 5+; age 7+ from 1981
- Full literacy (ULLAS): = 95%+ among those aged 15+ (10 Jul 2026)
## 2. FIRSTs
| FIRST | FACT |
| FIRST district: Ernakulam (Kerala) | First successful Total Literacy Campaign; India's first totally literate district in 1990 |
| First fully literate town (1989) | Kottayam |
| First fully literate UT (ULLAS) | Ladakh (2024) |
| First fully literate state (ULLAS) | Mizoram (2025) |
- Fully literate sequence (ULLAS): Ladakh → Mizoram → Goa, Tripura, Himachal Pradesh (5th State/UT), Sikkim (5th state) → Uttarakhand (6th, Jul 2026, 98%+)
- Exam angle: "first" facts (district, UT, state) and scheme age group (15+).
- ⚠ First UT Ladakh (2024); first state Mizoram (2025); first TLC district Ernakulam

### School education policy and schemes — NEP 2020, Operation Blackboard, Samagra Shiksha — NEP 2020
## 1. NEP 2020
- Status: First education policy of the 21st century; replaces the 34-year-old NPE 1986
| NEP stage | 5 (Foundational) | 3 (Preparatory) | 3 (Middle) | 4 (Secondary) |
| Ages | 3-8 | 8-11 | 11-14 | 14-18 |
- Structure: 5+3+3+4 (APPSC-GS 2025 key) = 12 years of school + 3 of pre-school
- Targets:
- 100% GER in school by 2030
- 50% GER in higher education by 2035
- 2 crore out-of-school children back
- 100% youth and adult literacy by 2030
- FLN by Grade 3: NEP's 2025 target moved → NIPUN Bharat (Grade-3 foundational literacy) now aims at 2026-27
### School education policy and schemes — NEP 2020, Operation Blackboard, Samagra Shiksha — Scheme → year → provision
## 1. Scheme → year → provision
| Scheme | Year | Provision |
| Operation Blackboard (1987, under NPE 1986) | 1987 | Basic facilities for primary schools in educationally backward areas (APPSC-GS 2025 key); at least two rooms, two teachers, teaching aids |
| Samagra Shiksha (2018) | 2018 | Umbrella scheme pre-school to Class 12; subsumes Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan, Rashtriya Madhyamik Shiksha Abhiyan and Teacher Education |

- Samagra Shiksha: Supports NEP 2020 + RTE Act 2009; covers children with disabilities under RPwD Act 2016 (IYB p.261)
- Nadu-Nedu nine facilities: Toilets, drinking water, furniture, paint, major/minor repairs, lights and fans, green chalkboards, English labs, compound walls
