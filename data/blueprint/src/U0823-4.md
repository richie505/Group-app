### Sodium-ion batteries — case for India
## 1. Lithium-ion status
- Li-ion price: ~$1,100/kWh (early 2010s) → ~$108/kWh (2025)
- PLI for Advanced Chemistry Cells (2021): ~40 GWh allocated, only ~1 GWh commissioned
- Cathode: lithium metal oxides — LiCoO₂, LiMn₂O₄, LiFePO₄; ⚠ anode = graphite (graphite is not a cathode material)
## 2. Sodium-ion advantages
- Energy density approaching LFP; layered-oxide chemistries outperform polyanionic and Prussian blue analogues
- Safety: lower thermal-runaway temperature; ⚠ not "Dangerous Goods" for transport; can be stored/shipped at zero volts (Li-ion at ≤30% charge)
- Aluminium current collectors on both electrodes (Li-ion uses copper); sodium from soda ash
- Global capacity: ~70 GWh (2025) → ~400 GWh by 2030; expected to undercut Li-ion costs by 2035
### PERT — single genome-editing strategy for many disorders
## 1. Study
- Journal: Nature; by Broad Institute, Harvard, University of Minnesota
- PERT: Prime-Editing-mediated Readthrough of Premature Termination codons — edits a tRNA gene into a suppressor tRNA
- Target: nonsense mutations (premature stop codon, e.g. TAG) — ~25% of disease-causing genetic changes
## 2. Key numbers
- Human cells have 418 tRNA genes; 4 promising — leucine, arginine, tyrosine, serine
- 17,000+ configurations tested; enzyme PE6c + PE3 strategy
- Efficiency: 60–80% vs HDR 10–20%
- Mouse models: Batten, Tay-Sachs, Niemann-Pick C1; Hurler syndrome — activity restored to 1.7% of normal

### Sodium-ion batteries — case for India
## 1. Lithium-ion status
- Li-ion price: ~$1,100/kWh (early 2010s) → ~$108/kWh (2025)
- PLI for Advanced Chemistry Cells (2021): ~40 GWh allocated, only ~1 GWh commissioned
- Cathode: lithium metal oxides — LiCoO₂, LiMn₂O₄, LiFePO₄; ⚠ anode = graphite (graphite is not a cathode material)
## 2. Sodium-ion advantages
- Energy density approaching LFP; layered-oxide chemistries outperform polyanionic and Prussian blue analogues
- Safety: lower thermal-runaway temperature; ⚠ not "Dangerous Goods" for transport; can be stored/shipped at zero volts (Li-ion at ≤30% charge)
- Aluminium current collectors on both electrodes (Li-ion uses copper); sodium from soda ash
- Global capacity: ~70 GWh (2025) → ~400 GWh by 2030; expected to undercut Li-ion costs by 2035
### PERT — single genome-editing strategy for many disorders
## 1. Study
- Journal: Nature; by Broad Institute, Harvard, University of Minnesota
- PERT: Prime-Editing-mediated Readthrough of Premature Termination codons — edits a tRNA gene into a suppressor tRNA
- Target: nonsense mutations (premature stop codon, e.g. TAG) — ~25% of disease-causing genetic changes
## 2. Key numbers
- Human cells have 418 tRNA genes; 4 promising — leucine, arginine, tyrosine, serine
- 17,000+ configurations tested; enzyme PE6c + PE3 strategy
- Efficiency: 60–80% vs HDR 10–20%
- Mouse models: Batten, Tay-Sachs, Niemann-Pick C1; Hurler syndrome — activity restored to 1.7% of normal
