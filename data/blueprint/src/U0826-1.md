### Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine 2026 — optogenetics
## 1. Laureates
- Karl Deisseroth (USA), Peter Hegemann (Germany), Georg Nagel (Germany)
- For: light-gated ion channels and optogenetics — switching selected neurons on/off with light
## 2. Science
- Channelrhodopsin-2 (ChR-2) from green alga Chlamydomonas (eyespot) — "molecular light switch"; opens in ~0.2 ms
- Hegemann: studied Chlamydomonas (early 1990s); Nagel: expressed ChR-1/ChR-2 in frog egg cells; joint paper 2003
- Deisseroth: ChR-2 into rat neurons (2005); named "optogenetics" in 2006; mouse whisker control (2007)
- Francis Crick (1970s) proposed light for controlling neurons
## 3. Nobel background
| Category | Awarding body |
| Physics, Chemistry, Economic Sciences | Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences |
| Physiology or Medicine | Nobel Assembly at Karolinska Institutet |
| Literature | Swedish Academy |
| Peace | Norwegian Nobel Committee (ceremony in Oslo) |
- First awarded 1901; Economics prize created 1968 by Sweden's central bank; max 3 per prize; generally not posthumous
- Tagore (1913) — first Indian laureate; Mother Teresa (1979) — first Indian woman laureate
- Indian-origin laureates:
- Har Gobind Khorana (1968): first Indian-origin Nobel laureate in medicine
- V. Ramakrishnan: Chemistry 2009 (ribosome structure)
## 4. Extra points
- Alga species: Chlamydomonas reinhardtii (unicellular green alga); field — neuroscience
- ⚠ Economics prize: one source says created 1968, another says introduced 1969 by Sweden's central bank

### Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine 2026 — optogenetics
## 1. Laureates
- Karl Deisseroth (USA), Peter Hegemann (Germany), Georg Nagel (Germany)
- For: light-gated ion channels and optogenetics — switching selected neurons on/off with light
## 2. Science
- Channelrhodopsin-2 (ChR-2) from green alga Chlamydomonas (eyespot) — "molecular light switch"; opens in ~0.2 ms
- Hegemann: studied Chlamydomonas (early 1990s); Nagel: expressed ChR-1/ChR-2 in frog egg cells; joint paper 2003
- Deisseroth: ChR-2 into rat neurons (2005); named "optogenetics" in 2006; mouse whisker control (2007)
- Francis Crick (1970s) proposed light for controlling neurons
## 3. Nobel background
| Category | Awarding body |
| Physics, Chemistry, Economic Sciences | Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences |
| Physiology or Medicine | Nobel Assembly at Karolinska Institutet |
| Literature | Swedish Academy |
| Peace | Norwegian Nobel Committee (ceremony in Oslo) |
- First awarded 1901; Economics prize created 1968 by Sweden's central bank; max 3 per prize; generally not posthumous
- Tagore (1913) — first Indian laureate; Mother Teresa (1979) — first Indian woman laureate
- Indian-origin laureates:
- Har Gobind Khorana (1968): first Indian-origin Nobel laureate in medicine
