### Forest types → features
## 1. Features
- Tropical Evergreen: Dense, multi-layered, evergreen vegetation
- Tropical Moist Deciduous: Trees shed leaves during the dry season
- Tropical Dry Deciduous: Occur in relatively wetter parts of peninsular India; a transition zone toward thorn forests in drier areas; a major forest type of Central India
- Tropical Thorn: Open, stunted forests — bushes, small trees; long roots; thorns or spines
- Teak forests: Teak is the dominant or important species — the Madhya Pradesh–Chhattisgarh–Odisha region is a major teak belt
### Himalayan altitudinal zones → altitude → species
- Sequence up the slope:
- Tropical (sal) foothills → subtropical (chir pine) → temperate (oak, deodar, blue pine, silver fir) → subalpine (birch, rhododendron) → alpine meadows (to 4,750 m+)
- ⚠ Some notes give the order subtropical-temperate-alpine
| Zone | Altitude |

| Sub-alpine (fir, birch, rhododendron) | - |
| Alpine meadows | 2,900-6,000 m |
| Dry alpine scrub (juniper) | Above 4,000 m |
- Eastern Himalaya: Oaks, laurels, maples, rhododendrons, alder, birch; tree line higher than in the west
- Slope contrast: Southern (windward) slopes = denser forest vs northern slopes

### Terms → meaning: forestry, forest cover classes and silviculture
## 1. Terms
- Afforestation: Creation or establishment of forest or tree cover
- Reforestation: Re-establishment of forest cover where forests have been depleted
- Social forestry: Tree or forest development for local or community needs — reduces pressure on natural forests
- Forest cover definition: Land > 1 ha with canopy density ≥ 10% regardless of ownership
| Class | Canopy density [GK] |
| Very Dense Forest (VDF) | ≥ 70% |
| Moderately Dense Forest (MDF) | 40-70% |
| Open Forest (OF) | 10-40% |
| Scrub | < 10% (not counted as forest cover) |
| Coppice | Regeneration from stool shoots after felling; short rotation (fuelwood) |
| High forest | Crop raised from seed; long rotation (timber) |
| Selection system | Mature trees removed singly or in groups; uneven-aged forest maintained |
- ⚠ Coppice = shoots, short rotation; High forest = seed, long rotation

### Tropical forest types
## Biodiversity and biomass
- ★ Tropical moist / rain forests — greatest diversity of plant and animal species; maximum biodiversity
- ★ Tropical evergreen forests — maximum plant diversity; need > 200 cm rain, 15–30 °C; about 7% of the earth's land
- ★ Tropical rain forest — maximum plant biomass
- ★ Tropical forests grow near the equator (10°N–10°S); no dry season, so leaves are not shed all together
- ★ Tropical rain forest — leaf litter decomposes fastest; epiphytes and climbers reach the canopy
- ★ Tropical deciduous forests — most widespread in Asia
## Rainfall and share of forest area
| Forest type | Rainfall | Share of India's forest area |
| Tropical evergreen | > 200 cm | wet evergreen ~8% |
| Tropical moist deciduous | 100–200 cm | ~37% |
| Tropical dry deciduous | 70–100 cm | ~28.6% |
| Tropical thorn | < 70 cm |  |
- ★ Tropical moist deciduous — largest area in India; central India, eastern slopes of Western Ghats, Himalayan foothills, Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand
- Descending share: moist deciduous > dry deciduous > wet evergreen
- ★ Teak and Sal — products of the tropical deciduous forests
- Moist deciduous — sub-Himalayan Bhabar and Terai
## Species
| Forest type | Species |
| ★ Tropical (wet) evergreen | mahogany, ebony, rosewood, gurjan, ironwood |
| Littoral / tidal | Rhizophora, Avicennia |
| Thorn | Acacia, Ziziphus, Prosopis |
| Dry deciduous (South India) | sandalwood |
- ★ Sita Nadi area — mainly Sal (also teak, bamboo); sanctuary 553.36 sq km
- Khair — dry/arid areas; yields katha
- Palash — deciduous; 'Flame of the Forest'
## Thorn forests
- Thorn forests — open, stunted, long roots, thorns/spines; western Andhra Pradesh (not eastern), Rajasthan, Kutch
- Deodar, pine, fir — coniferous, not thorn forests
## Fire, law and management
- Dry deciduous — most vulnerable to forest fires (seasonal dryness, dry biomass)
- Forest (Conservation) Act, 1980 — diversion of forest land needs Central Government approval
- CAMPA — manages compensatory afforestation funds
- High forest + selection system — seed-raised, long-rotation timber; uneven-aged, mature trees removed singly or in groups
## Places
- Banni grassland — Kutch, Gujarat
- Nallamala — biggest forest area of AP; AP's forests mainly dry deciduous
- AP National Parks — Papikonda largest, Rajiv Gandhi smallest, Sri Venkateswara (1989) first
- Harike — Punjab (Beas–Sutlej confluence); Sultanpur — Haryana; Keoladeo — Bharatpur, Rajasthan (birds)
- Wild Ass Sanctuary — Little Rann of Kutch; Narayan Sarovar — Kutch
- Black-necked crane — breeds in Ladakh; Siberian crane wintered at Keoladeo
### Other vegetation
- Shola forests — temperate short evergreen patches with grasslands in the Nilgiris, Anaimalai, Palani hills (Western Ghats)
- Jackfruit — evergreen tree of the Western Ghats (not Himalayan alpine meadows)
- Salim Ali Bird Sanctuary — mangroves of Chorao Island on the Mandovi, Goa
### Himalayan zones (top first)
## Altitude zones
- Order upward: tropical sal → subtropical chir pine → temperate → subalpine birch-rhododendron → alpine meadows
- ★ Deodar — highest of the trees asked; 1500–3000 m, western Himalaya
- Sal — sub-Himalayan
- Chir pine — about 1,000–1,800 m; moist temperate forests 1,500–3,000 m
- Alpine meadows — above the tree line, up to about 4,750 m (also given as 2,900–6,000 m)
- Dry alpine scrub (above ~4,000 m) — juniper
- Alpine forests — above about 3,000 m to the snowline; not on the Western Ghats
- Southern (windward) slopes — denser forests
- Coniferous (deodar, pine, fir) — cold, high-altitude conditions
## Other types
- Thorn forests — Rajasthan, Gujarat and Rayalaseema; 25–75 cm rain
- Mangroves — stilt and knee roots; flooded at high tide; line deltas on both coasts
- ISFR 2023: AP mangrove cover 421.43 sq km (+13.01 sq km), no very dense class
- Coringa WLS — near Kakinada, AP; Bhitarkanika — Odisha; Pichavaram — Tamil Nadu

- Forest Survey of India (1981) — Dehradun; publishes the biennial ISFR
- Village forest — reserved forest given to a village community
- ★ Tiger reserves — Bandipur, Manas, Sunderbans (not Bhitarkanika)
- Palamau TR — Jharkhand; Kanha — MP; Askot — Uttarakhand
- Great Indian Bustard — Rajasthan and Gujarat; fewer than 150 birds
