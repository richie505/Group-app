### Resource gap of 2014-15 — AP's claim vs Centre's calculation
- Mandate: Centre to bridge AP's resource gap for the first year, 2014-15, via Finance Commission route
- Released: Rs 3,979.50 crore between 2 June 2014 and 2016-17 (APPSC-GS 2018)
- Rs 3,979 crore counted as revenue deficit grant within the Special Assistance Measure (SAM, Rs 24,350 crore announced)
| Side | Position |
| AP | The true 2014-15 deficit was far higher: about Rs 16,078 crore[GK] |
| Centre | Used standardised accounting that excluded new spending commitments; worked out about Rs 4,117.89 crore[GK] and released Rs 3,979.50 crore |
| Mechanism | Disputes over financial assets and liabilities go to a Central order on the CAG's advice (Sec 47(4)) |
- ⚠ Rs 16,078 crore = AP's claim; Rs 4,117.89 crore = Centre's calculation; Rs 3,979.50 crore = released
- Exam angle: the Rs 3,979.50 crore figure, and which Finance Commission removed the special-category distinction (the 14th).

### Tax arrears and refunds → basis and Section
| Item | Basis | Section |
| Arrears of property tax / land revenue | State where the property lies | 50 |
| Arrears of other taxes | Place of assessment | 50 |
