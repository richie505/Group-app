### Three editors
- ★ Gadicharla Harisarvottama Rao (editor of Swarajya) — jailed for sedition in 1908; first Telugu journalist / political prisoner so jailed in South India
- Gadicherla founded the Edward Corporation Library (now Victoria Reading Room) at Nandyal, with Kaderbad Narasinga Rao (not Kurnool)
- Mutnuri Krishna Rao — made Krishna Patrika a political weekly and hub of freedom fighters
- Kasinathuni Nageswara Rao (1867–1947) — title "Desoddharaka"; used his Amrutanjan wealth for public work (trusteeship)
### Events

- Sreebagh — Kasinathuni Nageswara Rao's house in Madras; Pact signed there on 16 November 1937
- Bala Bharati Samithi (Rajahmundry, February 1907) — Chilukuri Veerabhadra Rao, Gante Laxmanna and Unguturi Sriramulu
