### Wildlife Protection Act 1972 — PA categories → declared by → rights
## 1. The Act
- The first comprehensive legislation for wildlife protection in India — passed by Parliament; Presidential assent on 9 September 1972
- Protected areas under it: National Parks, Wildlife Sanctuaries, Conservation Reserves, Community Reserves
## 2. Categories
| Category | Declared by / on | Rights and use |
| National Park | State (or Centre) [GK] | Highest protection; no grazing or private rights [GK] |
| Wildlife Sanctuary | State | Some activities/rights regulated but allowed |
| Conservation Reserve | State, on govt-owned land next to or linking PAs (Sec 36A), after consulting locals | Local rights unaffected; managed by committees with panchayats (LENS May 2025) |
| Community Reserve | State, on private/community land volunteered by the community | Rights unaffected; land must not be inside an NP/WLS/CR |
- Mining buffer: SC extended a 10-km mining buffer to all wetland conservation reserves
- ⚠ Conservation Reserve = govt-owned land; Community Reserve = private/community land

### FIRSTs and superlatives — Jim Corbett, parks, sanctuaries, reserves, institutions
## 1. Protected areas and institutions
- Jim Corbett National Park: The first national park in India and mainland Asia — established 1936; originally called Hailey National Park; Project Tiger first launched there in 1973; in Uttarakhand

- Forest Research Institute, Dehradun: Roots in the Imperial Forest Research Institute (1906)
- Montreal Protocol: Adopted 1987; in force 1989 — related to the ozone layer

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 4:41
### WPA 1972 categories
## Protected-area categories
- National Parks give the highest protection; sanctuaries allow some controlled activities and rights
- Conservation Reserve (Section 36A) — declared by the State Government on government-owned land beside / linking PAs, after asking local people
- local rights unaffected; panchayats sit on management committees
- Community Reserve — declared by the State on private or community land freely offered; rights unaffected
- land cannot already be inside a NP, WLS or Conservation Reserve
## Parks
- ★ Rani Jhansi Maritime NP — 1996, Andaman and Nicobar; 320.06 sq km
- Pench NP (MP and Maharashtra) — inspired Kipling's The Jungle Book
- Khangchendzonga NP (Sikkim) — mixed World Heritage Site, 2016
- Nokrek NP — Garo Hills, Meghalaya (not Khasi Hills)
- Kandaleru Deer Park — Rapur mandal, Nellore; Kotappakonda Deer Park — Narsaraopeta, Palnadu
- AP notified forest area — 37,421.11 sq km, 22.96% of the State
