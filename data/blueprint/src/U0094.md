# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:93 - Rani Rudrama Devi and Prataparudra; Marco Polo's account; fall to the Delhi Sultanate
### Rudrama Devi
- Fall of Warangal (1323) ended the Kakatiyas and opened the Musunuri-led liberation movement
- Daughter of Ganapatideva; raised as putrika
- Co-regent from 1258 as Rudradeva Maharaja; ruled 1262-1289
- Married Chalukya prince Veerabhadra
- Daughter Mummadamma = mother of Prataparudra II
### Rudrama's wars

| Yadava Mahadeva | Repulsed → Rayagajakesari |

### Death & Marco Polo
- Death 1289
- Killed fighting Ambadeva
- Chandupatla inscription (Puvvula Mummadi)
- Marco Polo

### Prataparudra II
- Grandson of Rudrama; crowned 1289; Kumara Rudradeva; ruled 1289-1323
- 1291: beat Ambadeva at Tripurantakam
- Nayankara system: 72 Nayankaras (⚠ other notes: 75 (or 77))
- Nayakas: a fort, troops, fixed tribute; one-fourth of land
- → Vijayanagara Amara Nayankara → Poligars
### Delhi invasions of Warangal
- 1303 — Malik Fakhruddin Juna: repulsed at Upparapalli (APPSC G2 2012)
- 1309-10 — Malik Kafur: siege; submits, pays tribute
- 1318 — Khusrau Khan (Mubarak Khalji); renewed vassalage
- 1321-22 — Ulugh Khan: false rumour; army chased back
- 1323 — Ulugh Khan: 5-month siege; Bobbareddi deserts; Warangal falls
### After the fall
- Captor Ulugh Khan (APPSC G2 2019)
- Died in the Narmada (Vilasa grant)
- Warangal renamed Sultanpur
- Andhra under Malik Burhanuddin; Malik Maqbul at Warangal
- Causes: disunity, weak cavalry, betrayal, repeated raids

### Rudrama Devi → accession, reign, family and FIRSTs
- Parentage: Kakatiya; daughter of Ganapatideva; mother Kupama Devi
- Training: Groomed from childhood as a son (putrika) in war, statecraft and administration
- Reign:
- Ruled as 'Rudradeva Maharaja' from 1258 (co-regency); Tripurantakam inscription (1266) still calls her Ganapati's vassal
- Full coronation after Ganapati's death; ruled 1262-1289, about 26 years
- 4th sovereign Kakatiya (after Rudradeva, Mahadeva, Ganapatideva)
- Marriage: Chalukya prince Veerabhadra
- Status: Second great woman ruler of medieval India after Razia Sultana
- Nayankara system: Some notes say she introduced it in her father's time

### Rudrama Devi → revolts, wars and death in 1289
## 1. Opponents → episode
| Opponent | Episode |
| Yadavas and Pandyas | Outside attacks; after Muttukur (1263) Nellore under the Pandyas |
| Half-brothers Harihara and Murari | Internal revolt → crushed with Prasadaditya Kayastha, Gona Gannareddy, Jannigadeva, Tripurari, Malyala Gundiya, Induluri Annayya, Viriyala Suri |
| Kalinga - Ganga Narasimhadeva | Occupied Vengi in 1262 → regained 1278 when Poti Nayaka + Prolinayaka beat Virabhanudeva |
| Yadava Mahadeva | Attacked → repulsed; commemorated by her title Rayagajakesari (see the titles page) |
| Kayastha Ambadeva | Kakatiya loyalist made head of the Kadapa region in 1272 → revolted with Pandya support; his victories in the Tripurantakam inscription |
## 2. Death
- Death (1289): Killed fighting rebel Ambadeva, with general Mallikarjuna Nayaka
- Recorded in Chandupatla inscription (Nalgonda district), set up by her servant Puvvula Mummadi → settled the debate about her death
- ⚠ Tripurantakam = Ambadeva's victories; Chandupatla = Rudrama's death
### Marco Polo → visit and observations of the Kakatiya kingdom
## 1. The visit
- Traveller: Venetian Marco Polo; work Il Milione / The Travels
- Visit: In Rudramadevi's reign (APPSC G2 2011 key) → landed at Motupalli (Mutfili) port
- Travelled 1271-1295; reached the Coromandel coast 1292 → at the very end of or just after her reign; exam keys say Rudrama
## 2. Observations
| Theme | Observation |
| Trade | Flourishing sea trade; no oppressive taxes on merchants |
| Wealth | Diamond trade/diamond mines (Golconda region); fine textiles |
| Climate & food | Hot climate (people wore loincloths); rice = the only local grain |
### Prataparudra II → succession, campaigns and the Nayankara system
## 1. Succession
- Relationship: Prataparudra II = Rudrama's grandson through her daughter Mummadamma (son of Mummadamma and Mahadeva)
- Accession: Trained by her; crowned in 1289; title 'Kumara Rudradeva'; ruled 1289-1323
- Early revolts: Kayastha, Velama, Reddy factions; Ambadeva's son Tripurari II
## 2. Campaigns
| Campaign | Year | Result |
| Against Ambadeva at Tripurantakam | 1291 | Commanders Kolanu Munumagammayya, Induluri Annayya won; his principality annexed |
| Against Ranganatha of Nellore (Pandya ally) | 1290s | Replaced by Manuma Gandapalu |
| Against the Yadava southern borders | 1290s | Commander Gonavithala pushed control toward Devagiri |
## 3. Nayankara system and economy
- Reform - Nayankara system: see A-78
- Reorganised into 72 Nayankaras; other notes: 75 (or 77) nayakships/principalities held by military officers
- Each Nayaka held a fort; one-fourth of the land under Nayakas
- Nayakas kept troops + paid a fixed tribute
- Later continued by Vijayanagara as the Amara Nayankara system
### Khalji and Tughlaq invasions of Warangal, 1303 to 1323 — year, commander, outcome
| Invasion | Year | Sultan / commander | Outcome |
| 1st | 1303 | Alauddin Khalji / Malik Fakhruddin Juna | Defeated (repulsed) at Upparapalli by Pothuganti Aili and Recherla Venna - first attack on Orugallu (APPSC G2 2012 key) |
| 2nd | 1309-10 | Alauddin / Malik Kafur | Fort besieged; Prataparudra defeated, submits, pays a large tribute and accepts Delhi overlordship (Amir Khusrau accompanied) |

- 1294/1295: Alauddin's first Deccan raid hit the Yadavas of Devagiri, not Andhra

- 1316: Alauddin's death → Prataparudra stopped tribute
- 1323 siege: Returned from Devagiri + besieged Orugallu; Prataparudra fought 5 months → surrendered
- ⚠ 1303 – 1310 – 1318 – 1323 = Fakhruddin Juna – Malik Kafur – Khusrau Khan – Ulugh Khan
- Exam angle: "first Muslim attack on Orugallu" → 1303 (APPSC 2012).
### Fall of Warangal → captivity, death, renaming and causes
- End: Held captive by Ulugh Khan (APPSC G2 2019 key)
- Drowned himself / died on the way to Delhi in the Narmada (Vilasa Tamra Sasanam / Vilasa copper plate / Vilasa grant; Musunuri Prolaya Nayaka's record)
- One account (some accounts) says the Kaveri
- Renamed: Orugallu/Warangal → Sultanpur ('Sultanpur' (Sultanpurti))
- Administration after the fall: Andhra placed under Malik Burhanuddin, wazir/vizier of Devagiri
- Malik Maqbul stationed at Warangal = converted captured Brahmin Jagannatha Pandit
- Causes of the fall: Disunity of southern kingdoms, weak cavalry, betrayal by chiefs (internal betrayal - Bobbareddi), exhaustion from repeated raids
- Exam angle: captor = Ulugh Khan (not Malik Kafur, not Alauddin).

### Universal angles
- Significance:
- Rudrama = leading example of a woman monarch in medieval South India
- Fall of Warangal (1323) ended the Kakatiyas → opened the Musunuri-led liberation movement
- Of 75 generals, Prolaya Nayaka, Racherla Singama, Prolaya Vema survived → led the liberation → Musunuri + Reddi states
- Criticism & debates: Fall blamed on southern disunity, weak cavalry, internal betrayal (Bobbareddi)
- FIRST/LAST: First Muslim attack = 1303; last Kakatiya ruler - Prataparudra II
- Comparative note: Nayankara system → Vijayanagara Amaranayakas → later Poligars

## Rudrama's end

- Killed 1289 fighting rebel Kayastha Ambadeva; general Mallikarjuna Nayaka

## Marco Polo
- Venetian traveller (Venice); visited during Rudramadevi's reign (APPSC G2 2011)
- Reached the Coromandel coast in 1292, at the very end of her reign or just after
- Landed at Motupalli = Mutfili port
- Noted rice as the local grain; sea trade, fine textiles, diamonds; merchants not heavily taxed

- Title Kumara Rudradeva (at accession)
- Nayankara system — principalities and forts held by military officers, the Nayakas; kept troops, paid fixed tribute
- Succession of the institution: Nayankara → Vijayanagara Amaranayakas → later Poligars
