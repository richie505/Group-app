### Kulottunga I and the Chalukya-Chola merger — FIRSTs and viceroys
## 1. End of the dynasty - Chalukya-Chola merger
- Kulottunga I / Kulothunga I (Rajendra Chalukya):
- Married Madhurantaki, daughter of the Chola Rajendra
- Chola throne in 1070 (Chola king in 1070) → Vengi in 1075 after Vijayaditya VII (last Eastern Chalukya) died
- → First Chalukya-Chola king (APPSC 2011)
- Achievements:
- 72-merchant mission to China
- Conquered Kalinga by sea → recorded in Kalingattuparani (Kalingattupparani) by Jayangondar
- Built Kulottunga Cholapuram (Visakhapatnam)
## 2. Viceroys and feudatories
- Viceroy with Eastern Chalukya titles: Sarvalokasraya, Sri Vishnuvardhana; crowned at Jananathapura (Draksharama)
- PYQ book = Rajaraja Chodaganga; APPSC 2025 key in your bank = Vikrama Chola → check the final key
- Last of the line: Parantaka Chola
- Andhra feudatories:
- Velanati Gonka I = Samasta Senadhipati of Vengi (fought off Kalyani Chalukyas and Kalingas)
- Kota Bhimaraju I (Kalinga campaigns)
- Kona Haihayas
- Mandaleswaras raised by Kulottunga I: Velanati, Nellore, Telugu, Konidena and Pottapi Chodas; Giri Paschima Durjayas; Natavadis; Chagis; Kona Haihayas

## 1. Velanati Chodas
- Velanati naming: Rulers named sons after their Chalukya-Chola overlords (Rajendra Choda was the first)
- End of Velanati line: Kakatiya Ganapatideva killed its last member
- Some notes: Mummadi Rajendra Choda; Nellore Choda Tikkana helped Ganapati kill Prithvisvara
## 2. Matsyas of Oddadi - northern Andhra chieftains
- Region: Northern Andhra chieftains of upland Srikakulam-Vizianagaram zone; grouped with the Ananda Gotrikas
### Kota chiefs → rulers, territory and overlords
| Ruler | Point |
| Betaraju I | Founder; seat Dhanyakatakam, Kammanadu |
| Bhimaraju I (1108-1127) | Backed the Chalukya-Cholas; Kalinga campaigns with the Velanati Chodas |
| Bhimaraju II | Palnadu war era (1182); absorbed Kondapadamati; titles Dannala Kota Padaveshtita Kadana |
- Kota territory: Includes Amaravati (Dhanyakatakam) and Tripurantakam
- Kakatiya marriage: A Kota chief married Ganapamba, daughter of Kakatiya Ganapatideva (some notes name Bhimaraju II)
- Kota overlords in turn: Chalukya-Cholas → Velanati Chodas → Kakatiyas (Kota Vamsiyas of Venadu became Kakatiya vassals)
- ⚠ Betaraju I founded both the Kota line and the Palnadu Haihaya line in your notes - match by seat (Dhanyakatakam vs Gurajala)
### Palnadu war (Palnati Yuddham), 1182 — site, factions and persons
- Date & site: 1182 CE at Karempudi
- Rival factions: All sons of Palnadu Haihaya Alugaraju (Anugaraju)
- Allies: Kota Bhimaraju II joined Kakatiya Rudradeva to back Nalagamaraju
- APPSC GS 2018 key: Velamas, "intruders" into Reddi-dominated Palnadu, driven out in the Battle of Karempudi
- Epic: Srinatha's Palnati Veera Charitra
| Person | Role |
| Nalagamaraju | Son of Mailama Devi, daughter of Velanati Gonka II |
| Malideva and brothers | Half-brothers (sons of Veeravijjaladevi), rival faction |
| Nagamma | Minister on Nalagama's side; Shaivite; built Veerabhadra Swamy temple at Gurajala; opposed Brahmanaidu's reforms |
| Brahmanaidu | Minister and leader of the Malideva faction; social reformer (interdining, Chapakudu) |
