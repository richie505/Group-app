### Issues → weak scrutiny and bypass routes
## 1. Bills passed without committee scrutiny
- Committee referral: less than a fifth of Bills go to committees vs UK, where every Bill is examined by committees of both Houses
- DRSCs: systematic committee system only from 1993
## 2. Ordinance and Money Bill routes as bypass
- Ordinances: heavy use of ordinances (Art. 123) + delegated legislation grows as legislative engagement falls
- Proposals: define "immediate necessity" by law; courts to test urgency per Krishna Kumar Singh (2017); publish reasons for each ordinance
- Money Bill route: bypasses Rajya Sabha for laws that may not strictly fit Art. 110
- Example: Aadhaar Act, 2016
### Opposition and Leader of Opposition → recognition and tools
## 1. Leader of Opposition
- First formally recognized: 1969
- Threshold: The largest opposition party must hold at least 10% of the total House strength — approximately 55 seats in the Lok Sabha
- LoP recognition: by the Speaker if party has at least one-tenth of the House's strength (quorum-size: 55 in LS)
- Statutory status: Salary and Allowances of Leaders of Opposition in Parliament Act, 1977
## 2. Opposition tools
- Opposition tools: Question Hour, motions, committees (PAC chaired by Opposition since 1967)
- Monsoon 2026: united opposition pushed the FCRA (Amendment) Bill, 2026 to a JPC
- Not in your sources: current Leaders of Opposition in Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha.
