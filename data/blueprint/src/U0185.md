### Why it failed
- No all-India character: mainly North and Central India
- Limited territorial and social base; no unified ideology or leadership
- Leaders quarrelled: Begum of Awadh vs Maulvi Ahmadullah
- Swords, old muskets vs Enfield rifles; British electric telegraph
- British power intact in East, West, South → troops sent north
