### Barani and Amir Khusrau → patrons, titles and views
## 1. Amir Khusrau
- A poet, historian and musician — served Alauddin Khalji and several Delhi Sultans (other Delhi rulers); associated with the Delhi Sultanate courts
- Known as Tuti-i-Hind
- Wrote Miftah-ul-Futuh, Khazain-ul-Futuh, Ashika, Nuh Sipihr and Tughluq Nama
| Angle | Fact |
| Title | Tuti-i-Hind (Parrot of India), given by Alauddin Khalji (patron) |
| Guru | Disciple of Nizamuddin Auliya (Chishti) |
| Sultans served | From Balban's time to Ghiyasuddin Tughluq (about seven Sultans); poet laureate under Ghiyasuddin |
| Hindavi | Riddles + poetry in Hindavi (early Hindi-Urdu) → grew into Rekhta/Urdu; pioneer of Hindavi-Urdu poetry |
| Sabaq-i-Hindi / Sabak-i-Hindi | Founded the Indian style of Persian poetry |
- ⚠ Tuti-i-Hind title from Alauddin Khalji; poet laureate under Ghiyasuddin Tughluq
## 2. Ziauddin Barani
- Patrons: Companion (nadim) of MBT; wrote under Firoz Tughlaq
- Views on kingship (jahandari):
- Persian model; needs awe and force
- Sultan protects the sharia but rules through zawabit (state laws) → state not truly Islamic (UPSC 2002)
- Social view: Office for the high-born; opposed promoting low-born men

### Music — Khusrau's forms, Sufi terms, instruments and treatises → author
## 1. Amir Khusrau's contribution
- Instruments: Traditionally credited with sitar (Persian setar + veena) and tabla (from the pakhawaj)
- Forms: Pioneered qaul, qalbana, tarana and khayal; tradition credits him with qawwali
- Ragas: Aiman (Yaman), Zilaf and Sazgiri
- Status: Among the prominent composers of Hindustani music
## 2. Sufi music terms
| Term | Meaning |
| Sama | Devotional music to induce ecstasy; core Sufi practice; Chishtis popularised sama |
| Qawwali | Institutionalised in Chishti khanqahs using Indian ragas |
- Bahmanis: Brought the rebab and oud to the Deccan; combined with veena and mridangam

### Universal angles
- Significance:
- Persian = elite language of administration and culture
- Indo-Persian historiography began (Minhaj, Barani, Afif, Isami) → main narrative sources for Sultanate and Deccan history
- Hindavi grew into Urdu in camps and bazaars
- Current relevance: PT365 (Apr 2026), on India-Iran cultural ties: Persian contributions = sitar, santur, qawwali, ghazal + Sufi traditions
- AP angle:
- Fall of Warangal (1323) described by Isami, Barani, Ferishta
- Golconda = Persian literary centre under Mir Momin Astarabadi
- Abdullah Qutb Shah and Abul Hasan Tana Shah first issued farmans in Telugu
- Addanki Gangadhara = first poet to dedicate a Telugu work (Tapati Samvaranopakhyanam) to a Muslim Sultan, Ibrahim Qutb Shah
- Comparative note: Qutb Shahi court used three languages
- Persian = court
- Telugu = people, revenue records
- Dakhni Urdu = camps, bazaars

### Amir Khusrau
## Language and poetry
- ★ First writer to use Urdu (emerging Hindavi) for poetic expression; Sufi poetry in Persian and Hindavi
- Riddles and poetry in Hindavi → later Rekhta / Urdu
- Founded Sabak-i-Hindi (Indian/Hindustani style of Persian poetry)
- Title Tuti-i-Hind (Parrot of India) — given by patron Alauddin Khalji
- Other titles: Homer of the East = Firdausi; Andhra Bhoja = Krishnadevaraya; Kaviraja = Samudragupta
## Music
- ★ Tabla and Sitar credited to Amir Khusrau (13th/14th c.); real name Abul Hasan Yaminuddin Khusrau
- ★ Creator of Qawwali; started forms such as qaul
- Ragas credited to him: Aiman (Yaman), Zilaf, Sazgiri

### Barani and Amir Khusrau → patrons, titles and views
## Nizamuddin Auliya
- ★ Shaikh Nizamuddin Auliya saw the reign of seven (more than seven) Sultans; never visited their court
- ★ Refused to meet Alauddin Khalji; when Jalaluddin tried to meet him through Amir Khusrau, he moved to Ajodhan
## Amir Khusrau
- ★ Born at Patiyali; called himself the Parrot of India
- ★ Linked with courts of many rulers: Nasiruddin Mahmud, Balban, Kaiqubad, Kayumars, Jalaluddin and Alauddin Khalji, Shihabuddin Umar, Mubarak Shah, Ghiyasuddin Tughluq, Muhammad bin Tughluq
- ★ Scholar of both Hindi (Hindavi) and Persian
- ★ Father of Sabak-i-Hindi (Hindustani style); said "Na Lafze Hindiwast aj Farsi Kam" (Hindi words are no less than Persian)
- ★ First Persian poet to depict the Indian environment
- ★ Father of Hindi Khari Boli
- ★ Not associated with the Bhakti movement
- ★ Works: Qiran-us-Sadain, Miftah-ul-Futuh, Nuh Sipihr, Ashiqa, Tughluq Nama, Khazain-ul-Futuh
## Barani's term
- ★ Zawabit = state laws
### Music — Khusrau's forms, Sufi terms, instruments and treatises → author
- ★ Sitar — invented by Amir Khusrau by combining the Irani Tamboora and Indian Veena; best example of Hindu-Muslim harmony in instruments
- ★ Tabla credited to Amir Khusrau
- ★ Sarangi — the MPPSC key's answer for "not of Indo-Islamic origin" (Turks brought instruments like the Rabab)
- ★ Chronology: Kitab-ul-Hind (Al-Biruni) → Prithviraja-Raso (Chandbardai) → Tabaqat-i-Nasiri (Minhaj-i-Siraj) → Fatawa-i-Jahandari (Barani)
- ★ Amir Khusrau wrote all four: Khazain-ul-Futuh, Nuh Sipihr, Miftah-ul-Futuh, Qiran-us-Sadain
### Sanskrit-Persian translations → translator and patron
- ★ Khwaza Ziya-uddin Nakhshabi — translated Chintamani Bhatt's Suka Saptati into Persian as Tutinama
- ★ Nakhshabi: 14th-c. Persian physician and Sufi saint; moved to Badayun (UP)
