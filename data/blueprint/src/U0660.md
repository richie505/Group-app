### Problems of large cities — waste, pollution, flooding, heat
## 1. Problem → data
| Problem | Fact |
| Waste | About 62 million tonnes MSW a year; only 70% collected, 30% processed (Jul 2025); cities produce more than 1.5 lakh tonnes/day (SBM-U); DRAP targets 214 legacy dumpsites by Dec 2026 |
| Pollution | 8 of the world's 10 most polluted cities in India; Delhi = most polluted capital |
| Congestion | Commuters lose 1.5-2 hours a day in traffic (BCG); One planner per 1 lakh people vs 1:5,000 in developed countries (NITI Aayog) |
- ES 2025-26 view: Integrate informality rather than eradicate it + reform land markets ("dead capital", Bhu-Aadhaar)
## 2. Urban flooding and heat island
- Causes of urban flooding (26 Aug 2026):
- Built-over lakes, wetlands and floodplains
- Concrete replacing permeable soil → rain runs off
- Blocked drains
- Urban heat island: Dense construction, asphalt, lost tree cover trap heat
- UEA study of 104 cities: Patiala could warm at double the rate models project (5 Feb 2026)
