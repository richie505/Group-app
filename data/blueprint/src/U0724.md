### Spaceports → location, history and features (Sriharikota, Kulasekarapattinam) — Sriharikota — location and history
## 1. Sriharikota — location and history
- Satish Dhawan Space Centre (SDSC-SHAR): ISRO's primary spaceport on the barrier island of Sriharikota
- District: Now Tirupati district; earlier Nellore district
- Nellore = answer in APPSC 2019 PYQs, before the 2022 district reorganisation
- Geography: Between Pulicat Lake (west) and the Bay of Bengal (east)
- History: Established 1971 as Sriharikota High Altitude Range (SHAR); renamed in 2002 after former chairman Satish Dhawan
- Why Sriharikota:
- East coast → rockets launch eastward over the sea, gain from Earth's rotation
- Close to the equator
- Sea for safe fall of spent stages; thinly populated
- Exam angle: "Sriharikota is in which district?" - answer by the date of the paper (Nellore earlier, Tirupati now).
### Spaceports → location, history and features (Sriharikota, Kulasekarapattinam) — Sriharikota — facilities and launches
## 1. Sriharikota — facilities and launches
- Launch pads: Two operational launch pads with Vehicle Assembly Buildings and Mobile Launch Pedestals
- First: PSLV, SSLV
- Second: GSLV, LVM3; standby for PSLV
- SPROB: Solid Propellant Space Booster Plant (SPROB) - one of the largest solid-propellant plants in the world; casts the S200 boosters of LVM3
- Launch View Gallery: Public; opened 2019
| Launch no. | Mission | Date / pad |
| 100th launch | 17th GSLV flight | 29 Jan 2025 |
| 105th | PSLV-C62/EOS-N1 | 12 Jan 2026 (first launch pad) |
| 107th | GSLV-F17/EOS-05 | 4 Sep 2026 (second launch pad) |
## 2. Second spaceport for SSLV — Kulasekarapattinam
- SSLV Launch Complex, Kulasekarapattinam: Thoothukudi (Tuticorin) district, Tamil Nadu
- Mainly for SSLV launches as private players enter
- Reason: Direct southward launch into polar/sun-synchronous orbit, without the fuel-costly manoeuvre around Sri Lanka needed from Sriharikota
- ⚠ Sriharikota = AP (eastward launches); Kulasekarapattinam = Tamil Nadu (southward, SSLV)
