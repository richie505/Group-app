### Minority — meaning, notified communities, FIRST/last — Meaning
## 1. Meaning
- Definition: "Minority" not defined in the Constitution; only religious and linguistic minorities recognised
- Constitution: Uses "minority" in Arts 29, 30, 350A, 350B but never defines it
- NCM Act: Does not define "minority" → under Section 2(c), a minority = any community notified by the Central Government
- Limits: Minority rights are not absolute (APPSC G2 2017) → State may regulate standards, not take away management
### Minority — meaning, notified communities, FIRST/last — Unit for minority status
## 1. Unit for minority status
- State-level: Religious and linguistic minorities identified at the State level (State-wise), not nationally → the State, not the whole of India, is the unit for status (T.M.A. Pai, 2002)
- Yet Sec. 2(c) of the NCM Act lets the Centre notify minorities nationally → critics: Sec 2(c) gives Centre "unbridled power"
- State power: States may notify (designate) their own minorities → Jains were State minorities in 11 States before 2014 (UPSC)
- Exam angle: G2 2017 - minority status is decided by the State's demography (true); "minority rights are absolute" is the untrue statement (APP's "absolute right" wording for Art. 30 should not be used in MCQs).

### Articles → provisions: 29, 30, 347, 350, 350A, 350B — Articles 29 and 30
- Articles 29 and 30: The first Constitutional provisions for minority rights; Article 30 — the first Minority Educational Rights provision
## 1. Articles 29 and 30
| Article | Protection |
| 29(1) | Any section of citizens with a distinct language, script or culture has the right to conserve it |
| 29(2) | No denial of admission to State-maintained or State-aided institutions on grounds of religion, race, caste or language |
| 30(1) | All minorities, religious or linguistic, may establish and administer educational institutions of their choice ("Charter of Education Rights"); no discrimination in grant-in-aid |
| 30(2) | The State shall not discriminate against minority-managed institutions in granting aid |
- Art. 29: Protects any section of citizens (not only minorities) → wider; covers religious and linguistic minorities; applies only to citizens
- Art. 29 protects minorities but is not confined to them
- 29(2): No citizen denied admission to a State-maintained or State-aided institution on grounds only of religion, race, caste or language
- ⚠ Trap: sex and place of birth are not listed
- Language promises: Political promises to conserve a section's language ≠ "corrupt practice" under the Representation of the People Act (RPA, 1951; some notes say "1957")
- Art. 30: Applies only to minorities; Madrasas are run under Art. 30
- 44th Amendment: Added Art. 30(1A) → removing the right to property does not affect minorities' right to establish institutions; compensation on acquisition
- ⚠ Art. 29 = all citizens; Art. 30 = only minorities

### Meaning & notified minorities
## Who is a minority
- NCM Act does not define 'minority'; Section 2(c) — any community notified by the Central Government
- States can name their own minorities (for State purposes); 11 States had declared Jains a minority before 2014
- After T.M.A. Pai Foundation (2002) — minority status decided by population in the State concerned, not all India
## Notified communities
- 1993: Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Parsis; 2014: Jains — so six notified communities (Jews not included)
- ★ Jains — Cabinet decision 20 January 2014; notified as Sixth Minority Community on 27 January 2014
## Related bodies
- NCMEI — decides minority status, settles affiliation disputes, issues minority certificates; civil court powers; cannot amend the Constitution
- Criminal Tribes Act, 1871 (branded Denotified Tribes) — repealed in 1952
- SEED portal — DNT online applications (health insurance, coaching, housing); run by Department of Social Justice, not NTA
### Constitutional articles
## Articles
| Article | Provision |
| 29(1) | protects language, script or culture of any distinct section of citizens |
| 29(2) | no denial of admission to State-maintained/aided institutions on grounds only of religion, race, caste or language (sex and place of birth not listed) |
| 30 | State cannot discriminate in aid to minority institutions; may regulate standards without destroying minority character |
| 30(2) | no discrimination in aid on ground of minority management (religious or linguistic) |
| 30(1A) | protects minority institutions on compulsory acquisition of property — 44th Amendment |
| 350A | mother-tongue instruction at primary stage |
| 350B | Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities — appointed by and reports to the President |
- ★ Special Officer for linguistic minorities — Article 350B
- 'Minority' used in Articles 29, 30, 350A and 350B, never defined
- 350A and 350B inserted by the 7th Amendment, 1956 (States reorganisation)
- Religious minorities — NCM and Ministry of Minority Affairs; linguistic minorities — identified State-wise, Special Officer under 350B
## Cases & bodies
- T.M.A. Pai Foundation (2002) — 11-judge Bench; State as unit; regulation but not control of minority institutions
- NCM functions: evaluate development, monitor safeguards, examine complaints, recommend measures (not conducting elections to minority institutions); civil court-like powers (summon witnesses, call records)
- NCMEI — 2004 Act; statutory State Minority Commissions in 19 States/UTs incl. Andhra Pradesh
- Section 2(c) criticised for giving Centre 'unbridled power' to notify minorities
- Justice Mohammed Sardar Ali Khan — first NCM chairman; Ranganath Misra Commission studied religious and linguistic minorities

## Waqf
- Order: Waqf Act 1954 → Waqf Act 1995 → Amendment 2013 → Amendment 2025
- Waqf Act, 1995 — administration of waqf properties
- Central Waqf Council — statutory, 1964; advises Centre on State Waqf Boards
- 2013 Amendment — Central Waqf Council can direct State Boards; bars sale, gift, mortgage, exchange, transfer of waqf property
- 2025 Amendment — waqf by user only for properties registered before the law; non-Muslim members; central portal within six months; UMEED portal launched June 2025
- SC interim order September 2025 — stayed five-years-practising-Islam condition and Collector's power; accepted Limitation Act change
