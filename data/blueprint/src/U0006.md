### Extent & names
## Names
- "Harappan" favoured — Harappa was the first site found and the civilisation went beyond the Indus valley; "Sindhu-Sarasvati" also used
- Meluha — Mesopotamian name for the Indus region; Dilmun (Bahrain), Magan (Oman/Makran) — middle stops
- Site meanings: Mohenjodaro — "Mound of the Dead"; Kalibangan — "Black bangles"; Lothal — "Mini Harappa" (not City of the Dead); Chanhudaro — cotton production
## Extent
- About 1.3 million sq km; about five million people
- Ghaggar–Hakra system — most sites, about 250 sites, six cities
- Importance — India's first urbanisation; largest Bronze Age civilisation by area
## Order and theories
- Excavation order: Harappa (1921) → Mohenjodaro (1922) → Chanhudaro (1931) → Lothal (1954)
- Mackay — came from Sumer; Marshall — grew locally; Wheeler — spread of ideas
- Nature: Indus — urban; Rigvedic — pastoral; Later Vedic — farming; Medieval — landlordism

### IVC discovery and excavation timeline
- ★ Main source of knowledge of the Indus people — seals (more than 3000 found)
