### Pala rulers → achievements, and the Sena successors
## 1. Gopala - founder
- Accession: Elected by notables to end matsyanyaya (anarchy), c. mid-8th c. (UPSC)
- Source: Khalimpur plate of Dharmapala records the election
- Conquests: Displaced later Guptas of Magadha and Khadgas of east Bengal
- Building: Built Odantapuri
- UPPSC 2021: Kings chosen by the people = Gopala and Nandivarman II Pallavamalla

### Nalanda and the Pala-era mahaviharas → founder, present location
## 1. Nalanda
- Nalanda University: Founded by Kumaragupta I in the 5th century AD — a Gupta foundation; located in Bihar
- A major Buddhist centre of higher learning — Buddhist higher education
- Attracted students from China, Tibet, Greece, Persia and other regions
- Nava Nalanda Mahavihara: Associated with Bhikshu Jagadish Kashyap — located in Bihar; a research centre; established — its foundation stone laid — on 20 November 1951

### Mahavihara features, destruction and odd-one-out
## 1. Features
- Vikramashila:
- Centre of Tantric/Vajrayana Buddhism and logic; trained monks for Tibet
- ⚠ Trap (BPSC 2023): in Bhagalpur (not Banka); founded by Dharmapala (not Gopala)

- Valabhi: Hinayana centre of Harsha's time
