### Articles → Union's duty to protect states and emergency Articles (352-360, 355, 356, 365)
## 1. Emergency (Articles 352 to 360)
- Article 352: National Emergency proclamation; Article 353: its effect
- Article 354: Distribution of revenues during an Emergency
- Article 355: Duty of the Union to protect States
- Article 356: President's Rule — failure of constitutional machinery; Article 357: legislative powers under Article 356
- Article 358: Suspension of Article 19 during an Emergency; Article 359: Suspension of enforcement of Fundamental Rights
- Article 360: Financial Emergency
## 2. Union's duty to protect states
| Article | Provision |
| Art 355 | Protect every state against external aggression and internal disturbance + ensure governance per the Constitution (UPSC 2003; UPPSC 2001 key) |
| Art 356 | Justified by the Art 355 duty (Link to Art 356) |
## 3. IMDT Act case
- Illegal Migrants (Determination by Tribunals) Act, 1983 (IMDT Act): Struck down in Sarbananda Sonowal v. Union of India (2005)
- Reason: It violated Article 355 — the Union's duty to protect States against external aggression and internal disturbance
### Articles → Union's protective and emergency powers (355, 356, 365, 250, 353, 354) — national emergency effects
## 1. National emergency - effects on Centre-State relations
| Sphere | Effect | Article |
| Legislative | Parliament legislates on any State List subject. These laws lapse 6 months after the emergency ends | 250 |
| Executive | The Centre can direct a state on "any" matter. State governments are not suspended | 353[GK] |
| Financial | The President can reduce or cancel transfers until the end of that financial year. Orders are laid before Parliament | 354[GK] |
- Nature: Constitution becomes unitary rather than federal
- Lok Sabha term: Extendable by 1 year at a time
- ⚠ 250 – 353 – 354 = legislative – executive – financial

### Emergency Articles 352-360
- ★ Sarkaria Commission — to review Union-State relations; chaired by Justice R.S. Sarkaria, formed 9 June 1983; members B. Shivaraman, S.R. Sen
- All India Services serving Union and States — a unitary feature
- National Emergency — federation becomes quasi-unitary, Union powers grow
- Article 355 — Union's duty to protect States; Article 359 — enforcement of Fundamental Rights suspended

### National Emergency effects
- Art. 353 — Union may direct a State on any matter; State Governments not suspended
- Art. 354 — President may reduce/cancel transfers to States until end of the financial year; order laid before Parliament
- Art. 250 laws on State List — end six months after the Emergency ends
### Art. 355 → 356 chain
- Art. 355 — Union to protect every State against external aggression and internal disturbance, and ensure governance per the Constitution
### Areas of friction
- Article 356 — used over 125 times, often against opposition-led governments
- Excluding cesses and surcharges, divisible pool ≈ 81% of gross tax revenue
- CBI investigation consent — Section 6, DSPE Act (not Article 156, which concerns the Governor)
