### Doppler effect and radar terms → meaning
## 1. Doppler effect
- The apparent change in frequency due to relative motion between a source and an observer
- Approaching source: Higher apparent frequency and pitch
- Receding source: Lower apparent frequency and pitch
- Applications:
- Radar speed guns (police radar): Vehicle / traffic speed
- Medical Doppler ultrasound: Blood-flow velocity
- Astronomy: Red shift and blue shift — the expanding universe and star motion (Doppler effect for light)
- SONAR: Speed of submarines and underwater objects
- Weather radar: Storms and wind speed
- Bats: Echolocation with frequency shifts
## 2. Radar types
| Radar type | Principle / use |
| Doppler | Measures speed from the frequency shift; used in weather radars |
| SAR | Synthetic Aperture Radar: imaging at night and through clouds |
| AESA | Active Electronically Scanned Array, with a T/R module on every element; electronically steered beam, no moving antenna: UTTAM (tested on Tejas Mk1A), MF-STAR (P17A) |
| VHF anti-stealth | Long wavelengths pick up stealth aircraft (e.g. Surya, below) |
| Over-the-horizon | HF waves bounced off the ionosphere to see beyond the horizon |
## 3. AWACS vs AEW&C
- AWACS: Air-based long-range radar surveillance and control system for aircraft (APPSC 2018 key); rotating radar → full coverage
| AWACS | AEW&C (Netra) |
| Larger platform, long-range surveillance + full control of air battle | Smaller jet, early warning + command-and-control, shorter range |
