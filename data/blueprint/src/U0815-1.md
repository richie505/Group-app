### AP raises judicial officers' retirement age to 62
## 1. Key facts
- 61 → 62 years; Ordinance by Governor S. Abdul Nazeer on 1 October 2026; effective from 31 March 2026
- AP HC Full Court recommended on 24 August 2026; SC had directed 7 States to raise to 62
- Earlier 60 → 61 from 1 November 2024; Law Minister N.M.D. Farooq
## 2. Background
- Article 233 — district judges; 234 — other judicial services; 235 — HC control over subordinate courts
### Justice M. Satyanarayana Murthy proposed as APSHRC Chairman
## 1. Key facts
- Selection Committee (chaired by CM Naidu, 12 October 2026) recommended retired HC judge Justice Mallavolu Satyanarayana Murthy as Chairman, AP State Human Rights Commission
- Members recommended: Narella Srinivasa Rao, J.V. Manohar Reddy; attended by Speaker Ayyanna Patrudu, Council Chairman Moshen Raju, Home Minister Vangalapudi Anitha
- Born 14 June 1960, Machilipatnam; permanent judge 2 March 2016; retired 13 June 2022

- PHRA 1993 — Sec 21 (SHRC), Sec 22 (Governor appoints on committee advice); Chairman + 2 members; tenure 3 years or age 70
- APSHRC office: Kurnool; NHRC est. 12 October 1993

### SSAAT wins SKOCH Gold Award
## 1. News

- ⚠ First State Social Audit Organisation in India to win it

### Adivasis build 100-metre wooden bridge in ASR district
## 1. News
- ~100 Adivasis from 8 villages built a 100-metre wooden bridge between Lothugedda and Boyapadu, Chintapalli mandal, ASR district
- Built August 8–15, 2026 (completed on Independence Day); earlier Panchayat Raj bridge washed away in 2024
- Similar bridge at T. Korukonda built a year earlier
- Culvert at Chinna Gangavaram (Darakonda panchayat, G.K. Veedhi mandal) washed away; ₹1.89-crore bridge sanctioned on Ganugurolu–Bangarubudi road, G. Madugula mandal
## 2. Background
- Fifth Schedule — Scheduled Areas (other than Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram — Sixth Schedule); Article 46; PESA 1996
### AP FIRST moved to Science & Technology Department
## 1. News
