# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 3:83 - Monetary Policy Committee and the flexible inflation targeting framework
### Road to FIT
- Target 4% CPI ±2%: set by the Centre with RBI (Sec 45ZA), achieved by the MPC (Sec 45ZB)
- 1985 — Chakravarty: monetary targeting
- 1987 — Vaghul: money market
- 1998 — Multiple-indicator approach (opaque)
- 2014 — Urjit Patel Committee: FIT, CPI anchor, 4% ±2%
- 2015 — Monetary Policy Framework Agreement (Govt + RBI)
- 2016 — RBI (Amendment) Act: Sec 45Z to 45ZO; MPC first meets October 2016
### Key sections
| Section | Provision |
| 45ZA | Centre, with RBI, sets target once every five years |
| 45ZB | MPC constituted (statutory) |
| 45ZN | Failure: CPI above 6% or below 2% for three consecutive quarters; RBI reports reasons, remedy, time |
### MPC composition (6 members)
- ▲ RBI Governor — Chair; casting vote on a tie
- ▸ Deputy Governor (monetary policy) — Ex officio
- ▸ One RBI officer — Nominated by Central Board
- ▸ Three external members — Appointed by Central Government; 4-year term; no reappointment
### MPC procedure
- Meets at least 4 times a year (in practice six)
- Quorum 4, incl. Governor or Deputy Governor
- Decides policy repo rate; binding on RBI
- Primary objective: price stability; growth second
### Target periods
| Notified | Period | Target |
| August 2016 | to 31 March 2021 | 4% |
| March 2021 | April 2021 to March 2026 | Retained |
| 25 March 2026 | 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2031 | Retained (second time) |
### Repo decisions
- May 2022 - Feb 2023 — Raised 4% to 6.5%; tightening
- Apr 2025 — 6.0%; stance accommodative
- Jun 2025 — Back to neutral; CRR cut 100 bps
- Dec 2025 — 5.25%; FY26 easing 100 bps
- 5 Aug 2026 — Unanimous hold, neutral; minutes hint at a hike
### CPI anchor & debates
- Anchor: CPI-C, new base 2024 (from 12 Feb 2026); NSO (MoSPI)
- Failure clause first invoked 2022
- Debate: headline vs core targeting; food shocks outside MPC control
- AP inflation 4.9% (May 2026) vs 3.9% national
### Exam corner
- Target section = 45ZA, not 45ZB
- 45ZN = three consecutive quarters (⚠ other notes: months)
- RBI Act amended for FIT in 2016 (APPSC 2022)
- MPC fixes repo (APPSC 2017, 2019); target 4% (APPSC 2017)
- 6 members – quorum 4 – 4 meetings – 4 years
### Framework — objectives and phases from monetary targeting (1985) to FIT (2016)
## 1. Objectives
- Primary objective / mandate: Price stability
- Growth second, "keeping in mind" the target (RBI Act)
- Financial stability also a goal
- Not an objective:
## 2. Phases of the framework
| Phase | Approach |
| 1985-1998 | Monetary targeting (Chakravarty) [GK] |
| 1998-2014 | Multiple-indicator approach: inflation, growth, exchange rate, credit, etc. [GK for the start year] |
| 2014-2016 | Glide path: Urjit Patel Committee, then the 2015 Agreement |
| 2016 onwards | Flexible inflation targeting with the MPC |
- Multiple-indicator approach: Criticised as opaque → markets could not predict RBI when indicators pointed different ways
- Challenges after 1991: Exchange-rate volatility (APPSC 2023 key) + open capital account + external shocks + fiscal dominance
### Committees → recommendation
| Committee | Year | Contribution / recommendation |
| Sukhamoy Chakravarty Committee | 1985 | Organised money market (UPSC); monetary targeting (money supply growth as the anchor) with feedback [GK] |
| N. Vaghul | 1987 | Blueprint for the money market; led to Commercial Paper (1990) (UPSC; APP) |
| S.S. Tarapore | 1997; 2nd report 2006 | Roadmap to capital account convertibility (UPSC; APP) |
| Urjit Patel Committee (Expert Committee to Revise and Strengthen the Monetary Policy Framework; set up by RBI) | 2014 | Drop the multiple-indicator approach for FIT; CPI as nominal anchor (CPI-C); 4% ±2% target; a committee (MPC) to decide rates (CDI) |
- Recent committee: Committee to strengthen the monetary policy framework headed by Urjit Patel (APPSC-G2 2017 key)
- ⚠ 1985 Chakravarty – 1987 Vaghul – 1997 Tarapore – 2014 Urjit Patel
### Acts, agreements and Sections → provision
## 1. Agreement and Act
| Act / agreement | Year | Provision |
| Monetary Policy Framework Agreement | Signed by Government of India and RBI in 2015 | Formally adopted flexible inflation targeting; Government sets the target; RBI keeps CPI inflation at 4% +/- 2%; RBI answerable to the Government when it misses |
| RBI (Amendment) Act, 2016 | 2016 | Inserted Sections 45Z to 45ZO into the RBI Act, 1934; APPSC-GS 2022 key: amended in 2016 |
- Exam angle: "In which year was the RBI Act amended for FIT?" The answer is 2016.
## 2. Sections
| Section | Provision |
| Section 45ZA | Central Government, in consultation with RBI, sets the target once every five years; notified in the Official Gazette |
| Section 45ZB | MPC constituted as a statutory body |
| Section 45ZN | Failure = average CPI above 6% or below 2% for three consecutive quarters; RBI reports to the Government (the Centre) the reasons, remedial action and time to return to target (time needed) |
- ⚠ Trap: Target is 45ZA, not 45ZB; many books wrongly print 45ZB
- 45ZN wording: The Act says quarters
- Conflict: Some notes say "3 consecutive months"; others (2026) say quarters → quarters is correct
- First invoked: 2022, after CPI stayed above 6% from January 2022

### Monetary Policy Committee — basis, composition, voting, quorum
## 1. Statutory basis and role
- Basis: Statutory body constituted under Section 45ZB of the RBI Act, created by the 2016 amendment
- Role: MPC fixes the policy repo rate needed to reach the inflation target; decisions bind the RBI (APPSC-G2 2017; APPSC-GS 2019 keys)
## 2. Composition and procedure
| Member | How |
| RBI Governor | Chairperson (ex officio); casting vote on a tie |
| Deputy Governor in charge of monetary policy | Ex officio |
| One RBI officer | Nominated by the RBI Central Board |
| Three external members | Appointed by the Central Government, 4-year term |
- Members: 6 members = 3 from RBI + 3 external members appointed by the Central Government
- Meetings: At least 4 times a year (UPSC); in practice bi-monthly (six times)
- Quorum: 4, including the Governor or, in his absence, the Deputy Governor
- Reappointment: External members not eligible
- ⚠ 6 members – quorum 4 – meets at least 4 times – external term 4 years
### Policy stances and the MPC decision record, 2022–2026
## 1. Stances
| Stance | Meaning |
| Accommodative | Ready to cut rates or add liquidity to support growth [GK] |
| Neutral | Can move rates either way depending on the data [GK] |
| Withdrawal of accommodation | Tightening bias; draining excess liquidity [GK] |
## 2. Decision record
| Period | Repo decision | Stance |
| May 2022 - Feb 2023 | Raised 4% to 6.5%, then held till early 2025 (TH 22 Apr 2026) | Tightening |
| Apr 2025 | Cut 25 bps to 6.0% (2nd cut of 2025) | Neutral to accommodative |
| Jun 2025 | Cut to 5.50% [GK]; CRR cut 100 bps | Back to neutral (ES 2025-26) |
| Dec 2025 | Cut 25 bps to 5.25%; FY26 GDP 7.3%, CPI 2.0% | Neutral |
| Feb, Jun, Aug 2026 | Held at 5.25%, unanimous; 5 Aug 2026 = unanimous hold, neutral stance | Neutral |
- Recent stances:
- Neutral → accommodative in April 2025 (repo cut to 6%)
- Neutral now (held in Feb, Jun and Aug 2026)
- August 2026 minutes: Point to a likely hike
- Governor Malhotra says hardening inflation "may suggest a recalibration"
- Headline inflation averaged 3.93% so far this year

### Issues — global monetary tightening and spillovers to India
- June 2026 policy: Major central banks gearing up to tighten, yet MPC held at 5.25% + cut FY27 growth forecast
- Spillover channels: Capital outflows, rupee pressure, imported inflation
### Universal angles
- Current relevance:
- 4% +/- 2% retained to March 2031 (26 Mar 2026)
- Repo at 5.25% since the Dec 2025 cut (125 bps of cuts since Feb 2025), held through Aug 2026
- Criticism & debates:
- After a decade of FIT, evidence mixed on whether it curbs inflation without hurting output and jobs

## Committees and phases

## Statute
- RBI (Amendment) Act, 2016 — added Sections 45Z to 45ZO; statutory backing for FIT and MPC
- 2015 Monetary Policy Framework Agreement — not a statute; Central Government sets the target, RBI conducts policy to achieve it
- MPC decisions — binding on the RBI
### Key sections
| Section (RBI Act) | Provision |
| 45ZA | Inflation target — set by Central Government with RBI, every five years, notified in Gazette |
| 45ZB | Constitution of the MPC |
| 45ZN | Failure to meet the target |
- Failure — average CPI inflation above 6% or below 2% for three consecutive quarters
- On failure RBI reports reasons, remedial actions and estimated time to return to target
### MPC composition (6 members)
- Three external members — appointed by the Central Government (not RBI Board)
- Term four years; not eligible for reappointment
### MPC procedure
- Primary objective — price stability with growth in mind; financial stability also recognised
- MPC must meet at least four times a year by law; six (bi-monthly) is practice
### Target periods
| Notification | Period |
| August 2016 | up to March 2021 |
| March 2021 | April 2021 – March 2026 |
| 25 March 2026 | April 2026 – March 2031 |
