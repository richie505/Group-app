### Places → canals, links and diversion (GWDT award)
- Canals:
- Right Main Canal → Krishna delta
- Left Main Canal → Visakhapatnam (Revised Administrative Sanction for LMC, Jun 2026; LMC up to Visakhapatnam targeted Mar 2027, as of 15 Aug 2026)
- Godavari Water Disputes Tribunal: Set up 1969; award 1980
- Award clauses:
- Polavaram cleared with FRL +150 ft
- 80 TMC Godavari water diverted into Krishna above Vijayawada (water transfer via Right Main Canal)
- Split: 45 TMC to AP + 35 TMC to Karnataka and Maharashtra (21 + 14)
- Link projects:
- Proposed Polavaram-Nallamala Sagar (earlier Banakacherla) link
- Telangana: link violates APRA 2014 + 1980 GWDT award (Jul 2025; Jan 2026)
- ⚠ 80 TMC = 45 (AP) + 35 (Karnataka 21 + Maharashtra 14)
### Acts and sections — APRA Section 90 and the Polavaram Ordinance
| Sub-section | What it says |
| 90(2) | Expedient in public interest that the Union takes under its control the regulation and development of the project for irrigation |
| 90(3) | Consent of the successor State of Telangana is deemed to have been given |
| 90(4) | Central Government shall execute the project and obtain all clearances - environmental, forest, R&R |
- Execution in practice: Government of AP executes the irrigation component on behalf of GoI; claims reimbursement/advance from Centre
- Exam angle: "which section" (answer 90; one old key says Section 30, and some CA notes say Section 89 - both wrong); statement-pairs on 90(1)-(4).
## 1. Polavaram Ordinance
- Ordinance: AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Ordinance, 2014 = "Polavaram Ordinance Bill"
- Amended territorial provision → Khammam submergence villages/mandals stay with AP
- Base rule in Act: Khammam → Telangana excluding villages in G.O.Ms. No. 111 (27 June 2005)
- Bill: Passed by Lok Sabha on 11 July 2014
- Seven mandals moved from Khammam to AP: Kukunoor, Velerupadu, VR Puram (Vararamachandrapuram), Chintoor, Kunavaram, Bhadrachalam (except the temple town), Burgampadu
- Effect: AP's area 1,60,205 → 1,62,970 sq km
### Persons and bodies → roles (Polavaram Project Authority)
- Constitution: By the Union after AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 to oversee the national project
- Role: Central body routing national-project funding + mediating inter-state coordination

### Submergence and R&R issues
## 1. Other States and submergence
- Objecting States: Odisha and Chhattisgarh (protective embankments due by 2014-15, CAG) + Telangana (backwater at Bhadrachalam)
- Court cases: Odisha + Chhattisgarh submergence cases pending in Supreme Court
- CAG (performance audit to Mar 2023): Settle submergence issues with neighbouring States in consultation with PPA + build protective embankments
- 2026: ~189 villages in submergence zone on alert in Polavaram district during Godavari-Sabari floods (Jul 2026)
## 2. R&R
- Displaced: Mostly Koya and Kondh tribals
- Progress:
- Dec 2025: LA and R&R only 27.40% complete - main bottleneck
- Aug 2026: 94% land acquisition, 52% relocation of project-affected families; R&R colonies targeted Dec 2026
- Compensation:
- ₹1,943 crore paid in three phases (Mar 2026)
- House-site value doubled from ₹1 lakh to ₹2 lakh in R&R colonies (Cabinet, 1 May 2026)
- Follows RFCTLARR Act, 2013
