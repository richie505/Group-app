### World Bank and ADB projects — APIIATP, roads, Amaravati
## 1. World Bank projects
- Climate-resilient agriculture (APIIATP): climate resilience + productivity in 565 minor-tank villages across 22 districts; concluded 31 Oct 2025 (SES 2025-26)
- Roads: World Bank-funded AP Road Sector Project - repair/strengthening of 850 km of MDRs and state highways, climate-resilient (Feb 2026)
- Disaster recovery: AP Disaster Recovery Project after Cyclone Hudhud (2014)
## 2. Amaravati capital city funding
- Phase-I lenders: World Bank and Asian Development Bank (APPSC G2 2025)
- Union Budget 2026-27: ₹432.09 crore for Amaravati Integrated Urban Development Programme through IBRD (2 Feb 2026)
- Review: World Bank-ADB delegation reviewed APCRDA's capital works on 11 May 2026 (12 May 2026)
