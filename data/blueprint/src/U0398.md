### Vasak's generations (1977)
- Every Fundamental Right is a human right, but not every human right is a Fundamental Right
- ▲ First: liberty — Civil & political: life, speech, fair trial, vote
- ▸ Second: equality — Economic, social, cultural: work, education, health
- ▸ Third: fraternity — Collective: healthy environment, development, peace
### UDHR 1948
- Adopted by UNGA on 10 December 1948 = Human Rights Day

- Not included: universal basic income (JPSC 2023)

### Human rights — concept and Karel Vasak's three generations
## 1. Concept
- Meaning: Inherent, inalienable rights held simply because one is human
- Basis: Rest on human dignity; universal across cultures
- Indivisible and interdependent: Civil-political + socio-economic rights stand together (affirmed by Vienna Declaration, 1993)
- FR vs human right: Every Fundamental Right is a human right, but not every human right is an FR
- Right to vote = only a statutory right (Sec 62, RP Act)
## 2. Generations
- Proponent: Karel Vasak (1977) → three generations mapped to liberty, equality and fraternity
| Generation | Rights |
| First (liberty) | Civil and political rights: life, speech, fair trial, vote |
| Second (equality) | Economic, social and cultural rights: work, education, health, eradication of social discrimination (JPSC 2016) |
| Third (fraternity/solidarity) | Collective rights: healthy environment, development, peace (UPSC) |
- Overlap: Generations overlap, e.g. education is both a second- and a third-generation right (UPSC)
- ⚠ 1st – 2nd – 3rd = liberty – equality – fraternity
### UDHR, 1948 — adoption, features, rights included and not included
## 1. Features
- Universal applicability; upholds equality and non-discrimination
- Protects civil, political, economic, social and cultural rights
- Human dignity is at its core; contains a preamble and 30 articles (UPSC)
## 2. Adoption and structure
- Adopted: By the UNGA on 10 December 1948 (JPSC 2021)
- Human Rights Day: 10 December
- Drafting chair: Eleanor Roosevelt
- Legal status: Not a treaty → not legally binding
## 3. Rights included and not included
- Included: Dignity, liberty and equality; life; adequate standard of living; education
- Not included: A right to universal basic income (JPSC 2023)
- Privacy: JPSC 2016 key treats right to privacy as not a UN-listed human right
- ⚠ Trap: In fact UDHR Art. 12 + ICCPR Art. 17 protect privacy → expect this option only in badly set questions
### International Bill of Human Rights — ICCPR, ICESCR, protocols → year
- Components: UDHR + ICCPR + ICESCR + optional protocols
| Instrument | Adopted | In force [GK] | Key content |
| ICESCR | 16 Dec 1966 | 3 Jan 1976 | Work, social security, health, education; realised progressively |

- India's status: Party to the ICCPR since 1979 but not signed the First Optional Protocol

- ⚠ ICESCR in force Jan 1976 before ICCPR Mar 1976
