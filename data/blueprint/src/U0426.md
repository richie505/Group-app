## 1. Look East → Act East
- Drivers of Look East: Countering China + new markets + instability in West and Central Asia (UPSC)
- Scope: Act East conceived as economic → extended to political, strategic and cultural fields (true statement, APPSC-G1 2024)
- North-East: Gateway to ASEAN
- Exam angle: statement pairs on "economic origin, strategic extension" and on which projects are ASEAN-linked.
## 2. Neighbourhood First
- SAARC leaders invited to the PM's swearing-in (May 2014)
- BIMSTEC leaders invited in 2019
- Examples:
- COVID vaccines to neighbours, Bhutan hydropower, Kaladan, Trilateral Highway (UPSC)
- Maldives High Impact Community Development Projects Phase III; Sri Lanka rooftop solar
## 3. Link West
- GCC: India's largest socio-economic partner
- Over $160 billion trade + 10 million diaspora
- PM Modi: 15 visits to GCC states + two to Israel (Mar 2026)

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 2:152
### Policies
- Act East — launched 2014 at the East Asia Summit in Myanmar (not a SAARC Summit), PM Modi
- Act East began as an economic policy; later political, strategic, cultural, Indo-Pacific
- Look East reasons: countering China, new markets, instability in West and Central Asia
- Link West (Think West) — the Gulf, Iran and Israel
- Chabahar (Iran, Gulf of Oman) — access to Afghanistan, Central Asia, INSTC, avoiding Pakistan
- In 2026 the USA ended (not extended) India's Chabahar sanctions waiver
### Maritime visions
- SAGAR (2015) and MAHASAGAR (2025) — both announced in Mauritius
- SHANTI — rules framework for shared sea spaces; first in the Bay of Bengal
- Free, open, inclusive Indo-Pacific vision (ASEAN centrality) — Shangri-La Dialogue, June 2018
- IPOI — proposed at the 14th EAS, Bangkok, November 2019; open, non-treaty, seven pillars, builds on SAGAR
### IPOI: 7 pillars
- Seven pillars: Maritime Security; Maritime Ecology; Maritime Resources; Capacity Building and Resource Sharing; Disaster Risk Reduction and Management; Science, Technology and Academic Cooperation; Trade, Connectivity and Maritime Transport
- Nuclear Disarmament Verification — not a pillar
### Terms
- Act East 3Cs — Commerce, Culture, Connectivity; 4th C — Capacity building (not Climate action)
- Gujral Doctrine — non-reciprocity, non-interference, no use of territory against a neighbour
- SAGAR — India as net security provider in the IOR; partly an answer to China's String of Pearls
### Projects & places
- ASEAN-linked: Kaladan project and India-Myanmar-Thailand Trilateral Highway; Akhaura-Agartala rail — Bangladesh
- Coastal surveillance radar chain — Sri Lanka, Maldives, Mauritius, Seychelles
