### Terms → meaning: trade theory, terms of trade, remedies, exchange rate — Trade terms
## 1. Trade barriers and terms
| Term | Meaning |
| Tariff | A tax on imports |
| Import quota | A quantity restriction on imports — a quantitative restriction on the amount of imports (PYQ key) |
| Anti-dumping measures | Protect against goods exported below normal value |
| Dumping | Exporting at a price below home-market price or cost [GK] → met with anti-dumping duty |
| Free trade | Little or no tariff and little or no quantitative restriction |
| Import licensing | An administrative restriction on imports |
| Rules of origin | Determine the country of origin for trade-policy purposes; decide which goods qualify for FTA concessions |
| Entrepot trade | Importing goods, storing or trading imported goods and re-exporting them (re-export of imports) |
| Intra-industry trade | A country imports and exports the same product (PYQ 2025; PYQ key) |
| Absolute advantage (Adam Smith) | Producing a good at lower absolute cost than others [GK] |
| Comparative advantage (Ricardo) | Specialise in the good with lower opportunity cost (APPCA) |
| Countervailing duty | Offsets foreign subsidies (e.g., US preliminary CVD of 125.87% on an Indian product, Mar 2026) |
| Volume | Money value of exports and imports |
| Composition | Items traded |
| Direction | Country-wise/region-wise pattern |
- Strategies: Import substitution = inward-looking; export promotion = outward-looking
- ⚠ Adam Smith = absolute advantage; Ricardo = comparative advantage

### Terms → meaning: trade theory, terms of trade, remedies, exchange rate — Terms of trade
## 1. Terms of trade
- Terms of trade: Export price index / import price index x 100 [GK]
- Revised base, Mar 2026: NTT = export price index / import price index
- GTT = import quantity / export quantity
- ITT = NTT x export quantity index
## 2. Exchange rate and trade
- Devaluation/depreciation: Makes exports cheaper abroad and imports dearer at home
- Commerce Secretary credited the weak rupee in part for the Aug 2026 export surge (16 Sep 2026)
- J-curve: After depreciation the trade balance first worsens (import bills rise at once), improves only as volumes adjust
- Marshall-Lerner condition: Devaluation improves the trade balance only if export + import demand price elasticities sum to greater than 1

### Trade terms
## Theory and strategy
- Absolute advantage — Adam Smith
- Comparative advantage — Ricardo (lower opportunity cost)
- Export promotion — outward-looking; import substitution — inward-looking
## Types of trade
| Term | Meaning |
| Intra-industry trade | Import and export of the same product (e.g. cars) |
| Inter-industry trade | Exchange of different products (comparative advantage) |
| Entrepot trade | Import, store/trade, then re-export |
| Dumping | Exporting below home-market price or cost |
## Trade remedies and controls
- Countervailing duty — balances foreign subsidies
- Anti-dumping duty — imports below home-market price
- Rules of origin — decide the national source
- Tariff — tax on imports; import quota — quantity limit; import licensing — administrative limit
## Institutions
- Department of Commerce — frames and monitors Foreign Trade Policy
- SEZ duty-free enclave — treated as foreign territory for trade and tariff purposes
### Terms of trade & exchange rate
## Devaluation
- ★ Devaluation — imports costly, exports cheaper; increase of exports, decrease of imports
- Immediate effect — exports cheaper abroad, imports dearer at home
- J-curve — trade balance first worsens (import bills rise), improves after volumes adjust
- Marshall–Lerner condition — devaluation improves trade balance if sum of export and import demand elasticities > 1
## Terms and concepts
- Net terms of trade = export price index / import price index × 100
- Gross terms of trade — import quantity / export quantity
- ★ Payment of foreign trade — related to Balance of Payment
- Closed economy — neither exports nor imports; open economy has both
- SEZs promote exports, investment, jobs, infrastructure because of simple procedures and business-friendly environment
