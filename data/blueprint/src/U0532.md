### Financial inclusion — meaning, inclusion vs exclusion
## 1. Financial inclusion
- Extends financial services to the poor population; expands banking infrastructure in underserved and rural areas
- Provides access to credit for weaker sections
- Promotes financial literacy through awareness and informed use
- Facilitates payments and remittances through the formal financial system
- Promotes access to insurance for financial security
## 2. Inclusion vs exclusion
| Financial inclusion | Financial exclusion |
| Access to formal financial services | A lack of access to formal financial services |
| Access to a bank account | No or limited banking access |
| Access to insurance | A lack of insurance access |
| Payments and remittances | Limited payment facilities |
| Financial literacy | A lack of awareness |

### NPCI — basics and milestones
## 1. Basics
- NPCI: National Payments Corporation of India — incorporated in December 2008 (set up 2008)
- A not-for-profit company — incorporated as a Section 25 company under the Companies Act, 1956
- Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013 corresponds to Section 25 of the Companies Act, 1956 (Section 8 not-for-profit company, Apr 2026)
- An initiative of the Reserve Bank of India and the Indian Banks' Association (RBI and the Indian Banks' Association (IBA))
- RBI: The central bank and regulator; IBA: a banking-industry association
- Operates under the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007 (Payment and Settlement Systems Act 2007)
- The umbrella organisation for retail payment and settlement systems — a payment-system infrastructure and operations organisation

### NPCI products → purpose (UPI, RuPay, AePS, NFS, RTGS vs NEFT)
## 1. NPCI products
| Product | Purpose |
| UPI (2016) | Instant account-to-account payments; launched April 2016 (21 banks) on the IMPS framework; real-time transfers using a VPA (e.g. name@bank); many bank accounts in one app, P2P and P2M, 24x7 |
| UPI 2.0 (2018) | Overdraft-account linking, mandates (pre-authorisation), invoice check, signed QR; limit ₹2 lakh/day |
| IMPS | Immediate interbank electronic fund transfers (24x7 instant interbank transfer) |
| RuPay | A domestic card-payment network (debit/credit); accepted in Singapore, Bhutan, UAE, Bahrain, Saudi Arabia |
| AePS | Aadhaar-enabled banking transactions — Aadhaar-enabled Payment System; banking at micro-ATMs using Aadhaar (biometric) authentication |
| NACH | High-volume recurring electronic transactions — bulk, repetitive electronic payments: salaries, dividends, bills |
| NETC | Electronic toll collection through FASTag (RFID toll payment) |
| BHIM | A UPI-based payment app (NPCI's UPI mobile payment app) |
| Bharat BillPay | An interoperable bill-payment system |
| NFS | National Financial Switch - largest interoperable ATM network (APPSC GS 2024) |
| e-RUPI | Person- and purpose-specific prepaid digital voucher; used in 13 govt schemes |
| 123Pay | UPI for feature phones, in 20 languages |
## 2. UPI and AePS
- UPI: Unified Payments Interface — developed by NPCI; an instant payment system — enables bank-to-bank payments using interoperable payment infrastructure
- AePS: Enables basic banking transactions through Aadhaar authentication; designed to support financial inclusion and interoperable banking
- Payments and inclusion: NPCI's payment infrastructure supports digital payments — digital payments provide wider access, which contributes to financial inclusion
- Particularly relevant to AePS, UPI, IMPS, RuPay and NACH
### New UPI features and RTGS vs NEFT
- New UPI features: Credit Line on UPI, Hello! UPI, UPI LITE X (offline)
| Basis | RTGS | NEFT |
| Settlement | Real-time, gross (one by one) | Deferred net, half-hourly batches |

### Terms → meaning: payments banks and digital finance architecture
## 1. Payments banks
- Deposit cap: Up to ₹1 lakh per customer (initial cap)
- Restrictions: Cannot lend or issue credit cards
- Allowed: Issue ATM/debit cards; park funds in G-secs
- Contrast: SFBs lend (50% of loans up to ₹25 lakh)
## 2. Digital public infrastructure for finance
| Term | Meaning |
| Account Aggregator (AA) | Consent-based sharing of financial data (bank transactions, GST records); speeds up credit appraisal for first-time borrowers; AAs = RBI-regulated NBFC-AAs |
| ONDC | Open network to democratise e-commerce and break platform monopolies; a DPIIT initiative |
| India Stack | ONDC is part of it with Aadhaar, UPI, DigiLocker, Account Aggregator |
| OCEN (Open Credit Enablement Network) | Common protocols linking lenders + marketplaces for small-ticket credit |
- Digital wallets: India = world's largest digital wallet market (2025; 440 million+ downloads)
### Crypto vs CBDC vs UPI — features and FIRSTs
## 1. Crypto vs Digital Rupee vs UPI
| Basis | Cryptocurrency | Digital Rupee (e₹, CBDC e-rupee) | UPI |
| Nature | Private, decentralised, cryptography-secured digital asset, no central issuer (Bitcoin 2009); not legal tender | Legal tender issued by RBI in digital form | A payment system (interface) that moves bank-account money, not a currency |
| Issuer liability | None | RBI | Bank deposits |
| Tax / rules | 30% flat tax on gains, 1% TDS (since 2022), no loss set-off | RBI Act amended (Finance Act 2022) to cover digital currency | NPCI under PSS Act 2007 |
- Court: SC (2020) struck down RBI's 2018 banking ban
- Legislation: 2021 bill to ban private crypto still pending
## 2. CBDC e-rupee - issuer, pilots, vs UPI and crypto
- e-rupee pilots:
- Wholesale (e₹-W) from 1 Nov 2022
- Retail (e₹-R) from 1 Dec 2022
- Benefits: Programmable, targeted subsidy delivery, cheaper remittances, traceability for tax compliance (Sep 2025)
- Other CBDCs:
- Bahamas Sand Dollar (world's first live retail CBDC, 2020)

- Payments banks cannot lend → cannot issue credit cards; may take deposits up to initial ₹1 lakh, issue debit cards, hold G-secs
- Crypto gains taxed at 30%, with 1% TDS
### Financial inclusion — meaning, inclusion vs exclusion
- ★ PMMY (8 April 2015) — brings small entrepreneurs into the formal financial system; loans up to Rs 10 lakh to non-corporate, non-farm small/micro enterprises
- ★ Shrinking of banking infrastructure — not an aim; aims include taking services to the poor and into rural areas
- ★ Swabhimaan (10 February 2011) — take banking to the doorsteps of the rural poor
- ★ L.B.S. — Lead Banking Scheme, a financial inclusion measure
### Committees → chair, subject (financial inclusion and digital payments)
- ★ Committee on Financial Inclusion — chaired by C. Rangarajan; formed 26 June 2006; final report 4 January 2008
- ★ Payments Banks recommended by the Nachiket Mor Committee (formed 23 September 2013)
### PMJDY — launch, objective and features
- ★ PMJDY — launched 28 August 2014 for promoting financial inclusion; a bank account for each family
### MUDRA — ministry, categories, limits, refinance
- ★ MUDRA (Micro Units Development and Refinance Agency) — funds micro and small enterprises; collateral-free credit to non-corporate, non-farm businesses
- ★ PMMY collateral-free loans up to ₹10 lakh
- ★ Kishore — more than Rs 50,000 up to Rs 5,00,000
### SHG-bank linkage — pioneers, NRLM, AP SERP/DWCRA
- ★ SHG-Bank linkage pilot launched by NABARD
### NPCI — basics and milestones
- ★ NPCI links all ATMs in India — took over the National Financial Switch (NFS) in 2009 from IDRBT
- ★ NFS designed and launched by RBI's IDRBT in 2004
### NPCI products → purpose (UPI, RuPay, AePS, NFS, RTGS vs NEFT)
- ★ RuPay card — by NPCI; domestic card network cutting dependence on Visa and Mastercard
- ★ UPI — Unified Payments Interface; by NPCI, 2016; P2P and P2M inter-bank transfers
- ★ Result of UPI — mobile wallets not necessary for online payments (direct bank-to-bank transfer)
- ★ Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) — fee a bank charges a merchant for accepting card payments; shared by issuing bank, PoS bank and payment network
- ★ Mudra Yojana — easy financial assistance to set up small business
- ★ Facebook's cryptocurrency (2019) — Libra (later Diem)
### Policy and Acts — digital payments ministry, incentive scheme, zero MDR
- ★ RTGS — settlement almost instantaneous; NEFT — in batches, may take time (around two hours)
- ★ No charge on the beneficiary for inward RTGS credit
### Indices — RBI-DPI and FI-Index
- ★ RBI FI-Index sub-indices — Access (35%), Usage (45%), Quality (20%)
### Data — PMJDY coverage and UPI progress
- ★ Microfinance services: credit, savings, insurance, cash transfers, leasing — for low-income households
- ★ Launch order: PMJDY (28 Aug 2014) → Digital Gender Atlas (9 Mar 2015) → Mudra (8 Apr 2015) → PMSBY (9 May 2015)
- ★ Bitcoin — created January 2009 by Satoshi Nakamoto; not issued or tracked by banks or governments; peer-to-peer — anyone with a Bitcoin address can send and receive
- ★ PMMY — under the Ministry of Finance (DFS)
### Places — UPI abroad, country → year
- ★ UPI merchant payments accepted in UAE, France, Singapore (not Germany, Bangladesh); facility via NIPL
- ★ UPI live in Bhutan, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Mauritius, Qatar as well (as of May 2026)
### Terms → meaning: payments banks and digital finance architecture
- ★ Payments banks — allowed promoters include NBFCs, individuals, corporations, mobile phone companies, supermarket chains, real estate cooperatives, public sector entities; no credit risk allowed
- ★ ONDC — open, interoperable digital commerce network across platforms (buyers, sellers, logistics, payments)
- OCEN (Open Credit Enablement Network) — common protocol linking lenders and marketplaces for small-ticket credit
### Crypto vs CBDC vs UPI — features and FIRSTs
- ★ UPI money is commercial bank money — a liability of your bank; the Digital Rupee liability does not lie with users and their banks
