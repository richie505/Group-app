### Garimella Satyanarayana → life, song, imprisonment
- Life: 14 July 1893 to 18 Dec 1952; born at Gonedapadu, Narasannapeta taluk (Srikakulam); left studies at Gandhi's call
- The song: "Maakoddee Tella Doratanamu" - Non-Cooperation era. Grew to 162 lines
- Cyclostyled a thousand times; praised by Gandhi
- Imprisonment:
- Jailed in 1922 for one year for the song (some notes say 6 months)
- Later 2.5 years RI; wife, father and grandfather died while he was in jail
- Other works: Harijana Paatalu, anti-liquor songs, Samajika Navala, Palletullalo
- Legacy: First people's poet of the freedom struggle; inspired Nazar, Subbarao Panigrahi and Gaddar
### Rayaprolu Subbarao and Bhavakavitvam → features and poets
- Title: Father of Telugu Romanticism; founder of Bhavakavitvam - pioneered Bhava Kavitvam (drew on Shelley and Keats)
- Contribution: Broke with translating Sanskrit epics → original, emotive Telugu verse; freed Telugu verse from rigid prosody
- Patriotic lyric: "Ye desamegina ... pogadara nee talli bhoomi Bharatini" (*Janmabhoomi*) (poem title [GK])
## 1. Terms → meaning
| Term | Meaning |
| Bhava Kavitvam | Romantic and personal emotion, nature, love, freedom from classical prosody + patriotic strands |
## 2. Main poets
- Basavaraju Apparao

- Devulapalli Krishna Sastri
- Venkata Parvateeswara Kavulu (Ekanta Seva, the "Telugu Gitanjali")
- Criticism: Progressive writers such as Sri Sri called his romanticism "escapist"

## Rayaprolu Subba Rao
- Janmabhoomi ("Ye desamegina ... pogadara nee talli bhoomi Bharatini") — Rayaprolu Subbarao

- First people's poet of the freedom struggle
- Song copied a thousand times; praised by Mahatma Gandhi
- Jailed 1922 for Maakoddee Tella Doratanamu; later served 2.5 years RI
