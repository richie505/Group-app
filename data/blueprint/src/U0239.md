### Salt marches outside Andhra → region and leader
## 1. Salt
- The Salt Tax was a major target of Gandhian protest — associated with the Civil Disobedience Movement
- Gandhi broke the Salt Law through salt-making at Dandi
- C. Rajagopalachari: Led the Vedaranyam Satyagraha — collected salt from the sea
## 2. Marches outside Andhra
- Dharasana: Sarojini Naidu led the non-violent march on the Dharasana Salt Works; volunteers took unarmed beatings
- Foreign press: Webb Miller (United Press) reported it
