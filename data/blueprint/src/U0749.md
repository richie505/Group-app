### Electricity Act 2003 and regulators → year and role
- Electricity Act 2003: Notified 10 June 2003
- Consolidates laws on generation, transmission, distribution, trading
- Set up CEA, Regulatory Commissions, APTEL (Appellate Tribunal)
- Reforms: Delicensed generation; SEBs to be unbundled; open access + SERCs
| Body | Key fact |
| CEA, APTEL, CERC | statutory bodies under Ministry of Power (IYB 2026) |
| CERC | central tariffs and inter-State matters; regulates power exchanges such as IEX (CDCA, Aug 2026); set up 1998 [GK] |
| SERCs | members appointed by State Govt; fix retail tariffs for all consumer classes, license, enforce standards, settle disputes |
| APTEL | est. 24 Aug 2004; hears appeals against CERC/SERC orders; Chairperson usually a retired SC judge |
- APERC: Set up under AP Electricity Reform Act 1998; after bifurcation served both States for up to six months (AP Reorganisation Act)
- MNRE demand: Asked parliamentary committee to be treated as "Central Government" for renewables under the Act (21 Mar 2026)
- ⚠ CERC = central tariffs, power exchanges; SERCs = retail tariffs; APTEL = appeals

### DISCOM finances → causes, terms and data — Why SEBs went sick
## 1. Why SEBs went sick
- SEB financial sickness (BPSC 2011 key, all four correct):
- Sale of power to agricultural and domestic consumers below cost
- High T&D losses
- Poor cost recovery
- ⚠ Trap (APPSC-G2 2017 key): States' autonomy over power generation and distribution is not a problem
- Real problems = cost recovery in distribution, rising commercial losses of SEBs, delays in commissioning projects
## 2. Subsidies and procurement
- Subsidy burden: States spend ~₹2.4 lakh crore/yr subsidising farm + domestic power (12 Apr 2026)
- Subsidy reform: Since FY 2021-22 States pay full subsidy + clear legacy subsidy dues
- PPA shortfalls: DISCOMs with fixed long-term PPAs buy shortfalls on exchanges at volatile peak prices (21 May 2026)
