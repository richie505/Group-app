### Per capita income — terms, formula and limits
| Term | Meaning |
| PCI | Net State Domestic Product (NSDP) ÷ population (net measure, after depreciation) |
| Per capita GSDP | Gross measure; current or constant prices |
| Current prices | Include inflation |
| Constant (2011-12) prices | Show real growth |
| District Domestic Product (DDP) | District GDP, compiled by State Directorate of Economics and Statistics (DES); DES = also nodal agency for GSDP |
- ⚠ Trap: "GSDP ÷ population" is loose; the Survey's PCI is NSDP-based
- ⚠ Trap: never compare AP's per capita GSDP with India's per capita income - match gross with gross, net with net (per capita income = NSDP/NNI; per capita GSDP = gross)
- PCI rises only when: national income grows faster than population
- Slow growth causes: population growth, low productivity, low-income/low-saving "vicious circle"
- Limits: average hides inequality; ignores cost-of-living differences

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 3:110
### PCI terms
- Per capita income is based on Net State Domestic Product (net of depreciation), at current or constant prices
- Per capita GSDP (gross) and PCI (net NSDP) are not interchangeable
### AP vs India PCI (current, ₹)
- 2025-26 AP PCI growth — 10.43% vs India 6.9%; AP PCI above national average throughout
- Fastest PCI growth — 15.26% in 2021-22; slowest 4.10% in 2019-20

- Rayalaseema — 43% of AP's horticulture area, 52% of its output
- District Domestic Product — compiled by the State Directorate of Economics and Statistics (not NSO)
### Key PCI facts
- PCI = income / population — rises only if income grows faster than population
- PCI is an average — hides inequality and cost-of-living differences
- India's PCI fastest growth — 2021-22, 18.6% (post-COVID rebound)
- Swarna Andhra PCI target 2047 — above $42,000, about ₹55 lakh; ₹5.43 lakh is the 2028-29 per capita GSDP target
- AP PCI above India's every year since 2014-15; 2025-26 AP PCI about 1.34 times the national figure
- P4 scheme — targets the gap between high average PCI and lasting poverty and inequality
### Historical baselines
- National Income Committee (1951) — India's PCI for 1948-49 at ₹225
- AP's PCI — ₹7,781 in 1960 to about ₹2.07 lakh in 2021-22
