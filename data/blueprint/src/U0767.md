### Terms → meaning (EIA and related tools)
| Tool / term | Scope / meaning |
| EIA | Impacts of a specific project before it starts (APPSC 2025 answer; PYQ) |
| LCA (Life-Cycle Assessment) | Impacts of a product/process across its life - whole life cycle, cradle to grave (raw material to disposal) (APPSC 2025 answer; PYQ) |
| SEA (Strategic Environmental Assessment) | Impacts of policies, plans and programmes - upstream of projects |
| Cumulative Impact Assessment | Combined effect of several projects in one area/basin |
| Environmental audit | Compliance check of an operating unit, after the fact |
| Cleaner production | Preventive strategy - cut waste and pollution at source (process change, input substitution) vs end-of-pipe treatment |
| Green chemistry | Design products/processes that reduce or eliminate hazardous substances |
| PARIVESH | Pro-Active and Responsive facilitation by Interactive, Virtuous and Environmental Single-window Hub |

- APPCB's mandate includes encouraging cleaner production technologies
- Link to assessment: Both shrink impacts at design stage → EIA/LCA find fewer residual impacts to mitigate
- EIA requires assessment of alternatives in location and process technology (p.167)

### EIA process — stages
## 1. What EIA does
- EIA — Environmental Impact Assessment: Assesses the likely environmental consequences of a proposed project or activity before implementation
- Identifies, predicts and evaluates the environmental impacts of development projects
- Linked with environmental clearance and project and development impacts
- ⚠ R2 Code of Practices is not EIA — it is for environmentally responsible electronics recycling
## 2. Stages
| Stage | What happens |
| Screening | Does the project need EIA; which category (CDI) |
| Scoping | Key issues and Terms of Reference (CDI) |
| Baseline data | Existing environment surveyed (APP) |
| Impact prediction, alternatives, EMP | Alternatives compared; mitigation plan + Environmental Management Plan; risk assessment (UPSC notes) |
| Public consultation | Public hearing + written responses (CDI) |
| Appraisal | Expert committee evaluates the EIA report (CDI) |
| Decision | EC granted with conditions or rejected (CDI) |
| Monitoring | EMP implementation monitored (UPSC notes) |
- Stages under EIA 2006: Screening → Scoping → Public consultation (hearing) → Appraisal → Decision
### EIA Notifications 1994, 2006 and Draft 2020 — provisions
## 1. EIA Notification 1994
- Notified: 1994 (27 Jan 1994, under EPA)
- Scope:
- Schedule I projects need MoEF clearance
- Two-stage (site + final) clearance for pithead thermal and valley projects
- Public hearing: Mandatory - added by the 1997 amendment
## 2. EIA Notification 2006
- Notified: 2006 (14 Sep 2006 / 14 September 2006) under EPA 1986; replaced the EIA Notification 1994
- Shift: Focus from investment to impact potential (investment size → impact potential)
- EC compulsory: 39 project categories
| Category | Appraisal | Clearance by | Note |
| A | Expert Appraisal Committee (EAC) | MoEFCC | No screening; always full EIA |
| B1 | State Expert Appraisal Committee (SEAC) | SEIAA | EIA required (needs EIA) |
| B2 | State Expert Appraisal Committee (SEAC) | SEIAA | No EIA |
- ⚠ A → EAC → MoEFCC; B1/B2 → SEAC → SEIAA; only B2 has no EIA
## 3. Draft EIA Notification 2020 - contested provisions
- Status: Wide protest; widely debated, not been finalised (Aug 2026)
- Post-facto clearance: Proposed retrospective EC on payment of penalties - via appraisal, penalties, compensation, remediation plans
- Shorter public consultation period: Public-hearing notice cut 30 to 20 days
- Other contested provisions:
- More projects moved to B2/exempt
- Compliance reports yearly instead of half-yearly
- Violations reportable only by the violator or government
### Public hearing — notice, conduct, exemptions
- Nature: Mandatory two-fold public consultation for scheduled projects (Aug 2026)
- Conducted by: SPCB, District Collector presiding (22 May 2026)
- Timeline (EIA 2006): Notice at least 30 days; hearing completed within 45 days
- Exempt:
- Defence, national-security and strategic projects (details kept confidential); defence and strategic projects exempt from public consultation (Aug 2026)
- B2 projects + units inside notified industrial estates
- Executive exemptions: EIA framework exempts security/strategic projects from public consultation "as determined by the Central Government" - criticised as arbitrary (2 Jan 2026)
- ⚠ Notice 30 days (EIA 2006) vs 20 days (Draft 2020); hearing within 45 days
### Clearance bodies — EAC, SEIAA, SCEIA/SAEIA, PARIVESH
## 1. PARIVESH portal
- Launched 2018 for online environment, forest, wildlife and CRZ clearances (single window)
- Current role: platform for 120-day escalation to SCEIA (23 Jul 2026)
## 2. Jul 2026 amendment
- Removal power: Centre may remove EAC/SEIAA/SEAC members mid-term for persistent delays
- Escalation: If SEAC misses 120 days → proposal escalates (auto-transfer via PARIVESH) to new SCEIA
- Backup bodies (2026): SAEIA stands in for SEIAA; SCEIA for SEAC - step in when a State's SEIAA or SEAC lapses
- Tenure 6 + 6 months (max 1 year)
- Category B no longer defaults to central EAC when a state committee lapses
- MoEFCC had proposed SCEIA and SAEIA (Standing Authority on EIA) earlier (Mar 2026)
- Member tenure (SEIAA, EAC, SEAC): 3 to 4 years; max two terms; age limit 70 (23 Jul 2026)
- Concern: Bureaucrats replace scientists; speed over science
- ⚠ SAEIA ↔ SEIAA; SCEIA ↔ SEAC

### Cases on post-facto clearance → holding — Final Vanashakti verdict, 29 July 2026
## 1. Final Vanashakti verdict, 29 July 2026
- Bench: Led by CJI Surya Kant (LENSD 31 Jul 2026; TH 21 Aug 2026)
- Holding:
- Nullified/quashed the 2021 Office Memorandum (retrospective EC on paying penalties)
- Ruling prospective - earlier ECs under the 2017 Notification and 2021 OM survive (31 Jul 2026)
- Ex post facto EC must meet Arts 14 and 21 (test of proportionality); SC keeps power under Article 142
- Fresh violators cannot seek regularisation under the 2017 Notification or 2021 SOP (21 Aug 2026)
- ⚠ Vanashakti: May 2025 struck down → Nov 2025 recalled (2:1) → 29 July 2026 final, prospective
- Exam angle: "primary issue in case X" - match case to the exemption or post-facto question it decided.
- Exam angle: prior EC is mandatory under EIA 2006; post-facto approval only as a narrow exception.
