### Gaganyaan — definitions, modules and FIRSTs
## 1. Programme

## 2. Spacecraft modules
| Part | Role (TH, 15 Jul 2026) |

### Weightlessness and microgravity effects on the human body
## 1. Weightlessness
- Apparent weight is zero inside a freely falling elevator and an orbiting satellite — in free fall the normal force becomes zero; apparent weight N is zero when effective gravity is zero
- An orbiting satellite and its astronaut both accelerate towards Earth with the same g
- Astronauts in space stations experience continuous free fall
- Zero-g training: Aircraft parabolic flights
- Microgravity experiments: Fluid behaviour, crystal growth, human physiology; satellite instruments are designed for a weightless environment
## 2. Microgravity effects on the human body
| Condition | What happens [GK] |
| Bone loss (spaceflight osteopenia) | 1-1.5% bone mass lost per month without load |
| Muscle atrophy | Postural muscles weaken - Ax-4 tested muscle regeneration |
| Fluid shift | "Puffy face, bird legs" - fluids move to the head |
| Space Adaptation Syndrome | Space motion sickness in first days |
| SANS (Spaceflight-Associated Neuro-ocular Syndrome) | Optic disc swelling, vision changes |
- Research: Space medicine studied through ISS experiments + ground analogues (Leh, HOPE)

### Weightlessness and microgravity effects on the human body
- ★ Weightlessness — zero gravity; no supporting force on the body (free fall)
- ★ Astronauts cannot stand erect — no gravity (microgravity); muscles and bones weaken
- ★ If Earth's gravity disappeared — weight becomes zero, mass stays the same

- ★ Apple released from an orbiting spaceship — moves along with it at the same speed
- Apparent weightlessness in orbit — satellite and astronaut fall with the same g; no normal force
