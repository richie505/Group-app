### Pattiseema and Telugu Ganga → purpose and beneficiaries
## 1. Pattiseema
- Type: Lift irrigation scheme (APPSC-GS 2018)
- Function: Lifts Godavari water into the Polavaram Right Main Canal → Krishna at Prakasam Barrage
- 2026 data: Diverted 17.52 tmc ft this season by 10 Aug 2026 (TH, 10 Aug 2026), while ~521 tmc ft of Godavari water flowed to the sea
## 2. Telugu Ganga project
- Source → beneficiary: Krishna surplus water from Srisailam → drinking water to Chennai (Tamil Nadu)
- Route: Srisailam → Penna basin (Somasila) → Kandaleru → Poondi (TN)
- Irrigation: Also irrigates Rayalaseema and Nellore; 2.75 lakh acres in Kurnool and Kadapa (2026)
- Conceived by: N.T. Rama Rao; inaugurated in the 7th Plan (1985-90)
- Water shares: Maharashtra, Karnataka and AP each gave up 5 tmc = 15 tmc for Chennai
- 2026: 10 tmc ft to be released for Chennai's drinking water
- Industry: Bulk water to the Naidupeta industrial cluster (2025-26)
