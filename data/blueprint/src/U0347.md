### Reservation and backward classes — FIRSTs
## 1. Reservation and backward classes
- Kolhapur State — under Shahu Maharaj — the first State to implement reservation: the first reservation policy in India (1902); Shahu Maharaj was the first ruler to introduce reservation
- Articles 15(4) and 16(4): enabling provisions for reservation (16(4) in the original Constitution; 15(4) added by the 1st Amendment, 1951)
- 1st Amendment Act of 1951: The first Constitutional Amendment — enacted 1951 — related to reservations; the first Constitutional recognition of OBC reservation
- Kaka Kalelkar Commission: The first Backward Class Commission — 1953; Kaka Kalelkar — its first Chairman
- Mandal Commission: The first Commission to recommend 27% reservation — B. P. Mandal, the first Mandal Commission Chairman

### Commissions — composition
- NCSC / NCST (each): Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson and 3 members
- Appointed by the President by warrant under hand and seal
- Service conditions and tenure fixed by the President; term 3 years under the rules
- NCBC: Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson and three members (2026)
- 102nd Amendment: Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson + 3 members appointed by the President; civil court powers
- 1993 statutory NCBC: 5 members, headed by a retired SC/HC judge
- ⚠ Statutory NCBC (1993) = 5 members; constitutional NCBC (338B) = Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson + 3
### Functions and powers — NCSC, NCST, NCBC — Functions of NCSC / NCST
## 1. Functions of NCSC / NCST
- Core functions:
- Investigate and monitor safeguards
- Inquire into specific complaints of deprivation of rights
- Advise on socio-economic planning (planning of socio-economic development) + evaluate progress; recommend measures
- Reports to the President: Annually (or when it deems fit)
- ⚠ Trap: Not the Prime Minister (APPSC 2022)
- President lays them before Parliament; state-related reports go to the Governor
- Mandatory consultation: Union and every State Government must consult the Commission on all major policy matters
- SCs = Art. 338(9); STs = Art. 338A(9)
- NCBC: Same pattern - safeguards, complaints, advise on development of socially and educationally backward classes, annual report to the President
- NCST extra work: Minor forest produce ownership, mineral and water rights, rehabilitation of displaced tribals (CDX p.69)
