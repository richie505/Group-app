### Arya Samaj, 1875 → founding, base, programme
- Founder: Swami Dayananda Saraswati
- Founding: Bombay in 1875 (April 1875)
- Name: "Society of Nobles"
- Base: Strongest in Punjab; HQ later shifted to Lahore; also strong in Rajasthan + United Provinces
- Ten Principles: Drafted by Dayananda (truth, rational thought, moral action, well-being of all)
- Programme:
- Rejected idol worship, polytheism, ritualism, caste by birth
- Opposed untouchability, child marriage, purdah
- Supported widow remarriage, inter-caste marriage, women's education
- Legacy: Samaj = "nursery of militant nationalists" - Lala Lajpat Rai, Swami Shraddhanand, Bhagat Singh's family
- Exam angle: statement traps - "founded in Calcutta" (wrong; Bombay) and "promotes idol worship" (wrong; rejects it).
### Arya Samaj movements → Shuddhi and the Hyderabad satyagraha
## 1. Shuddhi movement
- Started by: Arya Samaj
- Aim: Ritual "purification" to reconvert Hindus who had converted to Islam or Christianity + admit outsiders into the Hindu fold
- Later leader: Swami Shraddhanand
- Led the 1920s Shuddhi campaign through the Bharatiya Hindu Shuddhi Sabha (1923)
- Impact: Contributed to the communalisation of social life → later communal political consciousness
- Exam angle: "Shuddhi was started by ___" → Arya Samaj (not Brahmo Samaj, Satyashodhak Samaj or Ramakrishna Mission).
## 2. Arya Samaj in Hyderabad State - satyagraha of 1938-39
- Satyagraha: 1938-39, satyagraha in Hyderabad State against the Nizam's curbs on religious and civil rights
- Ran alongside the Hyderabad State Congress satyagraha
- Impact: Helped build political awakening against Nizam rule in the Telangana region
### Universal angles
- Current relevance: International Aryan Summit 2025 (Jnana Jyoti Festival / International Arya Mahasammelan), New Delhi
- Marked 200th birth anniversary of Dayananda + 150 years of the Arya Samaj (Nov 2025)
