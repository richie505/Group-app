### Haryanka rulers and persons — queens, succession, physician
## 1. Haryanka dynasty
- Bimbisara:
- Founded the Haryanka dynasty — the real (actual) founder of the (first) Magadhan Empire
- The first great ruler according to Buddhist texts
- A major role in the expansion of Magadha — conquered Anga; marriage alliances as an important diplomatic strategy
- Died in 492 BCE
- Ajatashatru — Ajathasatru, also known as Kunika:
- The son and successor of Bimbisara — the next major ruler; killed Bimbisara
- Ruled approximately 492 to 460 BCE
- Founded Pataliputra; defeated and annexed Vajji; patronized the First Buddhist Council
- Udayin: The son of Ajatashatru — succeeded him; the shift of the capital from Rajgriha to Pataliputra is attributed to him
## 2. Queens and succession
| Queen | Kingdom |
| Kosaladevi (Mahakoshala) | Kosala - Kashi as dowry |
| Chellana | Lichchhavi (Vaishali); mother of Ajatashatru |
- Gains: Kashi revenue + security on western and northern flanks
- Patricide chain (Buddhist texts): Bimbisara → Ajatashatru → Udayin
- Udayin probably killed at the instigation of Palaka of Avanti
- People then chose Shishunaga
- Royal physician Jivaka: Sent by Bimbisara to treat Pradyota of Avanti (jaundice)
- ⚠ Chellana (Lichchhavi) = mother of Ajatashatru; Kosaladevi brought Kashi

### Causes of supremacy
- Elephants from the east strengthened Magadha's military power
- Nearby iron ore and Ganga-Son river transport also aided its rise
- Geography: alluvial soil, ample rainfall and navigable rivers (agriculture, transport, trade)
- Society relatively unorthodox, with little Vedic hold
- Pataliputra at the confluence of the Ganga and Son (Gandak nearby): a water-fort, central in the Ganga plain
- Safe capitals also helped the rise
