### Epidemiological transition (Omran)
- 1. Age of Pestilence and Famine ↓
- 2. Age of Receding Pandemics (sanitation, vaccination) ↓
- 3. Age of Degenerative and Man-Made Diseases: NCDs dominate
### Key data
- NCDs = 57% of deaths (ES 2025-26); ⚠ other notes: 61.8% in 2016
- Leading cause: cardiovascular diseases
- Since 1990: MMR down 86%, U5MR down 78%
- Cancer: 1.41 million (2022) → 2.46 million (2045); notifiable in 17 states
- Overweight: 24% women, 23% men (NFHS-5)

### Disease burden — terms → meaning and transition stages

### Disease burden data — NCD share, leading causes, life expectancy, cancer
- NCD share of deaths: NCDs cause 57% of all deaths (ES 2025-26)
- India: Cardiovascular diseases = leading cause (ES 2025-26)
- Most countries cut NCD deaths 2001-2019, but India's probability of dying from an NCD rose - esp. women over 40, men over 55 (Lancet)
- World: Ischaemic heart disease = single biggest killer (WHO estimates)
- Data source: ICMR-PHFI-IHME India State-Level Disease Burden Initiative (part of Global Burden of Disease study)
- Life expectancy: 49.7 (1973) to 70.3 years (2023) (ES 2025-26)
- Since 1990: MMR has fallen 86%; U5MR 78% (ES 2025-26)
- Cancer (4 Jul 2026):
- Not notifiable nationally; 17 states made it notifiable (Telangana most recent)

- Main risk factors: Unhealthy diet, physical inactivity, tobacco, alcohol, urbanisation, ageing (UPSC)

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 5:115
### Burden terms
- DALY = YLL (years of life lost) + YLD (years lived with disability); does not add years gained through treatment
- India State-Level Disease Burden Initiative — ICMR, PHFI and IHME; part of the Global Burden of Disease study
- Dual burden of disease — lasting communicable diseases (TB, malaria, dengue) existing alongside rising NCDs
- undernutrition with obesity = dual burden of malnutrition; adding injuries = triple burden
### Epidemiological transition (Omran)
- Omran's stages: Age of Pestilence and Famine (first) → Receding Pandemics → Degenerative and Man-Made Diseases
### Key data
## Mortality and transition
- Cardiovascular disease — leading cause of death in India
- Ischaemic heart disease — world's single biggest killer (WHO)
- NCDs — 57% of all deaths in India
- Under-five mortality — fallen by 78% since 1990
## Vectors
- Japanese encephalitis — Culex mosquito
- Dengue, chikungunya, Zika — Aedes
## NCD risks and cancer
- FSSAI limits trans fat in fats and oils to 2% (trans fats common in bakery products made with hydrogenated fats)
- Main NCD risk factors — unhealthy diet, physical inactivity, tobacco, alcohol (also urbanisation, ageing)
- Cancer not nationally notifiable; 17 States have notified it, Telangana the most recent
### Communicable vs lifestyle
- ★ Coronary artery disease — result of plaque buildup in the arteries
- Malaria — communicable (Plasmodium, spread by mosquitoes), not a lifestyle disease
- Lifestyle NCDs — diabetes, hypertension, obesity
### Programmes & targets
## Fats
- ★ Trans fat is harmful because it lowers HDL ("good cholesterol"); high HDL reduces heart-disease risk
- FSSAI limits trans fat in fats and oils to 2%
- Excess saturated fat — raises blood lipids and cholesterol
## Programmes and targets
