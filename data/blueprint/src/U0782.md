### Greenhouse effect → mechanism, natural vs enhanced
## 1. Greenhouse effect
- Natural warming caused by the absorption and re-radiation of heat by greenhouse gases
- Joseph Fourier: Proposed the concept (the greenhouse effect concept) in the 1820s
- Without it, Earth's average temperature would be about −18°C
- Earth's mean surface temperature about -18°C (-18 C) instead of about +15°C (~15 C)
## 2. Mechanism
- Incoming: Atmosphere transparent to incoming solar (short-wave solar radiation)
- Outgoing: Opaque to outgoing terrestrial (long-wave infrared) radiation
- Re-emission: Surface re-emits infrared at 8,000-25,000 nm
- CO2 + water vapour absorb strongly at 14,000-25,000 nm
- GHGs re-radiate in all directions, partly back to the surface
- Global warming (APPSC GS 2021): Heat from sunlight re-radiated by the earth absorbed by CO2 and other gases
## 3. Natural vs enhanced effect
| Natural greenhouse effect | Enhanced (anthropogenic) greenhouse effect |
| Occurs without human intervention (no human role); keeps Earth habitable - without it Earth would be frozen | Caused by human activities that raise GHG concentrations |
| GHG levels stable for thousands of years (millennia) | Fossil fuels, deforestation, industry, agriculture, waste |
| Essential for life | Leads to global warming and climate change |
- APPSC-GS 2025:
- Greenhouse effect = rise in Earth's temperature due to concentration of gases
- Primary cause of global warming = increase in GHGs (APPSC 2025 key)
- ⚠ Transparent to short-wave in; opaque to long-wave infrared out

### Climate science terms → meaning (forcing, sensitivity, feedbacks, tipping, records)
| Term | Meaning |

| Climate sensitivity | Warming after a doubling of CO2 over pre-industrial levels |
