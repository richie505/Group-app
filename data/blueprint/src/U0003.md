### Rock art sites → features
## 1. Bhimbetka
- Location: Near Bhopal, MP; UNESCO site (2003)
- Habitation: Continuous from Lower Palaeolithic to medieval times
- Upper Palaeolithic art: About 300 caves and shelters
- Mesolithic paintings: Hunting; people carrying baskets
- After contact with Chalcolithic farmers of Malwa → style became static (12 May 2026)
## 2. AP rock art and petroglyphs
- AP sites: Borra Caves (Visakhapatnam), Kethavaram (Kurnool)
- Common animal painted: deer
- Petroglyphs: Carved, not painted; in India from the Upper Palaeolithic-Mesolithic

### Chalcolithic cultures → date, pottery and type site
## 1. Sites and cultures

## 2. Daimabad
- Maharashtra — on the Pravara River; Chalcolithic, with a later Harappan-associated cultural horizon
- Daimabad: Fortified settlement + copper objects; also southernmost Harappan site
## 3. Relation to the Harappans
- Harappan contemporaries: Ahar and Kayatha
- Post-Harappan Chalcolithic cultures (Ahar, Malwa, Jorwe): Chalcolithic cultures came before and after the Harappan; many post-Harappan, some overlapped
### Megaliths → types, terms and AP districts
## 1. Nature of megaliths
- Definition: Graves with weapons, tools, pottery, surrounded by big stones (PYQ 2018)
- Age: Iron Age, with black-and-red ware; c. 1500/1000 BCE to 300 CE
- AP names: "Rakshasa Gullu" (also Pandava Gullu, Kurambara Gullu, Morivara Gullu)
- Social ranks: Burial goods show ranks - dolmens for elites, pits for commoners
## 2. Types → form and AP distribution
| Type | Form | AP distribution |
| Cist | Stone-slab box burial (chamber) | Common in AP; not numerous in Visakhapatnam (Visakhapatnam claim keyed wrong - see below) |
| Dolmen | Stone table-like chamber | Krishna, Chittoor, East Godavari |
| Sarcophagus | Terracotta coffin, often with legs | More in Chittoor and Anantapur; one found near Nallavaram |
| Menhir | Upright memorial stone, 2-6 m | Krishna district |
| Anthropomorphic | Human-shaped stone | Tirupati region: largest collection |
- AP megalithic sites: Nagarjunakonda (centre), Brahmagiri, Gudivada, Jonnawada (Nellore), Boodigapalli, Peddaveedu
- Types: About 12 types in all; others - cairn circle, urn burial
## 3. PYQ key
- APPSC G2 2025 key: The incorrect statement = "many cist burials in Visakhapatnam"
- Black-and-red ware occurs at all Andhra sites except Visakhapatnam
