### Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya → life, titles, Ramadandu
## 1. Life and education
- Leader: Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya (APPSC 2025 key)
- Born: 2 June at Penuganchiprolu (Krishna district); 1884-1929
- Education: M.A. (Hons) Economics, University of Edinburgh, 1916; Secretary, Edinburgh Indian Association
- Influence: Ananda K. Coomaraswamy introduced him to Gandhi in London (1915) as "the coming man of your country"
- Career: Resigned as professor at Rajahmundry Government College → founded Andhra Vidyapeetha Goshthi near Chirala (national education)
- Earlier activity: Vandemataram movement + 2nd Andhra Students Conference (1909)
## 2. Roles and titles

- ⚠ Trap: Ramadandu, not Shanti Sena
- Titles: "Andhra Ratna"; also called "Ramadasu"
- After NCM: Joined the Swaraj Party (C.R. Das, Motilal Nehru) as one of its Andhra secretaries
### Chirala-Perala Satyagraha 1921 → cause, exodus, arrest, outcome
## 1. Cause
- Chirala and Perala (Bapatla taluk; weavers and small farmers, ~15,000 people) made a municipality - proposed Nov 1919, formed Jan 1920 (some notes: municipality in 1919)
- Tax rose from Rs 4,000 to Rs 40,000 a year
## 2. Exodus
- On Gandhi's advice; at midnight on 25 April 1921, 13,572 of 15,326 residents (~13,000) left
- New camp "Ramnagar" (Ramanagaram) outside municipal limits
- Parallel all-caste assembly + arbitration court; lasted 11 months
## 3. Arrest
- Speech at the Andhra Mahasabha, Berhampur (Sept 1921) → banned from speaking for 2 months → broke the ban
- Arrested 1 October 1921; tried by Rutherford at Srikakulam; jailed 1 year (some notes say the arrest was at Trichinopoly)
## 5. Outcome
- Municipality abolished in 1938 by Bezawada Gopala Reddy (Local Self-Govt Minister, Rajaji ministry)

## 1. Pedanandipadu no-tax campaign - detail
- Place & date: Pedanandipadu firka, Guntur district; Jan-Feb 1922
- Not in your sources: the taluka of Pedanandipadu firka.
- Unique features:
- (1) Mass resignation of village officers - 18 of 19 in the firka (29 in Guntur district), led by Chandavolu karanam Machiraju Ramamurthy

- Withdrawal: 11 Feb 1922 - Guntur DCC got Gandhi's message to Konda Venkatappayya to suspend → suspended; ended with the NCM

### Rampa Rebellion 1922-24
## Earlier rebellions (1879-80)
- Rekapalle rebellion — caused by an increase in the axe tax during a famine
- Rampa rebellion 1879-80 — came from Madhava Bhupati's exploitation
- Sullivan removed Madhava Bhupati; rebellion crushed in November 1880
## Nature and causes (1922-24)
- Lasted January 1922 to May 1924 (about 2 years 5 months), Agency tracts of East Godavari and Visakhapatnam
- Armed tribal rebellion in the Agency area; not tied to the Congress; worried the British more than the Non-Cooperation Movement
- Causes: limits on forest and podu rights, begar for road building, harsh Muttadari system, actions of Tahsildar Bastian
- Permanent settlement was NOT a cause
