### Partition of Bengal 1905 — dates, division, motives and annulment
## 1. Partition — 1905
- Announced in 1905 — Viceroy Lord Curzon; became effective on 16 October 1905
- Bengal was the main province divided — the new province: Eastern Bengal and Assam
| Province | Capital |
| Eastern Bengal and Assam | Dacca (Dhaka) |
| Western Bengal | Calcutta |
- Nationalists interpreted it as a "Divide and Rule" policy — the Swadeshi Movement was the major response
## 2. Annulment — 1911
- The Partition was annulled in 1911 — the capital shifted from Calcutta to Delhi in 1911
## 3. Event → date
| Event | Date |
| Partition announced (Viceroy Lord Curzon) | 19/20 July 1905 (some notes give 19 July, others 20 July) |
| Partition took effect | took effect 16 Oct 1905 (16 October 1905) - day of mourning, hartal, Ganga bath, Rakhi-bandhan |
| Partition annulled | annulled on 12 Dec 1911 at the Delhi Durbar of King George V (Viceroy Hardinge II) |
- Exam angle: APPSC 2022 A-R - 1911 Durbar true; Edward VII's coronation (celebrated at the 1903 Durbar [GK]) is not its explanation.
- ⚠ 1903 Durbar = Edward VII's coronation; 1911 Durbar = annulment (George V)
## 4. Division → population
| Part | Population |
| Eastern Bengal and Assam | 31 million |
| Rest of Bengal | 54 million (18 million Bengalis, 36 million Biharis and Oriyas) → Bengalis a minority in their own province |
## 5. Motives
- Stated motive: Administrative convenience (province "too big")
- Real motive: Divide Bengali Hindus from Muslims + weaken Bengali nationalism

### Swadeshi and Boycott — launch, techniques and phases
## 1. Movements
- Swadeshi and Boycott movement: Occurred during the period of the Partition of Bengal — the Congress and the nationalist movement; promoted the boycott of foreign goods, indigenous goods and national education
- Home Rule Movement: Began in 1916 (Tilak's League Apr 1916, Besant's Sept 1916) — Annie Besant, who became INC President at Calcutta in 1917
## 2. Launch
- Launch date:
- Formal proclamation = 7 Aug 1905 - boycott resolution at Calcutta Town Hall
- Mass vow = 16 Oct 1905 (some notes date the movement from this)
- ⚠ Trap: Answer 7 Aug for "launched/proclaimed"
- National Handloom Day: 7 August (APPSC 2019; CDI); observed on 7 Aug since 2015
## 3. Techniques
| Technique | Form |
| Boycott | Of foreign cloth, salt and sugar; bonfires of cloth; extended to courts, schools, government service; priests refused rituals/marriages using foreign goods |
