- Reign: Crowned at Agra (c.) 1628; deposed 1658

### Universal angles
- Significance:
- Jahangir-Shah Jahan era = Mughal high noon; 1636 treaties made Deccan sultanates tributaries → set up Aurangzeb's annexations
- Aurangzeb: greatest territorial extent, yet Deccan wars, Rajput alienation and the jagir crisis set off decline
- AP angle:
- Golconda's Inqiyad Nama (1636) under Abdullah Qutb Shah; paid Mughal tribute from 1636
- Muhammad Qutb Shah let rebel Khurram pass through to Bengal
- Jahangir sent envoy Mir Makki to Golconda
- Abul Hasan Tana Shah employed Madanna and Akkanna, helped Shivaji
- Annexation of Golconda (1687) brought Andhra under the Mughals
- Comparative note:
- Nur Jahan's name on coins parallels no other Mughal consort; standard "first/only" question
- Jahangir and Shah Jahan broadly kept Akbar's tolerance (Shah Jahan somewhat more orthodox); Aurangzeb reversed it

- Balkh-Badakhshan campaign (1646-47) — Murad Bakhsh, then Aurangzeb (not Dara Shikoh)
