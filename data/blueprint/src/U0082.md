### Renati Cholas: basics
- Gave Telugu its first royal dignity, five centuries before Nannaya
- Suryavamsa; claim descent from Karikala Chola
- Capital: Peddachapalli (Kadapa); Dhanunjaya's: Chimbapuram
### Rulers in order
- ▲ Nandivarma — progenitor
- ▸ Dhanunjaya Varma — founder; Kalamalla inscription
- ▸ Punyakumara (625-650) — greatest; Pormukharama, Purushashardula, Prithvivallabha
- ▸ Satyaditya — Chola Maharaja; decline after him
- ▸ Elachola Maharaja — last ruler
### Inscriptions
| Inscription | Ruler | Point |
| Kalamalla (Kadapa) | Dhanunjaya Varma | 575 CE; first Telugu inscription |
### Telugu in records
- Before 575 — Only names in Telugu
- 575-848 — Telugu in full prose sentences
- Total — 33 Telugu inscriptions, mostly dana sasanalu; Jammalamadugu, Proddatur

### Inscriptions → ruler and the FIRST of Telugu
## 1. Inscriptions → ruler, language, date
| Inscription | Ruler | Significance |
| Kalamalla (Kadapa district) | Dhanunjaya Varma (Erikal Mutturaju Dhananjaya) | 575 CE; first Telugu inscription (G2 2012 key); prose; not fully available today |
- ⚠ Trap: "Erragudipadu inscription of 575 CE" as first complete Telugu inscription merges two records - the 575 CE record is Kalamalla, of Dhanunjaya
- Count: 33 Renati Chola Telugu inscriptions, mostly donation records (dana sasanalu)
## 2. Credited with establishing Telugu in Andhra's history
- Credit: "Keeping Telugu in the history of Andhra" (G2 2019 key)
- FIRST: First to make Telugu a royal and legislative language - replaced Prakrit + Sanskrit in records
- Periodisation:
- Before 575 CE: only names in Telugu
- 575 to 848 CE: Telugu in full prose sentences
### Overlords, successors and legacy
- Overlords: First feudatories of the Pallavas → later of the Badami Chalukyas
- Successors: Banas and Vaidumbas; Vaidumbas issued Telugu hero-stone (veerakal) inscriptions
- Legacy: Telugu epigraphic practice taken up by the Eastern Chalukyas; Renadu art shows Pallava influence
- Other PYQ: Kakatiya-period rule - woman's property passes to daughters and daughters' daughters → Vignaneswaram of Ketana (APPSC G2 key)
