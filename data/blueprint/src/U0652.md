### SC population data — India and Andhra Pradesh
## 1. India (Census 2011)
- National count: 20.14 crore (201.4 million) = 16.6% of India's population; ≈ 20% more than in 2001
## 2. Rural-urban distribution
- Distribution (2011): ≈ 76% rural vs ≈ 24% urban
- Share of population: SCs ≈ 18.5% of rural vs 12.6% of urban India
- Comparison: SCs more likely than general population to live in rural, low-income areas (UPSC); STs even more rural (89.97%) (UPSC)
## 3. Andhra Pradesh
- AP: 84.69 lakh SCs = 4.21% of India's SCs ≈ 17% of AP's 495.77 lakh
- AP growth: 32.73 lakh (1971) → 84.69 lakh (2011)

## 2. No notified SC population
- No SC notified: Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Andaman & Nicobar Islands and Lakshadweep

## 3. District-level concentration
- Highest SC share district: Koch Bihar (West Bengal), about 50%
- Pattern:
- Densest in fertile alluvial plains (Punjab, Ganga plain, West Bengal, coastal deltas) → settled farming used landless labour
- Sparse in hill, forest and north-eastern tribal districts

### State extremes
| State | SC share (2011) |
| Punjab | 31.9% (highest share) |
| Himachal Pradesh | 25.2% |

| Mizoram | about 0.1% |
- Largest SC population by number: Uttar Pradesh (about 4.1 crore, ~20% of India's SCs), then West Bengal, Bihar

### Concentration
- No notified SCs: Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep — populations overwhelmingly tribal; SC status linked to untouchability in the Hindu caste order
- Small but non-zero SCs: Mizoram (~0.1%), Meghalaya (~0.6%); Manipur has notified SCs
- SCs dense in fertile alluvial plains and coastal deltas (Punjab, Ganga plain, West Bengal) — settled farming used landless labour
### Andhra Pradesh
- AP SCs about 84.69 lakh — about 4.21% of India's SCs
### Legal basis
- 83rd Amendment (2000) — removed SC seat reservation in Arunachal Pradesh panchayats (wholly tribal)
- SC status: Hindus, Sikhs (added 1956), Buddhists (added 1990); Jains not included
### Exam corner
- Census 2027 — first enumeration of all castes in independent India (not only SCs and STs)
