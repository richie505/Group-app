### PRAHAAR — India's first national counter-terrorism policy
## 1. Policy
- Released by: MHA on 23 February 2026 — ⚠ India's first-ever national counter-terrorism policy
- Drafting announced November 2024; earlier National Policy and Action Plan for LWE (2015); Pahalgam attack (22 April 2025) spurred NIA–state coordination
## 2. Seven pillars
- Prevention; Response; Aggregating internal capacities; Human rights and rule-of-law processes; Countering radicalisation; Alignment with global efforts; Recovery and resilience (whole-of-society)
## 3. Provisions
- Zero tolerance — deny funds, weapons, safe havens
- Threats on land, air (drones), water; critical sectors — power, railways, aviation, ports, defence, space, atomic energy
- CBRNED: Chemical, Biological, Radiological, Nuclear, Explosive, Digital
- Recognises cyber-attacks by nation-states, crypto funding, dark web
- ⚠ Does not link terrorism to any religion, ethnicity or civilisation
