### AP revenue receipts 2026-27 and revenue trends
## 1. Revenue receipts 2026-27
- Revenue receipts: ₹2,34,140 crore → own resources 59% + central transfers 41%
- Own tax revenue ₹1,25,846 crore (6.4% of GSDP):
- SGST ₹45,078 cr (largest, 36%)
- State Excise ₹30,067 cr; VAT ₹24,126 cr; stamps ₹18,701 cr
- Largest own-tax source: SGST
## 2. Trends
- Own revenue: ₹93,354 crore (2023-24) → ₹96,127 crore (2024-25); target ₹1,34,208 crore for 2025-26
- 2025-26 RE: Own tax ₹98,025 crore vs central transfers ₹1,04,493 crore
- Commercial taxes: Up 16% YoY in Jun 2026 (Q1 FY27 ₹15,323 crore)
- Current relevance: record revenue, Budget 2026-27, SES
- Revenue: State GST and commercial-tax revenue hit record highs in June 2026; CM said the economy is "back on track" (Aug 2026)
### AP debt, guarantees and past deficits
- Outstanding debt: ₹6,35,177 crore = 36.04% of GSDP (2025-26 RE)
- Expected ₹7,11,094 crore by end 2026-27; steady at about 36% of GSDP (15 Feb 2026)
- Liabilities: ~36% of GSDP (vs 33% in 2021-22)
- Exclusion: AP's ₹11,000 crore of 50-year interest-free capex loans fall outside it
- Guarantees availed: ₹3,11,182 crore (outstanding principal ₹1,77,034 crore) → growing off-budget borrowing
- 2024-25 actuals (Finance Accounts 2024-25): RD ₹60,285 crore (3.79% of GSDP), FD ₹81,362 crore (5.11%)
- A Feb 2026 planning review quoted ₹60,481 crore and ₹85,269 crore
## Criticism & debates
- Criticism & debates: debt, pensions, Super Six
- Debt: ~36% of GSDP; ₹1.28 lakh crore off-budget debt; heavy guarantees and off-budget loans
- Centre: FD target for 2026-27 = 4.3% of GDP
