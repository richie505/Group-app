### Vandemataram movement in Andhra — name, phase, trigger, slogan
- Name: Vandemataram Movement
- Andhra phase: 1906-1911
- Trigger: Surendranath Banerjee's arrest (1906) → protests in Rajahmundry, Kakinada, Vizianagaram, Gooty
- Slogan: "Vandemataram - Manade Rajyam"
- Greeting: "Vande Mataram" became a greeting; officials' curbs on it → Rajahmundry and Kakinada incidents
- Madras bodies (1906): Industrial Association (to promote Swadeshi goods) + Swadeshi League

### Rajahmundry college student incident, 1907
- Rajahmundry College: A training college — an incident in 1907 marked the beginning of student participation in the freedom struggle from Andhra Pradesh — principal Mark Hunter
- Gadicherla Harisarvothama Rao: A teacher trainee — restricted from college during the Vandemataram Movement
- Ban: Principal Mark Hunter, Rajahmundry Arts / Teachers Training College, banned Vandemataram badges + the slogan
- Date: April 1907 (some notes: 24 April 1907)
- Defiance: Gadicherla presented an address to Pal; J. Ramachandra Rao co-organised
- Punishment:
- 138 students debarred from exams for two years
- Gadicherla dismissed + blacklisted from government jobs
- Significance: Active entry of Andhra students into the freedom struggle
