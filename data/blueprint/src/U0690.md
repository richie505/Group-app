### Science vs technology
- Science = know-why; technology = know-how
| Basis | Science | Technology |
| Output | Laws, theories | Products, processes |
### Research types; invention vs innovation
| Type | Meaning |
| Basic | Curiosity-driven |
| Applied | Solves a set problem |
| Translational | Takes lab results to use |
| Invention | First creation of product/process |
| Innovation | Turning it into value in market/society |
### Scientific method
- 1. Observation ↓
- 2. Question ↓
- 3. Hypothesis ↓
- 7. Replication / peer review
### Science, pseudoscience, theory, law
- Science: empirical, testable/falsifiable, reproducible, systematic, self-correcting
- Pseudoscience: claims scientific status, cannot be tested
- Theory = well-tested explanation of why; Law = what always happens (Newton's laws)
- ⚠ A theory does not "become" a law
- Branches: natural, formal (maths, logic, statistics), social, applied (engineering, medicine, agriculture)
- Interdisciplinary: biotechnology, geoinformatics (GIS, RS, GPS), data science, cyber-physical systems
