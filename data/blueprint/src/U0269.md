### Movements in order
- Post-1956 streams moved Telugu literature from classical/romantic themes to caste, class, gender and minority identity
- 1965 — Digambara kavulu - "naked poets"; Digambara Kavitvam (1965-68)

- 1985 — Karamchedu → Dalit Mahasabhas (⚠ other notes: 1982)
- 1993 — Feminist Neeli Meghalu, ed. Volga
- 1990s — Muslim minority anthology Jaljala

- Base of feminist and Dalit fiction: Gidugu-Gurajada's spoken-Telugu revolution
- Karamchedu incident (1985): After it Dalits formed independent Dalit Mahasabhas
