- Negrito: dark skin, woolly hair, short stature
- Proto-Australoid: dark skin, wavy hair, long head, broad flat nose
- Risley's Dravidian: long head, broad nose, dark skin

### Basis of racial classification — terms and indices
- Traditional criteria: Skin, eye and hair colour and form; cephalic, nasal and stature indices; blood groups
| Term | Meaning |

| Mesocephalic (medium) | Living head CI 77-81.99; dry skull CI 75-79.99 |

| Leptorrhine (narrow nose) | Nasal index under 70 |

### Major races of the world and subgroups
## 1. Major races
| Race | Features | Distribution | Includes |
| Caucasoid — also called Caucasian | Light to olive skin; straight or wavy hair; narrow noses; medium to tall stature | Europe, North Africa, the Middle East, parts of Central Asia and South Asia | Europeans, Arabs, Persians, many North Indians |
| Mongoloid | Yellowish to brown skin; straight black hair; an epicanthic fold; almond-shaped eyes; broad faces | East Asia, Southeast Asia, Central Asia; the Arctic; includes Native Americans | Eskimos, Chinese, Japanese, Koreans, Mongols, American Indians — all included among Mongoloids |
| Negroid | Dark brown to black skin; woolly or curly hair; broad noses; thick lips | Most populations of West, Central and Southern Africa — Sub-Saharan Africa | — |
| Australoid | Dark skin; broad noses; wavy or curly hair; medium stature | Australia; Australian Melanesia | Associated with some Indian tribes, Aboriginals, some Melanesians; Proto-Australoids occur in India |
## 2. Subgroups

### Scholars → classifications and statements
| Person | Statement / contribution |
| H.H. Risley | Census Commissioner for the 1901 Census; organised its ethnographic survey; 7 types; exponent of the racial theory of caste → caste as a by-product of racial difference |
| Haddon (1924) | Hair form (leiotrichi, cymotrichi, ulotrichi) as main basis for world populations |
| Eickstedt (1930s) | Four main groups (Weddid, Melanid, Indid, Mongolid) on physical + regional criteria |
| J.H. Hutton (Census Commissioner, 1931) | India peopled by successive waves of migration |
| S.S. Sarkar | Six ethnic elements based on the cephalic index (head shape; dolichocephals and others) |
| Griffith Taylor | Migration-zone theory → primitive races pushed to the periphery (e.g. Andaman Islands) |
| B.S. Guha | Based on 1931 Census data → 6 main racial groups (6 main races) with 9 sub-types |
- Risley's method: Used anthropometry, mainly the nasal index, to rank castes (Risley relied on the nasal index)
- Risley's dictum: "The social status of a caste varies inversely with its nasal index"
- Now rejected as colonial race science
- Book → author: The People of India (1908) = Risley
- ⚠ Risley = nasal index; Sarkar = cephalic index; Haddon = hair form
- ⚠ Risley = 1901 Census; Hutton and Guha = 1931 Census
### Risley's seven types → region
| Risley type (Risley: 7) | Region / traits |
| Turko-Iranian | Baluchistan, NWFP (NW Frontier) |
| Indo-Aryan | Punjab, Rajasthan, Kashmir |
| Scytho-Dravidian | Western India: Maharashtra, Saurashtra, Coorg |
| Mongoloid | Himalaya, Assam and north-east; broad head, flat face, yellowish skin |
| Dravidian | South India, Chota Nagpur, central India; long head, broad nose, dark skin; e.g. Paniyan, Santals |
- "Dravidian": Risley's type for South India and Chota Nagpur (BPSC 2016 key: South India; Dravidian races confined to South India)
- Properly names a language family; India's two main ethno-linguistic groups = Indo-Aryan + Dravidian
### Guha's six racial groups → region and peoples
- Six groups:
- (1) Negrito
- (2) Proto-Australoid
- (3) Mongoloid = Palaeo-Mongoloid (long-headed + broad-headed; north-east and sub-Himalaya) + Tibeto-Mongoloid (Sikkim, Bhutan) [GK]
- (4) Mediterranean = Palaeo-Mediterranean (south) + Mediterranean (north) + Oriental (Punjab, Rajasthan) [GK]
- (5) Western Brachycephals = Alpinoid + Dinaric + Armenoid
- (6) Nordic
| Racial type (Guha) | Region | Representative peoples / community |
| Negrito | Andaman Islands; traces in the hills of the far south | Andamanese: Onge, Jarawa, Great Andamanese, Sentinelese; Kadar(s), Irula(s) traces [GK] |
| Proto-Australoid | Central and southern India (tribes) | Santhal, Munda, Oraon, Ho, Bhil, Chenchu |
| Mongoloid | Himalayan and north-eastern tribes (NE hills and forests) | Palaeo-Mongoloid: Sema Nagas, Lepchas; Tibeto-Mongoloid: Tibetans of Sikkim (massive head, tall, light-brown skin); Shompen (Great Nicobar) |
| Mediterranean | Palaeo-Mediterranean: Dravidian-speaking south and much of the peninsula; Mediterranean: Punjab, Rajasthan, Ganga plain | Dravidian-speaking peoples |
| Western Brachycephals | Broad-headed: Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka (Coorg), Bengal-Odisha | Alpinoid, Dinaric, Armenoid (Parsis) sub-types |
| Nordic | North-West India: Punjab, Rajasthan, NW Frontier [GK] | Tall, fair, long-headed |
- Significance: Most widely cited classification of the Indian population

### Criticism of race classification and genetic studies
| Factor | Effect |
| Shared genome | All humans share over 99% of genetic material → racial division subjective; one species, Homo sapiens |
| Modern science | Rejects ranking by race |
| Colonial basis | Old schemes rest on visible traits + colonial ethnography; Risley's race-caste theory rejected |
| Genetics (2009) | Most Indians descend from a mix of Ancestral North Indians (ANI) + Ancestral South Indians (ASI) |
