### Standard incentives → rate (tax, land, power, employment, interest) — Standard incentives (all categories)
## 1. Standard incentives (all categories)
- De-carbonisation subsidy up to 6% of FCI (capped at 6% of FCI)
- PLI top-up = 10% of GoI PLI incentive (cap 5% of FCI)
- Local procurement subsidy 1% of export turnover for 3 years
## 2. Tax incentives
- SGST (IDP 4.0, APSES 2024-25): 100% of net SGST for 5 years from commercial production
- On final products made, sold and registered in AP
- Some notes give a sliding scale of 100% / 50% / 25% for micro / small / medium-large; flat 100% net SGST followed here
- SGST (IDP 2020-23): Reimbursement for medium, large, mega units tied to employment creation, for 5 years (APPSC 2023)
- Stamp duty: 100% stamp and transfer duty reimbursement
- On land purchase, lease of land/shed/buildings, mortgages, hypothecations
