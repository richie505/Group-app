### Governance — definitions by body and FIRSTs
| Body | Definition |
| World Bank | "The manner in which power is exercised in the management of a country's economic and social resources for development"; stresses accountability, transparency, rule of law |
| UNDP (1997) | Governance = exercise of economic, political and administrative authority to manage a country's affairs at all levels; good governance is participatory, transparent, accountable and responsive |
| UNESCAP | Stresses efficiency, effectiveness, equity, sustainability |
- Origin of "good governance": World Bank first brought it into development discourse (APPSC-GS 2018) → 1989 report on Sub-Saharan Africa
- Government vs governance: Governance broader → involves market + civil society, not just the state
- Democracy and development: Democracy encourages citizen participation and accountability (APPSC-GS 2025)
- ⚠ World Bank 1989 = first "good governance"; UNDP 1997 = governance definition
### Good governance — UN/UNDP attributes, four pillars and tools
## 1. The 8 attributes
- The 8 attributes: Consensus oriented; participatory; rule of law; effective and efficient; Accountable; transparent; responsive; equitable and inclusive
- Most critical three: transparency, accountability, responsiveness (UNDP)
## 2. Four pillars
- Ethos: service
- Ethics: honesty, integrity
- Equity: empathy for weaker sections
- Efficiency: speedy delivery using ICT
## 3. Tools
- Tools for good governance: People's participation (most foundational), RTI, Sevottam, Citizen Charters, e-governance
- Exam angle: "which is NOT a feature" - look for centralisation, secrecy, discretion or majoritarian rule as the odd one out.
### Second ARC reports → title and content
- Second ARC: Set up 2005; 15 reports

| 12th Report, "Citizen Centric Administration - The Heart of Governance" | 2009 | Citizen's Charters, grievance redress, Sevottam model, people's participation |
- ⚠ 4th = Ethics (2007); 12th = Citizen Centric (2009, Sevottam)
### World Bank WGI and DARPG Good Governance Index — dimensions
## 1. World Bank Worldwide Governance Indicators
- Six dimensions:
- Voice and Accountability
- Political Stability and Absence of Violence/Terrorism
- Government Effectiveness; Regulatory Quality; Rule of Law; Control of Corruption
- Coverage: 200+ countries since 1996; DARPG = India's nodal department
- India (WGI 2023): Moderate government effectiveness + rule of law; low political stability vs global averages
## 2. Good Governance Index
- Launch: Good Governance Day 2019 → uniform tool to rank states, UTs and districts
- 10 sectors: agriculture and allied; commerce and industry; human resource development; public health; public infrastructure and utilities; economic governance; social welfare and development; judicial and public security; environment; citizen-centric governance
- ⚠ WGI = 6 dimensions (since 1996); GGI = 10 sectors (from 2019)

### DARPG tools and governance institutes — NCGG, CGG AP
## 1. Other DARPG tools and institutes
- Tools: NeSDA (2019), Citizen's Charter portal, CPGRAMS, State Collaboration Initiatives scheme (revised 2015-16)
- NCGG: Autonomous institute, set up by upgrading National Institute of Administrative Research (LBSNAA)
- Head office New Delhi; branch Mussoorie
## 2. Centre for Good Governance, Andhra Pradesh
- Set up: By Government of (undivided) AP in 2001 (APPSC-GS 2021 and 2023 keys)
- Collaboration: DFID (UK) and the World Bank (APPSC-GS 2021)
- Mandate: Governance-reform think tank → advises on administrative reforms, e-governance, research, training
- ⚠ NCGG = New Delhi (branch Mussoorie); CGG = Hyderabad, 2001
### Universal angles
- Current relevance: DARPG's 50th CPGRAMS report includes GRAI for June 2026 → ranks ministries on 11 indicators in 4 dimensions (9 Aug 2026)
- AP angle: Mana Mitra WhatsApp Governance → 500+ services; Rs 100 crore for RTGS in 2026-27 Budget; target 1,000 services
- Criticism & debates: Perception-based indices (WGI, V-Dem) contested for relying on expert opinion
