### Punchhi Commission → appointing government, chairman, members, report
| Item | Detail |
| Formal name | Commission on Centre-State Relations |
| Set up | By the Government of India (UPA government) in April 2007 |
| Chairman | Justice Madan Mohan Punchhi, former Chief Justice of India |
| Report | Submitted 2010, 7 volumes |
- Mandate: review Centre-State arrangements in light of changes since the Sarkaria Commission (1983-88)
- Panchayati Raj, economic reforms, coalition politics, internal security
- ⚠ Trap: "Punchhi Commission (2010)" = report year; appointed 2007
- Exam angle: "When was it appointed?" Answer 2007, not 2010. Remember the order 1969, 1983, 2007.
### Governor → Punchhi's recommendations
- Tenure: Fixed tenure of 5 years → insulated from Central pressure
- Universities: not be made Chancellors of universities or given other statutory roles → office stays out of day-to-day clashes with State governments
- Bills: six-month deadline to decide on bills passed by the Assembly, by constitutional amendment
- Selection: an eminent person not in active politics, even at the local level, for a couple of years before appointment
- Exam angle: impeachment of the Governor, the 5-year tenure and the chancellor role are Punchhi's. Periodic "rotation of Governors" and "abolish the office" are distractors.
### Articles → proposed amendments (156, 243G, 243W, 355, 356)
| Article | Punchhi recommended |
| Article 156 | Delete "during the pleasure of the President"; removal only by impeachment by the State Legislature, like the President's impeachment |
| Arts. 355 and 356 | Amendment: recommended amending Arts. 355 and 356, not leaving them as they are |
| Article 356 | Used sparingly, only as a last resort, never in a partisan way |
- "Localised emergency": Centre takes over a specific troubled area (district or part of a district) for a limited period instead of a whole State
- Communal violence: Centre may deploy central forces suo motu, without the State's consent, for a short period; consent obtained afterwards
- Origin: 1st ARC recommended deploying central armed forces "either on their request or otherwise"
- Exam angle: "The Commission said there is no need to amend Articles 355 and 356" is the wrong statement (APPSC-G2 2017).
### Councils and Lists → meeting frequency, Concurrent List, Union List
| Forum | Meet |
| Inter-State Council (Art. 263) | At least thrice a year; a working forum for Centre-State and inter-state issues; agenda set in consultation with the States |
| National Integration Council | At least once a year |
- Concurrent List: Centre should reach broad agreement with the States via the Inter-State Council before Parliament legislates
- New Union List entry: environment, ecology and climate change
### Financial recommendations → Finance Commission, backward States, cesses
- Coordination: Finance Commission + Planning Commission, time periods synchronised with the Five Year Plans
- Cesses and surcharges: limited
- Water: National Water Resources Council to play a bigger role
### Status and cases → implementation, time limits for bills
- Report: referred to the Inter-State Council
- 11th ISC meeting (16 July 2016) - first in about a decade - took up its recommendations
- Implementation: major recommendations not been implemented
- Art. 156 unchanged → Governors still hold office "during the pleasure of the President"
- Governors still act as Chancellors
| Case / opinion | Date | Held |
| State of Punjab v. Principal Secretary to the Governor | 2023 | Governor cannot keep bills pending indefinitely |
| State of Tamil Nadu v. Governor of Tamil Nadu | 8 April 2025 | Fixed timelines |
| Presidential Reference opinion | 20 Nov 2025 | Courts cannot impose fixed timelines or "deemed assent" |
- ⚠ Trap: APPCA notes call it a "2023 Tamil Nadu case" giving timelines "binding force" - outdated
### Universal angles
- 16th Finance Commission:
- Chairman Dr. Arvind Panagariya; period 2026-31
- Vertical share kept at 41%; Centre accepted it (Budget 2026-27, 2 Feb 2026)
- ₹1.4 lakh crore in FC grants for 2026-27
- No recommendation on cesses and surcharges; proposed a "grand bargain" (para 7.67) (2 Mar 2026)
- Cesses (Lok Sabha): FM said cesses and surcharges are for specific purposes, outside the divisible pool = about 81% of gross tax revenue in 2025-26 (12 Feb 2026) → same issue Punchhi raised
- AP angle: 16th FC's revised formula raises the share of the five southern States, including Andhra Pradesh (2 Feb 2026)
# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 2:57
### Commission at a glance
- Chairman — Justice Madan Mohan Punchhi, former Chief Justice of India
- Officially the Commission on Centre-State Relations; named by the Government of India (UPA), April 2007
- Report — 2010
### Governor
- Fixed five-year tenure, shielded from Central pressure
- Governors not to be Chancellors or hold statutory university roles
- Constitutional amendment — six months for Governor to decide on Bills
- Governor — eminent person not in active politics (even locally) for a couple of years
### Proposed amendments
- Delete "during the pleasure of the President" from Article 156
- Removal only by impeachment by the State Legislature, like the President (not rotation of Governors)
- Amend Articles 355 and 356; Art. 356 sparingly, last resort, never partisan
### Security & forums
- National Integration Council — meet at least once a year
- PM and Chief Ministers — meet regularly
- Inter-State Council — at least thrice a year
- Localised emergency — Centre takes over a district or part of a district for a limited period
- Communal violence — central forces sent suo motu, without prior State consent, short period; consent afterwards
### Lists & finance
- Concurrent List laws — broad agreement with States through the Inter-State Council
- New Union List entry — environment, ecology and climate change
- Cesses and surcharges — to be limited
### Status & cases
- 8 April 2025 Tamil Nadu case — fixed timelines for Governors on Bills
- 20 November 2025 Presidential Reference — courts cannot impose fixed timelines or deemed assent
- Article 156 unchanged — Governors still hold office during the pleasure of the President
