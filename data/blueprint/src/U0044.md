### Terms → administration, economy and contributions
## 1. Saka-Kshatrapas
- Gold-to-silver ratio: 1:35 (the Saka-Kshatrpa ratio) - 1 suvarna = 35 karshapanas (UKPSC 2021; Nasik record)
- Chastana: Karddamaka dynasty — silver and copper coins; his coins were rare — the copper coins relatively rare
## 2. Terms
| Term | Meaning |
| Satrapas and Mahasatrapas | Titles under which the Sakas ruled (UPSC) |
| Satrap system | Saka administration (UPSC); Sakas absorbed into society as Vratya Kshatriyas |
| Kolika | Weavers; interest on guild deposits generally 12% |
- Coinage: numerous silver coins from western India (UPSC); Kardamaka silver coins carry Saka-era dates
- Dress and military (shared with Kushanas): trousers, long coats, helmets, boots; better cavalry (UPSC)
### Eras → starting year + founder; Saka era as the national calendar
## 1. Eras — began in
| Era | Began | Associated with / founder | Note |
| Kali Era (Kali) | 3102 BC | — | The beginning is traditional |
| Vikrama Samvat | 57/58 BC | Vikramaditya - traditionally a king of Ujjain | Also called Krit Samvat and Malav Samvat; traditionally linked with victory over the Sakas |
| Saka Samvat (Saka) | 78 AD (78 CE) | Kanishka (UPSC); APP credits Gautamiputra Satakarni (Salivahana era) | The National Calendar of India |
| Kalachuri Era | 248 AD | The Kalachuris | Also called the Chedi Era |
| Gupta Era | 319 AD | Chandragupta I | Marks the beginning of the Gupta imperial era — Srigupta was the founder of the Gupta dynasty |
| Harsha Era | 606 AD | Harshavardhana | Begins with Harshavardhana's accession |
| Lakshmana Sena Era | 1119 AD | Lakshmana Sena | Bengal |
- ⚠ Sources differ: other notes say the Vikrama Samvat began in 57 BC only
## 2. Conversions
- Vikrama and Saka: A difference of 135 years — Vikrama equals Saka plus 135 years; the Vikrama year is obtained by adding 135 to the Saka year
- Saka 465 corresponds to Vikrama 600 or 601, depending on the convention
- AD and Saka: The AD year equals the Saka year plus 78; the Saka year equals the AD year minus 78 (CE = Saka + 78)
- Saka 556 corresponds to 634 AD; 2009 AD corresponds to Saka 1931
- CE = Vikrama - 57
- ⚠ Trap: UPSC notes print "58 CE" for the Vikram Samvat - a slip; it began 57 BC
- Saka calendar: Chaitra is the first month — Chaitra 1 falls on 22 March; in a leap year, on 21 March (1 Chaitra = 22 March)
- National calendar: Saka era adopted as the national calendar from 22 March 1957
### Universal angles
- Current relevance (2026): recaps Gautamiputra's defeat of Nahapana (Kshaharata) + restriking of the coins, the Nasik Prashasti, capitals Pratishthana (Paithan) and Amaravati
- AP angle:
- After Rudradaman's defeat of Pulumavi → Satavahana centre shifted to Amaravati
