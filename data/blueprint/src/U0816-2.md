### SC on SIR — ECI can examine but not decide citizenship
## 1. Verdict
- Case: Association for Democratic Reforms v. Election Commission of India
- Upheld: ECI order for Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls — conducted before November 2025 Bihar Assembly elections
- Held: SIR broadly proportionate; ECI is best placed to decide method of revision
- Citizenship: ECI may examine citizenship prima facie and contextually (for electoral roll only)
