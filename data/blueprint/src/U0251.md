### Communal Award, 1932 — national era
## 1. National era
- Indian National Congress: Sought to remove caste, sect and regional biases — promoted national unity
- Communal Award: Announced by Ramsay MacDonald — separate electorates and representation for the Depressed Classes
- Poona Pact: Followed the Communal Award — a subsequent development; concerned the Depressed Classes
- B.R. Ambedkar: The Depressed Classes — advocated political representation; participated in the anti-caste struggle
- Gandhi: Worked for the removal of untouchability — a Gandhian social reform
## 2. Communal Award
- Communal Award: Announced in 1932 — the background to Gandhi's fast
- Announced: By Ramsay MacDonald, 16 August 1932
- Provision: Separate electorates for minorities, including depressed classes (gave 71 seats)
### Poona Pact — Yerwada fast, signatories, seats, electorate
## 1. Poona Pact — 1932
- Poona Pact: Concluded in 1932 — Gandhi and Ambedkar; joint electorates and reserved seats for the Depressed Classes
- ⚠ The representatives or followers signed it — the PDF states that Gandhi himself did not sign the Poona Pact
- ⚠ Sources differ: other notes say the signatories were Dr B.R. Ambedkar (depressed classes) + Madan Mohan Malaviya (caste Hindus), Malaviya mediating
## 2. Yerwada fast, signatories, seats, electorate
- Gandhi's stand: Fasted against separate electorates, fearing a permanent split in Hindu society
- Yerwada fast: Gandhi, jailed in Yerwada (Pune) Jail, began a fast unto death (20 Sept 1932)
- Date: September 1932 (24 Sept; some notes give 25 Sept)
- Signatories: Dr B.R. Ambedkar (depressed classes) + Madan Mohan Malaviya (caste Hindus), Malaviya mediating
- Popularly called a Gandhi-Ambedkar pact
| Feature | Communal Award | Poona Pact |
| Electorate | Separate (+ general vote) | Joint electorate with reserved seats |
| Provincial seats for depressed classes | 71 | 147 (APP: 148) |
- Terms:
- Separate electorates dropped; joint electorates retained
- Reserved seats in provincial legislatures raised from 71 to 148 / 148 seats (some notes: 147 reserved seats)
- Similar principle at the Centre: reservation / depressed-class share in the central legislature
- Fair representation / fair share in civil services + a share of education grants
- ⚠ 71 = Communal Award; 147 / 148 = Poona Pact
