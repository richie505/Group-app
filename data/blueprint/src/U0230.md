### Gandhism compared — Marxism, socialism, anarchism, individualism
| Point | Gandhism | Marxism |
| Final goal | Stateless society | Stateless society - the common ground (UPSC 2020) |
| Method | Non-violence, change of heart | Class struggle, revolution |

| Basis | Moral/spiritual | Economic determinism |

- Socialism: Indian Preamble socialism = blend of Marxism + Gandhism, leaning to Gandhian socialism
### Universal angles

- Gandhian non-violence inspired the Baltic Way + Singing Revolution → Baltic re-independence in 1991 (1 Sep 2026)

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:229
### Key terms
- Truth is the end; non-violence is the means
- Means are everything: the means decide the nature of the end
- Between cowardice and violence, Gandhi preferred violence; ahimsa is a quality of the strong, not the weak
- Satyagraha — born 1906 in the Transvaal; early English name: passive resistance
- Swaraj — wider than political freedom: self-rule, self-control and ability of the masses to rule themselves without force
- ★ Family planning — self-control is the best means
### Quotations
- ★ "Wrong means never take us to right ends" — M.K. Gandhi; means and ends are two sides of a coin
- ★ Politics for Gandhi = activity for public welfare (social welfare)
- Key suggestion: decentralisation of power — encourage village panchayats
- "God is Truth" = earlier form; "Truth is God" = later form (truth given independent, central place)
- Politics without religion (morality of truth and non-violence) is a death-trap
- Gandhi's Talisman — before a step, recall the face of the poorest and weakest person
### Forms of satyagraha
| Struggle | Year | Gandhi's "first" |
| Champaran | 1917 | first civil disobedience |
| Ahmedabad | 1918 | first hunger strike |
| Kheda | 1918 | first non-cooperation |
| Rowlatt Satyagraha (hartal) | 1919 | first all-India mass movement |
### Rules for satyagrahis
- A Satyagrahi must never insult the opponent
- Rules: non-retaliation, accepting arrest, and obeying jail discipline
### Gandhism vs Marxism
- Linked principles of Swaraj: swadeshi, ahimsa, satyagraha, decentralisation, education for all
- Class dictatorship is NOT a Gandhian principle
- Trusteeship as basis of property relations — Gandhism; Marxism seeks abolition of private property
### Other comparisons
- ★ Non-violence — a way to attain truth; ahimsa is the means, truth the end, inseparable
- Truth = reality; described as existence, consciousness and bliss
- Gandhi a philosophical anarchist — ideal of enlightened anarchy; State = violence in concentrated form
- Socialism of the Indian Preamble — blend of Marxism and Gandhism, with a Gandhian tilt
### Exam corner
- ★ Strike — the last step in the Gandhian strategy of Satyagraha
- ★ Fasting — the first step; Gandhi called it the "Fire Arrow"
### Gandhian terms → meaning
- ★ Heavy industrialisation — favoured by Nehru, not by Gandhi
- Nehru shared Gandhi's social principles (truth, non-violence, against untouchability) but differed on economics
### Gandhi → statements and quotations
- ★ Gandhian politics = morality, religion and humanity; no place for authority — aim a stateless society
- ★ Cruellest form of violence — persistence of poverty
- ★ "Which is morally wrong, can never be politically right" — M.K. Gandhi
- ★ Aim of a Satyagrahi is not to defeat the enemy; suffering is the driving force of Satyagraha
### Gandhism compared — Marxism, socialism, anarchism, individualism
- ★ Common goal of Gandhism and Marxism — a stateless society (approaches differ)
- Gandhian stateless society founded on non-violence, truth, simplicity
