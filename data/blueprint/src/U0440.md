### Employment schemes → year, from IRDP to VB-G RAM G
| Scheme | Year | Note |
| IRDP | 1978-79; nationwide 1980 | Sixth Plan self-employment scheme [GK] |
| NREP | 1980 | Wage employment [GK] |
| RLEGP | 1983 | Employment for landless households [GK] |
| JRY | 1989 | NREP and RLEGP merged [GK] |
| EAS | 1993 | Employment Assurance Scheme [GK] |
| SGSY | 1 April 1999 | Merged IRDP, TRYSEM, DWCRA and allied schemes [GK] |
| SGRY | 2001 | EAS + JGSY [GK] |
| MGNREGA | 2005 | 100 days per household per year |
| NRLM | 2011 | Restructured from SGSY; renamed DAY-NRLM in 2016 (CDI; LENS Nov 2025) |
| VB-G RAM G | Act passed within two days of its introduction (16 Dec 2025) | Replaces MGNREGA; 125 days |
- Bharat Nirman (2005): Six rural components = irrigation, roads, housing, drinking water, electrification, telephony
- ⚠ JRY = NREP + RLEGP; SGRY = EAS + JGSY; SGSY = IRDP + TRYSEM + DWCRA
- Exam angle: the APPSC 2025 key gives SGSY as 1995. The scheme actually started on 1 April 1999 [GK] (some keys say 1995). Learn 1999 and recognise the key's option.
### Acts → provisions: MGNREGA 2005 vs VB-G RAM G
| Feature | MGNREGA 2005 | VB-G RAM G (replaces it) |
| Guarantee | 100 days per household per year, unskilled manual work (IYB) | 125 days (CDI) |
| Status | Repealed | Presidential assent 20 Dec 2025; notified 11 May 2026; in force 1 July 2026 (CDI) |
| Implementing body | Gram Panchayats [GK] | Panchayati Raj Institutions (CDI) |
| Funding | Centre paid wages; material 75:25 [GK] | CSS 60:40, NE/Himalayan 90:10, UT without legislature 100%. Total ₹1,51,282 crore, Centre ₹95,692.31 crore (CDI) |
| Unemployment allowance | If no work within 15 days; paid by states [GK] | Allowance and delay compensation borne fully by states (CDI) |
- MGNREGA features:
- One-third of workers had to be women
- Gram Sabha ran the social audit
- Links to SDG 1 (No Poverty) + SDG 8 (Decent Work)
- Section 4(5): Centre fixes state-wise normative allocations
- ⚠ Dates of VB-G RAM G: introduced 16 Dec → assent 20 Dec 2025 → notified 11 May 2026 → in force 1 July 2026
### Rural development and land schemes → ministry and features
## 1. MP-linked schemes
- SAGY (Saansad Adarsh Gram Yojana): Ministry of Rural Development
- Launched 11 Oct 2014 = Jayaprakash Narayan's birth anniversary
- Each MP develops model gram panchayats
- MPLADS (1993): Run by MoSPI
- Funds non-lapsable
- Earmark: 15% for SC + 7.5% for ST areas
## 2. DAY-NRLM (MoRD; Centrally Sponsored)
- Funding 60:40; NE incl. Sikkim 90:10; UTs 100% Centre (Aug 2026)
- Older quiz (Nov 2025) says 75:25 = original NRLM ratio
- Implemented via State Rural Livelihood Missions; Delhi + Chandigarh excluded
- Sub-schemes: SVEP, AGEY, MKSP, DDU-GKY, RSETIs
- Lakhpati Didi target: 3 crore women earning over ₹1 lakh a year
## 3. Rural infrastructure
- Hariyali (2003): Watershed development through PRIs
- SPMRM (Shyama Prasad Mukherji Rurban Mission, 2016): 300+ Rurban clusters; "rural soul, urban amenities"
## 4. Land records and land reforms
| Body / scheme | Year | Fact |
| National Council for Land Reforms | Set up 2008 | Chaired by Prime Minister |
| DILRMP 3.0 | Began as NLRMP (2008); Central Sector in 2016 | Guidelines issued |
| SVAMITVA | 24 Apr 2020 | Ministry of Panchayati Raj; Drone survey of village houses + property cards |
### Livelihood schemes for artisans, SCs, micro-enterprises and street vendors → benefit
| Scheme | Objective / key facts |
| PM SVANidhi (2020, MoHUA, SIDBI) | Working-capital loans for street vendors: first loan ₹10,000, 7% interest subsidy, digital cashback (CDX). 75 lakh+ vendors covered (CDI) |
| PM-AJAY (2021-22, MoSJE, CSS) | Development of SC-majority villages. Its Adarsh Gram component (formerly PMAGY, 2009-10) covers villages with 500+ people and over 40% SC, at ₹20 lakh per village. 47,300 villages covered, 16,700 declared Adarsh Grams (LENSD, 20 Jul 2026) |
| PM Vishwakarma (2023, Ministry of MSME) | Artisans in 18 traditional trades: ID card, training with a ₹500/day stipend, toolkit up to ₹15,000, collateral-free credit up to ₹3 lakh at 5% (CDX). 24.50 lakh craftspeople trained and given credit (LENSD, Jul 2026) |
- ⚠ Trap: PM Vishwakarma is not for "fishermen with non-mechanised boats" (APPSC 2023)
