### EPZs and free trade zones — terms, places and FIRSTs
## 1. Export Processing Zones — FIRSTs
- Kandla, Gujarat: India's first and Asia's first — the first Asian EPZ — Export Processing Zone (EPZ) — established 1965; later associated with Kandla SEZ
- Also India's first Free Trade Zone
- Surat: India's first private-sector EPZ — established 1997; converted into an SEZ on November 1, 2000
## 2. Terms → meaning
- Free trade zone: Area where goods can be landed, stored, processed and re-exported without customs duty
- Conversion: Existing EPZs and EOUs could convert into SEZs

### SEZ policy, Acts, rules and committees → year and provision
## 1. SEZ milestones
| Date | Milestone |
| April 1, 2000 | India's first SEZ policy announced — the SEZ policy introduced in 2000; announced in the EXIM Policy (APPSC-GS 2025 key: EXIM Policy 2000-01) |
| 2005 | SEZ Act passed by Parliament (enacted) (May 2005) — President's assent on June 23, 2005 |
| February 10, 2006 | The SEZ Act and Rules came into effect — single-window clearance for Central and State matters |
| SEZ (Amendment) Rules 2025 | Minimum land for semiconductor/electronic-component SEZ cut from 50 ha to 10 ha |
- EPZs came first, from 1965 (Kandla first)
- Policy "announced": both 2000 and 2006 (APPSC-G1 2018)
- SEZ Act in force: 2006 (APPSC-GS 2018)
- ⚠ Policy 2000 → Act 2005 → in force 2006
## 2. Committees and proposed law
- Baba Kalyani Committee (2018):
- Rename SEZs as "3Es" – Employment and Economic Enclaves
- Separate rules for manufacturing/services
- Unified IFSC regulator
- DESH Bill (Development of Enterprise and Services Hubs):
- Announced Budget 2022-23 to replace the SEZ Act; still pending
- Removes positive NFE condition + allows DTA sales
### SEZ features → developers, approval, FDI, tax
## 1. Setting up SEZs
- Developers: Set up jointly or severally by Central Government, State Government or any person (public, private, joint sector)
- Purpose: Manufacturing, services, or as a Free Trade Warehousing Zone
- Approval route: Proposals via State → Board of Approval
- Ownership: SEZs under the Act mainly private
- ⚠ Trap: "SEZ is set up primarily to generate employment" is NOT true – primary aim is exports (APPSC-G2 2017 key)
## 2. Investment and tax provisions
| Provision | Content |
| FDI | 100% FDI under automatic route for most manufacturing in SEZs |
| IFSC (s.18) | Allows an IFSC in an SEZ – only one IFSC per SEZ |
| Tax (s.10AA) | 100% income-tax exemption for 5 years; 50% for next 5; 50% of reinvested profit for 5 more |
- Tax holidays phased out 2017-2020 (sunset clause)
- ⚠ s.18 = IFSC; s.10AA = tax holiday (100-50-50, 5+5+5)

### EPZ to SEZ
## EPZ era
- Kandla — India's and Asia's first EPZ, 1965

## SEZ policy and Act
- SEZ Policy — announced April 2000 through the EXIM Policy 2000-01
- SEZ Act — passed May 2005; Presidential assent 23 June 2005; in force 10 February 2006
- SEZ may be set up by the Central Government, a State Government or any person (public, private, joint sector), together or alone
- Section 18 — allows an IFSC in an SEZ; only one IFSC per SEZ
- DESH proposal — removes the positive Net Foreign Exchange condition, allows DTA sales; still pending, has not replaced the SEZ Act
