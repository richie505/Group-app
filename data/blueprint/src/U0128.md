### Panipat I (1526) → tactics, terms and FIRSTs
## 1. Terms
| Term | Meaning |
| Tulughma | Flanking units wheel round to encircle the enemy; Uzbek origin |
| Rumi method / araba | Carts chained as a barrier for matchlockmen and guns = Ottoman field-defence system |
- ⚠ Trap: Common error says it used war elephants (Apr 2026 correction)
- Artillery: Commanded by Ottoman gunners Ustad Ali and Mustafa
- Panipat numbers: Babur's 12,000 vs about 1,00,000 of Ibrahim's troops
## 2. Gunpowder debate
- Panipat = first large-scale use of field artillery in North Indian warfare
- Firearms not wholly unknown in India before Babur
## 3. Gains
- Founder: Babur, after Panipat I (1526) → displaced the Lodis
- Gains from Panipat: Delhi and Agra (APPSC-GS 2023) + the treasure of the Lodis
- Treasure: Shared out lavishly → nickname "Qalandar"; treasury left depleted for Humayun
### Khanwa (1527) → coalition, religious appeal, title
- Coalition: Rana Sanga's Rajput confederacy + Afghan Mahmud Lodi + Hasan Khan Mewati
- Tactics: Trenches and barricades vs Sanga's elephants; artillery decisive again
- Religious appeal (before battle): Declared a jihad; renounced wine; remitted the tamgha for Muslims
- Title (after victory): "Ghazi"
- ⚠ Babur's titles: "Qalandar" = for sharing the Lodi treasure; "Ghazi" = after Khanwa
### Babur's death and empire → extent and limits
- Extent at death: Kabul and Badakhshan → Punjab, Delhi, Agra → borders of Bihar (after Ghaghra)
- Death: 1530, at Agra
- Humayun took the throne on 29 Dec 1530
- Buried at Kabul (Bagh-e-Babur)
- Limits of Babur's 4-year rule:
- Mughal authority had shallow roots
- Bengal unconquered; Afghans still claimed Delhi
- Gujarat-Malwa united under Bahadur Shah
- No administrative system; treasury empty
- ⚠ Trap: Mansabdari system = Akbar's, not Babur's

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:127
### Babur's career
- ★ Babur's given name — Zahir-ud-din Muhammad; 'Babur' = tiger (title / nickname)
- Descent: Timur on father's side, Chingiz Khan on mother's side
- ★ Samarkand taken 1501 (held 8 months); after taking Kabul (1504) dropped 'Mirza' and took the title 'Padshah'
- Mansabdari system — introduced under Akbar, not during Babur's four-year rule
- Mughals did not interfere in the Deccan in Babur's time; Babur contemporary of Krishnadevaraya and Golconda's Sultan Quli
### Invited by
| Inviter | Who |
| Daulat Khan Lodi | Governor of Punjab |
| Alam Khan | Ibrahim Lodi's uncle |
| Rana Sanga | Mewar |
### Panipat I vs Khanwa
- After Panipat: generous sharing of the Lodi treasure — nickname 'Qalandar'
- Khanwa: Rana Sanga's Rajput confederacy joined by Mahmud Lodi and Hasan Khan Mewati
- Before Khanwa: declared jihad, renounced wine, removed tamgha for Muslims
- After Khanwa: title 'Ghazi'
- Khanwa: trenches and barricades to counter Rana Sanga's elephants; artillery decisive
### Key terms
- ★ Shahrukh / Shahrukhi — silver coin popularised by Babur (Timurid style); he also issued copper coins
- Rumi method (araba) — carts chained as a barrier for matchlockmen and guns (not elephants)
- Babur introduced the charbagh garden
### Tuzuk-i-Baburi
- ★ Tuzuk-i-Baburi (Baburnama) — written in Turkish (Chagatai Turki); Persian was the Mughal court language
- ★ Hindu states named in the Baburnama — Mewar and Vijayanagara
- Babur praised Krishnadevaraya as the most powerful ruler in India
- Babur set up no administrative system (shallow roots, empty treasury)
### Buildings & limits
- At death, dominions: Kabul, Badakhshan → Punjab, Delhi, Agra → borders of Bihar
- First Mughal garden — Ram Bagh (Aram Bagh), Agra, on the charbagh plan
### Exam corner
- First Battle of Panipat — first large-scale use of field artillery in North Indian warfare
- Babur defeated Ibrahim Lodi, the last Delhi Sultan
- firearms were not fully unknown in India before Babur
### Panipat I (1526) → tactics, terms and FIRSTs
- ★ Babur's empire included Kabul, Punjab and modern Uttar Pradesh (not modern Rajasthan)
- ruled through jagirs and paraganas; Badakhshan given to Humayun
- ★ Babur's arrival led to the establishment of the Timurid dynasty in the region
- gunpowder entered India before Babur (used to break fort walls); Babur credited with its use in cannons and guns
- not the introduction of the arch and dome
### Books, buildings and coins → Tuzuk-i-Baburi, gardens, mosques
- ★ Mughal rulers — Chagatai Turks (name from Genghis Khan's second son, ruler of Central Asia and Turkistan)
- ★ Babur wrote his memoirs in Turki, but Turki was not the official court language
