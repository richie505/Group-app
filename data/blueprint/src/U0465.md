### Principles → meaning
| Principle | Meaning |
| Precautionary principle | Act to prevent damage (stop the activity) even without full scientific certainty; burden of proof shifts to the actor to show the activity is benign |
| Polluter Pays | The person or company responsible bears the cost of repairing the damage (remediation + compensation) (APPSC 2025; PYQ) |
| Public trust doctrine | The State holds natural resources in trust for the public |
| Absolute liability | For hazardous industries - no exceptions |
- NEP 2006 principles: Precautionary approach + polluter pays + public trust doctrine
- Exam angle: APPSC 2024 and 2025 both asked what the precautionary principle means: "act/stop even without full scientific certainty".

### NEP 2006
- Main idea: conservation and livelihood security can go together
- Objectives: conservation of critical resources, inter-generational equity, integration of environment into development plans
- NOT an objective: ending all development activity in ecologically sensitive areas
