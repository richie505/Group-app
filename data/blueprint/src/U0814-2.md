### India's first non-GMO high-expansion popcorn maize hybrid (Musunuru)
## 1. News
- ⚠ India's first non-GMO, high-expansion popcorn maize hybrids launched at Musunuru, Eluru district
- Network: 17,500+ farmers, 8 States, ~40,000 acres
- ⚠ Hybrid is not the same as GM; maize is India's 3rd most important food crop (after rice, wheat)
## 2. Release details (CA-360)
- Released on: 24 August 2026 by Vice-President C.P. Radhakrishnan at the Gourmet Popcornica facility, Musunuru; CM Chandrababu Naidu attended
- Developed with: ICAR–Indian Institute of Maize Research (IIMR); work began February 2021
- Maize: *Zea mays*; origin Central America/Mexico

### AP Shoreline Management Plan
## 1. What happened
- Centre identified vulnerable stretches on AP's 1,027-km coastline; Shoreline Management Plan prepared by Ministry of Earth Sciences
- NCCR (National Centre for Coastal Research) mapped erosion/accretion with satellite data 1990–2022, 2011 Census, geomorphology
| District | Erosion share of coastline |
| Kakinada | 52.99% — ⚠ worst affected |
| West Godavari | 52.54% |
| Krishna | 42.71% |
| Srikakulam | 32.14% |
| Visakhapatnam | 26.85% |
| Vizianagaram | only 3.26% — high accretion |
## 2. Vulnerable stretches
- Kakinada: Coringa–Uppada coast, Godavari mouth spit near Yanam, Coringa mangroves, Hope Island
- Tirupati: Pulicat Lake, Sriharikota
- Krishna: Machilipatnam Beach, Krishna delta
- Srikakulam: Kalingapatnam, Vamsadhara and Nagavali river mouths
- Visakhapatnam: Yarada, Bheemunipatnam
- Measures: beach nourishment, dune restoration, ecosystem-based protection (mangroves), engineering works, inlet management
