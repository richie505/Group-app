# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 3:16 - Human Development Index - components, methodology, India's rank and trend
### Origin & idea
- People and their capabilities, not growth alone, are the ultimate criteria of development
- Conceived by Mahbub ul Haq with Amartya Sen (team incl. Inge Kaul)
- Sen: capability approach (Development as Freedom)
- Martha Nussbaum: central human capabilities
- First HDR 1990, UNDP; compares education, health, per-capita income
- Same GNI, different HDI → national policy choices (Malaysia vs Chile)
### Dimensions & goalposts
| Dimension | Indicator | Min-Max |
| Long & healthy life | Life expectancy at birth | 20-85 |
| Education | Expected years of schooling | 0-18 |
| Education | Mean years of schooling | 0-15 |
| Standard of living | GNI per capita (PPP $), log | $100-$75,000 |
### Method
- 1. Dimension index = (actual - min)/(max - min) ↓
- 2. Education = arithmetic mean of 2 sub-indices ↓
- 3. Income: log → diminishing returns ↓
- 4. HDI = geometric mean of 3 (since 2010)
### Related indices
| Index | Year | Measures |
| GDI | 1995 | Ratio of female to male HDI |
| HPI | 1997 | Deprivation; replaced by MPI 2010 |
| IHDI | 2010 | HDI discounted for inequality; IHDI < HDI |
| GII | 2010 | Reproductive health, empowerment, labour |
| MPI | 2010 | OPHI + UNDP; overlapping deprivations |
| PHDI | 2020 | HDI adjusted for CO2, material footprint |
### HDI categories

### Human development — approach, persons and origin
## 1. Human development approach
- Focuses on people, opportunities and choices
- Developed by Mahbub-ul-Haq; Amartya Sen provided the intellectual foundation through the capability approach
- First Human Development Report: Published 1990 — by the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)
- HDR compares: education, health and per-capita income (APPSC-GS 2025 key: "Human Development Report")
## 2. GDP vs human development
| Basis | GDP or per-capita income | Human development |
| Nature | Primarily an economic measure | A broader, people-centred approach |
| Focus | Income and output | Capabilities and well-being |
| Dimensions | A one-dimensional income measure | Multidimensional |
- Core idea: people and their capabilities, not growth alone, are the ultimate criteria of development
- GNI vs HDI: GNI cannot replace HDI - income is a means, not the end
- Same GNI, different HDI: shows role of national policy choices (APPSC-G1 2024)
- Example: Malaysia has higher GNI per capita than Chile, but Chile has ~7 years more life expectancy + 3 more years of expected schooling → much higher HDI
## 3. Persons → contribution
| Person | Contribution |
| Amartya Sen | Capabilities approach (Development as Freedom) - development = expanding real freedoms; co-conceived the HDI |
| Martha Nussbaum | extended it with a list of central human capabilities (Women and Human Development) |
| Mahbub ul Haq | Conceived the HDI with Amartya Sen (team also incl. Inge Kaul) |
### HDI methodology — components, goalposts, formula
| Dimension | Indicator | Minimum | Maximum |
| Long and healthy life | Life expectancy at birth (years) | 20 | 85 |
| Education | Expected years of schooling | 0 | 18 |
| Education | Mean years of schooling | 0 | 15 |
| Decent standard of living | GNI per capita (PPP $) (2011 PPP $), log of income | $100 | $75,000 [GK] |
- Dimension index: (actual - min)/(max - min)
- Education index: arithmetic mean of its two sub-indices
- Income: uses logarithm of GNI → diminishing importance of income (diminishing returns)
- Income indicator: HDI uses GNI, not GDP, per capita (some notes say GDP per capita)
- GNI + the two schooling indicators came in with 2010 revision
- Formula: HDI = geometric mean of three normalised dimension indices; introduced 2010 - weak dimension not offset by a strong one
- PPP dollars: fair cross-country comparison across price levels (conversion from 2011 ICP surveys); goalposts first used 2011 ICP PPP, later Reports moved on
- ⚠ Education index = arithmetic mean; HDI = geometric mean
- ⚠ Expected schooling max 18; mean schooling max 15
### HDI vs MPI and related indices → year introduced and what they measure
## 1. HDI vs MPI
| Basis | Human Development Index (HDI) | Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI) |
| Measures | Human development | Multidimensional poverty |
| Considers | Health, education and standard of living | Health, education and living standards |
| Associated with | The UNDP; the Human Development Report (HDR) | OPHI and UNDP — first included in the Human Development Report in 2010 |
## 2. Related indices
- Limits: only part of human development - no inequality, poverty, human security, empowerment, environment
- Not included (odd-one-out): MPI, inequality, poverty, gender gaps, human security, empowerment, participation, environment
- Criticism: HDI ignores inequality, sustainability, gender → hence IHDI, GDI, GII, MPI
| Index | Introduced (HDR) | Measures |
| Capability Poverty Measure | 1996 [GK] | Lack of basic capabilities |
| Human Poverty Index (HPI-1 developing, HPI-2 developed) | 1997 [GK] | Deprivation in HDI dimensions; replaced by MPI in 2010 [GK] |
| GDI | 1995 [GK] | Ratio of female to male HDI (health, education, earned income) |
| IHDI | 2010 [GK] | HDI discounted for inequality; IHDI < HDI whenever inequality exists; gap = loss |
| GII | 2010 [GK] | Reproductive health, empowerment, labour market |
| MPI | 2010 [GK] | Overlapping deprivations |
| Planetary pressures-adjusted HDI (PHDI) | 2020 [GK] | HDI adjusted for CO2 emissions and material footprint [GK] |
- IHDI vs HDI: no inequality → HDI = IHDI; HDI = "potential", IHDI = "actual" human development
- ⚠ GDI = ratio (1995); GII = inequality (2010)

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 3:16
### Origin & idea
## Origin
- First Human Development Report — 1990, published by UNDP; based on Mahbub-ul-Haq's human development approach
- HDR compares countries on education, health and per-capita income
- GDP — mainly an economic measure; HDI measures human development; MPI multidimensional poverty
- MPI first included in the HDR in 2010; OPHI works with UNDP on the MPI
## The idea
- Human development approach — broader than an economic measure; focuses on people, opportunities and choices
- Income (GNI) is a means of development; higher GNI per capita does not guarantee a higher HDI
- Malaysia has higher GNI per capita than Chile, but Chile has the higher HDI (better life expectancy, schooling) — policy choices matter
- Martha Nussbaum — extended Sen's capability approach with a list of central human capabilities
- Human capital formation — investment in education, health, skills (on-the-job training), migration and information
## What HDI leaves out
- HDI components: life expectancy, schooling indicators, GNI per capita — not environmental quality
- HDI does not capture human security, inequality, poverty, empowerment, participation or environment
- India's HDI 0.685 (HDR 2025) — medium category (0.550–0.699), just below the high threshold of 0.700
### Dimensions & goalposts
| Indicator | Goalposts |
| Life expectancy | 20–85 years |
| Expected years of schooling | 0–18 |
| Mean years of schooling | 0–15 |
- Education index — arithmetic mean of expected-years and mean-years schooling sub-indices
- Dimension index = (actual − minimum) / (maximum − minimum)
- e.g. actual 50, min 20, max 80 → (50−20)/(80−20) = 0.50
- MPI measures multidimensional poverty; HDI measures human development
### Method
- ★ HDI indicators: income level, education, life expectancy — not condition of environment
- Logarithm of GNI per capita — shows diminishing returns to income
- Since 2010, HDI = geometric mean of its three dimension indices — a strong dimension cannot fully offset a weak one
- IHDI is lower than HDI whenever inequality exists
- ★ HDI 2022 (HDR 2021/22, for 2021), descending: Hong Kong (4th) > Australia (5th) > Japan (19th) > Russia (52nd)
### Related indices
| Index | Meaning / note |
| HPI-1 / HPI-2 | HPI-1 for developing, HPI-2 for developed countries; replaced by MPI in 2010 |
| PHDI (2020) | HDI adjusted for planetary pressures — CO2 emissions and material footprint |
| GDI | ratio of female HDI to male HDI |
| IHDI | HDI discounted for inequality |
| MPI | produced by OPHI and UNDP together |
