### Parliamentary committees — role, types, origin and FIRSTs
## 1. Role and types
- Role: A primary mechanism of parliamentary control over administration — detailed and complex legislative scrutiny
- Broadly classified into two types:
- Standing Committees: Permanent and continuous; reconstituted annually or periodically — e.g. Financial Committees, Department-Related Standing Committees
- Ad-hoc Committees: Temporary — for a specific inquiry or task; cease to exist after their reports — e.g. Joint Parliamentary Committees, Inquiry Committees
- Department-Related Standing Committees: 24 — 16 of the Lok Sabha + 8 of the Rajya Sabha (joint ratio about 2:1)
| Type | Committees |
| Standing — financial | Estimates, PAC, COPU |
| Standing — departmental | 24 DRSCs |
| Standing — other | Petitions, Privileges, Subordinate Legislation, Government Assurances, Ethics, Business Advisory, Welfare of SCs and STs |
| Ad hoc | Inquiry committees + Select/Joint Committees on Bills |
## 2. Committee → origin

### Financial committees → strength, chairman and role
| Committee | Strength | Rajya Sabha members | Chairman |
| Estimates | 30 (largest) | None - all from LS | Appointed by Speaker, invariably ruling party |
| Public Accounts (PAC) | 22 (15 LS + 7 RS) | Yes (associate) | Appointed by Speaker; Opposition member since 1967 (convention) |
| Public Undertakings (COPU) | 22 (15 LS + 7 RS) [GK] | Yes | From LS members, appointed by Speaker [GK] |
- Election: Members elected annually by proportional representation (STV)
- Term: 1 year
- Bar: Ministers ineligible
- Role: Keep "an unremitting vigil" over expenditure and performance
- Control is after expenditure vs budget (before expenditure) → complementary
- ⚠ Estimates = 30, no RS member, ruling-party chairman; PAC = 22, Opposition chairman
### PAC → scrutiny, reporting, limits and chairman
- Scrutiny: Appropriation accounts, finance accounts + CAG audit reports
- Checks money spent "within the scope of the demand"
- Tests: Efficiency, economy and propriety, not only legality; may take up matters on its own
- Reports to: The Lok Sabha (Speaker)
- Limits: Recommendations not binding; cannot examine policy, only its execution
- Chairman:
- Opposition chairman by convention, not rule (APPSC-G2 2017)
- 2026-27: K.C. Venugopal
### DRSCs and other standing committees → strength and functions — Departmentally Related Standing Committees
## 1. Departmentally Related Standing Committees
- Number: 24 DRSCs = 16 under LS, 8 under RS
- Strength: Each 31 members: 21 LS + 10 RS; quorum 11
- Bar: Ministers ineligible
- Functions: Scrutinise demands for grants, bills referred, annual reports and long-term policy of their ministries

### Types of committees
## Standing committees
- Business Advisory Committee — gives time for government and other business; chaired by the Speaker (Lok Sabha)
- Other roles: privilege questions — Committee of Privileges; members' conduct — Ethics Committee; delegated rule-making — Committee on Subordinate Legislation
- Watchdog group: Subordinate Legislation, Government Assurances, Estimates, PAC, COPU, DRSCs — not the Business Advisory Committee
- 24 DRSCs: 16 under Lok Sabha, 8 under Rajya Sabha (22 = strength of the PAC)
## Ad hoc committees
- Two kinds: inquiry committees on a particular subject; Select / Joint Committees on Bills (e.g. Joint Committee on a Bill)
- Inquiry committees are ad hoc, not standing committees
- Bill sent to a Select / Joint Committee by a motion in the House
### Financial committees
- Estimates Committee — largest financial committee; 30 members, all Lok Sabha; chairman from the ruling party
- PAC — 22 members (15 LS + 7 RS)
- Estimates Committee = 'continuous economy committee' (suggests savings and alternative policies)
- Nicknames: PAC — 'watchdog' on expenditure; Petitions Committee — 'Ombudsman'
- Committee of Privileges: Lok Sabha 15 members nominated by the Speaker; Rajya Sabha 10
### Origins
| Committee | Origin |
| PAC | 1921 |
| Estimates Committee (present form) | 1950 (John Mathai) |
| COPU | 1964, Krishna Menon Committee recommendation |
| DRSCs | 1993, Rules Committee recommendation |
- DRSCs started with 17 committees (1993); 24 from 2004
- Ethics Committees: Rajya Sabha first — 4 March 1997; Lok Sabha — 16 May 2000
### PAC at work
## Financial committees
- Members elected yearly by PR-STV; term one year; Ministers ineligible
- Budget = control before expenditure; financial committees = control after expenditure; they support, not replace, the budget
## PAC
- Examines Appropriation Accounts, Finance Accounts and CAG audit reports
- Checks spending stayed within the demand for grant approved by Parliament
- Examines only execution of policy, not policy itself; recommendations not binding
- Chairman 2026-27: K.C. Venugopal (Opposition, by convention)
- Estimates Committee (not PAC) suggests economies and alternative policies; PAC = watchdog on departmental expenditure and irregularities
## Other committees
- DRSC quorum = 11 (not 15)
- DRSCs examine demands for grants, referred Bills, annual reports and long-term policy of their ministries
- Committee on Welfare of SCs and STs — examines welfare programmes and National Commissions' reports; Ministers cannot serve
- Lok Sabha-only: Committee on Private Members' Bills and Resolutions, Committee on Absence of Members
- In both Houses: Petitions, Privileges, Rules, Papers Laid, Subordinate Legislation, Government Assurances
- Viksit Bharat Shiksha Adhisthan Bill, 2025 — before a JPC (both Houses), not a single-House Select Committee

- Committee on Petitions — 'Ombudsman' of Parliament; studies petitions on bills and matters of public interest
