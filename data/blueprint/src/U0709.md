### Types — public vs private; PoW vs PoS
## 1. Public vs private
| Public / permissionless | Private / permissioned |
| Anyone can join, read and validate (Bitcoin) [GK] | Only authorised entities join and validate [GK] |

| Proof of Work (PoW) | Proof of Stake (PoS) |

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 5:19
### Definition & Bitcoin
- ★ Cryptocurrency — encrypted (cryptographic) peer-to-peer fund transfers without a central bank
- Blockchain — time-stamped chain of immutable records kept by a group of computers not owned by one party
- Records safe because stakeholders hold copies and changes need consensus
- Bitcoin — introduced 2009 by Satoshi Nakamoto (pseudonymous)
### How a chain links
- Distributed ledger — shared ledger copied across many nodes; one source of truth; no central checker
- A block holds transaction details, a timestamp and the hash of the previous block (not private keys)
- Immutability — data nearly impossible to change without consensus; no central authority changes it
### Key terms
- Stablecoins — tied to fiat currencies, commodities or crypto-assets
- Tokenisation — turns ownership rights in an asset into a blockchain token; mining checks transactions
- NFT — Non-Fungible Token; unique, not swappable equally; can stand for physical assets
- Smart contract — self-running code; DeFi uses them for lending/trading without banks
### Types & consensus
- The Merge — Ethereum's switch from Proof of Work to Proof of Stake (September 2022); Bitcoin still uses PoW
### Strategies & platforms
- MeitY's Distributed Centre of Excellence in Blockchain — Shamshabad pilot is in Telangana, not AP
- National Strategy on Blockchain (December 2021) — MeitY
- Vishvasya — Blockchain-as-a-Service stack across NIC data centres
### AP pilots
- AP DigiVerify — permissioned-blockchain PaaS for tamper-proof certificate verification
- Mee Bhoomi-Mee Hakku — tamper-proof pattadar passbooks
### Exam corner

- Proof of Stake — validators picked by coins staked; Proof of Work — miners race to solve puzzles (far more energy)
- Cryptocurrency — not legal tender in India; decentralised and peer-to-peer
- VDA service providers brought under PMLA — March 2023
- NPCI's Drunix — rebuilt from Hyperledger Fabric; second open-source blockchain after Falcon
