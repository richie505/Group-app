### US suspends major IT firms from PERM programme
## 1. News
- US suspended firms from the Permanent Labor Certification (PERM) programme — Cognizant, Infosys, TCS, Wipro, HCL Technologies, Capgemini, Microsoft, Adobe
- Reasons cited: fraudulent filings, wage undercutting, displacement of US workers ("America First")
- Firms had >230,000 H-1B approvals and >100,000 labour certifications since 2009 (US claim)
## 2. PERM basics
- Administered by: US Department of Labor; employer-filed — Form ETA-9089; then Form I-140 with USCIS
- Filing date = priority date; certification valid 180 days
- ⚠ PERM is neither a visa nor a Green Card
| Basis | H-1B | PERM | Green Card |
| Nature | Temporary work status | Labour certification | Permanent residence |
| Authority | USCIS (+ LCA to DOL) | Department of Labor | USCIS / State Dept |
| Duration | Up to 6 years | Procedural | Permanent |
## 3. Visa backlog
- Sept 2026 Visa Bulletin: India EB-2 unavailable
- Oct 2026: EB-2 India final action 1 Nov 2013; EB-3 India 1 Jan 2014
- EB-2: advanced degrees / exceptional ability; EB-3: skilled workers
- H-1B extension beyond 6 years if PERM/I-140 pending 365 days; up to 3 years at a time
