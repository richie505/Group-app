### Downsizing the State — measures, definitions and traps
- LPG-era reforms cutting State size and cost:
- Disinvestment (selling/offloading PSU shares)
- FDI inflows: growth + external-sector measure, NOT a cut in State size
- ⚠ Trap: keyed set = downsizing, disinvestment, privatisation of higher education (UPSC 2010)
| Term | Meaning |
| Privatisation | Shedding government ownership/management (withdrawal or outright sale) |
| Disinvestment | Sale of part of a PSU's equity to the public |

### Thinkers and books → ideas (NPM and its critics)
| Thinker | Book / year | Idea |

| David Osborne and Ted Gaebler | Reinventing Government (1992) | "steering, not rowing"; ten principles |

- Common critique: New Public Service and Public Value both reject NPM's reduction of citizen → customer and State → service seller
### NPM doctrines and Reinventing Government principles
## 1. New Public Management — core idea
- Core idea: private-sector techniques in government (Phase 4 of Indian public administration after 1991)
- Performance auditing
- Contracting out non-core services
- Citizen as "customer"
## 2. Hood's NPM doctrines
- Hands-on professional management
- Explicit standards and performance measures
- Emphasis on output controls
- Competition in the public sector
## 3. Osborne and Gaebler's ten principles
| Principle [GK] | Meaning |
| Catalytic | Steer rather than row |
| Community-owned | Empower rather than serve |
| Competitive | Inject competition into service delivery |
| Mission-driven | Rules to mission |
| Enterprising | Earn rather than spend |
| Decentralised | Hierarchy to participation and teamwork |
### Commissions, committees and codes → year and recommendation

### Lateral entry, contractualisation and union data
## 1. Lateral entry
- Normal route: JS and Director posts at Centre filled by tenure deputation (Central Staffing Scheme, DoPT)
- Timeline:
- First lateral-entry joint secretaries joined 2019
- Aug 2024: Centre asked UPSC to cancel advertisement for 45 lateral posts after reservation debate
- Debates: domain expertise + fresh ideas vs no reservation for single posts, conflict of interest, demotivation of career officers
## 2. Outsourcing and contract labour
- Outsourcing: hiring regular services from external sources (legal, IT, advertising, security) earlier done in-house → grew with IT + fast communication
- Union impact: since 1991 many organised-sector workers have no direct employer relation → weakens union mobilisation
## 3. Data
| Indicator | Figure |
| Contract labour share (Annual Survey of Industries) | 20% (1999) → 40.7% of manufacturing workforce (2022-23) |
| Contract-labour-intensive firms | 31% lower productivity (Aug 2025) |
### Performance management and benchmarking tools
## 1. Performance management tools
- Results Framework Document (RFD): introduced 2009 under Performance Monitoring and Evaluation System (Cabinet Secretariat)
- Each ministry lists objectives, success indicators, targets → work as Key Result Areas
- Other tools:
- Performance-based incentives (Second ARC)
- Referral Board to review adverse ACR remarks
- 360-degree multi-source feedback
## 2. Benchmarking tools (DARPG) and e-governance indices
| Tool | Detail |
| NeSDA | Started 2019; the NeSDA 2025 portal was launched in Jun 2026; modelled on the UN e-Government Survey's Online Service Index; covers G2C and G2B (IYB; LENS, 1 Jun 2026) |
| Good Governance Index | Uniform framework to assess governance across states and UTs (IYB) |
| GRAI | Ranks central ministries on CPGRAMS grievance disposal using 11 indicators in 4 dimensions: efficiency, feedback, domain, organisational commitment. The 50th CPGRAMS report covered June 2026 (LENS, 9 Aug 2026) |
- UN EGDI: India ranks 97th in UN E-Government Development Index 2024 (UN DESA)
### Citizen as customer — instruments, AP examples and criticisms
## 1. Citizen as customer
- NPM view: citizen deserves quality service like a customer
- Instruments: Citizens' Charters, service standards, grievance redressal (Second ARC; RTI Act 2005)
- Criticisms:
- Citizen has rights, not just purchasing power
- Equity + accountability suffer when efficiency is the only yardstick
## 2. AP context
| AP body / case | Detail |
| APCOS (AP Corporation for Outsourced Services) | Set up 2020 by AP govt → recruits outsourced staff + pays directly, cutting out contractor middlemen |
| APSRTC | About 8,000 outsourced employees paid via contractors → irregularities in wages, PF, ESI; demand equal pay (TH, 28 Jul 2026) |
| NEDCAP | Staff recruited after 1987 kept only as contract/outsourced workers (TH, 29 Jan 2026) |
| Registration Service Kendras | Passport Seva Kendra model; statutory powers stay with Sub-Registrars; CM: they do not privatise SROs (CDCA, Jul 2026) |
| "One-Time Delete Option" | On recruitment portals (Jun 2026) |
### Universal angles
- Current relevance: AP raised upper age limit for direct recruitment to non-uniform services 42 → 44 years until 30 Sep 2027 (G.O. Ms. No. 187)
- AP angle:
- E-governance line: e-Seva (2001) → MeeSeva → Gram/Ward Sachivalayams (2019) (10,000+ secretariats, 500+ services) (May 2026)
- Civil Services Board: created to curb political interference in postings
- Criticism & debates: contractualisation lowers job security + productivity; unions weakened
