### Afforestation and agroforestry → terms and meaning
| Term | Meaning |
| Afforestation | Establishing or increasing forest or tree cover — especially on denuded, degraded or unproductive land |
| Reforestation | Re-establishing forest cover in existing forest areas |
| Replantation | Replanting trees in existing forests |
| Social forestry | Increasing tree / forest cover with emphasis on societal or community needs; term used by National Commission on Agriculture (1976); components: farm forestry, community forestry, extension forestry (roadside, canal and rail strips), agroforestry |
| Agroforestry | Integrating trees with agricultural systems — trees + crops + livestock on the same land |
| Miyawaki method | Dense, multi-layered mini-forests of native species in urban areas (UPSC 2022 key) |

- Social forestry - criticism: Drifted into commercial monocultures → fix: shift to mixed native species
- Agroforestry - benefits: diversifies farm income, conserves soil, improves fertility, stores carbon, climate resilience
- ⚠ Extension forestry = roadside, canal and rail strips; Miyawaki = urban mini-forests

### CAMPA and compensatory afforestation → provisions
## 1. Compensatory afforestation (CA) basics
- Principle: CA replaces "land by land" and "trees by trees" on non-forest land
- Exception: Central PSUs, or projects with no non-forest land available → may use twice the area of degraded forest land
- Payment: Agency diverting forest land pays CA + NPV of lost forest ecosystem before the project starts
## 2. CAMPA under the CAF Act 2016
- Law: Compensatory Afforestation Fund Act, 2016 - set up National and State Compensatory Afforestation Funds
- Non-lapsable, interest-bearing public accounts (Jul 2026)
- Two tiers:
- National CAMPA: Headed by Union Environment Minister; sets policy
- Split: 90% to State funds + 10% to National fund (Feb 2026)
- Uses: Artificial and natural regeneration; wildlife management; catchment treatment; relocating villages out of PAs; human-wildlife conflict; forest-fire prevention
- Utilisation: Fund held over ₹66,000 crore by 2024, but only 30-35% used
- Exam angle: CAMPA exists at both national and state levels, which is correct. "People's participation is mandatory" under the 2016 Act is wrong (UPSC 2019).
- ⚠ 90 : 10 = State : National; non-forest land else twice the area of degraded forest
### Compensatory afforestation rules → land banks, Green Credit, consent
## 1. Land banks and Green Credit link
- Forest (Conservation) Rules 2022: Introduced Accredited Compensatory Afforestation
- Developers use plantations raised in advance (effectively land banks) to meet CA obligations
- Green Credit Programme: Afforestation raised under it can be exchanged for CA
- Allowed under the Van (S.E.S.) Rules 2023
## 2. Consent controversy
- Dropped clause: FC Rules 2022 removed the requirement of forest-dwellers' consent before diversion
- Dispute: NCST disputed this with MoEFCC
- ⚠ FC Rules 2022 = land banks + dropped consent; Van (S.E.S.) Rules 2023 = Green Credit swap
### Schemes and programmes → body, target and funding — Nagar Van Yojana (NVY)
## 1. Nagar Van Yojana (NVY)
- Run by: National Afforestation and Eco-development Board (NAEB)
- Target: 600 Nagar Vans and 400 Nagar Vatikas between 2020-21 and 2026-27; some older notes give "200 urban forests"
- Funding: National CAMPA
- Progress: 620 projects in 28 States and 3 UTs, costing ₹654.30 crore; first instalment ₹458.53 crore released
- Not in your sources: how many NVY projects AP got for 2020-21 to 2023-24. The APPSC 2023 key was left blank, so check the MoEFCC reply.
