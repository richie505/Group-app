### FIRST and LAST — order of factories at Machilipatnam; Nellore
## 1. European factories and traders
| Factory | Established by | Year / note |
| Machilipatnam (Masulipatnam) | Dutch East India Company | The first Dutch factory in Andhra Desa / Andhra Pradesh |
| Machilipatnam | English East India Company | 1611 — permission granted by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah |
| Nellore | British | 1626 |
- First Dutch factory in Andhra: Masulipatnam (Machilipatnam); First English factory: Machilipatnam, 1611 — permission granted by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah
## 2. Order of factories at Machilipatnam
| Order (CDX) | Power | Machilipatnam factory | Permission |

### Colonial firsts in Andhra; traders and trade taxes
## 1. Colonial administration
- Ryotwari: First introduced in the Madras Presidency at Baramahals
- Zilla-bandi system: Introduced by Salarjung in 1865 in Hyderabad State
- Mulki conditions: Issued in 1888 in Telangana — during the reign of the sixth Nizam, Mir Mahboob Ali Khan
## 2. Traders and taxes
- Tavernier: A 17th-century trader — visited the Andhra mines in search of precious stones and diamonds of the best quality
### Policies → power → meaning
| Policy | Power | Meaning |
| Blue Water Policy | Portuguese (Francisco de Almeida) [GK] | Control the sea, not land |
| Spice monopoly; own coins; no middlemen | Dutch | Traded directly for profit (APP) |
| Dadni advances; dubashes | English | Advance payments to weavers through local intermediaries (CDI) |
- Portuguese: Held the Arabian Sea trade monopoly until the 17th century
- Decline began with the Dutch arrival in 1605
### French settlements in Andhra — Machilipatnam (1669) to Yanam (1723)

- Pondicherry 1673-74 (Francois Martin)
- Yanam (1723): Only French territorial possession in Andhra

## Settlements
- Armagaon (1626) — English factory in the Nellore region; at Durgarajapatnam, about 35 miles north of Pulicat
- Yanam — gained 1723; only French territorial possession in Andhra
## Wars and battles
- 1753 — Bussy got the Northern Circars by a grant from Salabat Jung (not Muhammad Ali)
- Bobbili War (1757) — the French supported Vizianagaram
- ★ Battle of Chandurthi (1758) — English under Colonel Forde (sent from Bengal), aided by Ananda Gajapati, defeated the French under Conflans; led to surrender of the Northern Sarkars to the British
- Wandiwash (1760) — Eyre Coote defeated Count de Lally
- Order: Circars grant (1753) → Bobbili (1757) → Chandurthi (1758) → fall of Masulipatnam (1759)

- Zilla-bandi system — Hyderabad State, 1865, by Salar Jung
