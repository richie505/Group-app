### IGMDP missiles → type, range class and service
| Missile | Type | Range / class | Propellant / notes | Service |
| Prithvi | Surface-to-surface ballistic SRBM | Short range (150-350 km) | Liquid fuel; the first IGMDP missile | Army (Prithvi-I), Air Force (II) |

### SAMs → Akash and Trishul variants, outcome and successors
## 1. Akash variants
| Variant | Range / platform | What it is / key point |
| Akash Mk-I | ~25 km | All-weather, multi-target; Army and IAF; command guidance with Rajendra radar |
| Akash Prime | 25-30 km | Upgraded Akash Mk-I with an indigenous active RF seeker. First tested 27 Sep 2021 (APPSC 2021 key); shot down (destroyed) high-speed targets at high altitude in Ladakh |
| Akash-NG | about 70 km | Canister-launched, quick reaction; cleared for induction in 2023 (Army and IAF) |
| Akash S1 | 30-35 km | Better ECCM (Army) |
- Base Akash: Mach 2.5; launch weight 720 kg; altitude 18 km (older notes: range 30 km)
- Akashteer (BEL + Army): Fully automated air-defence control system → NOT a missile (Jun 2026)
## 2. Trishul
- Type: Short-range quick-reaction SAM
- Specs:
- Speed above Mach 2
- Solid propellant + thrust vector control
- Range ~9 km (some notes say 12 km)
- Outcome: Never inducted; programme shut on 27 Feb 2008
- Reasons: failed operational requirements, delays, cost overruns
| Service | Replacement |

| Navy | Barak-1 |
| Army | Akash |
- Indigenous successor: QRSAM (Quick Reaction SAM)
- ⚠ Trishul replaced by Spyder (IAF) – Barak-1 (Navy) – Akash (Army)
