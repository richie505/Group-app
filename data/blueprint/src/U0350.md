## 1. Central Information Commission (CIC)
- Set up: Constituted 2005 (12 Oct 2005) under Section 12
- Composition: CIC + up to 10 ICs
- Nature: Statutory body with civil-court powers
- Unique power: Only body that can declare an organisation a "public authority"
## 2. CBI
- Precursor: Special Police Establishment (1941) to probe bribery/corruption in war-time supplies → statutory cover by DSPE Act 1946
- Set up: CBI set up in 1963 (APPSC 2019 key) by a Ministry of Home Affairs resolution (resolution of the Ministry of Home Affairs; 1 April 1963)
- Status: Neither constitutional nor statutory (Not statutory, not constitutional)
- Powers from the Delhi Special Police Establishment (DSPE) Act, 1946
- Administrative home: Now an attached office under the Ministry of Personnel (DoPT)
- Founder Director: D.P. Kohli (APPSC 2018 PYQ/key)
- Annual D.P. Kohli Memorial Lecture: 22nd lecture by CJI Surya Kant (22 Apr 2026)
## 3. Central Vigilance Commission (CVC)
- Origin: Set up in 1964 on the Santhanam Committee's recommendation by executive resolution
- Status: Statutory under the CVC Act, 2003; not constitutional
- ⚠ CBI = 1963, executive resolution, still non-statutory; CVC = 1964, statutory only in 2003

- Vineet Narain v Union of India (1997; reported (1998) 1 SCC 226) (Vineet Narain v. UoI (1997)):

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 2:76
### Status & origin
- CBI — formed 1963 on advice of the Santhanam Committee on Prevention of Corruption (1962–64)
- CVC — set up 1964 by executive order; statutory under the CVC Act, 2003; top vigilance body, not constitutional
- No constitutional bar on CBI probing economic offences — it does handle them
### Key milestones
## CBI
- Not a constitutional body — set up by MHA resolution; powers from the DSPE Act, 1946
- Sequence: Special Police Establishment (1941) → DSPE Act (1946) → CBI (1963) → CVC (1964)
- Handles corruption plus economic and special crimes
- Director: minimum tenure two years, extendable to five years (2021)
- Director of Prosecution — appointed on CVC's recommendation for two years
- BHARATPOL (January 2025) — real-time Interpol assistance requests; developed by the CBI
## Vineet Narain v. Union of India (1997)
- Gave the CVC superintendence over the CBI; fixed two-year tenure for the CBI Director
- Did not give investigation decisions to the CVC — CBI alone decides whether to investigate
- DSPE Act changed in 2003 accordingly
## RTI
- RTI (Amendment) Act, 2019 — Centre may fix tenure and salaries of CIC and SIC members
- 2019 Rules — Information Commissioner's term fixed at three years
