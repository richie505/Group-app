### Awards: instituted & body
## Padma awards
- Padma Vibhushan — exceptional and distinguished service; 2nd highest
- Padma Bhushan — distinguished service of a high order; 3rd highest
- Padma Shri — distinguished service in any field; 4th
- Started 1954; announced on Republic Day; given by the President at Rashtrapati Bhavan (around March–April)
- Balaji Raghavan case (1996) — Padma awards not titles under Article 18; not usable as prefix/suffix
- 2026 list (25 Jan 2026) — 131: 5 PV, 13 PB, 113 PS; 2025 list — 139 (7/19/113)
- Vijay Amritraj — Padma Bhushan 2026, sports; Shibu Soren — Padma Bhushan 2026, posthumously, public affairs
