### Krishnadevaraya's works → language — Amuktamalyada and the Sanskrit works — Amuktamalyada
## 1. Amuktamalyada
- Amuktamalyada: Telugu; also called Vishnuchittiyam
- Meaning: "one who offers the garland she has worn" ("one who offers the garland she wore")
- Circumstance (legend): Andhra Mahavishnu of Srikakulam (Krishna district) commanded the king in a dream to write it; written during the Kalinga campaign
- Dedication: Dedicated to Lord Venkateswara
- Subject:
- Goda Devi (Andal) + her marriage to Sri Ranganatha
- Sub-stories: Yamunacharya Charitra + Maladasari Katha
- Statecraft, social life + the genealogy from Bukka to Krishnadevaraya
- Political maxims: Practical advice on statecraft, kingship and administration
- Records the king's political ideas + social, religious and economic life of the period
- Preface: "Desa Bhashalandu Telugu Lessa" ("Telugu is the best of the regional languages")
- Status: One of the Telugu Pancha Kavyas; first Telugu work named after its heroine
- PYQ (APPSC G2 2003): It does not describe his invasions
- ⚠ Trap: option not in the poem = "Describes the invasions of Krishnadevaraya"; it does contain Godadevi's marriage, political matters + genealogy from Bukka to Krishnadevaraya

- ★ Allasani Peddana — greatest of them; title Andhra Kavita Pitamaha from Krishnadeva Raya
- ★ Krishnadevaraya's reign — golden age of Telugu literature
- ★ Krishnadevaraya — wrote Amuktamalyada; also called Andhra Bhoja
- ★ Peddana — poet of Telugu and Sanskrit (not Sanskrit and Tamil)
