### Councils → chairman, members, nature — Inter-State Council
## 1. Inter-State Council
- Set up: Inter-State Council, 1990 under Art 263 (APPSC-G1 2017 key)
- Recommending bodies:
- Sarkaria: wanted a permanent body called the "Inter-Governmental Council" (RPSC 2023 key)
- Sarkaria also proposed renaming the NDC the National Economic and Development Council with constitutional status under Art 263
- Earlier backers: ARC (1966-69) + Rajamannar Committee (1969)
- Chairman: Prime Minister
- Members:
- CMs of all states and of UTs with legislative assemblies
- Administrators of UTs without assemblies
- Governors of states under PR
- 6 Union Cabinet ministers nominated by the PM
- Standing Committee: chaired by the Union Home Minister
- Secretariat: Inter-State Council Secretariat (MHA)
- Other Art. 263 councils: Central Council of Health, Central Council of Local Self-Government, Transport Development Council

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 2:53
### Articles at a glance
## Inter-State Council and water
- ★ Article 263 — Inter-State Council; PM (head), 6 Union Cabinet Ministers, CMs of States and UTs with legislatures
- Purpose — study and discuss subjects of common interest (Union–States, among States) and recommend
- Sarkaria proposed a renamed National Economic and Development Council under Article 263
- ★ Article 262 — adjudication of inter-State river water disputes
- Art. 262(2) — Parliament can bar courts, so Article 131 does not cover water disputes
## Trade and commerce (301-307)
- Article 301 — freedom of trade, commerce and intercourse throughout India
- Art. 304(b) — State bill limiting inter-State trade needs previous sanction of the President
- Article 306 (Part B States) — repealed
### Inter-State Council (1990)
- Council chaired by Prime Minister; Standing Committee chaired by Union Home Minister
- Members include six Union Cabinet Ministers nominated by the PM
- Governors — only for States under President's Rule (not permanent members)
### ISC: origin & nature
- Constitutional, recommendatory body under Art. 263 (not judicial)
- Set up 1990 on Sarkaria Commission recommendation; ARC and Rajamannar had earlier supported it
### Zonal Councils
- Chief Ministers — Vice-Chairmen by annual rotation
- Deliberative and advisory; advice not binding
- Southern Zonal Council — Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala, Telangana, Puducherry
