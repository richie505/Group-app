- 21 February 1948: Draft Constitution published (CDX)
- 14 Nov 1949: Third reading begins
- 26 November 1949: Constitution adopted and enacted — on the last day of the eleventh session; observed as Constitution Day (the Preamble's date) (APPSC-G2 2025 key)
- 24 January 1950: Final (last) meeting — for signatures; National Anthem adopted
- 26 January 1950: Constitution fully came into force; Republic of India established (Commencement - on the date of Purna Swaraj Day (1930, Lahore Congress 1929 decision))
- 17 April 1952: Provisional Parliament ceased; new Parliament in May 1952
- Early-commencing provisions (26 Nov 1949): Citizenship, elections, provisional Parliament, transitional articles - in force the same day as adoption

- ★ First met 9 December 1946, Constitution Hall, New Delhi — 207 representatives incl. nine women
- ★ Provisional President — Dr Sachchidananda Sinha (French practice: oldest member)
- ★ First nominated (temporary) Deputy Chairman — Frank Anthony, 9 Dec 1946; founded All India Anglo-Indian Association (1942)
## Office-bearers
- ★ President — Dr Rajendra Prasad, elected December 1946
- ★ Constitutional Advisor — Sir Benegal Narsing Rau (B.N. Rau); first draft 243 Articles, 13 Schedules
- ★ Drafting Committee chairman — Dr B.R. Ambedkar
## Flag
- ★ Wheel (from Ashoka Chakra, Dharmachakra, 24 spokes) — wheel of law, symbolises Justice
### Committees → chairmen
- ★ Finance and Staff Committee — Rajendra Prasad
- ★ Union Constitution Committee and Union Powers Committee — Jawaharlal Nehru
- ★ K.M. Munshi — member of the Drafting Committee
- ★ Nehru — NOT a member of the Drafting Committee
- ★ Drafting Committee — formed 29 August 1947, chairman Ambedkar
- ★ Flag committees — 2 April 1931, Karachi, Sardar Patel; ad-hoc National Flag Committee 23 June 1947, Dr Rajendra Prasad
### Dates → events (Assembly chronology)
## 1946–47
- ★ First meeting / established — 9 December 1946, New Delhi (Constitution Hall, now Central Hall of old Parliament House); first session ended 23 December 1946
- ★ First day chaired by Dr Sachchidananda Sinha (not the first permanent President)
- ★ Muslim League boycotted the first meeting
- ★ Nehru WAS invited (statement that he was not — false)
- ★ Objectives Resolution — presented by Nehru, 13 December 1946, fifth meeting of the first session; adopted unanimously 22 January 1947; became the basis of the Preamble
- ★ Drafting Committee — appointed by resolution, 29 August 1947
- ★ Cabinet Mission arrived — 24 March 1946
## 1949–50
- ★ Adopted and enacted — 26 November 1949 ("twenty-sixth day of November, 1949" in the Preamble); by the Constituent Assembly
- ★ Constitution Day — 26 November
- ★ In force — 26 January 1950; chosen because Congress observed it as Independence Day (Purna Swaraj, Lahore 1929)
- ★ Jana Gana Mana adopted — 24 January 1950 (final session); Vande Mataram also adopted that session
- ★ Final session 24 January 1950 — Rajendra Prasad declared elected President of India
- ★ National Flag — 24 spokes
### Numbers game → time, sittings, cost, size
- ★ Duration — 2 years, 11 months, 18 days
- ★ 165 days of sittings; Constitution adopted on 26 November 1949
- 11 sessions (165 days) to draft the Constitution, plus one meeting on 24 January 1950 for signatures — 12 in all

- ★ Jana Gana Mana — adopted as National Anthem 24 January 1950; formal rendition 52 seconds

### Statements and criticisms → who said
- ★ Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas), 26 November — to promote constitutional values; commemorates adoption of the Constitution
- ★ Ambedkar's 125th birth anniversary — 2016 (14 April 2016); coins of Rs 10 and Rs 125
- ★ Delaying adult franchise for 15 years — Maulana Azad; opposed by Rajendra Prasad and Nehru
- ★ Assembly — multiparty body; worked through committees; NOT adult suffrage, NOT direct election (provincial assemblies as electoral college)
- ★ "Constituent Assembly was Congress and Congress was India" — Granville Austin, *The Indian Constitution*
- ★ "A Constitution, like a machine, is a lifeless thing…" — Dr Rajendra Prasad, 26 November 1949
