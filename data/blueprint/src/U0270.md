# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:269
### Cinema & media firsts
- Bhakta Prahlada (first Telugu talkie) — directed by H.M. Reddy (not C. Pullaiah)
- Dadasaheb Phalke Award — started 1969; first recipient Devika Rani
- Nandi Awards — started 1964 by the Government of Andhra Pradesh; films, television and drama (incl. Nandi Natakotsavam)
- NFDC runs the National Film Awards
- Doordarshan Kendra Hyderabad — 1977
- Gemini TV and ETV — both began 1995; ETV founded by Ramoji Rao (not NTR)
### Film personalities
## First Indian talkie
- ★ Alam Ara (1931) — first Indian sound film; director Ardeshir Irani
- premiered Majestic Cinema, Bombay, 14 March 1931
- ★ Raja Harishchandra (Dadasaheb Phalke) — first full-length feature film, but silent
## Telugu film-makers

- K. Viswanath — Sankarabharanam
- L.V. Prasad — Phalke Award 1982
