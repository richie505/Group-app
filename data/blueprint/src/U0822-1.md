### US Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act, 2026 — implications for India
## 1. The Act
- Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act, 2026 — signed by President Donald Trump
- Tariffs up to 100% on imports from countries buying large Russian crude/gas; ⚠ not automatic
- Applies after 30 days to top 5 importers of Russian energy (last 12 months) or top 5 sanction-evasion facilitators
- Iran sanctions extended 5 years to 2031; tariff is in addition to other duties
- Presidential waiver needs written national-interest certification + report to Congress; USTR review within 180 days
## 2. India's exposure
- Russia supplied 51%+ of India's crude imports in July 2026; oil above $100/barrel; Strait of Hormuz constrained
- US ≈ 20% of India's goods exports
- Earlier 25% punitive tariff (Executive Order) — broader regime struck down by US Supreme Court in February 2026
- Section 301 (Trade Act 1974): 10% tariff on India (forced labour probe); Section 232 (Trade Expansion Act 1962): 50% on steel, aluminium, copper
- India's exports to US: +18% (Apr–Aug 2025); slowed to 3.8% (Apr 2025–Feb 2026)
