### Guidelines and directives → points
## 1. Prakash Singh directives (2006) → 7
- State Security Commission; DGP from UPSC panel with 2-year tenure; 2-year tenure for field officers
- Separate investigation from law and order; Police Establishment Board; National Security Commission
- Police Complaints Authorities at State and district levels
## 2. Police Complaints Authority
- PCA: State PCA (retired judge) handles serious misconduct incl. custodial death, rape, grievous hurt
## 3. PUCL encounter guidelines → 16 points
- Record tip-off; register FIR; independent probe by CID or another police station; magisterial inquiry
- Inform NHRC/SHRC; medical aid; inform kin; forensic check of weapons
- No out-of-turn promotion or gallantry award until cleared
## 4. NHRC norms
- NHRC norms: Encounter deaths = serious violations → mandatory reporting, post-mortem videography, independent investigation
### Death penalty and mercy → cases and Articles
## 1. Death penalty cases
| Case [GK] | Holding |
| Jagmohan Singh v State of UP (1973) | Death penalty constitutional |
| Bachan Singh v State of Punjab (1980) | "Rarest of rare" doctrine; life is the rule, death the exception |
| Machhi Singh v State of Punjab (1983) | Categories of rarest cases; balance aggravating vs mitigating factors |
| Epuru Sudhakar v Govt of AP (2006) | Pardon/remission open to limited judicial review |
| Shatrughan Chauhan v UOI (2014) | Inordinate, unexplained delay in deciding mercy petitions = ground to commute death to life |
- Shatrughan Chauhan: Solitary confinement before rejection is illegal; 14 days between rejection and execution
## 2. Procedure and Articles
- Confirmation: Sessions Court death sentence needs High Court confirmation
- Appeal to SC: Under Arts 132, 134, 136
- Art. 72 (President): Pardon incl. death sentence and court-martial
- Art. 161 (Governor): Cannot pardon a death sentence, but can commute/remit/suspend
- BNS: IPC's capital offences now in the BNS (e.g. murder, s.103)
- ⚠ Art 72 = President can pardon death sentence; Art 161 = Governor cannot
