### Historians and court writers → origin, patron, contribution
## 1. Al-Biruni
- Origin: Khwarazm, Central Asia (present Uzbekistan); came with Mahmud of Ghazni
- Period + patron: 11th century (c. 1030 CE) under Mahmud of Ghazni (who also patronised Firdausi, Utbi)
- Work: Kitab-ul-Hind / Tahqiq-i-Hind (Tarikh al-Hind), in Arabic
- Records Diwali on Kartika Amavasya
- Scholarship: Learned Sanskrit; wrote on Indian religion, philosophy, astronomy, caste system → Indian sciences and society

- Court bias: Histories glorify the patron
- Amir Khusrau = court poet
- Barani = orthodox Sunni viewpoint; also theorised kingship
- Disagreement on Warangal invasions:
