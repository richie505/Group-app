### Thinkers and books → population theories
## 1. Thinker → theory
| Thinker | Theory / statement |
| Thomas Malthus (18th century) | Population grows geometrically (exponentially); food/resources grow only arithmetically; unchecked growth → scarcity + famine |
| Paul R. Ehrlich (neo-Malthusian) | Population Bomb; opens with "The battle to feed all of humanity is over" (APPSC 2019 key) |
- Overpopulation: Malthus = scientist whose theory says population is too large with severe consequences (APPSC key)
## 2. Book → author
| Book | Author | Year |
| An Essay on the Principle of Population | Thomas Malthus | 1798 |
| The Population Bomb | Paul R. Ehrlich | 1968 |
- Exam angle: book-author pairs and "Malthusian theory relates to population".
## 3. Malthus's checks
| Check (Malthus) | Acts on | Examples |
| Preventive | Lowers births | Moral restraint, late marriage, celibacy [GK] |
| Positive | Raises deaths | Famine, disease, war [GK] |
- ⚠ Preventive = fewer births; Positive = more deaths
## 4. Terms and criticism
- "New Population Bomb": Rapid urban growth in the Third World
- Outpaced predictions: Technology, innovation, sustainable resource management
