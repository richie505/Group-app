### Judiciary — courts, judges and penalties
| Court | Cases | Presided by |
| Dharmasthiya | Civil (marriage, contract, inheritance) | 3 dharmasthas/amatyas [GK] |
| Kantakashodhana ("removal of thorns") | Criminal | 3 pradeshtris/amatyas [GK] |
- ⚠ Dharmasthiya = civil; Kantakashodhana = criminal
- Court ladder: Village (Grama Sabha) → Sangrahana → Dronamukha → Janapada → king (final authority, supreme judge)
- Pradvivaka: Chief judicial adviser/judge (APPSC G2 2019: judicial system)
- Punishments: Severe - fines, amputation, mutilation, death
- Severity depended on varna
- No written code per Megasthenes
### Revenue terms → meaning and sources
| Term | Meaning |
| Sita | Income from crown (state) land (crown lands) |
| Bhaga | King's share of produce - normally 1/6 (one-sixth); Megasthenes: 1/4 to 1/6 |
| Bali | Additional levy/tribute |
| Pindakara | Tax assessed on groups of villages |
| Pranaya | Emergency tax ("gift of affection") |
| Vivita | Pasture land and its dues |
| Hiranya | Tax paid only in cash |
| Setu | Income from gardens, orchards, root crops (some notes: Kara on fruit/flower gardens) |
| Vana | Income from forests |
- Land revenue: Main income; Megasthenes: king owned all land, revenue collected directly by royal officials
- Other income: Ferries, forests, mines, pastures, licence fees, fines
- Lumbini: Exempted from bali; bhaga cut to one-eighth (Rummindei pillar)
- Evasion: Tax evasion on sales punishable by death (Megasthenes)
### Universal angles
- Significance: First centralised bureaucratic empire; census and standardised measures
- AP angle: Suvarnagiri (Dakshinapatha) = southern capital; governed the Deccan, Andhra in its orbit
- Comparative note: Later dynasties (Eastern Chalukyas - Ashtadasha Tirthas; Kakatiyas) used the Saptanga and 18 Tirthas model
- Criticism & debates: Arthashastra describes an ideal; how fully it worked is disputed
- Kautilya: a kingdom cannot run on one wheel
