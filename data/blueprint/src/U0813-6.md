### Andhra Pradesh Budget 2026-27
## 1. Budget profile
- Presented by: Finance Minister Payyavula Keshav
- Focus: asset creation, higher capital expenditure, fiscal consolidation
| Item | Amount |
| Total outlay | ₹3,32,205 crore |
| Revenue expenditure | ₹2,56,143 crore |
| Capital expenditure | ₹53,915 crore |
| Revenue deficit | ₹22,002 crore |
| Fiscal deficit | ₹75,868 crore |
- Presented: 14 Feb 2026 (his third Budget); total outlay ₹3.32 lakh crore (15 Feb 2026)
- Total expenditure: ₹3,10,058 crore (+12% over 2025-26 RE)
- Revenue expenditure: some notes say ₹2,56,142 crore
- ⚠ Sources differ: other notes say capital outlay / expenditure ₹48,698 crore; some notes say ₹48,697 crore (+47% over the previous year; RE 2025-26 was ₹39,498 crore)
- Revenue deficit = 1.11% of GSDP (2025-26 RE: 2.3%; also given as 2.33%)
- Fiscal deficit: 3.84% of GSDP (also given as 3.8%) (2025-26 RE: ~4.6%) - still above the 3% norm
- Interest payments: ₹37,281 crore; GSDP (current): ₹19,75,073 crore (+12%)
- ⚠ RD 1.1% / 1.11% vs FD 3.84%; capital outlay +47%, total expenditure +12%
- Current relevance: Budget 2026-27 (14 Feb 2026) cut revenue deficit to 1.11% + raised capex
- Telangana vs AP: Telangana 2026-27 Budget ₹3.24 lakh crore vs AP ₹3.32 lakh crore
- Super Six: Opposition said items (₹1,500 a month to women, unemployment allowance) got no specific allocation (15 Feb 2026)
- Exam angle: outlay (₹3.32 lakh crore) and deficit ratios (RD 1.11%, FD 3.84%) are the likely direct questions.

## 4. Telangana — rank 7
