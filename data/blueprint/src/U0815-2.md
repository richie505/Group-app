### Vijayanagara-era inscriptions found in Seshachalam forest
## 1. Discovery
- Found by: ASI epigraphy team led by K. Muniratnam Reddy (Director of Epigraphy, ASI, Mysuru)
- Where: Sadasivakona, Seshachalam forest, Tirupati district (after survey in Nallamala Hills)
- What: 3 rare inscriptions, 16th century, dated 1554 — reign of Sadasiva Deva Raya (Vijayanagara)
- Languages: Telugu, Tamil, Kannada
- Main record: Sadasiva Raya visited for a sacred bath, made royal donations; a Siva temple and a mutt built at Papavinasam
- Copied as: estampages (impressions of inscriptions)
- Related: Gudimallam Siva shrine — 2nd century BCE
- ASI: est. 1861; DG Yadubir Singh Rawat; Vijayanagara Empire est. 1336 by Harihara I and Bukka Raya I
## 2. Reddy dynasty inscription at Kondaveedu
- Found at: Kondaveedu, Palnadu district — 14th-century Reddy period, on a Shiva temple doorway pillar
- Inscription: on Rudrapadam (Adam Shafi Hill) — records two Shiva temples built in the reign of Anavema Reddy; Kondaveedu Fort — 14th-century hill fort
- Reddy Kingdom: est. 1325 by Prolaya Vema Reddy; first capital Addanki, later Kondaveedu; promoted Telugu culture and literature
- History: Vasantotsavam = most popular festival under the Reddi kings (APHQ)
