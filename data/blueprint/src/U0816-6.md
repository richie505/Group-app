### Census 2027 — first fully digital census
## 1. Key facts
- Started: 1 April 2026 — India's first fully digital census
- Number: 16th overall; 8th after Independence; after a gap of 16 years
- Budget: ₹11,718.24 crore
- Personnel: more than 3 million officials, enumerators and supervisors
- Self-enumeration: online portal in 16 languages; households get a Self-Enumeration ID
- Pre-test: November 2025
## 2. Phases and reference dates
| Item | Detail |
| Phase I | April–September 2026 — house listing, housing conditions, amenities, assets |
| Phase II | February 2027 — population, socio-economic, education, caste |
| Reference date (most of India) | 1 March 2027 |
| Reference date (snow-bound) | 1 October 2026 — Ladakh, J&K, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand |
## 3. Caste enumeration
- ⚠ First since 1931 — caste of every individual recorded (earlier only SC/ST)
- Legal basis: Section 3 of the Census Act, 1948
## 4. Background
- Law: Census Act, 1948 and Census Rules, 1990; Gazette notification by Ministry of Home Affairs
- Authority: Registrar General & Census Commissioner, India (MHA)
- Constitution: Article 246, Union List Entry 69; Articles 81 and 82 — Lok Sabha seats and delimitation based on census
- History: first census 1872; first complete census 1881; first after Independence 1951; last 2011 (121.08 crore)
- ⚠ 2021 Census postponed due to COVID-19
