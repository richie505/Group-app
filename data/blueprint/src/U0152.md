- Charter: Monopoly between Cape of Good Hope and Magellan Straits (15 years)
- 1707: Rival English companies merged into the "United Company of Merchants"
- Exam angle: APPSC-G2 2025 asked the EIC year thrice - 1600. APPSC 2024 keyed the 1600 Charter as "exclusive trading rights" - not territorial or legislative powers.

### Terms, policies and farmans → meaning
## 1. Policies — aimed to
- Blue Water Policy: Portuguese naval and trading supremacy
- Alauddin's Market Policy: To control prices — supported the maintenance of a large army
## 2. Terms → meaning
| Term | Meaning |
| Blue Water Policy | Almeida's policy - naval mastery of the Indian Ocean, not land |
| Cartaz | Naval pass system - a maritime monopoly tool |
| Caes | Convoy - a maritime monopoly tool |

- Maritime monopoly tools: Cartaz (naval pass) system, convoy (Caes), customs duties
