### Literary sources
- Matsya Purana — 30 Satavahana kings ruled for 456 years

### Ikshvaku & neighbour inscriptions
## Ikshvaku and Pallava records
- Mayidavolu plates of Pallava Sivaskandavarman — first inscriptional use of "Andhrapatha"
- Nagarjunakonda inscription — Ikshvaku record; Santamula founded the kingdom at Vijayapuri
- Manchikallu inscription — Pallava Simhavarman's conquest of Vijayapuri
- Chejerla — Ikshvaku–Anandagotrika wars
## Brihatpalayanas
- Kondamudi copper plate of Jayavarma — only record of the Brihatpalayanas (Ikshvaku feudatories)
- Jayavarma's capital — Koduru

- Last Ikshvaku ruler Rudrapurushadatta — defeated by Pallava Simhavarman
