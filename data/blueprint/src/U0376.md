### Issues → independence, accountability and overlap
- Standard safeguards for independence: Fixed tenure + selection-committee appointment + removal only on statutory grounds
- Accountability mechanisms: Annual reports + accounts laid before Parliament; examined by Departmentally Related Standing Committees

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 2:102
### Key terms
- Regulators as "mini-states" (Economic Survey 2025-26) — combine rule-making, investigation, adjudication: legislative, executive and judicial functions
- Regulatory capture — regulator serves the industry it regulates rather than the public
- Theory of regulatory capture — George Stigler (1971)
- FSLRC — headed by Justice B.N. Srikrishna; reported 2013 with a draft Indian Financial Code; area-based → task-based regulation
- Regulatory capacity is part of State capacity — because regulation is where the State meets the economy
### Independence with accountability
- Safeguards of independence — fixed tenure, selection-committee appointment, statutory grounds for removal
- Unrestricted removal by the parent ministry weakens independence
- Regulators' annual reports and accounts — laid before Parliament; examined by Departmentally Related Standing Committees
### Acts, Bills, cases
- Securities Markets Code Bill, 2025 — board members disclose direct, indirect and family interests; removal for prejudicial interest
| Body | Administered by / depends on |
| NGT | MoEFCC |
| Railway Claims Tribunal | Zonal Railways (budget) |
| AERB | Department of Atomic Energy |
- Madras Bar Association (2025) — SC gave four months to set up a National Tribunals Commission
- L. Chandra Kumar (1997) — High Court judicial review of tribunals is part of the basic structure
### Appellate structures
- Accountability measures — parliamentary reviews and published orders; management accountable to governing boards; proportionate sanctions (intent, scale, harm)
- APTEL — set up 24 August 2004 (2016 is NCLAT's year)
| Appellate body | Hears appeals from |
| SAT | SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA |
| APTEL | CERC, SERCs, PNGRB |
| NCLAT | NCLT, IBBI, CCI, NFRA |
### Capacity gaps
- Economic Survey 2025-26 — proposes a School for Regulations; India has no dedicated cadre trained in regulation
### Overlap & reform proposals
- AERB (1983, under the Atomic Energy Act, 1962) — works under the DAE via the Atomic Energy Commission
- DAE both promotes and regulates → NSRA Bill proposed
- FSDC — coordinates financial regulators; addresses overlap
- CCI penalised WhatsApp for abuse of dominance (data sharing); DPDP Act separately restricts data cross-use
- TV Ratings Policy, 2026 — made the MIB (not TRAI) sole regulator of rating agencies
- FSLRC's draft Indian Financial Code — proposed a Financial Sector Appellate Tribunal (not its abolition)
