### NAPCC — definitions, scope and FIRSTs
## 1. Release
- Launched on 30 June 2008 — India's first National Action Plan on Climate Change
- Released: by PM Dr Manmohan Singh
- FIRST: India's first overarching domestic climate policy framework; implemented through nodal ministries
- Prepared under: PM's Council on Climate Change (constituted 2007)
## 2. Scope
- Scope: both mitigation and adaptation (APPSC 2023 answer)
- ⚠ Trap: Not only GHGs, biodiversity or air pollution
- Four of the eight missions focus on adaptation
- CoP30: Belem, Brazil; India's National Statement on 17 Nov 2025

- Nuclear and Green Hydrogen missions → advance net zero by 2070
### Mission objectives → targets — GIM, NWM, PAT, NMSA
## 1. Green India Mission (GIM)
- One of the eight NAPCC missions — to protect, restore and enhance forest cover
- Responds to climate change through adaptation and mitigation
- 3 sub-missions:
- Forest quality
- Forest/tree cover and ecosystem restoration
- Incomes of forest-dependent people
- Targets:
- 50-60 Mt (MT) extra annual sequestration by 2020
- Additional sink of 2.5-3.0 bn t CO2e by 2030
- CAG audit (Aug 2026): 16 States/UTs, 2015-16 to 2024-25
| Component | Achieved | Target | Shortfall |
| Quality improvement | only 0.11384 Mha | 1.4 Mha | 91.87% shortfall |
| Cover increase | 0.03409 Mha | 1.4 Mha | 97.57% shortfall |
## 2. National Water Mission (NWM)
- 5 goals:
- Public water database
- Focus on over-exploited areas
- 20% increase in water-use efficiency = raise water use efficiency by 20% (basin-level IWRM)
- Funding: State Specific Action Plans (₹50 lakh for major States)
## 3. PAT under NMEEE
- PAT (Perform, Achieve and Trade): Market mechanism under NMEEE
- Energy-intensive units beating targets earn tradeable Energy Saving Certificates
## 4. NMSA
- NMSA: Launched 2014-15 under MoAFW
- Evolution: Green Revolution-Krishonnati (2018-19) → PM-RKVY since 2022-23 (PM-RKVY from 2022-23)
- Components: Rainfed Area Development, On-Farm Water Management, Soil Health Management + Sub-Mission on Agroforestry
- Benefits:
- Up to 55% subsidy for micro-irrigation
- Soil Health Cards issued free every two years
