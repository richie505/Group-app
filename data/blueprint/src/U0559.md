### Pattiseema and Polavaram — features and status
## 1. Pattiseema
- Type: Lift irrigation scheme (PYQ) on Godavari at Pattiseema
- Route: Lifts water into Polavaram Right Main Canal → Krishna at Prakasam Barrage = first Godavari-Krishna link
- Beneficiary: Krishna delta farmers (PYQ 2018, 2017)
- Features: 24 pumps; built 2015-16 (first water Sept 2015)
- Nature: Temporary until Polavaram's gravity flow starts
- ⚠ Trap: "canal runs 100 km through West Godavari" = the false statement in the PYQ
- Performance: ~450 TMC transferred; kept Krishna delta supplied during 2026 El Nino deficit
- Exam angle: "Pattiseema is a lift irrigation project" and "not true" statement sets on its canal and purpose.
## 2. Polavaram
- Status: National Project on Godavari
- GWDT award (1980): Permitted Polavaram diversion of 80 tmc Godavari water to Krishna
- Polavaram status: 88% civil works, 93% land acquisition, 45% R&R done; left-canal water via Purushothapatnam lift from July (May 2026)
- Jalayagnam: Polavaram (started 2005) still a Jalayagnam project

### Schemes and programmes → aim and data — APMIP and micro-irrigation
## 1. APMIP and micro-irrigation
- Launch: APMIP in 2003; now run as RKVY-PDMC
- Coverage: 37.90 lakh acres + 13.49 lakh farmers since inception; 75% drip, 25% sprinkler
- Recent: 1.90 lakh ha covered Jun 2024-Dec 2025
- Subsidy: 100% for SC/ST
- Targets: Micro-irrigation on 35% of gross sown area by 2030; 50% by 2047
- Potential: Saturation could raise groundwater-based net irrigation to 40 lakh acres
- Not in your sources: APMIP drip and sprinkler area and AP's national rank.
- Per Drop More Crop (2015-16): 55% assistance to small and marginal farmers and 45% to others for drip/sprinkler
- Under RKVY since 2022-23; 102 lakh ha covered nationally since 2015-16
- Micro Irrigation Fund (NABARD, Rs 10,000 crore corpus): AP is among the eight states funded
- ⚠ Trap: PDMC 55% small/marginal vs 45% others; APMIP 100% for SC/ST
## 2. Groundwater status
- Status: Groundwater development stage ~30% (safe); 3,005 telemetry wells
- Bore-well pressure: 40,000-50,000 new bore wells added yearly → area irrigated per well falling
### Terms → meaning
| Term | Meaning |
| APFAMGS | AP Farmer Managed Groundwater Systems - FAO-supported NGO project in drought-prone districts |
| Farmer monitoring | Farmers collect own rainfall + well-level data; monitor water levels |
| Crop-water budgeting | On hydrological-unit basis; voluntary pumping regulation |
| Tanks vs canals | Tank irrigation important due to peninsular plateau's undulating relief + impervious rocks; canals dominate Godavari-Krishna deltas |
### Universal angles
- AP angle: Rayalaseema depends on Krishna-lift projects (HNSS, GNSS, Telugu Ganga) that "often run dry"
- Criticism & debates: Potential created vs utilised; Telangana's objections to Godavari links
- Current relevance:
- AP Irrigation White Paper (Jul 2026): complete 36 projects by Dec 2028 at Rs 35,555 crore; backed by Irrigation Action Calendar
- Polavaram completion before the 2027 Godavari Pushkaralu; ~200 TMC Godavari water to Nagarjunasagar ayacut
- Significance: PMKSY components = AIBP + Har Khet Ko Pani + Watershed + PDMC
- Monitoring: National Hydrology Project set up AP's real-time groundwater monitoring
