- Quota: 85,000 a year since 2004 (incl. 20,000 for US advanced degrees); lottery-based
- Indians: ~71% of H-1B recipients; ~60% earn below $100,000

### India–US corn trade dispute
## 1. Facts
- India's corn imports 2024-25: 0.97 mt — Myanmar 0.53 mt, Ukraine 0.39 mt, US only 1,100 t
- Tariff-rate quota: 0.5 mt at 15% duty; beyond that 50%
- GM ban: India bans GM corn import; ~94% of US corn (2024) is GM; NITI Aayog proposal for GM maize for ethanol withdrawn
- US: largest producer/exporter — production 377.63 mt, exports 71.70 mt (2024-25)
- China's US corn purchases: $5.21 bn (2022) → $331 mn (2024)
- Prices: US ~₹15/kg; Indian wholesale ₹22–23/kg; MSP 2025-26 ₹24/kg
- Top maize states: Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Bihar
### European nations recognise Palestine
## 1. News
- Recognised: Spain, Ireland, Norway; France signalled readiness
- Reluctant: Germany, Austria (Holocaust legacy)
- Norway: hosted Oslo Accords (1993)
- EU: Common Foreign and Security Policy needs unanimity — so recognition by individual states
- ICJ: South Africa's genocide case against Israel
- Eurobarometer 2023: 72% Europeans back upholding international law

- ~10 countries moving to recognise — Britain, France, Canada, Australia, Belgium
- PLO declared statehood in 1988; 147 of 193 UN members recognise Palestine (latest Mexico, Jan 2025)
- Governance: Palestinian Authority — parts of West Bank; Hamas runs Gaza since 2007; ~40 countries have consulates in Ramallah/East Jerusalem
### Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger withdraw from the ICC
## 1. News
- Who: military-led Burkina Faso, Mali, Niger (coups 2020–2023) — called ICC a "neo-colonial instrument"
- Grouping: Alliance of Sahel States (AES); closer to Russia, away from France
- Withdrawal effective: one year after notice to UN Secretary-General (Rome Statute)
## 2. Background — ICC
- Established: 2002; seat The Hague, Netherlands — first permanent international criminal court
- Crimes: war crimes, crimes against humanity, genocide, aggression; principle of complementarity
- Putin under ICC arrest warrant since March 2023
### Migration — skilled, Gulf labour and climate-induced
## 1. Points
- Skilled migration since 1990s (e.g. US H-1B); GCC countries host mainly temporary labour migrants
- Climate change expected to drive large forced migration; agencies — IOM, UNHCR
### Nepal eliminates rubella
## 1. News
- Declared: 18 August 2025 by WHO — Nepal eliminated rubella as a public health problem
- ⚠ Nepal first in WHO South-East Asia Region to adopt a laboratory algorithm for rubella surveillance
| Year | Step |
| 2012 | rubella vaccine campaign (children 9 months–15 years) |
| 2016 | second dose in routine immunisation |
| Early 2024 | over 95% children got at least one dose |
## 2. Background
- Rubella: German measles; can cause Congenital Rubella Syndrome (CRS) — ~1,00,000 infants a year globally
- MMR vaccine: two doses (⚠ not three)
- 2022: 17,865 rubella cases in 78 countries
- 2024: 14.3 million children missed all vaccines; DTP3 85%; measles first dose 84%
