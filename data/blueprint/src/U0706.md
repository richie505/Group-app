### Ethical concerns, threats and challenges
## 1. Ethical concerns
- Bias: From non-representative training data
- Health AI trained on urban/Western data misses India's diversity (Aug 2026)
- Deepfakes: Synthetic media made with GANs, diffusion models and large neural nets
- Privacy and surveillance: Identification without consent, mass surveillance, predictive policing threatening due process
- Job displacement: Both manual and intellectual work
- Explainability: Deep models are "black boxes" → decisions hard to justify
## 2. Challenges for India
- R&D spend: India's GERD only ~0.64-0.7% of GDP
- Hardware: GPU shortage + dependence on imported chips
