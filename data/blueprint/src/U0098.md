### Temple components and orders → terms
## 1. Basic parts
- Core: Garbhagriha (sanctum) + mandapa (hall)
- Tower: Shikhara in the north vs vimana in the south
- Axis: Vahana and dhvaja pillar set axially before the sanctum
- Nandi mandapa: Introduced in the Badami Chalukya phase at Pattadakal
- Trikutalaya: Three shrines on one plan
- Kalyana mandapa: Vijayanagara thousand-pillared hall = peak of the pillared-hall tradition
## 2. Nagara, Dravida and Vesara
| Order | Marker | Andhra-region example |
| Nagara | Curvilinear shikhara; sanctum under the tallest tower | Kalinga variant on the north coast - Mukhalingam, Simhachalam [GK] |
| Dravida | Pyramidal vimana, enclosure walls, gopurams | Eastern Chalukya delta temples; Vijayanagara temples |
| Vesara | Blend of both; stepped shikhara | Kakatiya temples (CDX) |
- Pattadakal: Nagara (Papanatha) and Dravida (Virupaksha) side by side
- ⚠ Shikhara = north (Nagara); vimana = south (Dravida); Vesara = blend (Kakatiya)
### Styles by dynasty → features and examples
| Dynasty | Style | Features | Examples |
| Eastern Chalukya | Dravidian, drawing on Pallava and Badami Chalukya forms, growing more local | Clustered in the Krishna-Godavari delta; laterite and limestone | Draksharama Bhimeswara (Chalukya Bhima I), Kumararama at Samalkot, Biccavole group; Vijayaditya II credited with 108 temples |
| Chalukya- Chola | An "independent" style joining Pallava planning to early Chalukya elements | Became the model for the Godavari region | Draksharama (APPSC 2025) |
| Kakatiya | Transitional between early Chalukyan and Vijayanagara; Chalukyan/Vesara (CDX) | Star-shaped platforms, sandbox foundations, floating bricks, ekasila basalt carving | Ramappa (1213), Thousand Pillar (1163) |
| Reddi | Renovation and endowment of older shrines; fort building | Additions and rich decoration to existing fabric | Kondaveedu fort; Draksharama, Samarlakota, Srisailam |
| Vijayanaga ra | Draws on Chalukya and Chola styles | Sahasra Stambha Mandapa (thousand-pillared hall), composite carved pillars, ornate gopurams | Virupaksha and Vithala (Hampi); Krishnadevaraya's gifts to Simhachalam and Mangalagiri |
- APPSC 2025: Rajarajeswara temple at Biccavole is not Rajaraja Narendra's (the wrong statement)
- Correct: Vijayaditya II (108 temples); Chalukya Bhima I (Draksharama)
## Building materials
| Material | Dynasty / region | Example |

### Temple sculpture — features
## 1. Ramappa (Palampet)
- Madanika bracket figures: Carved in dance postures from Jayapa Senani's Nrittaratnavali (APPSC 2019)
- Treatise covers marga and desi dance
- UNESCO: World Heritage Site 2021
## 2. Later traditions
- Vijayanagara: Composite pillars carry dense iconography
- Painting: Kakatiya painting (Machaladevi) led on to the Lepakshi style
- Exam angle: treatise → temple → author matching (Nrittaratnavali - Jayapa - Ramappa).
