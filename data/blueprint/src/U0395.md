### Student and youth movements → FIRSTs, state and issue
## 1. FIRSTs in youth movements
- Swadeshi Movement: The first student-led national movement — 1905
- Young Bengal Movement: The first youth organisation of India — founded by Henry Louis Vivian Derozio, founder of the first youth reform movement
- JP Movement of 1974: The first nationwide student mobilisation after Independence — Jayaprakash Narayan led the first major post-Independence youth movement
- India Against Corruption Movement: The first major anti-corruption youth movement in modern India — 2011
## 2. Movements → state and issue
| Movement | State | Issue / outcome |
| Nav Nirman (1974) | Gujarat | Students against corruption and prices; CM Chimanbhai Patel resigned (Feb 1974) (UPSC) |
| JP movement (1974) | Bihar | Student agitations; fed into the Emergency (June 1975) (UPSC) |
| AASU (1979-85) | Assam | Against illegal migrants; Assam Accord 1985[GK] |
| Anti-Mandal (1990) | North India | Against 27% OBC reservation [GK] |
| Telangana students (1969; 2009-14) | Osmania University | Separate statehood [GK] |
- Others: Anna Hazare movement, Nirbhaya protests, Patidar (2015), Jallikattu (2016-17)
## 3. Andhra Pradesh agitations

### National Youth Policy → FIRSTs and NYP 2014
