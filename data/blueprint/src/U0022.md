### Four Vedas — content, priests, Upavedas and FIRSTs
## 1. The four Vedas
| Veda | Association | Content | Priest | Upaveda [GK] |
| Rigveda | The oldest Veda — contains hymns | Hymns of praise | Hotri (reciter) | Ayurveda = medicine |
| Yajurveda | Rituals — sacrificial formulas and procedures | Ritual formulas, prose manual; Shukla (White) and Krishna (Black) | Adhvaryu (executor) | Dhanurveda = warfare |

## 2. Vedanta
- A philosophical system — literally Vedic philosophy; the end of the Vedas or the climax of Vedic ideology
- Prasthanatrayi: The foundations of Vedanta — the Upanishads, Brahma Sutra and Bhagavad Gita
- The Prime Prasthan: The Upanishads; the Bhagavata Prasthan is also associated with Vedanta
## 3. Upanishads — facts
- Number: 108 Upanishads; about 13 principal
- Date: Composed c. 800-600 BCE
- Name: Also called Vedanta (end of the Veda)
- Teaching: Anti-ritual/anti-ritualistic; Atman = Brahman (atman-brahman identity); Karma, Samsara, Moksha
- vs Puranas: Upanishads = Shruti; Puranas = Smriti (later, legend-based)
