### National Social Assistance Programme — components and coverage
## 1. Basics
- Launch: 1995 (15 Aug); Ministry of Rural Development (MoRD); Centrally Sponsored
- Basis: Fulfils Article 41; for BPL households
- Aim: Old age pension to the very poor (UPPSC 2008)
- Status: Core of Core scheme since 2016
| NSAP component | Target / benefit |
| IGNOAPS | ₹200/month (60-79 yrs), ₹500 (80+) |
| IGNDPS | Severely disabled (BPL), ages 18-79 with 80%+ disability: ₹300/month (Rs 300) |
| Annapurna | Free foodgrains to needy elderly not getting IGNOAPS (10 kg/month) |
## 2. Coverage and delivery
- Maternity: National Maternity Benefit Scheme moved to MoHFW in 2001
- Scale: About 3.09 crore beneficiaries; ₹9,652 crore for FY 2025-26
- State top-up: ₹50-3,200/month
- Apps: SAMBAL app + Digital Life Certificate app
### Universal angles
- Current relevance: NSAP old-age pension lost nearly half its real value to inflation (May 2026)
- Criticism & debates: Kerala's move to end doorstep pension delivery for DBT drew protests (8 and 11 Aug 2026) - DBT vs doorstep delivery for elderly
- Significance:
- 24.8 crore people escaped multidimensional poverty in a decade (Aug 2026)
- Old-age share to rise 10.1% to 14.9% (2021-2036) → higher pension needs
