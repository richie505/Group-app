### SPCBs and APPCB — functions
- SPCB functions: Inspect treatment plants, analyse samples, enforce standards, grant consents
- Consent to Establish (before setting up) + Consent to Operate (before production/discharge) under Water and Air Acts
- Can levy compensation under S.33A Water / S.31A Air Act where harm is clear or imminent (Aug 2025)
- APPCB:
- Set up 1976 by AP Government under Water Act 1974
- Body entrusted to implement environmental laws and rules in AP (APPSC 2025 answer)
- Enforces Water, Air, EP Acts + Hazardous, Bio-medical, Plastic, Solid waste rules

### Acts → NGT (predecessors, Acts covered, exclusions)
## 1. Predecessor laws
- National Environment Appellate Authority (NEAA): Established under the NEAA Act, 1997 — dealt with appeals on environmental clearances and restrictions
- Dissolved after the NGT was established — no longer exists in its original form; its role taken over by the NGT
| Law | Purpose | Fate |
| National Environment Tribunal Act, 1995 (APPSC 2023 answer) | Strict liability for damage from hazardous-substance accidents [GK] | Never brought into force; repealed by NGT Act 2010 [GK] |
| National Environment Appellate Authority Act, 1997 | Appeals against environmental clearances [GK] | NEAA replaced by NGT; Act repealed 2010 [GK] |
- NGT role: Combines original jurisdiction over environmental disputes + the appellate role the NEAA had

### National Green Tribunal — establishment, procedure, benches — Establishment and nature
## 1. Establishment and nature
- Established on 18 October 2010 under the NGT Act, 2010; statutory (not constitutional) body
- Purpose: Speedy disposal of environmental cases — environmental justice; reduces the litigation burden on higher courts
- Covers: Environmental matters, forests (forest conservation), natural resources
- Constitutional anchor: Article 21 (Art. 21) — the right to a healthy environment
- First Chairperson: Justice Lokeshwar Singh Panta
- Dates: Act passed 2 June 2010; Tribunal set up 18 Oct 2010; started functioning July 2011
- Purpose in the Act: Effective and expeditious disposal of cases on environmental protection and conservation of forests and natural resources; enforcing environmental legal rights; relief and compensation for damage
- i.e., to handle environmental protection and pollution-control cases (APPSC-G2 2025 answer)
### National Green Tribunal — establishment, procedure, benches — Procedure and composition
## 1. Procedure and composition
- Procedure: Not bound by CPC 1908; guided by principles of natural justice; powers of a civil court
- Appeal: To Supreme Court within 90 days
| Bench type | Location (IYB 2026) |
| Principal Bench | New Delhi |
| Zonal Benches | Pune, Kolkata, Bhopal, Chennai |
| Circuit Benches | Shimla, Shillong, Jodhpur |
- Composition: Chairperson + Judicial and Expert Members (each 10 to 20)
- Chairperson: retired SC judge; Act allows a judge of the SC or Chief Justice of a High Court
- Current: Justice Prakash Shrivastava (8 Sep 2026)
- NGT vs CPCB: NGT adjudicates disputes and awards compensation; CPCB = regulator that sets standards, monitors, advises the Centre
- Cases in 2026: Dhanauri wetland, Ganga pollution, Saparnika river (Jun 2026)
- ⚠ Act 2 June 2010 → set up 18 Oct 2010 → functioning July 2011; disposal 6 months; appeal 90 days
- Exam angle: APPSC 2018 asked for the date it was set up (18 October 2010). APPSC 2025 asked its purpose (environmental and pollution-control cases).
