### Viswabharath Allamsetti — first Telugu to finish UTMB
## 1. News
- Runner: Viswabharath Allamsetti, born Guntur, lives in France — ⚠ first Telugu runner to complete Ultra Trail du Mont Blanc (UTMB)
- Time: 44 hours
- Race: 176.5 km loop around Mont Blanc massif, elevation gain over 10,000 m; through France, Italy, Switzerland, ends at Chamonix, France; 2025 edition began 29 August
### World Transplant Games 2025, Dresden
## 1. News
- Venue: Dresden, Germany — ~2,500 transplant recipients, 17 sports, 51 countries
- Silver: Forest Section Officer Nagavasu Naidu Sunkara (Jangareddygudem Forest Division) — 5 km racewalking, organ recipient category
- liver disease 2015; liver transplant 2020 (mother donor); switched from cycling to racewalking
- Bronze: Raj Sarki (33, Jaigaon, Dooars) — running event, kidney transplant
- Ambassador: Ivan Klasnic (ex-Werder Bremen, three-time kidney recipient)
- Games: biennial, by World Transplant Games Federation
- ⚠ Silver in racewalking (not gold, not cycling); held in Germany (not Hyderabad); source names his state as Telangana
