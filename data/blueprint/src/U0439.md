### Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY → launch, funding, cover, eligibility
## 1. Launch & funding
- Launch: September 2018 at Ranchi (Sept 2018, UPSC notes); earlier = National Health Protection Scheme
- Subsumed: RSBY (2008)
- Ministry: MoHFW; centrally sponsored
- Funding pattern: 60:40; NE + Himalayan = 90:10
- Allocation 2026-27: ₹9,500 crore
- Implementation: National Health Authority (NHA) [GK]; State Health Agencies in States (G2-GS 2024 key)
## 2. Cover & eligibility
- Cover: ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary + tertiary hospitalisation
- The largest publicly funded health assurance scheme in the world (APP)
- Eligibility: SECC 2011 deprivation criteria (6 rural, 11 urban occupational)
- Covers bottom 40% (~10.74 crore families)
- Ayushman Bharat target: Poor and vulnerable, rural and urban
- Target group: rural and poor households (G2 2025 key); APPSC 2025 key: "rural and poor populations"
- ⚠ SECC criteria: 6 rural, 11 urban

### Social security terms → meaning; ILO Convention 102
| Social insurance | Social assistance |
| Contributory, tied to employment and risks (EPF, ESI, APY, PM-SYM) | Non-contributory, tax-funded and means-tested (NSAP, NFSA, PM-JAY) |
- Proposed model (Aug 2026): "contributory + tax-funded" model + Universal Social Protection Floor (health, maternity, disability, old-age, basic income)
- ILO Convention No. 102: Social Security (Minimum Standards) Convention, 1952
- Minimum standards for nine branches: medical care, sickness, unemployment, old age, employment injury, family, maternity, invalidity, survivors
- India has not ratified it

- UPS (2025): 50% of last 12 months' average basic pay after 25 years; minimum ₹10,000

### EPF and ESI → coverage, wage ceiling, contribution rates
## 1. EPF
- Contribution: 12% of wages from employee and employer (10% for notified establishments) (4 Jul 2026)
- Coverage: Establishments with 20+ employees
- EPF Scheme, 2026: Replaced EPF Scheme 1952 under Code on Social Security
- Voluntary contributions above ceiling allowed
- Principal employer liable for contract workers' PF
- Aadhaar + PAN + UAN linking mandatory
- Wage ceiling: Raised from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 a month (Cabinet, 17 Sep 2026)
- ₹15,000 ceiling dated from Sep 2014
- Benefits: PF + EPS pension + EDLI insurance
## 2. ESI
- ESI (ESI Act 1948): Ceiling ₹21,000 a month
- Employer 3.25%, employee 0.75%
- Benefits: medical, sickness, maternity, disablement, dependants'
- SPREE (Jul 2025-Jan 2026): Added 1.03 crore employees (17 Jan 2026)
- ⚠ EPF ceiling ₹25,000 (was ₹15,000); ESI ceiling ₹21,000
### Code on Social Security 2020 → gig workers, e-Shram
## 1. The Code
- Merger: 9 laws (EPF, ESI, Maternity Benefit, Gratuity, Unorganised Workers' Social Security Act, etc.)
- In force: Four Labour Codes from 21 Nov 2025, with 2026 Central Rules
- Gig & platform workers: First legal recognition of gig workers, platform workers and aggregators
- Aggregators pay 1-2% of annual turnover (capped at 5% of payouts) into social security fund
- National Social Security Board recommends schemes (Jul 2025)
- Maternity & creche: Maternity leave stays 26 weeks; creche required at 50+ employees
## 2. e-Shram
- Launch: 26 Aug 2021, MoLE
- Database: Aadhaar-verified National Database of Unorganised Workers; 12-digit UAN
- Eligibility: Age 16-59; excludes income-tax payers + EPFO/ESIC members
- One-Stop Solution (2024): Joins up 15 schemes
- Registrations: 31.87 crore (26 Aug and 16 Aug 2026)
