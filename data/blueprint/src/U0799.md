### Agrochemicals — impacts, restricted pesticides and remedies
## 1. Fertilizers — environmental impact
- Excess nitrogen fertilizers: Soil acidification; nitrate leaching to groundwater; eutrophication
- Continuous chemical fertilizer use: Decline in soil organic matter; reduced microbial activity; lower nutrient density in food
- Solutions: Organic manure, vermicompost, biofertilizers; neem-coated urea — slows fertilizer release
## 2. Pesticides → class → examples
| Class | Examples / facts |
| Insecticides — against mosquitoes; in agriculture | DDT; BHC (benzene hexachloride); allethrin and prallethrin (mosquito repellents); Sevin; Bt |
| Fungicides — crop protection; water tanks | Bordeaux mixture (CuSO₄ + Ca(OH)₂); blue vitriol (CuSO₄); chloropicrin |
| Herbicide | 2,4-D — against broadleaf weeds |
| Rodenticide | Zinc phosphide — rat poison |
- Chloropicrin: Broad-spectrum — a fungicide, herbicide, insecticide and tear gas
## 3. Agrochemical impacts, restricted pesticides and remedies
- Contamination (PYQ): Synthetic fertilisers and pesticides cause soil and water contamination
- Indiscriminate use: → Secondary pest outbreaks, pesticide resistance + ecosystem contamination
- Nitrogen loss: Broadcasting granular fertiliser loses 50-70% of applied nitrogen as ammonia or nitrate leaching into aquifers
- Food chain: Pesticide residues bioaccumulate
| Pesticide | Action |
| Carbosulfan | Centre proposed banning - breaks down to highly toxic Carbofuran; harms birds, aquatic life (29 Aug 2026); recommended by Registration Committee under the Insecticides Act 1968 |
- Remedies:
- Neem-coated urea
- PM-PRANAM - states keep 50% of saved fertiliser subsidy as grants
- ⚠ Carbosulfan (proposed ban) → breaks down to Carbofuran
### Fertilizers → effect; green manure and organic manures

## 2. Green manure
- A cover crop grown and ploughed in while green or just after flowering
- Plants: Mainly legumes — clover, vetch, sesbania
- Benefits: Adds organic matter and humus; fixes atmospheric nitrogen through root nodules; improves soil structure, water retention and microbial activity
## 3. Organic manures and amendments
| Input | Composition | Benefit |
| Vermicompost | Organic waste digested by earthworms (e.g. Eisenia fetida [GK]) | Restores soil microbes and organic carbon (CDI) |
| Farmyard manure | Dung, urine and litter of farm animals [GK] | Slow-release nutrients, better soil structure [GK] |
| Compost | Decomposed crop residues and organic wastes | Recycles nutrients; AP plans 1,500 MT/day manure from 107 waste-processing centres (APPCA Feb 2026) |
| Green manure | Leguminous crops ploughed in green - sunn hemp, dhaincha | Adds nitrogen and organic matter |
| Biochar | Charcoal from pyrolysis of biomass [GK] | Long-term carbon sequestration, water and nutrient retention [GK] |
