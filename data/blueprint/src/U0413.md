### Nutrition missions → launch, funding and targets
## 1. Saksham Anganwadi and Poshan 2.0
- Basics: 2021; MWCD; CSS; period 2021-22 to 2025-26
- Merges: Anganwadi Services (SNP), Poshan Abhiyaan and the Scheme for Adolescent Girls (14-18, Aspirational Districts and NER)
- Funding:
- General = 60:40
- NE/Himalayan + J&K = 90:10
- UTs without legislature = 100%
- Three verticals: Nutrition support (incl. adolescent girls); ECCE (3-6 yrs); Anganwadi infrastructure
- Upgrades: 2 lakh AWCs as Saksham Anganwadis at 40,000 a year (Dec 2025)
- Co-location guidelines: On 3 Sep 2025 MWCD issued guidelines to co-locate AWCs with schools - over 2.9 lakh AWCs now in school premises
## 2. One Nation One Anganwadi
- Purpose: Migrant families moving to another State keep ICDS benefits for children below 6 + pregnant/lactating women (APPSC 2023; G2-GS 2023 key)
- Access: Services open on demand; child Aadhaar not mandatory
## 3. POSHAN Abhiyaan
- Full form: Prime Minister's Overarching Scheme for Holistic Nourishment; earlier National Nutrition Mission
- Launch: 2018 - on 8 March 2018 at Jhunjhunu, Rajasthan
- Goal: Suposhit Bharat, a Jan Andolan
- Targets: Reduce stunting, undernutrition, anaemia and low birth weight (also underweight)
- Mission goal: cut stunting to 25% by 2022
- ⚠ POSHAN Abhiyaan = Jhunjhunu 2018; BBBP = Panipat 2015

### Mid-day meal to PM POSHAN → origin, norms and features — Aims and features
## 1. Aims and features
- Aims: Better nutrition; address classroom hunger; raise enrolment, attendance, retention and concentration of disadvantaged children
- Features:
- One hot cooked meal
- Tithi Bhojan: Community contributes meals on special days; first introduced by Gujarat
- School nutrition gardens
- Mandatory social audit in every district
- Extra nutrition in aspirational and high-anaemia districts
- FPO/SHG participation

### Maternal and girl-child schemes → launch, ministry and benefit — Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP)
## 1. Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP)
- Launch: 22 January 2015 at Panipat
- Ministries: MWCD nodal (with Health and Education)
- Funding: 100% central funding; covers all districts
- Current home: Runs under Sambal (Mission Shakti)
- Aim: Reverse falling Child Sex Ratio; secure girl child's survival and education
- Design: No DBT and no asset creation; behaviour-change campaign; district funds linked to Sex Ratio at Birth
## 2. Other girl-child schemes

| Balika Samriddhi Yojana | 1997 | Cash grant at birth + scholarships for girls of BPL families |

### Child marriage → Act, data and models
## 1. Legal framework
- Prohibition of Child Marriage Act, 2006:
- Minimum age = 18 (girls) / 21 (boys)
- Punishment: up to 2 years RI + fine up to Rs 1 lakh for adult male contracting, conducting or promoting it
- Child marriage = voidable at the option of the minor party
- Child Marriage Prohibition Officers: Appointed by State Governments
- Amendment Bill 2021: Raise women's age to 21; sent to standing committee, lapsed with 17th Lok Sabha
## 2. Data
- NFHS-5: 23.3% of women aged 20-24 married before 18
## 3. Models and campaigns → place
| Initiative | Fact |
| ALIVA | Data-driven model to end child marriage in Nayagarh district, Odisha (APPSC-G2 2025) |
| '100-Day Bal Vivah Mukt Bharat' | Launched by MWCD in December 2025 |
