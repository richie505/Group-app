## 2. Naxalbari - slogans and causes
- Slogan: "Land to the tiller"

- Failed land reforms (zamindari abolition, ceilings, tenancy)
## 3. Srikakulam - nature, tribes, party, outcome
- Nature: Tribal-peasant uprising in the Srikakulam Agency vs landlords, moneylenders and forest officials
- Tribes: Savaras (Soras) and Jatapus
- Party:
- Started under the CPI(M) (PYQ key for the "1967 Srikakulam uprising")
- Shifted to the CPI(ML) in 1969
- Outcome: Suppressed by the State, but seeded the Naxalite movement and revolutionary literature in Andhra
- ⚠ Srikakulam began under the CPI(M), shifted to the CPI(ML) in 1969

### State responses → year and key fact (1975-2014)
## 1. Emergency encounters and Bhargava Commission
- Emergency (1975-77): Many Naxalite cadres killed in alleged "encounters" in AP
- Bhargava Commission: Justice Vashist Bhargava, 1977 - inquired into these encounter killings
- Not in your sources: the Bhargava Commission's findings.
## 2. Response → key fact
| Response | Key fact |
| Greyhounds | Elite anti-Naxal special police force of AP Police (a police special forces unit, not a dog squad or disaster unit), raised in 1989[GK] |
| Ban | PWG banned in AP in 1992[GK] |
| Surrender and intelligence-led policing | Under DGP H.J. Dora (1996-2002), Greyhounds neutralised several top Naxalite leaders and secured the surrender of over 1,200 cadres (1998-2001) (TH, 14 Mar 2026) |
| Peace talks | Government-Maoist talks in Hyderabad in October 2004 (YSR government) [GK] |
- Greyhounds Training Centre (post-bifurcation): Built at Anandapuram (near Visakhapatnam) under Sec. 9 of the AP Reorganisation Act, 2014
- Credited with sharply reducing Naxal activity in Alluri Sitharama Raju and Parvathipuram Manyam districts along the Odisha-Chhattisgarh borders
### Universal angles
- Significance: Srikakulam made AP a core base of Indian Maoism; via Virasam and Jana Natya Mandali it reshaped Telugu literature
- AP angle: Greyhounds base at Anandapuram secures the ASR and Parvathipuram Manyam LWE corridor
