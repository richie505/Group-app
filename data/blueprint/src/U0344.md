### Finance Commission — nature, Articles and the first FC
## 1. Nature
- Constitutional body under Article 280 — Part XII; not a permanent body — constituted every five years (or earlier); an independent, quasi-judicial body — an anchor of fiscal federalism
- Prep: Quasi-judicial constitutional body
- Constituted by the President every five years or earlier when necessary — ⚠ not strictly every five years (Art. 280: President every fifth year or earlier)
- First Finance Commission: Constituted under a Presidential order in 1951; set up on 6 April 1952 — Chairman K.C. Neogy (1952 to 1957)
- Terms of reference: 16th FC's came straight from the Constitution, without detailed Central directives
## 2. Articles
| Article | Provision |
| Article 280 | Constituted by the President every fifth year or earlier |
| Art. 281 | President lays recommendations + explanatory memorandum on action taken before each House of Parliament |
| Article 290 | Pensions [GK] - not the FC |
- ⚠ Trap: APPSC-G2 2025 incorrect statement - "Article 290 provides for the FC" (it is Art. 280; Art. 290 deals with pensions [GK]).
### Finance Commission — composition, qualifications and status
## 1. Composition — Article 280(1) and (2)
- Five members — one Chairman + four other members — appointed by the President
- Art. 280(1): Chairman + 4 members (Chairman and 4 other members), appointed by the President
- Qualifications: Determined by Parliament by law — Article 280(2) — ⚠ not by the President or the Union Cabinet
- Act: Qualifications fixed by Parliament in the Finance Commission (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1951 (APP calls it the Finance Commission Act, 1951)
- Powers of a civil court: Summoning of persons and production of documents — a quasi-judicial character
- Term: As the President specifies; eligible for reappointment
## 2. Qualifications - FC (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1951
| Post | Qualification |
| Chairman | Experience in public affairs |
| Member | High Court judge or qualified to be one |
| Member | Expert in government finance and accounts |
| Member | Wide experience in financial matters and administration |
| Member | Special knowledge of economics |
## 3. Status of recommendations
- Status: Advisory, not binding
- P.V. Rajamannar (4th FC): should not be rejected without very compelling reasons
