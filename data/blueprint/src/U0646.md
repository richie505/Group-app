### Terms → meaning: density measures, BIMARU, population-resource regions
## 1. Density measures
| Concept | Formula |
| Arithmetic density | Total population / total area |
| Physiological density | Total population / net cultivated area |
| Agricultural density | Agricultural population / net cultivable area |
- Density change:
- Up = natality + immigration
- Down = mortality + emigration
- ⚠ Physiological = all people / net cultivated area; agricultural = farm people / net cultivable area
## 2. BIMARU
- Coined by: Demographer Ashish Bose, mid-1980s (APPSC-GS 2019 key: 1986)
- Delimitation debate: UP, Bihar, MP, Rajasthan grew faster than Kerala, TN, Karnataka, AP
## 3. Population-resource regions
- Classification (population vs resources):
- progressive (resource-developed) regions
- problem regions → population pressure on limited/degraded resources
- deficit regions → resources exceed use
- UPPSC 2010 key: Ganga-Yamuna Doab and Tamil Nadu = progressive population-resource areas
- ⚠ Trap: Not coastal Odisha or Rajasthan
### Factors and places → uneven distribution in India
## 1. Factors
| Factor | Dense | Sparse |
| Water | River valleys | Thar desert |
| Soil | Loamy alluvium | Thin, rocky soils |
- Exception: Himalayan valleys (Kashmir, parts of HP) have higher density
## 2. Places of high density
- Fertile alluvial plains and deltas: Ganga plain, Ganges-Brahmaputra delta (among densest on Earth), coastal deltas (Godavari-Krishna) → intensive rice farming
- Dhanbad: Most densely inhabited district of Chotanagpur plateau
- Reason: mining industries and industrialisation (UPPSC 2007 key); coal (BCCL) based there
