## 2. The three kingdoms
- Collective name: Muvendar (three crowned kings)
| Kingdom | Capital | Port | Emblem | Heartland river |
| Chera | Vanji (Karur / Karuvur) | Muziris (Musiri), Tondi | Bow (and arrow) | Periyar [GK] |
| Chola | Uraiyur, later Puhar | Kaveripattinam (Puhar) | Tiger | Kaveri |
| Pandya | Madurai | Korkai | Fish | Vaigai[GK] |
- Territories:
- Chera = central/north Kerala + Kongu
- Chola = Kaveri delta (Tiruchi) up to southern Andhra
- Pandya = southern Tamil Nadu
- Pandya river: Vaigai
- ⚠ Trap: UPPSC 2010 keyed the Pandya "lifeline" as option "Vengi"
- Chera-Rome link: Traded with Rome; said to have built a temple of Augustus near Muziris
- Odd one out: Pallavas were not a Sangam dynasty → rose from the 4th c. CE (UPPSC 2010)

## 2. Kallanai and water works
- Kallanai (Grand Anicut): Built across the Kaveri, attributed to Karikala Chola, with irrigation canals
- Aug 2026 find (15 Aug 2026): Remains of an ancient Sangam-period water-management structure in the Kollidam river
## 3. Pattini cult
- Pattini cult: Founded by Chera Senguttuvan
- Worship of Kannagi as the ideal wife
- Idol stone brought from the Himalayas (Himalayan expedition)
- Consecration: Sri Lankan king Gajabahu said to have attended
## 4. Decline
- Decline: Pandya power waned under the Kalabhra invasion

## Dynasties
- ★ Sangam literature mentions Chola, Chera, Pandya; Kadamba not mentioned
- ★ Pallava — not a ruling dynasty of the Sangam Age
- ★ Udiyanjiral — Chera; first historical king of the Sangam period; legend: fed the fighters of the Mahabharata war

## Trade & ports
- ★ Cheras — first to trade with the Roman empire; Roman objects at Arikamedu
- ★ Western-coast ports: Tondi and Mushiri (Korkai, Puhar not western); port list in the Periplus
- ★ Pandya king sent an ambassador to Rome in 26 BC; capital Madurai
- ★ Korkai, Poompuhar, Muchiri — ports; Poompuhar (Kaveripattinam) also early Chola capital
