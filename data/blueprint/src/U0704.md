### E-commerce — models and ONDC
| Model | Who trades with whom |
| B2B | Business to business (wholesale, IndiaMART) [GK] |
| B2C | Business to consumer [GK] |
| C2C | Consumer to consumer (OLX-type resale) [GK] |
| D2C | Brand sells directly to the consumer, no middleman [GK] |
- ONDC: Open, network-based protocol, not a platform
- Connects any buyer app to any seller across B2C and B2B; helps MSMEs
- Beta-tested in Bengaluru
- Not in your sources: the current FDI rule for e-commerce. Textbook position: 100% FDI under the automatic route in the marketplace model , no FDI in the inventory model; verify against the latest FDI policy.
### Acts and rules on digital platforms → year and provision
| Act / rules | Year | Provision |
| Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 | 2020 | Under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019; cover marketplace and inventory entities; require a grievance officer + display of the country of origin |
| IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 | 2021 | Framed under Section 87 of the IT Act; replaced the 2011 guidelines; regulate social media, OTT and digital news; Code of Ethics + three-tier grievance redressal system |
| IT Amendment Rules, 2023 | 2023 | Platforms ignoring government orders on false content lose safe harbour (Sec 79); amended again in Feb 2026 |
| Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 | 2025 | Banned real-money gaming |
| Code on Social Security, 2020 | 2020 | Gig and platform workers defined for the first time; aggregators contribute to their social-security fund |
- Online Gaming Act details:
- Rules notified Apr 2026; effective 1 May 2026
- Created Online Gaming Authority of India (OGAI) - digital attached office of MeitY
- Also called the "Online Gaming (Regulation) Act, 2025" in some summaries
- Not in your sources: the current rollout status of the Code on Social Security.
