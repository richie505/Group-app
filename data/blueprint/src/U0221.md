# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:220
### AITUC
## Founding (1920)
- ★ AITUC founded at Bombay in 1920 (31 October 1920)
- ★ First session chaired by Lala Lajpat Rai
- Diwan Chaman Lal — first General Secretary
- N.M. Joshi — co-founder, later General Secretary
## Later unions & splits
- AITUC moderates broke away in 1929 → RTUC, later the National Trades Union Federation
- INTUC — set up by the Congress in 1947
- Madras Labour Union — founded by B.P. Wadia
- S.A. Dange and communists — Girni Kamgar Union
- India's first May Day — Madras, 1923, by the Labour Kisan Party of Hindustan under M. Singaravelu Chettiar
## Nehru and socialism
- ★ First All India Socialist Youth Congress — Calcutta, 27 December 1928, chaired by Jawaharlal Nehru
- ★ "I am a socialist and a republican and do not believe in Kings and Princes" — Jawaharlal Nehru
### Labour laws
- Chronology: Bombay factory commission (1875) → first Factories Act (1881), limiting child labour → Factories Act (1891), weekly holiday and limited women's working hours
- Trade Unions Act, 1926 — from N.M. Joshi's March 1921 resolution; legal status for registered unions; shield from conspiracy cases
- ★ Trade Disputes Act, 1929 (passed 11 April 1929) — tribunals to settle disputes and a ban on strikes that "coerced" the Government or caused hardship
- ★ On trial for five years; Board of Conciliation and Court of Inquiry
- Banned sympathetic strikes, long notice periods
- Motilal Nehru called the 1929 Act the 'Slavery of India Bill'
### Communist Party of India
## Founding
- ★ CPI founded at Tashkent, October 1920 (17 October) — headed by M.N. Roy
- ★ With Abani Mukherjee, Mohd. Ali, Mohd. Shafique, Evelyn Roy Trent, Rosa Fitingov, M.P.T. Acharya
- CPI formally founded in India at Kanpur, 26 December 1925
- ★ CPI and RSS both founded in 1925 (RSS: 27 September 1925, Nagpur, K.B. Hedgewar)
- ★ Revolutionary Communist Party of India — Saumyendra Nath Tagore, 1934, broke away from the CPI
- CPI split in 1964 → CPI and CPI (Marxist)
## Conspiracy cases & bans
- ★ Kanpur Conspiracy Case (1924) — against leaders of the Communist movement (M.N. Roy, Muzaffar Ahmed, S.A. Dange, Shaukat Usmani, Singaravelu Chettiar, Nalini Gupta and others)
- ★ M.A. Ansari was not associated with it
- Meerut Conspiracy Case (1929) — 31 labour and communist leaders tried (Dange, Muzaffar Ahmad, Usmani, Philip Spratt)
- CPI banned 23 July 1934; ban lifted in 1942
- Hyderabad State banned the CPI in 1946
## Other
- Ahmedabad Congress (1921) — complete independence proposed by Hasrat Mohani and Swami Kumarananda
### Congress Socialist Party (1934)
## Founding of the CSP
- ★ CSP founded at Patna, 1934 (May 1934, during the Congress Committee meeting); policies settled at Bombay, October 1934
- ★ Founders: Acharya Narendra Dev, Jayaprakash Narayan, Ram Manohar Lohia and others
- ★ Acharya Narendra Dev — President; Jayaprakash Narayan — General Secretary
- ★ Jayaprakash Narayan — Convenor at Patna, 1934; Ashok Mehta among founder members
- Lohia — founder, not General Secretary
## Andhra unit
- Andhra CSP founded at Vijayawada, 23 June 1934
- First conference — Guntur, 1935, under Tenneti Viswanatham
- First President (APPSC 2019 key) — Madduri Annapurnaiah; other sources give N.G. Ranga as President with Annapurnayya as secretary
- Madduri Annapurnayya — led the Forward Bloc Andhra branch
## Communists
- Communist Party backed the "People's War" line from June 1941
