### Decentralisation and devolution — terms → meaning
| Term | Meaning |
| Democratic decentralisation (Balwant Rai Mehta's term) | Power moves to elected local bodies |
| Administrative delegation or deconcentration | Work moves to field officers who stay inside the bureaucracy |
| Vertical devolution | Share of the divisible pool (Art. 270 taxes, excluding cesses and surcharges) going to states |
| Horizontal devolution | Allocation of that share among states by a formula |
| Vertical fiscal imbalance | Centre has the more buoyant taxes; states carry heavier spending duties (health, education, agriculture, police) |
- Order of depth: Deconcentration → delegation → devolution
- ⚠ Divisible pool excludes cesses and surcharges
### Articles → local body powers and finances
| Article | Provision |
| 243G | Eleventh Schedule (29 subjects) is only a menu: Art. 243G says "may" |
| 243H | Own taxes, duties, tolls, fees only if the State Legislature authorises (Art. 243H, "may") |
| 243I | SFC (243I); grants-in-aid from the State Consolidated Fund on SFC advice |
| 243J / 243Z | State legislature provides for maintenance and audit of LB accounts (Art. 243J / 243Z) |
| 243K | State Election Commission (243K) |
| 243X | Art. 243X = municipal equivalent of 243H |
| 270 | Taxes forming the divisible pool |
| 280 | Union FC grants; Central FC grants routed through states |
## 73rd Amendment — mandatory features
- Constitutional status, three tiers, direct elections, 5-year term, reservation, State Election Commission (243K), SFC (243I)
- ⚠ 29 subjects of the Eleventh Schedule are not mandatory ("may")
- Taxing power: No independent taxing power under the Constitution

### Horizontal devolution — criteria weights, 13th to 16th FC, and AP — 16th FC changes
## 1. 16th FC changes
- Income distance: from the average of the top three large States' per capita GSDP
- Demographic performance = population growth 1971-2011 (not TFR; 15th FC used change in TFR)
- FC-15 used 2011 population; 14th FC used 1971 plus the change since
- Forest: includes open forests + increase in forest area (2015-2023)
- GDP contribution replaces tax effort: contribution to GDP = square root of State GSDP over the sum of square roots; average nominal GSDP 2018-19 to 2023-24 excluding 2020-21
- Exam angle: "highest weight" and "new criterion" questions for each FC.
