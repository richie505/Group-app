### Committees and Acts → year and provisions
## 1. Prakasam Committee, 1937-38
- Appointed: 1937 by Madras Congress ministry (Rajaji CM + Prakasam Revenue Minister)
- Also called: Estate Land / Zamindari Enquiry Committee
- Report: Completed 7 Nov 1938
- Land belongs to the tiller; zamindar = only a tax collector (p.481)
- Prakasam regretted it did not say outright whether zamindari should be abolished (p.485)
## 2. Madras Estates (Abolition and Conversion into Ryotwari) Act, 1948
- Year: 1948 (G2 2008 PYQ: pick 1948)
- ⚠ Trap: Some notes call it the "1949" Act, effective 1950; the Act's name year is 1948
- Provisions:
- Abolished zamindari and inamdari estates
- Tenants became direct ryots of the state
| Step | Date |
| Bill introduced by Revenue Minister Kala Venkata Rao | 11 Nov 1947 |
| Passed by Madras Assembly | Nov 1948 |
| Assent of Governor-General C. Rajagopalachari | 2 Apr 1949. The first law of independent India to get the GG's assent |
| Came into force | 7 Sept 1949. Estates taken over from 1950 (APP) |
- Chronology (G2 2025): Guntur acquired (1788) → Padmanabham (1794) → Ryotwari → abolition
- Constitutional shield: 1st (1951) and 4th (1955) Amendments shielded abolition laws
- ⚠ Trap: Prakasam = Revenue Minister in 1937 (Committee); Kala Venkata Rao = Revenue Minister who introduced the 1947 Bill
### Universal angles
- Significance: Andhra's agitations helped make Madras's 1948 Act one of the first abolition laws of independent India
- Current relevance (PT365, Apr 2026): Communists pioneered the demand for land to the tiller + zamindari abolition
- AP angle: In Madras Presidency, 70% of zamindari land lay in Vizag, Nellore, the two Godavaris, Krishna and Chittoor
# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:217
### Zamindari exactions
- Gothi — hereditary bonded labour in the Agency
- Nazarana — annual renewal fee for tenancy
- Prakasam Committee report (1938) — land belongs to the tiller; zamindar only a tax collector
### Causes
- About 40 percent of Andhra's land still under zamindars at Independence
- Pattabhi — first called for an anti-zamindari struggle, 1920
- Vizag Zamindar Rythu Sabha (3 Nov 1929) — demanded amendment of the Madras Estates Land Act, 1908 (which did not abolish estates)
- Madras Estates (Abolition and Conversion into Ryotwari) Act, 1948 (APPSC G2 2008 PYQ: pick 1948)
- Bill introduced 11 November 1947; assent 2 April 1949; in force 7 September 1949 (hence "1949 Act" in some sources); estates taken over from 1950
### Associations & conferences
- Nellore Zamindari Rythu Sangham — first met 19 October 1929 at Dodla Ramireddy's house
- Bhupathiraju Venkatapathiraju — President, Andhra Rashtra Zamindari Rythu Sangham

### Road to abolition
- 1937 — Rajaji's Madras Congress Ministry appointed a committee on the zamindari system (Prakasam Revenue Minister)
- Yatra from Ichchapuram to petition the committee — Gouthu Latchanna and Pulleti Shyamasundara Rao
- 11 November 1947 — Madras Estates Abolition Bill introduced by Revenue Minister Kala Venkata Rao
- Tenants became direct ryots of the state; zamindari and inamdari estates abolished; zamindars compensated
- Laws protected by the 1st (1951) and 4th (1955) Constitutional Amendments
