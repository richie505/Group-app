## 1. What
- Agency: Energy Efficiency Services Limited (EESL) — PSU under Union Ministry of Power
- Aim: energy efficiency and water efficiency in AP MSME sector — called one of India's largest MSME efficiency programmes
- Implemented under: Centre's RAMP Programme + AP MSME & Entrepreneur Development Policy 4.0
- Work order: handed to EESL at the AP MSME Growth Summit
## 3. Background
- RAMP: Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance — World Bank-supported Central Sector Scheme, launched 2022, by Ministry of MSME, period 2022-23 to 2026-27
- EESL: known for LED lighting, e-mobility, smart meters programmes

### VB-GRAM in AP from 1 July 2026

- Scheme: Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Aajeevika Mission (Grameen) [VB-GRAM] — implemented in AP from 1 July 2026
