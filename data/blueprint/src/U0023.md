### Upanishadic concepts → terms and meaning
## 1. Upanishadic philosophy
- Philosophical literature — philosophy for all times and for all
- Universal approach — does not oppose any school of thought, religion or scriptural interpretation
- Moksha: Liberation — part of Upanishadic philosophy; first discussed in the Upanishads
- Atman: The concept — the core — of the self; not the body or mind
- The human soul is associated with the life–death cycle — the continuous cycle of life and death is not the actual aim of the human soul
- Katha Upanishad: A conversation between Yama and Nachiketa — on death and what lies beyond it
## 2. Upanishadic concepts
| Concept | Meaning |
| Brahman | Ultimate reality (cosmic principle) |
| Atman | The self |
| Karma | Connects action with consequence; core Upanishadic concept |
| Moksha | Liberation |
- Identity: atman = brahman
- Critique: Of mechanical sacrifice; meditation + guru-disciple teaching
- Transmigration: Brihadaranyaka Upanishad introduced the doctrine
