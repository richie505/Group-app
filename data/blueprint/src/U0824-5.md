### Emissions may cut surface solar radiation over India by up to 10%
## 1. News
- Study: Ashwin Jadhav and Rohini Bhawar, Savitribai Phule Pune University — journal Energy and Climate Change
- Finding: up to 10% fall in surface solar radiation (SSR) over central, southern, eastern India under intermediate/high emissions — due to aerosols, turbidity, post-monsoon clouds
- Mean SSR loss: ~1.10 W/m² per decade (intermediate), ~1.32 W/m² per decade (high)
- ⚠ Indo-Gangetic Plains and NE may see marginal increase (low emission); north-west India most stable
- India solar capacity: 123,130.13 MW (31 Aug 2025); renewables 37% of capacity but ~18% of generation

### Sea slug Phidiana unilineata rediscovered off Visakhapatnam after 160 years
## 1. News
- Species: Phidiana unilineata — a nudibranch (soft-bodied marine gastropod mollusc)
- When: City Nature Challenge 2025 (April) — global citizen science initiative
- Found by: Sri Chakra Pranav, founder, East Coast Conservation Team (ECCT)
- First recorded: 1864, from Visakhapatnam, by British zoologists Joshua Alder and Albany Hancock
- ⚠ No sighting since 1864 — possible endemic to Visakhapatnam coast (rocky shores, hydroids)
- Alder and Hancock recorded over 20 sea slug species from Visakhapatnam in the 19th century
- Indicator: sea slugs indicate healthy marine ecosystems (depend on corals, hydroids)
