### Rainfall — seasonal shares of the two monsoons
- ★ India gets maximum rainfall from the South-West Monsoon (about 75%); Jharkhand too (June–September)
- ★ Monsoon arrives first in Kerala
- ★ Summer monsoon flows South-West to North-East; retreating monsoon North-East to South-West
- ★ Maximum NE-monsoon rainfall — Tamil Nadu (40–50% of its annual rain)
- ★ Tamil Nadu monsoon months — November–December (NE monsoon season October–December, its main rainy season)
- ★ NE-monsoon rain — Coromandel Coast; also south-east Karnataka, south-east Kerala
