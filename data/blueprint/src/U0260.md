## 2. Milestones of Telugu
- Sri Krishnadevaraya Andhra Bhasha Nilayam: Established in Hyderabad in 1901 — the first Telugu library; associated with Komarraju Lakshmana Rao; established to develop Telugu language and literature in Telangana
- Gidugu Rama Murthy: The father of the movement for the people's dialect in Telugu
- First World Telugu Conference: 1975 — first held to encourage Telugu language, literature, and culture
