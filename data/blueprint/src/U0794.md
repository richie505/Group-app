### Carbon terms → meaning: footprints, scopes, sinks
## 1. Carbon emissions and sinks
- Sources: Fossil-fuel burning — a major source; automobiles use petrol and diesel; thermal power uses coal — coal combustion produces CO₂, NOx and SO₂
- Sinks: Forests; blue carbon — oceans and coastal ecosystems: mangroves, seagrass, salt marshes
- Common Carbon Metric: Building operations
- Photosynthesis removes CO₂; respiration and decay release it
- Bhutan: A carbon-negative country; net zero — emissions balanced by removals
## 2. Footprints
| Footprint | Measures |
| Carbon | Total GHGs emitted by human activities, in tonnes CO2e per year (APPSC-GS 2025; APP) |
| Ecological | Land and water needed to produce what we consume and absorb our waste (APPSC-GS 2018) |
| Water | Total freshwater used to produce goods and services: blue, green and grey water [GK] |
## 3. Scopes
| Scope | Meaning |
| Scope 1 | direct emissions from owned sources |
| Scope 2 | emissions from purchased electricity or heat |
| Scope 3 | rest of the value chain |
## 4. Sink and emission terms
| Term | Meaning |
| Natural sinks | Forests, soil, oceans |
| REDD+ (Reducing Emissions from Deforestation and Forest Degradation) | India takes part; forest conservation can earn tradable credits |
| Tropical peatlands | Store more carbon per hectare than any other terrestrial ecosystem |
| Carbon fertilization / CO2 fertilisation | Increased plant growth due to CO₂; 70% of Earth's recent greening |
| Carbon bombs | Fossil projects that could emit over 1 Gt CO2 in their lifetime |
| Unit (carbon credit) | 1 carbon credit = 1 tonne CO2e avoided or removed |
- ⚠ Carbon footprint = GHGs; Ecological = land and water; Water = blue, green, grey
### Carbon capture, sequestration and removal → meaning and applications
- Carbon sequestration: Capture and long-term storage — geological storage in coal seams, depleted oil and gas reservoirs, deep saline formations
## 1. Capture and removal terms
| Term | Meaning |
| CCS | Captures CO2 at stationary sources such as power plants and stores it in geological or ocean formations (APP) |
| CCUS | Captures CO2, then uses it or stores it permanently; supports coal gasification and hydrogen (APP) |
| Sequestration | Capturing and storing atmospheric CO2: geological (saline aquifers, depleted oil and gas fields), ocean, terrestrial (forests, soil) (APP; types [GK]) |
| Direct Air Capture | Pulls CO2 straight from ambient air (CDI) |
| Enhanced rock weathering | Crushed silicate rock (e.g. basalt) spread on land binds CO2 as carbonate [GK] |
| Ocean alkalinity enhancement | Adding alkaline minerals to seawater so it absorbs more CO2 [GK] |
| BECCS | Bioenergy with carbon capture and storage → net-negative emissions |
- APPSC-GS 2024 key: "Captures and stores CO2 from industrial processes" = carbon sequestration
- Carbon capture = the other option the key allowed
- ⚠ CCS = store only; CCUS = use or store
## 2. Applications
- DST: Five Carbon Capture and Utilisation (CCU) testbeds in the cement sector - first-of-its-kind research cluster
- Net-zero gas-cycle power: NET Power's Allam(-Fetvedt) cycle plant (La Porte, Texas)
- Burns gas in oxygen with supercritical CO2; captures nearly all CO2

### Carbon markets — Kyoto, standards, CCTS 2023, Net Zero Portal
## 1. Origin and voluntary standards
- Origin: Kyoto Protocol (1997) → Clean Development Mechanism issues CERs (Certified Emission Reductions)
| Standard | Run by |
| Verified Carbon Standard (VCS) | Verra |
| Gold Standard | founded by WWF |
- Maruti Suzuki modal-shift project expects about 1.7 lakh credits over FY24-FY33 under CDM methodology AM0090 (Feb 2026)
## 2. CCTS
- CCTS: Notified June 2023 under Sec. 14AA of the Energy Conservation Act, 2001
- Trades Indian Carbon Credits (ICCs)
- Oct 2025: GHG emission-intensity targets for aluminium, cement, chlor-alkali and pulp & paper; 282 obligated entities
- World Bank 2026: 7 sectors, about 490 industries, about 477 Mt CO2e - only China, EU and South Korea cover more
## 3. Disclosure and outlook
- Net Zero Portal (MoEFCC, Sep 2026): Voluntary disclosure of net-zero commitments
- Scope 1 and 2 reporting encouraged; Scope 3 voluntary
- Budget 2026-27 "carbon credit programme": Follows DST roadmap - industrial, not a farmer scheme (Mar 2026)
- Potential: India could earn $20-40 bn from voluntary credits by 2030; 242 agri-food projects registered (Dec 2025)
### Carbon pricing — EU and UK CBAM → provisions
- EU CBAM: Carbon levy on imports to stop carbon leakage
- Transitional phase: 2023; definitive phase started 1 Jan 2026
- Covers iron and steel, aluminium, cement, fertilisers, hydrogen, electricity + some downstream goods (screws, bolts)
- Importers buy CBAM certificates priced on the EU ETS
- Impact on India: Shifts decarbonisation costs onto exporters; revenue stays in Europe
- Art. 9 of the CBAM Regulation: Carbon price already paid (e.g. under CCTS) can be deducted (4 May 2026)
- UK CBAM: Starts 1 Jan 2027
- Sep 2026: UK recognised India's CCTS as a qualifying carbon price → lowers CBAM liability on Indian exports (8 Sep 2026; 9 Sep 2026)
- ⚠ EU CBAM definitive = 1 Jan 2026; UK CBAM = 1 Jan 2027
