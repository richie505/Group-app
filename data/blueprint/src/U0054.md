### Ikshvaku art FIRSTs and sculptures → significance
- Discovered in Jaggayyapet: Harati, Mahajana, Kumara Swami, Pushpabhadra Swami, Ashtabhoji Narayana Swami and Mandhata sculptures
- School: Nagarjunakonda = main centre of the Amaravati School
- Reached its final, most mature phase under the Ikshvakus
- Followed Mahayana themes
- Early Ikshvaku style: Less stylisation + low relief = "last flicker" of the Satavahana Amaravati tradition
| Sculpture / feature | Significance |
| Mandhata sculpture, Jaggayyapeta | Most important Ikshvaku sculpture; first depiction of a Chakravarti in Indian art |
| Sati panel | First depiction of sati in Indian art |
| Five Aparamahavinaseliya sculptures | Buddhist sculptures of that sect found at Nagarjunakonda |
| Sculptors' names carved | Started the "Sculptor's Style" (e.g. Badantacharya at the Mahavihara) |
- FIRST/LAST:
- First Sanskrit inscription in South India
- First structural Hindu temples
- First sculptor signatures
### Stupa and temple terms → meaning
| Stupa type | Built over |
| Dhatugarbha | Bodily relics of the Buddha or great monks (e.g. the Maha Chaitya) |
| Paribhogika | Objects the monks used (alms bowls) |
| Uddeshika | Nothing; purely commemorative |
- Big stupas: Wheel-shaped (spoked) plan + Ayaka platforms in the four directions, each with five pillars
- Temples (Agamic): Garbhagudi, antarala, mantapa, dhvajastambha, prakara
- Largest stupa in AP: Amaravati (G2 2019)
- ⚠ Dhatugarbha = relics; Paribhogika = used objects; Uddeshika = nothing
### Secular structures at Vijayapuri → purpose
| Structure | Purpose |
| Rajaprasadam (palace complex) and prakaras | Royal residence; compound walls |
| Amphitheatre (Kreeda Vedi) | Sports arena, thought to be built with Roman help; found nowhere else in India |
| Acoustic building | Designed on principles of sound; called the site's most unique find |
| Kummakundam | Water tanks / bathing pools |
| Yeleswaram | Town credited to commander Elisri, who also built the Sarvadevalaya |
### Inscriptions → language
- Early inscriptions: Prakrit - stayed the court language (G2 key: early inscriptions are Prakrit)
- Sanskrit introduced by: Ehuvala Santamula
| Inscription | Language / note |
| Swarna Devalayam (Golden Temple), Nagarjunakonda, his 11th regnal year | First Sanskrit inscription of Andhra and South India |
| Elisri's Sarvadeva inscription | Also Sanskrit |
| His Gummadidurru record (Guntur) | Prakrit |
- Transition: Sanskrit replaced Prakrit from the 4th c. CE
- Telugu words: Bapi, Remmanaka, Adavi, Ehuvala, Anikki
- ⚠ Ehuvala's Gummadidurru record is Prakrit, not Sanskrit

### Nagarjuna Sagar project (1955) — relocation and island museum
- Nagarjuna Sagar Project: Foundation stone laid by Nehru on 10 December 1955 (G2 2012)
- Submergence: Valley submerged after 1960
- Salvage archaeology (ASI): Monuments dismantled + rebuilt on higher ground
- At Anupu (Krishna bank) and Nagarjunakonda island
- Called "transplanted monuments"
- Island museum: Nagarjunakonda has the only island museum in India
### Universal angles
- Current relevance:
- Palnadu Collector reviewed the Nagarjunakonda Museum + Swadesh Darshan works (2 Apr 2026)
- Significance: Ikshvakus laid the base of Dravida temple architecture; Amaravati school at its peak
