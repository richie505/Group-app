- Cession needs an Art. 368 amendment; a boundary dispute needs only executive action

- 1956 — States Reorganisation Act: 14 states + 6 UTs; Andhra Pradesh, Kerala; Zonal Councils

- 1966 — Haryana from Punjab
- 1975 — Sikkim - 22nd state (35th, 36th Amendments)
- 1987 — Mizoram, Arunachal; Goa (30 May 1987, 56th)
- 2014 — Telangana - newest state
- 2019 — J&K → two UTs (effective 31 Oct 2019)

### Cession of territory → cases, agreements and amendments
| Instance | Year | Outcome |

| SC (1969) | 1969 | Settling a boundary dispute needs no amendment - executive action suffices |

### Union Territories — number game and administration
## 1. Current count
- Count (2026): 28 states and 8 Union Territories (APPSC 2021 key)
- 8 UTs: Andaman and Nicobar, Chandigarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, J&K, NCT of Delhi, Ladakh, Lakshadweep, Puducherry
- Merger: DNH + Daman and Diu merged in 2019-20, effective 26 Jan 2020
- Pending name change: Union Cabinet approved renaming Kerala as "Keralam" → Article 3 procedure started
## 2. Administration
- Origin: UTs replaced the Part C/D states under the 7th Amendment 1956
- Art. 239: President administers each UT through an administrator
- Art. 239(2): Governor of an adjoining state may be administrator - e.g. Punjab Governor for Chandigarh
## 3. With and without legislature
- J&K Reorganisation Act 2019: UTs of J&K (with legislature) and Ladakh (without)
| With legislature | Without legislature |
| Delhi (Art. 239AA, 69th Amendment 1991) [GK]; Puducherry (Art. 239A, 14th Amendment) [GK]; J&K (J&K Reorganisation Act 2019) | Ladakh, Chandigarh, Lakshadweep, Andaman and Nicobar, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu |
