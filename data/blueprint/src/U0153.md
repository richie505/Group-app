- Cartaz: = naval trade licence (pass) (APPSC-GS 2018)
- Every ship sailing the Indian Ocean had to buy one
- Ships without it confiscated or sunk
- Enforcement: Backed by the Caes (convoy system) + customs duties; also used in the Bay of Bengal
- Goa Inquisition (16th c.): Against religious dissent; state machinery used for conversions
### Portuguese contributions — New World crops, printing press, forts
- Crops: tobacco, potato, lady's finger, chilli, pineapple, sapota, groundnut
- Tomato introduced to Telugu soil (APPSC-G2 2025 key)
- Printing: Printing press (1556); Bible printed in Kannada and Malayalam
- Culture: Vindaloo (from vinha d'alhos); Indo-Portuguese houses with balcaos
- Forts: Aguada, Bassein, Diu Fort (1535-41)

- Piracy: From Hugli and Chittagong, Portuguese (with Arakanese pirates) raided the Bay of Bengal
- Mughal response: Shah Jahan took Hugli; Shaista Khan took Sondip and Chittagong

- Goa statehood: May 1987
