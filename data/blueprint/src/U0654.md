### Components and drivers of urban growth
## 1. Components of urban growth
- Components of urban growth:
- (a) natural increase
- (b) rural-to-urban migration
- (c) reclassification: new towns forming / villages turning into towns
- (d) Town boundaries expanding to take in villages
- Migration vs natural increase: Natural increase differs little between rural and urban → migration explains most of the gap
## 2. Drivers of urbanisation
| Factor | Effect |
| Pull | Industrialisation + jobs, trade and commerce, better health and education, transport links |
