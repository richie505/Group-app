### Acts, sections and schemes → provisions — tax incentives, GST, BRGF
## 1. Section 94(1) tax incentives
- Section 94(1): Centre to take fiscal measures, including tax incentives, for industrialisation
- Notified in 2015:
- Additional depreciation of 35% + investment allowance of 15% on new plant and machinery
- Units set up 1 Apr 2015 - 31 Mar 2020 in notified backward districts of AP and Telangana
- Criticism: fell short of comprehensive package AP wanted; SCS (90% central share of CSS, tax exemptions) denied
- ⚠ Depreciation 35%; investment allowance 15%
