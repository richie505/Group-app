# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:155 - French in India - Colbert, Compagnie des Indes, Pondicherry, Dupleix and his policy
### Compagnie des Indes
- French = only serious European rival to the British; lost for want of naval supremacy + steady state support
- 1664, Jean-Baptiste Colbert, minister of Louis XIV
- Compagnie des Indes Orientales; 50-year monopoly + Madagascar
- State-owned, on subsidies → vulnerable
- Last European company to arrive
### Settlements
| Settlement | Year | Detail |
| Surat | 1668 | First factory, François Caron |
| Masulipatnam | 1669 | Caron + Marcara; Abdullah Qutb Shah |
| Pondicherry | 1673-74 | François Martin; grant of Sher Khan Lodi; Fort St. Louis |
| Chandernagore | 1673 / 1690 | Shaista Khan's permission |
| Yanam | 1723 | Only French territory in Andhra |
| Mahe / Karaikal | 1725 / 1739 | Malabar / Coromandel |
### Governors in order
- ▲ François Martin — first governor; built Pondicherry
- ▸ Dupleix (1742-54) — peak of French power
- ▸ Godeheu (1754) — replaced Dupleix
- ▸ Count de Lally — lost Wandiwash (1760) to Eyre Coote
### Dupleix's intervention policy
- Exploited succession disputes: Nasir Jang vs Muzaffar Jang; Anwaruddin vs Chanda Sahib
- Ambur (1749): Anwaruddin killed
- Treaty of Pondicherry (1754) → Dupleix recalled
- Bussy made Salabat Jang Subedar; seven years in Hyderabad
### French in Andhra
- 1753 — Northern Circars to French: Mustafanagar, Eluru, Rajahmundry, Chicacole
- 1757 — Bobbili (24 Jan): Vijayaramaraju + Bussy vs Ranga Rao; Tandra's revenge
- 1759 — Colonel Forde takes Machilipatnam; first British-Hyderabad treaty
- 1763 — Treaty of Paris: only trading enclaves left
- 1954 — De facto merger (de jure 1962) → UT of Puducherry; Chandernagore 1950

- Arrival: The last European company to arrive

## 1. Pondicherry - founder
- Land grant (1673): Sher Khan Lodi (Bijapur governor of Valikondapuram) → François Martin (director at Masulipatnam)
- Founded: Pondicherry 1674; Fort St. Louis
## 2. Enclaves and merger with India
- After Wandiwash + Treaty of Paris (1763): French kept only trading enclaves
- Pondicherry, Karaikal, Mahe, Yanam, Chandernagore
- Merger with India:
- Chandernagore → India by plebiscite (1950)
- Other four (incl. Yanam): de facto 1954, de jure 1962 → UT of Puducherry

## 1. Dupleix - intervention policy, titles and jagirs
- Succession disputes exploited:
- Hyderabad: after Nizam-ul-Mulk's death (1748) → Nasir Jang vs Muzaffar Jang
- Carnatic: Anwaruddin vs Chanda Sahib
- Titles/jagir: Dupleix = Nawab of the Mughal territory south of the Krishna; jagir Valapadu
## 2. Bussy
- Bussy: Escorted Muzaffar Jang
- After his murder at Rayachoti → made Salabat Jang Subedar
- Bobbili: Vijayaramaraju took French help (APPSC G2 2019)

### Northern Circars → grant and loss (1753 to 1788)
- 1753: Salabat Jang gave the French the Northern Circars to pay for Bussy's troops
- Mustafanagar (Kondapalli), Eluru, Rajahmundry and Chicacole (Srikakulam)
- Peak of French power/influence in the region
- 1758-59: After Chendurthi + fall of Machilipatnam, Salabat Jang switched to the British
- Gave them Machilipatnam, Nizampatnam, Vakal Mannar and part of Kondapalli (see A-136)
- 1759 treaty = first British-Hyderabad agreement
- Ended French power north of the Krishna, except in Guntur
- Later transfers: Other Circars British in 1768; Guntur in 1788 (see S1-64)

### Compagnie des Indes
- 1664 — founded by Jean-Baptiste Colbert, minister of Louis XIV
- French — last European company to arrive
- Charter: 50-year monopoly in the Indian and Pacific Oceans + rights over Madagascar
- Weakness — state-owned, dependent on subsidies
- Lost mainly for lack of naval supremacy and steady state support
### Settlements
## Founding
- Surat (1668) — first French factory, under François Caron
- Machilipatnam — permission from Abdullah Qutb Shah
- Sher Khan Lodi (Bijapur governor of Valikondapuram) — gave land to François Martin in 1673; Pondicherry founded 1674
- Fort St. Louis — built by François Martin at Pondicherry; French headquarters in India
- ★ Chronology: Surat → Pondicherry → Mahe → Karaikal → Yanam
## Locations
- ★ Yanam (Godavari delta) — the French settlement in Andhra; only French territory in Andhra
- under French control until 1954; now part of UT of Puducherry
- Mahe — French settlement on the Malabar coast; Chandernagore was not in Malabar
### Governors in order
- François Martin — first French governor; builder of Pondicherry; died 31 December 1706
- Count de Lally — lost at Wandiwash to Eyre Coote
### Dupleix's intervention policy
- After Nizam-ul-Mulk's death (1748), Dupleix used two successions:
- Hyderabad — Nasir Jang vs Muzaffar Jang
- Carnatic — Anwaruddin vs Chanda Sahib
- Battle of Ambur (1749) — Anwaruddin killed; Muzaffar Jang Subedar; Chanda Sahib Nawab
- Muzaffar Jang murdered at Rayachoti; Bussy set up Salabat Jang as Subedar of the Deccan
- Treaty of Pondicherry (1754) — no interference in Indian princes' quarrels; Dupleix recalled
### French in Andhra
## Northern Circars
- 1753 — given by Salabat Jang to pay for Bussy's troops; high point of French influence
- Circars given: Mustafanagar (Kondapalli), Eluru, Rajahmundry, Chicacole — Nellore not included
- Other Circars British in 1768; Guntur in 1788
## Bobbili
- Battle of Bobbili — 24 January 1757
- Bussy helped Vijayaramaraju of Vizianagaram; Ranga Rao of Bobbili killed
- Tandra Papayya later killed Vijayaramaraju
## After Wandiwash
- Treaty of Paris (1763) — French kept only trading enclaves: Pondicherry, Karaikal, Mahe, Yanam, Chandernagore
- Chandernagore — joined India by plebiscite, 1950
- Other four enclaves — in fact 1954, in law 1962
