### WHO declares Ebola outbreak a PHEIC
## 1. News
- WHO declared Ebola outbreak in DR Congo and Uganda a PHEIC; strain Bundibugyo ebolavirus; ⚠ not a pandemic emergency
- Cases: 8 confirmed in Ituri Province (DRC), 246 suspected, 80 suspected deaths; 2 confirmed in Kampala (1 death)
## 2. Background
- PHEIC: highest WHO alert under International Health Regulations (IHR)
- Ebola: known since 1976; largest outbreak West Africa 2014–16 (Guinea, Liberia, Sierra Leone) — 28,600+ cases, 11,325 deaths
- Viruses causing large outbreaks: Ebola, Sudan, Bundibugyo
- Incubation: 2–21 days; contact tracing 21 days
- Treatments: mAb114 (Ansuvimab), REGN-EB3 (Inmazeb); vaccines: Ervebo, Zabdeno + Mvabea (ring vaccination)
- ⚠ Bundibugyo strain has limited vaccine options
## 3. CA360 figures
- 300+ cases and 88 deaths (later count); ⚠ 17th Ebola outbreak in Congo since the virus was found there in 1976
