### Ease of Doing Business — India's rank, indicators and successor
## 1. India's rank
| World Bank Doing Business report | India's rank |
| DB 2015 (2014) | 142 |
| DB 2019 (released 2018) | 77/190 |
| DB 2020 (2019) | 63/190 |
- Rank movement: 142 → 63 over five years
- APPSC 2019 key: "rank in 2018" = 77/190
## 2. What counts and purpose
- What counts: Single Window System counts; per-capita income, inflation, literacy do NOT (APPSC 2019)
- Purpose: A ready reckoner for foreign investors (APPSC 2019 key)
## 3. India's weakest indicators
- Lags:
- Paying taxes = weakest cited (APPSC-GS 2019 key)
- Enforcing contracts = 163/190
- DB 2020: enforcing contracts and registering property remained the worst
## 4. Successor and allied indices
- Successor: World Bank B-READY (the Doing Business report has been discontinued)
- India in the 3rd report, expected around Sep 2026
- Allied: IMD World Competitiveness rank 43 (2021) → 41 (2025)
### Issues — India's objections to GHI and EPI
| Index | India's objection |
| GHI | GoI view: Flawed - 3 of the 4 indicators cover only children; undernourishment rests on a small opinion-poll survey (about 3,000 respondents) |
| EPI | MoEFCC rejected the EPI methodology in 2022 |
- EPI objections:
- Favours absolute emissions over per-capita (per-capita bias)
- Uses contested indicators
- Counter-evidence (2026):
- India 23rd in CCPI 2026
- 50% non-fossil installed power capacity in 2025 = five years before the 2030 NDC target
### Universal angles
- EPI 2026: India 176th of 177
- Sub-ranks: Air Quality 175th; Climate Mitigation 130th
- StartupBlink: 54th in Innovators Business Environment Index 2026
