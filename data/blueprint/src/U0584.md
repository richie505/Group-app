### Act commitment — wording, body and constitutional basis
| Item | Fact |
| Act wording (Thirteenth Schedule, Infrastructure) | Indian Railways shall, within six months from appointed day, examine establishing a new railway zone in successor State of AP + take expeditious decision |
| Nature of promise | Only obliges Railways to examine (feasibility promise); same wording pattern as steel plant, refinery, airport items |
| Telangana item | Railways to examine a Rail Coach Factory for Telangana |
| Constitutional basis | Railways = Union subject (Entry 22, List I); zone created by executive decision + gazette notification of Ministry of Railways |
- ⚠ Trap: Do not mix the two (AP = railway zone; Telangana = Rail Coach Factory)
- Exam angle: "which body", "within what period" (six months), "examine" vs "establish".
