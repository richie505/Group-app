### AP — FIRSTs
## 1. Firsts
- First linguistic state: Andhra Pradesh — the first state in India formed on linguistic grounds
- Asia's first rubber dam: On the Janjavathi River — a tributary of the Nagavali
- First private-sector gold mine in India: Jonnagiri, Kurnool district, Andhra Pradesh
### Formation, territorial adjustments, area and rank — FIRSTs, Act and data
## 4. Territorial adjustments
- Tiruttani–Tiruvallur adjustment (1960): Followed the Pataskar Commission's decision (Pataskar award)
- Tamil Nadu received a large part of Tiruttani Taluka of Chittoor district
- Some villages of Tiruvallur Taluka of Tamil Nadu were added to Andhra Pradesh
- 2014: Seven mandals of Khammam transferred to Andhra Pradesh
- Polavaram mandals: 7 mandals of Khammam (Polavaram submergence area — Bhadrachalam, Kunavaram, Chintur, Velerupadu, Kukunoor, V.R. Puram) moved to AP by the 2014 Polavaram ordinance and amendment
- Chinturu division now in the new Polavaram district

### Regions → districts; Rayalaseema vs Coastal Andhra
| Basis | Rayalaseema | Coastal Andhra |
| Location | Inland southwestern Andhra Pradesh | Eastern Andhra Pradesh |
| Physical setting | A plateau region — inland uplands and plateau terrain; physically associated with the Deccan Plateau | A low-lying coastal region — coastal plain and (river) deltas |
| Climate | Drought-prone — historically frequent droughts and famines | — |
| Agriculture | Comparatively less favourable | Fertile and well-developed |
| Population | Very low population density (recurring drought) | The highest population concentration |
- Rayalaseema districts: Kurnool, Nandyal, Anantapur, Sri Sathya Sai, YSR (Kadapa), Annamayya, Tirupati, Chittoor (8); borders TN, Karnataka, Telangana
| Region | Districts (2022 set of 26) |
