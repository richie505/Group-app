## 1. FIRSTs
- First Dutch factory in AP: Machilipatnam (APPSC G2 2017)
- APPSC-GS 2024 match: Dutch first factory at Masulipatnam 1605, British 1611
- Portuguese earlier: Traded at Machilipatnam earlier (APPSC 2019 key)
- Minting: Dutch first to get the right to mint coins in Andhra (privilege granted by local rulers)
- Earliest factory in India: Masulipatnam (1605)
- Chief: Pulicat until 1690 → HQ moved to Nagapattinam (APPSC G2 2005 key: Dutch HQ in India = Nagapattinam)
- ⚠ Trap: Mahe is listed among Dutch centres in some notes - Mahe was French
