## 1. Qutb Shahis → founder and facts
- Golconda (Hyderabad) state: Founded by Sultan Quli Qutb Shah in 1518 - APPSC 2011 key "Who founded the Hyderabad State?" = Quli Qutb Shah
- City of Hyderabad: Founded by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah in 1591 (Charminar 1594); became capital (some notes: from 1592)
- ⚠ State = Sultan Quli (1518); city = Muhammad Quli (1591)
- Last ruler: Abul Hasan Tana Shah
- ⚠ Trap: Older notes date the dynasty from 1512; Nizami Mosque inscription records independence in 1518
### Feudatory families → kingdoms
| Family | Earlier overlord | Became |
| Kakatiyas | Rashtrakutas, then Kalyani Chalukyas | Sovereign in 1163 (Rudradeva) |

| Kota family | Kakatiya subordinate (married Ganapatideva's daughter Ganapamba) | Local rulers of Dharanikota [GK] |
| Kayasthas | Kakatiya loyalists (Ambadeva, Kadapa region 1272) | Revolted under Ambadeva with Pandya support |
| Qutb Shahis | Bahmani governor (tarafdar) of Telangana, 1496 | Independent 1518 |
### Contested regions and battles → year and sides
## 1. Contested regions
- Raichur Doab (Raichur Antarvedi): Land between Krishna and Tungabhadra
- Fertile black soil + strategic = main cause of Bahmani-Vijayanagara wars (APPSC 2003, 2017)
- Krishna-Godavari region / coastal Andhra: contested by Reddis, Velamas, Gajapatis, Vijayanagara and the Bahmanis (three-way contest on the Krishna-Godavari coast)
- Golconda fort: Kapaya Nayaka ceded it to Bahmani Muhammad I after son Vinayaka was killed (1361)
- Qutb Shahi annexation: Sultan Quli Qutb Shah annexed Rajahmundry, Eluru and the K-G region
- After Talikota (1565): Golconda took Kondavidu, Machilipatnam + coastal districts

## After 1323
- Musunuri Nayakas, Reddis, Velamas — three of Prataparudra's generals who survived the Kakatiya fall (1323)
- 1323 — end of Telugu imperial unity until the Qutb Shahis reunited the region
- Telangana succession — Kakatiyas, Delhi Sultanate (1323), Musunuri Nayakas, Velamas/Bahmanis, Qutb Shahis
- Prolaya Vemareddy — Reddis; Singama — Velamas; Harihara-Bukka — Vijayanagara; Hasan Gangu — Bahmanis
- Anavema Reddy — shifted Reddi capital from Addanki to Kondavidu
- Imad Shahis (Berar) — founder Fathullah Imad-ul-Mulk; capital Ellichpur (Achalpur)
## Unifiers and early dynasties
- Unifiers of Telugu lands — 1st Satavahanas, 2nd Kakatiyas, 3rd Qutb Shahis
- Vijaya Deva Varma — Salankayana dynasty
- Shantamula I — founder of the Ikshvaku dynasty (Krishna valley); Simuka — Satavahana founder
### Coastal Andhra succession
- ★ Muslims invaded Andhra under the Kakatiyas — from 1303, five invasions, ending 1323
- Reddis came after the Kakatiyas (not before)
### Five Deccan Sultanates
- Mahmud Gawan executed 1482 → Bahmani kingdom split into five Deccan Sultanates
- Berar annexed by Ahmadnagar in 1574
### Qutb Shahis
- Hyderabad founded 1591; Charminar built 1594
- Last ruler — Abul Hasan Tana Shah (Shia, Persian-using state)
- After Talikota (1565), Golconda took Kondavidu, Machilipatnam and the coastal districts
- Sultan Quli Qutb Shah took Rajahmundry, Eluru and the Krishna–Godavari region
