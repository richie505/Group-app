### Household electricity and clean cooking — provisions
## 1. Saubhagya
- Achievement: claimed 100% household electrification (figures on the data page)
- Leftovers under RDSS: ₹6,521 crore sanctioned for 13.65 lakh households, incl.:
## 2. PM Ujjwala Yojana
- Ujjwala 2.0: free first refill and stove; migrants via self-declaration
- Cash assistance ₹1,600 per 14.2 kg connection
- Refill support:
- Older notes: ₹200 per 14.2 kg refill, up to 12 a year; 5 kg double-bottle and swap options
- Since Oct 2023: targeted subsidy ₹300 per 14.2 kg refill, up to 9 refills a year
- ⚠ Refill subsidy: older ₹200 × 12; since Oct 2023 ₹300 × 9
### Distribution reforms — IPDS, UDAY, RDSS provisions and targets
## 1. IPDS
- Merger: subsumed R-APDRP
## 2. UDAY - discom debt takeover
- Debt takeover: States took over 75% of discom debt (as of 30 Sep 2015) through state bonds
- 50% in 2015-16, 25% in 2016-17
- Coverage: 97% of total outstanding discom debt covered by participating states (PYQ 2017)
- Targets by 2018-19: AT&C losses down to 15% + ACS-ARR gap to zero
## 3. RDSS
- Targets: pan-India AT&C loss 12-15% + ACS-ARR gap zero
- ⚠ AT&C target: UDAY 15% by 2018-19; RDSS 12-15%
### Solar for farmers and homes — PM-KUSUM and PM Surya Ghar features
## 1. PM-KUSUM components
| Component | What |
| A | Decentralised grid-connected solar/RE plants on farm/barren land [GK] |
| B | Standalone solar pumps |
| C | Solarisation of grid-connected pumps |
- Subsidy sharing (B and C): 30% Centre, 30% State, 40% farmer (bank loan possible)
- Benefit: decentralised solar gives daytime irrigation + extra income to over 21.77 lakh farmers
## 2. PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana
| Item | Fact |
| Outlay | ₹75,021 crore (IYB) |
| Target | Rooftop solar in 1 crore households by FY 2026-27 (March 2027) |
| Free power | Up to 300 units per month - households become "prosumers" (CDI) |
| Subsidy (CFA) | 60% of cost for 2 kW; 40% for the extra capacity up to 3 kW (CDCA Aug 2026) |
| Other goals | 30 GW RTS; 1,000 BU power and 720 MT CO2 cut over 25 years (VIS 2026) |
- Subsumed: Phase II Grid-Connected Rooftop Solar Programme (2019)
- Model village: one Model Solar Village per district
- Tag: world's largest domestic rooftop solar scheme
- City Accelerator Programme (Jun 2025): technical help to ULBs and discoms for urban rooftop solar
- PYQ (UPSC 2025): statements on the 1 crore target and MNRE installer training were all correct

### Andhra Pradesh — free power, PM Surya Ghar, PM-KUSUM, RDSS
## 1. Free power and subsidy
- Free power: up to 200 units/month to 20.26 lakh BPL SC/ST households
- Power subsidy (FY 2024-25): ₹10,449.92 crore
- RDSS (AP): ₹15,083.14 crore sanctioned under RDSS for smart metering and grid modernisation
## 2. PM Surya Ghar in AP
- Discom progress (Aug 2026): SPDCL 47%, CPDCL 35%, EPDCL 32%
- APCPDCL: 50,114 rooftop units, 180 MW
- SC/ST households: Free rooftop solar through Virtual Net Metering
- Targets (Energy Minister Gottipati Ravi Kumar):
- 6 lakh connections = 6,000 per constituency
- A model solar village in every district
## 3. PM-KUSUM and RE capacity
- PM-KUSUM (AP): solarisation of 2.93 lakh agricultural pump sets
- AP RE capacity: 10,639.75 MW = solar 5,614.52 + wind 4,397.67
### Universal angles
- Significance: access moved from connections (DDUGJY, Saubhagya) → quality and discom viability (UDAY, RDSS) → prosumers (PM Surya Ghar)
- Current relevance:
- AP rooftop solar drive (Aug 2026)
