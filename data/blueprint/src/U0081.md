### Vengi school: style
- Draksharama Bhimeswara = Chalukya-Chola style: Pallava + early Chalukya features in one regional style
- Mainly Dravida + Pallava (Kanchi) + Badami Chalukya influences + local features
- Also called Vengi School / Vesara (Chalukya) style
- Materials: local laterite and limestone; Krishna-Godavari delta
- Model: Draksharama Bhimeswara = Chalukya-Chola style (G2 2025)

## 1. Draksharama vs Samalkot
- Common: Both two-storeyed Bhimeswara shrines of Chalukya Bhima I, closely similar plan; both later renovated by the Reddis
- Draksharama: Massive lingam, huge Nandi mandapa, intricate carving; one of the Trilinga kshetras
- Samalkot (Bheemarama/Kumararama): Core sanctum + black-granite lingam = Chalukya Bhima; towering gopuram added later
### Debates and exam traps
- ⚠ 2025 trap: Key marked "Rajaraja Narendra built the Rajarajeswara temple at Biccavole" as the incorrect statement
- Some notes: he built a "Rajaraja temple" at Bikkavolu in his own name; others list a Raja Raja temple in Gunaga Vijayaditya's Bikkavolu group
- Dating debate: Some notes (Block10) treat Bikkavolu as a "Chalukya-Reddi transition" group (later Reddi additions)
- Chalukya Bhimavaram error: Some notes (Block09) wrongly list a "Chalukya Bhimavaram Shiva temple (Palakollu)"
### Universal angles
- Comparative note: Badami Chalukyas developed Vesara in Karnataka (Alampur Navabrahma temples in AP); Vengi line carried a Dravida-based regional style into the Chalukya-Chola phase
- AP angle: Rajahmundry = cultural capital of the Andhras

### Vengi school: style
- Eastern Chalukyas mainly followed the Dravida style — mix of Pallava and Badami Chalukya influences with local Andhra features = Vengi School
