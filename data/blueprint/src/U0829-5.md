### Khaled El-Enany nominated UNESCO Director-General
## 1. News
- Date: 6 October 2025 — 222nd session of UNESCO Executive Board nominated Khaled El-Enany (Egypt)
- Defeated: Édouard Firmin Matoko (Republic of Congo)
- Final appointment: UNESCO General Conference in Uzbekistan
- Profile: Egyptologist; Egypt's Minister of Antiquities (2016–2019), later Tourism and Antiquities; backed by African Union and Arab League
- ⚠ Would be the first Arab Director-General
- Context: USA withdrew from UNESCO in July 2025
### Carlo Acutis — first millennial saint
## 1. News
- Canonised by: Pope Leo XIV at St. Peter's Square, Vatican — ⚠ Catholic Church's first millennial saint
- Also canonised: Pier Giorgio Frassati — Italian, died 1925 aged 24 of polio
### West Godavari Collector nominated for AP State Red Cross Award
## 1. News
- Who: Chadalawada Naga Rani, Collector, West Godavari
- Award: AP State Red Cross Award for 2023–24 and 2024–25 (two consecutive years)
- For: flood relief during Budameru river floods
- Announced by: A.K. Parida, General Secretary and CEO, IRCS AP branch
- Occasion: World Red Cross Day and World Thalassemia Day (8 May)
## 2. Background — Indian Red Cross Society
- Established: 1920 under Indian Red Cross Society Act (Act XV of 1920); amended 1992, rules 1994
- President: President of India; Chairman: Union Health Minister
- Managing Body: 18 members — 6 + Chairman nominated by President, 12 elected by branches
- Movement: ICRC, National Societies, IFRC
### Dr Guduru Jagadish — 'Vishva Vaidya Divyang Bandhu' from Mauritius PM
## 1. News
- Who: Dr Guduru Jagadish, former chief of TTD's BIRRD Trust Hospital
- Title: 'Vishva Vaidya Divyang Bandhu' (World Healer and Friend of the Differently-Abled), by Mauritius PM Ramgoolam
- Occasion: Ugadi celebrations of Mauritius Telugu Maha Sabha
- For: free polio screening and corrective surgery camps
