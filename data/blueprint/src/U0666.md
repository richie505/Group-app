### Urban centres → rank, nickname and ULBs
## 1. Largest cities
- Largest city: Visakhapatnam (APPSC-GS 2019 key; APPSC 2019 PYQ)
- Million-plus agglomerations (2011): Visakhapatnam + Vijayawada = AP's million-plus
- Cities with more than 1 lakh people (2011): 32 (APPSC-GS 2019 answer key)
- Other major centres: Vijayawada, Guntur, Nellore, Kurnool, Rajamahendravaram, Tirupati, Kakinada
- With Vizag + Amaravati = main growth poles
