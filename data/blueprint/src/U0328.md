### Commissions → key recommendations
| Body | Key recommendations |

### Articles → Inter-State Council, All India Services, Article 371
## 1. Inter-State Council (Art. 263)
- Set up: 1990 (Art. 263) on Sarkaria's advice; recommendatory
- Members: CMs, UT administrators, Governors of states under President's Rule + 6 Union Cabinet ministers
- Meetings: 11th meeting on 16 July 2016; LENS (Feb 2026) urges reviving it
- Not in your sources: any ISC meeting held after 2016.
## 2. All India Services (Art 312)
- Art 312: AIS = IAS, IPS, Indian Forest Service (IFoS)
- New AIS: needs a Rajya Sabha resolution by two-thirds present and voting
- Recruitment: by the UPSC, but officers serve the states
- Control: Ultimate control with the Centre, immediate control with the states; one service with uniform pay scales across states
