### HDI — definition, persons and reports
## 1. HDI concept
- HDI origin: UNDP, first HDR 1990; conceived by Mahbub ul Haq with Amartya Sen
- Formula: Geometric mean (since 2010) of three indices
- Health: life expectancy
- Education: mean + expected years of schooling
- Income: GNI per capita, PPP, log
