## 2. Sarnath
- Ancient Buddhist Site of Sarnath inscribed at 48th WHC session, Busan, South Korea, July 2026
- India's 45th WHS; UP's 4th (others: Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Fatehpur Sikri)
- Components: Chaukhandi Stupa + archaeological remains; criteria (iii) and (vi)

- ⚠ Tentative listing does not confer WHS status; India: ASI and INCCU propose
- Danger list examples: Bamiyan Valley, Zabid (Yemen), Bam (Iran)

### World AIDS Day and NACP
## 1. News
- Date: December 1 — World AIDS Day
- Theme 2025: "Overcoming disruption, transforming the AIDS response"
- Context: why HIV remains incurable after 42 years of research
## 2. HIV facts
- HIV identified: 1983 by Françoise Barré-Sinoussi and Luc Montagnier (Institut Pasteur)
- Attacks: CD4 white blood cells (CD4+ T-cells); AIDS = advanced stage of untreated HIV
- India prevalence: 0.33% (2010) → 0.20% (2024)
- India supplies: 70% of global ART medicines
- Advanced HIV Disease (WHO): CD4 < 200 cells/mm³ or WHO Stage 3/4; all children < 5 years with HIV are AHD
- Window period: antibodies develop in about 28 days; infants need virological testing at birth or 6 weeks
- PEP: within 72 hours of exposure
- WHO PrEP options: oral TDF-based PrEP, Dapivirine vaginal ring, long-acting injectable cabotegravir, long-acting injectable lenacapavir
- WHO strategy: Global Health Sector Strategies (GHSS) 2022–2030; end AIDS by 2030 (SDG 3.3)
- Why hard to treat: retrovirus integrates into host DNA (reverse transcription), latent reservoirs, high mutation rate, destroys immune cells
## 3. National AIDS Control Programme phases
