- BASIC — formed 2009, before COP15 Copenhagen
- First Quad leaders' summit — virtual, March 2021; first in-person — Washington, September 2021
- Colombo Security Conclave — five pillars: maritime safety and security; counter-terrorism and radicalisation; trafficking and organised crime; cyber security and critical infrastructure; HADR
