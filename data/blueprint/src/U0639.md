### Forest types → region, rainfall and species
## 1. Forest types of AP
- Forests in AP are predominantly deciduous forests
| Forest type | Rainfall | Where | Associated with |
| Moist deciduous — the maximum rainfall | 125–200 cm | Erstwhile Srikakulam, Visakhapatnam and Agency areas of East Godavari | Wetter areas |
| Dry deciduous | 75–100 cm | Erstwhile Kadapa and Kurnool — a major belt around the Seshachalam–Nallamala hill system | The relatively drier parts of AP — the long Eastern Ghats pass runs through these forests |
| Short thorn | Less than 70 cm | Erstwhile Kadapa, Kurnool, Chittoor and Anantapur | The driest / dry areas |
| Tidal — mangrove trees; also called Koringa forests | — | The mouths of the Krishna and Godavari rivers | Coastal or tidal environments |
## 2. Forest type → region and species
| Forest type | Region | Main species |
| Tropical dry deciduous (largest area) | Eastern Ghats, Nallamala, Seshachalam, plateau | teak, Terminalia, red sanders, Anogeissus |
| Southern moist deciduous / hill forests | northern Eastern Ghats (Araku, Paderu), Papikondalu | bamboo, sandalwood, medicinal plants |
| Tropical thorn and scrub | Rayalaseema (Anantapur, Kadapa, Kurnool) | Acacia, Ziziphus, Prosopis (invasive) |
| Littoral and mangrove | Godavari-Krishna deltas (Coringa), Pulicat | Rhizophora, Avicennia, Excoecaria |
