### Five Year Plans → model, priority
| Plan | Model | Priority / outcome |
| First (1951-56), launched 1 Apr 1951 | Harrod-Domar | Agriculture, irrigation, power (Bhakra Nangal, Hirakud); growth 3.6% vs target 2.1% |
| Second (1956-61) | P.C. Mahalanobis (Nehru-Mahalanobis-Feldman) | Heavy/capital-goods industry; neglected agriculture → food shortages, inflation |
| Third (1961-66), "Gadgil Yojana" | John Sandy & S. Chakravarty | Self-sufficiency in food, "growth with stability"; derailed by wars of 1962 and 1965 and drought |
- Third Plan failure: → Plan Holiday 1966-69 (three annual plans); rupee devalued
- ⚠ First = Harrod-Domar + agriculture; Second = Mahalanobis + heavy industry
- Exam angle: plan → model → priority matching; NDC year and chairman.

### Articles and Acts — secularism, language, Hindu Code Bill
## 1. Secularism
- Indian secularism: Sarva Dharma Sambhava - no state religion; State neutral but may reform social practices
- vs Western model of strict separation
| Article | Provision |
| Art. 30 | Protects minority educational institutions |
| Art. 343 | Hindi in Devanagari = official language, with English for 15 years |
## 2. Hindu Code Bill and Ambedkar's posts
- Labour Member: Viceroy's Executive Council (1942-46)
- First Law Minister: Of free India in Nehru's first cabinet (1947)
- Hindu Code Bill aim: Equal rights for Hindu women in inheritance, marriage, divorce and adoption; introduced 1947
- Fate: Stalled and diluted → Ambedkar resigned as Law Minister in 1951
| Act (passed in parts) | Year |
| Hindu Marriage Act 1955 | 1955 |
| Hindu Succession Act 1956 | 1956 |
| Hindu Minority and Guardianship Act 1956 | 1956 |
| Hindu Adoptions and Maintenance Act 1956 | 1956 |
- ⚠ Marriage Act = 1955; the other three = 1956
