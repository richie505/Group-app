### Coinage and trade → metal, symbols, find-spots
- Metal: Iron with a thin coating of copper (copper-iron alloy) - APPSC GS 2018
- Some notes say gold, silver and copper coins with lion or vase symbols - conflicts with the key; follow copper-coated iron
- Discovery: Rayaprolu Subrahmanyam; 16 types
- Symbols:
- Ship, trishula
- Sea trade evidence: Ship-marked coins in coastal Andhra + MV II's title Trisamudradhipati
- Roman coin: Roman gold coin of Constantine at Kudali Sangameswaram near Alampur
- Cowries: Fahien - cowrie shells (gavvalu) also used as money
### Gods, sacrifices and FIRSTs in Vishnukundin religion
- Family deity: Sriparvataswami; kings called themselves "Sriparvataswami-padanudhyata"
- Identified with Srisaila Mallikarjuna / presiding deity of Srisailam
- Ganapati worship began: Dantamukha Swami installed at Velpuru in MV II's 33rd year
### Buddhist viharas and Jainism → donors and decline
| Vihara / grant | Donor |
| Paramabhattarika Mahadevi Vihara, Indrapalanagara (for Chaturdasharya bhikshus) | Queen of Govinda Varma I; he granted Penkapura and Enmadala |
| Grant of Irundhera to the same vihara | Vikramendra Varma II (Tummalagudem-2) |
| Govinda Vihara, Chaitanyapuri (Hyderabad) | Govinda Varma I (Prakrit inscription on the Musi) |
- Buddhism's decline: From MV II onward
- Called a "destroyer of Buddhism" - turned Buddhist sites at Undavalli, Mogalrajapuram, Indrakeeladri, Bhairavakonda into Hindu shrines
- Jainism: Declined as Kapalika Shaivas rose
- Jain shrines such as Srisailam converted
- Kumarilabhatta attacked Jainism
### Grants → donees (agraharas)
| Grant | Donee / ruler |
| Mutukali (Mruthukali) | Agrahara to Indravarma and Agnivarma (MV III per some notes; Vikramendra I per others) |
| Tundi | Village to scholar Shivavarma (Vikramendra II, Tundi plates) |
| Ipur plates | Cows, gold, land to Brahmins; donees Agnisarma, Easwarasarma |
- Role: Agraharas = centres of Vedic learning
- Feudalisation debate: Tax-free Brahmadeyas with administrative rights → landed intermediary class
### Education — Ghatikas and scholar-kings
- Ghatikas: Higher Sanskrit/Vedic colleges
- First mention in Andhra = Indrabhattaraka Varma's inscriptions
- Special Ghatikas: Amaravati and Vengi
- Curriculum (14 vidyas): 4 Vedas + 6 Vedangas + Mimamsa + Nyaya + Dharmashastra + Puranas
| Scholar | Title / note |
| Govinda Varma | "Shadabhigna" |
| MV II | "Vidyadvijaguru" |
### Books → authors and language
| Work / poet | Author / note |
| Janasraya Chhandovichiti | Sanskrit work on prosody (chandas); credited to Madhava Varma II (title Janasraya); some say poet Gunaswami (Ganaswami) wrote it in the king's name |
| Vikramendra Varma I | Called "Mahakavi" (Indrapalanagara plates); no surviving Sanskrit work named |
- Official language: Sanskrit
- Telugu in records: Chikkulla plates use "Vijaya rajya samvatsarambul"; village names Telugu
### Universal angles
- Significance: Vedic-Puranic revival, temple + agrahara economy, first Hindu cave temples in Andhra
- Criticism & debates: Varna rules hardened; women's status fell (more child marriage and sati)
