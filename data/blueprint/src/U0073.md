### Cave sites → location, groups, patron: Ellora and Elephanta — Ellora - chronology and patronage
## 1. Ellora - chronology and patronage
- Chronological order (APPSC-G1 2022 key): Dashavatara → Ravan ki Khai → Dumar Lena → Kailasa (III, I, IV, II)
- Patronage:
- Buddhist caves = earliest (c. 600-730 CE, before the Rashtrakutas); some notes list them as Rashtrakuta
- Hindu caves = mainly Rashtrakuta
- Jain caves = 9th-10th century; some notes credit the Yadavas (later additions)
- Exam angle: cave count, the 12-17-5 split, and ordering of named caves.
## 2. Elephanta - patron debate, Trimurti
- Location: Island cave complex near Mumbai
- Patron: Rashtrakutas per some notes
- MPPSC 2021 key: Ellora and Elephanta = Rashtrakuta
- Debate: many historians date the main cave to the 6th century Kalachuris
- Masterpiece: Trimurti (Maheshamurti) - three-headed Shiva
- "Trimurthi (Elephanta)" + Kailasa = Rashtrakuta zenith of art
### Kailasa temple (Ellora Cave 16) → ruler, technique, features
- Ruler: Krishna I (r. 756-773 CE), Rashtrakuta
- Identity: Cave 16 of Ellora (APPSC-GS 2025)
- Technique:
- monolithic, carved top-down from a single basalt cliff
- Deity: Shiva as lord of Mount Kailasa (early Rashtrakuta rulers = staunch Shaivas)
| Feature | Detail |
| Tower | three-tiered Dravida vimana although in Maharashtra (Nagara territory) |
- Style: Rashtrakutas shaped the Vesara synthesis (Nagara + Dravida) → Deccan style
- Exam angle: "carved during the reign of" - Krishna I, not Dantidurga or Amoghavarsha.
### Universal angles
- Current relevance:
- India-Jordan twinning agreement between Petra and Ellora (UNESCO sites) (2026)
- Ellora is on the list of Buddhism-related UNESCO sites in India
- Significance: Rashtrakuta era raised Kannada to a classical medium + gave Ellora its Hindu peak
- AP angle: Rashtrakuta Krishna III helped Danarnava kill Eastern Chalukya Ammaraja II (970 CE) and seize Vengi
- FIRST/LAST:
- Decline by late 10th century under Chalukya-Chola pressure
