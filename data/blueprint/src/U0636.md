### Eastern Ghats — structure, features, rocks and limits
## 1. Structure
- Location: Between the eastern side of the Deccan Plateau and the western side of the Coastal Plain
- The major hill / mountain system of Andhra Pradesh
| Section | Name | Located in |
| Northern part | Eastern Ranges | North Andhra Pradesh |
| Southern part | Kadapa Ranges | South Andhra Pradesh |
- Division: Godavari and Krishna split the AP Ghats into these northern and southern parts
- Khondalite rocks / hills / mountains — bauxite-bearing mountain areas; the Eastern Ghats are the highest bauxite-bearing mountains
- Khondalite rocks mainly in erstwhile Visakhapatnam and East Godavari
- Rocks: Mainly charnockites and khondalites; Archaean, over 2.5 billion years old
- Character: Discontinuous, rugged, highly eroded range, not a continuous chain — discontinuous and eroded hills, cut by the Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri (PYQ, APPSC-GS 2025 key; May 2026)
- Vs Western Ghats: Older and lower
- Height: Average 400-600 m (some notes say 600-900 m)
- Limits: Begin with Mahendragiri in the north (in AP); beyond AP run through Javadi and Shevaroy hills → join the Western Ghats near the Nilgiris
- Meeting point at the Nilgiris (Gudalur) via Biligirirangan + Male Mahadeshwara hills
- Rivers rising here: Vamsadhara, Nagavali and Sabari

## 3. The long mountain pass — Seshachalam to Nallamala
- Seshachalam Hills and Nallamala Hills: Part of the Eastern Ghats — in Southern Andhra Pradesh
- The long mountain-pass zone lies between them — extends northwards from Seshachalam to the Nallamala Hills (the northern mountain-pass zone) for more than 300 km
- Ganjikunta: A notable mountain pass in the Eastern Ghats
- Connects sections between the Seshachalam and Nallamala hill ranges
- Dry deciduous forests and rocky elevations typical of peninsular India's eastern hill ranges

### Coastal lakes
## Pulicat
- Separated from the Bay of Bengal by Sriharikota Island
- India's second-largest brackish lagoon, shared by AP and Tamil Nadu
- Greater flamingos — Pulicat (not Kolleru)
- Threats: launches, thermal plants, sand mining
## Kolleru
- Freshwater lake between the Godavari and Krishna deltas
- Tammileru drains into Kolleru
- Birds: painted storks, grey pelicans, glossy ibises; threat — aquaculture encroachment
- Glacial erosion by Himalayan ice fields — not a threat to AP coastal lakes
## Other
- Rollapadu — dry grassland (rangeland) for the Great Indian Bustard, not a lagoon
