# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:244 - INA trials and the naval mutiny of 1946; impact on British decision to quit
### Events in order
- The INA trials and the RIN revolt showed the Indian Army - the Raj's sword - could no longer be trusted
- 1934 — RIN established
- Nov 1945 — INA trials begin, Red Fort, Delhi (to May 1946)
- 12 Nov 1945 — INA Day
- 21 Nov 1945 — Calcutta student protest; firing killed two
- 11 Feb 1946 — Calcutta protests over Rashid Ali's 7-year sentence
- 18 Feb 1946 — RIN mutiny, HMIS Talwar, Bombay
- 19 Feb 1946 — Attlee announces Cabinet Mission
- 20 Feb 1947 — British to leave by June 1948
### The accused: unity symbol
- Shah Nawaz Khan
### Trial & defence
- Verdict: guilty of waging war; life transportation remitted by Auchinleck (Jan 1946)
- INA Defence Committee set up by Congress
- Lead counsel Bhulabhai Desai (last major contribution)
- With Sapru, Asaf Ali, Sarat Chandra Bose, K.N. Katju, Nehru
- Not: C.R. Das, Motilal Nehru (dead)
- Congress, League, Communists united behind accused
### RIN mutiny: who did what
| Person / group | Role |
| M.S. Khan | President, Naval Central Strike Committee |
| Aruna Asaf Ali, Communists | Backed the revolt |
| Bombay mill, railway workers, students | Joined the ratings |
| Patel and Jinnah | Persuaded surrender |
### RIN: scale & causes
- About 20,000 personnel, 78 ships, 20 shore establishments
- Spread to Karachi
- Congress + League flags hoisted together
- Events 18-22 February; then surrender
### Impact on British exit
- 1. INA trials + RIN revolt ↓
- 2. Loyalty of armed forces destroyed ↓
- 3. RIAF, Army also disaffected ↓
- 4. 2.5-million Indian Army no longer trusted ↓

### INA trials — accused, defence, verdict
| Accused | Community |
| Shah Nawaz Khan | Muslim |
- Verdict: Guilty of waging war; transportation for life remitted by C-in-C Auchinleck (Jan 1946)
- Rashid Ali: Later 7-year sentence on this INA officer sparked protests
- Political support: Congress, League and Communists united in support of the accused
## INA Defence Committee
- Set up by: Congress
- Lead counsel: Bhulabhai Desai (his last major contribution)
- Other lawyers: Tej Bahadur Sapru, Asaf Ali, Sarat Chandra Bose, K.N. Katju, Jawaharlal Nehru
- Did not appear: C.R. Das and Motilal Nehru (dead by then)
- ⚠ Trap: UPPSC 2013 key: Sapru, not Jinnah
- ⚠ Trap: BPSC 2020 key: of Rajendra Prasad, Bhulabhai Desai, K.M. Munshi, Patel - only Bhulabhai Desai
- Exam angle: APPSC 2018 - Bhulabhai Desai was counsel, not an accused; UPSC 2021 - the three were INA officers.
### RIN mutiny — ship, city, numbers, leaders
- Grievances: Ratings struck over poor food, low pay, racial discrimination
- Spread: About 20,000 personnel, 78 ships, 20 shore establishments; also Karachi
| Person / group | Role |
| M.S. Khan | President, Naval Central Strike Committee |
| Aruna Asaf Ali + the Communists | Backers (Communists supported the revolt) |
| Bombay mill + railway workers and students | Joined the ratings |
| Patel and Jinnah | Persuaded the ratings to surrender |
- Unity: Congress and Muslim League flags hoisted together
- Surrender: Events of 18-22 February; Congress + League leaders did not back a violent revolt
- ⚠ Congress and League flags flew together, yet both leaderships (Patel, Jinnah) asked for surrender; only Communists + Aruna Asaf Ali backed it
### Impact on the British decision to quit — factor → effect
| Factor | Effect |
| Loss of loyalty | With the INA trials, the RIN mutiny "destroyed the loyalty" of the British Indian armed forces |
| Military reliance | The British relied on the 2.5-million Indian Army; INA trials + RIN revolt showed this "sword" could no longer be trusted |
| Armed forces | Sections of the RIAF and Army showed disaffection during the RIN revolt |
- Attlee's view: Gandhi's role in the immediate transfer of power "minimal" vs the loss of military loyalty

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:244
### Events in order
- INA Day — 12 November 1945
- RIN Mutiny — 18 February 1946, ratings of HMIS Talwar, Bombay
- Cabinet Mission announced by Attlee — 19 February 1946; arrived 24 March 1946
- Attlee's statement of 20 February 1947 — British to leave by June 1948; power actually transferred August 1947
### Trial & defence
- Sentences of Sahgal, Shah Nawaz Khan and Dhillon cancelled January 1946 by C-in-C Claude Auchinleck
- INA Defence Committee — set up by the Indian National Congress
- Lead counsel — Bhulabhai Desai (his last major contribution)
- Tej Bahadur Sapru and Asaf Ali in the defence; Motilal Nehru did not appear (dead by then)
### RIN mutiny: who did what
- M.S. Khan — president of the Naval Central Strike Committee
- Patel and Jinnah persuaded the ratings to surrender
### RIN: scale & causes
- Spread to Karachi too — not limited to Bombay
- About 20,000 personnel, 78 ships, 20 shore establishments
- 80th anniversary observed 18 February 2026
### Impact on British exit
- ★ Order: Second Round Table Conference (Sept–Dec 1931) → Quit India resolution (8 Aug 1942, Gowalia Tank) → RIN Mutiny (18 Feb 1946)
- RIN revolt was of naval ratings, not civilian workers; sections of the RIAF and Army also showed discontent
- British depended on the 2.5-million-strong Indian Army — INA trials and RIN revolt showed it could no longer be fully trusted
### Exam corner
- Attlee: Gandhi's role in the immediate transfer of power small compared with the loss of military loyalty
