### Articles → Centre's directions to states (256, 257, 339, 350A, 365)
| Article | Provision |
| 256 | States must exercise executive power in compliance with Parliament's laws; Union may direct |
| 257 | State executive must not impede the Union; Centre can direct on Means of communication of national or military importance and on protection of railways |
| National Emergency | Directions on any matter |
| Art. 365 | State fails to follow Union directions → President may hold its government cannot be carried on per the Constitution → President's Rule (Art. 356) (PYQ 2017, 2018) |
### Articles → delegation of functions (258, 258A)
| Article | Who entrusts | To whom | Consent |
| 258(1) | President | State govt or its officers | With state consent (by consent) |
| 258(2) | Union by law | State govt or its officers | by law without consent |
| 258A (7th CAA, 1956) | Governor | Union govt or its officers | With GoI consent - State entrusts functions to the Union with the Centre's consent (APPSC-GS 2023) |
- ⚠ Trap: APP writes "Parliament" for 258(1) - it is the President
- ⚠ Trap: UPSC notes swap headings of 258 and 258A - 258A is the state entrusting functions to the Union (PYQ 2023 answer)
- 258(1) notification: A legislative act; specific or general
- Purpose: Avoid rigidity and deadlock
### All India Services — Article 312, origin, removal, classification — Creation and existing services
## 1. Creation and existing services
- Art. 312: Parliament may create new AIS (incl. an All India Judicial Service) after a Rajya Sabha resolution by 2/3 of members present and voting
- Existing AIS: IAS, IPS, IFoS (Indian Forest Service)
- Indian Forest Service created 1966
- Governed by the All India Services Act, 1951
## 2. Origin - FIRSTs and persons
| Item | Fact |
| Sardar Patel | Championed AIS as the "Steel Frame" |
| UPSC | Replaced the Federal PSC in 1950 |
## 3. Recruitment and control
- Recruitment: UPSC on an all-India basis; Centre recruits and trains; officers serve in state cadres
- Removal: Dismissal/removal only by the Union Government (dismissed or removed only by the Union government) (PYQ 2017, 2019; APPSC-G1 2019)
- Exam angle: "AIS officer can be removed only by the Union Government" is the correct statement.
### All India Services — Article 312, origin, removal, classification — Federal critique and friction
## 1. Federal critique and friction
- Unitary feature: Restricts state autonomy; officers governed by Central rules, states lack full control
- APPSC-G2 2025: A and R both true, R explains A
- IAS (Cadre) Rules, 1954 amendments: Centre can order central deputation without state or officer consent; Centre's order final after a set time
## 2. All-India vs Central vs State services - classify
| Service | Category | Nature |
| IAS, IPS, Indian Forest Service (IFoS) | All India Services (All-India Services) | Common to Union and states - same kind of service in both (PYQ 2007) |
| Indian Foreign Service (IFS) | Central Services - Central Service (APP) | Union subjects only; e.g. Indian Foreign Service (APP MCQ) |
| Indian Revenue Service (IRS) | Central Service [GK] | Union subjects only |
- ⚠ IFoS = Indian Forest Service (AIS); IFS = Indian Foreign Service (Central Service)
### Armed and central forces in states — Art. 355, Entry 2A, CAPF dispute
- Deployment: Centre may deploy armed/central forces with or without state consent for its Art. 355 duty
- Basis: Entry 2A, Union List (42nd CAA)
- State objection: Public order and police are State List (Entries 1, 2)
- CAPF dispute:
- Centre has not implemented 2025 SC order granting CAPF officers Organised Group A status + reducing IPS deputation
- Central Armed Forces (General Administration) Bill expected in response
### Commissions, PSCs and tribunals — JSPSC, CAT, ARC, Sarkaria
- Art. 315: UPSC; an SPSC for each state; a JSPSC for 2+ states if their legislatures so resolve
- Created by an Act of Parliament; members appointed by the President
- Art. 323A (42nd CAA, 1976): Administrative tribunals for Union and states, individually or jointly
- CAT (Administrative Tribunals Act 1985): principal bench New Delhi + 18 benches
- 69 members = 35 judicial + 34 administrative
| Commission | Key facts |
| ARC (1966) | Chaired by Morarji Desai, then K. Hanumanthaiya; M.C. Setalvad study team (1969); urged Inter-State Council, non-partisan Governors |
| Sarkaria (June 1983) | Justice R.S. Sarkaria, B. Sivaraman, S.R. Sen; 247 recommendations; permanent ISC; Art. 356 sparingly |
- ⚠ JSPSC = by Act of Parliament, members by President; CAT = Art. 323A, 42nd CAA, 1976
### Cooperation devices — constitutional vs extra-constitutional
| Type | Devices |
| Constitutional devices | Inter-State Council (Art. 263); Art. 262: Water disputes; Art. 307: Inter-state trade authority |
| Extra-constitutional devices | NDC, Central Council of Health, Central Council for Local Government, NE Council, Governors' Conference (President), CMs' Conference (PM), Chief Secretaries' Conference |
- Art. 261 (full faith and credit): Full faith and credit throughout India to public acts, records and judicial proceedings of the Union and every state (APPSC-GS 2023)
- Art. 261(3): Final civil court judgments can be executed anywhere in India
- Integrated judiciary: HC judges appointed by President consulting CJI + Governor; common HC for 2+ states possible
- Grants: Art. 275 grants for ST welfare and Scheduled Area administration
- ⚠ Governors' Conference = chaired by President; CMs' Conference = PM
### Zonal Councils and North Eastern Council — Acts, chairman, AP's zone
## 1. Zonal Councils - number, chairman, composition
- Basis: Statutory bodies under Part III of the States Reorganisation Act, 1956
- Number: 5 councils - Northern, Central, Eastern, Western, Southern; proposed by Nehru in 1956
- Chairman: Union Home Minister (common to all five)
- Vice-chairman: CMs of member states by annual rotation
- Members: CM + two other ministers from each state; Administrators/LGs of member UTs
- Advisers (no vote): NITI Aayog representative, Chief Secretaries, Development Commissioners
- Secretariat: Inter-State Council Secretariat, MHA
- Nature: Deliberative and advisory; recommendations not binding
- Southern Zonal Council: Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala, Telangana, Puducherry
- ⚠ Older APP table leaves out Telangana
## 2. Acts → year → provision
| Act | Year | Provision |
| States Reorganisation Act, 1956 | Passed November 1956 | Created 14 states and 6 Union Territories (MPPSC 2025 key) |
| NEC Act, 1971 (North-Eastern Council Act, 1971) | 1971 | NEC as a separate body |
- Andhra Pradesh (1 Nov 1956): Telangana merged with Andhra State
- NEC Chairman: Since 2018, Union Home Minister ex-officio
- ⚠ Zonal Councils = statutory (SRA 1956), not constitutional; NEC = separate Act (1971)
