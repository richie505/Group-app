### Socialists, communists and the KMPP / PSP → parties, years, elections
## 1. Socialists and communists (1934-1940)
- CPI ban: 23 July 1934
- Entry into socialists: Andhra communists (Sundarayya, Chandra Rajeswara Rao, Krovi Lingaraju, Gadde Lingayya) joined Ranga's Andhra Socialist Party on Prof. Gout's advice to work inside Congress
- Separate organisation: Formally founded Andhra Communist Party at Kakinada in January 1936; Sundarayya secretary
- Break:
- Nationally CSP expelled the communists in 1940
- WWII: communists backed "People's War" (1941) + opposed Quit India → widened the break
## 2. KMPP and PSP
- National merger: KMPP + Socialist Party → PSP in 1952
| Election | Result (socialist-type parties) |
| 1952 Madras (375 seats) | KMP 35, Socialists 13; United Front (164) chose Prakasam, but Governor Sri Prakasa invited Rajaji |
| 1955 Andhra State | Congress 119, KLP 22, PSP 13, CPI 15, Praja Party 5 |
- Capital demand (1953): PSP wanted Tirupati as Andhra State capital (G2 2025); Kurnool chosen instead
