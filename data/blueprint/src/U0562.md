### AP Tourism Policy 2024-29 — features, incentives, targets
## 1. Policy framework
- Current policy: AP Tourism Policy 2024-29, aligned with Swarna Andhra @2047
- Industry status: Tourism + hospitality across all segments
- Mega projects: Tailor-made packages for Mega/Ultra Mega projects
- Incentives: Fiscal, recurring and special
- Non-fiscal support:
- Investment tracker; Destination Management Organisations
- Targets:
- Tourism > 20% of state GVA and 20% of the workforce
- AP among India's top three destinations
- Budget hotels: Promoted mainly to attract tourists (APPSC G2 2008 key)
## 2. New in 2026
- Vision: Swarna Andhra Tourism Vision 2047 unveiled at the FAITH conclave
- Award: "Best Tourism Promotion State" at GTM 2026

### Tourism places and circuits — APTDC, Buddhist sites, beaches, cruise
## 1. APTDC
- Status: Fully state-owned operational arm
- Revenue: ₹106.80 crore (2024-25)
- Runs: Haritha hotels, boating, sound-and-light shows, ropeways (Srisailam)

- Thematic circuits: More than 25 thematic circuits = 2 Buddhist, 10 temple, 5 beach, 4 river cruise, 3 eco-tourism, 2 sea cruise, 1 seaplane
