### National e-Governance Plan, 2006 — core infrastructure and MMP categories
- Approval: 18 May 2006 with 27 MMPs and 10 components; later expanded to 31 MMPs
## 1. Three-tier core infrastructure
| Tier | Component | Role |
| Front end | CSCs | Village ICT kiosks run by local entrepreneurs |
| Network | SWAN | State HQ to block/taluka links |
| Back end | SDC | State data centres hosting applications |
## 2. MMP categories
| Category | Examples |
| Central | MCA21, Passport, Income Tax, UID, Pensions, Posts [GK] |
| State | Land Records, Treasuries, e-District, e-Panchayat, CCTNS (police) (APP; IYB) [GK] |
| Integrated | eCourts, e-Procurement, CSC, India Portal, e-Biz [GK] |
- Other NeGP pieces:
- MSDG (2011) for mobile services
- Meghraj / GI Cloud
- National Portal india.gov.in = single window
- ⚠ 27 MMPs at approval → 31 MMPs (NeGP) → 44 MMPs under e-Kranti

### Digital India and e-Kranti — launch, vision areas, nine pillars, principles — Digital India - launch and nature
## 1. Digital India - launch and nature
- Launch: PM Narendra Modi on 1 July 2015 (APPSC 2025 key)
- Coordinator: MeitY (APPSC 2025 key); Central and State ministries as partners
- Nature: An umbrella programme ("IT + IT = IT"); PPP preferred wherever feasible
- Aim: A knowledge-based economy + digitally empowered society
## 2. Three vision areas
| Vision area | Examples |
| Digital infrastructure as a utility | High-speed internet, cradle-to-grave digital identity, CSC access, cloud locker |
| Governance and services on demand | Real-time online services, cashless transactions, GIS decision support |
| Digital empowerment of citizens | Universal digital literacy, services in Indian languages, entitlements on cloud |
### Digital India and e-Kranti — launch, vision areas, nine pillars, principles — Nine pillars, with latest figures
## 1. Nine pillars, with latest figures
| No. | Pillar | Status / flagship / data |

### Digital India and e-Kranti — launch, vision areas, nine pillars, principles — e-Kranti (NeGP 2.0)
## 1. e-Kranti (NeGP 2.0)
- e-Kranti = NeGP 2.0: Approved 2015 under Digital India; 44 MMPs (pillar 5 of Digital India)
- Vision: "Transforming e-Governance for Transforming Governance"
- APPSC 2017: mission for electronic delivery of all government services = e-Kranti
- Principles:
- Transformation not translation
- Integrated not individual services
- cloud by default, mobile first

### India Stack, DPI and education platforms → role
## 1. India Stack layers
- India Stack: Aadhaar + UPI + DigiLocker + CoWIN + ONDC + Account Aggregator → open, interoperable public rails
- Three layers (Aug 2026):
- Identity = Aadhaar
- Payments = UPI
- Data = DEPA / Account Aggregator (consent-based)
## 2. DPI platforms → role
| Platform | Role |
| UPI / BHIM | Real-time payments; UPI live in 11 countries; PayNow-UPI link (Singapore, 2023) |
| DigiLocker | Cloud document wallet; 56.06 crore users, 970.70 crore documents (IYB) |
| Bhashini | Translation Mission (Jul 2022); 260 open-source API-based AI models; AI language platform integrated with NIC tools such as CollabFiles |
| BhashaNet | Universal Acceptance portal (NIXI and MeitY, 21 Mar 2024) |
- DigiLocker: Launched Feb 2015; 10 MB, extendable to 1 GB; CBSE marksheets from 2015
## 3. Education platforms → purpose
| Platform | Purpose |
| SWAYAM | MOOCs for higher education - free courses from IITs, IIMs, central universities |
| DIKSHA | National school e-content repository; QR-coded textbooks, teacher training; 10 crore+ users |
| PM eVIDYA (2020) | One Class, One TV Channel (DTH), radio/podcasts; integrates DIKSHA and SWAYAM |
- ⚠ SWAYAM = higher education; DIKSHA = school
### Impact data and the digital divide
## 1. Impact indicators
- UPI: 10 years old; transaction volume up 13,000-fold (25 Aug 2026)
- Aadhaar: Reaches 145 crore+ people (Aug 2026)
- Internet access:
- ~500 million came online 2016-2021 as data cost fell from ~$4 to under 30 cents/GB (Jul 2026)
## 2. Digital divide
- Rural gaps: GP fibre (BharatNet) + 4G coverage in villages
- Language: Bhashini AI language platform (see India Stack and DPI)
- PMGDISHA: Rural digital literacy scheme (2017), one person per household
### Universal angles
- Current relevance: DARPG launched NeSDA 2025 portal (Jun 2026); IVFRT extended to 2031 (Apr 2026)
- Significance: DPI raised the digital economy to 12-14% of GDP (Aug 2026)
- AP angle:
- Singapore delegation studied AP's RTGS Data Lake and Command Centre (Jul 2026)
- FIRST/LAST: NeGP 2006 → Digital India 2015 → e-Kranti (NeGP 2.0) 2015
- Criticism & debates: Digital divide, shared devices in rural areas, data privacy
