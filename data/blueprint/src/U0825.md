### World Bank Water–Food Nexus Framework report
## 1. News
- World Bank report released ahead of World Water Day 2026 — proposes a "Water-Food Nexus Framework"
- Population to reach ~10 billion by 2050; current systems can sustainably feed only ~3.4 billion
- Climate change by 2050: food production down 6–14%; up to 1.36 billion in severe food insecurity
## 2. Framework
- Two dimensions: water stress level + position in food trade (net importer/exporter)
- Four categories: water-secure importer, water-secure exporter, water-stressed exporter, water-stressed importer
- ⚠ India = water-stressed food exporter — groundwater over-extraction in Punjab, Haryana, Delhi, western UP
- Data: geospatial + household surveys across 58 countries
## 3. Numbers
- 10% rise in farm productivity cuts poverty by 2.5–3%
- Irrigation expansion could create 245 million jobs (218 million in Sub-Saharan Africa) — ~4 jobs per hectare
- Investment needed: $600 billion – $1.8 trillion; of $663 billion agri spending (2023) only $27 billion on irrigation
- Drip irrigation reduces weeds and erosion — ⚠ not salinity
