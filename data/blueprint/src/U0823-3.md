- 500 MW, sodium-cooled; built by BHAVINI (Bharatiya Nabhikiya Vidyut Nigam Ltd, under DAE); 200+ Indian industries
## 2. Concepts
- Criticality: self-sustaining chain reaction — k = 1 (subcritical k<1, supercritical k>1)
- FBR: produces more fissile fuel than it consumes; fast neutrons, no moderator; MOX fuel (Pu + U); U-238 blanket → Pu-239; liquid sodium coolant (low dissolved oxygen for corrosion resistance)
- Chain reaction idea — Leo Szilard (1933)
## 3. Three-stage programme (Homi J. Bhabha)
| Stage | Reactor | Fuel |
| I | PHWR | Natural uranium → Pu-239 by-product |
| II | FBR | Pu-based MOX |
| III | Thorium reactors | Th-232 → U-233 |
- Plants: Tarapur, Rajasthan, Kalpakkam, Narora, Kakrapar, Kaiga, Kudankulam; Kalpakkam has MAPS, IGCAR

### Gut microbiome and food systems
## 1. Gut microbiome facts
- Trillions of microbes (~equal to human cells); weight 1–2 kg (like the brain)
- 2–20 million microbial genes vs ~20,000 human genes; ~2,000 bacterial species, mostly in the colon
- ~90% beneficial — Bacteroidetes, Firmicutes; unique like a fingerprint
- Dysbiosis → "leaky gut", inflammation; SCFAs (short-chain fatty acids) reduce inflammation; synthesises vitamin K and some B vitamins; gut–brain axis
## 2. Disruptors
- Sugar favours E. coli, Candida; reduces Bifidobacteria, Lactobacilli
- Estrobolome — gut bacteria regulating estrogen metabolism; disrupted by pesticides
- Antibiotics in livestock → AMR; ⚠ bacteria, viruses and fungi can all develop AMR
- Fat digested by lipases; proteins by trypsin/chymotrypsin; starch by amylase
