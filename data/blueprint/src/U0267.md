# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:266
### Organisations
- Progressive Organisation of Women (POW) — 1974
- Stree Shakti Sanghatana — 1978

- Bhumika (Telugu feminist magazine, 1993) — editor Kondaveeti Satyavathi
### Women leaders: firsts
- ★ Muthulakshmi Reddy — India's first woman legislator; nominated to Madras Legislative Council, 1926
- Ammannaraja — anti-dedication (Devadasi) bill, Madras Assembly, 1937, after Muthulakshmi Reddy's campaign
- Pratibha Bharati — first woman Speaker, AP Legislative Assembly, 1999
- Sharda Mukherjee — first woman Governor (1977)
### Laws → year
| Law / provision | Year | Point |
| Madras Devadasi Act | 1947 | earlier Act |
| AP Devadasis (Prohibition of Dedication) Act | 1988 | — |

- Article 243D(3) — at least one-third seats for women in Panchayats (73rd Amendment, 1992)
- Article 243T — Municipalities
### Roots and data
- Janana and Satihita Bodhini — journals of Kandukuri Veeresalingam; women's education, widow remarriage
- 1919 Machilipatnam Adi-Andhra conference (Bhagya Reddy Varma) — demanded abolition of Jogini and Devadasi systems
