# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:223 - Depressed classes movement, Communal Award and the Poona Pact 1932
### Communal Award (1932)
- Gandhi: separate electorates for Dalits would permanently divide Hindu society
- Announced 16 August 1932 by British PM Ramsay MacDonald
- Context: 2nd RTC failed over minority representation
- Covered 11 groups
- Women: 3% of seats in all provinces except NWFP
### Separate electorates for
- Religious minorities
- Muslims, Sikhs
- Indian Christians
- Others
- Europeans
- Anglo-Indians
- Depressed Classes
- Double vote: separate + general electorate
### Responses
| Who | Response |
| Gandhi | Fast unto death from 20 September 1932, Yerwada Jail, Poona |
### Depressed classes: milestones
- 1930 — Simon Commission report: first used "Scheduled Castes"
- 1932 — 2nd RTC fails → Communal Award → Gandhi's fast → Poona Pact
- 1935 — Term "Scheduled Castes" fixed by GoI Act 1935
- 1936 — First SC list

- 1. Separate electorate for Depressed Classes (Award) ↓

### Communal Award, 1932 → date, provisions, persons, response
## 1. Announcement
- Date: August 1932 (16 Aug)
- By: British PM Ramsay MacDonald
- Context: 2nd RTC failed over minority representation
## 2. Provisions
- Groups: 11 groups
- Separate electorates: Muslims, Sikhs, Europeans, Indian Christians, Anglo-Indians + Depressed Classes (declared a minority)
- Women: 3% of seats in all provinces except NWFP
- Depressed classes: Double vote - one in separate electorate + one in general electorate
## 3. Response
| Person / body | Response |
| Gandhi | Fast unto death from 20 September 1932, Yerwada Jail, Poona. Argued separate electorates for Dalits would permanently divide Hindu society |
- ⚠ Award = 16 Aug 1932 (MacDonald); Gandhi's fast = 20 Sept 1932 (Yerwada)
