# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 5:52 - Defence indigenisation - Make in India in defence, DAP 2020, iDEX, defence corridors, FDI in defence
### Policy milestones
- Buy (Indian-IDDM) = Indigenously Designed, Developed and Manufactured: the most preferred category
- May 2001 — Private participation 100%; FDI up to 26%
- 2016 — DPP 2016: Buy (Indian-IDDM) added (Dhirendra Singh committee)
- 2017 — Strategic Partnership model added
- April 2018 — iDEX launched by PM
- 2020 — DAP 2020: IDDM top priority; offsets dropped in G2G deals
- 2021 — OFB dissolved → 41 factories into 7 DPSUs
- 2025 — Year of Reforms; DPM 2025 effective 1 Nov 2025
### DAP 2020 priority
- ▲ 1. Buy (Indian-IDDM) — Top priority
- ▸ 2. Buy (Indian)
- ▸ 3. Buy and Make (Indian)
- ▸ 5. Buy (Global - Manufacture in India)
### Innovation schemes

### Policies, committees and reforms → year and provision
| Policy / reform | Year | Provision |
| OFB corporatisation | 2021 | 200-year-old OFB dissolved → 41 factories regrouped into 7 DPSUs |
| DPP 2016 | 2016 | Replaced DPP 2013; first added Buy (Indian-IDDM) on Dhirendra Singh committee's advice |
| Strategic Partnership (SP) model | 2017 | Added to DPP; selected Indian private firms team up with foreign OEMs to build major platforms in India |
| DAP 2020 | 2020 | Buy (Indian-IDDM) top priority; dropped offset clauses in government-to-government + single-vendor deals |

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 5:52
### Policy milestones
- May 2001 — defence opened to 100% Indian private participation, FDI up to 26% under licensing
- 2021 — OFB (200 years old) dissolved; its 41 factories regrouped into 7 DPSUs (incl. Gliders India, Troop Comforts)

- DAP 2020 dropped offset clauses in government-to-government and single-vendor deals
- MoD declared 2025 the Year of Reforms; began reviewing DAP 2020 to support JVs and technology transfer for private sector
### DAP 2020 priority
## Indigenisation
- Buy (Indian-IDDM) — most preferred category under DAP 2020
- Five Positive Indigenisation Lists (bar imports of listed items after set dates); SRIJAN portal supports indigenisation
- Challenges: dependence on imported engines and semiconductors, small R&D share, low FDI
- India still outside the top 25 arms exporters; still imports jet engines and defence chips
## Partnerships
- L&T + Navantia (Spain) — Teaming Agreement, July 2023, New Delhi, for P-75(I) bid
- K9 Vajra-T self-propelled gun — India–South Korea (2020 Roadmap for Defence Industries Cooperation)
