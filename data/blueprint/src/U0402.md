### Gender mainstreaming and gender audit → meaning
## 1. Gender mainstreaming
- The process of integrating gender perspectives into policies, programmes, laws and institutions
- Gender lens in every policy → assessing every policy's impact on women and men; a Beijing (1995) strategy [GK]
- Promoted globally during the Fourth World Conference on Women — Beijing, 1995
- Aims: Achieve gender equality; reduce gender discrimination; promote women's participation; ensure equitable development
- Promotes: Women's empowerment; inclusive governance; social justice; sustainable development
## 2. Gender Audit
- A systematic assessment of policies, programmes, institutions and budgets — checking whether they affect women and men equally
- Evaluates gender equality; promotes gender justice; identifies gender gaps; improves women's participation; ensures gender-sensitive governance
- Includes: Gender budgeting; equal opportunities; workplace safety; decision-making participation

### Reproductive rights laws → provisions
## 1. MTP (Amendment) Act, 2021
- Limits:
- 24 weeks: special categories (2026)
- Beyond 24 weeks: only for substantial foetal abnormality → State Medical Board decides; no gestation limit applies
- Prohibited: Disclosing the woman's identity [GK]
## 2. Surrogacy (Regulation) Act 2021
- Core rule: Bans commercial surrogacy; allows altruistic surrogacy (medical costs, insurance only [GK])
- Couple: Needs certificates of essentiality and eligibility
- Surrogate: A woman can be a surrogate only once
- Also prohibited: Sex selection; abandoning the child [GK]
- Abortion of surrogate foetus: Not banned outright → needs the surrogate's written consent + authority's approval/authorisation under the MTP Act
- ⚠ Trap: "Abortion of the surrogate child is prohibited" was NOT in the 2019 Bill → the "NOT proposed" answer (APPSC 2018)
## 3. ART (Regulation) Act 2021 [GK]
- Permitted: Registered ART clinics and banks; national registry
- Prohibited: Sex-selective ART; sale of gametes/embryos
## 4. PCPNDT Act 1994
- Ban: Sex selection and pre-natal sex determination; tests allowed only for genetic abnormality
### Work, pay and maternity laws → provisions — Equal pay
## 1. Equal pay
