### Wind energy terms → meaning — conversion, HAWT vs VAWT, CUF, onshore vs offshore
- Conversion: Wind's kinetic energy → blade/rotor rotation → generator → electricity (UPSC S&T)
- i.e. kinetic energy → rotational (mechanical) energy of blades/rotor → electrical energy in the generator
| Term | Meaning [GK] |
| HAWT | Rotor axis parallel to wind; 3-blade, needs yaw control; standard utility turbine |
| VAWT | Axis vertical (Darrieus, Savonius); takes wind from any direction; lower efficiency |
| Betz limit | Max 16/27 = 59.3% of wind's kinetic energy can be captured |
| Power law | Power varies with cube of wind speed and swept area |
| Capacity factor | Actual output / output at full rated power over time |
- Capacity Utilisation Factor: Actual output / maximum possible output
- Wind CUF 36-40% vs solar ~21% vs coal ~85% (Aug 2026)
- Turbine classes (IEC I, II, III): Set by site wind speed - Class I for high-wind, Class III for low-wind sites
- Onshore vs offshore:
- Onshore: On land; cheaper, easier upkeep; the cheapest new electricity globally - costs fell 4% in 2025 (IRENA, Jul 2026)
- Offshore: Sea/large lakes; stronger, steadier winds → higher capacity factor, no land conflict (UPSC)
- Offshore cons: costly, harder to maintain, corrosion, undersea cables, navigation and marine-ecology risks
- Floating turbines for deep water
- ⚠ Darrieus and Savonius = VAWT; Betz limit = 59.3%; power ∝ cube of wind speed

### Policies and schemes → year and provision — offshore, hybrid, repowering — Offshore wind
## 1. Offshore wind
- National Offshore Wind Energy Policy 2015: Framework for offshore farms in India's EEZ
- MNRE nodal ministry; NIWE nodal agency
- VGF scheme for offshore wind: Approved 2024 - Rs 7,453 crore (₹7,453 cr) for the first 1 GW (500 MW each off Gujarat and TN/Tamil Nadu)
## 2. Wind-solar hybrid and round-the-clock power
- National Wind-Solar Hybrid Policy (2018): Large grid-connected wind-solar PV hybrids - wind turbine generators and solar PV at the same point of grid connection
- ISTS charges waived: For projects commissioned by 30 June 2025
- Green Energy Corridor (from 2015): Intra-State GEC-I in 8 states: Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu
### Policies and schemes → year and provision — offshore, hybrid, repowering — Repowering and incentives
## 1. Repowering and incentives
- Repowering: Replacing ageing, less efficient turbines with bigger, more powerful modern units on the same windy sites to raise output/generation
- Many old Indian turbines are inefficient (Jun 2025)
- Policy: National Repowering Policy 2016 → revised as Repowering and Life Extension Policy 2023
- Other supports:
- Wind RPO trajectory to 2030
- ⚠ Offshore policy 2015 → Repowering 2016 → Hybrid policy 2018 → Repowering and Life Extension 2023 → offshore VGF 2024
