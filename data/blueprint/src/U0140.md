### Asaf Jahi Hyderabad → founding, administration and Nizams in sequence
## 1. Founding
- Founder: Mir Qamar-ud-din Khan Siddiqi, the first Nizam (APPSC 2018, 2022)
- Background: Hyderabad first envisaged by Deccan viceroy Zulfiqar Khan, governed through Daud Khan from 1708
- Founding: Farrukh Siyar made him Mughal Governor of the Deccan
- Defeated Mubariz Khan at Shakar Kheda (1724) → virtually independent state; 1724 = founding year
- Administration: Nizam-ul-Mulk tolerant: Puran Chand as Diwan; Mughal-type jagirdari administration
- Marathas: Kept them out in his lifetime; after his death (1748) Hyderabad vulnerable

### Causes of decline → factor and effect
| Factor | Effect |
| Aurangzeb's policies | Empire at greatest extent: Kashmir to Jinji, Hindukush to Chittagong; intolerant policies + long Deccan wars → weakened foundations, drained treasury |
| Weak successors and noble factions | Sayyids' control bred corruption and mismanagement; nobles acted independently; rivalries broke central authority |
| Jagirdari and agrarian crises | Shrinking fiscal base → peasant and zamindar revolts |
| Other causes | Maratha rise, invasions of Nadir Shah and Abdali, European companies |
