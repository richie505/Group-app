### Rural, urban and tribal — definitions, elements and features
- Elements of rural community: Territory, we-feeling (community sentiment), relative self-sufficiency, small size, homogeneity
- ⚠ Trap: Cultural diversity is NOT an element (UPPSC 2021)
- Features of urban life: Competition, impersonal relationships, loss of humanistic values, anonymity, weak social control
- ⚠ Trap: Informal ties are NOT a feature (JPSC 2021)
- Urbanism vs urbanisation: Urbanism = social, cultural, behavioural side of urban life; urbanisation = process of population shifting to urban areas (UPSC)
- Tribal features: Territorial community, common name, dialect, claim of common ancestor, animism/totemism, hereditary chieftainship, clan system, youth dormitories, simple economy
- Imperial Gazetteer: Families with common name, dialect and territory, originally endogamous
- Exam angle: "Which is NOT an element/feature"; village → scholar matching.

### Comparisons — village vs town, tribe vs peasant, primary vs secondary urbanisation
| Feature | Village | Town/City |
| Occupation | Mainly agriculture | Mainly non-agricultural |
| Size and density | Small, sparse | Large, dense |
| Relations | Primary, face-to-face | Secondary, impersonal |
| Social control | Informal (family, caste, custom) | Formal (law, police) |
- A-R (UPPSC 2009):
- "Dividing line between village and town begins to be passed with traders and manufacturers" = true
- "They wholly depend on village resources" = false
| Tribe | Peasant society |
| Relatively isolated, kinship-based, broadly egalitarian | Part of a wider society; stratified by caste and class |
| Subsistence economy, common control of land and forest | Market-linked; individual land and tenancy |
| Primary urbanisation | Orthogenetic - folk culture is carried forward into a Great Tradition |
| Secondary urbanisation | Heterogenetic - new modes of thought clash with or replace older cultures |
- Source: UPPSC 2013 (primary urbanisation = development of the Great tradition); typology by Redfield and Singer
- ⚠ Orthogenetic = primary = moral order; heterogenetic = secondary = technical order

### Urban problems and migration — causes and effects
| Factor | Effect |
| Industrialisation (Driver) | Drives urbanisation by creating jobs; rural-to-urban migration = main component |
| Density | Rising crime |
- Problems: lack of social control, maladjustment, environmental degradation, visible rich-poor segregation, alienation
- ES 2025-26 view:
- Informality (slums, informal labour, unregistered firms) = structural outcome of rapid urbanisation; integrate, not eradicate
- Low-income workers choose informal housing near jobs; peripheral formal affordable housing lacks connectivity
- Cities lack fiscal autonomy (institutional fragmentation)
- Caste in cities: Persists through slum concentration, residential segregation, caste networks for jobs
- Migration: Main source States: UP and Bihar
- Push: monsoon failure, rural unemployment
- Migration leaves ageing parents behind
- Policies against distress migration:
- SPMRM (Rurban Mission, 2016): "rural soul, urban amenities"
- PMEGP through KVIC

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 2:115
### Village vs town
- Village — agricultural, small and sparse, homogeneous, close face-to-face ties, informal control
- Village-to-town transition begins with traders and manufacturers (not wholly dependent on village resources)
- Tribal society — fairly isolated, kinship-based; subsistence economy; common control of land and forest
- Peasant society — part of wider caste- and class-divided, market-linked society; individual land, tenancy

- Rural community elements — territory, we-feeling, fair self-sufficiency, small size, homogeneity (not cultural diversity; UPPSC 2021)
- Diversity and heterogeneity — urban traits
- Urban life — competition, impersonal relations, anonymity, weak social control (not informal ties)
