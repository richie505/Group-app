### MNRE evolution
- India = first country in the world to set up a ministry for non-conventional energy sources
- 1981 — CASE set up
- 1982 — DNES in Ministry of Energy
- 1992 — Separate ministry MNES
- 2006 — Renamed MNRE

### Funds → National Clean Energy Fund (NCEF)
- Source: Clean energy cess on coal (lignite and peat too), on domestic + imported coal
- Started at ₹50/tonne → raised to ₹400/tonne
- Purpose: Funding research and innovative projects in clean energy technologies
- = Promote renewable energy technologies and reduce carbon emissions (APPSC-G2 2025 key)
- Status: Coal cess subsumed into GST Compensation Cess (July 2017) → ended NCEF's dedicated inflow
