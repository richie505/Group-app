# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 4:21
### Evidence of warming
## Temperature
- 2024 — hottest year on record, about 1.55°C above pre-industrial
- 2025 — warmest La Niña year
- India warmed about 0.7°C since 1900 — less than global land areas (about 1.59°C)
## Ice, sea and Sun
- Gangotri Glacier — shrank about 1,700 m since 1935; cause: global warming
- Global sea level up about 11 cm since 1993
- Sea-level rise comes from thermal expansion and melting ice; higher snow albedo is not a cause (only reflects more radiation)
- Solar output: no net increase since 1978 (satellites), so recent warming is not a brighter Sun
### Natural causes
## Ice ages
- ★ Great Ice Age — Pleistocene (major glaciations) and Pliocene (came before it, cooling began)
- ★ Glaciation of peninsular India — Pleistocene Ice Age
- ★ Little Ice Age — PYQ key: 1650–1870 A.D.; much colder winters in Europe and North America than in the 20th century
- Little Ice Age also dated about 1500–1850
## Astronomical (Milankovitch) theory
- ★ Proposed in the 1920s by Milutin Milankovitch: eccentricity, axial tilt and precession of Earth's orbit drive climate (orbital forcing)
- ★ Solar irradiance is not part of the astronomical theories
| Term | Meaning |
| Precession | wobble of the rotational axis |
| Eccentricity | orbital shape |
| Obliquity | axial tilt |
## Volcanoes
- Sulphur dioxide and ash form aerosols that reflect sunlight — short-term cooling
### IPCC basics
- 195 members; reviews existing studies, does no research of its own
- 2007 Nobel Peace Prize — shared with Al Gore
| Working Group | Subject |
| I | physical science basis |
| II | impacts, adaptation and vulnerability |
| III | mitigation |
### Reports & findings
- SR1.5 — global CO2 emissions to fall about 45% from 2010 levels by 2030; net zero by 2050
- AR6 Synthesis Report — emissions must peak before 2025
- UNEP Emissions Gap Report 2025 — current policies lead to about 2.8°C; full NDC implementation about 2.3–2.5°C
- CMIP7 scenarios drop the worst-case SSP5-8.5 pathway; world near 2.8°C by 2100
### Impacts
- Ocean acidity up about 30% since the Industrial Revolution
- AR6: up to 25% of mountain species may face extinction
- Warmer, wetter conditions widen the range of vector-borne diseases (malaria, dengue)
- Tipping points: 1.2°C warm-water coral reef threshold already crossed
- Beyond about 2°C parts of the Amazon could shift to savanna and become a carbon source (not a sink)
### Geoengineering
- ★ Cirrus cloud thinning and stratospheric sulphate aerosol injection — proposed to reduce global warming
- Stratospheric aerosol injection — copies the cooling of volcanic eruptions: SO2 forms reflective sulphuric-acid aerosols
- main risk: termination shock if stopped suddenly
- Cirrus cloud thinning — seeds high cirrus clouds so more outgoing heat escapes (less insulating effect)
- Marine cloud brightening — sea-salt spray acts as cloud condensation nuclei
