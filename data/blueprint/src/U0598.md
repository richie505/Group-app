### Volcano terms → meaning — magma, lava, pyroclasts, gases, instruments
## 1. Magma, lava and the volcano
- Magma: Molten rock or material below Earth's surface — produces volcanism (in a magma chamber)
- Lava: Magma after it reaches Earth's surface — molten material that reaches the surface (through the vent); dominated by silica
- Volcano: An outlet for magma, lava and gases — releases lava, ash and gases
- Water vapour: The most abundant volcanic gas, then CO2, sulphur dioxide
- SO2 → aerosols scatter sunlight, cool Earth for years; ash falls back within weeks
| Term | Meaning |
| Crater | Funnel-shaped mouth at the top |
| caldera | Cauldron-shaped depression; magma chamber empties after a big eruption → summit collapses |
- ⚠ Magma vs lava: below surface = magma; at surface = lava
## 2. Lava types
| Acidic (felsic) lava | Basic (mafic) lava |
| Silica-rich, viscous, explosive [GK] | Silica-poor, fluid, flows far [GK] |
| Steep cones, stratovolcanoes (andesitic magma) | Shield volcanoes and lava plateaus (basalt) |
## 3. Pyroclasts
- Pyroclasts: ash, lapilli, volcanic bombs; pyroclastic flows and lahars (volcanic mudflows) from stratovolcanoes
## 4. Instruments → what they measure (APPSC 2018)
| Instrument | Measures |
| Tiltmeter | Ground tilt over faults and volcanoes |
| creepmeter | Fault slip |

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 4:9
### Terms
- Magma: molten rock below the surface; lava: once it reaches the surface
- Caldera: pot-shaped hollow from summit collapse after the magma chamber empties
- Crater: funnel-shaped mouth at the top of a cone
- Commonest volcanic gas: water vapour, then CO2 and SO2
- A volcano gives lava, ash and gases; magma causes volcanism
### Lava types
- Acidic (felsic) lava: silica-rich, viscous, explosive eruptions; steep cones, stratovolcanoes
- Pyroclastic flows and lahars (volcanic mudflows): stratovolcanoes
- Basaltic lava plateaus (e.g. Deccan) weather into fertile black soil
### Volcano forms
- Hayli Gubbi (Ethiopia): shield volcano; erupted November 2025 after ~10,000–12,000 years dormant
- Composite (strato) volcanoes: steep, alternating lava and ash layers, explosive
### World belts
- Pacific Ring of Fire: most eruptions and earthquakes (e.g. Fuji, Mount St. Helens)
- Stromboli: Mediterranean belt (not East African Rift)
- East African Rift: Kilimanjaro, Afar triple junction
### India's volcanoes
- Barren Island: India's only active volcano
- Narcondam: dormant; Dhinodhar: extinct volcanic hill
- ★ Baratang Island (Andaman): mud volcanoes — erupt mud, water, gases
- Diglipur mud volcano: throws mud, water, methane; driven by tectonic pressure on the Andaman Arc subduction zone
- Deccan Traps: fissure flood basalt, Maharashtra Plateau, end of the Cretaceous; linked to the Reunion hotspot
- ★ Stromboli: active volcano
- Dormant: quiet long but may erupt again; extinct: no eruption expected
