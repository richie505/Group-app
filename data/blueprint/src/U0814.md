### Vijayanagara-era inscription at Penukonda
## 1. Discovery
- Found at Sri Naga Lingeshwara Swamy Temple, Penukonda hill, Sri Sathya Sai district
- Date: July 16, 1363 CE (Saka 1285), Shravana Shuddha Panchami — Naga Panchami, Sunday
- Reign: Bukka Raya I (1356–1377 CE); script — ancient Kannada-Telugu
- Records Anantharasu Chikka Odeyar (Prime Minister of Bukka Raya I) consecrated the Shiva Linga and built the pond Nagula Bavi ("Well of Serpents")

- ⚠ Penukonda — second capital of the Vijayanagara Empire
### Valmiki Caves near Gooty
## 1. Key facts
- Near Gooty Fort, Gooty–Mantralayam Road; Erramala hills, Rayalaseema
- Among the deepest cave systems in South India; stalactites, stalagmites; some formations sound like tabla or bell
- Oxygen isotopes in stalagmites show stronger monsoons after the last Ice Age (~11,700 years ago)
- Underground water bodies — Edu Bavulu / Gundalu
- Linked to Sage Valmiki (Ramayana); worship on Maha Shivaratri
