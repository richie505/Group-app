### Arie Halachmi → five models of e-governance
| Model (Arie Halachmi) | Meaning |
| Broadcasting / wider-dissemination | Government information put in the public domain |
| Critical flow | Critical information channelled to a targeted audience (e.g., corruption data → ombudsman) |
| Comparative analysis | Learn from best practices elsewhere |
| E-advocacy / mobilisation and lobbying | Online civil-society networks influence decisions |
| Interactive service | Two-way G2C services (filing, payments, feedback) |
- Five models of Arie Halachmi: broadcasting, critical flow, comparative analysis, e-advocacy, interactive service
- ⚠ Corruption data → ombudsman = critical flow, not broadcasting

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 2:88
### Halachmi's five models
- Arie Halachmi — five models of e-governance: broadcasting, critical flow, comparative analysis, e-advocacy, interactive service
- Critical flow — key information sent to a targeted audience (e.g. corruption data to an ombudsman)
- Comparative analysis — learning from best practices elsewhere
- E-advocacy — online civil-society networks influencing decisions
- e-Pragati — a unified state service portal and state enterprise architecture, not a network of district treasuries
### Interaction types
| Type | Meaning | Examples |
| G2C | Government to Citizen | online birth, income, caste certificates (UMANG, MeeSeva) |
| G2B | Government to Business | MCA portal, GSTN, e-Procurement |
| G2E | Government to its employees | services to employees |
| G2G | between governments | inter-government interaction |
### National platforms
## Central platforms
- UMANG — Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance
- UMANG — Central and State services (tax, EPF, gas, passport) via web, SMS and IVR
- MyGov — citizen-engagement platform for participative governance
- ERSS (Emergency Response Support System) — linked to single emergency number 112
- CSCs (Common Service Centres) — village-level service outlets (unrelated to ERSS–112 link)
- Aadhaar — launched 2009; Aadhaar Act 2016 (amended 2019)
- NAeG (National Awards for e-Governance) — began 2003, started by DARPG; given at National Conference on e-Governance
## AP platforms
- RTGS (Real Time Governance Society) — set up 2017; Data Lake and real-time dashboards
- e-Pragati — single state service portal and state enterprise architecture; faceless, paperless, cashless governance
### AP initiatives
| Initiative | Year | Purpose |
| eSeva | 2001 | integrated citizen service centres (bills, certificates) |
| e-Procurement | 2003 | online tendering; stop collusion |
| MeeSeva ("At your service") | 2011 | single-window G2C service centres |
### e-Governance conferences
- 28th NCeG — Visakhapatnam Declaration: extend NeSDA framework to low-connectivity areas (Ladakh, North-East)
- 29th NCeG — Jaipur Declaration: DPI, AI-enabled governance, cybersecurity, data governance
### National Awards (NAeG)
- 2025 NAeG (with Ministry of Panchayati Raj) — first-ever dedicated category for Gram Panchayats
