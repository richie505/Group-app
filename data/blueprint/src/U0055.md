### Vishnukundin rulers → period and contribution — Madhava Varma II — sacrifices and conquests
## 1. Madhava Varma II — sacrifices and conquests
- Sacrifices:
- 11 Ashvamedhas, 16 Rajasuyas
- 1 Purushamedha, Hiranyagarbha, Agnistoma
- Conquests (100+ battles):
- Vakatakas - up to the Godavari coast
- Pallavas - took Karma-rashtra up to the Gundlakamma
- Salankayanas - took Vengi
- Justice legend (12th-c. Bezawada inscription): Sentenced his own son to death after the prince's chariot killed a poor woman's son
## 2. Wars and decline
- Indrabhattaraka:
- Defeated by Maukhari Ishanavarma, whose son married his daughter (Jaunpur inscription)
- Killed in the Kalinga war by Prithvimula of the Ranadurjayas + a Kalinga king
- End: Pulakeshin II (Badami Chalukya) defeated Manchana Bhattaraka at Kunala (Kolleru), 624 CE
- Brother Kubja Vishnuvardhana founded the Eastern Chalukyas of Vengi
### Vishnukundin capitals → ruler and location
| Capital | Where / which ruler |
| Amarapura | First capital; the kings called themselves "Amarapurishulu". Identified as Munnanuru (Amrabad, Nagarkurnool), where yaga-vedikas were found; B.S.L. Hanumantha Rao says Velpuru (Guntur) |
| Indrapalanagara (Sukrapuram; Tummalagudem, Yadadri-Bhuvanagiri/Nalgonda) | Built by founder Indravarma (Tummalagudem inscription); capital of Madhava Varma I and Govinda Varma |
| Bezawada (Vijayawada) | Used for a time after they took the Krishna region; they carved the Indrakeeladri and Mogalrajapuram caves here |
| Denduluru (Dendulurapura), near Vengi/Pedavegi, Eluru dt | Madhava Varma II moved the capital here from Amarapuri because of constant Pallava wars, and fortified Amarapuri |
- Other capitals listed:
- Keesaragutta (possibly their start as Vakataka feudatories)
- Indrapalanagara location: Some notes say "near Denduluru/Eluru"
- ⚠ Wrong: it is Tummalagudem in Telangana
- Exam angle: "Amarapuri → Denduluru" = Madhavavarma (G2 2025).

- AP angle: Inscriptions and capitals across both Telugu states
