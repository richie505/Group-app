### National policies and plans → year, objectives, nodal agency — National Cyber Security Policy 2013
## 1. National Cyber Security Policy 2013
- Release: 2013 by the IT ministry (now MeitY)
- Vision: A secure and resilient cyberspace for citizens, businesses and government
- Workforce target: 5 lakh cyber security professionals in 5 years
- Objectives:
- Protect public and private infrastructure; safeguard citizens' data
- Build capacity; promote R&D; public-private partnership
- Indigenous security technology; conformity assessment with incentives
- Culture of cyber security; an Information Security Assurance Framework
| Function | Nodal agency |
| Incident response and crisis management | CERT-In |
| Critical infrastructure | NCIIPC |
| Situational awareness + timely information-sharing | NCCC (National Cyber Coordination Centre) |
### National policies and plans → year, objectives, nodal agency — Draft National Cyber Security Strategy
## 1. Draft National Cyber Security Strategy
- Follow-up: A draft National Cyber Security Strategy followed (some notes call it an "updated NCSP 2020")
- ⚠ Trap: "NCSP updated in 2020" / "National Cyber Security Policy 2020" is WRONG - the 2020 document is a draft strategy, not released; the 2013 policy remains operative
- Status: Listed as "Proposed" - next-generation framework for cyber resilience and critical-infrastructure security; still pending/not notified
- Prepared under: National Cyber Security Coordinator, in the National Security Council Secretariat (NSCS)
- Not in your sources: the pillars of the draft strategy.
## 2. Cyber Crisis Management Plan
- Cyber Crisis Management Plan (CCMP):
- MeitY framework for countering cyber attacks and cyber terrorism
- Implemented by all Central and State Governments
- CISO: Every ministry/department/organisation must designate a Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
