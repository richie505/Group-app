- Second Chairperson — Justice A.M. (Ajay Manikrao) Khanwilkar, since 10 March 2024

## Working
- Section 11 — inquiry wing (headed by Director of Inquiry) and prosecution wing (headed by Director of Prosecution)
- Reported as still not fully staffed
- Property got by corruption can be attached and confiscated while prosecution is pending
- CVC — supervises CBI in Prevention of Corruption Act cases generally (CVC Act, 2003); Lokpal — only in cases referred by it
### State Lokayuktas
- Every State to set up a Lokayukta within one year of the Act's start
- Lokpal cannot inquire into complaints made after seven years from the offence
- No uniformity — each State has its own Lokayukta Act; powers, CM coverage, judicial qualifications differ
- Yearly combined report given to the Governor
- Weakness: many lack an independent investigating wing; rely on State police or ACB

### Exam corner
- AP Lokayukta features: informal procedure, easy access, almost free and quick, independent of the executive
- Interpreting the Constitution — not a feature
- January 2026 SC split verdict on Section 17A, PC Act:
- Justice B.V. Nagarathna — held 17A unconstitutional
- Justice K.V. Viswanathan — upheld prior approval only if based on a binding opinion of the Lokpal/Lokayukta
- Surya Kant — CJI; Khanwilkar — Lokpal Chairperson (neither part of the split)
