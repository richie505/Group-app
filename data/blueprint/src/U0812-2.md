### Talliki Vandanam Scheme 2026
## 1. What happened
- DBT disbursal began: 22 July 2026 — ₹13,000 credited to mothers/guardians
- Talliki Vandanam (also spelt Thalliki Vandanam; was Amma Vodi, Jan 2020): School Education; ₹15,000 per child per year credited to the mother (2025-26)
- Annual assistance: ₹15,000 per student; ₹2,000 deducted for school maintenance, sanitation, hygiene
- Sanctioned (AY 2026–27): ₹10,120.78 crore; 67,47,190 students; 42,70,802 mothers/guardians
- Coverage: Class 1 to Class 12 (incl. Intermediate) — govt, aided, private, residential schools
- Coverage: every school-going child, Class 1 to 12, from 2025-26 (some notes say Classes 1-10); now every eligible child in a family (13 Jun 2026)
- Type: Conditional cash transfer with 75% attendance
- Aim: Lower dropout + higher GER (G2-GS 2025 key) (May 2026)
- ⚠ Benefit for every eligible child — not limited to one per family
## 2. Eligibility
| Criterion | Rule |
| Income | Rural below ₹10,000/month; urban below ₹12,000/month |
| Rice Card | Mandatory |
| Land | Wet up to 3 acres / dry up to 10 acres |
| Electricity | below 300 units/month |
| House | below 1,000 sq ft |
| Attendance | at least 75% |
- Account: Aadhaar-seeded and NPCI-mapped
- Excluded: ITI and Polytechnic students
- Second year of the scheme — funds released July 22, 2026 (CA360 spells Thalliki Vandanam); DBT launched nationally in 2013
- Spend: ₹10,090 crore to 67.27 lakh students (TH, 1 Jan 2026; APPCA, Jan 2026)
- Budget 2026-27: ₹8,456 crore; BE 2026-27 also given as ₹9,668 cr
