### Srisailam copper plates (1420 CE) — first use of 'Swarajya' in a Telugu inscription
## 1. News
- Date: Saka 1341, Sarvari, Magha Sudha Trayodasi = 28 January 1420 CE
- ⚠ Headline says 14th century but 1420 CE is 15th century
- Format: three copper leaves, bilingual — Sanskrit and Telugu
- Term: 'Swarajya Punarudhharana' (restoration of independent state) — ⚠ first-time use in a Telugu inscription (per Dr K. Munirathnam Reddy, Director (Epigraphy), ASI Mysore)
- Ruler: Recharla Lingama Nayaka (Recharla dynasty) — reclaimed areas from the Turakas; restored eight agraharas
## 2. Link to freedom movement
- Bal Gangadhar Tilak: "Swarajya is my birthright"
- Tanguturi Prakasam started the newspaper 'Swarajya' in three languages
