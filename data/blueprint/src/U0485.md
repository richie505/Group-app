### Feminisation of agriculture — causes and PLFS data
- Causes:

- Overall FLFPR = 30% → 40%

- 93.2% agricultural households indebted → highest among major States (NSSO 77th Round)

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 3:41
### Rural credit milestones
- 1951 — moneylenders gave about 70% of rural credit
- All India Rural Credit Survey Committee (RBI, 1951) — headed by A.D. Gorwala; led to SBI (1955)
- verdict: "Rural credit has failed, but cooperation must succeed"
### Sources of rural credit
- Non-institutional: traders, relatives; institutional: commercial banks, PACS, RRBs
### Kisan Credit Card
- KCC — open to tenants, sharecroppers, SHGs, JLGs (not only landowners)
- Small Finance Banks lend to small businesses, small and marginal farmers, rural entrepreneurs — regulators (FSDC members) are not borrowers
### Key terms
- ★ Claudia Goldin — 2023 Nobel (Sveriges Riksbank Prize) in Economic Sciences; prize set up 1968, awarded since 1969
- U-shaped curve of women's work: high in farming in the 1980s, fall 2004-05 to 2012 (machines), rise after 2020 (partly distress)
- Informal loans by repayment: temporary (repaid after harvest), chronic, debt-trap (new loan to repay old)
- Distress migration — forced by debt, landlessness, crop loss; seasonal migration — for a season, then return
- Feminisation of agriculture — growing share of women among cultivators, labourers, unpaid family workers as men move to non-farm/urban work
### Rural non-farm sector
- DAY-NRLM microfinance route: SHGs, JLGs, BC Sakhis
- SVEP (DAY-NRLM) — funds rural non-farm enterprises
- PMEGP — run by KVIC (statutory, KVI Act 1956, Ministry of MSME)
- NABARD — apex rural development bank; SIDBI — finances MSMEs
### Migration: push vs pull
- Pull: better wages in cities; push: crop failure, poor rural healthcare, rural unemployment
- Parliamentary panel on DDU-GKY: low wages and drop-outs
### Distress data (ES 2025-26)
- KVIC — statutory body under the KVI Act, 1956 (amended 1987, 2006); Ministry of MSME
- NREGA — passed 2005, in force 2006; renamed MGNREGA in 2009
- 100 days per household; wages within 15 days (not 30); one-third participation for women
- 26.8% of rural Indians were migrants in 2020-21 (ES 2025-26)
- Rural women — 80% of the rise in female workers, 2017-18 to 2023-24
- Feminisation of agriculture ≠ land ownership by women; it is about participation in farm work
### Exam corner
- AP farm indebtedness: NABARD study — about 50% of AP farmers depend on non-institutional credit (not 25%)
- AP distress-migration belts: Rayalaseema and north Andhra (Srikakulam)
- AP Gulf migration: mainly YSR Kadapa and Godavari/Konaseema districts
### Data — rural distress indicators and the ES 2025-26 debate
- ★ Rural-urban migration — combination of push (rural poverty, few jobs, poor services) and pull (urban jobs) factors
- ★ MGNREGA positive results: higher purchasing power of rural poor, less distress migration (not lower bargaining power of labour)
