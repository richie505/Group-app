# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:161 - Third Carnatic War - Battle of Wandiwash, Treaty of Paris (1763); reasons for English success
### Three Carnatic Wars
- Chandurti (1758) = foundation of British rule in Andhra, as Plassey (1757) is for India
| War | Years | Result |
| First | 1746-1748 | Madras back to British; Aix-la-Chapelle (1748) |
| Second | 1749-1754 | Inconclusive; Ambur (1749) |
| Third | 1758-1763 | French power ended; Treaty of Paris (1763) |
### Third War: events
- 1756 — Tumnapalem: Bussy aided by Vijayaramaraju
- 24 Jan 1757 — Bobbili battle
- 1758 — Lally takes Fort St. David; fails at Madras, Thanjavur
- 7 Dec 1758 — Chandurti (near Rajahmundry): Forde + Vizianagaram beat Conflans
- April 1759 — Machilipatnam taken; Salabat Jang changes sides
- 22 Jan 1760 — Wandiwash: Eyre Coote routs Lally; Bussy taken prisoner
- 16 Jan 1761 — Pondicherry falls after eight months
### Andhra theatre
- Chandurti drove French out of the Northern Circars; Malleson: small battle, great results
- Salabat Jang gave English Machilipatnam, Nizampatnam, Vakal Mannar, part of Kondapalli
- Machilipatnam (1759): first British-Hyderabad agreement
- Ananda Gajapati beat Jagapati Raju at Konduru; died of smallpox 1760
### Treaty of Paris (1763)
- Ended Seven Years' War + Third Carnatic War
- French settlements restored only as trading posts; could not be fortified
- Enclaves: Pondicherry, Karaikal, Mahe, Yanam + Chandernagore
- European link: Indian theatre of Seven Years' War (1756-63)
- Bengal: Plassey (1757) + Chandernagore → revenue paid for Carnatic campaign
### Why English won, French lost
| Factor | English | French |
| Company | Private; quick decisions | State-controlled; bureaucratic |
| Navy | Naval superiority | Fleet beaten; Lally without sea support |
| Money | Bengal's wealth after Plassey | Funds short; unpaid troops |
| Bases | Calcutta, Bombay, Madras | Only Pondicherry |
| Leaders | Clive, Lawrence, Coote | Lally quarrelled; Dupleix recalled |
### Persons
- Colonel Forde
- Sent by Clive; won Chandurti
- Eyre Coote
- Count de Lally
- Surrendered Pondicherry
- Conflans
- French; beaten at Chandurti
- Pocock
- English admiral

### Carnatic Wars → years, sides and results
## 1. Carnatic Wars
| War / battle | Years | Between | Result |
| First Carnatic War | 1746 to 1748 | The English and the French | The British regained Madras; the Treaty of Aix-la-Chapelle (1748) |

| Second Carnatic War | 1749 to 1754 | English- and French-backed Indian factions | Inconclusive |

### War → European link, Bengal resources and Treaty of Paris
## 1. European link and Bengal
- European link: Third war = Indian theatre of the Seven Years' War (1756-63)
- Bengal by 1758: Clive had won Plassey (1757) + taken French Chandernagore
- Bengal revenue paid for the Carnatic campaign
## 2. Treaty of Paris (1763)
- Ended: Seven Years' War + Third Carnatic War
- Terms: French factories/settlements restored only as trading posts – could not be fortified (demilitarised)
- Retained enclaves: Pondicherry, Karaikal, Mahe, Yanam + Chandernagore
- Some notes list only "Pondicherry, Yanam, Mahe" – incomplete
- Exam angle: "Third Carnatic War concluded by the Treaty of ___ in 1763" - Paris (APPSC 2025).

### Reasons for English success and French failure
| Factor | English | French |
| Company structure | Private company, board elected yearly; quick decisions | State-controlled, dependent on royal subsidy; bureaucratic |
| Navy | Naval superiority; could supply and cut off | Fleet beaten (d'Ache); Lally without sea support |
| Money and base | Bengal's wealth and men after Plassey | Chronic shortage of funds; unpaid troops |
| Bases | Calcutta, Bombay, Madras | Only Pondicherry |
| Leadership | Clive, Lawrence, Coote | Lally quarrelled with officers, did not understand Indian conditions; Dupleix recalled |
- Exam angle: UPPSC 2016 - both "Bengal wealth and manpower" and "naval superiority" are correct reasons.
