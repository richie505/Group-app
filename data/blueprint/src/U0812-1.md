### Jal Jeevan Mission 2.0 — Amarajeevi Jaladhara
## 1. Launch
- Launched by: CM N. Chandrababu Naidu at Proddatur, Kadapa district on 5 October 2026
- Investment: ₹30,156 crore; State programme under Jal Jeevan Mission 2.0 — household tap connections
- Coverage: 90 lakh households, ~48,000 rural habitations, ~3.30 crore people
- ⚠ Not limited to urban households — rural habitations are the focus

- JJM: launched 2019, Ministry of Jal Shakti — Functional Household Tap Connections
### India's first 3D concrete-printed government houses — Burripalem
## 1. Key facts
- ⚠ First in India: 3D Concrete Printing (3DCP) in govt housing — 100 houses pilot at Burripalem, Tenali constituency
- Scheme: PMAY-G 2.0; each house 325 sq ft; extra cost shared by Centre and State
- Builder: CBRI (Central Building Research Institute, Roorkee, under CSIR); agreement on 4 October with CM Naidu present
- Process: 3D CAD design → concrete mix → robotic/gantry printer → layer-by-layer
### Andhra Pradesh Omnibus Act–2026 in force
## 1. Key facts
- In force from: 1 October 2026 (govt orders)
- Aim: simplify industrial approvals — "Speed of Doing Business" reforms
- Mechanism: simplified single-window system for approvals and clearances
- AP Omnibus (Speed of Doing Business) Bill, 2026: amends Fire Service Act 1999, Single Window Clearance Act 2002, Public Service Delivery Guarantee Act 2017
