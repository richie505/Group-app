# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:230
### Key terms
- ★ Gandhian Innovation — produce more from less input for more people; coined by Prof. Prahlad and R.A. Mashelkar
- Sarvodaya — welfare/uplift of all, reaching the last person
- Swadeshi and khadi — promote rural employment and self-reliance; link towns and villages; Gandhi popularised the charkha
### Books behind the ideas
- ★ Unto This Last — John Ruskin; Gandhi's Gujarati retelling titled Sarvodaya (1908)
- Influenced his ideas on social equality, labour and welfare of all
- The Kingdom of God is Within You — Leo Tolstoy; Gandhi took love and non-violence from it
### Roots of the ideas
- Bread labour — sources: Tolstoy (from Bondaref) and Ruskin's Unto This Last; linked by Gandhi to yajna
- Thoreau (Civil Disobedience) — gave the right to break unjust laws, not bread labour
- Trusteeship — applied to both capitalists and zamindars (e.g. Ahmedabad mill owners)
### Gandhian economy
- Non-violent economy needs decentralisation and self-reliance; production for need, not profit
- 1925 — All India Spinners' Association
- 1934 — All India Village Industries Association
### Persons
- ★ Sarvodaya Plan (1950) — drafted by Jayaprakash Narayan
- ★ Gandhian Plan — Shriman Narayan Aggarwal
- ★ Bhoodan (Vinoba Bhave) began at Pochampalli (now Telangana), 1951 (first land donation 18 April 1951)
- ★ Vinoba organised Sarvodaya Samaj; landlords persuaded to gift at least one-sixth of their land
- Ambedkar — village a seat of caste domination; "a sink of localism, ignorance and communalism"; preferred the individual as the basic unit
- Tummala Sitaramamurthy — wrote Sarvodaya Ganam (inspired by Vinoba); translated Gandhi's autobiography into Telugu
### Gandhian DPSPs
| Article | Gandhian Directive Principle |
| 40 | village panchayats |
| 43 | cottage industries |
| 47 | prohibition of intoxicating drinks |
### Exam corner
- Ponduru (Srikakulam dist.) — finest khadi in India
- Guravareddypalem — linked with Prakasam's Khadi Development Centre
- Gandhi's rural development approach — Samagra Gramseva
### Features of the Gandhian economy and trusteeship in practice
- ★ Each country's economy to be defined by its climate, land and nature of its citizens
- ★ Hence Gandhi did not oppose mechanisation in the USA
### Persons → contributions to Sarvodaya and village thought
- ★ Message Gandhi took from Ruskin's Unto This Last (read in South Africa): "The good of the individual is contained in the good of all"
