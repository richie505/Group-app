### ZBNF → APCNF
- APCNF: the world's largest agro-ecological transition, first Indian finalist of the Food Planet Prize
- Mid-1990s — ZBNF developed by Subhash Palekar (Padma Shri)
- Oct 2014 — RySS (Rythu Sadhikara Samstha) incorporated: implementing agency, not-for-profit
- 2016 — ZBNF launched in AP → renamed APCNF
- 11 July 2024 — Gulbenkian Prize for Humanity, Lisbon
- June 2026 — Food Planet Prize, $1.5 million

| PMDS | Pre-Monsoon Dry Sowing on residual moisture |
