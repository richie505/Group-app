# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:12 - IVC religion - Pashupati seal, Mother Goddess, tree and animal worship, fire altars

- Mother Goddess, Pashupati, linga-yoni, pipal, ritual bathing = continuities into later Hinduism

- Idolatry practised (figurines, images)

### Pashupati seal and Mother Goddess — features and identification

| Feature | Detail |

- Both deities: With male Pashupati figure → worship of both male and female deities (UPSC 2013)

- Amulets: Show deities or persons in yogic poses

- Grave goods: Pottery, ornaments, mirrors, beads, food → belief in afterlife + status differences

- UPSC 2013: No palaces and no temples; no horse-drawn chariots in warfare

- Planned cities + drains → municipal governance
- Fortified citadel → ruling group (possibly priestly or merchant) - debated
- Stratification: House sizes, burial goods, ornaments; four-fold division (scholars, warriors, traders, labourers) = speculative

## 2. Harappan vs Vedic religion
| Harappan religion | Later Vedic religion |
| Nature- and fertility-oriented (Mother Goddess, pipal, animals) | Sacrificial and ritualistic (Indra, Agni, Varuna; yajna) |
- Contrast - Rig Vedic religion: Male nature gods (Indra, Agni, Varuna); open-air yajnas; no temples or idols; no animal worship
### Universal angles
- Significance: Earliest material evidence of cults (Shiva-like deity, mother goddess, linga) that later entered Hinduism - Mother Goddess, Pashupati, linga-yoni, pipal, ritual bathing = continuities into later Hinduism
- Criticism & debates: Proto-Shiva reading of the Pashupati seal, "priest-king" label and fire-altar-yajna link = interpretations of an undeciphered culture, not proven
- Comparative note: Harappan religion nature- and fertility-oriented vs Later Vedic religion sacrificial and priest-led
- AP angle: Gudimallam (Satavahana era) = oldest Shaivite temple in AP; called first Shiva Lingam in AP and India - linga tradition in historic form
- Rakhigarhi (Jun 2026): Rare cylindrical steatite seal with an alligator motif and five Harappan signs + gold foundry + gemstone factory; only Harappan site with ancient DNA
