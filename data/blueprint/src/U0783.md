### Ocean acidification → cause, chemistry and terms
## 1. Cause and names
- Primary cause: Dissolution of atmospheric CO2 in seawater (APPSC-GS 2024)
- Called "evil twin of global warming", "the other CO2 problem"
- Uptake: Oceans absorb about one-third of anthropogenic CO2 (also cited 30-40%)
## 2. Chemistry
- CO2 + H2O → carbonic acid → releases H+ + bicarbonate
- Extra H+ binds carbonate ions → less carbonate for shells
## 3. Data - pH change
- pH change: Surface pH 8.2 (pre-industrial) → ~8.1 today = roughly 30% rise in acidity; ocean still alkaline
## 4. Worst areas
- Worst areas: Cold/temperate and Arctic waters (higher CO2 solubility; melting sea ice exposes water)
- Eutrophic bloom collapse also raises CO2 → worsens acidification
- ⚠ pH 8.2 → ~8.1 yet acidity up ~30%; ocean is still alkaline

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 5:93
### Chemistry of acidification
- Hydrogen ions bind carbonate ions → less carbonate left for making shells
### Key facts
- Ocean acidification — 'evil twin' of global warming (another result of human CO2)
- pH 8.2 → 8.1 ≈ 30% rise in acidity; ocean still alkaline
- Worse in cold/Arctic waters — CO2 more soluble in cold water; melting sea ice exposes more water
### Who gets hit
- Pteropod — 'sea butterfly'; shells dissolve; food for fish
### Related stressors
- Marine heatwaves — doubled since 1982; cyclones over them up to 20% stronger winds
- Marine heatwaves — strongest global driver of coral-cover decline
- Dead zones — oxygen used up by decay of algal blooms (from nutrient wash-off)
### Coral restoration
- NGT (Feb 2026), Great Nicobar — MoEFCC to ensure coral protection and regeneration by a proven scientific method
- Gulf of Mannar — India's first marine biosphere reserve; 3D-printed reef modules nearby under PMMSY
### Monitoring & missions
- SAMUDRA app coral bleaching alerts — INCOIS (not NCCR)
- NCCR, Chennai — reef health monitoring via D-COIN
- Varaha — deep-sea mining system, tested at 5,270 m
- Matsya 6000 — Samudrayaan submersible
### Exam corner
- ★ Biorock technology — restoration of damaged coral reefs; invented 1976 by Wolf Hilbertz

- Lakshadweep — entirely atoll reefs; giant Pavona clavus (potato patch coral) at Kadmat
