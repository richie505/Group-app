# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 2:123 - Poverty, unemployment and social exclusion as social problems
### Concepts & firsts
- Social exclusion: a process that shuts people out of facilities, benefits and opportunities others enjoy
- Exclusion bases: caste, gender, disability, religion, region

- Social exclusion: Process that shuts people out of facilities, benefits and opportunities others enjoy

## 3. MGNREGA — provisions
- Core guarantee: Legal right to 100 days of unskilled wage work per rural household a year
- No work within 15 days → unemployment allowance due
- One-third of workers must be women
- Gram Sabha holds social audits
- VB-G RAM G Bill, 2025: Raises guarantee to 125 days + changes funding pattern
- ⚠ 100 days = MGNREGA; 125 days = VB-G RAM G
