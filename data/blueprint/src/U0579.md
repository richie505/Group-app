### Ninth and Tenth Schedule institutions — count, examples, sections
| Feature | Schedule IX (companies, corporations) | Schedule X (institutions) |
| What it lists | Government companies and corporations | State institutions whose facilities continue for both States |
| Count | 91 (CDI) | 107 (APPSC 2021 key); CDI gives 142 |
| Examples | APSFC, Sports Authority of AP | APSCHE, Shilparamam Arts, Crafts and Cultural Society, tribunals |
| Governing section | Sec 68[GK] (continue to function) and Sec 53 (division) | Sec 75[GK] (continuance of facilities) |
| Division rule | Operational units by location; headquarters by population ratio | Assets by location (Supreme Court APSCHE ruling [GK]); facilities continue for the other State |
| Time limit | - | Terms for continuing facilities to be fixed within 1 year |
- Location: most Schedule X institutions in or around Hyderabad
- Section 66 cases: some institutions in no schedule
- AP: bring them into Schedule IX or X under Section 66
- Telangana: share only cash balances; institutions go by location (10 Aug 2026)
- Exam angle: the number in Schedule X (107), and which schedule a named body falls in (APSCHE is X; APSFC is IX).

### Committees and Sections on employees and education
## 1. Committees → subject
| Committee | Subject |
| Sheela Bhide Committee (set up by the undivided AP government, APPSC 2021) | Assets, liabilities and employees of Schedule IX companies and corporations |
| (C.R.) Kamalanathan Committee | Allocation of State-level employees between the two States |
| Pratyush Sinha Committee | Allocation of All India Services officers (IAS/IPS/IFoS) [GK] |
- ⚠ Sheela Bhide = Schedule IX; Kamalanathan = State employees; Pratyush Sinha = All India Services
## 2. Part VIII — Provisions as to Services
- Part: Part VIII, "Provisions as to Services" (Sections 76-83)
- Section 76: separate State cadres of IAS, IPS, IFoS under 1954/1966 Cadre Rules
- Section 77: State employees; does not apply to All India Services
- Advisory Committees: Centre could set up within 30 days of enactment (Kamalanathan Committee for State employees)
- Allocation: employees opt for a State; non-opters allotted by Centre on native (domicile) status
- PSUs and corporations: staff stayed in place 1 year; corporation itself decided division (Sec 82)
- Disputes: seniority and pensions (Sec 59, Eighth Schedule) → AP Reorganisation Tribunal backlogged
## 3. Education — Section 95 and Art 371D
- Section 95, Part XI (Access to Higher Education):
- Art 371D admission quotas in all higher, technical, medical institutions (government/private, aided/unaided)
- Common admission process to continue for 10 years
- Section 97: amended Art 371D to cover "Andhra Pradesh or Telangana"
### Power sharing and other unresolved issues — Twelfth Schedule, dues, SCCL, debt
## 1. Electricity (Twelfth Schedule)
- APGENCO plants by location; existing PPAs continue
- AP: got about 46% of power for 58% of population
- ⚠ Trap: APPSC-GS 2019 key marked "Gadgil formula" for "electricity distribution formula"; Act's rule = 5-year consumption basis; if options force a choice, key's answer is Gadgil
- Surplus power: deficit State has first right of refusal on other State's surplus for 10 years
- Power dues: APGENCO says Telangana's DISCOMs owe thousands of crores for power supplied just after bifurcation; Telangana counter-claims under other heads
## 2. Other issues
| Issue | Effect |
| SCCL (Schedule XII) | equity 51% Telangana and 49% GoI → AP holds no equity; existing coal linkages continue |
| Unapportioned debt | > Rs 24,000 crore of public debt on AP's books → eats into FRBM borrowing room |
| Central subsidy | subsidy due to both States credited to AP; Southern Zonal Council directed AP to pay Telangana its share - still unpaid (10 Aug 2026) |
### Universal angles
- Current relevance: Southern Zonal Council's standing committee keeps dropping Telangana's bifurcation issues unsettled; a newspaper analysis argues Union should mediate (10 Aug 2026)
- Criticism & debates: schedules not exhaustive; "Headquarters" and "utmost importance" undefined → endless litigation
- Comparative note: 1953 Andhra State partition committee chaired by T.T. Krishnamachari (APHQ 2025)
