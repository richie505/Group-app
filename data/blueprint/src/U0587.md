### SCS — definition, origin and FIRSTs
- Origin: Introduced 1969 on recommendation of 5th Finance Commission (APPSC key, GS 2023; APPSC-GS 2023)
- Legal status: Not in the Constitution; executive/Planning Commission-era classification
| FIRST | FACT |
| First year of SCS | 1969 |
| Recommended by | 5th Finance Commission |
| First three SCS states | Jammu & Kashmir, Assam, Nagaland |
| First Plan-transfer formula | Gadgil formula (1969) |
- Gadgil Formula (1968-69): Endorsed by National Development Council (NDC) for distributing Plan funds → SCS states got preferential central assistance
- Plan transfers: Gadgil formula (1969) → later Gadgil-Mukherjee formula (1991)
- SCS states got reserved first share of Normal Central Assistance
- Exam angle: "SCS was first introduced in 1969 on the recommendation of which FC?" - 5th.
### SCS criteria and benefits — features
## Criteria (features weighed)
- (1) Hilly and difficult terrain
- (2) Low population density and/or sizeable tribal population
- (3) Strategic location along international borders
- (4) Economic and infrastructural backwardness
- (5) Non-viable state finances
- Summary: Disadvantage from geography + economic and infrastructural backwardness
- Rationale: Larger tax share meant to cover the gap
## Benefits
| Benefit | SCS state | General state |
| Central loans | Concessional interest | Normal |
| Industry | Tax breaks and incentives (income tax/excise type) | None special |
| Unspent funds | Do not lapse; carried forward [GK] | Lapse |
- Other examples:
- Consumer Welfare Fund seed money: 90:10 for SCS vs 75:25 (2026)
- PMGSY: habitations of 250+ in SCS vs 500+ in plains (2026)
- GST exemption threshold lower (₹10 lakh) for SCS
- ⚠ 90:10 = SCS vs 75:25 general (Consumer Welfare Fund); PMGSY 250+ SCS vs 500+ plains

### Finance Commissions → position on SCS and devolution
| Finance Commission | Chair / period | Position |
| 5th FC | - | Recommended SCS (1969) |
| 14th FC | Chair Y.V. Reddy; award 2015-20 | States' share of divisible pool 32% to 42%; accepted 24 Feb 2015 – largest increase ever; No distinction between general and special category states |
| 15th FC | - | 41% |
| 16th FC | Chair Arvind Panagariya; award 1 Apr 2026-31 Mar 2031 | Keeps 41%; adds "Contribution to GDP" criterion (10% weight) |
- Centre on 14th FC: higher devolution subsumed SCS benefits (some notes phrase it as "recommended SCS be abolished" – core point same)
- ⚠ 42% = 14th FC; 41% = 15th and 16th FC; 32% = share before 14th FC
### AP and SCS — 2014 assurance and controversy timeline
- Assurance: 20 February 2014, PM Manmohan Singh assured Rajya Sabha → SCS for AP for five years
- Described as "a solemn assurance... for five years" during 2014 debates
- Duration: five years (APPSC-GS 2018 key)
- Legal gap: Not written into the Reorganisation Act → Centre later treated it as non-binding
| Year | Event |
| 2015 | 14th FC award used by the Centre to deny SCS |
| Sep 2016 | Special Assistance Package offered instead (see C-131) |
| 2018 | TDP broke from the NDA (TH 18 May 2026); TDP moved a no-confidence motion in the Lok Sabha, July 2018, defeated [GK] |

| 2026 | Demand revived: Congress MP Manickam Tagore in Lok Sabha during the AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Bill debate (TH 2 Apr 2026); YSRCP's Botcha Satyanarayana (TH 5 Apr 2026); APCC chief Y.S. Sharmila (TH 20 May 2026) |
- AP's argument: Commitment on floor of Parliament carries constitutional weight
- 42% devolution applied to all states; did not address AP's asymmetric loss of Hyderabad
- ⚠ Assurance given in Rajya Sabha (2014); no-confidence motion moved in Lok Sabha (2018)
