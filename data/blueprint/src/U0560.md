## 2. Data — industrial GVA share of residual AP
- Post-bifurcation: Residual AP ~60% agriculture-dependent; industry "only around 10.5% of GSDP"
- ⚠ Trap: that figure matches manufacturing, not whole industry sector (which includes mining, construction, utilities)

## 1. Section 94 incentives in practice
- Backward-area funds: 7 backward districts (4 Rayalaseema + 3 North Coastal); initial allocations, then disbursement stalled

- Government move: Hyderabad → Velagapudi (Amaravati); e.g. APSWAN state HQ to Velagapudi Secretariat in 2017

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 3:116
### What AP lost
- HITEC City and the Financial District (Hyderabad) — IT and ITES export centres lost by residual AP
- Genome Valley and the Hyderabad pharma belt went to Telangana; AP kept only a bulk-drug base at Visakhapatnam
### Industrial base after 2014
- Manufacturing — about 10.5 percent of GSDP just after bifurcation (manufacturing only, not the whole industry sector)
- State about 60 percent agriculture-dependent
- Coastline: older figure 974 km; after remeasurement 1,053 km — supports port-led EXIM strategy
### APRA 2014 provisions
| Provision | Subject |
| Schedule IX | 91 companies and corporations |
| Schedule X | 142 institutions, mostly in Hyderabad |
| Section 5 | Hyderabad common capital till 2024 |
| Section 5(3) | inserted by AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Act, 2026; Amaravati sole capital |
| Section 94 | backward-area programmes in seven districts (4 Rayalaseema + 3 North Coastal) |
- Section 94 units: 15% extra depreciation and 15% investment allowance
### Relocation challenges
- Corporate and bank regional headquarters stayed in Hyderabad — residual AP left without a commercial nerve centre
- Investment slowdown after 2014: capital uncertainty, no ready administrative set-up, and agitations (not lack of coastline)
### Exam corner
- Residual AP: about 58% of population and debt, but only about 46% of revenue (loss of Hyderabad's revenue base)
- Rayalaseema industrial hub: Kia, JSW, Royal Enfield (near Tirupati), Space City
