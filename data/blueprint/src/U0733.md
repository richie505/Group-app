### Launch records → foreign co-passenger and academic satellites on PSLV
## 1. Record launch
- PSLV-C37 (15 Feb 2017): Record 104 satellites in one launch (UPSC)
- Cartosat-2 series (714 kg) + 2 ISRO nanosats + 101 foreign nanosats from 6 countries
- Total 1,378 kg; 506 km SSO
## 2. Totals
- Over 400 foreign satellites launched on Indian rockets (1 Jan 2026)
- NSIL: 141 satellites, incl. 138 international/customer, as of July 2026 (earlier: 127)
## 3. PSLV-C62 / EOS-N1 failure
- PSLV-C62/EOS-N1 (12 Jan 2026):
- 64th PSLV flight; 105th launch from Sriharikota; 9th dedicated NSIL commercial mission
- Carried 15 co-passengers from Indian and foreign start-ups and academic institutions incl. a KID re-entry capsule
- Failed after roll-rate disturbance at end of third stage
### Universal angles
- Current relevance:
- EOS-05: India's first earth-imaging satellite for geosynchronous orbit (~36,000 km) - floods, cyclones, fires, security (7 Sep 2026)
- Two consecutive PSLV failures (C61 in 2025, C62 in Jan 2026) hit surveillance satellites
- AP angle: StudSat consortium included colleges from Andhra Pradesh (then undivided); all these satellites launched from Sriharikota
- FIRSTs: EDUSAT first education satellite; ANUSAT first university satellite; WESAT first all-women-student satellite
