### Union Budget 2026–27 — key announcements
## 1. Basics
- Presented by: FM Nirmala Sitharaman — a "Yuva Shakti-driven" Budget
- Presented: 1 Feb 2026; Prepared by: DEA, Ministry of Finance
- ⚠ First Union Budget prepared in Kartavya Bhawan
- Three Kartavya (duties): (1) accelerate and sustain economic growth; (2) fulfil aspirations and build capacity; (3) Sabka Sath, Sabka Vikas — access for every family, community, region, sector
- Reforms since Independence Day 2025: 350+ reforms — GST simplification, Labour Codes notified, QCO rationalisation
- Six intervention areas (Kartavya 1): manufacturing in 7 strategic/frontier sectors; legacy industries; Champion MSMEs; infrastructure; energy security; City Economic Regions
## 2. Fiscal numbers
| Item | RE 2025–26 | BE 2026–27 |
| Fiscal deficit (% GDP) | 4.4% | 4.3% |

- Fiscal deficit 2026-27: 4.3% of GDP = ₹16.96 lakh crore; Revenue deficit: 1.5%
- Debt-GDP target 50 ± 1% by 2030-31

### Sustainability in rice production
## 1. News
- ⚠ 2025: India overtook China as world's largest rice producer
- India supplies ~40% of global rice exports — over 20 million metric tonnes
## 2. Facts
- Staple for ~65% of Indians; ~25% of total cropped area
- Kharif crop: temperature >25°C, rainfall >100 cm
- Transplanted paddy: 25–27 irrigations/season; 1 kg rice = 3,000–4,000 litres water
- Punjab–Haryana water table fell ~30 ft → 80–200 ft; extraction exceeds recharge by 35–57%
- Rice = 10–20% of India's agricultural GHG emissions (methane); Punjab spends ₹39,000/ha on power and fertiliser subsidies
- Climate change may cut yields 6–10%
## 3. Solutions
- DSR (Direct Seeded Rice), AWD (Alternate Wetting and Drying — cuts methane), SRI, micro-irrigation under PMKSY
- ICAR NICRA programme; fortified rice under NFSA and PM-POSHAN
- ⚠ Flooded transplanted paddy increases methane (anaerobic)
## 4. Official figures
- Announced by Shivraj Singh Chouhan: India 150.18 million tonnes vs China 145.28 million tonnes
- 184 improved varieties of 25 crops by ICAR released — 122 cereals, 6 pulses, 13 oilseeds, 11 fodder, 6 sugarcane, 24 cotton–jute, 1 tobacco

## 1. News
- ⚠ 2025: India overtook China as world's largest rice producer
- India supplies ~40% of global rice exports — over 20 million metric tonnes
## 2. Facts
- Staple for ~65% of Indians; ~25% of total cropped area
- Kharif crop: temperature >25°C, rainfall >100 cm
- Transplanted paddy: 25–27 irrigations/season; 1 kg rice = 3,000–4,000 litres water
- Punjab–Haryana water table fell ~30 ft → 80–200 ft; extraction exceeds recharge by 35–57%
- Rice = 10–20% of India's agricultural GHG emissions (methane); Punjab spends ₹39,000/ha on power and fertiliser subsidies
- Climate change may cut yields 6–10%
## 3. Solutions
- DSR (Direct Seeded Rice), AWD (Alternate Wetting and Drying — cuts methane), SRI, micro-irrigation under PMKSY
- ICAR NICRA programme; fortified rice under NFSA and PM-POSHAN
- ⚠ Flooded transplanted paddy increases methane (anaerobic)
## 4. Official figures
- Announced by Shivraj Singh Chouhan: India 150.18 million tonnes vs China 145.28 million tonnes
- 184 improved varieties of 25 crops by ICAR released — 122 cereals, 6 pulses, 13 oilseeds, 11 fodder, 6 sugarcane, 24 cotton–jute, 1 tobacco

### Trump's "rice dumping" claim — India's rice trade
## 1. News
- Claim: US President Donald Trump said India (and Thailand) are "dumping" rice in the US; tariffs could fix it — at a White House farmers' roundtable with Brooke Rollins and Scott Bessent
- ⚠ Not dumping — US imports premium aromatic rice at high prices
## 2. India's rice numbers
- Production 2024–25: 150 million tonnes — 28% of world; #1 producer and exporter
- Exports: 198.65 lakh tonnes worth $12.95 billion; about 40% of global rice trade; to 172 countries (+26 planned incl. Philippines, Indonesia, UK, Mexico)
- Export projection: 22.5 MT (2024–25) → 25 MT (2025–26)
- USA: 7.05 MT, #13 globally; exports 3 MT, imports 1.6 MT
- India's share in US rice imports: 26.1% (2024)
## 3. Markets
- Basmati: mainly West Asia — Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Iran, UAE; to US 2.74 lakh tonnes ($337.10m) of 60.65 lt
- Non-basmati: mainly West Africa — Benin, Togo, Côte d'Ivoire, Liberia, Sierra Leone, Guinea, Senegal
- LT Foods' "Royal" brand: 55% of North American basmati market

### Bitcoin's slide after October record and crypto regulation
## 1. News
- Bitcoin: record $126,000 (October 2025) → below $85,000; about $1 trillion crypto value wiped out; fears of "crypto winter"
## 2. Reasons
- US Fed: cut rates by 25 bps on December 10 but signalled no further cuts
- 10/10 crash: October 10 — Trump threatened 100% tariffs on Chinese goods; $19 billion leveraged trades liquidated; $500 billion wiped out in hours
- Long-term holders: 800,000 Bitcoins sold within a month
- ⚠ Bitcoin behaves like a risk asset, not "digital gold"
## 3. Why winter fears may be exaggerated
- Halving: four-year cycle; last April 2024
- Bitcoin ETFs: approved in US January 2024; hold 1.4 million BTC (6.8% of supply)
- US policy: US Bitcoin Reserve executive order (March 2025); GENIUS Act (June 2025) on stablecoins
