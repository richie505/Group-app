### Bhakti → origin and saints; Vedantic acharyas — chronology, contemporaries and FIRSTs
## 1. Bhakti
- A religious movement — loving devotion towards God; originated in South India — the Dravida or Tamil region
- Alvars: Devotees of Vishnu; Nayanars: devotees of Shiva
| Saint | Key facts |
| Chaitanya Mahaprabhu | Vaishnava Bhakti — a devotee of Krishna; Bengal |
| Kabir | Works: Sakhi, Sabad, Ramaini |
| Mirabai | A devotee of Krishna |
| Raskhan | A Muslim devotee of Krishna — wrote Prem Vatika |
## 1. Chronological order
- Order: Shankara (788-820) → Ramanuja (1017-1137) → Nimbarka → Madhva (13th c.) → Ramananda (1400-1470) → Vallabha (1479-1531) → Chaitanya (1485-1533)
- Nimbarka usually placed in the 12th-13th c.
- ⚠ Trap: Nimbarka was NOT a contemporary of Akbar (UPSC 2019)
- Contemporaries: Vallabha and Chaitanya
## 2. FIRST / pioneer
| FIRST | FACT |
| Forerunner / pioneer of the Bhakti cult | Shankaracharya (Advaita) |
| First true exponent of the Bhakti movement | Ramanuja |
| First Bhakti saint to preach in Hindi | Ramananda |
| First medieval reformer to denounce caste outright | Ramananda |
- ⚠ Shankara = forerunner (Advaita, knowledge); Ramanuja = first true exponent (devotion)
### Acharyas → school, teaching and deity
## 1. Philosophical schools → founder
| Acharya | School | Teaching emphasis | Deity |

| Ramananda | - | - | Rama |
| Chaitanya | - | - | Krishna |
## 2. Teachings
- Ramanuja:
- Best means of salvation = devotion (not Vedas, logic or meditation) (UPSC 2022)
- Vallabha: Worship of child Krishna (vatsalya bhakti)
- Ramananda: Replaced Vishnu worship with devotion to Rama
- ⚠ Advaita = Shankara; Vishishtadvaita = Ramanuja; Dvaita = Madhva; Dvaitadvaita = Nimbarka; Shuddhadvaita = Vallabha
- Exam angle: "which statement is NOT correct" - e.g. Madhva propounded Nirguna bhakti is wrong (RPSC 2024); Shankara = Advaita (APPSC-G1 2012).

## 2. Sri Vaishnavism in Andhra
- Theological base: His Vishishtadvaita (11th-12th c.) grounded Bhakti in Andhra
- Spread: Sri Vaishnavism = most widely spread sect of the Vijayanagara era
- Patrons: Velanati Cholas + Palnati Haihayas
- Influence: Inspired Brahmanayudu's Veera Vaishnava movement in Palnadu (aimed at casteless society)
- Inclusion: Gave Panchamas temple entry
- Prime example: Krishnadevaraya's Amuktamalyada ("Mala Dasari Katha")
- Tirumala: Credited with ordering temple worship + installing Govindaraja at Tirupati
- PYQ: Movement Ramanuja led in Andhra (devotion to Vishnu + social equality) = Bhakti movement (APPSC-GS 2022)
## 3. Commemoration
- Statue of Equality: Hyderabad; world's second-tallest sitting statue, inaugurated by the PM (UPSC 2022)
### Universal angles
- Current relevance (2026 discourse pieces):
- Ramanuja as "Ilaya Azhwar"; held to be incarnation of Adisesha/Lakshmana

- Narahari Tirtha (Madhva's disciple) spread Dvaita in Andhra-Kalinga from Srikurmam
- Vedanta Desika spread Vadakalai in Andhra (14th c.)
- Significance: Acharyas gave Bhakti a philosophical base against Advaita; opened temple worship to lower groups
