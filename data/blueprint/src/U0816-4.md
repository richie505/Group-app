### SHANTI Act — nuclear sector opened to private players
## 1. Act
- Name: Sustainable Harnessing and Advancement of Nuclear Energy for Transforming India (SHANTI) Act, 2025 — amends liability regime of CLNDA 2010
- ⚠ Sources differ: other notes say SHANTI replaced Atomic Energy Act 1962 and CLNDA 2010

- Bodies: Atomic Energy Redressal Advisory Council; Nuclear Damage Claims Commission; appeals to Appellate Tribunal for Electricity (Electricity Act 2003)
## 3. Background
- Atomic Energy Act 1962 replaced 1948 Act; amended 1986, 1987, 2015; CLNDA 2010 — no-fault liability
- Nuclear share ~3% of electricity; targets missed — 10 GW by 2000 (got 2.86 GW), 20 GW by 2020 (got 6.78 GW); new target 100 GW by 2047
- ⚠ Reactor principle: controlled nuclear fission

### Political funding — electoral trusts after electoral bonds verdict

## 2. Electoral trusts
- Introduced: Electoral Trusts Scheme, 2013 by CBDT

- Not disclosed: which company funded which party
- Prudent Electoral Trust: ₹33,330.54 crore (86.38% among top 10); FY 2024–25 received ₹2,668.49 crore, gave ₹2,180.71 crore to BJP and ₹216.34 crore to Congress

## 2. Parliamentary privileges
- Articles: 105 and 122 (Parliament); 194 and 212 (State Legislatures)
- Extend to: Attorney General — ⚠ not the President
- Article 105(3): until defined by law, privileges as of British House of Commons on 26 January 1950 — no law enacted yet
