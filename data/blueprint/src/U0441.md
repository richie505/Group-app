## 1. MUDRA

- Aim: "fund the unfunded"; SC/ST enterprises get priority
| Category | Loan size (CDX) |

### Digital programmes → purpose
- Aadhaar: 12-digit number (APPSC 2023); started 2009

- 9 pillars: from Broadband Highways to Early Harvest; incl. universal mobile access, public internet access
| Programme | Purpose |
