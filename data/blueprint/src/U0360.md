### Legislature — law-making, controls and options against the Court
## 1. Law-making
- Law-making: Parliament = chief law-making body, but Cabinet decides substance + timing of bills; bureaucracy drafts
- Private members' bills rarely pass
## 2. Controls on the executive
- Controls on executive: Question Hour (most effective), Zero Hour, half-an-hour discussions, adjournment motions, financial control, ratification of laws
- Committee scrutiny falling: Bills referred dropped from 71% (15th Lok Sabha) → 16% (17th Lok Sabha) (30 Jul 2026)
- ⚠ 71% = 15th Lok Sabha; 16% = 17th Lok Sabha
## 3. Options against a Supreme Court decision
| Option against an SC decision | Limit |
| Amend the law to remove the basis of the judgment (validating law) | Cannot simply overrule a verdict [GK] |
| Constitutional amendment (Art. 368) | Must respect the basic structure (Kesavananda Bharati) |
| Review or curative petition by the Government | Before the same Court [GK] |
### Judiciary — PIL origin, activism and its critics
- PIL origin: Began 1979 with a case filed on behalf of prisoners, not by them
- Basis: Art. 32, 226 + Art. 142 ("complete justice")
- Criticism: Upendra Baxi's typology of fears → ideological, epistemic, management, legitimation, democratic
- Overreach → policy paralysis + backlog
- Current: An MP called SC's orders on NCERT Class 8 chapter on "corruption in judiciary" "judicial overreach" (14 Mar 2026)
- ⚠ PIL 1979 = filed on behalf of prisoners, not by them

| T.S.R. Subramanian (2013) | Civil Services Boards, fixed tenure for civil servants, written instructions from political executives [GK] |

### Bureaucracy and political parties
## 1. Bureaucracy — advice, drafting, implementation discretion
- Permanent executive: All-India Services, State services, local + PSU staff
- Helps ministers formulate and implement policies; bound by legislature-approved policy
- Recruitment: UPSC + State PSCs; members removable for misbehaviour only after a Supreme Court inquiry (Art. 317; other grounds like insolvency need no inquiry)
- Concerns: Insensitivity to citizens, political interference, front-line discretion
- Accountability tools: Vigilance, conduct rules, CAG, CVC, Lokpal
## 2. Political parties and manifestos in agenda setting
- Manifesto → policy: 33.33% BC quota in AP urban local bodies = NDA 2024 poll promise, implemented by G.O. Rt. No. 1065 (20 Aug 2026)
- Delivery tracking: AP publishes Action Taken Reports (from 22 Jun 2026) against seven sector white papers

- ⚠ Key: (A) true, (R) the correct explanation (APPSC-GS 2018)
- Free press + new media: Free press necessary for vibrant democracy; new media enable participatory communication
- ⚠ Key: both true (APPSC-GS 2018)
- Government communication:
