### Space debris — definition, sources, terms and FIRSTs
## 1. Definition, sources, scale
- Definition: All non-functional man-made objects, incl. fragments, in orbit or re-entering
- Sources: (UPSC)
- Defunct satellites, spent rocket stages
- Fragmentation from collisions and explosions (main source)
- Scale: Over 1.2 million objects larger than 1 cm (ESA Space Environment Report 2025); a 10-cm object can shatter a satellite
## 2. Terms → meaning
| Term | Meaning |
| Kessler Syndrome | Debris density so high that collisions create more debris in a self-sustaining cascade → orbits unusable; named after NASA scientist Donald Kessler (1978) |
| MMOD | (micrometeoroids and orbital debris) top risk in LEO |
## 3. India's ASAT test
- Mission Shakti (27 March 2019): DRDO kinetic-kill ASAT test destroying Indian satellite Microsat-R at about 300 km - raised debris concerns (UPSC)

### Rules and mitigation → re-entry rules, DFSM 2030, POEM, ADR
## 1. India's new re-entry rules (2026)
- India's new re-entry rules (2026): (24 Aug 2026)
- Warn aircraft and ships in re-entry area at least 45 days before re-entry

- Gap: No international duty-of-care standard for orbits; licensing conditions differ → operators pick permissive regimes (1 Apr 2026)
## 2. Debris mitigation measures
| Measure | Fact |

| POEM | ISRO tests drag-augmentation devices (inflatable sails) so it re-enters within months, not decades |

### Treaties → year, core rule and India's status
| Instrument | Core rule | India's status [GK] |
| Outer Space Treaty 1967 | Space free for all; no national sovereignty/appropriation; Art. VI national responsibility incl. private actors; Art. VII liability; Art. IX due regard/environment | Party (ratified 1982) |
| Rescue Agreement 1968 | Return of astronauts and objects | Party |
| Liability Convention 1972 | Absolute liability for damage on Earth; fault liability in space | Party |
| Registration Convention 1975 | Register space objects with UN | Party |
| Moon Agreement 1979 | Moon as common heritage of mankind | Signed, not ratified |
- ⚠ Liability Convention: absolute liability on Earth, fault liability in space
- ⚠ Moon Agreement = only one India signed but not ratified
- Sovereignty: "The Outer Space Treaty does not recognise sovereignty in outer space" - landing at a lunar site gives no ownership (5 Aug 2026)
- ATLAC: UN working group building consensus on lunar landing-site coordination + debris rules

| Body / accord | Year | Fact / India |

### Debates → weaponisation of space, PAROS and India's stance
- PAROS: (Prevention of an Arms Race in Outer Space) long-running UN Conference on Disarmament agenda item, backed by UNGA resolutions

- Confidence-building (advance notices, hotlines)
