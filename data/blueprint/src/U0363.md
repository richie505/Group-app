### Citizens' Charter — principles, elements and adoption in India
## 1. Purpose and principles
- Six principles: Quality, choice, standards, value, accountability, transparency
- Purpose: Written commitment on service standards = time-bound service delivery and grievance redress (APPSC 2025 key)
- Basic elements:
- Clear service standards
- Grievance redress mechanism
- What it is not: A legally enforceable right; charters in India are non-justiciable
- 2nd ARC recommended statutory status
## 2. Adoption in India
- Adopted: 1997
- Coordination: DARPG coordinates formulation; nodal officers named in every ministry
- Website: goicharters.nic.in
- Citizens Charter Bill 2011: Time-bound delivery + grievance redress; lapsed with dissolution of Lok Sabha in 2014
- Exam angle: "main purpose of a Citizen's Charter" - time-bound service delivery and grievance redress.
- ⚠ UK 1991 → India 1997; Bill 2011 lapsed 2014

### Grievance redress — CPGRAMS, GRAI and AP platforms
## 1. CPGRAMS
- Full form: Centralised Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System
- Launch: 2007 by DARPG (Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions)
- Access: 24x7 via web, app and UMANG
- Disposal time limit: DARPG circular of 23 Aug 2024 cut max from 30 days to 21 days
- Features:
- Grievances through 5.1 lakh CSCs
- GRAI (Grievance Redressal Assessment & Index): Ranks ministries on 11 indicators, 4 dimensions
- Dimensions: efficiency, feedback, domain, organisational commitment
- Pensioners: CPENGRAMS
- ⚠ Disposal limit 30 → 21 days (23 Aug 2024); GRAI = 11 indicators, 4 dimensions

### Social audit, Lokpal and ombudsman — mechanisms compared
## 1. Social audit
- MGNREGA mandate: MGNREGA 2005 (Section 17); Gram Sabha audits all works
- Social audit = accountability tool of MGNREGA (APPSC 2018)
- AP society: AP Society for Social Audit, Accountability and Transparency (SSAAT), set up 2009 as an independent society
- 2nd ARC: Institutionalise social audits in all major welfare schemes
## 2. Grievance vs corruption vs maladministration
| Mechanism | Deals with | Nature |
| CPGRAMS / state portals | Service-delivery grievances | Executive, time-bound disposal |
| Lokpal / Lokayukta | Corruption allegations against public functionaries | Statutory (Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act 2013) |
| Classic ombudsman | Maladministration and citizen complaints | Independent officer of the legislature |
- ⚠ Lokpal = corruption (statutory, 2013 Act); CPGRAMS = service grievances (executive)
### Universal angles

- Criticism & debates:
- Charters lack legal force
