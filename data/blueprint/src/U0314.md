### Chief Minister — position and appointment (Article 164)
## 1. Position
- Real executive head of the State and head of the Government; heads the State Council of Ministers
- Leader of the majority party or coalition in the Legislative Assembly; leader of the Assembly; chief spokesperson of the State Government
- Article 163: Council of Ministers headed by the CM to aid and advise the Governor
- Article 164: Appointment, tenure, responsibility and size limit of Ministers
## 2. Appointment — Article 164
- Appointed by the Governor — normally the leader of the majority party; in a hung Assembly the Governor has discretion
- Art. 164(1): Governor appoints the CM (APPSC-GS 2021)
- Hung Assembly: Governor's discretion (no constitutional criteria)
- Sarkaria (1987) + Punchhi (2010) order: pre-poll alliance → single largest party with support → post-poll coalition → post-poll alliance with outside support
- Early floor test follows (May 2026)
- Member of the Legislative Assembly or the Legislative Council (if any) — need not be a member when appointed, but must become one within six months
- Non-member: can be CM/minister for 6 months (Art. 164(4)); ceases unless elected
- Oath by the Governor; resignation addressed to the Governor
- Oath: administered by the Governor (Art. 164(3))
- Delhi and Puducherry: CM appointed by the President — Article 239AA (Delhi); Article 239A / GUT Act 1963 (Puducherry)
### Chief Minister — tenure and responsibility
## 1. Tenure and responsibility
- Confidence: CM stays only while he has the Assembly's confidence
- Loses it → Governor can ask him to resign or dismiss the government
- Literal "pleasure" reading cautioned against in the Constituent Assembly
- Collective responsibility: to the Legislative Assembly (Art. 164(2)) (BPSC 2022)
- Salaries: fixed by the state legislature (Art. 164(5))
- ⚠ Clauses of Art. 164: (1) appointment, (2) collective responsibility, (3) oath, (4) 6 months rule, (5) salaries
### CM's powers and duties towards the Governor — Article 167
## 1. Powers and duties
- Ministers: Selects them; allocates portfolios; can recommend the removal of any Minister; advises the Governor on appointing other Ministers
- CM's powers: term not fixed; recommends portfolios, can drop ministers, can advise dissolution of the Assembly
- Article 167 — link between the Governor and the Council of Ministers:
- Communicates all decisions on administration and proposals for legislation
- Furnishes information required by the Governor
- Submits for the Council's consideration any matter decided by a single Minister (submit for Council consideration, if Governor requires)
- Advises the Governor on: summoning and prorogation of the Legislature; dissolution of the Assembly
- Chief link: CM = chief link between Governor and Council
- Seeking information under Art. 167 = a constitutional discretion of the Governor
