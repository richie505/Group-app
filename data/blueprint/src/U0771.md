### Agenda 2030 — origin, adoption, nature
## 1. Framework
- 17 SDGs, 169 targets — target year 2030; period 2015-2030
- Official framework: "Transforming our World: The 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development" — the 2030 Agenda was adopted in 2015 with 17 SDGs
- Adopted by: UN General Assembly, 70th Session, 2015
- Proposed at Rio+20 in 2012
- In India: NITI Aayog implements the SDGs
## 2. Origin
- Conceptualised at the UN Conference on Sustainable Development (Rio+20), Rio de Janeiro, 2012
- Outcome document "The Future We Want" called for global goals to follow the MDGs
- Colombia and Guatemala first proposed the SDG idea there
## 3. Nature
- Universal: Applies to all nations (developed, developing, least developed)
- Human-rights basis: Universal Declaration of Human Rights (1948) + international human-rights treaties
- ⚠ Conceived at Rio+20 (2012); adopted at UNGA 70th session (2015)

### Goal-specific targets — Goal 4, Goal 5, Goal 10
## 1. Goal 4
- Free, equitable primary and secondary education
- Early-childhood care
- Affordable technical, vocational and tertiary education
- End gender disparity in education; youth and adult literacy
## 2. Goal 5
- End all discrimination and violence against women and girls (the PYQ answer)
- End child marriage and female genital mutilation
- Recognise unpaid care work; women's full participation in leadership
## 3. Goal 10
- Reforms: better regulation of global financial markets; greater voice for developing countries in global institutions; safe and orderly migration; remittance costs below 3%

### Indices and reports → publisher and data
## 1. Sustainable Development Report
- Publisher: UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (SDSN); scores run from 0 to 100
- India: Still below Bhutan, the Maldives, Nepal and Sri Lanka; lags on 13 of 17 goals → SDG 2 the biggest worry
- 2025 top three: Finland (87), Sweden, Denmark
- Global progress: Only 17% of targets on track worldwide
## 2. MoSPI SDG-NIF
- Roles: MoSPI develops the National Indicator Framework (NIF); NITI Aayog coordinates SDG work
- SDG-NIF Progress Report 2026:
- 277 indicators across all 17 SDGs
- Modelled on the UN Global Indicator Framework
- Multidimensional poverty: 24.85% (2015-16) → 14.96% (2019-21)
- ⚠ SDR = SDSN; NIF = MoSPI; coordination = NITI Aayog
## 3. SDG localisation
- NER District SDG Index:
- 1st edition (2021-22) released August 2021
- Localised SDGs in Gram Panchayats (Ministry of Panchayati Raj): 9 themes
- Self-sufficient infrastructure; socially just and secure; good governance; women-friendly village
- AP - Swarna Andhra KPI Monitoring Framework: Launched 13 Dec 2024; aligns with SDGs through Vision Monitoring Units at state, district and constituency level
### Universal angles
- Current relevance: India's best-ever SDR rank = 94th in 2026
- AP angle: 74, Front Runner, 9th, with SDG 7 at rank 1
