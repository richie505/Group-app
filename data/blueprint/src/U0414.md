### RPwD Act 2016 → provisions and number game
- Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act — the RPwD Act (2016 (passed); came into force on April 19, 2017): A rights-based disability framework; replaced the 1995 Act; expanded disability categories from 7 to 21 — recognizes 21 categories; 4% reservation in government jobs (public employment) and 5% in higher education for persons with benchmark disabilities; penalizes discrimination
- Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act: The first rights-based disability law — 2016
- Sudha Kaul Committee: Constituted 2010 by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment — concerned Persons with Disabilities (PwD)
- Drafted the initial replacement bill for the PwD Act, 1995; aligned India's domestic legal architecture with the UNCRPD
- Its work culminated in the RPwD Act, 2016
## 1. Enactment and scope
- ⚠ Trap: "Passed in 2018" is WRONG (UPSC 2026)
- Section 2(s): PwD = long-term physical, mental, intellectual or sensory impairment that, with barriers, hinders full participation
## 2. Provisions → value
| Provision / entitlement | Value / quantum |
| Replaced | PwD (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995 (welfare-based to rights-based) |
| Specified disabilities | 21 (from 7), e.g. autism, acid attack victims, sickle cell disease; Centre may add more |
| Benchmark disability | At least 40% of a specified disability (Dec 2025) |
| Reservation in government jobs | 4% |
| Reservation in higher education | 5% of seats |
| Free education | Children with benchmark disabilities aged 6-18 |
| Monitoring | Chief Commissioner for PwDs, Sec. 75(1) |
- ⚠ 4% jobs vs 5% higher education; 21 disabilities vs 7 in 1995; benchmark 40%
