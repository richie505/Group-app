### Communist Party in Andhra → founding, conferences, bans (event → year)
## 1. Founding and first units
- 1934: Secret meeting in Katragadda Narayana Rao's garden, Vijayawada → 7-member committee, P. Sundarayya secretary
- January 1936, Kakinada: Andhra Communist Party formally set up (1st Maha Sabha on 29 Jan 1936)
- Some notes credit Darsi Chenchaiah with founding it at Kakinada in 1936
- Telangana's first unit: Hyderabad State Comrades Association, 1939 (G2 2012 key; some notes say 1940)
- Members: Makhdoom Mohiuddin, Raj Bahadur Gour, Syed Ibrahim

### Universal angles
- Significance: Communists shifted Andhra politics from language to land → 41 of 145 Andhra-region seats in 1952 + drove Visalandhra
- Current relevance (PT365, Apr 2026):
- National chronology: CPI banned 1934 → ban lifted 1942 → 1964 split
- Criticism & debates: "People's War" line + opposition to Quit India cost the CPI the 1945-46 elections
