### Law Commission — history and nature
- Colonial precursor: Macaulay's Law Commission produced the IPC (1860)
- First post-independence Commission: 1955, chaired by M.C. Setalvad
- Nature: Neither constitutional nor statutory
- An executive body set up by a Government of India order for a fixed 3-year term
- Reports to the Ministry of Law and Justice, not the President
- ⚠ Trap: All the statements in the 2024 PYQ were incorrect
