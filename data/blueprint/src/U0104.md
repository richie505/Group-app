### Society & temple style
- Vishti — forced labour (R. S. Sharma's Indian feudalism, with sub-infeudation, closed village economy)
- Jauhar — women burning themselves to avoid capture; e.g. Rani Padmini of Chittor (vs Alauddin Khalji); sati also practised
- Latina / Rekha-prasada — single curvilinear tower; Phamsana — sloping stepped roof; Valabhi — barrel-vaulted
- Maru-Gurjara (11th–13th c.) — Solanki Nagara sub-style of Gujarat and Rajasthan; joins Maha-Maru and Maha-Gurjara
- feature — Kirti-torana
### Exam corner
- Four Agnikula clans — Paramara, Pratihara, Chauhan, Solanki (not Chandela)
- Rajput warfare — personal valour in face-to-face fighting; chivalry, clan honour
- Bhojshala — MP High Court declared it a Hindu temple on ASI findings; ASI survey ordered 2024
- 2003 ASI arrangement — worship by the two communities on separate days
