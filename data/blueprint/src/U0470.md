### Phases of population growth
## Phases
- ★ 1921 — 'Great Divide' / 'Demographic Divide' year; only Census with negative decadal growth (−0.31%); faster growth after it
- Population fell in 1911-21; 1951 is NOT the demographic divide
- Phases: 1901-21 stagnant, 1921-51 steady, 1951-81 rapid, 1981-2011 high but slowing; 1961-71 the 'explosion'
- ★ India's population since 1951 — about 36 crore (1951) to over 102 crore (2001), almost tripled
