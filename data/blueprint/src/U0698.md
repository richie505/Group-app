### Supercomputing journey
- AP = first State with a dedicated quantum computing policy
- 1991 — PARAM 8000, C-DAC: first Indian supercomputer; Vijay Bhatkar
- 2015 — NSM: DST + MeitY; C-DAC Pune + IISc
- Later — PARAM Shivay, IIT (BHU): first NSM machine
- ISC 2023 — AIRAWAT (C-DAC) 75th in Top500
- Jan 2026 — PARAM Shakti, IIT Madras: 3.1 PF, Rudra board
