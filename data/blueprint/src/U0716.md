### Connectivity and electronics schemes → body, year and target — BharatNet
## 1. BharatNet
- Origin: NOFN, approved 25 Oct 2011 for 2.5 lakh GPs
- SPV: BBNL registered 25 Feb 2012; merged with BSNL in Feb 2023
- Implementing CPSUs: BSNL, RailTel, PGCIL
- Target: 100 Mbps per GP, scalable to 1 Gbps
| Phase | Focus |
| I and II (from 2011) | Fibre to GPs through CPSUs and states (APPCA May 2026) |
| III / Amended BharatNet (ABP) | Rs 1.39 lakh crore; PPP model, bigger role for states, revenue sharing, last mile, links with 5G (APPCA May 2026; APPCA Apr 2026) |
## 2. USOF / Digital Bharat Nidhi
- USOF: non-lapsable fund for telecom in commercially unviable rural and remote areas
- Funded NOFN
- Renamed Digital Bharat Nidhi (DBN) under the Telecommunications Act 2023
- DBN allocation: 5% of collections (~Rs 500 cr/yr) → Telecom Technology Development Fund
- DBN runs the Amended BharatNet MoCs
- ⚠ USOF → renamed Digital Bharat Nidhi (Telecommunications Act 2023); BBNL merged with BSNL (Feb 2023)
