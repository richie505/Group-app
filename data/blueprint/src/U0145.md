### Saints → teachings and contributions
## 1. Kabir
- Birth legend: Born to a Brahmin widow; raised by Muslim weaver Niru and wife Nima
- Stream: Nirguna Bhakti (APPSC-GS 2024)
- Attack on ritual: Condemned rituals + idol worship of both Hindus and Muslims; opposed caste
- Mission: Hindu-Muslim unity; regarded as greatest saint of the Bhakti cult
- ⚠ Trap: Kabir was not influenced by Shaikh Ahmad Sirhindi, who lived later (1564-1624) (UPSC 2019)
## 2. Chaitanya
- Turn to sainthood: After his wife's death; passionate devotion to Krishna
- Sect: Founded Gaudiya Vaishnavism
- Sankirtan: Popularised collective devotional singing; later written up as Padavali kirtans
## 3. Maharashtra saints → contribution
| Saint | Key fact |
| Namdev | Tailor family; abhangs in Marathi and Hindi; hymns in the Guru Granth Sahib; had the greatest influence on the people |
| Eknath | Varkari saint; Marathi bhakti |
| Tukaram | Abhangas to Vithoba; Shivaji's contemporary |
| Ramdas | Stressed strength, discipline and devotion to Rama; role in Maratha political awakening; Shivaji's guru |
| Janabai | Namdev's disciple; low caste; servant in his home; 300+ poems |
