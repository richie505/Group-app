## 3. Central Assembly bomb (1929)
- Event: 8 April 1929, Central Legislative Assembly, Delhi
- Participants: Bhagat Singh and Batukeshwar Dutt threw smoke (non-lethal) bombs + leaflets
- Protest against: Public Safety Bill + Trade Disputes Bill (curbing civil liberties and workers)
- Slogans: "Inquilab Zindabad"; leaflet: "To make the deaf hear"
- Strategy: Courted arrest to use the court as a platform
- ⚠ Saunders was killed in place of Scott; Tegart's would-be killer hit Ernest Day

## 2. Lahore Conspiracy Case (1929-1931)

- Hunger strike: Undertrials' 1929 Lahore jail hunger strike for political-prisoner status (Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Dutt, Jatin Das)
- Executed: Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev and Rajguru on 23 March 1931 (Lahore jail); cremated at Hussainiwala (Punjab)
- Transportation for life: Kishori Lal, Mahabir Singh, Bejoy Kumar Sinha, Shiv Verma, Gaya Prasad, Jaidev Kapur, Kamalnath Tewari
- Batukeshwar Dutt: life transportation in the Assembly bomb case
