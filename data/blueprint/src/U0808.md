## 2. Forms and burdens of malnutrition
- WHO definition: Malnutrition in all its forms =
- Undernutrition (wasting, stunting, underweight)
- + Inadequate vitamins/minerals
- + Overweight/obesity and diet-related NCDs
| Term | Meaning |
| Double burden | Undernutrition + overnutrition together (5 Jun 2026) |
| Triple burden (SOFI 2025) | Undernutrition + micronutrient deficiency/anaemia + overweight |
- NFHS-6 + Vellore cohort study (birth to 9 years): thinness and overweight rise sharply between ages 7 and 9
- State extremes (Jun 2026):
- Madhya Pradesh: high child wasting and underweight in NFHS-6
- Meghalaya: decline in stunting
