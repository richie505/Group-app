### Amendments → changes to financial relations (60th, 80th, 88th, 101st) — 101st Amendment - GST

### GST Council — Article 279A, composition, voting, Mohit Minerals, compensation

### Grants-in-aid — statutory (Art. 275) vs discretionary (Art. 282)
## 1. Grants and disputes
- Article 275: Statutory grants-in-aid to States — principles recommended by the Finance Commission
- Article 282: Discretionary grants — by the Union or a State, for any public purpose, even outside its legislative purview
- Centre–State legal and constitutional disputes: Resolved by the Supreme Court under its Original Jurisdiction — Article 131 — ⚠ not by the Finance Commission
## 2. Statutory vs discretionary grants
| Feature | Statutory grants | Discretionary grants |
| Article | 275 (includes grants for ST welfare and Scheduled Areas) | 282[GK] (any public purpose) |
| Recommended by | Finance Commission | Earlier Planning Commission (extra-constitutional; the larger part of Central grants); now Union ministries |
| Charged on CFI | Yes - charged on the Consolidated Fund of India | No [GK] |
| Purpose | Needy states; ST welfare, Scheduled Areas | Any public purpose; historically the larger share |
- Statutory grants: On the Finance Commission's advice under Art. 275
- ⚠ Trap: NOT on NITI Aayog's recommendation/advice (PYQ 2019; APPSC-GS 2019)
- Design rationale: Art. 275 grants are a foundational design element - devolution formulas cannot capture state-specific needs (6 Aug 2026)
