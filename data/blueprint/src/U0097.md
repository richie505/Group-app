### Kakatiya style — features, terms and influences
## 1. Local innovations
| Innovation | What it is |
| Stellate (star) plan | raised star-shaped platform; more wall surface for sculpture, more light and air |
| Sandbox foundation | deep pits filled with sand, stone chips, earth - shock absorber on black cotton soil; why Ramappa has survived earthquakes |
| Floating bricks | light porous bricks in Ramappa's vimana - float on water; reduce the dead weight of the tower |
| Ekasila carving | monolithic Nandi and pillars in black basalt, as in the Thousand Pillar Nandi; granite |
| Lathe-turned pillars, Kirtimukha motif | polished granite pillars; "face of glory" motif |
- Brackets: Madanika/bracket figures projecting from outer walls in dance poses
## 2. Influences and phases
- Influence: Western Chalukyas of Kalyani (carved pillars, star-shaped platforms, ornate doorways, sculptured ceilings)
- APPSC G2 2025 statement II correct
- Follows Chalukyan (Vesara) style per some notes; others call it a transitional style between early Chalukyan and Vijayanagara
- Jain phase: Early Kakatiya kings built Jain temples (APPSC G2 2025 statement I)
- e.g. Padmakshi temple, Hanamkonda (Prola II), originally a Jain shrine
- ⚠ Floating bricks = lighter tower; sandbox foundation = earthquake cushion
### Thousand Pillar and Ramappa temples — features
## 1. Thousand Pillar Temple, Hanamkonda
- Builder & date: Rudradeva (Prataparudra I), 1163 CE
- Other names: Rudreshwara (Rudreswara Swamy) temple / Veyi Stambhala Gudi
- Plan: Trikuta (three-shrine) temple on a star-shaped platform
- Deities: Rudreshwara (Shiva), Vasudeva (Vishnu), Surya
- Surya takes the place Brahma has in the usual Trinity (APPSC G2 2025 statement III)
- Pillars: 369 pillars; lathe-turned pillars give the illusion of a thousand
## 2. Ramappa (Rudreshwara) temple, Palampet
- Builder & date: c. 1213 CE by Kakatiya general Recherla Rudra, in Ganapatideva's reign
- Names: Rudreshwaralayam; deity = Shiva as Ramalingeswara
- Sculptor: Named after sculptor Ramappa
- UNESCO: World Heritage Site on 25 July 2021
- Type: APPSC G2 2012 answer key says Trikuta
- ⚠ Trap: In your notes the three-shrine Trikuta plan belongs to the Thousand Pillar Temple
- Engineering: Star-shaped platform; floating bricks in the shikhara/vimana; sandbox foundation (details under Kakatiya style)
- Stone: Granite/dolerite pillars + brackets (some notes say sandstone brackets)
- Sculpture:
- Madanika (dancing-girl) bracket figures on outer walls
- Statues of the Recherla Rudra couple + 28 elephant-lion figures (Gajakesari title)
- Painted ceilings: Ramayana-Mahabharata scenes
- Exam angle: builder-ruler-place matching, the UNESCO year, and "floating bricks / sandbox" statements.
- ⚠ Both called Rudreshwara: Thousand Pillar = 1163 (Rudradeva, Hanamkonda); Ramappa = 1213 (Recherla Rudra, Palampet)
