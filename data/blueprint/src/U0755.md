### Solar technologies — PV vs thermal, types and applications
| Type | Principle / material |
| Crystalline silicon (mono, poly) | Photovoltaic effect; dominant market [GK] |
| Thin-film (CdTe, CIGS, a-Si) | Cheaper, flexible, lower efficiency [GK] |
| Perovskite | IIT Bombay (MNRE project): 26% cell, world's 2nd highest; 30.2% perovskite-silicon tandem (IYB 2026) |
| Solar thermal / CSP | Flat-plate collectors, concentrators, solar power towers; heat for water, steam, power; can store heat (UPSC) |
| Photovoltaic (PV) | Photovoltaic effect frees electrons → DC current; inverter converts to AC; cells: monocrystalline, polycrystalline silicon, thin-film |
| Solar thermal / CSP | Mirrors concentrate sunlight to heat a fluid → steam turbine |
- Capacity utilisation: Solar PV ~21% vs wind 36-40% vs coal ~85% (Aug 2026)
- Applications:
- Solar pumps (PM-KUSUM B, C)
- Floating solar - see floating page
- ⚠ PV = light → electricity directly (DC); CSP = heat → steam turbine; only CSP can store heat
