### Aadhaar — FIRSTs, format, biometrics and scale
## 1. FIRSTs
| FIRST | FACT |
| First Aadhaar number | Ranjana Sonawane, Tembhli village, Nandurbar district, Maharashtra, 29 Sept 2010 |
| First Aadhaar village | Tembhli (APPSC 2025 asked) |
## 2. Basics
- Number format: 12-digit random number (no caste, religion or geography coding) issued by UIDAI
- Lifelong, free, one enrolment per person
- Biometrics: 10 fingerprints, 2 irises and a face photo
- Scale (31 Jul 2025): 142 crore numbers generated; ~133.65 crore live after adjusting for deaths
- PIB (Aug 2026): Aadhaar access for "over 145 crore" people
- Uses: DBT (JAM trinity), Jeevan Pramaan digital life certificate, bank and mobile KYC
- Aadhaar e-KYC simplified banking and public services
- ⚠ Aadhaar = 12-digit (UIDAI); ABHA = 14-digit; launched 2009, first number 2010

### Payment systems and products → operator
## 1. UPI
- UPI: launched April 2016 by NPCI (UPSC)
- Single app routes payments from several bank accounts using a VPA (e.g. name@bank)
- UPI 2.0: overdraft accounts linkable, pre-authorised payments, signed merchant QR, Rs 2 lakh/day limit (UPSC)
## 2. NPCI products
| Product | Key fact |
| BHIM | NPCI's UPI app; launched 30 Dec 2016 after demonetisation (APP) |
| Bharat QR | Interoperable QR code standard by NPCI with card networks [GK] |
| e-RUPI | One-time, cashless, person- and purpose-specific voucher; NPCI with DFS, MoHFW and NHA (APPSC 2022); launched Aug 2021 [GK] |
| UPI Lite | On-device wallet for small-value payments without a UPI PIN [GK] |
## 3. Systems → operator
| System | Operator |
| NEFT, RTGS | RBI[GK] |
| IMPS, UPI, AePS, NACH, RuPay | NPCI[GK]; AePS = cash and balance enquiry at micro-ATMs with Aadhaar (CDI) |
| FASTag (NETC) | NPCI with NHAI/IHMCL [GK]; counted among DPI (ES 2025-26) |
| iPay gateway | IRCTC (APPSC 2019) |
- ⚠ NEFT, RTGS = RBI; IMPS, UPI, AePS = NPCI; iPay = IRCTC
## 4. International acceptance
- France = first country in Europe to accept UPI (APPSC 2025)
- PayNow-UPI link with Singapore (2023)
