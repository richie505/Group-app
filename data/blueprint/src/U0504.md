### India's output & ranks
## India
- Golden Revolution — horticulture boom, 1991–2003
- Leading fruit — Banana (ahead of mango, apple, grapes)
- Milk — India first, about 25% of world output
- Eggs — India 2nd in the world
- Silk — India second; only country producing all five varieties
- MPEDA — HQ Kochi; top body for marine exports under Ministry of Commerce and Industry
## States
| Product | Leading State |
| Mulberry silk | Karnataka |
| Tasar silk | Jharkhand |
| Muga and eri silk | Assam |
| Eggs | Andhra Pradesh (18.37%), then Tamil Nadu |
| Fruits | Andhra Pradesh |
- ★ Karnataka — leading mulberry silk State (2024-25); AP, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal 2nd–4th
- AP also leads in shrimp, oil palm, eggs; AP is seventh in milk (main national lead: aquaculture)
- AP export mangoes — Banganapalli and Totapuri
### Sector data
- Horticulture — about 33% (one-third) of agricultural GVA, not half
- AP horticulture — 46.43 lakh acres, 406.38 lakh MT; Rayalaseema to be a Horticulture Hub
- Fisheries — fastest-growing allied sub-sector, about 9.08% a year since 2014-15
- Inland fisheries — more than 75% of India's fish output
- Growth: livestock 7.1%, fisheries 8.8% (fisheries faster)
- AP — fisheries largest and fastest-growing allied sub-sector (17.69%)
### Livestock & horticulture schemes
- MIDH — Centrally Sponsored, since 2014-15; includes the National Horticulture Mission (2005-06)
- National Livestock Mission sub-schemes — breed development (poultry, sheep, goat, piggery), feed and fodder, innovation and extension (incl. livestock insurance)
- NLM covers productivity, feed/fodder, health, entrepreneurship; indigenous bovine breeds = Rashtriya Gokul Mission
- Credit Guarantee Scheme for the Livestock Sector — Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (not NABARD, RBI, Finance Ministry)
### Fisheries (Blue Revolution)
- PMMSY — launched 2020, about ₹20,050 crore, extended to FY 2025-26
- Training and capacity building — National Fisheries Development Board (not MPEDA); under the Central Sector component (with quarantine, harbour modernisation, data systems)
- Funding of the Centrally Sponsored component:
| Area | Centre:State |
| General | 60:40 |
| North-Eastern and Himalayan States | 90:10 |
| Union Territories | 100% central |
- PM-MKSSY (Matsya Kisan Samridhi Sah-Yojana) — Central Sector sub-scheme under PMMSY; formal fisheries, helps micro-enterprises
- FIDF — fishing harbours and landing centres
### Bodies & processing
- ★ Mega Food Parks — to improve infrastructure for food processing; link agriculture to market (farmers, processors, retailers)
- Cluster approach links farms → processing facilities → markets
- PM Kisan SAMPADA Yojana — umbrella scheme of Ministry of Food Processing Industries
- PMFME — One District One Product (ODOP)
- India largest producer of turmeric; National Turmeric Board — Nizamabad
- Operation Flood (1970–1996) — three phases; by NDDB (1965, Anand) on the Amul model
- Sweet Revolution — honey/apiculture; Golden Fibre = jute; Silver Fibre = cotton; Blue Revolution = fisheries
- National Beekeeping and Honey Mission (2020, Atmanirbhar Bharat) — via the National Bee Board
### Andhra Pradesh ranks
- AP — 4th in coconut; 1st in oil palm (2.27 lakh ha)
- AP — largest aquaculture and farmed shrimp producer; main variety Vannamei
- AP — 2nd in raw silk (61,394 MT cocoons, 2024-25); belt around Hindupur and Chittoor
### AP places
- Rayalaseema horticulture hub — banana, oil palm, cashew, coconut, Araku coffee (not tea)
- AP sericulture belt — Sri Sathya Sai/Anantapur (around Hindupur) and Chittoor
### Exam corner
- ★ FPO (Food Products Order) mark — mandatory on all processed fruit products (jams, squashes, pickles etc.)
- Processed food — around 23% of agricultural exports (2024-25); agri exports = 11.86% of merchandise exports
