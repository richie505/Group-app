### NABARD — establishment, status, refinance and merged bodies
## 1. NABARD
- National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development — established on 12 July 1982 under Act 61 of 1981 of Parliament, during the Sixth Five Year Plan
- Created under the NABARD Act, 1981; recommended by CRAFICARD; initial corpus Rs 100 crore
- Headquarters: Mumbai
- An apex institution (or bank) for agricultural refinance — the apex agricultural and rural finance institution; an apex refinancing institution
- Status: Apex development finance institution for agriculture and rural credit; coordinates rural financing system (APPSC 2024 key); premier institution for agricultural credit (APPSC 2019 key)
- Associated with financing agriculture and rural development — ⚠ not agriculture alone; concerned with agriculture and rural development
- Mainly refinances institutions — refinance to Scheduled Commercial Banks, Regional Rural Banks, State Land Development Banks and State Cooperative Banks; also listed as providing direct rural credit
- Primary role: Refinance institution (APPSC 2025 key) - refinance and development; also refinances NBFC-MFIs
- ⚠ Sources differ: other notes say NABARD does not lend to farmers directly
- Other roles: Supervises RRBs + cooperative banks; runs SHG-Bank Linkage Programme
## 2. Bodies merged and ownership
- Bodies merged:
- RBI's Agricultural Credit Department (ACD)
- Rural Planning and Credit Cell (RPCC)
- Agricultural Refinance and Development Corporation (ARDC)
- Ownership: RBI sold its stake in 2019 → fully owned by Government of India
- ⚠ Act 1981; established 12 July 1982

### Kisan Credit Card and interest subvention — features and limits — KCC basics

### Kisan Credit Card and interest subvention — features and limits — Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (MISS)
## 1. Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (MISS)
- MISS (Central Sector; RBI + NABARD):
- Short-term KCC crop loans at 7%
- 1.5% subvention to lenders
- 3% prompt repayment incentive → effective 4%
- Limit: ₹5 lakh (Aug 2026); earlier crop loans up to Rs 3 lakh at 7%
- Post-harvest: 7% loans for up to 6 months against WDRA negotiable warehouse receipts (small and marginal farmers)
- Reach: Subsidy since inception ₹1.87 lakh crore (Rs 1.87 lakh crore); ~5.9 crore beneficiaries (Feb 2025)
- Kisan Rin Portal (Sept 2023): Integrates KCC + subvention claims
- Flagged ₹1,080.88 crore (Rs 1,080.88 crore) duplicate/excess claims out of Rs 37,506.53 crore (2025-26)
- Not in your sources: whether the Rs 3 lakh MISS ceiling has been raised in a recent Budget.
- ⚠ 7% loan − 3% prompt repayment = 4% effective; lender gets 1.5%
