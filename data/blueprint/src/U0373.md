### Functions and powers → RBI, MPC, SEBI, IRDAI
## 1. RBI functions
- Functions:
- Monetary authority
- Currency issue, except the ₹1 note and coins (issued by Ministry of Finance)
- Regulator and supervisor of banks and NBFCs
- Forex manager under FEMA 1999
- Public Debt Management Cell: Set up to reduce RBI's conflict of interest between monetary policy and debt management
## 2. Monetary Policy Committee
- MPC: Statutory six-member body under the RBI Act, 1934; fixes the repo rate
- Composition:
- 3 from the RBI: Governor (chair, casting vote) + a Deputy Governor + one officer
- 3 external members appointed by the Centre for 4 years
- Objectives: Price stability primary; growth secondary
## 3. SEBI mandate & powers
- Regulates:
- Securities market and stock exchanges
- Listed companies and intermediaries
- Mutual funds
- Predecessor: Replaced the Controller of Capital Issues
- Quasi-judicial powers: Investigates, summons, passes orders and penalties
- Appeal route: Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) → Supreme Court
- IRDAI and PFRDA orders also appealable to SAT since 2014
## 4. IRDAI
- Statutory body: Under the IRDA Act, 1999; apex regulator of insurance and reinsurance companies
- Private entry: The Act opened insurance to private participation
### Acts, Sections and amendments → provisions
| Act / Section | Provision |
| Sec. 45ZA (RBI Act) | Centre, in consultation with the RBI, sets the inflation target every 5 years |
| Sec. 45ZN (RBI Act) | Band breached for 3 consecutive quarters → RBI reports to the Centre with reasons, remedies and time to restore |
| Section 114A of the Insurance Act, 1938 | IRDAI's regulation-making power |
| Sabka Bima Sabki Raksha (Amendment of Insurance Laws) Act, 2025 | Passed 17 Dec 2025; allows up to 100% FDI in insurance companies |
| Sec. 16A of the IRDA Act (inserted 2025) | Policyholders' Education and Protection Fund |

- Adopted: 2016
- Target: 4% CPI-Combined ±2%
- Nominal anchor: headline CPI (base year 2024)
- Insurance FDI cap timeline: 26% (2000) → 49% (2015) → 74% (2021) → 100% (2025-26)

### RBI & Monetary Policy Committee
## Monetary Policy Committee & inflation targeting
- MPC — six members; three external members appointed by the Centre for 4 years
- Governor chairs the MPC, with a casting vote
- Section 45ZA, RBI Act — Centre, asking the RBI, sets the inflation target every five years
- Target — 4% CPI-Combined, tolerance band ± 2% (CPI-Combined, not WPI)
- Price stability is the primary objective; growth is secondary
- Recommended by the Urjit Patel Committee (2014); framework adopted in 2016
- Narasimham — banking reforms; Malhotra — insurance reforms; Raghuram Rajan — financial-sector reforms
## RBI functions
- One-rupee note and coins — issued by the Ministry of Finance, not RBI
- RBI — monetary authority, foreign-exchange management under FEMA, regulation and supervision of banks and NBFCs
- Housing finance companies — regulated by the RBI since 2019; NHB remains the apex housing refinancer; Centre took full ownership of NHB in 2019
- DICGC (1978) — wholly-owned subsidiary of the RBI (not Ministry of Finance); insures bank deposits; NBFC deposits not covered
- Banking Ombudsman Scheme — began 14 June 1995, under Section 35A of the Banking Regulation Act; quasi-judicial
## Other bodies
- SEBI — set up by resolution in 1988; statutory under the SEBI Act, 1992
- SEBI replaced the Controller of Capital Issues
- FSDC (2010, non-statutory) — chaired by the Union Finance Minister; financial stability and inter-regulatory coordination
### SEBI appeal route
- Appeals against SEBI orders — Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT), then the Supreme Court (not NCLAT)
### Insurance FDI cap
- Insurance FDI cap — 26% (2000) → 49% (2015) → 74% (2021) → 100% (2025-26)
- Sabka Bima Sabki Raksha Act, 2025 — added Section 16A to the IRDA Act: Policyholders' Education and Protection Fund; also allows up to 100% FDI in insurers
### Committees & councils
- FSDC — set up 2010; non-statutory apex body; chaired by the Union Finance Minister (not RBI Governor)
- Financial Sector Legislative Reforms Commission — chaired by Justice B.N. Srikrishna
- FSLRC proposals — single Indian Financial Code, Unified Financial Agency, Public Debt Management Agency
- Not proposed: a separate constitutional court for financial disputes
### Who regulates it now
- SEBI — listed companies, intermediaries, mutual funds
- Housing finance companies — RBI since 2019
- Forward Markets Commission (commodities regulator) — merged into SEBI in September 2015
- BIFR — formed under SICA 1985; dissolved 1 December 2016; cases moved to NCLT/NCLAT under the IBC
- NFRA — oversees auditing and accounting standards under the Companies Act, 2013
- PFRDA's NPS e-Shramik — pension coverage for informal workers
### Recent actions (2025-26)
- SWAGAT-FI (SEBI) — single-window framework for trusted FPIs and FVCIs
- Ind AS 109 and Ind AS 117 — replaced IGAAP for insurers; IRDAI enforces adoption
- D-SIIs, FY 2025–26 — LIC, GIC Re, New India Assurance (HDFC Bank is a D-SIB, not a D-SII)
