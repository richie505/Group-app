### Composition and first session → features, numbers
## 1. Composition - Cabinet Mission scheme
- Provincial seats: Split among General, Muslim, Sikh in proportion to population
- Each community's members elected by its own members in the provincial assembly by proportional representation (STV)
- Princely states: Members nominated by rulers; seats initially unfilled
- Non-members: Nearly every leader a member except Gandhi and Jinnah
- Women: 15 women members (e.g. Durgabai Deshmukh, Sarojini Naidu, Hansa Mehta)
## 2. Numbers
- First sitting: 9 December 1946; League boycotted; 211 members attended
- Strength: League members from Indian territory withdrew → fell from 389 to 299
- ⚠ 211 attended first sitting; 389 → 299 after partition

### Acts, status and criticisms
## 1. Status - Indian Independence Act 1947
- Indian Independence Act 1947: Made it a fully sovereign body with two roles
- Constitution-maker (chaired by Prasad)
- Dominion Legislature = first Parliament (chaired by Mavalankar)
- Continued as the Provisional Parliament till 1952
## 2. Criticisms
- Dissolution demand: Dissolution and re-election on adult suffrage demanded by the Socialist Party (RPSC 2024)
- Main charges:
- Not elected on adult franchise - indirect election under the 1935 Act's restricted franchise (tax, property, education)
- Congress dominance ("Congress Assembly Party")

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:253
### Origin of the idea
- ★ Sardar Patel — "Can you show me one free country where there are separate electorates? ... The British element is gone, but they have left mischief behind"
- M.N. Roy — first put forward the idea of a Constituent Assembly, 1934
- Some sources credit Tilak (1895)
- INC made it an official demand — 1935
- Accepted in principle — August Offer, 1940
### Composition
- Provincial members elected by proportional representation by single transferable vote; seats roughly by population
- Gandhi and Jinnah — not members
### Numbers
- First sitting 9 December 1946 — 211 members; Muslim League boycotted
- Strength fell 389 → 299 — exit of League members from areas that went to Pakistan
### Posts
| Post | Person |
| Constitutional Adviser | B.N. Rau |
| Vice-President | H.C. Mukherjee |
| Drafting Committee chairman | B.R. Ambedkar |
### Andhra members
- Nehru accepted the Andhra demand in the Assembly — 27 November 1947
- Andhra dropped from the Draft Constitution — 21 February 1948; Dhar Commission named 17 June 1948
- Durgabai Deshmukh — founded the Andhra Mahila Sabha
- Pattabhi Sitaramayya — chaired the House Committee; Ranga — peasant leader
### Status & criticism
- After the Independence Act — a fully sovereign body
- Rajendra Prasad chaired it as constitution-making body; Mavalankar as Dominion Legislature
- Socialist Party — demanded its dissolution and re-election by adult suffrage
### National symbols
- National Flag adopted — 22 July 1947
- Pingali Venkayya — born 1876, Bhatlapenumarru; 'Jhanda Venkayya'
- Showed his 1921 tricolour to Gandhi at the Bezwada AICC session; Gandhi added the charkha, later replaced by the Ashoka Chakra
- Vande Mataram: composed 1875 → in Anandamath 1882 → first sung by Tagore, INC 1896
- Jana Gana Mana first sung — Calcutta INC session, 27 December 1911
### Exam corner
- Patel's "The British element is gone, but they have left mischief behind" — about separate electorates
### Acts, status and criticisms
- ★ Idea of a constitution first seen in the Swaraj Bill (1895); officially stated at the Faizpur Congress session, 1936
- ★ Socialist Party National Executive (1946) — demanded dissolution and re-election by universal adult suffrage
- Leaders: Jayaprakash Narayan, Ram Manohar Lohia
### National symbols → adopted, composer, designer
- ★ Pingali Venkayya (Andhra Pradesh) — designer of the National Flag; adopted with the Ashoka Chakra in 1947
- ★ Venkayya attended the 1906 AICC session under Dadabhai Naoroji, which inspired his flag design
- ★ Jana Gana Mana — by Rabindranath Tagore (Bengali, first of five stanzas of a Brahmo hymn); first sung at the 1911 Calcutta INC session (27 December)
