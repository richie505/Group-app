### AP tops Anaemia Mukt Bharat Q1 2025-26 review
## 1. News
- Review: Union Health Ministry first-quarter review (April–June 2025, FY 2025–26) — Andhra Pradesh ranked 1st in anaemia prevention
- Rank 2 / 3: Haryana, Telangana
- Improvement: 88% increase in anaemia prevention index; AP was 3rd in 2024–25 and 2nd in Q1 last year
- Categories: AP topped 5 of 6 categories — exception adolescents (10–19 yrs) where AP was 3rd after Haryana and Telangana
| Category | AP coverage / distribution |
| Children 5–9 years | 95%; IFA tablets to 30,35,641 |
| Pregnant women | Highest coverage; 2,69,952 |
| Lactating mothers | Highest coverage; 1,63,177 |
## 2. Background — Anaemia Mukt Bharat
- Launched: 2018 under National Health Mission (NHM)
- Target: 3% reduction per year in anaemia prevalence
- Funding: 60:40 Centre–State (90:10 for NE/special category states)
- Dosage: children 6–59 months 1 ml IFA syrup twice a week; 5–9 yrs and 10–19 yrs 4 IFA tablets per month; pregnant & lactating women 180 IFA tablets free
- ⚠ Not fully centrally funded — 60:40 sharing
- SDGs: SDG 2 (Zero Hunger), SDG 3 (Good Health)

### AP Minor Mineral Policy 2025 approved
## 1. Why
- Mining share in GSDP: 3.53% (2018–19) → 2.71% (2023–24); 2022 auction-only rule stalled applications
## 2. Key reforms
| Reform | Detail |
| Pending applications | over 6,000 revived — first-come first-served; LOI within 1 year |
| Dual grant | application-based for minor minerals (building material, granite); auction-based for industrial minerals |
| Lease premium | cut from 10x to 5x dead rent; limestone slabs 3x |
| Lease period | granite and industrial minerals 20 → 30 years; road metal (captive) 15 → 30 years |
| 'Consideration Amount' | abolished (introduced during COVID) |
| OTS scheme | rationalised penalties; closure of abandoned leases |
### G.O. Ms. No. 75 — fund utilisation rules for urban local bodies

- Issued by: S. Suresh Kumar, Principal Secretary, MA&UD — April 2025

- Priorities: sanitation, leak-proof water, pothole-free roads, drainage, streetlights
- Monitoring: ERP dashboard; fortnightly (Directorate) and monthly (State) reviews
- Goal: 15%+ GSDP growth; Swarna Andhra@2047; 3Fs — Funds, Functions, Functionaries
