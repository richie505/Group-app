### Policies → vision, objectives and targets (NPIT 2012, NPE 2019) — NPIT 2012 - vision and objectives
## 1. NPIT 2012 - vision and objectives
- NPIT 2012: ICT as a driver of growth, jobs, governance reform and inclusion
- Twin goals:
- India as Global Hub and Destination for IT and ITES Services by 2020
- Context (2011-12):
- Indian IT = USD 100 billion industry; 80% of revenue from exports
- Six hubs (Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Pune, NCR) had ~90% of industry, near saturation → push into Tier II and Tier III cities
- Objectives: Strengthen global IT position; generate jobs; ICT in education, health, skills, financial inclusion and governance; e-literacy for all
### Policies → vision, objectives and targets (NPIT 2012, NPE 2019) — NPIT 2012 targets
## 1. NPIT 2012 targets
| Target (by 2020) | From → To |
| IT-ITES revenue | $100 bn (2012) → $300 bn |
| IT exports | $69 bn → $200 bn |
| Skilled manpower | 10 million additional skilled ICT workers |
| e-literacy | At least one e-literate person in every household |
- Exam angle: match target figure to category; "$300 bn by 2020" is the most-asked figure.
## 2. NPE 2019 targets
- NPE 2019 aim: India as global hub for ESDM (Electronics System Design and Manufacturing), including chipsets
| NPE 2019 target (by 2025) | Value |
| Electronics manufacturing | US$ 400 bn (~₹26 lakh crore) |
| Mobile handsets | 1 billion a year, of which 600 million for export |
| Mobile manufacturing value | US$ 190 bn; mobile exports US$ 110 bn |
- ⚠ NPIT 2012 targets are by 2020 ($300 bn); NPE 2019 targets are by 2025 (US$ 400 bn)
### Schemes → outlay and support (PLI, SPECS, MPMS, ISM, C2S)
## 1. Electronics schemes
- Schemes under NPE (2020):
- PLI for large-scale electronics manufacturing
- SPECS (Scheme for Promotion of Manufacturing of Electronic Components and Semiconductors)
- Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme (MPMS): ₹62,500 crore, FY 2026-27 to 2030-31; replaces PLI (Large Scale Electronics) (24 Aug 2026)
- Targets: ₹39 lakh crore production, ₹15 lakh crore exports
## 2. India Semiconductor Mission - Semicon 2.0 pillars
| Semicon 2.0 pillar | Support |
| Fabs | Up to 40% capital subsidy (CMOS silicon); 35% (compound, display) |
| Packaging (OSAT/ATMP) | Up to 35% (advanced); 25% (conventional) |
| R&D | Up to 75% of project cost |
| Talent | 1,00,000 more engineers and technicians |
- Other two pillars: Design + Machines/Materials
## 3. Chips to Start-up (C2S)
- Chips to Start-up (C2S) (2022): chip-design talent
- Target: 85,000 B.Tech/M.Tech/PhD over 5 years
- Implemented by C-DAC

### Outcomes data — IT exports, digital economy, electronics output
- IT exports: India = one of the world's largest IT-service exporters (TCS, Infosys, Wipro)
- Digital economy: 12-14% of GDP; projected 20% by 2029-30
- Employment: IT + e-commerce employed 14.67 million (2022-23)
- Electronics production: ₹13.11 lakh crore in 2025-26 (sevenfold under PLI) (15 Aug 2026)
- Electronics = India's 3rd-largest export item
- Global Capability Centres (GCCs): offshore units run by multinationals for their own engineering, R&D, analytics (not outsourcing vendors)
- Not in your sources: latest IT-BPM revenue and IT-sector share of GDP (NASSCOM/ES).
