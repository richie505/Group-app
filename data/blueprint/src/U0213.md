# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:212 - Revolutionary literature in Telugu - Sri Sri, Abhyudaya movement
### Writers' bodies
- Telugu poetry turned from the nationalist to the class struggle
- 1936 — AIPWA, Lucknow, presided by Premchand
- 1943 — Arasam (Abhyudaya Rachayitala Sangham): AIPWA's Telugu counterpart
- July 1970 — Virasam (Viplava Rachayitala Sangham), Hyderabad: Sri Sri Founding President
- 1971 — Jana Natya Mandali: from Gaddar's Art Lovers (1970)
### Path of Telugu poetry
- 1. Nationalist poetry ↓
- 2. Abhyudaya (1930s-40s, Arasam) ↓
- 3. Sri Sri's Shashtipurti, Vizag (Feb 1970) ↓
- 4. Viplava (1970, Virasam, after Srikakulam)
### Sri Sri
- Person
- Srirangam Srinivasa Rao
- Born 2 Jan 1910, Vizag
- Mahakavi; Marx, Hegel
- Sahiti Samiti with Puripanda Appalaswamy
- Mahaprasthanam
- Written 1933-47, published 1950
- First poem *Jayabheri* (1933)
- Preface by Chalam
- Founding text of Abhyudaya kavitvam
- Other works
- *Khadga Srushti*: armed change
- *Maro Prasthanam*: post-1970
- *Siprali*; *Anantham* (autobiography)
- Firsts
- National Award lyricist: "Telugu Veera Levara" (1974)

## 1. AIPWA and Arasam
- AIPWA role: Through the AIPWA and the AISF, communists spread anti-British revolutionary ideas
- Arasam role: Organised the Abhyudaya current started by Sri Sri's poems
- Not in your sources: Arasam's founding venue and first president.
## 2. Praja Natya Mandali
- Nature: Communist cultural front; spread ideas through drama, songs and folk forms
- Main folk form: Burrakatha
- Not in your sources: the founding year and founder of Praja Natya Mandali.
## 3. Virasam (1970) - the Viplava phase
- Meaning: the Revolutionary Writers' Association
- Spark: his Shashtipurti in Vizag (Feb 1970)
- ⚠ Trap: Calling Virasam the "Progressive Writers' Association" is loose - Arasam is the progressive body
- Virasam collections: Jhamjha (1970), Le, Viplavam Vardhillali
- Banned: Le and March
### Sri Sri → titles, awards, FIRSTs, works
## 1. Person
- Born: 2 Jan 1910, Vizag - Sri Sri (Srirangam Srinivasa Rao)
- Title: Mahakavi; influenced by Marx and Hegel
- Sahiti Samiti (Vizag): Co-founded with Puripanda Appalaswamy
- Award: National Award for best lyricist for "Telugu Veera Levara" (Alluri Seetharama Raju)
- Dated 1974; called the first National Award for a Telugu lyricist
## 2. Works → note
| Work | Note |
| *Mahaprasthanam* | about 41 poems written 1933-47, published 1950. First poem *Jayabheri* (1933). Dedicated to Kompella Janardhana Rao, with a preface by Chalam. Founding text of Abhyudaya kavitvam |
| *Khadga Srushti* | the necessity of radical, armed change |
| *Maro Prasthanam* | his revolutionary (post-1970) phase |
| *Siprali* | Sirisirimuvva satakam plus limericks. His other works include *Anantham* (autobiography) |

| Tripuraneni Gopichand | *Asamarthuni Jeevayatra* (psychological novel dedicated to his father; G2 2017 key), *Pandita Parameswara Sastri Veelunama* (Sahitya Akademi). First Director of Information of Andhra State (1953) |

### Universal angles
- Significance: Path from Abhyudaya (1930s-40s, Arasam) → Viplava (1970, Virasam, after Srikakulam); Telugu poetry turned from the nationalist to the class struggle
- Criticism & debates: Traditionalists said Sri Sri's free verse destroyed the "sweetness" of Telugu; supporters credit him with making it modern
- Current relevance: CM Naidu cited Sri Sri among the greats of Telugu literature at the World Telugu Mahasabhalu (6 Jan 2026)
