### Cropping seasons — kharif, rabi, zaid: months and crops
| Season | Sowing | Harvest | Major crops |
| Kharif | June-July (SW monsoon onset) | Sep-Oct; some notes say Oct-Nov | Rice, maize, jowar, bajra, cotton, groundnut, soybean, jute, sugarcane |
| Rabi | Oct-Dec (after monsoon withdrawal) | March-April (Mar-Apr); some notes say Apr-May | Wheat, barley, gram, mustard, peas, linseed |
| Zaid | March-June (short summer) | June-July | Watermelon, muskmelon, cucumber, vegetables, fodder |

- Zaid Rabi: Feb-Mar sowing
- Rabi water: Winter rain from western disturbances + irrigation
### Crop classification — food vs cash crops, both-season crops
## 1. Food and cash crops
- Food crops: Cereals, millets, pulses
- Cash/commercial crops: Cotton, jute, sugarcane, tobacco, oilseeds, plantation crops
| Group | Crops |
| Foodgrains = cereals + pulses | Rice, wheat, coarse cereals/millets (Shree Anna), pulses |
| Oilseeds | Groundnut, mustard, soybean, sunflower, sesame |
| Commercial / cash | Cotton, jute, sugarcane, tobacco |
- AP traditional pattern:
- Food crops: paddy, jowar, bajra, ragi, pulses
- Traditional commercial crops: groundnut, cotton, tobacco, sugarcane
## 2. Both-season crops
- Both seasons in AP: Paddy, maize, jowar, bajra and pulses are grown in both kharif and rabi
- Pulses: Pulses - tur/arhar (kharif), gram/chana (rabi)
- Rice: Rice in irrigated deltas - AP 2024-25 paddy area 14.04 lakh ha kharif + 6.93 lakh ha rabi (2nd AE)
- Millets: Mainly kharif; some grown in the southern rabi
- ⚠ Tur/arhar = kharif pulse; gram/chana = rabi pulse
### Cropping pattern trends — India
- Long-run shift: Food crops → commercial and high-value crops; drivers = irrigation, prices, technology (UPSC Economy p.189)
- Rice: Largest cropped area in India at about 51-52 Mha
- Pulses and oilseeds: Stagnant yields
- Rice-wheat expansion (2015-2025): Punjab, Telangana, Madhya Pradesh - assured MSP procurement
- Horticulture vs foodgrains (2024-25): Horticulture 367.72 MT overtook foodgrains 357.73 MT
- Horticulture = 33% of agricultural GVA
- Rainfed pulses and millets:
- Irrigation covers ~67% of rice area but only ~26% of pulses and <15% of millets
- ~51-52% of net sown area is rainfed
- Policy direction: Shift ~20% of rice area to pulses/legumes → cut urea use + save groundwater
