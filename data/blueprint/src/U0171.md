## 1. Nizam of Hyderabad — alliances and Ceded Districts (Rayalaseema)
- First Mysore War: Nizam + Marathas + British allied against Hyder

### Paramountcy — grounds for intervention; Rajputana alliances
- Lord Hastings: turned subsidiary alliances into paramountcy; imposed humiliating treaties on the Peshwa and Scindia
- Rajputana (1818): Udaipur, Jaipur, Jodhpur and others accepted subordinate alliances = British protection in exchange for giving up external relations
- Status of Indian states: ran own internal administration but had "no international life"
- Grounds for intervention:
- To give effect to their international commitments (APPSC-G2 2017 key)
- Misgovernance (Coorg, Awadh)
### Universal angles
- Significance:
- Lapse annexations of 1848-54 + Awadh (1856) = princely grievances that fed the Revolt of 1857
- These wars removed the last strong Indian powers; after 1818 British paramountcy unchallenged
- Comparative note: keep the four grounds apart
- Conquest (Sindh, Punjab)
- vs Lapse (Satara to Nagpur)
- vs Misgovernance (Awadh, Coorg)
- vs Abolition of titles (Carnatic, Tanjore)
- AP angle:
- Mysore wars + subsidiary alliance → Rayalaseema to the Company in 1800
- Carnatic Nawab's lands in Nellore and Chittoor annexed in 1801 on a charge of collusion with Tipu - an early "pretext" annexation (p.368)
- Wellesley merged the Andhra region into the Madras Presidency

### Anglo-Maratha Wars (1775-1818)
## First War (1775-82) — Warren Hastings
- Warren Hastings — first Governor-General of Bengal, 1774-1785 (1772-1785 in another key); tenure covered the Rohilla War (1774), First Anglo-Maratha War, Second Anglo-Mysore War
- Treaty order: Surat (1775) → Purandar (1776) → Convention of Wadgaon (1779) → Salbai (1782)

- Salbai (1782) — British-Maratha peace; old position restored, British kept Salsette, about twenty years of peace
- Other years: Madras 1769, Allahabad 1765, Bassein 1802
- ★ British lost the first Anglo-Maratha war badly; Marathas fought the British hardest, put down after three wars

- Trigger — Holkar defeated Peshwa Baji Rao II at Poona (1802) → Baji Rao fled and signed the Treaty of Bassein
- Bassein — Peshwa gave up his chauth claims on the Nizam (did not gain new ones)
- GG — Lord Wellesley
## Third War (1817-18) — Lord Hastings
