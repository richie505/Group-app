### Nuclear weapon technology — types and terms
## 1. Types
| Fission (atomic) weapon | Fusion (thermonuclear / hydrogen) weapon |
| A heavy nucleus (U-235 / Pu-239) splits in a neutron chain reaction | Light nuclei (hydrogen isotopes) fuse |
| Example: Pokhran-I | A fission "primary" supplies the heat to trigger fusion; tested in Pokhran-II |
- U-235 fission: Neutron hit → two smaller nuclei (e.g. barium + krypton) + 2-3 neutrons → sustain chain reaction
## 2. Terms → meaning
- Trigger: Krytron = high-speed switch firing a nuclear bomb's detonators
- ⚠ Trap: NOT a MOSFET, solid-state relay or solenoid
## 3. Enrichment levels (data)
| Grade | U-235 share |
| Natural uranium | ~0.7% U-235 |
| Reactor-grade LEU | 3-5% |
| HEU | 20%+ |
| Weapons-grade | ~90% |
