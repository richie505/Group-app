### Sufism — meaning, origin, FIRSTs and Sufis vs Ulema
## 1. Meaning and origin
- Origin of 'Sufi': From Suf (coarse wool), Safa (purity) or Greek Sophia
- Beliefs: Accepted the Quran and the Prophet; absorbed Vedanta, Yoga, Neo-Platonism
## 2. FIRSTs
| FIRST | FACT |
| First prominent Sufi in India | Ali Hujwiri (Data Ganj Bakhsh) - author of Kashf-ul-Mahjub |
| Most popular order | Chishti |
## 3. Sufis vs Ulema
- Ulema contrast:
- Sufis = inner experience over ritual; freely used music, which orthodox Islam did not generally sanction
## 4. Ba-shara vs be-shara orders
- Ba-shara: Followed Islamic law (Sharia) - Chishti, Suhrawardi, Qadiri, Naqshbandi, Firdausi
- Be-shara: Not bound by it - wandering Qalandars, Madaris
### Sufi terms → meaning
- Sufism: Islamic mysticism — an ascetic and mystical tradition
| Term | Meaning |
| Khanqah | A residence of Sufi saints; Sufi hospice where disciples lived and trained; it also gave food and shelter to the poor (training and service) |
| Sama | A musical and spiritual assembly; devotional music meant to produce ecstasy; raqs = ritual dance ('Majlis-i-Sama' of Chishtis and Suhrawardis) |
| Auliya | Saints — friends of God |
| Pir / Murshid | Spiritual master / guide; the pir-murid bond (preceptorship) is called Piri-Muridi |
| Murid | Disciple pledged in absolute submission to the pir |
| Wali | Friend of God, a saint [GK] |
| Dargah | Shrine at a saint's tomb [GK] |
| Urs | Death anniversary of a saint, seen as the soul's union with God [GK] |
| Fana / Baqa | Annihilation of the self (the ego) in God / subsistence in God |
| Ba-shara / Be-shara | Sufis who follow the Shariat / Sufis outside it (e.g. qalandars) [GK] |
| Tariqa | Spiritual path through stages; also means an order (Silsila / Tariqa = order / path) |
| Malfuzat | Recorded conversations |
| Maktubat | Letters |
| Tazkiras | Biographies of saints |
- ⚠ malfuzat = conversations; maktubat = letters; tazkiras = biographies
### Doctrines, stages and practices — Wahdat-ul-Wujud, maqamat, yogic borrowings
## 1. Doctrines
| Doctrine | Meaning | Associated with |
| Wahdat-ul-Wujud | "Unity of Being": Creator (Haq) and creation (Khalq) are one | Ibn Arabi [GK]; followed by the Qadiris and Chishtis |
| Wahdat-ush-Shuhud | "Unity of Appearance/Witness": creation only reflects God | Shaikh Ahmad Sirhindi (Naqshbandi) - his counter-doctrine |
- ⚠ Wujud = Being (Ibn Arabi, Qadiri, Chishti); Shuhud = Appearance (Sirhindi, Naqshbandi)
## 2. Stages
- 10 stages (maqamat): From Tauba (repentance) → Wara, Zuhd, Faqr, Sabr, Shukr, Khauf, Raja, Tawakkul → Riza (submission)
## 3. Practices and yogic borrowings
- Practices: zikr, meditation, fasting, asceticism, sama, solitude or retreat (chilla)
- Amritakund: Hatha-yogic text translated into Arabic and Persian in the 13th c.
- Afterwards breath control vital in almost every order except the Naqshbandis
- Alberuni: Noted similarities between Sufi ideas and Patanjali's Yoga Sutra + the Gita

- ★ Muinuddin Chishti — held devotional music (sama) a way to come close to God
## Other orders and saints
- ★ Shaikh Bahauddin Zakaria — started the Suhrawardi order in India
- ★ also of this order: Sadr-ud-din Arif, Rakn-ud-din Abul Fateh, Jalaluddin Surkh
- ★ Shah Muhammad Ghaus (Shattari) — saw Krishna as an Auliya
- ★ close to Humayun and Tansen; wrote Jawahir-e-Khamsa; translated Amritkund
- ★ Regions: Chishti — Delhi and around; Suhrawardi — Sindh; Firdausi — Bihar (a part of the Chishti order)
- ★ Auliya — not an order but a title given to Nizamuddin
- ★ Nizamuddin Auliya — Chishti, Mahboob-i-Ilahi; born Badayun; lived with Baba Farid at Ajodhan, then Delhi — not Multan
### Silsilas → arrival, regions, PYQs and traps
- ★ Suhrawardi — limited mostly to Punjab, Multan and Sindh; founded in India by Baha-ud-din Zakariya
- ★ Shaikh Abdul Jilani — not of the Chishti order
- ★ Chishti: Muinuddin Chishti, Qutbuddin Bakhtiyar Kaki (Delhi), Nizamuddin Auliya
### Chishti saints → dargahs and dates
- ★ Haji Waris Ali Shah — yearly fair at his tomb at Dewa Sharif (Barabanki, UP)
- ★ Akbar — often visited the tomb of Khwaja Muinuddin Chishti at Ajmer
- ★ Nasiruddin Mahmud — Chirag-i-Delhi (Chirag-i-Dehlavi); disciple of Nizamuddin Auliya
- ★ Fariduddin Ganj-i-Shakar (Baba Farid) — compositions in the Adi Granth / Guru Granth Sahib
- ★ born at Kothewal (Multan district); disciples incl. Alauddin Sabir Kaliyari
- ★ Nizamuddin Auliya — disciple of Baba Farid; saw the reign of seven (more) Sultans but never visited their court
- ★ titles Mehboob-i-Ilahi (Favourite of God) and Sultan-ul-Mashaikh (King of Saints)
### Sufi saints → titles (called as / known as)
- ★ Shaikh Nizamuddin Auliya — Mahboob-i-Ilahi; saw more than seven Sultans, never visited their court
