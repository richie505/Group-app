# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 2:20 - Recent amendments - 97th, 100th, 101st (GST), 102nd, 103rd (EWS), 104th, 105th, 106th (women's reservation)
### Recent amendments in order
- The 106th (2023) is the latest enacted amendment - not the 105th
- 2011 — 97th: co-operative societies
- 2014 — 99th: NJAC - struck down 2015 (judicial independence)
- 2015 — 100th: Bangladesh Land Boundary Agreement, 111 Indian / 51 Bangladeshi enclaves
- 2019 — 103rd: 10% EWS reservation
- 2019 — 104th: SC/ST seats to 2030; Anglo-Indian nomination ended (January 2020)
- 2021 — 105th: States/UTs again identify own SEBCs
- 2023 — 106th: one-third seats for women (Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam)
### 97th Amendment (2011)
| Provision | Content |
| Art. 19(1)(c) | Forming co-operatives = Fundamental Right |
| Art. 43B | New DPSP: voluntary, autonomous, democratic co-ops |
| Part IXB | 243ZH-243ZT; 243ZJ: max 21 directors |
| Board | One SC/ST + two women; 5-year term |
| Audit | Within six months of financial year end |
### Rajendra N. Shah (2021)
- SC struck Part IXB for State co-operatives - no State ratification
### Pending / failed Bills
- 129th Bill, 2024
- Amends 83, 172, 327
- 130th Bill, 2025
- Remove PM, CMs, Ministers detained 30 consecutive days
- Amends Arts 75, 164, 239AA
- 131st Bill, 2026
- Lok Sabha 850; delimitation
### Women's quota & delimitation
- 106th quota still unimplemented; tied to delimitation
- 84th: freeze on 1971 Census to 2026; now tied to 2027 Census
- Debates: no BC sub-quota; southern States fear smaller share
- Local bodies: one-third for women since 1992 (Arts 243D, 243T)
### Exam corner

- Trap: 105th is NOT the latest - 106th (2023) is
- 129th = Art. 82A; 130th = 30 days detention; 131st = 850 (defeated)
- 97th: max 21 directors; struck for State co-ops (2021)

- Board of maximum 21 directors
- One SC/ST + two women seats on the board
- Audit within six months of close of financial year
## Case and data
- Rajendra N. Shah (2021): SC struck down Part IXB for State co-operatives (want of State ratification); survives for multi-State co-operatives

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 2:20
### Recent amendments in order
- ★ 97th Amendment (notified 12 January 2012) — co-operative societies; added 'Cooperative Societies' in Art. 19(1)(c), Art. 43B, Part IX-B (Arts. 243ZH–243ZT)
- 99th (2014) — NJAC; struck down 2015
- 100th — India-Bangladesh Land Boundary
- 104th (2019) — SC/ST seat reservation (Art. 334) extended to 2030; ended Anglo-Indian nomination
- Women's reservation (2023 law) — implementation linked to delimitation
### 97th Amendment (2011)
- Latest enacted amendment: 106th (2023) (105th of 2021 is earlier)
- Union of India v. Rajendra N. Shah (2021) — Part IXB (Arts. 243ZH–243ZT) struck down for State co-operatives; no State ratification
- Article 43B (DPSP) — voluntary formation, autonomous functioning, democratic control, professional management of co-operatives
- Co-operative board: five-year term; max 21 directors; one SC/ST seat; two women seats; audit within six months
### Rajendra N. Shah (2021)
- Part IXB not valid for State co-operatives — needed ratification by the States missing
- Still applies to multi-State co-operative societies
### Pending / failed Bills
- 129th Amendment Bill, 2024 — simultaneous elections; adds Article 82A; changes Arts. 83, 172, 327
- 130th Amendment Bill, 2025 — removal of PM, CMs and Ministers detained 30 consecutive days on serious charges; Arts. 75, 164, 239AA (incl. Delhi)
### Women's quota & delimitation
- 106th Amendment women's quota — still unimplemented
- Articles 243D and 243T — one-third seats for women in Panchayats and Municipalities, since 1992
