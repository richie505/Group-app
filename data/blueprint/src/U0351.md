# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 2:77
### NITI bodies
- Governing Council — PM, all State CMs, CMs of UTs with legislatures, LGs of other UTs, plus special invitees
- Regional Councils — specific regional issues for a fixed period; chaired by the PM or his nominee; members: CMs and LGs of the region
- SETUs (State Economic and Transformation Units, State Support Mission) — help States prepare State Vision@2047 plans
- Team India Hub — leads State–Centre engagement
- Atal Innovation Mission — innovation and entrepreneurship (Atal Tinkering Labs, incubators)
### Aspirational districts vs blocks
- Aspirational Districts Programme — run by NITI Aayog; launched January 2018; 112 districts, 49 indicators, five themes
- Five themes: Health and Nutrition, Education, Agriculture and Water Resources, Financial Inclusion and Skill Development, Basic Infrastructure
- Urban Transport and Housing — not a theme
- Aspirational Blocks Programme — January 2023; 513 blocks in 329 districts, 27 States and 4 UTs
### ADP strategy: 3 Cs
- ★ Convergence — good use of funds and services of different departments
- ADP strategy: Convergence, Collaboration and Competition
### ADP theme weights
- Agriculture and Water Resources — 20 percent
- Financial Inclusion and Skill Development — 10 percent (not 20)
### Indices & reports
| Index / report | Measures |
| SDG India Index | States'/UTs' progress towards SDGs |
| Export Preparedness Index | States' export readiness |
| India Electric Mobility Index | e-mobility across States |
| GROW report | Greening and Restoration of Wasteland with Agroforestry |
| School Education Quality Index | school education |
- Export Preparedness Index 2024 (released January 2026) — AP fifth among large States
- SDG India Index, 4th edition (2023-24, released July 2024) — 113 indicators; AP scored 74, "Front Runner"
- NITI Aayog (earlier Planning Commission) — nodal agency for poverty estimation; publishes the National MPI

- Original AP aspirational districts — Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam, YSR Kadapa
