- Empire not founded by Parantaka I or Rajendra I

- Decline:

- Chalukya-Cholas declined with rise of the Pandyas
- Last ruler Rajendra III gone by 1279

### Srivijaya expedition and the "Chola Lake"
- Srivijaya expedition (1025 CE): By Rajendra I against revived Srivijaya empire (Malay Peninsula, Sumatra, Java)
- Defeated the Sailendra rulers/king (APPSC 2000, 2003); took Kadaram (Kedah)
- Cause: Chola-Sailendra ties, once cordial, soured
- Srivijaya blocked Indian traders on the China route; Cholas cleared obstacles + widened trade with China
- Rajaraja I had earlier sent a naval expedition against the Sailendras
- K.A. Nilakanta Sastri: Cholas turned the Bay of Bengal into a "Chola Lake"
- Maritime reach carried Dravidian art to South-East Asia (Angkor Wat, Borobudur)
- Exam angle: "Who led the campaign against Srivijaya?" - Rajendra I (asked by UPSC 2025 and the APPSC Group-II papers).

### Kulottunga I → parentage, FIRSTs and achievements
- Birth & parents: Born Rajendra Chalukya; son of Rajaraja Narendra + Ammanga Devi
- Succession:
- 1070: Ousted from Vengi by uncle Vijayaditya VII
- 1070-71: Virarajendra and Adhirajendra died → took Chola throne as Kulottunga I
- 1075: After Vijayaditya VII's death, merged Vengi → ruled both = "Chalukya-Chola"
- First king of the Chalukya-Chola line (APPSC G2 2011)
- Achievements:
- Sent 72 merchants to China (UPPSC 1992); a 72-member merchant mission
- Conquered Kalinga (Jayamkondar's Kalingattuparani)
- Built Kulottunga Cholapuram (Visakhapatnam)
- Title Sungam Tavirtta Chola (abolished tolls)
