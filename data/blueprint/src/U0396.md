### Exam corner
- Suicide — attempt punishable (Section 309 IPC), but completed act cannot be punished (person is dead)
- Murder, dacoity, kidnapping — punishable whether attempted or completed
### Articles, Acts and cases → provision
- ★ Suicide — crime punishable when attempted, but the doer cannot be punished when it is done; attempt punishable under Section 309 IPC
- Law Commission of India, 210th report — recommended deleting it
