# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 3:49 - Green Revolution - components, achievements and criticism; White, Yellow, Blue and Rainbow revolutions
### GR package & HYV traits
- Wheat gained the most; Punjab the pioneer - but groundwater, soil and equity paid the price
- Package: HYV seeds, assured irrigation, chemical fertilisers + pesticides, mechanisation, institutional credit, MSP / assured procurement
- Mature quicker → double cropping
- First HYV introduction: 1966–67; first crop: wheat; pioneer state: Punjab
- First Agri University: Pantnagar (1960); first KVK: Puducherry (1974)

### Green Revolution outcomes — gains, disparities and ecological costs
## 1. Gains
- Crops: Wheat gained the most; rice gained in a wider, later phase
- Regions: Punjab, Haryana and western UP; rice gains in AP and TN deltas
- Self-sufficiency: India became self-sufficient, stopped relying on PL-480 imports
- Foodgrains: about 70-75 MT (mid-1960s) → record 357.73 MT in 2024-25
## 2. Costs
| Factor | Effect |
| Disparity | Regional + interpersonal; farmers with more than 10 ha benefited most |
| Groundwater depletion | Punjab and Haryana extract 35-57% more than recharge |
| Soil and ecology | Salinity, loss of biodiversity, skewed N:P:K of 10.9:4.1:1 |
- Exam angle: assertion-reason questions linking HYV + fertiliser + irrigation to both higher output and ecological damage.
### Universal angles
- Current relevance:
- India's first genome-edited rice, Pusa Rice DST1 (from AP's MTU1010), due for commercial launch
- White Revolution 2.0 under way
- Criticism & debates: GR's water-intensive rice-wheat monoculture vs need for crop diversification
- Johl Committee: Shift 20% of paddy area
