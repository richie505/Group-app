### Terms of reference and findings → why Tamil Nadu set it up
- Task:
- Study Centre-State relations (APPSC 2018 key)
- Findings:
- Despite the federal form, the Centre had become dominant → states turned into "administrative units of the Centre"
- Arts 256, 257 and 365 enabled this overreach
### Rajamannar recommendations → institutions and Articles
| Subject | Rajamannar recommended |
| Inter-State Council | Set up a permanent ISC under Art 263 immediately (APP; LENS Apr 2025) |
| Article 356 | Delete it entirely (APP) - complete elimination → with loss of Arts 256, 257 and 365, would end the Centre's override |
| All-India Services | Abolish IAS, IPS and IFS (APP) - to cut central administrative control over state governments |
| Planning Commission | Abolish it; it was an extra-constitutional body that alone decided grants to states (APP; LENS) |
| Finance Commission | Revitalise it so states depend less on the Centre (LENS Apr 2025); widen revenue devolution (APP) |
- Not recommended: "strengthening the Governor system" (APPSC-G2 2017 key); did not seek a stronger Governor (APPSC 2017 key)
- Rajamannar wanted Central control reduced - Centre's control over states reduced
- Ending IAS and IPS: the Rajamannar Commission (UPSC 1993 key)
- ⚠ The Dhebar, Kalelkar and Kher commissions did not
- Exam angle: "Which is NOT a Rajamannar recommendation" - look for any option that strengthens the Centre or the Governor.
### Seventh Schedule → transfers to State List and residuary powers
- Transfers to State List: from the Union and Concurrent Lists to the State List
- Examples: stock exchanges, futures markets, oil fields (regulation and development), trade unions, industrial disputes, inland waterway navigation, factories, electricity, publishing
- End the Centre's control over education and health; move more Concurrent subjects to the State List
- Residuary powers: to go to the states
- ⚠ Contrast Sarkaria: residuary powers other than taxation → Concurrent List
### Aftermath and commissions compared → year, appointing body
- Resolution: TN Assembly adopted a state autonomy resolution in April 1974, based on the report
- DMK slogan: "autonomy for the States, federalism at the Centre" continues today (2026)
- Centre's response: Centre ignored the report; no action taken
- Later outcome: Inter-State Council (1990) came through Sarkaria, not Rajamannar

- Memory chain: State, then Centre twice (1969, 1983, 2007), then a state again in 2025
### Universal angles
- Current relevance: Tamil Nadu's Kurian Joseph Committee (April 2025) consciously follows Rajamannar
- Part I report of 16 Feb 2026 tabled in the Assembly
- Significance: the first state-appointed inquiry; intellectual base for later autonomy demands (Anandpur Sahib Resolution, West Bengal memorandum)
- FIRST/LAST: the first committee on Centre-State relations appointed by a state
# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 2:55
### Committee at a glance
- Chairman Dr. P.V. Rajamannar — former Chief Justice of the Madras High Court
- Appointed 1969, reported 1971
- Members — A. Lakshmanaswami Mudaliar, P. Chandra Reddy
- First committee on Centre-State relations appointed by a State
### Task & findings
- Aim — reduce central control, incl. over education and health
- Found States turned into "administrative units of the Centre"
- Overreach Articles listed — 256, 257, 365 (not 280, 282)
### Recommendations
- Permanent Inter-State Council under Article 263 at once; renewed Finance Commission
- Article 356 — complete deletion
- Planning Commission — extra-constitutional, alone decided grants to States; abolish
- All-India Services — abolish, to cut central administrative control
- Transfer to State List — inland waterway navigation, industrial disputes, oil fields regulation (not foreign affairs)
- Residuary powers — Rajamannar: to the States; Sarkaria: non-tax residuary to the Concurrent List
- Inter-State Council (1990) — set up through Sarkaria's influence
### Seventh Schedule transfers
- End central control over education and health; move more Concurrent subjects to the State List
### Aftermath & successors
- Tamil Nadu Assembly State autonomy resolution — April 1974
- Union Government ignored the report
- Later: Sarkaria (1983), Punchhi (2007), Kurian Joseph Committee (April 2025)
### Exam corner
- P.V. Rajamannar also chaired the Fourth Finance Commission
### Rajamannar recommendations → institutions and Articles
- ★ "Municipal relations" — Centre's control of States in the planning process
- ★ Centre-State relations depend on constitutional provisions, conventions and practices, judicial interpretations, mechanisms for dialogue (all four)
- ★ Rajamannar recommended ending the IAS and IPS
