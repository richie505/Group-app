### Unnava Lakshmibayamma → Sarada Niketanam and movements
## 1. Visits during the national movement
- Bipin Chandra Pal: Visited the Andhra districts during the Vandemataram Movement — a guest of Ramadasu Naidu at Machilipatnam
- ⚠ Sources differ: other notes name Kompalle Hanumantha Rao as Pal's Machilipatnam host (APPSC 2008 key)
- Mahatma Gandhi: Visited Hyderabad on 9 March 1934 for Harijan uplift work
- Unnava Lakshminarayana: Visited the Palnadu area in July 1921 to study the Forest Satyagraha
## 3. Sarada Niketanam and movements
- Founders: Unnava Lakshminarayana with wife Unnava Lakshmibayamma (APPSC G2 2019 key: Unnava Lakshminarayana)
- 1902: The couple ran a widow asylum; she held the first widow remarriage there
- Year: Women's Gurukulam founded in 1922
- The 1902 widow asylum later merged into it
- Role: Sheltered widows and destitute girls, taught girls; a "hotbed" of the freedom movement
- NCM:
- Led Guntur hartals after her husband's arrest over the Palnadu forest (pullari) satyagraha
- Palnadu forest satyagraha saw 8 women arrested with 28 men
- Took personal responsibility for Guntur women's participation in all Congress movements
- Salt Satyagraha, 9 April 1930: Led women in Krishna and Guntur districts, with Rukmini Lakshmipathi
### Duvvuri Subbamma and Durgabai Deshmukh → roles and posts
## 1. Duvvuri Subbamma
- Institution: National school Sanatana Vidyalayam, Rajahmundry; attended the Vijayawada AICC in 1921
- Movements:
- CDM, April 1930: Among the first to make salt at Chollangi (East Godavari); arrested at Rajahmundry → Nellore jail
- QIM: Faced a lathi charge at Peddapuram
## 2. Durgabai Deshmukh - early role
- April 1921, Kakinada: During Gandhi's visit
- Collected and donated Rs 5,000
- Arranged his meeting with Devadasis and Muslim women
- Translated his speech
- Nehru episode: As a volunteer, stopped Jawaharlal Nehru at the gate (no pass)
- Placed at the Vijayawada AICC (1921), where she also translated Gandhi's Hindi speeches into Telugu; many accounts place it at the Kakinada Congress (1923)
- CDM: Prominent in the Salt Satyagraha in Madras city, with K. Nageswara Rao and T. Prakasam
## 3. Durgabai Deshmukh - later posts
- After independence:
- Member of the Constituent Assembly - one of its "founding mothers" (17 Apr 2026)
- First chairperson of the Central Social Welfare Board (1953)
- Padma Vibhushan (1975)
- Exam angle: APPSC 2023 asked which statements on her were NOT true; APPSC later withdrew the question for a discrepancy (she did receive the Paul G. Hoffman award and was called 'Iron Lady').
