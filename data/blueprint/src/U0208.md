### Komagata Maru and the Ghadar rising → date, place, outcome (1914-1915)
## 1. Komagata Maru
- Ship: Japanese ship chartered by Gurdit Singh (Punjabi businessman)
- Voyage: 376 Sikh/Muslim passengers from Hong Kong (via Shanghai, Moji, Yokohama) to Vancouver, Canada (1914)
- Denial: Canada's racist immigration laws (the "continuous journey" rule) → forced back to Calcutta
- Budge Budge (near Calcutta, Sept 1914): Police firing killed 19 passengers (Jun 2025; older notes say 22 dead in all)
- Impact: Radicalised immigrants + accelerated the Ghadar movement; fury in Punjab
## 2. 1915 uprising
- Ghadar Conspiracy (1915): Pan-India revolt in the British Indian Army planned for 21 February 1915 in Punjab
- Betrayed by informers, crushed; 45 leaders hanged under the Defence of India Act
- First Lahore Conspiracy Case: Kartar Singh Sarabha executed on 16 November 1915, aged 19, with Vishnu Ganesh Pingle (Jun 2025)
