- Nodal ministry — Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways (renamed 2020)

- Five pillars: port modernisation, port connectivity, port-led industrialisation, coastal community development, coastal shipping with inland water transport

- Sagarmala 2.0 (2025) — shipbuilding, ship repair, ship breaking and recycling
