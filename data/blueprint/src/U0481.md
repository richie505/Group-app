### Anti-poverty programmes
## Direct-attack programmes
- IRDP, NREP, RLEGP and JRY — direct-attack / target-group poverty programmes
- ★ MRTP is not an anti-poverty programme — MRTP Act (1969) controls monopolies and restrictive trade practices
- JRY (1989) — formed by merging NREP with RLEGP (rural landless employment)
- SGSY replaced IRDP's individual-beneficiary approach with an SHG (group) approach
## Antyodaya and self-employment
- ★ Antyodaya — first started in Rajasthan (1977, 1977-78), by the Rajasthan State Government
- means development (Udaya) of the people at the bottom: the poorest of the poor
- RUDSETI — set up at Ujire, Karnataka in 1982; trains rural youth for self-employment; model for present RSETIs
## Women's programmes
- Swalamban — training and employment of poor women; former NORAD scheme
- ★ Mahila Samridhi Yojana — 2 October 1993; micro-finance to uplift rural women
- ★ Mahila Swa-shakti Project = Women Self-Empowerment Plan ("Women Empire Plan – 1989" is a wrong match)
### Restructuring chain
- SGSY — launched 1 April 1999 by merging six programmes: IRDP, TRYSEM, DWCRA, SITRA, Ganga Kalyan Yojana, Million Wells Scheme
- Ganga Kalyan Yojana — bore-well and tube-well irrigation for small and marginal farmers (not urban employment guarantee)
- Sequence: SGSY → NRLM 'Aajeevika' → DAY-NRLM
- NRLM (Aajeevika) restructured 2010, launched 2011; renamed DAY-NRLM in 2016
### DAY-NRLM structure
- DAY-NRLM three tiers: SHG → Village Organisation → Cluster-Level Federation
### DAY-NRLM basics
## Earlier schemes
- IRDP — subsidy plus bank credit to lift rural families above the poverty line; all blocks from 2 October 1980
- Swayamsiddha (2001) — SHG-based, integrated empowerment of women
- Rashtriya Mahila Kosh (1993) — micro-credit; STEP — skills and employment
## DAY-NRLM features
- Centre-State funding: 60:40 general; 90:10 North-Eastern States incl. Sikkim; 100% for UTs
- Up to 30% of SHG members may be slightly above the poverty line
- beneficiaries identified by PIP, checked by the Gram Sabha
- Lakhpati Didi — SHG member earning ₹1 lakh or more a year
- SVEP (Start-up Village Entrepreneurship Programme, 2016) — non-farm enterprises for SHG households
## Andhra Pradesh links
- Indira Kranthi Patham (2004-05) — merger of Velugu (2000, World Bank-aided) and DWCRA; not Velugu's earlier name
- Unnati (SERP) — the poorest SC/ST SHG households
- Stree Nidhi — credit to SHG members in rural and urban areas
- AP's SERP/Velugu model — template for NRLM; AP leads in SHG-bank linkage
### DAY-NULM (urban)
- NULM (2013) replaced the urban SJSRY (1997); later renamed DAY-NULM
- SGSY (rural) → NRLM; IRDP → SGSY (1999); PMRY → PMEGP (2008)
- Ministry: originally Housing and Urban Poverty Alleviation, now Housing and Urban Affairs
- PAISA — Portal for Affordable Credit and Interest Subvention Access
- AP implementing agency: MEPMA — Mission for Elimination of Poverty in Municipal Areas
### SHG pioneers
- SHG-Bank Linkage Programme — pilot in 1992 by NABARD; world's largest microfinance programme
- NABARD role: refinances banks, trains and promotes SHGs; does not replace commercial banks as direct lenders
- MYRADA — pioneered bank linkage of SHGs in the mid-1980s (not the national programme)
- RBI — regulator; SIDBI — lends to small industries
### SHG movement in AP
- SERP — Society for Elimination of Rural Poverty; registered society of the AP Government, 2000
- SAPAP (1995) — UNDP-supported, in Mahabubnagar, Kurnool, Anantapur; World Bank aided Velugu (2000)
- Indira Kranthi Patham — 2004-05 merger of Velugu and DWCRA; implemented by SERP
- Pavala Vaddi — interest subsidy for SHGs, 2004
