- PET: Detects positron emitters, mainly F-18 FDG

### Radiometric dating and RTG — isotope → application
## 1. Radiometric dating
| Method | Dates | Half-life / range |
| Carbon-14 | Once-living (organic) remains: wood, bone, charcoal | Half-life ~5,730 years; up to ~50,000 years |
| Uranium-238 → Lead-206 | Oldest rocks and minerals (zircon), age of the Earth | ~4.5 billion years; millions to billions of years |
| Potassium-40 → Argon-40 | Volcanic rocks and the fossil-bearing layers (fossils or hominid remains) | ~1.25 billion years; >~100,000 years; thousands to billions of years |
- Use: Radioisotopes date artefacts and rocks/minerals (UPSC GS)
## 2. Radioisotope power sources - RTG
- RTG principle: Turns decay heat (heat of natural radioactive decay) into electricity through thermocouples (Seebeck effect)
- Not a fission reactor - not reactors; no fission, no moving parts
- Fuel: Plutonium-238, a by-product of weapons programmes/development
- UPSC 2024 key: statements 2 and 3 only correct
- Uses: Spacecraft power for deep-space missions and planetary rovers where solar/sunlight is weak
- Voyager, Cassini, Curiosity, Perseverance
- ⚠ RTG fuel = Pu-238 (not Pu-239); C-14 only for once-living remains
### Agriculture — mutation breeding, pest control and tracers
## 1. Mutation breeding
- Principle: Radiation changes seeds at the genetic level so desired traits can be chosen
- History: First shown by L.J. Stadler (1928, X-rays); now gamma and neutron radiation
- BARC's Trombay varieties: 72 released (2026)
- Crops: groundnut, rice, mustard, mung bean, cowpea, chickpea, wheat; non-GMO, climate-resilient
- Newest: banana TBM-9 (with NRCB Trichy), sorghum RTS-43 (15-20% higher yield)
- Older notes count 23 BARC varieties: 10 pulses, 9 groundnut, 2 mustard, 1 rice, 1 jute
- Not in your sources: BARC's latest total of released varieties (the older count is old).
## 2. Pest control
- Pheromones: BARC makes cheap pheromones for sweet-potato and cotton moths
## 3. Tracers and farm tools
- Tracers: P-32 labelled fertiliser tracks nutrient uptake → basis of "optimised fertiliser use"
- Moisture gauges: Nuclear gauges time irrigation
- Tissue culture: BARC leads in tissue culture (banana, pineapple)
- ⚠ TBM-9 = banana; RTS-43 = sorghum; 72 varieties now vs older count 23
### Food irradiation — process, sources and exports
- Process: Controlled radiant energy on packed or bulk food; works by radiolysis of water
- Sources: Gamma (Co-60), X-rays, electron beams (2026)
- Uses: Stops sprouting, delays ripening, kills insects, parasites and pathogens (microbes) → extends shelf life
### Industry — radiography, gauging and radiotracers
## 1. Radiography and gauging
- Gamma radiography: Non-destructive testing of welds and castings with radiography cameras (Ir-192, Co-60 sources)
- Gauging:
- Thickness gauges for plates and pipes; fill levels
- Automotive sensors
- Smoke detectors use an alpha emitter - Americium-241 (UPSC GS)
## 2. Radiotracers
- Radiotracers: BARC is Asia-Pacific's leading centre
- Leaks in buried oil/gas pipes, dam seepage, flow measurement
- Port sediment transport → fewer desilting campaigns (longer gaps between desilting)
### Universal angles
- Current relevance:
- ISOMED 2.0 (May 2025); 72 Trombay varieties; new Lu-177 therapy (2026)
- Significance: Non-power uses - cancer care, food security, export earnings - justify the nuclear programme beyond electricity
- Nuclear technology serves health, farming and industry, not just power (the "atoms for peace" dividend)
- FIRST/LAST: Tc-99m is the most used diagnostic isotope

### Radiometric dating and RTG — isotope → application
- ★ Carbon dating (Carbon-14, 6C14) — age of fossils, wood, bones, shell (time since death of plant or animal)
- based on decay of Carbon-14 in dead organisms; called radiocarbon dating
- ★ Radioactive dating — measures the age of rocks; main source of absolute age of geological features
- ★ Uranium-Lead process — age of the most ancient mountain rocks and of the Earth
- Clair Cameron Patterson — American geochemist, pioneer of uranium-lead dating
