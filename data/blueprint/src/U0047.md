### Satavahana names, FIRSTs and dynastic sequence
- Satavahanas: The first historical ruling dynasty of the Deccan
- One account makes them Brahmins by caste
- Puranic names: Andhras / Andhrabhrityas
- Andhrabhritya: = "servants of the Andhras", or Andhras who had been feudatories
- Origin: began as feudatories of the Mauryas; independent in the Deccan after Mauryan decline
- First in Andhra: Satavahanas = first kings to issue inscriptions in Andhra itself (Ashoka's were earlier)
- Sequence: Mauryas → Sungas → Kanvas (Magadha) → Satavahanas
- A Satavahana king defeated Kanva Susarma and held Magadha (Matsya/Vayu Puranas)
- Successors:
- Ikshvakus (Andhra), Abhiras (Maharashtra), Vakatakas (Vidarbha), Chutus, Pallavas
- Later Chalukyas, then Rashtrakutas
- Ikshvakus — Virapurushadatta: A popular king — his reign was the golden age of Buddhism (Buddhism flourished); Upasika Bodhisri contributed to Buddhism

### Gautamiputra Satakarni and the Nasik Prasasti
- Reign: GPS (c. 106-130 CE)
- Victory: Defeated Nahapana (Western Kshatrapa, Kshaharata branch) → regained lost territory (G2 2000)
- Recovered Malwa, Saurashtra, Konkan, Nasik (APPSC 2000 key)
- Coin proof: Restruck Nahapana's coins → Jogalthambi hoard (Nasik district): Nahapana's silver coins restruck by Gautamiputra
- Periplus: notes Saka-Satavahana conflict
- Nasik Prasasti — language & form: Prakrit prose, Brahmi script
- Content: Expeditions, victories, empire and titles
- Praises him as "Destroyer of Sakas, Yavanas and Pahlavas"
- Gifts: With Balasri to monks on the Trirashmi hill; Balasri called wife of a Rajarshi
- King's number: Gautamiputra = 23rd king
- ⚠ Trap: "28th ruler" in some notes is an error
- Varna: Classes Balasri's line as Brahmana varna
- Status debate: "Crusher of Kshatriya pride" claim sits beside his Brahmin status → Satavahanas were Brahmins, not Kshatriyas
- Buddhist patronage:
- Village near Trirashmi gifted to monks
- 100 nivartanas of his own land donated
- Sanchi toranas: some notes credit him, but the south torana inscription names Siri Satakarni (Satakarni I/II), not Gautamiputra

### Origin theories
- ★ V.S. Sukthankar — Satavahanas were not Andhras ("Andhras and Satavahanas are not the same"); original home in the Kannada (Bellary) region
- Bhandarkar — tied to the Bellary original-home view
- D.C. Sircar — Satavahanas were Andhras, not Andhrabhrityas
- Nilakanta Sastri — separated tribal, dynastic and family names (Andhra a tribal name)
- R.S. Sharma — linked them with the Dravidians
- B.S.L. Hanumantha Rao — belonged to the original Andhra janapada
- Puranas: Andhras and Andhrabhrityas (= servants / former feudatories of the Andhras)
- Periplus — notes the Saka–Satavahana conflict
- Successors: Vakatakas in Vidarbha, Ikshvakus in Andhra, Abhiras in Maharashtra
### Gautamiputra & Nasik Prasasti
## Wars with the Western Kshatrapas
- Beat Nahapana (of the Kshaharata branch) and regained Malwa, Saurashtra, Konkan and Nasik
- Jogalthambi hoard — Nahapana's silver coins restruck by Gautamiputra
- Ayama, Kshaharata minister — Junnar inscription; Ushavadata — Nahapana's son-in-law
- Junagadh inscription — Rudradaman beat Satakarni twice but spared him as a near relative
## Nasik Prasasti
- Main source for Gautamiputra's reign; Prakrit prose, Brahmi script
- Praises him as destroyer of Sakas, Yavanas and Pahlavas; records his campaigns, empire and titles
- Gift to monks — a village near Trirashmi and 100 nivartanas of his own land

### Sources
- Rise: began as Mauryan feudatories; free in the Deccan after Mauryan decline
- Matsya Purana — 30 kings, 456 years
- Vayu and Vishnu Puranas — 17 kings, 272 years; not treated as standard
- First kings to issue inscriptions in Andhra itself (Ashoka's were earlier in time)
- Earliest inscriptions — Maharashtra (Naneghat, Kanha's Nasik record)
- later: coastal Andhra (Amaravati), Karnataka (Myakadoni, Bellary), Kodavali-type records
- all in Prakrit, Brahmi script
### Exam corner
- Works on early Satavahana history — Kathasaritsagara, Pratishthanapura-kalpa, Dvatrimsatputtalika; not Gathasaptasati
- Megasthenes — Andhras lived in towns
- Pliny — Natural History; Ptolemy — Geography
- Kotilingala (Karimnagar, Telangana) — claimed early Satavahana political centre
### Satavahana names, FIRSTs and dynastic sequence
- ★ Andhras won freedom from the Mauryas under the Satavahanas; first king Srimukha
- ★ Satavahanas seen as Brahmins — Nasik inscription calls Gautamiputra 'Eka Brahmana'
- ★ Oldest Deccan dynasty — Satavahanas (c. 200 BC – 200 AD)
- ★ Early Satavahana inscriptions found in Maharashtra — Naneghat (Queen Naganika) and Nashik caves; centre later moved east to Amaravati, Dharanikota
### Scholars → theories on origin
- ★ V.S. Sukthankar — 'Andhras and Satavahanas are not the same'; Kannada (Bellary) origin
- ★ Capital — first capital thought to be Amaravati; real capital Paithan (Pratisthan)
- ★ Founder per the Puranas — Simuka
### Puranas, books and foreign accounts → authors and content
- ★ Matsya Purana — compiled in Yajnasri Satakarni's reign; longest list of Satavahana kings (30)
- ★ Early Satavahana history not in Gathasaptasati (700 rural romantic verses)
- ★ First to make tax-free land grants to Buddhists and Brahmanas — Satavahana kings
- Brihatkatha — Gunadhya, in Paisachi
### Inscriptions → issuer, ruler and findspot
- ★ Nashik Prasasti — records Gautamiputra's victories; issued by mother Gautami Balasri in the 19th regnal year of Pulumavi
- ★ Trisamudradhipati — Gautamiputra Satakarni (horses drank the waters of three seas)
- ★ Village given for Mahasanghika bhikshus at Karle — Yajnasri Satakarni
### Caves → location and features
- ★ Nasik prashasti of Gautami Balashri — achievements of Gautamiputra Satakarni

### Capitals, AP places and regions conquered

### Rulers → period → contribution
- ★ Founder — Simukha (Srimukha); greatest — Gautamiputra Satakarni; last — Pulumavi III
- ★ Gautamiputra faced the Sakas — defeated Nahapana (Kshaharata); "destroyer of the Sakas"

### Gautamiputra Satakarni and the Nasik Prasasti
