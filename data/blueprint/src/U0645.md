- Actual growth = natural increase (births − deaths) + net migration
- Over-urbanisation — urban population grows outpaces jobs and services; often slums
- Dependency ratio — population aged 0–14 and 65+ to working-age population
- Economic Survey 2025–26 — doubling dependency ratio makes geriatric care a growing job sector
- India's demographic dividend window closes after 2041; skilling and jobs urgent priorities
