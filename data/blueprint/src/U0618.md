### UNCLOS maritime zones

### UNCLOS maritime zones, EEZ and Acts
- UNCLOS: Adopted 1982, in force 1994
- Set up International Seabed Authority (ISA) - regulates seabed mining beyond national jurisdiction
| Zone (from baseline) | Limit | Coastal-state rights |
| Territorial sea | 12 nm | Full sovereignty (innocent passage for others) |
| Contiguous zone | 24 nm [GK] | Enforce customs, fiscal, immigration, sanitary laws [GK] |
| EEZ | 200 nm | Sovereign rights to explore, exploit, conserve all living and non-living resources |
| Continental shelf | Edge of margin, extended shelf up to 350 nm [GK] | Sovereign rights over seabed and subsoil resources |
| High seas | Beyond national jurisdiction | Freedom of navigation, overflight, research |
- EEZ scope: Excludes the territorial sea + continental shelf beyond 200 nm
- India's EEZ: ~2.37 million sq km (~24 lakh sq km); world's 18th largest
- Andaman-Nicobar + Lakshadweep islands add much of it
- Indian law: Territorial Waters, Continental Shelf, EEZ and Other Maritime Zones Act, 1976
- ⚠ 12 nm = sovereignty; 200 nm EEZ = sovereign rights only

### World fisheries → fishing grounds, conditions and nations
| Fishing ground | Location [GK] |
| Grand Banks | Off Newfoundland, NW Atlantic (Gulf Stream meets Labrador Current) |
| NW Pacific | Off Japan (Kuroshio meets Oyashio) |
- Favourable conditions:
- Wide shallow continental shelves
- Mixing of warm and cold currents (plankton-rich), upwelling
- Cool temperate water; indented coasts with harbours
- Limited farmland pushing people to sea
- Fishing-dependent countries: Norway, Japan, Iceland (Iceland's exports lean most heavily on fish)
- Pearls:
- Japan (cultured Akoya pearls) = classic answer for largest pearl producer
- By volume China now leads (freshwater pearls)

### Blue economy — terms, sectors and SAGAR
- Definition: Sustainable use of ocean, coastal and inland waters for growth, jobs and conservation

- EEZ potential (2026): ~US$24 billion
- Policy: Draft Blue Economy Policy Framework (MoES, 2021)
- SAGAR: "Security and Growth for All in the Region" - PM's Indian Ocean vision (2015, Mauritius)
