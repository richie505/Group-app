### MSME definition → evolution and limits — Evolution
## 1. Evolution
| Year | Milestone |

## 2. Original 2006 limits (investment only)
| Original 2006 limits [GK] | Manufacturing (plant & machinery) | Services (equipment) |
| Micro | up to Rs 25 lakh (≤ ₹25 lakh) | up to Rs 10 lakh (≤ ₹10 lakh) |
| Small | up to Rs 5 crore (≤ ₹5 crore) | up to Rs 2 crore (≤ ₹2 crore) |
| Medium | up to Rs 10 crore (≤ ₹10 crore) | up to Rs 5 crore (≤ ₹5 crore) |
### MSME definition → evolution and limits — Revised composite criteria (2020)
## 1. Revised composite criteria (2020)
- Notified: 1 June 2020 (Aatmanirbhar package)
- Basis: Investment in plant & machinery/equipment AND annual turnover (composite); Investment AND annual turnover used together (APPSC-GS 2024)
- No longer a manufacturing/services distinction
- AP follows the same definition (APSES 2024-25)
- Current thresholds under 2020 notification (as sources give them):
| Threshold (2020 notification) | Investment (P&M/equipment) ≤ | Annual turnover ≤ |
| Micro | up to Rs 1 crore (₹1 crore) | up to Rs 5 crore (₹5 crore) |
| Small | up to Rs 10 crore (₹10 crore) | up to Rs 50 crore (₹50 crore) |
| Medium | ₹50 crore | ₹250 crore |
- AP Survey 2024-25 + AP CA (Feb 2026) still use these limits
- Memory aid: investment 1-10-50, turnover 5-50-250

### Acts → year and provisions (MSMED Act, 2006)
- MSMED Act, 2006: Introduced as Small and Medium Enterprise (SME) Development Bill on 12 May 2005 → Act, in force from 2 October 2006
- Chapter V (delayed payments):
- Buyers must pay within 45 days
- After that: compound interest at 3 times the RBI bank rate (Sec. 16)
- Disputes → MSE Facilitation Council
- MSMED (Amendment) Bill 2026: Passed by Rajya Sabha (Aug 2026)
- National Digital Platform for registration
- Public Procurement Policy for MSEs Order, 2012:
- Central ministries + CPSEs must buy 25% of annual procurement from MSEs (raised from 20% in 2018)
- Sub-targets: 4% SC/ST-owned MSEs; 3% women-owned MSEs
- Achieved (FY26, to 1 Dec 2025): 44.76%, above the mandate
- GeM: Rs 18.4 lakh crore GMV; 11 lakh+ MSEs (Aug 2026)
- ⚠ Bill introduced 12 May 2005; Act in force 2 October 2006
- ⚠ Procurement mandate 25% (from 20%): 4% SC/ST + 3% women
