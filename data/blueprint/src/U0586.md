### Institutions and schemes → role (NICDC, NICDIT, AP-ICDA, APIIC, BHAVYA)
| Body | Facts |
| NICDC | National Industrial Corridor Development Corporation, formerly DMICDC, incorporated Jan 2008; under Ministry of Commerce & Industry (DPIIT); implements the NICDP and the new BHAVYA park scheme |
| NICDIT | National Industrial Corridor Development and Implementation Trust; funds node trunk infrastructure (Kopparthy, Krishnapatnam, Orvakal); ₹3,000 crore in BE 2026-27 |
| AP-ICDA | AP Industrial Corridor Development Authority under the A.P. Industrial Corridor Development Authority Act, 2017; industrial parks notified under Section 3 of the Act |
| APIIC | State nodal agency for industrial parks and corridor land; incorporated 1973 (APPSC 2023) |
- APIIC: Nodal agency for all industrial infrastructure (IT, biotech, apparel parks, SEZs) via PPP model
- Parks managed: 571 industrial parks = 264 APIIC + 283 UDL
- NICDP: 11 corridors; Aug 2024 Centre approved 12 more projects → total 20
- BHAVYA (2026-27 to 2031-32): ₹33,660 crore; 100 plug-and-play parks via NICDIT-State SPVs; six new AP parks proposed
- VCIC machinery:
- Executing agency: Department of Industries and Commerce
- Four PIUs: APIIC, APRDC, AP Transco, GVMC
- APSEZ incentives:
- 100% stamp duty exemption
- 3% interest subsidy
- ⚠ NICDC = Corporation (ex-DMICDC, 2008); NICDIT = Trust (funds trunk infrastructure)

- 3,475 m animal underpasses (1,400 m continuous) cleared by SC-NBWL
- Raipur-Visakhapatnam: Greenfield expressway (Chhattisgarh-Odisha-AP); flagship Central project for AP
- New expressway proposals:
- Bengaluru-Kadapa-Vijayawada Economic Corridor → finish by 2027

- Mumbai-Pune, Pune-Hyderabad, Hyderabad-Bengaluru, Hyderabad-Chennai, Chennai-Bengaluru, Delhi-Varanasi, Varanasi-Siliguri
- Total cost ₹16 lakh crore; Hyderabad-Chennai in 2 h 55 min
