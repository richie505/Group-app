### Committees → method (calorie norms, recall, price updating) — Dandekar and Rath (1971)
## 1. Dandekar and Rath (1971)
- Data: 1960-61 NSS data
- Basis: Poverty line = expenditure adequate for 2,250 kcal per day, for both rural and urban
## 2. Committee → method
| Committee | Method |
| 1962 Working Group | ₹20 rural / ₹25 urban per capita per month (1960-61 prices) [GK] (APP says "per year") |
| Alagh | Nutrition norm 2,400 kcal rural / 2,100 kcal urban |
| Lakdawala | Kept the calorie basis; State-specific lines; price updating by CPI-AL (rural), CPI-IW (urban); stopped scaling to National Accounts |
| Tendulkar | Moved away from the calorie anchor; added private health and education spending; MRP in place of URP; one urban PLB used for both rural and urban |
| Rangarajan | Separate food and non-food needs: calories 2,155 rural / 2,090 urban plus protein and fat norms [GK]; MMRP data |
- Exam angle: match-the-committee items pairing Alagh (1979) with the nutrition norm and Dandekar-Rath (1971) with the first systematic study (APPSC-GS 2022).
### Committees → method (calorie norms, recall, price updating) — Terms → meaning
## 1. Terms → meaning
| Term | Meaning |
| URP | 30-day recall for all items (used up to 1993-94) |
| MRP | 365-day recall for five low-frequency items (clothing, footwear, durables, education, institutional medical care) |
- URP and MRP: Both measure incidence of poverty (APPSC 2018 key)
- Data: NSS consumer expenditure rounds every five years (now HCES 2022-23 and 2023-24)
- RBI (2025): Rangarajan line updated with HCES 2022-23 using new index on PLB food + non-food weights, not plain CPI
