### Indo-Greek coinage — FIRSTs, legends, metals, deities
## 1. Indo-Greek coins
- Indo-Greeks: Popularised — introduced the circulation of — gold coins in northwestern India (north-western India) — issued gold coins in the 2nd century BC
- Strato II: Lead coins - important Indo-Greek lead coins
- Agathocles: Silver coins featuring Sankarshana and Vasudeva — bilingual legends in Greek and Brahmi
- ⚠ Sources differ: other notes give the Indian side of Indo-Greek bilingual legends as Kharoshthi (Prakrit)
- Sixteen coins of Menander were discovered at Bairat — a site of coin discovery
| FIRST | FACT |
| Coins definitely attributed to kings | First in India = Indo-Greeks |
| First to issue gold coins in India | Indo-Greeks |
| Bilingual legends | Greek on front + Kharoshthi (Prakrit) on back |
| Portraits | Realistic royal portraits (helmets, diadems) |
| Metals | Mainly silver and copper; gold rare |
- Deities:
- Indian: Vasudeva, Sankarshana (Agathocles' coins); Buddhist wheel (Menander's)
- Significance: main source for the list of more than 30 Indo-Greek kings
## 2. Coinage of foreign rulers
- Kushanas: Extensively used gold and copper coins — promoted them on a wider scale
- Rudradaman and the Saka-Kshatrapas: Silver and copper coinage
- Chandragupta II: Silver Rupaka coins after his victory over the Sakas
- Mahmud Ghazni: Silver coins with Arabic and Sanskrit legends
- Muhammad Ghori: Coins bearing Lakshmi; some bore the Arabic Kalma
