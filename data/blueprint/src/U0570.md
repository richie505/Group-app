### Health insurance schemes → cover and scheme matching
## 1. Health insurance schemes
- Dr NTR Vaidya Seva (earlier Dr YSR Aarogyasri):
- Cashless treatment for BPL families + families with income up to ₹5 lakh
- Cover: ₹25 lakh per family per year; no limit for cancer
- 3,257 procedures; 1.43 crore families benefited (APSES 2024-25)
- Budget 2026-27: ₹4,000 cr to the Trust
- Universal Health Policy (announced 2026): Free treatment up to ₹2.5 lakh per citizen via insurers; Vaidya Seva Trust covers the poor beyond that, up to ₹25 lakh (1 Mar and 4 Jun 2026)
- ⚠ Income ceiling ₹5 lakh; Vaidya Seva cover ₹25 lakh per family; Universal Health Policy ₹2.5 lakh per citizen
