### Articles and amendments on distributive justice

### Persons → growth-first vs redistribution (Bhagwati-Sen debate)
- Core issue: economic growth first vs investment in social infrastructure (health, education) first (APPSC-G1 2017)
|  | Jagdish Bhagwati (with Arvind Panagariya) | Amartya Sen (with Jean Dreze) |
| Priority | Rapid, market-led growth; growth creates jobs and revenue that later fund social spending | Public investment in education, health and nutrition first; growth alone does not remove deprivation |
| Book (2013) | Why Growth Matters [GK] | An Uncertain Glory [GK] |
- Amartya Sen: for backward countries primary education and health get highest priority (APPSC-GS 2007 key)
- Capability approach: Development as Freedom; "Kerala Model" = high health and education at modest income
- ⚠ Why Growth Matters = Bhagwati-Panagariya; An Uncertain Glory = Sen-Dreze
### Approaches to poverty — trickle-down vs direct attack
| Approach | Logic | Example |

| Direct attack | Targeted wage/self-employment, food and cash transfers | 5th Plan "Garibi Hatao" onwards [GK]; VB-G RAM G, DAY-NRLM |
| Welfare | Safety nets, health, education | Nordic states (UPSC) |
| Bottom-up | Community participation | SHG movement (UPSC) |
