## 1. KWDT-II reference
- AP's stand: Add Telangana as separate party → allocations redone among four States
- Oct 2023: Centre gave KWDT-II further terms of reference under Sec. 5(1) of ISRWD Act → divide AP share between AP and Telangana
- Jurisdiction: AP asked Centre to notify KRMB's jurisdiction under Section 87 (APPSC-GS 2021 key)
- Jal Shakti Ministry notified both Boards' jurisdiction in July 2021 (15 July 2021)
### Apex Council and River Boards → members and functions
## 1. Apex Council
- Role: Supervises GRMB and KRMB - Godavari River Management Board (GRMB) + Krishna River Management Board (KRMB), set up under Sec 85
| Member | Role |
| Union Minister of Water Resources (now Jal Shakti) | Chairperson |
| Chief Minister of Andhra Pradesh | Member |
| Chief Minister of Telangana | Member |
- Membership: 3 members in all (APPSC-GS 2025, APPSC-G1 2022, APPSC-GS 2021 keys)
- ⚠ Trap: NOT PM, Home Minister or Governors
- Functions (Sec. 84(3)):
- Supervise both Boards
- Approve new projects on Krishna/Godavari after appraisal by Board + CWC
- Settle disputes by negotiation
- Refer disputes not covered by KWDT to a tribunal under ISRWD Act, 1956
- Exam angle: "power to sanction new projects" = Apex Council, not the Boards or the Ministry.
## 2. Board functions (Schedule XI)
- Regulate water + power supply from notified projects/headworks per tribunal awards
- Release water per States' shares
- ⚠ Trap: Allocating or redetermining shares is NOT a Board function
- Current (2026):
- KRMB three-member committee allowed only drinking water from Srisailam + Nagarjuna Sagar (El Niño, poor inflows; 25 Aug 2026)
- Advised Telangana after Srisailam storage fell due to its power generation (5 Sep 2026)
