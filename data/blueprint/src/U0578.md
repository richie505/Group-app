### Special Category Status and the package in lieu — origin, benefits, terms
## 1. Special Category Status
- Origin: SCS began 1969 (Fifth FC era) for J&K, Assam, Nagaland
- Benefits: 90:10 CSS funding vs 60:40; concessional loans; tax breaks
- 14th FC (2015): removed special/general distinction in devolution
- Demand: AP, Bihar, Odisha demand SCS; AP given special packages instead (Jan 2026)
## 2. Special assistance measure in lieu of SCS
- Basis: given in lieu of Special Category Status
- EAP route: Centre repays loans + interest of externally aided projects signed 2015-16 to 2019-20
- Centre bears 90% of EAP cost
- Not in your sources: year-wise amounts released under the special assistance measure.
- Central transfers to AP, 2023-24 RE, ranked: FC grants > other grants-in-aid > EAP > small savings
- ⚠ 90:10 = SCS CSS funding; 90% = Centre's share of EAP cost under the package
