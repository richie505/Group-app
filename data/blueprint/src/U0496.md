### Crop insurance — PMFBY, RWBCIS and AP — PMFBY
## 1. PMFBY
- PMFBY (2016): Replaced NAIS (1999) and MNAIS (2010); "One Nation, One Crop, One Premium"
- Type: Central Sector Scheme - Centre's premium subsidy goes straight to insurers
| Season / crop | Farmer's premium (max) |
| Kharif food and oilseeds | 2% |
| Rabi food and oilseeds | 1.5% |
| Commercial / horticultural (annual) | 5% |
- Premium sharing: Rest of the premium shared by Centre and states
- Voluntary: Voluntary for all farmers since 2020 (Kharif 2020); made voluntary in 2020
- Coverage: Pre-sowing to post-harvest, localised calamities/risks
- From Kharif 2026: also wild-animal attack and paddy inundation losses
- Scale:
- 4.19 crore farmers insured in 2024-25 (FY25), 32% more than 2022-23
- 9 years: 78.62 crore applications; claims ₹1,82,136 crore (₹1.82 lakh crore) vs farmers' premium ₹35,864 crore (≈ ₹508 per ₹100)
- Tech: YES-TECH (remote-sensing yield estimation), WINDS (weather stations), DigiClaim
- ⚠ 2% kharif, 1.5% rabi, 5% commercial
