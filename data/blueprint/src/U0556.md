# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 3:112 - Impact of bifurcation on state revenue - revenue deficit, loss of Hyderabad revenues, resource gap
### Loss of Hyderabad → deficit
- Hyderabad region gave nearly 50% of the undivided State's revenue: its loss left a structural revenue deficit
- 1. Hyderabad (+ Rangareddy, Medak) ≈ 50% of revenue ↓
- 2. Lost HITEC City, Genome Valley, head offices, real estate ↓
- 3. Narrower tax base: GST, stamp duty, motor-vehicle tax ↓ ↓
- 4. Structural revenue deficit
### 2014-15 resource gap: who said what
| Source | Figure |

| Union Finance Ministry | about ₹4,118 crore |
| APPSC 2022 key | more than ₹15,000 crore |
### Resource gap basics
- First year = 2 June 2014 (appointed day) to 31 March 2015
- Resource gap = revenue deficit for first ten months
- ⚠ Assent 1 March 2014; division 2 June 2014 (not "2 March")
- Centre left out new State schemes (e.g. farm-loan waiver); State counted actual spending

### Resource gap — meaning and competing estimates

### Demands, SCS and central assistance — APRA sections and Finance Commissions
## 1. Special Category Status demand
- SCS demand: CM N. Chandrababu Naidu demanded Special Category Status (SCS) (APPSC-GS 2018 key)
- SCS for 5 years promised on floor of Rajya Sabha in 2014
- Under SCS: Centre bears 90% of CSS costs (vs 60%) + tax breaks
- Centre's refusal: Cited 14th FC → ended general/special category distinction; devolution raised 32% to 42%
- Political fallout: SCS row → TDP left NDA in 2018
## 2. APRA sections → demands and grants
| Provision | Demand / grant |
| Sec. 94(1) | Tax holidays (demanded) |
| Sec. 46 | Backward-area packages (demanded) |
| Sec. 94(3) | ₹2,500 crore for capital buildings |
- Other demands: Revenue deficit grants; full Polavaram funding at revised cost; capital-city grants
## 3. Central assistance and Finance Commissions
- Revenue deficit grant (2014-15): ₹3,979 crore, released in instalments up to 2016-17
- Part of Special Assistance Measure (SAM) → Centre funds 90% share of externally aided projects instead of SCS
- Institutions: 11 central institutions: IIT Tirupati, IIM Visakhapatnam, NIT Tadepalligudem, AIIMS Mangalagiri
| Finance Commission | Effect on AP |
| 14th FC (from 2015-16) | Post-devolution revenue deficit grants (₹22,113 crore over 2015-20) |
| 16th FC | Ended revenue deficit grants for all States; AP's divisible-pool share rose to 4.22% |
- ⚠ SAM = 90% of externally aided projects, given instead of SCS (which meant 90% of CSS costs)
