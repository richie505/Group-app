### Corporate tax and MAT — sections → rates

- Corporate tax: 22% without exemptions; effective ~25.17% with surcharge and cess

## 2. Minimum Alternate Tax
- MAT: Taxes "zero-tax companies" on book profits (15%)
| MAT milestone | Fact |
| Introduced | 1986-87 Budget (APPSC 2019), rate 15.75%; Finance Act 1987, effective AY 1988-89 |
| Withdrawn / back | Discontinued (dropped) 1990-91; reintroduced 1996-97 (effective 11.87%) |
| Standard rate before 2026 | 15% of book profits + surcharge and cess |
| Budget 2026-27 | Rate cut to 14% and made a final tax from 1 April 2026; no new MAT credit; old credit set off up to 1/4 (25%) of liability, only in the new regime |
- ⚠ 115BAA = 22% (existing) vs 115BAB = 15% (new manufacturing); MAT 15.75% (1986-87) → 15% → 14% (2026-27)
