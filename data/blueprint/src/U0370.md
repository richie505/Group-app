- Central debt ≤40% of GDP by 31 Mar 2025

### Schemes — CSS rationalisation and DBT
## 1. Centrally Sponsored Schemes — 2015 rationalisation
- 2015 NITI Aayog Sub-Group of Chief Ministers: restructured CSS into three groups
- Core of the core: MGNREGA, NSAP + umbrella schemes for SCs, STs, minorities, other vulnerable groups
- Core schemes: mostly 60:40 Centre:State; 90:10 for NE + Himalayan states
- After 2015: most CSS moved to higher state shares → PMMVY and ICDS = 60:40 (TH, 2 Feb 2026; 9 Jul 2026)
- ⚠ Trap: 60:40 = core schemes; 90:10 = NE + Himalayan states
## 2. Direct Benefit Transfer — JAM trinity
