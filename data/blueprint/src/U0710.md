## Total internal reflection
- ★ Happens from denser to rarer medium when angle of incidence > critical angle
- ★ Refractive index: Diamond > Glass > Water > Air — TIR possible from diamond to glass
- ★ Optical fibre — works on total internal reflection; thin strand of glass (silica) or plastic
- ★ Fibre optics: TIR principle, extremely low power consumption, free from radio frequency interference
- ★ Endoscope — total internal reflection within a fibre-optic bundle

- ★ UHF (300 MHz – 3 GHz) — travels by space waves; neither follows the ground nor bounces off the ionosphere
