### Unemployment approaches — definitions and types (UPS, UPSS, CWS, CDS)
| Measure | Reference period | Employed if | Captures |
| Usual Status (US) - Usual Principal Status (UPS) | 1 year or 365 days | Major time (183+ days) spent working | Chronic (long-term) unemployment |
| Usual Principal + Subsidiary Status (UPSS/ps+ss) | 365 days | Principal status, or subsidiary work for at least 30 days | Chronic; broadest count of workers |
| Current Weekly Status (CWS) | 1 week (7 days) | Worked at least 1 hour on any 1 day of the week | Chronic + seasonal unemployment |
| Current Daily Status (CDS) | Each day of the reference week (the 7 days) | Unit = half day; 4+ hours = employed for the day | Daily/intermittent; most inclusive |
- CDS: Gives highest rate - counts days of unemployment even of persons "employed" under CWS
- Census (not an NSS approach):
- Main worker = 183+ days a year
- Marginal worker = < 183 days
- ⚠ 365 days – 7 days – half day = UPS – CWS – CDS; UPSS adds subsidiary work of 30 days
- ⚠ 183 days = UPS "major time" and also the Census main/marginal worker line - but Census is not an NSS approach
- Exam angle: odd-one-out lists slip in a non-method such as "Current Monthly Status" or "Rangarajan formula" among UPS/CWS/CDS.
### Labour and work concepts; labour indicators (LFPR, WPR, UR)
## 1. Labour and work concepts
- WPR: Employed population divided by total population
- LFPR: The labour force divided by the working-age population
- Casual worker: Non-regular or casual employment; fixed-term employment: for a predetermined period
- Real sector: Goods and services; financial sector: money and financial assets
## 2. Labour indicators
| Indicator | Formula |
| Labour force | Employed + unemployed (seeking/available for work) |
| UR | Unemployed / labour force x 100 |
| Proportion Unemployed (PU) | Unemployed / population |
- Identity: WPR = LFPR x (1 - UR)
- Not seeking work (students, homemakers) = outside labour force
- ⚠ UR divides by labour force; PU divides by population
- Exam angle: "percentage of employed persons in the population" = Worker Population Ratio (APPSC 2023).

### PLFS — agency, launch, design and 2025 revamp
- Agency: National Statistical Office (NSO), MoSPI
- Design: Standing Committee on Labour Force Statistics (SCLFS), set up on National Statistical Commission's advice
- Launch: April 2017; first annual report (2017-18) released May 2019
| Feature | Original design | Revamp from January 2025 |
| Monthly | - | Monthly all-India CWS estimates (LFPR, WPR, UR) |
| Quarterly | Quarterly estimates: urban only (CWS) | Quarterly estimates extended to rural areas |
| Annual | Annual estimates: rural + urban, UPSS and CWS | Annual report on calendar year (Jan-Dec) |

- Status: Now standard NSO mechanism for India's unemployment rate (APPSC G2 2025)

### Four approaches
| Approach | Reference / unit | Counted employed if |
| UPS | 365 days | worked major part, generally 183 days or more |
| UPSS | 365 days | also subsidiary work for at least 30 days |
| CWS | 7 days | worked at least one hour on any day — captures short-term/seasonal unemployment |
| CDS | half a day | 4 or more hours = employed for that day |
- CDS gives the highest unemployment rate — counts days of unemployment even for persons employed under CWS
### Labour indicators
- Unemployment Rate — unemployed ÷ labour force; Proportion Unemployed — unemployed ÷ population
- Worker Population Ratio — employed ÷ total population; WPR = LFPR × (1 − UR)
- LFPR — employed + unemployed per 100 persons (labour force over working-age population)
- PLFS — conducted by the National Statistical Office under MoSPI

- Bhagwati Committee (1973) — reviewed unemployment estimates and employment-oriented policies
