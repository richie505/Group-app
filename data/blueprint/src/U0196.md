- Origin: Rajahmundry (Godavari district), today East Godavari = Andhra's first Renaissance centre
- Led by: English-educated middle class

### Brahmo and Prarthana Samaj in Andhra — samaj → year → founder
## 1. Samajs (ROCKET)
- Brahmo Samaj (associated with combined Andhra Pradesh): Founder — Kandukuri Veeresalingam; R. Venkataratnam was responsible for spreading the idea of Brahma Samaj in Andhra — it spread in Andhra
- ⚠ Sources differ: other notes say Veeresalingam founded the Prarthana Samaj, Rajahmundry (1878), and the first Brahmo Samaj in Andhra was at Machilipatnam (1882) under Raghupathi Venkataratnam Naidu
- Dasa Samaj: Established in Andhra by Brahma Naidu
## 2. Origins
- Parent body: Brahmo Samaj founded by Raja Ram Mohan Roy, 1828, Calcutta - monotheism, no idol worship
- Madras root: Veda Samajam; secretary Chembeti Sridharulu Naidu renamed it Dakshina Bharatha Desa Brahmo Samajam (Dakshina Bharata Brahmo Samaj)
- Andhra branch: Rajahmundry (1878) per some notes

### Reformers and patrons → contributions
## 1. Raghupathi Venkataratnam Naidu
- Inspiration: Joined Brahmo Samaj inspired by Pandit Sivanath Sastri
- Spread: Took reform from the Godavari districts to North Coastal Andhra (Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam, Srikakulam); centre = Kakinada
- Bodies:
- Samajika Shuddhi Samajam (Social Purity Association), 1891 - most important
- Karunalayam, Kakinada (with Pithapuram Maharaja)
- Anti-nautch (Bhogam / Devadasi) campaign: Raghupathi Venkataratnam Naidu (PYQ 2019) began the anti-nautch (Devadasi) movement in Madras
- Main force behind ending the Bhogam (Devadasi) caste custom (APHQ)
- APPSC 2019: main factor in eliminating the 'Bhogam' caste
- Offices:
- Principal, Pithapuram Raja's College, Kakinada (1905-18)
- First Andhra Vice-Chancellor of Madras University; Andhra University Bill came during his tenure
## 2. Desiraju Pedda Bapayya (Bapatla)
- First practising (Anushtanika) Brahmo in Andhra; discarded sacred thread
- Founded Temperance and Social Purity Sangham, Rajahmundry → boycott of Bhogam melas
## 3. Zamindari patrons
- Pithapuram Raja (Rao Venkatakumara Mahipati Suryarao, 1885-1964):
- Karunalayam (1909) = only orphanage in Andhra
- Brahmopasana Mandiram (Kakinada); Brahma Mandir (Pithapuram)
- Rs 1 lakh to the Brahma Dharma Prachara Nidhi → train preachers + translate Bengali Brahmo books
- Vizianagaram:
- Kanyasulkam (2nd edn 1909) dedicated to Ananda Gajapati
- First staged by the Vizianagaram troupe (1892)
