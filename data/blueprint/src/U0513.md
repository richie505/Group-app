### Pipelines → period, value, sectors (NIP and NMP)
## 1. National Infrastructure Pipeline
- Period: FY2019-20 to 2024-25
- Investment: ₹111 lakh crore
- Coverage: Both economic and social infrastructure
- ⚠ Trap: "only economic" is WRONG (APPSC-G1 2024)
- Sector shares: Energy ~24%, roads ~18%, urban ~17%, railways ~12%
- Now: Pipeline of ₹185-213 lakh crore across 30+ sectors incl. digital and social
## 2. National Monetisation Pipeline
- Assets: Brownfield core infrastructure; ownership stays with government
- Announced: Budget 2021-22
| Item | NMP 1.0 | NMP 2.0 |
| Period | FY22-FY25 | FY26-FY30 |
| Value | ₹6 lakh crore | ₹16.72 lakh crore (incl. ₹5.8 lakh crore private) |
| Largest sector | Roads (APPSC-GS 2021 key) | Highways/MMLPs/ropeways 26%, power 17%, railways 16%, ports 16%, coal 13% |
| Result / launch | ₹5.3 lakh crore monetised (~89-90%); coal gave most (₹2 lakh crore) | Launched 23 Feb 2026, prepared by NITI Aayog |
- NMP 2.0: Budget 2025-26 figure of ₹10 lakh crore still cited in some 2026 notes (superseded)
- Instruments: PPP concessions, InvITs, ToT, securitisation
- ⚠ NIP = ₹111 lakh crore, largest share Energy; NMP 1.0 = ₹6 lakh crore, largest sector Roads
- ⚠ NMP 1.0: Roads = largest planned sector, but coal gave most actual monetisation
### Financing institutions → year, Act and role
| Institution | Key facts |
| NIIF | Govt-anchored; manages Category II AIFs; quasi-sovereign wealth fund; set up 2015 [GK] |

- ⚠ IIFCL 2006 → InvIT regulations 2014 → NIIF 2015 → NaBFID Act 2021
### PM Gati Shakti National Master Plan → launch, engines, structure
- Nature: Whole-of-government, GIS-based integrated planning
- Launch: 2021 (APPSC-GS 2025 key) – on 13 Oct 2021
- 7 engines (APPSC-GS 2024 key): Railways, Roads, Ports, Waterways, Airports, Mass Transport, Logistics Infrastructure
- Coverage: Brought 16 ministries on one digital platform (6 Jul 2026)
- NMP portal: 1,700+ data layers; developed by BISAG-N (Gandhinagar)
- Railway economic corridors: 434 projects, ₹11.17 lakh crore mapped on the portal
- ⚠ GatiShakti Oct 2021 precedes National Logistics Policy Sept 2022
### National Logistics Policy 2022 → target, data, platforms, index
- Launch: 2022 (Sept 2022); complements PM GatiShakti
- Target: Logistics cost to single digits of GDP by 2030
- Logistics cost: 7.97% of GDP (FY24), down from 8.84% (FY23); older estimates 13-14%
| Term | Meaning |
| ULIP | Unified Logistics Interface Platform |
- LEADS (DPIIT, 2018; modelled on World Bank LPI): LEADS 2025 - four tiers ( Exemplars : Tamil Nadu, UP, Mizoram, Delhi); AP was an "Achiever" among coastal states in LEADS 2023 (CDI).
