- Ramsar since 2002; freshwater lake between Godavari and Krishna deltas
- Sanctuary 308.55 sq km; spot-billed pelican breeding
- Threat: aquaculture tanks inside sanctuary
- Operation Kolleru (2006): tanks demolished up to +5 ft contour

### Wetlands → Kolleru Ramsar site
- Ramsar status: Ramsar site since 2002
- Nature: Large freshwater lake between the Godavari and Krishna deltas
- Sanctuary area: 308.55 sq km
- Birds: Spot-billed pelican breeding + migratory waterfowl
- Encroachment: Aquaculture tanks inside the sanctuary
- Operation Kolleru (2006): Demolished illegal fish tanks up to the +5 ft contour
- Ramsar count: India has 100 Ramsar sites (as of 2026); some notes say 96

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 5:88
### AP's PA network
- Sri Venkateswara NP — lies in the Seshachalam hills
- Godavari-estuary mangroves — protected by Coringa WLS (not Sri Venkateswara NP)
### Key sites
- Rajiv Gandhi NP (Rameswaram NP) — about 2.30 sq km; smallest NP in AP
- Cycas beddomei — endemic to AP / Eastern Ghats; Sri Venkateswara-Seshachalam region
- Boswellia ovalifoliolata — endemic to AP; Seshachalam-Tirumala hills
- Syzygium stocksii — not an AP endemic
### Sanctuaries & their species
- Coringa WLS — Godavari-estuary mangroves; Krishna WLS — Krishna-estuary mangroves
- AP mangrove genera — Avicennia and Rhizophora
- Koundinya WLS (Chittoor) — part of Rayala Elephant Reserve, AP's only elephant reserve
### Kolleru Ramsar site
- Kolleru — freshwater lake between the Godavari and Krishna deltas (the brackish lagoon is Pulicat)
- Kolleru sanctuary area — about 308.55 sq km
- Operation Kolleru (2006) — removed illegal fish tanks up to the +5 ft contour
### ESZ norms
- Supreme Court, June 2022 — minimum 1 km ESZ around protected areas mandatory
- Wildlife Action Plan 2002–16 — had suggested 10 km
- ESZs notified by MoEF&CC under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 (not WPA 1972)
### Current & coastal
- Nagarjuna-Srisailam TR, June 2026 — monsoon ban on safari vehicles
- Hope Island (near Coringa) — threatened by a proposed satellite launch facility
### Exam corner
- Kasu Brahmananda Reddy NP — Hyderabad, Telangana (not present-day AP)
### Places → national parks and endemic plants of AP
- ★ Endemic plant of AP — Boswellia ovalifoliolata
