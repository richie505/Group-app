## 2. Boundary lines
| Line | Year / author | What it does |
| Johnson Line | 1865 (W.H. Johnson) | Puts Aksai Chin in Ladakh - India's claim |
| Macartney-MacDonald Line | 1899[GK] (some notes say 1893) | Puts Aksai Chin under China - China's preferred line |
| McMahon Line | Simla Convention, 1914 (Henry McMahon) | Eastern boundary (Tibet-India); Chinese delegate initialled but China later refused to recognise it |
- ⚠ Johnson = India's claim; Macartney-MacDonald = China's preferred; McMahon = eastern sector
### Boundary lines, claims and LAC sectors → year and claimant — Chinese occupation and claims
## 1. Chinese occupation and claims
- Chinese occupation/claims:
- Occupies about 38,000 sq km in Aksai Chin
- Claims about 90,000 sq km in the north-east (most of Arunachal Pradesh, called "South Tibet")
- Shaksgam Valley (Trans-Karakoram Tract): Illegally ceded by Pakistan to China under the 1963 Sino-Pakistan Boundary Agreement; India reaffirmed it as integral (2026)
- Arunachal claims in practice:
- China denied visas to Arunachal residents in India's delegation to the Fourth World Conference on Women (Beijing, 1995)
- USA reaffirmed Arunachal as integral part of India (BPSC 2023)

### 1962 war → causes, sectors and report
- Causes:
- Unsettled boundary (British left no clear line)
- Tibet question + Dalai Lama's asylum (1959)
- India's Forward Policy
- Sectors: Western (Aksai Chin/Ladakh) + Eastern (NEFA)
- Duration: 20 Oct-21 Nov 1962 → ended in China's unilateral ceasefire
- Henderson Brooks-Bhagat Report (1963): Reviewed India's military failure
- Effect: Blow to NAM idealism; India sought Western military aid
