### Gandhi — early life and first campaigns
## 1. Early life and South Africa
- Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi: Born on 2 October 1869 in Porbandar (Gujarat) — a barrister; legal training at the Inner Temple in London
- Education: Samaldas College, Bhavnagar → law in London 1888-1891 at the Inner Temple
- Practice: Rajkot and Bombay - did not succeed
- 1894: Founded the Natal Indian Congress in South Africa
- 1903: Indian Opinion — a newspaper established in South Africa; published in English, Gujarati, Hindi and Tamil; first issue in June 1903
- ⚠ Sources differ: other notes say English and Gujarati editions [GK]
- 1904: Established Phoenix Ashram in Durban — Gandhi's first ashram
- 1910: Gandhi and his colleagues established Tolstoy Farm
- 1915: Returned to India
