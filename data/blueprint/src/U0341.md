### Telugu Desam Party — founding and FIRSTs
## 1. Founding and first victory
- Founder: NTR in 1982
- Plank: Telugu self-respect (Telugu atma gauravam) against perceived slights from the Centre
- First victory: Won power within nine months (Jan 1983 Assembly election) → one of the fastest ascents by any party
- 1983 Assembly election: TDP ended Congress's unbroken rule in Andhra Pradesh since the State's formation (1956)
- Ideology: No specific ideology beyond Telugu pride + welfare populism
## 2. National role and FIRSTs
- 8th Lok Sabha (1984): TDP (30 seats) = main opposition party in the 8th Lok Sabha
- First regional party to do so
- National Front: NTR = chairman of the National Front (1988) → formed the V.P. Singh government in 1989
### Impact of regional parties — effects
| Factor | Effect |

| Federalism | More genuinely bargained after 1989 |
| Positive | Backward-region representation, balanced development |
| Negative | Parochialism, instability |
- Special category status: Demanded for residuary AP after 2014
- TDP left the NDA over it in 2018
### Universal angles
- Current relevance:
- After Trinamool's 2026 West Bengal defeat + defections (AITC, AAP Rajya Sabha MPs, NCP-SP) → analysts: vote share, not seats, shows regional support (2026)
- BJD MP: BJD won 182 Lok Sabha seats between 1999 and 2024
- AP angle: Regional party (TDP) leads AP's ruling coalition with BJP support
- Criticism & debates: "One Nation, One Election" feared to weaken regional parties; first four general elections (1951-52, 1957, 1962, 1967) were simultaneous
