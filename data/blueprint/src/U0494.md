### Causes of yield gaps and pathways
## 1. Causes and determinants
| Factor | Effect / figure |
| Irrigation gap | Rice ~67% irrigated vs pulses ~26% |
| Inputs | Skewed NPK ratio, low SRR / HYV seed replacement, lack of balanced fertiliser, mechanisation |
| Post-harvest | 30% cold-chain losses |
| Soybean | Below 2 t/ha in many districts (rainfed) |
- Climate:
- Yields fall about 8% per 1°C; a 20% rainfall deficit or 1 deg C rise cuts yields ~8%
- Wheat falls 6-10%
- 1 deg C wipes ~4 years of rice/wheat productivity gains
- El Niño years (pulses):
- Area / acreage = -2 to -9% (falls 2-9%)
- Yield falls 5-25%; output -6 to -30%
## 2. Pathways
- Seed: Better seed through SRR/VRR and genome editing
- Nutrition: Balanced nutrition via Soil Health Card
- Mechanisation: Via CHCs
