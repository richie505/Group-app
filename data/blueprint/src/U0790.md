# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 5:100 - Bharat Stage emission norms - evolution from BS-I to BS-VI and their implications
### Norms in order
- BS-V = the only stage India skipped; BS-IV straight to BS-VI on 1 April 2020
- 1991 / 1992 — First vehicle norms: petrol / diesel
- 1 April 2000 — BS-I ("India 2000") nationwide; BS-II in Delhi
- 1 April 2005 — BS-III in NCR + 10 cities (incl. Hyderabad-Secunderabad); BS-II nationwide
- 2010 — BS-III nationwide; BS-IV in 13 cities
- April 2017 — BS-IV nationwide
- 2016 — Decision to skip BS-V
- 1 April 2020 — BS-VI nationwide (= Euro 6)
- April 2023 — BS-VI Phase 2: Real Driving Emissions
- 2026-27 — BS-VII planned

### Bharat Stage norms → year — FIRSTs, Euro equivalence and notifying authority
## 1. Vehicle emission norms
- National Auto Fuel Policy: Recommended Euro emission standards — to control vehicle pollution; the sequence: Mashelkar Committee → National Auto Fuel Policy → Euro norms
- Euro-I: Europe, 1992; BS-IV: implemented in India from 1 April 2017; BS-VI: implemented from 1 April 2020
## 1. FIRSTs and origins
| FIRST | FACT |
| India's first vehicle norms | 1991 (petrol); 1992 (diesel) |
| Bharat Stage name | Euro-based norms from 2000 (APPSC-GS 2019 key: 2000) |
| Euro I in Europe | 1992 (some notes say 1983) |
| Euro II in India | Delhi on 1 April 2000 (= BS-II) |
| Tractor norms | First notified 1999; Trem Stage II = 1 June 2003; Stage III = 1 Oct 2005 |
- Equivalence: BS-I = Euro I; BS-II = Euro II; BS-III = Euro III; BS-IV = Euro IV; BS-VI = Euro 6
- India typically lagged Europe by ~5 years
- Exam angle: "since when Euro norms under the BS name" = 2000; BS-I also called "India 2000".
## 2. Stage → year
| Stage | First (cities) | Nationwide |
| BS-I ("India 2000") | - | 1 April 2000 (APP) |
| BS-II | Delhi from 1 April 2000 (APP) | 2005 (APP) |
| BS-III | NCR + 10 big cities (incl. Hyderabad-Secunderabad) from 1 April 2005 (APP) | 2010[GK] |
| BS-IV | 13 cities, 2010[GK] | April 2017 (APP) |
| BS-V | Skipped | - |
| BS-VI | - | 1 April 2020 (LENS Dec 2025) |
| BS-VI Phase 2 (RDE) | - | April 2023 (CDI) |
- Next stage: BS-VII (Euro 7 equivalent) planned for 2026-27
## 3. Notifying authority
- Authority: Set on CPCB / MoEFCC advice; notified under Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989 by MoRTH
- ⚠ Notified by MoRTH, not MoEFCC
### BS-V skipped — Supreme Court deadline and why
- Leapfrog: BS-V skipped; decided in 2016 → BS-VI for all new vehicles from 1 April 2020
- Why:
- Urgent urban air pollution (Delhi-NCR)
- Close the gap with Europe in one step instead of two changeovers
- Supreme Court: No sale or registration of BS-IV vehicles from 1 April 2020 (APPSC-GS 2018 key)
### Vehicular pollutants → health effect; what Euro norms limit
## 1. Effects
- Carbon monoxide: Combines with haemoglobin → carboxyhaemoglobin
- Acid rain: Sulfur dioxide and nitrogen oxides — pH below 5.6
- Photochemical smog: Nitrogen oxides, hydrocarbons and sunlight
- Radioactive pollution: Hereditary changes; carcinogenesis — Chernobyl, a nuclear pollution accident
- Nanoparticles: Pollution of water, soil and the food chain
- Bioremediation: Microorganisms remove or detoxify pollutants — cadmium and lead associated with heavy-metal limitation
## 2. Vehicular pollutants → health effect
| Pollutant | Health effect |
| CO | Binds haemoglobin (carboxyhaemoglobin), cuts oxygen supply [GK] |
| NOx | Respiratory illness, children most susceptible; forms ground-level ozone and acid rain (UPSC) |
| PM2.5 | Penetrates deep lungs/bloodstream; heart and lung disease [GK] |
| Benzene | Carcinogen (leukaemia) [GK] |
| Lead | Neurotoxin, harms children's brain development; leaded petrol phased out in India by 2000[GK] |
- Euro norms limit: carbon monoxide (RPSC 1999)
- Covered list = CO, hydrocarbons, lead → "all of the above" (UPPSC 2013 key)
### Data — fuel sulphur and emission limits per stage
## 1. Fuel sulphur per stage
| Stage | Fuel sulphur |
| BS-II | 500 ppm (APP) |
| BS-III | 350 ppm (APP) |
| BS-IV | 50 ppm (APP) |
| BS-VI | 10 ppm (-80% vs BS-IV) (APP; LENS) |
- Euro II ultra-low sulphur diesel: 0.05% or less (= 500 ppm) (UPPSC 2013 key)
## 2. Emission limits
| Stage | Limits |
| BS-VI | NOx 60 mg/km petrol, 80 mg/km diesel; diesel PM 4.5 mg/km |
- BS-VI expected NOx cut: diesel cars ~70%; petrol cars ~25%
### Emission control technologies → function
- Earlier stages:
- BS-I era: catalytic converters convert CO to CO2
- BS-II: carburettor → multi-point fuel injection
| Technology | Function (LENS Dec 2025) |
| SCR | Converts diesel NOx to N2 + water using urea solution AdBlue |
| DPF | Traps soot, periodically oxidises it |
| OBD-II | Continuously monitors emission performance, alerts driver |
| Electronic Fuel Injection | Mandated shift for two-wheelers |
- Real Driving Emissions (RDE): On-road (not only lab) tests under BS-VI Phase 2 from April 2023
### Implications and allied measures — CAFE III, E20, scrappage, PUC
## 1. Implications
- Refineries: Had to produce 10 ppm BS-VI fuel nationwide
## 2. Norms and notifications → year
| Measure | Provisions |
| CAFE III draft (third draft, 16 Jul 2026) | Fuel-efficiency norms for cars from April 2027; credits for ethanol, CBG, biofuels; flatter weight curve; targets relaxed ~21% vs Sept 2025 proposal; industry average weight 1,229 kg |
| E20 rollout | Notified 17 Feb 2026; all States/UTs to sell E20 with min RON 95 from 1 Apr 2026; replaces the 2021 E10 notification |
| Vehicle Scrappage Policy (2021) | Fitness-based de-registration via Automated Testing Stations + Registered Vehicle Scrapping Facilities |
| PUC certificate | Mandatory under CMV Rules 1989; transport officers trained at ARAI Pune and IIP Dehradun |
