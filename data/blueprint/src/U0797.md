### Acts and regulations → year → provisions (1897 Act, DM Act, IHR 2005)
## 1. Epidemic Diseases Act, 1897
- Role: Legal framework for biological disasters; used in COVID-19
- Notification: E.g. 20 states made human rabies notifiable (Jan 2026)
- Urgent cases reported within 24 hours; routine within 3 days
- 2020 amendment (ordinance April, Act September 2020):
- Violence against health workers = cognizable and non-bailable
- 3 months to 5 years jail + Rs 50,000 to Rs 2 lakh fine
## 2. Other laws
| Law / instrument | Year | Provision |
| Disaster Management Act, 2005 | 2005 | National COVID lockdown (March 2020) ordered by NDMA + MHA under it; MHA Empowered Groups ran the pandemic response |
| Draft Public Health (Prevention, Control and Management of Epidemics, Bio-Terrorism and Disasters) Bill, 2017 | 2017 | Meant to replace 1897 Act; not enacted |
| IHR (2005) | in force since 2007 | Legally binding WHO instrument |
| WHO Pandemic Agreement | Adopted 20 May 2025 | - |
## 3. IHR obligations
- Build core capacities for surveillance and response
- Report possible PHEICs to WHO within 24 hours
- Name a National IHR Focal Point
- 2024 amendments: Added higher "pandemic emergency" tier
- May 2026 Ebola outbreak = PHEIC but did not meet the pandemic-emergency criteria (18 May 2026)
- ⚠ 24 hours = both urgent notifiable cases (1897 Act) and PHEIC report to WHO (IHR)

### DM cycle for epidemics → phase → measure
- Epidemics: Count as biological disasters
| Phase | Epidemic example |

### Epidemic Diseases Act, 1897
## Indian law
- 2017 draft Public Health Bill — meant to replace the 1897 Act but never enacted
- 2020 amendment (ordinance April, Act September 2020) — violence against health workers made cognizable and non-bailable
- NDMA (chaired by the PM) — Biological Disaster Management guidelines, 2008, under the DM Act
## Surveillance bodies
- NCDC (formerly NICD) — runs IDSP, sends Outbreak Investigation Teams, and itself runs the National Public Health Emergency Operations Centre
- DGHS — MoHFW's technical arm
- INSACOG — set up December 2020
- PM-ABHIM — 744 Integrated Public Health Laboratories (one per district); 631 Critical Care Hospital Blocks
- AP zoonotic disease action plan — under One Health approach, with NCDC, US CDC and Jhpiego
## International
- IHR (2005) — report a possible PHEIC to WHO within 24 hours; build surveillance capacity; name a National IHR Focal Point
- 2024 IHR amendments — added a higher "pandemic emergency" tier above PHEIC
- BRICS New Delhi Declaration — sub-working group on an integrated early-warning system for infectious diseases
### Laws & frameworks
- March 2020 COVID-19 lockdown — ordered by NDMA and MHA under the Disaster Management Act, 2005
- NDMP (2016) — matches Sendai Framework (2015-30), the SDGs and the Paris Agreement; covers all phases of DM
- WHO Pandemic Agreement — adopted 20 May 2025
- IHR (2005) — legally binding, in force since 2007
### Institutions
- MoHFW — nodal ministry for epidemics (biological disasters); DGHS its technical arm, NCDC the surveillance agency
- MHA — coordinates disasters in general
- ICMR-NIV, Pune — apex virology laboratory, BSL-4 containment
