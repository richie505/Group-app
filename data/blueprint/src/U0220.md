### Santhal Hul (1855-56)
- Rajmahal Hills: British failed with Paharias → invited Santhals (APPSC G1 2017)
- Damin-i-Koh = tract for Santhal settlement
- Began at Bhognadih, 30 June 1855 (Hul Divas)
- Leaders: Sidhu, Kanhu, Chand, Bhairav, sisters Phulo-Jhano; about 30,000 proclaimed Santhal Raj
- Crushed 1856; over 15,000 killed → Santhal Parganas district
### Birsa Munda & the Ulgulan
- Birsa
- Born 1875; Dharti Aba
- Founded Birsait faith
- Against liquor, witchcraft, animal sacrifice
- Ulgulan (1899-1900)
- About 6,000 Mundas; aim Abua Raj
- Captured Feb 1900; died Ranchi jail, June 1900
- Region: Jharkhand (APPSC 2024)
- Legacy
- CNT Act 1908: bars tribal land transfer; Khuntkatti rights
- Janjatiya Gaurav Divas: 15 Nov

### Santhal Hul (1855-56)
- Damin-i-Koh — Rajmahal Hills area where the British settled the Santhals after failing to control the Paharias
- Leaders — Sidhu and Kanhu Murmu, Chand, Bhairav, sisters Phulo-Jhano
- After the Hul — Santhal Parganas made a separate district with laws protecting tribal land (Chotanagpur Tenancy Act came in 1908)
- Hul Divas — 30 June
### Birsa Munda & the Ulgulan
- ★ Working area — Ranchi (expanded to Bhagalpur); aimed at social reform in tribal areas
- Dharti Aba ('Father of the Earth'); Ulgulan (1899-1900) in Chotanagpur, present-day Jharkhand (APPSC 2024)
- Janjatiya Gaurav Divas (birth anniversary) — 15 November
- Birsait faith — monotheist; mixed Munda tradition, Hinduism and Christianity
- Reform programme — against liquor, witchcraft, animal sacrifice, spirit worship (Khuntkatti was a landholding system, not a reform)
- Abua Raj = self-rule; about 6,000 Mundas
- Chotanagpur Tenancy Act, 1908 — barred transfer of tribal land to non-tribals; recognised Khuntkatti rights
- Other regions: Odisha — Paika Bidroha; Maharashtra — Ramosi and Bhil risings; Andhra — Rampa
### Rampa & Rekapalle 1879 (AP)
- Ambul Reddy raided Vaddagudem police station, 10 July 1879; Rekapalle rising caused by a rise in the podu tax during a famine
### Bhils & others
- Govind Giri (Guru) — led the Bhils against bonded labour (Rajasthan and Gujarat)
- Mangarh massacre (1913) — "Adivasi Jallianwala"; Mangarh Dham a National Monument in 2022
