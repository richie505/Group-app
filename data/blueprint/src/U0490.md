### Schemes and Acts → year and provisions (AIBP, Atal Bhujal, APWALTA, PM-KUSUM, APMIP) — AIBP
## 1. AIBP
- AIBP: Speeds up ongoing major/medium projects
- 99 prioritised projects funded through NABARD's LTIF since 2016
- Potential created: 26.71 lakh ha vs 34.63 lakh ha target
- 8 AP projects among the 99
## 2. Atal Bhujal Yojana
- Launch: Dec 2019; World Bank-aided Central Sector scheme under Ministry of Jal Shakti
- Coverage: 7 states - Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, MP, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, UP
- Focus: Demand-side community management via GP-level Water Security Plans
## 3. APWALTA
- APWALTA: AP Water, Land and Trees Act, 2002 - AP law against indiscriminate borewell digging (PYQ)
- Rainwater harvesting: Required on plots of 200 sq m and above (PYQ key)
- Over-exploited villages: Notified for strict regulation
- GEC 2024-25: 258 villages proposed
### Schemes and Acts → year and provisions (AIBP, Atal Bhujal, APWALTA, PM-KUSUM, APMIP) — PM-KUSUM
## 1. PM-KUSUM
- Launch: 2019 under MNRE
| Component | Content |
| A | 10,000 MW of decentralised grid-connected solar plants (0.5-2 MW) near substations |
| B | 14 lakh standalone solar pumps (up to 7.5 HP) to replace diesel pumps; none in dark groundwater zones |
| C | Solarisation of 35 lakh grid-connected pumps, including feeder-level solarisation |
- Objective: De-dieselise agriculture + add farmer income
- JPSC PYQ: "agricultural solar pump system"
## 2. APMIP and AP irrigation plan
- APMIP: Running since 2003
- 42.25 lakh acres; 15.09 lakh farmers; Rayalaseema = 64% of area
- White Paper on Irrigation (Jul 2026): 36 projects by Dec 2028 at ₹35,555 crore
- ⚠ Atal Bhujal = Jal Shakti (demand-side groundwater); PM-KUSUM = MNRE (solar pumps)

- Global share: India ≈ 25% of global extraction
- AP (GEC 2024-25): Stage of development 31.5% (Safe)
- 679 mandals: 12 are over-exploited, 3 critical, 24 semi-critical, 39 saline, 601 safe (CGWB 2025 report)
