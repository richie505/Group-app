### Kadapa steel plant — events by year and JSW plant features
- Act: SAIL to examine within six months feasibility of integrated steel plant in YSR (Kadapa) district (+ one in Khammam, Telangana)
- The Centre/SAIL did not build it; the State formed its own SPV AP High Grade Steels Ltd (2018) [GK]. Not in your sources: SAIL/MECON feasibility findings.
| Year | Event |
| YSR era (2004-09) | Land allotted to Brahmani Steels; project stalled (TH 4 Jul 2026) |
| Dec 2018 | Foundation stone by CM N. Chandrababu Naidu at M. Kambala Dinne, Mylavaram mandal (APPSC 2018 key) |
| 2019 and 2023 | Stones laid again by CM Y.S. Jagan Mohan Reddy (TH 4 Jul 2026) |
| 3 Jul 2026 | Construction launched: JSW Rayalaseema Integrated Steel Plant at Sunnapurallapalli and Peddandluru villages, Jammalamadugu constituency, Kadapa |
- JSW plant:
- Cost: ₹16,350 crore in two phases (₹4,500 crore Phase I + ₹11,850 crore)
- Technology: scrap-based Electric Arc Furnace green steel
- Water: 2 tmc ft/year from Gandikota via 15-km pipeline
- ⚠ 2018 stone at M. Kambala Dinne; 2026 JSW plant at Sunnapurallapalli
### Ports — Dugarajapatnam and Ramayapatnam → place, developer, status — Dugarajapatnam port
## 1. Dugarajapatnam port
- Act wording: GoI "shall develop" a new major port at Duggirajupatnam (only port named in the Schedule; APPSC-type question)
- Dropped due to non-feasibility (CDI). Not in your sources: the specific grounds for non-feasibility.
- Replacement: State built Ramayapatnam on its own as substitute greenfield port
- Revival (2026):
- G.O. (2 Jan 2026): APMB to set up greenfield port + mega shipbuilding cluster at Dugarajapatnam, Tirupati district, under Centre's Shipbuilding Development Scheme (SbDS)
- SPV: National Shipbuilding and Heavy Industries Park Andhra Pradesh Ltd (G.O. Ms. No. 7, 2 Mar 2026) = 50:50 JV of VPA and APMB
- Land: ~3,500 acres identified; cluster worth ₹30,000 crore
- One of five national cluster sites (others: TN, Gujarat, Odisha, Maharashtra)
- ⚠ Act's port "shall develop" (not "examine") = Duggirajupatnam; built substitute = Ramayapatnam
### Ports — Dugarajapatnam and Ramayapatnam → place, developer, status — Ramayapatnam port
## 1. Ramayapatnam port
- District: Prakasam (2026)
- 2024-25 data placed it in SPS Nellore district, Ulavapadu mandal before district boundaries redrawn
- Location: 93 km north of Krishnapatnam; 5 km east of Thettu on NH-16 (nearest station Thettu)
- Developer: State SPV Ramayapatnam Port Development Corporation Ltd (RPDCL)
- Permitted Feb 2020; contract signed 30 Jul 2021
- Phase-I: ₹4,929.39 crore; 4 berths; 34.04 MTPA; 850 acres core land
| Date | Physical progress |
| Oct 2025 | 74.34%, highest of the four greenfield ports (ES 2025-26 analysis, CDI) |
- Recent approvals:
- ₹1,638.52 crore approved for Phase-II of Ramayapatnam, Mulapeta, Machilipatnam
### Refinery and metro rail → place, agency, status
## 1. Petroleum refinery-cum-petrochemical complex
- Act: IOC or HPCL to examine, within six months, a greenfield crude refinery + petrochemical complex
- BPCL refinery-cum-petrochemical complex: Near Ramayapatnam port, between Prakasam and Nellore districts
- Villages: Chevuru and Ravuru
- Cost ~₹1.45 lakh crore (earlier quoted ~₹1 lakh crore)
- Capacity 9 MTPA; ~5.4 MT of Euro-6 fuels
- Kakinada: MoUs for greenfield petrochemical facility cleared by State pollution board
- Kakinada lies in Visakhapatnam-Kakinada PCPIR
- Not in your sources: the viability-gap-funding figures for the Kakinada (HPCL-GAIL) complex.
## 2. Metro rail for Visakhapatnam and Vijayawada
- Implementing agency: Andhra Pradesh Metro Rail Corporation Ltd (APMRCL)
- Visakhapatnam metro: 4 corridors, 76.70 km; cost ₹11,498 crore
- Phase-1 needs 99.11 acres (only 7.57 acres private); ₹600 crore sought for compensation
- Double-decker metro-cum-road corridor on NH-16 being surveyed
- Vijayawada : Governor's address said the Visakhapatnam and Vijayawada metro projects were approved by the State (TH 27 Jan 2026). Not in your sources: Central approval status of Vijayawada metro.
