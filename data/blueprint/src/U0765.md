### Causes → effects of degradation — Deforestation
## 1. Deforestation
- Impacts:
- Faster erosion, falling fertility, desertification
- Loss of CO2 sink adds to greenhouse effect
- Highest loss: Tropical forests (Amazon, Congo basin, SE Asia)
## 2. Desertification and erosion
- Main cause in semi-arid regions: Overgrazing and deforestation (APPSC 2025)
| Process | How it degrades land |
| Overgrazing | Strips vegetation cover |
| Deforestation | Exposes soil to wind and water erosion |
| Water erosion | Loss of topsoil; sedimentation of rivers and reservoirs |
| Wind erosion | Topsoil blown away in dry areas (e.g. Anantapur) |
| Salinisation/waterlogging | Poor drainage and over-irrigation (AP delta regions) |
### Causes → effects of degradation — Agriculture-driven degradation
## 1. Agriculture-driven degradation
- Effects of modern agriculture (intensive agriculture): Soil degradation, pollution, ecological imbalance - all of these (APPSC 2018)
- Chemicals: Synthetic fertilisers and pesticides contaminate soil and water
- Excess urea has skewed the NPK ratio in Punjab and Haryana (CSE)
- Over-irrigation + poor drainage: → waterlogging and salinity; by 2050, 50% of world arable land could be affected
## 2. Urban heat islands
- UHI causes:
- Concrete/asphalt (low albedo, heat storage)
- Loss of vegetation
### Data — forest loss and degraded land — Global forest data
## 1. Global forest data
- FAO FRA 2025 (Oct 2025):
- Forests cover about 32% of Earth's land
- Over 54% of forests in five countries: Russia, Brazil, Canada, USA, China
- Net forest loss: 10.7 Mha/yr (1990s) → 4.12 Mha/yr (2015-2025)
- Asia = only region with net gain 1990-2025, led by China and India
- India rose to 9th in total forest area (10th in 2020); stays 3rd in annual forest gain
- Older FAO FRA (FRA 2010 basis): Net loss 8.3 million ha/yr (1990s) → 5.2 million ha/yr (2000-2010) (APPSC 2018)
- Forest Declaration Assessment 2025 (Climate Focus with UNDP/UNEP):
- Forest loss 8.1 Mha in 2024
- About 94% of loss in tropical forests
- ⚠ FAO net loss: 10.7 → 4.12 Mha/yr (FRA 2025) vs 8.3 → 5.2 million ha/yr (FRA 2010 basis)
- Exam angle: fill-in-the-blank on FAO net-loss figures; check which FRA edition the question uses.
- Not in your sources: latest FAO Global Forest Resources Assessment (2025) net-loss figures.

### UNCCD, days and conferences
## 1. Convention basics
- UNCCD: Treaty on desertification, land degradation and drought
- Adopted 1994 (Paris); in force 1996; secretariat Bonn
- Only legally binding framework on desertification and drought
- One of three Rio conventions (with UNFCCC and CBD)
- India a party; ratified 1996
- Global Land Outlook: UNCCD flagship (2017, 2022); 2025 thematic report on ecological connectivity
## 2. Days and decades
| Day / decade | Date / detail |
| World Day to Combat Desertification and Drought | 17 June (UNGA, 1994); 2026 host Kenya; theme "Rangelands: Recognise. Respect. Restore." |
| 2025-2034 UN Decade on Combating Sand and Dust Storms | Declared by UNGA + a day on 12 July |
- Dust storms: Hit drylands of NW India (Rajasthan) and West Asia/Sahel
## 3. COPs
| COP | Place / year | Outcome |
| COP14 | New Delhi, 2019 | India raised restoration target to 26 Mha by 2030 (26 million ha by 2030); earlier pledge: 21 Mha at Paris 2015 |
| COP17 | Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia (2026) | India (MoEFCC) launched first-ever guide to Grasslands and Open Natural Ecosystems - over 10% of India's area, under 5% protected; key to LDN targets |
- LDN: Target year 2030 (SDG 15.3); India works towards LDN
- ⚠ UNCCD adopted 1994, in force 1996; Desertification Day 17 June; dust-storm day 12 July
