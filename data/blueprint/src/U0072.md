### Rashtrakuta rulers → period, capital and contribution
- Dantidurga established the Rashtrakuta dynasty — Empire — in 753 AD; performed the Hiranyagarbha ritual at Ujjayini
- Capital: Manyakheta (built later by Amoghavarsha I; Dantidurga's seat was Elapura/Ellora region)
- Amoghavarsha I: An important ruler — born in 800 AD at Shribhavan (on or near the Narmada) in a military camp
- Participated in the Tripartite Struggle for Kannauj — Govind III defeated Gurjara-Pratihara ruler Nagabhata II during a North Indian campaign

- ⚠ Trap (APPSC G1 2024 wrong pair): "Krishna III - rock-cut Shiva temple at Ellora" - it was Krishna I
## 2. Dantidurga - founding and ritual
- Establishment: 753 CE (some notes date him 753-56 AD, contemporary of Eastern Chalukya Vijayaditya I)
- Ritual: Hiranyagarbha (UPPSC 2015), at Ujjain
### Capitals, religion, Ellora and the end of the dynasty
- Religion: Patronized Shaiva, Vaishnava and Shakta traditions; an inclination toward Jainism — Jinasena, a Jain scholar, authored the Adipurana
- Kailasa Temple at Ellora: Built by Krishna I — Cave 16; Dravidian architecture; designed to recall Mount Kailasa — Shiva's abode
- Ellora: Buddhist, Hindu and Jain rock-cut caves coexist
- The Elephanta Caves were built by the Rashtrakutas
## 1. Capitals
- Early seat: Elapura (Ellora) region; Dantidurga's inscription in Ellora's Dashavatara cave
- Manyakheta (Malkhed, Karnataka): Built by Amoghavarsha I (UPSC)
- ⚠ Trap: some notes say Dantidurga made Manyakheta his capital - that is Amoghavarsha's work
## 2. LAST - end of the dynasty
- End of dynasty:
- Paramara Siyaka II defeated Khottiga on the Narmada and sacked Manyakheta
- Tailapa II of Kalyani overthrew the line (c. 973)
### Battles and conflicts → victor: Vengi, Takkolam
## 1. Takkolam
- Battle of Takkolam — the early medieval period: 949 CE — the Cholas (led by Rajaditya) and a Rashtrakuta-led confederacy (Western Gangas and Vaidumbas, led by Krishna III) — Rajaditya and Krishna III were the leaders; the Rashtrakuta side won; a major Chola–Rashtrakuta conflict
- Krishna III defeated Chola Parantaka I; Chola prince Rajaditya killed
- Battle of Thakkolam: Took place in 949 CE — the Rajaditya Cholan RTC at Thakkolam was renamed in memory of Prince Rajaditya Chola
- Chola Gangam — also called the Ponneri artificial lake: Rajendra Chola I — commemorates the victory over the rulers of the Ganga region
## 2. Vengi - war for ~200 years
| Rashtrakuta side | Eastern Chalukya side | Outcome |
| Dhruva (through Vemulavada Arikesari I) | Vishnuvardhana IV | Dhruva won → Vishnuvardhana gave daughter Shila Mahadevi to Dhruva |
| Rashtrakuta forces | Vijayaditya II | fought 108 battles |
| Amoghavarsha | Eastern Chalukyas | Amoghavarsha won at Vingavalli |
| Krishna II (after Amoghavarsha's death) | Gunaga Vijayaditya III | Gunaga Vijayaditya III won |
- ⚠ Vijayaditya I = rival of Dantidurga; Vijayaditya II = 108 battles; Gunaga Vijayaditya III = beat Krishna II

### Universal angles
- Significance: Golden age of Kannada; zenith of rock-cut art (Ellora Kailasa, Elephanta)
- AP angle: Vijayaditya II's 108 Shiva temples and naming of Vijayawada; Pampa hailed as a Telugu-born poet
- Comparative note: Sulaiman visited in Amoghavarsha I's reign → called him one of the four great kings of the world
- Criticism & debates:

- Some notes make Pampa contemporary of Vijayaditya I - a slip

### Rashtrakuta rulers → period, capital and contribution
- ★ Dantidurga — laid the base of the Rashtrakuta empire (dynasty c. 753–982 CE); former Chalukya feudatory; Hiranyagarbha at Ujjayini
- ★ Kailasa (Shiva) temple, Ellora — built by Krishna I (Rashtrakuta dynasty)
- ★ Amoghavarsha I — born in a military camp at Shribhavan (800 AD) as father Govinda III returned from northern campaigns
### Capitals, religion, Ellora and the end of the dynasty
- ★ Ellora — Buddhist, Hindu and Jain rock-cut caves together
- ★ Kailasa temple, Ellora — Rashtrakutas; built by Krishna I in Dravidian style
- ★ A wonder of rock-cut architecture; Cave 16 of Ellora
### Battles and conflicts → victor: Vengi, Takkolam
- ★ Takkolam — between the Cholas (Rajaditya, son of Parantaka I) and the Rashtrakutas (Krishna III with Western Gangas and Vaidumbas)
### Literary works → authors; court poets → patrons
- ★ Pampa — Telugu-land scholar; wrote Vikramarjuna Vijayam (Pampa Bharata) in Kannada
- ★ Court poet of Vemulavada Chalukya Arikesari II; brother of Jinavallabha
- ★ Vikramarjuna Vijaya = Kannada version of the Mahabharata
