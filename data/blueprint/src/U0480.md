### MGNREGA features
- 100 days per household per financial year; adults, unskilled manual work
- Objective: livelihood security (APPSC 2023); Article 41; Ministry of Rural Development
- Women ≥ one-third (actual > 50%)
- Wages within 15 days; allowance if no work in 15 days: 1/4 wage for 30 days, then 1/2
- Monitoring: Bhuvan (ISRO) GeoMGNREGA; NREGASoft; Gram Sabha social audit
- World Development Report 2014: "stellar example of rural development"

### MGNREGA, 2005 — Act, objective, entitlement and wages
| Item | Fact |
| Act | National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, 2005; passed September 2005 (first proposed in 1991 by P.V. Narasimha Rao) |
| Launch | 2 February 2006 in 200 districts [GK] (APPSC: launched 2006) |
| Phases | 130 more districts in 2007-08 [GK]; all districts from 1 April 2008 |
| Renaming | "Mahatma Gandhi" added on 2 October 2009[GK] (MGNREGA) |
| Guarantee | At least 100 days of wage employment per financial year |
| Unit of entitlement | Household (not individual) |
| Eligibility | Rural households whose adult members volunteer for unskilled manual work (not urban, not skilled) |
| Wages | State-specific notified wage; paid within 15 days of the work done[GK]; delay attracts compensation; DBT to Aadhaar-linked accounts |
| Ministry | Ministry of Rural Development |
| Constitutional anchor | Rights-based, demand-driven, anchored in Article 41 (right to work, DPSP) |
- Name and coverage: Was NREGA; began in 200 districts
- ⚠ Trap: APP notes say "Mahatma Gandhi Employment Guarantee Act" and 625 districts - both wrong; 625 = all-district coverage of 2008
- Objective: "Enhancing livelihood security of rural households" via at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment (APPSC 2023 answer)
- World Development Report 2014 (World Bank): Called it a "stellar example of rural development"
- ⚠ 2005 Act - 2006 launch - 2008 all districts - 2009 "Mahatma Gandhi" added
- Exam angle: APPSC 2017 - mandate = "100 days to every household whose adult members volunteer", not "100 days to every adult".
### MGNREGA — key features and monitoring
## 1. Key features
- Women: At least one-third of beneficiaries; actual share consistently above 50% of person-days
- Unemployment allowance: If work not given within 15 days of application
- At least 1/4 of wage for first 30 days
- 1/2 thereafter
- Permissible works: Water conservation, land development, rural connectivity, durable community assets
- Convergence: 90/95 person-days to PMAY-G beneficiaries via AwaasSoft-NREGASoft link
- Scale: 5-6 crore households + 200-250 crore person-days a year
## 2. Monitoring
| Tool | Use |
| Bhuvan mobile platform (ISRO) | Geo-tagging - "GeoMGNREGA" (APPSC 2018) |
| Gram Sabha | Mandatory social audit; Gram Sabhas plan and monitor works |
- ⚠ 15 days twice: wages within 15 days of work; allowance if no work within 15 days of application
### Acts compared — MGNREGA 2005 vs VB-G RAM G Act 2025
- Status (2026): MGNREGA has been replaced by Viksit Bharat - Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025 (VB-G RAM G)
- Passed by Parliament 18 Dec 2025
- Effective + MGNREGA repealed from 1 July 2026 (13 May 2026; 2 Sep 2026)
| Aspect | MGNREGA 2005 | VB-G RAM G Act 2025 |
| Guarantee | 100 days/household | 125 days/household (operated over 305 days) |
| Seasonal pause | None | States may pause works up to 60 days in sowing/harvest |
| Funding | Mostly Central, demand-driven | 60:40 (90:10 NE/Himalayan States, UTs); normative State-wise allocation |
| Wage payment | Within 15 days | Weekly or within a fortnight |
| Works | Many scattered categories | 4 priority areas: water security, core rural infrastructure, livelihood infrastructure, extreme-weather/disaster works |
| Admin cost ceiling | 6% | 9% |
- Other VB-G RAM G points:
- Statutory wage floor ₹300/day
- Job cards → Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Cards
- No contractors; assets on VB-NRIS stack
- Central and State Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Councils
- Unemployment allowance retained
- ⚠ 125 – 60 – 9% = guarantee days – seasonal pause – admin ceiling (VB-G RAM G)

### MGNREGA features
## Entitlement
- Objective — livelihood security through at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment per financial year
- Unit of guarantee — the rural household (not each adult)
- For adult members volunteering for unskilled manual work
- Constitutional basis — Article 41 (right to work)
- Olga Tellis (1985) — linked livelihood with Article 21; MGNREGA the only law built on the right to work
## Wages and allowance
- Wages within 15 days; delay brings compensation; DBT to Aadhaar-linked accounts
- No work within 15 days → unemployment allowance at least one-fourth of wage for first 30 days, one-half thereafter
- Women — at least one-third of beneficiaries (actual share over 50% of person-days)
## Administration
- Nodal Ministry — Ministry of Rural Development
- ★ CAPART — autonomous body under the Ministry of Rural Development
- Social audit — mandatory, by the Gram Sabha (which also plans and monitors works)
- Bhuvan (ISRO) — GeoMGNREGA geo-tagging; NREGASoft — MIS
### MGNREGA vs VB-G RAM G Act 2025
- Viksit Bharat–Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025 — replaced MGNREGA from 1 July 2026
- 125 days per household; programme operated over 305 days
- States may pause works up to 60 days in sowing/harvest seasons
- Administrative cost ceiling — 6% under MGNREGA, raised to 9%

### MGNREGA, 2005 — Act, objective, entitlement and wages
## The Act
- ★ Enacted 2005 — passed 23 August 2005, notified 7 September 2005, in force 2 February 2006 in 200 backward districts
- ★ NREGA renamed MGNREGA — 2 October 2009
- ★ Legal guarantee of 100 days of wage employment
- ★ Eligible — adult members of any (rural) household
- ★ Does not guarantee 100 days to every adult member — the guarantee is per household
- ★ Largest rural employment programme — MGNREGA; VB-G RAM G Act passed December 2025 to overhaul it
