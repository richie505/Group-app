### Finance Commission (Art. 280)
- 16th Finance Commission shifts transfers from entitlement to performance
- Quasi-judicial body; President, every 5 years; Chairman + 4 members
- Qualifications: FC (Misc. Provisions) Act, 1951; report before Parliament (Art. 281)
- 280(3)(a): share net tax proceeds - vertical + horizontal
- (b): grants-in-aid principles (Art. 275); (bb), (c): Panchayats & Municipalities (73rd/74th, 1992)
- Not its job: maintaining income & expenditure accounts (APPSC-G2 2017); not GST, cesses

### Finance Commission — Article 280 provisions and functions — Constitution and composition
## 1. Constitution and composition
| Article / Act | Provision |
| Art. 280 | Quasi-judicial body constituted by the President every 5 years (5th year) or earlier |
| Art. 281 | Report + action-taken memorandum laid before Parliament |
| Qualifications | Finance Commission (Misc. Provisions) Act, 1951 |
## 2. Functions under Article 280 - what it does
| Art. 280(3) | Function |
| (a) | Distribution of net proceeds of shareable taxes: Centre-States (vertical) and among States (horizontal) |
| (b) | Principles for grants-in-aid (Art. 275) from the Consolidated Fund of India |
| (bb), (c) | Measures to augment a State's Consolidated Fund to support Panchayats and Municipalities - added by 73rd and 74th Amendments, 1992; linked to Art. 243I/243Y |
| (d) | Any matter referred by the President - e.g. disaster-management financing under the Disaster Management Act, 2005 (15th FC terms of reference) (UPSC) |
### Finance Commission — Article 280 provisions and functions — What it does NOT do
## 1. What it does NOT do
- Advisory: Not binding on the government
- Not covered: Cesses/surcharges; GST revenue sharing (GST Council); plan/discretionary grants
- Not its job: Maintaining income and expenditure accounts of Centre and States (APPSC-G2 2017 key)
## 2. Sixteenth Finance Commission (16th FC)
- Timeline:
- Constituted 31 Dec 2023
- Award period 1 Apr 2026 – 31 Mar 2031
- Accepted in Budget 2026-27
- Transparency: Recommends annual CAG-certified disclosure of net tax proceeds
- Exam angle: "the present Finance Commission" now means the Sixteenth.
### Terms → meaning: devolution, divisible pool, channels of transfer
## 1. Devolution terms
| Term | Meaning |
| Vertical devolution | Sharing of net tax proceeds between Union and States |
| Horizontal devolution | Distribution of States' share among States |
| Divisible pool | Taxes under Art. 270 (corporation tax, income tax, CGST, Centre's IGST share); excludes cesses, surcharges + cost of collection → shrinking base |
| Tax devolution | tax devolution (Art. 270), untied |
| Grants | grants (Art. 275 statutory, Art. 282 discretionary): Finance Commission grants = Art. 275; other transfers = Art. 282 |
| Centrally Sponsored Schemes (CSS) | with matching share |
## 2. Divisible pool — data
- Share of gross tax revenue: 89.2% (FC-13) → 82.1% (FC-14) → 78.3% (FC-15) (11 Mar 2026)
- About 81% in 2025-26 (12 Feb 2026)
- 80th Amendment, 2000 (from 1 Apr 1996): Made all central taxes shareable

### Articles 275 vs 282 grants and CSS
## 1. Statutory vs discretionary grants
| Feature | Art. 275 statutory grants | Art. 282 discretionary grants |
| Recommended by | Finance Commission | Union ministries (earlier Planning Commission) |
| Charged on CFI | Yes | No |
| Nature | Revenue-deficit, local body, disaster grants; ST welfare/Scheduled Areas under Art. 275(1) | Mostly tied, scheme-based - route for most CSS |
- Exam angle: "Grants under Art. 275 are charged on CFI" (true); "Art. 282 needs FC recommendation" (false).
## 2. Centrally Sponsored Schemes (CSS)
- Nature: Centre-designed schemes (MGNREGA/VB-G RAM G, PMAY, NHM) needing State matching share
- CSS allocation 2026-27: ₹5,48,798 crore (+30.6%)
- 16th FC: CSS linked to measurable outcomes; high-powered committee to close inefficient schemes

### Gadgil and Gadgil-Mukherjee formulae → criteria weights
## 1. Formula-based plan assistance
- Normal Central Assistance: For State Plans, distributed by formula
- Endorsement: National Development Council in 1968-69; used from the 4th Plan (1969)
- Gadgil formula (1969, Fourth Plan):
- Special Category States (initially Assam, J&K, Nagaland) = first claim; 90% grant and 10% loan
- General-category states = 30% grant and 70% loan
- Gadgil-Mukherjee (1991): Revised formula — plan assistance weights below
| Criterion | Gadgil (1969) [GK] | Gadgil-Mukherjee (1991) [GK] |
| Population | 60% | 60% |
| Per capita income | 10% | 25% |
| Tax effort / fiscal performance | 10% | 7.5% |
| Ongoing irrigation and power projects | 10% | - |
| Special problems | 10% | 7.5% |
- Exam angle: highest weight under Gadgil-Mukherjee = population (UPPSC 2008).
- ⚠ Special Category 90:10 grant-loan vs general 30:70
## 2. End of plan transfers
- Abolition: Formula transfers ended when NITI Aayog replaced the Planning Commission (1 Jan 2015); lapsed with the Planning Commission (2015)
- Parallel channels: Plan grants from Planning Commission ran alongside Finance Commission transfers until abolition
- Art. 282 grants: Criticised as political

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 3:100
### Finance Commission (Art. 280)
## Constitution and report
- FC formed by the President every fifth year or earlier; quasi-judicial; recommendations advisory
- Article 281 — President places the FC report before each House of Parliament with an explanatory memorandum on action taken
## Duties under Article 280(3)
- Clause (b) — principles for grants-in-aid under Article 275
- Clause (d) — any other matter referred by the President
- Boosting State Consolidated Fund for Panchayats and Municipalities — added by 73rd and 74th Amendments (1992)
- NOT an FC duty: maintaining income-expenditure accounts of Union and States
- GST revenue sharing — handled by the GST Council, not the FC
## Concepts
- Vertical devolution — sharing net tax proceeds between Union and States
- Horizontal devolution — sharing the States' share among individual States
- Article 275 grants — for revenue deficits, local bodies, disasters, tribal welfare; NOT for funding every CSS
## Sixteenth FC and recent
- 16th FC — tied CSS to measurable outcomes; proposed a high-powered committee to close inefficient schemes
- 16th FC — State fiscal deficit capped at 3% of GSDP
- SASCI — 50-year interest-free capex loans; stopped pro-cyclical compression of State capital expenditure (Economic Survey)
### States' share of divisible pool
| Finance Commission | States' share |
| 10th | 29% |
| 11th | 29.5% |
| 12th | 30.5% |
| 13th | 32% |
| 14th | 42% |
| 15th | 41% |
- Cut from 42% to 41% (15th FC) — because Jammu and Kashmir becoming a Union Territory
### Channels of transfer
- Tax devolution (Article 270) — untied transfer
- Article 282 grants — discretionary
- Centrally Sponsored Schemes — designed by the Centre, need a matching State share
- 16th FC discontinued revenue-deficit, sector-specific and State-specific grants
- "41% illusion" — cesses and surcharges lie outside the divisible pool, so stated share exaggerates States' real resources
### Gadgil formula weights
| Criterion | Weight |

### Divisible pool & grants data
## Divisible pool
- FC recommendations — advisory, not binding on the Government
- 80th Amendment (2000) — put into effect 10th FC's Alternative Scheme of Devolution (29%); made all central taxes shareable, from 1 April 1996
- Excluded from divisible pool — cesses and surcharges, and cost of collection; pool taxes shared under Article 270
- Divisible pool — about 81% of gross tax revenue in 2025–26
- Biggest-ever rise in States' share — 14th FC (32% to 42%, +10 points)
- 16th FC retained 41%; demand of 18 of 28 States for 50% not accepted
- 16th FC — move from entitlement-based to performance-oriented transfers
## Transfer channels and grants
- Channels (Economic Survey): tax devolution, FC grants, CSS and other transfers; Article 123 (ordinance power) is NOT a channel
- Article 282 grants — discretionary, mostly tied, sent through Union ministries
- Article 275 grants — statutory, charged on the CFI
- 15th FC revenue-deficit, sector-specific and State-specific grants — about ₹10.1 lakh crore (vs ₹9.47 lakh crore under 16th FC)
- Recommended to be notified as disasters — heatwave and lightning
- 15th FC — ₹4,800 crore (2022-23 to 2025-26) to reward educational outcomes
## Gadgil formula
- Started in 1969; formula-based plan transfers ended when NITI Aayog replaced Planning Commission (1 January 2015)
- First Special Category States — Assam, Jammu and Kashmir, Nagaland
- Special Category States — 90% grant, 10% loan; general-category States — 30% grant, 70% loan
### Exam corner
- 16th FC — contribution to GDP weight 10% (blamed for rewarding size, not need)
- Tax effort criterion (2.5% under 15th FC) — dropped by 16th FC
