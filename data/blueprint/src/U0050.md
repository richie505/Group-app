### Inscriptions → issuer and religious content
## 1. Naneghat inscription
- Issuer: Devi Naganika
- Language: Prakrit
- Yajnas recorded: Satakarni I performed two Ashvamedhas + a Rajasuya
- Titles of Satakarni I: Apratihatachakra, Dakshinapathapati
- Deities invoked: Indra, Vasudeva, Sankarshana, Surya, Chandra, Yama, Varuna
- Vedic gods = Indra, Yama, Varuna
- Bhagavata deities = Vasudeva, Sankarshana
## 2. Other inscriptions
| Inscription | Content |
| Nasik inscription | Calls Gautamiputra Agama nilaya (abode of traditional learning) |
| Alluru inscription | Of the Ikshvaku Virapurushadatta; records the emperor's donations to many communities and institutions (G2 2017) |
- ⚠ Trap: Alluru distractors = Karle, Veerapuram, Ramagiri
### Gods and temples → Bhagavata religion and Shiva worship
## 1. Bhagavata religion
- Founder: Vasudeva-Krishna; centred at Mathura
- Spread: Reached Andhra under Krishna (the 2nd king) → became the "Vasudeva religion" by Hala's time
- Symbols: Gautamiputra's inscriptions carry Bhagavata symbols
## 2. Shiva worship and Gudimallam
- Parasurameswara temple, Gudimallam (Chittoor; now Tirupati district):
- Has the oldest Shivalinga of the Satavahana period
- Called the oldest Shiva temple (G2 2008)
- ⚠ Distractors: Thondawada, Renigunta, Jogulamba
### Buddhism in Andhra → arrival, sects and centres
- Arrival: Buddhism reached Andhra in the 6th century BC
- Tibetan tradition: Buddha preached at Amaravati at the request of king Subendran
- Local form: "Andhra Buddhism"
- Doctrine followed: Pratitya Samutpada
| Sect | Centre / note |
| Chaityaka (Chaityakavada) | Founded by Mahadeva Bhikshu, the first Buddhist acharya in Andhra; centred on the worship of chaityas |
| Mahasanghika | Main centre Dhanyakataka; its followers were called Mahasanghis / Chaityakas |
| Purvasaila, Aparasaila | Hills around Dhanyakataka[GK] |
| Rajagirika | Sriparvatam (G2 2012 key) |
| Bhadrayaniya | Nasik (Gautami Balasri's gift) |
- Sri Parvataswamy: = the Buddha, identified by Kielhorn (G2 2019 key)
- ⚠ Trap: some notes credit N. Venkataramanayya
- Later: the Vishnukundins' family deity was also Sriparvataswami

## 2. Kings → Vedic faith
- Kings who performed yajnas: Satakarni I, Satakarni II, Gautamiputra, Yajna Sri
- Names as evidence: Vedasri and Yajnasri themselves show Vedic faith
- ⚠ Apratihatachakra / Dakshinapathapati = Satakarni I; Agama nilaya = Gautamiputra
## 3. Patron → gift to the sangha
- Royal women: Patronised both Buddhism and Jainism
| Patron | Gift |
| Gautami Balasri | Cave vihara at Nasik for the Bhadrayaniya sect, for her son's recovery |
| Gautamiputra Satakarni | Village at Tirasmri and 100 nivartanas to monks; toranas of Sanchi |
| Pulumavi II | Village to Mahasanghika monks at Karle |
| Yajna Sri Satakarni | Sriparvata vihara for Nagarjuna |

- Location: Nasik, Karle and Kanheri caves are in Maharashtra
- Support base: Public > royal support
- Guild banking: Guilds at Nasik, Junnar and Kanheri held money on deposit → interest to monks
- Example: Ushavadata's deposit with the Govardhana weavers

### Universal angles
- Current relevance (Jun 2026):
- Dynasty patronised both Brahmanism (Ashvamedha) and Buddhism (Amaravati, Nagarjunakonda)

- Religious pluralism — Ashvamedha alongside Amaravati and Nagarjunakonda

- Gautami Balasri — cave vihara at Nasik for the Bhadrayaniya sect (reportedly for her son's healing)

- Pulumavi II — village to monks at Karle; Krishna — a Nasik cave

### Brahmanism & Bhagavata religion
- ★ Originator of Bhagavata religion — Krishna (Vasudeva), Vrishni (Satvat) descendant, from Mathura; son of Devaki, disciple of Ghor Angirasa (Chhandogya Upanishad)
- Bhagavata religion centred at Mathura
- Yajna-performing rulers — Satakarni I, Satakarni II, Gautamiputra, Yajna Sri; names Vedasri, Yajnasri show Vedic links

- Samayasara (Jain) — Kundakundacharya, who preached Syadvada
