### Ethnicity — definitions, types and data
| Term | Meaning |
| Ethnic group | People identifying with each other on common language/dialect, history, society, culture or nation |
| Markers | Language, religion, race, region, culture |
| National minority | Non-dominant citizen group with own ethnic, religious or linguistic identity; term from European minority-rights law |
| Intra-ethnic conflict | Within a group |
| Inter-ethnic conflict | Between groups |
- Approaches:
- Consensus: groups coexist with separate identities
- Conflict: groups as interest groups competing unequally (South Africa, Sri Lankan Tamils)
