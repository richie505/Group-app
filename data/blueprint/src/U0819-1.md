### RoDTEP scheme extended till 31 December 2026

- Effective from: 1 January 2021; replaced MEIS (WTO objections) — WTO-compliant
- Notified by: Dept of Commerce via DGFT; implemented by Dept of Revenue via CBIC
### First post-Independence international cargo voyage from Bogibeel

### Panchayat-level climate action
## 1. Key points
- Climate plans to be integrated into Gram Panchayat Development Plan (GPDP) — no parallel bureaucracy
- Bela Gram Panchayat (Maharashtra): 90,000+ trees, solar panels, waste segregation — ⚠ called India's first net-zero panchayat in source
- Initiatives in Uttar Pradesh and Karnataka
## 2. Background
- 73rd Amendment — Part IX; Article 243G — devolution for economic development and social justice
- Eleventh Schedule — agriculture, minor irrigation, social forestry, drinking water, non-conventional energy etc.
- PESA Act, 1996 — Gram Sabha protects community resources in Scheduled Areas
- NAPCC: 2008, 8 national missions; SAPCCs at State level
### Drug-free India by 2029
## 1. News
- Amit Shah at a high-level meeting in Panaji, Goa — action plan for drug-free India by 2029; linked to Viksit Bharat 2047
- Target whole networks and supply chains, not just small offenders; four-tier NCORD (Narco-Coordination Centre) strengthened
## 2. Background
- NCB: 1986, MHA; NDPS Act, 1985
- Article 47 (DPSP) — prohibition of intoxicating substances except medicinal
- Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan: 2020, Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment
- International Day Against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking: June 26
### Digital Communications Commission abolished
## 1. News
- Centre abolished the Digital Communications Commission (DCC) — top telecom decision body for 37 years
- Projects of ₹500–1,000 crore that DCC examined now go to the Expenditure Finance Committee (Finance Ministry)
- Unchanged: Telecom Secretary approves up to ₹100 crore; Telecom Minister up to ₹500 crore
## 2. Background
- Former name Telecom Commission — est. 1989, renamed DCC 2018; under DoT, Ministry of Communications
### Mission Rangeen Machhli–2031
## 1. News
- Strategic action plan released by the Vice-President at Agatti, Lakshadweep — ornamental fisheries
- Department of Fisheries; linked with PMMSY; Lakshadweep capital Kavaratti
- National standards for broodstock, breeding, hatcheries, quarantine; traceability and certification for captive-bred fish (stop illegal coral-reef collection)
- Clusters with air-cargo links; training for coastal fishers, youth, women SHGs
