### Sapta Sindhu
- Land of seven rivers: Sindhu, Vitasta, Asikni, Parushni, Vipas, Sutudri, Sarasvati
- Extent: eastern Afghanistan, NWFP, Punjab, Haryana, fringes of western UP
- Early Vedic core = Punjab-Haryana; Later Vedic = Ganga-Yamuna Doab

### Rig Vedic tribes — Panchajana grouping and odd-one-out
- Panchajana (five tribes): Yadus, Turvasas, Anus, Druhyus, Purus
- Other tribes: Bharatas, Tritsu, Krivis, Srinjayas
- Bharatavarsha: Country's name traced to the Bharata tribe
- Kikatas: Placed in south Bihar/Magadha; not part of Panchajana
- ⚠ Trap: Usual odd-one-out
- Exam angle: "Which tribe is not in the Panchajana?" - Kikata.
### Dasarajna (Battle of Ten Kings) — site, persons, outcome
| Item | Fact |
| River site | Banks of the Parushni (Ravi) |
| Victor | Bharatas under king Sudas (Tritsu family) |
| Priest of Sudas | Vasishtha |
| Outcome | Bharatas dominant; defeated Purus later allied with them to form the Kurus; Kurus + Panchalas later ruled the upper Gangetic basin |
- Cause (traditional): Rivalry of two priests - Vasishtha vs Vishvamitra
- Historicity: Likely historical core (tribal confederacies competing for resources), but Rigveda = religious poetry, not a chronicle
- ⚠ Trap: Vasishtha = priest of the victor Sudas; Vishvamitra = priest of the losing confederacy
### Universal angles
- Significance: Sapta Sindhu geography → Early Vedic heartland placed in north-west, before eastward move to the Doab
- Comparative note: Early Vedic core = Punjab-Haryana vs Later Vedic core = Ganga-Yamuna Doab, eastern UP, Bihar (Kosala, Videha)
