### Munagala and Challapalli struggles → place, zamindar, events
## 1. Munagala paragana struggle, 1930-39
- Location: Nandigama taluk, Krishna district then; now Suryapet/Nalgonda (back to Nalgonda 1959)
- Zamindar: Nayani Venkata Ranga Rao - literary patron but harsh landlord
- Kalukova Satyagraha, 1938: Over a paddy wage of 80 seers per acre; Sub-Collector made the zamindar pay
- Brahmaiah Award, 15 Jan 1939: Gottipati Brahmaiah mediated settlement covering 142 villages
- Outcome:
- Munagala Day 11 June 1939
- Withdrawn 19 June 1939 after Prakasam and Ranga promised action

### Kisan leaders
## N.G. Ranga
- ★ Led peasant and agrarian movements in Andhra; founded the Indian Peasants Institute, Nidubrolu; led the Rythu Padayatra
- ★ Title — Rythu Bandhava / Rythu Bandhu; also Acharya; founder of the Andhra Rashtra Rythu Sangham
- Father of the Indian Peasant Movement
- Padma Vibhushan, 1991 (not Bharat Ratna)
- Books — Credo of World Peasantry, Revolutionary Peasantry, Fight for Freedom
- 1929 memorandum to the Whitley Commission — minimum wages
- Led the 1935 Guntur anti-resettlement movement
## Gottipati Brahmaiah
- ★ Pioneer of the Zamindari Ryot Movement; Secretary of the APCC
- Authored the Munagala Award (not a book of Ranga)
- Led the 1931 Divi Taluk Resettlement Mahasabha movement
## Others in Andhra
- Kommareddi Satyanarayana — village peasant associations (1935); led the 1937 Rythu Yatra
- Srikakulam kisan leaders — Pulleti Shyamasundara Rao, Gouthu Latchanna
## Swami Sahajanand Saraswati
- ★ Peasant movement in Bihar; founded the Bihar Provincial Kisan Sabha (1929); closely linked with the Kisan Sabha movement of the 1930s
- ★ Newspapers — Lok Sangraha; Hindi weekly Hunkar (Patna)
- ★ All India Kisan Sabha — founded April 1936, Lucknow: President Sahajanand, Secretary N.G. Ranga
- ★ Just before his death (1950) formed the All India United Kisan Sabha — demand: Nationalization of Land and Waterways
- Oudh Kisan Sabha (1920) — Nehru and Baba Ramchandra
### Associations
- Andhra Rashtra Rythu Sangham — founded 1928; merged into the AIKS in 1936
- founders Ranga, Narasimha Devara Satyanarayana, Maganti Bapineedu; first General Secretary B.V. Ratnam
- 1943 — Ranga left the AIKS (communist-led, People's War line) and founded the Andhra Rashtra Kisan Congress
### Munagala vs Challapalli
- Munagala paragana — then in Nandigama taluk, Krishna district (now Suryapet/Nalgonda)
- zamindar Nayani Venkata Ranga Rao — supported writers; harsh landlord
- Kalukova Satyagraha (1938) — Nanduri Prasada Rao; paddy wage of 80 seers per acre; Sub-Collector made the zamindar pay
- Challapalli struggle chronology:
- 1939 — zamindar grabbed 600 acres of temple land
- 1942 — Rajeswara Rao and Chapanayya
- 1946 — Kishkindapalem firing, four killed
- July 1947 — farmers occupied the zamindar's surplus land
- PM-KISAN — extended (August 2026) up to 2030-31, outlay Rs 3.15 lakh crore
### Rythu Rakshana Yatra
- Ichchapuram-Madras Rythu Rakshana Yatra (1937) — compared to the Dandi March
- ended with a memorandum to Speaker Bulusu Sambamurthy (Prakasam then Revenue Minister)
- AIKS — peasant wing of the Congress; demanded abolition of zamindari and cancellation of debts
