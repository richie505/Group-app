### Chenchu village offices
| Office | Clan |
| Raju | Bhoomani |
| Mantri | Kumakula |

### Chenchu social organisation
- Chenchus — endogamous tribe with exogamous clans (kulams/gothrams); patriarchal, patrilineal
- Ghotul (youth dormitory) — Muria of Chhattisgarh
### Economy, art & crafts
- Idital wall paintings — Savara (Sora)
- Pithora painting — Rathwa of Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh
- Araku agency economy — podu (shifting cultivation), minor forest produce, coffee, horticulture
### Festivals
- Eetela Panduga / Chaitrapurab — spring festival of the Araku tribes; Dhimsa danced
- Lambadis call Deepavali Kalimas; Jari Gundu = Heli Panduga
- Modakondamma — goddess of the Paderu agency; annual tribal jatara
### Dances
| Dance | Community |

| Gussadi | Gond (not Lambada) |

### Exam corner
- Guinness record, largest Dhimsa dance (July 2026) — Alluri Sitarama Raju International Airport, Bhogapuram (Vizianagaram); 13,000+ tribal women and girls; certificate to AP Tribal Welfare Dept
