### Castes, communities and social divisions → occupation
- Structure: Four varnas + about 18 castes
| Group | Occupation / notes |
| Brahmins | Vaidikas / Vaidikulu (priests) vs Niyogis / Niyogulu (officials, administrators) |
| Vipravinodins | Brahmin jugglers/magicians (conjurers) who entertained only Brahmins and lived on their gifts (APPSC G2 2017, 2019) |
| Settis (Chettis), Komatis, Balijas | Trade: Arya Vaishyas (vegetarian) and Kalinga Vaishyas, Shetti Balija, Veera Balija (CDX) |
| Sudras | Farmers = the "backbone" of the kingdom; many became Amaranayakas (per Manucharitra) |
| Vira Panchalas / Panchanam | Artisans: gold, iron, brass, wood and stone workers (metalworkers) [GK]; CDX's Panchanam community had 74 sub-sects (counted in an inscription of Devaraya I) |
| Weavers | Padmasali, Jandra, Kuruba; called Kaikkolar |
| Valangai (right-hand) | Mainly agricultural and trading groups (agrarian) |
| Idangai (left-hand) | Mainly artisans (the Panchala smiths) + some traders (artisan and mercantile) |
- Origin: Began in Chola times → continued under Vijayanagara
- ⚠ Vaidikas = priests; Niyogis = officials
### Women and social customs → terms and poetesses
- Freedom: No strict purdah; respectable women attended the Mahanavami; women served as bodyguards, wrestlers, accountants, even judges
