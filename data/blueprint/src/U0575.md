### Central assistance terms → meaning — Gadgil formula, CS vs CSS, SNA-SPARSH — CS vs CSS
## 1. CS vs CSS
- Definition: CSS = Centre-designed schemes on State subjects needing a matching State share
| Central Sector (CS) | Centrally Sponsored (CSS) |
| Union List subjects | State/Concurrent subjects |
| 100% Centre-funded, run by Central Ministry | Shared 60:40 for general States (90:10 special category/NE/Himalayan States) |
| PM-KISAN, Ayushman Bharat, MPLADS | NHM, PMAY, ICDS, PMKSY |
| 17% of Union spending (Budget 2026-27) | 8% of Union spending |

- Appraisal cycle: New 5-year CSS/CS cycle from 1 Apr 2026, aligned with 16th FC; 16th FC wants CSS tied to measurable outcomes
- ⚠ PM-KISAN / Ayushman Bharat = CS; NHM / PMAY = CSS
### Central assistance terms → meaning — Gadgil formula, CS vs CSS, SNA-SPARSH — SNA-SPARSH
## 1. SNA-SPARSH
- Mechanism: "Just-in-time" release of CSS funds from Centre & State Consolidated Funds via State IFMIS, RBI e-Kuber and PFMS
- Coverage: All 28 States and 3 UTs; 66 notified CSS
- Impact: "Credit-push" → "debit-pull" cut idle float Rs. 1.67 lakh Cr → Rs. 0.4 lakh Cr (ES 2025-26)

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 3:131
### Central Sector vs CSS
- Plan era: State Plans funded by own resources + Normal Central Assistance (Gadgil / Gadgil-Mukherjee formula)
- Centrally Sponsored Schemes — State or Concurrent subjects; jointly funded by Centre and States
- Central Sector Schemes — 100% Centre-funded, run by Central Ministries
| Scheme | Type |
| PM-KISAN | Central Sector |
| Ayushman Bharat | Central Sector |
| NHM | Centrally Sponsored |
| ICDS | Centrally Sponsored |
- APSES 2024-25 fiscal stress: lesser than anticipated flow of funds from the Government of India
- Sixteenth Finance Commission wants outcome-linked CSS
### Constitutional channels
- Article 275 — statutory grants: revenue-deficit, local-body and disaster-related grants
- Article 282 — discretionary grants
- Article 280(3)(bb),(c) — local bodies
### Devolution & SCS
- States' share in divisible pool: 32% → 42% (14th FC) → 41% (15th FC) → 41% (16th FC retained)
- 15th FC's cut to 41% — due to the change about Jammu and Kashmir
- Special Category Status began in 1969 on advice of the Fifth Finance Commission
- 14th FC (2015) stopped distinguishing special and general category States
### Gadgil formula & SNA-SPARSH
## Normal Central Assistance and sharing patterns
- Gadgil-Mukherjee NCA loan:grant — general category States 70:30; Special Category States 10:90
- CSS sharing: Special Category and Himalayan States 90:10; general States 60:40
- Fourteenth FC did not distinguish Special and General Category States in its devolution formula
- Criticism of CSS: matching shares may squeeze the fiscal space of States
## Budget and fund flow
- From 2017-18: Plan and Non-Plan distinction dropped (Outcome Budget came earlier, 2005)
- SNA-SPARSH — just-in-time release of CSS funds via State IFMIS, RBI e-Kuber and PFMS
- VB-G RAM G Act replaced MGNREGA; 60:40 Centre-State pattern for most States (MGNREGA: Centre paid 100% of unskilled wages)
### Package in lieu of SCS
- SCS not given; Special Assistance Measure (SAM) of Rs 24,350 crore instead
- RD grant within SAM: Rs 3,979 crore
- Centre pays 90% of cost of externally aided projects under the package
- Polavaram — Centre to fund as national project; older estimate Rs 55,548 crore; releases delayed
### Exam corner
- 2024-25 RE: grants fell below BE, but total central resources rose due to EAP loans
