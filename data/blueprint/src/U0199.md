### Persons → contributions: Surendranath Banerjee and Gazula Lakshmi Narasu Chetty
## 1. Surendranath Banerjee
- ICS episode:
- Passed the ICS (1869) → disqualified over an age dispute → reinstated
- Dismissed in 1874 on a minor irregularity
- British cut the ICS maximum age (21 to 19) to shut Indians out → he toured the country against it
- Organisations:
- Indian Association (1876) with A.M. Bose
- Convened the Indian National Conference (1883, 1885) → merged with the INC
- Stimulus in the South: S.N. Banerjee's tour for the Indian Association (1878) stirred sentiment
- Title: "Rashtraguru"
- Paper: The Bengalee
- INC president: Poona 1895 + Ahmedabad 1902
## 2. Gazula Lakshmi Narasu Chetty
- Founder + first president of the Madras Native Association (26 Feb 1852)
### Pre-Congress associations of Madras and Andhra → founders, years and activities — Madras Native Association
## 1. Madras Native Association
- Madras Native Association: first Madras political body; first such organisation in South India
- Founded: 26 Feb 1852 by Gazula Lakshmi Narasu Chetty - founder + first president
- Secretary: Somasundaram Chettiyar
- Origin: Adjunct of the British Indian Association → later independent
## 2. Madras Mahajana Sabha
- Madras Mahajana Sabha: second major body
- Founded: 16 May 1884
- Office-bearers: P. Rangaiah Naidu president; P. Anandacharlu secretary
- Other founders: M. Viraraghavachariar, G. Subramania Iyer
- Demands: Larger councils + more Indian representation
- Exam: APPSC-GS 2025 - 1852 and 1884 are correct
## 3. Kakinada (Coconada) Literary Association
- Memorandums (1884-85) to raise the ICS age limit + hold simultaneous exams
