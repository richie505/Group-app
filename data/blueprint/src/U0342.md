| # | Condition |

| Route | Condition |

### Laws, paragraphs and cases on registration and recognition
## 1. Political parties
- Registration: By the Election Commission of India under Section 29A of the RPA 1951
- ⚠ Trap: Not the Ministry of Law and Justice, not Parliament
- Recognition (National or State party) and allotment of symbols: Decided by the ECI under the Election Symbols (Reservation and Allotment) Order, 1968
- ⚠ Trap: The Order is not issued by Parliament, the Law Ministry or the President
- Disputes: The ECI adjudicates disputes concerning party mergers and splits
| Step | Law | Authority |
| Registration | Section 29A, RPA 1951 | ECI |
| Recognition (National/State) | Election Symbols (Reservation and Allotment) Order, 1968 | ECI |
- Nature of 1968 Order: Statutory order of the ECI under RPA 1951 and Article 324
- Not an Act of Parliament, not a constitutional provision
| Paragraph / case / report | Provision |
| INC v Institute of Social Welfare (2002) | ECI's power to deregister is limited (fraud, ceasing allegiance, unlawful declaration) |
| Law Commission 255th report | wants a limited deregistration power |
| Split disputes - Para 15 | ECI decides which faction is the party; test: Sadiq Ali (1971) test of majority |
| Para 16A | ECI may suspend or withdraw recognition for defying the Model Code of Conduct |
- ECI acts as a court in recognition and symbol disputes
### Registration — stages and conditions
- Three states of a party: Unregistered → registered but unrecognised (RUPP) → recognised
- Registration:
- Party constitution must pledge allegiance to the Constitution + socialism, secularism, democracy, national integrity
### National party criteria — paragraph 6B of the Symbols Order
## 1. National Party — any one criterion
- Seats: At least 2% of the total Lok Sabha seats (= 11 seats), from at least three different States
- Votes + seats: At least 6% of the total valid votes polled in four or more States in a Lok Sabha general election or a State Legislative Assembly election — and at least four Lok Sabha seats from any State or States
- Route 2: at least 6% of votes in four states and four Lok Sabha seats — the two recognition conditions under Route 2 are both mandatory
- State recognition: Officially recognized as a State Party in at least four different States
| # | Condition |
| 1 | Recognised as a State party in four or more States |
| 2 | Polled at least 6% of valid votes in four or more States at the last Lok Sabha or Assembly elections and won at least 4 Lok Sabha seats |
| 3 | Won at least 2% of total Lok Sabha seats (11 of 543) from not fewer than three States |
- Rule: National party if it satisfies any one of three conditions
- Memory hook: 4 / 6+4 / 2%-3
- Exam angle: distractors use "2 or more States", "4% / 10% / 15% of votes" - all wrong; APPSC-G2 2025: every listed condition was valid, so the key was "None of the above".
### National parties — number, list and FIRSTs
- May 2020: Eight national parties (APPSC 2019 key)
- ECI review (April 2023):
- Derecognised: CPI, NCP and AITC
- Recognised: AAP (national status in 2023)
- NPP (Meghalaya): First North-East party with national status
- Not National parties: DMK, Revolutionary Socialist Party, All India Forward Bloc, Peasants and Workers Party (APPSC 2018)

### State party criteria — vote share and seat combinations
| Route | Condition |
| 1 | 6% valid votes in the Assembly election + 2 Assembly seats |
| 2 | 6% valid votes in the Lok Sabha election + 1 Lok Sabha seat from the State |
| 3 | 3% of Assembly seats or 3 seats, whichever is more [GK] |
| 4 | 1 Lok Sabha seat for every 25 allotted to the State [GK] |
| 5 | 8% of valid votes in the State - no seat needed |
- 8% route: Added later; the one candidates forget
### Symbols — terms and recognition benefits
## 1. Terms → meaning
| Term | Meaning |
| Reserved symbols | Exclusively for recognised parties (e.g. Lotus - BJP, Hand - INC); two recognised parties in different States may hold the same symbol |
| Free symbols | For RUPPs + independents, from a notified list; latest list: May 2025 - 184 symbols |
| Common symbol for RUPPs | If they contest 2 Lok Sabha seats or 5% of Assembly seats |
## 2. Benefits
| Benefit | Recognised party | RUPP |
| Symbol | Reserved symbol | Common symbol on conditions |
| Proposers for nomination | One (instead of ten) | Ten |
| Star campaigners (travel outside candidate's expense limit) | Up to 40 | 20 |
- Other benefits:
- Two free sets of electoral rolls
- Broadcast/telecast time on Akashvani and Doordarshan
- Consultation by the ECI
## 3. RUPP obligations
- Tax exemption on donations: Section 12, Income Tax Act 2025
- Disclose donors above Rs 20,000
- Above Rs 2,000 only by cheque/bank transfer
- Must contest at least once in six years or face delisting
- ⚠ Proposers 1 vs 10; star campaigners 40 vs 20

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 2:68
### Three states of a party
- Three statuses: unregistered; registered but unrecognised (RUPP); recognised
- ★ 2016 (2 Sept): Trinamool Congress got National Party status (no longer a National Party now)
- ★ Aam Aadmi Party — a National Party
### Who decides
- Election Commission does both: registration (Section 29A, RPA 1951) and recognition (Election Symbols Order, 1968)
- Registration is under Sec 29A, NOT under the Symbols Order (which covers allotment of symbols)
- Paragraph 15: split disputes over name/symbol — Sadiq Ali test (majority support of factions)
- Paragraph 16A: suspend/withdraw recognition for breaking the Model Code of Conduct
### National party: any one
## Registration
- ECI's power to deregister is limited — used e.g. for fraud or ending allegiance
- Sec 29A: party constitution must promise loyalty to the Constitution and to socialism, secularism, democracy (and national integrity)
- Reserved symbols only for recognised parties; free symbols for RUPPs and independents
## National party routes
- 2% of Lok Sabha seats (11) from at least three States
- 6% votes in four States and four Lok Sabha seats (both mandatory)
### State party: any one
- 6% of valid votes in Assembly election + 2 Assembly seats
- or 6% in Lok Sabha poll + 1 Lok Sabha seat
- Seat route: 1 Lok Sabha seat per 25 seats allotted to the State
- 8% valid-vote route: no seat needed
- Any one route suffices
### National parties
- April 2023 review: CPI, NCP, AITC derecognised; AAP recognised
- NPP (Meghalaya) — first North-Eastern party to get National Party status
- Not National Parties: DMK, RSP, All India Forward Bloc, Peasants and Workers Party
### Recognised vs RUPP
| Benefit | Recognised party | RUPP |
| Proposers for nomination | 1 | 10 |
| Star campaigners | 40 | 20 |
- Recognised parties also get two free sets of electoral rolls, air time on Akashvani and Doordarshan, consultation by ECI
- RUPP must contest at least once in six years or be delisted
### Exam corner

- Not a National Party criterion: 15% votes in two States without a seat
- Valid: recognition in four States; 6% + four seats; 2% Lok Sabha seats
- RUPP donations: donor disclosure above Rs 20,000; above Rs 2,000 only by cheque/bank transfer
- AP: TDP and YSRCP — recognised State Parties (neither national)
