### All-India trend
- Tendulkar 2011-12 — rural 25.7%, urban 13.7%, all-India 21.9%; rural 33.8% in 2009-10
### Recent estimates
- World Bank $3-a-day extreme poverty — 27.1% (2011–12) → 5.3% (2022–23)

### Regional pattern
- Highest poverty ratio (Tendulkar 2011-12) — Chhattisgarh (39.93%); largest number of poor — Uttar Pradesh, not Bihar
- Concentrated-poverty belt — Bihar, UP, MP, Rajasthan, Jharkhand, Odisha, Chhattisgarh (not Kerala)
- National MPI 2023 (NFHS-5) — highest Bihar (0.16, 28th); lowest Kerala (0.002), then Goa and Tamil Nadu
- Highest poverty among social groups — Scheduled Tribes (rural ST about 45%), then Scheduled Castes
### AP vs India
- AP (Tendulkar 2011-12) — 9.20% (rural 10.96%, urban 5.81%) vs India 21.92%
### AP districts (MPI)
- Poorer regions — Rayalaseema and North Coastal Andhra; delta districts least poor
- Rayalaseema's agricultural poverty — depends on rain-fed agriculture; delta has better irrigation and productivity
