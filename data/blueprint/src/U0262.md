- States Reorganisation Commission: Set up on 22 December 1953 — ⚠ Kailashnath Wanchoo was not a member

- 29 December 1953 (29 Dec 1953): Formally constituted by Home Ministry Resolution No. 53/69/53 Public

- Use 22 Dec (announcement) / 29 Dec (resolution); in the exam, mark whichever appears among the options
- ⚠ Trap: "August 1953" (some notes, RPSC 2023) and "1954" (some notes) — both WRONG for the SRC

### Andhra State 1953 and Andhra Pradesh 1956 → formation facts and FIRSTs

- Andhra: The first linguistic state of India

- Andhra State: Formed on 1 October 1953 after separation from Madras State
- Andhra Pradesh Public Service Commission: Established in 1956

### States Reorganisation Act 1956 and the Seventh Constitutional Amendment
- Enactment: Passed by Parliament in 1956; in force from 1 November 1956
- Outcome: 14 states and 6 Union Territories
- States: Merged Andhra Pradesh, bilingual Bombay and Punjab, enlarged Madras, Mysore, Kerala
- Mergers:
- Telangana region of Hyderabad State + Andhra = Andhra Pradesh
| Act / amendment | Year | Provision |
| 7th Constitutional Amendment Act | 1956 | Abolished the Part A/B/C/D distinction → two-fold: states and Union Territories; Part C and D units became Union Territories; gave effect to the SRC recommendations: 14 states + 6 UTs (UPSC) |
| 7th Amendment Bill | 10 Aug 1956 | Introduced in the Lok Sabha |
| APRA 2014 | 2014 | Amended Section 15 of the SR Act 1956 to read "Andhra Pradesh and Telangana" |
- Other changes by the 7th Amendment:
- Abolished the Rajpramukh office
- Inserted Articles 350A-350B (mother-tongue instruction; Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities)
### Universal angles
## 1. Significance and FIRST/LAST
- Significance: Andhra (1953) set the precedent for nationwide linguistic reorganisation
- Settled the linguistic principle, balancing administrative + economic viability; ended the Part A-D system
- Centre overrode the SRC's "wait till 1961" formula → settled through a political agreement
- FIRST/LAST:
- Andhra State (1953) = first linguistic state of independent India
- Andhra Pradesh = first state formed under the SR Act 1956
## 2. AP angle: Andhra-Telangana question
- Merger: Andhra State (1953) + Telangana (1956) = Andhra Pradesh
- SRC plan kept Telangana out; merger done politically → fed the regional questions of 1969-72 and 2014
- 1956 safeguards (Regional Council, Mulki rules) fed the later Telangana movement (S1-93)
- Trigger chain: Andhra State's formation triggered the SRC; SRC's Hyderabad chapter decided the Andhra-Telangana question
## 3. Criticism & debates: unity and para 386
- Unity vs language: Linguistic reorganisation feared to weaken national unity, but made administration work in people's languages
- Border disputes remain, e.g. Belgaum (Maharashtra-Karnataka)
- Para 386 departure: Core grievance of the Telangana movements (1969, 2001-14)
## 4. Current relevance: reorganisation debates
- Private Member's Bill: Proposed a Permanent Framework for State Reorganisation (Feb 2026)
- BRS: K.T. Rama Rao (Jan 2026) cited the Fazal Ali Commission recommendation to blame Congress for the 1956 merger
