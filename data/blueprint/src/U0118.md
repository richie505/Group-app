## Zain-ul-Abidin — contributions
- Religious policy: Abolished jizya, temples rebuilt, fled Brahmins called back
- Translations: Mahabharata + Kalhana's Rajatarangini into Persian; Jonaraja continued the Rajatarangini
- Works: Zaina Kadal bridge (Srinagar), Zaina Lank island in Wular lake
- ⚠ Butshikan = Sikandar (imposed jizya); Bud Shah = Zain-ul-Abidin (abolished jizya)

### Notable rulers
## Kashmir
- ★ Zain-ul-Abidin = 'Akbar of Kashmir'; also Bud Shah (Great King); throne 1420 (Shahi Khan, brother of Ali Shah); ruled ~50 years; liberal religious policy, learning, art
- Ended jizya, allowed rebuilding of temples, called back the Brahmins
- Had the Mahabharata and Kalhana's Rajatarangini translated into Persian
## Bengal and Gujarat
- Chaitanya lived in Hussain Shah's reign; Hussain Shah and Nusrat Shah supported Bengali Mahabharata translations
- Mahmud Begarha — captured Girnar (Junagadh) and Champaner
- Mirat-i-Sikandari (Gujarat) and Riyaz-us-Salatin (Bengal) = original histories
- Akbar annexed Gujarat 1572; Bengal 1576
### Mahmud Gawan
- Mahmud Gawan — Persian Afaqi wazir of Muhammad Shah III
- Crushed Telangana revolts with Velama help (not Reddis)
- Divided the four tarafs into eight provinces; forts under central commanders — to control provincial nobles
- Bidar madrasa — three storeys, tiles; Persian (Herat/Samarkand) model (Cordoba comparison = Gulbarga Jama Masjid)
- Framed by rival nobles and executed (1481); his fall contributed to the Bahmani break-up
- Bidriware — 14th c., under the Bahmanis (not Vijayanagara)
- ★ Jama Masjid, Srinagar (completed by Zain-ul-Abidin): turrets, pagoda-like roofs (Buddhist), Persian style — all three

- ★ Independent Jaunpur state: Malik Sarwar (1394); last ruler Hussain Shah
