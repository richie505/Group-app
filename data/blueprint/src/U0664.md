### Scheduled Tribes — constitutional basis, numbers notified and FIRSTs
## 1. Constitutional basis
- Art. 342: President notifies STs after consulting the Governor; Parliament can amend the list
- Art. 366(25): Defines STs
## 2. Notified STs in AP
- SC and ST Orders (Amendment) Act, 1976: 33 STs notified in undivided AP (APPSC-G1 2022 key)
- 5 had area restrictions: Gowdu, Mali, Nayak, Thoti, Valmiki
- Gowdu, Nayak and Valmiki = STs only in the Agency Tracts (Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam, East and West Godavari districts of the time)
- Present AP: 34 ST communities (APSES 2024-25; some notes say 35)
- Feb 2024 additions: Porja and Savara sub-groups also added to AP's ST list
- Exam angle: "how many STs notified in AP under the 1976 Act" - answer 33.
## 3. Number of notified STs - India (1976 Amendment Act, later orders)
- India: Over 700 tribes notified under Art. 342, many overlapping more than one state (IYB 2026); Census 2011 counted 698
- Odisha: Largest number of notified tribes (62)

### Tribes of AP by region — Godavari valley, southern forests and plains
## 1. Godavari valley
- Koya: The Godavari Basin and River Valley

- Konda Reddi: The Godavari Valley — the Godavari river banks and hills adjoining them
- Hill tracts of East and West Godavari (Rampa); podu cultivators; PVTG
## 2. Southern forests and plains

### Tribes → district / region and identifiers — Chenchus and Konda Doras
## 1. Chenchus and Konda Doras
- Chenchus ("those who live under trees"):
- Semi-nomadic hunter-gatherers; the "aboriginal tribe" of AP (APPSC-GS 2025)
- Mythic origin: marriage of Ahobilam Narasimha with Chenchu girl Chenchita
- Sub-tribes: Koya, Konda, Yanadi, Ura Chenchus, Chenchu Dasarus
- ITDA records: 47,315 Chenchus in the Nallamala ranges of AP (2 Jul 2026)
- Konda Doras: Served as Mutadars (hill chiefs) of the Rampa agency
## 2. Odd-one-out tribes
- Not AP tribes (odd one out): Munda, Santhal, Bhil (APPSC 2018 and 2025 keys)
- ⚠ Trap: Munda and Santhal are not AP tribes (APPSC-GS 2018, two questions)

### Tribal issues and movements — bauxite, Polavaram and Rampa revolts

- Submergence: About 276 villages, mostly tribal, in ASR and Eluru (Jan 2026)
