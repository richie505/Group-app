### 13th, 14th and 15th FC → key recommendations
## 1. 13th FC
- 13th FC: 32% share
- Award to undivided AP apportioned between AP and Telangana on population ratio and other parameters (Sec 46(1), AP Reorganisation Act 2014)
## 2. 14th FC
- 14th FC:
- Tax devolution as main route = "compositional shift" from grants to devolution
- Ended Plan/non-Plan distinction
- Local body grants ₹2,87,436 cr (panchayats ₹2,00,292 cr); basic + performance split; 2011 population (90%) + area (10%)
- Income distance 50% weight; 2% floor for small States
## 3. 15th FC — terms of reference
- 15th FC ToR:
- Mandated 2011 population data → southern States objected (UPSC)
- Review disaster-management financing (UPSC)
- Consider a non-lapsable fund for defence and internal security

### Andhra Pradesh and the FC awards — data
## 1. Revenue deficit grants - dependence and end
- Dependence: Post-bifurcation AP depended heavily on 14th and 15th FC revenue deficit grants + disaster funds
- Advance draw: 15th FC grants for 2024-25 and 2025-26 drawn in advance by previous government
- End: 16th Finance Commission recommended no revenue deficit grants (nor sector- or state-specific grants) (11 May 2026)
- AP's loss: Up to ₹38,000 cr after FC-16 dropped special grants
- Revenue deficit ₹16,000 cr (2014-19); projected ₹48,000 cr (2025-26) (18 Aug 2026)
