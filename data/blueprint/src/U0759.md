# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 5:69 - Energy storage - battery technologies, PLI for advanced chemistry cells, grid storage
### Cell parts
- Storage = main bottleneck in integrating renewables; supports 24x7 RE
- Anode = oxidation (loses electrons) during discharge
- Cathode = reduction (gains electrons)
- Electrolyte: lets ions flow, keeps electrodes apart
- EV cathodes: lithium, nickel, cobalt; anode graphite (UPSC 2025)

### Batteries — cell parts and types — Cell parts
## 1. Cell parts
- Cell parts:
- Anode = oxidation (loses electrons) during discharge
- Cathode = reduction (gains electrons)
- Electrolyte = lets ions flow, keeps electrodes apart (prevents direct contact)
### Batteries — cell parts and types — Battery types → electrodes → uses
- Lead-acid: Conventional batteries; Ni-Cd: rechargeable; lithium compounds: modern Li-ion batteries
## 1. Battery types → electrodes → uses
| Chemistry | Electrodes / feature | Uses |
| Lead-acid | Lead and lead peroxide (PbO2), sulphuric acid electrolyte | Automotive / car starting, UPS, inverters, RE storage |
| Nickel-Cadmium (Ni-Cd) | Nickel and cadmium electrodes (PYQ 1995; UPSC 1995); rechargeable | Calculators, cordless appliances, transistors, torches, shavers (PYQ 2016; UKPSC 2016); now less common / declining - toxic cadmium |
| Nickel-Metal Hydride (NiMH) | Replaces cadmium with metal hydride | Hybrid vehicles, portable electronics / devices |
| Zinc-carbon (dry cell) | Zinc and carbon; not rechargeable | Torches, clocks |
| Lithium-ion | Graphite anode, lithium metal-oxide cathode | Phones, laptops, EVs, grid storage |
| Li-ion NMC | Nickel-manganese-cobalt cathode; high energy density, critical-mineral heavy (2026) | EVs, phones, laptops |
| Li-ion LFP (LiFePO4) | Safe, long cycle life | EVs, RE storage |
| Sodium-ion | Na ions; avoids cobalt, nickel, copper; lower peak temperature in thermal runaway; volumetric density still below LFP; Li-ion factories adaptable | Storage, low-cost EVs; global capacity ~70 GWh (2025) → ~400 GWh (2030) |
| Solid-state | Solid electrolyte - better safety, higher energy density | Emerging, EVs |
| Flow | Liquid electrolytes in tanks; energy and power capacity separate (scaled separately), easy to scale; e.g. vanadium redox | Large grid-scale storage |
- ⚠ Anode = oxidation; cathode = reduction (during discharge)
- ⚠ Zinc-carbon dry cell = not rechargeable; Ni-Cd = rechargeable
### Mechanical and thermal storage — method → principle
| Method | Principle |
| Pumped storage | Surplus power pumps water uphill; released through turbines at peak (APPCA 2026) |

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 5:69
### Cell parts
- ★ Oxidation = loss of electrons
- Oxidation happens at the anode; reduction (gain of electrons) at the cathode
- Electrolyte lets ions flow between electrodes and keeps them apart (no direct electron path)
- Li-ion battery: graphite usually the anode; lithium, nickel, cobalt usually in the cathode (cathode content does not explain graphite anode)
- Supply-chain risk: more than 70% of lithium processing and most cobalt refining held by a few countries
### Battery chemistries
## Types and uses
| Battery | Feature | Use |
| ★ Nickel-Cadmium (Ni-Cd) | rechargeable; nickel oxide hydroxide + metallic cadmium electrodes; KOH alkaline electrolyte | calculators, cordless appliances, transistors, portable power tools |
| Zinc-carbon dry cell | generally non-rechargeable | torches and clocks |
| Lead-acid | — | automotive starting and UPS |
| Flow batteries | — | grid-scale storage |
| NMC | nickel-manganese-cobalt cathode (not LFP) | — |
- Ni-Cd use fell because cadmium is toxic
## Sodium-ion
- Promoted as a safer, mineral-light alternative (not a full replacement for pumped storage)
- Volumetric energy density still below LFP
## Grid storage bids
- Remaining 10 GWh put to global bids in July 2026 for grid-scale storage
### Non-battery storage
| Storage | How it works |
| Compressed air | air compressed into caverns, later expanded through turbines |
| Flywheel | rotational kinetic energy |
| Pumped storage | pumping water uphill |
| Molten salt | thermal storage in concentrated solar power plants; generation after sunset |
- Pinnapuram project (Greenko): combines solar, wind and pumped storage
- Storage a bottleneck because it balances variable renewable generation and supports 24×7 renewable power
- ACC programme hurdles: visa delays, local-content rules, missing technology (not lack of pumped-storage reservoirs)

### EV powertrains
- PHEV — hybrid (engine + battery-motor) that can be plugged in; plain HEV is not plugged in
- FCEV — turns hydrogen into electricity on board via fuel cell; water is the exhaust
### Exam corner
- FCEV = Fuel Cell Electric Vehicle; BEVs and fuel cell-electric hybrids are also alternative powertrain vehicles
- World's first Indian-made high-energy storage device — unveiled at Amaravati, 6 August 2018
### Batteries — cell parts and types — Battery types → electrodes → uses
- ★ Dry cell electrolytes — Ammonium chloride and Zinc chloride; has no free liquid, works in any position
- ★ Dry cell cannot be charged — its chemical reaction cannot be reversed (zinc pot with manganese dioxide, sal ammoniac, carbon)
- ★ Rechargeable batteries (torchlights, electric shavers) — electrodes Nickel and Cadmium: cathode nickel hydroxide, anode cadmium, electrolyte potassium hydroxide
### Battery raw materials — critical elements, places and missions
- ★ EV battery cathodes: cobalt, lithium, nickel (e.g. LCO, NMC); graphite is used in anodes, not cathodes
- ★ Lithium inferred resources first established by GSI at Salal-Haimana, Reasi district, J&K
- ★ India is import dependent for lithium, nickel, cobalt (Indian Minerals Yearbook-2021)
- Rare Earth Corridors proposed for Odisha, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu
### Electric vehicles — types and powertrains
- ★ Hydrogen FCEV exhaust — water vapour (hydrogen + oxygen from air in fuel cell produce electricity)
- ★ Alternative powertrain vehicles (not solely ICE): full battery electric, hydrogen fuel cell, fuel cell-electric hybrid — all three
