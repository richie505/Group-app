### Kalyani Chalukya rulers → period and contributions
- Also known as the Western Chalukyas — approximately 973 to 1195 CE; a distinctive Deccan cultural tradition
- ⚠ Sources differ: other notes say the dynasty ended c. 1189 CE
- Overthrew the Rashtrakutas in 973 CE — led by Tailapa II
| Ruler | Achievement |
| Tailapa II (973) | Founder; overthrew last Rashtrakuta Karka II in 973 CE |
| Satyasraya | Sent Bayyalanambi against Vengi (1006); repulsed by Saktivarman I with Chola help |
| Someshvara I Ahavamalla (1042-68 [GK]) | Killed Chola Rajadhiraja I at Koppam (1053); took title Vengi Purveshwara; granted Hanamkonda to Kakatiya Prola I |

- Chalukya-Vikrama era:
- Founder: Vikramaditya VI; replaced the Saka era in royal records
- Start: 1076 CE (his accession)
- ⚠ Era trap: era start 1076 = accession of Vikramaditya VI, not of Tailapa II (973)
- Exam angle: reign period of Vikramaditya VI - 1076-1126 CE (APPSC 2018).

### Art and temples → place and feature
- Kalyani Chalukya style: Dravida influences with Deccan innovation — ornate pillars; stepped superstructures; decorated doorways; intricate wall motifs; balanced proportions
- Temples: Kasivisvesvara at Lakkundi; Mahadeva at Itagi; Doddabasappa at Dambal — the Lakkundi–Gadag–Itagi tradition was a regional tradition
- Style: Mature Vesara (Gadag style)
- Material: Chloritic schist (soapstone)
- Plan: Star-shaped (stellate) plans → model later used by the Hoysalas
