### SEZ Act 2005 — provisions, objectives and committees
## 1. Origin and provisions
| Item | Year / provision |
| SEZ policy | Announced 2000 under EXIM/FTP |
| Who can set up | Centre, a State or any person, jointly or severally; for goods, services or as a Free Trade Warehousing Zone |
| Approval | Proposals via state → Board of Approval |
| Status | Treated as foreign territory for trade and tariffs + single-window system |
| Baba Kalyani Committee (2018) | Rename SEZs "3Es" (Employment and Economic Enclaves) |
## 2. SEZ vs EOU
| SEZ | EOU |
| A demarcated enclave under an Act (2005) | A single unit located anywhere, under the FTP scheme (1981) [GK] |
| Developer plus units, with its own infrastructure | Unit only |
## 3. Five objectives
- (1) Generation of additional economic activity
- (2) Exports of goods and services
- (3) Promotion of investment (domestic and foreign)
- (5) Development of infrastructure
- BPSC 2022 key: All three (economic activity, exports, employment) are objectives
## 4. National data
- SEZ exports: Rs 14.64 lakh crore (about US$ 173 bn) = about 21% of India's exports
