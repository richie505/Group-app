# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:3 - Pre-historic cultures of Andhra - Palaeolithic, Mesolithic, Neolithic and Megalithic sites
### Stages in Andhra (oldest first)
- Most AP questions = site → phase (Billasurgam, Giddaluru, Patapadu) + megalith type → district
- ▲ Lower Palaeolithic — handaxe, cleaver, chopper; quartzite (Kadapa-Kurnool), limestone (north of Krishna)
- ▸ Middle Palaeolithic — flakes, scrapers, borers; chert, jasper
- ▸ Upper Palaeolithic — blades, bone tools (Kurnool caves, Jwalapuram)
- ▸ Mesolithic = Microlithic — microliths; weapons of dolomite
- ▸ Neolithic-Chalcolithic — ash mounds; copper tools, painted pottery
- ▸ Megalithic = Iron Age — black-and-red ware; grave goods

### Microliths
- Mesolithic = Microlithic period; tools by flaking and chipping
- Centres: Giddaluru, Nagarjunakonda, Kondapur, Khammam
- Resemble South Indian + Caspian microliths
- Discoverer of AP prehistory: Robert Bruce Foote (also Pallavaram)
### Megalithic phase
- Goods: pottery, beads, iron implements, gold ornaments (Brahmagiri)
- Sites: Nagarjunakonda (centre), Jonnawada, Boodigapalli, Gudivada
- South Indian Iron Age now c. 2100 BCE (Mayiladumparai)

### Megalithic phase → pottery, grave goods and sites
- Iron Age = Megalithic culture: black-and-red ware typical
- Grave goods: Pottery, beads, iron implements, gold ornaments (Brahmagiri)
- Settlements: Nagarjunakonda (centre), Jonnawada, Boodigapalli, Gudivada, Peddavadu
### Universal angles
- Current relevance:
- Iron Age in South India now dated to c. 2100 BCE (Mayiladumparai AMS dating) → earlier than the Gangetic Iron Age (Jun 2026)
- Upper Palaeolithic quartz tool + petroglyphs found at Manchirevula, Hyderabad (Mar 2026)
# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:3
### Stages in Andhra (oldest first)
- Lower Palaeolithic — handaxes, cleavers, choppers of quartzite and limestone
- Kadapa–Kurnool tools mainly quartzite; limestone north of the Krishna
- Middle — flakes; Upper Palaeolithic — blades; Mesolithic — microliths
- Manchirevula (near Hyderabad) — Upper Palaeolithic quartz tools and petroglyphs
- Megalithic phase — Iron Age; black-and-red ware
- Megalithic grave goods — pottery, beads, iron implements, gold ornaments (not glass mirrors)
### Key AP sites
- Billa Surgam caves — Kurnool (now Nandyal); stone and bone tools
- Kurnool caves and Muchatla Chintamani Gavi — the Upper Palaeolithic bone-tool sites; Jwalapuram also Upper Palaeolithic
- Patapadu — Chalcolithic painted pottery; Keesarapalli — copper tools
### Microliths
- Robert Bruce Foote — Andhra Palaeolithic tools; also found them at Pallavaram near Chennai
- Mesolithic = Microlithic period; tools made by flaking and chipping
- Giddaluru (Prakasam) — key microlithic centre; also Nagarjunakonda, Kondapur, Khammam
### Megalithic phase
- Nagarjunakonda — a centre among Megalithic living sites; others Jonnawada, Boodigapalli, Gudivada, Peddavadu
- Mayiladumparai AMS dating — South Indian Iron Age from about 2100 BCE, before the Gangetic Iron Age
