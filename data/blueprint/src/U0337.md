### Electoral systems — FPTP, State Assembly elections and the EVM
## 1. First-past-the-post
- First-Past-the-Post: A plurality system — the candidate with the highest number of valid votes wins; no absolute majority (over 50 percent) needed
- Only the highest vote count among contestants — wins; no absolute majority of 50 percent plus one vote required
- FPTP winner = candidate with the most votes (plurality), not a majority
## 2. State Legislative Assembly elections
- Lower / popular house — directly elected by citizens from single-member territorial constituencies
- Universal adult suffrage — Article 326; First-Past-the-Post
- Conducted by the Election Commission of India (Article 324) — not the State Election Commission
## 3. EVM
- An electronic vote-recording and vote-counting apparatus — ⚠ not an electoral system
- Deployed under the superintendence, direction and control of the Election Commission of India (ECI)
- Used for Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assembly elections
- India conducts direct general elections using the First-Past-the-Post (FPTP) system — the candidate with the highest valid votes wins; need not cross 50% of the votes
### Why PR rejected, deposits and types of elections
- Why PR rejected for Lok Sabha:
- Fragmentation and unstable governments
- Expensive; allows no by-elections; weakens voter-MP link
- Security deposit: forfeited if a candidate gets less than one-sixth of valid votes

### NOTA — None of the Above
## 1. NOTA
- An electoral option on ballot papers and Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs) — a negative voting choice button on ballot units
- Enables voters to exercise a negative vote — formally reject all contesting candidates in secret
- PUCL v. Union of India — landmark Supreme Court judgment dated 27 September 2013 — directed the mandatory introduction of the NOTA button on EVMs
- Linked the right to register a negative vote in secrecy with the Freedom of Speech and Expression — Article 19(1)(a)
- How: The voter attends the polling booth and presses the NOTA button on the EVM — secrecy fully preserved
- Effect: A symbolic expression only — does not determine the election outcome by itself; the candidate with the highest valid votes among the contestants is declared elected
- No electoral effect: even if NOTA gets the most votes, the highest-scoring candidate wins
- ⚠ Trap: "Election cancelled and a fresh poll held if NOTA gets the majority or highest votes" — false; under First-Past-the-Post the election is not cancelled merely because NOTA receives the maximum votes
- Not abstention: Exercising NOTA is not non-voting or abstaining — it is the active exercise of the statutory right to vote, registering a neutral or negative choice
- Scope: Limited to direct Lok Sabha and Assembly elections — not applicable in Rajya Sabha or Legislative Council elections (Supreme Court struck down NOTA for them in 2018)
- NOTA symbol: ballot with a black cross, designed by NID, Ahmedabad
## 2. NOTA vs Rule 49-O vs the right to reject
| Basis | NOTA | Old Rule 49-O (abstention) | Full international right to reject |
| How | Press the NOTA button on the EVM | The voter entered the polling booth and refused to vote before the Presiding Officer — recorded in Form 17-A | Voters reject all candidates via the ballot or EVM |
| Effect | Symbolic — highest valid votes wins | Did not affect the winning tally; struck down as ultra vires | Re-election mandated if rejection crosses more than 50% — threshold not applicable in India |

### Electoral systems
## First-past-the-post
- FPTP (Lok Sabha) — winner needs only a plurality (most votes) in a single-member constituency, not a majority
- State Assembly — directly elected from single-member territorial constituencies, by FPTP
- Reasons cited against proportional representation for Lok Sabha: difficulty for voters, fragmentation, unstable governments, expense, no by-elections, weaker voter–MP link
- "Guarantees direct election in every single-member constituency" is NOT such a reason
## Rolls
- Special Intensive Revision — remakes the whole roll; summary revision is annual
## NOTA in indirect elections
- NOTA not applicable in Rajya Sabha and Legislative Council elections — struck down by the Supreme Court in 2018
- NOTA applies only to direct Lok Sabha and Assembly elections
## Vice-President
- Elected by all MPs, elected and nominated, by single transferable vote — Article 66
- State Legislatures have no role in electing the VP
- President — elected by elected MPs and MLAs — Article 54

### NOTA, EVM & ink
## Indelible ink
- Made by Mysore Paints and Varnish Ltd; based on silver nitrate

## NOTA
- PUCL v Union of India (27 September 2013) — NOTA button on all EVMs

- Symbol (ballot paper with black cross) designed by NID Ahmedabad; first Lok Sabha use 2014
- NOTA is symbolic — even if it gets most votes, the top candidate wins; no fresh poll
