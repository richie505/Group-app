### Peasant satyagrahas → year, issue, outcome
## 1. Early campaigns
| Campaign | Year | Patel's role / issue | Outcome |
| Kheda | - | Gandhi spiritual head; Patel with Mohanlal Pandya and Narahari Parikh led mobilisation | Revenue suspended for poor peasants; confiscated property returned |
| Sarabandi (no-tax) campaign | 1922 | Led by Patel (BPSC 2019 key) | - |
| Borsad Satyagraha | 1923 | Patel led peasants against a punitive tax | - |
## 2. Bardoli Satyagraha, 1928
- Year & place: 1928, Bardoli taluk (Gujarat, Bombay Presidency)
- Leader: Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
- Issue: Land revenue enhanced by about 30% despite floods and famine
- Method: Disciplined no-tax campaign + village intelligence network
- Outcome: Government suspended the hike + ordered a fresh survey
- Maxwell-Broomfield inquiry: Cut the increase to about 6%
- ⚠ 1922 – 1923 – 1928 = Sarabandi – Borsad – Bardoli; Bardoli hike 30% → cut to 6%
- Exam angle: "who led Bardoli" and "which satyagraha was not led by Gandhi".

### Commemoration → monument, day and award
## 1. Statue of Unity
- Height: 182 m (597 ft), world's tallest; unveiled 2018
- Location: Sadhu Bet island, Ekta Nagar (Kevadia), Narmada district, Gujarat, facing the Sardar Sarovar Dam
- Sculptor: Ram V. Sutar
## 2. Day and award
| Commemoration | Fact |
| Rashtriya Ekta Diwas (National Unity Day) | 31 October - Patel's birth anniversary (born 31 October 1875, Nadiad) |
| Sardar Patel National Unity Award (instituted 2019) | Highest civilian award for unity and integrity; medal + citation, no cash; not more than three a year |
- ⚠ 2018 = Statue unveiled; 2019 = Unity Award instituted

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:246
### Peasant satyagrahas
- Order: Kheda (1918) → Sarabandi (1922) → Borsad (1923) → Bardoli (1928)
- Bardoli (1928) — led by Patel; Champaran, Ahmedabad Mill Strike, Dandi — Gandhi
- Bardoli outcome — Maxwell-Broomfield inquiry cut the increase to about 6 per cent
- Kheda outcome — revenue stopped for poor peasants, seized property returned
### Bardoli 1928
- Raised assessment stopped, fresh survey ordered
- Maxwell-Broomfield inquiry — increase reduced to about 6 per cent
### Titles
- 'Steel frame' (civil service) — if it bends, the whole structure will collapse
- 'Iron Man of India' — integrating about 565 princely states
- 'Patron Saint of Indian Civil Servants' — created and defended the All India Services
### Posts & organisation
- Congress Parliamentary Board (1937 elections) — headed by Patel
- First Deputy Prime Minister — Patel; also Home Minister, handled the States Department
- Constituent Assembly first met 9 December 1946 — temporary Chairman Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha (oldest member); Rajendra Prasad elected President 11 December 1946
- Patel — chairman of the Advisory Committee on Fundamental Rights and Minorities; Nehru — Union Powers and Union Constitution; Ambedkar — Drafting
### Patel vs Nehru
- Nehru referred Kashmir to the UN (Mountbatten's advice); Patel preferred a military solution
- Patel's letter warning about China (after Tibet) — 7 November 1950
### Commemoration
- Patel addressed first IAS probationers at Metcalfe House, Delhi — 21 April 1947 (Civil Services Day)
- Statue of Unity — in Gujarat, facing Sardar Sarovar Dam (not Hyderabad)
- Rashtriya Ekta Diwas — 31 October, Patel's birth anniversary; born 31 October 1875 at Nadiad
- Sardar Patel National Unity Award — started 2019; medal and citation, no cash, at most three a year; not limited to armed forces
