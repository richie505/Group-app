# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:147 - Sufi saints - Moinuddin Chishti, Nizamuddin Auliya, Baba Farid, Sheikh Ahmad Sirhindi
### Saint → contemporary ruler
- Nizamuddin (Chishti, sama, aloof from the court) vs Sirhindi (Naqshbandi, orthodox, political critic)
| Saint | Ruler / relation |
| Moinuddin Chishti | Came with Muhammad Ghori, 1192; settled at Ajmer |
| Qutbuddin Bakhtiyar Kaki | Iltutmish |
| Nizamuddin Auliya | Alauddin Khalji, Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq; kept away from court |
| Nasiruddin Chiragh | Muhammad bin Tughlaq, Firuz Tughlaq |
### Chishti line & dargahs
- 1173-1235 — Bakhtiyar Kaki: Delhi (Mehrauli); Qutub Minar's first storey dedicated to him
- 1238-1325 — Nizamuddin Auliya: born Badaun; dargah Delhi
### The four saints
- Moinuddin Chishti
- Founder of Chishti order in India
- Baba Farid
- Teacher of Nizamuddin Auliya
- Nizamuddin Auliya
- "Hunuz Dilli dur ast" (of Ghiyasuddin)
- Ahmad Sirhindi
- Mujaddid Alf-i-Sani
- Imprisoned by Jahangir at Gwalior fort
### Sirhindi vs Akbar
- Criticised Sulh-i-kul and Din-i-Ilahi
- Purge "un-Islamic" practices; opposed sama and visits to saints' tombs
- Doctrine: Wahdat-ush-Shuhud (Unity of Appearance) vs Wahdat-ul-Wujud (Unity of Being)
### Texts & authors
| Text | Author | Type |
| Fawaid-ul-Fuad | Nizamuddin's talks, by Amir Hasan Sijzi | Malfuzat |
| Maktubat-i-Imam Rabbani | Ahmad Sirhindi | Letters |
| Radd-i-Rawafiz | Ahmad Sirhindi (⚠ other notes: Radd-i-Khawafiq) | Anti-Shia tract |
### Chishti stance & legacy
- Avoided political power; later Deccan Chishtis accepted grants
- Amir Khusrau: disciple of Nizamuddin; introduced Qaul of Qawwali
### Exam corner
- Fawaid-ul-Fuad = Amir Hasan Sijzi (UPPSC 2021); traps: Amir Khusrau, Barani, Hasan Nizami
- Iltutmish = contemporary of both Bakhtiyar Kaki (Chishti) and Bahauddin Zakariya (Suhrawardi)
- Mahboob-i-Ilahi = Nizamuddin (UKPSC 2016); Chiragh-i-Delhi = Nasiruddin
- Sirhindi: Naqshbandi, Wahdat-ush-Shuhud; Wujud = Chishti/Qadiri
### Sufi saints → contemporary rulers
| Saint | Contemporary ruler / relation |
| Moinuddin Chishti | Came with Muhammad Ghori, 1192; settled at Ajmer |
| Qutbuddin Bakhtiyar Kaki | Iltutmish[GK] |
| Nizamuddin Auliya | Lived under Alauddin Khalji and Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq, keeping away from the court; "Hunuz Dilli dur ast" is said of Ghiyasuddin [GK] |
| Nasiruddin Chiragh | Muhammad bin Tughlaq and Firuz Tughlaq[GK] |
- Chishti stance: As a rule avoided political power; later Deccan Chishtis accepted grants
- ⚠ Iltutmish = contemporary of both Bakhtiyar Kaki (Chishti) and Bahauddin Zakariya (Suhrawardi)
### Sheikh Ahmad Sirhindi → order, doctrine and opposition to Akbar
- Order: Naqshbandi; disciple of Khwaja Baqi Billah; titled Mujaddid Alf-i-Sani
- Opposition to Akbar: Criticised Sulh-i-kul and Din-i-Ilahi
- Sought to purge "un-Islamic" practices; opposed sama and visits to saints' tombs
- Doctrine: Propounded Wahdat-ush-Shuhud vs Wahdat-ul-Wujud
- Imprisonment: Imprisoned by Jahangir, at Gwalior fort
### Sufi texts → authors and compilers
| Text | Author / compiler | Type |
| Fawaid-ul-Fuad | Conversations of Nizamuddin Auliya, compiled by Amir Hasan Sijzi (UPPSC 2021) | Malfuzat |
| Maktubat-i-Imam Rabbani | Ahmad Sirhindi | Letters |
| Radd-i-Rawafiz | Ahmad Sirhindi (some notes write "Radd-i-Khawafiq") | Anti-Shia tract |
- ⚠ Trap: Distractors were Amir Khusrau, Barani and Hasan Nizami
### Universal angles
- Current relevance: Dargah Committee runs Dargah Khwaja Saheb, Ajmer
- Calendar: Annual Urs of Garib Nawaz, Mini-Urs of Baba Farid, Urs of Usman Harooni and Bakhtiyar Kaki
- Comparative note: Nizamuddin (Chishti, sama, aloof from the court) vs Sirhindi (Naqshbandi, orthodox, political critic)
