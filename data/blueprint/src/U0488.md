### Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY and Ayushman Arogya Mandir — features and data
## 1. Cover & beneficiaries
- Cover: PYQ answer Rs 5 lakh
- Launch: 23 Sept 2018 at Ranchi
- Base: ~12 crore families / 55 crore people → bottom 40%
- Identified from SECC 2011 deprivation criteria; States may widen the base at own cost
- Including expansions: 15.14 crore families (2026 count)
- Expansions (2024): ASHA, Anganwadi workers and helpers and families
- Senior citizens: 86.51 lakh cards issued by 28 Oct 2025
- Coverage: 35 states/UTs; Odisha and Delhi the latest to join (April 2025)
## 2. Features & funding
- Features:
- 3 days pre- and 15 days post-hospitalisation
- Gender: Women hold ~49% of cards and ~48% of admissions (49% female beneficiaries - ES 2025-26)
- Funding: Centre-State 60:40; 100% Centre in UTs without legislature
## 3. Data - scale (28 Oct 2025)
| Indicator | Figure |
| Cards | 42 crore+ Ayushman cards |
| Hospitals | 33,000+ (17,685 public, 15,380 private) |
## 4. Ayushman Arogya Mandir
- Ayushman Arogya Mandir (ex-HWCs): 1.5 lakh centres for comprehensive primary care; 1.85 lakh+ now
- Rationale: Primary care meets 80-90% of needs
- ⚠ PM-JAY launched at Ranchi (2018); PM-ABHIM at Varanasi (2021)
