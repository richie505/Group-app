### DRDO — formation, predecessors, motto, vision and mission
## 1. Formation and size
- Formed: 1958, by merging three bodies:
| Predecessor | Short form |
| Army's Technical Development Establishments | TDEs/TDE |
| Directorate of Technical Development & Production | DTDP |
| Defence Science Organisation | DSO |
- Labs: Started with 10 → now about 52 laboratories (2026; older notes say "50+")
- Headquarters: DRDO Bhawan, New Delhi
## 2. Motto, vision and mission
- Motto: "Balasya Mulam Vigyanam" ("the source of strength is science") (RPSC 2021)
- ⚠ Trap: Tejasvinavadhitamastu is NOT DRDO's motto
- Vision (current): Empower the nation with state-of-the-art indigenous defence & security technologies
- Older notes: "make India prosperous by establishing a world-class S&T base and provide our Defence Services a decisive edge"
- Mission:
- Design, develop & lead to production sensors, weapon systems and platforms for the Services
- Optimise combat effectiveness + troop well-being
- Build infrastructure, manpower + indigenous technology base
- Overall goal: Aatmanirbharta + less import dependence
- Exam angle: motto-matching questions. Tejasvinavadhitamastu is not DRDO's motto; Balasya Mulam Vigyanam is.
### DRDO leadership → parent department, Chairman–Secretary and hierarchy
- Parent: Department of Defence R&D, Ministry of Defence
- Dual post: One person = Secretary, Department of Defence R&D and Chairman, DRDO
- Hierarchy: Ministry of Defence → Department of Defence R&D → Secretary DD R&D, who is also Chairman DRDO → Director Generals → lab directors
| Level | Post |
| I | Secretary DD R&D and Chairman DRDO |

| III | Directors of labs/establishments and corporate directorates |

### Schemes → Technology Development Fund and technology transfer
## 1. Technology Development Fund
- Nature: Ministry of Defence flagship programme executed by DRDO under Make in India
- Support: Industry (MSMEs + start-ups) gets a grant of up to ₹50 crore
- New TDF policies: For deep-tech and cutting-edge technologies → private firms in co-development of emerging tech
- Not in your sources: DRDO's allocation in Budget 2026-27.
## 2. Technology transfer to industry
- Patent policy: Free access to DRDO patents → since 2020, royalty-free access through ToT
- ToT record: 2,100+ free ToTs to Indian industry covering 132+ systems
- Missile ToT (Aug 2026): MoD approved ToT of all DRDO conventional missiles (Astra, Rudram, Nag, VSHORADS) to private industry
- Nuclear-capable Agni + K-series stay in state production (30 Aug 2026)
- Test facilities: Open to the private sector
## 3. Other enablers
- DFP-2026 (Delegation of Financial Powers to DRDO): Released by Defence Minister → speeds up projects (30 Jun 2026)
- Joint forums: India-USA Joint Technology Group, Indo-Israel Management Council, others
- ISRO support: DRDO helped test Gaganyaan crew-escape parachute recovery system
