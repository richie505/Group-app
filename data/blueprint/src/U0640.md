## 2. Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam Tiger Reserve
- Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam (NSTR) Tiger Reserve: In the Nallamala hills of the Eastern Ghats; river Krishna; spans AP + Telangana
- Declared 1983 (CDCA Jul 2026)
- Managed on a core-buffer model under NTCA (WPA 1972); Srisailam circle
- Area: About 3,728 sq km; India's largest tiger reserve by area; largest in India by core/critical tiger habitat (APPSC 2024 key; CDCA Jul 2026)
- Hence "largest tiger habitat is in AP" (UPPSC 2011)
- Core areas: Nandyal, Prakasam, Palnadu
- Gundla Brahmeswaram (extended core) in Nandyal and Prakasam; core about 3,325 sq km in total
- Tigers: 63-67 (All India Tiger Estimation 2022)
- Count: one tiger reserve (2024-25 and 2025-26) - follow the survey
- ⚠ Trap: Some notes list Sri Venkateswara and Papikondalu tiger reserves and call NSTR a BR; the survey supports neither
