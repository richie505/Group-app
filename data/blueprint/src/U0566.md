# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 3:122 - Rural credit cooperatives in Andhra Pradesh - structure, performance and problems
### Acts & amendment
- Nehru called cooperatives one of three pillars of democracy (with panchayats and schools)
- 1904 — Cooperative Credit Societies Act: started the movement
- 1912 — Cooperative Societies Act: non-credit societies allowed
- 1964 — AP Cooperative Societies Act: main law; Tribunal under Section 75
- 1995 — AP MACS Act: no government share capital (PYQ 2008)
- 2002 — National Cooperative Policy
- 2011 — 97th Amendment: Art. 19(1)(c), Art. 43B, Part IXB
### Short-term credit tiers
- ▲ State: APCOB — 18 branches; refinances DCCBs for long-term credit
- ▸ District: DCCBs — 13 DCCBs, 442 branches (APSES 2024-25)
- ▸ Village: PACS — 2,042 (APSES); SES 2025-26: 2,024, 436 branches

### Acts, amendment and committees on cooperatives
## 1. Acts → year
| Act / policy | Year | Provision |
| Cooperative Credit Societies Act | 1904 | Started the movement |
| Cooperative Societies Act | 1912 | Allowed non-credit societies |
| AP Cooperative Societies Act | 1964 | Main cooperative law; the AP Cooperative Tribunal is constituted under Section 75; APHOUSEFED was registered under it in 1966 |
| AP Mutually Aided Cooperative Societies (MACS) Act (PYQ 2008) | 1995 | Self-reliant societies without government share capital, run by members with minimal state control [GK] |
| National Cooperative Policy | 2002 | — |
| 97th Amendment | 2011 | Added Art. 19(1)(c) (right to form cooperatives), Art. 43B (DPSP), Part IXB |
- ⚠ 97th Amendment: Supreme Court struck Part IXB down for State cooperatives; kept it for multi-State societies
- ⚠ 1964 Act = main law, Tribunal under Section 75; MACS Act 1995 = no government share capital

### PACS → functions and computerisation

- Credit: crop and short-term loans + inputs (fertilizers, pesticides, seeds, cattle feed)
- Non-credit: paddy & maize procurement, seed processing, PDS, insurance, RO drinking water, petrol bunks, rice mills, warehousing
- NABARD supports turning PACS into Multi-Service Centres
- Model bye-laws: PACS can take up 25+ non-credit activities
