# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 3:25 - Trends in India's national income, per capita income and sectoral shares; inter-state GSDP comparison
### Key terms & firsts
- From agriculture-led at Independence to services over 50% of GDP: services > industry > agriculture
- "Hindu rate of growth": about 3.5% a year, 1950-80
- Miracle economies: 7%+ growth for a decade or more
- Income class: lower-middle income (World Bank, GNI per capita, Atlas method; updated each 1 July)
- Largest at Independence: agriculture (APPSC 2023)
- Largest now: Services = over 50% of GDP
- Largest institutional sector: household (largely unorganised)
### Growth record
- 1950-51 — GDP growth 3.6% (APPSC-GS 2021); industry about 13% of GDP
- 1979-80 — Contraction: drought + oil shock
- 1991 on — Nominal GDP grew almost 12 times (APPSC 2018)
- 2017-18 — Agriculture down to about 17% (APPSC 2019)
- 2020-21 — Contraction: COVID-19 → rebound 2021-22
- Aug 2025 — S&P upgrade BBB- → BBB: first major in nearly two decades
