### Universal angles
## 1. Significance and debates
- Significance: Individual heroic action (assassinations, "swadeshi dacoities") after the Surat Split + failure of mass Swadeshi; kept radical nationalism alive
- Criticism & debates: Not a mass movement; strongly Hindu-revivalist symbolism (worship of Bhavani) limited Muslim participation
## 2. AP angle
- AP angle: Tenali Bomb Case (6 April 1909): Vande Mataram phase
- Chukkapalli Ramayya, Lakkaraju Basavayya, Katamraju Venkatrayudu planted bombs for the Howrah Express near Tenali
- Kotappakonda (Feb 1909, Guntur): Chinnappa Reddy - only death sentence of the Vandemataram movement
- Alluri: Not linked to the INC but troubled the British more than the NCM
## 3. Current relevance
- Komagata Maru: Canada PM Trudeau's 2016 apology in the House of Commons (2 May 2026)

### Revolutionary societies
## Maharashtra: Savarkar and Abhinav Bharat
- V.D. Savarkar — founded Mitra Mela (1899)
- ★ Abhinav Bharat — V.D. Savarkar; Mitra Mela merged into it in 1904 at Nasik, Maharashtra
- ★ Abhinav Bharat extended to London in 1906 by Savarkar (went to study law)
- Savarkar — wrote The Indian War of Independence, 1857 (1909) and Hindutva
- Savarkar arrested in London, 1910; tried escape failed at Marseilles
## Bengal: Anushilan Samiti and Yugantar
- ★ Anushilan Samiti — a revolutionary organization; associated with P. (Pramath Nath) Mitra
- Founded at Midnapur, 1902 by Gyanendra Nath Basu; Calcutta branches 1902 (Satish Chandra Basu; P. Mitra, Bagha Jatin, Barindra Nath Ghosh)
- ★ Barindra Ghosh — Anushilan Samiti; younger brother of Aurobindo Ghosh
- Aurobindo wrote the pamphlet Bhawani Mandir (1905)
- Yugantar (1906) — founded by Barindra Kumar Ghosh
- Mukti Kon Pathe — Barindra Ghosh
- ★ Barrah Dacoity (1908) — first major venture in East Bengal (Dhaka district); planned by Pulin Behari Das
- Pulin Behari Das — founded the National School in Dhaka; led the Dhaka branch of Anushilan Samiti
## Punjab
- Bharat Mata Society — Ajit Singh with Lala Lajpat Rai, in Punjab (not Bengal, not Pulin Behari Das)
## HRA and HSRA
- ★ Hindustan Republican Association — Kanpur, October 1924; Sachindranath Sanyal (President)
- Founders also Jogesh Chatterjee, Ram Prasad Bismil; formed after suspension of the Non-Cooperation Movement
- HSRA — 1928, at Feroz Shah Kotla; Chandrashekhar Azad rebuilt the HRA as HSRA after Bismil's death
- Order: HRA 1924 → Kakori 1925 → Naujawan Bharat Sabha (Lahore) 1926 → HSRA 1928; Indian Independence League 1942
- Chandrashekhar Azad — shot himself at Alfred Park, Prayagraj, 27 February 1931
- Lahore jail — Jatin Das's hunger strike and the 1931 executions
## Writings and abroad
- The Philosophy of the Bomb — Bhagwati Charan Vohra; Why I am an Atheist — Bhagat Singh
- Indian Sociologist — Shyamji Krishna Varma (abroad); India House, London — Shyamji Krishna Varma
- Yugantar Ashram, San Francisco — headquarters of the Ghadar Party
- Yugantar and Sandhya — Bengal journals
- ★ Young India — not revolutionary; Gandhi's weekly for non-violence; Ghadar — revolutionary weekly
- Bhikaji Cama — revolutionary propaganda abroad; flag at Stuttgart, 1907; Congress Radio — Usha Mehta
- Weakness of the first phase — never a mass movement; Hindu-revivalist symbols (Bhavani worship) limited Muslim participation
