|  | Swarajists | No-Changers |

### Swarajist record
- Dec 1922 — Gaya: C.R. Das's council-entry resolution set aside
- 1923 — Kakinada Congress - local women as executive members, volunteers
- 1924 — Nationalist Party (about 70); Muddiman Committee; reunited at Belgaum
- 1925 — Vithalbhai Patel first Indian Speaker; C.R. Das dies (June) → split
- 1926 — Elections: Swarajists did poorly
- 1928 — Opposed Public Safety Bill
