### Telugu language — family, status and names
## 1. Family and status
- Family: Dravidian (CDCA 2026)
- Sub-branch: South-Central Dravidian; sister languages include Gondi and Kui
- Status: Official language of AP; one of the 22 Eighth Schedule languages
- Origin (old traditional view, not accepted linguistically - Telugu is Dravidian): grew out of the Desi bhasha (Paisachi) of Prakrit; D.C. Sircar found Desi words very close to Telugu
## 2. Names of the language → etymology

| Andhra / Andhrika | other names of the language |

## 1. Tikkana - philosophy and other works
- Philosophy: Harihara Advaita (unity of Shiva + Vishnu; some notes: Harihara Dvaitam) → dedicated Bharatam to Hariharanatha to reconcile Shaivas + Vaishnavas
- Independent works: Nirvachanottara Ramayanam (his first work; dedicated to Manumasiddhi), Kavi Vakbandham, Krishna Satakam
- Disciple: Ketana
## 2. Errana - patron and other works
- Patron: Court poet of Prolaya Vema Reddi (standard answer)

- Narasimha Puranam (Nrusimha Puranam) (G2 2017 key) = first Kshetra-mahatmya kavya in Telugu
- Harivamsam, dedicated to Prolaya Vema. Uttara Harivamsa is by Nachana Somana
- ⚠ Harivamsam = Errana; Uttara Harivamsa = Nachana Somana
### Nannaya → contribution
## 1. Eastern Chalukya age
- Nannaya (Nannayya): Court poet of Raja Raja Narendra of the Eastern Chalukyas — also teacher and adviser; r. 1018-1061 CE, at Rajamahendravaram, 11th century
- The first poet to translate the Mahabharata into Telugu — started the Telugu version; the first translator
- Wrote Andhra Sabda Chintamani (see Grammar below)
## 2. Nannaya → contribution
- Title as pioneer: Adi Kavi (first poet) of Telugu; began the work + set rules of Telugu grammar
- Translation: 2.5 parvas of the Mahabharata = Adi, Sabha and part of Aranya
- Andhra Mahabharatamu = first book in Telugu
- ⚠ Trap: "first three parvas and part of the fourth" (one note) is WRONG; all other notes say Adi, Sabha + half of Aranya
- Style:
- Pioneered Champu style (mixed prose + verse)
- Fitted Sanskrit metres to Telugu
- Used Akkara metre borrowed from Kannada
- Grammar: Andhra Shabda Chintamani = treatise on Telugu grammar written in Sanskrit (G2 2017, 2025 keys)
- Other works: Indra Vijayam, Chamundika Vilasam
- Titles: Adikavi, Vagadheeshwara, Vidyadvaitudu, Shabda Shasanudu
- Tikkana praised him as Kavitva Visharadudu
