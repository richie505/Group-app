### Mee Seva — meaning, service categories, departments, channels
- Meaning: "MeeSeva" in Telugu = "At your service" (APPSC 2023)
- Single-window G2C delivery that succeeded e-Seva (APPCA May 2026)
- Launch: 2011 in the combined state
| Category | Meaning |
| Category A | Over-the-counter services issued on the spot (e.g. certified copies) |
| Category B | Statutory services needing notices, hearings or field verification (APPSC 2023 key) |
- Departments inside MeeSeva: (APPSC 2023: all three pairs correct)
- EDS department prints MeeSeva certificates
- Education department issues migration certificates (passed candidates)
- Channels: MeeSeva franchise centres + online portal
- ⚠ Category A = on the spot; Category B = statutory (notices, hearings, field verification)
