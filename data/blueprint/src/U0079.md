### Eastern Chalukyas — origin, period, identity and FIRSTs
- Also known as the Chalukyas of Vengi — originated as a branch of the Badami Chalukyas (also Purva / Vengi Chalukyas)
- Founded by Kubja Vishnuvardhana — brother of Pulakeshin II
- Pulakeshin II took Vengi (Krishna-Godavari) from the Durjayas; some notes say from the Vishnukundins, 616 CE
- Made viceroy in 617 CE → declared independence in 624 CE
- Core region and capital: Vengi in present-day Andhra Pradesh; Rajamahendravaram — also Rajahmundry — later an important centre
- Region map: Badami — Karnataka; Vengi — Andhra Pradesh
- An important power in Andhra–Deccan politics — repeated political interaction and conflict with the Cholas
- Matwalin: A traveller associated with an account of China–India relations during Chalukya rule
- Rule: 624-1075 CE

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:78
### Origin & identity
## Origin
- Branch of the Badami Chalukyas: Kubja Vishnuvardhana, brother of Pulakeshin II, founded rule in Vengi
- Viceroy 617 CE; independence 624 CE
- Ruled c. 624–1075 CE (1076–1210 = Chalukya-Chola phase)
## Identity
- ★ Mother goddess Hariti; called Hariti-putras
- Hariti-putra also used by the Satavahanas, Ikshvakus, Kadambas
- Royal emblem: Boar (Varaha)
- Official language Sanskrit — most of about 100 donation inscriptions
- Turned Telugu into a literary language
## Rulers and rivals
- Greatest king: Gunaga Vijayaditya III (Tripura Martya Maheshwara)
- After 973 CE main rival: Kalyani Chalukyas (Satyasraya, Someshvara I)
- Kulottunga I = Rajendra Chalukya; united Eastern Chalukya and Chola lines

## Inscriptions and temples
- Nandampudi inscription — written by Nannaya; Rajaraja Narendra's grant to Narayana Bhattu
- Magallu inscription — first to mention the Kakatiyas
- Porumamilla tank inscription — Prince Bhaskara Bhavadura; 12 rules for tank building
- Dhananjaya Varma — Kalamalla inscription; Rudrapurushadatta was an Ikshvaku
- Pancharamas include Bhimarama and Draksharama
- Vengi school: local sandstone (not granite); Vesara/Chalukya; Dravidian vimana with Buddhist influence; raised jagati
### Chola link & end
## Dates
- 1006 CE — Saktivarman I defeated Satyasraya
- 1022 CE — Rajaraja Narendra crowned
- 1070 CE — last ruler Vijayaditya VII removed by Rajendra Chalukya (later Kulottunga I)
## Marriages and succession
- Rashtrakuta Dhruva defeated Vishnuvardhana IV (who backed Govinda III), married his daughter Sheela Mahadevi
- Vimaladitya married Kundavva (daughter of Rajaraja Chola I) and Melama (daughter of Jatachoda Bhima)
- Rajaraja Narendra married Ammanga Devi, daughter of Rajendra Chola
- Succession war: Rajaraja Narendra (son of Kundavva) backed by Rajendra I; Vijayaditya VII (son of Melama) by Kalyani Jayasimha II
- Koppam (1053) — Ahavamalla Someshwara killed Rajadhiraja I; Rajadhiraja had won at Dharanikota and burnt Kollipaka
### Capitals
- Order: Pithapuram (Kubja Vishnuvardhana) → Vengi (Jayasimha Vallabha I) → Rajamahendravaram
- Ammaraja I made Rajamahendravaram (Rajahmundry) capital; Rajaraja Narendra ruled from it and made it famous
- Core territory Venginadu = Krishna-Godavari doab; capitals Pithapuram, Pedavegi, Rajahmundry
- Telugu Mahabharata begun by court poet Nannaya, not the king himself
### Literature & patronage
## Women and patronage

## Religion
- Mother goddess Kushmandini (APPSC G2 2019 key); Hariti-putra = mother's name, not a goddess
- Shaiva sects: Kapalika, Kalamukha, Pasupata
## Literature
- Nannaya Bhattaraka — Adi Kavi; Andhra Mahabharatamu, first major Telugu literary work, in Champu (verse + prose)
- Nandampudi inscription — composed by Nannaya; agrahara to helper Narayana Bhattu under Rajaraja Narendra (APPSC G2 2017 key named Nannaya as receiver)
- Pavuluri Ganitamu — first Telugu scientific text; translates Mahaviracharya's Ganitasarasangraham
- Kalyanakaraka — medical book of Ugradityacharya
- Kavijanasrayam — Vemulavada Bhimakavi
### Exam corner
- Dandin (Dashakumaracharita) called Vengi Andhra Nagaram
- Other names: Mangalapura = Mangalagiri (Kopparam / Marturu inscriptions); Pishtapura = Pithapuram; Rajamahendra = title of Ammaraja I
- Addanki inscription — Pandaranga, general of Gunaga Vijayaditya III; first Telugu verse inscription
- Kalamalla inscription (c. 575 CE) — earliest Telugu inscription; Renati Choda Dhananjaya; pre-Nannaya
- Vipparla inscription — issued by Jayasimha Vallabha
### Eastern Chalukyas — origin, period, identity and FIRSTs
- ★ Official language Sanskrit (also Vishnukundins, Kakatiyas, Reddis); Prakrit under the Satavahanas and Ikshvakus
- ★ Pancharamas — built in the Vengi Chalukya period, by Chalukya Bhima I
- ★ Satavahana capitals: Kotilingala, Pratishthana, Dhanyakataka; Vengi — Eastern Chalukyas; Warangal — Kakatiyas
- ★ Cholas had close political and matrimonial ties with the Chalukyas of Vengi

- ★ Addanki inscription of Pandaranga — first Telugu poem, in Taruvoja metre

- ★ Vijayaditya II (Narendra Mrigaraja) — 108 battles in 12 years; 108 Shiva temples (Narendra Mrigeswaralayas) as penance
