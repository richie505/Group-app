### Kidneys, excretion and dialysis
## 1. Kidney functions
- Location: Either side of the vertebral column
- Main function: Filter blood and form urine — the main nitrogenous waste removed is urea (formed in the liver, filtered by the kidneys)
- Balance: Extracellular fluid volume (water balance); ionic balance; acid–base balance (pH regulation)
- Long-term regulation of blood pressure; removes metabolic wastes and toxins
- Urine pathway: Kidney → ureter → bladder → urethra
- Urine: About 1.5 litres per day (average normal volume); pH about 6.0
## 2. Dialysis
| Term | Meaning |
| Dialysis | recommended when the kidneys fail (APPSC-GS 2019); removes wastes and excess water from blood |
| haemodialysis | machine filter |
| peritoneal dialysis | peritoneum as membrane |

## 2. Electric fuse
- Principle: The heating effect of current
- Material: A low-melting-point alloy — tin-lead
- Melts when current exceeds its rated value — breaks the circuit; melts immediately during a short circuit
- Placed in series with the device or circuit — overcurrent protection prevents damage due to excess current
- Household wiring: A main fuse and individual circuit fuses; some appliances have built-in fuses
### Household and transport technologies → working principle — Household appliances
## 1. Uses of electromagnetic induction
- Generators — power generation; transformers — voltage conversion
- Induction cooktops — heating
- Wireless charging — mobile phones and electric vehicles

## 2. Osmosis and reverse osmosis
- Osmosis: Movement of solvent (water) through a semipermeable membrane — from low to high solute concentration; plant roots absorb water by osmosis
- Reverse osmosis: External pressure on the concentrated side — solvent moves from high to low concentration
- Uses: Desalination of seawater; purification of water — removes ions and bacteria
