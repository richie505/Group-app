### Three tiers — members, heads, elections and counts
- Three tiers: Gram Panchayat (village) → Mandal Parishad (intermediate, instead of Panchayat Samiti found in most States) → Zilla Parishad (district)
| Tier | Members | Head and mode | Term / age |
| Gram Panchayat | Ward members, directly elected | Sarpanch, directly elected [GK] | 5 years; minimum age 21 |
| Mandal Parishad (intermediate) | MPTC members, directly elected | Mandal Parishad President, elected indirectly from MPTCs | 5 years; 21 |
| Zilla Parishad | ZPTC members, directly elected | ZP Chairperson, elected indirectly from ZPTCs | 5 years; 21 |
- Chairpersons (Art. 243C): Mode of electing village-level chairperson left to State legislature; intermediate + district chairpersons elected indirectly (73rd Amendment)
- Minimum age to contest: 21 years (73rd Amendment floor, Art. 243F); other qualifications as for State legislature
- Zilla Parishads: Only 13 Zilla Parishads before reorganisation (May 2026)
- ⚠ AP intermediate tier = Mandal Parishad, not Panchayat Samiti

### State Election Commission — Articles, appointment, removal, AP SEC — Articles and functions
## 1. Articles and functions
- Articles: Art. 243K (panchayats) + 243ZA (municipalities) → Article 243ZA: same SEC gets municipal elections
- Functions: Superintendence, direction and control of electoral rolls + all local body elections, incl. rearrangement and republication of GP rolls
- Scope: Conducts local body elections only → does not conduct elections to State legislatures or co-operative bodies (APPSC 2025)
- Significance: Separate constitutional SEC ended States' power to postpone local polls indefinitely
- Structure: Uniform (single Commissioner, Governor-appointed, HC-judge removal); term + service conditions set by each State's law
### State Election Commission — Articles, appointment, removal, AP SEC — Appointment and removal

### Finances — State Finance Commission, FC grants, own revenue
## 1. State Finance Commission
- Art. 243I / 243Y: Governor constitutes a State Finance Commission every five years (SFC every five years)

- Part VI of 1994 Act gives effect to Art. 243I
## 2. Central Finance Commission grants
- 14th + 15th FC: Grants tied to drinking water and sanitation priorities
- 16th Finance Commission (2026-31): AP Rural Local Bodies get Rs 16,627 crore (AP Budget 2026-27)

## 3. Own revenue and schemes
- Main GP own-source revenue: Property (house) tax

- Eligibility: GP own-source revenue ≥ Rs 50 lakh; Block Panchayat ≥ Rs 1 crore; 3 years of tenure left
