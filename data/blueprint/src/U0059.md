# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:58 - Sangam Age - the three Sangams, sources; Tolkappiyam, Ettuthogai, Pattupattu, Pathinenkilkanakku
### The three Sangams
- About 2,400 poems by several hundred poets, including women: one of India's earliest secular literatures
| Sangam | Place | Survives |
| First | Madurai (Then Madurai), submerged | Nothing |
| Second | Kapatapuram, submerged | Only Tolkappiyam |
| Third | Madurai | Most of Sangam corpus |
### Basics
- Assemblies of poets under Pandya patronage
- Tradition from Iraiyanar Agapporul preface
- Accepted span: c. 300 BCE-300 CE; Tamil-Brahmi supports early limit
- Aham = love, emotion; Puram = war, valour, kings
- Rich in material culture + warrior ethic; poets knew varna (UPSC 2022)
### Corpus structure
- ▲ Melkanakku (18 major) — Ettuthogai + Pattupattu
- ▸ Ettuthogai (8 anthologies) — short poems; Padirruppattu = Chera kings; Purananuru, Akananuru
- ▸ Pattupattu (10 idylls) — long poems; Pattinappalai = Puhar, Karikala; Maduraikkanji
- ▸ Pathinenkilkanakku (18 minor) — ethics; incl. Tirukkural; largely post-Sangam
### Works & authors
| Work | Author | Subject |
| Tolkappiyam | Tolkappiyar | Oldest extant Tamil grammar |
| Tirukkural | Tiruvalluvar | 1,330 couplets: Aram, Porul, Inbam |
| Silappadikaram | Ilango Adigal | First Tamil epic; Kannagi-Kovalan |
| Manimekalai | Sittalai Sattanar | Buddhist epic, sequel |
| Sivaga Sindamani | Tiruttakkadevar | Jain epic |
### Foreign sources
| Source | Records |
| Periplus (anonymous Greek) | Ports, Muziris pepper, Arikamedu |
| Pliny, Natural History | Spices Chera, pearls Pandya, muslin Chola |
| Ptolemy, Geography | Classical account |
| Ashokan inscriptions | Chera, Chola, Pandya as neighbours (RE II) |
### Archaeological sites
- Arikamedu
- Near Puducherry
- Kaveripattinam (Puhar)
- Chola port
- Yavana settlements
- Keezhadi
- Most important Sangam urban site
- Contemporary with Second Urbanisation
- Pugalur
- 1st c. CE
- Three generations of Chera rulers

### Three Sangams → place, scholar, chronology
- "Sangam": Assemblies/academies of poets under Pandya patronage (all three Sangams)
- Source of tradition: Preface of Iraiyanar Agapporul (2026)
| Sangam | Place | Presiding scholar | Survives |
| First | Madurai (Then Madurai) - submerged | Agastya [GK] | Nothing |
| Second | Kapatapuram - submerged | Agastya / Tolkappiyar [GK] | Only Tolkappiyam |
| Third | Madurai | Nakkirar [GK] | Most of the Sangam corpus (few of many works) |
- ⚠ First and Third both at Madurai; Second at Kapatapuram
- Legendary chronology: Three-Sangam account (two submerged cities) = traditional, not historical
- Accepted span: c. 3rd century BCE-3rd century CE (300 BCE-300 CE)
- Tamil-Brahmi dating to the 3rd c. BCE supports the early limit
- Later works: Tirukkural + twin epics = post-Sangam
### Sangam literature — terms, corpus and anthologies
## 1. Language and corpus
- Language: Tamil (old Tamil)
- Corpus: About 2,400 poems by several hundred poets, including women poets
## 2. Terms → meaning
| Type | Theme |
| Aham (interior) | Love, personal emotion, nature |
| Puram (exterior) | War, valour, deeds of kings, ethics |
- Division: Both Ettuthogai and Pattupattu divided into aham and puram
- Melkanakku: Ettuthogai + Pattupattu = the 18 major works (Melkanakku)
- Pathinenkilkanakku = 18 minor works on ethics and morals, incl. the Tirukkural - largely post-Sangam
- ⚠ Melkanakku = 18 major; Pathinenkilkanakku = 18 minor
## 3. Anthologies → works
- Ettuthogai (8 anthologies): Short poems
- Padirruppattu - Chera kings
- Pattupattu (10 idylls): Longer poems on kings, cities, trade
- Tirumurugarruppadai, Porunarruppadai, Sirupanarruppadai, Perumpanarruppadai, Mullaippattu, Nedunalvadai, Maduraikkanji, Kurinjippattu, Malaipadukadam
- Pattinappalai - Puhar port, Karikala
## 4. Content
- Content: Polity, trade, cities, wars, economy
- Rich in material culture + warrior ethic
- Sangam poets knew the varna classification (UPSC 2022 key)
- Exam angle: statement questions - "devoid of material culture / no warrior ethic" are false.
### Tamil works → authors and subjects
- Tolkappiyam: Written by Tolkappiyar — the oldest extant Tamil grammar; grammar and poetics
- Silappadikaram: Written by Ilango Adigal — a Tamil work on Kannagi and Kovalan
- Sayana: A famous commentator on Vedic texts — wrote Vedic commentaries with Vijayanagara patronage
| Work | Author | Subject |
| Tolkappiyam | Tolkappiyar | Earliest extant Tamil work; also society and polity |
| Tirukkural | Tiruvalluvar | Ethics/philosophy - 1,330 couplets, 3 books Aram (virtue), Porul (wealth), Inbam (love) |
| Silappadikaram | Ilango Adigal | First Tamil epic; Kannagi-Kovalan (the anklet) |
| Manimekalai | Sittalai Sattanar | Buddhist epic, sequel to Silappadikaram |
| Sivaga Sindamani (Jivaka Chintamani) | Tiruttakkadevar [GK] | Jain epic [GK] |
- Other names of the Kural: "Laghuveda" (UPPSC 2013 key), "Tamil Veda", "Ulaga Podhumarai" (universal Veda)
- Silappadikaram: Ilango Adigal = younger brother of Chera Senguttuvan
- Merchant Kovalan, wife Kannagi, dancer Madhavi
- Kovalan executed by the Pandya king → Kannagi burns Madurai
- Manimekalai: Daughter of Kovalan-Madhavi becomes a Buddhist nun; reflects Buddhist philosophy
- UPPSC 2014 matching key:
- Tirukkural = philosophy
- Tolkappiyam = grammar
- Silappadikaram = love story
- Manimekalai = merchant story
### Foreign writers and inscriptions → what they record
| Writer / source | Work | Records |

| Pliny | Natural History | Spices from Chera, pearls from Pandya, muslin from Chola; Roman gold drain |

### Archaeological sites → findings
| Site | Note |
