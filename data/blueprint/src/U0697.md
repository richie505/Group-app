# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 5:7 - Programmes promoting S&T and innovation - INSPIRE, Ramanujan and J.C. Bose fellowships, Atal Innovation Mission, Atal Tinkering Labs
### INSPIRE ladder (DST, 2008)
- Brain drain → brain gain: VAJRA, Ramanujan Fellowship and INSPIRE
- ▲ AORC — INSPIRE Fellowship (PhD) + Faculty; 2025: 653 fellowships, 65% female
- ▸ SHE — Scholarship for Higher Education (not "Support for Her")
- ▸ MANAK — Classes VI-X, aged 10-15; ₹10,000 each
- ▸ SEATS — Early attraction: school students

### INSPIRE components → full form and target group
- Launch: DST flagship, launched 2008
| Component | Full form / scope | Target |
| SEATS | Scheme for Early Attraction of Talents for Science [GK] | School students |
| MANAK | Million Minds Augmenting National Aspiration and Knowledge | Classes VI-X, aged 10-15; 2025: 8.54 lakh ideas, 46,926 students given ₹10,000 each (IYB 2026) |
| SHE | Scholarship for Higher Education (NOT "Support for Her Education") | Top-ranked students in UG/PG natural and basic sciences |
| AORC | Assured Opportunity for Research Careers - INSPIRE Fellowship (PhD) and INSPIRE Faculty [GK] | Doctoral / post-doctoral researchers |
- INSPIRE Fellowship 2025: 653 doctoral fellowships; 65% female; ~31% former SHE scholars
### Fellowships and brain-gain schemes → awarding body and target
| Scheme | Awarding body | Target / purpose |
| VAJRA (Visiting Advanced Joint Research Faculty) | SERB (now ANRF) | Overseas scientists incl. NRIs/OCIs to work in Indian institutions for short spells [GK] |
| Ramanujan Fellowship | SERB, now under ANRF (IYB 2026) | Indian scientists abroad returning to India [GK] |
| Ramalingaswami Re-entry Fellowship | DBT | Indian life-science/biotech scientists abroad returning [GK] |
| J.C. Bose Fellowship (now JC Bose Grant, ANRF) | SERB/ANRF | Active senior scientists, in recognition of excellence (IYB 2026) |
| Swarnajayanti Fellowship | DST | Young scientists with proven record; begun for 50 years of Independence (1997) [GK] |
| PMRF (Prime Minister's Research Fellows) | Ministry of Education | PhD scholars in IITs, IISc, IISERs and other top institutions (2018) [GK] |
- Brain drain → brain gain schemes: VAJRA, Ramanujan Fellowship and INSPIRE (APPSC-G1 2022)
- ANRF schemes: PMECRG (PM Early Career Research Grant), Prime Minister Professorship, National Science Chair, N-PDF
- ⚠ Ramanujan = all scientists returning; Ramalingaswami = life-science only (DBT)
### Women and inclusion in STEM — schemes → target group
| Scheme | Body | Target group / purpose |
| Vigyan Jyoti | DST (agency: Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti) | Meritorious girl students of Class IX-XII (Class 9-12) [GK], to fix under-representation of women in STEM (CDX); 29,000+ girls covered (IYB 2026) |
| WISE-KIRAN (Knowledge Involvement in Research Advancement through Nurturing); WISE = Women in Science and Engineering | DST | Women scientists, esp. those with a career break - women's re-entry and career advancement in STEM; fellowships WISE-PhD, WISE-PDF, WIDUSHI - 340+ women selected (IYB 2026) |
| WIDUSHI | DST (under WISE-KIRAN) | Senior women scientists doing research in interdisciplinary S&T (APPSC-G1 2024) |
| CURIE | DST | 22 Women PG Colleges get state-of-the-art research facilities |
| WiSLP (Women in Space and Allied Science Leadership Programme) | DST, with British Council | Early/mid-career women scientists |
| GATI (Gender Advancement for Transforming Institutions) | DST | Institutions - gender-equity charter, modelled on UK's Athena SWAN [GK] |
| SERB-POWER (Promoting Opportunities for Women in Exploratory Research) | SERB (now ANRF) | Women researchers in academic/R&D institutions - research grants and fellowships [GK] |
| BioCARe | DBT | Women scientists (mainly unemployed/career-break) in biotechnology - first extramural grant [GK] |
- Beyond gender: SEED (Science for Equity, Empowerment and Development) has set up 52 STI Hubs for SC/ST
- Exam angle: senior women scientists + interdisciplinary = WIDUSHI; Vigyan Jyoti = women/girls in STEM (APPSC-GS 2023).

## 2. NM-ICPS and National Quantum Mission
- NM-ICPS: National Mission on Interdisciplinary Cyber-Physical Systems
- Cabinet 2018; ₹3,660 crore for nine years (older notes say five years)
- 25 Technology Innovation Hubs as Section-8 companies in host institutes
- Each hub has a technology vertical - AI/ML, robotics, cyber security, quantum etc.
- AP: IISER Tirupati TIH (IITTNiF) - Positioning & Precision Technologies
- National Quantum Mission (NQM): outlay ₹6,003.65 crore for 8 years
### Atal Innovation Mission and Atal Tinkering Labs — components and data
- AIM: NITI Aayog flagship, set up 2016 (announced in Budget 2015 - some notes say 2015)
| Component | Focus |
| Atal Tinkering Labs (ATLs) | Schools, grades 6-12: IoT, 3D printing, robotics; 10,000 ATLs in 700+ districts (IYB 2026) |
| Atal Incubation Centres (AICs) | Start-ups in universities/institutions |
| Atal Community Innovation Centres (ACICs) | Innovation in rural/under-served areas |
| ARISE | Atal Research and Innovation for Small Enterprises - MSMEs [GK] |
| Atal New India Challenges | Product innovation for national challenges (UPSC notes) |
## 1. AIM 2.0
- AIM 2.0 (2024): ₹2,750 crore (Rs 2,750 crore), runs till March 2028
- Shift: from building infrastructure → filling gaps in the innovation ecosystem and scaling innovations
- LIPI - 30 vernacular innovation centres, 22 scheduled languages
- 10 Atal Sectoral Innovation Launchpads
- First State Innovation Mission: Tripura under AIM 2.0 (Jun 2026)
