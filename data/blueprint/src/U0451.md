# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 3:7 - Economic planning in India - origins, Planning Commission, National Development Council
### Pre-independence plans
- NDC: neither constitutional nor statutory - an advisory body chaired by the Prime Minister
- 1931 — Karachi Resolution (INC)
- 1934 — M. Visvesvaraya: Planned Economy for India ("Father of Indian Planning")
- 1938 — National Planning Committee: Bose initiated, Nehru chaired
- 1944 — Gandhian Plan (Shriman Narayan Agarwal); Bombay Plan (J.R.D. Tata, G.D. Birla)
- 1950 — Planning Commission; first Deputy Chairman Gulzarilal Nanda
- 1952 — NDC (August 1952), executive resolution
- 2012-17 — Last Plan: Twelfth; last Deputy Chairman M.S. Ahluwalia
### NDC at a glance
- Status
- Non-constitutional, non-statutory
- Advisory; not binding
- Meets at least twice a year
- Composition
- Chairman: Prime Minister
- Members: CMs of all states
- Secretary: Planning Commission Secretary
- Role
- Now: NITI Aayog Governing Council
### Plan-making flow
- 1. Planning Commission drafts, assesses resources ↓
- 2. NDC approves (can modify) ↓
- 3. Union Cabinet ↓
- 4. Parliament → operative document

### National Development Council — set-up, status, composition, functions
## 1. Set-up and status
- Set up: August 1952 by executive resolution on the First Plan's recommendation
- Status: Neither constitutional nor statutory; advisory body to the Planning Commission
- Recommendations not binding; to meet at least twice a year
- Present: Formal resolution to abolish it still pending; role passed to NITI Aayog's Governing Council
## 2. Composition
- Chairman: Prime Minister
- Secretary: Secretary of the Planning Commission
- Members: CMs of all states
- Not members: President, Vice-President, Governors
- ⚠ Trap: The Finance Commission Chairman is not a member (UPSC 2013)
## 3. Functions & coordinating role
- Objectives:
- Secure states' cooperation in the plan
- Mobilise resources
- Promote common economic policies
- Balanced and rapid development of all regions
- Coordinating role: Approved the draft Five-Year Plans; linked the Planning Commission with the States
- National Planning Council (1965): Expert advisory body attached to the Commission
### Functions → body: Planning Commission vs NDC vs Finance Ministry
## 1. Planning Commission vs Finance Commission
| Basis | Planning Commission | Finance Commission |
| Nature | Non-constitutional | Constitutional — Article 280 of the Constitution |
| Dealt with | Planning and resource allocation | Tax devolution and grants |
| Old framework | Plan resources | Non-Plan resources |
## 2. Which body did what in plan-making
| Task | Body |
| Assess the country's resources; formulate the draft plan | Planning Commission |
| Prescribe guidelines for the national plan, incl. assessment of resources for it; approve the draft | National Development Council (apex approving body) |
| Final approval, making it the operative document | Union Cabinet, then Parliament |
| Budgetary allocation for plan spending | Finance Ministry [GK] |
- Flow: Commission staff draft → NDC approves (can modify) → Cabinet → Parliament
- Other notes put the Cabinet before the NDC
- APPSC-G1 2012: "guidelines for formulation of the national plan including assessment of its resources"
- ⚠ Trap: Worded as an NDC function in the notes; the Commission's own function is assessing the nation's resources → read the wording closely
### Terms → meaning: epithets of the Planning Commission
- Epithets: "super cabinet", "economic cabinet", "parallel cabinet", "the fifth wheel of the coach"
- Criticism: centralised decisions + eroded state autonomy
- Advisory Planning Board: "Common plan" idea induced centralisation → lasting source of Centre-state tension

- Composition: CM as Chairman + a Vice-Chairman + 6-9 members

### NDC at a glance
- NDC — approved draft Five-Year Plans; meant to meet at least twice a year
- Now inactive; role passed to NITI Aayog's Governing Council; formal abolition pending
- Non-constitutional, non-statutory, advisory; advice not binding
- Sarkaria Commission — constitutional status under Article 263; rename National Economic and Development Council
- Composition — PM chairs; Secretary of the Planning Commission as Secretary; Chief Ministers of all states members
- President not a member; Deputy Chairman of Planning Commission not its chairman
- NITI Aayog — think tank; bottom-up planning and cooperative federalism (not mainly a fund allocator)
### Planning vs Finance Commission
- ★ Finance Commission (Article 280) — constitutional body suggesting tax share of the States
- Finance Commission — tax devolution and grants; Planning Commission — planning and resource allocation
- Planning Commission (1950) and NITI Aayog (2015) — set up by Cabinet resolutions
- Planning Commission — in line with Article 39 (Directive Principles)
### Constitution & committees
- ★ Economic and social planning — Concurrent List (List III, Seventh Schedule), Entry 20
- Metropolitan Planning Committees — Article 243ZE; District Planning Committees — Article 243ZD
- Thungon sub-committee (1988) — Zila Parishad the centre of planning (not Gram Sabha)
### State & AP bodies
## Approval chain
- Draft Five Year Plan approved by the NDC; Planning Commission assessed resources and prepared it; Finance Ministry gave budgetary allocation
- State Planning Board — chaired by the Chief Minister; State draft plan sent to Planning Commission
- Draft district plan (from Panchayat and Municipal plans) sent to the State by the DPC Chairperson
- DPC — four-fifths elected by and from elected members of district panchayat and municipalities; MPC — two-thirds elected
## Status and criticism
- Planning Commission — "super cabinet", "economic cabinet", "parallel cabinet", "fifth wheel of the coach": centralised decisions, weakened State autonomy
- Zonal Councils — statutory; NDC and Planning Commission neither constitutional nor statutory; NITI Aayog Governing Council not statutory
- AP — district-level decentralised planning first in the Sixth Plan (1980–85); Rs 1 crore each to 23 districts; District Planning Councils
