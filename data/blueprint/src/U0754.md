### Renewable energy — definitions, classification and exam keys
## 1. Definitions
| Basis | Types |
| Renewable | Naturally replenished, usable indefinitely - solar, wind, hydro (incl. tidal/wave), geothermal, biomass |
| Non-renewable | Finite on a human timescale - coal, oil, natural gas, nuclear fuel |
| Conventional | Coal, petroleum, natural gas, hydel, firewood, cattle-dung cake |
| Non-conventional | By definition renewable (UPPSC 2023 key) |
## 2. Exam keys
- Nuclear: Nuclear is NOT renewable (APPSC 2024)
- Green energy: Natural gas is NOT green energy - algae and sunlight are (APPSC 2019)
- Most important for India: Solar energy (APPSC-G2 2017)
## 3. Sources not derived from the Sun
- Stored solar energy: Most energy - fossil fuels, biomass, wind, hydro
- NOT from the Sun:
- Nuclear (fission of heavy nuclei)
- Geothermal (Earth's internal heat) - UPPSC 2022 key accepted both
- Exam angle: "Which is NOT renewable / NOT green / NOT from the Sun" - nuclear, natural gas, geothermal are the usual answers.

### Obligations and programmes — RPO, RCO, Green Energy Corridors
## 1. Renewable Purchase / Consumption Obligation
- RPO: DISCOMs and large/designated consumers must buy a set share of power from renewables
- Solar RPO raised to 8% by March 2022 (2017)
- Wind RPO trajectory up to 2030 declared
- RCO : RPO recast as an obligation to consume renewable energy under the Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act 2022 [GK]. Not in your sources: the current year-wise RCO percentages.
## 2. Green Energy Corridors
- Purpose: Evacuate RE from RE-rich zones to load centres
- Intra-State GEC-I states: Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, HP, Karnataka, MP, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, TN
- Cost ₹10,141.68 cr = MNRE 40% : KfW (Germany) loan 40% : state utility 20%
- Intra-State GEC-II: Approved 2022 for 7 states, ~20 GW RE
- Inter-state GEC: Lines built by PGCIL
- ⚠ GEC-I funding 40 : 40 : 20 = MNRE : KfW : state utility
