### Chairpersons and members → appointment, removal and composition
- Appointment: By Central Government, usually on recommendation of a search-cum-selection committee
- Examples: DPBI; CCI (committee headed by Chief Justice of India or a nominee)
- Removal: By Central Government, only on grounds in the parent Act + after a hearing
- Usual grounds: insolvency, conviction for moral turpitude, incapacity, abuse of position
- CCI composition: Chairperson + 2 to 6 other members, all appointed by the Central Government
- Older notes say "Chairperson and six Members"; original 2002 Act allowed up to 10 members
- DPBI composition: 4-member board + online complaints portal (DPDP Rules 2025)
- Appointed by Central Government on recommendation of a Selection Committee

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 2:100
### Regulator → Act & year
- TRAI — set up 20 February 1997 under the TRAI Act, 1997
- Removal of a regulator's chairperson — by the Central Government, only on grounds in the parent Act, and after a hearing
- Data Protection Board — four members (DPDP Rules, 2025); set up under the DPDP Act, 2023
### Ministry & role
- TRAI — tariffs, interconnection, quality of service in telecom; TDSAT — disputes and appeals
- FSSAI — run by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (not DGHS); Eat Right India launched 2018
- PNGRB — under the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas; regulates City Gas Distribution networks; not electricity transmission
- CEA, APTEL, CERC — statutory bodies under the Ministry of Power
- DPDP Act, 2023 — Board's maximum penalty Rs 250 crore; no imprisonment
### Appeals
| Orders of | Appeal to |
| CCI | NCLAT from 26 May 2017 (earlier COMPAT); then Supreme Court |
| CERC and SERCs | APTEL |
| Data Protection Board | TDSAT within 60 days; then Supreme Court |
- CERC — inter-State matters; SERCs — mainly intra-State tariffs and consumer protection
### Laws replaced
- Competition Act, 2002 — replaced the MRTP Act, 1969 and the MRTP Commission
- Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 — joined eight laws and orders, incl. the Prevention of Food Adulteration Act, 1954
- Viksit Bharat Shiksha Adhishthan Bill, 2025 — brought in the Lok Sabha, December 2025; sent to a Joint Committee; not passed
- Three councils: Regulatory, Accreditation, Standards (no Funding Council)
### CCI powers & recent actions
- CCI vs WhatsApp 2021 privacy policy — abuse of dominant position
- CCI penalty on Meta/WhatsApp — ₹213.14 crore, for abuse of dominance
- Combinations — mergers and acquisitions reviewed by the CCI (not cartel agreements)
- ₹2,000 crore deal-value threshold — targets start-up 'killer acquisitions'
- Proposed Digital Competition Bill — pre-regulates Systemically Significant Digital Enterprises
### FSSAI, TRAI, AP
- Regulator chairpersons — appointed by the Central Government, usually on a search-cum-selection committee's recommendation (not elected by Parliament)
- TDSAT — set up May 2000; also hears appeals under the IT Act, 2000 and the DPDP Act
- CCI — a Chairperson and two to six other members, appointed by the Central Government
- From 1 July 2019 — newspaper and recycled plastics not to be used to wrap food
### Exam corner
- TDSAT (May 2000) — made by the TRAI (Amendment) Act; split adjudication from TRAI's regulation
- No separate 'TDSAT Act, 2000'
- CCI — statutory under the Competition Act, 2002 (amended 2007); under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs
### Laws replaced, repealed and amended
- ★ Match Act → year of enactment: Essential Commodities Act, Agricultural Produce (…) Act, and others; key I-C, II-D, III-A, IV-B (1954 is the year matched to the third Act; full lists not given)
