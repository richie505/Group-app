### PMGSY and PMAY-G — norms, phases and progress
## 1. PMGSY
- Objective: Single all-weather road to eligible unconnected habitations; now connects 99.6% of targeted eligible areas (Aug 2026)
| Area | Population norm |
| Hill States, NE, deserts, Schedule V/tribal areas | 250+ |
| LWE-affected blocks | 100+ |
- Phases:
- PMGSY-I (2000)
- PMGSY-II (2013, upgradation)
- PMGSY-III (2019, through routes and major rural links)
- ⚠ 500 – 250 – 100 = plains – hill/NE/tribal – LWE
## 2. PMAY-G
- Unit assistance: Rs 1.20 lakh (plain) vs Rs 1.30 lakh (hilly/difficult terrain) per house
- Beneficiary selection: SECC 2011 housing-deprivation data, verified by the Gram Sabha
- Progress: 3.13 crore rural homes completed by mid-2026
### Jal Jeevan Mission — FHTC, service level, JJM 2.0, Har Ghar Jal
## 1. Terms → meaning
| Term | Meaning |
| FHTC (Functional Household Tap Connection) | To every rural household + public institutions (schools, anganwadis, GP buildings, health centres, ashramshalas) |
| Service level | 55 litres per capita per day (LPCD) of prescribed quality |
| Har Ghar Jal certified village | Every household and institution has a tap, certified by Gram Sabha resolution; 2.12 lakh+ villages certified (Mar 2025) |
| Sujalam Bharat / Sujal Gaon ID | Digital ID for every rural piped scheme |
## 2. Data - coverage
- Target = 19.36 crore rural households
- Connected = 15.86 crore (81.94%)
- 2.89 lakh fully covered villages (Aug 2026)
## 3. Timeline, JJM 2.0 and funding
- Deadline: Original 2024 → Union Cabinet extended to December 2028 (Mar 2026) as JJM 2.0
- Shift: "From infrastructure creation to service delivery"
- JJM 2.0 outlay: Rs 8.69 lakh crore = Centre Rs 3.59 lakh cr + States Rs 5.10 lakh cr
| States | Sharing pattern |
| Himalayan/NE States + UTs with legislature | 90:10 |
| UTs without legislature | 100% Centre |
- Union Budget 2026-27: Rs 67,600 crore (2025-26: Rs 67,000 crore)
### Rurbanisation — PURA (Kalam) and SPMRM
## 1. Persons and terms
| Term | Meaning / originator |
| PURA = Provision of Urban Amenities in Rural Areas | Dr A.P.J. Abdul Kalam; elaborated in Target 3 Billion (co-author Srijan Pal Singh) |
| Rurbanisation | SPMRM/PURA → answer to rural-urban divide and distress migration |
| Rurban cluster | Rural areas showing urban signs → rising density, non-farm jobs, growing economic activity |
- PYQ: The model aimed at bridging the rural-urban divide
- ⚠ Target 3 Billion = Abdul Kalam + Srijan Pal Singh
## 2. Shyama Prasad Mukherji Rurban Mission (SPMRM)
- Launch: SPMRM (2016), 21 Feb 2016 at Kurubhat, Rajnandgaon (Chhattisgarh) → successor/expansion of PURA
- Aim: Develop 300+ Rurban clusters keeping the "rural soul" with "urban amenities"; curb distress migration
- Objective (PYQ): Stimulate local economic development, enhance basic services, create well-planned clusters
- Funding:
- Mainly convergence of existing schemes (MGNREGA, PMGSY, SBM, PMAY)
- + Critical Gap Funding (up to 30% of project cost)
- Centre:State = 60:40; 90:10 for NE and Himalayan States
- AP: MoRD classified 359 panchayats in AP under Rurban clusters
## 3. Rurban cluster size
- Cluster size: Contiguous villages
- Plain/coastal = 25,000-50,000 population
- 16th FC: One-time "Urbanisation Premium" for merging peri-urban villages + a Rural-to-Urban Transition Policy

### Jal Jeevan Mission

- ITDA/TADP — raise farm output (modern inputs, horticulture, fruit plantations), support tribal livelihoods
