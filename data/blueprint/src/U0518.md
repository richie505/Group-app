# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 3:74 - Foreign Trade Policy - objectives, incentives, EOUs; trade facilitation and logistics costs
### Trade policy history
- FTP 2023: from incentives to remission; $2 trillion exports by 2030
- 1985-88 — First EXIM policy
- 1991 — QRs → price-based regime
- 1992 — Partial convertibility: 60% market rate, 40% official
- 2000 — EXIM Policy announced March 31, 2000 (1997–2002 framework)
- 2004 — EXIM → Foreign Trade Policy
- 2023 — FTP 2023 from 1 April 2023, no end date
### FTP 2023 features
- Target $2 trillion exports by 2030 (⚠ Goyal Jul 2026: $1 trillion goal)
- Shift from incentives to remission; e-initiatives
- Districts as Export Hubs; new Towns of Export Excellence
- Trade settlement in INR; merchanting trade promoted
- One-time amnesty for EPCG/Advance Authorisation defaults
- Run by DGFT (Dept of Commerce), Vanijya Bhavan
### Export incentive schemes
| Scheme | What it does |
| RoDTEP | Replaced MEIS; refunds unrebated taxes; run by CBIC |
| RoSCTL | Replaced RoSL; apparel, made-ups |
| SEIS | Services reward, FTP 2015-20; discontinued |
| EPCG | Zero-duty capital goods vs export obligation |
| Advance Authorisation | Duty-free inputs for exports |
| Duty drawback | Refund of duty on inputs used in exports |

### Exam corner
- Import quota = quantitative restriction (PYQ 2019)

### Trade policy instruments, integration stages and history → meaning and year
## 1. Instruments → meaning
| Term | Meaning |
| Import quota | Quantitative restriction on the amount of imports (PYQ 2019) |
| Tariff | Tax on imported goods [GK] |
| Import substitution | Inward-looking; produce import substitutes at home - aimed at reducing import dependency (PYQ) |
| Export promotion | Outward-looking; push manufactured exports (APP) |
- Import substitution: Ran for about four decades
## 2. Stages of integration
| Stage | Features |
| Customs union | A common external tariff; free internal trade |
| Common market | A customs union with free movement of factors |
| Economic union | A common market with coordinated economic policies |
## 3. Policy history → year
| Year | Step |
| 1985-88 | First EXIM policy |
| 1991 | 1991 Trade Policy: Moved from QRs to a price-based regime |
| 1992 | 1992 convertibility: Partial current-account convertibility - 60% of forex at market rate; 40% at official rate; raw materials + capital goods under OGL |
| 2000 | EXIM Policy 2000: Announced on March 31, 2000 — part of the 1997–2002 framework |
| 2004 | EXIM → FTP: EXIM policies gave way to the Foreign Trade Policy from 2004 |
- Administration: Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industry through DGFT
### Foreign Trade Policy 2023 → targets and features
## 1. Timeline & targets
- In force: 1 April 2023, with no end date
- Export target: $2 trillion by 2030
- Board of Trade (Jul 2026): Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal - $1 trillion exports goal "difficult, yet possible" (TH 4 Jul 2026)
- ⚠ FTP target $2 trillion by 2030 vs Goyal's $1 trillion exports goal
## 2. Key features
- Approach: Shift from incentives to remission + entitlement-based support; e-initiatives
- Districts as Export Hubs: District-level export focus
- Rupee trade settlement: International trade in INR
- Towns of Export Excellence: New towns added
- Amnesty scheme: One-time amnesty scheme for EPCG/Advance Authorisation export-obligation defaults
- Merchanting trade: Promoted

- EOU / EHTP / STP: Export all output but can be located anywhere; duty-free inputs

- MEIS — goods exports; SEIS — services exports (FTP 2015-20)

- RELIEF Scheme 2026 — via ECGC; war-risk and logistics costs in West Asia

### Trade bodies
- ★ ECGC (1957, wholly govt-owned) — export financing and insurance; credit risk insurance

### Export documents & facilitation
- ★ Letter of Credit — produced by the importer

- ICEGATE — customs e-filing portal of CBIC
- SWIFT — single-window import clearance
### Exam corner

- Import quota — quantitative restriction; tariffs, countervailing and anti-dumping duties are price-based
