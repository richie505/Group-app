### Ecocide and international law
## 1. News
- Lebanon accused Israel of ecocide (2023–24 conflict); Iran alleged toxic "black rain" after strikes on fuel depots
- Demands to add ecocide as crime under Rome Statute; Council of Europe adopted a convention in 2025 criminalising severe environmental destruction
## 2. Key facts
- Term coined: 1970 by biologist Arthur W. Galston (Vietnam War, Agent Orange)
- Rome Statute Article 8: environmental damage a war crime only if intentional, widespread, long-term and severe
- Countries with ecocide laws: Vietnam, Russia, Ukraine, France, Belgium, Chile
- ENMOD Convention: bans hostile environmental modification techniques
