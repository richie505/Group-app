### Rivers of Africa and the Americas
## 1. Africa
- Nile:
- The White Nile originates in Lake Victoria; the Blue Nile in Lake Tana (Ethiopia)
- They meet at Khartoum (Sudan); the Nile flows into the Mediterranean Sea
- The Nile basin has 11 countries — ⚠ Chad is not in the Nile basin
- Egypt: Known as the "Gift of the Nile"
- Flows south to north; Uganda lies almost wholly in the Nile basin (LENSD, Aug 2026); longest river [GK]
- Congo: Drains into the Atlantic Ocean — the Congo Basin is the world's second-largest tropical rainforest and the world's largest tropical peatland
- Zambezi: Africa — has (associated with) Victoria Falls; Kariba Dam; forms part of the Zambia–Zimbabwe boundary; drains into the Indian Ocean
- Niger: West Africa — the "Oil River" of West Africa; associated with the historical palm-oil trade; an inland delta; the Niger Delta is a major petroleum region; drains into the Atlantic Ocean
## 2. South America
- Amazon: Originates in the Andes, in Peru — flows mainly through Brazil; drains into the Atlantic Ocean
- Peru, Colombia, Brazil; largest discharge [GK]
- Orinoco: Flows through Venezuela and Colombia
- Paraná–Paraguay river system: Important for the Southern Cone
## 3. North America
- Mississippi: A bird's-foot delta; originates in Lake Itasca (Minnesota); drains into the Atlantic Ocean
- Flows N to S; fertiliser run-off creates ocean "dead zone" at its outlet (UPSC)
- The Mississippi–Missouri is a combined river system — very long
- Colorado: USA — cuts (carved) the Grand Canyon; Lake Mead and Hoover Dam; ⚠ not on the Mississippi
- St. Lawrence: Connects the Great Lakes to the Atlantic Ocean
- Rio Grande: A major section of the USA–Mexico international boundary — called Río Bravo in Mexico
### Rivers of Asia and Europe
## 1. Asia
- Yangtze: The Three Gorges Dam — the world's largest hydroelectric power project; associated with the Pacific / East China Sea category
- Huang He — also known (called) as the Yellow River:
- Called the "Sorrow of China" — "China's sorrow"
- A high sediment load — heavy silt — due to erosion of the Loess Plateau
- Drains into the Bohai Sea
- Mekong: The "Danube of the East" — flows through China, Myanmar, Laos, Thailand, Cambodia and Vietnam; source: Tibetan plateau [GK]
- ⚠ Does not flow through Malaysia; drains into the South China Sea — not the Andaman Sea; associated with the Pacific / East China Sea category
- Amur: The Far East of Russia and China — Russia-China border; boundary river; mouth: Sea of Okhotsk [GK]
- Ob, Yenisei and Lena: Siberian rivers — flow north across Siberia into the Arctic Ocean; spring ice blockage contributes to flooding
- Amu Darya and Syr Darya: Central Asia — drain into the Aral Sea; their diversion for irrigation contributed to the shrinkage of the Aral Sea
- Tigris and Euphrates: The twin rivers of Mesopotamia — meet to form the Shatt al-Arab; ultimately drain into the Persian Gulf
- Mo Chhu: source Bhutan; joins Pho Chhu at Punakha → Sankosh (APPSC-GS 2019) [GK]
## 2. Europe
- Volga: Drains into the landlocked Caspian Sea — the Caspian Sea is endorheic; source: Valdai Hills, Russia; longest river of Europe [GK]
- Rhine: Flows through Europe — Switzerland, Germany and the Netherlands; enters the North Sea near Rotterdam; associated with a rift valley; an important waterway
- Source: Swiss Alps (Lake Toma); also Austria, Liechtenstein, France; flows north; forms Germany-France border (LENS, Aug 2026)
- Danube: Drains into the Black Sea — source: Black Forest (Germany); crosses most countries (10) incl. Austria, Slovakia, Hungary, Serbia, Romania [GK]
- Dnieper: Drains into the Black Sea — source: Russia (Valdai Hills); Russia, Belarus, Ukraine [GK]
- Thames: Drains into the North Sea — ⚠ not the Irish Sea
- Elbe: Czech Republic → North Sea; Germany (Hamburg) [GK]
