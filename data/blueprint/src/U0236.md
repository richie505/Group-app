### Palnad forest (Pullari) satyagraha, 1921–22 — place, grievance, action
## 1. Place and year
- Place & year: Palnadu, Guntur district; 1921-22 (APPSC 2012 key)
- The first forest satyagraha in Andhra (APPSC 2023 key)
- Some notes date the Pullari rebellion from Sept 1921
## 2. Grievance
- Heavy Pullari (grazing) tax = 12 annas per cow, Rs 1.50 per buffalo, goats banned
## 3. Action
- Social boycott of officials at Macherla (even the Collector and SP)
- 300 cattle driven into the forest untaxed → impounded at Minchalapadu
- Kanneganti Hanumanthu: Shot dead in police firing during the cattle-rescue at Minchalapadu, 26 Feb 1922 (a "2022" print is a misprint)
## 4. Repression
- ⚠ Macherla = social boycott; Minchalapadu = cattle impounded, Hanumanthu shot
