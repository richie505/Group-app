- AP beats Indian average in NH density on both measures
- 17.64 km per lakh population (India 12.09); 53.65 km per 1,000 sq km (India 44.46)
- PMGSY — launched 25 December 2000; fully funded CSS for all-weather road connectivity to unconnected rural areas
