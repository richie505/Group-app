### Coal gasification, CBM and UCG → terms and products
| Term | Meaning |
| Coal gasification | Coal partly oxidised with oxygen + steam at high temperature and pressure → syngas (CO + H2 + CH4 + CO2) |
| CBM | Methane held in coal seams; partly free in joints, partly adsorbed in micropores; released by drilling wells + pumping out water |
| "Sweet gas" | CBM; few heavier hydrocarbons |
| UCG | In-situ process; air/oxygen + steam injected into coal seams → syngas underground |
- Gasification products:
- Ammonia for fertilisers (urea), hydrogen, DME, ammonium nitrate, petrochemicals
- Syngas can also run IGCC power plants
- ⚠ Trap (UPSC 2025 key): Of ethanol, nitroglycerine and urea, only two (ethanol + urea) can be made this way
- UCG: Uses deep, thin or unmineable coal
- MoEFCC expert panel refused to waive the 300 m minimum depth for UCG pilot projects
### Coal gasification → mission, schemes and projects
## 1. Mission and incentives
- National Coal Gasification Mission: Gasify 100 MT of coal by 2030; Coal Ministry says target will be met (26 Apr 2026)
| Scheme | Outlay and features |
| Coal gasification incentive scheme (2024) | ₹8,500 crore for public and private projects (VIS 2026) |
| Scheme for Promotion of Surface Coal/Lignite Gasification (Cabinet, 13 May 2026) | ₹37,500 crore; incentive up to 20% of plant and machinery cost, capped at ₹5,000 crore per project; targets about 75 MT toward the 100 MT goal; coal linkage tenure extended to 30 years (TH 14 May 2026; LENS) |
- ₹37,500-crore scheme status: No bidders turned up; bid windows planned every two months (5 Sep 2026)
- Import bill (substitutable products): LNG, urea, ammonia, coking coal, methanol, DME ≈ ₹2.77 lakh crore in FY2025 (Cabinet, May 2026)
- Revenue-share rebate: Commercial blocks get 50% rebate on revenue share if ≥10% of output goes to gasification
## 2. Projects → place
| Project | Place / feature |
| JSPL | Angul (Odisha): only commercial-scale gasification plant |
| Talcher | First gasification-based fertiliser plant due by Dec 2027 |
| BCGCL | BHEL-CIL JV formed 21 May 2024; first commercial coal-to-ammonium-nitrate plant at Lakhanpur, Jharsuguda (Odisha); 2,500 TPD coal in → 2,000 TPD ammonium nitrate out |
