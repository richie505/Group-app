### Wood's Despatch, 1854 and the three universities of 1857
## 1. Wood's Despatch
- Issued in 1854 — associated with Sir Charles Wood
- Proposed universities at Calcutta, Madras and Bombay on the model of London University
- Epithet: "Magna Carta of English Education in India"
- Provisions:
- Graded hierarchy: vernacular primary schools at the base → Anglo-vernacular high schools → affiliated colleges → universities
- Grants-in-aid system to encourage private (largely missionary) enterprise
- Universities in presidency towns: Calcutta, Bombay, Madras (1857)
- Government supervision of vernacular education; training of teachers
- English for higher + vernacular for lower education
## 2. Three universities of 1857
| University | Associated with | Located in | Year — established |
| Calcutta University | Under Lord Canning | Calcutta | 1857 |
| Madras University | The British Government | Madras | 1857 |
| Bombay University | Sir Charles Wood | Bombay | 1857 |
- Each was one of the three universities established following Wood's Despatch
