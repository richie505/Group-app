### GoI Act 1935 → the single largest source
## 1. Government of India Act, 1935 — the main blueprint

### Traps, disputes and PYQ keys
- Emergency source dispute:
- ROCKET sheets give the Emergency provisions to the GoI Act 1935 (backbone) and also list Weimar Germany for Emergency Provisions except the Financial Emergency
- UPSC and CDI notes tie Emergency Arts. 352, 356, 360 to Germany ("German Reich")
- Standard lists give the emergency provisions to the 1935 Act and only the suspension of FRs to Weimar
- Procedure established by law: Standard answer = Japan
- ⚠ Trap: UPSC notes also list it under Britain
- Election vs nomination (Rajya Sabha):
- ⚠ Trap: Questions test election versus nomination
- ⚠ Election of RS members = South Africa; nomination to RS + President's election = Ireland
- Exam angle: APPSC-G2 2023 matching key - Emergency provisions → GoI Act 1935; Suspension of FRs → Weimar Germany; Concurrent List → Australia; Single citizenship → Britain.
- Exam angle: APPSC asked "bicameralism from?" (GS 2024), "single citizenship from?" (GS 2019) and "which borrowed feature is from Britain?" (cabinet system, GS 2019). The answer each time is Britain. Distractors are USA and Canada.
- Exam angle: the traps are residuary powers (Canada, not USA; in the US they rest with the states[GK]) and the Concurrent List (Australia, not Canada).
- Exam angle: List I (feature) - List II (country) matching, and "which of these is from Britain?" (APPSC-GS 2019 key: Cabinet system).
### Universal angles
- Significance: "Drawn from various sources" (a listed salient feature)
- Philosophical part (FRs, DPSPs) + political part (parliamentary system) borrowed; structure largely from the 1935 Act
- Criticism & debates: Called a "borrowed constitution" or "bag of borrowings"
- Reply: features adapted to Indian conditions; many provisions India's own (DPSP as a "novel feature", the federal-unitary switch)
- Comparative note:
- Single citizenship (Britain) vs dual citizenship of the USA
- Ireland itself took DPSP from Spain
- Original text 395 Articles, 22 Parts, 8 Schedules → now about 470 Articles, 25 Parts, 12 Schedules

### GoI Act 1935: largest source
- Government of India Act, 1935 — the single largest source of the Constitution
- federal scheme, Governor's office, judiciary, Public Service Commissions, emergency provisions, administrative details
