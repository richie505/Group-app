### Varnas → role; groups outside the varna order — The fourfold order
## 1. Varna and Jati
- Varna: A four-fold social classification — the four Varnas are Brahmana, Kshatriya, Vaishya and Shudra
| Varna | Functions — associated with | Role in later Vedic texts |
| Brahmana | Priestly and religious functions | Priests and teachers; seeker of livelihood; initially one of 16 classes of priests, became the dominant priestly class; APP notes call them the most powerful class |
| Kshatriya (Kshatriya/Rajanya) | Warrior and ruling functions | Rulers, warriors; political power and protection |
| Vaishya | Agriculture, cattle-rearing and trade | Farmers, traders, cattle-rearers, some artisans; the tribute payers (tribute-payers) |
| Shudra | Servile and manual occupations | Service to upper three; no Upanayana, no right to study the Vedas |
- Jati: Consists of numerous birth-based social groups — ⚠ distinct from Varna
## 2. The fourfold order
- Origin: Purusha Sukta (Rigveda, 10th mandala) = first statement of the four-fold order
- "varna" and "shudra" words also occur in the Rigveda
- Dvija: Upper three = dvija / Dvija (twice-born, via upanayana)
- Aitareya Brahmana: Shudra = "servant of another", could be expelled and beaten/slain/struck at will; Vaishya = tribute payer
### Varnas → role; groups outside the varna order — Rigidity and emergence of caste
## 1. Rigidity and emergence of caste
- Later Vedic shift: Birth-based occupation + rigid hierarchy set in during the later Vedic period; social mobility fell
- Jatis: Proliferation (sub-castes, mixed castes) is post-Vedic
- Gupta-era diversification; post-Satavahana mixed castes in Andhra
- Exam angle: "caste as a new form of social organisation" questions test that varna rigidified after the Rig Vedic phase.
## 2. Occupational groups, guilds and FIRSTs
- Money-lending: Satapatha Brahmana = first reference to money-lending
- Guilds: Guild-like bodies of Vaishyas (gana, headed by a shreshthin) in later Vedic texts
## 3. Outside the varna order
- Outside the varna order: Vratyas, Nishadas / Vratyas and Nishadas
- Discrimination: Against Shudras and Chandalas in later Vedic society
- Chandala: Named as a despised birth in the Chhandogya Upanishad
- Formal rule: Untouchability as a formal rule = post-Vedic Dharmasutras
### Gotra and kinship terms → meaning
## 1. Gotra
- Meaning: Literally "cow-pen" → lineage from a common male ancestor
- Institution: Established in Later Vedic times
- Rule: gotra exogamy = no marriage within the same gotra
- + caste endogamy + sapinda prohibition ("sasan" rule) → North Indian pattern
## 2. Kinship terms
| Term | Meaning |
| Vamsa / vamsha | Lineage/dynasty |
| Kosa | Treasury |
- ⚠ Trap: Kosa (treasury) is the odd one out (UPSC 1996)
## 3. Scholar → work

### Ashramas and purusharthas — stages, duties and goals
## 1. Four ashramas
| Ashrama | Stage | Key duty |
| Brahmacharya | Student | Vedic study under a guru |

### Marriage — eight forms and types
## 1. Eight forms of marriage (Manu)
| Form | Nature | Approved? |
| Daiva | Daughter given to the officiating priest in lieu of sacrificial fee | Approved |
| Arsha | Groom gives a token cow and bull to the bride's father | Approved |
| Prajapatya | Given without dowry or demand, with the blessing; couple told to jointly perform dharma together | Approved |
