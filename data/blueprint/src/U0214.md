## 1. Surabhi - from Tholu Bommalata to family theatre
- First play Keechaka Vadham, staged at a landlord's wedding

- Still a family theatre about 140 years on

## 2. Terms → meaning
- Padya Natakam: Classical Telugu theatre with dialogue mainly in verse
| Feature | Padya natakam | Social drama |
| Content | mythological or epic (Keechaka Vadham, Gayopakhyanam, Prahlada Charitamu) | contemporary evils (bride-price, dowry, widowhood) |
| Language | sung verse (padyam) [GK] | prose in spoken Telugu (vyavaharika bhasha) |
| Examples | Surabhi repertoire, Gayopakhyanam | Kanyasulkam, Vyavahara Dharmabodhini, Varavikrayam |
### Nationalist role → Swadeshi troupes and banned plays
- Themes: Drama companies carried social-reform themes (Kanyasulkam, Veeresalingam's drama) before nationalist ones
- Swadeshi & NCM years: Troupes such as the Jatiya Nataka Rangam promoted swadeshi goods and national education
- Viplava Parivartanamu: Published and banned during the Non-Cooperation Movement
- Author: Gollapudi Sitarama Sastri per the APPSC G2 2019 key vs Kodali Anjaneyulu per the APPSC 2018 key
- Gollapudi Sitarama Sastri supported the NCM boycott of elections in 1920
- Why banned: A drama company could put a political idea before thousands of illiterate people in one evening → colonial officials watched troupes closely
- Not in your sources: other proscribed Telugu plays and their authors (add from the full CDX AP History notes).
- Exam angle: "banned during which movement" plus "who wrote it".
