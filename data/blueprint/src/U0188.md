### Battle of Padmanabham, 1794 → background, parties, outcome
## 1. Background
- Ruler: Chinna Vijayarama Raju (ruled 1760-94) removed step-brother and Diwan Sitarama Raju for misrule (1784)
- Sitarama Raju bribed Council members → got his post back
- Company demand: Rs 3 lakh / ₹3 lakh tribute + disbanding of troops → Raja (Chinna) Vijayarama Raju refused
- 2 Aug 1793: British seized the Vizianagaram fort
- Estate split into 3 parts (some notes: three collectorates)
- Raja ordered to live in Machilipatnam on a pension of Rs 1,200
## 2. The battle
| Point | Fact |
| Battle | Padmanabham (Battle of Padmanabha), between Bhimunipatnam and Vizianagaram |
| Date | 10 July 1794 (CDX, APHQ; CDI gives 8 July) |
| Parties | East India Company troops vs Vizianagaram zamindar Pusapati (Chinna) Vijayarama Gajapati Raju (II) |
| British commander | Colonel Prendergast |
| Result | British victory; the Raja and about 1,000 Kshatriya warriors (over a thousand) killed; compared to Culloden |
- ⚠ Sources differ: other notes say Col. "Fender Gast" in CDX's spelling (Pendergast)
## 3. Outcome
- Vizianagaram reduced to an ordinary zamindari
- Minor son Narayana Babu (hid at Makkuva) restored on accepting the Permanent Settlement (1802) + Rs 6 lakh peshkash / ₹6 lakh peshkash
- Exam angle: "Padmanabham decided whose defeat?" = Vijayarama Raju of Vizianagaram, not Bobbili.

### Limited response in Andhra → reasons; aftermath → repression, rewards, Crown rule
## 1. Reasons for the limited response (assertion-reason)
- Background grievances: 1800 cession; Wahabi conspiracy of 1839; 1853 Berar treaty (Berar, Raichur, Osmanabad/Dharashiv)
- Reasons for loyalty:
- Nizam and Salar Jung loyal; Madras Presidency quiet
- Zamindars gained from the Permanent Settlement
- People valued the stability of Company rule over the "earlier anarchy"
- Public loyalty meetings at Visakhapatnam, Rajahmundry, Vijayawada
- Kadiri (Rayalaseema): Attempt made to incite rebellion (APPSC G2 2008)
- APPSC-G2 2025 key: answer (4)
- A ("unaffected because Andhra had no local leaders") = false - Turrebaz Khan, Subba Reddy, Dandasena etc. existed

## 2. Repression
- Executions: Subba Reddy, Dandasena, Fida Ali hanged; Sheikh Peer Sahib arrested; across India thousands executed after mock trials
## 3. Rewards to loyalists
- Afzal-ud-Daula received the "Star of India"; Salar Jung (Mir Turab Ali Khan) honoured
- Raichur Doab + Dharashiv (Dharaseo, Osmanabad) restored to Hyderabad; its debt written off

### Universal angles
## 1. Significance
- Significance / local revolts: South India's revolts were local and scattered; the Nizam's loyalty kept the Deccan closed to 1857

### Uyyalawada Narasimha Reddy
- From the Palegar family of Uyyalawada, Koilakuntla taluk, Kurnool district
- After the 2022 reorganisation — Nandyal district
- Younger son of Pedda Mallareddy; maternal grandson of Nosam Palegar Jayarama Reddy
- Revolt began July 1846; ambush at Mundlapadu; Rudravaram and Koilakuntla looted
- Captured 7 October 1846 at the Jagannatha Swamy temple, Pairamalla Konda
- Sentenced by the Faujdari Adalat (January 1847); publicly hanged near Koilakuntla on 22 February 1847
- Orvakal airport (Kurnool, from 25 March 2021) — named Uyyalawada Narasimha Reddy Airport
### Hyderabad 1857: order
## Background
- Nawabs of Kurnool (from Golconda nobles) — weakened and removed 1839
- Mubariz-ud-Daula (Nizam's brother) — led the anti-British Wahabi Movement (1839); arrested 1839
- Background grievances — 1800 cession, 1839 Wahabi conspiracy, 1853 Berar treaty (Berar, Raichur, Osmanabad/Dharashiv)
## 1857
- Afzal-ud-Daula — followed Nasir-ud-Daula on 16 May 1857; ruled to 1869
- Mir Turab Ali Khan — Salar Jung I, Diwan; both pro-British
- 1st Cavalry of the Hyderabad Contingent — disarmed at Aurangabad (June 1857); Mir Fida Ali hanged
- Turrebaz Khan and Maulvi Allauddin — attacked the Residency to free Jamedar Chida Khan
- Attack defeated by Resident Davidson; Turrebaz Khan caught and shot near Toopran (1859)
## Aftermath
- Afzal-ud-Daula — got the Star of India; Salar Jung honoured separately
- Given back to Hyderabad — Raichur Doab and Dharashiv; debt cancelled
- Limited impact in South India — revolts local and scattered; Nizam's loyalty kept the Deccan out
