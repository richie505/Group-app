### Green Revolution technology — origins, timeline and phases
## 1. Scientific core and origins
- Scientific core: Use of HYV seeds (APPSC 2024)
- Wheat: From Mexican dwarf wheats (Kalyan Sona, Sonalika)
- Mexican wheat: In 1966, 18,000 t of Mexican wheat imported + crossed with local varieties in Punjab
- Wheat production rose from 5 to 17 MT
- Rice: IR-8 from IRRI, Philippines
- Pioneer state: Punjab = "Granary of India"
## 2. Timeline
- Start: Mid-1960s; HYV programme full-fledged from 1966, after the 1965-66 and 1966-67 droughts (foodgrains fell to 72.3 and 74.2 MT) + PL-480 dependence
- Launched during the Annual Plans (1966-69) → agricultural growth 4.16%
- Chronology (APPSC 2023): Intensive Agriculture District Programme (IADP, 1960-61) → first Green Revolution period ended → M.S. Swaminathan Research Foundation (1988)
| Phase | Years |
| Pre-Green Revolution | 1951-68 |
| Early Green Revolution | 1968-81 |
| Later Green Revolution | 1987-92 |
## 3. Second Green Revolution focus areas
- Pulses, oilseeds, millets (Shree Anna)
- Precision farming with drones and AI
- Micro-irrigation
- Integrated nutrient and pest management
- Climate-resilient varieties

### Acts and regulators — seeds, GM crops and pesticides
| Act / body | Year | Provision |
| Seeds Act 1966 | 1966 | Regulates quality of notified seeds |
| PPV&FR Act, 2001 | 2001 | Protects breeders; set up the PPV&FR Authority; terminator (GURT) seeds banned |
| GEAC | Rules 1989 under the Environment Protection Act 1986 | Approves GM crops (under MoEFCC) |
| Insecticides Act, 1968 + Rules, 1971 | 1968 / 1971 | Set up the Central Insecticides Board and the Registration Committee |
| Draft Pesticides Management Bill, 2025 | Comments due by 4 Feb 2026 | Would replace them; higher penalties + promotes biological pesticides |
| Endosulfan ban | 2011 | Banned by the Supreme Court |
- PPV&FR: Gives farmers the right to save, use, exchange and sell farm-saved seed - only as unbranded seed (not branded) (PYQ key)
- GM mustard DMH-11: Cleared by GEAC in 2022; under Supreme Court scrutiny
- Genome-edited rice (May 2025): DRR Dhan 100 Kamala (from Samba Mahsuri) + Pusa DST Rice 1 (from MTU1010)
- Pusa Rice DST1 due for commercial launch
- SDN-1 edits carry no foreign DNA → bypass GMO rules
- Banned elsewhere: India still uses at least 66 pesticides banned elsewhere
- Aldrin (banned): Killed mugger crocodiles in Kota
