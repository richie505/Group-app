# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 3:144 - Special Assistance Package vs Special Category Status - Government's stand and measures taken
### SCS vs package
- SCS = status with automatic 90:10 flows; package = equivalent money tied to projects
| Benefit | SCS | Package |
| CSS funding | 90:10 grant, automatic | Extra share only via EAP repayments |
| Tax concessions | Yes | Only Sec 94(1); weaker |
| Use of funds | All schemes | Tied to external projects |
| Duration | Promised 5 years | 2015-16 to 2019-20 |
### Package components
| Component | Content |
| Special assistance | Extra central share (90%) via EAPs |
| Polavaram (Sec 90) | Centre bears 100% of irrigation cost from 1 Apr 2014 |
| Tax (Sec 94(1)) | 10-year tax holiday delayed / subsumed in GST |
| Backward areas (Sec 46(2)) | Rayalaseema & North Coastal |
| Revenue deficit (Sec 46) | 2014-15 resource gap |
### EAP route
- 1. AP borrows from World Bank, ADB ↓
- 2. Centre repays loan + interest ↓
- 3. Up to SCS-equivalent 90% ↓
- 4. Problem: long approvals, heavy compliance
### Centre's stand
- Arun Jaitley: package announced September 2016
- 14th Finance Commission removed distinction, devolution 42% → absorbed SCS
- "Constitutional and technical constraints" for any new state

### Special Assistance Package — definition and Act provisions
- Announcement: September 2016 by Union FM Arun Jaitley in place of SCS
- Also called Special Assistance Measure (SAM) or "Special Package"
- Period: Five years 2015-16 to 2019-20 = matching promised SCS period
| Component | Content |
| Special assistance | Money equal to the extra central share AP would have got as an SCS state (90% grant), routed through Externally Aided Projects (CDI) |
| Polavaram | National project (Sec 90); Centre bears 100% of the remaining irrigation-component cost from 1 Apr 2014 (CDI) |
| Tax incentives | Industrial incentives under Sec 94(1) - promised 10-year tax holiday was delayed, diluted or subsumed in GST (CDI) |
| Backward areas | Special development package for Rayalaseema and North Coastal districts - Sec 46(2) (APP) |
| Revenue deficit | Centre to bridge the 2014-15 resource gap - Sec 46 (CDI) |
- ⚠ Sec 90 = Polavaram; Sec 94(1) = tax incentives; Sec 46(2) = backward areas; Sec 46 = revenue deficit
### Externally Aided Projects and SASCI — how the schemes work
## Externally Aided Projects route
- Mechanism: State borrows from multilateral agencies (World Bank, ADB, etc.) → Centre repays the loan and interest up to SCS-equivalent amount (no open grant)
- Share = SCS-equivalent 90%
- ⚠ Conflict: some notes say "Centre to bear 40% cost for EAPs"; fuller account + package design give 90%
- Problems: Long approval cycles + heavy compliance → AP says red tape and delays
- Current use: ₹15,000 crore for Amaravati raised via multilateral lenders
## SASCI
- SASCI: Run by Ministry of Finance; began Oct 2020
- 50-year interest-free loans, over and above net borrowing ceiling
- Outside 16th FC's 3% of GSDP deficit ceiling
- Capital works only; line ministries run sub-schemes
- Not in your sources: the SPV route proposed for special-assistance funds and NITI Aayog's role in the package.

### SCS vs Special Assistance Package — benefit by benefit

# MCQ NOTES OF APP TOPIC 3:144
### SCS vs package
- Package period: 2015-16 to 2019-20 (five years, the promised SCS period)
| Feature | Under SCS | Under Special Assistance Package |
| CSS funding | automatic 90:10 grant | equivalent amount only through EAP repayments |
| Use of funds | across all schemes | tied to approved externally aided projects |
| Industry | tax concessions for industry | only the weaker Section 94(1) incentives |
### Package components
- Polavaram treated as a national project under Section 90
- Industrial incentives linked to Section 94(1) (unrelated to Polavaram's status)
- Promised ten-year tax holiday: delayed, weakened or merged into GST; not fully done
### EAP route
- State borrows from agencies like World Bank and ADB; Centre repays loan and interest up to the SCS-equivalent amount
- AP's criticism: long approval cycles and heavy paperwork, causing red tape and delays
### Centre's stand
- 14th Finance Commission: removed the general-special category distinction and raised devolution to 42%; Centre said this covered SCS benefits
- Constitutional and technical constraints on giving SCS to a new State
- Feared similar demands from Bihar and Odisha
### Assistance released
- From 2024-25: ₹15,000 crore for Amaravati, ₹12,157 crore for Polavaram
- Special assistance for 2016-17: ₹4,058.04 crore (APPSC-G1 2017 key)
- ₹3,979 crore = 2014-15 revenue deficit grant; ₹24,350 crore = total SAM package; ₹2,500 crore = promised for the capital
### SASCI & recent
- SASCI (Scheme for Special Assistance to States for Capital Investment): Ministry of Finance, began October 2020
- 50-year interest-free loans for capital works only
- above the net borrowing ceiling, outside the 3% of GSDP deficit ceiling
- not for revenue expenditure, not EAP repayments
- August 2026: AP FM Payyavula Keshav said the State lost up to ₹38,000 crore because the 16th Finance Commission dropped revenue deficit grants
- revenue deficit: ₹16,000 crore (2014-19) → projected ₹48,000 crore (2025-26)
### Exam corner
- Package (also called SAM) announced September 2016 by Union FM Arun Jaitley
- Manmohan Singh gave the 2014 Rajya Sabha assurance on SCS
- Total Special Assistance Measure: ₹24,350 crore; of which ₹3,979 crore revenue deficit grant, rest for infrastructure
