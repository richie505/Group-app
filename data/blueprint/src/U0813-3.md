### AP urea use stable despite 36% rise in cropped area — e-Panta
## 1. News
- Rabi 2025–26: AP cropped area +36%, urea availability only +3%; per-hectare urea 513 kg → 338 kg (~34% cut)
- Driver: expansion of e-Panta digital crop mapping — farmers 17.56 lakh → 35.51 lakh; area 46.07 lakh → 73.55 lakh acres (~60%)
## 2. Fertilizer facts
- Urea: 46% nitrogen; only 30–40% absorbed by crops
- Ideal N:P:K = 4:2:1
- DAP = N + P; ⚠ MOP (Muriate of Potash) = potassic (not phosphatic); SSP = phosphatic
- Chlorophyll — N and magnesium (⚠ not phosphorus); phosphorus → roots, flowering
- Rhizobium — legumes; ⚠ zinc sulphate is a chemical micronutrient fertilizer, not biofertilizer
### AP Mushroom Mission — ₹13,000 crore
## 1. News
- AP (CM N. Chandrababu Naidu) preparing a "Mushroom Mission" — ₹13,000 crore; ~62 lakh production units (~5,000 sq ft each)
- Target: 67,500 tonnes/year — to surpass Bihar (current largest, ~45,000 tonnes)
- Subsidy: ~₹5,184 crore (~40%) — Centre + State; mushrooms to be classified as agricultural produce; SHGs central
## 2. Varieties and facts
| Variety | Feature |
| Milky mushroom | Heat-resistant — suits AP |
| Paddy straw mushroom | Fast-growing; paddy regions |
| Button mushroom | High demand; controlled environment; dominates global trade |
- ⚠ Mushrooms are heterotrophic fungi (not autotrophic); cycle 30–60 days; protein 20–30% dry weight
- Spawn = pure culture of fungal mycelium (the "seed"); fruiting triggered by lower CO₂ + more O₂
- ⚠ Units conflict: CARE monthly says ~1.62 lakh production units (daily page said 62 lakh)
- India's mushroom pioneers (CA360): Jitendra Kumar Singh, Dr Dayaram, S.S. Saini
### AP Rabi paddy procurement target 23 lakh MT
## 1. News
- Target: 23 lakh MT of paddy — Rabi season from April 4, 2026
- Procurement centres: 1,917; payment within 24 hours
- Gunnysacks: ~5.4 crore; 17,200 lorries tracked by GPS
- Previous Kharif: ~51 lakh MT procured; ₹11,300 crore paid to ~7.8 lakh farmers
## 2. Background
| Aspect | Rabi | Kharif |
| Sowing | October–December | June–July |
| Harvest | March–April | September–October |
- ⚠ Paddy is grown in both Kharif and Rabi seasons (not only Kharif); gunnysacks are traditionally jute
### Rubber plantation expansion in Polavaram for tribals
## 1. News
- Ministry of Tribal Affairs proposed rubber plantations over 3,000 acres in Polavaram district, AP — for Konda Reddi tribe
- Support: Rubber Board + ITDA (Integrated Tribal Development Agency)
## 2. Rubber Board and crop facts
- Rubber Board: under Rubber Act, 1947; Ministry of Commerce and Industry; HQ Kottayam, Kerala
- Climate: temp not below 21°C (ideal 25–35°C); rainfall ideal 200–400 cm, minimum ~150 cm, well distributed
- Vulcanisation: heating with sulphur → elasticity, strength
- Area: ~8.5 lakh ha; Kerala share fell ~90% → ~78%; Tripura + Assam >16%
## 3. Schemes
| Scheme | Key point |
| Rubber Production Incentive Scheme (RPIS) | Minimum price ₹150/kg |
| NE Mitra | Expand rubber over 2 lakh ha in NE |
| Rubber Plantation Development Scheme | Traditional + non-traditional areas |
| Proposed PLI | Rubber goods manufacturing |
### Free electricity for weavers and powerloom workers
## 1. Key facts
- AP scheme from April 1, 2026 — 200 units/month for 93,000 handlooms; 500 units/month for 11,488 powerlooms; annual cost ₹150 crore
- India's handloom sector — world's largest (~35 lakh families); National Handloom Day — August 7; Art 43 — cottage industries; Textiles Minister Giriraj Singh
### PCPNDT State Supervisory Board reconstituted
## 1. Key facts
- AP reconstituted Supervisory Board under PCPNDT Act (chaired by Health Minister); previous term ended February 7, 2024
- PCPNDT Act: 1994, amended 2003 — bans sex determination
### AP leads in cocoa cultivation
## 1. Rankings
| Crop | AP rank | Data |
| Cocoa | 1st | ~41% — 47,060 ha of 1.14 lakh ha; key district Eluru |
| Groundnut | 2nd | 2.01 lakh ha; Parvatipuram-Manyam |
- Funds: ₹51.13 crore (2024–25, 46.52% used); ₹105 crore (2025–26, 83.83% used)
