### Articles → provision; Fifth vs Sixth Schedule
## 1. Articles → provision
| Provision | Content |
| Art. 342 | President notifies STs for each state/UT after consulting the Governor. Only Parliament can change the list (ST Order, 1950). Art. 366(25) defines STs by reference to it |
| Art. 244(1) - Fifth Schedule | Scheduled Areas; Governor's special powers; Tribes Advisory Council |
| Art. 244(2) - Sixth Schedule | Autonomous districts in Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram |
| Art. 338A | National Commission for STs |
- Art. 342(2): Only an Act of Parliament can change the list; lists are state-specific
## 2. Fifth vs Sixth Schedule
| Feature | Fifth Schedule | Sixth Schedule |
| Area type | Scheduled Areas | Tribal Areas (autonomous districts/regions) |
| States | All states except Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram. Scheduled Areas are in 10: AP, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, HP, Jharkhand, MP, Maharashtra, Odisha, Rajasthan, Telangana (VIS) | Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram (IYB) |
| Machinery | Governor's special powers; Tribes Advisory Council (TAC) of up to 20 members, about 3/4 of them ST MLAs (IYB) | Autonomous District and Regional Councils (IYB) |

### PVTGs — criteria, timeline, state count and measures
## 1. Origin & criteria
- Origin: Dhebar Commission → PTG category (1973) → renamed PVTGs in 2006
- Four criteria:
- Pre-agricultural technology
- Low literacy
- Economic backwardness
- Declining/stagnant population
