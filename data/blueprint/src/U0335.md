### Articles → devolution provisions
| Article | Provision |
| 243G/243W and 243H/243X | The State "may" devolve → devolution is discretionary |
| 243K; 243ZA for municipalities | SEC: Appointed by the Governor; removable only like a High Court judge; service conditions cannot be varied to the SEC's disadvantage |
| 243E/243U | 5-year term; elections before expiry or within 6 months of dissolution |
| 243D | Women's reservation: constitutional floor one-third |
| DPC (Art. 243ZD), MPC (243ZE) | Integration of plans |
- Root cause: Neither Schedule transfers anything by itself; devolution is discretionary
- Before 1993: States postponed local polls at will
- Safeguards: SEC + 5-year term + 6-month rule → local government made permanent
- ⚠ Reservation floor one-third (Art. 243D); many states, AP included, give 50%
### Funds — own-source revenue and FC transfers (data)
## Own-source revenue
- ULB own revenue: Only ~0.3% of GDP
- Depend on tied grants; property registers outdated
- Property tax: Single most important own revenue of ULBs; under-assessed and under-collected
- Mismatch: Cities produce ~2/3 (about 67%) of GDP but get <1% of tax revenue
- GST impact: Cost ULBs ~19% of own revenue (octroi, entry tax)
- Bond market: Weak balance sheets keep most bodies out
## FC transfers
| FC | Rural LBs | Urban LBs | Other |
| 16th FC (2026-31) | ₹4.4 lakh crore | ₹3.6 lakh crore (about ₹3.56 lakh crore, ~₹75,000 crore a year) | Urban share 36% → 45%; 80% basic / 20% performance; basic grants 50% tied (sanitation, water) |

### Capacity-building schemes and institutions → features
## Rashtriya Gram Swaraj Abhiyan (RGSA)
- Core objective: Strengthen PRIs to achieve SDGs
- Key targets: Focus on 117 aspirational districts + convergence with Mission Antyodaya
- Timeline:
- Phase 1: Launched 2018-19
- Revamped RGSA: 1 April 2022 – 31 March 2026 (co-terminus with XV Finance Commission)
- ⚠ Trap: "1 April 2021 to 31 March 2026" is WRONG (UPSC 2026)
- Not in your sources: whether RGSA has been extended after 31 March 2026.
- Funding pattern:
- General states = 60:40 (Centre:State)
- NE & hilly states = 90:10
- UTs = 100% Central
- Merger: Backward Regions Grant Fund (BRGF) merged into RGSA (APPSC-GS 2018)
- Performance data:
- FY25: 35 lakh+ trained; Training of Trainers on own-source revenue
