### Party system — phases, social bases and terms
## 1. Phases
- One-party dominance / "Congress system" (1952-77)
- Transition + first alternation (1977-89, Janata)
- BJP majority since 2014
## 2. Character and social bases
- Multi-party character: India = multi-party system as it divides along region, language, caste, religion and class
- Social bases: caste, region/language, religion, class
- Regional parties rose on identity → TDP took power within 9 months on "Telugu atma gauravam"
## 3. Terms → meaning
| Term | Meaning |
| Kamaraj Plan (1963) | senior Congress ministers resigned to work in the party organisation |
| Inner-party democracy | parties seldom hold real internal elections; many RUPPs do not update office-bearer lists |
| Pressure groups → the "Anonymous Empire" | parties seek power; pressure groups seek influence |
## 4. Problems
- Party problems: money power, criminalisation (Vohra Committee 1993), casteism, defections, weak ideology
### Acts, sections, cases and committees on parties
| Law / case / committee | Year | Provision |

## 1. Registration conditions (Sec. 29A)
- Apply within 30 days of formation with party constitution
- Must pledge allegiance to the Constitution + socialism, secularism, democracy and national integrity
## 2. Funding rules
- Disclosure: parties must disclose donors above ₹20,000 every year
- Cash cap: donations above ₹2,000 only by cheque or bank transfer
- ⚠ ₹20,000 = disclosure limit; ₹2,000 = cash limit
### Recognition and classification — symbols, State party criteria, RUPPs
## 1. Symbols
- Recognised parties → reserved symbols
- RUPPs and independents → free symbols list (184 symbols in May 2025)
## 2. State party (any one)
- 6% of votes + 2 Assembly seats
- 6% in Lok Sabha poll + 1 LS seat
- 1 in every 25 LS seats
- 8% of votes alone
## 3. National status and RUPPs
- National status: AAP in 2023; NPP = first from the North-East
- RUPPs: 2,800+ registered, only about 750 contested the 2024 general election
- Delisting: non-compliant RUPPs now delisted after State CEOs verify them
