### Basics
- 1 nm = one billionth of a metre; nanoparticles are 1-100 nm
- Nanoparticle size: 1-100 nm (APPSC 2018)
- New properties: quantum effects + high surface-area-to-volume ratio
- Top-down: lithography, milling; bottom-up: self-assembly, CVD
- AFM works on non-conductors; STM only conductors
- Quantum dots: size-tunable colour; displays, solar cells

### Carbon allotropes — structure and properties (graphene, fullerene, nanotubes)
## 1. Structure
| Allotrope | Type | Hybridisation | Structure |
| Diamond | Crystalline | sp³ | A three-dimensional tetrahedral network — each carbon bonded to four others; a rigid lattice |
| Graphite | Crystalline | sp² | Layered hexagonal rings as planar sheets — layers held by weak van der Waals forces |
| Fullerene (C₆₀ — Buckminsterfullerene, Buckyball) | Crystalline or molecular | sp² | A hollow cage resembling a soccer ball — 12 pentagons, 20 hexagons, 60 carbon atoms |
| Graphene | Single-layer, two-dimensional | sp² | A single-atom-thick layer of graphite — hexagonal |
| Carbon nanotubes | Tubular | sp² | Cylindrical rolled graphene sheets; very strong, conductive |
| Amorphous carbon — coal, charcoal, coke, lampblack | Non-crystalline | Mixed | — |
## 2. Properties
| Allotrope | Properties |
| Diamond | The hardest natural substance — Mohs hardness 10; an electrical insulator; an excellent thermal conductor; transparent; high refractive index; high melting point |
| Graphite | Soft and slippery; a good electrical and thermal conductor; opaque, black; high melting point; chemically inert |
| Fullerene | A semiconductor; hollow cage; moderate hardness |
| Graphene | Extremely strong; an excellent electrical conductor; flexible; transparent |
| Amorphous carbon | Soft; porous — activated charcoal has a high surface area |
### Tools, synthesis and nanomaterials — terms → meaning
## 1. Nanomaterials
- Fullerenes and nanotubes: Lubricants, catalysts, research
- Quantum dots: Semiconductor nanostructures — size-tunable colour; used in displays and solar cells [GK]
- Nano-fabrication: Uses silicon — advanced semiconductor research
## 2. Tools and synthesis — terms → meaning
| Term | Meaning [GK] |
| AFM | Atomic Force Microscope; works on non-conductors too |
| Top-down | Cut bulk material down - lithography, milling, etching |
| Bottom-up | Build from atoms/molecules - self-assembly, CVD, sol-gel |
| Wells / wires / dots | Confinement in 1 / 2 / 3 dimensions (2D / 1D / 0D structures) |
- Other nanomaterials:
- Nanoporous materials: separation, catalysis
- Nanofibres: filtration, smart clothing, scaffolds
- Nanocrystals: LEDs, imaging

### Carbon allotropes
## Diamond
- ★ Diamond — hardest natural substance; least compressible, best heat conductor among natural materials
- Each carbon bonded to four others (tetrahedral 3D network)
- Electrical insulator (no free electrons); excellent thermal conductor (lattice vibrations) — two separate facts
- Transparent, very high refractive index
## Graphite
- ★ Graphite — lubricant for heavy machines; anti-seize, anti-friction
- ★ Soft and slippery — layers held by weak van der Waals forces, so they slide
- Opaque, black, good electrical conductor
## Graphene, fullerene, nanotubes
- Graphene — one-atom-thick sp² layer of graphite; strong single-atom sheet
- Rolled graphene cylinders — carbon nanotubes
- Fullerene (C₆₀) — hollow spherical cage of 60 carbon atoms
- ★ Most important property of nanomaterials (RAS 2013 key) — friction
