# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:215 - Prominent Andhra leaders - Konda Venkatappayya, Pattabhi Sitaramayya, Tanguturi Prakasam, Bulusu Sambamurthy
### The four leaders
- These four men led Andhra through NCM, CDM, Simon boycott, Sribagh Pact and statehood
- Konda Venkatappayya
- Born 22 February 1866, Guntur
- Dictator of Andhra Salt Satyagraha (G2 2008)
- First editor, Krishna Patrika (1902)
- Father of Andhra State Movement; Desabhakta
- Tanguturi Prakasam
- Born 23 August 1872, Vinodarayunipalem
- Andhra Kesari after Simon Go Back
- Swarajya (English daily, 1921)
- Premier, Madras, 1946; first CM, Andhra State
- Pattabhi Sitaramayya
- Andhra Bank (1923), Machilipatnam
- Lost Tripuri 1939 to Bose
- Presided Jaipur (1948); JVP Committee
- Governor, Madhya Pradesh (1952-57)
- Bulusu Sambamurthy
- "Maharshi"
- Reception chair, Kakinada session (1923)
- Speaker, Madras Assembly (1937)
### Events & roles
- 1920 — Calcutta special session: Konda opposed NCM resolution (1,886-884)
- 1921 — Vijayawada AICC: Pingali Venkayya shows flag to Gandhi (G2 2019)
- 3 Feb 1928 — Simon Commission arrives; Prakasam leads Madras boycott
- 16 Nov 1937 — Sribagh Pact: coastal signatories Prakasam, Sambamurthy
- 1951 — Prakasam → Kisan Mazdoor Praja Party with N.G. Ranga

- Andhra Kesari = Simon boycott, not QIM/NCM (G2 2008, 2019)

- Prakasam's Swarajya ≠ Gadicherla's Swarajya magazine
- Prakasam born 1872 - "1892, Valluru" is wrong
- ⚠ other notes: Konda = "Grandfather of the Andhra Movement"

## Tanguturi Prakasam
- Born 23 August 1872, Vinodarayunipalem (present Prakasam district)
- ★ Started Swarajya (1921), an English daily (APPSC 2011 key); Grama Swarajya — a separate weekly
- Andhra Kesari — for the Simon Go Back protest, Madras, 1928
- First Chief Minister of Andhra State; Bezawada Gopal Reddy second and last (28 Mar 1955 - 31 Oct 1956)
## Konda Venkatappayya
- Born 22 February 1866, Guntur; a lawyer who gave up practice for public work
- Dictator of the Andhra Salt Satyagraha / Civil Disobedience (APPSC G2 2008 key); jailed 1930, 1932, 1942
- Father of the Andhra State Movement
## Pattabhi Sitaramayya
- ★ Governor of Madhya Pradesh, 1952-1957
- Chaired Congress session at Jaipur, 1948; at Tripuri (1939) Gandhi's candidate, lost to Bose
- JVP Committee (Nehru, Patel, Pattabhi) — initially rejected linguistic States
- Wrote in The Hindu on the need for linguistic states
- Founded Andhra Bank (Machilipatnam, 1923), Andhra Insurance Company, Krishna Jilla Co-operative Bank
## Bulusu Sambamurthy
- Chairman, Reception Committee, Kakinada Congress session 1923
- Linked with the Simon Commission boycott campaign; Speaker of the Madras Assembly
### Events & roles
## Konda Venkatappayya
- Chaired the fifth Andhra Mahasabha, Nellore (1917)
- Co-founded Krishna Patrika (1902); opposed the Non-Cooperation resolution at Calcutta
## Tanguturi Prakasam
- 1937 Rajaji ministry — Revenue Minister; 1946 — Premier of composite Madras
- Coastal signatory to the Sribagh Pact — 16 November 1937
- 1951 — left Congress with N.G. Ranga; founded the Kisan Mazdoor Praja Party (later linked with the Praja Socialist Party)
- Naa Jeevita Yatra — four-part autobiography; three parts by Prakasam, fourth by Tenneti Viswanatham
## Others
- Bulusu Sambamurthy — Speaker of the Madras Legislative Assembly, 1937; toured Andhra for the Simon boycott
- Pingali Venkayya — book on national-flag designs 1916; showed the flag to Gandhi at the Vijayawada AICC (31 Mar - 1 Apr 1921, venue Gandhi Nagar) (APPSC G2 2019 key)
- N.G. Ranga — first Kisan school at Nidubrolu; School of Politics at Kanikapadu (1939)
