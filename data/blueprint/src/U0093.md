# NOTES OF APP TOPIC 1:92 - Kakatiyas - origins, Beta I, Rudradeva, Ganapatideva; Kakatiya-Yadava-Hoysala relations
### Origins
- 2nd dynasty after the Satavahanas to unify all Telugu-speaking regions
- First mention: Magallu grant 956 of Eastern Chalukya Danarnava (Gundana)
- Overlords: Rashtrakutas (APPSC G2 2005) → Kalyani Chalukyas (⚠ other key: Eastern Chalukyas)
- Samanta 956-1162 → sovereign 1163-1323
- Emblem Garuda → Varaha
- Early = Jains (APPSC G2 2011) → later Shaivas; deity Kakati Devi

### Kakatiya origins — progenitor, overlords, caste, emblem and religion
## 1. Origin and overlords
- Progenitor: Kakara Gundana (Magallu) or Venna Bhupati (Bayyaram)
- Bayyaram inscription traces the line from Durjaya
- Overlords:
- Rashtrakutas first (APPSC G2 2005 key) → then Kalyani Chalukyas
- Another key (APHQ 2017 / APPSC 2017) answers "Eastern Chalukyas" / Eastern Chalukyas of Vengi (first mention in Eastern Chalukya Danarnava's Magallu grant)
- ⚠ Trap: Read the options - write Eastern Chalukyas if Rashtrakutas is not an option (older notes say Eastern Chalukyas)
- Phases: Samanta (feudatory) 956-1162 → sovereign 1163-1323
- Base: Hanamkonda Vishaya (modern Karimnagar, Warangal, Khammam)
## 2. Caste, language, emblem, religion
| Basis | Fact |
| Emblem | Garuda (Rashtrakuta phase) → Varaha (Kalyani Chalukya phase) |
| Religion | Early Kakatiyas = Jains (APPSC G2 2011 key) → later Shaivas |
| Clan deity | Kakatamma (Kakati Devi) = Jain Kushmandini absorbed into Hinduism ("Kakati" = gourd) |
### Kakatiya rulers → period and contribution, Gundana to Ganapatideva
## 1. Kakatiyas
- The Eastern Deccan — 12th–14th centuries CE; capital Warangal
- Rulers: Rudradeva — also Pratap Rudradeva I; Mahadeva (after Rudradeva); Ganapati Dev — father of Rudrama Devi
- Rudrama Devi: Daughter of Ganapati Dev — the fourth independent ruler; a female ruler
- Defeated the Pandyas (of South Tamil Nadu), the Eastern Gangas (rulers of Orissa) and the Seuna rulers of Devagiri
- The Kakatiyas had a conflict with the Seuna, or Yadava, rulers of Devagiri
## 2. Kakatiya rulers

| Beta I (Garuda Beta) | 995/1000-1052 (also 995-1052) | First Kakatiya to come to Hanamkonda (Bayyaram inscription); got Anumakonda Vishaya via aunt Viriyala Kamasani; attacked Kanchi (1051) for Someshwara I |

### Ganapatideva - release, accession and succession
## 1. Ganapatideva - release and accession
- Early life: Held captive as a boy by Yadava Jaitugi; general Recherla Rudra crushed revolts + installed him
- Release: From Yadava captivity in 1199; first inscription Manthena, 26 Dec 1199
- Coastal Andhra: Still held firmly; title 'Andhra Deshadhiswara'; unified "Telugudesam"
- Coastal marriages: Married Pinnachodi's daughters Narama and Perama; made their brother Jayapa 'Gajasahani' (elephant-force chief)
## 2. Succession after Ganapatideva - daughters and marriages
- Daughters (no son):
- Rudramamba → married Nidadavolu Eastern Chalukya prince Veerabhadra
- Ganapamba → married the Kota chief
- Successor: Rudrama Devi, raised as a son (putrika); ruled 1262-1289; mother Kupama Devi guided her training
- Sisters: Mailamba + Kundamamba married sons of Natavadi Buddharaja (Rudra and his brother)
### Kakatiya capitals — Hanamkonda to Orugallu (Warangal)
- Early capital: Anumakonda (Hanamkonda)
- Made capital by Prola I; developed by Prola II
- Shift: Rudradeva shifted the capital from Hanamkonda to Orugallu (Warangal) (APPSC G2 2019 key)
- He began fortifying it = the Warangal fort
- Completion: Ganapatideva completed Orugallu + fully moved the court there (some notes say Ganapati made the final transfer)
- ⚠ Trap: "Who shifted" → answer Rudradeva

## Ganapatideva's campaigns
- Velanati Chodas:
- Ganapati + Nellore's Choda Tikka jointly killed Prithviswara in 1210 → both titled "Prithviswara Shirakhandika Kreeda Vinoda"
- Velanati kingdom ended 1210
- Coastal campaign (from 1201): General Mutyalachaundaraya took Divi
- 1257: Allied with Akkana + Bayyana (enemies of Nellore's Manumasiddhi) → killed Mummadi Rajendra Chola = final end of Velanati Chodas
- Tikkana dedicated his Nirvachanottara Ramayana to Manumasiddhi
- Nellore: Restored Manumasiddhi II to his throne; poet Tikkana went as envoy to seek his help
- Defeat: 1263 by the Pandyas (APPSC G2 2017 key; APPSC 2017) in the Muttukur (Muthukuru) war → Nellore fell after the Battle of Muttukur and came under Pandya control

## Rulers' deeds

### Ganapatideva
- 1210 — Ganapatideva and Choda Tikka of Nellore killed Prithviswara, ending the Velanati kingdom; title Prithviswara Shirakhandika Kreeda Vinoda
- Married Pinnachodi's daughters Narama and Perama; their brother Jayapa made Gajasahani (chief of elephant forces)

- Tikkana — envoy to Ganapatideva; Ganapatideva restored Manumasiddhi II to the Nellore throne
- Tikkana's Nirvachanottara Ramayana — dedicated to Manumasiddhi (not Ganapatideva)
- Battle of Muttukur (1263) — Pandyas defeated Ganapatideva; Nellore fell to them
- Rudrama Devi (ruled 1262–1289) — trained by her mother Kupama Devi; Mailamba was Ganapatideva's sister
- Defeated the Pandyas, Eastern Gangas, Seunas of Devagiri — not the Western Chalukyas (declined long before)

- Kakatiyas — ruled the eastern Deccan from Warangal, 12th–14th c.; Seunas the western Deccan; Eastern Gangas Odisha
### Exam corner
- ★ Prola I (Prolaraja I) — title Arigajakesari; built Kesari Samudram / Arikesari Samudram tank
- ★ Tank building key to Kakatiya farming and welfare policy
- Rudramadevi — title Rayagajakesari for victory over the Yadavas of Devagiri
- Ramappa Temple (Palampet) — UNESCO World Heritage Site 2021; star-shaped platform, floating bricks
### Kakatiya rulers → period and contribution, Gundana to Ganapatideva
## Capital
- ★ Kakatiya capital — Orugallu
- ★ Rudradeva I partly shifted the capital; Ganapatideva moved it completely from Hanumakonda to Warangal (Orugallu)
## Ganapatideva
- ★ Raja Guru — Visvesvara Siva (Sivacharya), head of the Golaki Matha
- ★ Sanigaram inscription — Mailamba
## Statements
- ★ Nayankara system — organised under Prataparudra II (military-feudal land grants for troops)
- ★ Beta II — initiated into Saivism by Rameswara Pandita of Srisailam
- ★ In this key, Prolaraja is not associated with the title "Arikesari"
## Rudrama
- ★ Rudrama — daughter of Ganapati Dev; fourth independent Kakatiya ruler of Warangal
- ★ Order: Rudradeva (sovereign empire) → Mahadev → Ganapati Dev → Rudrama
- ★ Defeated the Pandyas of south Tamil Nadu and the Eastern Ganga rulers of Orissa
### Kakatiya temples → location, builder and notes
- ★ Thousand Pillared (Rudreswara) temple, Hanumakonda — built by Rudradeva (Prataparudra I), 1163 AD
### Kakatiya inscriptions → issuer, year and fact
- ★ Motupalli — Ganapati Deva's Abhaya Sasanam: protection, shelter and concessions to foreign traders
- ★ Religious teacher of Ganapati Deva — Visvesvara Sivacharya
