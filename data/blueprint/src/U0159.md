### Ports of ancient and medieval Andhra → Motupalli, Ghantasala, Machilipatnam, Visakhapatnam
## 1. Motupalli
- Also called Desiyakondapattanam — the most important port
- Satavahana period: An east-coast seaport of the Satavahanas
- Ikshvaku period: An important port — with Ghantasala
- Kakatiya period: An important port-town - important Kakatiya port (APPSC 2011)
- Marco Polo visited it during the reign of Rudramadevi
- A Ganapathi Deva Abhaya inscription was found there - Ganapatideva's Abhaya Sasanam protected foreign traders
- Reddy period: An important port — occupied by Mallaya Reddy; Somaya issued the Abhaya Sasana for trade development (Reddi minister Somaya issued another)
## 2. Ghantasala
- In Krishna district — an important port during the Ikshvaku period
- Claudius Ptolemy: Mentioned it as the maritime emporium Kantakossyla
- ⚠ Sources differ: other notes list Ghantasala (Kantakasela) with Koddura as ancient east-coast ports of the Satavahana period (APP)
## 3. Machilipatnam (Masulipatnam)
- Golconda: Associated with it in the 16th and 17th centuries
- The most famous port for textile trade; famous for the diamond trade in the 1600s
- Ptolemy: Called it Mysolia — meaning great market
| Period / ruler | Point |
| Ancient name | Ptolemy's Maisolia / Masulia – great trading centre |
| Qutb Shahis of Golconda | Main maritime gateway; customs revenue made Golconda one of the richest states |
| Mughals | Aurangzeb annexed Golconda in 1687 |
- English calico bans (1700, 1721): Did not hurt Andhra – other markets stayed open
- Present lighthouse: Near Manginapudi, from 1984
## 4. Visakhapatnam
- A port in the 19th century — facilitated the transportation and trade of agricultural products
