### Industry types by raw material → examples
## 1. Industry types
| Type | Raw materials / features | Examples |
| Agricultural | Agro-based products — crops and animals; depend on farm produce; often rural and labour-intensive; seasonal | Cotton textile, jute, sugar, tea, coffee, edible oil, dairy, food processing, rubber, silk |
| Mineral-based | Minerals — metallic and non-metallic; capital-intensive; located near mines or ports | Iron and steel, aluminium, cement, copper smelting, fertiliser, jewellery |
| Forest-based | Forest products — timber and non-timber forest produce | Paper and pulp, furniture, plywood, matchsticks, lac, resin, bamboo products, rayon |
| Fuel-based — also called energy-based | Coal, petroleum, natural gas, nuclear energy (a raw material or energy source) — power generation and petrochemicals | Thermal power, petrochemicals, oil refining, gas-based fertilisers |
| Manpower-based — also called labour-intensive | High human labour requirements | Handloom, handicrafts, leather, garments, toy-making, small-scale food processing |

- Heavy industries — bulky inputs and outputs; usually capital-intensive
- Light industries — lighter inputs; make consumer goods

### Paper & world leaders
- Per capita paper consumption: India about 16 kg/year; world 57 kg
- IIP use-based classes: primary, capital, intermediate, infrastructure/construction, consumer durables, consumer non-durables (no "export goods")

- Rayon: led by China, India, Indonesia

### Policy & MSME
- Industrial Policy Resolution 1956: Schedule A (arms, atomic energy, iron and steel) — public sector; Schedule B — both public and private
- Ministry of MSME (2007) — merger of Ministry of Small Scale Industries + Ministry of Agro and Rural Industries
- 2020 MSME definition — one combined test: investment and turnover; no manufacturing/services difference
- ICI and IIP rebased in 2026; new series released July 2026
