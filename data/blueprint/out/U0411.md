## U0411 | CHILD RIGHTS – ARTICLES 21A, 24, 39(e)(f); UNCRC; RTE ACT 2009
### 1. CONSTITUTIONAL PROVISIONS FOR CHILDREN
| Article | Content | Origin / Amendment |
|---|---|---|
| 15(3) | Special provisions for women and **children** | Original |
| **21A** | Free and compulsory education, **6–14 years** (Fundamental Right) | **86th CAA, 2002** |
| 23 | Ban on **traffic in human beings** and forced labour | Original |
| **24** | No child **below 14** in factories, mines or hazardous employment (Part III) | Original |
| 39(e) | Tender age of children not abused; not forced by economic necessity into unsuited avocations | Original |
| 39(f) | Develop healthily in **freedom and dignity**; protection from exploitation and abandonment | Reworded by **42nd CAA, 1976** |
| 45 | **Early childhood care** and education till **6** | Substituted by 86th (originally free education till 14 within 10 years) |
| 51A(k) | Parent/guardian's duty to educate child **6–14** | Added by 86th |
#### 1.1 86th Amendment, 2002 – Three Changes
1. Inserted **Art. 21A**.
2. Rewrote **Art. 45** (care up to 6).
3. Added **Art. 51A(k)**.
- First amendment to make education a **Fundamental Right**. Bill introduced July 1997 after Unni Krishnan; enacted December 2002.
- Article 24 = the first constitutional protection for children.
!PAIR 21A | Fundamental Right | 6–14 || 45 | DPSP | care till 6
!TRAP 39(f) was reworded by the **42nd** (1976); 21A, 45 and 51A(k) all came from the **86th** (2002).

### 3. RTE ACT 2009 – PROVISIONS
- Full name: **Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2009**; gives effect to **Art. 21A**.
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| 3 | Age **6–14**; neighbourhood school; elementary education **Classes 1–8** |
| **12(1)(c)** | Unaided private schools (excluding minority) reserve **25%** entry-level seats for weaker sections and disadvantaged groups; State reimburses |
| **21** | **School Management Committees** in every government/aided school; **three-fourths** members parents/guardians |
| 38 | Rules by appropriate Government |
- **Pupil-teacher ratio**: **30:1** primary; **35:1** upper primary.
- **Teacher qualifications**: academic authority notified by the Centre = **NCTE**, not a State council; TET per NCTE; amended 2012 and 2017.
- No screening, no **capitation fee**; out-of-school child admitted to **age-appropriate class**.
- Monitoring and grievance redressal: **NCPCR and SCPCRs** (SMCs are school-level bodies).
#### 3.1 No-Detention
| Stage | Rule |
|---|---|
| Original Act | No detention till Class 8 |
| RTE (Amendment) Act **2019** | Regular exams in **Classes 5 and 8**; child failing re-exam can be held back |
#### 3.2 Included vs Not Included
| Included | Not included / wrong |
|---|---|
| Free and compulsory education 6–14 | **Centre bearing sole financial responsibility** (funds shared) |
| Neighbourhood schools by Govt and local authority | **Pre-school** (below 6) and secondary/higher education |
| Parent's duty to send child to school (51A(k)) | Minority schools under the 25% quota |
| 25% seats with State reimbursement | Teacher norms by a State council |
!KEY APPSC 2017 odd one out: "Central government has sole responsibility for funds" is NOT a feature. "6–14 years" and "enacted under Art. 21A" are both correct.
### 4. UNIVERSALISATION OF ELEMENTARY EDUCATION
- **SSA (2001)** → **Samagra Shiksha (2018)**: merged SSA, RMSA and Teacher Education.
- Funding **60:40**; **90:10** for North-Eastern and hill States. KGBVs up to Class 12; CwSN support ₹3,500.
- First National Education Policy: **1968**. NPE priority areas: universal elementary education, adult literacy, women's educational equality; **compulsory nutrition education to all is NOT** a priority area.
- **Tamil Nadu** pioneered the Mid-Day Meal. ICDS launched **1975**.
### 5. UNCRC 1989
| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Adopted | UNGA, **20 November 1989** |
| Articles | **54** (APPSC 2019) |
| India | Acceded **11 December 1992** – India's first international convention on child rights |
| Reporting | Periodic reports to UN Committee on the Rights of the Child |
- **Four rights**: survival, development, protection, participation (education is not a separate category). Right to development, expression and recreation are all child rights (UPSC 2010).
- **Four core principles**: non-discrimination; **best interests of the child**; right to life, survival and development; respect for the **views of the child** (not free secondary education).
| Optional Protocol | Year | India |
|---|---|---|
| Children in armed conflict | 2000 | Ratified 2005 |
| Sale of children, child prostitution, pornography | 2000 | Ratified 2005 |
| Communications procedure | 2011 | **Not a party** |

### 7. POCSO AND JUVENILE JUSTICE
#### 7.1 POCSO Act, 2012
- **Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012**; in force **14 November 2012**; child = **below 18**; gender-neutral.
- **Criminal Law (Amendment) Ordinance, April 2018**: death penalty possible for rape of a girl **below 12**.
- **POCSO (Amendment) Act, 2019** (notified 16 Aug 2019): **death penalty** for aggravated penetrative sexual assault on children.
- Debates: age of consent; SC's "**Romeo-Juliet**" suggestion (Jan 2026).
#### 7.2 Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act, 2015
- First Juvenile Justice law: **1986**.
- **16–18** accused of **heinous** offences: **Juvenile Justice Board** makes a **preliminary assessment**; case may go to the **Children's Court** (Sessions Court) to be tried as an adult.
- **Child Welfare Committees** handle children in need of care and protection. Debate: arbitrariness of transfer system.
- **2021 amendment**: **District Magistrate** issues adoption orders (in force 1 Sep 2022).
#### 7.3 Adoption
| Law / body | Fact |
|---|---|
| **CARA** | Statutory body under **JJ Act 2015** |
| Adoption Regulations 2022 | Surrendered child: **2-month** reconsideration period |
| **HAMA 1956** | Hindus, Buddhists, Jains, Sikhs |
| JJ Act route | Open to **all religions** |
- **DPDP Act 2023**: child = below 18; verifiable parental consent.
### 8. CHILD POLICY AND CHILD LABOUR LAWS
- **National Policy for Children**: first **1974**; **NPC 2013** (April 2013), child = **below 18**.
- NPC 2013 **four priority areas**: survival, health and nutrition; education and development; protection; participation.
- **National Plan of Action for Children 2016** operationalises NPC 2013.
- **Bal Vivah Mukt Bharat**: 100-day campaign against child marriage from Dec 2025.
- **Children (Pledging of Labour) Act, 1933** = first child labour law; **Child Labour (P&R) Act, 1986** = first comprehensive law.
!NUM Age thresholds: POCSO below 18 | child labour law below 14 | RTE 6–14
