## U0329 | RAJAMANNAR COMMITTEE (1969) – APPOINTMENT AND RECOMMENDATIONS
### 1. COMMITTEE AT A GLANCE
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Formal name | **Centre-State Relations Inquiry Committee** |
| Appointed by | **Tamil Nadu** – DMK government under **Karunanidhi** |
| Year | **1969** (report **1971**) |
| Chairman | **Dr. P.V. Rajamannar**, former Chief Justice of the **Madras High Court** |
| Members | **A. Lakshmanaswami Mudaliar**, **P. Chandra Reddy** (3 in all) |
- **First** committee on Centre-State relations appointed by a **State**.
- Took forward **C.N. Annadurai's 1967** view that the Union need not control health and education.
!TRAP P.V. Rajamannar also chaired the **4th Finance Commission** – do not confuse.
### 2. TERMS OF REFERENCE AND FINDINGS
- Task: **study Centre-State relations** (APPSC 2018) and suggest **State autonomy**; aim – reduce central control, including over **education and health**.
- Finding: despite the federal form, States had become "**administrative units of the Centre**".
- Overreach Articles: **256, 257, 365** (not 280, 282).
- Centre's control of States in the planning process termed "**municipal relations**".
- Relations depend on constitutional provisions, conventions and practices, judicial interpretations and mechanisms for dialogue (all four).
### 3. RECOMMENDATIONS
| Subject | Rajamannar recommended |
|---|---|
| **Inter-State Council** | Permanent ISC under **Art. 263**, immediately |
| **Article 356** | **Complete deletion** (with 256, 257, 365 ending Centre's override) |
| **All-India Services** | **Abolish IAS, IPS, IFS** – to cut central administrative control |
| **Planning Commission** | **Abolish** – extra-constitutional; alone decided grants to States |
| **Finance Commission** | **Revitalise**; widen revenue devolution |
- **NOT recommended:** strengthening the **Governor** system (APPSC-G2 2017).
- Ending IAS and IPS = **Rajamannar** (UPSC 1993) – not Dhebar, Kalelkar or Kher.
!KEY "Which is NOT a Rajamannar recommendation" – pick any option that strengthens the Centre or the Governor.
### 4. SEVENTH SCHEDULE AND RESIDUARY POWERS
- Transfer from Union and Concurrent Lists to the **State List**: **stock exchanges, futures markets, oil fields** regulation, **trade unions, industrial disputes**, **inland waterway navigation**, factories, electricity, publishing (not foreign affairs).
- Move more Concurrent subjects to the State List.
- **Residuary powers → States**.
!PAIR Residuary powers – Rajamannar | To the States | 1969 || Residuary powers – Sarkaria | Non-tax to Concurrent List | 1983
### 5. AFTERMATH AND INFLUENCE
- **April 1974:** TN Assembly adopted a **State autonomy resolution** based on the report.
- **Centre ignored** the report; no action.
- ISC (1990) came through **Sarkaria**, not Rajamannar.
- Base for later demands (**Anandpur Sahib**, West Bengal memorandum).
- DMK slogan: "**autonomy for the States, federalism at the Centre**".
