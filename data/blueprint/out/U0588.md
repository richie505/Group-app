## U0588 | SPECIAL ASSISTANCE PACKAGE VS SPECIAL CATEGORY STATUS - GOVERNMENT'S STAND AND MEASURES
### 1. THE PACKAGE - ANNOUNCEMENT AND PERIOD
- Announced **September 2016** by Union FM **Arun Jaitley**, in place of SCS.
- Also called **Special Assistance Measure (SAM)** or "Special Package".
- Union Cabinet approved it on **15 March 2017**.
- Period: **2015-16 to 2019-20** = five years, the promised SCS period (SCS assurance: **Manmohan Singh**, Rajya Sabha, 2014).
### 2. COMPONENTS AND ACT SECTIONS
| Component | Content |
|---|---|
| Special assistance | Money equal to the **extra central share** AP would get as an SCS State (**90%**), via **Externally Aided Projects** |
| **Polavaram (Sec 90)** | **National project**; Centre bears **100%** of irrigation-component cost from **1 Apr 2014** |
| **Tax (Sec 94(1))** | Industrial incentives; promised **10-year tax holiday** delayed, weakened or merged into GST |
| Backward areas (**Sec 46(2)**) | Rayalaseema and North Coastal - 7 districts (4 + 3); releases stalled |
| Revenue deficit (**Sec 46**) | Bridge the 2014-15 resource gap |
| Capital | **Rs 2,500 crore** promised |
!TRAP Sec 90 = Polavaram; Sec 94(1) = tax incentives (unrelated to Polavaram); Sec 46(2) = backward areas.
### 3. EXTERNALLY AIDED PROJECTS ROUTE
1. AP borrows from **World Bank, ADB** etc.
2. Centre **repays loan and interest** for EAPs signed and disbursed in 2015-16 to 2019-20.
3. Up to the **SCS-equivalent 90%** share - no open grant.
- AP's criticism: long approval cycles, heavy paperwork - **red tape and delays**.
- Jan 2018: the CM asked for the money as a grant routed through **NABARD** instead of external loans.
### 4. SCS vs PACKAGE
| Benefit | SCS | Special Assistance Package |
|---|---|---|
| CSS funding | **Automatic 90:10** grant | Equivalent amount **only via EAP repayments** |
| Tax for industry | Yes | Only weaker Sec 94(1) incentives |
| Use of funds | **All schemes** | **Tied to approved EAPs** |
| Duration | Promised 5 years | 2015-16 to 2019-20 |
!PAIR SCS | Status | Automatic flows and tax breaks || Package | Money | Project-linked loans
### 5. CENTRE'S STAND
- **14th Finance Commission** removed the general-special distinction and raised devolution to **42%** - Centre said this **covered SCS benefits**.
- **"Constitutional and technical constraints"** on SCS for a new State.
- **Domino fear**: identical demands from **Bihar and Odisha**.
### 6. AP GOVERNMENT'S STAND OVER TIME
| Period | Stand |
|---|---|
| TDP govt (NDA ally), 2014-18 | Balanced alliance vs SCS demand |
| YSRCP | Package a **"betrayal"**; SCS its **2019** campaign plank |
| AP govt 2024-26 (NDA ally) | Seeks project money (Amaravati, Polavaram, SASCI) |
- **Aug 2026**: AP FM **Payyavula Keshav** - loss up to **Rs 38,000 crore** as the **16th FC dropped revenue deficit grants**.
- AP revenue deficit: **Rs 16,000 crore (2014-19)** - projected **Rs 48,000 crore (2025-26)**.
### 7. ASSISTANCE RELEASED AND MEASURES
| Period | Amount |
|---|---|
| 2014-15 | Revenue deficit grant **Rs 3,979 crore** |
| **2016-17** | Special assistance **Rs 4,058.04 crore** |
| SAM total | **Rs 24,350 crore** (incl. Rs 3,979 crore RD grant; rest infrastructure) |
| 2024-25 onward | **Rs 15,000 crore Amaravati**; **Rs 12,157 crore Polavaram** |
| SASCI loans | Rs 7,900 crore (2024-25); Rs 8,255 crore (2025-26) |
- **SASCI** (Scheme for Special Assistance to States for Capital Investment): **Ministry of Finance**, since **Oct 2020**; **50-year interest-free** loans for **capital works only**; above the net borrowing ceiling, **outside the 3% of GSDP** limit; not for revenue spending or EAP repayment.
- 2026-27: AP can get up to **Rs 8,226 crore**.
- Aug 2026: nod for **Rayalaseema Global Horticulture Hub** (Centre Rs 10,000 crore + State Rs 30,000 crore).
