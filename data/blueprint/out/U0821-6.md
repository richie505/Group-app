### 9. IMF, WORLD BANK GROUP AND MULTILATERAL BANKS
#### 9.1 IMF
- **France** = **first borrower** from the IMF (not the U.S.).
- Lends only to members, mainly for **balance-of-payments** problems.
- **ESAF** had the softest terms.
- **Rapid Financing Instrument**: quick liquidity **without a full programme** (disasters, pandemics, post-conflict).
- **Structural Adjustment Programmes (1980s-90s)**: austerity, privatisation, devaluation; studies link them to rising poverty and inequality in the Global South.
#### 9.2 SDR, Quota and Voting Share
- **SDR** = IMF reserve asset, "**paper gold**"; basket of **five currencies**: **US dollar, euro, Chinese yuan, Japanese yen, pound sterling**.
- India's forex reserves = foreign currency assets + gold + **SDRs** + **reserve tranche**; India's **reserve position** rose to **$4.7 bn (2025-26)**.
- India's IMF **quota share about 2.75%**, **voting share about 2.63%** (after the 2010 reforms).
- **BRICS**: about **20% of world GDP** but **11% of IMF votes** in **2011**; now about **40%** of GDP with barely higher vote share; the **New Delhi Declaration** seeks quota and voting reform; quota-based voting not abolished.
