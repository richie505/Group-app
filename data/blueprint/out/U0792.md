## U0792 | SOIL, NOISE, THERMAL, MARINE AND RADIOACTIVE POLLUTION
### 1. POLLUTION TYPE → MAJOR SOURCE
| Pollution | Major primary source | Constitutional link |
|---|---|---|
| Air | **Industrial emissions**, vehicles | Art. 21 |
| Water | **Sewage discharge** | Art. 48A, 51A(g) |
| Soil | **Chemical fertilisers and pesticides** | Art. 48A |
| Noise | **Construction**, traffic, loudspeakers | Art. 21 |
| Thermal | Hot cooling water from thermal/nuclear plants | – |
| Marine | Oil spills, plastics, sewage, land runoff | – |
| Radioactive | Nuclear plants, tests, uranium/thorium mining, medical waste | – |
- APPSC G2 2025 key: air → industry; water → sewage; soil → fertilisers; noise → construction.
- **Art. 51A(g)** = citizen's duty; **Art. 48A** = DPSP for the State – separate provisions (48A does not explain 51A(g)).
### 2. SOIL POLLUTION
- Build-up of toxic chemicals or radioactive material in soil → toxic crops, fewer plants, soil fauna imbalance.
- Natural causes: floods, acid rain, weathered rock. Human: pesticides (**DDT**, aldrin, malathion), NPK, heavy metals, landfill **leachate**, e-waste, coal ash, mining.
| Heavy metal | Source | Disease |
|---|---|---|
| **Mercury** | **Chlor-alkali** effluent, fungicides | **Minamata** |
| **Cadmium** | **Phosphate fertilisers**, batteries, mining effluent | **Itai-itai** |
| Copper, zinc, lead | **Smelting**, industrial waste | – |
| **Chromates** | **Tanneries** | – |
- **DDT**: persistent organic pollutant under the **Stockholm Convention**; DDT and mercury biomagnify in marine food chains (dissolved oxygen does not).
- **Fly ash**: use in bricks, cement, roads within **300 km** of thermal plants (**50 km** = limit for using treated STP water).
- **Contaminated Sites Rules** (EPA 1986): test against **189 hazardous chemicals**; Polluter Pays; exempt radioactive waste, mining, marine oil, dumpsites.
- Environment (Protection) Fund Rules: Centre keeps **25%**.
- Control: less chemicals, **biofertilisers, IPM, crop rotation**, organic farming, afforestation, biomining of legacy dumps.
!TRAP Watersheds are NOT a soil-pollution control (APPSC 2019).
### 3. BIOREMEDIATION AND GREEN TECHNOLOGIES
| Type | Agent |
|---|---|
| **Bioaugmentation** | Adds pollutant-degrading microbes |
| **Biostimulation** | Adds nutrients to boost native microbes |
- **Oil Zapper**: bacterial consortium from **TERI** (not a chemical dispersant); eats oily sludge and crude from spills.
- Alternatives to conventional control: **green technology, bioremediation, carbon capture and storage**.
!TRAP **Incineration** is conventional – not an alternative (APPSC G2 2025).
### 4. NOISE POLLUTION
- Unit: **decibel (dB)** (APPSC 2025), logarithmic; **dB(A)** weighted to human hearing; **+10 dB ≈ loudness doubles**. Hertz = frequency.
| Sound / limit | Level |
|---|---|
| Rustling leaves | ~20 dB |
| WHO safe indoors | < 30 dB |
| Conversation | ~60 dB |
| Permanent hearing loss | > 85 dB |
| Heavy traffic | ~90 dB |
| Rocket engine | ~180 dB |
- Maximum noise among options: **jet flight**. ~27% industrial workers suffer deafness.
- Long exposure: narrowed blood vessels, digestive spasms, **impaired** night vision.
#### 4.1 Noise Rules, 2000 (under EPA 1986)
| Zone | Day dB(A) | Night dB(A) |
|---|---|---|
| Industrial | 75 | 70 |
| Residential | **55** | **45** |
| Silence | 50 | 40 |
- Day = **6 am–10 pm**. **Silence zone**: ≥ **100 m** around hospitals, educational institutions, courts.
- CPCB bans crackers above **125 dB at 4 m**.
- **NANMN**: CPCB, **70 stations in 7 metros** (Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Lucknow, Hyderabad); Delhi 65–85% sites breach limits.
#### 4.2 Control
- Silencers, **noise barriers**, green belts, zoning, loudspeaker limits, traffic management.
- **Green mufflers**: 4–5 rows of trees/shrubs along roads absorb noise; also absorb gases and dust.
### 5. THERMAL POLLUTION
- Change in water temperature from **hot coolant water** of thermal and **nuclear** plants and industries.
- **Warmer water holds less dissolved oxygen**; sudden changes stress or kill aquatic life.
- Control: **cooling towers, cooling ponds**, recirculation, regulated discharge temperature.
### 6. MARINE POLLUTION
- Oceans = final sink for radioactive waste, plastics, oil, detergents, sewage.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Microplastics** | < **5 mm**; microbeads in cosmetics |
| **Ghost nets** | Abandoned, lost or discarded fishing gear |
| **Dead zones** | Hypoxic waters from nutrient runoff (Gulf of Mexico) |
| **Blue Flag** | Beach eco-label by **Foundation for Environmental Education (Denmark)** |
| Instrument / body | Fact |
|---|---|
| **MARPOL 73/78** | **IMO**; **6 Annexes**; bans plastic dumping at sea |
| Ballast Water Convention | IMO; invasive species; in force **2017** |
| **GloLitter Partnerships** | **IMO and FAO (2019)**; funded by **Norway**; India a partner; sea-based litter from shipping and fisheries |
| **SACEP** | 1982; secretariat **Colombo**; 8 members incl. India; South Asian Seas litter work |
- **Samudra Pratap**: India's first indigenous **Pollution Control Vessel**, **Goa Shipyard**. Other tools: booms, skimmers, dispersants, Oil Zapper.
!TRAP GloLitter is IMO-FAO, not UNEP.
### 7. RADIOACTIVE POLLUTION
| Source | Examples |
|---|---|
| Natural | Cosmic rays, **radon-222**, U-235, Th-232, **potassium-40** |
| Building materials | **Thorium** (APPSC 2019); radon from granite |
| Medical | X-rays, I-125, P-32, C-14 |
| Man-made nuclear | Reactor waste, tests (**Sr-90**, Cs-137, I-131), uranium mining |
- X-ray machines = **ionising**; cell towers = **non-ionising**.
- **Radon**: colourless, odourless noble gas from radium decay in soil; **most important indoor pollutant**; **leading cause of lung cancer after smoking**; used in **cancer treatment** (APPSC G1 2018).
- Harm depends on intensity, exposure time, half-life; DNA damage, mutations.
| Quantity | Units |
|---|---|
| Activity | **Becquerel, curie** |
| Absorbed dose | **Gray, rad** |
| Biological dose | **Sievert, rem** |
!TRAP Rad is NOT a unit of activity.
### 8. RADIOLOGICAL EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT
- **AERB** (1983, Atomic Energy Act 1962): independent regulator; approves radioactive waste disposal.
- **DAE** = nodal technical agency, with the **Crisis Management Group**; CCS handles NBC emergencies.
- **NDMA guidelines** on nuclear and radiological emergencies: **2009**.
- **MRDS** (Mobile Radiation Detection Systems): NDMA plan for metros and State capitals to detect **orphan** radioactive material (APPSC 2021, twice).
- Precedent: **Mayapuri, Delhi (2010)** – cobalt-60.
- Environmental Survey Laboratory only monitors; NDRF CBRN battalions respond; **District Collector** = Incident Commander off-site.
- AP: **Tummalapalle** uranium mine and mill (**UCIL**), **YSR Kadapa** (not Kurnool).
### 9. LIGHT POLLUTION
- Dark sky place: protected night sky with controlled lighting; certified by **DarkSky International**.
| FIRST | Place |
|---|---|
| Dark Sky **Reserve** | **Hanle, Ladakh (2022)**, Changthang WLS |
| Dark Sky **Park** | **Pench (Maharashtra), 2024** |
- Ladakh: clear skies, low humidity, high altitude.
