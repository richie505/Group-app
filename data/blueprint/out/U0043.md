## U0043 | INDO-GREEKS – MENANDER, MILINDAPANHO AND INDO-GREEK COINAGE
### 1. BACTRIAN GREEKS – ORIGIN AND ENTRY
- After Alexander, Greeks settled in **Bactria** (**north Afghanistan**), early 2nd c. BC kingdom.
- **Diodotus I** revolted against the **Seleucids** (3rd c. BCE) and founded free Bactria.
- Pressed by the **Scythians (Sakas)** and helped by **Ashoka's weak successors**, they moved south into India.
- Raids reached **Saketa, Madhyamika**, towards **Pataliputra** (account in Patanjali's **Mahabhashya**).
- Invader order: **Greeks → Sakas → Parthians (Pahlavas) → Kushanas**.
!TRAP Scythia = land of the Scythians (Sakas); Gedrosia = today's Balochistan, not linked to Indo-Greeks.
### 2. INDO-GREEK RULERS
| Ruler | Contribution / coin feature |
|---|---|
| **Demetrius** | **First** to invade India (c. 180 BCE); defeated by **Pushyamitra**, and by **Kharavela** (Hathigumpha inscription) |
| **Menander I (Milinda)** | **Greatest**; 165-145 BCE; capital **Sakala (Sialkot)**; coins with Indian symbols (**Buddhist wheel**) |
| **Antialcidas** | Sent **Heliodorus** to Vidisha |
| **Agathocles** | Coins with **Sankarshana (Balarama)** and **Vasudeva (Krishna)**; ruled c. 190 BC; held Paropamisadae; first Greco-Bactrian ruler with **bilingual** coin legends |
| **Eucratides** | **Largest ancient gold coin** (20 staters) |
| **Strato** | **Menander's son** |
| **Hermaius** | **Last**; his defeat ended Greek rule south of the Hindu Kush |
!KEY First = Demetrius; greatest = Menander; last = Hermaius.
### 3. MENANDER AND MILINDAPANHO
- **Milindapanho** ("Questions of Milinda"), in **Pali**: dialogue between King Milinda (Menander) and Buddhist monk **Nagasena**; Menander turned **Buddhist**.
- It also explains **eight phases of agricultural work**.
- **Bairat (Rajasthan)**: hoard of **16 coins of Menander**.
- Greeks, Kushanas and Sakas took up **Buddhism**: it gave easier entry into Indian society (no rigid caste barriers).
!PAIR Match: Milindapanho | Menander || Taxila copper plate | Patika || Junagadh inscription | Rudradaman I || Gandhara art | Kushanas
### 4. INDO-GREEK COINAGE AND ITS SIGNIFICANCE
| Feature | Fact |
|---|---|
| Coins attributed to kings | **First** in India = Indo-Greeks |
| Gold coins | **First** issued in India by Indo-Greeks (2nd c. BC); but mostly **silver and copper** |
| Legends | **Bilingual**: Greek on front + **Kharoshthi (Prakrit)** on back; Agathocles also issued Greek + **Brahmi** coins |
| Portraits | **Royal portraits** |
| Lead | **Strato II** |
| Deities | Zeus, Athena, Heracles; Vasudeva, Sankarshana; Buddhist wheel |
- Coins are the main source for the list of **more than 30** Indo-Greek kings.
!TRAP Kushana coins did NOT come before Indo-Greek coins in portraits. Indo-Greeks = first gold; **Kushanas** (Vima Kadphises) = large-scale, regular gold.

### 9. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. **Sankarshana + Vasudeva** on coins = **Agathocles**, not Kushana or Gupta.
2. Milindapanho is in **Pali**, not Sanskrit; monk = **Nagasena**.
3. Menander's capital = **Sakala (Sialkot)**, not Taxila.
4. First gold = Indo-Greeks; large-scale gold = **Kushanas**.
5. Taxila copper plate = **Patika**, not Menander.
6. Heliodorus was sent by **Antialcidas**, not Menander.
7. Arabic Kalma on coins = **Muhammad Ghori**, not Chandragupta II.
8. Lead coins = **Strato II**; most Indo-Greek coins were silver.
