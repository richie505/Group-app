### 6. E-PANTA AND UREA USE
- Rabi 2025-26: cropped area **+36%**, urea availability only **+3%**.
- Urea per hectare **513 kg → 338 kg (~34% cut)**.
- Driver: **e-Panta** digital crop mapping - farmers **17.56 lakh → 35.51 lakh**; area 46.07 lakh → 73.55 lakh acres (~60%).
#### 6.1 Fertilizer Facts
- **Urea: 46% nitrogen**; only 30-40% absorbed by crops.
- Ideal **N:P:K = 4:2:1**.
- DAP = N + P; **MOP (Muriate of Potash) = potassic** (not phosphatic); SSP = phosphatic.
- **Chlorophyll - nitrogen and magnesium** (not phosphorus); phosphorus - roots and flowering.
- **Rhizobium** - legumes; **zinc sulphate** is a chemical micronutrient fertilizer, not a biofertilizer.
### 7. AP MUSHROOM MISSION - ₹13,000 CRORE
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Outlay | **₹13,000 crore** (CM N. Chandrababu Naidu) |
| Units | ~**1.62 lakh** production units (~5,000 sq ft each) |
| Target | **67,500 tonnes/year** - to beat **Bihar** (current largest, ~45,000 tonnes) |
| Subsidy | ~**₹5,184 crore (~40%)** - Centre + State |
| Status of produce | Mushrooms to count as **agricultural produce** (not industrial) |
| Main role | **SHGs** |
| Variety | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Milky mushroom** | **Heat-resistant - suits AP** |
| Paddy straw mushroom | Fast-growing; paddy regions |
| Button mushroom | High demand; controlled environment; dominates global trade |
- Mushrooms are **heterotrophic fungi** (not autotrophic); cycle 30-60 days; protein 20-30% of dry weight.
- **Spawn** = pure culture of fungal mycelium (the "seed"); fruiting triggered by **lower CO2 + more O2**.
- India's mushroom pioneers: Jitendra Kumar Singh, Dr Dayaram, S.S. Saini.
### 8. PADDY PROCUREMENT AND RUBBER FOR TRIBALS
#### 8.1 Rabi Paddy Procurement 2026
- Target **23 lakh MT**, from **4 April 2026**; **1,917** centres; payment within **24 hours**.
- Gunnysacks ~5.4 crore (traditionally jute); 17,200 lorries tracked by GPS.

| Aspect | Rabi | Kharif |
|---|---|---|

!TRAP Paddy is grown in both Kharif and Rabi, not only Kharif.
#### 8.2 Rubber Plantations in Polavaram District
- **Ministry of Tribal Affairs** proposed rubber over **3,000 acres** in **Polavaram district** for the **Konda Reddi** tribe; support from **Rubber Board + ITDA**.
- **Rubber Board**: **Rubber Act, 1947**; Ministry of Commerce and Industry; HQ **Kottayam, Kerala**.
- Climate: not below 21°C (ideal 25-35°C); rainfall ideal 200-400 cm, minimum ~150 cm.
- **Vulcanisation** = heating rubber with **sulphur** (not carbon).
- Area ~8.5 lakh ha; Kerala share fell ~90% → ~78%; Tripura + Assam >16%.
| Scheme | Key point |
|---|---|
| Rubber Production Incentive Scheme (RPIS) | Minimum price ₹150/kg |
| **NE Mitra** | Rubber over **2 lakh ha** in North-East |
| Rubber Plantation Development Scheme | Traditional + non-traditional areas |
| Proposed PLI | Rubber goods manufacturing |
