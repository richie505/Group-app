### 1. EXITS AND WITHDRAWALS FROM ORGANISATIONS
#### 1.1 UAE Exits OPEC and OPEC+
- **UAE** announced its exit from **OPEC and OPEC+** with effect from **1 May 2026**.
- Reasons: dissatisfaction with **production limits** and **strained ties with Saudi Arabia**.
- UAE had been an **OPEC member since 1967**.
- **OPEC+ = 12 OPEC + 10 non-OPEC** countries; together about **59% of global crude**.
- Output fall under the cuts: **UAE 3.60 → 2.34 mb/d**; **Saudi Arabia 10.40 → 7.80 mb/d**.
- **OPEC:** founded **1960**; HQ **Vienna (Austria)**.
- OPEC members (12, before the UAE exit): **Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Iran, UAE, Kuwait, Venezuela, Nigeria, Algeria, Libya, Gabon, Congo, Equatorial Guinea**.
- UAE: capital **Abu Dhabi** (not Dubai); currency **Dirham**; President **Mohammed bin Zayed Al Nahyan**.
!TRAP OPEC HQ is **Vienna**, not Riyadh or Abu Dhabi; the UAE capital is **Abu Dhabi**, not Dubai.
#### 1.2 Argentina and USA Leave the WHO
- **March 2026:** **Argentina** formally left the **WHO** after **one year's notice**; confirmed by Foreign Minister **Pablo Quirno**.
- President **Javier Milei** had announced the exit in **February 2025**.
- The **USA** withdrew from the WHO in **January 2026**.
- Withdrawal with one year's notice follows the **Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties (1969)**.
- **WHO:** established **1948**; HQ **Geneva**; Director-General **Dr. Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus**.
- Argentina: capital **Buenos Aires**; currency **Argentine Peso**.
!TRAP **USA** left the WHO in **January 2026**; **Argentina** in **March 2026** – do not swap the months.

### 2. INDIA–NEW ZEALAND FREE TRADE AGREEMENT
#### 2.1 Key Terms
- Signed on **27 April 2026** in **New Delhi** by **Piyush Goyal** and NZ Trade Minister **Todd McClay**.
- **New Zealand:** eliminates tariffs on **100%** of goods from India (zero tariff).
- **India:** removes/reduces tariffs on about **95%** of imports from NZ.
- NZ to facilitate about **$20 billion investment** in India over **15 years**; mobility of **professionals and students**.
- Talks began **March 2025**, concluded **December 2025** – among India's **fastest FTAs**.
- Needs **ratification by the NZ Parliament**.
- NZ PM **Christopher Luxon**; capital **Wellington**.
#### 2.2 FTA vs Customs Union
| Feature | Free Trade Agreement | Customs Union |
|---|---|---|
| Internal tariffs | Removed/reduced | Removed |
| External tariff | Each member keeps its **own** external trade policy | **Common external tariff** |
| Integration | Lower | Higher |
- **Rules of Origin** prevent **trade deflection** under an FTA.
- **SPS** (sanitary and phytosanitary) measures and **TBT** (technical barriers to trade) are **non-tariff barriers**.
!TRAP A **common external tariff** is a feature of a **Customs Union**, not of an FTA.
### 3. INDIA'S CHAIRSHIPS
#### 3.1 Kimberley Process (KP) 2026
- India chairs the **Kimberley Process** from **1 January 2026** – its **3rd** time (earlier **2008** and **2019**).
- India is a **founding member**; member since **2003**.
- KP launched **2000**; **KPCS** (certification scheme) established **2003**; **voluntary** scheme.
- **60 participants** (**59 + EU** counted as one); covers **99%+** of rough diamond trade.
- Conflict diamonds of the **1990s**: **Angola, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Rwanda**.
- Reforms India seeks: **wider definition of conflict diamonds** (human rights, child labour, environment); **digital certification and traceability**; capacity building.
- India: **largest cutting and polishing hub**; **largest exporter of cut and polished diamonds** – about **$13.3 billion (2024–25)**.
#### 3.2 Common Criteria Development Board (CCDB)
- India elected **Chair of the CCDB** for **April 2026 – April 2028**.
- Announced at the **CCRA** meeting in **Tokyo**, **14–16 April 2026**.
- **CCRA (Common Criteria Recognition Arrangement):** mutual recognition of **IT security certificates**.
- India a CCRA member since **16 September 2013**.
- India's certification authorities: **MeitY** and the **STQC** (Standardisation Testing and Quality Certification) Directorate.
!PAIR Kimberley Process | 2026 calendar year | 3rd time (2008, 2019) || CCDB (CCRA) | April 2026–April 2028 | announced in Tokyo
### 4. INTERNATIONAL SOLAR ALLIANCE – ROMANIA
- **Romania** ratified the **ISA Framework Agreement** (**March 2026**) and became a **full member**.
- Instrument submitted by Ambassador **Sena Latif**; received by JS **P.S. Gangadhar** (MEA).
- Romania had signed in **December 2023** as the **118th member**.
- **ISA:** launched **2015** by **India and France** at **COP21** (Paris); HQ **Gurugram**; open to **all UN members**.
- Romania: capital **Bucharest**; currency **Leu**; President **Nicușor Dan**.
!KEY ISA = India + France, 2015, COP21, HQ Gurugram; Romania = 118th signatory (Dec 2023), ratified March 2026.
### 5. WHO CERTIFICATIONS – CHILE AND LEPROSY
- WHO certified **Chile** for **eliminating leprosy as a public health problem**.
- Chile is the **first in the Americas** and **second globally after Jordan**.
- Elimination threshold: prevalence **below 1 per 10,000** population.
- **PAHO** (Pan American Health Organization): HQ **Washington, D.C.**
- Chile: capital **Santiago**; President **Gabriel Boric**.
!TRAP Chile is **second** in the world (after **Jordan**), but **first** in the Americas.
### 6. UNITED NATIONS, UNSC AND NUCLEAR TREATIES
#### 6.1 Guterres Calls for UNSC Reform
- UN Secretary-General **António Guterres**: **misuse of veto** has caused Council failures; **no permanent seat for Africa and Latin America** undermines legitimacy.
- **UN:** established **24 October 1945**; HQ **New York**.
- **UNSC:** **15 members** = **5 permanent** + **10 non-permanent**; **P5 – US, Russia, China, UK, France**.
#### 6.2 11th NPT Review Conference
- Held at **UN HQ, New York**; **concluded without consensus** on a final declaration.
- RevCons held **every 5 years since 1975**; about **190 states** are parties.
- **NPT:** opened for signature **1968**; in force **1970**; **indefinitely extended 1995**.
- **Nuclear-weapon states** = those that tested **before 1 January 1967**: **US, Russia, UK, France, China**.
- **India is not a signatory** – calls the NPT **discriminatory/unfair**.
- **IAEA:** est. **1957**; HQ **Vienna**; DG **Rafael Mariano Grossi**.
!TRAP India has **not signed** the NPT; the 11th RevCon ended **without** a consensus document.
### 7. ECOCIDE AND INTERNATIONAL LAW
#### 7.1 News
- **Lebanon** accused **Israel** of ecocide (2023–24 conflict); **Iran** alleged toxic **"black rain"** after strikes on fuel depots.
- Demands to add ecocide as a crime under the **Rome Statute**.
- **Council of Europe** adopted a **convention in 2025** criminalising severe environmental destruction.
#### 7.2 Key Facts
- Term **"ecocide"** coined in **1970** by biologist **Arthur W. Galston** (Vietnam War, **Agent Orange**).
- **Rome Statute Article 8:** environmental damage is a war crime only if **intentional, widespread, long-term and severe**.
- Countries with ecocide laws: **Vietnam, Russia, Ukraine, France, Belgium, Chile**.
- **ENMOD Convention:** bans **hostile environmental modification** techniques.
#### 7.3 Council of Europe vs European Union
| Council of Europe | European Union |
|---|---|
| Founded **1949**, HQ **Strasbourg** | Economic and political union; HQ **Brussels** |
| **46** members | **27** members |
| Runs **ECHR** / European Court of Human Rights | EU institutions |
- **Dec 2025:** CoE convention for an **International Claims Commission for Ukraine**; CoE also runs the **Budapest Convention on Cybercrime**.
- **IUCN:** founded **1948**; HQ **Gland, Switzerland**; publishes the **Red List**; organises the **World Conservation Congress**.
!TRAP Council of Europe (46, Strasbourg) is **not** an EU body (27, Brussels).
### 8. WORLD BANK, IMF AND WTO DEVELOPMENTS
#### 8.1 World Bank "Water Forward"
- Launched by WBG President **Ajay Banga** on **15 April 2026**.
- Goal: **water security for 1 billion people by 2030** = **400 million by WBG + 600 million by partners**.
- Delivered through **country-led Water Compacts**.
#### 8.2 Borrowers' Platform
- Launched by **developing countries** at the **IMF–World Bank Spring Meetings 2026**.
- A **coordination forum for debtor nations**; strengthens **debt management**.
!TRAP The Borrowers' Platform is **not a debt restructuring mechanism**.
#### 8.3 World Bank Group – Background
- **189 members**; World Bank set up **1944**; President **Ajay Banga**.
- Five arms: **IBRD, IDA, IFC, MIGA, ICSID**.
#### 8.4 WTO 14th Ministerial Conference (MC14)
- Held at **Yaoundé, Cameroon**, **26–29 March 2026**.
- Ended **without agreement** on its main issues, including **WTO reform**; issues sent back to **Geneva**.
- The **e-commerce customs duty moratorium** was **not renewed** and **lapsed**.
- **66 WTO members** decided to implement the already-negotiated **plurilateral e-commerce agreement**.
#### 8.5 IMF World Economic Outlook (April 2026)
- India's growth projected at **6.5%** for **FY27**; global growth for **2026** cut to **3.1%**.
