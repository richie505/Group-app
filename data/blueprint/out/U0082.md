## U0082 | ANDHRA CHOLAS / TELUGU CHOLAS – RENATI CHOLAS AND THEIR INSCRIPTIONS
### 1. RENATI CHOLAS – ANCESTRY, TERRITORY AND OVERLORDS
- Claimed **Suryavamsa Kshatriya** descent from **Karikala Chola**; culture and administration in **Telugu**.
- Territory: **Renadu "Saptasahasra"** (7,000 villages), also called Renati Cholanadi – where **Kadapa, Kurnool and Chittoor** meet, upper **Pennar** basin.
- **Principal capital: Peddachapalli** (Kamalapuram taluk, **Kadapa**); Dhanunjaya Varma's capital: **Chimbapuram (Chimamburu)**.
- Overlords: first feudatories of the **Pallavas**, later of the **Badami Chalukyas**; their Telugu inscription style was later copied by the **Eastern Chalukyas**. Renadu art shows **Pallava** influence.
- Successors: **Banas and Vaidumbas**; Vaidumbas issued Telugu **hero-stone (veerakal)** inscriptions.
### 2. RULERS – PERIOD AND CONTRIBUTION
| Ruler | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Nandivarma** | **Progenitor** |
| **Dhanunjaya Varma** | **Founder**, son of Nandivarma; **Kalamalla inscription** |
| Mahendra Vikramavarman | Grandson of Nandivarma; title **Muditha Silakshara Navarama**; father of Punyakumara |
| **Punyakumara (625–650 CE)** | **Greatest**; wife **Vasantapori**; titles **Pormukharama, Purushashardula, Uttamottama, Prithvivallabha**; Potladurthi and **Malepadu** records |
| **Satyaditya** | Title **Chola Maharaja / Chola Mahadhiraju**; decline began after him |
| **Elachola Maharaja** | **Last ruler** |
!TRAP **Nandivarma = progenitor**; **Dhanunjaya Varma = founder**.
### 3. INSCRIPTIONS AND TELUGU
| Inscription | Ruler | Point |
|---|---|---|
| **Kalamalla (Kadapa)** | **Dhanunjaya Varma (Erikal Mutturaju Dhananjaya)** | **575 CE**; **first Telugu inscription** (G2 2012); prose; not fully available today |
| **Erragudipadu** (Kamalapuram taluk) | Erikal Mutturaju | Stone record; script like Kalamalla, late 6th century |
| **Malepadu plates** (now in the **Madras Museum**) | **Punyakumara** | **Sanskrit** prose |
- **33** Renati Chola Telugu inscriptions, mostly gift records (**dana sasanalu**), around Jammalamadugu and Proddatur.
- Before **575 CE**: Telugu only in **names**; **575–848 CE**: Telugu in **full prose sentences**.
- First to make Telugu a **royal and legal language**, replacing **Prakrit and Sanskrit** – about **five centuries before Nannaya**.
- G2 2019 key: Renati Cholas = **"keeping Telugu in the history of Andhra"**.
!TRAP "Erragudipadu, 575 CE, first Telugu inscription" merges two records: the **575 CE** first Telugu inscription is **Kalamalla**.
