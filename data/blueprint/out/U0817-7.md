### 1. WHO ISSUES CURRENCY AND DEMONETISATION
| Law / event | Provision / fact |
|---|---|
| **Section 22, RBI Act** | **Banknotes** issued by **RBI** |
| **Coinage Act, 2011** | **Coins** issued by **Government of India** |
| **Demonetisation** | **₹500 and ₹1,000** notes; announced by the Government (**PM Narendra Modi**) on **8 November 2016**; ceased to be legal tender from midnight |
- Aims of demonetisation: curb **black money**, **counterfeit notes**, promote **digital payments**.
!TRAP **Banknotes = RBI**; **coins = Government of India** – not the other way round.
