### 7. PROBLEMS OF LARGE CITIES
- **Waste**: ~**62 million tonnes** MSW a year; **70% collected, 30% processed**.
- **Pollution**: **8 of the world's 10** most polluted cities in India; Delhi most polluted capital.
- **Congestion**: 1.5–2 hours a day lost; **one planner per 1 lakh** (developed countries 1:5,000).
- **Urban flooding**: building over lakes, wetlands, floodplains; concrete over porous soil; blocked drains.
- **Urban heat island**: dense construction, asphalt, lost tree cover.
!TRAP Building **basements** is NOT a flood-control measure.
