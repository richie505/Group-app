#### 2.2 National Planning Committee, 1938
- Set up **under Bose**; **Chairman: Jawaharlal Nehru**; Secretary: **K.T. Shah**.

- Idea of a Planning Commission in the British period = **Netaji Bose**.

- Tagore gave Bose the title **"Desh Nayak"** (1938, Shantiniketan).
### 3. TRIPURI CRISIS, 1939
- Contest: moderates vs radicals for the presidency.
- Bose **defeated Gandhi-backed Dr. Pattabhi Sitaramayya** (**1,580 to 1,377**) - APPSC 2018.
- Gandhi: **"Pattabhi's defeat is my defeat."**
- **Dispute:** over the **formation of the Congress Working Committee**; old guard refused to cooperate.
- **Pant Resolution:** Bose to name the Working Committee **according to Gandhi's wishes**.
- **Outcome:** Working Committee deadlock → Bose **resigned (April 1939)** → **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** became President.
- Bose was removed from party positions in **August 1939**.
### 4. FORWARD BLOC
- Founded by **Subhas Chandra Bose**, **1939** (April), after resigning the presidency.
- **Aim:** a bloc **within Congress** to unite **radical, left-wing and anti-imperialist** forces.
- **Sheel Bhadra Yajee** (Bihar) joined Bose in founding the All India Forward Bloc; also linked to the INA.
- **Jul 2026:** AIFB demanded an apology from the West Bengal BJP chief for calling Forward Bloc members "goons" (13 Jul 2026).
#### 4.1 Andhra Forward Bloc - Madduri Annapurnaiah
- **Madduri Annapurnaiah** - **first Andhra leader to join**; led it in Andhra with **Peta Bapayya** (APPSC 2018, G2 2019).
- Title: **"Andhra Netaji"**; born **1899**, **Pithapuram** taluk.
- Jailed **18 months** for the article **"Chicharapidugu"**; **4 years** in jail as a Forward Bloc member in WWII.
- Later edited **Jai Bharat, Navashakti, Velugu**.
### 5. BOSE ABROAD - ESCAPE, RADIO, LEGION
#### 5.1 Escape Route
- **January 1941**: Calcutta → **Peshawar → Kabul → Moscow → Berlin**.
- Saw the war as a godsend: **"England's need is India's opportunity"** (like "Need of England is an opportunity for Ireland").
#### 5.2 In Germany
| Organisation | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Free India Centre** | Berlin, with Hitler's approval |
| **Free Indian Legion** | Raised by Bose from Indian **POWs captured by Rommel in North Africa** |
| **Azad Hind Radio** | Started in **Germany**, broadcast from **Berlin** (APPSC 2018) |
- Bose reached Germany on **3 April 1941**.
- **Early 1943:** German submarine → Japanese submarine → **Singapore, July 1943**; reached **Tokyo June 1943**.
!TRAP Azad Hind Radio = **Germany**, not Japan.
### 6. INDIAN NATIONAL ARMY (AZAD HIND FAUJ)
#### 6.1 First INA, 1942
- Idea of **Giani Pritam Singh Dhillon** with **Capt. Mohan Singh** and **Major Iwaichi Fujiwara** (head of **F-Kikan**).
- **Capt. Mohan Singh**: **first commander**; INA formed from **Indian POWs after the fall of Singapore** (February 1942) with Japanese help; formally raised **September 1942** (~40,000 volunteers; first division **16,300** men).
- Formed in **Singapore** (then part of British **Malaya**), South-East Asia.
- **IIL:** **Rash Behari Bose** (Japan) - **Tokyo conference 28-30 March 1942**; **Bangkok Convention (June 1942)** met the Japanese to set up the INA; IIL = political wing of INA.
- **Dissolved December 1942**.
- **15 Jun 2026:** INA's roots traced to the IIL in **Thailand** - two leaders died in a March 1942 plane crash before the June conference.
!TRAP **Rash Behari Bose** (IIL, Japan) is NOT **Rash Behari Ghosh** (Moderate).
#### 6.2 Second Phase under Subhas Bose, 1943
- **4 July 1943:** Rash Behari Bose handed IIL/INA charge to Subhas Bose.
- **25 August 1943:** Bose became **Supreme Commander** of the INA.
- **HQ:** Singapore initially, later **Rangoon**.
- **Rani of Jhansi Regiment** (1943): **all-women combat unit**, under **Capt. Lakshmi Sahgal**; named after Rani Lakshmi Bai.
- Rash Behari Bose: "**Avenge the blood of Indian Martyrs**... Stand up against the entire Anglo-American enemy".
#### 6.3 Campaigns and Ethos
- **Imphal-Kohima campaign (1944):** INA flag hoisted on Indian soil at **Moirang, Manipur, 14 April 1944**; retreat after Japanese defeats and supply shortages.
- **INA motto:** **"Ittefaq, Itmad, Qurbani"** (Unity, Faith, Sacrifice).
- **INA anthem:** Hindustani **Jana Gana Mana** by **Abid Hasan 'Safrani'**.
- **Order:** first INA (1942) → Bose takes command (July 1943) → Azad Hind Government (21 Oct 1943) → Red Fort trials (Nov 1945).
### 7. AZAD HIND GOVERNMENT
- **Provisional Government of Free India** (**Arzi Hukumat-e-Azad Hind**), proclaimed **21 October 1943** at **Singapore** (not Rangoon).
- **Recognised by 9 countries** - Axis powers **Japan, Germany, Italy** and allies (**Burma, Thailand, Philippines, Manchukuo**).
- **Declared war on Britain and the USA**.
- A separate government; INA was its army.

- **Jan 2026:** PM marked the **82nd anniversary** of the Port Blair tricolour.

### 8. RED FORT TRIALS AND RIN MUTINY
#### 8.1 INA Trials, November 1945
- Venue: **Red Fort, Delhi**; joint trial 1945-46.
- Accused: **Shah Nawaz Khan, Prem Kumar Sehgal, Gurbaksh Singh Dhillon**.
- **Defence led by Bhulabhai Desai** with **Tej Bahadur Sapru, Nehru, Asaf Ali, K.N. Katju**; also C. Rajagopalachari.
- NOT in the defence: **Sardar Patel**. Did NOT face the trials: **Mohan Singh**.
- **Rashid Ali** sentenced to seven years.
- Linked to INA: Shah Nawaz Khan, P.K. Sahgal, **Shaukat Ali Malik**; NOT linked: **Major Kartar Singh, B.C. Dutta**.
- Shah Nawaz Khan later became a **Union minister**; Prem Kumar Sehgal married **Captain Lakshmi**.
#### 8.2 Royal Indian Navy Mutiny, 1946
- Began **18 February 1946** when ratings of **HMIS Talwar**, Bombay, refused duty (food, mistreatment).
- **Naval Central Strike Committee** led by rating **M.S. Khan**; spread to Karachi and other ports.
- Bombay ships surrendered **23 February 1946**.
!KEY INA trials + RIN Mutiny showed the British could **no longer rely on the loyalty of Indian armed forces**.

### 10. WARTIME CONTEXT - AUGUST OFFER, INDIVIDUAL SATYAGRAHA, QUIT INDIA
#### 10.1 August Offer and Individual Satyagraha, 1940
- Congress ministries resigned **Nov 1939**.
- **August Offer (8 Aug 1940)**, Viceroy **Linlithgow**: Dominion Status as goal; expanded Executive Council; War Advisory Council; constitution by Indians after the war - **rejected by Congress**.
- **Individual Satyagraha, 17 October 1940**, from **Paunar, Wardha**; first **Vinoba Bhave**, second **Nehru**.
#### 10.2 Quit India, 1942
- Immediate cause: **failure of the Cripps Mission (March 1942)** - Gandhi's **"post-dated cheque"**.
- **Wardha CWC 14 July**; **AICC at Gowalia Tank Maidan, Bombay, 8 August 1942** (not 6 August); arrests **9 August**.
- Gandhi at **Aga Khan Palace, Pune**; CWC at **Ahmednagar Fort**.
- Movement became leaderless and violent (railways, telegraphs, police stations).

- **Martyrs** shot leading flag processions: **Kanaklata Barua** (Assam, aged 17); **Matangini Hazra** (**Tamluk**, Bengal).
