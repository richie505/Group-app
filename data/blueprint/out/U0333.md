## U0333 | COOPERATIVE AND COMPETITIVE FEDERALISM – NITI AAYOG, GST COUNCIL, STATE RANKINGS
### 1. TYPES OF FEDERALISM
| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Cooperative federalism** | Centre and States work together on a **common platform**; NITI's version is **bottom-up**, also called **"marble-cake federalism"** |
| **Competitive federalism** | States compete **with each other and with the Centre** to draw **investment** and improve outcomes |
| **Competitive sub-federalism** | Competition **among local bodies / districts** (APPSC G1 2017), e.g. **Aspirational Districts Programme** rankings |
- Cooperative federalism was **first suggested by the Sarkaria Commission** (APPSC G1 2017); put into practice by **NITI Aayog (2015)**.
- NITI planning under cooperative federalism: sound **village-level plans combined upwards**.
- Competitive federalism tool: better policies and services; driver: **NITI's performance rankings**.
- Rankings push States to **improve social and development indicators** – the link to sustainability (SDG) targets (APPSC GS key).
!PAIR GST Council | Cooperative | joint decisions || NITI State rankings | Competitive | States compete
!TRAP Competitive federalism = **States** compete; competitive sub-federalism = **local bodies** compete.

### 2. KINDS OF BODY
| Type | Basis | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Constitutional** | Set up by the Constitution itself | Election Commission, PSCs, Finance Commission, CAG, Attorney General, Advocate General, National Commissions for SCs, STs, BCs, State Finance and Election Commissions |
| **Statutory** | Act of Parliament / State Legislature | **NHRC, Lokpal, NGT** |
| **Extra-constitutional / non-statutory** | Executive order or **Cabinet resolution** (power not found in the Constitution) | **NITI Aayog, Planning Commission, NDC** |
### 3. NITI AAYOG AND ITS PREDECESSORS
| Body | Created by | Year | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Planning Commission** | Cabinet resolution (non-statutory) | **1950** | Extra-constitutional; replaced by NITI |
| **NDC** | Executive resolution | **1952** | Inactive; role taken by NITI's Governing Council |
| **NITI Aayog** | **Union Cabinet resolution** | **1 January 2015** | Non-constitutional, non-statutory **think-tank**; advisory only |
- NITI has **no power to allocate plan grants** → Finance Commission's domain restored (Jan 2026).
!TRAP NITI Aayog was created by a **Cabinet resolution**, not an Act or Ordinance.
#### 3.1 Composition
| Post | Who |
|---|---|
| Chairperson | **Prime Minister** (no full-time Chairman) |
| Vice-Chairperson | Appointed by the PM; **Ashok Kumar Lahiri (April 2026)**, after Suman K. Bery |
| Members | Full-time; **max 2 part-time** (rotational); **max 4 ex-officio Union ministers** |
| CEO | **Secretary-rank**, fixed tenure |
#### 3.2 Hubs and Tools
- **Team India Hub** – leads **State engagement** with the Centre (APPSC GS 2018); **Knowledge and Innovation Hub** – think-tank arm; **DMEO** – monitoring and evaluation.
- **Regional Councils** – issues affecting **more than one State**; jointly headed by a **CM (by rotation)** and a **Union Minister**.
- **State Support Mission** – State Institutions for Transformation, e.g. **GRIT Gujarat, MITRA Maharashtra**.
### 4. GOVERNING COUNCIL OF NITI AAYOG
- **PM (chair)** + CMs of all States and of **UTs with legislatures (Delhi, J&K, Puducherry)** + **Lt Governors** of other UTs + ex-officio members + Vice-Chairperson + full-time members + special invitees.
- First constituted **February 2015**; reconstituted **February 2021**; took over the **NDC's** role.
- **11th meeting: 11 June 2026** – PM spoke on AI safeguards, El Nino and cooperative federalism; **Naidu presented Swarna Andhra @2047**.
!TRAP Regional Councils = CM by rotation + Union Minister; Governing Council = **PM chairs**.

### 6. GST COUNCIL – COOPERATIVE FEDERALISM MODEL
- **Article 279A**; Centre and States decide **jointly**.
- Voting: **Centre one-third, States two-thirds**.
- Critique: curtailed States' taxing powers (local taxes subsumed); **compensation disputes**.
### 7. INTER-STATE INSTITUTIONS
| Institution | Basis | Chair / rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Inter-State Council** | **Art. 263**; set up **1990** on Sarkaria's advice | **PM**; recommendatory |
| **Zonal Councils** | **States Reorganisation Act, 1956** (statutory); proposed by **Nehru** | **Union Home Minister** (all five); each State: CM + 2 ministers; advisory |
| **North Eastern Council** | **1971** | – |
| **River boards** | **River Boards Act, 1956** | – |
- **5 Zonal Councils:** North, South, Central, East, West.
!TRAP ISC chair = **PM**; Zonal Councils chair = **Union Home Minister**.
### 8. CENTRALLY SPONSORED SCHEMES
- Large part of Centre-State transfers; need **matching State contributions**; parameters set by the Centre.
- Core CSS funding: **60:40**; **North-Eastern and Himalayan States 90:10**.
- Criticism: CSS **squeeze State priorities**. Fixes: revisit CSS, greater State say in GST design, regular ISC meetings.
