## U0446 | SERVICES SECTOR – GROWTH, COMPOSITION AND CONTRIBUTION
### 1. WHAT IS (AND IS NOT) A SERVICE
| Tertiary (services) | Not services |
|---|---|
| Trade, hotels, restaurants | Mining & quarrying (primary) |
| Transport, storage, communication | **Forestry, fishing** (primary) |
| Finance, insurance, real estate, business services | Manufacturing, construction, utilities (secondary) |
| Public administration, defence, other services | Agriculture (primary) |
- **NIC** lists **15** types of services; **WTO** uses the UN's **UNCPC** (Central Product Classification).
- AP Survey uses **8** service sub-sectors.
| List | Services |
|---|---|
| Union | Telecom, postal, broadcasting, financial services, insurance |
| State | Health, real estate |
| Concurrent | **Education, accounting, engineering, electricity** |
### 2. SHARE IN GVA AND EMPLOYMENT – THE MISMATCH
- Services = **more than 50%** of GVA (Economic Survey 2025-26) – **highest** contributor to GDP; employment only **about a third**.
| Sector (2021-22) | Share of GDP | Share of workforce |
|---|---|---|
| Secondary | 24.29% | 22.24% |
| Tertiary | **53.89%** | **33.80%** |
- **Employment-output paradox** (NITI Aayog): high-end services (IT, finance) need high skills, so most new jobs are in low-value trade, hospitality, transport.
- Regular wages: **51.5%** of service workers vs about **1%** in agriculture.
- Growth: **9.1%** in FY2025-26 (FAE; new series 8.9%); **10%** in Q1 FY2026-27, led by finance, real estate, IT and professional services (**12.1%**).
### 3. SERVICE-LED GROWTH DEBATE
- Boom began in the **mid-1980s**; sped up after the **1990s reforms**.
- **EAC-PM Chairman** (Nov 2025): service-led growth cannot last without a **manufacturing base**; manufacturing creates demand for logistics, IT, finance and **steadies GDP cycles**.
- **Software** is a major export, but India lacks a strong **hardware** base.
### 4. SERVICES EXPORTS AND FDI
- India's share of world services trade: **2% (2005) → 4.3% (2024)**.
- Rank: **8th**-largest services exporter (WTO, April 2025 data); also **8th** in 2014.
- 2024-25: exports **USD 387.5 bn** vs imports USD 198.7 bn → surplus.
- **Software and business services = 77.8%** of services exports.
- Targets: share of world services exports **4.2% by 2022** (older target); services exports **USD 1 trillion** within the USD 2 trillion export goal by **2030**.
- FDI: services had the **largest share of FDI** in 1997-2000; in FY2024-25 the **services sector** was again the **top** FDI equity recipient (~**19%**, about $9.35 bn), ahead of computer software and hardware (16%).
### 5. INDICES AND TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **PMI** | Survey of purchasing managers; **above 50 = expansion**, below 50 = contraction (manufacturing and services) |
| **Index of Services Production (ISP)** | MoSPI's first trial (for April 2026) – services counterpart of the **IIP**; monthly, within **60 days**; base **2024-25**; Laspeyres index |
| **GCC** | Offshore unit of an MNC doing its **own core work** (not a third-party vendor) |
- GCCs: **over 1,800**; their forex earnings soften the merchandise trade deficit.

### 7. ANDHRA PRADESH AND STATES
- Services = **AP's largest sector** – **43%** of GVA (current prices).
- 2025-26: **Rs 3,59,931 crore (+10.67%)**, communications fastest (**15.13%**).
- Kerala **64.3%**, Bihar **58.7%** of GSVA from services.
- Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Telangana = about **40%** of India's services output.
- Largest services share in State GDP, 2008-09: **Delhi** > Chandigarh > Kerala > Maharashtra.
