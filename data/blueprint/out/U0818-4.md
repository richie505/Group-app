### 1. VB-G RAM G ACT, 2025 – REPLACES MGNREGA
#### 1.1 Basic Facts
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Full name | **Viksit Bharat–Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin)** – **VB-G RAM G** |
| Replaces | **MGNREGA (2005)** |
| Passed by Parliament | **19 December 2025** |
| President's assent | December 2025 (gazette notification **21 December 2025**); now the **VB-G RAM G Act, 2025** |
| Described as | **Largest structural change** to rural jobs in **20 years** |
| Central cost | **₹95,692 crore** |
| Total cost | **₹1.51 lakh crore** |
| Extra burden on States | About **₹30,000 crore a year** |
#### 1.2 MGNREGA vs VB-G RAM G
| Feature | MGNREGA | VB-G RAM G |
|---|---|---|
| Guaranteed days | **100** (up to 150 in special cases) | **125** (**Section 5(1)**) |
| Wage funding | **Centre 100%** | **60:40**; **90:10** for **NE and Himalayan** States |
| Allocation | **Bottom-up** labour budget from Gram Panchayat | **Normative** – set by the Centre |
| Agriculture pause | None | **60 days** in peak sowing/harvest |
| Cards | Job cards valid **5 years** | **Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Cards** valid **3 years** |
| Penalty (**Section 27**) | **₹1,000** | **₹10,000** |
- **Viksit Gram Panchayat Plans**: four areas – **water security**, **core rural infrastructure**, **livelihood infrastructure**, **extreme weather mitigation**; integrated with **PM Gati Shakti**.
- **Special cards**: single women, PwDs, elderly, released bonded labourers, **PVTGs**, transgender persons.
- **MGNREGA eligibility**: **adult members of any rural household**.
- Average MGNREGA employment: about **50 days per household**.
#### 1.3 Rural Labour Data
- **Rural female LFPR**: **24.6% (2017–18) → 47.6% (2023–24)**.
- **Nominal rural wages (2015–25)**: grew only **3.6–6.4% a year** (**Labour Bureau**).
!TRAP 125 days is in **Section 5(1)**; the ₹10,000 penalty is in **Section 27**. General sharing is **60:40**, not 90:10 (90:10 only for NE/Himalayan States).
!NUM 100 → 125 days; 5 → 3 years card validity; ₹1,000 → ₹10,000 penalty; 60 days agriculture pause

### 3. HEALTH PROGRAMMES – HPV VACCINATION AND NAP-AMR 2.0
#### 3.1 Free Nationwide HPV Vaccination for Girls
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Ministry | **Union Health Ministry** |
| Target | **Girls aged 14**; **free and voluntary (optional)** |
| Vaccine | **Gardasil** (**quadrivalent**) – **HPV 16 & 18** (cervical cancer) + **HPV 6 & 11** (warts) |
| Dose | **Single dose** (optional second dose after 3–5 years) |
| Procured via | **Gavi, the Vaccine Alliance** |
| Tracked on | **U-WIN** |
| Recommended by | **NTAGI** |
| Where given | Only government facilities – **Ayushman Arogya Mandirs**, CHCs, hospitals, medical colleges |
- Indigenous **Cervavac** is **not yet WHO-approved** for the programme.
- **Cervical cancer**: **2nd most common** cancer among Indian women; about **80,000 cases** and **42,000+ deaths** a year; **HPV 16/18 cause over 80%**.
- Vaccine **does not replace screening**: **Pap smear from 21 years**; **HPV DNA test after 30**.
- **WHO 90–70–90 targets by 2030 (WHA 73.2)**: **90%** girls vaccinated by age **15**; **70%** women screened (at **35 and 45**); **90%** treated.
- **Mission Indradhanush**: immunisation of children and pregnant women.
!TRAP HPV vaccine is for girls aged **14**, single-dose **Gardasil** (not Cervavac); it does **not** replace screening.
#### 3.2 National Action Plan on AMR 2.0 (2025–29)
- **NAP-AMR 2.0** for **2025–29**; earlier **NAP-AMR 1.0** in **2017**.
- Approach: **One Health** – human, animal and environmental health interlinked.
- Governance: inter-sectoral supervision under **NITI Aayog** via a **Coordination & Monitoring Committee**; States urged to set up **State AMR Cells**; national dashboard proposed.
- States with NAP 1.0 State plans (only 7): **Kerala, MP, Delhi, AP, Gujarat, Sikkim, Punjab**.
- **Watch-group** antibiotics: about **59%** of consumption (2022); about **8 in 10** hospital entrants carry drug-resistant bacteria.
- Animal antibiotics: India **4th largest** consumer; use to rise about **82% by 2030**.
- **Musi River (Hyderabad)**: antibiotic levels about **1,000×** safe limits.
- Newborns: **50,000+ deaths a year** from resistant sepsis; India has the **highest MDR-TB burden**.
- **Colistin banned in animal feed**.
- Resistance mechanisms: limiting uptake, target modification, **efflux pumps**, drug inactivation (**β-lactamases**).
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **NDM-1** | **New Delhi Metallo-β-lactamase** – resistance to β-lactams including **carbapenems** |
| **Schedule H1** | Drugs & Cosmetics Rules – select antibiotics **only on prescription**, register kept |
| **Red Line Campaign** | **Red vertical line** on antibiotic strips |
| **Delhi Declaration on AMR** | **2017** inter-ministerial commitment |
| **Chennai Declaration** | **Medical societies'** consensus on rational antibiotic use |
| **Kerala** | **PROUD** and **AMRITH** initiatives |
### 4. DISEASES IN NEWS AND ADOLESCENT MENTAL HEALTH
#### 4.1 Kyasanur Forest Disease (Monkey Fever)
- News: a **29-year-old man in Karnataka** died of KFD (**January 2026**).
- **Tick-borne viral haemorrhagic** disease; first identified **1957**, **Kyasanur Forest, Karnataka**.
- Virus: family **Flaviviridae**, genus **Flavivirus**; vector – **hard tick Haemaphysalis spinigera**.
- **No human-to-human transmission**; season starts **Oct–Nov**, peaks **Jan–April**.
- Fatality **5–10%**; **no antiviral**; **vaccine available**.
#### 4.2 Scabies (WHO)
- Contagious **parasitic** skin disease caused by the mite **Sarcoptes scabiei** – **not viral**; itching worse **at night**.
#### 4.3 Adolescent Mental Health Crisis
- Trigger: deaths of **three adolescent girls in Ghaziabad**.
- **National Mental Health Survey**: **7–10%** of adolescents have diagnosable conditions (**not 20%**); **5–7%** school children have **ADHD**.
- India has **fewer than 10,000 psychiatrists** for **1.4 billion+** people; **800 million+** internet users.
- **WHO guidelines (2019)** warned against excessive **screen exposure**.
- Response: **Economic Survey 2025–26**, **NMHP**, **Ayushman Bharat HWCs**, **Tele-MANAS** (TKDL is **not** a mental-health measure).
- Social-media regulation models: **Australia, France, South Korea**.
!TRAP KFD has **no human-to-human spread**; scabies is **parasitic**, not viral; adolescents with conditions = **7–10%**, not 20%.
