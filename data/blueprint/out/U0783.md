## U0783 | OCEAN ACIDIFICATION – CAUSES, EFFECTS ON MARINE ECOSYSTEMS AND CORAL REEFS
### 1. OCEAN ACIDIFICATION – CAUSE AND NAMES
- **Primary cause**: **dissolution of atmospheric CO2** in seawater (APPSC-GS 2024).
- Called the **"evil twin of global warming"** and **"the other CO2 problem"**.
- Oceans absorb about **one-third** of human CO2 (also cited 30-40%).
- **Cement** sector: about **5-8%** of global anthropogenic CO2.
### 2. CARBONATE CHEMISTRY AND pH
1. CO2 + H2O → **carbonic acid** → H+ + bicarbonate.
2. Extra **H+ binds carbonate ions**.
3. **Less carbonate** left for shells and skeletons.
- Surface pH **8.2 (pre-industrial) → ~8.1** = about **30% rise in acidity**; ocean **still alkaline**.
- Worst in **cold/temperate and Arctic waters**: CO2 more soluble in cold water; melting sea ice exposes more water.
- Eutrophic **bloom collapse** adds CO2 → worsens acidification.
!TRAP pH falls only 0.1, yet acidity rises ~30% (log scale). The ocean has not turned acidic.
### 3. CORAL REEFS AND BLEACHING
- Reef-building corals secrete **calcium carbonate** skeletons; symbiotic algae **zooxanthellae** give food and colour.
- **Bleaching**: heat-stressed corals lose zooxanthellae → lose colour, food and skeleton-building.
- **4th global bleaching event** (declared by NOAA-ICRI, Apr 2024): Jan 2023-Mar 2025 hit **84%** of world reefs in **82 countries/territories**. Earlier events: **1998, 2010, 2014-17**.

### 4. EFFECTS ON CALCIFYING ORGANISMS
| Organism | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Pteropods ("sea butterflies")** | Shells dissolve; key **food for fish** |
| Corals, bivalve larvae | Use **aragonite**, more soluble than calcite → more sensitive |
| Mussels, oysters | Calcification falls at high CO2 |
### 5. RELATED STRESSORS
| Stressor | Meaning / effect |
|---|---|
| **Marine heatwave (MHW)** | SST **3-4°C** above average for at least **5 days**; number **doubled since 1982** |
| **Deoxygenation** | Nutrient runoff → algal blooms → decay uses oxygen → **hypoxic dead zones** |
- **Cyclones over MHWs**: up to **20% stronger winds** + **12% more rain**.
- MHWs = **strongest global driver of coral-cover decline** (GCRMN 2026).
### 6. CORAL RESTORATION
| Method | How | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Biorock (mineral accretion)** | Low-voltage current on steel frames deposits limestone | **Gulf of Kutch** (ZSI) |
| **Coral translocation** | Colonies moved away from project sites | **Great Nicobar** project |
| **3D-printed reef modules** | Artificial reef | Off **Ramanathapuram** (TN), **PMMSY** |
- **Biorock** = restoration of damaged coral reefs (UPSC 2022); invented **1976** by **Wolf Hilbertz**.
- **NGT (Feb 2026)**: MoEFCC to ensure coral protection and regeneration by a **proven scientific method** for Great Nicobar.
