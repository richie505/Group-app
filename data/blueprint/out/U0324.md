## U0324 | CENTRE-STATE FINANCIAL RELATIONS – ARTICLES 264-293; TAXES, GRANTS-IN-AID, BORROWING
### 1. CONSTITUTIONAL FRAMEWORK – PART XII
- **Part XII** (Finance, Property, Contracts and Suits): **Arts 264-293**; financial relations = **Arts 268-293**.
- **Distribution of revenues** = **Arts 268-281** (Union taxes, grants-in-aid, Finance Commission).
- Budget procedure is in **Part V** (Arts 112-116).
| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| **265** | **No tax** levied or collected **except by authority of law** |
| **266** | **Consolidated Funds** and **Public Accounts** – CFI receives all Union revenues, loans raised and loan repayments |
| **267** | **Contingency Funds** |
| **274** | Bills affecting taxation of **agricultural income** |
| **275** | **Statutory grants-in-aid** |
| **276** | **Profession tax** (State) |
| **280 / 281** | **Finance Commission** (quasi-judicial) / its recommendations |
| **282** | **Discretionary grants** |
| **285 / 289** | **Union property** free from State taxes / **State property** free from Union taxes |
| **292 / 293** | **Borrowing** – Union / States |
| 148 | **CAG** – certifies **net proceeds** for the divisible pool |
- Mutual exemption exception: Centre can tax a State's **commercial operations** and State-owned companies.
### 2. TAXES – WHO LEVIES, COLLECTS AND KEEPS
#### 2.1 Articles 268-272
| Article | Levied by | Collected / kept by | Examples / status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **268** | **Union** | **States collect and appropriate** | **Stamp duties** on bills of exchange, cheques, promissory notes, insurance policies, share transfers |
| **268A** | Union | Union + States | **Service tax** – added by **88th (2003)**, **omitted** by **101st (2016)** |
| **269** | **Union** | **Union collects; assigned to States** (APPSC-GS 2018) | Inter-State sale/purchase of goods (**not newspapers**), **consignment**; proceeds **do not form part of the CFI** |
| **269A** | Union | Shared on **GST Council** advice | **IGST** on inter-State supplies (101st) |
| **270** | Union | **Shared on Finance Commission** advice (**divisible pool**) | **Income tax** (non-agricultural), **corporation tax**, **CGST**, customs; excludes 268, 269, 269A, surcharges, cesses |
| **271** | Union | **Union only** | **Surcharges** on 269/270 taxes |
| **272** | Union | Shared by law | Union excise – **omitted by 80th (2000)** |
- Art. 268 excise on medicinal/toilet preparations ended with GST.
!TRAP 268 = Union levies, States collect; 269 = Union collects, assigned to States; 271 surcharges are NOT shared (APPSC-G1 2024).
#### 2.2 Tax → Authority
| Tax | Levied | Kept |
|---|---|---|
| **Income tax** (non-agricultural) | Union | Shared (Art. 270) |
| **Corporation tax** | Union – never States | Shared since the **80th Amendment** |
| **Customs** | Union – not a State revenue | Divisible pool |
| **Land revenue** | State | State |
| **Agricultural income tax** | **States only** (State List **Entry 46**) | State |
| **Excise on liquor** | **States** | State |
| **Entertainment tax** | States – largely in GST except **local bodies** | State |
- **State-only taxes:** land revenue, agricultural income tax, stamp duties and registration, State excise on alcohol, taxes on **land and buildings**, taxes on **vehicles**.
### 3. AMENDMENTS CHANGING TAX DISTRIBUTION
| Amendment | Year | Change |
|---|---|---|
| **60th** | **1988** | Profession tax ceiling **₹250 → ₹2,500** |
| **80th** | **2000** (effective **1 April 1996**) | **10th FC "Alternative Scheme of Devolution"**; omitted **Art. 272**; all central taxes shareable |
| **88th** | **2003** | Inserted **Art. 268A** (service tax) |
| **101st** | **2016** | Inserted **246A, 269A, 279A**; omitted **268A** |
- **80th Amendment:** States got a share of **gross proceeds** of Union taxes – **29%** for 1996-97 to 1999-2000.
- It moved **stock-exchange taxes, estate duty, railway fares, newspapers** (old Art. 269) into the **Art. 270 pool**; **surcharges stayed out**.
!NUM 60th = 1988; 80th = 2000 (from 1996); 88th = 2003; 101st = 2016.
### 4. PROFESSION TAX – ARTICLE 276
- States tax **professions, trades, callings and employments** (Art. 276).
- **Art. 276(2):** the Constitution limits the total each person pays – **₹2,500 per person per year**.
- Raised from **₹250** by the **60th Amendment, 1988** (PYQ 2017).
### 5. GST AND FISCAL AUTONOMY
#### 5.1 GST Framework
- **101st Amendment, 2016**; GST in force **1 July 2017**; **destination-based**; replaced about **17** taxes (CGST, SGST, IGST, UTGST).
- **Art. 246A:** Parliament and States both make GST law; **only Parliament** for **inter-State supply**.
- **Art. 269A:** IGST; revenue to the **destination State**.
- Amended Arts 248-250, 268-271, 286, 366, 368 and the Sixth/Seventh Schedules.
- **GST 2.0:** effective **September 2025**.
- **AP HC (May 2026):** Central and State GST authorities probing the same entity must decide who proceeds – **no dual action**.
#### 5.2 GST Council – Article 279A
| Feature | Fact |
|---|---|
| Chair | **Union Finance Minister** |
| Members | Union MoS (Finance) + **State Finance Ministers** |
| Votes | **Centre 1/3**, **all States 2/3** |
| Decision | **3/4** of weighted votes – neither side alone |
| Legal status | **Mohit Minerals (2022)**: advice **persuasive, not binding** |
!PAIR Centre | States | Majority || 1/3 | 2/3 | 3/4
#### 5.3 Compensation and Critique
- **Compensation cess** on **luxury and sin goods** paid States' GST revenue loss; guaranteed for **5 years (2017-22)**; its end is a fiscal stress (Kerala, May 2026).
- Critique: States gave up independent indirect-tax powers; weighted voting gives the Centre an effective **veto**.
### 6. CESSES, SURCHARGES AND VERTICAL VS HORIZONTAL DEVOLUTION
- **Vertical devolution** = Centre-State share of the divisible pool; **horizontal** = distribution **among States (inter se)**; both recommended by the **Finance Commission** (Art. **280(3)**).
- **Cesses and surcharges** stay outside the pool: ~**18%** of Centre's gross tax revenue, so effective devolution is near **31-32%** against the **41%** award.
- **Chalo Delhi (2024):** southern States protested **population-based** devolution criteria.
### 7. FINANCE COMMISSIONS – SHARES
| FC | Fact |
|---|---|
| **10th** | Alternative Scheme of Devolution (80th CAA) |
| **14th** | **42%**; "compositional shift" – more devolution, fewer grants → more **unconditional** transfers (APPSC-G1 2017) |
| **15th** (2021-26) | **41%** |
| **16th** (2026-31) | **Retained 41%**; State fiscal deficit cap **3% of GSDP**; end **off-budget borrowing** |
- **Tax devolution 2024-25:** **₹12.87 lakh crore** (BE ₹12.47 lakh crore).
- Devolution is **formula-based and untied**; CSS grants carry central parameters and matching shares.
### 8. GRANTS-IN-AID – STATUTORY VS DISCRETIONARY
| Feature | Statutory (**Art. 275**) | Discretionary (**Art. 282**) |
|---|---|---|
| Recommended by | **Finance Commission** | Earlier **Planning Commission**; now Union ministries |
| Charged on CFI | **Yes** | **No** |
| Purpose | Needy States; **ST welfare**, **Scheduled Areas** | **Any public purpose**, even outside the law-making body's field |
- Art. 275 grants cover State-specific needs that devolution formulas miss (Aug 2026).
!TRAP Statutory grants are on the Finance Commission's advice, NOT NITI Aayog's (APPSC-GS 2019).
- **Shift from grants to devolution:** more **untied** money and **autonomy** for States; fewer conditional transfers.
### 9. PLAN TRANSFER FORMULAS AND SPECIAL CATEGORY
| Formula | Year / Plan | Weights (%) |
|---|---|---|
| **Gadgil** (D.R. Gadgil) | **1969**, **4th Plan** (also 5th) | Population **60**, per-capita income 10, tax effort 10, ongoing irrigation/power 10, special problems 10 |
| **Gadgil-Mukherjee** | **1991**, from **8th Plan** | Population **60**, per-capita income 25, performance 7.5, special problems 7.5 |
- Gadgil formula = **distribution of Central Plan assistance** – "**devolution of funds**" (APPSC-G2 2017).
- Both lost use when the **Planning Commission was abolished (2015)** and Plan grants ended; **NITI Aayog** has **no financial powers**.
#### 9.1 Special Category Status
- First charge on Plan assistance at **90% grant : 10% loan**.
- Parameters (NDC): **hilly terrain**; **low population density / large tribal population**; **strategic border location**; **economic and infrastructural backwardness**; **non-viable State finances**.
!TRAP High per-capita income is NOT a special category parameter.
- **AP:** post-2014 demand for special category status.
### 10. BORROWING – ARTICLES 292 AND 293
| Article | Rule |
|---|---|
| **292** | **Union** borrows on security of the **CFI**, within limits fixed by **Parliament** by law |
| **293(1)** | **State** borrows **only within India**, on its Consolidated Fund, within limits set by the **State Legislature**; **no direct foreign loans** |
| **293(2)** | **Union may lend** to States or **guarantee** their loans (APPSC-G2 2025) |
| **293(3)** | A State with an unpaid Union loan/guarantee needs **Union's consent** (with conditions) for a new loan |
- **Net Borrowing Ceiling** is imposed via **293(3)** and counts **off-budget** borrowings.
- **State of Kerala v UoI (2024):** **Art. 131** suit against the ceiling.
- Extra room: **NPS/UPS** contributions (**₹62,778 crore**, all States, 2024-25).
| Data | Figure |
|---|---|
| SDLs (5-30 years, RBI **e-Kuber**) | Nine States raised **₹20,461 crore** (18 Jun 2026) |
| **AP net borrowings 2024-25** | **₹60,485.40 crore** |
| AP net borrowings 2025-26 (to Dec) | ₹53,878.71 crore |
- SDLs ~35% of TN's revenue receipts (2024-25 RE).
### 11. EMERGENCIES AND FISCAL DISPUTES
- **Art. 354:** in National Emergency the President may **modify revenue distribution**; lasts till the end of the **financial year** in which the emergency ends.
- **Art. 360:** Union directs States on **financial propriety**; **Money/Financial Bills** reserved for the President.
- **Disputes:** Supreme Court's **original jurisdiction (Art. 131)**; matters referred to the **Finance Commission are excluded**.
!TRAP Centre-State fiscal disputes go to the Supreme Court (Art. 131), not to the Finance Commission.
