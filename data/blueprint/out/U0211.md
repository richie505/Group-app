## U0211 | ANDHRA IN THE FREEDOM MOVEMENT – ALLURI SITARAMA RAJU, RAMPA REBELLION, DUGGIRALA GOPALAKRISHNAYYA
### 1. ANDHRA IN THE NATIONAL MOVEMENT – BACKGROUND
#### 1.1 Vandemataram Phase and Bipin Chandra Pal
- **Bipin Chandra Pal** toured Andhra in **1907** (Vandemataram / Swadeshi movement) at the invitation of **Mutnuri Krishna Rao**, editor of the **Krishna Patrika (Machilipatnam)**.
- Host at Machilipatnam (APPSC 2008 key): **Kopalle Hanumantha Rao**, who founded the **Andhra Jatheeya Vidya Parishad** there on **17 Nov 1907**.
- His tour covered Visakhapatnam, Vizianagaram, Kakinada and **Rajahmundry** before Madras, where he gave five lectures on the Madras Beach (May 1907).
- Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya took part in the Vandemataram movement and the **2nd Andhra Students Conference (1909)**.
- Match: **Bipin Chandra Pal – Vande Mataram**; Kandukuri Veeresalingam – **Gadya Tikkana**.
#### 1.2 Non-Cooperation in Andhra – Place and Leader
| Place | Movement | Leader | Year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chirala-Perala (Bapatla taluk, Guntur) | Against municipal tax | **Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya** | 1921 |
| Palnadu (Guntur) | **Pullari (grass tax)** / forest satyagraha | **Kanneganti Hanumanthu** | 1921-22 |
| Pedanandipadu (Bapatla taluk, Guntur) | **No-tax campaign** (land revenue) | **Parvataneni Veeraiah Chowdary** | Jan-Feb 1922 |
| Rampa / Manyam (Agency) | Armed tribal rebellion | **Alluri Sitarama Raju** | 1922-24 |
- **"Glorious Decade"** of Andhra = **1921-1931**.
- 1921-22 Guntur saw the NCM's most original methods in Andhra: mass exodus, officials resigning, no-tax campaign.
#### 1.3 Link with AICC Decisions and the NCM Programme
| AICC meeting | Decision |
|---|---|
| **Bezawada AICC (1921)** | Brought Gandhi's programme into Andhra |
| **Delhi AICC (5 Nov 1921)** | Let provincial committees start civil disobedience, including **no-tax** → Guntur did |
- Chirala-Perala municipal tax fight and the Palnadu forest satyagraha were local forms of the same NCM call.
### 2. DUGGIRALA GOPALAKRISHNAYYA
#### 2.1 Life and Education
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Life | **1884-1929**; born 2 June at **Penuganchiprolu** (Krishna district) |
| Education | **M.A. (Hons) Economics, University of Edinburgh, 1916**; Secretary, Edinburgh Indian Association |
| Meeting Gandhi | **1915, London** – introduced by **Ananda K. Coomaraswamy** as "the coming man of your country" |
| Career | Resigned as professor, Rajahmundry Government College; founded **Andhra Vidyapeetha Goshthi** near Chirala (national education) |
| Titles | **"Andhra Ratna"**; also called **"Ramadasu"** |
| Volunteer corps | **Ramadandu (1921)** – not Shanti Sena |
| After NCM | Joined the **Swaraj Party** (C.R. Das, Motilal Nehru) as an Andhra secretary |
- **Ramadandu** supported satyagraha and civil disobedience; it built huts and dug wells for the people who moved to Ramnagar.
!TRAP Ramadandu = Duggirala (Chirala-Perala); Shanti Sena = Parvataneni Veeraiah Chowdary (Pedanandipadu).
#### 2.2 Chirala-Perala Movement, 1921
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Place | Chirala and Perala, **Bapatla taluk, Guntur district**; weavers and small farmers (~15,000 people) |
| Cause | Villages made a **municipality** (proposed Nov 1919, formed **Jan 1920**); yearly tax rose from **Rs 4,000 to Rs 40,000** |
| Exodus | On Gandhi's advice, midnight of **25 April 1921**: **13,572 of 15,326** residents left |
| New camp | **"Ramnagar" (Ramanagaram)** outside municipal limits |
| Self-rule | Parallel all-caste assembly and arbitration court; lasted about **11 months** |
| Arrest | Speech at the **Andhra Mahasabha, Berhampur (Sept 1921)** → two-month ban on speaking → broke it → arrested **1 October 1921**; tried by **Rutherford at Srikakulam**; jailed **1 year** |
| Failure | Lack of funds and local Congress rivalry |
| Outcome | Municipality **abolished in 1938** by **Bezawada Gopala Reddy**, Local Self-Government Minister, **Rajaji ministry** |
- Book: ***Chirala Perala Vanavasam*** – **Pisapati Narayana Sastri**.
- 12 women jailed; **first woman jailed: Ravuri Alivelu Mangatayaramma**; **Duvvuri Subbamma ("Desabandhavi")** also arrested, but was not the first.
### 3. NO-TAX MOVEMENTS OF GUNTUR
#### 3.1 Palnadu Pullari (Grass Tax) Movement, 1921-22
- Region: **Palnadu (Guntur)** villages – Macherla, Veldurthi, Sirigiripadu, **Minchalapadu**.
- Tax refused: **pullari** = grazing (grass) tax on forest land.
- Leader: **Kanneganti Hanumanthu**, killed in police firing at **Minchalapadu**; backed by Unnava Lakshminarayana.
#### 3.2 Pedanandipadu No-Tax Campaign, 1922
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Place | **Pedanandipadu firka, Bapatla taluka, Guntur district** |
| Date | **January-February 1922** (when the first land-revenue instalment fell due) |
| Tax refused | **Land revenue** |
| Leader | **Parvataneni Veeraiah Chowdary ("Andhra Shivaji")** with his **Shanti Sena** |
| Shanti Sena | Decided by **Guntur DCC, Dec 1921** |
| Resignations | **18 of 19** village officers in the firka (29 in Guntur district), led by Chandavolu karanam **Machiraju Ramamurthy** |
| Backed it | **Konda Venkatappayya**, **Gollapudi Sitarama Sastri** |
| Urged restraint | **Nyapathi Subbarao** |
| Inquiry committee | T. Prakasam, Kasinathuni Nageswara Rao, Dantu Narayana Raju – praised it, advised waiting for Bardoli |
| Withdrawal | **11 Feb 1922** – Gandhi's message to Konda Venkatappayya reached the Guntur DCC → suspended |
#### 3.3 Government Response
- Revenue Board member **L.T. Harris** blamed the Congress and was sent to Guntur.
- **Rutherford** was deployed; his plan to **ban the Shanti Sena was rejected by the Madras Government**.
- The government declined to accept the resignations of village officers; Gandhi's halt ended the campaign at its peak.

### 5. AGENCY TRIBES AND GRIEVANCES
| Grievance | Effect on tribals |
|---|---|
| **Madras Forest Act, 1882** | Reserved forests cut tribal rights to **podu** (shifting cultivation), minor forest produce and grazing |
| **Begar** | Forced **unpaid** coolie labour for road building (not voluntary paid labour) |
| **Muttadari system** | Extortion and forced labour |
| **Tahsildar Bastian** | Local Tahsildar whose excesses were the spark |
- **Permanent Settlement was NOT a cause.**
- **Endu Padal** wanted to be a muttadar but failed (was NOT a muttadar of Pedda Valasa).
!KEY The Rampa Rebellion of Alluri was fought against the Madras Forest Act, 1882.
### 6. EARLIER RAMPA REBELLIONS (19TH CENTURY)
#### 6.1 Rampa (1879-80) vs Rekapalle (1879)
| | Rampa (1879-80) | Rekapalle (1879) |
|---|---|---|
| Place | Rampachodavaram agency, Godavari district | **Bhadrachalam taluk**, Godavari district (taken by the British from the Nizam; joined Madras Presidency 1874) |
| Cause | Munsubdar **Madhava Bhupati** (made Munsubdar 1848) exploiting tribals and eroding the muttadars | **Axe tax** on podu raised from **4 annas to 4-12 annas** in a famine; forest limits on podu |
| Leaders | **Dwarabandala Chandrayya**, Pulikanta Sambaiah, Karu Tammanna Dora, Ambul Reddy | **Ambul Reddy** |
| Action | Chandrayya burnt Addateegala police station | 10 July 1879: Vaddagudem police station attacked |
| End | **Sullivan** removed Madhava Bhupati; crushed **Nov 1880** | Crushed Sept 1879 |
- **1879 = "the year of the Rampa Revolution"** (APPSC 2011).
- **Ambul Reddy** is linked with both the Rampa and Rekapalle revolts (APPSC 2017).
!TRAP Alluri did NOT lead the 1879-80 revolt – he was born in 1897.

### 8. RAMPA / MANYAM REBELLION, 1922-24
#### 8.1 Nature and Span
- **January 1922 – May 1924** (about 2 years 5 months), Agency tracts of **East Godavari and Visakhapatnam** (**Gudem hills**).
- Armed tribal guerrilla war, outside the Congress and non-violence, yet it worried the British more than the NCM in that region.
- Method: **raided police stations** to seize British arms and used them against the British.
#### 8.2 Chronology
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **22 Aug 1922** | **Chintapalli** – the **first police station attacked**; arms seized |
| Aug 1922 | **Krishnadevipeta** and **Rajavommangi** stations raided |
| 24 Sept 1922 | **Damanapalli ghat**: officers **Scott Coward and Heiter** killed |
| Sept 1922 | **Panjerighat (Damanghat)** – direct battle with British forces (APPSC 2012) |
| 6 Dec 1922 | **Peddagaddapalem** – first defeat, by the **Malabar Special Police**; Raju wounded at **Lingapuram** |
| 17 Sept 1923 | Gam Mallu Dora arrested |
| 7 May 1924 | Raju shot at Koyyuru |
| 7 June 1924 | Gam Gantam Dora killed – end |
- **Assam Rifles** and the Malabar Special Police were brought in to crush it.
!PAIR Chintapalli | First police station | 22 Aug 1922 || Panjerighat | Direct battle | Sept 1922
#### 8.3 British Officers
| Officer | Post / part |
|---|---|
| **Rutherford** | Special Commissioner to crush the revolt; exiled **55** helpers, warrants for **183** |
| **Goodall** | Major in whose presence Raju was shot |
| **Keirns** | Arrested Mallu Dora |
| Scott Coward, Heiter | Officers killed at Damanapalli ghat |
!TRAP Rutherford appears thrice: Rampa Special Commissioner, Duggirala's trial at Srikakulam, Pedanandipadu.
#### 8.4 Latest Developments (AP Angle)
- Agency district renamed **Alluri Sitarama Raju district**.
- **30-ft bronze statue** unveiled by the PM at **Bhimavaram** for his **125th birth anniversary** (Azadi Ka Amrit Mahotsav).
- **23 Jul 2026**: **Alluri Sitarama Raju International Airport, Bhogapuram (Vizianagaram)**; the CM invited the PM to inaugurate it on 1 August 2026.

### 10. WOMEN IN THE ANDHRA FREEDOM STRUGGLE
- **Maganti Annapurnamma**: **first Telugu woman to give all her ornaments to the Tilak Swaraj Fund** (and burnt foreign goods).
- **Durgabai** at Kakinada (April 1921): collected Rs 5,000 for Congress and translated Gandhi's speech; founded the **Andhra Mahila Sabha (Madras, 1938** – APPSC key).
- **"Desasevikalu"**: women's group within the Congress pledged to non-violent reform and Swaraj propaganda.
- **Yamini Purna Tilakam**: Guntur hartals.
- Chirala-Perala: first woman jailed **Ravuri Alivelu Mangatayaramma**.
