## U0696 | ACADEMIES AND PROMOTION BODIES - INSA, IASC, NASI, VIGYAN PRASAR, NIF
### 1. SCIENCE ACADEMIES AND ISCA
| Body | Year | Founder / origin | HQ |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NASI** | **1930** | **Meghnad Saha** (as UP Academy of Sciences); **oldest** | **Prayagraj** |
| **IASc** | **1934** | **C.V. Raman** | **Bengaluru** |
| **INSA** | **1935** | As National Institute of Sciences of India; renamed **1970** | **New Delhi** |
| **ISCA** | **1914** | J.L. Simonsen, P.S. MacMahon | **Kolkata** (not New Delhi) |
!KEY Order: NASI (1930) → IASc (1934) → INSA (1935). Raman = IASc; Saha = NASI.

### 3. VIGYAN PRASAR AND ITS PLATFORMS
- **Vigyan Prasar**: autonomous body of **DST**, set up **1989**, New Delhi - NOT under HRD/Education.
| Platform | Fact |
|---|---|
| **DD Science** | Daily one-hour science slot on DD National |
| **India Science** | Internet (OTT) channel run by Vigyan Prasar (not NCSM) |
| **Vigyan Dhara** | DST umbrella scheme merging 3 schemes: capacity building; R&D; innovation and technology deployment (Aug 2024) |
- DD Science and India Science launched together **15 January 2019**.

### 6. NATIONAL INNOVATION FOUNDATION
- Origin: **Honey Bee Network** (Prof. **Anil K. Gupta**, SRISTI, IIM Ahmedabad).
- **NIF-India, March 2000**: autonomous body of **DST**, Gandhinagar.
- Mandate: scout, support, spread **grassroots innovations** and traditional knowledge - not advanced research with foreign institutions.
- Competitions: Biennial Grassroots Innovation Awards, **IGNITE**, **Festival of Innovation (FOIN)** at Rashtrapati Bhavan.
### 7. SCIENCE DAYS AND IISF
| Day | Date | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| **National Science Day** | **28 February** | **Raman Effect** (1928) |
| **National Technology Day** | **11 May** | **Pokhran-II** (1998); declared 1999 |
| **National Mathematics Day** | **22 December** | **Ramanujan's** birth; declared 2012 |
- Science Day 2026 theme: "Women in Science: Catalyzing Viksit Bharat".
- **IISF**: begun **2015**, New Delhi; MoST + MoES + **Vijnana Bharati**. 11th IISF: **Panchkula**, 6-9 Dec 2025, "Vigyan Se Samruddhi: for Aatmanirbhar Bharat".
