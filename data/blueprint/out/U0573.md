## U0573 | SOCIAL WELFARE PROGRAMMES IN AP – SC/ST/BC/MINORITY WELFARE, PENSIONS, HOUSING, WOMEN AND CHILD WELFARE
### 1. PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM IN AP
#### 1.1 PDS Data (SES 2025-26)
| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| **Fair Price Shops** | **29,796** |
| Monthly rice allocation | 2.36 lakh MT |
| Beneficiaries (Apr–Dec 2025) | 1.84 crore; ₹1,554.06 cr |
#### 1.2 Entitlements
- Free **Sortex fortified rice** (iron, B12, folic acid).
- **PHH = 5 kg per person**; **AAY = 35 kg per card**.
- **Ragi and jowar up to 3 kg free** (not ₹20/kg); wheat atta ₹20/kg.
- Sugar ₹13.50/kg (AAY), ₹17/kg (PHH).
!TRAP PHH is per PERSON (5 kg); AAY is per CARD (35 kg).
#### 1.3 Delivery Schemes
| Scheme | Feature |
|---|---|
| **e-PDS** | food grains to **BPL families** through fair price shops via **biometric authentication** and real-time stock tracking |
| **Mana Biyyam (2013)** | **fine rice at ₹1/kg** to BPL; max **20 kg per family** |
| Door delivery (2021) | **9,260 Mobile Dispensing Unit (MDU)** vehicles (₹539 cr); discontinued **1 May 2025** |
### 2. WOMEN'S SHG AND LIVELIHOOD PROGRAMMES
| Programme | Full form / purpose |
|---|---|
| **DWCRA** (1982-83) | **Development of Women and Children in Rural Areas**; **sub-scheme of IRDP**; raise living standard of rural women and children through self-help and income |
| **Velugu** (2000) | SHG-based poverty removal; merged with DWCRA as **Indira Kranthi Patham**, run by **SERP** |
| **Stree Nidhi** | timely, affordable credit to SHG members; up to **₹1 lakh at 11%**; ₹26,540 cr lent |
| **Pasupu Kumkuma** (TDP, 2019) | **₹10,000** per DWCRA woman |
| **YSR Aasara** | repaid SHG loans **in instalments** |
| **YSR Cheyutha** | yearly aid to SC/ST/BC/minority women (Navaratnalu) |
| **AP Rural Inclusive Growth Project** (2015) | **World Bank**-aided, run by SERP; **five components**; end poverty, empower women, raise farm income; **150 most backward mandals** |
- SERP mobilises **84.47 lakh** rural women; AP **1st in SHG-bank linkage**.
!TRAP **Integrated Rural Energy Programme** is NOT a women's income programme – it gives basic home energy (cooking, heating, lighting) with a renewable focus.
!KEY DWCRA + Velugu = Indira Kranthi Patham (SERP).

| Scheme | Launch / beneficiaries |

#### 4.1 Budget 2026-27

#### 4.2 Education Support
- **Jagananna Vidya Deevena**: **100% fee reimbursement** beyond Intermediate for **SC, ST, BC, Minorities, Kapu, EBC and Differently Abled** students; maintenance support (₹20,000).
- **Vasathi Deevena**: **hostel** costs.
- ₹4,900 cr Vidya Deevena dues pending (Jan 2026).
- Minorities: **Imams/Mouzams** get ₹5,000–10,000 honoraria (Mar 2026).
!TRAP **YSR Jagananna Badugu Vikasam** is NOT a scheme to encourage OBCs and was NOT launched on 26 October 2022.

|---|---|

### 6. WOMEN AND CHILD WELFARE
#### 6.1 AP Disha Bill, 2019
- **7-day investigation + 14-day trial + death penalty**.
- **Never got assent**; Cabinet **withdrew it in August 2026** on Centre's advice after **BNS and BNSS**.
#### 6.2 Schemes
| Scheme | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Mission Shakti** | **One Stop Centres** in all districts + **181 helpline** |
| **Stree Shakti** (15 Aug 2025) | **free bus travel** for women and **transgender persons**; 71 crore+ journeys; women's share of passengers **40% → 65%** |
| Divyang Shakti (Mar 2026) | extends free travel to persons with disabilities |
| **YSR Kalyanamasthu** | marriage aid for SC, ST, BC, disabled, construction-worker brides (₹1 lakh SC/ST; ₹50,000 BC) |
| **YSR Shaadi Tohfa** | marriage aid for **minority** brides (₹1 lakh) |
- Kalyanamasthu/Shaadi Tohfa: bride and groom must pass **SSC**; ages 18 and 21.
### 7. WELFARE DELIVERY AND PACKAGES
- **Navaratnalu** (YSRCP, 2019–24): nine welfare pillars delivered by **DBT** and village/ward **volunteers** who also coordinated ration delivery.
- Secretariats now under **Swarna Gramam and Swarna Ward** department.
- **Super Six** (TDP-JSP-BJP, 2024): **Talliki Vandanam** (₹15,000 per school child), **Annadata Sukhibhava** (₹20,000/yr farmers), **Deepam-2** (3 free LPG cylinders), **free bus travel for women**, **Aadabidda Nidhi** (₹1,500/month women), **₹3,000/month unemployment allowance** + 20 lakh jobs.
- Fiscal strain: welfare (**17 paise per rupee**) now matches debt service; shift from rights to cash transfers ("new welfarism").
- AP **MPI** fell **0.053 → 0.025** (SES 2025-26).
