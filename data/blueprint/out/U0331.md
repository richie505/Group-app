## U0331 | M.M. PUNCHHI COMMISSION (2007) – RECOMMENDATIONS AND THEIR STATUS
### 1. THE COMMISSION AT A GLANCE
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Formal name | **Commission on Centre-State Relations** |
| Set up by | **Government of India (UPA government)**, **April 2007** |
| Chairman | **Justice Madan Mohan Punchhi**, former **Chief Justice of India** |
| Members | Dhirendra Singh, Vinod Kumar Duggal, Dr N.R. Madhava Menon, Vijay Shanker |
| Report | **2010** (March), **7 volumes**, 273 recommendations |
- Dr **Amaresh Bagchi** was a member only from July 2007 to February 2008.
- **Mandate:** review Centre-State arrangements in the light of changes since the **Sarkaria Commission (1983-88)** – Panchayati Raj, economic reforms, coalition politics and **internal security**.
!TRAP "Punchhi Commission (2010)" is the report year; it was **appointed in 2007**.
### 2. CENTRE-STATE COMMISSIONS – COMPARISON
| Body | Year | Set up by | Remember |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rajamannar Committee** | **1969** (report 1971) | **Tamil Nadu government** | Recommended an Inter-State Council consulted on matters of national importance |
| **Sarkaria Commission** | **1983** (report 1988) | Centre | Permanent, regular **Inter-State Council (Art. 263)** |
| **Punchhi Commission** | **2007** (report 2010) | Centre (UPA) | Governor impeachment, localised emergency, ISC thrice a year |
!NUM Order to remember: **1969 → 1983 → 2007**.
### 3. RECOMMENDATIONS ON THE GOVERNOR
#### 3.1 Appointment and Tenure
- **Fixed tenure of 5 years**, to shield the Governor from Central pressure.
- Should be an **eminent person** not in **active politics, even at the local level**, for **a couple of years** before appointment.
#### 3.2 Removal – Article 156
- Delete the words **"during the pleasure of the President"** from **Article 156**.
- Removal only by **impeachment by the State Legislature**, on the lines of the **President's impeachment**.
#### 3.3 Universities and Bills
- Governors should **not be Chancellors** of universities or hold other **statutory roles**, so the office stays out of clashes with State governments.
- A constitutional amendment giving the Governor **six months** to decide on **Bills** passed by the Assembly.
!TRAP Periodic **"rotation of Governors"** and **"abolish the office of Governor"** are distractors – Punchhi recommended neither.
### 4. ARTICLES 355 AND 356 – EMERGENCY PROVISIONS
- **Amend Articles 355 and 356**; do not leave them as they are.
- **Article 356** to be used **sparingly**, only as a **last resort**, never in a **partisan** way.
- **"Localised emergency":** the Centre takes over a specific troubled area – a **district or part of a district** – for a **limited period**, instead of a whole State.
!TRAP "The Commission said there is no need to amend Articles 355 and 356" = WRONG (APPSC G2 2017).
### 5. DEPLOYMENT OF CENTRAL FORCES
- In **communal violence**, the Centre may deploy **central forces suo motu**, **without the State's prior consent**, for a **short period**; **consent obtained afterwards** (PYQ 2018).
- Origin of the idea: the **1st ARC** recommended deploying central armed forces "either on their request or otherwise".
### 6. INTER-STATE COUNCIL AND OTHER FORUMS
| Forum | Punchhi recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Inter-State Council (Art. 263)** | Meet **at least thrice a year**; a working forum for Centre-State and inter-state issues; agenda set in consultation with States |
| **National Integration Council** | Meet **at least once a year** |
| **PM and Chief Ministers** | Meet **regularly** |
| **National Water Resources Council** | Bigger role |
!TRAP NIC "once in two years" = WRONG (PYQ 2018). NIC **once a year**; ISC **thrice a year**.
### 7. LEGISLATIVE LISTS
- **Concurrent List:** before Parliament legislates, the Centre should reach **broad agreement with the States** through the **Inter-State Council**.
- **New Union List entry:** **environment, ecology and climate change**.
### 8. FINANCIAL RECOMMENDATIONS
- **Finance Commission and Planning Commission** to coordinate; their time periods **synchronised with the Five Year Plans**.
- **Cesses and surcharges** (which are outside the divisible pool) **to be limited**.
#### 8.1 Latest – 16th Finance Commission
- **Chairman: Dr Arvind Panagariya**; period **2026-31**.
- Vertical share **kept at 41%**; Centre accepted it in **Budget 2026-27 (2 Feb 2026)**; ₹1.4 lakh crore FC grants for 2026-27.
- Made **no recommendation on cesses and surcharges**; proposed a **"grand bargain"** (para 7.67) (2 Mar 2026).
- Lok Sabha (12 Feb 2026): FM said cesses and surcharges are for specific purposes and outside the divisible pool, which is about **81% of gross tax revenue** in 2025-26 – the same issue Punchhi raised.
- **AP angle:** the 16th FC's revised formula **raises the share of the five southern States, including Andhra Pradesh** (2 Feb 2026).

### 9. STATUS OF THE REPORT AND COURT CASES
- Report **referred to the Inter-State Council**; the **11th ISC meeting (16 July 2016)** – the first in about a decade – took up its recommendations.
- **Major recommendations not implemented.** **Article 156 unchanged** – Governors still hold office **during the pleasure of the President**, and still act as **Chancellors**.
| Case / opinion | Date | Held |
|---|---|---|

| **Presidential Reference opinion** | **20 Nov 2025** | Courts **cannot impose fixed timelines** or **"deemed assent"** |

1. **2007 vs 2010**: appointed vs report.
2. **Impeachment by State Legislature**, not rotation or abolition of Governors.
3. **Amend** 355 and 356 – not "no need to amend".
4. **NIC once a year**, not once in two years; **ISC thrice a year**.
5. Central forces **suo motu**, consent **afterwards**.
6. New Union List entry = **environment, ecology and climate change**.
7. Art. 156 is **still unchanged**; major recommendations **not implemented**.
8. **8 April 2025** fixed timelines vs **20 Nov 2025** Presidential Reference – no fixed timelines, no deemed assent.
9. Chairman was a former **CJI**, not a retired High Court judge.
