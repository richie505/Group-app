## U0781 | FOREST RIGHTS ACT 2006, CAMPA, COMPENSATORY AFFORESTATION, JFM, AGROFORESTRY
### 1. FORESTRY TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Afforestation** | New forest on denuded, degraded or unproductive land |
| **Reforestation** | Re-establishing cover in existing forest areas |
| **Social forestry** | Trees for community needs; term by **National Commission on Agriculture (1976)** |
| **Extension forestry** | Roadside, canal and rail strips |
| **Agroforestry** | Trees + crops + livestock on the same land |
- Components include agroforestry, extension forestry. Criticism: drifted into **commercial monocultures**; fix: mixed native species.
- **Miyawaki method**: dense, multi-layered mini-forests of native species in **urban areas**; by Japanese botanist **Akira Miyawaki** (1970s), "Pot Plantation Method".
- **Vanamahotsav**: **K.M. Munshi, 1950**; first week of July.
### 2. AGROFORESTRY
- **National Agroforestry Policy, Feb 2014**: India = **first country** with such a policy.
- **GROW report (NITI Aayog)**: **~8.65%** of India's area under agroforestry (APPSC 2024).
- **ICAR-CAFRI**: **Jhansi**, UP. Funding: **Sub-Mission on Agroforestry (SMAF)** under **PM-RKVY**.
- **TOFI**: MoEFCC-USAID programme.
### 3. COMPENSATORY AFFORESTATION AND CAMPA
- CA replaces "**land by land**, trees by trees" on **equal non-forest land**; Central PSUs / no non-forest land: **twice the area of degraded forest**.
- Agency pays **CA cost + NPV** before the project.
- Ad hoc CAMPA by SC; statutory via **Compensatory Afforestation Fund Act, 2016**: National and State funds, **non-lapsable, interest-bearing** public accounts.
- Split: **90% State : 10% National**. **National CAMPA** headed by **Union Environment Minister**; State CAMPAs do field work.
- Uses: regeneration, wildlife, catchment treatment, village relocation from PAs, human-wildlife conflict, forest fires; **not** private urban housing.
- **₹66,000+ crore** held (2024); **30-35%** used.
!TRAP CAMPA at both national and state levels = correct; "people's participation mandatory" under CAF Act = **wrong** (UPSC 2019).
#### 3.1 Rules: Land Banks and Green Credit
- **Forest (Conservation) Rules 2022**: **Accredited CA** (plantations raised in advance = land banks); **dropped forest-dwellers' consent** before diversion; disputed by **NCST**.
- **Van (S.E.S.) Rules 2023**: afforestation under the **Green Credit Programme** can be exchanged for CA.

### 5. JOINT FOREST MANAGEMENT
- Origin: **Arabari, West Bengal (1971-72)**; **NFP 1988**; **guidelines 1 June 1990**.
- AP JFM committees = **Vana Samrakshana Samithis (VSS)**; Tribal Affairs Ministry ordered withdrawal of community titles given to **1,669 VSS**.
### 6. FOREST RIGHTS ACT 2006
- Nodal: **Ministry of Tribal Affairs** (not Environment). Cut-off date: **13 December 2005**.
- Eligible: forest-dwelling **STs**, and **OTFDs** with **3 generations (75 years)** residence.
- Individual title: cultivated land, max **4 ha**; community rights include **community forest resource** management.
- **Gram Sabha** starts claims; District Level Committee finalises.
- **Critical wildlife habitat**: NP/sanctuary area needing inviolate protection.
#### 6.1 AP Angle
- AP (2022): **2,12,650 titles** (**2,10,828 individual, 1,822 community**), in **ITDA** areas.
- **Koya and Kondh** oppose **Polavaram** over land rights and rehabilitation.
