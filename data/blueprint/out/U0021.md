## U0021 | LATER VEDIC SOCIETY – VARNAS, ASHRAMAS, GOTRA, STATUS OF WOMEN
### 1. FOUR VARNAS AND CASTE
| Varna | Role |
|---|---|
| **Brahmana** | Priests, teachers; most powerful |
| **Kshatriya (Rajanya)** | Rulers, warriors |
| **Vaishya** | Farmers, traders, artisans; **tribute-payers** |
| **Shudra** | Service; no upanayana, no Vedas |
- First reference to four varnas: **Purusha Sukta, Rigveda 10th Mandala**.
- **Dvija** (twice-born) = upper three, entitled to upanayana.
- Varna became **birth-based and rigid** in the Later Vedic period; spread of **jatis** is post-Vedic (Gupta era).
- **Aitareya Brahmana**: Shudra = "servant of another", could be driven out or beaten anytime.
- Outside the varna order: **Vratyas, Nishadas**.
#### 1.1 Untouchability, Guilds, Money-lending
- **Chandala** = despised birth in the **Chhandogya Upanishad**; formal untouchability = **post-Vedic Dharmasutras / Dharmashastra phase** (early Christian era).
- First reference to **money-lending**: **Shatapatha Brahmana**.
- Vaishya guilds (gana) headed by a **shreshthin**; **Sarthavaha** (Srenipramukh) = head of trade guilds; rules = **Samay, Srenidharma**.
- AP: **Gautamiputra Satakarni** protected varnashrama dharma; anuloma offspring = **Ugra**, pratiloma = **Chandala**.
### 2. ASHRAMAS AND PURUSHARTHAS
| Ashrama | Duty |
|---|---|
| **Brahmacharya** | Vedic study |
| **Grihastha** | Household; daily **Pancha Mahayajnas** |
| **Vanaprastha** | Gradual withdrawal |
| **Sannyasa** | Complete detachment; **added last** |
- First three in **Chhandogya Upanishad**; all four first in **Jabala Upanishad**. Varna + ashrama = **Varnashrama Dharma**.
- Pancha Mahayajnas: **Brahma, Deva, Pitra, Bhuta, Atithi (Nri/Manushya)**; Bhuta = living beings; Nri = hospitality.
- **Purusharthas**: Dharma (duty), Artha (wealth), Kama (desire) = **trivarga** (Later Vedic-Sutra phase); **Moksha** (liberation) added later (Upanishadic).
### 3. GOTRA AND KINSHIP
- Gotra = literally **cow-pen** (Rigveda); lineage sense first in **Atharvaveda**; as descent from one male ancestor, set up in Later Vedic times.
- **Gotra exogamy**: no marriage within the same gotra, with caste endogamy and **sapinda** rules.
- **Kula** = family; **Vamsha** = lineage; **Kosa** = treasury (**odd one out**).
- **Irawati Karve**: *Kinship Organization in India* (1953); four kinship zones.
### 4. SAMSKARAS AND EDUCATION
- Samskaras: **16** (shodasha). **Upanayana** = sacred thread, boy's 8th year; full description in **Shatapatha Brahmana**; Shudras and women excluded.
| Varna | Upanayana metre |
|---|---|
| Brahmana | **Gayatri** |
| Kshatriya | **Trishtubh** |
| Vaishya | **Jagati** |
- **Gurukula**: pupil lived with the guru; education mostly for Dvija.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Acharya** | Initiated the pupil; taught whole Veda **free** |
| **Upadhyaya** | Taught Veda/Vedangas for a **livelihood** |
| **Adhvaryu** | Performed sacrificial acts |
| **Hotri / Udgatri** | Recited Rigveda / chanted Samaveda |
| **Purohita** | Chief priest |
### 5. MARRIAGE
| Form | Nature |
|---|---|
| **Brahma** | Daughter given to a Veda-learned man of good conduct |
| **Daiva** | Given to the officiating priest |
| **Arsha** | Groom gives a token **cow and bull** |
| **Prajapatya** | No dowry; couple jointly perform dharma |
| **Asura** | **Purchase** of bride |
| **Gandharva** | Mutual consent |
| **Rakshasa** | **Capture** |
| **Paishacha** | Seducing a sleeping/drunk girl; **lowest** |
- First four approved; 8 forms in Manu and Kautilya.
- **Anuloma** = higher-varna man, lower-varna woman; **Pratiloma** = reverse (disapproved).
### 6. STATUS OF WOMEN
- **Declined**: lost upanayana, limited education, barred from **Sabha** and public rites; child marriage and sati appear.
| Text | Statement |
|---|---|
| **Aitareya Brahmana** | Daughter = source of misery; son = protector |
| **Shatapatha Brahmana** | Wife is half her husband |
| **Brihadaranyaka Upanishad** | **Gargi** debated **Yajnavalkya** |
