## U0018 | EARLY VEDIC SOCIETY AND ECONOMY – PASTORALISM, GAVISHTI, VARNA ORIGINS, POSITION OF WOMEN
### 1. PASTORAL ECONOMY
- Economy **mainly pastoral**, agriculture secondary (mixed). **Cattle breeding** = main activity.
- **Cow** = **most valuable property**, **standard unit of value and exchange**; **Gau** named about **176 times**; a deity in some Suktas.
- Land not yet main wealth; horses and gold valued. Priests paid in cows, horses, gold.
- Animals kept: cattle, sheep, goats, horses, dogs, asses (buffalo not prominent). Known: lion, elephant, bear.
#### 1.1 Cow-derived terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Aghanya** | "**Not to be killed**" – the **cow** |
| **Gavishti** | "Search for cows" – **war** |
| **Gopati** | Lord of cattle – the chief |
| **Gomat** | Rich man (many cattle) |
| **Godhuli** | **Time** – dusk, when cattle return |
| **Gavyuti** | **Distance** |
| **Duhitri** | **Daughter** – "one who milks" |
!TRAP Aghanya = cow; Gavishti = war; Gavyuti = distance; Godhuli = time.
!NUM Jana 275 times vs Gau 176 times.
### 2. AGRICULTURE
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Yava** | **Barley** – chief Rigvedic grain |
| Vrihi | **Rice** – not named in the Rigveda; important in Later Vedic texts |
| Godhuma | **Wheat** – chief crop only in Later Vedic period |
| **Mudga** | **Mung bean** (green gram) |
| Ikshu | Sugarcane |
- Rice, wheat, cotton – **not main Rigvedic crops**.
!TRAP RAS 2021 key matched **Yava → millet** (barley not an option); standard meaning = barley. Same key: Vrihi = rice, Mudga = mung, Ikshu = sugarcane.
#### 2.1 Implements, land and irrigation
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Langala** | **Wooden ploughshare** (not iron) |
| **Sira** | Plough drawn by bulls |
| **Sita** | Furrow |
| Datra / Supra / Titau | Sickle / winnowing fan / sieve |
| Kshetra / Urvara / Karishu | Field / fertile land / manure |
| **Avata** | **Wells** |
| **Kulya** | **Canals** |
- Cattle-worked water lifts used.
!TRAP UPSC 2026: "well irrigation spread Rigvedic farming from river margins into the Punjab-Haryana plains" = WRONG.
### 3. CRAFTS AND OCCUPATIONS
| Craftsman | Term |
|---|---|
| Carpenter (chariots, boats) | **Takshan** |
| Metal worker / smith | **Karmara** |
| Goldsmith | **Hiranyakara** |
| Physician | **Bhishak** |
| Potter | Kulala |
| Weaver | Vaya (weaving largely by women) |
- **Ayas** = **copper or bronze**, not iron; **iron = krishna ayas** (later).
- Hymn: "**I am a poet, my father a physician, my mother a grinder of corn**" → **occupational mobility**; occupations not birth-based.
### 4. EXCHANGE, TRADE AND TAX
- Trade limited, by **barter**; **no coinage** in the Rig Vedic age.
- **Nishka**: Rigveda = **gold neck ornament** ('**Nishka-Griva**'), used in exchange; later = unit of value / **gold coin**.
- **Panis**: **traders**, rich in cattle, greedy (hid cattle), non-Aryan (MPPSC 2019).
| Phase | Tax |
|---|---|
| Rig Vedic | **Bali** = **voluntary** gift to the rajan, plus war loot; no regular tax or collector |
| Later Vedic | Bali **compulsory** + **bhaga** (1/6 of produce) + **shulka**; collector = **Bhagadugha** |
!TRAP Nishka was not yet a coin in the Rigveda.
- 'Stupa' word first in the Rigveda = "tree stem"; stupa raised by **Varuna** above the forest.
### 5. FAMILY AND SOCIAL UNITS
- Family: **patriarchal, patrilineal, joint**; sons preferred, daughters treated kindly once born.
- Compare: in the **Satavahana** period too, family and monarchy were patriarchal (kingship father to son) and women had a respectable position.
- Ascending order: **Kula** (family) → **Grama** (village) → **Vis** (clan, vispati) → **Jana** (tribe).
- **Janapada** never mentioned in the Rigveda.
- Slavery existed (Dasa/Dasyu captives; slave women given to priests) – not the base of the economy.
### 6. VARNA ORIGINS
| FIRST | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Purusha Sukta** | **Rigveda, Mandala X (10.90)**, a late addition; first statement of the **four-fold varna** |
| Word **varna** | First in the **Rigveda** |
| Word **Shudra** | First in the **10th Mandala** |
| **Gotra** (lineage) | First in the **Atharvaveda**; Rigveda word = **cow-pen** |
- Purusha Sukta: **Brahmana – mouth**, **Kshatriya – arms**, **Vaishya – thighs**, **Shudra – feet**.
- **Vaishya** grew from **vis** (mass of tribespeople).
- **Arya varna vs Dasa varna**: cultural, linguistic (partly colour) distinction; **not rigid caste**. Broad groups: priests, warriors, common people.
- **No untouchability**; gotra exogamy = Later Vedic.
### 7. POSITION OF WOMEN
- Status **relatively high**: education, composed hymns, joined sacrifices, attended **Sabha and Vidatha**; could own **cattle** (no proof of land ownership).
- Marriage: mostly **monogamous**; **adult marriage**, choice; **no child marriage**; widow remarriage.
- **Niyoga** = childless widow's union with her **husband's brother** (levirate).
| Woman | Period |
|---|---|
| **Apala, Ghosha, Lopamudra, Vishvavara** | **Rigvedic** seers (rishika) |
| **Gargi** | Later Vedic / Upanishadic; dialogue with **Yajnavalkya** (Brihadaranyaka Upanishad) |
| Maitreyi | Later women scholar |
- **Lopamudra**: Brahmavadini, wife of sage **Agastya**. Brahmavadini = women who explained Vedic knowledge.
- Later Vedic decline: barred from **Upanayana**, rituals, assemblies.
!TRAP Gargi is **not** Rigvedic (BPSC 2025 odd-one-out).
### 8. FOOD, DRINK AND PASTIMES
- **Soma** = sacred drink (Mandala IX); **sura** = fermented drink, few Rigvedic references.
- Pastimes: **chariot racing**, **dicing** (gambler's hymn, **Rigveda X.34**), music, dance, hunting.
### 9. RIG VEDIC RELIGION
- **Naturalistic polytheism**; **Henotheism / Kathenotheism** – each god supreme in turn.
- **No temples or idols**; hymns and open-air yajnas for **cattle, sons, victory**.
- **Rita** = cosmic and moral order (natural law), upheld by **Varuna** (**Ritasyagopa**).
| Deity | Role | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Indra** | War, rain, thunder | **Purandara** (destroyer of forts); **~250 hymns**, most named |
| **Agni** | Fire; link between gods and men | **~200 hymns**; second |
| **Varuna** | Water, Rita | Third; ethically highest |
| **Soma** | Plants, soma drink | Whole **Mandala IX** |
| **Dyaus** | Sky father | = Greek **Zeus**, Roman **Jupiter** |
| **Mitra** | Solar, ally of Varuna | At **Boghazkoi** with Indra, Varuna, Nasatyas |
| **Aditi** | **Mother of the gods** | Female; also **Usha** (dawn) |
| Maruts / Rudra / Parjanya | Storms / destruction and healing / rain | |
| Prithvi / Aranyani / Pushan | Earth / forest / cattle and roads | |
| **Vishnu** | Minor | **3 hymns** |
!TRAP "Prithvi was mother of all gods" is wrong – that is **Aditi**.
