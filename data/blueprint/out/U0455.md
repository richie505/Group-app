## U0455 | OVERALL ACHIEVEMENTS AND FAILURES OF PLANNING IN INDIA; END OF THE PLAN ERA (2017)
### 1. PLAN ERA AND GROWTH RECORD
- **First Plan** launched **1 April 1951**; focus **agriculture** and irrigation. Plan system ran **1951-2017**.
- Plans I-XI averaged **~4.5%** growth vs **~1%** before Independence; agriculture **2-3%** (vs 0.3%); industry **6-8%**.
### 2. THIRD PLAN FAILURE AND PLAN HOLIDAY
- Third Plan grew only **2.2%** vs **5.6%** target.
- Causes: **Sino-Indian War (1962)**, **Indo-Pak War (1965)**, **1965-66 droughts**.
- **Plan Holiday: 1966-69** – only Annual Plans.
### 3. FOURTH PLAN – GADGIL STRATEGY
- **Growth with stability** + progress towards **self-reliance**.
- Targets: agriculture **5%** a year; industry 8-10%; **exports ~7% a year**.
- Result: only **3.3%** vs **5.7%** (droughts, 1971 war); **14 banks nationalised (1969)**.
- State plan aid: **Gadgil formula (1969)** → Gadgil-Mukherjee (1991); **Special Category** 90% grant : 10% loan, **General Category** 30 : 70.
### 4. ACHIEVEMENTS VS FAILURES
| Achievements | Failures |
|---|---|
| Rise in NDP, **savings, investment**, per-capita income | High **rural poverty** |
| Self-sufficiency in basic, capital, consumer-goods industries | Rising **unemployment** – root cause of poverty |
| **Foodgrain self-sufficiency**, food security: Green Revolution 1966-67, **FCI (1965)** | **Income inequality** widened after reforms |
| Infrastructure – transport, irrigation, telecom | **Land reforms** poorly implemented; unequal land ownership |
| Education, **scientific manpower**, IT, space; IITs | **Regional disparities** persist |
!KEY **Chief drawback** of Indian planning = **implementation failure**.
!TRAP Regional disparities = a **failure**, not an achievement.
### 5. CRITICS OF PLANNING
| Book | Author |
|---|---|
| **Planning and the Poor** (1974) | **B.S. Minhas** |
| **India: Planning for Industrialization** (1970) | **Padma Desai and Jagdish Bhagwati** |
- **Padma Desai** – critic of planning; **first Asian woman** with a Harvard Economics PhD (1960).
### 6. PLANNING AFTER 1991
- After the Seventh Plan, public outlay cut; private investment rose.
- **"Directed" → "indicative"**: plan shows **priority sectors**; private sector invests.
- ★ Plans kept after 1991 because they give a **long-term perspective** in a market economy.
!TRAP Planning is **not a constitutional requirement**.
### 7. END OF THE PLAN ERA
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Last plan | **Twelfth (2012-17)**, ended **31 March 2017** |
| Planning Commission | Dissolved **2014**; last Deputy Chairman **M.S. Ahluwalia** |
| Successor | **NITI Aayog**, **1 Jan 2015**, by government resolution |
| NDC (1952) | Inactive; functions to **NITI Governing Council**; formal abolition pending |
- Abolition ended **discretionary plan grants**; NITI has **no financial powers** – Governing Council coordinates, does not redistribute.
- 11th NITI Governing Council (19 Jun 2026): PM urged states to estimate **district-level GDP**.
- AP: three economic regions – Visakhapatnam, Amaravati (**9 districts**, 44,962 sq km, ~35.15% of GSDP), Tirupati.
