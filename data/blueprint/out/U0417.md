## U0417 | TRANSGENDER PERSONS – NALSA JUDGMENT, 2019 ACT; PROVISIONS FOR THE ELDERLY
### 1. NALSA v. UNION OF INDIA (2014)
- Delivered **15 April 2014** by Justices **K.S. Radhakrishnan** and **A.K. Sikri**.
- Recognised transgender persons as the **"third gender"**.
- **Gender identity is self-determined** – not by biological examination or State verification.
#### 1.1 Directions
- Treat as **SEBC** (socially and educationally backward classes) for reservation in education and jobs → a **vertical, OBC-type** quota.
- Separate **public toilets**, health facilities and welfare schemes.
!TRAP NALSA gave a **vertical** quota; the community demands **horizontal** reservation.

### 4. TRANSGENDER PERSONS (PROTECTION OF RIGHTS) ACT, 2019
#### 4.1 Definition and Identity
- **Definition:** person whose gender does not match that assigned at birth; includes **trans men/women**, **intersex variations**, **genderqueer** and socio-cultural identities (**kinner, hijra, aravani, jogta**).
- Objects cited NALSA and the right to **self-perceived gender identity**.
- Administered by **MoSJE**.
- **Certificate of identity:** issued by the **District Magistrate**; a **revised certificate after surgery**.
#### 4.2 Offences and Criticism
- Forced/bonded labour, denial of public places, removal from household, abuse = **6 months to 2 years + fine**.
- Criticism: **lighter penalties** than for similar crimes against women; **DM gatekeeping** of identity; **no reservation**.
#### 4.3 2019 Act vs 2026 Amendment
| Aspect | 2019 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Self-perceived (Objects) | **Self-perceived excluded**; limited to socio-cultural identities, intersex/congenital variations, those forced to present as transgender |
| Section 4(2) | Self-determination | **Omitted** |
| Certificate | DM alone | DM on a **medical board's** recommendation |
| Offences | 6 months-2 years + fine | Adds **kidnapping**, forcing a person to present as transgender |
- 2026 Act challenged in SC by **Laxmi Narayan Tripathi** and **Zainab Javid Patel** as contrary to NALSA; Aug 2026: Centre assured older ID cards remain valid.
### 5. RULES 2020 AND NATIONAL COUNCIL
- **Rules 2020:** application for certificate through the **National Portal**.
- **National Council for Transgender Persons (NCTP):** **statutory** body (Section 16, 2019 Act), constituted **21 August 2020**.
- **Chairperson:** Union **Minister for Social Justice and Empowerment** (ex officio); members from the community, five states, 10 central departments, NHRC, NCW; non-official term **3 years**.
- Role: **advises and monitors** policy only.
- **NHRC Advisory 2.0** (May 2026): separate **intersex, trans men, trans women** categories in **Census 2027**; admission by self-identified gender; ban on non-consensual intersex surgery on children; more Garima Grehs.
### 6. SMILE, GARIMA GREH AND NATIONAL PORTAL
- **SMILE** (Support for Marginalized Individuals for Livelihood and Enterprise): **2022**, **MoSJE**, umbrella **Central Sector** scheme; outlay **Rs 365 crore**; goal **"Bhiksha Vritti Mukt Bharat"** (begging-free India); about 12,710 rehabilitated.
| Component | Content |
|---|---|
| Comprehensive Rehabilitation for Welfare of Transgender Persons | Scholarship Rs 13,500/year (Class IX-PG); skilling via PM-DAKSH; PM-JAY health package; Transgender Protection Cells |
| **Garima Greh** | **Shelter homes** – **23 in 17** States/UTs (ES 2025-26) |
- **National Portal for Transgender Persons:** online certificate and ID card; certificate mandatory for benefits.

### 9. DATA AND AP
- **Census 2011:** about **4.88 lakh** transgender persons; highest **Uttar Pradesh**, then **undivided AP**.
- AP: **3,125** certificates via the National Portal; council for transgender welfare; State Transgender Policy 2025.
- **NTR Bharosa Pension:** **Rs 4,000/month** for elderly (60+), widows and **transgender persons**.
- AP senior citizens: about 50 lakh (Census 2011).
### 10. PROVISIONS FOR THE ELDERLY
#### 10.1 Constitutional Basis
- NSAP rests on **Articles 41 and 42**.
- **"Invalidity and old age pensions"** = **Concurrent List Entry 24** (not State List Entry 9).
#### 10.2 Senior Citizens Act 2007 and Policies
- Children (biological, adopted, step) and legal heirs must maintain parents; **Maintenance Tribunal** (sub-division, RDO) and **Appellate Tribunal** (district, Collector); max **Rs 10,000/month**; 90 days.
- Earlier law: **Himachal Pradesh, 2001**; **2019 Amendment Bill** (remove cap) lapsed.
- **NPOP 1999** (older person = 60+); **NPSC 2011** (elderly women, ageing in place).
#### 10.3 Schemes
- **Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana:** physical aids to BPL seniors; launched **1 April 2017, Nellore**.
- **IGNOAPS** (NSAP, MoRD); **AVYAY** (2021, umbrella of 4, MoSJE); **SACRED portal** (re-employment); **Elder Line 14567**.
