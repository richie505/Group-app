#### 1.1 GPS basics
- **GPS = Global Positioning System**; begun **1973**, first satellite 1978.
- **24 satellites in 6 orbits** at **20,200 km**; each circles Earth **twice a day**; about 30 now (Mar 2026).
- Signals **L1** (1,575.42 MHz) + **L2**.
- **1 microsecond** clock error ≈ **300 m** position error.
### 2. NAVIC – CONSTELLATION AND SERVICES
- **NavIC = Navigation with Indian Constellation**; formerly **IRNSS**; fully operational **2018**.
- Genesis: US denied GPS data in the **1999 Kargil War**.
- **7 satellites: 3 GEO + 4 inclined GSO** (at **29°**).
- Coverage: India + **1,500 km** beyond its borders.
- Bands: **L5 and S**; NVS satellites add civilian **L1**.
| Service | Users | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Positioning Service (SPS) | Civilians | Better than **20 m** |
| Restricted Service (RS) | Military/authorised | Encrypted |
- Nodal body for NavIC and GAGAN: **Satellite Navigation Programme Office**, ISRO.
!TRAP GPS = 24 satellites, 6 orbits, global; NavIC = 7, regional, L5 + S (L1/L2 are GPS).
### 3. IRNSS AND NVS SATELLITES
- **IRNSS-1A**: first dedicated navigation satellite, **PSLV-C22, 1 Jul 2013**; IRNSS-1 series on **PSLV**.
- **11** navigation satellites since 2013, all from **Sriharikota**.
| Satellite | Vehicle / date | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **NVS-01** (2nd generation) | **GSLV-F12**, May 2023 | First **indigenous rubidium clock** |
| **NVS-02** | **GSLV-F15**, Jan 2025 | **100th Sriharikota launch**; valve glitch → wrong orbit |
#### 3.1 Clock troubles
- First-generation rubidium clocks from Swiss firm **SpectraTime** failed → indigenous **SAC** clocks (2018).
- **13 Mar 2026**: **IRNSS-1F** last clock failed → only **3** PNT satellites (**1B, 1I, NVS-01**), below the **4** needed.
### 4. APPLICATIONS AND ADOPTION
- **Elena Geo Systems**: India's **first NavIC-based device**, for precise directions on infrastructure projects.
- **Qualcomm Snapdragon** chipsets support NavIC.
- **MoRTH AIS-140**: NavIC trackers compulsory in **public transport vehicles** (not all private cars).
- Timing (Indian Standard Time, power, telecom, banking), surveying, **fleet management**, precision farming, disaster management, marine/aerial navigation.
- Reconfigured **IRNSS-1A** sends cyclone, tsunami, high-wave alerts to **fishermen**; useful on AP's long coast.
- Strategic autonomy: forces need not rely on foreign systems in conflict.
### 5. GAGAN AND OTHER SBAS
- **GAGAN = GPS Aided GEO Augmented Navigation**; by **ISRO + Airports Authority of India** (not DRDO).
- **SBAS**: ground stations compute live corrections for orbit, clock, **ionospheric delay** → better **accuracy, integrity, availability**.
- Payloads on **GSAT-8, GSAT-10, GSAT-15**; GSAT-10 launched 29 Sep 2012, Ariane-5, Guiana.
- **DGCA certified** it for aviation approach guidance in **2015**.
| SBAS | Region |
|---|---|
| **WAAS** | USA |
| **EGNOS** | Europe |
| **MSAS** | Japan |
| **GAGAN** | India |
!PAIR GAGAN | augments GPS | aviation || NavIC | independent | regional
### 6. LIMITATIONS
- **Regional** coverage only.
- Thin **receiver** ecosystem: L5/S rarely in phone chips; L1 on NVS helps.
- Clock failures + NVS-02 failure → NavIC could not fully replace GPS (Mar 2026).
