## U0059 | SANGAM AGE – THE THREE SANGAMS, SOURCES; TOLKAPPIYAM, ETTUTHOGAI, PATTUPATTU, PATHINENKILKANAKKU
### 1. THE THREE SANGAMS
- **Sangam** = "Conference of Poets" / "Forum of Scholars"; all three under **Pandya** patronage (not Chola).
- Tradition comes from the preface of **Iraiyanar Agapporul**.
| Sangam | Place | Presiding scholar | Survives |
|---|---|---|---|
| First | **Then Madurai** (submerged) | Agastya | Nothing |
| Second | **Kapatapuram** (submerged) | Tolkappiyar (also linked to Agastya) | **Only Tolkappiyam** |
| Third | **Madurai** | Nakkirar | **Most of the extant corpus** |
!TRAP First and Third Sangams were both at Madurai; Second at Kapatapuram.
### 2. CHRONOLOGY
- Accepted span: **c. 300 BCE – 300 CE**; **Tamil-Brahmi** dated to the 3rd c. BCE supports the early limit.
- The three-Sangam account (two submerged cities) is **traditional, not historical**.
- Tirukkural, twin epics and Pathinenkilkanakku are **post-Sangam**.
### 3. LITERATURE – LANGUAGE AND THEMES
- Language: **old Tamil**; about **2,400 poems** by several hundred poets, including **women**.
| Type | Theme |
|---|---|
| **Aham** (interior) | Love, personal emotion, nature |
| **Puram** (exterior) | War, valour, royal deeds, ethics |
- Rich in **material culture** and **warrior ethic**; poets knew the **varna** classification, but society was not rigidly stratified (UPSC 2022). **Vellalars** = rich peasants, landowners, the fourth caste.
- Sangam literature is **silent on the trade routes** of ancient India.
!TRAP "Sangam literature is devoid of material culture" is FALSE.
### 4. ANTHOLOGIES
| Group | Count | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Ettuthogai** | **8** anthologies | Short poems; **Padirruppattu** (Chera kings), Purananuru, Akananuru |
| **Pattupattu** | **10** idylls | Long poems; **Pattinappalai** (Puhar, **Karikala**), Maduraikkanji, Mullaippattu, Tirumurugarruppadai, Nedunalvadai, Kurinjippattu |
| **Pathinenkilkanakku** | **18** minor | Ethics; includes **Tirukkural**; kept Aham/Puram themes but post-Sangam |
- **Melkanakku** = Ettuthogai + Pattupattu = **18 major** works; both divided into aham and puram.
!PAIR Melkanakku | 18 major | Sangam proper || Pathinenkilkanakku | 18 minor | post-Sangam ethics
### 5. TAMIL WORKS – AUTHORS
| Work | Author | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| **Tolkappiyam** | **Tolkappiyar** (disciple of Agastya) | **Oldest extant Tamil grammar**; formula style; 3 parts **Ezhuttu, Col, Porul** |
| **Tirukkural** | **Tiruvalluvar** | **1,330 couplets**; **Aram, Porul, Inbam** |
| **Silappadikaram** | **Ilango Adigal** | **First Tamil epic**; Kannagi–Kovalan (anklet) |
| **Manimekalai** | **Sittalai Sattanar** | **Buddhist** epic, sequel |
| **Sivaga Sindamani** | **Tiruttakkadevar** (Jain ascetic) | **Jain** epic of prince Jivaka; called **Mana Nool** (Book of Marriages) |
- Kural is also called **Laghuveda** (UPPSC 2013), **Tamil Veda**, **Ulaga Podhumarai**.
- UPPSC 2014 match: Kural = philosophy; Tolkappiyam = grammar; Silappadikaram = love story; Manimekalai = merchant story.
### 6. TWIN EPICS
- **Ilango Adigal** = younger brother of Chera **Senguttuvan**; grandson of Chola **Karikala**.
- Silappadikaram: merchant **Kovalan**, wife **Kannagi**, dancer **Madhavi**; Kovalan executed by the Pandya king → Kannagi burns **Madurai**.
- Manimekalai: daughter of Kovalan and Madhavi becomes a **Buddhist nun**. Both epics are **post-Sangam**.
### 7. FOREIGN AND INSCRIPTIONAL SOURCES
| Source | Records |
|---|---|
| **Periplus** (anonymous Greek) | Ports, **Muziris** pepper, Arikamedu |
| **Pliny**, Natural History | Spices (Chera), pearls (Pandya), muslin (Chola); Roman gold drain |
| **Ptolemy**, Geography | Classical account |
| **Ashokan RE II** | Cheras, Cholas, Pandyas, **Satiyaputras, Keralaputras** as southern neighbours |
| **Hathigumpha** (Kharavela) | Broke a **Tamil confederacy** that had existed for **113 years** |
- Satavahanas used **Prakrit**; Sangam literature used Tamil.
### 8. ARCHAEOLOGICAL SOURCES
| Site | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Arikamedu** (near Puducherry) | Indo-Roman trading settlement |
| **Kaveripattinam (Puhar)** | Chola port, **Yavana** settlements (Pattinappalai) |
| **Keezhadi** | Most important Sangam urban dig; matches Gangetic **Second Urbanisation** |
| **Pugalur** (1st c. CE) | **Three generations of Chera** rulers |
- **Feb 2026:** ~30 Indian, mostly Tamil-Brahmi, inscriptions (1st–3rd c. CE) found in the **Theban tombs of Egypt**.
