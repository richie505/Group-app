## U0512 | SEZs - POLICY, PERFORMANCE AND CRITICISM; INDUSTRIAL CORRIDORS (DMIC, CBIC, AKIC) AND NICDC
### 1. EPZs AND FREE TRADE ZONES
- **Kandla** (Gulf of Kutch, Gujarat), **1965**: **India's and Asia's first EPZ**; also India's first Free Trade Zone; later Kandla SEZ.
- **Surat**, **1997**: **first private-sector EPZ**; turned into an SEZ on **1 November 2000**.
- **Free trade zone**: area where goods are landed, stored, processed and re-exported **without customs duty**; set up to **promote export industries**.
| EPZ | Place |
|---|---|
| Kandla | Gujarat |
| **Falta** | **West Bengal** (UPPSC 2023) |
| **SEEPZ** | **Santacruz, Mumbai** |
| MEPZ | Chennai |
| Others | Noida, Cochin, Visakhapatnam |
!TRAP Kandla was keyed as the city "without an SEZ" (UPPCS 2001) because it was known for its EPZ. From 2006, old EPZs and EOUs could convert into SEZs (e.g. Kandla).
### 2. SEZ POLICY EVOLUTION
| Date | Milestone |
|---|---|
| **1 April 2000** | SEZ policy announced in **EXIM Policy 2000-01** |
| **May 2005** | SEZ Act passed by Parliament |
| **23 June 2005** | President's assent |
| **10 February 2006** | SEZ Act and **SEZ Rules 2006** in force |
| 2025 | SEZ (Amendment) Rules: semiconductor/electronic-component SEZ land cut **50 ha → 10 ha** |
!KEY Policy 2000 → Act 2005 → in force 2006. "SEZ policy announced in" = **both 2000 and 2006** (APPSC 2018 key); Act in force = **2006**.
### 3. SEZ ACT 2005 - OBJECTIVES AND FEATURES
#### 3.1 Objectives (full list)
1. Generation of **additional economic activity**.
2. Promotion of **exports of goods and services**.
3. Promotion of **investment from domestic and foreign sources**.
4. Creation of **employment** opportunities.
5. Development of **infrastructure** facilities.
!TRAP "Exports of services only" is NOT an objective. "SEZ is set up primarily to generate employment" is FALSE - SEZs are **export-oriented** enclaves (APPSC-G2 2017).
#### 3.2 Features
- A **duty-free enclave** for exports.
- Developer: **Central Government, State Government or any person** (public, private, joint sector), together or alone. SEZs under the Act are mainly private.
- Purpose: manufacturing, services or **Free Trade Warehousing Zone**.
- Route: proposal through the **State Government → Board of Approval**.
- **Single-window clearance** for Central and State matters.
- **100% FDI** under automatic route for most manufacturing.
- Minimum area (Rules 2006): multi-product **1,000 ha**, sector-specific **100 ha**, IT/ITES 10 ha; reduced in 2019 (sector-specific to **50 ha**).
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| **s.18** | IFSC in an SEZ - **only one IFSC per SEZ** |
| **s.10AA** (Income Tax Act) | **100%** tax exemption 5 years; **50%** next 5; 50% of reinvested profit for 5 more |
- Tax holidays **phased out 2017-2020** (sunset clause), not 2010-2013.
- **SEZ India mobile app**: launched **15 January 2017** by Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
### 4. SEZ PERFORMANCE
- FY 2024-25 exports **₹14,63,669 crore (~US$173 bn)**, +8%, about **21%** of India's exports.
- Export growth below 4% in FY25; **466 units shut** in five years across seven SEZs (100 in FY25).
- SEZs got **less than 3% of FDI**; DTA sales capped.
- Highest growth in SEZ exports over the previous year (among options asked): **2007-08**.
| State with most SEZs | Fact |
|---|---|
| Andhra Pradesh (combined) | Most approved and operational SEZs (June 2012) |
| **Tamil Nadu** | Most **operational** SEZs now |
| **Telangana** | Most **approved** SEZs now |
### 5. CRITICISM AND REFORM PROPOSALS
#### 5.1 Criticism
- **Nandigram** (Purba Medinipur, West Bengal), **2007**: WB Government allowed the **Salim Group** a chemical hub under SEZ policy; violent protests against **land acquisition**.
- Revenue loss, under-used land, WTO-inconsistent export subsidies.
#### 5.2 Reforms
| Proposal | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Baba Kalyani Committee (2018)** | Rename SEZs as **3Es - Employment and Economic Enclaves**; separate rules for manufacturing/services; unified IFSC regulator |
| **DESH Bill** (Development of Enterprise and Services Hubs) | Announced Budget 2022-23 to replace SEZ Act; removes positive **NFE** condition; allows **DTA sales**; **still pending** - has NOT replaced the SEZ Act |
| **BHAVYA** - Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojana (2026) | **₹33,660 crore**, FY27-FY32; **100 plug-and-play industrial parks** (not 50) |
### 6. INDUSTRIAL CORRIDORS AND NICDC
#### 6.1 NICDC
- **National Industrial Corridor Development Corporation**, under **DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce and Industry**; formerly **DMIC Development Corporation**.
- Knowledge partner to **NICDIT** (the trust) and to city- and project-level SPVs set up with State Governments.
- Programme: **11 industrial corridors** in 4 phases.
#### 6.2 Corridors → States and Partner
| Corridor | States / facts | Partner |
|---|---|---|
| **DMIC** (Delhi-Mumbai) | UP, Haryana, Rajasthan, Gujarat, MP, Maharashtra | **Japan** (JICA, JBIC) |
| **AKIC** (Amritsar-Kolkata) | Punjab, Haryana, Uttarakhand, UP, Bihar, Jharkhand, West Bengal | - |
| **CBIC** (Chennai-Bengaluru, Kochi extension) | Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, AP, Kerala | **Japan** (JICA perspective plan) |
| **BMIC** (Bengaluru-Mumbai) | Karnataka, Maharashtra | - |
| **VCIC** (Visakhapatnam-Chennai) | AP; **800 km**, India's **first coastal corridor** | **Asian Development Bank** |
| **East Coast Economic Corridor** | ~2,500 km; VCIC is Phase 1 | ADB lead partner |

### 7. AP CORRIDORS AND SEZs
- **VCIC** funded by **ADB**; nodes include **Visakhapatnam, Kakinada**, Amaravati, Yerpedu-Srikalahasti.
- VCIC 2035 targets include 24x7 power.
- **Sri City**: multi-product SEZ in Satyavedu and Varadaiahpalem mandals.
- Visakhapatnam: old EPZ, later SEZ.
### 8. COASTAL ECONOMIC ZONES
- **Sagarmala National Perspective Plan (2016)** proposed **14 CEZs** under its **port-led industrialisation** pillar.
- Industrial clusters and SEZs near ports; integrated with corridors to cut logistics costs (Jan 2026).
