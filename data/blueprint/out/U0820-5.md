### 1. 25TH SCO SUMMIT, TIANJIN (31 AUG – 1 SEP 2025)
#### 1.1 Summit Facts
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Edition | **25th** SCO Summit |
| Venue | **Tianjin, China** (China hosted for the **5th time**) |
| Dates | **31 August – 1 September 2025** |
| Theme | **"Upholding the Shanghai Spirit: SCO on the Move"** |
| Outcome | **Tianjin Declaration**, adopted **1 September 2025** by the **10-member** SCO |
#### 1.2 Tianjin Declaration – Outcomes
- **Terrorism**: condemned the **22 April Pahalgam attack** (J&K), plus the **Jaffer Express** and **Khuzdar** attacks (Pakistan).
- **Opposed** unilateral coercive measures (sanctions, tariffs) that violate the UN Charter/WTO rules.
- **Iran**: condemned the **US–Israel strikes on Iran's nuclear facilities** (June 2025); reaffirmed **UNSC Resolution 2231**.
- **Xi's proposals**: **Global Governance Initiative (GGI)**; **¥2 billion** grants in 2025; **¥10 billion** loans to the **SCO Interbank Consortium** over **3 years**; **SCO Development Bank** (agreed in principle).
- **Partnership reform**: **observer + dialogue partner** merged into a single **"SCO Partner"** status.
- **Laos** admitted as partner → **10 members + 17 partners = 27 countries**.
- **India refused to endorse the Belt and Road Initiative (BRI)** (CPEC runs through **PoK**); Modi on Ukraine: "it is humanity's call".
#### 1.3 SCO Membership
| Type | Countries |
|---|---|
| Full members (**10**) | **China, Russia, India, Pakistan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Uzbekistan, Iran, Belarus** |
| Partners (**17**) include | **Turkey, Sri Lanka, Azerbaijan, Egypt, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Armenia, Laos** |
- SCO founded **2001**; **India and Pakistan** became full members in **2017**; **RATS** (counter-terrorism body) in **Tashkent**.
- Context: the term **CRINK** came up around the SCO summit (Section 9.4).
!TRAP India was the only member that did not endorse BRI; Laos joined as a **partner**, not a full member.
### 2. MODI–XI MEETING AND PANCHSHEEL
#### 2.1 Tianjin Meeting (31 August 2025)
- PM **Modi** and President **Xi Jinping** met on the sidelines of the SCO Summit.
- Outcomes: **revival of Panchsheel** principles; **direct India–China flights** to resume after about **5 years**.
- India reopened **tourist visas** for Chinese nationals in **July 2025**.
- Pre-2020: **over 80 weekly flights**; suspended from **January 2020** (COVID-19, then **Galwan 2020** tensions).
- Background: **50% US tariffs** on Indian goods by **Donald Trump**, citing India's purchase of Russian oil.
#### 2.2 Panchsheel – Timeline
- Signed **29 April 1954**: in the preamble of the **Agreement on Trade and Intercourse between the Tibet Region of China and India**.
- **Five principles**: mutual respect for territorial integrity and sovereignty; mutual non-aggression; mutual non-interference; equality and mutual benefit; peaceful coexistence.
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **1954** | Panchsheel signed (29 April) |
| **1955** | **Bandung Conference**: included in the **Ten Principles** (**29** Afro-Asian countries) |
| **1957** | **UNGA adopted** a resolution on peaceful coexistence (by **India, Yugoslavia, Sweden**) |
| **1961** | **Belgrade NAM Conference**: Panchsheel as NAM's principled core |
| **2025** | Revived at the Modi–Xi meeting, Tianjin |
!TRAP The UNGA **adopted** (not rejected) the Panchsheel-based resolution in **1957**.
### 3. ASEAN, EAST ASIA SUMMIT AND TIMOR-LESTE
#### 3.1 47th ASEAN Summit, Kuala Lumpur (October 2025)
- Venue **Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia** (**Malaysia** chair); theme **"Inclusivity and Sustainability"**; PM Modi addressed it **virtually**.
- **22nd ASEAN-India Summit** held virtually as part of the 47th ASEAN Summit.
- Outcomes (Nov 2025): **ASEAN-India Comprehensive Strategic Partnership Plan of Action 2026–2030**; call for an early **AITIGA review**.
- **2026** declared the **"Year of ASEAN–India Maritime Cooperation"**.
- **ASEAN Community Vision 2045** linked with **Viksit Bharat 2047**; "21st century is India and ASEAN's century".
- Together: about **one-fourth** of world population; GDP **> $6 trillion**.
#### 3.2 20th East Asia Summit (EAS)
- **Kuala Lumpur, 2025**: **Kuala Lumpur Declaration** (peace and stability in the Indo-Pacific).
- EAS founded **2005**, **Kuala Lumpur**; chair **rotates annually** among ASEAN members; now **19 members** (**11 ASEAN + 8 partners**).
#### 3.3 Timor-Leste – 11th ASEAN Member

- Admission criteria: **Article 6, ASEAN Charter**.
- **Reservations** by **Singapore and Myanmar**; Timor-Leste has a **2023 Comprehensive Strategic Partnership with China**; faces a **US 10% tariff**.
- President: **José Ramos-Horta**.
- **ASEAN Community Vision 2045** adopted **May 2025**; ASEAN **60th anniversary** in **2027**.
#### 3.4 ASEAN and India–ASEAN Background
- ASEAN founded **8 August 1967**; founders **Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand**; HQ **Jakarta**; **11 members** (Timor-Leste newest).

### 4. BRICS, G20 AND G7 IN 2025
#### 4.1 17th BRICS Summit, Rio de Janeiro
- **6–7 July 2025**, **Rio de Janeiro**, under **Brazil's** chairship; theme **"Strengthening Global South Cooperation for a More Inclusive and Sustainable Governance"**.
- Outcome: **Rio de Janeiro Declaration**; also a **Leaders' Framework Declaration on Climate Finance** and a **Leaders' Statement on Global Governance of AI**.
- First summit of the expanded BRICS; **India** chairs BRICS in **2026** (18th summit).
#### 4.2 G20 Johannesburg Summit
- **22–23 November 2025**, **Johannesburg**, under **South Africa's** presidency: the **first G20 summit in Africa**.
- The **US boycotted** it; leaders adopted the declaration **at the start** of the summit; the **US** takes the next presidency (2026).
#### 4.3 G7 and Quad
- **51st G7**: **Kananaskis, Canada**, June 2025.
- **Quad Summit** in India (planned 2025) was unlikely to be held in 2025.
### 5. US–CHINA TRADE WAR, BUSAN AND "G-2"
#### 5.1 Developments Before Busan
- **9 October 2025**: two Chinese agencies announced **export controls** on rare earth technologies and lithium battery materials ("**dual-use regulation**").
- **Trump**: **100% tariff** on all Chinese goods from **1 November 2025** + export controls on critical software.
- China's share: **60–70%** of rare earth production, **over 80%** of processing (China refines **>90%** of rare earths and makes ~**80%** of solar modules in another estimate).
- **Unreliable Entity List**: **14** foreign (mostly US) entities added.
- China's **SAMR** antitrust probe into **Qualcomm** over its acquisition of Israel's **Autotalks** (earlier probe into **Nvidia**); Qualcomm gets ~**50%** of revenue from China.
#### 5.2 Busan Summit (30 October 2025)
- **Trump–Xi** meeting in **Busan, South Korea**, on the sidelines of **APEC**.
- US tariffs on Chinese imports **cut to 47%**; **one-year truce** on tariffs and rare earth export curbs.
- **India and Brazil** now face the **highest US tariffs – 50%** (for India: **25% retaliatory + 25% Russia-oil levy**).
- Trump called US–China the **"G-2"**: term first used for **Obama–Hu Jintao (2009)**.
- Trump to visit **China in April 2026**; MEA spokesperson **Randhir Jaiswal**: India "examining implications".
- UK spy row: China's proposed **20,000 sq m embassy in London**.
- **Wen Jiabao**: Premier of China **2003–2013**, under President **Hu Jintao**.
#### 5.3 US Tariffs on Russian Oil Route via India
- US imposed tariffs on products **refined from Russian oil**, including those re-exported by India.
- India seen as a **"force for equilibrium"** between the West and the Global South.
- China's **BRI** vs India's alternative: **India–Middle East–Europe Economic Corridor (IMEC)**.
!TRAP 47% = cut US tariff on Chinese goods (Busan); 50% = US tariff on India and Brazil.
### 6. UN, WHO AND CLIMATE CONFERENCES
#### 6.1 UN at 80 and Beijing+30
- **UN founded 1945**; **80th UNGA** session in **2025**.
- **Beijing+30**: High-Level Meeting on the **30th anniversary** (**not 35th**) of the **Fourth World Conference on Women**, **New York**.
- **Beijing Declaration and Platform for Action**: **1995**.
- **Guterres**: gains "hard-won but under attack"; AI is male-dominated and biased; push girls in STEM.
- **Annalena Baerbock**: women's rights an "**unfinished revolution**"; **1 in 3** women face sexual violence.
- Call for **universal ratification of CEDAW**.
#### 6.2 78th World Health Assembly (May 2025)
- Adopted the **world's first WHO Pandemic Agreement** by consensus on **20 May 2025**.
#### 6.3 Third UN Ocean Conference (UNOC3)
- **Nice, France**, **9–13 June 2025**; co-hosted by **France and Costa Rica**.
- Outcome: **Nice Ocean Action Plan** (political declaration "Our ocean, our future: united for urgent action" + voluntary commitments); supports **SDG 14**.
#### 6.4 COP30, Belém (November 2025)
- UNFCCC **COP30** at **Belém, Brazil**: closed with the **"Global Mutirão"** decision.
- Finance path: **USD 1.3 trillion a year by 2035** for developing countries; call to **triple adaptation finance**; no fossil-fuel transition roadmap in the final text.
- India's climate pledge context: **Panchamrit** (COP26).
### 7. INDIA'S BILATERAL SUMMITS
#### 7.1 India–Mauritius: PM Ramgoolam in Varanasi (11 September 2025)
- PM Modi and Mauritius PM **Navinchandra Ramgoolam**; Modi: "not just partners but a **family**".
- Special economic package: **500-bed Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam National Hospital**; **first Jan Aushadhi Kendra outside India**; veterinary school and animal hospital; **AYUSH Centre of Excellence**.
- Education: **Mission Karmayogi** modules; MoUs of **IIT Madras** and **Indian Institute of Plantation Management** with the **University of Mauritius**.
- **Hydrography** project: joint surveys, navigation charts, EEZ mapping.
- Ramgoolam attended **Ganga Aarti**, visited **Kashi Vishwanath Dham**.
- Policies: **Neighbourhood First**, **SAGAR**.
#### 7.2 India–Australia Renewable Energy Partnership
- Australian Climate Change and Energy Minister **Chris Bowen** visited **New Delhi (October 2025)**.
- **REP**: launched by **Modi and Albanese** in **2024**; **eight areas** incl. solar PV, green hydrogen, storage, supply chains; **Track 1.5 Dialogue**.
- India: **500 GW non-fossil by 2030** (**280 GW solar**); **50% non-fossil installed capacity** reached in **July 2025** – **five years early**.
- Australia: **62–70%** emission cut below **2005** by **2035**; **net zero 2050**; **Net Zero Jobs Plan**.
#### 7.3 India–South Africa Submarine Pacts
- **9th Joint Defence Committee (JDC)**, **Johannesburg** (**not Cape Town**), **24–25 June 2025**: **two agreements on submarine cooperation**.
- Leaders: India – Defence Secretary **Rajesh Kumar Singh**; South Africa – Acting Secretary for Defence **Dr Thobekile Gamede**.
- Separate sub-committees: **Defence Policy & Military Cooperation**; **Defence Acquisition, Production, R&D**.
- Shared groupings: **BRICS, IORA, IBSA, NAM**.
#### 7.4 Buddha Relics in Kalmykia, Russia
- Sacred Relics of Lord Buddha (a **National Treasure of India**) shown in **Elista**, capital of **Kalmykia**.
- Venue: **Geden Sheddup Choikorling Monastery** – "**Golden Abode of Shakyamuni Buddha**" (inaugurated **1996**); **over 90,000** devotees.
- Delegation led by UP Deputy CM **Keshav Prasad Maurya**.
- **Kalmykia** = the **only European territory where Buddhism is the main religion**.
- Organisers: **Ministry of Culture** with **International Buddhist Confederation (IBC)**, **National Museum**, **IGNCA**.
- Recalls the **19th Kushok Bakula Rinpoche** (Ladakh), who revived Buddhism in **Mongolia, Kalmykia, Buryatia, Tuva**.
#### 7.5 PM-KUSUM and PM Surya Ghar to Africa via ISA
- India to showcase **PM-KUSUM** and **PM Surya Ghar** in **Africa and island nations** through the **International Solar Alliance (ISA)**.
- Africa: only **4%** of arable land irrigated; food imports ~**$400 billion**.
- PM-KUSUM (2019) components: **100 GW** solar plants on farmer land; **14 lakh** standalone solar pumps; solarise **35 lakh** grid-connected pumps.
- Progress (**September 2025**): standalone pumps ~**70%**; grid-connected **16–25%**; decentralised plants ~**6%**; target revised to **March 2026** (may be extended).
### 8. WEST ASIA AND COUNTER-TERRORISM
#### 8.1 Trump–Netanyahu 20-Point Gaza Peace Plan
- Ceasefire; **Hamas** to release all hostages (**48** held, **20** believed alive) within **72 hours**.
- Israel releases **250 life-term prisoners + 1,700** Gazans detained; **15 Palestinian bodies per hostage body**.
- Hamas **disarmed**, no role in governance; **amnesty** for those pledging peace.
- **International security force** (with **Egypt**) trains Palestinian police.
- **Board of Peace**: headed by **Donald Trump and Tony Blair**; oversees technocrat administration and reconstruction.
- **PA** role conditional on reforms; statehood only vague.
- Support: **Egypt, Jordan, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Indonesia, Turkey, Pakistan**; opposed by Israeli FM **Bezalel Smotrich**.
#### 8.2 Doha Emergency Summit – "Arab-Islamic NATO"
- **Doha, Qatar, September 2025**: **40+** Arab and Islamic nations, after Israel's strike on Hamas leaders in Doha.
- **Pakistan** pushed a NATO-style **"Arab-Islamic task force"**; PM **Shehbaz Sharif** and Turkish President **Recep Tayyip Erdogan** attended.
- **Turkey** (a NATO member) preferred **economic sanctions**, not military action.
- Pakistan: **only nuclear-armed Muslim nation**, ~**170 warheads**.
- India's arms imports from Israel: **over $2 billion a year**.
#### 8.3 UN 1267 Committee – BLA Listing Blocked
- **Pakistan and China** sought to list the **Balochistan Liberation Army (BLA)** and its suicide wing **Majeed Brigade**.
- **Blocked by US, UK, France**: no evidence of a link to Al-Qaida/ISIL; the US had itself designated both as **Foreign Terrorist Organisations**.
- 1267 Committee: **all 15 UNSC members**; decides by **consensus**; any member can place a **"hold"**.
- Targets **Al-Qaida, Taliban, ISIL** and associates: asset freeze, travel ban, arms embargo; **Ombudsperson** for delisting.
#### 8.4 Pakistan's UN Counter-Terrorism Roles
- **June 2025**: Pakistan chosen **Chair of the UNSC Taliban Sanctions Committee** and **Vice-Chair of the Counter-Terrorism Committee (CTC)**; **1 July 2025** – monthly **UNSC Presidency**.
- Context: **Pahalgam attack (22 April 2025)** → India's **Operation Sindoor**.
- **May 2025**: Pakistan announced **₹14 crore** compensation to terrorists' families (incl. kin of **Masood Azhar**).
- **IMF**: **$1 billion** loan (May 2025) under a **$7 billion** facility.
- **FATF**: Pakistan removed from the **grey list** in **October 2022**.
- CTC works with **UNOCT, FATF**, UN sanctions committees (**IMF is not** a formal CTC partner).
### 9. GLOBAL FLASHPOINTS
#### 9.1 White House Summit on Ukraine (August 2025)
- US President **Donald Trump** hosted European leaders and Ukraine's **Volodymyr Zelenskyy** at the **White House**; preceded by the **Trump–Putin meeting in Alaska**.
- Europe plans a European-led **"reassurance force"**; seeks US security guarantees as **NATO membership is ruled out** for Ukraine.
- Russia demands control over **Donbas**; willing to **freeze the southern frontline**.
- War began **February 2022**.
#### 9.2 Madagascar Military Coup
- Elite military unit **CAPSAT** seized power after the National Assembly **impeached President Andry Rajoelina**.
- Triggers: **power and water shortages**, **youth-led protests**.
- CAPSAT was also central to the **2009 coup** that brought Rajoelina to power.
- Capital **Antananarivo**; regional bloc **SADC**; the **African Union** likely to suspend it.
#### 9.3 Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam (GERD)
- Inaugurated by Ethiopian PM **Abiy Ahmed**: **Africa's largest hydroelectric project**.
| Feature | Value |
|---|---|
| River | **Blue Nile**, near the **Sudan** border |
| Storage | **74 billion m³** |
| Capacity | **5,150 MW** |
| Construction began | **2011** |
#### 9.4 'Axis of Upheaval' (CRINK)
- **CRINK** = **China, Russia, Iran, North Korea**: an **informal alignment**, **not a formal alliance** like NATO.
- Popularised **April 2024** in **Foreign Affairs** by **Andrea Kendall-Taylor and Richard Fontaine** (Center for a New American Security, CNAS).
- Earlier use: **2009** by historian **Niall Ferguson**, for unstable states (Somalia, Mexico, Afghanistan).
