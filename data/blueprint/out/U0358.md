## U0358 | PANCHAYATI RAJ AND URBAN BODIES IN ANDHRA PRADESH – STRUCTURE, ELECTIONS, FINANCES, PERFORMANCE
### 1. EVOLUTION OF PANCHAYATI RAJ IN AP
#### 1.1 Order among States
| FIRST | Fact |
|---|---|
| Basis | **Balwant Rai Mehta Committee (1957)**; accepted by NDC **January 1958** |
| First State | **Rajasthan** – Nehru inaugurated on **2 October 1959** at **Nagaur** (not Jaipur) |
| Second State | **Andhra Pradesh – 1 November 1959** |
!TRAP APPSC 2019: 1960, 1961 and 1965 were the distractors; AP = 1 Nov 1959.
#### 1.2 AP laws and reforms
| Law / reform | Year |
|---|---|
| AP Panchayat Samithis and Zilla Parishads Act (first AP law) | **1959** |
| AP Gram Panchayats Act (village level) | **1964** |
| **Mandal system** (NTR Government): Panchayat Samithis → **Mandal Praja Parishads**; ZPs → **Zilla Praja Parishads** | **1986** |
| **AP Panchayat Raj Act (Act 13 of 1994)** – gives effect to the 73rd Amendment | **21 April 1994** |
| AP PESA amending Act | 1998 |
- 1994 Act: **278 sections, 8 Parts** – II Gram Panchayat; III Mandal Parishads; IV Zilla Parishads; V elections; VI Finance Commission.
- **Kasu Brahmananda Reddy** – Minister for Local Bodies in N. Sanjiva Reddy's cabinet. First polls triggered village and caste clashes.
- The 1986 Mandal system drew on **Ashok Mehta**'s "Mandal Panchayat" of 15,000–20,000 people.
#### 1.3 Committees
| Committee | Key recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Balwant Rai Mehta (1957)** | **3 tiers**, indirect election; Panchayat Samiti executive; ZP advisory with **Collector as Chairman** |
| **Ashok Mehta (1977-78)** | **2 tiers** (ZP + Mandal Panchayat); party participation; compulsory taxation; 132 recommendations |
| **G.V.K. Rao (1985)** | PRIs "**grass without roots**" |
| **L.M. Singhvi (1986)** | **Constitutional recognition**; Gram Sabha as direct democracy |
| **Thungon (1988)** | ZP as pivot; supersession max **six months** (not one year); SFC in each State |
### 2. THREE TIERS UNDER THE AP PANCHAYAT RAJ ACT, 1994
| Tier | Members | Head and mode | Term / age |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gram Panchayat** | Ward members (Panch), directly elected | **Sarpanch, directly elected** | 5 years; **21** |
| **Mandal Parishad** | **MPTC** members, directly elected | **MPP President, indirect** from MPTCs | 5 years; 21 |
| **Zilla Parishad** | **ZPTC** members, directly elected | **ZP Chairperson, indirect** from ZPTCs | 5 years; 21 |
- AP's intermediate tier = **Mandal Parishad**, not Panchayat Samiti. (Elsewhere, e.g. Jharkhand PR Act 2001 s. 32, Panchayat Samiti members are directly elected.)
- **Article 243C:** village chairperson's mode left to State; intermediate and district chairpersons indirect. Other qualifications as for State Legislature.
- Village(s) split into **wards**, each directly electing a Panch.
- **13 Zilla Praja Parishads → 28** (not 26) after district reorganisation.
### 3. GRAM PANCHAYAT WORKING
#### 3.1 Who does what
| Function | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Convenes** GP meetings; keeps records | **Panchayat Secretary** |
| **Implements resolutions** of GP and committees | **Panchayat Secretary** (APPSC-GS 2019) |
| **Marriage Officer** (appointed by Governor) | **Panchayat Secretary** (APPSC-GS 2018) |
| Political, elected head; presides | **Sarpanch** |
| Gram Sabha (Art. 243A) | All registered voters of the panchayat area; powers by State law |
!TRAP Convenes / implements / Marriage Officer = **Secretary**, not Sarpanch.
#### 3.2 Obligatory vs discretionary
| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Obligatory** | Sanitation, drinking water, drains, street lighting, village roads, **burial grounds** (APPSC 2018) |
| **Discretionary** | Tree planting, **libraries**, markets, **mother-child welfare homes**, **modern farming practices** |
### 4. AP STATE ELECTION COMMISSION
- **Art. 243K** (panchayats) + **243ZA** (municipalities); superintendence of electoral rolls and all local polls – **not** State legislature or **co-operative** polls (APPSC 2025).
- **Commissioner:** appointed by the **Governor** for **five years**; **status of a High Court Judge**; removed only like a **HC judge**. Both statements correct (APPSC).
- Separate constitutional SEC ended States' power to postpone local polls indefinitely.
- Original AP SEC formed **17 June 1994**; after bifurcation (2 June 2014) the Governor constituted the SEC for residuary AP on **30 January 2016** (APPSC-GS 2022 key).
- **Anil Chandra Punetha** – present SEC (appointed Apr 2026). **8 Sep 2026:** photo rolls for MPTC/ZPTC polls; ward rolls for **41 ULBs** (12 corporations + 29) for 22 Sep 2026.
#### 4.1 Reservation and polls
- SC/ST by population, **by rotation**; women **at least one-third**.
- **AP Cabinet, 18 Aug 2026:** **34% BC** in rural bodies; Mandal Parishad Presidents stay indirect.
- MPP terms end **23 Sep 2026**, ZPP **24 Sep 2026**; second ordinary MPTC/ZPTC polls under preparation.
- **Unanimous-election incentives (Jan 2021):** ₹5 lakh (population < 2,000), ₹10 lakh (2,001–5,000), ₹15 lakh (5,001–10,000), ₹20 lakh (> 10,000). 2021 panchayat polls held in four phases, Jan–Feb 2021.
### 5. FINANCES AND DEVOLUTION
- **SFC** (243I/243Y) – formed by the **Governor** (not SEC) every five years; Part VI of the 1994 Act.
- **AP SFCs:** 4th SFC report Oct 2019; **5th SFC** constituted 20 March 2023, report for 2025–30.
- **14th and 15th FC** grants favoured **drinking water and sanitation**.
- **16th FC (2026-31):** AP rural local bodies **₹16,627 crore** (AP Budget 2026-27); only **duly constituted** bodies get grants – ₹12,150 crore at risk if polls slip past October 2026.
- **Main own revenue of a GP – property (house) tax** (FC grants, GST share are transfers; stamp duty is a State tax).
- **Atmanirbhar Panchayat Program** (MoPR, RGSA): GP own revenue **≥ ₹50 lakh**, Block **≥ ₹1 crore**; **3 years** of term left; **written Gram Sabha consent**.
- **3Fs** – Funds, Functions, Functionaries – not fully devolved; PRIs remain implementers (May 2026).
### 6. URBAN LOCAL BODIES IN AP
#### 6.1 Numbers
- **AP SES 2025-26:** **123 ULBs = 17 Municipal Corporations + 76 Municipalities + 30 Nagar Panchayats**.
- Polls due in 100 of 123 ULBs; merger of 23 municipalities deferred (Aug 2026).
#### 6.2 Municipal laws
| Law | Key fact |
|---|---|
| **AP Municipalities Act, 1965** | In force **2 April 1965** (APPSC-GS 2021); Municipalities and Nagar Panchayats |
| **AP Municipal Corporations Act, 1994** | Corporations; applies the **Hyderabad Municipal Corporations Act, 1955** as parent law |
- Municipal grades (by annual income): **Special, Selection, Grade I, II, III**.
- **Nagar Panchayat** – transitional area (243Q); classification by population, density, revenue, % non-farm employment.
- **33.33% BC quota** in ULBs (G.O. Rt. 1065, 19 Aug 2026) – under the **1994 and 1965 Acts** (not the PR Act).
#### 6.3 Amendment Bills
- **AP Municipal Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2024** – Minister **P. Narayana** (MA&UD); passed **without debate**; **removed the two-child norm** for municipal polls, reversing a 1990s restriction (APPSC-GS 2024: both statements correct). Companion **AP Panchayat Raj (Amendment) Bill** did the same (Nov 2024).
- **AP Municipal Laws (Third Amendment) Bill 2026** (17 Aug 2026) – **Uniform Mutation Fee** on title transfers.
#### 6.4 Mayor election and corporations
- Mayor so far **indirect**; **AP Cabinet (18 Aug 2026)** approved **direct election** of Mayors and Municipal/Nagar Panchayat Chairpersons.
- Corporation = Council (Mayor) + **Standing Committees** + Commissioner; **GVMC** has zonal commissioners under the Municipal Commissioner. Major: **Greater Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Guntur**, Tirupati, Kurnool.
- First Municipal Corporation – **Madras (1688)**; Bombay and Calcutta 1726.
- 74th Amendment – Part IX-A, 243P–243ZG, **18** Twelfth Schedule subjects; 1 June 1993.
#### 6.5 Urban development authorities
- **APCRDA** (Amaravati capital region, **APCRDA Act 2014**); **VMRDA** (Visakhapatnam, 2018).
- UDAs set up by State Act or resolution; **not under ULBs** – fragment urban governance.
- Three-level uniform building checks and Occupancy Certificates across NPs, municipalities, corporations, UDAs (Aug 2026).
- **AIIB**-aided AP Urban Water Supply Project – ₹300 crore, 3.3 million people, 50 ULBs.
### 7. SERVICE DELIVERY – SECRETARIATS AND E-GOVERNANCE
#### 7.1 Village/Ward Secretariats
- Launched **2 October 2019** – a secretariat for each village or ward cluster, working alongside the GP.
- **Ward Secretariats and Volunteer system** – under the **Municipal Administration Department**; 2024-25 target of quality urban services across all ULBs.
#### 7.2 Projects → purpose
| Project | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Mee Seva** | Single-window counter for citizen services (AP, **since 2011**) |
| **e-Panchayat MMP** | Digitises panchayat planning, budgeting, service delivery via **e-Gram Swaraj** |
| **PuraSeva** | Municipal app (by eGovernments Foundation) for services and **grievance redressal** |
- Digitised bill processing for urban works – payment time cut from 20–25 days to about a week (Jul 2026).
