## U0759 | ENERGY STORAGE – BATTERY TECHNOLOGIES, PLI FOR ACC, GRID STORAGE
### 1. CELL BASICS
- **Anode = oxidation** (loss of electrons); **cathode = reduction** (gain) during discharge.
- **Electrolyte** lets ions flow and keeps electrodes apart.
- Storage is the **bottleneck** for RE: balances variable output, supports **24x7 RE**.
### 2. BATTERY CHEMISTRIES
| Battery | Electrodes / feature | Uses |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead-acid** | Lead + PbO2, sulphuric acid | Car starting, UPS |
| **Ni-Cd** | Cathode nickel (oxide) hydroxide, anode cadmium, **KOH** electrolyte; **rechargeable** | **Calculators**, shavers, torchlights, power tools; declining (toxic cadmium) |
| NiMH | Metal hydride replaces cadmium | Hybrid vehicles |
| **Zinc-carbon dry cell** | Zinc pot, MnO2, carbon; **NH4Cl + ZnCl2** paste, no free liquid; **not rechargeable** (reaction irreversible) | Torches, clocks |
| **Li-ion** | **Graphite anode**; NMC (nickel-manganese-cobalt) or LFP (safe, long life) cathode | Phones, EVs, grid |
| **Sodium-ion** | No cobalt/nickel; safer; density below LFP | Storage, low-cost EVs |
| Solid-state | Solid electrolyte | Emerging EVs |
| **Flow** (vanadium) | Liquid electrolytes in tanks; energy and power scaled separately | **Grid-scale** |
!TRAP Sodium-ion is a safer, mineral-light alternative, not a replacement for pumped storage.
### 3. BATTERY MINERALS
- EV **cathodes: lithium, nickel, cobalt**; **graphite = anode** (only three of four make cathodes).
- India **import dependent** for lithium, nickel, cobalt; **>70%** of lithium processing in a few countries.
- Lithium: **Salal-Haimana, Reasi (J&K)**, ~**5.9 million tonnes** inferred (GSI, Feb 2023); **Marlagalla, Mandya** (Karnataka).
- **KABIL**: lithium land in **Argentina**.
- **National Critical Mineral Mission (2025): ₹16,300 crore**; recycling scheme ₹1,500 crore.
- Budget 2026-27: **Rare Earth Corridors** in Odisha, Kerala, **Andhra Pradesh**, Tamil Nadu.
### 4. PLI FOR ACC BATTERIES
- Approved **12 May 2021**; **50 GWh**, **₹18,100 crore**; **Ministry of Heavy Industries**.
- **Ola Electric 20 GWh** (gigafactory, **Krishnagiri, Tamil Nadu**); **Reliance 15 GWh** (Jamnagar, Gujarat).
- **July 2026:** remaining **10 GWh** put to global bids for grid storage.
- Hurdles: **visa delays**, local-content rules, missing technology.
### 5. GRID STORAGE AND BESS
- Need **336 GWh by 2029-30** (CEA) vs ~**500 MWh** BESS (Sept 2025).
- **Energy Storage Obligation** (Ministry of Power, 2022): **1% (2023-24) → 4% (2029-30)**.
- Framework: ESS a power-system element; **ISTS waiver** for co-located storage; consumers may own/lease.
- **Draft National Electricity Policy 2026** (BESS, smart grids, ToD tariffs) replaces NEP 2005. Related: Electricity Act 2003, UDAY 2015, Energy Conservation Amendment 2022.
- **Khavda, Gujarat** (Adani Green): world's largest single-location BESS outside China (May 2026).
### 6. MECHANICAL AND THERMAL STORAGE
| Method | Principle |
|---|---|
| **Pumped storage** | Pump water uphill; release at peak |
| **CAES** | Air compressed into caverns, expanded through turbines |
| **Flywheel** | Rotational kinetic energy |
| **Molten salt** | Thermal storage in CSP; power after sunset |
- **Tehri PSP** (Uttarakhand): India's first **variable-speed** PSP, 4 x 250 MW.
### 7. EV TYPES AND SCHEMES
| Type | Power source |
|---|---|
| BEV | Battery only |
| HEV / **PHEV** | Engine + battery-motor; only PHEV plugs in |
| **FCEV** | Hydrogen fuel cell; exhaust **water vapour** |
- BEV, FCEV and fuel cell-electric hybrid are all **alternative powertrains**.
- **Battery swapping policy**: Budget 2022-23. **PM E-DRIVE** replaced **FAME-II** (2024).
- FY 2025-26: >25 lakh EVs, ~8.5% share; goal **30% by 2030**.
### 8. BATTERY WASTE MANAGEMENT RULES 2022
- **MoEFCC**; replaced Batteries (Management and Handling) Rules, **2001**.
- **EPR**: producers/importers collect and recycle; **landfill and incineration banned**.
- **90%** material recovery by **2026-27**.
- **BPAN** (Battery Pack Aadhaar): only EV and industrial batteries **>2 kWh**.
### 9. ANDHRA PRADESH
- **World's first indigenous high-energy storage device: Amaravati, 6 August 2018** (APPSC 2018).
- **Pinnapuram** (Kurnool, Greenko): solar + wind + pumped storage.
- CEA plan for AP: 3,931 MW battery + 7,370 MW pumped storage (Aug 2026).
