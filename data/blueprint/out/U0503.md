## U0503 | MALNUTRITION AND FOOD SECURITY – INDICATORS, POSHAN ABHIYAAN, FORTIFICATION, PM POSHAN
### 1. MALNUTRITION INDICATORS AND TERMS
| Indicator | Measure | Type |
|---|---|---|
| **Stunting** | Low **height-for-age** | **Chronic** |
| **Wasting** | Low **weight-for-height** | **Acute** |
| **Underweight** | Low **weight-for-age** | Composite |
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Undernutrition** | Protein-energy malnutrition + micronutrient deficiency + stunting + wasting (+ underweight) (APPSC 2019) |
| **Overnutrition** | Overweight and obesity |
| **Double burden** | Under- and overnutrition in the same population; NFHS-6 confirms India faces it |
!TRAP Stunting = height-for-age; wasting = weight-for-height. Obesity is overnutrition, not undernutrition.
### 2. NFHS DATA – INDIA AND AP
| Indicator (under 5) | NFHS-5 | NFHS-6 (2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Stunting | 35.5% | **29.3%** (about 17% fall) |
| Severe wasting | 7.7% | 5.2% |
| Underweight | 32.1% | **31.8%** (barely moved) |
| Breastfed within 1 hour | 41.8% | 50.1% |
| Complementary feeding (6-8 months) | 45.9% | 59.5% |
- **Anaemia (NFHS-5)**: children under 5 about **67%** (up from 58.6%); women about 57%.
- **AP (NFHS-5)**: stunting **31.2%**, wasting **16.1%**, underweight **29.6%**.

### 4. CAUSES OF MALNUTRITION
- **Biggest factor: poor dietary habits and food insecurity** (APPSC G2 2025).
- Others: cereal-heavy diets, **open defecation and unsafe water** (disease-linked nutrient loss), adolescent pregnancy, weak counselling and follow-up.
- Malnutrition is **multi-causal** → combine nutrition with **WASH**.
- Debate: cereal-centric PDS vs dietary diversity; FSSAI ban on **HFSS foods within 50 m of schools**.
### 5. NATIONAL NUTRITION PROGRAMMES
| Programme | Launch | Ministry |
|---|---|---|

| **NP-NSPE / Mid-Day Meal** | **15 Aug 1995** (idea from **Tamil Nadu**) | Education |
| **MAA** (Mothers' Absolute Affection) – breastfeeding | **2016** | Health and FW |

| **PM POSHAN** (renamed MDM) | **2021** | **Education** |

- **ICDS services**: supplementary nutrition, **pre-school non-formal education**, primary healthcare, **immunisation**, health check-up, referral, health and nutrition education for children under 6. NOT: **family planning**, free books, school dress.
- National Nutrition Programmes include NIDDCP, National Iron Plus Initiative, Prevention and Control of Fluorosis; **NOT National Oral Health Programme**.
!TRAP "NIDDCP – 1999" is a wrong pair (APPSC 2024); NIDDCP = 1992. MAA 2016 is correct.
### 6. POSHAN ABHIYAAN / MISSION POSHAN 2.0
- Goal: **Suposhit Bharat** through **convergence, technology, Jan Andolan**.
- Targets: reduce **stunting, underweight, anaemia** (children 6-59 months; women and girls 15-49) and **low birth weight** – not adult obesity.
- **Saksham Anganwadi and Poshan 2.0**: **2021-22 to 2025-26**; **2 lakh** Saksham AWCs (40,000 a year); millets in supplementary nutrition; 4.35 lakh Poshan Vatikas.
- **POSHAN Tracker**: real-time monitoring of Anganwadi services.
- **Rashtriya Poshan Maah = September**; 9th Poshan Maah (Sep 2026, Varanasi): "**Hamari Anganwadi Hamari Jimmedari**". **Poshan Pakhwada** (April) = two-week drive; 8th in 2026.
### 7. ANAEMIA MUKT BHARAT
- Interventions: **IFA tablets, deworming**, outreach for **6 target groups**.
- **WIFS**: Weekly Iron and Folic Acid Supplementation, adolescents **10-19**.
- Does **NOT** give prophylactic **calcium** supplementation.
### 8. PM POSHAN (MID-DAY MEAL)
- **NP-NSPE** began **15 August 1995** as a Centrally Sponsored Scheme in **2,408 blocks**; renamed **PM POSHAN in 2021**.
- Covers **Bal Vatika and classes I-VIII** in government and aided schools.
- Ministry of HRD (now **Education**); Centre-State share **60:40** (non-NER States and UTs).
- Material (cooking) cost for Bal Vatika and primary raised to **₹6.78** per child per day (from ₹6.19, 2025); covers vegetables, pulses, oil, condiments, fuel; grain given separately.
- Partner: **Akshay Patra Foundation** (NGO, 2000, Bengaluru). A **contractor** does not start or organise the scheme.
### 9. FOOD FORTIFICATION
| Phase | Year | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot | 2019 | Rice fortification pilot |
| **I** | **2021-22** | **ICDS and PM POSHAN** |
| **II** | **2022-23** | **291** Aspirational and high-burden districts under TPDS |
| **III** | **2023-24** | All TPDS districts |
- Complete by **March 2024**; continued to **Dec 2028**, Centre-funded **₹17,082 crore**.
- **FSSAI logo: "+F"**. **Double-fortified salt = iodine + iron**.
### 10. GLOBAL HUNGER INDEX AND SOFI
- **GHI 2025**: India **102nd of 123**, score **25.8** – "**serious**"; 105th in 2024.
- GHI indicators: **undernourishment, child stunting, child wasting, child mortality**.
- **Undernourished share** (FAO **SOFI 2025**): **12.0%**.
- **Government objection**: methodologically flawed; child-only indicators cannot measure hunger of the whole population.
### 11. ANDHRA PRADESH NUTRITION SCHEMES
- **Bala Sanjeevani** (plain areas, **47,296 AWCs**; earlier **YSR Sampoorna Poshana**): THR for pregnant and lactating women – rice 3 kg, dal 1 kg, oil 500 ml, ragi, chikki, jaggery, dates + ₹250 kit.
| Group | Plain areas | Tribal package (earlier YSR Sampoorna Poshana Plus) |
|---|---|---|
| 36-72 months | Hot meal + egg + **100 ml** milk | Meal + egg + **200 ml** milk |
| 6-36 months | 2.5 kg Balamrutham + **25 eggs** + 2.5 l milk | 2.5 kg + **30 eggs** + 6 l milk |
- **Anna Amrutha Hastham**: one full meal a day at the AWC for **pregnant and lactating women**.
- **Jagananna Gorumudda** (school meal, Jan 2020) renamed **Dokka Seethamma Madhyahana Badi Bojanam** (July 2024).
- **AP ICDS (2024-25)**: **55,607 AWCs**, **257 projects** (200 rural, 33 urban, 24 tribal), 26 districts; 19.13 lakh children under 6.
- Poshan 2.0: 14,942 adolescent girls in **ASR, Parvathipuram Manyam, YSR Kadapa**; ₹99.58 crore for 9,958 AWCs; **192 new AWCs under PM JANMAN**.
