## U0482 | SOCIAL SECURITY AND DBT – PM-JDY, JAM TRINITY, AADHAAR-ENABLED TRANSFERS, PENSION SCHEMES
### 1. PM JAN DHAN YOJANA (PMJDY)
- Launched **28 Aug 2014**; implemented by the **Department of Financial Services**, Ministry of Finance.
- Aim: **financial inclusion** – banking access to all households.
- **Zero-balance** account; **RuPay** debit card.
| Feature | Old | Now |
|---|---|---|
| RuPay accident cover | ₹1 lakh | **₹2 lakh** |
| Overdraft | ₹5,000 | **₹10,000** |
| Focus (after 14 Aug 2018) | Every household | **Every unbanked adult** |
- **Phase II (2015-18)**: overdraft; Credit Guarantee Fund for overdraft defaults; unorganised-sector pensions (Swavalamban).
- Accounts (19 Aug 2026): **59.09 crore**; women 32.92 crore; rural 45.95 crore. Deposits (28 Aug 2026): ₹3,16,514 crore.
### 2. JAM TRINITY AND INDIA STACK
#### 2.1 JAM
| Component | Role |
|---|---|
| **Jan Dhan** | Bank account – destination of funds |
| **Aadhaar** | Biometric identity – verifies beneficiary |
| **Mobile** | Communication channel |
- JAM first proposed in **Economic Survey 2014-15**.
- Aadhaar launched **29 Sep 2010** at **Tembhli** (Nandurbar, Maharashtra).
- **Biometrics** = reading physical or behavioural traits unique to a person to confirm identity.
#### 2.2 India Stack layers (digital public infrastructure)
| Layer | Enabler |
|---|---|
| Presence-less | Aadhaar authentication |
| Paperless | e-KYC, e-Sign, DigiLocker |
| Cashless | UPI, AePS |
| **Consent** | DEPA; **Account Aggregator** – consent-based data sharing |
- **APBS** (Aadhaar Payment Bridge System) = DBT routing; **AePS** = cash withdrawal at micro-ATMs.
!TRAP Consent layer = data sharing via Account Aggregators, NOT ATM cash withdrawal.
!KEY APPSC G1 2024 A-R: "India Stack enabled online, paperless, cashless access" – A true, R false.
### 3. DIRECT BENEFIT TRANSFER
- Piloted **1 Jan 2013**: **7 schemes, 20 districts** (UPA-II); scaled up in **2015** with JAM.
- FY 2024-25: **₹6.9 lakh crore**, **327 schemes**.
#### 3.1 PAHAL and Ujjwala
| Scheme | Fact |
|---|---|
| **PAHAL** (Pratyaksha Hastaantarit Laabh) | DBT for **LPG subsidy**; national launch **1 Jan 2015**; **first JAM application**; Guinness record (2015) as **world's largest cash transfer**; Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas |
| **PM Ujjwala Yojana** | **Free LPG connections** to BPL women; **1 May 2016, Ballia (UP)**; "Swachh Indhan Behtar Jeevan"; ₹8,000 crore |
!PAIR PAHAL | 2015 | LPG subsidy into account || Ujjwala | 2016 | Free LPG connection
### 4. AADHAAR – ACT, SERVICES AND PUTTASWAMY
- **Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act, 2016** – **Sec 7**: Aadhaar can be required for subsidies from the **Consolidated Fund of India**.
- **Jeevan Pramaan** (2014): pensioners' **digital life certificate** via Aadhaar biometrics (APPSC 2024).
| Case | Year | Bench | Ruling |
|---|---|---|---|
| **K.S. Puttaswamy I** | 2017 | **9 judges** | Privacy a fundamental right under **Art. 21** |
| **K.S. Puttaswamy II** | 2018 | **5 judges, 4:1** | Sec 7 (welfare use) **upheld**; Sec 57 (private use) **struck down** |
- Challenges: **biometric failure** exclusion (elderly, labourers); data privacy.
### 5. INSURANCE SCHEMES
#### 5.1 PMJJBY vs PMSBY (both 9 May 2015, DFS)
| Feature | PMJJBY | PMSBY |
|---|---|---|
| Type | **Life** – one-year renewable term | **Accidental** death and disability |
| Age | **18-50** | **18-70** |
| Premium | **₹436**/yr | **₹20**/yr (₹12 till 31 May 2022) |
| Cover | ₹2 lakh (death from any cause) | ₹2 lakh death/total disability; ₹1 lakh partial |
| Enrolments | 27.33 crore | 57.92 crore |
- PMSBY premium raised **1 June 2022** (first since 2015); **no suicide cover**; 34.18 crore by Apr 2023 (APPSC 2022).
#### 5.2 Other social insurance
- **Aam Aadmi Bima Yojana** – launched **2 Oct 2007**; LIC; life and disability cover for rural landless households.
- **Weavers' Health Insurance Scheme** (2005-06): family of four (self, spouse, two children); at least **50% income from handloom**; men and women; **no age cap**. Life cover: Mahatma Gandhi Bunkar Bima Yojana.
- **RSBY**: health insurance for unorganised BPL workers, enrolment from **1 Apr 2008**; now part of **Ayushman Bharat – PMJAY**.
- **Krishi Shramik Samajik Suraksha Yojana** (1 July 2001): pension + insurance for agricultural workers.
- **Ashraya Bima Yojana** (2001): workers who lose jobs; up to **30% of salary**.
!TRAP Weavers' scheme: "covers up to 60 years" is NOT a condition (APPSC 2022).
### 6. PENSION SCHEMES
| Scheme | Target / entry age | Contribution | Benefit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Atal Pension Yojana** (9 May 2015) | Unorganised workers **18-40**; not taxpayers | Age-linked; Govt co-contribution 50% or ₹1,000/yr for 5 yrs (joined before 31 Dec 2015) | **₹1,000-5,000**/month at 60 |
| **PM-SYM** (2019) | Unorganised workers **18-40** (not 21-40), income **≤ ₹15,000**/month; not in NPS/ESIC/EPFO | Voluntary, age-specific contribution; Centre matches | **₹3,000**/month at 60 |
| **PM Kisan Maan-dhan** (**12 Sep 2019, Ranchi**) | Small and marginal farmers 18-40 | ₹55-200/month (APPSC 2019); Centre matches | ₹3,000/month at 60 |
| **NPS** | Citizens (resident/NRI) and OCI **18-70**; minors via NPS Vatsalya | Voluntary, **defined contribution**, market-linked | Corpus + annuity |
| **Unified Pension Scheme** (2025) | Central Govt employees under NPS | Govt-backed | **50% of average basic pay** of last 12 months for 25 years' service |
- **PFRDA** regulates NPS and administers APY (Central Sector Scheme) under NPS architecture.
- NPS compulsory for Central Govt civilian employees joining on/after **1 Jan 2004**; State Govt employees joined later.
!TRAP NPS: an account **cannot be opened for a third person** (APPSC 2024 "not a feature").
!KEY 9 May 2015 = launch day of **PMJJBY, PMSBY and APY** together.
### 7. NATIONAL SOCIAL ASSISTANCE PROGRAMME
- From **15 Aug 1995**; **Ministry of Rural Development**; Centrally Sponsored **Core of Core** (since 2016); fulfils **Article 41**; BPL households; aim = old-age pension to the very poor.
| Component | Who | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| **IGNOAPS** | 60-79 / 80+ | ₹200 / **₹500** a month |
| **IGNWPS** | BPL widows | Monthly pension |
| **IGNDPS** | BPL, 18-79, **80%+ disability** | **₹300** a month |
| **NFBS** | Family losing breadwinner | Lump sum |
| **Annapurna** | Needy elderly not on IGNOAPS | **10 kg** free foodgrains/month |
- National Maternity Benefit Scheme moved to **MoHFW in 2001**.
- ~3.09 crore beneficiaries (FY 2025-26).
- May 2026: old-age pension lost nearly **half its real value** to inflation.
!TRAP NOT in NSAP / not social security: **Mahila Kisan Sashaktikaran Pariyojana**, **Skill Development Programme**, **SJSRY** (employment generation).
### 8. SOCIAL SECURITY LAWS
| Law | Year | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **ESI Act** | 1948 | Covers hotels, restaurants, motor transport, newspapers, private medical institutions; 4.09 crore employees (Mar 2025) |
| **EPF & MP Act** | 1952 | Establishments with **20 or more** employees |
| **Unorganised Workers' Social Security Act** | **2008** | Notified 30 Dec 2008; merged into Code on Social Security |
| **Code on Social Security** | 2020 | One of **4 labour codes** replacing **29 laws** |
- **eShram** (26 Aug 2021): National Database of Unorganised Workers with a **UAN**.
- Social protection coverage: **64.3%** (2025). NHFDC: Jan 1997, Social Justice Ministry.
### 9. AP SOCIAL SECURITY PENSIONS
- **YSR Pension Kanuka**: launched **2019**; doorstep delivery through Village/Ward Secretariat staff.
- Renamed **NTR Bharosa Pension** (June 2024): **₹3,000 → ₹4,000** for old age, widows, weavers, single women (GO 13 Jun 2024); disabled **₹6,000**.
- First distribution: **65.31 lakh** beneficiaries, 28 categories.
- Kerala (Aug 2026): **DBT vs doorstep delivery** of pensions debate.
