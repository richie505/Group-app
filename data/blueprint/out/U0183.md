## U0183 | REVOLT OF 1857 – ECONOMIC, SOCIO-RELIGIOUS, ADMINISTRATIVE AND MILITARY CAUSES
### 1. POLITICAL AND ADMINISTRATIVE CAUSES
| Cause | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Doctrine of Lapse** | Satara, Jhansi, Nagpur |
| **Awadh (1856)** | "Misgovernance"; **Wajid Ali Shah** removed |
| Mughal emperor | Red Fort and title |
| **Exclusion** | Indians shut out of high civil and military posts |
| **Corruption** | Lower courts, police; favoured the rich |
### 2. ECONOMIC CAUSES
| Class | Grievance |
|---|---|
| Peasants | High, rigid land revenue → debt → land to moneylenders |
| **Taluqdars of Awadh** | About **21,000** dispossessed – **Summary Settlement 1856** |
| Estate owners | **Inam Commission (1852, Bombay**, not Madras) – about **20,000** estates confiscated |
| **Artisans** | British goods **duty-free**, Indian goods taxed |
- **"Absentee sovereignty"** and **"drain of wealth"**.
!TRAP 21,000 = taluqdars (1856); 20,000 = Inam Commission estates (1852).
### 3. SOCIO-RELIGIOUS CAUSES
| Measure | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Abolition of **Sati** | **1829** (**Bentinck**, not Dalhousie) | Custom |
| **Lex Loci / Religious (Caste) Disabilities Act** | **1850** | Converts inherit property |
| **Hindu Widow Remarriage Act** | **1856** | Orthodoxy |
- Charter Act grant – 1813. **Missionaries** (police protection), **Western education, railways, telegraph** → fear of conversion.
- After 1858 the Crown promised **religious neutrality**.
### 4. MILITARY GRIEVANCES
| Grievance | Detail |
|---|---|
| **General Service Enlistment Act 1856** (**Canning**) | Overseas service → loss of caste |
| **Bhatta** | Foreign service allowance stopped for **Sindh and Punjab** |
| Promotion | Highest Indian rank **Subedar**; lower pay |
| Troop ratio | **2.3 lakh** sepoys : **45,000** Europeans (about **5:1**) |
| Bengal Army | **Awadh = "nursery of the Bengal Army"**; over **75,000** sepoys from Oudh |
- Sepoys = **"peasants in uniform"**.
!TRAP Officers' **inefficiency** was NOT a cause (BPSC 2018).
### 5. EARLIER MUTINIES AND IMMEDIATE CAUSE
| Event | Date | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Vellore** | **July 1806** | Leather cockade; caste marks banned |
| **Barrackpore** (47th NI) | **Nov 1824** | Refused Burma by sea |
| 38th NI | 1852 | Refused Burma |
| Santhal revolt | 1855-56 | Civil, not sepoy |
| **Enfield rifle** | **Dec 1856** | Replaced **Brown Bess**; training at **Dum Dum, Ambala, Sialkot** |
| Berhampur (19th NI) | Feb 1857 | Refused cartridges |
| **Mangal Pandey** (34th BNI), Barrackpore | **29 Mar 1857** | Attacked Lt Baugh; hanged **8 Apr** |
| 85 sepoys, 3rd Native Cavalry | 9 May 1857 | 10 years' sentence |
| **Meerut rising** | **10 May 1857** | "Delhi Chalo" |
| Delhi | 11-12 May 1857 | Bahadur Shah II = Shahenshah-e-Hindustan |
- Cartridges greased with **cow and pig fat**, bitten open; symbols **Lotus and Chapatis**.
- Mangal Pandey born **19 July 1827** (PM tribute, 21 Jul 2026).

### 9. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. Lex Loci = 1850, not 1856.
2. Barrackpore 1824 (47th) ≠ 1857 (34th).
3. Great Rebellion = Mehta; Great Mutiny = Hibbert.
4. Inam Commission = Bombay.
5. Sati abolition = Bentinck.
6. Santhal revolt not a sepoy revolt.
7. Meerut, 10 May 1857.
8. Inefficiency of officers not a cause.
