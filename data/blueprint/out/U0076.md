## U0076 | BADAMI CHALUKYA ART – AIHOLE, BADAMI, PATTADAKAL (UNESCO); ORIGIN OF THE VESARA STYLE
### 1. THREE PHASES OF CHALUKYA ART
- Order: **Badami** (rock-cut) → **Aihole** (experimental structural) → **Pattadakal** (mature) = birth of **Vesara**.
- **Percy Brown**: Chalukyas first broke the monopoly of the **rock-cut tradition** in the Deccan.
- Freestanding (mortar and stone) temples are said to begin in the **Gupta** period.
### 2. TEMPLE STYLES – NAGARA, DRAVIDA, VESARA
| Style | Region | Defining feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Nagara** | North India (north of the Vindhyas) | **Curvilinear shikhara** |
| **Dravida** | South India (Krishna–Kaveri) | **Pyramidal vimana**, gopurams |
| **Vesara** | Deccan (Vindhyas–Krishna) | **Hybrid** of Nagara + Dravida; Dravida plan with Nagara details; Chalukya style |
- Nagara, Dravida, Vesara = the **three main styles** of Indian temple architecture.
- Kanchi = Pallavas; Tanjore = Cholas; Pattadakal = Chalukyas.
- **Khajuraho**: all compartments connected internally and externally.
### 3. BADAMI CAVE TEMPLES
- **Four** main sandstone caves around **Agastya lake** (6th–7th c.); earliest under **Mangalesha**.
| Cave | Deity |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Shiva** – 18-armed Nataraja |
| 2 | Vishnu (Varaha, Trivikrama) |
| 3 | Vishnu; **largest**; Mangalesha's inscription, 578 CE |
| 4 | **Jain** |
!KEY Caves 1–3 Hindu; Cave 4 Jain.
### 4. AIHOLE
- Epithet: "**Cradle of Indian temple architecture**" (**K.A. Nilakanta Sastri**) – basis: experimental, early structural phase.
- **Lad Khan**: one of the **earliest** temples (RPSC 2018: early Chalukya temple); **earliest massive bracket capital**; rectangular, **flat slab roof**, stone grilles, pillared porch, **no shikhara**; Shiva; 12 pillars.
- **Durga temple**: **apsidal (gajaprishtha)** plan + pillared gallery round the shrine.
### 5. TEMPLE → SITE → SHIKHARA STYLE
| Temple | Site | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Lad Khan | Aihole | No shikhara |
| Durga | Aihole | Nagara on apsidal plan |
| **Meguti** (Jain) | Aihole | **Dravida**; bears Aihole inscription |
| **Huchimalligudi** | Aihole | **Nagara** |
| **Ravana Phadi** | Aihole | Rock-cut cave, **no tower** |
| **Malegitti Shivalaya** | Badami | **Dravida** |
| **Virupaksha** | Pattadakal | **Dravida** (model: Kailasanatha, Kanchi) |
| Mallikarjuna | Pattadakal | Dravida |
| Sangameshvara | Pattadakal | Dravida |
| **Papanatha** | Pattadakal | **Nagara** |
| Kashi Vishwanatha | Pattadakal | Nagara |
| Galaganatha | Pattadakal | Nagara |
!TRAP Malegitti, Virupaksha, Meguti, Sangameshvara = Dravida; Papanatha and Kashi Vishwanatha = Nagara (not Dravida).
- Nagara shikhara pair: **Huchimalligudi (Aihole)** and **Dashavatara (Deogarh)**.
### 6. PATTADAKAL
- **UNESCO World Heritage Site (1987)**; royal **coronation** site.
- Mature phase: **Nagara and Dravida side by side**.
- **Nandi mandapas**; friezes (**Ramayana, Mahabharata, Panchatantra**); polished pillars.
- Patron queens: **Lokamahadevi** (Virupaksha, for Vikramaditya II's victory over the Pallavas) and **Trailokyamahadevi** (Mallikarjuna).
- Line: Pallava **Kailasanatha** (Kanchi) → **Virupaksha** → Rashtrakuta **Kailasa** (Ellora).
### 7. ARCHITECTS AND INSCRIPTIONS
| Person | Role |
|---|---|
| **Gunda Anivaritachari**, **Sarvasiddhi Acharya** | Architects (**sutradharis**) named in Virupaksha inscriptions |
| **Ravikirti** | **Poet**, composer of the Meguti (Aihole) inscription – not its builder |
### 8. ALAMPUR AND ANDHRA ANGLE
- **Navabrahma temples**: **nine** early Badami Chalukya **Shiva** temples, **Nagara** (rekha shikhara), on the **Tungabhadra**, 7th–8th c.
- District: **Jogulamba Gadwal, Telangana** (earlier Mahabubnagar).
- Alampur = **Dakshina Kashi**; **Jogulamba Shakti Peetha**; **western gateway of Srisailam**.
- Eastern Chalukyas carried the style to **Bikkavolu** and **Draksharama**.
- Oldest Saivite temple: **Parasurameswara, Gudimallam**; temple building in Andhra began under the **Ikshvakus**.
- **Arasavalli** = Surya temple; Hampi much later.
