## U0253 | AMBEDKAR'S SOCIAL THOUGHT – ANNIHILATION OF CASTE, STATE SOCIALISM, CONVERSION TO BUDDHISM
### 1. ANNIHILATION OF CASTE (1936)
- **Undelivered** address for the **Jat-Pat Todak Mandal, Lahore**; session cancelled, so Ambedkar **published it himself**.
- Caste has **religious sanction in the Shastras/Vedas**, so reform within Hinduism is futile; the **whole caste system**, not just untouchability, must go.
- **Inter-caste marriage** = the "real remedy".
- **Gandhi replied in Harijan**.
#### 1.1 Statements → Speaker
| Statement | Speaker / context |
|---|---|
| "Nothing can emancipate the outcaste except the **destruction of the caste system**" | B.R. Ambedkar |
| Caste is "not just a division of labour, but a **division of labourers**" | B.R. Ambedkar |
| Village a "**sink of localism**, a den of ignorance, narrow-mindedness and communalism" | Constituent Assembly, **4 Nov 1948** |
| "Born a Hindu... will not die a Hindu" | **Yeola, 13 Oct 1935** |
!KEY Annihilation of caste was needed for freeing the marginalised; political power was necessary for the **Depressed Classes** to protect their interests.
### 2. BOOKS AND THEMES
| Work | Year | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| **Who Were the Shudras?** | 1946 | Shudras originally **Kshatriyas**, degraded after conflict with Brahmins (denied **upanayana**) |
| **States and Minorities** | 1947 | Memorandum for the **Scheduled Castes Federation**; state socialism |
| The Untouchables | 1948 | Origin of untouchability – "**broken men**" theory |
| **Thoughts on Linguistic States** | 1955 | Smaller states |
| **The Buddha and His Dhamma** | 1957 (posthumous) | Rational, social reading of Buddhism = basis of **Navayana** |
!TRAP Shudras = degraded Kshatriyas; Untouchables = "broken men".
### 3. STATE SOCIALISM – STATES AND MINORITIES (1947)
- **State ownership** of key industries and of **agricultural land**; **collective farming**; **nationalised insurance**.
- To be **written into the Constitution** so a legislative majority could not undo it.
### 4. VILLAGE, GANDHISM AND CONGRESS
- **Individual**, not the village, the basic unit.
- Rejected **Gram Swaraj** and **trusteeship**; wanted a **strong, centralised** democracy.
- Criticised Congress for refusing to treat caste as a political issue.
### 5. AMBEDKAR VS GANDHI
| Issue | Gandhi | Ambedkar |
|---|---|---|
| Caste | Varna = division of labour | Annihilate it |
| Term | "**Harijan**" | Rejected; Dalit / Scheduled Castes |
| Method | Change of heart | Law, political rights |
| Electorate (1932) | Opposed separate electorates; fasted | Demanded them; settled for **147 reserved seats** (**Poona Pact**) |
| Religion | Reform from within | Buddhism (1956) |
- Common ground: uplift of the marginalised and **education**.
- Later felt the Poona Pact made Dalits **dependent on caste Hindus**.
### 6. WOMEN, LABOUR AND EDUCATION
| Field | Contribution |
|---|---|
| Women | **Hindu Code Bill** (inheritance, marriage, divorce) – **resigned 1951** when it stalled |
| Labour | **Independent Labour Party (1936)**; Labour Member **1942-46** – backed the **8-hour working day** |
| Education | People's Education Society (1945); Siddharth College (1946) |
### 7. THOUGHTS ON LINGUISTIC STATES (1955)
- Accepted "**one state, one language**", not "one language, one state".
- Warned against huge states; divide UP, Bihar, MP, Maharashtra; **Hyderabad as second capital**.
### 8. CONVERSION TO BUDDHISM
| Event | Date | Place / fact |
|---|---|---|
| Yeola declaration | 13 Oct 1935 | Depressed Classes Conference |
| **Conversion** | **14 Oct 1956** | **Deekshabhoomi, Nagpur**; with wife and about **5 lakh** followers (mostly **Mahars**); **22 vows** |
| Death | 6 Dec 1956 | Delhi |
- **Sikhism** considered – backed by **B.S. Moonje**; opposed by Gandhi, Rajagopalachari, Malaviya.
- **Navayana** ("new vehicle", Neo-Buddhism) → **Dalit Buddhist (Bauddha) Movement**; rejects Karma, rebirth, monasticism, meditation and the four noble truths.
- **Supreme Court, 24 Mar 2026** : conversion out of Hinduism, Sikhism or Buddhism = immediate loss of SC status (Clause 3, Constitution (SC) Order, 1950); Sikhs added 1956, Buddhists 1990.

### 11. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. Conversion **1956**, not 1935 (Yeola = declaration only).
2. "One state, one language" – not "one language, one state".
3. Gandhi: division of labour; Ambedkar: **division of labourers**.
4. Neo-Buddhism / Navayana = Ambedkar.
5. Annihilation of Caste was **never delivered**.
6. "Harijan" = Gandhi's term, rejected by Ambedkar.
7. Moonje backed Sikhism; Gandhi opposed.
