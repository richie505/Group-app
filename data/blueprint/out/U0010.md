## U0010 | IVC SEALS, WEIGHTS AND MEASURES, HARAPPAN SCRIPT
### 1. SEALS AND SEALINGS – MATERIAL AND SHAPE
- Seals mostly of **steatite (soapstone)** – easy to carve; also **terracotta, copper, ivory**.
- Generally **square** (or oblong plaque).
| Shape | Feature / site |
|---|---|
| **Square** | Standard seal: **animal motif + inscription** |
| **Rectangular** | **Inscription only** |
| **Mesopotamian-type cylinder** | **Rare**, not common; Mohenjodaro, Kalibangan |
| Button seal, Persian Gulf-type seal | **Lothal** |
!TRAP Cylinder seals are Mesopotamian-type and rare; the normal Harappan seal is a square steatite seal.
### 2. USES AND VALUE AS A SOURCE
- A **sealing** is the clay impression of a seal pressed on a package; it proved the goods were untouched.
- Uses: **trade regulation, authentication, ownership, identity marking** and **long-distance communication** – not coins.
- Seals are a **distinctive feature** of the IVC (APPSC GS 2024) and, with the script unread, the main source on the people's beliefs, animals and trade.
- **Uniform seals and weights** = main proof of **standardisation and regulated trade**.
!KEY APPSC G1 2017: long-distance communication → **seals and sealings**.
### 3. FAMOUS SEALS → SITE
| Seal | Site |
|---|---|
| **Pashupati seal** ("Proto-Shiva": seated yogic figure ringed by animals) | **Mohenjodaro** |
| **Bull seal** (zebu) | **Mohenjodaro** |

| Persian Gulf-type seal | Lothal |
- **Jun 2026**: **Rakhigarhi** – cylindrical **steatite** seal with **alligator motif** and **five** Harappan characters; chert trade weights also found.
### 4. ANIMALS ON SEALS AND TERRACOTTA
| Shown | Not shown |
|---|---|
| **Unicorn** (one-horned, most common), humped bull (zebu), **elephant**, **tiger**, **rhinoceros**, buffalo, deer, **sheep** | **Cow**, **horse**, **lion** |
- Pashupati figure is ringed by **elephant, tiger, rhinoceros and buffalo**, with deer below.
!TRAP "Animal not depicted on Harappan seals" = **Horse**; the cow is also never shown.
### 5. WEIGHTS
- Material: mostly **chert**; also limestone and steatite.
- Shape: **cubical** (not spherical).
| Denomination | System |
|---|---|
| Lower | **Binary**: 1 : 2 : 4 : 8 : 16 : 32 : 64 |
| Higher | **Decimal** multiples: 160, 320, 640, 1600, 3200 |
- Same weights over about **8,00,000 sq km** → **central regulation** of trade.
- Unit weight about **13.63 g** (the "16" ratio).
!PAIR Lower weights | binary | 1 to 64 || Higher weights | decimal | 160, 320, 640
### 6. MEASURES – SCALES
| Scale | Site | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| **Ivory** scale | **Lothal** (APPSC G2 2011) | Smallest division about **1.70 mm** (27 divisions in about 46 mm) |
| **Shell** scale | **Mohenjodaro** | Unit about **6.7 mm** |
| Copper scale | Harappa | – |
- Ivory weight balance reported at Mohenjodaro and Lothal.
!TRAP Ivory = Lothal; shell = Mohenjodaro; copper = Harappa.
### 7. HARAPPAN SCRIPT – FEATURES
| Feature | Fact |
|---|---|
| Nature | Mostly **pictographic** + some abstract signs |
| Direction | **Right to left** (signs crowded at the left end); some **boustrophedon** (Kalibangan) |
| Number of signs | About **400** (estimates 400-700) |
| Average inscription | About **5 signs** |
| Longest inscription | **26 signs** |
| Corpus | About **5,000** inscribed objects |
| Found on | Seals, tablets, pottery, copper plates, tools |
| Status | **Undeciphered** |
- Indus script is among the world's **earliest writing systems**; first noticed by **J. Fleet, 1912**.
- Still unread because **no bilingual text** (like the **Rosetta Stone**) has been found.
### 8. DECIPHERMENT ATTEMPTS → SCHOLAR
| Hypothesis | Scholar | Standing |
|---|---|---|
| **Proto-Dravidian** | **Iravatham Mahadevan**, **Asko Parpola** | Most widely supported |
| **Indo-Aryan** (early Sanskrit) | **S.R. Rao** | Minority view; Rig Veda post-dates Mature Harappan |
- The language of the script (Dravidian vs Indo-Aryan) remains **open**.
!TRAP Mahadevan and Parpola = Proto-Dravidian; S.R. Rao = Indo-Aryan.
### 9. DHOLAVIRA SIGNBOARD
- **Ten large signs** – the famous **10-sign inscription**, at the **North Gate** of Dholavira.
- Dholavira excavated by **R.S. Bisht, 1990**; **only** Harappan site on the **UNESCO World Heritage List (2021)**.
- Signboard (10) is the largest in sign size; the **longest** text has **26** signs.
