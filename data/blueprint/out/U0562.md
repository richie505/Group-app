## U0562 | TOURISM, LOGISTICS AND OTHER SERVICES IN ANDHRA PRADESH
### 1. AP RETAIL TRADE POLICY 2015-20
- Aims: make AP a **preferred retail destination**, build retail logistics, promote **e-commerce**, use retail for socio-economic development.
- Targets: **Rs 5,000 crore** investment and **20,000 jobs by 2020**; train 1 lakh youth in 5 years.
### 2. AP TOURISM POLICY 2024-29
- Linked to **Swarna Andhra @2047** (not Viksit Bharat @2047).
- **Industry status** to tourism and hospitality across all segments.
- Incentives: fiscal, recurring, special, non-fiscal; custom packages for **Mega and Ultra Mega** projects.
- Targets: AP among India's **top three destinations**; tourism **above 20% of State GVA** and **20% of workforce**.
- **Budget hotels**: promoted to **attract tourists** (affordable stay) (APPSC 2008).
- **Swarna Andhra Tourism Vision 2047** launched at the **FAITH conclave**; AP won **Best Tourism Promotion State** at **GTM 2026**.
### 3. TOURIST ARRIVALS
- Arrivals **36.42 crore** (2025-26).
- **France**: most tourists in 2006 (World Tourism Organization) and most visited in 2024, followed by Spain and USA.

### 5. TOURISM BODIES, CIRCUITS AND PLACES
- **APTDC**: fully State-owned operational arm: **Haritha hotels**, boating, sound-and-light shows, **Srisailam ropeway**.
- 25+ circuits: 2 Buddhist, 10 temple, 5 beach, 4 river cruise, 3 eco, 2 sea cruise, 1 seaplane.
- AP = birthplace of **Mahayana Buddhism**; about **40** Buddhist monuments.
- Buddhist sites: Amaravati, Thotlakonda, Bavikonda, Bojjannakonda, Sankaram, Salihundam, Ghantasala (**Srisailam not** in list).
- **Rushikonda Beach**: **Blue Flag**.
- Vizag Beach Road: AP sought shift **CRZ-3 → CRZ-2**; **beach-shack policy** based on **Goa**.
### 6. LOGISTICS
- Multimodal logistics parks identified in AP: **Visakhapatnam** and **Ananthapur**.
- **LEADS**: AP an **Achiever** in 2022; slipped to **Fast Mover** in 2024.
