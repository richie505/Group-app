## U0583 | COMPENSATION FOR LOSS OF REVENUE AND DEVELOPMENT OF BACKWARD DISTRICTS (RAYALASEEMA AND NORTH COASTAL)
### 1. BACKWARD DISTRICTS PACKAGE
- **Section 46(2)-(3)** of the AP Reorganisation Act: Centre to make **appropriate grants** and ensure a **special development package**, with incentives, for **Rayalaseema and North Coastal AP**; **Section 94(2)**: support for backward-area programmes.
| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Districts | **7** = 4 Rayalaseema + 3 North Coastal |
| Rayalaseema | Anantapur, Kurnool, YSR Kadapa, Chittoor |
| North Coastal | Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam |
| Amount | **Rs 50 crore per district per year** = Rs 350 crore a year (2016-17) |
| Model | **KBK** (Koraput-Bolangir-Kalahandi, Odisha) and **Bundelkhand** packages |
| Status | Centre decided to **continue** support; disbursements **stalled** |
- Adequacy: first allocations made, then releases irregular; Rs 50 crore per district is small against the loss of Hyderabad's revenue.
!TRAP Rs 50 crore for 7 districts - not Rs 100 crore for 7, not Rs 25 crore for 13.
### 2. SECTION 94(1) TAX INCENTIVES (2015)
- Section 94(1): Centre to take **fiscal measures incl. tax incentives** for industrialisation; notified **2015**.
- **Additional depreciation 35%** + **investment allowance 15%** on new plant and machinery.
- Units set up **1 Apr 2015 - 31 Mar 2020** in notified backward districts of **both AP and Telangana**.
- Criticism: short of what AP wanted; **Special Category Status** (refused) would have given **90% central share in CSS** and tax exemptions.
### 3. SCHEMES AND AMENDMENTS
| Scheme / Act | Fact |
|---|---|
| **101st Amendment, 2016** | GST compensation to States for revenue loss |
| **BRGF** | Launched 2006-07; delinked from central support 2015-16; now defunct |
| **Aspirational Districts Programme** | **NITI Aayog** (not Finance Commission), **Jan 2018**, PM Modi; **112** districts; 5 themes; States lead |
- ADP motto: **Convergence, Collaboration, Competition** (not Compensation).
- BRGF covered **250** districts in India; **13** in undivided AP - 9 Telangana districts (all but Hyderabad) + **Anantapur, Chittoor, Kadapa, Vizianagaram**.
- After BRGF: States use own incentive policies (capital subsidies, power-tariff concessions).
### 4. COMPENSATION FOR REVENUE LOSS
- 2014-15 resource-gap grant: only **Rs 3,979.50 crore**.
- Revenue deficit grants released for some years; the **16th Finance Commission** stopped RD grants for **all States** - AP loss up to **Rs 38,000 crore** (15th FC grants for 2024-25 and 2025-26 drawn in advance).
- AP's complaint: transfers below **14th and 15th FC** recommendations and the **PM's Rajya Sabha** assurances.
- **Polavaram** revised cost: Centre limits liability to **old price levels**; R&R costs disputed; funds lapse under SNA.
### 5. RAYALASEEMA
- Now **8 districts**: Kurnool, Nandyal, Anantapur, Sri Sathya Sai, YSR, Annamayya, Tirupati, Chittoor (Palnadu, Bapatla, NTR are coastal).
- **Low population density** due to recurring **droughts and famines**; groundwater stress (Sri Sathya Sai 57.44%).
- Strengths: **43%** of horticulture area, **52%** of output, **71%** of micro-irrigation; six AP districts in India's top 10 for micro-irrigation = five Rayalaseema + Prakasam.
- Industrial backwardness: investment skewed to coast, no anchor industries - migration, agrarian crisis.
- **Sri Bagh Pact (16 Nov 1937)**: irrigation priority to Rayalaseema for **10 years**.
| Project | Fact |
|---|---|
| Godavari-Penna link Ph I | **73 TMC** Godavari flood water to Pennar, **5-stage lift**; about 21% done |
| Veligonda | Krishna to **Gundlakamma**; **71.5%** complete |
| Polavaram-Nallamala Sagar link | Godavari surplus to Rayalaseema |
| Handri Neeva | 24.20 TMC seasonal use |
!TRAP Package covered 4 Rayalaseema districts; the region now has 8.
### 6. NORTH COASTAL ANDHRA (UTTARANDHRA)
- **"Kalinga Andhra"**, 6 districts: Alluri Sitharama Raju, Anakapalli, Parvathipuram Manyam, Visakhapatnam, Vizianagaram, Srikakulam.
- Tribes: **Savara, Jatapu** (Srikakulam side); Gadaba, Bagata, Kotia, Porja, Khond (Araku/Visakhapatnam agency).
- **Lowest literacy: ASR (44.67%)**; lowest urbanisation (2.84%) and density (54): new Polavaram district (HQ Rampachodavaram).
- Srikakulam, Vizianagaram: no anchor industries - **distress migration**.
- ASR **1st** in India on **Basic Infrastructure** theme, ADP (March 2026).
- Projects: **Vamsadhara-II** 93.5%; Nagavali-Champavathi link via **Thotapalli barrage**; **Bhogapuram airport** (Vizianagaram), flights targeted June 2026.
### 7. LATEST DEVELOPMENTS
- **13 Nov 2025**: Visakhapatnam Economic Region (**10 districts**) launched, not part of ADP; Tirupati Economic Region too.
- Jan 2026: AP Cabinet tracked Rs 8.55 lakh crore investment for North Andhra and Rayalaseema.
- 2026: Karnataka's M. Govinda Rao committee - 177 of 236 taluks backward.
