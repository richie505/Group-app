## U0251 | DR. B.R. AMBEDKAR – LIFE, MAHAD AND KALARAM SATYAGRAHAS, WRITINGS, ROUND TABLE CONFERENCES
### 1. LIFE, EDUCATION AND TITLES
- Born **14 April 1891**, **Mhow** (now Dr. Ambedkar Nagar, MP), **Mahar** family; among the first Dalit students at **Elphinstone High School**, Bombay.
- Slogan: **"Educate, Agitate, Organise"**; titles **"Modern Manu"**, **"Messenger of Dalits"**.
| Institution | Degree |
|---|---|
| **Bombay University** (Elphinstone College) | BA (**1912**) |
| **Columbia University**, New York | MA, PhD; **honorary LL.D (1952)** |
| **London School of Economics** | MSc, DSc |
| **Gray's Inn**, London | Barrister-at-law |
| **Osmania University**, Hyderabad | **Honorary D.Litt (1953)** |
!TRAP Honorary LL.D = Columbia (1952); honorary D.Litt = Osmania (1953).

### 4. MAHAD SATYAGRAHA, 1927
| Event | Date | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Bole Resolution**, Bombay Legislative Council | **1923** | Opened public watering places to untouchables |
| Mahad Municipality opens **Chavdar Tank** | **1924** | Only on paper |
| **Mahad Satyagraha** ("Water Satyagraha") | **20 March 1927** | Ambedkar **drank water from the Chavdar Tank** |
| **Manusmriti Dahan** | **25 December 1927** | Public burning of the Manusmriti at Mahad |
- Held at the **Kolaba District Depressed Classes conference**; organised by the Bahishkrit Hitakarini Sabha.
- Upper castes "purified" the tank; a court injunction stalled the second satyagraha. Ambedkar compared Mahad to the **French National Assembly of 1789**.
### 5. KALARAM TEMPLE SATYAGRAHA, 1930
- **Kalaram Temple, Nashik**, **1930** – **temple entry**; continued for over five years.

### 7. COMMUNAL AWARD AND POONA PACT, 1932
| Feature | Communal Award | Poona Pact |
|---|---|---|
| Date | **16 August 1932**, **Ramsay MacDonald** | **24 September 1932** |
| Electorate | **Separate** | **Joint**, reserved seats |
| Provincial seats, depressed classes | **71** | **148** |
| Signatories | – | **Ambedkar + Madan Mohan Malaviya** (also M.M. Jayakar, Devdas Gandhi) |
- Award: seats by separate electorate for Indian Christians, Muslims, Europeans, Sikhs, Depressed Classes.
- **Gandhi's first fast unto death** began **20 September 1932** at **Yerwada Jail**; he feared a permanent split in Hindu society.
- Pact: fair share in **civil services** and **local bodies**; paved the way for SC/ST reserved seats.
!TRAP **Gandhi did not sign** the Poona Pact (the "Gandhi-Ambedkar Pact").
#### 7.1 Aftermath
- **All India Anti-Untouchability League** (Gandhi, **30 September 1932**) → renamed **Harijan Sevak Sangh**; weekly **Harijan** (**1933**).
- **AP angle:** Gandhi launched temple entry at **Siddantham** (Krishna district), **17 December 1933**.

### 9. POSTS HELD AND REMARKS
- **Labour Member**, Viceroy's Executive Council, **1942–46**.
- **First Law Minister** of independent India; **President of the Drafting Committee**.
- **Resigned 27 September 1951** over the stalled **Hindu Code Bill**.
- On Gandhi: "**Mahatmas, like fleeting phantoms, raise dust but not the level**" (14 August 1931, Mumbai); rejected the term **"Harijan"**.
| Gandhi | Ambedkar |
|---|---|
| Reform Hinduism, keep varna, "Harijan" | Annihilate caste through law and political power |
