## U0824-6 | ENVIRONMENT IN NEWS – SPECIAL TOPIC: RAMSAR SITES OF INDIA (UP TO JUNE 2026)
### 1. RAMSAR CONVENTION AND INDIA
#### 1.1 The Convention
- **Ramsar Convention** – signed/adopted in **1971** at **Ramsar, Iran**.
- Subject: **wetlands of international importance**.
- India became a party on **1 February 1982**.
!TRAP India joined in **1982**, not 1971; 1971 is the year the Convention was signed at Ramsar, Iran.
#### 1.2 India's Numbers (June 2026)
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Total Ramsar sites | **100** (June 2026) |
| Area | about **14 lakh hectares** |
| Rank in Asia | **1st** |
| Rank in the world | **3rd** |
| Sites till 2014 | **26** |
| January 2026 | 98 |
| Added after 2020 | 56 |
- Growth: **26 sites (till 2014) → 98 (January 2026) → 100 (June 2026)**.
!NUM NUMBER GAME: 1971 = Convention signed; 1982 = India joined; 1981 = first Indian sites; 100 = India's total (June 2026).
#### 1.3 National and Global Wetland Initiatives
- **Amrit Dharohar** – announced in the **Union Budget 2023-24**, launched in **2023** to promote wise use of Ramsar sites.
- Four components of Amrit Dharohar: **Species and Habitat Conservation**, **Nature Tourism**, **Wetlands Livelihoods**, **Wetlands Carbon Assessment**.
- Other support: **National Plan for Conservation of Aquatic Ecosystems (NPCA)** (centrally sponsored) and the **Wetlands (Conservation and Management) Rules, 2017**.
- **Ramsar COP15** – **Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe**, **23–31 July 2025**.
  - Adopted the **Fifth Strategic Plan (2025–2034)**.
  - **Victoria Falls Declaration** adopted at the ministerial segment.
  - **Zimbabwe** took over the Convention presidency for **2025–2028**, after **China**.
  - New **Ramsar Wetland Cities**: Victoria Falls (Zimbabwe), Kasane-Kazungula and Shakawe (Botswana).
!KEY Ramsar COP15 = Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe (July 2025); Strategic Plan 5 runs 2025–2034.
### 2. INDIA'S 100TH RAMSAR SITE – SURHA TAL
#### 2.1 What and Where
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Site | **Jayaprakash Narayan Bird Sanctuary (Surha Tal)** |
| District | **Ballia**, Uttar Pradesh |
| Milestone | India's **100th** Ramsar site (**June 2026**) |
| Type | **Freshwater** wetland |
| Birds | Habitat for **migratory and resident** birds |
| Roles | Ecological balance, **groundwater recharge**, **fisheries**, local livelihoods |
#### 2.2 The Run-up to 100
- **97th and 98th**: **Patna Bird Sanctuary (Etah, UP)** and **Chhari-Dhand (Kutch, Gujarat)** – **World Wetlands Day 2026**; announced by Union Environment Minister **Bhupender Yadav**.
- **99th**: **Shekha Jheel Bird Sanctuary, Aligarh (UP)** – **22 April 2026**.
- **100th**: **Surha Tal, Ballia (UP)** – June 2026.
- With these, **Uttar Pradesh has 13** sites – second only to **Tamil Nadu (20)**.
!TRAP Surha Tal is a **freshwater** wetland in **Ballia** – not saline, not in Aligarh. Shekha Jheel is the Aligarh site (99th).
!TRAP **Patna Bird Sanctuary** is in **Etah, Uttar Pradesh**, not in Bihar.
