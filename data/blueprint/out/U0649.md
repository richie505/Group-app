## U0649 | INDIA - SEX RATIO AND CHILD SEX RATIO: TRENDS, REGIONAL PATTERN, CAUSES
### 1. CONCEPTS AND DEFINITIONS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Sex ratio (India) | **Females per 1,000 males** (APPSC 2018); an indicator of gender equality |
| Sex ratio (UN / international) | **Males per 100 females** |
| Child sex ratio (CSR) | Girls per 1,000 boys aged **0-6** |
| Sex ratio at birth (SRB) | Girls born per 1,000 boys born |
| Biological norm | About **105 boys per 100 girls** = roughly **950** girls per 1,000 boys |
| Missing women | Shortfall of women, esp. in Asia; coined by **Amartya Sen (1990)** |
- More boys are born, but **male child mortality is naturally higher** (UPSC 1999: assertion false, reason true).
!TRAP India = females per 1,000 males; UN = males per 100 females.

!TRAP 2001: sex ratio 933 but CSR 927; 2011: sex ratio 943 but CSR 918 - CSR fell while overall ratio rose.

### 6. ANDHRA PRADESH
#### 6.1 Census and NFHS
| Census | 1971 | 1981 | 1991 | 2001 | 2011 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Residual AP | 981 | 978 | 976 | 983 | **997** |
- Declined till 1991, then improved; AP 997 > India 943. Adult sex ratio in health table **996** (separate figure).
- Undivided AP: 978 (2001) → 993 (2011).
- Improvement credited to women's empowerment and aid for girls' education and marriage.
- **NFHS-4: AP 1,020** (APPSC 2017); **NFHS-5: AP 1,045** (rural 1,055, urban 1,024).
!TRAP India NFHS-5 = 1,020 = AP NFHS-4 - do not mix them.
#### 6.2 Districts and CSR
- 13-district era: most favourable **Vizianagaram, Srikakulam**; East Godavari, West Godavari, Visakhapatnam, Guntur crossed 1,000 in 2001-11.
- 28-district recast: highest **Polavaram 1,051**, lowest **Markapuram 962**.
- CSR: residual AP **944**; undivided AP **939** (APPSC 2019), down from 961 (2001); India 918.
- Undivided AP mandals 2011: lowest CSR Nagayalanka (Krishna) 780; highest Venkatapuram (Khammam) 1,047.
- AP child population 64.19 lakh (1991) → 52.44 lakh (2011); girls 25.47 lakh.
### 7. CAUSES
- Accepted causes (UPPSC 2005, all four): **high maternal mortality, high female child mortality, female foeticide, more male births**.
- Roots: **son preference, patriarchy**, neglect of girls' health, intra-household discrimination, male migration.
- MMR: **254 (2004-06) → 88 (2021-23)**.
- After curbs on prenatal sex selection, firstborn girls show **25% higher child mortality** than firstborn boys - bias shifts from birth to survival.
### 8. LAWS, SCHEMES AND CASES
| Measure | Year | Aim |
|---|---|---|
| Cradle Baby Scheme (Tamil Nadu) | 1992 | Against female infanticide |
| **PCPNDT Act** | **1994** | Bans sex selection and prenatal sex determination; tests only for genetic/chromosomal abnormalities |
| **Beti Bachao Beti Padhao** | **22 Jan 2015, Panipat (Haryana)** | Reverse falling CSR; survival, protection, education of girls; multi-sectoral action in gender-critical districts |
- BBBP does **not** allow prenatal sex determination.
- **Supreme Court (Jun 2026)**, Justices Sanjay Karol and P.K. Mishra: CSR shows "clear signs of improvement" but bias is **deep-seated** (son preference, patriarchy).
- Related data: NFHS-5 women's literacy 71.5%. British India: literacy below 16%, female literacy about 7%, IMR about 218, life expectancy about 32 years (APPSC 2024).
