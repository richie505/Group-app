### 2. RIVERS OF AFRICA AND THE AMERICAS
#### 2.1 Africa
- **Nile**: White Nile from **Lake Victoria**, Blue Nile from **Lake Tana** (Ethiopia; upper course **Abbay**); meet at **Khartoum**; into the Mediterranean. Egypt = "**Gift of the Nile**".
- Nile basin (11): Burundi, Rwanda, DR Congo, Ethiopia, Sudan, South Sudan, Tanzania, Kenya, Eritrea, Uganda, Egypt – **NOT Chad**.
- **Congo**: Atlantic; its basin = second-largest tropical rainforest and **largest tropical peatland** (~29 billion t carbon, about three years of global emissions; UNEP 2023).
- **Zambezi**: Victoria Falls, **Kariba Dam** (Zambia–Zimbabwe); into the Indian Ocean.
- **Niger**: rises in **Fouta Djallon**; "**Oil River**" (palm-oil trade, Niger Delta petroleum); **inland delta in Mali**; mouth Gulf of Guinea (Atlantic).
- South Sudan: landlocked; White Nile; capital **Juba**.
#### 2.2 Americas
- **Amazon**: rises in the Andes of **Peru**; Peru, Colombia, Brazil → Atlantic; navigable ~1,600 km.
- **Mississippi**: rises at glacial **Lake Itasca** (Minnesota); tributaries Ohio, Arkansas, Red; into the **Gulf of Mexico** near New Orleans.
- **St Lawrence** links the Great Lakes to the Atlantic.
- **Colorado**: Grand Canyon, **Hoover Dam**.
### 3. RIVERS OF ASIA AND EUROPE
#### 3.1 Asia
- **Yangtze**: Tibetan Plateau → **East China Sea**; **Three Gorges Dam** (largest hydel project).
- **Huang He** (Yellow River): "**Sorrow of China**"; Loess Plateau silt raises its bed → floods, course changes.
- **Mekong**: "**Danube of the East**"; Tibetan Plateau → Yunnan (China), Myanmar, Laos, Thailand, Cambodia, Vietnam → **South China Sea** – NOT Malaysia or Bangladesh.
- **Amur** (2,897 km): Russia–China (–Mongolia) border; into the **Sea of Okhotsk**.
- **Ob, Yenisei, Lena**: north to the Arctic; spring floods as upper parts thaw while mouths stay frozen.
- **Amu Darya, Syr Darya** → **Aral Sea** (diversion shrank it). **Tigris + Euphrates** → **Shatt al-Arab** → Persian Gulf.
- **Mo Chhu** joins **Pho Chhu** at **Punakha** (Bhutan) → **Sankosh** (APPSC 2019).
- Lop Nor: saline lake, China. APPSC key: **Siam (Khorat) plateau** – between the Irrawaddy and Sittang.

- Canals: **Volga-Don** (Russia), **Sault-Sainte Marie** (Canada), **Ludwigs** (southern Germany).

### 5. LAKES OF THE WORLD
#### 5.1 Records
| Lake | Record | Location |
|---|---|---|
| **Caspian** | **Largest lake** by area and volume (~371,000 km²); saline | Five states (see 7.2) |
| **Superior** | **Largest freshwater by area** (~82,100 km²) | USA–Canada; Duluth second-largest city on it |
| **Baikal** | **Largest freshwater by volume**; **deepest (1,642 m)**; **oldest** (>25 million yrs); ~20% of unfrozen freshwater; "Pearl of Siberia"; Baikal seal | Siberia, Russia |
| **Tanganyika** | **Longest freshwater**; **second deepest (1,470 m)**; second by volume; wholly south of the Equator | Tanzania, DRC, Burundi, Zambia |
| **Victoria** (Victoria Nyanza) | **Largest tropical**; second-largest freshwater by area; source of White Nile; Africa's largest | Tanzania 51%, Uganda 43%, Kenya 6% |
| **Titicaca** | **Highest navigable**; 280 m deep | Peru–Bolivia |
| **Turkana** | Largest **desert** lake, alkaline | Kenya (Eastern Rift) |
| **Van** | Largest **alkaline** lake | Turkiye |
| **Balkhash** | West half fresh, east half saline; largest of Central Asia, 15th in world | Wholly Kazakhstan |
| **Maracaibo** | Lagoon; largest lake of South America (~36 million yrs) | NW Venezuela |
| Aral Sea | Shrinking from irrigation | Kazakhstan–Uzbekistan |
- Area order: Superior (82,097) > Victoria (68,870) > Baikal (31,500) > Great Bear (31,153); also Superior > Victoria > Michigan > Chilika.
- Lake → country: **Urmia** – Iran; **Reindeer, Athabasca, Great Bear, Great Slave** – Canada; **Onega** – NW Russia (Karelia), not Canada.
- Equator crosses none of Tanganyika, **Tonlé Sap** (Cambodia floodplain lake), Patos Lagoon.
- **Finland** = "Garden of lakes" (~187,888); largest Saimaa. **Pangong Tso** is not freshwater.
#### 5.2 Great Rift Valley Lakes (APPSC 2024)
- Form a chain, mostly freshwater; **Malawi** at the southern end.
- Western (Albertine) branch: **Tanganyika, Albert, Edward, Kivu**.
- **Victoria** lies **between** the two branches, not in the rift.
