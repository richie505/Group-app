## U0510 | MAKE IN INDIA, PLI, NATIONAL MANUFACTURING POLICY AND EASE OF DOING BUSINESS
### 1. MANUFACTURING POLICY TARGETS
| Policy | Year | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **National Manufacturing Policy (NMP)** | **4 Nov 2011** | Manufacturing share **16% → 25%** of GDP within a decade (by 2022); **100 million jobs**; growth **12-14%** |
| **Make in India** | **2014** | 12-14% growth; share 16% → 25% (later by 2025); 100 million jobs (NOT 50% by 2030) |
| **National Manufacturing Mission** | Budget 2025-26 | **25%** share and **143 million jobs by 2035** |
- Status: manufacturing still about **16.3-17.5%** of GVA → **25% target missed**; strategy moved to scale, design and **global value chains**.
!TRAP 100 million jobs = NMP 2011 and Make in India; 143 million jobs = National Manufacturing Mission.
### 2. NATIONAL MANUFACTURING POLICY 2011 AND NIMZs
- Infrastructure on **PPP basis**; **skilling of rural youth**; sector-, location- and technology-neutral.
- **NIMZ** (National Investment and Manufacturing Zone): large **integrated industrial township** – zoned land use, clean technology, social infrastructure; instrument of NMP 2011.
- NIMZs differ from SEZs in size, infrastructure planning, governance, exit policy and incentives.
- **India's first NIMZ was proposed in Andhra Pradesh.** In-principle approval for **Chittoor and Prakasam** (14 Aug 2013); **Prakasam** got final approval (2016); TEFS report submitted (2025-26).
- Industrial corridors: DMIC, CBIC, BMEC, AKIC.

- **Logo: a lion made of cogwheels.** Links: "Vocal for Local", "Make in India for the World".

### 5. PLI AND SECTORAL SCHEMES
#### 5.1 PLI Scheme (2020)
- **14 sectors, ₹1.97 lakh crore**; incentive on **incremental sales** of goods made in India (electronics **4-6%**); backs sunrise industries.
- Sectors include **white goods, solar PV modules, specialty steel**, textiles (MMF, technical), drones – NOT coal mining equipment.
- Results (to Sept 2025): investment >₹2.0 lakh crore; incremental production >₹18.70 lakh crore; **12.60 lakh jobs**.
- **PLI-Auto:** ₹25,938 crore; **≥50% domestic value addition**.
#### 5.2 PM E-DRIVE and Electronics Schemes
- **PM E-DRIVE:** **Ministry of Heavy Industries**, **29 Sept 2024**, **₹10,900 crore**; replaced **FAME-II** (₹11,500 crore, 2019-24); subsumed EMPS-2024; e-2W/3W support till 31 Mar 2026, others till 31 Mar 2028.
- **Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme (FY27-FY31):** ₹62,500 crore; replaces PLI-LSEM; **2.25-5%** on eligible sales; up to **1.5%** extra for domestic sourcing; **3%** extra for design and R&D by Indian brands.
- **ECMS:** outlay ₹22,919 crore → ₹40,000 crore (Budget 2026-27).
!PAIR PLI | 4-6% | incremental sales || MPMS | 2.25-5% | eligible sales
### 6. ELECTRONICS AND SEMICONDUCTORS
#### 6.1 Electronics Data
- India = **2nd-largest mobile phone producer after China** (ICA data 2017; retained 2023, 2025-26).
- Mobile output ₹18,900 cr (2014-15) → **₹5.45 lakh cr (2024-25)**; electronics ₹1.90 → ₹11.32 lakh cr; **99.2%** of phones used are made in India; **smartphones = largest export category (2025)**.
#### 6.2 India Semiconductor Mission
- Under **Digital India Corporation (MeitY)**; supports fabs, OSAT/ATMP, chip design; **up to 50% capital assistance**.
- **ISM 1.0:** ₹76,000 crore; **12 projects**, about **₹1.64 lakh crore**; Gujarat 6, Odisha 2, Assam, UP, Punjab, AP 1 each.
- **ISM 2.0:** 70-75% self-sufficiency by 2029.

### 7. SEZs AND ZONES
- **SEZ Act, 2005** with SEZ Rules – in force **10 February 2006**; SEZ Policy 1 April 2000.
- Who can set up: **Central Govt, State Govt, or any person** (private, joint or foreign developer).
- **Sec 10AA:** **100%** tax exemption on export income for **5 years**, **50%** next 5, 50% of ploughed-back profit for a further 5.
- **Sunset clause:** only units operational by **30 June 2020** get 10AA.
- **DESH** (Development of Enterprise and Service Hubs, Jun 2026): no compulsory positive NFE; **DTA sales allowed**; WTO-compliant.
### 8. ATMANIRBHAR BHARAT AND LOGISTICS
- **Five pillars:** **Economy, Infrastructure, System, Vibrant Demography, Demand** (NOT privatisation).
- **May 2020 package:** about ₹20 lakh crore (~10% of GDP).
- **PM GatiShakti (Oct 2021)**, **National Logistics Policy (Sept 2022)** – cut logistics cost (historically 13-14% of GDP).
- **"China+1":** India as an added manufacturing base in global supply chains.
### 9. WORLD BANK DOING BUSINESS AND B-READY
- Published by the **World Bank Group** (conceptualised with **IFC**); mainly **guides foreign investors** (APPSC 2019).
- **10 parameters:** starting a business, construction permits, getting electricity, registering property, getting credit, protecting minority investors, paying taxes, trading across borders, enforcing contracts, resolving insolvency.
| Edition | India's rank |
|---|---|
| DB 2015 (2014) | **142** |
| DB 2018 | **100** – jump of 30 |
| **DB 2020** | **63** (last edition) |
- **Discontinued 2021** – data irregularities, ethics concerns.
- Weakest indicators: **enforcing contracts, registering property**.
- **B-READY** (Business Ready) – successor; **3 pillars: regulatory framework, public services, operational efficiency**; India in the third report (around Sept 2026).
!NUM Doing Business = 10 parameters | B-READY = 3 pillars | NITI Investment Friendliness Index = 8 pillars
### 10. EoDB REFORMS, PLATFORMS AND BOTTLENECKS
#### 10.1 Reforms Credited
| Reform | EoDB area |
|---|---|
| **GST (2017)**, faceless assessment, e-filing (APPSC 2025) | Paying taxes, unified market |
| **SPICe+** (2020, 11 services, 3 ministries), MCA21 V3 | Starting a business |
| Online building-plan approvals | Construction permits |
| **IBC 2016** | Resolving insolvency |
| Faster electricity connections | Getting electricity |
| Land records (DILRMP, ULPIN) | Registering property |
| e-Sanchit; commercial courts | Trading across borders; contracts |
!TRAP "Removal of all foreign ownership restrictions", nationalisation, PSU expansion are NOT EoDB reforms.
#### 10.2 Single Window and Decriminalisation
| Platform | Facts |
|---|---|
| **e-Biz** | **Mission Mode Project under NeGP** – online registration, filing, payments |
| **National Single Window System** | 686 central + 7,498 state approvals (Aug 2026); **FIF portal merged Aug 2022** |
| **Invest India (2009)** | Not-for-profit JV of DIPP, FICCI, CII, NASSCOM and States |
| DPIIT SWS Guidebook (2025) | 80 services = 44 essential + 36 desirable |
- **Jan Vishwas 2.0 Bill:** introduced 18 Aug 2025; **16 Acts, 355 provisions, 288 decriminalised**; passed 2026 – first-time Legal Metrology lapses get an "Improvement Notice".
#### 10.3 Remaining Bottlenecks
- **Legal:** contract enforcement, property registration.
- **Labour:** **four labour codes** (consolidation, formalisation) – yet 80%+ workers informal.
- **Compliance burden** → DPIIT Reducing Compliance Burden exercise; "Assemble in India" vs value addition; EoDB vs "ease of living".
- IMD Competitiveness rank: 43 (2021) → 41 (2025).

| **BRAP 2024 (7th)** | **435 reform points** – Simplification, Elimination, Digitisation, Decriminalisation |

- **Asian Competitiveness Institute (ACI)**, **Singapore**, also releases EoDB rankings of Indian States on 3 **"ABC" pillars** – Attractiveness to investors, Business friendliness, Competitiveness policies.
- **ACI EDB Index 2018** (21 States/UTs): **AP 1st** (up from **5th in 2016**), Maharashtra 2nd, Delhi 3rd.
- **NITI Aayog Investment Friendliness Index (Jul 2026):** AP among **Frontrunners** (45-50); Top Performers – Gujarat, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Goa, Odisha.

- **AP Electronics Manufacturing Policy 4.0 (2024-29):** plug-and-play clusters; semiconductors = a **propelling sector**.
- Rayalaseema hub: JSW Steel Kadapa, Kia, Royal Enfield Tirupati.
