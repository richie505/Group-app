### 2. PREVENTION OF CORRUPTION ACT 1988
- Origin: Santhanam Committee's push for tougher bribery laws → consolidated as **PCA 1988**.
- **Public servant (Sec 2(c))**: any government or **local-authority** employee, any **Judge**, anyone whose office requires a **public duty** – not only permanent Union employees.
#### 2.1 PC (Amendment) Act 2018
| Change | Detail |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Align with **UNCAC** |
| **Bribe-giving** | Made a **direct offence**, up to **7 years**; a coerced bribe-giver is protected if he reports within **7 days** (not 30) |
| Commercial organisations | Liable for bribes offered on their behalf; offence framed around **"undue advantage"** |
| **Sec 17A** | Government's **prior approval** before inquiry/investigation into official decisions |
| Punishment | Bribe-taking public servant: **3 to 7 years** + fine |
| Trial | Within **2 years**, extendable to max **4 years** |
### 3. SANCTION FOR PROSECUTION
| Stage | Provision |
|---|---|
| Before inquiry/investigation | **Sec 17A PCA** – government approval |
| Before court takes cognizance | **Sec 19 PCA** – prior sanction; covers retired public servants since 2018 |
| General protection | **Sec 218 BNSS** (earlier Sec 197 CrPC; BNSS from 1 July 2024) |
- **Vineet Narain (1997)** and **Subramanian Swamy (2014)** struck down prior-sanction rules.
- **CPIL v UoI (Jan 2026)**: **split verdict** on 17A – **Justice Nagarathna** struck it down (Art. 14); **Justice Viswanathan** upheld it if approval rests on a binding **Lokpal/Lokayukta** opinion; pending before a larger Bench.
- Debate: "sanction paralysis" vs protecting honest officers from a "play-it-safe syndrome".
!TRAP 17A = approval before investigation; 19 = sanction before prosecution.
### 4. WHISTLE BLOWERS PROTECTION ACT 2014
- **Whistle-blower**: **any public servant or other person, including an NGO**, making a **public interest disclosure** to the Competent Authority (APPSC 2019 key) on corruption, wilful misuse of power or a criminal offence.
| Complained against | Competent authority |
|---|---|
| Central employees | **CVC** |
| State employees | State Vigilance Commission |
| **Union Minister** | **Prime Minister** |
| **State Minister** | **Chief Minister** |
| MPs | **Rajya Sabha Chairman / Lok Sabha Speaker** |
| MLAs | Presiding officer of the House |
- **Identity** given to the authority and kept **confidential**; protection against **victimisation**.
- Complaint within **7 years**; **no anonymous** complaints.
- Penalties: revealing identity = up to **3 years + ₹50,000**; knowingly false complaint = up to **2 years + ₹30,000**.
- **SPG** excluded from the rules.
- The Act exists but is **not operationalised**.
!TRAP The Bill passed the Lok Sabha in **2011**, but the Act is of **2014**.
#### 4.1 Amendment Bill 2015
- Passed by **Lok Sabha, May 2015**; would bar disclosures in **RTI Sec 8(1)-type** categories (sovereignty, security, Official Secrets, Cabinet papers).
- **Lapsed in 2019** with the 16th Lok Sabha.
### 5. AGENCIES – BASIS AND POWERS
| Agency | Basis | Parent | Key powers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CBI** | **DSPE Act 1946**; **non-constitutional, non-statutory** | Ministry of Personnel | Director chosen by **PM, CJI (or nominee), LoP**; needs **state consent (Sec 6 DSPE)** |
| **ED** | **PMLA 2002** and **FEMA 1999** | Ministry of Finance | Director can **attach "proceeds of crime"** (APPSC 2022) |
| **CVC** | **CVC Act 2003** – independent **statutory** body | – | **Superintendence over CBI** in corruption cases; CVC + up to **2** VCs, **4 years or 65** |
| **State ACB** | PCA 1988 | State government | Bribery traps |
- **CBI data (CVC report, Sep 2026)**: conviction rate **71.71%** (2025); **7,229** cases pending (earlier 7,072; 379 over 20 years).
### 6. LOKPAL AND LOKAYUKTAS
#### 6.1 Lokpal (2013 Act)
- **Chairperson + up to 8 members**; **50% judicial**; at least 50% from **SC, ST, OBC, minorities, women**.
- **Selection committee**: **PM** (chair), **Speaker**, **LoP**, **CJI** or nominated SC judge, an **eminent jurist**; appointed by the **President**.
- **First Lokpal**: **Justice Pinaki Chandra Ghose** (March 2019); **Justice A.M. Khanwilkar** chairperson from **Feb 2024**.
- The Act obliged every State to set up a **Lokayukta within one year**.
#### 6.2 AP Lokayukta
- **AP Lokayukta and Upa-Lokayukta Act, 1983**; Lokayukta = **higher functionaries**, Upa-Lokayukta = the rest.
- **2024 amendment**: retired HC Chief Justice or Judge, on recommendation of a **selection committee chaired by the CM**; term **5 years**.
- **25 Aug 2026**: **Justice T. Mallikarjuna Rao** sworn in; oath by Governor **S. Abdul Nazeer**.
