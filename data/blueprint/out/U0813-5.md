#### 5.3 Minor Mineral Policy 2025
- Why: mining share in GSDP **3.53%** (2018-19) → **2.71%** (2023-24); the 2022 auction-only rule stalled applications.
| Reform | Detail |
|---|---|
| Pending applications | **6,000+ revived**, first-come first-served; **LOI within 1 year** |
| Dual grant | application-based for minor minerals (building material, granite); auction for industrial minerals |
| Lease premium | 10x → **5x** dead rent; limestone slabs 3x |
| Lease period | granite and industrial minerals **20 → 30 years**; road metal (captive) 15 → 30 years |
| Consideration Amount | **abolished** (COVID-era levy) |
| OTS scheme | penalties **rationalised** (not abolished); closure of abandoned leases |
### 6. HUMAN DEVELOPMENT AND HEALTH RANKINGS
#### 6.1 Anaemia Mukt Bharat – Q1 2025-26 Review
- Union Health Ministry review (April–June 2025): **1. AP, 2. Haryana, 3. Telangana**.
- 88% rise in anaemia prevention index; AP was 3rd in 2024-25 and 2nd in Q1 last year.
- AP topped **5 of 6** categories; **adolescents (10–19 years): AP 3rd** after Haryana and Telangana.
| Category | AP coverage |
|---|---|
| Children 5–9 years | 95%; IFA tablets to 30,35,641 |
| **Pregnant women** | **Highest coverage**; 2,69,952 |
| Lactating mothers | Highest coverage; 1,63,177 |
- Scheme: launched **2018** under **NHM**; target **3% cut a year**; funding **60:40** Centre–State (90:10 for NE/special category).
- Dosage: 6–59 months 1 ml IFA syrup twice a week; 5–9 and 10–19 years 4 IFA tablets a month; pregnant and lactating women 180 IFA tablets free.
- Linked to **SDG 2** and **SDG 3**.
!TRAP Anaemia Mukt Bharat is **not fully centrally funded**: 60:40.
#### 6.2 Education Support

- Stipend **₹1,500/month**, ₹1,000 for books; **26 BC Study Circles**.
- **Mega DSC: 16,347** teacher vacancies.
