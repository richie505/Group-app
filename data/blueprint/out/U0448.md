## U0448 | OTHER SERVICES – TOURISM, BANKING, INSURANCE, HEALTH AND EDUCATION
### 1. INSURANCE – MILESTONES AND REGULATOR
| Act / committee | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **LIC Act** | **1 September 1956** | LIC formed; life insurance nationalised |
| **GIC** | **1972** | General insurers nationalised |
| **Malhotra Committee** | **1993-94** | Open insurance to **private** companies + independent regulator |
| **IRDA Act** | **1999** | Set up **IRDAI** – statutory regulator, HQ **Hyderabad** (not Mumbai); set up **April 2000** |
| **Sabka Bima Sabki Raksha (Amendment of Insurance Laws) Act** | Passed **17 Dec 2025** | **FDI cap 74% → 100%** |
- The 2025 Act amends the **Insurance Act 1938, LIC Act 1956, IRDA Act 1999** and creates a **Policyholders' Education and Protection Fund**.
- IRDAI vision: **"Insurance for All by 2047"**.
- FY25: premium income **Rs 11.9 lakh crore**; **health** passed **motor** as the top non-life segment.
- Life and individual health policies GST-exempt from September 2025; IRDAI sub-committee reviewing private health insurance (Apr 2026).
- **Actuaries** = insurance experts who judge risk and estimate premium.
!TRAP IRDAI was **not** set up on the **Gadgil Committee**'s recommendation – it came from the **Malhotra Committee**.
### 2. INSURANCE TERMS AND PRINCIPLES
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Insurance penetration** | Total premium as **% of GDP** |
| **Insurance density** | **Premium per capita** (usually in US dollars) |
| **Principle of indemnity** | Restores the insured to the **pre-loss position**, no profit; applies to **general** insurance (fire, marine, motor, health), **not life** |
| **Cyber insurance** | Data breach, hacking, **ransomware**, identity theft |
| **Aviation hull** | Physical damage to the **aircraft** itself |
| **P&I** | **Shipowners' third-party** liabilities |
- First sovereign-backed P&I cover: **Bharat Maritime Insurance Pool** (Jul 2026).
!TRAP Neither penetration nor density = "insured persons per 100 people" → APPSC 2017 answer "none of these".
!KEY Fire and marine insurance are contracts of indemnity; life insurance is not.
### 3. PARA-BANKING
- Legal basis: **Section 6, Banking Regulation Act, 1949**, with **RBI** approval.
- Banks may sell **insurance** as a **corporate agent** or through a **joint venture**.
- Banks may run para-banking activities **departmentally or through subsidiaries** (subsidiary route: Section 19(1) of the same Act).
- Bank credit-deposit ratio: 62-year high of **82.6%** in Q1 FY27 (Jul 2026).

### 5. HEALTH SERVICES
| NHA edition | Government health spending |
|---|---|
| 2018-19 | **1.28%** of GDP |
| Latest (Sep 2026) | **1.43%** of GDP vs **NHP 2017** target **2.5%** |
- Health share of total government (Centre + States) spending: **6.12%** (2021-22) → **4.89%** (2022-23), below 5.02% (2019-20).
- **PM-JAY (2018)**: **Rs 5 lakh** per family; with State schemes covers ~**80%** of citizens; only ~**35%** of insured used it; coverage roughly **tripled** since launch (NSS 80th round).
- A committee found **40 crore+** Indians in a healthcare gap (Sep 2026).
#### 5.1 AP – Aarogyasri
- Launched **1 April 2007** in undivided AP by the **Y.S. Rajasekhara Reddy** government.
- Named **Rajiv Aarogyasri** after **Rajiv Gandhi**.
- **Cashless** health insurance for **BPL** families (not all families), run through a State trust.
- State + central schemes cover **70%** of AP households (2026).
!TRAP 80% = citizens covered nationally; 70% = AP households covered.
