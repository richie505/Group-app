## U0334 | DEVOLUTION OF POWERS AND FINANCES TO LOCAL LEVELS – FINANCE COMMISSION AND STATE FINANCE COMMISSION TRANSFERS
### 1. DECENTRALISATION – KEY TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Democratic decentralisation** (Balwant Rai Mehta's term) | Power moves to **elected local bodies** |
| **Deconcentration / administrative delegation** | Work moves to **field officers** who stay inside the bureaucracy |
| **Vertical devolution** | States' share of the **divisible pool** (Art. 270 taxes) – between Union and States |
| **Horizontal devolution** | Split of that share **among States** by formula |
| **Vertical fiscal imbalance** | Union has the buoyant taxes; States bear heavier spending (health, education, agriculture, police) |
- Increasing depth: **deconcentration → delegation → devolution** (devolution transfers most authority).
- **Cesses and surcharges are not in the divisible pool**; pool ≈ **81% of gross tax revenue** (2025-26).
#### 1.1 What the 73rd Amendment Made Mandatory
- Constitutional status, three tiers, direct elections, 5-year term, **reservation**, **State Election Commission (243K)**, **State Finance Commission (243I)**.
!TRAP The **29 subjects of the Eleventh Schedule are not mandatory** – Art. 243G says "**may**"; no automatic transfer.
### 2. CONSTITUTIONAL ARTICLES ON LOCAL BODY FINANCE
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **243G** | Eleventh Schedule (29 subjects) – only a menu |
| **243H / 243X** | Own taxes, duties, tolls, fees **only if the State Legislature authorises**; grants from the State Consolidated Fund (panchayat / municipal) |
| **243I** | **SFC**, formed by the **Governor**; reviews panchayat finances |
| **243Y** | Same SFC reviews **municipal** finances |
| **243J / 243Z** | State law provides for **maintenance and audit** of accounts |
| **243K** | State Election Commission |
| **270** | Taxes forming the divisible pool |
| **280** | **Union Finance Commission**, formed by the **President**; FC grants routed through States |
- **Article 280(3)(bb) and (c)** (added in **1992** by the 73rd and 74th Amendments): Union FC recommends measures to **augment the Consolidated Fund of a State** to supplement the resources of **panchayats (bb)** and **municipalities (c)**, **on the basis of the SFC's recommendations**.
!PAIR Union FC | Art. 280 | President || SFC | Art. 243I | Governor
### 3. STATE FINANCE COMMISSION
- Constituted by the **Governor** **every five years** (Art. 243I); reviews the **financial position** of panchayats and (243Y) municipalities.
- Recommends to the **Governor** on: sharing and **assignment** of State taxes, duties, tolls, fees; **grants-in-aid** from the State Consolidated Fund; measures to improve local finances.
- Governor lays the report, with an **explanatory memorandum** on action taken, before the **State Legislature**.

### 4. SOURCES OF PANCHAYAT REVENUE
| Source | Basis |
|---|---|
| **Own taxes, duties, tolls, fees** | Only if State Legislature authorises (**Art. 243H**, "may") |
| **Assigned taxes** | State-levied taxes handed over to panchayats, on SFC advice |
| **Shared taxes** | Share of State taxes as per SFC |
| **Grants-in-aid** | State Consolidated Fund (243H / 243I) |
| **Union FC grants** | Art. 280, routed through States |
| **Scheme funds** | Centrally Sponsored Schemes |
- Panchayats have **no independent taxing power** under the Constitution.
- **Major own-source revenue: property (house) tax** (APPSC GS 2018); same for ULBs.
#### 4.1 AP – Assigned Revenues
- **AP Panchayat Raj Act, 1994**: a gram panchayat may add a **surcharge on seigniorage** collected on village-quarried materials (s. 60(4)); **seigniorage fees** are credited to the GP Fund.
- **Transfer duty** (surcharge on stamp duty) on property transfers (s. 69) goes to the **local body**.

| Tax and fiscal effort | – | – | **2.5** | – |
| **Contribution to GDP** (new) | – | – | – | **10** |
- **Highest weight in every FC: income distance.**
- 15th FC criteria besides population, area and income distance: **demographic performance, forest and ecology, tax and fiscal effort** (three).
#### 7.1 16th FC Changes and AP
- Income distance from the **average of the three largest States'** per capita GSDP.
- Demographic performance = **population growth 1971-2011** (15th FC used change in TFR).
- Forest includes **open forests** + increase in forest area (2015-2023).
- **Contribution to GDP** (square root of GSDP) **replaced tax effort**; it is **not** a 15th FC criterion; it helped AP.
- AP's **"prosperity paradox"**: above-average per capita GSDP works against it under income distance.
- **AP share 4.217%** (from 4.05%); all five southern States gained.
