## U0334 | DEVOLUTION OF POWERS AND FINANCES TO LOCAL LEVELS – FINANCE COMMISSION AND STATE FINANCE COMMISSION TRANSFERS
### 1. DECENTRALISATION – KEY TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Democratic decentralisation** (Balwant Rai Mehta's term) | Power moves to **elected local bodies** |
| **Deconcentration / administrative delegation** | Work moves to **field officers** inside the bureaucracy |
| **Vertical devolution** | States' share of the **divisible pool** (Art. 270 taxes) – between Union and States |
| **Horizontal devolution** | Split of that share **among States** by formula |
| **Vertical fiscal imbalance** | Union has buoyant taxes; States spend more (health, education, agriculture, police) |
- Depth: **deconcentration → delegation → devolution** (most authority).
- **Cesses and surcharges are not in the divisible pool**; pool ≈ **81% of gross tax revenue** (2025-26).
#### 1.1 What the 73rd Amendment Made Mandatory
- Three tiers, direct elections, 5-year term, **reservation**, **SEC (243K)**, **SFC (243I)**.
!TRAP The **29 Eleventh Schedule subjects are not mandatory** – Art. 243G says "**may**".
### 2. CONSTITUTIONAL ARTICLES ON LOCAL BODY FINANCE
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **243G** | Eleventh Schedule (29 subjects) – only a menu |
| **243H / 243X** | Own taxes, duties, tolls, fees **only if the State Legislature authorises**; grants from State Consolidated Fund |
| **243I** | **SFC**, formed by the **Governor**; reviews panchayat finances |
| **243Y** | Same SFC reviews **municipal** finances |
| **243J / 243Z** | State law provides for **maintenance and audit** of accounts |
| **243K** | State Election Commission |
| **270** | Taxes forming the divisible pool |
| **280** | **Union Finance Commission**, formed by the **President**; FC grants routed through States |
- **Article 280(3)(bb) and (c)** (added in **1992** by the 73rd and 74th Amendments): Union FC suggests how to **augment the Consolidated Fund of a State** for **panchayats (bb)** and **municipalities (c)**, **based on SFC recommendations**.
!PAIR Union FC | Art. 280 | President || SFC | Art. 243I | Governor
### 3. STATE FINANCE COMMISSION
- Constituted by the **Governor** **every five years** (Art. 243I); reviews **finances** of panchayats and (243Y) municipalities.
- Advises the **Governor** on sharing and **assignment** of State taxes and **grants-in-aid**; Governor lays it, with an **explanatory memorandum**, before the **State Legislature**.

### 4. SOURCES OF PANCHAYAT REVENUE
| Source | Basis |
|---|---|
| **Own taxes, duties, tolls, fees** | Only if State Legislature authorises (**Art. 243H**, "may") |
| **Assigned taxes** | State-levied taxes handed over to panchayats, on SFC advice |
| **Shared taxes** | Share of State taxes as per SFC |
| **Grants-in-aid** | State Consolidated Fund (243H / 243I) |
| **Union FC grants** | Art. 280, routed through States |
| **Scheme funds** | Centrally Sponsored Schemes |
- Panchayats have **no independent constitutional taxing power**.
- **Major own-source revenue: property (house) tax** (APPSC GS 2018); same for ULBs.
#### 4.1 AP – Assigned Revenues
- **AP Panchayat Raj Act, 1994**: GP may add a **surcharge on seigniorage** on village-quarried materials (s. 60(4)); **seigniorage fees** go to the GP Fund.
- **Transfer duty** (surcharge on stamp duty, s. 69) goes to the **local body**.

### 7. HORIZONTAL DEVOLUTION – CRITERIA (%)
| Criterion | 13th | 14th | 15th | 16th |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Income distance** (13th: fiscal capacity distance) | 47.5 | 50 | **45** | 42.5 |
| Population 1971 | 25 | 17.5 | – | – |
| Population 2011 | – | 10 | **15** | 17.5 |
| Area | 10 | 15 | 15 | 10 |
| Forest cover / forest and ecology | – | 7.5 | **10** | 10 |
| Fiscal discipline | 17.5 | – | – | – |
| **Demographic performance** | – | – | **12.5** | 10 |
| Tax and fiscal effort | – | – | **2.5** | – |
| **Contribution to GDP** (new) | – | – | – | **10** |
- **Highest weight always: income distance.**
- 15th FC's other three criteria: **demographic performance, forest and ecology, tax and fiscal effort**.
#### 7.1 16th FC Changes and AP
- Income distance from the **average of the three largest States**.
- Demographic performance = **population growth 1971-2011** (15th FC used change in TFR).
- Forest includes **open forests**.
- **Contribution to GDP** (square root of GSDP) **replaced tax effort** (not a 15th FC criterion); helped AP.
- AP's **"prosperity paradox"**: above-average per capita GSDP hurts it under income distance.
- **AP share 4.217%** (from 4.05%); all five southern States gained.

### 10. AUDIT OF LOCAL BODIES
- **CAG – Technical Guidance and Support (TGS)** over local-body audit, as recommended by the **11th Finance Commission**.
- **AP State Audit Act, 1989**: **Local Fund Audit Dept** renamed **State Audit Dept**; **Director of State Audit** (Finance Dept) audits local bodies, can levy **surcharge** for losses (s. 10).
