### 3. KABIR
- Birth legend: born to a **Brahmin widow**; raised by Muslim weaver **Niru and Nima**.
- Guru: **Ramananda**. Stream: **Nirguna Bhakti** (APPSC-GS 2024).
- One God called by many names: **Ram, Rahim, Allah, Khuda, Hari, Govinda**.
- Condemned **rituals and idol worship** of both Hindus and Muslims; opposed **caste**; stood for **Hindu-Muslim unity**; called the greatest saint of the Bhakti cult.
- **Dohas and sakhis** collected in the **Bijak**, the main text of the **Kabirpanthis**.
- "**Pothi Padh Padh Jag Mua ... Dhai Akhar Prem Ka**" - Kabir.
- AP parallel: **Vemana** and **Potuluri Veerabrahmam** attacked caste like Kabir.
### 4. GURU NANAK
- Born **1469 at Talwandi** (Nankana Sahib, near the Ravi); founded the **Sikh religion**; one formless God (**Ik Onkar**).
- Started the free community kitchen **Guru Ka Langar** (common meal, no caste).
- "**God knows man's virtues and inquires not his caste**" - Guru Nanak.
- Companion/disciple: **Mardana**. Language **Punjabi** (Guru Angad later introduced the **Gurmukhi** script).
### 5. CHAITANYA MAHAPRABHU
- Born **1486 at Nabadwip (Nadia, West Bengal)**, Brahmin family; parents **Jagannath Mishra and Shachi Devi**; childhood name **Nimai**.
- Turned to sainthood after his wife's death; deep devotion to **Krishna** (Vaishnava).
- Founded **Gaudiya Vaishnavism**; popularised **Sankirtan** (collective devotional singing), later written up as **Padavali kirtans**.
### 6. MAHARASHTRA SAINTS AND THE VARKARI SECT
- **Varkari sect** worships **Vitthala (Vithoba)** of **Pandharpur** (on the **Chandrabhaga/Bhima**, **Solapur** district); annual pilgrimage to Pandharpur. Its saints: **Jnaneshwar, Namdev, Eknath, Tukaram**.
| Saint | Key fact |
|---|---|
| Jnaneshwar | **Jnaneshwari** - Marathi rendering of the Bhagavad Gita |
| Namdev | Tailor family; teacher **Visoba Khechar**; abhangs in **Marathi and Hindi**; hymns in the **Guru Granth Sahib**; influenced by Islam; greatest influence on the people |
| Janabai | Namdev's disciple; low caste; servant in his home; 300+ poems |
| Eknath | Varkari saint; Marathi bhakti |
| Tukaram | **Abhangas** to Vithoba; **Shivaji's contemporary** |
| Ramdas | **Dasbodh**; strength, discipline, devotion to Rama; **Shivaji's guru**; Maratha political awakening |
- Tukaram and Ramdas laid the spiritual base of the Maratha movement under Shivaji.
!TRAP Namdev was NOT a contemporary of Shivaji; Tukaram was.
