## U0822-6 | OVERVIEW AND DATA: INTERNATIONAL - BILATERAL VISITS, DEFENCE DEALS AND GEOPOLITICAL DEVELOPMENTS
### 1. INDIA'S FOREIGN POLICY DOCTRINES
#### 1.1 Doctrines – Origin and Essence
| Doctrine | Origin | Essence / feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Neighbourhood First** | **2014** (SAARC leaders invited to the PM's swearing-in) | Priority to neighbours: aid, infrastructure, connectivity, disaster response |
| **Act East** | **2014**; upgraded from **Look East (1991)** | Strategic ties with **ASEAN, Japan, South Korea**; meets Thailand's **"Act West"** |
| **Link West** | – | Gulf / West Asia: **energy and diaspora** ties |
| **IPOI** (Indo-Pacific Oceans Initiative) | **East Asia Summit, Bangkok, 2019** | **7 pillars** |
| **SAGAR** | **2015** | Indian Ocean Region (IOR) |
| **MAHASAGAR** | **2025** | Global South |
| **SHANTI** | **2026** | **Securing Holistic Advancement through Norms, Trust, and Integrity** – cooperative maritime security in the **Bay of Bengal** |
| **Nuclear doctrine** | **2003** | **No First Use**, credible minimum deterrence |
- Nuclear doctrine unchanged despite **12 "operationally deployed" warheads** (30 Jun 2026).
!TRAP SAGAR (2015) = IOR; MAHASAGAR (2025) = Global South; SHANTI (2026) = Bay of Bengal.
!TRAP India's Act East meets Thailand's "Act West"; India's own Gulf policy is **Link West**, not Act West.
#### 1.2 Related Terms
- **Soft Power** – coined by **Joseph Nye** (late 1980s): shaping others' choices through attraction and persuasion, not force.
- **Minilateralism** – term credited to **Miles Kahler (1992)**.
- **Raksha (3 Sep 2026)** – Defence Ministry's **10-year framework for defence diplomacy**.
