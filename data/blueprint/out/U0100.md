## U0100 | MUSUNURI NAYAKS AND THE VELAMA (RECHERLA) CHIEFS OF RACHAKONDA–DEVARAKONDA
### 1. FALL OF WARANGAL AND THE LIBERATION MOVEMENT
- **1323**: Warangal fell; **Muhammad bin Tughlaq** renamed it **Sultanpur (Sultanpurti)**; Andhra under **Malik Burhanuddin**, deputy **Malik Maqbul**.
- Of Prataparudra's **75** generals only three survived: **Prolaya Nayaka, Recherla Singama, Prolaya Vema Reddi**.
- **Vimochanodyamam** = **first organised resistance to Muslim rule** in Andhra; Andhra freed within about 2 years of Tughluq misrule (from 1326).
- **Leader** of the confederacy: **Musunuri Prolaya Nayaka** (APPSC GS 2018; G2 2019).
- Other chiefs (took part, did **not** lead): Prolaya Vema Reddi, Recherla Singama, **Koppula Prolaya Nayaka**, Kolani Rudra, Vengi Bhupala, Manchikonda Ganapati Nayaka, Kapaya Nayaka.
- **Kaluvacheru grant (1423)**, issued by Reddi queen **Anitalli**, names **75 Nayakas** who backed Prolaya.
- Successor states: **Musunuri (1325–1368), Recherla Velama (1325–1475), Reddi (1325–1448)**; alongside Vijayanagara (**1336**) and Bahmanis (**1347**).
### 2. MUSUNURI NAYAKS
#### 2.1 Prolaya Nayaka
- Son of Potinayaka; **Kamma** clan (**Chaturthanvayas**).
- Freed land between Krishna and Godavari; capital **Rekapalli** (on the Godavari, forest near **Bhadrachalam**) from **1325** (APPSC G2 2017).
- **Vilasa copper-plate grant (1330)**: Tughluq atrocities, Prolaya's rise, agrarian relief, **agraharas restored** to Brahmins.
- Childless → succeeded by cousin **Kapaya Nayaka**.
#### 2.2 Kapaya Nayaka (1336–1368)
- **1336**: with Hoysala **Veera Ballala III**, Prolaya Vema Reddi and Singama took **Orugallu** from Malik Maqbul; ruled from Warangal.
- Singama cleared **Nalgonda–Mahabubnagar**; Prolaya Vema cleared **Kammanadu–Pakanadu**.
- Nephew **Anapota Nayaka** = governor at **Vengi**.
- **Polavaram inscription**: names 14 members of the lineage, only 3 ruled.

### 3. KAPAYA AND THE BAHMANIS – END OF MUSUNURI RULE
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **1347** | Kapaya helped **Hasan Gangu (Zafar Khan)** found the Bahmani kingdom at **Gulbarga** |
| 1350 | Lost **Kaulas** |
| **1356** | Lost **Bhuvanagiri** |
| 1361/62 | Son **Vinayaka Deva** burnt alive by **Muhammad Shah I** |
| **1364–65** | Truce: **Golconda** ceded + 400 elephants, 250 horses, **33 lakhs**, turquoise throne; Golconda became the boundary |
| **1367–68** | **Battle of Bhimavaram** (near Orugallu): **Anapota Nayaka** killed Kapaya – Musunuri line ends; Telangana to the Velamas |
- Allies sought: **Bukka I** of Vijayanagara; **Firoz Shah Tughlaq**.
- **Tarikh-i-Ferishta** (Persian) calls Telangana **"Tilang"**; describes Bahmani–Kapaya wars.
### 4. RECHERLA VELAMAS (c. 1325–1475)
- Also **Padmanayakas**; began as Kakatiya feudatories; family history **Velugoti Varivamsavali**; founder of lineage **Betala Nayudu**.
| Ruler | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Singama Nayaka I** | Founder of the independent kingdom; freed Nalgonda, Mahabubnagar; reached the Krishna-Tungabhadra doab; **killed by the Jallipalli chiefs** (not at Bhimavaram) |
| **Anapota Nayaka** | Singama's son; capital **Amanagallu → Rachakonda**; won **Bhimavaram** |
| **Sarvagna Singa Bhupala (II)** | Sanskrit scholar-king of Rachakonda |
#### 4.1 Devarakonda Line
- **Mada Nayaka**, younger brother of Anavota (Anapota) Nayaka, was made ruler of **Devarakonda** (Nalgonda), the second branch capital.
- His son **Pedda Vedagiri** succeeded him.
- End: **Sultan Quli Qutb Shah** (Golconda, 1518–43) annexed **Rachakonda and Devarakonda**.
#### 4.2 Forts
- **Rachakonda fort**: **cyclopean masonry**, **without mortar**.
- **Devarakonda**: **14th-century hilltop fort** of the Recherla kings.
- Rachakonda and Devarakonda (with Bhongir, Golconda) = **Vastu Shilpa** centres.
### 5. VELAMA BATTLES AND VELAMA–REDDI CONFLICT
| Battle | Opponents | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Jallipalli** | Singama vs Jallipalli Kshatriyas (aided by Reddis) | Singama killed |
| **Dharanikota** (Dannala Kota) | **Madanayudu and Naganayudu** vs **Anavota Reddi** | Velamas won (revenge for Singama); titles Jagadabbaganda, Rayavesa Bhujanga |
| **Bhimavaram (1367–68)** | Anapota vs Kapaya | Kapaya killed |
- After Kapaya's decline: **Reddis took coastal Andhra**; **Velamas took Musunuri Telangana**.
!TRAP APPSC G2 2019 key on Bhimavaram: Reddis beat the Velamas and drove them out of Palnadu. Mark "Bhimavaram" when asked, but know it also as the Velama–Musunuri battle.

### 11. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. Liberation leader = **Prolaya Nayaka**, not Singama or Prolaya Vema.
2. Musunuri capital = **Rekapalli**, not Rachakonda.
3. Vilasa grant = Prolaya Nayaka; Kaluvacheru = Anitalli.
4. Singama I died at **Jallipalli**, not Bhimavaram.
5. Anapota **Nayaka** (Velama) killed Kapaya; Anapota **Reddi** moved the Reddi capital.
6. Sangeeta Sudhakaram = Velama Singabhupala II, not a Reddi.
7. Damerla = Kalahasti; Velugoti = Venkatagiri; Ravu = Bobbili.
8. Golconda ceded in 1364–65; Bhuvanagiri lost in 1356.
