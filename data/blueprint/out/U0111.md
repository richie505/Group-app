## U0111 | DELHI SULTANATE – THE FIVE DYNASTIES IN OUTLINE
### 1. THE FIVE DYNASTIES
- Delhi Sultanate: **1206-1526**; opened by **Second Tarain (1192)**, ended at **First Panipat (1526)**.
- Five dynasties: **Turkish** (first four in origin, Sayyids claimed Prophet's descent) then **Afghan** (Lodi).
| Order | Dynasty | Period | Founder | Origin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Slave / Mamluk** | **1206-1290** | **Qutbuddin Aibak** | Turk |
| 2 | **Khalji** | **1290-1320** | **Jalaluddin Khalji** (crowned at **Kilokhari**) | Turk (Khalji) |
| 3 | **Tughlaq** | **1320-1414** | **Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq (Ghazi Malik)** | Turk (father Turkish slave, mother Jat) |
| 4 | **Sayyid** | **1414-1451** | **Khizr Khan** (Timur's viceroy) | Claimed descent from the Prophet |
| 5 | **Lodi** | **1451-1526** | **Bahlol (Bahlul) Lodi** | **Afghan** (first Afghan Sultan) |
- Slave phases: **Qutbi** (1206-11), **First Ilbari** under Iltutmish (1211-66), **Second Ilbari** under Balban (1266-90).
- **Sayyids** were called **Kulah-Daran** (wore a pointed cap, **kulah**); fourth dynasty, between Tughlaqs and Lodis.
- After 1526: Mughal rule by **Babur**; **Sur** dynasty = **Sher Shah Suri**.
!TRAP Order: Slave → Khalji → Tughlaq → Sayyid → Lodi. Questions jumble Sayyid and Khalji.
### 2. LONGEST AND SHORTEST
| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Longest dynasty** | **Tughlaq**, 94 years (1320-1414) |
| **Shortest dynasty** | **Khalji**, about 30 years (1290-1320) |
| **Longest reign** | **Bahlol Lodi**, 38 years (1451-89) |
- Longest-reign distractors: Firoz Tughlaq (1351-88), Alauddin (1296-1316), MBT (1325-51), Balban (1266-87).
### 3. RULERS – REIGN AND CONTRIBUTION
| Ruler | Reign | Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| **Qutbuddin Aibak** | 1206-10 | Founded Slave dynasty; **Quwwat-ul-Islam** mosque; **began Qutub Minar** |
| **Iltutmish** | 1211-36 | Consolidated; **tanka and jital**; **completed Qutub Minar** |
| **Razia** | 1236-40 | **First and only woman ruler** |
| **Balban** | 1266-87 | Strong monarchy; Mongol frontier |
| Jalaluddin Khalji | 1290-96 | Khalji founder |
| **Alauddin Khalji** | 1296-1316 | **Market control**, military reforms, southern expeditions |
| **Muhammad bin Tughlaq** | 1325-51 | **Token currency**; capital to **Daulatabad** |
| **Firoz Shah Tughlaq** | 1351-88 | **Canals**, public works, Ashokan pillars |
| Khizr Khan | 1414-21 | Sayyid founder |
| Bahlol Lodi | 1451-89 | Lodi founder |
| **Sikandar Lodi** | 1489-1517 | **Founded Agra** |
#### 3.1 Key Facts by Dynasty
- **Aibak**: born in **Turkistan**; capital **Lahore**; patron of **Hasan Nizami** and **Fakhr-i-Mudabbir**.
- **Iltutmish**: "**slave of a slave**" (slave of Aibak, who was Ghori's slave); married Aibak's daughter; took Delhi after **Aram Shah's** death; **first Sultan to shift his capital to Delhi**.
- **Qutub Minar**: begun by Aibak (basement only), completed by Iltutmish; **fourth floor damaged in Firoz Shah's time**; Alauddin Khalji had **no part**.
- **Balban**: full name **Ghiyasuddin Balban**; ninth Mamluk Sultan; wazir and heir of **Nasiruddin Mahmud**.
- **Alauddin**: raised a powerful army to guard the frontier; **Amir Khusrau** in his reign.
- **MBT**: completed the **conquest of South India** (begun by Alauddin through **Malik Kafur**); **first to introduce token currency**; **Diwan-i-Kohi** (agriculture ministry); **Ibn Battuta** visited in his reign; **first Sultan to join the public celebration of Holi**.
- **Badauni** (Muntakhab-ut-Tawarikh) on MBT's death: "the king was **freed from his people** and they from their king".
- **Firoz Tughlaq**: **maximum canals**; brought **Ashoka's Pillar** to Delhi; **Translation Department** (Sanskrit to Persian); **Employment Exchange** for the unemployed; planted **1,200 orchards** near Delhi and restored **30 gardens** of Alauddin.
- **Sikandar Lodi**: founded **Agra (1504)** and made it the capital (not Delhi).
### 4. SUCCESSION ORDER
| Dynasty | Order |
|---|---|
| **Slave** | Aibak → **Aram Shah** → **Iltutmish** → **Ruknuddin Firoz** → **Razia** → Bahram Shah → Alauddin Masud → **Nasiruddin Mahmud** → **Balban** → **Kaiqubad** |
| **Khalji** | Jalaluddin → **Alauddin** → Shihabuddin (child set up by **Malik Kafur**) → **Mubarak Shah** |
| **Tughlaq** | Ghiyasuddin → **MBT** → **Firoz Shah** → ... → **Nasiruddin Mahmud Shah** |
| **Sayyid** | **Khizr Khan** → **Mubarak Shah** (murdered 1434) → **Muhammad Shah** (d. 1445) → **Alam Shah** |
| **Lodi** | **Bahlol** → son **Sikandar** (1489-1517) → son **Ibrahim** (1517-26) |
!TRAP Two Nasiruddin Mahmuds: one in the Slave line (before Balban), one the last Tughlaq.

### 7. FIRST AND LAST RULERS
| Item | Ruler |
|---|---|
| **First Sultan** | **Qutbuddin Aibak** (master: **Muhammad Ghori**) |
| First woman ruler | **Razia** (daughter of Iltutmish) |
| First Afghan ruler | **Bahlol Lodi** (all earlier were Turks) |
| Last Slave ruler | **Kaiqubad**, killed 1290 (infant son **Kayumars** nominal last) |
| Last Khalji | **Qutbuddin Mubarak Shah** (1316-20), killed by **Khusrau Khan** |
| Last Tughlaq | **Nasiruddin Mahmud Shah** (1394-1412) |
| Last Sayyid | **Alauddin Alam Shah**, handed throne to Bahlol |
| Last Delhi Sultan | **Ibrahim Lodi**, defeated at **Panipat 1526** |
- **Portuguese arrival**: **Vasco da Gama** at **Calicut, 17 May 1498** → Delhi Sultan **Sikandar Lodi**; Calicut under the **Zamorin**; Vijayanagara under **Immadi Narasimha**.
### 8. BATTLES AND RIVALS OF ILTUTMISH
| Battle | Year | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Second Tarain** | 1192 | **Ghori** beat **Prithviraj**; North India opened |
| **Chandawar** | 1194 | Ghori beat **Jaichand** of Kannauj |
| **Tarain** | 1215-16 | Iltutmish beat **Tajuddin Yalduz** of Ghazni |
| **Khatoli** | 1517 | **Rana Sanga** of Mewar badly defeated **Ibrahim Lodi** |
- Iltutmish's rivals: **Yalduz** (Ghazni), **Nasiruddin Qabacha** (Multan and Sindh, crushed **1228**), **Ali Mardan** (Lakhnauti). **Malik Altunia was not one**.
### 9. MONGOLS AND TIMUR
- **Chengiz (Genghis) Khan** reached the **Indus in 1221**, under **Iltutmish**, chasing Khwarezm prince **Jalaluddin Mangbarni**; Iltutmish **refused him shelter**.
- **Balban**: held the north-west frontier with a **chain of forts** and a strong army; **Alauddin adopted and completed** this policy.
- **Timur (1398)**: under **Nasiruddin Mahmud**, the last Tughlaq, **not MBT**; **Khizr Khan** = Timur's viceroy.
### 10. BREAKAWAY KINGDOMS
| Kingdom | How it broke away |
|---|---|
| **Bengal** | **Tughril Khan** revolted (1279) against Amin Khan's appointment; crushed by **Balban**. **Haji Iliyas** made Bengal independent; Firoz failed in two campaigns (second **1359-60**) |
| **Vijayanagara (1336)** | MBT sent **Harihara and Bukka** against Deccan rebels; they returned to Hinduism under **Vidyaranya**, founded **Anegondi**, which grew into Vijayanagara |
| **Bahmani (1347)** | Revolt against Delhi in the Deccan |
| **Jaunpur (Sharqi)** | Founded by **Khwaja Jahan** under the last Tughlaq; **annexed by Bahlol (1476)** |
- **AP angle**: Delhi invaded Andhra **five times (1303-1323)**; in **1323 Ulugh Khan** (future MBT) captured **Prataparudra II**; Kakatiya kingdom annexed.
- **Rajon ki Baoli**: four-tier stepwell in Mehrauli Archaeological Park, built by **Daulat Khan Lodi in 1506** (Sikandar Lodi's reign); restored by **ASI**, **World Monuments Fund India** and **TCS Foundation**.
