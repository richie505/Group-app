## U0087 | CHOLA LOCAL SELF-GOVERNMENT – UTTARAMERUR INSCRIPTIONS, UR, SABHA, NAGARAM, VARIYAMS
### 1. VILLAGE AUTONOMY – THE CHOLA HALLMARK
- The **Cholas** are the dynasty known for **village autonomy** and elaborate **local self-government** (MPPSC 2021, BPSC 2020).
- **K.A. Nilakanta Sastri**: a remarkable example of **"grassroots democracy"**; often called an early **Panchayati Raj** system (village governments run by self-governing elected panchayats, developed by the Dravidian Cholas).
- Every village had its own committee – the **Sabha**; special feature of the Chola era = autonomy of village administration.
- We know more about the Cholas than their predecessors because **Rajaraja I** and **Rajendra I** had long **inscriptions on temple walls**.
#### 1.1 Dynasty Comparison
| Dynasty | Local government |
|---|---|
| **Cholas** | Culmination: elected assemblies, committees, lottery |
| Pallavas | Sabha and ur assemblies already appear; Cholas built on this base |
| **Badami Chalukyas** | **Highly centralised** |
| Rashtrakutas | Village assemblies had a significant role in administration and disputes |
- Distractors: Chalukya, Pallava, Pandya, Chera, Solanki, Parmar.
!TRAP Pallava, Chola and Rashtrakuta records show active village assemblies; the Badami Chalukya polity was highly centralised.
### 2. TERRITORIAL UNITS AND NADU ASSEMBLY
- **Ur** = village; **Nadu** = group of villages.
- **Nattar** = assembly of the nadu, made up of leading **vellala** (peasant) landholders; handled **revenue collection** and **disputes above village level**.
- The nadu's **nagaram** (market town) worked alongside it.
- Nattar is a Chola body, **not** a 73rd Amendment body.
### 3. VILLAGE ASSEMBLIES → TYPE
| Assembly | Where | Composition |
|---|---|---|
| **Ur** | Ordinary, **non-brahmadeya (vellanvagai)** villages | General assembly of local residents/landholders; land sale, gift, tax exemptions; generally fewer than ten members |
| **Sabha / Mahasabha** | **Brahmadeya** villages (agraharas, rent-free) | **Brahmans only**; criteria: property, family, learning, good conduct |
| **Nagaram** | Market and commercial towns | **Merchants** |
| **Variyams** | Within the sabha | Village committees |
!TRAP Ur = non-brahmadeya; Sabha = brahmadeya (Brahmans only); Nagaram = merchants.
### 4. UTTARAMERUR INSCRIPTIONS
- Two inscriptions of **Parantaka I**, dated **919 and 921 CE**.
- Place: **Uttaramerur**, **Kanchipuram district** (Tamil Nadu).
- **Most detailed record of village administration in ancient India**; main source for Chola village government (**APPSC G2 2000, 2003**).
- Contents: the sabha, qualifications and disqualifications, the **kudavolai** system and the **variyams**.
!TRAP Source is Uttaramerur – not Kudimiamalai, Gangaikondacholapuram, Silappadikaram, Tolkappiyam or Periya Puranam. The Thanjavur inscription of Rajaraja I is about military feats (Chera navy at Kandalur), not village government.
#### 4.1 Qualifications and Disqualifications
| Qualifications | Disqualifications |
|---|---|
| Own at least **1/4 veli** of land | **Failed to submit accounts** |
| **Own house** (residence) | Committed sins |
| Age **35–70** | **Stolen property** |
| Knowledge of the **Vedas** | **Served on a committee in the past three years** |
### 5. ELECTION PROCEDURE – KUDAVOLAI
1. Village divided into **30 wards (kudumbus)**.
2. Names of eligible persons written on **palm leaves**.
3. Leaves put in a **pot (kudam)**.
4. A young boy/girl **drew the names by lot**.
5. Chosen members served a term of **360 days**; removed at once if found guilty of an offence.
- Those who had served in the **last three years** were barred.
- **Aug 2025**: PM Modi cited the Uttaramerur **"ballot pot"** (kudavolai) as proof of India's early democratic traditions.
### 6. TERMS → MEANING (APPSC MATCH)
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Variyam** | Committee system / working committee overseeing village work |
| **Kudumbu / Kudumba** | **Ward** |
| **Kudavolai** | **Lottery** (palm-leaf ballots in a pot) |
| **Perumakkal** | **Members of the village assembly** |
| **Variyapperumakkal** | Elected committee members |
!KEY APPSC G1 2022, 2023 match: Variyam = committee; Kudumbu = ward; Kudavolai = lottery; Perumakkal = assembly member.
### 7. VARIYAMS → FUNCTION
| Variyam | Function |
|---|---|
| **Samvatsara** variyam | Annual committee – annual works; also justice |
| **Eri** variyam | **Tanks**; distribution of water to fields |
| **Pon** variyam | **Gold** – finance, currency checking |
| **Thotta** variyam | **Gardens** / orchards |
| **Panchavara** variyam | **Standing committee** (supervision) |
- Uttaramerur committees covered irrigation, gardens, justice and finance; others handled law and order.
### 8. POWERS OF ASSEMBLIES
- **Land**: assembly was absolute proprietor of village lands and newly acquired lands.
- **Finance**: collected **royal dues in cash, kind or both**; levied taxes; **raised loans**; **auctioned defaulters' land**.
- **Tanks and water**: through the eri variyam.
- **Justice**: law and order and disputes through committees.
- **Temples**: Rajaraja I directed the **Cholamandalam sabhas** to serve the **Brihadeshvara** temple.
### 9. CHOLA ASSEMBLY VS 73RD AMENDMENT
| Point | Chola village assembly | 73rd Amendment PRIs |
|---|---|---|
| Selection | Lottery (kudavolai) among qualified | Direct election (**Art. 243C**) |
| Eligibility | Property, age 35–70, Vedic learning | Universal adult franchise; age 21 |
| Term | 360 days; 3-year bar on re-entry | **5 years** (**Art. 243E**); election within **6 months** of dissolution |
| Committees | Variyams | Standing committees under state law |
| Women | **No quota** | At least **1/3** seats and chairperson posts (**Art. 243D**); SC/ST reservation |
- **Art. 243A** = Gram Sabha; **3 tiers**, middle tier optional where state population is **not over 20 lakh**.
#### 9.1 Criticism
- Democratic in form but **elitist**: sabhas limited to propertied, Vedic-learned Brahmans; no universal adult franchise.
- **Burton Stein**: **segmentary state** – local autonomy stayed intact at the periphery.
- AP parallel: Kakatiya and Velanati inscriptions show village **mahajana** assemblies – a weaker parallel.
