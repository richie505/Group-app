## U0813-7 | ANDHRA PRADESH - BUDGET AND SOCIO-ECONOMIC SURVEY HIGHLIGHTS OF THE CURRENT YEAR (2026-27)
### 1. AP BUDGET 2026-27 - SIZE AND DEFICITS
#### 1.1 Main Budget
- Main Budget presented by Finance Minister **Payyavula Keshav** (his third) on **14 February 2026**.
- Total outlay: **₹3,32,205 crore** (about ₹3.32 lakh crore), nearly the same as 2025-26.
- Revenue expenditure: **₹2,56,143 crore**.
- **Revenue deficit** target 2026-27: **₹22,003 crore = 1.1% of GSDP** (down from ₹41,118 crore in 2025-26 RE).
- **Fiscal deficit** target 2026-27: **₹75,868 crore = 3.8% of GSDP** (2025-26 RE: 4.6%; 2025-26 BE: 4.4%).
- GSDP projected for 2026-27: **₹19,75,073 crore** (current prices), growth of **12%**.
- Centre's fiscal-deficit target 2026-27 = **4.3% of GDP** (compare with AP's 3.8% of GSDP).
!TRAP Main Budget = Payyavula Keshav; Agriculture Budget = K. Atchannaidu. Both on 14 Feb 2026, both their third.

### 2. AGRICULTURE AND ALLIED SECTORS BUDGET 2026-27
#### 2.1 Basic facts
- A **separate** Agriculture and Allied Sectors Budget, presented by Agriculture Minister **K. Atchannaidu** (his **third**) on **14 Feb 2026**.
- Outlay: about **₹53,753 crore**.
- **Fisheries** growth target 2026-27: **20%**.
- Agriculture share of GSDP: **33.20%** (about one-third) = ₹5,39,454 crore of ₹17,62,357 crore (2025-26 AE, current prices).

### 3. SWARNA ANDHRA @2047 AND OTHER VISION DOCUMENTS
- **Swarna Andhra @2047** vision document unveiled by CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu** on **13 December 2024** at Vijayawada; theme **"10 Principles - One Vision"**.
- **10 guiding principles = Padi Sutralu**, among them **zero poverty**, employment generation, skill and human resource development, **water security** (river interlinking), agri-tech, clean energy and energy cost optimisation, logistics, and deep-tech.
- Aim: a **wealthy, healthy and happy** (WHH) Andhra Pradesh.
- Target: a **$2.4 trillion economy by 2047**, with sustained growth of **15%** a year or more.
- **P4** = **Public-Private-People Partnership**, linked to the zero-poverty goal.
- **AP State Development Planning Society (APSDPS)** is the main execution body for the roadmap.
!PAIR Swarna Andhra @2047 | 10 Padi Sutralu | $2.4 trillion || P4 | Public-Private-People Partnership | Zero poverty
### 4. GSDP, PER CAPITA INCOME AND DISTRICT DATA
- GSDP 2025-26 (Advance Estimates, current prices): **₹17.62 lakh crore**, up from ₹15.91 lakh crore = nominal growth **10.75%**.
- **Per capita income** 2025-26 (AE): **₹2,94,507**, up from ₹2,66,699 (about **10.43%** growth).
- Sector share: **agriculture 33.20%** of GSDP (2025-26 AE).
- District: **Visakhapatnam** = highest per capita income district (₹6,58,964, 2025-26 AE); **Krishna** next, with growth of 17.85%; Rayalaseema districts lag behind.
### 5. REVENUE RECEIPTS 2026-27 - OWN TAX vs CENTRAL TRANSFERS
#### 5.1 Receipts
- Revenue receipts **₹2,34,140 crore** = **own resources 59% + central transfers 41%**.
- Own tax revenue **₹1,25,846 crore** (6.4% of GSDP).
| Own-tax source | 2026-27 |
|---|---|
| **SGST** (largest) | **₹45,078 crore (about 36%)** |
| State Excise | ₹30,067 crore |
| VAT | ₹24,126 crore |
| Stamps and registration | ₹18,701 crore |
- **Revenue-deficit grants ended under the 16th Finance Commission.**
#### 5.2 Trends
- Own revenue: ₹93,354 crore (2023-24) → ₹96,127 crore (2024-25); target ₹1,34,208 crore for 2025-26.
- 2025-26 RE: own tax ₹98,025 crore vs central transfers ₹1,04,493 crore (transfers larger).
- Commercial taxes up **16%** YoY in June 2026 (Q1 FY27 ₹15,323 crore); SGST and commercial-tax revenue at record highs in June 2026; CM said the economy is "back on track" (Aug 2026).
!TRAP Largest own-tax source = **SGST**, not State Excise or VAT.
### 6. DEBT, GUARANTEES AND PAST DEFICITS
- Outstanding debt **₹6,35,177 crore = 36.04% of GSDP** (2025-26 RE); expected **₹7,11,094 crore** by end of 2026-27; steady at about **36%** of GSDP (vs 33% in 2021-22).
- AP's ₹11,000 crore of **50-year interest-free capex loans** are outside this debt figure.
- **Guarantees** availed about **₹3,11,182 crore** (outstanding principal ₹1,77,034 crore) → sign of growing **off-budget borrowing**.
- **2024-25 actuals** (Finance Accounts): revenue deficit ₹60,285 crore = **3.79%**; fiscal deficit ₹81,362 crore = **5.11%** of GSDP.
- Criticism: debt about 36% of GSDP, ₹1.28 lakh crore off-budget debt, heavy guarantees, pensions and Super Six costs.
