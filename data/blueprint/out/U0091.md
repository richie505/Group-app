## U0091 | HOYSALAS – VISHNUVARDHANA AND RAMANUJA; BELUR, HALEBID AND SOMANATHAPURA TEMPLES
### 1. CAPITALS
| Name | Point |
|---|---|
| **Belur** | Earlier Hoysala capital (Hassan district) |
| **Dwarasamudra** | Main Hoysala capital (UPPSC 2014) |
| **Halebid / Halebidu** | Modern name of Dwarasamudra (UPPSC 2003); **Hassan district, Karnataka** |
- Halebidu = **"old city"**: the name came after Dwarasamudra was sacked by the Delhi armies.
#### 1.1 Capitals of Southern Dynasties
| Dynasty | Capital |
|---|---|
| Yadavas | **Devagiri** |
| Kakatiyas | **Warangal** |
| Hoysalas | **Dwarasamudra** |
| Pandyas | **Madurai** |
| Pallavas | **Kanchi** |
!TRAP Dwarasamudra = old name; Halebid = modern name. Not the other way round.
### 2. RULERS AND ACHIEVEMENTS
| Ruler | Achievement |
|---|---|
| **Nripa Kama II** | Early chief under the Western Chalukyas |
| **Vishnuvardhana** | Expanded the kingdom; took **Talakad** from the Cholas; built Belur |
| **Veera Ballala II** (1170–1220) | Defeated **Yadava Bhillama**; made the Hoysalas **independent** |
| **Narasimha II** (1220–1254) | Fought the Yadavas |
| **Narasimha III** (1254–1295) | Defeated by Pandya **Maravarman Kulasekhara**; Somanathapura built in his reign |
| **Ballala III** (1295–1342) | Beat Yadava Ramachandra's forces (1305); defeated by Kakatiya commander Pedarudru after 1316; last great king |
- Region: present **Karnataka** and most of the Tamil region; **independent by the close of the 12th century**; ended at the **beginning of the 14th century**.
!TRAP Hoysala, Gahadavala, Kakatiya, Yadava – none set up their kingdoms in the early 8th century AD.
### 3. VISHNUVARDHANA AND RAMANUJA
- Original name **Bittideva (Bittiga)**.
- Converted from **Jainism to Vaishnavism** under **Ramanuja**, who had fled **Chola persecution** into Hoysala land.
- Queen **Shantala Devi** remained a **Jain**.
- Defeated the Cholas at **Talakad** → title **Talakadugonda**; built Belur temple (**1117**).
- **Aug 2026:** a **Veeragallu** (hero stone) with a Kannada inscription found at **Neeragunda, Arsikere taluk** (unearthed 28 June 2026) may settle the disputed date of Vishnuvardhana's death.
### 4. TEMPLES – BUILDER, DEITY, SITE
| Temple | Site | Deity | Builder / Year |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chennakeshava** | Belur | **Vishnu** | **Vishnuvardhana**, 1117 |
| **Hoysaleswara** | Halebid | **Shiva** | **Ketamalla**, officer of Vishnuvardhana, c. **1121** |
| **Keshava** | Somanathapura | **Vishnu** | **Somanatha**, general of Narasimha III, **1268** |
- Hoysaleswara: built in dark schist; name means **"Lord of the Hoysalas"**.
- Keshava, Somanathapura = **best-preserved star-shaped plan** (UPSC).
- Monuments at **Halebid and Belur** = Hoysala (UPSC 2001).
!TRAP Hoysaleswara = Shiva (not Vishnu); Belur and Somanathapura = Vishnu.
### 5. HOYSALA STYLE
| Feature | Hoysala practice |
|---|---|
| Style family | **Vesara** in exam keys (hybrid, neither fully Nagara nor Dravida); also called an evolution of Dravida |
| Ground plan | **Stellate (star-shaped)**, on a raised **jagati** about **1 m** high |
| Stone | Soft **chloritic schist (soapstone)** – jewel-like carving |
| Pillars | **Lathe-turned**, finely polished (not rough); 3–4 sections on one base, leaping animal heads → influenced Vijayanagara |
| Halls | Open mantapas; kalyanis, pushkaranis |
| Sculpture | Friezes; **Madanika (Salabhanjika)** brackets at **Belur** |
- Sculptors: **Jakanachari** (Belur), **Mallitamma** (Somanathapura).
- Star plan began under the **Chalukyas (6th c.)**, peaked under the Hoysalas (**12th c.**); Kalyani Chalukyas passed on soapstone and star plans.
### 6. UNESCO SACRED ENSEMBLES OF THE HOYSALAS
- Inscribed on the World Heritage List in **2023**.
- Three components: **Chennakeshava (Belur), Hoysaleswara (Halebid), Keshava (Somanathapura)**.
### 7. CONFLICTS AND DECLINE
| Opponent | Episode |
|---|---|
| Yadavas of Devagiri | Constant wars; **Singhana** defeated the Hoysalas; **Mahadeva** Yadava was defeated by them |
| Pandyas | Defeated Narasimha III |
| Malik Kafur | Took Dwarasamudra in **1311**; his campaign hit Yadavas, Kakatiyas, Hoysalas, Pandyas |
- **1342:** Ballala III killed by **Ghiyas-ud-din**, Sultan of Madurai, at **Kannanur**; Hoysala land passed to **Vijayanagara**.

### 10. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS

2. Converted by **Ramanuja**; queen Shantala stayed Jain.
3. **Talakadugonda** = victory over Cholas at Talakad.
4. Hoysaleswara builder = **Ketamalla**, not the king himself.
5. Somanathapura builder = **Somanatha**, general of Narasimha III.
6. Madanikas = **Belur**, not Somanathapura.
7. Stone = **soapstone**, not granite; pillars lathe-turned and polished.
8. Veera Ballala II beat **Bhillama**; Narasimha III lost to **Pandyas**.
