## U0723 | EVOLUTION OF THE INDIAN SPACE PROGRAMME - INCOSPAR, VIKRAM SARABHAI, KEY MILESTONES
### 1. INSTITUTIONAL BEGINNINGS
#### 1.1 INCOSPAR, ISRO, Department of Space
| Body | Year | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **INCOSPAR** (Indian National Committee for Space Research) | **1962** | Under the **Department of Atomic Energy**; set up with **Homi Bhabha**, chaired by **Vikram Sarabhai** |
| **ISRO** | **15 Aug 1969** | Superseded INCOSPAR |
| **Department of Space** + **Space Commission** | **1972** | ISRO brought under DoS |
!TRAP INCOSPAR (1962) came first; ISRO (1969) replaced it; DoS and Space Commission came in 1972.
#### 1.2 TERLS, Thumba
- **Thumba Equatorial Rocket Launching Station**, Thumba (**Kerala**); chosen because it lies close to the **magnetic equator** (study of the equatorial electrojet).
- First sounding rocket: **Nike-Apache** (American-built), **21 Nov 1963**.
- **RH-75**: first **Indian-made** sounding rocket, **20 Nov 1967**.
- Satellite Telecommunication Earth Station, **Ahmedabad**: **1 Jan 1967**.
### 2. EARLY SATELLITES AND EXPERIMENTS
| Date | Satellite / experiment | Vehicle / site |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Aug 1975-76 | **SITE**: first use of space technology for **education**; with **NASA**, **ATS-6** satellite; about **2,400 villages**, nearly **5 million** people | NASA ATS-6 |
| **19 Apr 1975** | **Aryabhata**: first Indian satellite; **360 kg**; India entered the space age | **Soviet Kosmos-3M**, **Kapustin Yar** (USSR) |
| 1977-79 | **STEP** (Satellite Telecommunication Experiments Project) | Franco-German **Symphonie** satellite |
| 1979-81 | **Bhaskara I** (1979) and II: experimental earth observation | Soviet Intercosmos rocket, **Baikonur** |
| **18 Jul 1980** | **Rohini**: first satellite on an **Indian rocket**; India **6th** nation | **SLV-3**, **Sriharikota** (AP) |
| **19 Jun 1981** | **APPLE**: first experimental **geostationary** communication satellite | **Ariane-1**, **Kourou**, French Guiana |
| 1983 | INSAT-1B | US Space Shuttle **Challenger** |
| 1988 | **IRS-1A**: first operational remote sensing | Soviet Vostok, Baikonur |
- Aryabhata was conceived by Sarabhai, launched after his death; satellite team led by **U.R. Rao**.
- Chronology: **SITE (1975) → STEP (1977) → APPLE (1981)**; satellites **Aryabhata → Bhaskara → Rohini → APPLE**.
!TRAP NASA's ATS-6 was used for SITE, not to launch APPLE. APPLE went up on Ariane-1.
!PAIR Aryabhata | Kosmos-3M | Kapustin Yar || Rohini | SLV-3 | Sriharikota
### 3. KEY MILESTONES (1994-2026)
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1994 | **PSLV** operational - the workhorse |
| 2008 | **Chandrayaan-1** (India's first Moon mission spacecraft) |
| 2014 | **Mangalyaan**: Mars orbit on **maiden attempt**; **first Asian**, India **4th** country |
| 2017 | **104 satellites** on one PSLV |
| 2019 | **Mission Shakti** (ASAT test); **NSIL** formed; Chandrayaan-2 |
| 2023 | **Chandrayaan-3**: first landing near the **lunar south pole**; **Aditya-L1** to the L1 point |
| 30 Dec 2024 / 16 Jan 2025 | **SpaDeX** launched on **PSLV-C60** / docking done; India **4th** (after US, Russia, China) |
| 24 Dec 2025 | **LVM3-M6** lifts **BlueBird Block-2** (**6,100 kg**), heaviest payload from Indian soil |
| 18 Jul 2026 | Skyroot's **Vikram-1**: first privately developed Indian rocket to reach orbit |
- **SpaDeX** = **Space Docking** technology demonstrator: two small spacecraft launched by PSLV.
!KEY India is 4th in both: Mars orbit (Mangalyaan) and space docking (SpaDeX).
### 4. VIKRAM SARABHAI AND ISRO CHAIRMEN
#### 4.1 Vikram Sarabhai (1919-71)
- **Father of the Indian Space Programme**; first ISRO Chairman till his death in **1971**.
- **Padma Bhushan 1966**; **Padma Vibhushan posthumously 1972**; succeeded **Homi Bhabha** at the Atomic Energy Commission (**1966**).
- **COSPAR Vikram Sarabhai Medal** (since 1990): 2026 winner **Annapurni Subramaniam** - 4th Indian, first Indian woman.

### 6. INDIAN SPACE VISION 2047
- **Bharatiya Antariksh Station** by **2035**; Vision horizon **2047**.
- **Gaganyaan**: crew of **3**, **400 km** orbit, **3 days**; splashdown in Indian waters.
- Next missions: **Chandrayaan-4**, **Chandrayaan-5**, **Venus Orbiter Mission** (Rs **1,236 crore**, approved).
- Space economy: **56** active space assets; FDI up to **100%** in non-sensitive segments; over **400** foreign satellites launched; IN-SPACe chairman **Pawan Kumar Goenka** says ISRO's role is "by no means diminishing".
- AP angle: Rohini (1980) and all orbital launches from **Sriharikota (SHAR)**, AP.

### 9. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. Aryabhata was launched by a **Soviet** rocket from **Kapustin Yar**, not from Sriharikota.
2. Bhaskara launched from **Baikonur**; APPLE from **Kourou**.
3. First **Indian-made** sounding rocket = **RH-75 (1967)**; Nike-Apache (1963) was American.
4. **ATS-6** served SITE, not APPLE.
5. Chandrayaan-1 = Madhavan Nair; Mangalyaan = Radhakrishnan; Chandrayaan-3 = Somanath.
6. India was the **6th** nation to orbit a satellite with its own rocket.
7. INCOSPAR was under the **DAE**, not the Department of Space.
8. Bharatiya Antariksh Station target is **2035**, not 2047.
