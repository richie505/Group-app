## U0071 | GURJARA-PRATIHARAS – NAGABHATA I AND II, MIHIRA BHOJA; RESISTANCE TO ARAB EXPANSION
### 1. NAME AND ORIGIN
- **"Pratihara" = door-keeper**: claimed descent from **Lakshmana**, Rama's door-keeper brother.
- **Agnikula legend** (**Prithviraj Raso**): **Pratiharas, Chaulukyas, Paramaras, Chauhans** born from the fire-pit at **Mount Abu**.
- "Gurjara" from their link with the **Gurjars**, first mentioned in the **Aihole inscription** of Pulakesin II and Banabhatta's **Harshacharita**.
- Ruled much of north India from the mid-8th to the 11th century.
### 2. CAPITALS
| Stage | Capital |
|---|---|
| Early Gurjara seat | **Bhinmal** (Rajasthan) |
| Nagabhata I | **Ujjain (Avanti)** |
| From Nagabhata II; held firmly by Mihira Bhoja (c. 836) | **Kannauj (Mahodaya)** |
- Barah copper plate of Bhoja mentions a camp (skandhavara) at Mahodaya.
### 3. RULERS IN ORDER
| Ruler | Period | Achievement |
|---|---|---|
| **Nagabhata I** | c. 730–760 | **Founder**; resisted the Arabs |
| **Vatsaraja** | c. 775–800 | Took Kannauj, beat Dharmapala; defeated by **Dhruva** – start of tripartite struggle |
| **Nagabhata II** | c. 800–833 | Ousted **Chakrayudha**; beat Dharmapala near **Monghyr**; made **Kannauj** capital; defeated by **Govinda III** |
| **Mihira Bhoja (Bhoja I)** | c. 836–885 | Zenith; greatest Pratihara king |
| **Mahendrapala I** | c. 885–910 | "Maharajadhiraja of Aryavarta"; patron of Rajashekhara |
| **Mahipala I** | c. 912–944 | Kannauj looted by **Indra III** c. 916 (not the reverse) |
- Order: **Vatsaraja → Nagabhata II → Mihira Bhoja → Mahendrapala I → Mahipala**.
!TRAP **Bharatrabhatta I** is not a Pratihara ruler. A later Pratihara king **Devapala** (c. 948–954) is not the Pala Devapala.

### 4. MIHIRA BHOJA
- **Son of Ramabhadra** and queen **Appa Devi**; **grandson** (not son) of Nagabhata II.
- Titles **Adivaraha** and **Prabhasa**; **Vaishnava**; Adivaraha legend on his **coins**.
- Captured **Kannauj c. 836 AD**.
- Early defeats by Palas, Rashtrakutas, Kalachuris, then a comeback; checked by **Sankaravarman of Kashmir**, Rashtrakuta **Krishna II** and Pala **Devapala**.
### 5. ARAB TRAVELLERS
| Traveller | Statement |
|---|---|
| **Sulaiman** (mid-9th c., Bhoja I's reign) | Called the kingdom/king **Juzr (Al-Juzr)**; praised army, wealth, security and the **best cavalry** in India; named Rashtrakuta **Amoghavarsha I** one of the **four great kings** of the world |
| **Al-Masudi** | Called the king **Baura** |
!TRAP Juzr = Sulaiman; Baura = Al-Masudi.
### 6. RESISTANCE TO ARAB EXPANSION
- **Muhammad-bin-Qasim** took **Sindh in 712** (711–12), defeating and killing **Raja Dahir**.
- Arab raids under **Junaid's** officers pushed towards Ujjain; the **Gwalior inscription** of Mihira Bhoja says **Nagabhata I** repulsed the **mleccha** (Arab) army.
- Result: Arab rule stayed **confined to Sindh and Multan**; the Pratiharas were the main barrier.
### 7. RAJASHEKHARA
- Sanskrit poet and dramatist at the court of **Mahendrapala I** (also **Mahipala I**).
| Work | His? |
|---|---|

| **Karpuramanjari** | Yes |
| **Viddhasalabhanjika** | Yes |
| **Prabandhachintamani** | **No – Merutunga** |
### 8. DECLINE
- **Indra III's** raid on Kannauj (c. 916).
- **Mahmud of Ghazni** attacked Kannauj in **1018**.
- Breakaway feudatories: **Chandelas** (Khajuraho), Chauhans, Paramaras, Chaulukyas.

### 11. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. Mihira Bhoja = **grandson**, not son, of Nagabhata II.
2. **Nagabhata I** = founder, Arabs; **Nagabhata II** = Kannauj capital.
3. Vatsaraja lost to **Dhruva**; Nagabhata II to **Govinda III**.
4. **Prabandhachintamani** = Merutunga.
5. Juzr (Sulaiman) vs Baura (Al-Masudi).
6. Sulaiman's "four great kings" = **Amoghavarsha I**, a Rashtrakuta.
7. **Indra III** looted Kannauj, defeating Mahipala I.
8. Adivaraha = Vishnu devotion, not Shaiva.
