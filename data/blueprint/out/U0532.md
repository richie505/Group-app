## U0532 | FINANCIAL INCLUSION – PMJDY, MUDRA, MICROFINANCE, SHG-BANK LINKAGE, DIGITAL PAYMENTS AND UPI
### 1. FINANCIAL INCLUSION – MEANING AND COMMITTEES
#### 1.1 Meaning
- **Financial inclusion**: basic banking and financial services to **everybody, whatever their income or savings**.
- **Rangarajan definition**: access to financial services and **timely and adequate credit** for **vulnerable groups** (weaker sections, low-income groups) at **affordable cost**.
- Aims: take banking to the poor and **underserved and rural areas** (not metros); credit for weaker sections; **financial literacy**; formal payments and remittances; insurance. It **raises** financial security.
!TRAP **Shrinking of banking infrastructure** is NOT an aim of financial inclusion.
| Financial inclusion | Financial exclusion |
|---|---|
| Access to a bank account, credit, insurance | No or limited banking/insurance access |
| Payments and remittances | Limited payment facilities |
| Financial literacy | Lack of awareness |
#### 1.2 Earlier Inclusion Measures
- **Lead Bank Scheme (LBS)**: a financial inclusion measure.
- **Swabhimaan (10 February 2011)**: banking to the doorsteps of the rural poor.
#### 1.3 Committees → Chair → Subject
| Chair | Year | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| **C. Rangarajan** | Formed **26 June 2006**; final report **4 January 2008** | **Committee on Financial Inclusion** – widening banking access |
| **Y.H. Malegam** | 2010 | **Microfinance sector** after the AP crisis (RBI) |
| **Nachiket Mor** | Formed **23 Sept 2013** | **Comprehensive Financial Services for Small Businesses and Low-Income Households**; account for every household; basis of **PMJDY**, **payments banks** and small finance banks |
| **Deepak Mohanty** | 2015 | Medium-term path on financial inclusion (RBI) |
| **Ratan Watal** | 2016 | **Digital payments** (MoF) |
| **N. Chandrababu Naidu** | 2016-17 | **Chief Ministers' Committee on Digital Payments**; cashless transactions after demonetisation |
- **FSDC** is chaired by the **Union Finance Minister**, not Rangarajan.
- **National Strategy for Financial Inclusion 2019-24** (RBI).
!TRAP Malegam = microfinance; Watal = digital payments; Naidu = CMs' committee; Mor = payments banks.
### 2. PRADHAN MANTRI JAN DHAN YOJANA (PMJDY)
#### 2.1 Launch and Objective
- **National Mission for Financial Inclusion**: announced **15 August 2014**, launched **28 August 2014**; nodal: **DFS, Ministry of Finance**.
- Objective: **a bank account for every household** (APPSC GS 2024).
- Tenets: **banking the unbanked, securing the unsecured, funding the unfunded**.
- Groundwork: **Nachiket Mor Committee (2013)**.
#### 2.2 Features
| Feature | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Account | **Zero-balance BSBD** account; **earns interest** |
| **RuPay debit card** | **₹2 lakh accident insurance** |
| **Overdraft** | Up to **₹10,000** (after 6 months of good conduct) |
| Life cover | ₹30,000 |
| Others | **DBT**, financial literacy; linked to **PMJJBY, PMSBY, APY** |
#### 2.3 Coverage (19 Aug 2026)
| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Total accounts | **59.09 crore** (14.72 crore in 2015) |
| Rural/semi-urban | 45.95 crore (about three-fourths) |
| Women | 32.92 crore (**over half**) |
| Deposits | **₹3,16,514 crore** (₹15,670 crore in Mar 2015) |
- 90.2% of operative accounts Aadhaar-seeded (30 Jul 2025). Concern: **dormant Jan Dhan accounts**.
### 3. SOCIAL SECURITY TRIO
- **PMJJBY, PMSBY and APY** launched together on **9 May 2015** (Budget Speech 2015-16).
| Scheme | Age | Cover / benefit | Premium | Enrolment (2026) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PMJJBY** | **18-50** | **₹2 lakh life cover**, death from any cause | **₹436/yr** (revised 2022; earlier ₹330) | 27.33 crore |
| **PMSBY** | **18-70** | **₹2 lakh** accidental death/full disability; **₹1 lakh partial** | **₹20/yr** | 57.92 crore |
| **APY** | **18-40** | Pension **₹1,000-5,000/month from 60**; administered by **PFRDA** (not IRDAI); replaced Swavalamban | Age-linked; no income-tax payers after 1 Oct 2022 | 8.96 crore |
!TRAP PMJJBY = life, ₹436, 18-50; PMSBY = accident, ₹20, 18-70; APY = pension, 18-40.
### 4. MUDRA
- **PM MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)**: launched **8 April 2015**; **Ministry of Finance (DFS)**.
- **MUDRA = Micro Units Development and Refinance Agency**; MUDRA Ltd set up **18 March 2015** as a **SIDBI subsidiary**; **refinances** banks, NBFCs, MFIs.
- **Collateral-free** loans to **non-corporate, non-farm micro and small enterprises** (not large corporates); brings small entrepreneurs into the formal system.
| Category | Loan size |
|---|---|
| **Shishu** | Up to **₹50,000** |
| **Kishore** | **₹50,001 – ₹5 lakh** |
| **Tarun** | **₹5–10 lakh** |
| **Tarun Plus** | **₹10–20 lakh** (from **24 Oct 2024**, Budget 2024-25), for Tarun borrowers who repaid |
- Limit was **₹10 lakh** at launch; now **₹20 lakh**. Guarantees via **CGFMU**; **MUDRA Card** (RuPay).
- Performance: 57 crore+ loans worth ₹40 lakh crore; about two-thirds to **women**; nearly half to SC/ST/OBC.
- **Launch order**: PMJDY (28 Aug 2014) → Digital Gender Atlas (9 Mar 2015) → MUDRA (8 Apr 2015) → PMSBY (9 May 2015).
!TRAP MUDRA Ltd (18 March 2015) came **before** PMMY (8 April 2015).
### 5. MICROFINANCE
- **Microfinance services**: credit, savings, insurance, cash transfers, leasing for **low-income households**.
- **AP microfinance crisis (2010)**: coercive recoveries and borrower suicides → AP **Micro Finance Institutions (Regulation of Money Lending) Ordinance, 15 October 2010**: MFIs had to **register with a state-appointed authority**; replaced by an Act (Dec 2010).
- The crisis **changed national regulation** of MFIs → **Malegam Committee (2010)**.
- **NBFC-MFI**: a new RBI category of NBFC created on **2 December 2011**.
- **MFIN (Microfinance Institutions Network)**: self-regulatory national body of RBI-regulated MFIs, formed **October 2009**.
### 6. SHG-BANK LINKAGE
| Body / programme | Year | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **MYRADA** | Mid-1980s | **Pioneered** linkage |
| **NABARD SHG-Bank Linkage pilot** | **1992** | Launched by **NABARD**; world's **largest microfinance project** |
| **DAY-NRLM** | **2011** | **Ministry of Rural Development**; rural livelihoods (not urban); 90.90 lakh+ SHGs, 10.05 crore rural women households, repayment 98%+ |
#### 6.1 Andhra Pradesh Model
- **DWCRA** (1982-83) – early women's group model; **SERP** runs the SHG model (not NABARD directly).
- 8.50 lakh SHGs, 89.21 lakh women.
- **AP ranks 1st**: about **36% national share**, **99.66% recovery**, ~₹32,190 crore disbursed.
### 7. NPCI AND PAYMENT SYSTEMS
#### 7.1 NPCI Basics
- **National Payments Corporation of India**: set up **December 2008** by the **RBI and Indian Banks' Association (IBA)**.
- **Not-for-profit**, **Section 25 company** (Companies Act 1956) = Section 8 of the 2013 Act.
- Works under the **Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007**; umbrella body for retail payments.
#### 7.2 Milestones
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 2004 | **NFS** designed and launched by RBI's **IDRBT** |
| 2007 | **PSS Act** |
| 2008 | NPCI incorporated |
| 2009 | NPCI took over **NFS** from IDRBT |
| 2010 | **IMPS** and **AePS** |
| 2012 | **RuPay**; **NACH** |
| 2016 | **UPI** (April, **21 banks**); **BHIM**; **NETC FASTag** |
#### 7.3 Products → Purpose
| Product | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **UPI** | Instant account-to-account P2P and P2M payments via a VPA; runs on the **IMPS** framework (not NEFT); 24x7 |
| UPI 2.0 (2018) | Overdraft linking, mandates, signed QR |
| **IMPS** | 24x7 instant interbank transfer |
| **RuPay** | **Domestic card network**; cuts dependence on Visa and Mastercard |
| **AePS** | Basic banking at micro-ATMs through **Aadhaar** authentication; supports inclusion |
| **NACH** | **Bulk recurring** payments: salaries, dividends, bills |
| **NETC / FASTag** | Electronic toll collection (**RFID**) |
| **BHIM** | NPCI's **UPI app** |
| **Bharat BillPay** | Interoperable **bill payment** |
| **NFS** | **Largest interoperable ATM network**; links all ATMs (APPSC GS 2024) |
| **e-RUPI** | Person- and purpose-specific **prepaid voucher** |
| **123Pay** | UPI for **feature phones** |
- Result of UPI: **mobile wallets are not necessary** for online payments (direct bank-to-bank).
#### 7.4 RTGS vs NEFT
| Basis | RTGS | NEFT |
|---|---|---|
| Settlement | **Real-time, gross**, almost instantaneous | **Deferred net, half-hourly batches** |
| Minimum | **₹2 lakh** | **No minimum** |
- **No charge** on the beneficiary for inward RTGS credit; RBI removed NEFT/RTGS charges.

#### 8.1 New UPI Features
- **UPI LITE X** = **offline** payments; **Hello! UPI** = voice payments; **UPI123Pay** = feature phones; **Credit Line on UPI**.
#### 8.2 Progress
| UPI metric | Figure |
|---|---|

- **Over 90% of UPI users still use cash** (ES 2025-26).

### 9. DIGITAL PAYMENTS POLICY AND INDICES
#### 9.1 Nodal Ministry and MDR
- Promotion of digital payments **moved from MeitY to DFS (Ministry of Finance)** (APPSC-G1 2024).
- **MDR (Merchant Discount Rate)**: fee a bank charges a merchant for accepting card payments; shared by **issuing bank, PoS bank and payment network**.
- **Zero MDR** on **RuPay debit and BHIM-UPI** since **1 Jan 2020** (**Sec 10A, PSS Act 2007**).
- **RuPay-BHIM UPI incentive scheme**: **0.15%** on P2M payments up to **₹2,000** to small merchants; run by **DFS**; **NPCI** nodal agency.
- **Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026**: UPI stays **free for consumers**; MDR may apply to **high-value** payments (Aug 2026). Zero-MDR blamed for cost burden on PSPs.
#### 9.2 RBI Indices
| Index | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **RBI-DPI** | **Base March 2018 = 100**; published from **January 2021**; semi-annual; **5 parameters**: payment enablers, payment infrastructure (demand side), payment infrastructure (supply side), payment performance, consumer centricity; 516.76 (Sep 2025) |
| **FI-Index** | Since **2021**; annual (**July**); **0-100**; **97 indicators** (banking, investment, insurance, postal, pension); **Access 35%, Usage 45%, Quality 20%**; 67.0 (2025) |
!TRAP APPSC G1 2024: "RBI-DPI has 2021 as base year" = **FALSE**; base is **March 2018**.
### 10. JAM TRINITY AND NEW DIGITAL FINANCE ARCHITECTURE
- **JAM** = **Jan Dhan – Aadhaar – Mobile**; PMJDY is its first layer → made **DBT** possible (paperless, presence-less delivery).
- **India Stack**: Aadhaar, UPI, DigiLocker, Account Aggregator, ONDC.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Account Aggregator** | **Consent-based sharing** of financial data (bank transactions, GST records); RBI-regulated NBFC-AAs |
| **ONDC** | **Open, interoperable digital commerce network** (buyers, sellers, logistics, payments); cuts dominance of e-commerce platforms; **DPIIT** initiative |
| **OCEN** | Common protocol linking **lenders and marketplaces** for small-ticket credit |
#### 10.1 Payments Banks vs Small Finance Banks
- **Payments banks** (Nachiket Mor): deposits up to **₹1 lakh** initially (₹2 lakh from 2021); **cannot lend** → **no credit cards**; may issue ATM/debit cards and hold G-secs; **no credit risk**.
- Promoters allowed: NBFCs, individuals, corporations, **mobile phone companies**, supermarket chains, real estate co-operatives, public sector entities.
- **Small finance banks**: **50% of loans up to ₹25 lakh**.
### 11. CRYPTOCURRENCY VS DIGITAL RUPEE VS UPI
| Basis | Cryptocurrency | Digital Rupee (e₹, CBDC) | UPI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nature | Private, decentralised; **not legal tender** | **Legal tender** issued by **RBI** | **Payment system**, not a currency |
| Liability | None | **RBI** | **Commercial bank money** – your bank |
| Rules | **30% tax on gains + 1% TDS** (2022) | **RBI Act amended by Finance Act 2022** | NPCI under PSS Act 2007 |
- **Bitcoin**: created **January 2009** by **Satoshi Nakamoto**; peer-to-peer; not issued by banks or governments.
- Facebook's cryptocurrency (2019): **Libra** (later Diem).
- **Supreme Court, 2020** struck down RBI's **2018** circular barring banks from crypto; 2021 bill to ban private crypto pending.
- **e-rupee pilots**: wholesale (**e₹-W**) **1 Nov 2022**; retail (**e₹-R**) **1 Dec 2022**. Benefits: programmable, targeted subsidy, cheaper remittances.
- **Sand Dollar (Bahamas, 2020)**: world's first live retail CBDC.
!TRAP The Finance Act 2022 did **not** make crypto legal tender; e-rupee is money itself, UPI only moves bank money.
