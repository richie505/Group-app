## U0547 | PUBLIC DEBT - INTERNAL AND EXTERNAL, DEBT-GDP RATIO, SUSTAINABILITY, STATE DEBT AND OFF-BUDGET BORROWING
### 1. CENTRE'S LIABILITIES
- **Art. 292**: Union borrows on the security of the **Consolidated Fund of India**. Internal debt = **96.7%** of public debt; **G-Secs** over 90% of Central debt.
| Head | Items |
|---|---|
| Internal - marketable | **Market loans** (G-Secs), T-Bills (91/182/364-day), Cash Management Bills |
| Internal - non-marketable | **NSSF special securities**, 14-day intermediate T-Bills, compensation/saving bonds |
| Other liabilities (**Public Account**) | **Small savings**, post-office deposits, **provident funds**, reserve funds |
| External | Loans from foreign governments, multilaterals |
- Funds: Consolidated Fund = main account, receives loans and loan recoveries; Public Account = money held in trust (State Provident Fund credited here); Contingency Fund = urgent needs; NIF = permanent corpus.
!TRAP Market loans, T-Bills, special securities to RBI = internal debt; small savings, PF = other liabilities. Provident Fund = **contractual savings**.
### 2. MARKET BORROWINGS
- **Market borrowings = largest source of capital receipts**; borrowings give maximum resources for plan expenditure.
- 2026-27: gross ₹**17.20 lakh crore**, net ₹11.73 lakh crore.
- Household savings reach government via bank deposits (**SLR**), small savings/NSSF, insurance/PF.
### 3. EXTERNAL DEBT
- End-Dec 2024: **USD 718.2 bn = 18.4% of GDP**; end-Dec 2025: **USD 765.5 bn = 20.4%**.
- Mostly **non-sovereign** (companies, banks); government external debt ~2.6% of GDP; only 3.3% of Centre's liabilities.
- Largest component: **commercial borrowings** (about 38% till 2017; still largest end-Dec 2025). ADB largest multilateral lender (32.8%).
- Currency (Dec 2025): **US dollar 54.8%**, rupee 30.1%, yen 6.3% - not all in dollars.
- World Bank classed India **less indebted**. GDF 2010 most indebted: Russia, China, Turkey, Brazil, India. Rise 2000-01: commercial borrowings, NRI deposits; crossed USD 300 bn end-2010.
- "India's External Debt: A Status Report" - **DEA, Ministry of Finance**; RBI compiles quarterly data.
### 4. DEBT SUSTAINABILITY
- **Debt trap**: borrowing to pay interest on past debt. **Zero primary deficit** = borrowing only for interest.
- **r - g**: g > r, debt-GDP falls; **r > g, primary surplus needed**.
- Interest ≈ **40% of revenue receipts**. Rising debt-GDP hurts fiscal administration.
### 5. DEBT-GDP TARGETS
| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Centre BE 2026-27 | **55.6%** (peak 61%, 2020-21) |
| New anchor (Budget 2026-27) | **50 ± 1% by 31 March 2031**, replacing fiscal deficit |
| FRBM (2018) / N.K. Singh (2017) | General govt **60%** = Centre **40%** + States **20%** |
### 6. STATE DEBT
- **Art. 293**: States borrow only **within India** via **SDLs**; external loans through Centre since **2005-06** (back-to-back).
- **16th FC**: State fiscal deficit cap **3% of GSDP** (2026-31). Ratios: debt-GSDP, interest/revenue receipts.
| State (2023-24) | Debt % GSDP |
|---|---|
| **Punjab** | 42.9 |
| West Bengal | 38.3 |
| Rajasthan | 37.9 |
| Andhra Pradesh | 34.6 |
- **AP** 2025-26 RE: ₹**6,35,177 crore = 36.04%**; market borrowing ₹4,83,093 crore largest; crosses ₹7 lakh crore (BE 2026-27).
### 7. OFF-BUDGET BORROWING AND DEBT MANAGEMENT
- PSU/SPV loans serviced from budget, outside deficit; hides true debt, hurts ratings.
- **FCI borrowing from NSSF** for food subsidy - ended **Budget 2021-22**. FRBM: new guarantees ≤ **0.5% of GDP** a year.
- AP off-budget ₹1.28 lakh crore (9.7% GSDP, March 2023). 16th FC: stop it, bring debt on-budget.
- **RBI** manages debt (RBI Act 1934). **PDMC** 2016 in **DEA**, headed by Joint Secretary (Budget). Statutory **PDMA** proposed 2015-16, **not created**.
