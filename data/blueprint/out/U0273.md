## U0273 | TRIBAL CULTURE OF ANDHRA – MAJOR TRIBES, SOCIAL ORGANISATION, FESTIVALS, DANCES AND ART FORMS
### 1. TRIBE – DEFINITION AND LAW
- **Marshall Sahlins**: tribe = **"segmental organisation"** of equal multi-family groups.
- **Article 342**: **President** notifies STs after consulting the **Governor**; only **Parliament** amends the list (**342(2)**).
- **Census 2011**: ST = **8.6%** of India; **Bhil** largest tribe, then Gond; **Madhya Pradesh** has the largest tribal population (not Odisha).

### 3. PVTGs OF AP
- Undivided AP had **12 PVTGs**, second after **Odisha (13)**; India 75.
- List: **Bodo Gadaba, Gutob Gadaba, Bondo Poroja, Khond Poroja, Parengi Poroja, Chenchu, Dongria Khond, Kutia Khond, Kolam, Konda Reddi, Konda Savara, Thoti**.
### 4. SOCIAL ORGANISATION – THE CHENCHUS
- **Endogamous tribe**, **exogamous clans** (kulam/gothram); no clan hierarchy; **patriarchal, patrilineal**.
| Village office | Clan |
|---|---|
| **Raju** | **Bhoomani** |
| **Mantri** | **Kumakula** |
| **Kolagadu** | Dasari / Uttaluri / Mandla |
- These three officers, from different clans, **officiate marriages**.
- Youth dormitory **Ghotul** = **Muria** of Chhattisgarh.
!TRAP Raju = Bhoomani; Mantri = Kumakula.

### 6. FESTIVALS
| Festival | Tribe | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Eetela Panduga (Chaitrapurab)** | Araku tribes | Spring; Dhimsa danced |
| **Seethala Panduga** (Ashada; Balula) | Lambadi | Cattle; Seethala Mata guards against **smallpox** |
| **Heli Panduga = Jari Gundu** | Lambadi | Newborn sons; **Jojer Nrutyam** |
| **Kalimas** | Lambadi | = Deepavali |
| **Modakondamma jatara** | **Paderu** agency | Annual goddess jatara |
- World Adivasi Day festival at Tummalapalli Kalakshetram, Vijayawada (11 Aug 2026).
### 7. DANCES
| Dance | Tribe |
|---|---|
| **Dhimsa** | Bagata, Gadaba, Kondadora, Poraja, Valmiki; also Savara, Jatapu, Khond, Kotia |
| **Kommu** | **Koya** – **bison-horn** headgear |
| Lambadi | Lambada/Sugali women |
| **Gussadi** | **Gond** (Telangana side) |
- Dhimsa: **15-20 women** in circles, hands on shoulders; dappu, tudumu, mori, kidgi, gilka, jodukommulu; ASR and Parvathipuram Manyam; origin **Koraput**.
- **Guinness record** (Jul 2026): largest Dhimsa, **13,000+** women at **Bhogapuram** airport (Vizianagaram); certificate to AP Tribal Welfare Dept.
