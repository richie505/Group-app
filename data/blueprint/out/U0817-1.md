### 5. PAYMENT SYSTEMS - UPI AND NPCI
#### 5.1 UPI Merchant Discount Rate (from 15 Oct 2026)
- **NPCI MDR framework effective 15 October 2026**; NPCI set **0.4% MDR on P2M payments above ₹2,000** (16 Sep 2026); **UPI stays free for consumers**.
| Transaction | MDR |
|---|---|
| Merchant payments **up to ₹2,000** | **Nil** |
| Merchant payments **above ₹2,000** | **0.4%** |
| **Essential sectors** (railways, telecom, fuel, electricity, education, agri inputs, also insurance), ₹2,000 or more | **Flat ₹5** |
| **P2P, P2PM, AutoPay** | **Nil** |
- Cap **₹300** at **₹75,000+**.
- **P2PM** = micro-merchants up to **₹1 lakh/month**; moved to P2M if over the limit for **3 consecutive months**.
- **MDR split**: payer bank **40%**, payee bank **30%**, UPI app (TPAP) **20%**, PSP **10%**; **5%** of MDR collections to a **small-merchant adoption fund**.
- **Section 10A, Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007**: **zero-MDR on UPI since Jan 2020**.
- **Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026** let the government notify chargeable UPI transactions (6 Aug 2026).
- Follows the government's exemption of UPI payments up to ₹2,000 and **RuPay debit cards** from bank charges.
#### 5.2 UPI data

### 6. BANKING, INSURANCE AND SAVINGS
#### 6.1 Bharat Maritime Insurance Pool (BMIP)
- **India's first collective domestic platform** for insuring large maritime risks.
- Approved **18 April 2026**; launched/operationalised **12 May 2026**.
- Capacity **₹13,906.50 crore (~USD 1.5 billion)**; **sovereign guarantee ₹12,980 crore (~USD 1.4 billion)**.
- Duration **10 years**, extendable to a maximum of **15 years**.
- Administrator: **General Insurance Corporation of India (GIC Re)**.
- Claims up to **USD 100 million** from reserves/reinsurance; **sovereign guarantee only after reserves are exhausted**.
- Covers: **Hull & Machinery, Cargo War, Protection & Indemnity (P&I), War-perils**.
- Eligible: **Indian-flagged ships + vessels owned/managed by Indian entities + cargo to/from India**.
- By **7 Sep 2026**: **3,000 Cargo War**, **92 Hull War-risk**, **3 P&I** policies.
- **First policy (12 May 2026)**: **New India Assurance** to **Hoger Offshore and Marine Pvt Ltd** (H&M War-risk).
- **First P&I policy (30 July 2026)**: to **Shipping Corporation of India** by **New India Assurance**.
- Background: International Group of P&I Clubs - **13 clubs** insure ~**90%** of the world's large ships; P&I premium outflow ~USD 45-60 million/year.
- Maritime data: trade **~95% by value, 70% by volume**; ports **12 major, 217 non-major**; cargo ~**1,668 MMT** (2025-26); coastline ~**11,098 km**; **EEZ 2.4 million sq km**; inland waterways >14,500 km; fleet (mid-2026) **1,609 ships**, 14.33 million GT (~36% growth since 2015).
!TRAP BMIP is NOT only for Indian-flagged ships; it is run by GIC Re, not New India Assurance (which issued the first policies).
