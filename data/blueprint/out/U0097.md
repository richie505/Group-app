## U0097 | KAKATIYA ART – THOUSAND PILLAR TEMPLE, RAMAPPA (UNESCO 2021), WARANGAL FORT
### 1. KAKATIYA RULERS AND RELIGIOUS PATRONAGE
#### 1.1 Rulers – Chronology
| Ruler | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Rudradeva** (Prataparudra I) | Ascended **1158**; built the **Thousand Pillar Temple (1163)**; gave **Maddi Cheruvu** village for its upkeep; founded **Orugallu** and began fortifying it |
| Mahadeva | Ruled after Rudradeva, **before** Ganapatideva |
| **Ganapatideva** | Greatest Kakatiya; shifted the capital from **Hanumakonda to Warangal** (by **1254**); completed Warangal Fort; extended the kingdom south to **Kanchi** (Nellore conquered on the way); built the **Swayambhu Shiva temple (1254)**; religious teacher **Visvesvara Sambhu** |
| **Rudramadevi** (Rudramba) | Daughter and **immediate successor** of Ganapatideva, **1260**; title **Rayagaja Kesari**; **Marco Polo** visited Andhra during her reign; added a step-arch to Warangal Fort |
| Prataparudra | Ascended **1296** |
- Order: Rudradeva (1158) → Mahadeva → Ganapatideva → Rudramadevi (1260) → Prataparudra (1296).
- Ketana of the Kakatiya period had the title **Abhinava Dandi**.
#### 1.2 Patrons and Builders
| Person | Contribution |
|---|---|

| **Vellanki Gangadhara** | **Vaishnava minister** of Rudradeva; built **Prasanna Keshava** temple, Hanamkonda |
| Jagadala Mummadi (Mummadi Nayaka), son of Bayyana Nayaka | **Pakhal tank**, under Ganapatideva |
| Sculptor **Ramappa** | Temple named after him |
| **Ulugh Khan** | Delhi Sultanate invasion, **1323** – ruined the fort and the Swayambhu temple |

### 3. THOUSAND PILLAR TEMPLE, HANAMKONDA
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Builder and year | **Rudradeva**, **1163 CE** |
| Other names | **Rudreshwara** (Rudreswara Swamy) temple; **Veyi Stambhala Gudi** |
| Plan | **Trikuta** (three-shrine, Trikutalaya) on a **star-shaped platform** |
| Deities | **Rudreshwara (Shiva)**, **Vasudeva (Vishnu)**, **Surya** |
| Pillars | Actually **369**; lathe-turned pillars give the illusion of a thousand |
| Nandi | **Monolithic** (Ekasila) black basalt Nandi |
- **Surya takes the place of Brahma** in the usual Trinity (APPSC G2 2025 statement III – correct).
- **13 May 2026:** ASI finished restoring the **Kalyana Mandapam** (dance pavilion) of the temple.
!KEY APPSC G2 2025: The statement on the Thousand Pillar Temple, Hanamkonda (Shiva, Vishnu, Surya) is correct.
### 4. RAMAPPA (RUDRESHWARA) TEMPLE, PALAMPET
#### 4.1 Basic Facts
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Place | **Palampet**, **Mulugu district** (former Warangal district), Telangana |
| Builder | **Recherla Rudra**, general of **Ganapatideva** |
| Year | **c. 1213 CE** |
| Names | **Rudreshwaralayam**; deity Shiva as **Ramalingeswara** |
| Named after | Its sculptor **Ramappa** – **only temple in India known by its sculptor's name** |
| UNESCO | **World Heritage Site on 25 July 2021** |
| Type | APPSC G2 2012 answer key: **Trikuta** |
#### 4.2 Features
- **Star-shaped platform**; **floating bricks** in the shikhara/vimana; **sandbox foundation**.
- Pillars and brackets of **granite/dolerite** (some accounts say sandstone brackets).
- **Madanika** (dancing-girl) **bracket figures** on outer walls in dance poses.
- Statues of the Recherla Rudra couple and **28 elephant-lion figures** (Gajakesari title).
- Painted ceilings with **Ramayana–Mahabharata** scenes.
!TRAP Both are called Rudreshwara: Thousand Pillar = 1163, Rudradeva, Hanamkonda; Ramappa = 1213, Recherla Rudra, Palampet.
### 5. KAKATIYA STYLE – TECHNIQUES AND FEATURES
| Innovation | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Stellate (star) plan** | Raised star-shaped platform; more wall surface for sculpture, more light and air |
| **Sandbox foundation** | Deep pits filled with **sand, stone chips and earth**; shock absorber on **black cotton soil**; why Ramappa survived earthquakes – a cited heritage-engineering example |
| **Floating bricks** | Light porous bricks in Ramappa's vimana that **float on water**; reduce the dead weight of the tower |
| **Ekasila** | Monolithic carving – Nandi and pillars in black basalt/granite |
| **Lathe-turned pillars** | Polished granite pillars |
| **Kirtimukha** | "Face of glory" motif |
| **Madanika brackets** | Bracket figures projecting from outer walls in dance poses |
!TRAP Floating bricks = lighter tower; sandbox foundation = earthquake cushion. Do not swap them.
### 6. STYLE INFLUENCES AND PHASES
- Influence of the **Western Chalukyas of Kalyani**: carved pillars, star-shaped platforms, ornate doorways, sculptured ceilings (APPSC G2 2025 statement II – correct).
- Style: **Chalukyan / Vesara**; also described as **transitional** between early Chalukyan and **Vijayanagara**.
- **Jain phase:** the **Padmakshi temple**, Hanamkonda (Prola II), was originally a Jain shrine. But per the APPSC G2 2025 key, the statement "early Kakatiya kings were known for building Jain temples" was **not** accepted.
- **Pillalamarri paintings** paved the way for the Vijayanagara **Lepakshi** style.
!KEY Chalukya temple architecture influenced Kakatiya temples – continuity from Chalukyan traditions.

### 8. DANCE SCULPTURES AND TREATISES
#### 8.1 Works and Authors
| Work | Author | Post / court | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nritta Ratnavali** (Sanskrit, 8 prakaranas) | **Jayapa Senani** | **Gaja Sahini** (head of the elephant force) under Ganapatideva | Ramappa madanika poses follow it |
| Gita Ratnavali, Vadya Ratnavali | Jayapa Senani | same | Music and instruments (now lost) |
| **Sangita Ratnakara** | **Sarangadeva** | **Yadava court of Devagiri** (Singhana II) | General music-dance treatise |
| **Kridabhiramam** | **Vinukonda Vallabharaya** (Vallabhamatya) | – | Street play (**Veedhi natakam**) on life in Warangal |
- Ramappa's madanika brackets follow **Nritta Ratnavali** (APPSC GS 2018; G2 2019) – **not** Natya Shastra or Sangita Ratnakara.
- Jayapa Senani was a **general and scholar** of Ganapatideva's reign.
- Nritta Ratnavali covers **Marga** (classical) vs **Desi** (regional/folk) dance and describes **Perini**.
#### 8.2 Dance Forms Revived
- **Perini Shiva Tandavam**: warrior dance by male warriors before battle to invoke Shiva; postures carved at Ramappa.
- Revived by **Nataraja Ramakrishna** (also shaped Devadasi Nrityam, Navajanardhanam).
### 9. WARANGAL FORT AND KAKATIYA KALA THORANAM
#### 9.1 Fort
- Founded by Rudradeva; **completed by Ganapatideva**; Rudramadevi added a step-arch.
- **Three concentric lines** of defence:
| Line | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Puttakota** | Innermost enclosure |
| Mud fort | About **20 ft**; moat; **4 cardinal gates** |
| **Kalukota** | Outer **stone** fort with **77 towers** |
- **Kridabhiramam** calls Orugallu an abode of drama artists and actors and shows its social life, sports and festivals. English translation **"A Lover's Guide to Warangal"** by **David Shulman and Velcheru Narayana Rao** (not Jayapa).
#### 9.2 Kakatiya Kala Thoranam
- **Four ornate stone gateways** around the **Swayambhu Shiva temple** inside Warangal Fort.
- Temple built under Ganapatideva; ruined in **Ulugh Khan's** invasion (**1323**) – he did not build the Thoranams.
- Today: **official emblem of Telangana State**.

### 12. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. **1163 vs 1213**: Thousand Pillar (Rudradeva) vs Ramappa (Recherla Rudra).
2. Recherla Rudra was a **general**, not a Kakatiya king.
3. Thousand Pillar has **369** pillars, not 1,000.
4. Ramappa dance figures follow **Nritta Ratnavali**, not Natya Shastra or Sangita Ratnakara.
5. Sangita Ratnakara is by **Sarangadeva**; Nritta Ratnavali is by **Jayapa**.
6. Kridabhiramam is by **Vinukonda Vallabharaya**; the translation is by Shulman and Narayana Rao.
7. **Puttakota** = innermost; **Kalukota** = outer stone fort.
8. Ulugh Khan **ruined** the Swayambhu temple; he did not build the Thoranams.
9. Rudramadevi succeeded **Ganapatideva**; Mahadeva ruled before Ganapatideva.
10. Surya, not Brahma, is the third deity of the Thousand Pillar Temple.
