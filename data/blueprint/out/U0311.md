## U0311 | PRODUCTIVITY AND DECLINE OF PARLIAMENT – DEBATE QUALITY, DISRUPTIONS AND REFORMS
### 1. SITTING DAYS
- Average sittings: **~135 days/year** (**1st Lok Sabha**) → **~55** (**17th**, 2019-24).
- Lok Sabha now sits **60-70 days** a year vs **150+** in the **UK House of Commons**.
- **Art. 85(1)**: gap between sessions **≤ six months**; **no minimum sitting days**. States: **Art. 174**.
- 17th LS: **Question Hour** ran only **60%** of scheduled time.

### 2. DISRUPTIONS AND SUSPENSION RULES
| House | Rule | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Lok Sabha | **373** | Speaker orders withdrawal **for the day** |
| Lok Sabha | **374** | Suspension by **House motion**, up to rest of session |
| Lok Sabha | **374A** | **Automatic** suspension for grave disorder – **5 sittings** or rest of session |
| Rajya Sabha | **255** | Chairman directs withdrawal for the day |
| Rajya Sabha | **256** | Suspension by House motion, not beyond the session |
!TRAP 373/255 = withdraw for the day; 374/256 = House motion; 374A = automatic.
### 3. WEAK COMMITTEE SCRUTINY
| Lok Sabha | Bills referred to committees |
|---|---|
| 14th | 60% |
| 15th | **71% (highest)** |
| 16th | 25% |
| 17th | **16%** |
- 17th LS: **58%** of Bills passed within two weeks; **35%** debated under one hour.
- India: **less than one-fifth** of Bills go to committees; **UK**: every Bill examined by committees of both Houses. Regular **DRSC** system only from **1993**.
!NUM 60% = Bills referred (14th LS) and Question Hour run (17th LS); 16% = referred (17th).
### 4. BYPASS ROUTES – ORDINANCE AND MONEY BILL
- Heavy use of **ordinances (Art. 123)** and delegated legislation.
- Proposals: define "**immediate necessity**" by law; courts to test urgency per **Krishna Kumar Singh (2017)**; publish reasons.
- **Money Bill route** (Art. 110) bypasses the **Rajya Sabha** for laws not strictly fitting it – e.g. **Aadhaar Act, 2016**.
### 5. OPPOSITION AND LEADER OF OPPOSITION
- LoP first officially recognised: **1969**.
- Threshold: largest opposition party with **10%** of House strength (**~55** LS seats).
- Statute: **Salary and Allowances of Leaders of Opposition in Parliament Act, 1977**.
- Tools: Question Hour, motions, **PAC chaired by Opposition since 1967**.
- **Monsoon 2026:** united opposition pushed the **FCRA (Amendment) Bill, 2026** to a JPC.
### 6. REFORM PROPOSALS AND DATA SOURCES
| Body / source | Proposal / role |
|---|---|
| **NCRWC (2000-02)** | Minimum **120** sitting days for **LS**, **100** for **RS** |

- Other ideas: **fixed calendar**, stronger Question Hour, **compulsory committee scrutiny**, protected opposition time, whips only for confidence votes and Money Bills.
### 7. STATE LEGISLATURES AND AP
- **PRS Review of State Laws 2025**: Assemblies met **avg 24 days** (**Odisha 43**, **Nagaland 7**); many met only to satisfy **Art. 174**; 600+ Bills, **33%** passed in one sitting.
- **AP Budget Session 2026**: **11 Feb – 7 Mar 2026**; agenda by **BAC** chaired by Speaker **Chintakayala Ayyanna Patrudu**.
- **86th AIPOC**, **Lucknow**: "**Strong Legislature - Prosperous Nation**"; AIPOC since **1921**.
- **131st Amendment Bill**: LS **550 → 850**; LS:RS **2.2:1 → 3.3:1**; joint sittings used only **3** times.
