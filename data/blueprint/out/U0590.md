## U0590 | EARTH – SHAPE, SIZE, GEOID; LATITUDES AND LONGITUDES, THE GRATICULE
### 1. SHAPE OF THE EARTH
- **Geoid** = "earth-shaped"; Earth is an **oblate spheroid** (flat at poles, bulging at Equator).
- First to say Earth is round: **Aristotle** ("On the Heavens").
- Proofs: **circular shadow on the Moon** in a lunar eclipse; ship's mast seen first; circumnavigation; satellite images.
- Weight: **minimum at Equator**, maximum at poles (centrifugal force maximum at Equator).
- Surface point nearest the centre: **poles** (Arctic Ocean floor near North Pole).
- At the Equator, stars rise **perpendicular** to the horizon.

### 3. GRATICULE
- Parallels at 1°: **179** (89 N + 89 S + Equator; poles are points). Meridians: **360**.
- Latitudes run **east–west**, parallel to the Equator, and show distance north or south.
- **Great circles**: Equator (only great-circle parallel) and opposite meridian pairs (**180° + Prime Meridian**). **60°N is not** a great circle.
- **0°, 0°** = **Gulf of Guinea**, Atlantic Ocean.
- 1° latitude ≈ **111 km** everywhere; 1° longitude: 111 km at Equator → **55.5 km at 60°** → 0 at poles.
- **Antipode**: (x°N, y°E) → (x°S, (180 − y)°W); **20°N 80°E → 20°S 100°W**.
!TRAP 179 parallels, not 181 (BPSC 2023).

- Tropic of Cancer crosses **8 states**: Gujarat, Rajasthan, MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, Mizoram (**not Odisha**).
