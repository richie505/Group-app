## U0088 | CHOLA CENTRAL AND PROVINCIAL ADMINISTRATION, LAND CLASSIFICATION, REVENUE AND IRRIGATION
### 1. KINGSHIP
- King named his **successor (yuvaraja) in his own lifetime** and made him **co-regent** (UPPSC 2001); e.g. Rajendra I co-regent 1012, king 1014.
- Rajaraja I = **Ulakalanda Perumal** ("one who measured the earth").
- King compared to gods; **images of kings and queens beside deities**; ideal king = warrior, patron of arts, protector of **varnashrama dharma**.
- Royal orders: **Sanskrit preamble** (genealogy, conquests) + **Tamil operative part**.
### 2. CENTRAL OFFICIALS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Perundaram** | Highest officials |
| **Sirudaram** | Lower officials |
| **Tirumandira-olai** | Royal secretary; wrote king's oral orders on palm leaf |
| **Olai-nayagam** | Chief secretary; checked and **countersigned** orders |
| **Adhikari** | Royal officer supervising local bodies |
| **Nattar** | Members of the nadu assembly |
### 3. DIVISIONS AND PROVINCES
- Lowest to highest: **Ur → Nadu (kurram) → Kottam / Valanadu → Mandalam**.
- **Eight mandalams**, each under a viceroy, often a **prince** or noble.
- Provinces = annexed kingdoms + **samanta** lands (defeated king restored on accepting overlordship, tribute and army duty).
| Mandalam | Region |
|---|---|
| **Cholamandalam** | Core Chola country (Kaveri) |
| **Jayankondacholamandalam** | Former Pallava **Tondaimandalam** |
| **Mummudicholamandalam** | **Northern Sri Lanka** (Rajaraja I) |
- Vengi: Kulottunga I's sons as viceroys (**Rajapratinidhi**).
- **Burton Stein**: segmentary state; Nilakanta Sastri: centralised.
### 4. REVENUE AND TAXES
- Rajaraja I's **land survey**.
- Land revenue = **1/6 of produce** (as Pallavas, Kakatiyas; religious texts), in cash, kind or both, via village assemblies. Guptas ~1/7; **Mughals, Sher Shah 1/3**.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Kadamai** | Land revenue **in kind** |
| **Antarayam** | Rural tax **in cash** |
| **Vetti**, Muttaiyal, Kudimai | **Forced labour** |
| **Nettal** | Compulsory labour |
| **Eccoru** | Duty to **feed visiting officials** |
| **Ayam** | Royal dues |
| **Irai** | Tax on land |
| **Kasu** | Gold coin |
- Remission: exemptions granted through the **ur**; Kulottunga I = **Sungam Tavirtta Chola** (abolished tolls).
### 5. LAND CLASSIFICATION
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Eripatti** | Land whose revenue maintained the **village tank** |
| **Vellanvagai** | Non-Brahmin peasant land (ur villages) |
| **Brahmadeya** | Rent-free land to **Brahmins** (sabha) |
| **Devadana** | Land to **temples** |
| **Shalabhoga** | Land for a **school** |
| **Pallichchandam** | Land to **Jaina** institutions |
| **Taniyur / Kottam** | Very large village run as one unit |
| **Karanmai / Mitachi** | Right to cultivate / superior possessory right |
| Kaniyudaiyar / Ulukudi | Land-owning farmers / tenants |
| **Ghatikas** | Colleges attached to temples |
!TRAP UPSC 2016: Eripatti and Ghatikas correct; Taniyur = "village donated to a single Brahmin" is WRONG.
### 6. ARMY AND NAVY
| Term / ruler | Point |
|---|---|
| **Kaikkolar** | Royal infantry regiments |
| **Velaikkarar** | Bodyguards sworn to die with the king |
| **Kadagams** | Cantonments |
| **Rajaraja I** | **Built the navy**; Chera navy at Trivandrum; north Sri Lanka, Maldives |
| **Rajendra I** | Whole Sri Lanka; **1025** Srivijaya → **Kadaram (Kedah)**, parts of Sumatra |
- Navy made the **Bay of Bengal a "Chola Lake"** (Nilakanta Sastri).
### 7. IRRIGATION AND TEMPLE
- **Eri variyam** ran village tanks and water distribution; eripatti paid repairs.
- **Kallanai (Grand Anicut)** on the Kaveri = **Karikala**; delta channels kept up by later Cholas. Vijayanagara tanks: Porumamilla.
- Temple = biggest **landholder, employer, lender**; Rajaraja I made Cholamandalam sabhas serve the **Brihadeeswara**.
### 8. INSCRIPTIONS AND PLATES
- Chola inscriptions most voluminous in South India; **Tamil** = administrative language.
| Record | Point |
|---|---|
| **Anbil** | Sundara Chola; Sanskrit genealogy |
| **Tiruvalangadu** | Rajendra I; genealogy, conquests |
| **Larger Leiden (Anaimangalam)** | Ordered by Rajaraja I, engraved under Rajendra I; Anaimangalam to the **Chulamanivarma-vihara** (Nagapattinam) of Srivijaya king **Sri Mara Vijayotunga Varman**; Sanskrit + Tamil |
| **Smaller Leiden** | 3 sheets; Kulottunga I added 4,500 kalam to 8,943 kalam on two **Javanese envoys'** request |
- **16 May 2026**: Netherlands returned the plates at The Hague – first return of Chola copper plates.
- Eastern Chalukya parallel: **Ashtadasha Tirthas** (18 departments); councils = **Varagoshti**.
- Today: **DILRMP** 2008–16, 2016–26 (ULPIN = Bhu-Aadhaar).
