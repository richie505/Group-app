## U0257 | WANCHOO COMMITTEE AND THE PROBLEMS OF THE NEW ANDHRA STATE
### 1. FROM ANNOUNCEMENT TO STATE
| Date | Step |
|---|---|
| 1949-50 | **Partition Committee** under **Kumaraswamy Raja** – asset split and administrative arrangements |
| 19 Dec 1952 | **Nehru's statement** after Potti Sriramulu's death; **Wanchoo Committee** appointed (December 1952) to report on the **financial and other implications** |
| **7 Feb 1953** | **Wanchoo report** presented |
| **25 Mar 1953** | **Nehru's announcement** of the Centre's decisions |
| 1-15 May 1953 | Justice Mishra's Bellary hearings |
| 14 Sep 1953 | Andhra State Act, 1953 (Act 30 of 1953) |
!TRAP **7 February 1953** = Wanchoo report; **25 March 1953** = Nehru's announcement of the Centre's decisions on it.
### 2. WANCHOO COMMITTEE
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Type | **Single-member** |
| Member | **Justice Kailas Nath Wanchoo**, **Chief Justice, Rajasthan High Court** |
| Terms | **Boundaries, financial settlement, division of assets, administrative arrangements** of the new Andhra State (not the capital of India) |
- **C.M. Trivedi** – Centre's **Special Officer** for setting up the state.
### 3. MADRAS CITY AND INTERIM ARRANGEMENT
| Body / date | Stand on Madras |
|---|---|
| JVP Committee (Apr 1949) | Andhra only if Telugus gave up their claim to Madras |
| **Wanchoo (1953)** | Andhra Government to work **temporarily from Madras for 3-5 years** (no capital infrastructure) |
| **Nehru (25 Mar 1953)** | **Rejected** – "no question" of Madras as capital; capital **inside Andhra** |
- Nehru: temporary capital to be decided **before 1 July 1953**; some offices could stay in Madras for a while.
!KEY Wanchoo's Madras recommendation was **not accepted**.
### 4. CAPITAL AND SECRETARIAT
- **Kurnool** = temporary capital.
- Assembly = members elected to the **Madras Assembly in 1952**; **no Legislative Council**.
### 5. FINANCES, ASSETS AND SERVICES
- Assets and administration divided between Andhra and **residual Madras**; the Wanchoo apportionment of **assets and liabilities** was largely reproduced in the Act's schedule.
- The 25 Mar 1953 statement left **services** and **financial implications** to be considered separately later.
- New state "**administratively and economically orphaned**": no developed capital, no commercial centre.
### 6. HIGH COURT AND BELLARY DISPUTE
- **Madras High Court** kept jurisdiction until the Andhra High Court was set up at **Guntur in 1954**.
- **Justice Mishra** (Chief Justice, **Hyderabad High Court**) – Bellary inquiry.
| Bellary area | Went to |
|---|---|
| **Adoni, Alur** | Andhra – **Kurnool** district |
| **Rayadurg** | Andhra – **Anantapur** district |
| Siruguppa, Hospet, Sandur, Harpanahalli, Hadagali | Mysore |
| **Bellary taluk** | **Mysore** (Mishra report) |
- Bellary district court to serve Adoni, Alur, Rayadurg for the time being.
### 7. TUNGABHADRA PROJECT
| Pact / Act | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **Sreebagh Pact** | 1937 | Irrigation priority for **Rayalaseema and Nellore** on Tungabhadra, Krishna, Penna – **10 years** or as needed |
| **Andhra State Act** | 1953 | **Tungabhadra Board** to manage the works |
- After Bellary's division, works shared between **Andhra and Mysore**.
- **26 Jun 2026**: C.R. Patil opened **33 new gates** of the Tungabhadra dam with the three CMs; a committee on AP-Telangana-Karnataka disputes is planned.
