## U0376 | REGULATORY GOVERNANCE – INDEPENDENCE, CAPACITY, ACCOUNTABILITY, CAPTURE, APPELLATE STRUCTURES
### 1. KEY TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **"Mini-states"** (**Economic Survey 2025-26**) | Regulators combine rule-making (legislative), investigation (executive), adjudication (judicial) → need internal checks |
| **Regulatory capture** | Regulator serves the **industry** it regulates, not the public; theorist **George Stigler (1971)** |
| Conflict of interest | Board member's interest may prejudice duties |
| Task-based regulation | **FSLRC**: replace "area-based" regulation |
- Regulation is where the State meets the economy → **regulatory capacity is part of State capacity**.
- After the **SHANTI Act, 2025**, commentators urged more **AERB** autonomy to avoid capture.
### 2. INDEPENDENCE AND ACCOUNTABILITY
- Safeguards: **fixed tenure**, **selection-committee appointment**, removal only on **statutory grounds**.
- **Unrestricted removal** by the parent ministry weakens independence.
- **Securities Markets Code Bill, 2025**: members disclose **direct, indirect and family** interests; removal for prejudicial interest.
- Accountability: **annual reports and accounts laid before Parliament**; examined by **Departmentally Related Standing Committees**.
- ES 2025-26: parliamentary reviews, published orders, management accountable to boards, sanctions proportional to **intent, scale, harm**.
| Body | Depends on |
|---|---|
| NGT | **MoEFCC** (funds, administers) |
| Railway Claims Tribunal | **Zonal Railways** (budget) |
| **AERB** (1983, Atomic Energy Act 1962) | **DAE** via Atomic Energy Commission |
!TRAP DAE both promotes and regulates nuclear power → **NSRA Bill** proposed.
### 3. APPELLATE STRUCTURES
| Tribunal | Appeals from | Further |
|---|---|---|
| **TDSAT** (May 2000) | TRAI, IT Act officers, Data Protection Board | Supreme Court |
| **SAT** | **SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA** | SC, points of law |
| **APTEL** (**24 Aug 2004**) | **CERC, SERCs, PNGRB** | Supreme Court |
| **NCLAT** (1 Jun 2016) | **NCLT, IBBI, CCI** (26 May 2017), **NFRA** (7 May 2018) | Supreme Court |
- **L. Chandra Kumar (1997)**: High Court review of tribunals = **basic structure**.
- **Madras Bar Association (2025)**: **National Tribunals Commission** within **four months**.
!TRAP APTEL = 2004; 2016 is NCLAT's year.
### 4. OVERLAP AND CONFLICTS
- **ULIP dispute (2010)**: SEBI barred 14 insurers; ordinance of **18 June 2010** gave ULIPs to **IRDA**, set up a **joint mechanism** chaired by the **Finance Minister** (RBI Governor vice-chair); replaced by the **Securities and Insurance Laws (Amendment and Validation) Act, 2010**.
- **FSDC (2010)** now coordinates financial regulators.
- **CCI** penalised **WhatsApp** for abuse of dominance (data sharing); **DPDP Act** separately restricts data cross-use.
- **TV Ratings Policy, 2026**: **MIB** (not TRAI) sole regulator of rating agencies.
### 5. REFORM PROPOSALS
- **FSLRC** (**Justice B.N. Srikrishna**, **2013**): draft **Indian Financial Code**; task-based regulation; **Unified Financial Agency**; Financial Redressal Agency; **Financial Sector Appellate Tribunal** (not abolition).
- **Regulatory Reform Bill, 2013** (Planning Commission draft): common framework for infrastructure regulators.
- **VBSA Bill, 2025**: one higher-education regulator, three councils, replacing **UGC, AICTE, NCTE**. **Jul 2026**: Joint Committee warned of risks to institutional autonomy.
- Standing Committee on Finance sought a framework for **Virtual Digital Assets**.
### 6. CAPACITY GAPS AND AP SINGLE DESK
- **No dedicated regulatory cadre**; reliance on retired administrators → ES 2025-26 proposes a **School for Regulations**.
- **TDSAT**: staff fixed at **52 posts since 2001**.
- **Railway Claims Tribunal**: legal aid at **9 of 23** benches.
- **NGT**: no IT division.
- **IBC**: **67%** of cases exceed the **330-day** limit.
- **AP Single Desk Portal** (Apr 2015): all clearances within **21 working days** through a Single Desk Bureau.
