### 3. NATIONAL HEALTH MISSION
#### 3.1 Structure
- **NRHM launched 2005**: accessible, affordable, quality care for rural India.
- **NHM = NRHM + NUHM** (NUHM 2013).
- **ASHA = Accredited Social Health Activist**: village-level community health worker; with the **ANM** links rural households to the health system. AP has **42,060 ASHAs** (2026).
- **Funding**: Centre:State **60:40**; **North-Eastern and Himalayan States 90:10**.
#### 3.2 Components
| Component | What to remember |
|---|---|
| **JSY** (Janani Suraksha Yojana) | **Cash** assistance for **institutional delivery**; **100% centrally sponsored**; AP 1,76,966 beneficiaries (Apr–Dec 2024) |
| **JSSK** (Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram) | **Free ("zero expense") delivery and newborn care** at public facilities (with PMSMA, SUMAN) |
| **RKSK** (Rashtriya Kishor Swasthya Karyakram) | Adolescents **10–19 years**; Adolescent Friendly Health Clinics, weekly IFA, peer educators, menstrual hygiene |
| **Anaemia Mukt Bharat** (2018) | **6x6x6** strategy; preventive **IFA supplementation in all six target groups**, deworming, testing and treatment, fortified foods, behaviour change |
| Kilkari / Mobile Academy | 72 free weekly audio messages from 2nd trimester till child turns one; Mobile Academy = audio course for ASHAs |
| Free Drugs Service Initiative | Free generics; **DVDMS** manages drug supply |
!PAIR JSY | Cash | Institutional delivery || JSSK | Free | Zero-expense delivery
### 4. IMMUNISATION – MISSION INDRADHANUSH AND UIP
- **Mission Indradhanush (Dec 2014)**: catch-up drive under **UIP**; reaches **zero-dose and partly immunised** children; target **90% full immunisation**.
- **U-WIN**: digital registry of routine immunisation for pregnant women and children.
#### 4.1 Age → Vaccine
| Age | Vaccines |
|---|---|
| **Birth** | **BCG, OPV-0, Hepatitis B birth dose** |
| **6, 10, 14 weeks** | **OPV 1–3**, Pentavalent 1–3, Rotavirus, fIPV; **PCV (6 and 14 weeks)** |
| **9–12 months** | **MR-1**, JE-1 (endemic districts), PCV booster, Vitamin A |
| 16–24 months | MR-2, DPT booster-1, **OPV booster**, JE-2 |
| **5–6 years** | **DPT booster-2** |
| **10 and 16 years** | **Td** (tetanus and adult diphtheria) |
!TRAP Wrong pairs in PYQ: Td at 5–6 years; OPV-1 at 16–24 months; DPT booster-2 at 6 weeks. MR-1 at 9–12 months is correct.
### 5. AYUSHMAN BHARAT – PILLARS
| Pillar | Role |
|---|---|
| **Ayushman Arogya Mandir** (earlier AB-HWCs) | **Comprehensive primary care**; target **1,50,000**; **over 1.73 lakh** converted; primary care meets **80–90%** of health needs |
| **PM-JAY** | **Hospitalisation assurance** |
| **ABDM** | **Digital backbone** |
| **PM-ABHIM** | **Health infrastructure** |
- **AAM services**: NCD screening (hypertension, diabetes, oral/breast/cervical cancer), mental health, ENT, eye, oral, geriatric, palliative and trauma care.
#### 5.1 PM-ABHIM
- Launched **25 Oct 2021** at **Varanasi** by the Prime Minister.
- Outlay **₹64,180 crore** (FY 2021-22 to 2025-26) – largest pan-India health infrastructure scheme.
- **Critical care blocks**, **Integrated Public Health Labs** in all districts, **BSL-3 labs**.
### 6. AYUSHMAN BHARAT DIGITAL MISSION
#### 6.1 Basics
- Launched **September 2021**; implemented by the **National Health Authority (NHA)**.
- Aim: **consent-based, interoperable** digital health ecosystem on Digital Public Infrastructure.
#### 6.2 Components
| Type | Components |
|---|---|
| **Registries (4)** | **ABHA (Health ID)**, **Healthcare Professionals Registry (HPR)**, **Health Facility Registry (HFR)**, **Drug Registry** |
| Gateways | **Health Information Consent Manager**, **UHI**, **National Health Claim Exchange (NHCX)** |
| Testing | **Sandbox** |
!TRAP **Arogya Mitra is NOT part of ABDM** – it is the PM-JAY hospital facilitator.
#### 6.3 Data and Orders
- **ABHA** = **14-digit** health ID; **over 90 crore** accounts (Jun 2026).
- Aug 2025: 79.91 crore ABHA; 67.19 crore linked records; 4.19 lakh facilities on HFR; 6.8 lakh professionals on HPR.
- **Andhra Pradesh** = **first State to link electronic health records of one crore people** to the ABDM database.
- **NMC (Mar 2026)**: all medical colleges to make and link ABHA IDs for all OPD, IPD and emergency patients – catches **fake patients** during inspections.
- Benefit: **digital records improve sharing and use of patient information** across providers; consent-based; support doctors.
- **eSanjeevani**: telemedicine, now with a **Clinical Decision Support System**.

### 8. ICMR
- Set up **1911** as **IRFA**; renamed **ICMR in 1949** – among the world's oldest medical research bodies.
- **Strategic Plan 2017-24 ("Vision 2030")**, released Oct 2017: **5 pillars** – **capacity building, data management, leveraging traditional medicine, evidence to policy, strengthening programme implementation through research**; 15 goals (e.g., raise health research output 30% by 2030).
- ICMR initiatives: **MERA India**, **India TB Research Consortium**, **India Hypertension Control Initiative**, Mission Delhi, India Cancer Research Consortium, Stroke Network.
!TRAP "India Food Security Consortium" is NOT an ICMR initiative.
### 9. GLOBAL HEALTH – WHO
- **HQ: Geneva**; founded **7 April 1948** (Constitution in force) → **World Health Day = 7 April**.
- **World Health Day 2026** theme: "**Together for health. Stand with science**."
- **Director-General: Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus**; second term ends **15 Aug 2027**; **7 candidates** nominated to succeed him (announced 29 Oct 2026).
- Membership: 166 (Apr 1991) → 193 (2009) → **194 (2013)**.
### 10. ANDHRA PRADESH HEALTHCARE INTERVENTIONS
- **Telemedicine in all Ayushman Arogya Mandirs** in AP.
- **Family Doctor (Family Physician) programme**: launched **6 Apr 2023** at **Lingamguntla, Chilakaluripet mandal (Palnadu)**; doctors give **preventive care** through **10,032 YSR Village Health Clinics**.
- **Sanjeevani digital health** (from **Aug 2026**): digital health profile + **ABHA ID for every citizen**; links PHCs, hospitals, labs, e-Aushadhi, 108, 104, telemedicine + AI; **Sanjeevani Citizen Wellness App** (Jul 2026).
- **PM-ABHIM in AP (Sep 2026)**: J.P. Nadda inaugurated **10 Critical Care Blocks and 12 IPHLs** (₹250 crore) from **Vijayawada**.
