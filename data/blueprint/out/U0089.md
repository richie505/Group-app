## U0089 | CHOLA ECONOMY AND MERCHANT GUILDS – AYYAVOLE, MANIGRAMAM; OVERSEAS TRADE WITH CHINA AND SRIVIJAYA
### 1. MERCHANT GUILDS
| Body | Base / activity |
|---|---|
| **Ayyavole-500** (Ainnurruvar, "Five Hundred") | From **Aihole** (Ayyavolu), Karnataka; pan-regional, inter-regional and overseas |
| **Manigramam** | **Foreign / long-distance** trade corporation (UPSC 1997) |
| **Nanadesi** (Pekkundru) | Foreign-trade guild ("of many countries") |
| **Ubhaya Nanadesi** | **Both** domestic and foreign trade |
| **Nagaram / Nakaram** | **Local** merchant assembly of a market town |
| **Anjuvannam** | Guild of West Asian foreign merchants |
| Valanjiyar | Another merchant body |
| **Sreni** | Body for (foreign) trade |
!TRAP Nagaram = local; Nanadesi / Manigramam = foreign; Ubhaya Nanadesi = both. UPPSC 2018 key accepted Nanadesis or Manigramam for foreign trade.
#### 1.1 Guild Features
- Had **symbols and militias**; settled disputes; worked as **bankers**; donated to temples.
- As Chola power fell, guilds became **more independent**, with own codes and collective decisions.
#### 1.2 AP Angle
- Ayyavole and Manigramam active in Andhra, especially **Penugonda** = main trade centre (**APPSC G2 2012**), **18 branches** across Vengi.
- Eastern Chalukya **Nakaras** headed by **Shreshthis**.
- **Tagara** = trade centre between Kalyan and Vengi.
### 2. MARITIME NETWORK AND OVERSEAS PARTNERS
- Route: **Sri Lanka → Malay Peninsula → Sumatra → South-East Asia**.
- **China**: Chola embassies reached the Song court in **1015** and **1033**; **Kulottunga I** sent **72 merchants** in **1077**.
- **Srivijaya**: friendly under Rajaraja I; turned hostile under Rajendra I (1025 expedition) over the China route.
- **Arabs** traded at Quilon.
- Guild networks carried Chola trade and **Dravidian culture** across the Bay of Bengal.

### 6. TEMPLE ECONOMY AND CHUDAMANI VIHARA
- Temple income: **guild donations** + royal grants of land and paddy.
- **Chudamani Vihara**, Nagapattinam: Buddhist vihara built (**1006**) by Srivijaya king **Sri Mara Vijayottunga Varman**; **Rajaraja I** granted it Anaimangalam village (Larger Leiden plates).
