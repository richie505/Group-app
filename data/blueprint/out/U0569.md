## U0569 | APPROACHES AND INITIATIVES IN AP INDUSTRY – INDUSTRIAL POLICY, SINGLE DESK, MSMES, INDUSTRIAL NODES
### 1. AP INDUSTRIAL BASE – PROFILE AND MAJOR SECTORS
#### 1.1 Endowments and data
- Five strengths: **974-km coastline** (2nd longest), **six operational ports**, minerals, farm base, **surplus power**.
!TRAP "National surplus of coal reserves" is **not** one of AP's strengths.
- **Manufacturing** = about **11% of GSDP** (APSES 2024-25). After bifurcation, the MSME profile shifted towards **capital-intensive** units.
- **Rank**: AP **7th in industrial output** (RBI Handbook of Statistics on Indian States, 2020-21).
- National frame (Jun-Jul 2026): IIP base revised to **2022-23**; core index now **nine** industries (iron ore added), weight **32.88%** (earlier eight, 40.27%).
- APEDB: AP contributed **5.8%** of India's exports by 2020-21.
#### 1.2 District → main sectors
| District | Main sectors |
|---|---|
| Srikakulam | Agro and food processing, chemicals, petrochemicals |
| East Godavari | Chemicals, petrochemicals |
| Guntur | Food processing, cement |
| Prakasam / Kurnool | Mineral-based industries |
| Nellore | Leather, electronics, auto components |
| Kadapa / Anantapur | Textiles (Anantapur also electronics) |
#### 1.3 Major units and minerals
- **Visakhapatnam Steel Plant (RINL)**: **shore-based** integrated plant; **USSR** technical collaboration; dedicated to the nation **1 August 1992**.
- **Pharma**: **Jawaharlal Nehru Pharma City**, **Parawada** (Anakapalli) – bulk drugs/APIs (Hetero, Laurus).
- **Cement**: **26** operational plants (2014-15), in **YSR Kadapa, Kurnool, Guntur**; limestone base.
- Minerals: limestone, **barytes**, beach sands, granite. **Mangampet** (YSR Kadapa) = world's single largest barytes deposit, mined by **APMDC**.
- Aug 2026: NGRI found traces of **lithium and gallium** at **Tummalapalle** (Kadapa); Adani beach-sand titanium project at Srikakulam.

### 3. ENTERPRISE CATEGORIES AND INCENTIVES
#### 3.1 Categories (FCI = Fixed Capital Investment, land excluded)
| Category | Threshold | Investment subsidy |
|---|---|---|
| Sub-Large | **Rs 51-500 crore** (above Rs 50 crore) | **12%**, +5% Women/BC/SC/ST/specially-abled; 5 instalments |
| Large | Rs 501-1,000 crore; 3-year period | Up to **12%** (6% at 50% capacity; 0.5% cut per month of delay); **7 instalments** |
| Mega | Rs 1,001-5,000 crore or **2,000+ jobs** | Up to **15%**, tailor-made |
| Ultra-Mega | Above Rs 5,000 crore; 4-year period | Up to **15%**, tailor-made |
- **Early Bird**: **30%** for first **200** projects with CFE in 18 months; **40%** for PLI sectors with CFE in 24 months.
!TRAP G2 2025: "15% capital subsidy on **Foreign** capital investment" is NOT a standard incentive; FCI = **Fixed** Capital Investment.
#### 3.2 Standard incentives
| Incentive | Rate |
|---|---|
| Net **SGST** | **100% for 5 years** from commercial production (made, sold, registered in AP) |
| Stamp and transfer duty | **100%** reimbursement |
| Land-conversion fee | **100%** reimbursement |
| Fixed power cost | **Rs 1 per unit for 2 years** |
| De-carbonisation | Up to **6%** of FCI |
| PLI top-up | 10% of GoI PLI, cap 5% of FCI |
| Local procurement | 1% of export turnover, 3 years |
| Employment Creation Subsidy | Up to **10%** of FCI (>5 workers); 8-9% (5 or fewer); 5 years |
- IDP 2020-23: SGST for medium/large/mega **tied to jobs**; **16.2%** of APIIC plots for **SC** entrepreneurs; APIIC 25% land rebate up to Rs 20 lakh.
- **Pavala vaddi**: State repays interest above **3%** a year → unit pays **25 paise per Rs 100 per month**. APSEZ units: 3% interest subsidy.
- **Local employment Act, 2019**: **75%** of industrial jobs for locals.
- **Escrow Guarantee**: promised subsidies paid on time.
### 4. SINGLE WINDOW AND SINGLE DESK
| Item | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **AP Industrial Single Window Clearance Act** | **2002** | Statutory backing; **deemed approval** if a department misses its deadline |
| **Single Desk Portal (SDP)** | **April 2015** | Under Single Desk Policy 2015-20 |
| AP Omnibus (Speed of Doing Business) Bill | 2026 | Amends 2002 Act; passed Aug 2026 |
- SDP: **93+ clearances** from **19 departments** within **21 days**; **pre-establishment and pre-operation** approvals in 21 days (IDP 4.0 keeps it).
- Purpose: one G2B point for **clearing investment proposals** – online application, payment, tracking, certificate; **no physical visits**.
- Performance: 1,17,954 approved (1 Jan 2025); 16,783 received Apr 2024-Jan 2025 (16,458 within SLA). SDP 2.0 adds dashboards.
- Deemed approval in practice: City Gas Distribution policy (G.O.Ms.8, Mar 2026). Tourism: 13 pre-establishment + 11 pre-operation clearances.
- National: **e-Biz** portal, Ministry of Commerce, **28.01.2013**.
!TRAP **2002** = Act; **2015** = portal.

### 6. MSMES – POLICY, DEFINITIONS AND PROFILE
#### 6.1 MSME & Entrepreneur Development Policy (4.0) 2024-29
- **G.O.Ms.69**, 26 Oct 2024; guidelines G.O.Ms.28 (25 Feb 2025). Target **Rs 50,000 crore**.
- **175+ MSME parks** (one per assembly constituency); **Rs 500 crore** corpus (CGTMSE, revival, R&D); 500+ MSME Champions.
| Capital subsidy | General | Special (women, SC/ST/BC, minority, PwD, transgender) |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | 25%, cap Rs 25 lakh | **45%**, cap Rs 45 lakh |
| Small | 25%, cap Rs 1.5 cr | **45%, cap Rs 4.5 cr** |
| Medium | 25%, cap Rs 7 cr | 35%, cap Rs 7 cr |
- Extra **5%** for women **and** SC, ST, BC, specially-abled (not only women). MSME Policy 2026 revamp: women-led MSMEs (Feb 2026).
#### 6.2 Definitions (investment / turnover)
| Category | 2020 limits | From **1 April 2025** |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | Rs 1 cr / Rs 5 cr | Rs 2.5 cr / Rs 10 cr |
| Small | Rs 10 cr / Rs 50 cr | **Rs 25 cr / Rs 100 cr** |
| Medium | Rs 50 cr / Rs 250 cr | Rs 125 cr / Rs 500 cr |
#### 6.3 MSME profile (IDP 4.0 baseline)
- About **9 lakh** registered (Udyam) MSMEs (Feb 2024; 3.23% of India); about **79% services**.
- MSMEs = **23.5% of GSDP**; **28** exclusive MSME parks. Later: over **20 lakh** MSMEs (AP MSMEDC CEO, 6 Apr 2026).
- India: over 7 crore Udyam units; MSMEs ≈ **30% of GDP, 46% of exports, 28 crore jobs**; women own **20.37%**.
- Credit tie-up: **APIIC-Union Bank** MoU (**July 2022**), MSME loans in **39 industrial parks**.
- Women/SC/ST: Stand Up India, MUDRA (Tarun) + AP's extra 5%.
