## U0735 | APPLICATIONS OF SPACE TECHNOLOGY – AGRICULTURE, DISASTER MANAGEMENT, TELEMEDICINE, TELE-EDUCATION, NAVIGATION
### 1. REMOTE SENSING BASICS
- **Remote sensing:** information about Earth's surface **without physical contact**, from reflected or emitted radiation.
| Type | Energy | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Active** | Sends its **own signal** | **RISAT radar** |
| **Passive** | Natural radiation (sunlight) | **Resourcesat, Oceansat** |
- **Microwave / SAR** sees **through cloud and darkness** → flood mapping of cloud-covered areas (Assam's planned flood satellites may carry SAR, Mar 2026).
- **InSAR (Interferometric SAR):** compares the **phase of two or more radar images** of the same area → **ground deformation** – earthquakes, **land subsidence**, landslides, volcanoes, glaciers.
- **NISAR:** radar (**L-band and S-band**); centimetre-scale ground movement.
- **Soil moisture:** best measured by **microwave/SAR** sensors.
#### 1.1 What remote sensing can measure
- Land cover, vegetation, **chlorophyll**, forest cover, **surface and groundwater prospects**, snow and glaciers, soil, wetlands, **mineral and hydrocarbon zones**, crop area and **crop yield** (YES-TECH).
!TRAP Assessing **soil nutrient levels through chemical testing** is NOT a remote-sensing application.
!KEY APPSC 2024: InSAR = mapping ground deformation.
### 2. SPECTRAL INDICES AND GIS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **NDVI** | **Normalized Difference Vegetation Index = (NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red)**; healthy vegetation reflects strongly in **near-infrared**; range −1 to +1; **most widely used index for operational drought assessment** |
| **VCI** (Vegetation Condition Index) | Based on NDVI; used for **drought declaration** (mandal-level in AP) |
| **NH Green Cover Index 2025-26** | NHAI + NRSC; **India's first satellite-based green-cover assessment of national highways**; chlorophyll detection over ~30,000 km in 24 States |
#### 2.1 GIS components
| Component | Examples |
|---|---|
| Hardware | Computer, **digitizer** (tablet/table converting analog data to digital), scanner, GPS receiver, plotter |
| Software | **QGIS** – free, open-source, cross-platform desktop GIS to analyse/edit spatial data and compose maps |
| Data | Spatial (vector: points, lines, polygons; raster: satellite images) + attribute tables |
- GIS = **storing and manipulation of data**; each theme is a **layer**; **overlay analysis** combines layers.
- Situational awareness: real-time layers for disaster response, flood alerts, shoreline mapping.
- **Decision Support System:** data systems for planning that help organisations decide.
!TRAP **News channels** are NOT a source of real-time data for GIS in disaster response.

### 6. DISASTER MANAGEMENT
#### 6.1 Can vs cannot be monitored
| Can be monitored from space | Cannot |
|---|---|
| Floods, cyclones, drought, forest fires, tsunami inundation, landslides, post-quake ground deformation | **Spread of a disease among humans** (APPSC 2022), **heart attack** (APPSC 2025); **earthquake prediction** |
!TRAP "Which CANNOT be monitored" – the answer is the human-health event.
#### 6.2 Satellites and fire hotspots
| Satellite | Use |
|---|---|
| RISAT / SAR | Flood mapping through clouds |
| **INSAT-3DS** | Cyclone tracks |
| **Cartosat** | Damage mapping |
| Oceansat | Ocean-state data |
- **Bhuvan** combines RISAT/SAR and INSAT-3DS data for flood-inundation maps and cyclone warnings.
- Fire hotspots: thermal-infrared **MODIS** (NASA Terra/Aqua) and **VIIRS** (Suomi-NPP); received at **Shadnagar** → **Forest Survey of India** alert system (not IMD).
- **Ganga-Brahmaputra basin** = most flood-prone area in India (NRSC).
#### 6.3 Disaster support institutions
- **Disaster Management Support Programme (DMSP)** of ISRO: INSAT and IRS for **imaging and communication** support (floods, cyclones, droughts, fires, landslides, earthquakes).
- **Decision Support Centre** at **NRSC** – single window for satellite inputs; **States must request data from NRSC**.
- **NDEM = National Database for Emergency Management** – GIS repository for the full disaster cycle, at NRSC.
- **NDMA** and SDMAs use GIS for planning, decision support, guidelines.
- **INSAT Disaster Warning System:** Cyclone Warning Centre **Chennai → C-band uplink → S-band downlink** → coastal receivers.
- **ISRO** led the **International Charter Space and Major Disasters** for **6 months from April 2025**.
!TRAP Earthquake prediction is NOT one of NRSC's disaster-support activities.
### 7. FISHERIES AND MARITIME SAFETY
- **PFZ advisories:** from satellite **chlorophyll and sea-surface temperature**; issued by **INCOIS, Hyderabad** using **EOS-06 (Oceansat-3)**; INCOIS also issues the **Ocean State Forecast**.
| Device | Developer | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Nabhmitra** | **ISRO SAC, Ahmedabad** | Fishermen safety device; **two-way messaging via GSAT**; tested at **Neendakara (Kerala), Aug 2023** (APPSC 2023) |
| **GEMINI** | **INCOIS with AAI** (uses GAGAN) | PFZ, OSF and disaster alerts at sea |
| NavIC messaging receiver | **SAC, Ahmedabad** | Alerts to boats; pilot sets to Kerala and Tamil Nadu fishermen (2018) |
| **DAT-SG** (Distress Alert Transmitter – Second Generation) | ISRO | Has a NavIC module; sends **acknowledgement** back to fishermen; technology transfer via NSIL |
- **AP:** **RTGS hub, Vijayawada** – satellite alerts to coastal fishermen on cyclones, high waves, fish shoals.

### 9. TELEMEDICINE, TELE-EDUCATION AND NAVIGATION
- INSAT/GSAT enable **tele-education (EDUSAT, GRAMSAT)**, **telemedicine** and **Village Resource Centres**.
- **ISRO Telemedicine Network** links border posts and rural clinics to specialists.
- **VRCs:** ISRO single-window centres – tele-education, telemedicine, weather, land/water advisories.
- **Swayam Prabha:** **40 free DTH channels on GSAT-15**.
#### 9.1 Navigation applications
- **APMVTS:** **AIS-140** devices on **50,000 mineral vehicles** (AP).
- Satellite timing: **banks time-stamp transactions**; **power grids synchronise phase**.
- Govt wants **NavIC for Indian Standard Time** (GPS is current reference).
- **13 Mar 2026:** IRNSS-1F clock failed – only **3 PNT-capable** satellites; still gives one-way messaging. **NVS-02** stranded in an incorrect orbit.
- **GAGAN** (ISRO-AAI) payloads on **GSAT-8, 10, 15**.
### 10. ISRO-GEOSPHERE BIOSPHERE PROGRAMME – NATIONAL CARBON PROJECT
- Goal: assess **carbon pools, fluxes and net carbon balance** of India's terrestrial biosphere.
- Three components: **vegetation carbon pools**, **soil carbon pools**, **soil and vegetation fluxes** (flux-tower network).
