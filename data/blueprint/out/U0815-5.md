### 7. HERITAGE, CULTURE AND LANGUAGE
#### 7.1 Erra Matti Dibbalu (Red Sand Hills) – UNESCO Tentative List
- Along the **Visakhapatnam–Bheemunipatnam Beach Road** (~30 km coastal stretch).
- Added to the **UNESCO World Heritage Tentative List** – **one of seven Indian sites** added; one of India's **34 National Geo-heritage Sites**.
- Formed during the **Last Glacial Maximum (~18,500 years ago)**; process began ~20,000 years ago; LGM coastline ~**50 km** inland; height **10–30 feet**.
- **Red colour** – **ferrous minerals** reacting with moisture.
#### 7.2 Classical Telugu – CESCT, Nellore

### 9. ENVIRONMENT, SCIENCE AND OTHER INITIATIVES
#### 9.1 Striped Dolphins off Visakhapatnam
- **Striped dolphin (*Stenella coeruleoalba*)** pod filmed by a fisherman, confirmed by the **East Coast Conservation Team (ECCT)**.
- IUCN: **Least Concern** (not endangered); **all cetaceans in Schedule I, WPA 1972**.
- Common in AP waters: **Indian Ocean humpback** (*Sousa plumbea*), **spinner** (*S. longirostris*), **spotted** (*S. attenuata*).
- Network: **Marine Mammal Research and Conservation Network of India (MMRCNI)**.
#### 9.2 Swachh Ratham and Circular Economy
- **Swachh Ratham** pilot (Swachh Andhra): villagers exchange **dry waste for groceries** (rice, soaps, shampoos) of equal value; waste **sold to recyclers** (not biogas).
- Circular Economy plan: **2–3 recycling parks**; link Rathams with **50 waste processing centres**; wet-waste manure marketed via **MarkFed** for **ZBNF**.
- CM target: cut **chemical fertiliser use by 4 lakh tonnes** next year.
#### 9.3 Chittoor Mango Covers
- Led by Collector **Sumit Kumar**: **biodegradable iron-wire mesh cover** over each mango – protects from fruit flies, birds, hail, rain.
- Cost **₹2** per cover in market; Horticulture Dept gives at **₹1** (subsidy).
- Chittoor varieties: **Totapuri, Benishan, Mallika, Alphonso** (not Banganapalle); **7.5 lakh tonnes** a year; turnover **₹1,000 crore+**.
#### 9.4 Defence, Space and Skills
- **DRDO:** est. **1958**, Ministry of Defence, HQ New Delhi; **Agni, Prithvi, Akash, Nag**; led **Mission Shakti** (India's first ASAT test); **no major DRDO lab headquartered in AP** (only test facilities and collaborations).
- **Satish Dhawan Space Centre (SHAR), Sriharikota** – ISRO; PSLV, GSLV, SSLV; **named 2002**; **two launch pads**; near-equator = fuel-efficient launches.
- **Unnati Foundation** with **APSSDC** – vocational training centre for about **2,000 youth** in **Kakinada**.
- **East Godavari girls' Science Exposure Tour** to Delhi – **6–8 November 2025**, under **Samagra Shiksha** with **AP Science City**; visited **Russian Centre of Science and Culture, National Science Museum, Nehru Planetarium**.
