## U0196 | SOCIAL AND RELIGIOUS REFORM IN ANDHRA – VEERESALINGAM, GURAJADA, VENKATARATNAM NAIDU, GIDUGU
### 1. ORIGIN OF ANDHRA SOCIAL REFORM
- Began in **Rajahmundry** (Godavari district, today East Godavari) = Andhra's **first Renaissance centre**; led by the **English-educated middle class**.
- **Atmuri Lakshmi Narasimham** – first guru of the early Andhra reform movement.
- Peak **1870-1935**; main centres **Rajahmundry and Kakinada**; then Machilipatnam, Bapatla, Guntur.
- Rajahmundry = centre of Telugu social reform, **modern prose, journalism and the novel** (19th century).
- Cradle of the **language movement** = **North Coastal Andhra** (Vizianagaram, Srikakulam, Parlakimidi).

### 2. KANDUKURI VEERESALINGAM (1848-1919)
#### 2.1 Life
- Born **1848**, Rajahmundry; died **27 May 1919**, Madras. Wife **Rajyalakshmamma (Bapamma)**, married 1860.
- Method: the **press, not direct political activity**.

### 3. WIDOW REMARRIAGE
- **Hindu Widows' Remarriage Act, 1856** (Act XV) – Vidyasagar's campaign; passed under **Lord Canning**; first Bengal remarriage **7 Dec 1856**.
- **First Andhra remarriage**: **11 Dec 1881**, Rajahmundry, **Ananda Udyana Mandapam** behind his house; groom **Gogulapati Sriramulu**, bride **Seethamma** (Gauramma).
- **Widow Remarriage Association (1880)**: with **Challapalli Bapayya** and **Basavaraju Gavarraju**; **Mrs Manning** willed 50 pounds to it.
- **Kakinada**: **Paidah Ramakrishnayya** (municipal president) funded remarriages and built houses for grooms → Kakinada Widow Remarriage Association.
- Opponents: **Vedam Venkataraya Sastri** – **Stree Punarvivaha Durvada Nirvaranam (1883)**, first major anti-remarriage book; **Kasibhatta Brahmayya Sastri** – **Aryananda Brundavani Sabha**, Kakinada.
- Related laws: **1829** Bentinck – Sati Regulation; **1872** – Native Marriage Act; **1891** Lansdowne – Age of Consent Act.
#### 3.1 Social Evils and Methods
- Evils fought: ban on widow remarriage, **child marriage, dowry, caste, bride-price**, superstition, **nautch/Bhogam**, **official corruption**. Scientific education was an aim, not an evil.
- Methods: journals and satire, lectures, schools and co-education, remarriages at his own cost, widow homes, Prarthana Samaj worship without idols.

### 6. BRAHMO AND PRARTHANA SAMAJ IN ANDHRA
- Parent: **Brahmo Samaj**, Raja Ram Mohan Roy, **1828**, Calcutta (Atmiya Sabha 1814; sati ban 1829).
- Madras root: **Veda Samajam** → renamed **Dakshina Bharata Brahmo Samaj** by **Chembeti Sridharulu Naidu**.
- Rajahmundry Prarthana Samaj founded under the influence of **Mannava Buchchayya Pantulu**.
| Year | Samaj | Founder |
|---|---|---|
| **1878** | **Prarthana Samaj, Rajahmundry** (first in Andhra) | **Veeresalingam** |
| **1882** | **Brahmo Samaj, Machilipatnam** | **R. Venkataratnam Naidu** |
| 1887 | Brahmo Samaj, Rajahmundry | – |
| 1888 | Kakinada Samaj | **Tayi Surya Prakasarao Naidu** |
| 1898 | Guntur Samaj | **Pillarisetty Sitaramayya** |
- Order: **Rajahmundry (1878) → Machilipatnam (1882) → Kakinada (1888) → Guntur (1898)**.
- Founder of the Brahmo Samaj in (united) Andhra: **Veeresalingam**, with **Venkataratnam Naidu** who spread it.
- **Brahmo Trinity**: Veeresalingam, Venkataratnam Naidu, **Desiraju Pedda Bapayya**.
- Prarthana Samaj did **not** reject idol worship or caste outright; Brahmo Samaj did.
- Decline: splits after Venkataratnam Naidu joined the **Justice Party**.
- **Pithapuram Raja** gave **Rs 1 lakh** to the Brahma Dharma Prachara Nidhi to train preachers and **translate Bengali Brahmo books**.
!TRAP 1878 = Prarthana Samaj, Rajahmundry; 1887 = Brahmo Samaj, Rajahmundry.
### 7. RAGHUPATHI VENKATARATNAM NAIDU (1862-1939)
- Joined the Brahmo Samaj inspired by **Pandit Sivanath Sastri**; centre **Kakinada** (Brahma Mandir); took reform to **North Coastal Andhra**.
- **Social Purity Association** (Samajika Shuddhi Samajam), **1891**, against **Bhogam melas**.
- Led the **anti-nautch (Bhogam / Devadasi)** campaign (Madras) – main force behind ending the **Bhogam caste** custom (APPSC 2019).
- Journals: **Brahma Prakasika**, Fellow Worker, People's Friend.
- Offices: Principal, **Pithapuram Raja's College, Kakinada (1905-18)**; **first Telugu Vice-Chancellor of Madras University**.
- Titles: **Brahmarshi** (1932, from students); **knighthood 1924** (first non-official educator knighted); Rao Bahadur, Diwan Bahadur, Kulapati, Abhinava Socrates.
#### 7.1 Desiraju Pedda Bapayya (Bapatla)
- **First practising (Anushtanika) Brahmo**; dropped the sacred thread; helped Veeresalingam become an Anushthana Brahma.
- Founded **Temperance and Social Purity Sangham, Rajahmundry** (not Veeresalingam) → boycott of Bhogam melas.
!PAIR Veeresalingam | Prarthana Samaj | widow remarriage || Venkataratnam Naidu | Brahmo Samaj | anti-nautch, social purity
### 8. GURAJADA APPARAO (1861-1915)
- Worked alongside Veeresalingam in the **literary and cultural renaissance**; with Gidugu, champion of **vyavaharika** Telugu; patron court **Vizianagaram** (Ananda Gajapati).
| Work | Genre | Theme / fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Kanyasulkam** | Social play in spoken Telugu | **Bride-price**, child marriage, plight of widows |
| **Mutyala Saralu** | Poetry (1910) | Introduced a **new metre** of the same name |
| **Diddubatu** | Short story | **First modern Telugu short story** |
| **Purnamma** | Poem | Girl married off for money |
| **Kanyaka** | Poem | – |
| **Saranga Dhara** (1883) | English poem with brother Shyamala Rao | In Indian Leisure Hour; not a Telugu novel |
- Other works: Kalinga Desa Charitra, Neelagiri Paatalu.
#### 8.1 Kanyasulkam
- **Written 1890 → first staged 13 Aug 1892** (Jagannatha Vilasa Nataka Sangham, Vizianagaram) **→ published 1897** (Vavilla) **→ 2nd edn 1909**, dedicated to **Ananda Gajapati**.
- First **vyavaharika play**; **Srikakulam dialect**.
- Characters: **Madhuravani** – reformed courtesan; **Girisham** – educated fraud; **Ramappa Pantulu** – hypocrite; **Buchamma** – widow; **Soujanya Rao** – reformer.
!KEY APPSC-GS 2024: "Kanyasulkam written by Kommaraju Lakshmana Rao" – **false**; "a satirical play on social evils" – **true**.
#### 8.2 Famous Line
- **"Desamante matti kadoyi, desamante manushuloyi"** – a country is its people, not its soil.
- From the patriotic song **Desamunu Preminchumanna** (Desabhakti section of **Mutyala Saralu**); the **APPSC 2025 key** accepted **Kanyaka** as the book.
### 9. GIDUGU RAMAMURTHY AND THE VYAVAHARIKA MOVEMENT
#### 9.1 Gidugu (1863-1940)
- Born **29 Aug 1863, Ponduru (Srikakulam)**; died 22 Jan 1940; Telugu teacher at **Parlakimidi**.
- **Savara** (Ganjam Agency): created a script, **Savara-Telugu dictionaries**, **A Manual of Savara Language**.
- Works: **A Memorandum of Modern Telugu Prose**, Bala Kavi Saranyam.
- Titles: **Vyavaharika Bhashodyama Pitamaha**, Abhinava Vaganushasanudu, **Rao Saheb**, **Kaisar-e-Hind (1934)**.
- **Telugu Language Day = 29 August** (his birthday). Telugu got **classical language** status in **2008**.
#### 9.2 Granthika vs Vyavaharika
| Feature | Granthika | Vyavaharika |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Sanskritised classical | Spoken Telugu |
| Aim | Only "pure" literary medium | Close gap between written and spoken |
| Supporters | **Kokkonda Venkataratnam**, **Jayanti Ramaiah Pantulu** (Andhra Sahitya Parishad and its magazine), orthodox pandits | **Gidugu, Gurajada, Tapi Dharma Rao**, Navya Sahitya Parishat |
- **J.A. Yates** inspired the movement.
- Bodies: **Vyavaharika Bhashodyama Samstha (1910)** after a Visakhapatnam meeting; **Varthamana Vyavaharikandhra Bhasha Parivartaka Samaj** – **Gidugu** (APPSC 2017).
- 1912: Gidugu defended modern Telugu after a Telugu Pandits' conference at Madras.
- **Madras University controversy:** in **April 1913** the University set up a **Telugu Composition Committee** (ten members, incl. **P.T. Srinivasa Iyengar**) to fix the style of Telugu composition for the **Intermediate** course.
- **Jayanti Ramayya Pantulu** – **A Defence of Literary Telugu (1913)**; urged the University not to back the "new school".
- Outcome: the Sub-Committee majority (**Vedam Venkataraya Sastri**, K.V. Lakshmana Rao) kept the **literary (classical) dialect**; **Gurajada** wrote a **Minute of Dissent (1914)** for the polite spoken dialect.
- Grammars: Malladi Suryanarayana Sastri, Vadlamudi Gopalakrishnayya, Vajjala Venkateswarlu.
- Outcome: Andhra Mahasabha endorsed colloquial Telugu.
!TRAP Andhra Sahitya Parishad = **classical** side, not Gidugu's.
