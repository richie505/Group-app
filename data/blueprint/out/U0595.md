## U0595 | ROCKS AND MINERALS – IGNEOUS, SEDIMENTARY, METAMORPHIC; THE ROCK CYCLE
### 1. COMPOSITION OF THE EARTH'S CRUST
#### 1.1 Crustal Elements by Rank
| Rank | Element | % by weight of crust |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Oxygen** | 46.6 (mostly in oxides and silicates – SiO₂, Al₂O₃) |
| 2 | **Silicon** | 27.7 |
| 3 | **Aluminium** | 8.1 – **most abundant metal** |
| 4 | **Iron** | 5.0 – **second-most abundant metal** |
- Whole Earth by mass: **iron 32.1%** > oxygen 30.1% > silicon 15.1% > magnesium 13.9%.
- Suess: **SIAL** – granitic, continental; **SIMA** – basaltic; **NIFE** – nickel-iron core.
!TRAP Most abundant metal in the crust = aluminium (not iron); whole-Earth question = iron.
### 2. ROCK TYPES AND PETROLOGY
- **Petrology** = study of rocks (branch of geology). Three types: igneous, sedimentary, metamorphic.
| Rock | Examples |
|---|---|
| Igneous (**"primary"**, APPSC 2019) | Granite, basalt, gabbro, dolerite |
| Sedimentary | Sandstone, limestone, shale, conglomerate, coal, chalk |
| Metamorphic | Marble, slate, quartzite, gneiss, schist |
- **Granite is igneous**, not metamorphic; **coal is sedimentary** (organic); **gneiss is metamorphic** (usually from granite).
- **Weathering** breaks rocks into sediments, clays, soils. Rain wears rocks because rainwater holds dissolved **CO₂ and oxygen** (CO₂ → carbonic acid).
- **Terra rossa** (red clay soil) forms on **limestone** (and dolomite).
### 3. IGNEOUS ROCKS
- Rock from cooling of lava = igneous (intrusive and extrusive types); igneous rocks are both **crystalline and non-crystalline**.
| Type | Formation | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Intrusive (plutonic) | Slow cooling at depth → coarse grains | **Granite** (APPSC G2 2024), gabbro |
| Extrusive (volcanic) | Lava cools fast at surface → fine grains | **Basalt**, rhyolite, andesite |
| Rock | Type |
|---|---|
| Rhyolite | Felsic extrusive |
| Granite | Felsic intrusive |
| Diorite | Intermediate intrusive |
| Basalt | Mafic extrusive |
- **Felsic**: silica-rich, light-coloured, fairly light.
- **Mafic (basic)**: rich in **Fe, Mg, Ca**; dark, dense; olivine, pyroxene.
!TRAP APPSC 2024 A-R: "Felsic rocks contain high iron, magnesium and calcium" is FALSE – that describes mafic rocks.
### 4. SEDIMENTARY ROCKS
- Formed at Earth's surface by the **hydrological system**; need weathering of pre-existing rocks. Sediments are transported, deposited, compacted and **lithified** in layers (strata) → **"stratified rocks"**.
- Cover **75% of Earth's surface**; hold most **fossils** and mineral oil.
| Mode | Examples |
|---|---|
| Mechanically (clastic) | **Sandstone**, conglomerate, shale, loess |
| Chemically | **Rock salt**, gypsum, halite, chert, limestone |
| Organically | **Coal**, chalk, coral limestone, geyserite |
- Sandstone is flaky because it forms **underwater**.
#### 4.1 Porosity vs Permeability
| Term | Rocks |
|---|---|
| Porous and permeable | **Chalk** (very permeable, aquifers), sandstone |
| Porous but impermeable | **Clay** (very fine particles; least permeable) |
| Neither | Granite |
### 5. FOSSILS AND FOSSIL FUELS BY ROCK TYPE
- Sedimentary rocks = main store of fossils; hold **coal, petroleum, natural gas**. Conglomerate, sandstone, shale have fossils.
- **Granite unlikely to have fossils** (plutonic). Archaean rocks formed **before life** → fossil-free.
- **Dravidian** (Palaeozoic): many marine fossils.
### 6. METAMORPHIC ROCKS
- Igneous or sedimentary rocks recrystallise under **heat and pressure** (full melting not needed – that gives magma). Come from **both** igneous and sedimentary rocks ("change of form").
| Parent rock | Metamorphic rock |
|---|---|
| Limestone | **Marble** |
| Shale / clay | **Slate** (then schist) |
| Sandstone | **Quartzite** (quartz-rich, non-foliated) |
| Granite | **Gneiss** |
| Basalt | Amphibolite, schist |
| Coal | **Graphite** (via anthracite) |
- Typical metamorphic minerals: **garnet**, kyanite, sillimanite, staurolite; **halite is not** (chemical sedimentary).
!TRAP Odd one out: marble, slate, quartzite are metamorphic; limestone is sedimentary (parent of marble).
### 7. MINERALS – CLASSES, CRYSTALLISATION, HARDNESS
- **Feldspar**: about **one-half** of the crust ("one-fourth" is WRONG – APPSC G1 2024: statement I only). 
- **Quartz** = SiO₂; hard, weathering-resistant; major part of granite and sandstone.
| Silicate class | Example |
|---|---|
| Inosilicate – single chain | **Augite** (pyroxene) |
| Inosilicate – double chain | **Hornblende** (amphibole) |
| Phyllosilicate (sheet) | **Muscovite**, biotite (mica) – APPSC 2024 |
| Tectosilicate (framework) | **Albite**, orthoclase (feldspar), quartz |
- **Bowen's reaction series** = order of crystallisation from cooling magma (APPSC 2024): olivine (first) → pyroxene → amphibole → biotite → K-feldspar → muscovite → **quartz last** (most stable). Mafic minerals form first at highest temperature.
- **Mohs scale**: talc 1, gypsum 2, calcite 3, fluorite 4, **apatite 5**, **orthoclase 6**, **quartz 7**, **topaz 8**, corundum 9, diamond 10.
### 8. THE ROCK CYCLE
- Magma → (cooling) igneous → (weathering, erosion, deposition, lithification) sedimentary → (heat, pressure) metamorphic → (melting) magma.
- **Any rock type can become another**; exposed metamorphic rock can weather into sediment.
- **Enhanced rock weathering**: crushed basalt spread on farmland locks CO₂ as **bicarbonate**.

### 10. ROCK SYSTEMS OF INDIA
- Order: **Archaean → Dharwar → Cuddapah → Gondwana → Deccan Trap → Tertiary** (youngest).
| System | Age / rocks | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| Archaean | >2,500 million yrs; granite, gneiss, schist | Oldest; no fossils |
| Dharwar | Oldest metamorphosed sediments | **Most mineralised**: iron ore, manganese, copper, lead, gold |
| Cuddapah (Purana) | ~1,400–600 million yrs; mainly **clay rocks** (shale, slate) + quartzite, limestone | Barytes, asbestos, copper, nickel, cobalt, diamonds; **no coal** |
| Vindhyan (Purana) | Sandstone, limestone; Semri → Kaimur → Rewa → Bhander | Building stone, limestone, **Panna diamonds** |
| Gondwana | Permo-Carboniferous; freshwater | **Nearly 98% of coal** (Damodar, Mahanadi, Godavari) |
| Deccan Trap | ~65 million yrs; fissure basalt | **Regur** (black cotton) soil – best for cotton |
| Tertiary | ~60–7 million yrs | Petroleum (Assam, Gujarat); **Neyveli lignite** |
- Gondwana coal is younger and lower in carbon than Carboniferous coal; also in Assam and Sikkim.
- APPSC 2021 keyed Cuddapah composition as **"clay"**. Cuddapah and Vindhyan = Purana (Proterozoic), not Archaean.
- **Khondalite** = Eastern Ghats (Archaean), **not Vindhyan**.
### 11. ROCKS OF ANDHRA PRADESH
| Formation | Districts | Resources |
|---|---|---|
| Dharwar | Anantapuramu, Chittoor, YSR Kadapa | Iron ore, copper, gold (**Ramagiri** belt) |
| Cuddapah basin (largest sedimentary basin) | YSR Kadapa, Nandyal, Kurnool | **Barytes (Mangampeta)**, limestone, asbestos |
| Kurnool group | Kurnool, Nandyal | Cement-grade limestone; no metallic minerals; petroleum potential |
| Eastern Ghats Mobile Belt | Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam, ASR | **Khondalite, charnockite**; bauxite (**Araku**), manganese, graphite |
| Gondwana | East Godavari, ASR | Coal |
| Deccan Trap patches | Prakasam, Palnadu, Guntur | Basalt → black cotton soil |
- Khondalite, charnockite: Archaean (2.5+ billion yrs). Oil in AP rock systems: **Pleistocene**.
- **Vindhya rocks** rich in limestone (also sand, china clay) – basis of cement industry; Kadapa–Kurnool limestone makes Rayalaseema a cement hub.
- Gold mining reviving at Ramagiri (Jul 2026).
