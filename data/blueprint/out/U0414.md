## U0414 | RIGHTS OF PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES – RPwD ACT 2016, UNCRPD, SUGAMYA BHARAT
### 1. CONSTITUTIONAL BASIS
| Provision | Content |
|---|---|
| **Art. 41** (DPSP) | Public assistance in **disablement** |
| Art. 46 | Weaker sections |
| **State List Entry 9** | "Relief of the **disabled and unemployable**" |
- Spending about **0.02% of GDP** (South Africa 0.12–0.15%); proposed national minimum disability pension ₹8,000 a month under Art. 41 (13 Jun 2026).
### 2. LAWS AND MILESTONES
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| **1981** | UN **International Year of Disabled Persons** |
| **1992** | **RCI Act** – training of rehabilitation professionals (APPSC 2018) |
| **1995** | **PwD (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act** – first comprehensive, **welfare-based**; created the Chief Commissioner |
| **1999** | **National Trust Act** – autism, cerebral palsy, mental retardation, multiple disabilities |
| 2006 | First **National Policy** for PwDs |
| **13 Dec 2006** | UNGA adopted **UNCRPD** |
| **Oct 2007** | India **ratified** UNCRPD |
| 2010 | **Sudha Kaul Committee** (MoSJE) drafted the replacement bill |
| 2016 | **RPwD Act** enacted |
| **19 Apr 2017** | RPwD Act in force |
- Order: UNCRPD adopted → India ratified → RPwD enacted → in force.
- India has **not** ratified the **Optional Protocol** (individual complaints).
### 3. RPwD ACT 2016 – NUMBER GAME
- First **rights-based** disability law; replaced the 1995 Act; meets UNCRPD obligations.
- **Sec. 2(s)**: PwD = long-term physical, mental, intellectual or sensory impairment which, with barriers, blocks full participation.
| Provision | Value |
|---|---|
| Specified disabilities | **21** (from **7**), e.g. autism, **acid attack victims**, sickle cell disease; Centre may add |
| Benchmark disability | At least **40%** |
| Government jobs | **4%** |
| Higher education | **5%** seats |
| Free education | Benchmark-disability children **6–18** |
| Monitoring | **Chief Commissioner**, Sec. 75(1) |
- Survivors of **forcible acid ingestion** recognised as acid attack victims (SC, 5 May 2026).
!TRAP "Passed in 2018" is wrong: passed 2016, in force 19 April 2017. 4% jobs vs 5% higher education.

### 5. ACCESSIBILITY – SUGAMYA BHARAT
- **Accessible India Campaign (Sugamya Bharat Abhiyan)**: launched **3 December 2015**; DEPwD flagship.
- **Three verticals**: **built environment, transport, ICT**.
- **Sugamya Bharat App**: crowdsourced reporting of accessibility gaps.
- **Accessible elections**: accessible booths, **Braille-enabled EVMs**.
- **Sugamya Pustakalaya**: online library – DAISY, audio, Braille books.
- SC: **safe footpaths** part of Art. 21, minimum width **1.8 m**; **digital access** part of Art. 21, citing UNCRPD; **prison manuals** to be revised (Dec 2025).
!KEY 3 December = Disability Day **and** launch of Accessible India Campaign (2015).
### 6. SCHEMES AND PORTALS
| Scheme / portal | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **ADIP** | Free aids and appliances (plus corrective surgery) below income **₹22,500/month**; subsidised up to ₹30,000 |
| **DDRS** | Grants-in-aid to **NGOs** for rehabilitation |
| **UDID (Swavlamban)** | Single national disability ID and digital certificate; centralised database |
| **PM-DAKSH-DEPwD portal (2023)** | UDID-based skilling + **Divyangjan Rozgar Setu** (jobs); MoSJE, not Minority Affairs |
| **SIPDA** | Grant-in-aid to implement the RPwD Act |
| IGNDPS (NSAP) | Pension to severely disabled BPL persons |
| Scholarships | 6 components: pre-matric, post-matric, Top Class, National Fellowship, Overseas, Free Coaching |
| **PRASHAST App** | **NCERT/DoSEL** (Sept 2022), not DEPwD; teachers screen **21** disabilities |
!TRAP ADIP = aids to individuals; DDRS = grants to NGOs; EKAM = entrepreneurship.
### 7. INCLUSION AND ENTREPRENEURSHIP
- **Inclusive India initiative** (MoSJE / National Trust): mainstreaming persons with **intellectual and developmental disabilities** (APPSC G2 2017).
- **EKAM Fest** (Entrepreneurship, Knowledge, Awareness, Marketing) by NDFDC: Divyangjan entrepreneurship (APPSC 2018).
- **Divya Kala Shakti** (DEPwD): cultural showcase of PwD artistic talent.
- **Special education** targets **children with disabilities (CwSN)**, not gifted children (APPSC 2008). **Samagra Shiksha** inclusive component: assessment, aids, assistive technology, therapy; all RPwD disabilities.

### 10. DATA AND ANDHRA PRADESH
- **Census 2011**: **2.68 crore** PwDs = **2.21%**; largest: **movement 20.3%**, hearing 18.9%, seeing 18.8%. WHO estimate ~16% → under-reporting.
- **Census 2027**: adds acid attack, neurological and blood disorders; autism and dwarfism left out.
- **AP**: **11.04 lakh** PwDs (Census 2011); largest type orthopaedic.
- **Divyang Shakti** (AP): **100% free APSRTC travel** from **18 March 2026** for 40%+ disability; escort 50%; SADAREM certification.
- AP pension **₹6,000** a month; 52,263 assistive devices (11 Jun 2026).
- **AP Differently Abled and Senior Citizens Assistance Corporation**: aids, appliances, economic rehabilitation.
- AP Samagra Shiksha: **Best State** in implementing RPwD Act (2024-25); ₹3,500 per CwSN; 679 Bhavitha Centres; 9 DDRCs, 70 NGOs under DDRS.
