## U0127 | BATTLE OF TALIKOTA (1565), THE ARAVIDU DYNASTY AND THE LEGACY OF VIJAYANAGARA
### 1. BATTLE OF TALIKOTA – 1565
- **23 January 1565**: Vijayanagara **defeated**; Hampi **looted about six months** (severe, not total damage).
- Names: **Talikota** (Sewell), **Rakshasi-Tangadi / Rakshasa Tangadi** (Heras), **Bannihatti** (Sherwani).
- King: **Sadasiva Raya** (nominal, last Tuluva); real power with regent **Aliya Rama Raya** (son-in-law of Krishnadevaraya).
- Rama Raya was beheaded by **Hussain Nizam Shah**; Muslim contingents deserted.
#### 1.1 The Confederacy (full list)
| Sultanate | Ruler |
|---|---|
| Bijapur | Ali Adil Shah |
| Ahmadnagar | Hussain Nizam Shah |
| Golconda | **Ibrahim Qutb Shah** (main organiser) |
| Bidar | Ali Barid Shah |
!TRAP **Berar (Imad Shahi)** stayed out. The enemy was the Deccan Sultanates, NOT the Bahmani kingdom (already broken up), Mughals or Gajapatis.
#### 1.2 Rama Raya's Policy and Causes
| Cause | Fact |
|---|---|
| Divide and rule | 1546-47 backed Ahmadnagar vs Bijapur and Bidar, took the **Raichur doab**; 1557-63 backed Bijapur and razed Ahmadnagar |
| Marriage links | **Chand Bibi** – Ali Adil Shah; **Bibi Jamal** – Ibrahim Qutb Shah |
| Shelter to rebel | **Jagadeva Rao** of Golconda |
#### 1.3 Results
- Sadasiva and **Tirumala** fled to **Penukonda**; centre moved to the **Telugu country**; Nayakas became autonomous.
- Sewell called the city a **"graveyard"** – a view that may be exaggerated.
### 2. ARAVIDU DYNASTY (1570-1680)
- **Fourth** Vijayanagara dynasty; **7 rulers**; named after **Araviti (Aravidu)** village, **Kurnool** district.
#### 2.1 Rulers in Order
| Ruler | Key fact |
|---|---|
| Tirumala Raya (1570-72) | **Founder**, Rama Raya's brother; removed Sadasiva; crowned at **Penukonda, 1570** |
| Sriranga I (1572-85) | Eldest son; lost Kondaveedu to Ibrahim Qutb Shah |
| **Venkatapati II** (1585-1614) | Greatest, longest (29 years) |
| Sriranga II (1614) | Killed by Jagga Raya |
| Ramadeva (1616-30) | **Battle of Toppur (1616)** = "second Talikota"; Jagga Raya killed |
| Venkatapati III (1630-43) | Grant of Madras |
| **Sriranga III** (1643-80) | **Last**; defeated at **Vandavasi (1642)** by **Mir Jumla** of Golconda |
- Toppur was won by **Velugoti Yachama Nayaka** and **Raghunatha of Tanjore**.
#### 2.2 Capital Shifts
!KEY Hampi (till 1565) → **Penukonda** (1570, Tirumala) → **Chandragiri** (Venkata II) → **Vellore** (1600).
### 3. VENKATAPATI RAYA II (1585-1614)
- Titles: **"Second Krishnadevaraya"**, **"Second Andhra Bhoja"**.
- **Vilapakam inscription (1601)**: genealogy of Tuluva and Aravidu; **Siddhavatam fort (Kadapa) inscription (1605)** also his.
- Allowed **Dutch** trade (factory) at **Pulicat**.
- Allowed **Jesuit churches at Nellore and Chandragiri** (APPSC 2022).
- Court: **Appayya Dikshita** (Shaivarka Mani Dipika).
### 4. GRANT OF MADRAS – 1639
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Reign | Venkatapati Raya III |
| Grantor | **Damarla Venkatadri Nayaka** |
| Grantee | **Francis Day**, English East India Company, Machilipatnam council |
### 5. NAYAKA KINGDOMS AND ANDHRA PALEGARS
| Kingdom | Founder | Contemporary ruler |
|---|---|---|
| Madurai | **Vishwanatha Nayaka** (granted it after capturing rebel father Nagama); son **Krishnappa** made it independent c. 1565 | Krishnadevaraya / Achyutaraya |
| Tanjore | **Sevappa Nayaka** | Achyutaraya |
| Gingee | **Krishnappa Nayaka** | Tuluva period |
| Mysore | **Raja Wodeyar** (Srirangapatna, 1610) | **Venkata II** |
| Ikkeri (Keladi), c. 1500 | Chowdappa Nayaka | – |
- **Tirumala Nayaka** of Madurai (1623-59) built the **Tirumala Rayala Mahal**.
- **Amara-nayakas** became hereditary **Palegars** – forerunners of the **zamindars** (Kalahasti, Chittoor).
- **Mir Jumla** melted temple idols into cannon at **Gandikota, 1657**.
!TRAP Tirumala Raya = Aravidu founder (Penukonda); Tirumala Nayaka = Madurai.
### 6. LEGACY AND REDISCOVERY OF HAMPI
- Raya gopurams and kalyana mandapas spread via Nayaka courts.
- **Vasucharitra** – Ramaraja Bhushanudu; **Shaivarka Mani Dipika** – Appayya Dikshita.
| Person | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Caesar Frederick** (Venetian merchant) | Eyewitness of the ruined capital, **1567** |
| **Colin Mackenzie** | Surveyed and mapped Hampi, **1800**; later first Surveyor General of India |
| **Robert Sewell** | **A Forgotten Empire** (1900) |

3. King was Sadasiva; Rama Raya only regent.
4. Capitals: Penukonda → Chandragiri → Vellore.
