## U0706 | ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND MACHINE LEARNING – CONCEPTS, APPLICATIONS, NATIONAL AI STRATEGY, ETHICAL CONCERNS
### 1. AI – MEANING, PIONEERS AND TYPES
- **AI**: machines copying thinking functions of human intelligence (see, understand, respond).
#### 1.1 Pioneers
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Father of AI** | **John McCarthy** – coined the term "artificial intelligence" |
| Event of the term | **Dartmouth** summer workshop (**1956**), proposed in 1955 by McCarthy, Minsky, Rochester and Shannon |
| **Turing test** | **Alan Turing**, paper "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" (**1950**, journal *Mind*); original name **"the imitation game"**; opens with "Can machines think?" |
#### 1.2 Types of AI
| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Narrow / Weak AI (ANI)** | Made for **one task** (Siri, Alexa, Netflix recommendations); cannot move across fields |
| **General / Strong AI (AGI)** | Human-level reasoning across domains; **not yet achieved** |
| **Artificial Superintelligence** | **Hypothetical**; would beat humans in every way |
!TRAP AGI is not yet achieved; Siri and Alexa are narrow AI.
### 2. KEY CONCEPTS AND HARDWARE
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Machine learning** | A **branch (subset) of AI**; learns patterns from data without explicit programming; improves with experience. Types: **supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement** |
| **Deep learning** | Subset of ML using **multi-layered neural networks** (self-driving cars, ChatGPT) |
| **Random forest** | Group of **decision trees**; reduces **overfitting** |
| **NLP** | Machines understand and generate human language (chatbots, translation) |
| **Computer vision** | Interprets images (**facial recognition**) |
| **Generative AI** | Creates new text, images, music |
| **LLMs** | General-purpose models trained on huge data; do many language tasks **without task-specific retraining** |
| **Recommender systems** | Suggest content from **past behaviour** (Netflix) |
| **GPU** | Massively parallel processing; **workhorse of AI training** |
| **FPGA** | **Field Programmable Gate Array** – IC re-settable after manufacturing; reconfigurable |
| **TPU** | **Google's** AI chip |
- **What AI can do well**: cut electricity use in industrial units, **disease diagnosis**, **text-to-speech** – not "meaningful short stories and songs" (keyed 1, 3 and 4).

### 5. NATIONAL AI STRATEGY AND INDIAAI MISSION
#### 5.1 NITI Aayog Strategy (2018)
- **National Strategy for AI**, **2018**, tagline **"#AIforAll"**.
- **Five focus sectors**: **healthcare, agriculture, education, smart cities and infrastructure, smart mobility and transport** (not defence manufacturing).
- **RAISE** = **Responsible Artificial Intelligence for Social Empowerment**.
#### 5.2 IndiaAI Mission (2024)
- **MeitY**; approved **March 2024**; **₹10,371.92 crore over 5 years**; sovereign compute of **10,000+ GPUs**.
| IndiaAI pillar | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Compute Capacity | Affordable GPU compute |
| **Innovation Centre** | **Indigenous foundation models** / LMMs |
| **Datasets Platform** | **Public-sector datasets** |
| Application Development | Sector solutions |
| FutureSkills | AI courses; Data and AI Labs in smaller towns |
| Startup Financing | Capital for deep-tech startups |
| Safe & Trusted AI | Responsible-AI tools |
- National Program on AI (4 pillars): Skilling, Responsible AI, Data Management Office, National Centre on AI.

### 7. ETHICAL CONCERNS AND CHALLENGES
| Concern | Point |
|---|---|
| **Bias** | Sources: **data collection, algorithm design, human interpretation** ("ability" is not a source); ML copies bias in training data |
| Health AI bias | Trained on urban/Western data, may miss India's social, genetic, demographic diversity (Aug 2026) |
| **Deepfakes** | Made with **GANs, diffusion models**, large neural networks |
| **Privacy / facial recognition** | Identification without consent, mass surveillance, predictive policing |
| **Job displacement** | Both **manual and intellectual** work |
| **Explainability** | Deep models are **"black boxes"** – decisions hard to justify |
- Challenges for India: **GERD ~0.64–0.70% of GDP**; GPU shortage and imported chips.
- **Economic Survey 2025-26**: favour **small, application-specific models** and a state-built **"AI-OS"** modelled on **UPI and Aadhaar**.
- **14 Aug 2026**: Supreme Court sought Centre's reply on lack of a legal framework for **high-risk AI in welfare delivery**.
