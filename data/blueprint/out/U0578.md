## U0578 | SPECIAL ASSISTANCE AND THE A.P. REORGANISATION ACT COMMITMENTS
### 1. BACKGROUND – THE ACT AND ITS PROMISES
- **AP Reorganisation Act (APRA), 2014**: President's assent **1 March 2014**; appointed day (bifurcation) **2 June 2014**.
- Residuary AP got about **46% of estimated revenue** at bifurcation but carried a **larger share of debt**.
- **Census 2011 population ratio**: AP **58.32** : Telangana **41.68** – the basis of most liability splits.
- Promises are in the **sections** (finance, Polavaram, capital, Governor) and in the **Thirteenth Schedule** (education and infrastructure).
- **3 March 2022**: AP High Court order on the capital.
!NUM 58.32 : 41.68 = population ratio; 46% = AP's share of revenue; 58% population got 46% of power.

- Tax incentives come into effect only after AP **identifies and notifies** the eligible backward areas.
- Section 95: **equal opportunities** for quality higher education; Article 371D quotas and **common admission** for **10 years**.
### 2. SPECIAL CATEGORY STATUS (SCS)
#### 2.1 Origin and Benefits
- SCS began in **1969** (**Fifth Finance Commission** era); first given to **Jammu and Kashmir, Assam and Nagaland**.
- Benefits: Centrally Sponsored Schemes funded **90:10** (Centre : State), against **60:40** for other States; **concessional loans**; **tax breaks**.
#### 2.2 14th Finance Commission Position
- The **Fourteenth Finance Commission (2015)** removed the **special/general category distinction** in tax devolution.
- AP was given **special packages instead of SCS**.
- **Demand history**: AP, **Bihar** and **Odisha** still demand SCS (position as of January 2026).
!TRAP SCS is NOT granted by the APRA 2014. The Act has no SCS clause; the demand rests on assurances outside the Act.
### 3. SPECIAL ASSISTANCE MEASURE (PACKAGE IN LIEU OF SCS)
#### 3.1 Components
- Approved by the **Union Cabinet on 15 March 2017**.
- **Component 1 – Externally Aided Projects (EAPs)**: Centre repays **loans and interest** of EAPs signed in **2015-16 to 2019-20**; Centre bears **90%** (not 60%) of EAP cost.
- **Component 2 – Polavaram**: Centre funds **100% of the remaining cost of the irrigation component** for the period from **1 April 2014** (cost as on that date).
#### 3.2 SCS vs Special Assistance
| Point | Special Category Status | Special Assistance Measure |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | 1969: J&K, Assam, Nagaland | Given **in lieu of SCS** |
| Funding | **90:10** CSS (vs 60:40) | Centre bears **90% of EAP cost** |
| Other benefits | Concessional loans, tax breaks | EAPs signed 2015-16 to 2019-20; Polavaram irrigation |
| Status | Ended by 14th FC (2015) | Cabinet decision (2017) |
!TRAP **90:10** = SCS CSS funding pattern; **90%** = Centre's share of EAP cost under the package. Do not mix them.

### 6. POLAVARAM IRRIGATION PROJECT
#### 6.1 Section and Status
- **Section 90**: Polavaram declared a **national project**; Telangana's consent deemed given.
- APPSC 2021 (twice) and 2022 (statement question: all three parts of Sec 90 true).
!TRAP Polavaram = **Section 90**. Section **89** extends the **KWDT** term; it is often wrongly printed for Polavaram.
#### 6.2 Executing Authority and Features
- **Executing authority**: **Government of AP** executes the irrigation component **on behalf of GoI** and seeks reimbursement or advances.
- **CWC** clears safety and designs.
- **Irrigation**: **2.91 lakh ha** in former **East Godavari, Visakhapatnam, West Godavari and Krishna** districts.
#### 6.3 Clearances, Money and Friction
- **April 2026**: **Central Water Commission** cleared structural safety of the new **spillway blocks**, in line with the **Dam Safety Act, 2021**.
- **Union Budget 2026-27 (2 February 2026)**: **₹3,320 crore** towards the revised cost; storage up to **+41.15 m**.
- **Friction**: **revised cost estimates**, **land acquisition**, **R&R funding delays** (not coal shortage).

### 10. NON-FINANCIAL PROVISIONS – CAPITAL AND GOVERNOR
#### 10.1 Common Capital and Governor
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| 3 | Formation of **Telangana** |
| **5** | **Hyderabad (GHMC area)** = common capital for **not more than 10 years** (ended 2024) |
| 6 | **Expert committee** for AP's new capital |
| **7** | **One Governor** for both States, for a period set by the **President** |

- Under Sec 8 the Governor uses **individual judgment** after consulting the **Telangana Council of Ministers**; his decision is **final**.
- He is assisted by **2 advisers appointed by the Centre**.
#### 10.2 Amendment Act, 2026
- **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Act, 2026** amended **Section 5**: **Amaravati** = AP's **sole and permanent capital** (statutory status).
- **Retrospective from 2 June 2024** (end of the 10-year common-capital period).
- Lok Sabha debate on **1 April 2026**.
!KEY First time **Parliament named a State capital by law**.
