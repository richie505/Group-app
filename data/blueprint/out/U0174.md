## U0174 | COMPANY'S CONSTITUTIONAL FRAMEWORK – REGULATING ACT 1773, PITT'S INDIA ACT 1784, ACT OF 1786
### 1. BACKGROUND – PARLIAMENT BEFORE 1773
1. **1764:** Battle of **Buxar**.
2. **1765:** **Shah Alam** granted the **Diwani of Bengal, Bihar, Orissa** to the Company.
3. **1767:** Parliament capped the Company's **dividend at 10 per cent** and made it pay **£400,000 a year** to the state for its territorial revenues.
4. **1772:** Parliament appointed a **Select Committee (headed by Burgoyne)** and a **Secret Committee** to inquire into Company affairs.
5. **1773:** their reports led to the **Regulating Act** – the **first Parliamentary control** of the Company.
### 2. REGULATING ACT, 1773
- **First step** by the British Parliament to **control** the Company; **recognised its political and administrative functions**.
- **Governor of Bengal → Governor-General of Fort William in Bengal**, with supervisory powers; **Bombay and Madras subordinated** to Bengal.
- GG had a **council of four** (named in the Act: **Philip Francis, Clavering, Monson, Barwell**); decisions by majority; GG had only a **casting vote**.
- **Supreme Court at Calcutta (1774):** **1 Chief Justice + 3 puisne judges**; **civil and criminal** jurisdiction – India's first Supreme Court, at **Fort William**.
- **Court of Directors** had to report on **revenue, civil and military** affairs to the British Government.
- It **did not abolish the Court of Directors** (APPSC-GS 2019 "incorrect statement").
#### 2.1 Firsts
| First | Person |
|---|---|
| First GG of Bengal under the Act | **Warren Hastings** |
| First Chief Justice, Supreme Court Calcutta | **Sir Elijah Impey** |
!TRAP The 1773 Act did **not** abolish the Court of Directors and did **not** create the Ministry of Law and Justice.
### 3. DEFECTS OF 1773 → REMEDIES
| Defect | Remedy |
|---|---|
| GG had **no veto**; could be **outvoted** by his council (Hastings – Francis, Clavering, Monson opposed him) | **Act of 1786** – override power |
| Supreme Court's jurisdiction over the Council and over Indians **undefined** → Council–Court clashes | **1781 Act of Settlement** – official acts and revenue exempted |
### 4. AMENDING ACT, 1781 (ACT OF SETTLEMENT)
- **GG, Council and Company servants** exempted from the Supreme Court's jurisdiction for acts in their **official capacity**.
- **Revenue** matters excluded from the Court's jurisdiction.
- Court told to respect the **personal laws of Hindus and Muslims**.
### 5. PITT'S INDIA ACT, 1784
- Created the **Board of Control** – up to **six commissioners**, including a **Secretary of State** and the **Chancellor of the Exchequer**.
- **Commercial** affairs stayed with the **Court of Directors**; **political** affairs went to the **Board of Control** → **dual system ("Double Government")**.
- Stricter supervision of the Company's rule in India.
- The dual system lasted till **1858**, when the **Government of India Act, 1858** ended "Double Government".
!PAIR Court of Directors | Company | Commercial || Board of Control | Crown | Political
### 6. ACT OF 1786
- **Why passed:** to meet **Lord Cornwallis's** condition for accepting office.
- **Powers:** GG could **override his council** in special cases (right to reject the Council's decision) and also be **Commander-in-Chief**.
- Fixed the 1773 defect under which **Hastings was repeatedly outvoted**.
- **Charter Act of 1793** extended the overriding power to the **governors of the presidencies** (greater control over Bombay and Madras).
!KEY 1781 = Act of Settlement (curbs the Supreme Court); 1786 = override power (Cornwallis).
### 7. ACT → BODY OR POST CREATED – COMPARISON
| Act | Body / post / key provision |
|---|---|
| **1773 Regulating** | **GG of Bengal**; **Supreme Court**, Calcutta |
| **1781 Amending** (Act of Settlement) | Official acts and revenue exempted; personal laws respected |
| **1784 Pitt's India** | **Board of Control**; dual system till 1858 |
| **1786** | GG's **overriding power** + Commander-in-Chief |
| **1793 Charter** | Override power to presidency governors |
| **1833 Charter** | Law Member, first Law Commission – origin of the **Ministry of Law and Justice** |
- **Order:** Regulating Act 1773 → Amending Act 1781 → Pitt's India Act 1784 → Act of 1786.
- **AP angle:** the **Madras Supreme Court (1801)** served British subjects in the Madras Presidency, including Andhra.
- **Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955** is post-independence (earlier Untouchability (Offences) Act, 1955; renamed 1976) – not a British-era landmark.

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- First Parliamentary control: **Regulating Act, 1773**.
- First GG of Bengal: **Warren Hastings**; first Chief Justice: **Elijah Impey**.
- First Supreme Court in India: **Fort William, Calcutta (1774)**.
- Override power first given for: **Cornwallis (1786)**.
