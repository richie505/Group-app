#### 3.1 Short Site Profiles
- **Mohenjodaro**: largest city; Great Bath, Great Granary, pillared assembly hall, Pashupati seal, Dancing Girl, Priest-King.
- **Harappa**: first site found; granaries in two rows of six near the Ravi; Cemetery H and R-37; red sandstone male torso.
- **Lothal**: port town on the **Gulf of Khambhat**; brick-lined tidal dock **218 m x 37 m** with sluice gates; warehouse on a raised platform; Persian Gulf seal.
- **Kalibangan**: citadel and **fortified lower town**; ploughed field; seven fire altars; camel bones.
- **Dholavira**: citadel, middle town, lower town; built in **sandstone and limestone**; ten-sign signboard at the **North Gate**.
- **Surkotada**: fortified Kachchh settlement; disputed horse bones (c. 2000 BCE).
- **Rangpur**: rice husks; Late Harappan.
- **Banawali**: barley finds; radial streets.
- **Chanhudaro**: only city without a citadel; craft town of beads, bangles, shell ornaments and seals.

### 7. LOCAL NAMES AND HABITATION SPAN
| Site | Meaning / local name |
|---|---|
| Mohenjodaro | "**Mound of the Dead**" (Sindhi) |
| Kalibangan | "**Black bangles**" |
| Dholavira | Local name **Kotada** (large fort) |
- **Dholavira**: settled in seven stages, **c. 3000-1500 BCE**.
- **Banawali**: three phases – pre-Harappan, Harappan, post-Harappan.
- **Rangpur**: **Late Harappan** (Rojdi culture) phase.
- **Surkotada**: horse bones from all three periods, c. 2100-1700 BCE.
### 8. LARGEST SITES AND CURRENT RELEVANCE
#### 8.1 Size Ranking
- **Five largest** cities: **Mohenjodaro, Harappa, Ganweriwala, Rakhigarhi, Dholavira**.
- **Rakhigarhi** = **largest Harappan site in India** (about 350 ha; 11 mounds per Shinde).
- **Dholavira** = one of the biggest Harappan townships.
#### 8.2 UNESCO
| Site | Status |
|---|---|
| **Dholavira** | **UNESCO WHS 2021** (India's **40th**); **only** inscribed Harappan site in India |
| Mohenjodaro (Pakistan) | Inscribed **1980** |
| **Rakhigarhi** | **Tentative List** |
#### 8.3 Rakhigarhi DNA
- The female skeleton **lacked the "Steppe Pastoralist" gene**.
- Read as **indigenous ancestry**; it **challenges the Aryan-invasion** view.
#### 8.4 Lothal – National Maritime Heritage Complex (NMHC)
- Approved by the **Union Cabinet** in **October 2024**; Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways.
- **Sep 2026**: Phase 1A about **82%** complete; target revised to **31 Oct 2026**. Phase 1B to add eight more galleries and a lighthouse museum.
