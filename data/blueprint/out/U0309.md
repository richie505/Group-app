## U0309 | PARLIAMENTARY COMMITTEES – PAC, ESTIMATES, PUBLIC UNDERTAKINGS, DRSCs, AD HOC
### 1. ROLE AND TYPES OF COMMITTEES
- Committees are a primary tool of **parliamentary control over administration**: they do the **detailed scrutiny** the whole House has no time for.
- Parliament controls the administration through committees such as **PAC, Estimates** and **Standing Committees**.
#### 1.1 Two Types
| Type | Nature | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Standing** | **Permanent**; reconstituted annually or periodically | Financial committees, DRSCs, Petitions, Privileges |
| **Ad hoc** | **Temporary**; for a specific task; end after the report | **Inquiry committees**; **Select / Joint Committees on Bills**; JPCs |
#### 1.2 Full Classification
| Group | Committees |
|---|---|
| Standing – financial | **Estimates, PAC, COPU** |
| Standing – departmental | **24 DRSCs** |
| Standing – other | Petitions, Privileges, Subordinate Legislation, Government Assurances, Ethics, Business Advisory, Welfare of SCs and STs, Papers Laid on the Table, Rules |
| Lok Sabha-only | Committee on **Private Members' Bills and Resolutions**; Committee on **Absence of Members** |
| Ad hoc | Inquiry committees (e.g. MPLADS, Security in Parliament Complex, Railway Convention); Select / Joint Committees on Bills |
!TRAP **Inquiry committees** are **ad hoc**, not standing committees.
### 2. FINANCIAL COMMITTEES
| Committee | Strength | Rajya Sabha members | Chairman |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Estimates** | **30** (**largest**) | **None** – all Lok Sabha | Appointed by Speaker; invariably **ruling party** |
| **PAC** | **22** (15 LS + 7 RS) | Yes (associate) | Appointed by Speaker; **Opposition** member since **1967** (convention) |
| **COPU** | **22** (15 LS + 7 RS) | Yes | From LS members, appointed by Speaker |
- Members **elected yearly** by **proportional representation – single transferable vote**; term **1 year**; **Ministers ineligible**.
- Role: keep "an **unremitting vigil**" over spending and performance.
- **Budget** = control **before** expenditure; financial committees = control **after** expenditure – they support, not replace, the Budget.
!KEY Estimates = 30, no RS member, ruling-party chair; PAC = 22, Opposition chair.
### 3. PUBLIC ACCOUNTS COMMITTEE (PAC)
- Examines **Appropriation Accounts**, **Finance Accounts** and **CAG audit reports**.
- Checks that money was spent "**within the scope of the demand**" approved by Parliament; looks at waste and nugatory expenditure.
- Tests **efficiency, economy and propriety**, not only legality; may take up matters on its own.
- **Reports to the Lok Sabha** (Speaker).
- **Limits:** recommendations **not binding**; examines only **execution of policy, not policy** itself – a **post-mortem** check.
- **Chairman 2026-27: K.C. Venugopal** (Opposition, by convention).
!TRAP The Opposition chair since **1967** is a **convention**, not a constitutional or statutory rule (APPSC G2 2017).
### 4. COMMITTEE NICKNAMES AND ROLES
| Committee | Nickname / role |
|---|---|
| **Petitions** | **"Ombudsman"** of Parliament (APPSC 2025) |
| **Estimates** | **"Continuous economy committee"**; suggests **economies** and **alternative policies**; **financial control reforms** (APPSC G1 2018) |
| **PAC** | **"Watchdog"** on departmental expenditure and irregularities (APPSC G2 2017) |
- **Watchdog group** (full list): Subordinate Legislation, Government Assurances, Estimates, PAC, COPU, DRSCs – **not** the Business Advisory Committee.
!PAIR Ombudsman | Petitions | Public grievances || Watchdog | PAC | Expenditure
!TRAP The **Estimates Committee**, not the PAC, suggests economies and alternative policies.
### 5. ORIGIN OF COMMITTEES
| Committee | Year | Basis / recommending body |
|---|---|---|
| **PAC** | **1921** | **Government of India Act, 1919** |
| **Estimates** | **1950** (present form) | **John Mathai**; traces to 1921 Standing Financial Committee |
| **COPU** | **1964** | **Krishna Menon Committee** |
| **DRSCs** | **1993** (17 committees) | **Rules Committee**; **24 since 2004** |
| **Ethics** | RS **4 Mar 1997**; LS **16 May 2000** | — |
!NUM 1921 PAC – 1950 Estimates – 1964 COPU – 1993 DRSCs – 1997 Ethics (RS)
### 6. DEPARTMENTALLY RELATED STANDING COMMITTEES
- **24 DRSCs** = **16 under Lok Sabha + 8 under Rajya Sabha** (about 2:1).
- Each: **31 members = 21 LS + 10 RS**; **quorum 11** (not 15); **Ministers ineligible**.
- Functions: examine **Demands for Grants**, **Bills referred**, **annual reports** and **long-term policy** of their ministries.
- 24 DRSCs and ad hoc committees may review **independent regulators** (telecom, insurance, electricity) in their departments.
- **Latest:** MPs' average attendance at 16 DRSCs and the PAC only **53%** since the September reconstitution; **five meetings** postponed for lack of quorum.
!TRAP 22 is the strength of the **PAC**, not the number of DRSCs (24).
### 7. OTHER STANDING COMMITTEES
| Committee | Function | Strength / date |
|---|---|---|
| **Petitions** | Petitions on Bills and public-interest matters | LS 15, RS 10 |
| **Privileges** | Questions of privilege referred by House / Chair | LS **15** (nominated by Speaker), RS **10** |
| **Subordinate Legislation** | Checks use of **delegated rule-making** | LS 15, RS 15 |
| **Government Assurances** | Tracks ministers' assurances till implemented | LS 15, RS 10 |
| **Ethics** | **Members' conduct** | RS 1997; LS 2000 |
| **Business Advisory** | Allots **time** for government and other business | LS 15; **Speaker chairs** |
- In **both Houses**: Petitions, Privileges, Rules, Papers Laid, Subordinate Legislation, Government Assurances.
- **Latest:** **Jairam Ramesh**'s privilege notice against Education Minister **Dharmendra Pradhan** over remarks on a standing committee.
!TRAP Ethics came to the **Rajya Sabha first (1997)**, Lok Sabha later (2000).
### 8. COMMITTEE ON WELFARE OF SCs AND STs
- **30 members = 20 LS + 10 RS**; **Ministers cannot serve**.
- Examines reports of the **National Commissions for SCs and STs**.
- Examines **welfare programmes** for SCs/STs in Union ministries and bodies.
!NUM 31 (21+10) = each DRSC; 30 (20+10) = SC/ST Welfare; 30 all-LS = Estimates
### 9. AD HOC COMMITTEES – SELECT, JOINT AND JPCs
- A Bill goes to a **Select Committee** (one House) or **Joint Committee** (both Houses) by a **motion in the House**.
#### 9.1 JPCs on Scams
| Scam | Year |
|---|---|
| **Bofors** | **1987** |
| **Harshad Mehta** securities scam | **1992** |
| **Ketan Parekh** | **2001** |
| **Soft-drink pesticides** | **2003** |

#### 9.2 Currently before JPCs
- **Constitution (129th Amendment) Bill, 2024** (One Nation One Election).
- **FCRA (Amendment) Bill, 2026**.
- **Viksit Bharat Shiksha Adhisthan Bill, 2025** – before a **JPC** (both Houses), not a single-House Select Committee.
- **Debate:** PAC is a post-mortem check, its advice is not binding, and frequent **guillotine** weakens financial control.
