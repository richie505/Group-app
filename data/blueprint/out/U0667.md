## U0667 | HUMAN DEVELOPMENT IN ANDHRA PRADESH – INDICATORS AND DISTRICT-LEVEL VARIATION
### 1. HDI BASICS AND AP's HDI
- Published by **UNDP**; first Human Development Report **1990**; conceived by **Mahbub ul Haq** with **Amartya Sen**.
- Three dimensions: **health** (life expectancy), **education** (mean + expected years of schooling), **income** (**GNI per capita, PPP**, used as a logarithm).
- Since **2010**: dimension indices combined by a **geometric mean**.
- HDI hides inequality within a State → **IHDI**, district indices.

#### 2.1 Multidimensional Poverty (NITI Aayog)
- National MPI: NITI + UNDP + OPHI; **3 dimensions, 12 indicators**.
- AP MPI **0.053 → 0.025** (2015-16 to 2019-21, 50% fall); headcount **6.06%** vs India **14.96%**.
- AP the **9th least-poor** State (APSES 2024-25).
- Lowest-poverty districts: **West Godavari (2.42%)**, Guntur 4.36%, Krishna 4.38%, Chittoor 5.66%.
!TRAP AP is **9th** in both SDG India Index and MPI.
### 3. AP DEVELOPMENT VISION
- **Sunrise AP Vision 2029**: HDI of over **0.9** by **2029**; aim to be the best State by 2029.
- **Swarna Andhra @2047**: GSDP **USD 2.4 trillion**, per capita income over **USD 43,000**, **100% literacy**.
### 4. SOCIAL COMPOSITION
- APSES 2024-25: **SCs 17.08%**, **STs 5.53%** of AP population.
- Census 2011: AP SC population **84.69 lakh** = **4.21%** of India's SCs.
- Highest SC share (Census 2011): **Prakasam (23.19%)**, also the largest SC number.
