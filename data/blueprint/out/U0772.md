## U0772 | SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT IN PRACTICE – GREEN ECONOMY, CIRCULAR ECONOMY AND ESG
### 1. GREEN ECONOMY AND GREEN TECHNOLOGY
- **Green economy**: reduces environmental risks and ecological scarcity; **decouples growth from resource use and pollution**; **central theme of Rio+20 (2012)**.
- **Green jobs**: work in farming, industry, services or administration that **preserves or restores environmental quality**.
- **Green technology**: pollutes less, uses resources sustainably, recycles more of its waste.
!TRAP Incorrect statement: "green technology is not expensive for developing countries". **High cost is its main barrier** – green technology is expensive for developing countries.
### 2. CIRCULAR ECONOMY
- Economic system built on **reuse, recycling and waste minimisation**; waste becomes a resource.
- PYQ answer: "promotes the **reuse, recycling and repair** of materials to reduce waste".
| Linear model | Circular model |
|---|---|
| Take, make, dispose | Reduce, reuse, repair, recycle; keep materials in use |
| Waste = **end point** | Waste = **resource**; growth decoupled from resource use |
- How it cuts GHG emissions: it **reduces raw-material use** and **reduces production waste** (both correct).
- India's action plans: **NITI Aayog + MoEFCC** committees → plans for priority waste streams (**plastics, e-waste, batteries, tyres, metals**).
!TRAP Circular economy does **not** focus exclusively on carbon or fossil fuels.
### 3. INTERNATIONAL GREEN ECONOMY INITIATIVES
- **UNEP Green Economy Initiative**: launched **2008**.
- **PAGE** (Partnership for Action on Green Economy): launched **2013** in response to the **Rio+20** call; brings together **UNEP, ILO, UNIDO, UNDP, UNITAR**.
#### 3.1 European Green Deal (December 2019)
- **Climate-neutral EU by 2050**; at least **55% GHG cut by 2030 vs 1990**; legislative package = **"Fit for 55"**.
| Component | Content |
|---|---|
| Farm to Fork | Sustainable food system |
| Circular Economy Action Plan | Resource efficiency, recycling |
| **Biodiversity Strategy** | **Protect and restore ecosystems** |
| **Just Transition Fund** | **Support for affected regions** |
| Renovation Wave | Energy-efficient buildings |
!NUM Order: GEI 2008 → Rio+20 2012 → PAGE 2013 → GSDP 2017 → Green Deal 2019 → Green Credit 2023
### 4. GREEN SKILL DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMME
- Launched **2017** by **MoEFCC**, building on the **ENVIS** network.
- Trains youth for green jobs: **biodiversity conservation, pollution monitoring, waste management, forestry and wildlife**, allied environment sectors.
### 5. GREEN CHEMISTRY AND CLEANER PRODUCTION
- **12 Principles of Green Chemistry**: **1998**, **Paul Anastas and John Warner**; core idea – **waste prevention rather than cleanup**.
- **Green chemistry + cleaner production** reduce waste and pollution across the **whole product life cycle** → serve the goals of **EIA** and **Life-Cycle Assessment (LCA)** (PYQ key).
- **Green Pharmacy** = green chemistry applied to design, use and disposal of **medicines**.
### 6. GREENWASHING
- **Greenwashing** = **false or exaggerated claims** that a product, firm or investment is environment-friendly.
- Remedy: a **clear taxonomy**, so regulators can curb **greenwashing and "transition washing"**.
### 7. GREEN FINANCE
| Instrument | Use of proceeds / fact |
|---|---|
| **Green bond** | Renewables, green transport, climate; pioneers **EIB 2007**, **World Bank 2008** |
| **Sovereign Green Bonds** | Announced **Budget 2022–23**; framework 2022; **first issue 2023, Rs 8,000 crore** |
| **Blue bond** | Ocean and water projects; world's first sovereign – **Seychelles 2018** |
| India's first blue bond | **Sagarmala Finance Corporation**, Rs 1,000 crore, planned FY 2026–27 |
| **RBI Green Deposit Framework** | **2023**; banks raise deposits earmarked for green lending |
- **Climate finance taxonomy**: announced **Budget 2024–25** (**Ministry of Finance**); **Ministry of Steel** has a **Green Steel Taxonomy**.
- Gaps: **no final all-sector green taxonomy**; Rs 35 crore per-borrower PSL cap.
- Municipal green bonds are rare (**Ghaziabad 2021, Indore 2023**); most ULBs lack credit ratings.
- **MSE-GIFT**: green finance for MSMEs (2023–26).
### 8. ESG AND BRSR
- **ESG** = **Environmental, Social, Governance** (three pillars).
- **BRSR** (Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting): set by **SEBI**; **top 1,000 listed companies** publish **audited ESG metrics every year**.
- **ESG ratings**: **SEBI regulates ESG Rating Providers**.
- Public financial institutions publish Green Bond Frameworks + ESG financing policies; carry ESG impact ratings.
- SEBI BRSR and the taxonomy gap were discussed at the **Bonn Climate Conference 2026**.
### 9. RESOURCE EFFICIENCY, EPR AND GREEN CREDIT
- **Draft National Resource Efficiency Policy, 2019** (**MoEFCC**): promotes **secondary raw materials** + a **National Resource Efficiency Authority**.
- **EPR regimes**: **plastic packaging, e-waste, batteries, tyres**. 2025–26: **308 lakh tonnes** recycled; **296.53 lakh tonnes** of EPR certificates.
- **Green Credit Programme**: notified by **MoEFCC, October 2023**; **market-based incentive** for voluntary actions (tree plantation, water conservation).
- **Green Hydrogen Mission**: outlay **Rs 19,744 crore**; **5 MMT a year**; linked to **net zero 2070**.
- **AMRUT 2.0**: reuse treated water for **20% of city demand + 40% of industrial demand**.
