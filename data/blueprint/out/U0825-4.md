#### 9.3 CEEW – "Cattle and Community in a Changing Climate (2026)"
- By **Council on Energy, Environment and Water (CEEW)**, released **20 January 2026**.
- **38%** of cattle rearers (~**30 million households**) **do not sell milk**; 31% rear mainly for household milk; ~5.6 million households fully non-dairy.
- In 9 of 15 States, less than half cited milk sales as the main motive.
| State | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Jharkhand** | **71%** do not prioritise milk sales |
| West Bengal, Himachal Pradesh | over 50% do not sell milk |
| Maharashtra, Karnataka | over 30% prioritise dung/draught |
- 50% own just 1–2 animals; 82% own only one bovine type; smallholders – 29% of milk production, 22% of sales.
- ~75% cite feed affordability; silage/ration-balancing adoption ~5%.
- Climate impacts reported: 54% buffalo, 50% crossbred, 41% indigenous cattle rearers.
- Dairy supports **80 million+ livelihoods**; ~**5% of GDP**.
- **Rashtriya Gokul Mission**: launched **2014** – indigenous breeds, Gokul Grams.
- Livestock GHG: **90%+ from enteric fermentation**; **crossbred cattle ~43 kg methane/yr** vs **28 kg indigenous**.
- **Buffaloes produce over 50% of India's milk** though they are fewer than cattle.
