## U0732 | HUMAN SPACEFLIGHT – GAGANYAAN AND BHARATIYA ANTARIKSH STATION
### 1. GAGANYAAN PROGRAMME
- India's **first human spaceflight programme**; approved **2018**.
- Goal: **crew of 3** to a **400 km LEO** for a **3-day** mission; Crew Module splashes down in **Indian sea waters**.
- Vehicle: **HLVM3** (human-rated LVM3, HRLV) from Sriharikota; GSLV Mk-III structures redesigned; **Vikas** engine for L110 stage qualified in **3 tests** (730 s).
#### 1.1 Spacecraft modules
| Part | Role |
|---|---|
| **Orbital Module** | **Crew Module + Service Module**; put into orbit by HLVM3 |
| **Crew Module** | Pressurised habitat for 3; survives re-entry; parachutes, sea splashdown |
| **Service Module** | On-orbit support; fires thrusters to **de-orbit**, then burns up |
!TRAP Gaganyaan = **two** parts; Russia's **Soyuz** and China's **Shenzhou** = **three** modules (orbital, descent, service).
#### 1.2 Flights and timeline
- Sequence: uncrewed **G1 → G2 → G3 → crewed H1**; H1 now targeted **Q1 2027**.
- Original target: December 2021 (stated 2018) → repeatedly revised; quote with the statement date.
- Own crewed launch: **USSR (Gagarin, Vostok, 1961), USA, China** → India would be **4th**.

### 6. ANALOGUE MISSIONS
- **India's first analogue space mission**: **Leh, Ladakh**, **Nov 2024** (with **AAKA**, **IIT Bombay**) – simulates life in an interplanetary habitat; data for selection, **ECLSS**, work-rest schedules, ground-crew protocols.
- **HOPE** (Himalayan Outpost for Planetary Exploration): **Tso Kar**, Ladakh; early-Mars-like – high UV, low pressure, cold, saline permafrost.
- **MITRA**: crew **behavioural** study at Leh with **IAM**; hypoxia, cold, isolation.
### 7. MICROGRAVITY AND THE HUMAN BODY
- **Weightlessness** = free fall: satellite and astronaut fall with the same **g**, so the **normal force is zero**; mass stays the same.
- No candle flame in space; astronauts cannot stand erect; an apple released moves along with the ship.
- Moon weight = **1/6** of Earth's; at **Earth's centre**, true g is zero.
| Condition | Effect |
|---|---|
| Bone loss | 1–1.5% bone mass a month |
| Muscle atrophy | Postural muscles weaken |
| **Fluid shift** | "**Puffy face, bird legs**" |
| **Space Adaptation Syndrome** | Space motion sickness |
| **SANS** | **Optic disc swelling**, vision changes |
