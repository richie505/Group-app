## U0661 | HUMAN DEVELOPMENT – CONCEPT, HDI COMPONENTS AND INDIA'S POSITION
### 1. CONCEPT – HUMAN DEVELOPMENT VS ECONOMIC GROWTH
- Human development = **enlargement of human choices**, not just a rise in national income.
- **Economic growth** = quantitative (income-linked); **development** = quantitative + qualitative.
- Two countries with the same GNI per capita can reach different human outcomes.
- **Amartya Sen** – capability approach: capabilities = **substantive freedoms** to lead lives people value; he avoided a fixed universal list. Book: "**Development as Freedom**" (1999); **Nobel in Economics 1998**.

### 3. HDI – DIMENSIONS, INDICATORS, CALCULATION
| Dimension | Indicator | Goalposts |
|---|---|---|
| Long and healthy life | **Life expectancy at birth** | 20–85 years |
| Knowledge | **Mean years of schooling** (adults 25+) + **expected years of schooling** | MYS 0–15; EYS 0–18 |
| Standard of living | **GNI per capita** (PPP, log) | $75,000 cap |
- HDI = **geometric mean** of the three indices since **2010** (replaced arithmetic mean); literacy and gross enrolment then gave way to MYS and EYS.
| Category | HDI value |
|---|---|
| Very High | 0.800+ |
| High | 0.700–0.799 |
| Medium | 0.550–0.699 |
| Low | below 0.550 |
!TRAP NOT in HDI: inequality, poverty, gender gaps, human security, empowerment, environment, **military expenditure**, MPI.

### 5. RELATED INDICES
| Index | Measures |
|---|---|
| **IHDI** | HDI discounted for inequality; IHDI < HDI unless no inequality; gap = loss due to inequality |
| **GDI** | Female-to-male HDI ratio; near 1 = parity |
| **GII** | Reproductive health, empowerment, labour market; **0 = equality, 1 = worst** |
| **MPI** | Deprivations in health, education, living standards (UNDP-OPHI) |
| PQLI | Literacy, infant mortality, life expectancy (pre-HDI) |
| Human Poverty Index | Forerunner of MPI |
- HDI criticisms: ignores inequality, sustainability, gender, political freedom (it does include health and education). Top 10% earn 13x bottom 10% (World Inequality Database, 2023-24).
### 6. NATIONAL MPI AND STATES
- **NITI Aayog** (Jan 2024): multidimensional poverty fell **29.17% (2013-14) → 11.28% (2022-23)**; **24.82 crore** moved out; biggest fall **UP** (5.94 crore), then Bihar, MP. 12 indicators, Alkire-Foster method.
- **First NHDR of India: 2001** (Planning Commission, released 23 Apr 2002); Kerala top.
- State HDI: **Kerala + Goa** above 0.86; **Bihar lowest 0.650** (sub-national series, India 0.732 – do not mix with UNDP's 0.685).
- MPI extremes: Kerala 0.002 vs Bihar 0.16.
- **AP**: MPI halved 0.053 (2015-16) → 0.025 (2019-21); headcount **6.06%** vs national 14.96%; lowest district **West Godavari (2.42%)**.
