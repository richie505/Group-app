## U0134 | MUGHAL ADMINISTRATION – MANSABDARI, JAGIRDARI AND THE JAGIRDARI CRISIS
### 1. MANSABDARI SYSTEM
- Introduced by **Akbar**; "Mansab" = **Arabic** for **rank/position**. Model borrowed from **Central Asia / Mongolia** (Genghis Khan's decimal army grading).
- One grading for **army and civil service**; aim – clean administration; run by the **Mir Bakhshi**.
- Ranks **10 to 10,000**; Abul Fazl: **66 grades** in theory, **33** in practice; highest reserved for princes.
- **Not hereditary** – rank and jagir ended with the holder. **Not all were military** – high civil officials were also mansabdars.
- Paid in cash (**naqd**) or by a revenue assignment (**jagir**). Mughal state = basically a **military state**.
### 2. ZAT, SAWAR AND CHANGES
| Term / class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Zat** | Personal rank – status and salary |
| **Sawar** | Cavalrymen to be maintained |
| First / Second / Third class | Sawar = zat / half or more of zat / less than half |
- **Horse rule**: every **10 sawars → 20 horses**.
!TRAP Sawar could NOT exceed zat under the system.
| Change | Emperor | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Du-aspa sih-aspa** | **Jahangir** | Part of sawar counted double; zat not raised |
| Month-scale | **Shah Jahan** | **Shashmaha** = half-yield; **sihmaha** = quarter-yield |
| Reduced obligation | Shah Jahan | Keep 1/5 to 1/3 of sawar |
| **Mashrut** | **Aurangzeb** | Temporary, conditional rise in sawar for a task |
### 3. ARMY AND MUSTER TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ahadis** | Gentlemen troopers recruited and paid by the **emperor**; served singly, emperor as their colonel |
| **Dakhili** | State-paid troops placed under mansabdars |
| **Tabinan** | Mansabdars' own contingents |
| **Walashahi** | Emperor's bodyguard |
| **Dagh** / **Chehra** / **Tashiha** | Horse branding / descriptive roll / muster check |
- Akbar **revived** Dagh and Chehra; army mostly mansabdars' cavalry.
### 4. CENTRAL OFFICIALS
| Official | Function |
|---|---|
| **Wakil** | Chief adviser/regent; made symbolic by Akbar |
| **Wazir / Diwan** | Revenue and finance |
| **Mir Bakhshi** | Military, mansabdars, intelligence |
| **Mir Saman** | Royal household, **karkhanas** |
| **Sadr-us-Sudur** | Religion, charity, **madad-i-maash** |
| **Qazi-ul-Quzat** | Chief justice (sharia) |
- Espionage: **waqia-navis** (news-writers), **khufia-navis** (secret), **harkaras** – under the Mir Bakhshi.
### 5. PROVINCIAL SET-UP AND REVENUE OFFICIALS
- Ascending: **Village → Pargana → (Chakla) → Sarkar → Subah**. **Chakla** = group of parganas (Shah Jahan).
- Subahs: **12 (1580) → 15** under Akbar; **21** under Aurangzeb.
| Level | Officials |
|---|---|
| Subah | **Subahdar** (law and order); **Diwan** (revenue, check on Subahdar) |
| Sarkar | **Faujdar** (military, law and order); **Amalguzar** (revenue); **Bitikchi** (land records, revenue papers); **Khazanadar** (treasurer) |
| Pargana | **Shiqdar** (executive, police); **Amil** (revenue); **Qanungo** (head of patwaris, records); **Karkun** (clerk) |
| Town / Village | **Kotwal** (town police, markets); **Patwari** (village accountant) |
### 6. JAGIRS – TYPES; JAGIRDAR VS ZAMINDAR
| Type | Nature |
|---|---|
| **Tankhwah** | In lieu of salary; transferable |
| **Watan** | Hereditary home of zamindar/Rajput chief; not transferred |
| **Mashrut** | Conditional |
| **In'am** | Free of service |
| **Altamgha** | Perpetual |
| **Khalisa** | Crown land – NOT a jagir |
| Jagirdar | Zamindar |
|---|---|
| Revenue right in lieu of salary; no land ownership, no judicial/police duty | Hereditary local rights |
| Often transferred | State–peasant intermediary |
### 7. JAGIR TRANSFERS AND JAGIRDARI CRISIS
- Transfers every **3–4 years** – to stop local roots and feudal power.
- **Bejagiri** – mansabdars waiting without jagirs → discontent, faction.
- Under **Aurangzeb**: more mansabdars, less good land (**paibaqi**) → peasants squeezed → main reason for **Mughal decline**.
| Historian | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Satish Chandra** | Shortage of jagirs |
| **Irfan Habib** | Agrarian crisis |
| **Athar Ali** | Influx of Deccani and Maratha nobles |
- AP angle: **Asaf Jahi Hyderabad** kept Mughal-style jagirdari.
