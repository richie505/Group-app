## U0115 | SULTANATE SOCIETY – NOBILITY, ULEMA, WOMEN, SLAVERY, CONVERSION AND SYNCRETISM
### 1. SOCIAL GROUPS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Kulah-daran** | **Sayyids** (pointed cap) |
| **Dastar-bandan** | **Ulema** (turban) |
| **Ashraf** | Muslims of foreign descent – elite (Turks, Persians, Afghans, Sayyids) |
| **Ajlaf** | Lower-status Muslims |
| **Rais / Ranas** | Local Hindu chiefs who paid tribute and collected taxes |
!TRAP Kulah (cap) = Sayyids; Dastar (turban) = Ulema – do not swap.
### 2. ULEMA, QAZIS AND BARANI
- **Sadr-us-Sudur**: religious grants + **Chief Qazi**.
- **Qazi-ul-Quzat** = chief judge; qazis applied the Sharia as explained by the ulema.
- **Barani**: Sultanate **not truly Islamic** because Sultans framed their own regulations (**zawabit**) beside sharia; also because most people did not follow Islam.
- Barani's ideal of governance: ***Fatawa-i-Jahandari***; his history: ***Tarikh-i-Firoz Shahi***.
### 3. RELIGIOUS POLICY OF SULTANS
| Sultan | Approach |
|---|---|
| Iltutmish | Tolerant; gave non-Muslims posts |
| **Jalaluddin Khalji** | **First** Sultan benevolent to Hindus |
| **Alauddin Khalji** | Kept ulema out; told **Qazi Mughisuddin** he ordered what was good for the state |
| **Khusrau Shah (1320)** | Hindu gods worshipped in the royal palace |
| Muhammad bin Tughlaq | Leaned on **Sufis** against ulema; visited Ajmer shrine |
| **Firoz Tughlaq** | Followed ulema; theocratic; respected **Egyptian Caliph**; extended jizya to **Brahmins** |
| **Sikandar Lodi** | Orthodox; destroyed **Mathura** temples, broke **Jwalamukhi** images at **Nagarkot**; imposed jizya |
- Temple destruction: also **political** (legitimacy) and economic (temple wealth).
### 4. JIZYA
- **Protection tax** on non-Muslims in place of military service.
- Exempt: **women, children, old, disabled**, and at first Brahmins.
- **Qutb Shahis of Golconda never imposed jizya**; Shia rulers who supported Hindu temples.
### 5. POSITION OF WOMEN
- **Purdah** (seclusion, veiling) spread among upper-class women of north India from the 13th century.
- **Sati** documented by **Ibn Battuta**.
- **Jauhar** against Alauddin: **Ranthambhor (1301)**, **Chittor (1303)**.
- **Razia Sultana (1236–40)**: only woman ruler of Delhi; opposed because she was a woman.
!TRAP Not Delhi rulers: **Lal Ded** (Kashmir saint), **Rudrama Devi** (Kakatiya), **Chand Bibi** (defended Ahmadnagar).
### 6. SLAVERY
- Sources: **war captives**, vassal tribute, abduction; town slave markets.
- **Alauddin's third market**: horses, slaves, cattle (besides grain market and cloth market **Sarai-i-Adl**).
- **Firoz Tughlaq**: about **1,80,000** slaves; created **Diwan-i-Bandagan**.
- **Aibak, Iltutmish, Balban** were slaves (bandagan) – hence **Slave/Mamluk dynasty**.
### 7. CONVERSION THEORIES
| Theory | Status |
|---|---|
| Sword (forced mass conversion) | Rejected as colonial trope; coercion isolated |
| Political patronage | Partial factor (office, tax relief) |
| Social liberation | Partial; caste-like ranks survived |
| **Sufi influence + agrarian expansion** | **Major** in **Bengal, Punjab** (pirs, dargahs) |
- **Meenakshipuram (Feb 1981)**: 558 Dalits embraced Islam – "social liberation" case.
### 8. SYNCRETISM
| Person / text | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Al-Biruni** | Compared Sufi divine love with the **Gita** (not Arthashastra); Sufi soul with Yoga Sutra |
| **Amritakund** | Hatha-yoga text into Arabic and Persian |
| **Sheikh Nuruddin** | Founded Kashmir's **Rishi** movement; vegetarian; served all castes |
| **Chishtis** (Muinuddin, Nizamuddin Auliya, Baba Farid) | Spoke in **Hindavi**; khanqahs, dargahs, Urs, qawwali |
| **Amir Khusrau** | Credited with **sitar** and **qawwali** |
| **Malik Muhammad Jayasi** | ***Padmavat*** – human love as divine love |
| ***Dalail-i-Firozshahi*** | Sanskrit texts into Persian under **Firoz Tughlaq** |
- **Bhakti** had native roots (**Alvars, Nayanars**); Islam strengthened its egalitarian, anti-ritual side.
#### 8.1 AP and Deccan Angle
- **Gesu Daraz (Bandanawaz)** moved to **Gulbarga** under Firoz Shah Bahmani; wrote ***Mirqat-ul-Ashyaq*** (Persian, early Dakhni).
- Golconda: foreign Muslims = **Afaqis**; Brahmins got agraharas and **Mustajir** (tax-farming) rights.
- **Abul Hasan Tana Shah** granted villages to **Bhadrachalam** temple; **Akkanna, Madanna** = "Andhra Sultans".
- Vijayanagara slavery = **Benabaga**.
