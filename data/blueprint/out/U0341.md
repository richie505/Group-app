## U0341 | REGIONAL PARTIES IN INDIA AND IN ANDHRA PRADESH – RISE, ROLE AND IMPACT
### 1. RISE OF REGIONAL PARTIES
#### 1.1 Causes
- **Regional economic neglect** (not uniform development).
- **National parties' failure** to accommodate State leaders.
- **Identity**: language, caste, region – e.g. TDP's "**Telugu atma gauravam**".
- After **1989**: coalition arithmetic raised the weight of regional parties.
- Roots of regionalism: **historical, geographical, linguistic (22 scheduled languages), religious, ethnic**.
- Strong regional parties grow from regional aspirations (true, but this does **not** explain why India has a written Constitution).
#### 1.2 Andhra Social Base
- In the **Justice Party** era, non-Brahmin agrarian castes – **Reddys, Kammas, Kapus, Velamas** – awakened politically; this became the base for Andhra regional parties.
!KEY Andhra regional politics rests on non-Brahmin agrarian castes awakened in the Justice Party era.

### 4. TELUGU DESAM PARTY – FOUNDING AND FIRSTS
#### 4.1 Founding and First Victory
- Founded by **NTR in 1982** on **Telugu self-respect (Telugu atma gauravam)** against perceived slights from the Centre.
- Won power within **nine months** – **January 1983** Assembly election – one of the fastest rises of any party.
- Ideology: no clear ideology beyond **Telugu pride and welfare populism**.
#### 4.2 End of Continuous Congress Rule in AP
- The **1983** election ended Congress's **unbroken rule since the State's formation (1956)**; the winner was the **TDP**.
- AP then became bipolar: **Congress vs TDP**; since **2014**: **TDP vs YSRCP**.
#### 4.3 National Role
- **8th Lok Sabha (1984)**: TDP with **30 seats** = **main opposition party** – the **first regional party** to reach that position.
- **1988**: **NTR** = **chairman of the National Front**, which formed the **V.P. Singh** government in **1989**.
- **30 April 2002**: TDP (backing NDA from outside) **abstained** on the opposition censure motion on the Gujarat riots in the Lok Sabha.
- **2018**: TDP **left the NDA** over **Special Category Status** for residuary AP.
- Now: TDP leads AP's ruling coalition with **BJP support**.
!NUM 1982 founded → Jan 1983 power → 1984: 30 LS seats, main opposition → 1988 National Front chairman → 2018 left NDA.

### 7. REGIONAL VS NATIONAL PARTIES
- **Six national parties**: **BJP, INC, CPI(M), BSP, NPP, AAP**.
- Everything else – **TDP, YSRCP, Jana Sena, DMK, AITC, BJD, SP, Shiv Sena, BRS** – is a State or unrecognised party.
- **Akali Dal** = regional party of **Punjab**.
- A regional party can rule **alone** or **dominate a coalition**.

### 8. IMPACT OF REGIONAL PARTIES
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Coalition role | **Kingmakers** – supplied **Speakers, Union Ministers, survival votes** |
| PM's position | Coalitions made the PM "**Primus Inter Pares**" (first among equals), not all-powerful |
| Federalism | More genuinely **bargained after 1989** |
| Merits | Representation of **backward regions**, balanced development |
| Demerits | **Parochialism**, instability |
- **Special Category Status**: demanded for residuary AP after 2014; TDP left NDA over it (2018).
- **One Nation, One Election** is feared to **weaken regional parties**; the first four general elections (1951-52, 1957, 1962, 1967) were simultaneous.
