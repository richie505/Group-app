### 2. POWER DISCOMS AND REFORMS
#### 2.1 Present DISCOMs
| DISCOM | Full form | HQ | Areas |
|---|---|---|---|
| **APEPDCL** | AP **Eastern** Power Distribution Company Ltd | Visakhapatnam | Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam, East and West Godavari |
| **APCPDCL** | AP **Central** Power Distribution **Corporation** Ltd | **Vijayawada** | Krishna, Guntur, Prakasam |
| **APSPDCL** | AP **Southern** Power Distribution Company Ltd | **Tirupati** | Nellore, Chittoor, Kadapa, Anantapur, Kurnool |
#### 2.2 Reforms
- **2000 unbundling**: four DISCOMs – EPDCL (Visakhapatnam), CPDCL (**Hyderabad**), NPDCL (Warangal), SPDCL (Tirupati).
- APRA 2014: Anantapur and Kurnool moved to APSPDCL.
- Present Vijayawada APCPDCL **carved out of APSPDCL in 2020**, not one of the 2000 four.
- **UDAY** (Ujwal Discom Assurance Yojana, approved **Nov 2015**): financial turnaround, lower interest burden, operational efficiency. Replaced by **RDSS (2021)**.
- Smart meters and feeder segregation cut AT&C losses; APEPDCL and APSPDCL among top performers (Jan 2026).
!TRAP PYQ key: APCPDCL = "Corporation", the other two = "Company".
### 3. ENERGY POLICIES
#### 3.1 AP Integrated Clean Energy Policy 2024
- Launched **June 2024**; absorbed the **AP Green Hydrogen and Green Ammonia Policy, 2023** (asked G1 Prelims 2024).
- Targets: **160 GW+** clean energy, **Rs 10 lakh crore**, **7.5 lakh jobs**, **Net Zero by 2047** (not 2030).
- Progress: approvals Rs 2.88 lakh crore; Partnership Summit, Visakhapatnam (14-15 Nov 2025) Rs 4.54 lakh crore.
#### 3.2 Green Hydrogen
- **Green Hydrogen Valley Declaration**, Amaravati, **19 July 2025**: AP as "Green Hydrogen Capital"; cut cost Rs 460/kg to Rs 160-170/kg; nodal **SRM University**.
- National Green Hydrogen Mission hubs **outside AP**: Paradip (Odisha), Kandla (Gujarat), Tuticorin (Tamil Nadu).
