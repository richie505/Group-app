## U0727 | COMMUNICATION SATELLITES – INSAT AND GSAT SERIES
### 1. ORBITS AND THE GEOSTATIONARY ORBIT
| Orbit | Fact |
|---|---|
| Low Earth | 160–2,000 km; **ISS ~400 km** |
| Geostationary (GEO) | About **36,000 km** (precisely **35,786 km**) over the **equator**; period **24 hours** |
| Polar | Passes over the poles |
| Elliptical | Apogee and perigee differ |
| Circular | Centripetal force equals gravitational force |
- A geostationary satellite revolves at a **fixed height**; the height does **not depend on its mass**.
- The centripetal force keeping it in orbit is the **Earth's gravity**.
- Launcher → elliptical **GTO** → own motor raises it to GEO.
### 2. INSAT SYSTEM
#### 2.1 Firsts
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1981 | **APPLE** (Ariane Passenger PayLoad Experiment) – first experimental comsat, on Ariane |
| 1982 | **INSAT-1A** – first INSAT (INSAT-1 series bought abroad) |
| 1983 | INSAT system commissioned; **INSAT-1B** made it operational |
| 10 Jul 1992 | **INSAT-2A**, Kourou – first of the fully **indigenous** INSAT-2 series (1992-99) |
| Sep 2002 | **KALPANA-1 (METSAT)**, PSLV – first exclusive weather satellite |
#### 2.2 Design and management
- **Multipurpose**: telecom, TV, **meteorology**, disaster warning, search-and-rescue on one GEO platform; key: "**Meteorological Satellite**".
- Joint venture of **DoS, DoT, IMD, AIR, Doordarshan**; coordinated by **ICC**; **Master Control Facility, Hassan** (Karnataka).
- **NSIL** took 10 in-orbit comsats (2022); **19** operational comsats (2026).
- **INSAT-4C** (2,168 kg, Rs 350 crore) lost when **GSLV-F02** exploded 60 s after lift-off, **10 Jul 2006** → **INSAT-4CR** (Sep 2007, GSLV-F04).

### 6. TRANSPONDER BANDS
| Band | Use |
|---|---|
| C / extended C | Telecom, TV distribution |
| Ku | **DTH, VSAT** |
| Ka | High-throughput broadband (GSAT-11, GSAT-N2) |
| S | Mobile satellite services, DWS downlink |
- **MF band is NOT used**. High-throughput: GSAT-19, -29, -11, N2; support **BharatNet**.
### 7. APPLICATIONS AND DISASTER MANAGEMENT
- **DTH**, **VSAT**, **DSNG** (news gathering), **MSS** (mobile), GRAMSAT, telemedicine.
- **Swayam Prabha**: **GSAT-15**, 40 free DTH education channels.
- **DWS**: Cyclone Warning Centre **Chennai**; uplink **C**, downlink **S** to addressable coastal receivers.
- Search and rescue: **COSPAS-SARSAT**.
- **AP**: SDSC-SHAR gives APSDMA cyclone-track data; RTGS–SHAR MoU (**Jun 2025**); **satellite phones** in emergencies.
