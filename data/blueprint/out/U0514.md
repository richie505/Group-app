### 2. INSTALLED POWER CAPACITY
- India (31 July 2026): about **552 GW**; **non-fossil 300.5 GW = over 54%**.
- Non-fossil mix: **solar 164.59 GW**, wind 58.14 GW, hydro 57.24 GW, bio-power 11.75 GW, nuclear 8.78 GW.
- FY 2025-26: 55.29 GW non-fossil added (44.6 GW solar).
- AP (31 July 2025): **27,586.93 MW**; thermal 13,907.67 MW; RE **13,488.39 MW** (solar 5,523.28, wind 4,397.78, large hydro 2,810 MW). AP added 3,865.63 MW RE in 2025-26.
- Battery storage: about **500 MWh** (Sep 2025) vs **336 GWh** needed by 2029-30.
- **Primary energy** = energy in natural resources before human conversion (raw fuels); "Primary Energy - Tidal Power" keyed as NOT correctly matched.

### 4. BIOFUELS
- **National Policy on Biofuels 2018** (amended 2022) / EBP: **20% ethanol blending by 2025** - to cut oil imports and help sugarcane farmers. **E20 achieved in 2025**.
- Ethanol feedstocks: sugarcane juice, sugar beet, sweet sorghum, **damaged food grains**, rotten potatoes, maize, cassava.
- **National Biofuel Coordination Committee (NBCC)**: headed by **Minister of Petroleum and Natural Gas**; approves use of **surplus food grains** for ethanol.
!KEY PYQ 2025 (A-R): NBCC approval is needed for surplus food grains because in surplus years farmers risk not getting remunerative prices - **A and R true, R explains A**.
### 5. AP ENERGY POLICIES
| Policy | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **AP Solar Power Policy 2018** | Nodal **NREDCAP**; ~**300 sunny days**; Govt to develop 4,000 MW initially |
| **AP Green Hydrogen and Green Ammonia Policy 2023** | Make AP a **global green hydrogen hub**; **0.5 MTPA green hydrogen + 2 MTPA green ammonia** in 5 years; nodal NREDCAP |

| **AP Integrated Clean Energy Policy 2024** | One framework for RE, pumped storage, green hydrogen, biofuels, EV charging, RE manufacturing; capital subsidies and tax exemptions |
!TRAP "1,00,000 solar pump sets in five years" is NOT correct about AP Solar Policy 2018 (APPSC 2018).
- Central schemes in AP: **PM-KUSUM** for **2.93 lakh pumps**; **PM Surya Ghar** with Virtual Net Metering (SC/ST focus); **RDSS ₹15,083.14 crore**.
- **PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana** (13 Feb 2024): rooftop solar on **1 crore houses**; up to **300 units free** per month. MNRE runs local training on rooftop installation.
### 6. COAL SECTOR
- **Nationalisation**: coking coal mines 1971-72; non-coking under **Coal Mines (Nationalisation) Act, 1973**.
- **Coal Mines (Special Provisions) Act 2015**: after the Supreme Court cancelled coal block allocations; allocation by **auction**, run by the **Nominated Authority**.
- **Coal Controller's Organisation** (Ministry of Coal; HQ **Kolkata**): checks **coal quality and grading**, settles quality complaints, collects coal statistics.
- **Energy Statistics India** - published by **NSO, MoSPI** (2026 edition, 30 March 2026); coal supplies about 79% of domestic energy.

- **BPCL**: under MoPNG; Government majority stake (exam key: 50-60%).
### 8. ELECTRICITY ACT 2003 AND DISTRIBUTION SCHEMES
- Replaced **Indian Electricity Act 1910, Electricity (Supply) Act 1948, ERC Act 1998**.
- **Section 63** = tariffs through **competitive bidding**.
- National Electricity Policy: "reducing per unit price of electricity" was NOT an objective.
| Scheme | Year | Objective |
|---|---|---|
| **DDUGJY** | 2014 | Rural **feeder separation** |
| **UDAY** (Ujwal DISCOM Assurance Yojana) | Cabinet **5 Nov 2015** | DISCOM financial turnaround; States take over **75% of DISCOM debt** |
| **Saubhagya** | 2017 | **Household** electrification |
| **RDSS** | 2021 | Cut **AT&C losses**; **smart metering** |
- AT&C losses **22.62% (FY14) → 15.04% (FY25)**; demand-supply gap **4.2% → zero** (Nov 2025).
### 9. ENERGY SECURITY
- Imports: about **85% of petroleum** (over 80%), about **50% of gas**.
- **Strategic Petroleum Reserves**: **Visakhapatnam, Mangaluru, Padur** - about **9.5 days** cover vs IEA norm **90 days**.
- Price decontrol: **petrol 26 June 2010**; **diesel 19 October 2014**.
