## U0575 | CENTRAL ASSISTANCE TO ANDHRA PRADESH – FINANCE COMMISSION TRANSFERS AND CENTRALLY SPONSORED SCHEMES
### 1. CENTRAL SECTOR VS CENTRALLY SPONSORED SCHEMES
| Feature | Central Sector (CS) | Centrally Sponsored (CSS) |
|---|---|---|
| Subjects | Union List | **State/Concurrent** |
| Funding | **100% Centre**, run by Central Ministries | **60:40** general; **90:10** special category/NE/Himalayan |
| Examples | **PM-KISAN, Ayushman Bharat**, MPLADS | **NHM, ICDS**, PMAY, PMKSY |
| Union spending 2026-27 | 17% | 8% |
- **VB-G RAM G Act** replaced MGNREGA (Centre paid 100% of unskilled wages) with **60:40** for most States.
- New 5-year CS/CSS appraisal cycle from 1 Apr 2026; **16th FC wants outcome-linked CSS**.
- **AP ranked first** among States in **28 CSS and central sector schemes** (up to Nov 2018).
- **SNA-SPARSH**: **just-in-time** release of CSS funds via **State IFMIS, RBI e-Kuber and PFMS**; 28 States + 3 UTs, 66 CSS; idle float ₹1.67 → 0.4 lakh cr.
!TRAP Criticism: CSS matching shares squeeze States' fiscal space.
### 2. PLAN-ERA FINANCING (HISTORICAL)
- State Plans = **own resources + Normal Central Assistance** via **Gadgil (Gadgil-Mukherjee) formula**.
- NCA loans:grants = **70:30** general; **10:90** special category.
- **Plan/non-Plan** distinction dropped from **2017-18 Budget** (Outcome Budget came earlier, 2005).
- 2017-18 Budget also moved to **1 February** and **merged Railway Budget**; it did NOT increase the number of CSS.
!TRAP Gadgil 70:30 (loans:grants) vs CSS 60:40 (sharing).

- SCS not given; **Special Assistance Measure ₹24,350 cr**, incl. **₹3,979 cr RD grant**.
