## U0218 | ANTI-ZAMINDARI MOVEMENTS IN ANDHRA – CAUSES, LEADERS AND OUTCOMES
### 1. CAUSES OF THE ANTI-ZAMINDARI MOVEMENT
- About **40%** of Andhra's land was still under zamindars at **Independence**.
- In Madras Presidency, **70%** of zamindari land lay in Andhra districts (Vizag, Nellore, the two Godavaris, Krishna, Chittoor).
- **Pattabhi** first called for an anti-zamindari struggle in **1920**.
- **Inam ryots** fought for abolition of inam estates under the **Madras Estates Land Act, 1908**.
#### 1.1 Grievances and Exactions – Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Nazarana** | Annual **renewal fee** for tenancy |
| **Vetti** | **Forced free labour** |
| **Pullari** | **Grazing tax**; collected at Venkatagiri even in drought |
| **Bedalli** | **Eviction** for non-payment |
| **Gothi** | **Hereditary bonded labour** in the Agency |
| **Kancha paddhati** | Grazing land **auctioned yearly** (Venkatagiri) |
- Other exactions: forest-produce, water and cattle taxes; rent enhancement; loss of forest and grazing rights.
!TRAP Pullari = grazing tax; Nazarana = renewal fee; Bedalli = eviction; Gothi = bonded labour.
### 2. MADRAS ESTATES LAND ACT, 1908 AND AMENDMENTS
| Act | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **Madras Estates Land Act** | **1908** | Regulated landholder-ryot relations; did **not** abolish estates |
| Amendment Act (Madras Act VIII) | **1934** | Substituted section 8; "an occupancy ryot" replaced by "a ryot" |
| **Third Amendment** (Madras Act XVIII) | **1936** (in force **31 Oct 1936**) | **Occupancy rights** for those in direct possession on **30 June 1934**; brought some inam villages within "estate" |
- **Vizag Zamindar Rythu Sabha (3 Nov 1929)** demanded amendment of the 1908 Act.

| Estate | District | Zamindar / year | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bobbili** | Vizianagaram | **1922**; led by **Simhadri Suryanarayana** | Unfair land tax; resolution reduced land tax |

### 6. PRAKASAM COMMITTEE (1937-38)
- Appointed **1937** by **Rajaji's** Madras Congress ministry; **Prakasam** was **Revenue Minister**.
- Also called the Estate Land / Zamindari Enquiry Committee.
- Report completed **7 Nov 1938**.
- Finding: **land belongs to the tiller; the zamindar is only a tax collector**.
- Prakasam regretted that it did not say outright whether zamindari should be abolished.
### 7. MADRAS ESTATES (ABOLITION AND CONVERSION INTO RYOTWARI) ACT, 1948
| Step | Date |
|---|---|
| Bill introduced by Revenue Minister **Kala Venkata Rao** | **11 Nov 1947** |
| Passed by Madras Assembly | Nov 1948 |
| Assent of **Governor-General C. Rajagopalachari** | **2 April 1949** – first law of independent India with the GG's assent |
| Came into force | **7 Sept 1949**; estates taken over from **1950** |
- Year of the Act = **1948** (G2 2008: pick 1948); it is sometimes called the "1949 Act" because of assent and enforcement.
- Provisions: abolished **zamindari and inamdari** estates; tenants became **direct ryots of the State**; zamindars **compensated**.
- Shielded by the **1st (1951)** and **4th (1955)** Constitutional Amendments.
- Andhra's agitations made it one of the **first abolition laws** of independent India.
- Chronology (G2 2025): Guntur acquired (1788) → Padmanabham (1794) → Ryotwari → abolition.
- Communists pioneered the demand for land to the tiller and zamindari abolition (Apr 2026 review).
!PAIR Prakasam | Revenue Minister 1937 | Committee || Kala Venkata Rao | Revenue Minister 1947 | Abolition Bill

4. Gampalagudem grievance = rent in **kind**, not cash.
5. **Pullari** = grazing tax, not renewal fee.
6. Estates Land Act **1908** regulated tenancy; it did **not** abolish estates.
7. Mandasa firing = **1940**, Srikakulam; Palemkota = **1933**, Venkatagiri.
8. Munagala wage cut = **80 to 60** seers.
