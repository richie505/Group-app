## U0013 | IVC RELIGION – PASHUPATI SEAL, MOTHER GODDESS, TREE AND ANIMAL WORSHIP, FIRE ALTARS
### 1. NATURE OF HARAPPAN RELIGION
- IVC was **predominantly secular**: religion present but not dominant.
- **No temple** found; worship domestic or open-air. **Great Bath** (Mohenjodaro) = ritual purification.
- **Idol worship** began in the **Pre-Aryan** period; common in the IVC. People worshipped **both male and female deities**.
- PYQ key: Indus people believed in **Spirit and Brahma**; they worshipped **Shiva** (Pashupati).
!TRAP UPSC 2013: "no palaces, no temples, no horse chariots" – only "worshipped both male and female deities" is correct.
### 2. PASHUPATI SEAL AND MOTHER GODDESS
| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Site | **Mohenjodaro** |
| Figure | **Three-faced**, horned headdress, **yogic posture** |
| Animals | **Elephant, tiger, rhinoceros, buffalo**; **deer** below |
| Identification | **Proto-Shiva / Pashupati** (lord of animals) – **John Marshall** |
| Debate | Deity of animals and fertility; script undeciphered |
- **Mother Goddess**: terracotta female figurines → **fertility cult**; Harappa, Kot Diji, Kalibangan, Gujarat sites. Probably the main deity.
- **Horned deity on a triangular terracotta cake** – **Kalibangan**; other side shows a human pulling a goat by a rope. Clay mask of horned god – Kalibangan and Gujarat.
### 3. ANIMAL, TREE AND SYMBOL WORSHIP
- Sacred animals: **humped bull, unicorn** (mythical), elephant, tiger.
- **Pipal** = most depicted tree on seals, in ritual scenes; motif from **Early Harappan** phase.
- **Linga and yoni**, round **pierced stones** (Harappa) = fertility objects; early Shaivite elements. **Swastika** = later auspicious symbol.
- Seals: mostly **steatite** (few terracotta, agate, chert); mostly **square** (few cube, circle, cylindrical).
- **Lion** remains not found; cow and elephant found; horse disputed.
### 4. FIRE ALTARS AND BURIALS
- **Row of seven fire altars** on mud-brick platforms – **Kalibangan**; also **Lothal**. Link to Vedic yajna debated.
- Amulets show deities in yogic poses. Grave goods (pottery, ornaments, mirrors, beads, food) → **afterlife** belief and status differences.

| Artefact | Site | Material |
|---|---|---|
| **Dancing Girl** | Mohenjodaro | **Bronze**, lost-wax (cire perdue) |

- Dancing Girl: found **1926** by **Ernest Mackay** (under Marshall); about **four inches**; bangles on **left arm**, right hand on hip; among earliest bronzes in the world.
- **Pottery**: **red slip with black** painted designs – pipal leaves, fish scales, intersecting circles.
- **Script**: **undeciphered**, pictographic; written **right to left** (some boustrophedon); about **400+ signs**; short inscriptions.
- Toys, masks: terracotta; **dice** at Harappa; **lipstick** at Chanhudaro; mirrors, combs; gold, silver, shell ornaments.
### 6. POLITY, CLASSES AND CONTINUITIES
- Planned cities and drains → municipal governance; citadel → ruling group (debated); house sizes and grave goods → stratification.
| Harappan | Later form |
|---|---|
| Pashupati | Shiva / **Rudra** |
| Mother Goddess | **Shakti** cults |
| Linga-yoni | Linga worship |
| Pipal | Sacred pipal |
| Great Bath | Ritual bathing |
- Harappan = nature and fertility; Rig Vedic = male gods (**Indra** warrior, **Agni** fire, **Varuna** cosmic order), open-air yajnas, no temples, no idols. **9th Mandala** = Soma.
- Lakshmi on coins of **Muhammad Ghori**; Chaitanya = Krishna bhakti; Guru Nanak = Nirgun Brahma.
- **AP**: **Gudimallam** (Chittoor, Satavahana era) = oldest Shiva lingam / Shaivite temple in AP.
- **Rakhigarhi (Jun 2026)**: cylindrical steatite seal with alligator motif and five signs; gold foundry; only Harappan site with ancient DNA.
