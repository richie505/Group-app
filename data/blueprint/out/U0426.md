### 2. LOOK EAST → ACT EAST
- Look East drivers: **countering China**, new markets, instability in West and Central Asia.
- Act East was conceived as **economic**, later extended to **political, strategic and cultural** fields (true statement, **APPSC G1 2024**).
- **3Cs**: **Commerce, Culture, Connectivity**; 4th C = **Capacity building** (not Climate action).
- **North-East** = gateway to ASEAN.
- ASEAN-linked projects: **Kaladan Multi-Modal** (India–Myanmar) and **India-Myanmar-Thailand Trilateral Highway**.
!TRAP **Akhaura–Agartala rail** is with **Bangladesh**, not ASEAN (UPSC 2026 answer "1 and 2").

### 3. NEIGHBOURHOOD FIRST
- Launch: **SAARC leaders invited to the PM's swearing-in (May 2014)**; **BIMSTEC** leaders in **2019**.
- Roots: **Gujral Doctrine** — **non-reciprocity**, non-interference, no use of territory against a neighbour.
- Examples: COVID vaccines to neighbours, Bhutan hydropower, Kaladan, Trilateral Highway, Maldives **High Impact Community Development Projects Phase III**, Sri Lanka rooftop solar.

### 5. LINK WEST / THINK WEST
- **GCC**: India's largest socio-economic partner — over **$160 billion** trade, **10 million** diaspora.
- PM Modi: **15 visits** to GCC states and **two** to Israel (Mar 2026).
- **I2U2 ("West Asia Quad")**: **India, Israel, UAE, USA**; first leaders' summit **virtual, 2022**; handled by MEA's **Economic Diplomacy Division**.
### 6. SAGAR, MAHASAGAR AND SHANTI
| Vision | Year / place | Full form |
|---|---|---|
| **SAGAR** | **2015, Mauritius** | **Security and Growth for All in the Region** |
| **MAHASAGAR** | **2025, Mauritius** | Mutual and Holistic Advancement for Security and Growth Across Regions |
| **SHANTI** | **13 July 2026**, **EAM Jaishankar** | Securing Holistic Advancement through Norms, Trust and Integrity |
- SAGAR casts India as **net security provider** in the IOR; partly an answer to China's **String of Pearls**.
- SHANTI: rules framework for shared sea spaces; first in the **Bay of Bengal**; launched with India's **UNSC 2028–29** campaign.
- Debate: US sinking of Iran's **IRIS Dena** (Mar 2026) challenges the "net security provider" claim.
!TRAP SAGAR and MAHASAGAR both in Mauritius; SHANTI by the EAM, not the PM.
### 7. INDO-PACIFIC VISION AND IPOI
- **Shangri-La Dialogue keynote, June 2018**: free, open, inclusive Indo-Pacific with **ASEAN centrality**. Dialogue held in **Singapore since 2002**, run by London-based **IISS**.
- **IPOI** (Indo-Pacific Oceans Initiative): proposed at the **14th East Asia Summit, Bangkok, November 2019**; open, **non-treaty**, builds on SAGAR.
#### 7.1 Seven IPOI Pillars
1. Maritime Security
2. Maritime Ecology
3. Maritime Resources
4. Capacity Building and Resource Sharing
5. Disaster Risk Reduction and Management
6. Science, Technology and Academic Cooperation
7. Trade Connectivity and Maritime Transport
!TRAP **Nuclear Disarmament Verification** is NOT an IPOI pillar.
### 8. MARITIME SECURITY PARTNERSHIPS
- **IFC-IOR** (Information Fusion Centre – Indian Ocean Region): inaugurated **22 December 2018** at **IMAC, Gurugram**; hosted by the **Indian Navy**; maritime domain awareness through information sharing.
- **Coastal surveillance radar chain**: **Sri Lanka, Maldives, Mauritius, Seychelles**; to be strengthened along with **IORA** (Mar 2026).
- **Colombo Security Conclave**: began as a **2011 trilateral** of India, Sri Lanka, Maldives; now also Mauritius, Bangladesh and Seychelles.
- **AP angle**: **MILAN 2026** at **Visakhapatnam** ties AP to Act East and SAGAR.
