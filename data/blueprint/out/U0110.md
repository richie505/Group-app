## U0110 | TIMUR'S INVASION; SAYYID AND LODI DYNASTIES; FIRST BATTLE OF PANIPAT, 1526
### 1. FROM TIMUR TO BABUR – TIMELINE
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **1398** | **Timur** sacks Delhi (Sultan **Nasiruddin Mahmud Shah Tughlaq**) |
| 1412-13 | Last Tughlaq dies (1412); **Daulat Khan** Sultan (1413) |
| **1414** | **Khizr Khan** takes Delhi; **Sayyid** dynasty |
| **1451** | **Alam Shah** gives throne to **Bahlol Lodi** |
| 1498 | **Vasco da Gama** arrives; Delhi ruler **Sikandar Lodi** |
| **1504** | Sikandar Lodi founds **Agra** |
| 1517 | **Khatauli**: **Rana Sanga** defeats Ibrahim |
| **21 April 1526** | **First Panipat**: Babur kills Ibrahim |
### 2. DELHI SULTANATE DYNASTIES
| Order | Dynasty | Period | Founder | Origin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mamluk (Slave) | 1206-90 | Qutbuddin Aibak | Turk |
| 2 | Khalji | 1290-1320 | Jalaluddin Khalji | Turk (Khalji) |
| 3 | Tughlaq | 1320-1414 | Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq | Turk |
| 4 | **Sayyid** | **1414-51** | **Khizr Khan** | Claimed descent from the Prophet |
| 5 | **Lodi** | **1451-1526** | **Bahlol Lodi** | **Afghan** |
- **Longest** dynasty: Tughlaq (94 years); **shortest**: Sayyid (37 years) after the Khaljis (30 years).
- **Lodis** = **first Afghan** dynasty and **last** dynasty of the Sultanate; **Ibrahim Lodi** = last Delhi Sultan.
- Chronology item: **Rukn-ud-din** (1236) → **Mubarak Khan** (1316-1320) → **Firuz Shah Tughluq** (1351-88) → **Alam Shah** (1445-51).
- Rukn-ud-din ruled after Iltutmish's death though Iltutmish had named **Raziyya**.
### 3. TIMUR'S INVASION, 1398
- Route: crossed the **Indus** → Punjab via **Multan** and **Bhatnair** → sacked Delhi (**December 1398**).
- Sultan on the throne: **Nasiruddin Mahmud Shah Tughlaq**, the last Tughlaq (d. 1412).
- Effects: Tughlaq state broke into regional kingdoms (**Jaunpur, Malwa, Gujarat**); Timur's nominee **Khizr Khan** later founded the Sayyid dynasty.
!TRAP The Mughal Empire was not an effect of Timur; it was founded by Babur in 1526.
### 4. SAYYID DYNASTY (1414-51)
- **Khizr Khan** (1414-21), Timur's **viceroy**: arrested/defeated **Daulat Khan Lodi**, took Delhi in 1414.
- Ruled as Timur's **deputy, without the title of Sultan**.
| Order | Ruler | Reign |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Khizr Khan** | 1414-21 |
| 2 | **Mubarak Shah** | 1421-34 |
| 3 | **Muhammad Shah** | 1434-45 |
| 4 | **Alauddin Alam Shah** | 1445-51 |
- **Mubarak Shah**: failed against the **Khokhars**; **Tarikh-i-Mubarak Shahi** by **Yahiya bin Ahmad Sarhindi** written in his reign; his **octagonal tomb** has **blue enamelled tiles** and **guldasta** motifs.
- End: **Alam Shah voluntarily** gave the throne to **Bahlol Lodi** (1451), without a battle.
!KEY Sayyid order: Khizr Khan → Mubarak Shah → Muhammad Shah → Alam Shah.
### 5. LODI DYNASTY (1451-1526)
| Ruler | Reign | Key points |
|---|---|---|
| **Bahlol (Bahlul) Lodi** | 1451-89 | Founder; Governor of Punjab; throne from Alam Shah; conquered **Jaunpur**; **ruled longest** |
| **Sikandar Lodi** | 1489-1517 | Founded **Agra**; **Gaz-i-Sikandari**; pen name **Gulrukhi** |
| **Ibrahim Lodi** | 1517-26 | Arrogant to nobles; **killed at Panipat** |
- Order: Bahlol → Sikandar → Ibrahim (son follows father each time).
- Lodi kingship: Sultan as "**first among equals**" among Afghan nobles.
#### 5.1 Sikandar Lodi
- **Nizam Khan**, third son of Bahlol; crowned **17 July 1489**.
- Founded **Agra (1504)**; shifted the capital there from Delhi.
- **Gaz-i-Sikandari** (Gaj-i-Sikandari): yard to **measure land**.
- Tax remissions: abolished the **tax on grains**, several taxes and trade restrictions; used **audits and spies**; punished embezzlement by nobles.
- Rule from **Punjab to Bihar**; took Jaunpur; defeated **Bengal** rulers and **Rajput** chiefs.
- Wrote **Persian poetry** under the pen name **Gulrukhi**.
- Destroyed **Jwalamukhi (Nagarkot)** images and **Mathura** temples, imposed **jizya**; yet had a **Sanskrit medical work translated into Persian**.
- Built **Moth ki Masjid** and royal tombs of the **Lodi Gardens**.
#### 5.2 Ibrahim Lodi
- Nobles plotted to enthrone his brother **Jalal Khan**.
- Seized **Gwalior** (which had sheltered Jalal Khan); **Vikramajit** of Gwalior made a vassal.
- **Khatauli (1517)**: defeated by **Rana Sanga of Mewar**.
- Contemporaries: **Krishnadevaraya**, **Sultan Quli Qutb-ul-Mulk** (Golconda, independent 1518), **Rana Sanga** (not Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq).
### 6. INVITATION TO BABUR
| Person | Role |
|---|---|
| **Daulat Khan Lodi** | Governor of **Punjab**; invited Babur |
| **Alam Khan** | Ibrahim's **uncle** and pretender to the Delhi throne; invited Babur |
| **Rana Sanga** | Offered to join against Ibrahim (as per Babur) |
- Cause: Ibrahim's arrogance **alienated the Afghan nobles**.
- Babur's descent: **Timur** (father's side), **Chengiz Khan** (mother's side).
- **Krishnadevaraya** (1509-29) was a contemporary of Babur.
### 7. FIRST BATTLE OF PANIPAT, 1526
- Date: **21 April 1526**; Babur (about **12,000**) beat Ibrahim (about **1,00,000**) by **skilled warfare** and **skilled commandants** despite a smaller army.
#### 7.1 Tactics
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Tulughma** | **Flanking** (side) attack |
| **Araba** | **Carts chained** together as a defence line (**"Rumi"/Ottoman** device) |
| Field artillery and muskets | **Decisive factor**; gunners **Ustad Ali Quli** and **Mustafa** |
- **Artillery first used in India** at Panipat; Babur first mentions guns at **Bajaur (1519)**.
#### 7.2 Consequences
- Ibrahim = **only Delhi Sultan to die on the battlefield**.
- Babur captured **Delhi and Agra** and the Lodi treasure.
- **End of the Delhi Sultanate**, start of the **Mughal Empire** (founded by Babur; consolidated by Akbar; Sher Shah = Sur dynasty).
- **21 April 2026** = **500th anniversary** of the battle.
### 8. BABUR'S BATTLES
| Battle | Year | Defeated |
|---|---|---|
| **First Panipat** | 1526 | Ibrahim Lodi |
| **Khanwa** | 1527 | **Rana Sanga** |
| **Chanderi** | 1528 | **Medini Rai** |
| **Ghaghra** | 1529 | The **Afghans** |
- **Khanwa (16 March 1527)**: Babur declared **Jehad**, took the title **Ghazi**; broke Rajput power.
- Khanwa was **more decisive** than Panipat: Rana Sanga was a stronger enemy; it set up Mughal supremacy in North India.
- Mughal-period battle order: **Khanwa → Ghaghra → Chausa → Samugarh**. Later: Second Panipat **1556**; Plassey **1757**.
