## U0304 | PARLIAMENT – COMPOSITION OF LOK SABHA AND RAJYA SABHA, QUALIFICATIONS, TERMS, SPECIAL POWERS OF RAJYA SABHA
### 1. PARLIAMENT – ARTICLE 79 AND THE PRESIDENT'S PLACE
- **Article 79**: Parliament = **President + Council of States (Rajya Sabha) + House of the People (Lok Sabha)**. Parliament is in **Part V, Articles 79–122**.
- President is a **part of Parliament** because **no Bill becomes law without his assent** (Art 111) – like the **Crown-in-Parliament** (UK), unlike the US President, who is separate from Congress.
- But **Article 59(1)**: the President is **not a member** of either House or a State Legislature; an MP/MLA elected President gives up his seat. He does not sit or take part in discussions.
- President's legislative powers: summons and prorogues Parliament, dissolves the LS; **Art 86** – may address either or both Houses; **Art 87** – **special address** at the first session after each general election and the first session of each year; assent – **absolute, suspensive (return once, not a Money Bill), pocket veto**; nominates **12** to RS; **ordinances (Art 123)** when Parliament is not in session.
!KEY Assertion-Reason: "Parliament consists of the President and two Houses" – true; the President's opening address is a supporting reason.

### 2. LOK SABHA – COMPOSITION (ARTICLE 81)
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Maximum | **550** = up to **530** from States + up to **20** from UTs |
| Present | **543** = **524** States + **19** UTs (after J&K reorganisation 2019 and DNH–DD merger) |
| Election | Direct, adult suffrage (Art 326, 18 years), first past the post |
| First Lok Sabha | **489** elected seats |
| Lok Sabhas so far | **18** |
- Older maximum **552** (530 + 20 + **2 Anglo-Indians**, Arts 81, 331); earlier "sanctioned strength" **545** = 543 + 2 nominated.
- **Article 331**: President could nominate **2 Anglo-Indians** to LS (Governor to Assemblies) if not properly represented – ended by the **104th Amendment Act, 2019** (from **25 Jan 2020**), which also extended SC/ST reservation by 10 years (**till 2030**). No nominated LS members now.
- Strength history: **7th Amendment, 1956** – LS **520**; **31st Amendment, 1973** – **525 → 545** (States 500 → 525; UTs 25 → 20), and the 530 + 20 limits.
- Of **543** constituencies (2009), **530** were from States (older split 530 + 13).
- **Article 330**: SC/ST seats in proportion to population; first for **10 years**, repeatedly extended.
!TRAP 550 = present maximum; 552 = old figure with Anglo-Indians; 543 = present elected. An APPSC question took 552 as the maximum.

#### 3.1 Nominated members (Art 80(1)(a), 80(3))
- **12** nominated by the **President** (not the VP) for special knowledge in **literature, science, art and social service** – **sport is not listed**.
- Take full part in debates; **vote in the VP's election, not the President's**; may join a party within **6 months**, after that disqualified (Tenth Schedule). No nominee has so far been in the Council of Ministers.

### 6. TERMS OF THE HOUSES (ARTICLE 83)
#### 6.1 Lok Sabha – Art 83(2)
- **5 years from its first meeting**; may be **dissolved earlier** by the President on the PM's advice – cannot be challenged in court.
- **National Emergency**: extended by law **1 year at a time**, not beyond **6 months after** the Emergency ends.
- **5th LS** (15 Mar 1971 – 18 Jan 1977, about 6 years) – extended during the 1975–77 Emergency.
- Dissolved early: **4th (1970), 6th (1979), 9th (1991), 11th (1997), 12th (1999), 13th (2004)**.
- **9th LS**: 2 Dec 1989 – 13 Mar 1991. **12th LS**: elections Feb–Mar 1998, constituted 10 Mar 1998, dissolved 26 Apr 1999. **13th LS**: 10 Oct 1999 – 6 Feb 2004.
#### 6.2 Rajya Sabha – Art 83(1)
- **Permanent House**, never dissolved; about **one-third retire every second year**.
- Member's **6-year term** fixed by the **RPA, 1951**, not the Constitution; casual vacancy filled for the **rest of the term**.
- First RS (1952): members to retire first chosen by **lottery**.
### 7. QUALIFICATIONS AND MINIMUM AGES (ARTICLE 84)
- **Article 84**: citizen; oath before an **EC-authorised person** (Third Schedule form); **25 years (LS)**, **30 years (RS)**; other conditions by Parliament.
- **RPA, 1951**: must be an **elector in a parliamentary constituency**; SC/ST to contest a reserved seat.
- **No educational qualification**.
- RS **domicile** (voter in the same State) required only before **6 Apr 2003**; removal upheld in **Kuldip Nayar v. UoI (2006)** with **open ballot**.
| Age | Office | Article |
|---|---|---|
| 18 | Voter | **326** – 21 → 18 by **61st Amendment, 1988**, in force **28 Mar 1989**; first used in Nov 1989 LS polls |
| 21 | Panchayat / Municipality member | **243F(1)(a)** / **243V(1)(a)** |
| 25 | LS, Assembly; PM/CM from lower House | 84(b), 173(b) |
| 30 | RS, Legislative Council | 84(b), 173(b) |
| 35 | President, VP, **Governor (not 30)** | **58(1)(b), 66(3)(b), 157** |
- 35 is the highest entry age in the Constitution.
### 8. DISQUALIFICATION AND VACATION OF SEATS
#### 8.1 Article 102 and Article 103
- Grounds: **office of profit** (Union/State, exempted posts apart), **unsound mind** (court-declared), **undischarged insolvent**, **loss of citizenship**, a **law of Parliament** (RPA 1951) – **not lack of a degree**.
- **Article 103**: **President** decides, on the **Election Commission's opinion** (binding); decision final.
- "Office of profit" is **not defined** in the Constitution or RPA; exemptions by **Parliament (Prevention of Disqualification) Act, 1959** – amended **five times** (1993, 1999, 2000, 2006, 2013).
#### 8.2 Tenth Schedule (defection)
- Decided by the **Speaker (LS) / Chairman (RS)**, not the President; **subject to judicial review** – **Kihoto Hollohan (1992)**.
- Grounds: giving up party membership; voting/abstaining against the whip unless **condoned within 15 days**; independent joining a party; nominated member joining after **6 months**. The term "**political party**" is in the Tenth Schedule.
#### 8.3 Vacation – Article 101
| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| Elected to **both Houses** | Choose within **10 days**, else **RS seat** vacant |
| Member of one House elected to the other | First seat vacant |
| Two seats in one House | Choose one, else both vacant |
| **Parliament + State legislature** | Resign State seat within **14 days**, else Parliament seat vacant (Prohibition of Simultaneous Membership Rules, 1950) |
| **Absence 60 days** (Art 101(4)) | House may declare seat vacant; prorogation and adjournments of more than **4 consecutive days** are excluded |
- Resignation in writing to Speaker / Chairman; **33rd Amendment, 1974** (Art 101(3)(b)) – may be rejected if **not voluntary or genuine**.
!PAIR 10 days | Both Houses | RS seat lost || 14 days | Parliament + State | MP seat lost

### 10. SESSIONS AND PROCEDURE
- **Article 85**: President summons each House; **maximum six months** between sessions → LS meets **at least twice a year**; no minimum sitting days. Usually three sessions: **Budget, Monsoon, Winter** (no fixed calendar).
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sitting** | Daily meeting during a session |
| **Adjournment** | Ends a sitting for a set time – **Speaker/Chairman** |
| **Adjournment sine die** | Ends a sitting without a date – Speaker/Chairman |
| **Prorogation** (85(2)) | **President** ends the **session**, not the House |
| **Recess** | Gap between prorogation and new session – ordinances possible |
| **Dissolution** | Ends the House – **Lok Sabha only** |
- Sequence: adjournment sine die → prorogation → recess → new session.
- **Quorum (Art 100(3))**: **one-tenth** of total membership – an APPSC key gave 15%.
- **Art 118**: each House makes its rules. **Art 120(1)**: business in **Hindi or English**; mother tongue with presiding officer's permission.
- Secretariats independent: LS under the **Speaker**, RS under the **Chairman**.
- **2026**: Monsoon Session 20 Jul – sine die 13 Aug; productivity **LS 19%, RS 39%**; FCRA (Amendment) Bill, 2026 sent to a JPC. **11 Mar 2026**: resolution under **Art 94(c)** to remove Speaker **Om Birla** rejected by voice vote.
### 11. PARLIAMENTARY PRIVILEGES (ARTICLES 105, 122)
- **Art 105**: freedom of speech in the House and committees (not outside); no court case for anything said or vote given.
- **Art 105(3)**: privileges **not codified** – historically those of the **British House of Commons**; codification urged for press freedom.
| Collective (House) | Individual (members) |
|---|---|
| Publish or **prohibit publication** of proceedings | Freedom of speech |
| Exclude strangers; secret sittings | **No civil arrest** during session and **40 days before and after** – not criminal or preventive detention |
| Make rules; punish breach and contempt | Exemption from jury service; **freedom from attending as witness** |
| **Be informed** of a member's arrest, detention, release | – |
| No arrest in precincts without Presiding Officer's permission | – |
| **Art 122**: courts cannot inquire into proceedings | – |
- Also cover **ministers and the Attorney-General**; co-terminous with membership.
- **Sita Soren (2024)**, 7 judges: no privilege for a **bribe** to vote or speak – overruled **P.V. Narasimha Rao (1998)**.
!TRAP APPSC-G2 2017: "the House as a whole has no privileges" is wrong – both collective and individual exist.
### 12. RAJYA SABHA VS LOK SABHA – EQUAL AND UNEQUAL
| Equal | Lok Sabha superior |
|---|---|
| Ordinary and **constitutional amendment** bills (no joint sitting – bill fails) | **Money Bill** only in LS (Art 109); RS cannot reject/amend, **returns in 14 days** |
| Election and **impeachment** of President (either House can start); election of VP | **Speaker certifies** Money Bill; presides over **joint sitting** (Art 108) |
| Ordinances; **all three emergencies** | RS discusses Budget but **cannot vote on demands for grants** |
| Removal of judges, CEC, CAG | Only LS can **discontinue a National Emergency** |
| **CAG, UPSC, Finance Commission reports** | **No-confidence** only in LS (Art 75(3)) |
- **Financial Bill (I)**: introduced only in LS; equal powers in passage.
- **No tax without LS approval**.
!KEY APPSC G1 2024: equal status = **considering the CAG's report** and **approving Emergency** – not Money Bills.
### 13. SPECIAL POWERS OF THE RAJYA SABHA
| Article | Power | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **249** | Authorise **Parliament** to legislate on a **State List** subject in national interest | **Two-thirds present and voting**; valid **1 year**, renewable a year at a time |
| **312** | Create new **All-India Services** | Same two-thirds resolution |
| **67(b)** | Start removal of the **VP** | Only in RS |
- **LS dissolved**: Emergency (Arts 352, 356, 360) survives if **RS approves**; new LS must approve within **30 days** of its first sitting.
- Returning a Money Bill with recommendations is its power.
!TRAP APPSC-G1 2017: RS authorises **Parliament**, not State Assemblies.
### 14. FUNCTIONS OF PARLIAMENT AND POWER OVER STATES
| Function | Content |
|---|---|
| Legislative | Union and Concurrent Lists (State List in special cases); **only Parliament can change its existing law** (APPSC-GS 2024); reviews delegated legislation |
| Executive control | Questions, motions, no-confidence (LS) |
| Financial | Budget, taxes, grants |
| Constituent | Art 368 – not the basic structure |
| Judicial | **Impeaches President**, removes VP, recommends removal of **judges, CEC, CAG**; punishes breach of privilege |
| Electoral | **Elects the VP** (all MPs), takes part in **President's** election (elected MPs), LS elects **Speaker and Deputy Speaker**, RS elects only the **Deputy Chairman** – **not** appointing the CEC (President) |
- Election laws: RPA 1950, 1951; Presidential and Vice-Presidential Elections Act, 1952.
- **14 days**: President's impeachment notice (signed by **one-fourth**), Money Bill in RS, simultaneous membership, removal of Speaker / **RS Deputy Chairman (Art 90(c))** – not 30.
| Article | Power over States |
|---|---|
| **3** | New States, boundaries, names – Bill needs **President's recommendation**; State's views **not binding**; Andhra State Act, 1953 and AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 |
| **249** | State List – RS resolution |
| **250** | State List during National Emergency |
| **252** | Law for **two or more States** on their legislatures' resolutions; others may adopt it |
| **253** | Implement international treaties |
| **356–357** | Laws for a State under President's Rule |
### 15. EXPANSION AND DELIMITATION DEBATE (2026)
- Raising LS by ~50% (to **~815**) after the 1971 freeze: LS–RS ratio **2.2:1 → 3.3:1**; 15% ministerial cap **81 → 122**.
- **Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill, 2026** (Apr 2026, with Delimitation Bill): LS maximum **550 → 850** (815 States, 35 UTs) and faster women's reservation – **defeated** in LS, **298–230** of 528 present, short of the Art 368 special majority.
- Southern States would lose share; **Tamil Nadu Assembly** resolved to keep LS at **543** and the 2.2:1 ratio.
- **Law Commission 170th Report (1999)**: 25% more seats by a **party list**.
