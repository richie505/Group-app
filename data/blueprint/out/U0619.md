## U0619 | CONTINENTAL RESOURCES – LAND, FOREST, WATER, MINERAL AND ENERGY RESOURCES; CONSERVATION
### 1. CLASSIFICATION OF RESOURCES AND CONSERVATION MILESTONES
#### 1.1 Bases of Classification
| Basis | Types | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | **Biotic** (living) vs **Abiotic** (non-living) | Biotic: plants, animals, **micro-organisms**; Abiotic: land, water, **minerals**, rocks, metals |
| Exhaustibility | **Renewable** vs **Non-renewable** | Renewable: forests, fish, water, wildlife; Non-renewable: fossil fuels, minerals (exhausted with use) |
| Status of development | Potential; Developed; **Stock**; **Reserve** | Stock: hydrogen from water |
- **Stock** = resource exists but the **technology to use it is lacking** (e.g. hydrogen from water).
- **Reserve** = the part (subset) of stock usable with **present technology**, kept for future use.
!TRAP Stock = technology lacking; Reserve = usable now and a part of stock. Do not swap them.
#### 1.2 Resource Depletion
- **Overuse** of natural resources leads to resource depletion (APPSC 2025).
| Resource | Cause of depletion (APPSC 2025) |
|---|---|
| Groundwater | **Deep bore tubewells** for irrigation |
| Topsoil | **Brick production** |
| Biodiversity habitats | Expansion of agriculture |
#### 1.3 Global Conservation Milestones
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| April 1968 | **Club of Rome** founded (Accademia dei Lincei, Rome) |
| 1972 | Club of Rome report **"Limits to Growth"** |
| 1987 | **Brundtland Commission** report **"Our Common Future"** – defined **sustainable development** |
| 1994 | **UNCCD** adopted – global response to land degradation |
- UNCCD defines **desertification** as land degradation in **arid, semi-arid and dry sub-humid** areas.
- Desertification is least in **Europe** – the only continent without a desert.
!KEY India has **4%** of the world's renewable water and **2.4%** of world land for **over 18%** of the world's population (2026).
### 2. LAND RESOURCES
#### 2.1 Nine-fold Land-use Classification (full list)
1. Forests
2. Land put to non-agricultural uses
3. Barren and unculturable land
4. Permanent pastures and grazing land
5. Miscellaneous tree crops and groves
6. Culturable wasteland
7. Current fallow
8. Other fallow
9. Net sown area
!TRAP "Seasonal plantation reserve" is NOT a land-use category.
#### 2.2 Fallow Ladder
| Category | Uncultivated for |
|---|---|
| Current fallow | Up to **1 year** (cropland left unsown in the current year) |
| Other fallow | **1–5 years** |
| Culturable wasteland | **More than 5 years** |
- **Highest net-sown share**: **Punjab and Haryana** (over 80% of land sown).
#### 2.3 Wasteland and Land Degradation
- **Wasteland** = degraded (damaged) land that can be brought under vegetative cover with **reasonable effort**.
- Mapped by the satellite-based **National Wastelands Monitoring**, using IRS remote-sensing data (IRS commissioned 1988).
- Land degradation causes: **soil erosion, waterlogging, salinisation, nutrient depletion, falling water tables, soil pollution** – mostly human-induced.
!TRAP "Natural increase of nutrients" is NOT a cause of land degradation.
#### 2.4 Watershed Management
- **IWMP** (Integrated Watershed Management Programme): CSS from **2009 (2009-10)**, run by the **Department of Land Resources** (Ministry of Rural Development).
- IWMP **merged DPAP, DDP and IWDP**; till **1 April 2008** the Ministry of Rural Development ran IWDP, DPAP and DDP.
- IWMP objective: enhance **agricultural productivity and water conservation** on degraded and rainfed land.
- Merged into **PMKSY in 2015** – now the **Watershed Development Component (WDC-PMKSY)**; WDC-PMKSY 2.0 for degraded and rain-fed areas.
- Integrated watershed benefits: prevents **soil runoff**, rainwater harvesting, **groundwater recharge**, regeneration of vegetation – NOT linking perennial and seasonal rivers.
- **Command Area Development and Water Management** is a PMKSY component, **not** part of IWMP.
- **Neeranchal**: World Bank-assisted national watershed project.
- **Watershed Development Fund**: set up by **NABARD**.
!TRAP DPAP, DDP, IWDP were implemented by the Department of Land Resources; pairing them with the Agriculture or Environment ministry is wrong.
### 3. FOREST RESOURCES
#### 3.1 Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Silviculture** | Art and science of growing and tending forest crops (regeneration, thinning, rotation) |
| **Agroforestry** | Trees grown with crops or livestock on the same land |
| **Shifting cultivation (jhum)** | NE hill slopes (e.g. Arunachal); shorter fallow cycles degrade forests and raise soil erosion |
- India's first agroforestry policy: **National Agroforestry Policy 2014**.
- **Largest forest area** among states: **Madhya Pradesh** (77,073 sq km, ISFR 2023).
#### 3.2 National Forest Policy 1988
- Target: **33%** of the country under forest; **two-thirds** in the **hills**.
- Formally introduced **Joint Forest Management (JFM)**.
!TRAP APPSC 2024: "At least two-thirds of the country under forest" is NOT in NFP 1988 – two-thirds is only for hill areas.
#### 3.3 Joint Forest Management and Forest Rights
- JFM: Centre's (MoEF) guidelines **1990** – village committees share forest protection and usufruct.
- Forerunner: **Arabari (West Bengal, 1972)**.
- **Forest Rights Act 2006**: individual and community titles to STs and other traditional forest dwellers; **Gram Sabhas** manage, protect and regenerate community forest resources.
#### 3.4 Forest Movements
| Movement | Place / date |
|---|---|
| Chipko | Himalayas |
| Appiko | **Salkani forest, Sirsi, Karnataka**, 8 Sep 1983 |
#### 3.5 World Forest Types
- **Amazon Basin**: largest continuous tropical rainforest; **Congo Basin** second.
- Dense forests gather near the **equator**; rainforest soil after clearance is **nutrient-poor**, regeneration slow.
- **Taiga** (boreal coniferous forest): **largest terrestrial biome** – Russia, Scandinavia, Canada; NOT in Amazonia.
- **Bhutan**: Constitution keeps at least **60%** of area under forest (about **70%** now).
- **Canada**: largest producer and exporter of **softwood** timber and wood pulp.

#### 4.2 Groundwater (NEW: utilisation by state)
- **CGWB categories**: safe, semi-critical, critical, over-exploited, saline – NOT "perennial".
- Worst over-exploitation: **Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan and Delhi**.
- Most area irrigated by **tubewells**: **Uttar Pradesh**.
#### 4.3 Tank Irrigation
- Tank irrigation is linked with **Peninsular / South India**; tank systems are a mark of **semi-arid** watersheds – tanks made by embanking water from seasonal rivers.
- **Andhra Pradesh ranks first** in tank irrigation (about **28–29%** of India's tank-irrigated area); districts Nellore and Warangal.
- **Tamil Nadu**: over **23%**; tanks are called **Eris**.

- Criticism (Sep 2026): monsoons and climate change make "surplus" basins unreliable; India's water crisis is "as much institutional as hydrological" (May 2026).

### 9. MINERAL RESOURCES AND LEGISLATION
#### 9.1 Indian Mineral Facts
- **Peninsular plateau** (Chota Nagpur, Dharwar, Eastern Ghats) = India's **mineral heartland** (iron ore, coal, bauxite, mica).
- Diamond mines: **Madhya Pradesh** (Panna, Satna). Leading manganese producer: **Madhya Pradesh** (IMYB 2024).
- Chromite: **Sukinda Valley, Odisha**. **Navgam** oil field: **Gujarat** (also Ankleshwar, Kalol, Bakrol, Mehsana).
- Highest solar potential: **Rajasthan**.
- AP: commercial gold mining at **Jonnagiri** (Tuggali mandal, **Kurnool**); six gold blocks at **Javakula (Anantapur)** under G2 exploration before auction (29 Jun 2026).
#### 9.2 MMDR Act and Amendments
| Act / amendment | Provision |
|---|---|
| **MMDR Act 1957** | Parent law |
| **2015** | **Auction** mandatory; **District Mineral Foundation** (funded by miners' royalty-linked contributions, NOT the Consolidated Fund); **NMET** |
| **2023** | Centre auctions **critical and strategic minerals** (e.g. lithium); **exploration licence** introduced |
| **2026** (14 Aug 2026) | States may tax mineral rights / mineral lands only within **limits prescribed by the Centre**; Union regulates land by mineral density |
- **Mineral Area Development Authority v. SAIL (2024, nine judges)**: **royalty is not a tax**; States tax mineral rights under **Entry 50** and mineral land under **Entry 49** (State List). Critics say Section 9D of the 2026 Act undercuts it.
!PAIR Entry 50 | Mineral rights | State List || Entry 49 | Mineral land | State List
#### 9.3 Deep-sea Minerals
- **India = first country** to sign an **ISA** contract for **polymetallic sulphides** on the **Carlsberg Ridge**, Arabian Sea (Sep 2025).
- Sulphides: copper, zinc, lead, iron, silver, gold; **nodules**: manganese, cobalt, nickel, copper.
- **ISA** HQ **Jamaica** (under UNCLOS); **ITLOS** – Hamburg.
