## U0162 | THIRD CARNATIC WAR – BATTLE OF WANDIWASH, TREATY OF PARIS (1763); REASONS FOR ENGLISH SUCCESS
### 1. THE THREE CARNATIC WARS
| War | Years | Ended by |
|---|---|---|
| First | 1746-48 | **Aix-la-Chapelle (1748)** |
| Second | **1749-54** (not 1750-55) | **Pondicherry (1754)** |
| Third | **1758-63** | **Paris (1763)** – French power ended |
### 2. SEVEN YEARS' WAR LINK AND BENGAL RESOURCES
- Third war = **Indian theatre of the Seven Years' War (1756-63)**; its Andhra events open in 1756 (Tumnapalem).
- By 1758 **Clive** had won **Plassey (1757)** and taken French **Chandernagore**.
- **Bengal revenue funded** the English Carnatic campaign.
### 3. ANDHRA THEATRE – CHANDURTI AND MACHILIPATNAM
| Event | Date | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Tumnapalem** | 1756 | **Bussy** helped by Vizianagaram ruler **Vijayaramaraju** |
| Bobbili battle | 24 Jan 1757 | – |
| **Chandurti (Condore)**, near **Rajahmundry** | **7 December 1758** | **Col. Forde** (sent by **Clive** from **Bengal**) with **Ananda Gajapati's** Vizianagaram army beat the French under **Conflans** |
| **Machilipatnam taken** | **April 1759** | **Salabat Jang changed sides** |
| Konduru | – | **Ananda Gajapati** beat **Jagapati Raju** of Peddapuram |
- Chandurti **drove the French out of the Northern Circars**; Malleson: "**a small battle with great results**".
- 1759: Salabat Jang gave the English **Machilipatnam, Nizampatnam, Vakal Mannar** and part of **Kondapalli** – the **first agreement between the British and Hyderabad**.
- Ananda Gajapati died of **smallpox in 1760**.
- Chandurti and Machilipatnam = English **first foothold in the Northern Circars**; **Yanam stayed French**.
!KEY Chandurti (1758) is to British rule in Andhra what Plassey (1757) is to British rule in India.
| Later British-Hyderabad milestone | Year |
|---|---|
| Treaty of Masulipatnam | 1768 |
| First Subsidiary Alliance with Hyderabad | 1798 |
### 4. LALLY'S CAMPAIGN AND BATTLE OF WANDIWASH
- **1758**: Lally took **Fort St. David** (Cuddalore); failed at **Madras** (no money or naval support) and **Thanjavur** (raid for funds).
- **Wandiwash (Vandavasi), 22 January 1760**: **Eyre Coote** (English) routed **Count de Lally** (French); **Bussy taken prisoner**.
- Outcome: British beat the French (BPSC 2020, UPPSC 2015) → end of French political power.
- With Plassey, Wandiwash **removed the last serious European rival**.
!TRAP Bussy was captured **at Wandiwash**; Lally surrendered **at Pondicherry (1761)**.
### 5. SIEGE AND FALL OF PONDICHERRY
- Lally held Pondicherry **eight months**; surrendered **16 January 1761**; **Jinji** and **Mahe** also fell.

### 7. TREATY OF PARIS (1763)
- Ended the **Seven Years' War** and the **Third Carnatic War**.
- French settlements **restored only as trading posts**; **could not be fortified**.
- Enclaves kept: **Pondicherry, Karaikal, Mahe, Yanam, Chandernagore**.
- **Machilipatnam not kept** – it went to the English through the Hyderabad agreement.
- Allahabad (1765) followed Buxar – a different treaty.
### 8. WHY THE ENGLISH WON
| Factor | English | French |
|---|---|---|
| Company | **Private**; board elected yearly; quick decisions | **State-controlled**, royal subsidy; bureaucratic |
| Navy | **Naval superiority** | Fleet beaten (d'Ache); no sea support |
| Money | **Bengal's** wealth and men after Plassey | Funds short; unpaid troops |
| Bases | **Calcutta, Bombay, Madras** | Only **Pondicherry** |
| Leaders | Clive, Lawrence, Coote | **Lally** quarrelled with officers, poor grasp of India; Dupleix recalled |
- UPPSC 2016: "Bengal wealth" and "naval superiority" are **both** correct.

### 11. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. Lally surrendered at **Pondicherry (1761)**; Bussy was taken at **Wandiwash**.
2. English admiral **Pocock**; French admiral **D'Ache**.
3. Paris (1763): settlements **unfortified** trading posts only.
4. Five enclaves: **Pondicherry, Karaikal, Mahe, Yanam, Chandernagore**; not Machilipatnam.
5. Chandurti: Forde beat **Conflans**, not Lally.
6. Second war = **1749-54**, not 1750-55.
7. French company was **state-controlled**.
