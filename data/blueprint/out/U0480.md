## U0480 | EMPLOYMENT GENERATION SCHEMES - MGNREGA, PMEGP, DDU-GKY, PM-SVANIDHI, ATMANIRBHAR PACKAGES
### 1. WAGE EMPLOYMENT VS SELF-EMPLOYMENT PROGRAMMES
#### 1.1 Classification
| Wage employment | Self-employment |
|---|---|
| NREP, RLEGP, **JRY**, **EAS** | **IRDP**, TRYSEM, **DWCRA** |
| JGSY, **SGRY**, **NFFWP** | **SGSY**, **NRLM** |
| **MGNREGS**, **VB-G RAM G** | **PMRY (1993)**, REGP, **PMEGP** |
- **PMRY** (Prime Minister's Rozgar Yojana, **1993**): self-employment for **educated unemployed youth**; the poverty programme of the reform period; later merged into PMEGP.
- **PMEGP (2008)** = merger of **PMRY + REGP**.
!TRAP PMRY is **not** a wage employment programme (APPSC 2017); JRY, EAS, SGRY are wage programmes.
#### 1.2 Urban Programmes
- **SJSRY (1 December 1997)** absorbed **NRY, UBSP** and the PM's Integrated Urban programme; it did **not** include PMRY.
- **Indira Gandhi Urban Employment Guarantee Scheme** (Rajasthan, **2022**): **100 days**, raised to **125 days in 2023**.
- Not employment-related: **RMSA** (March 2009, secondary education); MGNREGA and NRLM are.

### 3. MGNREGA, 2005: THE ACT
#### 3.1 Milestones
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Name | **National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, 2005** (first proposed in 1991 by P.V. Narasimha Rao) |
| Passed | **23 Aug 2005**; assent 5 Sept 2005; **notified 7 Sept 2005** |
| Launch | **2 Feb 2006** in **200** backward districts |
| Phase 2 | 130 more districts in 2007-08 |
| All districts | **1 April 2008** (625 districts; NREGS extended to the whole country) |
| Renaming | **"Mahatma Gandhi" added 2 Oct 2009** (MGNREGA) |
| Repealed | **1 July 2026**: VB-G RAM G Act in force |
!NUM 2005 Act - 2006 launch - 2008 all districts - 2009 "Mahatma Gandhi" added
!TRAP 625 = all-district coverage of 2008, not the launch figure; NFFWP merged in **2006**, not 2009. The correct name is Mahatma Gandhi **National Rural** Employment Guarantee Act.
#### 3.2 Entitlement and Wages
- **At least 100 days** of guaranteed wage employment **per financial year**.
- **Unit = household** (not each adult); for **adult members** who volunteer for **unskilled manual work**; **rural** only.
- Wages: State-notified wage, **within 15 days** of work; delay brings compensation; **DBT** to Aadhaar-linked accounts.
- **Ministry of Rural Development**; rights-based, demand-driven, anchored in **Article 41** (right to work, DPSP).
!KEY APPSC 2017: mandate = "100 days to every **household** whose adult members volunteer", not "100 days to every adult".
### 4. MGNREGA OBJECTIVE
- "**Enhancing livelihood security** of rural households" through at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment (APPSC 2023).
- Target group: rural, **unskilled**, adult volunteers (not urban, not skilled).
- **Olga Tellis (1985)** linked livelihood with **Article 21**; MGNREGA is the only law built on the right to work.
- **World Development Report 2014** (World Bank): "**stellar example of rural development**".
- Largest rural employment programme; safety net in agrarian distress and **COVID-19**.
### 5. MGNREGA FEATURES AND MONITORING
#### 5.1 Features
- **Women:** at least **one-third** of beneficiaries; actual share over **50%** of person-days.
- **Unemployment allowance:** if no work within **15 days** of application: at least **1/4 of wage for first 30 days**, **1/2 thereafter**.
- **Permissible works:** water conservation, land development, rural connectivity, durable community assets.
- **Convergence:** 90/95 person-days to **PMAY-G** beneficiaries (AwaasSoft-NREGASoft link).
- Scale: 5-6 crore households, 200-250 crore person-days a year.
#### 5.2 Monitoring
| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| **Bhuvan** mobile platform (**ISRO**) | Geo-tagging of assets: **GeoMGNREGA** (APPSC 2018) |
| **NREGASoft** | MIS |
| **Gram Sabha** | **Mandatory social audit**; plans and monitors works |
- **CAPART** and **NIRDPR (Hyderabad, Telangana)**: autonomous bodies under the Ministry of Rural Development.
!TRAP 15 days twice: wages within 15 days of work; allowance if no work within 15 days of application.
### 6. MGNREGA VS VB-G RAM G ACT, 2025
- **Viksit Bharat - Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025**: passed by Parliament **18 Dec 2025**; replaced MGNREGA from **1 July 2026**.
| Aspect | MGNREGA 2005 | VB-G RAM G 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Guarantee | 100 days/household | **125 days**/household (over **305 days**) |
| Seasonal pause | None | States may pause up to **60 days** (sowing/harvest) |
| Funding | Mostly Central, demand-driven | **60:40** (90:10 NE/Himalayan States, UTs); normative allocation |
| Wages | Within 15 days | Weekly or within a fortnight; floor **₹300/day** |
| Works | Many categories | 4 priority areas: water security, core rural infrastructure, livelihood infrastructure, extreme-weather works |
| Admin ceiling | **6%** | **9%** |
- Job cards → **Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Cards**; no contractors; assets on VB-NRIS stack; Central and State Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Councils; unemployment allowance retained.
- Apr-Jul 2026: only **70 crore** person-days (MGNREGA + VB-G RAM G).
- 13 May 2026: unions sought rollback, 200 days and ₹700 wage. 21 Aug 2026: Supreme Court bench to examine whether right to work should be on par with Article 21.
!NUM 125 - 60 - 9% = guarantee days - seasonal pause - admin ceiling
### 7. SCHEMES → MINISTRY AND TARGET
| Scheme | Ministry / agency | Target / feature |
|---|---|---|
| **PMEGP (2008)** | **MSME**; implemented by **KVIC** | New non-farm micro-enterprises; margin money **15-35%**; cap **₹50 lakh** (mfg) / **₹20 lakh** (service); earlier ₹25 / ₹10 lakh |
| **DDU-GKY (2014)** | **Rural Development** | **Placement-linked skilling** of rural poor youth; roots in SGSY special projects |
| **PM-SVANidhi** | **Housing & Urban Affairs** | Collateral-free working-capital loans to **street vendors** (75 lakh+) |
| **PM Vishwakarma** | MSME | **18 traditional artisan trades**: training, toolkits, credit |
| **Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyan (2020)** | Rural Development | **125-day** campaign for **returnee migrants**, 116 districts of 6 States |
| **National Career Service (2015)** | **Labour & Employment** | Job-matching portal; 47.21 lakh+ employers |
| **Recognition of Prior Learning** | Skill Development (**PMKVY**, 2015) | Certifies existing skills against **NSQF** |
- PMEGP met target of **4.02 lakh** micro-enterprises; 80% rural, 40% women (9 May 2026).
- **PM-Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana**: formal job incentive, launched **15 Aug 2025**.
!TRAP PMEGP = MSME ministry but run by **KVIC**; PM-SVANidhi = **Housing & Urban Affairs**, not MSME.
### 8. ANDHRA PRADESH
- Person-days fell from **2,422.84 lakh (2024-25)** to **1,859.77 lakh (2025-26)**; average days per household **51.62 → 43.37**.
- **Jun 2026:** AP pooled rural development funds to give **125 days** in distressed rural pockets.
- Convergence: MGNREGA days for PMAY-G houses.
