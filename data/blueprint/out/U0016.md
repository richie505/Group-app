## U0016 | RIG VEDIC PERIOD – SAPTA SINDHU, RIVERS, TRIBES, BATTLE OF TEN KINGS
### 1. CHRONOLOGY AND SOURCES
- Early Vedic (Rig Vedic) period: c. **1500–1000 BCE**.
- **Rigveda** = religious poetry, not a chronicle. **Family books = Mandalas II–VII** – oldest core, each linked to one rishi family.
- Ganga mentioned once – in the **10th mandala** (a late book).
- Archaeological correlates: **Gandhara Grave culture** (Swat; horse remains) linked to early Indo-Aryans; **Painted Grey Ware** link to Later Vedic geography still open.
### 2. SAPTA SINDHU
- **Land of seven rivers**: Sindhu, Vitasta, Asikni, Parushni, Vipas, Sutudri, Sarasvati.
- Extent: **eastern Afghanistan**, NWFP, **Punjab, Haryana**, fringes of western UP.
- Early Vedic core = **Punjab–Haryana**; Later Vedic core shifted east = Ganga-Yamuna **Doab**, eastern UP, Bihar (Kosala, Videha).

### 5. TRIBES AND PANCHAJANA
- **Panchajana** (five tribes) – only: **Yadus, Turvasas, Anus, Druhyus, Purus**.
- Others: **Bharatas, Tritsu, Krivis, Srinjayas**.
- **Kikatas**: south Bihar / **Magadha**; outside Panchajana.
- **Bharatavarsha**: name from the **Bharata** tribe.
!KEY Odd one out of the Panchajana: **Kikata** (also Srinjaya, Bharata).
### 6. DASARAJNA – BATTLE OF TEN KINGS
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Source | Rigveda **Mandala VII** |
| River site | **Parushni (Ravi)** |
| Victor | **Bharatas** under king **Sudas** (**Tritsu** family) |
| Priest of Sudas | **Vasishtha** |
| Priest of the confederacy | **Vishvamitra** |
| Cause | Rivalry of the two priests |
| Outcome | Bharatas dominant; defeated **Purus** later joined them → **Kurus**; Kurus + Panchalas later ruled upper Ganga basin |
- Likely historical core: tribal confederacies competing for resources.
!TRAP Battle not on Vitasta (Jhelum), Asikni (Chenab) or Sutudri (Sutlej).
!PAIR Vasishtha | Priest | Sudas (victor) || Vishvamitra | Priest | Ten-king confederacy
