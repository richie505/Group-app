## U0427 | INDIA-USA RELATIONS – STRATEGIC, DEFENCE, TRADE, TECHNOLOGY (iCET), DIASPORA
### 1. RELATIONSHIP STATUS AND US DOCTRINES
- Present status: **Comprehensive Global Strategic Partnership**.
- The **2008 civil nuclear deal** = turning point in India-US ties.
| Doctrine / law | Year | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Pressler Amendment** | 1985 | Stopped US aid to **Pakistan** unless the President certified it had no nuclear device; used in **1990** |
| **Pivot to Asia / Rebalance** | 2011 (Obama era) | US foreign-policy shift of focus to the **Asia-Pacific** |
| **CAATSA** | 2017 | Sanctions on buyers of **Russian arms**; India sought a waiver over the **S-400** |
- **Larry Pressler** = US Senator; linked aid to non-proliferation; criticised Clinton-era help to Pakistan over its nuclear spread.
!TRAP Pressler Amendment targeted **Pakistan**, not India. 'Pivot to Asia' is a plan of the **USA** (UPPSC 2015).
### 2. CIVIL NUCLEAR COOPERATION
- Order: **Hyde Act 2006** (made the deal possible under US law) → **123 Agreement 2008** → **NSG clean waiver, Sept 2008** (allowed civil nuclear trade).
- Under iCET: talks on **Westinghouse AP1000** reactors and SMRs/AMRs.
!KEY AP angle: **Kovvada (Srikakulam district)** = proposed site for the Westinghouse AP1000 reactors.
### 3. DEFENCE AGREEMENTS AND STATUS
| Agreement | Year | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **GSOMIA** | 2002 | Protection of shared military information |
| **LEMOA** | 2016 | Use of each other's facilities for refuelling and replenishment |
| **COMCASA** | 2018 | Encrypted communications data and equipment |
| **BECA** | 2020 | Geospatial (satellite) data for navigation and missile targeting |
- **Major Defense Partner** – 2016.
- **STA-1** (2018) – licence-free access to US military and dual-use technology, like NATO allies, **without NATO membership**.
- **10-year defence framework** – Oct 2025, signed by Hegseth and Rajnath Singh.
!NUM 2016 = LEMOA + Major Defense Partner; 2018 = COMCASA + STA-1
### 4. TECHNOLOGY PARTNERSHIP
| Initiative | Year | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **iCET** (Initiative on Critical and Emerging Technology) | 2023 | Relaunched as **TRUST** in 2025 |
| **GE F414** jet engines | 2023 | Co-produced with **HAL** (not BEL) |
| **Artemis Accords** | June 2023 | India became the **27th** signatory |

- **TRUST** (Feb 2025) added **critical minerals** (exploration, processing, recycling; lithium, cobalt, rare earths) and biotechnology to semiconductors, AI and quantum.
### 5. TRADE ISSUES
- **GSP** benefits withdrawn by the US – **June 2019**.
- **Mission 500** (Feb 2025 Modi-Trump joint statement) – bilateral trade of **$500 billion by 2030**.
- **Tariffs**: total US duty on Indian goods rose to **50%** on 27 Aug 2025 (extra 25% over Russian oil purchases).
- **H-1B**: $100,000 fee on new petitions from 21 Sept 2025; Indians get about **71%** of approved H-1Bs.
- Apr-Nov 2025: labour-intensive exports to the US fell; shifted to other regions.

### 8. INDIAN DIASPORA IN USA
- US has the world's **largest PIO community** (Malaysia second) (Feb 2026).
- 2020 Census: about **4.4 million** "Indian-alone" – largest Asian-alone group.
- **Remittances**: US is the largest source – **27.7%** (RBI survey, 2023-24), ahead of the UAE.
- **Telugu** = fastest-growing foreign language in the US (2010-17).
