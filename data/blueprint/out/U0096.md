## U0096 | KAKATIYA SOCIETY AND RELIGION – VEERASHAIVISM, JAINISM, TEMPLE INSTITUTIONS
### 1. CASTES AND CASTE ASSOCIATIONS
- **Samaya / Samayamu** = **caste associations** (APPSC G2 2019); controlled social and work conduct (e.g. of the Vaishyas).
| Group | Name of its body |
|---|---|
| Brahmins | **Mahajanas** (Vaidikas vs Niyogis) |
| Vaishyas (Komatis) | **Nakaram** |
| Panchavarmavaru | **Saaminumani vaaru** |
| **Panchanamuvaru** | The **five artisan** (smith) groups |
- **Ashtadasha kulas** = **18 occupational groups** inside the Warangal fort (**Kreedabhiramam**).
- Other castes: Telikas, Gollas, Sales (weavers), Boyas, Medharas, Gandla, Upparas.
- **Padmanayakas** (Recherla **Velama** chiefs) were key Kakatiya commanders and later founded the Velama kingdom.
| Movement | New caste groups |
|---|---|
| **Veerashaivism** | **Lingayats, Balijas, Jangamas** |
| **Vaishnavism** | **Satanis, Dasaris** |
### 2. NON-BRAHMANICAL TRENDS
- Non-Brahmanisation of religion and politics **culminated under the Kakatiyas** (APPSC 2019).
- Kakatiyas were **Sudras** (originally of **Pulinda** origin before adopting the varna order) → **Sudra kingship**.
- Groups involved: reform sects (**Veerashaiva, Veeravaishnava**) that opposed Brahminical practice; officials from warrior and farmer groups (Nayankara).
!KEY Question "in which dynasty did non-Brahmin trends culminate?" → Kakatiyas.
### 3. RELIGIOUS SHIFT – JAIN TO SHAIVA
1. Early Kakatiyas were **Jains** (APPSC G2 2011).
2. Turned **Shaiva** from **Prola II / Rudradeva**.
3. Jainism declined through **Kalamukha and Veerashaiva hostility** (also persecution by some officials); the Kakatiyas are credited with its **final eradication** in the region; the **Lingayats** hurt Jainism badly.
- Kakatiya age = the **"Shaiva era"**; dynastic deity **Svayambhu Shiva** (not Surya).
- Yet Vaishnava shrines were endowed → **tolerance and syncretism**.
#### 3.1 Jain Centres
- **Padmakshi temple, Hanumakonda** – built by **Prola II**; **originally a Jain shrine**.
- **Bodhan, Vemulawada, Patancheru, Kolanupaka, Potlacheruvu**.
- **Kadalalaya Jain basadi, Anumakonda** – built by **Mailama**, wife of minister Bethanamatya.
### 4. SHAIVA SECTS – PASUPATA, KALAMUKHA, KAPALIKA
| Sect | Founder / trait |
|---|---|
| **Pasupata** | **Oldest** Shaiva sect; founded by **Lakulisa (Nakulisa)**, 1st–2nd c. AD; popular into the **Reddi period** (APPSC G2 2011); declined under Vijayanagara |
| **Kalamukha** | **Kalasana (Kalanana)**, disciple of Lakulisa; **temple managers**; gave **anna dana** |
| **Kapalika** | **Offshoot of Pasupata** (APPSC G2 2019); **skull-bowl** ascetics |
| **Aradhya** | Brahmin Shaivas under **Mallikarjuna Panditaradhya**; kept Vedic ritual and **varna** |
- Kakatiyas patronised the "extremist" **Pasupata and Kalamukha** sects.
- Kalamukha / Shaiva centres: **Srisailam, Alampur, Vemulawada, Kaleshwaram, Tripurantakam, Mahanandi**.
!TRAP Kapalikas grew out of Pasupata, not Kalamukha. Popular religion of the Reddi Rajas = Pasupata.
### 5. VEERASHAIVISM
- Founder **Basaveshwara**, minister of **Kalachuri Bijjala** (Karnataka); spread into Andhra in the Kakatiya period.
- Followers = **Lingayats**; gurus = **Jangamas**; texts = **Agamas**.
- **Rejected** Vedas, caste, untouchability, child marriage, animal sacrifice.
- In Andhra the word "Veerashaiva" is absent from inscriptions; followers called **Veeravratulu / Asankhyakulu**.
- Main Veerashaiva centre in Andhra: **Srisailam** (Kolanupaka = Jain centre).
- **Panditaradhya Charitra**: Mallikarjuna Panditaradhya's debate with a Buddhist acharya.
- Kakatiya stance: **neutral** – grants to orthodox Brahmin sects, no persecution of Lingayats.
!PAIR Aradhyas | Brahmin Shaivas | kept varna || Radical Veerashaivas | Basava's followers | rejected caste
### 6. TEMPLES AS INSTITUTIONS
- Temples were **landed corporations** with lands and organised staff; **Srisailam Mallikarjuna** held lands in many villages.
- Staff: **Sthanapatis, Manulas, Sanus, Nibandhakaras**.
- Attached **monasteries and feeding houses**.
- **Temple women** and courtesans (**Kalavatis**) sustained **dance and music**; festivals were royal occasions.
### 7. WOMEN, LAW AND ROYAL GRANTS
- **Vignaneswaram** (Telugu Mitakshara) of **Ketana**: a woman's property passes wholly to her **daughters and daughter's daughters** (APPSC G2 2019) → a progressive legal outlook.
| Book | Author | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| **Vignaneswaram** | **Ketana** | Women's property |
| **Purushartha Saramu** | **Sivadevayya** | **72 Niyogas**; king's daily council routine |
| Nitisara | Rudradeva | Polity |
| **Niti Shastra Muktavali** | **Baddena** (not Sivadevayya) | Polity |
- Royal women were active donors to temples and tanks:
| Donor | Grant |
|---|---|
| **Mailamba (Mailama)**, Ganapatideva's sister | **Bayyaram tank** and its inscription (1219) |
| Mailama, wife of Bethanamatya | Jain basadi at Anumakonda |
| **Kupama Devi (Kuppamamba)** | Ganapatideva's queen; trained Rudrama |
| **Lakshmidevi**, Prataparudra II's queen | **Ramanatha temple, Yelledu** |
- **Prataparudra II** endowed the **Chennakeshava** temple (**1321**).
!TRAP Women's property → Vignaneswaram (Ketana), not Purushartha Saramu, Nitisara or Niti Shastra Muktavali.
