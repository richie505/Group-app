### 2. 97TH AMENDMENT, 2011 – CO-OPERATIVES
| Provision | Content |
|---|---|
| **Art. 19(1)(c)** | Forming **co-operative societies** = Fundamental Right |
| **Art. 43B** (DPSP) | **Voluntary formation, autonomous functioning, democratic control, professional management** |
| **Part IXB** | **Arts 243ZH–243ZT** |
| **243ZJ** | Board of **max 21 directors** (not 31) |
| Board seats | **One SC/ST + two women** |
| Term | **5 years** |
| Audit | Within **six months** of close of financial year |
- Notified **12 January 2012**.
- **Union of India v. Rajendra N. Shah (2021)**: Part IXB **struck down for State co-operatives** – **no State ratification**; still applies to **multi-State co-operatives**.
### 3. 101ST AMENDMENT, 2016 – GST
- Assent **8 September 2016**; GST from **1 July 2017**.
- Inserted: **246A** (Parliament and States both may make GST laws), **269A** (GST on inter-State supply), **279A** (**GST Council**).
- **GST Council**: chaired by **Union Finance Minister**; decisions by **three-fourths** majority; added to provisions needing **State ratification**.
- Omitted: **Article 268A** (service tax, added by 88th Amendment); Union List entries **92 and 92C** (newspaper sales tax, service tax).
### 4. 102ND AND 105TH AMENDMENTS – BACKWARD CLASSES
| Item | 102nd (2018) | 105th (2021) |
|---|---|---|
| Assent | **11 August 2018** | **18 August 2021** |
| Change | **Art. 338B** – **NCBC** made a constitutional body; **Art. 342A** – President notifies SEBC list | **342A(3)** – **States/UTs** may identify their own SEBCs |
| Effect | Central list for SEBCs | Restored State power |
- The 105th also added a proviso to **338B(9)**: no NCBC consultation needed for 342A(3) lists.
### 5. 103RD AMENDMENT, 2019 – EWS
- Passed as the **124th Amendment Bill, 2019**; assent **12 January 2019**.
- Inserted **15(6)** (education) and **16(6)** (jobs) – up to **10%** for EWS (household income up to ₹8 lakh).
- **Janhit Abhiyan v. Union of India (7 Nov 2022)**: upheld **3:2**; the 50% ceiling applies to 15(4), 15(5), 16(4), not EWS.
### 6. 104TH AMENDMENT, 2019
- Extended **SC/ST seat reservation** in Lok Sabha and Assemblies (**Art. 334**) to **2030**.
- **Ended Anglo-Indian nomination** to Lok Sabha and Assemblies (effective **January 2020**).
### 7. 106TH AMENDMENT, 2023 – WOMEN'S RESERVATION
- **Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam**; assent **28 September 2023**.
- About **one-third** seats for women in **Lok Sabha (330A)** and **State Assemblies (332A)**; **334A** on timing; **239AA** amended for **Delhi**.
- Starts only after **delimitation** on the **first Census after commencement**; lasts **15 years**, extendable by Parliament by law.
- Still **unimplemented** (Aug 2026); LoP asked why it was tied to delimitation.
- **84th**: seat freeze on **1971 Census** to **2026**; now tied to the **2027 Census**.
- Debates: **no BC sub-quota**; **southern States** fear a smaller share.
- Local bodies already have **one-third for women** since **1992** (**243D, 243T**).
