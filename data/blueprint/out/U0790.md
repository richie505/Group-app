## U0790 | BHARAT STAGE EMISSION NORMS – BS-I TO BS-VI
### 1. ORIGINS AND AUTHORITY
- First vehicle norms: **1991 (petrol), 1992 (diesel)**.
- **Mashelkar Committee → National Auto Fuel Policy (2003) → Euro norms**.
- Euro-based **Bharat Stage** name from **2000** (APPSC 2019); BS-I also called **"India 2000"**.
- **Euro I** in Europe: **1992**; India lagged Europe by **~5 years**.
- Notified by **MoRTH** under **CMV Rules, 1989** on CPCB/MoEFCC advice – not MoEFCC.
- Tractor norms: 1999; Trem Stage II 1 June 2003; Stage III 1 Oct 2005.
### 2. STAGE → YEAR
| Stage | Euro equivalent | First | Nationwide |
|---|---|---|---|
| BS-I | Euro I | – | **1 April 2000** |
| BS-II | Euro II | **Delhi, 1 April 2000** | 2005 |
| BS-III | Euro III | NCR + 10 cities (incl. Hyderabad-Secunderabad), 1 April 2005 | 2010 |
| BS-IV | Euro IV | 13 cities, 2010 | **April 2017** |
| BS-V | Euro 5 | **Skipped** | – |
| **BS-VI** | **Euro 6** | – | **1 April 2020** |
| BS-VI Phase 2 | – | – | April 2023 (**Real Driving Emissions**) |
| BS-VII | Euro 7 | Planned **2026-27** | – |
### 3. WHY BS-V WAS SKIPPED
- Decision **2016**: BS-IV straight to BS-VI.
- Reasons: urgent **Delhi-NCR** air pollution; close the gap with Europe in **one step** instead of two changeovers.
- **Supreme Court (2018)**: no **sale or registration of BS-IV** vehicles from **1 April 2020** (APPSC 2018).
!TRAP BS-V is the only stage India skipped.
### 4. POLLUTANTS AND LIMITS
| Stage | Fuel sulphur |
|---|---|
| BS-II | 500 ppm |
| BS-III | 350 ppm |
| BS-IV | 50 ppm |
| BS-VI | **10 ppm** (−80% vs BS-IV) |
- Euro II ultra-low sulphur diesel: **0.05%** or less = 500 ppm (UPPSC 2013).
- Euro norms limit **CO** (RPSC 1999); cover **CO, hydrocarbons and lead** → "all of the above" (UPPSC 2013).
- BS-VI limits: NOx **60 mg/km petrol, 80 mg/km diesel**; diesel PM **4.5 mg/km**. Expected NOx cut: diesel cars ~70%, petrol ~25%.
### 5. VEHICULAR POLLUTANTS → HEALTH EFFECT
| Pollutant | Effect |
|---|---|
| CO | Forms **carboxyhaemoglobin**; cuts oxygen supply |
| NOx | Respiratory illness, children most at risk; forms **ground-level ozone and acid rain** |
| PM2.5 | Deep lungs/bloodstream; heart and lung disease |
| **Benzene** | **Carcinogen – leukaemia** |
| Lead | Neurotoxin; leaded petrol phased out by 2000 |
- **Photochemical smog**: NOx + hydrocarbons + **sunlight**. Acid rain: SO2 + NOx, pH < 5.6.
- **Chernobyl** = nuclear (radioactive) pollution: hereditary changes, cancer. **Nanoparticles** pollute water, soil, food chain.
- **Bioremediation** uses microorganisms; heavy metals (**cadmium, lead**) limit it by killing microbes.
### 6. CONTROL TECHNOLOGIES
| Technology | Function |
|---|---|
| Catalytic converter (BS-I era) | CO → CO2 |
| Multi-point fuel injection (BS-II) | Replaced carburettors |
| **SCR** | Diesel NOx → **N2 + water** using urea **AdBlue** |
| **DPF** | Traps soot, oxidises it |
| **OBD-II** | Monitors emissions, alerts driver |
| Electronic Fuel Injection | Mandated for two-wheelers |
### 7. IMPLICATIONS AND ALLIED MEASURES
- Refineries upgraded to supply **10 ppm** fuel nationwide; sulphur cut 500 → 10 ppm in ~15 years.
- **CAFE III** third draft (**16 July 2026**): cars from **April 2027**; credits for ethanol, CBG, biofuels; targets **~21% relaxed** (not stricter) vs Sept 2025 proposal; average weight 1,229 kg.
- **E20**: notified 17 Feb 2026; all States/UTs from **1 April 2026**, min **RON 95** (not 91); replaces 2021 E10 notification.
- **Vehicle Scrappage Policy (2021)**: Automated Testing Stations + Registered Vehicle Scrapping Facilities.
- **PUC** mandatory under CMV Rules 1989; officers trained at **ARAI Pune** and IIP Dehradun.
