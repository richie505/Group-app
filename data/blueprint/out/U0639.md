## U0639 | ANDHRA PRADESH – NATURAL VEGETATION AND FORESTS; FOREST COVER AND TYPES
### 1. FOREST PROFILE OF AP
- AP forests are **predominantly tropical deciduous** (dry and moist); thorn and mangrove forests are smaller.
- **Largest forest type by area = tropical dry deciduous**.
- **Nallamala** = the **biggest forest** (largest forest block) of AP; the Gundlakamma forest zone is part of it (Kurnool).
- **Seshachalam** hills lie in southern AP; **Tirumala**: TTD reports **89.40% green cover**; its Forest Department manages 2,719 ha (May 2026).
### 2. FOREST TYPES BY RAINFALL
| Forest type | Rainfall | Where | Species |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Moist deciduous** (wettest) | **125–200 cm** | old **Srikakulam, Visakhapatnam**, East Godavari **Agency**; Araku, Paderu, **Papikondalu** | **bamboo, sandalwood**, medicinal plants |
| **Dry deciduous** (largest) | **75–100 cm** | old **Kadapa, Kurnool**; **Seshachalam–Nallamala**, Eastern Ghats, plateau | **teak, Terminalia, red sanders, Anogeissus** |
| **Short thorn / scrub** | **less than 70 cm** | **Kadapa, Kurnool, Chittoor, Anantapur** (Rayalaseema) | **Acacia, Ziziphus**, Prosopis (invasive) |
| **Tidal / mangrove (Koringa)** | — | **mouths of Godavari and Krishna** (Coringa), **Pulicat** | **Rhizophora, Avicennia, Excoecaria** |
- Moist deciduous = old Srikakulam, Visakhapatnam, East Godavari agency; dry deciduous and thorn = Rayalaseema.
- Littoral/mangrove = Godavari–Krishna deltas and Pulicat (**not** Anantapur, Kadapa, Kurnool).
- **Coringa** tidal forests = Godavari–Krishna mouths (**not** Seshachalam–Nallamala).
!TRAP Seshachalam = **dry deciduous** (APPSC 2023 key); **Papikonda NP** = **moist deciduous**.

#### 2.2 Mangroves
- Godavari–Krishna estuaries are called India's **2nd-largest mangrove ecosystem** – true only on the **east coast**.
- By ISFR mangrove-cover area AP ranks **behind West Bengal, Gujarat and Andaman & Nicobar** – not second in India.
- ISFR 2023: AP mangrove cover rose by **13.01 sq km** over 2021.
- **MISHTI** (mangrove restoration): announced in **Union Budget 2023–24**, launched **5 June 2023** – not 2019.
### 3. FOREST COVER – ISFR DATA
- ISFR 2023 (India): forest + tree cover **8,27,357 sq km = 25.17%** of India; gain since 2021 **1,445 sq km**, of which **true forest only 156 sq km** (rest tree cover).
- AP: ISFR 2023 forest cover rose by about **95.59 sq km** across **10 districts**.
- India ranked **third globally** in forest-area increase, **2010–2020**.
- India: **Madhya Pradesh** has the **maximum forest area** (forest cover and recorded forest area), then Arunachal, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, Maharashtra; MP also has the largest dense deciduous cover.
- Critique: **"Ek Ped Maa Ke Naam"** (140 crore saplings) adds **tree cover, not natural forest**.

### 6. FOREST FIRES
- **2023–24 fire season: AP first in India** in forest area affected – **5,286.76 sq km** (APPSC 2025 key).
- 2026 fire-prone zones: **Chittoor and Annamayya** – Horsley Hills, Mallappa Konda (near Kuppam), Mallaiahkonda, Somala belt; temperatures above 45 °C in April–May.

### 9. AGENCIES AND PLANTATIONS
| Agency | Function |
|---|---|
| **GCC** (Girijan Co-operative Corporation), Visakhapatnam | **buys minor forest produce** from tribals |
| **TRIFED** | **1987**, under **Multi-State Co-operative Societies Act 1984**; **Ministry of Tribal Affairs**; nodal agency for **MSP for MFP** |
| **APFDC** | Government's agent for **exporting red sanders wood and beedi leaf** |
| **CAMPA** | funds **compensatory afforestation** via an **Annual Plan of Operation** |
- **APFDC** = **largest Eucalyptus grower among PSUs** in India (**33,584 ha**); largest APFDC plantation area (to Dec 2021) = **Eucalyptus**.
- APFDC **coffee**: Visakhapatnam + East Godavari; **cashew**: Guntur–Prakasam–Nellore coast, East and West Godavari, Chittoor (**not only the agency**).
### 10. PROGRAMMES, DIVERSION AND LAW
- **Vanam-Manam**: green cover **outside reserved forests**; **5.29 crore seedlings** in 2024–25 (till Dec).
- Mixed Plantation Scheme: 87 km of avenue plantations.
- **Trees Outside Forests in India (TOFI)**: **USAID + MoEFCC**, five years, launched **8 Sep 2022**; **AP is one of 7 States** (with Assam, Haryana, Odisha, Rajasthan, TN, UP).
- Diversion under **Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam, 1980**: **26,924.34 ha** up to 2017-18; **27,641.252 ha** in all (later total).
- Van Adhiniyam changes (Jan 2026): **commercial plantations** allowed on **degraded forest land**; national goal **33%** forest cover.
- **16th Finance Commission**: counts **open forests** in the forest criterion **for the first time**, with **density-based weights** (Feb 2026).
- **Rekapalli revolt (1879)** against British forest laws.
