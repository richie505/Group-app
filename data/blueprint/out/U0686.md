## U0686 | TRADE OF ANDHRA PRADESH - EXPORTS, PORT-BASED TRADE, SEZs
### 1. AP'S EXPORT PROFILE
- After bifurcation AP lost Hyderabad's industrial clusters and turned to **port-led development**.
- AP = **6th largest exporting State** (FY 2024-25; also 6th in FY 2023-24).
- Spread: **824 commodities to 194 countries**.
- Main products: **marine products, pharmaceuticals, food products, textiles, engineering goods**.

### 3. EXPORT ITEMS, DISTRICTS AND ITEMS NOT EXPORTED
| Item | District / place |
|---|---|
| Shrimp (mainly **white-leg**) | **West Godavari, Krishna, Nellore**; to US and EU |
| Chillies | **Guntur** |
| Pharma | **Parawada** (Anakapalli) |
| Petroleum products | **Visakhapatnam refinery** (not Kurnool) |
- AP supplies ~**70% of India's shrimp exports** (Jan 2026).
- **Crude oil is NOT an AP export** (APPSC 2019); AP exports minerals and fish.
- **50% US tariff** hit AP aqua: about Rs 25,000 crore exports and 2.5 lakh farm families at risk.
- Debates: shrimp farming salinised coastal soils; Pharma City effluents pollute water.
### 4. PORT-BASED TRADE
- Ports: **Visakhapatnam** (only major port of AP) + non-major **Krishnapatnam, Kakinada**, Gangavaram.
- Visakhapatnam Port handled a record **91.17 MMT in 2025-26** (82.62 MMT in 2024-25); 4th among major ports, 2nd on the east coast.
- AP coastline: **1,053.7 km**.

### 8. SEZ ACT 2005
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| SEZ policy | **2000**, under EXIM Policy |
| Act | **2005** |
| Who sets up | Centre, a State or **any person**, jointly or alone; also FTWZ |
| Approval | State to **Board of Approval** |
| Status | **Foreign territory** for trade and tariffs; single window |
| Baba Kalyani Committee (2018) | Rename SEZs **"3Es" - Employment and Economic Enclaves** |
- Objectives: additional **economic activity**, **exports** of goods and services, **domestic and foreign investment**, **employment**, **infrastructure** (BPSC 2022: all correct).
- SEZ exports: Rs 14.64 lakh crore (~US$ 173 bn) = ~21% of India's exports.
- Kandla = Asia's first EPZ (1965).
#### 8.1 SEZ vs EOU
| SEZ | EOU |
|---|---|
| Demarcated **enclave** under an Act | **Single unit** anywhere under FTP scheme (1981) |
| Developer + units | Unit only |

### 10. EXPORT INFRASTRUCTURE
- **Sri City dry port** proposal: under **Inter-Ministerial Committee** review, for on-site customs clearance.
- CBIC norm: no new dry port within **200 km of a seaport** or **100 km of an ICD**; AP is a "Blue-zone" State.
- **VCIC** = India's **first coastal industrial corridor**, part of **ECEC**; nodes **Visakhapatnam, Srikalahasti-Yerpedu, Kopparthy**.
- **Orvakal** = node of **HBIC**, not ECEC.
