### 1. AP BUDGET 2026-27 – SIZE AND DEFICITS
- **AP Budget 2026-27: ₹3,32,205 crore**, presented by Finance Minister **Payyavula Keshav** on **14 February 2026**.
#### 1.1 Budget at a Glance
| Item | ₹ crore |
|---|---|
| **Total outlay** | **3,32,205** |
| Revenue expenditure | 2,56,143 |
| Capital expenditure | 53,915 |
| Revenue deficit | 22,002 (about **1.11%** of GSDP) |
| Fiscal deficit | 75,868 (about **3.84%** of GSDP) |
| Primary deficit | about 1.95% of GSDP |
- GSDP assumed for 2026-27: **₹19,75,073 crore** (about 12% growth).

#### 1.2 Debt and Borrowings
- Outstanding debt about **36% of GSDP** in 2026-27, flat over the previous two years.
- Debt about **₹7.11 lakh crore** (2026-27) against ₹6.35 lakh crore (2025-26).
- Interest payments: about **16%** of revenue receipts.
!TRAP Fiscal deficit ₹75,868 crore is far bigger than revenue deficit ₹22,002 crore; do not swap them.

### 8. OTHER ECONOMIC DATA
- **Agricultural credit**: ₹1.27 lakh crore (2023) → **₹2.25 lakh crore** (2025), **77% growth** (national about 41%); stated by MoS Finance **Pankaj Chaudhary**.

### 9. VISION DOCUMENTS AND EXTERNALLY AIDED PROJECTS
#### 9.1 Swarna Andhra @2047
- Vision unveiled by CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu** in December 2024, aligned with **Viksit Bharat @2047**.
- Targets by **2047**: **$2.4 trillion** economy; per capita income about **$42,000** (about ₹55 lakh); annual growth **15%** or more.
- Guided by **10 principles (Padi Sutralu)**: zero poverty, employment, water security (river interlinking), agri-tech, energy costs, logistics, deep tech and others.
- **P4 model** = Public–Private–People Partnership, for **zero poverty**.

### 10. LOCAL BODIES AND CRIME DATA 2025
#### 10.1 BC Quota in Local Bodies
- **Rajeev Ranjan Mishra Commission** on BC reservation in local bodies.
- **Article 243D** = reservation in Panchayats; **Article 243T** = reservation in Municipalities (73rd/74th Amendments, 1992).
#### 10.2 Crime 2025 vs 2024 (DGP data at CM's review)
- **Overall crime down 5.5%**; 16 districts fell, 10 rose.
- Highest rise: **Annamayya (9.5%)**, Konaseema (8.5%), Nellore (7.8%), Guntur and Tirupati (6.5%).
| Category | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Crime against women | 16,913 | 16,288 |
| **Economic offences** | 7,539 | 8,035 (**+6.6%**) |
| Cyber crime | 2,853 | 1,771 |
- Cyber loss ₹751 crore; frozen ₹89 crore; returned ₹2.21 crore.
- **Operation TRACE** (missing women/girls): 1,177 girls and 2,483 women traced in four months.
- NDPS: 6,139 arrested; 721 vehicles seized; properties about ₹9 crore attached; PIT-NDPS on 68.
- Drones: 347 drones; 6,993 cases detected.
- Rowdy sheeters 26,518; PD Act cases 153; externed 15.
- LWE: joint operations with **Chhattisgarh and Odisha**; **58 Maoists arrested**; **16 top cadre neutralised**.
!TRAP Economic offences **rose 6.6%** while overall crime fell 5.5%.
