## U0207 | REVOLUTIONARY NATIONALISM – ANUSHILAN SAMITI, YUGANTAR, ALIPORE AND KAKORI-ERA CASES, SAVARKAR
### 1. BACKGROUND AND NATURE
- Rose after the **Surat Split (1907)** and the failure of mass Swadeshi: individual heroic action (assassinations, "**swadeshi dacoities**") kept radical nationalism alive.
- Roots: **akhadas** (gymnasiums) and secret societies (**Anushilan**, **Atmonnati Samiti**).
- **Earliest root:** **Vasudev Balwant Phadke** founded the **Ramosi Krishak Jatha** (Maharashtra, **1879**), turning **Ramoshi** tribes into a combat force; life sentence; died **1883** on hunger strike in **Kala Pani**.
- **Weakness:** never a **mass movement**; Hindu-revivalist symbols (**Bhavani worship**) limited Muslim participation.
- Aurobindo wrote the pamphlet **Bhawani Mandir (1905)**.

#### 2.2 Anushilan Samiti – details
- Founded at **Midnapur, 1902** by **Gyanendra Nath Basu**; **Calcutta** branches 1902 (**Satish Chandra Basu**; P. Mitra, Bagha Jatin, Barindra Ghosh).
- Grew from **Barindra Ghosh's** activities; Barindra = **younger brother of Aurobindo**.
- **Pulin Behari Das**: founded the **National School, Dhaka**; led the Dhaka branch; planned the **Barrah Dacoity (1908)** – first major venture in East Bengal (Dhaka district), to fund the movement.
- **Sachindra Nath Sanyal**: Anushilan branch at **Patna, 1913**; author of **Bandi Jiwan**.
- **Yugantar Party** later led by **Jatindranath Mukherjee (Bagha Jatin, 1879–1915)**.
#### 2.3 Abhinav Bharat, HRA and HSRA
- Abhinav Bharat was named after **Mazzini's Young Italy**; took part in killing British officials.
- **HRA** formed after the **suspension of the Non-Cooperation Movement**.
- **HSRA:** Azad rebuilt the HRA as HSRA after **Bismil's** death; goal: socialist "**Federal Republic of United States of India**".
- India House hub: **Savarkar, Hardayal, Madan Lal Dhingra**; Ghadar HQ: **Yugantar Ashram, San Francisco**.
!TRAP Anushilan (1902) = **P. Mitra**; Yugantar (1906) = **Barindra Ghosh**, its offshoot. Bharat Mata Society = **Punjab** (Ajit Singh), not Bengal, not Pulin Behari Das.
!KEY APPSC 2018, 2019: Abhinav Bharat = **Savarkar**. APPSC G1 2024: HRA founders = **Sanyal, Chatterjee, Bismil**.
### 3. EARLY ACTIONS – CHAPEKARS, MUZAFFARPUR, NASIK, VANCHI AIYAR
#### 3.1 Chapekar brothers (Poona, June 1897)
- **Damodar and Balkrishna Chapekar** shot **Plague Commissioner W.C. Rand** (and **Lt Ayerst**); protest against plague-control excesses.
#### 3.2 Muzaffarpur bomb (30 April 1908)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Place | **Muzaffarpur (Bihar)** |
| Accused | **Khudiram Bose** and **Prafulla Chaki** |
| Target | Magistrate **Douglas Kingsford** (earlier Chief Calcutta Presidency Magistrate) |
| Result | Bomb killed **two British women** instead |
| Fate | Khudiram **hanged** at 18 (**11 August 1908**); Prafulla Chaki **shot himself** before arrest |
!TRAP **Kingsford** was the target; he survived; two British women died.
#### 3.3 Nasik and London (1909)
- **Anant Kanhere** killed **A.M.T. Jackson**, Collector of **Nasik** → **Nasik Conspiracy Case**.
- **Madan Lal Dhingra** shot **Curzon Wyllie** in **London** (1 July 1909).
#### 3.4 Revolutionaries in the South – Vanchi Aiyar
- **17 June 1911:** **Vanchi Aiyar** shot **Robert Ashe**, Collector of **Tirunelveli**, at **Maniyachi railway station**, then shot himself.
- Links: **Bharata Matha Association**; **Swadeshi Steam Navigation** agitation of **V.O. Chidambaram Pillai**.
### 4. ALIPORE BOMB CASE (1908)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Trigger | Police raid on the **Maniktala (Maniktalla) bomb factory**, Calcutta, after Muzaffarpur |
| Accused | **49**, incl. **Aurobindo** and **Barindra Ghosh**; **19 sentenced** |
| Defence counsel | **C.R. (Chittaranjan) Das** |
| Verdict | **Aurobindo acquitted**; **Barindra – life** (Andaman Cellular Jail) |
| Jail | **Alipore jail, Calcutta** |
- Approver **Narendranath Goswami** was shot inside the jail by **Kanailal Dutta** and **Satyendranath Bose**.
!TRAP Alipore: Aurobindo **acquitted** (C.R. Das); Barindra = **life**.
### 5. DELHI CONSPIRACY CASE – HARDINGE BOMB (1912)
- **23 December 1912:** bomb at Viceroy **Lord Hardinge** during his **state entry** into the new capital Delhi, procession at **Chandni Chowk**; **injured, not killed**.
- Organisation: **Anushilan Samiti** (UPPSC 2023 key); led by **Rash Behari Bose** with **Sachin Sanyal**; bomb thrown by **Basanta Kumar Biswas**.
- **Executed:** Basant Kumar Biswas, **Amir Chand** (Master Ami Chand), **Avadh Behari**, Bhai Balmukund.
- **Not caught:** **Rash Behari Bose** escaped in disguise, later to **Japan**.

### 7. V.D. SAVARKAR
- Societies: **Mitra Mela (1899)** → **Abhinav Bharat (1904)** (college days, Pune); in London worked from **India House**.
- Arrested in **London (1910)**; failed escape at **Marseilles**; **Nasik case** → life in the **Andamans (Cellular Jail)**.
- Books: **The Indian War of Independence, 1857 (1909)**; **Hindutva (1923)**; a biography of **Mazzini**.

### 11. KOMAGATA MARU AND SINGAPORE MUTINY
- **Komagata Maru (1914):** **376** mostly **Sikh** passengers refused entry into **Canada**; forced back to India → **Budge Budge** firing killed **22**; radicalised the Ghadar movement.
- **2 May 2026:** the 2016 apology of Canada's PM **Trudeau** in the House of Commons was recalled.
- **Singapore Mutiny (Feb 1915):** Indian troops (**5th Light Infantry**) rose under Ghadar influence.
### 12. REVOLUTIONARIES IN ANDHRA
#### 12.1 Alluri Sitarama Raju – Rampa Rebellion (1922–24)
- Met Bengal revolutionaries at **Chittagong (1921)**.
- Against the **Muttadari system**, **forest laws**, Tahsildar **Bastian**.
- First attack: **Chintapalli police station, 22 August 1922** → Krishnadevipeta, Rajavommangi.
- Lieutenants: **Gam Mallu Dora**, **Gam Gantam Dora**; officers **Scott Coward** and **Haytes** killed.
- **Panjerighat** = a later direct battle, not a police-station raid; **Malabar Special Police** brought in.
- Raju killed **7 May 1924** (not 1922).
- **Not linked to the INC**, yet troubled the British more than the Non-Cooperation Movement.
