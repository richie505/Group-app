### 2. PRIVATE SPACE – VIKRAM-1 AND THE SPACE ECONOMY
#### 2.1 Vikram-1 (Skyroot) – India's First Private Orbital Launch
- Launched **18 July 2026**, 12:05:30 PM, **SDSC Sriharikota** – **India's first private orbital rocket launch from Indian soil**; **Mission Aagaman**.
- Developer **Skyroot Aerospace, Hyderabad** (est. 2018; founders **Pawan Kumar Chandana, Naga Bharath Daka**).
- India = **third country after USA and China** with private orbital launch capability.
- Early July: first stage stacked at First Launch Pad – first privately built orbital-class rocket stacked there.
- Orbit ~**450 km LEO**; payload injected ~15 minutes after lift-off.
| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Stages | **4** – 3 solid + liquid **Orbital Adjustment Module** |
| Upper-stage engine | **RAMAN-I** – **3D-printed** liquid engine (tested at LPSC) |
| Payload | up to **350 kg to LEO** |
| Diameter | ~1.7 m |
| Firsts | All-carbon composite structure; first 3D-printed liquid engine in an Indian rocket; **Pneumatic Separation System** |
| Payload | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **SCOPE** | Skyroot's own flight-data satellite |

- Also carried a lab-grown, lotus-shaped diamond.
!TRAP **Vikram-S (2022)** was **sub-orbital** (first private Indian rocket to reach space); **Vikram-1** is orbital.
