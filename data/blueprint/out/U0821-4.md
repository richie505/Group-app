### 1. INDIA–EU FREE TRADE AGREEMENT
#### 1.1 News
- India and the EU **concluded negotiations** on a comprehensive FTA in **January 2026**; called the **"Mother of All Deals"**.
- The **EU is India's 22nd FTA partner**; India's FTA count: **15 FTAs covering 27 countries**.
- Runs alongside an **Investment Protection Agreement** and a **GI Agreement**.
- **90.7%** of India's exports become **duty-free at once**.
- Next steps: **legal scrubbing**, translation, then **ratification by the 27 member states and the European Parliament**.
- Talks began **2007**; revived after **2022**.
#### 1.2 Commitments
| Item | EU | India |
|---|---|---|
| Tariff lines liberalised | **97%** | **92.1%** |
| Services subsectors | **144** | **102** |
- **Labour-intensive gainers** (about **USD 33 billion** of exports duty-free; current tariffs **4–26%** removed): **textiles, apparel, leather, footwear, marine products, gems and jewellery, toys, sports goods**.
- **India protected:** **dairy, cereals, poultry, soymeal**; gradual opening for **automobiles, wines and spirits**.
- Car tariffs: **70–110% → 40% → 10%** in phases; wine **20–30%**, spirits **40%**, beer **50%**; **olive oil** duty eliminated.
- **Rules of origin:** self-certification via **Statements of Origin**; flexibility for **shrimps** and **downstream aluminium**.
- Combined: about **25% of global GDP** and **one-third of global trade** (India **4th**, EU **2nd** largest economy).
#### 1.3 Concerns
- **CBAM** (steel, aluminium, cement, chemicals): Indian steel may face a **20–35% cost rise** from **2026**.
- **EUDR** – deforestation after **2020**; **CSDDD** – applies from **2027**; EU objections to India's **QCOs**.
- Average tariff: **India 10–12%** vs **EU 3–4%**.
#### 1.4 India–EU Trade and Background
- EU = India's **largest goods partner** – **USD 135 billion (FY24)**; services **USD 53 billion**; EU FDI **USD 117 billion+**.
- Bilateral trade about **USD 190 billion (2024–25)**; EU about **17%** of India's exports; EU FDI **USD 117.4 billion (2020–24)**.
- EU evolution: **ECSC 1951 → Treaties of Rome 1957 → Maastricht 1992 → Brexit 2020** (now **27 members**).
- **Schengen** includes **4 non-EU states**; **Bulgaria and Romania** full Schengen members from **1 January 2025**.
- **EU Stability and Growth Pact** – limits budget deficits.
- Other links: **Operation Atalanta** (EUNAVFOR); EU joined **IPOI (2023)**; **CECP (2016)**; **IMEC**; ISRO launched ESA's **Proba-3 (2024)**; India an **associate member of CERN**.
#### 1.5 India–Euratom Cooperation
- India and the EU to strengthen **peaceful (civilian only)** nuclear cooperation under the **India–Euratom Agreement**.
- **Euratom:** est. **1957** by the **Treaty of Rome**; HQ **Brussels**.
### 2. INDIA'S OTHER TRADE AGREEMENTS
#### 2.1 India–Oman CEPA
- Signed in the presence of **Narendra Modi** and **Sultan Haitham bin Tarik**.
| Side | Tariff lines | Trade covered |
|---|---|---|
| Oman (duty-free for India) | **98.08%** | **99.38%** of India's exports |
| India (liberalised) | **77.79%** | **94.81%** of imports from Oman |
- **Excluded by India:** dairy, tea, coffee, rubber, tobacco, **gold/silver bullion and jewellery**, footwear, sports goods, base metal scrap (petroleum/crude is **not** excluded).
- **Mode 4:** **Intra-Corporate Transferees** quota **20% → 50%**; **Contractual Service Suppliers** stay **90 days → two years** (+2 years extension).
- **100% FDI** by Indian firms in major services sectors in Oman.
- Oman's **first bilateral trade agreement since 2006**; India's **second CEPA with a GCC country** after the **UAE (2022)**.
- Trade 2024–25: exports to Oman **$4.06 billion** (**0.93%** of India's exports; petroleum products **35.1%**); imports **$6.5 billion** (crude/petroleum gases **38%**, fertilisers **16.3%**).
- **Oman:** capital **Muscat**; GCC member; **oldest independent state in the Arab world**; borders **Yemen, Saudi Arabia, UAE**; Arabian Sea and Gulf of Oman.
- Features: **Rub al-Khali** (Empty Quarter), **Hajar Mountains** (north), **Dhofar Range** (south).
- **Exercise Naseem Al Bahr:** India–Oman naval exercise since **1993**; Oman = **first GCC country** to hold a bilateral naval exercise with India.
#### 2.2 India–GCC FTA Talks Restarted (February 2026)
- India and the GCC signed the **Terms of Reference** for an FTA in **New Delhi**; chaired by **Piyush Goyal**; GCC chief negotiator **Raja Al Marzouqi**.
- **GCC:** est. **1981**; HQ **Riyadh**; members **Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain**.
#### 2.3 AYUSH in Trade Deals
- AYUSH got **dedicated annexures** in trade agreements with **Oman and New Zealand** (finalised **December 2025**).
- AYUSH exports up **6.11%**: **USD 649.2 million (2023–24) → USD 688.89 million (2024–25)**.

|---|---|---|

| India–UAE **CEPA** | 2022 | **1st CEPA in the GCC**; trade crossed **USD 100 billion** (2024–25) |
| India–Australia **ECTA** | Dec 2022 (signed) | – |
| India–EFTA **TEPA** | 2024–25 | **USD 100 billion FDI** commitment (Switzerland, Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein) |

### 5. MARITIME LABOUR CONVENTION 2006 – 20 YEARS
- **ILO and IMO** marked **20 years** of **MLC 2006** – the **"Seafarers' Bill of Rights"**.
- Adopted **23 February 2006** at the **International Labour Conference, Geneva**; consolidated **60+** earlier ILO maritime conventions.
- Called the **"fourth pillar" of international maritime law**; enforced via **Flag State–Port State inspections**.
- **ILO:** Geneva; est. **1919**; **tripartite**. **IMO:** London.
### 6. INDIA–UK AND OTHER BILATERAL PACTS
#### 6.1 India–UK Social Security Contributions Agreement
- Signed **10 February 2026**, **New Delhi** – avoids **double contributions** for postings up to **36 months**.
- Signed by FS **Vikram Misri** and UK HC **Lindy Cameron**; agreed during the **CETA** talks (**July 2025**).
#### 6.2 India–UK Offshore Wind Taskforce
- Launched **18 February 2026** at the **4th India–UK Energy Dialogue** – **Pralhad Joshi** and **David Lammy**; under **India–UK Vision 2035**.
- Initial zones: **Gujarat and Tamil Nadu**; **₹7,453 crore VGF** for early projects.
- India's capacity (2026): non-fossil **272 GW+**; solar **141 GW+**; wind **55 GW**; FY 2025–26 added **35 GW solar**, **4.61 GW wind**.
#### 6.3 India–WFP Rice Supply Agreement
- India signed a **five-year rice supply agreement** with the **UN World Food Programme**.
- **WFP:** est. **1961**; HQ **Rome**; ED **Cindy McCain**; **Nobel Peace Prize 2020**. (FCI set up **1965**.)
#### 6.4 India–Netherlands Hydrogen Fellowship Programme
- Launched **6 February 2026**; coordinated by **DST** (est. **1971**).
- Academic partnership between the **University of Groningen** and **19 IITs**; joint research on **green hydrogen**.
#### 6.5 IGGEFIL's First International Green Loan – Zambia
- **IREDA Global Green Energy Finance IFSC Ltd (IGGEFIL)** sanctioned **USD 22.5 million** to **Swarna Solar Ltd** – **100 MW** solar in **Serenje district, Central Province, Zambia**.
- Zambia: capital **Lusaka**; currency **kwacha**; President **Hakainde Hichilema**.
### 7. US WITHDRAWALS FROM INTERNATIONAL BODIES
- **US withdrew from the WHO** in **January 2026** after the **mandatory one-year notice**; may create about an **18% funding gap**.
- **WHO:** est. **7 April 1948**; HQ **Geneva**; **194** members.
- Jan 2026 memorandum to quit **66 bodies** – **35 non-UN + 31 UN** organisations and treaties.
- These include **WHO, UNFCCC** (US the **first country to attempt formal withdrawal**), **IPCC, ISA, IRENA, UNFPA, UN Women, UNHRC, UNCTAD** and **UNESCO** (**third exit**, after **1984** and **2017**).
- The US left the **Paris Agreement a second time** in **January 2026**.
!TRAP The UNESCO exit is the US's **third** (1984, 2017, 2026).
### 8. EUROZONE, GAZA AND OTHER WORLD DEVELOPMENTS
#### 8.1 Bulgaria Adopts the Euro
- Bulgaria replaced the **lev** with the euro – the **21st EU country** to adopt the euro (Eurozone had **20** members before it).
- EU member since **2007**; capital **Sofia**; **ECB** formed **1998**.
- Eurozone entry via **Maastricht criteria**: deficit **< 3%**, debt **< 60%** of GDP, **ERM II**.
#### 8.2 Gaza Peace Council
- Chaired by **Donald Trump**; announced on **Truth Social**; second phase of the **Gaza ceasefire**.
- Executive Committee Director-General: **Nikolay Mladenov**; members – **Steve Witkoff, Jared Kushner, Tony Blair, Ajay Banga**.

- **Indonesia:** revised **Criminal Code** in force **2 January 2026** (passed **December 2022**, **3-year** transition); extramarital sex – up to **one year**; cohabitation – up to **six months**. Largest Muslim-majority country; capital **Jakarta**; President **Prabowo Subianto**.
- **Turkmenistan:** legalised **crypto mining and trading** from **January 2026**; law signed by President **Serdar Berdimuhamedow**; licences from the **central bank**; capital **Ashgabat**; currency **manat**.
- **Ecuador:** **60-day state of emergency** by President **Daniel Noboa** in **nine provinces and three municipalities**; signed **31 December**, in force **1 January**. Provinces include **Guayas, Manabí, Santa Elena, Los Ríos, El Oro, Esmeraldas, Pichincha, Santo Domingo, Sucumbíos**. Capital **Quito** (not Guayaquil); currency **US dollar**.
