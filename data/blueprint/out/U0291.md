## U0291 | FUNDAMENTAL DUTIES – ARTICLE 51A, SWARAN SINGH COMMITTEE, ENFORCEABILITY
### 1. BASICS – PART IVA AND ARTICLE 51A
- Located in **Part IVA** (not Part VI-A) – contains a **single article, Article 51A**, placed after Part IV (DPSP).
- Opening words: "It shall be the duty of **every citizen of India**."
- Added by the **42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976** ("**Mini Constitution**"); in force **3 January 1977**; added a **new chapter** – duties are **not part of Fundamental Rights**.
- The original 10 duties are called the **"10 commandments"**.
- Recommended by the **Swaran Singh Committee (1976)**.
- Number: originally **10**; presently **11** (11th by the **86th Amendment, 2002**).
- Part IVA is one of **four Parts added after 1950**: **IVA, IXA, IXB, XIVA**.
- Other Parts: **Part VI = The States (Articles 152–237)**; Part XI is NOT The Municipalities (that is IXA).
!NUM 8 – 10 – 11 = suggested by Swaran Singh – added in 1976 – total after 2002
### 2. ALL 11 FUNDAMENTAL DUTIES – CLAUSE-WISE
| Clause | Fundamental Duty | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| 51A(a) | Abide by the Constitution; respect its ideals, institutions, **National Flag** and **National Anthem** | Civic |
| 51A(b) | Cherish and follow the **noble ideals of the freedom struggle** | Moral |
| 51A(c) | Uphold and protect **sovereignty, unity and integrity** of India | Civic |
| 51A(d) | **Defend the country** and render national service when called upon | Civic |
| 51A(e) | Promote harmony and **common brotherhood**; renounce practices **derogatory to the dignity of women** | Civic/Moral |
| 51A(f) | Value and preserve the rich heritage of our **composite culture** | Moral |
| 51A(g) | Protect and improve the **natural environment** – forests, lakes, rivers, wildlife; **compassion for living creatures** | Civic |
| 51A(h) | Develop **scientific temper, humanism**, spirit of inquiry and reform | Moral |
| 51A(i) | **Safeguard public property** and abjure violence | Civic |
| 51A(j) | Strive towards **excellence** in every sphere, so the nation rises to higher levels of endeavour | Moral |
| 51A(k) | **Parent or guardian** to provide opportunities for **education** to child/ward aged **6–14 years** | Civic |
- **Moral duties: (b), (f), (h), (j)**; civic duties: (a), (c), (d), (g), (i).
- Order mnemonic: Constitution – Freedom ideals – Sovereignty – Defence – Brotherhood – Culture – Environment – Scientific temper – Public property – Excellence – Education.
!KEY Article 51A(i): citizens protect **public property** (not Article 48A).
### 3. WHAT IS NOT A FUNDAMENTAL DUTY
- **To protect the elderly, differently abled and children** (APPSC G2 Mains 2025, asked twice); the closest is 51A(k).
- **To vote** in public/general elections.
- **To pay taxes**; **family planning**.
- To **protect monuments** and places of national importance – DPSP, **Article 49**.
- To **protect weaker sections** from social injustice – DPSP, **Article 46**.
- To strive towards **abolition of untouchability** – FR, **Article 17**.
- To **organise village panchayats** – DPSP, **Article 40**.
!TRAP Real duties include scientific temper 51A(h), safeguarding public property 51A(i), abiding by the Constitution 51A(a). Check the list word for word.

#### 4.2 Recommendations NOT Accepted
1. Parliament to prescribe **penalty/punishment** for non-compliance.
2. Such penalty laws **not to be challengeable in courts** for violating FRs or any other provision.
3. **Duty to pay taxes** as a Fundamental Duty.
!TRAP Swaran Singh suggested **8** duties, not 10.
### 5. CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENTS
| Particular | 42nd CAA, 1976 | 86th CAA, 2002 |
|---|---|---|
| Duties | 10 – clauses (a)–(j) | 11 – added (k) |
| Change | Inserted **Part IVA**, **Art. 51A** | Added **51A(k)** – education of child 6–14 |
| Same Act also | Added **48A**; moved forests, wild animals and birds to Concurrent List (**Entries 17A, 17B**) | Inserted **Art. 21A**; substituted **Art. 45** |
- **51A(g)** (environment) came in **1976** by the **42nd Amendment**, after the **1972 Stockholm Conference**.
- **Right to Education** became an FR by the **86th Amendment**.
!TRAP 11th duty = **86th, 2002** – not "96th", not "2001" (APPSC G2 Mains 2025).
### 6. APPLICABILITY AND SOURCE
#### 6.1 To Whom
- Apply **only to citizens** – not to foreigners.
- Unlike FRs under **Articles 14, 20, 21, 25**, which are open to non-citizens.
- **51A(k)** is the **only duty addressed to parents or guardians**.
- "Available against the State" describes FRs, not duties.
!TRAP APPSC statement: "Article 51A(i) also applies to resident foreigners" is FALSE.
#### 6.2 Source and Comparison
| Country | Point |
|---|---|
| **USSR** | Source – Constitution of the former **Soviet Union** (Russian) |
| **Japan** | Among major democracies, **only Japan** has a comparable list of citizens' duties |
| Socialist States | Give rights and duties **equal weight** |
| USA | Source of many FRs, not duties |
### 7. ENFORCEABILITY AND JUSTICIABILITY
#### 7.1 Legal Status
- **Non-justiciable**: courts **cannot directly enforce** or punish; **cannot be enforced by writs**; no legal sanction in the Constitution.
- **Moral obligations** of citizens; called a "**code of moral precepts**"; critics call them **superfluous**.
- **Parliament can enforce** them by **legislation**.
- Courts use them to **interpret ambiguous laws**, to test **constitutionality**, and to judge the **reasonableness** of a law restricting an FR.
- They do **not** give a ground for **suspending Fundamental Rights**.
#### 7.2 FR, DPSP and FD
| Feature | Fundamental Rights | DPSP | Fundamental Duties |
|---|---|---|---|
| Part | III | IV | IVA |
| Article | 12–35 | 36–51 | 51A |
| Nature | Justiciable | Non-justiciable | Non-justiciable |
| Addressee | State | State | Citizens |
#### 7.3 J.S. Verma Committee – 1999
- On **operationalising** Fundamental Duties; found **existing laws** already give effect to some.
| Existing Law | Duty served |
|---|---|
| **Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971** | 51A(a) – Constitution, Flag, Anthem |
| **Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955** | 51A(e) – harmony, no caste/religion offences |
| **Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, 1967** | 51A(c) – sovereignty and integrity |
| Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972; Forest (Conservation) Act, 1980 | 51A(g) – environment |
- Imputations prejudicial to national integration: now **Sec. 197, BNS 2023** (earlier IPC Sec. 153B).

### 8. RIGHTS AND DUTIES – JUDICIAL INTERPRETATION
#### 8.1 Correlative Relationship
- **Rights are correlative with duties** – every right has a corresponding duty.
- **Holland**: others holding back (from interfering) is called a duty.
- Example: **Article 19(1)(a)** (free speech) goes with **51A(c)** (sovereignty, unity, integrity).
- Rights must be exercised with restraint and respect for others' rights.
#### 8.2 Cases
| Case | Year | Held |
|---|---|---|
| **Bijoe Emmanuel v. State of Kerala** | 1986 | Duties **cannot override FRs**; respect for national symbols needs respectful conduct, **not compelled participation** |
| **AIIMS Students' Union v. AIIMS** | 2002 | Rights and duties **correlative**; duties as important as rights |
| **M.K. Ranjitsinh v. Union of India** | – | **CSR includes environmental protection**, linked to 51A(g) |
| SC on corporations | Mar 2026 | Corporations, as **legal persons**, also carry the 51A(g) duty (CSR = at least 2% of average net profit, Sec. 135, Companies Act, 2013) |
| SC on Pollution Control Boards | Aug 2025 | Linked PCB enforcement to 51A(g) |
- **Vande Mataram** recitation debate (Aug 2026): duties under 51A **cannot curtail Articles 25 and 26**.

### 10. CRITICISM AND SIGNIFICANCE
- **Not exhaustive**: omits **voting, paying taxes, family planning**.
- **Vague**: "noble ideals", "scientific temper", "composite culture".
- **Non-justiciable**: a code of moral precepts.
- **Placement**: an **appendage to Part IV** instead of after Part III – reduces their value.
- Significance: remind citizens of obligations; help courts on reasonableness; back action against **anti-national activities**; promote civic virtue and patriotism.
