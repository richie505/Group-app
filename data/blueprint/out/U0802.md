## U0802 | REGULATION AND ETHICS OF BIOTECHNOLOGY – GEAC, BIOSAFETY, BIOETHICS, BIOPIRACY, IPR
### 1. GEAC – GENETIC ENGINEERING APPRAISAL COMMITTEE
- **Apex statutory body regulating GMOs** in India (APPSC G2 2023) – **NOT FSSAI, CPCB or ICAR**.
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Ministry | **MoEFCC** |
| Basis | **Rules, 1989** under the **Environment (Protection) Act, 1986** |
| Chair | **Special/Additional Secretary, MoEFCC**; co-chair from **DBT** |
| Renaming | "Approval" → **"Appraisal"** Committee, **July 2010** |
- Functions: **field trials**, **environmental release**, **commercial cultivation**, large-scale use of **hazardous microorganisms and recombinants**.
- GEAC permission is needed to cultivate any GM crop (e.g. Bt cotton).
!TRAP Fixing the MRP of commercial seeds is NOT a GEAC function.
#### 1.1 Related Bodies (match-type)
| Body | Law |
|---|---|
| **NGT** | NGT Act, 2010 |
| **CGWA** | **Section 3(3)** of EPA |
| **CPCB** | Water Act, 1974 |
- **Eco-Sensitive Zones** (under EPA): activities **regulated, not necessarily prohibited**.
### 2. RULES 1989 – COMMITTEES AND FIELD TRIALS
- Six competent authorities: **RDAC, IBSC, RCGM, GEAC, SBCC, DLC**; plus rDNA Guidelines 2017.
| Committee | Level | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **RDAC** | DBT | Advisory on rDNA policy |
| **IBSC** | **Each institution (first tier)** | Lab-level biosafety; now clears **genome-edited crops** (Dec 2025) |
| **RCGM** | **DBT** | Monitors ongoing research; **advises GEAC** (does not approve release) |
| **GEAC** | **MoEFCC** | Environmental release, commercial use |
| **SBCC** | **State** | **Inspect, investigate, punitive action** |
| **DLC** | **District** | Field-level monitoring |
- Sequence: **IBSC → RCGM → GEAC → SBCC → DLC**.
- Wider framework: **Biological Diversity Act 2002**; **National Biosafety Framework 2009**.
- Field trials: **Biosafety Research Level (BRL-I, BRL-II)** trials at universities/ICAR plots; commercial clearance only if superior without ecological harm.
- **State NOC** mandatory for field trials (since 2010); several States refuse.
### 3. GM CROPS – TRAIT AND SOURCE
| GM crop | Trait | Gene / developer |
|---|---|---|
| **Bt cotton** | **Insect (bollworm) resistance** | Cry1Ac (Bollgard I); **Cry1Ac + Cry2Ab** (Bollgard II) from ***Bacillus thuringiensis*** |
| **Bt brinjal** | Resistance to **fruit and shoot borer** (*Leucinodes orbonalis*) | **Cry1Ac**; MAHYCO + UAS Dharwad + TNAU |
| **GM mustard DMH-11** | Hybrid seed, higher yield | **Barnase-barstar** from ***Bacillus amyloliquefaciens***; **Delhi University**, **Deepak Pental** |
| **Golden Rice** | **Beta-carotene** (Vitamin A precursor) | Transgenic *Oryza sativa* |
| **Flavr Savr tomato** | **Delayed ripening** – first commercial GM food (USA, 1994) | Antisense polygalacturonase |
!TRAP The Bt gene comes from a soil bacterium, NOT a soil fungus.
### 4. GM CROPS APPROVED AND THE TIMELINE
- **Bt cotton**: approved **March 2002**, three **MAHYCO** hybrids, for **three years**; the **only GM crop** commercially cultivated; leading State **Maharashtra**.
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **2002** | Bt cotton approved |
| 2006 | **PIL** against GM crops in Supreme Court |
| 2009 | GEAC recommends Bt brinjal |
| **2010** | **Jairam Ramesh** moratorium on Bt brinjal; State NOC mandatory |
| 2012 | Parliamentary Standing Committee (37th report): end GM field trials |
| 2013 | SC-appointed **Technical Expert Committee**: **10-year moratorium** on trials |
| 2014 | GEAC clears trials of **11 crops and 21 varieties** |
| 2017 | GEAC cleared DMH-11; Ministry sent it back for more study |
| **2022** | GEAC clears DMH-11 **environmental release** (18 Oct 2022) |
| **July 2024** | **SC split verdict** on DMH-11; both judges direct Centre to frame a **national GM crop policy** |
- **Maharashtra and Haryana**: farmers defied rules and grew **unapproved Bt cotton and Bt brinjal**, protesting delays.
### 5. THE GM DEBATE
- **Main environmental concern = cross-pollination / gene flow** to non-GM crops (APPSC G2 2023).
- **Genetic modification** flagged for **biodiversity** concerns (APPSC GS 2023).
| Risk type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Environment | Insect resistance build-up, harm to **non-target organisms**, **weediness**, **genetic homogenisation** |
| Health | **Toxicity, allergenicity, antibiotic resistance** |
| NOT a GM risk | **Ozone depletion** |
- Resistance: safety fears, monoculture, **patent-holding seed companies**, herbicide tolerance (DMH-11 carries the **bar gene**); public resistance to **Bt brinjal** (PYQ).
- Delays: overlapping **DBT, GEAC, CDSCO, FSSAI, NBA** → **7-10 years** for approval vs **3-5 globally**; **no biosafety liability law**.
### 6. GENOME-EDITED CROPS
- **SDN-1 and SDN-2**: small edits, **no foreign DNA** → **exempt from GMO rules** (2022 guidelines); need only **IBSC approval**, skip GEAC.
- **SDN-3**: transgenic → full GM control.
| Variety | Developer | Trait |
|---|---|---|
| **Pusa Rice DST1** | **ICAR-IARI** | Drought + salinity; edited **MTU1010**; **DST gene knockout (SDN-1)**; 10-30% more yield under stress |
| **Kamala (DRR Dhan 100)** | **ICAR-IIRR, Hyderabad** | SDN-1; higher yield, early maturity |
- Pusa DST1: India's first genome-edited rice for commercial launch (1 Sep 2026); regulated **like a conventional crop**.
### 7. GM FOOD – IMPORT AND LABELLING
- Basis: **Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006**; **FSSAI GM food framework 2024**.
- **Import of GM food without competent-authority approval is prohibited**.
- From **1 March 2021**: imported major food crops need a **"non-GM cum GM-free" certificate**.
- Draft labelling rule: label food with **1% or more** GM ingredient (EU: 0.9%).
- **India does import US apples**: after the **June 2023** WTO settlement, India lifted extra duties on apples, walnuts, almonds (UPSC 2024).

### 9. IPR IN BIOTECHNOLOGY
| Provision / case | Point |
|---|---|
| **Patents Act Sec 3(j)** | **Plants, animals, seeds, essentially biological processes** not patentable; **microbial processes** patentable |
| **Sec 3(d)** | New form of known substance needs enhanced efficacy → blocks **evergreening** |
| **Novartis v Union of India (2013)** | SC upheld rejection of **Glivec (imatinib)** patent |
| **PPVFR Act 2001** | Protects plant varieties and **farmers' rights** |
| **PepsiCo potato (2019)** | **FL-2027 (FC5)**; sued Gujarat farmers, withdrew; registration **revoked 2021** |
- UPSC 2019: a **biological process to create a seed cannot be patented**.
- Farmers may **save, use, sow, resow, exchange, share, sell** seed – **not branded seed** of protected varieties.
#### 9.1 Terminator Seeds (GURT)
- **GURT** yields **sterile seeds** → buy fresh seed each season.
- **PPVFR Act bars registration** of GURT varieties.
- Objections: **dependency on companies**, recurring cost, loss of seed-saving.
### 10. BIOPIRACY AND TKDL
| Case | Product | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Turmeric** | Wound healing (US patent 1995) | Revoked after **CSIR** challenge |
| **Neem** | Anti-fungal | India won at the **European Patent Office** |
| **Basmati** | **RiceTec** (USA) patent, 1997 | CSIR challenge; key claims disallowed |
| **Hoodia** | Appetite suppressant (P57) | Benefit-sharing with **San** people (2003) |
- **TKDL** (2001): by **CSIR and Ministry of AYUSH**; gives patent offices **prior-art** evidence of traditional knowledge.
### 11. BIOSAFETY, BIOSECURITY AND BIOETHICS
- **Cartagena Protocol on Biosafety**: supplementary to the **CBD**; Cartagena is in **Colombia**.
| Level | Agents |
|---|---|
| BSL-1 | Not known to cause disease |
| **BSL-2** | Moderate risk |
| **BSL-3** | Serious airborne (e.g. **TB**) |
| **BSL-4** | **Lethal, no cure** (e.g. **Ebola**); NIV Pune |
- **BWC**: opened **1972**, in force **1975**; **India a Party**.
- **WMD Act, 2005**: bans biological weapons.
- Gap: **no unified national biosecurity framework**; laws lag synthetic biology and gene editing.
- **National Guidelines for Stem Cell Research, 2017** – **ICMR and DBT**.
- **He Jiankui** (China, 2018): **"CRISPR babies"**.
- **"Designer babies"**: selecting or modifying traits in offspring; also germline editing, **three-parent baby**, **informed consent**.
