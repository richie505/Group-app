## U0636 | ANDHRA PRADESH – COASTLINE, DELTAS, HILL RANGES (EASTERN GHATS, NALLAMALA, SESHACHALAM)
### 1. AREA, POSITION AND BORDERS
- **Area: about 1,62,970 sq km** (~4.96% of India); **7th largest State**.
- **1,60,205 sq km** = area left after the 2014 bifurcation, **before the 7 Polavaram mandals** were added.
- **Extent: 12°41' N to 19°07' N** and **77° E to 84°40' E**.
- **82.5° E meridian (Indian Standard Time)** passes through **Kakinada**.
| Direction | Border |
|---|---|
| North | **Odisha, Chhattisgarh** |
| West | **Telangana, Karnataka** |
| South | **Tamil Nadu** |
| East | **Bay of Bengal** |
- **Yanam** (Puducherry, 30 sq km) is an enclave in the Godavari delta.
!KEY IST meridian 82.5° E passes through Kakinada (AP).
### 2. COASTLINE – LENGTH, RANK AND END POINTS
#### 2.1 Recalculated Length
- **Recalculated AP coastline: 1,053.07 km** (2023-24 remeasurement; Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, 2025; APPSC GS 2025 key).
- **Rank: 3rd longest** after **Gujarat** and **Tamil Nadu (1,068.69 km)**; still the **longest on the east coast**.
- Earlier figure: **974 km**, taught as **2nd** (some APSDMA material still says 2nd).
- **India's coastline: 7,516.6 km → 11,098.81 km** in the same exercise – due to **GIS retracing at 1:250,000 scale**, not physical change.
!TRAP The 2025 paper offered both 1,068.69 km and 1,053.07 km: 1,068.69 is Tamil Nadu's figure. "2nd longest" is the pre-2025 answer.
#### 2.2 End Points and Districts
| Point | District / fact |
|---|---|
| North end | **Srikakulam** – **longest district coast, 193 km** |
| South end | **Tirupati/Nellore** at **Pulicat** |
| Shortest district coast | **West Godavari (23 km)** |
| Coastal districts | **12** districts touch the coast (after 2022) |
#### 2.3 Coastal Erosion
- **NCCR (18 Aug 2026)**: **38% of the east coast** eroding vs **30% of the west**; **Uppada (Kakinada)** = worst erosion spot.
### 3. PHYSIOGRAPHIC REGIONS OF AP
- Order **east to west**: **Coastal plains – Eastern Ghats – Western plateau**.
| Region | Districts | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Coastal plains** | Srikakulam to Nellore/Tirupati (18 coastal-Andhra districts) | Alluvial deltas; "**Rice Bowl**" |
| **Eastern Ghats** (hill region) | ASR, Parvathipuram Manyam, Nandyal, Prakasam, Kadapa, Tirupati hills | 750–1,680 m; forests, tribal belts |
| **Western plateau (Rayalaseema)** | Kurnool, Anantapur, **Sri Sathya Sai** (plateau); Chittoor, Kadapa, Annamayya (Mysore plateau extension) | **150–600 m**, crystalline rocks, **red soils**, drought-prone |
### 4. COASTAL PLAIN – TERMS, SHORELINE AND MANGROVES
#### 4.1 Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Eastern Coastal Plain** | **Emergent**, broad coast built by **river deposition**, with large deltas – the AP coast belongs here |
| **Northern Circars** ("Circar coast") | AP coast **north of the Krishna** |
| **Coromandel Coast** | Coast **south of the Krishna** |
- Dimensions: length **~950 km**, from **Mahendragiri** (north) to **Pulicat Lake** (south); average width **~60 km** (narrow in far north and south); elevation **up to 150 m**.
- Coastal plains run from old **Srikakulam to Nellore** (not just to Kakinada).
- Shoreline: **beach ridges** and coastal sand belts.
- Eastern Ghats form **sea-nack-like projections** into the sea; **Yarada Hills (Dolphin's Nose, 335 m)** on the Visakhapatnam coast shield **Visakhapatnam Port** from the open sea.
!PAIR North of Krishna | Northern Circars | AP north coast || South of Krishna | Coromandel | Nellore–TN coast
#### 4.2 Mangrove Fringes

| **Krishna WLS** | **Krishna estuary** mangroves |

### 5. DELTAS
| Delta | Districts | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Godavari delta = Konaseema** | Konaseema, East and West Godavari, Kakinada | Central delta ~150 km wide; **coconut and banana** |

### 6. COASTAL LAKES, LAGOONS AND BARRIER ISLANDS
#### 6.1 Kolleru vs Pulicat
| Feature | Kolleru | Pulicat |
|---|---|---|
| Type | **Freshwater lake** – largest in AP | **Brackish (saltwater) lagoon** – **India's 2nd largest after Chilika** |
| District | **Eluru / West Godavari and Krishna**, between Godavari and Krishna deltas | **Tirupati** (earlier **Nellore**); **shared with Tamil Nadu** |
| Special | AP's **Ramsar site (2002)**; **Tammileru** drains into it; Atapaka bird sanctuary | Cut off from the Bay by **Sriharikota island** (Satish Dhawan Space Centre); **Buckingham Canal** passes through it |
| Birds | Painted storks, grey pelicans, glossy ibises | **Greater flamingos** (not Kolleru), pintails, gulls, terns |
| Threat | **Aquaculture encroachment** | ISRO launches, thermal plants, sand mining |
- Glacial erosion by Himalayan ice fields is **not** a threat to AP coastal lakes.

### 7. EASTERN GHATS – CHARACTER, ROCKS AND LIMITS
#### 7.1 Character
- Lie between the **eastern Deccan Plateau** and the **Coastal Plain**; the major hill system of AP.
- **Discontinuous, rugged, highly eroded** hills – not a continuous chain; cut by the **Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri** (APPSC GS 2025 key).
- **Older and lower** than the Western Ghats.
- **Rivers rising here: Vamsadhara, Nagavali, Sabari.**
#### 7.2 Sections in AP
| Section | Name | Located in |
|---|---|---|
| Northern | **Eastern Ranges** | North AP |
| Southern | **Kadapa Ranges** | South AP |
- The **Godavari and Krishna** split the AP Ghats into these two parts.
#### 7.3 Rocks and Bauxite
- Mainly **Archaean khondalite and charnockite**, over **2.5 billion years** old.
- **Khondalite** hills (not granite) = **highest bauxite-bearing** mountains; mainly in erstwhile Visakhapatnam and East Godavari.
- **AP's bauxite reserves lie mainly in the Eastern Ghats.**
#### 7.4 Northern and Southern Limits
- Begin with **Mahendragiri** in the north; beyond AP run through **Javadi** and **Shevaroy** hills → **join the Western Ghats near the Nilgiris** (Gudalur), via **Biligirirangan** and Male Mahadeshwara hills.
- **Tirumala, Kalvarayan, Garhjat, Shevaroy** hills = all part of the Eastern Ghats.
- North to south: **Nallamalai – Javadi – Nilgiri – Anaimalai**.
| Hills outside AP | Location |
|---|---|
| Shevaroy (Yercaud) | **Salem**, Tamil Nadu |
| Javadi (Jawadhu) | **Vellore–Tiruvannamalai**, TN |
| Kolli Malai | **Tamil Nadu** (not Karnataka) |
| Nandi Hills | Karnataka |
| Biligirirangan | South-east Karnataka |
### 8. EASTERN GHATS RANGES → LOCAL NAMES AND DISTRICTS
| Range (local name) | District(s) | Key feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Mahendragiri (Mahendra Girulu)** | **Srikakulam**, running into Odisha | Local name of the Ghats in Srikakulam (APPSC G2 2025) |
| **Papikondalu** | **Godavari districts** (now ASR–Eluru side; not Krishna) | **Godavari gorge**; **Papikonda NP** (1,012.86 sq km; WLS 1978, NP 2008); average height 915 m |
| **Nallamala** | Kurnool, Nandyal, Prakasam, Guntur, Kadapa (also Telangana) | **Largest continuous range and forest**, between the **Krishna and Penna**; parallel to Coromandel Coast; **Srisailam on its flat top**; **NSTR**; **Chenchu** PVTG (ITDA Srisailam) |
| **Erramala** | **Kurnool–Nandyal** | Water divide: **Krishna (north) / Penna (south)** |
| **Seshachalam** | **YSR Kadapa, Chittoor/Tirupati** (APPSC GS 2024) | **Tirumala hills**; **Red Sanders** (endemic); **Seshachalam Biosphere Reserve** |
| **Palakonda** | YSR Kadapa–Tirupati | Tirupati pilgrim centre in this range |
| **Velikonda** | **SPSR Nellore–Tirupati** (Prakasam side) | **Easternmost** of the southern ranges |
| **Simhachalam, Yarada** | Visakhapatnam | Coastal hills |
- **Tirupati** lies on the **Seshachalam range** (Eastern Ghats).
- **Ganjikunta pass** links Seshachalam and Nallamala; the pass zone runs **more than 300 km** northwards; dry deciduous forests.
!TRAP Seshachalam belongs to the **Eastern Ghats**, NOT the Nilgiri Hills (APPSC GS 2025 key).
#### 8.1 Wildlife and Tribes
- **NSTR** (est. **1983**, ~**3,728 sq km**; Telangana part = Amrabad TR) in the Nallamala = **India's largest tiger reserve** (Jul 2026).
- **Gaur** to be moved from Central India to NSTR from **Sep 2026** (WII support); two tigresses each from Maharashtra (Chandrapur) and MP. Push for a unified **Eastern Ghats Wildlife Sanctuary**.

### 9. HIGHEST PEAKS
| Peak | Height | Location |
|---|---|---|
| **Arma Konda (Aroma / Sitamma Konda)** | **1,680 m** | **Chintapalli, ASR district** – **highest peak of AP and of the Eastern Ghats** |
| Jindhagada | 1,690 m (printed) | Near Araku; often treated as the same summit |
| **Galikonda** | 1,643 m | Northern Ghats |
| **Sintram (Sinkram) Gutta** | 1,620 m | Northern Ghats |
| **Mahendragiri** | 1,501 m | **Gajapati, Odisha** (on AP border) – NOT the highest |
| Dolphin's Nose | 335 m | Visakhapatnam – lowest hills merge here |
- Maximum height of Eastern Ghats in AP: **Chintapalli (Paderu) area**, ASR district.
- Arma Konda, Gali Konda, Sinkram Gutta = **peaks of the Eastern Ghats**.
!TRAP Mahendragiri (1,501 m) is lower than Arma Konda – follow the heights.

7. Mahendragiri (1,501 m) is in Odisha and is not the highest peak.
