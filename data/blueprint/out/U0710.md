### 2. MOBILE GENERATIONS 1G–6G
| Generation | Core technology / feature |
|---|---|
| 1G | Analog, FDMA |
| 2G | Digital; GSM (TDMA) and CDMA; SMS |
| 2.5G / 2.75G | **GPRS** / **EDGE** (faster GSM data) |
| 3G | Mobile data; **CDMA-based** (WCDMA/CDMA2000) |
| 4G | **LTE**, OFDMA; **VoLTE** = voice over LTE data network |
| 5G | OFDMA; latency as low as **1 ms** |
| 6G | **Early 2030s**; "omnipresent wireless intelligence" |
- **WiMAX** = World Wide Interoperability for Microwave Access (**IEEE 802.16**); wireless data over long distances.
- **WLL** = Wireless Local Loop; radio link replacing the copper last mile.
- TRAI prefers **C-V2X** for vehicle communication.
### 3. 5G – SPECTRUM, SLICING, STACK, LABS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **sub-6 GHz** (low/mid) | Wider coverage, better penetration |
| **mmWave** | Very high data rates, short range |
| **Network slicing** | One network split into virtual slices |
| **5G Standalone (SA)** | No 4G core → lower latency, better slicing |
- 5G enables **IoT, edge computing, network slicing**, AR/VR, remote surgery.
- Indigenous **Open-RAN 5G**: **Airtel–Tata** partnership; **TTDF** (2022) gets **5% of Digital Bharat Nidhi** (~Rs 500 cr/yr), funds India 5G Stack, 6G, quantum encryption.
- **5G & Beyond Hackathon 2023**: run by **DoT (not DST)**; open to individuals, students, start-ups, academics (APPSC 2022).
- **AP**: **IIITDM Kurnool** ranked 1st among **100 5G Use Case Labs** in DoT's Jan–Jun 2026 gradation, in all four categories.
### 4. 6G ROADMAP
- **Bharat 6G Vision** (**March 2023**): India to lead design, development and deployment of 6G by **2030**.
- **Bharat 6G Alliance (B6GA)**: launched 2023; seven Working Groups.
### 5. SPECTRUM AND THE TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT 2023
- **Telecommunications Act 2023**: licensing → **authorisation**; **First Schedule**: terrestrial spectrum **auctioned**, satellite spectrum **assigned administratively**.
- **TRAI (Feb 2026)**: auction all spectrum across **nine bands**; uniform **35%** cap; new-entrant net worth **Rs 100 cr → Rs 50 cr** per service area.
- **Supreme Court (14 Feb 2026, Aircel case)**: telcos **do not own spectrum**; IBC cannot rework spectrum rights.
### 6. SATELLITE COMMUNICATION – GEO VS LEO
| | GEO | LEO |
|---|---|---|
| Altitude | ~**35,786 km**; fixed over one spot | Below ~**2,000 km** |
| Latency | Higher | Low |
| Examples | **GSAT** series | **Starlink**, Eutelsat **OneWeb**, Amazon **Kuiper** |
- **Starlink** (8,000+ LEO satellites): **GMPCS** licence in India in **2025**; OneWeb (Bharti) earlier got a letter of intent.
- **GMPCS** = Global Mobile Personal Communication by Satellite (DoT licence); conditions: geofencing, no cross-border spillover, lawful surveillance, terminals **made in India within 5 years**.
- **ITU**: orbital slots first-come, first-served; de-orbit within **25 years** of mission end.
### 7. OPTICAL FIBRE
- Works on **total internal reflection (TIR)**; signals travel as **pulses of light**; thin strand of glass (silica) or plastic.
- **Fastest** medium vs copper, radio, satellite (APPSC 2025); main use: communication; very low power, free from radio-frequency interference.
- **Single-mode** = long distances; **multi-mode** = short runs.
- **TIR**: denser → rarer medium, angle of incidence > critical angle; refractive index **Diamond > Glass > Water > Air**. Uses: **endoscope**, glowing **glass beads** on traffic signals.
#### 7.1 Undersea Cables and National Fibre
- Undersea network ~**1.5 million km**; carries ~**99%** of international traffic; cheaper, lower latency than satellites.
- **CANI** (Chennai–Andaman and Nicobar Islands): ~2,300 km; inaugurated **10 Aug 2020**; by BSNL; links Port Blair and seven other islands.
- **NOFN**: all **2,50,000 Gram Panchayats**; funded by **USOF (not NIIF)**; GPON technology.
- PM Gati Shakti NMP (2026): ~13.5 lakh route km OFC; 8.26 lakh towers; **PM-WANI 3.15 lakh** hotspots.
### 8. ELECTROMAGNETIC WAVES IN COMMUNICATION
| Wave | Wavelength | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Radio | > 1 m (longest) | Radio, TV, mobile, Wi-Fi, MRI |
| Microwave | 1 mm–1 m (300 MHz–300 GHz) | Satellite, radar, 5G, point-to-point links |
| Infrared | 700 nm–1 mm | **Night vision**, **TV remotes**, muscle-pain therapy |
| Visible | ~400–700 nm | Li-Fi |
| UV / X-rays / Gamma | 10–400 / 0.01–10 / < 0.01 nm | Ionising; not for communication |
- **Energy rises** Radio < Microwave < IR < Visible < UV < X-ray < Gamma; photon energy ∝ frequency (200–280 nm UV highest among options).
- **Decreasing frequency**: Gamma > X-rays > UV > Radio.
- Visible: violet shortest, **red longest** (least scattering, used in signals); blue highest energy among colours.
- **Wavelength** = distance between two successive crests or troughs.
- **UHF** (300 MHz–3 GHz) travels by **space waves**.
- **Supersonic waves are not electromagnetic**; radio waves are not bent by electric/magnetic fields.
- Dish TV lost in rain: raindrops absorb and scatter energy.
!TRAP Common TV remote = infrared; one exam key marked radio waves.
### 9. LI-FI, WI-FI AND ALTERNATIVE ACCESS
| Li-Fi (Light Fidelity) | Wi-Fi |
|---|---|
| **Visible light from LEDs**; term by **Harald Haas (2011)** | Radio waves |
| More bandwidth, efficiency, security | Longer range; wireless LANs |
| Does not pass through walls | Passes through walls |
- **VLC**: ~375–780 nm; not long-range.
- **Wi-Fi 6 = 802.11ax; Wi-Fi 7 = 802.11be**; Wi-Fi uses 2.4 or 5 GHz.
- **2.4–2.5 GHz ISM band** (licence-free): Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, cordless phones, microwave ovens.
- **Bluetooth**: 2.4 GHz; developed **1994** by **Jaap Haartsen**. **NFC**: a few cm.
- **RFID**: radio tags read wirelessly (Kavach track tags).
- **FSO**: data by laser beams without fibre; world's first 2 Tbit/s FSO link with small terminals (2026).
- **Project Loon** (Google/Alphabet): stratospheric helium balloons for internet; shut **January 2021**.
- **Mumbai Central**: first station with Google free public Wi-Fi (**Jan 2016**).
- India **49th** in Network Readiness Index 2024 (from 60th).
