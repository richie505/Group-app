## U0233 | CHAMPARAN, AHMEDABAD MILL AND KHEDA SATYAGRAHAS; ROWLATT SATYAGRAHA AND JALLIANWALA BAGH
### 1. GANDHI BEFORE 1917 – SOUTH AFRICA AND RETURN
#### 1.1 South Africa and Return
- To **South Africa in 1893**; **Phoenix Settlement (1904, near Durban)**; **Tolstoy Farm (1910, near Johannesburg)**; newspaper **Indian Opinion**.
- Influences: **Tolstoy**, **Ruskin's Unto This Last**, **Thoreau**; political mentor **Gopal Krishna Gokhale**.
- Returned **9 January 1915** → now **Pravasi Bharatiya Divas**; spent a year touring India without political positions (Gokhale's advice).
- **First major public speech in India: BHU, February 1916**.
- **First to start Satyagraha** in the freedom struggle = **Mahatma Gandhi**; **Satyagraha = Satya (truth) + Agraha (insistence)** = "insistence on truth".
#### 1.2 Journals, Writings and Ashrams
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Journals | **Indian Opinion** (South Africa), **Young India**, **Navajivan**, **Harijan** |
| Writings | **Hind Swaraj**, autobiography (**The Story of My Experiments with Truth**) |
| Ashrams in order | **Satyagraha Ashram, Kochrab (Ahmedabad), 1915** → **Sabarmati (1917)** → **Sevagram, Wardha (1936)** |
!TRAP **Mathrubhumi** was NOT Gandhi's journal (APPSC 2019).
#### 1.3 Titles
- **"Mahatma"** – **Rabindranath Tagore** (prefix added during the Champaran Satyagraha; APPSC 2019 accepted "given in 1914").
- **"Father of the Nation"** – **Subhas Chandra Bose**.
- APPSC 2019 also accepted: Gandhi was **absent** at Nehru's **"Tryst with Destiny"** speech.
### 2. THE THREE EARLY SATYAGRAHAS – AT A GLANCE
| Campaign | Year, place | Issue | Result | FIRST |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Champaran** | **1917, Bihar** | **Tinkathia** indigo; illegal dues | **25% refund**; Tinkathia abolished | First **civil disobedience** / first satyagraha in India |
| **Ahmedabad Mill Strike** | **Feb-Mar 1918, Gujarat** | **Plague bonus**; workers asked **50%**, owners offered **20%** | Tribunal gave **35%** | First **hunger strike** |
| **Kheda** | **1918 (Mar-Jun), Gujarat** | Crop failure; **revenue remission refused** | Revenue only from those **able to pay** | First **non-cooperation** |
!KEY Champaran = first satyagraha; Ahmedabad = first fast; Kheda = first non-cooperation.
- Gandhi **first used a hunger strike as a weapon** in the **Ahmedabad Mill Strike (1918)**.
- All three took place under **Viceroy Lord Chelmsford (1916-1921)**.
### 3. CHAMPARAN SATYAGRAHA (1917, BIHAR)
#### 3.1 The Indigo System
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Tinkathia / Teen Kathiya** | Tenants bound to grow **indigo on 3/20th** of their land for **European planters** |
| **Sharahbeshi** | **Enhanced rent** |
| **Tawan** | **Lump-sum compensation** demanded when indigo lost value |
- Agreements made by European planters from the **early 19th century**.
- **German synthetic dyes** made indigo unprofitable in the world market by the early 20th century → planters **raised** taxes and imposed **illegal exactions** (they did not reduce them).
- Struggle was against **forced indigo cultivation and illegal exactions** (not forest tax); for solving the problems of **indigo workers**.
- Precursor: **Bengal Indigo Revolt of 1859** – Champaran came nearly 60 years later as Gandhi's "launch pad" (**14 Jan 2026** article).
#### 3.2 Sequence of Events
1. **Rajkumar Shukla**, a local **indigo cultivator**, met Gandhi at the **Lucknow Congress, 1916** and persuaded him to come.
2. Gandhi reached Champaran on **10 April 1917** with **Brajkishore Prasad, Rajendra Prasad, Anugrah Narayan Sinha, Acharya Kripalani**.
3. Defied the order to leave the district (**Motihari**).
4. Toured villages with lawyers, **recording peasants' complaints** → government ordered an inquiry.
5. **Champaran Agrarian Committee** appointed; Gandhi a member.
6. **Champaran Agrarian Act, 1918**: **Tinkathia abolished**; **25% refund** of illegal charges.
#### 3.3 Persons and Professions
| Person | Role / profession |
|---|---|
| **Rajkumar Shukla** | Local indigo cultivator (agriculturist); **invited** Gandhi |
| **Rajendra Prasad, Brajkishore Prasad, Anugrah Narayan Sinha** | **Lawyers (vakils)** of Bihar |
| **J.B. Kripalani** | **Teacher** – history professor at **Muzaffarpur College**, hence "Acharya"; met Gandhi in 1917 at Champaran |
| **Mahadev Desai** | Gandhi's **secretary**; joined the Champaran work in 1917 (not a planters' leader) |
| **F.G. Sly** | Chairman, Champaran Agrarian Committee |
- **"Mahatma Gandhi and Bihar, Some Reminiscences"** – **Dr. Rajendra Prasad**.
- **N.G. Ranga** opposed the Champaran Satyagraha.
!TRAP Inviter = **Rajkumar Shukla**, not Rajendra Prasad. **Ram Manohar Lohia** and **Jai Prakash Narayan** – not connected with Champaran.
#### 3.4 Champaran Agrarian (Enquiry) Committee, 1917
- Appointed by the **Bihar and Orissa government** – by **agreement between the Government and Gandhiji**.
- **Chairman: F.G. Sly**; **members: D.J. Reid, Gandhi (representing the ryots), L.C. Adami, Raja Harihar Prasad Narayan Singh, G. Rainy, M.E.L. Tainer**.
!TRAP **Anugrah Narayan Sinha** was NOT a member of the committee.
#### 3.5 Exam Points
- Champaran is in **Bihar, not Gujarat** (APPSC 2019); year **1917, not 1915**.
- First satyagraha on Indian soil; **first farmers' movement**; first successful satyagraha after South Africa.
- Key feature: **drastic decrease in cultivation of plantation crops** (indigo).
- Champaran was **not** Gandhi's first national movement – it was localised; first all-India one = **Rowlatt Satyagraha (1919)**.
### 4. AHMEDABAD MILL STRIKE (1918)
- **Feb-Mar 1918**, Gujarat; mill owners **withdrew the "plague bonus"**.
- Workers demanded **50%** wage rise (wartime inflation); owners offered **20%**.
- Workers approached **Anusuya Sarabhai** → she appealed to Gandhi; her brother **Ambalal Sarabhai** led the **mill owners**.
- Gandhi advised **35%**, went on **fast** (first hunger strike in India); strike lasted **21 days (not 35)**; tribunal awarded **35%**.
- **Ahmedabad Textile Labour Association (Majoor Mahajan Sangh)** – founded by **Gandhi with Anusuya Sarabhai**, **25 February 1920**; worked on arbitration and non-violence.
!PAIR Anusuya Sarabhai | Workers' side | Co-founded TLA || Ambalal Sarabhai | Owners' side | Her brother
### 5. KHEDA SATYAGRAHA (1918)
- **Kheda district, Gujarat**, **1918**.
- Grievance: **crop failure (drought / famine)**; peasants sought **remission / suspension of land revenue**; government refused; also called a struggle **against high taxes**.
- Peasants refused to pay despite threats of land confiscation.
- **Gandhi – spiritual head**; organisers **Vallabhbhai Patel** (field commander), **Mohanlal Pandya, Narahari Parikh**.
- Began the **Gandhi-Patel alliance**.
- **Called off** when the government agreed to collect revenue **only from those able to pay**, suspend it for poor peasants and return confiscated property (APPSC 2023).
### 6. SIGNIFICANCE
- **1917-1919** established Gandhi as a **national leader** and Satyagraha as the method of struggle.
- Connected Gandhi with **peasants** (Champaran, Kheda) and **labour** (Ahmedabad).
- Recruited leaders: **Rajendra Prasad, Kripalani, Mahadev Desai, Vallabhbhai Patel**.
- **AP angle**: Champaran (1917) and Kheda (1918) inspired **Andhra peasant movements**.
- **Feb 2026**: a Gandhi profile repeated the triad "first civil disobedience / first hunger strike / first non-cooperation".
### 7. ROWLATT ACT AND ROWLATT SATYAGRAHA (1919)
- **Rowlatt Act** = **Anarchical and Revolutionary Crimes Act, 1919**: political suspects could be held **up to two years without trial**; trials by **three High Court judges** with **no jury and no appeal**; in effect suspended **habeas corpus**.
- Gandhi organised the **Satyagraha Sabha** (**Bombay, 24 February 1919**); members took an oath to disobey the Act.
- **6 April 1919**: nationwide **hartal**; peaceful in **Vizag, Rajahmundry, Guntur, Vijayawada, Nellore**.
- **First venture in all-India politics** – Rowlatt Satyagraha; Champaran, Kheda and Ahmedabad were **localised**.
### 8. JALLIANWALA BAGH MASSACRE (13 APRIL 1919)
#### 8.1 The Incident
- **Amritsar, Punjab**; **13 April 1919**; crowd gathered on **Baisakhi**, during Rowlatt protests.
- Protest against the arrest of **Dr Satyapal** and **Dr Saifuddin Kitchlew** (arrested **9 April 1919**; symbols of Hindu-Muslim unity) and the Rowlatt Act.
- **10 April**: troops fired on a crowd demanding their release.
- Lt. Governor **Sir Michael O'Dwyer** handed Amritsar to **Brig.-Gen. Reginald Dyer**, who banned assemblies.
- Dyer **blocked the only exit**, fired **without warning** for about **10 minutes**, **1,650 rounds**.
| Office | Person |
|---|---|
| Viceroy | **Lord Chelmsford** |
| Lt. Governor of Punjab | **Michael O'Dwyer** |
| Ordered firing | **Brig.-Gen. Reginald Dyer** |
#### 8.2 Casualties and Aftermath
- **Official: 379 dead, 1,100 injured**; **Congress estimate: over 1,000** dead.
- Martial law, **crawling order**, floggings.
- **Hunter Committee** condemned Dyer; the **House of Lords backed him**.
- **Udham Singh** killed **Sir Michael O'Dwyer** in London.
#### 8.3 Who Gave Up What
| Protester | Gave up |
|---|---|
| **Rabindranath Tagore** | **Knighthood** |
| **Gandhi** | **Kaiser-i-Hind** medal (awarded 1915; surrendered during Non-Cooperation) |
| **Sir C. Sankaran Nair** | Resigned from the **Viceroy's Executive Council** |
- **Apr 2025**: PM recalled Sir C. Sankaran Nair on the **106th anniversary** of Jallianwala Bagh.
!TRAP O'Dwyer = Lt. Governor; Dyer = Brigadier-General who fired. Udham Singh killed O'Dwyer, not Dyer.
### 9. FIRST ARREST AND SEDITION TRIAL OF GANDHI (1922)
- Arrested at his ashram on **10 March 1922**; tried for **sedition** at **Ahmedabad** on **18 March 1922** – **"The Great Trial"**.
- Charge based on three **Young India** articles: "Tampering with Loyalty", "The Puzzle and its Solution", "Shaking the Manes".
- Gandhi **pleaded guilty**; Judge **Broomfield** sentenced him to **six years** (following Tilak's case); printer **Shankerlal Banker** got one year.
