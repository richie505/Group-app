### 2. COLONIAL SCHOLARS OF TELUGU
#### 2.1 Scholar → Contribution
| Scholar | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Benjamin Schultze** | German Lutheran, **Danish-Halle Mission**; came 1719; Telugu grammar; returned 1743; Telugu books printed at Halle |
| **Colin Mackenzie** (1754-1821) | **First Surveyor General (1815)**; collected **Kaifiyats** with the **Kavali brothers** (Borraiah, Venkataramaiah, Lakshmaiah); reached the **Amaravati Stupa** in 1797 |
| **William Carey** | **Serampore Mission**; complete Telugu Bible (1812-19); Telugu typefaces |
| **A.D. Campbell** | First systematic **Telugu Grammar (1816)** and Telugu-English dictionary |
| **Thomas Munro** | Kept **Telugu as an administrative language** |
| **C.P. Brown** (1798-1884) | **Collector of Cuddapah**; learnt Telugu from Kodandarama Pantulu; **Grammar 1840**; **dictionaries 1853**; ~2,000 **Vemana** poems, **Verses of Vemana (1829)** |
#### 2.2 C.P. Brown
- Edited **Nannaya, Tikkana, Pothana**; rescued palm-leaf texts through missionary presses.
- Preserved Mackenzie's **Kaifiyats** in the Madras Library.
- Called Telugu the **"Italian of the East"** – phrase first used by **Nicolo de Conti**.
- **C.P. Brown Memorial Trust Library = Kadapa (Cuddapah)** (APPSC G2 2001, 2005).
!TRAP First Telugu grammar: Schultze (early) vs **A.D. Campbell** (first systematic, 1816); Brown's Grammar 1840, dictionaries 1853.

### 6. "FATHERS" AND KEY PERSONS
- **Kandukuri Veeresalingam** – **Father of the Telugu Renaissance**; his **Vivekavardhini** (from 1874) attacked Kokkonda's **Sanjeevani**.
- **Kokkonda Venkataratnam** – orthodox; Andhra Bhasha Johnson; Mahamahopadhyaya 1907.
- **Prolaya Nayaka** – led the post-Kakatiya liberation confederacy.
- **Bhakti Movement** – Saint **Ramanuja**; devotion to Vishnu and social equality.
### 7. LITERARY ASSOCIATIONS AND CULTURAL BODIES
#### 7.1 Andhra Sahitya Parishat
- Founded **1911, Madras** → later based at **Kakinada**.
- Sessions at **Rajahmundry**: **23 Apr 1912** and **22 Apr 1913**, presided by **Kokkonda Venkataratnam**.
- Stood for the classical (granthika) side.
!TRAP Parishat founded at **Madras** (1911) → based at **Kakinada**; sessions at **Rajahmundry**.

#### 7.3 Vijnana Chandrika Mandali and Andhra Bhasha Nilayam
| Body | Founders | Work |
|---|---|---|
| **Sri Krishnadevaraya Andhra Bhasha Nilayam** (Hyderabad, 1901) | **Komarraju Lakshmana Rao**, Ravichettu Ranga Rao, Nayani Venkata Ranga Rao | Library for Telugu in Hyderabad |
| **Vijnana Chandrika Mandali** | **Komarraju Lakshmana Rao**, Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao (first editor), Ayyadevara Kaleswara Rao, Shyamji Rama Rao | Publishing agency, **Vijnana Chandrika Granthamala**; Lakshmana Rao later editor |
- Komarraju also published the **Andhra Vijnana Sarvasvam** (Telugu encyclopaedia) from Madras.
#### 7.4 Rajahmundry and Kakinada
- **Rajahmundry** – Veeresalingam's reform base; **cultural capital since Eastern Chalukya** times.
- **Kakinada** – seat of the Andhra Sahitya Parishat.
