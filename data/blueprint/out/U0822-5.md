### 2. IRAN – US STRIKES, 12-DAY WAR AND SNAPBACK
#### 2.1 US Strikes on Nuclear Sites (June 2025)
- The US joined the **Israel–Iran conflict** (reignited June 2025).
| Site | Detail | Weapon |
|---|---|---|
| **Natanz** | ~220 km SE of Tehran; **main enrichment site**; up to 60% enrichment; partly underground | Tomahawk + bunker busters |
| **Fordow** | **Under a mountain near Qom**, ~100 km SW of Tehran; exposed **2009** | **12 GBU-57 MOPs** from **six B-2** bombers |
| **Isfahan** | ~350 km SE of Tehran; **three Chinese-built research reactors** | Tomahawk |
- **GBU-57 MOP** = **30,000-pound conventional bunker buster**.
!TRAP **Fordow** is the underground site; **Natanz** (not Fordow) is the **main enrichment centre**.
#### 2.2 Reactions, Retaliation and Ceasefire
- Iran FM **Abbas Araghchi**: "**grave violation**" of the **UN Charter and NPT**; Israeli PM **Netanyahu**: "**historic**".
- **UN Charter Article 2** bars the use of force.
- Iran struck the US **Al-Udeid Air Base, Qatar** (heart of **CENTCOM**) a day after the US strikes – **with advance notice, no casualties**.
- Trump announced a "**Complete and Total Ceasefire**" – war ended after **12 days**; **Israel's campaign lasted 11 days** (IRGC HQ, air defences, leadership).
- Iran's missile: solid-fuel **Kheybar Shekan**.
- Restraint: **no blockade of Hormuz**; proxies stayed silent.
| Proxy | Country |
|---|---|
| **Houthis** | **Yemen** |
| **Hezbollah** | **Lebanon** – leader **Hassan Nasrallah** killed 2024; now **Naim Qassem** |
| **Hashd al-Shaabi** (Popular Mobilization Forces) | **Iraq** |
- India's stakes: Hormuz ~**40%** of India's oil imports; **8 million+ Indians** in West Asia; India imports **~50%** of crude from West Asia.
- Arab nuclear plans: **UAE runs a nuclear power plant**; **Saudi Arabia** fast-tracking.
#### 2.3 Nuclear Order
- Iran **signed the NPT 1968, ratified 1970**; parliament debating an NPT-exit bill – **no formal withdrawal** (June 2025).
- **Israel is not an NPT signatory** – undeclared arsenal, **no IAEA inspections**.
- **JCPOA 2015**: Iran + **US, UK, France, Russia, China, Germany** (P5+1); **US withdrew 2018 (Trump)**.
#### 2.4 UN Snapback Sanctions (September 2025)
- **UNSC sanctions reimposed** under the **JCPOA snapback** – triggered by the **European parties**.
- Measures: **asset freezes, travel bans, cargo inspections** (incl. oil tankers), **ban on enrichment at any level**, ballistic-missile curbs, **arms embargo**.
- President **Masoud Pezeshkian**: "**unjust and illegal**"; Iran **recalled envoys from France, Germany, UK**; enrichment to **60%**, ~**400 kg HEU**.
- **Russia and China opposed**; China = main buyer of Iranian crude.
- Economy: rial **1,126,000 per USD**; inflation **over 40%**.
### 3. GAZA AND QATAR
#### 3.1 Gaza Declaration – Sharm el-Sheikh
- Signed **13 October 2025**, **Sharm el-Sheikh, Egypt** – by **Donald Trump** with **Egypt, Qatar, Türkiye** as **guarantors**.
- Hamas released the **last 20 surviving hostages**; to return bodies of **27**; Israel released **1,968 prisoners** (incl. 250 security detainees).
- Trump praised **Abdel Fattah El-Sisi**; met **Mahmoud Abbas**; Israeli minister **Bezalel Smotrich** opposed the peace plan.
- Challenges: **Hamas refuses to disarm**; **incomplete Israeli withdrawal**.
- Background: **7 October 2023** Hamas attack – **251 hostages, 1,219 killed**.
- **Abdel Fattah el-Sisi** – won a **third term** as President of Egypt (**December 2023, 89.6%** of the vote); **sworn in again in 2024**.
- India was **absent** from the Gaza peace process (US with Türkiye, Egypt, Qatar).
#### 3.2 US Executive Order on Qatar's Security (September 2025)
- "**Assuring the Security of the State of Qatar**" – signed by **Donald Trump**.
- Trigger: **Israeli airstrikes in Qatar** on **Hamas leaders** (~3 weeks earlier) – Qatar called it "**state terror**"; Netanyahu apologised to Qatar.
- Language **mirrors NATO Article 5**; part of Trump's **20-point Gaza plan**.
- An executive order is **not legally binding like a treaty** – **no Senate approval**; **can be repealed by a future president**.
- Qatar: **Major Non-NATO Ally (MNNA) since 2022**; hosts **Al-Udeid Air Base** – **largest US base in the Middle East**; hosts the **Hamas political office**.
- **May 2025 Trump visit** – **$1.2 trillion** economic agreements; gifted a **$400 million Boeing 747**.
### 4. AFGHANISTAN AND THE AF–PAK CLASHES
#### 4.1 Muttaqi's Visit and the Kabul Embassy
- **Amir Khan Muttaqi**, Taliban **Acting Foreign Minister**, visited India **9–16 October 2025** – **first official visit by a top Taliban leader** since the return to power in **2021**.
- After the Jaishankar–Muttaqi meeting, India upgraded its **technical mission in Kabul** (reopened **June 2022**) to a **full embassy** headed by a **Charge d'Affaires** – **still no formal recognition** of the Taliban regime.
- Muttaqi assured **Afghan soil won't be used against India**; shared concern on **Daesh (ISIS)**; Afghanistan to send diplomats; Taliban allowed to appoint the **Afghan Consul General in Mumbai**.
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1996–2001 | No ties; India backed the **Northern Alliance** |
| 2017 | **Moscow Dialogue** |
| 2020 | Intra-Afghan talks in **Doha** |
| August 2021 | **US withdrawal**; Indian Ambassador to Qatar met the Taliban in Doha |
| June 2022 | Technical mission reopened in Kabul |
| October 2025 | Mission upgraded to embassy |
- India's development investment: **over USD 3 billion since 2001**; trade via **Chabahar**.
- Indian projects (separate projects): **Afghan Parliament building**, **Zaranj–Delaram Highway**, **Salma (India–Afghanistan Friendship) Dam**.
#### 4.2 Durand Line Clashes and Doha Ceasefire
- Clashes along the **Durand Line** during Muttaqi's visit – **Pakistani airstrikes** in Afghanistan; **Afghan forces captured Pakistani posts**; Pakistan's **Operation Ghazab Lil Haq** along the line.
- **Durand Line**: drawn **1893 by the British**; **never recognised by Afghanistan**; divides the **Pashtuns**; ~**2,611 km**.
- **Doha ceasefire (19 October 2025)** – mediated by **Qatar and Türkiye**; both Defence Ministers attended; **Saudi Arabia supported**; joint committee to monitor; follow-up talks in Doha.
- Trigger: Pakistani airstrikes in **Paktika** province on the **Hafiz Gul Bahadur** group after a suicide bombing in **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa**; Afghan **cricket-series boycott**.
- Groups: **TTP** = Tehreek-e-Taliban Pakistan; **PTM** = Pashtun Tahafuz Movement; threats **IS-K**, al-Qaida affiliates.

#### 5.2 Nepal's Gen Z Protests (September 2025)
- Trigger: **4 September 2025** – Ministry of Communications and IT **blocked 26 platforms** (Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram, X, YouTube); **Nepal Telecommunication Authority (NTA)** told to deactivate them.
- Protest began **8 September 2025** near the **Mandala monument, Maitighar, Kathmandu**; Parliament complex stormed; **at least 19 killed, 400+ injured**.
- Coordinated by **Hami Nepal** (non-profit, 2015); organised via **Discord, Instagram**; Gen Z = **40%** of population.
- Slogans against "**nepo babies**"; justice for the **2017 Airbus scandal**.
- Resignations: Home Minister **Ramesh Lekhak**, Communications Minister **Prithvi Subba Gurung**, then **PM K.P. Sharma Oli**.
- Outcome: ban revoked; probe panel to report in **15 days**; **Sushila Karki** PM. India a bystander to Nepal's "Gen Z revolution".
- India youth unemployment (20–24): **35.9%** (CMIE, July 2025).
#### 5.3 Lipulekh – India Rejects Nepal's Objection
- India–China agreed to **resume border trade via Lipulekh Pass (Uttarakhand)**; Nepal objected.
- India (MEA spokesperson **Randhir Jaiswal**): Nepal's claims "**not based on historical facts**", unilateral.
- Nepal's constitutional map places **Kalapani, Limpiyadhura, Lipulekh** east of the **Mahakali**; former PM **K.P. Sharma Oli (CPN-UML)** would "take back" territories through dialogue.
- Trade via Lipulekh began **1954**; disrupted by COVID-19; **Pithoragarh district**, tri-junction **India–Nepal–China (Tibet)**.
- Dispute root: **source of the Mahakali** – India: near **Kalapani**; Nepal: near **Limpiyadhura**; **Sugauli Treaty (1815)**.
#### 5.4 Bangladesh
- **October 2025** rallies in **Chattogram and Rangpur division** for the **$1 billion Chinese Teesta River Comprehensive Management and Restoration Project**.
- Concern: **Siliguri Corridor** (**20–22 km** wide); **Joint Rivers Commission**; **1996 Ganga Water Sharing Treaty expires 2026**.
- **Teesta**: origin **Tso Lhamo Lake, Sikkim**; ~**315 km in India**, **121 km in Bangladesh**; joins the **Brahmaputra (Jamuna)** near **Chilmari**; **2011 draft**: India **42.5%**, Bangladesh **37.5%**.
- **US military activity in Chittagong**: US **C-130J Super Hercules** landed at **Shah Amanat International Airport** (usually based at **Yokota Airbase, Japan**); exercises **Operation Pacific Angel-25**, **Tiger Lightning-2025**; government led by **Muhammad Yunus** (since Aug 2024); **no permanent US base**.
#### 5.5 Sri Lanka – Fisheries and Katchatheevu
- **Katchatheevu**: uninhabited islet in the **Palk Strait** – placed under Sri Lanka by the **1974 India–Sri Lanka Maritime Boundary Treaty**.
- Sri Lanka **banned bottom trawling in 2017**; PM Modi visited **Colombo in April 2025**.
- **UNCLOS Article 123**: cooperation in **semi-enclosed seas** (Palk Bay, Gulf of Mannar).

#### 6.3 EU – New Strategic EU–India Agenda
- **Five priorities**: trade, technology, defence & security, connectivity, multilateral engagement.
- EU = **India's largest trading partner**; FTA target end-2025 → **16th India–EU Summit (January 2026)**: FTA negotiations **concluded** ("mother of all deals"; EU = **India's 22nd FTA partner**); formal signing expected **December 2026** (as of 8 Sep 2026).
- **Security and Defence Partnership** = EU's **3rd in Asia** (after **Japan and South Korea**).
- Mechanisms: **Trade and Technology Council (TTC)**; Security of Information Agreement talks; **European Legal Gateway** for mobility; EU–India Business Forum.
- **Horizon Europe (€93.5 bn)**: India **in talks on joining** – 3rd TTC meeting, **Brussels (July 2026)**; same meeting – pilot linking **DigiLocker** with the **EU Digital Identity Wallet**.
- Connectivity: **IMEC** and **Global Gateway**; irritants: India's **Russian military exercises** and **Russian oil** purchases.
#### 6.4 Israel – Bilateral Investment Treaty
- Signed **8 September 2025, New Delhi** – **Nirmala Sitharaman** and Israeli FM **Bezalel Smotrich**.
- **Israel = first OECD member** with which India signed a BIT under its **new investment-treaty framework**.
- Features: fair and equitable treatment, protection against expropriation, independent arbitration; **no tariff provisions** (that is for an FTA); next – **FTA talks**.
- Trade ~**$4 bn** a year; since 2000 India's ODI in Israel **$443 million**, Israel's FDI in India ~**$334.2 million**.
#### 6.5 US – 100% Pharma Tariff
- **100%** on **branded and patented** drug imports from **1 October 2025** – **exempt if the firm is building a US plant**; **generics excluded**.
- India's US generic exports ~**$20 bn**; India supplies ~**40%** of US generics; US = ~**30%** of India's pharma exports; India exported **$10.5 bn+** formulations to the US in FY25.
- **EU and Japan capped at 15%** – together ~**75%** of US pharma imports; affected: specialty drugs (Danish **Wegovy, Ozempic**); UK, Switzerland, Singapore may face the full 100%.
- Generics = **90% of US prescriptions but 13% of spending**; Ernst & Young: a 25% tariff could add **$51 bn** a year to US drug costs; **APIs** inclusion uncertain – **PhRMA** objected.
- Also: **50%** kitchen cabinets & bathroom vanities; **30%** upholstered furniture; **25%** heavy trucks; US tariffs on some Indian exports – **50%**.
