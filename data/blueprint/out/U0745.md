### 2. INTEGRATED ENERGY POLICY 2006 AND PRICING REFORMS
#### 2.1 Aim and Recommendations
- Aim: meet energy needs of all at **least cost**, reliably and sustainably, till **2031-32**.
- **Market-determined / trade-parity pricing** of tradable fuels.
- **Targeted subsidies to the poor**, not a general price subsidy.
#### 2.2 Pricing Reforms
| Reform | Year |
|---|---|
| Petrol deregulated | **June 2010** |
| Diesel deregulated | **October 2014** |
| LPG subsidy via DBT (**PAHAL**) | 2015 |
### 3. IESS 2047 AND DRAFT NATIONAL ENERGY POLICY 2017
#### 3.1 IESS 2047
- NITI Aayog's open-access energy **modelling / scenario tool**; models supply and demand to 2047 under **Business-As-Usual (BAU)** and **Ambitious** scenarios.
- NITI study "**Scenarios Towards Viksit Bharat and Net Zero**" (Feb 2026): nuclear about **100 GW by 2047**, **200-300 GW by 2070**.
#### 3.2 Draft NEP 2017 – Four Objectives
1. **Access at affordable prices**
2. **Improved security and independence**
3. **Greater sustainability**
4. **Economic growth**
- Fossil fuels = nearly **90%** of commercial primary energy supply, increasingly imported.
!KEY Energy independence goal = **2047**; net zero = **2070**.
### 4. ENERGY TRANSITION TARGETS
#### 4.1 Panchamrit – COP26, Glasgow (2021)
1. **500 GW** non-fossil capacity by 2030
2. **50%** of energy requirement from renewables by 2030 (not 60%)
3. Cut projected cumulative emissions by **1 billion tonnes** by 2030
4. Cut carbon intensity by **45%** by 2030
5. **Net Zero by 2070**
#### 4.2 NDCs and Strategy
| Commitment | Intensity cut | Non-fossil capacity |
|---|---|---|
| Updated NDC (2022) | **45%** by 2030 over 2005 | **50%** by 2030 |
| New NDC (2031-35) | **47%** by 2035 | **60%** by 2035 |
- **Long-Term Low Emission Development Strategy**: submitted to UNFCCC at **COP27 (2022)**.
- 2026: non-fossil **52.27%** of capacity; RE crossed **200 GW**.

### 7. AP ANGLE – RANKS AND STATE POLICY
- **SECI Round I (2022)**: Gujarat topped larger states, then Kerala, Punjab; **AP = "Achiever"**.
- **SEEI 2024**: AP ranked **first in Group 2**.
- **AP Integrated Clean Energy Policy 2024 (ICEP-2024)**: **160 GW+** clean energy, ₹10 lakh crore, **Net Zero by 2047** (national = 2070).
- Bodies: **NEDCAP** nodal RE agency; APGENCO generation; APTRANSCO transmission; APERC regulator.
- **Virtual net metering** with focus on SC/ST households.
### 8. ENERGY FINANCE – NCEF, IREDA, GREEN BONDS
#### 8.1 National Clean Energy Fund
- Created from a **clean energy cess on coal** (Budget **2010-11**, ₹50/tonne; also on imported coal); rose to **₹400/tonne** by 2016-17.
- Purpose: clean energy research and projects. After GST (2017) the cess became the **GST Compensation Cess**; no fresh inflows.
#### 8.2 IREDA and Bonds
- **IREDA**: set up **1987** under **MNRE** (not Ministry of Power); public-sector financial institution for RE; **listed Nov 2023**; **Navratna Apr 2024**.
- **Green bonds**: proceeds ring-fenced for climate projects. **Sovereign green bonds**: first issued by RBI for the Government, **January 2023**.
- Net-zero finance need: over **$20 trillion** by 2070.
### 9. RPO, DERs, OPEN ACCESS AND ELECTRICITY POLICY
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **RPO** | DISCOMs + open-access/captive users must buy a share of RE; fixed by **SERCs** (Electricity Act) |
| **Distributed energy resources** | Small, decentralised units near point of use: **battery storage, biomass generators, fuel cells, rooftop solar PV** (all four) |
| **Prosumer** | Also produces/stores power, sells surplus |
| **Net metering** | Billing that credits solar owners for power fed to grid |
- **ISTS charge waiver**: 100% for solar/wind commissioned by **30 June 2025**; then graded (75% waiver to June 2026).
- **Tariff Policy** 2006, revised **2016**. Draft NEP 2026: **100 GW pumped storage by 2047**, **Time-of-Day tariffs**.
- EC Act 2001: emission-intensity targets for **208** industrial units → domestic carbon market (Jun 2026).
### 10. ENERGY ACCESS AND PROGRAMMES
| Programme | Launch | Aim |
|---|---|---|
| DDUGJY | 2014 | Rural feeder separation, village electrification |
| **PMUY (Ujjwala)** | May 2016, **Ballia (UP)** | LPG to BPL women |
| **Saubhagya** | 2017 | Last-mile household electrification |
| PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli | 2024 | Rooftop solar, **1 crore** households |

- Debates: "**Entropy Effect**" (ES 2025-26): fast decarbonisation without buffers raises grid volatility (Netherlands, Spain); ~**25 GW** gas capacity stranded.
