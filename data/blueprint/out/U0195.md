## U0195 | ANTI-CASTE AND NON-BRAHMIN MOVEMENTS – PHULE, NARAYANA GURU, PERIYAR, AMBEDKAR, JUSTICE PARTY, ADI-ANDHRA
### 1. JYOTIBA PHULE AND THE SATYASHODHAK SAMAJ
#### 1.1 Jyotiba Phule (1827-1890)
- Born **1827**, a gardener's son – **Mali community**, Maharashtra.
- Title **"Mahatma"** given by **Vithalrao Krishnaji Vandekar (1888)**; called **"Father of Indian Social Revolution"**.
- **1848**: girls' school, **Pune**; taught by **Savitribai Phule** – **India's first woman teacher**. The Indian women's movement was inspired largely by Phule.
- **Savitribai** headed the Samaj's women's section; worked for lower-caste girls.
#### 1.2 Satyashodhak Samaj ("Society of Truth Seekers")
- Founded **24 September 1873, Pune**; Phule its **first president**; anti-caste movement in Maharashtra.
- Worked for **Shudras and Ati-Shudras** against **Arya-Brahmin** exploitation (not for Arya-Brahmins).
- Objectives: end caste and untouchability; break **priestly monopoly** over ritual; education for women, Shudras, Ati-Shudras; social service.
- **Satyashodhak marriages**: **without priests**; allowed **inter-caste** marriage.
- Symbol: **King Bali** (the oppressed) against the Brahmanical symbol of **Rama**.
- **Din Bandhu** (Marathi weekly, **Pune, 1877**) – **Krishnarao Bhalekar**; spread the movement to remote Maharashtra; later edited from Bombay by **N.M. Lokhande**.
#### 1.3 Works of Phule
| Work | Note |
|---|---|
| **Gulamgiri** (1873) | "Slavery"; compared caste oppression with slavery |
| **Shetkaryacha Asud** | "The Cultivator's Whipcord" |
| **Sarvajanik Satyadharma Pustak** | Published posthumously, 1891 |
| **Tritiya Ratna**, **Brahmananche Kasab** | Also Phule |
!TRAP Phule's symbol = **King Bali**; Justice Party symbol = **Scales**.
- Latest: the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment began a two-year nationwide celebration of Phule's **200th birth anniversary** on **11 April 2026**.

### 4. TEMPLE ENTRY AND RIGHTS SATYAGRAHAS
#### 4.1 Vaikom Satyagraha (1924-25)
- Place: **Vaikom (Travancore, Kerala)**.
- Issue: right of lower castes (Ezhavas and others) to use the **roads around the Vaikom temple** – part of the **Temple Entry Movement** (APPSC-G2 2023).
- Leaders: **T.K. Madhavan, K.P. Kesava Menon, K. Kelappan**; backed by Narayana Guru.
- **Periyar** jailed → title **"Vaikom Veerar"**.
- **Gandhi**: visited in **1925**, negotiated with Travancore → compromise opened some roads; **not directly a participant** (Gandhi led Rajkot, Kheda, Non-cooperation, not Vaikom).
- Outcome → **Travancore Temple Entry Proclamation, 1936**.
#### 4.2 Comparison

|---|---|---|
| Vaikom | Travancore, 1924-25 | Madhavan, Menon, Kelappan; Periyar |

!TRAP Mahad = **water rights**, not a temple. Vaikom Veerar = Periyar, not Narayana Guru.

#### 5.2 Poona Pact and Neo-Buddhism
- Poona Pact: separate electorates of the **Communal Award** replaced by **reserved seats in joint electorates** – **148 seats** vs **71**; accepted unwillingly **to save Gandhi's life**.
- **Neo-Buddhism**: mass conversion at **Deeksha Bhoomi, Nagpur, 14 Oct 1956**, with **22 vows**.
- Sought annihilation of caste, legal rights, separate electorates; **Gandhi** saw varna as division of labour and untouchability as a corruption.

### 6. JUSTICE PARTY (SOUTH INDIAN LIBERAL FEDERATION)
#### 6.1 Background, Aim and Social Base
- Brahmins about **3%** of Madras Presidency population but over **90%** of government jobs.
- Aim: **protect interests of non-Brahmins** (APPSC 2008); end Brahmin dominance in jobs, education, politics, religion.
- Social base: non-Brahmin landed gentry, **intermediate landed castes** (Andhra: **Reddys, Kammas, Kapus**). Dalits built a parallel "Adi" movement.
- British backed it partly to weaken **Home Rule**; used against Home Rule and Gandhi's movements.
- **Andhra Mahasabha (Vijayawada, 1917)** sought reserved seats for non-Brahmins – **Ponaka Pattabhirama Reddy** and **Tripuraneni Ramaswamy Chowdary**.

- 26 Nov 1923: no-confidence motion against Panagal by **C.R. Reddy**.
- 1926: Swaraj leader **C.V.S. Narasimha Raju** declined to form a ministry.

- **1937** defeat → Periyar dominant → **Dravidar Kazhagam (1944)**.
### 7. PERIYAR AND THE SELF-RESPECT MOVEMENT
- **E.V. Ramasamy Naicker** – President and secretary of the provincial **Congress**; even then supported the Religious Endowments Act.
- **1925**: left Congress over **communal representation** and caste segregation at the **Cheranmadevi Gurukulam**.
- **Self-Respect Movement (1925)**: rationalism; against caste hierarchy and Brahmin priests; **self-respect marriages without priests** (from 1928); women's equality and property rights.
- In Andhra: inspired Bhagya Reddy Varma and Jala Rangaswamy to adopt the "Adi" identity and demand temple entry.

### 8. ADI-ANDHRA AND ADI-HINDU MOVEMENTS
#### 8.1 Terms
- **"Adi"** = original inhabitants; rejected "Panchama". **Adi-Andhra** = Dalits of the Andhra (Madras) region; **Adi-Hindu** = Dalits of Hyderabad State.
- Early root: **Adi-Andhra Sabha (1890)**, first called the Pariya Mahasabha.
#### 8.2 Bhagya Reddy Varma (born 1888, Hyderabad)
- Original name **Madari Bhagaiah**; **"Father of the Dalit movement"** in Hyderabad/Andhra; coined **Adi-Andhra and Adi-Hindu**.
- **Jagan Mitra Mandali (1906)** → Manya Sangham (1911) → **Adi-Hindu Social Service League**; journal **Adi Hindu**.
- **Sahapankti bhojanams** and **Buddhism** for Dalit consciousness; against child marriage, liquor, Devadasi system.
- Presided over the **All India Adi Hindu Conference, Lucknow, 1930** – chose **Ambedkar** for the Round Table Conferences.
