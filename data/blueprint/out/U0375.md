## U0375 | QUASI-JUDICIAL BODIES – NATIONAL GREEN TRIBUNAL, NCLT/NCLAT, CAT, SAT, CONSUMER COMMISSIONS
### 1. CONSTITUTIONAL BASIS – ARTICLES 323A AND 323B
- **Part XIVA** (Articles **323A and 323B**) was inserted by the **42nd Amendment, 1976**, in force from **1 March 1977**.
| Feature | Article 323A | Article 323B |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | **Administrative** tribunals – public service matters | **Other matters** |
| Who can create | **Parliament** only | **Parliament and State Legislatures** |
| Example | CAT, State Administrative Tribunals | Tax, land reform, rent tribunals |
#### 1.1 Full List of Article 323B Subjects
1. Taxation
2. Foreign exchange
3. Industrial and labour disputes
4. Land reforms
5. Ceiling on urban property
6. Elections to Parliament / State Legislature (except Articles 329 and 329A matters)
7. Foodstuffs
8. Rent and tenancy rights
!TRAP Only **Parliament** can set up a 323A tribunal; states also act under 323B.
### 2. NATIONAL GREEN TRIBUNAL
#### 2.1 Basic Facts
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Parent Act | **National Green Tribunal Act, 2010** |
| Operational from | **18 October 2010** |
| Principal bench | **New Delhi** |
| Zonal benches | **Bhopal** (Central), **Pune** (West), **Kolkata** (East), **Chennai** (South) |
| Civil jurisdiction | **7 Acts** in **Schedule I** |
| Disposal mandate | Within **6 months** of filing |
| Appeal | Directly to the **Supreme Court** within **90 days** |
- Schedule I includes the **Water Act, 1974** and the **Water Cess Act, 1977**.
- Funded by **MoEFCC**; criticism: NGT has **no IT division**.
!TRAP NGT appeals go straight to the **Supreme Court**, not to a High Court.
#### 2.2 Landmark NGT Orders
| Order | Issue |
|---|---|
| Ban on **rat-hole mining**, Meghalaya (**2014**) | Unscientific, unsafe coal mining (**APPSC 2024**) |
| Ban on **diesel vehicles older than 10 years**, Delhi-NCR (**2015**) | Air pollution |
| **Art of Living** fined (**2016**) | World Culture Festival damaged the **Yamuna floodplain** |
| **BRO** to plant at least **10,000 trees** in Uttarakhand within two months (**Aug 2023**) | Tree felling for road works |

### 3. NCLT AND NCLAT
- **NCLT** – Sec. **408**, **NCLAT** – Sec. **410** of the **Companies Act, 2013**; both from **1 June 2016**.
- NCLT is the **adjudicating authority** for companies under the **IBC**; IBC appeals go to NCLAT (from 1 Dec 2016) under **Sec. 61** within **30 days** (+15 days on cause).
- Companies Act appeals to NCLAT: within **45 days** (Sec. 421).
- **BIFR** cases moved to NCLT/NCLAT on its dissolution (1 Dec 2016).
| NCLAT hears appeals from | Since |
|---|---|
| NCLT | 2016 |
| IBBI | — |
| **CCI** | **26 May 2017** (replaced COMPAT) |
| **NFRA** | — |
- Further appeal: **Supreme Court**.
### 4. CENTRAL ADMINISTRATIVE TRIBUNAL
- Set up under the **Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985**, made under **Article 323A(1)**.
- Jurisdiction: recruitment and service matters of **Central government employees**.
- **L. Chandra Kumar v. Union of India (1997)**: clauses 323A(2)(d) and 323B(3)(d) excluding High Courts were struck down; judicial review under **Articles 226/227** is **basic structure**.
- Appeal route after 1997: CAT order → **Division Bench of the High Court** → Supreme Court.
!TRAP After L. Chandra Kumar, CAT orders do **not** go directly to the Supreme Court.
### 5. OTHER APPELLATE TRIBUNALS
| Tribunal | Appeals from | Further appeal |
|---|---|---|
| **SAT** (**SEBI Act, 1992**) | **SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA** | SC on **points of law only** |
| **TDSAT** (May 2000) | TRAI, IT Act officers, **Data Protection Board** | Supreme Court |
| **APTEL** (24 Aug 2004) | **CERC, SERCs, PNGRB** | Supreme Court |
!TRAP SAT does **not** hear CCI appeals; CCI appeals go to **NCLAT**.
- **DPDP appeal chain**: Data Protection Board order → TDSAT → Supreme Court.
### 6. CONSUMER COMMISSIONS
- Three-tier body under the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019**; limits revised by **Consumer Protection (Jurisdiction) Rules, 2021** (Dec 2021).
| Commission | Pecuniary limit (2021) | Earlier |
|---|---|---|
| **District** | Up to **₹50 lakh** | Up to ₹1 crore |
| **State** | Above ₹50 lakh to **₹2 crore** | ₹1-10 crore |
| **National (NCDRC)** | Above **₹2 crore** | Above ₹10 crore |
### 7. TRIBUNALS REFORMS ACT 2021 AND MADRAS BAR ASSOCIATION
- **Tribunals Reforms Act, 2021** dissolved several appellate bodies and moved work to **High Courts** / commercial courts:
  - Appellate Tribunal under the **Cinematograph Act, 1952** (film certification) → High Court
  - **Airport Appellate Tribunal** (AAI Act)
  - **Authority for Advance Rulings** (Customs Act) → High Court
  - Appellate Boards under **Trade Marks, Copyright, Patents** Acts (IPAB) → High Court / Commercial Court
- On abolition, members ceased office; pending cases shifted to courts.
- **19 Nov 2025 – Madras Bar Association v. Union of India** (CJI **B.R. Gavai**): struck down key provisions that re-enacted rules already quashed; Centre to set up a **National Tribunals Commission within four months**.
- Principles of **Madras Bar Association-4 (2020)** and **-5 (2021)** continue to govern appointment, tenure and service of members.

### 11. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. NGT appeal → **Supreme Court in 90 days**, not High Court.
2. 323A = Parliament only; 323B = Parliament and states.
3. Part XIVA came by the **42nd** Amendment, not 44th.
4. CAT → **High Court Division Bench** after L. Chandra Kumar (1997).
5. SAT hears SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA; **CCI → NCLAT**.
6. SAT → SC on **questions of law only**.
7. District limit **₹50 lakh**, not ₹1 crore (post-2021).
8. NCLT/NCLAT: Companies Act **2013**, not the IBC.
9. NGT zonal bench in the south = **Chennai**, not Hyderabad.
