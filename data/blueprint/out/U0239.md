## U0239 | ANDHRA IN THE CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE MOVEMENT AND THE SALT SATYAGRAHA
### 1. ANDHRA CDM ORGANISATION – THE DICTATOR
- The **Andhra Congress Committee** appointed **Konda Venkatappayya** as the **Dictator for Andhra** for the Salt Satyagraha / CDM (APPSC 2008, 2017) – the **first leader of the CDM in Andhra**.
- Every district had its **own dictator, war council and camp (shibiram)**.
| Camp | Place |
|---|---|
| Guntur camp | Guntur |
| **Pallepadu Ashram** | **Nellore** |
| Bellary camp | Rayalaseema |
| **Vedavanam** (Prakasam's house) | **Madras** (not Krishna) |
| Eluru National School | Volunteers joined (May 1930) |
!TRAP Vedavanam, Prakasam's house, was in Madras, not in Krishna district.

### 4. PROGRAMME – BOYCOTT AND PICKETING
- Main target of the CDM (1930): the **salt tax**.
- **Towns:** picketing of **foreign-cloth shops**.
- **Villages:** **making salt**, **cutting toddy palms**, **picketing toddy and arrack shops**, **raiding salt depots**.
- **Eluru National School** volunteers joined in May 1930.
- Later echo: an **anti-arrack agitation** was launched by women at **Dubagunta (Nellore) in 1991**.
### 5. WOMEN IN THE SALT SATYAGRAHA
| Woman | Role |
|---|---|
| **Achanta Rukmini Lakshmipathi** | **First woman arrested** (and jailed) in the Salt Satyagraha – at Vedaranyam after Rajaji's arrest, **15 May 1930**; **first woman graduate in Andhra** |
| **Durgabai Deshmukh** | Led **20 women** to make salt at the **High Court beach, Madras** |
| **Unnava Lakshmibayamma** | **"Swarajya Lakshmi Vratam"** door-to-door on **Ugadi**, Guntur |
| **Sarojini Naidu** | Led the **Dharasana** march |
| **Kamaladevi Chattopadhyay** | Led women into the Salt Satyagraha |
| **Kasturba Gandhi** | Protests; imprisoned |
- Andhra women joined in **large numbers** – salt-making, picketing, imprisonment.
### 6. DHARASANA RAID AND THE FOREIGN PRESS
- Gandhi was arrested on the night of **4-5 May 1930** at **Karadi** near Dandi and taken to **Yerwada Jail**.
- **First and second declared leaders:** **Abbas Tyabji** (arrested **12 May 1930**), then **Sarojini Naidu**.
- **Sarojini Naidu** led the non-violent march on the **Dharasana Salt Works** (**21 May 1930**, about 2,500 satyagrahis); volunteers took **unarmed beatings**.
- Reported by **Webb Miller** (**United Press**, American) – he had been with Gandhi during the Dandi March.

### 8. MARTYRS AND REPRESSION
- **Thota Narsayya** – injured at **Tilak Chowk, Machilipatnam**, raising the flag.
- **Venneti Satyanarayana** – lathi-charged at **Rajahmundry**.
- **15 January 1932 – Berhampore firing**, in the repression under **Lord Willingdon**.
### 9. CDM CHRONOLOGY AND VICEROYS
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 26 Jan 1930 | Nehru's flag on the Ravi; Thota Narsayya's flag at Machilipatnam |
| **2 Mar 1930** | Letter to **Irwin** with **11 demands** |
| **12 Mar – 6 Apr 1930** | Dandi March, **78 followers, ~240 miles**; reached Dandi **5 April** ("I want world sympathy in this battle of Right against Might") |
| 6 Apr 1930 | First Andhra salt satyagraha, Machilipatnam |
| **5 Mar 1931** | **Gandhi-Irwin Pact**; CDM suspended; Gandhi at the 2nd RTC |
| 1932-34 | Second phase; repression under **Willingdon** |
| 1934 | CDM withdrawn |
- **Only Telugu Dandi marcher:** **Yerneni Subramanyam**.

### 12. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. **Andhra Dictator** = Konda Venkatappayya, not Prakasam.
2. **Bulusu** = Kakinada; **Kasi Viswanadham** = Vadarevu; **Dandu Narayana Raju** = Mattapalem.
3. **Machilipatnam** = Pattabhi's "Mecca"; first in Andhra.
4. **Gandhi-Irwin Pact = 1931**, not March 1930.
5. **Abbas Tyabji first**, Sarojini Naidu second.
6. **Webb Miller** (United Press) reported Dharasana.
7. **Kalamkari** = Machilipatnam; **Jamdani** = Uppada; **salt** = Pedaganjam-Chinaganjam.
8. **Kalluri Subbarao** led at Bellary – Rayalaseema had no coast.
9. **Malabar** = K. Kelappan; **Odisha** = Gopabandhu Chaudhuri; **Tamil Nadu** = Rajaji.
10. **Willingdon**, not Irwin, was Viceroy during the 1932-34 repression.
