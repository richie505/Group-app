### 2. ARTHASHASTRA OF KAUTILYA
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Author | **Kautilya = Chanakya = Vishnugupta** (APPSC GS 2023) – **not Pushyagupta** |
| Subject | Statecraft – **administration, economy, espionage, taxation**, diplomacy, welfare |
| Rediscovery | **R. Shamasastry**, **Mysore, 1905** |
| Published | **1909** (also by Shamasastry) |
| Structure | **15 books** called **adhikaranas** |
- Mentions **dasas** (slaves) – contradicts Megasthenes.
!TRAP Found 1905, published 1909 – both by Shamasastry. Pushyagupta is the Sudarshana lake governor, not Kautilya.
### 3. INDICA OF MEGASTHENES
- **Megasthenes** (Greek) – envoy of **Seleucus I Nicator** to the court of **Chandragupta Maurya** at **Pataliputra** (c. 302 BCE).
- Original **lost**; fragments in **Diodorus, Strabo, Pliny, Arrian** (Strabo and Arrian only kept parts).
- **Deimachus** – a later envoy of **Antiochus I** to **Bindusara** (not Megasthenes).
#### 3.1 What Indica Says
| Topic | Account |
|---|---|
| Society | **Seven classes**, not the fourfold varna; noted **endogamy** and **hereditary occupations** |
| Slavery | Claimed **no slavery** in India – **wrong**; Arthashastra and Ashokan edicts mention dasas |
| Pataliputra | **Palibothra** (also **Polibrotha**) = Patna (UKPSC 2025); palace; **wooden palisade** |
| City administration | **Six committees of five members each**; town officials **Astynomoi** |
| **Andhras** | **First foreign work** to mention the Andhras: **30 fortified towns**, **1,00,000 infantry**, **2,000 cavalry**, **1,000 elephants** |
!KEY APPSC G2 2002: first to mention forts in Andhra = Megasthenes (Indika, 4th c. BC).
!TRAP Andhra forts = 30, not 80. The 80 belongs to the 80-pillared hall at Kumrahar.
!NUM 7 classes | 6 committees x 5 members | 30 Andhra forts | 1,00,000 infantry | 2,000 cavalry | 1,000 elephants
### 4. MUDRARAKSHASA AND OTHER LITERATURE
- **Mudrarakshasa** – Sanskrit drama by **Vishakhadatta**; the **overthrow of the Nandas** and rise of Chandragupta Maurya.
- **Mamulanar** (Sangam Tamil poet) – refers to the Mauryas' **southward push**.
- Other texts: **Kalpasutra, Jatakas, Dighanikaya**; **Milindapanho** is about **Menander** (not Mauryan).
### 5. PURANIC SOURCES
- Number of Puranas = **18**.
- **Vishnu Purana** – usually used for **Mauryan king lists and genealogies**.
### 6. BUDDHIST AND JAIN SOURCES – RULER EACH COVERS

| **Dipavamsa, Mahavamsa** | Sri Lankan Pali chronicles | **Ashoka** – conversion, mission to Sri Lanka |
| **Ashokavadana, Divyavadana** | Buddhist Sanskrit | **Ashoka** legends |
| **Parishishtaparvan** (Hemachandra), Avasyaka Sutra Vritti | Jain | **Chandragupta** – Jainism under **Bhadrabahu**, death at **Shravanabelagola** |

### 7. EPIGRAPHIC SOURCES
| Inscription | Mauryan fact |
|---|---|
| **Ashokan edicts** | First deciphered by **James Prinsep, 1837**; Ashoka called them **Dhammalipi** (APPSC GS 2023) |
| **Greek-Aramaic edict** | **Shar-i-Kuna (Kandahar)** |
| **Junagadh (Girnar) inscription of Rudradaman I** (c. 150 CE) | **Sudarshana lake** built under **Chandragupta Maurya** by governor **Pushyagupta** |
| **Hathigumpha inscription** of Kharavela | Also cited for Mauryan references |
!TRAP Kandahar = Greek-Aramaic Ashokan edict; Girnar/Junagadh = Rudradaman's inscription.
### 8. ARCHAEOLOGICAL SOURCES
| Site / find | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Kumrahar** (Patna) | **80-pillared hall** of Pataliputra; ASI excavation from **1913**; rare Mauryan secular architecture |
| **Bulandibagh** (Patna) | **Wooden palisade** (dug 1926–27), thought to be the one Megasthenes saw |
| **Punch-marked coins** | **Silver**; Mauryan issue continued Magadha's punch-marked coinage; hoards across India |
| **NBPW** (Northern Black Polished Ware) | Fine pottery of the middle Ganga plains; late phase matches the Mauryan age; found at Pataliputra, Kumrahar |
