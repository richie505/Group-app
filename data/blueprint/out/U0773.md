## U0773 | INTERNATIONAL TREATIES ON CHEMICALS AND WASTE – BASEL, ROTTERDAM, STOCKHOLM POPs, MINAMATA; MONTREAL PROTOCOL AND KIGALI AMENDMENT
### 1. OZONE AND THE OZONE LAYER
#### 1.1 Ozone
- Formula **O₃** – three oxygen atoms; formed by **UV radiation + O₂ → O₃** (ozone–oxygen cycle).
- Concentrated in the **stratosphere** (about **10–50 km**), with most ozone near **20–25 km**; **absorbs harmful UV-B**.
- Stratospheric ozone is naturally controlled by **nitrogen dioxide**.
#### 1.2 Measurement – Dobson Unit
- **Dobson Unit (DU)** measures the thickness/concentration of ozone in a **column of air from ground to top of atmosphere**.
- **1 DU = 0.01 mm** of pure ozone at **0°C and 1 atm (STP)**.
- **"Ozone hole"** = total column ozone **below 220 DU**.
### 2. OZONE HOLE
- First detected over **Antarctica**; first reported **1985** by **British Antarctic Survey** scientists.
- Season: **Southern Hemisphere spring, August–November**.
- Causes of the Antarctic hole: a prominent **polar front (vortex)**, **polar stratospheric clouds** and inflow of **CFCs**.
- **Polar stratospheric clouds** form in extreme cold; **nitric acid** on their surfaces reacts with CFCs → stable chlorine becomes **active chlorine** → destroys ozone **when sunlight returns**.
- **Tibetan ozone low ("halo")** over the Tibetan Plateau (2005) – **G. W. Kent Moore**, University of Toronto.
- Dense stratospheric ozone pocket at **21–23 km** over the **North Bay of Bengal** (PIB, 16 Aug 2026).

### 3. CFCs – MAIN CAUSE OF DEPLETION
- **CFCs (chlorofluorocarbons)** are the **main cause** (APPSC 2025, twice): under UV they release **chlorine** in the stratosphere, which breaks ozone.
- **Chlorine and bromine** destroy ozone **catalytically** – not consumed in the first reaction.
- An increase in **halocarbons** depletes the ozone layer.
- CFCs are also **greenhouse gases** (~15% of the greenhouse effect) – they cause both warming and ozone loss.
- CFC uses: refrigerators, ACs, **aerosol propellants (pressurising agents)**, **plastic foams**, **cleaning electronic components**, solvents – **not tubeless tyres**.
!TRAP CO₂, methane and N₂ are the usual wrong options – they are not the main ozone depleters.
- **APPSC-G2 2025 assertion-reason**: A = "CFCs are GHGs and increase UV"; R = "CFCs are man-made (fridges, ACs, aerosols)" → **both true, R is NOT the explanation** (R only says where CFCs are used).
### 4. ODS VS NON-ODS – USES
| Substance | ODS? | Main uses / sources |
|---|---|---|
| **CFCs** | Yes – main culprit | Refrigerators, ACs, aerosols, foams, solvents |
| **Halons** (bromine; 1211, 1301) | Yes – ~**100 times** more potent than CFCs | **Portable fire extinguishers** |
| **HCFCs** | Yes, weaker | **Interim/transitional** CFC substitute |
| **Carbon tetrachloride, methyl chloroform** | Yes | Solvents, chemical feedstock |
| **Methyl bromide** | Yes | **Agricultural fumigant**, quarantine |
| Nitrous oxide | Ozone depleter | Laughing gas (N₂O) |
| **HFCs** | **No (non-ODS)** – but **high GWP** | ODS substitutes in ACs, refrigeration → **Kigali** |
- **HFCs**: entirely **man-made**; used in refrigeration, ACs, insulating foams, aerosols, foam-blowing, **fire suppression (clean agents)**; minor use as solvents; **not lubricants**.
#### 4.1 Refrigerants and Fire Extinguishers
| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| **Freon** (CFC; DuPont trade name, stable halocarbon) | **Domestic refrigerators** |
| **Mafron** | Refrigerator gas (halogenated hydrocarbon) |
| **Liquid ammonia** | **Large cold-storage plants** |
| SO₂ | Early refrigerant |
| **Soda-acid extinguisher** | **NaHCO₃ + dilute H₂SO₄ → CO₂** |
| CCl₄ ("Pyrene") | Older liquid extinguisher; also an ODS |
- Ammonia liquefies at room temperature only at **high pressure** (not low).
### 5. UV RADIATION EFFECTS
| Target | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Human health** | **Skin cancer**, **cataracts**, blindness, **immune suppression**, DNA damage; 1% ozone loss raises harmful UV ~2% |
| **Crops** | Reduced growth/photosynthesis in rice, wheat, corn, soybean; 1% UV-B rise may cut food output ~1% |
| **Phytoplankton / marine** | Damage to plankton and larvae of fish, shrimp, crab; food-chain disruption |
| Materials | Degrades wood, plastic, rubber, fabrics |
### 6. VIENNA CONVENTION AND MONTREAL PROTOCOL
#### 6.1 Vienna Convention (1985)
- **Framework treaty** for the **protection of the ozone layer** – cooperation on research, monitoring, protective measures.
#### 6.2 Montreal Protocol (1987)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Signed | **16 September 1987**, **Montreal, Canada** (not Helsinki) |
| In force | **1 January 1989** |
| Nature | **Action treaty** under Vienna; legally binding |
| Aim | Phase out **production and consumption of ODS** |
| Coverage | ~100 chemicals: CFCs, halons, CTC, methyl chloroform, HCFCs, methyl bromide |
| Parties | **198** – **first and only UN treaty universally ratified** |
| Secretariat | Ozone Secretariat, **Nairobi** |
- Results: **~99% of ODS phased out** (2024); avoids **0.5–1°C** warming by 2050.
- **Multilateral Fund (1991)**: technical and financial aid to developing countries; India argued for it from 1989, secured it in 1990.
- **MOP37** focused on implementing **Kigali**.
!KEY FIRST: Vienna Convention + Montreal Protocol = first treaties with **universal ratification**.
### 7. WORLD OZONE DAY
- **16 September** = **International Day for the Preservation of the Ozone Layer**; declared by **UNGA on 19 December 1994**; marks the **signing of the Montreal Protocol (1987)**.
- **2026 theme: "Global Action for a Cooler Planet"** – marks **10 years of the Kigali Amendment**.

### 8. KIGALI AMENDMENT, 2016
- Adopted **2016, Kigali, Rwanda**; **amends the Montreal Protocol**; in force **1 January 2019**.
- Targets **HFCs** – not ODS but **very high GWP**; gradual reduction of their production and consumption.
- Global target: cut HFC use **80–85% by late 2040s**; avoids up to **0.5°C** warming by 2100.
| Group | Baseline | Freeze | Final cut |
|---|---|---|---|
| Developed | 2011–13 | Reductions from 2019 | 85% by 2036 |
| Group 1 (incl. China) | 2020–22 | 2024 | 80% by 2045 |
| **India (Group 2**, with Gulf states, Iran, Iraq, Pakistan) | **2024–26** | **2028** | **85% by 2047** |
- India **ratified Kigali in 2021**; steps **10% by 2032 → 20% by 2037 → 30% by 2042**.
- MoEFCC: **no environmental clearance** for new/additional HFC production **after 31 December 2027**.
!TRAP Kigali amends **Montreal**, not Kyoto (Kyoto belongs to UNFCCC).
### 9. INDIA'S OZONE AND COOLING ACTION
#### 9.1 Ozone Cell and ODS Rules
- **Ozone Cell (MoEFCC)** = India's **National Ozone Unit**.
- **ODS (Regulation and Control) Rules, 2000** – under the **Environment (Protection) Act**.
| Substance | Phased out by |
|---|---|
| Halons (except essential use) | **1 Jan 2001** |
| CFCs in manufacturing (except MDIs) | **1 Jan 2003** |
| CTC, methyl chloroform, CFCs in MDIs | **1 Jan 2010** |
| Methyl bromide | **1 Jan 2015** |
| HCFCs | Allowed up to **1 Jan 2040** |
#### 9.2 HCFC Phase-out Management Plan (HPMP)
- **Stage-I**: met 2013 freeze + 10% cut by 2015; phased out 341.77 ODP tonnes.
- **Stage-II**: India **first developing country** to fully phase out **HCFC-141b** in rigid foam; 44% cut vs 35% target by Jan 2020.
- Over **20,000** RAC technicians trained.
#### 9.3 India Cooling Action Plan (ICAP)
- Launched by **MoEFCC, March 2019**; among the world's **first** national cooling plans; **20-year** horizon to **2037–38**.
- Targets: cut **cooling demand 20–25%**, **refrigerant demand 25–30%**; train **1 lakh** servicing technicians.
- Thematic areas: **space cooling in buildings, cold chain, servicing sector, domestic production, R&D, transport** – **not coal-based power**.
### 10. BASEL CONVENTION, 1989
- Adopted **Basel, Switzerland, 1989**; in force 1992; India ratified **June 1992**.
- Controls **transboundary movement of hazardous wastes and their disposal**; reduces generation; **environmentally sound management**.
- Problem addressed (APPSC-G2 2025): **physical transfer of hazardous waste across borders** for reprocessing/storage (dumping from rich to poorer countries).
- India implements Basel's **PIC mechanism** under the **Hazardous and Other Wastes (Management and Transboundary Movement) Rules, 2016**.
- **Ban Amendment**: bans hazardous-waste exports from **OECD/EU + Liechtenstein** to other countries; in force **5 December 2019**.
- Recent: plastic waste amendment operative; e-waste provisions strengthened.
!TRAP "Biodiversity" is the usual wrong option for Basel.
### 11. ROTTERDAM, STOCKHOLM AND MINAMATA
#### 11.1 Rotterdam Convention
- **Prior Informed Consent (PIC)** for hazardous **chemicals and pesticides** in international trade – **importing country must agree** before a listed chemical is exported.
- In force **2004**; India **acceded 2005**; Designated National Authorities: **Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilisers + Ministry of Agriculture**.
- Annex III: pesticides and industrial chemicals banned or severely restricted by Parties.
- **BRS**: Basel, Rotterdam, Stockholm share **one joint secretariat** under UNEP; India – MoEFCC's **Hazardous Substances Management Division**.
#### 11.2 Stockholm Convention (POPs)
- Adopted **2001**, in force **2004**; India ratified **2006**; **7 more POPs ratified 2020** (chlordecone, hexabromobiphenyl, pentachlorobenzene, HBCD, hexachlorobutadiene etc.).
- **POPs**: toxic, **long-lasting, bioaccumulate, travel long distances**; **POPRC** assesses candidates.
- Initial list: **12 POPs, "dirty dozen"** – DDT, PCBs, dioxins, furans, aldrin; now **over 30** chemicals.
| Annex | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| A | Elimination | Aldrin |
| B | Restriction | **DDT** (malaria control) |
| C | Unintentional by-products | **Dioxins, furans** |
- **2025 Annex A additions**: **chlorpyrifos** (neurotoxic insecticide – not in the dirty dozen), **long-chain PFCAs** (PFAS **"forever chemicals"** in cookware), **medium-chain chlorinated paraffins** (plastics).
#### 11.3 Minamata Convention (Mercury)
- Adopted **2013**, in force **2017**; protects from **man-made emissions of mercury** across its lifecycle.
- Major sources: **coal-fired power plants**, **artisanal and small-scale gold mining** (one of the largest), industrial processes, waste – **not solar PV panels** in normal operation.
- Phase out **dental amalgam by 2034**.
- Name: **Minamata, Japan** – factory wastewater mercury poisoned fish-eaters (**Minamata disease**, 1950s; 1953).
