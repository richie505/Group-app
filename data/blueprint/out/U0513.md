## U0513 | INFRASTRUCTURE - NATIONAL INFRASTRUCTURE PIPELINE, PM GATI SHAKTI, NATIONAL MONETISATION PIPELINE, LOGISTICS POLICY
### 1. PM GATI SHAKTI NATIONAL MASTER PLAN
- Launched **13 October 2021** (APPSC-GS 2025 key: **2021**).
- **Whole-of-government, GIS-based** integrated planning platform for **multimodal connectivity** to economic zones.
- Brings **16 ministries** (incl. Railways, Roads, Ports) on one digital platform.
- National Master Plan portal developed by **BISAG-N, Gandhinagar**; **1,700+ data layers**.
- Structure: **Empowered Group of Secretaries** (headed by **Cabinet Secretary**) → **Network Planning Group**.
#### 1.1 Seven Engines (full list)
1. Railways
2. Roads
3. Ports
4. Waterways
5. Airports
6. Mass Transport
7. Logistics Infrastructure
!TRAP **Power transmission is NOT one of the 7 engines** (APPSC-GS 2024 key).
- 2026: railway economic corridors - 434 projects, ₹11.17 lakh crore mapped on the portal; Ballari-Guntakal 3rd/4th lines (₹1,264 crore) planned via GatiShakti (Jul 2026); gram panchayat plans to use GatiShakti data (6 Jul 2026).
### 2. NATIONAL INFRASTRUCTURE PIPELINE (NIP)
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Period | **FY2019-20 to FY2024-25** |
| Investment | **~₹111 lakh crore** |
| Coverage | **Both economic and social** infrastructure |
| Funding | Centre **39%**, States **40%**, private **21%** |
| Sector shares | **Energy ~24%**, roads ~18%, urban ~17%, railways ~12% |
- Now a pipeline of ₹185-213 lakh crore across 30+ sectors incl. digital and social.
!TRAP "NIP covers only economic infrastructure" is WRONG (APPSC-G1 2024). Coal mines are NOT an example of economic infrastructure (exam key); roads and railways are.
- Infrastructure **multiplier**: ₹1 spent yields **₹2.5-3.5 of GDP**.
- Centre's capex: ₹5.92 lakh crore (FY22) → ₹11.21 lakh crore (FY26) → **₹12.2 lakh crore** (Budget 2026-27).
### 3. NATIONAL MONETISATION PIPELINE (NMP)
- Announced in **Union Budget 2021-22**; monetises **brownfield core infrastructure**; **ownership retained by Government**.
- Instruments: **PPP concessions, InvITs, TOT, securitisation** (not sale of public ownership); monitored by CGAM.
| Item | NMP 1.0 | NMP 2.0 |
|---|---|---|
| Period | FY22-FY25 | **FY26-FY30** |
| Value | **₹6 lakh crore** | **₹16.72 lakh crore** (incl. ₹5.8 lakh crore private) |
| Largest sector | **Roads** (APPSC-GS 2021) | Highways, MMLPs, ropeways 26% |
| Result / launch | ₹5.3 lakh crore monetised (~89-90%); **coal** gave most (₹2 lakh crore) | Launched **23 Feb 2026**; prepared by **NITI Aayog** |

- Network: 69,439 route km (Mar 2025).
- Electrification: **50.91%** of routes by **March 2019** (APPSC-G1 2022) → about 98% of broad gauge by end of FY 2024-25 → **99.1%** by Oct 2025. Earlier target: 100% electrification by 2023.

- **National Rail Plan 2030**: raise rail's freight share **28% → 45%**; expand **Kavach**.

### 6. INFRASTRUCTURE FINANCING AND PPP
| Institution | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **IIFCL** | Set up **2006**; long-term PPP finance; **NBFC-IFC since 2013** |
| **InvITs** | **SEBI regulations 2014**; pool operating infrastructure assets |
| **NIIF** | Set up **2015**; government-anchored; manages **Category II AIFs** |
| **NaBFID** | **NaBFID Act 2021**; paid-up **₹20,000 crore + ₹5,000 crore grant**; ₹2,03,029 crore sanctioned (Mar 2025) |
- **NHAI funding**: budget support, tolls, ToT and InvITs (₹1.52 lakh crore monetised); first public InvIT planned 2026; PPP pipeline 13,400 km (₹8.3 lakh crore).
- **Deepak Parekh Committee**: on **financing infrastructure**; report May 2007; a later Parekh panel gave an interim report in October 2012.
- **Vijay Kelkar Committee (2015)**: on **revisiting and revitalising the PPP model**; report 19 November 2015; proposed a **PPP Project Review Committee** and an **Infrastructure PPP Adjudication Tribunal**.
!KEY Order: IIFCL 2006 → InvIT regulations 2014 → NIIF 2015 → NaBFID Act 2021.
### 7. NATIONAL LOGISTICS POLICY 2022
- Launched **September 2022**; complements PM GatiShakti.
- Target: **logistics cost to single digits of GDP by 2030**.
- Logistics cost: **7.97%** of GDP (FY24), down from 8.84% (FY23); older estimates 13-14%.
- **ULIP** = **Unified Logistics Interface Platform**.
- **LEADS** index (**DPIIT, 2018**), based on the **World Bank's Logistics Performance Index**. LEADS 2025: four tiers (Exemplars: Tamil Nadu, UP, Mizoram, Delhi). **AP = "Achiever"** among coastal states in LEADS 2023.
!TRAP GatiShakti (Oct 2021) came BEFORE the National Logistics Policy (Sept 2022).
### 8. AIRPORTS AND PORTS (INDIA)
- **Cochin International Airport** - owned by a company, **CIAL**; **first airport in India built under PPP**; world's first fully solar-powered airport (2015).
- **Navi Mumbai International Airport** - commercial operations from **25 December 2025** (Adani 74%, CIDCO 26%).
- **Vizhinjam (Kerala)** - India's first dedicated **deep-water transhipment port**; developed by Adani Ports under PPP; commissioned **2 May 2025**.
