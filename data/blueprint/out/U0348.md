## U0348 | STATUTORY BODIES – NHRC AND SHRCs, NATIONAL COMMISSION FOR WOMEN, NCPCR, NATIONAL COMMISSION FOR MINORITIES
### 1. CONSTITUTIONAL vs STATUTORY BODIES
- Only **NCSC (Art. 338), NCST (Art. 338A), NCBC (Art. 338B)** are constitutional commissions; the rest depend on **Acts** Parliament can change or let lapse.
#### 1.1 Body → Act and Year
| Body | Status / basis | Set up |
|---|---|---|
| **NHRC / SHRCs** | Statutory – **Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993** | **12 Oct 1993** (Ordinance 28 Sep 1993, Art. 123) |
| **NCW** | Statutory – **NCW Act, 1990** (in force 30 Aug 1990) | **January 1992** |
| **NCM** | Statutory – **NCM Act, 1992** | 17 May 1993 (MHA resolution body 1978) |
| **NCPCR** | Statutory – **CPCR Act, 2005** | **2007** |
| **NCSK** | **NCSK Act, 1993** (lapsed 2004) → non-statutory | constituted 1994 |
| CVC | CVC Act 2003 | 1964 resolution |
| NCBC | Statutory 1993 → **constitutional 2018** (102nd, Art. 338B) | 1993 |
| Lokpal / NGT / CIC | Acts of 2013 / 2010 / RTI 2005 | – |
| Zonal Councils | States Reorganisation Act, 1956, **Sec. 15**; chair = **Union Home Minister** | – |
| Finance Commission, State FC (243I), Parliament Secretariat (98) | **Constitutional** | – |
- NCBC = **102nd** Amendment, NOT the 89th (which split NCSC and NCST). There is no "Art. 338C" for NCW.
- **MWCD** statutory bodies: **NCW, NCPCR, CARA** – NOT NCSK (Social Justice Ministry).
!TRAP G2 2023: "NHRC is constitutional" = FALSE; no Article of the Constitution is behind it.
### 2. NHRC – COMPOSITION AND APPOINTMENT
- Headquarters **Delhi**; conforms to the **Paris Principles** (Paris, Oct 1991; UNGA 20 Dec 1993).
| Item | Before 2019 | After PHR (Amendment) Act 2019 |
|---|---|---|
| Chairperson | Former **CJI** only | Former CJI **or SC Judge** |
| Human-rights members | 2 | **3**, at least **one woman** |
| Term | 5 years or 70 | **3 years or 70**; reappointment allowed |
| Deemed members | Chairs of NCM, NCSC, NCST, NCW | + **NCBC, NCPCR, Chief Commissioner for PwD** |
- Appointed by the **President** on a **six-member committee**: **PM (chair)**, Lok Sabha Speaker, **RS Deputy Chairman**, LoPs of both Houses, **Union Home Minister**.
- **Removal**: President, after a **Supreme Court inquiry** (misbehaviour/incapacity); no parliamentary resolution.
- 2019: Centre may give UT human-rights work to SHRCs; **Delhi stays with the NHRC**.
### 3. POWERS AND LIMITS OF HUMAN RIGHTS COMMISSIONS
- **Civil court** powers in inquiries (summon, documents, affidavits); counts as a **Civil Court** for IPC Sections 175, 178, 179, 180, 228.
- Recommendations **not binding**; government must respond; cannot compel.
- **Time bar**: cannot inquire **after one year** from the violation.
- 28 Jun 2026: NHRC notices to AP DGP, Chief Secretary and AP SHRC on **Gade Sai Krishna** custodial death (Art. 21).
### 4. SHRC
- Under **PHRA 1993**; covers only **State List and Concurrent List** matters not already before NHRC or another statutory commission.
- Chair (2019): former **Chief Justice or Judge of a High Court**.
- Appointed by the **Governor** on a committee of **CM, Speaker, Home Minister, LoP** (+ Council Chairman and its LoP); **removed only by the President**.
- **AP SHRC** functions from **Kurnool**.
- 11 Feb 2026: SC asked whether SHRC chairs could act as State Information Commissions.
!TRAP The Governor is NOT on the SHRC recommending committee; he only appoints.
### 5. NATIONAL COMMISSION FOR WOMEN
- **NCW Act, 1990**; constituted **January 1992** (G2-GS 2019 key).
- Composition: **Chairperson + 5 members** (at least **1 SC, 1 ST**) + **Member-Secretary** – all nominated by the **Central Government** (not the President); 3 years.
- Functions – **Section 10**: review safeguards and laws, suggest amendments; complaints and **suo motu** notice; special studies; Centre must **consult** NCW on major policies.
- Forms committees, co-opts outsiders, regulates its own procedure; does **NOT** impose criminal sentences.
- **Mahila Jan Sunwai** = public hearing of women's grievances.
### 6. NCPCR
- **CPCR Act, 2005**; set up **2007**; with **SCPCRs** in States.
- Keeps laws in line with the Constitution and **UNCRC** (1989; India acceded **1992**).
- Monitors **RTE Act 2009**, **POCSO Act 2012**, **JJ Act 2015**.
- 2026: SC ruling on RTE **25%** free seats (Sec. 12(1)(c)); menstrual-hygiene ruling (Feb 2026).

### 7. NATIONAL COMMISSION FOR MINORITIES
- **Chair + Vice-Chair** (added **1995**) **+ 5** = **7**; five incl. Chair from minorities; appointed by the **Central Government**; 3 years.
- Six notified minorities: **Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Parsis, Jains** (latest – **27 Jan 2014**).
- **15** State Minorities Commissions incl. **AP**, Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Delhi, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Maharashtra, MP, Manipur, Rajasthan, TN, Uttarakhand, UP, West Bengal.
- Debate: NCM empty for **15 months**.
### 8. NCSK
- Under **MoSJE**; monitors **Manual Scavengers Act, 2013**.
- **January 2022**: extended **3 years** beyond 31 Mar 2022 → **31 March 2025** (APPSC 2021); next to **31 March 2028**.
- Vice-Chairperson **Hardeep Singh Gill**; missed-call grievance line on foundation day, **23 August** 2026.

| AP State Commission for Women | State Act | Chair **Rayapati Sailaja** |
