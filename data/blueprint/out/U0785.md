## U0785 | INTERNATIONAL CLIMATE INITIATIVES – UNFCCC, KYOTO PROTOCOL, CDM, PARIS AGREEMENT, COP OUTCOMES, IPCC REPORTS
### 1. UNFCCC – THE FRAMEWORK
| Treaty | Adopted | In force | Remember |
|---|---|---|---|
| **UNFCCC** | **9 May 1992, New York** | **21 March 1994** | Opened for signing at **Rio Earth Summit (June 1992)**; **198 Parties**; Secretariat **Bonn** |
| **Kyoto Protocol** | **11 Dec 1997, Kyoto (COP3)** | **16 Feb 2005** | **First legally binding** climate treaty; **192 Parties** |
| **Paris Agreement** | **12 Dec 2015 (COP21)**, 194 nations | **4 Nov 2016** | **India ratified 2 Oct 2016** |
- **Objective**: stabilise GHG concentrations at a level that prevents **dangerous anthropogenic interference**.
- **IPCC AR1 (1990)** fed into the UNFCCC.
- **COP** = supreme body, meets annually (the term COP is also used for the CBD). Two instruments: **Kyoto Protocol (meeting = CMP)** and **Paris Agreement (meeting = CMA)**.
!TRAP UNFCCC adopted in New York (May 1992), opened for signature at Rio (June 1992). Secretariat Bonn (UNFCCC) vs Geneva (IPCC).
#### 1.1 Annex Groups
| Group | Members | Obligation |
|---|---|---|
| **Annex I** | Industrialised countries + economies in transition | Lead emission cuts |
| **Annex II** | **OECD members of Annex I** | Give **finance and technology** to developing countries |
| **Non-Annex I** | Developing countries incl. **India** | No binding cuts; special care for LDCs, SIDS |
#### 1.2 CBDR-RC
- **Common But Differentiated Responsibilities and Respective Capabilities**: foundational UNFCCC principle; developed countries, with most **historical emissions**, must lead.
| Text | Reference |
|---|---|
| Rio Declaration, 1992 | **Principle 7** |
| UNFCCC | **Article 3.1** |
| Paris Agreement | **Article 2.2** ("in the light of different national circumstances") |
### 2. KYOTO PROTOCOL
- **Binding targets for Annex I only**; called on industrialised nations to cut emissions significantly (APPSC 2019).
- **USA never ratified**; **Canada withdrew (2011)**.
- **Six gases**: CO2, CH4, N2O, HFCs, PFCs, SF6; **NF3** added for the 2nd period.
| Period | Commitment |
|---|---|
| **1st (2008-2012)** | 37 countries; about **5% (5.2%)** below 1990 |
| **2nd (2013-2020)**, **Doha Amendment (2012)** | **18%** below 1990; Doha Amendment in force **31 Dec 2020** |
#### 2.1 Flexible Mechanisms
| Mechanism | Between | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| **International Emissions Trading** | Annex I and Annex I | Traded allowances |
| **CDM (Article 12)** | Annex I (Annex-B Party) runs project **in a developing country** | **CERs** |
| **Joint Implementation** | Annex I project in **another Annex I** country | **ERUs** |
- **Annex-I** = a Kyoto grouping (not Cartagena); agreed second-round targets with the EU.
!PAIR CDM | CERs | Developing country || JI | ERUs | Other Annex I
### 3. CARBON MARKETS – CDM TO PARIS ARTICLE 6
- **Carbon credit concept**: from the **Kyoto Protocol (1997)**. **1 credit = 1 tonne CO2e** emitted, reduced or removed; tradable.
- **CERs** are issued by the **CDM Executive Board** (not Nagoya). India earned CERs as a host country.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Article 6.2** | **Bilateral** cooperative approaches, e.g. **India-Japan JCM** (signed Aug 2025; rules Jan 2026) |
| **Article 6.4** | UN-run **Paris Agreement Crediting Mechanism (PACM)**; credits = **A6.4ERs**; **replaces CDM** |
| Corresponding adjustments | Prevent double counting |
- Article 6 rules finalised and **fully operational at COP29**.
- **First Article 6.4 credits**: clean-cooking project in **Myanmar** (with South Korea); credits may be overstated 7-14 times.
- **GHG Protocol**: international accounting tool for governments and businesses to measure GHGs.
!TRAP 6.2 = bilateral (JCM); 6.4 = UN-run PACM.
### 4. PARIS AGREEMENT
- **Goal**: well **below 2°C**, pursue **1.5°C** (APPSC 2025).
- **INDCs** (Intended Nationally Determined Contributions): non-binding plans before Paris → **NDCs**, every **5 years**.
| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| 2.2 | CBDR-RC wording |
| 6.2 / 6.4 | Carbon markets |
| **9.1** | Finance from **developed countries** |
| **13** | **Enhanced Transparency Framework**; Biennial Transparency Reports (first due **end-2024**) |
| **14** | **Global Stocktake** every **5 years**; first at **COP28** → "severely off-track" → UAE Consensus |
| **28** | Withdrawal: notice **3 years** after entry into force; effect **1 year** after notice; no unanimity needed |
- **Ratchet mechanism**: each new NDC must **go beyond** the last, informed by the Global Stocktake.
- **NDC 3.0**: economy-wide, all-gas, 1.5°C-aligned; due for COP30.
#### 4.1 Legal Nature (G2 Mains 2025)
- A **legally binding treaty**, but the binding part is **procedural** (prepare, communicate, report NDCs).
- **NDC targets are not legally binding**: no party (**USA, China, India or EU**) is bound to its emission target.
!TRAP "Paris targets legally binding on..." → answer **"None of these"** (APPSC-G2 2025 key).
#### 4.2 India's NDC
| NDC | Emissions-intensity cut (2005 base) |
|---|---|
| INDC 2015 | **33-35%** by 2030 |
| Updated 2022 | **45%** by 2030 |
| 2031-35 NDC | **47%** by 2035 |
#### 4.3 US Withdrawal and Re-entry
- Withdrawal announced **2017**, effective **4 Nov 2020**; **rejoined 19 Feb 2021**; **left a second time in January 2026**.
- Presidential memorandum ends US participation in **UNFCCC, IPCC, ISA and IRENA**.

### 6. COP27, COP28, COP29, COP30 – DECISIONS
#### 6.1 COP27 Sharm el-Sheikh (2022)
- **Sharm el-Sheikh Implementation Plan**: historic decision on **loss and damage funding arrangements, incl. a fund**.
- **Mitigation Work Programme**: at least two global dialogues a year to 2030; strengthen 2030 targets by end-2023; **phase down unabated coal**, phase out inefficient fossil subsidies.
- **Reaffirmed** well below 2°C + pursuit of 1.5°C.
- Initiatives: **Sharm el-Sheikh Adaptation Agenda** (30 outcomes, 4 billion people by 2030); **AWARe** (water); **MARS** (UNEP methane satellite alerts); **IDRA**.
- APPSC-G1 2022: multi-statement COP27 question (statements I, III, IV correct).
#### 6.2 COP28 Dubai (2023)
- **First Global Stocktake** concluded. **UAE Consensus**: first COP decision on **"transitioning away from fossil fuels in energy systems"**.
- By 2030: **triple renewables to 11,000 GW**; **double energy-efficiency gains to 4%/yr**.
- **UAE Declaration on Climate and Health**: **India did not sign**.
- AP grassroots: **Saraswathi of Malluvalasa** (natural farming); APPSC-GS 2024 key: **"Sustaina India 2025"**.
#### 6.3 COP29 Baku (2024) – NCQG
- Old goal: **US$100 billion/yr** (unmet; eroded trust).
- **NCQG core**: developed countries to provide/mobilise at least **US$300 billion/yr by 2035**; broader **non-binding** call **US$1.3 trillion/yr** from all sources. India: **inadequate**.
#### 6.4 COP30 Belém (2025)
- **Belém Political Package** ("mutirão" spirit; 6 thematic axes).
- **At least triple adaptation finance**; target **US$120 billion/yr**; **59 voluntary indicators** under the Global Goal on Adaptation.
- **Baku-to-Belém Roadmap** to US$1.3 trillion; **Belém 4x pledge**: quadruple sustainable fuels by 2035.
!NUM $100 bn (old) → $300 bn core by 2035 → $1.3 trillion broader; adaptation $120 bn

### 10. IPCC – BODY, WORKING GROUPS AND REPORTS
- Set up **1988** by **WMO and UNEP**; Secretariat **Geneva**; **195 members**; **no original research** (assesses literature); each report has a **Summary for Policymakers** (not legally binding).
- **Nobel Peace Prize 2007**, shared with **Al Gore**.
- **WMO**: from IMO (**1873**); WMO **1951**; HQ **Geneva**.
| Working Group | Subject |
|---|---|
| **WG I** | Physical science basis |
| **WG II** | Impacts, adaptation, vulnerability |
| **WG III** | Mitigation |
| Task Force | National GHG Inventories |
#### 10.1 Assessment Reports
- **AR1 1990, AR2 1995, AR3 2001, AR4 2007, AR5 2014**.
| AR6 part | Released |
|---|---|
| WG I: Physical Science Basis | 9 Aug 2021 |
| WG II: Impacts, Adaptation, Vulnerability | 28 Feb 2022 |
| WG III: Mitigation | 4 Apr 2022 |
| **Synthesis Report** | **20 Mar 2023** |
- **AR7**: began **July 2023**; WG reports from ~mid-2028; Synthesis by late 2029; includes a **Special Report on Climate Change and Cities** (planned 2027).
#### 10.2 Key Findings
| Report | Finding |
|---|---|
| **SR1.5 (Oct 2018)** | ~1.0°C so far; for 1.5°C, net CO2 down **~45% from 2010 by 2030**; **net zero ~2050**; 1.5°C reached between **2030-2052**; cuts must be deep, rapid, sustained |
| **SRCCL (Aug 2019)** | Land use, desertification, degradation, food security |
| **SROCC (Sep 2019)** | Oceans took >90% excess heat; **marine heatwaves more frequent** (not fewer); coral bleaching, fisheries |
| **AR6 SYR (2023)** | **1.1°C** (2011-2020); emissions highest ever; **1.5°C likely crossed in early 2030s**; peak **before 2025**; net zero mid-century; **70-85%** renewable electricity by 2050 |
| AR6 WG I | Carbon budget **~500 GtCO2** from 2020 (50% chance of 1.5°C) |
#### 10.3 AR6 – India
- India **warming faster than global average**; more heatwaves; erratic monsoon; sea-level rise threatens **Mumbai and Chennai**.
- High mountains warming at **twice** the global rate; **Gangotri retreated ~1,700 m since 1935**; **black carbon on snow lowers albedo** → faster melting; up to 25% of mountain species may face extinction by 2100.

#### 11.3 Equity, Constraints and Justice
- India's per capita emissions **much lower** than industrialised nations (about 2.2 t CO2, 2024); **lowest per-capita** among listed (2012: below USA, UK, China).
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Historical responsibility | Developed countries emitted most of the GHG stock |
| **Carbon space** | Room for developing countries to grow |
| **Climate justice** | Those who emitted least suffer most; L&D Fund seen as ethical win |
- **Constraints**: few alternatives, restricted R&D, development needs, **coal dependence**; wants clean, affordable **technology and finance**.
- Coal measures: coal washing, ESPs, gasification, wet scrubbers, low-NOx burners, CCS/CCUS; **not ocean fertilisation**. LINGO carbon bombs: **Gevra, Rajmahal**.
- **Net zero 2070** (later than developed 2050).
- Sea-level-rise refugees fall **outside the 1951 Refugee Convention**.
