## U0347 | NATIONAL COMMISSION FOR SCs (338), STs (338A) AND BACKWARD CLASSES (338B); SPECIAL OFFICER FOR LINGUISTIC MINORITIES (350B)
### 1. ARTICLE AND AMENDMENT – EVOLUTION
| Body | Article | Created / changed by |
|---|---|---|
| Special Officer (Commissioner) for SCs and STs | 338 (original) | Constitution, 1950 |
| Combined National Commission for SCs and STs | 338 | **65th Amendment, 1990** |
| **NCSC** (separate) | 338 | **89th Amendment, 2003** (bifurcation); from 2004 |
| **NCST** | **338A** | **89th Amendment, 2003**; set up **19 Feb 2004** |
| **NCBC** | **338B** | **102nd Amendment Act, 2018** |
| Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities | **350B** | **7th Amendment, 1956** |
- 65th Amendment: multi-member constitutional commission replaced the Special Officer and the commission set up by the **1987 Resolution**.
- **1999**: separate **Ministry of Tribal Affairs** → opened the way for a separate NCST.
!TRAP NCST = 89th Amendment, not the 65th. Combined commission = 65th.
### 2. COMPOSITION
- NCSC / NCST / constitutional NCBC (each): **Chairperson + Vice-Chairperson + 3 members** (five).
- Appointed by the **President** by **warrant under his hand and seal**; service conditions and tenure fixed by the President; **term 3 years** under the rules.
!TRAP Statutory NCBC (1993) = **5 members** headed by a **retired SC/HC judge**; constitutional NCBC (338B) = Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson + 3.
### 3. FUNCTIONS
1. **Investigate and monitor** constitutional safeguards.
2. **Inquire into specific complaints** of deprivation of rights.
3. **Advise on socio-economic planning** and evaluate progress; recommend measures.
4. **Report annually** (or when it deems fit) to the **President** → laid before Parliament with an action-taken memorandum; State-related reports go to the **Governor**.
- **Mandatory consultation**: Union and every State must consult on major policy matters – **Art. 338(9)** (SCs), **338A(9)** (STs); no prior approval or list-changing power.
- **NCST extra**: ownership of **minor forest produce**, mineral and water rights, **rehabilitation of displaced tribals**.
- **De-reservation** proposals are first examined by NCSC/NCST/NCBC; in 2026 the NCSC began a full review (13 Aug 2026).
- NCSC acts "under the powers conferred by **Article 338**" (27 Apr 2026).
- NOT criminal courts; **no power to punish**; recommendations **advisory**.
!TRAP APPSC 2022: Reports go to the **President**, not the Prime Minister.
### 4. CIVIL COURT POWERS
- Under **Art. 338(8), 338A, 338B**, while investigating or inquiring:
- **Summon** persons and examine on oath; **require documents**; **receive affidavits**; **requisition records**; **issue commissions** for examining witnesses.
- APPSC-G1 2022: all civil-court powers listed were correct.
- NHRC and NCM also have civil-court powers in inquiries (the NCM Act gives them).
### 5. NCBC – CASE TO CONSTITUTION
| Case / Act / Amendment | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **Indra Sawhney v UoI** | **16 Nov 1992** | Upheld **27%** OBC quota; **creamy layer**; ordered a **permanent body** for OBC lists |
| **NCBC Act** | **1993** (in force 2 April 1993) | **Statutory** NCBC, 5 members |
| **102nd Amendment** | **2018** | Art. 338B; NCBC **constitutional**; **President notifies SEBCs**; took backward classes out of NCSC's scope |
| **105th Amendment** | **2021** | Restored **States' power** to make their own SEBC lists (after the Maratha quota judgment) |
- AP aligned its definitions with central thresholds (Apr 2026).
!KEY APPSC 2022: NCBC got constitutional status by the **102nd Amendment**.

### 7. SPECIAL OFFICER FOR LINGUISTIC MINORITIES – ART. 350B
- Also called the **Commissioner for Linguistic Minorities**; inserted with Art. 350A by the **7th Amendment, 1956**.
- **Appointed by the President**; investigates safeguards for linguistic minorities and **reports to the President**, who lays reports before Parliament and sends them to States.
- Works under the **Ministry of Minority Affairs**; headquarters **New Delhi** (moved from Allahabad, 2015).
- **Art. 350A**: States to provide **instruction in the mother tongue at the primary stage** for linguistic minority children.
!PAIR Art. 350A | mother-tongue instruction | primary stage || Art. 350B | Special Officer | reports to President

### 9. AP ANGLE
- **AP State Commission for SCs and STs**: created by a **2003** State Act.
- Split into two: Assembly passed separate **SC and ST Commission Bills** (Dec 2019); **AP State Commission for Scheduled Tribes Act, 2020**.
- AP also has a **State Minorities Commission**; AP SC Commission field reviews (Aug 2026).
