## U0344 | FINANCE COMMISSION – COMPOSITION, TERMS OF REFERENCE, FUNCTIONS; 15TH AND 16TH FINANCE COMMISSION
### 1. NATURE AND CONSTITUTIONAL BASIS
- Finance Commission (FC) = **constitutional body** under **Article 280**, **Part XII**.
- **Quasi-judicial** body; the anchor of **fiscal federalism**.
- **Not permanent**: constituted by the **President** within two years of the start of the Constitution, then **every fifth year or earlier** when necessary (Art. 280(1)).
- Independent; recommendations **advisory, not binding**.
- **P.V. Rajamannar** (4th FC) view: recommendations should not be rejected without very compelling reasons.
#### 1.1 Articles – Number Game
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| 280 | Constitution, composition and functions of the FC |
| 280(2) | **Parliament** fixes qualifications by law |
| 281 | **President** lays recommendations + **explanatory memorandum** on action taken before **each House of Parliament** |
| 290 | **Pensions** – NOT the FC |
!TRAP APPSC-G2 2025: "Article 290 provides for the Finance Commission" is INCORRECT – it is Article 280.
### 2. COMPOSITION AND QUALIFICATIONS
- **Chairman + four other members** (5 in all), appointed by the **President** (Art. 280(1)).
- Term: as the **President specifies**; members are **eligible for reappointment**; no fixed term in the Constitution.
- Qualifications fixed by **Parliament** in the **Finance Commission (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1951** – NOT by the President or the Union Cabinet.
- Has **powers of a civil court**: summoning persons, production of documents.
#### 2.1 Qualifications – FC (Misc. Provisions) Act, 1951
| Post | Qualification |
|---|---|
| Chairman | Experience in **public affairs** |
| Member | **High Court judge** or qualified to be one |
| Member | Special knowledge of **government finance and accounts** |
| Member | Wide experience in **financial matters and administration** |
| Member | Special knowledge of **economics** |
- **No** member post for an expert in **monetary policy operations**.
!TRAP NOT "a Chairman and six members"; the FC does NOT report to NITI Aayog – it reports to the President.
### 3. FUNCTIONS – ARTICLE 280(3)
1. **Distribution of net proceeds of taxes** between Centre and States (**vertical**) and among States (**horizontal**).
2. **Principles of grants-in-aid** to States from the **Consolidated Fund of India** (Art. 275).
3. **Measures to augment the Consolidated Fund of a State** to supplement resources of **Panchayats and Municipalities** (on the basis of State Finance Commission recommendations).
4. **Any other matter** referred by the President in the interest of sound finance.
- Main function: decide the **share of States in central taxes**.
- **Divisible pool** = gross tax revenue − **cesses, surcharges** and cost of collection; net amount certified by the **CAG**.
- Rising **cesses and surcharges** shrink the divisible pool.
!KEY The FC does not fix GST rates and does not itself release funds; it only recommends.

### 5. 14TH FINANCE COMMISSION (2015–20)
- Constituted **2 January 2013**; recommendations for **1 April 2015 – 31 March 2020**; GoI accepted the report on **24 February 2015**.
- Share raised **32% → 42%** = **largest increase ever**.
- Made **tax devolution** the main route (not grants); ignored the **Plan/non-Plan** distinction.
- Made **no distinction** between **special and general category** States.
- Population base: **1971 and 2011**.
- Criteria: income distance **50%**, population 1971 **17.5%**, population 2011 **10%**, area **15%**, forest cover **7.5%**.
- Local-body grants **₹2,87,436 crore** (panchayats ₹2,00,292 crore), as basic and performance grants.
- After 42%, the Centre increased **non-shareable cesses**.
### 6. 15TH FINANCE COMMISSION (2020–26)
- Formed **27 November 2017**; Chairman **N.K. Singh**.
- **First FC to cut** the vertical share: **42% → 41%** due to the change in **J&K's status**.
- Used **2011 population** only (not 1971).
- **Added** two new criteria: **demographic performance** and **tax effort**.
- Local-body grants **₹4.36 lakh crore**; urban local bodies share **36%**.

#### 7.1 Meaning
!PAIR Vertical devolution | Centre ↔ States | share of divisible pool (41%) || Horizontal devolution | among States | criteria and weights

| Criterion | 15th FC | 16th FC |
|---|---|---|
| **Income distance** | 45 | **42.5** |
| Population (2011) | 15 | 17.5 |
| Area | 15 | 10 |

- 16th FC is the **first** to use a **Contribution to GDP** criterion.
- 16th FC forest criterion now includes **open forests** too.

### 8. 16TH FINANCE COMMISSION (2026–31)
#### 8.1 Constitution and Terms of Reference
- Constituted **31 December 2023**; Chairman **Arvind Panagariya**; award **2026-27 to 2030-31**.
- Members: **T. Rabi Sankar, Annie George Mathew, Manoj Panda, Soumya Kanti Ghosh**.
- Terms of reference came straight from the **Constitution**, without detailed Central directives.
- Population base 2011 (demographic performance measured against 1971).
#### 8.2 Report and Recommendations
- Report to the President **November 2025**; tabled in Parliament **1 February 2026** (Budget day).
- Devolution accepted by the Centre in the **Budget 2026-27** speech; States got the full 41%; total transfers **₹25.44 lakh crore** for 2026-27.
- 41% applies only to the **divisible pool** (excludes cesses, surcharges, cost of collection).
- Total grants **₹9.47 lakh crore**; **revenue-deficit grants discontinued**.
- **Local-body grants ₹7.91 lakh crore**: rural ~**₹4.35 lakh crore**, urban ~**₹3.56 lakh crore** (= **45%**); urbanisation premium **₹10,000 crore**.
- **Disaster corpus ₹2.04 lakh crore**; sharing **90:10** NE/Himalayan States, **75:25** others.
- **Heatwaves and lightning** proposed as new notified disasters.
- **State deficits capped at 3% of GSDP**.
- All five **southern States** gained: Karnataka **4.131%** (15th: 3.64%; 14th: 4.71%), Tamil Nadu **4.097%**.
- **Kerala**: fiscal stress – neither revenue deficit grants nor State-specific grants.
#### 8.3 Criticism and Debates
- Rising **cesses and surcharges** are kept out of the divisible pool, so the real share of States in gross tax revenue is lower than 41%.
- After the 14th FC raised the share to 42%, the Centre increased **non-shareable cesses**.
- Southern States call the use of population and income distance **unfair** to better-performing States, even though all five gained under the 16th FC.
- Ending **revenue-deficit grants** hurts States such as **Andhra Pradesh** and **Kerala**, which must now cut their revenue deficits on their own.
- Local-body grants need **duly constituted** elected bodies, which pushes States to hold timely ULB and panchayat elections.
!KEY Devolution is "tax sharing" (formula-based, untied); grants are "transfers for a purpose" – the 14th FC shifted the weight from grants to tax sharing.

- Grants under **Art. 275** are for needy States; the FC does not decide **Plan** transfers.
### 10. SPECIAL CATEGORY STATUS
- Introduced in **1969** on the recommendation of the **5th Finance Commission** (Mahavir Tyagi); funds allotted by the **Gadgil formula** (NDC).
- First States: **Assam, Jammu & Kashmir, Nagaland**.
- Criteria: **hilly and difficult terrain**, low population density or large **tribal** share, **strategic border** location, economic and infrastructure **backwardness**.
- **14th FC** made no distinction between special and general category States.
