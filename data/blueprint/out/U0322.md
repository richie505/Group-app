## U0322 | DISTRIBUTION OF EXECUTIVE POWERS BETWEEN THE UNION AND THE STATES
### 1. UNION EXECUTIVE – PRESIDENT
- **Art. 52:** there shall be a President of India.
- **Art. 53(1):** executive power of the Union **vests in the President**, exercised **directly or through subordinate officers**, per the Constitution (so **not unlimited**).
- **Art. 53(2):** **Supreme Commander** of the armed forces, **regulated by law**.
- President = **nominal / constitutional head**; **Head of State**, not of Government; **First Citizen**; **Rank 1** in Order of Precedence; part of Parliament (**Art. 79**).
- Real executive: **Council of Ministers** under the **PM** (**Art. 74(1)**), collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha.
- **Art. 77:** all executive action is taken **in the President's name**.
### 2. EXTENT OF EXECUTIVE POWER
| Article | Level | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **73** | Union | Matters on which **Parliament** can legislate + **treaty/agreement** rights |
| **154** | State | Vests in the **Governor** |
| **162** | State | Matters within **State Legislature's** competence, **within the State** – heading "**Extension of Executive Power of State**" |
| **163(1)** | State | CoM advises the Governor, except **discretionary** matters |
| **371A / 371H** | State | Governor's **special responsibility** for law and order: **Nagaland / Arunachal Pradesh** |
!TRAP Art. 154 vests power in the Governor, though real power lies with the Council of Ministers.
- **Art. 161 remission:** Governor bound by **State Cabinet advice** (SC, Apr 2026).
### 3. CONCURRENT LIST – WHO EXECUTES?
- **Default:** executive power stays with the **States**.
- **Exception:** Centre executes where the **Constitution or a Parliamentary law** expressly says (proviso to **Arts 73 and 162**).
### 4. ENTRUSTMENT AND COMPLIANCE
| Article | Direction | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **258(1)** | Union → State | **President**, with the **State's consent** |
| **258(2)** | Union → State | **Parliament by law**, **no consent** needed |
| **258A** | State → Union | State entrusts functions with the **Union's consent** (APPSC-GS 2023) |
| **256 / 257(1)** | States | Comply with Union laws; must **not impede** Union executive power |
| **365** | Non-compliance | Breakdown of machinery → opens **Art. 356** |
!TRAP 258A is NOT about river valley agreements.
### 5. EMERGENCIES AND EXECUTIVE RELATIONS
| Emergency | Effect |
|---|---|
| **National (352, via 353)** | Union directs any State on **any matter**; State governments **not suspended** |
| **President's Rule (356)** | State **CoM dismissed**; **High Court untouched** |
| **Financial (360)** | **Financial propriety**; Money and Financial Bills reserved; **salary cuts of State employees** |
- Financial Emergency **never used** so far.
- Criticism: **258(2)** and **365** let the Union act without State consent.
### 6. UNION TERRITORIES
- **Art. 239:** UTs run by the **President** through an **Administrator / Lieutenant Governor**.
- **69th Amendment, 1991:** Delhi made **National Capital Territory**; **Art. 239AA** – Assembly + Council of Ministers.
- Outside Delhi Assembly's power: **public order, police, land** (not public health).
- **Ladakh (2026):** **Art. 371-like safeguards** instead of the Sixth Schedule (Jul); **sui generis** elected body (Sep).
