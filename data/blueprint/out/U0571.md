## U0571 | URBANISATION AND SMART CITIES IN AP – AMARAVATI CAPITAL REGION, VISAKHAPATNAM, KAKINADA, TIRUPATI
### 1. URBANISATION IN ANDHRA PRADESH
- **Census 2011** (residuary 13-district AP): urban population **1.46 crore** of 4.96 crore = about **29.5%** (24.1% in 2001); India average **31.2%**.
- Most urbanised district: **Visakhapatnam (47.45%)**; second **Krishna (40.81%)**.
- Most districts stay **below the national average**; West Godavari only about 20.6% urban.
!TRAP AP's urban share (~29.5%) is BELOW the all-India figure (31.2%), not above it.
### 2. AMARAVATI AND APCRDA
#### 2.1 APCRDA
- **APCRDA** = **Andhra Pradesh Capital Region Development Authority**, formed under the **APCRDA Act, 2014**; headquarters **Rayapudi**.
- Location: **greenfield city on the Krishna**, between **Vijayawada and Guntur**, in **Thullur mandal**.
- Developed on the banks of the Krishna as **India's first smart capital**.
#### 2.2 Area
| Unit | Area |
|---|---|
| Capital region | **8,352.69 sq km** (expanded from 7,068.20; Guntur, Palnadu, Bapatla and others; Aug 2026) |
| Capital city | **217.23 sq km** (about 217 sq km) across **29 villages** |
!TRAP Region 8,352.69 sq km vs city 217.23 sq km – do not mix.
#### 2.3 Master Plan
- Master plan: **Surbana Jurong (Singapore)**.
- **Water-centric** city; **51% green/blue space**; **30-minute-city** design.

### 5. LAND POOLING, WORKS AND FUNDING
#### 5.1 Land Pooling
- **LPS Phase I**: **93.1% of 37,921 acres** pooled; **70,550 returnable plots** allotted (62,150 registered).
- **LPS 2.0**: about **16,666 acres** in **7 villages** (begun Apr 2026).
- Cabinet approved land pooling of **6,212.50 acres** for the **Inner Ring Road** (4 Sep 2026).
#### 5.2 Works
- **96 works** worth **₹57,592.71 crore** sanctioned; 85 worth ₹56,288 crore contracted.

### 6. SMART CITIES MISSION IN AP
- AP has **4 smart cities**: **Visakhapatnam, Kakinada, Tirupati, Amaravati**.
- **Visakhapatnam** and **Kakinada** were in the **first round (Jan 2016)**; **Amaravati** joined in the third round (June 2017).
- By July 2025, 280 projects worth about ₹6,589 crore taken up; Kakinada and Tirupati lead, Amaravati lags.
!TRAP Vijayawada and Guntur are NOT Smart Cities Mission cities in AP.

### 9. CITY ECONOMIC REGIONS AND RANKINGS
#### 9.1 City Economic Regions
- **Three City Economic Regions** set up (7 Jul 2026): **Visakhapatnam, Amaravati, Tirupati**.
- **Visakhapatnam ER**: **10 districts**, **NITI Aayog pilot**, launched 13 Nov 2025, has a master plan; target **US$750–800 billion by 2047**.
- Amaravati and Tirupati ER master plans due within 6 months.
- Criticism: **land-pooling farmers' grievances**; 762 MLD urban water deficit; no PMAY-U 2.0 proposal yet for Amaravati (31 Jul 2026).
