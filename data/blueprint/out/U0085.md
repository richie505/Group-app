## U0085 | KALYANI (WESTERN) CHALUKYAS – TAILAPA II, VIKRAMADITYA VI, BILHANA, MITAKSHARA
### 1. DYNASTY AT A GLANCE
- Also called the **Western Chalukyas**; ruled c. **973–1189 CE** (543–753 CE is **Badami**, not Kalyani).
- Capital: **Kalyani** = present **Basavakalyan** (Karnataka); made capital under Someshvara I (some books credit Tailapa II).
- Branches in order: **Badami** (6th–8th c.) → **Vengi** (7th–11th c.) → **Kalyani** (late 10th–12th c.).
- Significance: **checked Chola expansion northwards**; Mitakshara shaped Hindu inheritance law for about a millennium.
### 2. RULERS → PERIOD AND ACHIEVEMENT
Order: **Tailapa II → Satyasraya → Someshvara I → Vikramaditya VI**.
| Ruler | Achievement |
|---|---|
| **Tailapa II** (973) | Founder; overthrew last Rashtrakuta **Karka II** in **973 CE** |
| Satyasraya | Sent **Bayyalanambi** against Vengi (1006); repulsed by Saktivarman I with Chola help |
| **Someshvara I Ahavamalla** (1042–68) | Killed Chola **Rajadhiraja I** at **Koppam** (1053); title **Vengi Purveshwara**; granted **Hanamkonda** to Kakatiya **Prola I** |
| **Vikramaditya VI** (**1076–1126**) | Greatest; ruled 50 years; title **Tribhuvanamalla**; started Chalukya-Vikrama era; controlled the Vengi feudatories; court of Bilhana and Vijnaneshwara |
| **Someshvara III** (1126–1138) | Wrote **Manasollasa** |
#### 2.1 Chalukya-Vikrama Era
- Founded by **Vikramaditya VI** in **1076 CE** (his accession); replaced the **Saka era** in royal records.
!TRAP Era start 1076 = Vikramaditya VI, not Tailapa II (973).
### 3. CHOLA-CHALUKYA WARS
- Cause: rivalry over **Vengi** (coastal Andhra) and the Raichur doab.
- **Koppam** (1053): Someshvara I killed Rajadhiraja I; Rajendra II succeeded him.
- **Kudalasangamam** (c. 1062): at the **Krishna–Tungabhadra** confluence; Cholas heavily defeated Someshvara I.
### 4. LITERATURE → AUTHORS
| Work | Author | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Vikramankadevacharita** | **Bilhana** (Kashmiri) | Biography of Vikramaditya VI; reign likened to **Ramarajya**; Chalukya origin at "Brahmachulakam" |
| **Mitakshara** | **Vijnaneshwara** | At Vikramaditya VI's court; commentary on **Yajnavalkya Smriti** |
| **Manasollasa** (Abhilashitartha Chintamani) | **Someshvara III** | 100-chapter encyclopaedia: statecraft, agriculture, medicine, astronomy, music, dance, cuisine |
| Kavyavalokana, Karnataka Bhashabhushana | **Nagavarma II** | Kannada |
| Vachana literature | Basavanna, Allama, Akka | Virashaiva movement |
- Languages patronised: **Sanskrit** (Bilhana, Vijnaneshwara) and **Kannada** (Nagavarma II).

### 6. ART – GADAG STYLE
- Mature **Vesara (Gadag)** style in **chloritic schist (soapstone)**, not granite; **star-shaped plans** later a Hoysala model.
- Features: decorated doorways and pillars, detailed wall designs, stepped superstructures.
