### 1. AMARAVATI – THE CAPITAL STORY
#### 1.1 Timeline
| Year / Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 1956 | Andhra Pradesh formed |
| **2014** | **AP Reorganisation Act** (made under **Article 3**; created Telangana); **Section 5**: **Hyderabad** = **common capital** for **not more than 10 years** from **2 June 2014** (till 2024); it **named no capital** – only said "there shall be a new capital" |
| 2019 | Then government proposed **three capitals**: **Visakhapatnam (executive)**, **Amaravati (legislative)**, **Kurnool (judicial)** |
| 2020 | **AP Decentralisation and Inclusive Development of All Regions Act, 2020** – **repealed in 2021**; judicial capital never operationalised |
| 2022 | **AP High Court**: State **lacked legislative competence** to shift the capital; **legitimate expectation** of land-pooling farmers; rule of law; judicial review under **Articles 32 & 226** |
| **28 Mar 2026** | AP Legislative Assembly **resolution** asking for the amendment – reflects **cooperative federalism** |
| **1–2 Apr 2026** | **Parliament passes** the **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Bill, 2026** (Lok Sabha 1 April, Rajya Sabha 2 April) |
| Assent | President **Droupadi Murmu** (**Article 111**) → **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Act, 2026**; in force after Gazette notification by the **Ministry of Law** |
| Effect | **Retrospective from 2 June 2024** |
#### 1.2 What the 2026 Amendment Does
- Amends **Section 5 (5(2))** of the 2014 Act: inserts the words **"at Amaravati"** – "there shall be a new capital" → **"Amaravati shall be the new capital"**.
- Declares Amaravati the **sole and permanent capital**; **future changes to the capital area need Parliament's approval**.
- **Amaravati** = the area notified under the **APCRDA Act, 2014**.
- Broad support in Parliament, except **YSR Congress Party** (had pushed the three-capital model).
#### 1.3 Amaravati Capital Project
- Planned area ~**217 sq km**; developed through the **Land Pooling Scheme** (farmers get developed plots + compensation).
- **APCRDA Act, 2014**: special planning body – **Town Planning Schemes**, **Betterment Charges**; overriding powers over Gram Panchayats in the capital region.
- APCRDA hired a consultant (**Mar–Apr 2026**) for an Amaravati **governance framework** in line with the **73rd and 74th Amendments** (local self-government).
- 2014 Act: the Governor of the common capital (Hyderabad) had **special responsibility for law and order and internal security**; **institutions of national importance stay with the Union**.
!TRAP The 2014 Act did **not** name Amaravati or any city; the **Constitution does not specify** how a State capital is chosen – statutory law fills the gap. The three-capital plan was a **later** proposal.
#### 1.4 National Judicial Academy and India's First Justice City
- CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu** urged the **Supreme Court** to set up the **National Judicial Academy (NJA)** in Amaravati, at a state-level **mediation conference** of the **AP State Legal Services Authority**; chief guest **CJI Justice Surya Kant**.
- State offer: land, full administrative support; Amaravati as a **Model Judicial City** – a futuristic **blue-green field capital** aligned with **Viksit Bharat–2047**.
- AP is set to set up **India's first Justice City** (capital region): an integrated legal hub – **High Court, NJA, Legal Services Authority, Mediation Centre, International Law University, Bar Council of India**, law colleges.
- CJI: **mediation is a pillar of justice**, not merely an alternative mechanism; called for rigorous mediator training and transparent selection.

#### 4.3 AP Electronic Processes Rules, 2025 – e-Summons via WhatsApp
- Full name: **AP Electronic Processes (Issuance, Service and Execution) Rules, 2025** – made by the **AP High Court**.
- Gazette: **G.O.Ms.No.107, Home (Courts-B)**, dated **22 May 2026**.
- Summons, warrants, notices via **WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram, email**; encrypted, **digitally signed** by the judicial officer/court superintendent.
- Proof: police submit screenshot/delivery report; **physical service continues where e-service fails**.
- Background: **IT Act, 2000** – legal recognition of electronic records and signatures; during **COVID-19** the SC allowed service by email, WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal.

### 6. SOCIAL WELFARE, DISABILITY AND WOMEN
#### 6.1 Divyang Shakti – Free Bus Travel
- Inaugurated **18 March 2026** at **Mangalagiri (Guntur district)** by CM Naidu; free travel on **RTC buses** for differently-abled persons.
- Eligibility: **more than 40% disability**; **21 categories**; helpers may accompany.
- Buses: **City Ordinary, Metro Express, Palle Velugu, Ultra Palle Velugu, Express** – urban and rural.
- ~**12.76 lakh** beneficiaries; ~**₹207 crore** a year.
#### 6.2 Disability Framework and Akshaya Kshetram
- **Akshaya Kshetram** (near Tirumala, Tirupati): residential home for persons with **intellectual disabilities**; founded **2 October 1996** (Gandhi Jayanti) as a **daycare centre**; completed **30 years** (awareness campaign, 5 km walk).
- **RPwD Act, 2016**: categories **7 → 21**. **Accessible India Campaign** = **Sugamya Bharat Abhiyan**. **DDRS** (Deendayal Disabled Rehabilitation Scheme) funds NGOs.
- **National Trust Act, 1999**: **four** disabilities – autism, cerebral palsy, mental retardation, multiple disabilities. **UNCRPD**: India signatory – rights-based.
