## U0682 | TRANSPORT IN INDIA – WATERWAYS, SHIPPING, PORTS AND CIVIL AVIATION
### 1. SHIPPING AND PORT TYPES
- Shipping carries about **95%** of India's trade by **volume** and **68%** by **value** (2026).
| Port type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Natural** (sheltered, west coast) | **Mumbai, Mormugao, Kochi** |
| **Artificial** (east coast) | **Chennai** (oldest), **VOC Tuticorin**, New Mangalore (lagoon) |
| **Landlocked**, deepest | **Visakhapatnam** |
| **Riverine** | **Kolkata-Haldia** |
| **Tidal** | **Deendayal (Kandla)** |
| **Corporatised** | **Kamarajar** (Ennore) |

### 3. EAST VS WEST COAST – PHYSICAL REASONS
- East coast has fewer ports: **shallow continental shelf** + **cyclones and storms** (APPSC 2023 key: statements 1 and 2).
- **Deep water close to shore** is found on the **west coast**, not the east.
- West = sheltered natural harbours; east = mostly artificial.
!TRAP "Deep water close to the shore on the east coast" is FALSE.
### 4. PORT TRAFFIC AND RANKINGS
- Major ports: record **915.17 million tonnes** in **2025-26**, up **7.06%**; beat **904 mt** target (6 Apr 2026).
- **Largest cargo-handling major port: Paradip** (2026); it overtook **Deendayal**, which was the APPSC 2019 key.
- **Largest container port: JNPA.**
| LPPI 2024-25 | Leader |
|---|---|
| Container | **Mundra** |
| Dry bulk | **Paradip** |
| Liquid bulk | **Sikka** |
- World Bank **CPPI 2024**: 2 Indian ports in top 30, 7 in top 100; turnaround ~30 hours.
- **States with three major ports:** **Tamil Nadu** (Chennai, Kamarajar, VOC; APPSC 2018 key) and **Maharashtra** (Mumbai, JNPA, Vadhavan).
!TRAP Largest container port = JNPA; LPPI container leader = Mundra.
### 5. NATIONAL WATERWAYS
- **IWAI** set up **1986**, HQ Noida; **National Waterways Act 2016** declared **111 NWs**.
| NW | Route | Length |
|---|---|---|
| **NW-1** | Ganga-Bhagirathi-Hooghly, **Haldia-Prayagraj** | **1,620 km** |
| **NW-2** | Brahmaputra, **Dhubri-Sadiya** (Assam) | **891 km** |
| **NW-3** | West Coast Canal, **Kollam-Kottapuram** (Kerala) | **205 km** |
| **NW-4** | **Kakinada-Puducherry**, Godavari-Krishna + canals (AP, TN) | - |
| **NW-5** | **Talcher/Angul - Paradeep/Dhamra**, Brahmani-Mahanadi (Odisha) | - |
- NW-1 terminals include **Haldia, Kolkata, Varanasi, Prayagraj**; NW-2 terminals include **Dhubri, Jogighopa, Tezpur, Dibrugarh, Sadiya**.
- **Coastal shipping** grew **118%** in a decade.

### 7. TRANSSHIPMENT AND SHIPBUILDING HUBS
| Hub | Location | Facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Vizhinjam** | Near Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala | India's **first deep-water transshipment hub** + **first semi-automated port**; natural depth **18-20 m** close to shore; **DBFOT** project of Kerala Govt + **Adani Ports**; customs port by CBIC (Aug 2026) |
| **Galathea Bay** | Great Nicobar, near **Malacca Strait** | Container Transshipment Terminal under the ₹72,000-crore Great Nicobar Project; **leatherback turtle** nesting site (not olive ridley) |
- Nearest to Great Nicobar: **Sumatra** (~1,192 km); Sri Lanka ~1,437 km, Java ~1,817 km, Borneo ~2,398 km.
- Great Nicobar Project faulted for threatening **tribal reserves and biodiversity**; protests against **Vadhavan** at Dahanu.
- AP seeks central support for a **Dugarajapatnam** mega shipbuilding cluster.
### 8. CIVIL AVIATION
- **First flight:** airmail **Allahabad (Prayagraj) - Naini, 18 Feb 1911**.
- **Nationalised 1953** under the **Air Corporations Act** → **Indian Airlines** + **Air India International**.
- **UDAN** (Ude Desh ka Aam Nagrik): launched **2016**; works through **viability gap funding**.
- Operational airports **74 (2014) → 164 (2025)**; India = **3rd largest domestic** aviation market.
- **Cochin (CIAL)** = world's **first fully solar-powered airport**.
- **Busiest airport:** **Delhi IGI** (~78.1 million passengers in 2025; 11th in world); Mumbai 2nd (~55.5 million).
