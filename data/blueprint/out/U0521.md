## U0521 | INDIA AND THE WTO - AGREEMENTS, MINISTERIAL CONFERENCES, PUBLIC STOCKHOLDING, FISHERIES SUBSIDIES, APPELLATE BODY CRISIS
### 1. GATT - THE PREDECESSOR
- **Earlier name of the WTO = GATT** (General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade).
- Signed **30 October 1947** at **Geneva** by **23** founding contracting parties; tariff concessions in force by **30 June 1948** through a "**Protocol of Provisional Application**"; GATT office at Geneva, 1948.
- Focus: cut **tariffs and quantitative restrictions**; **8 rounds** of negotiations. GATT survives as part of the WTO agreements.
- India: GATT member since **8 July 1948**; founding member of **both GATT and WTO**.
!TRAP GATT founders = 23; "110 countries" is later membership.
| Basis | GATT | WTO |
|---|---|---|
| Set up | 1947 (in force 1948) | **1 January 1995** (not 1994) |
| Nature | An agreement | An **international organisation** |
| Coverage | Mainly goods | Goods, **services, IPR, investment** |
### 2. URUGUAY ROUND AND DUNKEL DRAFT
- **8th GATT round**; launched **September 1986** at **Punta del Este**; concluded **1993**; **123** contracting parties.
- Took rules beyond goods: **GATS** (services), **TRIPS** (IPR), **TRIMS** (investment). Aims: cut farm subsidies, open services, cover investment and IPR.
- **Arthur Dunkel**: GATT DG (1980-1993) and chair of the Trade Negotiations Committee. **Dunkel Draft** (Draft Final Act) tabled **20 December 1991**; linked to IPR; seen as the last chance for a breakthrough.
- Per key: acceptance compulsory for India in all sectors; agriculture - cut in farm subsidies; textiles - did not repeat the **Multi-Fibre Arrangement**.
- **Marrakesh Agreement** (signed April 1994) created the WTO.
### 3. WTO BASICS
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Established | **1 January 1995** |
| HQ | **Geneva** (ILO Geneva; UN New York; FAO Rome) |
| Members | **166** (April 2026); 164 from 29 July 2016 (**Afghanistan 164th**); **Nepal 147th** (23 April 2004); **Serbia** = observer only |
| DG | **Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala** (Nigeria) - **7th DG, first woman**, appointed Feb 2021, took over March 2021 |
| Earlier DG | **Roberto Azevedo** (Brazil), replaced Pascal Lamy on 1 Sep 2013; 6th DG; second term 2017 |
- WTO = **third pillar** of the world economy, with the **IMF and World Bank**; "security guard" of world trade; largest intergovernmental body regulating trade.
- Aims: free trade and resource flows, IPR protection, balanced trade (all correct).
- WTO **does** have a dispute-settlement system.
- India: **18th** in merchandise exports, 1.8% share (April 2025).
#### 3.1 Principles
| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **MFN** | Advantage given to one partner must go to **all** WTO partners (non-discrimination among partners) |
| **National treatment** | Imported product, once inside, treated like the domestic product (tax, regulation) |
- US unilateral tariffs (2025-26) violate MFN and bound-rate obligations.
### 4. MINISTERIAL CONFERENCES
- **Highest decision-making body** (trade ministers); meets **at least once every two years**.
| MC | Venue, year | Key outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **MC1** | **Singapore, 1996** | First MC; "**Singapore issues**" (investment, competition, procurement transparency, trade facilitation); **ITA** |
| MC2 | Geneva, 1998 | **E-commerce customs-duty moratorium** begun |
| MC3 | Seattle, 1999 | Collapsed amid protests |
| **MC4** | **Doha, 2001** | **Doha Development Agenda**; TRIPS-public health declaration |
| MC5 | Cancun, 2003 | Collapsed; **G-33** formed |
| MC6 | Hong Kong, 2005 | Farm export subsidies to end; duty-free quota-free for LDCs |
| MC7-8 | Geneva, 2009, 2011 | Stock-taking |
| **MC9** | **Bali, 2013** | **Bali Package: TFA + interim Peace Clause** |
| **MC10** | **Nairobi, 2015** | **Elimination of agricultural export subsidies** |
| MC11 | Buenos Aires, 2017 | No declaration; fisheries work programme |
| **MC12** | **Geneva, June 2022** | **Fisheries Subsidies Agreement** (Phase 1); COVID TRIPS waiver; moratorium extended |
| **MC13** | **Abu Dhabi, 26 Feb-2 Mar 2024** | Moratorium extended to 31 Mar 2026; no PSH solution |
| **MC14** | **Yaounde, Cameroon, 26-29 Mar 2026** (closed 30 Mar) | **No declaration** (only a draft "Yaounde package"); moratoria lapsed; seen as a failure |
- MC14: **India blocked** adding the **Investment Facilitation for Development (IFD)** plurilateral to **Annex 4**, though **129 of 166** backed it; **TRIPS non-violation moratorium** also lapsed.
!KEY MC9 Bali 2013 = TFA + Peace Clause; MC12 Geneva 2022 = AFS; MC14 Yaounde 2026 = no declaration.
### 5. DOHA ROUND AND COUNTRY GROUPS
- **Doha Round** (launched at the 4th MC, 2001): "development round"; **central issue agriculture**; also NAMA, services; **still unfinished**.
| Group | Members / focus |
|---|---|
| **G-33** | 2003 (Doha process); 40+ developing countries incl. India; **food security, Special Safeguard Mechanism, special products** |
| **NAMA-11** | Developing countries (incl. India) in Non-Agricultural Market Access talks; seek flexibilities to limit opening in industrial goods |
| **G-90** | African Group + ACP countries + LDCs |
- **Special Safeguard Mechanism**: lets developing countries raise tariffs briefly against import surges or price falls.
### 6. AGREEMENT ON AGRICULTURE
- Negotiated in the Uruguay Round; in force **1 January 1995**. **Three pillars: domestic support, market access, export competition** - NOT anti-dumping/compensatory duties.
#### 6.1 Subsidy boxes
| Box | Covers | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Green** | Research, disease control, infrastructure, food security, decoupled income support | Not trade-distorting; **no limits** |
| **Blue** | Direct payments under **production-limiting** programmes | Trade-distorting but **exempt from cuts** |
| **Amber** | Price/quantity-linked support: **MSP**, power, fertiliser, seed, irrigation (measured as **AMS**) | Trade-distorting; **to be reduced** |
- Traffic-light idea: green permitted, amber reduce, red forbidden - **AoA has NO red box**; Red, Orange, Yellow boxes are distractors.
- Per key: the difference between MSP and market price paid directly to farmers = **Blue box**.
#### 6.2 Mechanics
| Term | Rule |
|---|---|
| **De minimis** | Amber support allowed: **5%** of production value (developed), **10%** (developing) |
| **Total AMS cut** | **20%** over 6 years (developed); **13.3%** (developing) |
| **Blue Box conditions** | Payments on **fixed areas and yields**, or on **85% or less** of base production, or fixed number of livestock |
| **Tariffication** | Non-tariff barriers → tariffs; cut **36% over 6 years** (developed), **24% over 10 years** (developing) |
| **Export subsidy cut** | Developed: **36% by value or 21% by volume** over 6 years (from 1986-90 levels) |
- India is a developing country, but the claim that it need not reduce subsidies is **false**.
### 7. PUBLIC STOCKHOLDING AND THE PEACE CLAUSE
- **Peace Clause (Bali MC9, 2013)**: shields developing-country **public stockholding (PSH)** for food security from disputes, even if de minimis or AMS limits are breached, until a permanent solution; limited to existing programmes and traditional staples; requires notification and no harm to others' food security.
- **India first to invoke it** - for **rice** (2020, for marketing year 2018-19).
- India's complaint: MSP procurement for the **NFSA** counted as Amber Box on an **outdated historical reference price**.
- India + **African Group** want a **permanent solution**; not achieved at **MC13** or **MC14** (deadlocked).
### 8. FISHERIES SUBSIDIES AND E-COMMERCE
#### 8.1 Agreement on Fisheries Subsidies (AFS)
- Adopted **MC12 (June 2022)**; **in force September 2025**.
- Bans subsidies to **IUU fishing**, unregulated high seas fishing, **overfished stocks** (unless rebuilding).
- Covers **marine wild capture** and at-sea support; **aquaculture and inland fishing exempt**. Developing countries/LDCs get a 2-year EEZ exemption; a **Fish Fund** helps poorer members.
- **WTO's first agreement with an environmental-sustainability objective**. TFA and AFS = the **only two new agreements in 30 years**.
- **India = 123rd** member to deposit its Instrument of Acceptance (**July 2026**).
- **Phase 2** (overcapacity, overfishing): deferred at MC14 to the **General Council, Geneva**.
- **AP angle**: AFS rules matter for **AP's marine fisher folk**; Indian fisher bodies want a "**fisher-centric**" approach.
!NUM 123 = Uruguay Round contracting parties AND India's AFS acceptance number.
#### 8.2 E-commerce
- **Moratorium** on customs duties on electronic transmissions (since **1998**) **lapsed on 31 March 2026** for the first time; developing countries lose tariff revenue.
- **E-Commerce Agreement** (plurilateral): **66 members** signed separately. India: plurilaterals (IFD, E-Commerce) may **fragment** the WTO by weakening consensus.
### 9. OTHER WTO AGREEMENTS AND INDIA'S COMPLIANCE
| Agreement | Subject | India's compliance |
|---|---|---|
| **TRIPS** (1995) | Minimum IPR standards | **Patents Act amended 2005** (product patents); **GI Act 1999**; PPV&FR Act 2001 |
| **TRIMS** | Investment measures in **goods** trade (not services); quantitative limits on imports by foreign investors prohibited | Phase-out of local-content rules |
| **GATS** | Services | Liberalisation commitments |
| **SPS / TBT** | Food safety, plant/animal health / technical standards | Food Safety and Standards Act 2006 |
| **TFA** (Bali 2013) | Simplify customs | **Ratified 2016** |
| **ITA** (1996) | Zero duty on IT products | Signatory |
- **SPS**: WTO uses the **Codex Alimentarius Commission** (FAO and WHO, **1963**) for food-safety standards.
- **GI of Goods (Registration and Protection) Act, 1999** - for TRIPS; GI = an IPR. India relies on TRIPS for GI; **not in the Lisbon Agreement**. Per the official key, **Darjeeling Tea** was named as not protected under the GI Act. **Nizamabad black clay pottery** (Azamgarh, UP) = GI 2015.
- **Patents Act** in force **1972**, based on the **N. Rajagopala Ayyangar Committee (1957)**; **Patents (Second Amendment) Act 38 of 2002**, in force 20 May 2003.
- **SCM Agreement** subsidies: **prohibited ("red light")**, actionable (countervailing duty), non-actionable (R&D, regional, environmental). Amber Box is AoA, not SCM.
- India prohibits GM food imports without approval; India **does** import US apples (six disputes settled, June 2023).
- US threatened WTO sanctions for not following ILO conventions (true).
- EXIM Policy 2002-07 liberal; GATT/WTO aided liberalisation (both true, not cause and effect). **BTIA** = India-EU, talks from June 2007, Brussels.
### 10. DISPUTE SETTLEMENT AND APPELLATE BODY CRISIS
- **Appellate Body** non-functional since **December 2019** - **US blocked appointments**.
- **MPIA** (Multi-Party Interim Appeal Arbitration Arrangement, **EU-led, 2020**) = stop-gap; **India has not joined**.
- **BRICS New Delhi Declaration (Sep 2026)**: IMF/World Bank quota and voting reform + restoration of the two-tier WTO dispute settlement.
| Dispute | Subject | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **DS456** (US) | **Solar cells** - domestic content, National Solar Mission | India lost, **2016** |
| **DS541** (US) | Export subsidies: **MEIS, SEZ, EOU** | Against India **2019**; appealed into the void |
| **Sugar** (Brazil, Australia, Guatemala) | Cane prices, export subsidies | Against India **2021**; appealed |
| **ICT tariffs** (EU, Japan, Taiwan) | Duties on phones/ICT goods vs ITA | Against India **2023** |
