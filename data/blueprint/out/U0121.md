## U0121 | SALUVA AND TULUVA DYNASTIES; KRISHNADEVARAYA - CONQUESTS, AMUKTAMALYADA, THE ASHTADIGGAJAS
### 1. THE TWO USURPATIONS
#### 1.1 Background – Decline of the Sangamas
- **Nuniz** called **Virupaksha II** a drunkard and **Praudha Raya** worthless.
- Gajapati and Bahmani invasions ate into the kingdom.
#### 1.2 Usurpation Timeline
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1485 | **First usurpation**: **Saluva Narasimha** of **Chandragiri** expels Praudha Raya; ends the Sangama dynasty, begins the **Saluva** dynasty |
| 1505 | **Second usurpation**: **Vira Narasimha** (son of Tuluva Narasa Nayaka) kills **Immadi Narasimha**, the last Saluva, at **Penugonda**; begins the **Tuluva** dynasty |
| 1509 | **Krishnadevaraya** crowned on **8 August 1509** (Hampi inscription) |
| 1529 | **Achyutaraya** crowned at **Tirupati** |
| 1543 | **Sadasiva** made a figurehead under **Aliya Rama Raya** |
| Jan 1565 | **Talikota** (Rakshasi-Tangadi / Bannihatti): Rama Raya beheaded; flight to **Penukonda** |
### 2. SALUVA NARASIMHA AND SALUVA RULE (1485-91)
- He sent his general **Tuluva Narasa Nayaka** to take the throne; he was called **Raya Maharasu**.
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Capital | Kalyanapuram → **Chandragiri** |
| Army | **Arab horses** from **Ormuz**, Arab cavalry; introduced **Samugaris** (gunmen) |
| Lost | **Udayagiri** to Gajapati **Purushottama** |
| Court poet | **Rajanatha Dindima** – **Saluvabhyudayam** |
| Contemporary | **Annamacharya** (1408–1503); 32,000 kirtanas to Venkateswara (APPSC G2 2011) |
### 3. TULUVA DYNASTY – ORIGIN AND SUCCESSION
#### 3.1 Origin
- Progenitor: **Timmaraju**; mother tongue **Kannada**, but **Telugu** favoured at court.
- **Tuluva Narasa Nayaka**: Saluva general and real ruler under Immadi Narasimha; died 1503.
- **Founder = Vira Narasimha** (APPSC G2 2002).
!TRAP Tuluva Narasa Nayaka helped both usurpations (1485 as general; 1505 as father of the usurper), but the founder of the Tuluva dynasty is **Vira Narasimha**.
#### 3.2 Succession and Immediate Successors
- Vira Narasimha (1505-09) → **Krishnadevaraya** (1509-29, his half-brother) → **Achyutaraya** (1529-42) → Venkata I (1542) → **Sadasiva** (1543-70/76).
| Ruler | Immediate successor |
|---|---|
| Praudha Deva Raya | Narasimha Saluva |
| Immadi Narasimha | Vira Narasimha |
| Vira Narasimha | Krishnadevaraya |
| Krishnadevaraya | Achyutadeva Raya |
- Other transitions: **Tirumala Deva Raya** began **Aravidu** rule; **Pushyamitra Shunga** overthrew Brihadratha (Maurya → Shunga); Shunga → Kanva under **Vasudeva Kanva**.
- Prime Minister of Krishnadevaraya: **Saluva Timmarusu (Appaji)**.
### 4. KRISHNADEVARAYA'S CAMPAIGNS
| Campaign | Year | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Diwani / **Kovilkonda** | 1509–10 | Bahmani "jihad" crushed; **Yusuf Adil Khan** killed |
| **Raichur Doab and Bidar** | 1510 | Raichur taken; freed Sultan **Mahmud Shah** from Ali Barid → title **Yavanarajya Sthapanacharya** |
| **Ummattur / Sivasamudram** | 1512–13 | **Gangaraja** defeated; new province at **Srirangapatna** |
| **Kalinga** | 1513–1519 | Udayagiri, Kondaveedu, Kondapalli, Simhachalam, Cuttack |
| **Battle of Raichur** | 1520 | **Ismail Adil Shah** of Bijapur routed with **Portuguese help**; **Gulbarga** taken 1523 |
!TRAP Yusuf Adil Khan killed at Kovilkonda (1509-10); Ismail Adil Shah routed at Raichur (1520).
### 5. KALINGA (ANDHRA) CAMPAIGN AND PRATAPARUDRA GAJAPATI
#### 5.1 Forts Taken (1513-1519)
- Aim: win back coastal Andhra and Telangana from **Prataparudra Gajapati** of Odisha.
| Fort / place | What happened |
|---|---|
| **Udayagiri** (1513-14) | Fell after an **18-month siege**; **Balakrishna idol** taken to Hampi (Krishna temple built for it) |
| **Kondaveedu** | Taken with **Saluva Timmarusu**'s help (split Veerabhadra Gajapati's army); king performed **Talapurusha Danam** at **Amaravati** |
| **Kondapalli** and Bejawada | Praharajeshwara and Prataparudra's forces defeated |
| Vinukonda | Also taken from the Gajapatis |
| Telangana forts | Devarakonda, Nalgonda, Kambhamettu, Jallipalli, Warangal |
| **Cuttack** | Reached in **1518**; Gajapati sued for peace |
#### 5.2 Victory Pillars and Temple Grants
- Victory pillars: **Potnuru** and **Simhachalam**.
!KEY APPSC 2025: pillar marking the conquest of coastal Andhra, set up on returning from the eastern campaign = **Potnuru**.
- **Simhachalam**: diamond necklace to Varaha Narasimha.
- **Mangalagiri**: land to **Lakshmi Narasimha**. Latest: the temple's renovation (₹6 crore) includes restoring the **Sri Krishna Devaraya Mandapam**.
#### 5.3 Peace Terms
- **Marriage alliance**: Prataparudra Gajapati gave his **daughter** to Krishnadevaraya.
- Lands **north of the Krishna** went back to the Gajapati.

### 8. KRISHNADEVARAYA'S WORKS
#### 8.1 Amuktamalyada
- **Telugu**; also called **Vishnuchittiyam**; meaning "one who offers the garland she has worn".
- **Legend**: **Andhra Mahavishnu of Srikakulam** (Krishna district) told the king in a **dream** to write it; written during the **Kalinga campaign**; dedicated to **Lord Venkateswara**.
- **Theme**: **Goda Devi (Andal)** and her marriage to **Sri Ranganatha**; sub-stories **Yamunacharya Charitra** and **Maladasari Katha**.
- **Political maxims**: statecraft (**rajaniti**), kingship and administration; social, religious and economic life; genealogy from **Bukka** to Krishnadevaraya.
- Preface: "**Desa Bhashalandu Telugu Lessa**" (Telugu is the best of the regional languages).
- One of the **Telugu Pancha Kavyas**; **first Telugu work named after its heroine**.
!TRAP APPSC G2 2003: Amuktamalyada does NOT describe the invasions of Krishnadevaraya.
#### 8.2 Works → Language
| Work | Language |
|---|---|
| Amuktamalyada | **Telugu** (his only Telugu work) |

| Usha Parinayam | Sanskrit |

### 10. BHUVANA VIJAYAM AND THE ASHTADIGGAJAS
#### 10.1 Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Bhuvana Vijayam** | "Conquest of the world"; court hall of the poets; **House of Victory** |
| **Ashtadiggajas** | "Eight elephants" of the eight directions → eight pillar-poets |
| **Kavula Samstha** | Institution of poets – the **Tippalur inscription**'s name for them |
- Tippalur inscription records the gift of **Tippalur agrahara** (Kadapa) to them. Patron: **Krishnadevaraya**, not Achyutaraya (APPSC G2 2019).
- Achyutaraya's hall: **Venkatavilasa Mantapam**.
#### 10.2 Poet → Work → Title
| Poet | Main work | Title / note |
|---|---|---|
| **Allasani Peddana** | **Manucharitramu** (Swarochisha Manu Sambhavam) | **Andhra Kavita Pitamaha**; greatest of them; Telugu and Sanskrit poet |
| **Nandi Timmana** | **Parijatapaharanamu** | **Mukku Timmana**; king's favourite |
| **Dhurjati** | **Sri Kalahasteeswara Mahatyam** and **Satakam** | Shaiva poet of Kalahasti |
| **Madayyagari Mallana** | **Rajasekhara Charitramu** | Early social narrative |
| **Ayyalaraju Ramabhadrudu** | **Ramabhyudayamu**, Sakalakathasara Sangrahamu | Ramayana in Telugu |
| **Pingali Surana** | **Raghava Pandaviyamu**, Kalapurnodayamu | **Dvyarthi kavya** (double meaning) |
| **Ramarajabhushana** (Bhattumurti) | **Vasucharitramu**, Narasabhupaliyamu | Vasucharitra dedicated to Tirumala Raya |
| **Tenali Ramakrishna** | **Panduranga Mahatmyamu** | Court wit |
- The king himself fixed the **Gandapenderam** anklet on Peddana's foot.
!TRAP "Prabandha Parameshwara" is **Errana**'s title, not Nandi Timmana's.
- Reign = **golden age of Telugu literature** (Prabandha era).

### 12. ACHYUTARAYA, SADASIVA AND RAMA RAYA'S REGENCY
- **Achyutaraya** (1529-42): named heir; the **only king crowned at Tirupati** (and Srikalahasti).
- **Aliya Rama Raya**: Krishnadevaraya's **son-in-law**, of the **Aravidu** family; killed Salakam Tirumala (1543); made **Sadasiva** a figurehead.
- Talikota (January 1565): Rama Raya beheaded; Sadasiva and Tirumala fled to **Penukonda**. **Last Tuluva ruler = Sadasiva**.
- **Sadasivakona inscriptions** (found by ASI, June 2026): three **trilingual** (Telugu, Tamil, Kannada) inscriptions of **1554 CE**, Seshachalam forest, Tirupati district; record Sadasiva Raya's holy bath and transfer of **Kaanika** taxes from Gudimallam temple lands.
- AP economy: **Pedaganjam and Chinaganjam** were famous for **salt**; salt pans at Pedaganjam were taxed (PYQ 2019).
