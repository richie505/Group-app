## U0721 | NATIONAL CYBER SECURITY POLICY 2013 AND THE CYBER CRIME POLICY FRAMEWORK
### 1. NATIONAL CYBER SECURITY POLICY (NCSP) 2013
- Released in **2013** by the IT Ministry (now **MeitY**).
- **Vision:** secure and resilient cyberspace for citizens, businesses, government.
- **Target:** **5 lakh** cyber security professionals in **5 years**.
- Aims (separate aims): an **Information Security Assurance Framework**; a trained cyber security workforce; protect public and private infrastructure; safeguard citizens' data; R&D, public-private partnership, **indigenous** security technology.
- Every ministry/department/organisation names a **Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)**.
| Function | Nodal agency |
|---|---|
| Incident response, crisis management | **CERT-In** |
| Critical information infrastructure | **NCIIPC** |
| Situational awareness, timely information-sharing | **NCCC** (National Cyber Coordination Centre) |
- **NAYAN**: software by **C-DAC** against cyber attacks and data theft.
- Features of cyber security: **compliance**, defence against **internal threats**, **threat prevention**.
### 2. POLICY EVOLUTION
#### 2.1 Draft National Cyber Security Strategy
- Prepared under the **National Cyber Security Coordinator**, in the **NSCS**.
- Status: **draft, still pending/not notified**; NCSP 2013 remains operative.
!TRAP "National Cyber Security Policy 2020" is WRONG: 2020 = draft strategy; the 2013 policy is in force.
#### 2.2 Cyber Crisis Management Plan (CCMP)
- **MeitY** framework against cyber attacks and **cyber terrorism**; followed by **all Central and State Governments**; **205 workshops** till July 2025.
### 3. CYBER PREPAREDNESS AND AWARENESS
- **Cyber Surakshit Bharat** (MeitY, **2018**): first **PPP** initiative to train **CISOs**; deep-dive training run by **NeGD**.
- **Cyber Jagrookta Diwas**: **first Wednesday of every month**, under MHA's **I4C**; began **October 2021**.
- **ISEA** (MeitY) = **Information Security Education and Awareness**: capacity-building and awareness project.
- **#OnlineButUnafraid**: Telangana Cyber Security Bureau campaign for women journalists.
### 4. CYBER CRIME PREVENTION AGAINST WOMEN AND CHILDREN (CCPWC)
- Outlay **Rs 132.93 crore**; cyber forensic-cum-training labs in **33 States/UTs**.
- Linked to the **National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal** and **Nirbhaya Fund** projects.
- **Cyber Shakti**: trains **women government officials** - **305** beginner (online), **53** advanced (offline).

### 6. INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION
| Forum / body | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Cyber Diplomacy Division** | **MEA** nodal point (cyber security, data protection, cybercrime, internet governance) |
| APCERT | India a member; CSIRTs of **13 economies**; first conference **March 2002, Tokyo** |
| Colombo Security Conclave | India, Sri Lanka, Mauritius, Maldives, Bangladesh, Seychelles (**not Nepal**); cyber security = one of **five pillars** |
| Quad | Senior Cyber Group, cybersecurity partnership |
| Counter Ransomware Initiative | US-led (**2021**); India leads the **Resilience Working Group**; CERT-In exercise **"Synergy"** for 13 countries |
### 7. ITU GLOBAL CYBERSECURITY INDEX AND AP ANGLE
- **GCI 2024**: India **Tier-1 "Role Modelling"**, score **98.49**, strong in all **five pillars**; uses **tiers, not ranks**.
- **GCI 2020**: India ranked **10th**.
- **AP Cyber Security Policy 2017**: implemented by **APTS**; **four verticals** (incl. securing critical government infrastructure, capacity building).
