## U0308 | BUDGET IN PARLIAMENT – STAGES, CUT MOTIONS, APPROPRIATION AND FINANCE BILLS, FUNDS
### 1. ANNUAL FINANCIAL STATEMENT – ARTICLE 112
- The **Budget** = **Annual Financial Statement (AFS)**, **Article 112** (APPSC 2018, 2023).
- The word **"Budget" is not used in the Constitution**. ("bougette" = French for little bag.)
- **President** causes the AFS to be **laid before both Houses** every year; presented in the **Lok Sabha** by the **Finance Minister** (Union Budget 2023-24: **Nirmala Sitharaman**, APPSC 2023).
- Financial year: **1 April – 31 March**.
- Prepared by the **Budget Division, Department of Economic Affairs (DEA)**, **Ministry of Finance**; process starts with the **Budget Circular** (usually **September**).
- **Halwa ceremony** (North Block) marks the start of printing of Budget documents.
- **Economic Survey** is presented **before** the Budget each year.
- Covers all **three funds**; presented as **Revenue Budget + Capital Budget**.
!KEY FIRST Union Budget of independent India: **26 November 1947**, **R.K. Shanmukham Chetty** (APPSC 2018).
#### 1.1 Budget Articles in Order
| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| 112 | Annual Financial Statement; 112(3) charged items |
| 113 | Procedure for estimates – **Demands for Grants** |
| 114 | **Appropriation Bill** |
| 115 | **Supplementary, additional, excess** grants |
| 116 | **Vote on account, vote of credit, exceptional grants** |
| 117 | Financial Bills |
| 266 / 267 | Consolidated Fund and Public Account / Contingency Fund |
### 2. STAGES OF THE BUDGET
1. **Presentation** of the AFS.
2. **General discussion** – both Houses; no vote.
3. **Scrutiny by Departmentally Related Standing Committees**.
4. **Voting on Demands for Grants** (Art. 113) – **Lok Sabha only**; it may **pass, reject or reduce** (cut) a demand. Estimates are sent as Demands for Grants (Art. 113(2)).
5. **Guillotine** – on the last allotted day, all remaining demands are put to vote **without discussion**.
6. **Passing of the Appropriation Bill** (Art. 114).
7. **Passing of the Finance Bill** – gives effect to the tax proposals.
!TRAP Vote on account, vote of credit and exceptional grants are **NOT stages** of normal financial law-making.
- Rajya Sabha can **discuss** the AFS but **cannot vote** on Demands for Grants.
### 3. CUT MOTIONS
| Cut motion | Reduces the demand | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Policy cut** | **To Re 1** | Complete **disapproval of the policy** behind the demand |
| **Economy cut** | **By a specified amount** | **Economy** in expenditure |
| **Token cut** | **By Rs 100** | Air a **specific grievance** within government responsibility |
- A cut motion = proposal to **reduce** a demand for grant; aims to draw the House's attention to the matter named.
- Moved **only in the Lok Sabha**, during **voting on Demands for Grants**.
- If **passed** → **loss of confidence** → the Government must **resign**.
!PAIR Re 1 | Policy cut | Disapproval || Rs 100 | Token cut | Grievance
### 4. APPROPRIATION BILL AND FINANCE BILL – ARTICLE 114
- Authorises **withdrawal from the CFI** for **voted grants + charged expenditure**, not exceeding the AFS amount.
- **Art. 114(3)**: no money withdrawn from the CFI **except under appropriation made by law** – even charged expenditure needs an Appropriation Act.
- **No amendment** may vary the amount or alter the destination of a voted grant, or vary charged expenditure.
- Carries an **automatic repeal clause** (APPSC G1 2024).
- Appropriation Bill is a **Money Bill**; **Finance Bill** puts the Budget's **tax proposals** into effect.
!TRAP The **President, Prime Minister, Finance Minister and CAG** cannot withdraw from the CFI on their own authority. Right to issue money from the CFI belongs to none of them – only Parliament by law.
- **Parliament's financial control**: placing the AFS; withdrawal only after the Appropriation Act; rules on supplementary grants.
### 5. CHARGED VS VOTED EXPENDITURE
- **Charged** = **non-votable**; not put to vote but **can be discussed** in both Houses. Ensures **independence** of constitutional offices.
- Three separate questions: who **fixes** the salary (law), which **fund pays**, and whether **charged or voted**.
#### 5.1 Charged on the Consolidated Fund of India – Art. 112(3) (full list)
1. **President** – emoluments, allowances, office expenses.
2. **Chairman and Deputy Chairman** of RS; **Speaker and Deputy Speaker** of LS – salaries and allowances.
3. **Supreme Court judges** – salaries, allowances, pensions.
4. **High Court judges** – **only pensions** (Art. 112(3)(d)(iii)).
5. **CAG** – salary, allowances, pension, administrative expenses.
6. **Debt charges** – interest, sinking fund charges.
7. Sums to satisfy a **judgment, decree or arbitral award**.
#### 5.2 Who Fixes, Who Pays, Charged or Voted
| Office | Article | Salary paid from | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| President | 59(3) | CFI | Charged |
| PM and Ministers | **75(6)** | CFI | **Voted** (Demands for Grants) |
| Speaker, Deputy Speaker, Chairman, Deputy Chairman | **97** | CFI | Charged |
| SC judges | **125** | CFI | Charged |
| HC judges | **221** | **State fund** (Art. 202(3)(d)); pension **CFI** | Charged |
| CAG | **148(3)** | CFI | Charged (148(3), 148(6)) |
| Governor | **158(3)** | State fund | Charged |
| Election Commission | — | CFI | **Voted**, not charged |
- **Vice-President**: **no salary as VP**; paid only as **Chairman of the Rajya Sabha** (charged).
- **Art. 158(3A)**: one Governor for two or more States – emoluments shared in the proportion the **President** fixes by order.
- **Second Schedule** – emoluments, allowances, privileges; **Third Schedule** – forms of oaths.
### 6. SPECIAL GRANTS – ARTICLES 115 AND 116
| Grant | Article | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Supplementary** | 115 | Sanctioned amount **insufficient** |
| **Additional** | 115 | **New service** not in the Budget |
| **Excess** | 115 | Spent more than granted; needs **PAC** approval first |
| **Vote on account** | 116 | **Advance** for part of the year (about two months, one-sixth); 3-5 months in election years |
| **Vote of credit** | 116 | **Blank cheque** for an emergency; details cannot be stated |
| **Exceptional** | 116 | Not part of the **current service** of any year |
| Token grant | — | For **re-appropriation**; must be voted by Lok Sabha |
- **Vote on account** is needed when the regular Budget will not pass in time (e.g. elections); allows withdrawal from CFI before the Budget (Art. 116). Since the **1 February Budget (2017)**, usually needed only in **interim-Budget** years.
- **Latest:** Lok Sabha approved the **second batch of Supplementary Demands for Grants 2025-26** (Art. 115).
!NUM 115 = supplementary, additional, excess; 116 = vote on account, vote of credit, exceptional
### 7. CONSOLIDATED FUND, PUBLIC ACCOUNT, CONTINGENCY FUND
| Basis | Consolidated Fund of India | Public Account of India | Contingency Fund of India |
|---|---|---|---|
| Article | **266(1)** | **266(2)** | **267(1)** |
| States | 266(1) | 266(2) | **267(2)** |
| Receipts | **All revenues**, loans (**Treasury Bills**, ways and means advances, market and foreign loans), **loan repayments** | Money in **trust**: **provident funds**, **small savings**, judicial deposits, savings bank deposits, remittances | **Imprest** for **unforeseen expenses** |
| Controlled by | **Parliament** | **Executive** | **President**; held by **Finance Secretary, DEA** (not Expenditure) |
| Withdrawal | Only by **Appropriation Act** | **No vote** needed | Advance; **approval later** |
| Nature | Main treasury account | Government acts as **banker/trustee** | Fixed corpus |
- Contingency Fund of India: set up by **Parliament by law** – **Contingency Fund of India Act, 1950**; corpus **Rs 30,000 crore** from **FY 2021-22** (earlier Rs 500 crore).
- **State Contingency Fund**: set up by the **State Legislature** by law (Art. 267(2)); at the **Governor's** disposal.
- **CAG audits** all three funds of both Union and States.
!TRAP Public Account is **266(2)**, not 266(1).

### 11. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS

1. "Budget" is **not** in the Constitution; it is the AFS (Art. 112).
2. **HC judges' salaries** – State fund; **pensions** – CFI.
3. **Election Commission** and **Ministers** – **voted**, not charged.
4. Contingency Fund held by **Finance Secretary, DEA**, not Expenditure.
5. **115** vs **116** grants – do not mix.
6. Policy cut **Re 1**, token cut **Rs 100**.
7. Even charged expenditure needs an **Appropriation Act** (Art. 114(3)).
8. Vote on account etc. are **not stages** of the budget.
9. Union Budget 2026-27 on **1 Feb 2026**; **14 Feb 2026** is the AP Budget.
