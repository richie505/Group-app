### 2. NSO, CSO AND STATISTICAL BODIES
- **NSO** = **CSO + NSSO** merged in **2019**, under **MoSPI**; head = **Chief Statistician of India** (also Secretary, MoSPI).
- **National income is estimated by the NSO** under MoSPI. (Older keys: NSSO; one APPSC Group-1 key gave "Indian Standard Institution", but official compiling is by CSO under MoSPI.)
- Functions: national income (GDP, GNI, NNI, PCI), **IIP**, CPI, energy, labour, gender statistics, National Industrial Classification. **Monetary policy is NOT an NSO function**.
- NSO follows **IMF-SDDS** norms; nodal agency for the **ICP** (India 3rd by PPP, ICP 2021).
- **CSO**: formed **2 May 1951**; **National Accounts Division** = GDP, consumption, capital formation; **Economic Statistics Division (ESD) compiles IIP** (APPSC 2019); **Industrial Statistics Wing, Kolkata** conducts the **ASI**.
| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| **NSC (2006)** | Part-time Chairperson + **4** part-time members; **CEO of NITI Aayog** ex officio |
| **ISI** | Autonomous, **ISI Act 1959** |
| **State DES** | State Domestic Product |
### 3. STATISTIC -> PUBLISHING BODY
| Statistic | Body |
|---|---|

- **IIP**: monthly, on the **28th**; mining, manufacturing, electricity; **14 source agencies**. 2010 PYQ key: IIP "released by RBI"; IIP base 1993-94 -> 2004-05 on **10 June 2011**, later 2011-12.
### 4. BASE YEAR – MEANING AND SEQUENCE
- **Base year** = year whose prices value real GDP; index base = 100. Revised every **5-10 years**.
- Sequence: **1948-49, 1960-61, 1970-71, 1980-81, 1993-94, 1999-2000, 2004-05, 2011-12 (released 2015), 2022-23 (current)**.
- **2009-10 was never a base year**. Feb 1999 series used 1993-94.
| Index | New base |
|---|---|
| GDP | **2022-23** (from 2011-12) |
| IIP | **2022-23** |
| **CPI** | **2024** (from **calendar year 2012**, not 2011-12) |
| Trade indices | 2022-23 (from 2012-13) |
- **Feb 2026**: new GDP, IIP and CPI series released; first CPI print **2.75%** (Jan 2026).
### 5. 2011-12 SERIES (2015)
- Changed **both base year and method**: 2004-05 -> 2011-12 (APPSC 2017, 2019, 2023); **GDP at factor cost dropped**; headline = **GVA at basic prices**.
- **MCA-21** company database (e-governance for corporate services) partly replaced ASI: 1,35,802 MCA companies vs 67,649 ASI factories.
- **Back series** recalculated earlier years on the new method.
- **Criticism**: manufacturing growth not matching physical indicators; private corporate sector **overestimated**. **Arvind Subramanian** (2019): 2011-17 growth about **4.5%**, not 7%.
### 6. 2022-23 SERIES (27 FEB 2026)
- Released by NSO; follows **UN SNA 2025**. Why 2022-23: a "normal" year – not **2017-18** (GST), **2019-21** (COVID), **2021-22** (base effects).
- Changes: **double deflation**; **Supply and Use Tables**; GST, e-Vahan, PFMS data; PFCE benchmarked on **HCES 2022-23**; **four institutional sectors** – government, public corporations, private corporations, households (**not exports**).
| Item | Revision |
|---|---|
| 2023-24 growth | **9.2% -> 7.2%** |
| 2024-25 growth | **6.5% -> 7.1%** |
| Size | GDP cut **3-4%**; 2022-23 nominal **Rs 269 -> Rs 261 lakh crore** |
- Debate: manufacturing GVA **Rs 38.6 lakh crore** (14.7% of GDP, 2023-24) does not match ASI + ASUSE; unincorporated share only **13.9%**.
### 7. RELEASE CALENDAR AND QUARTERLY GDP
1. **First Advance (FAE)** – early **January**; FY26 **7.4%**
2. **Second Advance (SAE)** – end-**February**; FY26 **7.6%** (new series)
3. **Provisional (PE)** – moved from last working day of May to **7 June from 2026** (after ACNAS)
4. **First, Second, Third Revised** (FRE, SRE, TRE)
- Advance estimates use the **benchmark-indicator method**.
- **Quarterly GDP**: about **two months** after the quarter (Q1 2026-27 end-August); high-frequency indicators, GST data; Q3 FY26 **7.8%**. IMF: lag in capital formation data.
!KEY Quote the stage: AP 2023-24 livestock growth +4.79% at AE vs −0.51% at FRE.
### 8. INFORMAL SECTOR AND NSO SURVEYS
- Household/unincorporated sector **benchmarked on ASUSE**, extrapolated with indicators; **MCA-21** = companies; **PLFS** = employment.
| Survey | Measures |
|---|---|
| **PLFS** | Employment, unemployment, LFPR, WPR; **monthly from 2025** |
| **HCES** | Consumption; CPI weights, PFCE benchmark |
| **ASI** | **Registered factories** |
| **ASUSE** | **Unincorporated non-agricultural enterprises** |
| **TUS** | Time use |
- Reforms: ASUSE quarterly (2025); district estimates by 2026; Household Income Survey; SAS 2026-27.
### 9. SAVINGS, CAPITAL FORMATION AND DATA DEBATES
- **MoSPI/NSO** estimates saving and capital formation by sector: **household, private corporate, public**. **K.N. Raj Committee (1981)**.
- Household saving **18.6% -> 18.1%** of GDP (2022-23 -> 2023-24, NAS 2025); private corporate investment **11.2%** (FY24).
- Debates: back series; **HCES 2022-23** first full survey since 2011-12 (2017-18 withheld); Census delayed – houselisting Apr-Sep 2026, enumeration **Feb 2027**.
### 10. AP – GSDP BY DES
- **DES, AP** estimates GSDP in **9 sectors** (APPSC-G2 2017); **APSES uses 2011-12** (2019-20 is a trap); 2025-26 FAE **Rs 17.62 lakh crore**; moving to 2022-23 under MoSPI guideline.
