#### 4.2 High Court – Constitutional Provisions
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **214** | High Court for each State |
| **215** | HC is a **Court of Record** – power to punish for **contempt** |
| **217** | Appointment by **President** after consulting **CJI and Governor** |
| **217(1)(b) + 124(4)** | Removal only for **proved misbehaviour or incapacity** |
| **222** | **Transfer** of HC judges – consultation with CJI mandatory; **judge's consent not needed** |
| **226** | Writs for Fundamental Rights **and other legal rights** – **wider than Art 32** |
| **235** | HC control over **subordinate courts** |
- Retirement: **HC 62, SC 65**; oath before the **Governor**; HC judge resigns by writing to the **President**.
- Removal: motion signed by **100 Lok Sabha** or **50 Rajya Sabha** members; inquiry under **Judges Inquiry Act, 1968** (SC judge, HC CJ, jurist); special majority in both Houses.
!TRAP Transfer = **Art 222**, not Art 217. The Constitution does **not** use the word "impeachment" for judges.

### 7. GODAVARI PUSHKARAMS 2027 AND AP TOURISM
#### 7.1 Pushkarams 2027 Preparations
- **Tent cities at 21 temple locations** on Godavari banks; largest at **Bridge Lanka, Rajamahendravaram**, under the **Godavari Riverfront Project**.
- Target: **50,000 homestay rooms in 5 years**, starting with **Konaseema**.
- **Environmental Protection Task Force** (river pollution) – chaired by **Deputy CM Pawan Kalyan**; **APPCB** (est. **1976**, Vijayawada) is **convener**.
- Pushkaram: every **12 years**, when **Jupiter (Brihaspati)** enters the river's zodiac sign – for Godavari, **Leo (Simha Rasi)**.
- First 12 days – **Adi Pushkaram**; last 12 days – **Antya Pushkaram**.
- "**Lanka**" = alluvial river islands in the Godavari delta (Bridge Lanka is not Himalayan).
!NUM 21 = tent-city temple locations; 12 = years in the Pushkaram cycle. Do not confuse.
#### 7.2 Godavari and AP Geography Facts
- **Dakshin Ganga**; rises at **Trimbakeshwar (Nashik)**; ~**1,465 km**; **second-longest** after the Ganga.
- **Left-bank:** Indravati, Sabari; **right-bank:** Pravara, Purna, **Manjira**.
- Rajamahendravaram – **Dowleswaram Barrage** (**Sir Arthur Cotton**); **Papi Kondalu** – gorge cut by the Godavari through the **Eastern Ghats**.
- **Kondapalli** (Krishna district) – **chromite** deposits; Kondapalli Thermal Power Station; **Kondapalli Bommalu** toys.
- AP rainfall: **South-West Monsoon ~60–65%** (not 85%); **Rayalaseema** – rain shadow, semi-arid; **North-East Monsoon** vital for **Nellore, Prakasam**.
- **Watershed Mahotsav** launched in **Guntur** – community-led water conservation.

#### 8.3 Pattadar Passbooks and Land Registration
- Revised **pattadar passbooks** with the **State Emblem** (replacing political imagery) given to farmers in **Nandyal district**.
- Passbook = part of **Record of Rights (RoR)** – ownership, extent, survey number; a **facilitating document, not conclusive proof of title**.
- **AP Land Titling Act** (conclusive titling) **repealed** by the Naidu government.
- **Presumptive titling** (India) – State does not guarantee title; **conclusive titling** – State guarantees title.
- **State Emblem of India (Prohibition of Improper Use) Act, 2005** regulates emblem use.
- **Settipalle village, Tirupati** – **first land registration in nearly five decades**; coincides with a **₹1,766 crore township** proposed by **TUDA**.
- **Section 22A, Registration Act, 1908** – States can bar registration of certain lands (govt, assigned, disputed) – **not limited to agricultural land**.
- **Inam lands** – **pre-Independence** grants for services; abolished by Inam Abolition laws.
- Land = **Entry 18, State List**; Parliament may legislate under **Articles 249, 250, 252**.
- **Stamp duty** – collected by States; **mutation does not confer title**; **DILRMP** digitises land records.
