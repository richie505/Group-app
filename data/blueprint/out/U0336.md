## U0336 | ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA – COMPOSITION, APPOINTMENT (2023 ACT), POWERS AND FUNCTIONS
### 1. CONSTITUTIONAL STATUS
- A **permanent, independent, constitutional body** (APPSC GS 2024), **common to the Centre and the States**.
- **Part XV, Articles 324-329**, titled **"Elections"**; constitutional basis **Article 324**.
- **Art. 324(1):** vests the **superintendence, direction and control** of elections and **electoral rolls** for **Parliament, State Legislatures, President and Vice-President** in the ECI.
- Established **25 January 1950** → observed as **National Voters' Day since 2011**.
- Seat: **Nirvachan Sadan**, New Delhi; training arm: **IIIDEM**, New Delhi.
- Art. 324 is a **"reservoir of power"** (Mohinder Singh Gill, 1978) – usable only where **no law holds the field**; it cannot normally override an existing law.
### 2. COMPOSITION AND DECISION RULE
- **One CEC + as many ECs as the President fixes**; now **CEC + 2 ECs** = **three-member body** (APPSC G2 2025); CEC presides.
- **Equal powers**, **equal salaries and allowances**; differences settled by **majority**.
- Appointed by the **President** – **Art. 324(2)**, subject to a law of Parliament.
- **Regional Commissioners** may be appointed by the President after consulting the EC.
- PYQ key: **status of the CEC = Governor of a State**; salary = **Supreme Court judge**.
#### 2.1 Single-Member to Multi-Member
| Period | Status |
|---|---|
| 1950 – Oct 1989 | **Single-member** (CEC only) |
| **16 Oct 1989** | **Two ECs added** – first three-member Commission, after the **61st Amendment** lowered voting age **21 → 18**; served only briefly |
| 1990 | Single-member again |
| **1 Oct 1993 onwards** | **Multi-member continuously**; majority rule upheld in **T.N. Seshan v. Union of India (1995)** |
### 3. APPOINTMENT – THE 2023 ACT AND ANOOP BARANWAL
#### 3.1 The Road to the Act
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **1975** | **Tarkunde Committee**, set up by **Jayaprakash Narayan** – PM + LoP + CJI panel; three-member Commission |
| Before 2023 | President appointed on the **PM's advice alone** |
| **2023** | **Anoop Baranwal v. Union of India** – Constitution Bench: **until Parliament makes a law**, CEC and ECs chosen by **PM + LoP (or leader of largest opposition party) in Lok Sabha + CJI** |
| **2023** | **CEC and Other ECs (Appointment, Conditions of Service and Term of Office) Act, 2023** (**Act No. 49 of 2023**) – **replaced the 1991 Act**; **CJI replaced by a Union Cabinet Minister** |
#### 3.2 Procedure under the 2023 Act
| Stage | Composition / rule |
|---|---|
| **Search Committee** | Headed by the **Law Minister** + **two Secretary-rank** members; shortlists **five names** |
| **Selection Committee** | **PM (chair)**, **LoP** (or largest opposition party leader) in Lok Sabha, **a Union Cabinet Minister nominated by the PM**; **may pick names outside the shortlist** |
| Appointment | **President**, on the Selection Committee's recommendation |
- Criticism: gives the government a **2:1 majority**; challenged by **ADR** and **Jaya Thakur** (Jaya Thakur v. Union of India, 2024 – pending as of May 2026).
- **May 2026:** SC said the **CJI's place was only temporary**, until a law; questioned the lack of "one absolutely neutral person"; asked whether the LoP was "ornamental".
- **March 2026:** **CJI Surya Kant** moved the petitions to a Bench without a future CJI.
!TRAP Anoop Baranwal panel had the **CJI**; the 2023 Act put a **Union Cabinet Minister** in his place.
### 4. TENURE, SALARY, REMOVAL AND GAPS
| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Term | **6 years or age 65**, whichever earlier; **no reappointment** (2023 Act) |
| Salary | = **Supreme Court judge**; service conditions **cannot be varied to their disadvantage** |
| **CEC removal** | **Like a Supreme Court judge** – **Art. 324(5)** (Sec. 11(2) of the 2023 Act repeats it); proven misbehaviour or incapacity; special majority in both Houses; motion needs **100 Lok Sabha or 50 Rajya Sabha** signatures |
| **ECs and Regional Commissioners** | Removed by the President **only on the CEC's recommendation** (Sec. 11) |
| **State Election Commissioner** | Like a **High Court judge** (Art. 243K(2)) |
#### 4.1 Constitutional Gaps (Listed Flaws)
- **No qualifications** and **no term** prescribed for members in the Constitution.
- **No bar on post-retirement office** for retiring ECs (APPSC G1 2024).
- Contrast: **CAG (Art. 148(4))** and **UPSC Chairman (Art. 319)** have constitutional bars.
- ECI depends on **DoPT, Law and Home Ministries**; demand to charge its expenses on the **Consolidated Fund of India**; no power over inner-party democracy or party finances.
!TRAP "The Constitution bars the CEC from post-retirement office" = FALSE.

### 6. POWERS AND FUNCTIONS
- ECI decides **every step** from announcement of schedule to **declaration of results** (APPSC G1 2022).
#### 6.1 Administrative
- Prepares and revises **electoral rolls**; **Special Intensive Revision (SIR)** under **Art. 324 + Section 21, RPA 1950**.
- Notifies schedules, scrutinises nominations, regulates expenses.
- Decides territorial constituencies under the **Delimitation Commission Act**. **J&K delimitation (2022)** – Commission under **Justice Ranjana Desai**; **Assam** delimited by the **ECI itself** (effective Aug 2023).
- **Model Code of Conduct** – **not a statute**; rests on **Art. 324**; applies **from the day the schedule is announced** (**Harbans Singh Jalal, 1997**). Part VII = conduct of the party in power.
#### 6.2 Quasi-Judicial and Advisory
- **Recognises parties and allots symbols** under the **Election Symbols (Reservation and Allotment) Order, 1968** (issued under RPA 1951 + Art. 324); settles symbol disputes.
- Advises the **President on MPs' disqualification (Art. 103)** and the **Governor on MLAs' (Art. 192)**.
- **Anti-defection (Tenth Schedule)** → **Speaker/Chairman**, not the ECI.
- **Recommending President's Rule** is **not** an ECI function.
!PAIR Disqualification | Art. 103/192 | ECI advises || Anti-defection | Tenth Schedule | Speaker/Chairman
#### 6.3 Voter Facilitation
| App / campaign | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **SVEEP** | Voter awareness and education for inclusive, accessible elections |
| **cVIGIL** | Citizens report **MCC and expenditure violations** with time-stamped evidence; status within **100 minutes** |
| **Saksham-ECI** | Registration and polling help for **persons with disabilities**; developed with the **Department of Empowerment of PwDs** |
| **Voter Helpline** | Search name in roll, submit forms, complaints; toll-free **1950** |
- **Home voting:** voters **above 85** and PwDs with **40% benchmark disability**.
#### 6.4 VVPAT
- Prints a paper slip of the vote so the voter can verify it.
- **8 April 2019:** SC raised verification from one to **five randomly selected EVMs-VVPATs per Assembly segment**.
- **26 April 2024 (ADR v. ECI):** **100% VVPAT verification rejected**; **symbol loading units sealed** and kept in the strong room **45 days** after results; verification request within **7 days** at the candidate's cost.
### 7. ELECTIONS UNDER ECI AND NOT UNDER ECI
| Under ECI | Not under ECI |
|---|---|
| Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha | **Panchayats and municipalities** → **SEC** (Arts. 243K, 243ZA) |
| State Assemblies and Councils | **Speaker, Deputy Speaker** (Art. 93; States Art. 178) |
| **President and Vice-President** | **Deputy Chairman, RS** (Art. 89(2)); **Chairman, Legislative Council** (Art. 182) |
- Presiding officers are **chosen by their own Houses**.
### 8. ECI VS STATE ELECTION COMMISSION AND CHIEF ELECTORAL OFFICER
| Basis | ECI | SEC | Chief Electoral Officer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basis | **Art. 324**, Part XV | **Arts. 243K, 243ZA**, Parts IX, IX-A (73rd/74th Amendments, 1992) | **Sec. 13A, RPA 1950** – **not a constitutional authority** |
| Elections | Parliament, legislatures, President, VP | Panchayats (all three tiers), municipalities | LS and Assembly polls, under ECI control |
| Appointed by | President | **Governor** | **ECI**, in consultation with the State Government, from senior State officers |
| Removal | Like SC judge | Like **HC judge** | **By the ECI** |
- SEC prepares rolls and conducts local polls (Arts. 243K(1), 243ZA(1)); **243K = panchayats, 243ZA = municipalities**.
- **Decision to hold panchayat elections** = **State Government**; panchayat is **entry 5 of the State List**.
- Constitutional authorities: **SEC 243K**, **SFC 243I**, **District Panchayat 243B**.
- District: **Collector = District Election Officer**; appoints **Returning and Presiding Officers**.
- **Rajasthan SEC** constituted **July 1994** (not December), one-member; its secretary is also the CEO.
- **AP:** SEC **Anil Chandra Punetha** for local-body polls; **ASR district** – 100% SIR digitalisation.
!TRAP MPPSC 2019 key gave **Art. 243K** for urban-body polls; textbook article for municipalities is **243ZA**.
### 9. ELECTION DISPUTES AND POLL DISRUPTION
- **Art. 329(a):** delimitation and seat allotment laws **cannot be questioned in court**.
- **Art. 329(b):** Parliament/Assembly election challenged **only by an election petition** under **RPA 1951** – tried by the **High Court**, appeal to the **Supreme Court**.
- **Art. 71:** President/VP disputes → **Supreme Court**.
- ECI **cannot review a declared result** and **does not hear election petitions**.
#### 9.1 Polling-Day Situations (RPA 1951)
| Section | Situation | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **57** | **Riot, open violence, natural calamity** or other sufficient cause | **Presiding or Returning Officer adjourns** the poll; adjourned poll resumes with ECI approval; only those who had not voted may vote |
| **58** | EVM/ballot box **unlawfully taken, destroyed, lost, damaged or tampered** | ECI declares poll **void**, fixes a **fresh poll** |
| **58A** | **Booth capturing** | Adjournment or **countermand** of election |
