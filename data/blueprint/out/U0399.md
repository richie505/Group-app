### 4. PROTECTION OF HUMAN RIGHTS ACT, 1993
- **Ordinance 28 Sept 1993**; Act in force from **28 Sept 1993**; whole of India. **NHRC constituted 12 Oct 1993**.
- Follows **Paris Principles** (Paris, Oct 1991; UNGA **20 Dec 1993**). NHRC is **statutory**; advice **non-binding**.
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| **2(1)(a)** | **Armed forces** = naval, military, air + other Union forces; **State armed police NOT included** |
| **2(1)(d)** | **Human rights** = life, liberty, equality, dignity |
| 2(1)(f) | International Covenants = **ICCPR + ICESCR** |
| **2(1)(m)** | **Public servant** = Sec. 21 IPC (now **Sec. 2(28) BNS**, 1 July 2024) |
| **3 / 21** | NHRC / SHRC |
| **30** | **Human Rights Court**: Court of Session per district, State Govt + **HC Chief Justice** concurrence |
| **31** | **Special Public Prosecutor**: advocate of **7 years** |
| 39 | Members and officers = public servants |
- Civil court powers (**CPC 1908**); no sentencing. Judicial proceedings for **IPC 193** (false evidence), **228** (insulting a public servant); BNS from 1 July 2024.
- **Armed forces:** cannot investigate; calls for a **Central Government report**.

### 5. 2019 AMENDMENT
- Chairperson: former **CJI or a Supreme Court judge**; SHRC chair: also any **HC judge**.
- Term **5 → 3 years** (or 70); reappointment allowed.
- Members **2 → 3**, one a **woman**; deemed members add chairs of **NCBC, NCPCR**, and **Chief Commissioner for PwD**.
