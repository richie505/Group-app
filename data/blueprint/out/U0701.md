## U0701 | SCIENCE AWARDS – BHATNAGAR PRIZE, RASHTRIYA VIGYAN PURASKAR, INDIAN-ORIGIN NOBEL LAUREATES
### 1. NOBEL PRIZE BASICS
- **Alfred Nobel** (Swedish) invented **dynamite (1867)**, using nitroglycerine; will **1895**; Nobel Foundation **1900**; first prizes **1901**.
- Original five: Physics, Chemistry, Physiology or Medicine, Literature, Peace (**Norwegian Nobel Committee, Oslo**).
- **Economics** (Riksbank Prize, Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences): added **1968**, first given **1969** – not in the will.
- Laureate status cannot be transferred, shared or withdrawn; only the medal can change hands.
- **RDX** (cyclonite, patented 1898) – **George Friedrich Henning**, Germany, not Nobel.

### 3. S. CHANDRASEKHAR
- **Chandrasekhar limit**: maximum stable **white dwarf** mass, about **1.44 solar masses**; above it → **neutron star or black hole**; hence "**principle of the black hole**".
- Used special relativity and quantum physics. Nephew of **Raman**; US citizen **1953**; 50+ PhD students.
- **NASA Chandra X-ray Observatory (1999)**.

### 5. SHANTI SWARUP BHATNAGAR PRIZE
- By **CSIR** since **1958**; named after **S.S. Bhatnagar**, founder-director and first DG of CSIR; for outstanding contribution to science; age limit **45**.
- **7 disciplines**: biology, chemistry, earth/atmosphere/ocean/planetary, engineering, mathematics, medicine, physics (**not political science**).
- 2010 awardees included **Sanjeev Galande**; **Madhav Gadgil** (died Jan 2026) – People's Biodiversity Registers, **Gadgil Commission 2010** (Western Ghats).
- **CECRI Karaikudi**: founded 1948, came into being 14 Jan 1953 (Alagappa Chettiar, Bhatnagar, Nehru).
