## U0011 | IVC SOCIETY AND POLITY – STRATIFICATION, BURIALS, ABSENCE OF TEMPLES AND PALACES
### 1. SOCIAL STRATIFICATION – EVIDENCE
| Evidence | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Town layout | **Citadel (west)** – public buildings, elite vs **lower town (east)** – commoners |
| Houses | Vary in size; **single-roomed barracks** at Harappa |
| **Burial goods** | Variation in goods and ornament quality = **best sign** of social differentiation |
- **Uniform bricks** prove organisation and authority, **not status**.
- **No written proof** of a rigid caste system; caste became fixed in the **Later Vedic** period.
- Ranks and polity are rebuilt only from **material remains**; the script gives **no rulers' names**.
### 2. VIEWS ON THE POLITY
| View | Based on |
|---|---|
| **Priestly rule** | Citadels, Great Bath, steatite **Priest-King** (not conclusive) |
| **Merchant elites** / civic administrators | Trade-driven uniformity; no clear kings or palaces |
| **Centralised state** | Uniformity over a huge area; but **no single capital** identified |
- Several large cities and no capital support a **city-state** reading.
- Political structure **not clear**; it **seems corporate**. Later Vedic = **hereditary monarchy**, weakening **Sabha and Samiti**.
### 3. UNIFORMITY AS PROOF OF AUTHORITY
- Standard **1:2:4 bricks**, **binary weights**, **uniform seals**, **grid towns** across ~**8,00,000 sq km** → significant **central authority**.
### 4. NO PALACES, NO TEMPLES
- **No royal palaces or temples** found. "Possessed great palaces and temples" = **WRONG** (UPSC 2013).
- Readings: worship **domestic or open-air**; power **not personalised** in a king.
- Public buildings mainly **civic**: Great Bath, granary, pillared hall.
### 5. BURIAL TYPES → SITE
| Burial practice | Site |
|---|---|
| **Extended** burials with goods; **coffin** burial | **R-37**, Harappa (head generally north) |
| **Post-cremation / fractional**, Late Harappan | **Cemetery H**, Harappa |
| **Fractional** (bones buried after exposure) | Mohenjodaro, Harappa |
| **Joint / double** burial | **Lothal** (UPPSC 2016) |
| **Dog with human**, oval pit | **Ropar** |
| **Pot** burials | **Surkotada** |
| North–south burials; women with jasper, agate beads, shell bangles | **Rakhigarhi** (Mound 7) |
| Large cemetery of human burials | **Sanauli** (Baghpat, UP) (UPPSC 2004) |
- **Kalibangan – three types**: extended burials in rectangular pits; pot burials; circular pits with grave goods but **no skeleton**.
- Grave goods (pottery, ornaments, mirrors, food) → belief in an **afterlife** and status differences, not cremation.
### 6. DRESS, ORNAMENTS, COSMETICS
- **Cotton and wool**; two garments; **Priest-King wears a shawl**; **no proof of stitching**.
- **Lipstick** = **Chanhudaro**; also **bronze mirrors, ivory combs, collyrium sticks**.
### 7. PASTIMES
- **Toy factory** = **Chanhudaro**; terracotta carts, animals, marbles, rattles, whistles, tops. **Dice** at Harappa; board games, possibly proto-chess.
### 8. WARFARE AND WEAPONS
- **Maces, axes, spears**, bows and arrows of **copper and bronze**; few and simple → relatively **peaceful**.
- **No horse-drawn chariots** (UPSC 2013).
- **Huddled skeletons** at Mohenjodaro – once "massacre", now **multiple burials**.
