## U0215 | WOMEN IN THE NATIONAL MOVEMENT IN ANDHRA – DURGABAI DESHMUKH, UNNAVA LAKSHMIBAYAMMA, DUVVURI SUBBAMMA
### 1. BACKGROUND
- **Gandhi's 1921 coastal tour** (Kakinada, Rajahmundry, Eluru, Machilipatnam, Chirala, Nellore) drew women into the struggle.
- Women joined the NCM (1921), the Salt Satyagraha (1930) and the Quit India Movement (1942).
- **Annie Besant** = first woman INC president (**1917**); **Sarojini Naidu** = first **Indian** woman president (**1925**).

### 3. DURGABAI DESHMUKH
#### 3.1 Early Role
- **April 1921, Kakinada**, during Gandhi's visit: collected and donated **Rs 5,000**; arranged his meeting with **Devadasis and Muslim women**; **translated his speech** into Telugu.
- As a volunteer, she **stopped Jawaharlal Nehru at the gate** for having no pass (Vijayawada AICC 1921, or Kakinada Congress 1923 in many accounts).
- **CDM**: prominent in the **Salt Satyagraha in Madras city**, with K. Nageswara Rao and T. Prakasam.
#### 3.2 Institutions, Titles and Posts
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Founded | **Andhra Mahila Sabha** (Madras, 1938); **Andhra Aatmiya Sabha** |
| Titles | **"Andhra Mahila"**; **"Iron Lady"**; honorary doctorate from **Andhra University** |
| After 1947 | Member of the **Constituent Assembly** – one of its "founding mothers" |
| Social welfare | **First chairperson, Central Social Welfare Board (1953)** |
| Awards | **Paul G. Hoffman award**; **Padma Vibhushan (1975)** – not 1965 |
- APPSC 2023 asked which statements on her were NOT true; the question was **withdrawn** for a discrepancy.
- **17 Apr 2026**: Pawan Kalyan named Durgabai among the Constitution's founding mothers while welcoming the **Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam Amendment Bill, 2026** (33% reservation), introduced in the Lok Sabha.
### 4. UNNAVA LAKSHMIBAYAMMA AND SARADA NIKETANAM
#### 4.1 Sarada Niketanam, Guntur
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Founders | **Unnava Lakshminarayana** with wife **Unnava Lakshmibayamma** (G2 2019 key: Unnava Lakshminarayana) |
| 1902 | The couple ran a **widow asylum**; she held the **first widow remarriage** there |
| 1922 | **Women's Gurukulam** founded; the widow asylum merged into it |
| Role | Sheltered widows and destitute girls, taught girls; a "hotbed" of the freedom movement |
#### 4.2 Movements
- **NCM**: led the **Guntur hartals** after her husband's arrest over the **Palnadu forest (pullari) satyagraha**; 8 women were arrested with 28 men in that satyagraha.
- **Unnava Lakshminarayana** visited Palnadu in **July 1921** to study the Forest Satyagraha (not the Vandemataram Movement) and was arrested.
- Took personal responsibility for Guntur women's part in all Congress movements.
- **Salt Satyagraha, 9 April 1930**: led women in **Krishna and Guntur**, with Rukmini Lakshmipathi.
- Described as the **dancer who followed Gandhi** (as Amrapali followed the Buddha).
### 5. DUVVURI SUBBAMMA
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Title | **"Desabandhavi"** |
| FIRST | **First Andhra woman jailed**; **second in India** (first: **Parvati Devi of Punjab**) |
| Institution | National school **Sanatana Vidyalayam, Rajahmundry** |
| NCM | Attended the **Vijayawada AICC (1921)**; arrested in Chirala-Perala |
| CDM | **6 April 1930**: among the first to make salt at **Chollangi (East Godavari)**; arrested at Rajahmundry → **Nellore jail** |
| QIM | **Lathi charge at Peddapuram (1942)** |
!TRAP Rukmini Lakshmipathi = first woman arrested in the Salt Satyagraha; Duvvuri Subbamma = first Andhra woman jailed.

### 10. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. **Andhra Mahila Sabha** = Durgabai (1938), not Sarojini Naidu.
2. **Devarakonda 1931** Mahila Sabha = Nizam's Andhra Mahasabha (T. Varalakshmamma), not Durgabai's.
3. Durgabai's **Padma Vibhushan = 1975**, not 1965.
4. Subbamma = **second** in India jailed; first = Parvati Devi.
5. **Chollangi** = Subbamma; **Madras city** = Durgabai; **Krishna-Guntur** = Lakshmibayamma.
6. Sarada Niketanam: **1902** asylum vs **1922** Gurukulam.
7. Besant (1917) = first woman INC president; Sarojini (1925) = first Indian woman.
8. Unnava Lakshminarayana's July 1921 visit was to **Palnadu** (forest satyagraha).
