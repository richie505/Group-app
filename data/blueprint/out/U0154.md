## U0154 | DUTCH IN INDIA – VOC, FACTORIES (PULICAT, MACHILIPATNAM, NAGAPATTINAM), BATTLE OF COLACHEL
### 1. VOC – FORMATION, CHARTER, ASIAN HQ
- **Vereenigde Oostindische Compagnie (VOC)**, formed **20 March 1602** by **merging Dutch companies**.
- Charter from the **Dutch States-General**: sovereign powers to **wage war, build forts, sign treaties**.
- **Asian HQ**: **Batavia (Jakarta)**; focus on the **Spice Islands** (Indonesia), not India.
!TRAP VOC = 1602; "1592" is a misprint.

### 2. DUTCH FACTORIES IN ANDHRA – YEAR AND ORDER
| Factory | Year | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Machilipatnam** | **1605** | **First Dutch** (and first formal European) factory in Andhra; **Van der Haghen**; permission of **Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah** (Golconda) |
| **Petapuli** (Nizampatnam) | 1606 | **Krishna delta** weaving centre (not the Dutch HQ) |
| **Pulicat** | **1610** | Permission of the **Chandragiri** (Vijayanagara) king; **Fort Geldria**; Coromandel HQ till **1690** |
| **Bheemunipatnam** | **1641** | Dutch cemetery |
| **Palakollu (Palicol)** | **1652** | **Godavari delta** weaving centre |
| **Draksharama** | – | Dutch trading post |
| **Jagannathapuram / Koringa** | 1736 / 1802 | Koringa – shipbuilding |
- Subordinate factories in the **Krishna-Godavari weaving belt**: Draksharama, Nizampatnam, **Narsapuram**, **Gollapalem**, Petapoli.
- Other Dutch posts: Jagannayakapuram, Gollapalem, Narasapur, Dronadam.
- The **Portuguese traded at Machilipatnam earlier** (APPSC 2019 key).
- Dutch Andhra network ran from **Machilipatnam to Bheemunipatnam**.
- Dutch were the **first in Andhra to get the right to mint coins** (privilege from local rulers).
!KEY APPSC G2 2017: first Dutch factory in AP = Machilipatnam. APPSC-GS 2024 match: Dutch Masulipatnam **1605**, British **1611**.
!TRAP Pulicat: factory and HQ from **1610** (some give the settlement as 1609); a Coromandel Directorate sat there from 1616. Among PYQ options, "earliest" = Pulicat when Machilipatnam is absent.

### 5. TRADE – COROMANDEL PORTS AND ITEMS
| Trade | Items |
|---|---|
| **Exports** | Gems, **diamonds**, **indigo**, cotton, **Kalamkari**, coarse cloth, fine **muslins** |
| **Imports** to Coromandel | **Silk and porcelain** |
| **Carrying trade** | Textiles for East Indies **spices**; **saltpetre (Bihar)**, **opium**, rice; **pepper** from Malabar |

### 6. BATTLES – COLACHEL AND BEDARA
| Battle | Year | Opponents | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Colachel** | **1741** | **Travancore** (**Marthanda Varma**) vs Dutch | Travancore won – **ended Dutch ambitions in Malabar** |
| **Bedara / Chinsura** | **1759** | English (**Clive**; commander **Francis Forde**) vs Dutch (**J.B. Russell**) | Dutch defeated; **withdrew to Indonesia** |
!PAIR Colachel 1741 | Indian ruler vs Dutch | Marthanda Varma || Bedara 1759 | English vs Dutch | Francis Forde
### 7. DECLINE OF THE DUTCH
1. Priority was the **Indonesian spice monopoly**, not Indian territory; Malabar was less profitable than Coromandel cloth.
2. **English rivalry**: Anglo-Dutch conferences of **1613 and 1615** failed.
3. Defeats at **Colachel** and **Bedara**; loss of Andhra strongholds in the Anglo-French wars.
4. **Cochin** passed to the British (**1795**).
5. **Anglo-Dutch Treaty, 1824**: **Pulicat** and remaining settlements transferred; Dutch occupation **1605-1825**.
