## U0074 | BADAMI (VATAPI) CHALUKYAS – PULAKESIN I, PULAKESIN II, AIHOLE INSCRIPTION OF RAVIKIRTI
### 1. DYNASTY AND CAPITAL
- Badami (Vatapi) Chalukyas: **c. 543–753 CE**; rule mainly in the **Deccan and South**, NOT North India.
- Capital **Vatapi** = modern **Badami, Bagalkot district, Karnataka**; sandstone hills around **Agastya lake**.
- Vatapi **fortified by Pulakesin I** – **Badami cliff inscription, 543 CE**. (Kanchi was the Pallava capital.)
### 2. RULERS → ACHIEVEMENT
| Ruler | Reign | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Pulakesin I** | 543–566 | **Real founder**; **Ashvamedha**; walled Vatapi |
| **Kirtivarman I** | 566–598 | Took **Kadambas, Gangas, Mauryas of Konkan**; "**night of doom**" to Nalas, Mauryas, Kadambas (Aihole); Bahusuvarna-Agnishtoma (Mahakuta pillar) |
| **Mangalesha** | 597–609 | **Regent**; **earliest rock-cut caves at Badami** |
| **Pulakesin II** | **610–642** | Zenith; greatest Badami Chalukya |
| **Vikramaditya I** | 655–680 | Revived power after Pallava occupation of Vatapi |
| **Vikramaditya II** | 733–746 | Took **Kanchi thrice but spared it**; queen **Lokamahadevi** built **Virupaksha temple, Pattadakal** |
| **Kirtivarman II** | 746–753 | **Last**; overthrown by Rashtrakuta **Dantidurga** |
- Other sacrifices: **Vajapeya, Hiranyagarbha** – claim to **Kshatriya** status.
### 3. SUCCESSION WAR
- Kirtivarman I left minor sons → brother **Mangalesha** ruled as regent.
- Pulakesin II killed Mangalesha at **Elapattu-Simbige** (Bana territory, c. 609–10 CE).
### 4. PULAKESIN II
#### 4.1 Events
| Year (CE) | Event |
|---|---|
| 617 | **Satara inscription**: brother **Kubja Vishnuvardhana** viceroy of Vengi |
| 618–19 | Stopped **Harsha** on the **Narmada** |
| 624 | Vengi independent = **Eastern Chalukyas** (capital **Pithapuram**) |
| c. 626 | Embassy with Persian king **Khusrau II** (Ajanta Cave 1 painting of it now doubted) |
| 634–35 | **Aihole inscription** |
| 642 | Pallava **Narasimhavarman I** destroyed Vatapi → title **Vatapikonda** |
- Eastern campaign: **Kosala & Kalinga** → Vengi → defeated the **Durjayas** → then **Mahendravarman I**.
#### 4.2 Titles and Foreign Accounts
- **Dakshinapatheshwara** ("Lord of the Deccan") vs Harsha's Uttarapatheshwara; **Paramesvara** after beating **Harsha** (not Mahendravarman I); **Satyashraya, Prithvivallabha**.
- **Hiuen Tsang** visited; praised him alongside Harsha and Narasimhavarman.
- China–India relations under the Chalukyas: **Ma Twan-lin** (Matwalin).
- **Lohnara inscription (630 CE)** does NOT list Harsha; Aihole does.

### 5. AIHOLE INSCRIPTION
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Author | **Ravikirti**, **Jain** court poet of Pulakesin II |
| Language / script | **Sanskrit** prasasti, **Southern Brahmi** |
| Date | **634–35 CE** (Saka era) |
| Place | **Meguti Jain temple**, Aihole |
| Poets named | **Kalidasa** and **Bharavi** (Ravikirti claimed fame like theirs) |
- Victories: **Harsha** (Narmada), **Kadambas** (Banavasi), Gangas, Alupas, Mauryas of Konkan, Latas, Malavas, Gurjaras, **Kosala, Kalinga, Pishtapura** (Pithapuram), **Pallavas**.
### 6. ANDHRA INSCRIPTIONS AND PLACE NAMES
| Inscription | Event / name |
|---|---|
| **Maruturu (Marturu)** | Calls **Mangalagiri "Mangalapura"** |
| Maruturu and **Kopparam** | Defeated **Mahendravarman I**; won land between **Krishna and Godavari** |
| Kubja Vishnuvardhana | **Chipurupalli**, Kopparam, Chejarala, Timmapuram |
| **Pune plates (669)**, Vishnuvardhana II | First reference to **Guntur as Kumturu** |
- **Dandin** called Vengi **Andhra Nagaram**.
!TRAP Mangalapura = Marturu inscription (APPSC-GS 2018 key), not Kopparam.

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- Real founder: Pulakesin I; last: Kirtivarman II.
- First South Indian gold coins: Pulakesin II.
- Earliest Badami caves: Mangalesha.

5. **Vatapikonda** = Narasimhavarman I.
6. **Paramesvara** came after Harsha, not Mahendravarman I.
