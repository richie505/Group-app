## U0672 | INDIA – HORTICULTURE, PLANTATION CROPS, LIVESTOCK, FISHERIES AND AGRO-BASED ACTIVITIES
### 1. INDIA'S WORLD RANK
| Rank | Commodity |
|---|---|
| **1st** | **Milk**, **dry onions**, coconuts; rice (overtook China, 150.18 vs 145.28 MT, Jan 2026) |
| **2nd** | **Fruits, vegetables** (China 1st), potatoes, wheat, sugarcane, **eggs**, **silk** (China 1st), **fish** |
- **Milk**: **247.87 MT** (2024-25, NDDB); per capita **485 g/day** (239.30 MT and 471 g/day in 2023-24; 146.31 MT in 2014-15; CAGR 5.62%).
- **Eggs**: India 2nd; **149.11 billion** (2024-25).
- Fruit share of world: key **15%**; 2014 data – India 2nd with 13.6%, China 20.9%. India 1st in "other fruits n.e.c." (FAOSTAT 2024).
- APPSC G2 2025: "largest producer of milk" + "2nd largest of fruits, vegetables and sugar" → **both correct**.
!TRAP APPSC G1 2019: "India is the biggest producer of vegetables" – **incorrect** (2nd).
### 2. HORTICULTURE – STATES AND REGIONS
- Horticulture **362.08 MT** (2024-25) – above foodgrains; ~**33% of agri GVA** from under **14%** of cropped area.
- **MIDH**: 60:40; **90:10** for NE and Himalayan States.
| State / place | Produce |
|---|---|
| **Andhra Pradesh** | **Largest fruit producer (~15.6%)**; **2nd in mango**; 1st in cocoa productivity |
| Uttar Pradesh | Largest **mango** producer |
| Maharashtra (Nashik) | **Grapes** |
| **Kinnaur (HP)**, J&K | **Apples** (temperate belt) |
| **Tripura** | Best pineapple; largest **true potato seed** producer |
| **Meghalaya** | Pineapple leader; **Lakadong turmeric** (not Tripura) |
| Bihar | Litchi |
| Arunachal Pradesh | Over 50% of kiwi; **"Orchid State"** – most orchid species; **Sikkim 2nd** |
- **Apple** is NOT tropical – needs cold for flowering.
- **Amrapali** mango = **Dasheri × Neelam**, 1971, IARI Pusa.
- **Pineapple** = *Ananas comosus* (Bromeliaceae).
- **Portuguese** brought cashew, tomato, chilli, potato, tobacco, papaya, pineapple, guava.

### 5. DAIRYING AND LIVESTOCK CENSUS
- **Rashtriya Gokul Mission**: development and conservation of **indigenous bovine breeds**.
- AP **Gokul Gram**: **Chadalawada** cattle-breeding farm, near Ongole, **Prakasam district** (APPSC key marked **Nellore** district).
- **Chintaladeevi (AP)** – **National Kamadhenu Breeding Centre** under Rashtriya Gokul Mission.
- **Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Development Fund**: milk- and meat-processing capacity.
- **Operation Flood** – 1970, **NDDB**; **Verghese Kurien** = Father of White Revolution.
- Livestock GVA grew ~**195%** (FY15-FY24); livestock ~7.1%, fisheries ~8.8% growth.

### 6. FISHERIES
| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Inland** | **Raising and capturing fish in fresh water** (rivers, tanks, reservoirs) + brackish (estuaries, lagoons) (APPSC 2018) |
| **Marine** | Coastal and deep-sea fishing |
| **Capture** | Harvest of **wild stock** |
| **Culture (aquaculture)** | **Controlled farming** – freshwater, brackish, mariculture |
| Cold-water | 5-25°C; trout above 1,500 m; ~3% of inland catch |
- India **2nd** largest fish producer; fisheries = **fastest-growing farm sub-sector** (~8.8%).
- **AP**: nearly **29%** of India's fish; leads **fish and shrimp** and aquaculture; plans to be the world's "**Aqua Hub**" (action plan Jul 2026).
- **West Bengal** – leading fish producer when asked (A true); 90% from inland water, not coastal (R false).
- **Blue Revolution** = pisciculture (fish raising in tanks/enclosures).
- **PMMSY**: **2020**, Department of Fisheries; **NFDB** trains; Central Sector part **100% central**; review Sep 2026.
| Institute | Location |
|---|---|
| CIFT, CMFRI | **Kochi** |
| **CIFA** (freshwater aquaculture) | **Bhubaneswar** |
!TRAP Controlled farming is NOT capture fishery.
### 7. SERICULTURE AND ALLIED ACTIVITIES
| Silk | States | Food plant |
|---|---|---|
| **Mulberry** (most common) | **Karnataka** (largest), AP, TN, WB | Mulberry |
| **Tropical tasar** (kosa) | AP, Telangana, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, MP | **Terminalia** (Nallamaddi), **Sal** |
| **Oak (temperate) tasar** | Manipur, Uttarakhand, HP, J&K | **Oak** |
| **Eri** (endi, "**peace silk**") | Assam, Meghalaya, NE | **Castor**, tapioca |
| **Muga** (golden) | **Assam** (65.6%, GI), Meghalaya (16.67%) | **Som and Soalu** |
- **Muga** endemic to Assam (*Antheraea assamensis*) (APPSC G1 2017); one of the rarest silks.
- India = **only country producing all five** silks; **2nd** in raw silk after **China** (FAO 2023).
- **Karnataka**: largest silk State – 44.15% of mulberry, 41.69% of raw silk (2023-24); **AP 2nd** in raw silk.
- Karnataka + AP once over three-fourths of raw silk; now ~58.99%.
- **Central Silk Board** – **Bengaluru**.
- AP tasar: **East Godavari + ASR (Rampachodavaram)**; 1,660 tribal farmers, 45 villages; cocoons 61,394 MT (2024-25).
| Activity | Product |
|---|---|
| **Apiculture** | Honey, bee-wax (life cycle egg → larva → pupa → adult) |
| **Sericulture** | Silk |
| **Pisciculture** | Fish |
| **Lac culture** | Lac (lac insects) |
| **Floriculture** | Flowers |

### 9. AGRO-PROCESSING
- Processed food ~**23%** of agri-food exports (2024-25) (APPSC 2025).
- **APEDA Act, 1985**.
| Scheme | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **PM Kisan SAMPADA** | Approved **2017** | Central Sector; began as SAMPADA; integrated cold chain; 1,256 units co-financed |
| **Mega Food Parks** | From **2008**; now part of PMKSY | AP: **3 sanctioned, 2 operational** – Srini (Chittoor), Godavari Mega Aqua (West Godavari); APIIC Krishna |
| **PMFME** | **2020** | Aatmanirbhar Bharat; **One District One Product** |
- Agro-industries locate near raw material: AP sugar mills – Godavari districts, Visakhapatnam, Chittoor; dairy – **Anand**.
