## U0281 | CITIZENSHIP – ARTICLES 5-11, CITIZENSHIP ACT 1955, OCI AND CAA 2019
### 1. PART II – ARTICLES 5 TO 11
#### 1.1 Constitutional Framework
- Citizenship = **Part II, Articles 5–11**; it names only who were citizens **at commencement (26 January 1950)**; the rest is left to Parliament (**Art. 11**).
- Neighbouring Parts: **Part I (Arts. 1–4)** Union and territory; **Part III (Arts. 12–35)** Fundamental Rights.
#### 1.2 Article-wise
| Article | Deals with |
|---|---|
| **5** | **At commencement** – **domiciled** in India + (a) born in India, or (b) **either parent born in India**, or (c) **ordinarily resident 5 years** before 1950 |
| **6** | **Migrants from Pakistan to India** – permit cut-off **19 July 1948** |
| **7** | **Migrants to Pakistan** after **1 March 1947** (returnees under resettlement permit) |
| **8** | **Persons of Indian Origin abroad** – register at an Indian **diplomatic or consular** office |
| **9** | **Voluntary acquisition of foreign citizenship → automatic loss** |
| **10** | **Continuance** of rights of citizenship, subject to Parliament's law |
| **11** | **Parliament** regulates acquisition, termination and all related matters by law |
!TRAP Art. 6 = migrants FROM Pakistan; Art. 7 = migrants TO Pakistan.
### 2. COMPETENT AUTHORITY TO REGULATE CITIZENSHIP
| Authority | Role |
|---|---|
| **Parliament (Art. 11)** | **Alone** legislates on citizenship → **Citizenship Act, 1955** |
| **Union Home Ministry** | Frames **Citizenship Rules** (2009, amended 2026) under **Section 18** |
| **Election Commission** | Checks citizenship **only for electoral rolls** (Art. 324); doubtful cases go to the Centre |
- **No power** to make citizenship laws: **State Legislatures**, the **President**, the **Election Commission**.
!TRAP One exam key gave "the President" for "exclusive power of determining citizenship"; constitutionally, rules for getting citizenship are set by **Parliament (Art. 11)**.
### 3. SINGLE vs DUAL CITIZENSHIP
- India: **single citizenship** and a **single domicile** for Union and States – a **unitary feature**; allegiance only to the Union; **no separate State citizenship**.
- **Source**: **Britain**; **Canada** also has single citizenship.
- **USA** and **Switzerland**: **dual** – national + State/cantonal.
- India does **not allow dual citizenship** (Art. 9) – APPSC 2023: A and R both true, R explains A.
| Country | Citizenship |
|---|---|
| India, Britain, Canada | Single |
| USA, Switzerland | Dual (national + state) |
#### 3.1 Citizen-only Rights and Offices
- Rights: **Arts. 15, 16, 19, 29, 30**; right to **vote and contest**.
- Offices: **President, Vice-President, judges, Governor, Attorney General**.
- **President (Art. 58)**: in India both a citizen **by birth and a naturalised** citizen are eligible; in the **USA only a natural-born** citizen.
### 4. ACQUISITION – FIVE MODES (B-D-R-N-I)
#### 4.1 Modes and Sections
| Mode | Section | Key condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Birth** | **3** | **jus soli** (modified) – see 4.2 |
| **Descent** | **4** | Born **outside India**; **either parent** an Indian citizen at birth; birth registered at an Indian consulate – **jus sanguinis** |
| **Registration** | **5** | **PIO ordinarily resident 7 years**; **spouses** and minor children of citizens |
| **Naturalisation** | **6** | **12 years** (11 of preceding 14 + last 12 months); **oath of allegiance**; waivable for distinguished service to science, art, literature, world peace |
| **Incorporation of territory** | **7** | Centre notifies citizens when a territory joins – **Goa, Pondicherry, Sikkim** |
#### 4.2 Citizenship by Birth – Three Periods
| Born in India | Rule |
|---|---|
| **26 Jan 1950 – 30 Jun 1987** | Citizen **regardless of parents' nationality** |
| **1 Jul 1987 – 2 Dec 2004** | **At least one parent** an Indian citizen |
| **From 3 Dec 2004** | **Both** parents citizens, or **one Indian and the other not an illegal migrant** |
- **Excluded** from birth: children of **foreign diplomats** and **enemy aliens**.
#### 4.3 What is NOT a Mode
- **Marriage** – not a separate mode (a registration category).
- **Depositing money in Indian banks**.
- **Ownership of land or other property** in India.
!KEY Five modes (APPSC 2023): Birth, Descent, Registration, Naturalisation, Incorporation of territory.
### 5. LOSS – THREE MODES (R-T-D)
| Mode | Section | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Renunciation** | **8** | **Voluntary**, by declaration; **minor children** lose it too but may **resume within 1 year of majority** |
| **Termination** | **9** | **Automatic** on voluntarily acquiring foreign citizenship (linked to **Art. 9**) |
| **Deprivation** | **10** | **Compulsory**, by the **Centre**; only citizens by **naturalisation, registration or Art. 5(c)** |
- Deprivation grounds: **fraud**, **disloyalty**, **unlawful trade with / helping an enemy** during war, **2 years' jail within 5 years** of naturalisation/registration.
- **Neither give nor take** citizenship: bank deposits, property, **marrying a foreigner**, **criticising the government**.
!TRAP "A naturalised citizen can never be deprived of citizenship" – FALSE (Section 10).
#### 5.1 Other Key Sections
| Section | Subject |
|---|---|
| **7A** | Registration of **OCI cardholders** |
| **14A** | **National Register of Indian Citizens** and national identity card |
| **18** | Power to make **Rules** |
### 6. CITIZENSHIP (AMENDMENT) ACTS
| Year | Change |
|---|---|
| **1986** | Birth from **1 July 1987** needs **one parent** an Indian citizen |
| **2003** | Birth rule from **3 Dec 2004** (other parent not an illegal migrant); **OCI** registration; **Sec. 14A (NRC)** |
| **2005** | **OCI widened** |
| **2015** | **PIO card merged with OCI** → "OCI Cardholder" |
| **2019 (CAA)** | Route for **6 communities from 3 countries**; naturalisation residence cut to **5 years** |
- **No amendment in 2008** (APPSC GS 2023 key).
- Basis moved from **jus soli to jus sanguinis**.
!TRAP The 1986 Act took effect on 1 July 1987 – there is no separate "1987 amendment".
### 7. CITIZENSHIP AMENDMENT ACT, 2019
#### 7.1 Passage
| Stage | Date |
|---|---|
| Lok Sabha | **9 December 2019** |
| Rajya Sabha | **11 December 2019** |
| **President's assent** | **12 December 2019** |
| **Rules effective** | **11 March 2024** |
#### 7.2 Provisions
- Countries: **Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Pakistan**.
- Communities: **Hindu, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi, Christian** – **Muslims not covered**.
- Cut-off: entered India **on or before 31 December 2014**; citizenship counts **from date of entry**.
- Naturalisation residence cut to **5 years** (Third Schedule of the Act amended).
- **Exempt**: **Sixth Schedule** tribal areas of **Assam, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Tripura** and **Inner Line Permit** areas (**Arunachal, Nagaland, Mizoram, Manipur**).
#### 7.3 Latest Developments (2026)
- **Citizenship (Amendment) Rules, 2026**: affidavit on any **Pakistani, Afghan or Bangladeshi passport**; **surrender within 15 days** of approval.
- **19 Aug 2026 MHA order**: applications in **8 States/UTs** (incl. West Bengal, Assam, J&K, Ladakh) processed by **District Collectors**, not Empowered Committees.
- **Challenge** under **Art. 14** (religion- and country-based classification): **250+ petitions** before a Bench under **CJI Surya Kant**; final hearing from **5 May 2026**; still pending.
### 8. DIASPORA TERMS AND OCI
#### 8.1 Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **NRI** | **Indian citizen** ordinarily residing abroad; holds an Indian passport |
| **PIO** | **Foreign citizen** whose ancestors were Indian nationals; card **merged into OCI (2015)** |
| **OCI** | Foreign national of Indian origin registered under **Sec. 7A** |
| **Commonwealth citizen** | Citizen of a member country of the **Commonwealth of Nations** |
| **Single citizenship** | One citizenship for the whole of India |
!PAIR NRI | Indian citizen | Lives abroad || PIO / OCI | Foreign citizen | Indian origin
#### 8.2 OCI Cardholder
- **Eligible**: foreign citizens of Indian origin whose ancestors were Indian nationals, with **spouses and children**.
- **Barred**: if **self, parent or grandparent** was a citizen of **Pakistan or Bangladesh**.
- **Benefit**: **lifelong visa**; OCI card alone enough to enter India (**since 2016**).
- **Not available**: **voting**, contesting elections, constitutional offices, public employment as a rule; OCI holders are **not citizens**.
- **Rules 2026**: **e-OCI cards**, **central OCI database**, online application and renunciation; a **minor cannot hold another passport** with an Indian one.
### 9. PROOF OF CITIZENSHIP, NRC AND NPR
- **Not proof**: **Aadhaar** (identity only, **Sec. 9, Aadhaar Act**), **voter ID**, **ration card** (MEA, Jun 2026), **passport** – a travel document; **Sec. 20, Passports Act, 1967** lets non-citizens get passports.
- **NRC** (citizens only) is a **subset of NPR** (all usual residents); **NRC draft published for Assam**.
### 10. UNION AND ITS TERRITORY – ARTICLES 1 TO 4
#### 10.1 Union vs Territory
| Union of India | Territory of India (Art. 1(3)) |
|---|---|
| **States only** | **States + UTs + territories that may be acquired** |
- India can **acquire foreign territory** (cession, treaty, conquest) – **Puducherry, Goa**.
- **Art. 1**: "India, that is Bharat, shall be a Union of States"; States listed in the **First Schedule**.
- **Art. 3**: Bill on the **President's recommendation**; President refers it to the **State legislature concerned** for views; passed by **simple majority**.
- **Art. 4**: such laws are **not Art. 368 amendments**.
#### 10.2 Formation of States and UTs
| Unit | Date | Instrument |
|---|---|---|
| **Andhra State** (first linguistic) | **1 Oct 1953** | Andhra State Act, 1953 – after **Potti Sriramulu's** death (**15 Dec 1952**) |
| **Andhra Pradesh** | **1 Nov 1956** | **States Reorganisation Act, 1956** |
| **Sikkim** | Associate 1974; full **1975** | **35th** (Art. 2A, Tenth Schedule), then **36th** – **22nd State** |
| **Mizoram** | **20 Feb 1987** | **State of Mizoram Act, 1986**; **53rd Amendment** |
| **Telangana** | **2 Jun 2014** | AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 |
| **J&K and Ladakh UTs** | **31 Oct 2019** | J&K Reorganisation Act, 2019 |
- **Government of Sikkim Act, 1974** set up its first elected government.
- **States Reorganisation Commission**: **Fazl Ali** (chairman), **K.M. Panikkar, H.N. Kunzru**; **SRA 1956**: **14 States, 6 UTs**; **Zonal Councils**.
!NUM Order: Andhra State 1953 → Andhra Pradesh 1956 → Mizoram 1987 → Telangana 2014 → J&K UTs 2019.
!TRAP APPSC G2 2025 key said 31 Oct 2020 for the J&K Act; correct date = 31 Oct 2019.
#### 10.3 Cession and UT Administration
- **Berubari reference (1960)**: territory cannot be ceded under Art. 3 → **9th Amendment, 1960** (Art. 368).
- **8 UTs** now, after **Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu** merged on **26 Jan 2020**.
- **Art. 239**: UTs run by the **President through an Administrator**.
