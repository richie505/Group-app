## U0743 | INDIA'S ENERGY NEEDS AND DEFICIT – PRIMARY ENERGY DEMAND, PER-CAPITA CONSUMPTION, PEAK DEFICIT
### 1. TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **1 unit** | **1 kWh** = 1,000 W × 3,600 s = **3.6 × 10⁶ J**; units = watt × hours / 1000 |
| **Energy intensity** | Energy per unit of GDP; falling = growth **decoupling** |
| **Peak demand** | Highest draw in a **15-minute** interval |
| Energy / peak shortage | Unmet energy in **MU** / unmet peak in **MW** |
| **Energy security** | Four A's: availability, accessibility, affordability, acceptability (**not accumulation**) |
- Energy crisis = danger that fossil fuels run out.
!NUM 100 W × 10 h = 1 unit (₹5 at ₹5/unit); 100 W × 4 h = 0.4 unit; 5 × 100 W × 20 h = 10 units; 60 W × 5 h × 30 days = 9 units.

### 3. DEMAND, MIX AND PER CAPITA
- India = **third-largest energy consumer**, **second-largest coal consumer**; demand to **triple by 2047**.
- **Coal**: ~55% of energy mix, **74%+ of generation**. Second source after fossil fuels: **solar** (UPPSC 2021).
- **Non-fossil capacity**: 32% (2014) → **51% (2025)**; 50% target crossed five years early.
- **Per-capita electricity**: **957 kWh (FY14) → 1,460 kWh (FY25)**.
- Per-capita energy ~**one-third of world average**; large urban-rural gap.
- **HDI link** (**APPSC 2016**): a minimum per-capita consumption is needed for basic well-being.
### 4. SECTORS AND DRIVERS
- **Industry** uses ~**50%** of electricity and stays largest by 2040 (8,764 TWh BAU); **cooking** demand falls as clean fuels replace biomass.
- Household electrification ~99%; **Saubhagya** ~26 million households.
- Drivers: **EVs, farm pumps, appliances, cooling, data centres, telecom**; peak **183 GW (Dec 2020) → 250+ GW (Apr 2026)**, ~37%.
### 5. PEAK DEFICIT AND REGIONAL GAPS
- Shortage: **10–15%** earlier → **4.2% (FY14)** → **0.03% (2025)** → **zero by Nov 2025** (Economic Survey 2025-26).
- Record peak **270.8 GW (May 2026)**, ~90 GW above 2019.
- 25 Apr 2026: no solar-hour shortage; **2% non-solar deficit** → **evening peak** (6–11 p.m.) drives the **storage** push; Delhi uses **ToD tariffs, smart meters**.
### 6. FUEL GAP, INTENSITY AND PLANS
| Fuel | Gap |
|---|---|
| Crude oil | ~88% imported |
| Gas / LNG | **~50–51%** imported; **Hormuz** risk to **LPG** (2026) |
| World reserves share | 17% of population vs **oil 0.4%**, gas 0.6%, **coal 7%** |
| NDC | Intensity cut vs 2005 | Non-fossil capacity |
|---|---|---|
| Aug 2022 | **45% by 2030** | 50% |
| 2031–35 | 47% by 2035 | **60%** (not 50%) |
- Emission intensity already **36% below 2005**; **BEE + NMEEE** (NAPCC) under **Energy Conservation Act 2001** via **ESCOs**.
- **AT&C losses 22.62% → 15.04%** (FY25).
- **Draft National Electricity Policy 2026** (Ministry of Power): replaces 2005 policy; **100 GW pumped storage by 2047**.
- **COP31 "35 by 35"**: electricity = 35% of final energy by 2035. **Nuclear**: 24×7 baseload, cuts imports.
- **AP**: CEA Resource Adequacy Plan – peak **30,927 MW** by 2035-36, ~**5,588 MW** new thermal; **APERC** cleared first **1,600 MW**; Sep 2026 roadmap: **1,500 MW/6,000 MWh BESS**, **NHPC VGF 500 MW/1,000 MWh**, **APGENCO may import coal**.
