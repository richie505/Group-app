## U0516 | SECOND-GENERATION REFORMS AND THE REFORM RECORD SINCE 2014; LABOUR CODES, IBC, GST AS REFORMS
### 1. REFORM GENERATIONS
| First (1991-2000) | Second (2000s on) | Third |
|---|---|---|
| Tariff cuts, delicensing, FDI automatic route, financial opening | **Factor markets** (land, labour, capital), public sector, oil sector, government institutions, CCI and regulators | **Functional PRIs** |
!TRAP **Legal System Reforms** NOT on Government's second-generation list (UPPSC 2009 key). "Greater rigour in labour laws" is NOT a reform.
- Factor markets: **land** - DILRMP, ULPIN, NGDRS; **labour** - four Codes; **capital** - IBC, capital-market reform. Next-generation pillars start with land, labour.
### 2. FOUR LABOUR CODES
- **29** central laws → **4 Codes**; sections 1,232 → 480. Labour = **Concurrent List**.
- In force **21 November 2025**; draft rules **Dec 2025**; Central Rules May 2026.
| Code | Laws | Key laws / provisions |
|---|---|---|
| **Wages, 2019** | **4** | **Minimum Wages 1948, Payment of Wages 1936, Payment of Bonus 1965, Equal Remuneration 1976**; universal minimum wage; Centre's **national floor wage**; no gender discrimination; 8-hour day |
| **Industrial Relations, 2020** | **3** | Trade Unions 1926, Standing Orders 1946, **Industrial Disputes 1947**; lay-off permission **100 → 300** |
| **Social Security, 2020** | **9** | EPF, ESI, **Employees' Compensation**; **26-week maternity**; gig workers |
| **OSH, 2020** | **13** | **Factories Act**, **Contract Labour Act 1970** |
!TRAP Contract Labour Act → **OSH Code**, NOT Wages Code (4 laws, not 29).
- **2018 Standing Orders Rules**: **fixed-term employment in all sectors**; no termination notice for temporary workmen.
- Gains (ES 2025-26): formalisation 60.4% → 75.5%; unions call them anti-worker.
### 3. GST AS STRUCTURAL REFORM
- **101st Amendment Act, 2016** (122nd Bill); in force **1 July 2017**; **Kelkar** Committee; **Canadian dual model**; **Assam** ratified first.
| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| **246A** | Power to levy GST |
| **269A** | **IGST** on inter-State supply |
| **279A** | **GST Council** |
| **366(12A)** | Definition |
| 286 | No State tax on supplies outside State / import-export |
- Destination-based; input tax credit ends cascading; subsumed **State VAT** and Central excise, service tax, CST.
- **GST 2.0** (Aug 2025): rate rationalisation, **40% slab**: sin/luxury goods.
### 4. IBC 2016
- CIRP **180 days** + one **90-day** extension; outer limit **330 days** (2019 amendment). **NCLT** adjudicates; **IBBI** regulates.
### 5. INSURANCE REFORM
- **Malhotra Committee (1993)** recommended **private entry** → **IRDA Act 1999**.
- **Sabka Bima Sabki Raksha Act 2025**: FDI **74% → 100%**.

#### 6.1 Ease of Doing Business
- World Bank rank **142 (2014) → 63 (2019)**.
- **AP No. 1 since BRAP 2016**; Single Desk Portal tied to VCIC EoDB loan. BRAP 2020 Achievers: MP, HP, Maharashtra, Odisha, Uttarakhand, UP.
#### 6.2 Power and PSB Reforms
- **UDAY** (2015): States absorb **75% of DISCOM debt**; **RDSS** (2021-26, ₹3,03,758 crore): AT&C losses **12-15%**, **ACS-ARR gap zero** by 2024-25; smart meters.
- **PSB recapitalisation**: **Indradhanush** (Aug 2015) ₹70,000 crore; **₹2.11 lakh crore** (Oct 2017), ₹1.35 lakh crore via **recap bonds**.
- **EASE** (Jan 2018): PSB agenda: CLEAN, SMART banking; EASE 9.0: FY2026-27.
