### 4. KEY INTERVENTIONS – ARTICLES
| Article / provision | Ambedkar's view |
|---|---|
| **Article 32** (Constitutional Remedies) | The **"heart and soul"** of the Constitution |
| **Article 17** | Abolishes **untouchability**, makes it punishable |
| **Articles 15–16** | Non-discrimination, equal opportunity |
| **Article 46** | Welfare of **SCs/STs** |
| **DPSP** | Equally important for social and economic justice; a "**novel feature**", like the instruments of instructions |
| **Emergency (Art. 356)** | Hoped it would remain a **"dead letter"** |
- **Universal adult suffrage**, including women, from the start.
!PAIR Article 32 | "heart and soul" | remedies || Article 356 | "dead letter" | emergency
### 5. FEDERALISM AND THE 1935 ACT
- A **"Union of States"**: the Union is **indestructible**; states have **no right to secede**.
- Can work as **unitary or federal** as times require; favoured a **strong Centre** to protect weaker sections.
- Criticised as a **"copy of the Government of India Act, 1935"** and a **"lawyers' paradise"**.
- Reply (**4 November 1948**): no need to **apologise** for borrowing tested provisions from the **1935 Act**.
- **Constitutional morality** "is not a natural sentiment; it **has to be cultivated**" – term taken from **George Grote** (4 Nov 1948).
### 6. FINAL SPEECH – 25 NOVEMBER 1949
- "**Political democracy cannot last unless there lies at the base of it social and economic democracy.**"
#### 6.1 Three Warnings
1. Give up **bloody methods**; civil disobedience, non-cooperation and satyagraha now are the **"grammar of anarchy"**.
2. Do not lay liberties at the feet of a great man – **bhakti or hero-worship** in politics leads to **dictatorship**.
3. Turn **political democracy into social democracy** – on **26 January 1950** India enters **"a life of contradictions"** (political equality, social and economic inequality).
!TRAP He did NOT call for abolishing the parliamentary system. 4 Nov 1948 = 1935 Act reply + constitutional morality; 25 Nov 1949 = three warnings.
### 7. HINDU CODE BILL
- Aim: codify Hindu personal law – **monogamy**, **divorce**, **inheritance rights of daughters and widows**.
- **Fate:** the omnibus bill **stalled** in Parliament; Ambedkar **resigned as Law Minister in September 1951**.
- Later passed in parts (**1955–56**): **Hindu Marriage Act 1955** (monogamy, divorce), **Hindu Succession Act 1956** (daughters as Class I heirs), Hindu Minority and Guardianship Act, Hindu Adoptions and Maintenance Act.
- **2005** amendment to the Succession Act gave daughters equal **coparcenary** rights.
