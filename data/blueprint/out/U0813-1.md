### 1. AP BUDGET 2026-27 - SIZE AND DEFICITS
#### 1.1 Budget at a Glance
- Presented on **14 February 2026** by Finance Minister **Payyavula Keshav**.
| Item | 2026-27 (BE) |
|---|---|
| Total outlay | **₹3,32,205 crore** (~₹3.32 lakh crore) |
| Revenue expenditure | ~₹2,56,143 crore |
| Capital expenditure | ~₹53,915 crore (₹53,915.38 crore; ₹39,497.56 crore in 2025-26 RE) |

| Revenue deficit | ~₹22,003 crore = **1.1% of GSDP** |
| Projected GSDP 2026-27 | ₹19,75,073 crore (12% growth) |

!TRAP Fiscal deficit target 2026-27 is 3.8% of GSDP; 4.6% is the 2025-26 revised figure.
#### 1.2 Separate Agriculture Budget 2026-27
- **₹53,752.12 crore** for agriculture and allied departments, presented by Agriculture Minister **K. Atchannaidu**.

!KEY The agriculture budget is a part of the ₹3.32 lakh crore outlay, not an addition to it.

### 4. DEBT, BORROWINGS AND REVENUE
- Public debt to rise to **₹7.11 lakh crore** in 2026-27 (₹6.35 lakh crore in 2025-26); about **36% of GSDP**.
- Total borrowings proposed in 2026-27: ₹97,315.41 crore.
- Own tax revenue (BE 2026-27): ₹1,25,845.78 crore.
- Share in central taxes (devolution, BE 2026-27): ₹64,362.16 crore.
!TRAP ₹97,315 crore is fresh borrowing in one year; ₹7.11 lakh crore is the outstanding debt stock.
### 5. SASCI 2026-27 - CENTRAL CAPITAL ASSISTANCE
- AP to get **₹8,226 crore** under **Special Assistance to States for Capital Investment (SASCI) 2026-27**; **₹3,486 crore** already released.
- SASCI = **50-year interest-free loans** to States for capital works; under the **Ministry of Finance**.

### 9. HUMAN DEVELOPMENT, VISION AND AIDED PROJECTS
#### 9.1 Human Development
- AP **Maternal Mortality Ratio**: **45** (2018-20) vs India 97.
- **Abhyasa School, Vijayawada**: **1st in India, Budget Schools** category, **Cfore survey** (**949 points**; 64,567 respondents); award in New Delhi, 22-23 September; parameters - teaching, leadership, personalised learning, infrastructure, life skills, value for money.
#### 9.2 Swarna Andhra @2047 Vision
- Launched **December 2024** by CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu**, prepared with **NITI Aayog** inputs; action plan March 2025.
- Targets by **2047**: **USD 2.4 trillion** economy (₹308 lakh crore GSDP); per capita income **USD 42,000**; zero poverty.
- **10 guiding principles (Padi Sutralu)**: e.g. zero poverty, employment, water security, deep tech.
#### 9.3 Externally Aided Project
- **AP Economic Restructuring Project** - **World Bank**; loan signed 4 Feb 1999 ($543.2 million); aim: primary health, nutrition, primary education, rural assets.
