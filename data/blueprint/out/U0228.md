## U0228 | GROWTH OF COMMUNAL POLITICS – SEPARATE ELECTORATES, TWO-NATION THEORY, LAHORE RESOLUTION 1940
### 1. OVERVIEW
- Exam: **Group 1 Prelims** | Tier: **LOW** | Row: A-188 | PYQs: APPSC 0, other exams 24
- Separate electorates **institutionalised religion** in representation → seed of the **Two-Nation Theory**.
### 2. SEPARATE ELECTORATES, 1909-1935
| Act / event | Step |
|---|---|
| **Indian Councils Act 1909 (Morley-Minto)** | **First** separate electorates for **Muslims** + **weightage**; legalised communalism; **Minto** = "Father of Communal Electorate" |
| **Lucknow Pact 1916** | Congress accepts them + weightage + **communal veto** (three-fourths rule) |
| **GoI Act 1919** | **Extended** to Sikhs, Indian Christians, Anglo-Indians, Europeans |
| **Communal Award 1932** | Extended to **depressed classes**; **Poona Pact** replaced it with reserved seats in joint electorates |
| **GoI Act 1935** | Further to SCs, women, labour; Muslims **33%** of Federal Legislature seats |
- Jinnah's **Point 5 (1929)**: separate electorates continue; any community may opt for joint electorates.
!KEY APPSC-G2 2025: communal electorate introduced by the **Morley-Minto Reforms (1909)**.
### 3. TWO-NATION THEORY – ORIGIN AND MILESTONES
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| **1887-88** | **Sayyid Ahmad Khan** used by **Dufferin** as an anti-Congress front with **Raja Shiva Prasad of Benares** and the Raja of Bhinga; **United Indian Patriotic Association (1888)** |
| **1906** | **Shimla Deputation (Aga Khan)** demands separate electorates; **Muslim League** founded |
| **1930** | **Iqbal's Allahabad address** (League session): consolidated Muslim state in the **North-West** |
| **1933** | **Rahmat Ali** coins "Pakistan" |
| **1940** | **Lahore Resolution** – Two-Nation Theory formally set out |
| **1946** | **Direct Action Day** |
- **Jinnah** first worked for **Hindu-Muslim unity**, later championed the Two-Nation Theory.
### 4. THE NAME "PAKISTAN"
- Coined by **Chaudhry Rahmat Ali**, **1933**, as a **Cambridge** student.
- Pamphlet: "**Now or Never**".
- Acronym: **P**unjab + **A**fghan (NWFP) + **K**ashmir + **S**indh + Baluchis**tan**.
### 5. LAHORE RESOLUTION, 23 MARCH 1940
| Role | Person |
|---|---|
| Session president | **M.A. Jinnah** |
| Drafter | **Sikandar Hayat Khan** (JPSC 2016) |
| Mover | **A.K. Fazlul Huq** (23 March 1940) |
- Demand: **independent states** for Muslims in **Muslim-majority areas**; soon called the "**Pakistan Resolution**".
- AP angle: in reply, **Periyar** demanded **Dravidistan** (Erode Conference, 1940).
!TRAP President – Drafter – Mover = **Jinnah – Sikandar Hayat Khan – Fazlul Huq**.
