## U0539 | DIRECT TAX REFORMS – INCOME TAX SLABS, CORPORATE TAX CUTS, FACELESS ASSESSMENT, DTC DEBATE
### 1. INCOME TAX – FIRSTS AND NATURE
- **Income tax** first introduced **1860** by **James Wilson**, first Finance Member (Act assent **24 July 1860**; income in four schedules).
- Income tax = a **progressive direct tax**.
- Budget **1997-98** (FM **P. Chidambaram**, "dream budget"): cut almost every tax; income-tax rates **10, 20, 30%** (from 15, 30, 40%).
- Budget 2005-06: senior citizens exempt up to **₹1.50 lakh**.
- **One-by-six scheme**: compulsory return filing on lifestyle signs; telephone connection dropped in Budget 2002-03.
### 2. INCOME TAX REGIMES AND SLABS
#### 2.1 New Regime (Section 115BAC)
- **Default, exemption-free**; old regime with deductions stays **optional**.
- Slabs from **Budget 2025-26**, **unchanged in 2026-27**: 0-4 lakh **nil**; 4-8 lakh **5%** … **20-24 lakh 25%**; **above 24 lakh 30%**.
- **Section 87A rebate**: income up to **₹12 lakh** tax-free; **₹12.75 lakh** for salaried with **standard deduction ₹75,000**.
!TRAP ₹12 lakh = rebate limit; ₹12.75 lakh = salaried, after ₹75,000 standard deduction.
#### 2.2 Budget by Budget
| Budget | Key change |
|---|---|
| **2020-21** (first new optional regime) | 0-2.5 lakh nil; 2.5-5 lakh 5%; **5-7.5 lakh 10%** (APPSC 2019); 7.5-10 lakh 15%; 10-12.5 lakh 20%; 12.5-15 lakh 25%; above 15 lakh 30% |
| **2023-24** | Up to 3 lakh nil … above 15 lakh 30%; **top surcharge (above ₹2 crore) cut 37% → 25%** (new regime) |
| **2025-26** | Up to ₹12 lakh tax-free |
| **2026-27** | No slab change |
### 3. CORPORATE TAX CUTS AND MAT
#### 3.1 Taxation Laws (Amendment) Act, 2019
- Sept 2019 ordinance → **Act, 2019**: domestic companies **30% → 22%**; **no MAT** for those opting in.
| Section | Who | Base | Effective |
|---|---|---|---|
| **115BAA** | Existing domestic companies forgoing exemptions | **22%** | ~**25.17%** |
| **115BAB** | **New manufacturing** companies (incorporated **after 1 Oct 2019**) | **15%** | ~**17.01%** |
#### 3.2 Minimum Alternate Tax
- Taxes **"zero-tax companies"** – book profits but little or no regular tax.
| Milestone | Fact |
|---|---|
| Introduced | **Budget 1987-88** (Finance Act 1987, Sec 115J; effective AY 1988-89); rate 15.75% |
| Withdrawn / restarted | Withdrawn **1990**; restarted **Budget 1996-97** |
| Before 2026 | **15%** of book profits |
| **Budget 2026-27** | **14%**, a **final tax**; **no new MAT credit** after 1 April 2026; old credit set off up to 1/4 of liability (new regime only) |
- **Corporate-related**: FBT, MAT, tax on company profit; **capital gains tax** is **not** (levy on investor's gain).

| **Wealth tax** | **1957** (Wealth Tax Act; on the richer people) | Abolished 2015 |

#### 4.1 Capital Gains
- Arises on **transfer** (sale, exchange, relinquishment) of a capital asset; tax on sale of **inherited property** = **capital gains tax**.
- **Short-term**: held **up to 24 months** (general rule); LTCG rate **12.5%** (Aug 2026).
- **Indexation**: **Cost Inflation Index**, notified by **CBDT**, base year **2001-02**.
- **Rural agricultural land** – **not a capital asset**; agricultural income tax-free.
- **Poultry, wool rearing** income is **not agricultural income** – may be taxed.

### 6. BLACK MONEY DISCLOSURE AND DISPUTE SCHEMES
- Main worry from black money: **loss of revenue** to the Exchequer through evasion.
| Scheme | Key fact |
|---|---|
| **VDIS 1997** | Voluntary disclosure of black income |
| **IDS 2016** | 1 June – 30 Sept 2016; **45%** levy |
| **PMGKY 2016** | After demonetisation; **50%** |
| **Vivad se Vishwas** | Direct Tax Vivad se Vishwas Act, **2020**; second scheme **2024** – settle pending disputes |
| **FAST-DS 2026** | Foreign Assets of Small Taxpayers – Disclosure Scheme (Budget 2026-27); **30% tax + equal amount = 60%**; limit **₹1 crore**, or **₹5 crore** for previously taxed but unreported assets (₹1 lakh fee); file till 31 Dec 2026; immunity under Black Money Act, 2015 |
!NUM IDS 45% → PMGKY 50% → FAST-DS 60%
### 7. RETROSPECTIVE AND CROSS-BORDER TAXATION
- **Vodafone** (SC, **2012**) → **Finance Act 2012** taxed **indirect transfers retrospectively**.
- **Taxation Laws (Amendment) Act, 2021** withdrew retrospective demands (**Vodafone, Cairn**).
- **BEPS** (OECD/G20): **15 actions** against MNC profit shifting to low-tax places.
- **OECD Pillar Two**: **15% global minimum** corporate tax for **large MNEs** (not all companies).
- **Equalisation levy**: **6%** on online ads (2016; abolished from 1 Apr 2025); **2%** on e-commerce (2020, dropped 2024).
### 8. DIRECT TAXES CODE AND INCOME-TAX ACT, 2025
- Overhaul of the 1961 Act (effective 1 April 1962) announced July 2024.
- **Income-tax Act, 2025** replaced it **w.e.f. 1 April 2026** (not 2025).
- Single 12-month **Tax Year** from 1 April replaces **Assessment Year and Previous Year**; simpler language.
- **Direct Tax Code** idea: replace the 1961 Act with a single law.
!TRAP Enacted name is **Income-tax Act, 2025**, not "2026".
### 9. TAX ADMINISTRATION
- **CBDT**: statutory under **Central Boards of Revenue Act, 1963** (APPSC 2019); in the **Department of Revenue** (not Expenditure).
- **Faceless assessment + Taxpayers' Charter**: **13 August 2020** ("Transparent Taxation – Honouring the Honest"); **faceless appeals** Sept 2020.
- **Project Insight**: launched 2017, working 2019 – AI and data analytics.
- **AIS**: all reported transactions; pre-filled returns (2021). **e-Sahyog**: online compliance check of mismatches.
- **PAN** (e.g. AFZPK7190K): 10 characters – 5 letters, 4 numbers, last a letter; **4th character** = holder type (**P** individual, **C** company); **not proof of address**.
- **TCS on LRS** for education and medical cut **5% → 2%** (2026-27).
