## U0108 | ALAUDDIN KHALJI'S MARKET CONTROL, REVENUE AND MILITARY REFORMS
### 1. WHY MARKET CONTROL
1. **Mongol invasions** →
2. need for a **large standing army paid in cash** →
3. **low, fixed salaries** →
4. **fix prices** of daily goods →
5. soldiers live comfortably on low pay (**APPSC G2 2017** key).
- Military need directly shaped economic policy.
- Aims: **fixed prices**, sure **supply**, **controlled distribution** (rationing), **end of monopolies**, enforcement by a separate administration.
!TRAP Price control was NOT meant to pay lower wages to palace artisans.
### 2. MARKETS AND OFFICIALS
#### 2.1 The Four Markets
| Market | Goods |
|---|---|
| **Mandi** | **Food grains** |
| **Sarai Adl** | **Cloth**, sugar, dry fruits, butter, oil |
| Third market | **Horses, slaves, cattle** |
| Fourth market | Miscellaneous goods |
!TRAP Mandi = grain; Sarai Adl = cloth and other goods.
#### 2.2 Officials and Departments
| Official / Department | Role |
|---|---|
| **Shahna-i-Mandi** | Superintendent of each market (e.g. **Malik Kabool**) |
| **Diwan-i-Riyasat** | Ran the whole market system, under the **Naib-i-Riyasat** |
| **Barids** | Intelligence officers; daily reports |
| **Munhiyan** | Secret spies; daily reports |
| **Diwan-i-Mustakhraj** | Recovered **revenue arrears** from officials |
| **Ariz-i-Mumalik** | Army minister |
| **Malik Naib** | Deputy; rank Malik Kafur rose to |
- **Slave boys** made test purchases; cheating merchants punished.
!TRAP Diwan-i-Riyasat = markets; Diwan-i-Mustakhraj = revenue arrears.
### 3. REVENUE REFORMS
- **First Sultan to measure land (masahat)** for revenue; standard unit **biswa**; assessed on measured area and yield.
- Measurement limited to **Delhi and nearby areas**, not the whole empire.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Kharaj** | Land tax = **50% (half)** of yield (BPSC 2018) |
| **Ghari** | **House tax** (new) |
| **Charai** | **Grazing tax** on milch animals (new) |
| **Khuts, muqaddams, chaudharis** | Intermediaries; privileges **abolished**, taxed like others |
| **Kismat-i-khuti** | Headman's cess; **abolished** |
| **Milk, waqf, inam** | Reclaimed → **khalisa** expanded |
- State collected revenue **more directly**; also curbed the powers of provincial governors.
!TRAP Kharaj = one-half, not one-fifth. Middlemen's privileges were abolished, not expanded. Ghari = house; Charai = grazing.
### 4. MILITARY REFORMS
- **First Sultan with a large permanent standing army**, **paid in cash**.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Dagh** | **Branding of horses** |
| **Huliya / Chehra** | **Descriptive roll** of each soldier |
- Purpose: stop fraud and false musters.
- Mongols beaten: **Jalandhar (1298)**, **Kili (1299)**, **Amroha (1305)**, **Ravi (1306)**; Ravi became the boundary river; frontier forts repaired.
- Later copy: **Mahmud Gawan** (Bahmani) brought in dagh, chehra, land measurement and a larger khalisa.
!TRAP Dagh is NOT a land-revenue measure; huliya is NOT an assessment of nobles' wealth.
### 5. ORDINANCES AND SOCIAL MEASURES
| Cause of Revolt (Barani) | Ordinance |
|---|---|
| **Nobles' wealth** | Confiscated religious grants and free lands (inam, waqf) |
| **Weak spy system** | Reorganised spies (**barids, munhiyan**) |
| **Drinking** | Prohibited **liquor**, intoxicants and **gambling** |
| **Nobles' intermarriage and gatherings** | Feasts and marriage alliances needed **royal permission** |
- **Severe punishments**; rape punished severely.
- **APPSC G1 2018**: prohibition + market control + severe punishment = Alauddin.
### 6. KINGSHIP, RELIGION AND THE ULEMA
- **Rejected the ulema's advice**; acted in the **interest of the state** (told Qazi Mughisuddin of Bayana so).
- Revived **Balban's** harsh, centralised rule, but **without** depending on religious sanction.
- Title **Sikandar-i-Sani**; plans of **world conquest** and a **new religion** dropped on advice of **Kotwal Ala-ul-Mulk**.
- Earlier posts under Jalaluddin: **Amir-i-Tuzuk** and **Ariz-i-Mamalik** (army, not law).

#### 7.2 Malik Kafur's Deccan Campaigns
- **Eunuch** bought by **Nusrat Khan** in the Gujarat campaign (1299); "**Hazar Dinari**" (1,000 dinars); rose to **Malik Naib**.
- Earlier: Alauddin raided **Devagiri (1294-96)** as governor of **Kara** = first Turkish attack on the south.
| Year | Kingdom | Ruler |
|---|---|---|
| 1307-08 | **Yadavas of Devagiri** | **Ramachandra** (tribute) |
| 1309-10 | **Kakatiyas of Warangal** | **Prataparudra II** (treaty, tribute) |
| 1310-11 | **Hoysalas of Dwarasamudra** | **Vira Ballala III** |
| 1311 | **Pandyas of Madurai** | Kingdom sacked |
- Aim: **plunder and tribute** to pay the army, **not annexation**; financial ventures (K.S. Lal: greed of wealth).
#### 7.3 AP Angle: Warangal
- **1303**: first attack on Orugallu by **Malik Fakhruddin Juna** failed at **Upparapalli**, beaten by **Potuganti Maili** and **Recherla Venna**.
- **1309-10**: **Kafur** and **Khwaja Haji** besieged Warangal; Prataparudra submitted; loot carried on **1,000 camels**; **Koh-i-Noor**, by tradition, part of the treasure.
- **Amir Khusrau** went on the 1310 campaign; **Khazain-ul-Futuh** describes the **double-walled fort**, moat and **diamond mines**.
### 8. SOURCES, EFFECTS AND END OF REFORMS
- **Main source**: **Ziauddin Barani** (Tarikh-i-Firoz Shahi); Khazain-ul-Futuh by Amir Khusrau.
- Effects: **prices stable**, army strong enough to beat the Mongols; but **espionage and repression** bred discontent; hurt merchants and peasants; controls may have been confined to Delhi.
- UPPSC keys call the market control "**highly successful**".
- **Qutbuddin Mubarak Shah (1316-20)** ended price controls, returned seized lands, withdrew harsh rules.
- Dynasty fell soon after his death in **1316**.
#### 8.1 Delhi Sultanate Dynasties → Key Contribution

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- First state-run price-control and rationing system in India.
- First land measurement for revenue.
- First permanent standing army paid in cash.
- Reign = "imperial period" of the Sultanate.
