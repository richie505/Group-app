## U0670 | INDIA – AGRICULTURE: MAJOR FOOD CROPS (RICE, WHEAT, MAIZE, PULSES), CROPPING SEASONS AND REGIONS
### 1. CROPPING SEASONS – KHARIF, RABI, ZAID
| Season | Sown | Harvested | Crops |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kharif** | **June-July** (first rains) | **Oct-Nov** | Rice, jowar, maize, bajra, ragi, **moong**, **cotton**, soybean, groundnut, sugarcane, jute, red chilli |
| **Rabi** | **Oct-Nov** | **Mar-Apr** | Wheat, **gram**, peas, **mustard**, lentil (masoor), linseed, lathyrus, French bean |
| **Zaid** | Short **hot summer** between rabi and kharif | – | **Watermelon, muskmelon, cucumber, bitter gourd**, vegetables, **fodder**, sunflower; irrigated **moong and urad** |
- **Pulses in both seasons**: kharif (tur/arhar, moong) and rabi (gram, masoor).
- **Maize** – grown **throughout the year**; "**Queen of Cereals**".
- **Aman rice** – sown June-July, harvested Nov-Dec.
- **Jute** – indigenous; kharif, sown Feb-June, harvested July-Oct (A-R: both true, R not explanation).
- **Onion** – grown by **transplanting** seedlings; **rice** – a transplanted crop (less seed, more labour).
!TRAP Moong is **kharif**, not rabi. Gram, mustard, masoor = rabi (NOT kharif). Lady's finger is NOT rabi. Cotton is kharif, not zaid.
### 2. FOOD CROPS – GROWTH CONDITIONS
| Crop | Rank / season | Temperature | Rainfall | Soil |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Rice** | **1st cereal**; staple of over half of India; kharif | **~25°C** (20-35°C) | **Above 100 cm**; high humidity | Clayey/loamy, **deltaic and coastal alluvium** |
| **Wheat** | **2nd cereal**; **rabi** | Cool growing season (10-15°C winter); **bright warm sun at ripening** | **50-75 cm** | Alluvium of **Ganga-Satluj plains**; **black soil of the Deccan** |
| **Maize** | Year-round | Warm | Moderate | – |
| **Jowar** | Kharif/rabi | Warm; drought tolerant | Rainfed | Peninsular, semi-arid |
| **Bajra** | Kharif | **Hot, dry** | Driest | **Sandy** |
| **Ragi** | Kharif | Warm, dry | Low | **Red** soils |
| **Pulses** | Both | – | Rain-fed, low moisture | Fix **atmospheric nitrogen**; used in rotation |
| Sugarcane | – | **21-27°C** | **75-100 cm** | Frost at ripening lowers sucrose |
| Cotton | Kharif | 21-27°C | 50-75 cm | Frost harmful |
- Rice originated in **South-East Asia**; **100 cm isohyet** ≈ western limit of rice.
- **Groundnut**: most suitable dryland crop (50-125 cm); needs **gypsum** in large quantity.
- Mostly rainfed: **groundnut, sesamum, pearl millet**.
!TRAP APPSC G1 2024: "Black soil of the Deccan is not suitable for wheat" – **incorrect**; black soil suits wheat.
#### 2.1 Crop Types
- **Cash crops**: sugarcane, cotton, jute, tobacco, oilseeds, groundnut, bananas – NOT jowar, wheat, rice, millet, bajra.
- **Stimulant crops**: tea, coffee, tobacco, opium poppy, datura, hemp, betel nut.
- **Subsistence-farmed**: coarse cereals and rice.

### 5. GREEN REVOLUTION
#### 5.1 Persons
| Person | Role |
|---|---|
| **Norman Borlaug** | Father of Green Revolution in the **world**; **Mexican dwarf wheat**; Nobel Peace **1970** |
| **M.S. Swaminathan** | Father of Green Revolution in **India**; brought HYV wheat; **Evergreen Revolution** |
| **C. Subramaniam** | Union Agriculture Minister who backed it |
| William Gaud | Coined the term, 1968 |
| B.P. Pal | First DG of ICAR |
#### 5.2 Origin, Inputs, Crops
- **New Agricultural Strategy, 1965** (drought years 1965-67); **HYV Programme from Kharif 1966** (1966-67).
- Aim: **foodgrain self-sufficiency** – India moved from **PL-480** wheat imports to self-sufficiency.
- Inputs: **HYV seeds**, chemical fertilisers, pesticides, assured irrigation, mechanisation, **MSP**, rural electrification, rural roads, marketing.
- Dwarf wheat: **Lerma Rojo 64-A, Sonora-63, Sonora-64, Mayo-64, S227**; Kalyan Sona; Sonora-64 developed by induced mutation at IARI.
- Crops most benefited: **wheat** (output 2.5 times; first phase), then **rice** (later). Pulses, oilseeds, coarse cereals lagged.
- Phases: 1st (mid-1960s-1980s) wheat; 2nd (1980s) spread east and west, growth 3.4% vs 2.3%; **3rd = price policy on MSP and procurement** (PYQ 2017).
#### 5.3 Regions
- First stage: **Punjab, Haryana, western UP** (Trans- and Upper Gangetic Plains); AP and TN rice deltas gained via irrigation.
- Eastern belt (MP, Bihar, Odisha, WB, Assam) lagged.
#### 5.4 Limitations
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Regional imbalance | Rainfed and hilly areas left out; disparities aggravated (A-R: R not explanation) |
| Crop imbalance | Pulses, oilseeds, millets neglected |
| Equity | Big landowners (>10 ha) gained most |
| Environment | **Groundwater depletion, salinity**, rice-wheat monoculture |
| Fertiliser | **N:P:K 10.9:4.1:1** vs ideal **4:2:1** |
- Procurement confined to rice, wheat, sugarcane – these use **over two-thirds of urea** (May 2026); MSP + free power favour paddy in Punjab.
- Small and marginal farmers: **86.2%** of farmers, 47.3% of area (2015-16).

### 7. MILLETS AND DRYLAND FARMING
| Group | Millets |
|---|---|
| **Major** | **Bajra** (pearl; ~**60%** of millet output), **jowar** (sorghum), **ragi** (finger) |
| **Minor** | **Foxtail (kangni), little (kutki), kodo (kodon), proso (chena)** |
- **2023** = UN **International Year of Millets** (pushed by India); millets branded **Shree Anna**.
- Millets: mostly kharif; climate-resilient, low-input; drought-tolerant (sorghum, kodo, kangani, ragi).
- **Dryland farming**: rainfall **below 75 cm (750 mm)**, no assured irrigation → millets, pulses.
- 2026: El Nino + conflict pushed farmers to bajra/jowar; India monsoon deficit 13% vs **AP 51%** (to 25 Aug).
### 8. CROPPING SYSTEMS
| System | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Double / multiple** | Two or more crops in succession on same land in a year (paddy then wheat) |
| **Intercropping** | Crops together in **definite rows** (tur + jowar or maize) |
| **Mixed cropping** | Seeds mixed, **no row pattern**; insures against failure |
| **Parallel cropping** | Crops of different habit/duration, no competition (**maize + green gram**; wheat + mustard) |
| **Relay cropping** | Next crop sown into the **standing crop** |
| **Ratoon** | Regrowth from stubble |
| **Crop rotation** | Planned sequence; pulse-cereal leaves fixed nitrogen |
| **Mixed farming** | Crops + rearing animals |
- **Rice-wheat** = major cropping system **by area** (Indo-Gangetic plains). Best cycle, eastern UP: **paddy – maize – wheat**.
- AP cropping intensity: **1.21 (2023-24) → 1.88 (2024-25)**; AP diversifying to fruits, vegetables, spices, oil palm.
- Cereal yield = output per unit harvested area (kg/ha).
!TRAP Intercropping = rows; mixed cropping = no rows. Paddy does NOT help N-fixation.

| Crop | Disease | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Wheat | **Black rust** (also yellow, brown) | *Puccinia graminis tritici* |
| Wheat | Loose smut / Karnal bunt | *Ustilago nuda tritici* / *Tilletia indica* |

- **Aflatoxins** – moulds in badly stored grain; not destroyed by cooking.
- Rice biofertiliser: **blue-green algae** (Nostoc, Anabaena), **Azolla-Anabaena**. Green manure in arid land: **lobia**.
- **Cobalt** essential for pulses (Rhizobium N-fixation). Pulse + fodder + green manure: cowpea, green gram, pigeon pea.
