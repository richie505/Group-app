## U0351 | NITI AAYOG – STRUCTURE, FUNCTIONS, INITIATIVES; CONTRAST WITH THE PLANNING COMMISSION
### 1. PLANNING COMMISSION AND NDC
- **Planning Commission**: set up **15 March 1950** by a **Cabinet resolution**; first chairman **Jawaharlal Nehru** (PM); non-constitutional, non-statutory.
- Cabinet repealed the 1950 resolution on **13 August 2014** (Modi government); replaced by NITI Aayog.
- **National Development Council**: **6 August 1952**, by **executive order**; PM, all State CMs, Planning Commission members; linked the Commission and States; restructured into NITI's **Governing Council**.
### 2. NITI AAYOG – STATUS AND STRUCTURE
- Set up **1 January 2015** by a **Union Cabinet resolution** – **neither constitutional nor statutory**; a **think-tank**.
- **Chairperson: PM**; **Vice-Chairperson** (Cabinet rank) is executive head – **Ashok Kumar Lahiri** replaced Suman K. Bery (order 25 April 2026); full-time members (MoS rank), ex-officio Union Ministers, special invitees, a **CEO**.
- **NITI** = nodal agency for **poverty estimation** (earlier Planning Commission).
#### 2.1 Bodies and Hubs
| Body | Composition / role |
|---|---|
| **Governing Council** | PM, all State CMs, CMs of UTs with legislatures, LGs of other UTs, VC, full-time members, special invitees |
| **Regional Councils** | Specific regional issues, fixed period; chair **PM or his nominee**; CMs and LGs of the region |
| **Team India Hub** | **States–Centre engagement** (APPSC 2018) |
| **Knowledge and Innovation Hub** | Think-tank capacity |
| **State Support Mission** | **SETUs** (State Economic and Transformation Units) → **State Vision@2047** |
- Governing Council: first **Feb 2015**, reconstituted Feb 2021, meets about yearly; **11th meeting 11 June 2026**, Rashtrapati Bhavan (CM Naidu attended).
### 3. NITI vs PLANNING COMMISSION
| Feature | Planning Commission | NITI Aayog |
|---|---|---|
| Approach | **Top-down** | **Bottom-up**, **cooperative federalism** (Team India) |
| Funds | Allocated funds to States | **No fund allocation** (Finance Ministry / Finance Commission) |
| Basis | Cabinet resolution 1950 | Cabinet resolution 2015 |
### 4. INDICES AND REPORTS
| Index / report | Subject |
|---|---|
| **SDG India Index** | States'/UTs' SDG progress; **4th ed. 2023-24** (July 2024), **113 indicators**; **AP 74, "Front Runner"** |

| **Export Preparedness Index** | Export readiness (with Institute for Competitiveness); first **2020**; **EPI 2024** (Jan 2026): **AP 5th** among large States (8th earlier) |

| India Electric Mobility Index | 2nd ed. Sep 2026, with WRI India |
| School Education Quality Index | School education |
- National MPI: India 24.85% (2015-16) → **14.96%** (2019-21); **AP 11.77% → 6.06%**.
- AP: SDG 7 – 1st; SDG 14 – 2nd.
### 5. INITIATIVES
| Initiative | Launch | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| **Atal Innovation Mission** | 2016 | Innovation (Atal Tinkering Labs, incubators) |
| **Women Entrepreneurship Platform** | 2018 | Support for women entrepreneurs |
| **Aspirational Districts (ADP)** | **January 2018** | **112 districts**, **49 indicators**, 5 themes |
| **Aspirational Blocks (ABP)** | **Jan 2023** | **513 blocks in 329 districts** (27 States, 4 UTs); about 40 indicators |
- ADP themes: **Health and Nutrition 30%**, Education, **Agriculture and Water Resources 20%**, **Financial Inclusion and Skill Development 10%** (not 20), Basic Infrastructure 10%; **Urban Transport and Housing is NOT a theme**.
- **3 Cs**: **Convergence** (funds and services of departments), Collaboration, Competition; monthly delta ranking.
- Sampoornata Abhiyan 2.0 saturates KPIs in both.
- **AP**: original – **Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam, YSR Kadapa**; now **Alluri Sitarama Raju, Parvathipuram Manyam, YSR Kadapa**; ASR literacy **44.67%**; AP Reorganisation Act 2014 – package for **7 backward districts (4 Rayalaseema + 3 North Coastal)**.
!TRAP Kurnool and Anantapur are NOT AP aspirational districts.
