### 2. BRICS, SCO, BIMSTEC AND ASEAN
#### 2.1 BRICS
- **17th BRICS Summit**: Rio de Janeiro, July 2025; chaired by Brazil's **Lula da Silva** (not Xi Jinping).
- **18th BRICS Summit**: **Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, 12–13 September 2026**; chair passes from **India to China for 2027**.
- **New Delhi Declaration (BRICS, Sep 2026)**: unanimously adopted; **first BRICS declaration since 2022 with no reference to Ukraine**.
| Year | BRICS membership change |
|---|---|
| 2001 | Term **BRIC** coined |
| 2011 | **South Africa** joined |
| 2024 | **Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, UAE** joined |
| January 2025 | **Indonesia** joined |
#### 2.2 SCO
- **25th SCO Summit, Tianjin (2025)**: China hosted for the **fifth time**; linked to the **"Shanghai Spirit"** (not ASEAN-India).
- **Tianjin Declaration (SCO, 2025)**: criticised the **Pahalgam** and **Jaffer Express** attacks.
- **Bishkek Declaration (SCO, 2026)**: one of **28 documents**; approved an **SCO-CSTO-CIS roadmap**.
- Chair succession: **China → Kyrgyzstan (2026) → Pakistan (2026–27)**; summit planned at **Islamabad**.
#### 2.3 BIMSTEC
- Full form: **Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation**.
- **6th BIMSTEC Summit (April 2025)**, Bangkok, hosted by Thailand; PM Modi attended. Theme: **"BIMSTEC: Prosperous, Resilient and Open"**.
#### 2.4 ASEAN-India and East Asia Summit
- **22nd ASEAN-India Summit (October 2025)**: held **virtually under Malaysia's chair**, part of the **47th ASEAN Summit, Kuala Lumpur**.
- Results: **maritime cooperation year (2026)**, **2026–2030 partnership plan**, **early AITIGA review**.
- **20th East Asia Summit (2025)**, Kuala Lumpur: theme **"Inclusivity and Sustainability"**; **Kuala Lumpur Declaration** on peace and stability in the **Indo-Pacific**.
- **East Asia Summit = 19 members**: 11 ASEAN members + 8 partners.
!TRAP Tianjin Declaration = SCO 2025; Bishkek = SCO 2026; Kuala Lumpur = EAS 2025; New Delhi = BRICS 2026.
### 3. G20 - STRUCTURE, INDIA'S 2023 PRESIDENCY, JOHANNESBURG 2025
#### 3.1 Structure
- Works through **Finance track + Sherpa track**.
- Presidency rotates annually among **five regional groups**.
- **Troika = past + present + next** presidencies.
#### 3.2 India's 2023 Presidency (18th G20)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Summit | **18th G20 Summit, New Delhi (Delhi), 9–10 September 2023**, Bharat Mandapam |
| Theme | **"One Earth, One Family, One Future"** (Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam) |
| Presidency term | **1 December 2022 – 30 November 2023** |
| Meetings | about **60 cities** in all States/UTs |
| Headline outcome | **African Union admitted as permanent member** |
- Launched under India's Presidency: **Global Biofuels Alliance (GBA)**; **MAHARISHI** (millets research initiative); **Global Initiative on Digital Health**; **"G20 satellite"** for climate monitoring.
#### 3.3 20th G20, Johannesburg (November 2025)
- Host **South Africa**; **first G20 Summit on African soil**.
- Theme: **"Solidarity, Equality, and Sustainability"**; spirit of **Ubuntu** ("I am because we are").
- **Johannesburg Leaders' Declaration** (adopted unusually early): critical-minerals value-addition framework; MDB reform; **first standalone (separate) debt declaration since COVID**; **Cost of Capital Commission** on borrowing costs for developing economies.
- **African Union**: permanent member (2023), **fully operationalised at Johannesburg 2025**.
- **Digital Innovation Alliance** (G20 2025): by **India, Brazil, South Africa** - DPI as a Global South alternative.
- India's proposals: **Africa Skills Multiplier** (**1 million trainers in a decade**); **Open Satellite Data Partnership**.
- The **U.S. skipped** the 2025 G20 Summit.
!TRAP 18th G20 = New Delhi 2023; 18th BRICS = New Delhi 2026 - both at Bharat Mandapam.
### 4. G7, NATO, QUAD AND BILATERAL SUMMITS
#### 4.1 G7 and NATO
- **Russia** joined in **1998** (creating the **G8**); suspended in **2014** after Crimea.
- **36th NATO Summit**: **Ankara, Türkiye, July 2026**; defence spending **5% of GDP by 2035** = **3.5% core defence** + related spending.
#### 4.2 Quad
- **Six leader-level summits in 2021–2024**; **none in 2025**; Quad Leaders' Summit stalled.
#### 4.3 Bilateral and regional
- **16th India-Japan Annual Summit (July 2026)**: New Delhi, with PM **Sanae Takaichi**; **Joint Declaration on Economic Security** covering **five sectors**.
- **4th India-Africa Forum Summit** delayed (May 2026) due to an **Ebola outbreak in DR Congo and Uganda** (WHO declared a **PHEIC**).
- **India-Central Asia Summit**: first held **virtually on 27 January 2022**, chaired by PM Modi; adopted the **Delhi Declaration**; leaders agreed to meet **every two years**.
- **IBSA** leaders met on the sidelines of **G20 Johannesburg (Nov 2025)**.

### 7. AI AND TECHNOLOGY SUMMITS
#### 7.1 AI summit series
| Summit | Host | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI Safety Summit** | **Bletchley Park, UK** | 2023 | **first** in the series |
| **AI Seoul Summit** | South Korea | 2024 | - |
| **AI Action Summit** | **Paris**, France | Feb 2025 | **co-chaired by Macron and Modi** (India co-chair) |
| **AI Impact Summit** | **New Delhi (Bharat Mandapam)** | **16–20 Feb 2026** | **first in the Global South** |
| **WAIC 2026** | **Shanghai** | 2026 | **29 countries** agreed to form **WAICO** (World AI Cooperation Organisation), HQ **Shanghai** |
#### 7.2 AI Impact Summit 2026
- Structure: **7 Chakras** (working groups) + **3 Sutras** (principles).
- Based on India's **M.A.N.A.V.** human-centred AI vision; hosted under the **IndiaAI Mission**.
- **New Delhi Declaration on AI Impact** ("**Sarvajan Hitaya, Sarvajan Sukhaya**"): signed incl. the **U.S. and China**; endorsements: **88** when the summit closed (21 Feb 2026), **91** by 24 Feb 2026, later **92** countries and organisations. It is voluntary and non-binding.
- Also: **New Delhi Frontier AI Impact Commitments**.
- Launches: **Global Digital Capacity Building Alliance** (20 Feb 2026; builds on **Mission Karmayogi**); ISA's **Global Mission on AI for Energy**.
- Sovereign models: **Sarvam AI** LLMs; **BharatGen Param2 17B**; **Gnani.ai Vachana** (text-to-speech).
#### 7.3 Other
- **AI for Good Global Summit**: organised by the **ITU**, **Geneva**.
!KEY AI series: Bletchley (2023) → Seoul (2024) → Paris (2025, India co-chair) → New Delhi (2026).

#### 8.2 UNFCCC COPs
| COP | Venue | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **COP21 (2015)** | **Paris** | **Paris Agreement** (adopted **12 Dec 2015**, in force **4 Nov 2016**) |
| **COP29 (2024)** | **Baku** | **NCQG: US$300 bn/yr by 2035**; **Baku-to-Belém Roadmap** to **US$1.3 tn/yr by 2035** |
| **COP30 (Nov 2025)** | **Belém, Brazil** | **first COP in the Amazon**; **Belém Political Package** ("global mutirão"); **tripling adaptation finance by 2035**; **Belém Action Mechanism** (just transition); **60 GGA indicators**; **no fossil-fuel roadmap**; U.S. absent |
| **COP31 (Nov 2026)** | **Antalya, Türkiye** | Türkiye presidency; **Australia presides over negotiations** (co-president); **"35 by 35"** electrification target |
| **COP32 (2027)** | **Addis Ababa, Ethiopia** | - |
- COP30 pledges: **voluntary, not legally binding**.
- **COP33 (2028)**: **India withdrew its bid** (Environment Ministry letter, **2 April 2026**, reported 9 Apr 2026); **South Korea** the only other contender.
- India has hosted **only COP8 (2002)**; COP26 was at **Glasgow**.
!NUM NCQG = US$300 bn/yr; Baku-Belém Roadmap = US$1.3 tn/yr (both by 2035).
