## U0270 | TELUGU CINEMA AND MASS CULTURE IN ANDHRA PRADESH
### 1. FIRSTS OF TELUGU CINEMA
| Film | Milestone | Year | Director |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bhishma Pratigna** | First Telugu **silent** film | **1921** | Raghupathi Venkaiah Naidu, R.S. Prakash |
| **Bhakta Prahlada** | First Telugu **talkie** | **1931** | **H.M. Reddy** (not C. Pullaiah) |
| **Lava Kusa** | First full-length Telugu **colour** film | **1963** | C. Pullaiah, C.S. Rao |
- **Alam Ara** (1931): first Indian sound film, **Ardeshir Irani**; premiered Majestic Cinema, Bombay, **14 March 1931**.
- **Raja Harishchandra** (Dadasaheb Phalke): first full-length feature film, but **silent**.
!NUM Silent 1921 → talkie 1931 → colour 1963

### 6. TELEVISION AND MASS MEDIA
- **Doordarshan Kendra Hyderabad**: **1977**.
- **Gemini TV** and **ETV**: both **1995**; ETV by **Ramoji Rao** (not NTR).
- **Prasar Bharati** = Akashvani + Doordarshan; **NFDC** (runs National Film Awards) and **CBFC** are separate.
