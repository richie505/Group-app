## U0066 | GUPTA RELIGION – BHAGAVATISM, VAISHNAVISM, SAIVISM; BEGINNINGS OF TEMPLE ARCHITECTURE (DEOGARH, BHITARGAON)
### 1. NATURE OF GUPTA RELIGION
- **Theistic and devotional** Hinduism replaced **sacrificial Brahmanism**: shift from the Vedic fire altar to **temple idol worship and bhakti**.
- Supreme deities: **Brahma, Vishnu, Mahesh**.
- **Purusharthas:** Dharma, Artha, Kama, Moksha; the first three = **trivarga**.
- Smriti texts: **Yajnavalkya, Narada, Brihaspati, Katyayana**; Smarta practices spread; the **six schools of philosophy** took final shape.
- Comparison: Mauryan religion = Vedic + Shramana; Gupta change = sacrifice → temple and bhakti.
### 2. BHAGAVATISM AND VAISHNAVISM
#### 2.1 Origin and Doctrine
- Centre of worship: **Vasudeva-Krishna** (son of Devaki) of the **Vrishni (Satvata)** clan.
- **First worshippers** of Vasudeva-Krishna = the **Bhagavatas (Vasudevaks)** (UPPSC 1997); the name is known in **Panini's** age.
- Doctrine: **avatara** theory – **ten incarnations** of Vishnu; foundations = **karma, bhakti, ahimsa**.
- Vaishnavism developed from the Bhagavata religion.
#### 2.2 Heliodorus Pillar
- **Earliest epigraphic evidence** of Bhagavatism = **Garuda pillar at Besnagar** (Vidisha).
- Set up to Vasudeva by **Heliodorus**, Greek envoy of Indo-Greek king **Antialcidas** at the court of **Sunga Bhagabhadra**; he called himself a Bhagavata – a **foreign patron**.
#### 2.3 Gupta Royal Religion
- The **Guptas** made the greatest contribution to the Bhagavata sect (BPSC 1994) – virtually the state religion; most rulers titled **Parama Bhagavata**.
- **First Gupta to take "Parama Bhagavata": Samudragupta** (UPPSC 2009, 2015); **Chandragupta II's** coins also carry the title.
- **Dynastic emblem: Garuda**, Vishnu's mount.
- Vaishnava symbols: **chakra, shankha, Garuda**; Saiva symbol: **linga**.
- **AP angle:** **Salankayanas** followed Vaishnavism (APHQ 2011), though their rulers used the Shaiva title **Parama Mahesvara**; **Nandivarman II's Pedavegi** inscription = first in AP on Vishnu temples.
#### 2.4 Varaha Panel at Udayagiri
- Colossal **Varaha (boar) Vishnu** panel at **Udayagiri** (MP) carved under **Chandragupta II**; the cave was his **personal temple**.
### 3. PURANIC HINDUISM
- **18 Mahapuranas** (APPSC G2 2011); compiled over centuries, **final shape in the Gupta period** (c. 4th–6th c. CE).
- Principal Puranas: **Vishnu, Bhagavata, Matsya, Vayu, Brahmanda, Markandeya, Shiva, Kurma**.
- **Pancha-lakshana** (five topics): creation, re-creation, genealogies, manvantaras, dynastic history.
- **Kalika Purana** (Kali / Sati Purana) – Shaktism; an **upapurana**, likely written in **Assam**; credited to sage Markandeya.
### 4. SAIVISM – SECTS AND LINGA FORMS
| Sect | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Pashupata** | **Oldest** Shaiva sect; founder **Lakulisha (Nakulisha)**; pashu = individual soul, pati = Supreme soul |
| **Kapalika** | Extremist, **skull-bearing** sect; Bhairava, cremation ashes; came from the **Misra Saivam** school (APPSC GS 2018); **Bhairavakonda** caves (AP) belong to them |
| **Kalamukha** | Extreme sect; followers of Kalanana, a disciple of Lakulisha; strong in medieval **Andhra (Simha Parishat)** and **Karnataka (Shakti Parishat)** |
| **Mattamayura** | Found in **Chedi** inscriptions |
| **Nathpanth** | **Matsyendranath, Gorakhnath**; Shiva as Adinath |
| Lingayats | Followed **Basava** |
- **Kalamukha and Pashupata** later flourished under the **Kakatiyas**.
| Site / form | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Gudimallam** | Parasurameswara temple near **Renigunta, Tirupati district** – **earliest known Shiva linga**, with a standing figure of Shiva (APPSC G2 2008) |
| **Ekamukhalinga** | Linga with **one face** of Shiva – Gupta form (Udayagiri Cave 4, Khoh) |
- Sectarian symbols on Gupta coins and seals: **linga, trident, bull**.
!TRAP APPSC keys differ on the Kapalika parent school (Pashupata in G2 2019); the Misra Saivam answer is the one to remember.
### 5. SHAKTI AND SURYA WORSHIP
- **Mother goddesses:** **Durga, Amba, Kali, Chandi**; **Gaja-Lakshmi** on seals.
- **Ardhanarishvara** = union of Shiva and Parvati (Shaivism + Shaktism).
- **Tantrism:** from about the **5th century**; rituals centred on **shakti**; **open to women and Shudras**; touched Shaivism, Vaishnavism, Buddhism and Jainism.
- **Surya:** sun temples appear – the **silk-weavers' Sun temple at Mandsaur** (Kumaragupta I) and a lamp endowment for a **Sun temple** in the **Indore copper plate** (Skandagupta).
### 6. BUDDHISM UNDER THE GUPTAS
- **Nalanda** founded by **Kumaragupta I (Shakraditya)**.
- **Mahayana** images flourished in Gupta art; Fa-Hien saw Mahayana and Hinayana active but decay at **Kapilavastu, Gaya, Kushinagar**.
- Buddhism lost royal patronage; persecuted by the Huna **Mihirakula**.
| Scholar | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Vasubandhu** (4th c.) | **Abhidharmakosa** |
| **Dinnaga** (5th c.) | Founder of **Buddhist logic** |
| **Buddhaghosa** (5th c.) | **Visuddhimagga** (Pali, Theravada) |
### 7. JAINISM UNDER THE GUPTAS
- **Vallabhi council** (5th c. CE) under **Devarddhi Kshamashramana** – compiled and wrote down the **Svetambara canon**.
- **Mathura** – major centre of **Jain art** (Tirthankara images).
- **Udayagiri** (MP): 1 Jain cave among its 20.

### 10. GUPTA TEMPLE ARCHITECTURE
- **No free-standing Hindu temple survives from before the Guptas**; the earliest surviving ones are Gupta.
- Elements fixed: square **garbhagriha**, pillared **mandapa**, **pradakshina** path, raised **jagati** platform.
#### 10.1 Stages of Evolution
| Stage | Examples (deity) | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **First** | **Sanchi Temple 17, Tigawa** | Square sanctum, **flat roof**, shallow four-pillared portico, low platform |
| **Second** | **Nachna** (Parvati), **Bhumara** (Shiva) | Roofed **ambulatory**, raised platform, two storeys; Bhumara has **two flanking shrines** |
| **Third** | **Deogarh** (Dashavatara, Vishnu), **Bhitargaon** | Flat roof gone; low square **shikhara**; Panchayatana plan |
- **Panchayatana:** main shrine + **four subsidiary shrines** at the corners; square main temple with long mandapa.
- **Earliest Panchayatana** = **Dashavatara temple, Deogarh** (Lalitpur, near Jhansi) – earliest in North India.
- **Garhwa** fort (Shankargarh, Prayagraj) – Gupta temple complex and sculptures.
- **Sirpur** – centre of Buddhism, Shaivism, Vaishnavism and Jainism.
!TRAP **Bhitargaon** is a Gupta **Hindu temple** (third stage), not a Buddhist chaitya. Flat roof = first stage; shikhara = third.
