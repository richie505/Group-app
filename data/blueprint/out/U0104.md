## U0104 | RAJPUT STATES AND SOCIETY; CHANDELLA, SOLANKI AND PARAMARA CONTRIBUTIONS
### 1. ORIGIN OF THE RAJPUTS
- Rajput states rose after the **collapse of the Pratihara Empire**: Chauhans (Ajmer), Chandelas (Bundelkhand), Paramaras (Malwa).
| Theory | Proponent |
|---|---|
| **Agnikula** (born from a sacrificial fire at **Mount Abu**) | **Chand Bardai** (Prithviraj Raso) |
| **Foreign origin** (Scythians, Hunas, Gurjaras) | **Col. James Tod**, **V.A. Smith** |
| **Kshatriya descent** | **G.H. Ojha**, **C.V. Vaidya** |
!TRAP The four Agnikula clans are Paramara, Pratihara, Chauhan, Solanki - NOT Chandela.

| Dynasty | Founder | Region / Capital | Rulers to know |

| **Gahadavala** | Chandradeva | **Kanauj, Varanasi** | Govindachandra, Jaichand |

#### 2.2 Capitals Not to Confuse

!TRAP The Mehrauli Iron Pillar is a Gupta work only moved to Delhi; it is not Anangapala's.
### 3. RAJPUT SOCIETY AND WARFARE
| Term / Feature | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Feudal structure | Land grants carried **revenue, judicial and police rights** |
| **R.S. Sharma's "Indian feudalism"** | **Sub-infeudation**, **vishti** (forced labour), closed village economy |
| **Jauhar** | Women burning themselves to avoid capture; **Rani Padmini** of Chittor vs **Alauddin Khalji** |
| **Sati** | Also practised |
- **Warfare**: **direct, face-to-face combat** with emphasis on **personal valour** (APPSC GS 2025); chivalry and clan honour came first.
!TRAP Rajput warfare was NOT guerrilla, naval war or gunpowder-based.

#### 4.1 Works

#### 4.2 Prashasti and Monuments
- **Udaipur Prashasti**: Paramara genealogy; praises Bhoja.
- **Bhojshala, Dhar**: centre of Sanskrit learning; **Saraswati (Vagdevi)** temple; turned into a mosque in the 14th c. (Muslims call it **Kamal Maula Mosque**).
- **Bhojpur temple (Bhojeshwar)**: unfinished **Shiva** temple with a huge linga, **Raisen district**, near Bhopal; built on the bank of a **man-made lake** created by Bhoja.
- Latest: **ASI survey** ordered 2024; under a **2003 ASI arrangement** both communities worshipped on separate days; **MP High Court** declared Bhojshala a **Hindu temple** on ASI findings (**May 2026**).
### 5. SOLANKI RULERS → CONTRIBUTION
| Ruler | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Mularaja I** | Founder; capital Anhilwada |
| **Bhima I** | **Somnath sacked 1025** in his reign; built **Modhera Sun temple**; queen **Udayamati** built **Rani-ki-Vav** (UNESCO) in his memory |
| **Jayasimha Siddharaja** | Patron of **Hemachandra** (Siddha-Hema grammar); **Sahasralinga tank** at Patan; gave aid (1 lakh Balotras) to rebuild a mosque at **Khambhat** (says Muhammad Aufi) |
| **Kumarapala** (1143-1172) | Jain, disciple of Hemachandra; **rebuilt Somnath**; curbed animal killing; sent ministers to stop animal cruelty in **Kashi** |
- Solankis were Shaivas who also patronised Jainism.
!TRAP Rani-ki-Vav was built by queen Udayamati, not by Bhima I himself.
### 6. TEMPLES → DYNASTY, DEITY, STATE
| Temple | Dynasty / Builder | Deity | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Khajuraho group** (UNESCO), 950-1050 CE, Chhatarpur | **Chandelas** | Shiva, Vishnu, Jain | Madhya Pradesh |
| **Kandariya Mahadeva** (largest) | **Vidyadhara** | Shiva | MP |
| **Lakshmana temple** | Chandela | Vishnu | MP |
| **Modhera Sun temple** | **Bhima I** (Solanki) | **Surya** | Gujarat (**Mehsana** district) |
| **Vimal Vasahi**, Dilwara | **Vimal Shah**, Bhima I's minister, c. **1031** | **Rishabha (Adinatha)** | Rajasthan (Mount Abu) |
| **Luna Vasahi**, Dilwara | Ministers **Vastupala and Tejpal** (Vaghela), **1230** | **Neminatha** | Rajasthan |
| **Somnath** | Rebuilt by Bhima I, then Kumarapala; after Khalji's 1299 attack by Mahipala I | **Shiva** (jyotirlinga) | Gujarat |
- Somnath style: **Maru-Gurjara** (garbhagriha, sabha mandapa, nritya mandapa); "**Old Somnath**" by **Ahilyabai Holkar (1783)**; present temple **1951**.
- **Modhera fully solarised** (MNRE scheme, **6 MW** + **15 MWh** storage, Rs 76.66 crore), inaugurated **9 October 2022**.

### 12. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS

3. Rani-ki-Vav = **Udayamati**.
4. Bisaldeva Raso = **Narapati Nalha**; Alha Khand = **Jagnik**.
5. Khuman Raso = **Dalpati Vijay**, not Karnidan.
6. Samarangana Sutradhara = **Bhoja**.
7. Modhera = **Bhima I**, Surya.
8. Vimal Vasahi = Rishabha; Luna Vasahi = Neminatha.
9. Iron Pillar is **Gupta**.
