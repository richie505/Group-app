## U0024 | THE SIXTEEN MAHAJANAPADAS – LIST, CAPITALS, MONARCHIES VS GANASANGHAS
### 1. SOURCES LISTING THE MAHAJANAPADAS
| Text | Type | List |
|---|---|---|
| **Anguttara Nikaya** (Sutta Pitaka) | **Buddhist** | **16 Mahajanapadas** |
| **Bhagavati Sutra** | **Jain** | Also **16**, some different names; adds **Vanga, Malava, Ladha/Radha** |
| **Ashtadhyayi** (Panini) | Grammar | **22 Janapadas**; names **Magadha, Ashmaka, Kamboja** |
- **Panini**: born at **Pushkalavati** (Gandhara); Ashtadhyayi = **4,000 sutras in 8 chapters**.
- Anguttara Nikaya order: **Anga, Magadha, Kashi, Kosala, Vajji, Malla, Chedi, Vatsa, Kuru, Panchala, Matsya, Shurasena, Ashmaka, Avanti, Gandhara, Kamboja**.
### 2. MAHAJANAPADA → CAPITAL → REGION
| Mahajanapada | Capital | Region / river |
|---|---|---|
| **Anga** | **Champa** | Bhagalpur, Munger (East Bihar) |
| **Magadha** | **Rajagriha / Girivraja** → Pataliputra | Patna, Nalanda, Gaya |
| **Kashi** | **Varanasi (Rajghat)** | Ganga-Varuna confluence |
| **Kosala** | **Shravasti, Saket** (Ayodhya) | **Sarayu**; Awadh |
| **Vajji** | **Vaishali** | Vaishali, Muzaffarpur, Darbhanga (North Bihar) |
| **Malla** | **Kushinara, Pava** | Kushinagar, Deoria |
| **Chedi** | **Sotthivati / Suktimati** | Eastern Bundelkhand |
| **Vatsa** | **Kaushambi** | **Yamuna**; Prayagraj |
| **Kuru** | **Indraprastha** (also Hastinapura) | Meerut, Delhi, Thaneshwar |
| **Panchala** | North **Ahichchhatra**, south **Kampilya** | Rohilkhand, central Doab |
| **Matsya** | **Viratnagar** (Bairat) | **Jaipur, Alwar, Bharatpur** (Rajasthan) |
| **Shurasena** | **Mathura** | Braj (Yamuna) |
| **Assaka / Ashmaka** | **Potana / Potali = Bodhan** | **Godavari** (between Godavari and Narmada) |
| **Avanti** | North **Ujjayini (Ujjain)**, south **Mahishmati** | Malwa; **Sipra**, Narmada |
| **Gandhara** | **Taxila** | Peshawar-Rawalpindi |
| **Kamboja** | **Rajapura / Hatak** | NW Frontier, Hazara; **Kubha (Kabul)**; horse-breeding |
- **8 of 16** lay in present **Uttar Pradesh**; in **Bihar**: Anga, Magadha, Vajji.
- Ujjain's ancient name = **Avantika**. Chedi's Sotthivati = **Suktimati of the Mahabharata**.
- Kashi was ruled by the **Brahmadatta** dynasty until **Kosala annexed** it.
!TRAP Kosala capital = Saket and Shravasti, not Ahichchhatra. Avanti river = Sipra, not Mahanadi; Kamboja is not on the Vipas.
!KEY APPSC G1 2024: Avanti–Ujjain, Kosala–Shravasti, Vatsa–Kaushambi, Anga–Champa.
### 3. ASSAKA – THE ANDHRA-TELANGANA LINK
- **Only Mahajanapada south of the Vindhyas**, on the **Godavari**; on the **Dakshinapatha** route; named in Anguttara Nikaya and Bhagavati Sutra.
- Capital **Potana / Potali** = **Bodhan, Nizamabad (Telangana)** (APPSC G2 2012); Assakas lived near **Paithan (ancient Pratishthana)**.
- **Suttanipata**: sage **Bavari's** ashram on the Godavari in Assaka – **earliest Andhra-region link** in Buddhist texts.
- Area south of the Ganga = **Anarta** (MCQ term).
### 4. MONARCHIES VS GANASANGHAS
| Aspect | Gana-sangha | Monarchy |
|---|---|---|
| Rule | Assembly of clan heads | Single hereditary king |
| Hall | **Santhagara** | Royal court |
| Strength | Collective rule | Quick decisions |
| Weakness | Slow, divided | Succession fights |
- Gana-sanghas: **Vajji, Malla** (Kamboja later, per Arthashastra); rest monarchies.
- **Vajji confederacy**: 8-9 clans (**Lichchhavis, Videhas, Jnatrikas, Vajjis**); capital **Vaishali** – an **early republic**; led by **Chetaka**, maternal uncle of **Mahavira**.
- **Malla**: 9 clans.

### 5. RULERS CONTEMPORARY WITH THE BUDDHA
| Ruler | Kingdom |
|---|---|
| **Bimbisara, Ajatashatru** | Magadha |
| **Prasenajit (Pasenadi)** | Kosala |
| **Udayana** | Vatsa |
| **Chanda Pradyota** | Avanti |
| **Pukkusati** | Gandhara |
- Kingdoms in the Buddha's life: **Kosala and Magadha**. Buddha born **563 BCE, Lumbini**; father **Shuddhodana**, Shakya leader (Shakyas part of Kosala).
- **Udayana** married **Vasavadatta**, daughter of Pradyota – **Bhasa's Svapnavasavadatta**.
- Buddha visited **Kaushambi** under Udayana; Udayana turned Buddhist under **Pindola Bharadwaj**; donated **Ghoshitarama Vihara**.
### 6. RIVALRY OF FOUR POWERS
- **Magadha, Kosala, Vatsa, Avanti** dominated.
- **Pradyota** rivalled Bimbisara and Udayana.
- Kosala annexed **Kashi**; Magadha later got it as **dowry** and by war.
- **Shishunaga** ended Avanti's power → Magadha supreme.
### 7. MAGADHA DYNASTIES
| Dynasty | Rulers and landmarks |
|---|---|
| **Haryanka** (c. 544-460 BCE) | **Bimbisara** (544-492 BCE; founder; took Anga); **Ajatashatru** (492-460; beat Kosala, Vajjis); **Udayin** (moved capital to Pataliputra) |
| **Shishunaga** (c. 413-345 BCE) | **Shishunaga** (founder; ended Avanti); **Kalashoka** (Second Buddhist Council, Vaishali) |
| **Nanda** (345-321 BCE) | **Mahapadma Nanda** – "**Ekarat**", first non-Kshatriya dynasty; **Dhana Nanda** – last, ruled at Alexander's invasion |
- Order: **Haryanka → Shishunaga → Nanda → Maurya**; Chandragupta Maurya came to power c. **321 BCE**.
- First Magadhan empire: **6th century BCE**, Bimbisara.
- **Bimbisara's policy**: marriages with **Kosala** (part of Kashi as dowry), **Lichchhavis**, **Madra**; friendship with **Gandhara**.
- **Ajatashatru**: fought uncle Prasenajit; took Kashi; beat the Vajjis (led by his grandfather Chetaka) after a **16-year war** by sowing dissension; weapons **Mahashilakantaka** (catapult), **Rathamusala** (chariot with mace). Udayin was a Jain devotee per Jain tradition.
- **Hathigumpha inscription** of **Kharavela**: a canal dug by **Nandaraja** (Mahapadma Nanda), extended by Kharavela → Nanda control over **Kalinga**; Mahapadma took the statue of **Jina** (Jinsen) from Kalinga.
!TRAP Bimbisara's dynasty = Haryanka (APPSC 2025), not Shishunaga.
### 8. RISE OF MAGADHA
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Iron** ore | Tools and weapons |
| **Elephants** | Army strength |
| Fertile land | Rich agriculture (not pastoral economy) |
| Rivers **Ganga, Son** | Transport and defence |
| Capitals | **Rajagriha** ringed by **five hills**; **Pataliputra** at **Ganga-Son confluence** |
- Most powerful Mahajanapada, **6th-4th centuries BCE** (APPSC 2018).
- Mahajanapadas + Magadha = **second urbanisation** and the **first large Indian state**.
- Chandragupta Maurya adopted **Jainism**; with **Bhadrabahu** took it south.
- Sep 2026: BHU opened 22 boxes of **Rajghat** finds (1957-69 digs) → Varanasi urban c. **800 BCE**; MP to re-excavate **Vaishya Tekri** near Ujjain.

### 10. HETERODOX SECTS – CAUSES AND TEACHERS
#### 10.1 Causes
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Ritualism** | Costly yajnas, animal sacrifice, Sanskrit-only religion – out of reach of common people |
| **Varna rigidity** | Kshatriyas resented Brahmin dominance (Mahavira, Buddha were Kshatriyas) |
| **Agrarian economy** | Sacrifices consumed cattle needed for iron-plough farming; ahimsa suited farmers |
| **Vaishyas** | Newly rich from trade; wanted higher status and a creed that accepted trade and money-lending |
#### 10.2 Ajivikas and Charvaka
| Sect | Founder / leader | Doctrine |
|---|---|---|
| **Ajivikas** | Founded by **Nanda Vachcha**, then **Kisa Sankichcha**; popularised by **Makkhali Gosala** | **Niyati** (strict fatalism); rejected karma and free will |
| **Charvaka = Lokayata** | **Brihaspati** | Materialism; only **perception** valid; no soul, rebirth, afterlife |
- Other teachers: Ajita Kesakambali (materialist), Purana Kassapa, Pakudha Kaccayana.
- **Barabar caves** gifted to Ajivikas by **Ashoka and Dasharatha**.
- **Syadvada** = Jain; **Satkaryavada** = Samkhya.
