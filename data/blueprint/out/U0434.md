## U0434 | PLURILATERAL GROUPINGS – G20, G7, BRICS, QUAD, I2U2, IBSA, BASIC, COLOMBO SECURITY CONCLAVE
### 1. G7 / G8
- **Members (7):** US, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Canada, Japan. The **EU attends** meetings.
- **Not members** (distractors): Russia, Netherlands, New Zealand, India, China.
- **Russia** joined 1997-98 → **G8**; suspended **2014 after Crimea** → back to G7.
- **India:** regular **outreach invitee**, not a member; entry into G7 itself uncertain (Jul 2025). Jun 2026: PM Modi met President Trump at the G7 summit in **France**.
- **PGII** (Partnership for Global Infrastructure and Investment) – G7 plan launched **2022 (Germany)**; counter to China's **BRI**.
### 2. G20
#### 2.1 Origin and FIRSTs
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Planned at | **G7 Cologne summit, June 1999** |
| Set up | **26 Sep 1999** as a forum of **finance ministers and central bank governors** (after Asian Financial Crisis) |
| First meeting | **Berlin, Dec 1999** |
| First leaders' summit | **Washington D.C., 2008** (global financial crisis) |
- **Members:** **19 countries** (incl. Argentina, Mexico, South Africa, Turkey) + **EU** + **African Union** (permanent member since **2023**).
- **No permanent secretariat** or HQ; annual rotating presidency.
- **Troika:** previous + current + next presidencies; 2022 = **Indonesia, Italy, India**.
!TRAP G20 first meeting = **Berlin 1999**; first leaders' summit = **Washington 2008**.
#### 2.2 Presidencies and India's Summit
- **Saudi Arabia** took the chair from **Japan** (2019); hosted the **15th Summit, Riyadh, 21-22 Nov 2020**.
- **18th G20 Summit:** **Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, 9-10 Sep 2023** – India's **maiden presidency**; theme **"Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam – One Earth, One Family, One Future"**.
- **New Delhi Declaration (2023):** **African Union** admitted as permanent member; **Global Biofuels Alliance** launched.
- 2024 Summit – **Rio de Janeiro**; 2025 Summit – **Johannesburg**, South Africa.
- **Brisbane-eThekwini Goal** (revised, G20 Development Ministers 2025): cut gender gap in labour participation by **25% by 2030** from 2012.
### 3. BRICS
#### 3.1 Origin and Expansion
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 2001 | Term **"BRIC"** coined by **Jim O'Neill** (Goldman Sachs) |
| 2006 | BRIC formalised |
| 2009 | **First summit, 16 June, Yekaterinburg** (Russia) – Brazil, Russia, India, China |
| 2010-11 | **South Africa** invited Dec 2010; first attended at **Sanya, China (2011)** |
| Aug 2023 | New members named incl. **Argentina** (Argentina later declined, Dec 2023) |
| 2025 | **Indonesia** joined |
- Members now **10**; **Saudi Arabia's** status ambiguous (yet to formally join, 14 Sep 2026).
- India is a **founder**; India **avoids de-dollarisation**.
- Debate: "anti-West" (Russia, China, Iran) vs "non-West" (India, Brazil, South Africa).
#### 3.2 Chair Rotation (B-R-I-C-S order)
| Year | Chair / venue |
|---|---|
| 2024 | Russia (Kazan) |
| 2025 | Brazil (17th, Rio de Janeiro) |
| 2026 | **India (18th, New Delhi, 12 Sep 2026, Bharat Mandapam)** |
| 2027 | China |
- India's 2026 theme: **"Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability"**; earlier Indian hostings 2012, 2016, 2021 (virtual).
- **BRICS New Delhi Declaration (Sep 2026)**, by consensus despite the Iran-UAE conflict: UNSC expansion (China, Russia back a greater role for India and Brazil); **IMF/WB quota reform**; restoring the **WTO Appellate Body**; West Asia de-escalation; zero tolerance on terrorism.
#### 3.3 Institutions
- **NDB** – set up at **Fortaleza, 2014**; HQ **Shanghai**.
- **Contingent Reserve Arrangement** (2014) – **$100 billion** liquidity pool; BRICS, **not Quad**.

### 6. QUAD
- Origin: **Tsunami Core Group** after the **2004 Indian Ocean tsunami**; first meeting **2007**; **revived 2017**.
- First leaders' summit – **virtual, March 2021**; first in-person – **Washington, Sep 2021**.
- Dec 2024 joint statement marked the **20th anniversary** of Quad cooperation.
- Initiatives: **Quad Vaccine Partnership**, **Critical and Emerging Technologies group**, **IPMDA** (Indo-Pacific Partnership for Maritime Domain Awareness, **2022**).
### 7. IBSA AND BASIC
#### 7.1 IBSA
- Founded **2003, Brasilia Declaration**.
- **IBSA Trust Fund** – working from **2004**, funds LDC projects, run by **UNOSSC**.
- **IBSAMAR** – IBSA naval exercise.
- **5th IBSA Summit – Pretoria, Oct 2011**; Leaders' meetings: Rio de Janeiro (Nov 2024), **Johannesburg (Nov 2025)** – UNSC expansion "not an option but an imperative".
- India's proposals (Nov 2025): **IBSA Fund for Climate-Resilient Agriculture**, **IBSA Digital Innovation Alliance** (UPI, CoWIN), NSA-level dialogue.
#### 7.2 BASIC
- Formed **2009**, before **COP15 Copenhagen**.
- Stand: **CBDR-RC**; climate finance from developed countries.
### 8. COLOMBO SECURITY CONCLAVE
- Began **2011** as India-Sri Lanka-Maldives **maritime trilateral**; renamed CSC **2020**; secretariat **Colombo**.
- **Members:** India, Sri Lanka, Maldives, **Mauritius**, **Bangladesh**; **Seychelles – observer**.
- **Five pillars:** maritime safety and security; counter-terrorism and radicalisation; trafficking and organised crime; cyber security and critical infrastructure; **HADR**.
