### 3. WOMEN'S CRICKET
#### 3.1 Harmanpreet Kaur – most-capped woman
- On **19 February 2026** in **Canberra**, Harmanpreet played her **356th** international – the **most-capped player in women's international cricket**, overtaking **Suzie Bates** (355).
- Break-up: 189 T20Is, 161 ODIs, 6 Tests; famous **171 vs Australia**, 2017 World Cup semi-final.

- Earlier PYQ facts: the four Grand Slams have a **shared governance structure** (Grand Slam Tennis, run jointly by the four tournaments) and are **open for entry to all**.

### 9. ADVENTURE, ENDURANCE AND FIRSTS
#### 9.1 Indian expedition to Mount Aconcagua
- Flagged off **5 February 2026** by **Rajnath Singh**, New Delhi.
- **Aconcagua** – Andes, **Argentina**; ~**6,960 m** – **highest in South America** and **highest outside Asia**.
- Organised by **Nehru Institute of Mountaineering (NIM), Uttarkashi** (est. 1965) with **JIMWS, Pahalgam** (est. 1983); six-member team led by **Col. Hem Chandra Singh**.
#### 9.2 Satendra Singh Lohia – Cook Strait
- Indian para-swimmer **Satendra Singh Lohia** (38) swam the **Cook Strait** (New Zealand) in **9 h 22 min** (~23.6 km) – **first Asian para-swimmer** to cross it; part of the "**Oceans Seven**"; **Padma Shri 2024**.
- **Cook Strait** – between the **North and South Islands of New Zealand**; links the **Tasman Sea** and the **South Pacific**.
- Earlier PYQ place fact: **Tweed Valley** – **Scotland** (River Tweed; Scotland–England border area).
