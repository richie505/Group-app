## U0544 | SHARING OF RESOURCES AND DEVOLUTION – HORIZONTAL AND VERTICAL DEVOLUTION CRITERIA, GRANTS
### 1. FINANCE COMMISSION – ARTICLE 280
- **Quasi-judicial** body formed by the **President** every **fifth year or earlier**; **Chairman + 4 members**.
- Qualifications: **FC (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1951**.
- **Art. 281**: President lays the report before each House with an **explanatory memorandum** on action taken.
- Recommendations are **advisory**, not binding.
#### 1.1 Duties under Article 280(3)
| Clause | Duty |
|---|---|
| (a) | Share **net tax proceeds** Centre-States (vertical) and among States (horizontal) |
| (b) | Principles for **grants-in-aid** from CFI (Art. 275); non-plan grants |
| (bb), (c) | Augment State Consolidated Fund for **Panchayats and Municipalities** (73rd/74th Amendments, **1992**) |
| (d) | Any matter referred by the **President** (e.g. disaster financing under DM Act, 2005 for 15th FC) |
#### 1.2 What it does NOT do
- **Maintaining income-expenditure accounts** of Union and States (APPSC G2 2017).
- **GST revenue sharing** (GST Council), cesses/surcharges, plan/discretionary grants.
- 16th FC ToR (notified **31 Dec 2023**): NOT to review present arrangements on financing (RAS 2024).
!TRAP NOT an FC duty: maintaining accounts of Union and States.

### 4. DIVISIBLE POOL AND THE "41% ILLUSION"
- Pool = Art. 270 taxes (corporation tax, income tax, CGST, Centre's IGST share); **excludes cesses, surcharges, cost of collection**.
- Pool as share of gross tax: **89.2% (FC-13) → 82.1% (FC-14) → 78.3% (FC-15)**; about **81%** in 2025-26.
- **"41% illusion"**: cesses and surcharges outside the pool → stated share exaggerates States' real resources.
- 16th FC: annual **CAG-certified** disclosure of net tax proceeds.

### 6. CHANNELS OF TRANSFER AND GRANTS
| Channel | Article | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| Tax devolution | 270 | **Untied** |
| FC grants | 275 | **Statutory**, charged on CFI |
| Discretionary grants | 282 | Mostly **tied**, via Union ministries; route for most CSS |
| CSS | 282 | Centre-designed; **State matching share** |
- **Art. 123** (ordinance) is NOT a transfer channel.
- Art. 275 grants: revenue deficit, local bodies, disasters, **tribal welfare** — NOT for every CSS.
- **CSS 2026-27**: ₹5,48,798 crore (+30.6%); 16th FC tied CSS to **measurable outcomes**, proposed a **high-powered committee** to close inefficient schemes.
#### 6.1 Grants by FC
- **15th FC**: revenue deficit, **sector-specific, State-specific** grants; total **₹10.1 lakh crore**; **₹4,800 crore** (₹1,200 crore a year, 2022-23 to 2025-26) for **educational outcomes**.
- **16th FC**: total **₹9.47 lakh crore**; share in FC transfers 19.4% → 8.3%; only **local body (₹7.91 lakh crore)** + **disaster** grants; **discontinued** revenue deficit, sector- and State-specific grants.
- Disaster: SDRF/SDMF corpus **₹2,04,401 crore** (Union ~₹1.55 lakh crore); cost-share **90:10** NE/Himalayan, **75:25** others; **heatwave and lightning** to be notified disasters.
### 7. LOCAL BODY GRANTS – TIED vs UNTIED
- 15th FC rural grants: **60% tied** (drinking water, rainwater harvesting, sanitation/waste); **40% untied** (local needs, **not salaries**).
- Condition: provisional and **audited accounts** in public domain by at least **25%** of rural local bodies; **State Finance Commission** mandatory for release from 2024-25.
### 8. GADGIL AND GADGIL-MUKHERJEE FORMULAE
| Criterion | Gadgil (1969) | Gadgil-Mukherjee (1991) |
|---|---|---|
| Population | 60% | **60%** |
| Per capita income | 10% | **25%** |
| Tax effort | 10% | **7.5%** |
| Irrigation and power projects | 10% | – |
| Special problems | 10% | **7.5%** |
- Endorsed by **NDC 1968-69**; used from the **4th Plan (1969)**.
- First **Special Category States**: **Assam, J&K, Nagaland** → **90% grant, 10% loan**; general States **30:70**.
- Formula transfers ended when **NITI Aayog** replaced the Planning Commission (**1 January 2015**).
### 9. TRANSFERS AND OUTCOMES; BORROWING; GST COMPENSATION
- **ES 2016-17**: no positive link between **redistributive transfers** and per capita consumption, GSDP growth or governance; suggestive **negative** effect on **own tax effort**; transfers should be linked to fiscal and governance effort.
- **ES 2025-26**: transfers "increased significantly"; **SASCI** 50-year interest-free capex loans stopped **pro-cyclical** compression; State capex 2.17% (FY22) → 2.37% of GDP (FY25).
- Budget 2026-27: devolution **₹15,26,255 crore**; total transfers ₹26,20,769 crore; capex loans ₹1,85,000 crore.
- **Borrowing**: State fiscal deficit capped at **3% of GSDP** (16th FC); extra **0.5% of GSDP** for **power-sector reforms** (2021-22 to 2024-25, again 2025-26); extra borrowing equal to **NPS/UPS** contributions (₹62,778 crore in 2024-25).
- **GST compensation**: cess paid 5 years (July 2017 – June 2022); continued to repay COVID loans; ended 22 Sep 2025 except tobacco; unused balance **50:50** Centre-States (by **SGST revenue**).
### 10. AP ANGLE
- FC grants = AP's largest central transfer: **₹62,188 crore of ₹1,04,493 crore** (2025-26 RE).
- 16th FC: rural LBs **₹16,627 crore**, urban LBs **₹12,158 crore**, disaster **₹6,125 crore**.
