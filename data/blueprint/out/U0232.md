## U0232 | GANDHI ON CASTE, UNTOUCHABILITY, RELIGION, WOMEN AND EDUCATION (NAI TALIM)
### 1. UNTOUCHABILITY AND CASTE
- Untouchability = **"a blot on Hinduism"**; reform **from within** Hindu society.
- Route to uplift: **moral persuasion, temple entry, funds, schools and hostels**, constructive work.
- Post-1937 Congress ministries gave **free education to Harijans**.
- **"Harijan"** ("children of God") – term taken from Gujarati saint **Narsi Mehta**.
- **Ambedkar** and Dalit leaders **rejected the term "Harijan"** and criticised caste-Hindu paternalism.

### 5. WOMEN
- Opposed **purdah, child marriage, dowry**; supported **remarriage of child widows**.
- Role in satyagraha: **picketing liquor shops**, making salt.
- Andhra: **Desasevikalu** women's wing; women gave **jewellery for the Tilak Fund**; **Durgabai** met Gandhi at **Kakinada (1921)** and raised **Rs 5,000**.
- **Kolla Tayaramma** (Tenali) and **Alladi Annapurna Devi** (Nellore) – Harijan schools and hostels.
### 6. RELIGION
- **Sarva dharma samabhava** – equal respect for all religions; **ashram prayers** drew on many faiths.
- **Hindu-Muslim unity** = bedrock of nationalism; backed **Khilafat**.
### 7. NAI TALIM – WARDHA SCHEME (1937)
- **Basic education** propounded by **Gandhi**; vocational, **craft-centred**; self-sufficient villages; dignity of manual labour.
- Adopted at the **Wardha Educational Conference, 1937**; drawn up by the **Zakir Hussain Committee**.
- Principles: **free and compulsory**, **7 years (age 7-14)**; **mother tongue** medium; learning through a **productive craft**.
### 8. GANDHI IN ANDHRA
- Toured Andhra on the Harijan tour to collect funds and campaign against untouchability; spoke at **Mogalrajpuram, Vijayawada (16 December 1933)**.
