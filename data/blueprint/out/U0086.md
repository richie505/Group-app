## U0086 | IMPERIAL CHOLAS – VIJAYALAYA, RAJARAJA I, RAJENDRA I; GANGAIKONDA TITLE AND NAVAL EXPEDITIONS
### 1. ORIGIN AND CORE REGION
- Imperial Cholas flourished c. **850–1279 CE** (9th to 13th c.); rose from the **Kaveri delta**.
- **Coromandel** comes from **Cholamandalam** (Tondaimandalam was the **Pallava** country).
- Empire covered the Coromandel Coast and parts of the Deccan (Tungabhadra to Krishna).
- Chola kings recorded achievements in inscriptions → most voluminous inscriptions in South India.
- Cholas were famous for elaborate **local self-government** and excellent village administration (Uttaramerur inscriptions of Parantaka I).
- Kings named their **successor during their own rule** (co-regency).
#### 1.1 Founder
- **Vijayalaya**, a **Pallava feudatory at Uraiyur**, captured **Thanjavur** from the **Muttaraiyars** in **850 CE** → founder of the imperial line.
- Successor: **Aditya I** (not Parantaka I).
!TRAP Founder = Vijayalaya, not Parantaka I or Rajendra I. Aditya I, not Vijayalaya, followed the founder.
### 2. SUCCESSION – RULERS IN ORDER
| Order | Ruler | Title | Key achievement |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Vijayalaya** (c. 850) | Founder | Took Thanjavur |
| 2 | **Aditya I** | – | Defeated **Pallava Aparajita**; annexed Tondaimandalam; expanded north |
| 3 | **Parantaka I** | **Madurai-konda** (Maduraikondavan) | Defeated Pandyas and Sri Lanka; Uttaramerur inscriptions; lost **Takkolam (949)** |
| 4 | Parantaka II (Sundara Chola) / Uttama Chola | – | Recovered Tondaimandalam from Rashtrakutas; beat Pandya-Sri Lanka combine |
| 5 | **Rajaraja I** (Arumolivarman, **985–1014**) | **Mummudi Chola**, **Ulakalanda Perumal** | Largest state in South India; Brihadeeswara temple (1010); Maldives; Anuradhapura destroyed |
| 6 | **Rajendra I** (co-regent 1012, king **1014–1044**) | **Gangaikonda Chola**, **Kadaram Kondan**, **Pandita Chola** | Greatest territorial and maritime extent; Ganga (1022) and Srivijaya (1025) expeditions |
| 7 | **Rajadhiraja I** (from 1044) | Vijaya Rajendra; **Yanai-mel-thunjina Devar** | Killed at **Koppam (1053)** on an elephant's back |
| – | **Rajendra II** | – | **Crowned on the battlefield** of Koppam; victory pillar at **Kolhapur** |
| 8 | **Virarajendra** → Adhirajendra | – | Won Kudalasangamam; line ended 1070–71 |
| 9 | **Kulottunga I** (**1070–1122**) | Sungam Tavirtta Chola | First Chalukya-Chola king |
| Last | **Rajendra III** (1246–1279) | – | End; rise of the Pandyas |
- Short order often asked: **Vijayalaya → Aditya I → Rajaraja I → Rajendra I**.
- Rajaraja I (accession 985) was the **first Chola king to create a naval army**.

### 5. NAVAL EXPEDITIONS AND THE "CHOLA LAKE"
#### 5.1 Srivijaya Expedition (1025 CE)
- By **Rajendra I** against the revived **Srivijaya** empire – a sea empire under the **Sailendra** dynasty (Malay Peninsula, **Sumatra**, **Java** and nearby islands).
- Defeated the **Sailendra king**; took **Kadaram (Kedah)** in Malaya and parts of Sumatra (**APPSC 2000, 2003**; UPSC 2025).
- Cause: once-cordial Chola-Sailendra ties soured; Srivijaya **blocked Indian traders on the China route**; the expedition widened Chola trade with China.
- Rajaraja I had earlier sent a naval expedition against the Sailendras.
#### 5.2 Significance
- **K.A. Nilakanta Sastri**: Cholas turned the **Bay of Bengal into a "Chola Lake"**.
- Cholas = **first Indian power with overseas naval campaigns**; spread Dravidian art to South-East Asia (Angkor Wat, Borobudur).
| Ruler | Naval destination |
|---|---|
| Rajaraja I | Chera fleet (Kandalur Salai), north Sri Lanka, Maldives |
| Rajendra I | Whole Sri Lanka; Srivijaya / Kadaram (1025) |
| Kulottunga I | Kalinga |

### 7. BATTLES → PARTICIPANTS AND VICTOR
- Chola vs Kalyani Chalukya wars = struggle for **Vengi** (succession disputes, each side backing a claimant) + supremacy in the Deccan.
| Battle | Participants | Victor |
|---|---|---|
| **Sri Purambiyam** (c. 885) | Pallava Aparajita vs Pandya **Varaguna II** | Aparajita |
| **Takkolam (949)** | Rashtrakuta **Krishna III** vs Parantaka I | **Rashtrakutas** |
| **Kalidindi** (near Eluru) | Rajendra I's army vs Vijayaditya VII + Kalyani Chalukyas | Cholas; Rajaraja Narendra built **three Shiva temples** for fallen Chola commanders |
| **Dharanikota** | Rajadhiraja I vs Vijayaditya VII and Kalyani army | Rajadhiraja I; then **burnt Kollipaka** |
| **Koppam (1053)** | Rajadhiraja I / Rajendra II vs **Someshvara I Ahavamalla** | Rajadhiraja I killed; Rajendra II claimed victory |
| **Kudalasangamam** (c. 1062) | **Virarajendra** vs Someshvara I, at the Krishna–Tungabhadra confluence | **Virarajendra** (Cholas) |
!TRAP Takkolam was a Chola defeat (Rashtrakutas won). At Koppam the Chola king died but Rajendra II was crowned on the field.
### 8. CHOLA–EASTERN CHALUKYA MARRIAGES
| Princess | Father | Husband (Eastern Chalukya) |
|---|---|---|
| **Kundavai** | Rajaraja I | **Vimaladitya** – not Saktivarman (APPSC G2 2025) |
| **Ammanga Devi** | Rajendra I | **Rajaraja Narendra** (1019–1061), married **16 Aug 1022** |
| **Madhurantaki** | **Rajendra II** | **Rajendra Chalukya = Kulottunga I** |
- **Saktivarman I**: Vimaladitya's elder brother; ruled Vengi with Rajaraja I's help after **Jatachoda Bhima** was removed.
- Son of Rajaraja Narendra and Ammanga Devi = **Rajendra Chalukya (Kulottunga I)**.
!KEY These marriages made Kulottunga I heir to both the Chola and Vengi thrones.
### 9. VENGI VICEROYS AND THE CHALUKYA-CHOLA MERGER
| Viceroy | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Rajaraja (Mummadi Chola)**, son of Kulottunga I | Titles **Vishnuvardhana, Sarvalokashraya**; ruled from Jagannathapuram; commander **Gonka I**; installed **Anantavarman Chodaganga** in Kalinga |
| **Vira Chola**, son of Kulottunga I | Viceroy twice; crowned at Jagannathapuram |
| **Rajaraja Chola Ganga** | Titles **Sarvalokasraya, Sri Vishnuvardhana**; crowned at **Jananathapura (Draksharama)**; authority up to Mahendragiri; **Tekkali** inscription |
- Last Chalukya-Chola ruler in Vengi: **Parantaka Chola**; **Vijayaditya VII** = last Eastern Chalukya.
- **1075**: after Vijayaditya VII's death, Kulottunga I **merged Vengi** with the Chola dominions → united Tamil (Chola) and Andhra (Vengi) kingdoms.
- AP angle: coastal Andhra (Vengi) was the prize of the Chola-Chalukya wars; **Mandalika Yugam** followed (**1076 to c. 1210**).
### 10. KULOTTUNGA I AND THE LATER CHOLAS
#### 10.1 Kulottunga I
- Born **Rajendra Chalukya**, son of Rajaraja Narendra + Ammanga Devi.
- **1070**: ousted from Vengi by uncle Vijayaditya VII; Virarajendra and Adhirajendra died → took the Chola throne (1070).
- **First king of the Chalukya-Chola line** (APPSC G2 2011).
- Sent **72 merchants to China** (**1077**, goodwill mission; UPPSC 1992).
- Conquered **Kalinga**; built Kulottunga Cholapatnam (Visakhapatnam); **abolished tolls**.
#### 10.2 Sri Lanka Relations and Decline
- **Vijayabahu I** expelled the Cholas from Sri Lanka around **1070**.
- Kulottunga I **gave freedom to Sri Lanka** (Vijayabahu declared independence) and married his daughter to Sinhala prince **Virapperumal**.
- Decline after Kulottunga I: **weakening central bureaucracy**, growth of **local chieftains**, then the **rise of the Pandyas**; Rajendra III gone by **1279**.
#### 10.3 Latest Developments
- **1 Feb 2026**: Grand Chola Museum, Thanjavur – 125-ft Rajaraja Chola statue planned; 52 acres; Rs 56.41 crore.
- **16 May 2026**: Netherlands returned the **Anaimangalam (Leiden) copper plates** at The Hague – ordered by Rajaraja I, engraved under Rajendra I, extended by Kulottunga I.
- **8 Sep 2026**: Tamil Nadu began the third phase of underwater exploration off **Poompuhar** (early Chola capital).
