## U0419 | RIGHTS OF WORKERS AND MIGRANTS – LABOUR CODES, UNORGANISED SECTOR, E-SHRAM
### 1. THE FOUR LABOUR CODES
| Code | Year |
|---|---|
| **Code on Wages** | **2019** |
| **Industrial Relations Code** | 2020 |
| **Code on Social Security** | 2020 |
| **Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions (OSH) Code** | 2020 |
- Together they replaced **29** central labour laws (e.g. **Industrial Disputes Act 1947** into the IR Code).
- All four came into force on **21 November 2025**.
- Critique: **Indian Labour Conference** not held since 2015; digital exclusion.
### 2. INDUSTRIAL RELATIONS CODE – THRESHOLDS
| Provision | Number |
|---|---|
| Prior permission for **layoff, retrenchment, closure** | **300** workers (earlier **100**) |
| **Standing Orders** | 300 |
| **Contract labour** | **20 → 50** |
| **Strike notice** | No strike without notice within **60 days** before; or within **14 days** of notice |
| **Fixed-term** workers | Same wages and benefits as permanent; **gratuity after 1 year** |
!NUM 300 = layoff/closure + Standing Orders; 50 = contract labour; 60/14 days = strike notice
### 3. CODE ON SOCIAL SECURITY 2020 – GIG AND PLATFORM WORKERS
#### 3.1 Key Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Gig worker** | Works **outside** a traditional employer-employee relationship |
| **Platform worker** | Works through **online platforms** |
| **Aggregator** | Digital intermediary; first recognised by the **Code on Social Security 2020** |
- The Code is the **first law** to recognise **aggregators, gig and platform workers**.
- Gig workers **register from age 16**; **National and State Social Security Boards**.
- Limits: registration gives **no employee status**, **no minimum wage** guarantee, no industrial-dispute remedies.
- Gig workers: **NITI** estimate **77 lakh** (2020-21); Economic Survey **120 lakh**.
#### 3.2 State Platform Worker Laws
| State | Law |
|---|---|
| **Rajasthan** | Platform-Based Gig Workers (Registration and Welfare) Act, **2023** – first; welfare board funded by a **cess on transactions** |
| **Karnataka** | Platform Based Gig Workers (Social Security and Welfare) Act, **2025** |
| Telangana | Gig and Platform Workers law (draft 2025) |
### 4. UNORGANISED SECTOR – 2008 ACT AND NCEUS
#### 4.1 Unorganised Workers' Social Security Act, 2008
- Set up a **National Social Security Board**; welfare schemes in **Schedule I**.
- **Schedule I schemes:** IGNOAPS, **National Family Benefit Scheme**, **Janani Suraksha Yojana**, Janashree Bima Yojana, **Aam Aadmi Bima Yojana**, **RSBY**, and schemes for weavers, artisans, fishermen.
- **Atal Pension Yojana is NOT** in Schedule I.
- Merged into the **Code on Social Security, 2020**; critics say limited impact.
#### 4.2 NCEUS and Informal Workforce
- **NCEUS** (National Commission for Enterprises in the Unorganised Sector): set up **2004**, chaired by **Arjun Sengupta**; found **77%** living on **under Rs 20 a day**.
- **Over 90%** of India's roughly **600 million** workers are informal; workforce **61.6 crore** (PLFS 2025).
!TRAP 77% = NCEUS (under Rs 20 a day); 77 lakh = NITI gig estimate.
### 5. SOCIAL SECURITY SCHEMES
#### 5.1 Earlier Schemes
| Scheme | Year | Target group |
|---|---|---|
| **Ashraya Bima Yojana** | **2001** (LIC) | Workers who **lost their jobs** |
| **RSBY** | **2008** | **BPL** families; **Rs 30,000** hospitalisation cover; later subsumed in **AB-PMJAY**; a social protection programme |
| **Swavalamban** | **2010** | Unorganised workers in **NPS-Lite** (govt co-contribution); replaced by **APY in 2015** |
#### 5.2 Current Pension and Insurance Schemes
| Scheme | Launch | Age | Benefit / contribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PM-SYM** (Shram Yogi Maan-dhan) | March 2019 | **18-40** | **Rs 3,000/month** from 60; income up to **Rs 15,000/month**; Rs 55-200/month matched by Centre |
| **NPS-Traders** | Sept 2019 | 18-40 | Rs 3,000/month from 60; turnover up to **Rs 1.5 crore** |
| **Atal Pension Yojana** | Budget **2015-16** | 18-40 | Rs **1,000-5,000**/month from 60; run by **PFRDA** |
| **PMJJBY** | 2015 | **18-50** | **Rs 2 lakh** life cover (death from any cause); premium **Rs 436/year** |
| **PMSBY** | 2015 | 18-70 | **Accidental death and disability**: Rs 2 lakh (Rs 1 lakh partial) for **Rs 20/year** |
- PM-SYM / NPS-Traders **exclude** EPFO, ESIC or govt-funded NPS members and income-tax payers.
!TRAP "NPS members are also eligible for PM-SYM" is WRONG. 18-50 = PMJJBY only; PM-SYM, NPS-Traders, APY = 18-40.
#### 5.3 ESIC, EPFO and Employment Incentive
- **ESIC:** coverage now **pan-India**; **voluntary** for units with **fewer than 10** employees; wage ceiling **Rs 21,000/month** (since 2017).
- **EPFO:** wage ceiling raised from Rs 15,000 to **Rs 25,000** (Sept 2026).
- **PMVBRY:** **Rs 99,446 crore** for **3.5 crore** jobs; first-time employees get one month's wage, up to Rs 15,000.
### 6. E-SHRAM PORTAL
- Launched **26 August 2021** by the Ministry of Labour and Employment.
- India's **first Aadhaar-seeded** **National Database of Unorganised Workers (NDUW)**.
- **Eligibility:** unorganised workers aged **16-59**; **not income-tax payers**; **not EPFO/ESIC** members.
- **12-digit UAN** (Universal Account Number) → benefits **portable** anywhere in India.
- **e-Shram One-Stop Solution (2024):** links **15 central schemes** (PM-SVANidhi, PMSBY, PMJJBY, AB-PMJAY, PM-SYM etc.).
- Registrations: over **31.78 crore** nationally; **AP 88.8 lakh** (14 July 2026).
- Five years on, most gig workers still off the portal.
!TRAP e-Shram age = 16-59; gig registration under the Code = from 16.
### 7. INTER-STATE MIGRANT WORKERS
- **Inter-State Migrant Workmen Act, 1979**: registration of establishments, licensing of contractors; **displacement allowance = 50% of monthly wages**; journey allowance.
- **Subsumed** in the **OSH Code, 2020** (repealed 21 Nov 2025); the Code gives an **annual to-and-fro journey allowance** instead; coverage threshold **10 workers** (earlier 5).
- **One Nation One Ration Card**: migrants draw PDS ration anywhere in India.
### 8. BONDED LABOUR
- **Article 23** bans **begar** and forced labour → **Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act, 1976**.
- **District Vigilance Committees** identify and free bonded labourers.
- **Central Sector Scheme for Rehabilitation of Bonded Labourer (2016)**: Rs **1 lakh / 2 lakh / 3 lakh** by category; 100% Central funding; revised 2021 (immediate aid raised to Rs 30,000).
