### 2. LAW COMMISSION OF INDIA
#### 2.1 History and Nature
- Colonial precursor: **Macaulay's** Law Commission produced the **IPC (1860)**.
- First post-independence Law Commission: **1955**, chaired by **M.C. Setalvad**.
- Nature: **neither constitutional nor statutory**; an **executive, advisory** body set up by a Government of India order for a **fixed 3-year term**.
- Reports to the **Ministry of Law and Justice**, not the President.
- **23rd Law Commission**: constituted **September 2024** for 3 years; Chairperson **Justice Dinesh Maheshwari** (former SC judge), took charge **15 April 2025**; term up to **31 August 2027**.
- **21st Law Commission**: 2018 Consultation Paper on Family Law → **UCC "neither necessary nor desirable at this stage"** (18 Sep 2026).
!TRAP 2024 APPSC PYQ on the Law Commission: **all the statements were incorrect**.

#### 3.1 Kovind Committee on Simultaneous Elections
- Set up **2 September 2023**; report to the President on **14 March 2024** (**18,626 pages**).
- Proposed **Article 82A** (synchronise terms of Lok Sabha and State Assemblies) and **Article 324A** (municipal and panchayat polls with general elections).
- Lok Sabha and Assembly polls first; local body polls to follow; amendment for Art. 82A needs **no State ratification**.
- **Constitution (129th Amendment) Bill, 2024** introduced in the Lok Sabha in **December 2024** and sent to a **JPC**.

### 6. HIGHER-EDUCATION REGULATOR – NCHER TO VBSA BILL
- **NCHER** (National Commission for Higher Education and Research): proposed after the **Yashpal Committee (2009)**; announced by HRD Minister **Kapil Sibal**; **never enacted**.
- **Viksit Bharat Shiksha Adhishthan (VBSA) Bill, 2025** (Ministry of Education): **single apex body** to replace **UGC, AICTE, NCTE** and other regulators (10 Jul 2026).
- Sent to a **Joint Parliamentary Committee (JPC)**, which flagged **centralisation**.
- In the JPC, **Andhra Pradesh, Meghalaya and Madhya Pradesh** objected to centralising provisions (10 Jul 2026).
- After AP objected, the Centre agreed that **State consent** is needed for decisions on colleges of State universities (18 Jul 2026).
- **Not passed** in the Monsoon Session 2026 (14 Aug 2026).
!PAIR NCHER | Yashpal 2009 | never enacted || VBSA Bill 2025 | replaces UGC, AICTE, NCTE | in JPC
