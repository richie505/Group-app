### 2. DIRECT ATTACK ON POVERTY – IRDP AND ITS FAMILY
#### 2.1 Programmes → year and aim
| Programme | Full form / aim | Start |
|---|---|---|
| **IRDP** | Integrated Rural Development Programme: subsidy + bank credit so poor rural families **come above the poverty line** | Pilot 1976; small way 1978-79; all blocks **2 Oct 1980** (6th Plan, 1980-85) |
| **TRYSEM** | Training of Rural Youth for Self Employment; extra part of IRDP | **15 Aug 1979** |
| **NREP** | Rural employment; successor of Food for Work | Oct 1980 (6th Plan) |
| **RLEGP** | Rural Landless Employment Guarantee Programme | 1983 (6th Plan) |
| **JRY** | Merger of **NREP + RLEGP**; labour-oriented additional employment | 1989 (7th Plan) |
| **DWCRA** | Development of Women and Children in Rural Areas; women's group approach | 1982 (1982-83) |
| **SITRA** | Supply of Improved Toolkits to Rural Artisans | 1992 |
| **Ganga Kalyan Yojana** | Bore-well/tube-well irrigation for small and marginal farmers | 1997 |
| PMRY / REGP | Self-employment / rural employment generation | 1993 / 1995 |
- IRDP main aim: employment to poor rural households; **Seventh Plan** strategy = **total household approach**.
- IRDP, TRYSEM and NREP = all for eradication of rural poverty; **NREP and RLEGP** both in the 6th Plan.
!TRAP Odd-one-out: **MRTP** (1969 Act on monopolies), **NSSF** (2011, ₹1000 crore), **CRY**, crop insurance or a statistics body are NOT anti-poverty programmes; MGNREGA, NRLM, SJSRY, TRYSEM, JRY, IRDP are.
!KEY Chronologies: IRDP → RLEGP → PMRY → REGP; IRDP → NREP → DWCRA → JRY; IRDP (1980) → SGSY (1999) → MGNREGA (2006) → PM Jan-Dhan.

### 4. RESTRUCTURING CHAIN – OLD → NEW
| Old | New | Year |
|---|---|---|
| IRDP + TRYSEM + DWCRA + SITRA + GKY + Million Wells Scheme (6) | **SGSY** (Swarnjayanti Gram Swarozgar Yojana) | **1 April 1999** |
| SGSY | NRLM **"Aajeevika"** | Restructured 2010; launched **June 2011**; fully replaced SGSY 1 April 2013 |
| NRLM | **DAY-NRLM** | 2016 |
| SJSRY (1997) | **NULM** → DAY-NULM | 2013 |
| PMRY | PMEGP | 2008 |
- **IRDP = individual family approach → SGSY = SHG/group approach** (group financing; bring assisted poor above poverty line by organising them into SHGs).
!TRAP **JRY is not part of SGSY.** SGSY → NRLM = rural chain; SJSRY → NULM = urban chain.
### 5. DAY-NRLM
#### 5.1 Basics
- 2011; **Ministry of Rural Development**; Centrally Sponsored.
- Funding (Aug 2026): general **60:40**; NE incl. Sikkim **90:10**; UTs **100%**.
- Borrowers: **women SHGs**; poor identified by **Participatory Identification of Poor (PIP)**, vetted by **Gram Sabha**; up to **30%** members may be slightly above poverty line.
- Three tiers: **SHG → Village Organisation → Cluster-Level Federation**.
#### 5.2 Scale and sub-initiatives
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Coverage (3 Feb 2026) | ~10 crore households; 91 lakh SHGs; 5.35 lakh VOs; 33,558 CLFs |
| **Lakhpati Didi** (2023) | ₹1 lakh+ a year; 2 crore crossed (Feb 2026); target **6 crore by March 2029** (Aug 2026) |
| **SVEP** (2016) | Start-up Village Entrepreneurship Programme: non-farm enterprises; 4.32 lakh; women ≥60% (Jul 2026) |
### 6. DAY-NULM (URBAN)
- Ministry: **Housing and Urban Poverty Alleviation** (now Housing and Urban Affairs) (APPSC 2023); skill development for urban poor.
| Component | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **SM&ID** | Social Mobilisation & Institution Development |
| **SUSV** | Support to Urban Street Vendors – vending spaces, credit, skills |
| **SUH** | Shelter for Urban Homeless – permanent all-weather shelters |
| Others | Skill training/placement; self-employment |
| **PAISA** | Portal for Affordable Credit and Interest Subvention Access (APPSC 2019) |
- AP agency: **MEPMA** – Mission for Elimination of Poverty in Municipal Areas (1 Sep 2007).
- **UNDP – DAY-NULM** partnership (June 2023): women's well-informed career choices.
- Urban poverty is rooted in rural areas: low rural literacy; migrants do low-quality work.
### 7. SHG CONCEPT, PIONEERS AND WOMEN'S SCHEMES
#### 7.1 Pioneers and SHG-Bank Linkage
| Person / body | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Muhammad Yunus**, Grameen Bank | SHG idea as poverty tool; collateral-free loans to poor women (project 1976) |
| **MYRADA** | Pioneered bank linkage, mid-1980s (not the national programme) |
| **NABARD** | **SHG-Bank Linkage pilot 1992**; world's largest microfinance programme |
- NABARD refinances, trains, promotes SHGs; not a direct lender. RBI = regulator; SIDBI = small industries.
- SHG programme first started by **NABARD, not SBI**; SHGs a formal tool from 7th Plan; purpose = microfinance + thrift for poor women.
#### 7.2 Women-focused schemes
| Scheme | Objective |
|---|---|
| Rashtriya Mahila Kosh (1993) | Micro-credit to poor women, unorganised sector |

| **Swalamban / Swawlamban** (1982-83) | Training in traditional and non-traditional trades; former NORAD Mahila Aarthik Karyakram |
| STEP | Support to Training and Employment Programme for Women |

### 8. SHG MOVEMENT IN ANDHRA PRADESH
| Programme | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **SAPAP** | 1995 | **UNDP**; Mahabubnagar, Kurnool, Anantapur |
| Janmabhoomi | 1997 | AP rural development programme |
| **SERP** | 2000 | Society for Elimination of Rural Poverty; registered society |
| **Velugu** | 2000 | **World Bank** District Poverty Initiatives Project (six poorest districts); federated SHGs into VOs, mandal samakhyas |
| **IKP** | 2004-05 | Velugu + DWCRA merged; one anti-poverty programme via women's organisations; by SERP |
| Pavala Vaddi | 2004 | Interest subsidy for SHGs |
- **Stree Nidhi**: credit to SHG members, rural and urban. **Unnati**: poorest SC/ST SHG households.
- **Vaddi Leni Runalu** (interest-free loans): rewards prompt repayment. **Abhaya Hastham**: pension + insurance for SHG women.
- SHG-Bank Linkage: AP **1st**, 36% share, 99.66% recovery; ~₹32,190 crore rural disbursement (2024-25); 10 lakh+ SHGs.
- AP's SERP/Velugu = template for NRLM; rural = SERP, urban = MEPMA.
- Challenges: uneven quality, weak market links, debt stress, political interference.
!TRAP IKP is not Velugu's earlier name; SAPAP = UNDP, Velugu = World Bank.
