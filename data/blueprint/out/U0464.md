## U0464 | SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT – DEFINITION, BRUNDTLAND REPORT, SDGS AND INDIA'S PROGRESS
### 1. MEANING OF SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT
- **Sustainable development** = meeting present needs **without reducing the options of future generations**.
- Core goal = **intergenerational equity** within Earth's **carrying capacity**.
- Simple meaning: **balancing the need to use resources and conserve them for the future**.
- Base of sustainable development = **environmental approach**.
#### 1.1 Basic goal vs NOT goals (odd-one-out)
| Is a goal of sustainable development | NOT a goal |
|---|---|
| Meet present needs while keeping choices for future generations | **Growth at any cost** |
| Stay within Earth's carrying capacity | **Exploiting resources for present gains** |
| Balance economy, society and environment | **Profit maximisation** |
| Intergenerational equity | **Prioritise individual wealth accumulation** |
| | **Unrestricted resource exploitation** |
!TRAP "Prioritize individual wealth accumulation" is NOT an objective of sustainable development.
### 2. MILESTONES – FROM STOCKHOLM TO AGENDA 2030
#### 2.1 Timeline
| Year | Milestone | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| 1968 (Apr) | **Club of Rome** founded | Meeting of **30 people from 10 countries** at the **Accademia dei Lincei, Rome** |
| **1972** | **Stockholm** – UN Conference on the Human Environment (5-16 June) | First put environment on the global agenda; created **UNEP** |
| **1972** | **"Limits to Growth"** | **Club of Rome**; Earth's **finite carrying capacity** |
| **1980** | **World Conservation Strategy** (**IUCN, UNEP, WWF**) | Term **"sustainable development"** introduced |
| **1987** | **"Our Common Future"** – **Brundtland Report** | **First formal definition** |
| **1992** | **UNCED, Rio** (Earth Summit) | **Rio Declaration**, Agenda 21, UNFCCC |
| **2002** | **Johannesburg** – World Summit on Sustainable Development ("Rio+10") | Johannesburg Plan of Implementation |
| **2012** | **Rio+20** – UN Conference on Sustainable Development | Decision to **develop the SDGs** |
| **2015** | **Agenda 2030** adopted, **25 September 2015**, **New York** | 17 SDGs |
!KEY APPSC 2025: Sustainable development was first defined by the **Brundtland Report** – not Paris, Montreal or Kyoto.
!TRAP The concept was **popularised** by the Brundtland Commission Report **1987**, not the Rio Declaration 1992. The Brundtland Report is NOT "The Limits to Growth".

### 3. BRUNDTLAND COMMISSION
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Official name | **World Commission on Environment and Development (WCED)** |
| Chairperson | **Gro Harlem Brundtland**, Prime Minister of **Norway** |
| Report | **"Our Common Future"** (1987) |
| Contribution | First formal definition of sustainable development |
- The Brundtland definition (meet present needs without harming future generations) is tied to **carrying capacity**.
- Finite carrying capacity is the shared concern of "Limits to Growth", "Our Common Future" and UNCED 1992.
!PAIR Limits to Growth | Club of Rome | 1972 || Our Common Future | WCED (Brundtland) | 1987
### 4. THREE PILLARS OF SUSTAINABILITY
| Pillar | Also called |
|---|---|
| **Economic growth** | Economic |
| **Social inclusion** | Social equity |
| **Environmental protection** | Environmental |
- Approach: **balance all three; do not maximise any one**. The centre of the overlap = sustainability.
!TRAP Odd-one-out: "political", "military" or "technological" is not one of the three pillars.

### 6. RELATED CONCEPTS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Carrying capacity** | Maximum population (number, density or biomass) an environment can support **indefinitely without degradation** |
| **Earth Overshoot Day** | Date when humanity's yearly demand on nature **exceeds what Earth can regenerate** that year; by **Global Footprint Network** |
| **Plastic Overshoot Day** | Analogue of Earth Overshoot Day for plastics |
| **Intergenerational equity** | Fairness between present and future generations; core of the Brundtland definition |
### 7. AGENDA 2030 – ROOTS AND PRINCIPLES
#### 7.1 Roots
- Two roots: **Millennium Development Goals (MDGs)** + the **Rio+20 outcome**.
- **MDGs**: **8 goals**, period **2000-2015**.
- Agenda 2030 is grounded in the **Universal Declaration of Human Rights**, **international human rights treaties**, the **Millennium Declaration** and the **2005 World Summit Outcome**.
#### 7.2 Principles
- **Universal**: applies to **all countries** – developed, developing and least developed alike.
- **Leave no one behind**.
- Specific focus group: **ending all discrimination and violence against women and girls** (APPSC-GS 2025 key).
!PAIR MDGs | 8 goals | 2000-2015 || SDGs | 17 goals | 2016-2030

### 9. INDIA'S SDG PROGRESS
#### 9.1 SDSN Sustainable Development Report
| Report | India |
|---|---|
| **SDR 2025** | **99th of 167**, score about **67**; **top 100 for the first time**; Finland, Sweden, Denmark top |
| **SDR 2026** | **94th**, highest ever (up from 99th); **18 places** better than 2015; behind **Bhutan, Maldives, Nepal, Sri Lanka**; lags on **13 of 17** goals; **SDG 2** the main concern |
- **Jun 2026**: SDR 2026 presented at the **3rd Hamburg Sustainability Conference** – India 94th.
- SDR 2026 successes: **rural electrification (SDG 7)**, **digital connectivity (SDG 9)**.
#### 9.2 NITI Aayog SDG India Index 2023-24 (4th edition, Jul 2024)
| Item | Score |
|---|---|
| India | **71** |
| Top: **Kerala and Uttarakhand** | **79** |
| Last: **Bihar** | **57** |
#### 9.3 Voluntary National Reviews (VNR)
- VNRs are **presented to the UN**.
- India's **first VNR: 2017**, covered **7 SDGs: 1, 2, 3, 5, 9, 14, 17**.
- **VNR 2025** prepared by **NITI Aayog**.
- Criticism: India trails smaller South Asian neighbours; off track on 13 goals.
### 10. ANDHRA PRADESH AND THE SDGs
- AP's SDG monitoring and reporting framework is part of the **Real-time Outcome Monitoring System (ROMS)** of the **Planning Department**; indicators drawn from UN recommendations and the department's KPI bank.
- Indicators tracked at **state, district** and **village/ward secretariat** levels.
- **Jul 2026**: AP signed an MoU with **UN-Habitat** (HQ **Nairobi**) for a **Centre of Excellence for Urban Affairs, Amaravati** – aligned with **SDG 11** (sustainable cities and communities).
