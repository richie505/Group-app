## U0130 | AKBAR – CONQUESTS, RAJPUT POLICY, RELIGIOUS POLICY, IBADAT KHANA, DIN-I-ILAHI, SULH-I-KUL
### 1. ACCESSION AND REGENCY
- Born **15 October 1542** at **Amarkot**; crowned **14 February 1556** at **Kalanaur (Gurdaspur, Punjab)**, aged **13** (under 14), after Humayun's death, by **Bairam Khan**.
- **Panipat II – 5 November 1556**: **Hemu** hit in the eye by an arrow and captured; Afghan power ended.
| Phase | Years | Who dominated |
|---|---|---|
| **Bairam Khan's regency** | 1556-60 | Bairam Khan – made **Wazir** and given the title **Khan-i-Khanan** at that time; removed 1560; killed **1561** on the way to Mecca |
| **Petticoat government** | 1560-64 | **Maham Anaga** and her son **Adham Khan** |
- **Adham Khan** was killed by Akbar himself (**1562**) for murdering **Atagh Khan**.
#### 1.1 Other Coronations Often Mixed Up
| Ruler | Fact |
|---|---|
| Aurangzeb | Crowned twice – **21 July 1658 at Delhi** (not Kalanaur) and **5 June 1659** after Khajwa and Deorai |
| Shivaji | **1674, Raigarh**, title **Chhatrapati**; by **Gaga Bhatta (Vishweshwar)** of Varanasi |
| Shivaji II | 1689 – Tarabai placed her four-year-old son on the throne |
| Iltutmish | Crowned at **Delhi** (1211); backed by **Turkish nobles** |
| Rajaraja I / Krishnadeva Raya | 985 / 1509 |
| Ashoka | Worshipped at **Lumbini** (Rummindei) in 20th regnal year; bali tax exempted, land revenue cut to one-eighth |

### 3. RAJPUT POLICY
- Twin policy of **conquest and conciliation**: surrender followed by restoration of **autonomy and honour**; high posts, marriages, toleration.
- **First marriage alliance – 1562**, daughter of **Raja Bharmal (Bihari Mal) of Amber (Kachhwaha)**, on Akbar's pilgrimage to **Ajmer**.
- **Jahangir** (not Akbar) married the sister of **Raja Man Singh**.
- Top Rajput mansabdars: **Man Singh, Bhagwan Das**; also Raja **Todar Mal** (revenue) and Raja **Birbal**.
#### 3.1 Mewar – the Holdout
- **Haldighati – 18 June 1576**: **Maharana Pratap** vs Akbar's army under **Man Singh of Amber**; Mughals won the field.
- **Hakim Khan Sur** led Pratap's army (about 5,000 Afghans).
- Haldighati was part of the **Mewar** campaign, not the Gujarat campaign.
- Mewar submitted only under **Jahangir** – **Amar Singh** treaty **1615**; son **Karan Singh** became a mansabdar.
### 4. RELIGIOUS POLICY – YEAR AND MEASURE
| Year | Measure |
|---|---|
| **1563** | **Pilgrimage tax** abolished |
| **1564** | **Jizya** abolished |
| **1575** | **Ibadat Khana** at **Fatehpur Sikri** – discussions with Ulemas, Sayyids, Shaikhs; opened to all (Hindus, Jains, Christians, Zoroastrians) in **1578** |
| **1579** | **Mahzar** – Akbar supreme arbiter in religious disputes; drafted by **Shaikh Mubarak** |
| **1582** | **Din-i-Ilahi** |
- Guiding principle: **Sulh-i-Kul** – "**peace with all**" (universal harmony), instead of the orthodox Islamic idea.
- Coins with **Rama and Sita**, Devanagari legend **"Rama-Siya"** (UPPSC 2011).
- Acted against **child marriage and Sati**.
- Akbar's reign is known for annexation, provincial and judicial administration – **not** religious intolerance. Called **"an enlightened despot"**.
#### 4.1 Taxes – Who Imposed or Abolished
| Tax / measure | Ruler |
|---|---|
| Jizya, pilgrimage tax abolished | **Akbar** |
| Jizya reimposed | **Aurangzeb, 1679** |
| Pilgrim tax at **Banaras and Allahabad** ended | **Shah Jahan**, after **Kavindracharya** and Banaras pandits asked |
| Grain tax ended | **Sikandar Lodi** (not Akbar) |
| Jizya and cow slaughter ended | **Zain-ul-Abidin** of Kashmir |
| Privileges of Khuts, Muqaddams, Chaudharis ended | **Alauddin Khalji** |
| **Sijda** (prostration) ended | **Shah Jahan** |
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ushr | Land tax on Muslims |
| Zakat | Alms tax on Muslims |
| **Khums** | **One-fifth of war booty** |
| Jizya | Tax on non-Muslims |
| Kharaj, Ushr, Muktai | Taxes on land produce |
| Haqq-e-Sharb | Irrigation tax |
- Aurangzeb's jizya: **12 / 24 / 48 dirhams** for incomes below 200 / 200-10,000 / above 10,000; exempt **women, slaves, children below 14, beggars, unemployed**.
### 5. DIN-I-ILAHI (TAUHID-I-ILAHI), 1582
- Features: **one God**, reverence for **sun and fire**, avoiding meat; piety, prudence, kindness.
- **Never forced** – no compulsory conversion; a voluntary small circle, not a mass religion.
- Members: about **18** – **Abul Fazl** (its "prophet"/chief exponent), Faizi, Shaikh Mubarak; **Birbal the only Hindu**.
- **Ended with Akbar's death**.
- Debate: an ideology based on **Sufi pantheism**, not a religion; **Mohsin Fani** (Jahangir's contemporary) called it a religion in **Dabistan-i-Mazahib**.
#### 5.1 Orthodox Reaction
- Critics of Din-i-Ilahi and Sulh-i-Kul: **Badauni** and **Shaikh Ahmad Sirhindi** (Naqshbandi).
| Order | Founder | Stand / follower |
|---|---|---|
| **Naqshbandi** | **Baha-ud-din Naqshband** (14th c.) | Most orthodox; strict Shariat; came with **Babur**; **Ahmad Sirhindi** – **Mujaddid Alf-i-Sani**, **Wahdat-ul-Shuhud** |
| **Qadiri** | **Abdul Qadir Jilani** | Wahdat-ul-Wujud; **Dara Shikoh** a follower |
| Suhrawardi | Shahabuddin | Brought to **Multan** by **Bahauddin Zakariya** |

### 7. COURT, NAVRATNAS AND TRANSLATION BUREAU
| Figure | Field | Fate |
|---|---|---|
| **Birbal** (Mahesh Das) | Wit, adviser; only Hindu in Din-i-Ilahi | Killed **1586** fighting the Yusufzai |
| **Abul Fazl** | **Akbarnama, Ain-i-Akbari**; Panchatantra as **Ayar-i-Danish** | Murdered **1602** by **Bir (Vir) Singh Bundela** on **Prince Salim's** orders; killer later rewarded by Jahangir |
| **Faizi** (Abul Faizi; Ata Ali Khan) | Poet laureate, Abul Fazl's brother; Persian **Lilavati**; supervised translations | Died 1595 |
| **Todar Mal** | Revenue minister; earlier served **Sher Shah** | Died 1589 |
| **Tansen** (Ramtanu Pandey) | Musician; **Miyan ki Malhar, Miyan ki Todi**; first patron **Raja Ramchandra Singh of Rewa**; Akbar gave the title **"Miyan"** | Title "Tansen" from the Gwalior / Rewa king, not Akbar |
| **Man Singh I** | General (Kachhwaha of Amber) | Led Mughals at Haldighati |
| **Abdur Rahim Khan-i-Khanan** | Son of Bairam Khan; **Hindi dohas**; **Baburnama into Persian** | Literary circle |
- **Sheikh Mubarak** – father of Abul Fazl and Faizi.
- Death order: **Sheikh Mubarak (1593) → Faizi (1595) → Abul Fazl (1602) → Prince Daniyal (1605)** (UPPSC 2024).
- Dhrupad under Akbar: **Tansen and Swami Haridas**; **Vilas Khan** – Jahangir's court.
#### 7.1 Maktab Khana and Translations
- **Maktab Khana** ("House of Translation") – Akbar's translation bureau at **Fatehpur Sikri**, set up around **1574**.
- **Razmnama** = Persian **Mahabharata** (Naqib Khan, Badauni and others; Faizi polished); **Atharvaveda**, **Rajatarangini** and the Baburnama also translated.
- **Nizamuddin Panipati** translated the **Yogavasistha** into Persian.
#### 7.2 Fatehpur Sikri
- **Buland Darwaza** – commemorates the **Gujarat conquest (1572-73)**; **Ibadat Khana** (1575) stood here.
### 8. MANSABDARI AND REVENUE SYSTEMS
#### 8.1 Mansabdari
- Introduced by **Akbar**. **Dagh** (horse branding) system – **1573-74**.
- Dual ranks **Zat** (personal rank) and **Sawar** (cavalry) – about **1595-96**.
- **Du-aspa sih-aspa** (troopers with two or three horses, without raising zat) – introduced by **Jahangir**, first for **Mahabat Khan**.
- Land grants: **Iqta** – Delhi Sultans; **Jagir** – Mughals; **Amaram** – Vijayanagara; **Mokasa** – Marathas.
- **Jagirdari crisis** (Aurangzeb): too many mansabdars for too few jagirs; pressure on peasants.
#### 8.2 Revenue Systems
| System | Features |
|---|---|
| **Zabti / Bandobast / Dahsala** (Ain-i-Dahsala, 1580) | Designed by **Raja Todar Mal** (helped by Khwaja Shah Mansoor, Muzaffar Khan Turbati); **10-year average** produce and prices; state **1/3**, cultivator 2/3; paid in cash |
| **Gaz-i-Ilahi** | **Bamboo** measure, about **82 cm** |
| **Batai / Ghallabakshi** | Crop-sharing |
| **Kankut** | Estimate of the **standing crop** |
| **Nasaq** | Based on the peasant's **past payments** |
| Class | Status |
|---|---|
| **Polaj** | Cultivated every year |
| **Parauti** | Fallow 1-2 years |
| **Chachar** | Fallow 3-4 years |
| **Banjar** | Uncultivated 5+ years |
- Based on **Sher Shah's** survey system.
### 9. MUGHAL EMPERORS – EDUCATION, TITLES, MEMOIRS AND RELIGIOUS POLICY
| Emperor | Learning / title / memoir | Religious policy |
|---|---|---|
| Babur | **Tuzuk-i-Baburi** in **Turki**; title Padshah | Orthodox Sunni; jihad at Khanwa, title **Ghazi** |
| Humayun | Learned (Persian, Arabic, Turkish, astronomy); foundation of Mughal painting | – |
| **Akbar** | **Unlettered** but great patron; **educational reforms** – arithmetic, logic, geometry, astronomy, accountancy, agriculture, ethics, medicine, history | **Sulh-i-Kul** |
| Jahangir | **Nur-ud-din Muhammad Jahangir**; **Tuzuk-i-Jahangiri** in **Persian**; peak of painting | Kept tolerance; **Guru Arjan Dev** executed (1606) for backing rebel **Khusrau** |
| Shah Jahan | Title given to **Prince Khurram** by Jahangir | Slightly more orthodox; destroyed some new temples; kept many Hindu nobles |
| Aurangzeb | **Alamgir**; **"Zinda Pir"**, Shahi Darvesh | **Jizya 1679**; banned **Navroz**; executed Guru Tegh Bahadur; largest empire (Bijapur, Golconda) |
!TRAP Jahangir wrote the **Tuzuk-i-Jahangiri**, not the Tuzuk-i-Baburi.
#### 9.1 Hindu Nobles
- Most Hindu nobles/generals: **Aurangzeb – 31.6%** (mostly Marathas from Deccan wars); Akbar **22.5%**; Shah Jahan **22.4%**.
#### 9.2 Dara Shikoh
- Qadiri; **Majma-ul-Bahrain** (Sufi and Vedanta); **Sirr-i-Akbar** – Persian Upanishads, **1657**.
### 10. SIKH GURUS AND THE MUGHALS
| Guru | Mughal relation |
|---|---|
| **Ram Das** (4th) | Land from Akbar – Amritsar |
| **Arjan Dev** (5th) | Compiled **Adi Granth (1604)**; Harmandir; executed by **Jahangir, 1606** |
| **Hargobind** (6th) | Built **Akal Takht**; imprisoned at **Gwalior**; fought at **Kartarpur** (did not compile Adi Granth) |
| **Tegh Bahadur** (9th) | Born Tyag Mal (1621); founded **Anandpur Sahib**; 116 hymns; beheaded at **Chandni Chowk, Delhi, 24 Nov 1675** by Aurangzeb |
| **Gobind Singh** (10th) | **Khalsa – 13 April 1699** (Baisakhi), Panj Pyaras; 14 wars vs Aurangzeb; **Zafarnama** |
- **Sis Ganj** = execution site; **Rakab Ganj** = cremation site. Shaheedi Divas: **24 November**.
- **Chali Mukte** (40 liberated ones) died at **Muktsar** – remembered as **Maghi**.
- **Banda Singh Bahadur**: took **Sirhind, 1710** (Sirhind Fateh Diwas, not Baisakhi); capital **Lohgarh**; executed 1716.
- AP angle: **Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah** of Golconda kept friendly ties with Akbar; **Bhakta Ramadasu** was nephew of **Madanna and Akkanna**.
