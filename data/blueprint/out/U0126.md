## U0126 | FOLK ARTS, FESTIVALS AND FAIRS OF ANDHRA PRADESH
### 1. FOLK ARTS – BASICS AND FIRSTS
- **Folk arts (Janapada Kalalu)** = arts of the **Janapadam** (village).
- **Biruduraju Ramaraju** wrote the **first book on the theory of folk knowledge** in Telugu.
- **World Folk Day: 22 August**.
- **1857–1956** = "golden era" of dance.
- **Gathasaptasati** records **couple and choir dances** of rural folk.

### 4. KUCHIPUDI – ORIGIN AND DISTINCTION
- Classical dance of AP; named after **Kuchipudi (Kuchelapuram) village, Krishna district**; shaped by **Siddhendra Yogi**; one of India's **eight classical** forms; "global ambassador of Indian culture".
!KEY APPSC GS 2024: "Kuchipudi originated in Krishna district" = correct (2019 option: "Krishna and Guntur").
| Kuchipudi | Bharatanatyam |
|---|---|
| Andhra; **dance-drama** from **Bhagavata Mela** (Brahmin male troupes) | **Tamil Nadu**; **solo** temple dance |
| Dancers **speak dialogue/sing** | No dialogue; geometric solo repertoire |
| **Tarangam** – dancing on the **rim of a brass plate** | — |
- **Lakumadevi**, court dancer of **Kumaragiri Reddy**, was famed for **Bharatanatyam**, not Kuchipudi.
| Classical dance | State |
|---|---|
| Bharatanatyam | Tamil Nadu |
| Kuchipudi | Andhra Pradesh |
| Sattriya | Assam |
| Kathak | Uttar Pradesh |

### 13. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. **Bidri, Cherial, Nirmal** = Telangana, not AP.
2. Kolattam = **Eastern Chalukya** era, not Kakatiya.
3. **Lakumadevi** = Bharatanatyam, not Kuchipudi.
4. **Tarangam** and dialogue = Kuchipudi, not Bharatanatyam.
5. **Atla Taddi** = Ashwayuja, not Chaitra.
6. **Sirimanu** = Paidithalli, Vizianagaram.
7. Dakshina Kasi = **Draksharamam**; Dakshina Kailasam = **Srikalahasti**.
8. Kalamkari: Srikalahasti = pen; Machilipatnam-Pedana = block.
9. Dhimsa origin = **Koraput**; Kolattam = Jadakollu in Karnataka.
10. Narasapur = crochet; Udayagiri = cutlery.
