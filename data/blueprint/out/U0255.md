## U0255 | NEHRU ERA – FOREIGN POLICY, PLANNING, SECULARISM, INSTITUTION BUILDING
### 1. NEHRU – PROFILE
- PM **15 Aug 1947** till death **27 May 1964** (interim government + 1952, 1957, 1962 elections); seat **Phulpur**.
- Books: **The Discovery of India** (written in **Ahmednagar Fort** jail), Glimpses of World History, Letters from a Father to His Daughter.
- Influences: **Fabian socialism**, **British liberalism**, **Gandhi**, **Soviet planning** – not German nationalism.
- Gandhi: decentralised village and cottage-industry economy; Nehru: **heavy industry, science, central planning**.
- Quote: "**Everything else can wait, but not agriculture**" – Nehru.

### 3. FIVE YEAR PLANS
| Plan | Model | Priority / outcome |
|---|---|---|
| First (1951-56) | **Harrod-Domar** | Agriculture, irrigation; growth 3.6% vs target 2.1% |
| Second (1956-61) | **Mahalanobis** | Heavy industry; neglect of agriculture → food shortages, inflation |
| Third (1961-66) | **Sandy-Chakravarty**; "**Gadgil Yojana**" | Food self-sufficiency; upset by **1962 and 1965 wars** and drought |
- **Plan Holiday 1966-69**: three annual plans; rupee devalued.

### 6. CHRONOLOGY AND 1962 WAR
- Order: **Khrushchev-Bulganin visit (1955)** → **Dalai Lama flees (Mar 1959)**; China ended Tibet's autonomy 1959 → **Chou En-lai visit (Apr 1960)** → **Goa – Operation Vijay (Dec 1961)** → **war (Oct-Nov 1962)**.
- Causes: **Aksai Chin road**, **McMahon Line**, Dalai Lama's asylum, **Forward Policy**.
- Aftermath: **Emergency 26 Oct 1962**; **Krishna Menon** resigned; China's unilateral ceasefire **21 Nov 1962**.

### 8. SECULARISM AND HINDU CODE
- Indian secularism: **no State religion**; State neutral but **may reform social practices**.
- **Art. 30** minority educational institutions; **Art. 343** Hindi in Devanagari + English for **15 years**.
- **Ambedkar**: Labour Member (1942-46); first Law Minister (1947); resigned **27 Sep 1951** over the stalled **Hindu Code Bill**.
- **Hindu Marriage Act 1955**; Succession, Minority and Guardianship, Adoptions and Maintenance Acts **1956**.
