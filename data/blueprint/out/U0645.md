## U0645 | WORLD POPULATION GROWTH, DEMOGRAPHIC TRANSITION AND REGIONAL PATTERNS
### 1. GROWTH TERMS AND FORMULAS
#### 1.1 Key Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Natural increase** | Births - deaths |
| **Actual growth** | Natural increase + **net migration** |
| Positive growth | Births > deaths, or permanent net in-migration |
| **RNI (%)** | (CBR - CDR) / 10 (both per 1,000) |
| **Over-urbanisation** | Urban growth faster than urban **jobs and services**; leads to slums |
#### 1.2 Worked RNI Example (APPSC-G1 2018, 2019)
1. CBR = **44** per 1,000.
2. CDR = **11** per 1,000.
3. 44 - 11 = **33 per 1,000 = 3.3%** natural increase a year.
!TRAP RNI is CBR minus CDR divided by 10; the answer is 3.3%, not 33% or 0.33%.

### 7. DEPENDENCY RATIO
- **Dependency ratio** = population aged **0-14 and 65+** / working-age population.
- Lower ratio = potential **demographic dividend**.
- Young dependency (0-14) dominates in Africa; old dependency (65+) in Japan and Europe.
- **Economic Survey 2025-26**: India's dependency ratio will **double**, making **geriatric care** a growing job sector.
### 8. DEMOGRAPHIC DIVIDEND
- Gain when the **working-age share (15-64)** is larger than the dependent share.
- **Window of opportunity**: India about **2005-06 to 2055-56**, longer than any other country.
- India's dividend window closes **after 2041**; **skilling and jobs** are urgent, time-bound priorities.
- World's highest working-age share: **UAE 85.7%**, **Qatar 85.6%** (2021).
