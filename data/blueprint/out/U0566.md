## U0566 | RURAL CREDIT COOPERATIVES IN ANDHRA PRADESH – STRUCTURE, PERFORMANCE AND PROBLEMS
### 1. COOPERATIVE LAWS
| Act / policy | Year | Key provision |
|---|---|---|
| Cooperative Credit Societies Act | 1904 | Started the movement |
| Cooperative Societies Act | **1912** | Allowed **non-credit** societies |
| **AP Cooperative Societies Act** | **1964** | Main law; **AP Cooperative Tribunal** under **Section 75** |
| **AP MACS Act** | **1995** | Self-reliant, member-run societies; **no government share capital**, little State control |
| National Cooperative Policy | **2002** | — |
| **97th Amendment** | **2011** | Art. **19(1)(c)**, Art. **43B** (DPSP), **Part IXB** |
- **AP Cooperative Tribunal**: headed by a **judicial officer**; hears **appeals** under the 1964 Act; **original jurisdiction** under the MACS Act, 1995.
!TRAP Supreme Court struck down Part IXB for **State** cooperatives; it survives only for **multi-State** societies.
### 2. CREDIT STRUCTURE – SHORT-TERM AND LONG-TERM
| Tier | Institution | Number |
|---|---|---|
| State | **APCOB** | 18 branches |
| District | **DCCBs** | 13 DCCBs; 442 branches (2024-25), 436 (2025-26) |
| Village | **PACS** | 2,042 (2024-25); 2,024 (2025-26) |
- Short-term cooperative credit is **three-tiered**.
- **Long-term**: nationally, **ARDBs** give long-term investment credit. AP was the only State to **integrate short- and long-term** credit (from **1986**); primary agricultural development banks abolished in **1987**.
- **APCCADB merged into APCOB** from **30 April 1994** (Single Window Act, 1994); APCOB now refinances DCCBs for long-term credit.
### 3. PERFORMANCE AND NUMBER OF SOCIETIES
| Credit through PACS/DCCBs, 2024-25 | Amount |
|---|---|
| Production credit | Rs 5,933.92 crore |
| Long-term agricultural credit | Rs 1,345.51 crore |
| Tenant farmers | Rs 125.90 crore to 14,425 farmers |
- Number of AP societies **decreased** compared with **2001-02**.
- Being wound up (2024-25): **1,756** Labour Contract Cooperative Societies; **210** Cooperative Joint Farming Societies.
- India: about **8.5 lakh** cooperatives, over **30 crore** members (Jul 2026).
### 4. PACS – FUNCTIONS AND COMPUTERISATION
- Credit: crop and short-term loans; inputs (fertilisers, pesticides, seeds, cattle feed).
- Non-credit: paddy and maize procurement, PDS, rice mills, warehousing, petrol bunks, RO water.
- **Model bye-laws**: **25+** non-credit activities; **NABARD** backs PACS as **Multi-Service Centres**.
- AP computerised (CSS): **2,037** PACS; 1,553 completed pre-migration; DCCB platform B@ncs24. ERP nationally: about **80,000** PACS (Jul 2026).
- **1,246 of 1,490** PACS (assigned to fertilizer companies) became **PM Kisan Samruddhi Kendras**.
- PACS in **207 blocks** eligible for **NCDC** equity grants as FPOs; **APCOB = CBBO**.
### 5. PROBLEMS
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Political capture | Boards shield defaulters → **overdues** |
| Inactivity | About **one-third** of PACS idle (audits in MP, UP) |
| Capacity gaps | No professional management or digital capacity |
| State dominance | Little spontaneity |

### 7. MINISTRY OF COOPERATION INITIATIVES
- Set up **2021**; vision "**Sahkar se Samriddhi**".
- **Grain storage plan** ("world's largest grain storage programme in cooperatives"): piloted **31 May 2023**, storage at PACS via AIF, AMI; by Jul 2026, godowns in **313** AP PACS, **1.80 LMT**.
- Sep 2026: AMI subsidy **25% → 33.33%**; margin money 20% → 10%; with 3% AIF subvention PACS pay about **1%**.
- **Tribhuvan Sahakari University** (IRMA, Anand): India's **first** national cooperative university. **NUCFDC**: urban cooperative banks.
- 2 lakh new societies planned; 24,417 set up (2026). **White Revolution 2.0**: 50% more milk procurement.
