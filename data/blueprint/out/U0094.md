## U0094 | RANI RUDRAMA DEVI AND PRATAPARUDRA; MARCO POLO'S ACCOUNT; FALL TO THE DELHI SULTANATE
### 1. RUDRAMA DEVI – ACCESSION AND FAMILY
- **Kakatiya** dynasty; daughter of **Ganapatideva** and **Kupama Devi**; Ganapati had **no sons**.
- Raised as a son (**putrika**); trained in war, statecraft and administration.
- **1258:** co-regent with her father under the male regnal name **Rudradeva Maharaja**; the **1266 Tripurantakam** inscription still calls her a vassal.
- Fully crowned after Ganapati's death; ruled **1262–1289** (about 26 years).
- **4th sovereign** Kakatiya (after Rudradeva, Mahadeva, Ganapatideva); **first woman ruler of the Andhra region**; **second great woman ruler of medieval India after Razia Sultana**.
- Married Chalukya prince **Veerabhadra** (Nidadavolu).
| Daughter | Married / son |
|---|---|
| **Mummadamma** | Mother of **Prataparudra II** (with Mahadeva) |
| Rudramma | Married Yadava prince **Ellanadeva** |
- Royal styles: Maharaja, Parameshwara, Tirthakrit.
### 2. RUDRAMA'S WARS, REVOLTS AND DEATH
| Opponent | Episode |
|---|---|
| Half-brothers **Harihara and Murari** | Internal revolt; crushed with **Prasadaditya Kayastha**, Gona Gannareddy, Jannigadeva, Tripurari, Malyala Gundiya, Induluri Annayya, **Viriyala Suri** |
| Kalinga – **Ganga Narasimhadeva** | Occupied **Vengi (1262)**; regained **1278** when **Poti Nayaka and Prolinayaka** beat **Virabhanudeva** |
| **Yadava Mahadeva** | Invaded; **repulsed** → title **Rayagajakesari** |
| Pandyas | After **Muttukur (1263)** Nellore passed to them |
| **Kayastha Ambadeva** | Made head of **Kadapa** in **1272**; revolted with Pandya support; his victories in the **Tripurantakam inscription** |
- **Death, 1289:** killed fighting rebel **Ambadeva**, with general **Mallikarjuna Nayaka**.
- Recorded in the **Chandupatla inscription** (Nalgonda district), set up by her servant **Puvvula Mummadi**.
!TRAP Tripurantakam = Ambadeva's victories; Chandupatla = Rudrama's death.

### 4. MARCO POLO'S ACCOUNT
- **Venetian** traveller; work **Il Milione / The Travels**; travelled 1271–1295.
- Visited in **Rudramadevi's reign** (APPSC G2 2011); landed at **Motupalli (Mutfili)** port.
- Reached the Coromandel coast in **1292**, at the very end of her reign or just after.
| Theme | Observation |
|---|---|
| Trade | Flourishing sea trade; merchants **not heavily taxed** |
| Wealth | **Diamond** mines of the **Golconda** region; **fine textiles** |
| Climate, food | Hot climate (loincloths); **rice** the only local grain |
### 5. PRATAPARUDRA II – SUCCESSION, CAMPAIGNS AND REFORMS
- **Grandson** of Rudrama through her daughter **Mummadamma**; trained by her; crowned **1289**; ruled **1289–1323**; **last Kakatiya**.
- Early revolts: Kayastha, Velama, Reddy factions; Ambadeva's son **Tripurari II**.
| Campaign | Year | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Ambadeva at Tripurantakam** | **1291** | Commanders **Kolanu Munumagammayya, Induluri Annayya** won; principality annexed |
| **Ranganatha of Nellore** (Pandya ally) | 1290s | Replaced by **Manuma Gandapalu** |
| Yadava borders | 1290s | **Gonavithala** pushed towards Devagiri |
#### 5.1 Nayankara System
- Kingdom divided into **77 Nayankaras** held by military officers (**Nayakas**).
- Each Nayaka held a **fort**, kept **troops** and paid a **fixed tribute**; about **one-fourth** of the land was under Nayakas.
- Continued as Vijayanagara's **Amara Nayankara** → later **Poligars**.
### 6. DELHI SULTANATE INVASIONS OF WARANGAL
| No. | Year | Sultan / commander | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **1303** | Alauddin / **Malik Fakhruddin Juna** | **Repulsed at Upparapalli** by **Pothuganti Aili and Recherla Venna** – **first attack on Orugallu** (APPSC G2 2012) |
| 2 | **1309–10** | Alauddin / **Malik Kafur** | Siege; Prataparudra **submits, pays tribute**, accepts Delhi overlordship |
| 3 | **1318** | Mubarak Khalji / **Khusrau Khan** | Defeated again; surrendered over 100 elephants and 12,000 horses; renewed tribute |
| 4 | **1321–22** | Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq / **Ulugh Khan (Juna Khan)** | False rumour of the Sultan's death; army chased back to Devagiri |
| 5 | **1323** | Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq / **Ulugh Khan** | **5-month siege**; **Bobbareddi** withdrew his forces; Warangal fell |
- 1294–96: Alauddin's first Deccan raid hit the **Yadavas**, not Andhra.
- **1311:** Prataparudra's forces under **Muppidi Nayaka** joined Kafur's march on **Madurai**.
- **1316:** after Alauddin's death he **stopped tribute**.
- Order: Fakhruddin Juna → Malik Kafur → Khusrau Khan → Juna Khan (failed) → Juna Khan (final).
### 7. FALL OF WARANGAL (1323)
- Captor: **Ulugh Khan** (Juna Khan, later **Muhammad bin Tughlaq**) (APPSC G2 2019) – not Kafur, not Alauddin.
- Prataparudra **drowned himself in the Narmada** on the way to Delhi (**Vilasa copper plate** of **Musunuri Prolaya Nayaka**; one account says the Kaveri).
- Orugallu renamed **Sultanpur**; Andhra placed under **Malik Burhanuddin** (vizier of Devagiri); **Malik Maqbul** (converted Brahmin Jagannatha Pandit) at Warangal.
- **Causes:** southern disunity, weak cavalry, betrayal by chiefs, exhaustion from raids – **not** a European sea blockade.
- Survivors **Prolaya Nayaka, Racherla Singama, Prolaya Vema** led the liberation → **Musunuri** and **Reddi** states.
