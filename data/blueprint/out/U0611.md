## U0611 | OCEANS – RELIEF OF THE OCEAN FLOOR, CONTINENTAL SHELF, RIDGES, TRENCHES
### 1. OCEANS AND EARTH'S WATER
- Size order: **Pacific > Atlantic > Indian > Southern > Arctic**; **Arctic = smallest** (**APPSC GS 2018**).
- **Pacific**: largest, deepest; larger than all land combined.
- Oceans cover about **70–71%** of Earth's surface (**APPSC GS 2019**) and hold about **97%** of its water (**APPSC GS 2025**); ice ~2%; usable fresh water ~1%. **Groundwater** stores far more fresh water than lakes and rivers.
### 2. DEPTH MAPPING AND OCEAN ZONES
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Bathymetry** | Measuring ocean depth and mapping sea-floor relief (**APPSC GS 2018**); narrower than oceanography |
| **Fathom** | Unit of depth (**APPSC GS 2019**) = **6 feet** (about 1.83 m) |
| **Isobath** | Line joining points of **equal depth** |
| **Hypsometric curve** | Share of Earth's surface at each height and depth; shows ocean bottom topography |
| **Echo sounding / SONAR** | **Reflection of ultrasonic waves**; depth = speed of sound × half the echo time |
- **SONAR** uses: **fish location** (fishing), **submarine detection** (military), **depth measurement**, seabed mapping.
| Zone | Extent |
|---|---|
| Neritic | Water over the continental shelf |
| Pelagic | Open-ocean water beyond the shelf |
| **Mesopelagic ("twilight zone")** | **200–1,000 m**; drives the biological carbon pump |
| Benthic / hadal | Sea floor / trenches |
### 3. OCEAN BOTTOM RELIEF
| Division | Depth | Key feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Continental shelf** | To about **200 m** | "Submerged prolongation of land"; richest fishing; **most oil deposits** |
| **Continental slope** | 200–3,000 m | **Canyons and trenches** (**APPSC G2 2023**) |
| Continental rise | Foot of slope | Sediment apron |
| **Deep sea (abyssal) plain** | **3,000–6,000 m** | **Most extensive** (over 76%); flattest, smoothest surface; **polymetallic nodules** |
| **Oceanic deeps / trenches** | Deepest | **Volcanoes and earthquakes** (**APPSC G2 2023**); subduction zones; mostly **Pacific** |
- Depth order: deeps > deep-sea plains > slopes > shelves.
- Deep sea below 200 m = **oldest, largest biome**.
!TRAP Canyons and trenches = slope; volcanoes and earthquakes = deeps; most extensive = deep-sea plain.
### 4. MINOR RELIEF FEATURES
| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Seamount** | Undersea volcanic peak that **does not reach the surface**; cobalt-rich crusts |
| **Guyot** | **Flat-topped** seamount, planed by waves, then sunk |
| **Atoll** | **Ring- or horseshoe-shaped** coral reef round a central **lagoon** (Lakshadweep, Maldives; **Addu Atoll** = southernmost Maldives) |
| **Submarine canyon** | Deep, steep-sided valley across shelf and slope, often off river mouths: Hudson Canyon; **Mahadevan Canyon off Visakhapatnam** |
- **Deep Ocean Mission** surveys: about **25 seamounts** in the Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal; 2 active + 2 inactive hydrothermal vents.
- **Tsunamis** are most common in the Pacific.

### 6. CONTINENTAL SHELF OF INDIA
| Coast | Shelf |
|---|---|
| **West** | **Wide**; broadest off **Gujarat** and part of Maharashtra |
| **East** | **Narrow** |
- **Bombay (Mumbai) High**: oilfield on the wide western shelf, **160 km** off Mumbai (not 60); run by **ONGC**; found by a Russian-Indian team.
- **40th Amendment Act, 1976**: minerals of the shelf and EEZ vest in the **Union**.
- **2025**: India widened its shelf claim by ~**10,000 sq km** in the central Arabian Sea before the UN **CLCS** (data: **NCPOR Goa**).
- **AP**: **1,053 km** coast (3rd longest) fronts the narrow east-coast shelf; **Visakhapatnam** = only major port.
### 7. OCEAN DEPOSITS
- **Terrigenous**: land gravel, sand, silt, mud on **shelves and slopes**; **finer away from shore**. Coastal gravel = terrigenous.
- **Pelagic**: deep-sea oozes and red clay on deep plains and deeps.
| Pelagic type | Make-up |
|---|---|
| **Calcareous ooze** | Lime shells (**globigerina, pteropods**) |
| **Siliceous ooze** | Silica skeletons (**diatoms, radiolarians**); colder, deeper water |
| **Red clay** | Fine inorganic clay + volcanic dust; **most widespread**, largest in deep **Pacific**; not from lime shells |
!TRAP **APPSC G1 2024**: "Coarseness increases seaward" is WRONG; deposits get finer seaward.
### 8. CORAL REEFS
- Built of **calcium carbonate** from coral **polyps**; polyps live with algae (**zooxanthellae**).
- Need warm (rarely below ~18–20°C), **shallow, clear, sunlit, saline** water free of silt.
| Type | Form |
|---|---|
| **Fringing** | Platform close to the shore |
| **Barrier** | Farther out, with a deep lagoon/channel between reef and land |
| **Atoll** | Ring reef round a central lagoon, no central island |
- **Darwin (1842)** subsidence theory: fringing → barrier → atoll as land sinks.
- **India**: **Gulf of Kachchh** (least diverse: turbidity), **Gulf of Mannar**, **Palk Bay** (fringing), **Andaman & Nicobar** (most extensive, richest), **Lakshadweep** (atolls).
### 9. TEMPERATURE, DENSITY AND SALINITY
- **Horizontal**: falls equator to poles; **NH oceans warmer** (more land). **Tropics: western side warmer** (trades pile warm water west, warm currents); **temperate: eastern side warmer** (westerlies, North Atlantic Drift). Annual range smaller in the **Pacific** than the Atlantic.
- **Vertical**: warm surface layer (to ~**500 m**; 20–25°C in tropics) → **thermocline** (~500–1,000 m; rapid fall) → cold deep layer (0–4°C).
- **Density** depends on temperature and salinity: colder and saltier = denser; cold water sinks to fill the deep.
- Oceans absorb **over 90%** of excess heat; record heat content 2025.
- **Salinity**: grams of salt per 1,000 g of water; ocean ~**35 ppt**; brackish **5–35 ppt**. Raised by **evaporation, freezing**; lowered by **rain, rivers, ice melt**. Highest in the **subtropics (20–30°)**; lower at the rainy equator.
| Water body | Salinity (‰) | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Lake Van** (Turkey) | **330** | Closed soda lake |
| **Dead Sea** | **238** | Closed, intense evaporation |
| **Great Salt Lake** (Utah) | **220** | Closed basin |
| Red Sea | ~41 | High evaporation |
| Black Sea | 17–18 | – |
| **Baltic** | Lowest (under ~10) | Many rivers, cold |
!KEY **APPSC G2 2023**: Lake Van > Dead Sea > Great Salt Lake.
- **Deep Ocean Mission**: approved **June 2021**, **₹4,077 crore**, **MoES**; **MATSYA-6000** passed tests; mining machine **Varaha** trialled at **5,270 m**.
