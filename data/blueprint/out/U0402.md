## U0402 | WOMEN'S RIGHTS – CONSTITUTIONAL PROVISIONS AND THE LEGAL FRAMEWORK
### 1. STATUS OF WOMEN THROUGH HISTORY
- **Vedic/early:** honourable place, **'Ardhangini'** (better half); role in sacred rites.
- **Later Vedic and medieval:** decline; confined to home, **purdah** (not early Vedic), social evils.
| Custom | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Niyoga** | Childless widow/wife has a son by the **husband's brother or kinsman** (not a ban on widow remarriage) |
| **Sati** | Widow immolation; **Bengal Sati Regulation 1829**; **Commission of Sati (Prevention) Act 1987** |
| **Devadasi** | Girls dedicated to temples; **Madras Act 1947**, **AP Devadasis (Prohibition of Dedication) Act 1988** |
- Colonial laws: **Female Infanticide Prevention Act 1870** (APPSC 2018) → Sati 1829 → **Widow Remarriage Act 1856** → **Sarda Act 1929** → Hindu Marriage Act 1955.

### 3. WOMEN'S MOVEMENT
| Organisation | Year | Founders / focus |
|---|---|---|
| Bharat Stree Mahamandal | **1910** | First women's organisation |
| **Women's Indian Association (WIA)** | **1917** | **Annie Besant, Margaret Cousins**: votes, seats in councils |
| **NCWI** | **1925** | Linked to International Council of Women |
| **AIWC** | **1927** | **Margaret Cousins**: education, social upliftment, legal rights |
| **Andhra Mahila Sabha** | **1937** | **Durgabai Deshmukh** (also first chair, **Central Social Welfare Board, 1953**) |
- 19th century: sati, widow remarriage, education; early 20th: **political rights**.
- **Committee on the Status of Women in India (1971)**, chair **Phulrenu Guha**, Member-Secretary **Vina Mazumdar** → **'Towards Equality' (1974)**: women's declining status; sparked the **modern movement**.
- Post-1970s: **Neera Desai** (first Women's Studies Centre, **SNDT**); Vina Mazumdar (**CWDS**); **Madhu Kishwar** (**Manushi**).
- Later committees: **Pam Rajput** (High-Level Committee, 2012); **Justice J.S. Verma** (2013, sexual violence laws).
- Critique: neglects lower-strata women (UPSC Mains 2018); "proxy" women panchayat heads.
#### 3.1 Andhra Movements
- **Anti-Arrack Movement:** **1992**, **Dubagunta (Nellore)**, literacy-campaign women → arrack ban **1993** → prohibition **1995**.
- **Chalam** (Gudipati Venkata Chalam): novel ***Stree***, women's personal and sexual freedom.
!TRAP 1917-1925-1927 = WIA-NCWI-AIWC; Margaret Cousins in WIA and AIWC.

### 5. GENDER CONCEPTS AND INDICES
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Patriarchy** | Men hold main power and status in family and society |
| **Gender discrimination** | Unequal treatment of men and women by society (APPSC 2024) |
| **Gender mainstreaming** | Gender perspective in all policies, laws, institutions; adopted at **Beijing 1995** |
| **Gender audit** | Systematic check of policies, programmes, institutions **and budgets** (not budgets alone): gender budgeting, equal opportunity, **workplace safety**, decision-making |
| Index | Measures |
|---|---|
| **GDI** | Female-to-male ratio: health, education, command over resources |
| **GII** | **0 (equality) to 1 (worst)** |
| **Global Gender Gap Index (WEF)** | Economic participation, education, health and survival, political empowerment (not military spending) |
- **Global Gender Gap Index 2025:** India **131st of 148** (129th in 2024); Iceland first.
- Women, Peace and Security Index 2023: India **131/177**.
#### 5.1 Sex Ratio and Work
- NFHS-4 **991** → NFHS-5 **1020**. **SRB 904 (2017-19) → 917 (2021-23)** (Women and Men in India 2025).
- Most male-skewed: **Haryana, Punjab**; best: **Kerala, North-East**.
- Causes: **son preference, female foeticide/infanticide, dowry**, neglect of girls (not equal inheritance or schooling).
- Women's LFPR **37% (2022-23)** vs 23.3% (2017-18). Crimes against women 2024: **4,41,534**; spousal violence 32% (NFHS-5).
!TRAP The 0-1 scale belongs to **GII**, not GDI.

#### 6.1 106th Amendment (Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam, 2023)
- **One-third (33%)** seats in **Lok Sabha, State Assemblies, Delhi Assembly**; waits for **Census and delimitation**.
- In effect in **J&K and Puducherry** Assemblies from **17 Apr 2026**.
- **17 Apr 2026:** the **Constitution (131st) Amendment Bill, 2026** (to redistribute Lok Sabha seats and speed up the quota) was **defeated**.
#### 6.2 Local Bodies and AP
- **73rd/74th** Amendments: first women's reservation in local bodies; ~**46%** of PRI representatives are women (26 Jan 2026).
- **AP:** **50%** in panchayats and municipalities; **50% of nominated posts** and work contracts (14 Apr 2026); **33 1/3% horizontal** reservation in direct recruitment.
### 7. LAWS ON WOMEN – CHRONOLOGY
| Act | Year | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| Indian Succession Act | 1925 | Christians and others |
| **ITPA** | 1956 (amended 1986) | Penalises brothels, soliciting, living on earnings, **not prostitution itself** |
| **Dowry Prohibition** | **1961** (not 1976) | Min **5 years** + ₹15,000 or dowry value |
| **Indecent Representation of Women** | **1986** | Ads, publications, paintings |
| **Commission of Sati (Prevention)** | **1987** | Sati and its glorification |
| **PCPNDT** | 1994; in force **1 Jan 1996** | Bans prenatal **sex determination** |
| **Domestic Violence** | 2005; in force **26 Oct 2006** | Civil remedy; **Right to Residence (Sec. 17)**; Protection Officers; 60 days |
| **PCMA** | 2006; in force **1 Nov 2007** | Replaced Sarda Act; **18 F / 21 M**; child marriage **voidable** |
| **POSH** | **2013** | **Internal Complaints Committee** at **10+** employees; **Local Committee** at district |
| Criminal Law (Amendment) | 2013 | After Nirbhaya |
- **Marital rape exception (BNS s.63)** under challenge in SC (10 Sep 2026).
!TRAP 1986 = Indecent Representation; 1987 = Sati Act.
### 8. PERSONAL LAW REFORMS
| Law | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Special Marriage Act 1954** | Civil, secular marriage irrespective of religion, after **public notice** |
| **Hindu Marriage Act 1955** | Monogamy, divorce grounds, restitution of conjugal rights |
| **Hindu Succession Act 1956** | Equal inheritance; ended female heir's **limited estate**; **s.2(2)** excludes notified **STs** |
| **HSA (Amendment) 2005** | Daughters = **equal coparceners** |
| **AP HSA Amendment** | **24 Sept 1985** under **NTR**: unmarried daughters coparceners, before 2005 |
| **Muslim Women (Divorce) Act 1986** | After Shah Bano |
| **Muslim Women (Marriage) Act 2019** | Instant **talaq-e-biddat void, illegal, criminal**; up to **3 years**; cognisable; passed 30 July 2019, retrospective from 19 Sept 2018 |
- Maintenance: **Sec. 125 CrPC → Sec. 144 BNSS**.
- **SC (8 Oct 2025):** upheld HSA exclusion of STs.

| Case | Ruling |

- **Sabarimala (2018):** bar on women aged **10-50** struck down **4:1**; **Justice Indu Malhotra** dissented; larger-bench review pending.
- **Haji Ali (Bombay HC, 2016):** entry to inner sanctum allowed; petition by **Bharatiya Muslim Mahila Andolan** (Zakia Soman, Noorjehan Safia Niaz).

### 10. REPRODUCTIVE RIGHTS LAWS
| Law | Permitted | Prohibited / limit |
|---|---|---|
| **MTP 1971 (in force 1 Apr 1972); Amendment 2021** | **24 weeks** for special categories; beyond 24 only for foetal abnormality (**State Medical Board**) | **Disclosing woman's identity** |
| **Surrogacy (Regulation) Act 2021** | **Altruistic** only (medical costs, insurance); essentiality and eligibility certificates | **Commercial** surrogacy; **sex selection**; surrogate **only once** |
| **ART (Regulation) Act 2021** | Registered clinics/banks; national registry | **Sex-selective ART**; sale of gametes |
| **PCPNDT 1994** | Tests for genetic abnormality | Sex determination |
- Abortion of a surrogate foetus: allowed with surrogate's **written consent** + MTP approval.
!TRAP APPSC 2018: "prohibiting abortion of the surrogate child" was **NOT** in the 2019 Bill.
### 11. WORK, PAY AND MATERNITY
- **Equal Remuneration Act 1976** (Art. 39(d)): first equal pay law. **Code on Wages 2019** subsumes it + **Minimum Wages 1948**, Payment of Wages 1936, Bonus 1965 (**not** Maternity Benefit); floor wage; covers transgender persons; Central Rules **8 May 2026**.
- **OSH Code 2020:** women may work **before 6 a.m. / after 7 p.m.** with consent and safety.
| Item | 1961 Act | 2017 Amendment |
|---|---|---|
| Leave | **12 weeks**, 10+ employees | **26 weeks** for fewer than two children (max **8** before birth) |
| Third child | – | 12 weeks |
| Adopting (child under 3 months) / commissioning | – | **12 weeks** from hand-over |
| Crèche | – | **50+** employees, **4 visits** a day |
| Work from home | – | By mutual agreement |
!TRAP UPSC 2019: "3 months pre + 3 months post-delivery" is wrong.
### 12. INSTITUTIONS, POLICIES AND INTERNATIONAL COMMITMENTS
- **NCW:** **Act 1990**, set up **Jan 1992**; Chairperson + **5 members** (at least one SC, one ST) + Member-Secretary; reviews laws, complaints, suo motu.
- **AP Women's Commission:** **A.P. Women's Commission Act 1998** (amended 2023); chair **Rayapati Sailaja** (NCW list, Dec 2025).
- **Family Courts Act 1984:** **953** courts in 29 States/UTs (31 Jul 2025). **First women's court:** **West Bengal (Malda, 2013)**.
- **NPEW 2001** (2001 = **Year of Women's Empowerment**): advancement, equal access to health/education/employment, ending violence, mainstreaming (**not** commercial surrogacy). **8th Plan**: development → empowerment.
- **Draft National Policy 2016:** health incl. **food security and nutrition**, education, economy, governance, violence, environment.
- Mission for Empowerment of Women **2010** (not 2015); **Beti Bachao Beti Padhao 2015**.
