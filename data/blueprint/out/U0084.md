## U0084 | MINOR DYNASTIES OF ANDHRADESA – CHALUKYA-CHOLAS, KONA HAIHAYAS, MATSYAS, PUSAPATI AND OTHERS
### 1. KULOTTUNGA I AND THE CHALUKYA-CHOLA MERGER
- **Kulottunga I (Rajendra Chalukya)** = **first Chalukya-Chola king** (APPSC 2011).
- Married **Madhurantaki**, daughter of the Chola **Rajendra**.
- Took the **Chola throne in 1070**; took **Vengi in 1075** after the death of **Vijayaditya VII**, the last Eastern Chalukya.
- Sent **72 merchants to China**; built **Kulottunga Cholapuram** (Visakhapatnam).
- Conquered **Kalinga by sea** → recorded in **Kalingattuparani** by **Jayangondar**.
- After him, Andhra became a web of **nadus** under vassals who accepted **Velanadu**, later **Kakatiya**, overlordship.
#### 1.1 Viceroys of Vengi and Andhra Feudatories
- Chalukya-Chola **viceroys** of Vengi took the Eastern Chalukya titles **Sarvalokasraya, Sri Vishnuvardhana**.
- Crowning place: **Jananathapura (Draksharama)** – APPSC 2025 key names **Vikrama Chola** as the viceroy crowned there.
- Last of the line: **Parantaka Chola**.
- **Vikrama Chola** (son of Kulottunga I) served as viceroy of Vengi after **Rajaraja Chodaganga**; recalled in **1118** to be his father's **co-regent**.
- **Velanati Gonka I** = **Samasta Senadhipati of Vengi** (fought off Kalyani Chalukyas and Kalingas).
- **Mandaleswaras raised by Kulottunga I**: Velanati, Nellore, Telugu, Konidena and Pottapi Chodas; Giri Paschima Durjayas; Natavadis; **Chagis**; Kona Haihayas.
!TRAP Viceroy crowning place = **Draksharama (Jananathapura)**, not Rajahmundry.

- **Matsyas of Oddadi – legend**: the founder claimed descent from a **fish** (*matsya*); the founder **Satyamartanda** married **Prabhavati**, daughter of **Jayatsena, king of Utkala (Odisha)**, who made him ruler of the **Oddadi country**.
- Oddadi lies about **16 miles from Anakapalle** (old Vizagapatam district); rule c. **1200–1470 CE**; emblem **double fish**.
- Line ended with **Singaraja (1471)**; replaced by the **Pusapati** and **Madgole** zamindaris under the **Gajapatis**; the **Madgole** zamindars claim Matsya descent.
!TRAP Betaraju I is named as founder of both the **Kota** line (Dhanyakatakam) and the **Palnadu Haihaya** line (Gurajala) – match by **seat**.
### 3. VELANATI CHODAS
- **Not related to the Telugu Chodas**; ancestors = **Kirtipura lords of Madhyadesha**.
- Named sons after their Chalukya-Chola overlords – **Rajendra Choda** was the first.
- **Pithapuram inscription** of Velanati **Prithvisvara** names **Kolleru Lake** (APPSC 2017).
- End: Kakatiya **Ganapatideva** killed its last member (**Mummadi Rajendra Choda**; Nellore Choda Tikkana helped Ganapati kill Prithvisvara).
### 4. KOTA CHIEFS (AND CHAGIS)
| Ruler | Point |
|---|---|
| **Betaraju I** | Founder; seat **Dhanyakatakam**, Kammanadu |
| **Bhimaraju I (1108–1127)** | Backed the Chalukya-Cholas; **Kalinga campaigns** with the Velanati Chodas |
| **Bhimaraju II** | Palnadu war era (1182); absorbed Kondapadamati; title **Dannala Kota Padaveshtita Kadana** |
- Territory includes **Amaravati (Dhanyakatakam)** and **Tripurantakam**.
- A Kota chief (Bhimaraju II) married **Ganapamba**, daughter of Kakatiya **Ganapatideva**.
- Overlords in order: **Chalukya-Cholas → Velanati Chodas → Kakatiyas**.
- **Chagis**: Mandaleswaras under Kulottunga I; ruled **Natavadi and Vijayavati** vishayas from **Gudimetta** (also **Vinukonda**).
- Chagi overlords in turn: **Chalukya-Cholas → Kakatiyas → Gajapatis**.
- Kakatiya **Ganapatideva's sister Melambika** married a son of Natavadi chief **Chagi Buddharaja**.
### 5. PALNADU WAR (PALNATI YUDDHAM), 1182
- Fought at **Karempudi, 1182 CE**.
- Rival factions: all sons of Palnadu Haihaya **Alugaraju (Anugaraju)**.
- **Kota Bhimaraju II** joined **Kakatiya Rudradeva** to back **Nalagamaraju**.
- Epic: **Srinatha's Palnati Veera Charitra**; also **Palnati Veerula Katha**.
| Person | Role |
|---|---|
| **Nalagamaraju** | Son of **Mailama Devi**, daughter of Velanati **Gonka II** |
| **Malideva** and brothers | Half-brothers (sons of **Veeravijjaladevi**) – rival faction |
| **Nagamma (Nayakuralu)** | Nalagama's minister; **Shaivite**; built **Veerabhadra Swamy temple, Gurajala**; opposed Brahmanaidu's reforms |
| **Brahmanaidu** | Minister and **leader of the Malideva faction**; reformer (**interdining, Chapakudu**) |
| **Balachandra** | Hero who died in the war (named as the cause in one key) |
- The war arose from the rivalry of **Nayakuralu Nagamma and Brahmanaidu**.
- APPSC GS 2018 key: **Velamas**, "intruders" into **Reddi-dominated Palnadu**, were driven out in the **Battle of Karempudi**.
!PAIR Nagamma | Nalagamaraju side | Opposed reforms || Brahmanaidu | Malideva side | Interdining reformer
### 6. PUSAPATIS AND VATSAVAYIS
- **Pusapatis** – seat **Vizianagaram**; ruler **Gajapati Vijayarama** (APPSC GS 2022).
- **Battle of Padmanabham (10 July 1794)**: Raja **Vijayarama Raju** was killed fighting the **East India Company**; the state became a **tributary zamindari**.
- **Permanent Settlement (1802)**: Vizianagaram settled as a **zamindari estate** under the Madras Presidency.
- **Ananda Gajapati Raju (1850–1897)**: later Maharaja, noted patron of learning and culture.
- **Vatsavayis** – seat **Peddapuram** (APPSC GS 2022).
