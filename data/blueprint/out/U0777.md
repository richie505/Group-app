## U0777 | CONSERVATION – IN-SITU (PROTECTED AREAS, BIOSPHERE RESERVES) AND EX-SITU (ZOOS, GENE BANKS, SEED BANKS)
### 1. IN-SITU vs EX-SITU
| Basis | In-situ | Ex-situ |
|---|---|---|
| Where | Within natural habitat | Outside, in a controlled environment |
| Examples | NP, sanctuary, biosphere reserve, reserved/protected forest, nature reserve, **sacred grove**, tiger reserve, community reserve, gene sanctuary, OECM | **Botanical garden**, zoo, safari park, seed/gene bank, **cryobank**, pollen bank, field gene bank, tissue culture, captive breeding |
| Saves | **Whole ecosystem** | Species or its genes only |
- **In-situ is the most effective**: species keep interacting with their environment.
- Ex-situ values: education, research, reintroduction; limits: needs space and money, cannot save the ecosystem.
- **Captive breeding** for species whose habitat is permanently lost, e.g. **Gangetic gharials** bred and released; 10 captive-bred **Asian Giant Tortoises** released in **Ntangki NP (Nagaland)**, Dec 2022.
- **Meristem culture** = virus-free propagation.
!TRAP PYQ: "Not an in-situ site" = **Botanical Garden**.
- "Biological diversity" first used by **Raymond F. Dasmann** (1968).
### 2. PROTECTED AREA CATEGORIES (WPA 1972)
| Category | Legal basis / feature |
|---|---|
| **National Park** | WPA 1972; no rights, **no grazing**, no biomass collection; boundary altered only by **State Legislature resolution on NBWL recommendation** |
| **Wildlife Sanctuary** | WPA 1972, notified by State (not UNESCO); some activity; grazing regulated by **Chief Wildlife Warden** |
| **Conservation Reserve** | 2002 amendment; **government land**, usually linking PAs |
| **Community Reserve** | 2002 amendment, **Sec 36C**; **private/community land** when a community volunteers; declared by **State Government** for fauna, flora, traditional and cultural values |
- Four PA categories only; a **Biosphere Reserve is NOT one**. Tiger reserves are notified under the NTCA chapter.
- Land already an NP, sanctuary or conservation reserve cannot become a community reserve. First community reserve: **Keshopur-Chhamb (Punjab**, not Rajasthan). WB plans the first community reserve for the **Gangetic dolphin**.
!TRAP Amchang and Bhagwan Mahavir are notified as wildlife sanctuaries, not community reserves.
- **Network (2026):** **1,134 PAs** (981 in 2020): 109 NPs, 573 WLS, 127 conservation reserves, 75 community reserves; 58 TRs; only **5.32%** of India's area (Aug 2026).
#### 2.1 BR vs NP vs Sanctuary
| Feature | Biosphere reserve | National park | Sanctuary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legal basis | UNESCO MAB recognition; no change in land ownership | WPA 1972 | WPA 1972 |
| Human use | Settlements in transition zone | None | Some rights |
| Boundaries | Core-buffer-transition | Clearly demarcated | Not strictly specified |
- Projects affecting wildlife need clearance from the **Standing Committee of NBWL**.
### 3. ECO-SENSITIVE ZONES
- **"Shock absorber"** around an NP or sanctuary: transition from high protection to regulated development.
- Notified by **MoEF&CC** under the **Environment (Protection) Act, 1986** (Sec 3 with Rule 5), **NOT WPA 1972** (APPSC 2019 trap).
- **MoEF 2011 guidelines:** up to **10 km** around a PA. **Supreme Court:** no mining within **1 km** of any NP/WLS (larger notified ESZ prevails); mining banned inside PAs; mining within 10 km of wetland conservation reserves needs wildlife clearance (Aug 2026).
| Prohibited | Regulated | Permitted |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial mining, quarrying, polluting industries, major hydro projects | Tree felling, hotels and resorts, road widening | Ongoing agriculture, organic farming, rainwater harvesting |
### 4. GLOBAL COVERAGE, 30x30 AND OECMs
- **2014:** PAs covered **15.2% of land** and **8.4% of coastal-marine** areas (APPSC 2018).
- **Apr 2026:** nearly **10%** of ocean protected; only ~3% fully/highly protected; **1.66%** of high seas.
- **WDPA:** run by **UNEP-WCMC (Cambridge)** with IUCN; tracks 30x30.
- **30x30 = Target 3** of **Kunming-Montreal GBF** (CBD COP-15, 2022): protect 30% of land and oceans by 2030.
!TRAP APPSC G1 2024: 30x30 is NOT a renewable-energy target.
- **OECM:** area outside the PA network giving long-term in-situ conservation; recognised by **outcomes**; no change in ownership or legal authority (sacred groves, greenbelts, wetlands). India's **first OECM: Aravalli Biodiversity Park, Gurugram (2022)**, a restored mining site. APSBB plans OECMs to meet 30x30 (Jun 2026).
### 5. BIOSPHERE RESERVES AND MAB
- **UNESCO MAB Programme, 1971** (not IUCN). **IN-MAB Committee (MoEF&CC)** recommends sites; States prepare management action plans.
- Criteria: protected core with viable populations of all trophic levels; management involving local communities (no land transfer to UNESCO). **Seville Strategy (1995)** added social, cultural, economic needs.
- **Funding (CSS):** **60:40**; **90:10** for North-Eastern and three Himalayan States.
#### 5.1 Zonation
| Zone | Activities |
|---|---|
| **Core** | Legally protected (NP/WLS); no human activity |
| **Buffer** | Surrounds core; regulated: research, education, training, limited recreation |
| **Transition** | Outer edge: settlements, farming, forestry, tourism |

### 7. MARINE CONSERVATION
- India has **106 Important Coastal and Marine Biodiversity Areas** (APPSC 2018); **Naupada ICMBA**, Srikakulam (AP).
- MPAs = marine NPs + marine sanctuaries (Gulf of Mannar Marine NP, Malvan).
- **First marine NP / sanctuary: Gulf of Kachchh**, Jamnagar (~42 islands, ~52 corals). Gulf of Mannar marine NP, TN, 1986; coral restoration at Vaan Island (Jun 2026).
- **National Centre for Marine Biodiversity: Jamnagar**, Gujarat.
- **Palk Bay** (448 sq km): first **Dugong Conservation Reserve**.
- **Hope Spots:** named by **Mission Blue (Sylvia Earle)**; **Andaman and Nicobar** and **Lakshadweep** named India's first Hope Spots in **2013** (with IUCN).

### 9. GENE BANKS, SEED VAULTS AND CRYOPRESERVATION
- **Gene bank:** cold store of germplasm; each sample = an **accession**.
| ICAR Bureau | Resource | Location |
|---|---|---|
| **NBPGR** (National Gene Bank) | Plants | New Delhi |
| **NBAGR** | Livestock | Karnal |
| **NBFGR** | Fish | Lucknow |
| **NBAIM** | Microorganisms | Mau |
- **National Gene Bank** (1996): ~**4.7 lakh** accessions. **2nd National Gene Bank** (Budget 2025-26): **10 lakh** lines; safety duplicate.
- **Field gene banks:** living collections of vegetatively propagated/recalcitrant crops (mango, coconut, banana); NBRI's **Banthara** station holds one.
- **International Rice Genebank:** **IRRI, Los Banos, Philippines** (largest rice collection).
#### 9.1 Seed Vaults
| Vault | Location | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Svalbard Global Seed Vault** | **Spitsbergen, Norway** (APPSC G2 2017) | Inside a mountain in permafrost; global safety duplicate, holds India's seeds; capacity **4.5 million** samples (capacity, not holdings) |
| **Millennium Seed Bank** | Kew, **Wakehurst, UK** | Largest store of wild plant seeds |
| **Chang La** seed vault | Ladakh (~17,300 ft) | **DRDO (DIHAR) with NBPGR**; natural cold store |
#### 9.2 Cryopreservation
- Medium: **liquid nitrogen at -196°C** (APPSC 2021); gas = **nitrogen**; halts metabolism.
- Stores gametes, embryos, seeds, pollen, meristems, tissues, DNA; bull semen kept in liquid nitrogen.
- **LaCONES** (CSIR-CCMB, Hyderabad): India's **frozen zoo**; assisted reproduction and DNA banking; bred the **Indian mouse deer** with Nehru Zoo for reintroduction.
- **National Wildlife Genetic Resource Bank:** Nehru Zoological Park; DNA, tissues, gametes, embryos. Seychelles gifted two giant tortoises there (2018).
