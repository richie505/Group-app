## U0065 | GUPTA SOCIETY AND ECONOMY – VARNA AND JATI, POSITION OF WOMEN, FA-HIEN'S ACCOUNT, TRADE DECLINE
### 1. FA-HIEN'S ACCOUNT
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Ruler | **Chandragupta II** (UPSC 2025) – but he **never named the king**; main interest Buddhism |
| Period in India | c. **399–412 CE** |
| Account | **Fo-kwo-ki** ("Record of Buddhist Kingdoms") |
| Route | Overland via **Central Asia and Gandhara**; returned **by sea via Tamralipti and Sri Lanka** |
| Places | **20 viharas at Mathura**; Pataliputra; decay at **Kapilavastu, Gaya, Kushinagar** |
| Charity | **Free hospital at Pataliputra**, funded by citizens / leading families – poor patients given doctors, food, medicine; rest houses on highways |
| Chandalas | Lived **outside villages and towns**; **struck a piece of wood** to announce their approach; an exception to the usual food rules |
| Exchange | **Cowrie shells** in daily exchange |
!TRAP Fa-Hien came by land and **returned by sea** via Tamralipti.
### 2. VARNA AND JATI
| Group | Status under the Guptas |
|---|---|
| **Brahmanas** | Gained through **brahmadeya** land grants |
| **Jatis** | Castes multiplied into sub-castes by absorbing **foreigners and tribes** (esp. via land grants) |
| **Kayasthas** | Caste of **scribes and accountants** / record-keepers of land grants; a **new class** from the land-grant bureaucracy; **prathama kayastha** sat on town councils |
| **Chandalas** | **Outside the four varnas** and settlements; **untouchability intensified** |
| **Slaves** | **Narada Smriti** lists **15 types** of slaves |
### 3. POSITION OF WOMEN
- **Marriage:** Dharmashastras favoured **pre-puberty marriage**; **svayamvara** gave way to marriages arranged by parents.
- **Widows:** expected to stay **celibate**.
- **Property:** no general property rights, but control over **stridhana** (marriage gifts).
- **Education:** most women denied education; treated as property.
- **Earliest sati inscription:** **Eran, 510 CE** – wife of **Goparaja**, general of **Bhanugupta**; sati was rare under the Guptas.
!TRAP Eran 510 CE = wife of a **general** (Goparaja), not a king's wife.

### 6. GUILDS (SHRENIS)
- **Autonomous** associations of artisans and merchants; production units.
- **Rule-making:** own rules and laws, executive committees, **own seals**; regulated trade; settled internal disputes.
- **Banking:** received **public deposits** and gave **loans**.
- **Military role:** some kept their **own armed bodies** for protection (APPSC G1 2023).
- **Charity:** temples, rest houses, assembly halls, gardens.
- Terms: **Shreni** = guild; **Sreshthi / Setti** = head; **Sarthavaha** = caravan trader.
- **AP angle:** Satavahana weavers' guild = **Kolika Shreni** (APHQ 2019).
!TRAP "NOT a feature of guilds" – the answer is always "**non-autonomous bodies**" (APPSC G1 2022, 2023).
#### 6.1 Guild Inscriptions
| Inscription | Content |
|---|---|
| **Dashpur / Mandsaur** (Kumaragupta I, governor **Bandhuvarman**) | **Silk-weavers' guild (Tantuwayas)** from **Lata** built a **Sun temple** (436–37 CE), repaired it (473 CE); composed by **Vatsabhatti**; "**world's first advertisement**" (describes silk fabrics); calls Dashpur Pashchimapur; weavers changing jobs shows falling silk demand |
| **Indore copper plate** (Skandagupta) | Endowment with the **guild of oil-men** of Indrapura to keep a **lamp** burning in a Sun temple |
| **Damodarpur copper plates** (Kumaragupta I) | Guilds acting as **banks**; town council of nagarashreshthi, sarthavaha, prathama kulika, prathama kayastha |
!TRAP Mandsaur composer is Vatsabhatti – **not Bhava / Bhavabhuti** (MPPSC 2021, 2024).
### 7. PORTS AND TRADE CENTRES
| Port / centre | Region | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Tamralipti (Tamluk)** | Bengal, east coast | Chief port for North Indian trade with SE Asia, China, Lanka, Java, Sumatra |
| **Bhrigukachchha (Bharuch / Broach / Barygaza)** | Gujarat, mouth of the **Narmada** | **Largest west-coast port**; North India's trade with the west |
| **Kalyan, Chaul** | **Maharashtra (Konkan)** coast | Chaul ~23 miles south of Mumbai |
| **Ghantasala (Kontakossylla), Kadura (Koddura)** | **Krishna district, Andhra** | East-coast ports |
| **Sopara, Barbaricum** | Western India | Trade with Rome, SE Asia |
| **Pratishthana** | Deccan | Trade centre |
| **Arikamedu, Kaveripattanam (Puhar), Muziris** | South India | International (incl. Roman) trade centres |

- **AP angle:** **Motupalli and Koduru** served SE Asian trade under the **Vishnukundins**.
!KEY UPSC 2020: **Ghantasala, Kadura and Chaul** handled foreign trade.
!TRAP Kalyan is in **Maharashtra**, not Karnataka.

### 9. TRADE DECLINE AND URBAN DECAY
#### 9.1 Causes
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Eastern Roman (Byzantine)** | Silk and spice trade lasted till about **550 CE**; fell once the Romans **learnt to rear silkworms** |
| **Fall of the West Roman Empire** | Standard cause of North Indian trade decline (APPSC G2 2017) |
| **Sassanid decline** | After the rise of Islam; hurt overland trade |
| **Hunas** | Blocked the north-western route; loss of western India cut inflow of gold and silver |
| Dharmashastras | Banned sea travel |
#### 9.2 New Directions
- After the fall of Rome, merchants relied more on **South-East Asian** trade via Tamralipti; it did not revive the northern economy.
- Trade with China grew – Chinese silk = **Chinansuka**.
#### 9.3 Urban Decay Debate and Timing
- **R.S. Sharma:** shrinking towns, self-reliant villages, few gold coins; **vishti** and feudal obligations underpin the critique.
- **Declining towns:** **Sanghol, Hastinapur, Mathura, Shravasti, Kaushambi**, Sonpur (APPSC GS 2023); **Pataliputra** had become a village when Hiuen-Tsang came.
- Gradual decline of towns = feature of the Gupta period; visible in the 7th century; stagnation **c. 750–1000 CE**; recovery with the Arab empire from the 10th century.
- Post-Gupta farm structure: growth of **feudalism** – landowning middle class and dependent peasants paying rent and services.
!TRAP "Towns declined only by the mid-9th century" is **false**.
