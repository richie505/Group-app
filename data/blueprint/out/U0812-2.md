### 2. EDUCATION SCHEMES AND DECISIONS
#### 2.1 Talliki Vandanam 2026
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Earlier name | **Amma Vodi** (January 2020) |
| Department | **School Education** |
| Benefit | **₹15,000 per child per year** credited to the **mother/guardian**; **₹2,000** deducted for school maintenance, sanitation, hygiene |
| DBT start (2026) | **22 July 2026** – **₹13,000** credited (second year of the scheme) |
| Sanctioned (AY 2026–27) | **₹10,120.78 crore**; **67,47,190 students**; **42,70,802 mothers/guardians** |
| Budget 2026-27 (BE) | **₹9,668 crore** (2025-26: ₹9,407 crore) |
| Previous spend | **₹10,090 crore** to **67.27 lakh** students (reported Jan 2026) |
| Coverage | **Class 1 to Class 12** (incl. Intermediate), from 2025-26 – government, aided, private, residential schools |
| Type | **Conditional cash transfer** – **75% attendance** |
| Aim | Lower **dropout**, higher **GER** (G2 Screening 2025 key) |
- **Every eligible child** in a family gets it (13 Jun 2026) – **not one per family**.
- Extended to **orphaned children**.
- Account: **Aadhaar-seeded and NPCI-mapped**. DBT was launched nationally in **2013**.
##### Eligibility
| Criterion | Rule |
|---|---|
| Income | Rural below **₹10,000/month**; urban below **₹12,000/month** |
| Rice Card | **Mandatory** |
| Land | Wet up to **3 acres** / dry up to **10 acres** |
| Electricity | Below **300 units/month** |
| House | Below **1,000 sq ft** |
| Attendance | At least **75%** |
!TRAP Excluded: **ITI and Polytechnic** students. Benefit is per child, not per family.
#### 2.2 Prashast 2.0 – Disability Screening in All Schools
- App **Prashast 2.0** screens **21 types of disabilities**; year **2026–27**, per **Union Ministry of Education** directions; integrated with **UDISE+**.
- Coverage: **all schools** – government, local-body, aided **and private**; target 100%.
- **Part-I**: by **class teachers** – by **September 2026**. **Part-II**: by **special educators and Bhavita Centre** staff – by **October 2026**.
- **IERPs** = Inclusive Education Resource Persons in Bhavita Centres; Samagra Shiksha runs **679 Bhavitha centres** for children with special needs.
#### 2.3 PM SHRI, KGBVs, Canva, Higher Education
- AP ranked **1st** in India in utilisation of **PM SHRI** (Prime Minister's Schools for Rising India) funds – reviewed by HRD Minister **Nara Lokesh**. PM SHRI = **model government schools** aligned with **NEP 2020** (not private).
- **KGBVs** (Samagra Shiksha) in AP: **352**; seats **75% SC/ST/OBC/minority**, **25% BPL**.
- **Canva MoU** (School Education Dept): free **Canva for Education** to students, teachers, officials in **4 phases** (Canva est. **2013, Sydney**).
- **AP Higher Education Fee Regulatory and Monitoring Committee**: Chairman **Dr. Justice V.R.K. Krupasagar** (former AP HC judge); **T.V.R.S. Bhanu Murthy** a member.
- **Four higher-education Bills (August 2026)**: the **Regulatory Commission** turned into a **fee-regulation committee**; **private universities need less land**.

### 3. AGRICULTURE AND APCNF
#### 3.1 APCNF Wins the 2026 Food Planet Prize
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|

| Prize | **2026 Food Planet Prize – $1.5 million** |

### 4. WEAVERS, FISHERMEN AND BC WELFARE
#### 4.1 Netanna Sevalo (Nethannala Sevalo)

- **₹179 crore** to **71,536** handloom families – **₹25,000 each** a year to **loom-owning families**, irrespective of age or number of looms.
- Handloom dependants in AP: about **1.27 lakh**.
- Weaving centres: **Chirala, Venkatagiri, Dharmavaram, Uppada, Mangalagiri, Ponduru, Pedana, Srikalahasti, Narasapuram**.

#### 4.2 BC Welfare and Chirala Area
- **Draft BC Protection Act** prepared; **₹505 crore** over two years to **1.34 lakh** fishing families (ban period); mining leases for **Vaddera**; extra **₹20,000** rooftop solar subsidy for BCs; proposed **₹2.5 lakh** universal health cover.
- **Suryalanka–Chirala tourism corridor**: **₹97 crore** under **Swadesh Darshan**; **Bay of Bapatla** website; **Bapatla LEAPS** youth skill programme.
#### 4.3 Fishermen – Satellite Transponders
- **Free satellite transponders** on boats, linked to smartphones by **Bluetooth**; under **PM Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY)**; distributed at **Singarayakonda (Prakasam)**.
- Uses: **weather/cyclone alerts**, **SOS** to control rooms, warning on **border crossing**, faster rescue.
- Fishing-ban assistance **₹20,000**; pension from **50 years**; fisheries development boards at **Visakhapatnam and Bhimavaram**.
### 5. POPULATION, HEALTH AND CHILD CARE
#### 5.1 Pillale Sampada
- Launched by CM Naidu in **Vijayawada** on **World Population Day (11 July 2026)**; aim: tackle **declining birth rate and ageing**.
- **₹30,000** for the **third child**; **₹40,000** for the **fourth child**.
- World Population Day: set up **1989** by **UNDP**. AP **TFR below 1.5** → **Population Management Policy**.
#### 5.2 Sanjeevani Digital Health Project
- AI-based digital health project, **piloted in Kuppam**; to be expanded **statewide** (RFPs invited) – an **"AI doctor"** for every citizen.
- **14-member** technical committee to pick the agency; chair **K.V.N. Chakradhar Babu**; tenders via **APMSIDC**.
- Sanjeevani = digital health profile + **ABHA ID**. **ABDM** launched **2021** (agency **National Health Authority**); national **e-Sanjeevani** telemedicine – **2019**, Health Ministry.
#### 5.3 Biofoundry Task Force (Cancer)
- AP estimates ~**4.73 lakh** cancer patients (~70% aged 40–69); **76,000+** new cases and **40,000+** deaths a year.
- Cases up **39.5%** in AP vs **12.8%** nationally (2020–2025); women ~**59–61%** of patients (breast, cervical); **88.2%** of screened suspects skip further tests.
- Plan: **State biobank** (with **CSIR–IGIB, CSIR–CCMB**), digital histopathology; **HPV vaccination for 3.45 lakh girls** (HPV = major cause of **cervical cancer**).
- Cancer centres: **Guntur, Visakhapatnam, Kurnool, Kadapa**; **17** oncology units; ~**₹746 crore** a year via **Dr NTR Vaidya Seva Trust**.
#### 5.4 Shishu Maapan and Urban Crèches
- **Shishu Maapan**: **contactless AI app** (with **Wadhwani AI**, est. 2018); **ASHAs/ANMs** estimate newborn **weight, height, head/chest circumference, shoulder width** from **smartphone video**; alerts for growth abnormalities.
- **MEPMA** (Mission for Elimination of Poverty in Municipal Areas; under **Municipal Administration Dept**) = implementing agency; **Mobile Crèches** = technical partner.
- **15 crèches** for children **6 months–6 years** (~375 children) in **Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam, Tirupati, Guntur**; 2026–27: train **45 SHG women**, certify **15 master trainers**; Telugu materials; MIS.
- Convergence: **ICDS, PHCs, RBSK** (4 Ds – defects, diseases, deficiencies, developmental delays).

### 7. LOCAL BODIES AND RURAL EMPLOYMENT
#### 7.1 33.33% BC Reservation in ULB Polls
- AP Cabinet approved **33.33% BC reservation** for **Urban Local Body** elections.
- Laws: **Sections 6 and 14(A), AP Municipal Corporations Act 1994**; **Sections 8 and 24, AP Municipalities Act 1965**.
- AP has **123 ULBs**; only **100** polled in 2021 (23 held up by court cases); **87** tenures over, **12 end in November 2026**.
