## U0368 | IMPACT OF LPG ON THE ROLE OF THE STATE – PROVIDER TO REGULATOR AND ENABLER
### 1. THE 1991 SHIFT
- Trigger: 1991 crisis + LPG (PM **P.V. Narasimha Rao**, FM **Dr. Manmohan Singh**): **state-controlled → market-oriented** economy.
- **Government to Governance**: State became **facilitator, regulator and catalyst**; it did **not fully withdraw**.
#### 1.1 Models of the State
| Model | Role | Indian marker |
|---|---|---|
| Welfare / provider | Owns and runs production; "commanding heights" | IPR 1956 |
| Regulatory | Sets rules; independent regulators watch markets | SEBI, TRAI, CERC, IRDA |
| Facilitator / enabler | Delivery through private or civil-society actors | **PPPs, DBT, GeM, UMANG** |
- **RBI** after 1991: regulator → **facilitator**; freer bank decisions, entry of private and foreign banks; still regulates.
- Housing: State moved from provider → enabler of private real estate (22 Apr 2026).
- **AP – Deepam-2**: 3 free LPG cylinders a year to BPL families via **DBT** = provider → enabler (Jan 2026).
!TRAP SEBI = regulatory state; PPP/DBT/GeM/UMANG = enabler state.
### 2. ROLLING BACK THE STATE
- Licensing abolished except alcohol, cigarettes, hazardous chemicals, industrial explosives, electronic aerospace and defence (drugs since delicensed).
- Public-sector reserved list: **17 (Schedule A, 1956) → 8 (1991) → 3 → now 2**: **atomic energy and railway operations**.
- **Defence production opened to private sector in 2001**; last SSI items de-reserved **2015**.
- PSE autonomy: **MoUs** and **Navratna / Maharatna** status.
- Disinvestment began **1991-92** (target **Rs 2,500 crore**): first to bridge the fiscal deficit, later for efficiency.
- **LIC**: SEBI deadline **16 May 2027** for 10% minimum public shareholding (4 Aug 2026).
- Ending **MRTP asset limits** removed curbs on concentration, entry, expansion, mergers and acquisitions.
### 3. INDEPENDENT REGULATORS
| Regulator | Year / Act | Sector |
|---|---|---|
| SEBI | **1992**, SEBI Act; replaced Controller of Capital Issues | Securities |
| TRAI | 1997 | Telecom |
| CERC | 1998; now Electricity Act 2003 | Power tariffs, inter-state transmission; **carbon trading** |
| IRDA | 1999 | Insurance |
| PNGRB | 2006 | Petroleum, gas pipelines |
- **Carbon Credit Trading Scheme**: run by **BEE**; trading regulated by **CERC** (not SEBI).
- **Economic Survey 2025-26**, Ch. 3 "**Refining the Regulatory Touch**" (31 Jan 2026): dedicated regulatory careers for **younger professionals**, not retired bureaucrats; RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA moving reactive → **anticipatory**.
### 4. MINIMUM GOVERNMENT, MAXIMUM GOVERNANCE
- Slogan from **2014**: a leaner State that governs better through **rules, technology, accountability**.
- E-governance cuts discretion and middlemen: **Aadhaar, DBT, GeM**, UMANG (not Navratna status); RTI Act 2005.
### 5. MARKET FAILURE VS GOVERNMENT FAILURE
| Case | Basis |
|---|---|
| Market failure → planning-era State | Public goods, externalities, monopoly, information asymmetry |
| Government failure → 1991 | PSU low efficiency, losses, political interference, no autonomy, labour problems, project delays |
- **Public choice theory (James Buchanan)** = basis of the government-failure case.
- Critique: PSU assets **undervalued**; proceeds plugged revenue gaps; agri public investment fell; social spending curbed; overlap (TRAI vs MIB on TV ratings).
### 6. WASHINGTON CONSENSUS
- **John Williamson, 1989**; IMF + World Bank + US Treasury (**not WTO**).
- Prescriptions include fiscal discipline, reordering public spending, tax reform, **market-determined interest rates**, competitive exchange rate, trade and FDI liberalisation, privatisation, deregulation, property rights.
- India 1991: interest deregulation (**Narasimham**), tax cuts (**Chelliah**), market-determined exchange rate (**1993**).
### 7. STATE CAPACITY MODELS
| Thinker | Idea |
|---|---|
| **Lant Pritchett** | **Flailing state** (2009, India): "head" works, not linked to "limbs" |
| **Chalmers Johnson** | **Developmental state** (1982, Japan MITI) |
| **Peter Evans** | **Embedded autonomy** (1995) |
- Economic Survey 2025-26: **hybrid equilibrium** (political mediation substitutes for market discipline); **Capable State** reduces uncertainty.
