### 2. LIFE OF THE BUDDHA
- Born **563 BC** at **Lumbini**, Vaishakha Purnima.
- **Four sights**: old man, sick man, corpse, ascetic → **Great Renunciation (Mahabhinishkramana)** at **29**.
- **Six years** of austerities with **five ascetics**, later abandoned.
- Tempted by **Mara**; **enlightenment at 35** (c. 528 BCE) under the **Bodhi (Pipal) tree**, **Uruvela (Bodh Gaya)**, on the **Niranjana**.
- Last rainy season at **Vaishali (Beluva)**, aged 80, with Ananda; food poisoning at **Pava** after **Sukaramaddava** offered by **Chunda**.
- **Mahaparinirvana** at **Kusinara (Kushinagar)**, in the **Malla republic**, aged **80**, traditionally **483 BCE**.
- Last words: **"appamadena sampadetha"** – "All composite things decay, strive diligently".
- Stressed a **moral life** over philosophical problems; later scholars built the philosophy.
!NUM 29 – 35 – 80 = Renunciation – Enlightenment – Parinirvana

!TRAP Kushinagar was the Malla capital – true, but that is not why the Mahaparinirvana happened there.
#### 3.1 Bodh Gaya Facts
- **Mahabodhi Temple**: UNESCO site since **2002**.
- Original Bodhi tree destroyed by **Pushyamitra Sunga** and **Shashanka** (7th c., per Hiuen Tsang); present tree is the **fifth generation**.
- **Maha Bodhi Society (1891)**: **Anagarika Dharmapala**, to restore Bodh Gaya to Buddhists.
### 4. FIRST SERMON, MAHAPARINIRVANA AND VESAK
- **Dhammachakra Pravartana** ("Turning the Wheel of Dharma") at **Sarnath**, after enlightenment at Bodh Gaya.
- Audience: **five former companions** – **Kondanna, Bhaddiya, Vappa, Mahanama, Assaji** – who became **Arhats**.
- Content: **Four Noble Truths** and **Noble Eightfold Path**.
- **Buddha Purnima / Vesak** (Vaisakha full moon): **birth, enlightenment, Mahaparinirvana** – **not the first sermon**.
- **1 May 2026**: Buddha Purnima greetings by the President and PM stressed India–Nepal Buddhist ties.

### 7. CORE TEACHINGS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Nirvana** | **Main goal**; "extinction of the flame of desire"; end of dukkha and samsara (rebirth) |
| **Middle Path** (Madhyama Marga) | Rejects both **extreme asceticism** and **sensual indulgence** |
| **Anicca** | Impermanence |
| **Anatta** | No permanent self (rejects atman) |
| **Dukkha** | Suffering |
| **Karma** | **Intention-based** morality; present state from past actions; rebirth till nirvana |
| Raft simile | Teaching as a raft – **upaya** (skilful means), not dogma |
- **Three marks**: Anicca, Anatta, Dukkha.
- Rejected **Vedic authority**, sacrifice and caste; **God neither affirmed nor denied**; belief in God not needed for the Sangha.
### 8. FOUR NOBLE TRUTHS (ARYA SATYA)
| Truth | Content |
|---|---|
| 1. **Dukkha** | Life is suffering ("sabbam dukkham") |
| 2. **Samudaya** | **Cause** = craving (**tanha**), ignorance, attachment |
| 3. **Nirodha** | **Cessation** = Nirvana |
| 4. **Magga** | **Path** = Eightfold Path |
- Order: suffering → cause → cessation → path.
- Buddha and **Mirabai** both held the world full of sorrow (first Noble Truth).
### 9. EIGHTFOLD PATH (ASHTANGIKA MARGA)
| Group | Components |
|---|---|
| **Prajna** (wisdom) | Right View (**samma-ditthi**), Right Intention (**sankappa**) |
| **Sila** (ethics) | Right Speech (**vaca**), Right Action (kammanta), Right Livelihood (ajiva) |
| **Samadhi** (mental discipline) | Right Effort (vayama), Right **Mindfulness** (sati), Right Concentration (**samadhi**) |
!TRAP Right Mindfulness belongs to mental discipline (Samadhi), not ethics.
### 10. PRATITYASAMUTPADA
- **Dependent origination**: every phenomenon arises from a cause; a **12-link** chain (**12 nidanas**).
| Order | Links |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | **Avidya** (ignorance) → **Samskara** → **Vijnana** (consciousness) → **Namarupa** |
| 5–8 | Shadayatana (six senses) → Sparsha (contact) → Vedana (feeling) → Trishna (craving) |
| 9–12 | Upadana (clinging) → Bhava (becoming) → Jati (birth) → **Jara-marana** (old age–death) |
!KEY Cause of Samskara = **Avidya**, root of all suffering.

| Person | Role |

- **Jun 2026**: relics of Sariputta and Mahamoggallana sent from **Sanchi to Mongolia** by the Ministry of Culture.
#### 11.2 Triratna
- **Buddha** (teacher), **Dhamma** (teaching), **Sangha** (order of monks and nuns) – three refuges.
#### 11.3 Sangha Rules and Terms
- Open to all **above 15**, **caste no bar**; barred: **criminals, lepers, those with infectious diseases**.
- **Women** admitted at **Vaishali** at **Ananda's** request; first nun **Mahaprajapati Gautami**.
- **Sanghamitta** took the Bhikkhuni Sangha to Sri Lanka; it lasted 1,000+ years until the **Chola invasion of 1017 AD**.
