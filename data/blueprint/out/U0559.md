### 4. PATTISEEMA AND POLAVARAM
#### 4.1 Pattiseema
- **Lift irrigation scheme** on the Godavari at Pattiseema; **24 pumps**; first water Sept **2015**.
- Route: Godavari → **Polavaram Right Main Canal** → Krishna at **Prakasam Barrage** = **first Godavari-Krishna link**.
- Beneficiary: **Krishna delta farmers** (PYQ 2017, 2018); ~450 TMC moved.
- **Temporary** until Polavaram's gravity flow starts.
!TRAP "The canal runs 100 km through West Godavari" was the false statement in the PYQ.
#### 4.2 Polavaram
- **GWDT award (1980)** permits diversion of **80 TMC** Godavari water to the Krishna.
- First proposed as **"Ramapada Sagaram"** by Sir Shonthi Venkataramamurthy.
- Started 2005; still a **Jalayagnam** project. May 2026: 88% civil works done.

### 8. MICRO-IRRIGATION (APMIP)
- **APMIP launched 2003**; now run as **RKVY-PDMC**.
- 37.90 lakh acres, 13.49 lakh farmers since inception; **75% drip, 25% sprinkler**.
- **AP 1st** nationally in micro-irrigation (2024-25); **100% subsidy for SC/ST**.
- Per Drop More Crop (2015-16; under RKVY since 2022-23): **55%** small/marginal, **45%** others.
- **Micro Irrigation Fund**: Rs 10,000 crore via **NABARD**; AP among eight States.
- PMKSY = AIBP + Har Khet Ko Pani + Watershed + Per Drop More Crop.
### 9. GROUNDWATER REGULATION AND COMMUNITY MANAGEMENT
- **APWALTA, 2002** (AP Water, Land and Trees Act): checks random borewell digging (PYQ 2018); **rainwater harvesting mandatory on premises of 200 sq m or more** (PYQ 2019).
- **APFAMGS**: farmers collect rainfall and well data, prepare **crop-water budgets**, voluntarily limit pumping (FAO-supported).
- 40,000-50,000 new bore wells a year: area irrigated per well falling.
- **National Hydrology Project** set up AP's real-time groundwater monitoring.
- Debate: gap between irrigation **potential created and utilised**.
