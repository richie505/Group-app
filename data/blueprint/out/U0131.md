## U0131 | AKBAR'S INSTITUTIONS – MANSABDARI SYSTEM, TODAR MAL'S BANDOBAST, DAHSALA, ZABTI AND OTHER REVENUE SYSTEMS
### 1. ROAD TO THE DAHSALA
- Mansabdari + zabti-dahsala = a **salaried, transferable nobility** + **predictable cash revenue**.
!KEY Sher Shah's survey system → **Karori experiment (1574)** → karoris collect revenue and cultivation data → **Dahsala (1580)**, Todar Mal.
#### 1.1 Karori Experiment – 1574
- Core provinces split into **circles**, each to yield **one crore dams**, under officers called **karoris**.
- Karoris collected revenue and reported cultivation data – groundwork for the dahsala.
!TRAP **1574 = Karori experiment; 1580 = Dahsala.**
### 2. RAJA TODAR MAL
- Earlier served **Sher Shah**; built on his survey-based system.
- **Diwan** (finance minister) of the empire; one of the **Navratnas**.
- **Muzaffar Khan Turbati** was first given the proper status of Diwan by Akbar, with Todar Mal as assistant; Todar Mal and **Khwaja Shah Mansur** came later.
| Unit | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Gaz-i-Ilahi** | Unit of length, about **82 cm** |
| **Jarib** | **Bamboo pieces joined with iron rings** – replaced hemp rope, which shrank or stretched |
| State share | **One-third** of average produce, paid in **cash (dam)**; kind allowed in some areas |
### 3. REVENUE SYSTEMS
| System | Meaning | Region / year |
|---|---|---|
| **Zabti** (Bandobast) | Land measured; **dasturs** with cash rates per crop per **bigha** | Core North Indian provinces (Lahore to Allahabad, Malwa, Gujarat) |
| **Dahsala** (Ain-i-Dahsala) | Average produce and prices of the **last ten years (1571-1580)**, turned into cash | **1580** (APPSC G2 2017) |
| **Batai / Ghalla-bakhshi** (Bantai) | **Crop-sharing** – actual produce divided between state and peasant | Thatta, Kabul, Kashmir |
| **Nasaq** | Rough assessment from **past revenue records**, no fresh measurement | Mainly **Bengal** |
| **Kankut** | Yield **estimated on the standing crop** | Where measuring was impractical |
| Plough assessment | By **number of ploughs** | Parts of the **Deccan** (J.N. Sarkar, History of Aurangzeb) |
- **"Bandobast system" = Dahsala** (JPSC 2013); also called "Zabti or Bandobast".
- Zabti was introduced by **Akbar**.
- Sher Shah's three systems: **Nasq / Muktai / Kankut**, **Nakadi or Jabti**, **Galla Bakshi or Bantai**.
- **Iqta** = Sultanate revenue assignment (**Iltutmish**).
!PAIR Batai | crop-sharing | Kashmir, Kabul || Kankut | standing-crop estimate | no measurement

### 5. REVENUE AND GRANT TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Maal (mal)** | Land revenue – the **principal state income** |
| **Khalisa** | **Crown land**; revenue straight to the imperial treasury; reserve for cash (naqd) salaries |

### 6. MANSABDARI SYSTEM
- Introduced by **Akbar**; of **Central Asian / Mongol** military origin.
- Mansabdars held **combined civil-military, non-hereditary** ranks, paid by **jagir** (or cash).
- Ranks: **10 to 5,000** for nobles (later raised to **7,000**); higher ranks only for **royal princes**.
- **Zat** = personal rank and pay; **Sawar** = number of horsemen to maintain – dual ranks from **1595-96**.
- Three classes by the sawar-zat ratio: sawar equal to zat = 1st class.
- **Dagh** (horse branding) – **1573-74**.
- **Du-aspa sih-aspa** – troopers with two/three horses without raising zat – introduced by **Jahangir**.
!PAIR Mansabdar | civil-military, non-hereditary | paid by jagir || ICS | purely civil | fixed salary
### 7. JAGIRDARI SYSTEM
| Type | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Tankha** | In lieu of salary; **transferable every 3-4 years** |
| **Watan** | Zamindar's homeland; hereditary, **not transferable** |
| **Mashrut** | Conditional, tied to specified duties |
| **In'am** | Reward; no service obligation |
- Frequent transfers prevented local power bases.
