## U0630 | NATIONAL PARKS, WILDLIFE SANCTUARIES, BIOSPHERE RESERVES AND CONSERVATION RESERVES
### 1. WILDLIFE PROTECTION ACT 1972 – PROTECTED AREA CATEGORIES
- First comprehensive wildlife law of India; **Presidential assent 9 September 1972**.
- PAs under it: **National Parks, Wildlife Sanctuaries, Conservation Reserves, Community Reserves**.
| Category | Declared by / on | Rights and use |
|---|---|---|
| **National Park** | State (or Centre) | **Highest protection**; no grazing or private rights |
| **Wildlife Sanctuary** | State | Some activities/rights **regulated but allowed** |
| **Conservation Reserve** (**Sec 36A**) | State, on **government-owned land** beside or linking PAs, after consulting locals | Local rights unaffected; committees with **panchayats** |
| **Community Reserve** | State, on **private/community land** freely offered | Rights unaffected; land cannot be inside an NP, WLS or CR |
- Conservation and community reserves were added by the **WPA (Amendment) 2002**.
- **Supreme Court**: **10-km mining buffer** extended to all **wetland conservation reserves**; ordinary eco-sensitive buffer around NPs/WLS = **1 km**. Final ESZ notifications: **353**.
!PAIR Conservation Reserve | Government land | Sec 36A || Community Reserve | Private/community land | Rights unaffected

#### 8.2 UNESCO MAB (World Network) Status
- **18** BRs in India; **13** in UNESCO's World Network of Biosphere Reserves.
- Listed examples: **Nilgiri (2000, first)**, Gulf of Mannar (2001), Sundarbans (2001), Nanda Devi (2004), **Agasthyamalai (2016)**, **Khangchendzonga (2018)**, **Panna (2020)**, **Cold Desert (Sep 2025, latest)**.
- **Kachchh** (largest) is not in the World Network.
- **Agasthyamalai**: TN eviction notices after **A. John Kennedy v. State of Tamil Nadu** (2025) – tribal-rights concerns.
- AP: **Seshachalam** BR (4,755.997 sq km).
