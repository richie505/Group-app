#### 1.3 Leiden (Anaimangalam) Copper Plates Returned
- The **Anaimangalam Copper Plates** (**Leiden Plates**) were returned from the **University of Leiden**.
- Set: **21 large + 3 small** copper plates + a royal **bronze ring**; taken in the **18th century** during **Dutch control of Nagapattinam**.
- They record the grant of **Anaimangalam village** to the **Chulamanivarma Vihara** (Buddhist), built by the **Srivijaya** ruler **Sri Mara Vijayotunga Varman**.
- Grant **promised by Raja Raja Chola I**, **executed by Rajendra Chola I**.
- Language: **Sanskrit (Grantha script)** for the genealogy; **Tamil** for the boundaries.
- Seal: **Chola Tiger, Pandya Fish, Chera Bow**.
| Copper plates | Key fact |
|---|---|
| **Anaimangalam (Leiden)** | Chola grant to a Buddhist vihara; 21 + 3 plates |
| **Karandai** | Record **57-plate** Chola charter of **Rajendra I** |
| **Sogaura** | **Mauryan famine relief**; **Prakrit** language, **Brahmi** script |
!PAIR Raja Raja Chola I | promised the grant | Leiden Plates || Rajendra Chola I | executed the grant | also Karandai 57 plates

### 4. WHO: EBOLA PHEIC AND THE 79TH WORLD HEALTH ASSEMBLY
#### 4.1 Ebola Declared a PHEIC
- **WHO** declared the Ebola outbreak in **DR Congo and Uganda** a **PHEIC** (Public Health Emergency of International Concern), the **highest WHO alert** under the **International Health Regulations (IHR)**.
- It is **not a pandemic emergency**.
- Strain: **Bundibugyo ebolavirus**; it has **limited vaccine options**.
- First count: **8 confirmed** in **Ituri Province** (DRC), **246 suspected**, **80 suspected deaths**; **2 confirmed in Kampala** (1 death). Later count: **300+ cases and 88 deaths**.
- This is the **17th Ebola outbreak in Congo** since the virus was found there in **1976**.
- Effect: led to the postponement of **IAFS-IV** (Section 6).
#### 4.2 Ebola Background
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Known since | **1976** |
| Largest outbreak | **West Africa 2014–16** (**Guinea, Liberia, Sierra Leone**): **28,600+ cases, 11,325 deaths** |
| Viruses causing large outbreaks | **Ebola, Sudan, Bundibugyo** |
| Incubation | **2–21 days**; contact tracing for **21 days** |
| Treatments | **mAb114 = Ansuvimab**; **REGN-EB3 = Inmazeb** |
| Vaccines | **Ervebo**; **Zabdeno + Mvabea** (ring vaccination) |
#### 4.3 79th World Health Assembly (WHA79), Geneva, May 2026
- Theme: **"Reshaping Global Health: A Shared Responsibility"**.
- **J.P. Nadda** addressed the plenary.
- Adopted **13 resolutions**, including the **Global Action Plan on AMR 2026–2036**.
- WHA = **WHO's highest decision-making body**; meets **annually in Geneva**.
- WHO est. **7 April 1948**; DG **Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus**.

#### 5.4 Finland – Background
- On the **Fennoscandian Peninsula**; borders **Russia, Sweden, Norway** (**not Denmark**); seas: **Gulf of Bothnia, Gulf of Finland, Baltic Sea**.
- **"Land of Thousand Lakes"**: nearly **188,000** glacial lakes; largest **Lake Saimaa**; **Lapland** – **Sámi** people.
- Joined **NATO April 2023**; **EU since 1995**; one of **8 Arctic Council** members (India is an **observer**).
- **Most forested** country in Europe (**70%+**); carbon neutrality target **2035**; **world's first national circular economy roadmap (2016)**.
- **World's happiest country** for **eight consecutive years (2018–2025)**.
#### 5.5 NATO Membership
