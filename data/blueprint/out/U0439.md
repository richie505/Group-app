## U0439 | FLAGSHIP SOCIAL SECTOR PROGRAMMES AND SOCIAL SECURITY – HEALTH, EDUCATION, NUTRITION, HOUSING, PENSIONS, PDS
### 1. HEALTH PROGRAMMES
#### 1.1 Objective and Launch Year
| Programme | Objective | Launch |
|---|---|---|
| **Pulse Polio** | Polio eradication; WHO certified India **polio-free in 2014** | **1995** |
| **Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)** | Cash aid for **institutional delivery**; under NHM; **100% centrally sponsored** | **2005** |
| **Mission Indradhanush** | **Full immunisation** of children and pregnant women; a special catch-up campaign under the **Universal Immunisation Programme**; MoHFW | **Dec 2014** |
| **Ayushman Arogya Mandir** | Comprehensive primary care at about **1.5 lakh** upgraded Health and Wellness Centres | 2018 |
| **PM-ABHIM** | District critical-care blocks, Integrated Public Health Labs, BSL-3 labs, pandemic preparedness; outlay **₹64,180 crore over 6 years** | 2021 |
| **Sickle Cell Anaemia Elimination Mission** | Eliminate sickle cell anaemia by **2047**; launched at **Shahdol (Madhya Pradesh)** | **July 2023** |
- **NHM** has two sub-missions: **NRHM + NUHM**; **ASHAs and ANMs** are its backbone.
- Mortality decline (1990-2023): under-5 mortality fell **78%**; neonatal mortality fell **70%**.
!TRAP Pulse Polio began in 1995, but polio-free certification came in 2014 – the same year as Mission Indradhanush.
### 2. AYUSHMAN BHARAT PM-JAY
#### 2.1 Launch, Funding and Agency
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Launch | **23 September 2018**, **Ranchi (Jharkhand)**, by PM Modi |
| Earlier name | **National Health Protection Scheme** |
| Subsumed | **RSBY (2008)** |
| Ministry | **MoHFW**; Centrally Sponsored |
| Funding | **60:40**; North-Eastern and Himalayan States **90:10** |
| Implementing agency | **National Health Authority (NHA)**; **State Health Agencies** in States |
| Allocation 2026-27 | ₹9,500 crore |
#### 2.2 Cover and Eligibility
- **₹5 lakh per family per year** for **secondary and tertiary hospitalisation**; cashless and portable.
- **The world's largest** (not second-largest) health assurance scheme **fully paid by the government**.
- Eligibility: **SECC 2011** deprivation criteria – **6 rural** and **11 urban occupational** criteria.
- Covers the **bottom 40%** of households, about **10.74 crore families**; poor and vulnerable in both **rural and urban** areas.
!KEY APPSC 2025 key: Target group of Ayushman Bharat = "rural and poor populations".
#### 2.3 Senior-Citizen Expansion and Data
- **Oct 2024 – Ayushman Vay Vandana:** every person aged **70+**, **irrespective of income**, gets ₹5 lakh a year; an extra ₹5 lakh top-up if the family is already under PM-JAY. About 1.20 crore seniors enrolled.
- 2025-26: PM-JAY covers **6 crore senior citizens**; **49%** of beneficiaries are women.
- 16 Aug 2026: **45.51 crore** Ayushman cardholders; **1.86 lakh** Ayushman Arogya Mandirs.
#### 2.4 AP Convergence
- AP's state scheme (begun as **Rajiv Aarogyasri, 2007**) now runs as **Dr NTR Vaidya Seva**, **converged with PM-JAY**.
- Sep 2025: AP Cabinet approved extending the **Universal Health Policy** under PM-JAY–Dr NTR Vaidya Seva in **hybrid mode**.
- **AP Universal Health Policy**: **₹2.5 lakh** free cover to all (1 Mar 2026).

### 5. FOOD SECURITY AND PDS
#### 5.1 PDS Evolution
| Phase | Year | Key change |
|---|---|---|
| Wartime rationing | **1940s** (WW-II, Bengal Famine 1943) | Import-dependent |
| **FCI + Agricultural Prices Commission** | **1965** | Food deficits; supply side |
| Universal PDS | 1970s-1992 | Grain for all households |
| **Revamped PDS (RPDS)** | **June 1992** | **First** targeting of **hilly, tribal and remote** areas |
| **Targeted PDS (TPDS)** | **June 1997** | BPL targeting |
| **AAY and Annapurna** | **2000** | Special schemes for the poorest and destitute elderly |
| **NFSA** | **2013** | **Legal right to food** |
| **ONORC** | **2019** | Ration portability; all **36 States/UTs** |
!TRAP RPDS 1992 → TPDS 1997 → NFSA 2013.
#### 5.2 Two Special Schemes of 2000
- **Antyodaya Anna Yojana**: poorest of the poor; **35 kg per household** a month (not per person).
- **Annapurna**: **10 kg free grain** a month to poor elderly **eligible for IGNOAPS but not drawing it**; part of NSAP.
!KEY APPSC 2025: "two special schemes of 2000 for target groups" = AAY and Annapurna.
#### 5.3 NFSA Entitlements, PMGKAY, ONORC
| Entitlement | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Priority households | **5 kg per person** a month |
| AAY | **35 kg per household** |
| Maternity benefit | **₹6,000** |
| Coverage | **79.8 crore** under NFSA |
- **PMGKAY**: began **2020 (COVID-19)**; free NFSA grains for **5 years from 1 January 2024**.
- **One Nation One Ration Card**: NFSA ration portability anywhere; national launch **2019**.
- 16 Aug 2026: free grain to **81.35 crore** people; 99.8% of FPS use Aadhaar authentication.
- Draft NFSA (Amendment) Bill 2026 (Jul 2026): 7 kg per person for AAY, capped at 35 kg per household.
### 6. NUTRITION, SANITATION AND DRINKING WATER
#### 6.1 Nutrition
- **POSHAN Abhiyaan** (National Nutrition Mission): **8 March 2018, Jhunjhunu (Rajasthan)**; nodal **Ministry of Women and Child Development**.
- Converges **ICDS, NHM and Swachh Bharat**; tackles **stunting, under-nutrition, anaemia, low birth weight**.
- **Mission Poshan 2.0** (Saksham Anganwadi and Poshan 2.0): MWCD, **2021-22 to 2025-26**; diet diversity, fortification, millets.
- **Poshan Maah** (September) and **Poshan Pakhwada** (March): **bi-annual** (not June/December) awareness drives at village and Anganwadi level; **Sept 2023 = 6th Rashtriya Poshan Maah**.
#### 6.2 Sanitation
- **Swachh Bharat Mission** launched **2 October 2014** (target: clean India by 2 Oct 2019).
- **SBM-Gramin Phase II**: **ODF Plus** villages; **SBM-Urban 2.0**: **garbage-free cities**.
- **India Sanitation Coalition**: launched **25 June 2015** by **FICCI** to support SBM through business and civil society.
- **NAMASTE**: ends hazardous **manual cleaning of sewers and septic tanks**; nodal **MoSJE + MoHUA** (not Education); worker profiling, PM-JAY cover, training with ₹3,000 stipend, PPE.
#### 6.3 Drinking Water
- **Jal Jeevan Mission (2019)**: **Functional Household Tap Connection** for every rural home; nodal **Ministry of Jal Shakti**; **Village Water and Sanitation Committees** plan and run supply.

### 7. HOUSING
#### 7.1 PMAY-Gramin (2016)
- Restructured from **Indira Awaas Yojana** (began **1985**, independent **1996**).
- Unit: at least **25 sq m**; **₹1.20 lakh (plains) / ₹1.30 lakh (hilly)**.
- Target: 2.95 crore houses (2016-17 to 2023-24) + **2 crore more** (2024-25 to 2028-29); **extended to 2028-29**. Completed 10 years in 2026.
#### 7.2 PMAY-Urban (2015)
- Pucca house for **EWS/LIG and MIG** categories, including slum dwellers; original **Housing for All by 2022** target.
- **Four components**: in-situ slum redevelopment; credit-linked subsidy; affordable housing in partnership; beneficiary-led construction (rural tap water is NOT one).
- **PMAY-U 2.0 (2024)**: **1 crore** urban houses. Budget 2026-27 cut from ₹19,794 crore to ₹18,625 crore.
- **Rajiv Awas Yojana**: aimed at a **slum-free India**; **subsumed into PMAY-U in 2015**.
- Houses registered in women's names make PMAY a **gender-budget** scheme (26 Jan 2026).

### 9. PENSION SCHEMES
#### 9.1 NPS and UPS
- **NPS**: from **1 January 2004** for Central Government employees (**armed forces excluded**); open to **all citizens from 1 May 2009**; applies to State employees joining after that State's notification.
- Regulator **PFRDA** (Act passed 19 Sep 2013, notified 1 Feb 2014); **defined-contribution**; PRAN; **Tier I mandatory, Tier II voluntary**; extra **₹50,000 under 80CCD(1B)**.
- **OCIs may join**; an account **cannot be opened or run for a third person**.
- 31 Mar 2026: **2.17 crore+** subscribers; AUM about ₹15.95 lakh crore.
- **UPS (2025)**: after **25 years**, **50%** of last 12 months' average basic pay; minimum **₹10,000**. **NPS Vatsalya (2025)**: for minors; minimum ₹250.
#### 9.2 Unorganised-Sector Pensions
| Scheme | Who | Age | Pension at 60 | Contribution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **APY** (9 May 2015; replaced Swavalamban) | Unorganised workers with bank account | **18-40** | **₹1,000-5,000** | Own; PFRDA; support to FY 2030-31 |
| **PM-SYM** (2019; MoLE; LIC + CSC) | Workers earning **≤₹15,000/month** | 18-40 | **₹3,000** | **1:1 Centre** (₹55 at 18 to ₹200 at 40) |
| **PM-KMY** (2019; MoA&FW) | **Small and marginal farmers** | 18-40 | ₹3,000 | Equal matching |
| **NPS-Traders** (2019; MoLE) | Traders, turnover ≤₹1.5 crore | 18-40 | ₹3,000 | Matching |
- PM-SYM, PM-KMY, NPS-Traders: **spouse gets 50%** as family pension.
- APY: 8.96 crore subscribers (2025-26).
#### 9.3 NSAP and State Pensions
- **NSAP (1995)**: Ministry of **Rural Development**; gives effect to **Article 41** (DPSP); BPL households; about 94% paid by DBT (May 2026).
| Component | Eligibility | Central amount |
|---|---|---|
| **IGNWPS** | Widows 40-79 / 80+ | ₹300 / ₹500 |
| **IGNDPS** | 18-79, disability **≥80%** | ₹300 / ₹500 |
| **NFBS** | Death of breadwinner aged **18-59** | **₹20,000** lump sum |
| **Annapurna** | Elderly eligible for IGNOAPS but not drawing it | 10 kg free grain |
- **Mukhyamantri Vishwakarma Pension Yojana** (workers, street vendors, folk artists at 60) = **Rajasthan** (G1 2024).
- AP's **NTR Bharosa**: weavers, fishermen, toddy tappers, artisans, dappu artists; **₹4,000 a month**.
### 10. LABOUR SOCIAL SECURITY – EPF, ESI, CODE AND e-SHRAM
#### 10.1 EPF and ESI
| Feature | EPF | ESI |
|---|---|---|
| Coverage | Establishments with **20+** employees | ESI Act **1948** |
| Wage ceiling | **₹25,000** (raised from ₹15,000 of Sep 2014; Cabinet 17 Sep 2026) | **₹21,000** a month |
| Contribution | **12% each** (10% notified establishments) | Employer **3.25%**, employee **0.75%** |
| Benefits | **PF + EPS pension + EDLI** insurance | **Medical, sickness, maternity**, disablement, dependants' |
- **EPF Scheme, 2026** replaced EPF Scheme 1952; **Aadhaar + PAN + UAN** linking mandatory; principal employer liable for contract workers' PF.
- ESI **SPREE** (Jul 2025-Jan 2026) added 1.03 crore employees.
#### 10.2 Code on Social Security, 2020
- Merged **9 laws** (EPF, ESI, Maternity Benefit, Gratuity, Unorganised Workers' Social Security Act, etc.).
- **All four Labour Codes in force 21 November 2025**; Central Rules 2026.
- **First legal recognition** of **gig workers, platform workers and aggregators**; aggregators pay **1-2% of turnover**, capped at **5% of payouts**.
- **National Social Security Board** recommends schemes for gig workers; maternity leave **26 weeks**; **creche at 50+** employees.
#### 10.3 e-Shram
- Launched **26 August 2021** by **MoLE**; Aadhaar-verified National Database of Unorganised Workers; **12-digit UAN**.
- Age **16-59**; **excludes income-tax payers and EPFO/ESIC members**; One-Stop Solution (2024) joins 15 schemes; **31.87 crore** registrations (Aug 2026).
### 11. SOCIAL SECURITY CONCEPTS
| Social insurance | Social assistance |
|---|---|
| **Contributory**, tied to employment (EPF, ESI, APY, PM-SYM) | **Non-contributory, tax-funded, means-tested** (NSAP, NFSA, PM-JAY) |
- **ILO Convention No. 102** – Social Security (Minimum Standards) Convention, **1952**: **nine branches** – medical care, sickness, unemployment, old age, employment injury, family, maternity, invalidity, survivors. **India has not ratified it.**
- Debates: NSAP pension not inflation-linked; insurance penetration fell to 3.7% (FY25); proposed Universal Social Protection Floor (Aug 2026).
