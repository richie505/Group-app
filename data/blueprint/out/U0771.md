## U0771 | GOALS OF SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT – THE 17 SDGs, TARGETS AND INDIA'S SDG INDEX PERFORMANCE
### 1. AGENDA 2030 – ORIGIN AND ADOPTION
#### 1.1 Origin at Rio+20
- SDGs were **conceptualised** at the **UN Conference on Sustainable Development (Rio+20)**, Rio de Janeiro, **2012**.
- Rio+20 outcome **"The Future We Want"** called for global goals to **follow the MDGs**.
- **Colombia and Guatemala** first proposed the SDG idea there.
#### 1.2 Adoption
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Framework title | **"Transforming our World: the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development"** (not 2035) |
| Adopted by | **All UN Member States**, **UNGA 70th session**, **September 2015** |
| Goals / targets | **17 goals, 169 targets** (not 196) |
| Period | **2015–2030** – a **15-year plan** |
| In force | **1 January 2016** (from 2016) |
| Motto | **"Leave no one behind"** |
- A **universal call to action** to end poverty and protect the planet.
!TRAP Conceived at **Rio+20 (2012)**; adopted at **UNGA 70th session (2015)**; in force **2016**.
### 2. NATURE OF AGENDA 2030
- **Universal**: applies to **all nations** – developed, developing and least developed (MDGs were mainly for developing countries).
- **Human-rights basis**: guided by the **Universal Declaration of Human Rights (1948)** and international human-rights treaties – **not** the Paris Agreement or Magna Carta.
- Integrated: three dimensions – **economic, social, environmental**.
- **USDG vision**: universal access to **clean water, sanitation and healthcare** is essential for **human dignity**.
- In India: **NITI Aayog** coordinates and monitors SDGs (incl. the SDG India Index); **MoSPI** builds the **National Indicator Framework**.

#### 3.1 Grouping
- **Goals 1–7**: extend MDG concerns.
- **Goals 8–10**: jobs, infrastructure, distribution.
- **Last seven (11–17)**: sustainability.
#### 3.2 What is NOT an SDG Title
- **Blue Economy** (maps to SDG 14), **Green Economy**, **Digital Inclusion**, **"Life in sky"**.
- Problem → goal: **food insecurity → Goal 2**; **inadequate healthcare → Goal 3**.
!TRAP "Life in sky" and "Green Economy" are not SDGs; the two "life" goals are **Life Below Water (14)** and **Life on Land (15)**.
### 4. GOAL-SPECIFIC TARGETS
#### 4.1 Goal 4 – Quality Education
- **Free, equitable primary and secondary** education.
- **Early-childhood** care.
- **Affordable technical, vocational and tertiary** education.
- End **gender disparity** in education; **youth and adult literacy**.
- **Target 4.7**: by 2030 all learners acquire knowledge and skills to promote **sustainable development**, including **global citizenship**, a culture of **peace and non-violence** and appreciation of **cultural diversity**.
#### 4.2 Goal 5 – Gender Equality
- **End all discrimination and violence against women and girls** (PYQ answer).
- End **child marriage** and **female genital mutilation**.
- **Recognise unpaid care work**; women's **full participation in leadership**.
#### 4.3 Goal 10 – Reduced Inequalities
- Reforms needed: **better regulation of global financial markets**; **greater voice for developing countries** in global institutions; **safe and orderly migration**; **remittance costs below 3%**.
!KEY Goal 10 target = remittance costs **below 3%**.

### 6. MDGs VS SDGs
| Feature | MDGs | SDGs |
|---|---|---|
| Goals / targets | **8 / 21** | **17 / 169** |
| Period | **2000–2015** | **2015/16–2030** |
| Source | **Millennium Declaration, 2000** | 2030 Agenda, 2015 |
| Scope | **Mainly developing** countries | **Universal** |
| Focus | Poverty, basic well-being | Social, economic, environmental; "leave no one behind" |
- **MDG results**: under-5 mortality **90 → 43** per 1,000 live births; maternal mortality ratio down **45%**.
- India **achieved the MDG target** on eradicating extreme poverty and hunger (**halving extreme poverty**) before 2015.
### 7. CHRONOLOGY OF DEVELOPMENT MILESTONES
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1951 | First Five Year Plan (India) |
| 2000 | Millennium Declaration; **8 MDGs** |
| 2012 | Rio+20 – SDG idea |
| 2015 | Agenda 2030 adopted |
| 2016 | SDGs in force |
| **2018** | **First SDG India Index** (NITI Aayog) |
| 2021 | NER District SDG Index, 1st edition (Aug) |
- Sequence: **Rio+20 2012 → Agenda 2030 2015 → SDGs in force 2016 → SDG India Index 2018**.
### 8. NITI AAYOG SDG INDIA INDEX
- First edition **2018**, based on the **17 SDGs**; prepared by **NITI Aayog**.
| Edition | India composite score |
|---|---|
| 2018 (1st) | **57** |
| 2020–21 (3rd) | **66** |
| 2023–24 (4th, July 2024) | **71** |
- **2023–24**: **Kerala and Uttarakhand** top (79 each); **Bihar last**; state scores range 57–79.
- **Andhra Pradesh**: score **74**, **Front Runner**, rank **9th**; **SDG 7 – rank 1**.
- **SDG India Index 2020–21**: scores **below 50** in **Goal 2 (Zero Hunger)** and **Goal 5 (Gender Equality)**; overall score up **6 points**, driven by **Goal 6 (Clean Water)** and **Goal 7 (Clean Energy)**.
!TRAP SDG India Index = **NITI Aayog**; SDR = **UN SDSN**; NIF = **MoSPI**.
### 9. SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT REPORT (UN SDSN)
- Publisher: **UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (SDSN)**; scores **0–100**.
| Edition | India's rank |
|---|---|
| Earlier | 112th, 109th |
| SDR 2025 | **99th** – first time in **top 100** |
| **SDR 2026** | **94th** – **best ever** |
- SDR 2026: India **lags on 13 of 17** SDGs; **SDG 2** the biggest concern; still **below Bhutan, Maldives, Nepal, Sri Lanka**.
- 2025 top three: **Finland (87)**, Sweden, Denmark.
- Globally only **17%** of targets on track.
### 10. MoSPI NATIONAL INDICATOR FRAMEWORK
- **MoSPI develops the NIF**; NITI Aayog coordinates SDG work.
- **SDG-NIF Progress Report 2026**: **277 indicators** across all 17 SDGs; modelled on the UN Global Indicator Framework.
- Multidimensional poverty: **24.85% (2015–16) → 14.96% (2019–21)**.
!KEY NIF is MoSPI's, **not NITI Aayog's**.
### 11. SDG LOCALISATION
#### 11.1 NER District SDG Index
- **1st edition (2021–22)** released **August 2021** – district-level index for the North Eastern Region.
#### 11.2 Localised SDGs in Gram Panchayats
- Ministry of **Panchayati Raj**; **nine themes**, e.g. poverty-free, healthy, child-friendly, **water-sufficient**, clean and green, **self-sufficient infrastructure**, **socially just and secure**, **good governance**, **women-friendly village**.
- **"Industrially advanced village" is NOT one** of the nine themes.
#### 11.3 Andhra Pradesh
- AP **adopted all 17 SDGs**; **212 indicators** to monitor progress to 2030.
- **Swarna Andhra KPI Monitoring Framework** launched **13 December 2024**; aligns with SDGs through **Vision Monitoring Units** at **state, district and constituency** level; SDG targets built into Swarna Andhra @2047.
