- AP has about **25%** of India's beach sand mineral reserves – **second only to Tamil Nadu**; about **211 million tonnes** of India's ~**838 million tonnes**.

- Belts: **Srikakulam, Visakhapatnam** (Gara, Etcherla, Ranasthalam, Bheemunipatnam, Nakkapalli); also West Godavari, Krishna, Konaseema.
- **Placer deposits**: Godavari and Krishna bring sediment; waves and tides concentrate heavy minerals.
| Mineral | Use | AP rank |
|---|---|---|
| **Ilmenite** | Titanium dioxide – paints, plastics, paper | 2nd-largest |
| **Rutile** | Higher-grade titanium ore – titanium metal (aircraft, defence) | Largest |
| **Monazite** | Rare earths + **thorium** (nuclear); highly regulated | Largest |
| **Zircon** | Ceramics, nuclear reactors | — |
| **Garnet** | Abrasive – sandblasting, waterjet cutting | — |
| **Sillimanite** | Refractory – furnace linings, kilns | — |
- Thorium from monazite feeds India's **three-stage nuclear programme**; regulation by **CRZ** norms + **EIA**.
#### 6.5 Illegal sand mining, K. Pallepalem (Prakasam)
- Illegal beach sand mining near **K. Pallepalem beach**, **Kothapatnam mandal**, Prakasam – heavy machinery and tractors, for **shrimp hatchery** construction; similar extraction in the **Bahuda River** near the AP–Odisha border.
- Concerns: coastal erosion, groundwater salinisation, marine biodiversity loss.
- **CRZ rules** (under the **Environment Protection Act, 1986**) **prohibit mechanised beach sand mining**.
- **MMDR Act, 1957**: sand = **minor mineral**, regulated by **States** (not Centre); **Section 15** – State rules; amended 2015 (auctions).
- **Sustainable Sand Mining Management Guidelines (SSMMG), 2016** – MoEFCC; **District Survey Report** mandatory; replenishment studies; GIS monitoring; basis for **Enforcement & Monitoring Guidelines, 2020**. Alternative: **M-sand**.
- **Bahuda**: rises in the Eastern Ghats; flows Odisha to AP; an **independent east-flowing river** to the Bay of Bengal (**not a Godavari tributary**).
!TRAP Sand is a minor mineral controlled by the States, not the Centre.
