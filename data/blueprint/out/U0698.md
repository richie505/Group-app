## U0698 | NATIONAL S&T MISSIONS – QUANTUM, SUPERCOMPUTING, DEEP OCEAN, BIO-E3, ANRF
### 1. INDIAN SUPERCOMPUTER HISTORY
- India's supercomputing programme began in the **late 1980s**; India's supercomputer family is **PARAM**, designed and assembled by **C-DAC, Pune**.
- **PARAM 8000 (1991)** – **India's first** (first indigenously developed) supercomputer, C-DAC under **Vijay Bhatkar**, the **father of Indian supercomputing**.

### 2. NATIONAL SUPERCOMPUTING MISSION (NSM, 2015)
- Run jointly by **DST and MeitY**; implementing agencies **C-DAC Pune + IISc Bengaluru**.
- Machines linked on the **National Supercomputing Grid** over the **National Knowledge Network**.
- Plan: **40 systems (>64 PF)**; 37 commissioned (39 PF); 3 more on the indigenous RUDRA server (~27 PF, incl. a 20 PF National Facility).
- Indigenous stack: **Rudra server board** + **Trinetra** interconnect + open-source (AlmaLinux) software.
#### 2.1 PARAM Systems → Host
| System | Host |
|---|---|
| **PARAM Shivay** (2019) | **IIT (BHU) Varanasi** – first NSM machine (first indigenously assembled) |
| **PARAM Porul** | **NIT Tiruchirappalli** (May 2022) |
| **PARAM Pravega** | **IISc Bengaluru** |
| **PARAM Ananta** | **IIT Gandhinagar** |
| **PARAM Kamrupa** | **IIT Guwahati** – 14th machine, opened by President **Droupadi Murmu**, 13 Oct 2022 |
| **PARAM Rudra** | 3 systems: Pune (GMRT), Delhi (IUAC), Kolkata (S.N. Bose Centre) |
| **PARAM Shakti** | **IIT Madras** – 3.1 PF, Rudra board, 40% capacity for outside researchers (Jan 2026) |
| **AIRAWAT** (AI supercomputer) | **C-DAC Pune** – **75th** in Top500, **ISC 2023** (Germany, May 2023) |
!TRAP Rudra = a server board the Rudra machines are built on (not an operating system); AUM = C-DAC's separate HPC chip programme.
!TRAP PARAM Shivay is at IIT (BHU), NOT IIT Guwahati.
### 3. NATIONAL QUANTUM MISSION (NQM)
- **Qubit**: basic unit of quantum computing; through **superposition** it can be 0 and 1 together.
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Approved | Union Cabinet, **19 April 2023** |
| Outlay | **₹6,003.65 crore** |
| Period | **2023-24 to 2030-31** (8 years) |
| Nodal | **DST** |
#### 3.1 Targets
- Computing: intermediate-scale computers of **50–1000 physical qubits** in 8 years (superconducting, photonic).
- Communication: **satellite QKD over 2000 km** within India; inter-city QKD over 2000 km.
- Sensing: **magnetometers** and **atomic clocks**.
#### 3.2 Thematic Hubs → Host
| T-Hub | Host |
|---|---|
| Quantum Computing | **IISc Bengaluru** |
| Quantum Communication | **IIT Madras** |
| Quantum Sensing and Metrology | **IIT Bombay** |
| Quantum Materials and Devices | **IIT Delhi** |
- Central Fabrication Facilities: IIT Delhi, IISc, IIT Bombay, IIT Kanpur (~₹720 crore); 8 start-ups backed, e.g. **QNu Labs** (QKD), **QpiAI** (64-qubit QPU).
### 4. DEEP OCEAN MISSION
- Nodal: **Ministry of Earth Sciences**; Cabinet **June 2021**, **₹4,077 crore** for five years; six components.
- **Samudrayaan** is a sub-project of the Deep Ocean Mission – India's first crewed deep-ocean mission.
- **Matsya 6000**: indigenous crewed submersible by **NIOT Chennai**; **3 people** to **6,000 m**; 12 hours normal, 96 hours emergency endurance.
### 5. BIO-E3 POLICY (2024)
- **Bio-E3 = Biotechnology for Economy, Environment and Employment**; Cabinet **24 Aug 2024**; steered by **DBT**.
- Core aim: a **bio-manufacturing ecosystem**; India a global hub of high-performance biomanufacturing.
- Six thematic sectors: bio-based chemicals and enzymes; smart proteins; precision biotherapeutics; climate-resilient agriculture; carbon capture; marine and space research.
- Infrastructure: **Biomanufacturing hubs**, **Bio-AI hubs** and **biofoundries**.
- India's bioeconomy (2024): **over US$165 billion**.
- Budget 2026-27: **Biopharma SHAKTI**, ₹10,000 crore over 5 years (biologics, biosimilars).
### 6. ANUSANDHAN NATIONAL RESEARCH FOUNDATION (ANRF)
- Created by the **ANRF Act, 2023**; it **subsumed SERB**.
- **Prime Minister** = ex officio **President** of its Governing Board.
- Custodian of the **₹1 lakh crore RDI Fund** through a **two-tier Special Purpose Fund**.
- 6 Jun 2026: India-Germany quantum and deep-tech cooperation through **IGSTC and ANRF**.

### 8. AP QUANTUM PUSH – AMARAVATI QUANTUM VALLEY
- **Andhra Pradesh** = **first State** with its own **quantum computing policy**; aim: top-five global quantum hub by **2030**.
- Foundation stone: **Uddandarayunipalem**; 50 acres; policy drew 50+ proposals, 7 approved.
- Incentive: **100% exemption from stamp duty and registration fees** for tech firms in the Valley.
- **14 April 2026** (World Quantum Day): India's first **Quantum Reference Facility** test beds – **1S at SRM University-AP**, **1Q at Medha Towers, Gannavaram**; components from TIFR, IISc, DRDO, IIT Bombay.
- IBM-TCS quantum computer promised for 2026. Seven MoUs (Feb 2026): **IBM to train 1 lakh youth**; a NIELIT Quantum AI deemed university.
- **NIELIT Quantum and AI campus** (MeitY, ₹730.70 crore): classes from **Sept 2026** at **Acharya Nagarjuna University, Guntur**.
- PanIIT Summit on deep tech: Vijayawada, 3 Oct 2026.

9. **Pratyush** = IITM Pune; **Mihir** = NCMRWF Noida.
10. **Matsya 6000** is the submersible; **Samudrayaan** is the mission.
