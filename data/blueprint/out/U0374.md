### 2. COMPETITION COMMISSION OF INDIA
#### 2.1 Statutory Basis and Predecessor
- Statutory body under the **Competition Act, 2002**, as amended in **2007**; under the **Ministry of Corporate Affairs**.
- The Act replaced the **MRTP Act, 1969** and its **MRTP Commission**.
- Composition: **Chairperson + 2 to 6 other members**, appointed by the Central Government (original 2002 Act allowed up to 10).
!KEY APPSC 2022: "statutory body under the Competition Act 2002, as amended in 2007" was the only true statement.
#### 2.2 Three Areas of Action
| CCI area | What the CCI does |
|---|---|
| **Anti-competitive agreements** | Acts against **cartels, bid-rigging** |
| **Abuse of dominant position** | Penalises dominant firms, e.g. **WhatsApp's 2021 privacy policy** |
| **Combinations** | Reviews **mergers and acquisitions** (not cartels) |
- **₹2,000 crore deal-value threshold** targets start-up **"killer acquisitions"** (Feb 2026).
- **Meta/WhatsApp** fined **₹213.14 crore** for abuse of dominance; NCLAT upheld the abuse finding.
- CCI set up a **Digital Markets Division**; proposed **Digital Competition Bill** pre-regulates **Systemically Significant Digital Enterprises**.
#### 2.3 Appellate Forum
- Appeals to **NCLAT from 26 May 2017** (earlier **COMPAT** – Competition Appellate Tribunal), then **Supreme Court**.
### 3. FSSAI – FOOD REGULATOR
#### 3.1 Basis and Laws Replaced
- Under the **Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006**; set up **2008**.
- The Act consolidated **eight laws and orders**, including the **Prevention of Food Adulteration Act, 1954**, Fruit Products Order 1955, Meat Food Products Order 1973.
- Administered by the **Ministry of Health and Family Welfare**; an independent statutory authority.
!TRAP FSSAI is **not** under the **Director General of Health Services** (UPSC 2018).
#### 3.2 Standards, Packaging and Eat Right India
- From **1 July 2019**: **no recycled plastics or newspaper** to wrap food (APPSC 2019).
- **Eat Right India** (2018): movement for safe, healthy and sustainable food.
- **2026 reforms**: licences and registrations get **perpetual validity**.
| FSSAI tier (2026) | Turnover |
|---|---|
| Registration | Up to **₹1.5 crore** (earlier ₹12 lakh) |
| State licence | ₹1.5-50 crore |
| Central licence | Above **₹50 crore** |
### 4. TRAI AND TDSAT
- **TRAI** regulates **tariffs, interconnection and quality of service** in telecom.
- **TDSAT** (May 2000) created by the **TRAI (Amendment) Act** to **separate adjudication** (disputes, appeals) from TRAI's regulation.
- TDSAT also hears appeals under the **IT Act, 2000** and the **DPDP Act**.
- **TRAI MyCall app** revamped for rating call quality (Aug 2026), after **DND** and **MySpeed** apps.
!TRAP There is no separate "TDSAT Act, 2000".
### 5. ELECTRICITY REGULATORS
| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| **CERC** | **Inter-State** electricity matters; regulator of carbon trading under the **Carbon Credit Trading Scheme** (administered by **BEE**) |
| **SERCs** | **Intra-State tariffs** and consumer protection; coordinate with CERC |
| **APTEL** | Appeals from CERC, SERCs and **PNGRB** → then Supreme Court |
#### 5.1 AP Angle – APERC
- **APERC** constituted **1999** under the **AP Electricity Reform Act, 1998**.
- **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014**: APERC stayed joint regulator for **not more than six months** until separate SERCs were formed.
### 6. PNGRB AND AERA
- **PNGRB** regulates **City Gas Distribution (CGD)** networks and authorises **Geographical Areas**; it does **not** regulate electricity transmission.
- **AP (Jul 2026)**: PNGRB approved **10 Geographical Areas** in AP for CGD; **East Godavari** has the most connections.
- **AERA** regulates airport tariffs (aeronautical charges) at major airports.
### 7. DATA PROTECTION BOARD OF INDIA
- **DPDP Act, 2023**: enacted **August 2023**; **consent-based** processing of digital personal data.
- DPBI: independent **adjudicatory** body; **4 members** (DPDP Rules 2025); online complaints portal.
- Members appointed by the Central Government on a **Selection Committee**'s recommendation.
- Powers: penalties up to **₹250 crore**; **no imprisonment**.
- Appeal: **TDSAT within 60 days** → Supreme Court.
- **Feb 2026**: Delhi HC issued notice on a plea calling the Board and its appellate tribunal "Executive-controlled".
- Criticism: DPDP Act amends **RTI Sec. 8(1)(j)** → wider exemption for personal information.
!TRAP DPBI appeals do **not** go directly to the Supreme Court.
### 8. APPOINTMENT, REMOVAL AND APPEALS
#### 8.1 Chairperson – Appointment and Removal
- Appointed by the **Central Government**, usually on a **search-cum-selection committee**'s recommendation (not elected by Parliament). CCI's committee is headed by the **Chief Justice of India** or a nominee.
- Removed by the Central Government **only on grounds in the parent Act** and **after a hearing**: insolvency, conviction for moral turpitude, incapacity, abuse of position.
#### 8.2 Appeals Table
| Order of | Appeal to | Further |
|---|---|---|
| TRAI | TDSAT | Supreme Court |
| CCI | NCLAT (earlier COMPAT) | Supreme Court |
| CERC / SERC / PNGRB | APTEL | Supreme Court |
| DPBI | TDSAT (60 days) | Supreme Court |
### 9. PROPOSED REGULATORS AND BILLS
- **Viksit Bharat Shiksha Adhishthan (VBSA) Bill, 2025** – current form of the **HECI** proposal.
- Introduced in **Lok Sabha, Dec 2025** → **Joint Committee of Parliament**; not passed.
- Would repeal **UGC Act 1956, AICTE Act 1987, NCTE Act 1993**.
- Three councils: **Regulatory, Accreditation, Standards** (no Funding Council).
- **Digital Competition Bill** (proposed) – ex-ante rules for big digital firms.
