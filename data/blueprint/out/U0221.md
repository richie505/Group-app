## U0221 | WORKING-CLASS MOVEMENT AND AITUC; THE LEFT – COMMUNISTS, CONGRESS SOCIALIST PARTY
### 1. EARLY TRADE UNIONS
| Union | Year | Founder |
|---|---|---|
| **Madras Labour Union** – **first trade union** | **1918** | **B.P. Wadia** (with V. Kalyanasundaram); workers of **Buckingham and Carnatic Mills** |
| **Ahmedabad Textile Labour Association** (**Majoor Mahajan Sangh**) | **25 Feb 1920** (after the **1918** mill strike) | **Gandhi** with **Anasuya Sarabhai** (its president for life) |
| **Girni Kamgar Union** (Bombay) | **1928** | **S.A. Dange** and communists |
| **Hind Mazdoor Sabha** | **Calcutta, December 1948** | Socialists – **Ashok Mehta, T.S. Ramanujam, G.G. Mehta** |
- **First May Day in India**: **Madras, 1923**, by the **Labour Kisan Party of Hindustan** under **M. Singaravelu Chettiar**.
### 2. ALL INDIA TRADE UNION CONGRESS (AITUC)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Founded | **Bombay, 31 October 1920** |
| First session president | **Lala Lajpat Rai** (APPSC G2 2025) |
| Vice President | **Joseph Baptista** |
| First General Secretary | **Diwan Chaman Lal** |
| Founder | **N.M. Joshi** – co-founder, later General Secretary |
| 1929 split | Moderates broke away → **RTUC**, later **National Trades Union Federation** |
| Later | Congress set up **INTUC in 1947** |
- Both Joshi and Lajpat Rai are named among the founders.
- Latest: AITUC general secretary **Amarjeet Kaur** criticised the **nine-judge SC Bench** ruling (**Aug 2026**) that the **1978 definition of "industry"** does not apply to the new **Industrial Relations Code** (which replaced the Industrial Disputes Act).
- Unions declined after the **1982 Bombay textile strike** collapsed; peak 1950s-70s (e.g. **1974 railway strike**).
!TRAP AITUC = **1920, Bombay**; INTUC = **1947**, set up by the Congress; HMS = **1948**.
### 3. LABOUR LAWS AND COMMISSIONS
| Law | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| Bombay factory commission | 1875 | Led to the first factory law |
| **Factories Act** | **1881** | **First** factory law; **child labour** limited |
| **Factories Act** | **1891** | **Weekly holiday**; women's working hours limited |
| **Trade Unions Act** | **1926** | **Registration** and legal status for unions; shield from civil and criminal **conspiracy** suits; came from **N.M. Joshi's March 1921** resolution |
| **Trade Disputes Act** | **1929** (passed **11 April 1929**) | **Tribunals** (Board of Conciliation, Court of Inquiry); banned **sympathetic strikes** and strikes that "coerced" the Government or caused hardship; long notice periods; on trial for **five years** |
| **Public Safety Bill** | **1929** | Against **foreign communists** such as **Philip Spratt** |
| **Whitley Commission** (Royal Commission on Labour) | Appointed **1929**; reported **1931** | Labour conditions |
- **Motilal Nehru** called the Trade Disputes Act 1929 **"the Slavery of India Bill"**.
- **2026** = **100 years** of the Trade Unions Act, 1926.
!TRAP Trade Unions Act = **1926**; Trade Disputes Act = **1929**.
### 4. COMMUNIST PARTY OF INDIA
#### 4.1 Founding
| Event | Fact |
|---|---|
| Founded abroad | **Tashkent, 17 October 1920** (APPSC GS 2022), after the 2nd Comintern Congress |
| Founders | **M.N. Roy** (head), **Abani Mukherjee**, Mohd. Ali, Mohd. Shafique, **Evelyn Roy Trent**, Rosa Fitingov, **M.P.T. Acharya** |
| Formal founding in India | **Kanpur conference, 26 Dec 1925**; organiser **Satyabhakta** |
| Leaders | S.A. Dange, Muzaffar Ahmad, Shaukat Usmani, P.C. Joshi, Ajay Ghosh, A.K. Gopalan, EMS |
- CPI dates itself from **1925 Kanpur**; CPI(M) from **1920 Tashkent**.
- **CPI and RSS both 1925** (RSS: **27 Sept 1925, Nagpur, K.B. Hedgewar**).
#### 4.2 Complete Independence Demand
- First demanded by communists through open letters to the **Ahmedabad (1921)** and **Gaya (1922)** sessions.
- At **Ahmedabad 1921** it was moved by **Maulana Hasrat Mohani** and **Swami Kumarananda**.
#### 4.3 Bans and Split
| Event | Year |
|---|---|
| British ban | **23 July 1934**; lifted **1942** |
| **Revolutionary Communist Party of India** broke away | **1934**, **Saumyendra Nath Tagore** |
| "People's War" line | From **June 1941** |
| **Banned in Hyderabad State** | **1946** (APPSC G2 2008) |
| Split | **1964** → CPI and CPI(M) |
### 5. CONSPIRACY CASES AGAINST COMMUNISTS
| Case | Year | Accused / consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Peshawar** | **1922-27** (five trials) | **Muhajirs** trained in **Moscow and Tashkent**, arrested on return across the North-West Frontier; set the tone for later British action |
| **Kanpur Bolshevik** | **1924** | **M.N. Roy, Muzaffar Ahmad, S.A. Dange, Shaukat Usmani, Singaravelu Chettiar, Nalini Gupta**; Muzaffar, Dange, Usmani, Gupta **jailed** |
| **Meerut** | **1929** | **31** labour and communist leaders (Dange, Muzaffar Ahmad, Usmani, **Philip Spratt**); the long trial made them martyrs |
!TRAP **M.A. Ansari** was NOT associated with the Kanpur case. Kanpur 1924 = conspiracy case; Kanpur 1925 = CPI founding.
### 6. CONGRESS SOCIALIST PARTY (1934)
#### 6.1 All-India CSP
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Founded | **Patna, May 1934** (during the Congress Committee meeting) |
| Policies fixed | **Bombay, October 1934** (not formed January 1934) |
| President | **Acharya Narendra Dev** |
| General Secretary | **Jayaprakash Narayan** (Convenor at Patna) |
| Founders | Narendra Dev, JP, **Ram Manohar Lohia**, **Ashok Mehta** and others |
| Programme | Socialism inside the Congress; work among workers and peasants |
- **Lohia** = founder, not General Secretary. **Nehru did not formally join** the CSP.
#### 6.2 Andhra Unit (Andhra Socialist Party)
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Founded | **23 June 1934, Vijayawada** (APPSC 2017) |
| First President | **Madduri Annapurnaiah** (APPSC 2019 key) |
| First session | **1935, Guntur**, presided by **Tenneti Viswanatham** |
| Members | **Alluri Satyanarayana Raju, Krovvidi Lingaraju, P. Sundarayya, Chandra Rajeswara Rao** |
- Other accounts make **N.G. Ranga** President with Annapurnayya as secretary; follow the **2019 key**.
- **Madduri Annapurnayya** later led the **Forward Bloc** Andhra branch.
- **Andhra Swaraj Party** – **12 Feb 1934, Vijayawada**; **Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao**.
- **Rajakeeya Pathashala** – set up by **Ranga** in **1939** at **Kankipadu** (APPSC G2 2012).
!TRAP All-India CSP = **Patna** (Narendra Dev); Andhra unit = **Vijayawada** (Annapurnaiah).
