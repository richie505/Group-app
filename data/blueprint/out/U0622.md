### 2. TROPIC OF CANCER
- Passes through **8 states**, west to east: **Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, Mizoram**.
- **Andhra Pradesh lies wholly south** of it; it does not cross present AP.
!TRAP Odisha and Bihar are near the Tropic of Cancer but it does not pass through them.
### 3. STANDARD MERIDIAN
- **Indian Standard Meridian: 82°30′ E**; IST = **UTC + 5:30**.
- Reference place: **Mirzapur** (Uttar Pradesh), near Prayagraj.
- Passes through **5 states** (north to south): **Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, Andhra Pradesh**.
- Chosen because it lies roughly midway between India's western and eastern longitudes.

#### 4.3 Neighbours of Neighbours and Other Facts
- **Afghanistan** borders Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan (north), China (Wakhan, north-east), India (106 km), Pakistan (east, south), Iran (west) – **no border with Russia**.
- **Myanmar** borders (besides India, China) **Thailand, Laos, Bangladesh**; total border 6,522 km.
- **SAARC** includes all neighbours **except China and Myanmar**. **Maldives** = smallest South Asian country.
- **Gwadar Port**: Pakistan (Balochistan, Arabian Sea), developed with China.
- Kanchenjunga – answer Nepal. Mahabharat Range (Lesser Himalaya): answer key gave Bhutan, though usually placed in Nepal.
- **KSLCDI** (Kailash Sacred Landscape Conservation and Development Initiative): India, China, Nepal.
- **Seven Sisters**: Arunachal, Assam, Meghalaya, Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland, Tripura – not West Bengal.
- Indian Himalayan Region: HP, Uttarakhand, Sikkim, Arunachal, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Tripura, J&K, Ladakh – **not Uttar Pradesh**.
- **Purvanchal**: Patkai Bum, Naga Hills, Arakan Yoma continuation.
- Tribes: Tharu – answer key Arunachal Pradesh; **Limbu** (Yakthung) – Sikkim, WB, Nepal, Bhutan, Tibet; Karbi – Assam; Dongaria Kondh, Bonda – Odisha.
- Among options, the **Nile** forms an international border.
### 5. BOUNDARY LINES AND DISPUTED TRACTS
| Line | Between | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Radcliffe Line** | India–Pakistan (and Bangladesh) | **1947** | Sir Cyril Radcliffe; Partition |
| **McMahon Line** | India–China (Arunachal) | **1914** | Sir Henry McMahon; **Simla Convention, 3 July 1914** (Britain–Tibet); east of Nepal |
| **Durand Line** | British India–Afghanistan; now Pakistan–Afghanistan | **1893** | Sir Mortimer Durand; buffer against Russia |
| **Line of Control** | India–Pakistan (J&K) | **1972** | 1949 ceasefire line renamed under the **Simla Agreement** |
| **Line of Actual Control** | India–China | Post-1962 | De facto; western, middle (625 km), eastern (1,140 km) sectors |
| **Johnson Line** | Aksai Chin | — | India's line (Aksai Chin Indian) |
| **McDonald Line** | Aksai Chin | — | China's line |
| **Maginot Line** | France–Germany | — | Not an Indian line |
- China calls the McMahon Line illegal.
- **Shaksgam Valley**: part of PoK, **5,180 sq km** ceded by Pakistan to China in **1963**; India opposes Chinese construction there.
- **Sir Creek**: India–Pakistan dispute; 96 km tidal estuary in the Rann of Kutch, between Gujarat and Sindh.
- India–Pakistan boundary = **superimposed boundary** (set by an outside power).
!TRAP LoC separates India and Pakistan; "LoC = India–China (1972)" is wrong.
### 6. COASTLINE
| Measure | Figure |

| **Revised coastline** | **11,098.81 km** (2023-24 remeasurement, MHA; APPSC 2025) |

- **4 coastal UTs**: Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep, Puducherry, Daman & Diu; excluding island UTs = **9 states + 2 UTs**. Chandigarh is landlocked.
- **Daman and Diu**: Gulf of Khambhat lies between them; capital Daman; under the **Portuguese** until liberation in **1961**.
### 7. SEAS AND ISLANDS AROUND INDIA
| Water body | Position |
|---|---|
| Arabian Sea | West |
| Bay of Bengal | East |
| Indian Ocean | South |
| **Lakshadweep (Laccadive) Sea** | South-west |
| Andaman Sea | East of A&N Islands |
| Gulf of Mannar | Between SE India and Sri Lanka |
- **Sea neighbours**: Sri Lanka and Maldives (two share only sea borders). **A&N** share maritime borders with Myanmar, Thailand, Indonesia – not Sri Lanka.
- **Lakshadweep**: **36 coral** islands (not volcanic); capital **Kavaratti**; earlier Laccadive, Minicoy and Amindive; near the **Malabar** coast of Kerala (not Coromandel). Maldives ~125 km south of it.
- **Andaman and Nicobar**: between Bay of Bengal and Andaman Sea; number of islands – **200** (key). **Tilanchong** belongs to A&N, not the Laccadives.

- India and Sri Lanka are separated by the **Palk Strait and the Gulf of Mannar** (APPSC 2025); Gulf of Mannar lies south of the Palk Strait.
- **Adam's Bridge (Ram Setu)**: chain of limestone shoals; bounds **Palk Bay** in the south.
- **Sethusamudram Project**: links Gulf of Mannar with Palk Bay; 167 km deep-water channel (proposed).
- **Persian Gulf** has no Oman coast; bordered by Iran, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait, Iraq.
