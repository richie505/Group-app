## U0039 | MAURYAN ECONOMY, SOCIETY, URBAN LIFE AND ART – PILLARS, SARNATH CAPITAL, BARABAR CAVES, DIDARGANJ YAKSHI
### 1. ECONOMY AND STATE CONTROL
- **Sita** = crown land farmed directly for the state under the **Sitadhyaksha**, using **slaves and hired labour** (not only free peasants).
- **Bhaga** (land revenue) = **1/4 to 1/6** (Megasthenes), collected by royal officials; **Kara** = tax on orchards.
- Rainfall measured in **drona** near granaries (records predate Korea's Cheugugi gauge, 1441 CE).
- Famine relief: seed, cattle, tools.
- **State monopoly/control**: mining, forestry, salt, liquor, arms, metallurgy.
- Interest **15% a year** (Arthashastra); debt relief for students, poor, sick.
- Pottery: **NBPW** (Northern Black Polished Ware).
### 2. GUILDS, COINS AND CURRENCY
- **Shrenis** (guilds), merchants (shreshthis) and artisans funded early Buddhist centres, including in **Andhra**; Arthashastra counts **shreni troops** among army types.
| Coin | Metal |
|---|---|
| **Pana (karshapana)** | **Silver** (APPSC G2 2011) |
| Mashaka | Copper |
| **Kakani** | Copper, **1/4 mashaka** |
| Suvarna | Gold |
- Currency: **punch-marked silver coins** with symbols (peacocks, hills), **no portraits**; go back to the Mahajanapada age (6th c. BCE).
- Punch-marked coins + NBPW found as far as Bangladesh, Odisha, Andhra, Karnataka.
!TRAP Mauryan silver coin = **Pana**, not Niska, Kakini or Shatamana.
### 3. TRADE ROUTES AND PORTS
| Route / port | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Uttarapatha** | Northern highway: **Taxila → Pataliputra → Tamralipti** |
| Royal highway | North-west to Pataliputra |
| **Dakshinapatha** | Great southern route; **Krishna–Godavari delta** a node |
| **Tamralipti** | East coast, main port of the **Ganga basin** (APPSC 2012) |
| **Bharukachchha (Broach), Sopara** | West coast |
| **Berenice (Bernis)** | Red Sea port – trade with **Egypt** |
- Caravans led by a **Sarthavaha**; state took road tax. Exports: diamonds, pearls, muslin, fine textiles.
### 4. SOCIETY – MEGASTHENES AND THE ARTHASHASTRA
#### 4.1 Megasthenes' Indica
- Megasthenes, Greek envoy of **Seleucus**, wrote **Indica** (Greek).
- **Seven classes** (BPSC 2017): philosophers, farmers, herdsmen/hunters, artisans and traders, soldiers, overseers/spies, councillors and assessors.
- Hereditary jobs, endogamy; **no slavery**; no written law; two types of philosophers – **Brahmins and Shramanas**.
#### 4.2 Slavery and Labour
- Kautilya: **nine categories of slaves (dasas)**; Ashokan inscriptions also mention dasas.
- Sources: birth, sale, war capture, judicial punishment. **No Arya could be made a slave**.
- Female slave who bore her master a son → **free**; son gets the master's son's legal status (UPSC 2022).
- Penalty for selling a pregnant slave without maternity care; freedom by payment.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Karmakara | Wage labour |
| **Vishti** | Unpaid forced labour → Telugu **Vetti** |
| **Moksha** | Divorce |
| **Stridhana** | Woman's own property (jewellery, maintenance) |
| Anuloma / pratiloma | Inter-varna marriages |
!TRAP Megasthenes: no slaves; Kautilya: nine categories of slaves.
#### 4.3 Women, Caste and Learning
- Divorce and **remarriage** allowed to a wife **deserted** by her husband; also for long stay abroad, physical handicap, mutual enmity. **Widow remarriage** allowed.
- **Antyavasayins** = five mixed castes outside Aryan society; **Sishtas** = learned men; Shudras could own land; **Chandalas, Shvapakas** grew.
- Centres of learning: **Taxila** (most famous), Ujjain, Varanasi.
- Arthashastra (polity, wealth) is compared to Machiavelli's **The Prince**.
### 5. RELIGIOUS CONDITIONS
| Faith | Patron / evidence |
|---|---|
| **Jainism** | **Chandragupta** followed **Bhadrabahu** to **Shravanabelagola** |
| **Ajivikas** | Ashoka and **Dasaratha** gave Barabar/Nagarjuni caves; PE VII names Ajivikas and Nirgranthas separately |
| Buddhism | Ashoka – stupas, Sangha |
| Brahmanism | Continued; Kautilya a Brahmana (**dvijarshabh**) |
- Policy: liberal and tolerant, yet varna and ashrama protected.
### 6. PATALIPUTRA – PALACE AND CITY
- City mainly of **timber** – wooden wall with towers and gates; polished stone pillars only in key buildings.
- Megasthenes compared the palace to **Susa and Ecbatana**; **Fa-Hien** (5th c. CE) admired it.
| Site | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Kumrahar** | **80-pillared hall** (8 × 10), polished pillars, wooden floors |
| **Bulandibagh** | Wooden **palisade** |
- Excavated by **D.B. Spooner**.
### 7. MAURYAN PILLARS AND CAPITALS
#### 7.1 Features
- **Monolithic**, free-standing, round shafts of **Chunar sandstone**, **no base**, narrowing upwards; "**Mauryan polish**" (metallic shine).
- Pillars are **independent monuments**, not parts of buildings.
- Order: shaft → bell-shaped inverted lotus (**ghanta**) → abacus → animal.
#### 7.2 Capital → Site
| Capital | Site |
|---|---|
| **Four lions** + Dharmachakra | **Sarnath** – **National Emblem** |
| **Bull** | **Rampurva** |
| **Single lion** | **Lauriya Nandangarh** (Champaran) |
| **Elephant** | **Sankisa** |
#### 7.3 Persian Influence Debate
| Achaemenid similarity | Mauryan difference |
|---|---|
| Polish, animal capitals, edict idiom "lipi/dipi" | **No base**, smooth shaft, distinct lotus bell, round abacus |
- Verdict: an **Indian adaptation**.
### 8. COURT ART AND POPULAR ART
- **Royal (court) art**: palaces, pillars, stupas, caves. **Folk art**: yaksha-yakshi statues, terracotta.
| Figure | Findspot |
|---|---|
| **Chauri-bearer Yakshi** (polished) | **Didarganj** (Patna) |
| **Manibhadra Yaksha** (inscribed) | **Parkham** (Mathura) – RPSC 2021 |
| Yakshi | **Besnagar** (Vidisha) |
| Yaksha | **Noh** (Bharatpur) |
| Rock-cut **elephant** | **Dhauli** (Daya river, near Bhubaneswar) |
| Nude male torso | **Lohanipur** (Patna) |
- Yakshas: earliest large human sculptures, revered in **Jainism, Buddhism and Hinduism**.
- Kanaganahalli Ashoka slab is **Satavahana-era**, not Mauryan.
!KEY APPSC G1 2024: Dhauli elephant, Didarganj Yakshi, Sanchi stupa, Kumrahar hall – **all four Mauryan**.
### 9. STUPAS
- Stupa idea predates Buddhism (Rig Veda, Atharva Veda). Ashoka redistributed relics of the **8** original stupas into **84,000**.
| Stupa | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Sanchi** (Kaknam) | Raisen, MP, west of Betwa; **Ashoka's brick core**; Sunga stone casing; Satavahana toranas; **best architecturally**; not linked to an event of Buddha's life |
| Sanchi No. 2 / No. 3 | Dhamma Mahamatras / **Sariputta and Moggallana** |
| **Bharhut** | Satna; **Sunga**, c. 150 BC; found by **Cunningham** |
| **Dharmarajika** | Sarnath and Taxila |
| Dhamek | Sarnath; Ashokan original, Gupta present form |
| Kanakamuni | Niglihawa, Nepal; enlarged in 14th year |
| Ranabhar | Kushinagar; built by Mallas |
| **Kesariya** | East Champaran; **largest** (about 104 ft) |
| Amaravati | Krishna right bank, AP; traced by **Colin Mackenzie, 1797** |
- Chronological order asked: Dhamek, Bharhut, Amaravati, Sanchi.
- Sanchi gateway: Ashoka visits the Bodhi tree with two queens.
### 10. BARABAR AND NAGARJUNI CAVES
- **Jehanabad**, Bihar (near Gaya); **granite**, mirror polish; given by **Ashoka and Dasaratha** to **Ajivikas**.
- **Oldest surviving rock-cut caves** (3rd c. BCE) – not Badami, Ajanta, Ellora.
| Hill | Caves |
|---|---|
| **Barabar (4)** | **Lomas Rishi**, Sudama, Viswamitra, Karna Chopar |
| **Nagarjuni (3)** | Vahiyaka, **Gopika** (largest), Vadathika |
- 4 + 3 = **Satghar**. **Three Barabar caves** carry Ashoka's Brahmi inscriptions ("King Piyadasi").
- **Lomas Rishi**: earliest stone **ogee arch**, copying a thatched hut.
- In news (27 May 2026): called the "blueprint" of Indian rock-cut architecture.
- AP: Guntupalli chaitya compared with Barabar.
!TRAP Nagarjuni caves are in Bihar, not Guntur (Nagarjunakonda); inscriptions are 3rd c. BCE, not 6th c. (UPPSC 2017).

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- Oldest stone structure: **Sanchi** (APPSC 2018; vs Ajanta, Konark, Brihadeeswara).
- Earliest rock-cut caves: **Barabar**; earliest ogee arch: Lomas Rishi.
- Earliest of Dhauli elephant, Udayagiri Varaha (5th c.), Mahabalipuram (7th–8th c.), Lingaraja (10th–11th c.): **Dhauli**.
