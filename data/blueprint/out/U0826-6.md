### 1. PADMA AWARDS – RULES AND ORDER
- Instituted **1954**; announced on **Republic Day** (eve, 25 January) through a **Ministry of Home Affairs notification**.
- Conferred by the **President** at **Rashtrapati Bhavan** (around March–April).
- Annual limit **120** (excluding **posthumous** and **foreigners**).
- **Posthumous** award normally not given – allowed only if **death within one year**.
- **No cash prize**.
| Award | Service recognised | Rank among civilian awards |
|---|---|---|
| **Padma Vibhushan** | Exceptional and distinguished service | **2nd** (after **Bharat Ratna**) |
| **Padma Bhushan** | Distinguished service of high order | 3rd |
| **Padma Shri** | Distinguished service in any field | 4th |
- **Fields:** Arts, Social Work, Public Affairs, Science and Engineering, Trade and Industry, Medicine, Literature and Education, Civil Services, Sports, Others.
- Earlier Padma facts: **2004** Padma awards – **Narlikar** (science), **Amrita Pritam** (literature), **Venkatachalaiah** (law/judiciary); odd one out **M.V. Kamath**. **Chandra Kochhar** (CEO & MD, **ICICI Bank**) – **Padma Bhushan 2011**, trade and industry.
!TRAP Precedence: Padma Vibhushan > Padma Bhushan > Padma Shri; Padma Vibhushan is 2nd only to Bharat Ratna.

- **Prof. Amartya Sen** is **not** a Jnanpith recipient (the award is for Indian literature).

#### 11.1 Netaji – background
- Born **23 January 1897**; died **18 August 1945**, air crash near **Taihoku (Taiwan)**.
- **125th birth anniversary** (23 January 2022) – statue near **India Gate** (sculptor **Arun Yogiraj**).
- **Forward Bloc** founded **3 May 1939**; **Indian National Army** (1943–45).
- **Azad Hind Government:** **21 October 1943, Singapore** – recognised by 9 countries; Bose was PM, Foreign and Defence Minister.
- **30 December 1943:** Tricolour hoisted at **Port Blair**.
- **6 July 1944:** first to call Gandhi "**Father of the Nation**" (radio, Singapore).
- **2018:** Ross Island renamed **Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose Island**.
- Azad Hind Fauj originally formed **29 October 1915** by Raja Mahendra Pratap Singh, Rash Behari Bose, Niranjan Singh Gill; INA handed to Bose by **Rash Behari Bose** on **4 July 1943**.
