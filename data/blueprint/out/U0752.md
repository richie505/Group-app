### 2. FIRSTS, LARGEST AND THE PHWR PROGRAMME
- **First NPP**: Tarapur (near Bombay), 2 BWRs, **1969**.
- **First PHWR**: RAPS-1 (with AECL, Canada; modelled on Douglas Point), **1972**.
- **First indigenous 700 MW**: KAPP-3, Kakrapar (2023).
- **Largest operating**: Kudankulam 2,000 MW → Kakrapar 1,840 MW; **largest planned**: Jaitapur 9,900 MW.
- PHWR sizes: **220 → 540 → 700 MW**, all indigenised (Apr 2026).
#### 2.1 Fleet Mode (2017)
- **Ten 700 MW PHWRs**: **Kaiga 5-6** (Karnataka), **Gorakhpur 3-4** (Haryana), **Chutka 1-2** (MP), **Mahi Banswara 1-4** (Rajasthan).
- Aim: cut construction time from **13 to 8 years**.
#### 2.2 NPCIL and BHAVINI
- **NPCIL**: PSE under DAE, registered **September 1987**; holds equity in **BHAVINI**.
- **PFBR** (500 MWe, Kalpakkam): **developed by BHAVINI** (APPSC G1 2024 key); designed by IGCAR.

### 6. CIVIL NUCLEAR AGREEMENTS AND NSG WAIVER
- **NSG waiver (September 2008)**: allowed civil nuclear trade (uranium, reactors); ended **34 years** of exclusion.
- **India–US 123 Agreement (2008)**: named after **Section 123 of the US Atomic Energy Act, 1954**; **Manmohan Singh** and **George W. Bush**.
| Partner | Agreement / fact |
|---|---|
| **France** | **First post-waiver deal, 30 September 2008**; lifetime fuel guarantee; Jaitapur |
| USA | 123 Agreement (2008); Kovvada |
| Russia | 1988 accord; DAE–Rosatom "Strategic Vision" (Dec 2014); **Action Plan** (5 Oct 2018, 19th Summit, New Delhi) – second site for Russian reactors |
| Australia | Signed **2014**, in force 2015; uranium under IAEA safeguards; Administrative Arrangement (Jul 2026) |
| Japan | **2016** |
- Uranium imports: **Kazakhstan ~80%**, plus Russia, Uzbekistan, Canada, Australia (Dec 2025).
### 7. IAEA AND SAFEGUARDS
- **IAEA**: founded **1957**; HQ **Vienna, Austria**; autonomous body in the UN system; ~180 members; **India a founding member**.
- Reports safeguards non-compliance to the **UN Security Council**.
- **2006 separation plan**: civil and military facilities separated; civil reactors under IAEA safeguards so NSG members could supply fuel.
- **Why some reactors are under safeguards**: they use **imported uranium**; others use domestic fuel.
- **India-Specific Safeguards Agreement**: signed **2 February 2009**.
- **Additional Protocol**: signed **2009**, in force **2014**; wider inspection access, only **declared civilian facilities**.
### 8. NSG, EXPORT-CONTROL REGIMES AND TREATIES
#### 8.1 Nuclear Suppliers Group
- Formed **1975** ("**London Club**") in response to **Pokhran-I (1974)**; **48 members**; consensus; **no permanent secretariat**; Iran is not a member.
- India applied **2016**; blocked mainly by **China** (India not in NPT). Australia reaffirmed support (Jul 2026).
- Benefits: **technology access + say in rule-making**; NOT automatic NPT membership.
- India's regimes: **MTCR 2016 → Wassenaar 2017 → Australia Group 2018**; NSG pending.
#### 8.2 Treaties
| Treaty | Content | India |
|---|---|---|
| **NPT** (signed 1968, in force 1970) | Only P5 are weapon states | **Never signed** – discriminatory |
| **CTBT** (1996) | Bans all test explosions | Not signed – no time-bound disarmament; subcritical tests allowed |
| **PTBT** (1963) | Bans tests in air, space, underwater | **Signed** |
| **TPNW** (in force 2021) | First binding ban on nuclear weapons | Not signed |
| SALT II | US–USSR arms limitation, **1979** (not 1967) | – |
- Doctrine: **No First Use**.
!TRAP India never signed the NPT; "signed in 2005" is a trap.
### 9. LIABILITY LAW – CLNDA 2010 TO SHANTI ACT 2025
#### 9.1 CLNDA 2010
- Operator liability **no-fault**, capped at **₹1,500 crore**; Centre pays beyond the cap.
- **Section 17(b)**: operator's right of recourse against suppliers for defective equipment; **Section 46** kept other civil laws open.
- Indian Nuclear Insurance Pool; Nuclear Damage Claims Commission; Nuclear Liability Fund.
#### 9.2 SHANTI Act 2025
- Introduced in Lok Sabha **15 Dec 2025**; assent **20 Dec 2025**.
- **Repealed Atomic Energy Act 1962 and CLNDA 2010**; ended NPCIL monopoly – Indian private firms and JVs can build, own, operate; foreign firms excluded, FDI capped at 49%.
| Facility | Liability cap |
|---|---|
| Large | ₹3,000 crore |
| Medium | ₹1,500 crore |
| SMR | ₹100 crore |
- Total cap **300 million SDR** (~₹3,900 crore); excess via **Nuclear Liability Fund**. Appeals: **APTEL**.
- Only amended **Section 4 of the Patents Act** (peaceful nuclear patents).
- Government keeps **enrichment, spent-fuel reprocessing, heavy water**.
- **Aug 2026**: Supreme Court (Bench headed by **CJI Surya Kant**) issued notice on a petition challenging the liability caps.
!PAIR CLNDA | Sec 17(b) | Supplier recourse || SHANTI | 2025 | Supplier liability removed
### 10. NUCLEAR SAFETY – AERB AND ZONES
#### 10.1 AERB
- Set up **15 November 1983** by **executive order** under the Atomic Energy Act 1962; worked under the **DAE** (the promoter) – the **independence debate**.
- **NSRA Bill** never enacted; **SHANTI Act 2025** gave the AERB **statutory** status, answerable to Parliament (Jan 2026).
- Powers: licensing plants and fuel-cycle facilities; inspections; can suspend or revoke licences; **radiation safety in medical and industrial uses**; waste; transport of radioactive material.
#### 10.2 Zones Around a Reactor
| Zone | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Exclusion zone** | **1–1.5 km** radius; no public habitation |
| **Emergency Planning Zone** | **16 km**; concentric zones for protective actions |
!KEY APPSC GS 2022 key: exclusion zone = 1 km.
#### 10.3 Safety Practice
- **Defence in Depth** (multiple independent protective layers); **ALARA** = As Low As Reasonably Achievable.
- Dose limits: **20 mSv/year** (5-year average); max **30 mSv** in a year; **100 mSv** cumulative.
- **Post-Fukushima**: 700 MW PHWRs carry advanced safety features; releases <1% of AERB limits (2026).
- **Convention on Nuclear Safety**: adopted **1994**, in force **1996**; India a party; requires on-site and off-site emergency plans.
