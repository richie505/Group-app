## U0287 | CULTURAL AND EDUCATIONAL RIGHTS – ARTICLES 29-30; THE RIGHT TO PROPERTY AND THE 44TH AMENDMENT
### 1. CULTURAL AND EDUCATIONAL RIGHTS – ARTICLES 29 AND 30
- **Two Articles**: Art. 29 – **protection of interests of minorities**; Art. 30 – minorities' right to **establish and administer educational institutions**.
#### 1.1 Article 29
| Clause | Provision |
|---|---|
| 29(1) | Any **section of citizens** with a **distinct language, script or culture** has the right to **conserve** it (not limited to minorities) |
| 29(2) | No citizen denied **admission** to a State-maintained or **State-aided** institution on grounds only of **religion, race, caste, language** |
#### 1.2 Article 30
| Clause | Provision |
|---|---|
| **30(1)** | **All minorities, religious or linguistic**, may establish and administer educational institutions **of their choice** – the "**Charter of Education Rights**"; madrasas run under it |
| **30(1A)** (**44th Amendment, 1978**) | On **compulsory acquisition** of a minority institution's property, the **amount fixed must not restrict or abrogate** the Art. 30 right |
- Art. 30 is **not absolute**: the State may regulate **standards**, but may **not take away the minority character**.
- **Pramati Educational and Cultural Trust v. Union of India (2014)**: the RTE Act's **25% quota does not apply to minority schools**.
- Critics: misuse of minority status to escape RTE; only **8.76%** disadvantaged enrolments in minority schools (NCPCR, Sep 2025).
- **Art. 350A**: every State and local authority to provide **instruction in the mother tongue at the primary stage** for children of linguistic minority groups.
!TRAP IYB 2026 prints the mother-tongue clause as "Article 360A" – a misprint; it is Article 350A.
### 2. WHO IS A MINORITY?
- "Minority" is used in **Arts. 29, 30, 350A, 350B** but **not defined** in the Constitution; **Article 30 does not define it**; the **NCM Act, 1992** does not define it either. "Linguistic minority" is also undefined.
- A minority = any community **notified by the Centre** (Jul 2026); NCM's definition is mainly religious.
- Test: **numerical inferiority**, **non-dominant** status, distinct identity; **unit = the State** (**T.M.A. Pai**).
| Basis | Determined by |
|---|---|
| **Religious** minority | NCM, Ministry of Minority Affairs – national notification |
| **Linguistic** minority | **State-wise** |
- **NCM**: **statutory** body under the NCM Act, 1992.
- **Six notified minorities**: **Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Parsis, Jains** (Apr 2026). Linguistic minorities are not among these religious categories.
- National notification brings a group within NCM and central schemes (scholarships, **PMJVK**, **PM VIKAS**).
- 10 Mar 2026: Kerala **Malayalam Bhasha Bill** (assented Mar 2026) contested as harming the **Kannada** linguistic minority in **Kasaragod**.

### 4. RIGHT TO PROPERTY – THE JOURNEY
| Stage | Position |
|---|---|
| **1950** | **FR**: Art. **19(1)(f)** (acquire, hold, dispose of property) + Art. **31** (no deprivation save by authority of law; acquisition for public purpose with compensation) – the most disputed FR |
| **1st Amendment, 1951** | **Arts. 31A, 31B** and **Ninth Schedule** to shield **land reform** laws |
| 4th (1955), 17th (1964), 25th (1971) | Narrowed compensation; **17th**: land under **personal cultivation** acquired only on paying **market value** |
| **44th Amendment, 1978** | **Deleted 19(1)(f) and 31** from Part III → **Art. 300A**, **Part XII, Chapter IV** – a legal (constitutional) right |
- Removed mainly so property rights would not block **land reform**.
- **FRs: originally seven, now six**.
#### 4.1 Saving Clauses – 31A, 31B, 31C
| Article | Inserted by | Saves |
|---|---|---|
| **31A** | 1st, 1951 | Laws on **acquisition of estates** etc. |
| **31B** | 1st, 1951 | Acts in the **Ninth Schedule** – immune from FR challenge |
| **31C** | **25th, 1971** | Laws giving effect to **Arts. 39(b), 39(c)**; 42nd extended it to all DPSPs – struck down in **Minerva Mills (1980)** |
- **I.R. Coelho (2007)**, 9 judges: Ninth Schedule laws inserted **after 24 April 1973** are open to **basic structure** review.
### 5. ARTICLE 300A AND LARR ACT 2013
- Text: "**No person shall be deprived of his property save by authority of law**".
- Available to **all persons** – citizens, non-citizens, **companies**. Enforced via **Art. 226**/ordinary suit, **not Art. 32**.
- **AP angle**: **GGR Housing v. State** (AP High Court, May 2026) – strict statutory due process in land acquisition under Art. 300A.
!TRAP PYQ G1 2019 (State cannot dispossess except by law): key said "Article 300"; correct is Article 300A.
- **Land Acquisition Act, 1894**: made by British rulers; in effect **1 March 1894**; purpose was **not land reform** but acquisition for public institutions, organisations and companies.
- **LARR Act, 2013** replaced the 1894 Act; **Social Impact Assessment compulsory** (a feature, not the reason it replaced the 1894 Act); compensation up to **4x** market value (**rural**), **2x** (**urban**); **unused land returned after 5 years**.
### 6. FUNDAMENTAL vs CONSTITUTIONAL / LEGAL RIGHT
- **Every FR is a constitutional right, but not every constitutional right is an FR.** A **legal right** comes from ordinary law.
| Right | Status | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Property** | Legal (constitutional/statutory) right | **Art. 300A** |
| **Vote** (and to be elected) | **Legal/statutory** right – not FR | Art. **326** (adult suffrage, 18+); **Sec. 62 RPA 1951**; registration Sec. 19 RPA 1950 |
| **Information** | Legal right (RTI Act, 2005); also read into **19(1)(a)** | — |
| **Work** | **DPSP** | **Art. 41** |
| **Education** (6-14) | **Fundamental Right** | Art. 21A |
- **Kuldip Nayar v. Union of India (2009)**: voting is only a **statutory/legal** right.
- **Voting paradox**: voting is statutory, yet the **right to know candidate details, NOTA and ballot secrecy** are recognised under **Art. 19(1)(a)**.

- First amendment to protect land reforms: **1st (1951)** – 31A, 31B, Ninth Schedule.
- Only FR ever deleted: **right to property**.
- 30(1A) is the only compensation guarantee left for property – for minority institutions.
