### 1. SHANTI ACT, 2025 – NUCLEAR SECTOR OPENED
#### 1.1 The Act
- Full name: **Sustainable Harnessing and Advancement of Nuclear Energy for Transforming India (SHANTI) Act, 2025** (Bill introduced in Lok Sabha, passed December 2025).
- **Replaces two laws**: the **Atomic Energy Act, 1962** and the **Civil Liability for Nuclear Damage (CLND) Act, 2010** – one unified framework.
- **Ends the NPCIL monopoly** – **first-ever private and foreign participation** in nuclear power generation.
- Private/foreign firms may **build, own, operate, decommission** plants, manufacture equipment, make fuel and **enrich U-235 up to the prescribed limit**.
- **Kept for the Centre**: enrichment beyond the limit, **spent fuel management**, fuel production, **heavy water**, radioactive waste, reprocessing, transport of sensitive materials.
- **AERB** (Atomic Energy Regulatory Board, set up **1983**) gets **statutory status**, accountable to Parliament.
- **Section 39**: Centre can declare nuclear information **"restricted"** – overrides the **RTI Act**.
- Bodies: **Atomic Energy Redressal Advisory Council**; **Nuclear Damage Claims Commission**; Claims Commissioners; Atomic Disputes Tribunal; appeals to the **Appellate Tribunal for Electricity** (Electricity Act 2003); draft Rules 2026.
#### 1.2 Liability
| Item | SHANTI Act |
|---|---|
| Operator liability cap | **300 million SDR**; graded by reactor size/capacity |
| Supplier liability | **Eliminated** (CLNDA allowed recourse against suppliers) |
| Above the cap | **Nuclear Liability Fund** |
| Exemption | "Grave natural disasters" |
| Overall ceiling | About **₹3,900 crore** |
!TRAP Supplier liability is **eliminated**, not retained. CLND Act 2010 = **no-fault** liability.
#### 1.3 Background and Targets
- **Atomic Energy Act, 1962 replaced the 1948 Act** (not the reverse); amended 1986, 1987, **2015** (JVs of government companies).
- Nuclear share ~**3%** of electricity; reactor principle – **controlled nuclear fission**.
- Missed targets: 10 GW by 2000 (got 2.86 GW); 20 GW by 2020 (got 6.78 GW).
- **IAEA safeguards** apply to reactors using **imported uranium**.

### 4. JUDICIARY – APPOINTMENTS, NJAC, REFORMS
#### 4.1 Judges Cases and NJAC
| Case | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **First Judges (1981)** | **Executive primacy** |
| **Second Judges (1993)** | **Created the collegium** |
| **Third Judges (1998)** | Collegium = **CJI + 4 senior-most** judges |
| **Fourth Judges / NJAC (2015)** | **99th Amendment, 2014 struck down** – violated basic structure (judicial independence) |
- **NJAC** – **six members**: CJI (Chairperson), two senior-most SC judges, Union Law Minister, two eminent persons (chosen by PM + CJI + LoP in Lok Sabha); **any two members could veto** a name.

- SC to examine a **plea to revive NJAC**; CJI **Surya Kant** called for a **National Judicial Policy** to reduce divergence across courts; pendency over **5 crore** cases; about one-third of HC posts vacant.

### 5. LANDMARK JUDGMENTS – ARTICLE 21 AND RELIGION
#### 5.1 Harish Rana – Passive Euthanasia
- SC **reserved judgment (January 2026)** in **Harish Rana v. Union of India** – plea to withdraw life support; petitioner in permanent vegetative state for **13 years** (since a 2013 fall), 100% quadriplegic; medical boards recommended withdrawal.
- **Active euthanasia illegal**; **passive legal** under guidelines; Art 21 includes right to die with dignity.
- BNS 2023: Sec 100 culpable homicide, 101 murder, 108 abetment of suicide.
| Case | Holding |
|---|---|
| **Maruti Dubal (1987)** | **High Court** recognised right to die |
| **Gian Kaur (1996)** | Right to die **rejected** |
| **Aruna Shanbaug (2011)** | **Passive euthanasia** permitted |
| **Common Cause (2018)** | **Living wills** recognised |
| SC simplification (2023) | Faster process |
- **2023 reforms**: two boards (primary – hospital; secondary – district); **three-member** board; experience **20 → 5 years**; **48-hour** deadline.
- Abroad: Netherlands, Belgium – active and assisted; Switzerland – assisted suicide.
#### 5.2 Menstrual Health – Dr Jay Thakur v Union of India
- **Menstrual health part of Art 21** (dignity, privacy, bodily autonomy); also tied to **Arts 14 and 21A**.
- **Free biodegradable sanitary pads** for girls in **Classes 6–12**; separate toilets incl. for differently-abled; vending machines, incinerators.
- Provision is a **constitutional responsibility, not charity**.
#### 5.3 Other Article 21 Rulings
- **SC: solid waste management** part of the right to a healthy environment (Art 21); guidelines under **SWM Rules, 2026**.
- **Delhi HC (6 January 2026, Justice Neena Bansal Krishna)**: dismissed husband's plea against 14-week abortion – under the **MTP Act** (1971, amended 2021) **woman's consent suffices; husband's consent not required**; forced pregnancy can be **mental cruelty**; IPC Section 312 – woman commits no offence.
#### 5.4 Child Trafficking – K.P. Kiran Kumar v State (2025)
- SC strict guidelines; trafficking violates Art 21; NCRB – 53,000+ children rescued (2024–25); conviction rate only **4.8%**.
- **BNS 2023 Section 143** – trafficking **includes organ removal**; Sections 98, 99 – selling/buying minors.
- Acts: ITPA 1956, POCSO 2012, JJ Act 2015, Criminal Law Amendment 2013.

| Bachpan Bachao Andolan (2011) | Rescue, rehabilitation directions |

### 8. DELIMITATION AND CENSUS 2027
#### 8.1 Delimitation after Census 2027
- Freeze on inter-State Lok Sabha seats **ends after Census 2027** – **first redistribution since 1976**.
- **42nd Amendment (1976)**: seats frozen on **1971 Census (54.8 crore)**; **84th (2001)**: freeze till the first Census after 2026, allowed internal readjustment on 2001 Census.

- **Four Delimitation Commissions** constituted (up to December 2023).
- Delimitation = fixing constituency boundaries so seats match population; Arts **82, 170, 330, 332**; orders **not challengeable in court**.
- Representation still reflects 548 million people vs today's 1.47 billion.
