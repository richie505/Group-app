## U0267 | WOMEN'S MOVEMENT IN ANDHRA PRADESH, 1956-2014
### 1. ROOTS OF THE MOVEMENT
- **Kandukuri Veeresalingam**: journals **Janana** and **Satihita Bodhini**; women's education and **widow remarriage**.
- **Bhagya Reddy Varma**: **1919 Machilipatnam** Adi-Andhra conference demanded abolition of **Jogini and Devadasi** systems.

### 3. ANTI-ARRACK MOVEMENT
- Began in **Dubagunta**, **Nellore**, led by rural women; grew out of **Aksharajyothi** literacy classes.
- Outcome: arrack prohibited in AP from **1 October 1993**.

### 6. LAWS AND CAMPAIGNS
| Campaign | Law | Year |
|---|---|---|
| Devadasi (Madras) | Madras Devadasis (Prevention of Dedication) Act | **1947** |
| Dowry deaths | **Dowry Prohibition Act** | **1961** |
| Property | **Hindu Succession (AP Amendment) Act**: daughters get **coparcenary rights** | **1986** |
| Devadasi / Jogini (AP) | **AP Devadasis (Prohibition of Dedication) Act** | **1988** |
| Local bodies | **73rd/74th Amendments**: one-third seats – **Art. 243D(3)** Panchayats, **243T** Municipalities | **1992** |
- Constitution (**131st**) Amendment Bill, 2026 sought faster women's reservation by redistributing Lok Sabha seats; **defeated, April 2026**.
!KEY AP gave daughters coparcenary rights in **1986**, before the **Central amendment of 2005**.
#### 6.1 AP Today
- **One Stop Centres** in all **26 districts** (22,692 cases); **181 Women Helpline** since **2016**; **28 Shakti Sadans**.
- **Beti Bachao Beti Padhao**: launched **2015**; covers all 26 districts.
- **Sashakt Panchayat Netri Abhiyan** (June 2026): leadership skills of women elected representatives.
