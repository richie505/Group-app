## U0408 | SC/ST (PREVENTION OF ATROCITIES) ACT 1989 AND PROTECTION OF CIVIL RIGHTS ACT 1955
### 1. PCR ACT 1955 VS PoA ACT 1989
| Item | PCR Act | PoA Act |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | **Untouchability (Offences) Act, 1955**; renamed **1976** | Enacted **1989** (assent **11 Sept 1989**) |
| In force | 1955; **Rules 1977** | **30 January 1990**; **Rules 1995** |
| Extent / scope | **Whole of India** (s.1(2); APPSC 2019) | Offences by **non-SC/ST** persons against SCs/STs |
| Amended | 1976 | **2015, 2018** (20 Aug 2018) |
- Both rest on **Article 17**; PoA rests on **protective discrimination (Art. 15(4))**. PoA has **23 sections**.
- **"Untouchability"** is defined neither in the Constitution nor the Act; **Mysore HC**: the historically developed caste practice.
- Since **1 July 2024**, IPC/CrPC references read as **BNS/BNSS**. PoA applies to **J&K since 2019**.
!TRAP PCR **1955** (renamed 1976, Rules 1977) vs PoA **1989** (in force 30 Jan 1990, Rules 1995).
### 2. PCR ACT – SECTIONS AND PUNISHMENT
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| 3 | **Religious** disabilities – entry/worship in public places of worship |
| 4 | **Social** disabilities – shops, hotels, water sources, roads |
| **5** | Refusing admission to **hospital, dispensary, educational institution** |
| 6 | Refusing to **sell goods or render services** |
| 7 | Insulting an SC member (7(1)(d)); **preaching or justifying** untouchability |
| 10 | **Abetment** – public servant wilfully neglecting investigation |
| **10A** | **Collective fine** on inhabitants of an area |
| **12** | Court **presumes** the untouchability ground when the victim is a **Scheduled Caste** member |
| **14** | **Offences by companies** – Director, Manager or Secretary held guilty |
| 15 | **Cognizable**, triable **summarily**, **non-compoundable**; no prior sanction |
| 15A | **State duties** – legal aid, special courts, surveys of prone areas, vigilance officers, annual reports |
- **First offence:** **1-6 months** and fine **₹100-500**.
- Convict is **disqualified** from elections to Parliament and State Legislatures.
- Not an offence when accused and victim belong to the **same social group**.
- **Implementation:** States/UTs; nodal Centre – **Social Justice and Empowerment** with **MHA** (not Minority Affairs).
- **Sukanya Shantha (2024):** Art. 17 bars caste-based **purity stigma**.
### 3. PoA ACT – KEY SECTIONS
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| **2(1)(bc)** | Defines **economic boycott** (2015) |
| 3 | List of **atrocities**; public servant committing one – **minimum 1 year** |
| **4** | **Wilful neglect of duty** by public servants |
| 6 | Applies IPC s.34, Chapters 3, 4, 5, 5-A, s.149, Chapter 23 |
| 8 | Court may **presume** abetment, common intention, common object |
| **14** | **Special Courts**; **Exclusive Special Courts** (2015) |
| 15 | **Special Public Prosecutors** |
| **14A** | **Appeal to High Court within 90 days** (incl. bail orders), on facts and law |
| 16 | **State Government** imposes and collects **collective fine** |
| **18** | **Anticipatory bail barred** (CrPC 438, now BNSS 482) |
| **18A** | No **preliminary enquiry** or **approval** before arrest (2018) |
| **19** | **No probation** |
| **23** | **Central Government rule-making** (Rules 1995) |
- Special Court: a **Court of Session** specified by the **State Government** with concurrence of the **HC Chief Justice**, in each district. It **cannot impose a collective fine** and cannot authorise a non-police person for further investigation.
- Accused **cannot claim private defence**. Offences are cognizable.
- Arms licences of potential offenders in **atrocity-prone areas** can be cancelled; SC/ST persons may get licences. No power to dissolve local authorities.
!TRAP PCR **s.12** = presumption, **s.14** = companies; PoA **s.14** = Special Courts, **s.18** = no anticipatory bail.
### 4. PoA RULES 1995 AND MACHINERY
- **Investigation:** officer **not below DSP**; charge-sheet within **60 days**.
- **Rule 17:** **District** Vigilance and Monitoring Committee in each district, headed by the **Collector/DM**.
- **State** Vigilance and Monitoring Committee: **Chief Minister**.
- **Rule 18:** material for the **Annual Report**.
- **Relief:** **₹85,000 to ₹8.25 lakh** (2016); relief for about 87,426 victims under the Centre's scheme.
- **National Helpline 14566** (NGRS): toll-free, **24/7**, under DoSJE.
- Centre's task force (July 2026): higher relief, counselling, State relief cells; FIRs sent to authorities within **24 hours**.
!PAIR State committee | Chief Minister | Rules 1995 || District committee | Collector | Rule 17
### 5. PoA AMENDMENT ACT 2015 (in force 2016)
- **New atrocities:** **tonsuring**, **garlanding with footwear**, forcing to dispose of carcasses, dig graves or do manual scavenging, dedicating a woman as **devadasi**, **abusing by caste name**, **witchcraft** accusations.
- **Social and economic boycott** defined and made atrocities.
- **Exclusive Special Courts + Exclusive Special PPs**; Special Courts take **direct cognizance**; trial within **60 days** of charge-sheet.
- New chapter on **rights of victims and witnesses**.
### 6. DILUTION AND RESTORATION – 2018 EPISODE
| Step | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Subhash Kashinath Mahajan v State of Maharashtra (20 March 2018)** | Seen as **diluting** the Act (APPSC G2 2024): **preliminary enquiry by DSP**, anticipatory bail if no prima facie case, **prior approval** before arrest of public servants |
| **PoA Amendment Act 2018** | Inserted **s.18A**: no enquiry, no approval; bail bar "notwithstanding any court order" |
| **Review (Oct 2019)** | SC **recalled** the Mahajan directions |
| **Prithvi Raj Chauhan v UOI (Feb 2020)** | **Upheld** the 2018 amendment; bail/quashing only where **no prima facie case** |
| **Kiran v Rajkumar Jivaraj Jain (2025)** | Cancelled HC anticipatory bail; **no "mini-trial"** at bail stage |
!TRAP The 2018 insertion is **Section 18A**, not "Section 4A".
### 7. MANUAL SCAVENGING LAWS
| Law / case | Key point |
|---|---|
| **1993 Act** | Employment of Manual Scavengers and Construction of **Dry Latrines** (Prohibition) |
| **2013 Act** | Bans manual scavengers and hazardous sewer/septic cleaning without gear; surveys; sanitary latrines in 3 years; **cognizable and non-bailable** |
| **Safai Karamchari Andolan v UOI (2014)** | **₹10 lakh** per sewer death since 1993 |
| **Balram Singh (2023)** | Raised to **₹30 lakh** |
- 2013 penalties: first breach **1 year / ₹50,000**; hazardous sewer cleaning **2 years / ₹2 lakh**.
- **NCSK** monitors; **NSKFDC (1997)** nodal for the Self-Employment Scheme; **NAMASTE (2023)** absorbed it – **zero sanitation fatalities**.
### 8. ENFORCEMENT DATA AND ANDHRA PRADESH
- **NCRB 2024:** **55,698** crimes against SCs (**-3.6%** from 57,789 in 2023); against STs **-23.1%**.
- Top States (2023): **UP, Rajasthan, MP**. PoA conviction rate about **32-33%** (2022), down from 39-40% (2020); pendency about **97%**.
- **AP:** **14 Special Sessions Courts** + **1 Exclusive Court (Laxmipet, Srikakulam)**; SOP of **Police, Revenue, Judiciary, Welfare**; crime rate 27.4 per lakh SC population; cases fell **24.32%** in 2025.
- **AP Devadasis (Prohibition of Dedication) Act 1989:** against the **Jogini** system.
