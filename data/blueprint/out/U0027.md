## U0027 | JAINISM – TIRTHANKARAS, MAHAVIRA, TRIRATNA, PANCHAMAHAVRATAS, ANEKANTAVADA AND SYADVADA
### 1. TIRTHANKARAS
- **Jina** = conqueror of passions (root of the word "Jain"). **Tirthankara** = "ford-maker" who shows the path to moksha – a **Jain term**.
- Jainism has **24 Tirthankaras** (1 Rishabhnath, 2 Ajitnath, 3 Sambhavnath ...).
- Only the **last two** – **Parshvanatha (23rd)** and **Mahavira (24th)** – are proven historical; the first 22 are uncertain.
#### 1.1 Tirthankaras – Number, Symbol, Nirvana Place
| No. | Tirthankara | Symbol (cognizance) | Key fact / nirvana place |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | **Rishabhadeva (Adinatha / Rishabhanatha)** | **Bull** | Born **Ayodhya** to Nabhi and Marudevi; moksha at **Mount Kailash (Ashtapada)** |
| 12th | **Vasupujya** | Buffalo | **Champapuri** (Bihar), not Pavapuri |
| 20th | **Munisuvrata (Munisuvrat Swami)** | **Tortoise** | **Sammed Shikhar** |
| 22nd | **Neminatha (Arishtanemi)** | **Conch** | Gujarat region; nirvana at **Girnar** |
| 23rd | **Parshvanatha** | **Serpent** | Born **Varanasi**; nirvana at **Sammed Shikhar** (Parasnath hill, Jharkhand) |
| 24th | **Mahavira** | **Lion** | Last Tirthankara; nirvana at **Pavapuri** |
!NUM Bull – Serpent – Lion = 1st – 23rd – 24th; Tortoise = 20th; Conch = 22nd
#### 1.2 Parshvanatha (23rd)
- Parents **King Asvasena and Queen Vama** of Varanasi; **Ikshvaku** dynasty; born c. **850 BC**.
- Renounced at **30**; enlightenment after **84 days** of penance; died at **100**; about **250 years before Mahavira**.
- Taught **four vows (chaturyama)**; eternity of matter; followers wore **white**.
#### 1.3 Who is NOT a Tirthankara
- **Nathamuni** – not a Jain Tirthankara.
- **Bahubali (Gomateshwara)**, Shravanabelagola – son of Rishabhanatha, **not a Tirthankara**.
!TRAP Gomateshwara statue = Bahubali, not a Tirthankara.
### 2. LIFE OF MAHAVIRA
| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| Birth | **Kundagrama (Kundalpur / Basukunda)** near **Vaishali**, c. **599 BCE** |
| Father / clan | **Siddhartha**, chief of the **Jnatrika (Gyatrik)** Kshatriya clan, Lichchhavi confederacy; royal family |
| Mother | **Trishala**, sister of Lichchhavi chief **Chetaka** |
| Brother | Nandivardhan (elder) |
| Wife / daughter | **Yashoda**; daughter Anojja (Priyadarshana) – Svetambara belief |
| Renunciation | At **30**; **12 years** of severe asceticism |
| Kaivalya (Kevala Jnana) | At **42**, at **Jrimbhikagrama**, bank of river **Rijupalika**, under a **sal tree** |
| Death | **Pavapuri (Pava)** near Rajgir, aged **72**, **527 BCE** |
!NUM 30 – 42 – 72 = renunciation – kaivalya – death
- **Titles**: Jina, **Nirgrantha** (free from bonds), **Kevalin**, Mahavira, **Arhat**. **Tathagata** is the Buddha's title, not Mahavira's.
- **11 disciples (Ganadharas)**; only **Arya Sudharma** survived him and became the **first Thera**.
- **Jamali** – first follower of Mahavira.
- **Lichchhavi republic** – first to accept Jainism.
!TRAP Mahavira did marry (Yashoda) – a Svetambara view.
### 3. CONTEMPORARIES OF MAHAVIRA
| Person | Association |
|---|---|
| **Bimbisara, Ajatashatru** | Magadha rulers |
| **Chetaka** | Lichchhavi chief, maternal uncle; daughter **Chellana** married Bimbisara |
| **Pradyota** | Avanti ruler |
| **Prasenjit** | Kosala king in the Buddha's time |
| Udayana | Vatsa (Kausambi) |
| **Makkhali Gosala** | Companion for about **six years**, later fell out; founded the **Ajivika** sect |
| **Gautama Buddha** | Contemporary teacher |
- Other rival teachers: **Purana Kassapa, Ajita Kesakambalin** (materialist), **Pakudha Kachchayana**, **Sanjaya Belatthiputta**.
!TRAP Gosala is not Mahavira's "guru"; Jain texts make him a disciple/companion.
### 4. JAIN PHILOSOPHY – DOCTRINES
#### 4.1 Doctrines and Meaning
| Doctrine | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Anekantavada** | **Many-sidedness** of reality; truth has many facets; basis of **tolerance and pluralism**; core Jain philosophy |
| **Syadvada** | **Relativity of knowledge** / conditioned predication; "syat" = may be; **Saptabhanginaya** (seven-fold predication) |
| **Nayavada** | Theory of **partial standpoints** |
- Two main doctrines: **Anekantavada and Syadvada**; every being has many properties (the permanent one = attribute).
#### 4.2 Exponents
| Person | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Kundakundacharya** | Digambara philosopher; **preached Syadvada**; wrote **Samayasara, Pravachanasara, Panchastikaya** |
| **Somadeva Suri** | Title **"Syadvadachala Simha"**; wrote **Yasastilaka Champu, Nitivakyamrita** |
| **Bhadrabahu** | Took Jainism to the south |
#### 4.3 Doctrines of Other Schools
| Doctrine | School |
|---|---|
| Anekantavada, Syadvada | **Jain** |
| **Sunyavada, Sarvastivada** | Buddhist |
| **Anatmavada** (no-self), from Pratityasamutpada | Buddhist |
| **Niyati** (fate) | Ajivika |
| Materialism | Charvaka |
### 5. JAIN CONCEPTS – JIVA, KARMA, LIBERATION
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Jiva / Ajiva** | Conscious soul / non-conscious matter |
| **Karma** | **Material** bondage of the soul causing rebirth |
| **Samvara** | Stopping karmic inflow |
| **Nirjara** | Shedding existing karma by penance |
| **Kevala jnana / Kaivalya** | Omniscience |
| **Siddha** | Fully liberated soul |
| **Tattva** | Origination, decay, permanence (utpada-vyaya-dhrauvya) |
- **Souls even in rocks and running water** – all objects possess a soul that feels pain → extreme ahimsa.
- Universe = living souls + **five non-living categories** (e.g. akasa); **eternal**, **no final annihilation**; **no creator god** (non-theistic).
- **Four forms of existence**: **Deva, Manushya, Naraki** (hell-beings), **Tiryancha** (animals/plants) – **not Yaksha**.
- Path: Karma → Samvara → Nirjara → Kevala jnana → Siddha.
### 6. TRIRATNA
| Religion | Three jewels | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| **Jainism (Ratnatraya)** | **Right Faith** (Samyak Darshana), **Right Knowledge** (Samyak Jnana), **Right Conduct** (Samyak Charitra) | Path / means to **moksha** |
| Buddhism | **Buddha, Dhamma, Sangha** | Three refuges |
!PAIR Right faith, knowledge, conduct | Jainism | Path to moksha || Buddha, Dhamma, Sangha | Buddhism | Refuges
### 7. PANCHAMAHAVRATAS AND PRACTICES
#### 7.1 Five Vows
| Vow | Meaning | Given by |
|---|---|---|
| **Ahimsa** | Non-violence | Parshvanatha |
| **Satya** | Truth | Parshvanatha |
| **Asteya** | Non-stealing | Parshvanatha |
| **Aparigraha** | Non-possession | Parshvanatha |
| **Brahmacharya** | Celibacy / chastity | **Added by Mahavira** |
- **Mahavratas** for ascetics; **Anuvratas** = milder vows for householders (**shravakas**): non-injury, non-stealing, non-adultery, truth, non-possession; plus daily charity of cooked food.
- Jain ahimsa is stricter, even in thought → vegetarianism; strict followers avoid agriculture (hence a trading base).
!TRAP 4 vows = Parshvanatha; the 5th (Brahmacharya) = Mahavira.
#### 7.2 Rituals
| Practice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sallekhana / Santhara** (Samadhi Maran, Sanyasna Maran) | Ritual **fast unto death** at life's end, slowly cutting food and drink, to purify karma; the **last vow** |
| **Paryushana** | Festival; last day **Samvatsari** = forgiveness day |
| **Pratikramana** | Introspection / self-review |
| **Padmasana / Kayotsarga** | Postures of Tirthankara images |
- **Chandragupta Maurya** performed Sallekhana at **Shravanabelagola**.
!KEY Santhara is a fast unto death among **Jains** (not Buddhists, Sufis or Parsis).
### 8. SECTS AND RELIGION MATCHING
| Religion / sect | Founder or leader | Text / idea |
|---|---|---|
| Jainism | Rishabhadeva (first), Mahavira (last) | **Agamas** in **Ardhamagadhi** |
| Buddhism | Gautama Buddha | **Tripitaka** in **Pali** |
| **Ajivika** | **Makkhali Gosala** | **Niyati**; regular sabhas |
| **Digambara** | – | Sky-clad; monks unclothed |
| **Yapaniya** | From Digambaras | Some Svetambara views; nude Tirthankara images |
- **Anuvrata** = Jain term; **Tripitaka** = Buddhist.
### 9. JAINISM AND BUDDHISM COMPARED
| Point | Jainism | Buddhism |
|---|---|---|
| Soul | **Eternal jiva** in all things | **Anatta** (no-self) |
| Karma | Material bondage | Intention-based |
| Penance | **Severe, self-mortification** | **Middle Way** |
| Ahimsa | Stricter | Less strict |
| Goal | Kevala jnana | Nirvana |
| Spread | Mostly India | Widely outside India |
- **Common**: indifference to **Vedas**, denial of rituals and sacrifice, rejection of Brahmana supremacy, **non-injury** to animals, **karma and transmigration** (both shramana traditions).
- **Not common**: **avoiding extremes** of penance and pleasure; **self-mortification**.
- Decline of Jainism: severe austerities, loss of royal patronage, **Digambara–Svetambara split**.
### 10. JAINISM IN ANDHRA
| Place | District | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Vaddamanu (Vardhamanapuram)** | Guntur | Basadi built by **Samprati** (Ashoka's grandson, "Ashoka of Jainism"); **Kharavela** built a vihara near it |
| **Danavulapadu** | Jammalamadugu, YSR Kadapa | Jain kshetra of **Rashtrakuta Indra III** |
| Kurnool–Anantapur | Rayalaseema | **Jain corridor** – Bhadrabahu–Chandragupta route to Shravanabelagola |
| Erramala, Nallamala hills | Rayalaseema | Ascetic caves |
- **Mahapadma Nanda** said to have conquered Andhra and spread Jainism.
- **Early Kakatiyas were Jains** (as Western Chalukya feudatories), later Shaivas; **Prolaraju's Hanamkonda inscription** begins with a Jain prayer.
- Other Andhra dynasties: **Ikshvaku** royal women (Chantisiri, Bodhisiri) backed **Buddhism** (Nagarjunakonda); **Satavahanas** were Vedic but supported Buddhism.
- Mahavir Jayanti (Apr 2025) brought the Tirthankaras, vows and Sallekhana back into the news.
