## U0724 | ISRO - ORGANISATIONAL STRUCTURE, MAJOR CENTRES AND THEIR FUNCTIONS
### 1. EVOLUTION OF ISRO
| Year | Step |
|---|---|
| 1961 | Space research placed under the **Department of Atomic Energy** (Nehru) |
| 1962 | **INCOSPAR** set up by **Homi Bhabha**, **Vikram Sarabhai** Chairman; work on **TERLS** (near Thiruvananthapuram) began |
| **15 Aug 1969** | **ISRO** established, replacing INCOSPAR |
| 1972 | **Space Commission** and **Department of Space (DoS)**; ISRO under DoS (**1 June 1972**) |
| 2019 | **NSIL**: CPSE, commercial arm (Antrix = older commercial arm) |
| 2020 | **IN-SPACe**: autonomous **single-window regulator**, HQ **Ahmedabad**; chairman **Pawan Kumar Goenka** |
| 2023 | **Indian Space Policy** |
!TRAP ISRO was formed in **August** 1969, not November.
!PAIR IN-SPACe | regulator | 2020, Ahmedabad || NSIL | commercial arm | 2019, CPSE
### 2. HIERARCHY AND LEADERSHIP
- **ISRO** = Indian Space Research Organisation; a constituent of the **Department of Space**; HQ **Bengaluru**.
- DoS works **directly under the Prime Minister**.
- **ISRO Chairman** = also **Secretary, DoS** + **Chairman, Space Commission**.
- Current: **Dr V. Narayanan**, 11th Chairman, from **14 Jan 2025**, succeeding **S. Somanath** (in office as of Jun 2026).
- India still has **no Space Activities Act** (2026).

### 4. ISRO CENTRES - FULL FORM, LOCATION, FUNCTION
| Centre | Full form | Location | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| **VSSC** | Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre | **Thiruvananthapuram** (Kerala) | Lead centre, **launch-vehicle** design and development |
| **LPSC** | Liquid Propulsion Systems Centre | Valiamala; Bengaluru unit | **Liquid and cryogenic** stages |
| **IPRC** | ISRO Propulsion Complex | **Mahendragiri (Tamil Nadu)** | Assembly and testing of liquid/cryogenic engines and stages |
| **SDSC-SHAR** | Satish Dhawan Space Centre | Sriharikota (AP) | Launch base; solid propellant processing, rocket assembly |
| **URSC** | U R Rao Satellite Centre (earlier ISAC) | **Bengaluru** | Satellite design and building |

| **NRSC** | National Remote Sensing Centre (forerunner **NRSA**) | **Hyderabad** (Telangana) | Receiving, processing, distributing satellite data |

| **MCF** | Master Control Facility | **Hassan** (Karnataka, **1982**); **Bhopal** (**2005**) | Control of **geostationary** satellites |
| **ISTRAC** | ISRO Telemetry, Tracking and Command Network | Bengaluru | Tracking launch vehicles and satellites |
!TRAP IPRC Mahendragiri is in **Tamil Nadu**, not Kerala.
### 5. GROUND NETWORK
- **ISTRAC** (HQ Bengaluru) runs the TTC network tracking launch vehicles through the ascent phase.
- Stations abroad: **Mauritius, Brunei, Biak (Indonesia)**.
- **Indian Deep Space Network**: **Byalalu**, near Bengaluru.
- ISRO-ESA Technical Implementing Plan for **ground-tracking support** for Gaganyaan.
### 6. SRIHARIKOTA (SDSC-SHAR)
#### 6.1 Location and history
- Barrier island between **Pulicat Lake (west)** and the **Bay of Bengal (east)**; it divides the lake from the sea.
- District: **Tirupati** now; **Nellore** earlier (APPSC 2019 answer, before the 2022 reorganisation).
- Set up **1971** as **Sriharikota High Altitude Range (SHAR)**; renamed **2002** after **Satish Dhawan**.
- Why chosen: **east coast** (eastward launch gains from Earth's rotation), **near the equator**, **sea** for spent stages, **thinly populated**.
#### 6.2 Facilities and launches
| Pad | Vehicles |
|---|---|
| First Launch Pad | **PSLV, SSLV** |
| Second Launch Pad | **GSLV, LVM3**; standby for PSLV |
| **Third Launch Pad** | For **NGLV**; approved **16 Jan 2025**, **Rs 3,984.86 crore**, **48 months**; also standby for the second pad |
- **SPROB** (Solid Propellant Space Booster Plant): one of the largest in the world; casts **S200** boosters of LVM3.
- **Launch View Gallery**: public, opened **2019**.
| Launch | Mission | Date |
|---|---|---|
| ISRO's 100th space mission | from Sriharikota | Sept 2012 |
| 100th launch from SHAR | 17th GSLV flight | **29 Jan 2025** |
| 105th | PSLV-C62/EOS-N1 (first pad) | 12 Jan 2026 |
| 107th | GSLV-F17/EOS-05 (second pad) | 4 Sep 2026 |
- **February 2004**: blast at a solid propellant plant at SHAR.
### 7. SECOND SPACEPORT - KULASEKARAPATTINAM
- **SSLV Launch Complex**, **Thoothukudi (Tuticorin)** district, **Tamil Nadu**; mainly **SSLV** as private players enter.
- Reason: **direct southward launch** to polar/sun-synchronous orbit, without the fuel-costly turn around **Sri Lanka** needed from Sriharikota.
!KEY Sriharikota = AP, eastward launches; Kulasekarapattinam = Tamil Nadu, southward, SSLV.
### 8. DoS AUTONOMOUS BODIES AND AP ASSETS
| Body | Location | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **PRL** - Physical Research Laboratory | **Ahmedabad** | Space and planetary science |
| **NARL** - National Atmospheric Research Laboratory | **Gadanki, Tirupati** (AP) | Atmospheric research - radar, lidar, monsoon dynamics |
| **NE-SAC** - North Eastern Space Applications Centre | **Umiam (Shillong)**, Meghalaya | Space applications for the North East |
| **SCL** - Semi-Conductor Laboratory | **Mohali**, Punjab | Microelectronics / chip fabrication |
| **IIST** - Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology | **Thiruvananthapuram** | **Deemed university** under DoS, **2007**; **Asia's first space university** |
- AP institutions: **SDSC-SHAR** (Sriharikota, Tirupati); **NARL** (Gadanki); **APSAC** = AP Space Applications Centre, **Vijayawada** (remote sensing, GIS); **NSTL** = Naval Science & Technological Laboratory, **Visakhapatnam**.
- SHAR and NARL = the State's **two DoS assets**; SHAR anchors an MSME network across Nellore-Tirupati-Chennai and gives cyclone data to APSDMA.
!TRAP IIST is at Thiruvananthapuram, not Gadanki.
### 9. IIRS AND TRAINING BODIES
- **IIRS** (Indian Institute of Remote Sensing), **Dehradun**; constituent unit of ISRO, DoS.
- Founded **1966** as the **Indian Photo-interpretation Institute (IPI)**.
- Disciplines: **remote sensing, photogrammetry, cartography, geo-informatics/GIS** - **not angiography**; field equipment does **not** include a **LINAC**.
- **CSSTEAP**: Centre for Space Science and Technology Education in Asia and the Pacific; **UN-affiliated**; **1995**; hosted at IIRS Dehradun.
