## U0396 | OTHER SOCIAL PROBLEMS – ALCOHOLISM, DRUG ABUSE, SUICIDE, BEGGARY AND HUMAN TRAFFICKING
### 1. SUICIDE – TYPES AND DATA
#### 1.1 Durkheim's Types (*Le Suicide*, 1897)
| Type | Cause |
|---|---|
| **Egoistic** | Too little **integration** |
| **Altruistic** | Too much integration |
| **Anomic** | Too little **regulation** (crisis, sudden change) |
| **Fatalistic** | Too much regulation |
#### 1.2 NCRB ADSI Data
- **ADSI 2024** (released 7 May 2026): **1,70,746 suicides**; **daily wagers ~31%**; homemakers 22,113; unemployed 14,778; students 14,488; **drug-overdose deaths 978 (up 50%)**.
- **ADSI 2015 – farmer suicides:** **bankruptcy/indebtedness** first, then farming-related issues (**APPSC G1 2017**).
### 2. ATTEMPT TO SUICIDE IN LAW
| Law / case | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Sec. 309 IPC** | Attempt punishable with simple imprisonment **up to one year** and/or fine |
| **P. Rathinam (1994)** | Held Sec. 309 **invalid** |
| **Law Commission, 210th Report** | Recommended **deleting** Sec. 309 |
| **Mental Healthcare Act 2017, Sec. 115** | Presumes **severe stress** → no trial or punishment; State must provide care |
| **BNS 2023** | No general attempted-suicide offence; **Sec. 226** = attempt to coerce a public servant |
- **Tele-MANAS:** tele-mental-health helpline, launched **10 Oct 2022**, toll-free **14416**.
- Suicide: attempt punishable, completed act cannot be; murder, dacoity, kidnapping punishable either way.
### 3. BEGGARY
- **Bombay Prevention of Begging Act, 1959**: punitive model copied by many States.
- **Delhi HC (2018, Harsh Mander)**: **decriminalised begging** in Delhi.
- **SC (2021)**: refused to ban begging; a **socio-economic problem**.
- **SMILE (MoSJE, 2022)**: sub-scheme for comprehensive, **rights-based rehabilitation** of persons begging.
- **West Bengal** tops the Social Justice Ministry's state list of beggars (BPSC 2017).
- **Indore**: **first beggar-free city (2025)**.
### 4. ALCOHOLISM
- **Art. 47 (DPSP)**: State to prohibit **intoxicating drinks** and harmful drugs **except for medicinal use**.
- **Anti-Arrack Movement**: began **1992**, **Dubagunta** village, **Nellore** district; neo-literate women of the literacy campaign; led by **Dubagunta Rosamma**.
- Precedent: **R. Venkataratnam Naidu's Social Purity Association (1891)**, a temperance campaign.
### 5. DRUG ABUSE
- India lies between the **Golden Crescent** and **Golden Triangle**.
- **National Survey on Extent and Pattern of Substance Use (2019, MoSJE with AIIMS-NDDTC)**: **alcohol 14.6%** → cannabis **2.8%** → opioids **2.1%**.
| Campaign | Ministry / year | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| **Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan** | **MoSJE, 15 Aug 2020** | First **272** districts, now all; run by **NISD**; focus on campuses |
| **Nasha Mukt Yuva for Viksit Bharat** | **MoYAS** via MY Bharat, Aug 2026 | **100-week** Jan Bhagidari plan; **Kashi Declaration** (July 2025) |
!TRAP NMBA = MoSJE (2020); Nasha Mukt Yuva = MoYAS (2026).
### 6. HUMAN TRAFFICKING
- **Immoral Traffic (Prevention) Act, 1956** is the main law on sex trafficking.
- **Anti Human Trafficking Units (AHTUs)**: **696** across States/UTs (NCRB *Crime in India 2020*); MHA has funded AHTUs covering all districts.
### 7. SOCIAL DEFENCE
- Scope: prevention and rehabilitation for drug and alcohol abuse, beggary, destitute elderly, transgender persons.
- **National Institute of Social Defence (NISD)**: under **MoSJE**; implements NMBA; trains officials (e.g., on children who beg).
- Shift from **punishment** (Sec. 309, begging laws) to **health and rehabilitation** (MHCA, SMILE, NMBA).
### 8. ANDHRA PRADESH ANGLE
- **Farmer suicide ex gratia:** **₹7 lakh** to families under **GO 43**.
- **Liquor policy 2024:** retail shifted to **private licensees** (lottery); **₹99** for a 180 ml quarter; licences **12 Oct 2024 – 30 Sep 2026**.
- **Chittoor Police:** "**Dharya Sparsa – Operation Flush-Out**" against drugs and illegal liquor (Jul 2026).
