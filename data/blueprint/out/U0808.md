## U0808 | HEALTH OUTCOMES IN INDIA AND ANDHRA PRADESH – IMR, MMR, TFR, LIFE EXPECTANCY AND NUTRITION INDICATORS
### 1. MORTALITY INDICATORS
#### 1.1 Definitions and Base
| Indicator | Definition (base) | Latest |
|---|---|---|
| **IMR** | Deaths of infants **under 1 year per 1,000 live births** | **25** (SRS 2023, Economic Survey 2025-26) |
| **NMR** | Deaths in the **first 28 days per 1,000** live births | Fell **70%** (1990–2023) |
| **U5MR** | Deaths **under 5 years per 1,000** live births | Fell **78%** (1990–2023); SRS 2020: 32 |
| **MMR** | Maternal deaths **per 1,00,000 live births** | **97** (SRS 2018-20) → 93 (2019-21) → **88 (SRS 2020-22)** |
!KEY IMR, NMR and U5MR use 1,000 live births; only MMR uses 1,00,000 live births.

### 3. LIFE EXPECTANCY
- At **Independence**: about **32 years**.
- **49.7 years (1973) → 70.3 years (2023)** (Economic Survey 2025-26); 68.3 = older SRS 2011-15 figure.
| SRS abridged life tables 2016-20 | Males | Females |
|---|---|---|
| India | 68.6 | **71.4** |
| Andhra Pradesh | 69.1 | **72.2** |
- Women live about **3 years longer**; AP female life expectancy is higher than India's.
### 4. TOTAL FERTILITY RATE
- **TFR** = average number of children a woman would bear in her reproductive years.
- **Replacement level = 2.1**.
- **NFHS-5**: India's TFR fell from **2.2 to 2.0** – below replacement.
- **Five States still above 2.1** (NFHS-5): **Bihar (3.0), Meghalaya (2.9), Uttar Pradesh (2.4), Jharkhand (2.3), Manipur (2.2)**.
- **AP TFR 1.5**; Telangana 1.5.
!TRAP Bihar has the highest TFR; AP and Telangana are well below replacement at 1.5.

### 7. FORMS OF MALNUTRITION
- **WHO "malnutrition in all its forms"**: **undernutrition** (wasting, stunting, underweight) + **low vitamins/minerals** + **overweight, obesity and diet-related NCDs**.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Double burden | Undernutrition + overnutrition together |
| **Triple burden (SOFI 2025)** | Undernutrition + micronutrient deficiency/anaemia + overweight |
- NFHS-6 + Vellore cohort (birth to 9 years): thinness and overweight rise sharply between ages **7 and 9**.
- NFHS-6: **Madhya Pradesh** – high child wasting and underweight; **Meghalaya** – decline in stunting.
#### 7.1 Deficiency Diseases
| Deficiency | Disease |
|---|---|
| Vitamin A | **Night blindness** |
| Vitamin B1 | **Beri-beri** |
| Vitamin B3 | **Pellagra** |
| Vitamin B12 | Pernicious anaemia |
| Vitamin C | Scurvy |
| Vitamin D | Rickets |
| Vitamin K | **Bleeding** |
| Iron | **Anaemia** |
| Iodine | **Goitre** |
- **Goitre**: enlarged thyroid (thicker neck); less thyroxine and triiodothyronine; common in **hilly areas** (naturally iodine-deficient soil and food).
### 8. DRIVERS OF MALNUTRITION
- **Main cause in India: poor dietary habits and food insecurity**.
- Food insecurity and poverty → cheap **energy-dense, nutrient-poor** food → both under- and overnutrition, anaemia, cognitive delays.
- **Poor maternal nutrition** impairs the child's brain development.
- **WASH** and **maternal education**: women with 10+ years of schooling = **41%** (NFHS-5).
### 9. NATIONAL NUTRITION PROGRAMMES
| Programme | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **POSHAN Abhiyaan** | **2018**, **Ministry of Women and Child Development**; cuts stunting, wasting, anaemia, low birth weight; convergence of **ICDS, NHM, Swachh Bharat**; 8th Poshan Pakhwada 2026 |
| **Anganwadi Services (ICDS)** | Children **6 months–6 years**, pregnant and lactating women; **six services**: supplementary nutrition, pre-school education, nutrition and health education, immunisation, health check-up, referral (**not old-age pension**); 2.9 lakh AWCs co-located with schools (Sep 2025) |
| **Anemia Mukt Bharat** | **2018**; **6x6x6** – six interventions, six target groups, six institutional mechanisms |
| **Rice fortification** | **Iron, folic acid, vitamin B12**; via PDS, ICDS, PM POSHAN |
| **PM POSHAN** | **2021**, renamed from **Mid-Day Meal**; Classes **I–VIII** in government/aided schools + **Bal Vatika** (pre-primary) |
### 10. ANDHRA PRADESH NUTRITION SCHEMES
| Scheme | Target / feature |
|---|---|
| **Anna Amrutha Hastam** | **Pregnant and lactating women** via Anganwadis; nutritious meal + **iron-folic acid tablets**; aim: cut infant and maternal mortality |
| **YSR Sampoorna Poshana** (Sep 2020) | Pregnant women, lactating mothers, children – urban and rural |
| **YSR Sampoorna Poshana Plus** (Sep 2020) | Same groups in **77 tribal mandals** |
