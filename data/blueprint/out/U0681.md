## U0681 | TRANSPORT IN INDIA – ROADS, RAILWAYS, PIPELINES, WATERWAYS AND AIRWAYS
### 1. ROADS – NETWORK AND NATIONAL HIGHWAYS
- Road network **63.45 lakh km**, **2nd largest** in the world; NHs **1.46 lakh km**.
- NHs = about **2%** of road length but carry about **40%** of traffic; ~34 km/day built; **10%** of the road budget earmarked for the **North-East**.
- **Numbering:** **odd** = north-south (rising **west to east**); **even** = east-west (rising **north to south**).
| NH | Route | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **NH-44** | **Srinagar – Kanyakumari** | **Longest, ~4,112 km**; crosses **11 States**; busiest; N-S corridor; south part mostly old NH-7 |
| NH-48 | Delhi – Mumbai – Chennai | GQ arm |
| NH-19 | Delhi – Kolkata (**old NH-2**) | GQ arm |
| NH-16 | Kolkata – Chennai, east coast (**old NH-5**) | Crosses coastal AP |
| NH-27 | **Porbandar – Silchar** | East-West corridor |
| **NH-966B** | Kundannoor – Willingdon Island, Kochi | **Shortest, 8 km** |
- **Longest NH length by State:** **Maharashtra** (17,756.6 km).
- **Road density:** highest in **Kerala**, followed by Tripura.
- **Highest motorable road:** **Mig La, Ladakh** (~19,300 ft), world's highest; built by **BRO** (est. 1960).
!TRAP Longest NH length by State = Maharashtra; longest GQ stretch = Andhra Pradesh.

### 3. RAILWAYS – FIRSTS, NETWORK AND PRODUCTION UNITS
- **First train: 16 April 1853**, **Bori Bunder (Bombay) to Thane, 34 km**; by **GIPR**, under **Lord Dalhousie**.
- Network **70,000+ route km** (Jun 2026); broad gauge >95%; **4th largest** in world, 2nd under single management.
- Freight **1,617 MT (2024-25)**: world's **2nd-largest freight railway**.
- **Konkan Railway** (KRCL, 26 Jan 1998, HQ Navi Mumbai) is a **PSU, not a zone**; connects Madgaon, Ratnagiri, Udupi; **does not connect Belgaum**.

### 5. RAILWAY ELECTRIFICATION, TRAINS AND MISSIONS
- **Electrification:** **99.6%** of BG (23 May 2026); **14 of 18 zones at 100%**. PYQ key: about **94% as on 1 Mar 2024**.
- India = **2nd largest** electrified network (after China). **Haryana** = first fully electrified State.
- **First hydrogen train:** **Jind-Sonipat** (Haryana), 10 coaches.
- **Mission 41K:** save **₹41,000 crore** in energy costs over a decade.
- **Vande Bharat:** first indigenous semi-high-speed, engine-less train; first **New Delhi-Varanasi (2019)**; first **Vande Bharat Sleeper: Guwahati-Kolkata (2026)**.
- **Kavach** = indigenous train protection system. Amrit Bharat Station Scheme: 1,324 stations.
- **National Rail Plan 2030:** rail freight share **28% → 45%**.
| DFC | Route | Funder |
|---|---|---|
| **Eastern** | **Ludhiana-Sonnagar** (1,337 km) | **World Bank** |
| **Western** | **Dadri-JNPT** | **JICA** |
| East Coast (proposed) | Kharagpur-**Vijayawada** | - |
| New (Budget 2026-27) | **Dankuni-Surat** | - |
- DFCs: 2,741 km operational; ~443 trains/day (10 Sep 2026).
- **Budget 2026-27:** **7 high-speed rail corridors** (Mumbai-Pune, Pune-Hyderabad, Hyderabad-Bengaluru, Hyderabad-Chennai, Chennai-Bengaluru, Delhi-Varanasi + one).

### 9. AIRWAYS
- **First official airmail flight:** **18 Feb 1911**, **Allahabad to Naini** (Henri Pequet).
- **RCS-UDAN** ("Ude Desh ka Aam Nagrik"): under **National Civil Aviation Policy 2016**; uses **VGF**; Modified UDAN: 120 new destinations in 10 years.
- **Greenfield Airports Policy 2008:** 13 operational incl. **Navi Mumbai**. Open Sky for 6 metro airports.
- **Bharatiya Vayuyan Adhiniyam 2024** (from Jan 2025) replaced the **Aircraft Act 1934**.
- **Budget 2026-27:** **Seaplane VGF** scheme.
- **Carbon neutral:** **Delhi** first in Asia-Pacific (Sep 2016); Hyderabad followed (Dec 2016).
- Debate: IndiGo dominance; failures - Kingfisher 2012, Jet 2019, Go First 2023.
### 10. PM GATI SHAKTI AND LOGISTICS
- **PM Gati Shakti (2021)** = **National Master Plan for Multimodal Connectivity**; **GIS-based**, 1,700+ data layers; whole-of-government.
- Brought **16 ministries** (Railways, Roads, Ports, Aviation etc.) onto one platform (PYQ 2025). Apex **EGoS** under Cabinet Secretary now has 23 ministries; tiers EGoS - **Network Planning Group** - Technical Support Unit.
- **National Logistics Policy 2022** complements Gati Shakti. LEADS index since 2018.
- **LPI rank:** **44 (2018) → 38 (2023)**.
!TRAP Gati Shakti = 2021; National Logistics Policy = 2022.
### 11. MODES COMPARED
| Mode | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Inland waterways** | **Cheapest freight**; fuel per tonne-km = **1/3 of rail, 1/7 of road** |
| Shipping | Cheapest for bulk; 95% of trade by volume, 68% by value |
| **Pipelines** | Cheapest for **oil and gas** |
| **Rail** | More energy-efficient; **~half the cost** of road; **~90% less CO2** |
| Road | Door-to-door; short distance and last mile |
| **Air** | **Fastest and costliest** per tonne-km |
