## U0478 | MEASUREMENT OF UNEMPLOYMENT - UPS, UPSS, CWS, CDS; PLFS METHODOLOGY AND RECENT FINDINGS
### 1. STATUS APPROACHES
#### 1.1 The Four Approaches
| Approach | Reference period / unit | Counted employed if | Captures |
|---|---|---|---|
| **UPS** (Usual Principal Status) | **365 days** | Worked **major time** (generally **183+ days**) | **Chronic** (long-term) |
| **UPSS** (Principal + Subsidiary) | 365 days | Also subsidiary work for at least **30 days** | Broadest worker count |
| **CWS** (Current Weekly Status) | **7 days** | Worked at least **1 hour** on any day | Chronic + **seasonal** |
| **CDS** (Current Daily Status) | Each day of the week; unit = **half day** | **4+ hours** = employed for the day | Daily/intermittent; **highest rate** |
- **CDS gives the highest rate**: counts days of unemployment even of persons employed under CWS.
- Persons unemployed for a major part of the year (365-day reference) = **usual status** unemployment.
!NUM 365 days - 7 days - half day = UPS - CWS - CDS
#### 1.2 Not an NSS Approach
- NSS uses **three reference periods** (year, week, day). **Current Monthly Status** is NOT an NSSO method; "Rangarajan formula" is also a distractor.
- **Census**: main worker = **183+ days** a year; marginal worker = less than 183 days.
!TRAP 183 days = UPS "major time" and also the Census main/marginal line, but **Census is not an NSS approach**.
### 2. KEY LABOUR INDICATORS
| Indicator | Formula |
|---|---|
| Labour force | Employed + unemployed (seeking/available for work) |
| **LFPR** | Labour force ÷ working-age population (per 100) |
| **WPR** | Employed ÷ **total population** |
| **UR** | Unemployed ÷ **labour force** × 100 |
| **PU** (Proportion Unemployed) | Unemployed ÷ **population** |
| Identity | **WPR = LFPR × (1 - UR)** |
- Students and homemakers not seeking work are **outside** the labour force.
- Casual worker = non-regular; fixed-term worker = predetermined period.
!KEY "Percentage of employed persons in the population" = **Worker Population Ratio** (APPSC 2023).
!PAIR UR | unemployed | ÷ labour force || PU | unemployed | ÷ population
### 3. PERIODIC LABOUR FORCE SURVEY (PLFS)
#### 3.1 Agency and Launch
- Conducted by the **National Statistical Office (NSO), MoSPI**.
- Designed by the **Standing Committee on Labour Force Statistics (SCLFS)**, set up on advice of the **National Statistical Commission**.
- **Launched April 2017**; **first annual report (2017-18) released May 2019**.
- Replaced quinquennial NSSO surveys; NSO's standard method since 2017 (APPSC G2 2025).
- **2019:** CSO and NSSO merged into NSO.
#### 3.2 Original Design vs 2025 Revamp
| Feature | Original | From January 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | - | **Monthly all-India CWS** (LFPR, WPR, UR) |
| Quarterly | **Urban only** (CWS) | Extended to **rural** |
| Annual | Rural + urban, UPSS and CWS | **Calendar year** (Jan-Dec) |
| Sample | - | Raised (not reduced) to about **2,72,304 households** (2.65 times) |

### 5. AGENCIES AND SOURCES
| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| **NSSO (now NSO)** | Quinquennial Employment-Unemployment rounds (27th to 68th); PLFS since 2017 |
| CSO | Official statistics; merged into NSO (2019) |
| **Labour Bureau (1920)**, Ministry of Labour and Employment | **EUS** and **Quarterly Employment Survey (QES)**; labour statistics incl. industrial disputes, closures, retrenchments, lay-offs |
| **CMIE** | Private; monthly **CPHS**, larger sample, different method, higher UR |
| DGE&T / Employment Exchanges | Job-seeker registration |
| Census of India | Main/marginal workers every 10 years |
- Before NITI Aayog, unemployment data came mainly from the **NSSO**; "collected by CSO and NSSO" (APPSC 2019).
- CMIE: about **53 million unemployed, Dec 2021** (APPSC 2021); UR ~7.95% in Sep 2023.

### 8. CRITICISM
- **CWS** (1 hour a week = employed) **masks underemployment** and misses **circular migration** (Mar 2026; 7 Sep 2026).
- CMIE vs PLFS divergence: monthly, larger sample, different methodology.
