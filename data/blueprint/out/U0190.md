### 2. YOUNG BENGAL MOVEMENT
- Leader: **Henry Louis Vivian Derozio**, an **Anglo-Indian** teacher at **Hindu College, Calcutta** (joined 1826).
- Inspired by the **French Revolution**.
- Followers: **"Derozians"**; forum = **Academic Association (1828)**.
- Ideas: **radical rationalism**, iconoclastic; attacked old traditions, decadent customs, **idol worship and casteism**; strongly for **women's rights** and education.
- **Dismissed from Hindu College in 1831**; died the same year.
!KEY APPSC GS 2022: **Derozio → Young Bengal**.
### 3. PARAMAHANSA MANDALI, 1849
- **Secret society** – not an open public organisation (the Arya Samaj was not secret).
- Founded by **Dadoba Pandurang** with **Durgaram Mancharam** of the **Manav Dharma Sabha, Surat**.
- Believed in **one God**.
- Members **broke caste rules** by eating food cooked by lower castes.
- Aims: against **caste discrimination**; for **women's education** and **widow remarriage**.
- Region: **Maharashtra (Bombay)**, with branches at Poona and Satara.
!KEY APPSC GS 2023: "Established in **Poona in 1849**, aimed to fight caste discrimination and promote women's education" → **Paramahansa Mandali**.
### 4. PRARTHANA SAMAJ, 1867
#### 4.1 Basics
- **Bombay (Maharashtra), 1867**; founder **Dr. Atmaram Pandurang**.
- Inspiration: visit of **Keshab Chandra Sen**; an **offshoot of the Brahmo Samaj**, reform **within Hinduism**.
- Leaders: **Justice M.G. Ranade** (chief leader in Maharashtra) and **R.G. Bhandarkar** (Sanskrit scholar; joined **1870**).
#### 4.2 Four-Point Social Agenda
1. **Disapproval of the caste system**.
2. **Women's education**.
3. **Widow remarriage**.
4. **Raising the age of marriage** for men and women.
- Also: inter-dining, inter-marriage, **uplift of depressed classes**.
- Method: **persuasion and social work**, not confrontation.
!TRAP Unlike the Brahmos, the Prarthana Samaj did **NOT forbid idol worship** or abandon caste practices – its focus was social, not theological.
#### 4.3 Comparison
| Feature | Paramahansa Mandali | Prarthana Samaj | Young Bengal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nature | Secret | Open, Brahmo offshoot | Radical student group |
| Method | Ate food cooked by lower castes | Persuasion, social work | Debate, attack on orthodoxy |
| Idols | One God | Did not forbid idols | Against idol worship |
| Leaders | Dadoba Pandurang, Durgaram Mancharam | Ranade, Bhandarkar | Derozio |

### 6. M.G. RANADE AND HIS INSTITUTIONS
- View: rigid religious belief blocks social, economic and political progress; **social and religious reform go together**; relied on **legislation**; against child marriage and purdah.

### 7. NATIONAL SOCIAL CONFERENCE, 1887
- Founders: **M.G. Ranade and Raghunath Rao**.
- Reason: the **Congress kept to political issues** and would not take up social reform (Dadabhai Naoroji, 1886 Calcutta session).
- Met at the **same time and place as Congress sessions** = social wing of the Congress.
- Aims: ending **polygamy, child marriage** and patriarchy.

- APPSC GS 2022 key: Dayananda–Arya, Rammohan–Brahmo, Atmaram Pandurang–Prarthana, Derozio–Young Bengal (A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2).
- APPSC GS 2023 body → work: Arya – Vedas; Satyashodhak – untouchables; Sharada Sadan – widows; Prarthana – women and depressed classes.
### 9. AP ANGLE
- **Kandukuri Veeresalingam** founded the **first Prarthana Samaj in Andhra** at **Rajahmundry, 1878** (APPSC 2019).
- **K. Seethamma** later organised its **women's wing**; weekly prayers for women of all castes.

### 12. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS

1. Prarthana Samaj founder = **Atmaram Pandurang**; Ranade was the leader.
2. Prarthana Samaj did **not** forbid idol worship.
3. Paramahansa Mandali was **secret**; Arya Samaj was not.
4. Arya Samaj founded in **Bombay**, not Calcutta.
5. Satyashodhak Samaj = **Phule**, not Dayananda.
6. Ranade was only a **patron** of the Deccan Education Society.
7. Ranade is linked to **Induprakash**; Dnyanodaya was a missionary journal.
8. National Social Conference = **1887**, Ranade and Raghunath Rao.
9. Deoband = religious education, not Home Rule.
10. Derozio was **Anglo-Indian**, teacher at Hindu College.
