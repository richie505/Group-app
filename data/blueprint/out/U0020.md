## U0020 | LATER VEDIC POLITY – TERRITORIAL KINGSHIP, ROYAL SACRIFICES, RATNINS
### 1. FROM TRIBE TO TERRITORY
- **Jana** (tribe) → **janapada** (territorial kingdom); tribal chiefdom → **hereditary territorial monarchy**.
- Kingship mostly **hereditary**, usually to the **eldest son** (Kshatriya); traces of election remained.
- **Rashtra** (territory/state) – word first appears in the Later Vedic setting.
- **Ekarat** = "sole ruler", paramount king (**Aitareya Brahmana**).
- Prepared the way for the **Mahajanapadas** (6th century BCE).
#### 1.1 Titles by Region (Aitareya Brahmana)
| Region | Ruler's title |
|---|---|
| East | **Samrat** |
| South | **Bhoja** |
| West | **Svarat** |
| North | **Virat** |
- State forms: **Rajya, Bhojya, Swarajya, Vairajya, Samrajya**. **Ganatantra is NOT** in the list.
!TRAP East = Samrat, North = Virat – do not swap.

### 3. DIVINE ORIGIN OF KINGSHIP
| Text | Statement |
|---|---|
| **Aitareya Brahmana** | Earliest account: gods, defeated by the **Asuras**, chose a king as war leader; king = **protector of Brahmins** |
| **Shatapatha Brahmana** | Linked the king with **Prajapati** |
- Military leadership + divine sanction; did **not** trace kingship to village election.
- Rajasuya and Vajapeya gave the king **divine qualities**.
### 4. ROYAL SACRIFICES → PURPOSE
| Sacrifice | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Rajasuya** | Royal **consecration**; supreme power; includes **Ratnahavimshi** (offerings at ratnins' houses) |
| **Ashvamedha** | Horse sacrifice; supremacy over **other kings** and control of the area the horse roamed |
| **Vajapeya** | "Drink of strength"; **chariot race** won by the king over his **kinsmen** |
| **Punarabhisheka** | Second anointing at the end of the Rajasuya; uses Aindra Mahabhisheka mantras |
- Titles via yajnas (Ikshvaku kings): **Vajapeya = Samrat/Virat; Ashvamedha = Swarat**.
- Large **Shrauta** sacrifices needed many priests and heavy **dakshina** → strengthened **Brahmanas**.
- AP angle: **Satavahanas, Ikshvakus, Vishnukundins** performed these rites to declare sovereignty; **Ashvamedha altar at Nagarjunakonda**.
!KEY Ashvamedha = over other kings; Vajapeya = over kinsmen; Rajasuya = supreme power.

- Ratnins belong to **Later Vedic** polity.
### 6. ASSEMBLIES AND TAXES
- **Vidatha disappeared**; **Sabha and Samiti continued** under chiefs, Brahmanas and rich nobles; village assemblies handled local cases.
| Tax | Nature |
|---|---|
| **Bali** | Regular, compulsory tribute (earlier voluntary) |
| **Bhaga** | King's share of produce |
| **Shulka** | Customs duty / toll |
- **Bhagadugha** collected; **Sangrahitri** managed; **Vaishyas** = main tribute-payers.
!PAIR Early Vedic | elective chief, voluntary bali | strong assemblies || Later Vedic | hereditary king, bali-bhaga-shulka | assemblies decline
