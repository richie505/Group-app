## U0003 | STONE AGE CULTURES – PALAEOLITHIC, MESOLITHIC, NEOLITHIC, CHALCOLITHIC SITES OF INDIA
### 1. STONE AGE PHASES – OVERVIEW
- Order: **Lower → Middle → Upper Palaeolithic → Mesolithic → Neolithic → Chalcolithic → Iron Age (Megalithic)**.
- The **Indus civilisation** belongs to the **proto-historic** age.
- Most AP questions = **site → phase** (Billasurgam, Giddaluru, Patapadu) and **megalith type → district**.
### 2. PALAEOLITHIC – TOOL TECHNIQUES AND SITES
#### 2.1 Stage-wise Table
| Stage | Technique / tools | Sites |
|---|---|---|
| **Lower** | **Acheulian = handaxe-cleaver**; **Soanian = chopper-chopping** | **Attirampakkam** (TN), **Hunsgi** (Karnataka), **Didwana** (Rajasthan), **Sohan valley** (Pakistan Punjab) |
| **Middle** (c. 50,000–25,000 BCE) | **Flake industry**; prepared-core **Levallois**; scrapers, borers, points | Nevasa, Bhimbetka, Narmada, Godavari, Krishna valleys; **Sembur** (TN) |
| **Upper** (c. 40,000–10,000 BCE) | **Blades, bone tools**; first microliths | **Kurnool caves, Jwalapuram, Patne**, Baghor I and III |
| **Mesolithic** | **Microlithic** (end of the Ice Age) | Bagor, Adamgarh, Langhnaj |
!TRAP Middle Palaeolithic = **flake-tool** stage, NOT the "microlithic stage". Microliths belong to the Upper Palaeolithic and Mesolithic.
!KEY PYQ 2023: **Levallois** technique was used at **Sembur** (TN). No Neanderthal fossil has been found in India.

### 4. NEOLITHIC SITES
| Site | Region | Distinctive find |
|---|---|---|
| **Mehrgarh** | **Baluchistan** | Earliest farming (**wheat, barley**), mud-brick houses |
| **Burzahom** | **Kashmir** | **Pit dwellings**, **dog buried with master** |
| Gufkral | Kashmir | Pit dwellings |
| Chirand | Bihar | Bone and antler tools |

- **Eastern Neolithic** (Birbhanpur, Chirand, Kuchai, Sankarjang): **pointed-butt celts**, **shouldered axes**; ties with **South-East Asia**.
- Growing of cereals began in the **Neolithic**: Koldihwa (rice), Mehrgarh (wheat).
### 5. NEOLITHIC–CHALCOLITHIC SITES OF ANDHRA AND SOUTH INDIA
- **Ash mounds** = burnt remains of seasonal camps of Neolithic herdsmen; at **Sanganakallu** (Ballari, Karnataka), **Piklihal** and **Utnur**.
- **Tekkalakota** (Ballari): Neolithic hill-dwellers; excavated by **M.S. Nagaraja Rao** (1963–64).
- **Palavoy** (Anantapur): four ash mounds and a Neolithic habitation; later phase used iron.
- **Keesarapalli** (Krishna): Chalcolithic **copper tools**. **Patapadu** (Kurnool): Chalcolithic **painted pottery**.
!PAIR Keesarapalli | Krishna | copper tools || Patapadu | Kurnool | painted pottery
### 6. CHALCOLITHIC CULTURES OF INDIA
#### 6.1 Culture-wise Table
| Culture | Type site / sites | State | Date | Pottery / feature |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ahar-Banas** | Ahar, **Gilund, Balathal** | **Rajasthan** (Mewar, Banas river) | c. 2800–1500 BCE | **White-painted black-and-red ware**; copper-using |
| **Kayatha** | Kayatha, Chambal valley | **MP** | 2450–1700 BCE | **Chocolate-slipped ware**; Early Chalcolithic |
| **Malwa** | **Navdatoli** (on the **Narmada**), Eran | **MP** | 1900–1400 BCE | Coarse Malwa ware |
| **Jorwe** | **Inamgaon**, Daimabad; also Savalda | **Maharashtra** | c. 1400–700 BCE | **Painted red ware**; village farming |
- Order of start: **Ahar-Banas → Kayatha → Malwa → Jorwe**.
- **Ahar and Kayatha** lived alongside the **Harappans**; many other Chalcolithic cultures (Malwa, Jorwe) came later.
- **Daimabad** (Pravara river, Maharashtra): fortified, copper objects, **bronze chariot**, buffalo; **southernmost Harappan site**.
- **Savalda**: a Chalcolithic tradition of Maharashtra.
!TRAP **Navdatoli** is a Malwa-culture settlement in **Madhya Pradesh**, not a Gujarat port.
!NUM First metal used by man = **Copper** (Chalcolithic).
### 7. ROCK ART
#### 7.1 Bhimbetka
- Near **Bhopal** (**Raisen**, MP); discovered by **Dr. Vishnu S. Wakankar, 1957** (1957–58); **UNESCO site 2003**.
- Rock shelter with the **most paintings** in India; continuous habitation from **Lower Palaeolithic** to medieval times.
- Upper Palaeolithic art in about **300 caves** and shelters; Mesolithic paintings show **hunting** and people carrying baskets.
- 12 May 2026: after contact with Chalcolithic farmers of **Malwa**, the style became **static**.
- **Mandu fort** is NOT a World Heritage Site; Khajuraho, Bhimbetka, Sanchi are.
#### 7.2 AP Rock Art
- **Borra Caves** (Visakhapatnam), **Kethavaram** (Kurnool); most often painted animal = **deer** (not elephant).
- **Petroglyph** = **carved**; a painting is applied on the surface; in India from the Upper Palaeolithic–Mesolithic.

### 9. MEGALITHS
#### 9.1 Nature
- **Graves with weapons, tools, pottery**, circled by **big stones**; burial sites away from homes; also **memorials**.
- **Iron Age**, with **black-and-red ware**; c. **1500/1000 BCE – 300 CE**.
- AP names: "**Rakshasa Gullu**", also Pandava Gullu, Kurambara Gullu, Morivara Gullu.
- About **12 types** in AP, studied by **N. R. Banerji**; grave goods show ranks – **dolmens for elites, pits for commoners**.
- Black-and-red ware at all Andhra megalithic sites **except Visakhapatnam**.
#### 9.2 Types and AP Districts
| Type | Form | AP distribution |
|---|---|---|
| **Cist** | Stone-slab box | Common in AP; listed for Visakhapatnam, but "many cist burials in Visakhapatnam" is keyed **incorrect** (G2 2025) |
| **Dolmen** | Table-like chamber of large slabs | **Krishna, Chittoor**, East Godavari |
| **Sarcophagus** | Terracotta coffin, often with legs | **Chittoor, Anantapur**; one near **Nallavaram**; not Visakhapatnam |
| **Menhir** | Upright stone, 2–6 m | **Krishna** |
| **Anthropomorphic** | Human-shaped stone | **Tirupati** – India's largest collection |
| Others | Cairn circle, urn burial | – |
- AP megalithic sites: **Nagarjunakonda** (centre), Brahmagiri, Gudivada, **Jonnawada** (Nellore), Boodigapalli, Peddaveedu.
- Kerala: rock-cut burial chamber at **Panayal** (Kasaragod).
