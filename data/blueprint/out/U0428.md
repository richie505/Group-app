### 2. INDIA-ISRAEL
- Full diplomatic relations: **1992**, **P.V. Narasimha Rao** government (he did not visit).
- **Narendra Modi** = first Indian PM to visit Israel (**2017**); visited twice by March 2026.
- Defence (incl. joint R&D) = main pillar.
- **Indo-Israel Centres of Excellence**: horticulture, water-efficient farming; AP centre at **Kuppam** – vegetables and flowers.
- Bilateral Investment Agreement – Sept 2025; FTA Terms of Reference – Nov 2025.
- Trade **$3.75 bn** (2024-25).
- **I2U2** = India, Israel, UAE, USA.
- **Abraham Accords**: Israel normalised ties with four Arab countries, incl. **Bahrain** (Aug 2020) and **UAE** (Sept 2020).
### 3. INDIA-RUSSIA
- Anchors: **1971 Treaty**; **Bhilai**, **Bokaro** plants.
- **S-400** deal 2018; **BrahMos** JV (Brahmaputra + Moskva); **Kudankulam** nuclear plant – **Tamil Nadu** (Kovvada – AP).
- **INSTC** – India-Iran-Russia via Chabahar.
- ~**96%** of trade in national currencies; crude imports from Russia fell Apr-Nov 2025.
- **23rd Annual Summit** (New Delhi): 25th anniversary; **$100 bn by 2030**; "Programme 2030"; Russia joined the **International Big Cat Alliance**.
### 4. INDIA-EU AND GERMANY
- **BTIA** (Broad-based Trade and Investment Agreement) – talks since **2007** (UPSC 2017).
- **Trade and Technology Council** – 2022; first Strategic Dialogue – 2025.
- **16th India-EU Summit** (India, 2026): FTA concluded → EU = India's **22nd FTA partner**; Security and Defence Partnership signed the same day.
- German Chancellor visited India – **Jan 2026**.
### 5. INDIA-JAPAN
- **Mumbai-Ahmedabad High Speed Rail** – **Shinkansen**, Japanese **ODA**.
- Act East Forum; **2+2**; **ACSA** (2020).
- **15th Summit** (2025): **10 trillion yen** (~$67 bn) over ten years; 16th Summit 2026.
- Trade **$27.47 bn** (2025-26); Japan = **5th-largest** FDI source (not largest).
### 6. INDIA-GULF
- UAE: **3rd-largest** trading partner (over **$100 bn**); **first foreign investor** in India's SPR, **Mangaluru**.
- **IMEC** (G20 New Delhi, Sept 2023): east (India-Gulf) + northern (Gulf-Europe) corridors.
- **Oman CEPA**: signed Dec 2025, in force **1 June 2026**; **99%** duty-free; India's **2nd** GCC CEPA after UAE. India-GCC FTA talks on (2026).
### 7. INDIA-AFRICA
| IAFS | Year | Venue |
|---|---|---|
| I | 2008 | New Delhi |
| II | 2011 (24 May) | **Addis Ababa** |
| III | 2015 | New Delhi (all **54** states) |
- IAFS-II: Manmohan Singh promised **$5 bn** lines of credit; **$2 mn** for the AU Mission in Somalia.
- New High Commission in **Sierra Leone** (Oct 2019).
- First **Mahatma Gandhi Convention Centre** – **Niamey, Niger** (Jan 2020).
- **African Union** joined G20 as permanent member (2023, India's presidency).
- **e-VBAB** (e-VidyaBharati, e-ArogyaBharati) – tele-education, tele-medicine.
- Tanzania: **IIT Madras Zanzibar** (first overseas IIT, 2023); **$1.1 bn** LoCs for water.
