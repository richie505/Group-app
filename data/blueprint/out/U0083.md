## U0083 | POLITICAL OVERVIEW OF ANDHRADESA, 11TH TO 16TH CENTURIES
### 1. DYNASTIES IN CHRONOLOGICAL ORDER
| Order | Dynasty | Start / period | Founder / marker | Capital(s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Eastern Chalukyas** | 7th–11th c. | Merged with the Cholas under **Kulottunga I** | Vengi, Rajahmundry |
| 2 | **Kakatiyas** | Feudatory 956; **sovereign 1163–1323** | Early chief Gundyana; **Rudradeva** declared independence **1163** | **Hanamkonda → Orugallu (Warangal)** |
| 3 | **Reddis** | **1325**; 14th–15th c. | **Prolaya Vemareddy** in Pakanadu | **Addanki → Kondavidu** |
| 4 | **Musunuri Nayakas** | 1330s | **Prolaya Nayaka**; **Kapaya Nayaka (1336–1368)** | Orugallu |
| 5 | **Velamas (Recherla)** | 14th–15th c. | **Singama** of Racherla | **Rachakonda, Devarakonda** |
| 6 | **Vijayanagara** | **1336–1646** | **Harihara and Bukka**; Sangama, Saluva (Narasimha), Tuluva (Veeranarasimha), Aravidu | Hampi; later **Penugonda, Chandragiri** |
| 7 | **Bahmanis** | **1347** | **Hasan Gangu** | **Gulbarga** |
| 8 | **Gajapatis (Odisha)** | **1435** | **Kapilendra** (1435–1467); later Gajapati Prataparudra | Odisha |
| 9 | **Imad Shahis (Berar)** | **1490** | **Fathullah Imad-ul-Mulk** | **Ellichpur (Achalpur)** |
| 10 | **Qutb Shahis (Golconda)** | **1518–1687**, 7 sultans | **Sultan Quli Qutb-ul-Mulk** | **Golconda** (old name Mankal / Mangalavaram) → **Hyderabad** |
- Order of emergence: **Vijayanagara (1336) → Bahmanis (1347) → Gajapatis (1435) → Imad Shahis (1490)**.
- APPSC order: **Kakatiyas of Warangal, Reddis of Kondaveedu, Vijayanagara Rayas, Bahmanis, Gajapatis of Orissa**.
- **Reddis came after the Kakatiyas** (not before).
- **Unifiers of all Telugu lands**: 1st **Satavahanas**, 2nd **Kakatiyas**, 3rd **Qutb Shahis**.
- Earlier founders: **Simuka** – Satavahana; **Shantamula I** – Ikshvaku (Krishna valley); **Vijaya Deva Varma** – Salankayana; **Pulakesi I** – Chalukya. **Vinayavatthu** (Buddhist text) mentions **Andhrapatha** (APPSC 2019).
### 2. KAKATIYA FALL AND THE NAYAKA KINGDOMS
- **Musunuri Nayakas, Reddis, Velamas** = three of **Prataparudra's generals** who survived the Kakatiya fall (**1323**); later the **76 Nayaks**.
- **1323** = end of Telugu imperial unity until the **Qutb Shahis** reunited the region.
- **Anavota Reddy** (c. 1355) shifted the Reddi capital from **Addanki to Kondavidu**, a fortified site, against the Velamas and Bahmanis; a later branch ruled from Rajahmundry.
- **Kapaya Nayaka** ceded **Golconda fort** to Bahmani **Muhammad I** after his son **Vinayaka** was killed (**1361**).
### 3. DELHI SULTANATE INVASIONS OF ANDHRA
| Year | Sultan | Commander / result |
|---|---|---|
| **1303** | Alauddin Khalji | **First Muslim attack on Orugallu** – repulsed |
| 1309–10 | Alauddin Khalji | **Malik Kafur** – Prataparudra became a **tributary** |
| 1318 | Khalji | Another attack |
| **1323** | **Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq** | **Ulugh Khan** (later **Muhammad bin Tughlaq**) **captured Prataparudra**; Warangal renamed **Sultanpur** |
- Muslims invaded under the Kakatiyas – **from 1303, five invasions, ending 1323**.
!KEY Ulugh Khan (1323) captured Prataparudra; Malik Kafur (1309–10) only took tribute.
### 4. FEUDATORY FAMILIES THAT BECAME KINGDOMS
| Family | Earlier overlord | Became |
|---|---|---|
| **Kakatiyas** | **Rashtrakutas, then Kalyani Chalukyas** | Sovereign **1163** (Rudradeva) |
| Musunuri, Reddis, Velamas | Kakatiya Nayakas | Independent after 1323 |
| **Kota family** | **Kakatiya** subordinate (married Ganapatideva's daughter **Ganapamba**) | Local rulers of **Dharanikota** |
| **Kayasthas** | Kakatiya loyalists | **Revolted under Ambadeva** (Kadapa region, **1272**) with **Pandya** support |
| **Qutb Shahis** | Bahmani **tarafdar of Telangana (1496)** | Independent **1518** |
!TRAP Kota family was subordinate to the **Kakatiyas**, not the Gajapatis.
### 5. CONTESTED REGIONS
- **Raichur Doab (Raichur Antarvedi)** – between the **Krishna and Tungabhadra**; fertile black soil and strategic = **main cause of Bahmani–Vijayanagara wars** (APPSC 2003, 2017), from **Harihara I's** time.
- **Krishna-Godavari doab / coastal Andhra** – a **separate** coastal dispute among **Reddis, Velamas, Gajapatis, Vijayanagara and the Bahmanis**.
- **Sultan Quli Qutb Shah** took **Rajahmundry, Eluru** and the Krishna-Godavari region.
- After **Talikota (1565)**, Golconda took **Kondavidu, Machilipatnam** and the coastal districts.
!TRAP Raichur Doab = Krishna–Tungabhadra; **not** the Krishna–Godavari doab.
### 6. BATTLES → CONTENDING DYNASTIES
| Battle | Year | Sides |
|---|---|---|
| **Palnadu** | 12th c. | **Nalagamaraju** (backed by Kakatiya Rudradeva) vs rivals |
| **Muttukur** | **1263** | **Ganapatideva vs Pandyas** |
| **Bhimavaram** | **1368** | **Recherla Velamas** killed **Kapaya Nayaka** (Musunuri) – Velamas got Telangana |
| **Devarakonda** | 1458 | Gajapati Kapilendra's prince **Hamvira** defeated the Bahmanis |
| **Kondavidu and Kondapalli sieges** | 1515–16 | **Krishnadevaraya vs Gajapatis** |
| **Raichur** | **1520** | **Krishnadevaraya** defeated the **Adil Shah** of Bijapur |
| **Rakshasi-Tangadi (Talikota / Bannihatti)** | **26 Jan 1565** | Vijayanagara (**Aliya Rama Raya killed**) vs **Golconda, Bijapur, Ahmadnagar, Bidar** |
- Vijayanagara empire was destroyed in the **Talikota** war, 1565; **Berar did not join**.
!TRAP Bhimavaram (1368) = **Velamas vs Musunuri Kapaya Nayaka**, not Reddis.
### 7. BAHMANI BREAK-UP AND QUTB SHAHIS
- **Mahmud Gawan executed 1482** → five Deccan Sultanates: **Golconda (Qutb Shahi), Bijapur, Ahmadnagar, Bidar, Berar (Imad Shahi)**.
- **Berar** annexed by **Ahmadnagar in 1574**.
- **Golconda (Hyderabad) state**: **Sultan Quli Qutb Shah, 1518** (Nizami Mosque inscription; APPSC 2011 key "Who founded Hyderabad State?").
- Later Asaf Jahi Hyderabad State: **Chin Qilich Khan (Nizam-ul-Mulk)**.
- **Hyderabad city**: **Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah, 1591**; **Charminar 1594**.
- Last: **Abul Hasan Tana Shah (Tanisha)** – Shia, Persian-using state; removed by **Aurangzeb** in **1687**.
!PAIR State | Sultan Quli | 1518 || City | Muhammad Quli | 1591
### 8. REGIONAL SUCCESSION – PREDECESSOR → SUCCESSOR
| Region | Sequence |
|---|---|
| **Telangana** | Kakatiyas → **Delhi Sultanate (1323)** → Musunuri Nayakas → Velamas / Bahmanis → Qutb Shahis |
| **Coastal Andhra** | Eastern Chalukyas → Velanati Chodas → Kakatiyas → Reddis → Gajapatis / Vijayanagara → Qutb Shahis (after 1565) |
| **Rayalaseema** | Kakatiyas (Kayastha chiefs) → Vijayanagara → Qutb Shahis |
- In news: new **Seshachalam inscriptions** (16th c.) linked to the **Tuluva or early Aravidu** period of Vijayanagara.
