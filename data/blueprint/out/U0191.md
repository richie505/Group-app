## U0191 | ARYA SAMAJ AND DAYANANDA SARASWATI – SATYARTH PRAKASH, SHUDDHI, DAYANAND ANGLO-VEDIC SCHOOLS
### 1. DAYANANDA SARASWATI – LIFE
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Original name | **Mool Shankar (Tiwari)** |
| Born | **1824**, **Tankara, Morvi (Gujarat)** |
| Guru | **Swami Virajananda (Virjanand) of Mathura** |
| Died | **1883**, **Ajmer** |
| Main work | **Satyarth Prakash** (1875), in **Hindi** |
| Body founded | **Arya Samaj**, Bombay, 1875 |
- **200th birth anniversary** of Dayananda and **150 years of the Arya Samaj** were marked at the **International Arya Mahasammelan 2025** (Jnana Jyoti Festival), **New Delhi** (Nov 2025).
!TRAP Guru = **Virajananda of Mathura**; died at **Ajmer**, not Lahore (Lahore was later the Arya Samaj HQ).

#### 2.1 "Back to the Vedas"
- The **four Vedas infallible** = **primordial (original) word of God**.
- Rejected later accretions of the **Puranas** (myths, rituals, idol worship).
- Claimed the Vedas held all true knowledge, including science → countered the colonial idea of Western intellectual superiority.
### 3. SATYARTH PRAKASH
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Author | **Swami Dayananda Saraswati**, **1875** – his greatest work |
| Language | **Hindi** |
| Meaning | **"The Light of Truth"** (also "The True Exposition") |
| Themes | His religious and social philosophy; Vedic religion explained; critique of Hindu orthodoxy and of other religions (Islam, Christianity, Jainism, Buddhism) |
| Attacked | **Orthodoxy, idolatry, caste by birth, child marriage** |
!TRAP Satyarth Prakash did **NOT support orthodoxy** – it attacked it. Its language is **Hindi**, not Sanskrit.
### 4. ARYA SAMAJ, 1875 – FOUNDING AND BASE
- Founder: **Swami Dayananda Saraswati**.
- Founded at **Bombay**, **10 April 1875** (Girgaon).
- Name means **"Society of Nobles"**.
- Strongest base: **Punjab**; **HQ later moved to Lahore**; also strong in Rajasthan and the United Provinces.
- **Ten Principles** drafted by Dayananda – truth, rational thought, moral action, **welfare of all**.
- The principles stress that God is the source of all true knowledge, that the **Vedas** are the books of true knowledge, that one must accept truth and give up untruth, and that the aim of the Samaj is to do good to the whole world – physical, spiritual and social.
- Caste was to be decided by **merit and conduct**, not by birth.
!TRAP Founded in **Bombay**, NOT Calcutta; it **rejects** idol worship (never "promotes idol worship").
#### 4.1 Programme
| Rejected / opposed | Supported |
|---|---|
| Idol worship, polytheism, ritualism | Widow remarriage |
| Caste by birth, untouchability | Inter-caste marriage |
| Child marriage, purdah | Women's education |
- Nature: **revivalism + reform**; revivalist like Deoband, unlike the reformist Brahmo and Prarthana Samajes.
#### 4.2 Arya Samaj vs Other Reform Bodies
| Feature | Arya Samaj | Brahmo Samaj | Prarthana Samaj |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder | Dayananda Saraswati | Rammohan Roy | Atmaram Pandurang |
| Year, place | 1875, Bombay | 1828, Calcutta | 1867, Bombay |
| Nature | **Revivalist** + reform | Reformist (reason) | Reformist, milder |
| Scripture | **Vedas infallible** | No scripture final; Vedas not faultless | Reform within Hinduism |
| Idol worship | **Rejected** | Rejected | Not forbidden |
| Main base | **Punjab** (HQ Lahore) | Bengal | Maharashtra |
| Conversion work | **Shuddhi** (reconversion) | None | None |
- Arya Samaj was an **open** body, not a secret society (unlike the Paramahansa Mandali).
- Like the Deoband movement, it looked back to its own sacred tradition for reform.
- **"Nursery of militant nationalists"**: influenced **Lala Lajpat Rai, Swami Shraddhanand, Bhagat Singh's family**.
### 5. REFORM BODIES – YEAR OF FOUNDING
| Body | Year | Founder |

### 6. SHUDDHI MOVEMENT
- Started by: **Arya Samaj** (Dayananda) – NOT Brahmo Samaj, Satyashodhak Samaj or Ramakrishna Mission.
- Aim: ritual **"purification"** to **reconvert** Hindus who had become **Muslims or Christians**, and admit outsiders into the Hindu fold.
- Later leader: **Swami Shraddhanand** – 1920s campaign through the **Bharatiya Hindu Shuddhi Sabha (1923)**.
- Shraddhanand gave a speech on **Hindu-Muslim unity** from the pulpit of **Jama Masjid, Delhi**, on **4 April 1919**; assassinated **1926**.
- Method: a purification ritual after which the person was accepted back into Hindu society; it also opened the fold to outsiders.
- Impact: contributed to the **communalisation** of social life and, later, to communal political consciousness.
!KEY "Shuddhi was started by ___" → **Arya Samaj**.
- Criticism: Shuddhi and harsh attacks on other faiths are blamed for **deepening communal divides**.
### 7. DAV AND GURUKUL – THE SPLIT
#### 7.1 Dayanand Anglo-Vedic Schools
- **DAV school, Lahore**, opened **1886** in Dayananda's memory, with **Lala Hansraj** as headmaster; grew into **DAV College, Lahore**.
- Blended Vedic values with **English-medium** modern education.
#### 7.2 Split of 1893
| Faction | Leaders | Stand |
|---|---|---|
| **College (DAV) party** | **Lala Hansraj, Lala Lajpat Rai** | Kept control of DAV College; modern English education |
| **Gurukul party** | **Munshi Ram (later Swami Shraddhanand)** | Vedic, **Gurukul** education; set up **Gurukul Kangri (1902)** near **Haridwar** |
!PAIR DAV College | Lahore | Hansraj, Lajpat Rai || Gurukul Kangri | Haridwar, 1902 | Munshi Ram (Shraddhanand)
### 8. ARYA SAMAJ IN HYDERABAD STATE
- **Satyagraha of 1938–39** in Hyderabad State against the **Nizam's curbs on religious and civil rights**.
- Ran **alongside the Hyderabad State Congress satyagraha**.
- Helped build political awakening against Nizam rule in the **Telangana** region.
!KEY Hyderabad satyagraha = **1938–39**, against the **Nizam's curbs** on religious and civil rights – not a Shuddhi campaign.
