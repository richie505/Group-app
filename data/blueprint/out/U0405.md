## U0405 | WOMEN'S POLITICAL PARTICIPATION – 73RD/74TH AMENDMENTS, 106TH AMENDMENT AND GENDER INDICES
### 1. WHERE WOMEN'S RESERVATION APPLIES
| Body | Article | Enabled by |
|---|---|---|
| Panchayats | **243D** (seats 243D(3); chairs 243D(4)) | 73rd, 1992 |
| Municipalities | **243T** (243T(3) / 243T(4)) | 74th, 1992 |
| Lok Sabha | **330A** | 106th, 2023 |
| State Assemblies | **332A** | 106th, 2023 |
| Delhi Assembly | **239AA(2)(ba)** (directly elected and SC seats; no ST seats) | 106th, 2023 |
| Rajya Sabha, Legislative Councils | **No provision** | – |
- **Arts 325 and 326** = equal franchise; **Arts 84/173** = women can contest.
- Before 2023, seats for women were reserved **only in PRIs/local bodies**; Arts 330-334 had no provision for women, so a **constitutional amendment** was needed.
!TRAP The 106th Amendment does **not** cover the **Rajya Sabha or Legislative Councils**.
### 2. 73RD/74TH AMENDMENTS – WOMEN AND SC/ST IN LOCAL BODIES
#### 2.1 Article 243D
- **243D(2):** not less than **one-third of SC/ST seats** for SC/ST women (not 50%).
- **243D(3):** not less than **one-third of total seats** in every Panchayat.
- **243D(4):** not less than one-third of **Chairperson** offices at all three levels.
- **243T** gives the same for municipalities; ST seats in municipalities = **243T(1)**.
- SC/ST seats in proportion to population; reserved seats and chairs **rotate**. **BC reservation** is left to States (optional).
- **Arunachal Pradesh** – no SC reservation (no SC population).
- Minimum age to contest: **21** (**243F, 243V**). Panchayat tenure **5 years** from first meeting (**243E(1)**).
- **73rd in force 24 April 1993** (National Panchayati Raj Day).
- **Sunset:** SC/ST reservation tied to Art. 334 (to **2030**, 104th Amendment; 243D(5)); **women's reservation has no sunset**.
!TRAP **243D(3)** = women's seats; **243D(4)** = women chairpersons.
#### 2.2 States at 50%
- One-third is a **floor, not a ceiling**. **Bihar** first with **50%** (2006); then MP, Uttarakhand, HP. **19 States/UTs** now give 50%.
- **Andhra Pradesh:** **50%** in panchayats and municipalities (APPSC 2019: "1/2 of the seats"). AP 2026: 34% BC (rural) and 33.33% (urban) approved.
- Women ~**46%** of PRI representatives (2026); over 49.7% of elected panchayat members nationally.
- UP Panchayat Laws (Amendment) Act 1994: one-third for women (in force 22 April 1994).

### 4. 106TH AMENDMENT – NARI SHAKTI VANDAN ADHINIYAM
- Articles inserted: **330A, 332A, 239AA(2)(ba), 334A**.
- **One-third** of seats, including one-third of SC/ST seats; **no OBC sub-quota**.
- **Art. 334A:** starts after the **first Census after the Act + delimitation** (2027 Census → not before **2034**); lasts **15 years**, extendable by Parliament; seats **rotate** after each delimitation.
- In force for **J&K and Puducherry** Assemblies from **17 April 2026**.
#### 4.1 131st Amendment Bill, 2026
- Aimed to raise Lok Sabha **550 → 850** on the **2011 Census** to start the quota from 2029.
- Vote **298-230** (528 present) – short of **352** needed under **Art. 368**.
- Women's groups demand removing the Census-delimitation condition.
!PAIR Art. 334A | Duration and rotation | 106th || Art. 368 | Special majority | 131st Bill failed

### 8. STUDIES, PROXY REPRESENTATION AND SOCIAL OUTCOMES
- **Chattopadhyay-Duflo (2004):** random pradhan reservation in **West Bengal and Rajasthan**; women leaders invested more in **drinking water, roads**.
- Reserving Pradhan/Pramukh/Adhyaksha posts brought a distinct change in working.
- **Proxy ("sarpanch-pati"):** male relative runs the office informally; does not legally replace her; declines in a second term.
- Dalit/tribal representatives: shield of **SC/ST (PoA) Act 1989** and **Protection of Civil Rights Act 1955**.
