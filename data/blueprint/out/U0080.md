## U0080 | EASTERN CHALUKYA ADMINISTRATION, SOCIETY AND RELIGIOUS CONDITIONS
### 1. ADMINISTRATION – STRUCTURE AND OFFICES
- The kingdom followed the **Saptanga** idea of the state (seven limbs).
- **Ashtadasa Tirthas** = **eighteen administrative offices**.
- **Official language: Sanskrit**; Jainism and Buddhism were patronised alongside Hinduism.
#### 1.1 Administrative Terms → Meaning
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ashtadasa Tirthas** | 18 offices |
| **Tirthulu** | **Council of ministers**; **Samaharta** = its chief member |
| **Vishaya / Mandala** | Province (state unit) under a **Vishayapati / Mandaleswara** |
| **Kottam** | Another territorial subdivision found in records |
| **Grameyaka / Gramani / Urgavunda** | **Village head** |
| **Vanneyus** | **Toll collectors** |
| **Sthanadhipati** | Officer managing **temples** and their land endowments |
!PAIR Tirthulu | Council of ministers | Chief = Samaharta || Ashtadasa Tirthas | 18 offices | Saptanga state
#### 1.2 Nadus → Region
| Nadu | Region |
|---|---|
| **Venginadu** | Parts of **Krishna and Godavari** |
| **Kammanadu** | **Bapatla, Ongole, Narasaraopet** |
| **Pakanadu** | **Nellore** |
- **Pithapuram and Vengi** were the Eastern Chalukya capitals (later Rajamahendravaram).
!TRAP Pakanadu = **Nellore**, not Krishna; Kammanadu = Bapatla–Ongole–Narasaraopet area.
### 2. SOCIETY
- Social order: **Varna-based**, with **Brahmin privilege**.
- **Agraharas**: **tax-free villages** given to **Brahmins**, who performed **Hiranyagarbha** and **Bahusuvarna** rituals.
- Inscriptions record merchants such as a **Setti Shreshthi** (chief merchant) in Chalukya Bhima I's Kakumanu inscription.
#### 2.1 Tribal Assimilation
- **Jayasimha II** brought the **Koya Kottalu** and others into the **Brahmanical fold**.
- **Panduranga** (commander of Gunaga Vijayaditya III) **subdued the Boyas**.
### 3. ECONOMY – GRANTS, GUILDS AND OVERSEAS TRADE
- Land grants: tax-free **agraharas** to Brahmins; land endowments to temples from kings and vassals; village grants to Jains.
- Tolls collected by **Vanneyus**.
- **Penugonda** = **headquarters of the merchant guilds** in medieval Andhra.
#### 3.1 Overseas Trade
| Fact | Ruler |
|---|---|
| **Coins found in Arakan (Burma)** | **Saktivarma I** |
| Trade with the **eastern islands** | **Rajaraja Narendra** |
| **72 merchants sent to China** | **Kulothunga I** |
!NUM 72 = merchants sent to China by Kulothunga I; 18 = Ashtadasa Tirthas
### 4. RELIGIOUS CONDITIONS – SECTS AND PATRONAGE
#### 4.1 Vedic Hinduism and Shaivism
- **Jayasimha I** performed the **Ashvamedha**; royal **prashastis** (praise-poems) boast of several Ashvamedhas.
- **Shaivism was dominant**; sects: **Kapalika, Kalamukha, Pasupata**.
- **Chalukya Bhima I** built the **Pancharamas**.
#### 4.2 Vaishnavism
- **Kubja Vishnuvardhana** – Vaishnava; built the **Kunti Madhava temple, Pithapuram**.
- **Danarnava** – Vaishnava; **Mandavya Narayana temple, Samalkot**.
- **Pancharatra and Vaikhanasa** = **Vaishnava** traditions (not Shaiva).
#### 4.3 Jainism and Buddhism
- **Potlacheruvu** = the leading **Jain** centre of the period (G2 2017 key) – **not Buddhist**.
- **Vimaladitya** = the **only Vengi Chalukya king who became a Jain**.
- Under Brahmanical pressure many Jains **migrated to Karnataka**, e.g. the family of **Basaveshwara**.
- **Buddhism**: declining but surviving at **Amaravati and Nagarjunakonda**.
!TRAP Jain persecution at Potlacheruvu by **Kalamukhas and Virashaivas** came **later, in the Kakatiya age**. The Eastern Chalukyas supported **Jainism, Buddhism and Hinduism** – a sign of religious tolerance.
#### 4.4 Eastern Chalukya Age vs Kakatiya Age – Religion
| Feature | Eastern Chalukya age | Kakatiya age |
|---|---|---|
| Royal policy | Tolerant – Hinduism, Jainism and Buddhism all patronised | Shaiva dominance |
| Jainism | Royal and queenly grants (Vijayawada, Ramatirtham, Mushikonda, Kaluchumbarru) | **Persecution** – Potlacheruvu attacked |
| Sects active | Kapalika, Kalamukha, Pasupata; Pancharatra, Vaikhanasa | Kalamukha and **Virashaiva** pressure on Jains |
| Buddhism | Declining; survives at Amaravati and Nagarjunakonda | Further decline |
| Official language | Sanskrit | Sanskrit |
#### 4.5 Rulers → Religious Act
| Ruler | Religious act |
|---|---|
| Kubja Vishnuvardhana | Vaishnava; Kunti Madhava temple |
| Jayasimha I | Ashvamedha |
| Vishnuvardhana III | **Mushikonda village to the Jains** |
| Chalukya Bhima I | Pancharamas |
| Ammaraja II | **Kaluchumbarru** and **Maliyapundi** grants to Jain temples |
| Danarnava | Mandavya Narayana temple, Samalkot |
| Vimaladitya | Became a Jain; **Ramakonda cave temple, Ramatirtham** |
### 5. JAIN BASADIS AND GRANTS → ROYAL DONOR
| Site / grant | Donor |
|---|---|
| **Nedumba Vasati, Vijayawada** | **Queen Ayyana Mahadevi**, wife of **Kubja Vishnuvardhana** (G2 2025 key) |
| **Ramatirtham (Vizianagaram)** – Ramakonda cave temple | **Vimaladitya**, for his Jain guru **Trikala Yogi Siddhanta Deva** (Ramateertham inscription) |
| **Mushikonda village** | **Vishnuvardhana III** |
| **Kaluchumbarru grant** | **Ammaraja II**, at the request of courtesan **Chamekamba** |

!KEY Vimaladitya – Ramatirtham – guru Trikala Yogi Siddhanta Deva; Ammaraja II – Chamekamba – Kaluchumbarru.
### 6. EDUCATION – GHATIKAS AND AGRAHARAS
- **Ghatikas** = **advanced Sanskrit schools** attached to **agraharas or temples**; settlements of Brahmin scholars studying scriptures, philosophy and Vedic learning.
- Heads of Ghatikas = **Ghatika Samanyulu**, e.g. **Mandasharma** at **Asanapura Ghatika**, Kammanadu.
- Subjects: **Vedas, Vedangas, Dharmashastras, Tarka, Vyakarana, Arthashastra, Dhanurveda, Ayurveda**.
- **Jain pupils** began studies with **"Om Namah Siddham"**.
!TRAP **Hallisaka** (a dance) was **not** a Ghatika subject.
### 7. TEMPLE INSTITUTIONS, MUSIC AND DANCE
- Temples received land endowments from kings and vassals, managed by the **Sthanadhipati**.
- **Musicians and dancers** were attached to temples and courts, e.g. **Chellavva**.
- Popular dance: **Hallisaka (= Kolatam)**.
- Sculptures of musical instruments at **Bhairavakona**.
- **Panchadarla** (Dharapalem, **Anakapalli** district) – **Sri Uma Dharmalingeswara** temple; pillars carry **Eastern Chalukya and 15th-century** inscriptions; **ASI recorded 17 inscriptions in 1899** (South Indian Inscriptions Vol. VI); in news **Aug 2026**.

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- **Only Jain king** of the dynasty: **Vimaladitya**.
- Leading Jain centre: **Potlacheruvu**.
- Chief of the council (Tirthulu): **Samaharta**.
