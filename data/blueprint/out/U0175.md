## U0175 | CHARTER ACTS OF 1793, 1813, 1833 AND 1853 – PROVISIONS AND SIGNIFICANCE
### 1. CHARTER ACTS COMPARED
| Act | Trade | Key provisions |
|---|---|---|
| 1793 | Monopoly extended 20 years | Charter renewed **20 years**; Board of Control members' **salaries paid from Indian revenues** |
| 1813 | Ended **India trade monopoly**; kept **tea and China** trade | **Rs 1 lakh** education grant; missionaries allowed; charter 20 years |
| 1833 | Ended **tea/China** monopoly; commercial functions ended | **GG of India**; Law Member; **Section 87**; laws called "**Acts**" |
| 1853 (**last**) | – | Separate legislature; **open competitive exam**; no fixed term |
### 2. TRADE MONOPOLY AND COMPANY STATUS
| Monopoly | Ended by |
|---|---|
| Trade with India (general) | **Charter Act 1813** |
| Tea trade and trade with China | **Charter Act 1833** |
- 1813 asserted the **sovereignty of the British Crown** over the Company's Indian possessions.
- 1833: commercial rights ended; Company purely **administrative**; Europeans free to settle.
- 1853: Company's authority renewed with no fixed term; territories held "**in trust for Her Majesty, her heirs and successors**" until Parliament provides otherwise.
!TRAP "1813 abolished the Company's trade monopoly" = not true; tea and China trade were kept.
### 3. CHARTER ACT 1813 – EDUCATION AND MISSIONARIES
- **Education grant: Rs 1,00,000 (1 lakh) a year** – first step towards State responsibility for education.
- **Missionaries** allowed to enter and preach (Evangelical pressure: **William Wilberforce, Charles Grant**).
### 4. CHARTER ACT 1833 – PEAK OF CENTRALISATION
- **GG of Bengal → GG of India**; first: **Lord William Bentinck** (also last GG of Bengal).
- GG-in-Council got **exclusive legislative power** for all British India; Presidencies lost law-making; laws called **Acts** (not Regulations).
- **Law Member** added as **4th member**: **T.B. Macaulay** (1834). The Act did not provide for an Indian Law Member.
- **Section 87**: no Indian barred from office by **religion, place of birth, descent or colour**.
- **Agra Presidency**: present UP separated from Bengal Presidency (1834) → renamed **North-Western Provinces (1836)**.
### 5. CHARTER ACT 1853 – SEPARATE LEGISLATURE AND OPEN COMPETITION
- Legislative and executive functions separated; **6 new legislative members** → Central Legislative Council ("**mini-Parliament**"), **12 members** in all.
- The 6: **4 appointed by provincial governments** (Madras, Bombay, Bengal, Agra) + **Chief Justice** + one Calcutta Supreme Court judge.
- Directors' **patronage abolished** → open competitive exam (Directors appointed till 1853).
- Board of Control salaries fixed by the Crown, paid from Company funds.
- **Macaulay Committee (1854)**: competitive exams instead of nomination; preference for graduates.
### 6. MACAULAY AND OTHER PERSONS
- **Macaulay**: "The Company is an **anomaly** but it is part of a system where everything is an anomaly" (Commons speech, 1833 Bill); headed **First Law Commission (1834–38)** → drafted **IPC**; **Minute on Education (1835)**.
- **Raja Ram Mohan Roy**: first demanded Indians in services (1832).
- **Fort William College** (1800, Wellesley) → **Haileybury** (1806).
### 7. CENTRALISATION TREND 1773–1861
| Act | Step |
|---|---|
| Regulating Act 1773 | GG of Bengal; Bombay and Madras subordinated |
| Pitt's India Act 1784 | **Board of Control** |
| Charter Act 1833 | GG of India; exclusive legislative power – **peak** |
| Charter Act 1853 | Central Legislative Council with provincial members |
| Indian Councils Act 1861 | Restored power to Bombay, Madras (decentralisation) |
- Chronology: Plassey 1757 → Buxar 1764 → Diwani 1765 → 1773 → 1784 → 1813 → **GoI Act 1858** (Crown rule).

### 10. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. "Held in trust" = **Charter Act 1853**, not Regulating Act.
2. Grant = **Rs 1 lakh**, not 10 lakh.
3. 1853 new members included the **Chief Justice**, not the Commander-in-Chief.
4. Law Member = **4th member**; no Indian provided for.
5. Open competition = **1853**; Macaulay Committee = **1854**.
6. Agra Presidency 1834 → NWP **1836**.
7. **1813** ended India trade; **1833** ended tea/China.
8. Board of Control = **1784**, not a Charter Act.
