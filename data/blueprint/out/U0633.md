## U0633 | INDIA – ROCKS AND MINERAL BELTS; DISTRIBUTION OF METALLIC AND NON-METALLIC MINERALS
### 1. ROCK SYSTEMS AND MINERALS
| Rock system | Where | Minerals |
|---|---|---|
| **Archaean / Dharwar** (late Archean; **oldest** schist belts, formed from changed Archean rocks) | Peninsular India | Metallic: **iron ore**, gold, manganese, mica, cobalt, chromium, copper, tungsten, lead – **economically most important** |
| **Cuddapah** | Kadapa basin, AP – one of India's largest Proterozoic basins | **Barytes, asbestos**, copper, iron, lead, diamond, uranium |
| **Vindhyan** (Semri = Lower; **Kaimur, Rewa, Bhander** = Upper) | Central India; Kaimur–Rohtas (Bihar) | **Limestone**, sandstone (Bhander mainly sandstone), **diamonds** (Panna, near Kaimur–Rewa horizon) |
| **Gondwana** (upper Palaeozoic–Mesozoic; **latest** of Dharwar–Cuddapah–Gondwana) | Damodar, Son, Mahanadi, Godavari valleys | **Coal** (~95%; 99.54% as of 1 Apr 2022) |
| Deccan Trap (basalt) | Deccan, Malwa | Black soil; poor in metallic ores |
| **Tertiary** sedimentary | Assam, Gujarat, Rajasthan, offshore | **Petroleum, natural gas** |
| Metamorphic | — | **Garnet** |
- Coal areas: Raniganj, **Jharia**, Karanpura, Bokaro (Damodar), Pench Valley; **Bisrampur** (Surajpur, Chhattisgarh). Over 90% of coal lies in more than one valley.
- **Gangetic plains** = recent alluvium, **poor in minerals**; peninsular plateau mineral-rich. Natural gas: marine sedimentary basins.
- **Rohtas** (Bihar, Vindhyan) has no Dharwar rocks; Bihar's Dharwar: Munger, Nawada, Jamui.
- **AP rock systems**: Dharwar – Anantapur, Chittoor, Kadapa (>2,500 million years; iron ore, gold); Kurnool group – cement limestone; Eastern Ghats Mobile Belt (khondalite, charnockite) – bauxite, manganese, graphite.
!TRAP Gondwana coal is not explained by "most minerals not linked to Gondwana".

### 3. DEPOSIT TYPES AND OCCURRENCE
| Type | Formation | Minerals |
|---|---|---|
| **Placer** | Heavy minerals in alluvial sands | **Gold** (APPSC 2024), tin, platinum |
| Veins / lodes | Cracks in igneous, metamorphic rocks | Tin, copper, zinc, lead |
| Sedimentary beds | Layers | Coal, gypsum, salt, potash |
| Residual weathering | Decomposed rock (laterite) | **Bauxite** |
- **Native state**: Au, Ag, Pt (and Cu). **Never free**: K, Na, Ca, Mg, **Al** (top of reactivity series).
- **Beach sands** (seven heavy minerals): **rutile, zircon, sillimanite, garnet, ilmenite, monazite, leucoxene** (brown ilmenite). Kerala sands: ilmenite, zircon, sillimanite (not tungsten); world-class between **Neendakara and Kayamkulam**. AP sands: Srikakulam, Visakhapatnam.
- **Monazite**: thorium, lanthanum, uranium, cerium; **not** in all Indian coastal sands; Kerala largest **thorium** producer (**IREL, Udyogamandal, Aluva**). India self-sufficient in thorium; **uranium** = most important nuclear fuel.
### 4. ORES → METAL
| Metal | Ores |
|---|---|
| Aluminium | **Bauxite** (Al₂O₃·xH₂O), cryolite (Na₃AlF₆), corundum (water-free Al₂O₃; ruby, sapphire), diaspore |
| Iron | **Haematite** Fe₂O₃, **magnetite** Fe₃O₄ (magnetic), limonite FeO(OH)·nH₂O, siderite FeCO₃, pyrites FeS₂ |
| Copper | **Chalcopyrite**, chalcocite (Cu₂S), cuprite, **malachite** Cu₂(CO₃)(OH)₂, azurite |
| Lead / Zinc | **Galena** PbS / **zinc blende** ZnS, smithsonite ZnCO₃ |
| Mercury / Tin | **Cinnabar** HgS / **cassiterite** SnO₂ |
| Silver | **Argentite** Ag₂S, horn silver |
| Manganese | **Pyrolusite** MnO₂, manganite, hausmannite |
| Titanium | **Ilmenite** FeTiO₃, **rutile** TiO₂ (not Mn, not thorium) |
| Thorium | **Monazite** |
| Tungsten | **Wolframite** (China leads) |
| Na / K / Mg / Ca | Rock salt NaCl / sylvine, **carnallite** KCl·MgCl₂·6H₂O / magnesite MgCO₃, dolomite CaMg(CO₃)₂ / limestone CaCO₃, gypsum CaSO₄·2H₂O, fluorspar CaF₂ |
!NUM Fe content: magnetite (70–72%) > haematite (60–70%) > limonite (40–60%) > siderite (lowest). Haematite = most used; largest Indian reserve.
- **Talc**: softest mineral, magnesium silicate (softness from weak layers). **German silver**: Cu 60, Ni 20, Zn 20. **Marble** = non-metallic.
- **Non-ferrous**: nickel, zinc, copper, aluminium, tin, titanium, lead, gold, silver, cobalt, tungsten, mercury.

### 7. NON-METALLIC MINERALS
#### 7.1 Mica
- In **pegmatite veins** of granite, gneiss, schist; varieties **phlogopite** (Mg mica), muscovite (APPSC G1 2023).
- Insulator; electrical, electronics, cosmetics. India **leads sheet-mica** production.
- Belts: **Koderma–Gaya–Hazaribagh–Munger** ("Capital of Mica"; ruby mica), Nellore (AP), Ajmer–Bhilwara. Reserves: Rajasthan, AP, Bihar, Jharkhand.
- Old PYQ keys: biggest mica producer – Bihar; Rajasthan "not producing mica".
#### 7.2 Others → Use and State
| Mineral | Use | Areas |
|---|---|---|
| **Limestone** | Cement, steel flux, glass | MP, Rajasthan, AP (Kurnool), Gujarat; **Kaimur Plateau** (Banjari cement) |
| **Gypsum** | Cement, fertiliser, plaster | **Rajasthan** |
| **Dolomite** | Steel flux, refractories | Chhattisgarh (Bilaspur, Janjgir, Bastar, Durg, Raipur), Odisha, AP; Sonbhadra (UP) |
| **Kyanite** | Refractories | **Lapsa Buru** (Jharkhand) |
| **Sillimanite** | Refractories | **Sona Pahar** (Meghalaya), Kerala sands |
| **Asbestos** | Fire-proofing | Rajasthan; AP – Pulivendula (Kadapa); old key – Bihar |
| **Barytes** | Oil-well drilling mud | **Mangampet** (Kadapa) – among world's largest |
| Marble | Building | **Makrana** – Taj Mahal, Victoria Memorial |
- Non-metallics also: phosphorite, garnet, wollastonite, vermiculite, feldspar, kaolin. Mirzapur: andalusite, calcite.
#### 7.3 Andhra Pradesh ("Ratna Garbha")
- **Rank 1**: mica (Nellore), asbestos (Kadapa), vermiculite and apatite (**Kasipatnam**; AP **sole apatite** producer), limestone (Kurnool – Bethamcherla, Koilakuntla).
- **Largest bauxite reserve**; bauxite highest share after beach sands; iron ore mainly haematite (Anantapur 1st).
- Agnigundala, Bondalamottu (Guntur): copper, zinc, silver. Diamond reserves 1,822,955 carats. **Anantapur: 15 REEs** (NGRI, 2023).
- **Swarnagiri (Jonnagiri)**, Tuggali, Kurnool: **first private gold mine** since Independence, from **24 Jun 2026**; Geomysore; open-pit, 30-year lease; 4% royalty.
- Boksampalli gold blocks (Anantapur; gold + PGE) to be auctioned (Jun 2026). Mineral revenue Rs 2,707 crore (to 8 Jan 2026).

### 10. MINING LAW AND POLICY
- **MMDR Act 1957**: Ministry of Mines (with GSI, IBM); all minerals except petroleum, gas, atomic. Major minerals – Centre (chromite, kyanite, sillimanite; not bentonite); **minor minerals – States** (**Section 15**).
| Amendment | Change |
|---|---|
| **2015** | Auction-only; **DMF** and **NMET** created |
| 2021 | NMET autonomous; captive mines may sell output |
| 2025 | New minerals added to leases; States approve in 30 days; captive-sale limit removed |
| 2026 Bill (Aug) | Limits States' power to tax mineral rights – fiscal federalism debate |
- **DMF** protects mining-affected persons (not exploration); **PMKKKY** (2015) with yearly audit.
- **MADA v. SAIL (2024)**: royalty is **not a tax**. SC (Jul 2026) upheld royalty on Average Sale Price including royalty, DMF, NMET.
- **Art. 297**: offshore minerals vest in Union; OAMDR Amendment 2023 – auction-only leases. **National Mineral Policy 2019** replaced 2008.
- Meghalaya Assembly opposed uranium mining (27 Aug 2026).
