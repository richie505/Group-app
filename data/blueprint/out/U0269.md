## U0269 | DEVELOPMENT OF TELUGU LITERATURE AFTER 1956 – DIGAMBARA, VIPLAVA, DALIT, FEMINIST AND MUSLIM MINORITY STREAMS
### 1. THE STREAMS IN ORDER
- Post-1956 streams moved Telugu literature from **classical/romantic themes** to **caste, class, gender and minority identity**.
- Base of feminist and Dalit fiction: **Gidugu–Gurajada's spoken-Telugu revolution**.
| Stream / body | Year | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Digambara kavulu** | **1965** | "Naked poets"; three volumes of Digambara Kavitvam (1965–68) |
| **Virasam** (Viplava Rachayitala Sangham) | **4 July 1970** | First president **Sri Sri** |
| **Dalit literature** | Roots in **Gurram Jashuva**; surge after **Karamchedu (1985)** | Dalit Mahasabhas formed |
| **Feminist poetry** | **Neeli Meghalu (1993)** | Edited by **Volga** and others |
| **Muslim minority poetry** | **1990s** | Anthology **Jaljala** |
!NUM 1965 = Digambaras; 1969 = Srikakulam struggle; 1970 = Virasam.
### 2. DIGAMBARA KAVULU
- Six **anti-establishment** "naked poets" of the mid-1960s.
| Pen name | Real name |
|---|---|
| **Cherabandaraju** | **Baddam Bhaskara Reddy** |
| **Mahaswapna** | **Kammisetti Venkateswara Rao** |
- **Cherabandaraju, Jwalamukhi, Nikhileswar** moved to **Virasam in 1970**.
### 3. VIPLAVA SAHITYAM – VIRASAM
- Context: **Srikakulam peasant struggle (1969)** under **Vempatapu Satyam** spread Naxalism in Andhra.
- Trigger: Visakhapatnam students' pamphlet **"Writers, where do you stand?"** (1970).
- Founded **4 July 1970**; first president **Sri Sri** (not Gaddar).
- **Sri Sri**: **Maha Prasthanam** broke Telugu **prosody**; a call to the oppressed. Also the **first National Award for a Telugu lyricist** ("Teluguveera Levara", film Alluri Seetharamaraju).
### 4. DALIT LITERATURE
#### 4.1 Gurram Jashuva
- **First modern Telugu Dalit poet**; works: **Gabbilam (1941), Firdausi, Kandiseekudu (The Refugee), Kreesthu Charitra**.
#### 4.2 Anthologies and Collections
| Work | Editor / author | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Nallapoddu** | **Gogu Syamala** | **First collection of Dalit women's writing** (G2 2017 key) |
| **Chikkanavutunna Pata** | **G. Lakshmi Narasaiah** and Tripuraneni Srinivas | Dalit poetry anthology (1995) |
| **Padunekkina Pata** | **G. Lakshmi Narasaiah** | Dalit poetry anthology (1996) |
!TRAP "Mallepoddu / Mallapoddu" in one version of the 2017 paper is the same question as Nallapoddu: answer **Syamala** in both.
### 5. PROTEST POEMS – CENTRAL IMAGE
| Poem | Image / messenger | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Gabbilam** | A **bat** carries a hungry Dalit's message to **Lord Shiva** | Bat outside the temple, in darkness = the **untouchable** (barred from the temple) |
| **Firdausi** | The **Persian poet** cheated of his reward by **Sultan Mahmud** | Poet as **rebel against patronage** |
- Gabbilam is modelled on **Kalidasa's Meghaduta** (cloud-messenger).
!TRAP Gabbilam did **not** win the Sahitya Akademi award (PYQ 2025).
### 6. FEMINIST POETRY
- **Neeli Meghalu (1993)**: feminist anthology edited by **Volga** and others.
- Poets: **Jayaprabha, Vimala, Kondepudi Nirmala**.
- **Bhumika** magazine: edited by **Kondaveeti Satyavathi**.
### 7. MUSLIM MINORITY LITERATURE
- **Jaljala**: anthology of Telugu **Muslim minority poetry of the 1990s**.
- **Puttumacha** ("Birthmark"): long poem on **Muslim identity** by **Khadar Mohiuddin**.
### 8. NOVELS – WORK AND AUTHOR
| Work | Author | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Jakkadona** | **R.C. Krishnaswamy Raju** | APPSC 2023 key |

- Distractors **Tulasidalam, Mahaprasthanam, Vennello Aadapilla** sit beside Veyi Padagalu in author-work questions.
### 9. TELUGU JNANPITH AWARDEES
| Awardee | Work | Year |
|---|---|---|
| **Viswanatha Satyanarayana** (first; "Kavi Samrat", "Abhinava Nannaya") | **Ramayana Kalpavrukshamu** | **1970** |
| **C. Narayana Reddy** | **Vishwambhara** | **1988** |
| **Ravuri Bharadwaja** | **Paakudu Raallu** | **2012** |
- Viswanatha wrote **over 100 works**.
- CM Naidu recalled all three awardees at the **World Telugu Mahasabhalu (January 2026)**.
- Telugu: one of India's **six classical languages**, **10 crore+** speakers (Jan 2026).
- Comparative note: Bengali Dalit poet **Kalyani Thakur Charal's** translated anthology won the **A.K. Ramanujan Prize** (Feb 2026).
!KEY Viswanatha's Jnanpith work = **Ramayana Kalpavrukshamu**, not Veyi Padagalu; Vishwambhara = **C. Narayana Reddy**.
