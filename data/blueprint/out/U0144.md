## U0144 | BHAKTI SAINTS AND THEIR TEACHINGS – RAMANUJA, MADHVA, NIMBARKA, VALLABHA, RAMANANDA
- **Exams:** Group 1 Prelims | **MED**
- **Rows:** A-121 Bhakti saints and their teachings – Ramanuja, Madhva, Nimbarka, Vallabha, Ramananda
- **PYQ importance:** APPSC 4 questions | other exams 16
### 1. BHAKTI BACKGROUND
- Loving devotion towards God; originated in **South India** (Tamil region): **Alvars – Vishnu; Nayanars – Shiva**.
- The acharyas gave Bhakti a **philosophical base against Advaita** and opened temple worship to lower groups.
| Saint | Key fact |
|---|---|
| Chaitanya | Krishna devotee of **Bengal**; Gaudiya Vaishnavism |
| Kabir | **Sakhi, Sabad, Ramaini** |
| Mirabai | Devotee of Krishna |
| Raskhan | Muslim devotee of Krishna; **Prem Vatika** |
| **Shankaradeva** | Brought Vaishnavism to **Assam (Kamarupa) and Cooch Behar**; **Ekasarana** sect – one deity (Krishna), against rituals and idolatry |
### 2. ACHARYA → SCHOOL, TEACHING AND DEITY
| Acharya | School | Teaching emphasis | Deity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shankaracharya** (788-820) | **Advaita** (non-dualism) | Brahman the only reality; mukti through knowing **Atman = Brahman**; knowledge | – |
| **Ramanuja** (1017-1137) | **Vishishtadvaita** (qualified non-dualism, "non-dualism with differentiation") | Salvation through intense devotion (**prapatti**) to Vishnu; souls and universe dependent parts of the Supreme; reaction against Advaita | **Vishnu / Narayana with Lakshmi** |
| **Madhvacharya** (13th c.) | **Dvaita** (dualism) | God, souls and world **eternally distinct**; **Saguna** bhakti | **Vishnu** (Krishna at Udupi) |
| **Nimbarka** (12th-13th c.) | **Dvaitadvaita** (dualistic non-dualism) | Devotion to Radha-Krishna | **Radha-Krishna** |
| **Vallabhacharya** (1479-1531) | **Shuddhadvaita** (pure non-dualism) | **Pushtimarg** – "path of grace"; **Vatsalya bhakti**; accepted **householder life** | **Child Krishna (Shrinathji)**; Radha-Krishna |
| **Ramananda** | – | Replaced Vishnu worship with devotion to **Rama** (and Sita) | **Rama** |
- Ramanuja: the best means of salvation is **devotion** – not Vedas, logic or meditation (UPSC 2022).
- Vallabha developed the views of **Vishnuswami**.
- **Abhinav Gupta** is **not** a Vedanta scholar; Vedanta scholars: Shankaracharya (8th c.), Ramanuja (11th c.), Madhva (13th c.).
!TRAP **Madhva did NOT preach Nirguna bhakti** (RPSC 2024). Advaita = Shankara; Vishishtadvaita = Ramanuja; Dvaita = Madhva; Dvaitadvaita = Nimbarka; Shuddhadvaita = Vallabha.
!KEY APPSC G1 2012: Shankara = **Advaita**.
### 3. CHRONOLOGY, CONTEMPORARIES AND FIRSTS
- **Order**: Shankara (788-820) → **Ramanuja** (1017-1137) → **Nimbarka** (12th-13th c.) → **Madhva** (13th c.) → **Ramananda** → **Vallabha** (1479-1531) → **Chaitanya** (1485-1533).
- Short order: Ramanuja → Nimbarka → Ramananda → Vallabhacharya; Shankaracharya (8th c.) → Ramanuja → Chaitanya (15th-16th c.).
- **Contemporaries**: **Vallabha and Chaitanya**.
| FIRST | Fact |
|---|---|
| Forerunner of the Bhakti cult | **Shankaracharya** (Advaita) |
| First true exponent of Bhakti | **Ramanuja** |
| First Bhakti saint to preach in **Hindi** | **Ramananda** |
| First medieval reformer to denounce caste outright | **Ramananda** |
!TRAP **Nimbarka was NOT a contemporary of Akbar** (UPSC 2019). Shankara = forerunner (knowledge); Ramanuja = first true exponent (devotion).

### 5. BIRTHPLACE AND MAIN CENTRE
| Acharya | Birthplace | Main centre |
|---|---|---|
| Shankaracharya | **Kaladi** (Kerala) | Mathas; Sringeri head **Vidyaranya** was the Vijayanagara-era proponent |
| **Ramanuja** | **Sriperumbudur** (Tamil Nadu) | **Srirangam** |
| **Madhva** | **Udupi** | **Udupi Krishna Matha** |

### 6. RAMANANDA – LANGUAGE, CASTE AND DISCIPLES
- Preached Vaishnavism in **North India**; first to use **Hindi** (vernacular, not Sanskrit). With his disciple **Kabir** he helped Hindi grow.
- Rejected caste and **Brahmin supremacy**: **"Let no man ask a man's sect or caste."**; pupils from all castes.
- Called a "disciple of Ramanuja" – read as a **follower of Ramanuja's school** (Ramanuja died 1137).
| Disciple | Social background |
|---|---|
| **Kabir** (one of his **12** disciples) | **Weaver** – born to a Brahmin widow near Varanasi; raised by **Neeru and Neema** |
| **Raidas (Ravidas)** | **Shoemaker** / leather worker |
| **Sena** | **Barber** |
| **Dhanna** | **Jat cultivator** |
| **Padmavati** | **Woman** disciple |
| **Pipa** | **Rajput ruler** (of Gagron) |
### 7. VIJAYANAGARA COURT AND DVAITA IN THE DECCAN
- **Vallabha** at **Krishnadevaraya's** court refuted Shaiva claims and **defeated Dvaita scholar Vyasaraya (Vyasa Tirtha)**.
- **Vyasa Tirtha**: **Rajaguru** of **Saluva Narasimha and Krishnadevaraya**.
- **Narahari Tirtha** (Madhva's disciple) spread Dvaita in **Andhra-Kalinga** from **Srikurmam**.
!TRAP Vallabha defeated Vyasaraya; Vyasa Tirtha = royal guru of Krishnadevaraya – not the reverse.
### 8. RAMANUJA AND ANDHRA / TIRUMALA
- Vishishtadvaita (11th-12th c.) grounded Bhakti in Andhra; **Sri Vaishnavism = most widespread sect of the Vijayanagara era** (Tirupati, Mangalagiri).
- Patrons: **Velanati Cholas** and **Palnati Haihayas**.
- Inspired **Palnati Brahmanaidu's Veera Vaishnava** movement (casteless society; **Chapakudu** – common dining).
- Gave **Panchamas** temple entry.
- **Tirumala**: credited with ordering temple worship and installing **Govindaraja** at Tirupati.
- **Vedanta Desika** spread Vadakalai in Andhra (14th c.).
- Called **"Ilaya Azhwar"**; held to be an incarnation of **Adisesha/Lakshmana**.
- **Statue of Equality**, Hyderabad – sitting statue honouring Ramanuja; world's second-tallest sitting statue, inaugurated by the PM (UPSC 2022).
!KEY APPSC GS 2022: Ramanuja's movement in Andhra (Vishnu devotion + social equality) = **Bhakti movement**.

### 11. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. Dvaita = Madhva; Dvaitadvaita = Nimbarka.
2. Madhva taught **Saguna**, not Nirguna.
3. Nimbarka was not Akbar's contemporary.
4. Vallabha and Chaitanya were contemporaries.
5. Vadakalai = Vedanta Desika.
6. Ramananda's deity = **Rama**, not Krishna.
7. Abhinav Gupta is not a Vedantin.
8. Sri Bhashya = Ramanuja; Anubhashya = Vallabha.
