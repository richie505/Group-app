## U0581 | CENTRAL GOVERNMENT'S ASSISTANCE FOR BUILDING A NEW CAPITAL – COMMITMENTS AND ACTUAL RELEASES
### 1. COMMITTEES ON THE CAPITAL
| Committee | Set up by / Chair | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Expert Committee (Sec 6)** | **Centre**; **K.C. Sivaramakrishnan** | To find locations, report within **6 months**; reported **August 2014**; opposed a single "**super capital**" (ecological fears) |
| **Capital City Advisory Committee** | **AP government**; **P. Narayana**, Municipal Administration Minister | Backed the **Vijayawada-Guntur** region |
| **G.N. Rao Committee + BCG study** | – | Basis of the **three-capitals** plan (2020) |
- **AP Cabinet** (not the Centre) chose **Amaravati** on the **Krishna**, between Vijayawada and Guntur.
!TRAP Sivaramakrishnan = Centre's Sec 6 committee (APPSC 2018); P. Narayana = State committee; G.N. Rao = three capitals.

### 3. CAPITAL CITY PROJECT – AMARAVATI
- **APCRDA Act, 2014** in force **30 December 2014**; voluntary **Land Pooling Scheme** announced **January 2015**; consent for about **33,000 acres** by **March 2015**.
- **22 October 2015**: PM **Narendra Modi** laid the **foundation stone** at **Uddandarayunipalem** on the Krishna (Guntur district).
- Pooling farmers exempted from capital gains tax and stamp duty on first sale.
- CPI(M) asked for a single-phase capital within the original **29 villages** (27 March 2026).
#### 3.1 Three-Capitals Detour (2020)
| Capital | City |
|---|---|
| Legislative | **Amaravati** |
| Executive | **Visakhapatnam** |
| Judicial | **Kurnool** |
- **3 March 2022**: AP High Court struck down the **CRDA Repeal Act (2020)**, upholding Amaravati.
- **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Act, 2026**: Amaravati = **sole and permanent capital**, retrospective from **2 June 2024**, with **₹15,000 crore** quick central assistance.
### 4. DIRECT CENTRAL GRANTS AND RELEASES
- Total direct grants under Sec 94(3): **₹2,500 crore** = **₹1,500 crore** initial (capital construction) + **₹1,000 crore** later.
- The ₹1,000 crore went to **underground drainage (UGD)** works in **Vijayawada and Guntur**.
- **Cost gap**: AP estimate about **₹1.2 lakh crore** vs Centre's commitment about **₹27,000 crore** → heavy borrowing.
- Central ministries audit claims; rejected bills need fresh "**mother sanctions**"; **₹828 crore** of bills rejected in one year-end rush.
### 5. UTILISATION CERTIFICATES DISPUTE
- **Finance Ministry**: balance ₹1,000 crore released in phases only after **UCs** for the ₹1,500 crore.
- **AP's position (2018)**: **NITI Aayog** did not reject its UCs.
- **Centre/BJP**: UCs were incorrect; little built despite ₹2,500 crore.
### 6. EXTERNAL FUNDING
| Lender | Amount | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| **World Bank + ADB** | **₹15,000 crore** | Concessional, **0.6% + TONA** |
| **HUDCO** | **₹11,000 crore** | Term loan for urban infrastructure |
| **NaBFID** | ₹7,500 crore | Infrastructure finance |
| **NABARD (NIDA)** | ₹7,387.70 crore | Infrastructure assistance |
- **Union Budget 2024-25**: promised **₹15,000 crore** of **Centre-facilitated multilateral** support; fast-tracked January 2026; sanctioned (18 August 2026).
- Dispute: AP wants **non-repayable grants**; Centre gives **loans with sovereign guarantees** (**Article 293(2)** approvals).
- Criticism: **repayment burdens**; may push out welfare and development spending.
- AP debt projected **₹7.11 lakh crore** (about **36% of GSDP**).
!TRAP ₹2,500 crore = **grants** (Sec 94(3)); ₹15,000 crore = **World Bank-ADB loans**.
