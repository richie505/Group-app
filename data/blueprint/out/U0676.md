#### 2.1 Awards
- **Prime Minister's Trophy** for best integrated steel plant (instituted **1992-93**): **SAIL Bhilai** holds the record (11 wins); **Tata Steel** won for 2014-15, 2015-16 and 2016-17.
### 3. LOCATIONAL FACTORS OF STEEL PLANTS
- Raw materials are **weight-losing**, so plants sit on the **Chota Nagpur** coal-ore belt (Jharkhand, Odisha, Chhattisgarh, West Bengal).
- **Coastal sites** (Vizag) use imported coking coal; domestic coking coal is **high-ash**.
- Plants put away from raw materials for regional balance: **Salem, Visakhapatnam, Vijayanagar**.
### 4. RAW MATERIALS, PROCESS MIX AND MINI STEEL
- Process mix (8 Sep 2026): **43% BF-BOF, 22% EAF, 35% induction furnace**; BF-BOF share projected at 56% by 2030.
- Mini/secondary units melt scrap or **sponge iron** in EAF and induction furnaces.
- India = **leading producer of sponge iron (DRI)**.
- **Leading sponge iron states**: **Odisha** (1st) and **Chhattisgarh** (2nd), then West Bengal and Jharkhand. Units working in 2020-21: 285 in all; Odisha 76, Chhattisgarh 69, West Bengal 36, Jharkhand 24.
- 2024-25: iron ore output 289.398 MT (imports 6.4 MT, adequate); coking coal output 66.49 MT but imports **57.07 MT**.
- Old framing (IAS 1993): India self-sufficient in coking coal for three decades. Planning Commission Energy Policy report: petroleum reserves to end in 22 years, gas 30, coal 80.
### 5. NATIONAL STEEL POLICY 2017, PLI AND GREEN STEEL
| Target by 2030-31 | Figure |
|---|---|
| Capacity | **300 MT** |
| Crude steel production | **255 MT** |
| Inputs needed | 437 MT iron ore + 161 MT coking coal |
- **PLI for Specialty Steel**: approved 2021.
- **National Green Hydrogen Mission**: Ministry of Steel has **₹455 crore** for green-hydrogen pilots in steel till 2029-30.
- Do not confuse: ₹25,938 crore = PLI-Auto; ₹33,660 crore = BHAVYA; ₹3,000 crore = NICDIT (BE 2026-27).
- Emissions: Indian steel **2.54 t CO2/t** vs world average **1.9**; 10–12% of national CO2; EU CBAM exposure.
- Draft notification brings iron and steel under the **Carbon Credit Trading Scheme**: cuts for 255 plants from FY 2026-27 (19 Jul 2026).
### 6. VIZAG STEEL – AP ANGLE
- 1965-66 agitation slogan: "**Visakha Steel – Andhra's Right**".
- Chosen over Bailadila, Goa, Neyveli, Salem and Hospet (not Durgapur).
- Foundation stone **20 January 1971**; first blast furnace named **Godavari** (foundation 1982).
- Explosion killed 8 (8 Jun 2026); 3-member probe panel (10 Jun 2026).
### 7. ALUMINIUM – COMPANIES AND PLANTS
- **2nd most important** metallurgical industry after steel; India **2nd** in primary aluminium; **7th** in bauxite reserves.
- **Odisha** = largest bauxite reserves and production; Eastern Ghats deposits (Koraput, Kalahandi).
- **Power-oriented**: about **14,000–15,000 kWh per tonne** (not negligible) → smelters near hydel or captive thermal power.
