## U0398 | HUMAN RIGHTS – CONCEPT, GENERATIONS, UDHR, ICCPR, ICESCR AND UN MACHINERY
### 1. CONCEPT OF HUMAN RIGHTS
- **Inherent, inalienable** rights held simply because one is human.
- Rest on **human dignity** (UDHR Preamble: dignity and justice); **universal** across cultures.
- **Indivisible and interdependent**: civil-political and socio-economic rights stand together (**Vienna Declaration, 1993**).
- Every **Fundamental Right** is a human right, but not every human right is a Fundamental Right.
- **Right to vote in India** = only a **statutory right** (**Section 62, RP Act**), not an FR or DPSP.
- First document of the human rights campaign: **Magna Carta (1215)** → 1688 → **1776** (US Declaration of Independence) → **1789** (French Revolution).
### 2. GENERATIONS OF HUMAN RIGHTS
- Proponent: **Karel Vasak (1977)**; linked to **liberty, equality, fraternity**.
| Generation | Ideal | Rights |
|---|---|---|
| **First** | Liberty | **Civil and political**: life, speech, fair trial, vote, participation in political life |
| **Second** | Equality | **Economic, social, cultural**: work, education, health, **eradication of social discrimination** |
| **Third** | Fraternity / solidarity | **Collective**: healthy environment, development, peace |
- Generations overlap: education is both a second- and a third-generation right.
!TRAP Eradication of social discrimination and Right to Education = **second** generation (JPSC 2016).
### 3. UDHR, 1948
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Adopted by | **UN General Assembly**, **10 December 1948** |
| Human Rights Day | **10 December** (UNGA resolution **423(V), 1950**) |
| Drafting chair | **Eleanor Roosevelt** |
| Structure | Preamble + **30 articles** |
| Legal status | **Not a treaty**, not legally binding |
- Covers civil, political, economic, social and cultural rights; guides the **SDGs Agenda**.
- **Included:** dignity, liberty, equality, life, adequate standard of living, education; right to work (economic-social); free speech, religion, equality before law (civil-political).
- **NOT included:** right to **universal basic income** (JPSC 2023).
- **Privacy:** UDHR **Art. 12**, ICCPR **Art. 17**; but the JPSC 2016 key treated privacy as not a UN-listed human right.
- Context: UN came into being **24 Oct 1945**; India's Constitution adopted **26 Nov 1949**.
!TRAP ICCPR **Art. 19** = expression, not privacy (privacy = Art. 17).
### 4. INTERNATIONAL BILL OF HUMAN RIGHTS
- Components: **UDHR + ICCPR + ICESCR + optional protocols**.
| Instrument | Adopted | In force | Key content |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ICESCR** | 16 Dec 1966 | **3 Jan 1976** | Work, social security, health, education; realised progressively |
| **ICCPR** | 16 Dec 1966 | **23 Mar 1976** | Life (Art. 6), no torture (Art. 7), fair trial (Art. 14), privacy (Art. 17), expression (Art. 19), **child rights (Art. 24)** |
| ICCPR First Optional Protocol | 1966 | – | Individual complaints |
| ICESCR Optional Protocol | **2008** | – | Complaints |
- **India:** party to ICCPR since **1979**; has **not signed the First Optional Protocol**, so Indians cannot take complaints to the UN (6 May 2026).
!PAIR ICESCR | in force | Jan 1976 || ICCPR | in force | Mar 1976
!KEY Child rights: ICCPR **Article 24**; also **Article 24** of the Indian Constitution (child labour).

### 7. UN HUMAN RIGHTS MACHINERY
#### 7.1 Human Rights Council
- Set up **2006**, replacing the **UN Commission on Human Rights**; based in **Geneva**.
- **47 members** elected by the UNGA; **3-year terms**, at most **two consecutive** terms.
- **Two-thirds** UNGA vote can suspend a member for gross violations.
| Region | Seats |
|---|---|
| Africa | **13** |
| Asia-Pacific | **13** |
| Latin America and Caribbean | **8** |
| Western Europe and Others | **7** |
| Eastern Europe | **6** |
- **Universal Periodic Review (UPR):** peer review of every UN member's record; cycles of about **4.5 years** since **2008**.
- India was a member for **2019-21** and **2022-24**.
#### 7.2 Other Bodies
| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| **OHCHR** | Set up **1993**, after the Vienna Conference |
| **Treaty bodies** | Expert committees monitoring each core treaty |
| **Special rapporteurs** | HRC "special procedures": **unpaid independent experts** on a theme or country |
- **Jun 2026:** OHCHR convened the **Global Alliance for Human Rights** (Imagine-Dialogue-Act model) ahead of the UDHR's **80th anniversary (Dec 2028)**.
### 8. PARIS PRINCIPLES AND NHRIs
- Adopted by the **UNGA in 1993**.
- Content: **independence and autonomy** from government, **pluralist** composition, adequate resources, **powers to investigate** (not reliance on the executive).
- **GANHRI** accredits NHRIs via its **Sub-Committee on Accreditation**: **"A" = fully**, **"B" = partly** compliant.
- **India's NHRC:** set up **12 Oct 1993** under the Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993; **statutory**, not constitutional; 2019 amendment: three human-rights members, including a **woman**.
!KEY 1993 = Vienna Declaration + OHCHR + Paris Principles + NHRC.
