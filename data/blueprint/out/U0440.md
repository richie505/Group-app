### 2. RURAL EMPLOYMENT – SCHEME LINEAGE
#### 2.1 Chronology from IRDP to VB-G RAM G
| Scheme | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **IRDP** | 1978-79 (selected blocks); **nationwide 1980** | Sixth Plan self-employment scheme; **TRYSEM** and **DWCRA** (1982-83) were its sub-programmes |
| **NREP** | **1980** (not 1983) | Wage employment |
| **RLEGP** | **1983** | Employment for landless households |
| **JRY** | **1989** | **NREP + RLEGP** merged |
| **EAS** | **1993** | Employment Assurance Scheme |
| **SGSY** | **1 April 1999** | Merged **IRDP, TRYSEM, DWCRA** and allied schemes |
| **SGRY** | **2001** | **EAS + JGSY** |
| **Bharat Nirman** | **2005** | Six rural components: **irrigation, roads, housing, drinking water, electrification, telephony** |
| **MGNREGA** | **2005** | 100 days per household per year |
| **NRLM** | **2011** | Restructured from SGSY; renamed **DAY-NRLM in 2016** |
| **VB-G RAM G** | Introduced 16 Dec 2025 | Replaces MGNREGA; **125 days** |
!TRAP JRY = NREP + RLEGP; SGRY = EAS + JGSY; SGSY = IRDP + TRYSEM + DWCRA.
### 3. MGNREGA AND VB-G RAM G
#### 3.1 MGNREGA Features
- **100 days** of **unskilled manual work** per household per year.
- **One-third** of workers had to be **women**.
- **Gram Panchayat** plans and carries out works on the **Gram Sabha's** recommendation; **Gram Sabha** ran the **social audit**.
- **Unemployment allowance** if work is not given within **15 days**; paid by States.
- Section 4(5): Centre fixes State-wise normative allocations.
- Links to **SDG 1 (No Poverty)** and **SDG 8 (Decent Work)**.
#### 3.2 Comparison
| Feature | MGNREGA 2005 | VB-G RAM G |
|---|---|---|
| Full name | Mahatma Gandhi NREGA | **Viksit Bharat–Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin)** |
| Guarantee | 100 days | **125 days** |
| Implementing body | Gram Panchayats | **Panchayati Raj Institutions** |
| Funding | Centre paid wages; material 75:25 | **CSS 60:40; NE/Himalayan 90:10; UTs without legislature 100%** |
| Allowance | States, after 15 days | Allowance and **delay compensation fully by States** |
| Outlay | – | Total ₹1,51,282 crore; Centre ₹95,692.31 crore |
| Status | **Repealed** | Assent **20 Dec 2025**; notified **11 May 2026**; in force **1 July 2026** |
- Tagline of VB-G RAM G: **"Rozgar Bhi, Samman Bhi"**; Act passed within two days of introduction.
- AP Budget 2026-27: **₹8,365 crore** to VB-G RAM G (15 Feb 2026).
- CAG audit (16 Aug 2026): planned Green India Mission–MGNREGS convergence not achieved.
!NUM VB-G RAM G dates: introduced 16 Dec → assent 20 Dec 2025 → notified 11 May 2026 → in force 1 July 2026
### 4. RURAL DEVELOPMENT AND LAND SCHEMES
#### 4.1 MP-Linked Schemes
| Scheme | Ministry | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **SAGY** (Saansad Adarsh Gram Yojana) | **Rural Development** | Launched **11 October 2014** – birth anniversary of **Lok Nayak Jayaprakash Narayan** (not Gandhi); MPs pick villages and develop **model villages** using existing programmes |
| **MPLADS** (1993) | **MoSPI** | Funds **non-lapsable**; earmark **15% SC** and **7.5% ST** areas |
#### 4.2 DAY-NRLM (MoRD; Centrally Sponsored)
- Funding **60:40**; **90:10 NE incl. Sikkim**; **UTs 100% Centre** (original NRLM ratio was 75:25).
- Implemented via State Rural Livelihood Missions; Delhi and Chandigarh excluded.
- Sub-schemes: SVEP, AGEY, MKSP, **DDU-GKY**, **RSETIs**.
- **Lakhpati Didi**: **3 crore women** earning over **₹1 lakh a year**.
#### 4.3 Rural Infrastructure and Land
| Scheme / body | Year | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **PMGSY** | 2000 | All-weather road connectivity to unconnected habitations |
| **Hariyali** | **2003** | **Watershed development through PRIs** |
| **SPMRM** (Shyama Prasad Mukherji Rurban Mission) | **2016** | 300+ **Rurban clusters**; "rural soul, urban amenities"; Centre-State **60:40** |
| **National Council for Land Reforms** | 2008 | Chaired by the **Prime Minister** |
| **DILRMP** | Began as NLRMP (2008); **Central Sector from 2016** | Land-record modernisation |
| **SVAMITVA** | **24 April 2020** (National Panchayati Raj Day) | **Ministry of Panchayati Raj**; **drone survey** of village houses; **property cards** |
### 5. LIVELIHOOD AND ENTERPRISE SCHEMES
| Scheme | Ministry | Objective / key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **PM Vishwakarma** (2023) | **MSME** | Artisans in **18 traditional trades**: ID card, training with **₹500/day stipend**, toolkit up to **₹15,000**, collateral-free credit up to **₹3 lakh at 5%**; 24.50 lakh trained (Jul 2026) |
| **PM SVANidhi** (**1 June 2020**) | **MoHUA**; SIDBI | Working-capital loans for **street vendors** after COVID-19: first loan **₹10,000**, **7% interest subsidy**, digital cashback; 75 lakh+ vendors |
| **PM-AJAY** (2021-22, CSS) | **Social Justice** | SC-majority villages; Adarsh Gram component (earlier **PMAGY, 2009-10**): villages with **500+ people and over 40% SC**, ₹20 lakh per village; 47,300 villages, 16,700 Adarsh Grams (Jul 2026) |
| **PMEGP** (Aug **2008**) | MSME; nodal **KVIC** | Merged **REGP + PMRY**; credit-linked subsidy for micro-enterprises; with KVIBs and **DICs** |
| **Stand-Up India** (**5 April 2016**) | Finance | Bank loans **₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore** for **greenfield** enterprises; at least **one SC/ST and one woman** borrower per bank branch |
!TRAP PM Vishwakarma is NOT for "fishermen with non-mechanised boats" (APPSC 2023).
