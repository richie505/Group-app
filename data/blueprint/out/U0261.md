### 3. AYYANKI VENKATA RAMANAYYA
#### 3.1 Associations and Journal
- **Secretary, Ramamohan Library**, Vijayawada (**1911**).
- Founder-secretary of the **Andhra Desa Library Association** (**1914**).
- Edited ***Grandhalaya Sarvasvam*** (**1915**).
- Chiefly organised the **All India Public Library Conference**, **Madras, November 1919**; elected General Secretary of the All India Public Library Association at its second conference (**December 1923**).
- Helped found library associations in order: ADLA (1914) → **All India Public Library Association (1919)** → **Bengal (1925)** → **Madras (1928)** → **Punjab (1929)**.
!TRAP All India Public Library Association = **1919** (Madras conference); **1933** = the **Indian Library Association**, formed at Calcutta.
#### 3.2 Ideas and Work
- **Conceived the library pilgrimage** (village tours, **1934–48**) – APPSC GS 2019.
- Ran **training camps** in **1920** and **1934**; respected by **S.R. Ranganathan**.
- Slogan: "**Public libraries are temples of knowledge.**"
### 4. GADICHERLA HARISARVOTTAMA RAO
#### 4.1 Newspapers and Jail
- Papers: **Swarajya (1907)**, **Navayugam**, Andhra Patrika, **Matru Seva**, **Panchayati Rajyam**.
- **Imprisoned** for Swarajya editorials such as "**Veerabali**".
- Blacklisted from government jobs after the **1907 Rajahmundry College incident**.
- Linked the movement to the **Home Rule League**.
!TRAP Gadicherla's papers did NOT include Grandhalaya Sarvasvam – that was Ayyanki's journal.
#### 4.2 "Rayalaseema" and Sreebagh
- **Renamed the Dattamandalam (Ceded Districts) "Rayalaseema"** at the **1928 Nandyal Andhra Mahasabha** (APPSC G2 2011, 2012).
- **Signed the Sreebagh Pact** (1937) for Rayalaseema.
#### 4.3 Library Presidency and Sarvottama Bhavan
- **President** of the library association in **1946**.
- **Komma Sitaramayya's** gift bought a building through the **Andhra Granthalaya Dharma Samstha (1946)** → named **Sarvottama Bhavan** in his honour, opened **1949**.
!NUM 1928 = "Rayalaseema" name; 1946 = library association president; 1949 = Sarvottama Bhavan opened
### 5. PATHURI NAGABHUSHANAM
- Started the **Bala Saraswati Bhandaramu** village library as a schoolboy; it later served **40 villages**.
- As Secretary of the **Guntur Library Association**: libraries in about **160 villages**, new buildings for **28**.
- **Boat library** on passenger boats on the **Bank Canal** for **seven years (1935–42)**; also a Buckingham Canal tour (APPSC GS 2019).
- Member of the **drafting committee of the AP Public Libraries Act (1960)**.

### 8. VIJNANA CHANDRIKA MANDALI AND PUBLISHING
- **Komarraju Venkata Lakshmana Rao** founded the **Sri Krishnadevaraya Andhra Bhasha Nilayam (1901)**.
- Founded the **Vignana Chandrika Mandali / Granthamala**, **Hyderabad, 1906**, with co-founders including **Gadicherla** and **Ayyadevara Kaleswara Rao**.
- Mandali published ***Andhrula Charitramu*** (**1910**).
