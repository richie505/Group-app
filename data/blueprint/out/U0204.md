## U0204 | SWADESHI AND VANDEMATARAM MOVEMENT IN ANDHRA – BIPIN CHANDRA PAL'S TOUR, STUDENT PARTICIPATION
### 1. THE VANDEMATARAM MOVEMENT – NAME, PHASE, TRIGGER
- In Andhra the Swadeshi Movement was popularly called the **Vandemataram Movement**.
- **Andhra phase**: **1906-1911**.
- **Trigger**: **Surendranath Banerjee's arrest (1906)** → protests at **Rajahmundry, Kakinada, Vizianagaram, Gooty**.
- **Slogan**: "**Vandemataram – Manade Rajyam**" (self-rule is ours).
- "**Vande Mataram**" became a **greeting**; officials' curbs on it led to the **Rajahmundry and Kakinada incidents**.
- **Madras, 1906**: an **Industrial Association** (to promote Swadeshi goods) and a **Swadeshi League**.
- The movement was tied to **national education, Swadeshi stores and student politics** – not limited to elites.
!KEY Significance: **first mass political awakening in Andhra**; national education, Swadeshi stores and student politics all began here.
#### 1.1 Japan's Influence
- **Japan's win over Russia (1904-05)** inspired Andhra youth; many went to **Japan for industrial training**.
- **Munagala Raja** named his sons **Togo and Nogi**.
- **Adipudi Somanatha Rao** wrote **Japan Charitra** (history of Japan in Telugu), dedicated to the Raja of Munagala; play **Japaniyam**.
### 2. BIPIN CHANDRA PAL'S TOUR OF ANDHRA, APRIL 1907
#### 2.1 Invitation and Organisers
- **Mutnuri Krishna Rao** appealed in **Krishna Patrika** for an Extremist leader → **organised Pal's tour** (April 1907).
- **APPSC 2011 key** named **Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao** as organiser – know both; Gadicherla **accompanied Pal** on the tour.
- Popularisers who toured Andhra for Swadeshi: **Nyapathi Subbarao** and **K. Venkataramana Rao**.
#### 2.2 Towns Visited
| Date (1907) | Town | Host / highlight |
|---|---|---|
| **16 April** | **Vizianagaram** | Start of the tour |
| **17 April** | **Kakinada** | Presided by **Kattiventi Perraju**; two speeches (Vedanta; Swadeshi-Swaraj) |
| **19-24 April** | **Rajahmundry** | Welcomed by **Gadicherla**; stayed at **Madella Sourayya's** house; **five lectures**; 23 Apr speech on **national education**; opened **Godavari Swadeshi Store** |
| **24 April** | **Vijayawada** | Hosts **Munagala Raja** and **Nayani Venkata Rangarao** |
| **27 April** | **Machilipatnam** | Host **Kompalle Hanumantha Rao** (APPSC 2008 key); final speech "**Matru Murthi**"; **Young Men's Swarajya Samiti** formed |
| **1 May** | **Madras** | **T. Prakasam presided** when no one else would |
- **Machilipatnam stay**: **Brahmo Ramadasu Naidu** arranged Pal's stay (Pal was his guest).
- **Pal's message**: "**Swaraj the goal, boycott the weapon**"; village-level organisation; **national schools**.
!TRAP Pal's tour = **April 1907**. The APPSC 2025 match key paired **Pal's tour – April 1907** and **Kakinada riots – July 1907**; a PYQ book's explanation swaps them.
### 3. RAJAHMUNDRY COLLEGE STUDENT INCIDENT, 1907
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| College | **Government Arts and Teachers' Training College, Rajahmundry** |
| Principal | **Mark Hunter** |
| Action | **Banned Vandemataram badges** and the slogan (**April 1907**) |
| Defiance | **Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao** (teacher trainee) presented an **address to Pal**; co-organised by **J. Ramachandra Rao** |
| Punishment | **138 students debarred from exams for two years**; **Gadicherla expelled**, dismissed and **blacklisted from government jobs** |
| Significance | **Beginning of student participation** in the freedom struggle in Andhra |
- Sequence: ban on badges → address to Pal → 138 students debarred → Gadicherla dismissed and blacklisted.
!TRAP The principal was **Mark Hunter**; the expelled student-leader was **Gadicherla**, not Mutnuri Krishna Rao.

### 7. TELUGU SONGS AND THE VANDEMATARAM SLOGAN
- "**Hinduvulara ennaall ee gati**" – **first Telugu national song**, by **Chilakamarti**.
- "**Bharata khandambu chakkani paadi yaavu**" – Chilakamarti's poem written during Pal's Rajahmundry visit.
- **Bans on the Vandemataram slogan and badges** (Rajahmundry College) and the greeting (Kakinada) sparked protests.
- **Later echo**: **1938 Osmania University** – students (incl. **P.V. Narasimha Rao**) expelled for singing Vande Mataram.
!TRAP **Desamata = Chilakamarti**; **Swarajya = Gadicherla**.
### 8. SEDITIOUS WRITINGS AND TRIALS
- **Swarajya**: Telugu weekly started by **Gadicherla** with **Bodi Narayana Rao** in **1908** (placed at Vijayawada).
- Article "**Viparita Buddhi**" – spoke of a **cruel foreign (English) tiger** devouring Indians → **sedition charge**.
- **Gadicherla and Bodi Narayana Rao arrested 18 July 1908**; another article praised **V.O. Chidambaram Pillai**.
- Sentence: **3 years' rigorous imprisonment** → **first Telugu political prisoners**; Gadicherla = first Telugu editor in Madras Presidency to be jailed.
!NUM Swarajya case: 1908 – 3 years RI – first Telugu political prisoners
### 9. RIOTS AND BOMB CASES, 1907-1909
| Event | Date | Place | Key facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kakinada riots** | **31 May 1907** (APPSC 2025 key pairs it with **July 1907**) | **Kakinada** | **Dr (Surgeon) Kemp** beat the boy **Kompelli Krishna Rao** for shouting Vande Mataram → **European Club attacked**; reserve police sent from Rajahmundry; defended by **Nyapati Subbarao** – **first criminal case of the movement** (APPSC 2019 key) |
| **Kotappakonda riots** | **February 1909** (18 Feb, Shivaratri) | **Guntur** (near Narasaraopet) | Police clash; **14 died**; **Chinnapa (Chennappa) Reddy** sentenced to death – **only death sentence in India** in the movement |
| **Tenali bomb case** | **April 1909** (6 Apr) | **Tenali** | **Chukkapalli Ramaiah, Lakkaraju Basavaiah, K. Venkatarayudu** accused of a bomb aimed at the **Howrah passenger**; witness Y. Jagganna; **T. Prakasam** won their release |
- Tenali bomb case took place **during the Vandemataram (Swadeshi) movement**, 1909.
### 10. LEGACY
- Fed straight into the **Andhra Mahasabha** era and the **Home Rule** branches led by the same men (**Gadicherla, Konda Venkatappaiah**).
- Built the base of Telugu nationalist journalism: **Krishna Patrika, Swarajya, Desamata**.

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- First criminal case of the movement: Kakinada riots (1907).
- Only death sentence in India: Chinnapa Reddy (Kotappakonda, 1909).
- First Telugu national poet: Chilakamarti; first Telugu national song: "Hinduvulara ennaall ee gati".
- First Telugu political prisoners: Swarajya case (1908).
- Beginning of Andhra student politics: Rajahmundry College incident (1907).
- Last stop of Pal's tour: Machilipatnam ("Matru Murthi"), then Madras.
