## U0342 | RECOGNITION OF NATIONAL AND STATE PARTIES – CRITERIA AND THE SYMBOLS ORDER
### 1. THREE STATES OF A PARTY
1. **Unregistered**
2. **Registered but unrecognised (RUPP)** – under **Sec. 29A, RPA 1951**; apply within **30 days** of formation
3. **Recognised** – **national or State party**, under the **Symbols Order, 1968**
- Sec. 29A: party constitution must pledge allegiance to the **Constitution** and to **socialism, secularism, democracy** and **national integrity**.
- RUPPs: over **2,800**; only about **750** contested 2024.
### 2. RECOGNISING AND REGISTERING AUTHORITY
| Step | Law | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Registration | **Sec. 29A, RPA 1951** | **ECI** |
| Recognition and symbols | **Election Symbols (Reservation and Allotment) Order, 1968** | **ECI** |
| Split disputes | **Para 15**; **Sadiq Ali (1971)** majority test | ECI |
| Suspend/withdraw recognition | **Para 16A** – defying the **Model Code of Conduct** | ECI |
| Deregistration | **INC v Institute of Social Welfare (2002)** | ECI – limited power |
- The ECI does **both** registration and recognition; registration is **not** under the Symbols Order.
- **Nature of the 1968 Order**: a **statutory order of the ECI** under the **RPA 1951 and Article 324** – not an Act of Parliament, not a constitutional provision; not issued by Parliament, the Law Ministry or the President.
- Deregistration only for **fraud**, **ceasing allegiance** to the Constitution, or the party being **declared unlawful**; Law Commission **255th** Report wants a limited deregistration power.
- The ECI acts as a **court** in recognition and symbol disputes.
!TRAP Registration authority is the **ECI** – not the Ministry of Law and Justice, not Parliament.
### 3. NATIONAL PARTY CRITERIA – PARAGRAPH 6B
Any **one** of three conditions:
| # | Condition |
|---|---|
| 1 | Recognised as a **State party in four or more States** |
| 2 | At least **6% of valid votes in four or more States** (Lok Sabha or Assembly election) **AND** at least **4 Lok Sabha seats** – both mandatory |
| 3 | At least **2% of Lok Sabha seats (11 of 543)** from not fewer than **three States** |
!NUM Memory hook: **4 / 6+4 / 2%-3**.
- Distractors: "**2 or more States**", "**4% / 10% / 15%** votes", "15% votes in two States without a seat" – all wrong.
- APPSC-G2 2025: every listed condition was valid → key "**None of the above**".
### 4. STATE PARTY CRITERIA
Any **one** route:
| Route | Condition |
|---|---|
| 1 | **6%** valid votes in Assembly election + **2 Assembly seats** |
| 2 | **6%** valid votes in Lok Sabha election + **1 Lok Sabha seat** from the State |
| 3 | **3% of Assembly seats or 3 seats**, whichever is more |
| 4 | **1 Lok Sabha seat for every 25** allotted to the State |
| 5 | **8% of valid votes** – **no seat needed** |
- The **8%** route was added later – the one candidates forget (not 10%).
### 5. NATIONAL PARTIES – NUMBER AND LIST
- **Six** national parties now: **BJP, INC, CPI(M), BSP, NPP, AAP**.
- **ECI review, April 2023**: **AAP recognised**; **CPI, NCP, AITC derecognised**.
- **Trinamool Congress** got national status on **2 September 2016**; it is no longer a national party.
- **May 2020**: **eight** national parties (APPSC 2019 key).
- **NPP (Meghalaya)**: first **North-East** party with national status.
- **Not** national parties (APPSC 2018): **DMK, Revolutionary Socialist Party, All India Forward Bloc, Peasants and Workers Party**.
- AP: **TDP and YSRCP** are recognised **State parties**, neither national.
- The ECI reviews status after each general election – quote the list with a date.
!PAIR April 2023 gained | AAP | national || April 2023 lost | CPI, NCP, AITC | derecognised
### 6. SYMBOLS – RESERVED AND FREE
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Reserved symbol** | Only for recognised parties (**Lotus – BJP**, **Hand – INC**); two recognised parties in different States may hold the same symbol |
| **Free symbol** | For RUPPs and independents from a notified list; latest **May 2025 – 184 symbols** |
| **Common symbol for RUPPs** | If they contest **2 Lok Sabha seats** or **5% of Assembly seats** |
- **Sadiq Ali (1971)**: split disputes over name/symbol decided by the **majority support** of factions (Para 15).
- 2026: Trinamool rebel MPs chose a **merger** over a Para 15 symbol claim; a satirical "**Cockroach Janta Party**" revived the Symbols Order debate.
### 7. RECOGNITION BENEFITS AND RUPP OBLIGATIONS
| Benefit | Recognised party | RUPP |
|---|---|---|
| Symbol | Reserved | Free / common on conditions |
| **Proposers** for nomination | **One** | **Ten** |
| **Star campaigners** (travel outside candidate's limit) | Up to **40** | **20** |
| Electoral rolls | **Two free sets** | – |
| Broadcast time | **Akashvani and Doordarshan** | – |
| ECI consultation | Yes | – |
- RUPP obligations: tax exemption under **Sec. 12, Income Tax Act 2025**; disclose donors above **₹20,000**; above **₹2,000** only by cheque/bank transfer; must **contest at least once in six years** or be **delisted**.

### 10. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. Registration = **Sec. 29A RPA**; recognition = **Symbols Order 1968** – both by the **ECI**.
2. Symbols Order is an **ECI statutory order**, not an Act.
3. National route 2 needs **6% in four States AND four LS seats**.
4. 2% seats = **11** from **three** States, not two.
5. State party vote-only route = **8%**, not 10%.
6. **Proposers 1 vs 10**; **star campaigners 40 vs 20**.
7. April 2023: **CPI, NCP, AITC** lost; **AAP** gained.
8. **DMK, TDP, YSRCP** are State parties.
9. Para **15** = split disputes; Para **16A** = MCC sanction.
