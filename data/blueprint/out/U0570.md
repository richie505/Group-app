### 2. HEALTH INSURANCE – DR NTR VAIDYA SEVA
#### 2.1 Key facts
- **Dr NTR Vaidya Seva** – earlier **Dr YSR Aarogyasri**.
- Cashless treatment for **BPL** families and families with income **up to Rs 5 lakh**.
- Cover: **Rs 25 lakh per family per year**; **no limit for cancer**.
- **3,257 procedures**; **1.43 crore families** benefited (APSES 2024-25).
- Budget 2026-27: **Rs 4,000 crore** to the Trust.
#### 2.2 Universal Health Policy (2026)
- Free treatment **up to Rs 2.5 lakh per citizen** through **insurers**; the Vaidya Seva Trust covers the poor beyond that, up to Rs 25 lakh (Mar and Jun 2026).
- Debate: hospitals halted services over about **Rs 3,000 crore** unpaid dues (11 Mar 2026); opposition called the insurance model a **dilution of Aarogyasri**.
| Number | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Rs 5 lakh | Income ceiling for eligibility |
| Rs 25 lakh | Vaidya Seva cover per family per year |
| Rs 2.5 lakh | Universal Health Policy cover per citizen |
!TRAP Ceiling Rs 5 lakh vs cover Rs 25 lakh vs universal Rs 2.5 lakh.

### 4. DIGITAL HEALTH
- **Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM)**: launched **2021** by the **Ministry of Health and Family Welfare**.
- AP **tops** in ABHA coverage of its population: **78%** (Jan 2024); about **98.5%** saturation (May 2026). Uttar Pradesh leads in absolute numbers.
- **Sanjeevani**: statewide from **August 2026**; piloted in **Chittoor/Kuppam**; every citizen gets a **digital health profile + ABHA ID**.
- Sanjeevani Citizen Wellness App links PHCs, labs, **e-Aushadhi**, 108, 104 and telemedicine (Jul 2026).

|---|---|---|
| **MMR** | **30** | 88 |
| **IMR** | **19** | 25 |
| U5MR | 21 | 29 |
| **TFR** | **1.5** | 1.9 |
