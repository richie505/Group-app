## U0023 | VEDIC RELIGION, RITUAL AND PHILOSOPHY; THE SIX ORTHODOX SCHOOLS
### 1. NATURE OF EARLY VEDIC RELIGION
- **Nature worship** and **polytheism**: gods are forces of nature (storm, fire, sky, dawn); prayers for cattle, progeny, victory.
- **Henotheism**: term applied by **Max Müller** – each god in turn praised as if supreme.
- **Rita** = cosmic and moral order; upheld by **Varuna**; later idea of **dharma** grows from it.
- Iranian parallels: **Mitra–Mithra, Rita–Asha, Soma–Haoma**.
#### 1.1 Early vs Later Vedic Religion
| Feature | Early (Rigvedic) | Later Vedic |
|---|---|---|
| Chief gods | **Indra, Agni, Varuna** | **Prajapati, Vishnu, Rudra** |
| Worship | Simple prayers, hymns | Elaborate, costly **sacrifices** |
| Priests | Fewer; Hotri leading | Many priests; heavy **dakshina** |
| Thought | Nature worship | Upanishadic **critique of ritual** |
- The growing power of priests and the cost of ritual later helped the rise of **heterodox sects** (Buddhism, Jainism, Ajivikas, Charvaka).
### 2. RIGVEDIC GODS
#### 2.1 God → Domain
| God | Domain | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Indra** | Lightning, storm, war | **~250 hymns** (most); **ranked first**; **Purandara** (fort-breaker) |
| **Agni** | Fire | About 200 hymns; **messenger between gods and men**; opens the Rigveda (1.1) |
| **Varuna** | Water, cosmic order | **Ranked third**; upholder of **Rita**; morally highest |
| **Dyaus** | Sky | Celestial father, like **Zeus / Jupiter** |
| **Soma** | Plant and ritual drink | Whole **9th Mandala**; **Haoma** of the Avesta; not a female dawn deity |
| **Surya** | Sun | Aspects: **Savitri** (Gayatri), Mitra, Pushan, Vishnu |
| **Ushas** | Dawn | Female |
| **Aditi** | **Mother of gods** | Female |
| **Aranyani** | Forest | Female |
| **Yama** | Death | – |
| **Rudra** | Disease | **Archer god** of mountains |
| **Pushan** | Paths, herdsmen, cattle | – |
- **Prithvi** = fertility goddess, **not** mother of all gods.
!TRAP APPSC G1 2022: "Prithvi is the mother of all gods" is FALSE – it is Aditi.
#### 2.2 Later Vedic Gods – Rise and Decline
| God | Change |
|---|---|
| **Prajapati** | Creator; became **supreme** |
| **Vishnu** | Rose as preserver |
| **Rudra** | Grew; later merged with Shiva |
| **Pushan** | Became god of the Shudras |
| **Indra, Agni** | Lost importance |
### 3. VEDIC RITUALS
| Ritual / term | Associated with |
|---|---|
| **Yajna** procedures | **Yajurveda** |
| **Purushamedha** | **Shatapatha Brahmana** |
| Ceremonial **chanting** | **Samaveda** |
| **Agnistoma** | Regular **Soma** sacrifice |
| **Rajasuya, Vajapeya, Ashvamedha** | Royal sacrifices |
| **Grihya Sutras** | **Domestic rites** |
| Burial and post-burial rites | **Megalithic culture** |
| Fire altars (Kalibangan, Lothal); Cemetery **R-37** (Harappa) | Indus fire and burial rituals |
- Priests: **Hotri** (Rig), **Adhvaryu** (Yajur), **Udgatri** (Sama).
- **Sanskaras: 16** = **3 before birth, 12 during life, 1 after death**; **Upanayana** most important (with wedding and after-death rites).
- AP angle: **Satavahanas** made Vedic religion official (**Nanaghat**: Indra, Vasudeva, Surya, Chandra, Yama, Varuna); Ikshvaku **Santamula** performed Ashvamedha, Rajasuya, Vajapeya, Agnistoma (**Ashvamedha altar at Nagarjunakonda**); Vakataka **Pravarasena I** did **four Ashvamedhas**; oldest Shivalinga at **Gudimallam (Chittoor)**.
### 4. UPANISHADIC PHILOSOPHY
| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Brahman** | Ultimate reality (cosmic principle) |
| **Atman** | The self – not body or mind |
| **Karma** | Links action with consequence |
| **Samsara** | Rebirth – doctrine introduced by **Brihadaranyaka** |
| **Moksha** | Liberation – **not used in the Vedas**; first discussed in the Upanishads |
- **Atman = Brahman**: individual self is one with the cosmic principle.
- Criticised **mechanical sacrifice**; stressed meditation and the guru-disciple relationship.
- Aim of the soul = **moksha**, freedom from the cycle of birth and death; **ignorance** = cause of sorrow.
- **Katha Upanishad**: **Nachiketa** asks **Yama** about death, Atman, immortality.
- Main theme = **philosophy**; universal approach that rejects no other school, religion or scripture.
### 5. THE SIX ORTHODOX SCHOOLS (SHAD-DARSHANAS)
| School | Founder | Key text | Core idea | Path to liberation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Samkhya** | **Kapila** | Samkhya Sutra, Tattvasamasa | Purusha-Prakriti dualism; **Satkaryavada** | Knowing Purusha apart from Prakriti |
| **Yoga** | **Patanjali** | **Yoga Sutras** | Discipline of body and mind | Practical method for Samkhya |
| **Nyaya** | **Gautama (Akshapada)** | Nyaya Sutra | Logic; **pramanas**; 16 categories | Valid knowledge |
| **Vaisheshika** | **Kanada** | Vaisheshika Sutra | **Atomism**; 9 dravyas | – |
| **Purva Mimamsa** | **Jaimini** | **Mimamsa Sutras** | Vedic authority, yajna, mantra; **Apurva** | Through **Karma** (ritual) |
| **Uttara Mimamsa (Vedanta)** | **Badarayana** | **Brahma Sutra** | Brahman the only reality | Knowledge of Brahman |
- **Samkhya = oldest** (APPSC GS 2018).
- Pairs: **Nyaya–Vaisheshika; Samkhya–Yoga; Mimamsa–Vedanta**.
- "Nyaya" = decision or logic. Kapila: man's life shaped by **forces of nature, not a god**; Samkhya accepts rebirth; self-knowledge gives liberation.
- Mimamsa holds the Vedas contain eternal truth; **theory of Karma** = Mimamsa (Karma/Purva/Dharma Mimamsa).
- **Purva Mimamsa = Karmakanda; Uttara Mimamsa = Jnanakanda**.
- **Apurva** = unseen force linking a deed (karma) with its result.
!TRAP Lokayata, Kapalika and Smriti are NOT among the six darshanas.
### 6. YOGA AND SAMKHYA CONCEPTS
#### 6.1 Ashtanga Yoga – Eight Limbs
| No. | Limb | Group |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Yama** | Bahiranga (outer) |
| 2 | **Niyama** | Bahiranga |
| 3 | **Asana** | Bahiranga |
| 4 | **Pranayama** | Bahiranga |
| 5 | **Pratyahara** | Bahiranga |
| 6 | **Dharana** | **Antaranga** (inner) |
| 7 | **Dhyana** | Antaranga |
| 8 | **Samadhi** | Antaranga |
!KEY Antaranga sadhana begins with Dharana; Pranayama is the 4th limb.
#### 6.2 Paths to Liberation
| School | Path |
|---|---|
| Samkhya | Discriminating knowledge |
| Yoga | Discipline and meditation |
| Mimamsa | Karma (ritual) |
| Advaita Vedanta | **Jnana** (knowledge of Atman-Brahman identity) |
### 7. DOCTRINE → SCHOOL; ASTIKA VS NASTIKA
| Doctrine | School |
|---|---|
| **Satkaryavada** (effect pre-exists in cause) | **Samkhya** |
| World formed by blending of nature (Prakriti) | Samkhya |
| **Atom** as basis of world | **Vaisheshika** |
| **Niyati** (fate) | **Ajivika** (Makkhali Gosala) |
| **Materialism** | **Lokayata / Charvaka** (credited to **Brihaspati**) |
| **Pramanas** | **Nyaya**, not Samkhya |
| **Syadvada** | **Jaina** (Kundakundacharya) |
| **Shunyavada / Madhyamika** | **Nagarjuna** |
- **Astika vs nastika** = belief in the authenticity of the Vedas.
- **Astika**: the six darshanas. **Nastika**: **Charvaka, Buddhism, Jainism, Ajivika**.
- **Bauddha** is nastika – it does not take the Vedas as valid knowledge.
!TRAP Odd-one-out lists slip one nastika school (Charvaka/Ajivika) among the six.
### 8. MIMAMSA AND VEDANTA EXPONENTS
| Exponent | School |
|---|---|
| **Kumarila Bhatta** | **Purva Mimamsa** (Slokavartika, Tantravartika) |
| **Prabhakara** | Purva Mimamsa |
| **Adi Shankaracharya** | **Advaita** |
| **Ramanujacharya** | **Vishishtadvaita** |
| **Madhvacharya** | **Dvaita** |
| **Lakulisha** | **Pashupata** Shaivism |
| Charvaka | Lokayata |
- **Kumarila** defended Vedic ritual (karma-kanda) and opposed Buddhist doctrines; seen as a link between Purva Mimamsa and Vedanta.
- **Shankara**: founded **four mathas**; called a "**crypto-Buddhist**". **Ramanuja**: first promoter of the Bhakti movement.
- **Vivekananda's Neo-Vedanta** reworked Advaita.
#### 8.1 Three Vedanta Schools Compared
| School | Teacher | View of Atman and Brahman |
|---|---|---|
| **Advaita** | Shankara | Non-dual: Atman and Brahman are **identical**; mukti through **jnana** |
| **Vishishtadvaita** | Ramanuja | Qualified non-dualism; first promoter of **Bhakti** |
| **Dvaita** | Madhva | Dualism ("two") |
- Upanishadic legacy: these concepts form the base of later Hinduism.
!TRAP Kumarila Bhatta = Purva Mimamsa, not Vedanta (APPSC G2 2012, 2019).
