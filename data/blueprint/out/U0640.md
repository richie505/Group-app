## U0640 | ANDHRA PRADESH – NATIONAL PARKS, WILDLIFE SANCTUARIES AND RAMSAR SITES
### 1. PROTECTED-AREA NETWORK AT A GLANCE
- AP (APSES 2024-25): **13 wildlife sanctuaries, 3 national parks, 4 deer parks, 2 zoological parks**; AP had **13 WLS in 2019** too.
- **1 tiger reserve** (NSTR), **1 biosphere reserve** (Seshachalam), **1 elephant reserve** (Rayala).
- Total PA network: **8,139.88 sq km** (4.99% of AP).
- Notified forest revised to **37,421.11 sq km** (2025-26); **48** community eco-tourism centres; **ZAAP** runs Vizag and Tirupati zoos.
- **Indira Gandhi Zoological Park** = Visakhapatnam.

### 5. NAGARJUNASAGAR–SRISAILAM TIGER RESERVE
- In the **Nallamala hills** of the Eastern Ghats along the **Krishna**; spans **AP + Telangana**; declared **1983**.
- Area about **3,728 sq km**; **India's largest tiger reserve** by area and by **core/critical tiger habitat** (APPSC 2024 key); hence "largest tiger habitat is in AP" (UPPSC 2011).
- AP **core**: **Nandyal, Prakasam, Palnadu**; core about 3,325 sq km incl. Gundla Brahmeswaram extended core.
- Managed by the **Srisailam circle** on a **core-buffer** model under **NTCA (WPA 1972)**.
- **Tigers: 63–67** (All India Tiger Estimation 2022).
- **AP's only tiger reserve**; Rajiv Gandhi WLS forms its core.
- A tiger roaming around Papikonda returned to East Godavari without attacking anyone (Jul 2026).
!TRAP There is **no** Sri Venkateswara or Papikondalu tiger reserve; NSTR is **not** a biosphere reserve. Chilika is not a tiger reserve.

### 6. SESHACHALAM BIOSPHERE RESERVE
- **AP's first and only BR**; notified **2010**; **4,755.997 sq km**; Tirupati and Kurnool circles.
- Seshachalam (Tirumala) hills; **red sanders** endemic.
!TRAP The BR is named **Seshachalam**, not "Sri Venkateswara BR".
### 7. ELEPHANTS AND CORRIDORS
- **Elephant reserve**: **Koundinya WLS + Rayala Elephant Reserve** (Anantapuramu circle).
- **Koundinya = AP's only elephant sanctuary** and only Asian elephant habitat.
- Elephants moved into **Palamaner/Koundinya in the 1980s** because of **drought in the Hosur–Dharmapuri reserve forests and Bannerghatta** (APPSC 2024 key).

### 9. FOREST REGIONS, MANGROVES AND FOREST COVER
| Extent | Hills |
|---|---|
| Northern | **Simhachalam** hills |
| Southern | **Palakonda** and Nallamala hills |
| Western | **Balaghat** hills |
| Region | Districts | Type / species |
|---|---|---|
| **Nallamala** | Nandyal–Prakasam–Palnadu | **biggest forest**; dry deciduous; NSTR |
| **Seshachalam** | Tirupati–Annamayya | dry deciduous; red sanders; BR |
| **Palakonda** | Kadapa–Annamayya | dry deciduous; red sanders belt |
| Krishna–Godavari mouths | Kakinada, Krishna, Bapatla | tidal mangrove ("Koringa forests") |
- Rainfall: moist/tidal belt **125–200 cm**; dry deciduous **75–100 cm**; thorn in the lowest-rainfall tracts.
- **Mangroves** = **Godavari and Krishna estuaries** (Coringa, Krishna WLS), **not Vamsadhara–Nagavali**.
- State surveys call them India's "second-largest mangrove ecosystem", but **ISFR 2023 puts Gujarat 2nd** (after West Bengal); AP is not 2nd in India.
- Forest cover: largest density class **Open Forest (14,363.50 sq km)**; **AP first in India in fire-affected area 2023-24 (5,286.76 sq km)**; most forest area **YSR Kadapa**, least **Krishna**.
### 10. BIRD SANCTUARIES, WETLANDS AND RAMSAR SITE
| Site | District | Birds / feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Pulicat** (600 sq km) | **Tirupati** (shared with TN) | **greater flamingo**, waders; **Buckingham Canal** passes through |
| **Nelapattu** | **Tirupati** | **spot-billed pelican** breeding colony |
| **Kolleru** | **Eluru**, West Godavari | one of the world's largest spot-billed pelican breeding grounds; grey pelican |
| **Telineelapuram, Telukunchi** | **Srikakulam** | painted stork, pelicans (winter migrants); Telineelapuram = **IBA** |
| **Uppalapadu** | **Guntur** | pelicans, painted storks |
| **Naupada** | **Srikakulam** | coastal and marine biodiversity area |
- **Kolleru** lies **between the Krishna and Godavari deltas** (not the Krishna alone); **WLS 1999, Ramsar site 19 Aug 2002**.
- **Kolleru is AP's only Ramsar site**; its Ramsar area is about **90,100 ha** (~901 sq km), much larger than the 308.55 sq km sanctuary.
- Ramsar sites in India keep rising (about 100 by 2026); none of the new additions is in AP.
- **Pulicat** is a brackish lagoon (salt + fresh water), while **Kolleru** is a **freshwater** lake – a common statement trap.
- Winter is the peak season at Pulicat, Nelapattu, Telineelapuram and Uppalapadu, when **migratory** pelicans, painted storks and flamingos arrive.
- Telineelapuram and Uppalapadu are **not** among the 13 notified sanctuaries.
- **Telineelapuram mass deaths** = **spot-billed pelican (*Pelecanus philippensis*)** – not flamingo, openbill or sarus (APPSC-G1 2022).
