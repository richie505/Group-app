### 9. JUDGMENTS, ORDINANCES AND THE JUDICIARY
#### 9.1 Presidential Reference on Governors' assent (20 Nov 2025)
- **Five-judge Constitution Bench** led by **CJI B.R. Gavai** gave its **advisory opinion** on a **Presidential Reference under Article 143** (sent by President **Droupadi Murmu**).
- Held: courts **cannot fix timelines** for the **Governor (Article 200)** or the **President (Article 201)** to act on State Bills, and there is **no "deemed assent"**.
- The Reference followed the **April 2025 Tamil Nadu ruling** that had set timelines and deemed assent.
!KEY An Article 143 opinion is **advisory**: it does not bind the President and is not "law declared" under Article 141.
#### 9.2 Ordinance to Act - Supreme Court judges
| Step | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Ordinance, 2026** | Promulgated **16 May 2026** under **Article 123** |
| Change | Section 2 of the **1956 Act**: **33 to 37** judges **excluding the CJI** (**34 to 38** including the CJI) |
| Replacement Bill | Lok Sabha **3 Aug 2026**; Rajya Sabha **5 Aug 2026** |
| Act | Assent **11 Aug 2026** (**Act No. 14 of 2026**); in force from **16 May 2026**; repeals the Ordinance |
!TRAP 37 is the number of judges besides the CJI; total sanctioned strength is 38.

#### 10.3 Renaming - Kerala to Keralam
- **Kerala (Alteration of Name) Bill, 2026**: Union Cabinet approval **24 Feb 2026**; Lok Sabha **11 Aug 2026**; Rajya Sabha **12 Aug 2026** (voice vote).
- Made under **Article 3** - amends the **First Schedule**; an ordinary law, **not an Article 368 amendment**. The Kerala Assembly endorsed it on the President's reference.
!TRAP Renaming a State needs only a simple-majority law under Article 3, not a constitutional amendment.
#### 10.4 Constitutional amendment Bills
- **Constitution (130th Amendment) Bill, 2025**: introduced in Lok Sabha **20 Aug 2025**; sent to a **Joint Parliamentary Committee**.
- Removal of the **PM, a CM or a Minister** held in custody for **30 consecutive days** on a charge punishable with **5 years or more**; amends **Articles 75, 164 and 239AA**.
- **July 2026**: the JPC **deferred** adopting its report.
