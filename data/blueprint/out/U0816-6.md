### 4. CENSUS 2027 - FIRST FULLY DIGITAL CENSUS
#### 4.1 Key facts
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Started | **1 April 2026** (house-listing in 2026) |
| Nature | India's **first fully digital census** |
| Number | **16th** overall; **8th after Independence** |
| Gap | **16 years** (not 12) since 2011 |
| Budget | **₹11,718.24 crore** |
| Personnel | More than **3 million** officials, enumerators and supervisors |
| Self-enumeration | Online portal in **16 languages**; households get a **Self-Enumeration ID** |
| Pre-test | **November 2025** |
- **2021 Census postponed** due to **COVID-19**.
#### 4.2 Phases and reference dates
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Phase I** | **April-September 2026** - house listing, housing conditions, amenities, assets |
| **Phase II** | **February 2027** - population, socio-economic, education, **caste** |
| Reference date (most of India) | **1 March 2027** |
| Reference date (snow-bound) | **1 October 2026** - **Ladakh, J&K, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand** |
!TRAP 1 Oct 2026 = snow-bound areas; 1 Mar 2027 = rest of India. Caste is counted in Phase II, not Phase I.
### 5. CASTE ENUMERATION AND LEGAL BASIS
#### 5.1 Caste count
- **First caste count since 1931**: caste of **every individual** recorded (earlier only **SCs and STs**).
- Legal basis: **Section 3 of the Census Act, 1948** (not the Census Rules).
#### 5.2 Law, authority and Constitution
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Act | **Census Act, 1948** |
| Rules | **Census Rules, 1990** |
| Notification | Gazette notification by the **Ministry of Home Affairs** |
| Conducted by | **Registrar General and Census Commissioner, India** (MHA) |
| Subject list | **Article 246, Union List Entry 69** |
| Seats link | **Articles 81 and 82** - Lok Sabha seats and **delimitation** based on census |
!KEY Articles 81 and 82 link Lok Sabha seats and delimitation with the census - the governance-reform angle of Census 2027.
