## U0019 | LATER VEDIC PERIOD – EASTWARD EXPANSION, IRON (KRISHNA AYAS), PAINTED GREY WARE
### 1. EASTWARD EXPANSION
#### 1.1 Course of Expansion
- Order: **Punjab → Ganga-Yamuna Doab** (Kurukshetra, Delhi-upper Doab) → **Kosala** (eastern UP), **Kashi**, **Videha** (north Bihar).
- Reached Kosala, Kashi and Videha by about **600 BCE**.
- **Kuru-Panchala** (Delhi-Meerut region; capital **Hastinapur**, Meerut district) = **centre of Aryan culture**, with gains in science, mathematics, astronomy, religion and philosophy.
- **Kurus** = union of the **Bharatas and Purus**.
#### 1.2 Videgha Madhava Legend
- Told in the **Shatapatha (Satapatha) Brahmana**.
- **Videgha Madhava** and his priest **Gautama Rahugana** followed **Agni Vaishvanara** as it burnt forests eastward from the **Sarasvati**.
- Crossed the **Sadanira (Gandak)**; the land beyond was settled as **Videha**.
- Shows the role of **iron axes and fire** in clearing the Ganga forests.
#### 1.3 Upanishadic (Kshatriya Scholar) Kings
| King | Kingdom |
|---|---|
| **Janaka** | Videha |
| **Ashvapati** | Kekaya |
| **Ajatashatru** | Kashi |
| **Pravahana Jaivali** | Panchala |
- These kings **taught Brahmins**.
!TRAP Ashvapati = Kekaya, not Kashi; Ajatashatru of the Upanishads = Kashi (not the Magadha king).
### 2. IRON – TERMS, DATES AND SITES
#### 2.1 Vedic Metal Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ayas** (Rigveda) | Copper or bronze |
| **Krishna ayas / Shyama ayas** ("black metal") | **Iron** (Later Vedic texts) |
| **Lohita ayas** | Copper |
| **Hiranya** | Gold |
| **Nishka** | Gold ornament; later a unit of value |
#### 2.2 Iron in North India
- Iron technology about **1000-800 BCE**; common at most **PGW sites**; smiths and smelters important after 1000 BCE.
- Sites: **Atranjikhera, Hastinapur, Kaushambi, Taxila** (Gandhara, c. 1000 BC).
- Iron axes + fire cleared Gangetic forests; iron weapons + horse-drawn chariots helped the eastward push.
#### 2.3 Earliest Iron – Tamil Nadu Dates
| Site | District (TN) | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| **Sivagalai** | Thoothukudi | Iron dated to about **2953 BCE** (2025) – now the oldest |
| **Mayiladumparai** | Krishnagiri | Iron AMS-dated to **2172 BCE**; the earliest claim before Sivagalai |
| **Thirumalapuram** | Tenkasi | Iron Age urn chamber |
| **Adichanallur** | Thoothukudi | Urn burials; **not a PGW site** |
- South Indian Iron Age now placed around **2100 BCE or earlier**, before the Gangetic Iron Age (Jun 2026).
- These dates push iron use far earlier than 1000 BCE; they say **nothing about where PGW began**.
!KEY Krishna ayas = iron; lohita ayas = copper; Rigvedic ayas = copper/bronze.
### 3. POTTERY SEQUENCE OF INDIAN PROTOHISTORY
| Order | Pottery | Period |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ochre Coloured Pottery (OCP)** | Post-Harappan, 2nd millennium BCE |
| 2 | **Black and Red Ware (BRW)** | Chalcolithic onwards; also Later Vedic; Megalithic south |
| 3 | **Black Slipped Ware (BSW)** | Later Vedic |
| 4 | **Painted Grey Ware (PGW)** | Later Vedic, **1000-600 BC**, Gangetic plain |
| 5 | **Red Ware** | Later Vedic |
| 6 | **Northern Black Polished Ware (NBPW)** | From about **600 BCE**; Mahajanapadas, second urbanisation |
- OCP named at **Hastinapur by B.B. Lal (1950-52)**; leaves an **ochre colour on the fingers** when rubbed.
- Four Later Vedic wares: **BRW, BSW, PGW, Red Ware**. NBPW is not in this group.
!TRAP Later Vedic wares exclude NBPW (APPSC G1 2024). Shiny black pottery = NBPW, not PGW.
### 4. PAINTED GREY WARE CULTURE
#### 4.1 Features
- Grey pottery with **linear painted designs**; mostly **bowls and dishes**; c. 1000-600 BCE; probably used by the upper strata.
- About **700 sites**, mainly in the **upper Ganga basin** (not the lower Indus basin).
- Finds: **iron weapons, earthen bowls and dishes, copper objects**; copper possibly from **Khetri** (Rajasthan).
- First found at **Ahichchhatra (1944)**; Hastinapur excavation (1950-52, B.B. Lal) established it as a culture.
#### 4.2 PGW Sites → Region
| Site | Region |
|---|---|
| **Hastinapur** | Meerut, western UP (Kuru capital) |
| **Atranjikhera** | Western UP (Ganga-Yamuna Doab) |
| **Ahichchhatra** | Bareilly, UP |
| **Bhagwanpura** | Haryana (house plan found) |
| **Noh** | Rajasthan |
!TRAP PGW is Later Vedic, not Early Vedic.
### 5. AGRICULTURE, SETTLEMENTS, CRAFTS AND TRADE
- **Rice = Vrihi**: main crop with ritual importance; **wheat** grown; **barley** continues.
- Ploughs yoked with **6, 8, 12 and even 24 oxen**.
- Settlements: mainly **villages** of mud and wattle houses; **burnt bricks not common**.
- **True towns** came only after the Vedic period, in the **NBPW phase** (second urbanisation: Shravasti, Kausambi, Hastinapur).
- Crafts: iron-working, copper, weaving (by women), leather, pottery, carpentry.
- **Shresthins** = merchant groups; **Nishka** = unit of value; **barter** still main.
- AP angle: Iron Age in Andhra = **Megalithic culture with BRW** (Brahmagiri, Nagarjunakonda, Gudivada, Jonnawada); Sankalia: rice first harvested in AP in this era.
- Sep 2026: BHU opened 22 sealed boxes of 10,000+ artefacts from **Rajghat** (Varanasi); pre-NBPW pottery ~3,000 years old dates Varanasi's urban origins to about **800 BCE**.
### 6. LATER VEDIC TEXTS AND PRIESTS
| Text | Content |
|---|---|
| **Samaveda** | Rigvedic hymns set to music (**Udgatri**) |
| **Yajurveda** | Ritual steps in prose (**Adhvaryu**); **Krishna and Shukla** branches |
| **Atharvaveda** | Spells and charms against disease; latest Veda |
| **Brahmanas** | Ritual commentaries (Shatapatha, Aitareya) |
| **Aranyakas** | "Forest texts"; symbolic reading of ritual |
| **Upanishads** | c. 800-600 BCE; 108 (13 principal); Atman, Brahman, Karma, Moksha |
- **Hotri** recited hymns; **Adhvaryu** did practical, manual work of sacrifice; **Upadhyaya** taught Veda and Vedanga.
### 7. KINGSHIP, RATNINS AND TAXES
#### 7.1 Rise of Territorial Kingship
- Tribe gave way to territory: **rashtra** and **janapadas**; kingship stronger and hereditary; prepared the rise of Mahajanapadas.
- **Sabha and Samiti declined**: dominated by chiefs, nobles and Brahmanas; the Sabha became a royal court.
#### 7.2 Officials and Ratnins
| Official | Function |
|---|---|
| **Bhagadugha** | **Revenue / tax collector** (king's share) |
| **Sangrahitri** | Treasurer |
| **Akshavapa** | Accountant; superintendent of dice |
| **Palagala** | Messenger |
| **Senani** | Army chief |
| **Purohita** | Chief priest |
| **Suta** | Charioteer and herald |
- **Ratnins ("jewels")**: king's council of **12** officials, honoured in the **Ratnahavimshi** rite of the **Rajasuya** (offerings at the Ratnins' houses).
- List: purohita, rajan, mahisi, senani, bhagadugha, sangrahitri, suta, kshata, govikartana, gramani, parivakti, akshavapa (some add palagala).
#### 7.3 Taxes
| Tax | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Bali** | Tribute |
| **Bhaga** | King's share, **one-sixth** of produce |
| **Shulka** | Customs / tolls |
- Taxes became **compulsory**; **Vaishyas** = tribute-payers.
!TRAP Bhagadugha = collector (not gambling officer, messenger or forest officer); Sangrahitri = treasurer.
#### 7.4 Royal Rituals
| Ritual | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Rajasuya** | Royal consecration; supreme power |
| **Ashvamedha** | Horse sacrifice; control over territory |
| **Vajapeya** | Chariot race; display of royal power |
### 8. LATER VEDIC GODS
| God | Position |
|---|---|
| **Prajapati** | Creator; became **supreme** |
| **Vishnu** | Rose as preserver (minor in Rigveda) |
| **Rudra** | God of animals; later merged with Shiva |
| **Pushan** | Cattle god; god of the **Shudras** |
| **Indra, Agni** | Lost importance |
- Worship centred on elaborate rituals, criticised by **Upanishadic thinkers**.
!PAIR Early Vedic | tribe, cattle | voluntary bali || Later Vedic | territory, agriculture | compulsory taxes
