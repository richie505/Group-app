## U0325 | CENTRE-STATE RELATIONS – THE CONSTITUTIONAL SCHEME AND AREAS OF FRICTION
### 1. CONSTITUTIONAL SCHEME
| Part | Relations | Articles |
|---|---|---|
| **Part XI** | Legislative + administrative | 245-255 + 256-263 |
| **Part XII** | Financial | 268-293 |
- Shaping factors: constitutional provisions, conventions, judicial interpretation, planning, party politics.
- **Unitary features:** single citizenship, integrated judiciary, **All India Services** (serve Union and States), emergency powers.
- **Sarkaria Commission** – to review Union-State relations; chaired by **Justice R.S. Sarkaria**; formed **9 June 1983**; members B. Shivaraman, S.R. Sen.
!KEY Administrative relations = **Part XI** (UKPSC 2024).
### 2. EMERGENCY ARTICLES 352-360
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| 352 / 353 | National Emergency (war, external aggression or armed rebellion; proclaimed by the President) / its effect |
| 354 | Distribution of revenues during Emergency |

| 356 / 357 | President's Rule / legislative powers under 356 |
| 358 / 359 | Suspension of Art. 19 / suspension of enforcement of FRs |
| 360 | Financial Emergency |
#### 2.1 National Emergency – Effects
| Sphere | Effect | Article |
|---|---|---|
| Legislative | Parliament legislates on any **State List** subject; laws end **6 months** after Emergency ends | **250** |
| Executive | Union may direct a State on **any** matter; State Governments **not suspended** | **353** |
| Financial | President may reduce/cancel transfers till **end of that financial year**; order laid before Parliament | **354** |
- Federation becomes **quasi-unitary**; Lok Sabha term extendable by 1 year at a time.
!NUM 250 – 353 – 354 = legislative – executive – financial
### 3. UNION'S DUTY TO PROTECT STATES – ART. 355 → 365 → 356
1. **Art. 355:** protect every State against **external aggression and internal disturbance** and ensure governance per the Constitution (UPSC 2003).
2. **Art. 365:** defying Union directions = breakdown of machinery.
3. **Art. 356:** President's Rule, justified by the Art. 355 duty.
- **IMDT Act, 1983** struck down in **Sarbananda Sonowal v. Union of India (2005)** for violating **Art. 355**.
### 4. AREAS OF FRICTION
| Area | Issue |
|---|---|
| Governor | Holds office at President's pleasure (**Art. 156**); assent delays (TN, Kerala) |
| Art. 356 | Used **over 125 times**, often against opposition governments |
| Fiscal | Cesses and surcharges excluded – divisible pool only **~81%** of gross tax revenue |
| AIS / river waters | Central control of officers; **Art. 262** water disputes |
| CBI | Needs State consent under **Sec 6, DSPE Act**; several States withdrew it |
!TRAP CBI consent = Sec 6 DSPE Act, NOT Article 156 (that concerns the Governor).
#### 4.1 Recent Friction
- **GST compensation** to States ended **June 2022**.
- States demand **50%** devolution (3 Feb 2026).
- **Borrowing limits:** Centre fixes a **Net Borrowing Ceiling** under **Art. 293** (about **3% of GSDP**); Kerala's suit under **Art. 131** was referred to a **five-judge bench** (April 2024).
- **Kurian Joseph Committee** (Tamil Nadu, Part I, 16 Feb 2026) – "**structural reset**" of federalism.
### 5. ANDHRA PRADESH ISSUES
- **SCS:** promised in Parliament by **PM Manmohan Singh**; never written into **APRA 2014**; SCS means 90% CSS funding.
- Unmet APRA promises (opposition claim, **~₹5 lakh crore**): **Kadapa steel plant, Dugarajapatnam port, metro rail**.
- **Polavaram:** national project under **Sec 90 APRA 2014**; **100% Centre-funded**; total sanctioned ₹12,157 crore; **89%** complete, to be dedicated by March 2027 (15 Jun 2026).
- Budget 2026-27: **₹3,320 crore** for storage up to **41.15 m** vs demand for full **45.72 m**.
!TRAP Polavaram = Sec 90, not Sec 89 or the LARR Act.
