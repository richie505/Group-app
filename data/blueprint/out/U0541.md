## U0541 | IMPACT OF GST ON COMMERCE, INDUSTRY AND STATE FINANCES; UNRESOLVED GST ISSUES
### 1. GST BASICS AND COLLECTIONS
- GST from **1 July 2017**; **101st Amendment Act, 2016**; key Articles **246A, 269A, 279A**.
| Period | Gross GST |
|---|---|
| Oct 2020 | Above ₹1 lakh cr (₹1,05,155 cr) |
| April 2024 | First month above **₹2 lakh cr** |
| FY 2024-25 | **₹22.08 lakh cr**; avg ₹1.84 lakh cr/month |
| FY 2025-26 | Highest ever **₹22.27 lakh cr**, +8.3% |
| April 2026 | Record **₹2.43 lakh cr**, +8.7%; net ₹2.11 lakh cr |
- **Economic Survey 2025-26**: GST 2.0 = **third pillar** of tax reform (after 2019 corporate tax cut, 2025 personal income-tax reform); rate-cut **volume effects** may cover revenue loss.
### 2. RATE STRUCTURE AND GST 2.0
- 2017 slabs: **5, 12, 18, 28%** + exempt + compensation cess over 28%; four slabs meant as **transitional**.
- **GST 2.0**: approved at **56th Council meeting (3-4 Sep 2025)**; effective **22 Sep 2025**: **0, 5% (merit), 18% (standard), 40% (demerit)**.
- ~**99%** of old 12% items → 5%; ~**90%** of old 28% items → 18% (rest 40%).
- Special: rough diamonds **0.25%**; gold, silver, jewellery **3%**.
!KEY Biggest drawback of multiple rates = **misclassification** and classification disputes (APPSC G1 2017).
### 3. IMPACT ON COMMERCE AND MSMEs
- **Common national market**; **ITC** at every stage ends cascading.
- **E-way bill**: above **₹50,000**; valid **1 day per 200 km**.
- Inter-State **check-posts removed** → less truck waiting and logistics cost.
- Formalisation: taxpayers **66.5 lakh (2017) → 1.51 crore (2025)**.
- **Composition scheme**: turnover up to ₹1.5 crore (₹50 lakh services); **1% goods, 6% services**; **no ITC**.
- Reform agenda: pre-filled returns, time-bound digital registration, automated exporter refunds.
### 4. KEY TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Inverted Duty Structure** | Input tax > output tax → ITC piles up, working capital blocked; refund allowed |
| **ITC fraud** | Fake entities issue invoices without supply |
| **Destination principle** | Tax goes to State of **consumption** |
| **IGST** | Levied by Centre on inter-State supply (Art. 269A); State share to destination State |
### 5. IMPACT ON STATES
- Gave up indirect-tax powers (VAT, entry, luxury, entertainment tax, octroi) + weak own revenue → more **dependence on transfers**.
- **Producing States** feared loss: Maharashtra, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka → 5-year compensation.
- **Compensation**: July 2017 – June 2022 at **14%** growth over **2015-16** base.
- Cess continued to repay **COVID-19 loans**; ended **22 Sep 2025** except **tobacco** (until loans repaid).
- Unused balance: **50% Centre, 50% States** in proportion to **SGST revenue**.
### 6. UNRESOLVED ISSUES
- **Petroleum** (crude, petrol, HSD, natural gas, ATF) and **electricity** outside; date set by Council under **Art. 279A(5)**.
- **Council voting**: Centre 1/3, States 2/3; needs **3/4** → Centre's **effective veto**.
- **Mohit Minerals (2022)**: recommendations **persuasive, not binding**; "flexible federation"; simultaneous power under Art. 246A.
- **GSTAT**: Principal Bench **New Delhi** + State benches; launched Sep 2025, hearings Dec 2025. Mid-2025: no functional benches in many States → appeals piled in High Courts.
- Cesses outside the **divisible pool** → vertical fiscal imbalance.
### 7. AP ANGLE
- **AP High Court (May 2026)**: overlapping Central/State probes — authorities decide **inter se** who proceeds.
- AP is a **consuming State**; Net GST = **SGST + IGST settlement share**.
- Own tax revenue 2024-25 RE: **GST 38% (₹36,296 cr)**, largest; State Excise 22% (₹21,300 cr).
