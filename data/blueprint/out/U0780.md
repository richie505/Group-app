## U0780 | FOREST AND WILDLIFE LEGAL FRAMEWORK – IFA 1927, FCA 1980, WPA 1972, BD ACT 2002

| 1952 | National Forest Policy | First post-Independence policy |
| 1960 | Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act | Animal Welfare Board (1962) |

!TRAP 1865 = first Forest Act; 1878 = three forest categories; 1927 = Act still in force.
### 2. CONSTITUTIONAL POSITION
- **42nd Amendment (1976)** moved forests and wildlife from **State List to Concurrent List**: **Entry 17A** = forests; **Entry 17B** = protection of wild animals and birds.
- Also inserted **Art 48A** (DPSP, State) and **Art 51A(g)** (Fundamental Duty, citizen).
- A **State Bill amending WPA** (e.g. Maharashtra 2026 on Sec 12, leopard relocation; proposal to move leopard Schedule I → II, called "regressive") needs **President's assent**, as it changes a central law.
### 3. INDIAN FOREST ACT 1927
| Category | Protection | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| **Reserved forest** | Strictest: felling, grazing, hunting barred **unless permitted** | Ch II, Sec 3-27 |
| **Village forest** | A **reserved** forest (not protected) assigned to a village | Ch III, Sec 28 |
| **Protected forest** | Activities allowed **unless prohibited** | Ch IV, Sec 29-34 |
| Unclassed | Outside both; least regulated | — |
- Level of protection: **Reserved > Protected > Village** forest.
- **Sec 26:** acts banned in a reserved forest; trespass punishable (APPSC 2024). **Sec 35:** State may regulate non-government forests.
- **2017 amendment** (ordinance 2017, Act 2018): **bamboo grown outside forests** removed from the definition of "tree" (**Sec 2(7)**); felled and moved without transit permit; benefits tribals and farmers.
### 4. NATIONAL FOREST POLICY 1952 vs 1988
| Feature | 1952 | 1988 |
|---|---|---|
| National target | One-third | One-third (**33%**) |
| Hills | **60%** (plains 25%) | **Two-thirds** |
| Thrust | National, protected, village forests and tree-lands (NPs not a class) | **Ecological stability**, people's participation |
- **NFP 1988 includes:** afforestation and wasteland development, reforestation, **wood substitutes and alternative fuels**, social forestry, biodiversity, soil and water conservation, tribal fuelwood and fodder needs, people's movement involving women, PA corridors, fuel-efficient chulhas.
!TRAP APPSC 2024: "Two-thirds of the country under forest" is NOT true (two-thirds applies only to hills). NFP 1988 never promoted wide use of **insecticides and pesticides**.
### 5. WILDLIFE (PROTECTION) ACT 1972
- Protects **wild animals, birds and plants** and manages habitats (APPSC 2025); not only animals. **66 sections, 7 chapters**; amended **1991, 2002, 2006, 2022**.
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| 2(1) | "Animal" = amphibians, birds, mammals, reptiles, their young, eggs of birds and reptiles |
| 2(37) | "Wildlife" |
| **9** | Hunting of scheduled animals prohibited |
| **11** | Animal endangering **human life or crops**; may be killed if dangerous, diseased or disabled |
| **12** | Permits for education, research, scientific management, **translocation** |
| **39(1)** | Wild animals = **State Government** property; in a Centre-declared sanctuary/NP = Central Government |
| **38V(3)** | Tiger Conservation Plan for every TR (2006) |
| **62** | Centre may notify a **non-Schedule I** animal as **vermin** for an area and period |
- Gharial, Indian wild ass, wild buffalo cannot be hunted except as the law permits. Mining prohibited inside NPs and sanctuaries; SC bars mining within **1 km** of every NP/sanctuary unless a larger ESZ is notified.
### 6. WPA SCHEDULES AND AMENDMENTS
| Before 2022 (6) | After 2022 (4) |
|---|---|
| I and II: absolute protection (tiger, rhino) | **I:** highest protection |
| III and IV: lesser | **II:** lesser protection |
| **V: vermin** (common crow, fruit bats, rats, mice) | **III:** protected plants |
| **VI:** specified plants (licence from Chief Wild Life Warden to cultivate) | **IV:** **CITES** specimens |
- **Schedule I effect:** hunting and trade banned except special cases; heaviest penalties; a **tortoise** in Schedule I gets the same protection as the tiger.
- **Specified plant:** cannot be picked, uprooted or traded without permission.
| Species | Schedule |
|---|---|
| Asiatic lion, tiger, elephant, snow leopard, clouded leopard, rhino, Gangetic dolphin, **dugong**, GIB, **blackbuck** | I |
| **Mugger** (Kurnool, Sep 2026), **rock eagle-owl** (Dharmavaram, Aug 2026) | I |
| **Indian flying fox** (greater Indian fruit bat, Pteropus) | Was vermin (V) → now **II** |
!TRAP APPSC 2024: **Schedule IV = CITES**, not invasive species; plants = Schedule III.
#### 6.1 Amendments
| Amendment | Changes |
|---|---|
| **2002** | **Conservation Reserves** (government land; e.g. Tsarap Chu, Hesaraghatta) and **Community Reserves** (private/community land; **CRMC** formed by State under **Sec 36D**); **NBWL** replaced Indian Board for Wildlife |
| **2006** | **NTCA** and **WCCB** |
| **2022** | Schedules **6 → 4**; vermin schedule deleted; **CITES** provisions (management and scientific authorities); Centre regulates **invasive alien species** (African catfish, Lantana); States may declare conservation reserves; Chief Wildlife Warden in every State; voluntary surrender of captive animals without compensation; minimum fine for specially protected animals raised to **Rs 25,000** (from Rs 10,000); maximum fine for general violations **Rs 25,000 → Rs 1 lakh** |
- Protected area categories: sanctuary, national park, conservation reserve, community reserve.
### 7. FOREST (CONSERVATION) ACT 1980 / VAN ADHINIYAM
- **Sec 2:** prior **Central approval** to de-reserve a forest, use forest land for non-forest purpose or clear trees. FCA checks deforestation; IFA 1927 sets basic forest law.
- **2023 amendment** (August 2023; renamed w.e.f. **29 Dec 2023**) covers only: land notified as forest under IFA 1927 or other law, and land **recorded as forest on or after 25 Oct 1980**; excludes land converted before **12 Dec 1996**.
- **Exemptions:** strategic linear projects within **100 km** of IB, LoC, LAC; eco-tourism; zoos and safaris; minor infrastructure.
- **Compensatory afforestation:** equal non-forest land, else **twice** the degraded forest land + **NPV**.
- **Van Amendment Rules 2025:** assisted natural regeneration and plantation on leased forest land = forestry activities; CA and NPV do not apply.
- **Godavarman (1995):** "forest" = every area recorded as forest, whatever the ownership; the 2023 amendment narrows this "deemed forest" idea. Critique: silent on **FRA**; no Gram Sabha consent for new exemptions.
- **AP:** **3,474.57 ha** diverted (1 Jan 2015-5 Feb 2019); **149.5687 ha** (1 Apr-31 Dec 2025).
### 8. BIOLOGICAL DIVERSITY ACT 2002 AND 2023 AMENDMENT
- Implements **CBD** and **access and benefit sharing**. Three tiers: **NBA (Chennai, 2003)**, **State Biodiversity Boards**, **BMCs** (prepare **People's Biodiversity Registers**). **Biodiversity Heritage Sites** under Sec 37, notified by State Government.
- **2023 amendment:** offences **decriminalised** (civil penalties); benefit-sharing exemption for **registered AYUSH practitioners** and **BMC-certified cultivated medicinal plants**; NBA approval **180 → 90 days** (IPR stays 180).
- Did **NOT** end benefit-sharing or free **foreign nationals/foreign-incorporated entities** from prior NBA approval.
- **BD Rules 2024:** NBA passes 85-90% of proceeds to SBBs. **ABS Regulations (Apr 2025):** **25-40%** to identifiable source institutions, **60-75%** to local communities via SBBs/UTBCs.
- **AP:** **AP State Biodiversity Board** set up under the BD Act; vision of zero biodiversity loss by 2050.
- **AP BMCs and PBRs (22 May 2026):** more than **13,500 BMCs** constituted and **12,000 People's Biodiversity Registers** prepared across AP.

### 10. WHICH ACT PROTECTS WHAT
| Act | What it does |
|---|---|
| WPA 1972 | Wild animals, birds, plants and habitats |
| EPA 1986 | Umbrella law: environment and pollution |
| FCA 1980 | Forest-land diversion |
| PCA Act 1960 | Cruelty to animals; parent Act of AWBI |
| BD Act 2002 | CBD, ABS |
- **Nature (Protection) Act, 2000** does **not exist**.
- **NGT** hears cases under **7 laws** (FCA, EPA, BD Act...) but **NOT WPA 1972 or FRA 2006**.
- Biodiversity-related Central Acts also include Import and Export (Control) Act 1947, MMDR Act 1957, Customs Act 1962, IFA 1927.
- Forest Policy, EPA 1986, Industrial and Educational Policy all relate to ecological balance.
- **Amrita Devi Smriti Award:** Rajasthan forest department, for protecting forests and wildlife.
- India's PAs: **1,75,169.42 sq km = 5.32%** of land (2026).
