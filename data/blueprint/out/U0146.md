## U0146 | BHAKTI IN ANDHRA - ANNAMACHARYA, BAMMERA POTANA, VEMANA, THE TALLAPAKA POETS
### 1. SOUTH INDIAN ROOTS - ALVARS AND NAYANARS
#### 1.1 Alvars vs Nayanars
| Feature | Alvars | Nayanars (Nayanmars) |
|---|---|---|
| Sect / deity | **Vaishnava (Vishnu)** | **Shaiva (Shiva)** |
| Traditional number | **12** | **63** |
| Woman saint | **Andal** - the only female Alvar | **Karaikkal Ammaiyar** (extreme asceticism) |
| Key saints | Nammalvar, Tirumangai, Andal, Periyalvar, Poigai, Pudam | Appar, Sundarar, Tirugnana Sambandar, Manikkavachakar |
| Canon | **Nalayira Divya Prabandham** | **Tevaram** - earliest, most sacred part of the **Tirumurai** |
- Period c. **6th-9th centuries**; hymns in **Tamil**. Alvars were the **first Vaishnavite devotees** of South India.
- Their hymns marked sacred sites that grew into temple and pilgrimage centres; they challenged caste and Brahmanical dominance (Nayanmars included Dalits, hunters and women).
- **Tirumurai**: **12-volume** Tamil Shaiva canon, called the **"Fifth Veda"**; with the Vedas and Saiva agamas the base of **Saiva Siddhanta**.
- Chola patronage: **Parantaka I** installed statues of Appar, Sambandar, Sundarar; **Rajaraja I** consecrated bronzes of all **63 Nayanmars at Chidambaram**.
!TRAP Alvars = 12 = Vishnu; Nayanars = 63 = Shiva. Karaikkal Ammaiyar is a Nayanar, not a female Alvar. "Tirugyan" was not an Alvar.
### 2. ACHARYAS AND THEIR PHILOSOPHY
| Acharya | Philosophy | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Shankaracharya** | **Advaita** (non-dualism) | Born Kaladi, Kerala |
| **Ramanuja** (1017-1137) | **Vishishtadvaita** | Srirangam; worship of **Narayana and Lakshmi**; first true exponent of Bhakti |
| **Madhvacharya** | **Dvaita** | Spread at **Srikurmam** by **Narahari Tirtha** |
| **Nimbarka** | **Dvaitadvaita** | — |
| **Basavanna** | **Veerashaivism**; theology **Shakti Vishishtadvaita** (soul and Shiva basically identical) | Karnataka, 12th century |
- APPSC GS 2022: Ramanuja's devotion to Vishnu + social equality = Bhakti movement.
!TRAP Ramanuja = Vishishtadvaita; Basavanna = Shakti Vishishtadvaita.
### 3. VIRASHAIVA (LINGAYAT) MOVEMENT AND ITS SPREAD INTO ANDHRA
#### 3.1 Persons
| Person | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Basavanna (Basaveshwara)** | Founder, 12th century; minister of Kalachuri ruler **Bijjala II**; led the Sharana movement; **Anubhava Mantapa** |
| **Akka Mahadevi** | Woman saint, 12th-century Karnataka, Vachana tradition |
| **Mallikarjuna Panditaradhya** | Brought it into **Andhra**; **Sivatattvasaram** (~400 kanda poems); founder of **Aradhya Saivism**; originator of the **Sataka** form in Telugu |
| **Palkuriki Somanatha** (Kakatiya period) | **Basava Puranam** in simple Telugu **Dwipada** (APHQ 2019); **Panditaradhya Charitra**, **Basavana Ragada**, **Vrushadhipa Satakam** |
- Pandita reformers (Mallikarjuna, Manchana and others) spread it in Andhra; initial impact was limited.
- Somanatha taught equality, devotion to Shiva, **dignity of labour**, opposition to caste.
#### 3.2 Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Anubhava Mantapa | Early "religious parliament" at **Kalyana** |
| Vachanas | Kannada prose-poems |
| Shunya Sampadane | Later compilation of Anubhava Mantapa discourses |
| **Kayaka** | Honest labour |
| **Dasoha** | Sharing surplus |
| Ishtalinga | Linga worn on the body; worship of Shiva outside temples |
| Aradhya Saivism | Moderate Andhra Shaiva sect; kept Vedic learning and varna while preaching Shiva bhakti |
- Virashaivas rejected caste, untouchability and ritualism, condemned **child marriage**, supported **widow remarriage**, and **questioned rebirth** (UPSC 2016).
- 30 Jun 2026: pro-Basava groups protested manipulation of Sharana Vachanas and claims that Basava endorsed Brahmanical traditions.
!TRAP Sivatattvasaram = Mallikarjuna Panditaradhya, not Palkuriki Somanatha. Kayaka = labour; Dasoha = sharing.
### 4. ANNAMACHARYA (1408-1503)
- Born at **Tallapaka**; contemporary of **Saluva Narasimha** (APPSC-G2 2011).
- About **32,000 sankirtanas** to **Lord Venkateswara of Tirumala**; about **12,000 survive on copper plates** at Tirumala.
- **Veturi Prabhakara Sastry** recovered and edited the copper-plate songs.
- Titles: **Padakavita Pitamaha**, **Sankirtanacharya**, **Harikirtanacharya**; father of the Telugu keertana; pioneer of the **pada / kriti** form of Carnatic music; preceded and influenced Tyagaraja.
- Other works: **Sringara Manjari** (258 dvipadas), **Sri Venkateswara Satakam** (first Telugu Shringara Satakam), Dvipada Ramayanam, Alivelumangamma Satakam; Sanskrit **Sankirtana Lakshanam**, Venkatachala Mahatyam.
- **Arudra** called him **"Incarnated Telugu"**. **Annamayya district** is named after him.
!TRAP Sri Venkateswara Satakam is Annamacharya's, not his son Peda Tirumalacharya's.
### 5. THE TALLAPAKA FAMILY
| Member | Relation | Works / title |
|---|---|---|
| **Tallapaka Timmakka (Tirumalamma)** | Annamacharya's (first) wife | **Subhadra Kalyanam** (Dvipada); **first woman poet of Andhra** |
| **Peda Tirumalacharya** | Son | **Vairagya Vachanamalika**, **Sudarshana Ragada**, Chakravala Manjari, sankirtanas |
| **China Tirumalacharya** | Grandson (eldest son of Peda) | Title **Ashtabhasha Kavi Chakravarti**; **Ashtabhasha Dandakam**, Sankirtana Lakshanamu |
| **Chinnanna (Tiruvenkatanatha)** | Grandson (4th son of Peda) | **Annamacharya Charitra** (Dvipada biography), Paramayogi Vilasam, Usha Kalyanam, Ashtamahishi Kalyanam |
!TRAP The first Telugu Dandakam is Potana's Bhogini Dandakam, not China Tirumalacharya's Ashtabhasha Dandakam.
### 6. BAMMERA POTANA (c. 1450-1510)
- Native of **Bammera** (Janagama district, Telangana); contemporary of Srinatha.
- Titles: **Sahaja Panditya (Sahaja Panditudu)**, **Nigarva Chudamani**.
| Work | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Veerabhadra Vijayam** | His **first work**, at the request of his guru **Ivaturi Somanaradhya** |
| **Andhra Maha Bhagavatamu** | Telugu Bhagavata Purana, dedicated to **Lord Rama**; Potana wrote **8 of 12 skandhas**; printed by **C.P. Brown, 1840** |
| **Bhogini Dandakam** | **First independent Telugu Dandakam**; on Sarvajna Singabhupala and Bhogini |
- Other skandhas: Bopparaju Gangana (5th), Erchuri Singana (6th), Veligandala Narayana (11th-12th).
- **Patronage**: vowed never to dedicate his work to a human king; **refused Sarvajna Singabhupala of Rachakonda**; offered the Bhagavatam to Sri Rama.
!TRAP Veerabhadra Vijayam is Potana's; the APPSC 2011 key gave it to Srinatha.
### 7. SRINATHA (c. 1385-1475)
#### 7.1 Courts and patrons
| Phase | Patron / place | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| Kondaveedu | **Pedakomati Vema Reddy** | **Vidyadhikari** (education minister); wrote the king's Sanskrit inscriptions |
| Rajahmundry (after Kondaveedu fell, **1424**) | **Veerabhadra Reddy**, via Bendapudi Anantamatya | Bhimakhandam, Kasikhandam |
| Vijayanagara | **Devaraya II** | Defeated **Gauda Dindima Bhatta**; Kanakabhishekam; title **Kavi Sarvabhauma** |
| After Rajahmundry fell (**1448**) | **Palnadu**, then Srisailam | Poverty; refuge with Santhalingayya of the Bhikshavritti matha |
- Titles: **Kavi Sarvabhauma**, Andhra Bhasha Naishadhabhava, Sarvavidya Parangata, Brahmadatta Varaprasad.
- Pioneer of the **Prabandha** style; his age = **Kavya Yuga**; first reference to **paper** as writing material in Andhra (APPSC 2012).
#### 7.2 Works
| Work | Dedication / point |
|---|---|
| **Haravilasamu** | To **Avachi Tippaya Setti**; Reddi-era **sea trade, imports and exports** (APPSC 2019) |
| **Sringara Naishadhamu** | Telugu Naishadham of Sriharsha; to minister **Mamidi (Madiki) Singana** |
| **Bhimakhandamu** (Bhimeswara Puranam) | Rajahmundry court of Veerabhadra Reddy |
| **Kasikhandamu** | Rajahmundry Reddi period |
| **Sivaratri Mahatmyam** | Story of Sukumara; to **Mummadi Santayya** |
| **Madanavilasa Bhanam** | Bhana-style work |
| Maruttaratcharitra, Salivahana Saptasati | Youth works; also Sanskrit Sringaradipika |
- **Palnati Veera Charitra**: dvipada ballad of the **Palnadu war (1182)**; traditionally credited to Srinatha, but the **APPSC 2025 key** made it the answer to "which was NOT written by Srinatha" (against Haravilasam, Madanavilasa Bhanam, Sivaratri Mahatmyam).
#### 7.3 Palnadu in literature
- Srinatha's **chatu** verses from Palnadu picture its hard life and coarse food.
- Palnadu social life: houses painted with Rama and Krishna avatars; 1,500 painters' houses at Orugallu.
### 8. VEMANA AND POTULURI VEERABRAHMAM
| Feature | Vemana | Potuluri Veerabrahmendra Swamy |
|---|---|---|
| Work | Vemana padyalu in **colloquial Telugu** | **Kalagnanam** (prophecies) |
| Form | **Ataveladi** metre; refrain **"Vishwadabhirama Vinura Vema"** | — |
| Themes | Satire on ritual, **caste by birth**, idol worship, superstition, hypocrisy; humanism | Condemned caste; disciples of all castes and faiths, incl. **Siddaiah, a Muslim** |
- **C.P. Brown** collected, edited and translated **"Verses of Vemana" (1829)**.
- Vemana's date is disputed (17th c. vs 14th-15th c.).
!NUM C.P. Brown: Verses of Vemana 1829 | Andhra Maha Bhagavatamu 1840. He also printed Bhimeswara Puranam, Kasi Khandam, Palnati Veera Charitra and Tallapaka works.
### 9. LATER ANDHRA COMPOSERS AND TEMPLES
#### 9.1 Bhakta Ramadasu (Kancherla Gopanna)
- Golconda revenue official (Bhadrachalam tahsildar); used state revenue to rebuild the **Bhadrachalam Rama temple**.
- Jailed **12 years in Golconda fort** by **Abul Hasan Tana Shah**.
- Works: **Dasarathi Satakam** (in prison) and **Ramadasu kirtanas**; Dasarathi Satakam translated into English by **Dr. Bhanumathi Ramakrishna** (APPSC-G2 2001).
#### 9.2 Other composers
| Figure | Deity / place | Work |
|---|---|---|
| **Kshetrayya** (Varadayya of **Movva**, Krishna dt.) | **Muvva Gopala** (Krishna) | 1,000+ Shringara padams; at **Abdullah Qutb Shah's** court |
| **Tyagaraja** | Rama; **Tiruvaiyaru** | Telugu kritis, **Pancharatna kritis**; created new ragas; Carnatic Trinity with **Muthuswami Dikshitar** and **Shyama Shastri** |
| **Tarigonda Vengamamba** | Venkateswara, **Tirumala** | **Venkatachala Mahatyamu** (18th century) |
| **Dhurjati** | Srikalahasti | **Srikalahasteeswara Satakam** |
#### 9.3 Temple → saint
| Temple | Saint |
|---|---|
| Tirumala | Annamacharya, Tallapaka family, Vengamamba |
| Bhadrachalam | Bhakta Ramadasu |
| Muvva | Kshetrayya |
| Tiruvaiyaru | Tyagaraja |
| Srikurmam | Narahari Tirtha (Dvaita) |
!TRAP Annamacharya and Tyagaraja were NOT contemporaries. Most Tyagaraja kritis are in Telugu and praise Rama, not Krishna.
### 10. TELUGU WOMEN POETS
| Poet | Work | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Tallapaka Timmakka** | Subhadra Kalyanam | First woman poet of Andhra |
| **Atukuri Molla** | **Molla Ramayanam** | Potter community; **first Telugu woman to translate the Ramayana**; from Gopavaram |
| **Gangadevi** | **Madhura Vijayam** (Veera Kamparaya Charitam), Sanskrit | Sangama princess; husband **Kumara Kampana's** conquest of Madurai; disciple of Agastya |
| **Oduva Tirumalamba** | **Varadambika Parinayam** (Sanskrit) | Marriage of **Achyuta Deva Raya** and Varadambika |
| Ramabhadramba | Raghunathabhyudayam | Sanskrit |
| Tarigonda Vengamamba | Venkatachala Mahatyamu | Tirumala |
- APPSC-G2 2023 and 2025: wrong match = **Gangadevi - Marichi Parinayam**.
- Others: **Amuktamalyada** = Krishnadevaraya (not Tirumalamba); Allasani Peddana his court poet; **Gatha Saptasati** = Hala; **Vignanesvaram** = Ketana.
- Poet titles: Nannaya **Adikavi**; Tikkana **Kavibrahma**; Errapragada **Prabandha Parameswara**; Peddana **Andhra Kavita Pitamaha**.
### 11. SOCIAL IMPACT OF BHAKTI
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Caste critique | Alvars, Nayanars, Basava, Vemana, Veerabrahmam; some promoted **common dining** |
| Vernaculars | **Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam** replaced Sanskrit |
| Forms | **Satakam and dwipada** carried bhakti to common people |
| Women saints | Andal, Karaikkal Ammaiyar, Akka Mahadevi, Atukuri Molla |
| Music | Padams, keertanas, satakas, dvipadas = Telugu base of Carnatic music |
- Bhakti did not uniformly uphold Brahmanical dominance. Other spread factors: Chola patronage (Thanjavur, Gangaikondacholapuram, Chidambaram); fusion of little and great traditions (Jagannath at Puri).
- Related: **Saraswati Gachchas** were set up by **Jains** to spread education; **Visvesvara Sivacharya** was guru of Kakatiya **Ganapati Deva**.
