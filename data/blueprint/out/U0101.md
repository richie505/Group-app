## U0101 | GAJAPATIS OF ORISSA IN ANDHRA; BAHMANI INCURSIONS INTO ANDHRADESA
### 1. BAHMANI KINGDOM – FOUNDER AND CAPITALS
- Founded **1347** (**14th century**) by **Alauddin Hasan Bahman Shah** – **Hasan Gangu / Zafar Khan** (PYQ UPPSC 2005, 2016).
- First capital **Gulbarga**, named **Hasanabad**; four regions: **Gulbarga, Daulatabad, Berar, Bidar**.
- **Kapaya Nayaka** helped Hasan Gangu found it.
- **Ahmad Shah I** shifted the capital from **Gulbarga to Bidar** (APPSC G2 2003).
- Kingdom was **largest under Mahmud Gawan**, minister of **Muhammad III**; Gawan was **executed in 1481** – this broke the kingdom apart.
!TRAP Capital shift to Bidar = Ahmad Shah I, not Hasan Gangu or Muhammad III.
### 2. BAHMANI INCURSIONS INTO ANDHRADESA
| Sultan / commander | Incursion |
|---|---|
| **Muhammad Shah I** | Fought **Kapaya Nayaka**; burned Kapaya's son **Vinayaka** alive (**1361**); forced Kapaya to cede **Golconda**, 400 elephants, 250 horses (**1364–65**) |
| **Firoz Shah** (1397–1422) | Beaten at **Kambhammetta (1396)** by **Gajarao Tippa**; repulsed at **Kandukuru (1398)** by **Machareddi**; allied with **Pedakomati** against **Rajamahendravaram (1417)** |
| **Muhammad III + Mahmud Gawan** | Put down **Telangana revolts** (from **1453**); subdued **Orugallu (Warangal)**; took **Kondaveedu** and pushed into **Odisha (1471)** |
### 3. GAJAPATI RULERS IN ANDHRA
- **Gajapati** = **"Lord of Elephants"**; capital **Cuttack**; rule **c. 1434–1541**; whole of **north coastal Andhra** under them.
- Order of Andhra campaigns: **Kapilendra → Purushottama → Prataparudra**.
| Ruler | Andhra campaign |
|---|---|
| **Kapilendra (Kapileswara)** – founder | Attacked **Tiran Andhra (1448)** (Penugonda inscription); annexed **Rajamahendravaram** after Deva Raya II's death, ending the Rajahmundry Reddis; took **Kondaveedu (c. 1450)**, Bellamkonda, Velugodu, Addanki, Srisailam; besieged Vijayanagara |
| **Hamvira** (Kapileswara's son) | **Defeated the Bahmani army attacking Devarakonda**; 1471 – Bahmanis installed him as a puppet → overturned by Purushottama |
| **Purushottama** | Won the throne after a struggle; **refused to help Muhammad III**; recovered ground from the Bahmanis |
| **Prataparudra** | Lost **Udayagiri, Kondaveedu, Kondapalli** to **Krishnadevaraya** (1513–1519) |
### 4. GAJAPATI CONQUESTS – PLACES
| Place | Conquest |
|---|---|
| **Rajamahendravaram** | Taken by Kapileswara after **1446**; Reddis had been helped by Vijayanagara commander **Mallappa Odayar** (Draksharama inscription, **1444**) |
| **Kondaveedu** | Rajamahendra, with **Velama** support, took Varam → marched on Kondaveedu; under Vijayanagara **till 1449** → fell to Gajapatis **c. 1450** |
| **Udayagiri** (Nellore) | Major Gajapati stronghold in southern Andhra |
### 5. GAJAPATI ADMINISTRATION IN ANDHRA
| Officer / unit | Function |
|---|---|
| **Naidu** | **Revenue officers in southern (Andhra) regions** (APPSC GS 2025) |
| **Nayakas and samantas** | Collected revenue and kept order |
| **Dandapata** | Large province (whole districts) with standing army |
- The **Reddi-era social order** was kept.
!KEY APPSC GS 2025: Title of Gajapati southern revenue officers = **Naidu**.
### 6. GAJAPATI–VIJAYANAGARA CONFLICT
#### 6.1 Saluva Narasimha
- Drove the Gajapatis out of south-eastern Andhra by **c. 1470**; occupied land up to the **Godavari**.
#### 6.2 Krishnadevaraya's Campaign (1513–1519)
1. **Udayagiri** – fell after an **18-month siege**.
2. Kandukuru, Vinukonda, Addanki, **Kondaveedu**.
3. **Kondapalli**, **Bejawada**.
4. March to **Cuttack (1518)**.
5. Marriage and peace, **c. 1518–19**.
#### 6.3 Peace Terms
- **Marriage**: Prataparudra's daughter **Annapurna Devi** (also named Bhadradevi / Tukkadevi / Jaganmohini) married Krishnadevaraya.
- **Lands north of the Krishna returned** to the Gajapati, who stayed as a feudatory; **Balakrishna idol** given.
- **Cuttack / Gajapati heartland NOT taken over.**
!TRAP "Treaty of Udayagiri (1480s)" is wrong – Krishnadevaraya reigned 1509–29; the settlement was c. 1518–19.

### 11. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. Bahmani founded **1347**, 14th century, at **Gulbarga**.
2. **Bidar** = Ahmad Shah I.
3. Naidu = Gajapati **revenue officer** in southern Andhra.
4. Hamvira **defeated** the Bahmanis at Devarakonda, then was their puppet in 1471.
5. Purushottama **refused** help to Muhammad III.
6. Cuttack was **not** annexed by Krishnadevaraya.
7. Udayagiri fell first, Cuttack reached last (1518).
8. Kambhammetta = Gajarao Tippa; Kandukuru = Machareddi.
9. Raichur Doab = Krishna–Tungabhadra, not Krishna–Godavari.
