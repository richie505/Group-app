## U0314 | CHIEF MINISTER, STATE COUNCIL OF MINISTERS AND ADVOCATE GENERAL
### 1. POSITION OF THE CHIEF MINISTER
- CM = **real executive head** of the State and head of the Government; the Governor is the nominal head.
- Leader of the majority party or coalition in the **Legislative Assembly**; leader of the House; chief spokesperson of the State Government.
- **Art. 163(1)**: Council of Ministers headed by the CM aids and advises the Governor, **except where he must act in his discretion** – so not all functions are on advice.
- CM = State counterpart of the **PM (Arts 74-75)**; the State has **no counterpart of the Vice-President**.
- **Secretariat**: top wing of State administration; main force shaping the procedure rules of State Government.
!KEY Executive is subordinate to the Legislature: the Council of Ministers is collectively answerable to the popular House.
### 2. ARTICLE 164 – CLAUSE BY CLAUSE
| Clause | Provision |
|---|---|
| 164(1) | **Governor appoints the CM** (not the President); other Ministers on CM's advice; hold office during Governor's pleasure |
| 164(1A) | Size limit – 15%, minimum 12 (91st Amendment) |
| 164(2) | **Collectively responsible to the Legislative Assembly** alone |
| 164(3) | **Oath administered by the Governor** |
| 164(4) | Non-member can be Minister for **six consecutive months**, then ceases |
| 164(5) | **Salaries fixed by the State Legislature** by law; until then, **Second Schedule** |
- Ministers hold office at the Governor's pleasure but are **not collectively answerable to him**; not answerable to the **Legislative Council**.
- CM must be a member of the Assembly or Council – need not be one when appointed, but must become one within **6 months**.
- Resignation addressed to the **Governor**. CM's **term is not fixed** by the Constitution.
- **Delhi and Puducherry**: CM appointed by the **President** (Art. 239AA Delhi; Art. 239A / GUT Act 1963 Puducherry).
!TRAP APPSC-GS 2021: CM is appointed under Art. 164(1) by the Governor. Clause order: (1) appointment, (2) responsibility, (3) oath, (4) six months, (5) salaries.
### 3. APPOINTMENT IN A HUNG ASSEMBLY AND FLOOR TEST
- Constitution gives **no detailed criteria**; the Governor uses **discretion**.
- **Sarkaria (1988) / Punchhi (2010)** order of invitation:
1. Pre-poll alliance
2. Single largest party with support
3. Post-poll coalition
4. Post-poll alliance with outside support
- Followed by an **early floor test** (applied in Tamil Nadu, May 2026).
#### 3.1 Tenure and Confidence
- CM stays only while he has the **Assembly's confidence**; on losing it – **resign or be dismissed** by the Governor.
- The Constituent Assembly cautioned against a literal reading of "pleasure".
### 4. SIZE OF THE COUNCIL OF MINISTERS
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Amendment | **91st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2003** |
| Maximum | **15%** of total Assembly strength, **including the CM** |
| Minimum | **12** (including the CM) |
| Article | 164(1A) |
| AP example | Assembly **175** seats → maximum **26** ministers (15% = 26.25) |
!NUM NUMBER GAME: 15% maximum; 12 minimum; 6 months non-member rule; 175 seats → 26 ministers in AP.
### 5. TRIBAL WELFARE MINISTER – ARTICLE 164(1)
- A Minister for **Tribal Welfare** (may also handle SC and backward class welfare) is **compulsory** in:
1. **Chhattisgarh**
2. **Jharkhand**
3. **Madhya Pradesh**
4. **Odisha**
- **Bihar** was dropped by the **94th Amendment, 2006**.
!TRAP Bihar is NOT in the present list (removed in 2006).
### 6. POWERS OF THE CM AND DUTIES TOWARDS THE GOVERNOR
#### 6.1 Powers
- Selects Ministers, **allocates portfolios**, can recommend removal of any Minister.
- Advises the Governor on **summoning and prorogation** of the Legislature and **dissolution** of the Assembly.
#### 6.2 Article 167 – Duties of the CM
1. **Communicates** to the Governor all Council decisions on administration and **proposals for legislation**.
2. **Furnishes information** the Governor calls for.
3. If the Governor requires, **submits for the Council's consideration** any matter decided by a single Minister.
- **Art. 167** defines the CM's duties; CM is the **chief link** between Governor and Council.
- Governor asking for information under Art. 167 = a **constitutional discretion** of the Governor.
| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| 163(1) | Aid and advice |
| 164 | Appointment, responsibility, size, salaries |
| 167 | CM's duties to the Governor |
| 174 | Sessions, prorogation, dissolution |

### 8. DEPUTY CHIEF MINISTER
- **Not mentioned in the Constitution**; a political arrangement; sworn in as a **Minister**.
- Precedence: ranks with Union Ministers of State at **No. 10**.
- AP instances: **K.V. Ranga Reddy** – Deputy CM of united AP (1956); **five** Deputy CMs in the 2019-24 government; **K. Pawan Kalyan** (Jana Sena) – Deputy CM (2026).
### 9. 130TH CONSTITUTION AMENDMENT BILL, 2025
- Amends **Arts 75, 164, 239AA**.
- PM, CM or Minister in custody for **30 consecutive days** for an offence punishable with **5+ years** → removed on the **31st day**.
### 10. ADVOCATE GENERAL – ARTICLE 165
| Feature | Fact |
|---|---|
| Article | **165** (State counterpart of the Attorney General, Art. 76) |
| Appointed by | **Governor** |
| Qualification | Qualified to be a **High Court judge** – citizen; 10 years judicial office or 10 years as HC advocate |
| Age limit | None prescribed |
| Term | **No fixed term**; holds office during the **pleasure of the Governor** |
| Remuneration | Determined by the **Governor** |
| Rights in Legislature | **Art. 177** – speak and take part in proceedings of the House and its committees, **no vote** |
- Not tied to the CM's term: need not resign when the CM resigns (Patna HC, 2022).
