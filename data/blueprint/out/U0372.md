## U0372 | CONSTITUTIONAL, STATUTORY, EXECUTIVE, REGULATORY AND QUASI-JUDICIAL BODIES
### 1. HOW TO TELL THEM APART
| Type | Basis | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Constitutional | An **Article** of the Constitution | FC, CAG, ECI, NCBC, ISC |
| Statutory | **Act of Parliament**; changed by amending that Act | NHRC, NCM, Zonal Councils |
| Executive | **Resolution / executive order**; advisory | NITI Aayog, NDC, Law Commission |
| Quasi-judicial | Statute; can summon, sanction; **not a court** | NHRC, SEBI, CIC |
### 2. UNION CONSTITUTIONAL BODIES
| Body | Article | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| Attorney General | **76** | President's pleasure; qualified as SC judge |
| CAG | **148** | **6 years or 65**; removed like an SC judge |
| GST Council | **279A** | **101st CAA, 2016** |
| Inter-State Council | **263** | Formed by the **President** |
| Finance Commission | **280** | **5 members** (Chairman + 4); qualifications fixed by Parliament by law |
| UPSC / SPSC / JSPSC | **315** | Removal only by **President** after SC inquiry (**Art. 317**); Governor cannot remove |

- **President appoints** AG, FC, CAG, ECI, NCSC, NCST, NCBC, 350B officer.
- FC: sharing of **net tax proceeds**.
- Order of precedence: UPSC Chairperson, CEC, CAG = **9A**; NCM, NCSC, NCST chairpersons = **17**.
### 3. CONSTITUTIONAL VS EXTRA-CONSTITUTIONAL – ODD ONE OUT
- **NITI Aayog** is the odd one among NCBC, CAG, FC (APPSC 2025).
- Among NITI Aayog, NCM, Art. 350B officer, NHRC → **only one** constitutional: **Art. 350B officer** (APPSC G2 2023).
- **ECI (Art. 324)** constitutional; **UGC, NHRC, CVC** statutory.
!TRAP NCM = statutory (1992 Act); Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities = constitutional (Art. 350B).
#### 3.1 Inter-State and Cooperation Bodies
| Body | Status | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Inter-State Council** | Constitutional | **Art. 263**; set up by President, **1990**, on **Sarkaria** advice |
| River water tribunals | **Statutory** | Inter-State River Water Disputes Act **1956** (Art. 262 enables) |
| **Zonal Councils** | Statutory, non-constitutional | States Reorganisation Act **1956 (Sec. 15)** |
| North Eastern Council | Statutory | **NEC Act 1971**; Governors + CMs; **8** NE States |
| National Security Council | Extra-constitutional | Executive order, **1998** |
- Of ISC, NSC, Zonal Councils → **only ISC** is created by the Constitution.
- **NDC and Governors' Conference**: extra-constitutional, extra-legal devices.
- NDC, Planning Commission, Zonal Councils: **not in** the Constitution.
- "Unity in diversity": ISC, NDC, FC, Zonal Councils, unitary judiciary, AIS.

### 5. EXECUTIVE BODIES
| Body | Created | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| **Planning Commission** | Resolution, **15 March 1950** | Advisory; PM chair; no State members |
| **NDC** | **6 August 1952**, executive order | PM, Cabinet ministers (**from 1967**), CMs, UT administrators |
| **NITI Aayog** | Cabinet Resolution, **1 January 2015** | Think tank; PM chair; Governing Council = all CMs and LGs |

#### 5.1 Law Commission
- Advisory research body: **not** constitutional, statutory or quasi-judicial; reports to **Ministry of Law and Justice**, not the President.
- **23rd** Law Commission: 1 Sep 2024 – 31 Aug 2027.
!TRAP APPSC G1 2024: statements calling the Law Commission quasi-judicial and adviser to the President = "All incorrect".

### 7. QUASI-JUDICIAL BODIES
- Not a court; can **interpret and apply law**, summon, sanction. Examples: **NHRC, SEBI, CIC**, Mental Health Review Commission.
- **Civil court powers** (summon, requisition records): **NCBC** (esp. since 2018), **NCSC, NCST, CIC**.
- Not quasi-judicial: **NITI Aayog, Law Commission** (advisory).
