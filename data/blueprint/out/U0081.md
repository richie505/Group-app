## U0081 | EASTERN CHALUKYA ART AND ARCHITECTURE – BICCAVOLU, DRAKSHARAMA AND SAMALKOT TEMPLES
### 1. THE VENGI SCHOOL – STYLE AND INFLUENCES
- The Eastern Chalukyas mainly followed the **Dravida** style – a mix of **Pallava (Kanchi)** and **Badami Chalukya** influences with **local Andhra** features = the **Vengi School**.
- Also described as **Vesara (Chalukya) style** – **Dravidian vimana + Buddhist art** influence.
- Materials: local **laterite and limestone**; most temples stand in the **Krishna-Godavari delta**.
- Comparative note: the **Badami Chalukyas** developed **Vesara** in Karnataka (in AP: **Navabrahma temples – nine – at Alampur**, 7th–8th c. CE); the **Vengi line** carried a **Dravida-based** regional style into the **Chalukya-Chola** phase.
| Feature | Badami Chalukyas | Eastern Chalukyas (Vengi School) |
|---|---|---|
| Main region | Karnataka (and Alampur in AP) | Krishna-Godavari delta |
| Style | **Vesara** developed | **Dravida-based** regional style |
| Influences | – | **Pallava (Kanchi)** + Badami Chalukya + local Andhra |
| Material | – | Local **laterite and limestone** |
| Example in AP | **Navabrahma temples, Alampur** | **Draksharama, Samalkot, Bikkavolu** |
| Later phase | – | Led into the **Chalukya-Chola** style |
#### 1.1 Chalukya-Chola Style – Model Temple
- **Draksharama Bhimeswara temple** = model of the **Chalukya-Chola style** – **Pallava + early Chalukya** features blended into one independent regional style (G2 2025 key, asked twice).
| Temple | Style / place |

### 2. TEMPLE-BUILDING CLAIMS → RULER
#### 2.1 Vijayaditya II – 108 Shiva Temples
- **Vijayaditya II (Narendra Mrigaraja)** fought **108 battles over 12 years** against the **Rashtrakutas** (**Govinda III and Amoghavarsha I**) and their **Gangavadi** allies.
- Built **108 Saiva temples**, one at each battle site – called **Narendreswara / Narendresvaralayas / Narendra Mrigeswaralayas** (G2 2008, 2012, 2019, 2025 keys).
- Source: the **Sataluru inscription**.
!TRAP The 108 temples are recorded in the **Sataluru** inscription; the **Kopparam** inscription is **Pulakeshin II's** record.
#### 2.2 Chalukya Bhima I – Pancharamas and Twin Bhimeswara Temples
- **Chalukya Bhima I** built the **Pancharamas** / made them **Shaiva kshetras**.
- Built the **twin Bhimeswara temples**: **Draksharama Bhimeswara** and **Kumararama Bhimeswara (Samalkot)**, on the Godavari region (G2 2025, statement correct).

### 5. BICCAVOLU (BIKKAVOLU) TEMPLE GROUP
- Old name **Birudankaprolu = Bikkavolu**.
- Shiva temples of **Gunaga Vijayaditya III's time (9th century)**: **Mukteswara, Vijayeswara, Chandrasekhara, Raja Raja** and **Golingeswara**.
- **Golingeswara** – the **only one of the group still standing**.
- **Vijayeswara** – carved **Ganga and Yamuna** figures.
- Style: Vengi-school **Dravida** temples with Pallava and Badami Chalukya features.
- Dating debate: some treat Bikkavolu as a **"Chalukya-Reddi transition"** group because of **later Reddi additions**; the core belongs to the 9th century.
!TRAP 2025 key: "Rajaraja Narendra built the **Rajarajeswara** temple at Biccavole" was marked the **incorrect** statement. A Raja Raja temple is listed in Gunaga Vijayaditya's Bikkavolu group.
### 6. DRAKSHARAMA VS SAMALKOT
| Feature | Draksharama | Samalkot |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Bhimeswara (Draksharama) | **Kumararama / Bheemarama** (Kumara Bhimeswara) |
| Builder | Chalukya Bhima I | Chalukya Bhima I (for his son) |
| Plan | **Two-storeyed** Bhimeswara shrine | **Two-storeyed**, closely similar plan |
| Lingam | **Massive lingam** | **Black-granite lingam** in the core sanctum |
| Special features | Huge **Nandi mandapa**, intricate carving; one of the **Trilinga kshetras** | Towering **gopuram added later** |
| Later work | Renovated by the **Reddis** | Renovated by the **Reddis** |
- Both are **twin Bhimeswara** temples of Chalukya Bhima I; the core sanctum of Samalkot is Chalukya Bhima's work.
- Both belong to the **Pancharama** circuit: Draksharama is itself a Pancharama, and Samalkot is **Kumararama**.
- Both were later **repaired by the Reddis**, so some later additions (like the Samalkot gopuram) are not Eastern Chalukya work.
!KEY Draksharama and Samalkot = same builder (**Chalukya Bhima I**), same two-storeyed plan; Samalkot honours his **son** (Kumara).
