## U0265 | NAXALITE MOVEMENT IN ANDHRA PRADESH – SRIKAKULAM ORIGINS, COURSE AND IMPACT
### 1. NAXALBARI – THE SPARK (1967)
- **Naxalbari peasant uprising**: **March–May 1967**, Siliguri sub-division, **Darjeeling** district, West Bengal.
- Leaders: **Charu Majumdar** and **Kanu Sanyal**.
- Slogan: **"Land to the tiller"**.
- **"Ballot to Bullet"** line: rejected the **CPI(M)'s parliamentary path**; favoured armed revolutionary struggle.
- Causes: **failed land reforms** (zamindari abolition, ceilings, tenancy); exploitation by landlords and moneylenders.
- **1967 Andhra extremist wing** split from CPI(M): **Tarimela Nagi Reddy, Chandra Pulla Reddy, Kolla Venkaiah, Nagabhushana Patnaik**.
### 2. SRIKAKULAM GIRIJAN STRUGGLE
#### 2.1 Key Facts
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Place | **Srikakulam Agency** (tribal hills) |
| Period | **1968–70** |
| Tribes | **Savaras (Soras)** and **Jatapus** |
| Leaders | **Vempatapu Satyam** and **Adibhatla Kailasam** (school teachers) |
| Spread by | Kanu Sanyal and Tarimela Nagi Reddy |
| Party | Began under **CPI(M)**; shifted to **CPI(ML) in 1969** |
| Nature | Tribal-peasant uprising against landlords, moneylenders and forest officials |
- Trigger: killing of two tribal activists (Koraana and Manganna) on **31 October 1967** at Levidi, on the way to a **Girijan Sangham** meeting.
- Vempatapu Satyam and Adibhatla Kailasam were killed in **July 1970**; the struggle broke up by the end of 1970.
- Outcome: **suppressed** by the State, but it seeded the Naxalite movement and revolutionary literature in Andhra.
!TRAP Srikakulam began under the **CPI(M)** (PYQ key for the "1967 Srikakulam uprising"), and shifted to the CPI(ML) only in 1969.

### 3. NAXALITE GROUPS
| Group | Founder(s) | Year | Base |
|---|---|---|---|
| AP extremist CPI(ML) wing | Nagi Reddy, Pulla Reddy, Venkaiah, Patnaik | **1967** | Andhra Pradesh |
| **CPI(ML)** | **Charu Majumdar** (with Kanu Sanyal) | **1969** | West Bengal |

- **Kishanji** – Maoist leader killed by security forces in **2012**.

#### 4.1 Virasam (Revolutionary Writers' Association)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|

| Founding president | **Sri Sri** |
| Vice-presidents | **Kodavatiganti Kutumba Rao** and **Rachakonda Viswanatha Sastry** |

#### 4.2 Jana Natya Mandali
- **Art Lovers** (1970), founded by **B. Narsinga Rao** and **Gaddar** → renamed **Jana Natya Mandali** (1971).
- Srikakulam changed Telugu culture through Virasam and Jana Natya Mandali.
!PAIR Virasam | 1970 | Sri Sri || Jana Natya Mandali | 1971 | Gaddar
### 5. IMPACT ON LAND REFORMS AND TRIBAL AREAS
| Measure | Year / place | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Regulation 1 of 1970** (AP Scheduled Areas Land Transfer) | 1970 | Barred transfer of Scheduled Area land from tribals to non-tribals; response to tribal unrest |
| **ITDAs** (Integrated Tribal Development Agencies) | **Fifth Plan (1974–75)**, Tribal Sub-Plan | Tribal development |
| **Girijan Cooperative Corporation (GCC)** | HQ **Visakhapatnam**; since **1956** | Buys **minor forest produce**, supplies essentials; frees tribals from moneylenders |
- GCC is a **PSU** working for tribal upliftment in AP.
!TRAP GCC (1956) is older than the movement; it is not a response created after Srikakulam.
### 6. EMERGENCY ENCOUNTERS AND BHARGAVA COMMISSION
- During the **Emergency (1975–77)** many Naxalite cadres were killed in alleged **"encounters"** in AP.
- **Bhargava Commission (1977)**, headed by **Justice Vashist Bhargava**, inquired into these encounter killings.
### 7. GOVERNMENT RESPONSES
| Response | Year | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Greyhounds** | **1989** | Elite anti-Naxal **special police force** of AP Police (not a dog squad) |
| Ban | **1992** | PWG banned in AP |
| Surrender policy | 1998–2001 | Under DGP **H.J. Dora** (1996–2002), over **1,200** cadres surrendered (as of Mar 2026 report) |
| **Peace talks** | **October 2004** | Government–Maoist talks in **Hyderabad** (YSR government) |
- **Greyhounds Training Centre**: **Anandapuram** (near Visakhapatnam), under **Sec. 9 of AP Reorganisation Act, 2014**.
- It secures the LWE corridor of **Alluri Sitharama Raju (ASR)** and **Parvathipuram Manyam** districts on the Odisha–Chhattisgarh borders.
- Significance: Srikakulam made **AP a core base of Indian Maoism**.
