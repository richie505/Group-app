### 6. LANDMARK JUDGMENTS AND PENDING MATTERS
#### 6.1 SIR verdict (27 May 2026)
- Supreme Court **upheld** the ECI's **Special Intensive Revision (SIR)** of electoral rolls; bench led by **CJI Surya Kant**.
- Basis of ECI's power: **Article 324** and the **Representation of the People Act, 1950**.
- The ECI **cannot determine citizenship**; SIR began in **Bihar** (order of 24 June 2025).
