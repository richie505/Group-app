## U0237 | SWARAJISTS AND NO-CHANGERS; CONSTRUCTIVE PROGRAMME IN THE 1920S
### 1. TWO CAMPS AFTER THE NCM
- After the NCM was suspended at **Bardoli (1922)**, Congress split into two camps.
| Point | Swarajists (Pro-changers) | No-Changers |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy | **Council entry** – "end or mend" the councils | **Constructive work**; continue the boycott |
| Leaders | **C.R. Das, Motilal Nehru, Vithalbhai Patel** | **C. Rajagopalachari, Vallabhbhai Patel, Rajendra Prasad, M.A. Ansari** |
| Strength | Blocked the Finance Bill; forced a review of the 1919 Act | Built village base: khadi, schools, unity |
| Weakness | Urban, legislature-bound; weak on Bengal peasants | Little political pressure on the government |
- Council work and constructive work proved **complementary**: together they kept the movement alive in the **1922-28 lull**.
!KEY Swarajists and No-Changers reunited at Belgaum (1924).
### 2. SWARAJ PARTY – FORMATION
- **Gaya session (Dec 1922, president C.R. Das):** Das's **council-entry resolution was defeated/set aside**.
- Das and Motilal Nehru then formed the **Congress-Khilafat Swaraj Party** (announced 31 Dec 1922; formed **1 January 1923**): **C.R. Das president, Motilal Nehru secretary**.
- Formed **after the suspension of the NCM**.
- **Delhi special session (Sept 1923)** allowed Swarajists to contest the **1923 elections**.
!TRAP Jawaharlal Nehru, Rajendra Prasad and C. Rajagopalachari were NOT Swaraj Party founders/members.
### 3. INC SESSIONS OF THE 1920S
| Year | Venue | President |
|---|---|---|
| 1920 | Nagpur (central India) | C. Vijayaraghavachariar |
| 1922 | **Gaya (Bihar)** | **C.R. Das** |
| 1923 | **Kakinada (Andhra)** | **Maulana Mohammad Ali** |
| 1924 | **Belgaum (Karnataka)** | **Mahatma Gandhi** |
| 1925 | **Kanpur** | **Sarojini Naidu** (first Indian woman president) |
| 1926 | **Guwahati** | **S. Srinivasa Iyengar** |
| 1927 | **Madras** | **M.A. Ansari** (boycott of Simon Commission) |
| 1929 | Lahore | Jawaharlal Nehru – **Purna Swaraj** |
!KEY Belgaum 1924 = the ONLY INC session presided over by Gandhi; it reunited Swarajists and No-Changers.
!TRAP Purna Swaraj = Lahore (1929); NCM suspended = Bardoli (1922). Do not mix them with Belgaum.
### 4. SWARAJISTS IN THE CENTRAL LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY
#### 4.1 Allies and Groups
- **Nationalist Party (1924):** Swarajists + **Jinnah's Independents** + **Malaviya's group**; about **70 members**; outvoted the government and **blocked the Finance Bill**.
#### 4.2 Record → Year
| Item | Year | Point |
|---|---|---|
| Nationalist Party | 1924 | About 70 members |
| **Muddiman Committee** | 1924 | Result of the Swarajist demand to **review the 1919 Act** |
| **Speaker election** | **1925** | **Vithalbhai Patel** – first elected Indian President of the Assembly |
| Elections | 1926 | Swarajists did poorly |
| **Public Safety Bill** | **1928** | **Opposed** by the Swarajists |
| Walk-out | 1930 | Swarajists left the councils after Lahore |
- Vithalbhai Patel won on **24 August 1925** by **58 to 56** votes against the government-backed **T. Rangachariar**.
### 5. DECLINE OF THE SWARAJISTS
- **C.R. Das died in June 1925** → the party split.
- **Responsivists:** wanted **cooperation with the government** (responsive cooperation) – **Lala Lajpat Rai, Madan Mohan Malaviya, N.C. Kelkar**.
- 1926 elections: poor results; 1930: walk-out after the Lahore session.
!PAIR Responsivists | Lajpat Rai, Malaviya, Kelkar | Cooperation || Nationalist Party | Swarajists, Jinnah, Malaviya | Opposition bloc
### 6. NO-CHANGERS AND THE CONSTRUCTIVE PROGRAMME
#### 6.1 Leaders
- **C. Rajagopalachari** (Madras), **Vallabhbhai Patel**, **Rajendra Prasad**, **M.A. Ansari**.
#### 6.2 Components of the Constructive Programme
1. **Khadi** and spinning.
2. **National schools/colleges**.
3. **Hindu-Muslim unity**.
4. **Removal of untouchability**.
5. **Prohibition**.
6. **Village panchayats**.
- The No-Changers focused on constructive work and **continued the boycott** of councils.

#### 7.2 Kakinada Congress, 1923
- Held in **Andhra**; president **Maulana Mohammad Ali**.
- **Local women** served as **executive members and volunteers**.
#### 7.3 Constructive Work in Andhra
- **Ponduru (Srikakulam)** khadi was famed nationally.
- Andhra women did **picketing and prohibition** work.
