## U0558 | POLAVARAM PROJECT – STATUS, FUNDING, NATIONAL PROJECT STATUS, SUBMERGENCE AND R&R
### 1. PROJECT FEATURES
| Feature | Fact |
|---|---|
| River / site | **Godavari**, near **Polavaram village, Eluru district** (earlier **West Godavari** – old PYQ key) |
| Dam | **2,467.50 m** earth-cum-rock fill dam; spillway **1,121.20 m** |
| Reservoir | Gross **194.6 TMC**; utilisation 322.73 TMC |
| Irrigation | **7.20 lakh acres (2.91 lakh ha)** – East & West Godavari, Eluru, Krishna, Visakhapatnam |
| Hydel | **960 MW (12 units × 80 MW)** |
| Drinking/industry | **23.44 TMC for Visakhapatnam**; 28.5 lakh people in 540 villages |
#### 1.1 Canals
| Canal | Destination |
|---|---|
| **Right Main Canal (RMC)** | **Krishna delta** – carries **80 TMC** of Godavari water to the Krishna (Pattiseema lift as interim) |
| **Left Main Canal (LMC)** | **Visakhapatnam** (Revised Administrative Sanction Jun 2026; LMC to Visakhapatnam targeted Mar 2027) |
!TRAP "120 MW and 3.6 lakh acres" (a Jan 2026 note) is wrong – correct: 960 MW and 7.20 lakh acres.

### 3. GWDT CLAUSES AND GODAVARI-KRISHNA DIVERSION
- Polavaram cleared with **FRL +150 ft**.
- **80 TMC** of Godavari water diverted into the **Krishna above Vijayawada** (via RMC).
- Split of the 80 TMC: **45 TMC to AP** + **35 TMC** to upper States – **Karnataka 21, Maharashtra 14**.
- **Polavaram-Nallamala Sagar link** (earlier **Banakacherla** link): **Telangana** says it violates **APRA 2014** and the **1980 GWDT award** (Jul 2025; Jan 2026).
!NUM 80 TMC = 45 (AP) + 21 (Karnataka) + 14 (Maharashtra)
### 4. NATIONAL PROJECT STATUS – APRA SECTION 90
| Sub-section | What it says |
|---|---|
| 90(1) | Polavaram Irrigation Project declared a **national project** |
| **90(2)** | **Expedient in the public interest** that the **Union takes control** of its regulation and development **for irrigation** |
| **90(3)** | **Consent of Telangana deemed to have been given** |
| **90(4)** | **Central Government executes** the project and obtains **environmental, forest and R&R clearances** |
- In practice: **GoAP executes the irrigation component on behalf of GoI** and claims reimbursement/advance.
!TRAP The section is 90 – not 30 (old key) and not 89 (some notes); clearances are the Centre's duty under 90(4), not AP's.
### 5. POLAVARAM ORDINANCE AND SUBMERGED MANDALS
- **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Ordinance, 2014** = "**Polavaram Ordinance Bill**" – kept Khammam **submergence villages and mandals with AP**.
- Base rule in the Act: Khammam → Telangana, excluding villages in **G.O.Ms. No. 111 (27 June 2005)**.
- Passed by **Lok Sabha on 11 July 2014**.
- **Seven mandals** moved from Khammam to AP:
  1. Kukunoor
  2. Velerupadu
  3. VR Puram (Vararamachandrapuram)
  4. Chintoor
  5. Kunavaram
  6. **Bhadrachalam (except the temple town)**
  7. Burgampadu
- Effect: AP's area **1,60,205 → 1,62,970 sq km**.
### 6. FUNDING AND COST
- From **1 April 2014** the **Centre funds 100% of the remaining cost of the irrigation component** – **not** drinking water or hydel.
| Item | Figure |
|---|---|
| Cost estimate | **₹55,548 crore** (older RCE) |
| Spending (Dec 2025) | ₹25,700.47 crore; GoI advance ₹5,052.71 crore |
| **Union Budget 2026-27** | **₹3,320 crore** (storage up to 41.15 m); down from ~₹5,000 crore |
| Pending from Centre | ~₹12,000 crore (CM, 26 Jan 2026) |
| AP seeks (11 Feb 2026) | **₹32,000 crore for Phase-II** + permanent lifting of **MoEF stop-work order** |
- Unresolved: clearance of the **revised cost estimate**; friction over RCE checks, land acquisition, R&R funding.

### 8. SUBMERGENCE AND R&R
#### 8.1 Other States
| State | Concern |
|---|---|
| **Odisha** | Submergence; **protective embankments**; SC case pending |
| **Chhattisgarh** | Submergence; protective embankments; SC case pending |
| **Telangana** | **Backwater at Bhadrachalam** |
- CAG (audit to Mar 2023): settle submergence issues with neighbours with PPA; build protective embankments (due by 2014-15).
- Jul 2026: ~189 villages in the submergence zone on alert during Godavari-Sabari floods.
#### 8.2 R&R
- Displaced: mainly **Koya and Kondh tribals**.
- R&R follows the **RFCTLARR Act, 2013**.
- **Dec 2025: LA and R&R only 27.40%** – main bottleneck.
- **Aug 2026: 94% land acquisition, 52% relocation**; R&R colonies targeted Dec 2026.
- **₹1,943 crore** paid in three phases (Mar 2026); house-site value **₹1 lakh → ₹2 lakh** (Cabinet, 1 May 2026).
### 9. CONSTRUCTION STATUS
| Date | Status |
|---|---|
| Dec 2025 | **Overall 60.8%**; head works 81.61%, RMC 92.79%, LMC 73.40% |
| **Aug 2026** | **Civil works 90%, overall 77%** |
- Done: spillway, radial gates, coffer dams, Gap-III concrete dam, buttress dam.
- **Diaphragm wall (1,372 m)** rebuilt from 18 Jan 2025 in **440 days** (announced 2 Apr 2026), ~₹1,000 crore extra.
- **Foreign expert panel** (6th visit, with CWC) reviewed Gap-1, Gap-2, D-wall (Jan 2026); **CWC cleared spillway safety** (Apr 2026).
- Target moved **Dec 2027 → March 2027**, before the 2027 **Godavari Pushkaralu**.
