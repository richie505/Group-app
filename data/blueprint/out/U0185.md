## U0185 | SUPPRESSION OF THE REVOLT; WHY IT FAILED; LOYAL AREAS
### 1. VICEROY AND COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF
| Officer | Role |
|---|---|
| **Lord Canning** | Governor-General (1856-58) → **first Viceroy** (1858-62); declared peace **8 July 1858** |
| **Sir Colin Campbell** (later **Lord Clyde**) | **Commander-in-Chief** after Gen. **Anson** died (1857) |
### 2. CENTRES RETAKEN
| Centre | Rebel leader | Retaken by |
|---|---|---|
| **Delhi** (Sep 1857) | Bahadur Shah II, **Bakht Khan** | **John Nicholson** (killed in the assault); **Hodson** captured the emperor |
| **Kanpur** (Dec 1857) | Nana Saheb, Tantia Tope | **Colin Campbell** (Wheeler had surrendered earlier) |
| **Lucknow** (Mar 1858) | Begum Hazrat Mahal | Relieved by **Havelock-Outram**; retaken by **Campbell** |
| **Bareilly** (May 1858) | Khan Bahadur Khan | **Campbell** |
| **Jhansi, Gwalior** (1858) | Rani Lakshmibai | **Hugh Rose** |
| Allahabad, Banaras | **Maulvi Liyakat Ali** | **Col. Neill** |
| Bihar | **Kunwar Singh** | **William Taylor** |
- Bakht Khan came from Bareilly (**3 July 1857**, APPSC-GS 2023); Azimullah Khan – Nana's adviser, linked rebel leaders.
#### 2.1 Officers Who Fell
- At **Lucknow**: **Sir Henry Lawrence** (defending the Residency), **Gen. Neill**, **Havelock** (UPPSC 2008).
- **Nicholson** fell at **Delhi**, not Lucknow.
!TRAP Delhi = Nicholson, not Campbell; Campbell = Kanpur, Lucknow, Bareilly.
### 3. FATE OF LEADERS
| Leader | End |
|---|---|
| **Bahadur Shah II** | Tried, exiled to **Rangoon**; died **1862** |
| **Nana Saheb** | Escaped to **Nepal** |
| **Begum Hazrat Mahal** | Fled to **Nepal**; son **Birjis Qadr** declared ruler |
| **Tantia Tope** | Guerrilla war after Gwalior; betrayed by **Man Singh, Raja of Narwar**; captured asleep; executed **April 1859** |
| **Rani Lakshmibai** | Widow of **Gangadhar Rao**; Jhansi lapsed **1854**; revolt from June 1857; took Gwalior; died **17 June 1858** near Gwalior vs **Hugh Rose** |
| **Kunwar Singh** | ~80-year-old zamindar; fresh revolt and death in Bihar, **April 1858** |
- **Rani Avantibai Lodhi** (Ramgarh, MP) – beat Waddington at **Kheri** near Mandla; died 1858 (remembered 1 Sep 2026).
### 4. REPRESSION
- Thousands publicly **executed after mock trials**; British atrocities.
- Delhi: emperor's **sons shot by Hodson**; **Mughal line ended**.
### 5. RULERS AND ARMIES
| Ruler / State | Stand |
|---|---|
| **Scindia** (Gwalior) | **Most loyal** (MPPSC 2010); troops joined the Rani; fled to **Agra** |
| **Holkar** (Indore) | Supported British |
| **Sikh chiefs** (Patiala, Nabha, Jind) | Actively helped |
| **Nizam Afzal-ud-Daula, Salar Jung I** | Loyal; kept Deccan quiet; Residency attack (**17 July 1857**) repulsed |
| Mysore, Rajput chiefs | Ignored Bahadur Shah's appeals |
| **Nagpur (Bhonsle)** | Already annexed (**1854**) – no ruler to join |
- Revolt chiefly of the **Bengal Army**; **Madras and Bombay armies** loyal; **Gorkhas** helped Campbell.
- Loyal princes = **"breakwaters to the storm"** – **Canning**.
### 6. CLASSES
| Group | Role |
|---|---|
| **Educated middle class** | **Neutral**; saw a backward-looking feudal reaction (UPSC 1998) |
| Big zamindars, moneylenders | "Breakwaters"; protected class interests |
| Merchants, urban elite | No support |
### 7. WHY IT FAILED
- **No all-India character** – North and Central India; Bengal, Madras, Bombay peaceful.
- No unified **ideology, programme, leadership**; Bahadur Shah old; quarrels (**Begum of Awadh vs Maulvi Ahmadullah**).
- **Swords, spears, old muskets** vs **Enfield rifles**; British **electric telegraph**.
- British power intact in East, West, South; troops back from **Crimea**; experienced generals.
- Loyal princes, traitors, non-cooperation of Sikhs/Punjabis and martial races.
!KEY BPSC 2023: military superiority, no programme, limited support – all correct.
