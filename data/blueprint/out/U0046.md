## U0046 | SCHOOLS OF ART – GANDHARA, MATHURA AND AMARAVATI COMPARED
### 1. THE THREE SCHOOLS COMPARED
| Feature | Gandhara | Mathura | Amaravati |
|---|---|---|---|
| Region | North-west (Peshawar, Taxila, Swat) | Ganga valley | Andhra |
| Patron | **Kushanas** (Kanishka) | Kushanas; began under Shungas; continued in Gupta age | **Satavahanas**, later **Ikshvakus** |
| Material | **Grey (green) schist**, later stucco | **Spotted red sandstone** | **White Palnadu limestone** |
| Themes | **Mahayana**; Buddha with Bodhisattvas | **Buddhist, Jain, Brahmanical** | **Jatakas**, life of the Buddha |
| Period | c. 1st-5th c. CE | c. 1st-3rd c. CE | c. 2nd c. BCE-3rd c. CE |
| Face / physique | Realistic, **Apollo-like**, spiritual calm | Muscular, smiling, fleshy | **Slender, dynamic, emotional** |
| Hair | **Wavy, top-knot** | Shaved or snail-shell curls | — |
| Drapery | **Heavy, toga-like** | **Transparent, clinging** | Local dress, city life |
| Halo / pose | Plain; standing in Greek attire | **Jnanachakra halo**; seated in meditation | Buddha on lotus in Padmasana |
| Composition | Single figures | Frontal, single icons | **Crowded narrative**; Yakshas, Gandharvas dance around Buddha |
!TRAP Schist = Gandhara; red sandstone = Mathura; white limestone = Amaravati.
### 2. GANDHARA SCHOOL
- Indian craftsmen + Greeks and Romans → **Greco-Buddhist / Indo-Greek / Greco-Roman** art.
- Influences: **Hellenistic Greek, Roman, Persian** (also Syrian); thrived in the **Saka-Kushan** era under **Kanishka**.
- Hellenistic realism, but linked with **Mahayana**, not Hinayana.
- **Maitreya** = future Buddha; **Bodhisattva** (one with Bodhicitta) = Mahayana concept.
### 3. MATHURA SCHOOL
- **Indigenous**, multi-religious: earliest iconic images of **Shiva, Vishnu, Surya**, **Jain Tirthankaras**, **Yaksha-Yakshi**.
- **Kushana royal portraits**: **headless standing statue of Kanishka** from **Mat**.
- **Standing Buddha** images prominent in its **Gupta phase**; Gupta stone sculpture continues Mathura with more softness (Manukwar seated Buddha).
### 4. BUDDHA IMAGE – FIRSTS AND DEBATE
- Before images: **aniconic** worship via throne, footprints, wheel, tree, stupa; **Hinayana** = symbols, **Mahayana** = images.
- First human form: **Kushana period (1st c. CE)**, in both Gandhara and Mathura; which came first is debated; Amaravati was not first.
- Exam keys: oldest Buddha sculpture = **Mathura style**; BPSC 2020 key = "**more than one** of the above".
- **First human statues worshipped** in India = the **Buddha's**.
- Standing statue of the Buddha: **Kushana period**.
- Andhra: **Chaitika Vada** (symbol worship) started by **Mahadeva**; Amaravati **Ayakapatas** show the Buddha through symbols.
### 5. MUDRAS
| Mudra | Gesture | Event |
|---|---|---|
| **Abhaya** | Raised right palm | Fearlessness; subduing elephant **Nalagiri** |
| **Dhyana** | Hands in lap | Meditation |
| **Dharmachakra** | Turning the wheel of law | **First sermon, Sarnath** (also Gandharan images) |
| **Bhumisparsha** | Right hand touching earth | **Enlightenment, Bodh Gaya**; earth witness against **Mara** |
| **Varada** | Lowered open palm | Boon-giving |
- Gupta Sarnath Buddha in Bhumisparsha = steadfastness; **Akshobhya** also has this mudra.
### 6. AMARAVATI SCHOOL IN AP
| Site | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Amaravati (Dhanyakataka)** | Buddha believed to have first taught **Kalachakra** here (Apr 2026) |
| **Nagarjunakonda** | Main centre under the **Ikshvakus** |
| **Ghantasala** | Another main centre |
| **Bhattiprolu** | **Oldest stupa in AP** |
| **Jaggayyapeta, Goli** | Other Amaravati-style sites |
- Carvings of plants, creepers and **lotus**; mature phase = Ikshvaku, Mahayana themes.
- **First depiction of Sati** in Indian art (Ikshvaku phase); only art showing **all events of the Buddha's life**.
- **Purna-Kumbha** = **AP State emblem**; sculptors = **Selavardhakulu**.
- Influence: **Sri Lanka (Anuradhapura)**, South-East Asia, Gupta art.
- **James Fergusson**: "**Tree and Serpent Worship**"; "Epitome of Indian architecture". **Percy Brown**: pinnacle, "**dynamic beauty**".
- Sculptures split between Amaravati Museum and **British Museum** (May 2026); Buddhist circuit Amaravati, Bavikonda, Thotlakonda (Feb 2026).
