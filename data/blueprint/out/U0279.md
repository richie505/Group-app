### 5. SCHEDULE NUMBER GAME
#### 5.1 Seventh Schedule – Three Lists
| List | Originally | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Union | **97** | **100** |
| State | **66** | **61** |
| Concurrent | **47** | **52** |
- Changes came through transfers such as the **42nd Amendment** (items moved from the State List).
- Strictly counted after the **101st Amendment**, the Union List has **98** and the State List **59** entries; the standard exam figures are 100 and 61.
#### 5.2 Ninth Schedule
- Linked with **Article 31B**; first **13 Acts** (1951) → about **284** Acts now.
- Laws added **after 24 Apr 1973** are open to **judicial review** (SC, 2007).
#### 5.3 Tenth Schedule – Anti-defection
- Came with the **52nd Amendment (1985)**; the **91st Amendment (2003)** changed it, removing the **'split' exception**.

- "**An indestructible Union of destructible States**".
### 9. SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR STATES – ARTICLES 371 TO 371J
#### 9.1 Full List (Part XXI)
| Article | State | Special provision |
|---|---|---|
| **371** | **Maharashtra, Gujarat** | Development boards – **Vidarbha, Marathwada, Saurashtra, Kutch** |
| **371A** | **Nagaland** | Shields Naga customs, religious/social practices, customary justice, **land ownership and transfer** from Parliament's laws unless the Assembly agrees |
| **371B** | **Assam** | Assembly committee of members from **tribal areas** |
| **371C** | **Manipur** | Assembly committee of members from the **Hill areas** |
| **371D** | **Andhra Pradesh (and Telangana)** | **Equitable opportunities in public employment and education** for regions |
| **371E** | **Andhra Pradesh** | **Central University** by parliamentary law |
| **371F** | **Sikkim** | Incorporation, assembly size, governance |
| **371G** | **Mizoram** | Mizo religious/social practices, customary law, land ownership |
| **371H** | **Arunachal Pradesh** | Governor's special responsibility for **law and order** |
| **371I** | **Goa** | Assembly at least **30** members (usual minimum **60**) |
| **371J** | **Karnataka** | **Hyderabad–Karnataka** region (six backward districts) – development board, reservation in education and jobs; **98th Amendment, 2012** |
#### 9.2 AP Angle
- **Arts. 371D and 371E**: inserted by the **32nd Amendment, 1973** to implement the **Six-Point Formula** after the **Jai Andhra and Telangana agitations** (APPSC G2 2019).
- APPSC key named the **98th Amendment Act** in a question linked to reorganising Andhra Pradesh into AP and Telangana.
!PAIR 371D | AP | Regional equity in jobs and education || 371E | AP | Central University
