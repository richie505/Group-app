## U0542 | CENTRE-STATE FINANCIAL RELATIONS – ARTICLES 268-293; DIVISIBLE POOL, CESSES AND SURCHARGES
### 1. LEVY, COLLECTION AND APPROPRIATION
| Article | Levied by | Collected / kept by |
|---|---|---|
| 268 | Union (stamp duties, excise on medicinal and toilet preparations) | Collected and kept by **States** |
| 269 | Union (inter-State sale/consignment of goods) | Collected by Union, **assigned to States** |
| 269A | Union (**GST on inter-State trade**) | Shared as Parliament provides on GST Council advice |
| 270 | Union (all other Union List taxes) | **Shared** with States as the **Finance Commission** recommends |
| 271 | Union (**surcharge**) | **Wholly for the Union** |
- **Corporation tax** (Union List Entry 85) and **customs** = Union taxes in the shared pool.
- **Agricultural income tax** (State List Entry 46), **State excise**, **entertainment tax** = State taxes.

- **Art. 293(3)**: a State needs **Centre's consent** for fresh loans if any Union loan or guarantee is **outstanding**; Centre may attach **conditions** (293(4)).
- Central corporations are **not immune** from State taxes.
### 3. ARTICLE 275 vs ARTICLE 282 GRANTS
| Feature | Art. 275 | Art. 282 |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Statutory | Discretionary |
| Recommended by | **Finance Commission** | Executive (formerly Planning Commission) |
| Charged on | Consolidated Fund of India | Voted |
| For | States in need; **ST welfare, Scheduled Areas** | Any public purpose; CSS |
- Plan grants ended with the Planning Commission (**2015**).
### 4. DIVISIBLE POOL, CESSES AND SURCHARGES
- **Divisible pool** = Art. 270 taxes; **excludes cesses and surcharges** → not shared.
- Per ₹100 of Central tax: **₹13.5 (2021-22, decade high)** → **₹11 (2025-26 est.)**.
- Headline devolution **41%**; effective only **30-32%** of gross tax revenue ("41% illusion"; ~34% of total tax receipts).
- **16th FC**: no cap on cesses; **"grand bargain"** (para 7.67): States take a smaller share of a larger pool if most cesses merge into regular taxes.
### 5. VERTICAL FISCAL IMBALANCE
- Cause: Union holds buoyant bases (**corporation tax, income tax, most of GST**) while States carry heavy spending.
- Worsened by cesses/surcharges, loss of indirect taxes to GST, **CSS conditions**, weak State revenue.
- Transfers 2026-27: **₹25.44 lakh crore**; ~42% of the rise = CSS (Feb 2026).
