## U0049 | SATAVAHANA ART AND ARCHITECTURE – AMARAVATI, NAGARJUNAKONDA AND ROCK-CUT CAVES
### 1. STUPA, CHAITYA AND VIHARA – BASIC TERMS
- **Stupa** = mound; usually a **repository of relics**; a votive and commemorative structure in Buddhism.
- Stupa tradition **predates Buddhism** (not Buddhist in origin); the Vedic funeral practice **pitramedha** collected ashes.
- **Pradakshina patha** = circular path around the stupa; **Garbhagriha** = where a deity's image is installed (temple term).
- Satavahana stupa, chaitya and vihara forms became the **base of later Hindu temple architecture**; the **first structural Hindu temples** in Andhra came under the **Ikshvakus**.
#### 1.1 Structure Types
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Chaitya** (Chaitya Shala / Chaitya Griha) | Prayer hall / place of worship and assembly; rectangular hall ending in an **apse** (horseshoe plan), pillars, barrel-vaulted roof, stupa at the far end |
| Chaitya window | **Horseshoe-shaped** opening on the facade |
| **Vihara** | Monastery; monks' shelter in the **rainy season**; cells around a central hall (**Madhyashala**) |
| Uposatha | Monks' meeting in the vihara hall **every 15 days** |
| **Arama** | Complex of stupa + vihara + chaitya + school together |
#### 1.2 Stupa Types
| Type | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Dhatugarbha** (saririka) | Bodily relics | Nagarjunakonda, Amaravati, Jaggayyapeta, Salihundam, Bhattiprolu |
| **Uddeshika** | Devotional / memorial, no relics | – |
| **Paribhogika** | Objects or texts used by the Buddha or monks | – |
### 2. ANDHRA STUPA FEATURES – AYAKA PLATFORM
- **4 ayaka platforms** project at the 4 cardinal points; each carries **5 ayaka pillars** = the **Pancha Kalyanakas** (five great events of Buddha's life).
- The ayaka platform with ayaka pillars is **unique to Andhra**: Amaravati, Nagarjunakonda, Ghantasala, Bhattiprolu, Jaggayyapeta – **not** the western Deccan caves.
- "Platform with Ayaka (Aryak) pillars" = **Amaravati stupa** (UPPSC 2022).
- Other ayaka symbols: **Purnakumbha**, Vinayaka.
- Nagarjunakonda ayaka inscription: "**Samma Sambhudha Dhatu Parigahita**".
#### 2.1 Buddha's Life → Symbol
| Event | Symbol |
|---|---|
| Birth | **Lotus and bull** |
| Great Departure (Renunciation) | **Horse** |
| Enlightenment | **Bodhi (peepal) tree** |
| First sermon (Mrigadava / Sarnath) | **Wheel** (wheel with deer) |
| Mahaparinirvana | **Stupa** |
| Meditation (deep concentration under Bodhi tree) | **Empty seat** |
!TRAP 4 platforms × 5 pillars; Horse = Great Departure; Wheel = first sermon; Stupa = Mahaparinirvana.
### 3. AMARAVATI MAHACHAITYA
- Ancient **Dhanyakataka** (Amaravati-Dharanikota), on the **Krishna**, Guntur/Palnadu region; Satavahana capital and today's AP capital.
- A **Dhatugarbha** stupa built over the **Buddha's relics**; **biggest stupa in AP** (G2 2019); Satavahana queens built **chaityas for Buddhist monks** (the kings were Brahmanical).
#### 3.1 Phases and Patrons
| Phase | Patron / act |
|---|---|
| Origin (Ashoka's age) | **Mahadeva Bhikshu** (sent by Ashoka; first Buddhist teacher in Andhra) enshrined Buddha's bone powder in a copper urn; Naga king **Nagashoka** helped build it at Dharanikota |
| Enlargement | **Vasishthiputra Satakarni** – limestone sculptures + stone railing (**vedika**) (APPSC 2001) |
| Inscription | **Pulumavi II** (on a gateway pillar); also "Nagabu"; stupa of the Chaitika Sangha |
| Prakara (enclosing wall) | **Acharya Nagarjuna** |
| Final enlargement | **Ikshvakus** |
- Artisan **Siddhattha** gifted five ayaka pillars at the eastern gate of the Mahachaitya.
#### 3.2 Discovery and Dispersal
| Person | Work | Year |
|---|---|---|
| **Col. Colin Mackenzie** (first Surveyor General of India) | Identified Amaravati; relics to London and Madras | **1797** |
| **Robert Sewell** | Excavated Amaravati | **1877** |
| **James Fergusson** | *Tree and Serpent Worship*; called it the "**Epitome of Indian architecture**" | – |
- Sculptures today: **British Museum, London**; **Amaravati Museum**; Government Museum, **Chennai**.
- **Amaravati Buddhist Stupa** is on India's **UNESCO tentative list**; Lepakshi (Veerabhadra Temple, monolithic Nandi) was added in 2022.
!TRAP Amaravati "next only to Sanchi in size" was marked incorrect (UPSC 2026 key). Railing = Vasishthiputra Satakarni; prakara = Nagarjuna; final = Ikshvakus.
### 4. NAGARJUNAKONDA
- Ancient names **Vijayapuri** / **Sriparvata**; capital of the **Ikshvakus**; guardian (tutelary) deity **Hariti** (APPSC 2012).
- **Kielhorn** identified "**Sri Parvataswamy**" with the Buddha (APPSC 2019).
- More than **30 stupas**, viharas and apsidal chaityas; **Ikshvaku queens** were major donors; a **Sinhalese vihara** for Sri Lankan monks.
- **Yajna Sri Satakarni** built the **1,500-room Sriparvata Sangharama** for **Acharya Nagarjuna**; he is also a donor at **Chinaganjam** (Prakasam).
- **Golden Temple (Swarna Devalayam)** of **Ehuvala Shantamula** bears the **first Sanskrit inscription** of Andhra/South India; family deities of Chamtamula: **Virupakshapati** (Shiva), **Mahasena** (Kartikeya).
- Buddhism peaked under **Veerapurushadatta** (Ikshvaku records at Nagarjunakonda and Jaggayyapeta).
#### 4.1 Excavation and Submergence
| Event | Fact |
|---|---|
| Discovered | **A.R. Saraswati, 1926** |
| Excavated | **A.H. Longhurst, 1927–31** |
| Submerged | **Nagarjunasagar dam** reservoir drowned Vijayapuri |
| Today | Monuments rebuilt on the hilltop → **island museum** in the reservoir |
- Sects at the site: Bahushrutiya, **Mahishasaka** (only Hinayana sect), Aparasaila; **Rajagirika** centre = **Sriparvatam** (APPSC 2012 key).
### 5. OTHER BUDDHIST SITES OF ANDHRA → DISTRICT AND FEATURE
| Site | District / river | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Bhattiprolu** | Old Guntur dist. (Krishna delta) | **Oldest stupa in Andhra**; gold relic casket; Brahmi casket inscriptions (3rd c. BC) name **Raja Kubiraka** and the **Nigama-sabha**; excavated by **Alexander Rea, 1892** |
| **Jaggayyapeta** | Old Krishna dist. | Relic stupa (2nd c. BCE); **Mandhata** sculpture; excavated by **James Burgess, 1882** |
| **Ghantasala** | Krishna | Stupa at ancient port **Kantakossyla / Kantakasala** |
| **Guntupalli** | Eluru | Only rock-cut Buddhist cave site in Andhra; "**house viharas**" |
| **Sankaram (Bojjannakonda, Lingalakonda)** | Anakapalli | Rock-cut caves on twin hillocks; **horseshoe-shaped** caves; name from **Sangharama** |
| **Thotlakonda, Bavikonda** | Visakhapatnam | Hilltop monasteries; Bavikonda from 3rd c. BCE |
| **Salihundam** | Srikakulam (Gara mandal) | Relic stupa; flourished 2nd–3rd c. CE |
| **Nelakondapalli** | Khammam (Telangana) | Post-Satavahana mahastupa; bronze and marble Buddhas; **sculptor's workshop** (V.V. Krishna Sastry) |
- **Bhairavakonda** (Nellore–Prakasam border): **8 rock-cut cave temples of Shiva** (7th–8th c., Pallava style) – Hindu, not Buddhist.
- Records: oldest in Andhra = **Bhattiprolu**; most ancient in India = **Bharhut**; largest in India = **Sanchi**; largest brick = **Kesariya**.
- Relic finds outside Andhra: **Piprahwa** casket with Brahmi "**Sakiyanam**"; **Dharmarajika, Taxila** bone-relic casket; **Devnimori** (Gujarat) monastery and stupa; **Kumrahar** Mauryan pillared hall.
### 6. RECENT EXCAVATIONS AND HERITAGE CIRCUIT
- **Thotlakonda**: spotted in an **Indian Navy aerial survey**; **1988–92** digs found the main stupa, votive stupas, **Satavahana and Roman coins**.
- **Phanigiri** (Suryapet, Telangana): Mahastupa, viharas, apsidal chaityagrihas; a **life-size stucco** sculpture; **2024** – pot of **3,730 lead coins** of the **Ikshvaku** period.
- **AP Buddhist International Tourism Circuit** (Socio-Economic Survey 2025-26): **Amaravati, Thotlakonda-Bavikonda, Bojjannakonda-Sankaram, Salihundam, Ghantasala** – for visitors from Japan, Korea, Sri Lanka, Thailand, Myanmar.

### 9. DONORS IN INSCRIPTIONS
- Main donors: **women**, especially queens – Satavahana queens (**Gautami Balasri's** vihara), Ikshvaku queens.

### 10. ROCK-CUT CAVES OF THE SATAVAHANA PERIOD
- Peak region: the **Sahyadris** (western Deccan) – Nasik, Naneghat, Karle, Bhaja, Kanheri, Junnar, Ajanta, Bedsa, Pitalkhora. Eastern Deccan chaityas and viharas are usually **structural**; Guntupalli is the rock-cut exception.
| Site | Type / feature |
|---|---|
| **Karle** (Pune) | **Largest, most evolved Hinayana chaitya hall**; **12 dance-couple** sculptures; funded by Bhutapala, Chulaghosha; **Pulumavi II** donated a village |
| **Bhaja** | Early caves – **earlier than Karle** |
| **Nasik** | **Viharas with Satavahana inscriptions**; chaitya funded by Vishnudatta (3,500 karshapanas) |
| **Kanheri, Junnar** | Large monastery complexes; chaityas |
| **Pitalkhora** (Aurangabad) | Early chaityas and viharas |
| **Bedsa** (Pune) | Chaitya with **bell-capital pillars** |
| **Ajanta Caves 9, 10** | Satavahana chaityas; Cave 10 = **Shwetagaja (Chhadanta) Jataka** painting (G2 2012) |
| **Bagh** (MP) | Cave viharas linked to the Satavahanas |
| **Guntupalli** (Eluru; earlier West Godavari) | Rock-cut chaityas and viharas; its chaitya = most ancient in Andhra |
!TRAP Some notes call Karle the most ancient chaitya; Bhaja is earlier. The Shwetagaja painting is at Ajanta, not Guntupalli.
### 11. AMARAVATI SCHOOL OF ART
- Region: lower **Krishna-Godavari** valley; period **2nd c. BCE–3rd c. CE** under the Satavahanas; **most mature phase under the Ikshvakus**.
- Material: **white Palnadu limestone** (marble-like); Ikshvaku phase also green stone.
- Style: **movement and emotion**; narrative reliefs of the **Jatakas** and the Buddha's life (Dharmachakra Parivartana, Buddha at Gaya, **3 group-dance scenes**, **Dimikudu offering a Purnakumbha**). The subject does not explain the style.
- **Early phase** aniconic: Bodhi tree, empty throne, footprints, wheel, stupa. **Later phase** Buddha in human form, seated on a lotus with dancing Gandharvas and Yakshas.
- Influence spread to **Sri Lanka and Southeast Asia**; one of India's three major ancient art schools.
- Ivory: **ivory carvers of Vidisha** carved the **Sanchi gateway**.
| Feature | Gandhara | Mathura | Amaravati |
|---|---|---|---|
| Patrons | Kushanas | Shungas/Kushanas | Satavahanas, Ikshvakus |
| Material | **Grey-blue schist**, later stucco | **Spotted red sandstone** | **White limestone** |
| Influence | Greco-Roman | Indigenous | Indigenous |
| Sites | Taxila, Hadda | Mathura | Amaravati, Nagarjunakonda |
