## U0002 | SOURCES FOR THE HISTORY OF ANDHRA – INSCRIPTIONS, COINS, LITERATURE AND FOREIGN ACCOUNTS
### 1. SOURCES – OVERVIEW
- Satavahana history rests on the **Puranas**, **Prakrit inscriptions** and **coins**; foreign accounts must be cross-checked against these.
- All Satavahana inscriptions: **Prakrit language, Brahmi script**.
### 2. LITERARY SOURCES
| Text | Content revealed |
|---|---|
| **Matsya Purana** | **30 Satavahana kings** ruled for **456 years** |
| Bhimasena / **Chulla-Kalinga Jatakas** | Andhra region; Kharavela defeated by **Asmaka king Satakarni** |
| **Vinayavatthu** | "**Andhapatha**" |
| **Gathasaptasati** (King **Hala**) | Prakrit, **700 verses**; society; early Telugu words "**atta, addam, potta**" |
| **Kashikhandam** and **Haravilasam** | Both by **Srinatha**; **sea trade of the Reddi period** |
!TRAP Reddi sea-trade question has two keys: Kashikhandam (G2 2011) and Haravilasam (G2 2019). Both are by Srinatha.
### 3. SATAVAHANA-ERA INSCRIPTIONS
| Inscription | Dynasty / issuer | What it records |
|---|---|---|
| **Erragudi** (Kurnool) | **Ashoka** | Prakrit/Brahmi; teachers to inculcate **Dhamma** in pupils |
| **Bhattiprolu relic caskets** (3rd c. BC) | Pre-Satavahana local king **Kubhiraka** (Kuberaka) | **Nigama-sabha** (town council); among the oldest Prakrit inscriptions of Andhra |
| **Naneghat** | **Queen Naganika**, wife of **Satakarni I** | Prakrit; **Vedic sacrifices**; gifts of cows, land, gold to Brahmins |
| **Nasik** | **Gautami Balasri**, under **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi** | Prakrit prose; victories and titles of **Gautamiputra Satakarni** |
| **Amaravati** | **Pulumavi II** | **First Satavahana inscription in Andhra**; word "**Nagabu**" |
| **Malkapuram** (1261 CE) | Kakatiya, **Rudramadevi's** reign | Musical instrument "**Jalakaranda**" |
- **Bhattiprolu** = **oldest stupa** in AP.
### 4. IKSHVAKU, PALLAVA, BRIHATPALAYANA AND SALANKAYANA INSCRIPTIONS
#### 4.1 Issuer and Record
| Inscription | Issuer | Records |
|---|---|---|
| **Nagarjunakonda** | Ikshvaku (**Veerapurushadatta**) | **Upasika Bodhisri**; Buddhism patronage; Prakrit; **Santamula** founded the kingdom at **Vijayapuri** |
| **Jaggayyapeta** | Ikshvaku, Virapurushadatta's reign | Buddhist donations |
| **Alluru** | Ikshvaku period | **Multiple gifts** to different communities and institutions; Ikshvakus were Satavahana feudatories |
| **Chejerla** | Kandararaju | **Ikshvaku – Anandagotrika** wars |
| **Manchikallu** | **Pallava Simhavarman**, near Vijayapuri | Conquest of Vijayapuri; proves the **Pallavas replaced the Ikshvakus** |
| **Mayidavolu** (c. AD 300) | **Pallava Sivaskandavarman** | Prakrit copper-plate grant; **first inscriptional use of "Andhrapatha"** |
| **Kondamudi** (copper) | **Jayavarma**, Brihatpalayana, 10th regnal year | **Only record of the Brihatpalayanas** (Ikshvaku feudatories); capital **Koduru**; gave a village to Brahmins |
| **Pedavegi** | **Nandi Varma II** (Salankayana) | Land grant to Vishnu; **first to mention Vishnu temples** in Andhra |
| **Kolleru** | **Nandi Varma II** | – |
| **Eluru** | **Devavarma** | "**Munyada**" = political employee |
| Pithapuram | – | Mentions **Kolleru lake** |
| **Kalamalla** | Erikal Mutthuraju **Dhananjaya Varma** | **First inscription in Telugu** (not Sanskrit) |
| **Allahabad Pillar** | **Samudragupta** | Names **Hastivarman of Vengi** (Salankayana) among southern kings defeated |
- **Kondamudi**: script/language Prakrit; seal and subject Sanskrit.
!TRAP G2 2025: **Manchikallu + Mayidavolu = Pallavas**, not Ikshvakus.
!TRAP G2 2017: Alluru = multiple gifts; Karle, Veerapuram and Ramagiri were the distractors.
#### 4.2 Fall of the Ikshvakus
1. Last Ikshvaku ruler **Rudrapurushadatta** (son of Ehuvala Chamtamula); his Nagarjunakonda pillar (11th year) honours his mother **Vammabhatta**; the **Phanigiri** pillar is of his 18th year.
2. Defeated by **Pallava Simhavarman** (Manchikallu).
3. **South of the Krishna → Pallavas**; north → Brihatpalayanas.
### 5. COPPER-PLATE GRANTS
- **Donor** = king; **donee** = Brahmins; gift of a **village/land**, often with **tax exemptions**.
- **Vishnukundin** grants measured land in **nivartanas**.
- Examples: Mayidavolu (Pallava), Kondamudi (Brihatpalayana).
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| nivartana | Land measure (Vishnukundin grants) |
| Nigama-sabha | Town council (Bhattiprolu) |
| Munyada | Political employee (Eluru) |
| Jalakaranda | Musical instrument (Malkapuram) |
### 6. COINS AS A SOURCE
- Satavahana coins issued in **lead, copper, bronze, potin** and silver; **ship-motif** coins of **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi** and **Yajna Sri** prove **maritime trade**.
- Roman coins show **Andhra–Rome trade**.

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- First Satavahana inscription in Andhra: Amaravati (Pulumavi II). First Telugu inscription: Kalamalla.
- First "Andhrapatha" in an inscription: Mayidavolu. First Vishnu temples: Pedavegi.
- Only Brihatpalayana record: Kondamudi. Oldest stupa in AP: Bhattiprolu. Last Ikshvaku: Rudrapurushadatta.

### 12. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. **Mayidavolu** and **Manchikallu** are **Pallava** records.
2. Rudrapurushadatta was defeated by **Simhavarman**, not by the Vishnukundins.
3. **Kondamudi** is the **only** Brihatpalayana record; capital **Koduru**.
4. **Pedavegi**, not Kolleru, first mentions Vishnu temples; both by Nandi Varma II.
5. **Kalamalla** is in **Telugu**, not Sanskrit.
6. **Nasik** was issued under **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi**, but records **Gautamiputra's** deeds.
7. **Amaravati (Pulumavi II)** = first Satavahana record in Andhra.
8. Roman **gold** hoards: **Attirala and Vinukonda**.
9. **Dharanikota** = habitation mound; Kondapur = mint.
