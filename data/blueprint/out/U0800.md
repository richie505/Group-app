## U0800 | PRECISION AGRICULTURE, DRIP IRRIGATION AND OTHER RESOURCE-EFFICIENT TECHNOLOGIES
### 1. MICRO-IRRIGATION – DRIP VS SPRINKLER
- **Evergreen Revolution**: "more output at **lower ecological cost**" – **M.S. Swaminathan**.
- **Localised irrigation**: low-pressure piped water given in small amounts near each plant – drip, micro-sprinkler, bubbler.
| Method | Efficiency | How water is applied |
|---|---|---|
| **Drip** | **~90% (highest)** | To the **root zone** through **emitters** on lateral pipes |
| **Sprinkler** | **70-80%** | Pressurised water through nozzles, like **simulated rainfall** |
| Flood / furrow | 30-50% | Surface methods |
- Drip: most efficient, cuts evaporation and runoff; limit = **higher initial cost**.
- Sprinkler: suits uneven terrain; loses more to **wind and evaporation** (water travels through the air).
- **Drip irrigation = best for water-scarce regions** (APPSC G2 2025); micro-irrigation **conserves water** in agriculture.
- Savings of water, labour, power and fertiliser.
#### 1.1 Fertigation
- **Fertiliser applied through irrigation water** (through the drip line).
- Advantages (UPSC 2020): **controls alkalinity** of water, **more nutrients available**, **less leaching**.
!TRAP Efficient application of rock phosphate is NOT an advantage of fertigation.

- Implemented **State-wide under PMKSY**; **AP ranked 1st in India** in micro-irrigation implementation (**2024-25**); led by **Ananthapuramu**.
- APMIP reports **30-40% higher productivity** (2025-26).

- **7 Jan 2026**: **automation-based micro-irrigation** (sensors + data analytics) – Agriculture Minister **K. Atchannaidu**; facility at the Integrated Water Resources Management and Farmer Training Centre, **Governorpet, Vijayawada**.
- **APSES 2024-25**: large-scale drip, sprinkler and rainwater harvesting; **precision farming, drones, IoT** as focus areas.
### 4. RESOURCE-CONSERVING TECHNOLOGIES
| Technology | Benefit |
|---|---|
| **Zero tillage** | Wheat sown **without burning residue**; paddy sown **directly without nursery**; **soil carbon sequestration** (UPSC 2020: all three) |
| Zero tillage + residue on field | **Conserves water** (UPSC 2017); gypsum before irrigation does NOT |
| **SRI** (alternate wetting and drying) | **Less seed, less methane, less electricity** (UPSC 2022: all three) |
| **Direct-seeded rice** | No nursery or transplanting; promoted with zero-till wheat under **NICRA** |
| **Laser land levelling** | Uniform field surface so water spreads evenly; saves water |
| **Sustainable Sugarcane Initiative** | **Single-bud chips** instead of setts (very low seed cost); wider spacing; **drip** used |
- Zero tillage does not need full residue removal.
- **Onion** is grown by **transplanting** nursery seedlings.
- 26 Jul 2026 study: better irrigation, fertiliser, residue management and reduced tillage → **~10% cut** in global rice GHG by mid-century.
### 5. PROTECTED CULTIVATION AND SOILLESS FARMING
| Technique | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Greenhouse / polyhouse**, polytunnel, shade net | Controlled environment; protection from weather, pests, diseases; year-round crops |
| **Hydroponics** | Without soil; roots in **nutrient-rich water / mineral solutions** |
| **Aeroponics** | Roots **suspended in air and misted** |
| **Aquaponics** | **Fish culture + hydroponics**; fish waste feeds plants (tilapia, catfish) |
| **Vertical farming** | **Stacked indoor layers**; year-round output even in non-tropical or harsh places (APPSC GS 2021) |
| **Phytotron** | Enclosed **research greenhouse** for controlled conditions |
!PAIR Soilless farming: Hydroponics | Water | Nutrient solution || Aeroponics | Air | Misted roots

### 7. DRONES IN AGRICULTURE
- **Kisan drones**: crop assessment, soil health analysis, spraying; less human contact with chemicals; save water up to **30%**; do NOT remove the need for irrigation.
- Regulated by **DGCA** under the **Drone Rules, 2021**.
- **Drone Shakti**: drone ecosystem via manufacturing and **Remote Pilot Certificate** training.
- **Namo Drone Didi**: drones to **women SHGs** (target **15,000**); outlay **Rs 1,261 crore**; **80%** central assistance (max Rs 8 lakh per drone); SHGs rent drones for spraying fertilisers, pesticides, **nano urea/DAP**.
- **Drone Corporation of AP (APDC)**: State-owned; **drone-as-a-service**; spraying extended to **all 26 districts** (Jan 2026).
