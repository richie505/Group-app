## U0527 | MONETARY POLICY COMMITTEE AND THE FLEXIBLE INFLATION TARGETING FRAMEWORK
### 1. OBJECTIVES AND AUTHORITY
- **Primary objective: price stability**, "keeping in mind" growth (RBI Act); financial stability also recognised.
- Monetary policy is made by the **RBI** (MPC fixes repo); the **inflation target** is fixed by the **Government of India in consultation with RBI**.
- **Inflation targeting** = public announcement of a **medium-term numerical** inflation target.
!KEY Fiscal policy = Government (Finance Ministry, Parliament), direct effect, slow (legislative lags); monetary policy = RBI/MPC, indirect, quick. The two are **complementary** against inflation: supply-driven food inflation needs fiscal steps (buffer stocks, duty cuts).
### 2. PHASES AND COMMITTEES
#### 2.1 Phases
| Phase | Approach |
|---|---|
| 1985–1998 | **Monetary targeting** with feedback (Chakravarty) |
| 1998–2014 | **Multiple-indicator approach** (inflation, growth, exchange rate, credit) – criticised as **opaque** |
| 2014–2016 | Glide path: Urjit Patel Committee, 2015 Agreement |
| 2016 onwards | **Flexible inflation targeting (FIT)** with the MPC |
- Challenges after 1991: **exchange-rate volatility** (APPSC 2023 key), open capital account, external shocks, fiscal dominance.
- Phases traced in the "**Seven Ages of India's Monetary Policy**" speech (Shaktikanta Das, 24 Jan 2020).
#### 2.2 Committees
| Committee | Year | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Sukhamoy Chakravarty** | **1985** | Monetary targeting with feedback; organised money market |
| **N. Vaghul** | **1987** | Money-market blueprint → **Commercial Paper (1990)** |
| **S. S. Tarapore** | **1997**; 2nd 2006 | Capital account convertibility |
| **Urjit Patel** (Expert Committee to Revise and Strengthen the Monetary Policy Framework, set up by RBI) | **2014** | Drop multiple-indicator approach; **CPI-C as nominal anchor**; **4% ±2%**; an MPC to set rates |
!NUM 1985 Chakravarty – 1987 Vaghul – 1997 Tarapore – 2014 Urjit Patel
### 3. AGREEMENT AND ACT
| Instrument | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Monetary Policy Framework Agreement** | Signed by **Government and RBI**, **20 Feb 2015**; **not a statute**; formally adopted FIT; aim CPI below 6% by January 2016; Government sets the target, RBI achieves it and is answerable on missing it |
| **RBI (Amendment) Act, 2016** (May 2016) | Inserted **Sections 45Z to 45ZO** into the RBI Act, 1934; statutory backing for FIT and MPC (APPSC GS 2022) |
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| **45ZA** | **Centre, with RBI**, sets the target **once every five years**; notified in the **Official Gazette** |
| **45ZB** | **MPC** constituted (statutory) |
| **45ZN** | **Failure**: average CPI **above 6% or below 2%** for **three consecutive quarters**; RBI reports to the Centre the **reasons, remedial action and time** to return to target |
!TRAP Target section = **45ZA**, not 45ZB. Failure = **quarters**, not months. First invoked **2022** (CPI above 6% from January 2022).
### 4. INFLATION TARGET
- **4% CPI-Combined**, tolerance **±2 percentage points** (band **2–6%**).
| Notified | Period | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **August 2016** | to 31 March 2021 | 4% (APPSC G2 2017) |
| March 2021 | April 2021 – March 2026 | Retained |
| **25 March 2026** | **1 April 2026 – 31 March 2031** | Retained (second time) |
- **Anchor**: headline **CPI-C**, compiled by **NSO (MoSPI)**; new series **base 2024**, released **12 Feb 2026** (earlier base 2012).
- New-series inflation: 2.75% (Jan 2026), 3.4% (Mar 2026).
### 5. MPC – COMPOSITION AND PROCEDURE
| Member | How |
|---|---|
| **RBI Governor** | **Chairperson**; **casting vote** on a tie |
| Deputy Governor (monetary policy) | Ex officio |
| One RBI officer | Nominated by **RBI Central Board** |
| **Three external members** | Appointed by the **Central Government**; **4-year** term; **no reappointment** |
- **6 members** = 3 RBI + 3 external.
- Meets **at least 4 times** a year by law; **six** (bi-monthly) in practice; first met **October 2016**.
- **Quorum 4**, incl. the Governor or, in his absence, the Deputy Governor.
- MPC **fixes the policy repo rate**; decisions **binding on RBI** (APPSC G2 2017; APPSC GS 2019).
!NUM 6 members – quorum 4 – at least 4 meetings – 4-year term
### 6. STANCES AND DECISION RECORD
| Stance | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Accommodative** | Ready to cut rates or add liquidity for growth |
| **Neutral** | Rates can move either way, data-dependent |
| **Withdrawal of accommodation** | Tightening bias; drains excess liquidity |
| Period | Repo | Stance |
|---|---|---|
| May 2022 – Feb 2023 | **4% → 6.5%**; held till early 2025 | Tightening |
| Apr 2025 | Cut 25 bps to **6.0%** | Neutral → **accommodative** |
| Jun 2025 | Cut to 5.50%; CRR cut 100 bps | Back to **neutral** |
| Dec 2025 | Cut 25 bps to **5.25%** (FY26 easing 100 bps; 125 bps since Feb 2025) | Neutral |
| Feb, Jun, **5 Aug 2026** | **Held at 5.25%, unanimous** | Neutral |
- August 2026 minutes point to a likely **hike**; Governor Malhotra: hardening inflation "may suggest a recalibration"; headline inflation averaged 3.93%; core projected 4.3% (2026-27).
### 7. GLOBAL TIGHTENING AND DEBATES
- **June 2026**: major central banks gearing up to tighten; MPC held 5.25% and cut FY27 growth forecast.
- Spillover channels: **capital outflows, rupee pressure, imported inflation**.
- Debates: decade of FIT gives mixed evidence; critics want **core** instead of **headline** target, since **food and supply shocks** sit outside MPC control.
- **AP angle**: AP retail inflation **4.9%** vs **3.9%** national (May 2026); above national for five months; rural 5.09% > urban 4.61%; on the State's "Needs Attention" watchlist (Jul 2026).

### 10. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. Target set by **Government with RBI**, not by MPC.
2. **45ZA** = target; **45ZB** = MPC; **45ZN** = failure.
3. Failure = **three consecutive quarters**.
4. External members appointed by **Central Government**, not RBI Board.
5. MPFA (2015) is an agreement, **not a statute**; Act = **2016**.
6. Governor has a **casting vote**, not a veto.
7. Law says **at least 4** meetings; six is practice.
8. Anchor = **CPI-C** (base 2024), not WPI.
