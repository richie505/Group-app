!KEY Newest members: ASEAN = **Timor-Leste**; EU = **Croatia**; NATO = **Finland + Sweden**; SCO = **Belarus**.
### 3. SAARC AND BBIN
#### 3.1 SAARC Basics
- Charter signed in **Dhaka, December 1985**.
- **Seven founders:** India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Maldives, Bhutan.
- **Afghanistan** joined at the **14th Summit, April 2007** (8th member).
- **31st SAARC Council of Ministers** (Feb 2009) – **Sri Lanka**.
- **SAARC Development Fund** – **Thimphu**.
#### 3.2 SAFTA and Stalled Summits
- **SAFTA:** signed **2004**, in force **1 Jan 2006**; replaced **SAPTA**; zero duty on eligible goods meeting Rules of Origin (DRI busted an areca-nut origin fraud, Aug 2026).
- Last summit: **18th, Kathmandu, 2014**. **19th Summit (Islamabad, 2016)** cancelled after the **Uri attack**.
- India's SAARC proposals: **South Asia Satellite (2017)**, **COVID emergency fund (2020)**.
- Jun 2026: SAARC and BIMSTEC both constrained by India's tensions with Islamabad and Dhaka.
#### 3.3 BBIN Motor Vehicles Agreement
- Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Nepal; signed at **Thimphu, 2015**.
- **Bhutan has not ratified** it.
### 4. BIMSTEC
#### 4.1 Name and Growth
- Full form: **Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation**.
- Began 1997 as **BIST-EC** (Bangladesh, India, Sri Lanka, Thailand); Myanmar joined 1997; **Nepal and Bhutan joined 2004**.
- Sectors **cut from 14 to 7** (not expanded); each has a lead country. India leads **security** (counter-terrorism, disaster management, energy).
#### 4.2 Summits
| Summit | Venue / date | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | **Bangkok, 31 July 2004** | – |
| 5th | **30 March 2022, virtual, hosted by Sri Lanka** (Colombo); addressed by PM Modi | **BIMSTEC Charter** adopted; Sri Lanka chair |
| 6th | **Bangkok, April 2025** (Thailand chair) | Theme "Prosperous, Resilient, and Open"; **Bangkok Vision 2030**; **Maritime Transport Cooperation Agreement**; India's **21-point Action Plan** |
- Current chair: **Bangladesh** (from April 2025, 2024-26 term).
!TRAP 5th Summit was hosted by **Sri Lanka**, not India.
#### 4.3 Centres and India's Initiatives
| BIMSTEC centre | Location |
|---|---|
| Cultural Industries Observatory | **Thimphu** |
| Energy Centre | **Bengaluru** (Host Country Agreement Nov 2024) |
| Centre for Weather and Climate | **Noida** |
| Technology Transfer Facility | **Colombo** |
- **MILEX 2018, Pune** – first BIMSTEC army exercise; SAARC and ASEAN have no military component.
- **1st BIMSTEC Youth Summit** – Gandhinagar (Feb 2025); **Cancer Care programme** at Tata Memorial (July 2025).
- Weaknesses: few summits, broad focus, Rohingya and border issues, BCIM rivalry.
### 5. EUROPEAN UNION AND COUNCIL OF EUROPE
- **Croatia** joined in **July 2013** (latest member); EU became 28.
- **Brexit:** under **Article 50 TEU** (Lisbon Treaty exit clause); UK left **31 January 2020** – EU back to 27.
- **Windsor Framework** (February 2023) – Northern Ireland, UK and EU.
- **Digital Single Market Strategy** – adopted **6 May 2015**; three pillars, incl. better access to digital goods and services.
- **Stability and Growth Pact** – limits budget deficits of member states.
- **India-EU FTA (2026):** EU commitments in **144 services subsectors**; social security agreements with all EU members within 5 years; post-study work framework for Indian students.
- **Council of Europe** (Strasbourg) is **not** an EU body; Russia removed March 2022.

#### 6.2 ARF, EAS, AIFTA and RCEP
- **ASEAN Regional Forum** – **1994**.
- **First East Asia Summit** – **14 Dec 2005, Kuala Lumpur**; 16 countries (10 ASEAN + India, Japan, China, South Korea, Australia, NZ).
- EAS now **19**: 11 ASEAN + India, China, Japan, US, Russia, Australia, NZ, South Korea. **20th EAS (Kuala Lumpur, 2025)** – Kuala Lumpur Declaration.
- **AIFTA (goods)** in force **2010**; India sought early completion of the **AITIGA review** (32nd ARF, Kuala Lumpur, July 2025).
- **RCEP:** ten ASEAN + Australia, China, Japan, NZ, Korea; signed **2020**; about **30% of world population**; **India stayed out (2019)**.

### 9. IORA AND THE AP ANGLE
- **IORA** (1997), Secretariat **Ebene, Mauritius**, **23** members.
- **India is chair (2025-27)**, taking over from Sri Lanka in Nov 2025; hosts Senior Officials Meeting (June 2026) and Council of Ministers (end 2026); **summit 2027**, first since **Jakarta 2017** (20th anniversary).
- **Priority areas:** maritime safety and security; trade and investment facilitation; fisheries management; disaster risk management; academic and scientific cooperation; tourism and cultural exchanges. Cross-cutting: blue economy, women's economic empowerment.
!KEY AP: **NACIN Palasamudram** (Rayalaseema) – a **World Customs Organization** regional training centre for officials of SAARC, ASEAN and African nations.
