## U0786 | INDIA'S CLIMATE COMMITMENTS – NDCs, PANCHAMRIT, NET-ZERO 2070, LiFE, ISA, CDRI, CARBON MARKET
### 1. INDIA'S NDCs (BASE YEAR 2005)
| Commitment | INDC 2015 (2030) | Updated NDC, Aug 2022 (2030) | NDC 3.0 (2035) | Progress |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Emissions-intensity cut of GDP | **33-35%** | **45%** | **47%** | **36%** by 2020 |
| Non-fossil share of **installed capacity** | **~40%** | **50%** | **60%** | Crossed 50% **June 2025** |
| Extra carbon sink | **2.5-3 bn t CO2e** | Unchanged | **3.5-4.0 bn t** | 2.29 bn t added |
- **Base year 2005** (APPSC 2019); Aug 2022 target **45%** (APPSC 2022). NDCs prepared by **MoEFCC**; approved by **Union Cabinet**.
| Version | Date | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| INDC | **Oct 2015**, before COP21 | 4 of 8 NAPCC missions on adaptation |
| Updated NDC | **Aug 2022** | Formalised **Panchamrit**; added **Mission LiFE** |
| NDC 3.0 (2031-35) | Cabinet, **Mar 2026** | LiFE embedded; adaptation (coasts, farms, Himalaya); low-cost finance |
- Updated NDC did **not remove adaptation**. By 31 Dec 2025, India and Argentina = only G20 members without a 2035 NDC.
!TRAP 33-35% (not 30-35%); target is **installed capacity**, not generation.
### 2. PANCHAMRIT AND NET ZERO 2070
- Announced by the PM at **COP26, Glasgow, 1 Nov 2021**. Hook: **500-50-1-45-2070**.
| # | Pledge | By |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **500 GW** non-fossil capacity | 2030 |
| 2 | **50%** energy from renewables (achieved June 2025) | 2030 |
| 3 | Cut projected emissions by **1 billion t** (not by 2050) | 2030 |
| 4 | **45%** intensity cut from 2005 | 2030 |
| 5 | **Net zero** | **2070** |
- **Net zero** = residual emissions **balanced by removals** (forests, carbon capture); not zero gross emissions. India's year **2070** (APPSC 2024), later than developed 2050; defended by **CBDR-RC** (India supports it) and development needs.
- **LT-LEDS** at **COP27 (Nov 2022)**; 7 transitions: low-carbon electricity, transport, urban adaptation and efficient buildings, decoupling industry, **CO2 removal**, forest cover, low-carbon finance. **Not**: fossil-fuel power expansion.
- **Net Zero Portal (Sep 2026)**: **voluntary** corporate disclosure (Scope 1, 2; Scope 3 voluntary) + NAPCC Dashboard.
- **IEA "Net-Zero by 2050"** roadmap: May 2021.
### 3. ENERGY TRANSITION AND CARBON SINK
- Non-fossil **51.5%** of capacity (262.74 of 509.64 GW, Nov 2025); **generation only ~25%** (storage shortage).
- **3rd** in RE capacity, after China and USA (IRENA 2026). Solar 157.05 GW, wind 56.81 GW (May 2026).
- **Green Hydrogen Mission**: **₹19,744 crore**; **5 MMT/yr by 2030**. **Nuclear Mission**: **5 indigenous SMRs by 2033**.
- **Pumped storage**: **258 GW** identified; **7 GW** operational; >17 GW by 2030. First standalone BESS: 180 MW (Gujarat).
- **AP**: **Pinnapuram 1,680 MW** integrated RE + pumped storage project.
- **Green India Mission**: key sink programme; **5 Mha new + 5 Mha improved**; MISHTI: 540 sq km mangroves. Forest and tree cover **~24.6%** (2021).
- India has **not signed** the Global Methane Pledge.
### 4. MISSION LiFE
- **LiFE = Lifestyle for Environment** (APPSC 2024).
- Idea at **COP26 (2021)**, not COP25; Global Movement **5 June 2022**; Mission launched **Oct 2022, Kevadia**, with the **UN Secretary-General**.
- **P3** = Pro-Planet People; **75 actions under 7 themes** (save energy, save water, no single-use plastic, sustainable food, reduce waste, e-waste, healthy lifestyles). Aligned: **UJALA** (LED), **PAT**.
### 5. CARBON MARKETS
| | **CCTS** | **Green Credit Programme** |
|---|---|---|
| Launch | Notified **June 2023** | **Oct 2023** |
| Law | **Sec 14AA, Energy Conservation Act 2001** (amended 2022) | **EPA 1986** |
| Run by | **BEE** (Ministry of Power) | **ICFRE, Dehradun** |
| Nature | **First mandatory compliance** market | Incentive for **voluntary** actions |
- CCTS: **compliance** (energy-intensive sectors; GEI targets → tradeable **Carbon Credit Certificates**) + **offset** (voluntary, 10 sectors). **Intensity-based, not cap-based**; meets the 45% target.
- **282 entities** (Oct 2025: aluminium, cement, chlor-alkali, pulp and paper); now 7 sectors. **UK CBAM** recognises CCTS from **1 Jan 2027**.
- GCP afforestation exchangeable for compensatory afforestation.
- **Surat ETS**: particulate matter cut **20-30%**. **Khasi Hills REDD+** (Meghalaya): India's **first Plan Vivo** REDD+, 2011.
### 6. ISA AND CDRI
| | **ISA** | **CDRI** |
|---|---|---|
| Launched | **COP21 Paris, 30 Nov 2015**, **India + France** | **2019, UN Climate Action Summit** |
| HQ | **Gurugram** | **New Delhi** |
| Status | **First treaty-based IGO HQ in India**; open to all UN members (began with sunshine countries) | Partnership, not treaty body |
| Members | **125** (Mar 2026) | 30+ governments |
- ISA **Founding Conference: New Delhi, 11 Mar 2018**; apex body **ISA Assembly**. **Malta = 119th member (Feb 2024)** (APPSC 2024). USA (joined 2021) announced exit Jan 2026.
- ISA initiatives: One Sun One World One Grid, Global Solar Facility, STAR-C.
- CDRI: **IRIS** launched at **COP26** for SIDS.
