### 1. AP BUDGET 2026-27 – PROFILE
- Presented by Finance Minister **Payyavula Keshav** on **14 February 2026** (his **third** Budget).
- Focus: **asset creation, higher capital expenditure, fiscal consolidation**.
- **Economic Survey = diagnosis; Budget = remedy.**
#### 1.1 Key Figures
| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| **Total outlay** | **₹3,32,205 crore** (about ₹3.32 lakh crore) |
| Total expenditure | ₹3,10,058 crore (+12% over 2025-26 RE) |
| Revenue expenditure | ₹2,56,143 crore |
| Capital expenditure (incl. loans) | **₹53,915 crore** (2025-26 RE about ₹39,498 crore) |
| Capital outlay | **₹48,698 crore**, **+47%** over 2025-26 RE (₹33,135 crore) |
| Loans by the State | ₹5,218 crore |
| Interest payments | **₹37,281 crore** |
| GSDP (current prices) | ₹19,75,073 crore (+12%) |
!TRAP ₹53,915 crore = capital expenditure including loans; ₹48,698 crore = capital outlay (the +47% figure).
!KEY Total outlay rises about 12% in expenditure terms, but capital outlay rises **47%**.
### 2. DEFICITS, DEBT AND FRBM
#### 2.1 Deficit Ratios
| Deficit | 2026-27 BE | 2025-26 RE |
|---|---|---|
| **Revenue deficit** | ₹22,002 crore = **1.11%** of GSDP | **2.33%** |
| **Fiscal deficit** | ₹75,868 crore = **3.84%** of GSDP | about **4.6%** |
- Fiscal deficit **3.84%** is still **above the 3% norm**.
- **Revenue deficit** = revenue expenditure − revenue receipts.
- **Fiscal deficit** = total expenditure − total receipts excluding borrowings.
#### 2.2 Debt
- Outstanding debt about **36% of GSDP** (2026-27); about ₹7.11 lakh crore.
- **FRBM Act** = **Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management Act, 2003**: controls fiscal deficit and debt.
!PAIR Revenue deficit | 1.11% | 2026-27 || Fiscal deficit | 3.84% | 2026-27
### 3. RUPEE COMES FROM / RUPEE GOES TO (AP 2026-27)
| Comes from | Share | Goes to | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| **State taxes** | **38%** | **Interest + debt repayment** | **18%** |
| Open market borrowings | 30% | Welfare | 17% |
| Share in central taxes | 19% | Other departments | 15% |
| Grants-in-aid | 10% | Education | 11% |
| Non-tax revenue | 3% | Water Resources | 6% |
| – | – | Health | 6% |
| – | – | MA&UD | 4% |
- Own taxes (38%) are the largest source; central taxes share (19%) + grants (10%) = 29% central transfers.
!TRAP Interest + debt repayment (**18%**) is larger than welfare (**17%**): a sign of **fiscal stress**.

### 6. GSDP, VISION AND EXTERNAL AID
#### 6.1 GSDP 2025-26 (Advance Estimates)
- GSDP **₹17.62 lakh crore** (from ₹15.91 lakh crore), nominal growth **10.75%**.
- Per capita income **₹2,94,507** (growth 10.43%); highest district **Visakhapatnam**, lowest **Parvathipuram Manyam**.
- Inflation **1.39%** (Economic Survey).
#### 6.1A Human Development Indicators
| Indicator | Andhra Pradesh | India |
|---|---|---|
| **Literacy** (Census 2011, residual AP) | **67.35%** (male 74.77%, female 59.96%) | 72.98% |
| **IMR** (SRS 2024, per 1,000 live births) | **18** (25 in 2019) | 24 |
| **MMR** (SRS 2021-23, per 1 lakh live births) | **30**: **lowest in India, tied with Kerala** | 88 |
| **Multidimensional poverty** (NITI Aayog, 2019-21) | **6.06%** (11.77% in 2015-16) | 14.96% |
!TRAP AP literacy (67.35%) is **below** the national average, but its MMR (30) is the **lowest** in India.

### 7. TELANGANA COMPARISON
- **Budget 2026-27**: Telangana **₹3.24 lakh crore** vs AP **₹3.32 lakh crore**.
- Telangana (2023-24): own tax revenue **80%** of total revenue; tax revenue up 13.4%.
- Non-tax revenue grew about 33% a year since 2019-20; grants-in-aid down to about 6%.
- Revenue deficit ₹6,254 crore (2019-20) → **surplus ₹5,943 crore** (2022-23).
- Fiscal deficit 3.29% → 3.33%; debt **₹5.17 lakh crore = 34.47% of GSDP** (2023-24).
