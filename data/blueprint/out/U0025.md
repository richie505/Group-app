## U0025 | SECOND URBANISATION – NBPW, PUNCH-MARKED COINS, GUILDS AND TOWNS
### 1. CAUSES OF SECOND URBANISATION
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Iron tools** | Cleared forests of the **Ganga plain**; agricultural surplus |
| Trade, crafts, coinage | Taxes and wages paid in money |
| **State formation** | Mahajanapada capitals became administrative centres |
| New merchant classes | **Vaishyas** backed **Buddhism and Jainism** |
- Over **60 towns** flourished in **600–300 BCE**.
### 2. NORTHERN BLACK POLISHED WARE (NBPW)
- Period **c. 700–200 BCE**; came **after Painted Grey Ware (PGW)**.
- Glossy, **deluxe ware of elites**; the **hallmark** of the second urbanisation.
- **Rajghat (Varanasi)**: pre-NBPW levels about 3,000 years old; punch-marked coins and **Greek-inscribed seals**; urban Varanasi about **800 BCE**.
!TRAP PGW came first; NBPW followed.
### 3. CHRONOLOGY OF ECONOMIC FIRSTS
| Development | Period |
|---|---|
| Iron at **Mayiladumparai** (AMS date) | c. **2172 BCE**; southern Iron Age c. 2100 BCE |
| Iron ploughshares, axes (Ganga valley) | Later Vedic, c. 1000–600 BCE |
| Urban Varanasi | c. 800 BCE |
| NBPW | c. 700–200 BCE |
| Punch-marked coins | **7th–6th c. BC** to the Mauryan period |
| **Gold coins in large numbers** | **Kushanas** (1st–2nd c. CE) |
!TRAP Southern iron (Mayiladumparai) is older than the Gangetic Iron Age.
### 4. PUNCH-MARKED COINS (PMC)
- Also **Aahat** coins; **earliest extant coins** of India; **mostly silver**, some copper; standard weights.
- Called **panas / karshapanas**; symbols punched: **sun, animals, trees, hills**.
- Silver PMC issued under Haryankas, Nandas and Mauryas.
- **First definite literary references** to coins: **Pali texts** (kahapana, nikkha, kamsa, kakanika), backed by excavated PMC.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Karshapana / kahapana | Standard silver or copper PMC |
| **Nishka / nikkha** | Originally a **gold ornament** or unit; later a coin |
| Satamana | Value or weight unit (100 mana) |
| Suvarna | Gold coin (Satavahana usage) |
- **Satavahanas** first used inherited PMC, then die-struck coins of **lead, potin, copper, silver**; **ship-mast coins** of **Yajnasri Satakarni**.
- **Vaishya Tekri** (near Ujjain): BCE stupa **bigger than Sanchi**; gave PMC and cast **Avanti** coins; digging to restart after about 90 years.
!TRAP PMC = mostly silver, not gold.
### 5. GUILDS (SHRENI) AND BANKING
- Head of a guild: **jetthaka** or **pamukha** (Buddhist texts); held by heredity or chosen by the members.
- Functions: bought raw materials, organised production, **controlled quality**, **fixed prices and wages**, found markets; own rules (**shreni-dharma**).
- **Nashik inscription**: **guild deposits at fixed interest** – guilds acted as banks.
- **Satapatha Brahmana**: first reference to **money-lending**; usurer = **kusidin**.
### 6. TRADE TERMS AND ROUTES
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sarthavaha** | Caravan / convoy of merchants, or its leader |
| **Setthi / Sedhi** | Rich merchant-banker |
| **Gahapati** | Wealthy householder / landowner |
| Vanija | Trader |
| Kusidin | Usurer |
- **Uttarapatha**: linked **Taxila** with the Ganga valley.
- **Dakshinapatha**: southern route to the Deccan, through **Assaka**.
- AP angle: Satavahana trade centres **Kotilingala, Dhulikatta, Kondapur**.
### 7. TOWNS OF THE PERIOD
| Town | Region | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| Rajagriha | Magadha | Ringed by **five hills** |
| **Shravasti** | Kosala | **Crescent-shaped** plan |
| Kaushambi | Vatsa | On the Yamuna; fortified |
| Ujjayini | Avanti | On the **Sipra**; Ptolemy's "Ozene" |
| Taxila | Gandhara | Learning centre; Uttarapatha junction |
| **Vaishali** | Vajji | Republican capital |
| Champa | Anga | Near Bhagalpur, on the Ganga |
- Fortified capitals (rajadhani): stone/mud-brick **walls and moats**.

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- Earliest coins: PMC; first coin references: Pali texts.
- First money-lending reference: Satapatha Brahmana.
- First gold coins in large numbers: Kushanas.
