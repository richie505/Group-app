## U0717 | ICT IN ANDHRA PRADESH – E-PRAGATI, RTGS, MEE SEVA AND STATE DIGITAL INITIATIVES
### 1. AP E-GOVERNANCE JOURNEY
- AP's e-governance legacy = **N. Chandrababu Naidu's SMART governance** model and the **HITEC City** IT push of 1995-2004.
- **e-Seva** began in **2001**; it was followed by **MeeSeva** in **2011** (combined state).
- Latest: AP now offers services on **WhatsApp** (Mana Mitra, 2025) and is building AI-based governance under RTGS.
#### 1.1 AP Projects → Department / Service
| AP project | Department / service |
|---|---|
| **CARD** (Computer-aided Administration of Registration Department) | Registration Department: computer-aided registration of property documents; electronic delivery of all registration services |
| **e-Seva** | Single-window citizen service centres (2001) |
| **MeeSeva** | Single-window G2C delivery (2011) |
| **AP Online** | Web portal for G2C services |
| **APSWAN** | State-wide area network linking HQ, districts and mandals |
| **Registration Service Kendras** (2026) | Property registration on the **Passport Seva Kendra** model; legal powers stay with **Sub-Registrar Offices** |
- RSK debate: effect on **document writers'** livelihoods; State assures SROs are **not being privatised**.
!TRAP CARD = Registration Department (property documents), not revenue land records.
### 2. MEESEVA
#### 2.1 Meaning and Basics
- **"MeeSeva"** in Telugu = **"At your service"**.
- **Single-window G2C** delivery; **succeeded e-Seva**; launched **2011** in the combined state.
- Delivery channels: **MeeSeva franchise centres** + **online portal**; also enrolment for Aarogya Raksha.
#### 2.2 Service Categories
| Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Category A** | Over-the-counter services issued **on the spot** (e.g. certified copies) |
| **Category B** | **Statutory** services needing **notices, hearings or field verification** |
#### 2.3 Departments inside MeeSeva
| Department | Role |
|---|---|
| **EDS Department** | **Prints MeeSeva certificates** |
| **Education Department** | **Migration certificates** (passed candidates only) |
!KEY APPSC 2023: MeeSeva = "At your service"; Category B = statutory; EDS prints certificates; Education Dept issues migration certificates – all pairs correct.
### 3. E-PRAGATI – AP STATE ENTERPRISE ARCHITECTURE
- **e-Pragati** = new name of the **AP State Enterprise Architecture (APSEA)**; approved by the state cabinet in **2015**.
- Outlay **Rs 2,358 crore**: **72 projects** in **14 packages**, over three years; state share about Rs 1,528 crore, rest via **PPP**.
- Covers **33 secretariat departments** and **300+ government agencies**; built with **Wipro**.
- Aim: all government services under one roof through a single architecture ("Digital AP").
!NUM e-Pragati: Rs 2,358 crore | 14 packages | 72 projects | 33 departments
### 4. RTGS – REAL TIME GOVERNANCE SOCIETY
#### 4.1 Set-up
- **Real Time Governance Society (RTGS)** formed on **6 September 2017**; works directly under the **Chief Minister**.
- **RTG State Centre** (command centre) inaugurated at the **Secretariat, Velagapudi** on **26 November 2017**.
- Dashboards pull data from surveillance cameras, drones, biometric systems and **AWARE** (AP Weather Forecasting and Early Warning Research Centre).
- Revived from 2024 as an **AI-driven** governance platform.
- **Swarna Andhra @2047** pillar: **"Effective Governance and AI-based Public Services"**; monitoring tracks KPIs; OBD/IVRS calls and bulk WhatsApp for citizen feedback.
- AP has **59 Chief Data Officers**.
#### 4.2 Mana Mitra – WhatsApp Governance
- Launched **30 January 2025**; run by **RTGS**.
| Stage | Data |
|---|---|
| Launch (Jan 2025) | **150** services |
| 2025-26 | **865** services |
| Cap per service | **5 user inputs, 2 documents** |
- Services: **rice card eKYC, electricity bills, 1-B Adangal, APSRTC tickets, TTD and temple darshan**.
- Temples: **Rs 22.26 crore** to **21 temples**; ~30% of darshan and seva bookings via WhatsApp; top earner = **Srikalahasti** (not Tirumala).
!TRAP Highest temple earner through Mana Mitra = Srikalahasti, not Tirumala.

### 7. RAJIV INTERNET VILLAGE
- **RAJiv (Rajiv Internet Village)** programme launched by CM **Y.S. Rajasekhara Reddy** in **August 2004**.
- Village kiosks run by local entrepreneurs; services: **utility bill payment**, state **bus tickets**, land records and information.
- Contract with **Bharat Electronics** (Dec 2004) for **5,550** RAJiv centres in 14 districts.
### 8. AP FIBERNET
- **AP State FiberNet Ltd (APSFL)**; Fibre Grid inaugurated by the **President** in **December 2017**.
- **Triple-play**: **broadband + TV + telephone** in one package; basic plan **Rs 149** (15 Mbps, ~250 TV channels).
- Reached **4,000+ tribal hamlets** in the Eastern Ghats.
- **APSWAN** initial capacity **2 Mbps**.
