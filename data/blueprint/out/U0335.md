## U0335 | CHALLENGES IN LOCAL DEVOLUTION – FUNDS, FUNCTIONS, FUNCTIONARIES; PARALLEL BODIES AND CAPACITY GAPS
### 1. THE 3Fs – PAPER VS REALITY
| F | Meaning | Reality |
|---|---|---|
| **Functions** | **Eleventh Schedule 29** (panchayats), **Twelfth Schedule 18** (municipalities) | Devolved on paper |
| **Funds** | Untied money matching functions | **Tied to schemes** |
| **Functionaries** | Staff answerable to the local body | Report to **State line departments** |
- **Arts 243G / 243W say "may"** → devolution is discretionary.
- **Roadmap for Panchayati Raj:** weak 3Fs, bureaucratic control, tied funds, grant dependence, unwillingness to tax → PRIs are **implementing agencies, not planning units**.
- **Devolution Index** ("Status of Devolution to Panchayats in States"): **MoPR with IIPA**; 2024 edition (released **Feb 2025**), **6 dimensions** – Framework, Functions, Finances, Functionaries, Capacity Enhancement, Accountability.
| 2024 Index | Top / score |
|---|---|
| Overall | **Karnataka 1st**, then Kerala, Tamil Nadu |
| Functions / Finances / Functionaries | **Tamil Nadu / Karnataka / Gujarat** |
| **AP** | **54.43**, medium band (Telangana 55.1) |
### 2. KEY ARTICLES
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **243K / 243ZA** | **SEC** appointed by **Governor**; removed **like a High Court judge** (CEC like a Supreme Court judge) |
| **243E / 243U** | **5-year term**; polls before expiry or **within 6 months** of dissolution |
| **243D** | Women at least **one-third**; **AP gives 50%** |
| **243ZD / 243ZE** | **DPC / MPC** – integration of plans (constitutional) |
- These made local government **permanent** after 1993.
### 3. FUNDS GAP
- **Property tax** = main ULB own revenue, **under-assessed and under-collected**.
- ULB own revenue ≈ **0.3% of GDP**; cities make ≈ **67% (2/3) of GDP** but get **<1% of tax revenue**.
- **16th FC:** rural ₹4.4 lakh crore, urban ₹3.6 lakh crore; **urban share 36% → 45%**; **80% basic, 20% performance**.
- 16th FC conditions: **constituted bodies, audited accounts, timely SFCs**; **SFC reports often ignored**.
### 4. PARALLEL BODIES AND BUREAUCRATIC CONTROL
| Body / control | Effect |
|---|---|
| **DRDAs, user groups, SHGs** | Run schemes outside PRIs |
| **MPLADS / MLA LADS** | Legislators pick works |
| **Janmabhoomi committees (2014-19)**; **village/ward secretariats and volunteers (2019)** – AP | Parallel to GPs, wards |

- **MPLADS:** MP recommends → **Implementing District Authority** executes; **RTI applies**; portal **eSAKSHI**.
- **Balwantrai Mehta:** **Collector chairs the Zilla Parishad**; sarpanches depend on block offices.
### 5. ELECTIONS AND AP
- AP Mandal and Zilla Parishads due by 23 Sep 2026; ₹12,150 crore at risk.
- **AP Cabinet (18 Aug 2026):** **BC reservation 34% rural, 33⅓% urban**; **direct election of Mayors and chairpersons**.
### 6. WOMEN REPRESENTATIVES – PROXY PROBLEM
- **"Sarpanch pati":** male relative rules in her name; proxy **falls in her second term**.
- **MoPR advisory committee (Sushil Kumar, 2025):** **exemplary penalties** for proven proxy cases.
- Also: **Sashakt Panchayat Netri Abhiyan**, **Model Women-Friendly GPs**.
