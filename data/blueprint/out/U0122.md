## U0122 | VIJAYANAGARA ADMINISTRATION - NAYANKARA AND AYAGAR SYSTEMS, AMARAM TENURE, REVENUE AND ARMY
### 1. NATURE OF THE STATE AND THE KING
- **King**: supreme head, commander and **fountain of justice**; acted within **Dharma** and with his ministers' counsel.
!PAIR Nilakanta Sastri | "military administration" | historian view || Burton Stein | "segmentary state" | historian view
### 2. CENTRAL ADMINISTRATION
#### 2.1 Officers
| Officer | Role |
|---|---|
| **Mahapradhani** | Chief minister heading the **pradhanis**; e.g. **Saluva Timmarusu** |
| **Dalavayi** | Commander-in-chief |
| **Rayasams** | Secretaries (Kondamarasu, Ayyamarasu) |
| **Karanikas** | Scribes |
#### 2.2 Departments
- **Rayavachakam** records **nine departments**, each headed by a **Samprati**.
| Department | Function |
|---|---|
| **Kandachara** | **Military** – standing army, headed by Senapati / **Dalavayi** (APPSC 2018, 2019) |
| **Athavane / Attavana** | **Revenue** / finance |
| **Bhandara** (Sri Bhandara) | **Treasury** |
| **Dharmasana** | **Judicial**; Pradvivaka = judge |
!TRAP Athavane = revenue; Kandachara = military; Bhandara = treasury.
### 3. POLITICAL IDEAS IN AMUKTAMALYADA
| Area | Maxim |
|---|---|
| Revenue | **Bulk of revenue to the army** |
| Dharma | Rule with an eye on Dharma; protect subjects |
| Trade | **Improve harbours**; encourage import of horses, elephants, gems; treat **foreign merchants generously** |
| Welfare | Build tanks; **win over forest tribes** rather than only crush them |
| Forts | **Brahmins** should command forts |
- Records the **genealogy** of the dynasty but **not his campaigns**; "political matters are discussed" (APPSC G2 2003).
### 4. PROVINCIAL ADMINISTRATION
#### 4.1 Units
| Unit | Head |
|---|---|
| **Rajya / Mandala** (province) | **Prince** (Kumara; Sangama princes titled **Odeyar**) or **Durgadhipati / Durga Dannayaka**; office called Chavadi |
| **Nadu / Seema** (district) | **Nadagauda**; **Parupatyagar** |
| **Sthala** / Kampana | **Reddi**, **Gauda**, **Sthalakaranam** |
| **Grama** (village) | **12 Ayagars** |
- About **17 rajyams** under **Achyutaraya**. Aravidu inscriptions divide Udayagiri, Penukonda and Hampi Rajyams into **Vanitas**.
#### 4.2 Governors and the Andhra Rajyas
- Powers: **mint coins**, remit old and levy new taxes, grant exemptions, keep **own armies**; frequently **transferred**; Brahmins posted as dannayakas in border provinces.
| Andhra Rajya | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Udayagiri** | Most important; Paka and Muliki districts (Nellore, Kadapa); governed by royal princes under the first dynasty |
| **Chandragiri** | **Saluva Narasimha** was its governor before 1485; later capital |
| **Penukonda** | New headquarters of Tirumala after Talikota |
| **Gooty** (Gutti) | Fort raised to rajya status |
### 5. NAYANKARA (AMARAM) SYSTEM
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Amaram** | Land granted to military chiefs **instead of cash salary** |
| **Amaranayakas** | Holders; also Nayaks, Palaiyagars, **Poligars** |
| **Mahamandaleshwara** | Their title; more autonomy than Durgadannayakas |
- Within the amaram: **collected taxes**, kept law and order, **administered justice**.
- Obligations: pay tribute (**Kappam**); keep a quota of troops, horses, elephants; attend court at **Mahanavami** with gifts; maintain forts, temples, irrigation.
### 6. AMARAM vs KAKATIYA NAYANKARA vs MANSABDARI
| Point | Kakatiya nayankara | Vijayanagara amara-nayankara | Mughal mansabdari |
|---|---|---|---|
| Origin | **Prataparudra II** (APPSC 2025) | Grew out of the Kakatiya system | Akbar |
| Scale | **75 or 77** nayakas holding mandalams and forts | About **200** nayakas, incl. Shudra chiefs | **Zat and sawar** ranks |
| Terms | Kept a share for troops, sent the rest | Land revenue kept; Kappam paid; troops kept | Cash or transferable jagir |
| Outcome | Discontent among older feudatories | Independent **Nayaka states** after 1565 (Madurai, Tanjore, Keladi) | Non-hereditary, central control |
!TRAP Kakatiya 75/77 nayakas vs Vijayanagara about 200.
### 7. PALEGARS OF RAYALASEEMA
- **Origin**: Amara-nayakas → **Palegars (Poligars)** when the centre weakened after **Talikota (1565)**; hereditary; **forerunners of the zamindars**.
- **Thomas Munro** with officer **Campbell** crushed them (APPSC G2 2011), then introduced **Ryotwari**.
### 8. AYAGAR SYSTEM AND VILLAGE OFFICERS
- **12 hereditary Ayagars** per village (APPSC 2012); Kakatiya villages also had twelve Ayagars.
| Group | Members |
|---|---|
| Officials | **Karanam** (accountant), **Reddi** (head), **Talari** (watchman), **Purohita** (priest) |
| Artisans / servants | Kamsali (goldsmith), **Kummari** (potter), **Vadrangi** (carpenter), **Kase** (mason/house-builder), Kammari (blacksmith), **Chakali** (washerman), Mangali (barber), Madiga |
- Payment: **no cash salary**; tax-free **Manyam** lands (Mirasi, Bhattu Vrutti, Jatikattu Manyalu) and a harvest share **Mera** → villages self-sufficient in chaos.
| Officer | Duties |
|---|---|
| Karanam | Accounts, land and crop registers, temple accounts |
| Reddi | Village head and revenue collector |
| Talari | Watchman; collected **Merulu** (grain gifts) |
| Begari | Supervised forced labour (vetti) |
!TRAP Mera = Ayagar harvest share; Merulu = Talari's grain gifts; Meruka = tax on sale and purchase.
### 9. REVENUE – LAND AND TAXES
#### 9.1 Land Classes and Share
| Term | Land |
|---|---|
| **Nanja** | Wet |
| **Punja** | Dry |
| **Bagayati** | Garden |
| **Banjara** | Waste |
- **Neerarambamu / Magani** (irrigated): tax in **grain**, higher; **Kaadarambamu / Metta** (dry): cash, lower.
- Share: usually **1/6**; **1/4 or 1/3** on fertile land; land-tax documents = **Adhuvanatantram**.
#### 9.2 Tax Terms
| Tax | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Navidadere / Kondaju** | **Barbers'** tax |
| **Maneyanna / Illari Pannu** | **House** tax |
| **Meruka** | Tax on **sale and purchase** (APPSC G2 2011) |
| **Putrodaya Kanuka** | Birth of a son |
| **Kalyana Kanika** | Wedding tax on both parties |
| **Siddhaya** | Fixed levy: Pinje (cotton ginners), Eidipi (toddy tappers), Jati (castes/professions) |
- **Marriage tax**: first remitted by Saluva Narasimha; **abolished by Krishnadevaraya**.
- Market (**santhalu**) tax rights auctioned to **Sunkandarlu**; private workshop owners paid an industries tax.
### 10. ARMY
- **Siddha (standing) army** under the Dalavayi via Kandachara; paid every **4 months**.
- **Bukka I** = first to use **cannon**; **Saluva Narasimha** introduced **Samugaris** (gunmen); firearms with Turkish and Portuguese experts.
- Four forts: **Giri** (hill), **Sthala**, **Jala**, **Vana** – under **Durga Dannayakas**.
- **Horses** (Arabian, Persian) via **Goa, Honnavar, Bhatkal**; trade run by Portuguese and Arab merchants.
### 11. JUSTICE AND POLICE
- **Pradvivaka**: adviser/judge helping the king; explained legal texts (APPSC G2 2019).
- Law: Yajnavalkya and Parashara Smritis via **Madhavacharya's Parashara Madhaviyam**.
- **Himsodbhava** = criminal; **Dhanodbhava** = civil.
- Punishments: treason – **death**; theft – **mutilation**; Brahmins – life imprisonment instead of death.
| Level | Police (Kavali system) |
|---|---|
| Village | **Talari** |
| Sthala | **Kavali Daru** |
| Towns | **Peta Kavalidaru** |
- Latest (June 2026): ASI's **Sadasivakona** inscriptions (1554 CE) show land grants by a temple Karanam.
