## U0554 | AP PER CAPITA INCOME, DISTRICT DISPARITIES AND AP'S POSITION AMONG STATES
### 1. PER CAPITA INCOME – CONCEPT AND FORMULA
#### 1.1 Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Per capita income (PCI)** | **NSDP ÷ population** – a **net** measure (after depreciation) |
| Per capita GSDP | **Gross** measure; current or constant prices |
| Current prices | Include inflation |
| Constant (2011-12) prices | Show **real** growth |
| **District Domestic Product (DDP)** | District GDP, compiled by the **State Directorate of Economics and Statistics (DES)** – not NSO; DES is also the nodal agency for GSDP |
- **Standard of living** is shown by **per capita income**.
- PCI = income ÷ population, so it **rises only when income grows faster than population**.
- PCI **can fall in a developing country even as national income rises** – when population growth exceeds income growth.
- Causes of slow PCI growth: population growth, low productivity, low-income/low-saving "vicious circle".
#### 1.2 Limits
- PCI is an **average**: it **hides inequality** and ignores **cost-of-living** differences; high PCI can coexist with poverty.
- The **P4 scheme** targets the gap between high average PCI and lasting poverty and inequality.
!TRAP Per capita GSDP (gross) and PCI (net, NSDP) are not interchangeable – never compare AP's per capita GSDP with India's per capita income.

### 4. AP PCI VS ALL-INDIA PCI – GAP
- AP's PCI has been **above India's every year since 2014-15**.
- 2025-26 gap ≈ **₹75,000**; AP ≈ **1.34 times** the national figure.
- All-India new series: average income per person ₹2,43,180 (₹20,265 a month).
- India = **lower-middle-income economy**, PCI about **$2,760**.
- **All-India PCI 2023-24 (PE) ₹1,84,205** was **lower** than AP's 2023-24 (AE) PCI.
!TRAP APPSC G2 2025: "India's 2023-24 PCI ₹1,84,205" and "it is lower than AP's" – both statements correct.
### 5. AP AMONG INDIAN STATES
#### 5.1 EAC-PM report
- **Relative Economic Performance of Indian States (1960-61 to 2023-24)** – EAC-PM working paper, Sept 2024; indicators: share in national GDP and relative per capita income.
- Share in national GDP 2023-24: **AP 4.7%**, **Telangana 4.9%** – Telangana's share higher.
- Five southern States (AP, Telangana, Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu) together **over 30%** of India's GDP (2023-24).
- Telangana's economy = **96.5%** of AP's size (2024-25), yet its GDP share is higher; metro Hyderabad = 47.5% of Telangana's output.
- **Largest State economy = Maharashtra** (2023-24).
#### 5.2 PCI of States
| State | PCI | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Telangana** | **₹3.54 lakh** (2022-23) | Richest major State |
| Karnataka | ₹3.44 lakh (2022-23) | Next: Haryana |
| Tamil Nadu | ₹3.09 lakh (2022-23); ₹4.08 lakh (2025-26) | 2nd after Karnataka in 2025-26 |
- Order 2022-23: **Telangana > Karnataka > Haryana, Tamil Nadu**.
#### 5.3 Per capita NSDP 2023-24 (ES 2025-26)
| State | PCI | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Sikkim** | ₹5,87,743 | **Highest** in India |
| Goa | ₹5,85,953 | Second |
| Karnataka | ₹3,39,813 | Highest among large States in options |
| **Bihar** | ₹62,201 | **Lowest** in India |
#### 5.4 Older ranking keys
- PCI 1998-99, descending: **Punjab > Maharashtra > Gujarat > Uttar Pradesh**.
- Per capita NSDP (2001 key): **Gujarat > Maharashtra > Tamil Nadu > Karnataka**.
- **BRICS highest PCI**: China now (2024); Russia in 2010.
- Bihar is a backward State; regional disparity is true but is **not the explanation** of its backwardness.
#### 5.5 Divergence among States
- Gini of State PCI: **0.230 (1994-95) → 0.261 (2025-26)** – States diverging.
- **16th Finance Commission**: **42.5%** weight to **income distance** → AP's above-average PCI **lowers** its devolution share.
- AP MPI headcount **6.06%** vs India **14.96%**.
### 6. REGIONAL DISPARITIES
#### 6.1 Regions
| Region | Feature |
|---|---|
| Coastal Andhra (**Krishna-Guntur belt**) | Historically more developed than North Andhra and Rayalaseema |
| Rayalaseema | Rain-shadow, drought-prone; now horticulture hub – **43% of horticulture area, 52% of output** |
| North Coastal Andhra | Backward; tribal hinterland |
- Three economic regions: **Visakhapatnam (10 districts), Amaravati (9), Tirupati (10)**.
#### 6.2 Backward districts
- **APRA (AP Reorganisation Act, 2014) package** – **7 backward districts**: **4 Rayalaseema** (Kurnool, Kadapa, Anantapur, Chittoor) + **3 North Coastal** (Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam).
- **₹50 crore per district a year**; releases stalled.
- MPI (NFHS-5, old 13 districts): **lowest West Godavari 2.42%**; **highest Kurnool 12.84%**.
!TRAP APPSC 2022: Nalgonda is a Telangana district – NOT an economically backward area of AP.

### 9. VISION TARGETS FOR PCI
- **Swarna Andhra @2047**: PCI **above $42,000 (about ₹55 lakh)** by 2047 (now ~$3,400).
- **₹5.43 lakh** = **per capita GSDP** target for **2028-29** (not PCI).
- Objective of AP visions: **double per capita income every five years**.

7. **Nalgonda** is not an AP backward area.
