## U0580 | ECONOMIC ISSUES ARISING OUT OF BIFURCATION – REVENUE DEFICIT GRANT, RESOURCE GAP, EMPLOYEE APPORTIONMENT
### 1. RESOURCE GAP OF 2014-15
- Mandate: Centre to **bridge AP's resource gap** for the **first year, 2014-15**.
- With Hyderabad going to Telangana, AP faced a revenue deficit of **more than ₹15,000 crore**.
| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **₹16,078 crore** | **AP's claimed** 2014-15 gap |
| **₹4,117.89 crore** | **Centre's** calculation (standardised accounting, excluding new commitments) |
| **₹3,979.50 crore** | **Released** (2 June 2014 to 2016-17) (APPSC-GS 2018) |
- The ₹3,979 crore was counted as a **revenue deficit grant** within the **Special Assistance Measure (SAM, ₹24,350 crore)**.
- **Section 47(4)**: disputes over financial assets and liabilities settled by a **Central order on the advice of the CAG**.
!TRAP 16,078 = AP's claim; 4,117.89 = Centre's figure; 3,979.50 = released.
### 2. FINANCE COMMISSIONS, SCS AND SAM
| Measure | Provision |
|---|---|
| **14th FC (2015-20)** | States' share **32% → 42%**; removed **general/special category** distinction; post-devolution **RD grant to AP ₹22,113 crore** |
| **SCS** | Promised in Parliament (**5 years**); denied, citing the 14th FC |
| **SAM** | **90%** grant equivalent only for **EAPs**; AP says it lacks **statutory permanence** |
| **16th FC** | **Discontinued RD grants** for all States → AP says it lost up to **₹38,000 crore** (18 August 2026) |
- Revenue deficit: about **₹16,000 crore** (2014-19) → projected **₹48,000 crore** (2025-26).
- After the 16th FC, Centre approved **₹15,000 crore for Amaravati**.
- **SASCI**: **50-year interest-free** loans, outside the **3% of GSDP** fiscal-deficit cap.
- Debate: Centre prefers **loans and guarantees**; AP wants **non-repayable grants**.
!TRAP 22,113 = 14th FC RD grant; 38,000 = loss claimed from the 16th FC ending grants.
### 3. SECTION 46 AND DEBT APPORTIONMENT
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| **46(2)-(3)** | Grants + **special development package** for backward areas, esp. **Rayalaseema and North Coastal AP**; first allocations made, then payments stopped |
| **54** | **Public debt** and Public Account liabilities by **population ratio**; loans re-lent for an activity in one State stay with that State |
| **55** | **Floating loans** stay with the State for whose purpose raised; rest by population ratio |
- AP took **58% of debt** but got **46% of revenue** assets (by location).
### 4. TAX ARREARS AND REFUNDS
| Item | Basis | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Property tax / land revenue arrears | State where property lies | 50 |
| Arrears of other taxes | **Place of assessment** | 50 |
| Deferred taxes of industrial units | State where unit is located | 51(1) |
| Refund of property tax collected in excess | State where property lies | 56 |
| Refund of other taxes | **Population ratio** | 56 |
- Oddity: arrears follow assessment (mostly **Hyderabad**), refunds follow population; cost to AP about **₹3,900 crore** (14th FC).
### 5. EMPLOYEES AND POWER
- **Section 59 / Eighth Schedule**: pre-appointed-day **pensions** by population ratio **58.32 : 41.68**.
- **Section 58**: **Provident Fund** follows the State of **permanent allotment**.
- AP paid **₹32,944 crore** interest in 2024-25 (committed spending crowds out capital outlay).
- **Twelfth Schedule**: **APGENCO** plants by **location**; existing **PPAs** with DISCOMs continue.
- **29 August 2022**: Union Power Ministry ordered Telangana to pay AP **₹6,756.92 crore** (principal ₹3,441.78 crore + surcharge) for power supplied **2 June 2014 – 10 June 2017**, within **30 days**.
- Telangana counter-claimed **₹12,940 crore**; Telangana High Court **set aside** the order (**19 October 2023**); AP appealed to the **Supreme Court**.
