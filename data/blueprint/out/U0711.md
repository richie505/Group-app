## U0711 | EMERGING ICT APPLICATIONS – AI, BIG DATA, CLOUD, BLOCKCHAIN, IOT AND 5G
### 1. FOURTH INDUSTRIAL REVOLUTION
- Term coined by **Klaus Schwab (WEF)** in **2016**.
- Meaning: convergence of **digital, physical and biological** technologies; **cyber-physical systems**; "smart factories".
| Revolution | Defining technology |
|---|---|
| IR1 | **Steam / mechanisation** |
| IR2 | **Electricity / mass production** |
| IR3 | Electronics / IT |
| IR4 | Cyber-physical systems (digital + physical + biological) |
- **4IR technologies**: **AI, machine learning, robotics, IoT, blockchain, quantum computing, geo-spatial mapping**.
- **AP**: WEF announced a **Centre for the Fourth Industrial Revolution (C4IR)** in Andhra Pradesh (2026) = India's **3rd**, after **Mumbai** and **Telangana**.
!PAIR Alvin Toffler | "Third Wave" | futurist || Tim Berners-Lee | World Wide Web | Nandan Nilekani: UIDAI

### 3. INTERNET OF THINGS (IOT)
- Devices networked to **communicate without human intervention**.
- **Four pillars**: **device, data, analytics, connectivity**.
#### 3.1 Three-Layer Architecture
1. **Perception layer**: sensors collect data.
2. **Network layer**: transmits data.
3. **Application layer**: user services.
!TRAP Layer order: perception → network → application (not network first).
#### 3.2 Uses
| Use | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Smart city** | Traffic and **air-quality monitoring**, **waste collection**, public safety |
| **Smart agriculture** | **Precision farming**, **soil monitoring**, livestock, yields |
| **Wearables** | **Fitness trackers**, health monitors |

### 5. BLOCKCHAIN – FEATURES AND GOVERNANCE USES
- **Features**: decentralised **distributed ledger**, **immutable**, **transparent**, **smart contracts**.
- **Tokenization**: **NFTs** (unique digital assets) and **real-world asset tokens** (land, bonds).
- **Governance uses**: **land records**, digital certificates, **pharma and agri supply chains**, **e-Notary** (blockchain e-Sign), farm insurance, e-voting, health records, SHG microfinance.

| Technology | Meaning |
|---|---|

### 7. BIG DATA – THE Vs AND GOVERNANCE USES
- Originally **3 Vs** (2001): **Volume** (amount), **Velocity** (speed of generation and analysis), **Variety** (structured, semi-structured, unstructured).
- Later added: **Veracity** (quality, accuracy, trustworthiness) and **Value** (usefulness) → **5 Vs**.
| Application | Agency / use |
|---|---|
| **AP State Data Lake** | Links **RCH health with POSHAN**, and **housing with land pattas** → plugs leakages |
| **I4C's NCAP** | Uses **AI and big data** to analyse crime trends |
| **NAFRS** | NCRB facial recognition |

### 9. 5G AND 6G – FEATURES, LABS, STACK
- Generations: **1G (1980s)** → 5G now; **6G expected early 2030s**.
- **5G features**: latency as low as **1 ms**, **massive device connectivity**, **high reliability**.
- **Network slicing**: one physical network split into virtual networks via **SDN/NFV**; ITU/3GPP use cases **eMBB, URLLC, mMTC**.
- Net neutrality: Airtel's **'Priority Postpaid'** raised a **net neutrality** debate (3 Jun 2026).
- **AP**: **IIITDM Kurnool** ranked **1st** in DoT's **100 5G Use Case Labs** gradation (Jan–Jun 2026; result July 2026).
- **Indigenous stack** (Bharat Telecom Stack): **C-DOT** core + **Tejas Networks** radio + **TCS** integration; deployed by **BSNL**; launched by PM on 27 Sep 2025 (4G, 5G-ready); about 1 lakh BSNL sites; puts India among the few countries with its own full telecom stack.
- **6G**: **Bharat 6G Vision** document and **Bharat 6G Alliance**, both **2023**.
- **Wi-Fi 7**: **IEEE 802.11be**, **Multi-Link Operation**, up to **46 Gbps**.
