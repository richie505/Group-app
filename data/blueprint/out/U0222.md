## U0222 | ROLE OF SOCIALISTS IN ANDHRA – LEADERS, ORGANISATION AND PROGRAMME
### 1. ANDHRA SOCIALIST PARTY (CSP ANDHRA UNIT), 1934
- Founded **1934** (23 June, **Vijayawada**); founder and first President **N.G. Ranga**; first Secretary **Madduri Annapurnaiah**.
- First session **1935**, presided by **Tenneti Viswanatham**.
!TRAP The APPSC 2019 key accepted **Annapurnaiah** as first President; know both names.

### 6. SOCIALISTS AND COMMUNISTS – SPLIT
- CPI banned **23 July 1934** → Sundarayya, Chandra Rajeswara Rao, Krovvidi Lingaraju, Gadde Lingayya joined Ranga's party on **Prof. Gout's** advice, to work inside the Congress.
- **Andhra Communist Party**: **Kakinada, January 1936**; Secretary **P. Sundarayya** (not Chandra Rajeswara Rao).
- CSP expelled the communists in **1940**; **"People's War" (1941)** and opposition to Quit India widened the break.
### 7. KMPP AND PSP
- **KMPP** formed by **J.B. Kripalani**, **1951**; P.C. Ghosh and Prakasam linked.
- KMPP + Socialist Party → **PSP, 1952**, Kripalani its head.
- **1952 Madras** (375 seats): KMP 35, Socialists 13; United Front (164) chose Prakasam, but Governor **Sri Prakasa** invited **Rajaji**.
- **1955 Andhra**: Congress 119, KLP 22, CPI 15, PSP 13, Praja Party 5.
- PSP wanted **Tirupati** as capital (**1953**, G2 2025); **Kurnool** chosen.
