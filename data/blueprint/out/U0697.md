## U0697 | PROGRAMMES PROMOTING S&T AND INNOVATION – INSPIRE, FELLOWSHIPS, ATAL INNOVATION MISSION
### 1. INSPIRE – DST FLAGSHIP (2008)
- **INSPIRE** is the **DST** flagship scheme, launched **2008**, to attract talent to science.
#### 1.1 INSPIRE Components
| Component | Full form | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **SEATS** | Scheme for **Early Attraction** of Talents for Science | School students |
| **MANAK** | **Million Minds Augmenting National Aspiration and Knowledge** | Classes **VI–X**, aged **10–15**; ₹10,000 each |
| **SHE** | **Scholarship for Higher Education** | Top-ranked UG/PG students in natural and basic sciences |
| **AORC** | **Assured Opportunity for Research Careers** – INSPIRE Fellowship (PhD) + INSPIRE Faculty | Doctoral and post-doctoral researchers |
- MANAK 2025: **8.54 lakh** ideas; **46,926** students given ₹10,000 each.
- INSPIRE Fellowship 2025: **653** doctoral fellowships, **65% female**, about 31% former SHE scholars.
!TRAP SHE = Scholarship for Higher Education, NOT "Support for Her Education" – it is not a women's scheme.
### 2. FELLOWSHIPS AND BRAIN-GAIN SCHEMES
#### 2.1 Scheme → Body → Target
| Scheme | Awarding body | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **VAJRA** (Visiting Advanced Joint Research Faculty) | SERB (now **ANRF**) | Overseas scientists incl. NRIs/OCIs, short spells in India |
| **Ramanujan Fellowship** | SERB, now ANRF | **All** Indian scientists abroad returning |
| **Ramalingaswami Re-entry** | **DBT** (not DST) | **Life-science/biotech** returnees only |
| **J.C. Bose Fellowship** (now JC Bose Grant) | SERB/ANRF | Active **senior** scientists, for excellence |
| **Swarnajayanti** | DST, **1997** (50 years of Independence) | Young scientists with a proven record |
| **PMRF** | **Ministry of Education** (2018) | PhD scholars in IITs, IISc, IISERs (not a career-break scheme) |
- **Brain drain → brain gain**: **VAJRA, Ramanujan Fellowship and INSPIRE**.
- ANRF schemes: PMECRG (PM Early Career Research Grant), Prime Minister Professorship, National Science Chair, N-PDF.
!PAIR Ramanujan | SERB/ANRF | all returnees || Ramalingaswami | DBT | life science only
### 3. WOMEN AND INCLUSION IN STEM
| Scheme | Body | Target group |
|---|---|---|
| **Vigyan Jyoti** | DST, with **Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti** | Meritorious **girls, Classes IX–XII**; 29,000+ covered |
| **WISE-KIRAN** (Knowledge Involvement in Research Advancement through Nurturing; WISE = Women in Science and Engineering) | DST | Women scientists, esp. **career break**; WISE-PhD, WISE-PDF, WIDUSHI; 340+ selected |
| **WIDUSHI** | DST (under WISE-KIRAN) | **Senior women** scientists, **interdisciplinary** S&T |
| **CURIE** | DST | **22 Women PG Colleges** – research facilities |
| **GATI** (Gender Advancement for Transforming Institutions) | DST | **Institutions** – gender-equity charter, like UK's **Athena SWAN** |
| **SERB-POWER** (Promoting Opportunities for Women in Exploratory Research) | SERB (now ANRF) | Women researchers in academic/R&D institutions |
| **BioCARe** | **DBT** | Women biotech scientists after a career break |
| **SEED** | DST | **52 STI Hubs** for **SC/ST** communities |
!KEY Senior women + interdisciplinary = WIDUSHI; GATI targets institutions, not individuals.

### 5. START-UP AND INNOVATION SUPPORT
#### 5.1 NIDHI – DST, 2016
- **NIDHI** = **National Initiative for Developing and Harnessing Innovations**.
- Components: **PRAYAS**, **Entrepreneur-in-Residence**, **Seed Support System**, **Technology Business Incubators** (hosted in academic/R&D institutes), **Accelerators**, **Centres of Excellence**.
#### 5.2 Other Start-up Support
| Scheme | Body | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **TIDE 2.0** (Technology Incubation and Development of Entrepreneurs) | **MeitY**, 2019 | ICT start-ups via incubators |
| Fund of Funds for Startups | **SIDBI** | ₹10,000 crore |
| Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups | **NCGTC** | Separate scheme, NOT part of NIDHI |
- **RDI Scheme** (Cabinet, **1 July 2025**): **₹1 lakh crore** over six years (₹20,000 crore for 2025-26); guided by **ANRF Governing Board**; nodal = **DST**.

#### 7.2 NM-ICPS and Other Missions
- **NM-ICPS** (National Mission on Interdisciplinary Cyber-Physical Systems): Cabinet **6 Dec 2018**, **₹3,660 crore** approved for **five years**, spending spread over nine years.
- **25 Technology Innovation Hubs** as **Section-8 companies**; verticals such as AI/ML, robotics, cyber security, quantum.
- AP: **IISER Tirupati TIH (IITTNiF)** – **Positioning and Precision Technologies**.
- **National Quantum Mission** (2023): **₹6,003.65 crore**, 8 years. **IndiaAI Mission** (2024): ₹10,371.92 crore.
### 8. ATAL INNOVATION MISSION (AIM)
- Flagship of **NITI Aayog**, set up **2016** (announced in Budget 2015); builds innovation and start-up culture at school, university and other levels.
| Component | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Atal Tinkering Labs (ATLs)** | Schools, **grades 6–12**: IoT, 3D printing, robotics; **10,000 ATLs** in 700+ districts |
| **Atal Incubation Centres (AICs)** | Start-ups in universities/institutions |
| **ACICs** (Atal Community Innovation Centres) | **Rural and under-served** areas |
| **ARISE** (Atal Research and Innovation for Small Enterprises) | **MSMEs** |
| Atal New India Challenges | Product innovation for national challenges |
#### 8.1 AIM 2.0 and Latest
- **AIM 2.0 (2024)**: **₹2,750 crore**, till **March 2028**; shift from building infrastructure to filling ecosystem gaps and scaling.
- **LIPI**: 30 vernacular innovation centres in 22 scheduled languages; 10 Atal Sectoral Innovation Launchpads; 2,500 new ATLs.
- **Feb 2026**: Union Budget 2026-27 raised ATL funding nearly six-fold, **₹500 crore → ₹3,200 crore**; Generative-AI assistants added to ATLs.
- **Jun 2026**: **Tripura** – first **State Innovation Mission** under AIM 2.0; **India-France ATL Bridge** launched at **Nice** (Bharat Innovates 2026).
### 9. ANDHRA PRADESH INNOVATION ECOSYSTEM
- AP has **590 ATLs** (2024-25).
- **Andhra Pradesh Innovation Society (APIS)**: state body for start-ups; set up the **Ratan Tata Innovation Hub** as a Section-8 company.
- **Ratan Tata Innovation Hub (RTIH)**: inaugurated **20 Aug 2025** by CM N. Chandrababu Naidu; central hub at **Mangalagiri** (Amaravati) with five spokes – Visakhapatnam, Rajahmundry, Vijayawada-Guntur, Tirupati, Anantapur.
- **Amaravati Quantum Valley** – Andhra Pradesh; IISER Tirupati hosts IITTNiF.
