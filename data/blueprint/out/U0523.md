## U0523 | STRUCTURE OF THE INDIAN FINANCIAL SYSTEM – BANKS, NBFCS, DFIS, COOPERATIVES
### 1. ORGANISED VS UNORGANISED FINANCIAL SYSTEM
#### 1.1 Components of Each
| Organised (RBI-regulated) | Unorganised |
|---|---|
| Commercial banks, cooperative banks, foreign banks, **DFHI**, provident funds, **STCI**, mutual funds | **Indigenous bankers**, **moneylenders**, unregulated finance/loan companies, **chit funds**, nidhis |
| Instruments: call money, **Treasury bills**, commercial bills, **certificates of deposit**, CPs, repo, MMMFs | Serves farmers, artisans, small traders |
!KEY APPSC Group 2 (2017) key: **finance companies are NOT part of the organised money market**; commercial banks, DFHI and cooperative banks are inside it.
#### 1.2 Unregulated Non-bank Intermediaries – Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Indigenous banker | Accepts deposits and lends, combining banking with **trade or commission business**; deals in **hundis** (Shroffs, Seths, Mahajans, Multanis, Chettiars) |
| Moneylenders | Professional or agricultural; covered by **state money-lending laws** |
| Chit funds | Regulated by the **State Government** (Chit Funds Act, **1982**) |
| Shadow banking | Bank-like credit intermediation outside full bank regulation (NBFCs, money-market/liquid funds); seen as a cause of the global financial crisis; term coined by **Paul McCulley** (US economist), **2007** |
| Regulatory arbitrage | Using gaps in regulatory treatment to work outside the stricter banking framework |
!TRAP One who "**lends only in kind**" is NOT an indigenous banker.
- **Liquid debt mutual funds** = main short-term funding source for NBFCs.
- 2010 PYQ: the institution whose importance has **fallen** most = **pension funds** (superannuation funds; retirement income).
### 2. DEVELOPMENT FINANCE INSTITUTIONS
#### 2.1 DFIs – Year and Sector
| Institution | Established | Sector / role |
|---|---|---|
| **IFCI** | **1 July 1948** (special Act) | **First DFI**, first all-India term-lending institution; long-term industrial finance; now a public sector NBFC-ND-SI managing PLI projects |
| **ICICI** | **January 1955** | Industrial credit; **World Bank initiative**; private sector; later universal bank; first moving ATM, Mumbai, 12 Dec 2002 |
| **IDBI** | **July 1964** (under RBI; autonomous 1976) | Apex industrial finance (medium and large industries); now IDBI Bank, **private** after **LIC** took majority control (**2019**) |
| **EXIM Bank** | **January 1982** | Export-import finance; Lines of Credit abroad; HQ Mumbai |
| **NABARD** | **12 July 1982** (Act 1981) | Apex agricultural and rural credit; on **B. Sivaraman Committee (CRAFICARD)** advice |
| **NHB** | **9 July 1988** (NHB Act **1987**) | Apex housing finance; C. Rangarajan group |
| **SIDBI** | **2 April 1990** (Act 1989) | **MSME** finance; began as IDBI subsidiary; regulates State Financial Corporations |
| **NaBFID** | **2021** (NaBFID Act 2021) | Infrastructure; capital **₹20,000 crore** + ₹5,000 crore grant; HQ Mumbai |
- IFCI's setting up began the **era of development financing** (APPSC 2018).
- IFCI, ICICI and IDBI all gave **medium and long-term industrial finance**.
- **MUDRA Bank**: development and refinance for loans to micro units.
- IDBI Bank: Government + LIC selling a combined **60.72%** stake to a private buyer.
!NUM 1948 IFCI → 1955 ICICI → 1964 IDBI and UTI → 1982 EXIM, NABARD → 1988 NHB → 1990 SIDBI → 2021 NaBFID
!TRAP NHB **Act 1987**, NHB **set up 1988**. 1964 = **both** IDBI and UTI.
### 3. ALL-INDIA FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS (AIFIs)
#### 3.1 Classification
| Class | Institutions |
|---|---|
| **RBI-regulated AIFIs (5)** | EXIM Bank, NABARD, NHB, SIDBI + **NaBFID** (since 2022) |
| **Refinance institutions** | **NABARD, NHB, SIDBI** |
| Term-lending institution | EXIM Bank |
| Older AIFIs | IFCI, ICICI, IDBI, SIDBI, IIBI |
| Investment institutions (public sector) | **LIC, UTI, GIC** |
- APPSC 2024 picked **NHB** as a refinance institution against IFCI, TFCI and EXIM.
- Set up with **RBI help**: IDBI, SIDBI, NABARD, NHB, Exim Bank, NEDB (**not SEBI**); ownership since moved to the Government.
- Per APPSC key, IDBI is a bank under the control of the RBI.
- **Quasi-regulators**: NABARD (RRBs/cooperatives), SIDBI (SFCs), NHB (HFCs).

### 5. FINANCIAL REGULATORS
| Regulator | Sector / entities | Statutory basis |
|---|---|---|
| **RBI** | Banks, NBFCs, HFCs (since 2019) | RBI Act 1934 |
| **SEBI** | Securities markets, FPIs, **merchant bankers**, venture capital, stock brokers | Statutory 1992 |
| **IRDAI** | **Insurance companies** | IRDA Act 1999 |

- NHB was wholly **RBI-owned** until the Centre took it over in **2019**.
### 6. NBFCS – CATEGORIES AND REGULATION
#### 6.1 Registration and Categories
- Registered under the **Companies Act**; regulated by RBI.
- **Principal business (50-50) test**: financial assets > 50% of total assets **and** financial income > 50% of gross income.
- Categories: **AFC, Loan Company, Investment Company, IFC, NBFC-MFI, CIC, HFC, IDF**. "Regional Rural Banking Company" is NOT one.
| Category | Minimum Net Owned Fund |
|---|---|
| NBFC-MFI | **₹10 crore** (APPSC 2023 key, March 2022) |
| NBFC-ICC | ₹10 crore (raised from ₹2 crore; glide path to March 2027) |
| HFC | **₹20 crore** |
- **NBFC-MFI**: non-deposit-taking; at least **75%** of assets in microfinance loans.
- Microfinance loan (RBI, March 2022): **collateral-free**; household annual income up to **₹3 lakh**; repayment ≤50% of income.
#### 6.2 Scale-Based Regulation (SBR)
| Layer | Who / what applies |
|---|---|
| Base | Smaller non-deposit NBFCs |
| Middle | All deposit-taking NBFCs; HFCs, IFCs, CICs |
| Upper | Identified by RBI; bank-like norms for at least **5 years**; **listing within 3 years** |
| Top | **Kept empty**; only for a sharp rise in systemic risk |
- NBFC-UL list 2026-27: **17** companies incl. **Tata Sons** (15 in April 2026); RBI rejected Tata Sons' bid to surrender its licence and asked it to list (13 Sep 2026).
- Proposed: single **₹1 lakh crore** asset test for UL in place of parametric scoring.
#### 6.3 NBFC Ombudsman
- **NBFC Ombudsman Scheme 2018** → merged into **RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme 2021** (now also covers state and central cooperative banks).
### 7. NBFC VS BANK
| Basis | Bank | NBFC |
|---|---|---|
| Demand deposits | Yes | **No** (some take term deposits only) |
| Cheques on itself | Yes | **Cannot issue** |
| Payment and settlement system | Part of it | Not part |
| Deposit insurance (DICGC) | Covered | Not covered |
| CRR / SLR | Both | **No CRR**; deposit-taking NBFCs keep liquid assets |
| RBI LAF (repo) access | Yes | **No** |
- **Payments banks cannot lend**; deposits and payment services only. 11 Small Finance Banks.
### 8. COOPERATIVE CREDIT STRUCTURE
#### 8.1 Rural Tiers
| Tier | Short-term | Long-term |
|---|---|---|
| State | State Cooperative Bank (**StCB**) | State Cooperative ARDB |
| District | **DCCB / Central Cooperative Bank** | – |
| Village | **PACS** | **PCARDB** (Primary Cooperative ARDB) |
- **StCBs and DCCBs**: licensed by **RBI**, inspected by **NABARD**; **PACS** under **State Registrars**.
- Operates at **district** level = Central Cooperative Bank.
- **Consumer Cooperative Stores**: set up by members, registered by the Registrar of Cooperative Societies.
#### 8.2 Urban Cooperative Banks
- Earlier **dual control**: State Registrar of Cooperative Societies + RBI.
- **Banking Regulation (Amendment) Act 2020** (after **PMC Bank** crisis): UCBs and multi-state cooperative banks under RBI on management, capital, audit and resolution.
- RBI to resume **on-tap licensing** of UCBs after more than two decades; **1,457 UCBs** (6 Aug 2026).
- **NUCFDC** = umbrella body for UCBs; RBI nod to NABARD's **Sahakar Sarathi** shared-services entity.
- Per APPSC key, number of UCBs as on March 2012 = 65.
### 9. RURAL CREDIT – NABARD, RRBS, SHG-BANK LINKAGE
#### 9.1 Apex Bodies
- **NABARD** took over **RBI's agricultural-credit functions** and **ARDC's refinance** portfolio (housing refinance = NHB).
- Refinances **StCBs, RRBs, land development banks** (₹3,25,497.81 crore, 2024-25); manages **RIDF**, funded by banks' **PSL shortfalls** (₹42,714 crore, 2024-25); supervises RRBs and cooperative banks.
- **RRBs**: set up **1975-76**; **RRB Act 1976**; supervised by NABARD; now **28** RRBs.
- **Swabhimaan Scheme**: rural banking.
#### 9.2 SHG-Bank Linkage and JLG
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Launched by | **NABARD**, pilot **1992** |
| Implementing banks | Commercial banks, RRBs, cooperative banks |
| Idea from | Group lending of **Muhammad Yunus's Grameen Bank** (Bangladesh; Nobel Peace Prize) |
| Description | "**World's largest microfinance movement**" |
| JLG scheme | NABARD **2006**; 4-10 members, tenant farmers and oral lessees; mutual guarantee |
#### 9.3 Housing Finance – RDCL
- **RMBS Development Company Ltd**: **NHB** equity; develops residential mortgage-backed securities market; RBI Certificate of Registration **January 2025**.
- NHB launched **RESIDEX** (July 2007), India's first official housing price index.
### 10. MICROFINANCE AND STRESS EPISODES
#### 10.1 AP Crisis to Regulation
1. **AP crisis 2010**: multiple lending, over-indebtedness, coercive recovery, borrower suicides.
2. **AP MFI Ordinance, Oct 2010** (Act 2011): MFIs register with **district authorities**; limited doorstep recovery; capped interest; AP MFI loan book collapsed.
3. **Malegam Committee** (RBI sub-committee, **15 October 2010**, Y.H. Malegam): created **NBFC-MFI** category (2011); margin and interest caps; no more than **2 MFIs per borrower**.
4. **MFIN** (2009, association of NBFC-MFIs) and **Sa-Dhan**: RBI-recognised **SROs (2014)**.
- Borrowers: **95% women**, 80% rural; 627 lakh (FY25). Share (Mar 2025): **NBFC-MFIs 39%**, banks 32%, SFBs 16%, NBFCs 12%.
- Banks' MFI portfolio fell 40% to ₹65,687 crore (Q3 FY26); NCGTC guarantee scheme for MFIs to June 2026.
#### 10.2 IL&FS and DHFL
| Episode | Fact |
|---|---|
| IL&FS (**Sep 2018**) | Infrastructure NBFC defaults → liquidity crisis; board replaced through **NCLT** order |
| DHFL (2019) | **First financial services company resolved under IBC** (Sec 227); acquired by **Piramal (2021)** |
