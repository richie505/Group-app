## U0068 | VAKATAKAS; HARSHAVARDHANA – SOURCES, KANNAUJ AND PRAYAG ASSEMBLIES, ADMINISTRATION
### 1. VAKATAKAS
- Rose in the **Deccan (Dakshinapath)** after the weakening of the **Satavahanas**.
- Order: **Satavahanas → Vakatakas → Chalukyas**. Founders: Satavahanas – **Simuka** (1st c. BC); Vakatakas – **Vindhyashakti** (3rd c. AD); Chalukyas of Gujarat – Mulraj I (10th c. AD).
#### 1.1 Rulers and contributions
| Ruler | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Vindhyashakti** | **Founder** |
| **Pravarasena I** | His son (not the first king); **only Vakataka to take the title Samrat**; performed **Vajapeya** and **four Ashvamedhas** |
| **Rudrasena II** | Married **Prabhavatigupta** |
| **Prabhavatigupta** | **Daughter of Chandragupta II**; regent after Rudrasena II's death → Guptas' indirect control of the Deccan |
| **Pravarasena II** | Interested in literature; wrote the **Prakrit Setubandha** |
| **Harishena** (**Vatsagulma** branch) | Patronised **Ajanta Caves 16 and 17** through minister **Varahadeva** |
- AP angle: **Indra Varma**, founder of the **Vishnukundins**, began as a **Vakataka vassal**.
!TRAP Pravarasena I = Samrat, Ashvamedhas; Pravarasena II = Setubandha. Do not swap.
### 2. POST-GUPTA KINGDOMS AND THE MAUKHARIS
#### 2.1 Maukharis
- Originally **Gupta feudatories from Gaya (Bihar)**; earliest inscriptions there.
- **Capital: Kannauj**.
- Rulers: Harivarma, Adityavarma, Ishvaravarma (Ishan Varma), Sharvavarma, **Grahavarma**.
- **Grahavarman** (Maukhari) was the husband of **Rajyashri**, Harsha's sister; killed by **Devagupta of Malwa**, who held her at Kannauj.
#### 2.2 Kingdom – ruler – relation to Harsha
| Kingdom | Ruler / capital | Relation |
|---|---|---|
| Maukharis | Grahavarman; Kannauj | Brother-in-law |
| **Gauda (Bengal)** | **Shashanka (Sasanka)**; capital **Karnasuvarna** | Rival – killed **Rajyavardhana**, cut the **Bodhi tree** at Gaya |
| Malwa | Devagupta | Killed Grahavarman |
| **Kamarupa (Assam)** | **Bhaskaravarman** | **Ally** against Shashanka |
| **Valabhi (Maitrakas)** | **Dhruvasena II** (Dhruvabhata) | Defeated, made **vassal**; married Harsha's daughter (son-in-law) |
| Badami Chalukya | **Pulakesin II** | Rival – **defeated Harsha** |
| Nepal, Kashmir | – | Accepted overlordship / sent tribute |
!TRAP Shashanka killed Rajyavardhana; Devagupta killed Grahavarman.
### 3. PUSHYABHUTI / VARDHANA DYNASTY
- Founded by **Pushyabhuti** at **Thanesar (Thaneshwar)** (per Harshacharita); also called the **Pushyabhuti dynasty**; once Gupta feudatories.
- **Prabhakaravardhana** was the first great ruler; Harsha's father. Queen **Yashomati** (Harsha's mother) became sati in 605.
- Order: **Adityavardhana → Prabhakaravardhana → Rajyavardhana → Harshavardhana**.
- **Rajyavardhana**: elder brother, killed by Shashanka.
#### 3.1 Capitals
- First capital **Thanesar** → moved to **Kannauj** (former Maukhari capital) to help sister Rajyashri run it.
- Power shifted from **Pataliputra** to the **Ganga-Yamuna doab**; Kannauj replaced Pataliputra as the premier city.
### 4. HARSHA – CAREER, TITLES AND CONQUESTS
#### 4.1 Timeline
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **606 CE** | Accession at **16**; **Harsha era** begins |
| c. **618–619** | Defeated by **Pulakesin II** on the **Narmada** |
| **629** | Hiuen Tsang sets out from China |
| 631 | Hiuen Tsang at Harsha's court |
| **643** | **Kannauj assembly** |
| **647** | Dies **without heir** → Pushyabhuti line ends |
#### 4.2 Titles
- **Siladitya** (Shiladitya).
- **Sakalottarapathanatha** / Uttarapathanatha = lord of all North India.
#### 4.3 Conquests
- Freed sister **Rajyashri**; drove Shashanka from Kannauj.
- Defeated Dhruvasena II of Valabhi → vassal.
- Took **Magadha** and Gauda after Shashanka's death.
- Empire: Rajasthan, Punjab, UP, Bihar, Orissa; Nepal, Kashmir, Sind, Valabhi and Kamarupa acknowledged him.
#### 4.4 Battle of the Narmada
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Opponent | **Pulakesin II** (Chalukyas of **Badami**) |
| Result | Harsha defeated |
| Record | **Aihole inscription** (634 CE) of Pulakesin II + **Hiuen Tsang's** account |
| Title taken | Pulakesin took the title **Paramesvara** |
!TRAP The Aihole inscription is dated 634 CE, so the war came earlier (c. 618–619), not 637.

### 7. HARSHA'S RELIGION AND THE TWO ASSEMBLIES
#### 7.1 Religion
- Stages: **Saiva → Hinayana → Mahayana** (influence of sister Rajyashri and Hiuen Tsang).
- Banned animal food; punished killing of living beings; still patronised Brahmanical scholars.
- Like **Ashoka**, turned to Buddhism; last great Buddhist patron-king of the north before the Rajput states.
#### 7.2 Kannauj vs Prayag
| Point | Kannauj assembly | Prayag assembly (Maha Moksha Parishad) |
|---|---|---|
| Year / frequency | **643 CE, one-time** | **Every five years** (quinquennial) |
| Place | Kannauj | **Ganga-Yamuna Sangam**, Prayag |
| Purpose | Honour Hiuen Tsang; show excellence of **Mahayana** | **Charity** – Harsha gave away his whole treasury, keeping only personal clothes |
| Attendance | Rulers of **20 countries**, 1000 Nalanda scholars, Brahmins, Shramanas, courtiers | All religions honoured |
| Events | **23 days**; violence, arson, attempt on Harsha's life | Hiuen Tsang attended the **sixth**; Buddha, Sun and Shiva worshipped on successive days |
!TRAP Prayag was not only to spread Mahayana – it honoured Buddha, Surya and Shiva. Harsha's great Buddhist convention in UP = Prayag.
### 8. ADMINISTRATION
#### 8.1 Revenue
- State revenue in **four parts**: king's use (state affairs), public servants, **scholars**, religious activities (charity to Brahmanas and Buddhists).
- **Land tax = one-sixth** of produce (also the valid tax under Hindu law).
- Officials paid in **land grants**, not cash → **feudal trend**; **samantas** sent tribute.
#### 8.2 Officials (Harshacharita)
| Officer | Charge |
|---|---|
| **Avanti** | War and peace |
| **Simhanada** | Commander-in-chief |
| **Kuntala** | Cavalry |
| **Skandagupta** | Elephant force |
!TRAP Skandagupta here is Harsha's elephant-force officer, not the Gupta king.
#### 8.3 Units and punishments
- Basic unit: **village**.
- Harsh punishments: **four ordeals – water, fire, weighing, poison**; prisoners left to rot in dungeons.
### 9. NALANDA IN HARSHA'S TIME
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Founder | Gupta ruler **Kumaragupta I** |
| Head in Harsha's time | **Silabhadra** (Sheelbhadra) |
| Character | Mahayana centre (Valabhi = Hinayana) |
| Funding | Revenue of **100 villages** + patrons like Harsha |
| Students | About **10,000**, free boarding |
| Method | **Hard entrance exam**; medium **Sanskrit**, not Prakrit; debate |
| Harsha | Patron, not founder; built a **multi-storied monastery** |
- Hiuen Tsang studied at Nalanda (637 AD) under Silabhadra, learning **Yogashastra** for about one and a half years, and later taught there.
!TRAP Head was Silabhadra – not Harsha, Hiuen Tsang or Kumarajiva.
### 10. HIUEN TSANG (XUANZANG)
#### 10.1 Profile
- Chinese Buddhist monk, "**Prince of Pilgrims**" (also "King of Travelers").
- Left **Changan** (Tang capital) in **629 AD**; Tang emperor **Taizong** had banned foreign travel, yet he came by the **Silk Route** via Central Asia; no one sent him as emissary. Returned after **13 years** and was welcomed.
- Work: **Si-Yu-Ki** ("Records of the Western World").
- Praised **Harsha**, **Pulakesin II** and Pallava **Narasimhavarman**.
!PAIR Chinese pilgrims | Fa-Hien – Chandragupta II | Hiuen Tsang – Harsha || I-Tsing | came by sea via Srivijaya | studied at Nalanda (late 7th c.)
#### 10.2 Observations
| Aspect | Observation |
|---|---|
| Tax | Light; subjects not oppressed |
| Law and order | Well-governed, yet he was **robbed twice** |
| Economy | **Decline of trade**; self-sufficient farm economy; **Mathura** famous for cotton cloth; **Thanesar** prosperous through trade |
| Caste | Rigid; untouchables mentioned |
| Women | Position declined |
| Religion | Buddhism declining, Brahmanism rising; **no mention of Jainism** |
#### 10.3 Places described
| Place | Record |
|---|---|
| **Bhinmal** (Pi-lo-mo-lo) | **Gurjara capital** |
| **Sun temple of Multan** | Also described by Al-Masudi, Abu Zaid, Alberuni |
| Lumbini, Kapilavastu, Bodh Gaya, Sarnath, Kushinara | Buddha sites |
| Kannauj | Premier city |
- Pilgrimage to **Sambapur** = Sun worship.
### 11. HIUEN TSANG IN ANDHRA (AN-TO-LO)
- Visited **639–640 CE**, in **Kubja Vishnuvardhana's** time; Andhra = **"An-to-lo"**, capital **Vengi**.
| Place | Record |
|---|---|
| **Dhanyakataka** (Dharanikota) | About **20 Mahasanghika viharas**, about **1,000 monks** studying the **Abhidhamma Pitaka** |
| Vengi | About 20 viharas |
| Amaravati, Nagarjunakonda | **Decaying** |
| Sriparvata vihara | Lacked patronage |
- Tradition: Buddha first visited **Salihundam**, then **Dharanikota**.
