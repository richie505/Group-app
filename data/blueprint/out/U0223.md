## U0223 | ROLE OF COMMUNISTS IN ANDHRA – ANDHRA MAHASABHA, PEASANT MOBILISATION
### 1. COMMUNISTS IN ANDHRA – OVERVIEW
- Exam: **Group 2 Mains** | Tier: **HIGH** | Row: M1-A-74
- PYQ importance: **APPSC 14 questions** | other exams 0 | last asked 2025
- The communists **shifted Andhra politics from language to land**: jagirdari, vetti (forced labour) and taxes.
- Result: **41 of 145** Andhra-region seats in the **1952** elections, and they drove the **Visalandhra** demand.
!KEY Core idea: Andhra communists = land question + Visalandhra + armed struggles (Telangana 1946-51, Srikakulam 1968-70).
### 2. FOUNDING OF THE COMMUNIST PARTY IN ANDHRA
#### 2.1 Founding and First Units
| Event | Year / Place | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| Secret meeting | **1934**, Katragadda Narayana Rao's garden, **Vijayawada** | 7-member committee; **P. Sundarayya** secretary |
| Andhra Communist Party | **January 1936**, **Kakinada** | 1st Maha Sabha on **29 Jan 1936** |
| Hyderabad State Comrades Association | **13 December 1939**, Hyderabad | **Telangana's first communist unit** |
- Some accounts credit **Darsi Chenchaiah** with founding the party at Kakinada in 1936.
- Comrades Association members: **Makhdoom Mohiuddin**, **Raj Bahadur Gour**, **Syed Ibrahim**; **Kashinath Rao Vaidya** presided over its inaugural meeting.
!TRAP Comrades Association year = **1939** (G2 2012 key), not 1940.

#### 4.2 Communist Leadership Shift
- At **Bhuvanagiri (1944)** the Mahasabha came under **communist control**; a rival **Nationalist Andhra Mahasabha** broke away.
- By the 1940s it was effectively the communists' front.
- Agenda shifted from **language and culture** to **jagirdari, vetti and taxes**.
- It mobilised peasants for the **Telangana armed struggle**.
!TRAP First Mahasabha president = **Suravaram Pratap Reddy** (official key); one book wrongly names Madapati Hanumantha Rao.

### 6. TELANGANA AND SRIKAKULAM ARMED STRUGGLES
#### 6.1 Comparison
| Item | Telangana Armed Struggle | Srikakulam Girijan struggle |
|---|---|---|
| Period | **1946-1951** | **1968-1970** (G2 2017 key) |
| Trigger | **Doddi Komaraiah** killed at **Kadavendi (Jangaon)**, **4 July 1946**, by a deshmukh's men | **31 Oct 1967** attack on a tribal meeting near **Parvathipuram** (Mondemkhal incident); two tribals killed |
| Leaders | P. Sundarayya, Chandra Rajeswara Rao, Ravi Narayana Reddy | **Vempatapu Satyam (Satyanarayana)**, **Adibhatla Kailasam**, Panchadhi Krishnamurthy, Subbarao Panigrahi |
| End | **Ceasefire October 1951** after Soviet advice to work with the Congress | Satyam and Kailasam killed at **Barikonda, 10 July 1970**; crushed by **1971** |
#### 6.2 Key Details
- Telangana: peasants **seized land** and set up **village soviets**; the phase September 1948-October 1951 came after the police action.
- Srikakulam: **CPI(ML)** announced **22 April 1969**; **Panchadhi Krishnamurthy** killed at **Jalantra Kota, 27 May 1969**; area declared "disturbed" on **16 June 1970**.
- Related movements: **People's War Group (1980)** – **Kondapalli Seetharamaiah**; **1922-24** Alluri's **Rampa** rebellion; **1938-44** **Komaram Bheem's** Gond movement.
!TRAP Telangana = 1946-1951 (Doddi Komaraiah, Kadavendi); Srikakulam = 1968-1970 (Barikonda).

### 9. CULTURE AND YOUTH – PRAJA NATYA MANDALI
- **Praja Natya Mandali** was formed in **1943** as the Andhra wing of the **Indian People's Theatre Association (IPTA)**, the CPI's cultural front.
- Art forms used: folk forms, above all **Burrakatha**, to carry political messages to villages, especially during the Telangana struggle.
- **Garikapati Raja Rao** was a leading organiser.
- **Kothapatnam summer school (1937)**: a summer school of **politics and economics** for young activists; the **Madras Government banned it** and the police **lathi-charged the students**; **Nehru** condemned the ban in a press statement on **17 June 1937**.
!TRAP Praja Natya Mandali (1943, CPI/IPTA) is not **Jana Natya Mandali** (1972, Gaddar, People's War Group).
### 10. VISALANDHRA AND ELECTORAL POLITICS
- Communists were the **first to demand Visalandhra (1946)** and started the movement (G2 2012).
- They **did not oppose linguistic states** (G2 2025).
- The "People's War" line and opposition to **Quit India** cost the CPI the **1945-46 elections**.
- AP angle: **Prakasam's** first Andhra State ministry fell on **15 Nov 1954** after communist-led agitation; the CPI then won only **15 of 196** seats in **1955**.
- National chronology: CPI banned 1934, ban lifted 1942, split 1964.
