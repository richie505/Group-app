## U0637 | ANDHRA PRADESH DRAINAGE – GODAVARI, KRISHNA, PENNA, VAMSADHARA, NAGAVALI; RESERVOIRS AND CANALS
### 1. AP DRAINAGE AT A GLANCE
- AP has about **40 rivers** (major, medium and minor); **15 are inter-state** (2024-25).
- About **75%** of AP's area lies in the **Godavari, Krishna and Pennar** basins.
- **Largest basin area in AP = Godavari**; Godavari is also the **2nd-largest basin of India** (~10% of India) after the **Ganga**.
- Sharing framework: **Krishna and Godavari** waters by **tribunal awards**; **Bahuda, Vamsadhara, Nagavali, Pennar, Palar, Ponniar** by **inter-state agreements**.
#### 1.1 Longest River of AP – Two Answers
| Measure | Answer | Fact |
|---|---|---|

!TRAP Read the stem: "longest river flowing in AP" = Godavari; "longest course within AP" = Penna.
#### 1.2 AP Basins among Indian Rivers
- India has **20 basins** = **12 major** (drainage area over **20,000 sq km**) + **8 composite**.
- Peninsular basin order: **Godavari (largest) > Krishna > Mahanadi**.
- **Narmada and Cauvery** basins are about the same size.
- Length order: **Godavari 1,465 km > Krishna ~1,400 km > Penna 597 km > Vamsadhara 254 km**.

### 3. GODAVARI SYSTEM
- **Left-bank** tributaries: **Purna, Pranhita, Indravati, Sabari**; **right-bank**: **Pravara, Manjira, Manair**.
- **Pranhita** = largest tributary by drainage area.
- Joining in AP: **Sabari** (with its tributary **Sileru**), **Sileru**, and upstream Pranhita and Indravati (Telangana–Chhattisgarh reach).
- 2025: Godavari stretch near **Rajamahendravaram** named among India's **most polluted** river stretches (untreated factory and sewage waste).
- **Godavari floods, Jul–Aug 2026**: crossed the **3rd warning level at Bhadrachalam**; ~24,000 Koya and Konda Reddi people hit in Polavaram district; 150 relief camps; **5.2 lakh cusecs** at Dowleswaram; ~**521 tmc ft** went to sea by 10 Aug 2026.
### 4. KRISHNA SYSTEM
- Basin States: **Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana, AP**.
- **Right bank**: Koyna, Ghataprabha, Malaprabha, **Tungabhadra** (largest tributary).
- **Left bank**: **Bhima** (longest tributary), Dindi, **Musi**, Paleru, **Munneru**.
- **Tungabhadra**: from **Varaha hills**; joins Krishna at **Sangameswaram (Kurnool)**; forms the **Karnataka–AP boundary for 58 km**.
- **Hagari** = biggest tributary of the Tungabhadra; **Vedavathi** (Veda + Avathi) is in this system.
- **Raichur Doab** = between **Krishna and Tungabhadra**.
- **Vijayawada** is on the Krishna. **Basava Sagar Dam** is on the Krishna.
!TRAP Tungabhadra joins Krishna at Sangameswaram, not Antarvedi (Godavari mouth).
### 5. PENNA SYSTEM
- Enters AP in **Anantapur**; cuts the **Gandikota gorge ("canyon")** in **YSR Kadapa**; small delta in Nellore; **Somasila Dam** across it near Nellore.
- **Gandikota Fort** stands on the banks of the Pennar; Gandikota is one of 7 tourism anchor hubs (2025-26).

### 6. NORTH-COASTAL AND MINOR RIVERS
- **Vamsadhara**: **biggest river born in the Eastern Ghats**; enters AP at **Pathapatnam (Srikakulam)**; flows between the **Rushikulya and Godavari** basins; tributary **Mahendratanaya** (Mahendragiri hills; shared Odisha–AP).
- **Nagavali**: flows through Vizianagaram and Srikakulam; tributary **Janjavathi** – **Asia's first rubber dam**.
- **Champavathi**: tributaries Eduvampula, Chitti, Pothula, **Gadi Gedda**; **Denkada check dam** (near Saripalli, Vizianagaram), Tarakarama Thirtha Sagaram, Kumili Dam.
- **Gosthani**: basin on Khondalites; 3% of Visakhapatnam district.
- **Machkund**: **Duduma falls**, Machkund hydel project (AP–Odisha).
- **Swarnamukhi**: rises near Pakala; **Tirumala and Srikalahasthi** in its basin; **Dhurjati** calls it **"Mogaleru"**; tributary **Kalyani** (Kalyani Dam, Tirupati's water).
- **Tammileru**: boundary of Krishna and West Godavari; drains into **Kolleru Lake**; Tammileru Dam.
- **Mahendra Tanaya and Bahuda** are shared by AP and Odisha.

### 9. POLAVARAM
- Multipurpose **earth-cum-rockfill** dam on the **Godavari**; **National Project** (central funding) under **Section 90, AP Reorganisation Act 2014**.
- Design: **194.6 TMC** storage (FRL 45.72 m), **7.20 lakh acres**, **960 MW**, **80 TMC** diverted to the Krishna; drinking water.
- **Seven Khammam mandals** transferred to AP, so the submerged area lies in AP.
- First planned as **Ramapada Sagar** by **Dr. K.L. Rao**; early cost estimate **Rs 6,400 crore**; faced environmental-clearance and resettlement problems.
- Left Main Canal → **North Coastal Andhra and Visakhapatnam**; 27 TMC for Visakhapatnam incl. 5 TMC to Kanithi (Sep 2026); Godavari water reached North Coastal Andhra by pumps (2 Sep 2026).
- Progress: **78.64% (Dec 2024)** → **89% (2026)**; to be dedicated by **March 2027**, before the **Godavari Pushkaralu 2027**.
!TRAP National Project status is Section **90**, not Section 89.

#### 10.1 Telugu Ganga
- Route: **Srisailam → Somasila (Penna) → Kandaleru → Poondi (TN)** → drinking water to **Chennai (Madras)**.
- Planned by **N.T. Rama Rao**; 7th Plan (1985-90); joint venture of **TN, AP, Maharashtra, Karnataka** – the three Krishna States gave **5 tmc each = 15 tmc**.
- Irrigates Rayalaseema and Nellore; **2.75 lakh acres** in Kurnool and Kadapa; 10 tmc ft for Chennai (2026); bulk water to Naidupeta cluster.
!KEY Telugu Ganga beneficiary State = **Tamil Nadu**.

- **APRA 2014, Part IX (Sec. 84-91)** set up **KRMB** (HQ **AP**) and **GRMB** (HQ **Telangana**); **Apex Council** = Union Water Resources Minister + two CMs; Schedule 11: drinking water > irrigation > power.
- Srisailam water sharing under APRA = **Krishna River Management Board**; El Niño (25 Aug 2026): KRMB committee limited Srisailam and Nagarjunasagar to **drinking water only**.
- Krishna River dispute: **Karnataka & AP**.
- **Telangana objects** to Pattiseema, Polavaram, Banakacherla (overuse of Krishna share; breaks APRA 2014 and GWDT 1980); **AP objects** to **Kaleshwaram** and **Palamuru-Rangareddy**. Telangana withdrew its SC petition (Jan 2026); AP says it uses only floodwater going to sea (Feb 2026).
!TRAP KRMB HQ = AP; GRMB HQ = Telangana.
