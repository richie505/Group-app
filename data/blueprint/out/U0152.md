## U0152 | ADVENT OF EUROPEANS – PORTUGUESE, DUTCH, ENGLISH, FRENCH AND DANISH
### 1. EUROPEAN COMPANIES – FORMATION, CHARTER, FIRST FACTORY, HEADQUARTERS
#### 1.1 Master Table
| Power | Company formed | Charter / nature | First factory | Headquarters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Portuguese** | Arrival **1498** (no company) | State-controlled (**Estado da India**) | **Calicut 1500** (Cabral) | Cochin, then **Goa** |
| **English** | **31 Dec 1600** | **Elizabeth I** charter; private **joint-stock**, Court of Directors | **Surat** (farman **1613**) | Surat → Madras / Bombay / Calcutta |
| **Dutch** | **VOC 1602** | States-General gave **sovereign powers**: war, forts, treaties | **Masulipatnam 1605** | **Pulicat (1610)**, later **Nagapatnam** |
| **Danish** | **1616** | – | **Tranquebar 1620** | Tranquebar; **Serampore (1755)** |
| **French** | **1664** | **State-controlled** | **Surat 1668** | **Pondicherry** |
#### 1.2 Two Orders – Do Not Mix
| Order of | Sequence |
|---|---|
| **Arrival** | Portuguese → **Dutch** → **English** → Danes → French |
| **Company formation** | Portuguese (1498) → **English 1600** → **Dutch 1602** → Danes 1616 → French 1664 |
!TRAP English-before-Dutch is the order of FORMATION; Dutch-before-English is the order of ARRIVAL. The Dutch company is 1602, not 1592.
#### 1.3 English Charter of 1600
- Granted by **Elizabeth I** on **31 December 1600**.
- **15-year monopoly** of East Indies trade between the **Cape of Good Hope** and the **Magellan Straits**; later made perpetual.
- **1707**: rival English companies merged into the "**United Company of Merchants**".
!KEY APPSC 2024 key: the 1600 Charter gave **exclusive trading rights** – not territorial or legislative powers. APPSC-G2 2025 asked the EIC year thrice: **1600**.
!KEY First joint-stock company for India = English EIC (London, 1600); Dutch company 1602 – answer Dutch only when English is not an option.
#### 1.4 Why the British Won
- EIC was a **private joint-stock company** with steady finance; used rivalries among Indian rulers.
- Portuguese naval monopoly was ended by the rise of the **Dutch and English**.
- The Portuguese and French ventures were **state-controlled**; the English and Dutch were **merchant companies** with royal or state charters.
#### 1.5 Company Profiles at a Glance
- **Dutch (VOC)**: strongest in the spice islands; on the Coromandel coast held **Masulipatnam, Pulicat, Nagapatnam, Bheemunipatnam**; beaten by Travancore (1741) and by the English (1759).
- **Danes**: small trading power; bases at **Tranquebar** (Tamil coast) and **Serampore** (Bengal); sold both to the British in 1845.
- **French**: last to arrive; HQ **Pondicherry**; lost the struggle for power to the English at **Wandiwash (1760)**.
- **English**: began at **Surat**, then built the three Presidency towns – **Madras (1639)**, **Bombay (1668)** and **Calcutta (1690-1700)**.
### 2. VASCO DA GAMA AND THE PORTUGUESE ARRIVAL
- Portuguese = **first Europeans** to come for trade; first direct sea route.
- **Vasco da Gama** reached **Calicut** in **1498** with a **Gujarati guide**; route round the **Cape of Good Hope**; received by the **Zamorin**.
- **Cabral** set up the first Portuguese factory at **Calicut (1500)**.

### 3. PORTUGUESE GOVERNORS – POLICY
| Governor | Years | Policy / key acts |
|---|---|---|
| **Francisco de Almeida** | 1505-09 | **First viceroy**; **Blue Water Policy** – naval mastery of the Indian Ocean, not inland territory |
| **Afonso de Albuquerque** | 1509-15 | **Real founder** of Portuguese power; reversed Almeida's policy; took **Goa** from Bijapur (**Nov 1510**), **Malacca (1511)**, **Hormuz**; promoted **mixed marriages**; **abolished Sati** in his territories |
| **Nuno da Cunha** | 1529-38 | Moved HQ **Cochin → Goa (1530)**; treaty with **Bahadur Shah** of Gujarat (1535) → fort at **Diu**; got Diu and **Bassein** |
- Albuquerque served 1509-15 and died in **1515**.
- **Friar Luis** negotiated a treaty with **Krishnadevaraya** (1509-10); Vijayanagara naval commander **Timmaju** helped capture Goa.
!TRAP Blue Water Policy = **Almeida** (APPSC-G2 2023 and 2025) – not Albuquerque, Cabral or da Cunha. Goa was taken in 1510, not 1499.

### 10. PORTUGUESE CONTRIBUTIONS AND EUROPEAN EXITS
#### 10.1 Contributions
- **Printing press**: **Goa, 1556**.
- Crops and fruits introduced: **tobacco, potato, sweet potato, chilli, capsicum, maize, pineapple, papaya, guava, cashew, groundnut, sapodilla**.
!TRAP NOT brought by the Portuguese: **rubber, tea, coffee**.
#### 10.2 Exits
- **Danes** sold **Serampore** and **Tranquebar** to the British in **1845**.
- **Goa, Daman and Diu** freed in **1961** (**Operation Vijay**).
- Portuguese = **first to come, last to leave**.
