### 2. INDIA-UK CETA (IN FORCE 15 JULY 2026)
- Full name: **India-UK Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA)**.
- **Largest trade deal** India has concluded in recent years; called a **"gold standard"** deal; **Rules of Origin** notified (15 Jul 2026).
- **Duty-free access for 99%** of Indian products to the UK market.
- UK: **King Charles III**; PM **Keir Starmer** (to be succeeded by **Andy Burnham**, 20 July 2026).
### 3. INDIA-OMAN CEPA (IN FORCE 1 JUNE 2026)
#### 3.1 Key terms
- Duty-free access: **99.38% of India's exports by value**; covers **98.08% of Oman's tariff lines** (earlier only **15.33%** zero duty under MFN).
- **Pharma**: medicines approved by **USFDA, EMA, UK MHRA or TGA** get **Omani authorisation within 90 days**.
- Recognises **NPOP organic certification**, **Export Inspection Council certificates** and **halal certification**.
#### 3.2 Background
- **Oman = India's second-largest trading partner in the Gulf** (not largest); bilateral trade **USD 11.18 billion (FY 2025–26)**.
- Oman: capital **Muscat**; Sultan **Haitham bin Tarik**.
- **GCC**: est. **1981**, HQ **Riyadh**; **six members** - **Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE**.
### 4. INDIA-SACU AND INDIA-BHUTAN
#### 4.1 India-SACU ToR for a PTA
- Signed at **Vanijya Bhawan, New Delhi, August 2026**.
- **SACU**: est. **1910**; HQ **Windhoek, Namibia**; **five members** - **Botswana, Eswatini, Lesotho, Namibia, South Africa**; **world's oldest functioning customs union**.
#### 4.2 India-Bhutan Line of Credit
- **5th India-Bhutan Development Cooperation Talks**: **Thimphu, 31 July 2026**.
- LoC **₹4,000 crore (Nu. 40 billion)** between **EXIM Bank** and Bhutan's **Ministry of Finance**.

### 6. ORGANISATIONS IN NEWS
#### 6.1 India's peafowls to UN Office at Geneva
- **7 August 2026**: **four Blue Indian Peafowls + one rare White Peafowl** to **UNOG**.
- Home: **Ariana Park, Palais des Nations, Geneva**.
- Handed over by **Arindam Bagchi**, India's Permanent Representative at Geneva.
- Revives a tradition: PM **Indira Gandhi gifted two peafowls to UNOG in 1981**.
- Indian peafowl = ***Pavo cristatus***; **National Bird since 1963**; **Schedule I, WPA 1972**. ***Pavo muticus*** = **green peafowl**.
#### 6.2 Venezuela withdraws from the Rome Statute
- Venezuela **notified the UN** of withdrawal from the **Rome Statute (ICC)**; interim President **Delcy Rodríguez**.
- **Article 127**: withdrawal **effective one year after notice**; Venezuela joined in **2002**.
- **ICC**: est. **2002**, HQ **The Hague**; **Rome Statute 1998** - **genocide, crimes against humanity, war crimes, aggression**.
#### 6.3 UNRWA
- **11 June 2026**: India announced **USD 2.5 million** - **first instalment of its annual USD 5 million** - for Palestinian refugees in **Jordan, Lebanon, Syria, West Bank, Gaza**.
- Earlier: about **135 MT** of aid to Palestine in **4 consignments** (Oct 2023–Nov 2024); India backs the **Two-State Solution**.
#### 6.4 UNESCO and WHO (Jun–Aug 2026)
- **48th World Heritage Committee**: **Busan, South Korea, 20–29 July 2026**; **Ancient Buddhist Site of Sarnath** (near Varanasi, UP) inscribed as **India's 45th World Heritage Site**.
- **WHO, July 2026**: **El Salvador** validated for **eliminating trachoma** as a public health problem.
### 7. ENERGY TRADE AND TECHNOLOGY
#### 7.1 Russia imports India-refined gasoline
- Shift in energy trade: India usually imports Russian crude; now **Russia bought India-refined gasoline** (refinery disruptions at home; also buys from **Belarus**).
- About **42,000 tonnes**, refined at **Nayara Energy's Vadinar Refinery (Gujarat)**, transshipped via **Egypt's Damietta port** - **Russia's first gasoline import from India**.
- Loaded **18 June 2026**; first cargo reached Russia **August 2026**; Nayara has ownership links with **Rosneft**.
#### 7.2 India's oil and LPG imports
- **Russia >51% of India's oil imports in July 2026** = **all-time high**; had fallen **below 20% in Jan 2026** after **U.S. 50% tariffs**.
- Earlier PYQ fact: China's seaborne Russian crude **1.82 mn bpd** vs India's **1.36 mn bpd** (Mar 2024).
- **USA = India's largest LPG supplier** - **over 55%** of imports in **May 2026** (about **6.66 lakh tonnes**).
#### 7.3 Project Glasswing
- **India joined Anthropic's Project Glasswing**; on **2 June 2026** **Claude Mythos Preview** opened to about **150 critical infrastructure organisations** in India and **15+ countries**. Anthropic: est. **2021**, **San Francisco**.

#### 9.2 Bretton Woods institutions and WTO
- **IMF**: **191 member countries**. **India's quota SDR 13,114.4 million (about 2.75%)**, **voting share about 2.63%** - **8th-largest** quota holder; votes = **250 basic + 1 per SDR 100,000** of quota.
- **IMF 16th General Review of Quotas** (approved **2023**) needs consent of members holding **85% of quotas**; deadline extended (Nov 2025) to **May 2026**; still **not implemented (April 2026)**.
- **SDR ("paper gold") basket** (from **1 Aug 2022**): **US dollar 43.38%, euro 29.31%, renminbi 12.28%, yen 7.59%, pound 7.44%**; next review by **end-July 2027**.
- **WTO MC14** - **Yaoundé, Cameroon (March 2026)**: no consensus; the **e-commerce moratorium** (since **1998**) **lapsed**; co-sponsors keep it **among themselves from 8 May 2026**.
| WTO term | Subject |
