### 2. PROJECT TIGER AND NTCA
- Launched **1973** on advice of **Indira Gandhi's task force**, WWF support; began with **9 reserves (18,278 sq km)** incl. **Palamau** and **Manas**; now **58 reserves in 18 States**, 84,488 sq km (~2.56% of India) (Jul 2026).
- Newest: **Madhav TR, MP (58th, March 2025)**; 57th Ratapani (Dec 2024). **Chilka** is NOT a tiger reserve.
- **Largest TR: Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam (AP)**, Nallamala, core ~**5,443 sq km**. NSTR anti-poaching camps 89 → 150; AP to receive 4 female tigers from Maharashtra and MP (Jul 2026).
- **NTCA:** statutory via **2006 WPA amendment**; chaired by **Union Environment Minister** (PM chairs NBWL). Tiger Task Force also led to WCCB. **Sec 38V(3)**: Tiger Conservation Plan for every reserve.
- **MEE 2022:** 51 TRs assessed, 12 "Excellent", average 78%. Other efforts: **CA|TS**, ITHCP (IUCN), IBCA.
- Projects in tiger corridors need **NBWL Standing Committee** clearance; a 3rd railway line threatens the **Satpura-Melghat** corridor; tigers dispersing outside reserves.
#### 2.1 Tiger Estimation
- **All India Tiger Estimation** every **4 years** by **NTCA with WII**; camera-trap survey holds a Guinness record.
- **Pugmark** method replaced by **camera trapping + ExtractCompare** (stripe patterns).
- **M-STrIPES** = Monitoring System for Tigers – Intensive Protection and Ecological Status (NTCA-WII, 2010, software-based).
- **2022 (5th cycle):** **3,682** tigers (upper 3,925); 6.1% annual growth; India ~**70-75%** of world's wild tigers.

#### 2.2 Global Tiger Efforts
- **Global Tiger Forum:** 1993, **New Delhi**.
- **Global Tiger Summit:** **St Petersburg, 21-24 Nov 2010**; adopted **TX2** (double wild tigers by 2022; India met it early); began **International Tiger Day, 29 July**.
### 3. PROJECT ELEPHANT
- **Feb 1992**; objectives: protect elephants, habitats and **corridors**, reduce man-elephant conflict, **welfare of captive elephants** (not only wild).
- **33 Elephant Reserves in 14 States**; overlap TRs, sanctuaries, reserved forests.
- **MIKE** = Monitoring the Illegal Killing of Elephants (**CITES**).
- **Biology:** herds led by a **female** (matriarchal); gestation up to **22 months** (longest of any mammal); calf nursed 2-4 years; lifespan 60-70 years; only male Asian elephants have tusks (tuskless = **makhnas**).
- **National Heritage Animal: 2010**; India has **60%** of Asian elephants; population **22,446** (Apr 2026).
- **AP:** **Koundinya WLS** (Chittoor, borders TN) is part of **Rayala Elephant Reserve** (AP's only ER; not a Ramsar site); near Brahmagiri-Nilgiri-Eastern Ghats ER.
### 4. GANGETIC DOLPHIN AND PROJECT DOLPHIN
- **Ganges River Dolphin** (Platanista gangetica): **National Aquatic Animal, 5 October 2009** (not Olive Ridley, humpback dolphin or gharial; APPSC 2019). **5 October = National Dolphin Day**.
- IUCN **Endangered**; **Schedule I**; CITES App. I; India, Nepal, Bangladesh (Ganga-Brahmaputra-Meghna, Karnaphuli-Sangu).
- **Vikramshila Gangetic Dolphin Sanctuary: Bhagalpur, Bihar**; **National Dolphin Research Centre: Patna**.
- First national survey: **6,327** dolphins; 8,500 km, 28 rivers, 8 States.
- **Threats:** dams and barrages, pollution, **fishing-net bycatch**, poaching, killing for oil, boat traffic (not "more crocodiles").
- Jan 2026: Dolphin Rescue Ambulance and Aqua Life Conservation Monitoring Centre (WII, Namami Gange). WB plans a community reserve for it.
### 5. GREAT INDIAN BUSTARD
- *Ardeotis nigriceps* (**Godawan**), Rajasthan's state bird; **CR**; fewer than **150**.
- Range: mainly **Thar (Rajasthan)**; also Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka; **Rollapadu (AP)**.
- Main threat: **overhead power lines** in solar and wind zones.
- **M.K. Ranjitsinh v. Union of India (March 2024):** modified the 2021 blanket undergrounding order, set up an expert committee; recognised a **right against adverse effects of climate change** (Arts **14 and 21**) (APPSC 2024).
- Breeding at **Sam and Ramdevra, Jaisalmer**; on Species Recovery Programme and new national projects.
### 6. VULTURES
- **9** vulture species; **4 CR**: white-rumped, slender-billed, long-billed, red-headed.
- Decline noticed at **Keoladeo, 1998**; cause **diclofenac** in cattle carcasses → kidney failure (**visceral gout**); confirmed by **Peregrine Fund** research, May 2003.
- **Veterinary diclofenac banned 2006**; **aceclofenac and ketoprofen** banned for animals 2023; safe alternative **meloxicam**.
- **Vulture Recovery Plan 2006**; **Action Plan for Vulture Conservation 2020-25** (extended to 2030): **state-level vulture committees**, more breeding centres, Vulture Safe Zones. **Tamil Nadu** set up its committee (Oct 2022).
- First breeding centre: **Jatayu, Pinjore (Haryana)**; first portal "The Vulture Network"; **SAVE** = Saving Asia's Vultures from Extinction consortium.
- **World's first** release of captive-bred **slender-billed vultures**: 5 birds, BNHS Assam centre (Aug 2026); first Nilgiris release failed (Jun 2026).
### 7. PROJECT CHEETAH, IBCA AND PROJECT LION
- **Cheetah** ("hunting leopard") declared **extinct in India in 1952**; only large mammal lost in recent history. **Asiatic cheetah** survives only in **Iran**; India brought **African** cheetahs.
- Purpose: restore **open forest and grassland** (APPSC G1 2022). SC first ruled on reintroduction in 2012.
| Batch | Source | Date |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | **Namibia** (not Nigeria) | 17 Sep 2022, Kuno |
| 12 | South Africa | 18 Feb 2023 |
| 2 females | Botswana | May 2026 |
- **Kuno NP** (Sheopur, MP); ~30 cheetahs incl. India-born cubs; debates on deaths and carrying capacity.
- **Second site: Gandhi Sagar WLS (Mandsaur, MP)**: two males moved from Kuno, 20 April 2025.
#### 7.1 International Big Cat Alliance
- Launched **April 2023, Mysuru**, for 50 years of Project Tiger; treaty-based; **Secretariat New Delhi** (not Geneva).
- **7 big cats:** tiger, lion, leopard, snow leopard, cheetah, jaguar, puma.
- Open to **96 range countries** plus non-range countries (APPSC G1 2024); 27 members; first summit **New Delhi, 1-3 June 2026**.
#### 7.2 Project Lion
- **2020**; conserves last free-ranging Asiatic lions (**Gir**); **891** lions.
- **Palpur-Kuno** (Sheopur) first chosen as second home; SC ordered shifting **2013**; Gujarat did not act → Kuno went to cheetahs.
- **Barda WLS** (Gujarat) proposed; criticised as too close to Gir.
### 8. SNOW LEOPARD, RHINO, CROCODILES AND OTHERS
- **Snow leopard:** **718** (first assessment); **national symbol of Kyrgyzstan** (Dec 2023); 12 range countries; in India found in J&K, Ladakh-Himachal-Uttarakhand belt, Sikkim, Arunachal.
- Naturally found in India: Black-necked Crane, Flying Squirrel, Snow Leopard. Sloth bear = street-performing bear.
- **Rhinos:** over **4,000**; Assam 2,650 (2018).
- **National Chambal Sanctuary (1979):** MP-Rajasthan-UP; India's only **tri-state riverine PA**; only viable **gharial** population; SC took suo motu cognisance.
- Crocodile sub-projects: **Baula at Dangamal, Bhitarkanika**; **mugger at Ramatirtha** (1984).
- 10 captive-bred **Asian giant tortoises** released in **Ntangki NP, Nagaland**.
- **Kharai camel (Kutch):** swims up to **3 km** in seawater, grazes mangroves; coastal and dry ecosystems.
- **Whale shark:** India's largest fish (~50 ft); Gujarat protection programme since **2004** (not 2006).
### 9. SPECIES RECOVERY PROGRAMME AND FUNDS
- Under **CSS-Integrated Development of Wildlife Habitats (IDWH)** for **22 CR species**: snow leopard, Asiatic lion, GIB, Gangetic dolphin, dugong, vultures, Hangul, marine turtles. **Gharial and sloth bear** recommended for inclusion (Jun 2026).
- Recovery Programme for Critically Endangered Species: funded via **National CAMPA Advisory Council**, initially **5 years** (APPSC 2019).
- **CAMPA:** ad hoc by SC 2006; **Compensatory Afforestation Fund Act, 2016**: **90%** to State Funds, **10%** to National Fund.
- **Project HANUMAN (AP):** **3 March 2026** (World Wildlife Day), reduces human-wildlife conflict.
### 10. MIGRATORY SPECIES AND CMS
- **CMS (Bonn Convention):** 1979, under **UNEP**; India a party.
- **COP13: Gandhinagar, Feb 2020**; mascot **Gibi** (a GIB, not Amur falcon); India's **Asian elephant, GIB and Bengal florican** added to **Appendix I**.
- **COP14: Samarkand, Uzbekistan, Feb 2024**.
- **Amur falcon:** breeds in SE Russia and N China → southern Africa; non-stop Arabian Sea crossing; stopover at **Doyang reservoir, Nagaland**; **Pangti** = "Falcon Capital of the World".
### 11. MARINE AND COASTAL CONSERVATION
- **Hope Spots** (IUCN + Mission Blue): **Lakshadweep** and **Andaman and Nicobar** (APPSC G1 2024).
- **First Dugong Conservation Reserve: Palk Bay, TN (2022)**; dugong = vulnerable marine mammal.
- **Olive Ridley arribada:** **Rushikulya (Ganjam, Odisha)**; **Srikakulam and Vizianagaram beaches (AP)**. **National Marine Turtle Action Plan (2021)**.
- **MISHTI (2023):** mangroves, ~22,561 ha in 13 States/UTs.
