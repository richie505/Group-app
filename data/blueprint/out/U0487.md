### 2. JNNURM – THE FIRST MEGA URBAN MISSION
- Launched **3 December 2005**; a **7-year programme**.
- Urban infrastructure and basic services in **over 60 cities** (million-plus cities and others).
- Objectives: urban infrastructure, houses and basic services for the poor; **urban electrification was NOT an objective**.
- Two components: **BSUP** (Basic Services to Urban Poor) + **IHSDP** (Integrated Housing and Slum Development Programme).
#### 2.1 JNNURM Reforms
- **25% of municipal budget** kept for the urban poor.
- **25% of developed land** reserved for EWS/LIG.
- 7-point charter: tenure, housing, water, sanitation, education, health, social security.
!TRAP BSUP and IHSDP belong to JNNURM; CLSS and ISSR belong to PMAY-U.
#### 2.2 Earlier Sanitation Scheme
- **ILCS** (Integrated Low Cost Sanitation), **1980-81**: end dry latrines, free manual scavengers, new latrines for EWS.
- Funded on sharing basis; **central subsidy 75%**.
### 3. SWACHH BHARAT MISSION – URBAN AND SWACHH SURVEKSHAN
#### 3.1 SBM Basics
- Launched **2 October 2014**; goal **ODF and Clean India by 2 October 2019** (Gandhi's **150th** birth anniversary).
- Two components: **Rural (Grameen)** and **Urban (Shahari)**.
- Focus on **collective behavioural change** of the whole community; open defecation is part of the usual early-morning walk for many (mainly rural India).
- **SBM-U 2.0** launched **1 October 2021**: garbage-free cities.
#### 3.2 Certification Ladder
| Garbage Free City (GFC) star | Prerequisite certification |
|---|---|
| 1 Star | **ODF** |
| 3 Star | **ODF+** |
| 5 Star | **ODF++** |
| 7 Star | **Water+** |
- GFC vision: no garbage or litter visible in the city at any time of day.
#### 3.3 Swachh Survekshan Results
- **Indore**: cleanest city (population over 10 lakh), MoHUA, **31 December 2019**.
- **Swachh Survekshan 2024-25 (July 2025)**: Indore top for the **8th year in a row**; new **Super Swachh League** of 23 cities.
- Super Swachh League, 10 lakh+ group: **Indore, Surat, Navi Mumbai, Vijayawada (AP)**.
- Ranked "cleanest big city" (10 lakh+): **Ahmedabad**, then Bhopal, Lucknow.
- Visakhapatnam: SafaiMitra Surakshit Shehar award (sanitation worker safety).
- World Cities Day 2019 theme: **"Better City, Better Life"**.
!KEY Vijayawada is the AP city in the Super Swachh League (10 lakh+), 2024-25.
### 4. AMRUT AND AMRUT 2.0
#### 4.1 AMRUT (June 2015)
- Initially **500 cities**; 15 merged → **485**.
- Covers about **60% of urban population** (not "400 cities").
- Mission cities: all ULBs with **1 lakh+** population; other State/UT capitals; all **HRIDAY cities**; cities on stems of main rivers; hill-State, island and tourist cities.
- Outlay: **Rs 1 lakh crore**, incl. Rs 50,000 crore central (2015-16 to 2019-20); extended to Sept 2025.
- Delivery through **State Annual Action Plans**.
#### 4.2 Thrust Areas (full list)
1. Water supply
2. Sewerage and septage
3. Storm-water drains
4. Green spaces and parks (recreation centres for children)
5. Non-motorised urban transport / public transport
6. Capacity building
!TRAP Crop procurement is NOT an AMRUT thrust area.
#### 4.3 Rajasthan PYQ
- **Dausa was NOT** an AMRUT mission city of Rajasthan (till March 2024).
- Jaipur (State capital), Ajmer (HRIDAY city), Jodhpur (1 lakh+) qualified.
#### 4.4 AMRUT 2.0 (2021 to 2025-26)
- **Universal water supply in all statutory towns**; 100% sewerage/septage in the 500 AMRUT cities.
- Projects worth **Rs 2.79 lakh crore** sanctioned under AMRUT + AMRUT 2.0; urban tap connectivity up 64% since 2011.
### 5. SMART CITIES MISSION
#### 5.1 Selection
- Launched **June 2015** by MoHUA; **100 cities** via competitive challenge.
- Two-stage; **four rounds from January 2016**; last city June 2018.
- First phase (2015-16 to 2019-20): target 100; **100 cities selected**.
- City shares among States: **equal weight to urban population and number of statutory towns** (not districts).
#### 5.2 Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Area-Based Development | Retrofitting/redevelopment of **at least 50 acres** |
| Pan-City | Tech solutions; Integrated Command and Control Centres |
| SPV | One per city; company under **Companies Act, 2013**; **50:50** equity of State/UT and ULB |
| CITIIS 2.0 (2023) | **18 smart cities**; integrated waste management; partners **AFD, KfW, EU, NIUA** |
#### 5.3 Financing
- Centre **Rs 48,000 crore** (Rs 100 crore per city per year, ~Rs 500 crore per city), matched by State/ULB.
- Other sources: ULB own funds, Finance Commission grants, municipal bonds, PPP, loans, convergence.
- **Water and sewage financed through property tax**.
- Progress (11 Jul 2025): Rs 47,459 crore (**99%**) of central share claimed; mission extended to **2025-26**.
### 6. HRIDAY – HERITAGE CITIES
- Started **21 January 2015** by **MoHUA**; urban planning, economic development and heritage conservation.
- **12 cities**: Ajmer, **Amaravati (AP)**, Amritsar, Badami, Dwarka, Gaya, Kanchipuram, Mathura, Puri, Varanasi, Velankanni, **Warangal**.
!TRAP **Agra is NOT** among the 12 HRIDAY cities. Telugu-state HRIDAY cities = Amaravati and Warangal.
### 7. URBAN HOUSING – PMAY-U, PMAY-U 2.0, GHTC-INDIA
#### 7.1 PMAY-U (2015) Verticals
| Vertical | Type |
|---|---|
| **ISSR** – In-situ Slum Redevelopment (land as resource) | CSS |
| **CLSS** – Credit Linked Subsidy | **Central Sector** |
| **AHP** – Affordable Housing in Partnership | CSS |
| **BLC** – Beneficiary-Led Construction | CSS |
#### 7.2 PMAY-U 2.0 (2024)
- **1 crore more** urban houses.
- Verticals: **BLC, AHP, Affordable Rental Housing, Interest Subsidy Scheme**; **ISSR not in 2.0**.
- Sanctions: 1.25 crore+ houses under PMAY-U and 2.0.
#### 7.3 GHTC-India and Light House Projects
- **GHTC-India** (MoHUA, 2021 in PYQ): innovative, sustainable, eco-friendly, **disaster-resilient** construction technologies from across the globe.
- **Light House Projects** (foundation Jan 2021): Indore, Rajkot, Chennai, Ranchi, Agartala, Lucknow.
!TRAP CLSS = the only Central Sector vertical; ISSR, AHP, BLC = CSS.
### 8. URBAN FUNDS – UIDF AND URBAN CHALLENGE FUND
| Item | UIDF |
|---|---|
| Announced | **Union Budget 2023-24** |
| Manager | **National Housing Bank** (not NABARD) |
| Eligible cities | **Tier-2 and Tier-3** |
| Source | Banks' **priority sector lending shortfall**, not G-Sec bonds |
| Size | About **Rs 10,000 crore a year** |
