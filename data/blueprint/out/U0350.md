## U0350 | CENTRAL INFORMATION COMMISSION AND STATE INFORMATION COMMISSIONS; CBI AND ITS LEGAL BASIS
### 1. STATUS AND ORIGIN – CBI, CVC, CIC
| Body | Set up | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **CBI** | **1 April 1963**, **MHA resolution** | **Neither constitutional nor statutory**; powers from **DSPE Act, 1946** |
| **CVC** | **1964**, executive resolution on **Santhanam Committee** advice | **Statutory** only from **CVC Act, 2003**; not constitutional |
| **CIC** | **12 Oct 2005**, RTI Act **Section 12** | Statutory; **CIC + up to 10 ICs**; civil-court powers |
- Sequence: **Special Police Establishment (1941)** → **DSPE Act (1946)** → **CBI (1963)** → **CVC (1964)**.
- **Santhanam Committee on Prevention of Corruption (1962-64)**: chair **K. Santhanam** (parliamentarian) with four other MPs and two senior officers.
!TRAP CBI = 1963, executive resolution, still non-statutory; CVC = 1964, made statutory only in 2003. The CVC was at first neither constitutional nor statutory.
### 2. WHO PICKS WHOM – APPOINTING AUTHORITIES
| Post | Appointed by | Committee |
|---|---|---|
| **Chief Information Commissioner / ICs** | **President** | PM, LoP in Lok Sabha, Union Cabinet Minister nominated by PM (**no CJI**) |
| **State CIC / State ICs** | **Governor** | **CM**, LoP in Assembly, Cabinet Minister nominated by CM |
| **Central Vigilance Commissioner** | President | PM, **Union Home Minister**, LoP in Lok Sabha |
| **CBI Director** | Central Government | PM, LoP in Lok Sabha (or leader of largest opposition party), **CJI or SC judge** nominated by him |
| CEC and ECs (2023 Act) | President | PM, Union Cabinet Minister, LoP (no CJI) |
!KEY APPSC 2025: CIC by **President**, SIC by **Governor**; SIC panel = CM + LoP + CM-nominated minister – the Chief Justice is NOT a member.
### 3. INFORMATION COMMISSIONS UNDER THE RTI ACT
#### 3.1 CIC and SIC
- CIC constituted **2005** under **Section 12**; only body that can declare an organisation a "**public authority**".
- **RTI (Amendment) Act, 2019**: the **Centre fixes tenure and salaries** of CIC and SIC members; **2019 Rules** fixed the term at **3 years**.
- Earlier: State Chief IC pay = **Election Commissioner's** pay.
- **Removal of State CIC/IC (Section 17)**: by the **Governor** only after a **Supreme Court** inquiry on the Governor's reference finds proved misbehaviour or incapacity; direct removal on grounds such as conviction.
- **AP Information Commission**: State Chief Information Commissioner **R. Mahaboob Basha** (2025).
#### 3.2 RTI Sections
| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| **5** | **Public Information Officers** designated by **each public authority** (not by President or Governor) |
| **7** | Reply in **30 days**; life or liberty – **48 hours**; **free for BPL** |
| **8(1)(j)** | Personal information; **DPDP Act 2023** made it **fully exempt** and removed the public-interest override |
| **12** | Constitution of the CIC |
| **18** | **Complaints** – separate from appeals; no time limit |
| **19** | **First appeal** within the public authority; **second appeal** to CIC/SIC within **90 days** |
| **20** | **Penalty** on the PIO: **₹250 per day**, maximum **₹25,000**, after a hearing |
#### 3.3 RTI Dilution Debate
- **Economic Survey 2025-26**: called for RTI Act "**re-examination**", "**ministerial veto**", exemptions for service records and draft policy talks.
### 4. CBI – LEGAL BASIS AND DIRECTOR
- **Founder Director: D.P. Kohli** (APPSC 2018); annual D.P. Kohli Memorial Lecture.
- Attached office under the **Ministry of Personnel (DoPT)**.
- **Director**: minimum tenure **2 years**, extendable **one year at a time up to 5 years** (**2021**; same for ED Director).
- PM-headed selection committee brought by the **Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act, 2013** (until 2014, DSPE Act alone).
- **Director of Prosecution**: not below Joint Secretary rank, appointed on **CVC recommendation**, **2-year** term; SP and above chosen by a **CVC-led** committee.
- CBI = "**caged parrot**"; **Second ARC (2007)**: new law to replace the DSPE Act.
### 5. VINEET NARAIN CASE (1997)
- **Vineet Narain v Union of India (1997)**: quashed the "**Single Directive**" (prior sanction to probe senior officers).
- Gave the **CVC superintendence over the CBI**; fixed **2-year tenure** for the Director.
- Did **not** give investigation decisions to the CVC – **CBI alone decides** whether to investigate.
- DSPE Act revised in **2003** accordingly.
### 6. CBI UNITS, MANDATE AND PLATFORMS
- Mandate: **corruption, economic and special crimes**, mainly **Central Government and UT employees** and their PSUs; no constitutional bar on probing economic offences.
| Unit / platform | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **OCSAE** Prevention/Investigation Unit (2019) | Against **online child sexual abuse** material (APPSC 2018) |
| **BHARATPOL** | Developed by the CBI; launched **7 January 2025** by Home Minister **Amit Shah**; real-time **Interpol** assistance requests for central and State police |
| Interpol | CBI is India's **National Central Bureau** for Interpol |
| Economic offences | **Bank Securities and Fraud** cells |
### 7. CBI ACCOUNTABILITY – GENERAL CONSENT AND LOKPAL
- **Section 6, DSPE Act**: CBI needs the State's **general or specific consent**, since **police** is a **State List** subject (**Entry 2**).
- **Over 9 States** withdrew general consent; **AP withdrew Nov 2018**, restored **June 2019**.
- **Lokpal**: superintendence and direction over the CBI only in cases it refers.
### 8. CENTRAL VIGILANCE COMMISSION
- **Central Vigilance Commissioner + up to 2 Vigilance Commissioners**; appointed by the **President**; term **4 years or age 65**.
- **Superintendence over the CBI** for **Prevention of Corruption Act** cases; Centre's office for corruption inquiries (APPSC 2007).
- **Competent authority** under the **Whistle Blowers Protection Act**.
- Does **not** conduct trials of CBI cases.
- CVC annual report (2 Sep 2026): **755** corruption cases pending investigation, including **13 Lokpal references**.
### 9. ENFORCEMENT AGENCIES AND CORRUPTION INDEX
| Agency | Function | Ministry |
|---|---|---|
| **Enforcement Directorate** | **PMLA 2002**, **FEMA 1999**; attaches proceeds of crime; founded **1956** as "Enforcement Unit" under Dept of Economic Affairs | **Finance – Dept of Revenue** |
| **DRI** | Anti-smuggling, customs evasion | Finance – Dept of Revenue |
| **CBI** | Corruption, economic and special crimes | **Personnel (DoPT)** |
| **CEIB** | Nodal economic intelligence | Finance |
- **PMLA**: the **Director** can attach property believed to be proceeds of crime (APPSC G1 2022).
- **Corruption Perceptions Index**: published by **Transparency International**; scores perceived public-sector corruption 0-100. **CPI 2025** (10 Feb 2026): **India 91st of 182**, score **39** (96th, 38 in 2024).
- **Vohra Committee (1993)**: criminal-politician nexus.
!TRAP ED and DRI = Finance; CBI = Personnel.
### 10. ANDHRA PRADESH – ACB AND VIGILANCE COMMISSION
- **AP ACB**: State police agency for **PC Act** offences.
- **State Vigilance Commission (1964)**: took over part of the SPSC's disciplinary role.

### 13. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. CBI is **not statutory**; its powers come from the DSPE Act 1946.
2. **1963 vs 1964**: CBI vs CVC.
3. CVC panel has the **Home Minister**; CIC panel a **PM-nominated Cabinet Minister**; CBI panel the **CJI**.
4. SIC is appointed by the **Governor**; the Chief Justice is not on the panel.
5. Vineet Narain gave the CVC **superintendence**, not the power to decide investigations.
6. CBI is under **DoPT**, ED and DRI under **Finance**.
7. CBI needs **State consent** (Section 6, DSPE) because police is State List Entry 2.
