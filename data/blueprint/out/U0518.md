## U0518 | FOREIGN TRADE POLICY - OBJECTIVES, INCENTIVES, EOUS; TRADE FACILITATION AND LOGISTICS COSTS
### 1. TRADE POLICY INSTRUMENTS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Import quota** | **Quantitative restriction** on the amount of imports (PYQ 2019) |
| **Tariff** | Tax on imported goods (price-based, like countervailing and anti-dumping duties) |
| **Import substitution** | Inward-looking; produce substitutes at home to **reduce import dependence**; ran about **four decades** |
| **Export promotion** | Outward-looking; push manufactured exports |
#### 1.1 Stages of economic integration
| Stage | Feature |
|---|---|
| FTA | Free internal trade; members keep **own external tariffs** |
| **Customs union** | Free internal trade + **common external tariff** |
| **Common market** | Customs union + **free movement of factors** |
| **Economic union** | Common market + **coordinated economic policies** |
### 2. HISTORY - EXIM POLICY TO FOREIGN TRADE POLICY
| Year | Step |
|---|---|
| **1985-88** | **First EXIM Policy** |
| 1991 | QRs → **price-based regime** |
| **1992** | Partial current-account convertibility: **60% at market rate, 40% at official rate**; raw materials and capital goods under OGL |
| 2000 | EXIM Policy announced **31 March 2000** (1997-2002 framework) |
| **2004** | EXIM Policy → **Foreign Trade Policy** (FTP 2004-09 = FTP of the question period) |
| FTP 2015-20 | Extended (COVID-19, geopolitics) till **31 March 2023** |
| **2023** | FTP 2023 from **1 April 2023**, **no fixed end date** |
- Administered by the **Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industry**, through **DGFT**.
### 3. FOREIGN TRADE POLICY 2023
- Export target: **$2 trillion by 2030**. Board of Trade (Jul 2026): Commerce Minister **Piyush Goyal** called a **$1 trillion** exports goal "difficult, yet possible" (4 Jul 2026).
- Approach: shift from **incentives to remission** and entitlement-based support; **e-initiatives**.
- **Districts as Export Hubs**; new **Towns of Export Excellence**.
- **Rupee trade settlement**: international trade in INR; **merchanting trade** promoted.
- **One-time amnesty** for export-obligation defaults under **EPCG and Advance Authorisation**.
!TRAP FTP 2023 has NO end date; FTP target $2 trillion by 2030 is different from the $1 trillion goal mentioned in July 2026.
### 4. EXPORT INCENTIVE SCHEMES
| Scheme | Replaced / objective |
|---|---|
| **MEIS** | Goods exports reward (FTP 2015-20) |
| **SEIS** | Services exports reward (FTP 2015-20); discontinued |
| **RoDTEP** | **Replaced MEIS**; refunds unrebated central, state and local taxes (electricity duty, VAT on transport fuel); run online by **CBIC** |
| **RoSCTL** | **Replaced RoSL**; rebate for apparel and made-ups |
| **EPCG** | Export Promotion **Capital Goods**: zero-duty import of capital goods against an export obligation |
| **Advance Authorisation** | Duty-free import of **inputs** for export production |
| **Duty drawback** | Refund of customs (and excise) duty on inputs and service tax on input services used in exports - **not income tax** |
- Budget 2026-27: Advance Authorisation export period for leather/textile garments and footwear **6 months → 1 year**.
!PAIR EPCG | capital goods || Advance Authorisation | inputs
#### 4.1 New missions
- **Export Promotion Mission**: **₹25,060 crore**, FY 2025-26 to 2030-31, run by **DGFT**; merges **Interest Equalisation + Market Access Initiative**; for **MSMEs and first-time exporters**.
- **RELIEF Scheme 2026**: **₹497 crore** via **ECGC**; war-risk and logistics costs in **West Asia**; Egypt, Jordan added.
- West Asia conflict disrupted cargo → procedural flexibility in export authorisations (3 Mar 2026).
### 5. TRADE PROMOTION AND EXPORT CREDIT BODIES
| Body | Year / role |
|---|---|
| **DGFT** | Attached office of Dept of Commerce, **Vanijya Bhavan, New Delhi**; FTP authorisations; runs DEH and EPM |
| **ECGC** | **1957**, wholly government-owned; **export credit-risk insurance** |
| **EXIM Bank** | **EXIM Bank Act 1981**; set up **1 Jan 1982, Mumbai**; lines of credit, buyer's/seller's credit, project-export finance; **not insurance** |
| **STC** | **1956**; canalising agency for bulk goods; not insurance |
| **MMTC** | Minerals and metals; **largest trading government company** |
| **IBEF** | India Brand Equity Foundation, **1996**, trust of Dept of Commerce; promotes the India Brand |
| EPCs / Commodity Boards | Sector export promotion; channel MAI support |
- Channelise exports and imports: **MMTC, EXIM Bank, STC, FCI**. Promote foreign trade: ECGC, STC, MMTC (all).
- **IFFCO** is NOT an agricultural exporter (fertilisers); NAFED, STC, MMTC export agri goods.
- **NIRVIK** (Niryat Rin Vikas Yojana, Budget 2020-21; 2019 scheme): higher ECGC insurance cover, lower premium for small exporters.
#### 5.1 Status holders
- Present: **One-Star to Five-Star Export Houses** by export performance.
- Older ascending order: Export House → Trading House → **Star Trading House** → Super Star Trading House. High-quality export performance = Star Trading Houses.
### 6. AGRI-EXPORT PROMOTION
- **APEDA**: **APEDA Act, December 1985**, in force **13 Feb 1986**; under the **Ministry of Commerce and Industry** (not Agriculture); nodal agency for **Agri Export Zones**.
- **Agriculture Export Policy 2018** (Cabinet, Dec 2018): double agri exports to about **$60 bn by 2022**.
- First **Agriculture Export Facilitation Centre**: **Pune, May 2021**.
### 7. EOUs, EHTP, STP AND SEZ
| Unit | Covers / nature |
|---|---|
| **SEZ** | Duty-free **enclave** treated as **foreign territory**; **SEZ Act 2005**; 100% FDI automatic for most manufacturing |
| **EOU** | Goods generally |
| **EHTP** | Electronics hardware |
| **STP** | Software |
- EOU/EHTP/STP: **export all output**, **located anywhere**, duty-free inputs.
- Budget 2026-27: measures to use idle SEZ manufacturing capacity.
!TRAP SEZ = enclave; EOU/EHTP/STP = can be located anywhere.
### 8. DISTRICTS AS EXPORT HUBS - AP ANGLE
- Each district's plan prioritises **2-3 high-potential products/services**; products identified in **734 districts**, action plans for **590** (2025).
- 90-day DEH campaign from **1 June 2026**: AP priority districts **Ananthapur, Chittoor, East Godavari, Guntur, Krishna, Visakhapatnam**.
- CM **Chandrababu Naidu** sought regulation of fish-meal exports and export incentives for **aquaculture and tobacco** (21 Jul 2026).
### 9. EXPORT-IMPORT PROCEDURE AND DOCUMENTS
| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Indent** | Importer's order to buy goods |
| **Letter of credit** | Produced by the **importer**; issuing bank promises payment to exporter (beneficiary) |
| **Shipping bill** | Customs clearing document for export goods |
| **Mate's receipt** | Ship's mate acknowledges goods **loaded on board** |
| **Bill of lading** | Shipping company's receipt and **document of title** |
- **Rules of origin**: fix a product's **national source**; tell customs which duties, rules and fees apply; decide FTA concession eligibility (BPSC 2023: all statements correct).
