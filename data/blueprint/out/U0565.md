## U0565 | AGRO-CLIMATIC ZONES OF ANDHRA PRADESH AND THEIR CROPPING SUITABILITY
### 1. NUMBER OF ZONES
- Residual AP has **six agro-climatic zones**: North Coastal, Godavari, Krishna, Southern, Scarce Rainfall, **High Altitude and Tribal Areas**.
- Undivided AP (NARP scheme): **7 zones**, including North Telangana and Southern Telangana zones; Krishna and Godavari formed one **Krishna-Godavari** zone.
- Naming hook: rivers (Godavari, Krishna); direction (North Coastal, Southern); physical conditions (Scarce Rainfall, High Altitude).
!TRAP Not the six **Article 371-D** zones (administrative zones for public employment).
### 2. ZONES AND DISTRICTS
| # | Zone | Districts (13 old districts) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | North Coastal | Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam |
| 2 | Godavari | East Godavari, West Godavari |
| 3 | Krishna | Krishna, Guntur, Prakasam |
| 4 | Southern | Chittoor, YSR Kadapa, **SPS Nellore** |
| 5 | Scarce Rainfall | **Kurnool**, Anantapuramu |
| 6 | High Altitude and Tribal | Hill and tribal parts of Srikakulam, Visakhapatnam, East Godavari |
- Zones are still listed against the **13 old districts**; not redrawn after the **April 2022** reorganisation (AP now **28 districts**, Aug 2026).
### 3. SOILS, CROPS AND ANGRAU RESEARCH STATIONS
| Zone | Soils / setting | Crops | RARS |
|---|---|---|---|
| North Coastal | High rainfall, **laterite** soils | Plantation crops, paddy | **Anakapalle** |
| Godavari | Fertile **deltaic alluvium** | "**Rice bowl**", aquaculture hub | **Maruteru** (not Rajahmundry) |
| Krishna | Deltaic and coastal plains | Paddy, **chillies**, cotton | **Lam (Guntur)** |
| Southern | Varied: plains to hills | Mixed dryland and irrigated | Tirupati |
| Scarce Rainfall | Red and black soils | Groundnut, dryland crops | **Nandyal** |
| High Altitude and Tribal | Hill soils | Coffee, plantation crops | **Chintapalle** |
- AP lies in the **Southern cotton zone** with Telangana and Karnataka.
### 4. RAINFALL AND DROUGHT
| Zone | Annual rainfall |
|---|---|
| North Coastal | 1,000-1,100 mm |
| Krishna-Godavari | 800-1,100 mm |
| Southern | 700-1,100 mm |
| Scarce Rainfall | **500-750 mm** (lowest) |
| High Altitude and Tribal | above **1,400 mm** (highest) |
- **Drought-prone**: Scarce Rainfall Zone (Kurnool, Anantapuramu). Highest rainfall: North Coastal + tribal highlands.
- State 2024-25: **960.3 mm** vs normal **860.0 mm** = excess **11.7%**.
- **Kharif 2026**: all 28 districts in deficit; worst hit Annamayya, Prakasam, Chittoor, Markapuram, Visakhapatnam (Sep 2026).
### 5. AGRO-ECOLOGICAL REGIONS AND OTHER ZONING SCHEMES
- **Agro-ecological zone** = agro-climatic zone + overlaid **landform** (changes climate and length of growing period).
| Classification | Number |
|---|---|
| Planning Commission agro-climatic zones (1988, 8th Plan) | **15** |
| NBSS&LUP agro-ecological regions / sub-regions | **20 / 60** |
| Sengupta and Sdasyuk (1968) | 4 macro, 11 meso, 60 micro regions |
| Karnataka agro-climatic zones | 10 |
| AP agro-climatic zones | 6 |
- Planning Commission zones are based on soil, climate, rainfall; basis for state-wise agricultural roadmaps (Apr 2026).
- Sengupta and Sdasyuk: macro = climate, meso = physiography, micro = crop combinations.
### 6. CONTINGENCY CROP PLANNING
- Zones decide region-specific interventions; AP's farm-income disparities follow the six zones.
- District agricultural contingency plans: **ICAR-CRIDA**.
- **PMDS** (pre-monsoon dry sowing) under natural farming uses leftover soil moisture in drought-prone areas (Jul 2026).

### 9. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. Godavari RARS = **Maruteru**, not Rajahmundry.
2. Krishna = **Lam (Guntur)**; Scarce Rainfall = **Nandyal**.
3. "**Low** Altitude and Tribal Zone" does not exist; it is **High** Altitude and Tribal.
4. Kurnool is in the Scarce Rainfall Zone, not Southern.
5. Nellore is in the Southern Zone, not Krishna.
6. Six agro-climatic zones are not the six Article 371-D zones.
7. Zones use 13 old districts, not the 28 new ones.
8. India has 15 agro-climatic zones but 20 agro-ecological regions.
