## U0680 | ANDHRA PRADESH – MAJOR INDUSTRIES, INDUSTRIAL NODES AND CORRIDORS
### 1. INDUSTRIAL CORRIDORS THROUGH AP
- AP (through **APIIC**) develops three corridors under the **National Industrial Corridor Development Programme**.
- AP is the **only State with nodes on all three corridors**: **VCIC, CBIC, HBIC** (PYQ 2025).
| Corridor | AP node(s) and district | Key data |
|---|---|---|
| **VCIC** (Visakhapatnam-Chennai, ~800 km) | Visakhapatnam (Rambilli, Nakkapalli - Anakapalli); Chittoor (**Srikalahasti-Yerpedu**, Naidupeta); **Kopparthy** (YSR Kadapa) | 3 nodes, 33,000 acres; **ADB** funded; Kopparthy Phase-I **2,596 acres, ₹1,264 crore** |
| **CBIC** (Chennai-Bengaluru, 560 km; AP, TN, Karnataka) | **Krishnapatnam (North)** - KRIS City | 13,919.67 acres (Survey 2025-26); start-up ₹2,139 crore |
| **HBIC** (Hyderabad-Bengaluru) | **Orvakal** (Kurnool) | 9,800 acres (Survey 2025-26) |
- **NICDIT-funded nodes:** Kopparthy, Krishnapatnam, Orvakal.
- **Kopparthy + Orvakal:** approved by **CCEA on 28 Aug 2024** among **12 new industrial smart cities**; funds promised in **Union Budget 2024-25** (PYQ G2 2025).
- VCIC long-term nodes kept for later phases: **Donakonda** (Prakasam) and **Machilipatnam**.
- **Budget 2026-27:** AP named a **Rare Earth corridor**.
!TRAP VCIC → Vizag, Chittoor, Kopparthy; CBIC → Krishnapatnam; HBIC → Orvakal.
### 2. VCIC – FEATURES AND ADB FUNDING
- **India's first coastal corridor**; first phase of the **East Coast Economic Corridor** (Kolkata-Kanyakumari).
- Links India to **ASEAN and East Asia**: **Act East + Make in India**; aligned with Golden Quadrilateral (NH-16).
- **North:** metallurgy, pharma, petrochemicals, IT. **South:** food processing, leather, automobiles.
| ADB programme (US$ mn) | Policy-based loan | Project loan | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| ADB | 125 (100%) | 500 (70%) | **625** |
| Govt of AP | 0 | 215 (30%) | **215** |
| Total | 125 | 715 (70:30) | **840** |
- **Policy-based loan:** reforms, institutions, skills. **Project loan:** roads, power, water, parks.
- **Executing agency:** Department of Industries and Commerce; **PMU** under Commissioner of Industries.
!KEY PYQ G1 2022: VCIC is **partly funded by an ADB loan**, not JICA.

### 7. APIIC, SEZs, PCPIR AND PORTS
- **APIIC (1973):** nodal agency for industrial land, parks and SEZs; **PPP** model; after **1991** moved from direct manufacturer to **facilitator**.
- Managed ~571 parks earlier; Survey 2025-26: **635 clusters** (331 parks, 304 estates).
- **SEZs (2023):** 47 or more notified; **10 by APIIC**.
- **APSEZ:** multi-product SEZ at **Atchutapuram-Rambilli** (Anakapalli); notified **12 Apr 2007**; ~3,213 acres; part of PCPIR.
- **Budget 2026 SEZ reform:** SEZ units can sell in India at concessional tax.
- **Four PCPIRs:** **Visakhapatnam-Kakinada**, Dahej, Paradip, Cuddalore-Nagapattinam. AP PCPIR approved 2009, ~640 sq km; includes APSEZ and Kakinada SEZ (petrochemicals).
- **PCPIR Policy 2020-35:** minimum area **250 → 50 sq km**; up to **20% VGF**; target ₹10 lakh crore (₹20 lakh crore by 2035).
- Private PPP ports **Krishnapatnam + Gangavaram** drew heavy industry to the coast.
### 8. AP INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT POLICY 4.0 (2024-29)
- Replaced the **Industrial Development Policy 2023-27**; covers large, mega, ultra-mega units.
- Notified **26 Oct 2024**: **G.O. 68** (industry), **G.O. 69** (MSME).
- Companion policies: Food Processing, Private Industrial Parks, **Aerospace and Defence (2025-30)**.
| Category | FCI (excluding land) |
|---|---|
| Sub-Large | ₹125-500 crore |
| Large | ₹501-1,000 crore |
| Mega | ₹1,001-5,000 crore |
| Ultra Mega | above ₹5,000 crore |
| Incentive | Rate |
|---|---|
| Capital subsidy | **12%** (Sub-Large/Large); **15%** (Mega/Ultra Mega); +5% women/SC/ST/BC |
| **Net SGST reimbursement** | **100% for 5 years** |
- **Targets:** ₹30 lakh crore investment; 5 lakh jobs. **Electronics by 2029:** US$ 50 bn production, 5 lakh jobs.
!TRAP G.O. 68 = Industrial Development Policy; G.O. 69 = MSME.
### 9. MSMEs AND CLUSTERS
- **MSME Policy 4.0:** **"One Family, One Entrepreneur" by 2030**; capital subsidy **25%** general, **45%** special categories (caps ₹25 lakh / ₹1.5 crore / ₹7 crore).
- Udyam: about **15.4 lakh** registered MSMEs (Mar 2025).
- Udyam portal (from **1 Jul 2020**): **36,80,310** MSMEs registered in AP as on **15 Dec 2025**; district figures include **Anantapur 2,37,332** and **Prakasam 1,93,178**.
- **AP Cluster Development Programme:** G.O. 17, 10 Jan 2026; 45 MSME clusters/CFCs, ₹200 crore, up to 80% aid.
