## U0456 | NITI AAYOG – STRUCTURE, MANDATE, VERTICALS, DIFFERENCES FROM THE PLANNING COMMISSION
### 1. NITI AAYOG – BASICS AND STATUS
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Full form | **National Institution for Transforming India** |
| Set up | **1 January 2015** |
| Mode | **Union Cabinet Resolution** (executive), not an Act |
| Replaced | **Planning Commission** (15 March 1950; dissolved 2014) |
| Status | **Non-constitutional, non-statutory**; apex public **policy think tank**; advisory |
| Chairperson | **Prime Minister** (ex officio) – no full-time Chairman |
- A **think tank, not a fund allocator**.
- **Finance Commission (Article 280), CAG, NCBC** = constitutional; NITI is not.
- **Zonal Councils** = **statutory** (States Reorganisation Act, 1956); chaired by the **Union Home Minister**; a NITI representative attends as adviser.
!TRAP NITI was set up in **2015** – a printed key showing "2012" is wrong. How it was made (Cabinet resolution) does not explain why it was made.
### 2. COMPOSITION
| Post | Appointment / provision |
|---|---|
| **Chairperson** | Prime Minister (ex officio) |
| **Vice-Chairperson** | **Appointed by the PM**; **Cabinet Minister rank** |
| **CEO** | Appointed by the PM; fixed tenure; **rank of Secretary** to GoI |
| **Full-time members** | Appointed by PM; **number not fixed** (**5** at present) |
| **Part-time members** | **Maximum 2**, from universities/research bodies; ex officio, **rotational** |
| **Ex-officio members** | **Maximum 4 Union Ministers** nominated by PM |
| **Special invitees** | Domain experts and practitioners nominated by PM |
- **VC and CEO are separate posts** (not "Vice-Chairman-cum-CEO").
- Members key: **5 full-time + 2 part-time**; the 2015 set-up had 3 full-time – number changes with each reconstitution.
- Full-time member **Rajiv Gauba** (former Cabinet Secretary) in the reconstituted panel (Apr 2026); **Joram Aniya** and **R. Balasubramaniam** reported as new full-time members (Jun 2026).
!PAIR Part-time | maximum 2 | rotational || Ex-officio | maximum 4 | Union Ministers

### 4. MANDATE AND OBJECTIVES
#### 4.1 Four Mandate Areas
1. **Knowledge and innovation**
2. **NITI for States** (cooperative and competitive federalism)
3. **Driving transformational change**
4. **Monitoring and evaluation**
#### 4.2 Objectives in the Cabinet Resolution
- **Shared vision** of national priorities with active involvement of States.
- **Cooperative federalism**: structured support to States – strong States make a strong nation.
- **Village-level planning**: credible plans at village level, aggregated progressively upwards.
- National security in economic policy; partnerships with think tanks; knowledge, innovation and entrepreneurial support; monitoring and evaluation; capacity building.
#### 4.3 Guiding Approach
- **Bottom-up**; **cooperative ("marble-cake") federalism**; **Antyodaya**; inclusive, participatory development; focus on **small enterprises** (not sole preference for large enterprises).
- **State rankings** (e.g. SDG India Index) → **competitive federalism**.
!KEY Assertion-Reason (G2 Mains 2025): A (non-constitutional advisory body) and R (village plans aggregated upwards) are **both true, but R does not explain A**.
### 5. GOVERNING COUNCIL AND REGIONAL COUNCILS
#### 5.1 Governing Council
- **Chairperson:** Prime Minister.
- **Members:** **Chief Ministers of all States**; **CMs of UTs with legislatures (Delhi, Puducherry)**; **Lieutenant Governors** of other UTs; VC, full-time members, ex-officio members, special invitees.
- **Governors of States are NOT members.**
- First constituted **Feb 2015**; reconstituted **Feb 2021**; took over the work of the **NDC** (now inactive).
- No statutory frequency; usually **once a year**; **11 meetings** so far.
| Meeting | Date | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| 10th | 2025 | "Viksit Rajya for Viksit Bharat@2047" |
| **11th** | **11 Jun 2026** | "**Inclusive Human Development for Viksit Bharat@2047**"; **100% attendance – all 28 CMs** for the first time |
#### 5.2 Regional Councils
- For issues affecting **more than one State**; **convened by the PM**.
- Headed jointly by a **rotational CM** of the group and a **Central Minister**; ministers, secretaries, domain experts, with a support cell in NITI.

### 8. PLANNING COMMISSION VS NITI AAYOG
| Aspect | Planning Commission (1950) | NITI Aayog (2015) |
|---|---|---|
| Set up | 15 March 1950, Cabinet resolution; first chairman **Nehru** | 1 January 2015, Cabinet resolution |
| Funds | **Allocated plan funds and grants** | **No fund-allocation power**; advisory |
| Approach | **Top-down**, one-size-fits-all plans | **Bottom-up**; Vision-Strategy-Action Agenda |
| States' role | Limited to **NDC** and annual plan talks | **Every CM** on the Governing Council |
| Members | **Up to 8 full-time**; **no part-time** | Full-time + up to 2 part-time |
| Top officials | Deputy Chairman + Member-Secretary | Vice-Chairperson + CEO |
- Planning Commission was **never statutory or constitutional**; first chairman was Nehru (not Patel / Rajendra Prasad); its plans went to the **NDC** for approval.
- Other facts asked alongside: **Finance Commission** set up every five years; **RBI nationalised 1 January 1949**.
- Criticism: central dominance; some states skip meetings.
