## U0415 | RIGHTS OF THE ELDERLY – SENIOR CITIZENS ACT 2007 AND SCHEMES
### 1. MAINTENANCE AND WELFARE OF PARENTS AND SENIOR CITIZENS ACT, 2007
#### 1.1 Enactment and Obligations
- Enacted in **2007**; nodal ministry **MoSJE** (Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment).
- Brought into force **state by state** from **29 December 2007** (not one date for all).
- Creates a **legal obligation** on adult **children (biological, adopted, step)** and **legal heirs** to maintain parents and senior citizens who cannot maintain themselves.
- The Act **punishes neglect** of old parents by children.
- **Abandonment** of a senior citizen: up to **3 months** jail and/or **Rs 5,000** fine.
- **Old age homes:** at least **one per district**.
#### 1.2 Key Sections
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Section 20** | **Medical care** of senior citizens; basis of the **NPHCE** |
| **Section 23** | Property transfer/gift made on condition of care can be declared **void** (cancelled) by the Tribunal if the transferee neglects the parent/senior citizen |
#### 1.3 Tribunals (AP)
| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Maintenance Tribunal** | **Sub-division** level; in AP headed by **RDO / Sub-Collector** |
| **Appellate Tribunal** | **District** level; in AP headed by the **District Collector** |
| Maximum maintenance | **Rs 10,000 per month** |
| Time limit | Case decided within **90 days** |
| Appeal | Allowed, to the Appellate Tribunal |
!NUM Sec 23 = property; Sec 20 = medical care; cap Rs 10,000; 90 days; abandonment 3 months / Rs 5,000
#### 1.4 2019 Amendment Bill and Earlier State Law
- **2019 Amendment Bill:** proposed **removing the Rs 10,000 cap** and covering **in-laws and grandchildren**; it **lapsed** (not passed).
- **First State law:** **Himachal Pradesh** Maintenance of Parents and Dependents Act, **2001**.
!TRAP The 2019 Bill was never passed; the Rs 10,000 ceiling still stands.

### 3. CENTRAL SCHEMES FOR THE ELDERLY
#### 3.1 Scheme Table
| Scheme | Ministry / agency | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| **IGNOAPS** (under NSAP) | **MoRD** | Rs 200/month (60-79 years), Rs 500 (80+), BPL; Centre's share **frozen at Rs 200 since 2007**; about 2.2 crore beneficiaries (June 2026) |
| **Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana** | MoSJE; **ALIMCO** | Free **physical aids** (walking sticks, wheelchairs, hearing aids, dentures) to BPL seniors (60+, or income up to Rs 15,000/month); funded from the **Senior Citizens' Welfare Fund** (unclaimed deposits); at least **30% women** per district; launched **1 April 2017 at Nellore, AP** |
| **AVYAY** (Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana) | MoSJE | **April 2021**, renamed from National Action Plan for Senior Citizens; umbrella of **4**: Integrated Programme for Senior Citizens, State Action Plan, RVY, Elder Line; Rs 287.81 crore utilised (ES 2025-26) |
| **PMVVY** (PM Vaya Vandana Yojana) | Ministry of Finance; **LIC** | **2017**; assured pension **8% for 10 years**; max purchase **Rs 7.5 lakh** at launch (raised to **Rs 15 lakh** in 2018); max pension Rs 5,000/month (at launch); GST-exempt; closed for new subscription after 31 March 2023 |
| **Senior Citizen Savings Scheme** | India Post / banks | Safe fixed-interest savings for seniors |
| **Ayushman Vay Vandana card** | MoHFW (AB-PMJAY) | Health cover of **Rs 5 lakh** for **all aged 70+**, irrespective of income (2024) |
!PAIR PMVVY | Pension | LIC || Vay Vandana | Health, 70+ | AB-PMJAY || Vayoshri | Aids | ALIMCO
#### 3.2 Programmes under AVYAY
- **IPOP / Integrated Programme for Senior Citizens (IPSrC):** up to **90%** of project cost to **NGOs** for old-age homes, day-care centres, mobile medicare units.
- **SAGE** (Seniorcare Ageing Growth Engine): up to **Rs 1 crore** equity for elder-care start-ups (Govt stake below 49%) (Aug 2026).
!TRAP AVYAY components are IPSrC, State Action Plan, RVY and Elder Line. **National Family Benefit Scheme** is an NSAP component, not AVYAY.
### 4. ELDER LINE – NATIONAL HELPLINE
- **Elder Line 14567** – National Helpline for Senior Citizens; under **MoSJE**, run with State emergency response centres.
- Component of **AVYAY**; technical support from **Tata Trusts** and **NSE Foundation**.
- Not limited to pensions.
### 5. NSAP AND STATE OLD-AGE PENSIONS
#### 5.1 National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP)
- Launched **15 August 1995**; ministry **MoRD**.
- Five components:
1. **IGNOAPS** – old-age pension (BPL, 60+).
2. **IGNWPS** – widow pension (BPL widows 40-79).
3. **IGNDPS** – disability pension (80% or more disability).
4. **NFBS** – lump sum on death of the primary breadwinner (Rs 20,000).
5. **Annapurna** – **10 kg free food grains** a month to seniors eligible for but not getting IGNOAPS.
#### 5.2 State Schemes
| Scheme | State | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Mukhyamantri Vriddhajan Pension Yojana** | **Bihar** (2019) | Universal: all aged 60+ with no other pension; Rs 400 (60-79), Rs 500 (80+); no income bar |
| **NTR Bharosa Pension** (earlier YSR Pension Kanuka) | **Andhra Pradesh** | Old-age pension raised from **Rs 3,000 to Rs 4,000/month** (G.O. 43, 13 June 2024); minimum age **60** (ST **50**) |
- AP: 7 physiotherapy centres under the National Action Plan for Senior Citizens treated 87,240 patients (APSES 2024-25).
### 6. DEMOGRAPHIC AGEING
- **LASI** (Longitudinal Ageing Study in India), Wave 1 **2017-18**, by **IIPS**, Mumbai, under MoHFW; report released **2021**.
- Covered about **72,250** persons aged **45+** and their spouses.
- **India Ageing Report 2023** – by **UNFPA** with **IIPS**: elderly to **exceed children (0-14) by 2046**; key challenges **feminisation** of ageing (more elderly widows), **ruralisation**, and poverty among the old.
### 7. ELDER ABUSE AND RIGHTS ISSUES
- **HelpAge India:** runs elderly-care services; releases **elder-abuse surveys**.
- **Property right:** under **Section 23**, the Tribunal can **cancel a property transfer** if the child/heir stops care.
- AP: Senior Citizens Welfare Association flagged rising abuse and **cyber fraud** against elders living alone.
- **Shyam Saran Negi** (Kalpa, Kinnaur, HP) – honoured on **12 June 2010** by CEC **Navin Chawla**.
