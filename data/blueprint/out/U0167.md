## U0167 | ANGLO-MYSORE WARS I–IV – HAIDAR ALI, TIPU SULTAN, TREATY OF MANGALORE AND SRIRANGAPATNAM
### 1. FOUR WARS AT A GLANCE
| War | Years | Mysore leader | British head | Result / treaty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First | **1767–69** | **Haidar Ali** | **Harry Verelst** (Governor of Bengal) | Haidar on top near Madras; **Treaty of Madras (4 April 1769)** |
| Second | **1780–84** | Haidar + Tipu | **Warren Hastings** | Pollilur, Porto Novo; **Treaty of Mangalore (1784)** with **Tipu** |
| Third | **1790–92** | Tipu | **Lord Cornwallis** | **Treaty of Seringapatam (March 1792)**; ended Tipu's supremacy in the south |
| Fourth | **1799** | Tipu | **Lord Wellesley** | Srirangapatna captured; **Tipu killed 4 May 1799** defending it |
!TRAP **Treaty of Madras (1769) = with Haidar**; **Treaty of Mangalore (1784) = with Tipu**.
### 2. HAIDAR ALI
- Rose under the **Wodeyar** kingdom of Mysore; **Faujdar of Dindigul (1755)** under minister **Nanjaraja**.
- Adopted Western military techniques; **modern arsenal/armoury at Dindigul (1755)** with **French help**.
- Mysore army under Venkata Raya had conquered Dindigul in 1742; **18 Palayams** under Dindigul Semai.
- **First war diplomacy:** won over the **Marathas** and detached the **Nizam** from the British.
- **Second war:** formed a front with the **Nizam and the Marathas**.
- **Velu Nachiyar of Sivaganga** recovered her kingdom with Haidar's help; called the **first queen to wage war against the British** (APPSC-G1 2022).
!TRAP Chennamma (Kittur) and Lakshmibai (Jhansi) fought later; Durgavati fought the Mughals.
### 3. TIPU SULTAN – CAPITAL, EMBASSIES, REFORMS
- **Capital: Seringapatam (Srirangapatna)**.
- **Embassies** on modern lines: **France** (for European technology → **built a navy**, modernised army; APPSC-G1 2018), **Ottoman Empire (Constantinople/Istanbul)**, **Afghanistan (Zaman Shah)**, Iran, Arabia, **Mauritius**.
- **Jacobin Club** at Seringapatam (**1797**), founded by French officials; Tipu became a member as **"Citizen Tipu"** and planted a **Tree of Liberty**.
- **Reforms:** introduced **sericulture**; **new calendar, new coinage, new weights and measures**; used **war rockets** (did not discontinue them).
!KEY Dindigul arsenal = **Haidar**; French embassy + navy + Jacobin Club = **Tipu** (not Haidar or the Wodeyars).
### 4. BATTLES AND BRITISH COMMANDERS
| Battle | Date | War | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Siege of Arcot (by Haidar) | 1780 | Second | Before Pollilur |
| **Pollilur** | **10 Sep 1780** | Second | Haidar and Tipu beat **Col. William Baillie** – British disaster |
| **Porto Novo** | **July 1781** | Second | **Sir Eyre Coote** beat Haidar; reversed Pollilur |
| Tipu vs **General Meadows** | 1790 | Third | Tipu beat the British army |
| Bangalore | March 1791 | Third | **Cornwallis** took it via Ambur and Vellore |
| **Seringapatam** | 1792 | Third | Cornwallis's coalition won; Tipu sued for peace |
| **Seringapatam** | 1799 | Fourth | British won; Tipu killed |
- Coote was sent from Bengal with reinforcements and funds by Hastings.
- **Thomas Munro** served under **Hector Munro** and **Sir Eyre Coote** in the early wars.
- 1799 campaign: **General George Harris** and **Arthur Wellesley** (later Duke of Wellington).
### 5. TREATY OF SERINGAPATAM (1792)
- Tipu gave up about **half his territory**, paid **₹3 crore** indemnity, gave **two sons as hostages** (half paid at once; sons returned after the rest).
| Gainer | Share |
|---|---|
| **British** | **Malabar, Coorg** (protectorate), **Dindigul**, **Bara Mahal** (Salem region) |
| **Nizam** | Krishna to beyond the **Pennar**: half of Rayalaseema – **Bellary, Gurramkonda, Gandikota, Kambham, Kadapa** |
| **Marathas** | Around the **Tungabhadra** |
- **Bara Mahal** – where **Read and Thomas Munro** began the **Ryotwari** system.
### 6. FOURTH WAR AND SETTLEMENT OF MYSORE (1799)
- Tipu fortified Seringapatam; killed defending it on **4 May 1799**.
- **Hindu Wodeyar dynasty restored**: boy ruler **Krishnaraja Wodeyar III**, under a **Subsidiary Alliance**.
- Nizam got Gooty and Gurramkonda; the **Nizam's share** → handed to the Company in **1800** to pay for the subsidiary force = **Ceded Districts: Kadapa, Kurnool, Anantapur, Bellary**.
- Removed the French-backed threat; brought Mysore and Andhra (via the Ceded Districts) under the Company.
!TRAP Ceded Districts came in **1800** as a result of the Mysore wars – not the stated significance of the Third War.
### 7. NIZAM AND MARATHAS – SIDE IN EACH WAR
| War | Nizam | Marathas |
|---|---|---|
| First | Joined British, then **detached** by Haidar | Joined against Haidar, then **won over** |
| Second | In the **anti-British confederacy**, later bought off | Allied with Haidar; peace with British at **Salbai (1782)** |
| Third | **With British** (grand alliance) | **With British** |
| Fourth | **With British** (subsidiary ally since 1798) | Gave **no active help** |
!KEY Only the **Third** war had both the Nizam and the Marathas with the British.

### 11. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS

5. "Lion of Mysore" is not Tipu's title.
6. Nizam's 1792 share = Krishna–Pennar; Marathas = Tungabhadra.
7. Only the **Third** war had both Nizam and Marathas with the British.
8. Wodeyar restored in 1799 was **Krishnaraja Wodeyar III**.
