## U0782 | CLIMATE CHANGE – GREENHOUSE EFFECT, GREENHOUSE GASES, RADIATIVE FORCING, EVIDENCE AND IMPACTS
### 1. THE GREENHOUSE EFFECT
- **Greenhouse effect** = natural warming caused by the **absorption and re-radiation of heat** by greenhouse gases; in exam words, a **rise in Earth's temperature due to concentration of gases**.
- Concept proposed by **Joseph Fourier** in the **1820s**. Today the **WMO** is the main body reporting greenhouse gas levels.
- Without it, Earth's average temperature would be about **−18°C** instead of about **+15°C**.
#### 1.1 Mechanism – Step by Step
1. Atmosphere is **transparent** to incoming **short-wave solar radiation**.
2. Earth's surface re-emits **long-wave infrared** radiation (about 8,000-25,000 nm).
3. Atmosphere is relatively **opaque** to this outgoing radiation; CO2 and water vapour absorb strongly at 14,000-25,000 nm.
4. GHGs re-radiate heat **in all directions**, partly back to the surface.
- **APPSC GS 2021**: Global warming = heat from sunlight, **re-radiated by the earth**, absorbed by CO2 and other gases.
#### 1.2 Natural vs Enhanced Greenhouse Effect
| Feature | Natural | Enhanced (anthropogenic) |
|---|---|---|
| Cause | No human role | Human activities raise GHG levels |
| GHG levels | Stable for thousands of years | Rising: fossil fuels, deforestation, industry, agriculture, waste |
| Biggest contributor | **Water vapour** | **CO2** (APPSC 2025 key) |
| Result | Keeps Earth habitable; essential for life | Global warming and climate change |
- **Primary cause of present global warming** = **increase in GHG concentrations** (APPSC 2025 key); **ozone depletion** is a separate problem.
!TRAP Transparent to short-wave coming IN; opaque to long-wave infrared going OUT. Not the other way round.
### 2. HEATING OF THE ATMOSPHERE
- The atmosphere is heated mainly by **long-wave terrestrial radiation**, more than by incoming solar radiation.
- CO2 and other GHGs **absorb long-wave radiation** well.
| Insolation | Share |
|---|---|
| Reflected (albedo) | about **35%** |
| Absorbed by the atmosphere | about **14%** |
| Absorbed by the surface | about **51%** |
- Clouds and gases reflect about **one-fourth** of incoming solar radiation.
- Earth's surface does **not** re-emit heat as **ultraviolet** (it emits infrared).
- **Cloudy nights are warmer**: clouds stop heat escaping from the ground and send it back.
- Heat trapped by the atmosphere maintains the average temperature; "without atmosphere it would be below freezing everywhere" was marked **wrong** (average would be about −18°C).
- **Fresh snow** reflects the most sunlight: about **80-85%**.
### 3. GREENHOUSE GASES – TYPES, ORDER, NON-GHGs
#### 3.1 The Gases
| Gas | Formula | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| Water vapour | H2O | **Most abundant** and **most important** GHG; about half of Earth's greenhouse effect (NASA) |
| Carbon dioxide | CO2 | **Main anthropogenic driver**; about **66%** of radiative forcing by long-lived GHGs (WMO); ~0.04% of air |
| Methane (**marsh gas**) | CH4 | Paddy fields, wetlands, cud-chewing animals; also in biogas |
| Nitrous oxide | N2O | Nitrogen fertilisers; both natural and man-made |
| Ozone | O3 | Stratospheric ozone = UV protection; **tropospheric ozone = smog pollutant** and GHG |
| CFCs | — | Both **GHG and ozone-depleting** |
- **Water vapour**: humans raise it only **indirectly**, by warming the air (APPSC GS 2023). **Warmer air holds more water vapour.**
- **Primary GHGs**: water vapour, CO2, methane, N2O, CFCs, ozone. **Most important set**: CO2, methane, N2O, water vapour.
- **Most responsible for global warming**: **CO2 and methane**.
- **Oxygen** is not one of the four principal human-driven GHGs.
- **Soot (black carbon), SF6 and water vapour** all warm the atmosphere; soot absorbs solar and infrared radiation.
#### 3.2 Natural vs Synthetic
| Type | Gases |
|---|---|
| Natural | Water vapour, CO2, methane, N2O, ozone |
| Synthetic (entirely man-made) | **CFCs, HFCs, PFCs, SF6** |
#### 3.3 Contribution Order – Two Different Measures
| Measure | Order and shares |
|---|---|
| Share of the **enhanced** effect (textbook) | **CO2 60% > CH4 20% > CFCs 15% > N2O 5%** |
| Share of the **whole** greenhouse effect | Water vapour ~60%, CO2 ~26%, CH4 4-9%, O3 3-7%, N2O ~1.5% |
| Order among long-lived gases | CO2 > CH4 > N2O > CFCs |
!KEY Natural effect: water vapour biggest. Enhanced effect: CO2 biggest. Read which one the question asks.
#### 3.4 What is NOT a Greenhouse Gas?
| Not a GHG | Why |
|---|---|
| **Nitrogen (N2)** – 78.08% of air | Does not absorb infrared |
| **Oxygen (O2)** – 20.95% | Does not absorb infrared |
| **Argon (Ar)** – 0.93% | Inert gas (APPSC 2025) |
| **Hydrogen** | Not a GHG |
| **Sulphur dioxide (SO2)** | Causes **acid rain** |
| **Propane** | Its use reduces GHG emissions |
- **Carbon monoxide**: only an **indirect** GHG; also the answer to "does **not** cause tropospheric ozone depletion".
- **Oxides of sulphur and nitrogen** do not add to warming directly.
- **Nitrogen oxide** = answer to "not a GHG **found naturally**"; naturally found: CO2, methane, ozone, water vapour.
- CO2, N2O and methane: **all three are GHGs**.
!TRAP N2, O2, Ar make up the bulk of air but are NOT greenhouse gases.
### 4. GLOBAL WARMING POTENTIAL, LIFETIME AND SOURCE
- **GWP** = heat a gas absorbs over a period (usually **100 years**) compared with the **same mass of CO2**.
| Gas | GWP (100 yr) | Lifetime | Main anthropogenic source |
|---|---|---|---|
| CO2 | **1** (reference) | 100-1,000 years | Fossil fuels, deforestation, cement |
| Methane | **28-36** | **~12 years** | Paddies, livestock, landfills, wetlands, fossil fuels |
| N2O | 265-298 | ~114 years | Fertilisers, biomass burning |
| CFCs / HFCs | 1,000-23,000 | 50-500 years | Refrigerants, foams, aerosol sprays |
| **SF6** | **~23,900 (highest)** | Long-lived | **Dielectric fluid** in switchgear |
| Water vapour | Variable | ~9 days | Natural |
- Older IPCC values (CH4 21, N2O 310) are superseded.
- **Perfluoromethane** comes from **aluminium** production.
#### 4.1 Halocarbons (CFCs) – Ozone and Warming
- CFCs contain **carbon, fluorine and chlorine**; **entirely man-made** (refrigerants, aerosols).
- They are **greenhouse gases** and their **chlorine destroys ozone**: a double role.
!PAIR CFCs | GHG | Warm the planet || CFCs | Chlorine | Destroy stratospheric ozone
### 5. EMISSION SOURCES – SECTORS AND TOP EMITTERS
#### 5.1 Sectors
- **Largest source**: **fossil-fuel combustion** (coal, oil, gas) for energy, transport and industry.
| Sector (global GHG, 2016) | Share |
|---|---|
| **Energy** (electricity, heat, transport, industry energy) | **~73%** |
| Agriculture, forestry and land use | ~18% |
| Direct industrial processes (incl. **cement**) | ~5% |
- **Deforestation** destroys carbon sinks; burning or rotting trees release stored carbon.
- Aerosols: **black and brown carbon warm**; **sulphate droplets cool**.
#### 5.2 Methane and N2O from Farms
- **Low-land (flooded) rice**: methane under **no-oxygen (anaerobic)** conditions; paddy fields emit about **50-100 million t/year**.
- **Rice** = main human-made source of **both methane and N2O** (flooded paddies; burning straw and stubble).
- India's irrigated paddy: **3.9 million t methane/yr**, a little more than Germany's total methane.
- **Enteric fermentation** (cud-chewing livestock) → CH4; **nitrogen fertilisers** → N2O.
| Methane source | Type |
|---|---|
| Rice fields, coal mines, domestic animals | Human |
| **Wetlands** (largest natural), sea hydrates | Natural |
#### 5.3 Top Emitters (Global Carbon Budget 2025, data for 2024)
| Country | CO2, billion t/year |
|---|---|
| China | **12** |
| USA | **4.9** |
| **India (3rd)** | **3.2** |
- India's **per capita** CO2: about **2.2 tonnes** (2024), second-lowest among the 20 largest economies.
- **GHG Emission Intensity Target (Amendment) Rules 2025** under **EPA 1986**: **208 units** must cut emission intensity (GHG per unit of output, e.g. per tonne of cement).
- **Singapore**: **carbon tax from 2019** (announced Feb 2018, under Paris commitments).
!TRAP Fertilisers → N2O; paddy and enteric fermentation → CH4.
### 6. RADIATIVE FORCING, SENSITIVITY, FEEDBACKS AND TIPPING POINTS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Radiative forcing** | Change in Earth's energy balance (**W/m²**) caused by a factor such as a GHG |
| **Climate sensitivity** | Warming after a **doubling of CO2** over pre-industrial levels |
| **Albedo feedback** | Ice melts → lower albedo → more heat absorbed → more melting (**positive feedback**) |
| **Water vapour feedback** | Warmer air holds more water vapour → traps more heat |
| **Methane hydrates** | Methane locked in ice lattices in permafrost and seabed; warming releases it = positive feedback |
| **Tipping points** | Thresholds where small changes cause **self-feeding shifts, irreversible** on human timescales; non-linear |
- **WMO GHG Bulletin (Oct 2025)**: CH4 gives about **16%** of radiative forcing by long-lived GHGs; **CO2 (not methane)** drove about **81%** of the **54% rise** in warming effect, 1990-2024.
- Tipping example: beyond about **2°C** parts of the **Amazon** could turn into **savanna** (UNSG Scientific Advisory Board Brief #12). Beyond **3°C**, the land biosphere may turn from **carbon sink to source**.
- India sought clarity at the **Bonn talks** on using tipping points for policy.
### 7. SHORT-LIVED CLIMATE POLLUTANTS (SLCPs)
- Members: **black carbon, methane, HFCs, tropospheric ozone**; short life but strong warming. **Not water vapour.**
- Tackled by the **Climate and Clean Air Coalition (CCAC)**.
| SLCP | Source | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|
| **Black carbon** | **Incomplete combustion** of biomass and diesel | Short |
| Methane | Rice, livestock, waste, fossil-fuel operations | ~12 years |
| **Tropospheric ozone** | **Secondary**: NOx + VOCs in sunlight | Short |
- Black carbon **darkens snow and ice** → lower albedo → faster glacier melt (APPSC-G2 2025: "short-lived pollutant produced by incomplete combustion").
- Tropospheric (surface) ozone **cuts crop yields**; worst in summer.
#### 7.1 Methane Initiatives – Dated
- **Global Methane Pledge**: launched at **COP26, Glasgow (2021)**; cut methane **30% from 2020 levels by 2030**; **India has not signed** (Global Methane Status Report 2025, UNEP-CCAC).
- **24 Jun 2026**: UN Secretary-General's **Call to Action on Methane** at **London Climate Action Week** (waste, agriculture, fossil fuels).
### 8. MEASUREMENT AND EVIDENCE OF WARMING
- **Keeling curve**: continuous CO2 record at **Mauna Loa, Hawaii**, since **1958**, by **Charles David Keeling**.
- **Ice cores**: trapped air bubbles give past CO2 and temperature.
#### 8.1 WMO Greenhouse Gas Bulletin 2025 (data for 2024)
| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| CO2 | **423.9 ppm**; highest in about **2 million years** |
| Rise in 2024 | **3.5 ppm**, largest since **1957** |
| Pre-industrial CO2 | about **278-280 ppm** |
| CO2 vs pre-industrial | **152%** |
| CH4 vs pre-industrial | **266%** |
| N2O vs pre-industrial | **125%** |
!NUM 152 = CO2; 266 = CH4; 125 = N2O (% of pre-industrial level)
#### 8.2 Temperature Record
- **2024**: **first calendar year above 1.5°C** global mean temperature (WMO).
- Each of the last three decades warmer than any since **1850**.
- Long-term warming: about **1.3°C** above pre-industrial (UNSG brief, 24 Jun 2026).
### 9. OBSERVED IMPACTS
| Area | Observed change |
|---|---|
| Ocean heat | Oceans took **over 90%** of extra energy (1971-2010) |
| Ice sheets | **Greenland and Antarctic both losing mass** |
| Arctic sea ice | Down **3.5-4.1% per decade** (1979-2012) |
| Antarctic sea ice | Collapse since 2015; feedback stops new ice forming |
| **Ocean acidification** | Oceans absorb **CO2 → carbonic acid → lower pH** |
| Coral bleaching | Driven by warming; recovery takes 15+ years |
- Impacts: **melting glaciers, sea-level rise, weather changes, rising temperature** (all four correct); also extreme weather, species loss, health risks.
- Causes: fossil-fuel burning, oil-driven automobiles, **deforestation**; **solar flares are not a cause**.
#### 9.1 Effects Matching (APPSC-GS 2025)
| Issue | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Global warming** | **Rising sea levels** |
| **Air pollution** | **Respiratory illness** |
| **Sub-regional temperature** | Shows **patches of cooling** |
#### 9.2 Pollution Cause-Effect Statements (G2 Mains 2025)
| Cause | Correct effect |
|---|---|
| **Oil spillage** | **Oxygen depletion** in water bodies |
| **Burning plastic** | **Reduces** (not raises) seed germination |
| **Deforestation** | **Increases** CO2 (fewer sinks, stored carbon released) |
!TRAP "Deforestation is decreasing the amount of CO2" = INCORRECT statement (APPSC 2025; G2 2025).
### 10. CARBON ACCOUNTING, WARMING LIMITS AND PROJECTIONS
#### 10.1 Carbon Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Carbon footprint** | Total GHGs emitted by a person, organisation or product, in t CO2/yr (APPSC-GS 2025) |
| **Carbon sequestration** | Storing carbon in sinks: soil, biochar, agroforestry, forests |
| **CCUS** | Captures CO2 from **point sources** (factory flue gases); use or store underground |
| **Direct Air Capture (DAC)** | Removes CO2 from **ambient air**; stored underground or made into products |
| **Carbon credit** | Tradable permit to emit **one metric tonne** of CO2 or equivalent |
| **Social cost of carbon** | Money value of long-term damage from one tonne of CO2 emitted in a year |
| **Carbon bomb** | Fossil project able to emit **over 1 Gt CO2**; **LINGO** found **425** (India: Gevra, Rajmahal) |
- India's **CCUS road map** covers **industry only**; agriculture fits under CO2 removal (soil carbon, biochar, agroforestry).
- India's country-level **social cost of carbon = $86/t CO2**, highest globally.
- **UNFCCC** aim: stabilise GHG concentrations (emissions mitigation).
- **EU CBAM**: fully enforced from **1 Jan 2026**. **Green Hydrogen Mission**: 5 MMT/year.
#### 10.2 Warming Thresholds and Carbon Budget
- Paris limits: **1.5°C** and **2°C** above pre-industrial.
- Remaining **1.5°C carbon budget**: about **130 Gt CO2** from start of 2025; gone in a little over **3 years** at current rates (Jun 2025).
| Report | Projection |
|---|---|
| UNEP Emissions Gap Report 2025 ("Off Target") | Updated NDCs → **2.3-2.5°C** |
| UNEP Limiting Overshoot (Sep 2026) | **2.6°C** on current policies; **1.8°C** if all pledges delivered; "overshoot, peak and decline" back below 1.5°C by 2100 |
