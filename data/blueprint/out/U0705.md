## U0705 | ICT AND INDUSTRY – INDUSTRY 4.0, INTERNET OF THINGS, ROBOTICS AND AUTOMATION
### 1. INDUSTRIAL REVOLUTIONS 1.0 TO 5.0
#### 1.1 Stages and Defining Technology
| Stage | Defining technology |
|---|---|
| **Industry 1.0** | **Mechanisation, steam power** (steam-powered weaving looms) |
| **Industry 2.0** | **Electricity, mass production, assembly line** |
| **Industry 3.0** | **Automation, computers and electronics** |
| **Industry 4.0** | **Cyber-physical systems**, IoT, networks, **AI, big data**; smart factories |
| **Industry 5.0** | **Human-machine collaboration**; puts **people at the centre** of industrial innovation |
- The term **'Industry 4.0'** was coined in **Germany** (Hannover Fair, **2011**).
- **'Fourth Industrial Revolution'** was coined/popularised by **Klaus Schwab**, founder of the **WEF**, in **2016**: merging of **digital, physical and biological** technologies.
!TRAP 3.0 = automation and computers; 4.0 = cyber-physical systems; 5.0 = human-machine collaboration.
#### 1.2 Indian Initiatives for Industry 4.0
- **SAMARTH Udyog Bharat 4.0** (**Smart Advanced Manufacturing and Rapid Transformation Hub**) – **Ministry of Heavy Industries**; spreads Industry 4.0 among manufacturers through **demonstration centres**.
- **NM-ICPS** (National Mission on Interdisciplinary Cyber-Physical Systems): **robotics** is one vertical of its **25 Technology Innovation Hubs**.
- **AP angle**: **WEF Fourth Industrial Revolution Centre** in AP would be **India's third**, after **Mumbai and Telangana**; AP has an **Industry Policy 4.0** (24 Apr 2026).
- **Criticism**: automation and AI replace **intellectual as well as manual work** → job displacement.
### 2. AUTOMATION TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **PLC** | **Programmable Logic Controller** – rugged industrial computer controlling machines |
| **SCADA** | **Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition** – **remote monitoring of plants and grids** |
| **Digital twin** | **Virtual replica** of a physical object/system for simulation and monitoring |
| **Cyber-physical system** | Computing closely joined with physical processes; **core of Industry 4.0** and NM-ICPS |
| **Cobots** | **Collaborative robots** working safely **alongside humans**; spreading in Indian factories |
| **mMTC** | **Massive machine-type communication** – the **5G** slice serving dense IoT sensors |
!PAIR Cobot | works beside humans | robot || SCADA | remote monitoring | plants and grids
### 3. INTERNET OF THINGS (IoT)
#### 3.1 Meaning
- **IoT = Internet of Things** (APPSC 2023, 2024): networked **sensor-equipped devices** sharing data **without human intervention**.
- Examples: an alarm switching on the geyser; a smart mirror showing weather; a **smart thermostat** (IoT, not AI).
#### 3.2 Layers
| Layer | Job |
|---|---|
| **Perception** | **Senses** (sensors, RFID) |
| **Network** | **Transmits**; used for wireless connection in IoT devices |
| **Application** | **Smart services** |
- A four-stage version adds a **transport** layer: perception, transport, network, application.
#### 3.3 Variants
| Variant | Concerns |
|---|---|
| **IIoT** | **Industrial automation** |
| **IoNT** | **Nano-devices** |
| **Internet of Mobile Things** | Mobile devices/objects (not assembly lines) |
| **Mission-critical IoT** | **Military** applications |
#### 3.4 IoT vs AI
| IoT | AI |
|---|---|
| Collects and exchanges data through sensors | Analyses data, recognises patterns, decides |
| Smart thermostat, fitness wearable | Chatbot, image recognition |
#### 3.5 Applications and Policy
- Applications (Jul 2026): **smart farming, smart homes, smart grids**, healthcare, logistics, **smart cities**.
- **NDCP 2018** (National Digital Communications Policy) covers **5G, IoT and M2M**; IoT is part of Digital India.
- **IoT Centres of Excellence** (**MeitY-NASSCOM**): **Bengaluru, Gurugram, Gandhinagar and Visakhapatnam**.
!TRAP NDCP 2018 = 5G/IoT/M2M; Drone Rules 2021 = drones; IT Rules 2021 = intermediaries; E-Commerce Rules 2020 = online consumers.
### 4. RFID AND WEARABLES
#### 4.1 RFID Tags
- **RFID**: radio waves between a **tag** (carries identity data) and a **reader**.
| Tag | Power | Reply |
|---|---|---|
| **Passive** | **No internal battery**; powered by the reader's field | **Backscatters** the reader's signal; cheap, short range |
| **Semi-passive** | Battery runs **the chip only** | Uses the reader's field to reply |
| **Active** | **Own battery and transmitter** | Broadcasts itself; long range |
!TRAP APPSC-G1 2022: "Passive tags are powered by an internal circuit" is **wrong** (key: II and IV only). Tag types asked: passive and semi-passive.
#### 4.2 Wearables
- **Smartwatches, fitness bands**: track **health and fitness** (heart rate, steps, sleep), **biometric authentication**; typical **IoT end-devices**.
### 5. EXTENDED REALITY
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Virtual Reality (VR)** | **Fully computer-generated**, immersive world; **the user is still a real person** (APPSC 2024) |
| **Augmented Reality (AR)** | **Digital layers over the real world** (e.g. Pokemon Go); does **not** block out the physical world |
| **Mixed Reality (MR)** | Real and virtual objects interact in real time |
| **Extended Reality (XR)** | **Umbrella term for VR + AR + MR** |
| **Metaverse** | Persistent, shared 3D virtual space using XR, avatars, digital assets |
- **VARCoE** (VR/AR CoE – MeitY, STPI, Odisha) – **IIT Bhubaneswar**.
- **IMAGE CoE** (MeitY, STPI, Telangana) – gaming, VFX, computer vision, AI – **Hyderabad**.
!TRAP VARCoE = IIT Bhubaneswar; IMAGE CoE = Hyderabad; IoT CoEs include Visakhapatnam.

### 8. DRONES AND UAVs
#### 8.1 Types and Classes
- By design: **multirotor** (quadcopter, hexacopter, octocopter), **fixed-wing** (long range), **single-rotor** (heavy lift), **hybrid VTOL**.
| Drone Rules, 2021 class | Weight |
|---|---|
| Nano | up to 250 g |
| Micro | 250 g – 2 kg |
| **Small** | **2–25 kg** |
| **Medium** | **25–150 kg** |
| **Large** | **above 150 kg** |
- **Swarms**: many drones guided by software → surveillance, light shows or strikes.
!TRAP UAVs: **not all** land vertically, **not all** hover automatically, **not all** run on battery only.
#### 8.2 Policy and Schemes
- Aim: India a **global drone hub by 2030** – **liberalised Drone Rules 2021**, **DigitalSky** permission platform, **PLI**, **Drone Shakti**, DGCA-approved training institutes, **Bharat Drone Mahotsav**.
- **PLI for drones** (2021): **₹120 crore** for three years from 2021-22.
- **Kisan drones**: spraying crops and mapping land; **100 flagged off in February 2022**.
- **Namo Drone Didi**: drones to **women SHGs** for agri-services; **₹1,261 crore** (2023-24 to 2025-26) for **15,000** SHGs; 80% help up to ₹8 lakh.
- **AP Drone City**: **Orvakal, Kurnool**, **300 acres**; foundation laid **14 Nov 2025** at the CII Partnership Summit, Visakhapatnam.
### 9. 3D PRINTING
- **Additive manufacturing**: material joined or hardened under computer control, **layer by layer** from a **CAD** model; opposite of subtractive machining.
- **4D printing**: adds materials that **change shape over time**.
- Uses (all valid): **confectionery (food), bionic ears, automotive industry, reconstructive surgery**, data processing technologies, aerospace, construction.
- **India's first 3D-printed post office**: **Bengaluru** (Cambridge Layout, near Ulsoor), **2023**; **1,021 sq ft**, built in **43 days**.
- **FutureSkills PRIME**: additive manufacturing is a course area.
!TRAP 3D printing is additive, not subtractive.
