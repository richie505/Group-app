## U0567 | INSTITUTIONAL CREDIT TO AGRICULTURE IN ANDHRA PRADESH – BANKS, NABARD AND SHGS
### 1. RURAL CREDIT INSTITUTIONS – ESTABLISHMENT
| Institution | Date | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Andhra Bank** | 1923 | Founded by **Bhogaraju Pattabhi Sitaramayya** at **Machilipatnam**; merged into **Union Bank of India** on **1 April 2020** |
| **RRBs** | **2 October 1975** | **Narasimham Working Group**; first RRB = **Prathama Bank** (Moradabad) |
| **NABARD** | **12 July 1982** | **NABARD Act, 1981**; **B. Sivaraman Committee (CRAFICARD)**; HQ **Mumbai**; began with **Rs 100 crore** |
| EXIM Bank | 1982 | Same year as NABARD |
| **SIDBI** | **2 April 1990** | Principal institution for MSMEs |
| SHG-Bank Linkage | **1992-93** | NABARD programme |
| **KCC** | **1998** | NABARD model scheme; **R.V. Gupta Committee** |
- NABARD took over RBI's **ACD** and **RPCC** and the whole of **ARDC** (Agricultural Refinance and Development Corporation); **fully Government-owned since 2019**.
- **Narasimham Committee I (1991)**: began banking reforms; new private banks, freed interest rates.
!TRAP NABARD **Act 1981**, NABARD **set up 1982**. Order: RRBs (1975) → NABARD (1982) → SHG linkage (1992-93) → KCC (1998).
### 2. SOURCES OF INSTITUTIONAL FINANCE
- AP farmers borrow from **commercial banks, RRBs and PACS** – all are institutional sources.
- Institutional: cooperative societies and banks, commercial banks, RRBs, Government. **Non-institutional**: moneylenders and other private sources.
| Source (national pattern, Apr 2026) | Role |
|---|---|
| Scheduled commercial banks | **Over 70%** of formal farm credit |
| Cooperatives (PACS-DCCB-SCB) | Mainly **short-term** credit |
| **ARDBs** | **Long-term** credit |
- **Priority Sector Lending** applies to commercial banks, RRBs, SFBs, LABs, primary UCBs, with a mandated agriculture share.
- Exclusion: **tenant, tribal, women and oral-lessee** farmers are left out for lack of land records. NABARD Guntur study: formal rates about 2.5-10%.
| Loan term | Period |
|---|---|
| Short-term | Up to 1 year, maximum **15 months** |
| Medium-term | Above 15 months up to 3 (or 5) years |
| Long-term | Above 3 (or 5) years |
### 3. RRBS IN ANDHRA PRADESH
- Under "**One State, One RRB**", four AP RRBs merged into **Andhra Pradesh Grameena Bank** from **1 May 2025**: Chaitanya Godavari GB, Andhra Pragathi GB, Saptagiri GB, AP Grameena Vikas Bank.
- Sponsor bank: **Union Bank of India**. RRBs in India fell from 43 to **28**.

### 5. STATE-NABARD PROGRAMMES
#### 5.1 RIDF
- Created by GoI in NABARD, **1995-96**; starting corpus **Rs 2,000 crore** (not Rs 20,000 crore); **NABARD** is the nodal agency.
| Activity | RIDF category |
|---|---|
| Rural drinking water | Social sector |
| Rural roads | Rural connectivity |
| Minor irrigation | Agriculture and related |
- Loans to States: irrigation 30%, roads 26%, social 21%, agriculture 13%, bridges 10%; Rs 35,000 crore for 2024-25. **RIDF XXVIII** (2023-24): Rs 40,475 crore; total Rs 4,98,411 crore.
- AP: **68 lift irrigation schemes** modernised at Rs 74.22 crore to stabilise **1.03 lakh acres** (2024-25).
!TRAP Share-sale (disinvestment) money goes to **NIF**, not RIDF.
#### 5.2 Other programmes

### 6. CROP LOAN SCHEMES
| Scheme | Provision |
|---|---|
| **YSR Zero Interest Crop Loan** (payout 20 Apr 2021) | Rabi crop loans **up to Rs 1 lakh** repaid on time; **State pays interest** |
| Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (RBI, NABARD) | Up to **Rs 3 lakh at 4%** for prompt repayers; farmers, tenants, SHGs |
| KCC (RBI Directions, **19 Jun 2026**) | Loans from **1 Jan 2027**; max tenure **6 years** (earlier 5) |
- India farm credit 2024-25: **Rs 28.69 lakh crore** vs target Rs 27.50 lakh crore = **104%**.
### 7. SHG MOVEMENT – SERP, DWCRA, VELUGU
- **DWCRA** (Development of Women and Children in Rural Areas): launched **September 1982**; Centre:State funding 75:25.
- **SERP** = **Society for Elimination of Rural Poverty**, registered **2000**; runs **Velugu**/Indira Kranthi Patham with World Bank support; split into AP SERP and TG SERP after 2014.
- **AP leads India** in SHG-bank linkage: **36%** national share, **99.66%** recovery (2024-25).
- Problem: linkage loans too small; members lack personal **CIBIL** credit histories (Feb 2026).
### 8. STREE NIDHI, MEPMA AND BANK SCHEMES FOR WOMEN
| Body / scheme | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Stree Nidhi** | **Timely, affordable credit** to SHG members; credit cooperative federation promoted with SHG federations |
| **MEPMA** | **Mission for Elimination of Poverty in Municipal Areas**; **urban SHGs**; 9,018 slum federations |
| **PS-SBG** | **Pattabhi Sitaramayya-Self Business Group** for rural women entrepreneurs; launched by **Andhra Bank** |
| One Family-One Entrepreneur | 5 lakh women entrepreneurs (Jan 2026) |
- MEPMA limits: SHG bank linkage **up to Rs 20 lakh**; individual loans **Rs 50,000-2 lakh**; group loans **up to Rs 10 lakh**.
