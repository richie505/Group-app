## U0700 | PROMINENT INDIAN SCIENTISTS – SARABHAI, KALAM, SWAMINATHAN, KHORANA, RAMAKRISHNAN, C.N.R. RAO
### 1. VIKRAM SARABHAI
- **Father of India's space programme**. Founded **PRL Ahmedabad (1947)**, **TERLS** near Thiruvananthapuram (**1962**), **Community Science Centre (1965)**.
- Headed **INCOSPAR (1962)** → **ISRO (1969)**; **first ISRO Chairman** till death (**1971**); AEC Chairman 1966.
- Awards: Bhatnagar 1962, Padma Bhushan 1966, Padma Vibhushan (posthumous) 1972.
- Named after him: **Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre** (TERLS renamed), **Chandrayaan-2 lander "Vikram"**, **COSPAR Vikram Sarabhai Medal** (1990, COSPAR + ISRO, for developing-country space scientists).
- **2026**: **Annapurni Subramaniam** won the medal at the 46th COSPAR Assembly, Florence – **first Indian woman**, fourth Indian.
### 2. A.P.J. ABDUL KALAM
- **"Missile Man"**; project director of **SLV-3** (put Rohini in orbit, 1980); chief executive of **IGMDP** (Agni).
- **Pokhran-II** with Anil Kakodkar; **Bharat Ratna 1997**; autobiography **Wings of Fire (1999**, with Arun Tiwari).
- **11th President (2002-07)**; flew **Sukhoi-30 MKI** on **8 June 2006** (co-pilot Wg Cdr Ajay Rathore) – first Indian Head of State to fly a fighter.
- **Pratibha Patil** flew Sukhoi-30 on 25 Nov 2009 – first Indian woman in a fighter.
### 3. M.S. SWAMINATHAN
- **Architect of the Green Revolution** – high-yielding **dwarf wheat and rice** (with Norman Borlaug).
- **UNEP: "Father of Economic Ecology"**; chaired **National Commission on Farmers** (18 Nov **2004**) – "Swaminathan report".
- **First World Food Prize (1987)**; **Bharat Ratna 2024** (posthumous).
