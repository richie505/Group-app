## U0289 | DIRECTIVE PRINCIPLES OF STATE POLICY – ARTICLES 36-51, CLASSIFICATION, IMPLEMENTATION
### 1. BASICS OF DPSP
- Located in **Part IV** of the Constitution – **Articles 36 to 51** (16 articles).
- **Article 36**: "State" has the **same meaning as in Part III** (Article 12).
- **Article 37**: DPSPs "shall **not be enforceable by any court**", but are "**fundamental in the governance** of the country"; it is the **duty of the State to apply them in making laws**.
- Aim: **social and economic democracy** and a **welfare state** (political democracy is the aim of FRs).
- Called the **"Instrument of Instructions"**; with FRs, the **"conscience of the Constitution"** (Granville Austin).
- Welfare state idea is found in **both the Preamble and the DPSPs**; **Economic Justice** is in both (e.g. Article 38).
- Welfare State aims at caring for the welfare of **weaker sections**; it is a secular, democratic state – a **Religious State is NOT an objective**.
- Welfare state description: "The State is everywhere: it leaves hardly a gap."
!KEY DPSPs do not impose a legal duty on the State; their sanction is moral and political.
### 2. SOURCE AND HISTORICAL ANTECEDENTS
#### 2.1 Foreign Source
- Borrowed from the **Irish Constitution of 1937** ("Directive Principles of Social Policy").
- Ireland had itself copied the idea from the **Spanish Constitution**.
#### 2.2 Indian Precursors
| Antecedent | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Nehru Report (1928)** | Early charter of rights and welfare goals |
| **Karachi Resolution (1931)** | Socialist-nationalist economic programme of the freedom struggle |
| **Instrument of Instructions** (GoI Act, 1935) | Instructions to Governors – reborn as DPSPs |
| **Sapru Committee (1945)** | Split rights into **justiciable** and **non-justiciable** |
| Gandhian ideals | Village self-sufficiency, cottage industries |
!TRAP Source country is Ireland, not the USSR (USSR = Fundamental Duties) and not the USA (USA = FRs, judicial review).
### 3. ARTICLE 37 – JUSTICIABILITY AND THE FR–DPSP BALANCE
#### 3.1 FR vs DPSP
| Basis | Fundamental Rights | Directive Principles |
|---|---|---|
| Courts | **Justiciable** | **Non-justiciable** |
| Aim | Political democracy | Social and economic democracy |
| Sanction | **Legal** | **Moral and political** |
| Obligation | Mostly **negative** (restrain State) | Mostly **positive** (State must act) |
| In a conflict | Generally prevail – except **39(b) and 39(c)** in some cases | Subordinate, with exceptions |
#### 3.2 Courts' View
- **Minerva Mills (1980)**: the **balance between Part III and Part IV** is part of the **Basic Structure**.
- Courts use DPSPs as **interpretive aids** (harmonious construction); many welfare principles are **read into Article 21**.
- DPSPs are **guideposts for legislation**.
!KEY APPSC G2 Mains 2025 A-R: A – DPSPs are not independently enforceable (true); R – they act as a guidepost for legislation (true). Official key: **both true and R is the correct explanation of A**.
!TRAP Non-enforceability flows from **Article 37's clear bar**, not merely from their role as a guide to law-making.

#### 4.2 What is NOT a DPSP
- **Free education up to 14 years** (now FR, Article 21A); free education up to **16 years**.
- **Adult education** – DPSPs are silent on it.
- **Right to Information** – a legal right under the **RTI Act, 2005**.
- **Abolition of untouchability** (Article 17), prohibition of **child labour** (Article 24) and **forced labour** (Article 23) – all FRs.
- **Mother-tongue education at primary level** – **Article 350A**, outside Part IV.
- **Right to work** is NOT a Fundamental Right – it is a DPSP (Article 41).
!TRAP Article 44 (UCC) is a DPSP with no legal force; nutrition is Article 47, not 44.
### 5. ARTICLE 39 – CLAUSES (a) TO (f)
| Clause | Directive |
|---|---|
| 39(a) | **Adequate means of livelihood** for men and women equally |
| 39(b) | **Ownership and control of material resources** of the community distributed to subserve the **common good** |
| 39(c) | Economic system not to result in **concentration of wealth** and means of production to the common detriment |
| 39(d) | **Equal pay for equal work** for both men and women |
| 39(e) | Health and strength of **workers** and tender age of **children** not abused; none forced into unsuited work |
| 39(f) | Children to develop in **freedom and dignity**; protected against exploitation and abandonment (**42nd Amendment**) |
- Concentration of wealth violates **Articles 39(b) and 39(c)**.
- APPSC G2 Mains 2025: both the 39(b) and 39(c) statements **are** in the Constitution.
- Present wording through the 42nd Amendment; Article 39 is a DPSP (Articles **24 and 21A are FRs**).
!PAIR Equal pay for equal work | Article 39(d) | DPSP || Child labour ban | Article 24 | FR
### 6. CLASSIFICATION – SOCIALIST, GANDHIAN, LIBERAL-INTELLECTUAL
- The **Constitution does not classify** DPSPs; the three-fold scheme is an **academic grouping** for study.
| Group | Articles | Key ideas |
|---|---|---|
| **Socialistic** | 38, 39, 39A, 41, 42, 43, 43A, 47 | Welfare, equal pay, legal aid, right to work, living wage, workers in management |
| **Gandhian** (6) | **40, 43, 43B, 46, 47, 48** | Panchayats, cottage industries, co-operatives, weaker sections, prohibition, cow slaughter ban |
| **Liberal-intellectual** (7) | **44, 45, 48, 48A, 49, 50, 51** | UCC, ECCE, modern agriculture, environment, monuments, separation of judiciary, international peace |
- **43B** is Gandhian (38, 39A, 47 are socialist).
- Gandhian = village panchayats, cottage industries; **NOT Gandhian** = UCC (liberal-intellectual), right to work (socialist).
!TRAP Overlaps: **43** and **47** sit in both Socialist and Gandhian lists; **48** in both Gandhian and Liberal-intellectual.
### 7. AMENDMENTS ADDING OR CHANGING DPSP
| Amendment | Year | Change |
|---|---|---|
| **42nd** | 1976 | Added **39(f)**, **39A** (free legal aid), **43A** (workers in management), **48A** (environment) |
| **44th** | 1978 | Added **38(2)** – minimise inequalities in income, status, facilities, opportunities |
| **86th** | 2002 | Substituted **Article 45** – early childhood care and education until six (with Art. 21A and 51A(k)) |
| **97th** | 2011 | Added **Article 43B** – co-operative societies |
- Chronological order: **42nd → 44th → 86th → 97th** (= 39A/43A/48A → 38(2) → 45 → 43B). APPSC G1 Prelims 2024 asked two chronology questions on these.
- **39A** added in 1976, in force from **3 January 1977**.
- **UCC (44)** and **43B** were NOT added by the 42nd Amendment.
- Original Article 45: free and compulsory education for children **up to 14 years**; since 2002, 6-14 is an FR under **Article 21A**.
- **First Amendment, 1951**: added the **Ninth Schedule** and **Articles 31A and 31B** (to protect land reforms).
!NUM 1976 = 42nd (4 changes); 1978 = 44th (38(2)); 2002 = 86th (Art. 45); 2011 = 97th (43B)

### 10. IMPLEMENTATION – LAWS AND PROGRAMMES
| Directive | Implementation |
|---|---|
| **39(b), (c)** | **Land reforms** – zamindari abolition, ceiling laws, Ninth Schedule (1st, 4th, 17th, 25th, 42nd, 44th Amendments) |
| **40** | **73rd Amendment, 1992** – Part IX, constitutional status to panchayats (not the RTE Act) |
| **39A** | **Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987** – in force **9 November 1995**; NALSA → SLSAs → DLSAs |
| **41** | **MGNREGA, 2005** – made right to work a legal right; now replaced by the **VB-G RAM G Act** |
| **45 / 21A** | **RTE Act, 2009** (Sec. 3: free education 6-14 in a neighbourhood school) |
| **47** | **Mental Healthcare Act, 2017**; public health measures; prohibition laws |
| **48A** | **Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972**; **Environment (Protection) Act, 1986** |
| 42 / 43 | Labour laws |
| **44** | State UCCs – **Uttarakhand (first)**, **Gujarat (second)** |
- **MGNREGA** is also asked as implementing **Article 43** (living wage for all workers).
- **National Social Assistance Programme (15 August 1995)** meets DPSPs on right to work, education and public assistance (Art. 41).
#### 10.1 Legal Aid (Article 39A)
- **NALSA** formed under the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987; gives **free and competent legal services** to weaker sections; organises **Lok Adalats**.
- **9 November = National Legal Services Day**.
- First-ever national meet of DLSAs: **30-31 July 2022, New Delhi**.
- Legal aid under Art. 39A remains underfunded (Aug 2025).
#### 10.2 Uniform Civil Code (Article 44)
- **Goa** follows the **Portuguese Civil Code of 1867**.
- **Gujarat UCC Bill, 2026**: monogamy, equal inheritance, marriage registration in 60 days, live-in registration in 30 days; **Scheduled Tribes exempted** (Apr 2026).
- **Pavini Shukla v. Union of India (Jun 2026)**: SC suggested a UCC to end gender discrimination in personal laws.
- **Mar 2026**: a 3-judge SC bench considered deferring to Parliament on Art. 44 in a plea against Muslim inheritance law.
#### 10.3 Right to Work
- Fall in rural work under the **VB-G RAM G Act** revived the right-to-work debate (Sep 2026).

1. **Ireland**, not USSR or USA, is the source; Ireland copied **Spain**.
2. **39A, 43A, 48A, 39(f)** = 42nd; **38(2)** = 44th; **45** = 86th; **43B** = 97th.

5. **K.T. Shah** = cheque payable at bank's convenience; **T.T. Krishnamachari** = dustbin of sentiments.
6. **Granville Austin** = conscience of the Constitution, not Ambedkar.
7. **43A** = workers in management of **industries**, not agriculture.
8. **Right to work** (41), **UCC** (44) are DPSPs; **RTI**, untouchability abolition, child labour ban are not.
