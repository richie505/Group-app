## U0238 | CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE MOVEMENT – LAHORE SESSION AND PURNA SWARAJ, DANDI MARCH, SPREAD AND PHASES
### 1. RUN-UP: SIMON COMMISSION, NEHRU REPORT, CALCUTTA ULTIMATUM
- **Simon Commission** appointed **Nov 1927**; arrived **3 February 1928**.
- **Nehru Report (1928):** All Parties Conference committee; **chairman Motilal Nehru**, **secretary Jawaharlal Nehru**; constitutional framework asking for **Dominion Status**.
- **Calcutta session (1928):** accepted the Nehru Report with a **one-year ultimatum** – if not accepted within the year, a mass movement would follow.
- **Viceroy Lord Irwin (1926-31):** Simon Commission visit, Lahore Purna Swaraj, Dandi March all fall in his time.
!TRAP Purna Swaraj was adopted only at Lahore (1929); crediting Calcutta 1928 is WRONG.
### 2. LAHORE SESSION, 1929 – PURNA SWARAJ
#### 2.1 Session Facts
- Venue and date: **Lahore, December 1929**; resolution passed **31 December 1929**.
- **President: Jawaharlal Nehru** – he moved the Purna Swaraj resolution and declared the aim; Viceroy **Lord Irwin**.
- Nehru **hoisted the tricolour at midnight, 31 December 1929, on the banks of the Ravi**.
#### 2.2 Resolutions
1. **Nehru Report / Dominion Status rejected** (Calcutta deadline had lapsed).
2. Goal = **Purna Swaraj (Complete Independence)**.
3. No benefit from the Round Table Conference – to be boycotted.
4. **26 January** to be observed every year as **Independence Day**.
5. **Congress Working Committee authorised to launch Civil Disobedience**.
- **Abolition of untouchability was NOT among the Lahore resolutions.**
#### 2.3 Purna Swaraj Day
- **First Independence Day: 26 January 1930**.
- **26 January 1950** was chosen for the Constitution because Congress had celebrated 26 January 1930 as the first Independence Day.
- **AP angle:** **Bulusu Sambamurthy (East Godavari)** got a **Purna Swaraj resolution passed at district level first**, before Lahore.
!KEY Lahore 1929 = Jawaharlal Nehru = Purna Swaraj = flag on the Ravi = 26 January 1930.
### 3. DANDI MARCH, 1930
#### 3.1 Prelude
- Gandhi's **11-point ultimatum/letter to Viceroy Irwin** (30 Jan 1930; letter of 2 March 1930); main demand **abolition of the salt tax**; Irwin refused.
- **Why salt:** a **universal necessity** of rich and poor; the salt tax and monopoly were the most visible sign of colonial exploitation.
- Ultimate goal of the Salt Satyagraha: **Purna Swaraj**.
#### 3.2 The March
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Start | **12 March 1930** (APPSC 2025), not 15 March |
| From | **Sabarmati Ashram, Ahmedabad** |
| Marchers | **78 followers** |
| Distance | About **240 miles (385 km)**, ~241 miles, **24 days** – not 240 km |
| Destination | **Dandi, Navsari district, Gujarat coast** |
| Reached | **5 April 1930** |
| Salt law broken | **6 April 1930** – CDM begins |
| Viceroy | **Lord Irwin** |
- **Only Telugu marcher:** **Yerneni (Erneni) Subrahmanyam** of **Krishna district**; later founded an ashram at **Komaravolu**.
- Gandhi arrested **5 May 1930** (Yerwada Jail); successors: **1st Abbas Tyabji, 2nd Sarojini Naidu**.
#### 3.3 Remarks
| Person | Statement |
|---|---|
| **Subhas Bose** | Dandi March like **"Napoleon's return from Elba"** (march from Elba to Paris) |
| **Gandhi** | **"Sedition has become my religion"** (speech, 18 March 1930) |

### 5. KHUDAI KHIDMATGAR (RED SHIRTS)
- Founder: **Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan, "Frontier Gandhi"**, among the **Pathans of the NWFP**.
- Called for Pathan regional unity and **non-violent** struggle against colonialism.
- Role in CDM: protests in **Peshawar**; **Qissa Khwani firing (April 1930)**.
- **Garhwal Rifles** under **Chandra Singh Garhwali** refused to fire on the Peshawar crowd (1930).

### 10. GANDHI-IRWIN PACT AND KARACHI SESSION
#### 10.1 The Pact
- Signed **5 March 1931 (Delhi Pact)** – not 1930; CWC authorised Gandhi.
- **Mediators: Tej Bahadur Sapru and M.R. Jayakar** (also V.S. Srinivasa Sastri).
- Sarojini Naidu called Gandhi and Irwin the **"Two Mahatmas"**; Irwin's biographer **Alan Campbell Johnson** called Gandhi's gains **"Consolation Prizes"**.
| Irwin agreed | Irwin refused | Gandhi agreed |
|---|---|---|
| Release non-violent political prisoners | Public inquiry into police excesses | **Suspend the CDM** |
| Coastal villagers may make **salt for own use** | **Commuting death sentences of Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Rajguru** | **Attend the 2nd RTC** |
| Withdraw ordinances; remit fines; return unsold lands | – | – |
#### 10.2 Karachi Session (29-31 March 1931)
- President **Vallabhbhai Patel**; **endorsed** (did not oppose) the Pact; chose Gandhi for the 2nd RTC.
- Passed the **Fundamental Rights** and **National Economic Programme** resolutions – drafted by **Jawaharlal Nehru** (with M.N. Roy's help).
- Criticism: no commutation for Bhagat Singh (executed **23 March 1931**); no police inquiry; youth disillusioned.
### 11. ROUND TABLE CONFERENCES, COMMUNAL AWARD AND POONA PACT
#### 11.1 Round Table Conferences (London)
| RTC | Dates | Congress |
|---|---|---|
| **First** | **12 Nov 1930 – 19 Jan 1931** | **Boycotted** (CDM on) |
| **Second** | **7 Sept – 1 Dec 1931** | **Attended – Gandhi the sole Congress representative** (after the Gandhi-Irwin Pact) |
| **Third** | **17 Nov – 24 Dec 1932** | **Did not attend** |
- Gandhi (not Nehru) represented Congress at the 2nd RTC; it failed; **CDM resumed Jan 1932**, Gandhi arrested.
#### 11.2 Communal Award and Poona Pact
- **Communal Award: 16 August 1932** – separate electorates for depressed classes.
- Gandhi's **fast unto death in Yerwada Jail** began **20 September 1932**.
- **Poona Pact: 24 September 1932**, Yerwada Jail – signed by **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar** (depressed classes) and **Madan Mohan Malaviya** (caste Hindus); separate electorates dropped; **148 reserved seats** in provincial legislatures instead of **71** under the Award, within joint electorates.
- **Harijan Sevak Sangh** (first named Anti-Untouchability League) founded by Gandhi, **30 September 1932**; first president **G.D. Birla**, secretary **Thakkar Bapa**.
- Gandhi: **"Service to Harijans"** his top priority among constructive programmes.
