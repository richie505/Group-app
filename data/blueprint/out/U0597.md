## U0597 | FOLDING, FAULTING, MOUNTAIN BUILDING; ISOSTASY
### 1. DIASTROPHISM
- Chain: internal heat → endogenic processes → **mantle dynamics** (convection) → plate movement → folding, faulting, earthquakes, volcanism, mountains (A-R: both true, R not the explanation).
- **Diastrophism** = all processes that move, raise or build up the crust.
| Orogenic | Epeirogenic |
|---|---|
| **Mountain** building, severe folding in narrow belts | **Continent** building: uplift or warping of large areas |
| **Horizontal** (tangential) forces | **Vertical** (radial) forces (UKPSC 2024) |
| Folding comes from orogenic force | e.g. **Peninsular Block** (transgression, regression) |
- Includes orogeny, epeirogeny, earthquakes, plate movements; **"heterogenic" is NOT** included (MPPSC 2023; that key also treated plate tectonics as outside it). Endogenic forces come from thermal asymmetry inside Earth.
!TRAP Orogeny = mountain building, not continent building. Volcanism, diastrophism = endogenic; weathering, erosion = exogenic.
### 2. MOUNTAIN TYPES
| Type | Formed by | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Young fold | Recent compression (Tertiary/Alpine); high, rugged, sharp peaks | **Himalaya, Alps, Andes, Rockies**, Atlas |
| Old fold | Ancient folding + long denudation; low, rounded | **Aravallis** (Precambrian), **Appalachians** (Caledonian), Urals, **Pennines** |
| Block | Faulting (horst) | **Sierra Nevada**, **Vosges** (France), **Black Forest** (Germany; Feldberg 1,493 m), Salt Range |
| Volcanic | Lava accumulation; cone with crater | **Kilimanjaro**, **Fujiyama**, Etna (Sicily), Mount St. Helens (Washington, active), Cotopaxi |
| Dome | Magma bulges crust, no eruption | Black Hills, Bighorns |
| Residual | Erosion leaves resistant rock | Aravalli, Eastern Ghats (AP), Nilgiris |
- **World's highest peaks (Everest)** lie in young fold mountains. Everest and Mont Blanc are fold, not volcanic.
- Active: Cotopaxi (Ecuador), Etna, Fujiyama; **Aconcagua not active**. Highest volcano among Cotopaxi, Kilimanjaro, Pinatubo, Taal: **Cotopaxi**.
- **Andes** (~7,000 km) = longest range (young fold, not old); Himalayas = highest.
- Himalayan proof of youth: deep gorges, U-turn rivers, parallel ranges, steep landslide slopes. Tethys sediments raised by the northward Indian plate (Eocene–Pliocene); uplift began ~70 million years ago. **Shiwaliks** = youngest (outer) chain.
- **AP**: Eastern Ghats = residual hills of the Eastern Ghats Mobile Belt (khondalite, charnockite).
!TRAP APPSC G2 2024: of Appalachians, Aravallis, Alps, Urals – only the **Alps** are young fold. Sierra Nevada = block (MPPSC 2021).
### 3. THE ARAVALLI
- Rajasthan, Haryana, Delhi; India's **oldest** range, ~570 million years, from Dharwar sediments; residual (300–900 m).
- Highest: **Guru Shikhar, 1,722 m**, Mount Abu, **Sirohi**; without it, Kumbhalgarh (1,224 m).
- **SC (Nov 2025)**: hill = **100 m** above local relief (not 500 m); range = hills within 500 m; new mining leases paused. MoEF&CC **ban on new leases, 24 Dec 2025**. ~91% of mapped hills fall below the 100 m line; SC stayed its directions (Dec 2025) for fresh review.
### 4. FOLDS
- **Anticline** = up-arch; **syncline** = down-trough. Rising pressure: symmetrical → asymmetrical → overturned (overfold) → **recumbent** → **nappe** (broken, thrust far forward); isoclinal = parallel limbs.
- Siwaliks: Jura-type anticline–syncline folds, not block. Cuddapah sediments lie in **synclinal troughs**.
### 5. FAULTS, HORSTS AND RIFT VALLEYS
| Fault | Force | Movement |
|---|---|---|
| Normal | Tension | Hanging wall down |
| Reverse | Compression | Hanging wall up |
| Thrust | Compression | Low-angle reverse |
| Strike-slip | Shear | Horizontal |
- **Horst** = uplifted block (block mountain, APPSC 2022); **graben** = down-faulted block (rift valley).
- Grabens: **Rhine** (between Vosges and Black Forest), **Great Rift Valley** (Lake Malawi), **Death Valley** (California). Sierra Nevada = horst.
- **Narmada** flows west in a rift between Vindhyas (north) and Satpuras (south); forms **no delta** (A false, R true). Tapi also rift; **Damodar** = east-flowing rift river from down-warping.
- UPSC 2024: correct pairs only Appalachians–N. America–fold and Andes–S. America–fold; Vosges is not Central Asian fold, Alps not block.
- Contrasts: V-valley = river; U-shaped, hanging = glacial; rift = tectonic; stalactite hangs, stalagmite rises (lapiaz, dolines = surface karst); barchan crescent, seif long; spit by longshore drift, **tombolo** joins island to mainland; estuary = drowned mouth, delta = deposition. Collapsing hanging glaciers trigger GLOFs.
### 6. ISOSTASY AND GEOSYNCLINES
- **Isostasy**: crustal blocks float in balance on denser material.
| Airy | Pratt |
|---|---|
| **Uniform density**, varying thickness | **Varying density**, same compensation depth |
| Mountains have deep **roots** | Higher blocks are **lighter** |
- **Kober**: geosynclinal theory – long shallow troughs between rigid **kratogens** squeezed into mountains; Tethys → Himalaya.
- Stages: **lithogenesis** → **orogenesis** → **gliptogenesis**.
