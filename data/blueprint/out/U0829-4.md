### 5. GLOBAL RECOGNITION AND UN CHAMPIONS
#### 5.1 Sachin Tendulkar – UN Global Road Safety Champion
- Appointed **24 February 2026**.
- Part of the **UN–JCDecaux "Make A Safety Statement"** campaign.
- Campaign launched in **New Delhi** by **Jean Todt**, **UN Secretary-General's Special Envoy for Road Safety**.
