## U0653 | INDIA - SCHEDULED TRIBE POPULATION: DISTRIBUTION, MAJOR TRIBES AND TRIBAL REGIONS
### 1. NATIONAL PROFILE AND STATE EXTREMES
- STs **10.43 crore = 8.6%** (2011); **89.97% rural**; sex ratio **990**.
- **705** notified STs in **30** States/UTs (older: 698).
| Measure | State / UT |
|---|---|
| Largest ST population | **Madhya Pradesh** (153.17 lakh) |
| Highest share | **Lakshadweep 94.8%**; Mizoram 94.4% |
| Most notified tribes | **Odisha (62)** |
| Lowest NE share | **Tripura ~31.8%** (APPSC 2019) |
| No notified STs | **Punjab, Haryana, Chandigarh, Delhi, Puducherry** |
- NE ascending: **Tripura < Meghalaya < Nagaland < Mizoram**. "North-East has a little over half of India's STs" = not correct.
- Density: Bihar highest (1,106), Arunachal lowest (17); Arunachal CSR 972.
### 2. LARGEST TRIBES
- Order: **Bhil (1,70,71,049) > Gond (1,32,56,928) > Santhal > Mina** (then Naikda, Oraon). Bhil ≈ 1.7 crore = about **16%** of STs (not 38%).
- APPSC-G2 2011: largest share = **Bhils**; answer **Gond** only when Bhil is not an option.
- Minor tribes: Jarawa, Onge, Andamanese, Arandan.

### 5. CULTURE, ECONOMY AND LANGUAGE
- **Vidyarthi and Rai:** hunters Birhor, Chenchu; agriculturists Santhal, Munda; pastoralists Toda; artisans Irula.
- **Transhumance** (herds lowlands ↔ highlands): Bhotiya, Bakarwal (J&K), Gaddi; Maasai = Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda.
- Longpi pottery - **Tangkhul Naga**; last head-hunters - **Konyak Naga**.
- **Sarhul** (new year, tree worship) - Oraon, Munda, Santhal, Ho; Oraon dormitory **Ghumkaria**, chief Mahato; Oraon speak **Kudukh (Dravidian)**.
- Ho: 'Andi' (negotiation), 'Oportipi' (capture). **Agaria** iron-makers, worship Lohasur. **Kamar** bamboo baskets. **Khairwar** catechu. **Bilama** dance - Baiga. Gond and Korku - month-long fruit-tree festival.
- Hornbill - Nagaland; Bastar Dussehra - Chhattisgarh. Sahariya ('Sehara' = wilderness) - starvation deaths.
| Family | Languages |
|---|---|
| Austro-Asiatic | Santali, Ho, Mundari, Khasi |
| Tibeto-Chinese | Bhotia, Lepcha, Garo, Naga |
| Dravidian | Toda, Kota, Gondi, Kurukh, Kui |
| Indo-European | Bhili |
- **Santali**: Eighth Schedule, **92nd Amendment 2003**, script **Ol Chiki** (Warang Citi = Ho). 71st (1992): Konkani, Manipuri, Nepali; 96th (2011): Oriya → Odia.
- **Adi Vaani**: first AI translation platform for tribal languages (Bhili, Gondi, Mundari, Santali), under **Adi Karmayogi Abhiyan**.
### 6. CONSTITUTIONAL FRAMEWORK
| Provision | Content |
|---|---|
| **Art. 342** | Art. 366(25) defines STs by reference to it; President notifies after consulting Governor; **342(2)** only Parliament by law changes; State-specific lists |
| Art. 338A | NCST |
| **Art. 244(1) Fifth Schedule** | Scheduled Areas in **10 States**: AP, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, HP, Jharkhand, MP, Maharashtra, Odisha, Rajasthan, Telangana; Governor's powers; **TAC up to 20, about 3/4 ST MLAs** |
| **Art. 244(2) Sixth Schedule** | **Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram**; autonomous district councils |
| **Art. 164(1) proviso** | Tribal welfare minister: **Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, MP, Odisha**; **94th Amendment 2006** dropped Bihar |
- Supreme Court: **Hindu Succession Act does not apply** to notified STs.

### 8. PVTGS
- Criteria (4): **pre-agricultural technology, low literacy, economic backwardness, declining/stagnant population** (not Sixth Schedule residence).
- Timeline: Dhebar → **52 PTGs (1975)** → +23 (1993) = **75** → renamed **PVTGs 2006** → Feb 2024: 7 variants added (Odisha 4; AP 3: Bondo Porja, Khond Porja, Konda Savara).
- Count: **Odisha 13 > AP + Telangana 12** > Bihar incl. Jharkhand 9.
- Largest PVTG **Sahariya**; smallest **Sentinelese, Great Andamanese**. Baiga - MP; Chenchu - AP; Toda, Kota, Irula - TN.
- **Habitat rights (FRA Sec 3(1)(e))**: **Baiga** first in India (Dindori, MP, 2015-16), then Bharia and Kamar. "Critical Wildlife Habitat" is defined in **FRA 2006**.
