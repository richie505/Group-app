!NUM ORDER: Kheda 1918 → Sarabandi 1922 → Borsad 1923 → Bardoli 1928
#### 1.1 Bardoli Satyagraha, 1928
- Place: **Bardoli taluk, Surat district**, Gujarat (Bombay Presidency); begun by the **Mehta brothers (Kalyanji, Kunwarji)** and **Dayalji Desai**.
- Issue: revenue raised about **30%** despite floods, famine.
- Method: **no-tax campaign** + village intelligence network.
- Women: **Mithuben Petit, Bhaktiba, Maniben Patel**.
- Outcome: raised assessment stopped, fresh survey ordered; **Maxwell-Broomfield inquiry** cut the increase to about **6%**.
!TRAP Bardoli was led by Patel, not Gandhi. Champaran, Ahmedabad Mill Strike and Dandi were led by Gandhi.

### 3. INC PRESIDENCY AND ORGANISATION
- President of the **Karachi session, March 1931**; it adopted the resolution on **Fundamental Rights and Economic Programme** (drafted by **Nehru**).
- President of the **Gujarat Pradesh Congress Committee** from its formation in **1920 to 1945**.
- Headed the **Congress Parliamentary Board**: ran the **1937 provincial elections** under the **Government of India Act 1935**.

### 5. PATEL ON THE CIVIL SERVICES
- Addressed the first IAS probationers at **Metcalfe House, Delhi, 21 April 1947** → now **Civil Services Day**.
- Called the civil service the **"steel frame"**: "If it bends, the whole structure will collapse."

### 7. COMMEMORATION
- **Statue of Unity**: **182 m**, world's tallest; opened **October 2018**; **Sadhu Bet island, Ekta Nagar (Kevadia), Narmada district, Gujarat**, facing the **Sardar Sarovar Dam**; sculptor **Ram V. Sutar**; among the SCO's eight wonders.
- **Rashtriya Ekta Diwas** (National Unity Day): **31 October**, birth anniversary (born **31 October 1875, Nadiad**); announced by the Centre in **2014**.
- **Sardar Patel National Unity Award** (**2019**): medal + citation, **no cash**, at most **three a year**; not limited to armed forces.
- **31 Oct 2024:** two-year **150th birth anniversary** commemoration launched at the Statue of Unity.
!TRAP Statue of Unity: Gujarat, not Hyderabad. 2018 = statue; 2019 = award.
