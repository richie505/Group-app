### 2. LAND USE – TERMS AND INDIA STATISTICS
#### 2.1 Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **NSA** (Net Sown Area) | Counts a plot **once** |
| **GCA** (Gross Cropped Area) | Counts a plot **once per crop** |
| **Cropping intensity** | **GCA ÷ NSA × 100** |
!TRAP "Area sown more than once" included = **GCA**, not NSA. So GCA > NSA.
#### 2.2 India, 2021-22
| Item | Figure |
|---|---|
| Geographical area | **328.7 Mha** |
| Agricultural land | ~54.8% |
| Net sown area | **141 Mha (42.8%)** |
| Gross cropped area | **219.16 Mha** |
| Cropping intensity | 219.16 ÷ 141 × 100 ≈ **155.4%** |
| Forest cover (ISFR 2021) | **21.71%** of geographical area |
#### 2.3 Nine-fold Classification – Categories
1. Forests
2. Area under non-agricultural uses
3. Barren and unculturable land
4. Permanent pastures and grazing land
5. Land under miscellaneous tree crops
6. Culturable waste land
7. Current fallows
8. Fallows other than current fallows
9. Net sown area
!TRAP **Net irrigated area is NOT** one of the nine categories.

### 5. OPERATIONAL HOLDINGS – AGRICULTURE CENSUS
#### 5.1 Definition and Size Classes
- **Operational holding**: all land used for farm production and operated as **one technical unit** by one person, alone or with others, **regardless of title, size or location**.
- Agriculture Census uses **five size classes**:
| Class | Size |
|---|---|
| **Marginal** | **Below 1 ha** |
| **Small** | **1-2 ha** |
| Semi-medium | 2-4 ha |
| Medium | 4-10 ha |
| Large | 10 ha and above |
- "Small and marginal farmers" = holdings up to 2 ha.
#### 5.2 All-India Data

|---|---|---|

### 6. CROPPING SEASONS
| Season | Sowing | Harvest | Major crops |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kharif** | **June-July** (SW monsoon) | Sep-Oct or Oct-Nov | Rice, maize, jowar, bajra, **cotton**, groundnut, soybean, **jute**, sugarcane, tur |
| **Rabi** | **Oct-Dec** (after monsoon) | March-April | **Wheat**, barley, gram, **mustard**, peas, linseed, potato |
| **Zaid** | **March-June** | June-July | **Watermelon**, muskmelon, cucumber, vegetables, fodder |
- Rabi depends on winter rain from **western disturbances** + irrigation.
- **Zaid**: short, irrigation-dependent summer window **between rabi and kharif** (APPSC 2024).
- Zaid sub-seasons: Zaid Kharif (Aug-Sep sowing); Zaid Rabi (Feb-Mar sowing).
- Mustard: Rabi, sown Oct-Nov, harvested Feb-Mar.
- **Sugarcane**: annual/long-duration cash crop placed on the **Kharif** side.
!TRAP **Jute is Kharif**, not Rabi. Cotton = Kharif; wheat = Rabi; watermelon = Zaid.
#### 6.1 Crops Grown in Both Seasons
- AP: **paddy, maize, jowar, bajra and pulses** grown in both kharif and rabi.
- AP 2024-25 paddy: **14.04 lakh ha kharif + 6.93 lakh ha rabi**.
- Pulses: **tur/arhar = kharif**; **gram/chana = rabi**.
- Millets mainly kharif; some in southern rabi.
### 7. CROP CLASSIFICATION – FOOD VS CASH
| Group | Crops |
|---|---|
| Foodgrains = cereals + pulses | Rice, wheat, coarse cereals/millets (**Shree Anna**), pulses |
| Oilseeds | Groundnut, mustard, soybean, sunflower, sesame |
| Cash/commercial | **Cotton, jute, sugarcane, tobacco, rubber**, plantation crops, several oilseeds |
- PYQ cash crop list: jute, groundnut, sugarcane, tobacco, cotton, rubber; **jowar and soyabean NOT** cash crops in that key.
- **Cotton** = cash crop (textiles); rice, maize, wheat = food crops.
- AP traditional food crops: paddy, jowar, bajra, ragi, pulses; traditional commercial: **groundnut, cotton, tobacco, sugarcane**.
### 8. CROPPING PATTERN TRENDS – INDIA
- Long-run shift: **food crops → commercial and high-value crops** (drivers: irrigation, prices, technology).
- **Rice: largest cropped area** in India (~51-52 Mha).
- Pulses and oilseeds: stagnant yields.
- Rice-wheat expansion (2015-2025): Punjab, Telangana, Madhya Pradesh – assured MSP procurement.
- **Horticulture overtook foodgrains** (2024-25).
- Policy: shift **~20% of rice area to pulses/legumes** → cut urea use, save groundwater.
- Largest soyabean area: **Madhya Pradesh** (5.35 Mha, 2009-10), then Maharashtra (3.02 Mha).

#### 9.2 Expanding vs Shrinking
| Expanding | Shrinking |
|---|---|
| **Cotton ("white gold" of AP)** | Ragi, jowar (low MSP support) |
| **Maize**: Godavari-Krishna belt (poultry feed, starch) | **Groundnut**: 11.36 → **3.46 lakh ha** (2011-12 → 2024-25) |
| Horticulture; **oil palm** (East Godavari, Eluru, West Godavari); aquaculture | Oilseed area 15.46 → 5.35 lakh ha |
- **Kharif 2026** (El Niño): AP rainfall **51% below normal** (India 13%), 1 June-25 Aug; sown area 20.35 vs 25.63 lakh ha; planned shift to **pulses and drought-resilient crops**.
- AP problems: monsoon dependence (~50% NSA rainfed), holdings below 1 ha, soil-fertility loss, weak credit.

### 11. AGRICULTURE SINCE 1991
- After **1991 liberalisation**: **decline in public investment** in agriculture (APPSC 2023, correct statement).
- **WTO Agreement on Agriculture (1995)**: market access, domestic support, export subsidies (**not land ceilings**).
- **GM crops**: **Bt cotton (2002)** only major GM crop; GM mustard DMH-11 got GEAC environmental-release nod in 2022 (pending in Supreme Court); **Bt brinjal** under moratorium since 2010.
- **Agricultural Renewal Action Plan** (Group-II 2017): soil health; irrigation/water augmentation and demand management; credit and insurance; **not "boosting capital formation"**.
