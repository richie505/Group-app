## U0767 | ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT ASSESSMENT AND THE NATIONAL GREEN TRIBUNAL
### 1. EIA – MEANING AND HISTORY
- **EIA**: identifies, predicts and evaluates the likely environmental impacts of a proposed project **before it starts**; linked with environmental clearance (EC).
- Global origin: **NEPA, USA, 1969**.
- India: **1976-77** – Planning Commission asked **DST** to examine **river-valley projects** (first sector); **1978** – EIA first used; till 1994, EC was only an administrative decision.
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| **27 Jan 1994** | First EIA Notification under EPA |
| **1997** | Public hearing made **mandatory** |
| **14 Sep 2006** | EIA Notification 2006 replaced 1994 |
| 2011 | Amendment on river valley projects |
| **2018** | **PARIVESH** portal |
| 2020 | Draft notification – **not finalised** |
!TRAP R2 Code of Practices is NOT EIA – it is for responsible electronics recycling.
### 2. EIA VS RELATED TOOLS
| Tool | Focus |
|---|---|
| **EIA** | Specific **project** before it starts |
| **LCA** | **Product/process** across its life – cradle to grave |
| **SEA** | **Policies, plans, programmes** – upstream of projects |
| Cumulative Impact Assessment | Combined effect of several projects in one area |
| **Environmental audit** | Compliance of a running unit, **after the fact** |
- **Cleaner production**: preventive – cuts waste at source (process change, input substitution), not end-of-pipe; APPCB encourages it.
- **Green chemistry**: designs out hazardous substances; **12 principles – Paul Anastas and John Warner, 1998** (prevent waste, atom economy, safer solvents, renewable feedstocks, design for degradation).
- Both shrink impacts at design stage, leaving fewer residual impacts for EIA/LCA.
!KEY APPSC 2025: EIA = project; LCA = product life cycle.
### 3. EIA PROCESS
| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Screening** (first) | Is EIA needed; which category |
| **Scoping** | Key issues, **Terms of Reference** |
| Baseline data | Existing environment surveyed |
| Impact prediction | Alternatives (location, technology) compared; mitigation, **EMP**, risk assessment |
| **Public consultation** | Hearing + written responses |
| **Appraisal** | Expert committee reviews EIA report |
| Decision | EC with conditions or rejection |
| Monitoring | EMP implementation checked |
### 4. EIA NOTIFICATIONS 1994, 2006 AND DRAFT 2020
- **1994**: Schedule I projects needed MoEF clearance; two-stage (site + final) clearance for pithead thermal and valley projects.
- **2006**: basis shifted from **investment size to impact potential**; EC compulsory for **39 categories**.
| Category | Appraisal | Clearance | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **EAC** (central) | **MoEFCC** | No screening; full EIA |
| **B1** | **SEAC** | **SEIAA** | EIA needed |
| **B2** | SEAC | SEIAA | **No EIA** |
- **Draft EIA 2020** contested provisions: **post-facto EC** on penalty; hearing notice **30 → 20 days**; more projects in B2/exempt; yearly (not half-yearly) compliance reports; violations reportable **only by violator or government**.
### 5. PUBLIC HEARING
- Conducted by the **SPCB**, **District Collector** presiding; notice at least **30 days**; completed within **45 days**.
- Exempt: **defence, national-security, strategic** projects (status decided by the **Central Government** – criticised as arbitrary, Jan 2026); **B2** projects; units in notified **industrial estates**.
- APPCB hearing on a Water Aerodrome at **Prakasam Barrage** – draft EIA called error-ridden (May 2026).
### 6. CLEARANCE BODIES AND PARIVESH
- **PARIVESH** (2018): single-window online portal for environment, forest, wildlife and CRZ clearances.
- **July 2026 amendment**: SEAC delay over **120 days** escalates via PARIVESH to new **SCEIA**; **SAEIA** stands in for SEIAA (tenure 6 + 6 months); Centre may remove EAC/SEAC/SEIAA members mid-term for delays; Category B no longer defaults to central EAC.
- Members: **3-4 years**, max two terms, **age limit 70**. Concern: bureaucrats replace scientists.
!PAIR SAEIA | stands in for | SEIAA || SCEIA | stands in for | SEAC
### 7. POST-FACTO CLEARANCE CASES
| Case | Holding |
|---|---|
| **Common Cause (2017)** | Odisha mining beyond EC – pay **100%** of illegal mineral value |
| **Alembic v. Rohit Prajapati (2020)** | Ex post facto EC contrary to EIA regime; **compensation instead of closure** |
| **Noble M. Paikada (2024)** | 2020 exemption for **ordinary earth** in linear projects – blanket exemption **not upheld** (APPSC 2024) |
| **Vanashakti (May 2025)** | Retrospective EC a "gross illegality" |
| Recall (Nov 2025) | **2:1** majority recalled it |
| **Final (29 July 2026)** | CJI **Surya Kant** bench: **2021 OM nullified prospectively**; earlier ECs survive; must meet **Articles 14 and 21**; SC keeps **Article 142** power |
- S.P. Muthuraman (2025) also held retrospective ECs illegal.
!TRAP Prior EC is the rule; post-facto EC only a narrow exception.
### 8. NATIONAL GREEN TRIBUNAL
- **NGT Act 2010**; in force **18 October 2010**; specialised environmental tribunal.
- Principal seat **New Delhi**; other sittings **Bhopal, Pune, Kolkata, Chennai**.
- Appeal to the **Supreme Court within 90 days**; aims to dispose of cases within **6 months**.
- NGT is a **judicial tribunal**; **CPCB** is a **statutory regulator** (Water Act 1974) – NGT can direct it.
- Predecessors: **National Environment Tribunal Act 1995** (hazardous-substance accidents) and **National Environment Appellate Authority Act 1997** (appeals against clearances) – both **repealed by Section 38** of NGT Act; pending NEAA cases moved to NGT.
#### 8.1 Landmark NGT Orders
| Order | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Rat-hole mining, Meghalaya (2014)** | Banned |
| **BRO, Uttarakhand (Aug 2023)** | Plant **10,000 trees in one month** |
| **Maharashtra, West Bengal (2022)** | Fines for waste mismanagement |
| **LG Polymers styrene leak, Visakhapatnam (May 2020)** | Suo motu; **Rs 50 crore** interim deposit |
| Great Nicobar | High-Powered Committee; EIA criticised for **thin** baseline data |
| Suo motu (15 Jan 2026) | Rajasthan, MP, UP – sewage in drinking water |
### 9. AP PROJECTS AND CRITICISM
- **Polavaram**: submergence in **Odisha and Chhattisgarh**; **Telangana** sought EIA review in SC (APPSC 2018).
- **Amaravati**: NGT (**17 Nov 2017**) refused to set aside the 2015 EC; held it **Category B**; added conditions with supervisory and implementation committees.
- Criticism: EIA by **proponent-paid consultants**; **no central baseline data bank**; weak emergency plans; exempted hearings; post-facto ECs becoming "the design". Fix: **independent EIA authority**; sector-wide (strategic, cumulative) EIAs.
