## U0073 | RASHTRAKUTA ADMINISTRATION, TRADE AND RELIGION; KAILASA TEMPLE AT ELLORA; AL-MASUDI'S ACCOUNT
### 1. KAILASA TEMPLE – ELLORA CAVE 16
#### 1.1 Ruler, Technique, Deity
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Cave number | **Cave 16** of Ellora |
| Carved under | **Krishna I** (r. **756–773 CE**), Rashtrakuta |
| Technique | **Monolithic**, carved **top-down** from a single **basalt** cliff |
| Deity | **Shiva** as lord of Mount Kailasa |
| Tower | **Three-tiered Dravida vimana**, although in Maharashtra (Nagara territory) |
- Kailasa = **largest monolithic rock excavation in the world**.
- Early Rashtrakuta rulers were staunch **Shaivas**.
- Rashtrakutas shaped the **Vesara** synthesis (**Nagara + Dravida**) = Deccan style.
!TRAP Kailasa was carved under **Krishna I**, NOT Dantidurga, Amoghavarsha I or Krishna III (Krishna III was the wrong pair in APPSC G1 2024).
### 2. ELLORA CAVES
#### 2.1 Setting
- Local name **Verul Leni**; about **30 km** from **Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar** (Aurangabad district), **Maharashtra**.
- In the **Charanandri hills**, near the **Elaganga river**.
- On the old **Ujjain–Deccan** trade route; carved **c. 600–1000 CE**.
- Ellora relates to **Hinduism, Buddhism and Jainism**; it is on the list of Buddhism-related UNESCO sites in India.
#### 2.2 Number and Groups
| Group | Caves | Count | Period / patron |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buddhist | 1–12 | **12** | Earliest, c. 600–730 CE (before the Rashtrakutas) |
| Hindu | 13–29 | **17** | Mainly **Rashtrakuta** |
| Jain | 30–34 | **5** | Latest, 9th–10th century |
| Total | 1–34 | **34** | – |
- **Most Ellora caves** were built by the Rashtrakutas.
- Buddhist caves: Dhedwada group (1–5) and the larger later group (6–12).
!NUM 34 = 12 Buddhist + 17 Hindu + 5 Jain

#### 2.4 Chronology
- Order: **Dashavatara → Ravan ki Khai → Dumar Lena → Kailasa** (Kailasa latest of the four).
- **2026:** India–Jordan **twinning** agreement between **Petra** and **Ellora** (UNESCO sites).
### 3. ELEPHANTA CAVES
- **Island** cave complex near **Mumbai**.
- Masterpiece: **Trimurti / Maheshamurti** – three-headed Shiva.
- Patron debate: MPPSC 2021 key credited Ellora and Elephanta to the **Rashtrakutas**; many historians date the main cave to the **6th-century Kalachuris**.
- Trimurti (Elephanta) + Kailasa = zenith of Rashtrakuta-era art.
!PAIR Ellora | Charanandri hills | Kailasa (Cave 16) || Elephanta | Island near Mumbai | Trimurti
### 4. RELIGION – PATRONAGE
| Faith | Patronage |
|---|---|
| **Shaivism** | Early rulers firm Shaivas; Kailasa temple |
| **Jainism** | Peak under **Amoghavarsha I** (814–878), a devout Jain said to have **renounced the throne**; groundwork for Gomateshvara (Shravanabelagola) |
| **Vaishnavism, Buddhism** | Royal patronage, **no persecution** |
| **Islam** | Arab merchants allowed to trade, worship and **build mosques** in port towns |
### 5. ARAB TRAVELLERS AND THE TITLE "BALHARA"
| Traveller | Time / reign | Account |
|---|---|---|
| **Sulaiman** | **Amoghavarsha I** | Ranked the king among the **four great kings** of the world (with the **Caliph**, **Chinese emperor**, **Byzantine emperor**) |
| **Ibn Khurdadbeh** | 9th century | Called the **Balhara** the greatest king of India |
| **Al-Masudi** | c. **915–16 CE**, **Indra III** | "Balhara" = greatest king of India; had many **Arab / Muslim subjects** |
- **Balhara** = Arabic form of the Rashtrakuta title **Vallabha** (Vallabharaja).
!TRAP Sulaiman = Amoghavarsha I; Al-Masudi = Indra III.
### 6. ADMINISTRATION – UNITS AND OFFICERS
| Unit | Level | Head |
|---|---|---|
| **Rashtra** | Province | **Rashtrapati** |
| **Vishaya** | District | **Vishayapati** |
| **Bhukti** | Smaller unit | **Bhogapati** |
| **Grama** | Village | **Gramakuta / gavunda** |
- **Nad-gavundas**: **hereditary** officers of the **nadu**; helped collect **revenue**.
!KEY Rashtra (Rashtrapati) > Vishaya (Vishayapati) > Bhukti (Bhogapati) > Grama (Gramakuta)
### 7. TRADE
- Western ports: **Cambay (Khambhat), Thana, Sopara**.
- **Horses imported** by sea from **Arabia**; **textiles exported**.
- **Arab merchants** lived peacefully in the western ports (not barred).
### 8. AP ANGLE, SIGNIFICANCE AND DECLINE
- **Krishna III** helped **Danarnava** kill Eastern Chalukya **Ammaraja II** in **970 CE** and take **Vengi**.
- Rashtrakuta era raised **Kannada** to a classical medium and gave Ellora its Hindu peak.
- **Decline** by the late 10th century under **Chalukya and Chola** attacks.
