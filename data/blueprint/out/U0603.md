## U0603 | ATMOSPHERE – STRUCTURE (LAYERS) AND COMPOSITION
### 1. COMPOSITION OF THE ATMOSPHERE
#### 1.1 Gases by Volume (dry air)
| Gas | Share | Type / role |
|---|---|---|
| **Nitrogen** | **78.08%** (~78%) | Permanent; most abundant |
| **Oxygen** | **20.95%** (~21%) | Permanent; respiration, combustion; negligible by ~120 km |
| **Argon** | **0.93%** | Permanent, inert; **third** |
| **Carbon dioxide** | **~0.04%** | Variable; photosynthesis, greenhouse; reaches ~90 km |
| Water vapour | **0–4%** | Variable; most abundant GHG |
| Ozone | Trace | Variable; absorbs UV |
- Order: **N₂ > O₂ > Ar > CO₂** (APPSC-G1 2023). N₂ + O₂ = **99%**. N₂ ~78% (APPSC-GS 2025).
- Trace gases: **neon, helium, xenon, hydrogen, krypton**. Xenon = "**Stranger Gas**" (Greek *xenos*), found by **Ramsay and Travers, 1898**.
- Weather balloons are filled with **helium** (inert).
- Greenhouse gases include **methane and nitrous oxide**; **not** hydrogen, krypton, neon, helium, argon.
- Best CO₂ for natural balance: **0.03%**, kept within the troposphere.
- **CO₂ 2024: 423.9 ppm**, up 3.5 ppm – largest rise since 1957; ~152% of pre-industrial (WMO Greenhouse Gas Bulletin, Oct 2025).
!TRAP Third place = **Argon**, not CO₂.
#### 1.2 Variable Constituents and Distribution
- **Water vapour**: up to **4%** in warm wet tropics; **below 1%** in deserts and polar air; falls with height; absorbs sunlight and Earth's heat (**blanket effect**); decides air stability; absorbs most infrared in the lower atmosphere.
- **Dust**: sea salt, soil, smoke, ash, pollen, meteor dust; mostly in lower layers; **more in subtropical and temperate belts** (dry winds) than equatorial and polar; act as **hygroscopic nuclei** for condensation.
- Near the surface: mainly nitrogen and oxygen; **hydrogen** (light) goes higher than N₂ and O₂.
- **Early atmosphere**: **reducing**, little or no free oxygen.
- Rain cleans a polluted atmosphere by washing out particles.
### 2. GREENHOUSE EFFECT
- Atmosphere is **transparent to incoming short-wave solar** radiation but **absorbs outgoing long-wave terrestrial** radiation; it is not heated mainly by incoming sunlight.
- CO₂ and other GHGs are good absorbers of long-wave (infrared) radiation.
### 3. LAYERS – HEIGHT, TEMPERATURE, FEATURES
- **Basis of division: change of temperature with height**; composition gives homosphere/heterosphere.
- **99%** of mass lies within **32 km**.
| Layer | Height | Temperature | Key feature |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Troposphere** | 0 to **8 km (poles)**, **18 km (equator)**; avg **13 km** | Falls | **Weather**; life; water vapour, dust |
| **Stratosphere** | to **50 km** | **Rises** (inversion) | **Ozone**; calm, dry – jets |
| **Mesosphere** | **50–80 km** | Falls to **−100°C** (coldest) | **Meteors burn** |
| **Thermosphere** (ionosphere) | from **80 km** | Rises sharply | **Aurora**, airglow, **radio-wave reflection** |
| **Exosphere** | ~600–10,000 km | — | Outermost; light gases; merges into space; satellites |
- Ascending order: troposphere → stratosphere (15–50 km) → mesosphere → ionosphere (**80–640 km**) → exosphere.
- Geostationary satellites (~**36,000 km**) lie in the **exosphere** (above 640 km).
### 4. TEMPERATURE TREND AND BOUNDARIES
- Upward: troposphere **falls** → stratosphere **rises** (ozone absorbs UV) → mesosphere **falls** → thermosphere **rises** (high-energy radiation).
- **Reversal (inversion) layers: stratosphere and thermosphere.**
| Boundary | Height |
|---|---|
| Tropopause | 8–18 km |
| Stratopause | ~50 km |
| Mesopause | ~80 km |
### 5. TROPOSPHERE AND TROPOPAUSE
- **Normal lapse rate: 6.5°C per km** (**1°C per 165 m**).
- **Thickest at equator** (16–18 km) – high insolation → strong **convection** lifts the tropopause; ~8 km at poles.
- Almost all weather, clouds, storms; key for biological activity.
- **Agra and Darjeeling**: same latitude; Darjeeling colder in January because of **altitude** (lapse rate).
- **Tropopause**: boundary of troposphere and stratosphere; **higher at equator**, lower at poles; **higher in summer** than winter; caps weather and vertical mixing.
- Tropopause temperature: **−80°C over equator**, **−45°C over poles**.
### 6. HOMOSPHERE VS HETEROSPHERE
| Region | Height | Layers | Composition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Homosphere** | 0 to ~**90 km** | Troposphere, stratosphere, mesosphere | Gases **evenly mixed** |
| **Heterosphere** | Above ~90 km | Thermosphere, exosphere | Gases **layered by molecular weight**: N₂ → atomic O → He → H |
- Ionosphere is **not** in the homosphere.
### 7. PHENOMENA → LAYER
| Phenomenon | Layer |
|---|---|
| Weather, clouds, rain | Troposphere |
| Ozone layer | **Stratosphere** (APPSC 2025) |
| **Meteors burn** | **Mesosphere** (APPSC-G2 2025) |
| Aurora, airglow | Thermosphere / ionosphere |
| Radio-wave reflection, wireless signals | Ionosphere |
| Jet aircraft | **Lower stratosphere** – no clouds, very dry |
- Aurora: solar charged particles excite upper-air particles; **Borealis = north**, **Australis = south**.
!TRAP Meteors burn in the mesosphere, not the thermosphere; aurora is ionospheric, not mesospheric.
### 8. IONOSPHERE – D, E, F LAYERS
| Layer | Height | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **D** | 60–90 km | **Absorbs** medium/long waves; fades at night |
| **E (Kennelly-Heaviside)** | 90–150 km | Reflects **medium-frequency** waves |
| **F (Appleton)**; F1, F2 by day | 150–400 km | **Main reflector of short-wave** radio |
- Formed when solar UV and X-rays strip electrons; **expands by day, contracts at night**.
- Solar flares and geomagnetic storms disrupt **GNSS** signals (9 Jul 2026).
### 9. OZONE LAYER
- O₃ = three oxygen atoms; mainly in the **lower stratosphere** (stratosphere ~10–50 km); main layer **15–30 km**, densest **15–35 km**; exam height also **15–20 km**.
- About **90%** of ozone is stratospheric, 10% tropospheric.
- Absorbs harmful **UV-B and UV-C** (APPSC 2025); stratosphere warms because ozone turns UV to heat.
- **Unit: Dobson Unit (DU)** for column ozone.
| UV band | Wavelength |
|---|---|
| UV-A | 315–400 nm |
| UV-B | 280–315 nm |
| UV-C | **100–280 nm** (shortest, most harmful) |
- **Chapman cycle**: O₂ + UV → O + O; O + O₂ → O₃; O₃ absorbs UV and splits.
- Extra UV causes **cataract, skin cancer, weaker immunity, lower crop yield**.
- **Ground-level ozone** = secondary pollutant from **NOx + VOCs** in sunlight (photochemical smog).
!PAIR Stratospheric ozone | shield | good || Ground-level ozone | pollutant | bad
### 10. OZONE DEPLETION AND TREATIES
- **Main cause: halocarbons** (CFCs, HCFCs) releasing **chlorine and bromine** (APPSC 2023). Maximum damage: **CFCs**.
| ODS | Use |
|---|---|
| CFCs | Refrigeration, ACs, aerosols, foam |
| **HCFCs** | CFC substitute – **also deplete ozone** |
| Halons | Fire extinguishers |
| Methyl bromide | Fumigant |
| Carbon tetrachloride, methyl chloroform | Solvents |
- **HFCs** spare ozone but have high global-warming potential; CFCs harm ozone and are strong GHGs.
- **Antarctic ozone hole**: **Aug–Nov** (southern spring), aided by **polar stratospheric clouds**; reported in **1985** by a **British** team (Farman, Gardiner, Shanklin).
- **2025 hole** peaked at just over **21 million sq km**, closed early; record **29 million sq km (2006)**.
| Treaty | Year | Point |
|---|---|---|
| **Vienna Convention** | **1985** | Framework: research, monitoring |
| **Montreal Protocol** | **1987** | Legally binding; ends ~**100** ODS; **198 parties** (197 states + EU) – universally ratified |
| **Kigali Amendment** | **2016** (Rwanda) | **HFC phase-down 80–85%**; in force **2019** |
- India: **first developing country** to phase out **HCFC-141b** in rigid foam; HCFCs cut **44%** vs 35% target by Jan 2020.
- AP: **Mission SANA** (Red Balloon Aerospace) – India's first home-made **stratospheric super-pressure balloon**, launched from **Vijayawada** (May 2026).
