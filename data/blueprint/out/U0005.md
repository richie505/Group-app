## U0005 | PRE-HARAPPAN AND EARLY HARAPPAN CULTURES – MEHRGARH, AMRI, KOT DIJI, KALIBANGAN-I
### 1. EARLY FARMING SITES
| Site | Region | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Mehrgarh** | **Baluchistan**, near the **Bolan Pass** | c. **7000 BCE**; first farming, domestication, pottery; earliest settled life; remains from **Stone Age to Harappan** |
| Kili Gul Muhammad | **Quetta valley** | Early farming village |
| Rana Ghundai | **Zhob-Loralai valley** | Farming and pastoral village |
| Damb Sadaat | **Quetta valley** | Early Harappan-phase village |
- Neolithic Baluchistan and Indus plains: pastoralism and limited cultivation → permanent villages; crops **wheat, barley, dates, cotton**; main tamed animals **sheep and goats**.
### 2. MEHRGARH – PERIODS AND BURIALS
| Period | Evidence |
|---|---|
| I (**aceramic** Neolithic, c. 7000 BCE) | Barley, wheat; sheep, goat, cattle; **mud-brick houses** |
| II | **Pottery** begins; **cotton** |
| III onward | **Copper** working, **wheel-made painted pottery**, beads |
- **Burials**: under or near houses, often **flexed**, with ornaments, tools, sometimes **goats**.
!TRAP Period I has NO pottery; pottery and cotton begin in Period II.
### 3. EARLY HARAPPAN CULTURES
- Early Harappan phase: **3300–2600 BCE**; called the **Regionalisation Era** (not Integration Era); corresponds to the **Hakra phase**.
| Culture | Type site / area | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Amri** | Sindh | Transition pre- to Mature Harappan; **rhinoceros** remains; later Jhangar |
| **Kot Diji** | Sindh | **Fortified**, destroyed by **fire**; many stone implements |
| **Sothi-Siswal** | Rajasthan–Haryana | – |
| **Hakra** | Ghaggar-Hakra, **Cholistan** (not Gujarat) | – |
| **Damb Sadaat** | Quetta | – |
- Other sites: Padri, Kalibangan, Dholavira, Harappa, Balakot, Bhirrana, Gumla. **Banawali**: pre- and Mature; **Mitathal**: pre-, Mature and Late.
- Pottery: regional **Amri, Kot Diji, Sothi-Siswal, Hakra** wares; Early Harappans used the **potter's wheel**; Mature pottery became uniform.
### 4. KALIBANGAN-I
- **Earliest ploughed field**; **criss-cross furrows** = **mixed cropping**.
- **Mud-brick fortification**; pottery in **six fabrics**.
- Excavated **1961–69** by **B. B. Lal** and **B. K. Thapar**.
!TRAP Ploughed field = pre-Harappan level; fire altars = Mature phase.

### 6. EARLY TO MATURE TRANSITION
- Known in the Early phase: **copper, the wheel, the plough**; granaries and defence walls.
- Kalibangan and Kot Diji foreshadow Mature buildings and pottery → **slow, local urbanisation**, not brought from Mesopotamia.
- Unsolved: the **gap between Neolithic Mehrgarh and the urban phase**.
- 23 Jun 2026: **Rakhigarhi** (~550 ha) – unbroken Early to Mature occupation; Mound 7 yielded 56 skeletons. Haryana protected **Tighrana** and **Mitathal** (Bhiwani).
