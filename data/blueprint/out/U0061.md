## U0061 | SANGAM SOCIETY AND ECONOMY – TINAI, INDO-ROMAN TRADE, PORTS, RELIGION; THE KALABHRA INTERREGNUM
### 1. TINAI – THE FIVE-FOLD DIVISION OF LAND
- The five-fold division of land (**tinai**) is given in the **Tolkappiyam**.
#### 1.1 Five Tinais – Landscape, Occupation, Deity
| Tinai | Landscape | Main occupation | Deity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kurinji** | Hilly | Hunting, honey gathering | **Murugan (Seyon)** |
| **Mullai** | Pastoral / forest | Cattle rearing | **Mayon (Vishnu)** |
| **Marutam** | Agricultural (riverine plains) | Agriculture | **Vendan (Indra)** |
| **Neidal** | Coastal | Fishing, salt making | **Varunan** |
| **Palai** | Desert / dry land | Robbery | **Korravai** |
!TRAP Marutam = agriculture and Vendan (Indra); Neidal = coast and Varunan. Do not swap them.
### 2. SOCIAL GROUPS – OCCUPATION AND STATUS
#### 2.1 Tolkappiyam's Four Castes (in order)
1. **Arasar** – ruling class; governance, law and order.
2. **Anthanar** – Brahmins, priests; political and religious influence.
3. **Vanigar** – traders / merchants.
4. **Vellalar** – rich peasants / agricultural landowners; the **fourth** caste.
#### 2.2 Other Groups
| Group | Occupation / status |
|---|---|
| **Uzhavar** | Ordinary farmers (tillers) |
| **Kadaisiyar, Adimai** | Landless labourers and slaves (lowest rung) |
| **Panar** | Wandering bards / minstrels who sang for chiefs |
| **Parayar** | Drummers; worked with animal skins |
| **Viraliyar** | Dancers who performed at courts; patronised by kings and nobles |
- A rigid **varna** system had not fully developed in early Sangam society, so social mobility was possible.
!TRAP Vellalar = landowners (fourth caste); Uzhavar = tillers; Kadaisiyar = labourers.
!KEY APPSC G1 2024: "Vellalars were **rich peasants** who formed the **fourth caste**" (distractors: poets, labourers, forest tribes).
### 3. SANGAM CUSTOMS AND TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Vattakirutal** | A king defeated in battle commits ritual suicide by **starving himself** to death |
| **Kalavu** | Pre-marital (secret) love |
| **Karpu** | Married life / wedded chastity |
| **Pukal** | Glory – the hero's goal in **puram** poems |
| **Nadukal** | Hero stone for a warrior who fell in battle |
!KEY UPSC 2023: Vattakirutal = ritual suicide of a defeated king by starvation.
### 4. RELIGION
- Chief deity: **Seyon / Murugan**; his six abodes = **Arupadai Veedu**.
- Other deities: **Mayon** (Vishnu), **Vendan** (Indra), **Varunan**, **Korravai** (goddess of war/victory).
- **Hero stones (Nadukal):** raised for warriors who fell in battle; carved with their deeds and inscriptions; found across Tamil Nadu.
- **Megalithic burials** were also practised.
### 5. WOMEN
- Women poets: **Avvaiyar, Nachchellaiyar, Kakkaipadiniyar** – contributed to Sangam poetry.
- **Viraliyar** – women dancers at courts.
- **Sati** was prevalent mainly in the higher strata of society; the life of widows was hard.
### 6. PORTS – KINGDOM, COAST, CLASSICAL NAME
| Port | Kingdom / coast | Classical name / note |
|---|---|---|
| **Muziris (Mushiri / Muchiri)** | **Chera** chief port; west (Malabar) coast | = **Pattanam**, Ernakulam (KCHR excavations); pepper port; Periplus: "abounding with ships"; main meeting point of Indo-Roman trade |
| **Tondi** | Chera; west coast | **Tyndis**; spices, textiles |
| **Naura** | West coast | Sangam-age port |
| **Korkai** | **Pandya**; east coast | Pearl fishery; exported pearls and shells |
| **Puhar (Kaveripattinam / Poompuhar)** | **Chola** port and capital; east coast | Cotton, muslin, silk; prosperous **Yavana quarter** |
| **Arikamedu** | Near Puducherry; east coast | **Poduke** (Periplus); "market street of the Romans" |
| **Uraiyur** | **Inland Chola capital** | Excavated urban and craft centre; **not a port** |
| **Motupalli** | **Kakatiya** (Andhra) seaport | Trade inscription on exports, imports and customs; visited by Marco Polo |
- Other ports: **Nelkynda, Bakare** (Kerala), **Marakkanam**.
- Tamil ports traded with **Berenike** and **Myos Hormos** on the Egyptian Red Sea coast.
!TRAP Uraiyur is the inland Chola capital, not a port. Motupalli is a Kakatiya port, not a Sangam port.
!KEY UPSC 2023: Korkai, Poompuhar and Muchiri were ports.
### 7. INDO-ROMAN TRADE
#### 7.1 Items Traded
| Exports | Imports |
|---|---|
| Pepper and spices (ginger, cardamom, cinnamon, turmeric), cotton and **muslin**, pearls, **ivory**, precious stones | **Gold**, horses, **sweet wine**; Roman coins |
- **Yavanapriya** ('beloved of the Yavanas') = **black pepper**, Rome's most prized Indian import, valued in Roman cooking.
- **Amphora:** tall, double-handled jar for wine and oil; found at Arikamedu, Kodumanal, Uraiyur, Alagankulam.
#### 7.2 Partners, Embassies, Coins
- South Indian power associated with trade with Rome: **Cheras** (BPSC 2022).
- Indian embassies visited Rome in several reigns; best known: **Pandya embassy to Augustus**.
- Gold and silver coins of **Augustus, Tiberius and Nero** found across Tamil Nadu.
- Hoards: **Akkanpalle, Kondapur** and Poduke (Arikamedu).
#### 7.3 Evidence – Excavators and Writers
| Person / source | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Sir Mortimer Wheeler** | Excavated **Arikamedu, 1945** |
| **Periplus of the Erythraean Sea** | c. 60 CE, by an anonymous Greek merchant – main source |
| **Pliny the Elder** (Natural History) | Rome lost **55 million sesterces** a year to India |
- Arikamedu finds: Roman amphorae, **Arretine ware**, rouletted ware, beads and gems, an intaglio identified as **Augustus Caesar**, Tamil-Brahmi potsherds.
- Arikamedu = foremost Indo-Roman trading station in eastern India (JPSC 2021); its digs prove India–Rome trade in the early Christian era.
- Tamil-Brahmi (Tamizhi) potsherds reached Berenike, Quseir al-Qadim and the Valley of the Kings (Egypt).
- Peak: **Satavahana–Sangam age (1st c. BCE–2nd c. CE)**; did **not** flourish after the Satavahanas (G2 2025).
- **Jun 2026:** Pattanam (Ernakulam) excavations continue to identify it with Muziris.
### 8. THE KALABHRA INTERREGNUM
| Aspect | Fact |
|---|---|
| Period | Ruled the Tamil country after the Sangam age, **c. 3rd–6th century CE** |
| Religion | Patrons of **Jainism and Buddhism**, not Vedic Brahmanism |
| "Dark age" debate | Called the **dark age** of Tamil history – later Brahmanical records show them as evil rulers, and sources are scarce |
| End | **Late 6th century**, by **Pandya Kadungon** and **Pallava Simhavishnu** |

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- Foremost Indo-Roman station on the east coast: Arikamedu (Poduke).
- Chief Tamil deity: Murugan; only goddess among tinai deities: Korravai (Palai).
- Kalabhras ended by Kadungon (Pandya) and Simhavishnu (Pallava).
