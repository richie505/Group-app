## U0618 | MARINE RESOURCES – FISHERIES, MINERALS, POLYMETALLIC NODULES, EEZ, BLUE ECONOMY
### 1. UNCLOS MARITIME ZONES AND INDIA'S EEZ
| Zone (from baseline) | Limit | Coastal-state rights |
|---|---|---|
| **Territorial sea** | **12 nm** | **Full sovereignty** (innocent passage for others) |
| **Contiguous zone** | **24 nm** | Enforce customs, fiscal, immigration, sanitary laws |
| **EEZ** | **200 nm** | **Sovereign rights** over living and non-living resources |
| Continental shelf | Up to **350 nm** | Seabed and subsoil |
| High seas | Beyond | Freedom of navigation |
- **UNCLOS** adopted **1982**, in force **1994**; set up the **International Seabed Authority (ISA)**.
- **India's EEZ**: about **2.37 million sq km**, world's **18th largest**; much added by **Andaman-Nicobar and Lakshadweep**.
- Indian law: **Maritime Zones Act, 1976** (Territorial Waters, Continental Shelf, EEZ and Other Maritime Zones).
!TRAP 12 nm = **sovereignty**; 200 nm EEZ = **sovereign rights only**.
### 2. SEABED MINERALS AND ISA CONTRACTS
| Mineral | Where | Metals |
|---|---|---|
| **Polymetallic nodules** | Central Indian Ocean Basin | **Nickel, copper, cobalt, manganese** |
| **Polymetallic sulphides** | **Hydrothermal vents**, mid-ocean ridges | Copper, zinc, lead, iron, silver, gold, platinum |
| **Gas hydrates** | **Methane ice** in sediments – KG basin, Mahanadi, Andaman (National Gas Hydrate Programme) | — |
| Year | ISA milestone |
|---|---|
| **1987** | India **first Pioneer Investor**, Central Indian Ocean Basin |
| 2002 | Contract 1: nodules, CIOB |
| 2016 | Contract 2: sulphides, Central and South West Indian Ridge |
| **15 Sep 2025** | Contract 3: sulphides, **Carlsberg Ridge** |
### 3. DEEP OCEAN MISSION AND OFFSHORE MINERALS
- **Deep Ocean Mission**: **Ministry of Earth Sciences** (not Ports/Shipping); approved **June 2021**; **₹4,077 crore**, 5 years; focus areas incl. ocean energy and freshwater.
- **Samudrayaan – Matsya 6000**: indigenous submersible, **3 crew to 6,000 m**.
- **OAMDR Act 2002** (amended 2023): first tranche of **13 offshore blocks**, **28 Nov 2024**, composite licence.
| Resource | Places |
|---|---|
| Oil and gas | **Mumbai High**, **Krishna-Godavari**, Cauvery, Gulf of Khambhat |
| **Beach placers**: monazite (**thorium**), ilmenite (**titanium**) | **Chavara** (Kerala), **Manavalakurichi** (TN), **Chatrapur** (Odisha), AP |
!TRAP **Chavara** is a placer site, not an oil basin.
### 4. WORLD FISHERIES
| Ground | Location |
|---|---|
| **Grand Banks** | Off Newfoundland, NW Atlantic – **Gulf Stream meets Labrador** |
| **NW Pacific** | Off Japan – **Kuroshio meets Oyashio** |
| **Dogger Bank** | Shallow sandbank, **North Sea**, ~100 km off eastern England |
- Favourable: **wide shallow shelves**, current mixing, **upwelling**, indented coasts.
- Fishing-dependent: Norway, Japan, **Iceland** (exports depend most on fish).
- **Pearls**: **Japan** (cultured Akoya) = classic largest producer; by volume **China** now leads.
### 5. INDIAN FISHERIES
- India **2nd** fish producer after **China**, ~8–9% of world output; **inland >75%**; seafood exports **₹62,408 crore** (FY 2024-25), led by frozen shrimp.
- **National fish: Indian Mackerel**. Blue Revolution = fisheries/aquaculture.
| Zone | Rule |
|---|---|
| 0–12 nm | **State** subject; **61-day** mechanised fishing ban |
| 12–200 nm | Centre; **EEZ Rules 2025** – Access Pass for vessels **≥24 m** via **ReALCraft**, zero fee; traditional crafts exempt |
- **PMMSY (2020)**: Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying; subsidy **40%** general, **60%** women/SC/ST.
- **Sep 2026**: Supreme Court asked Tamil Nadu to frame a channel letting purse-seiners reach the EEZ.
### 6. BLUE ECONOMY AND SAGAR
- Sustainable use of ocean, coastal and inland waters for growth, jobs, conservation.
- **Draft Blue Economy Policy Framework, 2021 – MoES**.
- **SAGAR** = "Security and Growth for All in the Region" – **2015, Mauritius**.
### 7. MARINE RESOURCES OF AP
- ~**30%** of national fish, **31%** of shrimp exports (**Vannamei**); districts: West Godavari, Eluru, Krishna, Bapatla, Nellore, Konaseema, Kakinada.
- **10 fishing harbours** (₹3,699.09 crore) under **PMMSY, not Sagarmala**.
- Oil and gas: **KG basin**; beach sand minerals: Srikakulam–Bhimunipatnam coast.
- **Centre of Excellence on Blue Economy**: Vignan's University, **Vadlamudi (Guntur)**, Aug 2026.
