### 4. POWER DISTRIBUTION REFORMS - RDSS
- **RDSS** (Revamped Distribution Sector Scheme): launched **2021**, outlay **₹3.03 lakh crore**.
- About **₹10,000 crore** for **rural power networks** (17 Sep 2026).
- **State Distribution Reforms Committee** (chaired by the **Chief Secretary**) cleared **₹522 crore** of **loss-reduction works** for **Godavari Pushkaralu 2027**.

### 5. DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE AND DATA CENTRES
- **AP FiberNet Phase 3**: broadband to **more than 4,000 tribal hamlets** (Apr 2026).
- **Google AI hub, Visakhapatnam**: **$15 billion**, 2026-2030; announced **October 2025**, ground broken **April 2026**; gigawatt-scale data-centre campus with a new **international subsea cable gateway**; partners **Adani** (data-centre venture) and **Bharti Airtel**.

### 6. NATIONAL HIGHWAYS, EXPRESSWAYS AND RAILWAY ZONE
#### 6.1 Highways
- NH projects worth **more than ₹1.40 lakh crore** under way, to finish **by 2029** (30 Jan 2026); **64 NH projects** worth over **₹1 lakh crore** (May 2026).
- **Buttaigudem-Kovvur**: two-laning, **45 km**, **₹840 crore** (Aug 2026).
- **Port link**: **5-km four-lane road** from **Kakinada deep-sea port** to **NH-216**.
- **PYQ (APPSC 2024 key):** **July 2023**, **Nitin Gadkari** laid foundation stones for **three NH projects** at **Tirupati** (incl. **Thamminapatnam-Narikellapalle** and the **Krishnapatnam Port link**).
- **Greenfield expressways:** **Raipur-Visakhapatnam** and **Nagpur-Vijayawada**; other corridor: **Kharagpur-Visakhapatnam-Amaravati**.
#### 6.2 South Coast Railway zone
- Gazette notification (May 2026); **headquarters Visakhapatnam**; operational from **1 June 2026**; India's **18th railway zone**.
- Four divisions: **Guntakal, Guntur, Vijayawada** and **Visakhapatnam** (renamed Waltair division).

### 9. IRRIGATION - POLAVARAM-NALLAMALA SAGAR LINK
