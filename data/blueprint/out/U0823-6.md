### 1. INDIAN AND JOINT SPACE MISSIONS
#### 1.1 NISAR - NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar
- **NISAR** = the **first ISRO-NASA joint satellite** and the **world's first dual-frequency radar satellite**.
- Launched on **30 Jul 2025** by **GSLV-F16** from **Sriharikota**.
- Partners: **ISRO + NASA/JPL**.
- Dual radar: **L-band SAR by NASA** + **S-band SAR by ISRO**.
- Cost **$1.5 billion**: the **costliest Earth-observation satellite**.
- Scans nearly all of Earth **every 12 days**.
- Objectives: **land deformation** (earthquakes, landslides, **land subsidence**), **ice sheets and glaciers**, ecosystems and **biomass**, disasters.
!TRAP NISAR flew on GSLV-F16, not PSLV. L-band = NASA; S-band = ISRO (do not swap).

#### 1.3 Gaganyaan and Vyommitra
- **Gaganyaan**: ground-tracking support from **ESA** (not NASA).
- **Vyommitra**: **semi-humanoid** robot for the **uncrewed G1 and G2** flights.
#### 1.4 Criticism and Debates
- After **two consecutive PSLV failures**, ISRO staff unions questioned **outsourcing under the 2023 Space Policy** (the policy was **not abolished**).
- About **100 ISRO staff quit**; a **DoS memo (14 Jul 2026)** tried to stop the exodus.

### 10. BLOOD AND IMMUNITY TERMS
- **ABO blood groups** discovered by **Karl Landsteiner** (published **1901**).
- Antigens sit on red blood cells; antibodies are in the plasma.
| Blood group | Antigen on RBC | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **O negative** | No A, no B, no Rh | **Universal donor** (red cells) |
| **AB positive** | A and B, Rh | **Universal recipient** |
- **Rh factor**: present = **Rh positive (+)**; absent = **Rh negative (-)**.
!TRAP For plasma the universal donor is AB, not O.
