## U0345 | COMPTROLLER AND AUDITOR GENERAL – APPOINTMENT, DUTIES, TYPES OF AUDIT, AUDIT REPORTS AND PAC LINKAGE
### 1. STATUS AND NAME
- Official name: **Comptroller and Auditor-General of India** (not "Controller").
- **Part V, Chapter V**, Articles **148–151** (not Part XII); an **independent constitutional authority** created by Art. 148 – NOT a statutory body under the Finance Ministry.
- Head of the **Indian Audit and Accounts Department**; "**Guardian of the Public Purse**".
- **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar**: the **most important officer** under the Constitution.
- Four bulwarks of democracy: **CAG, Supreme Court, UPSC, Election Commission**.
#### 1.1 Articles 148–151
| Article | Content |
|---|---|
| 148 | Appointment, oath, term, removal; 148(4) post-retirement bar; 148(6) expenses charged |
| 149 | **Duties and powers** |
| 150 | **Form of accounts** – prescribed by the **President on CAG's advice** |
| 151 | **Audit reports**: Union → President (151(1)); State → Governor (151(2)) |
### 2. APPOINTMENT, TERM AND REMOVAL
- Appointed by the **President** by **warrant under his hand and seal** (Art. 148(1)) – not PM, FM or PAC.
- **Oath** before the President; form in the **Third Schedule**.
- **Term: 6 years or age 65**, whichever earlier (Art. 148(3) + CAG Act, 1971) (APPSC 2022).
- **Removal** like a **Supreme Court judge** (Art. 148(1) with Art. 124(4)): address by both Houses, **special majority**, proved misbehaviour or incapacity.
- Does **not** hold office at the President's pleasure; resigns to the President.
- **Constitution sets no qualifications** for the CAG.
- Other appointments by the President: Attorney General (Art. 76), Governor (Art. 155).
#### 2.1 Term Compared
| Office | Term / retirement |
|---|---|
| **CAG** | 6 years or 65 |
| CEC / ECs | 6 years or 65 (Election Commission Act 1991, now 2023) |
| UPSC members | 6 years or 65 |

!KEY APPSC-G2 2017: Longest term among President, VP, MP and CAG = **CAG**.
### 3. INDEPENDENCE SAFEGUARDS
- Salary = **SC judge**; salary and service conditions fixed by Parliament – **Second Schedule (Part E)**; cannot be varied to his disadvantage.
- **Leave** granted or refused by the **President**.
- Expenses (salaries, allowances, pensions of CAG and staff, office costs) **charged on the CFI** – **non-votable** (Art. 148(6)).
- **Complete bar** on further office under GoI or any State (Art. 148(4)); UPSC Chairman also barred, but retired judges often head commissions.
- **No Minister** represents the CAG in Parliament.
- Order of Precedence: CAG comes first among the usual options.
### 4. DUTIES – ARTICLE 149 AND THE 1971 ACT
- **CAG (Duties, Powers and Conditions of Service) Act, 1971**.
- **1976 amendment** (in force **1 March 1976**): **accounting separated from audit**; Union accounts now by the **CGA** (Ministry of Finance); CAG still keeps most **State accounts**.
#### 4.1 What the CAG Audits
- Receipts and expenditure of the **Consolidated Funds** of India, States and UTs with legislature.
- **Contingency Funds** and **Public Accounts**; trading, manufacturing, profit-and-loss accounts; stores and stocks.
- **Government companies** and bodies **substantially financed** by government.
- **Local bodies** only on request of President/Governor; Sixth Schedule councils covered.
- Audit is **post-expenditure** (ex-post facto).
- Upholds the Constitution and laws of Parliament in money matters; checks **legality and propriety**.
### 5. TYPES OF AUDIT
| Type | Nature | Tests |
|---|---|---|
| **Legal and regulatory** (compliance) | Obligatory | Money legally available, spent as rules require |
| **Financial attest** | Obligatory | Accounts true |
| **Propriety** | Discretionary | Wisdom, faithfulness, economy; flags extravagance |
| **Performance** | Discretionary | 3 Es: **economy, efficiency, effectiveness** |
- **Social audits** feed CAG performance audits; MGNREGA social audit standards **2016**.
!PAIR Obligatory | legal + regulatory, financial attest | must do || Discretionary | propriety, performance | may do
### 6. AUDIT OF PSUs AND LIMITS
| Category | Example |
|---|---|
| Audited directly by CAG | **DVC, ONGC** |
| Private auditors on CAG's advice + CAG supplementary audit | Central Warehousing Corp., **IFCI** |
| Wholly private audit (no CAG role) | **LIC, RBI** |
- Some public sector enterprises are audited by **private professional auditors**.
- **Secret service expenditure**: CAG accepts a **certificate** of the competent authority.
- Only an **Auditor General**, not a Comptroller: **no control over issue of money** from the Consolidated Fund (British C&AG does control it).
- **Cannot disallow** expenditure, enforce recovery or prosecute; only reports.
!TRAP APPSC-G2 2017: "CAG controls the entire financial system of the country" – WRONG.
### 7. AUDIT REPORTS – ARTICLE 151
#### 7.1 Report to Action
1. CAG report → 2. **President / Governor** → 3. Laid before **Parliament / Legislature** → 4. **PAC** examines (PSUs: **Committee on Public Undertakings**) → 5. **Action Taken Notes** by the executive.
- Three Union reports: **Appropriation Accounts, Finance Accounts, Public Undertakings** (not Estimates).
- **Final action** on reports rests with **Parliament** / State Legislature.
!TRAP Reports go first to the President/Governor, **not directly** to the PAC.
#### 7.2 Public Accounts Committee
- Set up **1921** (GoI Act, 1919); **22 members (15 LS + 7 RS)**, elected yearly by **STV**; **no Minister**.
- Chair appointed by the Speaker, from the **Opposition since 1967**; 2026-27 chair **K.C. Venugopal** (earlier: Murali Manohar Joshi); reports to the **Speaker**.
- CAG = PAC's "**friend, philosopher and guide**" (attends meetings; not its chairman).
- Recommendations **not binding**; backlog of Action Taken Notes (23 May 2026).
### 8. AP ANGLE – STATE FINANCES
- AP reports go to the **Governor of AP** (Art. 151(2)).
- After 2014, reports on undivided AP went to Governors of both **AP and Telangana**.
- CAG State Finances report (2022-23): revenue receipts **₹1,57,768 crore**; own tax revenue **₹78,026 crore** (about half); own tax **5.92%** and central transfers **5.64%** of GSDP.
