## U0227 | GOVERNMENT OF INDIA ACT 1935 – PROVISIONS, PROVINCIAL AUTONOMY, 1937 ELECTIONS AND CONGRESS MINISTRIES
### 1. OVERVIEW
- Exam: **Group 1 Prelims** | Tier: **MED** | Row: A-187
- PYQ importance: **APPSC 5 questions** | other exams 34 | last APPSC year 2023
- Chain: Act 1935 → Congress rejects (Lucknow 1936) → contests (Faizpur 1936) → elections 1936-37 → ministries 1937 → resignation 1939.
### 2. PROVISIONS – IMPLEMENTED OR NOT
| Provision | Implemented? |
|---|---|
| **All-India Federation** (provinces + princely states) | **No** – too few princes acceded |
| **Dyarchy at the Centre** | **No** – part of the federal scheme; Centre ran under the 1919 Act till 1947 |
| **Provincial autonomy** | **Yes** – from **1 April 1937** (Governors kept reserve powers) |
| **Federal Court** | **Yes** – **Delhi, October 1937** |
| **Council of India** (advised the Secretary of State) | **Abolished** |
| **Residuary powers** | Vested in the **Governor-General** |
| **Separate electorates** | Extended to **depressed classes, women and labour** |
!TRAP Residuary powers under the 1935 Act = **Governor-General**, not the provinces or the Centre.

#### 3.2 Discretionary Powers
- The **Governor** had **discretionary powers**, e.g. the **Section 63** address to the provincial legislature "in his discretion".
- Sec. 63 → **Article 175** of the Constitution; the speech now reflects the elected ministry's policy.
- Latest (26 Jan 2026): Governors' address rows in Opposition-ruled States were traced to Sec. 63 and Art. 175.

### 5. 1937 ELECTIONS – RESULTS BY PROVINCE
- Held in **winter 1936-37** in **11 provinces**.
| Province | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **Madras** | Congress majority; **Justice Party almost wiped out**; Pithapuram Zamindar's **People's Party lost all seats** |
| **Bombay** | Congress largest party but **no absolute majority**; formed ministry |
| **Bengal** | Congress **no absolute majority**; coalition led by **Fazlul Huq's Krishak Praja Party** |
| **Punjab** | **Unionist Party** – **Sikandar Hayat Khan**; no Congress ministry |
| **Sindh** | Coalition under **Allah Baksh** |
- **Muslim League** did poorly **even in Muslim seats** → alleged "**Hindu Raj**" and launched **mass-contact** campaigns.
### 6. MADRAS AFTER THE 1937 ELECTIONS
- Madras polls: **15-20 February 1937**; campaign led by **Tanguturi Prakasam** (APCC President) and **Bulusu Sambamurthy**.
- Only a few Justice Party men (like the Challapalli Zamindar) won; League and Hindu Mahasabha also did poorly.
- Congress ministry, **14 July 1937**: Premier **C. Rajagopalachari** (APHQ 2011).
| Andhra leader | Post |
|---|---|
| **T. Prakasam** | **Revenue** Minister |
| **Bezawada Gopala Reddy** | **Local Self-Government** |
| **V.V. Giri** | **Labour and Industries** |
| **Bulusu Sambamurthy** | **Speaker** |
- **Hindi** in Madras schools (Rajaji) → **Periyar's** anti-Hindi agitation (1937) → withdrawn.
- AP angle: provincial autonomy made an Andhra state look achievable; **Sri Bagh Pact (November 1937)** at **Kasinathuni Nageswara Rao's** house "**Sreerbagh**" to end the Coastal–Rayalaseema split.
!PAIR Prakasam | Revenue | Minister || Sambamurthy | Speaker | Madras Assembly
### 7. CONGRESS MINISTRIES, 1937-39
#### 7.1 Formation
- Ministries in **8 of 11** provinces: **Bombay, Madras, Central Provinces, Orissa, Bihar, United Provinces** (July 1937), later **NWFP** and **Assam**.
- **Clear majority** on its own in **5** provinces: Madras, UP, CP, Bihar, Orissa.
- Gandhi: hold office "**lightly, not tightly**" – a "**crown of thorns**".
#### 7.2 Legislative Record
| Area | Measure |
|---|---|
| Civil liberties | Emergency powers lifted; **political prisoners released**; bans on leftist bodies lifted; **press freedom** restored |
| Agrarian | **Tenancy Acts** (e.g. UP); **begar (forced labour) abolished**; debt relief |
| Labour | **Industrial Disputes Act (1938)**; trade-union rights |
| Harijans | Free education and other upliftment measures |
#### 7.3 Resignation
- **3 September 1939**: Viceroy **Lord Linlithgow** declared India at war with **Germany** without consulting Indian leaders.
- Congress ministries in all provinces **resigned Oct–Nov 1939** (APPSC 2018: reason).
- League's "**Deliverance Day**" – **22 December 1939**; **Lahore Resolution** – **23 March 1940**.
### 8. FEATURES BORROWED INTO THE CONSTITUTION
| Source | Borrowed |
|---|---|
| **GoI Act 1935** | **Federal scheme**, **office of Governor**, **judiciary**, **Public Service Commissions**, emergency provisions, administrative details |
| **Instrument of Instructions** (1935) | Model for the **Directive Principles** (APPSC 2023); critics called this part "holy phrases" |
| **GoI Act 1919** | **Provincial dyarchy** (not carried over), **bicameralism** and **direct elections** at the Centre; PSC; separate provincial budget |
!KEY Instrument of Instructions → **DPSP**, not Fundamental Rights.

### 11. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. **Federation** and **dyarchy at the Centre** never came into force.
2. Residuary powers = **Governor-General**.
3. Congress ministries = **8 of 11**; clear majority in **5**.
4. **No** Congress ministry in **Punjab**, Bengal or Sindh.
5. Congress had **no absolute majority** in **Bombay** or Bengal.
6. Resigned **Oct–Nov 1939** over the war, not over Quit India.
7. "Strong brakes but no engine" = **Nehru**; "thoroughly rotten" = **Jinnah**.
8. Prakasam = **Revenue**; Bezawada Gopala Reddy = **Local Self-Government**; V.V. Giri = **Labour**.
9. Sri Bagh Pact = **November 1937**, Kasinathuni Nageswara Rao's house.
