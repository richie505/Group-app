## U0498 | LAND RECORDS AND TITLING – DIGITAL INDIA LAND RECORDS MODERNISATION, SVAMITVA
### 1. KEY TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ULPIN (Bhu-Aadhaar)** | **14-digit alphanumeric** parcel ID from geo-coordinates of a parcel's vertices; standards ECCMA + OGC |
| **NGDRS** | "**One Nation One Registration Software**" under DILRMP – common, configurable application for sub-registrars and citizens |
| **N-LRSI** | **NCAER** Land Records and Services Index – grades States on digitisation and quality of land records |
- **Clear titles** → land as **collateral**, less litigation (~80% of AP public petitions are land disputes, Feb 2026).
### 2. PRESUMPTIVE VS CONCLUSIVE TITLING
| Presumptive (present) | Conclusive |
|---|---|
| Registration only **records a transaction**; title **can be challenged** | **State guarantees** title; cannot be disputed once issued |
| Registration, survey, revenue records kept separately | Single title register |
### 3. DILRMP
- Began as **NLRMP (2008)** = **CLR + SRA&ULR** merged (UPPSC 2016); renamed **Digital India Land Records Modernisation Programme**.
- **Central Sector, 100% central funding** from **1 April 2016** (earlier centrally sponsored); extended **2021–22 to 2025–26**.
- Components: modern record rooms, survey/resurvey, **digitised cadastral maps**, computerised registration, State data centre, core GIS.
- New: **consent-based Aadhaar seeding**, **computerisation of revenue courts**; mandatory **gender column** in RoR.
| Phase (DILRMP 3.0) | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 (2008–16) | Digitisation, resurvey |
| 2 (2016–26) | **ULPIN, NGDRS**, multilingual records, e-Court integration |
| 3 (2026–31) | **GIS Land Stack** |
- Progress: **RoR 99.8%**; cadastral maps **97.24%**; SROs **95.73%**; ULPIN in 29 States/UTs, 36.67 crore parcels; NGDRS in 18 States/UTs.
- **Land Stack** (National Digital Land Initiative): cloud GIS, a Bhu-Aadhaar for every parcel.
### 4. SVAMITVA
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Full form | **Survey of Villages and Mapping with Improvised Technology in Village Areas** |
| Launch | **24 April 2020** (National Panchayati Raj Day); nationwide 2021 |
| Ministry | **Panchayati Raj** (not Mines); Central Sector |
| Agencies | **Survey of India**; NICSI (technology) |
| Coverage | **Abadi areas only** – not agricultural land |
| Output | **Property Cards**; **2.76 crore+** |
| Target | **6.62 lakh villages**; ₹566.23 crore |
- Drone survey in 3.28 lakh villages (Dec 2025); AP among pilot States.
### 5. TITLING ACTS AND RULING
- **NITI Aayog Model Conclusive Land Titling Act, 2020**: adoption is each **State's choice**; fewer disputes, easier credit, lower costs.
- **SC (1 Jan 2026)**: **registration of a transfer deed is distinct from title**; Bihar's compulsory mutation-proof rule struck down.
- **AP Land Titling Act, 2022** (Act 27 of 2023): presumptive → conclusive, **State as guarantor**; criticised for limiting disputes to the High Court; **repealed 2024** (Act 11 of 2024).
### 6. AP LAND RECORDS
| Platform | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Bhu Bharathi** | Computerisation, united AP, **1992**; pilot **Nizamabad** |
| **Webland** | AP's digitised records platform |
| **CARD** | Complete computerisation of registration; anywhere registration, auto-mutation, at Grama Sachivalayams – **not a court** |
| **Mee Bhoomi-Blockchain** | Pilot **24 July 2026**, 7 mandals |
- **Resurvey** (Jagananna Saswatha Bhu Hakku and Bhu Raksha) from **Dec 2020**: drones, CORS, GIS; **6,901 villages, 81 lakh parcels**.
- **QR pattadar passbooks** (Jan 2026) with **State emblem**: **36 lakh** issued; 56 lakh more by March 2027.
- **N-LRSI**: AP **10th** (2020) → **6th** (2021); Madhya Pradesh top.
- **Karnataka Bhoomi (2000)**: first to computerise RTCs. Bhoomi, Bhu Bharathi = State projects; SVAMITVA, NAKSHA = later schemes.
