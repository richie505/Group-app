## U0625 | HIMALAYAN DRAINAGE SYSTEM – INDUS, GANGA AND BRAHMAPUTRA BASINS
### 1. HIMALAYAN DRAINAGE – CHARACTER AND RIVER GROUPS
#### 1.1 Four Groups of Indian Rivers
| Group | Example |
|---|---|
| Himalayan | Indus, Ganga, Brahmaputra |
| Deccan (Peninsular) | Godavari, Krishna, Mahanadi |
| Coastal | Short west-coast streams |
| Inland drainage | Western Rajasthan – **Ghaggar, Luni, Rupen** |
- **Inland drainage**: the river does not reach the sea; it ends in an inland lake or sand. River **Khari** is part of **indeterminate drainage**.
#### 1.2 Character of Himalayan Rivers
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Antecedent** | Rivers existed before the Himalayan uplift; kept their course by cutting deep gorges. Features: **valley-in-valley**, paired terraces, **entrenched meanders** |
| **Perennial** | Fed by **rain + snowmelt** |
| Plains behaviour | Gentle slope → meanders, shifting channels, **ox-bow lakes**, floodplains |
- Antecedent examples: **Indus** (gorge at Nanga Parbat), **Sutlej** (cuts across all Himalayan ranges; a **Trans-Himalayan** river), Brahmaputra (Dihang gorge).
- In the APPSC key, the **Yamuna** was also taken as an antecedent river older than the Himalayas.
!KEY APPSC A-R: "Himalayan rivers are perennial" (A) because "they are fed by rain and snowmelt" (R) – both true, R explains A.
### 2. RIVERS ORIGINATING NEAR MANSAROVAR – WHICH DO AND WHICH DO NOT
| Originate in the Mansarovar–Kailash area | Do NOT |
|---|---|
| **Indus** – Singge Khabab (Kailash range, 5,182 m) | Ganga/Bhagirathi – Gangotri (Gomukh) |
| **Sutlej** – **Rakas Tal** (Rakshastal), Manasarovar–Rakas lakes, 4,570 m | Yamuna – Yamunotri |
| **Brahmaputra** – **Chemayungdung glacier**, east of Mansarovar | Chenab – Baralacha La |
| **Karnali** (Ghaghara) | Jhelum – Verinag |
| | **Beas** – Beas Kund near **Rohtang Pass** |
| | Ravi |
- **Brahmaputra and Indus** rise at almost the same point – the Tibetan Plateau, "**Roof of the World**".
- Sutlej rises within **80 km** of the Indus source, flows north-west and enters India via **Shipki La** (HP); it does **not** originate in India.
- **Kailash Mansarovar** lies in Chinese **Tibet** (visa needed); Mt Kailash is in the **Kailash Range, Trans-Himalaya**.
!TRAP Beas does NOT rise near Mansarovar (Rohtang); Ganga, Yamuna and Chenab do not either.

### 3. INDUS SYSTEM
#### 3.1 Course
- Rises at **Singge Khabab** near Mansarovar; flows west between the **Ladakh and Zaskar** ranges.
- **Leh** lies on its **right bank** (UT of Ladakh since **31 Oct 2019**); **Zanskar** joins at **Nimu**.
- Turns south at **Nanga Parbat** through an antecedent gorge → **Arabian Sea near Karachi**; flows through north-western India and Pakistan.
#### 3.2 Tributaries (west to east) and Ancient Names
| River | Greek | Vedic | Source / note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jhelum | **Hydaspes** | Vitasta (Kashmiri: Vyeth) | Spring at **Verinag**, foot of Pir Panjal; Srinagar, **Wular Lake** |
| Chenab | **Acesines** | Asikni | **Largest** tributary; **Chandra + Bhaga** at **Tandi** (HP) |
| Ravi | **Hydraotes** | Parushni | Joins Chenab in Pakistan |
| Beas | **Hyphasis** | Vipasa | Beas Kund, Rohtang; joins Sutlej at **Harike** (Punjab) |
| Sutlej | Zaradros | Shutudri | Rakas Tal; receives Chenab |
- Chain: **Jhelum + Ravi → Chenab → Sutlej → Indus**. Only the **Sutlej** joins the Indus directly.
- Tributaries of Sutlej: **Baspa, Spiti, Beas**. Ravi is a tributary of the Chenab, only a sub-tributary of the Sutlej.
- **Spiti** rises at Kunzam La; joins Sutlej near **Khab** (Kinnaur). Ludhiana and Firozpur lie on the Sutlej's old bank.
- **Kishanganga** = **Neelum** in Pakistan; main tributary of the **Jhelum** (not Sutlej).
- **Chandra** rises near Chandra Tal, **Bhaga** near Bara Lacha La; both flow in **Lahaul** valley → Chandrabhaga.
- Rivers of Himachal Pradesh: Beas, Chenab, Ravi, Sutlej, Yamuna. **Mahanadi** is not an Indus tributary.
- Sutlej facts: antecedent, flows through **three countries**, rises on the Tibetan Plateau, important for irrigation.
!PAIR Hydaspes | Jhelum | Verinag || Hyphasis | Beas | Rohtang
#### 3.3 Indus Waters Treaty, 1960
| Rivers | Allotted to |
|---|---|
| Eastern – **Ravi, Beas, Sutlej** | India |
| Western – **Indus, Jhelum, Chenab** | Mainly Pakistan |
- **Tulbul** (navigation) project – Jhelum, mouth of Wular Lake; proposed **1984**, Pakistan protested **1987**.
- **Salal** – 690 MW, **NHPC**, Chenab – India's first project in the region under the treaty (Jan 2026).
- **Baglihar** – Chenab, **Ramban** district; run-of-the-river; Pakistan took it to the **World Bank**.
- **Dulhasti** – 390 MW, Chenab, **Kishtwar** district.
- **World's highest arch bridge** is on the Chenab; highest bridge in all categories is Duge Bridge, China.
### 4. GANGA – COURSE
#### 4.1 Headwaters and Course
1. **Bhagirathi** – Gangotri glacier at **Gomukh**, **Uttarkashi** district; cuts through Central and Lesser Himalayas.
2. **Alaknanda** – **Satopanth** glacier above Badrinath.
3. Meet at **Devprayag** (Tehri Garhwal district; on Rishikesh–Badrinath route) → called **Ganga**.
4. Plains at **Haridwar**; navigable up to **Prayagraj**, where the **Yamuna** joins.
5. Flows past **Rajmahal hills**; at **Farakka** (WB): left arm **Bhagirathi-Hooghly**, right arm **Padma** into Bangladesh.
6. Padma + Brahmaputra (Jamuna) + Meghna → **Bay of Bengal as the Meghna**.
- **Uttarkashi** is on the **Bhagirathi**, not the Ganga; **Fatehpur, Bhagalpur, Kanpur** are on the Ganga.
#### 4.2 Confluences (Panchprayag on the Alaknanda)
| Prayag | Rivers meeting Alaknanda |
|---|---|
| **Vishnuprayag** | Dhauliganga |
| **Nandprayag** | Nandakini |
| **Karnaprayag** | Pindar |
| **Rudraprayag** | Mandakini |
| **Devprayag** | Bhagirathi – Ganga begins |
| Prayagraj | Ganga + Yamuna |
- Order upstream to downstream: Vishnuprayag → Nandprayag → Karnaprayag → Rudraprayag → Devprayag.
#### 4.3 Length and Basin
- Ganga: **2,525 km**; basin drains about **26%** of India's area (about **8.6 lakh sq km** in India) and supports about **40%** of its population.
### 5. GANGA – TRIBUTARIES BY BANK
#### 5.1 Left vs Right Bank
| Bank | Tributaries |
|---|---|
| Left (Himalayan) | **Ramganga, Gomti, Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi, Mahananda** |
| Right (Peninsular) | **Yamuna, Son, Tons, Punpun, Damodar** |
- **Yamuna** = **westernmost and longest** tributary; **Son** = second-largest right-bank tributary.
- **Mahananda** = **last, easternmost** left-bank tributary; joins in **West Bengal**.
- Order below Prayagraj (west→east): Tons (R) → **Gomti** (Ghazipur) → **Ghaghara** (near Chapra) → **Son** (R) → **Gandak** (near Patna) → **Kosi** (Katihar) → Mahananda.
- **Burhi Dihing and Dhansiri** are **Brahmaputra** tributaries, not the Ganga's.
#### 5.2 Left-bank Tributaries in Detail
| River | Facts |
|---|---|
| **Ramganga** | Joins Ganga near **Kannauj**; **Kalagarh Dam** (Ramganga Dam), Uttarakhand, 715 m long, 128 m high |
| **Ghaghara** | **Mapchachungo Glacier**; joins at **Revilganj** (Saran, Bihar); receives **Sharda (Kali)** |
| **Gandak** | **Narayani/Gandaki** in Nepal; **Sadanira** of Shatapatha Brahmana; streams Kaligandak and Trishulganga; rises between Dhaulagiri and Everest; enters India at **Valmikinagar** (not Raxaul); joins at **Sonpur** near Patna |
| **Kosi** | **Maximum shifting** of course; dangerous floods; **7** tributaries – **Arun** most important (Sun Kosi, Tamur Kosi) |
| **Gomti** | Only plains-fed tributary |
- **Mahakali Treaty** – India–Nepal, **Feb 1996**; Mahakali = **Sharda** in India; Sharda and **Tanakpur** barrages.
- Nepal flash floods raised the Narayani-Gandak → alerts in Bihar and UP (28 Aug 2026).
#### 5.3 Son and Damodar
- **Son** flows **northwards** from Amarkantak; joins Ganga near **Maner (Patna)**. Tributaries: Johilla, Banas, Gopad, **Rihand**, Kanhar, **North Koel**.
- **Kaimur Range** – water divide between Yamuna and Son.
- **Damodar** – **fault (rift) valley** drainage; "**Sorrow of Bengal**"; flows into the **Hugli**; main tributary **Barakar** (also Konar, Jamunia, Barki).
- **Rajarappa** – confluence of Damodar and Bhera (Bhairavi); **Chinnamastika** temple, near Ramgarh.
- Catchments: **Son 70,055 > Gandak 45,731 > Ramganga 30,811 > Mahananda 23,700** sq km.
- **Bihar–Jharkhand MoU (2026)** on Son ended a 25-year dispute: of **7.75 MAF** under the **Bansagar Accord, 1973**, Bihar **5.75**, Jharkhand **2** MAF (1 Sep 2026).
#### 5.4 Yamuna and its Tributaries
- Rises at **Yamunotri Glacier**, western slopes of **Banderpunch (Bandar Poonch)**; joins Ganga at Prayagraj.
| Bank | Tributaries of the Yamuna |
|---|---|
| Right | **Chambal** (joins in **Etawah**), **Sindh**, **Betwa** (joins at **Hamirpur**), **Ken** |
| Left | **Tons**, **Hindon**, Hanuman Ganga, Sasur Khaderi |
- **Ramganga** is a Ganga tributary, not the Yamuna's. **Chambal** joins the Yamuna, not the Narmada. **Betwa** does not join the Chambal.
### 6. BRAHMAPUTRA SYSTEM
#### 6.1 Names Across Countries
| Region | Name |
|---|---|
| Tibet | **Tsangpo / Yarlung Zangbo / Sanpu**; turns south at **Namche Barwa** |
| Arunachal | **Dihang / Siang** |
| Assam | Brahmaputra (after **Dibang + Lohit** join near **Pasighat/Sadiya**) |
| Bangladesh | **Jamuna** (below Dhubri); joins Padma at **Goalundo** |
- Rises at **Chemayungdung Glacier** (Kailash range); Yarlung Zangbo is also traced to **Angsi glacier**, western Tibet.
- Length **2,900 km**, only **885 km** in India; **9th** largest river in the world by volume; navigable **1,280 km** up to **Dibrugarh**.
- Catchment: Tibet, Bhutan, India, Bangladesh. "**Sorrow of Assam**" – floods; partly man-made (roads without drainage, deforestation, silted channels – Aug 2026).
- **Zangmu** dam on the Brahmaputra in Tibet (China, from **Nov 2010**).
#### 6.2 Tributaries
| Right bank (north, Himalayan) | Left bank (south) |
|---|---|
| **Subansiri, Kameng (Jia Bharali), Manas, Sankosh**, Puthimari, Pagladiya, **Teesta** | **Burhi Dihing, Dhansiri** (Nagaland hills), Dikhu, Kopili |
- Also direct tributaries: **Dibang, Lohit, Manas, Kopili**.
- **Sankosh** – rises in northern Bhutan (Puna Tsang Chhu); forms the **Assam–West Bengal** boundary.
- **Teesta** – 414 km, Sikkim and West Bengal; joins in Bangladesh; largest tributary **Rangeet**; draft 2011 sharing treaty still stalled (Jul 2026).
- **Rangeet** joins the Teesta, not the Brahmaputra directly.
!TRAP Subansiri and Manas are RIGHT-bank (north) tributaries in the standard convention.
#### 6.3 Majuli
- **Majuli** (upper Assam) – **largest riverine island** in the world (60 km x 20 km); good paddy after floods.

### 12. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. **Hydaspes = Jhelum, Hyphasis = Beas, Acesines = Chenab** – not the reverse.
2. **Beas, Ganga, Yamuna, Chenab** do NOT rise near Mansarovar; Indus, Sutlej, Brahmaputra, Karnali do.
3. **Burhi Dihing, Dhansiri** = Brahmaputra (left bank); **Subansiri, Manas** = right bank.
4. **Son and Yamuna** are right-bank; Son does not join from the left.
5. **Gandak** enters India at **Valmikinagar**, not Raxaul.
6. **Kishanganga** is a Jhelum tributary, not Sutlej; **Ramganga** is a Ganga tributary, not Yamuna.
7. **Uttarkashi** lies on the Bhagirathi, not the Ganga.
8. **Jayakwadi** is on the Godavari, not Cauvery; **Tehri** on Bhagirathi.
9. In Bangladesh, **Padma = Ganga, Jamuna = Brahmaputra**.
10. **IWT**: eastern rivers (Ravi, Beas, Sutlej) to India; western to Pakistan.
