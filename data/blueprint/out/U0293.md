### 2. 1ST AMENDMENT, 1951
- Enacted by the **Provisional Parliament** under PM **Jawaharlal Nehru**; assent by President **Rajendra Prasad** on **18 June 1951** (75th anniversary, 18 June 2026).
- Amended Arts **15, 19, 85, 87, 174, 176, 341, 342, 372, 376**; added **31A** (saves estate-acquisition laws), **31B** (validates Ninth Schedule Acts), **Ninth Schedule**.
- **Art. 19(6)**: State monopoly and nationalisation protected.
| Ruling overcome | Issue | Response |
|---|---|---|
| **Kameshwar Singh v. State of Bihar** | Zamindari abolition | 31A, 31B, Ninth Schedule |
| **Champakam Dorairajan (1951)** | Communal G.O. in admissions | **Art. 15(4)** |
| **Romesh Thappar (1950)** | Free speech | New 19(2) grounds |
- Validity upheld in **Shankari Prasad (1951)**.
### 3. 42ND AMENDMENT, 1976 – "MINI-CONSTITUTION"
- Passed during the **Emergency** (PM **Indira Gandhi**); Bill introduced in Lok Sabha in **September 1976**.
- Called Mini-Constitution because it changed the **Preamble**, the **Seventh Schedule** and **54 Articles**.
| Area | Change |
|---|---|
| Preamble | Added **"Socialist", "Secular", "Integrity"** ("Justice" was already there) |
| State → Concurrent | **Education, forests, weights and measures, wild animals and birds, administration of justice** |
| Fundamental Duties | **Part IVA, Art. 51A** – 10 duties |
| DPSP | New **39A, 43A** (also 39(f), 48A); wider **31C** – DPSPs over FRs |
| Courts | **Part XIVA tribunals (323A, 323B)**; **368(4), (5)** barred review of amendments |
| Executive | **Ministerial advice binding** on the President |
| Others | **LS/Assembly term 6 years**; delimitation frozen on **1971 Census**; Emergency in **part** of India |
- What was undone: 44th restored **5-year term** and **judicial review**; Minerva Mills struck 368(4), (5) and the wider 31C. Preamble words and Duties **remain**.
- SC: **secularism** was always part of the **basic structure** (Jul 2025).
!TRAP The 42nd was NOT "repealed in 1977".
!KEY APPSC G2 Mains 2025 A-R on the 42nd: A and R both true, R explains A.
### 4. 44TH AMENDMENT, 1978
- Enacted by the **Janata government** (PM **Morarji Desai**); corrective to the **38th, 39th and 42nd**.
- **Right to Property**: Arts **19(1)(f) and 31 deleted** → legal right under **Art. 300A, Part XII**.
- **"Internal disturbance" → "armed rebellion"** for National Emergency.
- Emergency only on **written Cabinet recommendation**; **Lok Sabha can disapprove/revoke**.
- **Arts 20 and 21 cannot be suspended** during Emergency.
- President may **return ministerial advice once** for reconsideration.
- Restored courts' power over **election disputes** of President, VP, PM and Speaker (undid the 39th).
!PAIR 42nd | 1976 | term 6 years || 44th | 1978 | term back to 5 years

#### 7.1 101st – GST
- Assent **8 September 2016**; inserted **246A** (concurrent GST power of Parliament and States), **269A** (GST on inter-State supply) and **279A – GST Council** (chaired by Union Finance Minister; **three-fourths** majority).
- GST Council added to provisions needing **State ratification**.
#### 7.2 103rd – EWS
- Passed as the **124th Amendment Bill, 2019**; assent **12 January 2019**.
- Inserted **15(6)** and **16(6)** – 10% for EWS in education and jobs (income up to ₹8 lakh).
- **Janhit Abhiyan v. Union of India (7 Nov 2022)**: upheld **3:2**; 50% ceiling not applied to EWS.
#### 7.3 106th – Women's Reservation
- Assent **28 September 2023**; inserted **330A** (Lok Sabha), **332A** (Assemblies), **334A**; amended **239AA** (Delhi).
- Starts only after **delimitation** on the **first Census after commencement**; lasts **15 years**, extendable by Parliament by law.
- **131st Bill, 2026** (to lift the freeze / speed up the quota) failed in Lok Sabha **298-230** (17 Apr 2026).
