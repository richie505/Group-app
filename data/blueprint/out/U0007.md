## U0007 | IVC TOWN PLANNING – GRID LAYOUT, CITADEL AND LOWER TOWN, DRAINAGE, GREAT BATH, GRANARIES
### 1. TOWN PLANNING – HALLMARKS
- **Town planning** is the hallmark of the IVC (APPSC G2 2011); **no temples or palaces** clearly found (unlike Mesopotamia, Egypt).
- **Grid layout**: streets ran **north-south and east-west**, crossing at **right angles**, splitting cities into **blocks** (Mohenjodaro, Harappa).
- Main streets about **10 m (30 feet)** wide. **Banawali** = only city with **radial streets**.
- Standard bricks, grid, streets and drains point to a **civic authority** and early engineering skill; absence of palaces does not disprove it.
- IVC = **Bronze Age**; iron came much later (c. 1000 BC). Pottery is the main reason to place IVC before the Aryans; early pottery at **Mehrgarh**.
### 2. CITADEL AND LOWER TOWN
| Site | Plan |
|---|---|
| Mohenjodaro, Harappa | **Citadel (west)**, raised and fortified+ **lower town (east)** |
| Kalibangan | Citadel + **lower town also fortified** |
| **Dholavira** | Only city in **three parts**: citadel, middle town, lower town (CGPSC 2015) |
| **Chanhudaro** | **No citadel** |
| Lothal | Citadel and lower town in **one enclosure** |
| Sutkagendor | Citadel + lower city |
!TRAP Pataliputra is NOT an IVC site; Kalibangan, Ropar, Lothal are.
### 3. BRICKS AND MATERIALS
- **Burnt bricks** common; **mud bricks** for fillings and platforms and at **Kalibangan**. Both baked and raw bricks used.
- Standard ratio **1:2:4** (thickness : width : length) = 4:2:1.
- Same ratio, **different sizes**: house **7x14x28 cm**; city wall **10x20x40 cm**.
- **Dholavira**: **sandstone and limestone** blocks, not baked brick.
- **Ornamental bricks** = Kalibangan; **curved bricks** = Chanhudaro.
### 4. HOUSES AND WELLS
- Rooms round a **central courtyard**; **staircases** show multi-storey houses.
- Doors and windows opened on **side lanes**, not main streets.
- **Mohenjodaro** has the **most wells**, inside houses (UPPSC 2004). Well irrigation at **Allahdino** (Sindh).
### 5. DRAINAGE
- **Most advanced** IVC feature; better than Mesopotamia and Egypt.
- House drains → **covered** street drains (slabs) with **manholes** → **soak pits** / main channels; streets sloped for flow.
- **Kalibangan** = wooden drains; **Banawali** = no systematic drainage.
### 6. GREAT BATH
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Site | **Mohenjodaro** citadel mound, Sindh (UPPSC 2010) |
| Size | ~12 m x 7 m, **2.4 m** deep; steps at **north and south** |
| Material | Burnt bricks in **gypsum mortar**; **bitumen** waterproofing |
| Purpose | Ritual bathing (most view); public tank (others) |
### 7. GRANARIES AND SPECIAL STRUCTURES
| Structure | Site |
|---|---|
| **Great Granary**, largest building, on citadel | **Mohenjodaro** (APPSC G2 2008) |
| Granary of **12 rooms in two rows of six**, near river | **Harappa** |

| Dockyard (tidal; debated) – only port proof | **Lothal** |
| Pillared **assembly hall** | Mohenjodaro |
| **Fire altars** | Kalibangan (seven), Lothal |
- Granaries = stores of farm surplus (reading debated).
- Harappa finds: **Cemetery H**, **R-37**, coffin burial, wheat and barley in wooden mortar, dice, unicorn seal.
!TRAP Not Harappa: dockyard = Lothal; horse bones = Surkotada; bronze chariot = Daimabad.
### 8. DHOLAVIRA
- **Kachchh**, Gujarat; rectangular plan; one of **five largest** cities (with Mohenjodaro, Harappa, Ganweriwala, Rakhigarhi).
- **Reservoirs, dams, embankments**; bunds, check-dams and channels; **16 reservoirs** ≈ 10% of site; some **rock-cut** (UPSC 2021).
- Huge rock-cut reservoir ~**79 m x 12 m, 7 m deep**.
- **Stadium** (~283 x 47 m), special cemetery structures; **ten-sign signboard** at **North Gate** = largest inscription.
- Seven stages, **c. 3000–1500 BCE** (APPSC 2018 marked 3000–1800 BCE).
