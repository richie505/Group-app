## U0443 | ANDHRA PRADESH POLICIES AND PROGRAMMES – AGRICULTURE, INDUSTRY, IT AND INFRASTRUCTURE
### 1. AGRICULTURE PROGRAMMES
#### 1.1 Purpose and Benefit
| Programme | Year | Purpose / benefit |
|---|---|---|
| **APCNF** (AP Community-managed Natural Farming) | Since **2016** | Natural farming run by **Rythu Sadhikara Samstha (RySS)** |
| **YSR Rythu Bharosa–PM KISAN** | **2019** | **₹13,500 a year** per farmer family incl. **PM-KISAN ₹6,000**; succeeded by **Annadata Sukhibhava** (₹1,000 crore, 2024-25 BE) |
| **Rythu Bharosa Kendras (RBKs)** | **2020** | **Village one-stop centres** for seeds, fertilisers, advisory, procurement (not district loan offices) |
| **YSR Zero Interest Crop Loans** | **20 April 2021** | Crop loans **up to ₹1 lakh** repaid on time become **interest-free** (APPSC 2023) |
| **YSR Jala Kala** | – | **Free borewells** for small and marginal farmers; single or group farmers with **2.5 acres** – a policy against irrigation water shortage |
| **e-Crop** | – | **Digital booking of the crop on every field** each season; basis for insurance, input subsidy, procurement |
| **Tenant farmer support** | **2019** | **Crop Cultivator Rights Card (CCRC)** under the **AP Crop Cultivators Rights Act, 2019** |
!TRAP Years: APCNF 2016 → Rythu Bharosa 2019 → RBKs 2020 → Zero Interest loans 20 April 2021.
#### 1.2 Tenant Farmers – CCRC
- Gives tenant farmers access to **loans, insurance and input subsidy**.
- Does **NOT** give ownership title; the **landowner's title stays unchanged**.
#### 1.3 PMFBY in AP
- Farmer premium: **2% kharif, 1.5% rabi, 5% commercial/horticulture**.
- **Voluntary for all farmers since Kharif 2020** (earlier compulsory for loanee farmers).
- ₹1,023 crore in Budget 2024-25 BE.
### 2. SWARNA ANDHRA @2047
- Vision unveiled in **December 2024**; the **frame for the 2024-29 policies** and industrial corridors.
- Targets for 2047: GSDP of about **USD 2.4 trillion**; per capita income about **USD 42,000**.
- **Ten principles (Sutralu)** – the first is **zero poverty** (through **P4**: Public-Private-People Partnership); others include employment, skilling, water security, agri-tech, logistics, **Swachh Andhra**.
- Energy goal: **net zero by 2047** (not 2035, not 2070) – the same year as the vision.
- **PanIIT Summit 2026** (Vijayawada, **3 Oct 2026**): DeepTech roadmap; five panels – energy and fuel-cost optimisation; DeepTech (quantum, semiconductors, AI); space and defence manufacturing; BioValley (health); agri-tech, water and blue economy.
!KEY 2047 = Swarna Andhra vision year = net zero target year of the Clean Energy Policy.

#### 3.2 Startups and IT
- **Innovation and Startup Policy (4.0) 2024-29**: issued **April 2025** by the **ITE&C (Promotions) Department** (APPSC 2025) – not Industries & Commerce, not 26 Oct 2024.
- **IT & GCC Policy (4.0) 2024-29**: issued **12 Dec 2024**; 50% capital subsidy; incentives serviced by **APIIC** through a **Land Value Capture** model.
- **LIFT Policy (4.0) 2024-29** = **Land Incentive for Tech Hubs**: land for big tech firms at about **₹0.99 per acre**, linked to job targets.
#### 3.3 Project Classes and Terms
| Term | Meaning |

| **Mega project** | **2,000 or more direct jobs** |
| **Plug and Play** | Private industrial parks with ready infrastructure |
| **Bhudhaar** | Unique number per land parcel under BhuSeva |
!TRAP Large is defined by CAPITAL; Mega by JOBS.
### 4. ENERGY, GREEN AND AQUA POLICIES
- **AP Integrated Clean Energy Policy, 2024** (June 2024): solar, wind, hybrid, **pumped storage (PSP)**, **green hydrogen/ammonia**, biofuels, storage, **EV charging**; targets **160 GW** clean energy and **net zero by 2047**; aims to make AP a global green hydrogen hub.
- **Pinnapuram (Kurnool)**: Greenko's integrated solar-wind-**pumped storage** project; **1,200 MW** pumped storage.
- **NTPC Green Hydrogen Hub, Pudimadaka (Anakapalli)**: foundation by PM Modi, **January 2025**; **₹1.85 lakh crore**; JV of NTPC Green Energy and NREDCAP.
- **AP State Aquaculture Development Authority Act, 2020**: **Aqua Authority** regulates **aqua farms, seed and feed**.
- Power capacity: installed **27,392 MW**; renewable **7,522 MW** (2024-25).

### 7. AMARAVATI CAPITAL
- **2 June 2014** – AP's bifurcation date.
- **APCRDA** established under the **APCRDA Act, 2014** – capital region authority.
- **Foundation stone: 22 October 2015** by **PM Narendra Modi**.
- **2017**: AP government and a **Singapore consortium** formed a partnership for the **Amaravati Capital City Start-Up Area** Project.
- **Phase I funding**: **World Bank USD 800 million** (approved Dec 2024) + **ADB about USD 788.8 million** (Dec 2024) – about USD 1.6 billion in all; World Bank released USD 340 million by April 2026.
- ₹3,000 crore in Budget 2024-25 BE.
