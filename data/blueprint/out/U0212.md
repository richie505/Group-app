## U0212 | GROWTH OF NATIONALIST POETRY IN TELUGU – GURAJADA, RAYAPROLU, GARIMELLA SATYANARAYANA
### 1. EARLY NATIONALIST SONGS AND FIRSTS (1905-1909)
#### 1.1 FIRST → Claim
| FIRST | Fact |
|---|---|
| First nationalist song (often called) | **"The Cry of Mother India"** (Krishna Patrika, **15 Dec 1905**) |
| First nationalist song (per **C. Narayana Reddy**) | **"Melukonumi Bharata Putruda"** (Andhra Bharati, **Oct 1909**) |
| **First Telugu national poet** | **Chilakamarti Lakshmi Narasimham** (editor, *Desamata*) |
| **First Telugu national song** | **"Hinduvulara ennaallee gati"** (Chilakamarti) |
- Songs in **spoken Telugu** reached villagers whom pamphlets could not reach well.
#### 1.2 Chilakamarti Lakshmi Narasimham
- Occasion: translated **Bipin Chandra Pal's Rajahmundry speeches, April 1907** (Vande Mataram movement) and improvised **"Bharata khandambu chakkani paadi aavu"** there (G2 2019).
- Image: India is a **cow** whose milk the British were taking.
- Consequence/later verse: **"Bharata khandambe okka pedda bandikhana"** (India itself is one big prison).
!KEY Bharata Khandambu = Chilakamarti, Rajahmundry, 1907 – not Gurajada.
### 2. PATRIOTIC SONGS → POET → COLLECTION / OCCASION
| Song / line | Poet | Collection / occasion |
|---|---|---|
| **"Desamunu preminchumanna, manchi annadi penchumanna"** | **Gurajada Apparao** | ***Mutyala Saralu***; published in *Andhra Bharati*; "national anthem of Telugu people" |
| **"Desamante matti kadoi, desamante manushuloi"** | **Gurajada** | Same poem: a nation is its **people**, not its soil |
| **"Maakoddee Tella Doratanamu"** | **Garimella Satyanarayana** | Non-Cooperation era |
| **"Veera gandham tecchinaramu"** | **Tripuraneni Ramaswamy** | Salt Satyagraha era |
| **"Janmabhoomi"** ("Ye desamegina ... pogadara nee talli bhoomi Bharatini") | **Rayaprolu Subbarao** | Patriotic lyric |
| "Bharata khandambu chakkani paadi aavu" | Chilakamarti | Pal's speeches, 1907 |
| "Kollayi gattithenemi maa Gandhi" | **Basavaraju Apparao** | Gandhi song |
| "Ma Telugu Talliki mallepoodanda" | **Sankarambadi Sundarachari** | Later the **AP State song** |
| Yenki songs | **Nanduri Subba Rao** | Folk-love lyrics |
!TRAP Gurajada = "Desamunu"; Garimella = "Maakoddee"; Tripuraneni = "Veera gandham"; Rayaprolu = "Janmabhoomi".
### 3. GURAJADA APPARAO
- ***Mutyala Saralu*** pioneered a **colloquial metre** (mutyala saram) in Telugu verse.
- ***Kanyasulkam*** – play, **first staged 1892, Vizianagaram**.
- ***Desabhakti Geyam***.
- Made **vyavaharika bhasha** (spoken Telugu) a normal literary medium.
### 4. RAYAPROLU SUBBARAO AND BHAVAKAVITVAM
#### 4.1 Rayaprolu Subbarao
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Titles | **Father of Telugu Romanticism**; **"Abhinava Nannaya"** (APPSC G2 2025) |
| New concept | Founder of **Bhava Kavitvam**, drawn from **Shelley and Keats** |
| Change | Left translation of Sanskrit epics → **original, emotive verse**; freed Telugu verse from rigid prosody |
| Works | ***Andhravali***, ***Swatantrya Rathamu***, *Janmabhoomi* |
#### 4.2 Bhava Kavitvam – Features
- **Romantic and personal emotion**, nature, love; freedom from **classical metre**; patriotic strands.
#### 4.3 Main Poets
| Poet | Work / title |
|---|---|
| **Basavaraju Apparao** | "Kollayi gattithenemi" |
| **Duvvuri Ramireddy** | **Kavi Kokila**; *Matru Desa Sankeertanam* |
| **Devulapalli Krishna Sastri** | **Andhra Shelley** |
| **Venkata Parvateeswara Kavulu** | ***Ekanta Seva*** ("Telugu Gitanjali") |
- Criticism: progressive (Abhyudaya) writers such as **Sri Sri** called its romanticism **"escapist"**.
### 5. GARIMELLA SATYANARAYANA
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Life | **14 July 1893 – 18 Dec 1952**; born at **Gonedapadu**, Narasannapeta taluk (**Srikakulam**) |
| Call | Left studies at **Gandhi's call** |
| Song | **"Maakoddee Tella Doratanamu"**; grew to **162 lines**; copied a thousand times; praised by Gandhi |
| Arrest | **9 Feb 1922** |
| Jail | **1922, one year** for the song; later **2.5 years RI** (wife, father and grandfather died while he was in jail) |
| Other works | **Harijana Paatalu**, anti-liquor songs, **Samajika Navala**, **Palletullalo** |
| Legacy | **First people's poet of the freedom struggle**; inspired Nazar, Subbarao Panigrahi, Gaddar |
!TRAP Ramayana Kalpavrikshamu is NOT Garimella's – it is Viswanatha's.
### 6. PLAYS AND VERSE INSPIRED BY WORLD EVENTS
- **Japan's victory over Russia (1905)**:
- ***Japaneeyamu*** – **Sriram Veerabrahmam** of Bandar (Machilipatnam) (G2 2025).
- *Japan Charitra* – Adipudi Somanatha Rao; dedicated to the **Raja of Munagala**, who named his sons **Togo and Nogi**.
- ***Panchala Parabhavam*** – **Damaraju Pundarikakshudu**, on **Jallianwala Bagh**; **Dyer cast as Duryodhana**; discussed in the British Parliament; **25 students in Vizag** arrested for staging it.

!TRAP Abhinava Nannaya = Rayaprolu, not Viswanatha (Viswanatha = Kavi Samrat).
