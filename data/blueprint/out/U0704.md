## U0704 | ICT IN DAY-TO-DAY LIFE – MOBILE TELEPHONY, SOCIAL MEDIA, E-COMMERCE, DIGITAL MEDIA
### 1. MOBILE TELEPHONY – FIRSTS AND TERMS
#### 1.1 Firsts and Milestones
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| **First handheld mobile call** | **Martin Cooper** of **Motorola**, **3 April 1973** |
| **QR code** | **Quick Response** code – read quickly by a phone camera; designed **1994** by **Denso Wave (Japan)** for the **car industry** |
| Mobile Number Portability (India) | Pilot in **Haryana, 25 November 2010**; pan-India **20 January 2011**; full nationwide porting from 3 July 2015 |
| Generations | **1G (1980s)** → 5G now; **6G** expected in the **early 2030s** |
| **4G** | Runs on **LTE** (using **OFDM and MIMO**); **WiMAX lost out** to LTE |
| Android | **Android 16** released for Pixel phones in **June 2025** |
| India's market rank | **World's second-largest telecom market** (May 2025) |
!TRAP QR code was designed in **1994**, not "1996" (APPSC 2019 key).
- "G" in 2G means **Generation**.
#### 1.2 SIM and NFC
- **SIM** = **Subscriber Identity Module**: small card with the subscriber's account details that identifies the subscriber and lets the phone join a mobile network.
- **NFC (Near Field Communication)**: range of **a few centimetres**; **106–424 kbps**; easier pairing and **lower power than Bluetooth**.
- **RBI** allowed **offline UPI Lite** payments over NFC.
!PAIR NFC | few cm | low power || Bluetooth | ~10 m | more power

### 4. E-MAIL AND VIDEO CONFERENCING
#### 4.1 E-mail
- **E-mail** = **Electronic Mail**: digital messages over a computer network.
- Address parts: **username @ domain name** (e.g. name@gov.in).
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **SMTP** | **Sends** mail |
| **POP3 / IMAP** | **Receive** mail |
| Attachments | Files sent with a mail |
| CC | Sends copies |
| **BCC** (blind carbon copy) | **Hides recipients'** addresses from one another |
| **Video mail** | Sends a pre-recorded video by e-mail; can carry graphics and video clips |
#### 4.2 Video Conferencing
- Live **two-way video and audio** between places using telecom/internet technology.
- **Essentials**: **video camera, display, microphone**; also speaker, **broadband** link and conferencing **software (codec)**.
!TRAP A **telephone** is **not** essential for video conferencing; an **RFID reader** is not a component.

#### 6.3 ONDC
- **ONDC** (Open Network for Digital Commerce) is an **open, network-based protocol, not a platform**.
- Connects any buyer app to any seller app across **B2C and B2B**; helps **MSMEs**; **beta-tested in Bengaluru**.
!TRAP ONDC is **not** a payment gateway and **not** a single platform.
### 7. LAWS ON DIGITAL PLATFORMS
| Act / rules | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules** | **2020** | Under the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019**; cover **marketplace and inventory** entities; require a **grievance officer** and display of **country of origin** |
| **IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules** | **2021** | Framed under **Section 87** of the IT Act; replaced 2011 guidelines; regulate social media, **OTT** and digital news; **Code of Ethics** + **three-tier grievance redressal** |
| IT Amendment Rules | 2023 | Platforms ignoring government orders on false content **lose safe harbour (Section 79)**; amended again Feb 2026 |
| **Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act** | **2025** | **Banned real-money gaming**; created **Online Gaming Authority of India (OGAI)** – digital attached office of **MeitY**; rules notified Apr 2026, effective **1 May 2026** |
| **Code on Social Security** | **2020** | **Gig and platform workers defined for the first time**; **aggregators contribute** to their social-security fund; in force with all four labour codes from **21 November 2025** |
| **DPDP Act** | 2023 | Anyone **below 18 = child**; **verifiable parental consent** (**Section 9**) |
- Upcoming law: age-based social-media access with **graded limits**, not a blanket ban.
!NUM NUMBER GAME: Sec 87 = IT Rules 2021 framed; Sec 79 = safe harbour; Sec 9 DPDP = parental consent; 18 = child age.
