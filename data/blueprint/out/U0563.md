#### 1.2 MeeSeva and CARD
- MeeSeva grew out of the Naidu-era **eSeva** centres (not APSWAN offices); NeGP common service front-end.
- **First-year MeeSeva training through television** by the IT&C Department with **MANA TV** (APPSC 2022).
- 5.01 crore MeeSeva + 5.70 crore AP Seva records on **AP DigiVerify** (blockchain).
- **CARD**: one of ten finalists, International Innovation awards of the **Commonwealth Association for Public Administration and Management**.
- Village and Ward Secretariats now offer registration at the Gram Sachivalaya.

### 3. IT AND ELECTRONICS POLICIES (4.0, 2024-29)
#### 3.1 IT Policies
- **AP IT Policy 2021-24**: notified to boost growth and jobs; ended 30 Sep 2024.
- **IT & GCC Policy 4.0 (2024-29)**: in force **12 Dec 2024**; aim: **global knowledge economy hub**; capital subsidies, tax exemptions, employment-linked and rental incentives; focus on AI, cloud, cyber.
- **Co-working and neighbourhood workspaces** in Tirupati, Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada.
- **GCC** (Global Capability Centre) = a multinational's **own offshore unit**, not a third-party vendor.
- **LIFT Policy 4.0** (Land Incentive for Tech Hubs): land for GCCs and IT infrastructure developers.
#### 3.2 Electronics Policies
| Policy | Period | Key targets / incentives |
|---|---|---|
| **Electronics Manufacturing Policy 4.0** | 2024-29 | Production **US$50 bn** (Rs 4.2 lakh crore); investment **US$10 bn**; **5 lakh first-time jobs**; 50 CoEs |
| Electronics Components Manufacturing Policy | 2025 | Advance and matching incentives, interim support, plug-and-play facilities |
| **ESDM Policy** | 2021-24 | **Zone A/B**: **100% reimbursement of stamp duty, transfer duty, registration fee** (not income tax) |
| Semiconductor & Display Fab Policy 4.0 | 2024-29 | Fab and OSAT |
!TRAP "2 lakh first-time entrepreneurs" is NOT a target (APPSC G2 2025); the target is **5 lakh first-time jobs**.
#### 3.3 Data Centre and Drone Policies
- **Data Center Policy 4.0**: up to **1 GW** (Gujarat 2026-29 policy: 7.5 GW).
- **Drone Policy 4.0**: **100 drone companies**, 20+ pilot training organisations, **50 skilling centres**, **25,000 pilots**, **15,000 direct** + 25,000 indirect jobs.
!NUM Drone Policy: 25,000 pilots and 25,000 indirect jobs; direct jobs = 15,000
#### 3.4 Innovation and Startup Policy 4.0 (2024-29)
- Issued by the **ITE&C Department**; approved 24 Mar 2025; implemented by **AP Innovation Society (APIS)**.
- Targets: **20,000 startups** and **1 lakh jobs** in five years.
### 4. E-PRAGATI
- **Whole-of-government** digital platform (State Enterprise Architecture); launched under **CM N. Chandrababu Naidu (2014-19)** (APPSC 2021, asked twice).
- **14 packages / 72 projects / 33 departments**.
- Core hubs: **People, Land, Entity, GIS, IoT** (**no Finance Hub**).
- Single window for Revenue, Registration, Transport, Municipal services, on the **Seva model**.
- Goal: **faceless, paperless, cashless** governance.
### 5. RTGS AND DATA-DRIVEN GOVERNANCE
- **RTGS** (Real Time Governance Society, 2017): real-time monitoring, data analytics, AI, early warning; **Command and Control Centre** at the Secretariat; ~Rs 100 crore (Budget 2026-27).
- **State Data Lake**: "single source of truth" for **40+ departments**, 335+ HoDs, ~**400 applications**; concerns: **privacy, algorithmic bias**.
- **Mana Mitra**: **865 services** (SES 2025-26), target 1,000; 2.38 lakh Class-10 hall tickets; ~30% of temple bookings.
| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| **AWARE 2.0** | Disaster early warning; cyclones **Montha** and **Ditwa** |
| **AP DigiVerify** | Blockchain certificates; 9.85 crore records |
| **AI4PGRS** | Grievance AI |
- **Himachal Pradesh**: first State with a State Data Centre (June 2016); green data centre concept.
### 6. CONNECTIVITY – APSFL, BHARATNET, APSWAN
- **APSFL** (AP State FiberNet Ltd): **India's first State-owned ISP**; ISP, telecom and IPTV licences; triple play via 6,300+ cable operators.
- **Fiber Grid Phase-I**: **24,000 km**, **IP-MPLS ring** (not GPON), all **13 districts**; **NOC at Visakhapatnam**.
- **BharatNet Phase-II** (APSFL state agency): **54,502 km, 11,254 GPs**.
- APSWAN (2010) → APSDWAN (1 May 2021); SHQ moved Hyderabad → Velagapudi (2017).
!NUM 24,000 km + 54,502 km = 78,502 km OFC

### 9. DATA CENTRES, AI AND QUANTUM
#### 9.1 Data Centres
- **Visakhapatnam = Global AI hub city** (APPSC 2025).
- **Google AI data centre**: **1 GW** at **Tarluvada**; foundation **28 Apr 2026**, **601.4 acres**; largest Google AI hub outside the US; Rs 1.35 lakh crore (US$15 bn); "A.P.'s mother of all deals".
| Project | Company | Capacity | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google AI hub | Google via Raiden Infotech, with AdaniConneX and Airtel | 1 GW | Tarluvada, Adavivaram, Rambilli |
| AI data centre cluster | Adani Infra | 1,000 MW | Visakhapatnam-Anakapalli |
| Hyperscale + GCC Park | RMZ (US$10 bn) | up to 1 GW | Visakhapatnam |

#### 9.2 Amaravati Quantum Valley
- **India's first Quantum Valley**, under the **National Quantum Mission**: quantum computing, **QKD**, post-quantum cryptography.
- **Full stamp-duty and registration-fee exemption**; 7 projects approved; launch **January 2027**.
| Cluster | Place |

### 10. DRONES AND TECHNOLOGY SUMMITS
- **Drone City, Orvakal (Kurnool)**: **India's first dedicated drone cluster**; **600 acres** (120 in Phase-I); foundation Nov 2025.
- **Amaravati Drone Summit 2024**: **22-23 Oct 2024**, **Mangalagiri**; record **drone show at Punnami Ghat, Vijayawada** (PYQ 2024); hackathon with **AP Drone Corporation**, CII, DFI.
- APDC: MoU with Airbound for a capital-region drone delivery network. Other: AP VAGA XR Summit 2025.
### 11. IT EMPLOYMENT AND EXPORTS
- Pre-2014 IT exports from **HITEC City and Cyberabad**, Hyderabad (built under Naidu's 1990s IT push).
- After 2014 IT base rebuilt around **Visakhapatnam**.
- 2025: IT investment Rs 1,48,956.49 crore → **1,41,023 jobs**; CII Partnership Summit 2025: 54 IT MoUs, Rs 1,95,294 crore.
