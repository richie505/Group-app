## U0236 | ANDHRA IN THE HOME RULE MOVEMENT AND THE NON-COOPERATION MOVEMENT
### 1. HOME RULE MOVEMENT IN ANDHRA
#### 1.1 Home Rule League and Andhra
- **Annie Besant** founded the Home Rule League (1916); she became the **first woman President of the INC (1917)**.
- **Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao** was **secretary of the Andhra division** of the Home Rule League and led the opening of its branches in Andhra; he toured widely giving lectures.
- 1917: the **Home Rule flag** (5 red and 4 green stripes) was hoisted at **Coimbatore**.
#### 1.2 Women Presidents of the INC
| President | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Annie Besant** | **1917** | **First woman** President |
| **Sarojini Naidu** | **1925** | **First Indian woman** President |
| Nellie Sengupta | 1933 | Presided later |
!TRAP First woman INC President = Annie Besant (1917); first Indian woman = Sarojini Naidu (1925).
### 2. ANDHRA PREPARES FOR NON-COOPERATION
- **January 1921:** after the Nagpur session, the **APCC met at Vijayawada** and set up Congress committees at **district, taluk and village** level.
- Andhra carried out the Calcutta/Nagpur programme: **boycott of courts, colleges and councils**.
!TRAP APCC meeting (January 1921) is NOT the same as the AICC session at Vijayawada (March-April 1921).
### 3. AICC MEETING AT BEZAWADA (VIJAYAWADA), 1921
#### 3.1 Decisions
- **AICC at Vijayawada, 1921** (31 March – 1 April): political leaders **accepted Gandhi's leadership** of the national struggle (APPSC 2023).
- **Pingali Venkayya's flag design** (with the **charkha**) was endorsed by Gandhi.
- Pingali Venkayya was born at **Bhatlapenumarru**.

### 6. NCM EPISODES IN ANDHRA → PLACE AND DISTRICT
| Episode | Place, district | Leader |
|---|---|---|
| **Palnad forest satyagraha** | Palnadu, **Guntur** | **Kanneganti Hanumanthu** |
| **Chirala-Perala** | Chirala, **Guntur** | **Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya** (volunteer force Ramadandu) |
| **Pedanandipadu no-tax** | Pedanandipadu, **Guntur** | **Parvataneni Veerayya Chowdary** |
- **Delhi AICC, 5 November 1921** allowed provinces to start **civil disobedience, including no-tax campaigns**.
- 17 Nov 1921: Prince of Wales boycott; **black flags** in Andhra.
#### 6.1 Episode Details
- **Chirala-Perala (1921-22):** people opposed the merging of Chirala and Perala into a **municipality** with heavy taxes; residents left the town and lived in a camp at **Ramnagar**; Duggirala's volunteer corps **Ramadandu** kept discipline; 12 women were jailed.
- **Pedanandipadu (1921-22):** villages refused to pay land revenue; Parvataneni Veerayya Chowdary, called **"Andhra Shivaji"**, organised the **Shanti Sena**.
- **Palnadu (1921-22):** tribals, herders and forest users refused the **pullari** tax and boycotted forest officials.
- After Chauri Chaura, the Guntur no-tax campaign was suspended on **11 February 1922** after Gandhi's message to Konda Venkatappayya.
!PAIR Ramadandu | Chirala-Perala | Duggirala || Shanti Sena | Pedanandipadu | Parvataneni
!TRAP All three main NCM episodes of Andhra (Palnadu, Chirala-Perala, Pedanandipadu) were in the old Guntur district.
### 7. PALNAD FOREST (PULLARI) SATYAGRAHA, 1921-22
#### 7.1 Place, Year, Grievance
- **Palnadu, Guntur district; 1921-22** (APPSC 2012); some date it from Sept 1921.
- **First forest satyagraha in Andhra** (APPSC 2023).
- Background: **Forest Act (1878)**; **Pullari (grazing) tax = 12 annas per cow, Rs 1.50 per buffalo**; goats banned.
- **Failed 1921 rains** → fodder shortage.
#### 7.2 Course of Events
1. Pullari refused; **social boycott of officials at Macherla** (even the Collector and SP).
2. **300 cattle** driven into the forest untaxed → **impounded at Minchalapadu**.
3. **Kanneganti Hanumanthu shot dead** in the cattle-rescue action at **Minchalapadu, 26 February 1922**.
4. **28 men and 8 women** arrested.
!PAIR Macherla | Social boycott | Officials || Minchalapadu | Cattle impounded | Hanumanthu shot
#### 7.3 Leaders
| Person | Role |
|---|---|
| **Kanneganti Hanumanthu** | Hero of the satyagraha; **born in Guntur district** (APPSC 2022); also spelt Hanumantarao |
| **Unnava Lakshmi Narayana** + **Madabhushi Vedantam Narasimhachari** | Congress leaders who **studied and backed** the struggle |
| Unnava Lakshmi Narayana | Wrote the play **Nayakuralu** in jail |
| **Konda Venkatappayya** | Tried to calm/dilute it – Congress preferred **social boycott**, not tax refusal |
### 8. CHRONOLOGY – HOME RULE TO SUSPENSION OF NCM
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 1916-17 | Home Rule Leagues; Besant INC President (1917) |
| 1920 | NCM launched; Calcutta special (Sept), Nagpur (Dec 1920) |

| Mar-Apr 1921 | Bezawada AICC |
| Apr / Oct 1921 | Gandhi's Andhra tours |

| 17 Nov 1921 | Prince of Wales boycott |
