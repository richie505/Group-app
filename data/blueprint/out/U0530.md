## U0530 | NON-PERFORMING ASSETS – CAUSES, TWIN BALANCE SHEET PROBLEM, RECOGNITION AND PROVISIONING
### 1. NPA DEFINITION AND CLASSIFICATION
- **NPA**: loan or advance whose **interest or principal** stays overdue for **more than 90 days** (APPSC-G1 2022 key).
- **Farm loans**: short-duration crops = overdue **2 crop seasons**; long-duration crops = **1 crop season**.
| Class | Criterion |
|---|---|
| Standard | Regular; no overdue beyond 90 days |
| **SMA-0 / SMA-1 / SMA-2** | Overdue **1-30 / 31-60 / 61-90 days**: early warning, still standard |
| **Sub-standard** | NPA for **12 months or less** |
| **Doubtful** | NPA for **more than 12 months** |
| **Loss** | Loss identified by the bank, auditors or RBI inspection, but not wholly written off |
- Sequence: Standard → SMA-0/1/2 → NPA (sub-standard → doubtful → loss).
!TRAP SMA (up to 90 days) is still a **standard** asset, not an NPA.
### 2. STRESS INDICATORS AND KEY TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Gross NPA | All NPAs |
| **Net NPA** | Gross NPA − provisions |
| **Stressed assets** | NPAs + **restructured** loans + written-off assets |
| **Interest coverage ratio (ICR)** | **EBIT ÷ interest due**; below 1 = cannot cover interest; shows a borrower's **present and emerging risk** (UPSC 2020) |
| **Evergreening** | New loans to repay old ones → default stays hidden; ended by the AQR |
### 3. RECOGNITION AND PROVISIONING
- **IRACP** = **Income Recognition, Asset Classification, Provisioning**.
- **Income recognition**: interest on an NPA is booked as income **only when actually received**.
- **Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR)** = provisions held ÷ gross NPAs.
#### 3.1 New Rules from 1 April 2027
- **Borrower-level tagging**: one NPA loan → all that borrower's loans at the bank become NPAs; **upgrade only after all arrears** on every facility are paid; 90-day test stays; automated identification.
- **ECL model**: RBI moves from incurred-loss to a forward-looking **3-stage Expected Credit Loss** model (EIR method).
| ECL stage | Provision |
|---|---|
| Stage 1 (low risk) | **12-month** expected loss |
| Stage 2 (risk rises) | **Lifetime** loss |
| Stage 3 (over 90 days) | Full lifetime provision |
### 4. TWIN BALANCE SHEET PROBLEM
- Meaning: **over-leveraged corporates + capital-starved, NPA-laden banks** at the same time.
- Named in **Economic Survey 2016-17** (CEA **Arvind Subramanian**).
- Loan losses eat bank capital → banks stop new loans → firms cannot invest → **private investment falls**.
### 5. CAUSES OF NPAs
- **Aggressive lending (2010-15)** to **infrastructure, steel, power** without strict appraisal → stalled projects.
- Global shocks: 2008 crisis + weak exports. **Evergreening** hid defaults.
- Vicious cycle: lending + shocks → NPAs → provisioning → capital erosion → weak credit growth.
### 6. ASSET QUALITY REVIEW AND RESPONSE
- **AQR by RBI in 2015**: disclosed NPAs jumped, **peaking in 2018**; exposed the twin balance sheet problem.
- **4R**: **Recognise, Resolve, Recapitalise, Reform**, with **IBC**, recapitalisation and **NARCL**.
