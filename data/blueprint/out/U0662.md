### 3. GROWTH – AP VS INDIA
| Decade | AP | India |
|---|---|---|
| 1981–91 | **21.13%** (highest) | – |
| 1991–2001 | **12.32%** | 21.5% |
| 2001–11 | **9.21%** | 17.7% |
- 1991–2001: AP's **compound growth rate** below India's (APPSC-G2 2008); 2001–11 AP grew at about **half** the national rate.
- District variation (2001–11): **Kurnool** grew fastest (about 14.7%); **West Godavari** slowest (about 3.5%).
- Reasons for slowdown: falling fertility, literacy and **female education**, urbanisation, family planning.

| Region | Districts (13-set) | Pattern |
|---|---|---|

### 6. FERTILITY AND POPULATION POLICY
| Indicator (SRS) | AP | India |
|---|---|---|
| TFR | **1.5** | **1.9** (replacement 2.1) |
| CBR | **15.0** (not 13.5) | 18.4 |
| CDR | **6.9** (higher) | 6.4 |
| Child sex ratio | 944 | 918 |
- NFHS-5 gave AP TFR about **1.7**; 1.5 is the SRS estimate.
- **12 Feb 2026**: two-child cap for local body elections removed.
- **5 Mar 2026**: Draft Population Management Policy – raise TFR **1.5 → 2.1**; prepare for ageing by 2047.
- **1 Apr 2026**: Swarna Andhra Population Management Day (every fourth Saturday).
- **16 May 2026**: CM – **Rs 30,000** for 3rd child, **Rs 40,000** for 4th; "**Pillale Sampada**" (children are wealth) – shift to population promotion.
- Census 2027 data will feed the post-2026 **delimitation** debate.
