## U0151 | AGE OF DISCOVERY AND THE SEA ROUTE TO INDIA – VASCO DA GAMA; MOTIVES OF EUROPEAN EXPANSION
### 1. WHY EUROPE SOUGHT A SEA ROUTE
#### 1.1 The Old Route and Its Closure
- Before the 15th century the most important route was **Europe → Constantinople → Central Asia → India**.
- The trade was controlled by **Arabs**; **Venice** and **Genoa** distributed the goods in Europe.
- **1453**: the Ottoman Sultan **Mehmed II** captured **Constantinople**, closing the main overland route to India.
!TRAP Fall of Constantinople = 1453, not 1452.
#### 1.2 Motives – Factor and Effect
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Commodities | **Spices** (pepper, cinnamon, cardamom, ginger, cloves, nutmeg), **silk**; Indian textiles in huge demand |
| Fall of Constantinople (1453) | Overland route closed; need for a direct sea route |
| Religion | Spread of **Christianity** |
| Wealth and prestige | Profits of the spice trade |
| Rivalry | Spain, Portugal, Netherlands, England, France |
| Breaking Arab control | Direct access to Indian goods |
- Other motive: curiosity and exploration.
!TRAP "Roman imperial administration" is NOT a motive of European expansion.
#### 1.3 Contemporary Remark
- "Bear in mind that **the commerce of India is the commerce of the world**" – **Peter the Great** of Russia (APPSC Group 2, 2017). It refers to 18th-century India.
### 2. NAVIGATIONAL ADVANCES
- **Prince Henry "the Navigator"** (Portugal): set up **naval training centres**; first European to actively promote navigation.
- **Caravel** (ship) and **astrolabe** (instrument) made long voyages safer.

### 4. DIVIDING THE WORLD – PAPAL BULLS AND TORDESILLAS
| Act / treaty | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **Inter caetera** (papal bull) | 1493 | Pope **Alexander VI**; divided new lands between Spain and Portugal |
| **Treaty of Tordesillas** | 1494 | Meridian **370 leagues west of Cape Verde**; East → **Portugal**, West → **Spain** |
!KEY Order: 1488 Dias – 1492 Columbus – 1493 Inter caetera – 1494 Tordesillas – 1498 da Gama – 1500 Cabral.
### 5. VASCO DA GAMA
#### 5.1 First Voyage (1497-99)
- Sponsor: King **Manuel I** of Portugal.
- Left **Lisbon** on **8 July 1497** with **four ships** (São Gabriel, São Rafael, Berrio + supply ship).
- Route: **Lisbon – Cape of Good Hope – East African coast – Calicut**.
- Guided from **Malindi** to Calicut by the trader **Abdul Majid**; the pilot was a **Gujarati** (the old link with Arab navigator Ahmad ibn Majid is doubted).
- Reached **Calicut (Kozhikode)**, western (Malabar) coast, on **20 May 1498**, landing at **Kappad** near Calicut.
- Welcomed by the **Zamorin (Samuthiri)**, Hindu ruler of Calicut.
- Arrival ships: **São Gabriel** (Vasco), **São Rafael** (Paulo da Gama), **Berrio** (Nicolau Coelho).
- Back in Portugal 1499; spices sold at **60 times** the cost.
!KEY APPSC-GS 2019 key: Vasco da Gama reached Calicut on 20 May 1498.
#### 5.2 Three Voyages
| Voyage | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 1st (1497-99) | Calicut; Zamorin's welcome |
| 2nd (1502) | Zamorin refused trade; war; Calicut occupied; Portuguese settled at **Kochi** |
| 3rd (1524) | Came as **Viceroy**; died at **Kochi** the same year |
#### 5.3 Rulers in 1498 (Contemporaries)
| Kingdom | Ruler |
|---|---|
| Portugal | Manuel I |
| Calicut | Zamorin |
| Delhi | **Sikandar Lodi** |
| Vijayanagara | **Immadi Narasimha Raya** |
!TRAP CGPSC 2022: only "Zamorin ruled Calicut" was correct; da Gama did not stay a year in India.
### 6. CHRONOLOGY OF EUROPEAN ARRIVALS IN INDIA
| Power | Company / arrival | Key base |
|---|---|---|
| **Portuguese** | 1498 (Vasco da Gama) | Calicut, Cochin; first Viceroy **Almeida** |
| **English** | East India Company chartered **1600** | First factory at **Surat** (1613) |
| **Dutch** | VOC founded **1602** | First factory at **Masulipatnam (1605)** |
| **Danes** | Company **1616** | **Tranquebar** |
| **French** | Company **1664** | **Pondicherry** |
- Portuguese = **earliest Europeans** to land in India; first direct sea route; also the last to leave.
- Dutch **VOC (1602)** is widely called the **first joint-stock company** to sell shares to the public; the English company (1600) was also a joint-stock monopoly.
!TRAP Order of companies: English 1600 → Dutch 1602 → Danes 1616 → French 1664. The Portuguese came first of all (1498).
### 7. AP ANGLE AND CURRENT RELEVANCE
- **Machilipatnam** = Europeans' first Andhra port; the **Dutch** set up their first factory in India here (1605).
- Brazil's **Nelore cattle** (about **80%** of its livestock) come from **Nellore**, AP.
- **Jul 2025**: India-Brazil ties recalled on the PM's visit for the **17th BRICS Summit**; Cabral reached Brazil in 1500, two years after da Gama.
- 1453/1498 mark the start of the **Modern Age** for India and Andhra.
- Chronology: Qutub Minar (begun by Aybak, completed by Iltutmish) → death of Firuz Tughluq (1388) → arrival of Portuguese (1498) → reign of Krishnadeva Raya.

### 10. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. **20 May 1498**, not 1497 (1497 = departure from Lisbon).
2. Received by the **Zamorin**, not the Vijayanagara king.
3. Delhi ruler in 1498 = **Sikandar Lodi**, not Ibrahim Lodi.
4. **Tordesillas 1494**: east to Portugal, west to Spain.
5. **Cape of Good Hope** reached by Dias (1488), named by **John II**.
6. **Cabral**, not da Gama, reached **Brazil** (1500).
7. Da Gama died at **Kochi** (1524) as Viceroy, not at Calicut.
8. First Portuguese Viceroy = **Almeida**, not Albuquerque.
9. **Peter the Great** spoke of India's commerce, not a British writer.
