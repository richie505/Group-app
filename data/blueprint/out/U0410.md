## U0410 | SCHEDULED TRIBES – FRA 2006, FIFTH SCHEDULE, PESA, TSP, EKLAVYA SCHOOLS
### 1. ST IDENTIFICATION AND COMMITTEES
- **Art. 366(25)** defines STs; **Art. 342(2)**: Parliament amends the **Constitution (ST) Order, 1950**. Art. 338A = NCST; Art. 244(1) = Fifth Schedule.
- Procedure: State → **Registrar General of India** → NCST → Union Cabinet → Parliament.
- ST status is **religiously neutral** (unlike SCs).
| Body | Chair / year | Mandate |
|---|---|---|
| Dhebar Commission (Art. 339) | **U.N. Dhebar**, 1960-61 | Scheduled Areas and ST welfare |
| Lokur Committee | 1965 | 5 ST criteria: primitive traits, distinct culture, geographical isolation, shyness of contact, backwardness |
| Bhuria Committee | Dilip Singh Bhuria | Basis for **PESA** |
| High-Level Committee | **Virginius Xaxa**, 2013 | Socio-economic, health, educational status of tribes |
- **Diku** = outsider-exploiter (moneylender, trader, landlord, contractor); **Marang Buru** = Parasnath Hills revered by Santhals; a Jain site.
### 2. TRIBAL PROBLEMS AND APPROACHES
- Colonial moneylenders, revenue farmers → **indebtedness, land alienation**; forest laws cut off habitat.
- STs ≈ **9%** of population but **over 50%** of the development-displaced.
| Approach | Idea | Proponent |
|---|---|---|
| Isolation | Keep apart ("national park") | Early Verrier Elwin |
| Assimilation | "Backward Hindus", to be absorbed | **G.S. Ghurye**; N.K. Bose |
| Integration | Develop while keeping identity | **Nehru** (with Elwin) |
- **Panchsheel** source: Nehru's foreword to Elwin's **A Philosophy for NEFA**. (1) develop along their **own genius**; (2) respect land and forest rights; (3) train tribals for own administration; (4) avoid over-administration; (5) judge by **quality of human character**, not money spent.
- **T.K. Oommen**: modern tribal movements seek political autonomy, identity, control over resources.

### 4. FOREST RIGHTS ACT, 2006
- Undoes "**historical injustice**"; in force **31 Dec 2007**; rules **1 Jan 2008**; nodal ministry **Tribal Affairs**; **overrides** inconsistent laws.
- **Sec. 6(1): Gram Sabha** initiates determination of rights; its decision can be appealed. Six-member **Sub-Divisional and District Level Committees** issue titles.
| Category | Eligibility (cut-off **13 Dec 2005**) |
|---|---|
| FDST | Occupying forest land before cut-off |
| OTFD | **3 generations (75 years)** |
- Ceiling **4 ha** per family.
| Right | Type |
|---|---|
| Habitation, self-cultivation | Individual |
| Nistar, grazing, fishing | Community |
| Ownership of **MFP** | Community |
| CFR management, **PVTG habitat** | Community |
- **No ownership of minerals**. **Bamboo** = MFP under FRA. **IFA Amendment 2017** removed bamboo grown **outside forests** from "tree"; no right to fell bamboo in forests.
- **Critical Wildlife Habitat** defined only in FRA.
- First PVTG habitat rights: **Bharia** (MP); then **Kamar** and **Baiga** (Chhattisgarh).
- **Niyamgiri (2013)**: Dongria Kondh Gram Sabhas rejected bauxite mining.
- Data (2025-26): 23.92 lakh individual + 1.22 lakh community titles, 233.48 lakh acres. **AP**: 2,26,651 titles, 4,54,706 acres.
- Forest Conservation Rules 2022 dropped consent clause (NCST vs MoEFCC). **Forest (Conservation) Amendment Act 2023**: exempts strategic projects within **100 km** of borders.
- 31 Aug 2026: MoTA said FRA has **no provision** for Gram Sabha consent for Stage-II clearance. MoTA sought MoEFCC funds for CFR (12 Jan 2026); Agasthyamalai evictions ignored FRA.
- **RFCTLARR 2013**: SIA mandatory; consent **80%** (private), **70%** (PPP); Scheduled Areas acquisition = last resort with Gram Sabha consent.
### 5. PESA AND AP LAND LAWS
- PESA 1996 extends Part IX to Fifth Schedule areas. AP among **10** PESA States; **9** have rules, **Odisha** only drafts. ≥**50%** seats + all chairpersons STs.
- AP: **2,038** PESA Gram Sabhas (AP PESA Rules 2011).
| Law / case | Provision |
|---|---|
| Regulation **1 of 1959** | Bars land transfer to non-tribals |
| Regulation **1 of 1970** | Presumption land is tribal; bars transfer even between non-tribals; challenge pending in SC |
| **Samatha (1997)** | Leases to private mining companies **void**; mining only by State undertakings or tribal cooperatives |
| **G.O. No. 3 (2000)** | 100% teacher posts for STs; struck down **2020** |
- **Polavaram**: opposed by **Koya and Kondh**. ASR district bandh demanded 1/70 enforcement and a G.O. 3 replacement.
### 6. PVTGs AND AREA PROGRAMMES
- **75** PVTGs in 18 States + 1 UT; criteria: **pre-agricultural technology, low literacy, economic backwardness, stagnant/declining population**.
- **AP – 12**: Bodo Gadaba, Bondo Poroja, Chenchu, Dongria Khond, Gutob Gadaba, Khond Poroja, Kolam, Konda Reddi, Konda Savara, Kutia Khond, Parengi Poroja, Thoti.
| Scheme | Facts |
|---|---|
| **PM-JANMAN** | **Nov 2023**; ₹24,104 crore; 75 PVTGs; **11** interventions |
| **Dharti Aaba (DAJGUA)** | **Sept 2024**; ₹79,156 crore; **63,000** villages; **17** ministries |
| **PM-AJAY** | 2021-22; MoSJE; 100% central; merges Adarsh Gram + SCA to SCSP + **BJRCY**; targets **SCs** |
| Sickle Cell Mission | Health + Tribal Affairs; elimination by **2047** |
!TRAP PM-AJAY is for **SCs**, not STs (APPSC G1 2024).
### 7. TSP, INSTITUTIONS AND AP DATA
- **TSP**: Expert Committee **1972**; **5th Plan (1974)**; now **DAPST**; funds at least in proportion to ST population, over and above normal flows. Grants: **Art. 275(1)** + SCA (Art. 282 discretionary; Art. 280 FC). Monitoring to MoTA Jan 2017.
- **SCP**: 1979 → SCSP → DAPSC. **AP SC/TSP Act 2013**: first State law making allocation binding.
- **TADP/ITDA** objectives: improved seeds/fertilisers, fruit trees, income-raising work (APPSC 2019).
| Body | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Ministry of Tribal Affairs | **1999** | Split from MoSJE |
| NCST | 89th CAA 2003; from **19 Feb 2004** | Civil-court powers |
| TRIFED | 1987 | Tribes India; MSP for MFP |
| NSFDC | 1989 | SCs |
| NSKFDC | 1997 | Implements NAMASTE |
| NSTFDC | 2001 | STs up to double poverty line |
| Girijan Cooperative Corporation (AP) | 1956 | MFP at fair prices, PDS, credit |
- APSCCFC: self-employment, Jogin rehabilitation. AP: STs **27.39 lakh (5.53%)**, **34** communities; Scheduled Areas **10.15%**; tribal MMR and IMR above State average.
- Livelihood: **Van Dhan (2018)** – ~15 SHGs of up to 20, ≥60% ST; **4,172** VDVKs (AP 415 sanctioned, 258 working); **PM Janjatiya Vikas Mission** (2021-22, TRIFED); **Roshni** (MoRD, 2013, LWE youth).
### 8. EDUCATION AND SC/ST EMPOWERMENT
- **EMRS**: 1997-98; Central Sector, MoTA, via NESTS; modelled on **Navodaya**; block with **>50% ST and ≥20,000** tribals; target **740**, **511** running (Aug 2026); **Odisha** most.
- Scholarships: SC Post-Matric **60:40**; National Overseas **20** awards (17 ST + 3 PVTG), ₹6 lakh limit; Top Class, National Fellowship (central sector).
- **Stand-Up India**: **5 April 2016**, Finance; each branch ≥1 SC/ST and 1 woman; **greenfield**; ₹10 lakh-₹1 crore. **VCF-SC**: 2014-15, ₹10 lakh-₹15 crore.
- **NAMASTE (2022)**: ends manual sewer/septic cleaning; MoSJE + MoHUA; NSKFDC; ₹349.70 crore. **SHRESHTA**: NTA's NETS test for SC students.
- **DACE**: Civil Services coaching in Central Universities; launched 22 April 2022 at **BHU**.
