## U0035 | CHANDRAGUPTA MAURYA AND BINDUSARA – CONQUESTS, TREATY WITH SELEUCUS, CHANAKYA
### 1. NAMES, TITLES AND IDENTIFICATION
| Name / title | Identity |
|---|---|
| **Sandrocottus / Sandrokottas** | Greek name of **Chandragupta** (Justin, Strabo); Arrian and Plutarch – **Androkottus** |
| **Kautilya = Chanakya = Vishnugupta** | Author of the Arthashastra; **Vishnugupta** = childhood name; son of sage **Chanak** |
| **Amitraghata** ("slayer of foes", Mahabhashya) | Title of **Bindusara** = Greek **Amitrochates** (UPSC) |
| **Simhasena** | Birth name of Bindusara |
| **Subhadrangi (Dharma)** | Bindusara's queen, **mother of Ashoka** |
| **Durdhara** | Chandragupta's queen, mother of Bindusara |
| **Porus (Parvataka)** | Chandragupta's ally |
| **Pushyagupta Vaishya** | Chandragupta's governor of **Saurashtra** |
| **Tushaspha (Tushasp)** | **Ashoka's** Yavanaraja in Saurashtra |
- **Sir William Jones (1793)** first identified Sandrocottus with Chandragupta – the anchor of early Indian chronology (c. 322 BCE).
- **Justin** described the meeting of Sandrokottos with Alexander.
!TRAP Amitraghata (Sanskrit) = Amitrochates (Greek) = Bindusara; Sandrocottus = Chandragupta.
### 2. CHANDRAGUPTA MAURYA – ORIGIN AND RISE
#### 2.1 Origin Debate
| Tradition | Origin |
|---|---|
| **Brahmanical** | "Maurya" from a **Shudra woman Mura** |
| **Buddhist and Jain** | **Kshatriya** of the **Moriya clan** |
| **Mudrarakshasa** (commentary by **Dhundiraja**) | A descendant of the **Nanda house** |
- Reign **324/321–297 BCE**; founder of the **Maurya** dynasty (321–185 BC) – the oldest among Maurya, Kushan, Gupta and Vardhana.
- **First pan-Indian empire** = Mauryas; first Indian empire = Chandragupta Maurya (BPSC 2020, 2024) – uniting most of India, Pakistan, Bangladesh and parts of Afghanistan.
!TRAP Even if the Nandas are called empire-builders, the answer for "first (pan-Indian) empire" is Chandragupta Maurya.
#### 2.2 Overthrow of the Nandas
1. **Chanakya** guided him (linked with **Taxila**).
2. Army: **6,00,000 infantry, 30,000 cavalry**; **Justin** (Roman historian) called it a "**gang of dacoits**".
3. Eliminated the **Greek satraps**, with Porus (Parvataka) as ally.
4. Took the Magadhan throne in **321 BC** from **Dhana Nanda**.
- **Takshashila (Taxila)** – in **Gandhara**; centre of learning linked with Chanakya, Chandragupta, **Charaka** and **Jivaka**.
- **Pataliputra** – founded by **Ajatashatru** (Haryanka); **Udayin** moved the capital there from Rajgriha.
### 3. CHANAKYA (KAUTILYA)
- **Office:** **prime minister / chief minister** and guide of Chandragupta; taught at Taxila.
- The Arthashastra names its author **Kautilya**; one verse calls him **Vishnugupta**.
- **Taranath** and **Jain tradition**: continued as **Bindusara's minister**.
- **Mudrarakshasa** (Vishakhadatta): court intrigues of Chandragupta's time – **Chanakya versus Rakshasa**, minister of the Nandas.
- Other traditions: **Devi Chandraguptam**; **Vamsatthapakasini** (10th c.) on the Chanakya legend.
### 4. CHANDRAGUPTA'S CONQUESTS
| Region | Conquest / evidence |
|---|---|
| North-west | Expelled Greek satraps; won Seleucus's four provinces up to Afghanistan |
| Western India | **First to conquer Malwa, Gujarat and Maharashtra**; Saurashtra under **Pushyagupta**; proved by **Rudradaman's Junagadh inscription** |
| **Deccan** | **Mauryan conqueror of the Deccan** = Chandragupta |
| South | Jain tradition at **Shravanabelagola** (Karnataka); Tamil poet **Mamulanar** on the southward push (not a Greek chronicler) |
#### 4.1 Sudarshana Lake (Girnar, Saurashtra)
| Person | Role |
|---|---|
| **Pushyagupta** | Chandragupta's governor; **built** it (first lake in Girnar) |
| **Tushaspha** | Ashoka's governor; added **canals** |
| **Rudradaman I** | **Repaired** it, **without forced labour** |
| **Skandagupta** | Repaired it again later |
- Girnar resource management = Chandragupta Maurya and Ashoka (UKPSC 2024: lake built in Chandragupta's reign).
### 5. TREATY WITH SELEUCUS NICATOR (c. 305 BCE)
- **Seleucus I Nicator** – Alexander's general; tried to recover the north-west; **defeated by Chandragupta in 305 BC**.
| Provision | Detail |
|---|---|
| Ceded to Chandragupta | **Aria** (Herat), **Arachosia** (Kandahar), **Paropanisadai** (Kabul valley), **Gedrosia** (Balochistan) – **not Bactria** |
| Matrimonial alliance | Seleucus's daughter (**Helena**) married Chandragupta (not the reverse) |
| Chandragupta gave | **500 war elephants** |
| Ambassador | **Megasthenes** sent to Pataliputra |
### 6. CHANDRAGUPTA AND JAINISM
- Disciple of **Bhadrabahu** (sixth Jain Thera) – APPSC G2 2003 key; **not Sthulabhadra**.
- **12-year famine** in Magadha → Bhadrabahu migrated south; Chandragupta went with him.
- **First Indian king to abdicate** (for son Bindusara); became an ascetic; died by **Sallekhana** at **Shravanabelagola**, Karnataka.
- Source: **Parishishtaparvan** of **Hemachandra**.
- Famine led to the split: **Digambaras** (Bhadrabahu) and **Svetambaras** (Sthulabhadra).
### 7. BINDUSARA
- Reign **297–273 BCE**; son of Chandragupta and **Durdhara**.
- **Antiochus I** was asked for **sweet wine, dried figs and a philosopher** – the first two were sent; a philosopher **could not be sold**.
- Received envoys **Deimachus** and **Dionysius**.
- Religion: **Ajivika** (standard answer).
#### 7.1 Revolts at Taxila
| Reign | Revolt and prince sent |
|---|---|
| Bindusara | Revolt against oppressive officials (**dushtamatyas**); **Susima** (eldest prince) failed → **Ashoka** sent (Divyavadana) |
| Ashoka | Later Taxila revolt handled by **Kunala** |
### 8. GREEK ENVOYS AND INDICA
| Envoy | Sent by | Court |
|---|---|---|
| **Megasthenes** (c. 302 BCE) | **Seleucus I Nicator** | **Chandragupta** – wrote **Indica** |
| **Deimachus (Daimachos)** | **Antiochus I Soter** (Syria) | **Bindusara**; replaced Megasthenes (Strabo) |
| **Dionysius** | **Ptolemy II Philadelphus** (Egypt) | Bindusara |
- Megasthenes – **first Greek envoy to an Indian court**; mentions the **Andhras** – first to mention Andhra forts: **30 forts** (APPSC G2 2002).
- **Deimachus did not come with Alexander**.
- **Palibothra** (Pataliputra): municipal body of **30 members in six boards**; **64 gates, 570 towers**.
- **Seven classes**: philosophers, farmers, herdsmen, artisans, soldiers, overseers (spies), councillors – **no merchants**.
- Claimed no slaves – slavery did exist (Arthashastra, Ashokan edicts).
!PAIR Megasthenes | Seleucus | Chandragupta || Deimachus | Antiochus I | Bindusara

### 10. ASHOKA – TITLES AND ACCESSION
- Titles: **Devanampiya** ("Beloved of the Gods") and **Piyadasi** ("of gracious mien"); the name **Ashoka** appears in the **Maski** edict (found 1915) and the Junagadh inscription.
- Viceroy at **Taxila** and **Ujjain** under Bindusara.
- Accession struggle with **Susima**; Ceylonese chronicles speak of a **four-year struggle** among princes; accession c. **269–268 BC**.
### 11. RELIGION OF MAURYAN RULERS
| Ruler | Religion |
|---|---|
| **Chandragupta** | **Jainism** (Bhadrabahu) |
| **Bindusara** | **Ajivika** |
| **Ashoka** | **Buddhism** (after Kalinga); gave Barabar caves to Ajivikas |
| **Dasharatha** | **Buddhist** (CGPSC 2013 key); also patronised Ajivikas – **Nagarjuni caves** |
| **Samprati** | **Jainism** – "**Ashoka of Jainism**"; spread Jainism in Andhra |
- **Barabar caves** (Bihar) – **oldest surviving rock-cut caves**, carved in **granite**; **not at Girnar**; dedicated to the Ajivikas by **Ashoka and Dasharatha**.
- **Lomas Rishi** cave – Barabar Hills; famous carved façade.
- **Makkhali Gosala** – leader of the Ajivikas.

### 15. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. **Bhadrabahu**, not Sthulabhadra, was Chandragupta's teacher.
2. **Amitraghata** = Bindusara, not Chandragupta.
3. Lands ceded: Aria, Arachosia, Paropanisadai, Gedrosia – **not Bactria**.
4. **Helena** married Chandragupta – not the reverse.
5. **Deimachus** went to Bindusara; Megasthenes to Chandragupta.
6. Sudarshana lake **built** by Pushyagupta, canals by Tushaspha, repaired by Rudradaman.
7. **Dasharatha** = Buddhist (key) though an Ajivika patron.
8. **Rajula Mandagiri** has only a minor edict; Yerragudi has both.
9. Megasthenes' classes had **no merchants**.
10. **Mamulanar** is a Tamil poet, not a Greek writer.
