## U0720 | CYBER SECURITY ARCHITECTURE – IT ACT 2000, CERT-IN, NCIIPC, CYBER SWACHHTA KENDRA, I4C
### 1. IT ACT 2000
- Purpose: **legal recognition** to transactions by **electronic data interchange** ("**e-commerce**") and electronic records; framework against computer crimes.
- Concepts (Sec. 2): **electronic record, digital signature, certifying authority**.
- **Special law**: may override the IPC where an offence is fully covered.
- **Controller of Certifying Authorities (CCA)**: statutory regulator; licenses CAs, which issue Digital Signature Certificates.
- **2008 amendment**: technology-neutral "**electronic signature**"; added sexually explicit material, **video voyeurism**, data leakage by intermediaries, e-commerce fraud (not NCCC); inserted 70A, 70B.

### 3. SHREYA SINGHAL (2015) AND IT RULES
- **66A struck down** (Art. 19(1)(a); not saved by 19(2)); **69A upheld**; **79 read down** – "actual knowledge" = court order or government notice.
- **IT Rules 2021**: no due diligence = loss of safe harbour. Takedown within **three hours** (27 Mar 2026).
### 4. DIGITAL SIGNATURE AND PKI
- Gives **authentication, integrity, non-repudiation** – **not secrecy**; shows content is **unaltered**; asymmetric crypto + hash.
- **PKI** = digital security infrastructure; creates, manages, cancels certificates; **private key signs**, public key verifies.
!TRAP Record naming the issuing CA = digital certificate, not signature.
### 5. CERT-IN
- Since **January 2004**, **MeitY**; statutory via **70B** (2008); national incident response; **CVE Numbering Authority**; 200 auditors empanelled.
- **Directions, 28 Apr 2022**: service providers, intermediaries, data centres, body corporates, government, VASPs must report within **6 hours**; logs **180 days**; VPN data **5 years**.
- **Cyber Swachhta Kendra** = Botnet Cleaning and Malware Analysis Centre (CERT-In); free **USB Pratirodh, AppSamvid, M-Kavach**; ~98% users, 1,415 organisations (Jul 2025).
- **NCCC**: situational awareness.
