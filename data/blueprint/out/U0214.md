## U0214 | NATAKA SAMASTHALU – THEATRE COMPANIES AND THEIR NATIONALIST ROLE
### 1. ORIGINS OF TELUGU DRAMA COMPANIES
1. **Marathi Darwada Nataka Samajam** toured and set up a tent at **Rajamahendravaram (1880-81)**.
2. This inspired **Guntur's Hindu Nataka Samajam** and **Nellore's Lakshmi Vilasa Nataka Sabha**.
3. **Surabhi**: a family of **Tholu Bommalata** (leather-puppet) performers replaced puppets with live actors.
4. Surabhi is still a **family theatre**, about 140 years on.
- Drama companies were the **main mass entertainment** of the time → carriers of reform and nationalist messages.

### 3. BELLARY AND RAYALASEEMA THEATRE
- **Bellary** (then in the Madras Presidency) was an early base of Telugu theatre.
- **Dharmavaram Ramakrishnamacharyulu** – Sarasa Vinodini Sabha, Bellary (also listed as "Vinodini Sabha – Bellary").
- **Kolachalam Srinivasa Rao** – Bellary playwright known for **historical plays**.
- **Bellary Raghava** – celebrated stage actor, relative of Dharmavaram; a Bellary theatre is named after him.
- Rayalaseema companies: **Surabhi (Kadapa)**, **Rama Vilasa Sabha (Chittoor)**.
- **Lalita Kala Parishad, Anantapur** – opened by the Governor on **11 Feb 1956**; land donated by freedom fighter **Kalluru Subbarao**.

- **Jan 2022**: the AP government banned staging of ***Chintamani*** over the portrayal of the character Subbi Setty; the ban was challenged in the AP High Court.
### 5. PLAYS BANNED BY THE BRITISH
- **Viplava Parivartanamu**: published and **banned during the Non-Cooperation Movement**.
- Author: **Kodali Anjaneyulu** (APPSC 2018 key); the APPSC G2 2019 key named **Gollapudi Sitarama Sastri**.
- Gollapudi Sitarama Sastri supported the **NCM boycott of elections (1920)**.
- **Swadeshi and NCM years**: troupes such as the **Jatiya Nataka Rangam** promoted swadeshi goods and national education.
- Why watched: a drama company could put a political idea before **thousands of illiterate people in one evening**, so colonial officials watched troupes closely.
!TRAP For Viplava Parivartanamu learn both keys: Kodali Anjaneyulu (2018) and Gollapudi Sitarama Sastri (G2 2019).

### 7. PADYA NATAKAM VS SOCIAL DRAMA
| Feature | Padya natakam | Social drama |
|---|---|---|
| Content | **Mythological or epic** (Keechaka Vadham, Gayopakhyanam, Prahlada Charitamu) | **Contemporary evils** (bride-price, dowry, widowhood) |
| Language | Dialogue mainly in **sung verse (padyam)** | **Prose in spoken Telugu** (vyavaharika bhasha) |
| Examples | **Surabhi repertoire**, Gayopakhyanam | **Kanyasulkam**, Vyavahara Dharmabodhini, **Varavikrayam** |
