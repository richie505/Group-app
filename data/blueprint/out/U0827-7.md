### 1. CHESS – WORLD TITLE CYCLE 2024-26
#### 1.1 World Championship
- **D. Gukesh** – **World Champion**; beat **Ding Liren** in **Singapore, 2024**.
- He defends the title against **Javokhir Sindarov (Uzbekistan)** in **Geneva**, late 2026.
- Both players are aged **20** – the **youngest World title match ever** (29 July 2026).
- World Championship match 2026 venue = **Geneva**.
#### 1.2 Challenger – Javokhir Sindarov
- Won the **FIDE World Cup 2025** at **Goa** (November 2025).
- Won the **Candidates 2026** (Open) at **Pegeia, Cyprus** (April 2026).
- He thus plays Gukesh for the World title in Geneva.
!TRAP Sindarov won **both** the World Cup 2025 (Goa) and the Candidates 2026 (Cyprus). Title match = **Geneva**; Chess **Olympiad** = **Samarkand**. Do not mix the three venues.

#### 7.2 World Para Athletics Championships 2025 – More Facts
- **Brazil** topped the medal table (15 gold), improving on its second place of 2024; **China** did not top the table for the first time since 2013.
- India's **22 medals** beat its previous best of **17** at **Kobe 2024**.
- **Sumit Antil** (F64 javelin) and **Sandip Singh Sargar** (F44 javelin) won gold on **30 September 2025**.
!TRAP World Para Athletics 2025 at New Delhi: **Brazil** topped; **India** finished **10th** with a record 22 medals.
