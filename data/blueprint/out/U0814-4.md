### 1. GREEN AMMONIA AND GREEN HYDROGEN PROJECTS
#### 1.1 Kakinada green ammonia project (AM Green)
- **India's first and the world's largest green ammonia project**; India's **first commercial green ammonia** plant.
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Foundation | **17 January 2026**, **Kakinada** – by CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu** and Deputy CM **Pawan Kalyan** |
| Developer | **AM Green** (**Greenko Group**) |
| Investment | **₹18,000 crore** (~$2 billion) initially; ecosystem $10 billion |
| Area | **495 acres** |
| Capacity | **1.5 MTPA** green ammonia in phases: **0.5 MTPA by 2027**, **1 MTPA by 2028**, full **1.5 MTPA by 2030** |
| Renewables | 7.5 GW solar + wind; 2 GW round-the-clock power with pumped hydro storage |
| Brownfield | Conversion of the **Nagarjuna Fertilisers** plant |
| Policy | **Integrated Clean Energy (ICE) Policy, 2024** |
- Main buyer **Uniper (Germany)** – **first green energy export from India to Europe**; **5 lakh tonnes/year from 2028**, meeting the **EU RFNBO** standard. AM Green–Uniper documents exchanged in **Ahmedabad (January 2026)** before the PM and the German Chancellor.
- Exports also to **Germany, Japan, Singapore**.
- Funders: **ADIA (UAE)**, **Petronas (Malaysia)**, **GIC (Singapore)**.
- Final investment decision done; target **5 million tonnes** green ammonia by **2032** – about **20%** of National Green Hydrogen Mission targets; part of the **WEF Transitioning Industrial Clusters** initiative.
- **Krishnapatnam** and **Moolapeta** – among **JERA's** proposed green ammonia locations.
#### 1.2 India's first green urea plant, Poodimadaka
- **India's first large-scale indigenous green urea plant** – MoU between **NTPC Green Energy (NGEL)** and **Assago (Asago) Industries**, signed **2 February 2026** at NTPC Bhawan, New Delhi.
- Site: **Poodimadaka (Pudimadaka) Green Hydrogen Hub**, near Visakhapatnam (Anakapalli).
- Capacity: **1,000 tonnes/day** green urea; NTPC Green supplies **green ammonia, CO2 and RE power**; Assago builds and runs the urea unit.
- Hub products: **green ammonia, green methanol, sustainable aviation fuel (SAF), green urea**; planned green chemicals cluster of 2.5 MTPA.
- Aim: cut urea imports by **8–10 million tonnes** (of 35–40 million tonnes consumed).
!TRAP Green urea / green hydrogen hub = Pudimadaka; first commercial green ammonia = Kakinada.

### 6. IRRIGATION AND RIVER WATERS
#### 6.1 Polavaram project
- **National Multipurpose Irrigation Project** on the **Godavari** – irrigation, drinking water, hydropower, flood mitigation.
- Legal basis: **Section 90** of the **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014** (not Section 89).
- After bifurcation (on **NITI Aayog** advice) the **State Government builds** Polavaram and the **Centre gives the money**.
- In this period CM **Naidu** ordered completion by **July 2027** – six months before the official deadline (**December 2027**) – so it is ready before the **Godavari Pushkaralu 2027**.
- Latest target (Water Resources Minister **Nimmala Ramanaidu**, **15 Aug 2026**; CM Naidu, July and September 2026): completion by **March 2027**.
- Headworks **80%** complete; 100% spillway and powerhouse excavation; CWC cleared the spillway blocks (April 2026); **Budget 2026–27: ₹6,105 crore**.
- Inter-State coordination with **Telangana** and **Chhattisgarh**.
| Component | Detail |
|---|---|
| Diaphragm wall | 50,858 of 65,352 sq m (**78%**); target **February 2026** |
| ECRF Gap-1 | Embankment 25 m to 38.32 m; by March 2026 |
| ECRF Gap-2 | Target advanced to **July 2027** (from December 2027) |
| Initial height | **41.11 m** – **124.2 TMC** |
| Final FRL | **45.72 m** – **194.6 TMC** |

#### 8.2 Mangroves and the Great Green Wall
- Directive by Deputy CM and Forest Minister **K. Pawan Kalyan** – fast-track green cover, protect unclassified mangroves.
- About **10,000 acres** of unclassified mangroves identified by satellite – to be notified as **protected forests**; inter-departmental task force; road map by **end of January**.
- Flagship programmes: **Great Green Wall of AP** and the **50% Green Cover Programme**.
- **Great Green Wall** (AP Forest Department) – bioshield about **5 km wide** along the **974 km** coastline; protects about **33 lakh** people; focus on the Krishna–Godavari delta; three zones – mangroves at river mouths, windbreaks on canal bunds/roads, village agroforestry; disaster losses about **₹2.25 lakh crore** in 30 years.
- **MISHTI** (Mangrove Initiative for Shoreline Habitats & Tangible Incomes) – **2023**, **MoEFCC**, after India joined the **Mangrove Alliance for Climate** at **COP27 (2022)**; coastline and salt pan lands.
#### 8.3 New species and herbal garden
- New army ants **Aenictus chittoorensis** and **Aenictus lankamallensis** – near **Sri Venkateswara Wildlife Sanctuary**, southern Eastern Ghats; by scientists from Karnataka and Odisha; Aenictus = Old World army ants (prey on ants, termites).
- **Sri Venkateswara National Park**: est. **September 1989**; **353 sq km**; Seshachalam–Tirumala hills.
- **TTD Sacred Herbal Garden**, Tirumala – **4 acres** between lower and upper ghat roads near the **GNC Toll Gate**; ₹4.25 crore; **13 thematic zones** (health, spiritual traditions, ecology); operational by end-2026; conserves medicinal plants of the **Seshachalam** forests (**Seshachalam Biosphere Reserve**, notified **2010**; Red sanders).

- Legal basis: **Section 194(D)** of the **Motor Vehicles Act**; helmets compulsory for **rider and pillion**.
- About **45%** of road accident deaths involve two-wheeler riders; about **40%** preventable with helmets.
