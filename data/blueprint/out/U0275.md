## U0275 | INDIAN INDEPENDENCE ACT 1947; THE CONSTITUENT ASSEMBLY – DEMAND, COMPOSITION, FUNCTIONING
### 1. DEMAND FOR A CONSTITUENT ASSEMBLY, 1934–1946
#### 1.1 Timeline
| Year | Step |

| **1934** | **M.N. Roy** first put forward the idea |

### 2. COMPOSITION
#### 2.1 Seats
| Item | Figure |
|---|---|
| Total planned | **389** |
| British India | **296** = **292** Governor's provinces + **4** Chief Commissioners' provinces |
| Princely States | **93** |
| Ratio | **1 member per 10 lakh** population |
| After partition | **299** (members from Pakistan areas left) |
- Provincial seats split among **Muslims, Sikhs and General** by population.
#### 2.2 Method of Election (July–August 1946)
- **Indirectly elected** by members of the **Provincial Legislative Assemblies** (set up under the 1935 Act; provincial elections January 1946).
- **Proportional representation by single transferable vote**.
- **NOT adult franchise**: restricted franchise of the 1935 Act (tax, education, property).
- Princely representatives **nominated by rulers**; 93 seats at first unfilled; states joined from the third session (**28 April 1947**: Baroda, Bikaner, Jaipur, Patiala, Rewa, Udaipur).
- Nature: **partly elected, partly nominated**.
- Results: **Congress 208, Muslim League 73, others 15**.
- **15 women** members: Ammu Swaminathan, Annie Mascarene, Sarojini Naidu, Dakshayani Velayudhan, Durgabai Deshmukh, Hansa Mehta, Kamala Choudhury, Leela Roy, Malati Choudhury and others.
- **Ambedkar**: first elected from **East Bengal**; later from the Bombay (Pune) seat vacated by **M.R. Jayakar**.
!TRAP Elections were July–August 1946, not June–July. Not direct, not adult franchise.

### 5. LINGUISTIC PROVINCES – DAR COMMISSION AND JVP
- **Linguistic Provinces Commission** (**17 June 1948**): **S.K. Dar**, retired Allahabad HC judge (APPSC GS 2025).
- **Dar Commission and JVP Committee** (Jawaharlal, Vallabhbhai, Pattabhi) both **rejected linguistic states** for the time being; JVP left room for **Andhra**.
- AP angle: Madras Assembly resolved (four months before independence) to divide the Presidency into **Andhra, Tamil, Karnataka, Kerala**; Nehru accepted the Andhra demand **27 Nov 1947**; Andhra dropped from the Draft of 21 Feb 1948; demand led to **Andhra State, 1953**.

### 8. OTHER FUNCTIONS – SYMBOLS, LANGUAGE AND LEGISLATURE
#### 8.1 Symbols
- **Flag**: wheel from the **Ashoka Chakra (Dharmachakra)**, **24 spokes**, wheel of law (justice).
- **Jana Gana Mana**: adopted 24 Jan 1950 by Prasad's statement, not a vote; full rendition **52 seconds**.
- **Vande Mataram** (Bankimchandra): **Sanskrit**, in **Bengali script**; declared national song, **honoured equally**.
- **Art. 51A(a)** names the **Flag and Anthem**, NOT the Song; 2026 amendment of the Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act gives the Song equal statutory protection (cited 23 Aug 2026); **Bijoe Emmanuel v State of Kerala (1986)**.
#### 8.2 Language
- **Munshi-Ayyangar formula**: **Hindi in Devanagari**; **15-year** transition with English continued; **14** other languages in the Eighth Schedule – NOT immediate end of English.
#### 8.3 Dual Role
| Role | Chaired by |
|---|---|
| Constitution-maker | **Rajendra Prasad** |
| Dominion legislature | **G.V. Mavalankar** |
- After 26 Jan 1950: **Provisional Parliament** till the **1951-52** elections.

### 10. INDIAN INDEPENDENCE ACT, 1947
#### 10.1 Mountbatten Plan and the Bill
- **3 June 1947 Plan** (Mountbatten): accepted by Congress and League; basis of the Act.
- Bill introduced in the Commons **4 July 1947**; **royal assent 18 July 1947**.
- **Radcliffe** chaired the **Punjab** and **Bengal** Boundary Commissions; award published **17 August 1947**.
#### 10.2 Provisions
- Ended **British sovereignty**; two Dominions **India and Pakistan** from **15 August 1947**.
- **Governor-General** (common or separate) to act on Dominion ministers' advice; British monarch nominal head; **Crown lost its veto**.
- **Constituent Assembly** = sovereign constitution-maker + Dominion legislature; **Central Legislative Assembly ended**.
- **Paramountcy lapsed**: about **565** princely states theoretically free; expected to join India or Pakistan.
- Dominion status ended with the Republic, **26 Jan 1950**.
#### 10.3 Princely States
| Instrument | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Instrument of Accession** | Ceded **defence, external affairs, communications** (NOT finance) |
| **Standstill Agreement** | Existing arrangements continued; **Hyderabad** signed one in **November 1947** |
- **Kashmir** acceded **26 October 1947** after the tribal invasion; UN ceasefire **1 January 1949**.
#### 10.4 Governors-General
- **Mountbatten**: last Viceroy and first GG of independent India.
- **C. Rajagopalachari**: last and only Indian GG (June 1948-Jan 1950); assented to the **Madras Estates (Abolition) Act** on 2 April 1949.
- Significance: sovereignty came with partition; Congress accepted dominion status for a quick, peaceful transfer.
### 11. REMARKS AND CRITICISMS
| Statement | Speaker |
|---|---|
| Constitutional morality "is not a natural sentiment. It has to be cultivated" (borrowed from **George Grote**) | **Ambedkar**, 4 Nov 1948 |
| Civil disobedience = "**grammar of anarchy**"; "a life of contradictions" | **Ambedkar**, closing speech **25 Nov 1949** |
| Constitution a sacred document | **Ambedkar** |
| "A Constitution, like a machine, is a lifeless thing…" | **Rajendra Prasad**, 26 Nov 1949 |
| "A **one-party body** in an essentially one-party country"; "Assembly was Congress and Congress was India" | **Granville Austin** |
| "**A body of Hindus**"; lawyer-politician dominated | **Viscount Simon** |
| "**Drifting Committee**" | **Naziruddin Ahmed** |
| Represented "only one major community" | **Winston Churchill** |
| Delay adult franchise by 15 years | **Maulana Azad** (opposed by Prasad, Nehru) |
- Criticisms: **not representative** (no adult franchise); **not sovereign** (British creation); **time-consuming** (about 3 years vs about 4 months for the USA); **Congress-dominated**; **"borrowed"/patchwork**.
- Defence: multiparty body; worked through committees.
- **Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas)**: 26 November, since **2015**, Ambedkar's **125th birth anniversary** year (14 April 2016; Rs 10 and Rs 125 coins).
