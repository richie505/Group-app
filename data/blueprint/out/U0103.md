## U0103 | ARAB CONQUEST OF SINDH; GHAZNAVID AND GHURID INVASIONS; BATTLES OF TARAIN
### 1. INVASIONS IN ORDER
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 712 CE | **Muhammad bin Qasim** defeats **Raja Dahir**; Sindh taken |
| 1000-1027 | **Mahmud of Ghazni**: **17 raids** |
| 1025-26 | **Somnath** sacked (Solanki **Bhima I**) |
| 1175 | Ghori's first attack: **Multan** (Multan and Uch 1175-76) |

- Chronology: Mahmud Ghaznavi (1000-1027) → Muhammad Ghori (1175-1205) → Genghis Khan → Taimur.
- Mahmud **raided for plunder**; Ghori **conquered and stayed**.
### 2. ARAB CONQUEST OF SINDH (712 CE)
#### 2.1 Commander and Ruler Defeated
- **First Muslim invaders** of India: the **Arabs**, under **Muhammad bin Qasim**, Sindh, 712.
- Qasim: nephew and son-in-law of **Al-Hajjaj (Hajjaj bin Yusuf)**, the Umayyad governor; sent by **Caliph al-Walid** at the age of **17**.
- Removed **Raja Dahir**, capital **Alor (Aror/Arod)**, near modern Karachi.
- Places taken: **Debal, Nirun, Sehwan, Brahmanabad, Multan** (not Somnath).
!TRAP Dahir's capital was Alor (Aror); Brahmanabad was only one of the places taken.
#### 2.2 Chachnama
- Arabic chronicle of the conquest of Sindh (680-718); original author unclear.
- **Persian translation** by **Muhammad Ali bin Abu Bakr Kufi** (BPSC 2024).
#### 2.3 Causes and Effects
| Factor | Fact |
|---|---|
| Causes | Arab maritime interests, expansion, **piracy against Arab ships** |
| Religion | **No mass Islamisation**; Hindus and Buddhists given **dhimmi** status |
| Limited reach | Confined to Sindh by the **Thar** and **Aravallis**, strong **Pratiharas**, Rashtrakutas, Palas, weak resources |
| Knowledge transfer | Indian **decimal and place-value** maths, astronomy, medicine → Islamic world → Europe |
- **Nagabhata I (Pratihara)** drove back the Arabs, keeping them in Sindh; Arab writers said Pratiharas had the **strongest cavalry** in India.
### 3. GHAZNAVIDS AND MAHMUD OF GHAZNI
#### 3.1 Founders
| Person | Role |
|---|---|
| **Alptigin** | **Founder** of the Ghaznavid line; Turkish slave of the Samanid governors |
| **Sabuktigin** | **Real founder** |
| **Mahmud** (998-1030) | The raider; 17 raids |
!TRAP Alptigin = founder; Sabuktigin = real founder; Mahmud = the raider.
#### 3.2 Raids → Target
| Target | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Nagarkot** (Kangra) | 1009 | Temple treasure |
| **Thanesar** (Chakraswamin temple) | 1014 | |
| **Mathura** | 1018 | |
| **Kanauj** | 1018 | Pratihara king **submitted** |
| **Somnath** (Gujarat) | 1025-26 | Solanki **Bhima I**; loot said to be 20 million dinars |
- Raids were repeated, **not systematic conquest**; he did not aim at a permanent empire.
- Issued **silver coins** with **Arabic and Sanskrit** legends; built the Ghazni minarets.
- After Mahmud, the Ghaznavids weakened under **Seljuk** pressure.
#### 3.3 Indian Rulers Who Resisted
- **Jayapala** and **Anandapala** (Hindu Shahis): first to resist Mahmud.
- **Chandela Vidyadhara**: faced and **repulsed** Mahmud (1019-20), not defeated (MPPSC 2021). Chandelas ruled Central India, 9th-13th c.; first capital **Khajuraho**.
- **Bhimadeva I** ruled during the Somnath attack and **reconstructed** the temple.
#### 3.4 Motives and Effects
- Motives: more than religion; **temple wealth** to fund the state, political expansion.
- Effect: exposed India's military weakness and opened the **north-west frontier**.
- **Somnath Swabhiman Parv** (launched **January 2026**): 1,000 years since Mahmud's attack.

#### 4.1 Al-Biruni
- Born **973** in **Khwarizm (Khiva)**; came with Mahmud; "**father of Indian historical writing**".
- Kitab-ul-Hind: fair account of 11th-century Indian society, religion, science, customs. Translated into English by **Edward Sachau**, Hindi by Rajnikant Sharma.
- **Kitab Patanjal**: Arabic translation of Patanjali's **Yoga-Sutra**.
- Not a secular author: no kind word about the Somnath raid.
### 5. MUHAMMAD GHORI AND THE BATTLES OF TARAIN
#### 5.1 Route and Conquests
- Shihabuddin Muhammad Ghori (Ghurid, 1173-1206) entered through the **Gomal Pass**; aimed at **permanent occupation**.
- Multan (1175) → Peshawar (c. 1179) → Sindh (c. 1182) → Punjab and Lahore (c. 1190).
#### 5.2 Battles → Opponent + Victor
| Battle | Year | Opponent | Victor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gujarat (Kasahrada/Kayadara, near Mount Abu) | 1178 | **Mularaja II** (Bhimdev II), Chaulukya/Solanki; regent mother **Naika Devi** | Gujarat; Ghori's **first defeat** |
| **First Tarain** (near Karnal, Haryana) | 1191 | **Prithviraj Chauhan** (Prithviraja III, Chahamana of Ajmer) | **Prithviraj**; Ghori barely escaped |
| **Second Tarain** | 1192 | Prithviraj Chauhan | **Ghori**; turning point, opened North India |
| **Chandawar** (Firozabad, UP, on the Yamuna near Agra) | 1194 | **Jayachandra (Jaichand)**, Gahadavala of Kannauj | **Ghori** |
- Cause of Tarain I: **Tabarhinda (Bhatinda)**; Prithviraj took it but left no strong garrison.
- Aibak (Ghori's slave, Master of Stables) got the **first Iqta** in India (1192); capital **Lahore**; first Sultan of Delhi on Ghori's death (1206). Delhi Sultanate 1206-1526: Mamluk, Khalji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, Lodi.
!TRAP Somnath = Bhima I (Mahmud); Ghori beaten by Mularaja II (1178).
### 6. BAKHTIYAR KHALJI
- **Muhammad bin Bakhtiyar Khalji**: Ghori's slave/general; **real founder of Turk rule in Bihar** (BPSC 2022).
- Conquered Bihar and Bengal (**1193-1203**); sacked **Nalanda, Vikramashila, Odantapuri**.
- Entered **Nadiya**, drove **Lakshmana Sena** to East Bengal (1204); ruled from **Lakhnauti**.
### 7. CAUSES OF TURKISH SUCCESS
| Factor | Point |
|---|---|
| Political | **No political unity** in North India; rival Rajput states after the Pratihara collapse (UPPSC 2024: A and R true, R explains A) |
| Military | **Superior war technique**: mobile cavalry, **mounted archers** vs elephant-reliant armies (APPSC key) |
| Social | **R.S. Sharma**: "Indian feudalism", feudal levies, sub-infeudation, closed village economy; caste limited fighting to few groups |
- Debate: historians stress military organisation over religious zeal.
