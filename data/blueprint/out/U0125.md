## U0125 | FOLK CULTURE OF ANDHRA - BURRAKATHA, HARIKATHA, VEEDHI NATAKAM, OGGU KATHA, TOLU BOMMALATA
### 1. FOLK FORMS THROUGH THE AGES
- Folk performance = living proof of a **distinct Telugu culture**; it carried that consciousness to the villages during the **Andhra Movement**.
#### 1.1 Vijayanagara Period
| Form | Audience |
|---|---|
| **Yakshaganam** | **Royal courts and elites** |
| **Tolu Bommalata** | Most popular entertainment of **rural Andhra** (APPSC G2 2019) |
| **Vipra-Vinodins** | Brahmin jugglers/actors for Brahmin households |
#### 1.2 Qutb Shahi Period
- Yakshaganam became **folk theatre**; Bhagavata stories and **Parijatam** performed across the countryside; **Kuchipudi** flourished under Golconda.
### 2. YAKSHAGANAM AND VEEDHI NATAKAM
- **Koravanji** = ancient **precursor** of the Telugu Yakshagana.
- **Sugreeva Vijayam** by **Kandukuri Rudra Kavi** = **first published Telugu Yakshaganam**.
- Andhra Yakshaganam: performed in **Telugu**, rich costumes, epic and Puranic stories.
- **Veedhi Natakam / Veedhi Bhagavatam**: street dance-drama → forerunner of Kuchipudi.
### 3. HARIKATHA
- Also **Harikathalu**; a **single artist** sings, acts and dances (about **3 hours**) with cymbals (**Chidatalu**).
- Deeply rooted in **Vizianagaram**.
#### 3.1 Founder – Adibhatla Narayana Dasu (1864-1945)
- **Harikatha Pitamaha** / Father of Harikatha (APPSC G2 2012); born at **Ajjada**, **Vizianagaram** district; multilingual scholar.
| Work | Type |
|---|---|
| **Na Yeruka** | **Autobiography** |
| **Jagajyothi**, **Navarasa Tarangini**, Yadhartha Ramayanam | Kavyas |
| **Sri Krishna Kathamrutam** | Sanskrit Harikatha |
| Rama, Mukunda, Shiva, Mrutyunjaya Satakams | Satakams |
### 4. BURRAKATHA
#### 4.1 Form and Names
- **Earlier name: Jangam Katha** (APPSC GS 2018); also **Jamukula Kathalu**; in Rayalaseema called **Thandana Katha**.
- **Birthplace: Guntur**; folk form of **Andhra Pradesh** (APPSC GS 2019) and Telangana.
#### 4.2 Troupe Roles and Instruments
- **Group of three**: **main narrator** in the middle (plays the **burra**) + two **Vantalu** who respond and comment.
- Instruments: **Gummata, Burra, Tambura, Ukki**; chorus "**Thandana Taana**".
#### 4.3 Exponents and Political Use
- **Sheikh Nazar** = **Burrakatha Pitamaha**; born **5 Feb 1920**, **Pannekallu, Guntur**; works **Palnati Yuddham**, **Bengal Karuvu**.
- Popularisers: **Nazar, Nittala Brothers, Bhadrakali**.
- Narrators carried **news, reform and nationalist messages** to unlettered villages.
- **Praja Natya Mandali** used folk plays for **socialist ideals**; hit play **Mabhoomi** by **Sunkara Satyanarayana and Vasireddy Bhaskara Rao**.
- Sunkara Satyanarayana's Burrakathas: **Kashtajeevi, Raithu Vijayam, Stalin, Alluri Sitaramaraju, Veeresalingam**.
!PAIR Harikatha | solo | Chidatalu || Burrakatha | trio | Burra, Tambura, Gummata
### 5. TOLU BOMMALATA
- **Material**: thin, **translucent leather** figures **painted on both sides**.
- Moved behind a **white screen** lit by an **oil lamp**; puppeteer sings verses and dialogue from behind.
- **Unique feature**: **moving the heads** of the puppets.
- Themes: **Ramayana, Mahabharata, Puranas**, with classical music.
- Region and family: **Nimmalakunta** (Anantapur) – GI tag for AP leather puppetry (**2008**).
| Work mentioning it | Author |
|---|---|
| **Panditaradhya Charitra** | **Palakuriki Somanatha** |
| **Palnati Veeracharitra** | **Srinatha** |
| **Bhaskara Satakam** | **Marada Venkayya** |
#### 5.1 Surabhi Theatre
- **Vanarasa Govinda Rao**, of a **leather-puppet family** (not Bhagavata Mela), replaced puppets with **live actors** in **Keechaka Vadham**.
- Staged at a wedding in **Sorugu** village near **Jammalamadugu** (Kadapa) → Sorugu came to be called **Surabhi**; family theatre about **140 years** old.
### 6. KATHAS AND COMMUNITY FORMS
| Form | Performing community | Instrument / note |
|---|---|---|
| **Oggu Katha** | **Golla / Kuruma** (shepherds) | Small **oggu drum**; ballad of **Telangana**; rural life and struggle |
| **Asadi Katha** | **Asadi** sub-caste of **Madigas** | Story of **Ellamma**; Javiki, Chedika |
| **Bhagavatulu** (Krishna Leelas) | Mostly **Yadavas**; **Rayalaseema** | Large bells (Jeganta), ankle bells, cymbals; dance round a pillar with Sita-Rama-Lakshmana idols |
| **Pichukuntla Katha** | **Pichukuntla** troupe of 3 | Sings Srinatha's **Palnati Veeracharitra** over **15 nights**; turban, sword, shield |
| **Chindu Bhagavatam** | **Chindu** (Madiga sub-group), Telangana | Dance-drama |
| **Veeranatyam** | **Veeramushti** | "Dance of **Veerabhadra**" in Shiva temples |
!TRAP Oggu = shepherds; Asadi = Madigas; Bhagavatulu = Yadavas.
### 7. KUCHIPUDI AND THE BHAGAVATA MELA
1. **Bhagavata Mela** dance-drama of hereditary **Brahmin** families.
2. **Siddhendra Yogi** founded Kuchipudi.
3. **Abul Hasan Tana Shah** (last Qutb Shahi) **gifted Kuchipudi village** to the performers.
4. Named after **Kuchipudi village, Krishna district** – a village-theatre origin for a classical form.

### 9. CURRENT DEVELOPMENTS
- June 2025: Tholu Bommalata, Harikatha, Burrakatha, Padya Natakam flagged **close to extinction**.
- **27 Jul 2026**: Minister **Kandula Durgesh**, at the **Undavalli Caves**, announced an annual **Telugu Mahotsavam** and revival of **Nandi Natakotsavams**.
- Decline: lack of patronage, only 3–4 shows a year, youth disinterest, weak welfare for veteran artists.
- Related: **Vijayarama Raju** of Vizianagaram took **French** help (**Bussy**) in the **Bobbili War (1757)** (APPSC G2 2019).
