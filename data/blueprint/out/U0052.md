## U0052 | IKSHVAKU SOCIO-ECONOMIC CONDITIONS AND ROYAL WOMEN DONORS
### 1. ROYAL WOMEN DONORS
- Kings were **Vedic**; **royal women** were the main patrons of Buddhism at **Nagarjunakonda**, giving **without male intervention**.
| Donor | Relation | Gift |
|---|---|---|
| **Chamtisri** (Santisri) | Sister of Sri Santamula; paternal aunt of Virapurushadatta | **First woman donor**; built/restored the **Maha Chaitya** in Virapurushadatta's **6th regnal year**; **9 ayaka pillars**; **170 dinari-masakas** |
| **Adavi Chamtisri** | Sister of Virapurushadatta | Ayaka pillar, Maha Chaitya |
| **Harmyasri** | Sister of Santamula | Pillar for her mother (Aparamahavinaseliya chaitya); daughter **Chathasri** also |
| **Bapisri, Chatisri** | Queens of Virapurushadatta (his aunt's daughters) | Pillars at the Mahachaitya |
| **Rudradhara Bhattarika** | Queen; daughter of **Rudrasena of Ujjain** | Ayaka pillar |
| **Kodabalisri** | Daughter of Virapurushadatta | Vihara for **Mahishasaka** monks |
| **Bhatti Mahadevi** | Chief queen; mother of Ehuvala | Vihara + chaitya for **Bahushrutiya** |
| **Upasika Bodhisri** | **Non-royal**; daughter of **Revanta of Amaravati** | **Sinhala Vihara**; viharas for **foreign monks**; **first among women donors** |
!TRAP Chamtisri = first woman donor; Bodhisri = ranked first among women donors. Bodhisri did not build a vihara for the Aparamahavinaseliya sect.
### 2. DONATIVE INSCRIPTIONS
- Mostly **Prakrit ayaka-pillar** records of Virapurushadatta's **6th regnal year**; each names the donor by her tie to the king.
- Ikshvakus were **first to date** records by **year, month and day**.
- Bodhisri's record = evidence of **non-royal** patronage.
### 3. AGRARIAN ECONOMY AND CHAMTAMULA'S GIFTS
- **Vasishthiputra Sri Santamula** gave **gold, cows, ploughs and land**; brought **Brahmins from the north** and gave them **agraharas**; performed **Ashvamedha, Vajapeya, Agnistoma** (Ashvamedha altar at Nagarjunakonda).
- Revenue split **four ways**: administration + army; temples + festivals; poets + scholars; gifts to religious bodies. Some went to **tanks and canals** – not to overseas colonies.
- Shift from the Satavahana **trade economy** to an **agrarian, agrahara-based** early medieval order.
### 4. GUILDS AND ECONOMIC TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Bhaga** | Land tax, **1/6** of produce |
| **Halikas** | Farmers |
| **Nigamalu** (Negimalu) | Guilds; continued the Satavahana **sreni** tradition |
| **Parnika Sreni** | **Betel-leaf** traders |
| **Poosika Sreni** | **Sweetmeat** traders |
| **Sale / Telika / Kammaru** | Weavers / oil pressers / blacksmiths |
### 5. TRADE, PORTS AND COINS
- Roman trade **lower than under the Satavahanas**; one account says sea trade almost collapsed after Rome's fall.
- **Roman gold and silver coins** at Nagarjunakonda; **Roman-style amphitheatre** at Vijayapuri (Romans said to have built a stadium under Sri Santamula).
| Exports | Imports |
|---|---|
| Spices, iron, coconuts, cotton textiles | Gold, silver, wine, glassware |
- Coins of **lead and copper**; symbols **Ujjain symbol, elephant, lion**; found in Guntur, Krishna, Godavari districts.
### 6. SOCIETY
- **Shudras** the majority; Brahmins rose through **Brahmadeya** grants (exempt from **18 taxes**); a new **landlord class**.
- **Cross-cousin marriage** began with the Ikshvakus; alliances with the **Kshatrapas of Ujjain** and the **Chutus** (Kodabalisri married Vishnurudra Sivalananda Satakarni).
- Records show **matronymics** (Vasishthiputra), naming after **grandfather** (Ehuvala Santamula) and **sati**.

- First woman donor: **Chamtisri**; first among women donors: **Bodhisri**.
- First dating by year, month, day: **Ikshvakus**.
