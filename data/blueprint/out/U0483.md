## U0483 | FOOD SECURITY – NFSA 2013, PDS REFORM, BUFFER STOCKS, ONE NATION ONE RATION CARD, PMGKAY
### 1. FOOD SECURITY – CONCEPT AND DIMENSIONS
#### 1.1 Definition
- **FAO** (HQ **Rome**) / **World Food Summit 1996**: food security exists when **all people, at all times**, have **physical and economic access** to sufficient, safe and nutritious food for an active and healthy life.
!TRAP Food security does NOT mean access "to the **food of their choice**" (APPSC G2 2017).
#### 1.2 Dimensions
| Dimension | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Availability** | Production + imports + previous stocks |
| **Accessibility** | Food within reach of every person |
| **Affordability** | Enough money for sufficient, safe, nutritious food |
| **Absorption / utilisation** | Body's ability to use the food |
| **Stability** | Access at all times |
!TRAP **Accountability**, price, quantity of exports are NOT dimensions (UPPSC 2025).
#### 1.3 National vs household
| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **National** | Aggregate production + buffer stock |
| **Household** | Each household can actually access and afford food |
- "National but not household food security" = sufficient stock, but not all households can get it – hence PDS and NFSA.
- Food security is needed **even in surplus years**: starvation comes from unequal distribution and lack of access.
- In India both rich (wrong diet) and poor (poor diet) are malnourished.
### 2. ENTITLEMENT APPROACH
- **Amartya Sen**, *Poverty and Famines* (**1981**): famines come from **collapse of entitlements** (command over food via income, exchange, transfers), not merely a fall in availability.
- UPPSC 2025 A-R: both true, R explains A.
### 3. PDS TO NFSA – EVOLUTION
| Stage | Year | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| PDS | 1940s | **World War II rationing** (Bengal famine backdrop) |
| Buffer stock | **Fourth Plan (1969-74)** | Formally introduced |
| **RPDS** | June 1992 | 1,775 blocks |
| **TPDS** | **June 1997** (not 1985) | BPL/APL |
| NFSA | 2013 | Rights-based |
| PMGKAY | 2020 | Extra free grain |
- **PDS** = government shop chain giving basic food and non-food goods at low prices; main aim food security for the poor.
- **Procurement prices** = prices at which government buys grain for PDS and buffer stock.
- **Atal Khadyanna Yojana** – Uttarakhand (2011): APL 10 kg wheat + 10 kg rice at ₹4 and ₹6/kg; BPL at ₹2 and ₹3/kg.
### 4. NFSA 2013 – NATURE AND COVERAGE
- Passed **5 July 2013** (first as an Ordinance); notified **10 Sep 2013** with backdated effect; **not 2014**.
- Welfare → **rights-based**; legal, justiciable right. Ministry: Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution (**DFPD**).
- Objective: **food and nutritional security** at affordable prices for a **life with dignity** (APPSC 2025).
- Coverage: up to **75% rural** + **50% urban** = about **two-thirds (≈67%)**; ~80-81 crore people (APPSC G2 2017: 75%).
- Women empowerment: **eldest woman (18+)** = head of household for ration cards.
!TRAP NFSA objective distractors: "PDS Act", "FCI Act", "NFSA 2014".
### 5. NFSA ENTITLEMENTS
| Category | Entitlement | Issue price |
|---|---|---|
| **Priority Household** | **5 kg per person**/month | ₹3 rice / ₹2 wheat / ₹1 coarse grain |
| **AAY** | **35 kg per household**/month | Same; now free |
| Pregnant / lactating (**Sec 4**) | Free meal + maternity benefit **≥ ₹6,000** | – |
| Children 6 months-6 years | Take-home ration or hot meal via **ICDS/anganwadi** | – |
| Children 6-14 years | **Mid-day meal** / PM POSHAN | – |
- Issue prices ₹3/2/1 **not revised since July 2013** while MSP and subsidy rise.
- **Draft NFS (Amendment) Bill 2026** (DFPD, 24 June 2026): AAY → **7 kg per person, capped at 35 kg**; amends first proviso to Sec 3(1); ends small households getting more per head.
!TRAP PHH = per person (5 kg); AAY = per household (35 kg).

### 9. BUFFER STOCKS AND OMSS
- **CACP** advises MSP → **FCI** procures wheat and rice → **CCEA** fixes minimum norms **each quarter** (1 Apr, 1 Jul, 1 Oct, 1 Jan) → sold at issue price in deficit areas.
| Quarter | Norm (rice + wheat) |
|---|---|
| 1 April | 210.4 lakh tonnes |
| 1 July | **411.2** lakh tonnes (highest) |
| 1 October | 307.7 lakh tonnes |
| 1 January | 214.1 lakh tonnes |
- **Strategic reserve**: **5 million tonnes** above operational stocks (30 LT wheat + 20 LT rice).
- Stocks: latest ~602 LMT, about **3x** the norms.
- **OMSS**: FCI sells surplus grain at fixed prices – raises supply, cools prices, clears stock, cuts carrying cost; does **not** replace FCI procurement.
### 10. PMGKAY, ONORC AND PRICE INTERVENTIONS
#### 10.1 PMGKAY
- Launched **2020** (COVID/Atmanirbhar package): **5 kg free grain per person** on top of NFSA.
- From **1 Jan 2023** merged into NFSA – whole NFSA allocation **free** ("One Nation–One Price–One Ration").
- Extended **1 Jan 2024 – 31 Dec 2028**; ~₹11.80 lakh crore by Centre.
#### 10.2 One Nation One Ration Card
- Runs on **IM-PDS** (Integrated Management of PDS, **2018**) – nationwide portability and de-duplication.
- Started **Aug 2019** in 4 States (AP-Telangana, Gujarat-Maharashtra); **Assam** became the **36th** and last State/UT (**June 2022**).
- **Mera Ration** app (DFPD with NIC) for migrants.
#### 10.3 Bharat brand
- **Bharat Rice** (Feb 2024) **₹29/kg**; Bharat Atta and dal – via **NAFED, NCCF, Kendriya Bhandar** outlets and mobile vans to curb inflation.
### 11. ANDHRA PRADESH AND HUNGER INDICES
#### 11.1 AP PDS (2024-25)
- **29,796** Fair Price Shops; 1.48 crore cards.
- AAY 35 kg/card free; PHH 5 kg/unit free; **ragi** (all) and **jowar** (Rayalaseema) up to 3 kg/card free instead of rice; wheat 1 kg at ₹16.
- **Mobile Dispensing Units** (doorstep delivery, from 2021) ended **1 June 2025**; fair price shops restored; doorstep kept for 65+ and disabled.
- **Mee Mart** (Jul 2026): AP Civil Supplies + **NCCF**; first at **Lalupuram near Guntur**; to link with **Digital Rupee (CBDC)**.
#### 11.2 GHI
- **GHI 2025**: India **102nd of 123**, score **25.8** – "serious" (2024: 105th).
- India's objections: **three of four indicators** concern children only; undernourishment from a **small opinion-poll-type sample** (not "only income indicators").
