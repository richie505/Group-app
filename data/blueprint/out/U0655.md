## U0655 | RACIAL GROUPS OF INDIA – RISLEY AND GUHA CLASSIFICATIONS
### 1. BASIS OF RACIAL CLASSIFICATION – TRAITS AND INDICES
- Criteria: skin, eye, hair; **cephalic**, **nasal**, stature indices; blood groups.
- **Cephalic index** = maximum breadth ÷ maximum length × 100.
| Index | Value | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Cephalic | 82+ / 77–81.99 / 76.99 and below | **Brachy** (round) / Meso / **Dolicho** (long) |
| Nasal | 85+ / 70–84.9 / below 70 | **Platyrrhine** (broad) / Mesorrhine / Leptorrhine |
| Stature (Topinard) | 170 cm+ / under 160 cm | Tall / small |
| Hair | **leio / cymo / ulotrichi** | Straight (Mongoloid) / wavy (Caucasoid) / woolly (Negroid) |
### 2. PHYSICAL TRAITS → RACIAL TYPE
| Type | Diagnostic traits |
|---|---|
| **Mongoloid** | Yellowish complexion, **oblique eyes** (epicanthic fold), **high cheek bones**, sparse straight black hair, medium height, broad head, flat face |
| **Negrito** | Dark skin, woolly/frizzy hair, short stature, broad flat nose |
| **Proto-Australoid** | Dark skin, wavy hair, long head, **depressed nasal root**, broad flat nose |
| Dravidian (Risley) | Long head, broad nose, dark skin |
| Nordic | Tall, fair, long-headed, light eyes |
| Caucasoid / Negroid | High bridge, narrow nose / low bridge, broad nose |
- World: **Mongoloid** – China, Japan, Korea, Mongolia, **Inuit**, **Native Americans**; Caucasoid – Europe, N Africa, Middle East, many North Indians; Negroid – Africa, **Pygmies** (Congo); Australoid – Aborigines, Melanesians.
### 3. RISLEY CLASSIFICATION – 7 TYPES (1901 CENSUS)
- **H.H. Risley**, Census Commissioner, **1901 Census**, organised its ethnographic survey; book **The People of India (1908)**.
- Main basis: **nasal index**; dictum: "social status of a caste varies inversely with its nasal index" – **racial theory of caste**, now rejected.
| Type | Region |
|---|---|
| Turko-Iranian | Baluchistan, NWFP |
| Indo-Aryan | Punjab, Rajasthan, Kashmir |
| Scytho-Dravidian | Western India – Maharashtra, Saurashtra, Coorg |
| **Aryo-Dravidian** | Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, parts of Rajasthan |
| **Mongolo-Dravidian** | Lower Bengal, Odisha |
| Mongoloid | Himalaya, Assam, north-east |
| **Dravidian** | South India, Chota Nagpur, central India (Paniyan, Santals) |
!TRAP "Dravidian" properly names a **language family**; India's two main ethno-linguistic groups = Indo-Aryan + Dravidian.
### 4. B.S. GUHA CLASSIFICATION – 6 GROUPS (1931 CENSUS)
- Based on **1931 Census** data: **6 main groups, 9 sub-types**; most widely cited.
| Group | Region / peoples |
|---|---|
| **Negrito** | Andaman – Onge, Jarawa, Great Andamanese, Sentinelese; traces Kadar, Irula |
| **Proto-Australoid** | Central and south tribes – **Santhal**, Munda, Oraon, Ho, Bhil, **Chenchu**, Yeruva |
| **Mongoloid** | Palaeo-Mongoloid (Sema Nagas, Lepchas) + Tibeto-Mongoloid (Tibetans of Sikkim); Shompen |
| **Mediterranean** | Palaeo-Mediterranean (Dravidian south) + Mediterranean (Punjab, Ganga plain) + **Oriental** (Punjab, Rajasthan) |
| **Western Brachycephals** | Alpinoid, Dinaric, **Armenoid (Parsis)** |
| **Nordic** | North-West Frontier, Punjab, Rajasthan |
!KEY Only Guha lists Negrito and Proto-Australoid separately.
### 5. TRIBAL GROUPS AND AP ANGLE
- Andaman tribes = **Negroid (Negrito)**, a sub-race of Negroid; Nicobar **Shompen = Mongoloid**.
- **North-east** = Mongoloid (hilly, forested); **central India** = Proto-Australoid (Santhal).
- **AP**: **Chenchus (Nallamala) = Proto-Australoid**; AP plains = mainly **Mediterranean**, Dravidian-speaking.

### 7. ANTHROPOLOGICAL SURVEY OF INDIA AND CRITICISM
- **AnSI**: founded **1945 at Varanasi**, moved to **Kolkata (HQ) 1948**; first Director **B.S. Guha**.
- **People of India project (1985)**: **K.S. Singh**, about **4,635 communities**.
- AnSI classified **268** DNT communities for NITI Aayog.
- Humans share **over 99%** of genes – one species, Homo sapiens; race is subjective.
- **2009** genetics: Indians = mix of **ANI + ASI**; Rakhigarhi DNA – no **Steppe Pastoralist** gene.
- India ratified **ICERD in 1968**; UN CERD reviewed India first since **2007**; India says caste is not race.
