## U0500 | AGRICULTURAL MARKETING – APMC, E-NAM, CONTRACT FARMING, MODEL ACTS, FPOS, WAREHOUSING
### 1. APMC SYSTEM
- Agricultural markets = **State List**; **APMC Acts are enacted by States**.
- AP law: **AP (Agricultural Produce and Livestock) Markets Act, 1966** (Act 16 of 1966) – still the operative framework.
- Set-up order: Government **notifies the area** → **Market Committee** formed → **market yard** set up → run by the committee.
- Regulated markets: **open auction, standard weights, recorded prices, licensed buyers**; ~**2,477** regulated markets + 4,843 sub-yards.
- Market fee **1%**; AP AMCs collected ₹429.77 crore in 2025-26 (to Dec 2025).
### 2. MARKETING PROBLEMS AND PRICE SPREAD
| Problem | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Intermediaries** | Raise consumer prices (onion Rs 62/kg in Delhi blamed on hoarding and middlemen) |
| **APMC charges** | Market and licence fees, commission charges, **cartels** of licensed traders, too few mandis |
| **Poor storage** | ~40% of storage idle → **distress sale** |
| **Market density** | **Swaminathan (NCF)**: a regulated market within about **5 km** |
- **Price spread** = consumer price minus farmer's price (marketing margins and middlemen's share).
- **Farmer's share in consumer rupee** (RBI studies, Oct 2024): tomato ~**33%**, onion ~36%, potato ~37%; pulses higher – gram ~75%, moong ~70%, tur ~65%. Perishables give farmers a lower share.
### 3. GRADING AND STANDARDISATION – AGMARK
- **AGMARK** = quality mark under the **Agricultural Produce (Grading and Marking) Act, 1937** (trap: not "Grading and Marketing").
- Certifier: **Directorate of Marketing and Inspection (DMI)**, Ministry of Agriculture – **not FCI**.
| Laboratory | Place |
|---|---|
| **Central Agmark Laboratory** | **Nagpur** (APPSC 2018) |
| **Central Quality Control Laboratory** | **Nagpur** (APPSC 2025) |
- **AGMARKNET** (2000, NIC) price portal → Agmarknet 2.0 (2025). **One Nation, One Regulator**: end double certification under BIS and AGMARK.
### 4. MODEL ACTS AND CONTRACT FARMING
| Model Act | Key feature |
|---|---|
| **Model APMC Act, 2003** | Direct sale to contract sponsors; private markets; farmer-consumer markets |
| **Model APLM Act, 2017** | **Whole State one market area**; warehouses as yards; single-point fee, unified licence, e-trading |
| **Model Contract Farming Act, 2018** | Contract farming **outside APMC**; farmer the **weaker party** |
- 2018 Act: sponsor buys the **entire pre-agreed quantity**; **no right, title or interest in land** to sponsor; no permanent structure; registration by **RARC**; **CFFGs** at village/panchayat level; covers livestock and services.
- **Contract farming**: price, quantity and/or time agreed in advance; no land transfer. Pioneer: **Punjab (PepsiCo, late 1980s)**.
- **AP experience**: the AP Markets (Amendment) Act, **2005** added contract farming (Section 11A); sponsors register with the Registering Authority.
- **e-Choupal** = **ITC** internet kiosks (2000). **Small Farmer Large Field** = neighbouring small farmers synchronise operations to work as one large field (UPSC 2023).
### 5. FARM LAWS 2020
| Act (Sept 2020) | Core provision |
|---|---|
| **Farmers' Produce Trade and Commerce Act** | Trade in a "trade area" outside APMC yards, no State fee; **did not mention MSP** |
| **Farmers (Empowerment and Protection) Agreement on Price Assurance and Farm Services Act** | **Contract farming** |
| **Essential Commodities (Amendment) Act** | Removed stock limits on cereals, pulses, oilseeds, edible oils, onion, potato (except extraordinary situations) |
- Passed under **Concurrent List Entry 33**, though agriculture = **State List Entry 14**. **Repealed November 2021**.
### 6. E-NAM
- Launched **April 2016**; "**One Nation, One Market**"; virtual market linking physical APMC mandis (does not replace them).
- Lead agency: **SFAC**; Central Sector, via **Agri-Tech Infrastructure Fund**, now **ISAM-eNAM**; up to **₹75 lakh per mandi**.
- **Reforms required of States** (APPSC 2016): **e-trading**, **single State-wide licence**, **single-point levy**; NOT State price regulation.
- Advantages: nationwide access, transparent price discovery, **direct payment** to farmer's account; warehouse trading with **e-NWRs**.
- Coverage (Mar 2026): **1,656 mandis**, 23 States + 4 UTs; 1.80 crore farmers. Most trade is **intra-mandi**.
- **AP**: **33 markets** (dry chillies, cotton, turmeric, groundnut); only **11 mandis** in inter-mandi trade; online payments ₹26.88 lakh (2024-25).
### 7. FARMERS' DIRECT MARKETS AND RURAL HAATS
| Market | State |
|---|---|
| **Rythu Bazar (1999)** | **Andhra Pradesh** |
| **Uzhavar Sandhai** | **Tamil Nadu** (APPSC 2024) |
| **Apni Mandi** | **Punjab** |
| **Hadapsar Mandi** | **Maharashtra (Pune)** |
- AP Rythu Bazars: no commission agents; run by an **Estate Officer under the Revenue Department**, outside Market Committees; **129** functional (2025-26).
- **GrAMs**: Budget **2018-19**; upgrade **22,000** rural haats (not 10,000); ₹2,000 crore Agri-Market Infrastructure Fund; exempt from APMC, linked to e-NAM.
- **Krishi UDAN**: air transport of perishables (APPSC 2021). **Vishesh Krishi Upaj Yojana**: export push via transport costs.
### 8. FPOS AND COOPERATIVE MARKETING
- **10,000 FPO scheme (2020)**: Central Sector, **₹6,865 crore**; agencies **SFAC, NABARD, NCDC, NAFED**; one FPO per block; 15% in Aspirational Districts; **CBBO** handholding 5 years; ₹18 lakh management support over 3 years.
- **Equity grant ₹2,000 per member (max ₹15 lakh)**; **credit guarantee up to ₹2 crore**. Aim: bargaining power, scale, lower costs.
- 10,000 FPOs organised (Aug 2026). AP (2024-25): 455 FPOs; 207 on ONDC.
| Body | Fact |
|---|---|
| **NAFED** | **2 October 1958**; **apex** cooperative marketing body; PM-AASHA nodal (with NCCF) |
| **TRIFED** | 1987, MSCS Act 1984; tribal produce |
| **AP MARKFED** | ₹3,000 crore Price Stabilisation Fund |
- **PM-AASHA PSS**: pulses, oilseeds, copra at MSP.
### 9. WAREHOUSING
| Act / body | Year |
|---|---|
| **CWC** | **2 March 1957** (Corporations Act 1956 → Warehousing Corporations Act 1962) |
| **Warehousing (Development and Regulation) Act** | **2007**, in force 25 Oct 2010 |
| **WDRA** | **26 October 2010** – accredits warehouses, NWRs |
| e-Kisan Upaj Nidhi | 4 March 2024 |
- **e-NWRs** tradable on e-NAM, support pledge finance; 7% post-harvest loans up to 6 months.
- **World's Largest Grain Storage Plan (Cooperative Sector)**: Ministry of **Cooperation**, piloted **2023**; godowns at **PACS**; NCDC with NABARD, CWC, FCI; effective interest for PACS **1%**.
### 10. OPERATION GREENS AND AGRICULTURE INFRASTRUCTURE FUND
- **Operation Greens**: **Budget 2018-19** (APPSC 2018; distractors Operation Flood, Indradhanush, SAMPADA); **TOP**; "on the lines of **Operation Flood**"; Ministry of **Food Processing Industries**.
- **TOP to TOTAL**: Budget **2021-22**, **22** perishables. Subsidy: short-term **50%** transport and storage; long-term **50%** grant (**70%** for FPOs).
- **AIF (2020)**: **₹1 lakh crore**; **3%** subvention on loans up to **₹2 crore**; guarantee via **CGTMSE**; warehouses, cold stores, custom hiring centres; ₹84,681 crore sanctioned (Mar 2026).
