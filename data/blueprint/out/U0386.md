## U0386 | ETHNICITY – CONCEPT, ETHNIC IDENTITY AND ETHNIC MOVEMENTS IN INDIA
### 1. ETHNICITY – CONCEPT AND MARKERS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ethnic group** | People who identify with each other on **common language/dialect, history, society, culture or nation** |
| **Markers** | **Language, religion, race, region, culture** |
| **Intra-ethnic conflict** | Within a group |
| **Inter-ethnic conflict** | Between groups |
#### 1.1 Approaches
- **Consensus approach**: groups **coexist** with separate identities.
- **Conflict approach**: groups are **interest groups competing unequally** for power and resources (e.g. **South Africa, Sri Lankan Tamils**).
#### 1.2 Scholars on Ethnicity and Minority
| Scholar | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **T.K. Oommen** | India's foremost scholar on ethnicity; separates **nation, nationality, ethnicity and citizenship** (*Citizenship, Nationality and Ethnicity*) |
| **Arnold Rose** | Minority = group set apart by **race, nationality, religion or language**; sees itself and is seen by others as different, with negative connotations |
| **Giddens** | Minority marked by **disadvantage, group solidarity, physical/social isolation** |
### 2. VIEWS ON ETHNIC IDENTITY
| View | Core idea | Scholars |
|---|---|---|
| **Primordialist** | Identity **given by birth**; deep "primordial attachments" | **Clifford Geertz**, Edward Shils |
| **Instrumentalist** | **Elites mobilise** identity for power and resources | **Paul Brass**; **Rajni Kothari** (*Politics in India*); **Christophe Jaffrelot** (*India's Silent Revolution*) |
| **Constructivist** | Identity **socially made**, kept by **boundaries** | **Fredrik Barth** (*Ethnic Groups and Boundaries*); **Benedict Anderson** (*Imagined Communities*) |
!TRAP Barth = boundaries (constructivist); Geertz = primordial attachments; Kothari / Jaffrelot / Brass = instrumentalist.
### 3. ETHNIC GROUPS, LANGUAGES AND MINORITIES OF INDIA
#### 3.1 Linguistic Diversity
- **Census 2011**: **121 languages**, **270 mother tongues**; **22 Scheduled Languages**; **19,500+** mother tongues returned.
!NUM 121 languages | 270 mother tongues | 22 scheduled | 19,500+ returned
#### 3.2 North-East Groups
| Group | State | Marker |
|---|---|---|
| **Nyishi** | Arunachal Pradesh | Largest tribe there; **Pakke Paga Hornbill Festival** |
| **Tangkhul Naga** | Manipur | **Longpi pottery** |
| **Khasi, Garo** | Meghalaya | Official languages with English (2026); **not in the Eighth Schedule** |
!TRAP Khasi = **Austro-Asiatic (Mon-Khmer)**; Garo = **Sino-Tibetan (Bodo group)**.
#### 3.3 Notified Minorities (NCM Act, 1992)
- **Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Parsis**, and **Jains** (added **2014**).
### 4. ETHNIC MINORITIES AND NATIONAL MINORITY
- **National minority**: a **non-dominant citizen group** with its own **ethnic, religious or linguistic** identity; the term comes from **European minority-rights law**.
- **Ethnic Muslim minority groups** abroad:
  - **Uyghurs** and **Hui** (China) – China has **55** recognised minority groups; the **Hui** are China's largest Muslim group.
  - **Rohingya** – Sunni Muslim minority of **Rakhine State, Myanmar**; denied citizenship, effectively **stateless**.
- **China's law on "ethnic unity and progress" (Mar 2026)**: covers **55 minority groups** (Uyghurs, Hui, Tibetans); critics say it erodes non-Han identity; **Han over 91%**.
- **Sri Lanka (Jul 2026)**: Tamil and Muslim parties formed a **common platform**.
### 5. ETHNIC MOVEMENTS AND OUTFITS
| Movement | Region | Outfit / leader | Demand | State response |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Naga** | Nagaland, Naga areas of Manipur | **NNC (A.Z. Phizo)**; **NSCN** | Independence / **"Greater Nagalim"** | **State of Nagaland Act 1962**; state inaugurated **1 Dec 1963**; talks; AFSPA |
| **Mizo** | Mizoram | **MNF (Laldenga)** | Independence | **Mizo Accord 1986**; statehood **1987** |
| **Bodo** | North bank of the Brahmaputra, Assam | **ABSU (1967)**, **NDFB** | Udayachal (1960s), then **Bodoland** state (1987) | Bodo accords |
| **Khalistan** | Punjab | **Jarnail Singh Bhindranwale** | Separate Sikh state | **Operation Blue Star (1984)**, Golden Temple |
| **ULFA** | Assam | ULFA, founded **7 April 1979**; banned **1990** | **Sovereign Assam** for ethnic Assamese by armed struggle | **Ops Bajrang (1990)**, **Rhino (1991)**; **All Clear (2003, in Bhutan)** |
| **Gorkhaland** | Darjeeling hills, West Bengal | **GNLF (Subhash Ghising)** | Separate state | **DGHC 1988**; **Gorkhaland Territorial Administration 2012** |
!TRAP Blue Star = Punjab (Khalistan); Bajrang / Rhino / All Clear = ULFA (Assam).
### 6. ETHNIC CONFLICTS
| Conflict | Parties / issue |
|---|---|
| **Manipur** (since **May 2023**) | **Meitei vs Kuki**: Meitei demand for **ST status**; Kuki demand for **separate administration** |
| **Assam Movement (AASU)** | **Assamese vs Bengali**, over illegal migration; ABSU revived Bodoland demand after the Assam Accord (1985) |
| **Mizoram** | **Mizo vs Bru** |
| **Tripura** | **Indigenous tribes vs Bengali settlers**, after demographic change |
- **Apr 2026**: Manipur shutdowns hit **12 of 16 districts** after the **Tronglaobi (Bishnupur)** bomb attack (7 Apr) and the killing of two Tangkhul Nagas in **Ukhrul**.
- **Jun 2026**: Centre gave **₹10 crore** of rice for ~**40,000** refugees and displaced people in Mizoram.
### 7. ACCORDS
| Accord | Year | With | Key outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Assam Accord** | **1985** | AASU/AAGSP | **24 March 1971** cut-off for detecting foreigners |
| **Mizo Accord** | **1986** | MNF | Ended insurgency; statehood **1987** |

### 8. LINGUISTIC IDENTITY AND REORGANISATION
- **Andhra State (1953)** = **first linguistic state**, after **Potti Sriramulu's** fast.
- **SRC (1953)**, headed by **Fazl Ali** → **States Reorganisation Act, 1956** → **Andhra Pradesh formed 1 Nov 1956**.
| Year | Reorganisation |
|---|---|
| 1960 | Bombay split into **Maharashtra and Gujarat** |
| 1963 | **Nagaland** |
| 1966 | **Punjab/Haryana**; Chandigarh a UT |
| 1987 | **Goa, Arunachal Pradesh, Mizoram** become states |
| 2014 | **Telangana** |
| 2019 | **J&K and Ladakh** UTs |
!TRAP SRC report 1953 → Act 1956; Andhra State 1953 vs Andhra Pradesh 1956.
### 9. STATE RESPONSE – SIXTH SCHEDULE, ARTICLE 371, STATEHOOD
- **Sixth Schedule (Art. 244(2))**: autonomous district councils in **Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram**; **Tripura Tribal Areas ADC** gives local self-rule.
| Article | State |
|---|---|
| **371A** | Nagaland |
| **371B** | Assam |
| **371C** | Manipur |
| **371D** | **Andhra Pradesh** (public employment and education) |
| **371F** | Sikkim |
| **371G** | Mizoram |
| **371H** | Arunachal Pradesh |
- Other responses: **statehood**, **Inner Line Permit (1873)**, **AFSPA**, peace accords, **MDoNER**.
- Debate: regionalism aids democratisation (Tripura ADC) but can feed secessionism (Greater Nagalim) and vote-bank politics.
