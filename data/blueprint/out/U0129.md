## U0129 | HUMAYUN AND SHER SHAH SURI – SUR ADMINISTRATION, LAND REVENUE, SARAIS, CURRENCY, GRAND TRUNK ROAD
### 1. BABUR – THE BACKGROUND
- Babur came from **Farghana** (Central Asia); Timurid on father's side, Chingizid on mother's side.
- Invited by **Daulat Khan Lodi** (Governor of Punjab), **Alam Khan** (Ibrahim Lodi's uncle) and **Rana Sanga** (Mewar).
- Memoir: **Tuzuk-i-Baburi (Baburnama)** in **Chaghatai Turkish**; **Persian** was the Mughal court language. It praises **Krishnadevaraya**.
- After **Panipat I (1526)**, Babur took **Delhi and Agra** (APPSC 2023).
- Panipat I = first large-scale use of **gunpowder artillery** in Indian warfare.
#### 1.1 Babur's Tactics and Buildings
| Term / work | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Tulughma** | Flanks wheel round to encircle the enemy – **Uzbek** origin |
| **Rumi (Ottoman) method / araba** | Carts linked into a barrier sheltering matchlockmen and artillery – **Ottoman** origin |
| **Kabuli Bagh Mosque** | **Panipat**, marks the 1526 victory |
| **Ram Bagh** | **Agra**, first Mughal garden |
- **Babur** is the Mughal emperor with **no mausoleum in India** – buried in **Kabul**.
### 2. HUMAYUN – ACCESSION AND PROBLEMS
- Accession: **29 December 1530**, three days after Babur's death.
- Problems: rival brothers **Kamran, Askari, Hindal**; the **Afghans**; **Bahadur Shah** of Gujarat-Malwa.
- **Rao Jaitsi of Bikaner** defeated Babur's son **Kamran Mirza** (1534), whom Humayun had sent against him (Rao-Jaitsi-Ro-Chhanda).
#### 2.1 Humayun's Campaigns in Order
| Event | Year | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Kalinjar invaded | 1531 | Humayun's early campaign |
| **Devara (Daurah / Dourah)**, on the **Gomati** | 1532 | Humayun beat Afghans; drove **Mahmud Lodi** from **Jaunpur** |
| **Chunar Fort** | 1532 | Four-month siege; **Sher Khan accepted Humayun's supremacy** |
| Raisina fort | 1535 | Taken |
| Gujarat | 1535 | Pressed by Humayun, **Bahadur Shah let the Portuguese fortify Diu** |
| Sher Khan's rise | 1538 | Won at Surajgarh; took Gaur |
| **Chausa** | **26 June 1539** | **Sher Khan beat Humayun**; took the title "Sher Shah" |
| **Kannauj = Bilgram** | **17 May 1540** | Sher Shah beat Humayun, took the throne; Humayun into exile |
| **Sirhind** (and Machhiwara) | 1555 | Humayun beat **Sikandar Sur**; retook Delhi and Agra (July 1555) |
!KEY Order: Devara 1532 → Chausa 1539 → Kannauj/Bilgram 1540 → Sirhind 1555.
!TRAP Kannauj and Bilgram are ONE battle (1540), a Sher Khan victory. "Battle of Samugarh" was **1658** (Aurangzeb vs Dara Shikoh), not a Humayun battle.
#### 2.2 Exile, Return and Death
- Married **Hamida Banu Begum** (Akbar's mother); **Akbar born at Amarkot (1542)**.
- Went to **Persia**; the **Safavid Shah Tahmasp** helped him retake **Kandahar and Kabul**.
- Returned **July 1555**, using quarrels among Sher Shah's successors.
- Died **January 1556** – fell from the stairs of the **Sher Mandal** library, **Purana Qila (Dinpanah)**, Delhi.
- **Akbar** was crowned at **Kalanaur** after Humayun's death.
- **First ruler after the Delhi Sultanate to issue a gold coin – Humayun**; Sher Shah also issued one later.
### 3. SHER SHAH – RISE, TITLES, CONQUESTS AND DEATH
#### 3.1 Rise
- Born **Farid (Farid Khan)**, son of an Afghan jagirdar of **Sasaram**; of the **Sur** tribe.
- Left home in **1494**; studied at **Jaunpur ("Siraj-i-Hind")**; managed his father's jagir from **1511**.
- Got the title **"Sher Khan"** from **Bahar Khan** of Bihar for killing a **tiger**.
- Served Babur; became deputy regent (de facto ruler) of **Bihar**.
#### 3.2 Titles in Order
| Title | When |
|---|---|
| **Sher Khan** | From Bahar Khan (tiger) |
| **Hazrat-e-Ala** | After defeating **Nusrat Shah** of Bengal |
| **Sher Shah** (Sultan) | After **Chausa, 26 June 1539**; khutba read and coins struck in his name |
#### 3.3 Conquests and Rajput Resistance
- **Raisen** fort taken from **Puran Mal**.
- **Marwar**: **Maldeo Rathore** resisted; battle of **Samel (Sammel / Giri-Sumel), 1544**.
- Maldeo's generals **Jaita and Kumpa** fought so bravely that Sher Shah said: "I would have lost the empire of Hindustan **for a handful of bajra (millet)**."
#### 3.4 Death
- Died **22 May 1545**, wounded by a **gunpowder explosion** at the siege of **Kalinjar** (a Rajput fort) – his last campaign.
- Buried at **Sasaram**. Reign of only **five years** (1540-45).
- His skills as a ruler are more striking than his battle wins.
!KEY Sequence: Chunar submission 1532 → Chausa 1539 → Qila-i-Kuhna Mosque 1541 → death at Kalinjar 1545.
!TRAP "Sher Khan" came from Bahar Khan (tiger); "Sher Shah" was assumed after Chausa – not after Kannauj.
### 4. SUR ADMINISTRATION
#### 4.1 Officers
| Level | Officer | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Sarkar | **Chief Shiqdar** (shiqdar-i-shiqdaran) | **Law and order** |
| Sarkar | **Chief Munsif** (munsif-i-munsifan) | **Revenue / civil** |
| Pargana | **Shiqdar** | Law and order |
| Pargana | **Amin** (munsif) | **Assessment of revenue** |
| Pargana | **Fotedar** | **Treasurer** |
| Pargana | **Karkuns** | Hindi and Persian writers |
| Village | Qanungo, patwari | Revenue / local records |
- Officers were **frequently transferred** so they did not build local interests.
#### 4.2 Army
- **Dagh** = branding of horses; **chehra / huliya** = descriptive roll of soldiers – both against fraud.
- **Islam Shah** divided troops into units of **200, 250 and 500**.
#### 4.3 Police, Justice and Dak
- **Local headmen** had to trace crime; **censors of public morals**; efficient **spy network**.
- Justice was uniform and harsh.
- Sarais served as **dak-chowkis** (post and news relay).
!TRAP Shiqdar = law and order; Munsif / Amin = revenue. Do not swap them.
### 5. LAND REVENUE SYSTEM
#### 5.1 Measurement and Assessment
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Measurement | **Uniform** system of survey |
| Yard | **Gaz-i-Sikandari** (sikandari gaz); rope / **jarib** |
| Classes | **Three** – good, middling, bad (by produce) |
| State share | **One-third** of average produce |
| Payment | Cash or grain |
| Settlement | Direct state-peasant; illegal demands removed; tenant rights recognised |
| Legacy | Model for **Todar Mal's Bandobast** |
#### 5.2 Documents and Fees
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Patta** | Title deed stating the state's share; **given to the cultivator** |
| **Qabuliyat** | Deed of acceptance **signed by the peasant** |
| **Jaribana** | **Surveyor's** fee |
| **Muhasilana** | **Collector's** fee |
- Patta and qabuliyat were introduced by **Sher Shah**.
- Some call Sher Shah the first to introduce a ryotwari-type system.
#### 5.3 Sher Shah vs Akbar
| Point | Sher Shah | Akbar – Todar Mal |
|---|---|---|
| Share | 1/3 | 1/3 |
| Basis | Average produce | **Ten-year averages** |
| Land classes | **Three** | **Four** – polaj, parauti, chachar, banjar |
!TRAP APPSC G1 2024: three categories – TRUE; uniform measurement – TRUE; "one-fourth" share – FALSE.
### 6. CURRENCY, ROADS AND SARAIS
#### 6.1 Currency
| Coin | Metal | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Rupiya** | **Silver** | About **178 grains**; introduced 1540-45 (APPSC 2019); standard for centuries (APPSC G2 2017) |
| **Dam** | **Copper** | Retained by Akbar |
| **Mohur** | **Gold** | – |
- Minted pure gold and silver coins; the reform outlived the Sur dynasty.
#### 6.2 Grand Trunk Road
- **Sadak-e-Azam** (Grand Trunk Road) – **built / restored** by Sher Shah (APPSC 2018: he restored it).
- Route: **Sonargaon (Bengal) to the Indus** (APPSC G1 2022: "Indus to Sonargaon"); also given as Sonargaon to Kabul / East Bengal to Peshawar.
#### 6.3 Sarais
- Built along roads; served as **dak-chowkis** (news and mail relay, with horses).
- **Market towns** grew around them.
### 7. SUR SUCCESSORS, HEMU AND THE END OF AFGHAN RULE
| Ruler | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Islam Shah** (Jalal Khan) | Sher Shah's **second son**; ruled **1545-53**; troop units of 200/250/500 |
| **Muhammad Adil Shah** | Ruled the east; rival of Sikandar Sur |
| **Sikandar Sur** | Beaten by Humayun at Sirhind (1555) |
| **Hemu** (Hem Chandra **Vikramaditya**) | Adil Shah's Hindu **army chief and Prime Minister**; seized Agra and Delhi (1556) |
- **Panipat II – 5 November 1556**: Akbar (Bairam Khan) vs Hemu; Hemu struck in the **eye by an arrow** and captured; Afghan power ended.
- **Surs = last Afghan dynasty** to rule Delhi; their revenue, coinage and roads set the Mughal pattern.
!TRAP Panipat II was fought against Hemu, not Sher Shah.
### 8. MONUMENTS OF THE PERIOD
| Monument | Builder | Location / note |
|---|---|---|
| **Sher Shah's Tomb** | Sher Shah, for himself; completed by **Islam Shah** | **Sasaram, Rohtas, Bihar**; **octagonal**, set in a **tank**; brick, partly stone-veneered; fine Afghan style |
| **Purana Qila** (Old Fort, Dinpanah) | Begun by **Humayun** (citadel); present form by **Sher Shah** | Delhi |
| **Qila-i-Kuhna Mosque** | **Sher Shah, 1541** | Inside Purana Qila |
| **Sher Mandal** | Sher Shah | Inside Purana Qila; Humayun's library |
| **Rohtas Fort** | **Sher Shah**, begun **1541** under **Todar Mal** | Near **Jhelum, Pakistan**; angled walls and bastions; UNESCO 1997 |
| **Humayun's Tomb** | **Bega Begum** (Haji Begum), Humayun's first wife; architect **Mirak Mirza Ghiyas** | Delhi; built **1565-72**; UNESCO 1993 |
| Kabuli Bagh Mosque | Babur | Panipat |
- Built by Sher Shah: Sasaram Tomb, Qila-i-Kuhna Mosque – NOT the Tomb of Sikandar Lodi or a Jama Masjid.
- **Humayun-nama** – by **Gulbadan Begum**, Humayun's **sister**.

### 9. MUGHAL–AFGHAN BATTLES 1526-1576
| Year | Battle | Sides | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1526 (21 Apr) | **Panipat I** | Babur vs Ibrahim Lodi | Babur won; Mughal Empire founded |
| 1527 | **Khanwa** | Babur vs Rana Sanga | Babur won; Rajput power broken |
| 1528 | **Chanderi** | Babur vs **Medini Rai** | Babur won |
| 1529 | **Ghaghra** | Babur vs Afghans under **Mahmud Lodi** (backed by Nusrat Shah of Bengal) | **Babur's last battle** |
| 1539 | **Chausa** | Humayun vs Sher Khan | Sher Khan won |
| 1540 | **Kannauj / Bilgram** | Humayun vs Sher Khan | Sher Khan won |
| 1555 | **Sirhind** | Humayun vs Sikandar Sur | Humayun won |
| 1556 | **Panipat II** | Akbar (Bairam Khan) vs Hemu | Mughals won |
| 1565 | Talikota | Vijayanagara vs Deccan Sultanates | (not Mughal) |
| 1576 (18 June) | **Haldighati** | Akbar's general **Man Singh** vs **Maharana Pratap** | Mughals held the field; Pratap escaped |
| 1658 | Samugarh | Aurangzeb vs Dara Shikoh | Aurangzeb won |
| 1761 | Panipat III | Marathas vs **Ahmad Shah Abdali** | Abdali won |
- Babur's order: **Panipat I → Khanwa → Chanderi → Ghaghra** (UKPSC 2024).
- Rulers in order: Raziyya (1236-40) → Alauddin Khalji (1296-1316) → **Sher Shah (1540-45)** → Akbar (1556-1605).
!TRAP "Jahangir – Chausa" is a WRONG pair (APPSC G2 2025); Chausa was Humayun's. "Tipu vs Marathas" is not Panipat II.
- AP angle: **Sultan Quli Qutb Shah** of Golconda (**1518-1543**) was a contemporary of Babur, Humayun and Sher Shah; the Mughals did not interfere in the Deccan then.

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- Rupiya began with **Sher Shah**.
- Surs = **last Afghan dynasty** to rule Delhi.
- First gold coin after the Sultanate: **Humayun**.
- First true arch in India: **Balban's Tomb**.
- Babur's last battle: **Ghaghra (1529)**; Sher Shah's last campaign: **Kalinjar (1545)**.
- Mughal emperor with no mausoleum in India: **Babur (Kabul)**.

### 12. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. Sher Shah's share = **one-third**, not one-fourth.
2. **Patta** given by the state; **qabuliyat** signed by the peasant.
3. **Three** land classes (Sher Shah) vs **four** (Akbar).
4. **Chausa (1539)** gave the title Sher Shah; **Kannauj = Bilgram (1540)** gave the throne.
5. Chausa was Humayun's battle, not Jahangir's.
6. **Humayun-nama** = Gulbadan Begum; **Humayun's Tomb** = Bega Begum.
7. Sher Shah's tomb is at **Sasaram (Bihar)**, octagonal, in a tank – completed by Islam Shah.
8. Purana Qila: begun by Humayun, present form by Sher Shah.
9. Hemu (Vikramaditya) lost **Panipat II (1556)** to Akbar's Bairam Khan.
10. Jaita and Kumpa were **Maldeo's** generals at **Samel (1544)** – "handful of bajra".
