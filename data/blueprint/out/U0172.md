### 1. THE OFFICE – TITLE CHANGES AND FIRSTS
- **Order:** Governor of Bengal → **GG of Bengal (1773)** → **GG of India (1833)** → **Viceroy (1858)**.
| Title | First holder | Created by |
|---|---|---|

| **Viceroy and GG** | **Lord Canning** | **Government of India Act, 1858** |

#### 4.1 Bentinck – statement checks
- **Correct (APPSC-G1 2024):** Mahalwari revision (Regulation IX of 1833; introduced 1822 under Hastings), Sati, infanticide.
- **Key social reforms:** abolition of **Sati**, abolition of **slavery**, suppression of **Thugs** – not "removal of disabilities from change of religion".
- **Thugs** (Hindu and Muslim) worshipped **Kali, Durga, Bhavani**.
!TRAP "Established universities for the first time" is **wrong** for Bentinck – the first universities came in **1857 under Canning**.

### 6. IMPEACHMENT OF WARREN HASTINGS
- **Impeached in 1787** by **Edmund Burke** and the **Whigs** in the British Parliament for unjust, arbitrary acts.
- **Charges:**
1. **Rohilla War (1774)**.
2. **Chait Singh** of Benares.
3. Plunder of the **Begums of Awadh**.
4. **Judicial murder of Nand Kumar (1775)**.
- **Outcome:** trial **1788–1795** → **acquittal**; all charges dropped.
