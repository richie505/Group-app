## U0037 | ASHOKAN INSCRIPTIONS – ROCK EDICTS, PILLAR EDICTS, SITES, SCRIPTS, JAMES PRINSEP
### 1. DECIPHERMENT, FIRSTS AND TITLES
#### 1.1 Decipherment
| FIRST | Fact |
|---|---|
| Decipherer of Brahmi | **James Prinsep**, **1837** (also spelt "Princep"); first Briton to study the edicts; also read Kharosthi |
| First Ashokan edicts read | **Delhi-Topra pillar** edicts |
| FIRST ruler to inscribe messages to subjects and officials on natural rocks and polished pillars | **Ashoka** (APPSC-GS 2018) |
| Edict that proved "Devanampiya Piyadasi" = Ashoka | **Maski** Minor Rock Edict, found **1915** |
- Edict form (third person to first, the word "lipi/dipi") follows the Persian inscriptions of **Darius**.
#### 1.2 Titles and Epithets
- Usual title: **Devanampiya Piyadasi** – Devanampiya = "**Beloved of the Gods**"; Piyadasi (Priyadarshi) = "of gracious / pleasing appearance".
- **Buddhashakya** – Maski; **Dharmasoka** – Sarnath inscription; **Ashok Vardhan** – Puranas.
- **Bhabru (Bairat)**: calls himself "**Piyadasi Raja**" of **Magadha**; faith in Buddha, Dhamma, Sangha.
!PAIR Other titles (distractors): Param Saugat | Dharmapala || Sikandar-i-Sani | Alauddin Khalji
#### 1.3 Edicts That Name "Ashoka"
| Edict | Location | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Maski** | Raichur, Karnataka | "Buddhashakya Ashoka"; found 1915 |
| **Gujarra** | Datia district, Madhya Pradesh | On the Ujjain–Bharuch road |
| Nittur, Udegolam | Karnataka | Also carry the name |
| **Kanaganahalli** | Near Sannati, Kalaburagi, Karnataka, on the **Bhima** | Only **inscribed portrait** of Ashoka, with his queen; label "**Ranyo Ashoka**" (also read "Raya Asoko") = King Ashoka |
!TRAP APPSC-G1 2022: the name "Ashoka" occurs in the **Maski and Gujarra** edicts; everywhere else he is Devanampiya Piyadasi.
### 2. SCRIPTS AND LANGUAGES → REGION
| Script | Language | Where used |
|---|---|---|
| **Brahmi** (left to right) | Prakrit | Most edicts – Ganga plain, Deccan, Andhra (Erragudi, Rajula Mandagiri), Girnar (APPSC-GS 2024) |
| **Kharosthi** (right to left) | Prakrit | North-west: **Shahbazgarhi**, **Mansehra** (Pakistan); Gandhara |
| **Greek** | Greek | Afghanistan – Kandahar |
| **Aramaic** | Aramaic | **Taxila**, **Laghman**, Kandahar |
| Greek + Aramaic (bilingual) | – | **Kandahar (Shar-i-Kuna)** |
- Four scripts: **Brahmi, Kharosthi, Greek, Aramaic** (APPSC-G1 2022: statement true). Main language: **Prakrit**.
- Kharosthi is derived from **Aramaic** (Achaemenid Persian).
- Brahmi is the source of the **Telugu** script (but was not used only for Telugu).
- **Satavahana** inscriptions also use Prakrit in Brahmi.
- Greek and Aramaic versions carry Buddhist ideas but **do not name the Buddha**.
- No edict names the **Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path or Nibbana**; edicts never mention varna or sati.
!TRAP Brahmi = left to right; Kharosthi = right to left.
### 3. CLASSES OF INSCRIPTIONS AND MAJOR ROCK EDICT SITES
- Three classes: **rock, pillar and cave** (Barabar) inscriptions; **14 Major Rock Edicts**.
- Find-spots: **India, Nepal, Pakistan, Afghanistan** (some lists add Bangladesh). The spread of edict sites is the **main evidence for the extent** of the empire.
#### 3.1 Site → Present State
| Site | Type | Present location |
|---|---|---|
| **Girnar** | 14 Major Rock Edicts; same rock later bears Rudradaman's inscription | Junagadh, **Gujarat** |
| **Kalsi** | 14 Major Rock Edicts | Dehradun, **Uttarakhand** |
| **Shahbazgarhi** | Major Rock Edicts in Kharosthi | Mardan, **Pakistan** |
| **Mansehra** | Major Rock Edicts in Kharosthi | Hazara, **Pakistan** |
| **Dhauli** | Major RE + **Separate Kalinga Edicts** | Near Bhubaneswar, **Odisha** |
| **Jaugada** | Major RE + **Separate Kalinga Edicts** | Ganjam, **Odisha** |
| **Sopara** | Major Rock Edicts | Palghar/Thane, **Maharashtra** |
| **Erragudi (Yerragudi)** | Major RE + Minor RE | Now **Nandyal** district (earlier Kurnool), **AP** |
| **Kandahar** | Major RE (Greek) | **Afghanistan** |
| Rajula Mandagiri | Minor RE | Kurnool, AP |
| Maski | Minor RE naming Ashoka | Raichur, Karnataka |
| Rummindei | **Pillar** inscription | Lumbini, **Nepal** |
- Western limit sites: Junagadh, Mansehra, Kalsi, Sopara. Kashmir is linked through Kalhana's **Rajatarangini** (son **Jalauka**).
!KEY Exam angle: a Major Rock Edict is found at **Girnar**; Barabar = cave inscriptions; Allahabad and Delhi-Topra = pillars; Rummindei = pillar, not a Major Rock Edict site.
!TRAP Besnagar (Vidisha) has the **Heliodorus (Garuda) Pillar** – Bhagavata religion, not Ashokan. Sanchi = Ashokan stupas.
### 4. MAJOR ROCK EDICT NUMBER → SUBJECT
| RE | Subject |
|---|---|
| **I** | Ban on **animal sacrifice** and festive gatherings (**samaja**); killing in royal kitchen reduced |
| **II** | **Medical care** for men and animals; herbs and trees planted; names **Cholas, Pandyas, Satiyaputtas, Keralaputtas, Tamraparni** (Sri Lanka) and Yavana king **Antiyoka** (Antiochus) |
| III | **Five-yearly (quinquennial) tours** of officials (12th year) |
| **V** | **Dhamma Mahamattas** appointed, 13 years after consecration |
| VI | **Pativedakas** – king available for public business at all times |
| VIII | **Dhammayatras** replace royal pleasure tours; first to **Bodh Gaya** |
| **XII** | **Religious tolerance** – respect for all sects; restraint in speech about others' faiths |
| **XIII** | **Kalinga War**, remorse, **Dhammavijaya**, five Greek kings, **Andhras** named |
- RE XII quote: "Whosoever praises his religious sect or blames other sects…".
- Prisoners were freed on each anniversary of Ashoka's coronation (**Abhisheka**).
- Edicts naming the Sangam kingdoms: **RE II and XIII**.
!TRAP Sri Lanka (Tamraparni) was **not** in Ashoka's empire – it was a border/neighbour state like Chola and Pandya.
### 5. MINOR ROCK EDICTS – AP AND KARNATAKA
| Site | Location | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Erragudi** | Kurnool (now Nandyal), AP | **Teachers** to instil Dhamma in **students** (APPSC G2 2008, GS 2019); also 14 Major RE |
| **Rajula Mandagiri** | Kurnool, AP | **Guru-disciple** relationship; proves Mauryan rule in Andhra |
| **Maski** | Raichur, Karnataka | Name **Ashoka** ("Buddhashakya") |
| Brahmagiri, Siddapura, Jatinga-Rameshwara | Chitradurga, Karnataka | Minor RE I and II |
| Gavimath, Palkigundu | Koppal, Karnataka | Minor RE I |
| **Bhabru (Bairat)** | – | Buddha, Dhamma, Sangha; recommends Buddhist texts; proves he was Buddhist |
- AP edicts are in **Prakrit, Brahmi script**; they are the **earliest inscriptions in Andhra** and show Mauryan rule reached **Rayalaseema**.
!TRAP **Bhattiprolu** and **Dhanyakataka** are distractors – they are not Ashokan edict sites.
### 6. PILLAR EDICTS AND MOVED PILLARS
#### 6.1 Pillar → Content
| Pillar | Content |
|---|---|
| **Rummindei (Lumbini), Nepal** | Visit **20 years** after coronation; marks Buddha's birthplace; Lumbini freed from **bali**, **bhaga** cut from 1/6 to **1/8** (APPSC-G2 2023) |
| Nigali Sagar, Nepal | Kanakamuni stupa enlarged (14th year) |
| **Sarnath** | **Schism edict**; **lion capital** (national emblem) |
| **Allahabad-Kosam** | PE I–VI, Schism edict, **Queen's edict** (gifts of **Karuvaki**); later **Samudragupta's prashasti** |
| **Delhi-Topra** | **Only pillar with PE VII** – rajukas, Ajivikas, Nirgranthas (Jainas); Dhamma spreads better by **persuasion than by law** |
- **PE II** defines Dhamma: "few faults, many good deeds".
- **PE IV** (26th year): **Rajukas** get independent judicial power; **3-day respite** for condemned prisoners.
- **Schism edict**: dissenting monks and nuns expelled – at **Sarnath, Sanchi, Kaushambi**.
- Pillars cluster in the **Ganga plain** – river transport of heavy monoliths.
#### 6.2 Who Moved the Pillars
| Pillar | Moved to | By |
|---|---|---|
| Topra (Ambala) | Delhi (Firozabad) | **Firoz Shah Tughlaq** |
| Meerut | Delhi (Ridge) | **Firoz Shah Tughlaq** |
| Kosam (Kaushambi) | Allahabad fort | Traditionally **Akbar**; Jahangir added his inscription |
!TRAP Rummindei visit = 20 years after coronation; the **8th** year is the Kalinga War.
### 7. KALINGA WAR
- Date: **261 BC**, **8th regnal year**. Cause: Kalinga controlled east-coast land and sea routes.
- RE XIII: about **1,00,000 killed**, **1,50,000 captive**; many more died of famine and disease.
- Result: remorse, conversion to Buddhism, **no more wars of conquest**, **Dhammavijaya** replaces military conquest; **Bherighosha → Dhammaghosha**.
- **Separate Kalinga Edicts** (Dhauli, Jaugada) **replace RE XI–XIII** (RE XIII is omitted there); addressed to officers of **Tosali and Samapa** against unjust jailing and torture; "all men are my children".
!NUM 8th year = Kalinga War; 1,00,000 killed; 1,50,000 captive; 261 BC
### 8. ASHOKA'S DHAMMA
- Virtues: kindness, **charity**, truthfulness, gratitude, respect for elders, compassion to humans and animals.
- Vices: **violence, cruelty, anger, pride, jealousy**.
- Agents: **Dhamma Mahamattas** (RE V); **Dhammayatras** (RE VIII, from the 11th regnal year).
- Dhamma is a moral code, not Buddhist doctrine – no Noble Truths or Nibbana named.
### 9. BUDDHIST MISSIONS AND BUDDHISM IN ANDHRA
#### 9.1 Missions → Region
- Sent after the **Third Buddhist Council**, **Pataliputra**, under **Moggaliputta Tissa**.
| Missionary | Region |
|---|---|

| **Mahadeva** | **Mahisamandala** |
| Rakkhita | Vanavasi |
| Yona Dhammarakkhita | Aparantaka |
| Mahadhammarakkhita | Maharashtra |
| Maharakkhita | Yona (Greek) country |
| Majjhima | Himalayan region |

#### 9.2 Buddhism in Andhra
- **Amaravati**: Ashoka enlarged a stupa first built by **Mahadeva** and placed relics; plaque with his name. The Mahastupa was built and enlarged mostly under the **Satavahanas**.
- **Dharanikota** sculptures are older than **Sanchi and Bharhut**.
- **RE XIII** lists the **Andhras** among peoples following Dhamma – Buddhism was known in Andhra; the Puranic term "Andhra-bhrityas" is read as Satavahanas being Mauryan feudatories.

### 12. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. Name "Ashoka" – **Maski and Gujarra**, not Girnar or Sarnath.
2. **Kharosthi** – right to left, only in the north-west.
3. **PE VII** is only on **Delhi-Topra**.
4. Rummindei is a **pillar** in Nepal, not a rock edict site.
5. **RE XII** = tolerance; **RE XIII** = Kalinga; **RE II** = medical care and southern kingdoms.
6. Separate Kalinga Edicts at **Dhauli and Jaugada** replace RE XI–XIII.
7. **Bhattiprolu** is not an Ashokan edict site; Erragudi and Rajula Mandagiri are.
8. Topra and Meerut pillars were moved by **Firoz Shah Tughlaq**, Kosam pillar to Allahabad traditionally by **Akbar**.
9. Sri Lanka was a neighbour, **not part** of the empire.
10. Heliodorus pillar (Besnagar) is **not Ashokan**.
