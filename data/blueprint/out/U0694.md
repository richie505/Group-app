## U0694 | S&T INSTITUTIONS - CSIR AND ITS LABORATORIES, DST, DBT, DSIR, SERB
### 1. DEPARTMENTS AND COUNCILS - FOUNDING
| Body | Year | Status / fact |
|---|---|---|
| **CSIR** | **September 1942** | Autonomous body registered as a **society** (not statutory) |
| **DST** | **1971** | Nodal for new S&T areas, R&D infrastructure, missions, policy |
| National Biotechnology Board | 1982 | Upgraded to **DBT in 1986** |
| **DSIR** | **January 1985** | One of 3 departments of MoST; controls CSIR + 2 PSUs |
| **SERB** | **2008** | Statutory board (SERB Act 2008) |
| **BIRAC** | **2012** | DBT's not-for-profit **Section 8 company** for biotech start-ups |

!TRAP CSIR is a registered society, not a statutory corporation.
### 2. CSIR - GOVERNING STRUCTURE
- **President: Prime Minister** (ex officio); **Vice-President: Union S&T Minister**.
- **DG CSIR = Secretary, DSIR**; heads the Governing Body.
- Network: **37 national laboratories**, 39 outreach centres, 1 innovation complex, 3 units; 3,300+ scientists.
!PAIR CSIR | President | Prime Minister || BRIC | Chair | Union S&T Minister (DBT Secretary = ex officio DG)

### 6. DST - MANDATE, INSTITUTES, SCHEMES
- Autonomous bodies: **Bose Institute** (Kolkata, 1917, J.C. Bose; nodal for **FAIR**), JNCASR, Raman Research Institute, **ARIES** Nainital, **Wadia Institute**, **BSIP** Lucknow (non-CSIR), SCTIMST.
- Survey of India (NIGST, Hyderabad) is under DST. FAIR funded 50:50 by DST and DAE.
- Schemes: **INSPIRE**, **Vigyan Jyoti**, STI Hubs for SC/ST, **NM-ICPS (2018, Rs 3,660 crore)**, Nano Mission; DST is nodal for the **RDI Scheme**.
- 2026: CSIR, DAE, DoS, ICMR, ICAR allowed to bypass GeM for scientific equipment.
### 7. DSIR - PSUs AND R&D RECOGNITION
- Two PSUs: **NRDC** (National Research Development Corporation) and **CEL**.
- Recognises **2,000+ in-house R&D centres**, 900+ SIROs → **income tax and customs duty exemptions**.
- Umbrella Industrial R&D scheme: **PRISM, PACE, BIRD-crf, A2K+**.
!TRAP NRDC is a DSIR PSU, not a DST autonomous body.
