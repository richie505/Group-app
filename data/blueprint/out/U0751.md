## U0751 | NUCLEAR ENERGY – THREE-STAGE PROGRAMME, REACTOR TYPES, PHWR/FBR/AHWR, THORIUM STRATEGY
### 1. NUCLEUS, ISOTOPES AND NUCLEAR FUELS
#### 1.1 Nucleus Basics
- Nucleus = **protons + neutrons**; **mass number** = protons + neutrons.
- **Pu-242** (Z = 94) has **148 neutrons**.
- Hydrogen isotopes: **protium** (no neutron), **deuterium** (1 neutron, stable, heavy hydrogen), **tritium** (2 neutrons). They are isotopes of hydrogen, not carbon.
- **Tritium**: made by hitting **lithium** with neutrons.
- **Heavy water (D₂O)**: deuterium takes the place of hydrogen. **H. C. Urey** discovered deuterium and heavy water (Nobel Chemistry **1934**).
#### 1.2 Isotopes, Isobars, Isotones
| Term | Same | Different | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Isotope | Atomic number (protons) | Mass number (neutrons) | ¹H, ²H, ³H; ¹⁶O, ¹⁷O, ¹⁸O |
| Isobar | Mass number | Atomic number | ³H and ³He |
| Isotone (isoneutronic) | Number of neutrons | Atomic and mass numbers | ³H and ⁴He (2 each); ¹⁴C, ¹⁵N, ¹⁶O (8 each) |
#### 1.3 Fissile vs Fertile
| Isotope | Type | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| U-235 | **Fissile** | Only **0.72%** of natural uranium |
| U-238 | **Fertile** | **99.28%** of natural uranium; becomes **Pu-239** |
| Pu-239 | Fissile | Not natural; bred from U-238 |
| Th-232 | Fertile | Th-232 + neutron → fissile **U-233** (in FBR blankets) |
| U-233 | Fissile | Fuel of **Stage III** and of **KAMINI** |
- India's PHWRs use **natural uranium**.
- **Enrichment** = raising the share of **U-235** above the natural level by **isotope separation** (not U-238). Light water reactors need **3–5%** U-235.
#### 1.4 What is NOT a Nuclear Fuel
- Moderators, coolants and absorbers: **graphite, heavy water, sodium, boron, cadmium, zirconium**.
- Also not fuels: **helium** (inert gas), **lead, calcium**, **chromium** (not linked to atomic power). **Beryllium oxide** = a moderator.
- **Zirconium (Z = 40)** is not radioactive; elements above Z 80 are mostly radioactive; **technetium (43)** and **promethium (61)** are radioactive.
- **Uranium** = the metal used to produce electricity. Thorium is usable only by breeding to U-233.
!TRAP APPSC 2007 keyed "U-238" as the better fuel; U-238 is fertile, the fissile fuel made from it is Pu-239.
### 2. FISSION VS FUSION AND THE BOMBS
#### 2.1 Comparison
| Point | Fission | Fusion |
|---|---|---|
| Process | **Heavy nucleus splits** (U-235 + neutron → barium + krypton + 2–3 neutrons) | **Two light nuclei join** |
| Condition | Neutron hit; **critical mass** | Very high **temperature and pressure** (thermonuclear) |
| Energy | Large | **More per unit mass** |
| Where | Power reactors, **atom bomb** | **Sun and stars**, **hydrogen bomb** |
| Power use | All present-day nuclear electricity | Still at **R&D stage** |
- **Chain reaction**: released neutrons split more U-235 nuclei; each fission releases more neutrons. **Criticality** = self-sustaining chain reaction.
- **Critical mass** = minimum fissile mass that sustains a chain reaction.
- **Reactor**: chain reaction **controlled** (moderators, control rods); **atom bomb**: uncontrolled.
- Stars: **fusion** (hydrogen → helium), not fission.
#### 2.2 Atomic vs Hydrogen Bomb
| Feature | Atom bomb | Hydrogen bomb |
|---|---|---|
| Principle | Uncontrolled **fission** of uranium/plutonium | Uncontrolled **fusion** of deuterium (and tritium) into helium, triggered by a fission bomb |
| Person | **Otto Hahn** (with F. Strassmann, **1939**) – fission principle | **Edward Teller** – "father of the hydrogen bomb"; first US test **November 1952, Eniwetok** (Marshall Islands) |
| City | Bomb | Material |
|---|---|---|
| Hiroshima | **Little Boy** | **U-235** |
| Nagasaki (9 August 1945) | **Fat Man** | **Pu-239** |
!TRAP APPSC 2019: "Breaking apart of a nucleus is called fusion" is INCORRECT – that is fission.
### 3. RADIOACTIVITY
- Discovered by **Henri Becquerel (1896)**; a **nuclear** (not chemical) property; a radioactive substance emits **alpha, beta and gamma**.
- Measured by the **Geiger (Geiger–Muller) counter**.
| Emission | Nature | Charge | Stopped by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alpha | **Helium nucleus** (2p + 2n), mass ≈ helium nucleus | **+2** | Paper or skin |
| Beta | Electron (negative beta) or **positron** (positive beta) | −1 / +1 | Few mm of aluminium |
| Gamma | EM radiation | **Zero** | **Lead or concrete** |
- Zero charge: **neutrino, neutron**. **Deuteron** is not a sub-atomic particle.
- **Half-life** = time for half the nuclei to decay. Half-life 5 years → after 20 years **1/16** remains; half-life 10 days → **3/4** decays in 20 days.
- Natural decay series: **uranium** U-238 → Pb-206; **thorium** Th-232 → Pb-208. **Neptunium series = artificial**.
- **Xenon-135** = "nuclear poison" that absorbs neutrons.
### 4. REACTOR COMPONENTS AND REACTOR TYPES
#### 4.1 Components
| Part | Function | Substances |
|---|---|---|
| **Moderator** | **Slows neutrons** so U-235 can fission | **Heavy water (D₂O)**, light water, graphite, beryllium oxide |
| **Coolant** | **Carries heat** to make steam | Heavy water (PHWR), light water (LWR), **liquid sodium** (FBR) |
| **Control rods** | **Absorb neutrons**, control the rate | **Boron, cadmium**, silver, indium |
- Without control rods the chain reaction goes **out of bounds**; without a moderator a thermal reactor stops.
- **Ordinary water** can act as both moderator and coolant.
#### 4.2 Reactor Types
| Reactor | Fuel | Moderator | Coolant | Indian example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PHWR | Natural uranium | Heavy water | Heavy water | Stage I fleet |
| BWR | Enriched uranium | Light water | Light water (boils in core) | **Tarapur** |
| PWR / VVER | Low-enriched uranium | Light water | Pressurised light water | **Kudankulam** |
| FBR | MOX (Pu-239 + U-238) | **None** | Liquid sodium | PFBR, Kalpakkam |
| AHWR | Th-232 + U-233/Pu | Heavy water | Boiling light water | BARC design |
- **LWRs**: light water as moderator and coolant; **over 85%** of world capacity; India now prioritises LWRs for fast capacity addition.
- **IAEA safeguards** cover reactors using **imported uranium**, not Indian-supplied ones.
### 5. HOMI BHABHA AND THE THREE-STAGE PROGRAMME
- **Homi J. Bhabha** – "Father of the Indian nuclear programme"; 1950s plan to use **abundant thorium** against **scarce uranium**; aim: **self-reliance via a closed fuel cycle**.
| Stage | Reactor | Fuel | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | **PHWR** (D₂O moderator + coolant) | **Natural uranium** | Power + **Pu-239** from spent fuel |
| II | **FBR** (liquid sodium, no moderator) | **MOX: Pu-239 + U-238** | Breeds more Pu-239; Th-232 blanket → U-233 |
| III | **AHWR / thorium reactors** | **Th-232 + U-233** | Large-scale thorium use |
#### 5.1 Advanced Heavy Water Reactor (AHWR)
- **300 MWe** (920 MWth), thorium-based; designed by **BARC**; **not yet built or running**.
- Heavy-water moderated, **boiling light-water** coolant; fuel pins of (Th-U-233) MOX and (Th-Pu) MOX.
- Main purpose: **large-scale thorium use**; a **bridge from Stage I to Stage III** without passing through Stage II.
!KEY APPSC G2 2025 (A/R): A "Dhruva is India's largest research reactor" – TRUE. On R (AHWR and plutonium-bearing spent fuel) the official key took R as true but NOT the correct explanation of A. The AHWR's goal is thorium use.
#### 5.2 Latest Developments
- **6 April 2026**: PFBR first criticality = India enters **Stage II**.
- **May 2026**: **ANEEL fuel** (thorium + **HALEU**, US firm **Clean Core Thorium Energy**) usable in existing PHWRs; could hasten Stage III.
- **Stage III hurdles**: thin fissile inventory, 10–12-year builds, protests – not lack of thorium.
### 6. FAST BREEDER REACTORS
#### 6.1 Breeding Principle
- Core surrounded by a **U-238 blanket**; **fast neutrons** turn it into Pu-239 → **more fissile material than it burns** (electricity by fission).
- **No moderator**; **liquid sodium** coolant; fuel MOX from reprocessed Stage I spent fuel.
#### 6.2 PFBR, Kalpakkam (Tamil Nadu)
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Capacity | **500 MWe** (1,250 MWt); sodium-cooled, **pool type** |
| Designed by | **IGCAR** |
| Built and commissioned by | **BHAVINI** (PSU, 2003, DAE) |
| First criticality | **6 April 2026** |
| Cost | Cost doubled; criticality **16+ years late** |
- India = **2nd after Russia** with a commercial-scale FBR; PFBR = **first indigenous FBR**.
- Next: 2 x 500 MWe FBRs at Kalpakkam.
#### 6.3 FBTR (Fast Breeder Test Reactor)
- At **IGCAR, Kalpakkam**; coolant **liquid sodium**; fuel indigenous **plutonium-uranium mixed carbide**.
- Design based on French reactor **Rapsodie**; critical **18 October 1985**.
- IGCAR **2005** milestone: reprocessing FBTR's mixed-carbide fuel.
!TRAP APPSC G1 2024: PFBR was built by BHAVINI, not IGCAR (IGCAR designed it).

### 8. THORIUM, MONAZITE AND URANIUM MINES
- India: about **25%** of world thorium vs about **2%** of uranium; **3rd-largest** thorium reserve.
- Thorium vs uranium: more abundant, more energy per unit mass mined, less harmful waste.
- **Monazite** = principal thorium ore; also **rare earths** (lanthanum, cerium), phosphate, a little uranium; resources **13.15 million tonnes** (DAE).
- Beach placer sands of **Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Odisha, Andhra Pradesh**, Maharashtra, West Bengal – **not the entire coast**.
- Only government bodies (**IREL**) may process and export monazite.
| Mine | Location | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Jaduguda** | East Singhbhum, **Jharkhand** | India's **first** uranium mine; UCIL seat |
| **Tummalapalle** | **YSR Kadapa, AP** | UCIL mine + processing plant |
- About **80%** of imported uranium comes from **Kazakhstan** (Dec 2025).
### 9. FUSION RESEARCH
- **ITER** (International Thermonuclear Experimental Reactor): **Cadarache, southern France**; **tokamak**; world's largest **magnetic confinement** experiment.
- Members: **EU + six**: India, China, Japan, South Korea, Russia, USA. **Canada is NOT a member**. India joined **2005**; supplies the **cryostat**.
- **500 MW out from 50 MW in** (~10 times). Advantage for India: can build fusion power reactors.
- **Institute for Plasma Research, Gandhinagar** (not BARC): **Aditya** (first Indian tokamak) and **SST-1** (Steady State Superconducting Tokamak).
- **December 2022**: first fusion **ignition** / net energy gain – **National Ignition Facility**, **Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory**, USA (US DOE/NNSA), using **lasers**.

#### 10.2 Acts and Data
- **Atomic Energy Act, 1948** – limited atomic energy to peaceful uses.
- **SHANTI Act 2025** (assent **20 Dec 2025**) – repealed **Atomic Energy Act 1962** and **CLNDA 2010**; licensed private companies/JVs may build, own and operate plants.
- NPCIL fleet **25 reactors, 8,880 MW**; target **22.38 GW by 2031-32**.
- **Nuclear Energy Mission** (Budget 2025-26): **100 GW by 2047**; **₹20,000 crore** for SMRs.
#### 10.3 Small Modular Reactors
- Up to **300 MWe** per unit (~one-third of a conventional reactor); factory-built; refuelled every **3–7 years** (vs 1–2).
- **Bharat Small Reactors** (NPCIL): based on **220 MW PHWR**; captive plants for industry.
- **AP angle**: **CODENE** at **IIT Hyderabad** for SMR design and safety (Jun 2026); Tummalapalle uranium.
