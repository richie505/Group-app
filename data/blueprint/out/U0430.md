## U0430 | UN SPECIALISED AGENCIES AND PROGRAMMES – WHO, ILO, UNESCO, FAO, UNICEF, UNDP, UNEP, UNHCR
### 1. TYPES OF UN BODIES
| Category | Bodies | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| **Specialised agencies** | WHO, ILO, UNESCO, FAO, IFAD, ITU, UPU, WIPO, IMO, ICAO, UNIDO, WMO, **IMF**, **World Bank (IBRD, IFC)** | **Autonomous**, own treaty and budget, linked to **ECOSOC** |
| **Funds and programmes** | UNICEF, UNDP, UNEP, UNFPA, UN-Habitat, WFP | Created by the **General Assembly**; voluntary funding |
| **Office of the GA** | **UNHCR** (1950, Geneva) | Refugee protection |
| **Related organisations** | **IAEA, WTO, OPCW** | Not specialised agencies; report to GA/UNSC |
!TRAP IAEA, WTO, OPCW are **related organisations**, not specialised agencies. UNHCR is an **Office of the GA**.

### 5. ILO
- **Tripartite**: governments, employers, workers – in the **International Labour Conference** and **Governing Body** (not ECOSOC).
- Calls itself the leading normative agency on labour standards.
| Convention | India |
|---|---|
| Forced Labour (C29), Abolition of Forced Labour (C105) | Ratified |
| Equal Remuneration (C100), Discrimination (C111) | Ratified |
| Minimum Age (C138), Worst Forms of Child Labour (C182) | Ratified (2017) |
| **Freedom of Association (C87)**, **Right to Organise and Collective Bargaining (C98)** | **Not ratified** |
| **Domestic Workers (C189)**, adopted **2011** | **Not ratified** |
- Child labour (ILO-UNICEF): **160 million** (2020, released 2021); about **138 million** (2024, released 2025).
- ILO report (May 2026): **lifelong learning** = priority against AI disruption; social protection report: decouple social protection from single-employer contracts (gig work); Global Coalition for Social Justice works with India on AI observatories.

- World Water Development Report: India **120th of 122** on water quality.
