## U0551 | UNION BUDGET - STRUCTURE, PROCESS, KEY DOCUMENTS; HIGHLIGHTS OF THE LATEST UNION BUDGET
### 1. BUDGET - MEANING AND CONSTITUTIONAL BASIS
- Word from French **bougette** = "little bag". The Constitution does **not** use the word "Budget".
- **Article 112**: **Annual Financial Statement (AFS)** - estimated receipts and expenditure of the Government of India.
- The Budget is the main instrument of **fiscal policy**; functions: **resource allocation, income redistribution, stabilisation**.
- Prepared and presented by the **Budget Division, Department of Economic Affairs (DEA), Ministry of Finance**, with NITI Aayog and spending ministries. DEA also prepares budgets of States under President's Rule and UTs.
- **Revenue (tax) estimates**: **Department of Revenue** (CBDT and CBIC) in the **Ministry of Finance**.
!TRAP **Banking Division** is NOT a Department of the Ministry of Finance.
#### 1.1 Articles
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **110(1)(a)** | **Finance Bill** (tax provisions) as **Money Bill** |
| **112** | Annual Financial Statement |
| **112(3)** | List of **charged** expenditure |
| **113** | Estimates / **Demands for Grants** - only Lok Sabha votes |
| **114 / 114(3)** | **Appropriation Bill** / no withdrawal from CFI without an Appropriation Act |
| **115** | **Supplementary**, additional or excess grants |
| **116** | **Vote on account**, vote of credit, exceptional grants |
| **266 / 266(2)** | **Consolidated Fund** / **Public Account** |
| **267** | **Contingency Fund** - corpus **₹30,000 crore** from FY 2021-22 |
- **Public Account**: provident funds, small savings, deposits - government acts as **banker**; withdrawals need **no appropriation**.

### 3. BUDGET TYPES
| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Balanced** | Receipts = expenditure |
| **Surplus** | Receipts > expenditure; **checks inflation**, may slow growth |
| **Deficit** | Expenditure > receipts (India's normal case); counter-cyclical, risks inflation and debt |
| **Zero-based** | Each manager **justifies the entire budget from the beginning** (zero) every period |
| **Performance** | Links spending to **activities/work done** |
| **Outcome** | Links outlays to **outputs and outcomes** (Output-Outcome Framework) |
| **Gender** | Allocations for women: **Part A** (100%), **Part B** (30-99%), **Part C** (below 30%) |
| **Line-item** | Traditional; control over individual items |
- **Zero-based**: developed by **Peter Pyhrr** (1970s); first adopted in the **USA**; first used in government by **Jimmy Carter**.
- **Performance**: from **USA**, first **Hoover Commission (1949)**; India from the **late 1960s** on **ARC** advice.
- **Gender Budget Statement = Statement 13**, since 2005-06; 2026-27: **₹5.01 lakh crore (9.37%)** - highest ever.
- 16th Finance Commission ties Centrally Sponsored Schemes to measurable outcomes.
!PAIR Performance | spending to activities | Hoover 1949 || Outcome | outlays to outcomes | 2005-06
### 4. RECEIPTS CLASSIFICATION
#### 4.1 The Test
| Basis | Revenue receipts | Capital receipts |
|---|---|---|
| Liability | Create **no** liability | **Create** a liability |
| Assets | Do **not** reduce assets | Or **reduce** assets |
- **Correct order**: **Revenue Receipts → Tax Revenue → Non-tax Revenue → Capital Receipts**.
#### 4.2 Items
| Receipt | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Tax revenue** | Income tax, corporation tax (direct); GST, customs, excise (indirect) |
| **Non-tax revenue** | **Interest receipts**, **dividends and profits** (RBI surplus, PSU dividends), spectrum, fees, fines |
| **Debt capital** | **Market borrowings**, **T-Bills** (short-term domestic borrowing), external loans (World Bank), **small savings/PF**, RBI borrowing |
| **Non-debt capital** | **Recovery of loans**, **disinvestment**, other receipts |
- **Interest received on loans** does NOT create a liability - revenue receipt.
- **Small savings** are NOT in the revenue account (debt receipts, capital account).
- **Disinvestment / PSU stake sale** = capital receipt that **reduces assets** - NOT a revenue receipt, NOT a liability.
- **Paper taxes**: wealth tax, gift tax, estate duty - never large, now abolished; GST is not one.
#### 4.3 Revenue and Capital Accounts
- **Revenue account** = current-year transactions (taxes, interest, profits, dividends, revenue expenditure).
- **Capital account** = assets and liabilities (borrowings, disinvestment, loan recovery, asset creation).
- **Capital Budget** = capital receipts (market borrowings, T-Bills, foreign loans) + capital payments (land, buildings, machinery, investment in shares, loans to States/UTs).
- Rule: **borrow only to invest**. Mnemonic: Revenue = Running the government; Capital = Creating assets.
### 5. EXPENDITURE CLASSIFICATION
| Pair | Distinction |
|---|---|
| **Revenue vs capital** | Revenue creates no asset (**salaries, allowances**, pensions, interest, subsidies, grants to States); capital **creates assets or reduces liabilities** (capital works, loans given, **debt repayment**) |
| **Plan vs non-plan** | Plan = Central Plans + central assistance for State/UT Plans; non-plan = **interest**, salaries, pensions, defence, subsidies, statutory transfers, maintenance |
| **Developmental vs non-developmental** | Economic and **social services** vs **interest, defence, administration, pensions** |
- **Interest payments** = classic **non-plan** item; **largest non-plan item** (2015-16); **most important revenue-account expenditure**.
- Plan/non-plan replaced by **revenue/capital** from **2017-18** (not 2010-11).
- **Defence** = non-development, "committed" spending. **Revenue expenditure** = largest in amount.
- **Capital receipts are less than revenue receipts** (2010-11 and now).
### 6. CHARGED VS VOTED EXPENDITURE
- **Charged (Art. 112(3))**: comes straight from the Consolidated Fund; **discussable but not voted** (Art. 113(1)); shown separately in Demands.
#### 6.1 Charged Items - Full List
1. Emoluments and office expenses of the **President**.
2. Salaries of **Chairman/Deputy Chairman (RS)** and **Speaker/Deputy Speaker (LS)**.
3. **Debt charges** - interest on public debt, redemption.
4. Salaries, allowances, pensions of **Supreme Court judges**; **pensions of High Court judges**.
5. Salary and office expenses of the **CAG**.
6. **UPSC** chairman, members and office expenses.
7. Sums for **court decrees, judgments, awards**.
!TRAP SC judges = salaries + pensions charged; **HC judges = only pensions** charged.
### 7. BUDGET PROCESS IN PARLIAMENT
- **Budget circular** issued in **September**; **Halwa ceremony** marks printing at **North Block**.
- Presented in **Lok Sabha** on the first working day of February - in practice **1 February even on a Sunday** (2026). Economic Survey a day or more earlier.
#### 7.1 Six Stages
| Stage | Key facts |
|---|---|
| 1. Presentation | FM in Lok Sabha |
| 2. **General discussion** | Both Houses, 3-4 days; **no cut motions or voting** |
| 3. Committee scrutiny | Departmental standing committees examine demands |
| 4. **Voting on demands** | **Only Lok Sabha**; President's recommendation needed; RS cannot vote; **cut motions** here |
| 5. **Appropriation Bill** | Art. 114 |
| 6. **Finance Bill** | Money Bill; tax proposals |
#### 7.2 Control Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Policy cut** | Demand reduced to **Re 1** |
| **Economy cut** | Reduced by a **stated amount** |
| **Token cut** | Reduced by **₹100** |
| **Guillotine** | Last allotted day: all outstanding demands voted **without discussion** |
| **Vote on account** (Art. 116) | Part-year spending, about **two months** (one-sixth); 3-5 months in election years |
| **Interim budget** | Full statement of receipts and expenditure by an outgoing government in an election year |
- **Appropriation Bill**: no amendment may vary a voted grant, change its purpose or vary charged expenditure.
- **Finance Bill**: passed within **75 days** (**Provisional Collection of Taxes Act, 1931**).
- Vote on account still used in election years - not ineffectual after the 1 February shift.
- **Public Accounts Committee** = Parliament's audit control (examines **CAG** reports).
- Supplementary demands 2025-26 (second batch): **Economic Stabilisation Fund ₹1 lakh crore**.

### 11. DEFICITS - BE VS RE
| Year | Fiscal deficit (% GDP) |
|---|---|
| 2024-25 RE | **4.8** |
| 2025-26 BE / RE | **4.4** |
| 2026-27 BE | **4.3** |
- **FD RE 2024-25 (4.8%) > BE 2025-26 (4.4%)**.
- Formulas: **FD** = total expenditure - receipts excluding borrowings; **PD** = FD - interest; **RD** = revenue expenditure - revenue receipts.
- Worked: FD ₹80,000 crore - interest ₹25,000 crore = **PD ₹55,000 crore**.
- FY26: revenue expenditure up only 1.8%; FD at 62.3% of BE by Nov 2025. FY27: FD at **26.8%** of target by July (CGA, 1 Sep 2026); tax devolution Apr-Jul 2026 ₹3,72,354 crore, ₹56,190 crore lower than a year earlier.
- Criticism: interest takes **40% of revenue receipts**; underspending in JJM and PMAY in 2025-26.
- States' combined FD FY25 **3.2%** of GDP; **18 States** exceeded the 3% limit in FY24.
- **AP Budget 2026-27**: expenditure **₹3,10,058 crore** (+12%); capital outlay ₹48,698 crore. CAG flagged AP's revenue deficit three years running; revenue receipts only 48% of BE by Nov 2025.
