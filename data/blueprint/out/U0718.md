## U0718 | NETIQUETTES – DIGITAL ETIQUETTE, DIGITAL LITERACY, MISINFORMATION AND FAKE NEWS
### 1. NETIQUETTES – DOS AND DON'TS
- Core rule: engage in **constructive online dialogue** and **respect privacy**.
- "**Digital freedom** should be accompanied by **digital responsibility**."
| Medium | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| **Email** | Clear subject line, polite greeting, check recipients | Type in **ALL CAPS** (reads as **shouting**); forward chain mails |
| **Social media** | **Verify before sharing**, respect privacy, credit sources | Post others' photos/data **without consent** (a don't even if the source is credited); troll or abuse |
| **Online meetings** | Join on time, **mute when not speaking**, use chat/raise-hand | Talk over others; **record without permission** |
| **Online forums** | Stay on topic, civil discourse | Trolling, spamming |
- PYQ "which is NOT a social media app" – answer was **None of these** (all listed were social media apps).
### 2. ONLINE SAFETY PRACTICES
#### 2.1 Recommended vs NOT Recommended
| Recommended | NOT recommended |
|---|---|
| **Strong passwords** | Accepting **friend requests from unknown people** without caution |
| **Multi-factor / two-factor authentication** | Clicking a link or scanning a **QR code to receive money** |
| **Verifying information before sharing** | Paying a "friend" who urgently asks for money without checking |
| Caution with personal/sensitive data; privacy settings | Open, unsecured Wi-Fi |
| **WPA3** Wi-Fi; keep **digital footprint low** | Sharing OTPs |
- Friend urgently asks for money: verify through **another channel** (ID may be hacked).
- Cyber-hygiene campaign: Telangana Cyber Security Bureau, **#OnlineButUnafraid** (20 Mar 2026).
- Report cyber offences: **cybercrime.gov.in** and helpline **1930**.
!TRAP "Which is NOT a safe practice?" = accepting friend requests from unknown persons.
### 3. ONLINE BEHAVIOUR TERMS AND LEGAL REMEDIES
#### 3.1 Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Cyber venting** | Using websites to **pour out grievances** (e.g. against employer) to let out anger |
| **Trolling** | Deliberately provocative or abusive content |
| **Cyberbullying** | Repeated online harassment, esp. of children |
| **Doxxing** | Publicly sharing someone's **private info without consent** to harm/shame; **no universal legal definition** |
| **Cyberstalking** | Repeated unwanted monitoring or contact online |
| **Nomophobia** | Irrational **fear of being without one's mobile** (no signal, low battery, no balance) |
| **Digital footprint** | Trail of data a person leaves online |
| **Filter bubble** | Algorithms show content by preference, **isolating users** from other views |
#### 3.2 Sections → Offence
| Section | Offence |
|---|---|
| **IT Act 66E** | Privacy violation |
| **IT Act 67** | Obscene material |
| **BNS 78** | **Stalking**, incl. monitoring electronic communication |
- Doxxing is prosecuted under stalking, harassment and cyber-crime laws. Latest: doxxing of women protesters at Jantar Mantar (2026).
### 4. INFORMATION DISORDER
| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Misinformation** | False content, **no intent** to deceive (mistakes, rumours) |
| **Disinformation** | False content **deliberately** created for political/social/economic goals |
| **Malinformation** | **Genuine** information shared **to cause harm** (e.g. leaked private data) |
- **Echo chambers / filter bubbles** strengthen fake news; **deepfakes** = AI-made fake audio/video.
!PAIR Misinformation | False | No intent || Disinformation | False | Deliberate
### 5. DIGITAL CITIZENSHIP
- **Responsible digital citizen**: identifies misinformation, verifies sources, **protects data**, respects privacy, polite discussion.
- Rights side: **online grievance redressal, e-consultations, open government data** (e.g. AP Grama and Ward Sachivalayam system).
- Responsibilities: civil discourse, low digital footprint, no sharing of unverified content.
### 6. FACT-CHECK MECHANISMS AND IT RULES
#### 6.1 Timeline
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **Nov 2019** | **PIB Fact Check Unit** set up – debunks fake news about Government of India, ministries, PSUs |
| 2021 | **IT (Intermediary Guidelines & Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules** under **Sec. 87, IT Act 2000** |
| **April 2023** | Amendment: intermediary loses **safe harbour** if content on Central Government business is flagged "fake" by PIB FCU |
| **March 2024** | FCU formally notified; **Supreme Court stayed** it |
| **Sept 2024** | **Bombay High Court struck down** the 2023 amendment |
- IT Rules 2021: grievance officer; SSMIs need a Resident Compliance Officer and **monthly compliance reports**.
- SC hearing (Mar 2026): Centre says the Rules do not curb humour, satire or criticism.
- FCU reporting: WhatsApp, factcheck@pib.gov.in. Debate: government as judge of truth about itself.
#### 6.2 Platform and MHA Measures
| Measure | Body / feature |
|---|---|
| **WhatsApp tip line** | Against fake news before the **2019 Lok Sabha election** |
| **SAHYOG Portal** | Run by **I4C (MHA)** for takedown notices (~290 a day) |
| Takedown rule | Remove flagged unlawful content within **three hours** |
### 7. DEEPFAKES AND MEDIA-LITERACY MEASURES
| Response | Body |
|---|---|
| Mandatory **labels on AI content** in campaigns | **ECI** advisory |
| Penalties | **DPDP Act** |
| **Watermarking** of AI content | **EU AI Act** |
- Counter-measures: media-literacy programmes, fact-checkers, platform policies to remove or **down-rank** fake content (platform self-regulation).
- **NEP 2020**: stresses **critical thinking** and civic engagement – an answer to fake news.
### 8. DIGITAL LITERACY
- **Computer Literacy Day**: **2 December**.
- Digital India **Vision Area 3** (Digital Empowerment of Citizens) begins with **Universal Digital Literacy**.
| Scheme | Feature |
|---|---|
| **PMGDISHA** | **One digitally literate person in each rural family**; launched by PM at Gandhinagar |
| **ULLAS** | 2022-27, **Ministry of Education** (not MeitY), adults 15+; digital + financial literacy |
- NSS CAMS 2022-23 (age 15-24): **78.4%** could send e-mail with attachment; **71.2%** could copy-paste.
- Himachal Pradesh = 5th State/UT with full functional literacy.
- Other days: 21 March Universal Acceptance Day; 1 July Digital India anniversary; 10 Dec Human Rights Day.
