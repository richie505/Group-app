## U0748 | OIL AND NATURAL GAS – BASINS, REFINERIES, SPR, GAS GRID, CITY GAS DISTRIBUTION
### 1. ENERGY CLASSIFICATION AND PETROLEUM GEOLOGY
- **Coal, petroleum, natural gas** = conventional, **non-renewable** fossil fuels. **Natural gas = cleanest fossil fuel**; coal most polluting.
- Oil formed in the **Tertiary** period; petroleum = 90–95% hydrocarbons.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Source rock** | Organic-rich shale where oil forms |
| **Reservoir rock** | Porous, permeable sandstone or fissured limestone |
| **Cap rock** | Impervious bed sealing the trap |
| Traps | Anticlines, fault traps, salt domes; most reserves in anticlines + fault traps; commercial oil at **crests of anticlines** |
- Layers by density – from top: **gas, oil, water** (from bottom: water, oil, gas).
- **Wet gas** = associated; **dry gas** = non-associated; **sour gas** = H₂S-rich.
- Conventional gas: permeable sandstone. Unconventional: impermeable sandstone, shale, coal seams – costly technology.

### 3. UNCONVENTIONAL HYDROCARBONS
| Resource | Where held | Basins |
|---|---|---|
| **Shale gas/oil** | Fine-grained shale; **fracking + horizontal drilling** | **Cambay largest**; KG, Cauvery, Damodar, Assam-Arakan, Indo-Gangetic |
| **Coal bed methane** | Adsorbed in coal seams; released by pumping water | Damodar valley: **Raniganj** (first commercial), Jharia, Bokaro, Sohagpur |
| **Gas hydrates** | Methane-water crystals in deep-sea sediments | **KG, Mahanadi, Andaman** offshore |
- **Guar gum** (cluster bean) is used in fracking as a thickener to carry sand.
!TRAP Damodar Valley is a CBM region, not a gas-hydrate region.
### 4. FUELS AND PRODUCTS
| Fuel | Key facts | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **LNG** | Cooled below **−160°C**; volume shrinks 1,000 times; **lighter than air** | Import/transport of gas |
| **CNG** | Compressed to **200–250 kg/cm²**; mainly methane (80–90%) | Vehicles |
| **LPG** | **Propane + butane**; refinery/gas by-product; **heavier than air**; odourless – **ethyl mercaptan** added | Cooking |
| PNG | 85–95% methane, piped via CGD | Homes, commerce |
| Furnace oil | Heavy residue | Industrial boilers |
| Naphtha | — | Petrochemicals, fertilisers |
| Petrochemicals | — | Plastics, fibres, rubber |
- **Butane (C₄H₁₀)**: lighter fuel; a Natural Gas Liquid. Methane is piped and burnt directly; also in biogas.
- Benchmarks: **Brent** (North Sea), **WTI** (US). India = **net exporter of petroleum products since 2001-02**.
- 33 crore LPG connections; domestic gas could supply 30 crore via PNG (PNGRB, Apr 2026).
!TRAP LPG is not mainly methane; one PYQ key lists ethane, propane and butane – choose butane/propane.

### 7. GAS GRID AND PIPELINES
| Pipeline | Route / states |
|---|---|
| **HVJ** (GAIL) | **Gujarat – MP – UP**; not Maharashtra |
| **Pradhan Mantri Urja Ganga** (JHBDPL, GAIL) | UP, Bihar, Jharkhand, WB, Odisha + Barauni-Guwahati; 3,546 km authorised |
| **Indradhanush NE grid** | ~1,656 km, 8 NE states; JV of **IOCL, ONGC, GAIL, OIL, NRL** |
| **East-West** (Reliance) | **Kakinada (AP) → Bharuch** |
- National grid idea: **1955, S. H. Zaheer** (RRL Hyderabad, now CSIR-IICT). **One Nation One Gas Grid** began **2014**; over 32,000 km; all states by 2027.
- Gas share: **~6.7% → 15% by 2030**; imports ~50%.
!KEY APPSC-G2 2025: HVJ does not pass through Maharashtra.
### 8. PNGRB AND CITY GAS DISTRIBUTION
- **PNGRB**: statutory, **Act 2006**, under MoPNG; regulates **refining, storage, distribution, marketing and sale** – **not crude or gas production**.
- **AP CGD**: **10 GAs**; target **83.61 lakh** PNG connections by **March 2032**; East Godavari highest.

### 11. COAL – HISTORY, TYPES AND INSTITUTIONS
| Event | Year |
|---|---|
| First mine, Sitarampur (**Raniganj**) | **1774** |
| Coking / non-coking nationalised | **1971-72 / 1973** |
| Coal India Ltd | **1975** |
| SC cancelled **204** blocks | 2014 → CMSP Act 2015 |
| Commercial auctions | 2020 |
- Carbon order: anthracite > bituminous > sub-bituminous > lignite > peat. Fields: Jharia (Jharkhand), Raniganj (WB), Korba (Chhattisgarh), Talcher (Odisha), Singareni (Telangana); lignite Neyveli (TN), Barmer (Rajasthan).
- **Coal Controller's Organisation** (Kolkata): statistics, captive-block monitoring, objections on acquiring coal-bearing land. **Energy Statistics** = NSO, MoSPI.
- **SCCL**: 51% Telangana, 49% GoI. India **2nd-largest** producer (~10%) after China (51%); **1,040.08 MT** (FY 2025-26).
- Gasification: syngas (**CO, H₂, CH₄, CO₂**); first fertiliser plant **Talcher** (not Angul); **JSPL Angul** only commercial plant.
