## U0787 | NATIONAL ACTION PLAN ON CLIMATE CHANGE – EIGHT NATIONAL MISSIONS AND STATE ACTION PLANS
### 1. NAPCC – RELEASE AND SCOPE
- **NAPCC** released on **30 June 2008** by PM **Dr Manmohan Singh**.
- Prepared under the **PM's Council on Climate Change** (formed **2007**).
- **FIRST**: India's first overarching domestic climate policy framework; implemented through nodal ministries.
- Scope: **both mitigation and adaptation** (APPSC 2023) – not only GHGs, biodiversity or air pollution.
- Core missions = **8**; **four** of the eight focus on adaptation.
- NAPCC = domestic vehicle for India's **NDCs**. NDCs are delivered through the **National Adaptation Plan + national missions + SAPCCs**.
- Adaptation spending: **3.7% of GDP (FY16) → 5.6% (FY22)**.
- CoP30: **Belem, Brazil**; India's National Statement on 17 Nov 2025.
!TRAP Scope question: answer "mitigation and adaptation", not mitigation alone.

#### 3.2 DST Missions
- **NMSHE** assesses Himalayan vulnerability.
- **NMSKCC** builds S&T capacity: Centres of Excellence, **State Climate Change Centres**, Global Technology Watch Groups.
#### 3.3 What is NOT one of the eight
- Schemes: Swachh Bharat, Ujjwala, AMRUT.
- Wind, nuclear, green hydrogen, **National Mission on Clean Air (NCAP)**, biomass (SAMARTH).
- **National Mission for Sustaining the Coastal and Marine Ecosystem**.
- Nuclear and Green Hydrogen missions advance **net zero by 2070**.
### 4. MISSION OBJECTIVES AND INSTRUMENTS
#### 4.1 Green India Mission
- Protect, restore and enhance **forest cover**; tackles **both adaptation and mitigation**.
- **3 sub-missions**: forest quality; forest/tree cover with ecosystem restoration; **incomes of forest-dependent people** (not thermal power).
- Targets: **50-60 Mt** extra annual sequestration by 2020; additional sink of **2.5-3.0 bn t CO2e** by 2030.
- **CAG audit (Aug 2026)**, 16 States/UTs, 2015-16 to 2024-25:
| Component | Achieved | Target | Shortfall |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quality improvement | 0.11384 Mha | 1.4 Mha | **91.87%** |
| Cover increase | 0.03409 Mha | 1.4 Mha | **97.57%** |
#### 4.2 National Water Mission
- Goals include a **public water database**, focus on **over-exploited areas**, **20% gain in water-use efficiency** (basin-level IWRM).
- State Specific Action Plans: **₹50 lakh** for major States.
#### 4.3 PAT and NMSA
- **PAT (Perform, Achieve and Trade)**: market mechanism under NMEEE; energy-intensive units beating targets earn **tradable Energy Saving Certificates**.
- **NMSA**: Green Revolution-Krishonnati (2018-19) → **PM-RKVY** from 2022-23. Components: Rainfed Area Development, On-Farm Water Management, Soil Health Management, Sub-Mission on Agroforestry.
- NMSA benefits: up to **55% micro-irrigation subsidy**; **Soil Health Cards** free every **two years**.

### 7. STATE ACTION PLANS ON CLIMATE CHANGE (SAPCC)
- **34 SAPCCs** (28 States + 6 UTs) approved and operational.
| Phase | Nature |
|---|---|
| 2010-15 | Broad, **mitigation-centric** plans |
| 2020 onwards | **District-specific, risk-based adaptation** plans tied to social schemes and local institutions |
- Support: State Climate Change Centres (NMSKCC); state- and district-level vulnerability assessments; CCAP.
- Weakness: finance. Fix proposed: **climate budgeting** via State Finance Departments.
- Comparison: **Tamil Nadu** = Governing Council on Climate Change (headed by CM) + TN Green Climate Company.
### 8. ANDHRA PRADESH CLIMATE ACTION
- **Exclusive Climate Change Cell: November 2022** (APPSC 2022); now **AP State Climate Change Cell (APSCCC)** under Dept of **Environment, Forests and Science & Technology**.
- Revised **AP SAPCC 2025-30**; **Aug 2026**: APSCCC prescribed **eight energy-sector adaptation measures** (AP-Genco, NREDCAP, AP Green Energy Corporation).
- Vulnerable sectors: heatwaves, droughts, floods, cyclones → water, agriculture, food security, health.
- Green cover: **37% by 2030 → 50% by 2047**.
