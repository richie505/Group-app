### 2. MISSION FIRSTS AND RECORDS
#### 2.1 SpaDeX
- **Space Docking Experiment**: **PSLV-C60, 30 Dec 2024**, 474 km orbit.
- **Docked 16 Jan 2025** → India the **4th** country to dock in space, after **US, Russia, China** (not Japan).
#### 2.2 Records
| Record | Mission |
|---|---|
| Most satellites in one launch | **PSLV-C37, 104 satellites** (2017) |
| Heaviest payload by an Indian rocket | **BlueBird Block-2, 6,100 kg**, **LVM3-M6, 24 Dec 2025** |
| First dedicated commercial LVM3 flight | OneWeb, 36 satellites (2022) |
| First exclusive weather satellite | **Kalpana-1**, PSLV, 2002 |
- BlueBird Block-2: **223 sq m** phased array; **direct-to-smartphone broadband**.
!NUM 104 = PSLV-C37 record; 6,100 kg = BlueBird; 4th = docking nation; 433 = foreign satellites launched
