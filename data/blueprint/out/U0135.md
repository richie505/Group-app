## U0135 | MUGHAL PROVINCIAL AND LOCAL ADMINISTRATION; MANSABDARI-JAGIRDARI-ZAMINDARI LINKAGE
### 1. UNITS AND THEIR HEADS
| Unit (largest to smallest) | Head(s) |
|---|---|
| **Suba** (province) | **Subedar** (Nazim) + provincial **Diwan** |
| **Sarkar** (district) | **Faujdar** + **Amalguzar** |
| **Pargana** (≈ tehsil) | **Shiqdar** + amil, qanungo, chaudhari |
| Village | **Muqaddam**, patwari, chowkidar, panchayat |
- Ascending order: **Pargana → Sarkar → Suba** (UPSC 2021). Mughal "district" = **Sarkar**.
- **Chakla** – unit between suba and pargana, **not identical with the sarkar**; prominent in **Bengal** under **Murshid Quli Khan**.
- Subas: Akbar began with **12 (1580)**, **15** by the end of his reign; **Aurangzeb – 21** (Golconda the 21st).
- The state's effective rural reach **ended at the pargana**; villages mostly self-governing.
### 2. PROVINCIAL AND CITY OFFICIALS
| Official | Function |
|---|---|
| **Subedar** (sipahsalar/nazim) | Governor – law and order, imperial decrees |
| **Diwan** (provincial) | Revenue and finance; **independent of the Subedar – a check on him** |
| **Bakhshi** | Military pay, musters, news reports |
| **Faujdar** | Sarkar military command, law and order |
| **Kotwal** | City police chief; markets, watch on newcomers |
| **Muhtasib** | Public morals |
| **Waqia-navis** | News-writer |
| **Diwan-i-Tan** | Salaries (tankhwah) and jagir assignments |
| Sadr / Qazi | Grants / justice |
!KEY Subedar–Diwan separation = check and balance in the province.
### 3. REVENUE OFFICIALS
| Official | Level | Duty |
|---|---|---|
| **Amalguzar** (amil) | Sarkar | **Revenue collector in charge of a district** (APPSC G1 2022) |
| **Bitikchi** | Sarkar | Accountant – land records, assessment, revenue papers |
| **Potdar / Khazanadar / Fotadar** | Sarkar | **Treasurer** |
| **Qanungo** | Pargana | Hereditary keeper of land records; records of revenue payable by villages |
| Chaudhari | Pargana | Revenue collector (local notable) |
| **Karkun** | Pargana | Clerk – wrote revenue records |
| **Patwari** | Village | **Village accountant** |
| **Muqaddam** (Patel) | Village | **Village headman**; collected revenue |
!TRAP Amalguzar = collector; Bitikchi = accountant; Potdar = treasurer; Patwari = accountant; Muqaddam = headman.
### 4. LAND REVENUE SYSTEMS AND TODAR MAL
- **Zabt** – fixed cash assessment on measured land; formalised as the **Dahsala** (ten-year) settlement of **1580** by **Raja Todar Mal**.
- Revenue = average of the **past ten years'** yield; state share **one-third** of produce.
| System | Basis |
|---|---|
| **Zabti / Dahsala** | Measurement + 10-year average rates, in cash |
| **Batai** (ghallabakhshi / bhaoli) | Sharing of the crop |
| **Kankut** | Estimate of the crop |
| **Nasaq** | Estimate from past assessments; used in **Bengal and Orissa** |
### 5. LAND CLASSIFICATION
| Class | Condition |
|---|---|
| **Polaj** | Cultivated every year |
| **Parauti** | Left fallow for a short time (1–2 years) to recover; paid polaj rate when cultivated |
| **Chachar** | Fallow **3–4 years** |
| **Banjar** | Uncultivated **5 years or more** |
### 6. MANSABDAR–JAGIRDAR–ZAMINDAR LINKAGE
1. **Mansabdar** gets rank → paid by a **tankhwah jagir**.
2. Jagirdar's agents collect the assessed revenue (**jama**) through **zamindars**, who keep a **hereditary share**.
3. **Peasant** pays about **one-third** of produce.
| Jagirdar | Zamindar |
|---|---|
| Revenue in lieu of salary for state (mainly military) service | Hereditary rights over land/revenue in a locality |
| Not hereditary; transferred often | Hereditary, **saleable** rights |
- Conflicts (local face of the jagirdari crisis): short-tenure jagirdars pressed for more → zamindars resisted/rebelled → peasants fled.
### 7. ZAMINDAR CATEGORIES (S. NURUL HASAN)
| Category | Rights and role |
|---|---|
| **Autonomous chiefs** | Rajas ruling their own territories |
| **Intermediary** | Collected revenue from primary zamindars for the state; got a commission |
| **Primary** | Proprietary, hereditary rights over cultivated land – not temporary rights |
### 8. AP ANGLE – QUTB SHAHI GOLCONDA
- Units **tarafs** and **simalu**; under Tana Shah **37 sarkars, 517 parganas**.
- Village **mukaddam**, accountant **kulkarni**, pargana officer **desai**; revenue **farmed out by auction**.
- **Northern Circars** zamindars date from the Qutb Shahi era.

### 11. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. District = **Sarkar**, not Pargana.
2. **Chakla** is not the sarkar.
3. Diwan is **independent** of the Subedar.
4. **Bitikchi** = accountant, not treasurer.
5. Jagirs were **not hereditary** and carried no judicial/police duty (UPSC 2019: neither statement correct).
6. **Polaj** = every year; **banjar** = 5+ years.
7. Dahsala = **Todar Mal, 1580**.
8. Primary zamindars had **hereditary** rights.
COVERAGE: 1>3; 2>1; 3>2; 4>6; 5>4; 6>4; 7>5; 8>7
