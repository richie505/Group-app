## U0356 | 74TH AMENDMENT – PART IXA, TWELFTH SCHEDULE, TYPES OF URBAN LOCAL BODIES, WARD COMMITTEES, DPC AND MPC
### 1. ROAD TO THE 74TH – FIRSTS AND MILESTONES
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1688 | **First Municipal Corporation – Madras** (charter 30 Dec 1687; began 29 Sep 1688) |
| 1726 | Municipal Corporations of **Bombay and Calcutta** |
| 1870 | **Lord Mayo's** resolution – **financial decentralisation** |
| 1882 | **Lord Ripon's** resolution – "**Magna Carta**" of local self-government; Ripon = Father of Local Self-Government |
| 1919 | GoI Act – local self-government a **Transferred** subject under dyarchy (elected Ministers), not reserved |
| 2 Oct 1959 | Rajasthan (Nagaur) first Panchayati Raj State; AP second |
| 1969 | Planning Commission's **first district-planning guidelines** |
| 1984 | **Hanumantha Rao Committee** – made the **Collector central** to district planning |
| 1989 | Municipalities Bill **lapsed** |
| Sep 1991 | **P.V. Narasimha Rao** government's Bill → became the 74th |
- **Charles Metcalfe** called Indian village communities "**Little Republics**".
- Balwant Rai Mehta, ARC-I, Ashok Mehta and G.V.K. Rao **reduced** the Collector's role.
- Chronology: Madras Corporation → Ripon (1882) → 1919 Act → Rajasthan Panchayati Raj (1959).
!TRAP Mayo 1870 = financial decentralisation; Ripon 1882 = "Magna Carta".
### 2. THE 74TH AMENDMENT ACT, 1992 – DATES AND PROVISIONS
#### 2.1 Enactment
- The **Nagarpalika Act**; passed by both Houses **December 1992**; Presidential assent **April 1993**; in force **1 June 1993**.
- Inserted **Part IXA "The Municipalities"** (Articles **243P to 243ZG**, 18 Articles) and the **Twelfth Schedule** (18 items, Art. 243W).
- Gave **constitutional status to urban local governments**; uniform framework obligatory for States; State laws to conform within **one year** (243ZF).
- 'Local Government' stays in the **State List**.
!TRAP 24 April 1993 = 73rd (Panchayats, National Panchayati Raj Day); **1 June 1993 = 74th** (Municipalities).
#### 2.2 73rd vs 74th
| Basis | 73rd | 74th |
|---|---|---|
| Concerns | Rural – Panchayats | Urban – Municipalities |
| Part | IX (243–243O) | **IXA (243P–243ZG)** |
| Schedule | Eleventh – 29 | **Twelfth – 18** |
| Institutions | Gram Panchayat, Panchayat Samiti, Zila Parishad | Nagar Panchayat, Municipal Council, Municipal Corporation |
- Both are linked to district planning bodies (**DPC 243ZD**, **MPC 243ZE**) – both added by the **74th** (Part IXA).
- The 73rd recognised **Panchayats** (not urban bodies) as self-government; constitutional base of urban government = **Part IX-A only**.
- 73rd/74th created **SEC, DPC and SFC** – not a "State Property Tax Valuation Board". (A keyed answer also lists DPC and SEC under the 73rd.)
#### 2.3 Schedules and Parts added by amendments
| Added | By | Contents |

### 4. COMPULSORY VS VOLUNTARY
| Compulsory | Voluntary (State legislature) |
|---|---|
| Three types of municipalities (243Q) | Representation of MPs, MLAs, MLCs |
| Direct election from wards (243R) | **Manner of electing Chairperson/Mayor** |
| **Ward Committees** (3 lakh+) (243S) | **Reservation for backward classes** |
| SC/ST reservation; women **at least one-third** of seats and chairpersons (243T) | Powers and Twelfth Schedule subjects devolved (243W) |
| **5-year term**; election within **6 months** (243U) | Taxing powers and grants (243X) |
| SFC (243Y); SEC (243ZA) | Committees other than ward committees |
### 5. COMPOSITION, MEMBERSHIP, TERM AND ELECTIONS
#### 5.1 Composition (243R)
- Members **directly elected** from wards. State law may add: persons with **municipal-administration experience (no vote)**; Lok Sabha/Assembly members for the area; Rajya Sabha/Council members registered as electors; chairpersons of committees.
- Chairperson's election – left to the State.
- **Telangana SEC (15 Feb 2026):** only RS members and MLCs registered as voters in the ULB can vote as ex-officio members in Mayor/Chairperson polls.
#### 5.2 Qualifications (243V)
- Minimum age **21** (not 25); disqualifications as under the law for **State Legislature** elections or a State law.
- A councillor who, as a lawyer, acts against the Municipal Council – removed by the **Collector** (keyed answer).
#### 5.3 Term and elections (243U, 243ZA, 243Y)
- **Five years** from the date appointed for its first meeting; **reasonable opportunity of being heard** before dissolution.
- Election before expiry, or **within six months** of dissolution (not one year); not needed if less than six months remain.
- Electoral rolls and polls – **State Election Commission** (same as 243K). Finances – the **243I SFC** also reviews municipalities (243Y).
!PAIR 243K | 243ZA | SEC || 243I | 243Y | SFC
### 6. TYPES OF URBAN LOCAL BODIES
#### 6.1 Article 243Q
| Type | Area |
|---|---|
| **Nagar Panchayat** | **Transitional area** (rural to urban) |
| **Municipal Council** | **Smaller** urban area |
| **Municipal Corporation** | **Larger** urban area – big cities |
- Specified by the **Governor** by public notification, on population, density, revenue, share of non-agricultural employment, economic importance.
- **Industrial township** (243Q proviso): where an **industrial establishment** provides municipal services, no municipality need be constituted.
- Corporation's three authorities: **Council (Mayor)**, **Standing Committees**, **Commissioner**.
#### 6.2 Eight types of ULBs
- **Municipal Corporation, Municipality, Notified Area Committee, Town Area Committee, Cantonment Board, Township, Port Trust, Special Purpose Agency** – NOT Zonal Development Authority.
- **Cantonment Board** – not in 243Q; under **Ministry of Defence**. MoHUA – main ministry for ULBs; Home Affairs – ULBs in UTs.
- Panchayat vs Cantonment Board dispute – decided by **State Government with Central agreement** (s. 79(2), Chhattisgarh Municipalities Act, 1961).
### 7. TWELFTH SCHEDULE – 18 FUNCTIONS
- Urban/town planning; **regulation of land use and building construction**; planning for economic and social development.
- Roads and bridges; **water supply**; **fire services**.
- Public health, sanitation, conservancy, solid waste management; **urban forestry**, environment and ecology.
- Weaker sections incl. disabled; **slum improvement and upgradation**; **urban poverty alleviation**.
- Public amenities (street lighting, parking, bus stops, conveniences).
- **Cattle pounds**, prevention of cruelty to animals; **vital statistics**; **slaughter houses and tanneries**; burial grounds.
| Only Eleventh (Panchayats) | Only Twelfth (Municipalities) |
|---|---|
| Non-conventional energy | Cattle pounds |
| Small-scale industries | Fire services |
| **Technical and vocational education** | Slum improvement |
| Libraries; markets and fairs; PDS | Slaughter houses and tanneries; vital statistics |
!KEY APPSC G2 2025: municipal Twelfth Schedule function = **cattle pounds**.
- The Schedule is not only physical infrastructure – social, environmental, cultural, regulatory too.
- **Public order** is NOT a local self-government concern.
### 8. WARDS COMMITTEES AND AREA SABHAS
| Body | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Wards Committee (243S)** | Mandatory in municipalities of **3 lakh+**; one or more wards; State law decides make-up and area; ward councillor is a member |
| Nagar Panchayat wards committee (keyed) | Some wards; councillor member; two ward residents nominated |
| **Area Sabha** | **Model Nagara Raj Bill (2008)** – all voters of a **polling-booth area**, below the Wards Committee |
| **Community Participation Law** | Mandatory reform under **JNNURM** |
### 9. DPC AND MPC – ARTICLES 243ZD AND 243ZE
| Feature | DPC | MPC |
|---|---|---|
| Article | **243ZD** | **243ZE** |
| Area | **Every district** | Every metropolitan area – **10 lakh+** (may span districts) |
| Elected share | **At least four-fifths** | **Two-thirds** |
| Elected by | Elected members of district panchayat and municipalities | Municipal members and panchayat chairpersons |
| Basis | Rural:urban population ratio | Urban:rural **population** (not area) |
| Plan | Consolidates panchayat + municipal plans → **draft district development plan** | Draft plan for whole metropolitan area |
| Forwards to | **State Government** (by chairperson) | State Government |
- Chairperson's selection – **State law**, not Parliament.
- DPC plan considers spatial planning, water and resource sharing, infrastructure, environment and resources; MPC also Union/State objectives.
- MPC is a **constitutional body**, but has no sole responsibility – it only sends draft plans.
!TRAP APPSC-GS 2018: "DPC provided by 74th; consolidates plans; **all members elected**" – only the first two are correct.
#### 9.1 Working of DPCs
- DPCs weak in most States; **operationalising DPCs** remains a mandate of the **Ministry of Panchayati Raj**.
| State | DPC chaired by |
|---|---|
| **Rajasthan** | **Zila Pramukh** (RPSC 2024) |
| **AP** | **District Collector** (with MPs, MLAs, municipal chairmen) |
| **Kerala** | **District Panchayat President**; Collector only member-secretary; integrates People's Plan Campaign plans |
- UP District Plan includes Nagar Panchayat, Gram Panchayat and Kshetra Panchayat.
### 10. FUNCTIONARIES AND ANDHRA PRADESH ULBS
#### 10.1 Functionary → institution
| Functionary | Institution |
|---|---|
| **Sarpanch** | Gram Panchayat |
| **Pradhan** | Panchayat Samiti (block) |
| **Chairman / Chairperson** | Zilla Parishad; Municipal Council / Nagar Panchayat |
| **Mayor** (with Deputy Mayor) | Municipal Corporation |
| **Municipal Commissioner** | Chief executive of a Corporation; appointed by State (usually IAS) |
- AP: Sarpanch, Mandal Parishad President, ZP Chairperson; Mayor, Municipal Chairperson.
#### 10.2 AP data and laws
- **AP SES 2025-26:** **123 ULBs = 17 Municipal Corporations + 76 Municipalities + 30 Nagar Panchayats**. Older keys: 71 municipalities (APPSC 2018); 17 corporations (G1 2019).
- Department: **MA&UD**; **DIGIT** e-Governance platform.
- **AP Municipalities Act, 1965** (municipalities, Nagar Panchayats); **AP Municipal Corporations Act, 1994** (corporations), which applies the **Hyderabad Municipal Corporations Act, 1955** as its parent law.
- State Government controls ULBs in legislative, financial and personnel matters.
- **2026 developments:**
  - **18 Aug 2026:** Cabinet – **33.33% BC quota** in ULBs (34% in rural bodies); **direct election of Mayors** and chairpersons.
  - **19 Aug 2026:** **G.O. Rt. No. 1065** – BC reservation; polls due in **100 of 123 ULBs**; merger of 23 municipalities deferred.
  - SEC **Anil Chandra Punetha** – ward-wise photo rolls for **41 ULBs** (12 corporations incl. GVMC, Vijayawada, Guntur, Tirupati, Kurnool + 29); qualifying date 1 Jan 2026; rolls due 22 Sep 2026.
  - ULBs may lose **₹12,150 crore** if polls slip; 16th FC grants only to duly constituted ULBs.
#### 10.3 Metropolitan planning in AP
- **VMRDA** – formed 2018 (G.O.Ms. 302, 5 Sep 2018) dissolving **VUDA** (1978); covers Visakhapatnam, Vizianagaram, Anakapalli.
- **APCRDA** – set up December 2014 under the **APCRDA Act (11 of 2014)**; plans and develops the capital region and **Amaravati**.
- ULB finances: spend **< 1% of GDP**, employ ~10% of public workers – "**stepchild**" of federalism; **property tax** main own revenue, under-collected; cities house ~36% of population.
