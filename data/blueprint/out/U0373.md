## U0373 | FINANCIAL REGULATORS – RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA, IBBI, IFSCA – MANDATE AND POWERS
### 1. REGULATORS AT A GLANCE
#### 1.1 Regulator → Act, Set-up, HQ, Ministry
| Regulator | Act | Set up | HQ | Ministry |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **RBI** | RBI Act, 1934 | **1 Apr 1935**; nationalised **1949** | Mumbai | Finance |
| **SEBI** | SEBI Act, 1992 | **12 Apr 1988** (non-statutory, by resolution); statutory 1992 | Mumbai | Finance |
| **IRDAI** | IRDA Act, 1999 | **April 2000** | **Hyderabad** | Finance |
| **PFRDA** | PFRDA Act, 2013 | Interim body 2003; statutory **2014** | New Delhi | Finance |
| **IBBI** | Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 | **1 Oct 2016** | New Delhi | **Corporate Affairs** |
| **IFSCA** | IFSCA Act, 2019 | **27 Apr 2020** | **GIFT City, Gandhinagar** | Finance |
- SEBI regional offices: New Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai, Ahmedabad.
- The **Ministry of Finance** nominates representatives to the boards of SEBI, IRDA and RBI.
- IFSCA also promotes FinTech through regulatory and innovation **sandboxes**.
!TRAP IBBI is the only regulator here under the **Ministry of Corporate Affairs**; all others are under Finance.
!KEY APPSC 2018 answer key: SEBI got statutory status on **12 April 1992**.
### 2. RESERVE BANK OF INDIA
#### 2.1 Functions
- **Monetary authority**; banker and debt manager to government.
- Issues currency **except the ₹1 note and coins** (issued by the **Ministry of Finance**).
- Regulator and supervisor of **banks and NBFCs**.
- **Forex manager** under **FEMA 1999**.
- **Public Debt Management Cell**: set up to reduce RBI's conflict of interest between monetary policy and debt management.
#### 2.2 Recent RBI Actions
- **May 2025**: regulation-making framework – transparency, consultation, periodic review.
- **2026-27**: **17 Upper-Layer NBFCs** named, including **Tata Sons**; must list within **3 years**.
### 3. MONETARY POLICY COMMITTEE AND INFLATION TARGETING
#### 3.1 MPC Composition
| Feature | Fact |
|---|---|
| Basis | Statutory, under the RBI Act, 1934 |
| Size | **6 members** |
| From RBI | 3 – **Governor (chair, casting vote)** + a Deputy Governor + one officer |
| External | 3, appointed by the Centre for **4 years** |
| Job | Fixes the **repo rate** |
#### 3.2 Inflation-Targeting Mandate
- Recommended by the **Urjit Patel Committee (2014)**; adopted **2016**.
- Target **4% CPI-Combined ± 2%** (CPI, not WPI); nominal anchor headline CPI (base year 2024).
- **Price stability** is primary; growth secondary.
- **Sec. 45ZA**: Centre, in consultation with RBI, sets the target **every 5 years**.
- **Sec. 45ZN**: band breached for **3 consecutive quarters** → RBI reports to the Centre with reasons, remedies and time to restore.

### 4. SEBI – MANDATE AND POWERS
- Regulates the **securities market, stock exchanges, listed companies, intermediaries** and **mutual funds**.
- Replaced the **Controller of Capital Issues**.
- **Quasi-judicial** powers: investigates, summons, passes orders and penalties.
#### 4.1 Appeal Route
1. SEBI order
2. **Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT)**
3. **Supreme Court**
- IRDAI and PFRDA orders are also appealable to SAT **since 2014**.
!TRAP Appeal from SEBI goes to **SAT**, not NCLAT.
- **Sep 2025**: **SWAGAT-FI** – single-window framework for trusted **FPIs and FVCIs**, with the India Market Access Portal.
### 5. IRDAI – INSURANCE REGULATOR
#### 5.1 Basis and Origin
- Statutory body under the **IRDA Act, 1999**; apex regulator of **insurance and reinsurance** companies.
- Recommended by the **Malhotra Committee** (set up 1993, reported 1994) – private entry into insurance.
- **Section 114A, Insurance Act 1938**: IRDAI's regulation-making power.
- **Feb 2025**: seven-member expert panel under **Dinesh Khara** set up to review IRDAI regulations.
!TRAP "Which is NOT correct about IRDAI": it was recommended by **Malhotra**, not the **Gadgil** Committee.
#### 5.2 Insurance FDI Cap
| Year | Cap |
|---|---|
| 2000 | 26% |
| 2015 | 49% |
| 2021 | 74% |
| 2025-26 | **100%** |
- **Sabka Bima Sabki Raksha (Amendment of Insurance Laws) Act, 2025**, passed **17 Dec 2025**: up to 100% FDI; added **Sec. 16A** to the IRDA Act – **Policyholders' Education and Protection Fund**.
#### 5.3 Recent IRDAI Actions
- **Ind AS 117 and Ind AS 109** replace IGAAP for insurers; IRDAI enforces adoption.
- **D-SIIs, FY 2025-26**: **LIC, GIC Re, New India Assurance**.
!TRAP HDFC Bank is a **D-SIB** (bank), not a D-SII (insurer).
### 6. PFRDA, IBBI AND IFSCA
- **PFRDA**: pension funds and **NPS**; **NPS e-Shramik** extends pensions to informal workers.
- **IBBI**: regulator under the IBC 2016.
- **BIFR** (SICA 1985): dissolved **1 Dec 2016**; cases moved to **NCLT/NCLAT** under the IBC.
### 7. WHO REGULATES IT NOW – MERGERS AND SHIFTED MANDATES
| Entity | Regulator |
|---|---|
| Forward Markets Commission (commodities) | Merged into **SEBI, Sep 2015** |
| Housing finance companies | **RBI since 2019** (not NHB) |
| NBFCs | RBI |
| Merchant banks, VC funds, brokers | SEBI |
| Mutual funds / NPS | SEBI / PFRDA |
| Insurers | IRDAI |
| **Chit funds** | **State governments** (Chit Funds Act 1982) |
| NaBFID (NaBFID Act 2021) | RBI oversight |
- **NHB** (1988, NHB Act 1987) remains the apex housing refinancer; the Centre took full ownership in **2019**.
!TRAP Chit funds = states, not RBI; FMC → SEBI; HFCs → RBI.
### 8. INVESTOR AND DEPOSITOR PROTECTION BODIES
| Body | Parent / basis |
|---|---|
| **DICGC (1978)** | Wholly-owned subsidiary of **RBI** (not Finance Ministry); insures bank deposits, **not NBFC deposits** |
| **IEPF Authority** | Statutory, Companies Act 2013; **Ministry of Corporate Affairs** |
| Policyholders' Education and Protection Fund | IRDAI, Sec. 16A IRDA Act |
| **Banking Ombudsman** | RBI; began **14 Jun 1995** under **Sec. 35A, Banking Regulation Act**; quasi-judicial |
| **NFRA** | Companies Act 2013; auditing and accounting standards |
### 9. FSDC AND FSLRC
#### 9.1 Financial Stability and Development Council
- Set up **2010**; **non-statutory** apex body; chaired by the **Union Finance Minister** (not RBI Governor). Sub-committee chaired by the RBI Governor.
- Roles: financial stability, **inter-regulatory coordination**, macro-prudential supervision of large conglomerates, financial literacy and inclusion.
#### 9.2 Financial Sector Legislative Reforms Commission
- Chair **Justice B.N. Srikrishna**; set up 2011, report **2013**.
- Proposals: single **Indian Financial Code**; **Unified Financial Agency** (merging SEBI, FMC, IRDA, PFRDA); RBI keeps banking and payments; Financial Redress Agency; **Public Debt Management Agency**; Resolution Corporation; SAT expanded into a Financial Sector Appellate Tribunal.
!TRAP FSLRC did NOT propose a separate constitutional court for financial disputes.
#### 9.3 Current Debate
- **Economic Survey 2025-26** ("Refining the Regulatory Touch"): RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA moving to **anticipatory regulation**.
- Criticism: overlapping mandates; a decade of inflation targeting with mixed evidence on output and jobs.
- AP angle: IRDAI HQ Hyderabad was in the shared AP–Telangana capital until 2024.
