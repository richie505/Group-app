## U0651 | INDIA - OCCUPATIONAL STRUCTURE: MAIN AND MARGINAL WORKERS, WPR, CULTIVATORS AND AGRICULTURAL LABOURERS
### 1. CENSUS WORKER CATEGORIES
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Main worker** | Worked **183+ days** (6 months) in reference year |
| **Marginal worker** | Worked less than 183 days |
| Cultivator | Own or **leased** land |
| Agricultural labourer | **Another's land for wages** |
| Household industry | Production within household (e.g. handloom) |
| Other workers | Factory, trade, transport, services, government |
- Non-workers: students, household duties, dependants, pensioners, rentiers, beggars/vagrants, others (no "permanent industrial employers").
- **Census Town:** 5,000+ population; 75%+ male main workers non-agricultural; density 400+/sq km.
### 2. INDIA, CENSUS 2011
| Category | Share of workers |
|---|---|
| Other workers | **41.6%** (largest) |
| Agricultural labourers | **30.0%** |
| Cultivators | **24.6%** |
| Household industry | 3.8% |
- Workers **4,818.89 lakh**: main **75.2%**, marginal **24.8%**; about 60% non-workers.
- **WPR 39.8%**: male **53.3%**, female **25.5%**.
- 2011: agricultural labourers **outnumbered cultivators for the first time** (2001: cultivators 31.7% > labourers 26.5%).
- Agriculture's share: **58.2% (2001) → 54.6% (2011)**; secondary and tertiary rose. 2011-12: agriculture about 48.9%, services about 27%.
!TRAP "Services employed 36.7% in 2011-12" = NOT true (APPSC-G2 2017).
- Rural SCs: mostly marginal farmers and landless labourers.
### 3. STATE PATTERNS
| Pattern | States |
|---|---|
| High cultivators | **Himachal Pradesh, Nagaland** |
| High agricultural labourers | Bihar, AP, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, Jharkhand, West Bengal, MP |
| High other workers | **Delhi, Chandigarh, Puducherry** |
- WPR higher in less-developed, hill and tribal states - subsistence work needs manual labour.
### 4. PLFS INDICATORS (NSO, MoSPI)
| Indicator | Rule |
|---|---|
| LFPR | (Employed + unemployed) / population x 100 |
| **WPR** | **Employed / total population** x 100 (RPSC 2024) |
| UR | Unemployed / **labour force** |
| UPSS | Employed if worked **30+ days** in 365 days |
| CWS | Employed if worked **1 hour** on any day of the week |
- **Claudia Goldin U-curve:** India on the falling half till 2018-19.
### 5. PLFS DATA
| Release | LFPR | WPR | UR |
|---|---|---|---|
| PLFS 2025 (annual) | 59% | 57% | about 3% |
| Jan-Mar 2026 (CWS, 15+) | 55.5% | 52.8% | **5.0%** |
- Q2 FY26 (Jul-Sep 2025): **agriculture 42.4%** of employment; self-employment 55.8%; casual labour 18.9%.
- Falling UR may show distress entry into low-productivity work; the 1-hour CWS rule can hide underemployment.
### 6. FEMALE WORK PARTICIPATION
- Female LFPR (15+): **23.3% (2017-18)** → 30% (2019-20) → 37% (2022-23) → about 40% (2025).
- Rise: subsistence farming, unpaid family work, hardship, return migration, wider survey coverage.
- Low levels: mechanisation, more schooling of girls, no flexible hours, distance, safety, family duties.
- Women (2011): **55%** agricultural labourers, 24% cultivators, only **12.8%** of operational holdings; men's out-migration → **feminisation of farm work**.
### 7. ANDHRA PRADESH
- Workers **230.81 lakh**; WPR **about 46.6%** (÷ 495.77 lakh) - above India.
- Shares: **agricultural labourers 47.9%**, other workers 34.8%, cultivators 14.5%, household industry 2.9%.
- Main agricultural labourers **86.12 lakh** of 193.17 lakh main workers (about 45%); nearly **1 in 10** of India's.
- Districts (APPSC-G2 2017): **Chittoor** highest cultivators; **West Godavari** highest agricultural labourers.
- PLFS 2023-24: AP LFPR **47.5%**, WPR 45.6% (India 45.1, 43.7); female LFPR **35.8%** (India 31.7%); urban young women WPR **15.9%**; rural female WPR higher (MGNREGS).
### 8. INFORMAL SECTOR AND PROGRAMMES
- **e-Shram** (26 Aug 2021): database of unorganised workers, 12-digit UAN; **31.89 crore** registered (18 Aug 2026).
- **MGNREGA:** Act 2005; launched **2 Feb 2006 at Bandlapalli, Anantapur (AP)**; 100 days.
- **VB-G RAM G Act, 2025** replaced MGNREGA from **1 Jul 2026**: **125 days** guaranteed work.
- **PMEGP (2008):** merged PMRY + REGP; nodal agency **KVIC** (not NABARD).
