## U0726 | PROPULSION AND FUTURE VEHICLES - CRYOGENIC, SEMI-CRYOGENIC, RLV, SCRAMJET
### 1. PROPELLANT CATEGORIES
| Category | Example | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| Solid | **HTPB** (S200, first stages) | High initial thrust; **cannot be throttled or stopped** |
| Earth-storable liquid | **UH25 + N2O4**; hydrazine, MMH, UDMH | Throttleable, restartable |
| Hypergolic | **Hydrazine family + N2O4** | **Ignite on contact**; spacecraft manoeuvring; highly toxic |
| Cryogenic | **LH2 + LOX** | Most efficient |
| Semi-cryogenic | **Refined kerosene + LOX** | Dense, cheaper, non-toxic |
| Hybrid | Solid fuel + liquid oxidiser | **Can be stopped/restarted** |
### 2. CRYOGENIC TECHNOLOGY
- **Cryogenics**: Greek **'Kryos'** (frost); below about **-150°C**; uses liquid nitrogen, hydrogen, oxygen.
- Fuel **LH2 (-253°C)**, oxidiser **LOX (-183°C)**; exhaust only **water vapour**; Isp ~**450 s** (highest).
- LH2 density **0.071 g/ml**: huge tanks, cannot be stored long, so **unfit for military missiles**.
- Used in **rocket upper stages** and the **space shuttle** (Discovery: LH2 + LOX tanks).
- **Glavkosmos deal (1991)** → US **MTCR** sanctions (**1992**) stopped technology transfer → Russia supplied **7 engines** only.
- **CUSP** (Cryogenic Upper Stage Project): **1994**, **Rs 300 crore**; full stage test **2006**; **GSLV-D5** flew the indigenous CE-7.5 on **5 Jan 2014**.
- India = **6th** country (after US, Russia, France, Japan, China).
!TRAP India is NOT 4th in cryogenic technology - Europe and Japan came earlier.

### 5. ELECTRIC PROPULSION AND POEM
- **300 mN Stationary Plasma Thruster**: **Hall-type, xenon**; **1,000-hour** life test; Isp ~**6x** chemical; for **communication satellites**, not boosters.
- **POEM** = PSLV Orbital Experimental Module: spent **PS4** stage as orbital platform for start-up and academic payloads.
### 6. RAMJET, SCRAMJET, HSTDV
| Engine | Combustion | Speed |
|---|---|---|
| Ramjet | **Subsonic** airflow | Above **Mach 2** |
| Scramjet | **Supersonic** airflow | **Mach 5-6+** |
- Uses **atmospheric oxygen** → less oxidiser carried (still needs fuel).
- **ATV** (ISRO): scramjet flight test **Aug 2016**, Sriharikota; India **4th** (after US, Russia, China).
- **HSTDV** = **DRDO**; actively cooled full-scale combustor ran **20 minutes** on endothermic fuel (May 2026).
### 7. REUSABLE AND NEXT-GENERATION VEHICLES
- **RLV-TD "Pushpak"**: winged; completed **LEX-03**; aim **TSTO**; cost cut up to **80%**; needs a reusable **Thermal Protection System**.
- **NGLV "Soorya"**: **20,000 kg to LEO** (not 10,000; ~2.5x LVM3); semi-cryogenic; **Third Launch Pad**; for BAS (2035), lunar landing (2040).
