## U0531 | NPA RESOLUTION – SARFAESI, DRTs, IBC 2016, NARCL, PROMPT CORRECTIVE ACTION
### 1. NPA BASICS AND STRESS INDICATORS
- **NPA**: interest or principal overdue **more than 90 days**; SMA-0/1/2 (up to 90 days) still standard; sub-standard ≤12 months, doubtful >12 months, loss = identified but not written off.
- **ICR** = EBIT ÷ interest payable; **below 1** = cannot service interest. **Evergreening** hides NPAs; the **AQR (2015)** exposed it. **Twin balance sheet** = over-leveraged firms + capital-starved banks.
- **PCR** = provisions ÷ gross NPA; higher PCR → more resilient bank. SCB GNPA **1.93%**, NNPA 0.39% (Mar 2026, decadal low); fall due to 4R, provisioning, IBC, NARCL.
### 2. RECOVERY CHANNELS
| Channel | Law | Key point | FY24 share |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lok Adalats** | **Legal Services Authorities Act 1987** | Small NPAs, compromise | 3% |
| **DRTs** | **RDDBFI Act 1993** | Debts **above ₹20 lakh**; 39 DRTs, appeal to **5 DRATs** | 17% |
| **SARFAESI** | **2002** | Secured lenders, **no court** | 32% |
| **IBC** | **2016** | NCLT-led, time-bound | **48%** |
### 3. SARFAESI ACT 2002 AND ARCs
- Based on the **Narasimham Committee**. **Sec 13(2)**: lenders with **60%** of dues issue **60-day** notice; **Sec 13(8)**: redeem only by paying **full dues** (not 25%); **Sec 17**: appeal to **DRT within 45 days**.
- **ARCs** buy NPAs at a discount against security receipts; regulated by **RBI (not SEBI)**.
### 4. EARLIER RESTRUCTURING SCHEMES
| Scheme | Year | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **CDR** | 2001 | Out-of-court multi-bank restructuring |
| **5/25** | 2014 | Infra loans over 25 years, refinanced every 5 |
| **SDR** | 2015 | Debt → **51%+ equity** |
| **S4A** | 2016 | Split **sustainable vs unsustainable** debt; ended Feb 2018 |
| **Project Sashakt** (Sunil Mehta) | 2018 | **ICA** (July 2018, 33 banks, loans ₹50 crore+); binds all once **66%** approve |
| **Prudential Framework** | 7 June 2019 | 30-day review; ICA mandatory; resolve in **180 days** |
### 5. IBC 2016
- Enacted **28 May 2016** on the **T.K. Viswanathan Committee (2015)**; **debtor-in-possession → creditor-in-control**; replaced SICA 1985/BIFR.
- **Insolvency** = financial state of not paying debts when due; **bankruptcy** = legal declaration/process.
- **CIRP**: default **₹1 crore**; **Committee of Creditors** votes; **180 + 90**, outer **330 days**. **Sec 29A** bars wilful defaulters. **PPIRP for MSMEs (2021): 120 days**.
- Waterfall: resolution costs, workmen, secured creditors above government dues. **Lalit Kumar Jain (2021)**: personal guarantors stay liable.
- **IBC (Amendment) Act 2026**: admit/reject in **14 days**; plans in 30 days; liquidation 180 + 90; **CIIRP 150 days**, 51% creditors (Secs 54C-54P); "may" → "shall" in Sec 7(5)(a) (overturns **Vidarbha Industries**).
### 6. IBC INSTITUTIONS
| Institution | Role |
|---|---|
| **NCLT** (Sec 408, Companies Act 2013) | Companies and LLPs; replaced CLB, BIFR, AAIFR; bench at **Amaravati** |
| **NCLAT** (Sec 410) | Appeals from NCLT (Sec 61), IBBI, CCI, NFRA; Delhi + Chennai → Supreme Court |
| **DRT** | **Individuals and partnership firms** |
| **IBBI** | Regulates insolvency professionals, agencies, information utilities, valuers |
### 7. NARCL AND IDRCL
- **NARCL** ("bad bank", **Budget 2021-22**) aggregates loans **above ₹500 crore** (~₹2 lakh crore); **PSBs hold majority**.
- Pays **15% cash + 85% security receipts**; Govt guarantee **₹30,600 crore for 5 years**. **IDRCL** (majority private) resolves.
### 8. PROMPT CORRECTIVE ACTION
- **PCA = Prompt Corrective Action** (APPSC-GS 2019): RBI, **2002**; revised Nov 2021.
- Triggers: **capital** (CRAR/CET1), **asset quality** (net NPA), **leverage** (Tier 1 leverage ratio).
- Curbs: lending, branches, dividends, management pay; RBI may order capital infusion, management change, merger.
### 9. WILFUL DEFAULTERS AND FRAUD
- RBI Master Direction (30 July 2024): examine wilful default in NPAs of **₹25 lakh+**; classify within **six months**; no restructuring. **Fugitive Economic Offenders Act 2018**: ₹100 crore+.
