## U0384 | CASTE IN MODERN INDIA – DOMINANT CASTE, SANSKRITISATION, CASTE ASSOCIATIONS, CASTE AND POLITICS
### 1. SANSKRITISATION AND MOBILITY
- **Sanskritisation** (M.N. Srinivas): lower caste adopts rituals, vegetarianism, sacred thread of the twice-born; **positional, not structural** change.
- **Kshatriyaisation** (**Kulke**): Marathas, Jats, Yadavs, Thevars, Nairs claim warrior status.
- **Census petitions**: the colonial Census of **1871–72** ranked castes → groups formed associations to seek higher classification.
### 2. DOMINANT CASTE
- Coined by **M.N. Srinivas** from **Rampura village, Mysore** (1955).
- Traditional criteria: sizeable **arable land**, **numerical strength**, high **ritual status**.
- Later additions: Western education, administrative jobs, urban income.
- Pre-independence **Bihar**: Bhumihar, Rajput, Kayastha (**not Kurmi**).
!TRAP Mahuva = I.P. Desai (joint family), not Srinivas.
### 3. DOMINANT CASTES OF ANDHRA PRADESH
- **Reddys, Kammas, Velamas, Kapus** = martial, agrarian dominant castes; Kayasthas = administrative elite.
- Medieval Andhra: these castes **Kshatriyaised** through the **Nayankara** system.
- **Samayas** = Kakatiya-era caste associations.
- **Justice Party**: intermediate castes (Kammas, Reddys, Naidus); non-Brahmin movement → **Communal G.O. 1921** (Madras Presidency).
### 4. CASTE ASSOCIATIONS
- Voluntary, membership-based body of a caste acting as a **pressure group**.
- **Lloyd and Susanne Rudolph**, *The Modernity of Tradition* (**1967**): caste associations = **agents of democratisation** (**Nadars, Vanniyars**); coined **"bullock capitalists"**.
- Marathas, Gujjars, Patels seek OBC status.
### 5. CASTE AND POLITICS
- **Rajni Kothari** edited *Caste in Indian Politics* (**1970**).
- **Minto-Morley Reforms 1909** (separate electorates): root of vote-bank politics.
- **Jaffrelot**, *India's Silent Revolution*: lower castes rise through politics.
- Conflict now about **power**, not ritual status.
!KEY RAS/RTS 2013 A-R: castes are politicised and casteism in politics is rising – both true, but one does not explain the other.
### 6. FORCES OF CHANGE
| Force | Effect |
|---|---|
| Modern education | equality values; fall of Brahmin supremacy |
| Urbanisation | food taboos weaken |
| Reservation / Mandal | OBCs put at **52%+**; identity reinforced |
| Land reforms | **Gramdan** failed in dominant-caste heartlands |
- **Article 17** abolishes untouchability (first constitutional provision against it); only **24** PCR Act cases in 2023.

### 8. CASTE CENSUS DEBATES
- First Census **1872** (Lord Mayo, non-synchronous); first synchronous **1881**.
- **1931**: last full caste count; since **1951** only SCs and STs.
- **SECC 2011**: **Rural Development Ministry**, outside **Census Act 1948**; ~**46 lakh** caste names; data never released.
- **Bihar survey** (2 Oct 2023): EBCs **36%**, OBCs **27%** (together 63%).
- **Telangana SEEEPC** (Apr 2026): SCs and STs 3 times more backward than general castes; BCs 2.7 times.
- **Census 2027**: caste count approved **30 Apr 2025**; pre-test **6–20 Jul 2026** (open column); Population Enumeration **Feb 2027**; first digital Census, first caste count since 1931.
- Disputes: Centre dropped OBC lists as "classes, not castes"; Opposition wanted the questionnaire scrapped; **SC upheld** the count.
