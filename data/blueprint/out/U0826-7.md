### 2. PADMA AWARDS AND BHARAT RATNA
#### 2.1 The Padma Ladder
| Award | Rank among civilian awards | Given for |
|---|---|---|
| Bharat Ratna | 1st (highest) | — |
| **Padma Vibhushan** | **2nd** | Exceptional and distinguished service |
| **Padma Bhushan** | **3rd** | Distinguished service of a high order |
| **Padma Shri** | **4th** | Distinguished service in any field |
- Started **1954**; announced on **Republic Day**; given by the **President** at **Rashtrapati Bhavan** (around March–April).
- **Balaji Raghavan case (1996)**: Padma awards are **not titles under Article 18**; cannot be used as a prefix or suffix.
- **Second investiture** of the 2026 Padma awards: **Ganatantra Mandap**, Rashtrapati Bhavan.
