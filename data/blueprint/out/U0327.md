## U0327 | INTER-STATE RELATIONS – INTER-STATE COUNCIL, ZONAL COUNCILS, RIVER-WATER DISPUTES (ARTICLE 262), FULL FAITH AND CREDIT
### 1. ARTICLES AT A GLANCE
| Article | Content |
|---|---|
| **261** | Full faith and credit to public acts, records and judicial proceedings |
| **262(1)** | Parliament may provide for adjudication of inter-State river water disputes |
| **262(2)** | Parliament may bar the **Supreme Court and all other courts** |
| **263** | **President** may establish an **Inter-State Council** |
| 131 | SC original jurisdiction – **excludes water disputes** |
| 301 | Freedom of trade, commerce and intercourse throughout India |
| 302 / 303 | Parliament may restrict in public interest / no discrimination |
| 304(b) | State bill restricting inter-State trade needs **President's previous sanction** |
| 306 | Part B States – **repealed** |
| 307 | Authority for Arts 301-304 (e.g. Inter-State Commerce Commission) |
!TRAP Art. 262 = river water disputes; Art. 263 = Inter-State Council; Art. 131 excludes water disputes.
### 2. FULL FAITH AND CREDIT – ARTICLE 261
- **261(1):** public acts, records and judicial proceedings of the **Union and every State** get **full faith and credit** throughout India.
- **261(2):** manner of proof and their effect – decided by **Parliament** by law.
- **261(3):** **final judgments or orders of civil courts** can be **executed anywhere** in India according to law.
- Modelled on the **US** Full Faith and Credit Clause.
### 3. INTER-STATE TRADE AND COMMERCE – ARTS 301-307
- **Art. 301:** freedom of trade, commerce and intercourse throughout India.
- **Art. 302:** Parliament may impose restrictions in **public interest**.
- **Art. 304(b):** State may impose reasonable restrictions, but the bill needs the **previous sanction of the President** (PYQ 2018).
- **Art. 306** (Part B States) repealed; **Art. 307** – authority for Arts 301-304.
### 4. INTER-STATE COUNCIL (ART. 263)
#### 4.1 Origin and Nature
- **Established by the President** – Presidential order of **28 May 1990** (APPSC-G1 2017).
- Recommended by the **Sarkaria Commission**: a permanent body called the "**Inter-Governmental Council**" (RPSC 2023).
- Earlier backers: **ARC (1966-69)** and **Rajamannar Committee (1969)**.
- Sarkaria also proposed renaming the NDC as **National Economic and Development Council** under Art. 263.
- **Constitutional, recommendatory** body – not judicial; purpose: investigate and discuss subjects of common interest (Union-States, among States) and recommend policy coordination.
- Decides all questions by **consensus**; meetings **in camera**; to meet at least **3 times a year**, but has met only about 11 times since 1990.
#### 4.2 Composition
| Post | Holder |
|---|---|
| **Chairman** | **Prime Minister** |
| Members | CMs of all States and of **UTs with legislatures** |
| Members | Administrators of UTs without assemblies |
| Members | **6 Union Cabinet ministers** nominated by the PM |
| Members (only then) | **Governors of States under President's Rule** |
| **Standing Committee** chair | **Union Home Minister** |
| Secretariat | Inter-State Council Secretariat (MHA) |
- Other Art. 263 councils: **Central Council of Health**, Central Council of Local Self-Government, **Transport Development Council**.
!KEY ISC chairman = PM; Standing Committee = Union Home Minister.
### 5. ZONAL COUNCILS
- Statutory bodies under **Sections 15-22 of the States Reorganisation Act, 1956**; idea of **PM Nehru** (1956); set up 1957.
- **Five** zonal councils: Northern, Central, Eastern, Western, Southern.
- **Chairman:** **Union Home Minister** (each council).
- **Vice-Chairman:** a **CM by annual rotation**.
- Members: CMs, **2 other ministers per State**, UT Administrators/LGs.
- **Deliberative and advisory**; advice **not binding**; criticised as only recommendatory and needing more resources.
#### 5.1 Southern Zonal Council
- Members: **Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala, Telangana, Puducherry**.
- 29th meeting at **Tirupati**, chaired by Amit Shah.
- **31st meeting – 20 Aug 2026, Tamil Nadu**: Amit Shah urged early settlement of southern water disputes and river-linking. Telangana to raise Karnataka's unauthorised Krishna projects and seek national status for **Kaleshwaram** and **Palamuru-Rangareddy** (Aug 2026).
!TRAP Zonal Council chairman = Union Home Minister, not the PM; vice-chairman = CM by rotation.
### 6. NORTH EASTERN COUNCIL
- Set up under the **North Eastern Council Act, 1971** (statutory).
- **2002 amendment:** **Sikkim** added as the **8th** member State; NEC became a **regional planning body**; President nominates **3** members.
- Members: **Governors and CMs** of the 8 NE States.
- Chairman: Union Home Minister; Vice-Chairman: Minister of DoNER.
### 7. CONSTITUTIONAL VS NON-CONSTITUTIONAL BODIES
| Body | Status | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Inter-State Council | **Constitutional** | Art. 263 |
| River water tribunals | Statutory | ISRWD Act, 1956 (under Art. 262) |
| Zonal Councils | **Statutory** | States Reorganisation Act, 1956 |
| North Eastern Council | Statutory | NEC Act, 1971 |
| NDC | **Non-constitutional, non-statutory** | Executive resolution |
### 8. RIVER WATER DISPUTES – ARTICLE 262
#### 8.1 Laws
- **Inter-State River Water Disputes Act, 1956** – provides tribunals; a State complains to the **Central Government** under **Section 3**.
- **River Boards Act, 1956** (not 1970) – **no board ever set up** under it.
#### 8.2 Tribunals
| Tribunal | Set up | States |
|---|---|---|
| **Godavari** | 1969 | Maharashtra, Karnataka, AP, MP, Odisha |
| First Krishna (KWDT-I) | 1969 | Maharashtra, Karnataka, AP |
| Narmada | 1969 | Rajasthan, Gujarat, MP, Maharashtra |
| **Cauvery** | 1990 | Karnataka, Kerala, TN, Puducherry |
| Second Krishna (KWDT-II) | **2004** | Maharashtra, Karnataka, AP |
| **Vansadhara** | **2010** | **Odisha, AP** |
| **Mahadayi** | **2010** | **Goa, Karnataka, Maharashtra** |
!KEY Tribunals involving AP: Godavari, Krishna (I and II), Vansadhara.
#### 8.3 Mullaperiyar
- Dam (**1895**, Periyar) in **Kerala** but run by **TN** under a lease; SC set up a supervisory committee involving the **NDSA** (Dam Safety Act 2021).
### 9. KRISHNA AND GODAVARI – AP AND TELANGANA
- KWDT-I allotted **811 tmcft** to undivided AP; divided **512 (AP) : 299 (Telangana)** – a **66:34** ad hoc ratio from 2014-15.
- Centre set terms of reference for KWDT-II on the AP-Telangana dispute in **October 2023**.
- **KRMB** and **GRMB** set up under the **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014**, with an **Apex Council**.
- Disputes: control of boards, unapproved projects (**Rayalaseema Lift**), hydel generation.
- **Jan 2026 (first CWC-chaired inter-State committee):** AP rejected Telangana's **50:50** Krishna demand as against KWDT awards and Apex Council decisions; AP objected to **968 tmcft** of Godavari water for Telangana projects not quantified under the GWDT award.
