## U0766 | ENVIRONMENTAL LEGISLATION IN INDIA – WATER ACT 1974, AIR ACT 1981, ENVIRONMENT (PROTECTION) ACT 1986
### 1. CHRONOLOGY OF ENVIRONMENTAL ACTS
| Year | Act / body | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| 1972 | Wildlife (Protection) Act | Central Zoo Authority set up under it (1992) |
| **1974** | **Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act** | **India's first pollution-control law**; in force 23 March 1974; created the Boards |
| **1977** | **Water (P&CP) Cess Act** | Cess on water used by industries and local bodies; Cess Rules 1978 |
| 1980 | Forest (Conservation) Act | Central approval for diverting forest land |
| **1981** | **Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act** | Followed the Water Act framework |
| **1986** | **Environment (Protection) Act** | Umbrella law |
| 1991 | Public Liability Insurance Act | Relief for hazardous-substance accidents |
| 2002 | Biological Diversity Act | – |
| **2010** | **National Green Tribunal Act** | Specialised tribunal; latest in the chain |
- Amendments: Water Act **1988**; Air Act **1987** (and 1988).
!KEY Order: Water 1974 → Water Cess 1977 → Forest 1980 → Air 1981 → EPA 1986 → PLI 1991 → NGT 2010.
!TRAP The CPCB (1974) came before the Air Act (1981).
### 2. CONSTITUTIONAL PROVISIONS
| Provision | Covers | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| **Article 21** | Right to life – read by courts to include a clean and healthy environment | Fundamental Right |
| **Article 48A** | **State** shall protect and improve the environment and safeguard forests and wildlife | DPSP |
| **Article 51A(g)** | **Citizens** shall protect and improve the natural environment and show **compassion for living creatures** | Fundamental Duty |
| Article 252 | Parliament legislates for States that pass resolutions | Basis of **Water Act** |
| Article 253 | Laws to implement international agreements | Basis of **EPA** |
- Articles 48A and 51A(g) were inserted by the **42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976**.
!PAIR Article 48A | DPSP | Duty of the State || Article 51A(g) | Fundamental Duty | Duty of the citizen
!TRAP Water Act = Article 252; EPA = Article 253.
### 3. WATER ACT 1974 AND WATER CESS ACT 1977
#### 3.1 Water Act 1974
- Purpose: **prevent and control water pollution** and **maintain or restore the wholesomeness of water**.
- Passed under **Article 252** – applies in States that adopt it by resolution.
- Created the **Central Board for the Prevention and Control of Water Pollution (1974)** – renamed **CPCB** by the **1988 amendment** – and the State Boards.
- Structure: **8 chapters, 64 sections**; standards for **discharge of effluents**; prior consent of the Board needed before discharging effluent.
- **Section 33A**: directions, including **environmental compensation**.
- Penalties: **1.5 to 6 years' jail**. Rules: Water Rules 1975.
#### 3.2 Water Cess Act 1977
- Cess on water consumed by specified industries and local authorities – funds the Boards.
- Proceeds go to the **Consolidated Fund of India**; subsumed in **GST in 2017**.
### 4. AIR ACT 1981
- Purpose: **prevention, control and abatement of air pollution**; maintain **air quality standards** (APPSC G2 2025); Central and State Boards set and enforce standards.
- Enacted **29 March 1981**; in force **16 May 1981**; modelled on the Water Act.
- Structure: **7 chapters, 54 sections**.
- The existing **CPCB got air functions**; SPCBs enforce in States.
- **Noise** added to the definition of "air pollutant" by the **1987 amendment** (not 1991).
- **Air pollution control areas**: declared by the **State Government** – Chapter IV, Sections 19-31; a special power of the Air Act, not the Water Act.
- Emission standards for industry and automobiles. **Section 31A**: directions; Boards can levy compensation.
#### 4.1 Water Act vs Air Act
| Feature | Water Act 1974 | Air Act 1981 |
|---|---|---|
| Aim | Wholesomeness of water | Prevent, control, abate air pollution |
| Structure | 8 chapters, 64 sections | 7 chapters, 54 sections |
| Basis | Article 252 | Modelled on Water Act |
| Boards | Created CPCB and SPCBs | Same Boards given air functions |
| Special power | Effluent discharge standards | Air pollution control areas |
| Amended | 1988 (CPCB name) | 1987 (noise added) |
| Compensation | Section 33A | Section 31A |
- Common: both require **prior consent** of the Board to establish and operate a polluting unit.
### 5. ENVIRONMENT (PROTECTION) ACT 1986
#### 5.1 Background and Nature
- Trigger: **Bhopal Gas Tragedy (December 1984)**; gives effect to the **Stockholm Conference, 1972**.
- Basis: **Article 253**. Purpose: **protect and improve the environment and control pollution** (APPSC 2025).
- Called **umbrella legislation** – coordinates central and state authorities under the Water and Air Acts; the **Central Government** is the primary authority. Last amendment mentioned: 1991.
#### 5.2 Key Sections and Powers
| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| **3** | Centre's powers – emission/discharge standards, **location of industries**, hazardous waste, public health; supports EIA and CRZ notifications |
| **3(3)** | Constitute **authorities** |
| **5** | **Directions, including closure** |
| **15-21** | Penalties |
| **16** | **Environment Protection Fund** |
- Authorities under Section 3(3): **Central Ground Water Authority (January 1997)** – regulates groundwater development; **National Ganga River Basin Authority (2009)**.
#### 5.3 Environment Protection Fund
- In the **Public Account**; holds fines under the Air, Water and EP Acts; **75% to States/UTs, 25% to Centre**.
- **Environmental (Protection) Fund Rules, 2026** notified by MoEFCC.
!TRAP Water Cess → Consolidated Fund of India; EP Fund → Public Account.
#### 5.4 Notifications and Rules under EPA
| Notification | Key point |
|---|---|
| **Doon Valley (1989)** | No industry burning over **24 MT** coal/fuel a day |
| Revdanda Creek (1989) | No industries around the creek |
| **CRZ (1991)** | Regulates coastal stretches; no ash/waste dumping; CRZ under EPA confirmed (Jul 2026) |
| **Dhanu Taluka (1991)** | Ecologically fragile; **no power plants** |
| **EIA (1994, revised 2006)** | Prior environmental clearance |
| Eco-Sensitive Zones; Plastic Waste Management Rules | Notified under EPA |
| **Green Credit Rules (2023)** | Voluntary tree planting on degraded land earns Green Credits |
- Green Credit afforestation can be swapped for compensatory afforestation under Van (S&S) Rules 2023; guidelines 16 Sep 2025.
### 6. PENALTIES AND JAN VISHWAS ACT 2023
- EPA original penalty: up to **5 years or Rs 1 lakh**, plus **Rs 5,000 per day** for continuing offence.
- **Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2023** **decriminalised** the EPA penalty regime – monetary penalties fixed by an **adjudicating officer**; appeals go to the **NGT**. The Water and Air Acts were also amended.
- Minor offences decriminalised by amendment in 2024.
- **PLI Act 1991** (amended by Jan Vishwas): penalty instead of imprisonment; penalty rates moved to the rules; Environmental Relief Fund money can go to CPCB/SPCBs for restoration; relief for death raised from **Rs 25,000 to Rs 5 lakh**.
!TRAP The 5 years / Rs 1 lakh / Rs 5,000 per day figures belong to the EPA; Water Act jail is 1.5-6 years.
### 7. CPCB, SPCBs AND MoEFCC
#### 7.1 CPCB
- **Statutory body**, constituted **September 1974** under the **Water Act**; under **MoEFCC**; gives technical services to the Ministry under EPA 1986.
- National level; deals with water (1974 Act) and air (1981 Act) pollution; an **enforcement body under the EPA framework**.
- Functions: **coordinates SPCBs**, technical assistance, research, training, national monitoring.
| Item | CPCB | SPCB |
|---|---|---|
| Level | National | State |
| Chairperson nominated by | **Central Government** | **State Government** |
| Functions | Coordination, standards, research | Inspection, sample analysis, **granting consents** |
- Monitoring: **NAMP** – 1,646 stations in 603 cities (March 2026); **National AQI 2015**; **SAMEER app** gives real-time air quality (SAFAR is a separate forecasting system); **AUM Photonic** – indigenous real-time remote air monitoring.
- **NCAP (2019)**: **40% PM10 cut by 2025-26** over 2019-20 in **130 cities, 24 States/UTs**.
#### 7.2 MoEF / MoEFCC
- **Department of Environment 1980 → Ministry of Environment and Forests 1985 → MoEFCC 2014**.
- Bodies: CPCB (statutory), Central Zoo Authority (1992, under WPA 1972).
### 8. AP POLLUTION CONTROL BOARD
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Set up | **1976** under the Water Act as the State Water Board; later renamed APPCB (APPSC 2022) |
| Head office | **Vijayawada**; **3 Zonal and 13 Regional** offices |
| Laws implemented | Water, Air and EP Acts + hazardous, bio-medical, plastic and solid waste rules |
| Other functions | **Siting guidelines** for industries; SOPs for marine outfall monitoring and hazardous waste |
| Online consent | **OCMMS** (Online Consent Management and Monitoring System) – faster, transparent consents |
| Ease of doing business | Computerised inspection allocation – **high-risk industries inspected more often** |
- Davis Instruments Airlinks is NOT among the APPCB scientific division machines.
- **Environmental compensation**: SC (Aug 2025) upheld PCB power under **S.33A Water Act / S.31A Air Act**, only where harm is clear or imminent.
### 9. CONSENT MECHANISM AND INDUSTRY CATEGORIES
#### 9.1 CTE and CTO
- Two-stage consent from the **SPCB**: **Consent to Establish (CTE)** before setting up → **Consent to Operate (CTO)** before production.
- **29 Jan 2026 reform**: single application for Air and Water consents plus waste authorisations; **CTO valid until cancelled** (earlier periodic renewal).
#### 9.2 Colour Categories
| Category | Pollution Index | Level |
|---|---|---|
| **Red** | **60 and above** | Highly polluting (cement, petrochemicals) |
| Orange | 41-59 | Moderate |
| Green | 21-40 | Low |
| **White** | **20 or less** | Practically non-polluting – no consent/EC needed |
| **Blue** (new) | – | **Essential environmental services**, e.g. Compressed Biogas |
- Basis: pollution potential from **emissions, effluents and hazardous waste** (CPCB, Jun 2026).
### 10. JUDICIAL PRINCIPLES AND CASES
#### 10.1 Principles → Case
| Principle | Meaning | Landmark case |
|---|---|---|
| **Absolute liability** | Hazardous industry liable for all harm; no exceptions of strict liability | **M.C. Mehta v. Union of India – Oleum gas leak** (Shriram plant, Delhi, 1985; Bench led by CJ Bhagwati) |
| **Polluter pays** | Polluter bears cost of preventing and remedying damage | **Indian Council for Enviro-Legal Action (1996)** – Bichhri, Udaipur, H-acid sludge; first SC application |
| **Precautionary principle** | Act to prevent harm even without full certainty | **Vellore Citizens Welfare Forum (1996)** – tanneries; both principles part of sustainable development and law of the land |
| **Public trust doctrine** | State holds air, waters, forests in trust for the public | **M.C. Mehta v. Kamal Nath** – motel on the **Beas**; lease quashed |
#### 10.2 Recent Cases and Orders
| Case | Holding |
|---|---|
| **Vanashakti v. UoI** (31 Jul 2026) | SC nullified the **2021 Office Memorandum** on **ex post facto ECs** on paying penalty; ruling prospective; orders cannot override EIA 2006; ex post facto EC only via **Article 142** in fit cases |
| **M.K. Ranjitsinh v. UoI** | **CSR** inherently includes environmental protection |
| NGT suo motu (15 Jan 2026) | Notice to Rajasthan, MP, UP over sewage in drinking water; chairperson **Prakash Shrivastava** |
