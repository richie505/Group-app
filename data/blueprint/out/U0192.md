## U0192 | RAMAKRISHNA PARAMAHANSA AND SWAMI VIVEKANANDA; RAMAKRISHNA MISSION; THEOSOPHICAL SOCIETY
### 1. RAMAKRISHNA PARAMAHANSA (1836–1886)
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Original name | **Gadadhar Chattopadhyaya** |
| Role | Mystic and **priest** of the **Dakshineswar Kali Temple**, near Calcutta |
| Guru | **Totapuri** |
| Wife | **Sarada Devi** (married 1859); after 1902 the advisory head of the young monastic order |
| Chief disciple | **Swami Vivekananda** |
- Teaching: **"As many faiths, so many paths"** – all religions lead to the same truth; **Hari, Ram, Allah, Christ** = different names of one God (unity of religions).
- Paths to salvation: **devotion, meditation and renunciation** (devotion included).
!TRAP Gadadhar Chattopadhyaya = **Ramakrishna**; Narendranath Datta = **Vivekananda**.
### 2. SWAMI VIVEKANANDA (1863–1902)
#### 2.1 Life and Titles
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Birth name | **Narendranath Datta** |
| Born | **Calcutta, 12 January 1863**; died **1902** |
| National Youth Day | His birthday, **12 January** |
| Titles | **"Prophet of Indian Nationalism"**, **"Maker of Modern India"**; best representative of **Neo-Hinduism** (later 19th century) |
| Western disciple | **Margaret Noble (Sister Nivedita)** – "The British Empire is rotten to the core..." |
| Vedanta Societies | USA and Britain – **Vedanta Society of New York (1894)** |
#### 2.2 Order of Events
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Dec 1892 | Meditated on the **Kanyakumari rock** |
| Feb 1893 | Spoke at **Mahboob College, Secunderabad** |
| **11 Sep 1893** | **Chicago**: "**Sisters and Brothers of America**" |
| 1894 | Vedanta Society of New York |

| 1897 | Returned via **Colombo, Ramnad, Madras** |

#### 2.3 Ideas
- **Practical Vedanta**: serving the poor = serving God – **"Daridra Narayana"** (God manifest in the poor); "**Service to mankind is service to God**".
- Made **humanitarian relief a monastic duty**.
- Stressed **strength, self-confidence and youth** for national regeneration.
- **Condemned caste discrimination and empty ritual**; philosophy = pure Vedanta, truth of all religions.

### 5. RAMAKRISHNA MISSION, 1897
- Founded **1 May 1897** by **Swami Vivekananda**, at **Balaram Bose's house, Baghbazar, Calcutta**, soon after his return from the West; **named after his guru**.
- HQ: **Belur Math**, on the **Hooghly**, **Howrah** (est. **1898**) – permanent HQ of the Ramakrishna Order; architecture blends Hindu, Islamic and Christian motifs (unity of religions).
- Motto: **"Atmano Mokshartham Jagad Hitaya Cha"** – "for one's own liberation and the welfare of the world".
#### 5.1 Two Wings and Work
1. **Monastic order** – renunciation + practical spirituality.
2. **Lay disciples** – preaching, philanthropy, humanitarian work.
- Work: **schools, colleges, hospitals, orphanages; famine and disaster relief**.
- Nature: **social and spiritual – non-political, non-sectarian, humanitarian**.
!TRAP "Vivekananda founded the Ramakrishna Mission in **1879**" = WRONG; the year is **1897**.
- **1 Feb 2026**: RKM's **Vivekananda Human Excellence Centre, Vijayawada** (₹8.5 crore) inaugurated by RKM President **Swami Gautamananda**; **M. Venkaiah Naidu** called the Mission a "**university of values**".
### 6. THEOSOPHICAL SOCIETY
#### 6.1 Founding
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Year, place | **1875, New York** (same year as the Arya Samaj) |
| Founders | **Madame H.P. Blavatsky (Russian)** and **Col. H.S. Olcott (American)**, with **William Quan Judge** |
| Came to India | **1879** |
| Indian HQ | **Adyar (Madras), from 1882** – not founded at Madras |
| Telugu name | **Divya Gnana Samajam** |

#### 6.2 Beliefs
- **Universal brotherhood** of humanity; comparative study of ancient religions.
- **Karma and reincarnation**; drew on the **Upanishads, Samkhya, Yoga, Vedanta**.
- Condemned **child marriage**; uplift of outcastes and widows.
- A foreign-founded Hindu revival.

### 8. ARYA SAMAJ AND SHUDDHI
- Founder: **Dayanand Saraswati** (born **Mool Shankar**); **"Martin Luther of India"**.
- Founded **April 1875, Bombay (Mumbai)** – not Calcutta; HQ at **Lahore in 1877**.
- Text: **Satyarth Prakash**. Slogans: **"Go Back to the Vedas"**, **"India for the Indians"**.
- Firsts: first to use **'Swaraj'**; declared **Hindi** the national language; first to urge **Swadeshi** and boycott of foreign goods.
- Principles: one God; **idol worship useless**; **all may read the Vedas**; against caste by birth and child marriage; for widow remarriage and women's education.
- Gave Hindus **self-confidence and self-reliance**; aimed to restore Vedic religion in purer form. **Pandit Gurudutt** also carried on his work.
- **Shuddhi Movement**: launched by the **Arya Samaj** (APPSC G2 2025) – reconversion of those who had turned to **Islam or Christianity** + admission of outsiders.
#### 8.1 Split of 1893
| Faction | Leaders | Institution |
|---|---|---|
| **College / DAV party** | **Mahatma Hansraj, Lala Lajpat Rai** | First DAV School, Lahore, 1886 |
| **Gurukul party** | **Swami Shraddhanand (Munshi Ram), Pandit Lekh Ram** | Gurukul Kangri, Haridwar (1902) – Vedic curriculum |
- Cause: **Western vs Vedic education and diet**, after Dayanand's death.
