## U0235 | KHILAFAT AND NON-COOPERATION MOVEMENT – PROGRAMME, SPREAD, CHAURI CHAURA AND WITHDRAWAL
### 1. KHILAFAT MOVEMENT
#### 1.1 Aim and Trigger
- **Objective:** protect/restore the **Ottoman Sultan as Khalifa (Caliph)** and protect the **territorial integrity of the Ottoman Empire** after its defeat in World War I.
- **Trigger:** **Treaty of Sevres (May 1920)** dismembered Turkey; the British PM had promised Turkey would not be humiliated.
- **Organisation:** **Khilafat Committee (1919)**.
- **End:** lost meaning when Turkey **abolished the Caliphate in 1924**.
#### 1.2 Khilafat Leaders
| Leader | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Ali brothers** (Muhammad Ali, Shaukat Ali) | Toured India with Gandhi; at the **Karachi Khilafat conference (July 1921)** urged Muslims to quit the Army |
| Maulana **Abul Kalam Azad**, Dr. **M.A. Ansari** | Khilafat leaders |
| **Hakim Ajmal Khan** | Gave up the title **Haziq-ul-Mulk** |
| **Hasrat Mohani** | Proposed Swaraj = **complete independence** (Ahmedabad session, 1921); coined **"Inquilab Zindabad"** |
!TRAP Tej Bahadur Sapru was NOT a Khilafat leader; he was a mediator of the Gandhi-Irwin Pact (1931).
#### 1.3 Gandhi's Support
- Gandhi presided over the **All-India Khilafat Conference, Delhi, November 1919**: **non-violent non-cooperation first proposed** here.
- He merged the Khilafat cause with **Swaraj**; called it a **"golden opportunity"** for Hindu-Muslim unity "such as would not arise in a hundred years".
- Aitchison: "we could not play off the Mohammedans against the Hindus" – about the Khilafat-NCM phase (1919-22), the **high point of Hindu-Muslim unity**.
- **M.A. Jinnah did NOT support** the movement.
### 2. BACKGROUND AND LAUNCH OF NCM
#### 2.1 Causes
- **Khilafat issue**, **Rowlatt Act** and **Jallianwala Bagh (13 April 1919)**.
- "Inadequate" **Montagu-Chelmsford reforms** (Amritsar session 1919, president **Motilal Nehru**, called them inadequate and disappointing).
!TRAP Simon Commission Report and the Salt Law were NOT causes of the NCM (NCM: 1920 – February 1922).
#### 2.2 Road to Launch
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Nov 1919 | All-India Khilafat Conference, Delhi (Gandhi) – non-cooperation proposed |
| Feb 1920 | Joint deputation to the Viceroy failed |
| May 1920 | Treaty of Sevres |
| June 1920 | **Allahabad** all-party/Khilafat Committee meeting endorsed boycott and **asked Gandhi to lead** |
| **1 Aug 1920** | **NCM launched**; the day **Tilak died**; Gandhi returned his **Kaiser-i-Hind** medal |
| 31 Aug 1920 | Khilafat Committee started non-cooperation |
| **Sept 1920** | **Calcutta special session** (Lala Lajpat Rai) **approved** it |
| **Dec 1920** | **Nagpur session** (C. Vijayaraghavachariar) **ratified/adopted** it |
!KEY Year of launch of NCM = 1920. Gandhi: Swaraj within a year if non-cooperation is followed.
### 3. CONGRESS SESSIONS OF THE NCM PERIOD
#### 3.1 Session → President → Decision
| Session | President | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| **Calcutta special, Sept 1920** | **Lala Lajpat Rai** | Gandhi's resolution passed **1886 to 884**; C.R. Das opposed it here |
| **Nagpur, Dec 1920** | **C. Vijayaraghavachariar** | Ratified NCM unanimously; new constitution |
| Ahmedabad, 1921 | – | Hasrat Mohani's complete independence proposal |
| **Gaya, Dec 1922** | **C.R. Das** | Council entry rejected |
| Delhi special, Sept 1923 | – | Allowed Swarajists to contest elections |
| **Belgaum, 1924** | **Gandhi** | Gandhi's **only** Congress presidency |
- **Andhras at Calcutta:** **Konda Venkatappayya** first opposed, then **resigned from the Madras Legislature**; also Pattabhi Sitaramayya, Duggirala, A. Kaleswara Rao, Peta Bapayya.
#### 3.2 Nagpur Changes to the Congress Constitution
- Goal: **Swaraj by all legitimate and peaceful means**; programme in stages, with civil disobedience and non-payment of taxes later.
- **AICC of 350 members**; committees down to **village level**.
- **Provincial Congress Committees on linguistic lines**.
- Membership fee **four annas** (not one rupee).
- Congress first stated its policy on the **princely States**: full responsible government.
!PAIR Calcutta special 1920 | Lala Lajpat Rai | Approved || Nagpur 1920 | Vijayaraghavachariar | Ratified
### 4. NCM PROGRAMME
#### 4.1 Boycott and Constructive Items
| Boycott | Constructive |
|---|---|
| **Surrender of titles** | **National schools** and colleges |
| Legislatures (**Nov 1920 elections boycotted**) | **Khadi / charkha** |
| **Law courts** → panchayat courts | **Hindu-Muslim unity** |
| Government schools/colleges, durbars | **Prohibition** |
| **Foreign cloth** | Removal of untouchability |
| Final stage: **non-payment of taxes** | **Tilak Swaraj Fund** – target **Rs 1 crore** (collected) |
- **Strict non-violence**; Congress volunteer corps.
#### 4.2 What was NOT in the Programme
- **Retention of titles**.
- **Contesting elections** (the programme asked for boycott of legislatures and elections).
!TRAP Tagore called burning foreign cloth a "ruthless waste"; he preferred the constructive programme.

#### 6.2 Social Groups

- NCM made **Congress a mass force** for the first time and turned nationalism from an elite into a **mass movement**; Swaraj was not won in one year.

### 8. NCM IN ANDHRA
#### 8.1 AICC at Vijayawada (Bezawada), 1921
- **31 March – 1 April 1921**, under Gandhi.
- Targets: **Rs 1 crore** Tilak Swaraj Fund, **1 crore members**, **20 lakh charkhas**.
- **Pingali Venkayya** presented his **national flag design** to Gandhi.
- Gandhi's **Kakinada visit (April 1921)** inspired **Durgabai Deshmukh**.
- After the Nov 1920 boycott the **Justice Party won 63 of 98** seats; first ministry under **A. Subbarayalu Reddiar, 17 December 1920**.
#### 8.2 Andhra Struggles
| Movement | Place | Leader / key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Chirala-Perala (1921-22)** | Bapatla taluka, Guntur | **Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya**; opposed making Chirala a **municipality** (heavy taxes); residents moved to **Ramnagar**; volunteer force **Ramadandu**; 12 women jailed |
| **Pedanandipadu no-tax (1921-22)** | Guntur | **Parvataneni Veerayya Chowdary** – **"Andhra Shivaji"**; founded **Shanti Sena** |
| **Palnadu pullari / forest satyagraha (1921-22)** | Palnadu | **Kanneganti Hanumanthu**; refusal of **pullari (grazing) tax**, boycott of forest officials; **killed in police firing, 1922** |
| **Rampa Rebellion (1922-24)** | Godavari agency | **Alluri Sitarama Raju**; began **22 Aug 1922**; first raid on **Chintapalli** police station; Koyas and Konda Doras |
- The **forest satyagraha first started in Palnadu**; 28 men and 8 women arrested; Unnava Lakshminarayana and Madabhushi Vedanta Lakshmi Narasimhachari arrested.
- **Guntur no-tax campaign** suspended **11 Feb 1922** after Gandhi's message to Konda Venkatappayya.

### 9. CHAURI CHAURA AND WITHDRAWAL
#### 9.1 Chauri Chaura
- Date: **4 February 1922**; **Gorakhpur district, Uttar Pradesh**.
- Crowd protested the **arrest of their leader** while campaigning against liquor sale; police fired → crowd **burnt the police station**; **22 policemen killed**.
- Gandhi was at **Bardoli** when it happened.
- **Defence:** **Madan Mohan Malaviya** (with Krishna Kant); the Gorakhpur Sessions Judge sentenced **172 to death**; **19 finally hanged** (1923).
- **Chauri Chaura Centenary** inaugurated by **PM Narendra Modi, 4 February 2021**.
#### 9.2 Withdrawal and Reactions
- Gandhi fasted and called off the movement; **Bardoli resolution, 11-12 Feb 1922** → constructive work.
- Censure vote against Gandhi at the **AICC, Delhi, 24 Feb 1922** – **Dr. Moonje**.
- **Subhas Bose:** "national calamity", "sprinkling cold water"; **M.N. Roy:** weak leadership; C.R. Das also criticised.
- **Marxist view:** stopped to prevent a radical anti-landlord turn.
- **Results:** Khilafat issue died; Hindu-Muslim unity broke; communal riots 1921-27; revolutionary activity rose again 1923-28.
!TRAP NCM was withdrawn after Chauri Chaura, NOT after Jallianwala Bagh.

### 10. GANDHI'S ARREST AND THE GREAT TRIAL
- **Arrest:** **10 March 1922** at **Sabarmati, Ahmedabad**.
- **Charge:** **sedition, Section 124A IPC**, for three **Young India** articles (incl. "Tampering with Loyalty", "Shaking the Manes").
- **Great Trial:** **Ahmedabad, 18 March 1922**, Judge **C.N. Broomfield**; Gandhi **pleaded guilty** and asked for the highest penalty.
- **Sentence:** **6 years**; released **1924** on health grounds.
- Earlier, **April 1919**: Gandhi detained at **Palwal** on the way to Punjab.
!TRAP Gandhi's papers: Young India, Navajivan, Indian Opinion, Harijan. The Bengalee was Surendranath Banerjea's.
### 11. SWARAJ PARTY
- Formed after the suspension of NCM; announced **31 Dec 1922**, formed **1 January 1923** as the **Congress-Khilafat Swaraj Party**.
- Founders: **C.R. Das (president)** and **Motilal Nehru (secretary)**; **Jawaharlal Nehru NOT a founder**.
| Pro-changers (Swarajists) | No-changers |
|---|---|
| C.R. Das, Motilal Nehru, Ajmal Khan, **Vithalbhai Patel** | **C. Rajagopalachari**, Vallabhbhai Patel, **Rajendra Prasad**, M.A. Ansari |
| Enter councils to **"end or mend"** them; obstruct from within, expose "sham parliaments" | Constructive work, continue boycott |
- 1923 elections: large gains; **V. Ramadas** won a Central Legislative Assembly seat from Madras.
- **Andhra branch:** president **Ayyadevara Kaleswara Rao**, secretary **Unava Lakshmi Narayana**; **Pithapuram Raja** formed the People's Party to back the Swarajists.
!TRAP "C.R. Das and Jawaharlal Nehru formed the Swaraj Party" is WRONG (APPSC 2023). Rajendra Prasad and Rajagopalachari were not Swarajists.
