#### 4.2 State of Global Air 2025
- India 2023: ~**2 million** deaths – **+43%** from **1.4 million (2000)**; **89%** from NCDs.
- Death rate: **186 per 100,000** vs **17** in high-income countries (over 10x).
- States with over 1 lakh deaths: **Maharashtra, UP, Rajasthan, Bihar, West Bengal**.
- Air pollution share: 70% of COPD, 33% of lung cancer, over 25% of heart disease, ~20% of diabetes deaths.
- Global: **8 million deaths/year** – 4.9 million outdoor, 2.8 million household, rest ozone.
- **SDG 3.4**: cut premature NCD deaths by **one-third by 2030**.
#### 4.3 Lancet Countdown 2025
- India: **1.72 million deaths/year** from man-made (anthropogenic) PM2.5 – +38% since 2010; global **2.5 million**.
- Wildfire smoke: 10,200 deaths/year (2020–2024), +28%.
- Household pollution: 113 per 100,000 (2022) – rural 125, urban 99.
- 50% more heatwave days in 2024; **366 extra heatwave hours per person**.
- **419 lost labour hours per person** (+124% vs 1990s); income loss **$194 billion**; pollution cost 9.5% of GDP.
- Extreme drought area **+138%**; dengue (Aedes albopictus) potential doubled; Vibrio +46%.
#### 4.4 Air Quality Life Index 2025 (2023 data)
- Prepared by **Energy Policy Institute at the University of Chicago (EPIC)**.
- **All Indians** breathe air above the **WHO PM2.5 limit of 5 µg/m³**.
- Indian standard **40 µg/m³** – ~**46%** of the population lives where even this is crossed.
- **Indo-Gangetic Plain** most polluted; 544.4 million people exposed.
- **Delhi**: largest life-expectancy gain **+8.2 years** if the WHO limit is met; cleanest regions of India +9.4 months.
- **Bangladesh**: most polluted country – PM2.5 **12 times** the WHO limit; gain 5.5 years nationally, 7.1 years in Gazipur.
- China 40.8% cleaner than 2014 despite a 2.8% rise in 2023; **Bolivia** entered the top-10 most polluted for the first time since 2010.
- **North America**: 2023 spike due to **wildfires**; Cameroon, DR Congo – air pollution a greater threat than HIV/AIDS, malaria.
- Indian programmes: **NCAP (launched 2019)**, **GRAP (Delhi NCR)**.
#### 4.5 Swachh Vayu Sarvekshan 2025
- **Surat** (Gujarat) among the **top 3** cleanest-air cities for the **second consecutive year**; only city from Gujarat.
- **Guntur (AP): 6th** nationally (population below 10 lakh category) – only South Indian city in the top ranks.
- Ceremony: **Ganga Auditorium, Indira Gandhi Bhavan, New Delhi** – by the Union Environment Minister; evaluated by **CPCB**.
- Criteria: solid waste, mechanised sweeping, dust suppression, C&D waste, vehicular/industrial emissions, green energy.
#### 4.6 CEEW Heat-Risk Study
- Title: "How Extreme Heat is Impacting India: Assessing District-level Heat Risk" – by **CEEW** (Council on Energy, Environment and Water).
- **Rank 1: Andhra Pradesh** – most heat-risk-prone State.
- **100% districts** high/very high risk: **AP, Kerala, Maharashtra, Goa**.
- AP districts: 62% high, 38% very high risk.
- India: **57%** of districts high/very high risk – home to **76%** of the population.
- Major heatwaves: 2013, 2016, 2019, 2022, 2024.
- Nights warming faster: **70% of AP districts** have at least **5 extra 'very warm' nights** per summer (March–June).
- Urban heat islands: **Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Rajahmundry, Tirupati, Guntur**.
- J&K and Ladakh: 15+ more very hot days and warm nights per summer.
