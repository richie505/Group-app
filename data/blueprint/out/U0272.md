## U0272 | POST-INDEPENDENCE CONSOLIDATION – LAND REFORMS, TRIBAL AND BORDER POLICY, NATIONAL INTEGRATION
### 1. LAND REFORMS – PHASES AND TYPES
#### 1.1 Background and Phases
- **Prakasam Committee (1938)**: advised **abolition of intermediaries**.
- **Kumarappa Committee (1949)**: set Congress's land reform agenda.
- **Phase I (1947–70)** → **Phase II (1970 onwards)**.
- **First Constitutional Amendment** shielded zamindari abolition from legal challenge (75th anniversary 18 June 2026).
- **Raj Krishna's** classification: **liberative, distributive, organisational, developmental**.
#### 1.2 The Four Measures
| Measure | Key fact |
|---|---|
| **Abolition of intermediaries** | **Most successful**; about **2 crore tenants** in direct contact with the State; ~40% of cultivated area |
| **Tenancy reform** | Rent capped at **1/4 to 1/6** of produce; defeated by "voluntary surrenders"; exception **Operation Barga (1977), West Bengal** |
| **Ceiling** | Two rounds (1950s, early 1970s); **family** made the unit from **1972**; **benami** transfers |
| **Consolidation** | Succeeded in **Punjab, Haryana, western UP** |
- Failure on ceilings and tenancy helped cause the **Naxalbari revolt (1967)**; abolition only changed the revenue hierarchy.
- **National Council for Land Reforms**: set up **9 January 2008**, **chaired by the Prime Minister**.
!TRAP Operation Barga = West Bengal, not Kerala. First State to abolish zamindari = **UP (1950)**, though Madras's 1948 Act was earlier.
### 2. ABOLITION OF INTERMEDIARIES IN ANDHRA AND TELANGANA
| Area | Law | Year |
|---|---|---|

### 3. TENANCY AND CEILING LAWS OF AP
| Law | Year | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| **Hyderabad (Telangana Area) Tenancy and Agricultural Lands Act** | **1950** | Context: Telangana armed struggle (1946–51); **6 years'** cultivation = **protected tenant** (hereditary); buy at **60%** of price in **20 instalments**; 85,409 acres owned by 1964-68 |
| **AP (Andhra Area) Tenancy Act** | **1956** | **Subba Rao Committee**; written lease deeds; lease at least **6 years**; in force **1961** |
| **AP Ceiling on Agricultural Holdings Act** | **1961** | First AP ceiling law; weak |
| **AP Land Reforms (Ceiling) Act** | **1973** | Land Reforms Bill under CM **P.V. Narasimha Rao (1971-73)**; **10–27 acres wet / 35–54 acres dry** per family; max **54 acres of 'K' grade** land |
| **APSALT Regulation** | **1/1959**, amended **1/1970** | Bars transfer of Scheduled Area land to non-tribals |
- **AP New Tenancy Act, 2024** (planned): tenant entitlement cards, crop loans; rights on the **crop, not the land**.
- Eluru agency land dispute under 1/59 and 1/70 still in court (17 Jan 2026).
#### 3.1 Koneru Ranga Rao Committee
- Set up **23 December 2004** to review **land distribution to the poor**; chaired by Minister **Koneru Ranga Rao**.
- Report **2006**, released May 2007; **104 recommendations** on surplus land, tenancy, assigned land and **tribal land** restoration.

### 5. TRIBAL POLICY
- **Nehru**: **integration** respecting tribal identity; rejected isolation ("museum") and forced assimilation.
- **Tribal Panchsheel** (credited to **Verrier Elwin** under Nehru's influence): develop on their own genius; rights in land and forests; tribal languages; own administration; **avoid over-administration**.
- Elwin: **A Philosophy for NEFA** (cultural autonomy, limits on land alienation); **The Baiga** (suggested a "National Park" for the tribe).
- **Fifth Schedule**: Scheduled Areas outside the North-East; **PESA 1996** applies only there (not Sixth Schedule areas).
!TRAP Panchsheel says avoid over-administration, not "maximum administrative control".
