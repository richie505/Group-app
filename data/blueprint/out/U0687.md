## U0687 | SERVICES SECTOR GEOGRAPHY - TOURISM (INDIA AND AP), IT, BANKING
### 1. SERVICES SECTOR, IT AND BANKING
#### 1.1 National Data
| Indicator | Fact |
|---|---|
| Share of GVA | **More than half** |
| FDI | **Largest recipient** of FDI |
| Employment | **62% of urban workers** |
| World rank | **7th largest services exporter**; 4.3% share (2024), up from 2% (2005) |
| Services exports growth | 15.3% in FY26 |
- **Tourism** = major service sub-sector: employment and **foreign exchange earnings**.
#### 1.2 Banking and Financial Inclusion
- **PMJDY**: launched **28 August 2014**; over **54 crore** accounts.
- RBI Financial Inclusion Index: started 2021; base 43.4 (March 2017).
- AP banking (31 Dec 2024): most branches **Visakhapatnam (624)**; fewest **Alluri Sitharama Raju (52)**. AP services GVA growth 8.53% (2024-25).
#### 1.3 IT and ITES
- **STPI units** and **Global Capability Centres** spread IT to Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities.
- AP IT and GCC Policy 4.0 (2024-29): co-working and neighbourhood workspaces in Tirupati, Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada.
- **Google AI data centre**: **Visakhapatnam and Anakapalli**, **US$ 15 bn** (~Rs 87,520 crore) - Google's largest AI hub outside the US. TCS and Cognizant began operations in Vizag (2025-26).

### 4. TOURISM FORMATS AND PROJECTS
- **National Maritime Heritage Complex (NMHC)**: **Lothal, Gujarat**; Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways (announced Sept 2022); to be **Asia's largest maritime heritage museum**; Rs 3,500 crore; MoU with **Portugal** (Feb 2020) and Netherlands (20 Dec 2025).
