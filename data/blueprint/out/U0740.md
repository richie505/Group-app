## U0740 | MISSILE DEFENCE AND STRATEGIC FORCES – BMD PROGRAMME, NUCLEAR TRIAD, SSBN, NO-FIRST-USE POLICY
### 1. NUCLEAR TESTS – POKHRAN I AND II
| Test | Date | Code name | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pokhran-I** | **18 May 1974** | **Smiling Buddha** | "**Peaceful nuclear explosion**"; PM **Indira Gandhi**; team led by Raja Ramanna; fission device |
| **Pokhran-II** | **11 and 13 May 1998** | **Operation Shakti** (Shakti-98) | **Five** tests, fission + fusion; PM **A.B. Vajpayee**; led by **A.P.J. Abdul Kalam** (DRDO) and **R. Chidambaram** (DAE) |
- Site: **Pokhran Test Range, Jaisalmer district, Rajasthan** (Thar Desert).
- **11 May = National Technology Day**.
!TRAP **Operation Shakti** (1998 nuclear test) ≠ **Mission Shakti** (2019 ASAT test).
### 2. NUCLEAR WEAPON TECHNOLOGY
#### 2.1 Fission vs Fusion
| Fission (atomic) weapon | Fusion (thermonuclear / hydrogen) weapon |
|---|---|
| Heavy nucleus (**U-235 / Pu-239**) splits in a neutron **chain reaction** | Light nuclei (hydrogen isotopes) **fuse** |
| Example: Pokhran-I | A **fission "primary"** triggers fusion; tested in Pokhran-II |
- U-235 fission: neutron hit → two smaller nuclei (e.g. barium + krypton) + 2–3 neutrons.
- **Krytron** = high-speed switch that fires a nuclear bomb's detonators (not a MOSFET, relay or solenoid).
#### 2.2 Enrichment Levels
| Grade | U-235 share |
|---|---|
| Natural uranium | ~**0.7%** |
| Reactor-grade LEU | **3–5%** |
| HEU | **20%+** |
| Weapons-grade | ~**90%** |
### 3. NUCLEAR DOCTRINE AND COMMAND
#### 3.1 Timeline
- **Draft doctrine**: prepared by the **National Security Advisory Board (NSAB)**, **August 1999**.
- **No First Use** came from the **Vajpayee government after Pokhran-II** (not Pokhran-I) (UKPSC 2021).
- Formally adopted by the **CCS** on **4 January 2003**.
#### 3.2 Key Tenets
- **Credible minimum deterrence**.
- **No First Use**: only in retaliation against a nuclear attack on Indian territory or Indian forces.
- **No use against non-nuclear-weapon states**.
- Nuclear option kept against a **major biological or chemical attack**.
#### 3.3 Nuclear Command Authority
| Body | Chaired by | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Political Council** | **Prime Minister** | Only body that can authorise use |
| **Executive Council** | **National Security Advisor** | Advises; carries out directions |
| **Strategic Forces Command** | Tri-service (set up **2003**) | **Manages and operates** the nuclear arsenal |
- **CCS** oversees civilian political control; it does not manage the forces.
- Aug 2026: Agni user trials held "under the aegis of SFC".
### 4. NUCLEAR TRIAD – LAND, AIR, SEA
| Leg | Platforms |
|---|---|
| Land | **Prithvi** and **Agni** series (canisterised, road- and rail-mobile) |
| Air | **Jaguar** and **Mirage 2000** (free-fall bombs) |
| Sea | **K-15 / K-4** SLBMs from **Arihant-class SSBNs**; earlier **Dhanush** from ships (INS Subhadra, INS Rajput) |
- **SSBN = most survivable leg** → sure **second-strike** capability → backs NFU. Next step: **continuous at-sea deterrence**.
- India has a fully operational triad, with **USA, Russia, China** (Apr 2026).
- US triad = **Minuteman III** ICBMs + strategic bombers + Ohio/Columbia-class SSBNs.
- **Agni-4** user trial 6 Aug 2026: 4,000 km, two-stage solid, road-mobile.
- SIPRI (Jun 2026): **12** Indian warheads "operationally deployed", linked to SSBN patrols and canisterised Agni.

### 6. BALLISTIC MISSILE DEFENCE
| Interceptor | Tier |
|---|---|

| **AAD** (Advanced Air Defence) | Phase I, **endo-atmospheric**, lower altitude |

### 8. NON-PROLIFERATION REGIMES – INDIA'S STATUS
| Regime | India's status |
|---|---|
| **NPT** (1970) | **Not signed** – discriminatory ("**nuclear apartheid**"); only five states that tested before **1967** are NWS |
| **CTBT** | **Not signed** |
| **NSG** | **Not a member** (blocked by **China**); got **2008 waiver**, follows guidelines |
| **MTCR** | Member **2016** (**35th**) |
| Hague Code of Conduct | Joined **2016** |
| **Wassenaar** | Member **Dec 2017** |
| **Australia Group** | Member **Jan 2018** |
- India follows guidelines through the **WMD Act** and the **SCOMET** list; US support helped entry.
- **FMCT**: supports a universal, verifiable treaty that does not cap existing stockpile.
- India kept the bomb option open and would not open strategic plants to inspection.
