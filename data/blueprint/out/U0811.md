### 3. TB ELIMINATION
- Programme: **NTEP** – free diagnosis and treatment. **BCG** vaccine protects against TB.
| Scheme / term | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Ni-kshay** | Digital TB notification and tracking portal |
| **Ni-kshay Poshan Yojana** | Nutritional **DBT** to all notified TB patients (public + private) |

- Ni-kshay Mitras (Dec 2025): 3.78 lakh+ Mitras, 20 lakh+ patients, 45 lakh+ food baskets.

- **India TB Report 2022**: notifications in 2021 rose **19%** over 2020.
- India TB Report 2024: **25.52 lakh** notified in 2023, up from 24.22 lakh in 2022.
| TB outcome (2015–2024) | Change |
|---|---|

| Mortality | down **28%** |

### 4. MALARIA ELIMINATION
- **National Framework for Malaria Elimination (2016–2030)**: elimination by **2030**; cases fell **80%** (2015–2023).
- **MERA India** = Malaria Elimination Research Alliance India, led by **ICMR**.
- **NVBDCP** tools: IRS, LLINs, larvivorous fish, bio-larvicides, source reduction (**not antibiotic prophylaxis**). NVBDCP handles outbreaks of malaria, dengue, chikungunya; elimination targets for malaria, LF and kala-azar.
- **Quinine** (malaria medicine) comes from **Cinchona** bark.
- WHO-certified malaria-free: **Sri Lanka** (**5 September 2016**; second in SEAR after the **Maldives**), **China**, **Timor-Leste** (most recent of the three).
#### 4.1 Vaccines → Developer
| Vaccine | Fact |
|---|---|
| **RTS,S/AS01 (Mosquirix)** | First WHO-approved malaria vaccine; **GSK** |
| **R21/Matrix-M** | Malaria; **Oxford** Jenner Institute + **Serum Institute of India** |
| **Cervavac** (Sept 2022) | India's first indigenous **quadrivalent HPV** vaccine; **cervical cancer**; Serum Institute |
| **Shanvac-B** | India's first genetically engineered vaccine; **Hepatitis B** |
| ZyCoV-D | DNA-based COVID-19 vaccine |
| Toxoid vaccine | Prevents **diphtheria** |
| HIV/AIDS | **No licensed vaccine** yet |
### 5. HIV/AIDS
- **NACP** launched **1992**; led by **NACO** (MoHFW).
| NACP phase | Period |
|---|---|
| I | 1992–99 |
| II | 1999–2006 |
| III | 2007–12 |
| IV | 2012–17 (extended to 2021) |
| V | **2021–26** – 80% cut in new infections and AIDS deaths |
- **ICTC** = Integrated Counselling and Testing Centre (free, voluntary testing with counselling); **VCTC** = Voluntary Counselling and Testing Centre; **ART** free for life at ART centres.
- **HIV and AIDS (Prevention and Control) Act, 2017** – bars discrimination against people living with HIV.
- Projects: **Red Ribbon Express** (awareness train); **AVAHAN** (Gates Foundation prevention initiative).
- **ELISA** – first widely used HIV screening test; detects antibodies. **Zidovudine (Azidothymidine)** – most used anti-retroviral.
- HIV first recognised **1981**; killed over 25 million people. Dominant strain in India: **HIV-1 subtype C**.
- **95-95-95** (UNAIDS): know status → on treatment → suppressed viral load.
- India: over 2.5 million living with HIV; highest burden **Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka**.
- 2026: UNAIDS 'United to end AIDS' report (26th International AIDS Conference, Rio) – new infections at a 30-year low.
### 6. OTHER ELIMINATION MISSIONS
- **National Sickle Cell Anaemia Elimination Mission**: launched **1 July 2023**, **Shahdol** (MP); tribal focus; target 2047; screen 70 million. Inherited; misshapen red cells cannot carry oxygen properly.
- **Lymphatic filariasis**: MDA with **IDA** = **Ivermectin, DEC, Albendazole**.
- **Leprosy**: **NLEP** gives free **MDT** at PHCs (not NVBDCP); **Sparsh** awareness campaign.
!TRAP **Anti-Leprosy Day** (India) = **30 January** (Gandhi's death anniversary); **World Leprosy Day** = last Sunday of January.
### 7. NUTRITION-RELATED PROGRAMMES
| Programme | Launch |

| National Goitre Control Programme | **1962** |
| Renamed **NIDDCP** (Iodine Deficiency Disorders) | **1992** |
| Vitamin A prophylaxis against nutritional blindness | **1970** |

### 8. NCD PROGRAMME
- **NP-NCD** (earlier NPCDCS): population-based screening; finds high-risk people and links them to care.
- **India Hypertension Control Initiative** (with **ICMR**): cut deaths from heart attack, stroke and kidney failure.
- HPV vaccine **Cervavac** → Serum Institute → cervical cancer.
