### 4. TECHNOLOGY SUMMITS AND ALLIANCES
#### 4.1 India AI Impact Summit 2026
- Held at **Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi**, **16–20 February 2026** (Bharat Mandapam also hosted the **2023 G20 Summit**).
- Closed with the **New Delhi Declaration on AI Impact**.
- Attended by **Macron**; India joined **Pax Silica** during it.
#### 4.2 India Joins Pax Silica (20 February 2026)
- India signed to join the **US-led Pax Silica** on **20 February 2026** at the **AI Impact Summit**, in the presence of Union Minister **Ashwini Vaishnaw**, Bharat Mandapam; a **capability-based alliance**; **35 signatories**.
- Launched **December 2025** at the inaugural **Pax Silica Summit, Washington D.C.**
- **India is NOT a founding member**: invited later by US Ambassador **Sergio Gor**.
- India = **first major developing country** and **first non-US-ally strategic partner** to join.
- Name: **"Pax" = peace**; **"Silica" = chip-making compound**.
- Aim: secure supply chains for **critical minerals, semiconductors, AI infrastructure, logistics**; counter **China's rare earth** dominance.
- **Core members**: **US, Japan, South Korea, Australia**, UK, Israel, Singapore, UAE, Qatar, Greece.
- **First (inaugural) observers**: **Canada, EU, OECD, Taiwan**.
- Related: India in the **Supply Chain Resilience Initiative** (India–Japan–Australia, **2021**) and the **Quad Critical Minerals Initiative (2025)**; US hosted a **Critical Minerals Ministerial** on **4 February 2026** (**55 delegations**).
- **MeitY** established **2016**.
#### 4.3 Trusted Tech Alliance – Munich Security Conference
- **15 global tech companies** (including **Jio**) formed the **Trusted Tech Alliance (TTA)** at the **Munich Security Conference (MSC 2026), 13–15 February 2026**.
- Aim: joint **checkable/verifiable standards** for **transparency, security, governance, data protection** across connectivity, cloud, semiconductors, software, AI; **five principles**.
!TRAP Pax Silica was launched in December 2025 in Washington; India joined only in February 2026.

### 8. ALLIANCES AND MEMBERSHIP CHANGES
#### 8.1 US Exits 66 Organisations – Impact on ISA
- **January 2026**: the **US withdrew from 66 international organisations**, including the **International Solar Alliance (ISA)**.
- US joined ISA late (**2021**); contributed ~**$2.1 million** over three years (~**1%** of ISA funds) – limited financial impact; real risk for **Africa** and small developing economies (concessional finance).
- **ISA**: launched **2015** at **COP21 Paris** by **India and France**; HQ **Gurugram**; **120+** members.
- Originally for **tropical countries** (between the Tropics of Cancer and Capricorn); **2020 amendment** – open to **all UN members** (but it does **not include all UN members**).
- Target: mobilise **$1 trillion** solar investment by **2030**; ISA **does not build plants** – it facilitates finance, de-risking and capacity building.
- India's solar (late 2025): module capacity ~**144 GW**; cell capacity ~**25 GW**; China has ~**70%** of global solar cell capacity; India imported ~**$1.7 billion** of PV modules from China in **FY25**.
#### 8.2 Spain Joins the Indo-Pacific Oceans Initiative (IPOI)
- **Spain** presented its **Accession Declaration** to EAM **S. Jaishankar**.
- IPOI: launched by India at the **East Asia Summit, Bangkok, 2019**; **non-treaty, voluntary**; **seven pillars**.
#### 8.3 Arab League
- India's EAM met foreign ministers of **five Arab League** states.
- Founded **22 March 1945**, **Cairo**; founders **Egypt, Iraq, Transjordan, Lebanon, Saudi Arabia, Syria**; **Yemen** joined **5 May 1945**.
- Platforms: **India–Arab League Dialogue** and **Arab–India Cooperation Forum**.
!KEY ISA = India + France, COP21, 2015, HQ Gurugram; the US joined in 2021 and left in January 2026.
