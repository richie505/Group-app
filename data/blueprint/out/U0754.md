## U0754 | RENEWABLE ENERGY SOURCES AND INSTALLED CAPACITIES IN INDIA – OVERALL TARGETS
### 1. CLASSIFICATION OF ENERGY SOURCES
| Basis | Types |
|---|---|
| Renewable | Solar, wind, hydro (incl. tidal/wave), **geothermal**, biomass |
| Non-renewable | Coal, oil, natural gas, **nuclear fuel** |
| Conventional | Coal, petroleum, gas, **hydel**, **firewood**, cattle-dung cake |
| Non-conventional | Solar, wind, geothermal – **by definition renewable** (UPPSC 2023) |
- **Fossil fuels** (coal, oil, gas): formed from organic material over millions of years; limited.
- **Gobar gas** = mainly **methane**.
- **Natural gas is NOT green energy**; sunlight, wind, algae-based sources are (APPSC 2019).
- **Nuclear is NOT renewable** (APPSC 2024).
!TRAP Hydel is conventional yet renewable.
#### 1.1 Not Derived from the Sun
- Wind, hydro, biomass, fossil fuels = **stored solar energy**.
- NOT from the Sun: **nuclear** (fission), **geothermal** (heat from Earth's formation and radioactive decay, stored in rocks and fluids), **tidal** (Moon).
#### 1.2 Solar Energy Facts
- Most important non-conventional source and **greatest potential** for India (APPSC G2 2017); largest storehouse of alternative energy.
- No atmospheric pollution when turned into electricity; used most in organic form (**photosynthesis**).
- **Solar (photovoltaic) cell**: light directly to electricity; most eco-friendly.
### 2. SOURCE-WISE INSTALLED CAPACITY (AUG 2026)
| Source | Capacity |
|---|---|
| **Solar** (largest RE) | **164.59 GW** |
| **Wind** (second) | **58.14 GW** |
| Large hydro | about **52 GW** |
| Bio-power | about **11.75 GW** |
| Small hydro | 5.18 GW (~24.6% of 21 GW potential) |
| Nuclear (non-fossil, not RE) | 8.78 GW |
- Total ~552 GW, >54% non-fossil; coal ~42% of capacity.
- **Capacity is not generation**: 2024-25 renewables ≈ half of capacity but only **~22%** of generation; coal/thermal **~75%**.

### 5. RANKINGS AND POTENTIAL
- **IRENA 2026**: India **3rd** in RE capacity – China 2,258 GW, USA 468 GW, **India 250.5 GW**.
- India **4th in wind**, **3rd in solar** (2025-26).
- Solar potential **748 GW** (~4.5% used).
- Top RE potential: **Rajasthan** (23.70%); AP 9.10%. Top installed: **Gujarat** (**42.58 GW**, 16.5%, June 2026); Gujarat also leads wind.
### 6. RPO AND RCO
- **RPO**: DISCOMs and large/designated consumers must buy a share of power from renewables; **solar RPO 8% by March 2022**.
- **Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022**: RPO recast as **RCO** – a duty to **consume** renewable energy.
### 7. GREEN ENERGY CORRIDORS
- Purpose: carry RE from RE-rich zones to load centres.
- **Intra-State GEC-I**: **8 states** – AP, Gujarat, HP, Karnataka, MP, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu (**not Telangana**); funding **MNRE 40% : KfW (Germany) loan 40% : state utility 20%**.
- **GEC-II** (2022): 7 states, ~20 GW RE.
- **Inter-State GEC** lines built by **PGCIL**.
- Apr-Jun 2026: 8,133 GWh solar curtailed due to transmission mismatch.
### 8. IREDA
- **Indian Renewable Energy Development Agency**: **NBFC** under **MNRE**.
- **Navratna** status **April 2024**; listed on NSE and BSE **29 November 2023**.
- Functions: project financing, appraisal, consultancy, term/bridge/working-capital loans – **not setting tariffs**.
