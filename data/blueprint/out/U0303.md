## U0303 | TYPES OF LEGISLATURES – UNICAMERAL AND BICAMERAL; ARGUMENTS FOR A SECOND CHAMBER
### 1. UNICAMERAL VS BICAMERAL
- **Unicameral** = one House; **bicameral** = two Houses.
- Unicameral: **China** (National People's Congress – **2,977** members, biggest Parliament, a rubber stamp of the Communist Party), **Israel** (Knesset), New Zealand, Sweden, Denmark.
- Bicameralism at the Centre first by the **GoI Act, 1919** (Council of State + Central Legislative Assembly); **1935 Act** – in some provinces.
- **Article 79**: Parliament = **President + Council of States + House of the People**; the VP (RS Chairman, a non-member) and State Councils are not part of it.
### 2. UPPER AND LOWER HOUSES ABROAD
| Country (Parliament) | Upper House | Lower House |
|---|---|---|
| UK (Parliament) | House of Lords | House of Commons |
| USA (Congress) | Senate | House of Representatives |
| Russia (**Federal Assembly**) | **Federation Council** | **State Duma** |
| Australia (Parliament) | Senate | House of Representatives |
| Japan (**National Diet**) | **House of Councillors** | House of Representatives |
!KEY UPSC 2005: only "Russia's Parliament is the Federal Assembly" was correct.
### 3. SECOND CHAMBER – FOR AND AGAINST
| For | Against |
|---|---|
| **Double check** on hasty bills | **Delay** and **duplication** – does not speed law-making |
| Regional representation | – |
| Experts through **nomination** | – |
- **Abbé Sieyès**: "If it agrees it is **superfluous**, if it dissents it is **mischievous**."
- **N. Gopalaswami Ayyangar** (Constituent Assembly): RS as a House of **dignified debate** delaying hasty legislation.
### 4. RAJYA SABHA AS FEDERAL CHAMBER
| Point | Rajya Sabha | US Senate |
|---|---|---|
| Seats | By population (**Fourth Schedule**) | Equal – 2 per state (100) |
| Election | Indirect, elected MLAs, STV | Direct |
| Nominated | **12** | None |
- Seats: UP 31; **Himachal Pradesh 3** (lowest of the listed), J&K 4, Chhattisgarh 5, Jharkhand 6.
- Weaker: **Money Bill** returned within **14 days**; no vote on grants; **no no-confidence motion** (LS only).
- Exclusive powers: new **All-India Services** (**Art 312(1)**) and national-interest resolution – **two-thirds present and voting**.
!TRAP Rajya Sabha is neither dissolved nor abolished; Lok Sabha and Assemblies can be dissolved, not abolished; State Councils can be abolished (Art 169), not dissolved.
### 5. BICAMERALISM IN STATES
- **Part VI, Articles 168–212**. Unicameral = Governor + Assembly; bicameral = Governor + Assembly + **Council**.
- **6 bicameral States**: **Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Bihar, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh**. Largest Council: **UP (100)**.
- No Council: **Rajasthan**, **Tamil Nadu**, Goa, Mizoram, Sikkim, **Madhya Pradesh** (allowed by the **7th Amendment, 1956**, never created).
- **Art 169**: Parliament creates/abolishes a Council **by law** (not an Art 368 amendment) after an Assembly resolution by a **majority of total membership + two-thirds present and voting**.
- **Art 171**: Council not more than **one-third** of Assembly; minimum **40**.
| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| 74 / 163 | Union / State Council of Ministers |
| 80 | Council of States |
| 168, 169, 171 | Legislative Council |
- **AP angle**: Council **abolished 1985**, **revived 2007**; 2020 resolution to abolish again, **withdrawn 2021**.
### 6. LEGISLATURES IN UNION TERRITORIES
| UT | Basis | Assembly |
|---|---|---|
| Delhi | **Art 239AA** (69th Amdt, 1991) | 70 |
| Puducherry | **Art 239A** | 30 elected + up to 3 nominated |
| Jammu and Kashmir | J&K Reorganisation Act, 2019 | 90 elected |
- Only these three UTs elect RS members: **Delhi 3, Puducherry 1, J&K 4**.
- **70th Amendment**: Delhi and Puducherry vote in the President's election.
- **17 Apr 2026**: **UT Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026** extends women's reservation to Delhi, Puducherry and J&K legislatures.
