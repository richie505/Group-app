### 5. ICAR NETWORK AND KVKs
- **113 institutes** (72 research institutes). **KVKs**: district-level, **100% centrally funded**, under **11 ATARIs**.
- **First KVK: Puducherry, 1974** (pilot, under TNAU); Mohan Singh Mehta committee (1973).
- Seed spices: **Unjha** (Mehsana, Gujarat) = Asia's largest seed-spice trading hub; Gujarat ~80% of exports (Aug 2026).
- Sep 2026: entrepreneurship and skill centres in all KVKs by Dec 2028. **Lumpi-ProVacInd** - lumpy skin vaccine by two ICAR institutes.
