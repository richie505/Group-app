## U0148 | SUFI SAINTS - MOINUDDIN CHISHTI, NIZAMUDDIN AULIYA, BABA FARID, SHEIKH AHMAD SIRHINDI
### 1. THE CHISHTI LINE - GURU TO DISCIPLE
- Chain: **Usman Harooni → Moinuddin Chishti → Qutbuddin Bakhtiyar Kaki → Baba Farid → Nizamuddin Auliya → Nasiruddin Chiragh-i-Delhi**.
- **Usman Harooni** accepted Moinuddin as his disciple at **Haroon**; his Urs is also observed.
- **Amir Khusrau** - disciple of **Nizamuddin Auliya**; introduced **Qaul**, the opening hymn of **Qawwali**.
- Baba Farid's main disciples: **Nizamuddin Auliya** and **Alauddin Sabir Kaliyari**.
#### 1.1 Chronological order
| Saint | Dates | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Moinuddin Chishti | 1143-1235 | **First Chishti saint settled in India**; founder of the order in India |
| Qutbuddin Bakhtiyar Kaki | 1173-1235 | Disciple of Moinuddin |
| Baba Farid | 1178-1271 | Teacher of Nizamuddin |
| Nizamuddin Auliya | 1238-1325 | Born **Badaun** |
| Nasiruddin Mahmud | 1274-1356 | Last great Chishti of Delhi |
!KEY FIRST: Moinuddin Chishti came with **Muhammad Ghori, 1192**, and settled at **Ajmer** - earliest Chishti saint in India.

### 4. SAINTS AND RULERS
| Saint | Contemporary ruler / relation |
|---|---|
| Moinuddin Chishti | Came with Muhammad Ghori, 1192 |
| Qutbuddin Bakhtiyar Kaki | **Iltutmish** |
| Nizamuddin Auliya | **Alauddin Khalji**, **Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq**; kept away from the court |
| Nasiruddin Chiragh | **Muhammad bin Tughlaq**, **Firuz Tughlaq** |
| Ahmad Sirhindi | **Akbar** (critic) and **Jahangir** (jailed him) |
- **"Hunuz Dilli dur ast"** ("Delhi is still far") - Nizamuddin Auliya, said of **Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq**.
- Chishti stance: as a rule avoided political power; later Deccan Chishtis accepted grants.
!TRAP Iltutmish was a contemporary of BOTH Bakhtiyar Kaki (Chishti) and Bahauddin Zakariya (Suhrawardi).
### 5. NIZAMUDDIN AULIYA
- Chishti; disciple of **Baba Farid**; born Badaun, buried in Delhi.
- Stood for **sama** and distance from the court.
- His talks are recorded in **Fawaid-ul-Fuad**.
### 6. BABA FARID AND THE GURU GRANTH SAHIB
- **Fariduddin Ganj-i-Shakar**: born at **Kothewal** (Multan district); teacher of Nizamuddin.
- His compositions are included in the **Adi Granth / Guru Granth Sahib**.
### 7. MALFUZAT AND OTHER TEXTS
| Text | Author / compiler | Type |
|---|---|---|
| **Fawaid-ul-Fuad** | Conversations of **Nizamuddin Auliya**, compiled by **Amir Hasan Sijzi** (UPPSC 2021) | Malfuzat |
| **Maktubat-i-Imam Rabbani** | Ahmad Sirhindi | Letters |
| **Radd-i-Rawafiz** | Ahmad Sirhindi | Anti-Shia tract |
!TRAP Fawaid-ul-Fuad was compiled by Amir Hasan Sijzi, NOT Amir Khusrau, Barani or Hasan Nizami.
### 8. SHEIKH AHMAD SIRHINDI
- Order: **Naqshbandi**; disciple of **Khwaja Baqi Billah**; title **Mujaddid Alf-i-Sani**.
- Opposed Akbar's **Sulh-i-kul** and **Din-i-Ilahi**; sought to purge "un-Islamic" practices; opposed **sama** and **visits to saints' tombs**.
- Doctrine: **Wahdat-ush-Shuhud** (Unity of Appearance) against **Wahdat-ul-Wujud** (Unity of Being, Chishti/Qadiri).
- **Imprisoned by Jahangir at Gwalior Fort**; letters = **Maktubat-i-Imam Rabbani**.
!PAIR Nizamuddin | Chishti | Sama, aloof from court || Sirhindi | Naqshbandi | Orthodox political critic

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- First Chishti saint in India: Moinuddin Chishti (Ajmer).
- Last great Chishti of Delhi: Nasiruddin Chiragh-i-Delhi.
- Sufi with verses in the Guru Granth Sahib: Baba Farid.

### 11. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. **Mahboob-i-Ilahi** = Nizamuddin; **Chiragh-i-Delhi** = Nasiruddin.
2. **Garib Nawaz** = Moinuddin; **Ganj-i-Shakar** = Baba Farid.
3. **Mujaddid Alf-i-Sani** = Sirhindi (Naqshbandi), not a Chishti.
4. Fawaid-ul-Fuad = **Amir Hasan Sijzi**, not Amir Khusrau.
5. "Hunuz Dilli dur ast" = Nizamuddin vs **Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq**.
6. Sirhindi jailed by **Jahangir**, not Akbar.
7. Bakhtiyar Kaki = **Iltutmish**'s contemporary; dargah at Mehrauli.
8. Shuhud = Sirhindi; Wujud = Chishti/Qadiri.
