## U0079 | EASTERN CHALUKYAS OF VENGI – KUBJA VISHNUVARDHANA, DYNASTIC HISTORY, PATRONAGE OF TELUGU (NANNAYA)
### 1. ORIGIN AND IDENTITY OF THE DYNASTY
- Also called the **Chalukyas of Vengi**, Purva Chalukyas or Vengi Chalukyas; a **branch of the Badami Chalukyas** (Badami – Karnataka; Vengi – Andhra Pradesh).
- **Founder: Kubja Vishnuvardhana**, **brother of Pulakeshin II** of Badami.
- **Pulakeshin II** took the **Krishna-Godavari** region (Vengi) after defeating the remnants of the **Vishnukundins** (c. 616 CE).
- Kubja was made **viceroy in 617 CE** and declared **independence in 624 CE**.
- Rule: **624–1075 CE** (about 4.5 centuries); **1076–c.1210** = **Chalukya-Chola phase**. Some count the end as 1189 CE by including that phase.
- Core territory: **Venginadu = Krishna-Godavari doab**; capitals Pithapuram, Pedavegi, Rajahmundry.
- **Greatest king: Gunaga Vijayaditya III** (Tripura Martya Maheshwara); **last king: Vijayaditya VII** (1070–1075; died 1075 = end of the line).
- An important power in Andhra–Deccan politics, with repeated wars and marriages with the **Cholas**; after **973 CE** the main rival was the **Kalyani Chalukyas** (Satyasraya, Someshvara I).
- **Matwalin**: a traveller linked with an account of China–India relations during Chalukya rule.
#### 1.1 Identity Markers
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Royal emblem | **Boar (Varaha)** |
| Official language | **Sanskrit** – most of about 100 donation inscriptions |
| Matronymic | **"Hariti-putra"** (also used by Satavahanas, Ikshvakus, Kadambas) – read as a sign of southern origin |
| Seal sign manual | **"Tribhuvanankusa"** on copper-plate seals |
| Mother goddess | **Kushmandini** (APPSC G2 2019 key) |
| Main faith | **Shaivism**, tolerant of Jainism and Buddhism |
- Official language **Sanskrit** was shared with Vishnukundins, Kakatiyas and Reddis; **Prakrit** under the Satavahanas and Ikshvakus.
- Even so, the Vengi court **turned Telugu into a literary language**.
!TRAP Hariti-putra is a **mother's name (matronymic)**, not the dynasty's goddess. The key answer for "mother goddess" is **Kushmandini**.
### 2. FOUNDER KUBJA VISHNUVARDHANA
- Title **Vishamasiddhi** ("conqueror of the forest" / difficult places).
- **First capital: Pithapuram (Pishtapura)**; built the **Kunti Madhava temple, Pithapuram**.
- **Kopparam copper plates (631 CE)** of Pulakeshin II call Kubja **"Yuvaraja"**; Kopparam (and **Marturu**) inscriptions record the conquest of the Krishna-Godavari region and call **Mangalagiri "Mangalapura"** (G2 2019 key).
- **Chipurupalli (Cheepurupalli) plates**, Vizianagaram: grant of Kubja in his **18th regnal year** (632 CE), name him **Vishamasiddhi** and the scholar **Madhavasharma**.
- Queen **Ayyana Mahadevi** built the Jain **Nedumba (Nadumbi) Vasati** at Vijayawada (section 8).

### 4. WARS WITH THE RASHTRAKUTAS
| Ruler | Rival / event |
|---|---|
| **Vijayaditya I** | Conflict begins with **Dantidurga** (founder of the Rashtrakutas), who ended the Badami line (Vengi had helped Badami) |
| **Vishnuvardhana IV** | Backed **Govinda III** in the Rashtrakuta succession war; defeated by **Dhruva**, who **married his daughter Sheela Mahadevi** – the marriage saved his kingdom |
| **Vijayaditya II** | Fought the Rashtrakutas under **Amoghavarsha**; 108 battles |
| **Gunaga Vijayaditya III** | Defeated **Krishna II** and the Kalinga king **Devendravarma** |
| **Chalukya Bhima I** | Repelled **Krishna II**'s forces at **Niravadyapura and Peruvanguru** |
!TRAP Chalukya Bhima I fought **Krishna II**, not Dhruva. Dhruva defeated Vishnuvardhana IV.
### 5. TELUGU CHODAS, KALYANI CHALUKYAS AND THE CHOLAS
#### 5.1 Interlude and Recovery
- **Jatachoda Bhima** (Telugu Choda of Pedakallu) **killed Danarnava** and occupied Vengi.
- Danarnava's sons **Saktivarma I and Vimaladitya** fled to **Rajaraja Chola** and recovered Vengi with **Chola help**.
- **1006 CE**: Satyasraya of Kalyani sent **Bayyalanambi** → Saktivarma I beat him with Chola help.
#### 5.2 Marriages and Succession War
| Marriage / war | Detail |
|---|---|
| Vimaladitya (crowned May 1011) | Married **Kundavva (Kundavai)**, daughter of **Rajaraja Chola I** – mother of Rajaraja Narendra; also married **Melama**, daughter of **Jatachoda Bhima** |
| Succession war | **Rajaraja Narendra** (son of Kundavva) backed by **Rajendra I** vs **Vijayaditya VII** (son of Melama) backed by Kalyani **Jayasimha II** |
| Coronation | Rajaraja Narendra, **16 August 1022**; married **Ammanga Devi, daughter of Rajendra Chola** |
| Dharanikota, then **Koppam (1053)** | **Rajadhiraja I** won at Dharanikota and burnt **Kollipaka**, but was killed at Koppam by **Ahavamalla Someshwara**; Rajaraja Narendra had to accept **Kalyani suzerainty** |
!PAIR Kundavva | Chola princess | Mother of Rajaraja Narendra || Melama | Daughter of Jatachoda Bhima | Mother of Vijayaditya VII
#### 5.3 End of the Dynasty – Chalukya-Chola Merger
- **1070** – Vijayaditya VII ousted **Rajendra Chalukya**; **1075** – Vijayaditya VII died, end of the Vengi line.
- **Rajendra Chalukya = Kulottunga I**, the **first Chalukya-Chola king**; he united the Eastern Chalukya and Chola lines.
- The **Cholas** had close **political and matrimonial ties** with the Chalukyas of Vengi.

### 8. TEMPLES, RELIGION, QUEENS AND JAIN PATRONAGE
#### 8.1 Temples → Builder and Style
| Temple | Builder |
|---|---|
| **108 Shiva temples (Narendreswara / Narendra Mrigeswaralayas)** | **Vijayaditya II** |
| Kunti Madhava, Pithapuram | Kubja Vishnuvardhana |
| **Bikkavolu (Biccavolu)** – Mukteshwara, **Vijayeshwara** (Ganga and Yamuna figures), Chandrashekhara | **Gunaga Vijayaditya III** |
| **Pancharamas** (Bhimarama, Draksharama), **Bhimeswara** temples at **Draksharama and Samalkot** | **Chalukya Bhima I** |
| Kartikeya temple, Bezawada | Yuddhamalla |
| Rajaraja temple, Bikkavolu; Vishnu temple, Rajahmundry; three Shiva temples at **Kalidindi** (for fallen Chola soldiers) | Rajaraja Narendra |
- Style: **Vesara / Chalukya** – **Dravidian vimana** with **Buddhist influence**; the **Vengi school** used **local sandstone (not granite)** and a **raised jagati**; this tradition led into the later **Chalukya-Chola** style.
!TRAP "108 Shaivalayas" → **Vijayaditya II**, not Kubja or Vishnuvardhana III/IV. One Group-2 key printed Vijayaditya I.
#### 8.2 Religion
- **Shaivism dominant**; Shaiva sects: **Kapalika, Kalamukha, Pasupata**; Kapalikas came from the **Misra Saivam** school.
- Tolerant – patronised **Jainism and Buddhism**. Kubja, Vishnuvardhana III and Ammaraja II patronised Jainism.
- Mother goddess **Kushmandini**.
#### 8.3 Queens and Women → Foundations
| Person | Patronage / role | King |
|---|---|---|
| **Ayyana Mahadevi** | Built the Jain **Nedumba (Nadumbi) Vasati / Nedumbasadi** near Vijayawada (Musinikonda grant) | **Kubja Vishnuvardhana** (G2 2025 key) |
| **Chamekamba** | Courtesan; **Kaluchumbarru grant** for a Jain temple | **Ammaraja II** |
| **Chellavva** | Court singer and dancer; **Gandharva-Vidya-Visarada** (also "Samasta Gandharva Vidyakovida") | Chalukya Bhima |
| **Kundavai** | Chola queen | Vimaladitya |
| **Sheela Mahadevi** | Daughter married to Rashtrakuta Dhruva | Vishnuvardhana IV (father) |
!KEY APPSC 2025: Ayyana Mahadevi = queen of **Kubja Vishnuvardhana** (some books wrongly name Gunaga Vijayaditya III, c. 848–892).
### 9. NANNAYA AND TELUGU LITERATURE
- **Nannaya Bhattaraka** – **Adi Kavi**, court poet of **Rajaraja Narendra**; **began** the **Andhra Mahabharatamu** (c. **1045–65**) – the **first Telugu literary work**, in **Champu** style (verse + prose).
- The Telugu Mahabharata was begun by the **court poet Nannaya, not the king himself**.
| Person | Work / title / role |
|---|---|
| Nannaya | Andhra Mahabharatamu; titles Adikavi, Vagadheeshwara, Shabda Shasanudu; composed Nandampudi inscription |
| **Narayana Bhattu** | Ashtabhasha Kavisekhara; collaborator; received **Nandampudi agrahara** |
| **Pavuluri Mallana** | **Pavuluri Ganitamu** – Telugu verse version of **Mahaviracharya's Ganitasarasangraham**; **first scientific text in Telugu** |
| **Ugradityacharya** | **Kalyanakaraka** (medicine) |
| **Vemulavada Bhimakavi** | **Kavijanasrayam** |
| Chellavva | Gandharva-Vidya-Visarada |

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- First king: **Kubja Vishnuvardhana**; first capital **Pithapuram**.
- Last proper king: **Vijayaditya VII** (1075).
- First Chalukya-Chola king: **Kulottunga I (Rajendra Chalukya)**.
- First Telugu literary work: **Nannaya's Mahabharata**; first Telugu scientific text: **Pavuluri Ganitamu**.
- First Telugu inscription: **Kalamalla**; first Telugu verse inscription: **Addanki**.
- First mention of the Kakatiyas: **Magallu** inscription (Danarnava).
