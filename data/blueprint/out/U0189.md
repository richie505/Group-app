## U0189 | RAJA RAMMOHAN ROY AND THE BRAHMO SAMAJ; ABOLITION OF SATI; DEBENDRANATH AND KESHAB CHANDRA SEN
### 1. RAJA RAMMOHAN ROY (1772–1833) – LIFE AND TITLES
- Life: **1772–1833**; knew **Persian, Arabic, Sanskrit and English**; influenced by **Vedanta + Western rationalism**.
- Respected the traditional philosophies of the East (Upanishads, Vedanta) but wanted a **rational, scientific approach** (UPSC 2025: both statements correct).
- Went to **England in 1830** as envoy of Mughal emperor **Akbar II** to plead for his pension – among the first Indians to go abroad on such a mission.
- **Died at Bristol, 27 September 1833**; his **Samadhi is at Bristol**.
- Offered membership of **La Societe Asiatique of Paris** (est. 1822).
#### 1.1 Titles – Who Is Called What?
| Title | Person |
|---|---|
| **Father of Indian Renaissance**; Father of Bengal Renaissance; harbinger of the modern Indian Renaissance | Rammohan Roy |
| **First 'Modern Man' of India**; Maker of Modern India; Father of Modern India | Rammohan Roy |
| **'Raja'** | Rammohan Roy – conferred by Mughal emperor **Akbar II** (1830) |
| **Maharishi** | **Debendranath Tagore** (NOT Rammohan Roy) |
| **Lokahitawadi** | **Gopal Hari Deshmukh** (PYQ 2021) |
!TRAP 'Raja' was given by the **Mughal emperor Akbar II**, not by the British. 'Maharishi' = Debendranath.

### 4. ABOLITION OF SATI, 1829
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Law | **Bengal Sati Regulation, 1829** = **Regulation XVII** of the Bengal Code; Sati **illegal and punishable** |
| Governor-General | **Lord William Bentinck** (APPSC GS 2019, 2025) |
| Passed | **4 December 1829** |
| Extended | **Madras and Bombay** Presidencies, **1830** |
| Reformer behind it | **Rammohan Roy** – long campaign, appealed to Bentinck |
| Opposition | **Raja Radhakant Deb** – **Dharma Sabha (1830)** |
- **Sati** means 'holy and virtuous woman'.
- **Radhakant Deb (1784–1867)**: opposed reforms and the Sati ban, backed orthodoxy, yet **promoted Western education among girls**.
!KEY "Sati was abolished by ..." = **William Bentinck, 1829, Regulation XVII**.
### 5. BRAHMO SAMAJ – FOUNDING AND TENETS
#### 5.1 Founding
- Founded as **Brahmo Sabha**, **20 August 1828**, **Calcutta**, by **Raja Rammohan Roy** with Dwarkanath Tagore; later renamed **Brahmo Samaj** (by **Debendranath Tagore**).
- Founder = **Rammohan Roy** (APPSC GS 2018), NOT Vivekananda, Dayananda or Ramakrishna.
- APPSC G1 2008 asked the year with options 1826/1829/1834/1836 – correct **1828** was not offered.
#### 5.2 Tenets (statement type)
- **Monotheism**: one **formless, eternal God**; no human-form God; universal worship.
- Drew on the rational teachings of the **Upanishads, Bible and Koran**.
- **Banned** idols, animal sacrifice and sectarian symbols in its halls.
- Rejected **priestly ritual** (no need for a priestly class) and **caste**.
- **No scripture as final authority**; did **not** hold the Vedas faultless; **no belief in avatars**.
- **Collective (group) prayer**.
- A theistic (**Astika**) reform **within Hinduism**, not a new religion.
#### 5.3 Social Programme
- Against: child marriage, polygamy, female infanticide, caste.
- For: widow remarriage, women's education and property rights.
!PAIR Brahmo Samaj, Prarthana Samaj | reformist | reason and modern ideas || Arya Samaj, Deoband | revivalist | return to tradition
### 6. DEBENDRANATH TAGORE (1817–1905)
- Title: **"Maharishi"**.
- Founded the **Tattvabodhini Sabha** – **1839, Calcutta (Kolkata)** – to spread Rammohan Roy's ideas; organ **Tattvabodhini Patrika** (Bengali); merged into the Brahmo Samaj in **1859**.
- **Joined the Brahmo Samaj in 1843**; revived it after Roy's death.
- Made **Keshab Chandra Sen** acharya (1862), later took the title away (1865).
- Founder of the **Adi Brahmo Samaj** (after the 1866 split).
- Magazine **Brahman Sevadi** – defended Hinduism from Christian missionaries.
- Father of **Rabindranath Tagore** (youngest son), remembered on his death anniversary (10 Aug 2026).
### 7. KESHAB CHANDRA SEN (1838–1884)
- Most influential Brahmo of the later years.
- **1862**: made **acharya** by Debendranath – **first non-Brahmin acharya**.
- Took the Brahmo Samaj to **South India**; his **Bombay lectures inspired the Prarthana Samaj**.
| Year | Body / law |
|---|---|
| **1866** | **Brahmo Samaj of India** ('Bhartiya Brahmo Samaj') |
| **1870** | **Indian Reform Association** |
| **1872** | **Native Marriage Act** (Act III of 1872 = **Brahmo Marriage Act**): inter-caste marriage allowed; minimum marriage ages fixed |
| **1880–81** | **Nava Vidhan (New Dispensation)** |
!TRAP **Nava Vidhan = Keshab**, not Debendranath.
### 8. SPLITS OF THE BRAHMO SAMAJ
| Split | Cause | New body and leader | Parent body |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First, 1866** | Keshab's radicalism – inter-caste marriage, eclectic, Christian-influenced worship | **Brahmo Samaj of India** – Keshab | **Adi Brahmo Samaj** – Debendranath |
| **Second, 1878** | Keshab married his **under-age daughter** to the **Cooch Behar prince** (against the 1872 Act's spirit) | **Sadharan Brahmo Samaj** – **Ananda Mohan Bose, Shivnath Shastri** | Keshab's body |
!TRAP Adi Brahmo Samaj = **Debendranath's** body; Sadharan Samaj was **not** founded under Debendranath.
### 9. MAHARASHTRA REFORM
#### 9.1 Paramahansa Mandali
- **Poona, 1849**; founder **Dadoba Pandurang**; fought **caste discrimination**, promoted **women's education** (APPSC GS 2023).
- Religious reforms in Bombay were started by the Paramahansa Mandali.
#### 9.2 Prarthana Samaj
- **1867, Bombay**; founder **Atmaram Pandurang**; inspired by **Keshab Chandra Sen**.
- Leaders: **M.G. Ranade, R.G. Bhandarkar**.
- Worked for widow remarriage, women's education, **inter-caste dining** – by **persuasion**, not confrontation.
#### 9.3 Ranade and the National Social Conference
- Ranade: **Widow Marriage Association**, **Deccan Education Society**; **National Social Conference (1887)** with Raghunath Rao = **social wing of the Congress sessions**.
- Ranade is **NOT** linked to the Theosophical Society.

### 10. YOUNG BENGAL MOVEMENT
- Leader: **Henry Louis Vivian Derozio**, teacher at **Hindu College** (joined 1826); his students = **Derozians / Young Bengal**.
- **Academic Association (1828)**: debating club of Derozio and his students; read Voltaire, Hume, Locke and Tom Paine.
- Ideas: free thought, rationalism, attack on orthodoxy.
- Derozio was removed from Hindu College in **1831** and died of cholera in December 1831.

### 12. BRAHMO AND PRARTHANA SAMAJ IN ANDHRA
- First Andhra associated with the Brahmo Samaj: **Mannava Buchayya Pantulu** (not one of the Trinity).
- Andhra **"Trinity of the Brahma Samaj"**: **Kandukuri Veeresalingam, Raghupati Venkataratnam Naidu, Desiraju Peda Bapaiah**.
- First Andhra **Prarthana Samaj** centre: **Rajahmundry, 1878**, by **Veeresalingam** (APPSC 2019).
- **Bengal** = first Indian region to see a renaissance; in Andhra, **Rajahmundry** = centre of the renaissance.
