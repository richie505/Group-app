### 2. GENERATION, PLF AND GLOBAL RANK
- 2024-25: **1,824 TWh** – **thermal ~75%**, RE **~22%**, **nuclear ~3%** (nuclear only ~1.6% of capacity).
!TRAP Capacity is not generation: coal is ~42% of capacity but gives **over 70%** of electricity; non-fossil is over half of capacity but only ~22% of output.
| Source | PLF / capacity factor |
|---|---|
| Coal | **60–85%** |
| Nuclear | over 84% |
| Wind | **20–35%** |
| Solar | **20–25%** |
- **Southern Region** – highest thermal PLF (APPSC 2019).
- Global: India **3rd-largest electricity producer**; **3rd in RE capacity, 3rd in solar, 4th in wind** (Aug 2026).
- Per capita consumption: **1,460 kWh** (2024-25) from 957 kWh (2013-14).
- Coal generation fell 3.69% in 2025-26. NITI: coal's share of generation from ~**74% to 6–10% by 2070**.
### 3. NON-FOSSIL SHARE VS COMMITMENTS
- **50% non-fossil capacity** (Panchamrit/NDC 2030 goal) reached about **five years early** (Nov 2025); 51.55% on 30 Nov 2025.
- Debates: 40 GW RE without PPAs; ~2.1 TWh solar + wind curtailed; ~25 GW gas plants stranded.
### 4. TRANSMISSION AND PEAK DEMAND
- **One Nation-One Grid-One Frequency** = world's **largest synchronous grid**; over 5 lakh ckm.
- **Five regional grids** (N, E, W, NE, S); **Southern** synchronised last, **December 2013**.
- **POWERGRID (PGCIL)** = Central Transmission Utility.
- **Green Energy Corridor**: began with a **PGCIL study in 2012** (not CEA); implemented from **2015**.
- Record peak **270.82 GW** (21 May 2026). Peak shortage **4.2% (2014) → 0.03% (2025)**.
- **BESS** helps meet the **evening peak** with variable RE.
### 5. ELECTRICITY ACT 2003 AND REGULATORS
- Notified **10 June 2003**; consolidates laws on generation, transmission, distribution, trading; **delicensed generation**; **SEBs to be unbundled**; **open access**.
| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| **CEA** | Installed-capacity reports, **National Electricity Plan** |
| **CERC** | Central tariffs, inter-state matters; regulates power exchanges |
| **SERCs** | Retail tariffs; members appointed by State |
| **APTEL** | Appeals against CERC/SERC orders; set up **24 Aug 2004** |
- **APERC**: under **AP Electricity Reform Act, 1998** (before the 2003 Act). 6 Aug 2026: in-principle approval for **1,600 MW coal power** via tariff-based bidding under **Section 63**, **DBFOO**.
!PAIR CERC | central tariffs | exchanges || SERC | retail tariffs | APTEL appeals
### 6. RULES AND POWER MARKETS
| Rules | Provision |

### 7. DISCOM FINANCES AND LOSS METRICS
#### 7.1 Why SEBs Went Sick
- **Below-cost sale** to farm and domestic users; **high T&D losses**; **poor cost recovery**; rising commercial losses; project delays.
!TRAP APPSC-G2 2017: States' **autonomy** over generation/distribution was **not** a cause.
#### 7.2 Metrics
| Metric | Meaning | Trend |
|---|---|---|
| **AT&C loss** | Technical + commercial loss | **22.62% (FY14) → 15.04% (FY25)** |
| **ACS-ARR gap** | **Average cost of supply minus average revenue realised** per unit | ₹0.78 → **₹0.06 (6 paise)** |
- DISCOMs: loss ₹67,962 crore (2013-14) → **profit ₹2,701 crore** (FY25).
- States spend about **₹2.4 lakh crore/year** on farm and domestic subsidies.
- **PFC Integrated Rating** grades DISCOMs annually.
### 8. UDAY, RDSS AND SMART METERING
- **UDAY** (Ministry of Power, **November 2015**): **financial turnaround and revival** of DISCOMs – basically a **debt restructuring** plan; states took over **75%** of DISCOM debt (50% in 2015-16, 25% in 2016-17).
| Operational | Demand-side |
|---|---|
| Feeder/DT metering, smart meters, **transformer upgrades**, **coal swapping** | **LED bulbs (UJALA)**, efficient pumps, fans, ACs |
- **RDSS (2021)**: **₹3.03 lakh crore**; **reform-based, results-linked**; extended to **FY 2027-28**; nodal **REC and PFC**; smart meters on **TOTEX (PPP)** model.
- **EESL**: pools smart-metering demand; MoU with **PNGRB** for smart PNG meters.
!TRAP UDAY = debt takeover (financial); RDSS = results-linked infrastructure + reform grant.
