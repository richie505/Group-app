## U0095 | KAKATIYA ADMINISTRATION – NAYANKARA SYSTEM, TANK IRRIGATION, TRADE, MOTUPALLI ABHAYA SASANAM
### 1. NATURE OF THE KAKATIYA STATE
- Most scholars: a **"decentralised monarchy"**.
- **Romila Thapar**: **"metropolitan state"**; **Burton Stein**: compared it to a **"segmentary state"**.
- **Cynthia Talbot**: the Kakatiya age = **"Golden Age of the Shudras"**.
- Rulers were **non-Brahmins (Shudras)**; religion and politics became **non-Brahmin** (APPSC 2019); officials came from warrior and farmer groups.
- Prime ministers: **Malyala Hemadri Reddi** (Ganapatideva); **Muppidi Nayudu** (Prataparudra).
### 2. ADMINISTRATIVE UNITS AND OFFICERS
#### 2.1 Units (largest to smallest)
| Unit | Meaning | Head / note |
|---|---|---|
| **Rajyam / Rashtra / Mandala** | Largest province | King, prince or trusted feudatory |
| **Nadu / Bhumi** (also Seema, Padi) | District unit | **Naduvizhupati / Niyogi** |
| **Sthala** | A group of villages | Nayankara tenure; **Parupathyam Garu** |
| **Grama** | Village | Run by the **Ayagars** |
- Example Nadus: **Kammanadu, Pakanadu, Renadu, Velanadu, Sabbinadu, Venginadu**.

| Term | Meaning |

| Bahattara Niyogas | Collective name of all departments |
| **Pradvivaka** | **Judicial adviser** to the king (APPSC 2019) |

### 3. AYAGAR SYSTEM AND SOCIETY
- **Ayagars (Ayagarlu)** = **twelve (12)** village functionaries (APPSC G2 2012).
- Paid by **shares of produce + service lands**; held land **without any cess or tax**.
- **Samayas** = caste/occupational groups → **caste mobility**; the Kakatiyas themselves were Shudras.
- **Women donors:** **Mailamba** (Bayyaram tank) and **Kundamamba**; woman painter **Machaladevi**; **Surambika** (Govardhana Samudram, Reddi period).
!NUM 12 Ayagars (village) vs 72 Niyogas (centre) vs 77 Nayankaras.
### 4. NAYANKARA SYSTEM
- Military commanders (**Nayakas**) made administrators of **Sthalas/Mandalams**; each protected a **fort**.
- **Introduced/reorganised by Prataparudra II** (APPSC 2025); some say Rudrama began it in Ganapatideva's time and Prataparudra strengthened it.
- Number: **77 Nayankaras**.
- **Land instead of salary:** villages called **Nayakasthalam**; Nayankaras controlled **one-fourth** of the land.
- Obligations: collect revenue → **keep a fixed army for the king** → **pay a fixed annual sum**, remit the rest.
- Sources: **Baddena's Neeti Sastra Muktavali**; **Ekamranatha's Pratapa Charitra**.
| State | Arrangement |
|---|---|
| Kakatiya | **Nayankara** |
| Vijayanagara | **Amara Nayankara** – land **Amaram**, tribute **Kappam / Kist** |
| Mughal | **Jagir** |
- Break-up of the Amara-Nayankara system produced the **Poligars**.
### 5. MILITARY ORGANISATION
- **Chaturanga bala** = four arms.
- **Mula Balam** = king's own standing army; **Nayankara army** = Nayakas' contingents.
- Prataparudra's army: **100 elephants, 20,000 cavalry, 900,000 infantry**.
| Post | Holder |
|---|---|
| **Gaja Sahini** (elephants) | **Jayapa Senani** (Ganapatideva); Bendapudi Annayya, Induluri Annayya (Prataparudra II) |
| **Ashwa Sahini** (cavalry) | **Gangaya Sahini** (Ganapatideva); **Maraya** (Prataparudra II) |
| Commanders | **Padmanayakas** – Recherla Velama chiefs; later founded the Velama kingdom |
- Forts: **Giridurga** (hill), **Jaladurga** (water), **Vanadurga** (forest), **Sthaladurga** (plain).
- **Warangal fort**: **concentric walls and a moat**; the inner stone wall has **45 bastions**.
- Of **75 generals** in 1323 only 3 survived: **Prolaya Nayaka, Racherla Singama, Prolaya Vemareddy**.
### 6. LAND REVENUE AND TAXES
- **Ari / bhaga** (Bhumi sistu) = land revenue, generally **one-sixth** of produce.
- Land classes: **Magani** = irrigated **wet** land (food crops); **Metta / Velipolam** = rain-fed **dry** land.
- **Exempt:** temple lands, Brahmin grants, Ayagar lands, **Dashabandhamu** (tank upkeep) lands.
| Tax | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Koru** | Cess on **wet (Magani)** land |
| **Putti / Putti Wahindi** | Cess on **dry** land |
| Adhi | Land cess |
| **Pullari** | **Grazing** tax on grass |
| **Sunkam** | Customs/tolls |
| **Dari Sunkam** | **Road toll** |
| **Angadi Sunkam** | **Weekly-market** tax |
| **Kanika** | **Customary gift** to the king or officials |
| **Pellipannu** | **Wedding** tax |
| Alam | Tax on vegetables / produce |
#### 6.1 Not Kakatiya
| Tax | Dynasty |
|---|---|
| **Meruka** | **Reddis** – wet-land tax |
| Marriage tax | **Krishnadevaraya** removed it fully |
| **Moturpha** | **British** – tax on trades and looms (abolished 1861) |
!TRAP Kakatiya levies = Koru and Pullari; Meruka is a Reddi tax.
### 7. AGRICULTURE
- Crops: **rice** (fragrant variety near Orugallu), wheat, sorghum, **sugarcane**, oilseeds, sesame, **cotton**, ginger, horse gram, **betel**.
- **Maize was not grown** (reached India after 1500).
- Forest land cleared with **tax concessions**; new villages Ganapavaram, Ghanpur, Mahadevapur, Rudravaram, Bayyaram, Muppavaram.

| Chouda Samudram | Malyala Chauda Senani |

#### 8.1 Tank Terms
- **Samudrams** = large tanks; **Golusukattu cheruvulu** = **chain tanks** passing surplus water to the next.
- **Dashabandhamu** = tax-free land for tank upkeep.
- **Sapta Santanalu**: tank = one of **seven** merits (son, temple, garden, tank, agrahara, literature, treasure).
- **Porumamilla (Kadapa) inscription, 1369**: by **Bhaskara, son of Bukka I** (Vijayanagara) → **12 requisites of a good tank**.
- Kakatiya tank network still irrigates Telangana.

### 10. MERCHANT GUILDS AND TRADE ROUTES
| Guild / term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Samaya** | Caste/occupational group; merchant guild meeting in temple mandapams (APPSC G2 2019) |
| **Chitrameli** | Samaya (shreni) of **agricultural farmers** (APPSC G2 2019) |
| **Nakaram** | **Domestic** merchants |
| **Nanadesi** | **Foreign** (inter-regional) merchants |
| **Ubhaya Nanadesi** | **Both** – merchants of Desis, Paradesis and Nanadesis |
| **Ayyavole 500** | Guild named after **Aihole**; Nanadesi guild later joined it |
| **Virabanajam** | Licensed for Pakanadu, Vengi, Hanumakonda (**Tripurantakam inscription, 1282**) |
| Behari / Vyavaharam | Merchant / trade |
- Guild head: elected **Setti / Sreshthi**; members from many castes (Settis, Reddis, Nayakulu, Boyas, Dasaris, Komatis).
- Guilds had **seals, emblems, own militia**; settled disputes; acted as bankers; **no royal monopoly** over trade.
#### 10.1 Routes and Centres
- Kakatiyas controlled routes between the **Deccan interior and the eastern coast**.
- Poet **Ketana**: chief inland route **Kanchi → Nellore → Warangal → Ayodhya**.
- **Tripurantakam** linked Motupalli and salt towns like Pedaganjam to **Ballari** via Kurnool; short-cut Tripurantakam–Warangal via Nakarekal (Kridabhiramamu).
- Trade centres: Pedaganjam, Ghantasala, Gudivada, Macharla, Tripurantakam, Peruru, Manthena.
- **Exports:** textiles (fine muslin), spices, diamonds, gold, iron, rubies. **Imports:** **Arabian war horses**, pearls, silk, perfumes.
### 11. PORTS AND MOTUPALLI ABHAYA SASANAM
| Port | Note |
|---|---|
| **Motupalli** | Chief port; other name **Desiyakonda (Deseeyakkonda) Pattanam** (APPSC G2 2019) |
| **Krishnapatnam** | Nellore coast |
| **Hamsaladeevi** | Krishna delta |
- **Kara Pattanam** = ports collecting import/export duties.
#### 11.1 Abhaya Sasanam
- Issuer: **Ganapatideva, 1244 CE**, at Motupalli (pillar inscription).
- Content: **safety to foreign merchants**; cancelled unfair taxes; **compensation for shipwreck or theft**; no harassment by officials.
- Official posted: **Siddayya Devudu**.
- **Most important commercial document** of the period.
- Goods at Motupalli: **sandal, camphor, rose-water, ivory**, pearls, corals, copper, zinc, lead, silk, pepper, areca nuts.
- **Marco Polo** visited Motupalli in **Rudramadevi's** reign and described its diamonds and fine cloth.
