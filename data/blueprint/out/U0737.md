## U0737 | DRDO – VISION, MISSION, STRUCTURE, MAJOR LABORATORIES AND TECHNOLOGY CLUSTERS
### 1. FORMATION, MOTTO, VISION
- Formed **1958** by merging **TDEs** (Army's Technical Development Establishments), **DTDP** and **DSO** (Defence Science Organisation).
- **10 labs → about 52** (2026); HQ **DRDO Bhawan, New Delhi**.
- **Motto: "Balasya Mulam Vigyanam"** – the source of strength is science.
- Vision: strengthen the nation with modern **indigenous** defence technologies; goal **Aatmanirbharta**.
- Mission: sensors, weapons, platforms for the Services; combat effectiveness and troop well-being; indigenous technology base.
!TRAP **Tejasvinavadhitamastu** is NOT DRDO's motto.
### 2. STRUCTURE AND LEADERSHIP
- **Ministry of Defence → Department of Defence R&D** (parent).
- **Secretary DDR&D = Chairman DRDO**: **Dr Samir V. Kamat** (since 2022; Feb 2026); earlier Dr G. Satheesh Reddy.
- Level I Chairman; Level II **DG (Technology Clusters)** and DG (Corporate); Level III lab directors.

### 5. ACADEMIC CENTRES
- **JCBCAT** – **Jadavpur University, Kolkata**, set up with **DRDO** (not ISRO, BARC, CSIR).
- **DIA-CoEs** – **15** centres at IITs, IISc; **DIAT Pune** – deemed university.
- **Young Scientist Labs (2020)** – **5** labs, 5 futuristic areas.
- **Agnishodh** – **Indian Army's** cell at IIT Madras.

### 7. TDF, TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER, COLLABORATION
- **TDF**: MoD programme run by DRDO (Make in India); grants up to **₹50 crore** to MSMEs, start-ups.
- Since **2020** royalty-free patents; **2,100+** free ToTs; test facilities open to private sector.
- **Aug 2026:** ToT of **Astra, Rudram, Nag, VSHORADS** approved; **Agni and K-series** stay in state production.
- India-USA Joint Technology Group; DRDO tested **Gaganyaan crew-escape parachutes** for ISRO.
### 8. DRDO IN ANDHRA PRADESH
- **NSTL, Visakhapatnam**: **Varunastra**, mines, **SMART**.
- **Nagayalanka (Krishna)**: planned missile test range.
- **Kurnool**: ULPGM-V3 final trials.
- **Sri Sathya Sai district**: ADA flight-testing complex, ₹15,803 crore.
