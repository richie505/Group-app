## U0690 | NATURE, SCOPE AND CHARACTERISTICS OF SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY
### 1. SCIENCE VS TECHNOLOGY
| Basis | Science | Technology |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | **Know-why** | **Know-how** |
| Output | Laws, theories | **Products, processes** |
| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Basic research** | Curiosity-driven |
| **Applied research** | Solves a set problem |
| Translational | Takes lab results to use |
| **Invention** | **First creation** of a product or process |
| **Innovation** | Makes the invention **useful in market or society** |
### 2. CHARACTERISTICS OF SCIENCE
- Science: **empirical, testable (falsifiable), reproducible, systematic (orderly), self-correcting**; needs honesty and commitment to practical ideals.
- **Pseudoscience**: claims scientific status but **cannot be tested**.
- **J.V. Narlikar** (astrophysicist): **astrology** has not been considered a science (UPPSC 2018).
- Considered sciences: **astronomy** (celestial bodies), **cosmology** (origin and structure of the universe), **nanotechnology** (1-100 nm).
### 3. SCIENTIFIC METHOD
- **Observation, question, hypothesis, experiment, analysis, conclusion**, then replication / peer review.
- **Theory** = well-tested explanation of **why**; **Law** = what always happens, often mathematical (Newton's laws).
!TRAP A theory does **not** "become" a law.
### 4. BRANCHES AND INTERDISCIPLINARY FIELDS
| Type | Fields |
|---|---|
| Natural | Physics, chemistry, biology, earth, space |
| **Formal** | **Mathematics, logic, statistics** |
| Applied | Engineering, medicine, agriculture |
| **Biotechnology** | Biology + engineering |
| **Geoinformatics** (geospatial) | Geography + IT: **GIS, remote sensing, GPS** |
| **Data science** | Statistics + computing |
| Cyber-physical systems | Computing + physical systems via sensors |
- **ESSO** (2007, MoES): ocean, atmospheric and climate, geoscience, polar and cryosphere science.
### 5. TECHNOLOGY READINESS LEVELS (NASA)
| TRL | Stage |
|---|---|
| **1-3** | Basic principles to lab proof of concept |
| **4-6** | Validation, prototype |
| **7-9** | Operational demo, proven in actual use |
- **ANRF / RDI Scheme** funds projects at **TRL 4 and above**; strategic technologies; **Deep-Tech Fund of Funds**.
### 6. TECHNOLOGY TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Digital embossing** | Raised touch printing, **no chemicals or solvents**; **braille maps** (APPSC G1 2022) |
| **3D printing** | **Additive manufacturing**, layer by layer from a digital model; uses: confectionery, **bionic ears**, automotive, reconstructive surgery |
| **RFID** | Radio waves, **no line of sight**; **FASTag = passive RFID** (powered by toll reader) |
| NFC | 13.56 MHz, ~**4 cm**; **metro smart card = passive NFC** |
| Biometrics | Identification by unique physical (fingerprint, iris, face) or behavioural (voice, gait, signature) traits |
### 7. SCIENTIFIC TEMPER AND POLICY
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1962 | **Kerala Sastra Sahitya Parishad** |
| 1976 | **Article 51A(h)**, **42nd Amendment**: scientific temper, humanism, spirit of inquiry and reform |
| 1981 | **Statement on Scientific Temper** |
| 2001 / 2007 | DST Nano Science and Technology Initiative / **Nano Mission** |
| 2013 | **STI Policy** (first objective: spread scientific temper); **Maharashtra Anti-Superstition Act** |
- **Nehru** popularised "scientific temper" (The Discovery of India).
- **INSPIRE** (DST): all young students, no competitive exam, 3.75 lakh beneficiaries. **Vigyan Jyoti**: **girls only**, Classes 9-12, STEM.
- India: nanoscience publications **5th to 3rd**; **GII 2025: 38th**; **NRI 2024: 49th** (79th in 2019). National Science Day **28 Feb**.
