## U0818-6 | NATIONAL - NEW CENTRAL SCHEMES, MISSIONS AND THEIR FEATURES (OVERVIEW AND DATA)
**Group 1 Prelims | Group 2 Screening** | **HIGH** | Rows: G-7 National - new central schemes, missions and their features; S4-8 National - new central schemes, missions and programmes | PYQ importance: APPSC 102 questions | other exams 84
Period covered: overview and data – flagship schemes, new launches of 2025-26 and updates up to 2026.
!KEY Exam angle: **old name – new name** matching, and the **ministry** of newly launched schemes.
### 1. FLAGSHIP SCHEMES – MINISTRY AND OBJECTIVE
| Scheme | Ministry | Objective / latest status |
|---|---|---|
| **PMKVY (2015)** | **Skill Development & Entrepreneurship** | **Short-term skill training and certification** |
| **SVAMITVA (24 April 2020)** | **Panchayati Raj** | **Drone survey by Survey of India** gives **property cards** for **rural abadi land**; target **6.62 lakh villages** (2026) |
| **PAHAL (DBTL)** | Petroleum and Natural Gas | **Direct Benefit Transfer of LPG subsidy** into bank accounts; first phase in **54 districts** on **15 November 2014**, whole country from **1 January 2015**; relaunch of the **2013** DBTL scheme |
- **PAHAL** was recognised by **Guinness World Records (2015)** as the **largest cash transfer programme** for households.
- SVAMITVA was launched on **National Panchayati Raj Day**.
### 2. NEW LAUNCHES 2025-26 – MINISTRY AND KEY FACT
| Scheme | Ministry | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Adi Karmayogi Abhiyan** | **Tribal Affairs** | **Central Sector**; trains **20 lakh** grassroots **"Adi Karmayogis"** across **1 lakh tribal villages** |
| **Mission for Aatmanirbharta in Pulses** | **Agriculture** | **2025-26 to 2030-31**; **100% MSP procurement** of **tur, urad, masoor** for **4 years** |
| **Export Promotion Mission** | **Commerce & Industry** | **₹25,060 crore**, **FY26–FY31** |
| **RDI Scheme** (Research, Development and Innovation) | **DST (ANRF board)** | **₹1 lakh crore corpus**; approved **1 July 2025** |
| **Project BHISHM** | Under **Aarogya Maitri** | Portable **"cubes"** that work as **mobile hospitals**; **2 given to Sri Lanka** (April 2026) |
- **BHISHM** = **Bharat Health Initiative for Sahyog, Hita and Maitri**.
!TRAP Project BHISHM cubes are **mobile hospitals** under **Aarogya Maitri**; two went to **Sri Lanka**.
### 3. MISSIONS – MINISTRY, PERIOD AND TARGETS
| Mission | Ministry | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **National Quantum Mission** | **DST** (not MeitY) | **2023-24 to 2030-31**; **50–1,000 qubit** machines |
| **Gyan Bharatam Mission** | **Culture** | Announced **July 2025**; **₹491.66 crore**, **2025–31**; aims to digitise **1 crore+ manuscripts** |
- **Gyan Bharatam**: the **National Manuscript Survey** (began **March 2026**) has recorded **1.19 crore** manuscripts (**30 August 2026**).
- **AP cluster centre** of Gyan Bharatam: run by the **Sanatana Jeevan Trust** (palm-leaf manuscripts, **7 May 2026**).
!TRAP National Quantum Mission is under **DST**, not MeitY; Gyan Bharatam is under the **Ministry of Culture**.
### 4. REGIONAL AND RURAL DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMMES
#### 4.1 PURVODAYA (2024)
- Development plan for the **5 eastern States**: **Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Odisha, Jharkhand, West Bengal** (**not Chhattisgarh**).
- Infrastructure: includes the **Buxar–Bhagalpur expressway**.
- **Budget 2026-27** additions: **East Coast Industrial Corridor** (node at **Durgapur**); **5 tourism destinations**; **4,000 e-buses**.
#### 4.2 SPM Rurban Mission (2016)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Launched | **2016** |
| Clusters | **300+** |
| Plains/coastal cluster population | **25,000–50,000** |
| Desert/hilly/tribal cluster population | **5,000–25,000** |
| Funding | **Convergence** of existing schemes |
- **AP angle**: AP is a **PURVODAYA** State; **359 panchayats** classed as **Rurban** (March 2026).
!PAIR PURVODAYA | 2024 | 5 eastern States incl. AP || Rurban Mission | 2016 | 300+ clusters
### 5. EDUCATION, SKILL AND SPORTS PROGRAMMES
#### 5.1 Programmes – Ministry and Purpose
| Programme | Ministry | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **DIKSHA** | **Education** | **Digital content for school teaching** |
| **NSQF** (**National Skills Qualification Framework**) | **Skill Development** | **Competency-based qualification levels**; **Skill India certificates** mapped to it and **linked to DigiLocker**; care-giver training aligned to it |
| **APAAR** (**Automated Permanent Academic Account Registry**) | **Education** | **Lifelong student ID**; linked to the **Academic Bank of Credits** – **2,963 institutions** registered (**3 July 2026**) |
| **Unnat Bharat Abhiyan** | **MHRD** | Links **IITs, NITs and IISERs** with **villages** |
- **RUSA** (**Rashtriya Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan**, MHRD, higher education) now continues as **PM-USHA**, which ties central grants to **NEP 2020** compliance (not Samagra Shiksha).
#### 5.2 Khelo India (2018) – Revamp
- Revamped by the **Cabinet** in **August 2026**.
- Outlay: **₹29,054 crore** + **₹7,387 crore** for National Sports Federations = **₹36,441 crore** for **2026-27 to 2030-31**.
- New components: **Khelo India Feeder Schools** + **Utkrishta Vidyalayas**.
- Target: **Top-10 sporting nation by 2036**.
!NUM ₹36,441 crore = Khelo India 2026-31; ₹25,060 crore = Export Promotion Mission; ₹491.66 crore = Gyan Bharatam; ₹1 lakh crore = RDI corpus
