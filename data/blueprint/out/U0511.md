## U0511 | START-UP INDIA, STAND-UP INDIA, INNOVATION AND ENTREPRENEURSHIP ECOSYSTEM
### 1. WHO IS A START-UP?
#### 1.1 Definition and Criteria (DPIIT)
- **DPIIT** (Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade) is the **nodal agency for start-up recognition**.
- Eligible forms: **private limited company, registered partnership firm, LLP, cooperative**.
| Criterion | Start-up | Deep-tech start-up |
|---|---|---|
| Age (from incorporation) | Up to **10 years** | Up to **20 years** |
| Annual turnover | Not above **₹200 crore** | Not above **₹300 crore** |
- The older limit of ₹100 crore turnover has been revised to **₹200 crore**.
!TRAP An entity formed by **splitting or reconstructing** an existing business is NOT a start-up.
!PAIR Start-up | 10 years | ₹200 crore || Deep-tech start-up | 20 years | ₹300 crore
#### 1.2 Benefits of Recognition
- Income-tax deduction for **3 consecutive years out of 10** (Section **80-IAC**; for start-ups incorporated before 1 April 2030).
- **Self-certification** under labour and environment laws; **fast-track patents**.
- **Angel tax abolished for all investors** (Budget **2024-25**).
!TRAP There is NO permanent tax exemption - only a 3-of-10-years tax holiday.
### 2. START-UP INDIA
#### 2.1 Launch and Bodies
- Launched **16 January 2016**; **16 January = National Startup Day**. The decade of Startup India was marked on 16 January 2026.
- Nodal: **DPIIT**, Ministry of Commerce and Industry. (Udyam portal = MSME registration, a different thing.)
- **National Startup Advisory Council**: notified **22 January 2020**.
- **Startup India Yatra**: hunt for entrepreneurial talent in **Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities**.
- **Startup India Hub**: single online platform for all stakeholders of the ecosystem.
- **MAARG** (Mentorship, Advisory, Assistance, Resilience and Growth): national **mentorship** platform of Startup India (DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce and Industry); start-up onboarding from Nov 2022.
#### 2.2 States' Start-up Ranking Framework (DPIIT)
- Promotes **competitive federalism** by judging states' start-up policies and their implementation.
- Categories: **Best Performers, Top Performers, Leaders, Aspiring Leaders, Emerging Start-up Ecosystems**.
- **Andhra Pradesh = No. 1 (Best Performer) in States' Startup Ranking 2022**.
### 3. STAND-UP INDIA, 2016
#### 3.1 Key Features
| Feature | Fact |
|---|---|
| Launch | **5 April 2016** |
| Ministry | **Ministry of Finance** |
| Channel | **Scheduled commercial bank branches** |
| Target groups | **SC/ST and women** entrepreneurs aged **18+** |
| Loan range | **₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore** (composite: term loan + working capital) |
| Enterprise | **Greenfield** (first-time) only - manufacturing, services, trading, agri-allied |
| Branch norm | At least **one SC/ST and one woman borrower per bank branch** |
| Portal | **Standupmitra** |
| Refinance | **SIDBI** ₹10,000 crore |
!TRAP Stand-Up India covers **greenfield** projects only, NOT brownfield (expansion of existing units).
#### 3.2 Loan Terms
- Non-individual enterprises (company, firm): at least **51% shareholding and controlling stake** must be with an SC/ST or woman entrepreneur.
- Loan covers up to **85% of project cost**; borrower contribution at least **10%**.
- Working capital up to ₹10 lakh as an **overdraft** with a **RuPay debit card**; above that as a **cash credit limit**.
- Security: primary security of assets; collateral or guarantee of the **Credit Guarantee Fund for Stand-Up India Loans (CGFSIL)**.
- Repayment up to **7 years** with a moratorium of up to **18 months**.
- Status: extended for the 15th Finance Commission period, operational till **31 March 2025**; **2.75 lakh loans worth ₹62,790 crore** sanctioned (to 31 March 2025).
#### 3.3 Comparison
| | Start-up India | Stand-Up India | PM MUDRA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Launch | 16 Jan 2016 | 5 April 2016 | - |
| Ministry | DPIIT | Ministry of Finance | Ministry of Finance |
| Support | Tax, funds, self-certification | Bank loans ₹10 lakh-₹1 crore | Micro loans up to **₹20 lakh (Tarun Plus)** |
### 4. GOVERNMENT FUNDS FOR START-UPS
#### 4.1 Fund Table
| Fund | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)** | ₹945 crore; launched **19 April 2021**; up to ₹20 lakh grant (proof of concept) + ₹50 lakh investment; given **through incubators** (up to ₹5 crore each), not commercial banks |
| **Fund of Funds for Startups (FFS)** | **₹10,000 crore**; managed by **SIDBI**; invests through **SEBI-registered AIFs** (venture capital funds), not directly |
| FFS 2.0 | Another ₹10,000 crore for deep tech, space, AI |
| **Credit Guarantee Scheme for Start-ups (CGSS)** | Notified Oct 2022; collateral-free loans; guarantee via **NCGTC**; max **₹20 crore** per borrower; fee 1% for champion sectors |
- SISFS purpose: early-stage funding for **prototypes, proof-of-concept trials and market entry**.
- FFS purpose: sends capital into **venture capital funds** that invest in start-ups.
- CGSS replaced the earlier **Credit Guarantee Fund for Startups**.
!KEY SIDBI = manages Fund of Funds AND gives Stand-Up India refinance (both ₹10,000 crore).
### 5. START-UP FINANCING SOURCES
| Source | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Bootstrapping** | Founder's own savings and revenue |
| **Angel investors** | Individuals investing own money at idea/seed stage |
| **Venture capital** | Early-stage **equity investment with active guidance** for fast-growing firms |
| **Crowdfunding** | Small sums raised from many people, usually online |
| Incubators and accelerators | Space, mentoring, seed money |
| Bank loans and government programmes | e.g. MUDRA, Stand-Up India, CGSS |

#### 6.2 Atal Innovation Mission (NITI Aayog)
- Runs **Atal Tinkering Labs (ATLs)** and **Atal Incubation Centres**.
- **AIM 2.0 (2024)**: till **March 2028**; ₹2,750 crore; 2,500 new ATLs.
#### 6.3 Global Innovation Index (WIPO)
- **GII 2025: India 38th of 139 economies** (39th in 2024); 1st among lower-middle-income economies and in Central and Southern Asia.
- Top: Switzerland, Sweden, USA.
### 7. ECOSYSTEM DATA AND UNICORNS
- India = **3rd-largest start-up ecosystem** (17 Jan 2026).
- DPIIT-recognised start-ups: **2.47 lakh+** (12 Aug 2026); ~44,000 added in 2025 - highest ever.
- 20 lakh+ jobs since 2016; ~**48%** have a woman director; **53%** from Tier-2/3 cities.
- Indian tech start-ups raised **$9.1 bn in 2025**, up 23% (Nasscom-Zinnov, 26 Mar 2026).
#### 7.1 Unicorns
- **Unicorn** = privately held start-up valued at **US$1 billion or more** before listing.

### 8. ANDHRA PRADESH START-UP ECOSYSTEM
#### 8.1 AP Innovation Society (APIS)
- Set up in **2015**; **State nodal agency for start-ups and innovation** in AP.
- FY 2024-25: **6,935 start-ups**; 2,493 (36%) DPIIT-recognised; 1,203 women-led.
- **50 incubators** (7th largest start-up infrastructure); 175 mentors; 564 EISCs; **590 ATLs**.
- AP: **No. 1 in States' Startup Ranking 2022**; **9th in India Innovation Index 2022**.
#### 8.2 Ratan Tata Innovation Hub (RTIH)
- Launched Aug 2025; **hub-and-spoke** model - hub at **Amaravati (Mangalagiri)**; spokes Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Tirupati, Rajahmundry, Anantapuramu.
- Target over 5 years: **20,000 start-ups and 1 lakh jobs**; vision "**One Family, One Entrepreneur**".
- Under **AP Innovation & Startup Policy 2024-29**; ₹22-crore RTIH-SIDBI Seed Fund (7 Jul 2026).
- Davos: CM announced ₹50 crore entrepreneurship fund (20 Jan 2026).
#### 8.3 Other State
- **Student Startup and Innovation Policy (SSIP) 2.0** - **Gujarat**, January 2022 (11 Jan 2022 to 31 Mar 2027).

### 11. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS

3. Branch norm: **one SC/ST AND one woman** borrower per branch.
4. Turnover limit **₹200 crore**, not ₹100 crore; deep-tech ₹300 crore / 20 years.
5. Fund of Funds invests via **AIFs**, not directly in start-ups.
6. SISFS is given **through incubators**, not commercial banks.
7. Udyam Abhilasha = **SIDBI**, not Ministry of MSME.
8. Unicorn = **US$1 billion**, not ₹1,000 crore.
9. Tax benefit = 3 of 10 years, **not a permanent exemption**.
10. MUDRA ceiling **₹20 lakh** vs Stand-Up up to **₹1 crore**.
