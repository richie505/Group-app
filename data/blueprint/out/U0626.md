## U0626 | PENINSULAR DRAINAGE – EAST-FLOWING AND WEST-FLOWING RIVERS, RIVER BASINS, WATERSHED
### 1. DRAINAGE DIVISIONS – HIMALAYAN VS PENINSULAR
#### 1.1 Comparison
| Feature | Himalayan rivers | Peninsular rivers |
|---|---|---|
| Regime | **Perennial** (rain + snowmelt) | **Seasonal**, rain-fed (monsoon) |
| Valley | Deep **antecedent gorges** | **Broad, shallow, graded** valleys; older |
| Course | Meandering, shifting | Fixed, straight (hard rock) |
| Mouth | Large deltas | Deltas (east) / estuaries (west) |
| Use | Irrigation, navigation | Hydropower; delta irrigation (east) |
- **Antecedent rivers**: rise in the trans-Himalaya and kept their course by cutting gorges as ranges rose – **Indus, Sutlej, Brahmaputra**.
- **Chambal and Son** – **superimposed** rivers. **Damodar** – east-flowing in a **rift valley** (down-warping), Chota Nagpur, **Gondwana trough**.
- Deccan Plateau is higher in the west and slopes **east** – so most peninsular rivers flow east.
#### 1.2 Drainage Patterns
| Pattern | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Dendritic** | Tree-like branching on uniform rock/alluvium | Ganga plains; Godavari, Krishna |
| **Trellis** | Main streams in parallel valleys of folded hard and soft rocks; tributaries at right angles | Folded ranges |
| **Rectangular** | Right-angle bends along joints and faults | Vindhyan region |
| **Radial** | Streams flow outward from a central high point | **Amarkantak** – Narmada, Son, Mahanadi |
| **Centripetal** | Streams converge into a central low/lake | **Loktak** (Manipur) |
### 2. EAST-FLOWING VS WEST-FLOWING RIVERS
#### 2.1 Comparison
| Feature | East-flowing (Bay of Bengal) | West-flowing (Arabian Sea) |
|---|---|---|
| Rivers | Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri, Mahanadi, **Pennar, Palar**, Vamsadhara, Nagavali, Subarnarekha, Brahmani | **Narmada, Tapi, Sabarmati, Mahi, Periyar**, Mandovi, Sharavati, Kali, Vaitarna |
| Mouth | Large **deltas** | **Estuaries** → natural harbours |
| Use | Irrigation | Hydropower |
- East-flowing, north→south: **Subarnarekha, Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Pennar, Cauvery, Vaigai**.
- **Periyar** – west-flowing, via **Vembanad** to the Arabian Sea. **Krishna** does NOT reach the Arabian Sea (rises ~64 km from it, flows ~1,400 km to the Bay of Bengal).
- About **600** short, swift rivers drain the west coast.
- A-R: West-flowing rivers form **no deltas** because they do not carry alluvial sediments – both true, R explains A.
- **Tapi** – one of only **three** peninsular rivers running east to west; makes **no delta**.
- **Betwa** is not west-flowing; **Rapti** is a Ghaghara tributary flowing NW→SE.
#### 2.2 Rift-valley Rivers
| River | Rift between | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Narmada** | **Vindhya (north) and Satpura (south)** | Fault trough; **no delta** (leaves no silt) |
| **Tapi** | **Satpura and Ajanta** | Mouth at Suvali near Surat |
| **Damodar** | Chota Nagpur (Gondwana trough) | East-flowing |
#### 2.3 Inland and Aravalli-born Rivers
- **Luni** – rises in the Aravallis SW of **Ajmer** (called **Sagarmati**); **endorheic**, lost in the **Rann of Kutch** (not Gulf of Khambhat); largest river of the Thar; **511 km**; fresh upstream, saline downstream.
- **Sabarmati** – Aravallis (Rajasthan) → Gujarat; Ahmedabad.
- **Sahibi** – north-flowing river from the Aravallis.
- Ghaggar water is NOT used in the Indira Gandhi Canal.
!TRAP Luni ends in the Rann of Kutch; Mahi enters the Gulf of Khambhat.

### 5. GODAVARI SYSTEM
#### 5.1 Course
- **Dakshin / Vriddha Ganga**. Rises at **Trimbakeshwar (Nashik)** → Maharashtra (Nanded), Telangana, AP; basin also in Chhattisgarh, Odisha, MP, Karnataka, Puducherry.
- **2.7 km** wide at Rajahmundry; **Sir Arthur Cotton's anicut** at **Dowleswaram**.
- AP: ~**250 km**; delta **Konaseema**; **7 branches** – Gautami, Vasishta, Vainateya, Tulya, Bharadwaja, Kaushika, Athreya.
| Branch | Mouth |
|---|---|
| Gautami | **Yanam** |
| Vasishta | **Antarvedi** |
| Vainateya | Komaragiripatnam |
#### 5.2 Tributaries
| Bank | Tributaries |
|---|---|
| Left | **Purna, Pranhita** (Wardha + Wainganga + Penganga; joins near **Kaleshwaram**), **Indravati** (Kalahandi, Odisha → Bastar), **Sabari** (joins near **Kunavaram**) |
| Right | **Pravara, Manjira, Manair** |
- **Pranhita** = **largest** tributary by drainage; Sabari = main tributary in AP (with Sileru); also Kinnerasani, Maner.
- **Wainganga** joins via the Pranhita. Pranhita is a Godavari (not Mahanadi) tributary.
- **Sabari** = **Kolab** in Odisha; rises in Sinkaram Hills.
- Not Godavari tributaries: **Vamsadhara, Pennar, Ghataprabha**.
!KEY APPSC 2025: Indravati and Sabari = the Godavari pair; Ghataprabha is a Krishna tributary.
#### 5.3 AP Border Rivers
- **Sileru** – rises in AP, flows through Odisha; upper reaches = **Machkund**; joins the **Sabari** at the **AP–Chhattisgarh–Odisha tri-junction**.
- **Machkund (Matsya Kundala)** – Madugula Hills, Anakapalli; flows north then east into Odisha; **Duduma Falls**.
- **Tammileru** – boundary between Krishna and West Godavari districts; drains into **Kolleru**; Tammileru Dam.
- Other names: **Nagavali = Langulya**; **Arani = Araniyar**.
### 6. KRISHNA, MAHANADI AND KAVERI
#### 6.1 Krishna
- Rises near **Mahabaleshwar** (Sahyadri) → Maharashtra (Sangli), Karnataka, Telangana, AP → sea at **Hamsaladeevi**; second-largest east-flowing peninsular river.
- Cities/dams: **Vijayawada, Srisailam**, **Nagarjuna Sagar** (near Nandikonda), **Almatti** (Upper Krishna Project, Karnataka); flows through **Nagarjuna Sagar–Srisailam Tiger Reserve**.
- Splits at **Puligadda**, 64 km below Vijayawada; **Diviseema** between the branches. AP stretch ~485 km.
| Bank | Tributaries |
|---|---|
| Right | **Tungabhadra** (largest), **Ghataprabha** (**Gokak falls**), **Malaprabha**, Koyna, Yerla, Varna, Panchganga |
| Left | **Bhima** (joins in Telangana), Dindi, **Musi** (Anantagiri hills; Hyderabad) |
- **Tungabhadra** – 531 km (~149 km in AP); joins at **Sangameswaram** (**Nandyal** district). **Hagari = Vedavathi** (Veda + Avathi at Pura, Bababudangiri) joins it near **Siruguppa**; also Handri.
- **Raichur Doab** – between Krishna and Tungabhadra. **Tel** is a Mahanadi, not Krishna, tributary.
#### 6.2 Mahanadi
- Rises at **Sihawa** (Dhamtari); Chhattisgarh & Odisha (+ Jharkhand, MP, Maharashtra); **Cuttack**; delta near **Paradeep**; **29 dams**; does NOT pass through AP.
| Bank | Tributaries |
|---|---|
| Left | **Seonath (Sheonath), Hasdeo (Hasdo), Mand, Ib** |
| Right | **Ong, Tel, Jonk** |
- **Hirakud** – main dam 4,801 m, 15 km from Sambalpur. **Kuakhai** (Mahanadi delta) – Bhubaneswar; Bhubaneswar is not on the Mahanadi itself. **Jonk** is not in the Ganga basin.
#### 6.3 Kaveri (Cauvery)
- Talakaveri, Brahmagiri, Kodagu; mouth near **Poompuhar**; "**Ganga of the South**".
| Bank | Tributaries |
|---|---|
| Left | **Hemavathi**, Shimsha, Arkavathi |
| Right | **Kabini (Kapila)**, **Bhavani**, Amaravati |
- **Kabini** – from Panamaram + Mananthavady (Wayanad); joins at **Tirumakudalu Narsipur**.
- Forks thrice: islands **Srirangapatna, Sivasamudram, Srirangam**. **Mettur** dam. Basin projected dry until 2050.
#### 6.4 Pennar
- **Lifeline of Rayalaseema**; tributary **Jayamangali**; Nellore.
### 7. NARMADA, TAPI, MAHI AND OTHER WEST-FLOWING RIVERS
- **Narmada** – Jabalpur, **Bhedaghat**; projects **Sardar Sarovar, Indira Sagar, Omkareshwar** (Khandwa), **Bargi**. Tributaries: **Tawa** (longest; joins at Bandra Bhan, Narmadapuram), **Dudhi, Banjar, Hiran**.
- Narmada islands: **Aliabet** (largest), Sarfuddin, Dashan, Mehgam, Vegni.
- **Tapi** – Betul → MP, Maharashtra, Gujarat; Surat; via Gulf of Khambhat. **Purna** = Tapi tributary (not Narmada).
- **Mahi** – Minda (Dhar, MP) → Rajasthan (Banswara), Gujarat → **Gulf of Khambhat**; crosses the **Tropic of Cancer twice**.
!PAIR Mahi | Tropic of Cancer twice | India || Congo | Equator twice | Africa
- **Limpopo** ("Crocodile River") crosses the **Tropic of Capricorn** twice; drains into the Indian Ocean.
- **Kali (Kalinadi)** – rises near **Diggi**, Uttara Kannada; 184 km; Karwar, Kaiga.
- **Periyar** – Kerala; **Mullaperiyar** dam (Periyar lake is man-made); **Cheruthoni Dam** on a Periyar tributary, Idukki.
- **Mithi** – rises at **Vihar Lake**, Mumbai; Powai Lake water joins after 2 km; 18 km to the sea near **Mahim Bay**.

#### 8.4 Indus and Brahmaputra
- **Indus Waters Treaty (1960)**: eastern **Ravi, Beas, Sutlej** → India; western **Indus, Jhelum, Chenab** → mainly Pakistan. In **abeyance since 2025**; India rejected the Court of Arbitration award as "**null and void**" (May 2026).
- Indus flows rising vs Ganga flows falling (groundwater pumping).
- **Brahmaputra** – **Majuli**, Assam: world's largest inhabited river island, ~60 x 20 km; **first river island made a district**.
- **Wular** – fed by **Jhelum**; island **Zaina Lank** (Zain-ul-Abidin).
