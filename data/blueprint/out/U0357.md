### 2. FIFTH SCHEDULE – SCHEDULED AREAS
- Covers Scheduled Areas and STs in States **other than Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram**; **10 States**.
- **President** declares Scheduled Areas by Order (after consulting the Governor); can increase, decrease, alter boundaries, rescind.
- Tests: **preponderance of tribals**, compactness and fair size, viable administrative unit, **economic backwardness** (not industrial development).
- Does **not** end the State's executive power; the **Union may give directions**.
- **Governor** reports **directly to the President**, annually or when required (not CM to Home Ministry).
- **Para 5:** Governor may direct that any Act of Parliament or State **does not apply** or applies with modifications; makes **regulations for peace and good government** – ban/limit **tribal land transfer** to non-tribals, regulate land allotment and **money-lending**; after consulting TAC; needs **President's assent**.
- Parliament amends the Fifth and Sixth Schedules by **ordinary law** (not Art. 368).
!TRAP APPSC G2 2017: "Governor is NOT authorised to direct that an Act does not apply" is FALSE.
#### 2.1 Tribes Advisory Council (Para 4)
- Mandatory in every State with Scheduled Areas; President may direct one where STs exist but no Scheduled Areas.
- **Not more than 20** members, about **three-fourths ST MLAs**; advises on ST welfare referred by the Governor – advisory, no legislative/judicial power.

### 3. SIXTH SCHEDULE – TRIBAL AREAS
- **Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram** only; not Nagaland (371A), **Manipur** (371C), Arunachal, Sikkim.
- "Tribal Areas" = Parts I, II, IIA, III of the table to para 20.
- **10 ADCs:** Assam 3, Meghalaya 3, Tripura 1, Mizoram 3.
- **ADC: 30 members** – **26 elected**, **4 nominated** by Governor; 5 years (not 24 + 6).
- **Autonomous region** = unit within an autonomous district, with a **Regional Council**; Governor organises districts and alters boundaries.
- Powers: **legislative** (land, forests, inheritance, marriage, village administration, social customs – not citizenship); **judicial** (village courts; Governor sets High Court reach); **executive/financial** (land revenue, taxes).
- Laws apply as directed by the **Governor in Assam**, the **President** in Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram.
- Income Tax Act **s. 10(26)** – exempts STs in specified Sixth Schedule areas.
!TRAP APPSC G2 2025: "Sixth Schedule applies wherever STs exceed 40%" – FALSE; key "None".
### 4. FIFTH VS SIXTH
| Basis | Fifth | Sixth |
|---|---|---|
| Relates to | Scheduled Areas | Tribal Areas |
| Key body | TAC (advisory) | ADCs/Regional Councils |
| Declared by | President | Specified in Schedule |
| Union directions | Yes | Stays with State executive |
| Laws | Governor may exclude | Governor (Assam)/President |
| PESA | Applies | Does not apply |
- Purpose of both: **protect ST interests**, land and culture.

### 6. PESA ACT, 1996
- **Panchayats (Extension to the Scheduled Areas) Act** – passed 1996; in force **24 December 1996**.
- Need: **Art. 243M(1)** bars Part IX in Scheduled/Tribal Areas; **243M(4)(b)** lets Parliament extend it by ordinary law.
- Extends Part IX to **Fifth Schedule** areas only, in **10 States: AP, Telangana, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, HP, Jharkhand, MP, Maharashtra, Odisha, Rajasthan** (not Kerala). Not in **Assam, Meghalaya, Tamil Nadu** (RPSC 2016).
- Objectives: self-rule; traditional practice; **Gram Sabha as nucleus**; stop higher tiers usurping powers. NOT an objective: **autonomous regions** (UPSC 2013).
#### 6.1 Gram Sabha powers
| Subject | Power |
|---|---|
| **Minor forest produce** | **Ownership** |
| Land acquisition / resettlement | **Consultation** (not consent) |
| **Minor minerals** | Recommendation mandatory before licences/leases |
| Liquor; money-lending | Prohibit/regulate; control |
| Tribal land | Prevent alienation; restore |
| Village markets, social-sector institutions, Tribal Sub-Plan | Control |
- Habitation-level Gram Sabha (**para, majra, tola**).
- **Presiding officer:** ST member elected by the Gram Sabha (not Sarpanch), for one year; else oldest ST woman present.
- STs **at least 50%** seats; **all chairpersons ST**; State may nominate unrepresented tribes.
- **Zila Panchayat:** plans minor water bodies; controls social-sector institutions and Tribal Sub-Plans.
- Conflicting State laws lapse **one year** after assent.
### 7. CASES, ACTS AND GAPS
- **Samatha v. State of AP (1997, SC):** tribal land in Scheduled Areas leased to private parties for **mining** is **null and void**.
- **Niyamgiri (2013):** **Gram Sabhas** decided; rejected **Vedanta** bauxite project.
- **FRA 2006:** forest rights; MFP ownership; public utilities need Gram Sabha recommendation; OTFD – **three generations (75 years)** before 2005.
- **AP Land Transfer Regulation 1 of 1959**, amended by **Regulation 1 of 1970** – bans transfers tribal–non-tribal and among non-tribals.
- Gaps: **40%+** PESA representatives could not explain a Gram Sabha (**MoPR, Dec 2025**); MFP and minor-mineral rules least implemented; **Tana Bhagats** – no consent for **Bharatmala** (breach of **CNT Act 1908**). **Odisha** – only draft rules (Apr 2026).
### 8. ANDHRA PRADESH
- **Eastern Ghats agency tracts**; **12.9 lakh** STs; **34** communities.
- **Alluri Sitharama Raju** district – from Visakhapatnam; tribal-majority with PVTGs.

- **Tribal Medical College, Paderu** – 2024-25, 50 seats. **Araku** – ST Lok Sabha seat.
