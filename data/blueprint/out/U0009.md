## U0009 | IVC ECONOMY – AGRICULTURE, CROPS, ANIMALS, CRAFTS, TRADE, SEALS, WEIGHTS, MELUHHA
### 1. EARLIEST AGRICULTURE AND SETTLED LIFE
| FIRST | Site / fact |
|---|---|
| Earliest settled life (c. 7000 BC) and agriculture in subcontinent | **Mehrgarh** (Kachhi plains, Balochistan); earliest wheat; first farmers grew mainly **barley** |
| First agriculture per latest research; oldest **paddy** | **Lahuradeva** (Sant Kabir Nagar, UP, Ganga plain) |
| Once thought oldest paddy | **Koldihwa** (Belan valley, UP) |
| First animal domestication and agriculture | **Kili Gul Muhammad** (c. 4000 BC) |
| Rice and cattle pens (not barley) | **Mahagara** |
| First cereal used by man | **Barley** (c. 8000 BC) |
| Earliest iron in India | **Atranjikhera** – on the **Kali** river; found by **Cunningham** (1861-62); dug by **R.C. Gaur** (1962) |
- Kalibangan c. 3500 BC. IVC = **Bronze Age**; iron spread in North India **1000-600 BC**; Iron Age began in **Later Vedic** period, c. 1000 BC.
### 2. CROPS → SITE
| Crop | Site of evidence |
|---|---|
| **Wheat** (largest finds) | **Harappa**; Mehrgarh |
| **Barley** | **Banawali** |
| **Rice** husks (first in Gujarat, c. 1800 BCE) | **Lothal**, **Rangpur** |
| **Cotton** | **Mohenjodaro**; Mehrgarh |
| Millets and rice as staple | Rangpur, Surkotada |
| **Garlic** (earliest) | **Balu** (Haryana) |
| Indigo | Rojdi |
- Also: peas, lentil, chickpea, **sesame**, **mustard**, dates, melon, grapes.
- **Harappans first cultivated cotton** and made cotton textiles; Greeks called cotton **Sindon**. Uraiyur traded cotton.
- **Maize** came with the **Portuguese**; unknown in India before.
- Rig Vedic economy = **pastoral**; cotton, rice, wheat not mentioned in the Rig Veda.

| Evidence | Site |

### 4. ANIMALS – KNOWN AND UNKNOWN
| Status | Animals |
|---|---|

| Wild but known | **Elephant, tiger, rhinoceros**, deer, gharial |
| On seals | **Unicorn** (one-horned, most common), zebu bull, elephant, tiger, rhinoceros, buffalo, deer, sheep |

### 5. METALS AND RAW MATERIALS → SOURCE
- **Copper** = first metal used by man. Used: **copper, bronze, gold, silver, lead**, brass. **Iron NOT found** (CGPSC 2011).
- **Bronze = copper + tin**; tools, weapons, **Dancing Girl** by **lost-wax** casting.
- Harappans **pioneered silver** use; silver was **imported**.
| Material | Source |
|---|---|
| **Copper** | **Khetri** (Rajasthan), **Oman** |
| **Tin** | Afghanistan, Iran |
| **Gold** | **Kolar** |
| **Lapis lazuli** | **Badakhshan** via **Shortughai** (Afghanistan, trading colony) |
| **Chert** | **Rohri** – blades used alongside metal |
| Shells | Saurashtra coast, Balakot |
### 6. CRAFTS AND INDUSTRIES → CENTRE
| Craft | Centre |
|---|---|
| Bead-making (carnelian, steatite, lapis) | **Chanhudaro**, Lothal, Kalibangan |
| Shell work | **Nageshwar, Balakot**, Lothal |
| Spinning, weaving | Mohenjodaro; **Chanhudaro** = "**Lancashire of India**" |
| Ivory, copper, bead workshops | **Kuntasi** |
| Salt | **Kerala-no-dhoro** |
| Earliest furnace | **Kot Bala** |
- **Spindle-whorls** common in houses; **no spinning wheel** found (UPSC 2026).
- **Boat models**: **Mohenjodaro** (boat on seals) and **Lothal** (clay boats, terracotta ship, ship on a seal).
### 7. EXTERNAL TRADE – MELUHHA
| Mesopotamian name | Region |
|---|---|
| **Meluhha** | **Indus (Harappan) region** (APPSC 2025) |
| **Dilmun** | **Bahrain** |
| **Magan** | **Oman** or Makran |
- **Sargon of Akkad** (c. 2300 BCE) records name ships from Meluhha.
- **Exports**: carnelian beads, ivory, timber, cotton. **Imports**: silver, tin, woollens.
- Indus seals at **Susa, Ur, Nippur, Failaka, Bahrain**; seals, beads, weights in **Oman, Bahrain, Iraq, Iran** – **not Egypt**.
- In IVC: three Mesopotamian cylinder seals at Mohenjodaro.
- Network across the **Arabian Sea and Persian Gulf**; Mesopotamia = closest outside contact.
- **Nov 2025**: second **Dilmun temple** (c. 1900-1800 BCE) on **Failaka**, Kuwait.
### 8. INTERNAL TRADE AND PORTS
- Internal trade by **barter**; **bullock carts and boats**. Reach: South India, UP, Maharashtra, Bihar, Rajasthan.
- **Lothal** (Gujarat): **world's earliest tidal dockyard**, joined to the **Gulf of Khambhat**; brick-lined, **218 m x 37 m**; sluice gates; dug by **S.R. Rao, 1954** (not Wheeler, 1931); **Persian Gulf-type seal** → contact with Bahrain and Mesopotamia; city trading with West Asia (BPSC 2024).
- **Harappa** = inland city on the **Ravi**, not a port; Harappa and Mohenjodaro had warehouses.
| Port | Location |
|---|---|
| **Sutkagendor** | **Makran coast**, Baluchistan, near Iran border; **westernmost** |
| Balakot | Sindh coast; shells |
| Kuntasi, Bhagatrav, Prabhas Patan | Gujarat coast |
| **Shortughai** | Afghanistan – trading post for lapis |
### 9. SEALS
- Mostly **square** (animal + script); **rectangular** = script only; **cylindrical** (Mesopotamian type) = Mohenjodaro, Kalibangan; **button** and **Persian Gulf** seals = **Lothal**.
- **Pashupati seal** (Mohenjodaro): seated yogic figure ringed by animals; bull seal = Mohenjodaro (RPSC 2008).
- Uses: **authentication, ownership**, sealing packages, identity – **not coins**.
- Jun 2026: cylindrical steatite seal at **Rakhigarhi** – alligator motif, five characters.
### 10. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES
- Weights mostly **chert** (also limestone, steatite), **cubical**; unit ~**13.63 g**; binary ratio "16".
| Scale | Site |
|---|---|
| **Ivory** | **Lothal** (APPSC G2 2011) |
| **Shell** | **Mohenjodaro** |
| **Copper** | **Harappa** |
- Same scales and weights over **~8 lakh sq km** → central regulation of trade.
- Farm surplus + trade + crafts + standard weights → **urbanisation**.
