## U0436 | INDIA IN UN PEACEKEEPING; DEVELOPMENT PARTNERSHIP; DIASPORA POLICY; SOFT POWER
### 1. UN PEACEKEEPING – PRINCIPLES
- **Three principles:** **consent** of the parties, **impartiality**, **no use of force except in self-defence and defence of the mandate**.
- **UNSC** starts and ends missions by resolution; **Department of Peace Operations** manages; funded by a **separate assessment scale**.
- **CUNPK, New Delhi** (Indian Army) trains 10,000+ a year, ~2,000 foreigners (2026).
!TRAP "Force only for self-defence" or "force for political obedience" is NOT a principle.
