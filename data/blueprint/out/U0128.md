## U0128 | MUGHAL EMPIRE – BABUR: PANIPAT, KHANWA, CHANDERI, GHAGHRA; TUZUK-I-BABURI
### 1. BABUR – BACKGROUND AND CAREER
- Given name: **Zahir-ud-din Muhammad**; "Babur" = **tiger** (a nickname/title).
- Born **1483** in **Farghana** (Central Asia).
- Descent: **Timur** on father's side, **Chingiz Khan** on mother's side.
- Mughals were **Chagatai Turks** – named after Chagatai, **Genghis Khan's second son**, ruler of Central Asia and Turkistan.
- Babur's arrival set up the **Timurid dynasty** in India.

### 3. FIRST BATTLE OF PANIPAT – 1526
- Babur defeated **Ibrahim Lodi**, the **last Delhi Sultan** (Lodis = last Sultanate dynasty).
- Ended the Delhi Sultanate; began Mughal rule.
- Numbers: Babur's **12,000** vs about **1,00,000** of Ibrahim's troops.
- Gains: **Delhi and Agra** (APPSC 2023) + the **Lodi treasure**.
- He shared the treasure lavishly → nickname **"Qalandar"**; treasury left depleted for Humayun.
!TRAP Babur's side used **no war elephants** at Panipat. The Rumi carts were a barrier for guns, not elephants.
### 4. MILITARY TACTICS AND TECHNOLOGY
| Term | Meaning | Origin |
|---|---|---|
| **Tulughma** | Flanking units wheel round to encircle the enemy | **Uzbek** |
| **Rumi method / araba** | Carts chained as a barrier for matchlockmen and guns | **Ottoman** field defence |
| Artillery | Commanded by **Ustad Ali** and **Mustafa** | Ottoman gunners |
#### 4.1 Gunpowder Debate
- Panipat I = **first large-scale use of field artillery** in North Indian warfare.
- Gunpowder and firearms were **not wholly unknown** in India before Babur (used to break fort walls).
- Babur is credited with its use in **cannons and guns**.
!TRAP Babur did NOT introduce the arch and dome; those came with the Delhi Sultanate.
### 5. BATTLE OF KHANWA – 1527
- Coalition: **Rana Sanga's** Rajput confederacy + Afghan **Mahmud Lodi** + **Hasan Khan Mewati**.
- Tactics: **trenches and barricades** against Sanga's elephants; artillery decisive again.
- Religious appeal before battle: declared **jihad**, **renounced wine**, remitted the **tamgha** (trade tax) for Muslims.
- Title after victory: **"Ghazi"**.
!PAIR Qalandar | sharing the Lodi treasure | after Panipat || Ghazi | holy warrior | after Khanwa
### 6. EMPIRE AT BABUR'S DEATH – SCOPE AND LIMITS
- Extent: **Kabul, Badakhshan → Punjab, Delhi, Agra → borders of Bihar** (after Ghaghra).
- Included Kabul, Punjab and modern Uttar Pradesh – **not modern Rajasthan**.
- Ruled through **jagirs** and **paraganas**; **Badakhshan** was given to Humayun.
- Humayun took the throne on **29 December 1530**.
#### 6.1 Limits of the 4-year Rule
- Mughal authority had **shallow roots**; Bengal unconquered; Afghans still claimed Delhi.
- Gujarat and Malwa united under **Bahadur Shah**.
- **No administrative system** was set up; treasury empty.
!TRAP **Mansabdari** system = **Akbar's**, not Babur's.
- AP / South angle: Babur was a contemporary of **Krishnadevaraya** and Golconda's **Sultan Quli**; Mughals did not interfere in the Deccan in his time.
### 7. TUZUK-I-BABURI (BABURNAMA)
- Babur's memoirs, written in **Chagatai Turki** (Turkish).
- Translated into **Persian** (1589) under Akbar by **Abdur Rahim Khan-i-Khanan**; into English first by **Leyden and Erskine**, later by **Annette Beveridge** (from the Turki original).
- On India: **five Muslim and two Hindu powers**; Hindu ones = **Vijayanagara and Mewar**.
- Praised **Krishnadevaraya** as the most powerful ruler in India.
!TRAP Babur wrote in Turki, but the **Mughal court language was Persian** (UPPSC 2025: assertion true, reason false).

- First Mughal emperor: Babur; last Delhi Sultan: Ibrahim Lodi.
- First large-scale field artillery battle in North India: Panipat I.
- First Mughal garden: Ram Bagh, Agra.
