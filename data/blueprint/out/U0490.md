### 4. PMKSY AND WATERSHED DEVELOPMENT
#### 4.1 PMKSY
- Launched **1 July 2015**; umbrella irrigation scheme.
- Components: **AIBP**, **Har Khet Ko Pani** (assured irrigation; CAD and water management), **Per Drop More Crop** (micro-irrigation; now under PM-RKVY), **Watershed Development**.
- **Micro Irrigation Fund**: corpus **Rs 5,000 crore** with **NABARD**.
#### 4.2 Watershed
- **Hariyali guidelines**: from **1 April 2003**.
- Hariyali, IWDP, NWDPRA merged into **IWMP** (2008 common guidelines); IWMP subsumed in PMKSY as **WDC-PMKSY** (guidelines 26 Oct 2015); funding **90:10 → 60:40**; **WDC 2.0** adds springsheds.
- **Neeranchal National Watershed Project**: **World Bank** ($178.5 million, approved 2014; CCEA Oct 2015); **nine States incl. AP and Telangana**; 2016-2022.
- **Tarun Bharat Sangh**: Rajasthan NGO founded by **Dr Rajendra Singh**.
### 5. GROUNDWATER – CGWB AND ATAL BHUJAL
- CGWB categories (stage of extraction): **Safe, Semi-critical, Critical, Over-exploited**, + **Saline**; **not "adequately exploited"**.
- India stage: **60.63% (2025)**, up from 60.47% (2024).
- **Atal Bhujal Yojana**: **December 2019**; **World Bank**-aided **Central Sector**; **Ministry of Jal Shakti**.
- 7 States: **Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, MP, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, UP**; **AP NOT included**.
- Focus: **demand-side** community management via GP **Water Security Plans**.
### 6. APWALTA ACT, 2002
- **AP Water, Land and Trees Act, 2002**: against careless **borewell** digging; controls groundwater use; promotes **tree planting**.
- **Rainwater harvesting compulsory on plots of 200 sq m and above**.
- Over-exploited villages notified (GEC 2024-25: 258 villages proposed).
### 7. PM-KUSUM AND MICRO-IRRIGATION
| PM-KUSUM (2019, **MNRE**) | Content |
|---|---|
| A | **10,000 MW** decentralised grid-connected solar plants (0.5-2 MW) |
| B | **14 lakh** standalone solar pumps (up to 7.5 HP); none in dark zones |
| C | Solarisation of grid-connected pumps, incl. **feeder-level** |
- Objective: **end diesel/kerosene dependence**, energy security, raise farmers' income; helps raise non-fossil capacity to **40%**.
- JPSC PYQ: PM-KUSUM = **"agricultural solar pump system"**.
- Concern: solar pumps may encourage **over-drawing of groundwater**.
- **APMIP** (since **2003**): 42.25 lakh acres; 15.09 lakh farmers; **Rayalaseema 64%**; **AP ranks 1st** in micro-irrigation.
!TRAP Atal Bhujal = **Jal Shakti** (groundwater demand); PM-KUSUM = **MNRE** (solar pumps).
