## U0098 | TEMPLE ARCHITECTURE STYLES OF ANDHRADESA (11TH–16TH CENTURIES)
### 1. TEMPLE COMPONENTS AND THE THREE ORDERS
- Core: **garbhagriha** (sanctum) + **mandapa** (hall); **shikhara** (north) or **vimana** (south); axial **vahana** with **dhvaja** pillar.
- **Trikutalaya**: three shrines on one plan (e.g. **Thousand Pillar Temple**).
- **Kalyana mandapa**: the Vijayanagara thousand-pillared hall = peak of the pillared-hall tradition. **Ornate gopurams** mark the Vijayanagara phase; **Siddhavatam (Kadapa)** has a western gopuram recorded in a **1233 CE** Tamil-Grantha inscription of **Chola Rajendra III**.
| Order | Marker | Andhra example |
|---|---|---|
| **Nagara** | Curvilinear shikhara | Kalinga variant: Mukhalingam, Simhachalam |
| **Dravida** | Pyramidal vimana, gopurams | Eastern Chalukya delta temples; Vijayanagara |
| **Vesara** | Blend; stepped shikhara | **Kakatiya** temples |
- **Pattadakal**: Papanatha = Nagara; Virupaksha = Dravida.
### 2. STYLES BY DYNASTY
| Dynasty | Features | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Eastern Chalukya** | Dravidian, shaped by Pallava and Badami Chalukya forms, increasingly local; Krishna-Godavari delta; **laterite and limestone** | **Vijayaditya II – 108 temples**; Draksharama; Biccavole group |
| **Chalukya-Chola** | Pallava planning + early Chalukya elements; model for the Godavari region | **Draksharama** (APPSC 2025) |
| **Kakatiya** | Vesara/transitional; star platforms, **sandbox foundations**, **floating bricks**, ekasila basalt – not wooden superstructures | Ramappa (**1213**), Thousand Pillar (**1163**, Shiva, Vishnu, Surya – not Brahma) |
| **Reddi** | Renovation and endowment of old shrines; forts | **Kondaveedu** (Guntur–Palnadu, near Chilakaluripet); Srisailam |
| **Vijayanagara** | Chalukya and Chola roots; **Sahasra Stambha Mandapa**, composite carved pillars | Virupaksha, Vithala (Hampi); Krishnadevaraya's gifts to Simhachalam |
!TRAP APPSC 2025 wrong statement: Biccavole Rajarajeswara = Rajaraja Narendra's. Correct: Draksharama = Chalukya Bhima I.
!TRAP Vijayanagara did NOT draw mainly on Hoysala and Kalinga styles. Kondaveedu is NOT near Eluru.
### 3. BUILDING MATERIALS
| Material | Dynasty | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Sandstone hill, cut top-down, four storeys | **Vishnukundinas** | **Undavalli caves** (4th–5th c.) |
| Granite monolith | Vishnukundinas | Reclining **Ananta Padmanabha** |
| Black basalt (ekasila) | **Kakatiyas** | Thousand Pillar Nandi; Ramappa pillars |
| Floating bricks | Kakatiyas | Ramappa vimana |
- **Sandbox**: pits of sand, stone chips and earth protect against earthquakes on black cotton soil.

### 5. PANCHARAMA, JYOTIRLINGA AND SHAKTI PEETHA TEMPLES
| Pancharama | Place |
|---|---|
| Amararama | Amaravati |
| **Draksharama** | Draksharama – Chalukya Bhima I; Reddis repaired and funded it |
| **Somarama** | **Bhimavaram** |
| Ksheerarama | Palakollu |
| **Kumararama** | **Samalkot** (Chalukya Bhimavaram) – Reddi additions |
- **Srisailam**: **Prolaya Vema Reddy** completed steps to **Patalaganga (1346)**; **Anavema Reddy** built **Veera Siromandapam**.
- Shakti peethas: **Bhramaramba** – Srisailam; **Manikyamba** – Draksharama.
- Kotappa Konda (Palnadu): **Trikoteswara** (Shiva).
- **2026:** master plans for 22 major temples (Simhachalam, Annavaram, Srikalahasti, Kanipakam).
### 6. TEMPLE SCULPTURE
- **Ramappa madanikas** (bracket figures) follow **Jayapa Senani's Nrittaratnavali** (APPSC 2019); Jayapa = commander of **Ganapatideva**; text covers **marga and desi**.
- Ramappa: **UNESCO World Heritage Site 2021**.
- **Nataraja Ramakrishna** revived **Perini Shiva Tandavam** from Ramappa panels.
- Kakatiya painting (**Machaladevi**) led to the **Lepakshi** style.
