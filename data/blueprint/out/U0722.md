## U0722 | DATA PROTECTION AND PRIVACY - PUTTASWAMY, DPDP ACT, DATA LOCALISATION
### 1. RIGHT TO PRIVACY CASES
- **K.S. Puttaswamy v UoI** (**24 Aug 2017**): **9-judge**, **unanimous**; privacy intrinsic to life and liberty under **Article 21** and **Part III**.
- Overruled **M.P. Sharma (1954)** and **Kharak Singh (1962)** insofar as they denied privacy.
- Tests: **legality** (a law needed; public aim alone not enough), **legitimate aim**, **proportionality**.
- **Gobind v State of MP (1975)**: privacy part of personal liberty. **PUCL v UoI (1997)**: interception safeguards.
### 2. AADHAAR JUDGMENT (26 SEP 2018)
- **5 judges, 4:1**; dissent **Justice D.Y. Chandrachud**.
| Upheld | Struck down |
|---|---|
| Aadhaar Act; welfare use | Mandatory **bank account** and **mobile SIM** linking |
| **PAN/ITR linking** (Sec. **139AA**, IT Act) | Sec. 57 private-entity use |

### 4. DPDP ACT 2023 - SCOPE AND TERMS
- Only **digital** personal data; excludes **non-personal** data; covers foreign entities offering goods/services in India.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Data Principal | Individual whose data is processed; for a **child (under 18)**, parent/guardian |
| Data Fiduciary | Decides purpose and means (GDPR: **Data Controller**) |
| Significant Data Fiduciary | **DPO in India**, independent auditor, impact assessment (no consent exemption) |
| Consent Manager | Registered entity to give, manage, withdraw consent |
### 5. RIGHTS, DUTIES AND CHILDREN
- Rights: information/access, correction, **erasure (Sec. 12)**, grievance redress, withdrawal of consent, **nomination**.
- Duties: no false information, impersonation or frivolous complaints.
- Children: **verifiable parental consent** below 18; **no tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted ads**.
### 6. EXEMPTIONS AND RTI
- **Sec. 7**: legitimate uses (State processing without consent). **Sec. 17**: Centre may exempt notified **State agencies** (sovereignty, security, public order); research, archiving, statistics; possibly startups; **journalism - no exemption**.
- **Sec. 44(3)** amended **RTI Sec. 8(1)(j)**: all personal information may be refused; **public-interest override removed**.
- Challenge referred to a **Constitution Bench** (Aug 2026).
### 7. DATA PROTECTION BOARD AND PENALTIES
- Adjudicatory; HQ **New Delhi**; members appointed by the **Centre**; appeals to **TDSAT**.
| Breach | Maximum |

### 8. DPDP RULES 2025, LOCALISATION, SURVEILLANCE
- Draft **3 Jan 2025**; final notified **14 Nov 2025**: Board provisions immediately; **Consent Manager Nov 2026**; full compliance **May 2027** (18 months).
- Rules: clear, specific, informed consent; plain notices; purpose limitation; data minimisation; retention controls.
- **RBI (April 2018)**: payment data stored **only in India**.
- Cross-border: **blacklist** (allowed unless Centre restricts); GDPR = whitelist/adequacy.
- First national standards for **cloud, data centres, ethical AI** (bias, data sovereignty, privacy).
- **Pegasus**: SC committee overseen by **Justice R.V. Raveendran**; malware on **5 of 29** phones, not clearly Pegasus.
- AP: AP Police **AI4AP** pilot (Chittoor, Guntur, Annamayya) raises DPDP issues.
### 9. GDPR AND RIGHT TO BE FORGOTTEN
- **GDPR** (**EU**): adopted **April 2016**, applied **25 May 2018**; replaced 1995 Directive. Penalties financial + criminal; DPDP financial only.
- RTBF began in **Google Spain v AEPD (2014)**; **Right to Erasure = GDPR Art. 17**. In India it limits name-based searches; **does not erase judicial records**.
