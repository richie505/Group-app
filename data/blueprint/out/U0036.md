## U0036 | ASHOKA – ACCESSION, KALINGA WAR, DHAMMA AND ITS CONTENT, DHAMMA MAHAMATRAS
### 1. TITLES, EARLY CAREER AND ACCESSION
| Title / name | Meaning / where |
|---|---|
| **Devanampiya** | "Beloved of the gods" (shows he kept faith in Hinduism though Buddhist) |
| **Piyadasi** | "Of pleasing looks" |
| Name **Ashoka** | **Maski** ("Buddhashakya Ashoka") and **Gujarra** minor rock edicts |
| **Dharmasoka** | Sarnath inscription |
- Son of **Bindusara** and **Subhadrangi**; viceroy of **Taxila** (sent to crush a revolt – Divyavadana) and **Ujjain / Avanti** (won Avanti in Bindusara's time – **Samantapasadika** of Buddhaghosa).
- Succession struggle with **Susima**; Mahavamsa: killed **99 brothers**; RE V refers to his brothers' families.
- Acceded **c. 273/272 BCE**; **coronation 269 BCE** – the gap = four-year struggle.
### 2. KALINGA WAR
- Kalinga broke free in Bindusara's last years; **c. 261 BCE**, **8th regnal year**.
- **Rock Edict XIII**: remorse; **1,00,000 killed, 1,50,000 deported**.
- **Bherighosha** (war drum) → **Dhammaghosha**; **Dhammavijaya** replaced conquest.
- **Dhauli and Jaugada** have **no RE XIII**; **Separate Edicts** to officials at **Tosali and Samapa** on fair treatment.
- RE XIII names the **Andhras**.
!TRAP 1,50,000 = deported; 1,00,000 = killed.
### 3. ASHOKA AND BUDDHISM
- Initiated by **Nigrodha** (Sri Lankan tradition); **Upagupta** per Divyavadana.
- **Pilgrimage:** Lumbini, Kapilavastu, Gaya, Sarnath, Kushinagar, Shravasti.
- **Third Council** at **Pataliputra** under Ashoka. (First – Rajagriha; Second – Vaishali; Fourth – Kashmir, Kanishka.)
- **Bhabru Edict** – devotion to **Buddha, Dhamma, Sangha**. Ashoka and **Dasaratha** – Buddhist (CGPSC 2013).

### 5. DHAMMA
- A **moral code / social ethic**, not a religion: moral discipline, harmony among sects, welfare, loyalty to the state.
- Definition from the **Rahulovada Sutta**; based on Buddhist **upasaka** dhamma; spirit also in **Lakkhana Sutta** and **Dhammapada**.
- Respect for elders; courtesy to slaves and servants; regard for **Brahmanas and Shramanas**; avoid **samajas**.
- **PE II**: few faults, many good deeds. **RE I** bans slaughter and sacrifice.
- **Central point:** non-violence and toleration (BPSC 2017). Rajdharma on **longanimity, liberty, compassion**.
- "Minds unpolluted with jealousy" = **Ashoka** (UPPSC 2023).
