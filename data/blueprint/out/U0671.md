## U0671 | INDIA – AGRICULTURE: COMMERCIAL CROPS (COTTON, SUGARCANE, OILSEEDS, JUTE, TEA, COFFEE, RUBBER, SPICES) AND THEIR REGIONS
### 1. CASH VS FOOD CROPS
- **Cash crops**: cotton, sugarcane, jute, tobacco, oilseeds, groundnut, bananas – grown for sale.
- **Food crops**: wheat, rice, jowar, bajra, millets – NOT cash crops.
- **Plantation crops** (tea, coffee, rubber, coconut): high-value, **labour- and capital-intensive**; colonial plantations (tea, coffee, rubber, indigo) European-dominated, export-oriented.
- **Fibre crops**: cotton, jute, sunhemp. **Green Gold** = tea.
- **Tea** earns the most foreign exchange among cash crops (2024-25 exports 257.88 million kg).
### 2. GROWTH CONDITIONS
| Crop | Temperature | Rainfall | Soil / site |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cotton** | 21-37°C; **210 frost-free days**; hard frost harmful; subtropical | **50-100 cm** (light) | **Black regur** of the Deccan; light, well-drained, moisture-retaining |
| **Jute** | **24-35°C**, hot humid; kharif | **125-200 cm** | **Alluvial flood plains**, Ganga-Brahmaputra delta; low plain land |
| **Tea** | 20-30°C | **150-300 cm** | Deep, well-drained, **acidic hill slopes**, high humus; no waterlogging |
| **Coffee** | 15-28°C, **shade** | 150-250 cm | Well-drained, humus-rich; Western Ghats |
| **Rubber** | 25-35°C, hot humid, **equatorial** | **Over 200 cm** (200-300) | **Laterite**, Western Ghats; NOT delta flood plains |
| **Sugarcane** | Hot humid; **21-27°C**; **10-15 months** | 75-100 cm | Alluvial / black |
| **Black pepper** | 15-30°C, hot moist | **≥200 cm** | Hill slopes ~1,100-1,300 m |
| Soybean | Warm growing season | – | Kharif oilseed |
- **Black soils** (clay over 62%) → **cotton, chillies, tobacco**; **red soils** → groundnut, tobacco, ragi.
- **Sugarcane** = **least water-efficient** (1,400-2,300 mm subtropics, 2,000-3,500 mm tropics). Water-efficient: **millets, pulses, oilseeds**; paddy in Punjab and sugarcane in Maharashtra = subsidy distortion (Jun 2025).
!TRAP Rubber needs over **200 cm**, not 100 cm. Soybean is an oilseed AND needs a warm season – but being an oilseed does not explain the warm season.

### 4. TEA AND COFFEE – HISTORY, HILLS, VARIETIES
- First tea plantations: **first half of the 19th century**, **Brahmaputra valley, Assam** (APPSC G2 2025).
- **Darjeeling Tea** = **India's first GI tag**. GI is NOT granted via the "Paris Agreement and Protocol".
- **Barak Valley** (south Assam): tea; single largest unbroken tea region (rice main crop, sugarcane 2nd per 1996 key).
- Tea in Africa: first at **Durban Botanical Garden (1850)**; Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda.
| Hills / area | Crop | State |
|---|---|---|
| Brahmaputra valley | Tea (Assam) | Assam |
| **Darjeeling, Dooars** | Tea | West Bengal |
| **Nilgiris** | Tea (Nilgiri), coffee | Tamil Nadu |
| **Baba Budan hills, Chikkamagaluru**; Kodagu | Coffee (**Arabica** introduced) | Karnataka |
| **Araku, ASR district** | Coffee clusters | AP |
- Coffee varieties: **Arabica** and **Robusta**, grown in the Western Ghats.
### 5. SUGARCANE – TROPICAL VS SUB-TROPICAL
| Point | Tropical (south) | Sub-tropical (north) |
|---|---|---|
| States | Maharashtra, Karnataka, TN, AP | **UP**, Bihar |
| Yield | **Higher** | Lower |
| Area | Smaller | Larger |
| Crushing | **Oct to May/June** (longer) | **Nov to Feb** |
| Reason | **Cool nights with clear skies, latitude, long sunshine** (APPSC G1 2023: I, II, III) | **Alluvium rich in lime and potash**, level land, dense market, compact blocks |
- **Western UP** = main sugar-mill belt; UP = "**Sugar Bowl of India**" (2nd largest sugar producer, Dec 2025).
- Mills near fields: cane is **weight-losing** (loses weight and sugar after cutting). Disease: **red rot**; variety **Co. 1148**.
- UP output more than Maharashtra, productivity less; most Maharashtra mills cooperative (true, not explanation).
- Pricing: **FRP**, not MSP.
### 6. OILSEEDS
| Kharif | Rabi | Both |
|---|---|---|
| Groundnut, soybean, sesamum, castor, nigerseed | **Rapeseed-mustard**, linseed, safflower | **Sunflower** |
- **Rabi-only oilseed = mustard** (APPSC G2 2025). Mustard varieties: **Varuna, Pusa Bold, Jaikisan**.
- 2024-25 output (MT): **soybean 15.27 > rapeseed-mustard 12.67 > groundnut 11.94 > sesamum 0.83** (old key: soybean > groundnut > mustard > sesamum).
- **7 MSP oilseeds**: groundnut, rapeseed-mustard, soybean, sesamum, sunflower, safflower, nigerseed.
- **MSP 23 commodities**: 7 cereals (paddy, wheat, maize, bajra, jowar, ragi, barley), 5 pulses, 7 oilseeds (+ commercial crops); NOT areca nut, coffee, turmeric.
- Groundnut came to India from **Brazil**. Soybean = "**vegetable meat**".
- Oilseeds 2025-26: area, production, productivity all rose.
- **NMEO-Oilseeds** (groundnut, sesamum, castor, sunflower) + **NMEO-Oil Palm** → ~**70 MT by 2030-31**; **AP leads oil palm** area, production, productivity.
