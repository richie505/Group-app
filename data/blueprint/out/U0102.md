## U0102 | TELUGU LANGUAGE AND LITERATURE TO THE 14TH CENTURY – NANNAYA, TIKKANA, ERRANA, PALKURIKI SOMANATHA
### 1. TELUGU LANGUAGE – FAMILY, STATUS AND NAMES
#### 1.1 Family and Status
- **Dravidian** family; sub-branch **South-Central Dravidian** (sister languages **Gondi and Kui**); Tamil and Malayalam are **South** Dravidian.
- One of the **22 Eighth Schedule** languages; **official language of AP** (**Urdu** = second official language).
- **Classical language since 2008**.
- **Telugu Language Day – 29 August**, birth anniversary of **Gidugu Venkata Ramamurthy** (not Gurajada); Telugu Language Awards the same day; **Telugu Mahotsavam** every year in AP (announced by Culture Minister **Kandula Durgesh** at the Undavalli Caves valedictory, 2026).
- Old traditional view (not accepted linguistically): Telugu grew from the Desi bhasha (Paisachi) of Prakrit; D.C. Sircar found Desi words close to Telugu.
#### 1.2 Names and Epithets
| Name / epithet | Origin / coined by |
|---|---|
| **Telugu** (Trilinga theory) | From **Trilinga** – three Shaiva centres **Srisailam, Kaleshwaram, Draksharama** |
| Andhra / Andhrika; Tenugu | Other names; **Ketana** first used the words **Tenugu/Telugu** |
| **"Italian of the East"** | **Niccolo de' Conti**, 15th-c. **Venetian traveller** – words end in vowels (APPSC GS 2021) |
| **"Desa bhashalandu Telugu lessa"** | **Krishnadevaraya**, in **Amuktamalyada** |
!TRAP Italian of the East = a foreign traveller (Conti); Desa bhashalandu Telugu lessa = a king (Krishnadevaraya).
### 2. EARLIEST TELUGU WORDS AND SCRIPT
#### 2.1 Telugu Words in Prakrit Records
| Word / form | Record |
|---|---|
| **"Nagabu"** | **Amaravati** inscription – **first Telugu word** (identified by **Veturi Prabhakara Sastri**) |
| "Vepuru" | **Myakadoni** inscription |
| atta, addam, potta, tuppu, padi, pandi, pilla | **Hala's Gatha Saptasati** (Prakrit; contains writing by women) |
| "Andhrapatha" | **Mayidavolu** plates of Pallava Sivaskandavarman (c. 300 CE) |
| Plural **"-ambul"** (Vijayarajya Samvatsarambul) | **Chikkulla** plates (**Vishnukundin**); village names pure Telugu |
- Satavahana royal language: **Prakrit** (APPSC G2 2011 key); golden age under **Hala**.
#### 2.2 Script Evolution from Brahmi
| Stage | Script |
|---|---|
| Satavahana / **Bhattiprolu** (c. **400 BCE**, Guntur) | **Brahmi** (Bhattiprolu variant), Prakrit, with early Telugu place and personal names |
| Early medieval (Kadamba–Chalukya) | Common **Old Kannada–Telugu** script |
| Rashtrakuta / Eastern Chalukya age | Telugu and Kannada begin to differentiate |
| **11th–14th c.** | **Separate Telugu script** – rounded letters, vowel signs |

#### 3.1 Prose Inscriptions

|---|---|---|

| Pitikayagulla (near Bestavaripeta, Prakasam) | 8th c. | Post-Badami Chalukya influence on script (**not** first poetic); reported Jun 2026 |
#### 3.2 Poetic Inscriptions and Metres
| Inscription | Issuer | Metre |
|---|---|---|
| **Addanki** | **Panduranga**, commander of **Gunaga Vijayaditya III** | **First poetic inscription**; **Taruvoja** |
| Sataluru | Gunaga Vijayaditya | Champakamala with prasa |
| **Kandukur**, Dharmavaram | Gunaga Vijayaditya | **Seesa**, Ataveladi |
| **Bezawada** | **Yuddhamalla II** | **First Madhyakkara** (Nagamalleswara temple) |
- **Chebrolu** inscription shows **Marga and Desi** forms.
- **June 2026**: ASI survey of Nallamala Tiger Reserve copied **25 inscriptions** (2nd–16th c.); Telugu records from 7th to 16th c.
!TRAP Kalamalla = first inscription (prose); Addanki = first poetic (Taruvoja); Bezawada = first Madhyakkara.
#### 3.3 Court Language and Pre-Nannaya Telugu
- **Eastern Chalukyas**: official language **Sanskrit** (G2 2008 key); Telugu = people's language; bilingual records – **praise in Sanskrit, grant details in Telugu**.
- **Kakatiya**: most inscriptions **in Telugu**, which grew as an administrative language; Telugu grammar, poetics and desi metres grew.
- **Pre-Nannaya written Telugu**: Kalamalla, Vipparla, Addanki, Bezawada (**not Motupalli**, a later Kakatiya record) → written verse existed before the Mahabharata; no pre-Nannaya literary text survives.
- **Nannaya** made Telugu a language of high literature; fixed its grammar and literary style.
### 4. KAVITRAYAM – ANDHRA MAHABHARATAM
| Poet | Century / patron | Share | Titles |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nannaya** Bhattaraka | **11th c.**; **Rajaraja Narendra** (Eastern Chalukya, r. **1018–1061**), **Rajamahendravaram** | **Adi, Sabha + part of Aranya** (142 padyas) | **Adikavi, Vaganusasana** |
| **Tikkana** Somayaji | **13th c.**; **Manumasiddhi II** of Nellore (age of Kakatiya **Ganapatideva**) | Remaining **15 parvas** (**Virata to Swargarohana**) – "**Prabandha Mandali**" | **Kavibrahma, Ubhayakavimitrudu**, Kavijana Raja |
| **Errana** (Errapragada) | **14th c.**; **Prolaya Vema Reddi** (Addanki) | **Rest of Aranya Parva** (Aranya Parva Shesham) – linked Nannaya and Tikkana | **Prabandha Parameswara, Sambhudasa** |
- Telugu Mahabharata took three eras: **Eastern Chalukya, Kakatiya, Reddi**.
!TRAP Srinatha, Potana and Nannechoda are NOT in the Kavitrayam. Kamban and Kuttan are not Telugu translators.
!TRAP Nannaya did NOT do "the first three parvas and part of the fourth" – only Adi, Sabha and part of Aranya.
### 5. NANNAYA, NARAYANA BHATTU AND GRANTS
#### 5.1 Nannaya
- Court poet, teacher and adviser of Rajaraja Narendra; **first translator** of the Mahabharata; **Andhra Mahabharatamu = first book in Telugu**.
- **Andhra Shabda Chintamani** = Telugu grammar **written in Sanskrit** (G2 2017, 2025 keys); modern scholars doubt his authorship.
- Pioneered **Champu** (prose + verse); fitted Sanskrit metres; used **Akkara** metre borrowed from **Kannada**.
- Other works: Indra Vijayam, Chamundika Vilasam.
- Titles: Adikavi, **Vagadheeshwara, Vidyadvaitudu, Shabda Shasanudu**; Tikkana praised him as Kavitva Visharadudu.
#### 5.2 Narayana Bhattu and Grants
| Grant | Recipient | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Nandampudi** agrahara | **Narayana Bhattu**, from Rajaraja Narendra | **Nandampudi donation inscription** (written by Nannaya) = proof of his help in the translation (G2 2019 key) |
| **Navakhandavada** agrahara | **Pavuluri Mallana**, from Rajaraja Narendra | – |
| Pantur | Eight Brahmins (Jayavarma) | – |
| Pedavegi inscription | Grant to Vishnu (Nandi Varma II) | – |
- Narayana Bhattu: knew **eight languages** – **Ashtabhasha Kavisekhara**; Kaviraja Shekhara, Karibha Vajrankudu, **Vangmaya Durandharudu**; **Someshwara I of Kalyani** made him his representative at Rajamahendravaram.
!TRAP G2 2017 key and G2 2025 statement III accepted "Nandampudi given to Nannaya"; one key gave the **Kupama** inscription as the evidence. Follow the question's wording.
### 6. TIKKANA AND ERRANA – OTHER WORKS
- **Tikkana**: **Harihara Advaita** (unity of Shiva and Vishnu) – dedicated Bharatam to **Hariharanatha**; first work **Nirvachanottara Ramayanam** (to Manumasiddhi); **Kavi Vakbandham**, Krishna Satakam; disciple **Ketana**; first poet to receive a literary dedication. AP discussed a **Tikkana statue in SPSR Nellore** (Feb 2026).
- **Errana**: **Harivamsam** (to Prolaya Vema, not Nannaya); **Narasimha (Nrusimha) Puranam** = first **Kshetra-mahatmya** kavya (G2 2017 key); **first prabandha writer**.
!PAIR Harivamsam | Errana | to Prolaya Vema || Uttara Harivamsa | Nachana Somana | later work
### 7. SHIVA KAVIS AND PALKURIKI SOMANATHA
| Poet | Work | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Mallikarjuna Panditaradhya** | **Sivatattvasaram** (~400 kanda poems) | Originator of the Sataka form |
| **Nannechoda** | **Kumarasambhavam** (Kannada-influenced) | Title **Kavirajasekhara / Kaviraja Sikhamani**; began the **eight descriptions** of prabandha |
| **Palkuriki Somanatha** | **Basava Puranam**, **Panditaradhya Charitra**, **Vrushadhipa Satakam** | See below |
- **Palkuriki Somanatha** (Palakurthi; Kakatiya period): **Prathama Andhra Viplava Kavi**; led the **Desi kavita movement** against Sanskrit-heavy marga style; popularised **dwipada**, ragada, manjari, kanda.
- **Basava Puranam**: in **dwipada** (not Champu); life of **Basava** and **Veerashaivism**; not by Vemulavada Bhimakavi.
- **Panditaradhya Charitra**: called the first Telugu **encyclopaedia**.
- **Vrushadhipa Satakam**: 100 verses – a "first Satakam" claim.

### 12. SRINATHA AND THE PRABANDHA AGE
- **Srinatha** (c. 1365–1441): bridge between Kavitraya and Prabandha; patron **Pedakomati Vema Reddi**; also at **Deva Raya II's** court, who gave the title **Kavisarvabhauma**.
| Work | Note |
|---|---|
| **Sringara Naishadham** | From Sriharsha's Naishadhiyacharita (**Nala–Damayanti**) |
| **Haravilasam** | To merchant **Tippayya Setti**; sea trade |
| **Kashikhandam** | At **Kataya Vema's** request |
| **Bhimakhandam** | **Bhimeswara of Draksharama** |
| **Palnati Veera Charitra** | Battle of Palnadu; instruments Sannai, Mukha Veena |
| Sivaratri Mahatmyam, Marutratcharitra | – |
- **Veerabhadra Vijayam** is by **Bammera Potana**, NOT Srinatha. The APPSC 2025 key treated Palnati Veera Charitra as not his (options: Haravilasam, Madanavilasa Bhanam, Sivaratri Mahatmyam).
- **Prabandha**: epic kavya → prabandha; **strict metrical rules**, elaborate descriptions, strong shringara. Reddi courts → Srinatha and Errana; Vijayanagara Nayaka courts spread it; peak = **Ashtadiggajas**. Telugu literature "first influenced" by the **Vijayanagara Empire** (APPSC GS 2019 key).
