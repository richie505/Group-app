## U0149 | INTERACTION AND IMPACT OF THE BHAKTI AND SUFI MOVEMENTS ON SOCIETY AND LANGUAGE
### 1. COMMON PRINCIPLES
| Principle | Bhakti | Sufi | Common? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monotheism** | One God, many names (Kabir) | Tawhid; Wahdat-ul-Wujud | Yes |
| **Tolerance** | Kabir, Nanak criticised both orthodoxies | Unity of humanity | Yes |
| **Asceticism**, guide-disciple bond | Guru | Pir-murid | Yes |
| Devotion over ritual | Over Vedic ritual and priests | Over formal Shariat | Yes |
| **Idol worship** | Nirguna saints criticised it | Rejected | **No** |
!TRAP APPSC-G1 2022 and 2023: common principles = I, II and III only (monotheism, asceticism, tolerance); "idol worship" is the trap.
### 2. NATHPANTHI HATH YOGA AND SUFI PRACTICE
- **Hath Yoga** of the **Nathpanthis** (Gorakhnath; **Kanphata** yogis; Nathpanth from **Matsyendranath**) was adopted by Sufis - both correct (UPPSC 2019).
- **Amritakund** (Hath-yogic text) into Arabic and Persian, 13th c. → **breath control** in all orders except **Naqshbandis**.
- Sufi practices: meditation, breath control, ascetic exercises in a lonely place, holy songs, **Zikr**.
- **Nizamuddin Auliya** noted the yogic body split: **Siva** head to navel, **Sakti** below. **Hamiduddin Nagori** equated **Alakh Niranjan** with **Khuda** in Hindi verse.
### 3. MUSIC
| Tradition | Fact |
|---|---|
| Qawwali | Sufi shrines; **Amir Khusrau** introduced the **Qaul** |
| Sama | Chishti, Suhrawardi; opposed by Naqshbandis |
| Kirtan | Chaitanya's sankirtan, Padavali kirtans, Varkari abhangas; Telugu: Annamacharya, Kshetrayya, Tyagaraja |

- Other: **Venkatamakhin** - Melakarta raga system; **Shyama Shastri** - Carnatic composer; **V.D. Paluskar** - music for Vande Mataram.

### 5. CASTE AND SOCIAL REFORM
- Weakened priestly dominance; **khanqahs** fed all groups. Low-caste saints: Namdev **tailor**, Kabir **weaver**, Ravidas **tanner**, Sena **barber**, Dhanna **Jat cultivator**.
- **Ramananda** - first medieval reformer to **denounce caste** outright.
- **Basava** - against caste, **child marriage**; backed **widow remarriage**, gender equality.
- **Brahmanaidu** (Palnadu) - **Chapakudu** inter-dining; commander **Karnamadasu**, an untouchable.
- **Veerabrahmam** - disciples **Siddayya (Dudekula)**, **Kakkaiah (Madiga)**; admitted women (granddaughter **Eswaramma**).
- **Nasaraiah** - Sayyid reformer (to 1825), Madigas of **Vinukonda**; opposed untouchability.
### 6. WOMEN
- **Mirabai**, **Andal**, **Lal Ded (Lalla, Kashmir)**, **Janabai**, **Bahinabai**, **Molla** (Molla Ramayanam).
### 7. SYNCRETISM, SECTS AND AKBAR
| Sect | Founder / region |
|---|---|
| Sikhism | Guru Nanak, Punjab |
| Kabirpanth (Sant) | Kabir, Nanak, Ravidas, Dadu |
| Dadupanth | Dadu, **Rajasthan** |
| Mahanubhava | **Chakradhar Swami**, **Maharashtra** |
| Rishi movement | **Sheikh Nuruddin**, Kashmir; vegetarian |
| Lingayat | Basava |
- Sufi shrines became shared centres → **composite Indo-Islamic culture**.
- **Akbar**: **Sulh-i-kul** and **Din-i-Ilahi (1582)**; revered Salim Chishti. **Dara Shikoh** (Qadiri) continued it; **Sirhindi** (Naqshbandi) attacked it.
- Sects: Pashupata - Lakulisha; Faraizi - Haji Shariatullah; Haridasi - Haridas. **Heliodorus** - Besnagar Garuda pillar; Guptas - **Parama-bhagavata**; **Kharavela** - Jain, not Ajivika (Ajivika: **Makkhali Gosala**).
### 8. LIMITATIONS
- **Caste persisted**; sects hardened; Veerashaiva **Himsa** ("Sivapasa"); **purdah** stayed.
