## U0123 | VIJAYANAGARA SOCIETY, ECONOMY AND TRADE; ACCOUNTS OF NICOLO CONTI, ABDUR RAZZAK, DOMINGO PAES, NUNIZ
### 1. FOREIGN TRAVELLERS
#### 1.1 Traveller → Country → Ruler → Observations
| Traveller | Country | Ruler visited | Observations |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ibn Battuta** | Morocco | **Harihara I** | First foreign traveller to Vijayanagara |
| **Nicolo de Conti** | **Italy** | **Devaraya I** (1420–21) | **Earliest European** visitor; city **60 miles** round; army **90,000**; slavery, trial by ordeal; **Sati (sahagamana)**, polygamy, festivals |
| **Abdur Razzaq** | **Persia** (Timurid envoy of **Shah Rukh**) | **Devaraya II** (1443) | City "the **eye has not seen**"; **300 ports**, each equal to Calicut; **Sati** (APPSC G2 2017); prostitution tax paid the guards; Mahanavami; no purdah; wrote **Matla-us-Sadain** |
| **Athanasius Nikitin** | **Russia** | Bahmani kingdom (Muhammad III) | **Journey Beyond Three Seas**; rich-poor gap |
| **Duarte Barbosa** | Portugal | **Krishnadevaraya** | **Religious freedom** – Christians, Jews, Muslims lived freely; trade at Pulicat |
| **Domingo Paes** | Portugal | **Krishnadevaraya** (1520–22) | City "**as large as Rome**"; the king's looks; **House of Victory**; Mahanavami; **devadasis** |
| **Fernao Nuniz** | Portugal (**horse dealer**) | **Achyutaraya** (1535–37) | **Dynastic history**, foundation legend, army (**600,000** amara troops); Sati, polygamy |
| **Caesar Frederick** | Italy (**Venice**) | Sadasiva (**1567**) | Ruined capital after Talikota |
- Others: **Varthema** (Italy, Krishnadevaraya); Sassetti after Talikota.
- **Robert Sewell** translated Paes and Nuniz in **A Forgotten Empire (1900)**.
- Local sources besides travellers: **Amuktamalyada** and **Rayavachakam**.
!KEY Conti – Razzaq – Paes – Nuniz = Devaraya I – Devaraya II – Krishnadevaraya – Achyutaraya.

### 2. CASTES AND SOCIAL GROUPS
- **Four varnas** and about **18 castes**.
| Group | Occupation / note |
|---|---|
| **Vaidikas / Niyogis** | Brahmin priests / Brahmin officials |
| **Vipravinodins** | Brahmin **jugglers/magicians** who performed **only before Brahmins**, in their homes (APPSC 2017, 2019) |
| **Settis, Komatis, Balijas** (Balanjalu, Veerabalijas) | **Traders**, like the **Ayyavalu**; Arya Vaishyas and Kalinga Vaishyas |
| **Sudras** | Farmers – backbone of the kingdom; many became **amaranayakas** |
| **Vira Panchalas** (Panchanam) | **Artisans** – gold, iron, brass, wood, stone; 74 sub-sects in a Devaraya I inscription |
| **Kaikkolars**, Padmasalis, Jandra, Kuruba | **Weavers** |
!TRAP Vaidikas = priests; Niyogis = officials.
### 3. RIGHT-HAND AND LEFT-HAND DIVISIONS
| Division | Groups |
|---|---|
| **Valangai** (right-hand) | Mainly **agricultural and trading** groups |
| **Idangai** (left-hand) | Mainly **artisans** (incl. Panchala smiths) and some traders |
- Began in **Chola** times; continued under Vijayanagara.
### 4. WOMEN AND SOCIAL CUSTOMS
- **No strict purdah**; women served as bodyguards, wrestlers, accountants, dancers.
- **Sati, polygamy, dowry** existed; **child marriages** were common.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Kanyashulkam** | **Bride price** |
| **Varashulkam** | **Dowry** |
| **Benabaga** | **Slavery** |
| **Devadasis** | Temple dancers trained in music and dance; often **land-owning and respected**; temple dance = forerunner of Bharatanatyam |
- Prostitution state-recognised and **taxed** (12,000 panams a year per woman).
- Learned women: **Gangadevi** and **Tirumalamba**.
- Other famous queens: **Mallamma Devi** – Bobbili (1757); Surambika – Reddy; Devinaganika – Satavahana; **Rudramadevi** – Kakatiya.
### 5. RELIGION AND RELIGIOUS CENTRES
#### 5.1 Ruler → Faith
| Ruler / dynasty | Faith / act |
|---|---|
| **Sangamas** | **Shaiva**; family deity **Virupaksha**; guru **Kalamukha Kriya Shakti** |
| Saluva, Tuluva, Aravidu | **Vaishnava** (Venkateswara) |
| **Saluva Narasimha** | **Tirupati** rose under him |
| **Devaraya II** | Enlisted **Muslims** in the army (with jagirs); built a **mosque** |
| **Narahari Tirtha** | Spread **Dvaita** from **Srikurmam** |
| **Venkatapati Raya II** (Aravidu) | Venkateswara replaced Virupaksha as main deity |
#### 5.2 Christian Missions
- **Venkatapati Raya II** allowed a **church at Chandragiri** and protected the **Jesuit mission**.
#### 5.3 Centres in Andhra
| Centre | Type |
|---|---|
| **Srisailam, Tripurantakam, Srikalahasti** | Shaiva |
| **Tirupati, Kanchi, Mangalagiri, Pushpagiri** | Vaishnava |
| **Ahobilam, Kadiri** | Narasimha temples |
| **Penugonda (West Godavari)** | Main seat of **Vasavi Kanyaka Parameswari**, goddess of the Arya Vysyas/Komatis (APPSC G2 2019); HQ of trade associations (**Nagaram / Pekkandru**) |
!TRAP Penugonda (West Godavari) = Vasavi; **Penukonda** (Anantapur) = later capital, Siruveru tank.
### 6. FESTIVALS
| Festival | Facts |
|---|---|
| **Mahanavami (Dasara)** | On the **Mahanavami / Dasara Dibba** built by **Krishnadevaraya**; nayakas brought **tribute**; **financial year began** with it; **Burton Stein** compared it to an **Ashvamedha**; described by **Paes and Abdur Razzaq** |
| **Vasantotsava** | Spring festival; appears in Krishnadevaraya's **Jambavati Parinayam** |
### 7. PERFORMING ARTS AND FOLK ENTERTAINMENT
| Form | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Yakshagana** | Earlier form **Koravanji**; first Telugu Yakshagana **Sugriva Vijayam** by **Kandukuri Rudrakavi** |
| **Tholu Bommalata** | **Leather shadow-puppetry**; in Palkuriki's **Panditaradhya Charitra** and Palnati Veeracharitra; led to **Surabhi** theatre |
| **Kuchipudi** | From the Bhagavata Mela of a Krishna-district village; founded by **Siddhendra Yogi** |
| **Veedhi Bhagavatham** | Telugu **street** ("veedhi") folk theatre of Andhra |
| **Burrakatha** | Telugu storytelling form from the **Jangam Katha** tradition; popular in the Telangana rebellion |
- **Latest**: June 2025 – Tholu Bommalata, Harikatha, Burrakatha, Padya Natakam flagged close to extinction; **July 2026** – AP announced an annual **Telugu Mahotsavam** and their revival.
### 8. AGRICULTURE, IRRIGATION AND TEMPLE ECONOMY
#### 8.1 Porumamilla Inscription
- **12 rules (Dwadasa Sutras)** for building a tank (APPSC G2 2012, 2019); issued by **Bhaskara Mantri**; records the **Anantasagaram** tank (**1369**, Bukka I's time); in **Kadapa**.
!TRAP Distractors: Tummalagudem, Korlagallu, Bitragunta, Palampet, Pakhala.
#### 8.2 Tanks and Dams
| Ruler | Work |
|---|---|
| **Bukka I** | **Siruveru** tank near Penukonda |
| **Saluva Narasimha** | **Narasambudhi** tank (Anantapur) |
| **Krishnadevaraya** | **Toragallu** dam, **Nagalapuram** tank (Portuguese engineers), **Kambham** tank |
#### 8.3 Temple Economy
- **Main revenue**: land tax.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Devadaya** | Temple villages / endowments |
| **Sri Bhandara** | Temple treasury, kept by the **Karanam** |
| **Samayas** | Merchant **guilds**, led by **Ayyavali**; met in temple mandapas |
- Latest (25 Jun 2026): **Sadasivakona** inscriptions (1554 CE) show **Kaanika** income from Gudimallam temple lands diverted to a new temple.
