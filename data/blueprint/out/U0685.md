## U0685 | INTERNATIONAL TRADE AND INDIA'S FOREIGN TRADE, PORTS
### 1. INDIA'S TRADE (FY 2025-26)
| Indicator | Figure |
|---|---|
| Merchandise exports | **US$ 441.78 bn** (record, up 0.93%) |
| Imports | ~**US$ 775 bn** (up 7.45%; gold, silver) |
| Trade deficit | Record **US$ 333.2 bn** (FY25 282.5) |
| CAD | US$ 30.2 bn (**1.1% of GDP**) |
- 2024 rank: **18th in goods**, **7th in services**.
- **US$ 2 trillion** by 2030 = goods + services; NITI **Export Preparedness Index** = US$ 1 trillion merchandise.
- Ports: **95% by volume, 68% by value**; coastal shipping up 118% in a decade.
- West Asia war: Gulf exports fell ~57% (March 2026).
### 2. COMPOSITION OF TRADE
| Exports | Imports |
|---|---|
| **Petroleum products** top (**14.48%**, 2024-25) | **Crude petroleum** (largest) |
| Pharma ($31.11 bn), gems | **Gold, silver** |
| **Electronics** >$40 bn, 3rd in FY25 (after engineering, petroleum) | Edible oils, fertilizers, coal |
| Rice $11.53 bn (down 7.5%) | Components, machinery |
- Dependence: crude **88-89%**, gas **47-51%**, coal **26%**.
- **Bulk** imports: petroleum, cereals, pulses, edible oils, sugar, fertilizer, metals, ores. **Non-bulk**: capital goods, precious products, plastics, chemicals.
- Top farm import = **vegetable oils** (11.6 mt 2013-14 to 16.5 mt 2022-23).
- 2002-03: **gems and jewellery** first, chemicals next.
!TRAP **Iron ore** is exported, not imported (APPSC 2018). **Milk products** are not a significant export.

### 3. DIRECTION OF TRADE
- Export destinations 2024-25: **USA 19.77%**, UAE 8.37%, UK 3.32%, China 3.26% (USA, APPSC 2019).
- Shifts: UAE 8.5 to 6.5%; China 3.3 to 4.4%; Singapore 2.7 to 4.7%.
- Import sources: **China**, UAE, Russia, USA, Iraq.
- West Asia: ~**90% of LPG**, 40%+ of crude. Top crude supplier: Saudi Arabia earlier, **Iraq** 2017-18, **Russia** 2025.
- Post-1947 shift: **UK/W. Europe to USA to Asia**.
- China-border trade (Lipulekh, Shipki La, Nathu La) suspended since 2020.
### 4. TRADE POLICY EVOLUTION
| Year | Step |
|---|---|
| 1950-1990 | **Import substitution** (four decades) |
| 1965 | **Kandla EPZ**, Asia's first |
| July 1991 | Two-step **devaluation**; price-based mechanism; licensing eased |
| 1992 | Convertibility: **40% official, 60% market** |
| 1993 / 1999 | FERA liberalised; **FEMA 1999** |
| July 2022 | RBI rupee settlement: **SRVA** (less dollar dependence) |
| FTP 2023 | Incentives to **remission**; **Districts as Export Hubs** |
- **Export promotion**: East Asia adopted it in the 1970s and moved ahead.
- US tariff in talks: **25% + 25% = 50%**.

### 7. ROUTES, CHOKEPOINTS, WORLD PORTS
- **North Atlantic** ("Big Trunk") busiest: US north-east to NW Europe. **Suez** = shortest Europe-Asia; **Cape of Good Hope** = round Africa.
| Waterway | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Malacca** | Andaman Sea-South China Sea; Malay Peninsula vs Sumatra; Singapore at south; 1/3 seaborne oil; 80%+ China's oil |
| **Hormuz** | **Persian Gulf-Gulf of Oman**; ~20% of oil |
| **Suez** | Mediterranean-Red Sea; Egypt; 193.30 km; 1869; cuts 7,000 km; ~12% trade; greatest significance |
| **Bab-el-Mandeb** | Red Sea-Gulf of Aden; Yemen vs Djibouti/Eritrea |
| Panama | Atlantic-Pacific; locks; 1914 |
| Turkish Straits | Bosphorus, Dardanelles; Russia's warm-water access |
| Bering | Arctic-Pacific |
| Lombok | Bali-Lombok; Java Sea-Indian Ocean; Malacca alternative (with Sunda) |
| **SUMED** | Ain Sukhna (Red Sea) to Sidi Kerir (Mediterranean); Gulf oil to Europe |
- 2026: Hormuz closed early March; Houthis took **Perim Island** (Sept).
- **Shanghai** busiest container port (2024: Shanghai, Singapore, Ningbo); old key: second after Singapore; Shenzhen 4th vs Rotterdam. **Rotterdam** = Europe's largest; **Singapore** = transshipment hub (not London).
### 8. MAJOR PORTS OF INDIA
- **13 major** (12 operational + **Vadhavan**, Palghar; SPV **JNPA 74%, MMB 26%**; 2034); ~200 non-major (State Maritime Boards); **Major Port Authorities Act**.
- Coastline **~11,098 km** (was 7,517); Gujarat longest.
| Port | State | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Kandla (Deendayal)** | Gujarat | Tidal, Gulf of Kutch; 1950s (Karachi lost); first SEZ; top import tonnage 2023-24; no LNG |
| Mumbai | Maharashtra | Largest natural; island city; "biggest port" key |
| **JNPA (Nhava Sheva)** | Maharashtra | 1989; eases Mumbai; largest container; first 100% landlord |
| Mormugao | Goa | Zuari mouth |
| New Mangalore | Karnataka | Kudremukh iron ore |
| Kochi | Kerala | Vembanad Lake |
| **Chennai** | TN | Oldest, biggest artificial; 1881; third-oldest major; not deepest |
| **Kamarajar (Ennore)** | TN | 12th, coal; first company-registered; 24 km north of Chennai |
| Tuticorin (VOC) | TN | Artificial, north of Adam's Bridge |
| **Visakhapatnam** | **AP** | "Brightest Jewel"; deepest landlocked protected; not largest container |
| **Paradip** | Odisha | Mahanadi; 18 Apr 1966; dry bulk; eases Kolkata, Vizag; iron ore to Japan |
| **Kolkata-Haldia** | WB | Oldest; only riverine |
- Natural: Mormugao, Mumbai, Kochi. **Mundra** = largest private; **Sikka** = liquid bulk; **Vizhinjam** (Kerala) = first transshipment, now customs port.
- West coast N to S: Deendayal, Mumbai, Mormugao, Kochi; Janjira, Udupi (Malpe) west; Auroville Coromandel; Kakinada AP; Quilon minor; Dahej non-major, Khambhat.
- Gujarat port towns: Okha, Porbandar, Alang, Bedi, Bhavnagar, Dahej, Jafarabad, Mandvi, Navlakhi, Surat, Veraval; **not Jamnagar**.
- LNG: Dahej, Mundra (Gujarat), Kochi, Dabhol, Ennore, Dhamra.
### 9. PORT TYPES AND COASTS
- Artificial: Chennai, Tuticorin; tidal: Kandla; decongestion: JNPT, Haldia, Paradip. Bengaluru landlocked, no harbour.
- **West**: narrow, indented, deep near shore, estuaries = natural harbours. **East**: deltaic, **shallow shelf**, **cyclones**, **more** sediment = fewer ports (APPSC 2023).
- **Sagarmala** (2015, MoPSW): **port-led development**; five pillars incl. modernisation, connectivity, industrialisation.
