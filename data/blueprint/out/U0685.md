## U0685 | INTERNATIONAL TRADE AND INDIA'S FOREIGN TRADE, PORTS
### 1. INDIA'S TRADE AT A GLANCE (FY 2025-26)
| Indicator | Figure |
|---|---|
| Merchandise exports | **US$ 441.78 bn** (all-time high, up 0.93%; FY25 US$ 437.70 bn) |
| Merchandise imports | ~**US$ 775 bn** (up 7.45%), driven by gold and silver |
| Merchandise trade deficit | Record **US$ 333.2 bn** (FY25: US$ 282.5 bn) |
| CAD | US$ 30.2 bn (**1.1% of GDP**); services = surplus |
| Goods + services exports | US$ 860.09 bn |
- World rank 2024: **18th in merchandise** (1.8%) vs **7th in services** (4.3%).
- Target **US$ 2 trillion** exports by 2030 (goods + services); NITI **Export Preparedness Index** aligned to **US$ 1 trillion merchandise** by 2030.
- Ports carry **95% of trade by volume, 68% by value**; coastal shipping grew 118% in a decade.
- West Asia war cut exports to the Gulf by ~57% in March 2026; goods exports up 20% in July 2026.
### 2. COMPOSITION OF TRADE
#### 2.1 Exports vs Imports
| Exports | Imports |
|---|---|
| **Petroleum products** - top item 2024-25 (**14.48%**) | **Crude petroleum** - largest item (88-89% dependence) |
| Pharma (record $31.11 bn FY26), gems and jewellery | **Gold and silver** |
| **Electronics** >$40 bn - 3rd category FY25 (after engineering goods, petroleum products) | Edible oils, fertilizers, coal |
| Rice ($11.53 bn, down 7.5%), textiles, marine products, spices | Electronic components, machinery |
- Import dependence: crude **88-89%**, natural gas **47-51%**, coal **26%**.
- **Bulk imports**: petroleum, cereals and pulses, edible oils, sugar, fertilizer, metals and ores. **Non-bulk**: capital goods, precious products, plastics, chemicals.
- Highest agricultural import by value = **vegetable oils** (11.6 mt in 2013-14 to 16.5 mt, Rs 1,38,424 cr, 2022-23).
- 2002-03 exports: **gems and jewellery** first, chemicals next.
!TRAP **Iron ore** is exported, not imported (APPSC 2018). Refined petroleum and gems are exports. **Milk products** are not a significant export.

### 4. TRADE POLICY EVOLUTION
| Year | Step |
|---|---|
| 1950-1990 | **Import substitution**, inward-looking (four decades): QRs, high tariffs |
| 1965 | **Kandla EPZ** - Asia's first |
| July 1991 | New Trade Policy: two-step **rupee devaluation**; QRs to price-based mechanism; licensing eased |
| 1992 | Partial current-account convertibility: **40% official, 60% market** rate |
| 1993 / 1999 | FERA liberalised, then **FEMA 1999** replaced it |
| July 2022 | RBI rupee settlement via **Special Rupee Vostro Accounts (SRVA)** |
| FTP 2023 | From incentives to **remission of duties**; **Districts as Export Hubs**; in force 1 Apr 2023, no end date |
- **Export promotion** (outward-looking): East Asian economies adopted it in the 1970s and moved ahead of India.
- SRVA purpose: less dollar dependence, rupee internationalisation, trade with sanctioned partners (e.g. Tanzania).
- Export Promotion Mission (2025-26 to 2030-31): DGFT, via Niryat Protsahan and Niryat Disha.
- US tariff during deal talks: **25% + 25% = 50%**.

| India-ASEAN goods | 2009 (under review) |
| Korea CEPA / Japan CEPA | 2010 / 2011 |

| **India-Australia ECTA** | 2022 |

- June 2026: **15 FTAs covering 27 countries**; talks with 42 more. FTAs could cover ~75% of exports; used only **20-30%**.
- ASEAN deficit $10.4 bn (2012) to $51.2 bn (2025).
!TRAP ECTA = Australia; TEPA = EFTA; CETA = UK. $100 bn: UAE = trade, EFTA = investment.

### 7. SEA ROUTES, CHOKEPOINTS AND WORLD PORTS
#### 7.1 Routes
- **North Atlantic Route** ("Big Trunk Route") - busiest; US north-east to north-west Europe.
- **Suez route** - shortest Europe-Asia; **Cape of Good Hope route** - round southern Africa; Panama route; Trans-Pacific route.
#### 7.2 Chokepoints
| Waterway | Connects / fact |
|---|---|
| **Malacca** | Andaman Sea (Indian Ocean)-South China Sea; Malay Peninsula vs Sumatra; Singapore at south end; busiest strait, 1/3 of trade and seaborne oil; 80%+ of China's oil imports |
| **Hormuz** | **Persian Gulf-Gulf of Oman**; Iran vs Oman/UAE; ~20% of global oil |
| **Suez Canal** | Mediterranean-Red Sea; Egypt, Isthmus of Suez; 193.30 km; 1869; cuts Europe-Asia by up to 7,000 km; 12-15% of trade; greatest strategic significance |
| **Bab-el-Mandeb** | Red Sea-Gulf of Aden; Yemen vs Djibouti/Eritrea; ~29-30 km |
| Panama | Atlantic-Pacific; locks; completed 1914 |
| Turkish Straits | Bosphorus + Dardanelles; Black Sea-Mediterranean |
| Bering | Arctic-Pacific |
| Lombok | Bali-Lombok; Java Sea-Indian Ocean; alternative (with Sunda) to Malacca |
| **SUMED pipeline** | Egypt: Ain Sukhna (Red Sea) to Sidi Kerir (Mediterranean); Gulf oil to Europe |
- 2026: Hormuz closed in early March (US-Israel war on Iran); Houthis took **Perim Island** (Sept 2026).
#### 7.3 World Seaports
- **Shanghai** - busiest container port (WSC 2024: Shanghai, Singapore, Ningbo); old key: Shanghai second after Singapore; Shenzhen 4th among Shanghai, Singapore, Shenzhen, Rotterdam.
- **Rotterdam** - Europe's largest; **Singapore** - Malacca transshipment hub (not London); Jebel Ali - Dubai.
### 8. MAJOR PORTS OF INDIA
- **13 major ports** (12 operational + **Vadhavan**); ~200 non-major under State Maritime Boards; major under **Major Port Authorities Act**; MoPSW.
- Vadhavan: Palghar, Maharashtra; approved 19 June 2024; SPV **JNPA 74% + MMB 26%**; target 2034.
- Coastline ~**11,098 km** (2024; earlier 7,517 km); Gujarat longest.
| Port | State | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Kandla (Deendayal)** | Gujarat | Tidal, Gulf of Kutch; Asia's first EPZ; built 1950s after Karachi went to Pakistan; highest import tonnage 2023-24; no LNG terminal |
| Mumbai | Maharashtra | Largest natural port; Salsette Island |
| **JNPA (Nhava Sheva)** | Maharashtra | 1989; decongests Mumbai; largest container port; first 100% landlord port |
| Mormugao | Goa | Zuari mouth; natural; fastest cargo growth FY26 |
| New Mangalore | Karnataka | Kudremukh iron ore |
| Kochi | Kerala | Natural, Vembanad Lake |
| **Chennai** | Tamil Nadu | Oldest, largest artificial; 1881; third-oldest major port |
| **Kamarajar (Ennore)** | Tamil Nadu | 12th major port, coal; first corporatised; ~24 km north of Chennai |
| V.O. Chidambaranar (Tuticorin) | Tamil Nadu | Artificial, north of Adam's Bridge |
| **Visakhapatnam** | **Andhra Pradesh** | "Brightest Jewel"; deepest landlocked protected; record 91.17 MMT 2025-26 |
| **Paradip** | Odisha | Mahanadi delta; major port 18 Apr 1966; dry bulk leader; iron ore to Japan |
| **Kolkata-Haldia (SMP)** | West Bengal | Oldest; only riverine (Hooghly) |
- Tamil Nadu has 3 major ports. West coast north to south: Deendayal, Mumbai, Mormugao, Kochi.
- **Mundra** - largest private port, 1st in container performance; **Sikka** - liquid bulk; **Vizhinjam** (Kerala) - first transshipment port, now a customs port.
- Others: Udupi (Malpe) - Karnataka; Janjira - Maharashtra; Kakinada - AP; Dahej - Gulf of Khambhat (Bharuch), non-major; Quilon - minor.
- Gujarat port towns: Okha, Porbandar, Alang, Bedi, Bhavnagar, Dahej, Jafarabad, Mandvi, Navlakhi, Surat, Veraval - **not Jamnagar**.
- LNG: Dahej, Hazira, Mundra (Gujarat); Kochi; Dabhol; Ennore; Dhamra.
### 9. PORT TYPES AND COASTS
| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Natural | Mumbai, Kochi, Mormugao |
| Artificial | Chennai, Tuticorin |
| Landlocked | Visakhapatnam |
| Tidal | Kandla |
| Decongestion | JNPT (Mumbai), Haldia (Kolkata), Kamarajar (Chennai), Paradip (Kolkata, Vizag) |
- **West coast**: narrow, submerged, indented, deep water near shore, estuaries - natural harbours. **East coast**: broad, deltaic, **wide shallow shelf**, **cyclones** - fewer ports (APPSC 2023); it gets **more** sediment, not less.
- Bengaluru is landlocked, not a harbour.
