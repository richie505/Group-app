## U0028 | JAIN COUNCILS, DIGAMBARA–SVETAMBARA SPLIT, JAIN LITERATURE, SPREAD AND ROYAL PATRONS
### 1. JAIN COUNCILS
| Council | Place | Period / patron | President | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **First** | **Pataliputra** | c. **300 BCE**, reign of **Chandragupta Maurya** | **Sthulabhadra** | Canon arranged into **12 Angas**; Svetambara Agama edited; led to the **first split** |
| **Second** | **Vallabhi** (Gujarat) | **512 CE**, **Maitraka** patronage | **Devardhi Kshamashramana** (Devardhigani) | Final compilation; canon **written down**; **Upangas** added |
- Parallel councils at **Mathura and Vallabhi** are placed in the 4th c. CE; some accounts add 12th-c. councils at Vallabhi and Mathura under **Kumarapala**, with Hemachandra presiding.
- Andhra: a **Jain conference at Venkatathatipuri** (Satavahana era), presided over by **Arhabali** – **not one of the two councils**.
!TRAP The Angas were compiled at the 1st council (Pataliputra), not at Vallabhi.
!PAIR Pataliputra | Sthulabhadra | 12 Angas || Vallabhi | Devardhi Kshamashramana | Written down, Upangas
### 2. DIGAMBARA–SVETAMBARA SPLIT
#### 2.1 Origin
- **12-year famine** in Magadha under **Chandragupta Maurya** (told in **Parishishtaparvan**).
- **Bhadrabahu** (the **sixth Thera**) led monks, with Chandragupta, south to **Shravanabelagola** → **Digambara**.
- **Sthulabhadra** stayed in Magadha → **Svetambara**; he was the first founder of the Svetambara creed at the first partition.
- Formal split dated to about the **3rd century CE**; another view links it to the first council (c. 300 BCE).
#### 2.2 Points of Difference
| Aspect | Digambara | Svetambara |
|---|---|---|
| Leader | **Bhadrabahu** (went south) | **Sthulabhadra** (stayed in Magadha) |
| Dress | **Sky-clad**, nude monks | **White robes** |
| Women | Must be **reborn as men** for moksha | **Can attain liberation** |
| Mahavira | Never married | Married **Yashoda** |
| Monk's possessions | **Broom and water pot** only | **14 items** incl. clothes |
| Canon | Hold all original Angas **lost** | Keep the **12 Angas** |
#### 2.3 Sub-sects
| Sub-sect | Branch | Leader / feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Yapaniya** | Bridge sect (from Digambaras) | Tried to reconcile both; some Svetambara views; nude images; active in **Andhra** (Yapanacharyas) |
| **Sthanakvasi** | Svetambara offshoot | **Against idol worship**; Lonka Shah |
| **Terapanthi** | Svetambara offshoot | Founded by **Acharya Bhikshu**, 18th c. |
!TRAP Sthanakvasi and Terapanthi are Svetambara offshoots; Yapaniya is the bridge sect.
### 3. JAIN CANON
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Agamas / Siddhanta** | Sacred texts, in **Ardhamagadhi** (eastern Prakrit) |
| **14 Purvas** | **Earliest** scripture, now **mostly lost**; last known to **Sambhutavijay and Bhadrabahu** (time of the last Nanda) |
| **12 Angas** | Mahavira's teaching compiled by the **Ganadharas** (12, not 14) |
| **Acharanga Sutra** | **Code of conduct for monks** |
| **Bhagavati Sutra** | Doctrines |
#### 3.1 Svetambara Canon – Number Game
| Part | Number |
|---|---|
| Angas | 12 |
| Upangas | 12 |
| Prakirnas | 10 |
| Cheda Sutras | 6 |
| Mula Sutras | 4 |
- **Early Jain texts**: Acharanga Sutra, Sutrakritanga, Kalpasutra (Brihat Kalpa), Bhagavati.
#### 3.2 Odd-One-Out – Not Jain
| Text | Religion |
|---|---|
| **Nettipakarana, Avadanashataka** | Buddhist |
| **Therigatha** | Buddhist (verses of early Buddhist women, Theravada) |
| Dipavamsa, Mahavamsa, Divyavadana, Milindapanho | Buddhist |
### 4. JAIN WORKS AND AUTHORS
| Work | Author / note |
|---|---|
| **Kalpasutra** | **Bhadrabahu** – Jinacharitra, Sthaviravali, Samachari |
| **Samayasara, Yogasara**, Pravachanasara, Panchastikaya | **Kundakundacharya** (founded Saraswati gachcha at **Konakondla, Anantapur**) |
| **Tattvartha Sutra** | **Umasvati** – accepted by both sects |
| **Parishishtaparvan** (Sthaviravali Charitra) | **Hemachandra**, 12th c.; source for the split and early Jain teachers |
| **Mahapurana / Adipurana** (life of Rishabha) | **Jinasena** |
| Trishashtilakshana Mahapurana (Chavundaraya Purana, Kannada) | **Chamundaraya (Chamund Rai)** |
| **Kalyanakaraka** (medicine) | **Ugradityacharya** |
| **Jinendra Kalyanabhyudayamu** | **Appayacharya**, dedicated to Kakatiya **Prataparudra** |
| Yasastilaka Champu, Nitivakyamrita | Somadeva Suri |
| Harivamsha Purana | Jain version of the Mahabharata |
!TRAP Kalpasutra = Bhadrabahu; Samayasara / Yogasara = Kundakundacharya; Parishishtaparvan = Hemachandra.

### 6. SPREAD OF JAINISM
- South: Bhadrabahu–Chandragupta migration to **Shravanabelagola** (Karnataka), route through **Rayalaseema (Kurnool, Anantapur)**; ascetic camps in **Erramala and Nallamala** caves.
- Early Christian era centres: **Mathura and Ujjain**.
- Later: **Gujarat** (Chalukyas), **Karnataka** (Gangas, Rashtrakutas), Tamil country; survived in Gujarat, Rajasthan, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu.
!TRAP Jainism went south under **Bhadrabahu**, not Sthulabhadra.

### 8. JAINISM IN ANDHRA PRADESH
#### 8.1 Dynasties
- Royal dynasties that followed Jainism: **Satavahanas → Eastern Chalukyas → Kakatiyas**; **early Kakatiyas were Jains** (Siddeshwara Charitra).
- Under Eastern Chalukyas, **Shudras adopted Jainism** for social advancement.
#### 8.2 Centres
| Centre | Note |
|---|---|
| **Potla Cheruvu** | Famous Jain centre of the **Eastern Chalukya** period |
| **Nedumba Vasati, Bejawada (Vijayawada)** | By **Ayyana Mahadevi**, wife of Vishnuvardhana; village Mushinikonda donated |
| **Ramatirtham** (Ramagiri, Vizianagaram) | Eastern Chalukya Jain hill centre; **Ugraditya** wrote Kalyanakaraka here |
| **Vaddamanu** | Samprati's vihara (also Kharavela) |
| **Danavulapadu** (Kadapa) | Rashtrakuta **Indra III** |
| **Kathikabharana Jinalaya, Dharmavaram** | By **Durgaraja** (not Danavulapadu) |
| **Kadalalaya Basadi** | By **Mailama**, wife of Kakatiya minister **Betana** (1117 CE Padmakshi inscription) |
| Aryavatam (Bikkavolu), Kolanupaka, Munugodu | Other Jain centres |
- **Vijayanagara**: **Devaraya II** built a Jinalaya in Pan Supari Bazar.
#### 8.3 Decline
- **Ganapatideva** persecuted Jains (Siddeshwara Charitra).
- A Jain monk fled **Rajaraja Narendra's** persecution to **Hanamkonda** (Orugallu Kaifiyat).
- Jainism held rocky **Rayalaseema** and the north coastal borderlands; Buddhism held the Krishna–Godavari delta.
### 9. CONTRIBUTIONS OF JAINISM
- **Language**: early Jains used popular **Prakrit**, not Sanskrit, to reach common people; canon in **Ardhamagadhi**.
- Jains wrote the **earliest important Apabhramsa** works and its **first grammar**.
- Enriched **Prakrit, Apabhramsa, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu** – grammar, poetry, mathematics.
- **Social base**: **traders and merchants** in towns (not agriculturalists); banking; Jain merchant guilds on Satavahana trade routes.
- Stressed **conduct over birth**; challenged caste; promoted vegetarianism.
- **Decline**: no missionary zeal, loss of royal support, sect split, severe austerity, **Bhakti** revival.
!TRAP The claim "Jains never used Apabhramsa" is wrong.
