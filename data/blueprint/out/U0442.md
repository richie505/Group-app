## U0442 | ANDHRA PRADESH WELFARE AND SOCIAL SECURITY PROGRAMMES
### 1. WELFARE PACKAGES – NAVARATNALU AND SUPER SIX
#### 1.1 Navaratnalu (YSRCP, 2019-24)
- The previous government's package of **nine** flagship welfare promises; paid from **revenue expenditure** (with rice and power subsidies and pensions).
- Nine pillars: **Rythu Bharosa**, **fee reimbursement**, **Aarogyasri**, **Jalayagnam**, **prohibition**, **Amma Vodi**, **Pedalandariki Illu** (housing), **YSR Aasara/Cheyutha**, **Pension Kanuka**.
!TRAP Vahana Mitra (auto and taxi drivers) was NOT part of Navaratnalu; Stree Shakti is a Super Six guarantee, not Navaratnalu.
#### 1.2 Super Six (present government)
| Guarantee | Beneficiary | Benefit / design | Status / data |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Talliki Vandanam** (Thalliki Vandanam) | Mother of every school-going child (government or private school) | **₹15,000 per child per year** | ₹8,456 crore in Budget 2026-27 |
| **Stree Shakti** | Women and transgender persons (AP domicile); later PwD | **Free bus travel** in **5 APSRTC bus categories** (not all) | Launched **15 Aug 2025**; 71 crore+ free journeys (13 Jun 2026); women's share of bus travel 40% → 65% |
| **Deepam-2** | **83 lakh families** | **3 free LPG cylinders a year** | Launched 2024; about 2 crore cylinders, ₹2,684 crore (1 Jan 2026); earlier **Deepam (1999)** gave LPG connections |
| **Annadata Sukhibhava** | Farmers, **tenants included** | **₹20,000 a year = ₹14,000 State + ₹6,000 PM-KISAN**; replaced Rythu Bharosa | Instalments ₹7,000 / ₹7,000 / ₹6,000; Budget 2026-27 ₹6,600 crore |
| **Jobs** | Youth | **20 lakh jobs** in 5 years, or **₹3,000** unemployment allowance | ITI and skill placements |
| **Aadabidda Nidhi** | Women | **₹1,500 a month** | Promise |
- Talliki Vandanam: cuts the cost of schooling to raise the **Gross Enrolment Ratio** (APPSC 2025).
- Stree Shakti: 33.32 crore trips; 92% occupancy (AP SES 2025-26).

### 3. SOCIAL SECURITY PENSIONS – NTR BHAROSA
#### 3.1 Pension Evolution
| Period | Name | Old-age / widow amount |
|---|---|---|
| 2014-19 | **NTR Bharosa** | ₹1,000, later ₹2,000 |
| 2019-24 | **YSR Pension Kanuka** | Raised in steps to ₹3,000 (Jan 2022: ₹2,250 → ₹2,500) |
| June 2024 onwards | **NTR Bharosa** (renamed back) | **₹4,000** from **April 2024** |
#### 3.2 Categories and Age
| Category | Amount per month |
|---|---|
| **Old age (60+)** and **widows (18+)** | **₹4,000** (from ₹3,000) |
| **Disabled (40%+, no age limit)** | **₹6,000**; PwD ₹6,000-15,000 |
| **Thalassaemia** and similar patients | **₹10,000** |
| EWS communities (Arya Vysya, Brahmin, Kapu, EBC, Kamma, Reddy, Kshatriya) | Same ₹3,000 → ₹4,000 hike; 14,99,162 beneficiaries (2024-25) |
- Other categories: artists, **dappu artists**, fishermen, **toddy tappers**, Sainik welfare, weavers, artisans; spouse pensions to surviving wives.
- Overall range ₹4,000-15,000 a month.
#### 3.3 Data and Delivery
- **61.92 lakh** beneficiaries; ₹2,693 crore released for 1 Sep 2026; Budget 2026-27 **₹27,719 crore**; ₹73,506 crore spent since June 2024.
- Delivered **at the doorstep** with **Aadhaar biometric (ABBA)** authentication to remove ghost beneficiaries; nodal agency **SERP**; automated in Jan 2026.
- AP **tops up the central NSAP pension** from its own funds.
- **16th Finance Commission**: AP has the **highest per-capita social security pension spending** among States.
- Sep 2026: **family mapping** before new pensions; the **family is the unit** of Super Six delivery.
- Other States: **Mukhyamantri Vriddhajan Pension Yojana = Bihar** (poor above 60; APPSC 2019). Kerala pays ₹2,000 a month.
### 4. HEALTH WELFARE
#### 4.1 Aarogyasri → Dr NTR Vaidya Seva
| Year | Name |
|---|---|
| **2007** | **Rajiv Aarogyasri** (under **Y.S. Rajasekhara Reddy**) |
| 2014 | Dr NTR Vaidya Seva |
| 2019 | Dr YSR Aarogyasri |
| 2024 | **Dr NTR Vaidya Seva** |
- Cashless care to **1.43 crore families**, **3,257 procedures**; linked with **Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY**; ₹4,000 crore to the Trust (2024-25 BE).
- **New insurance model (May 2025)**, announced by Health Minister **Y. Satya Kumar Yadav**: cover up to **₹25 lakh per family per year** for about **1.65 crore poor and middle-class families** (not only BPL).
- **Universal Health Policy**: **every citizen ₹2.5 lakh** free treatment (insurance mode); **poor up to ₹25 lakh** via NTR Vaidya Seva Trust.
- **EHS** (Employees Health Scheme): cashless treatment for State employees and pensioners through the same Trust.
!TRAP ₹2.5 lakh = every citizen; ₹25 lakh = poor and middle-class families.
#### 4.2 Services and Indicators
| Service | Feature |
|---|---|
| **108** | **Emergency ambulance**; 768 ambulances, 9.22 lakh patients transported |
| **104** | **Mobile medical units** giving **fixed-day village** health services |
| **Family Physician** | Launched **6 April 2023** at Lingamguntla (**Chilakaluripet**, Palnadu); doctors visit **10,032 village health clinics** on fixed days |
- Health budget 2026-27: ₹19,305 crore; **MMR 45, IMR 24** (2024-25), better than national.
### 5. WOMEN'S SAFETY AND SUPPORT
- **Disha app** (8 Feb 2020, Rajamahendravaram): **SOS** button alerts the police control room; first **Disha police station** opened the same day at **Rajamahendravaram** (18 planned). In 2025 the app was replaced by **Suraksha**.
- **AP Disha Bill, 2019**: **investigation 7 days + trial 14 days = 21 days** for sexual offences against women; amended IPC and CrPC; **withdrawn** as **BNS and BNSS** replaced IPC/CrPC from **1 July 2024** (not 2023).
- **One Stop Centres**: police, medical, legal, counselling and shelter help under **one roof**; part of MWCD's **Mission Shakti** (with Shakti Sadan); backed by **Women Helpline 181** (universalised **2019**, not 2024).

### 8. EDUCATION WELFARE
- **Jagananna Amma Vodi** (Jan 2020): **₹15,000** a year to the bank account of the **mother/recognised guardian** of a poor child who enrols and attends school (APPSC 2023); replaced by **Talliki Vandanam**.
- **Vidya Deevena** = **fee reimbursement** (RTF); **Vasathi Deevena** = hostel/boarding support; both listed as **post-matric scholarships**.
- **Mana Badi Nadu-Nedu** (**14 Nov 2019**): upgrades government school infrastructure; now **Mana Badi – Mana Bhavishyathu**; Phase III (14,845 schools, ₹3,088 crore) yet to begin (Aug 2026).
- **Student kits**: Jagananna Vidya Kanuka → **Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan Vidyarthi Mitra** (bags, books); kits to 35 lakh+ students.
- Mega DSC coaching for SC/ST/BC/minority students.
### 9. FOOD, HOUSING AND SANITATION
#### 9.1 Food
- **Door delivery of ration** by mobile dispensing units began in **2021**.
- **Anna Canteens**: cheap meals for the **urban poor** at **₹5**; 62 rural canteens opened 15 Apr 2026 at **Dharanikota (Palnadu)** → total **269**.
- Proposal (12 Sep 2026): **5 kg rice per family member**, family as the unit; rice subsidy ₹2,345 crore (2024-25 BE).
#### 9.2 Housing
- **AP State Housing Corporation (APSHCL)**: Weaker Section Housing Programme, motto **"Housing for All"**.
- **Pedalandariki Illu** (Navaratnalu): house sites and houses for **poor women**; **YSR Jagananna housing / colony houses launched 3 June 2021**.
- Current policy: house sites of **3 cents (rural)** and **2 cents (urban)** for every eligible homeless household by **2029**; 7.60 lakh houses to finish under PMAY-U (BLC), PMAY-G and PM-JANMAN.
- Opposition seeks a probe into alleged fake house site pattas (2019-24).
#### 9.3 Sanitation and Water
- **CLAP** (Clean Andhra Pradesh): **door-to-door segregated garbage** collection; launched **2 Oct 2021** (Gandhi Jayanti), not 15 Aug 2025; segregation 96.07%.
- **Swachh Andhra**: one of the **10 Sutralu**; 500 ODF Plus villages (Jan 2026).
- **Jal Jeevan Mission**: 70.04 lakh households (73%) with taps; all rural households by **2027**.
