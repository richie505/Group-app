### 6. QUANTUM COMPUTING
#### 6.1 Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Qubit** | **Basic unit of quantum information**; quantum version of the binary bit; **two-state** system (e.g. electron spin) |
| **Superposition** | Qubit can be **0, 1 or in between** |
| **Entanglement** | Links qubits **regardless of distance** |
| **Decoherence** | Loss of quantum state through the environment = **main barrier** (9 Jul 2026) |
| **Entanglement sudden death** | Abrupt total loss of entanglement |
| **Shor's algorithm** | Can break **public-key cryptography** |
| **Q-Day** | Point when quantum computing (Shor's algorithm) can break public-key cryptography |
#### 6.2 Classical Bit vs Qubit
| Feature | Classical bit | Qubit |
|---|---|---|
| States | 0 **or** 1 | 0, 1 or **both at once** (superposition) |
| Linking | Independent bits | **Entangled** qubits share state at any distance |
| Weakness | Stable | **Decoherence** – environment destroys the state |
| Example | Transistor switch | **Electron spin**, photon (Jiuzhang), superconducting circuit (Sycamore) |
!KEY Qubit = quantum form of the classical bit; superposition + entanglement give quantum computers their power.
#### 6.3 Applications
- Quantum computing, **quantum communication**, **quantum sensing and metrology**, quantum materials and devices (the four thematic hubs of the National Quantum Mission).
- Security: **post-quantum cryptography** migration – DST quantum-safe report (31 May 2026).
- **Raman Research Institute** (16 Sep 2026): one well-timed local **"quantum flip"** can delay or stop entanglement sudden death.
- **Microsoft Majorana 2**: raised qubit stability to **20–60 s** (2026).

| Machine | Fact |
|---|---|

### 10. 3D PRINTING
- **Additive manufacturing**: object built **layer by layer** from a **CAD** model; 4D printing adds shape change over time.
| Sector | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Health | Bio-printed tissues, **bionic ears**, **reconstructive surgery** | Liver tissue, cornea |
| Food | Printed **confectionery** | Chocolate shapes |
| Construction | Printed buildings | Bengaluru post office (2023) |
| Automotive / aerospace | Light, complex parts | CNT-reinforced polymers (DRDO) |
| Defence / robotics | Custom parts, humanoid bodies | Manav (A-SET) |
- **Bio-printing**: **Pandorum Technologies (Bengaluru)** – first Indian company to 3D-print **human liver tissue**; **IIT Hyderabad + LV Prasad Eye Institute + CCMB** – 3D-printed **cornea** from decellularised corneal matrix and stem cells.
- **Construction**: India's first 3D-printed post office, **Bengaluru (2023)**, built in 43 days.
!TRAP 3D printing is **additive** (adds layers), not subtractive machining; **4D** = printed object changes shape over time.
