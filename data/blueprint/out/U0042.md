## U0042 | SUNGAS AND KANVAS – PUSHYAMITRA SUNGA, BHARHUT AND SANCHI, PATANJALI, THE BESNAGAR PILLAR
### 1. DYNASTIC SUCCESSION
| Dynasty | Period | Founder | End |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maurya | c. 321–187 BC | Chandragupta | **Brihadratha** killed by **Pushyamitra** |
| **Sunga** | c. 187–75 BC | **Pushyamitra** | **Devabhuti** killed by his **Brahmana minister (Amatya) Vasudeva Kanva** |
| **Kanva** | c. 73–28 BC, 4 kings | **Vasudeva Kanva** | **Susharman** overthrown by **Simuka**, Satavahana founder |
- **Kanvas directly followed** the Sungas; both were **Brahmins**.
- Chronology: **Maurya → Sunga → Kanva → Kushana** (BPSC 2025).
- Magadhan periods: Haryanka 544–412 BC; Nanda 344–321 BC; Maurya 321–187 BC; Sunga 187–75 BC.
- **Puranas**: Satavahanas defeated the Sungas and Kanvas. **Satakarni I** is said to have defeated Pushyamitra.
!TRAP APPSC GS 2023: Brihadratha was killed in **187 BC**.
#### 1.1 Founder → Ruler Replaced
| Founder | Replaced | Dynasty ended |
|---|---|---|
| Pushyamitra | Brihadratha | Maurya |
| Vasudeva Kanva | Devabhuti | Sunga |
| Simuka (Sindhuk, Shiprak) | Susharma(n) | Kanva |
| Mahapadma Nanda | last Shishunaga | Shishunaga |
- Magadha: **Haryanka → Shishunaga → Nanda → Maurya → Sunga → Kanva**.
- Mahapadma Nanda (Ugrasen): called **Sarvakshatrantaka** and **Aparoparashurama** in the Puranas.
### 2. PUSHYAMITRA SUNGA
- **Mauryan commander-in-chief (senani)**, a **Brahmin**; killed Brihadratha while showing him the army – a military coup.
- Ancestors of the Sungas came from **Ujjain**; heartland **Magadha, Malwa-Vidisha, Narmada valley**; dominions reached the Narmada, incl. **Pataliputra, Ayodhya, Vidisha**.
- Defeated Bactrian Greek **Demetrius**; Greeks had challenged his sacrificial horse.
- **Two Ashvamedhas** – **Ayodhya inscription of Dhana(deva)** (not three).
- North-west lost to the Yavanas; Deccan to the Satavahanas.
- Kept Mauryan institutions; feudatories rose – **Bhojas, Maharathis, Rastrikas**.
- Sunga court revived **classical Sanskrit** (Ashoka used Prakrit).
- **Manusmriti** took shape; foreigners = **Vratya Kshatriyas**.
!TRAP Pushyamitra did **not** help spread Buddhism; he was first a Mauryan general.
### 3. SOURCES FOR PUSHYAMITRA AND THE YAVANA INVASION
| Source | Author | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| **Gargi Samhita** (Yuga Purana) | Garga | Yavana march on **Saketa, Panchala, Mathura, Pataliputra** |
| **Mahabhashya** | **Patanjali** | Yavana siege of **Saketa and Madhyamika**; "we perform sacrifices for Pushyamitra" |
| **Malavikagnimitram** | **Kalidasa** | Hero **Agnimitra**; **Vasumitra** beats the Yavanas on the **Sindhu**; war with Vidarbha |
| **Divyavadana** | Buddhist text | Pushyamitra as **persecutor** of Buddhists |
| **Harshacharita** | **Banabhatta** | Pushyamitra killed Brihadratha while **showing him the army** |
| Ayodhya inscription | Dhana(deva) | Two Ashvamedhas |
!TRAP Yavana invasion is in Gargi Samhita, Mahabhashya, Malavikagnimitram – **NOT Visakhadatta's Mudrarakshasa**, which tells Chandragupta Maurya's rise (APPSC G1 2022).
### 4. SUNGA RULERS
| Ruler | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Pushyamitra** | Founder |
| **Agnimitra** | **Son**; viceroy at **Vidisha**; fought **Yajnasena of Vidarbha** |
| **Vasumitra** | **Grandson** (Agnimitra's son); defeated the **Yavanas** |
| **Bhagabhadra** | **Heliodorus** came to his court |
| **Devabhuti** | **Last Sunga** |
- **Malavikagnimitram**: Kalidasa's **first play**, **5 acts**; love story of **Agnimitra and Malavika**.
- Kalidasa and lexicographer **Amarasimha** were among the **Navratna of Chandragupta II**.
- **Mrichchhakatikam** is by **Sudraka**, not Kalidasa.
!PAIR Agnimitra | son | Vidisha viceroy || Vasumitra | grandson | beat the Yavanas
### 5. PATANJALI AND THE MAHABHASHYA
- **Patanjali** – **contemporary** and, it is believed, **royal priest** of Pushyamitra; supervised the two Ashvamedhas.
- **Mahabhashya** explains **Panini's Ashtadhyayi** through **Katyayana's Varttikas** – grammarian trio **Panini–Katyayana–Patanjali**.
- Evidence on 2nd-century-BCE society, geography, language and Yavana raids.
!TRAP **Patanjali**, not Panini, is linked to Pushyamitra; Panini's Ashtadhyayi is 3rd–4th c. BC.
### 6. PUSHYAMITRA AND BUDDHISM – THE DEBATE
| For persecution | Against |
|---|---|
| **Divyavadana**; Tibetan monk **Taranatha** (16th c.) – stupas destroyed, bounty on monks | **Romila Thapar, Etienne Lamotte**: exaggerated, sectarian |
| | Buddhist art flourished at **Sanchi, Bharhut, Bodh Gaya** |
| | Royal patronage fell, but **merchants and guilds** kept funding Buddhism |
- Hostility probably aimed at monasteries allied with the **Indo-Greeks**.
- Destruction of the original **Bodhi Tree**: tradition names Pushyamitra and later **Shashanka of Gauda** (7th c. CE). Listed in 2026 with the **Ashtamahasthana** (8 sacred Buddhist sites).
### 7. BESNAGAR (HELIODORUS) PILLAR
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Donor | **Heliodorus**, native of **Taxila**, a Yavana |
| Envoy of | Indo-Greek king **Antialcidas** |
| At court of | Sunga king **Bhagabhadra** |
| Site | **Besnagar** (Bhilsa/Vidisha), MP |
| Nature | **Garuda-dhvaja** dedicated to **Vasudeva** (Vishnu); Heliodorus calls himself a **Bhagavata** |
| Language/script | Prakrit / Brahmi |
- FIRST: **earliest inscriptional proof of Bhagavatism** (early Vaishnavism); first stone monument of the Bhagavata religion; shows a foreigner absorbed into Indian religion.
!TRAP Besnagar is NOT known for a Shaivite cave shrine. Bhaja = Buddhist cave shrine; Sittanavasal = Jain.
### 8. SUNGA ART – BHARHUT, SANCHI, BODH GAYA, MATHURA
- Transition from **wood/ivory to stone**.
| Site | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Sanchi** | Ashoka's brick stupa **encased in stone**; stone railings (**vedika**); **toranas** with **Jataka tales** and Buddha's life |
| **Bharhut** (Satna, MP) | Railings and gateways of early narrative art; inscriptions name **guilds of ivory workers and stone carvers** who paid; "fine example of Shunga architecture" (RPSC 2023) |
| **Bodh Gaya** | Early **stone railing** |
| **Mathura** | Mathura style of sculpture **began** under the Sungas |
- Sanchi **south torana**: inscription of **Ananda** links it to **Satakarni II** – gateways often dated to the Satavahana period.
!TRAP Gupta temple architecture did not begin under the Sungas.
