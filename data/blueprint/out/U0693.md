## U0693 | R&D SPENDING IN INDIA - GERD, INNOVATION INDICES, PATENTS ECOSYSTEM
### 1. GERD AS % OF GDP
| Country | GERD (% of GDP) |
|---|---|
| **India** | **0.84% (FY 2023-24)** - 15-year high, ending a decade-long fall |
| USA | about 3.5% |
| China | about 2.4% |
| **Israel** | about **6.3%** (2023) - world's highest |
| **South Korea** | about **5%** (2023) - 2nd |
- Earlier: **0.64-0.65%** (2020-21). National target: **2% of GDP**.
- Total R&D funding nearly doubled: Rs 1.33 lakh crore (FY20) to Rs 2.45 lakh crore (FY24).
### 2. GOVERNMENT VS PRIVATE SHARE
- **FY 2023-24**: private industry share **51.8%** - passed public funding **for the first time** (Aug 2026).
- 2020-21: private only **~36%** (USA ~70%, South Korea ~76%).
- Agency-wise share of central R&D (2020-21): **DOS 18.4%**, ICAR 12.4%, DAE 11.4%, CSIR 8.2%, DST 6.8%. **DRDO** has long been the largest (31.6% in 2017-18).
- Why private R&D lags: risk aversion, weak lab-industry links, lack of deep-tech capital.

### 7. STARTUP ECOSYSTEM
- **Startup India - 16 January 2016**: Fund of Funds Rs 10,000 crore, **3-year tax exemption**, exit in **90 days** (not 30), self-certification.
- India = **3rd-largest start-up hub**; **1.59 lakh** DPIIT-recognised start-ups (Sep 2025).
- Unicorns: India **4th with 61** (Hurun Global Unicorn Index, Jun 2026).
- Support: **AIM** (tinkering labs, incubators); **iDEX** with India-Korea **KIND-X**; **MANAGE, Hyderabad** - agri-start-up incubation. Deep-tech start-up eligibility notified Feb 2026.
### 8. R&D FUNDING REFORMS
- **ANRF Act 2023** - funds basic and applied research; ~80% of funds went to IITs (Aug 2026 critique).
- **RDI Scheme - 1 July 2025**: **Rs 1 lakh crore**; custodian **ANRF**; private R&D in AI, quantum, biotech, energy.
- Pharma-MedTech scheme: 7 CoEs at NIPERs.
