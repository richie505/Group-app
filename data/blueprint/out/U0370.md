## U0370 | IMPACT OF LPG ON FEDERALISM, WELFARE AND SOCIAL SECTOR SPENDING
### 1. COMPETITIVE VS COOPERATIVE FEDERALISM
- Post-1991 **delicensing** → states compete for private and foreign investment.
| Type | Symbol |
|---|---|
| Competitive | **NITI Aayog** (replaced Planning Commission's allocation role) |
| Cooperative | **GST Council** (Centre and States decide jointly) |
| Competitive and accountable | 16th FC's new **GDP-contribution** criterion |
- **BRAP** state ranking by **DPIIT** (first 2015): **AP first** in BRAP 2019 (Sept 2020), UP second, Telangana third.
### 2. FINANCE COMMISSIONS AND DEVOLUTION
| FC | Period | Chair | States' share |
|---|---|---|---|
| 14th | 2015-20 | **Y.V. Reddy** | **32% → 42%** (+10 points) |
| 15th | 2020-26 | **N.K. Singh** | **41%**; two reports (first for 2020-21) |
| 16th | 2026-31 | **Arvind Panagariya** | 41% retained (Budget 2026-27, 1 Feb 2026) |
- About **18%** of gross tax in non-shareable **cesses and surcharges** → actual devolution about **31-32%**, not 41%.
- **Southern states (2024)**: population weights punish good performance.
- **AP**: memorandum to 16th FC for higher devolution and post-bifurcation capital needs (Feb 2026); commercial tax ₹15,323 crore in Q1 FY 2026-27.
### 3. FISCAL RULES AND BORROWING
- **FRBM Act 2003** (amended 2018): fiscal deficit **≤3%** of GDP; general government debt **60%**, Central debt **40%** of GDP by 2025.
- Four statements in Parliament: Medium-term Fiscal Policy, Fiscal Policy Strategy, Macro-economic Framework, Medium-term Expenditure Framework.
- **Art. 293(3)**: a state indebted to the Centre needs Union consent to borrow.
- **State of Kerala v. Union (2024)**: **Art. 131** suit against the **Net Borrowing Ceiling** (counts off-budget borrowings).
- **16th FC**: state deficit cap **3% of GSDP**; end off-budget borrowing; rationalise subsidies.
- Economic Survey 2025-26: general government debt down **7.1 points** since 2020; Centre steers state capex through **SASCI**.
### 4. GST AND STATES' FISCAL AUTONOMY
- **GST Council (Art. 279A, 101st CAA 2016)**: Centre **one-third** vote, all States **two-thirds**; decisions by **three-fourths** weighted majority.
- **Compensation cess**: 5-year compensation ended **June 2022**; extended to repay COVID-era loans; discontinued for most goods from **22 Sept 2025**.
### 5. CENTRALLY SPONSORED SCHEMES (2015)
- Rationalised by **NITI Aayog Sub-Group of Chief Ministers (2015)**.
| Group | Detail |
|---|---|
| Core of the core | **MGNREGA, NSAP**, umbrella schemes for SCs, STs, minorities |
| Core | Mostly **60:40**; **90:10** for NE and Himalayan states |
| Optional | States' choice |
- **PMMVY, ICDS** now **60:40**. CSS grew **26.5% a year** since 2016-17; state plan allocations fell nearly **30%**.
### 6. SOCIAL SECTOR SPENDING
| Item | Figure |
|---|---|
| NHP 2017 target | Public health = **2.5% of GDP** |
| NEP 2020 target | Education = **6% of GDP** |
| Social protection | Only **4.0% of GDP** |
| States' share of school education spending | **75.2%** |
| States' unconditional cash transfers | **₹4.14 lakh crore** |
- Some states spend **over 200%** of their health budget on cash schemes → crowds out health, education, nutrition.
### 7. DBT AND JAM
- **DBT from 1 Jan 2013**; **PAHAL** = DBT of LPG subsidy via Aadhaar-seeded accounts.
- JAM streamlines delivery but may **exclude** those without digital access or documents.
### 8. WELFARE IN THE MARKET ERA
| Law | Provision |
|---|---|
| **MGNREGA (2005)** | **100 days** per rural household; about **90:10** funding |
| VB-G RAM G Act | Replaced MGNREGA; raises states' share |
| **NFSA 2013** | **75% rural / 50% urban**; **5 kg** per person per month |
- Rights → discretionary transfers turn rights-holders into **passive beneficiaries**.
### 9. REGIONAL DISPARITIES
- **Divergence view**: richer, industrialised states attracted most post-1991 private investment → gaps widened.
- Transfers try to correct imbalances; horizontal criteria spark disputes.
