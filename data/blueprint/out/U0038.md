## U0038 | MAURYAN ADMINISTRATION – CENTRAL, PROVINCIAL, LOCAL; ESPIONAGE, JUDICIARY, REVENUE
### 1. NATURE OF THE MAURYAN STATE
- Highly centralised "**paternal despotism**" – king heads executive, legislature and judiciary; the **first centralised bureaucratic empire** (census, standardised measures).
- Checks on the king: **Mantriparishad**, paura-janapada assemblies, public opinion – Kautilya: "**a chariot cannot move on one wheel**".
- The Arthashastra describes an ideal; how fully it worked is disputed.
### 2. SAPTANGA THEORY – SEVEN LIMBS IN ORDER
| No. | Element | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Swami** | King (comes first) |
| 2 | **Amatya** | Ministers |
| 3 | **Janapada** | Territory and people |
| 4 | Durga | Fort |
| 5 | Kosha | Treasury |
| 6 | Danda / Bala | Army |
| 7 | **Mitra** | **Ally** – "the ear of the state" |
- Allies help in peace and war; Kautilya separates true from fake allies; natural allies are better than artificial ones.
!TRAP **Shatru (enemy)** is NOT one of the standard seven; some lists add it only as an eighth.
### 3. CENTRAL OFFICERS – EIGHTEEN TIRTHAS
| Tirtha | Department |
|---|---|
| **Samaharta** | **Collector-general of revenue** (APPSC-GS 2023); cash and kind, helped by the **Bhagadhuk** |
| **Sannidhata** | **Treasurer**, keeper of state stores |
| **Dandapala** | Head of the **army** department (APPSC-G1 2022) |
| **Pradeshtri (Pradeshtha)** | Commissioner; **criminal justice**, heads kantakashodhana courts (MPPSC 2024) |
| **Antapala** | **Frontier** officer, border forts |
| Vyavaharika | Civil judge |
| Dauvarika | Palace gate |
| Prashasta | Prisons |
| Nayaka | City guard |
| Paura | Governor of the capital |
| Karmantika | Factories and mines |
| Mantri, Purohita, Senapati, Yuvaraja | Minister, chief priest, commander, crown prince |
!TRAP Samaharta = revenue collector; Sannidhata = treasurer – do not swap.
- The Saptanga + 18 Tirthas model was later used in Andhra by the **Eastern Chalukyas** (Ashtadasha Tirthas) and **Kakatiyas**.

### 5. PROVINCES AND SETTLEMENTS
#### 5.1 Provinces → Capital
| Province | Capital |
|---|---|
| **Uttarapatha** | **Taxila** |
| **Avantipatha (Avantirashtra)** | **Ujjain** |
| **Dakshinapatha** | **Suvarnagiri** – southern capital, ruled the Deccan and Andhra |
| **Kalinga** (added by Ashoka) | **Tosali** |
| Magadha (centre) | **Pataliputra** (under the emperor) |
- **Four** provinces under Chandragupta; ruled by **Kumaras** (princes, Kumaramatyas) or governors, helped by **Mahamatras**. Districts = **Vish / Ahara**.

| Unit | Villages |

### 6. LOCAL OFFICERS AND ASHOKAN OFFICIALS
| Officer | Level / role |
|---|---|
| **Pradeshika** | District – chief district administrator; revenue, police; tours every 5 years (RE III) |
| **Rajuka** | District – land survey, assessment, **judicial powers** (PE IV) |
| **Yukta** | Junior revenue / office officer; tours with pradeshikas |
| Sthanika | Tax collector over a larger division |
| **Gopa** | Accountant over **5–10 villages** |
| **Gramika** | **Village head**, with elders |
| Anta-mahamattas | Frontier forts |
| Rakshinas | City police |
- **Rajukas, Pradeshikas, Yuktas** = district-level officers named in Ashokan inscriptions (UPSC 2025).
- City: municipality under a **Nagaraka / Purmukhya**.
!TRAP Not Mauryan: **Mahabaladhikrita, Mahadandanayaka, Mahasandhivigrahika** (Gupta); **Bhagadugha, Sangrahitri** (Vedic). Gramika was also the Satavahana village head.
### 7. MEGASTHENES – CITY AND ARMY BOARDS
- **Agronomoi** = district officers (roads, irrigation, land measurement); **Astynomoi** = city officers, the municipal boards.
- Pataliputra: **30 members in 6 committees of 5**.
| Committee | Function |
|---|---|
| 1 | Crafts and industries |
| 2 | Foreigners |
| 3 | **Births and deaths** (census) |
| 4 | Trade, prices, weights and measures |
| 5 | Sale of manufactured goods |
| 6 | **Tax on sales** (one-tenth), incl. imports and exports – NOT land revenue |
- Army: also **6 boards of 5** – infantry, cavalry, chariots, elephants, navy, transport/commissariat. Chandragupta's army: **6,00,000 foot, 30,000 cavalry**.
!KEY Registration of births and deaths is municipal work unbroken since Mauryan times.
### 8. ESPIONAGE
- **Gudhapurushas** – spies who watched officials and provinces; under the mahamatyapasarpa.
| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Samstha** (stationary) | Kapatika, udasthita, grihapatika, vaidehaka, tapasa |
| **Sanchara** (wandering) | Satri, tikshna, rasada, parivrajika (women) |
### 9. JUDICIARY
| Court | Cases | Presided by |
|---|---|---|
| **Dharmasthiya** | **Civil** – marriage, contract, inheritance | 3 dharmasthas |
| **Kantakashodhana** ("removal of thorns") | **Criminal** | 3 pradeshtris |
- **Pradvivaka**: chief judge / legal adviser (APPSC G2 2019).
- Ladder: village court → **Sangrahana** → Dronamukha → Janapada → **king** (supreme judge).
- Punishments: fines, maiming, death; harshness varied by **varna**. Megasthenes: **no written code**.
- Civic penalty: **Pankodakasannirodhe** – fine for slushing the road (dirt, water logging).
### 10. REVENUE SYSTEM
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sita** | Income from **crown land** |
| **Bhaga** | King's share of produce, normally **1/6** (Megasthenes: 1/4 to 1/6) |
| **Bali** | Additional levy / tribute |
| **Pindakara** | Tax on **groups of villages** |
| **Pranaya** | **Emergency tax** ("gift of affection") |
| **Vivita** | Pasture land dues |
| **Hiranya** | Tax only in **cash** |
| Setu | Gardens, orchards, root crops |
| Vana | Forests |
- Land revenue was the main income; Megasthenes: **king owned all land**, officials collected directly.
- Other income: ferries, mines, pastures, licences, fines. Lumbini: exempt from bali, bhaga cut to **1/8**.
- **Sales tax evasion: death** (Megasthenes).

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- First centralised bureaucratic empire of India.
- Kalinga (Tosali) was the last province added, by Ashoka.
- Rajuka: only district officer with judicial powers.
