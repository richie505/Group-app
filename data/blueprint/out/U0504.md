## U0504 | ALLIED SECTORS – HORTICULTURE, ANIMAL HUSBANDRY, DAIRY, FISHERIES (PMMSY), AGRO-PROCESSING
### 1. ALLIED SECTORS – GROWTH AND SHARE
- Farm growth now comes from **livestock (7.1%)** and **fisheries (8.8%)** (ES 2025-26, decadal) – fisheries faster.
- **Livestock GVA** (2023-24, current prices): **₹15.06 lakh crore** = **30.87%** of agri GVA; 12.77% nominal CAGR (FY15-FY24).
- **Fisheries**: fastest-growing allied sub-sector – **9.08% a year since 2014-15**.
- **Horticulture**: about **33% (one-third)** of agri GVA, not half, on under 14% of area.
- Agri exports (2024-25): record **$51.9 billion** = 11.86% of merchandise exports; **processed food ≈ 23%** of agri exports (APPSC 2025).

### 3. HORTICULTURE
- Output **367.72 MT (2024-25)** – more than **foodgrains (357.73 MT)**.
- Top fruit: **banana** (~39,512 thousand MT, 2024-25), ahead of mango, apple, grapes. Leading fruit State: **Andhra Pradesh**.
- **MIDH**: Centrally Sponsored, **since 2014-15** (APPSC 2023); subsumed **National Horticulture Mission (2005-06)** and the NE/Himalayan horticulture mission.
- Export mangoes: **Alphonso** (most exported; Maharashtra), Kesar, **Banganapalli and Totapuri (AP)**. **Banganapalle GI: May 2017**.
- **Turmeric**: India **largest producer and exporter**; 30+ varieties; **National Turmeric Board – Nizamabad**; Maharashtra, Telangana, Karnataka, TN grow it.
- **Indo-Israel Centre of Excellence** in AP: **Vegetables and Flowers, Kuppam** (MIDH + MASHAV).
- **FPO (Food Products Order) mark**: mandatory on processed fruit products (jams, squashes, pickles).

### 6. DAIRY – OPERATION FLOOD AND NDDB
- **NDDB**: set up **1965 at Anand, Gujarat**; launched **Operation Flood**.
- **Operation Flood**: **1970-1996**, **three phases**, on the **Amul** cooperative model (Gujarat); built a national milk grid → India the world's largest milk producer.
### 7. FISHERIES – BLUE REVOLUTION
- Output: 95.79 lakh t (2013-14) → **197.75 lakh t (2024-25)**; India **2nd** in world (~8%); **China** largest (SOFIA 2024: 36% in 2022).
- **Inland fisheries** > **75%** of output; most important = natural inland fisheries.
- **PMMSY**: launched **2020**, **₹20,050 crore**; FY 2020-21 to 2024-25, extended to **2025-26**; Department of Fisheries.
| PMMSY component | Funding |
|---|---|
| **Central Sector** | 100% Centre: training, quarantine, harbour modernisation, data systems |
| **Centrally Sponsored** | **60:40** general; **90:10** NE/Himalayan; **100%** UTs |
- **PM-MKSSY** (Matsya Kisan Samridhi Sah-Yojana): **Central Sector sub-scheme** under PMMSY; formalises fisheries, supports micro-enterprises (APPSC 2024 – all statements correct).
- **FIDF**: fishing harbours and landing centres.
- **NFDB**: autonomous, **2006**, **HQ Hyderabad**; training and capacity building under PMMSY (not MPEDA).
- **MPEDA**: Act of Parliament **24 August 1972**; **HQ Kochi** (not Kolkata); under **Ministry of Commerce and Industry**; apex body for marine exports; absorbed Marine Products Export Promotion Council (1961).
### 8. FOOD PROCESSING
- **PM Kisan SAMPADA Yojana**: umbrella scheme of **Ministry of Food Processing Industries**; 'Unit' scheme – 32 projects sanctioned (2020).
- **Mega Food Parks**: improve processing infrastructure; **cluster approach** linking farmers, processors, retailers.
- **PMFME**: formalises **micro** food units under **One District One Product (ODOP)**.
- **Operation Greens (TOP)**: tomato, onion, potato price stabilisation under the same Ministry.
### 9. SERICULTURE, APICULTURE AND POULTRY
- India = **only country producing all five commercial silks**: mulberry, tropical tasar, oak tasar, eri, muga.
| Silk | Leading State |
|---|---|
| **Mulberry** | **Karnataka** (AP, TN, WB 2nd-4th) |
| **Tasar** | **Jharkhand** |
| **Muga, eri** | **Assam** |
- **National Beekeeping and Honey Mission (2020, Atmanirbhar Bharat)**: **Sweet Revolution** via **National Bee Board**.
- Top egg State: **AP**, then Tamil Nadu.
