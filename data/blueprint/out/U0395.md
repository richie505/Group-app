## U0395 | YOUTH UNREST AND AGITATION – CAUSES, FORMS, STUDENT MOVEMENTS AND REMEDIES
### 1. MEANING, TYPES AND THEORIES
- **Youth unrest:** collective frustration among young people when society's norms seem **ineffective or harmful**.
| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| **Persuasive** | Exam postponement |
| **Resistance** | Against attendance rules |
| **Revolutionary** | Overthrow the system (AASU, Bodo) |
- **Life cycle:** **Discontent** (first) → initiation → formalisation → public support → official action.
- **Theories:** discontent; **personal maladjustment**; **relative deprivation (Stouffer)**; **resource mobilisation** (tactics and leadership matter more than grievances).
### 2. CAUSES
- **Unemployment / jobless growth:** about **9.7 million** join the working-age group each year (2021-31).
- **Educational mismatch**, capitation fees; **identity crisis** and **generation gap** as families become nuclear; **political interference**.
| Campus causes | Off-campus causes |
|---|---|
| Exams, fees, hostels, teacher tensions | Sympathetic strikes, political interference, wider issues (CAA/NRC) |
### 3. FORMS
- Student agitations and protests; **secessionist** (Naga, Assam, Khalistan).
- **Militancy:** **Dalit Panthers (1972, Maharashtra)**; deviance: **drug abuse**.
- **New pattern:** **leaderless, digital** protests on encrypted apps, no formal central committee (Bangladesh 2024, Nepal Gen Z 2025).
### 4. STUDENT MOVEMENTS IN INDIA
| Movement | State | Issue / outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Swadeshi (1905)** | – | **First student-led national movement** |
| **Young Bengal** | Bengal | **First youth organisation**; founded by **Derozio** |
| **Nav Nirman (1974)** | **Gujarat** | Corruption and prices; CM **Chimanbhai Patel** resigned (Feb 1974) |
| **JP movement (1974)** | **Bihar** | **First nationwide** post-Independence student mobilisation; fed into Emergency (June 1975) |
| **AASU (1979-85)** | Assam | Illegal migrants → **Assam Accord 1985** |
| **Anti-Mandal (1990)** | North India | Against **27% OBC** reservation |
| Telangana students (1969; 2009-14) | Osmania University | Separate statehood |
| **India Against Corruption (2011)** | National | First major anti-corruption youth movement |
- Others: Nirbhaya protests, Patidar (2015), Jallikattu (2016-17).
- **2026:** **NEET paper leak** protests began at **Jantar Mantar** → nationwide → ministerial resignation (5 Aug 2026).
!TRAP Nav Nirman = Gujarat; JP movement = Bihar (both 1974).
### 5. ANDHRA PRADESH AGITATIONS
| Agitation | Year | Issue |
|---|---|---|

| Special Category Status | After 2014 | Youth demanding SCS promised at bifurcation |
- **29 Jul 2026:** YSRCP student/youth wings protested over alleged **Mega DSC** recruitment irregularities.
### 6. NATIONAL YOUTH POLICY
- First NYP: **1988**. **NYP 2014:** youth = **15-29 years** (NYP 2003: **13-35**); **5 objectives, 11 priority areas** (incl. participation in politics and governance, sports, social values, community engagement).
- **National Youth Day** (from **1985**): Swami Vivekananda. **Dept of Youth Affairs and Sports** 1985; separate Ministry from **2000**.
- **National Commission for Youth:** last (non-statutory) ran **2002-04**; a Bill seeks a constitutional body (Aug 2026).
### 7. DEMOGRAPHICS AND UNEMPLOYMENT DATA
- **Census 2011:** youth (15-24) about **231 million**, **~19.1%** of population; demographic dividend needs jobs for the 9.7 million yearly entrants.
| Survey | Figure |

| Global (ILO 2026) | **12.4%** (67 M); NEET 20%; women 27.4% vs men 13.1% |
