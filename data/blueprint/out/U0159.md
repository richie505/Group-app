## U0159 | TRADE CENTRES OF COASTAL ANDHRA – MASULIPATNAM, NIZAMPATNAM, BHEEMUNIPATNAM, YANAM
### 1. EXPORTS FROM COASTAL ANDHRA
#### 1.1 Export → Centre
| Export | Centre | Point |
|---|---|---|
| **Handloom cotton textiles**: chintz, palampores, muslins, kalamkari | **Machilipatnam** | The **handloom port** of the 16th-17th centuries (APPSC 2019) |
| **Saltpetre** / gunpowder | **Machilipatnam, Narsapur, Pulicat** | Bulk export |
| **Diamonds** | **Golconda** mines; **Kollur** (Guntur) | Kollur gave the **Koh-i-Noor** |
| **Indigo** | **Nagulavancha**, Nalgonda | Dye |
| **Salt** | **Pedaganjam, Chinaganjam** | APPSC 2019 |
| Cheap calico | Narasapuram | Coarse cloth |
| Taanis | Injaram | Cloth variety |
| Rumaals | Nellore | Kerchiefs |
- **Dominant pre-colonial industry = textiles**, second only to **diamonds** in the Qutb Shahi economy.
- Textile ports shipping to England, France and Holland: **Kalingapatnam, Srikakulam, Vizag, Bhimunipatnam, Corangi, Kakinada, Narsapur, Machilipatnam**.
!TRAP Salt = **Pedaganjam, Chinaganjam**; indigo = **Nagulavancha**; Koh-i-Noor = **Kollur**, not Golconda town.
#### 1.2 Textile Terms → Meaning
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Chintz** | **Glazed** textile; became **brighter after washing** |
| **Pintado** | **Printed** textile |
| Palampores, kalamkari | Masulipatnam textile names (painted and printed cotton) |
| **Odaberam** | **Sea-going ships** (mentioned in **Shuka Saptati**) |
### 2. MACHILIPATNAM (MASULIPATNAM)
#### 2.1 Ruler and Periods
| Period / ruler | Point |
|---|---|
| Ancient | Ptolemy's **Mysolia** / Maisolia – "great market" |
| **Qutb Shahis of Golconda** | **Principal maritime gateway**; customs revenue made Golconda one of the richest states |
| 1600s | Famous for **textile** and **diamond** trade |
| **Mughals** | **Aurangzeb** took Golconda in **1687**; Masulipatnam passed to the Mughals |
| Present | Lighthouse near **Manginapudi**, from **1984** |
- **Machilipatnam = most famous textile port** of the 16th-17th centuries (chintz/kalamkari); linked Andhra weavers to the European companies.
- English **calico bans (1700, 1721)** did not hurt Andhra – other markets stayed open.
#### 2.2 Merchant Communities
- **Komatis**: the key merchant community at Masulipatnam.
- Andhra weavers sold to all four European companies.
!KEY Machilipatnam was the Qutb Shahi port; Aurangzeb's capture of Golconda (1687) brought it under the Mughals.
### 3. SHIPBUILDING CENTRES
- **Narasapuram / Narsapur** (West Godavari, Godavari delta): **major shipbuilding centre** of the 16th-17th centuries; good timber and a safe river mouth.
- **Methold** saw large, strong ships built there under the Qutb Shahis.
- **Machilipatnam**: ship and boat building with Narasapuram.
- **Corangi**: **Company-era** shipbuilding site.
!TRAP The medieval shipbuilding centre on the Godavari = **Narasapuram**, not Machilipatnam alone.
### 4. EUROPEAN FACTORIES AT TRADE CENTRES
#### 4.1 Centre → Power → Year
| Centre | Power(s) | Year / note |
|---|---|---|
| **Masulipatnam** | Portuguese first to arrive; **Dutch** factory **1605**; **English 1611** | First English factory in South India |
| **Pulicat** | Dutch; English | **1610**; **1621** |
| **Nizampatnam** (Petapoli) | **Dutch and English** | Early 17th century |
| **Bheemunipatnam** | **Dutch → English** | Dutch subordinate factory; English from the late 17th century |
| **Palakollu** (Palicol) | **Dutch** | Godavari delta |
| **Madapollam** | **English** (not French) | In records from 1676 |
| **Vizagapatnam** | **English** | **1682** |
| **Yanam** | **French** | **1723** |
- **First English factory in South India = Masulipatam, 1611**.
!TRAP Madapollam = **English**; Yanam = **French (1723)**; Vizagapatnam = **English (1682)**.
### 5. IMPORTS INTO ANDHRA PORTS
| Import | Source / use |
|---|---|
| **Bullion** (gold, silver) | From **Europe** – financed English purchases of chintz, calico, muslin |
| **Horses** | **Ormuz / Persia** |
| **Elephants** | **Ceylon** (Simhala) |
| **Camphor** | **Punjab** |
| Golden sprouts | Jalanogi |
| Rice and grains | Brought back by coastal boats from **Bengal, Rangoon, Chittagong** |
- Company era: coastal boats carried **salt and groceries** to Yanam, Bengal, Rangoon and Chittagong and returned with **rice and grains**.
- Reddi-period merchant **Tippa** (Avachi Tippaya Setti) is credited with importing horses, elephants and camphor.
### 6. YANAM
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| European owner | **French** |
| Acquired | **1723** – only French territorial possession in Andhra |
| Merger with India | **Indian Union 1954**; **de jure** (in law) **1962** |
| Present status | Part of the **Union Territory of Puducherry**; an enclave in AP's Godavari delta |
!TRAP Yanam is **not** part of Andhra Pradesh; it belongs to **Puducherry UT**.
### 7. BHEEMUNIPATNAM (BHIMILI)
- Dutch **subordinate factory** → later an **English** factory.
- **Dutch cemetery**: tombs from **1661**, the oldest European tombs in the Northern Circars.
- A **Dutch fort** was allowed after Maratha troops plundered the town in **1754**.
- **St Peter's Church** – British-period church.
- **Bhimili = first municipality in Andhra (1861)**.
!KEY Bhimili: Dutch cemetery (1661) + first Andhra municipality (1861).
### 8. OTHER HISTORIC PORTS AND CENTRES
#### 8.1 Motupalli
- Also called **Desiyakondapattanam**; the **most important Kakatiya port** (APPSC 2011).
- **Satavahana** east-coast seaport; important **Ikshvaku** port with Ghantasala.
- **Marco Polo** visited it in **1287**, under **Rudrama Devi**, and described its busy trade.
- **Ganapatideva's Abhaya inscription** (Abhaya Sasanam) protected foreign traders.
- **Reddy** period: held by **Mallaya Reddy**; minister **Somaya** issued another Abhaya Sasana for trade. **Anavota Reddi** lifted customs duties; sea trade ran through Motupalli.
- Motupalli is the answer key for the APPSC "**handloom trade port**" PYQ.
#### 8.2 Ghantasala and Others
| Centre | Period | Point |
|---|---|---|
| **Ghantasala** (Kantakasela), Krishna district | Satavahana / Ikshvaku | Ptolemy's **Kantakossyla**; **Roman coins** found |
| **Koddura** | Satavahana | Ancient east-coast port with Ghantasala |
| **Penugonda** | Medieval | **Central trade centre of medieval Andhra** (APPSC 2012) |
| **Nagapattinam** | Dutch | **Dutch HQ in India from 1690** (APPSC 2005) |
| Kondapalli, Golconda, Narsapur, Madras | Post-European | Major trade centres |
| Visakhapatnam | 19th century | Port for agricultural products |
#### 8.3 Ptolemy's Names
| Ptolemy's name | Place |
|---|---|
| **Mysolia** | Machilipatnam |
| **Kantakossyla** | Ghantasala |
#### 8.4 Texts on Trade
- **Srinatha** dedicated **Haravilasam** to overseas trader **Avachi Tippaya Setti**: the **Reddi sea-trade text** (APPSC 2019).
- **Kashikhandam** is another Srinatha work, but **not** the sea-trade text.
- **Shuka Saptati** mentions odaberam ships; **Vedanta Rasayanam** by Subbarayudu.
!TRAP Reddi sea trade = **Haravilasam**, not Kashikhandam (APPSC 2019 key).
### 9. DECLINE AND PRESENT PORTS
- High Company-era duties ruined Andhra's domestic and foreign trade.
- Four new AP ports under construction: **Machilipatnam, Mulapeta, Ramayapatnam, Kakinada SEZ**.
- Proposed **Nizampatnam** (Guntur) and **Vodarevu** (Prakasam) ports under **PPP-BOOT** model – **on hold** during a **CBI investigation** (2024-25).
