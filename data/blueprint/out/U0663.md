## U0663 | ANDHRA PRADESH – SEX RATIO, LITERACY AND OCCUPATIONAL STRUCTURE
### 1. SEX RATIO – AP VS INDIA
#### 1.1 Census and Survey Figures
| Measure | AP | India |
|---|---|---|
| Overall sex ratio, Census 2001 | 983 | – |
| Overall sex ratio, Census **2011** | **997** | **943** |
| NFHS-5 (2019-21) total sex ratio | about 1,045 | – |
| Sex ratio at birth, NFHS-4 → NFHS-5 | **914 → 934** | **929** (NFHS-5) |
| Sex ratio at birth, CRS 2024 | – | **917** |
- AP's 2011 ratio (997) is **54 above** India's 943.
- Sex ratio = females per 1,000 males.
#### 1.2 Districts (13-district map, 2011)
| Measure | District |
|---|---|
| Highest sex ratio | **Vizianagaram** (about 1,016–1,019), then **Srikakulam** |
| Lowest sex ratio | **Anantapuramu** (977) |
- North coastal (Uttarandhra) districts have the best sex ratios; Rayalaseema the weakest.
#### 1.3 Son Preference
- **PCPNDT Act, 1994** (Pre-Conception and Pre-Natal Diagnostic Techniques) tackles **son preference** and sex-selective abortion.
- **13 Jun 2026**: the **Supreme Court** flagged persisting son preference under the PCPNDT Act.
!TRAP NFHS total sex ratio (above 1,000) and Census sex ratio (997) are different measures; sex ratio at birth (934) is lower still.
### 2. CHILD SEX RATIO (0–6)
- Child sex ratio falls behind overall sex ratio because of sex selection at birth.
- **2011 (undivided AP)**: child sex ratio fell to **939** from 961 (2001); still above India's 919.
- Highest district child sex ratio (2011): **East Godavari** (968).
!KEY Overall sex ratio rose (983 → 997) while child sex ratio fell – a classic statement trap.

### 6. OCCUPATIONAL STRUCTURE
#### 6.1 Main Workers in AP (2011)
- **Agricultural labourers = largest category** of AP main workers (**about 44.6%**), almost **three times cultivators**.
- Trend (undivided AP): agricultural labourers rose from **39.63% (2001) to about 43% (2011)**; cultivators fell from **22.52% to 16.47%** → farmers turning into labourers.
- Highest share of agricultural labourers: **West Godavari** (delta paddy district).
#### 6.2 Agriculture's Share of Workforce
| Measure | Figure |
|---|---|
| India (Census) | **58.2% (2001) → 54.6% (2011)** |
| AP latest (PLFS 2023-24) | **45.6%** of workers in agriculture |
!KEY Cultivators fall, agricultural labourers rise – the AP occupational shift across censuses.
### 7. WORK PARTICIPATION RATE
- Work participation rate (WPR) = workers as a share of total population.
- Undivided AP, 2011: overall WPR about **46.6%**.
- **Female** main-worker participation: **35.04%** (2011) up from **33.01%** (2001).
