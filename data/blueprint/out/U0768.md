## U0768 | ENVIRONMENTAL INSTITUTIONS – MoEFCC, CPCB AND SPCBs, NATIONAL GREEN TRIBUNAL, NATIONAL BIODIVERSITY AUTHORITY
### 1. MoEFCC – EVOLUTION
| Year | Step |
|---|---|
| **1980** | **Department of Environment** created |
| **1985** | **Ministry of Environment and Forests (MoEF)** (APPSC 2024 – correct statement) |
| **2014** | Renamed **MoEFCC** (Climate Change added) |
- Sequence: Dept of Environment 1980 → Air Act 1981 → MoEF 1985 → MoEFCC 2014.
- Bodies under it: CPCB (statutory), NBA, Central Zoo Authority, research institutes.
### 2. CENTRAL POLLUTION CONTROL BOARD (CPCB)
#### 2.1 Setup and Status
- Constituted **1974** under the **Water Act** as the "Central Board for the Prevention and Control of Water Pollution"; renamed **CPCB** by the **1988 amendment**.
- **Air functions added by the Air Act 1981**; EPA 1986 only widened its scope.
- **Statutory body** under **MoEFCC**; **oldest national pollution body**; chairman **nominated by the Central Government**.
- Role: **enforces environmental standards under the EPA framework** (APPSC G2 2025) – regulates, sets standards, monitors, advises the Centre.
#### 2.2 Functions
| Function | Detail |
|---|---|
| Coordination | Coordinates SPCBs; technical help, research, training |
| **NAMP** | National Air Monitoring Programme – 1,646 stations in 603 cities (March 2026); CAAQMS real-time |
| **National AQI** | Launched **2015**; daily AQI bulletins |
| Water | National Water Quality Monitoring; **RTWQMS** – real-time Ganga and Yamuna quality |
| **NCAP** | Coordinates with SPCBs; tracked on the **PRANA** portal |
- NGT told southern States and Puducherry to execute NCAP action plans; **86%** of spend went on road dust.
- Environmental (Protection) Fund Rules 2026 finance CPCB and SPCBs.
### 3. SPCBs AND APPCB
#### 3.1 SPCB Functions
- Inspect treatment plants, analyse samples, enforce standards, **grant consents**; SPCB chair nominated by the **State Government**.
- **Consent to Establish** – before setting up; **Consent to Operate** – before production or discharge (Water and Air Acts).
- Can levy **environmental compensation** under **S.33A Water Act / S.31A Air Act** where harm is clear or imminent (SC, Aug 2025).
#### 3.2 AP Pollution Control Board
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Set up | **1976** by the Government of AP under the **Water Act 1974** |
| Head office | **Vijayawada**; 3 Zonal and 13 Regional offices |
| Role | Body entrusted to **implement environmental laws and rules in AP** (APPSC 2025) |
| Laws enforced | Water, Air, EP Acts + hazardous, bio-medical, plastic, solid waste rules |
| Lab instruments | Davis Instruments Airlinks is **NOT** among its scientific division machines |
### 4. NATIONAL GREEN TRIBUNAL – ESTABLISHMENT AND NATURE
| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| NGT Act passed | **2 June 2010** |
| Tribunal set up | **18 October 2010** (APPSC 2018) |
| Started functioning | **July 2011** |
- **Statutory (not constitutional)** body; constitutional anchor **Article 21** (right to a healthy environment), not Article 28.
- **Purpose**: effective and expeditious disposal of cases on environmental protection and conservation of forests and natural resources; enforcing environmental rights; relief and compensation – i.e. **environmental and pollution-control cases** (APPSC G2 2025). Reduces litigation burden on higher courts.
- **Procedure**: **not bound by CPC 1908**; guided by **natural justice**; powers of a civil court.
- **Appeal** to Supreme Court within **90 days**; disposal target **6 months**.
#### 4.1 Composition and Persons
- Chairperson + **Judicial and Expert Members (10 to 20 each)**.
- Chairperson: a judge of the SC or Chief Justice of a High Court (retired SC judge in practice).
- **First Chairperson: Justice Lokeshwar Singh Panta**; current: **Justice Prakash Shrivastava** (Sep 2026).
#### 4.2 Benches
| Bench | Location |
|---|---|
| **Principal** | **New Delhi** (headquarters) |
| Zonal | **Pune, Kolkata, Bhopal, Chennai** |
| Circuit | **Shimla, Shillong, Jodhpur** |
!TRAP Zonal bench is at Pune, not Mumbai; Jodhpur and Shillong are circuit benches.
### 5. NGT JURISDICTION, PREDECESSORS AND ORDERS
#### 5.1 Acts under NGT
| Under NGT (7 – Schedule I) | Outside NGT |
|---|---|
| Water Act 1974; Water Cess Act 1977; Forest (Conservation) Act 1980; Air Act 1981; Environment (Protection) Act 1986; Public Liability Insurance Act 1991; Biological Diversity Act 2002 | **Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972**; **Forest Rights Act 2006** |
!KEY Act NOT under NGT: Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972.
#### 5.2 NGT vs CPCB
| NGT | CPCB |
|---|---|
| Judicial tribunal; NGT Act 2010 | Statutory regulator; Water Act 1974 |
| Adjudicates disputes, awards compensation | Sets standards, monitors, advises Centre |
#### 5.3 Predecessors
| Law | Purpose | Fate |
|---|---|---|
| **National Environment Tribunal Act, 1995** (APPSC 2023) | Strict liability for **hazardous-substance accidents** | **Never brought into force**; repealed by NGT Act 2010 |
| **National Environment Appellate Authority Act, 1997** | **Appeals against environmental clearances** | NEAA replaced by NGT; Act repealed 2010 |
- NGT **combines original jurisdiction** over disputes with NEAA's **appellate role**.
#### 5.4 Orders
- **Fly Ash Management and Utilization Mission** constituted by NGT (**January 2022**).
- CGWA case: NGT fined 12 stadiums **Rs 5,000 each** for not giving water-use data (Feb 2026).
- 2026 cases: Dhanauri wetland, Ganga pollution, Saparnika river.

### 7. NATIONAL BIODIVERSITY AUTHORITY
- **Autonomous statutory body** under MoEFCC, est. **2003** under the **Biological Diversity Act 2002**; HQ **Chennai** (not Bengaluru).
- Act amended **2023**; BD Rules **2024**.
- **Three tiers**: **NBA** (national) → **State Biodiversity Boards** (state) → **Biodiversity Management Committees** (local).
- **Against biopiracy**: foreigners need **NBA approval** to access bio-resources or traditional knowledge; NBA approval **precedes IPR**; access and benefit-sharing with local communities.
- NBA handles biodiversity governance, **not NCAP**.
- **AP State Biodiversity Board**: City Biodiversity Index; **biodiversity parks on flood plains of 5 polluted rivers** (NGT/MoEFCC direction).
