## U0349 | LOKPAL AND LOKAYUKTAS ACT 2013 – STRUCTURE, JURISDICTION, WORKING; CENTRAL VIGILANCE COMMISSION
### 1. OMBUDSMAN IDEA AND RECOMMENDING BODIES
#### 1.1 Origin and Term
- **Ombudsman** was first set up in **Sweden in 1809**.
- Indian versions: **Lokpal** (Union) and **Lokayukta** (State).
- The term **"Lokpal"** was coined by **Dr. L.M. Singhvi** in **1963**.
!TRAP Sweden 1809 = Ombudsman; Sweden 1766 = right to information law. Do not mix them.
#### 1.2 Committees and Commissions
| Body | Chair | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Santhanam Committee** (1962-64) | K. Santhanam | **CVC and CBI**; not the Lokpal |
| **First ARC** (1966-70) | **Morarji Desai** (later K. Hanumanthaiya) | **Two-tier**: Lokpal (Union) + Lokayukta (States), 1966 |
| **Second ARC** (2007) | – | Reiterated the Lokpal |
- First ARC report "**Problems of Redressal of Citizens' Grievances**" (1966) **first recommended state Lokayuktas**.
!PAIR Santhanam Committee | CVC + CBI | 1962-64 || First ARC | Lokpal + Lokayukta | 1966
### 2. LOKPAL BILL HISTORY AND THE 2013 ACT
#### 2.1 Bills
- **First Lokpal Bill: 1968**, introduced in the **Lok Sabha** (4th Lok Sabha); **passed by the Lok Sabha in 1969**, but **lapsed** when that House was dissolved before the Rajya Sabha passed it.
- Re-introduced in **1971, 1977, 1985, 1989, 1996, 1998, 2001, 2005, 2008** – every one lapsed.
| Stage (Lokpal Bill 2011) | Date |
|---|---|
| Introduced in the Lok Sabha by **V. Narayanasamy**, Minister of Parliamentary Affairs | **4 August 2011** |
| Sent to the Standing Committee on Public Grievances, Law and Justice (chair **Abhishek Manu Singhvi**); civil society called it "weak" | late 2011 |
| Passed by the Lok Sabha; Rajya Sabha sent it to a Select Committee | 27 December 2011 |
#### 2.2 The Act – Dates
| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Passed by the **Rajya Sabha** (with amendments) | 17 December 2013 |
| Passed by the **Lok Sabha** | 18 December 2013 |
| **President's assent** | **1 January 2014** |
| **Came into force** | **16 January 2014** |
| Lokpal began functioning | **27 March 2019** |
- **Statutory** body, not constitutional.
- Also amended the **DSPE Act** (CBI Director committee; Directorate of Prosecution).
!TRAP 1 January 2014 = assent; 16 January 2014 = in force (APPSC 2019 and G2 Mains 2025 key).
#### 2.3 Amendment Act 2016 – Asset Declaration
- Original Act: public servant declares assets and liabilities of self, spouse and dependent children within **30 days** of entering office.
- **2016 amendment**: declaration stays, but its **form and manner** are prescribed by the **Central Government**.
### 3. CIVIL SOCIETY MOVEMENT (2011)
- **India Against Corruption (IAC)**, led by **Anna Hazare**; fasts at **Jantar Mantar (April 2011)** and **Ramlila Maidan (August 2011)** pushed the **Jan Lokpal Bill**.
- **Joint Drafting Committee (April 2011)**: chair **Pranab Mukherjee**; co-chair **Shanti Bhushan**.
- Civil society members: **Anna Hazare, Arvind Kejriwal, Shanti Bhushan, Justice N. Santosh Hegde, Prashant Bhushan**.
### 4. LOKPAL – COMPOSITION AND SELECTION
#### 4.1 Composition
- **Chairperson + not more than 8 members**; minimum age **45**.
- **50% judicial** members (former SC judges or former HC Chief Justices) **and** at least **50%** from **SC, ST, OBC, minorities and women** – both rules apply together.
- Chairperson: former **CJI**, a sitting or former **SC judge**, or an **eminent person** with **25 years'** experience (anti-corruption, public administration, vigilance, finance, law, management).
- Term: **5 years or age 70**, whichever earlier; **no reappointment**; a member may become Chairperson within a 5-year total.
- Cannot be an **MP or MLA**.
#### 4.2 Selection Committee (5) and Search Committee
| Member | Role |
|---|---|
| **Prime Minister** | Chair |
| Speaker of the Lok Sabha | Member |
| Leader of the Opposition in the Lok Sabha | Member |
| CJI or an SC judge nominated by him | Member |
| Eminent jurist (nominated by the President on the others' recommendation) | Member |
- Appointing authority: the **President**, on the Selection Committee's recommendation.
- **Search Committee**: set up by the Selection Committee; **at least 7 persons**, at least half from SC, ST, OBC, minorities and women.
!TRAP The **Chairman of the Rajya Sabha is NOT a member** of the selection committee (UKPSC 2025).
### 5. LOKPAL – JURISDICTION, POWERS AND TIME LIMITS
#### 5.1 Who is Covered
| Covered | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Prime Minister** | Except international relations, external and internal security, public order, atomic energy, space; inquiry only if the **full bench** approves by **two-thirds**, held **in camera** |
| Union Ministers, MPs | Yes – but **not** for anything said or any vote in Parliament |
| Central officials | **Groups A, B, C and D**; C and D complaints go to the **CVC**, which reports back to the Lokpal |
| NGOs, trusts, societies | Foreign contribution above **₹10 lakh** |
| Public servants | **In and outside India** (posted abroad also covered) |
- Complaint bar: no inquiry into a complaint made after **7 years** from the alleged offence.
#### 5.2 Powers and Wings
| Power | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Inquiry Wing (Section 11)** | Headed by a **Director of Inquiry**; preliminary inquiry into PCA offences |
| **Prosecution Wing** | Headed by a **Director of Prosecution**; files cases before the Special Court |
| CBI | **Superintendence and direction** over the CBI only in **cases referred** by the Lokpal |
| Property | **Attach and confiscate** property got by corruption, even while prosecution is pending |
#### 5.3 Time Limits
| Stage | Limit |
|---|---|
| Preliminary inquiry | **90 days + 90 days** (reasons in writing) |
| Inquiry Wing report after reference | **60 days** |
| Investigation | **6 months + 6 months** |
| Special Court trial | **1 year**, extendable up to **2 years** |
### 6. LOKPAL IN OFFICE – PEOPLE AND WORKING
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| **First Lokpal** | **Justice Pinaki Chandra Ghose**, retired SC judge; appointed **19 March 2019**; oath **23 March 2019** by President **Ram Nath Kovind** |
| Started work | 27 March 2019 |
| Left office | 27 May 2022; post vacant about two years |
| **Present Chairperson** | **Justice A.M. (Ajay Manikrao) Khanwilkar**, since **10 March 2024** |
- Bench (Jun 2026): 6 of 8 posts filled; judicial member **Ritu Raj Awasthi**; non-judicial **Sushil Chandra** (former CEC).
- Complaints: **292 registered, 260 disposed of in 2024-25** (Lok Sabha reply, March 2026).
- Criticism: first Lokpal only in 2019; 2022-24 vacancy; wings **not fully staffed** (23 Mar 2026); dependence on the CBI.
- Delhi HC set aside the Lokpal's 12 Nov 2025 sanction order in the **Mahua Moitra** case.
- **22 Jan 2026**: SC **split verdict on Section 17A, PC Act** (prior approval before probe): **Justice B.V. Nagarathna** – unconstitutional; **Justice K.V. Viswanathan** – valid only if based on a binding Lokpal/Lokayukta opinion. CJI **Surya Kant** and Khanwilkar were not part of it.
!TRAP Section 17A belongs to the **Prevention of Corruption Act**, not the Lokpal Act; the Inquiry Wing is **Section 11** of the Lokpal Act.
!TRAP First Lokpal Bill (1968) and first Lokpal (2019) are both true but unrelated.
### 7. CENTRAL VIGILANCE COMMISSION
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Origin | **Santhanam Committee**; set up **1964** by executive resolution |
| Statutory status | **Vineet Narain case (1997)** directed it; **CVC Act, 2003** (assent 11 Sep 2003) |
| Nature | **Statutory**, multi-member; not constitutional |
| Composition | **Central Vigilance Commissioner** (chair) + **not more than 2 Vigilance Commissioners** |
| Term | 4 years or age 65 |
| Selection committee (Sec 4) | **PM** (chair), **Home Minister**, **Leader of the Opposition** in Lok Sabha; appointment by the **President** |
| Over CBI | Superintendence over CBI in **PCA cases generally** |
!PAIR CVC | Supervises CBI in all PCA cases | CVC Act 2003 || Lokpal | Supervises CBI only in cases it refers | Lokpal Act 2013
### 8. WHISTLE BLOWERS PROTECTION ACT 2014
- Act No. **17 of 2014**; President's assent **9 May 2014**.
- A **whistle blower** makes a **public interest disclosure** about corruption or wilful misuse of power by a public servant.
- **Competent authority** for central employees: the **CVC**.
- Protections: identity of the complainant protected; **anonymous complaints not taken up**; complaints barred after **7 years**; appeal to the **High Court within 60 days**.
### 9. STATE LOKAYUKTAS
#### 9.1 FIRSTs and Chronology
| State | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Odisha** | **FIRST to pass a law (1970)**; institution only in **1983** |
| **Maharashtra** | **FIRST to set up a Lokayukta** – Lokayukta and Upa-Lokayuktas Act, **1971**; came into force October 1972 |
| Bihar | 1973 |
| Rajasthan | 1973 |
| Uttar Pradesh | 1975 |
| Madhya Pradesh | 1982 |
| **Andhra Pradesh** | **1983** |
| Karnataka | 1984 |
| Assam | 1986 |
| **Uttarakhand** | First to pass the Lokayukta Bill in 2011 (1 November 2011) |
- Chronology: Sweden (1809) → first Lokpal Bill (1968) → Maharashtra Lokayukta (1971) → first Lokpal (2019).
- By 2013: **21 States and 1 UT (Delhi)** had Lokayuktas.
- **Mandate**: every State to set up a Lokayukta **within one year** of the Act's commencement, by its own law (**Section 63**).
!TRAP One PYQ key gives **Orissa (1971)** as first State to create a Lokayukta; Odisha 1970 = law, Maharashtra 1971 = institution.
#### 9.2 Structure, Appointment, Tenure
| Structure | States |
|---|---|
| Lokayukta + Upa-Lokayukta | Rajasthan, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra |
| Lokayukta only | Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh |
| Officials designated "Lokpal" | Punjab, Odisha |
- Appointed by the **Governor**, after consulting the **Chief Justice of the High Court** and the **Leader of the Opposition** (not the CEC).
- Yearly **consolidated report to the Governor**; recommendations **advisory** in many States, **binding** in some.
- **Rajasthan** cut tenure from **8 to 5 years** (APPSC G1 2018); **Narpat Mal Lodha committee** (2014, Rajasthan Advocate General) reviewed its Act.
#### 9.3 Coverage of CM, Ministers, MLAs
| Office | Included | Excluded |
|---|---|---|
| **Chief Minister** | HP, AP, **MP**, Gujarat, **Odisha** (Odisha Lokayukta Act 2014, Section 14) | Maharashtra, UP, Rajasthan, Bihar |
| MLAs | AP, HP, Gujarat, UP, Assam | – |
- Weakness: no uniform law; many lack an investigating wing and rely on **State police / ACB**.
#### 9.4 Karnataka Model
- **Karnataka Lokayukta Act, 1984**; own **Lokayukta Police** wing.
- **Justice N. Santosh Hegde** – **Bellary illegal iron ore mining report (2011)**; led to arrest of former minister **G. Janardhana Reddy**.
### 10. AP LOKAYUKTA
- Law: **AP Lokayukta and Upa-Lokayukta Act, 1983**; amended **2024**.
- **Lokayukta** for higher functionaries; **Upa-Lokayukta** for the rest; covers the **CM and MLAs**.
- Features: **informal procedure, easy access, almost free and prompt, independent of the executive**.
- **FIRST Lokayukta of integrated AP: Justice A. Sambasiva Rao** (APPSC 2018).
- **Aug 2026**: Justice (Retd) **T. Mallikarjuna Rao** appointed by Governor **S. Abdul Nazeer** under **Section 3(1)** on the selection committee's recommendation; **5-year term** (Section 5); G.O. MS No.157 (20 Aug 2026). The post was vacant most of 2025; Upa-Lokayukta **P. Rajani** held charge.
- **Seat**: after bifurcation it worked from Hyderabad; shifted to **Kurnool** in **2021**, now its **principal seat**.
- Annual report 2025: **7,431 cases** handled (31 Mar 2026).
!TRAP AP Lokayukta is **not an interpreter of the Constitution** (APPSC 2019).
| Body | Role in AP |
|---|---|
| Lokayukta / Upa-Lokayukta | Statutory ombudsman (1983 Act); takes complaints, inquires, recommends; reports to the Governor |
| **State Vigilance Commission** | From **1964**, on the **CVC** pattern; advises on vigilance and disciplinary matters |
| **Anti-Corruption Bureau** | Police agency for **PCA** offences (trap and disproportionate-assets cases) |
