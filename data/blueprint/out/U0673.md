#### 1.3 Dominant Crop and Shifts
- **Paddy dominates**: about **56%** of foodgrain area gives about **79%** of foodgrain output.
- **Diversification**: towards oilseeds, millets, horticulture and **oil palm**; from food grains to fruits, vegetables and spices.
- **Coarse cereals shrinking** in AP due to lack of **MSP procurement**.
- AP strategy: **30% agricultural growth in five years** through **17 growth engines**, which raise productivity of **11 crops**: paddy, maize, jowar, bajra, ragi, redgram, blackgram, bengalgram, groundnut, sunflower, sesamum (coffee NOT included).
- **Kharif 2026** (El Nino): AP rainfall 51% short (1 June–25 Aug 2026), so a **water-budgeted shift to pulses** (28 Aug 2026); 48.3% deficit by 15 July 2026, 24 of 28 districts deficient, Rayalaseema most vulnerable.

### 5. IRRIGATION PROFILE AND SOURCES
#### 5.1 Project Classes by Culturable Command Area (CCA)
| Class | CCA |
|---|---|
| Minor | Up to **2,000 ha** (AP: ayacut up to **5,000 acres**) |
| Medium | 2,000–10,000 ha |
| Major | Above **10,000 ha** |
- **Maximum irrigation potential** in India comes from **minor projects** (about 65% of irrigated area).
#### 5.2 Sources
- **AP main source = canals**; **India main source = tube wells**.
- AP 2023-24: gross irrigated **32.71 lakh ha**, net irrigated **26.03 lakh ha** (−8.86%; monsoon deficit 718.2 mm vs 860 mm normal).
- India 2021-22: net irrigated 77.9 MH; tube wells 47.3%, canals 24.7%, tanks 2.8%.
- India 2022-23: net irrigated **79,312 thousand ha**; tube wells **49.34%**, canals 22.85%, other wells 13.46%, tanks 2.82%, others 11.53%.
- **Micro-irrigation** (APMIP, since 2003): 2.05 lakh acres; 75,035 farmers; ₹793.67 crore subsidy (2024-25).
!TRAP Canals lead only in AP; in India tube wells lead.

### 9. FISHERIES – RANK, PRODUCTION AND EXPORTS
#### 9.1 AP FIRSTs and Share
- AP ranks **first in India in fish and shrimp production** (long coastline, large aquaculture).
- **Highest inland fish landings** in India in **2019-20**.
- Share of India's fish: about **29%** (2025-26); earlier about 30% (2024-25).
- Coastline **1,053 km**; **3rd longest** after the 2023-24 remeasurement, behind Gujarat and Tamil Nadu (earlier 974 km, 2nd) (May 2026).
#### 9.2 Production
| Year | Target | Production |
|---|---|---|
| 2022-23 | – | 51.06 LMT |
| 2023-24 | 52.55 LMT | 51.58 LMT |
| 2024-25 | 56.62 LMT | **55.39 LMT** |
| 2025-26 | 66.62 LMT | 47.28 LMT up to Dec 2025 (72%) |
#### 9.3 Exports
- AP = about **32%** of India's seafood exports; topped export value among States in 2024-25 (~US$ 2.53 bn).
- **Shrimp** = about **70%** of AP's export basket; **Vannamei (white-leg) shrimp** = main export item.
- India 2024-25: 16,98,170 MT seafood worth ₹62,408.45 crore (US$ 7.45 bn).
- US tariff on Indian seafood 50% from Aug 2025, cut to ~18% in Feb 2026; non-US markets took ~57% of AP shrimp.
!NUM ~29% = AP share of fish production; ~32% = AP share of seafood exports; ~70% = shrimp in AP's export basket
#### 9.4 National Frame
- India = **2nd largest fish producer** (about 8%; some estimates 9%, FAO SOFIA 2026); aquaculture 2nd (~12%); **world leader in inland capture** (2.2 MT).
- Inland fisheries give over **75%** of India's fish output.
- SOFIA 2026: aquaculture passed 100 million tonnes = 53% of aquatic animal output (22 Jun 2026).
### 10. INLAND FISHERIES, AQUACULTURE DISTRICTS AND KOLLERU
#### 10.1 Inland Fisheries
- Fish seed stocking; **reservoir and tank fisheries**; departmental tanks **leased to fishermen cooperative societies**.
- Registered aquaculture area 2.89 lakh acres; 2025-26 target +1,00,198 acres.
| Water | Species |
|---|---|
| Freshwater | **GIFT tilapia**, amur carp, Jayanti rohu, GI scampi, murrels |
| Brackish / marine | Cobia, lobster, mussel |
- **Black tiger shrimp** (P. monodon): brood multiplication at MPEDA's centre, Visakhapatnam.
#### 10.2 Leading Aquaculture Districts
- Shrimp backbone: **Prakasam, West Godavari, East Godavari, Nellore, Krishna**.
- West Godavari: 9.64 LMT fish and shrimp worth ₹24,345 crore (2025-26).
#### 10.3 Kolleru Lake
| Feature | Fact |
|---|---|
| Location | Between **Krishna and Godavari deltas** (Eluru / West Godavari) |
| Size | **Largest freshwater lake of AP**, about **305 sq km** |
| Feeder | **Tammileru** among others |
| Status | **Ramsar site since 2002** |
| Birds | Breeding ground of **spot-billed pelican**; painted storks |
| Sanctuary limit | **+5 ft contour** |
- **Encroachment**: lake bed turned into aquaculture ponds and fields → shrinkage and **eutrophication**.

#### 11.3 Regulation
- **Fishing ban (East Coast)**: **61 days, 15 April–14 June**; mechanised and motorised boats banned in territorial waters; **traditional non-motorised craft exempt**.
- Penalties: **AP Marine Fishing Regulation Act, 1995**; amendment planned against IUU fishing up to 12 nautical miles (24 Apr 2026).
- **AP State Aquaculture Development Authority Act, 2020** (Act 29 of 2020): sets up **APSADA**, which licenses aquaculture and aquaculture business; aquaculture zones in all **9 coastal districts**.
- **WTO Agreement on Fisheries Subsidies**: adopted MC12 (2022), in force Sept 2025; India **123rd** member to accept; aquaculture and inland fishing exempt.
