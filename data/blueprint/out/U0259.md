## U0259 | JOURNALISTS AND EDITORS OF THE ANDHRA MOVEMENT – MUTNURI KRISHNA RAO, KASINATHUNI NAGESWARA RAO
### 1. PRESS AND THE ANDHRA AWAKENING
- Editors made **journalism a tool of political awakening**; the same papers also funded and publicised the **library movement**.
- Most of them were also founders of libraries, publishing houses and samithis.

### 3. MUTNURI KRISHNA RAO
- Editor of **Krishna Patrika** from **1907 to 1945** (died 25 June 1945); called the **doyen of Telugu journalism**.
- Made Krishna Patrika a **political weekly** and a **hub for freedom fighters**.
- Wrote scholarly editorials attacking British government spending; also ran the magazine **Andhra Bharati** for some time.
- Invited extremist leader **Bipin Chandra Pal** to Andhra (**April 1907**).
!KEY APPSC GS 2024: Mutnuri was "largely responsible" for the success of Krishna Patrika.
### 4. KASINATHUNI NAGESWARA RAO
#### 4.1 Life and Businesses
- Lived **1867–1947**.
- Founded the **Amrutanjan** business; used the wealth for public work (Gandhian **trusteeship**).
- Publishing house **Andhra Granthamala**, named to popularise the word "**Andhra**".
- Papers: **Andhra Patrika (1908)**, **Bharati (1924)**.
#### 4.2 Library Movement Role
- Any group of **ten youths** opening a village library got his newspaper, books and *Bharati* **free**.
- Finished and published the **catalogue of printed Telugu books** begun by Velidanda Hanumantha Rao.
- **Granthalayodyama Trimurthulu**: Kasinathuni + **Ayyanki** (Venkata Ramanaiah) + **Gadicherla**.

### 5. GADICHERLA HARISARVOTTAMA RAO
- Editor of **Swarajya (1907)**.
- **Jailed for sedition in 1908** for the article "**Veerabali**"; the **first Telugu journalist / political prisoner** so jailed in South India.
- **Blacklisted** from government jobs after the **1907 Rajahmundry College incident**.
- With **Kaderbad Narasinga Rao**, founded the **Edward Corporation Library, Nandyal** (now **Victoria Reading Room**).
!TRAP Edward Corporation Library is at Nandyal, not Kurnool.
