## U0631 | INDIA – TIGER RESERVES, ELEPHANT RESERVES, RAMSAR SITES, IMPORTANT BIRD AREAS
### 1. RAMSAR CONVENTION
#### 1.1 The Convention
- Adopted in **1971** at **Ramsar, Iran**; purpose: **conservation and wise use of wetlands**. India joined in **1982**.
- **World Wetlands Day: 2 February** (marks the adoption of the Convention).
- Other days (distractors): 22 March World Water Day; 22 April Earth Day; 5 June World Environment Day.
#### 1.2 Wetland Definition
- Marsh, fen, peatland or water; **natural or artificial**; fresh, brackish or salt; includes **marine water up to 6 m deep at low tide**.
#### 1.3 Montreux Record
- Register of Ramsar sites where **changes in ecological character** have occurred, are occurring or are likely to occur (technology, pollution, other human interference).
- India's entries: **Keoladeo** and **Loktak**.
#### 1.4 Court Ruling
- Supreme Court: Ramsar status needs heightened scrutiny; **no mining within 10 km** of a Ramsar site without approval of **NBWL** (National Board for Wildlife) and **MoEFCC**; extended in 2026 to all wetland conservation reserves.
!KEY Montreux Record = Keoladeo + Loktak.
### 2. INDIA'S RAMSAR SITES – COUNT AND FIRSTS
#### 2.1 Count Milestones
- **India's count: 101 Ramsar sites (Aug 2026)** – most in Asia.
- **2022**: **28** new Indian wetlands declared Ramsar sites.
- Early/Apr 2026: 98; **99th – Shekha Jheel BS, UP** (Apr 2026).
- **5 Jun 2026: 100th – Surha Tal (JP Narayan Bird Sanctuary), Ballia, UP.**
- **3 Aug 2026: 101st – Glaw Lake, Arunachal Pradesh** – Arunachal's first; inside **Kamlang Tiger Reserve**; home to the CR **white-bellied heron**.

### 3. TIGER RESERVES
#### 3.1 Project Tiger and NTCA
- **Project Tiger** launched **1 April 1973** with **9 reserves**; formally launched at **Corbett** – **first tiger reserve (1973)**.
- **NTCA** (National Tiger Conservation Authority) set up in **2006** under the amended Wildlife (Protection) Act – a **statutory body**.
- **M-STrIPES**: app-based patrolling and monitoring tool used in tiger reserves.
- **All India Tiger Estimation 2022**: average **3,682 tigers** (2006 baseline: 1,411).
- Growing share of tigers live **outside** notified reserves → more human–tiger conflict.

### 4. ELEPHANT RESERVES
- **Project Elephant: February 1992**; **33 elephant reserves in 14 States**.
- ERs **overlap** tiger reserves, sanctuaries and reserved forests (not limited to areas outside TRs).
- **Most ERs: Assam and Tamil Nadu (5 each)**; Odisha has 3 – Mayurbhanj, Mahanadi, Sambalpur.
- DNA-based census (Oct 2025): **22,446** wild elephants; Karnataka has the largest population.
### 5. IMPORTANT BIRD AREAS AND BIRD DEATHS
- **IBAs**: named by **BirdLife International**; Indian partner **BNHS** (Bombay Natural History Society).
- **Telineelapuram and Telukunchi** (Srikakulam, AP): IBAs for breeding **painted storks** and **spot-billed pelicans**.
- **Rushikulya mouth** (Odisha): IBA hosting **olive ridley arribada**.
- **Sambhar Lake** (Rajasthan), **Nov 2019**: mass death of migratory birds from **avian botulism**.

### 8. MIGRATORY BIRDS AND FLYWAYS
- **Central Asian Flyway**: Arctic/Siberia → Indian Ocean; India = key wintering ground. India hosted **CMS COP13** at **Gandhinagar (2020)**.
- **Bar-headed goose**: breeds on high Central Asian lakes; **crosses the Himalaya** to winter in India; one of the **highest-flying** birds.
- **Amur falcon**: **longest raptor migration**; SE Siberia/N China → stopover in **Nagaland** (Doyang, Pangti; Oct–Nov) → winters in **southern Africa**.
