## U0598 | VOLCANOES – TYPES, MATERIALS, LANDFORMS, GLOBAL DISTRIBUTION
### 1. BASIC TERMS
- **Magma**: molten and semi-molten rock **below** the surface (about **600°C–1200°C**), held in a **magma chamber**; magma causes volcanism.
- **Lava**: magma once it **reaches the surface** through the **vent**; dominated by silica.
- **Volcano**: outlet that gives **lava, ash and gases**.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Crater | **Funnel-shaped** mouth at the top of a cone |
| Caldera | **Pot (cauldron)-shaped** hollow: magma chamber empties after a big eruption → summit collapses |
!TRAP Below surface = magma; at surface = lava.

| Instrument | Measures |
|---|---|

| **Creepmeter** | Fault slip |

### 2. VOLCANIC MATERIALS
#### 2.1 Gases
- Commonest gas: **water vapour**, then **CO₂**, then **SO₂**.
- SO₂ → aerosols scatter sunlight, **cool Earth for years**; ash falls back within weeks.
#### 2.2 Lava Types
| Acidic (felsic) lava | Basic (mafic) lava |
|---|---|
| **Silica-rich, viscous** | Silica-poor, **fluid**, flows far |
| **Explosive** eruptions | Quiet outpourings |
| Steep cones, stratovolcanoes (andesitic) | **Shield** volcanoes, **lava plateaus** (basalt) |
#### 2.3 Pyroclasts
- **Ash, lapilli, volcanic bombs**; **pyroclastic flows** and **lahars** (volcanic mudflows) come from stratovolcanoes.
### 3. TYPES OF VOLCANOES
#### 3.1 By Activity
| Type | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Active | Erupting in recent times | Barren Island, **Stromboli**, Etna |
| Dormant | Quiet for long but **may erupt** again | Narcondam |
| Extinct | **No eruption expected** | Dhinodhar |
#### 3.2 By Form
| Type | Form | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Shield** | Broad, gentle slopes of fluid basalt | **Mauna Loa** (Hawaii), **Hayli Gubbi** (Ethiopia) |
| **Composite / strato** | Steep cone of **alternating lava and ash** layers; explosive | **Fuji**, Vesuvius, Mount St. Helens, Fuego, Taftan |
| Cinder cone | Small, steep cone of loose ash, cinders and scoria around one vent | Cerro Negro (Nicaragua) |
| Complex | Several vents, overlapping cones | Taal, Dukono |
- **Hayli Gubbi** erupted **November 2025** after ~**10,000–12,000 years** dormant.
### 4. GLOBAL DISTRIBUTION
| Belt | Volcanoes |
|---|---|
| **Pacific Ring of Fire** | **Most eruptions and earthquakes** – Fuji, Mount St. Helens, Fuego, Mayon, Dukono, Popocatepetl, Krakatau, Tungurahua, Whakaari |
| Mid-Atlantic belt | **Iceland – Hekla** |
| **Mediterranean** belt | **Vesuvius, Etna, Stromboli** |
| Intraplate hotspots | **Hawaii** (Mauna Loa) |
| East African Rift | **Kilimanjaro**, Afar triple junction |
!TRAP Stromboli = Mediterranean belt, NOT East African Rift.

### 6. INTRUSIVE LANDFORMS
| Form | Shape / position |
|---|---|
| **Batholith** | Largest, deep dome-like mass; exposed only after erosion |
| **Laccolith** | Dome-shaped body fed by a pipe-like conduit from below |
| **Lopolith** | Saucer-shaped, sagging in the centre |
| **Phacolith** | Wavy mass at the base of synclines or crest of anticlines |
| **Sill / sheet** | Near-horizontal sheet along rock layers (thin = sheet, thick = sill) |
| **Dyke** | Near-vertical wall cutting across layers; feeders of the **Deccan Traps** (western Maharashtra) |

### 8. VOLCANOES IN INDIA
| Feature | Location | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Barren Island** | Andaman Sea | **India's only active volcano**; mild eruptions 13 and 20 Sep 2025, explosive activity 2 Oct 2025 |
| **Narcondam** | Andaman Sea | **Dormant**; wildlife sanctuary, endemic Narcondam Hornbill |
| **Baratang** | Middle Andaman | **Mud volcanoes** (and limestone caves); erupted 2 Oct 2025 |
| Diglipur | North Andaman | Fresh mud volcano: mud, water, methane |
| **Dhinodhar** | Kutch, Gujarat | **Extinct** volcanic hill |
| **Deccan Traps** | Maharashtra Plateau; AP – Prakasam, Palnadu, Guntur, Rajahmundry traps (Godavari) | **Fissure flood basalt**, end of Cretaceous (~65 million years); **Reunion hotspot** |
- Mud volcanoes erupt mud, water and gases (methane, CO₂, nitrogen), **not lava**; cool and shallow; driven by tectonic pressure on the **Andaman Arc subduction zone**.
- **AP**: Deccan Trap basalts of Prakasam, Palnadu, Guntur → black cotton soil.
!KEY APPSC 2021 key: mud volcanoes = **Baratang**; Barren Island (distractor) = magmatic active volcano.
