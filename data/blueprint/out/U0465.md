## U0465 | INDIA'S ENVIRONMENTAL POLICY FRAMEWORK – NEP, EIA, POLLUTER PAYS AND PRECAUTIONARY PRINCIPLES
### 1. ENVIRONMENTAL PRINCIPLES
| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Precautionary** | Act/stop the activity even **without full scientific certainty**; **burden of proof** on the actor to show it is harmless (APPSC 2024, 2025) |
| **Polluter Pays** | Polluter bears cost of **remediation + compensation** (APPSC 2025) |
| **Public trust** | State holds natural resources **in trust for the public** |
| **Absolute liability** | Hazardous industries – **no exceptions** |
### 2. CASES -> DOCTRINE
| Case | Year | Doctrine |
|---|---|---|
| **Ratlam Municipality** | 1980 | Municipal duty on **public nuisance**; health, sanitation |
| **M.C. Mehta (Oleum gas, Delhi)** | **1986** | **Absolute liability** |
| Subhash Kumar v Bihar | 1991 | Pollution-free water and air in **Art. 21** |
| **T.N. Godavarman** | 1995 | "Forest" regardless of ownership |
| **Vellore Citizens** | 1996 | **Precautionary + Polluter Pays**; tanneries, **Palar** (TN) |
| **Bichhri** | 1996 | **Polluter Pays**; Rajasthan chemical units |
| **M.C. Mehta v Kamal Nath** | 1997 | **Public trust** |
| **Taj Trapezium** | 1997 | Mandated **CNG** |
!TRAP Vellore = both principles; Bichhri = Polluter Pays only; Kamal Nath = public trust; Oleum gas = absolute liability.
### 3. CONSTITUTIONAL PROVISIONS
- **Article 48A** (DPSP) = duty of the **State**; **Article 51A(g)** (FD) = duty of the **citizen**.
- **Article 21** read to include a clean environment (Subhash Kumar, 1991).
### 4. ACTS AND BODIES
| Act / body | Year | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Wildlife (Protection) Act** | 1972 | Wildlife, habitats; regulates hunting |
| **Water Act; CPCB** | **1974** | CPCB formed under Water Act; later Air Act too |
| Forest (Conservation) Act | 1980 | Forests |
| **Air Act** | **1981** | Amended **1987** (stricter penalties) |
| **Environment (Protection) Act** | **1986** | Umbrella law; Sec. 3; **EIA and CRZ** under it |
| **Ganga Action Plan** | **1986** | Not 1982 (APPSC 2018 incorrect pair) |
| Environmental Protection Fund Rules | 2026 | Sec. 16, EPA; pools fines under three Acts |
- SPCB compensation powers: **Sec. 33A Water Act**, **Sec. 31A Air Act** (SC, Aug 2025).
### 5. NATIONAL ENVIRONMENT POLICY 2006
- Core idea: **conservation and livelihood security go together**.
- Objectives: conserve critical resources; **inter-generational equity**; livelihood security for poor tribal communities; integrate environment into development plans; efficient resource use.
- Principles: **precautionary + polluter pays + public trust**.
!TRAP NOT an NEP objective: ending all development in ecologically sensitive areas.
### 6. ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT ASSESSMENT
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|

| Notifications | **1994**; revised **2006** |

| Stages | **Screening -> Scoping -> Public consultation -> Appraisal** |
- **Draft EIA 2020** controversy: **post-facto clearance**; public notice cut from **30 to 20 days**; wider B2 exemptions.
### 7. NATIONAL GREEN TRIBUNAL
- **NGT Act 2010**; in force **18 Oct 2010**; replaced the National Environment Appellate Authority.
- **Principal Bench: Delhi**; zonal: **Bhopal, Pune, Kolkata, Chennai**.
- Civil cases on substantial environmental questions under **7 Acts** in Schedule I.
### 8. NAPCC 2008 – 8 MISSIONS
Solar; Enhanced Energy Efficiency; Sustainable Habitat; Water; Himalayan Ecosystem; **Green India**; **Sustainable Agriculture**; Strategic Knowledge for Climate Change. Climate change and human health is also listed (2026).
!TRAP **Coastal Security** is NOT a NAPCC mission.
### 9. ECO-MARK AND AP INSTITUTIONS
- **Eco-Mark (1991)**: environment-friendly products; logo **earthen pot**; certified by **BIS**, not CPCB.
- Products: soaps, detergents, paper, plastics, cosmetics, aerosols, food; **not drugs/antibiotics**.
- **APPCB**: constituted **24 Jan 1976** under the Water Act; air role **1981**; gives consents. **White** = practically non-polluting; **Green** = low pollution (Jul 2026).
- **EPTRI** (Hyderabad): environment training and research.
