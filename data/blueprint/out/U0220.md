## U0220 | TRIBAL MOVEMENTS IN COLONIAL INDIA – SANTHAL, MUNDA (BIRSA), BHIL, RAMPA, GOND RISINGS
### 1. CAUSES OF TRIBAL REVOLTS
- Common factor of 19th-century tribal revolts (UPSC 2011): **complete disruption of the old agrarian order** (Santhal, Kol, Munda Ulgulan).
| Cause | Led to |
|---|---|
| **Indian Forest Act, 1878** – forests = **state property**; curbs on **podu** (shifting cultivation); new land revenue | Forest risings |
| **Dikus** (outsiders: moneylenders, zamindars, traders) + police | Land alienation → **Santhal Hul** |
| Paikas lost **rent-free lands** after the **1803** conquest; salt policy and revenue burdens | **Paika Bidroha (1817)** |
| **Fall of the Peshwa** | **Ramosi** risings |
| Mansabdar **Madhava Bhupati** (Munsubar Madhava Bhupati Dev, appointed **1848**), oppressive taxes, podu curbs | **Rampa 1879-80** |
| Podu tax raised from **4 annas to 4-12 annas per axe** during a famine | **Rekapalle 1879** |
| Destruction of **Khuntkatti** by zamindars, **thikadars** and **missionaries** | **Birsa's Ulgulan** |
| Ban on human sacrifice (**Meriah**) and new taxes | **Khond** rising |
| **Madras Forest Act, 1882** | **Rampa revolt 1922** |

### 3. CHRONOLOGY AND YEAR MATCHING

### 4. SANTHAL HUL (1855-56)
#### 4.1 Background – Rajmahal Hills
- The British failed to control the **Paharias** → invited the **Santhals** to settle and cultivate (APPSC G1 2017).
- **Damin-i-Koh** = tract demarcated for Santhal settlement in the Rajmahal Hills.
- **Cleveland**, Collector of **Bhagalpur (1780s)** – conciliatory plan for the Paharias through **hill chiefs**.
#### 4.2 Course
- Began at **Bhognadih** on **30 June 1855** (**Hul Divas**).
- Leaders: **Sidhu (Siddho), Kanhu**, brothers **Chand and Bhairav**, sisters **Phulo-Jhano**; about **30,000** Santhals proclaimed **Santhal Raj**.
- Against zamindars, moneylenders, police and the **British revenue system**.
- **Major Baroz** lost a battle near **Bhagalpur**; **Gokko** – important leader of **Godda**.
- Crushed in **1856**; over **15,000** killed.
#### 4.3 Administrative Outcome
- **Santhal Parganas** made a separate **non-regulation district** (Act XXXVII of 1855) with laws protecting tribal land.
- **Santhal Parganas Settlement Regulation, 1872**; **Santhal Parganas Tenancy (Supplementary Provisions) Act, 1949**.
!TRAP CNT Act = **1908** (after Birsa); SPT Act = **1949** (Santhals).
### 5. BIRSA MUNDA AND THE ULGULAN (1899-1900)
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Born | **1875** |
| Titles | **Dharti Aba / Dharti Aaba** ("Father of the Earth"); **"Birsa Bhagwan"** |
| 1895 | Claimed healing powers; declared himself a **prophet**; revivalist movement |
| Faith | **Birsait** – monotheist; mix of Munda tradition, Hinduism, Christianity |
| Reform | Against **liquor, witchcraft, animal sacrifice, spirit worship** |
| Revolt | **Ulgulan ("Great Tumult")**, **1899-1900**; about **6,000** Mundas; aim **Abua Raj** (self-rule) / Munda Raj |
| Area | **Ranchi** (expanded to Bhagalpur); Chotanagpur, present **Jharkhand** (APPSC 2024) |
| Capture | **Jamkopai forest, Chakradharpur (West Singhbhum)**, **3 Feb 1900** |
| Death | **Ranchi jail, 9 June 1900** |
| Day | **Janjatiya Gaurav Divas – 15 November** (birth anniversary) |
- One PYQ key dates the Munda revolt to **1890**.
- **Chotanagpur Tenancy Act, 1908** – barred transfer of tribal land to non-tribals; recognised **Khuntkatti** rights.
- Bhagwan Birsa Munda Memorial Udyan cum Freedom Fighters Museum, **Ranchi (2021)**; Tribal Freedom Fighter Museums planned in **10 states**.
- In news: debates over Birsait faith and Adivasi religious identity; a Jharkhand project challenged under the **CNT Act 1908 and PESA**.
!TRAP Khuntkatti was a **landholding system**, not a reform item of Birsa.
### 6. PAIKA REBELLION AND RAMOSI UPRISINGS
#### 6.1 Paika Bidroha (1817)
- Leader **Bakshi Jagabandhu**; **Khurda, Odisha**.
- Cause: Paikas lost rent-free lands after the 1803 conquest; salt policy; revenue burden.
#### 6.2 Ramosis (Maharashtra)
- Rose after the **fall of the Peshwa**; **1822**, again 1825-26, around **Satara**.
- Leaders: **Chittur Singh, Umaji Naik, Bapu Trimbakji Sawant**.
!TRAP **Narayan Rao** is NOT linked with the Ramosis (APPSC 2019).

### 8. BHIL REFORM MOVEMENTS – RAJASTHAN AND GUJARAT
- **Govind Giri (Guru)** – led the **Bhils against bonded labour**; founded the **Samp Sabha (1883)**.
- **Lasodia Movement** (Bhils of **Mewar, Pratapgarh**) – **Govind Giri** with **Surji Bhagat**.
- **Mangarh massacre (1913)** – **"Adivasi Jallianwala"**; **Mangarh Dham** declared a **National Monument (2022)**.
- **Motilal Tejawat** – **Eki movement (1921-22), Mewar**.
