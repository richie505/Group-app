## U0432 | WORLD TRADE ORGANIZATION – STRUCTURE, AGREEMENTS (AoA, TRIPS, TRIMS, GATS), DISPUTE SETTLEMENT, INDIA'S ISSUES
### 1. WTO BASICS
- Formed **1 January 1995** under the **Marrakesh Agreement**, after the **Uruguay Round**; replaced the **GATT** (1947) system (GATT survives as a WTO agreement).
- HQ **Geneva** (not Vienna); **one-country-one-vote**, consensus – gives developing countries bargaining room.
- Members **166** (March 2026); latest: **Comoros** and **Timor-Leste** (2024).
- **GATT Article XXIV**: FTAs allowed as MFN exceptions if they cover "substantially all trade".
- DG: **Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala** – second term 1 Sept 2025 – 31 Aug 2029. **Pascal Lamy** – former DG; French; earlier EU Trade Commissioner.
!TRAP Marrakesh = WTO agreement; Uruguay = round that created it; Geneva = HQ.
### 2. STRUCTURE
| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| **Ministerial Conference** | Highest body; meets **at least once every two years** |
| **General Council** | Runs WTO between MCs; also sits as DSB and TPRB |
| **Dispute Settlement Body** | Panels and Appellate Body |
| **Trade Policy Review Body** | Regular reviews of members' trade policies |
| Council for Trade in Goods | Oversees GATT; agriculture, TBT, SPS committees |
| MC | Venue |
|---|---|
| MC1 | Singapore 1996 |
| Broke down | **Seattle 1999**, **Cancún 2003** |
| MC12 | Geneva, June 2022 |
| MC13 | **Abu Dhabi**, Feb 2024 |
| MC14 | **Yaoundé, Cameroon**, March 2026 |
### 3. AGREEMENTS
| Agreement | Subject |
|---|---|
| **AoA** | Market access, domestic support, export subsidies |
| **SPS** | Food safety, animal and plant health (science-based) |
| **TBT** | Technical regulations, standards, conformity assessment |
| **TRIPS** | Minimum standards for all IPR |
| **TRIMS** | Trade-distorting investment measures (local content) |
| **GATS** | Trade in services |
| **Anti-dumping** | Duties on goods sold below normal value |
| **SCM** | Subsidies: prohibited, actionable, non-actionable |
- Phytosanitary condition = **non-tariff barrier** on farm imports.
- Only new agreements in 30 years: **Trade Facilitation** and **Fisheries Subsidies**.
- India's **DGTR**: anti-dumping, countervailing, safeguard inquiries.
### 4. AGREEMENT ON AGRICULTURE
| Box | Support |
|---|---|
| **Amber** | Trade-distorting (price support, input subsidies); capped |
| **Blue** | Amber with production limits |
| **Green** | Non- or minimally distorting; no limit |
- **De minimis**: **10%** of production (developing), **5%** (developed).
- **Peace Clause** (Bali, 2013): shields developing-country **public stockholding (PSH)**; 2014 decision extended it until a permanent solution.
### 5. TRIPS
| Item | Year |
|---|---|
| **GI Act** (to meet TRIPS) | 1999 |
| **Doha Declaration** – TRIPS must not stop protecting public health | 2001 |
| Patents Act amended | 2005 |
| First **compulsory licence**: Natco-Bayer | 2012 |
| COVID waiver proposed by **India and South Africa** (not Brazil) | 2020 |
| MC12 limited waiver for vaccine patents | 2022 |
- **Section 3(d)** (anti-evergreening) + compulsory licensing = India's flexibilities.
- India not in Lisbon Agreement/Geneva Act (2015) on GIs. MC14: TRIPS non-violation moratorium lapsed.
### 6. DISPUTE SETTLEMENT
- **Appellate Body crisis**: **USA** blocked appointments; India's appeals go "into the void".
| Dispute | Complainant | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Solar panels** (JNNSM domestic content) | USA | India lost, 2016 |
| **Export subsidies** (MEIS, SEZ) | **USA** (not EU) | Panel against India 2019; appealed |
| **Sugar subsidies** | Brazil, Australia, Guatemala | Panel against India Dec 2021; appealed |
| **ICT tariffs** | EU, Japan, Chinese Taipei | Panel against India April 2023; appealed |
### 7. INDIA'S ISSUES
- **E-commerce moratorium** (since 1998) lapsed **31 March 2026**; **66** members signed a plurilateral pact banning such duties.
- **Fisheries Phase 2** (overcapacity, overfishing) unresolved at MC14.
- **Food security**: permanent PSH solution = core demand; none at MC14.
- **SDT**: US wants to deny it to China, India, Brazil, Indonesia; India defends it.
- India opposes **Investment Facilitation for Development** (120+ backers).
- May 2026: India raised the UK steel safeguard.
