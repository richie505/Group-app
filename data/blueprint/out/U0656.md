## U0656 | RELIGIOUS COMPOSITION OF INDIA – DISTRIBUTION AND CHANGE OVER DECADES
### 1. CENSUS 2011 – RELIGIOUS COMPOSITION
| Religion | Population | Share | Growth 2001–11 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hindu | 96.63 crore | **79.8%** (80.5% in 2001) | 16.8% |
| Muslim | **17.22 crore** | 14.2% (largest minority) | **24.6% (highest)** |
| Christian | **2.78 crore** (27.8 million) | 2.3% | 15.5% |
| Sikh | 2.08 crore | 1.7% | 8.4% |
| Buddhist | 0.84 crore | 0.7% | 6.1% |
| Jain | 0.45 crore | **0.4% (smallest)** | **5.4% (lowest)** |
| ORP | 0.79 crore | 0.7% | – |
- **Ascending order**: Jain < Buddhist < Sikh < Christian < Muslim < Hindu.
- **"No Religion" option first in 2011**.
- 2011: 121.09 crore; growth 17.64%; density **382**; sex ratio **943**; child **919**; literacy **74.04%** (M 82.14, F 65.46); ST 8.6%, SC 16.6%.
!KEY APPSC 2023 match: Sikhs 1.7, Christians 2.3, Buddhists 0.7, Jains 0.4. APPSC 2025: highest growth = **Islam**.
### 2. CHANGE OVER DECADES
- **Muslims** – only community growing faster than the national rate.
- Hindu share 80.5% → 79.8%: **first time below 80%**; Muslim 13.4% → 14.2%; Christian, Jain unchanged; Sikh down 0.2, Buddhist down 0.1 points.
- Muslim share **~10% (1951) → ~14% (2011)**: poorer economic status, less female education – not religion.
- **1881**: Indian-origin religions ~79% (95% Hindu); Christians ~0.7%.
- Series **10.7 million (1961) → 27.8 million (2011)** = **Christians** (UPSC 2001); Sikhs 20.8, Buddhists 8.4, Jains 4.5 million.

!TRAP Arunachal Pradesh is NOT Christian-majority: Christians largest group (~30%) but below 50%.

### 6. NOTIFIED MINORITIES – NCM ACT 1992
- **Section 2(c)**: minority = community notified by the Centre; criticised as "unbridled power" vs **T.M.A. Pai** State-level test.
- **1993** notification: **Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Parsis**; **Jains** added **27 January 2014** (latest).
- NCM Chairperson **Harjit Singh Grewal** – full 3-year term (Jul 2026).
### 7. MINORITY WELFARE SCHEMES
- **PMJVK** (formerly **MsDP**): revised 2022-23; all districts incl. 112 Aspirational.
- Scholarships: NSP 2.0, DBT; **30% for girls**; post-matric income below ₹2.5 lakh.
- **Jiyo Parsi** – Parsi population decline.
- **Waqf (Amendment) Act 2025** – in force 8 Apr 2025; **UMEED** portal; audit for waqfs earning over **₹1 lakh**.
- UCC: **Uttarakhand** since Jan 2025. **Census 2027**: six religions + "other"; parents' religion, birthplace.
### 8. ANDHRA PRADESH
- Residual AP 2011: **Hindus ~90.9%, Muslims ~7.3%, Christians ~1.4% (6.82 lakh)**.
- Muslims highest: **Kurnool, YSR Kadapa, Anantapur** (Rayalaseema); Christians: coastal districts, tribal agency areas.
- Minorities mostly urban, large BPL segment.
- Bodies: State Minorities Commission, Minorities Finance Corporation (post-matric RTF ₹36.34 crore, 50,767), **Christian Minorities Finance Corporation** (Jerusalem pilgrimage aid), **Waqf Board**.
- Imams and Mouzams ₹180 crore a year; **Urdu second official language (17 Mar 2026)**; minority budget **₹6,090 crore** (2026-27).
