## U0377 | CIVIL SOCIETY, NGOS, SHGS AND PRESSURE GROUPS IN GOVERNANCE; FCRA REGULATION
### 1. CIVIL SOCIETY – MEANING AND ROLE
- Civil society = **pressure groups + trade unions + NGOs**; the **"third sector"** alongside the **State** (first) and **market** (second).
- **World Bank**: NGOs, unions, faith-based groups, professional associations, foundations.
- CSOs = **development partners** and **democratic watchdogs**.
- Shift: **welfare delivery → rights and empowerment**; service delivery → **advocacy, policy formulation, watchdog** work (e.g. **ADR** on candidate disclosure).
!KEY APPSC 2019 key: NGOs as **"observationalists in development"** (standard texts call them partners of government).
### 2. PRESSURE GROUPS – DEFINITION AND TYPES
- **Organised group** influencing public policy **without contesting elections or seeking office**; presses government, legislators, bureaucracy for one section's interest.
- Also called **interest groups, lobby groups**; label: **"Anonymous Empire"**.
- **Almond and Powell**: **institutional, associational, non-associational, anomic**.
#### 2.1 Types → Indian Examples
| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Business | **FICCI**, CII, ASSOCHAM |
| Trade union | **AITUC**, INTUC, CITU, BMS, HMS |
| Professional | **Indian Medical Association**, Bar associations |
| Peasant | **Bharatiya Kisan Union**, All India Kisan Sabha |
| Caste | Maratha, Gujjar, Patel quota groups |
| Religious / student | Faith bodies; student unions |
!KEY APPSC 2007 key: most powerful pressure group = **AITUC** (texts often rank business groups first).
#### 2.2 Pressure Group vs Political Party
| | Political party | Pressure group |
|---|---|---|
| Elections | Contests; answers to **voters** | Does not contest; answers to **members** |
| Scope | Whole range of policy | **One section's** interest |
- Test: seeks to form government → party. **Trade union members** = pressure group; MPs, ministers, panchayat members are not (MPPSC 2017).
### 3. TECHNIQUES → MEANING
| Technique | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Lobbying** | Dealing with **legislators and officials** to promote or defeat legislation and regulation (**APPSC 2019**) |
| **Propaganda** | Shaping public opinion |
| **Electioneering** | Helping **sympathetic candidates** win |
| Petitioning | Written memoranda to authorities |
| Strikes, bandhs, dharnas | Direct disruption |
| **Litigation** | PILs and court cases (e.g. ADR's electoral cases) |
| Media campaigns | Shaping opinion to pressure government |

### 6. NGOS – LEGAL FORMS AND POLICY
| Legal form | Law |
|---|---|
| Society | **Societies Registration Act, 1860** |
| Private trust | **Indian Trusts Act, 1882** |
| Public charitable trust | **State laws**, e.g. Bombay Public Trusts Act, 1950 |
| Not-for-profit company | **Section 8, Companies Act, 2013** |
- India has about **1.5-3.3 million** NGOs; foreign-funded NGOs must also register under **FCRA**.
- **National Policy on the Voluntary Sector, 2007** (Planning Commission): enabling environment and independence, easier **resource mobilisation**, **partnership** (not subordination) with government.
### 7. FCRA REGULATION
#### 7.1 Timeline
| Year | Change |
|---|---|
| **1976** | First enacted |
| **2010** | FCRA 2010 replaced it |
| **2016** | Retrospective: Indian subsidiaries of foreign firms **not "foreign source"** (after Delhi HC 2014 ruling on BJP/Congress donations) |
| **2020** | **Designated FCRA account at SBI, New Delhi**; admin cap **50% → 20%**; **sub-granting banned** |
| **25 Mar 2026** | Amendment Bill in Lok Sabha: take over **assets** of NGOs with cancelled/suspended registration; **deferred** after protests |
| **22 Jun 2026** | Amendment Rules notified |
- Administered by **MHA**; FEMA 1999 by **RBI and ED**.
#### 7.2 Registration and Cancellation
- Validity **5 years**; **18,000+** cancelled since 2015; **14,538** active (15 Apr 2026).
- Grounds: breach of Act/rules/conditions, **non-functioning**, administrative defaults (returns not filed, late renewal) – **not** location of registered office.
- **Rules 2026**: five categories – **social, economic, educational, cultural, religious**; foreign citizens (other than **PIOs**) as key functionaries make NGO ineligible; renewal needs **₹10 lakh** foreign funds spent over two years.
- **Public Health Foundation of India**: put under **prior permission (2017)** for lobbying with parliamentarians; licence later cancelled (APPSC 2017).
- Debate: State control of civil society (critics) vs transparency and national security (government).
### 8. SHGS AND WOMEN'S MICROFINANCE
| Body | Year | Feature |
|---|---|---|

- DAY-NRLM resource persons: **Pashu, Krishi, Bank, Bima, Poshan Sakhi** (not Nyaya Sakhi).
#### 8.1 AP SHG Movement
- SHGs in AP are popularly called **DWCRA groups** (Development of Women and Children in Rural Areas).
- **SERP**: autonomous society of the Rural Development Dept; runs **Velugu**, renamed **Indira Kranthi Patham (IKP)** under Congress; name Velugu restored after 2014; split into AP and TG SERP.
- **MEPMA** (urban): G.O. Ms 414 of **4 Jun 2007**, launched **1 Sep 2007**; SHG → Slum Level Federation → Town Level Federation.
### 9. MEDIA AND PRESS COUNCIL OF INDIA
- **Fourth estate**: watchdog, agenda-setter. Press freedom read into **Article 19(1)(a)**.
- **APPSC 2018**: free press is necessary for democracy, and new media made communication participatory – **both true**.
- Problems: **paid news**, **fake news** and echo chambers, concentrated ownership, dependence on government adverts. India is in RSF's **"very serious"** category (May 2026).
#### 9.1 Press Council of India
- First set up **1966** under the **Press Council Act, 1965**; present law **Press Council Act, 1978**.
- **Statutory, quasi-judicial**; **Chairman + 28 members**; chairman by custom a **retired SC judge**, chosen by RS Chairman, LS Speaker and a PCI member.
- Can **warn, admonish, censure**; **no punitive powers**; **print media only**.
### 10. ANDHRA PRADESH EXAMPLES
- Pressure groups: **AP NGOs' Association** (employees), **rythu sanghams** (farmers); the Election Commission is a constitutional authority, not a pressure group.
- **Amaravati farmers' agitation**: began **Dec 2019** against the **three-capitals** plan; led by **Amaravati Parirakshana Samithi**; ended **June 2024** after **1,631 days** when the Naidu government took office.
