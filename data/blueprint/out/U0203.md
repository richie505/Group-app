## U0203 | PARTITION OF BENGAL 1905, SWADESHI AND BOYCOTT MOVEMENT; TECHNIQUES AND IMPACT
### 1. PARTITION OF BENGAL, 1905
#### 1.1 Key Facts
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Viceroy | **Lord Curzon** – called Bengal "**the nerve centre of Indian nationalism**" |
| Announced | **19/20 July 1905** |
| Took effect | **16 October 1905** (not 15 October) |
| New province | **Eastern Bengal and Assam** – capital **Dacca** |
| Rest of Bengal | Capital **Calcutta** |
| Annulled | **12 December 1911**, **Delhi Durbar of King George V** |
| Viceroy at annulment | **Lord Hardinge II** |
| Capital shift | **Calcutta → Delhi**, announced 1911 at the same Durbar; transfer completed 1912 |
#### 1.2 Division and Population
| Part | Population |
|---|---|
| Eastern Bengal and Assam | **31 million** |
| Rest of Bengal | **54 million** – 18 million Bengalis, 36 million Biharis and Oriyas → **Bengalis a minority** in their own province |
#### 1.3 Stated vs Real Motives
- **Stated**: **administrative convenience** – province "too big"; Curzon called it a redrawing of administrative boundaries.
- **Real**: **weaken Bengali nationalism**; divide **Hindus from Muslims** – nationalists saw it as **Divide and Rule**.
- **H.H. Risley** (Home Secretary): "**Bengal united is a power; Bengal divided will pull in several different ways.**"
- **Surendranath Banerjee**: partition "**fell like a bombshell**".
- **Japan's victory over Russia (1904-05)** fuelled the anger (Sumit Sarkar).
!TRAP The **1903 Durbar** celebrated **Edward VII's coronation**; the **1911 Durbar** (George V) **annulled** the partition (APPSC 2022 A-R). 1911 = the only Durbar a sovereign attended.
!TRAP King **George V** was the monarch at annulment; **Curzon** announced the partition; **Hardinge II** was Viceroy in 1911.
### 2. LAUNCH OF THE SWADESHI MOVEMENT
- **Immediate cause**: the partition by Lord Curzon.
- **Moderate campaign (1903-05)**: petitions and meetings by **S.N. Banerjee, K.K. Mitra, Prithwishchandra Ray** – failed.
- **K.K. (Krishna Kumar) Mitra** – **first suggested boycott**, in weekly **Sanjibani (13 July 1905)**; Brahmo Samaj leader.
- **7 August 1905** – **boycott resolution at Calcutta Town Hall**; Swadeshi **formally proclaimed**; boycott of British goods adopted as national policy.
- **16 October 1905** – partition day: **day of mourning, hartal, Ganga bath, Rakhi-bandhan**, Swadeshi vow.
- **National Handloom Day** = **7 August**, observed since **2015** (APPSC 2019).
- Leadership passed from **Moderates to Extremists in 1906**.
!KEY Answer **7 August 1905** for "launched / proclaimed"; 16 Oct 1905 = partition took effect.
!PAIR 20 July 1905 | partition decision | Curzon || 7 Aug 1905 | Swadeshi declared | Town Hall
### 3. TECHNIQUES AND PROGRAMME
| Technique | Form |
|---|---|
| **Boycott** | **Foreign cloth, salt, sugar**; bonfires of cloth; extended to **courts, schools, government service**; priests refused rituals using foreign goods |
| **Swadeshi / Atmashakti** | Self-reliance |
| **Samitis** | **Swadesh Bandhab Samiti, Barisal – Ashwini Kumar Dutt** (magic lanterns, social work, arbitration courts) |
| **Mass mobilisation** | **Jatras, folk songs, melas, volunteer corps, akharas, lathi clubs** |
| **National education** | National schools and colleges |
- Programmes against partition: **Boycott, Swadeshi, National education** – **NOT Non-cooperation**.
- **Barisal peasant agitation** – Ashwini Kumar Dutt, not Liaquat Hussain.
- **Calcutta INC 1906 (Naoroji)**: Swaraj declared the goal; resolutions on **Swaraj, Swadeshi, Boycott, National Education**.
- Movement not linked to **Gandhi** (he was in South Africa); period **1905-1911**.
!BOX Swadeshi = **first mass movement**; boycott and Swadeshi **first adopted as methods** here; birth of mass nationalism (UPSC 2016).

### 7. MUSLIM LEAGUE AND SURAT SPLIT
#### 7.1 All-India Muslim League
- Founded **30 December 1906** at **Dacca**, at the All-India Muhammadan Educational Conference.
- Proposal by **Nawab Salimullah**; **Aga Khan III** first (honorary) president.
- Context: **Simla Deputation (1906)** → **Morley-Minto Reforms (1909)** gave **separate electorates for Muslims**.
#### 7.2 Surat Split (December 1907)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Moderate nominee / president | **Rash Behari Ghosh** |
| Extremist demand | Session at **Nagpur** with **Tilak or Lajpat Rai** as president; keep the **Calcutta 1906 resolutions** |
| Venue | Moved to **Surat** (fearing Tilak's election) |
| Result | Session broke up; Extremists expelled |
| Minto | Called it "a great triumph for us" |
- Aftermath (**1908**): **Muzaffarpur bomb**, **Alipore case**, **Newspapers Act**, **Tilak sent to Mandalay**.
- Reunion: **Lucknow 1916** – Extremists re-entered; **Lucknow Pact** (Tilak, Jinnah).
!PAIR Banaras 1905 | Gokhale | backed Swadeshi || Calcutta 1906 | Naoroji | Swaraj
### 8. SWADESHI (VANDEMATARAM) MOVEMENT IN ANDHRA
#### 8.1 Beginnings (Andhra phase 1905-1911)
- **Madras Beach students' meeting (Sept 1905)** – presided by **G. Subramania Iyer**; **Subramania Bharati's** songs.
- **S.N. Banerjee's arrest (1906)** → protests at **Rajahmundry, Kakinada, Vizianagaram, Gooty**; slogan **"Vandemataram – Manade Rajyam"**.
- **Bala Bharati Samiti** – **Rajahmundry, Feb 1907**, local youths (**Chilukuri Veerabhadra Rao, Ganti Lakshmanna, Tanguturi Sriramulu, Kanchumarthi Ramachandra Rao**); member **T. Prakasam**; backer **Karanam Guneswara Rao**; organised meetings during Pal's visit.
!TRAP **Mutnuri Krishna Rao** organised Pal's tour – he did **not** found the Bala Bharati Samiti.
#### 8.2 Bipin Chandra Pal's Tour (16-27 April 1907)
| Date | Place | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| 17 Apr | **Kakinada** | Two lectures; chaired by **Kattiventi Perraju**; "Swaraj the goal, boycott the weapon" |
| 19-24 Apr | **Rajahmundry** | **Five lectures**; opened **Godavari Swadeshi Stores**; Rs 1,000 from Guneswara Rao for a national school; Chilakamarti's poem "**Bharata khandambu chakkani paadi yaavu**" |
| 27 Apr | **Machilipatnam** | Host **Kompalle Hanumantha Rao** (APPSC 2008 key); **Young Men's Swarajya Samithi**; foundation for a national college |
- **T. Prakasam** presided over Pal's **Madras** meetings, 1907.
- Rajahmundry host: **Young Men's Association** (APPSC 2017 key).
- APPSC 2025 key paired Pal's tour with July 1907 in a match – learn the key's pairing for that question.
#### 8.3 Incidents and Trials
| Incident | Date / place | Key facts |
|---|---|---|

### 9. IMPACT AND ASSESSMENT
- **Weaknesses**: few **peasants and Muslims** joined; **faded by 1908** under repression and the Surat Split.
- Achievements: first mass movement; new methods (boycott, Swadeshi, national education, passive resistance) later used by Gandhi; growth of Indian industry and national culture.
- **Phase classification**: Swadeshi (1905) and Surat Split (1907) = **Extremist phase**; Morley-Minto (1909) = government response; Home Rule (1916) and Lucknow Pact (1916) = reunion phase before the **Gandhian phase**.
- **Home Rule Leagues**: Tilak's **April 1916**, Besant's **Sept 1916**; Besant INC president **Calcutta 1917**.
- **Sumit Sarkar**, author of **The Swadeshi Movement in Bengal**, died **17 Aug 2026**; tribute noted his use of plays, novels and pamphlets.
- Tilak born **23 July 1856, Ratnagiri** – PM's birth-anniversary tributes (23 Jul 2026).
