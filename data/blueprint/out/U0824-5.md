### 1. CLIMATE NEGOTIATIONS AND CARBON MARKETS
#### 1.1 COP30 (Belém, Brazil, 10–21 November 2025)
- Final decision – **"Global Mutirão"**: **triple adaptation finance by 2035**; **59 indicators** for the Global Goal on Adaptation; launched the Global Implementation Accelerator and the **Belém Mission to 1.5**.
- No direct fossil fuel language in the decision; a separate voluntary roadmap to transition away from fossil fuels was backed by 80+ countries.
#### 1.2 National Designated Authority (Article 6)
- Notified by **MoEFCC** on **22 August 2025**; **21 members**; mandatory under **Article 6 of the Paris Agreement (2015)** (carbon markets).
- Article 6 rules finalised at **COP29, Baku (Nov 2024)**; **ITMOs** = Internationally Transferred Mitigation Outcomes.
| India's 2030 NDC | Target |
|---|---|
| Emissions intensity of GDP | Cut **45%** from 2005 levels |
| Non-fossil installed capacity | **50%** |
| Additional carbon sink | **2.5–3 billion t CO₂e** |
#### 1.3 Rio Earth Summit – 33 Years
- **UNCED, 1992, Rio** – 125 countries, 100 heads of state, 12,000 delegates.
- Outcomes: **UNFCCC** (in force 1994); **CBDR** in the Rio Declaration; **Biodiversity Convention**; **Desertification Treaty**.
- **Binding Forest Convention was NOT adopted** (blocked by Global South – G77, India); only **non-binding forest principles**.
- Paris Agreement (2015) shifted to voluntary NDCs; the **$100 billion a year** climate finance promise remains **unmet**.
#### 1.4 OCI Report "Planet Wreckers"
- By **Oil Change International** – fossil expansion by **Australia, Canada, Norway, USA**; oil and gas output +14 million boe/day (2015–2024), +40%.
- **USA – over 90%** of net global increase (+11 million boe/d); **Australia +77%** – highest growth rate among top 15; rest of the world –2%.
- Climate finance since 2015 only **$280 billion** vs need $1–5 trillion/yr; fossil subsidies **$465 billion**; six majors' profits $580 billion+.
- **COP28 (Dubai, 2023)**: "**transition away**" from fossil fuels; the 1.5°C budget may run out in **two years**.

#### 4.2 Southwest Monsoon 2025
- **8% above normal** (LPA **87 cm**).
| Region | Excess |
|---|---|
| **Northwest India** | **27%** (highest) |
| Central India | 15% |
| South Peninsula | 10% |
- Kharif area up 15 lakh ha to 1,110 lakh ha; rice up 8.45 lakh ha to 438 lakh ha; reservoir storage **157.8 → 163 BCM**.
- Floods/landslides: HP, J&K, Punjab; **August 2025 floods – Punjab and Delhi**; rain in **concentrated bursts**; "compound disasters".
- **Cloudburst (IMD)**: **at least 100 mm in one hour** over ~20–30 sq km (not 50 mm).
- Initiatives: National Monsoon Mission; Flash Flood Guidance for 30,000+ watersheds; NDMP 2019; DRIP. Preventive resilience: **NAPCC, SAPCCs, NDMA/SDMA district plans**; models – **floodplain zoning (Netherlands)**, **slope stabilisation (Japan)**, community preparedness (Philippines).
- **Compensatory afforestation does not restore the original biodiversity**.
#### 4.3 Northeast Monsoon 2025
- Onset **16 October 2025** (normal 20 October ± 7 days); IMD forecast above normal (over 112% of LPA).
- Gives **48% of Tamil Nadu's** annual rain and **over 30% of AP's**; also called the **retreating monsoon**; ~**three** cyclonic disturbances per season in the Bay of Bengal.
- Urea allocation raised to 36.65 lakh tonnes (Oct 2025) after TN CM M.K. Stalin raised a shortage.
#### 4.4 Thirstwaves
- Coined by Meetpal S. Kukal (Univ. of Idaho) and Mike Hobbins (NOAA) – *Earth's Future*, March 2025.
- **Three or more consecutive days** of high **atmospheric evaporative demand**; measured by **ET₀** (theoretical **12 cm grass crop**); drivers – temperature, low humidity, wind, solar radiation (**not temperature alone; not rainfall**).
| Aspect | Heatwave | Thirstwave |
|---|---|---|
| Based on | High air temperature | High evaporative demand |
| Main impact | Human health | **Crop water stress** |
#### 4.5 Darjeeling Floods (Oct 2025) and Himalayan Disasters
- **Dudhia bridge** over the **Balason** washed away, cutting the **Siliguri–Mirik** link.
- **ISRO Landslide Atlas 2023**: Darjeeling **35th** among 147 districts; 1968 floods killed over a thousand.
- 2023 **South Lhonak GLOF** (Sikkim): damages ₹25,000 crore (~60% of Sikkim's 2022–23 GDP).
- Himalayas: **210 million+** people in **7 countries** (50 million+ in India); Dharali (Uttarkashi) flash flood, Mandi landslides; towns follow **generic plains building codes** despite high seismicity; building over springs.

### 6. POLLUTION, WASTE AND ENERGY
#### 6.1 E-Waste
- 2025: India generated **2.2 million MT** – **third-largest after China and USA**; 150% rise from 0.71 million MT (2017–18); to double by 2030.
- 65 cities produce over 60%; hotspots Seelampur, Mustafabad (Delhi), Moradabad (UP), Bhiwandi (Maharashtra); PM2.5 in Seelampur over 300 μg/m³ (12× WHO 24-hour limit of 25).
- **322 registered formal recyclers**; only 43% processed formally (2023–24).
- **E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022** – **EPR**, mandatory registration of recyclers/dismantlers.
- Toxins: **beryllium, cadmium, chromium, mercury, lead** (UPSC 2013) – not PM2.5.
#### 6.2 Plastic Pollution
- **World Environment Day 2025 (5 June)** theme: **"Ending Plastic Pollution"**.
- OECD: production doubled 2000–2019 to 460 million t; waste **353 million t** – recycled **9%**, incinerated **19%**, landfilled 50%, mismanaged 22%; by source – **packaging 40%**, consumer goods 12%, textiles 11%.
- 11 million t/yr enter oceans; plastics 3.4% of emissions; 2060 → 1.2 billion t.
- **UNEA-5 (2022)**: all 193 members agreed to **negotiate a legally binding plastics treaty** – **not a production ban**; UNEP aim – cut plastic waste **80% in two decades**.
- **Minicoy (Lakshadweep)**: fire in ~**4,000 tonnes of dry waste** – **Porites coral** bleaching, leachate, harm to fisheries; elected panchayats replaced by appointed officials; NGT monitoring (tourism not halted); CRZ violations.
#### 6.3 Air: Stubble Burning and Indoor Air
| Punjab | Farm fires | Burnt area |
|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 36,663 | 19.14 lakh ha |
| 2024 | **10,909** (–70%) | **19.17 lakh ha** |
- Fewer fires but the same burnt area; **MODIS and VIIRS** (thermal) miss evening fires; **Sentinel-2** detects burn scars; solutions – Happy Seeder, Super SMS, **Pusa Decomposer**, pelletisation, biochar; **CPCB monitors**, SC mandated action.
- **Global Pledge for Healthy Indoor Air** – launched at the **80th UN General Assembly (2025)**, led by **Australian Academy of Science** and **Burnet Institute**; humans breathe 7,000+ litres of air daily; New Zealand lacks a national IAQ strategy.
#### 6.4 Fuels and Solar
- **EBP Programme** – **E20 by 2025**. **EU** considering a de-facto ban on ethanol products after **ECHA** flagged cancer risk (by-products **acetaldehyde, formaldehyde**).
- **ARAI** isobutanol–diesel blending pilot (~18 months; announced by Nitin Gadkari): isobutanol has **higher flash point**, better miscibility, but **lower cetane number**; up to 10% safe.
- **Surface solar radiation** may fall **up to 10%** over central, southern, eastern India (Ashwin Jadhav, Rohini Bhawar, SPPU); **Indo-Gangetic Plains and NE** may see a small rise under low emissions; India solar capacity 123,130 MW (31 Aug 2025).
- **ONGC well blowout, Sivasagar (Assam)**: 12 June 2025; 1,500+ evacuated; US experts; **controlled by 21 June**. Blowout = well pressure beats **blowout preventers and drilling mud** (faulty valves, wrong mud weight).

### 8. SPECIES CONSERVATION
#### 8.1 Dugong
- *Dugong dugon* – "**sea cow**"; **India's only herbivorous marine mammal**; eats seagrass (30–40 kg/day); ~3 m, ~420 kg; lives up to 70 years.
- **IUCN Vulnerable**; **CITES Appendix I**; **Schedule I** of WPA (not Schedule II); **not found along the entire coast**.
| Area | Estimate |
|---|---|
| **Gulf of Mannar – Palk Bay** (TN) | ~150–200 – **largest** |
| Andaman & Nicobar | Below 50 |
| Gulf of Kutch | Below 20 |
- **Palk Bay Dugong Conservation Reserve (2022)** – **448 sq km**, under WPA 1972 – **India's first** dedicated dugong conservation area; hailed by IUCN.
- **World Dugong Day: 28 May**; MoEFCC Task Force **2010**; **National Dugong Recovery Programme** (TN, Gujarat, A&N).
- Study (*Marine Pollution Bulletin*): arsenic, cadmium, chromium, mercury, lead in 46 stranded dugongs.
#### 8.2 Other Species
- **Asian giant tortoise** (*Manouria emys phayrei*; **Critically Endangered**; largest tortoise of mainland Asia): **28 hatchlings** (Aug 2025) at **Manipur Zoological Garden, Imphal** (also known for **Sangai** breeding) with **India Turtle Conservation Program** – **first artificial hatching in mainland Asia**. Indian range: **Assam, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland** – **not A&N**.
- **Saltwater crocodiles** rising in the **Sundarban BR** – credited to the **Bhagabatpur Crocodile Project** (captive breeding and release); apex predator, indicator of mangrove health, tolerates wide salinity; WPA 1972 criticised as reactive.
- **Kaziranga** (Golaghat, Nagaon, Karbi Anglong): Reserve Forest **1905**, NP **1974**, WHS **1985**; ~**two-thirds** of world's one-horned rhinos (**more than 2,500**).

### 11. GEO-PHENOMENA, WATER AND PLACES IN NEWS
#### 11.1 Volcanoes
- **Hayli Gubbi (NE Ethiopia, Afar Rift)** – erupted **23 November 2025**, **first in nearly 12,000 years**; **explosive** – ash and gases, **no lava**; ash plume at **10–14 km** across Arabian Peninsula, Arabian Sea → Gujarat, Rajasthan, Delhi, UP → China.
| Volcano type | Example |
|---|---|
| Active | **Kilauea** (Hawaii), **Santa Maria** (Guatemala) |
| Dormant | **Mount Kilimanjaro** |
| Extinct | **Dhinodhar Hill** (Gujarat) |
| Shield (basaltic, gentle) | **Mauna Loa** |
| Composite / stratovolcano | Mayon, Mount Fuji, Mount Rainier |
- Mid-ocean ridge system: over 65,000 km.
- **Baratang (A&N)** – **India's only active mud volcano**; eruption 2 Oct 2025; **GSI** (1851, Kolkata, Ministry of Mines) found **Mithakari Group** rocks of the **Oligocene (33.9–23 million years)** (~23 million years old); cause – **Indian Plate subducting beneath the Burma Microplate**, **shale diapirism**.
#### 11.2 Coasts, Aquifers and Reefs
- **Varkala Cliff (Kerala)** – added to the **UNESCO Tentative List** (Sept 2025; not full status); **only place in Kerala where laterite cliffs rise directly from the sea**; Mio-Pliocene; **Sivagiri Mutt** (Sree Narayana Guru), Janardana Swamy temple.
- **Offshore freshwater aquifer** under the Atlantic from **New Jersey to Maine** – could supply New York City for 800 years; offshore aquifers ~**1 million km³** (~**10%** of terrestrial freshwater); held by a **clay cap rock**; Ice Age recharge – **not refilled by present rainfall**.
- **Jalari Yendada (Visakhapatnam) artificial reefs**: launched **20 May 2025** by Fisheries Minister **Kinjarapu Atchannaidu**; Phase I **22 reef units** within **2.5 km** of shore (fuel cost cut up to 80%); **PMMSY** funding **60:40** Centre–State. PMMSY – **2020**, Blue Economy, **SDG-14**.
#### 11.3 Water and Cities
- **NITI Aayog**: ~**600 million** Indians face high–extreme water stress; NFHS-5 – women fetch water in ~71% rural households; FAO – female-headed households lose 3% more income to floods.
- Urban India: population near **1 billion by 2050**; cities **over 70% of new jobs** in 5 years; **144 million new homes** needed by 2070; UHI nights 3–5°C hotter; examples – **Ahmedabad Heat Action Plan**, **Kolkata flood forecasting**, **Chennai stormwater**.
