## U0474 | POVERTY ESTIMATION COMMITTEES – ALAGH, LAKDAWALA, TENDULKAR, RANGARAJAN; NITI AAYOG MPI
### 1. EARLY ESTIMATES
- **Dadabhai Naoroji**: at 1867-68 prices, **₹16-35 per capita per year**.
- **National Planning Committee (1938)**: ₹15-20 per month; **Bombay Plan (1944)**: ₹75 per year.
- Poverty estimated by the **Planning Commission** since the **6th Five Year Plan**.

### 3. FIRST SYSTEMATIC ASSESSMENT – DANDEKAR AND RATH
- Used **1960-61 NSS** data.
- Poverty line = expenditure adequate for **2,250 kcal per day**, for **both rural and urban**.
- APPSC-GS 2022: Alagh (1979) = nutrition norm; Dandekar-Rath (1971) = first systematic study.
### 4. COMMITTEE → METHOD
| Committee | Method / basis |
|---|---|
| **Alagh** | Line based **only on nutritional requirements**: **2,400 kcal rural / 2,100 kcal urban** |
| **Lakdawala** | Kept calorie-based consumption; **State-specific lines**; price updating by **CPI-AL (rural)** and **CPI-IW (urban)**; **stopped scaling to National Accounts** |
| **Tendulkar** | **Moved away from calorie anchor**; added private **health and education** spending; **MRP** in place of URP; **one urban PLB** used for both rural and urban |
| **Rangarajan** | **Separate food and non-food** needs; calories **2,155 rural / 2,090 urban** plus protein and fat norms; **MMRP** data |
- State poverty lines differ because **price levels vary** between States (Tendulkar: uniform basket, inter-state price differentials).
!TRAP Single urban PLB for both areas = Tendulkar, NOT Rangarajan.
### 5. DATA, RECALL PERIODS AND PRICE INDICES
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **URP** | **30-day recall** for all items (used up to 1993-94) |
| **MRP** | **365-day recall** for **five low-frequency items**: clothing, footwear, durables, education, institutional medical care |
| **MMRP** | 7-day recall for some food items |
| **CPI-AL** | Updates the **rural** line (Lakdawala) |
| **CPI-IW** | Updates the **urban** line (Lakdawala) |
- **URP and MRP** both measure the **incidence of poverty** (APPSC 2018) – not inflation, unemployment or inequality.
- **61st NSSO round (2004-05)** used both URP and MRP.
- NSS consumer expenditure rounds every five years; now **HCES 2022-23 and 2023-24**.
- **RBI (2025)**: Rangarajan line updated with HCES 2022-23 using a new index on PLB food and non-food weights, not plain CPI.

### 8. BPL IDENTIFICATION COMMITTEES
| Person / body | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **N.C. Saxena** (Ministry of Rural Development, report **Aug 2009**) | Method for the **rural BPL Census** – not poverty estimation |
| **S.R. Hashim** (Planning Commission working group) | Identify **urban BPL** families |

!TRAP Saxena = rural BPL; Hashim = urban BPL; Rangarajan (not Abhijit Sen) reviewed poverty methodology in 2012.
### 9. NITI AAYOG NATIONAL MPI
- Estimating body: **NITI Aayog**, with technical inputs from **OPHI and UNDP**.
- Method: **Alkire-Foster**; **3 equally weighted dimensions** – health, education, **standard of living**.
- **12 SDG-aligned indicators** (Global MPI has **10**).
- Based on **NFHS-4 (2015-16)** and **NFHS-5 (2019-21)**.
- Discussion paper (Jan 2024): **24.82 crore** people exited multidimensional poverty in 9 years; headcount **29.17% (2013-14) → 11.28% (2022-23)**; about 2.75 crore a year.
- **AP**: MPI **0.053 → 0.025**; ranks **9th**.
### 10. POVERTY LINE DEBATES
- Poverty lines are **politically sensitive**: a higher line raises **fiscal costs**, a lower one **excludes the needy** → action on reports may be delayed.
- No clear picture of how much poverty India has, and big shifts in anti-poverty strategy – both true, but the second does not explain the first.
