## U0522 | FDI AND FPI POLICY IN INDIA - ROUTES, SECTORAL CAPS, TRENDS; FTAS AND CEPAS SIGNED BY INDIA
### 1. FDI - MEANING AND WHAT COUNTS
- **FDI**: investment for **lasting interest and control over management**; a **largely non-debt-creating** capital flow; longer-term.
- Legal definition: capital instruments bought by a person resident outside India in an **unlisted** Indian company, or **10% or more** of post-issue paid-up equity (fully diluted) of a **listed** company.
- Instruments: equity shares, debentures, preference shares, share warrants.
- Brings capital, **technology**, management skills, competition, jobs, export competitiveness; finances the CAD with stable capital.
- **Counts as FDI**: subsidiaries of foreign companies, majority foreign equity in Indian companies, companies exclusively financed by foreign companies, a wholly owned subsidiary.
!TRAP Portfolio investment is NOT automatically FDI; buying bonds or stocks of a company overseas is NOT FDI.
| Type | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Greenfield** | New facility | Hyundai, Sriperumbudur |
| **Brownfield** | Acquisition | Vodafone-Hutchison Essar |
### 2. FDI VS FPI VS FII
| Basis | FDI | FPI / FII |
|---|---|---|
| **Threshold** | **≥10%** listed, or any unlisted stake | **<10%** listed (e.g. a 5% stake) |
| Nature | Long-term, stable, control | Liquid, short-term "**hot money**", more volatile |
| Brings | Capital + technology + management | Only financial capital; raises capital availability in general |
| Target | Specific sectors/enterprises | Securities (**secondary market**) |
| Regulator | DPIIT + RBI | **SEBI (FPI Regulations 2019)** + RBI |
- **10% threshold**: **Arvind Mayaram Committee**.
- **SEBI FPI Regulations, 2019** unified **FIIs, sub-accounts and QFIs**; the term FII has been replaced by FPI. FPI opened to India in **1992**.
- **Most unstable flows**: **FPI and NRI deposits** (interest-rate driven). Excess portfolio flows caused the **East Asian and Latin American crises**.
### 3. FORMS OF FOREIGN CAPITAL
- Foreign capital includes **FDI, trade credit, NRI deposits and ECBs** - all four (APPSC 2019).
| Form | Classification |
|---|---|
| FDI | Non-debt, long-term, control (≥10%) |
| FPI/FII | Non-debt, portfolio (<10%) |
| **ECB, trade credit, FCCB** | **Debt** |
| NRI deposits (FCNR(B), NRE) | Banking capital (debt) |
| **ADR/GDR** | Equity raised abroad = **Foreign Equity Investment**; counted as **portfolio** in BoP |
| **Participatory Notes** | **Offshore derivative instruments** |
| Masala bond | Rupee bond issued abroad |
- **P-Notes**: issued by registered **FIIs/FPIs** to overseas investors who get exposure to Indian stocks **without SEBI registration**.
- **FCNR(B)** replaced FCNR(A) on **15 May 1993**: fixed deposit held in foreign currency.
- Largest forex inflow in 2005-06 = portfolio investment; FII investment in calendar 2005 ≈ US $10.7 bn.
### 4. ROUTES AND POLICY BODIES
| Route | Approval |
|---|---|
| **Automatic** | **No prior RBI/Government approval**; post-facto reporting to RBI |
| **Government** | Prior approval of the concerned Ministry via the **FIF Portal** |
- **Negative-list** approach: **over 90%** of sectors automatic - not all.
| Act / body | Role |
|---|---|
| **FEMA 1999** | Replaced FERA 1973; **RBI regulates ECBs** under it |
| **FIPB** | **Abolished June 2017**; applications go to ministries via the FIF Portal (DPIIT) |
| **DPIIT** | **Nodal** for FDI policy and data; under Ministry of Commerce and Industry; Consolidated FDI Policy 2020 |
| **NSWS** | National Single Window System; FIF Portal part of it since **August 2022** |
| FEMA (Non-Debt Instruments) Rules 2019 | Amended to enable 100% insurance FDI |
### 5. SECTORAL CAPS
| Sector | Cap / route |
|---|---|
| **Insurance** | **100% automatic** (Union Budget **2025-26**; entire premium invested in India; subject to IRDAI) |
| **Defence** | **74% automatic**; up to 100% Government route |
| **Telecom** | 100% automatic |
| **Single-brand retail** | 100% automatic |
| **Multi-brand retail** | **51%, Government route** (in practice treated as barred) |
| **Private banks** | **74%** (49% automatic) |
| **Public sector banks** | **20%**, Government route |
| Civil aviation (scheduled) | 100% (49% automatic; NRIs 100% automatic) |
| **Pharma** | Greenfield 100% automatic; **brownfield 74%** automatic |
| Medical devices, NBFCs, construction, railway infrastructure, ports, civil aviation MRO | 100% automatic |
| Food products made in India - trading incl. e-commerce | 100%, Government route |
| Space | Up to 100% with conditions |
| Satellites, broadcasting, print media | Government route |
#### 5.1 History of caps
| Year | Change |
|---|---|
| 2001 | RBI new private bank guidelines: paid-up capital ₹200 crore → ₹300 crore in 3 years |
| 2001-02 | Tea: foreign firm to disinvest **26%** to an Indian partner in 5 years (not 33%) |
| 2003 PYQ | 49% FDI in private banks; raised to 74% in 2004 |
| Budget 2004-05 | Caps raised in telecom, insurance, civil aviation - **banking not revised** |
| 2006 | Single-brand retail up to 51% (first easing; retail opened - 2010 PYQ) |
| 2012 | Multi-brand 51%; single-brand 100%; insurance 26 → 49% proposed (4 Oct 2012) |
| Insurance | **26% → 49% (2015) → 74% (Budget 2021-22, from 19 May 2021) → 100% (2025)** |
- Post-2014: caps raised in defence, insurance, pension. Insurance = **Entry 47, Union List**; **IRDAI HQ Hyderabad**.
### 6. E-COMMERCE AND PROHIBITED SECTORS
| Model | FDI |
|---|---|
| **Marketplace** | **100% automatic** |
| **Inventory** | **Not allowed** (protect small traders, multi-brand curbs) |
- **July 2026**: DPIIT allowed inventory-model FDI where inventory is used to **export domestically manufactured goods**.
- **Prohibited**: **lottery**; gambling, betting, **casinos**; **chit funds**; **Nidhi companies**; trading in **TDRs**; real estate business and farmhouses; **cigars/cigarettes** of tobacco; **atomic energy**; **railway operations** (except permitted activities).
!TRAP Railway INFRASTRUCTURE is not prohibited - 100% automatic; only railway OPERATIONS are barred.
### 7. PRESS NOTE 3 - LAND-BORDER COUNTRIES
- **April 2020**: all FDI from a country sharing a **land border** needs **Government approval** (China not named).
- Countries: **China, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Myanmar, Bhutan, Afghanistan** - **Sri Lanka not covered**.
- **Amended March 2026, effective 1 May 2026** (Cabinet): firms with up to **10%** Chinese/Hong Kong beneficial ownership may use the **automatic route**; beneficial owner as per **PML Rules 2005**; not for entities registered in China, HK or another land-border country.
### 8. FDI TRENDS - SOURCES, SECTORS, STATES
#### 8.1 Flows
| Period | Figures |
|---|---|
| FY 2024-25 | Gross **$81 bn** (+14%); **net only $353 mn**; sources **Singapore (30%) > Mauritius (17%) > US (11%)** |
| FY 2025-26 | **Record gross $94.6 bn**; **net $7.6 bn**; net negative Sep 2025-Jan 2026; Feb 2026 net +$4.6 bn |

- About **$40 bn** of $560 bn equity "inflows" (2014-15 to 2025-26) were accounting changes; gross vs net debate (CEA vs critics). Manufacturing share of effective inflows **10.6%** (2026).

### 9. FPI FLOWS
- **G-sec limits (General Route)**: **6%** of Central G-secs, **2%** of State securities; also VRR and FAR routes.
- **Corporate bonds**: FPI limit raised to **15% (from 9%)** in Union Budget 2020-21.
- **JP Morgan EM bond index**: India's target weight **10%** → passive bond inflows.
- Drivers: **US Fed rates and global risk appetite** → "flight to safety" outflows.
- FY26 outflows about ₹2.2 lakh crore; net outflow $13.6 bn (March 2026). **Over ₹2 lakh crore** equity withdrawn by May 2026 (₹24,829 crore in May) → **rupee past 96/$**.
### 10. FTAs, CEPAs AND RCEP
| Agreement | Partner | Date |
|---|---|---|
| SAPTA | SAARC (7 members) | 1993 (in force 1995) |
| SAFTA | 8 SAARC members | In force 2006 |
| ASEAN-India FTA (goods) | ASEAN | In force 2010 |
| **CEPA** | **UAE** | In force **1 May 2022** |
| **ECTA** | **Australia** | In force 29 December 2022 |
| **TEPA** | **EFTA** | Signed 10 Mar 2024; in force **1 Oct 2025** |
| **CETA** | **UK** | Signed 24 July 2025; in force **15 July 2026** |
- **RCEP**: signed **15 Nov 2020** by **15** members (ASEAN 10 + China, Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand); in force **1 Jan 2022**.
- **India opted out in 2019**: China's market dominance (deficit **$53.5 bn in 2018-19 → $99.2 bn in 2024-25**), risks to farm, dairy and small industry.
