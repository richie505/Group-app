## U0602 | MAJOR LANDFORMS – GLACIAL, ARID/AEOLIAN, KARST AND COASTAL LANDFORMS
### 1. AGENT → LANDFORMS
| Agent | Landforms |
|---|---|
| Running water | Delta, V-valley, gorge, canyon, meander, alluvial fan, levee, **peneplain** |
| Glaciers | **Cirque**, arête, horn, U-valley, hanging valley, moraine, **till**, esker; fjord (with drowning) |
| Wind | **Sand dunes**, yardangs, mushroom rocks, loess |
| Karst (groundwater) | Sinkholes, doline, **stalactites**, stalagmites, blind valley |
| Waves | **Cliffs**, sea caves, arches, stacks; bars (waves + currents) |
| Longshore drift | Spits |
| Tectonic faulting | Rift valleys (Death Valley, Narmada), horsts, grabens |
!KEY APPSC-G2 2023 match: Delta – running water; Cirque – glaciers; Cliffs – waves and currents; Sand dunes – winds.
- **Esker** = glacial; yardang, mushroom rock = aeolian; **blind valley** = karst (not tectonic).
### 2. WEATHERING, MASS WASTING AND CYCLE OF EROSION
| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Physical | Exfoliation, frost wedging, block disintegration – no chemical change |
| Chemical | Solution, **carbonation**, **oxidation**, hydration, hydrolysis |
| Mass wasting (gravity) | Soil creep, slump, landslide, mudflow, rockfall |
- Oxidation is chemical weathering, **not** mass wasting.
- AP: **granite/gneiss → red soil**; **basalt → black soil**.
- **Cycle of erosion**: proposed by **W.M. Davis**; stages **youth → maturity → old age**; ends in a **peneplain** near sea level; renewed uplift = **rejuvenation**.
- **Peninsular Plateau** = rejuvenated peneplain.
### 3. FLUVIAL LANDFORMS AND DELTA TYPES
| Stage | Landforms |
|---|---|
| Youth (upper) | V-valley, gorge, **canyon** (APPSC 2022), waterfall, potholes, rapids |
| Mature (middle) | Meanders, floodplains, alluvial fans at hill foot |
| Old (lower) | **Oxbow lakes**, natural levees, braided channels, deltas |
- **Canyon**: step-like sides, wider at top, horizontally bedded sedimentary rock; **gorge**: hard rock, equal width.
- Oxbow lake = meander cut off.
- River terraces: vertical erosion into own floodplain; paired vs unpaired (slow uplift).
- Estuary: funnel mouth open to sea (ecotone); **Narmada and Tapi form estuaries**, not deltas.
| Delta type | Condition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Arcuate** | Sediment denser than sea water | Nile, Sundarbans |
| Cuspate | Density equal | Tiber |
| **Bird's foot** | Sediment lighter, carried far | **Mississippi** (Gulf of Mexico) |
### 4. REGIONAL PLAIN TERMS AND PLAIN TYPES
| Term | Meaning / region |
|---|---|
| **Bhabar** | Gravel-pebble belt, **8–16 km** wide, parallel to Shiwalik foothills; porous |
| **Terai** | Marshy forested belt south of Bhabar; streams reappear |
| **Bhangar** | Older alluvium above flood level, **with kankar** |
| **Khadar** | New floodplain alluvium, **no kankar** |
| **Doab** | Land between two rivers (Ganga-Yamuna Doab) |
| Prayag | Confluence: **Devprayag** = Bhagirathi + Alaknanda → Ganga |
| **Badland** | **Chambal** ravines; **Chos** of Hoshiarpur |
| **Karewas** | Lake deposits of Kashmir |
- Plain types: **alluvial** (Indo-Gangetic; youngest, most populous); **lacustrine** (Kashmir valley, **Imphal Basin**); **peneplain**; **till plains** (glacial).
- **Ganga Plain** = a **geosyncline** (trough filling with sediment). **Patland** (central India) = lava-capped mesas.
### 5. GLACIAL LANDFORMS
| Landform | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Cirque** (corrie) | Bowl/armchair hollow at valley head |
| **Arête** | Knife-edged ridge between two cirques |
| **Horn** | Sharp pyramidal peak (**Matterhorn**) |
| **U-shaped valley** | Glacier deepens and widens a river valley |
| **Hanging valley** | Tributary valley left high; joins by **waterfall**; agent = **glacier** (UKPSC 2024) |
| **Fjord** | Drowned glacial U-valley (Norway, Greenland, New Zealand) |
| **Moraine** | **Unsorted** debris (till): lateral, terminal, medial, ground |
| **Drumlin** | Egg-shaped till hill; field = "**basket of eggs**" |
| **Esker** | Winding ridge of **stratified** sediment from a sub-glacial tunnel |
!KEY APPSC 2017: **Till, moraine, boulder clay** = glacial activity.
!TRAP Moraine = unsorted; esker = stratified.
- **Hanging glaciers**: stranded on steep slopes as the main glacier thins; collapse → ice-rock avalanches, LLOFs, GLOFs (Nepal flash flood, 1 Sep 2026).
- From 2025 **CWC** monitors **2,843** glacial lakes/water bodies under NRSC's Glacial Lake Atlas (up from 902).
### 6. ARID (AEOLIAN) LANDFORMS
- Wind transport: deflation, **saltation**, surface creep.
| Landform | Key point |
|---|---|
| **Barchan** | Crescent dune, horns downwind |
| Seif | Long narrow dunes where sand is scarce |
| **Yardang** | Ridge **parallel to the wind** |
| Zeugen | **Table-like** ridge, hard cap over soft rock |
| **Mushroom rock** | Base abraded more than top |
| Deflation hollow | Wind removal |
| **Playa** | Dry salt lake bed after evaporation |
| **Loess** | Fine wind-blown silt (Loess Plateau, China) |
| **Inselberg** | Isolated residual hill (**Uluru**) |
| Mesa / butte | Flat-topped table hill / smaller remnant |
!TRAP A "table-like" description fits the zeugen, not the yardang.
### 7. KARST LANDFORMS
- **Karst**: groundwater dissolving **limestone/dolomite**; named after the Karst region (Slovenia).
- Rising size: **sinkhole → doline → uvala → polje**. **Sinkhole (doline)** = commonest karst landform.
- **Uvala** = joined dolines; **polje** = large hollow from cave collapse; **swallow hole** = stream sinks.
- **Blind valley**: ends suddenly where the stream sinks (steephead valley).
- **Stalactites hang from the roof** (APPSC 2022); **stalagmites rise from the floor**; meet → **pillars**. Formed by dissolving and re-depositing calcium carbonate.
- Longest cave: India **Krem Liat Prah** (Meghalaya); world **Mammoth Cave** (USA).
### 8. CAVES → PLACE → KARST OR NOT
| Cave | Place | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Belum** | **Nandyal** | Karst, black limestone; **2nd-longest** in India; found by Robert Bruce Foote (1884); GSI Geo-Heritage Site (Jun 2025) |
| **Borra** | **Ananthagiri hills, Araku (ASR district)** | Limestone karst; **Gosthani** flows through; found 1807; Mesolithic paintings |
| Undavalli | Near Vijayawada (Guntur) | Rock-cut **sandstone**, **Vishnukundins**; reclining Anantasayana Vishnu; **not karst** |
| Bojjannakonda | Sankaram, Anakapalli | Buddhist rock-cut caves and stupas; **not karst** |
| **Arwah** | Near Cherrapunji (Sohra), Meghalaya | Limestone with **fossils** (APPSC 2018) |
!KEY APPSC-G1 2017: Karst topography = **Belum** (not Undavalli, Bojjannakonda, Erravaram).
- **Billa Surgam**: **Kurnool/Nandyal**, not Anantapuramu – Palaeolithic cave with animal remains (APPSC-G2 2025).
- **Kethavaram** paintings (Kurnool): deer most painted; a half-painted deer found.
- **Gandikota** (YSR Kadapa): Penna gorge, "Grand Canyon of India".
### 9. COASTAL EROSION
- **Main agent: waves** (also tides, currents, tsunamis, humans).
- Processes: hydraulic action, abrasion, attrition, solution (**saltation is wind**).
- Sequence: **cliff + wave-cut platform → sea cave → arch → stack → stump**.
- Cave = weak zones eroded; arch = cave cuts a headland; stack = arch collapses.
- Sediment cut by dams, harbours, sand mining.
| Defence | Methods |
|---|---|
| Soft | **Beach nourishment**, dunes, mangroves |
| Hard | Seawalls, **groynes** (across longshore drift), breakwaters |
- East coast erodes more: soft low alluvium, no rocky cliffs. AP: severe at **Uppada (Kakinada)** and Krishna coast (Jul 2026).
### 10. COASTAL DEPOSITION AND COAST TYPES
| Landform | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Beach | Sand/shingle between tides |
| **Spit** | Ridge joined to land at one end |
| Bar | Ridge across a bay; closes it into a lagoon |
| **Lagoon** | Shallow water cut off by a bar (Chilika, **Pulicat**) |
| **Tombolo** | Bar joining an island to mainland |
- Pulicat = saltwater; **Kolleru** = freshwater.
| Coast | Formed by | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Fiord** | Drowned glacial valley | Norway |
| **Ria** | Drowned river valley; ranges **at right angles** | NW Spain |
| **Dalmatian** | Ranges **parallel** to coast; long islands | Adriatic (Croatia) |
- **Emergent coast**: **east coast** (Tamil Nadu/Coromandel) – broad plain, fewer ports. West coast mixed: northern part (**Konkan**) **submerged** by faulting; **Kerala** coast emergent.
- Geo-heritage: **Erra Matti Dibbalu** (red dunes, Visakhapatnam) + **Tirumala Silathoranam** (quartzite arch) on **UNESCO Tentative List (Sep 2025)**.
