### 2. NAGARJUNASAGAR-SRISAILAM TIGER RESERVE
- AP's **only** tiger reserve and **India's largest TR** by area; in the **Nallamala** hills, spread over **five districts** of AP and Telangana.
- Monsoon ban on safari vehicles (Jun 2026); peacock tarantula survey (1 May 2026).

- **Hope Island** (near Coringa) is threatened by a proposed satellite launch facility (Apr 2026).
### 5. MANGROVE AND ELEPHANT HABITATS
- Coringa and Krishna WLS together form the Godavari-Krishna mangroves; AP calls them India's 2nd-largest mangrove ecosystem, but **ISFR 2023 puts Gujarat second**.
- Godavari-estuary mangroves are protected by **Coringa**, not Sri Venkateswara NP.
- **Koundinya** (a sanctuary) is part of **Rayala Elephant Reserve** (AP's only elephant reserve).
### 6. KOLLERU RAMSAR SITE
- Ramsar site since **2002**; large **freshwater** lake between the **Godavari and Krishna deltas** (the brackish lagoon is **Pulicat**).
- Sanctuary **308.55 sq km**; spot-billed pelican breeding and migratory waterfowl.
- Threat: aquaculture tanks inside the sanctuary. **Operation Kolleru (2006)** removed illegal fish tanks up to the **+5 ft contour**.
- India has **100** Ramsar sites (2026).

### 8. ECO-SENSITIVE ZONES
- Notified by **MoEF&CC** under the **EP Act, 1986** (not WPA 1972).
- **Wildlife Action Plan 2002-16** suggested **10 km**; **Supreme Court (June 2022)**: minimum **1 km** ESZ mandatory.
