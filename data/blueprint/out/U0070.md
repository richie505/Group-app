## U0070 | PALAS OF BENGAL – GOPALA, DHARMAPALA, DEVAPALA; NALANDA AND VIKRAMASHILA, PALA ART
### 1. GOPALA – FOUNDER
- **Elected** by notables (mid-8th c.) to end **matsyanyaya** (anarchy, law of the jungle); born near **Pundravardhana** (per Taranatha).
- Source: **Khalimpur inscription of Dharmapala** records his election.
- Replaced the later Guptas of Magadha and the **Khadgas** of east Bengal; built **Odantapuri**.
!KEY Kings chosen by the people: **Gopala** and **Nandivarman II Pallavamalla**. Dharmapala, Devapala, Narasimhavarman I, Mahendravarman I, Harsha, Yashovarman inherited.
### 2. LATER PALA RULERS
| Ruler | Achievement |
|---|---|
| **Dharmapala** (c. 770–810) | Founded **Vikramashila** and **Somapura**; installed Chakrayudha at Kannauj; defeated by Dhruva and by Nagabhata II near **Monghyr** |
| **Devapala** | Gave **five villages** for the Nalanda monastery of **Balaputradeva** (Nalanda copper plate) |
| **Mahipala I** (c. 977–1027) | Revived Pala power; defeated by **Rajendra Chola** |
| **Ramapala** (c. 1072–1126) | Last strong ruler; **Kaivarta revolt**; Kamarupa, Kalinga; **Jagaddala** |
- Palas = **last great royal patrons of Buddhism** in India; took it to Tibet and SE Asia.
### 3. MAHAVIHARAS – FOUNDER AND LOCATION
| Mahavihara | Founder | Present location |
|---|---|---|
| **Nalanda** | **Kumaragupta I** (Gupta, 5th c. AD) | Nalanda, Bihar (ancient Magadha); Pala patronage |
| **Odantapuri** | **Gopala** | **Bihar Sharif**, Bihar; ~12,000 monks; model for Tibetan monasteries |
| **Vikramashila** | **Dharmapala** | **Antichak, Bhagalpur** district, Bihar, on the Ganga |
| **Somapura** | Dharmapala | **Paharpur, Naogaon**, Bangladesh |
| **Jagaddala** | **Ramapala** | **Varendra**, Naogaon, Bangladesh |
- Together = **Panchamahavihara** (Tibetan sources).
- Vikramashila: centre of **Tantric/Vajrayana** Buddhism and logic; trained monks for Tibet.
- Nalanda texts compiled under Dharmapala; Jagaddala translated books into **Tibetan**.
- Destroyed by **Bakhtiyar Khilji**, c. 1193–1200 (1194 for Nalanda in one tradition); Jagaddala looted early 13th c.
- **Nava Nalanda Mahavihara**: **Bhikshu Jagadish Kashyap**; foundation **20 November 1951**.
!TRAP Vikramashila is in **Bhagalpur** (not Banka) and was founded by **Dharmapala** (not Gopala).
### 4. ODD-ONE-OUT – CENTRES OF LEARNING
- Early medieval (Pala) centres: Nalanda, Vikramashila, Odantapuri, Somapura.
- **Taxila** = odd one out: early Buddhist centre, capital of **Gandhara** (Rawalpindi district, Pakistan); students – Chanakya, Chandragupta Maurya, Charaka, Jivaka.
- **Valabhi** = **Hinayana** centre of Harsha's time; Buddhist-period centres: Nalanda, Vikramashila, Valabhi.
### 5. ACHARYAS AND TIBET
| Acharya | Institution | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Shilabhadra** | Nalanda | Head in Harsha's time |
| **Shantarakshita** | Nalanda | Founded **Samye** monastery in Tibet |
| **Atisha Dipankara Srijnana** | **Vikramashila** | Went to Tibet (11th c.), revived Buddhism |
| **Sakya Sri Bhadra** | **Jagaddala** | Took Tantric Buddhism to Tibet |
- **Kautilya** was NOT a Nalanda acharya.
- AP angle: Andhra's **Dharmakirti** taught at Nalanda; studied under **Dharmapala of Kanchi**, head of Nalanda (not the Pala king).
### 6. SOUTHEAST ASIA
- **Balaputradeva**: **Shailendra** ruler of **Suvarnadvipa / Suvarnabhumi**; built a monastery at Nalanda; his envoy got **five villages** from Devapala.
### 7. PALA ART AND LITERATURE
| Form | Feature |
|---|---|
| Sculpture | **Bronze** and **black-stone (basalt)** |
| Painting | **Palm-leaf** illustrated Buddhist manuscripts |
| Terracotta | **Somapura** – grand scale; Vikramashila wall panels |
- Artists: **Dhiman** and son **Vitapala** (recorded by **Taranatha**).
- **Ramacharita** – **Sandhyakar Nandi**; on Ramapala and the **Kaivarta** peasant revolt.

### 11. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS

1. **Odantapuri** = Gopala; **Vikramashila** = Dharmapala; **Jagaddala** = Ramapala.
2. Nalanda = **Kumaragupta I**, not a Pala.
3. **Taxila** is not early medieval.
4. **Atisha** = Vikramashila; **Shantarakshita** = Nalanda, Samye.
5. **Dharmapala of Kanchi** ≠ Pala Dharmapala.
6. Mahipala I lost to **Rajendra Chola**.
7. **Ramacharita** = Sandhyakar Nandi.
8. Devapala, not Dharmapala, granted villages to Balaputradeva.
