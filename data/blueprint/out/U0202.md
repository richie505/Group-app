## U0202 | GROWTH OF THE NATIONALIST MOVEMENT IN ANDHRA, 1885-1905
### 1. OVERVIEW
- **Moderates, district associations and the press** built Andhra's political base before the **Vande Mataram movement (1905)**.
- **Krishna district led**: first district association (1892) and **Krishna Patrika** (1902).
!NUM Order: Madras Mahajana Sabha (1884) → first INC (1885) → first Telugu INC President (1891) → Krishna District Association (1892) → Krishna Patrika (1902)

### 3. ANDHRA AT THE EARLY INC SESSIONS
#### 3.1 First Session, Bombay 1885
- Andhra delegates: **P. Anandacharlu (Chittoor)**, P. Rangaiah Naidu, **Nyapathi Subbarao (Rajahmundry)**, Gutti Kesava Pillai (Gooty), Venkata Subbarayudu (Machilipatnam).
- Demands: **simultaneous ICS exams** in England and India; **elected legislative councils**; **military cuts**; **opposed annexation of Upper Burma**.
#### 3.2 INC Session Presided by an Andhra
| Session | Year | President |
|---|---|---|
| **7th, Nagpur** | **1891** | **P. Anandacharlu** – **first Telugu INC president** |
- Madras session 1914: **256 Andhra delegates**.
### 4. ANDHRA SPOKESMEN – MODERATE DEMANDS
| Person | Role |
|---|---|
| **P. Anandacharlu** | Secretary, Madras Mahajana Sabha (1884); attended 1st INC; president Nagpur 1891 |
| **Nyapathi Subbarao** | Andhra spokesman; titles **Rajabahadur, Padyavinoda**; Rajahmundry lawyer; **founded the Godavari District Association** |
| **Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao** | Later: **data-led drain critique of Madras against Andhra**, **7th Andhra Mahasabha, Guntur, 1919** |
- Andhra Moderates repeated the INC's **economic critique** (drain of wealth) and constitutional demands.
### 5. DISTRICT ASSOCIATIONS OF ANDHRA
#### 5.1 Krishna District Association (1892)
- **First district association in the Madras Presidency** (APPSC-GS 2021).
- Formed **on lines suggested at the first INC session**.
- Founder: **Ramaswamy Gupta**; aim – to fight British rule on social and public issues.
- Conference presided by **Nyapathi Subbarao**; about **223 delegates** → led to other district bodies.
- **First meeting: Machilipatnam** (APPSC 2012 key).
- Its **1899 resolution** led to **Krishna Patrika (1902)**.
!TRAP Some notes place the first conference at **Guntur (July 1892)** and the second at Machilipatnam (1893) – mark **Machilipatnam** as the APPSC key does.
#### 5.2 Godavari District Association
- Founded by **Nyapathi Subbarao** at **Rajahmundry** in **1895**, a year after he presided over the Krishna District Association session (1894).

### 6. EARLY NATIONALIST PRESS OF ANDHRA
| Paper | Founder / detail |
|---|---|
| **Satyadoota** | **First Telugu journal** – **Bellary Christian mission** |
| **Andhra Prakasika** | **First political magazine in Andhra** (APPSC 2012); Telugu news weekly from **Madras**, **A.P. Parthasarathi Naidu**; paired with **P. Anandacharyulu** as correct (APPSC 2019); supported the Congress |
| **Krishna Patrika** | **2 Feb 1902**, **Machilipatnam**; **Konda Venkatappayya** and **Dasu Narayana Rao**; later edited by **Mutnuri Krishna Rao** |

- Role: journals **criticised administrative failures** and spread nationalism and democracy.
!TRAP First Telugu journal = **Satyadoota**; first political magazine = **Andhra Prakasika**.
### 7. LOCAL POLITICS AND 1905
- **Rajahmundry**: Nyapathi Subbarao was its leading municipal figure and founder of the Hindu Samaj there.
- **Machilipatnam**: seat of the Krishna District Association meetings and Krishna Patrika.
- **Guntur**: district carved from Krishna in **1904**; Konda Venkatappayya moved there.
- **1905**: Moderate-Extremist divide; Andhras **opposed the transfer of Ganjam to Orissa** – the "**first victory**" of the Andhra movement.
