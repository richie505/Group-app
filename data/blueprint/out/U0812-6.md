### 2. NTR BHAROSA SOCIAL SECURITY PENSIONS
#### 2.1 Category → monthly pension
- **NTR Bharosa** (renamed from **YSR Pension Kanuka**) is run through **SERP**.
| Category | Monthly pension |
|---|---|
| **Old age (60+), widows (18+)**, weavers, toddy tappers, fishermen, single women, transgender persons, artists, others | **₹4,000** (raised from **₹3,000**, **April 2024**) |
| **Disabled (40%+ disability)**, leprosy | **₹6,000** |
| **Dialysis / CKDU** patients | **₹10,000** |
| **Fully bedridden** (bilateral elephantiasis, paralysis) | **₹15,000** (from ₹5,000) |
- **New in 2026**: **spouse pensions** for surviving wives; pensions for **dialysis and organ-transplant** patients (added, not abolished).
- Delivery: **at the doorstep on the 1st of every month** (**'Pedala Sevalo'** day).
#### 2.2 Outlay data
| Period | Outlay |
|---|---|
| **May 2025** | **₹2,758 crore a month** |
| June 2026 | ₹2,711.79 crore for 62.19 lakh |
| **1 September 2026** | **₹2,693 crore** to **61.92 lakh** beneficiaries |
| Cumulative since 2024 (as of 31 Aug 2026) | **₹73,506 crore** |
| **Budget 2026-27** (annual) | **₹27,719 crore** |
- The APPSC 2025 question on monthly pension spending (May 2025) was **voided** by APPSC (discrepancy in question/answer); there is no official key.
- **Disability pension**: AP = **India's highest** (up to ₹15,000) vs **Bihar ₹1,100** (Jun 2026).
- **16th Finance Commission**: AP has the **highest per-capita pension spending** among States.
!NUM ₹2,758 crore = May 2025; ₹2,693 crore = Sep 2026; ₹27,719 crore = Budget 2026-27 (annual).
### 3. HEALTH SCHEMES
#### 3.1 Dr NTR Vaidya Seva
- Renamed from **(Rajiv/YSR) Aarogyasri**; run by the **Dr NTR Vaidya Seva Trust**.
- **Cashless care** mainly for **BPL** families: **1.43 crore families** (1.43 crore health cards, APSES 2024-25); **3,257 procedures** (2024-25).
- **5 more cardiac procedures** added (Mar 2026).
- Opposition (Mar 2026) called the **₹2.5 lakh insurance plan** a **threat to Aarogyasri**.
#### 3.2 Other schemes under the Trust
| Scheme | For |
|---|---|
| **Employees Health Scheme** | government employees |
| **Working Journalists Health Scheme** | journalists |
| **Aarogya Raksha** | **APL** families |
| **Amrutha** | **orphans** (not marine fishermen) |
#### 3.3 SHRESTH
- **SHRESTH = State Health Regulatory Excellence Index** (**Aug 2025**): strengthens the health regulatory system by **benchmarking State drug regulators**; **MoHFW**, run by **CDSCO**.
