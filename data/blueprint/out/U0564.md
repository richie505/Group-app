## U0564 | APPROACHES AND INITIATIVES IN AP AGRICULTURE – APCNF/ZBNF, HORTICULTURE, AQUACULTURE, ALLIED SECTORS
### 1. AP AGRICULTURE – PROFILE, LAND USE AND BUDGET
#### 1.1 Significance and Land Use
- Agriculture supports about **60% of population**; agriculture and allied = **33% of GVA** (2025-26).
- Geographical area **162.97 lakh ha**; net sown area **59.14 lakh ha = 36.29%** (2024-25); **49.55 lakh ha = 30.40%** (2023-24, drought).
- NSA fell from 67.26 lakh ha (2008-09) to 49.55 lakh ha (2023-24).
- **NITI Aayog: cultivated area 10.1 million ha (2020-21) = 37% of geographical area** (PYQ 2023).
- **Cropping intensity** (GCA / NSA): **1.25** (2016-17); **1.21** (2023-24).
!TRAP NSA 36.29% (2024-25) vs 30.40% (2023-24) vs cultivated 37% (NITI Aayog 2020-21): match the figure to its year and report.
#### 1.2 Operational Holdings
- Average holding in AP **0.94 ha** (Agriculture Census 2015-16) vs India 1.08 ha; small and marginal (0-2 ha) dominate (86.21% nationally).
#### 1.3 Separate Agriculture Budget
- **First separate agriculture budget: undivided AP, 2013**.
- **Agriculture and Allied Budget 2026-27: ~Rs 53,753 crore**, presented by **K. Atchannaidu** for the **third time**: irrigation Rs 14,277 crore, power subsidy Rs 13,722 crore, Price Stabilisation Fund Rs 500 crore.
- Credit plan 2025-26: Rs 2,96,764 crore = 97% of target.
- **AP State Agriculture Mission** (G.O.Ms.No.57, **1 July 2019**): **Chairman = Chief Minister**; Vice-Chairman **M.V.S. Nagi Reddy** (farmer, aquaculturist).
### 2. CROPPING PATTERN, CROPS AND REGIONS
#### 2.1 Food Grains and Paddy
- **Paddy = largest area of any crop** (PYQ 2018): **20.97 lakh ha of 37.51 lakh ha** food-grain area (~**56%**, 2024-25); output 127.15 lakh t (kharif 78.79 + rabi 48.36); yield ~6.1 t/ha.
- **Rice yield above national average**: AP **3,392 kg/ha** vs India **2,798** (2021-22); 3,846 vs 2,844 (2022-23, 3rd AE).
- Food-grain area: 41.44 lakh ha (2021-22) → 33.24 (2023-24) → 37.51 (2024-25); output 161.86 lakh t.
- **Food-grain area up 12.85%** in 2024-25 after an **11.7% rainfall surplus** (previous year **16.5% deficit**).
- **Cotton ("white gold")**: commercial crop with the **largest area expansion** (PYQ 2022).
#### 2.2 Kharif vs Rabi and Drought 2026
- Kharif 2026: all 28 districts in deficit; only ~61% of normal area sown by 19 Aug (groundnut 34%, paddy 62%).
- **El Nino action plan (Jul 2026)**: control room, RSK advisories, shift to **red gram, black gram, castor**, **one Mobile Rythu Bazaar per district** (no compulsory shift to sugarcane).
- **Highest Kharif MSP 2026-27: sesamum Rs 10,346/qtl** (paddy Rs 2,441).
#### 2.3 Crop → Region

#### 2.4 Cropping Pattern Shifts
- Shift to **fruits, vegetables, flowers, spices** (profitability, export potential).
- Godavari delta **paddy land converted to aquaculture**.
- **Bondalu paddy** (Kakinada, Dec 2019): farmers advised to stop because the earlier buyer **Kerala** began growing it (PYQ 2018).
- Debate: paddy is a major **methane** source; experts want diversification with procurement support.
#### 2.5 Growth Engines (Swarna Andhra @2047)
- APSES 2024-25: **30% growth over five years** through **17 growth engines**.
- **11 productivity crops**: paddy, maize, jowar, bajra, ragi, redgram, blackgram, bengalgram, groundnut, sunflower, sesamum (**not sugarcane**).

### 5. FARMER SERVICE AND EXTENSION INSTITUTIONS
#### 5.1 Rythu Bazars
- **Started 1999**; farmers sell **directly to consumers**; **129** functioning.
- Run by an **Estate Officer** (Revenue Dept); a regulated yard by a **Market Committee**.
- Prices ~**25% above wholesale, below local retail**.
!TRAP "District Collector runs/coordinates Rythu Bazars" = false; it is the Estate Officer.
#### 5.2 Rythu Bharosa Kendras → Rythu Seva Kendras
- **RBKs launched 30 May 2020**; **10,778** village-level single-window centres beside village secretariats (SES 2023-24; asked twice): inputs, advisory, market linkage.
- Now **Rythu Seva Kendras**, rationalised to **8,489** functional (also cited 10,606).
- RSK services: **soil testing, e-Panta, CCRC issue, input subsidies, crop insurance, drone awareness, procurement** (**not industrial licences**).
- **Integrated Call Centre**: toll-free **155251**, May 2020, at **Gannavaram**; RBK Channel and Studio there (Mar 2021).
#### 5.3 ATMA and Polambadi
- **ATMA** (Agricultural Technology Management Agency): **centrally sponsored 60:40**; district-level autonomous body (Governing Board, Management Committee, Farmer Interest Groups); prepares **SREP** (Strategic Research and Extension Plan); all 26 districts.
- **Polambadi = Farmer Field Schools**; **GAP Polambadi** teaches Good Agricultural Practices; linked to **Ind.GAP** certification.
- Telugu farm monthly **Vyavasayam** is published by **ANGRAU**.
#### 5.4 Soil Health and e-Crop
- **Soil Health Cards** (under RKVY): promote **judicious use of fertilisers**; 5.52 lakh samples tested (2025-26).
- **e-Crop booking** (at RSKs): digital crop record; basis for **insurance, MSP procurement, input subsidy, loss assessment**.
### 6. TENANCY – CCRC
- **CCRC** = Crop Cultivator Rights Card under **AP Crop Cultivator Rights Act, 2019**; **valid 11 months**; issued from 2 Oct 2019.
- Gives tenants **loans, insurance, MSP, scheme access**; **no permanent ownership**; landlord's title unaffected.
- 8,24,620 active CCRCs (2025-26). **New AP Tenancy Act, 2024**: only planned.
- Debate: gap between CCRCs issued and holders actually financed.
### 7. NATURAL FARMING – ZBNF TO APCNF
#### 7.1 Origin and Agency
- **ZBNF** developed **mid-1990s** by **Subhash Palekar** (Maharashtrian agriculturist, **Padma Shri**).
- "**Zero budget**" = **no purchased fertilisers or pesticides** (not zero cost for all inputs or labour).
- Features: **minimum tillage, livestock integration, crop diversity, no chemicals**.
- AP launched ZBNF in **2016**; renamed **APCNF = Andhra Pradesh Community-managed Natural Farming**: the Agriculture Department's natural-farming initiative.
- **RySS (Rythu Sadhikara Samstha)**: **not-for-profit company** of the **AP Department of Agriculture**; incorporated **October 2014**; implements APCNF; earlier ran the Debt Redemption Scheme 2014.
!TRAP RySS 2014; ZBNF in AP 2016; ZBNF developed mid-1990s.
#### 7.2 Practices
| Practice | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Beejamrutham** | **Seed treatment** |
| **Jeevamrutham** | Bio-fertiliser: cow dung, urine, jaggery, pulse flour, soil |
| **Ghanajeevamrutham** | **Solid form** of Jeevamrutham |
| **Mulching** (Acchadana) | Crop residue/straw cover: **saves soil moisture, checks weeds** |
| **PMDS** | **Pre-Monsoon Dry Sowing** on residual moisture |
| **365-day green cover** | Soil covered all year by cover crops |
- Trap crops: marigold, castor. PMDS on 92,000 acres in Kurnool (Jul 2026).
#### 7.3 Coverage and Targets
- Now: **10.20 lakh farmers, 4.89 lakh ha**, 4,116 GPs.
- Targets: **all GPs by 2026-27**; **41 lakh farmers on 19 lakh ha by 2028-29**; "100% natural farming state" by 2047.
- Unit = **cluster of 5 villages (1,500-2,500 acres)**.
#### 7.4 Funding Partners
- **KfW** (German development bank): loan for expansion; **Azim Premji Foundation**: technical support grants.
#### 7.5 Awards and Replication
| Award | Detail |
|---|---|
| **M.S. Swaminathan Award for Environment Protection** | To RySS (2022-23) |
| **Gulbenkian Prize for Humanity** (shared) | **2024**; announced **Lisbon, 11 July 2024** |
| **Food Planet Prize 2026** | **US$1.5 million**, **Curt Bergfors Foundation**; **Bastad, Sweden**, June 2026; APCNF **first Indian finalist** |
- **ISHA Foundation awards** (Paddy and Food Mahotsav, Sripuram, Vellore, 28 July 2024): **4 AP natural farmers** from **Guntur, Krishna (Ranjith Kumar), West Godavari (G. Ramu), Srikakulam**.
- APCNF = "**world's largest agro-ecological transition**"; copied in other States and in **Zambia and Sri Lanka**; Kenyan and Gujarat delegations visited Guntur (2026).
#### 7.6 NMNF vs PKVY
- **NMNF** (natural farming) action plans: **7.78 lakh ha, 19.75 lakh farmers**.
- **PKVY** = **organic** farming; clusters of **50 farmers / 50 acres**; certified under **PGS-India**; 200 tribal clusters in Visakhapatnam.

### 8. HORTICULTURE AND OIL PALM
#### 8.1 Size and Ranks
- Area **46.43 lakh acres**, output **406.38 lakh MT** (Survey 2025-26).
- **Horticulture GVA Rs 1,52,950 crore**: AP **3rd** after **UP and Maharashtra**; **21% growth** (2024-25).
- Target: **Rs 3 lakh crore** horticulture GVA by 2029-30; **22 growth-engine crops**; **11 crops in 24 clusters**.
- **AP 1st in fruits** (15.6% of India).
- **Rayalaseema** = horticulture hub: ~**7.8 lakh ha**, ~**45%** of area, **52% of output**, 71% of micro-irrigation area.
| Rank | Crops |
|---|---|
| 1st in productivity | **Oil palm**, papaya, lime, cocoa, tomato, coconut |
| 2nd | **Mango** (production and productivity), sweet orange, turmeric; chillies 2nd (2025-26) |
!KEY APPSC G2 2025: "AP 1st in oil palm productivity" and "AP 2nd in mango productivity" are both correct.
#### 8.2 Oil Palm
- AP **1st in area, production, productivity**: **2.49 lakh ha = 48% of India**; FFB 22.81 lakh MT.
- Palm oil production **17.81 lakh tonnes** (2017-18).
- **NMEO-OP (2021)**: add **6.5 lakh ha** to reach **10 lakh ha**; CPO **11.20 lakh t by 2025-26**, **28 lakh t by 2029-30**.
- Missions: Oilseeds Technology Mission (**1986**) → **NMOOP (2014)** → **NMEO-OP (2021)**.
- Edible-oil import dependence: **63.2% (2015-16) → 56.25% (2023-24)**.
#### 8.3 MIDH and NHM
- **NHM**: started **May 2005** (2005-06); selected districts of **18 States and 5 UTs** (covers UP); **NE and Himalayan States not under NHM** (separate mission).
- NHM objectives: high horticulture growth, post-harvest management, human resources (**financial assistance not an objective**).
- **MIDH** (2014-15): merged **NHM and HMNEH**; funding **60:40** general, **90:10** NE/Himalayan, **100% central for NHB, CDB, CIH**.
#### 8.4 Micro-irrigation and Post-harvest
- **APMIP** since **2003-04**: **37.90 lakh acres, 13.49 lakh farmers**; 75% drip; **71% in Rayalaseema**.
- Targets: **35% of GSA by 2030**, **50% by 2047**, **15 lakh ha in five years**.
- Post-harvest target 2025-26: **1,030 units** (ripening chambers, cold storages, pack houses, collection centres).
- **Operation Greens (TOP)**: tomato, onion, potato; based on **Operation Flood**.
- Value-chain study crops: **mango, banana, tomato, pomegranate, sweet lime, papaya** (**not oil palm**).

#### 9.4 Credit, Insurance and Central Schemes

- **AHIDF** (run by **DAHD**): dairy and meat processing, feed plants, breed farms, waste-to-wealth, vaccine plants (**not irrigation canals**).
- **Credit Guarantee Scheme for livestock**: first ever, launched by **DAHD** (2023) under AHIDF; Rs 750 crore fund.

- **NLM** (2014-15, revised 2021-22): breed, feed and fodder, insurance.
- **Pashu Aadhaar** = **12-digit ear-tag** ID; **1962 app** replaced **e-Gopala**.
### 10. FISHERIES AND AQUACULTURE
#### 10.1 Output and Ranks
- **Coastline 1,053 km** (APSES 2025-26); older figure **974 km**.
- AP ~29-30% of India's fish; output 55.39 lakh t (2024-25). Inland fisheries dominate (inland ~82%, marine ~18% in 2017-18).
- **Shrimp**: AP **India's largest producer**.
- Aquaculture: core of AP's **Blue Economy**; **5 lakh+ families**; depends on foreign markets incl. US.
#### 10.2 Seafood Exports
| Share | Base |
|---|---|
| **36%** | India's **seafood export value**, end of 2018-19 (APPSC 2021) |
| **31%** | India's **shrimp** exports (2024-25) |
| **75%** | India's **cultured-shrimp** exports (2025-26) |
- **MPEDA**: apex export body under **Ministry of Commerce**; arms **RGCA, NETFISH, NaCSA**.
- **China = 2nd-largest** destination (2025-26), up **20.1%** by volume; record exports Rs 73,890 crore.
- **2019**: AP shrimp exports hit by **fall in international shrimp prices**.
#### 10.3 Regulation and Infrastructure
- **APSADA** (AP State Aquaculture Development Authority) **Act, 2020**: licenses **freshwater** aquaculture via **e-Matsyakar**.
- Farms **within 2 km of High Tide Line** → **Coastal Aquaculture Authority** (**CAA Act, 2005**).
- **Aqua-power tariff**: **Rs 1.50/unit** up to **10 acres** inside notified aqua zones; **Rs 3.85** for bigger farms or outside (10-acre cap lifted Aug 2026).
- **India's first Mega Aqua Food Park**: **Godavari Mega Aqua Food Park, Tundurru, West Godavari**.
- **10 fishing harbours** (Rs 3,699.09 crore) under **CSS-PMMSY** (**not Sagarmala**).
#### 10.4 PMMSY and PM-MKSSY
- **PMMSY (2020)**: **Rs 20,050 crore** (Blue Revolution); subsidy **40% general** (24% GoI + 16% GoAP), **60% women/SC/ST** (36% + 24%).
- **FIDF corpus Rs 7,522 crore**.
- **PM-MKSSY**: **central sector sub-scheme** of PMMSY; **2023-24 to 2026-27**; **Rs 6,000 crore**; institutional credit, aquaculture insurance, supply-chain traceability (**no compulsory trawler replacement**).
#### 10.5 Fishermen Welfare
- **Marine fishing ban: 61 days, 15 April – 14 June** (breeding season).
| Period | Ban relief per family |
|---|---|
| Before 2019-20 | Rs 4,000 |
| From 2019-20 | Rs 10,000 |
| From 2024-25 | **Rs 20,000** |
- **Ex-gratia Rs 10 lakh** to deceased fisherman's family (2024-25: Rs 315 lakh to 63 families). KCC to fishers since 2018-19.
