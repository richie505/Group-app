### 1. NATIONAL CONSTITUTION DAY (SAMVIDHAN DIVAS) 2025
#### 1.1 The Day
- Observed on **26 November**; 26 November 2025 marked **76 years** since the adoption of the Constitution.
- Declared in **2015**, the **125th birth anniversary year of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar**.
- Earlier observed as **Law Day**.
!TRAP Constitution Day is **not a public holiday**.
#### 1.2 Constitution: Key Dates
| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| First meeting of the Constituent Assembly | **9 December 1946** |
| Drafting Committee constituted (Chairman **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar**) | **29 August 1947** |
| Draft Constitution introduced | **4 November 1948** |
| Constitution adopted | **26 November 1949** (→ Samvidhan Divas) |
| Constitution came into force | **26 January 1950** |
- Time taken to frame the Constitution: **2 years, 11 months, 18 days**.
!TRAP The Drafting Committee was set up on **29 August 1947**, not on 26 November 1949 (that is the date of adoption).

### 3. 150 YEARS OF 'VANDE MATARAM'
#### 3.1 News
- PM Modi marked 150 years of Vande Mataram in the **127th Mann Ki Baat** (**26 October 2025**).
- 150th-year celebration runs from **7 November 2025** to **7 November 2026** (November 2025 – November 2026).
#### 3.2 History of the Song
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Written by | **Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay**, **1875** |
| First published | Literary journal **Bangadarshan**, 7 November 1875 |
| Novel | Later included in **'Anandamath' (1882)** |
| Language | **Sanskritised Bengali** |
| First sung | By **Rabindranath Tagore** at the **1896 INC session, Calcutta** |
| Swadeshi Movement (1905–1911) | Rallying cry of the movement |
| National Song | **First two stanzas** adopted; declared on **24 January 1950** |
| Meaning | "I bow to thee, Mother" |
!TRAP Declared National Song on **24 January 1950**, not 26 January 1950.
!TRAP The meaning "I bow to thee, Mother" does not by itself explain why it became the rallying cry of the Swadeshi Movement (assertion-reason trap).
### 4. SWADESHI IDEA AND NATIONAL HANDLOOM DAY
#### 4.1 National Handloom Day – 7 August
- Observed on **7 August** because the **Swadeshi Movement was launched on 7 August 1905**.
#### 4.2 Evolution of Swadeshi
| Period | Person / event | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| **1822** | Bengali weekly **Samachar Chandrika** | Use Indian goods for religious/social reasons |
| 19th century | **Gopal Hari Deshmukh** (1823–1892), **Rajnarayan Basu** (1826–1899) | Locally produced goods |
| Mid-19th century | **M.G. Ranade** (1842–1901) | Prefer domestic goods even if costlier |
| Late 19th century | **Dadabhai Naoroji** – Drain of Wealth; **R.C. Dutt** – *The Economic History of India* | Economic nationalism, deindustrialisation |
| 1905–1911 | Swadeshi Movement | Vande Mataram the rallying cry |
| Gandhi | Khadi, charkha | Economic + moral + cultural swadeshi; village self-sufficiency |
| Post-1947 | Mixed economy, import substitution | Support to small-scale industry |
!TRAP Gandhi's swadeshi was **not purely economic** – it was economic, moral and cultural.
!PAIR Economic nationalism | R.C. Dutt | The Economic History of India || Dadabhai Naoroji | Drain of Wealth | economic drain
### 5. INTERNATIONAL DAYS AND THEIR 2025 THEMES
#### 5.1 World Patient Safety Day – 17 September
- Observed by **WHO** on **17 September** every year.
- 2025 theme: **"Safe care for every newborn and every child"**.
- Harm affects **1 in 10** hospitalised patients; **4 in 10** in outpatient care.
- India: **National Patient Safety Implementation Framework (2018–2025)**; NABH accreditation – fewer than **5%** of hospitals fully accredited; **Society of Pharmacovigilance, India**.
- **Patient Advisory Councils (PACs)** are recommended by the **WHO Global Patient Safety Action Plan** – not yet widely implemented in India.
#### 5.2 World Food Day 2025 – 80 Years of India–FAO Partnership
- World Food Day (October) 2025: India and **FAO** marked **80 years** of partnership.
- India is a **founding member of FAO (1945)**.
- Book launched: **"Sowing Hope, Harvesting Success"**.
- India has **less than 4%** of the world's agricultural land and freshwater; **NFSA (2013)** covers **800 million+** people.

### 8. HERITAGE: ASI AND PPP CONSERVATION
#### 8.1 Archaeological Survey of India
- Under the **Ministry of Culture**; governed by the **AMASR Act, 1958**.
- Manages **3,650+** monuments of national importance.
- Founded **1861** by **Alexander Cunningham** – first Director-General, "**Father of Indian Archaeology**"; ASI strengthened (empowered) by **Lord Curzon**.
#### 8.2 PPP in Heritage Conservation
- PPP successes: **Elephanta Caves** and **Dr Bhau Daji Lad Museum, Mumbai**.
- **INTACH** and **ASI** are central to heritage management.
### 9. AP INSCRIPTIONS, ANNIVERSARIES AND FESTIVALS
#### 9.1 Srisailam Copper Plates (1420 CE)
- Date: **Saka 1341, Sarvari, Magha Sudha Trayodasi = 28 January 1420 CE** – **15th century** CE.
- Format: **three copper leaves**, bilingual – **Sanskrit and Telugu**.
- Term: **'Swarajya Punarudhharana'** (restoration of independent state) – **first use of 'Swarajya' in a Telugu inscription** (per Dr K. Munirathnam Reddy, Director (Epigraphy), ASI Mysore).
- Ruler: **Recharla Lingama Nayaka** (Recharla dynasty) – reclaimed areas from the **Turakas**; restored **eight agraharas**.
- Freedom-movement link: **Bal Gangadhar Tilak** – "Swarajya is my birthright"; **Tanguturi Prakasam** started the newspaper **'Swarajya'** in **three languages**.
!TRAP Headlines said "14th century", but 1420 CE is the **15th century**.
#### 9.2 Vankeswara Temple Inscription, Durgi (1518 CE)
- Where: **Vankeswara temple**, **Durgi** mandal HQ, **Palnadu district**; the inscription is **sinking**.
- Date: **22 November 1518 CE**, reign of **Sri Krishnadevaraya** (Vijayanagara).
- Commissioned by **Timmapa Nayanim Garu**, a **doorkeeper** in the royal court.
- Inspected by **E. Sivanagireddy**, CEO, **Pleach India Foundation**; tip from sculptor **Ch. Srinivasachary**.
#### 9.3 Sri Modakondamma Jatara, Paderu
- Where: **Modakondamma Temple, Paderu**, **Alluri Sitharama Raju district**, in the **Eastern Ghats**.
- When: annual tribal festival in **May** – 2025 on **11, 12, 13 May**.
- Deity: **Grama Devatha**, a form of **Goddess Durga**; revered by **Kondhs and Koyas**.
