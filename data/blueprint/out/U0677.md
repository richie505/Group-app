### 2. COTTON TEXTILES
- Began near **black-soil cotton tracts** of Maharashtra and Gujarat; then **dispersed** because cotton is **not weight-losing** (pure material) and markets, power and rail spread.
- **Coimbatore** (TN) spinning: **hydel power, cheap labour, local cotton**.
- Centres: Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Surat, Sholapur, Nagpur, Indore, Coimbatore, Madurai, Kanpur. **Ludhiana** = hosiery (industrial capital of Punjab).
- Woollen: Kanpur, Dhariwal, Ludhiana, Jamnagar, Srinagar. **Kota** = synthetic silk.

### 3. JUTE
- About **90%** of capacity in a **100 km x 3 km belt** along the **Hooghly** (most mills in the Hugli basin); **Bhatpara** = jute centre.
- Reasons: raw jute of the Ganga-Brahmaputra delta, **soft water for retting**, Hooghly waterway, **Kolkata port**, cheap labour from WB, Bihar, UP, gunny-bag market (NOT iron ore).
- **97 mills** (2026): West Bengal **71**, **AP 12** (2nd); WB over 80% of output. AP mills: **Chittivalasa, Nellimarla** (since the 1920s).
- Largest jute area: West Bengal (0.43 million ha, 2024-25). Largest exporter: **Bangladesh** (~75%).
| Act / scheme | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Jute Packaging Materials Act, 1987** | **100% foodgrains, 20% sugar** in jute bags |
| **National Jute Policy** | **2005** |
| **JUTE-ICARE** | Certified seed; microbial retting with **SONA** |
| JCI MSP | Raw jute ₹5,650/quintal |
- Raw-jute scarcity crisis in WB mills (2 Jan 2026).
### 4. SUGAR
#### 4.1 Location and Data
- **2nd largest agro-based industry** after cotton textiles.
- Mills within about **20 km** of cane: cane is **perishable**, yields only 9–12% sugar.
- India = **2nd largest producer after Brazil** (~19%) and **largest consumer**; 2nd in sugarcane (FAO 2024).
- 534 factories (2024-25), about equal private and cooperative.
| State | Long-run share |
|---|---|
| Uttar Pradesh | **35%** |
| Maharashtra | 22% |
| Karnataka | 10% |
| Tamil Nadu | 8% |
- **Latest season (2026)**: **Maharashtra led** (~9.9 MT), UP 2nd (~8.9 MT), Karnataka 3rd (ISMA, 31 Dec 2025: Maharashtra top).
- Belts: western UP (Meerut-Saharanpur-Muzaffarnagar), eastern UP (Gorakhpur-Deoria-Basti); AP: West and East Godavari, Visakhapatnam, Chittoor (not Nandyal).
- **Shakkar Nagar** (Bodhan, Nizamabad; now Telangana) grew around the Nizam Sugar Factory.
- Cuba = "Sugar Bowl of the world"; Brazil now largest exporter; **Ukraine** makes sugar almost entirely from **beet**.
#### 4.2 North–South Shift
| North (UP, Bihar) | South (Maharashtra, Karnataka, TN, AP) |
|---|---|
| Subtropical cane, lower sucrose | Tropical cane, **higher sucrose and yield** (TN ~105 t/ha vs UP ~81) |
| Season Nov–Feb (150–180 days) | Longer season (200–250 days) |
| Older **private** mills (Bihar mostly non-cooperative) | **Cooperative** mills – Nasik, Pune, Satara, Sangli, Kolhapur, Sholapur |
#### 4.3 By-products and Pricing
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Bagasse** | Boiler fuel, **paper pulp** |
| **Molasses** | Alcohol / **ethanol** |
| **Press mud** | Manure |
| **FRP** | Centre's cane price |
| **SAP** | Higher State price |
- **Rangarajan Committee**: **70:30** revenue sharing; remove minimum-distance rule.
- Sugar to ethanol fell from 12% (2022-23) to 9% (2025-26); about **3/4 of ethanol** now from grain.
- Duty-free import of up to 10 lakh tonnes raw sugar (27 Aug 2026).
### 5. CEMENT
- Inputs: **limestone** (main), silica, alumina, **gypsum**, coal; plants sit in limestone areas.
- India = **2nd** producer (over 8%; 426.29 MT in 2023-24); China 1st; per capita ~290 kg vs world 540 kg.
- **Largest state (2026): Rajasthan**, then AP, Telangana, Gujarat, Chhattisgarh, TN. South India over 30% of capacity.
- Clusters: **Satna, Katni** (MP), **Chittoor** (AP), **Nalgonda** (Telangana), Khilari, Lakheri, Chunar, Dalla, Churk.
- AP: **Kadapa-Kurnool limestone** makes Rayalaseema a cement hub – **Yerraguntla** (Kadapa), **Jaggayyapeta** (NTR), **Nandyal**.
- Green cements: **PPC** (fly ash from thermal plants) and **PSC** (slag from steel plants).

### 7. FERTILISERS AND PETROCHEMICALS
#### 7.1 Feedstock
- **Urea** = ammonia (**Haber process**) + CO2; **natural gas (methane)** is ammonia feedstock (HBJ pipeline).
- **Superphosphate** = **rock phosphate + sulphuric acid**; sulphur mainly from **oil refineries**.
- Inputs: naphtha, natural gas, coal, rock phosphate, sulphur, gypsum; steel slag and coke. **Caustic soda is NOT** a fertiliser input.
#### 7.2 Plants and Regions
| Region | Centres |
|---|---|
| **Gujarat–Maharashtra** (largest) | Vadodara, Kalol, Kandla, **Trombay** (RCF) |
| TN–Kerala | Neyveli, Tuticorin, Ennore, Alwaye, Kochi |
| North-West | **Nangal** (NFL), Bhatinda, Panipat |
| Chota Nagpur steel towns | Sindri, Jamshedpur, Rourkela, Durgapur, Bhilai |
| AP | **Kakinada** (Nagarjuna, Coromandel) |
- Coal-based FCI units: Talcher, Ramagundam, **Korba**; Ramagundam revived on gas (2021).
- **Phulpur** (Prayagraj) = biggest cooperative fertiliser factory. Haldia, Sri Ganganagar = fertilisers.
- **Gujarat** = largest fertiliser producer; **UP** = largest urea producer; India 2nd consumer. Urea record 314.07 LMT (2023-24).
#### 7.3 Petrochemicals and Refineries
- **Jamnagar** (Reliance, 1999) = world's largest refining complex and **largest petrochemical centre**; also Vadodara, Hazira, Dahej, Haldia, Nagothane, Paradip.

- Steel earns maximum foreign exchange; India a net steel importer in Apr–Oct 2025-26; output 168.4 MT (2025-26). Mission **Purvodaya** = Eastern India steel hub.
