## U0765 | LAND DEGRADATION, DESERTIFICATION, DEFORESTATION, SOIL EROSION AND URBAN HEAT ISLANDS
### 1. KEY TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Desertification** (UNCCD) | Land degradation in **arid, semi-arid and dry sub-humid** areas from climatic variation and human activity – NOT just the spread of existing deserts |
| Environmental degradation | Depletion of air, water, soil; destruction of ecosystems; extinction of wildlife |
| **Tragedy of the Commons** | Over-use of a shared pasture lowers land quality – **Garrett Hardin, 1968** (APPSC 2018) |
| **LDN** | Land Degradation Neutrality – no net loss of land health by **2030 (SDG 15.3)** |
| Soil Organic Carbon | Indian soils fell from 1% to 0.3% in 70 years |
| **Ridge to valley** | Watershed approach of the **PMKSY Watershed Development Component** |
### 2. PROCESSES AND CAUSES OF LAND DEGRADATION
- Main cause of desertification in semi-arid regions: **overgrazing and deforestation** (APPSC 2025).
| Process | How it degrades land |
|---|---|
| Overgrazing | Strips vegetation cover |
| Deforestation | Exposes soil to wind and water erosion |
| Water erosion | Removes topsoil; silts rivers and reservoirs |
| Wind erosion | Blows topsoil away in dry areas (Anantapur) |
| **Salinisation, waterlogging** | Poor drainage and **over-irrigation** (AP delta areas); by 2050, 50% of world arable land could be affected |
#### 2.1 Agriculture-driven Degradation
- Effects of **modern (intensive) agriculture**: soil degradation, pollution, ecological imbalance – **all of these** (APPSC 2018).
- Fertilisers and pesticides contaminate soil and water; **excess urea** upset the NPK ratio in **Punjab and Haryana**.
- Resource depletion → cause: groundwater – over-irrigation; topsoil – erosion from overgrazing and deforestation; habitats – deforestation; land – salinity and waterlogging.
### 3. SOIL EROSION AND CONSERVATION
| Erosion type | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Sheet** | Thin even layer of topsoil removed over wide areas |
| Rill | Small channels on slopes |
| **Gully** | Deep channels in clayey soil → **badlands**; called **ravines** in the **Chambal** basin |
| Wind | Topsoil blown off in dry areas |
| Method | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Contour ploughing** | Ploughing along contours slows water down slopes |
| **Terracing** | Steps on slopes; western and central Himalayas |
| **Strip cropping** | Grass strips between crops break wind force |
| **Shelter belts** | Tree rows as windbreaks; stabilised dunes in western India |
- Support: MGNREGA, state watershed projects, participatory forest management.
- **Soil Health Card**: launched **19 Feb 2015 at Suratgarh, Rajasthan**; card every **2 years**.
### 4. DEFORESTATION AND GLOBAL FOREST DATA
- Impacts: faster erosion, falling fertility, desertification; **loss of CO₂ sink adds to greenhouse effect**.
- Highest loss: **tropical forests** – Amazon, Congo Basin, Southeast Asia.
| FAO FRA 2025 (Oct 2025) | Fact |
|---|---|
| Forest share of land | About **32%** |
| Five countries with over 54% of forests | **Russia, Brazil, Canada, USA, China** |
| Net loss | 10.7 Mha/yr (1990s) → **4.12 Mha/yr** (2015-2025) |
| Only region with net gain | **Asia**, led by China and India |
| India | **9th** in forest area (10th in 2020); **3rd** in annual gain |
- Older FRA (APPSC 2018): net loss **8.3 Mha/yr (1990s) → 5.2 Mha/yr (2000-2010)**.
- Forest Declaration Assessment 2025: 8.1 Mha lost in 2024, about **94%** in tropical forests.
!TRAP Check which FRA edition the question uses: 8.3 → 5.2 (FRA 2010) vs 10.7 → 4.12 (FRA 2025).
#### 4.1 India State of Forest Report 2023
- Forest + tree cover **25.17%** (forest 21.76%, tree 3.41%); up 1,445 sq km since 2021.
- Top gains: **Chhattisgarh**, Uttar Pradesh, Odisha, Rajasthan; largest losses: **Madhya Pradesh**, Karnataka.
### 5. DESERTIFICATION GEOGRAPHY AND DATA
- **Desertification and Land Degradation Atlas 2021**: by **ISRO's Space Applications Centre (SAC), Ahmedabad**.
- **97.85 Mha = 29.77%** of India degraded; main contributors: **water erosion + chemical degradation**.
- About **one-fourth** of degraded land faces desertification; **Rajasthan** has over **23%** of desertified land.
- FAO: **one-third** of world soils degraded; Global Land Outlook: degradation costs over 10% of global GDP.
- Worst-hit continents: **Africa (Sahel) and Asia**.
- **Sand and dust storms** hit NW India (Rajasthan), West Asia and the Sahel.

### 9. URBAN HEAT ISLANDS
- **UHI**: cities markedly hotter than surroundings. Causes: **low-albedo concrete/asphalt** and **loss of vegetation** (separate causes).
- **Cool roofs**: white/reflective coatings – absorb less heat, lower cooling load.
- **AP Cool Surface Policy 2023** objectives: affordable urban heat solutions, lower heat and energy demand, reflective materials.
!TRAP APPSC 2023: "rural heat" is NOT an objective of the AP Cool Surface Policy.
- First Heat Action Plan: **Ahmedabad, 2013**; **NDMA** heat action plan guidelines **2016**.
- **Namo Van–Nagar Van Scheme**: urban forests against UHI (Jul 2026).
