## U0217 | PEASANT MOVEMENTS – INDIGO, DECCAN RIOTS, MOPLAH, BARDOLI, TEBHAGA, TELANGANA ARMED STRUGGLE
### 1. PEASANT MOVEMENTS – OVERVIEW
#### 1.1 Three Phases
- Phase 1 (pre-1917): **sporadic revolts** against zamindars, planters and moneylenders.
- Phase 2: **Gandhian satyagraha** (Champaran 1917, Kheda 1918, Bardoli 1928).
- Phase 3 (1930s-40s): **organised Kisan Sabhas** and Left-led struggles (Tebhaga, Telangana).
- Root cause in Andhra: the **Permanent Settlement (1793, Cornwallis)** made zamindars **proprietors** and cultivators **tenants**.
#### 1.2 Chronology – Year, Region, Key Point
| Movement | Year | Region | Key point |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sanyasi Revolt** | 1763-1800 | **Bengal** | After the **1770 famine** and curbs on pilgrimages |
| **Indigo Revolt** | **1859-60** | Bengal (**Nadia**) | Tinkathia; **Digambar and Bishnu Biswas** |
| **Pabna** | **1873-76** | East Bengal | Against **rent hikes**; Agrarian League → Bengal Tenancy Act 1885 |

| **Kheda** | 1918 | Gujarat | Patel, Mohanlal Pandya |
| **Awadh Kisan / Eka** | 1920-22 | UP | Baba Ramchandra; Eka under **Madari Pasi** |
| **Moplah** | **1921** | **Malabar** (Kerala) | Muslim tenants vs landlords and the British |
| **Bardoli** | **1928** | Gujarat | **Patel**, against a revenue hike; earned the title **"Sardar"** |

!TRAP Telangana armed struggle = Hyderabad State, NOT Malabar. Sidhu-Kanhu led the **Santhals**, not the Bhils.
### 2. INDIGO REVOLT (1859-60) AND PABNA
#### 2.1 Indigo Revolt
- European planters forced peasants to grow indigo under the **Tinkathia system** – indigo on **3/20 of the land** at poor prices.
- Leaders: **Digambar Biswas and Bishnu Biswas**; region **Nadia (Bengal)**.
- Outcome: **Indigo Commission (1860)** declared Tinkathia illegal.
#### 2.2 Nil Darpan – Who Did What
| Role | Person |
|---|---|
| Written by | **Dinabandhu Mitra** |
| English translation | **Michael Madhusudan Dutt** |
| Published by | **Rev. James Long** |
!TRAP Nil Darpan = written by Mitra, translated by Dutt, published by Long. Do not swap the three roles.
#### 2.3 Pabna (1873-76)
- **Pabna Agrarian League** – **Ishan Chandra Roy and Shambhu Pal**.
- Against **rent hikes by zamindars**; legal and peaceful; led to the **Bengal Tenancy Act, 1885**.
!TRAP Pabna was against rent hikes, NOT against indigo, and has no link with Dinabandhu Mitra.
### 3. DECCAN RIOTS (1875)
- Region: **Poona and Ahmednagar** (Maharashtra), under ryotwari.
- Cause: the **Limitation Law of 1859** let moneylenders (outsiders from **Gujarat and Marwar**) seize peasants' land.
- Target: **moneylenders** – peasants attacked their houses and **burned debt bonds**.
- Outcome: **Deccan Agriculturists' Relief Act, 1879**.
!NUM 1859 = Limitation Law (cause) | 1875 = Riots | 1879 = Relief Act
#### 3.1 Movement → Measure That Followed
| Movement | Measure |
|---|---|
| Bengal Indigo Revolt (1859-60) | **Indigo Commission (1860)** |
| Pabna movement | **Bengal Tenancy Act, 1885** |
| Deccan Riots | **Deccan Agriculturists' Relief Act, 1879** |
| Champaran (1917) | **Champaran Agrarian Act, 1918** |
| Tebhaga | **Bargadari Act (1950)** |
### 4. KISAN SABHAS IN UP AND OUDH
#### 4.1 Founders and Aims
| Body / movement | Year | Leaders | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| **UP Kisan Sabha** | **February 1918** | **Gauri Shankar Mishra, Indra Narayan Dwivedi**; backed by **M.M. Malaviya** | Peasant grievances |
| **Oudh Kisan Sabha** | **October 1920** | **Jawaharlal Nehru, Baba Ramchandra** | Taluqdars' **rent hikes and begar** (forced labour) |
| **Eka (unity) Movement** | **1921-22** | **Madari Pasi** | Rent **50% more** than recorded; aim: **conversion of rent into cash** |
- Eka began among farmers of **Hardoi, Bahraich and Sitapur** (UP); it was against excessive rent by zamindars and taluqdars.
!TRAP Nehru was NOT associated with the UP Kisan Sabha (1918); he was with the **Oudh** Kisan Sabha (1920).
#### 4.2 Eka vs Bardoli
| Point | Eka (1921-22) | Bardoli (1928) |
|---|---|---|
| Leader | Madari Pasi | Vallabhbhai Patel |
| Method | Turned **violent** | Disciplined, **non-violent** |
| Congress | **Outside** Congress control | Congress campaign |
| Issue | Excess rent; rent into cash | **30% increase in land revenue** |
### 5. MOPLAH REVOLT (1921) AND BARDOLI (1928)
#### 5.1 Moplah (Malabar)
- **Muslim tenants (Moplahs)** against **Hindu landlords (jenmis)** and the British.
- Grievances: **no security of tenure**, renewal fees, high rent.
- Leaders: **Variyankunnathu Kunjahammad Haji** and **Ali Musliyar**.
#### 5.2 Bardoli Satyagraha
- Gujarat, **1928**; against a **30% hike in land revenue**.
- Led by **Patel**, who got the title **"Sardar"** here.
### 6. ALL INDIA KISAN SABHA AND TEBHAGA
#### 6.1 All India Kisan Sabha (AIKS)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Founded | **April 1936** at the **Lucknow** session of the Congress |
| First president | **Swami Sahajanand Saraswati** |
| General secretary | **N.G. Ranga** |
| Kisan Manifesto | **August 1936** – abolition of zamindari, cancellation of rural debts |
| Flag | Red flag adopted in **October 1937** |
- Sahajanand's role: had earlier formed the **Bihar Provincial Kisan Sabha (1929)**; he led AIKS as its first president.
#### 6.2 Tebhaga (1946-47, Bengal)
- Led by the **Bangiya Pradeshik Kisan Sabha** (AIKS/CPI).
- **Bargadars / adhiars** (sharecroppers) against **jotedars**.
- Demand: crop share raised from **one-half to two-thirds** – that is, the landlord's share cut from **one-half to one-third**, as the **Floud Commission** had recommended; crop to be stored in the bargadars' own godowns.
- Outcome: **Bargadari Act (1950)**; short-run failure from limited spread and a Hindu-Muslim rift.
!KEY Tebhaga = "three shares": two-thirds to the bargadar, one-third to the landlord (UPSC 2013).
### 7. TELANGANA ARMED STRUGGLE (1946-1951)
#### 7.1 Cause and Course
- Against the **Nizam's feudal order** – **jagirdars, deshmukhs** and **Vetti** (forced labour); peasants and tribals joined.
- Led by the **CPI** through the **Andhra Mahasabha**.
- Land seized and redistributed; **village soviets** set up.
- Called off in **1951** after the Indian Army integrated Hyderabad (1948) and land reform was promised.
#### 7.2 Leaders and Martyrs
| Person | Role |
|---|---|
| **Doddi Komaraiah** | **First martyr**; shot at **Kadavendi (Jangaon / Janagam taluka, Nalgonda)** on **4 July 1946** by the Visnur deshmukh's men while defending a poor washerwoman's land |
| **Chakali Ailamma** | Washerwoman of **Palakurthi**; defended her land against the **Visnur deshmukh**; symbol of the revolt |
| **P. Sundarayya, Chandra Rajeswara Rao** | CPI leadership |
| **Mallu Swarajyam** | Leading woman fighter |
| **M. Suryanarayana** | President of the Andhra Mahasabha, **1941-46** term (APPSC G2 2019 key) |
| **Ravi Narayana Reddy** | Presided over the **8th Andhra Mahasabha, Chilkur (Huzurnagar, Nalgonda), June 1941** |
#### 7.3 Andhra Mahasabha's Shift
- Moved from **cultural work** to **leftist agrarian politics** (1930s-40s).
- **11th session, Bhongir (May 1944)** – fully dominated by communists.
- By the 1940s it was the **organisational front of the CPI**.
#### 7.4 Memoirs and Books
| Book | Author |
|---|---|
| **Veera Telangana – Naa Anubhavalu, Gnapakalu** | **Ravi Narayana Reddy** (APPSC G2 2025 key) |
| **Telangana People's Struggle and its Lessons** | **P. Sundarayya** |
!TRAP Veera Telangana = Ravi Narayana Reddy, NOT Mallu Swarajyam.
### 8. KISAN MOVEMENT IN ANDHRA – N.G. RANGA
#### 8.1 Pioneer and Institutions
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **1923** | India's **first Agricultural Labourers' Mahasabha**, **Nidubrolu**, under Ranga |
| **1928** | Ranga founds the **Andhra Provincial Ryots' Association** |
| 1931 | **Rytu Zamin Sanghams** formed |
| **1933** | **Ryot Agitation** of Andhra – led by **Parvataneni Veeriah Chowdary** (GS 2024 key) |
| **1933-34** | **First Kisan School in India**, **Nidubrolu (Guntur)** |
| 1934 | Rytu Sammelans; Kisan publications from Nidubrolu |
| 1934-37 | Summer schools in economics and politics (Kothapatnam, Mantena vari Palem) |
| 1937-38 | **Peasant march**, **Ichchapuram (Srikakulam) → Madras** (1,500 km/miles); Kommareddy Satyanarayana Murthy, Chalasani Vasudeva Rao |
| May 1938 | Andhra Rythu Vidyalayam, **Madanapalle** (T.N. Ramakrishna Reddy) |
| **1939** | **Rajakeeya Pathashala, Kankipadu** – founded by Ranga (G2 2012 key) |
- The Nidubrolu school is also called **Bharatiya Kisan School**, **Ramanayudu Rythu Vidyalayam** or Peasant Institute; founded by **Ranga**, his brothers and wife **Bharathi Devi** (est. 1933-34; often dated 1934).
!TRAP Nidubrolu = first Kisan School (1933-34); Kankipadu = Rajakeeya Pathashala (1939).
### 9. ANDHRA ANTI-ZAMINDARI STRUGGLES
#### 9.1 Estate → District → Leaders
| Estate / struggle | District | Key names |
|---|---|---|
| **Venkatagiri** | **Nellore** | Nellore Zilla Zamindari Rytu Sangam (**Dodla Ramireddy, 1929**); **Zamin Rytu Sangam** (**Vennelakanti Raghavaiah, 1930**); Vetluri Govinda Reddy; Ranga backed the revolt |
| **Munagala** | then Krishna, now **Nalgonda** | Zamindar **Nayani Venkataranga Rao** (Keesaravari); Munagala Peasant Association (**Batchala Jagannadh Das**) |

- **Ponaka Kanakamma** (Nellore) – woman activist who backed the **Zamindari Rytu movement** of Nellore district.
- Paper **Zamin Rytu** (Telugu weekly, Nellore, **1930**) – voice of the Zamin Rytu movement; started by **Nellore Venkatarama Naidu**; linked with Vennelakanti Raghavaiah's Sangam.
- Film **Rythu Bidda** – **Gudavalli Ramabrahmam**; dramatised the anti-zamindari issue.
- **Gandhi at Venkatagiri, 1933**.
#### 9.2 Towards Abolition
- **1937**: **Rajaji** set up the **Zamindari Abolition Committee**, chaired by **T. Prakasam**.
- **1947**: **Madras Zamindari Abolition Bill**.
