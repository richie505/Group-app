### 2. LOST-WAX (CIRE PERDUE)
1. Wax model coated with clay.
2. Heated: wax melts out.
3. Molten metal poured into the hollow mould.
4. Clay removed.
- Lost-wax objects: **Dancing Girl**, **copper dog and bird of Lothal**, **bronze bull of Kalibangan**, buffalo and goat figures.
- Casting continued into Late Harappan and Chalcolithic cultures (Daimabad bronzes).

### 4. POTTERY
- **Wheel-made**; typical **red pottery with black painted designs**; also **plain red** (most common) and painted red-and-black.
- Motifs: **geometric, floral, pipal leaf, fish-scale**.
- **Perforated jars**: large hole at the base, small holes over the wall – probably for **straining liquor**.
- **Kalibangan** = six types of pottery; **Lothal** = painted jar.

### 6. BEADS AND GEMS → CENTRE
| Centre | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Chanhudaro** | **Bead-making factory**, **furnace**, shell-working, bangles (APPSC G1 2022) |
| **Lothal** | **Bead workshops**; carnelian beads for export |
| **Rakhigarhi** | **Gemstone factory** – **3,000+** semi-precious stones |
- Materials: **carnelian** (from **Saurashtra**), agate, jasper, **steatite**, lapis lazuli, **faience**, **gold**.
- Beads exported to **Mesopotamia** and the **Persian Gulf**.
- Chanhudaro also: lipsticks, ink pot, ornamented elephant; **only city without a citadel**.
### 7. FAIENCE AND SHELL
- **Faience**: beads, bangles, **glazed ware** – called the **world's first glazed ware**; tie with **mosaic glass** = early clue to glass-making.
- Shell work: **Chanhudaro** shell-ornament factory; also Nageshwar, Balakot, Lothal.
- Seal carving and carnelian working = new techniques of the period.
### 8. SEAL CARVING AS ART
- Seals carved from **steatite**; **unicorn** = most common motif; also bull, elephant, tiger, rhinoceros; **Pashupati** figure; short lines of script.
