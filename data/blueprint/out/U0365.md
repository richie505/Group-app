## U0365 | CIVIL SERVICES IN INDIA – ROLE, NEUTRALITY, GENERALIST-SPECIALIST DEBATE; SECOND ARC RECOMMENDATIONS
### 1. CONSTITUTIONAL PROVISIONS (ARTICLES 308-323)
| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| **309** | Recruitment and conditions of service |
| **310** | **Doctrine of Pleasure** (defence, Union civil, all-India services) |
| **311** | **Safeguards**: no dismissal by an authority subordinate to the appointing one; inquiry and hearing |
| **312** | **All India Services** |
| 315 / 316 / 317 | PSCs for Union and States / appointment and term / removal and suspension |
| **320** | **Functions** of PSCs |
- **UPSC** member: **6 years or 65**; **SPSC/JSPSC**: 6 years or **62**.
- Removal for misbehaviour only after a **Supreme Court inquiry**.
!TRAP 310 = pleasure; 311 = safeguards; 312 = AIS.
### 2. ALL INDIA SERVICES AND SYSTEMS
| Service | Cadre-controlling authority |
|---|---|
| **IAS** | **DoPT** |
| **IPS** | **Ministry of Home Affairs** |
| **Indian Forest Service** | **MoEFCC** |
- **Tenure system** (Central Staffing Scheme): fixed term at the Centre, then return to state cadre.
- **Split system**: **policy** (Secretariat) separated from **implementation** (field agencies).
- Roles of civil servants: **policy advisers, implementers, stabilisers** (all three).

### 6. SECOND ARC
- Constituted **31 Aug 2005**; chairman **M. Veerappa Moily** (later V. Ramachandran); **15 reports** (June 2006 – May 2009).
| Report | Title |
|---|---|
| **1st** | **Right to Information: Master Key to Good Governance** (9 June 2006) |
| **4th** | **Ethics in Governance** |
| **10th** | **Refurbishing of Personnel Administration – Scaling New Heights** (APPSC 2018) |
| **12th** | **Citizen Centric Administration – The Heart of Governance** |
- Recommended: **lateral entry** at Joint Secretary level, **performance-based appraisal**, **rotational postings**, code of ethics, a Public Service Bill, **right to public services laws** (not abolition).
### 7. NEUTRALITY AND ANONYMITY
- **AIS (Conduct) Rules 1968** and **CCS (Conduct) Rules 1964** require political **neutrality and integrity**.
- **T.S.R. Subramanian case** and 2nd ARC: **fixed tenure** and **Civil Services Boards** to shield officers.
- **AP (Apr 2026)**: section officer dismissed under **Rule 9, AP CCA Rules 1991** (G.O. Ms. No. 43) for campaigning in 2024 elections.
- **Chhattisgarh (21 Apr 2026)**: circular barring party membership, quickly withdrawn.
### 8. GENERALIST VS SPECIALIST AND MISSION KARMAYOGI
- Lateral entry backed by **2nd ARC** and **Hota**; a **UPSC-led merit process** suggested for domain experts; demand for dedicated scientific cadres (Feb 2026).
- **Mission Karmayogi (2020)**: National Programme for Civil Services Capacity Building; platform **iGOT Karmayogi**; custodian **Capacity Building Commission (CBC)** – not UPSC.
- **GDCBA** launched by CBC on **20 Feb 2026** at India AI Impact Summit.
