## U0587 | SPECIAL CATEGORY STATUS - CRITERIA, 14TH FINANCE COMMISSION POSITION AND THE CONTROVERSY
### 1. ORIGIN AND FIRSTS
- SCS introduced in **1969** on the recommendation of the **5th Finance Commission**.
- **Not in the Constitution** - an executive, **Planning Commission-era** grouping.
- **Gadgil Formula (1968-69)**, approved by the **National Development Council (NDC)**, for distributing Plan funds; SCS States got a reserved first share of Normal Central Assistance.
- Later: **Gadgil-Mukherjee Formula (1991)**.
| FIRST | Fact |
|---|---|
| Year | 1969 |
| Recommended by | 5th Finance Commission |
| First three States | **Jammu & Kashmir, Assam, Nagaland** |
| Last of the 11 | **Uttarakhand** |
!TRAP 5th FC recommended SCS; the NDC approved the Gadgil Formula - do not swap.
### 2. CRITERIA
1. **Hilly and difficult terrain**
2. **Low population density** and/or sizeable **tribal population**
3. **Strategic location** along international borders
4. **Economic and infrastructural backwardness**
5. **Non-viable state finances**
!TRAP NOT a criterion: high population density, advanced infrastructure.
### 3. BENEFITS - SCS vs GENERAL
- Reason: a larger share of central funds to cover the gap from geographic disadvantage and backwardness.
| Benefit | SCS State | General State |
|---|---|---|
| Funding pattern | Automatic **90:10** | Normal |
| Central loans | **Concessional** interest | Normal |
| Industry | Tax breaks and incentives | None special |
| Unspent funds | **Do not lapse**, carried forward | Lapse |
| Consumer Welfare Fund seed money | **90:10** | **75:25** |
| PMGSY habitations | **250+** | **500+** (plains) |
| GST exemption threshold | Lower (Rs 10 lakh) | Higher |

| Status | States |

!TRAP Telangana is NOT an SCS State; Uttarakhand, Mizoram, Arunachal Pradesh, Tripura are.
### 5. FINANCE COMMISSIONS AND DEVOLUTION
| FC | Chair | Period | States' share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before 14th | - | - | 32% |
| **14th** | **Y.V. Reddy** | 2015-20 | **42%** |
| 15th | N.K. Singh | 2021-26 | 41% |
| 16th | **Arvind Panagariya** | 1 Apr 2026 - 31 Mar 2031 | 41% |
- 14th FC: **no distinction** between general and special category States; accepted **24 Feb 2015** - largest increase ever.
- Centre: higher devolution **subsumed** SCS benefits.
- AP: 42% went to all States; it ignored AP's **asymmetric loss of Hyderabad**.
- 16th FC added **"Contribution to GDP"** criterion (**10%** weight) and ended revenue deficit grants.
### 6. AP AND SCS - ASSURANCE AND CONTROVERSY
- **20 Feb 2014**: PM **Manmohan Singh** in the **Rajya Sabha** - SCS for AP for **five years** ("a solemn assurance").
- **Not written into** the AP Reorganisation Act - Centre treated it as non-binding.
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 2015 | 14th FC award used to deny SCS |
| **Sep 2016** | **Special Assistance Package** instead |
| 2018 | **TDP left NDA**; **no-confidence motion** in **Lok Sabha** (July), defeated |
| 2019 | SCS an "emotional referendum"; YSRCP called the package a "betrayal" |
| 2026 | Demand revived: Manickam Tagore (Lok Sabha, AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Bill debate), Botcha Satyanarayana, Y.S. Sharmila |
- AP's argument: a promise on the floor of Parliament carries constitutional weight.
