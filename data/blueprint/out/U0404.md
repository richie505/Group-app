## U0404 | CRIMES AGAINST WOMEN – TRAFFICKING, HONOUR CRIMES, ACID ATTACKS; CRIMINAL LAW AMENDMENTS 2013 AND 2018
### 1. TYPES, CAUSES AND FIRSTS
#### 1.1 IPC/BNS Heads vs Special and Local Laws (SLL) Heads
| IPC/BNS heads | SLL heads |
|---|---|
| Rape, attempt to rape, dowry death, abetment of suicide, acid attack | Dowry Prohibition Act 1961 |
| **Cruelty by husband or relatives** | **Domestic Violence Act (PWDVA) 2005** |
| **Kidnapping and abduction** of women | Immoral Traffic (Prevention) Act 1956 |
| Assault to outrage modesty; insult to modesty | POCSO (girl victims); **Indecent Representation** of Women Act; Commission of Sati Act |
- Other grouping: **criminal violence** (rape, murder) vs **domestic violence** (dowry deaths, battering).
#### 1.2 Causes
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Patriarchy, son preference | Foeticide, falling sex ratio |
| **Dowry** | Demands can grow into **bride burning** |
| **Under-reporting** | Stigma, fear, lack of awareness, **economic dependence**; marital rape poorly captured |
| Weak justice delivery | Only **11.7%** of police are women; low conviction; high pendency |
#### 1.3 FIRSTs
| FIRST | Fact |
|---|---|
| First all-women police station | **Kozhikode, Kerala (1973)** |
| First women's court | **West Bengal** (APPSC GS 2019) |

### 4. NIRBHAYA CASE, VERMA COMMITTEE AND CLA 2013
#### 4.1 Nirbhaya Case (Delhi, December 2012) → Outcomes
1. **Justice J.S. Verma Committee** (report Jan 2013)
2. **Criminal Law (Amendment) Act 2013**
3. **Nirbhaya Fund** (2013) – non-lapsable corpus
4. **JJ Act 2015** – 16-18-year-olds may be tried as adults for heinous crimes
- Not an outcome: the BNS. **Nirbhay Raho** (recent): Panchayati Raj + MWCD + NLSIU under Nirbhaya Fund.
#### 4.2 Verma Committee
- Members: **J.S. Verma, Leila Seth, Gopal Subramanium**.
- Recommended (not adopted in that form): **criminalise marital rape**, **no death penalty for rape**, police reforms, **age of consent kept at 16** → Parliament chose **18**.
#### 4.3 CLA 2013 ("Nirbhaya Act") – amended IPC, CrPC, Evidence Act
| IPC 2013 | Offence | Now BNS |
|---|---|---|
| **326A** | Acid attack (min 10 years-life) | 124(1) |
| **326B** | Attempted acid attack (5-7 years) | 124(2) |
| **354A** | Sexual harassment | 75 |
| **354B** | Disrobing | 76 |
| **354C** | Voyeurism | 77 |
| **354D** | Stalking | 78 |
| 370 | Trafficking redefined | 143 |
| **376E** | Death for repeat rapists | **71** |
- Rape definition widened (objects, body parts, oral acts); **minimum 7 years**; age of consent **18**.
- **Two-finger test** struck down in **Lillu v State (2013)**.
### 5. CLA 2018 AND BNS 2023
#### 5.1 CLA 2018
- Context: **Kathua and Unnao** cases (2018); led to the **FTSC scheme**.
- Minimum for rape: **7 → 10 years**.
| Victim | Punishment |
|---|---|
| Girl under 16 | Minimum **20 years** |
| Girl under 12 | 20 years to life or **death** |
| Gang rape, girl under 12 | Life or death |
- Investigation **2 months** + trial 2 months + appeals 6 months; **no anticipatory bail** for rape of girl under 16.
!TRAP Rape minimum **7** (2013) → **10** (2018).
#### 5.2 BNS 2023
- Keeps IPC provisions on rape, stalking, voyeurism.
- **Gang-rape age raised 16 → 18**.
- New **s.69**: intercourse by **deceitful means / false promise of marriage or job**, identity fraud.
- **s.103**: honour killings prosecuted as **murder**.
### 6. ACID ATTACKS AND HONOUR CRIMES
- **Laxmi v. Union of India (2013):** acid sold only against **photo ID** and recorded purpose, with a **register**; **no sale under 18**; minimum compensation **₹3 lakh**.
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Shakti Vahini v UOI (2018)** | **Khap** interference in marriages of consenting adults **illegal**; preventive, remedial, punitive steps |
| **Law Commission 242nd Report (2012)** | Draft law against interference in marriages "in the name of honour" |
| Constitutional angle | Honour killings violate **Arts 14, 15, 19, 21** |
| Witness Protection Scheme 2018 | Enforced under **Art. 141** till a law is made |
### 7. TRAFFICKING
- **Palermo Protocol (2000):** to prevent trafficking in persons, especially **women and children**; supplements **UNTOC**; ratified by India.
- UNODC GLOTIP 2016: victims and traffickers often share the same background.
| Law / Bill | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Art. 23(1)** | Prohibits traffic in human beings and forced labour |
| **BNS s.143** | Defines trafficking; **victim's consent immaterial** |
| **ITPA 1956** | Prostitution **not a crime per se**; punishes brothel-keeping, pimping, soliciting, living on earnings |
| **Trafficking of Persons Bill 2018** | Aggravated trafficking, shelters; **passed Lok Sabha, lapsed** |
| **2021 draft Bill** | **NIA** as national investigating agency |
- Related articles: **21A** education; **24** child labour in hazardous work; **39(f)** DPSP protecting children.

#### 9.2 Fast-Track and Special Courts
- Fast-track courts are **administrative**, not created by CLA 2013; **FTSC scheme (2019)** follows CLA 2018.
- FTSC = **centrally sponsored**, from **Nirbhaya Fund**; rape and POCSO cases.
- **POCSO:** a **Court of Session** designated Special Court in **each district**.
- **Odisha** announced **45** fast-track courts (Dec 2019).
- 2026: Guntur special court gave life to two in a dowry-death case.
### 10. ANDHRA PRADESH
#### 10.1 AP Disha Bill (December 2019)
- Response to the **2019 Hyderabad veterinarian** rape-murder.
- **Death** for rape and gang rape; verdict in **21 days** = **7 days investigation + 14 days trial** (vs CLA 2018: 2-month probe).
- **Special court in every district** (not only the capital); forensic evidence; cyber-stalking and deepfake penalties.
- Needs **President's assent** (criminal law in **Concurrent List**); **not in force** as passed.
#### 10.2 Initiatives → Purpose
| Initiative | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Disha App** | **SOS** app; alerts police, shares location (APPSC G1 2022) |
| Disha police stations | **18** women's police stations |
| **Abhayam** | **Chittoor** awareness campaign (Dec 2019) |
| **Abhay** | **Prakasam (Ongole)**: free pick-up and drop for women, **9 pm-5 am** |
| Mahila Samrakshana Karyadarsi | Women-protection functionaries in village/ward secretariats |
| **Trishul committees** | State/district: zero drugs, zero tolerance to atrocities against women, zero road accidents (Sep 2026) |
!TRAP **Abhayam** = Chittoor campaign; **Abhay** = Ongole pick-up. **SOS** = Disha SOS app and also **Sexual Offender Sheets**.
#### 10.3 AP Data
- Cases: **25,503** (2022) → **22,418** (2023, **11th** in India) → **19,952** (2024); arrests **17,149**; chargesheets 94.4%; sexual harassment 843 (2024).
- Further **4.4%** fall credited to **Shakti Teams**, Women's Safety Week rallies, Sexual Offender Sheets.
- SDG-16: 5.52 trafficking victims per 10 lakh (APSES 2024-25).
- **Conviction rate** (crimes against women, 2021): AP only **5.6%** (among the **lowest**; pendency **86.7%**) vs India **26.5%**.
- **District-wise** (2022): **Visakhapatnam** most rape cases (**69**), then **NTR** district (51).
