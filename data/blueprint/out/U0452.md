## U0452 | OBJECTIVES OF INDIAN PLANNING; TYPES OF PLANNING (IMPERATIVE, INDICATIVE, PERSPECTIVE, ROLLING)
### 1. PRE-INDEPENDENCE PLANNING AND PLAN MODELS
#### 1.1 Plan Models → Proponent
| Plan / model | Year | Proponent | Idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Planned Economy for India** | 1934 | **M. Visvesvaraya** | Ten-year plan to double national income |
| **National Planning Committee** (Congress) | 1938 | Initiated by **Subhas Chandra Bose**; chaired by **Nehru** | First Congress planning effort |
| **Bombay Plan** | 1944 | Industrialists – **J.R.D. Tata, G.D. Birla**, Kasturbhai Lalbhai, Purshottamdas Thakurdas, Ardeshir Dalal, John Mathai | State-led industrialisation with protection |
| **Gandhian Plan** | 1944 | **Shriman Narayan Agarwal** | Decentralised, self-contained villages |
| **People's Plan** | 1945 | **M.N. Roy** (Indian Federation of Labour) | Ten-year plan; priority to agriculture |
| **Sarvodaya Plan** | 1950 | **Jayaprakash Narayan** | Agriculture, cottage industry, land reform |
| **Nehru-Mahalanobis model** | 1956 | **P.C. Mahalanobis** | Heavy industry first (Second Plan) |
| **PURA** | 2003 | **A.P.J. Abdul Kalam** (announced by PM Vajpayee) | Urban amenities in rural clusters |
- **Advisory Planning Board (1946)**, headed by **K.C. Neogi**, recommended the Planning Commission.
- ★ **Mahatma Gandhi opposed planned development**; his self-reliance = simple living, local resources, local workforce for local production.
!TRAP **M.N. Roy** = People's Plan; he was **not** a Bombay Plan signatory. Sarvodaya = **J.P. Narayan**, not Vinoba's own plan.
### 2. PLANNING COMMISSION AND NDC
#### 2.1 Comparison
| Feature | Planning Commission | National Development Council |
|---|---|---|
| Set up | **15 March 1950** | **6 August 1952** (not 1950) |
| Basis | Executive resolution; modelled on **Soviet Gosplan** | Executive resolution; extra-constitutional |
| Status | Neither constitutional nor statutory | Apex body that **finally approved** Five Year Plans |
| Chairman | **Prime Minister** (first: Jawaharlal Nehru) | Prime Minister |
| Members | Deputy Chairman, Finance and Planning Ministers ex officio, **4-7 experts** (MoS rank), Member-Secretary | Union Cabinet Ministers, **all Chief Ministers**, UT representatives, PC members |
| Secretary | Member-Secretary | Secretary of the Planning Commission |
| Successor | **NITI Aayog, 1 January 2015** | NITI Governing Council |
#### 2.2 Planning Commission – Key Facts
- Set up on the **Advisory Planning Board's** recommendation; came into being with the **Budget of 1950-51**.
- **Deputy Chairman** = real (de facto) head; **Cabinet Minister rank**; attended Cabinet **without a vote**.
- **States / Governors were not represented** in it.
- Functions: assess material, capital and human resources; formulate plans; fix priorities and allocate resources; appraise progress; plan grants to states; **perspective planning**.
- "Body set up after Independence for a common plan" = **Planning Commission**.

### 3. OBJECTIVES OF PLANNING
#### 3.1 Long-term Objectives
- **Four long-term objectives:** **Growth, Modernisation, Self-reliance, Social justice** (equity) + employment and removal of poverty.
- **Economic growth = principal objective**; the others are secondary / supporting.
- **Chief objectives** listed: less **inequality of income and wealth**, higher **growth**, **removal of poverty**.
- Declared aim: rapid rise in the **standard of living** through efficient use of resources, more production and **employment for all**.
- ★ Major objective of the Five Year Plans: **self-reliance** and less dependence on foreign sources.
!TRAP "To achieve **social equality**" is **not** a chief objective; "literature" is the odd one out in objective lists.
#### 3.2 Socialistic Pattern of Society
- Adopted at the **Avadi Congress (1955)** → reflected in the **Second Plan (1956-61)** and **IPR 1956**.
### 4. PLANS → THRUST AND STRATEGY SHIFTS
#### 4.1 Plan → Main Thrust
| Plan | Main thrust |
|---|---|
| First (from **1 April 1951**) | **Agriculture**, irrigation, power (foodgrain imports, inflation); **3.6%** achieved vs **2.1%** target |
| Second (1956-61) | Rapid **industrialisation**, socialistic pattern (Mahalanobis) |
| Third (1961-66) | **Self-reliance**, food self-sufficiency, import substitution |
| Fourth (1969) | **Target-group** approach; weaker sections |
| Fifth (1974-79) | Self-reliance + raising consumption of the poor; **direct attack on poverty** |
| Sixth (1980-85) | **Poverty alleviation**; Minimum Needs Programme; IRDP |
| Seventh (1985-90) | Growth, modernisation, self-reliance, social justice |
| Eighth (1992-97) | **Indicative**; deficit financing largest source of public outlay |
| Ninth (1997-2002) | **Seven Basic Minimum Services** |
| Twelfth (2012-17) | Faster, sustainable, **more inclusive** growth (last plan) |
#### 4.2 Trickle-down → Direct Attack on Poverty
| Phase | Approach | Programmes |
|---|---|---|
| First–Third Plans | **Trickle-down**: growth benefits assumed to reach the poor | Community Development Programme (1952) |
| Fourth Plan (1969-) | **Target-group** approach | **SFDA, MFAL, DPAP** |
| Fifth Plan | **Direct attack on poverty** | Minimum Needs Programme |
| Sixth Plan | Removal of poverty foremost | **IRDP**, MNP |
### 5. GROWTH MODELS AND THE MAHALANOBIS STRATEGY
#### 5.1 Model → Plan
| Model | Plan | Logic |
|---|---|---|
| **Harrod-Domar** | **First Plan** | Growth depends on **savings rate** and **capital-output ratio** |
| **Feldman-Mahalanobis** | **Second Plan** | Build **capital-goods** capacity first, then consumption rises |
| **Wage goods model** (**C.N. Vakil and P.R. Brahmananda**) | Alternative to Mahalanobis | Expand wage goods (food, consumer goods) first |
- Feldman (Soviet, **1928**) and Mahalanobis (**1953**) built the model independently.
#### 5.2 Mahalanobis Strategy – Components
- **Included:** state-led **heavy and capital-goods industries**; large **public sector**; rapid industrialisation (Bhilai, Durgapur, Rourkela steel plants); inward-looking, limited foreign trade (import substitution).
- **Not given priority:** mechanisation of agriculture, wage goods, export promotion.
- Foreign-exchange crisis forced cuts in **1958**; model replaced by the Third Plan (1961).
!TRAP Harrod-Domar = **First** Plan; Mahalanobis = **Second** Plan.
### 6. IMPERATIVE VS INDICATIVE PLANNING
| Feature | Imperative (directive) | Indicative |
|---|---|---|
| Market mechanism | **Entirely replaced** by command | Plan **improves** the market |
| Ownership | State / collective | Mixed |
| Targets | **Binding quotas**, allocation, even prices | **Guidelines**, signals for private sector |
| Examples | **USSR (1928, Stalin)**, China | Mixed economies |
| India | Early years in public sector | From the **Eighth Plan (1992-97)**, after 1991 reforms |
!KEY India shifted to **indicative planning** from the **Eighth Plan (1992-97)**.
### 7. OTHER PLANNING DICHOTOMIES
| Pair | Distinction |
|---|---|
| **Centralised vs decentralised** | One central authority, top-down vs decisions shared with lower governments / private units within a broad policy |
| **Financial vs physical** | Allocation in **money** terms vs in **real** terms (materials, manpower, output targets) |
| **Direction vs inducement** | Command (imperative) vs incentives guiding private units (indicative) |
| **Democratic vs totalitarian** | Plan through elected bodies and consent vs state command |
- **India:** **democratic** planning; imperative in the public sector early on; after 1991 **indicative and by inducement**.
- Kerala **People's Plan Campaign** – a decentralised model.
### 8. PERSPECTIVE PLANNING
- **Meaning:** long-term planning over **15-20 years or more**; five-year and annual plans carved out of it.
- Perspective planning was a **Planning Commission function**.
- **Long-term perspective plan 1985-2000** accompanied the **Seventh Plan**.
- Lack of perspective = a standard criticism of Indian planning.
- **AP now:** GSDP target **Rs 308.11 lakh crore by 2047-48**; ten "**Padi Sutralu**"; **Swarna Andhra Vision 2047** built on consultations with **1.18 crore households** and 17 lakh people (2024-25).
- AP targets (10 Feb 2026): **15%** annual growth, GSDP **Rs 31 lakh crore**, PCI **Rs 5.15 lakh** by 2030.
- DMF five-year perspective plans from baseline surveys (2026).
### 9. ROLLING PLAN (1978-80)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Suggested by | **Gunnar Myrdal**, for backward countries, in "**Indian Economic Planning in its Broader Setting**" |
| Introduced by | **Janata Party government** (ended the Fifth Plan a year early) |
| Period | **April 1978 – March 1980** |
| Name | Janata **Sixth Plan (1978-83)** = "the rolling plan"; novel idea of the **draft Sixth Plan** |
| Outlay | **Rs 12,177 crore**; stressed employment |
| End | Janata fall → 1979-80 annual plan → Congress Sixth Plan from **1 April 1980** |
#### 9.1 Features
1. A plan for the **current year** (with the annual budget).
2. A plan for a fixed number of years (**3, 4 or 5**), **revised every year and extended by one year**.
3. A **perspective plan** of 10-20 years.
- Targets revised annually on the previous year's performance → **flexibility**.
- **Criticism:** yearly revision **weakened commitment** to targets.
!TRAP Janata Sixth Plan **1978-83** (rolling) vs Congress Sixth Plan from **1 April 1980**.
### 10. MULTI-LEVEL AND DECENTRALISED PLANNING
#### 10.1 Levels
| Level | Body |
|---|---|
| Centre | Planning Commission → **NITI Aayog** |
| State | State Planning Boards |
| District | **District Planning Committee** (Art. **243ZD**) |
| Metro | **Metropolitan Planning Committee** (Art. **243ZE**) |
| Block / village | Mandal plans; **GPDP** |
- **JP Narayan Committee (1961)** flagged growing centralisation.
- **SFDA, DPAP, ITDP, IADP** ran **outside the panchayats**.
- **73rd and 74th Amendments** restored local bodies' role in planning.
- **NITI Aayog** = think tank; **bottom-up**, **cooperative federalism**; **no financial powers**, not a fund allocator.
- NITI **State Support Mission** (Aug 2026): helps states set up State Institutions for Transformation.
- Kerala People's Plan Campaign: launched **17 Aug 1996**; **35-40%** of plan outlay to local bodies; "People's Plan 2.0" (25 Aug 2026).
- MoRD to train **2,000 BDOs** in GIS/MIS for **Viksit Gram Panchayat Plans** (5 Jun 2026).

### 11. PLAN FINANCING
- **Domestic savings:** budgetary resources, public sector surpluses, market borrowing.
- **Deficit financing:** borrowing from the **RBI** (money creation).
- **Foreign aid.**
- **Fourth Plan** aimed to reduce dependence on foreign aid.
- **Eighth Plan:** largest source of public sector outlay = **deficit financing**.
- Plan grants created overlap and central dominance (Jan 2026 view).
