## U0738 | INTEGRATED GUIDED MISSILE DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMME – PRITHVI, AGNI, AKASH, TRISHUL, NAG
### 1. IGMDP – LAUNCH, PHASES, CLOSURE
- Launched **1983** under **Dr APJ Abdul Kalam**; nodal lab **DRDL, Hyderabad**, with **RCI and ASL** (Missile Complex).
| Phase | Missiles |
|---|---|
| 1984–89 | **Prithvi, Trishul** |
| 1989–92 | **Nag, Akash** |
| 1992–2008 | **Agni** matured |
- **Officially completed 2008**; later missiles = separate projects.
- Spin-offs: long-range tracking, composites, **ring laser gyros**, radomes (not a satellite navigation constellation).
- Significance: India from missile importer to **missile exporter**; base for Agni-V MIRV, BMD.
- **Aug 2026:** ToT of conventional missiles such as **Nag** to private industry; **Agni stays in state production**.
### 2. THE FIVE MISSILES
| Missile | Name means | Type | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Prithvi** | Earth | **Surface-to-surface SRBM** | 150–350 km | **Liquid fuel**; **first IGMDP missile** |
| **Agni** | Fire | Surface-to-surface ballistic | ~700 to 5,000+ km | **Solid fuel**; Strategic Forces Command |
| **Akash** | Sky | **Medium-range SAM** | ~25–30 km | **Mach 2.5**; altitude 18 km; Army + IAF |
| **Trishul** | Trident | **Short-range, low-level quick-reaction SAM** | ~**9 km** | Above Mach 2; solid fuel + **thrust vector control**; **never inducted** |
| **Nag** | Cobra | **3rd-generation ATGM** | **500 m – 4 km** (land version) | **Fire-and-forget**; IIR seeker |
!TRAP Prithvi = liquid; Agni = solid. Nag has the shortest range (Prithvi-I min 150 km, Agni-I min 700 km).
### 3. AGNI SERIES
| Missile | Stages / fuel | Range |
|---|---|---|
| **Agni-I** | Single-stage solid; ~15 m | ~700 km (up to ~1,200 km with lighter payload) |
| **Agni-II** | Two-stage solid | 2,000–3,500 km (more than 2,000 km) |
| **Agni-III** | Two-stage solid; tested Feb 2010 | ~3,500 km |
| **Agni-IV** | Two-stage, **road-mobile**; ring-laser-gyro INS; CEP < 100 m | ~4,000 km |
| **Agni-V** | **Three-stage solid, canisterised**; ~17 m, ~50 t; >1 t warhead | **5,000+ km**; **MIRV** |
| **Agni-Prime** | Canisterised | 1,000–2,000 km; **rail-based launcher, 24 Sep 2025** |
- Agni-I: tested 28 Mar 2010 from Wheeler Island; first night trial 11 Apr 2014.
- **Mission Divyastra (2024):** first Agni-V test with **MIRV**, from **Dr APJ Abdul Kalam Island**.
- **Developer = DRDO** (not ISRO); **fully indigenous**; operator **Strategic Forces Command**.
- **ICBM threshold 5,500 km** → Agni-V (5,000 km) formally **IRBM** (often called ICBM); **Agni-VI** = proposed ICBM.
- 2026 tests: MIRV Agni (8 May); Agni-1, ITR Balasore (22 May); Agni-4 user trial, Chandipur (6 Aug).
!TRAP Agni-V "not exclusively indigenous" is a WRONG statement (UPPSC 2015).
### 4. PRITHVI VARIANTS
| Variant | Service | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Prithvi-I** | **Army** | 150 km, 1,000 kg |
| **Prithvi-II** | **Air Force** | 350 km, 500–1,000 kg; first test **27 Jan 1996, Chandipur**; inducted **2003**; night test by SFC 6 Oct 2018 |
| **Dhanush (Prithvi-III)** | **Navy** | **Ship-launched** |
- Prithvi-I, II and Agni-I nuclear-capable; Prithvi-II + Agni-I fired together (Jul 2025).
- **PAD (Pradyumna):** exo-atmospheric interceptor (50–80 km).
### 5. AKASH AND TRISHUL
| Variant | Range | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| Akash Mk-I | ~25 km | Command guidance, **Rajendra radar** |
| **Akash Prime** | 25–30 km | **Indigenous active RF seeker**; first test **27 Sep 2021** (APPSC 2021); Ladakh |
| **Akash-NG** | ~70 km | **Canister-launched**; cleared **2023** |
- **Akashteer** (BEL + Army) = air-defence control system, **not a missile**.
- **Trishul shut 27 Feb 2008** (technical flaws, delays, cost).

### 6. NAG FAMILY AND GUIDANCE
| Variant | Role |
|---|---|
| **NAMICA** | Tracked amphibious carrier (modified BMP-2), **up to six** missiles |
| **NAMIS / Nag Mk-2** | Tracked "tank killer", ~7–10 km |
| **HELINA** / **Dhruvastra** | Helicopter-launched – **Army Aviation** / **IAF** |
| **MPATGM** | Man-portable, ~2.5 km; DAC approved procurement (8 Jul 2026) |
| **SANT** | Air-launched stand-off, 15–20 km; **Lock-On After Launch** |
- **Fire-and-forget:** missile guides itself; operator takes cover.
- **Top attack:** climbs, dives onto thin top armour; Nag has top and direct attack.
- Seekers: Nag **IIR**; Akash Prime **active RF**; SANT LOAL.
