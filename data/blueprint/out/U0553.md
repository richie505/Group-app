## U0553 | ANDHRA PRADESH ECONOMY AFTER BIFURCATION – GSDP, GROWTH RATE, SECTORAL SHARES
### 1. BIFURCATION AND ITS IMPACT ON THE ECONOMY
#### 1.1 Bifurcation facts
- AP bifurcated on **2 June 2014** under the **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014**.
- Residual AP initially had **13 districts** (specified in the Act).
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Revenue loss | **Hyderabad** alone ≈ **48%** of undivided AP's total revenue of **₹70,548 crore** (2012-13) |
| AP got | Bulk of the **agrarian population**, irrigation-project liabilities, power-sector deficits |
| Telangana kept | IT, commercial and industrial assets and the **capital** |
| Result | AP became a **chronic revenue-deficit State** |
| Capital city | New capital **Amaravati**; major institutions built from scratch |
- Hyderabad-centred services and industry went to Telangana, so **agriculture's share in AP rose and industry's share fell**.
- IT base lost; new IT parks at **Visakhapatnam, Tirupati, Vijayawada**.
#### 1.2 The "missing middle"
- Manufacturing only about **11% of GSDP**: AP is moving from agriculture to services **without a manufacturing-led stage**.
- **Industrial Development Policy 4.0 (2024-29)** puts manufacturing at the centre to tackle the missing middle.
!KEY Hyderabad ≈ 48% of undivided AP's revenue (₹70,548 crore, 2012-13) – root of AP's chronic revenue deficit.

### 3. GSDP – CONCEPTS, COMPILATION AND ESTIMATE CYCLE
#### 3.1 Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| SDP | Value of all goods and services produced within the State in a year, **counted once** (no duplication) |
| GSDP | **GVA + product taxes – product subsidies** |
| Per capita income | Computed on **NSDP** |
#### 3.2 Compilation
- Compiled by the **Directorate of Economics & Statistics (DES), AP** – **not NSO** – at current prices and constant **2011-12** prices.
- **3 broad sectors, 17 sub-sectors**:
| Sector | Sub-sectors |
|---|---|
| Agriculture & allied (5) | Agriculture (crops), horticulture, livestock, forestry & logging, fishing & aquaculture |
| Industry (4) | Mining & quarrying, manufacturing, electricity-gas-water supply, construction |
| Services (8) | Trade, hotels & restaurants; railways; transport by other means & storage; communications; banking & insurance; real estate & ownership of dwellings; public administration; other services |
- Base year: AP Survey tables still use **2011-12** (2019-20 is wrong). **India's new GDP series** (MoSPI, released **27 Feb 2026**) has base **2022-23**; it revised India's 2023-24 growth to 7.2% and 2024-25 to 7.1%.
#### 3.3 Estimate cycle
- Advance estimates (AE/FAE) → First Revised (FRE) → Second Revised (SRE) → Third Revised (TRE).
- Example: 2021-22 "**record**" growth of **11.43%** (constant) was later revised to **7.14%** (TRE); current-price 17.36% (SRE).
!TRAP Always quote the estimate stage: 2024-25 real growth was 9.24% at FAE but 8.27% at FRE.

### 6. SECTORAL SHARES IN AP GVA
#### 6.1 Shares over time (current prices)
| Year (basis) | Agriculture & allied | Industry | Services |
|---|---|---|---|
| TE 2016-17 average | Primary 30% | Secondary 19% | **Tertiary 42%** (largest) |
| 2014-15 | 30 | 26 | 44 |
| 2021-22 | – | – | **42%** |
| 2022-23 | 36.19% | 23.36% | 40.45% |
| 2023-24 (AP Budget) | 37% | 23% | 40% |
| 2024-25 FAE | 35.29% | 23.18% | 41.53% |
| **2025-26 FAE** | **33%** | **24%** | **43%** |
- 2024-25 FAE at **constant** prices ≈ **30 : 28 : 42** (agriculture ₹2.32, industry ₹2.23, services ₹3.29 lakh cr) – agriculture's share is smaller in real terms.
- Agriculture & allied = **33.20% of GSDP** (₹5,39,454 crore of ₹17,62,357 crore; 2025-26 AE, current).
- NITI Aayog: agriculture contributes **more than one-third** of GSDP. **Industry excluding IT/ITES ≈ 15% of GSDP (2019-20)**.
- Communications ≈ 2% of GVA at current prices (SES 2024-25).
#### 6.2 Largest sub-sectors (current prices)
- **Livestock** = largest contribution to GVA at current prices (**2021-22 AE**) and largest of all sub-sectors in 2024-25 (**about 12%**); horticulture 10%, crops 4%, forestry 1% (2024-25).
- 2025-26: **Manufacturing = Livestock (10.84% each)**, then Fishing & Aquaculture 9.42%, Other services 8.81%, Horticulture 8.78%, Real estate 8.51%.
#### 6.3 AP vs India structure
| Indicator | AP | India |
|---|---|---|
| Agriculture & allied (GVA) | 35.29% (2024-25) | ~18% |
| Industry (GVA) | **23.18%** | **27.6%** |
| Services (GVA) | 41.53% → 43% | ~53% (new series 52.9%) |
| Workforce in agriculture | **45.6%** (manufacturing 22.8%, services 31.6%; SES 2024-25) | ~46% |
- **Dominant sector = services** in both AP and India; AP's agriculture share ≈ **twice India's**; AP's services share trails India's. Tamil Nadu services 53.54%.
- Rural workers: casual labour **33.8%** (India 22.5%); regular wage/salaried **13.7%**.
- Shift since bifurcation: 30 : 26 : 44 (2014-15) → 35 : 23 : 42 (2024-25). Telangana services = 1.25x AP's (2013-14) → 1.47x (2024-25).
