## U0715 | DIGITAL IDENTITY AND PAYMENTS – AADHAAR, UPI, DBT, INDIA STACK, ONDC, ACCOUNT AGGREGATOR
### 1. INDIA STACK – DIGITAL PUBLIC INFRASTRUCTURE
#### 1.1 The Four Layers
| India Stack layer | Component |
|---|---|
| Identity | **Aadhaar** |
| Paperless | **eSign, DigiLocker** |
| Cashless | **UPI** |
| Consent | **DEPA / Account Aggregator** |
- India Stack (Aadhaar, e-KYC, e-Sign, digital payments) allows **online, paperless and cashless** access to services and cuts the cost of e-KYC and verification.
- DPI examples (Economic Survey 2025-26): UPI, Aadhaar, FASTag, DigiYatra, BharatNet, 5G.
- Other open platforms: **CoWIN, Aarogya Setu, DigiLocker, DIKSHA**.
- Digital economy: **12-14% of GDP**; electronics output Rs 13.11 lakh crore in 2025-26.
!TRAP Consent layer = DEPA / Account Aggregator; paperless layer = eSign, DigiLocker; identity layer = Aadhaar.
#### 1.2 ONDC vs Account Aggregator
| Platform | Feature |
|---|---|
| **ONDC** (2022) | Open e-commerce network that breaks platform monopolies; 616+ cities, 7.64 lakh sellers (Jan 2025) |
| **Account Aggregator** | Consent-based sharing of financial data, giving faster credit decisions; under **DEPA** |
### 2. AADHAAR – FIRSTS, FORMAT AND SCALE
#### 2.1 Firsts
| FIRST | Fact |
|---|---|
| First Aadhaar number | **Ranjana Sonawane**, **Tembhli** village, **Nandurbar** district, Maharashtra, **29 September 2010** |
| First Aadhaar village | **Tembhli** |
| Launch of the scheme | **2009** |
- UIDAI had planned to issue the first set of unique identity numbers from February 2011; the first number actually came in September 2010.
#### 2.2 Format and Biometrics
- **12-digit random number** issued by **UIDAI**; free, lifelong, one enrolment per person.
- Does not show caste, religion or geography.
- Biometrics: **10 fingerprints, 2 iris scans, face photograph**.
- Scale: **142 crore** numbers generated (31 Jul 2025; ~133.65 crore live); Aadhaar access for **over 145 crore** people (PIB, Aug 2026).
- Uses: DBT (JAM), **Jeevan Pramaan** (Aadhaar-based digital life certificate), bank and mobile **e-KYC**.
!TRAP Aadhaar = 12-digit (UIDAI); ABHA = 14-digit health ID. Launched 2009, first number 2010.
### 3. AADHAAR LAW, RULES AND OPEN APIS
| Act / rule / policy | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **Aadhaar Act** | **2016** | UIDAI made a **statutory authority** under **MeitY**; enrolment, authentication, targeted delivery of subsidies, benefits and services |
| Amendment | 2019 | Amended the Aadhaar Act |
| Aadhaar Authentication for Good Governance Rules | 2020 | e.g. IBPS allowed voluntary Aadhaar authentication of candidates (2025) |
| MeitY **Policy on Open APIs** | **2015** | Led to **API Setu**; open APIs for authentication, e-KYC, e-Sign |
!TRAP UIDAI works under the **Ministry of Electronics and IT**, not the Home Ministry.
### 4. UPI AND NPCI PRODUCTS
#### 4.1 UPI
- Launched **April 2016** by **NPCI**; one app routes payments from several bank accounts using a **VPA** (name@bank).
- **UPI 2.0**: overdraft accounts linkable, pre-authorised payments, signed merchant QR, **Rs 2 lakh/day** limit.
- **UPI Lite**: on-device wallet for small payments **without a UPI PIN**.
#### 4.2 NPCI Products
| Product | Key fact |
|---|---|
| **BHIM** | NPCI's UPI app; launched **30 December 2016** after demonetisation |
| **Bharat QR** | Interoperable QR code standard |
| **e-RUPI** | One-time, cashless, person- and purpose-specific **voucher**; NPCI with **DFS, MoHFW and NHA**; launched **August 2021** |
| **Vajra** | NPCI platform for fast and secure payments |
| **NFS** | National Financial Switch – links all ATMs; largest shared ATM network; designed, built and launched by **IDRBT** in **2004** |
#### 4.3 International Acceptance
- **France** = first country in Europe to accept UPI.
- **PayNow-UPI** link with **Singapore** (2023).
### 5. PAYMENT SYSTEMS AND THEIR OPERATORS
| System | Operator |
|---|---|
| **NEFT, RTGS** | **RBI** |
| **IMPS, UPI, AePS, BBPS, NACH, RuPay** | **NPCI** |
| **FASTag** (NETC) | NPCI with NHAI/IHMCL |
| **iPay** payment gateway | **IRCTC** (own gateway) |
- **AePS** = cash withdrawal and balance enquiry at micro-ATMs using Aadhaar.
- **New Umbrella Entities (NUEs)**: RBI move to end **NPCI's monopoly** in retail payments; minimum capital **Rs 500 crore**.
!PAIR NEFT, RTGS | RBI | Central bank-run || IMPS, UPI, AePS | NPCI | Retail payments body
### 6. NODAL MINISTRY AND DIGITAL PAYMENTS INDEX
- **MeitY** was given "Promotion of Digital Transactions including digital payments" in **February 2017**.
- On **17 July 2023** "Promotion of Digital Payments" moved to the **Department of Financial Services (Ministry of Finance)**, which now gives the **Digital Payments Awards**.
- **RBI Digital Payments Index**: base **March 2018 = 100**; published **half-yearly**.
!KEY Nodal for digital payments now = DFS, Ministry of Finance; earlier = MeitY.

### 8. DBT – JAM TRINITY AND DBT MISSION
#### 8.1 JAM Routing
1. **Aadhaar**: biometric check of the beneficiary.
2. **Mobile**: transaction alerts.
3. **CPSMS (now PFMS)** + NPCI's **Aadhaar Payment Bridge**.
4. Benefit reaches the bank account.
- First major JAM application: **PAHAL** (LPG subsidy).
- **DBT Mission**: now in the **Cabinet Secretariat**.
#### 8.2 Key DBT Numbers
- Rs **44 lakh crore** transferred (May 2025); savings **Rs 3.48 lakh crore** (2015 to Mar 2023).
- **PMJDY**: **58.77 crore** accounts, deposits Rs 3,12,414 crore (Aug 2026); **more than half held by women**.
### 9. AADHAAR-BASED DELIVERY IN ANDHRA PRADESH
- DBT in AP inaugurated at **Gollaprolu, East Godavari**, on **6 January 2013** by **Jairam Ramesh** with CM **Kiran Kumar Reddy**.
- **Aadhaar-enabled PDS (e-PoS)**: Krishna district from **May 2015**; all 13 districts from **October 2015**, with portability between fair price shops.
- AP = first state to run **e-PoS in 100% of fair price shops**; dealer and card holder both authenticate biometrically.
### 10. DATA AND DEBATES
- UPI FY 2025-26: **24,160 crore** transactions worth **Rs 314 lakh crore** = **85%** of digital payments by volume; **49%** of the world's real-time payments.
- Digital payments: 2,071 crore (2017-18) to 22,831 crore (2024-25) = **41% CAGR**.
- Debates: biometric failures excluding the elderly and labourers; privacy; digital divide.
