## U0485 | RURAL CREDIT AND RURAL NON-FARM SECTOR; MIGRATION AND RURAL DISTRESS
### 1. RURAL CREDIT – HISTORY AND SURVEYS
- 1951: **moneylenders ~70%** of rural credit.
- **All India Rural Credit Survey Committee** (RBI, 1951; report 1954), chair **A.D. Gorwala**: "**Rural credit has failed, but cooperation must succeed**" → **SBI (1955)** from the Imperial Bank.
- **AIDIS 2019** (NSS 77th round): rural debt **66% institutional, 34% non-institutional**; ~35% rural households indebted.
- **NAFIS 2021-22** (NABARD): rural household income ₹8,059 → **₹12,698**/month (2016-17 to 2021-22); indebted households 47.4% → **52.0%**.
### 2. SOURCES OF RURAL CREDIT
| Institutional | Non-institutional |
|---|---|
| Commercial banks, RRBs, cooperatives/PACS, Small Finance Banks, SHG-bank linkage | Moneylenders, traders, landlords, relatives |
- **Kisan Credit Card**: valid 5 years; crop, post-harvest, consumption, allied needs; open to **tenants, sharecroppers, SHGs, JLGs** (not only landowners); MISS-KCC ~5.9 crore farmers; Kisan Rin Portal.
- **Small Finance Banks**: lend to small businesses, small and marginal farmers, rural entrepreneurs (regulators/FSDC members are not borrowers).
- **NABARD** = apex rural development bank; **SIDBI** = finances MSMEs.
### 3. INFORMAL DEBT AND MICROFINANCE
| Debt type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Temporary | Seasonal, repaid after harvest |
| Chronic | Long-standing |
| **Debt trap** | New loan to repay old |
- Usurious interest → distress sale of land/gold, bonded-labour-like ties; often the trigger in farmer suicides.
- Microfinance route (DAY-NRLM): **SHGs, JLGs, BC Sakhis**.
### 4. RURAL NON-FARM SECTOR
- Components: rural industries, Khadi & Village Industries, handloom/handicrafts, food processing, trade, transport, services.
| Body / scheme | Role |
|---|---|
| **KVIC** | Statutory, **KVI Act 1956** (amended 1987, 2006); Ministry of **MSME**; runs **PMEGP**; KVI sales ₹1.27 lakh crore (2025-26) |
| **SVEP** | Under DAY-NRLM; funds rural non-farm enterprises |
| MSMEs | 8.7 crore units, ~31.1% of GDP, 38.9 crore jobs |
| **DDU-GKY** | Placement-linked skilling; Parliamentary panel flags **low wages and drop-outs** |
### 5. RURAL DISTRESS INDICATORS
- Urban wages **1.7-2 times** rural (ES 2025-26).
- MGNREGA person-days **389.09 crore (2020-21) → 183.77 crore** (2025-26, to Dec 2025); rural unemployment 3.3% → 2.5%.
- **NREGA** passed 2005, in force 2006, renamed **MGNREGA 2009**: 100 days per household; wages within **15 days** (not 30); one-third women. Now **VB-G RAM G**.
- MGNREGA results: higher purchasing power, less distress migration (not lower bargaining power).
!TRAP UPSC 1996 key: rural incomes lag due to farmers' illiteracy and poor scientific knowledge ("I only").
### 6. MIGRATION – PUSH AND PULL
| Push (rural) | Pull (urban) |
|---|---|
| Few jobs, low wages, landlessness, crop failure, debt | High labour demand, better wages |
| Poor health and schools | Education, healthcare |
| Unbalanced development | Industry and services |
- **Distress migration**: forced by debt, landlessness, crop loss. **Seasonal**: for a season, then return. **Return migration** also a type.
- **26.8%** of rural Indians were migrants (2020-21).
- BPSC 2024: urban demand, unbalanced development, few rural jobs – **all are causes**.
### 7. FEMINISATION OF AGRICULTURE
- Meaning: rising share of women among cultivators, labourers, unpaid family workers as men move to non-farm/urban work.
- Causes: male out-migration; distress; subsistence and unpaid work; COVID return migration.
- **Claudia Goldin** (2023 Economics Nobel; prize set up 1968, awarded since 1969): **U-shaped curve** – high in 1980s, fell 2004-05 to 2012 (machines), rising after 2020.
- Rural women = **80%** of the rise in female workers (2017-18 to 2023-24).
!TRAP Feminisation ≠ land ownership by women; it is participation.
### 8. ANDHRA PRADESH
- **93.2%** agricultural households indebted – highest among major States (NSSO 77th Round).
- ~**50%** of AP farmers depend on non-institutional credit (NABARD; not 25%).
- Distress belts: **Rayalaseema, Srikakulam**. Gulf sources: **YSR Kadapa, Godavari/Konaseema**.
- **125 days** wage work in distressed pockets under VB-G RAM G (Jun 2026).
