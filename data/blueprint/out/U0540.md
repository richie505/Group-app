## U0540 | GOODS AND SERVICES TAX – STRUCTURE, SLABS, GST COUNCIL, IGST SETTLEMENT, COMPENSATION CESS
### 1. GST – BASICS AND OBJECTIVES
- **GST**: an **indirect tax**; **single, comprehensive, multi-stage, destination-based** tax on **every value addition**.
- **Taxable event: supply** of goods and services (not manufacture, not sale).
- **Destination principle**: revenue accrues where goods or services are **consumed**, **not where produced**.
- Introduced by the **101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016**; in force **1 July 2017**.
- Biggest **indirect-tax reform since Independence**; monthly GST data is now a macro indicator.
#### 1.1 Objectives
- Replace a fragmented structure with one tax – **"one nation, one tax"** (APPSC 2024).
- **End cascading** ("tax on tax"); **one national market**; transparency and compliance.
- **Non-inflationary**; cuts production costs.
- GST replaced **indirect** taxes (excise, service tax, State VAT), **not direct taxes**.

- **Revenue Neutral Rate**: the rate that keeps **GST revenue = pre-GST revenue**; its value depends on the assumptions used.

### 3. CONSTITUTIONAL BASIS – 101ST AMENDMENT
#### 3.1 Articles
| Article | Content |
|---|---|
| **246A** | **Parliament and State legislatures** both make GST laws (concurrent, simultaneous power); **Parliament alone** for **inter-State** supply |
| **269A** | **IGST** on inter-State trade – levied by the Union, apportioned between Centre and States on Council advice |
| **279A** | **GST Council**, constituted by the **President** |
| **268A** | Service tax – **omitted** |
- **Ratification**: the Amendment touched the federal distribution of powers → approval by **at least half the State legislatures**.
!TRAP Council = **Article 279A**, not 269A (APPSC G2 2025); 269A = IGST.
#### 3.2 Before GST
- **VAT** was a **State subject** (Entry 54, State List), not Central; it aimed to end double taxation and cascading of sales tax; levied on value added at each stage.
- **Central Sales Tax**: on **inter-State trade** and in **Union Territories**; SEZs exempt; now merged into GST.
### 4. GST COUNCIL
#### 4.1 Nature and Composition
- **Constitutional body** under **Article 279A**; **never statutory** (APPSC G1 2024: "statutory then constitutional" is **false**).
- **Chairperson**: **Union Finance Minister**.
- **Members**: **Union MoS in charge of Revenue or Finance** (only a member); **Finance/Taxation Minister of each State** and each UT with legislature.
- **Vice-Chairperson**: chosen by State members from among themselves.
#### 4.2 Voting
| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| **Quorum** | **One-half** of total members |
| **Centre's weight** | **1/3** of votes cast |
| **All States together** | **2/3** of votes cast |
| **Decision** | **3/4 majority** of weighted votes of members present and voting |
!NUM 1/2 quorum; 1/3 Centre; 2/3 States; 3/4 to decide
#### 4.3 Powers and Procedures
- Recommends on **tax rates, exempted goods, threshold limits**, model laws, revenue sharing, special rates, date for petroleum.
- **Dispute settlement**: Council sets up a mechanism for disputes between Centre and States or among States.
- Proceedings are **not invalid** due to a vacancy or defect in the Council's constitution.
- **Union of India v. Mohit Minerals (2022)**: recommendations are **persuasive, not binding**; Parliament and States have **simultaneous power** to legislate; the Council was **not** held statutory.
- Legislative power stays with **Parliament and State legislatures**; the Council only recommends.
### 5. TAXES SUBSUMED VS RETAINED
#### 5.1 Subsumed – 8 Central + 9 State
| Central taxes subsumed | State taxes subsumed |
|---|---|
| **Central Excise Duty**, Additional Excise Duties | **State VAT / Sales Tax**, **Central Sales Tax** |
| **Service Tax** | **Purchase Tax, Luxury Tax, Entry Tax** (all forms), **Octroi** |
| **CVD** and **SAD** on imports | **Entertainment Tax** (except by local bodies) |
| Central cesses and surcharges on supply (KKC, SBC) | Taxes on advertisements, lotteries, betting, gambling |
- "Declared goods of special importance" concept dropped.
#### 5.2 Not Subsumed
- **Basic Customs Duty** (customs duty is not in GST).
- **Stamp duty** (not merged), **electricity duty**, motor vehicle tax, property tax.
- **State excise on alcoholic liquor**; petroleum products.
!TRAP Stamp duty was **not** merged; purchase, luxury and entry taxes **were**.
### 6. ITEMS OUTSIDE GST
| Item | Position |
|---|---|
| **Alcoholic liquor for human consumption** | **Excluded by the Constitution** |
| **Five petroleum products**: **crude, petrol, high-speed diesel, natural gas, ATF** | **Within GST constitutionally**; levied only from a date the **Council recommends** |
| **Electricity** | Outside |
| Stamp-duty real estate transactions | Outside |
- **Lubricating oil** is **not** among the five petroleum products.
- APPSC G2 2025: no GST on petrol/diesel, liquor, electricity to consumers, services of charitable entities → **"All of the above"**.
- **Ghee is under GST**.
#### 6.1 Exempt vs Nil vs Zero-rated
| Term | Meaning | ITC |
|---|---|---|
| **Nil-rated** | 0% rate | **No ITC** |
| **Exempt** | Notified out of tax | **No ITC** |
| **Zero-rated** | **Exports, SEZ supplies** | **Full ITC refund** |
- **Exempt items** (old question): cereal grains hulled, chicken eggs cooked, newspapers with advertising; **not** processed canned fish.
- **GST 2.0**: exempts **individual life and health insurance premiums** (not every insurance service); rare-disease medicines fully exempt (Jun 2026).
### 7. DUAL GST COMPONENTS AND IGST SETTLEMENT
| Component | Levied / collected by | Supply |
|---|---|---|
| **CGST** | **Centre** | Intra-State |
| **SGST** | **States** | Intra-State (equal halves with CGST) |
| **IGST** | **Centre** (Art. 269A) | **Inter-State and imports** |
| **UTGST** | Centre, for UTs without legislature | Intra-UT |
| **Compensation cess** | Centre | Luxury / sin goods |
- **IGST revenue settles to the destination (consuming) State**; only **Parliament** legislates on IGST.
- Centre collects CGST and IGST; States collect SGST; GST Council recommends.
- **AP angle**: **SGST** is AP's **largest own-tax source** – ₹36,296 crore (**38%**, 2024-25 RE); State excise (22%) second.
- AP High Court (May 2026): Centre and State tax authorities must decide among themselves who investigates.
### 8. RATE STRUCTURE AND RATE-CHANGE PROCEDURE
#### 8.1 GST 2.0
- Old slabs **5, 12, 18, 28%** merged into **5% (merit)** and **18% (standard)** + **40%** for **sin / ultra-luxury (de-merit)** goods.
- Approved at the **56th Council meeting (3-4 Sep 2025)**; effective **22 Sep 2025**.
#### 8.2 Procedure
- Rates changed by **government notification on GST Council advice**, **not by amending the Acts**, within **statutory ceilings**.
- **Ceilings**: **CGST 20%**, **IGST 40%**.
### 9. GST MECHANISMS
| Mechanism | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Input Tax Credit (ITC)** | Credit of tax paid on inputs by a **registered person**, set off against output tax; **stops cascading and double taxation**; not available on exempt or non-taxable supplies |
| **Reverse Charge (RCM)** | Liability moves from **supplier to recipient** on specified supplies |
| **Anti-profiteering (Sec 171)** | Rate-cut / ITC benefit must reach consumers |
| **Destination principle** | Revenue to the consuming State |
| **Composition scheme** | Goods turnover up to **₹1.5 crore (1%)**, services **₹50 lakh (6%)**; **no ITC** |
| **Inverted duty structure** | Refunds allowed |
- **Anti-profiteering chain**: **National Anti-Profiteering Authority → CCI (December 2022) → GSTAT Principal Bench (from 1 October 2024)**.
!TRAP NAA's work went first to the **CCI**, then to **GSTAT** – not directly to GSTAT.
### 10. GSTN, E-WAY BILL AND E-INVOICING
- **GSTN**: IT backbone for registration, returns and payment; **GSTIN** is **15 digits**.
- **E-way bill**: electronic document for moving goods **above ₹50,000**; valid **one day per 200 km**.
- **E-invoicing**: digital reporting of invoices → **stops fake invoices**, **checks ITC**; HSN/SAC codes.
- Budget 2026-27: automated registration within **3 working days** for low-risk applicants (liability up to ₹2.5 lakh/month).
### 11. GST COMPENSATION AND CESS
| Step | Fact |
|---|---|
| **GST (Compensation to States) Act, 2017** | **5 years** of compensation at **14% annual growth** over **2015-16** base |
| June 2022 | **Compensation ended**; cess continued to repay loans taken to pay compensation |
| 22 Sep 2025 | Cess **scrapped for all goods except tobacco** |
| 31 March 2026 | Compensation cess **ended** |
| From **1 Feb 2026** | **Higher excise on tobacco** (Central Excise (Amendment) Act, 2025); **Health Security se National Security Cess** on **pan masala** units |
- The new cess is charged on **machine production capacity**; money goes to the **Consolidated Fund of India**, **not shared** with States.
- **Unutilised fund**: under the Compensation Act, any balance left at the end is split **50:50** – half to the Centre, half shared among the States.
### 12. COLLECTIONS

- July 2026 GST up **15.4%**; Centre's gross tax grew only **3.7%** in Q1 2026-27 as GST 2.0 cut revenue.
