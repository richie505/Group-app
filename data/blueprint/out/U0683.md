## U0683 | COMMUNICATION NETWORKS IN INDIA – POSTAL, TELECOM AND DIGITAL CONNECTIVITY
### 1. REGULATORS AND BODIES
| Body | Basis | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **TRAI** | TRAI Act **1997** | **Regulator of telecom and broadcasting**; tariffs; advises on spectrum, licensing |
| **TDSAT** | **2000** amendment of TRAI Act | Disputes; **appeals against TRAI** |
| **DoT** | Ministry of Communications | Policy, licensing, spectrum, digital connectivity, telecom R&D |
| **Prasar Bharati** | Act **1990** (Broadcasting Wing of MIB) | Broadcaster: runs **AIR and Doordarshan** (not DoT) |
| **PRGI** | **Press and Registration of Periodicals Act 2023** (replaced PRB Act 1867) | Newspaper registration; earlier **RNI** (not Press Council) |
- Minister: **Jyotiraditya Scindia**; MoS: **Pemmasani Chandra Sekhar**. Fraud tools: **Sanchar Saathi + ASTR**.
- Fastest indigenous IP/MPLS router launched in Bengaluru (Mar 2024).
!TRAP Regulator = TRAI; broadcaster = Prasar Bharati.
### 2. POSTAL NETWORK
- **PIN code:** **6 digits**, from **15 Aug 1972**. 1st = zone (**9 = Army Postal Service**); 2nd = sub-zone; 3rd = sorting district; last 3 = delivery office.
- **Digi PIN:** **10-digit alphanumeric**, on the **APT** platform built by **CEPT**; rollout in 23 circles done **4 Aug 2025** (1.64 lakh offices).
- **Speed Post: Aug 1986**, refund if late. **IPPB: 1 Sep 2018**, Department of Posts.
- India has the **largest number of post offices** in the world: **1,64,999** (31 Mar 2025). **AP: 10,660**.
### 3. TELECOM MILESTONES AND DATA
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| **1882** | First telephone exchange, **Calcutta** |
| 1994 / 1999 | Telecom Policies: liberalisation |
| 2011 / **3 Jul 2015** | **MNP** within circle / **nationwide** |
| 2018 | National Digital Communications Policy (5G, IoT, M2M) |
| **Oct 2022** | **5G** launched |
| **2023** | **Telecommunications Act** replaced **Telegraph Act 1885** |
- **Teledensity 86.76%** (2025-26; 75.23% in 2014). 5G: **99.9%** of districts, ~**40 crore** users in ~22 months.
- **5G Standalone** = independent of 4G core; low latency, network slicing. **Bharat 6G Vision (2030)**.
### 4. TRANSMISSION MEDIA
| Medium | Signal | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Optical fibre** | Light by **TIR** | **Fastest, highest bandwidth** (APPSC 2025) |
| Copper | Electrical | Low bandwidth, loss, interference |
| Satellite | Microwave | Remote reach; GEO ~**36,000 km** = high latency |
- Fibre **core index higher than cladding**; angle > critical angle. Uses: **endoscope** (one bundle lights, one carries image), **sensors** (temperature, strain), fibre lamps.

### 6. DIGITAL CONNECTIVITY
- **Digital India: 1 Jul 2015**, **9 pillars**, first = broadband highways.
- **BharatNet:** ex-**NOFN (2011)** (not NKN); funded by **USOF (Digital Bharat Nidhi)**; nodal **BBNL** (merged with BSNL); **2.18 lakh GPs**, **6.92 lakh km** fibre; Phase 3 = last mile + private players. Amended BharatNet: **₹1.39 lakh crore**.
- **PM-WANI: Dec 2020** (DoT); public Wi-Fi via **PDOs** and **PDOAs**. **National Broadband Mission: Dec 2019**.
- **CSCs: 5.69 lakh**. **Visakhapatnam Declaration** extended NeSDA to Ladakh and NE. SC: digital access part of **Article 21**.
### 7. SATELLITE COMMUNICATION
- **INSAT-1B (1983)**: first fully operational INSAT satellite.
- **GSAT-N2 (GSAT-20)**: broadband satellite launched by **SpaceX** Falcon 9 (Nov 2024).
### 8. MASS MEDIA
- **AIR** named **1936**; **Akashvani 1957**. **Doordarshan: 15 Sep 1959, Delhi**.
- First community radio: **Anna FM** (Anna University, **2004**); licensed by MIB.
### 9. AP DIGITAL CONNECTIVITY
- **APSFL:** **IP-MPLS ring** (over GPON); **triple play** (internet, IPTV, voice); 6,300+ cable operators.
- **AP FiberNet Phase 3:** **4,000+ tribal hamlets** in Eastern Ghats.
- **Amended BharatNet MoC (Feb 2026):** **3,942 villages**; every household in **18 months**.
- **RTGS:** formed **2017**; data-driven e-governance under the CMO.
