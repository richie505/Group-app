### 7. RANKINGS - WOMEN ENTREPRENEURS AND SOCIAL INITIATIVES
- **Union Ministry of Rural Development** report 2025-26: **AP 1st** in promoting women entrepreneurs - individual business loans to **1.47 lakh DWCRA women** (99% of target).
- Next: Telangana (1.28 lakh), Uttar Pradesh (1.09 lakh).
- AP also leads in **PMFME** use - **35,934** beneficiaries.
- DWCRA loans **₹33,614 crore** vs target ₹32,000 crore; CM target **5 lakh** women entrepreneurs.
- **PMFME**: launched **29 June 2020**, **Ministry of Food Processing Industries**.
- **Chetana** (Anantapur district police): **AP's first** such programme for **transgender persons** - free training in computers, tailoring, handicrafts; self-employment and private jobs; slogan "Steps towards the right path... foundations for a proud life".
- Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act - **2019**; National Council for Transgender Persons - **2020**.

### 9. AGRICULTURE - PULSES AND MANGO EXPORTS
#### 9.1 Pulses
- **AP 1st in pulse production growth** 2025-26 - **+57.73%**; UP 2nd (+17.7%).
- Production 9.96 → 15.71 lakh tonnes (+5.75 lakh t); area 28.45 → 34.70 lakh acres.
- **Green gram** = largest share.
- Reasons: self-sufficiency drive, quality seeds, pest management, value addition, higher MSP.
#### 9.2 Mango Exports
- Top importer 2025-26: **UAE** - ₹144.41 crore; shipments up 11,622 MT.
- AP varieties in demand: **Banganapalli**, Benishan, small Rasalu; AP share ~**8-10%** of India's mango exports.
- Markets: 52 countries (DGCIS, APEDA data); dominant varieties: **Alphonso, Kesar, Khader**.
- **APEDA**: statutory, **APEDA Act 1985**; Ministry of Commerce and Industry; HQ **New Delhi** (not Mumbai).
### 10. MSMEs AND CULTURE
#### 10.1 EESL MSME Sustainability Plan
- **EESL** (Energy Efficiency Services Limited) - PSU under the **Union Ministry of Power**; known for LED lighting, e-mobility, smart meters.
- Aim: **energy efficiency and water efficiency** in AP MSMEs - one of India's largest MSME efficiency programmes; audits 452 MSMEs (energy), 300 (water).
- Under Centre's **RAMP** + **AP MSME & Entrepreneur Development Policy 4.0**; work order at the **AP MSME Growth Summit**.
- **RAMP** (Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance): **World Bank-supported** Central Sector Scheme, **2022**, Ministry of MSME, 2022-23 to 2026-27.
#### 10.2 Amaravati Chitrakala Veedhi
- **Guntur, 8-9 August 2026**; inspired by Bengaluru's **Chitra Santhe**; AP Creativity and Culture Commission; ~**700 artists from 10 States**.
### 11. VISION, PRICES AND HUMAN DEVELOPMENT
- **Swarna Andhra @2047** (Dec 2024, with NITI Aayog inputs): **USD 2.4 trillion** economy, per capita income **USD 42,000** by **2047**; **10 guiding principles (Padi Sutralu)**.
- AP inflation 2025-26 (Apr-Dec): **1.39%** (India 1.72%); 4.41% in 2024-25.
