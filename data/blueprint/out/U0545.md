## U0545 | FISCAL DECENTRALISATION TO LOCAL BODIES – STATE FINANCE COMMISSIONS AND LOCAL REVENUE
### 1. CONSTITUTIONAL PROVISIONS
| Article | Body | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| 243H | Panchayats | State Legislature **may** authorise taxes, tolls, fees; assign State taxes; give grants |
| 243I | Panchayats | **Governor** constitutes **SFC** every **five years** |
| 243X | Municipalities | Same enabling power |
| 243Y | Municipalities | Same SFC (243I) reviews municipal finances; **no separate SFC** |
| 280(3)(bb), (c) | Both | Central FC augments State fund on **SFC recommendations** |
- "May" = **enabling**; State not forced to impose taxes.
- **Eleventh Schedule** (Art. 243G): **29** subjects; **Twelfth Schedule** (Art. 243W): **18** subjects; both 1992. Lists are enabling → **funds, functions, functionaries (3Fs)** rarely move together.
### 2. OWN REVENUE OF LOCAL BODIES
| Type | Sources |
|---|---|
| ULB tax | **Property tax** (most important), **professional tax** (ceiling ₹2,500), advertisement, entertainment, water, market taxes; octroi (traditional) |
| ULB non-tax | Rent, fees, fines, **user charges** (water, sewerage) |
| ULB other | Grants, SFC devolution, loans; **municipal bonds** in a few large cities |
- Weak municipalities = **under-assessment and under-collection** of property tax.
- **ES 2016-17**: **satellite imagery** estimated property-tax gap in **Bengaluru and Jaipur** (APPSC G1 2017).
- **SVAMITVA** drone maps → panchayat property-tax baseline; **AMRUT 2.0** rewards property-tax reform and bonds.
### 3. PANCHAYAT REVENUE
- **Property tax** = major own revenue of Panchayats; levy only **State-authorised** taxes: local fairs, house tax, fees, tolls.
- Sources: **FC grants**, **Central Cooperative Banks**, **CSS** allocations, **SFC** allocations.
!TRAP **NABARD** is NOT a direct panchayat source; **sales tax, land revenue** are State levies.
- Gram sabhas spend only **4%** of time on revenue (13% on local issues).

### 6. CENTRAL FC LOCAL-BODY GRANTS
- 15th FC ~₹4.36 lakh crore; **16th FC ₹7,91,493 crore** (rural 4.35, urban 3.56 lakh crore); urban share **36% → 45%** (urbanisation by 2031: 41%).
| 16th FC component | Share | Tied / untied |
|---|---|---|
| **Basic** | **80%** | Half tied (water, sanitation), half untied |
| **Performance** | **20%** | **Fully untied** |
| Special infrastructure (ULB) | ₹56,100 crore | Wastewater, cities 10-40 lakh |
| **Urbanisation Premium** | ₹10,000 crore | One-time; merging peri-urban villages, Rural-to-Urban Transition Policy |
- **16th FC entry conditions**: elected bodies, **online audited accounts**, timely SFCs (municipal bonds **NOT** a condition).
- **15th FC** ULB condition: audited accounts + **baseline property-tax rates**.
### 7. CHALLENGES
- **IIHS** (80 ULBs, 2012-17): transfers ≈ **40%** of ULB revenue.
- India's transfers ≈ **0.5% of GDP** vs global **2-5%**.
- No **accrual accounting**, weak balance sheets → no bond market.
- SFCs **not constituted regularly**.
### 8. AP LOCAL BODY FINANCES
- ULB terms ended **15 March 2026**, panchayats **4 April 2026** → **Special Officers**; FC grants at risk.
- 16th FC to AP: rural LBs **₹16,627 crore**, urban LBs **₹12,158 crore**.
