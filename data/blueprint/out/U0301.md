## U0301 | VICE-PRESIDENT; PRIME MINISTER AND THE UNION COUNCIL OF MINISTERS – ARTICLES 74, 75, 77, 78
### 1. VICE-PRESIDENT – OFFICE AND ELECTION
#### 1.1 Office (Articles 63, 64, 79, 89)
- **Article 63**: **second-highest office**; modelled on the **US Vice-President**.
- **Articles 64 and 89(1)**: VP is **ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha**; has only a **casting vote** in a tie.
- VP is **not a member of either House** – RS is chaired by a non-member.
- **Article 79**: Parliament = **President + Council of States + House of the People**; the VP is **not** a part of Parliament.
- Does not preside while a resolution for **his own removal** is under consideration.
- Ex officio **President of ICWA** (Indian Council of World Affairs).
#### 1.2 Election (Articles 66, 71)
- Elected (not appointed) by **members of both Houses** – **elected and nominated MPs** (788 when full); **State Legislatures have no role**.
- **PR-STV**, **secret ballot**; Presidential and Vice-Presidential Elections Act, 1952.
- **Article 71**: all disputes decided by the **Supreme Court** – final; acts done before a void election is declared remain valid.
- **39th Amendment (1975)** removed the SC's power over election disputes of President, VP, PM and Speaker; **44th Amendment (1978)** restored it.
!KEY State Legislative Assemblies vote in electing the **President, Rajya Sabha members and Legislative Council members** – not the VP.
### 2. VICE-PRESIDENT – QUALIFICATIONS, TERM, REMOVAL, ACTING PRESIDENT
#### 2.1 Qualifications and term (Article 67)
- Citizen; **35 years**; qualified for election to the **Rajya Sabha** (not Lok Sabha); no office of profit.
- Term **5 years**; continues till successor takes charge (Art 67(c)); **re-election any number of times**.
- Oath by the **President** (Art 69).
#### 2.2 Removal (Article 67(b))
- Resolution moved **only in the Rajya Sabha** by a **majority of all the then members**, **agreed to by the Lok Sabha**; **14 days' notice**; **no grounds** stated; no impeachment.
| Point | President | Vice-President |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified to be member of | Lok Sabha | Rajya Sabha |
| Resigns to | Vice-President (Art 56(1)(a)) | President (Art 67(a)) |
| Removal | Impeachment (Art 61), two-thirds, 14 days' notice | RS resolution + LS agrees |
| Vacancy filled | Within **6 months** (Art 62) | "As soon as possible" – no deadline (Art 68) |
- On the President's resignation the VP immediately informs the **Speaker** (Art 56(2)).
#### 2.3 Acting President (Article 65)
- On vacancy (death, resignation, removal) or the President's inability; **maximum 6 months**.
- While acting: **ceases to do Chairman's duties** – the **Deputy Chairman** presides; draws the **President's salary**, not the Chairman's.

### 4. PRIME MINISTER – APPOINTMENT, OATH, MEMBERSHIP
#### 4.1 Appointment (Article 75(1))
- **Appointed by the President**; by **convention** the leader of the **LS majority**. Other Ministers on the **PM's advice**; PM has **full discretion** in choosing them.
- Oath by the **President** (Art 75(4)).
- May be from **either House**; need not be an MP when appointed – must become one within **six months** (Art 75(5)).
- **UK** PM must belong to the House of Commons; **Indian** PM – either House.
- **Article 53(1)**: executive power in the **President** (nominal, de jure); real executive = **PM** (de facto). The President "**represents the nation but does not lead the nation**".
- Art 54 = President's election; 75 = PM and Ministers; 155 = Governor; 164 = CM and State Ministers.
#### 4.2 Tenure and responsibility
- **No fixed tenure**; formally at the President's pleasure, in practice while he has the **Lok Sabha's confidence**.
- PM resigning on losing majority (e.g. **Budget defeated** in LS) is a **convention**, not written in the Constitution.
### 5. PRIME MINISTER – POWERS, FUNCTIONS, BODIES CHAIRED
- Selects Ministers, **allocates portfolios**, can ask any Minister to resign; advises on **summoning/prorogation** and **dissolution of LS** – not on the CJI (collegium).
- **Article 78** (not 74) – PM's duties to the President (see section 6).
- Administering ministers' oaths is **not** an Article 78 duty.
| Body chaired by the PM | Note |
|---|---|
| **NITI Aayog**, **NDC**, **Inter-State Council**, **National Integration Council**, **National Defence Committee** | Chairman |
| **Planning Commission** (1950) | Ex-officio Chairman; Deputy Chairman = Cabinet rank, first **Gulzarilal Nanda** |
| **NDMA** | Disaster Management Act (23 Dec 2005) |
| **CSIR** | President |
- **Gandhi Peace Prize** (started **1995**, Gandhi's 125th birth anniversary): jury of **five** incl. **PM, CJI, Leader of Opposition in LS** – not the President.
### 6. ARTICLES 74, 75, 77, 78 – CONTENT
| Article | Content |
|---|---|
| 74(1) | CoM headed by PM to **aid and advise** the President; President may ask **reconsideration once**; then advice is **binding** |
| 74(2) | Advice **not inquired into by any court** |
| 75(1A) | Size cap 15% of LS (91st Amdt) |
| 75(1B) | Defector disqualified cannot be Minister till re-elected |
| 75(2) | Ministers hold office during the **President's pleasure** |
| 75(3) | **Collective responsibility** to the **Lok Sabha** |
| 77(1) | All executive action in the **President's name** |
| 77(2) | Authentication of orders |
| 77(3) | **Allocation of Business Rules, 1961** and **Transaction of Business Rules, 1961**; President allocates portfolios on PM's advice |
| 78 | PM communicates CoM decisions, furnishes information, puts a Minister's lone decision before the CoM |
- **Article 74(3) does not exist.**
- **During a National Emergency** the President gets **no discretion**; CoM advice stays binding.
!KEY "All executive action of the GoI is taken in the name of the President" = Article 77.
### 7. COUNCIL OF MINISTERS – RESPONSIBILITY AND NON-MEMBERS
#### 7.1 Collective and individual responsibility
- **Article 75(3)**: Council **collectively responsible to the House of the People** only – even if the PM or Ministers are RS members.
- **Individual responsibility**: Ministers hold office at the **President's pleasure** (Art 75(2)) – not legally the PM's; President removes a Minister only on PM's advice.
#### 7.2 Non-member Ministers (Article 75(5))
- Any person (incl. PM) may be a Minister; a non-member for **six consecutive months** ceases to be one.
- **S.R. Chaudhuri v. State of Punjab (2001)**: a non-member **cannot be re-appointed repeatedly** without getting elected.
#### 7.3 Ministers in Parliament (Article 88)
- A Minister may **speak** in both Houses and **joint sittings**, but **votes only** in his own House (an RS Minister or RS PM cannot vote in the Lok Sabha).
- **24 Jul 2026**: MoS **Ravneet Singh Bittu's** resignation accepted "under **Article 75(2)**" after his RS term ended.
- Giriraj Singh (Begusarai) – Union **Textiles** Minister, 18th LS.
### 8. SIZE OF THE COUNCIL OF MINISTERS – 91ST AMENDMENT, 2003
- Before 2003 the number of Ministers was **not in the Constitution**.
- **Article 75(1A)**: Ministers incl. PM **not more than 15%** of LS total strength (543 → max **81**).
- **Article 164(1A)** (States): 15% of Assembly incl. CM; **minimum 12**. West Bengal (294): 15% = 44.1 → **max 44**.
- Aims: limit ministry size; strengthen **anti-defection law**.
- Lok Sabha **quorum**: one-tenth of total membership.
!TRAP The APPSC key gave the "97th Amendment" for limiting cabinet size; the 97th Amendment (2011) dealt with **cooperative societies** – the size cap is the **91st (2003)**.
- **130th Constitution Amendment Bill, 2025**: PM/CM/Minister detained **30 consecutive days** for an offence punishable with **5+ years** is removed on the **31st day**; touches **Articles 75, 164, 239AA**.
### 9. CATEGORIES OF MINISTERS; CABINET; NON-CONSTITUTIONAL POSTS
#### 9.1 Council vs Cabinet
| Basis | Council of Ministers | Union Cabinet |
|---|---|---|
| Size | About 60–80 | About 15–20 |
| Composition | Cabinet Ministers, MoS (independent charge), MoS, Deputy Ministers | PM + Cabinet-rank Ministers only |
| Role | Arts 74–75 body | **Supreme decision-making body** |
- **Cabinet Ministers** head major ministries, attend Cabinet; **MoS (independent charge)** own ministry; **MoS** attached; **Deputy Ministers** only assist.
- The Constitution **does not classify** Ministers into ranks – **convention** and PM's choice.
- Word **"Cabinet"** not in the original Constitution; inserted in **Article 352(3)** by the **44th Amendment, 1978** (Cabinet's written advice for Emergency).
#### 9.2 Non-constitutional posts
| Post | Facts |
|---|---|
| **Deputy PM** | Not in the Constitution; political need; oath as ordinary Minister; no PM powers; first **Sardar Patel** (15 Aug 1947 – 15 Dec 1950), also Home Minister |
| **Leader of the House (LS)** | Not constitutional; PM if LS member, else his nominee; first Nehru; **Pranab Mukherjee** under Manmohan Singh (RS PM) |
| **PMO** | Extra-constitutional staff agency |
- **AP angle**: **1956 Gentlemen's Agreement** – united AP cabinet **60% Andhra : 40% Telangana**.

### 11. OATHS, RESIGNATIONS AND REMOVAL – EXECUTIVE HEADS
| Office | Oath Article | Oath by | Resigns to |
|---|---|---|---|
| President | 60 | **CJI** | VP |
| PM, Union Ministers | 75(4) | President | President |
| Governor | 159 | **HC Chief Justice** | President |
| CM, State Ministers | 164(3) | Governor | Governor |
- Governor holds office at pleasure (Art 156) – **no impeachment**; State Ministers dismissed by the Governor on the CM's advice.
- Oaths of President, VP and Governor are written in **Articles 60, 69, 159** – **not** the Third Schedule; ministers' oaths are in the Third Schedule.
- **Speaker** takes **no oath of office**; resigns to the **Deputy Speaker** (Art 94).
