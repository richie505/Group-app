## U0572 | SKILL DEVELOPMENT AND EMPLOYMENT IN AP – APSSDC, EMPLOYMENT SCHEMES
### 1. APSSDC
- **AP State Skill Development Corporation**, set up **2014** as a not-for-profit company on the lines of NSDC.
- **Nodal agency for placement-linked training** via **Skill Hubs, Skill Spokes**, colleges and **Sector Skill Councils**; 50 skill hubs in rural colleges (Jan 2026).
- **70% placement** minimum for all State skill schemes.
- All skill departments merging into one **Commissionerate of Skill, Employment and Training** – "**One District – One Skill**" (Apr 2026).
- FY 2025-26: 1,11,159 trained in Employability Skill Centres; **PM Internship Scheme 72,481** enrolments.
- PM-SETU: all **83 Govt ITIs** to be upgraded as 16 hubs + 67 spokes.
### 2. SKILL PROGRAMMES AND SCHEMES
| Scheme | Target group / purpose |
|---|---|
| **Naipunyam** | AI portal: skilling, certification, **job matching**; goal **20 lakh by 2029** |
| **Skill International** | **German (A1–B2)** training for overseas jobs, mainly **nurses** |
| LoI (6 May 2026) | with **Japan's FVCC** (not German) |
| **Skill Census 2024** | household survey of skills of **100% of youth** |
| **Nirudyoga Bruthi** (Super Six) | **₹3,000/month** to unemployed youth |
| **Yuva Nestham** | allowance for educated youth, **2014–19** TDP government |
- **Super Six** target: **20 lakh jobs in 5 years**.
- National: Union Budget 2023-24 – **30 Skill India International Centres**; **PMKVY 4.0** – Industry 4.0 courses (coding, AI, robotics, mechatronics, IoT, 3D printing, drones, soft skills).
- **World Youth Skills Day = 15 July** (UNGA, 2014); 2021 theme "**Reimagining Youth Skills Post-Pandemic**".
!TRAP Nirudyoga Bruthi = current Super Six; Yuva Nestham = 2014–19.
### 3. EMPLOYMENT ACTS
| Act | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **AP Employment of Local Candidates in Industries/Factories Act** | **July 2019** | **75%** private jobs for locals |
| **VB-G RAM G** (125-day Act) | began **1 Jul 2026** | **125 days**; pause up to **60 days** in peak season; **60:40** Centre:State |
- Local Act criticised nationally as a **deterrent to investors**.
- VB-G RAM G national launch: **Mukkavaripalli (Tirupati district)**.
### 4. UNEMPLOYMENT AND MGNREGS
- PLFS 2023-24: AP unemployment **4.1%** (all ages); youth unemployment about **17.5%**, far above national.
- **MGNREGS 2025-26**: AP **3rd** nationally; **1,635.10 lakh person-days** (labour budget 1,800 lakh); **women 60.54%**; 1.06 lakh households got 100 days.
- Wage rate 2025-26: **₹307/day** (from ₹300).
### 5. LIVELIHOOD MISSIONS
| Mission | Society |
|---|---|
| **DAY-NRLM** (rural) | **SERP** – SHG-bank linkage 1st in India |
| **DAY-NULM** (urban) | **MEPMA** – 2.74 lakh SHGs |
| **DDU-GKY** (rural youth) | **SEEDAP** |
- **Streenidhi**: loans up to ₹1 lakh at 11%. **20.31 lakh Lakhpati Didis**.
### 6. RECRUITMENT AND SECRETARIATS
- Mega DSC-2025: 15,941 teachers. Upper age limit **44 years till 2027**.
- Secretariats renamed **Swarna Gramam and Swarna Ward** department; staff as Village Skill Officers.
- AP **top State on iGOT Karmayogi** (1 crore+ enrolments).
### 7. OVERSEAS EMPLOYMENT
- **OMCAP** (Overseas Manpower Company AP Ltd): agency under **Emigration Act, 1983**.
- Gulf migration heavy among women from **Konaseema**; housemaids must go via OMCAP – **over 90%** of abuse complaints from others.
