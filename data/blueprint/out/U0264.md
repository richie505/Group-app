## U0264 | LATER REORGANISATION OF STATES – 1960s TO 2000; CREATION OF TELANGANA 2014
### 1. ARTICLES 2, 3, 4 – CONSTITUTIONAL PROCEDURE
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Article 2** | Admission/establishment of new states from **outside** India (e.g. **Sikkim**, 36th Amendment) |
| **Article 3** | Parliament may by law **form a new state**, increase/diminish area, alter boundaries or name |
| **Article 4** | Such a law is **not an amendment under Article 368** |
- Bill only on the **President's recommendation**.
- President refers it to the **affected state legislature** for views within a set time (time limit added by the **5th Amendment, 1955**).
- Views **not binding**; no fresh reference for each amendment to the Bill; no reference for a UT.
- Passed by **simple majority**.
- **APRA 2014** was made under **Articles 3 and 4** – not Article 2.
- Present count: **28 States and 8 UTs** (after J&K reorganisation 2019 and the Dadra & Nagar Haveli–Daman & Diu merger, **January 2020**).
!TRAP APRA basis = Article 3, not Article 2.

### 3. MULKI RULES – ORIGIN AND CHRONOLOGY
#### 3.1 Origin and Meaning
- **1888**, Hyderabad State: locals to get State posts; outsider only with the **Prime Minister's (Diwan's)** permission.
- **1919 Nizam's Farman**: outsider needed the **Nizam's personal permission**.
- **Mulki** = person with a fixed period of continuous residence: **15 years** (Nizam's rules); **12 years** (Gentlemen's Agreement, 1956).
#### 3.2 Chronology
| Year | Event | Stance |
|---|---|---|
| 1956 | Gentlemen's Agreement – domicile safeguards | Both regions |
| 1957 | **Public Employment (Requirement as to Residence) Act** | Residence condition for Telangana posts |
| **1969** | **Jai Telangana** | Telangana – **for** Mulki rules |
| **1972** | **Jai Andhra** | Andhra – **against** Mulki rules |
| **1973** | Six-Point Formula; **32nd Amendment** | Compromise |
| 1975 | Presidential Order – local cadres | Implementation |
| **1985** | **GO 610** | Telangana grievance |
| 2001–04 | **Girglani Commission** | Telangana grievance |
!KEY PYQ order: Jai Telangana → Jai Andhra → 32nd Amendment → GO 610 → Girglani Report.
!NUM 8 – 5 – 6 = 1969 – 1972 – 1973 (Eight-, Five-, Six-Point formulas)
### 4. JAI TELANGANA 1969
#### 4.1 Causes – Safeguard → Violation
| 1956 safeguard | Alleged violation |
|---|---|
| Mulki rules for Telangana posts | Non-locals recruited; local posts reclassified as state-cadre |
| Surplus spent only in Telangana | Surplus diverted |
| Regional Committee supervision | Political promises, not enforceable in court |
- Flashpoint **1968**: regional sub-committee extended five years; State asked Centre to extend Mulki rules.
#### 4.2 Course
- Began as a **student strike at Khammam, January 1969** (student leaders such as Mallikarjun); **first hunger strike** – student **Ravindranath**.
- Students → political movement under the **TPS**.
- Police firings: **over 360** killed – **Telangana martyrs of 1969**.
- **Prof. K. Jayashankar** (former VC, Kakatiya University) – ideologue; active in 1952 and 1969 (Jatipita title debate, Feb 2026).
#### 4.3 Telangana Praja Samithi
- Formed **February 1969**; convener **A. Madan Mohan**; president **Marri Chenna Reddy**; renamed Dec 1970; symbol spade.
- **1971 Lok Sabha election**: won **10 of 14** seats contested.
- **Merged with Congress (September 1971)** after agreement with Indira Gandhi; opponents formed the **Complete TPS** under **B. Satyanarayana Reddy**.
- **CM in 1969**: **Kasu Brahmananda Reddy** – forced to resign (Sept 1971); **P.V. Narasimha Rao** sworn in **30 Sep 1971**.
#### 4.4 Government Responses
- **Eight-Point Formula** – **11 April 1969**, PM **Indira Gandhi**, Delhi:
1. High-level committee under a Supreme Court judge.
2. Special committee on Telangana surplus funds.
3. Supervisory committee over Telangana schemes.
4. Committee to inspect development schemes.
5. Special powers for Telangana officers.
6. Legal opinion on constitutional job promises.
7. Advisory committee under the SR Act 1956.
8. PM's six-monthly review with central ministers and Planning Commission Vice-Chairman.
| Committee | Task |
|---|---|
| **Bhargava** (Justice Vashist Bhargava) | Telangana's **revenue surpluses** |
| **Lalit** (Kumar Lalit) | **Official accounting** of surpluses |
| **Wanchoo** (Justice K.N. Wanchoo) | Legal opinion on **job safeguards** |
### 5. COURT VERDICTS ON MULKI RULES
| Year | Case | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **1969** | **A.V.S. Narasimha Rao v. State of AP** | SC struck down the Telangana residence clause of the **1957 Act** – under **Art 16(3)** only Parliament may set residence conditions, not for part of a state |
| **Oct 1972** | **Director of Industries v. V. Venkata Reddy** | SC **upheld the older Mulki Rules** – valid and in force → triggered Jai Andhra |
| 1973 | Six-Point Formula | Mulki Rules repealed; **TRC dissolved 1 Jan 1974** |
!TRAP 1969 ruling = 1957 Act struck down; 1972 ruling = old Mulki Rules upheld.
### 6. JAI ANDHRA 1972–73
- **Year 1972** (into 1973), Coastal Andhra and Rayalaseema – **against Mulki Rules**.
- **Immediate cause**: SC judgment of **October 1972** upholding the Mulki Rules.
- **Main issue**: Andhras treated as "outsiders" in Hyderabad, a capital built with the whole State's revenue; demand – repeal or a **separate Andhra State**.
- **Five-Point Formula – 27 Nov 1972**: Mulki rules in twin cities till **1977** and Telangana till **1980**; **1 in 3** Secretariat posts to Telangana locals; first two gazetted posts localised; Asst. Surgeons and Asst. Engineers brought under Mulki rules. Indira ruled out a separate Andhra.
- Separate Andhra **flag hoisted 10 Dec 1972**.
- **CM**: **P.V. Narasimha Rao**; resigned **18 Jan 1973**.
- **President's Rule**: **18 Jan – 10 Dec 1973** (~11 months) – **first in AP**, due to breakdown of law and order.
- **Jalagam Vengala Rao** CM from **10 Dec 1973**.
- **Ended by the Six-Point Formula** (21 Sep 1973); agitation called off **1 Oct 1973**.
| Leader | Role |
|---|---|
| **Kakani Venkataratnam** | Congress leader who spearheaded it |
| **B.V. Subba Reddy** | Congress leader in it |
| **Tenneti Viswanatham** | Prominent Andhra leader of the movement |
| Students | Core of the movement |
!TRAP Jai Andhra ended by the **Six-Point Formula**, not repression or mere Mulki abolition.
### 7. SIX-POINT FORMULA AND 32nd AMENDMENT
#### 7.1 Six Points (Centre, 21 Sep 1973; approved 18 Dec 1973)
| No. | Clause | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mulki Rules and TRC abolished | Uniform legal space |
| 2 | Local preference in direct recruitment; tribunal for service disputes | Local cadres, **6 zones**, **AP Administrative Tribunal** |
| 3 | Employees' grievances committee | — |
| 4 | Backward-area development | **State Planning Commission** with local sub-committees |
| 5 | Local preference in admissions | **Central University, Hyderabad** (Art 371E) |
| 6 | Constitutional amendment | **32nd Amendment**, Presidential Order 1975 |
#### 7.2 32nd Amendment, 1973
- Inserted **Art 371D** and **Art 371E**; in force **1 July 1974** (APHQ 2019).
- **371D**: equitable opportunities in jobs and education for different parts; local cadres, local areas by **Presidential Order**; **Administrative Tribunal** (out of High Court jurisdiction).
- **371E**: Parliament may set up a **Central University** in the State.
!TRAP 32nd, not 33rd, Amendment.
### 8. PRESIDENTIAL ORDER 1975, GO 610, GIRGLANI
- **AP Public Employment (Organisation of Local Cadres and Regulation of Direct Recruitment) Order, 1975**: **6 zones** – **I–III Coastal Andhra**, **IV Rayalaseema**, **V–VI Telangana** (**Hyderabad in Zone VI**).
| Zone | Districts (undivided AP) |
|---|---|
| II | East Godavari, West Godavari, Krishna |
| VI | Hyderabad, Ranga Reddy, Nizamabad, Mahbubnagar, Medak, Nalgonda |
- Posts organised district/zone/multi-zone-wise; direct recruitment reserved for locals.
- **GO 610** – **30 Dec 1985**, **NTR** government: repatriate non-locals appointed against the 1975 Order; restore posts to Telangana locals.
- **Girglani Commission** – one-man (**J.M. Girglani**), set up **2001** by **Chandrababu Naidu**; report **2004**: large-scale violations, especially **Zone VI**.
- After 2014: AP kept **Zones I–IV** for its 13 districts. **AP Public Employment Order-2025**: **6 zones and 2 multi-zones** (Cabinet Apr 2026; mapping May 2026).

| Body | Chairman | Mandate |

#### 12.2 Rivers and Polavaram
- Most contentious rivers: **Krishna and Godavari** (APPSC 2025); **Apex Council** chaired by Union Water Resources Minister with both CMs.
- **Polavaram–Banakacherla** link: Telangana says it violates APRA and the **GWDT Award (1980)** (Jul 2025).
- **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Ordinance, 2014** amended **Sec 3** to move Khammam submergence villages (G.O.Ms. 111, 27 June 2005) to AP.
- Polavaram originally **Ramapada Sagaram**, proposed by **Sir Shonthi Venkataramamurthy**.
- **Special Category Status**: PM's assurance in the **Rajya Sabha**, not in the Act.
- **Aug 2026**: Telangana Deputy CM **Bhatti Vikramarka** sought closure of pending APRA issues and Krishna rights.
- AP districts: **13 (2014) → 26 (2022) → 28 (2025)**, new Polavaram, Markapuram; Governor **S. Abdul Nazeer**, CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu**.
