### 1. SCHEMES AT A GLANCE
| Scheme | Year | Ministry / Aim |
|---|---|---|
| **Grameen Vitrak Yojana** | 2009 | Rural share of LPG distributors **18% → 60%** |

- Access moved from connections → discom viability (UDAY, RDSS) → prosumers (PM Surya Ghar).
### 2. RURAL ELECTRIFICATION – DDUGJY AND SAUBHAGYA
- **DDUGJY:** **feeder separation** (agricultural vs non-agricultural feeders), rural network strengthening; subsumed **RGGVY**.
- Last village electrified: **Leisang (Manipur), 28 April 2018**.
- **Saubhagya** (Pradhan Mantri Sahaj Bijli Har Ghar Yojana): **2.86 crore** willing households (2.82 crore = older, Mar 2021); 100% claimed.
- Leftovers under RDSS: ₹6,521 crore for 13.65 lakh households.
### 3. UDAY vs RDSS
| Feature | UDAY | RDSS |
|---|---|---|
| Aim | Discom debt takeover | Smart metering, loss cut |
| Key fact | States took **75%** of discom debt (50% in 2015-16 + 25% in 2016-17) via bonds | Outlay **₹3,03,758 crore** |
| AT&C target | **15% by 2018-19** | **12–15%** |
| ACS-ARR gap | Zero | Zero |
- UDAY: participating states covered **97%** of discom debt (APPSC 2017).
### 4. PM-KUSUM
| Component | Covers | Progress |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Grid-connected solar/RE plants on farm/barren land | 667.31 MW |
| **B** | **Standalone solar pumps** | 9.42 lakh |
| **C** | Solarisation of grid-connected pumps | 10.99 lakh |
- B and C subsidy: **30% Centre, 30% State, 40% farmer** (bank loan possible); 21.77 lakh+ farmers benefit.
!TRAP Urban household rooftop solar = PM Surya Ghar, not PM-KUSUM.
### 5. PM SURYA GHAR MUFT BIJLI YOJANA
- Outlay **₹75,021 crore**; **1 crore households by March 2027** (FY 2026-27).
- **Up to 300 units/month free**; households become **prosumers**.
- **CFA: 60% for first 2 kW; 40% for extra up to 3 kW**.
- 30 GW rooftop; **one Model Solar Village per district**; **world's largest domestic rooftop solar scheme**; subsumed Rooftop Solar Phase II.
- Progress (2025): 14.43 lakh systems, 18.14 lakh households.
- **Net metering:** surplus sent to grid; household pays only the difference.
### 6. CLEAN COOKING – PMUY
- Phase I: **8 crore** connections by **2019**; now **9.5 crore+**.
- **Ujjwala 2.0:** free first refill and stove; migrants by **self-declaration**; **₹1,600** per 14.2 kg connection.
- Refill subsidy since **Oct 2023: ₹300 × 9 refills/year** (older ₹200 × 12).
- Refill issue: cost and habit barriers.
### 7. ANDHRA PRADESH
- **Free power up to 200 units/month** for **20.26 lakh BPL SC/ST** households; subsidy ₹10,449.92 crore (FY 2024-25).
- **SC/ST: free rooftop solar via Virtual Net Metering**.
- PM Surya Ghar: target **6 lakh** (6,000 per constituency); SPDCL 47%, CPDCL 35%, EPDCL 32% (Aug 2026).
- PM-KUSUM: **2.93 lakh** pumps solarised; RDSS ₹15,083.14 crore.
