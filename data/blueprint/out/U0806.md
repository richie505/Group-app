## U0806 | EPIDEMIC AND PANDEMIC CHALLENGES IN INDIA – MAJOR OUTBREAKS, LESSONS FROM COVID-19
### 1. OUTBREAK TERMINOLOGY
| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Endemic** | Always present at a steady level in a place or group | — |
| **Epidemic** | **Sudden, severe outbreak in a region** at a particular time; many cases in a short period | Nipah in Kerala |
| **Pandemic** | Epidemic spread **across countries and continents** | COVID-19 |
| **Epizootic** | Epidemic **among animals** | — |
!TRAP Epizootic is the animal epidemic; endemic is constant presence, not a sudden outbreak.
### 2. GLOBAL OUTBREAKS
#### 2.1 Virus, Year and Host
| Outbreak | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Ebola** | First **1976** – simultaneous outbreaks at **Yambuku (Zaire/DR Congo)** and **Nzara (Sudan)**; named after the **Ebola River**; family **Filoviridae** (not Coronaviridae); fruit-bat reservoir; Zaire species deadliest |
| Ebola 2014–16 | **West Africa**: Guinea first, then **Sierra Leone and Liberia** (worst hit); PHEIC **August 2014** |
| **Nipah** | **1998**, Malaysia (named after **Sungai Nipah**) |
| **SARS** (2002–03) | SARS-CoV-1; **civet cats** |
| **MERS** (2012) | MERS-CoV; **dromedary camels** |
| **H1N1** "swine flu" | Influenza A; North America; WHO pandemic **June 2009** |
| **H5N1** | Avian influenza A |
- **Robert Webster** – influenza virus (avian influenza expert).
#### 2.2 WHO PHEIC Declarations
- **H1N1 2009 → polio 2014 → Ebola 2014 and 2019 → Zika 2016 → COVID-19 (30 Jan 2020) → mpox 2022 and 2024**.
- **May 2026**: **Bundibugyo Ebola** outbreak in **DR Congo (Ituri) and Uganda** declared a **PHEIC**.
!TRAP The 2026 Ebola outbreak is a PHEIC, NOT a "pandemic emergency".
### 3. SARS-CoV-2 BASICS
- Virus = **SARS-CoV-2**; disease = **COVID-19 = "Coronavirus Disease 2019"**.
- First identified: **Wuhan, Hubei, China, December 2019**.
- Enters cells via the **ACE2 receptor**; reservoir **bats**, **pangolins** suspected intermediate host.
| Variant | First found |
|---|---|
| Alpha | UK |
| Beta | South Africa |
| **Delta** | **India** |
| Omicron | Southern Africa |
### 4. COVID-19 IN INDIA
| Event | Date |
|---|---|

| First wave peak | Sep 2020 |

### 5. VACCINES AND PLATFORMS
#### 5.1 COVID-19 Vaccines
| Vaccine | Developer | Platform / route |
|---|---|---|
| **Covishield** | Oxford-AstraZeneca; **Serum Institute** | Adenovirus viral vector |
| **Covaxin** | **Bharat Biotech + ICMR-NIV** | Inactivated whole virus (like **Sinovac/CoronaVac**, China) |
| **iNCOVACC** | Bharat Biotech | **World's first intranasal** for primary 2-dose + heterologous booster; adenovirus vector |
| **Corbevax** | **Biological E, Hyderabad** | Protein subunit (RBD); National Biopharma Mission |
| **ZyCoV-D** | **Zydus** | **World's first DNA vaccine** for humans; needle-free injector (**Biojector**) |
| Gemcovac-OM | Gennova | mRNA |
| **Sputnik V** (Gam-COVID-Vac) | **Gamaleya Institute, Russia** | Adenovirus vector |
| **Moderna**, Pfizer-BioNTech | Moderna (with NIAID, BARDA); Germany-USA | **mRNA** |
| Novavax | — | Recombinant protein nanoparticle |
- Free at government centres: Covaxin, Covishield, Corbevax.
#### 5.2 Platforms
| Platform | Uses |
|---|---|
| Live attenuated | Weakened living pathogen |
| Inactivated | Killed pathogen |
| **Viral vector** | A modified, harmless **different virus** carries antigen instructions |
| mRNA / DNA | Genetic instructions for antigen |
| Recombinant | Genetically engineered antigen protein |
| Toxoid | Inactivated bacterial toxin |
- COVID vaccines can also build immunity using **live attenuated** SARS-CoV-2.
#### 5.3 Pioneers and Classic Vaccines
| Scientist | Associated with |
|---|---|
| **Edward Jenner** (1749–1823) | **Father of immunology**; smallpox vaccine **1796**, the first vaccination |
| **Jonas Salk** | First successful **polio** vaccine (1952); poliovirus affects the CNS |
| Louis Pasteur | **Rabies** vaccine; germ theory |
| Robert Koch | Founded **bacteriology**; found TB bacterium – not father of immunology |
| Elie Metchnikoff | **Phagocytosis**, cellular immunity |
- Smallpox – **variola virus**, droplets in close contact; **BCG** – TB, given just after birth; DPT – diphtheria, pertussis, tetanus; MMR – measles, mumps, rubella; tetanus – *Clostridium tetani*.
- **TB kills the most** (WHO, outside COVID): **1.25 million** deaths in 2023 (3.23 lakh in India).

### 9. LESSONS FROM COVID-19
- Exposed: **health infrastructure and oxygen gaps**, **migrant crisis**, dependence on imported supplies (nearly all 20+ vaccine/diagnostic reagents imported in 2020).
- **IIT Bombay** (with Tata Consulting Engineering) converted a **nitrogen generator into an oxygen generator** (second wave).
- Digital tools: **CoWIN, U-WIN, ABDM, eSanjeevani**; One Health; equitable vaccine access.
### 10. ANDHRA PRADESH COVID RESPONSE
- **Door-to-door fever surveys** by ward/village volunteers and secretariats.
- COVID treatment added to **YSR Aarogyasri**.
- **Covid Pharma app** (25 Apr 2020): tracked **OTC sales of fever and cough medicines**.
- Testing: AP had the **highest tests per million among large states**, crossing **1 lakh tests per million** by Oct 2020.
