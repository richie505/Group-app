## U0245 | INA TRIALS AND THE NAVAL MUTINY OF 1946; IMPACT ON BRITISH DECISION TO QUIT
### 1. INA TRIALS - VENUE, ACCUSED, VERDICT
- Venue: **Red Fort, Delhi**; **November 1945 - May 1946** (first trial Nov 1945).
- Accused officers: **Prem Kumar Sahgal**, **Shah Nawaz Khan** (Muslim), **Gurbaksh Singh Dhillon** - a symbol of unity (UPSC 2021).
- Verdict: **guilty of waging war**; **transportation for life** remitted/cancelled by C-in-C **Claude Auchinleck** (**January 1946**).
- **Rashid Ali:** later **7-year sentence** sparked protests (Calcutta, **11 February 1946**).
### 2. INA DEFENCE COMMITTEE
- Set up by the **Indian National Congress**.
- **Lead counsel: Bhulabhai Desai** (his last major contribution).
- Others: **Tej Bahadur Sapru, Asaf Ali, Sarat Chandra Bose, K.N. Katju, Jawaharlal Nehru**.
- Did NOT appear: **C.R. Das** and **Motilal Nehru** (dead by then); **Jinnah** (UPPSC 2013: Sapru, not Jinnah).
- BPSC 2020: of Rajendra Prasad, Bhulabhai Desai, K.M. Munshi, Patel - **only Bhulabhai Desai**.
!TRAP APPSC 2018: Bhulabhai Desai was **counsel**, not an accused.
### 3. INA DAY AND PUBLIC CAMPAIGNS
- **INA Day: 12 November 1945** (UPPSC 2013 key).
- **21 November 1945:** Calcutta student protest; police firing killed **two**.
- **Congress, League and Communists** united in support of the accused.
### 4. ROYAL INDIAN NAVY MUTINY, 1946
- RIN established **1934**.
- Began **18 February 1946** - ratings of **HMIS Talwar**, **Bombay**.
- Grievances: **poor food, low pay, racial discrimination**.
- Scale: about **20,000** personnel, **78 ships**, **20 shore establishments**; spread to **Karachi** (not only Bombay).
| Person / Group | Role |
|---|---|
| **M.S. Khan** | President, **Naval Central Strike Committee** |
| **Aruna Asaf Ali**, Communists | **Backed** the revolt |
| Bombay mill, railway workers, students | Joined the ratings |
| **Patel and Jinnah** | **Persuaded surrender** |
- **Congress and League flags** hoisted together; events 18-22 February, then surrender.
- **18 Feb 2026:** **80th anniversary** observed; recalled at the 80th Independence Day (Aug 2026).
!KEY Flags flew together, yet both leaderships asked for surrender; only Communists and Aruna Asaf Ali backed it.

### 6. IMPACT ON THE BRITISH DECISION
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Loss of loyalty | INA trials + RIN mutiny **destroyed the loyalty** of the armed forces |
| Military reliance | The **2.5-million Indian Army** - the Raj's "sword" - could no longer be trusted |
| Armed forces | RIAF and Army also showed disaffection |
- **Attlee:** Gandhi's role in the immediate transfer of power **"minimal"** compared with the loss of military loyalty.
- RIN revolt was of **naval ratings**, not civilian workers.
