### 2. NPIT 2012 – VISION AND OBJECTIVES
- **Two goals**: bring **ICT within reach of all**; make India the **Global Hub and Destination for IT and ITES Services by 2020**.
- Context (2011–12): IT = **USD 100 bn** industry; **~80%** of revenue from **exports**; **six hubs** (Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Pune, NCR) ≈ **90%** of industry.
- Aimed to **spread IT beyond the six hubs** to Tier II and Tier III cities (not concentrate).
- Objectives: jobs; ICT in education, health, skills, financial inclusion, governance; e-literacy for all.

| e-literacy | **One e-literate person per household** |
### 4. NATIONAL POLICY ON ELECTRONICS 2019
- Aim: India a global hub for **ESDM** (Electronics System Design and Manufacturing), **including chipsets**.
| NPE 2019 target (by **2025**) | Value |
|---|---|
| Electronics manufacturing | **US$ 400 bn** (~₹26 lakh crore) |
| Mobile handsets | 1 billion a year; 600 million for export |
| Mobile manufacturing / exports | **US$ 190 bn / US$ 110 bn** |
- Schemes under NPE (2020): **PLI** (large-scale electronics), **SPECS** (components and semiconductors), **EMC 2.0** (notified 1 Apr 2020; electronics clusters + common facility centres).
- **MPMS** (Aug 2026): **₹62,500 crore**, FY 2026-27 to 2030-31; production ₹39 lakh crore, exports ₹15 lakh crore; replaces PLI (Large Scale Electronics).
!TRAP NPIT 2012 targets = 2020 ($300 bn); NPE 2019 targets = 2025 (US$ 400 bn).

| Pillar | Max support |

### 7. OUTCOMES
- Digital economy **~12–14% of GDP**; projected **~20% by 2029-30**.
- Electronics production 2025-26: **₹13.11 lakh crore**; electronics = **3rd-largest export** item.
- IT + e-commerce employment 14.67 million (2022-23).
- **GCCs**: offshore units of MNCs serving their **own parent**; outsourcing firms serve others.
### 8. AP IT POLICY
- **AP IT & GCC Policy 4.0 (2024-29)**: in force **12 Dec 2024**.
- AP Data Centre Policy: up to **1 GW**.
- **Visakhapatnam**: **cable landing station**, **AI data projects with Anakapalli**, **Infosys** campus (~7,000 jobs), **IoT CoE**.
- **Amaravati**: **Quantum Valley** (C-DAC centre), **NIELIT** campus.
