## U0234 | ROWLATT SATYAGRAHA AND JALLIANWALA BAGH; HUNTER COMMITTEE
### 1. ROWLATT ACT, 1919
#### 1.1 Name and Origin
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Official name | **Anarchical and Revolutionary Crimes Act, 1919** |
| Popular names | **"Black Act"** / "Black Law" (Gandhi's term) |
| Committee behind it | **Sedition Committee (Rowlatt Committee)**, appointed **1917**, headed by **Justice Sidney Rowlatt** |
| Bills presented | Two bills in the Imperial Legislative Council, **6 February 1919** – one dropped, one passed |
| Passed | **10 March 1919** |
| Basis | Extension of the **Defence of India Act, 1915** |
| Repealed | Under Viceroy **Lord Reading**, along with the **Press Act, 1910** |
#### 1.2 Provisions Opposed
- **Arrest without warrant** and **detention without trial** up to **two years**; summary procedures.
- **In-camera (secret) trials** by a **three-judge High Court panel**, **no appeal**; special tribunals.
- Aim: curb national and revolutionary activities; punish dissent.
- Slogan: **"No Vakil, No Dalil, No Appeal"** – an Act without lawyer, plea or appeal.
- Passed despite opposition of **all Indian members** of the Imperial Legislative Council.
- **Jinnah, Madan Mohan Malaviya, Mazhar-ul-Haq** resigned from the Council in protest (they did not draft the Hunter report).
!TRAP The claim that Mohammad Ali and Abul Kalam Azad resigned from the Council in 1916 is FALSE.
### 2. ROWLATT SATYAGRAHA
#### 2.1 1919 in Dates
| Date (1919) | Event |
|---|---|
| 24 February | Gandhi warned; **Satyagraha Sabha** formed at **Bombay** (using **Home Rule League** networks) |
| 10 March | Rowlatt Act passed |
| **6 April** | Nationwide **hartal** – fasting, prayer, "national humiliation" |
| 8 April | Gandhi at **Vijayawada** |
| **9 April** | **Kitchlew and Satyapal arrested** (not 13 April) |
| **13 April** | **Jallianwala Bagh** massacre; martial law in Amritsar and Lahore |
| **18 April** | Satyagraha **withdrawn** – violence in Punjab and Ahmedabad; **"Himalayan blunder"** |
| **31 May** | **Tagore** renounced knighthood |
| **14 October** | **Hunter Committee** set up |
| **26-30 December** | **Amritsar Congress session** |
#### 2.2 Nature of the Struggle
- Gandhi's **first all-India campaign**; **first all-India mass strike**; **urban-centred** (not limited to rural Punjab).
- Gandhi tried to use the **Home Rule League** for it.
- A **no-tax campaign** against the Act was suggested by **Swami Shraddhanand**.
!KEY Event just before the massacre = enactment of the Rowlatt Act (March 1919) and the country-wide strike of 6 April 1919.
#### 2.3 Andhra Response
- Peaceful hartals at **Vizag, Rajahmundry, Guntur, Vijayawada, Nellore**.
- Gandhi's speech at **Vijayawada (8 April 1919)** translated by **Ayyadevara Kaleswara Rao**.
- **Andhra Patrika** and **Krishna Patrika** opposed the Act.
- **Ganjam, Godavari and Krishna** district conferences condemned the massacre; **Prakasam** (Nellore meeting, Sept 1919) demanded fundamental rights.
- A **bar association** demanded the jailing of General Dyer.
### 3. JALLIANWALA BAGH MASSACRE – 13 APRIL 1919
#### 3.1 The Incident
- Place: **Amritsar, Punjab**; date: **13 April 1919**; occasion: **Baisakhi**.
- Reason for the gathering: to protest the arrest of **Dr Saifuddin Kitchlew** and **Dr Satyapal** (Punjab leaders, arrested **9 April 1919**) and the Rowlatt Act; also to celebrate Baisakhi.
- **Brig.-Gen. Reginald Dyer** gave **no warning**, **blocked the only exit**, fired for about **10 minutes**, **1,650 rounds**.
| Office | Person |
|---|---|
| Viceroy | **Lord Chelmsford** (his tenure also saw the Government of India Act, 1919 – dyarchy) |
| Lt. Governor of Punjab | **Sir Michael O'Dwyer** |
| Officer who ordered firing | **Brig.-Gen. Reginald Dyer** |
- Casualties: **official 379 dead, 1,100 injured**; Congress estimated more.
- Happened in the **context of the Rowlatt Satyagraha**.
!TRAP O'Dwyer = Lt. Governor; Dyer = officer who fired.
### 4. MARTIAL LAW IN PUNJAB AND THE CRAWLING ORDER
- Martial law in **Amritsar and Lahore** under **O'Dwyer's** regime.
- **Public floggings** and **aircraft strafing** of protestors.
- **Crawling order**: Indians had to **crawl on their bellies** through the Amritsar lane where English missionary **Marcella Sherwood** had been assaulted.
### 5. PROTESTS, RESIGNATIONS AND RENUNCIATIONS
| Person | Protest |
|---|---|
| **Rabindranath Tagore** | Gave up **Knighthood** ("Sir"), returned on **31 May 1919** |
| **Sir C. Sankaran Nair** | **Resigned** from the **Viceroy's Executive Council** – the only Indian there |
| **Gandhi** | Returned the **Kaiser-i-Hind** medal |
| **Montagu** | Called the action **"Preventive Murder"** |
- Tagore gave up knighthood **in protest at the massacre**, not to join the Non-Cooperation Movement.
- Sankaran Nair later **lost O'Dwyer's libel suit (11-1)**.
- **Apr 2025**: PM recalled Sir C. Sankaran Nair's resignation on the **106th anniversary**.
- **17 Jul 2026**: **K. Kamaraj** (birth anniversary) joined the freedom struggle after Jallianwala Bagh.
!TRAP Of the usual list, **only Sankaran Nair resigned from the Viceroy's Executive Council**.
### 6. HUNTER COMMITTEE (OFFICIAL INQUIRY)
- Also called the **Disorders Inquiry Committee**; set up **14 October 1919** by the government.
- Probed the **Jallianwala Bagh massacre** and disturbances in **Bombay, Delhi and Punjab**.
- Chairman: **Lord William Hunter**; **8 members – 5 British, 3 Indian**.
- **British members: Lord Hunter, Justice G.C. Rankin, W.F. Rice, Maj.-Gen. George Barrow, Thomas Smith**; the five British members wrote the **majority report**.
- **Indian members: Chimanlal H. Setalvad, Pandit Jagat Narayan, Sultan Ahmed Khan** – wrote a **minority report** more critical of Dyer.
- Called Dyer's action **"an error of judgement"**; Dyer censured and **relieved of command**, but no real punishment.
- **House of Lords backed Dyer**; Gandhi called the findings a **"whitewash"**.
!TRAP "Error of judgement" = Hunter Committee; "Preventive murder" = Montagu.
### 7. CONGRESS INQUIRY COMMITTEE (NON-OFFICIAL)
- Congress's own **Punjab Sub-Committee**.
- Members: **Motilal Nehru, C.R. Das, Abbas Tyabji, M.R. Jayakar, Gandhi**.
- Report **drafted by Gandhi**: firing **inhuman**; martial law **disproportionate**; demanded action against Dyer.
| Point | Hunter Committee | Congress committee |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Official | Non-official |
| Members | Hunter + 3 Indians | Motilal, C.R. Das, Tyabji, Jayakar, Gandhi |
| Verdict | "Error of judgement" | Firing inhuman |
### 8. UDHAM SINGH
- Shot **Sir Michael O'Dwyer** (former Lt. Governor of Punjab) on **13 March 1940** at **Caxton Hall, London**.
- He had witnessed the Amritsar massacre and was wounded there.
!TRAP Udham Singh's target was **O'Dwyer, not Dyer**.
### 9. IMPACT
- Faith in British justice declined; hostility to British rule deepened.
- **Turning point** in Gandhi's career; background to the **Khilafat-Non-Cooperation Movement (1920)**.
