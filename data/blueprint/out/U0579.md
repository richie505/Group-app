## U0579 | DIVISION OF ASSETS, LIABILITIES, EMPLOYEES AND INSTITUTIONS; NINTH AND TENTH SCHEDULE INSTITUTIONS
### 1. THE BASIC ASYMMETRY
- AP Reorganisation Act, 2014: assent **1 March 2014**; appointed day **2 June 2014**.
- Core rule: **assets by location**, **debts by population** (AP : Telangana = **58 : 42**, Census 2011).
- Result: AP got about **46% of revenue** but inherited about **58% of debt**.
- Financial division provisions sit in **Sections 47-64**.
!KEY Assets → location; liabilities → population ratio. This is the asymmetry AP complains of.
### 2. PRINCIPLES OF DIVISION – ITEM → BASIS → SECTION
| Item | Basis | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Land, stores, goods **inside** a State | **Location** | 48 |
| Stores, goods and property **outside** the existing State (e.g. **AP Bhavan**) | **Population ratio** | 48 |
| Treasury and bank **cash balances** | **Population ratio**, by adjusting entries in **RBI books** (no physical transfer) | 49 |
| **Arrears of taxes** (non-property) | **Place of assessment** | 50 |
| Securities against **Seventh Schedule** funds | Population ratio | 52 |
| Commercial undertakings: **operational units / headquarters** | **Location / population ratio** | 53 |
| Refund of taxes other than property taxes | **Population ratio** | 56 |
| **Provident Fund** | State the employee is **allotted to** | 58 |
| Pension liability | Eighth Schedule | 59 |
!TRAP **Calamity Relief Fund** was divided by **area ratio**, not population.
### 3. SEVENTH SCHEDULE FUNDS AND POWER SHARING
#### 3.1 Seventh Schedule
- Seventh Schedule = "**List of Funds**" in the **Public Account**: **provident, pension and insurance** funds and others (**APPSC 2022**).
- Securities held against them divided by **population ratio** (Sec 52).
#### 3.2 Electricity (Twelfth Schedule)
- **APGENCO plants** divided by **location** (not population); existing **PPAs continue**.
- **Central Generating Station** power shared on the **DISCOMs' actual consumption of the last 5 years**.
- AP got about **46% of power** for **58% of population**.
- **Surplus power**: the deficit State has the **first right of refusal** over the other State's surplus for **10 years**.
!TRAP APPSC-GS 2019 key marked "**Gadgil formula**" for the electricity distribution formula. The Act's rule is the **5-year consumption basis**; if the options force a choice, the key's answer is Gadgil.
### 4. SCHEDULE IX VS SCHEDULE X
| Feature | Schedule IX | Schedule X |
|---|---|---|
| What it lists | **Government companies and corporations** | **State institutions** whose facilities continue for both States |
| Count | **91** | **107** in the Act as enacted; **142** after **35** more were added by notification (**7 May 2015**) |
| Examples | **APSFC**, **Sports Authority of AP** | **APSCHE**, **Shilparamam** Arts, Crafts and Cultural Society, tribunals |
| Governing section | **Sec 68** (continue to function) and **Sec 53** (division) | **Sec 75** (continuance of facilities) |
| Division rule | Operational units by **location**; headquarters by **population ratio** | Assets by **location**; cash by population; facilities continue for the other State |
| Time limit | – | Terms for continuing facilities fixed within **1 year** |
| Committee | **Sheela Bhide Committee** | – |
!TRAP Schedule X: **107** = the Act's original list (APPSC 2021 answer); **142** = 107 + 35 added in May 2015. Pick 107 when the question is about the Act as passed.
- Most Schedule X institutions are **in or around Hyderabad**.
- **Section 66**: some institutions are in **no schedule**; AP uses Sec 66 to bring them into Schedule IX or X.
- Telangana's stand (10 August 2026): share only **cash balances**; institutions go by **location**.
!PAIR Which schedule? APSCHE | Schedule X | Sec 75 || APSFC | Schedule IX | Sec 68, 70
### 5. COMMITTEES ON APPORTIONMENT
| Committee | Chair | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| **Sheela Bhide Committee** | Sheela Bhide (retired IAS) | **Assets, liabilities and employees** of **Schedule IX** companies and corporations; set up by the **undivided AP government** (APPSC 2021) |
| **Kamalanathan Committee** | **C.R. Kamalanathan** | Allocation of **State-level employees** |
| **Pratyush Sinha Committee** | **Pratyush Sinha** | Allocation of **All India Services** officers (**IAS/IPS/IFoS**); formed under **Sec 80** |
- Sheela Bhide Committee recommended division of **89 of the 91** Schedule IX bodies; Telangana opposed its recommendations on **22** of them; the Union Home Ministry sought **legal opinion**.
- Its term expired in **August 2015**; the Centre later **revived** it.
- Comparative note: the **1953 Andhra State** partition committee was chaired by **T.T. Krishnamachari** (APHQ 2025).
!TRAP Sheela Bhide = Schedule IX; Kamalanathan = State employees; Pratyush Sinha = All India Services.
### 6. EMPLOYEE APPORTIONMENT – PART VIII
#### 6.1 Provisions as to Services
- **Part VIII**, "**Provisions as to Services**" (**Sections 76-83**).
- **Section 76**: separate **State cadres** of **IAS, IPS, IFoS** under the **1954/1966 Cadre Rules**.
- **Section 77**: **State employees**; does **not** apply to All India Services.
- **Advisory Committees**: Centre could set them up within **30 days** of enactment (Kamalanathan for State employees; Pratyush Sinha for AIS).
- Employees **opt** for a State; **non-opters** allotted by the Centre on **native (domicile) status**.
- **PSUs and corporations**: staff stayed in place for **1 year**; the corporation itself decided division (Sec 82).
#### 6.2 Disputes
- **Seniority and pension** disputes: **Sec 59** and **Eighth Schedule**; backlog in the **AP Reorganisation Tribunal**.
- **3 January 2024**: Telangana High Court ordered AIS allotment cases reconsidered under the Pratyush Sinha guidelines; DoPT set up a one-member **Deepak Khandekar** committee (**March 2024**).
- **October 2024**: Centre sent AIS officers serving in Telangana back to AP.
### 7. EDUCATION PROVISIONS
- **Section 95**, **Part XI** (Access to Higher Education): **equal opportunities** for quality higher education.
- **Article 371D** admission quotas continue in all higher, technical and medical institutions (government/private, aided/unaided).
- **Common admission process** to continue for **10 years**.
- **Section 97**: amended **Article 371D** to cover "**Andhra Pradesh or Telangana**".
!NUM 10 years = common admission (Sec 95) = surplus power right of refusal = common capital.
### 8. DISPUTED INSTITUTIONS – CASES AND HOME MINISTRY ORDERS
| Case / Body | Ruling / Rule |
|---|---|
| **APSCHE** (Schedule X), **Supreme Court 2016** | **Location** rule for assets; **cash and bank balances** by **population ratio** |
| Union Home Ministry after APSCHE ruling | Applied the **same formula** to other Schedule X institutions |
| **AP Bhavan, New Delhi** | Outside the undivided State → **population ratio**; Telangana claims it as **Nizam's property** |
| **APSFC** (Schedule IX) | Division under **Section 70**; shareholder-approved scheme → Centre for sanction; if not approved → a **High Court judge**; settlement **final** |
| **Supreme Court, December 2022** | AP moved for **quick division** of **Schedule IX and X** assets (APPSC-GS 2022) |
- **Status**: **over 140** corporations/institutions worth **more than ₹39,000 crore** still undivided.
- AP wants **population ratio (58:42)**; Telangana insists on **location** → deadlock.
!TRAP SC **2016** = APSCHE ruling; SC **December 2022** = AP's plea on Schedule IX and X assets.
