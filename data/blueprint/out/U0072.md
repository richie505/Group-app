## U0072 | RASHTRAKUTAS – DANTIDURGA, GOVINDA III, AMOGHAVARSHA (KAVIRAJAMARGA), KRISHNA I
### 1. FOUNDER – DANTIDURGA
- **Dantidurga** (753–756) founded the empire in **753 CE**; a former **Chalukya feudatory**.
- Overthrew Badami Chalukya **Kirtivarman II**.
- Performed the **Hiranyagarbha** ritual at **Ujjayini (Ujjain)**.
- Seat: **Elapura (Ellora)** region; his inscription is in Ellora's **Dashavatara cave**.
- Contemporary of Eastern Chalukya **Vijayaditya I**.
- Dynasty span: c. 753–982 CE.
!TRAP Dantidurga did NOT build Manyakheta – Amoghavarsha I did.
### 2. RULERS AND CONTRIBUTIONS
| Ruler | Reign | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Dantidurga** | 753–756 | Founder; Hiranyagarbha |
| **Krishna I** | – | Monolithic **Kailasa (Kailashnatha) temple**, **Ellora Cave 16** |
| **Dhruva** | 780–793 | **First to go north**: beat **Vatsaraja** and **Dharmapala**; subdued Eastern Chalukyas and Gangas of Talakad |
| **Govinda III** | 793–814 | Beat **Nagabhata II** and Dharmapala; reached the **Himalayas** (**Sanjan** and Radhanpur plates) |
| **Amoghavarsha I** | 814–878 | Built **Manyakheta**; wrote **Kavirajamarga**; Jain patron |
| **Indra III** | – | Looted **Kannauj** c. 916, defeating Pratihara Mahipala I |
| **Krishna III** | – | Beat the Cholas at **Takkolam (949)**; patron of **Ponna** |
- Order: **Dantidurga → Dhruva → Govinda III → Amoghavarsha I**.
- **Amoghavarsha I** was born in **800 CE** at **Shribhavan** near the Narmada, **in a military camp** (not Manyakheta), as father Govinda III returned from the north; a disciple of a Jain teacher.
!TRAP Wrong pair: "Krishna III – rock-cut Shiva temple at Ellora". It was **Krishna I**.
### 3. CAPITALS AND RELIGION
| Capital | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Elapura (Ellora)** | Early seat |
| **Manyakheta (Malkhed, Karnataka)** | Built by **Amoghavarsha I** |
- Supported **Shaiva, Vaishnava, Shakta** faiths; leaned to **Jainism**.
- **Kailasa** = Shaiva, **Dravidian** style, recalls Mount Kailasa.
- **Ellora**: Buddhist, Hindu and Jain caves together; Ajanta and Ellora in **Aurangabad (Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar)** district. **Elephanta** caves also Rashtrakuta.
- Golden age of Kannada; zenith of rock-cut art.

### 6. VENGI WARS (ABOUT 200 YEARS)
| Rashtrakuta | Eastern Chalukya | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Dhruva** (through Vemulavada **Arikesari I**) | **Vishnuvardhana IV** | Dhruva won; Vishnuvardhana gave daughter **Shila Mahadevi** to Dhruva |
| Rashtrakuta forces | **Vijayaditya II** | **108 battles** |
| **Amoghavarsha I** | Eastern Chalukyas | Won at **Vingavalli** |
| **Krishna II** | **Gunaga Vijayaditya III** | **Gunaga won** |
- AP angle: Vijayaditya II built **108 Shiva temples**; linked with naming **Vijayawada**.
!TRAP Vijayaditya I = Dantidurga's rival; Vijayaditya II = 108 battles; Gunaga Vijayaditya III = beat Krishna II.
### 7. BATTLE OF TAKKOLAM (949 CE)
- **Krishna III** with **Western Gangas** and **Vaidumbas** vs the **Cholas** of **Parantaka I**.
- Chola prince **Rajaditya** (son of Parantaka I) led and was **killed**; Rashtrakuta side won.
- The Rajaditya RTC at Thakkolam is named after him.
- Not to confuse: **Chola Gangam** (Ponneri lake) = **Rajendra Chola I**, for victory over the Ganga region.

### 10. END OF THE DYNASTY
- Paramara **Siyaka II** defeated **Khottiga** on the Narmada and sacked Manyakheta.
- **Tailapa II** (Kalyani Chalukya) overthrew the line c. **973**.
