## U0557 | KRISHNA AND GODAVARI WATER DISPUTES – TRIBUNALS, APEX COUNCIL, KRMB AND GRMB
### 1. CONSTITUTIONAL AND LEGAL BASE
- **Article 262**: Parliament may make law for adjudicating **inter-State river water disputes**.
- **Article 262(2)**: Parliament may provide that **neither the Supreme Court nor any other court** shall exercise jurisdiction over such disputes.
- **Inter-State River Water Disputes (ISRWD) Act, 1956** – enacted under Article 262.
  - **Section 11**: bars the Supreme Court and other courts from disputes that may be referred to a tribunal.
  - The Supreme Court held (Cauvery case, 1991) that this bar does **not** cover its appeal jurisdiction under **Article 136**.
  - **Section 5(1)**: used in **October 2023** to give **KWDT-II further terms of reference**.
- The **Apex Council** refers disputes not covered by KWDT to a tribunal under the ISRWD Act, 1956.
!KEY Article 262 = constitutional base; ISRWD Act 1956 = the law; Sec. 11 = court bar.

### 4. KWDT-II AND SECTION 89
- **Sec. 89, APRA**: term of KWDT extended for:
  - (a) **project-wise allocation** where no tribunal had made one;
  - (b) **operational protocol** for releases in **deficit flows**.
- Section 89 did **not** automatically divide undivided AP's share between AP and Telangana.
- **October 2023**: Centre gave KWDT-II further terms under **Sec. 5(1), ISRWD Act** → divide AP's share between AP and Telangana.
### 5. APEX COUNCIL (SECTION 84)
| Member | Role |
|---|---|
| **Union Minister of Water Resources (now Jal Shakti)** | **Chairperson** |
| Chief Minister of Andhra Pradesh | Member |
| Chief Minister of Telangana | Member |
- **3 members** in all (APPSC 2025, 2022, 2021 keys).
- **Sec. 84(1)**: constituted by the Centre from the appointed day.
- **Functions – Sec. 84(3)**:
  1. **Supervise** GRMB and KRMB.
  2. **Approve new projects** on Krishna/Godavari after appraisal by the **Board and CWC**.
  3. **Settle disputes by negotiation**.
  4. **Refer disputes** not covered by KWDT to a **tribunal** under the ISRWD Act, 1956.
- **Not** its function: allocating or redetermining States' shares.
!TRAP Apex Council does NOT include the PM, Home Minister or Governors.
### 6. KRMB AND GRMB (SECTIONS 85-87)
#### 6.1 Constitution and status
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| **85(1)** | Two Boards within **60 days** of the appointed day |
| **85(2)** | **HQ: KRMB in Andhra Pradesh; GRMB in Telangana** |
| **85(3)** | **Autonomous bodies** under **administrative control of the Centre** |
| **85(4)** | **Chairperson (Centre)** + **2 members per State** (technical + administrative) + **1 central expert**; full-time **Member Secretary** |
| 85(7) | **CISF** guards reservoirs |
| 86 | Staff on deputation equally from both States; States fund the Boards |
| **87** | Jurisdiction over projects **notified by the Centre** |
- AP's petition to notify **KRMB jurisdiction** was under **Section 87** (APPSC 2021 key).
- **Jal Shakti** notified both Boards' jurisdiction on **15 July 2021**.
#### 6.2 Functions (Schedule XI)
- **Regulate supply** of water and power from notified projects/headworks per **tribunal awards**.
- **Release water** per States' shares; advise on floods/droughts.
- **Not** a Board function: **allocating or redetermining** shares.
- **Sec. 92 with Schedule XI**: a defaulting State faces **financial and other penalties** from the Centre.
#### 6.3 Latest
- KRMB to install **telemetry** meters to monitor use by both States.
- KRMB three-member committee allowed **only drinking water** from **Srisailam and Nagarjuna Sagar** (El Niño, poor inflows; 25 Aug 2026).
- KRMB advised Telangana after Srisailam storage fell due to its power generation (5 Sep 2026).
!TRAP KRMB HQ = Andhra Pradesh; GRMB HQ = Telangana (Sec. 85(2)).
### 7. TUNGABHADRA BOARD AND POLAVARAM
- **Tungabhadra Board**: set up under **Section 66 of the Andhra State Act, 1953**.
- **Sec. 91(1)**: AP and Telangana **replace undivided AP** on the Board.
- **Sec. 91(2)**: canals regulated – **High Level Canal, Low Level Canal, Rajolibanda Diversion Scheme (RDS)**.
- **Sec. 90**: **Polavaram** declared a **national project**; planned **960 MW**.
!TRAP Polavaram is national under APRA Sec. 90 – not under the LARR Act 2013, and not 120 MW.
### 8. CONTESTED PROJECTS
| Project | River | Built by | Objection |

- **Telangana withdrew its SC petition** against the link (**Jan 2026**); a Jal Shakti committee under the **CWC Chairman** first met **30 Jan 2026**.
- 31st Southern Zonal Council (Mahabalipuram, Aug 2026; chaired by Union Home Minister): Telangana sought national status for Kaleshwaram and Palamuru-Rangareddy.
- Criticism: Boards lack full operational control; disputes over unapproved projects and hydel drawal continue.
