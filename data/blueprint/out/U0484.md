## U0484 | RURAL DEVELOPMENT – ISSUES, PROGRAMMES, RURAL INFRASTRUCTURE (PMGSY, PMAY-G, JAL JEEVAN MISSION)
### 1. EVOLUTION OF RURAL DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMMES
#### 1.1 Early community development
- **Etawah Pilot Project** – **September 1948**, Meheva village near Etawah (UP); by **Lt. Col. Albert Mayer** (American town planner); helped by UP Govt and the Point-4 programme; **first rural-development pilot**.
- **Community Development Programme** – **2 October 1952**; **first rural development programme**; overall development and standard of living of rural people; set the stage for Panchayati Raj.
- **National Extension Service** – **1953**; first programme of community development; CDP could not cover all India as funds were short.
- **S.K. Dey** – designer of community development; first Cooperation and Panchayati Raj Minister.
- **Balwantrai Mehta Committee** – reviewed CDP and NES; report **November 1957**; suggested **democratic decentralisation**.
!TRAP First pilot = Etawah (1948); first programme = CDP (1952). Order: CDP 1952 → NES 1953 → Mehta review 1957.

#### 1.3 Bharat Nirman (December 2005)
- Rural infrastructure; targets by end of **2009**.
- **Six components**: irrigation, drinking water, electrification, roads, housing, rural telephony.
- Includes Accelerated Irrigation Benefits Programme, river inter-linking, repair/renovation/restoration of water bodies.
- **National Rural Drinking Water Programme** – part of Bharat Nirman; renamed from ARWSP on **1 April 2009**.
!TRAP NOT in Bharat Nirman: **Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan**, **NRLM**, agro-based industries, rural hospitals.

### 4. PMGSY
- Objective: **single all-weather road** to eligible unconnected habitations; 99.6% of targeted eligible areas connected (Aug 2026).
| Area | Population norm |
|---|---|
| **Plains** | **500+** |
| Hill States, NE, deserts, Schedule V/tribal areas | **250+** |
| **LWE-affected blocks** | **100+** (not 250+) |
| Phase | Focus |
|---|---|
| PMGSY-I (2000) | New roads |
| PMGSY-II (2013) | Upgradation |
| PMGSY-III (2019) | Through routes and major rural links |
!NUM 500 – 250 – 100 = plains – hill/NE/tribal – LWE
### 5. PMAY-G
- Unit assistance: **₹1.20 lakh (plains)**; **₹1.30 lakh (hilly/difficult terrain)**.
- Beneficiaries: **SECC 2011** housing-deprivation data, verified by the **Gram Sabha**.
- Target: **2.95 crore** houses by March 2024; Cabinet (9 Aug 2024) approved **2 crore more** for **2024-25 to 2028-29** (outlay ~₹3.06 lakh crore).
- Progress: **3.13 crore** rural homes completed by mid-2026.

### 7. RURBANISATION – PURA AND SPMRM
#### 7.1 PURA
- **PURA** = Provision of Urban Amenities in Rural Areas – **Dr A.P.J. Abdul Kalam** (explained on the eve of the **54th Republic Day, 2003**).
- Elaborated in ***Target 3 Billion*** (co-author **Srijan Pal Singh**).
- Aim: **bridging the rural-urban divide** (PYQ).
#### 7.2 Shyama Prasad Mukherji Rurban Mission
- Launched **21 Feb 2016** (not 2015) at **Kurubhat, Rajnandgaon (Chhattisgarh)**; successor and expansion of PURA.
- Aim: **300+ Rurban clusters** – "rural soul with urban amenities"; curb **distress migration**.
- Objectives: stimulate **local economic development**, enhance basic services, create well-planned clusters; make rural areas socially, economically and physically sustainable.
- Funding: **convergence** (MGNREGA, PMGSY, SBM, PMAY) + **Critical Gap Funding up to 30%**; Centre:State **60:40**, **90:10** for NE and Himalayan States.
- Cluster: contiguous villages of **25,000-50,000** population (plain/coastal).
- **Rurban cluster** = rural areas showing urban signs: rising density, non-farm jobs, growing economic activity.
!TRAP SPMRM does NOT replace Gram Panchayats with urban local bodies.
### 8. RURAL DEVELOPMENT ISSUES
| Remaining gaps | Achievements |
|---|---|
| Piped water: ~18% households unconnected | Saubhagya: 100% willing-household electrification |
| O&M of water schemes, housing, last-mile roads | SBM-G: 12.19 crore toilets; 1,288 GOBARdhan plants |
| Poor quality, irregularities, cost overruns in JJM works | 5.68 lakh ODF Plus villages |
- Rural infrastructure gaps = piped water, O&M, housing, last-mile roads (**not high-speed rail**).
- **16th Finance Commission**: one-time "Urbanisation Premium" for merging peri-urban villages + a Rural-to-Urban Transition Policy.
### 9. ANDHRA PRADESH
#### 9.1 Jal Jeevan Mission in AP
- FHTC coverage (31 Mar 2024): **73%** (70.04 lakh households) vs national ~82%.
- 77,917 works worth ₹27,248.86 crore; **112 water testing labs**; 2,00,101 hand pumps.
- Plan: +5.40 lakh FHTCs (2024-25); +10 lakh each in 2025-26 and 2026-27.
- JJM 2.0 goal: safe water to **every household by 2028** (Centre-State MoU, Mar 2026).
- O&M policy (Sept 2025): maintenance to panchayats. **Madanapalle Water Grid** – drinking water for drought-prone Rayalaseema.
#### 9.2 Housing, Rurban and secretariats
- **Navaratnalu – Pedalandariki Illu**: about **30.75 lakh house sites** in **17,005 YSR Jagananna Colony** layouts.
- **Village and Ward Secretariats**: launched **2 Oct 2019** at **Karapa (East Godavari)**; ~1.26 lakh staff recruited; doorstep delivery of welfare.
- MoRD classified **359 AP panchayats** under Rurban clusters (Mar 2026).
- AP Budget 2026-27: JJM ₹4,000 crore; urban + rural housing ₹5,451 crore; VB-G RAM G (rural employment guarantee) ₹8,365 crore.
