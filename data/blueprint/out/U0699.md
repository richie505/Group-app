### 2. C.V. RAMAN
- Born **7 November 1888**. Worked at **IACS** outside office hours while in the Accountant General's Office, Calcutta (from 1907), on invitation of Amrit Lal Sircar.
- **Raman Effect** (change in wavelength of scattered light): **28 February 1928**, IACS Kolkata → **National Science Day, 28 Feb**.
- First to study the harmonic nature of **tabla and mridangam**; first Indian director of **IISc**.
- Founded **Indian Academy of Sciences (1934)** and **Raman Research Institute, Bengaluru (1948)**.
- Honours: knighted **1929**, **Nobel Physics 1930**, **Bharat Ratna 1954** – the scientist with both Nobel and Bharat Ratna; India's first science Nobel; first Asian and first non-White science Nobel.
### 3. J.C. BOSE
- Showed **plants have life** and respond to stimuli like animals; invented the **Crescograph** (measures tiny plant growth).
- Pioneer of **microwave/millimetre-wave radio** – **coherer** and **galena (semiconductor) detector**; research in both biology and physics.
- Founded **Bose Institute, Kolkata, 1917**. Knighted 1917; **FRS 1920**.
- Books: **Response in the Living and Non-Living (1902)**, **The Nervous Mechanism of Plants**, Bengali sci-fi **Niruddesher Kahini**.
### 4. S.N. BOSE
- Paper **"Planck's Law and the Hypothesis of Light Quanta" (1924)**, from a Dhaka University lecture, sent to **Einstein**, who translated it into German (Zeitschrift fur Physik) → **Bose-Einstein Statistics**.
- Particle class **"bosons"** named by **Paul Dirac**.
- **Padma Vibhushan 1954**; **FRS 1958**; National Professor. Tagore's only science book **Visva-Parichay (1937)** is dedicated to him.
### 5. SRINIVASA RAMANUJAN
- Born **22 December 1887, Erode** (TN); died 1920; collaborator **G.H. Hardy** at Cambridge.
- Works: number theory, partitions, infinite series, continued fractions, **mock theta functions**; **1729** = Hardy-Ramanujan number.
- **22 December = National Mathematics Day**; **2012 = National Mathematical Year** (125th birth anniversary).
- Biopic **"The Man Who Knew Infinity"** (2015 British film, from Robert Kanigel's 1991 book; Dev Patel as Ramanujan).
### 6. MEGHNAD SAHA
- From Dacca district (now Bangladesh). **Saha (ionisation) equation** from his theory of **thermal ionisation** (papers 1920-21) – gives temperature and ionisation state of stars; explained the Sun's and stellar spectra.
- Devised an instrument to measure the weight and pressure of solar rays.
- **Chief architect of river planning** – original plan of the **Damodar Valley Project**; elected to **Lok Sabha 1952**.
- Founded **NASI (1930)** and **Saha Institute of Nuclear Physics**, Kolkata; **FRS 1927**.
### 7. HOMI BHABHA
- **Father of India's Atomic Energy / Nuclear Programme**; first chairman of the **Atomic Energy Commission (1948)**.
- Conceived the **thorium-based three-stage** nuclear power programme; chaired the **first UN Conference on Peaceful Uses of Atomic Energy, Geneva 1955**.
- Founded **TIFR (1945, Mumbai)** and **AEET Trombay (1954)** – renamed **BARC (1967)** after his death in the Air India crash on Mont Blanc (**1966**).
- **Bhabha scattering** (electron-positron); cascade theory of cosmic-ray showers.
- **Adams Prize 1942**, **FRS 1941**, **Padma Bhushan 1954**. **Homi Bhabha Award** is for nuclear energy.
!TRAP BARC was NOT founded as BARC in 1945; TIFR = 1945, AEET = 1954, BARC name = 1967.

2. Bosons were **named by Dirac**; the paper was **translated by Einstein**.
3. **Sohonie** = first PhD (1939); **Chatterjee** = first D.Sc. (1944); **Joshi** = medicine (1886).
4. **Saha** = thermal ionisation + Damodar Valley; **Bhabha** = cosmic rays + nuclear programme.
5. Photoelectric effect: **discovered by Hertz**, explained by Einstein.
6. **28 Feb** = Science Day; **22 Dec** = Mathematics Day.
7. Bohr is **Danish**, not German.
8. BARC was named in **1967**, after Bhabha died in 1966.
