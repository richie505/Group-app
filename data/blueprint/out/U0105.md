## U0105 | SOURCES FOR MEDIEVAL INDIA - PERSIAN CHRONICLES, INSCRIPTIONS, TRAVELLERS' ACCOUNTS
### 1. NATURE OF MEDIEVAL SOURCES
- Literary (Persian/Arabic chronicles, Sanskrit and regional texts), **foreign travellers**, **inscriptions and coins**, **architecture and paintings**.
- Foreign accounts **supplement** Indian texts and must be **cross-verified** with inscriptions and archaeology.

### 3. AL-BIRUNI
- From **Khwarazm (Khiva)**, present **Uzbekistan**; came with **Mahmud of Ghazni**, **11th century** (c. 1030).
- Mahmud also patronised **Firdausi** and **Utbi**.
- Learned **Sanskrit**; wrote in **Arabic** on religion, philosophy, astronomy, **caste**.
- Recorded **Diwali** on **Kartika Amavasya**.
- Order of arrival: **Al-Biruni → Ibn Battuta → Tavernier → Manucci**.

### 8. ARCHITECTURE AND PAINTING AS SOURCES
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Trabeate → arcuate** | Shift to **true arch, dome, lime mortar** |
| **Naskh** | Qutb Shahi calligraphy for **Quranic** verses (Jami Masjid mihrab) |
| **Nastaliq** | Calligraphy for **Persian** texts |
- **Golconda painting** founded by **Mir Hashimi**; "**Quliyat Quli**" manuscript has **14 miniatures**.
- Evidence that Shah Jahan built a monument: chronicles, records, European travellers' accounts.
- Diwali's early evidence: Al-Biruni, Nicolo Conti, Ain-i-Akbari (Diwali on UNESCO Intangible Heritage list topic, Apr 2026).
### 9. BIASES IN THE CHRONICLES
- **Court bias**: histories glorify the patron; **Amir Khusrau** = court poet of **Alauddin Khalji**, went with **Malik Kafur's 1310** Warangal invasion.
- **Barani**: orthodox **Sunni** viewpoint; theorised kingship.
- Firishta's story of **Deva Raya kidnapping Nehal**: rejected by modern historians.
| Source Group | Works | Warangal invasions |
|---|---|---|
| Telugu | Pratapa Charitra, Vilasa, Kaluvacheru | **8** |
| Muslim | Amir Khusrau, **Wassaf** | **5** |
- AP angle: Marco Polo → Motupalli; Abdur Razzaq, Barbosa → Vijayanagara; Tavernier → Golconda; Isami, Firishta → Warangal.

### 12. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. Tabaqat-i-Akbari = **Nizamuddin Ahmad**; Badauni = Muntakhab-ut-Tawarikh.
2. Fatawa-i-Jahandari and Tarikh-i-Firoz Shahi = **Barani**.
3. Futuh-us-Salatin = **Isami**, not Khusrau.
4. Conti (Venice) → Vijayanagara; Marco Polo → **Motupalli**.
5. Abdur Razzaq = **Persia**, Deva Raya II; Nikitin = **Russia**, horse merchant.
6. **300 ports**: Abdur Razzaq's account, but the APPSC key gave **Barbosa**.
7. Ibn Battuta = **Morocco**, Muhammad bin Tughlaq.
8. Tavernier = **France**, Golconda diamonds.
9. Humayun-nama = **Gulbadan Begum**; Baburnama in **Turki**.
