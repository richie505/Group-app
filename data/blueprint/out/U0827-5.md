### 2. ENDURANCE AND TRANSPLANT GAMES
#### 2.1 Viswabharath Allamsetti – first Telugu to finish UTMB
- Born in **Guntur**, lives in **France** – **first Telugu runner to complete the Ultra Trail du Mont Blanc (UTMB)**.
- Time: **44 hours**.
- Race: **176.5 km loop** around the **Mont Blanc massif**, elevation gain over **10,000 m**; passes through **France, Italy, Switzerland**; ends at **Chamonix, France**; 2025 edition began **29 August**.
#### 2.2 World Transplant Games 2025 – Dresden, Germany
- Venue **Dresden, Germany**, 17–24 August 2025; ~**2,500** transplant recipients, **17 sports**, **51 countries**.
- **Biennial**, run by the **World Transplant Games Federation**.
- Ambassador: **Ivan Klasnic** (ex-Werder Bremen footballer, three-time kidney recipient).
| Indian medallist | Medal | Event | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nagavasu Naidu Sunkara** (Forest Section Officer, **Jangareddygudem Forest Division**) | **Silver** | **5 km racewalking**, organ recipient category | Liver disease 2015; liver transplant 2020 (mother donor); switched from **cycling to racewalking** |
| Raj Sarki (33, Jaigaon, Dooars) | Bronze | Running event | Kidney transplant |
!TRAP Sunkara won SILVER in RACEWALKING (not gold, not cycling), at Dresden in GERMANY (not Hyderabad).
### 3. SKATING AND BADMINTON
#### 3.1 Arun Kumar Rayudu – figure skating first
- **Arun Kumar Rayudu** (24), from **Visakhapatnam**, representing **Northeastern University (Boston)**.
- **First Indian to win gold** at the **Northeast Intercollegiate Figure Skating Championship, New York**.
- Record: **16-time national roller skating champion**; **six-time national ice skating champion**; **world No. 5 U-19 (2017)**; **Asia No. 2 senior**.

- **Arigela Bhargav Ram** (Guntur) and **Gobburu Viswa Tej** (Railway Koduru, erstwhile YSR Kadapa), both 17 – **World No. 1, BWF Junior Boys Doubles**; paired since the **2018 National Championship**.

### 7. CRICKET – NATIONAL TITLES OF THE PERIOD
#### 7.1 ICC Women's ODI World Cup 2025
- **India** won its **first-ever Women's ODI World Cup**, beating **South Africa by 52 runs** in the final at **DY Patil Stadium, Navi Mumbai**, on **2 November 2025**.
- India had lost the finals of 2005 and 2017. **Shafali Verma** and **Deepti Sharma** starred; South Africa captain **Laura Wolvaardt** scored a century in the final.
#### 7.2 IPL 2025
- **Royal Challengers Bengaluru** won their **maiden IPL title**, beating **Punjab Kings by 6 runs** at **Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad** (3 June 2025).
- RCB 190/9; PBKS 184/7. Captain **Rajat Patidar**; Virat Kohli's first IPL title.
#### 7.3 Asia Cup 2025 (T20)
- **India beat Pakistan by 5 wickets** in the final, **Dubai**, 28 September 2025 – India's **9th** Asia Cup title.
- Player of the Match (final) **Tilak Varma**; Player of the Tournament **Abhishek Sharma**.

### 9. HOSTING RIGHTS – 2030 COMMONWEALTH GAMES
- **Ahmedabad** awarded the **2030 Commonwealth Games** at the Commonwealth Sport **General Assembly, Glasgow**, **26 November 2025**; chosen over **Abuja (Nigeria)**.
- 2030 is the **centenary** edition (first Games: **Hamilton, Canada, 1930**).
- India's **second** CWG as host, after **Delhi 2010**.
- Other hosts in this period: Commonwealth Weightlifting Championships 2025 – **Ahmedabad**; FIDE World Cup 2025 – **Goa**; World Transplant Games – **Dresden**.
