## U0635 | ANDHRA PRADESH – LOCATION, EXTENT, DISTRICTS, PHYSIOGRAPHIC DIVISIONS
### 1. FORMATION OF ANDHRA PRADESH
#### 1.1 Formation → Date
| Event | Date / fact |
|---|---|
| **Potti Sriramulu** fasted to death | **1952**, for a linguistic Telugu state |
| **Andhra State** (carved from Madras State) | **1 Oct 1953**; capital **Kurnool**; **first linguistic State** |
| **Andhra Pradesh** | **1 Nov 1956**; **20 districts** |
| **Pataskar award** | **1960** |
| **Telangana** | **29th State**, **2 June 2014**; capital Hyderabad (28 states after J&K reorganisation) |
| 26 districts | **4 April 2022** |
| 28 districts | **31 Dec 2025** – Markapuram, Polavaram |
!TRAP Andhra State = 1953 (Kurnool); Andhra Pradesh = 1956. Both questions say "first linguistic state".
#### 1.2 Pataskar Award (1960)
- Followed the **Pataskar Commission**: **Tamil Nadu** received most of **Tiruttani taluk** of Chittoor district; some villages of **Tiruvallur taluk** (TN) were added to AP.
### 2. AREA AND RANK
- **Area: 1,62,970 sq km**, about **4.96%** of India (GoAP figure).
- 1,60,205 sq km = older post-bifurcation base figure. Undivided AP (1956–2014): about **2,75,000 sq km**.
- **Rank: 7th largest State** after Rajasthan, MP, Maharashtra, UP, Gujarat, Karnataka.
- AP was **8th until 31 Oct 2019**, when J&K became a UT.
!TRAP The APPSC GS 2023 key "8th" reflects the older position. Read whether the question says "States" or "States and UTs".
!NUM 1,62,970 sq km = 4.96% of India = 7th largest State.

### 4. PHYSIOGRAPHIC DIVISIONS
#### 4.1 Three Divisions
| Division | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Coastal plains** | Between Eastern Ghats and the Bay; **~950 km long**, ~60 km wide, up to 150 m; **Godavari and Krishna deltas**; "**Rice Bowl**" of AP |
| **Hill region (Eastern Ghats)** | **Discontinuous** ranges; **Mahendragiri** (Srikakulam), **Papikondalu** (on the Godavari), **Nallamala** |
| **Western (Rayalaseema) plateau** | Part of the **Deccan**; **drought-prone uplands** |
- **Oldest landmass**: **Archaean Dharwar rocks** of **Anantapur, Chittoor, Kadapa** – over **2,500 million years** old.
!TRAP Mahendragiri is in the Eastern Ghats hill region (Srikakulam), not the Rayalaseema plateau.
#### 4.2 Rayalaseema vs Coastal Andhra
| Basis | Rayalaseema | Coastal Andhra |
|---|---|---|
| Location | Inland, **south-western** AP | Eastern AP |
| Setting | Plateau / uplands (Deccan) | Low coastal plain and deltas |
| Climate | **Drought-prone**; historic droughts, famines | — |
| Agriculture | Less favourable, less fertile | Fertile; most farm output |
| Density | **Very low** | **Highest** |
| Borders | TN, Karnataka, Telangana | Bay of Bengal |
### 5. CAVES AND GEOMORPHIC LANDMARKS
| Landmark | District | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Belum Caves** | **Nandyal** (Kolimigundla mandal; earlier Kurnool) | Over 3,229 m long; **second-largest** known cave in the subcontinent after Krem Liat Prah (Meghalaya) |
| **Borra Caves** | **Alluri Sitharama Raju** (earlier Visakhapatnam) | Limestone karst in **Ananthagiri hills**, Araku Valley; **Gosthani** river; found by **William King (GSI), 1807** |
| **Billa Surgam Caves** | **Kurnool** (near Betamcherla) | **Prehistoric** site; stalactites, flowstone |
| **Gandikota** | **YSR Kadapa** | **Penna (Pennar) gorge** in the Erramala hills – "**Grand Canyon of India**" |

### 8. REVENUE DIVISIONS AND MANDALS
- After the Dec 2025 reorganisation: about **81 revenue divisions** and **688 mandals** across 28 districts.
- Polavaram district: 11 mandals, 2 divisions; Parvathipuram Manyam: 15 mandals; West Godavari: 19 mandals.
### 9. AP REORGANISATION ACT 2014 – TERRITORIAL CHANGES
- **Telangana** became the **29th State** on 2 June 2014; AP left with **13 districts**.
- **Seven mandals of Khammam** (Polavaram submergence area) moved to AP by the **2014 Polavaram ordinance** and the amendment Act (Lok Sabha, 11 July 2014): **Kukunoor, Velerupadu, Kunavaram, Chintur, V.R. Puram (Vararamachandrapuram), Bhadrachalam (except the town), Burgampadu (part)**.
- These now lie in the new **Polavaram district** (Chinturu division).
### 10. AP FIRSTS
- **First State formed on linguistic grounds** (Andhra State 1953; AP 1956).
- **Asia's first rubber dam**: on the **Janjavathi**, a tributary of the **Nagavali**.
- **First private-sector gold mine** in India: **Jonnagiri**, Kurnool.
