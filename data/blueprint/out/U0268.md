## U0268 | IMPORTANT CULTURAL EVENTS BETWEEN 1956 AND 2014 – LITERATURE, JOURNALISM, THEATRE AND CINEMA
### 1. NATIONAL AKADEMIS – THE BACKGROUND
| Akademi | Year / FIRST |
|---|---|
| **Sangeet Natak Akademi** | **First national arts academy** (music, dance, drama); inaugurated by **Rajendra Prasad**, **28 January 1953** |
| **Sahitya Akademi** | Set up **1952**, inaugurated **12 March 1954**; a **society, not statutory** |
| **Lalit Kala Akademi** | **1954**; a national academy, **NOT an AP award** |
!TRAP Sahitya Akademi: set up 1952 but inaugurated 1954; **AP Sahitya Akademi = 1957**.
### 2. CULTURAL INSTITUTIONS OF AP
| Institution | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **AP Sahitya Akademi** | **1957** | Telugu literature, translation, awards |
| **Ravindra Bharathi**, Hyderabad | Inaugurated **11 May 1961** | Built for **Tagore's birth centenary** (not Gandhi's) |
| **Nataka Vidyalayam**, Vijayawada (Andhra Nataka Kala Parishad) | **1 August 1964** | Training in drama |
| **International Telugu Institute** | **1975** | APPSC 2019 key |
| **Potti Sreeramulu Telugu University** | **1985** | Absorbed the State akademis and the International Telugu Institute |
!KEY Ravindra Bharathi was inaugurated by **Dr S. Radhakrishnan** (official APPSC G2 2017 key). It was built for Tagore's centenary, not opened by Tagore.

### 4. NEWSPAPERS AND JOURNALS
#### 4.1 Pre-1956 Base
- **Kasinadhuni Nageswara Rao**: **Andhra Patrika** and **Bharati (1924)**.
- **Prajasakti**: **first communist Telugu journal**.
- **Jaya Bharat**: **first Telugu journal after Independence**.

### 6. THEATRE
- **Surabhi**: family theatre company founded **1885** in **Surabhi village, Kadapa** by **Vanarasa Govinda Rao** and his brother; puppets gradually replaced by live actors.
- **Nandi Natakotsavams**: State drama festivals; revival "on a grand scale" announced (**27 July 2026**).
- **National Theatre Festival** (Sangeet Natak Akademi, **15 July 2026**): **150 locations**, on **150 years of Vande Mataram**; two AP plays selected.
### 7. CINEMA AND STATE AWARDS
- **Nandi Awards**: AP Government's awards for **films, television and drama**; instituted **1964**.
- **Dadasaheb Phalke Award**: **Swarna Kamal** + **Rs 15 lakh** (raised from Rs 10 lakh in 2024).

### 8. BODIES ABOLISHED, RESTRUCTURED OR REVIVED
- **1985**: the **NTR government merged the State akademis** into the new **Telugu University**.
- Previous (**YSRCP**) government let the **Nandi Awards** and **Kandukuri Veeresalingam Awards** lapse.
- **Coalition government** revived the Kandukuri Awards and announced revival of Nandi Awards and Nandi Natakotsavams (**March 2026**).
- **Telugu Mahotsavam** to be held **annually**: announced at **Undavalli Caves**, **July 2026**, Minister **Kandula Durgesh**.
- AP cultural awards (July 2026): **Ugadi Kala Ratna, Kandukuri, Telugu Language, Gurram Jashuva** awards.
