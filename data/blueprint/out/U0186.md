### 5. NATURE – STATEMENT JUDGEMENTS
- Began as a **military mutiny** → became a **popular rebellion**.
- A **traditional uprising** of classes against a common foreign enemy – **no idea of a nation-state** yet.
- Not a first war of independence: no common goal; largely **North India**; most of India **neutral**; elites uninvolved.
- **Failed**, but broke the myth of British invincibility; seeds of later nationalism; heroes invoked by Bose and Bhagat Singh.
- Same "mutiny or revolt?" debate on the **RIN revolt** (HMIS Talwar, 18 Feb 1946).
### 6. SOCIAL GROUPS AND HINDU-MUSLIM UNITY
| Group | Role |
|---|---|
| Sepoys | Spearhead – Meerut, Delhi |
| Deposed rulers | Led locally |
| Awadh taluqdars, peasants, artisans | Joined (Awadh, western UP) |
| **Big zamindars of Bengal** | **Loyal** |
| **Educated middle class** | **Neutral** – expected British to modernise; a reason for failure |
- **Bahadur Shah II** accepted by Hindu and Muslim rebels; cartridges offended both. Response: **divide and rule**.
- **AP:** Nizam pro-British (APHQ 2001, 2025); Turrebaz Khan, **Kadiri** attempt, Korukonda Subba Reddy, Dandasena – "Andhra had no leaders" is false. **Annapurnayya** ("Andhra Netaji") – 18 months RI for **"Chicharapidugu"**.
### 7. CAUSES OF FAILURE
- No **common strategy and central organisation**; weak Zafar leadership.
- Limited spread; major rulers (Rajput states) backed the British; modern British weapons.
