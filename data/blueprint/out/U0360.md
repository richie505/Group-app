## U0360 | ACTORS IN POLICY-MAKING – EXECUTIVE, LEGISLATURE, JUDICIARY, BUREAUCRACY, MEDIA, CIVIL SOCIETY, THINK TANKS
### 1. STAKEHOLDERS OF GOVERNANCE
| Set | Includes |
|---|---|
| **State** | Legislature, judiciary, executive, bureaucracy |
| **Market** | Private sector |
| **Civil Society** | NGOs, VOs, **media**, trade unions, religious and **pressure groups** |
!TRAP Media organisations belong to **civil society**, not the State.
### 2. EXECUTIVE
- **Cabinet and Cabinet Committees** take key decisions.
- **Cabinet Secretariat** – headed by the **Cabinet Secretary**, directly under the **PM**; runs the **Transaction of Business Rules**. **PMO** = PM's policy office.
- **NITI Aayog (2015)** replaced the Planning Commission (1950); **non-constitutional, non-statutory**; chaired by the **PM**; cooperative federalism. Vice Chairperson **Ashok Kumar Lahiri** (2026); Amitabh Kant was CEO.
### 3. LEGISLATURE
- Parliament = chief law-maker, but **Cabinet decides substance and timing**; **bureaucracy drafts** Government Bills; private members' bills rarely pass.
- **Question Hour** – most effective control; also Zero Hour, adjournment motions, financial control.
- Bills referred to committees: **71% (15th Lok Sabha) → 16% (17th Lok Sabha)**.
| Option against an SC decision | Limit |
|---|---|
| New law removing the basis of the judgment | Cannot simply overrule |
| Constitutional amendment (Art. 368) | **Basic structure** (Kesavananda Bharati) |
| Review / curative petition | Same Court |
- Both a new law and an amendment are open (APPSC key).
- Examples: **Muslim Women Act 1986** after **Shah Bano**; **SC/ST (PoA) Amendment 2018** after **Subhash Kashinath Mahajan**.
- **Article 143** reference – advisory opinion only, not binding.
- Accountability gaps: Governors and Speakers delay assent and **Tenth Schedule** decisions; RTI backlog.
### 4. JUDICIARY AND PIL
- **PIL began 1979** – case filed **on behalf of prisoners**, not by them; basis **Arts. 32, 226, 142**.
- **Upendra Baxi's** fears: ideological, epistemic, management, legitimation, democratic (**not electoral**).
- Overreach → policy paralysis, backlog; PIL misused for publicity or political ends.
| Case | Guidelines acting as law |
|---|---|
| **Vishaka (1997)** | Workplace sexual harassment – until **POSH Act 2013** |
| **Prakash Singh (2006)** | **Police reforms**; fixed DGP tenure |
| **T.S.R. Subramanian (2013)** | Civil Services Boards; fixed tenure for civil servants |
### 5. BUREAUCRACY AND PARTIES
- **Permanent executive:** All-India Services, State services, local and PSU staff; advises, drafts, implements; concern – **frontline implementation discretion**.
- Recruitment by **UPSC and State PSCs**; PSC members removed for misbehaviour after a **Supreme Court inquiry** (not Parliament).
- **Manifesto → policy:** **33.33% BC quota** in AP ULBs – NDA 2024 poll promise, via **G.O. Rt. No. 1065** (Aug 2026).
- AP **Action Taken Reports** on **seven white papers** – from **22 June 2026**.
### 6. MEDIA AND CIVIL SOCIETY
- Mass media **shape the image of political personalities**, because of outlet ideology and treatment of news – (A) true, (R) correct explanation (**APPSC-GS 2018**).
- **Free press** needed for democracy; **new media** allow participatory, two-way communication – both true, independently (APPSC-GS 2018).
- **PIB** – briefings, accreditation, social media; **PM's Communication Wing** – PMO/Cabinet publicity, daily newspaper feedback (separate bodies).
- Risks: **AI-driven foreign information manipulation** (Jun 2026); parties court Gen Z online (Sep 2026).
| Campaign | Law |

| **MKSS** (Aruna Roy, Rajasthan) | **RTI Act 2005** |
| Right to Work / NAC | **MGNREGA 2005** |
| Right to Food (**PUCL PIL 2001**) | **NFSA 2013** |
