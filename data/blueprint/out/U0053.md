## U0053 | IKSHVAKU RELIGIOUS CONDITIONS – BUDDHISM AT NAGARJUNAKONDA AND BRAHMANICAL TEMPLES
### 1. DUAL PATRONAGE – KINGS VS QUEENS
- **Kings** (all except Virapurushadatta) performed **Vedic sacrifices**; **queens, princesses and rich lay women** funded stupas and viharas.
- **Shantisri (Chamtisri)** and **Harmyasri** were sisters of **Vasishthiputra Santamula (I)**.
- Puranic **Shaivism, Vaishnavism and Shakti worship** all thrived; the Ikshvakus were the **last royal Buddhist zenith** in Andhra.
### 2. RELIGIOUS POLICY BY REIGN
| King | Religious policy |
|---|---|
| **Vasishthiputra Sri Santamula** (founder) | Vedic: **Ashvamedha, Rajasuya, Vajapeya, Agnistoma**; worshipped **Virupakshapati** (Shiva) and **Mahasena** (Skanda); **agraharas to northern Brahmins** |
| **Virapurushadatta** | **Only Ikshvaku king to adopt Buddhism** (earlier Vedic; did not fully drop Brahmanical ties); **golden age**; rebuilt the Maha Chaitya under **Acharya Bhadanta Ananda**; titles "**Ashoka of the South**", "**Sri Parvatadhipati**"; a sculpture shows a **Shivalinga trampled underfoot** |
| **Ehuvala Santamula** | **Revived Brahmanism**; first Hindu temples; first Sanskrit inscriptions; still built a **vihara for the Bahushrutiyas** in his mother's name; **Upasika Bodhisri** served Buddhism in his reign |
| **Rudrapurushadatta** (last) | Vedic; deity **Mahasena**; custom of **chhaya-stambhas** (shadow pillars) near graves began |
!KEY Greatest Buddhist patronage = **Veerapurushadatta** (G2 2008); Bodhisri's service = reign of **Ehuvala Chamtamula** (G2 2012 key).
### 3. TUTELARY DEITY – HARITI
- **Hariti (Harati)** = **tutelary (family) deity** of the Ikshvakus of Vijayapuri (G2 2012 key); **protector of children**; bangles offered.
- Installation of her image = beginning of **Shakti worship**. Hariti was also a mother goddess of the Early Chalukyas.
!TRAP Hariti = tutelary deity; Virupakshapati / Mahasena = deities the kings worshipped.

- Mahasanghika branches / related sects: Purvasailiya, Aparasailiya, Bahushrutiya, **Rajagirika**, **Siddharthika** – **Mahaviharavasin is not one**.
- **Acharya Bhadanta Ananda** (Aparamahavinaseliya) knew the **Digha and Majjhima Nikayas** by heart.
### 5. MAHACHAITYA AND BODHISRI
- A **Dhatugarbha (relic)** stupa over the Buddha's bodily relics: "**Samma Sambudhasa dhatuvara parigahita**".
- Earlier **Paravata Mahachaitya** built by **Yajnasri Satakarni** for **Acharya Nagarjuna**; rebuilt by **Chamtisri** in Virapurushadatta's **6th year** with **9 ayaka pillars**.
- **Bodhisri's** works: stone **mandapa at the eastern gateway** of the Maha Chaitya at **Kantakasala (Ghantasala)**; another **opposite the Paravata Vihara**; **seven viharas at Papili**.
### 6. FOREIGN BUDDHIST LINKS
- Bodhisri's viharas for monks of **Tamravarna (Sinhala), Kashmir, China, Gandhara**.
- Countries receiving the Dhamma (her inscription): **Kashmir, Gandhara, China, Chilata, Tosali, Aparanta, Vanga, Vanavasi, Yavana, Damila, Palura**.
- **Ceylonese monks** made Nagarjunakonda their **Hinayana** base; missionaries went to **China and Tibet**.
- **Mahadeva Bhikshu** took Buddhism from the **Pallava-bhoga** region to Sri Lanka (Mahavamsa).
- **Itsing (c. 700 CE)** saw Indian students memorising Nagarjuna's **Suhrllekha**, a letter to **Yajnasri Satakarni** (G2 2012).
- **Hiuen Tsang (7th c.)** found the **Sriparvata vihara neglected**.
!TRAP Suhrllekha study account = **Itsing**; Fahien and Hiuen Tsang are the distractors.
### 7. ACHARYA NAGARJUNA AND SRIPARVATA
- **Nagarjuna** = **Madhyamika** philosopher; **Sriparvata = Nagarjunakonda / Vijayapuri**, named after him.
- **Yajnasri Satakarni** built viharas and chaityas at Sri Parvatam for him.
- **Virapurushadatta** set up the **Sriparvata University**.
- Sriparvata = "**Dakshina Gaya**"; sacred to **Mahayana**, while **Bodh Gaya** is sacred to **Hinayana**.
- **Sri Parvataswamy = Buddha**: identified by **Kielhorn** (G2 2019 key).
!TRAP Another APPSC G2 key credits **N. Venkataramanayya (Venkataramanaiah)** with identifying Sri Parvataswamy with the Buddha. Do not confuse with **Sriparvataswami**, the **Vishnukundin family deity**, identified with **Srisaila Mallikarjuna**.
### 8. BRAHMANICAL TEMPLES AT NAGARJUNAKONDA
- Ikshvakus built **India's first structural Hindu temples**: **9 Shaiva + 1 Vaishnava** at Nagarjunakonda.
- Temples found in the excavations: **Harati, Mahajana, Kumara Swami, Pushpabhadra Swami, Ashtabhuja Narayana Swami**.
| Temple | Builder / deity |
|---|---|
| **Pushpabhadraswami** | Started by **Virapurushadatta**, finished by **Ehuvala**; **Shiva**; **Gajaprishtha** (elephant-back) plan |
| **Sarvadevadhivasam** | Commander **Elisiri** (of Ehuvala); for **Kumara Swami (Kartikeya)**; inscription in **Sanskrit** |
| **Ashtabhuja Narayanaswami** | **Shivasena**, commander of **Abhira king Vasusena**; **first Vaishnava temple in Andhra** |
- Inscriptions of Vasishthiputra Santamula found in the **Rentala** and **Kesavapath** temples near Nagarjunakonda.

### 13. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. **Purvasailiya = Amaravati**; **Aparasailiya = Nagarjunakonda**.
2. Mahaviharavasin is **Theravada**, not a Mahasanghika branch.
3. Suhrllekha study = **Itsing**, not Fahien or Hiuen Tsang.
4. Bodhisri served under **Ehuvala Chamtamula** (G2 2012 key).
5. **Ashtabhuja Narayana** was built by an **Abhira** commander.
6. **Sarvadevadhivasam** = Elisiri, for Kartikeya, Sanskrit record.
7. Pushpabhadraswami = **Gajaprishtha** Shiva temple.
8. Sriparvataswami of the Vishnukundins = **Srisaila Mallikarjuna**.
9. Hariti = guardian of **children** and the Ikshvaku family deity.
