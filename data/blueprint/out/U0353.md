## U0353 | DEMOCRATIC DECENTRALISATION – MEANING, OBJECTIVES AND PRECONDITIONS
### 1. MEANING OF DEMOCRATIC DECENTRALISATION
- **Democratic decentralisation**: transferring power to **elected local bodies** so people take part in decisions that affect them.
- **Local self-government** is best explained as an **exercise in democratic decentralisation** (UPSC 2017).
- The term was **coined by the Balwant Rai Mehta Committee** (1957).
- What PRIs brought: **formal representation of the weaker sections** in village governance (**APPSC 2019**).
- Why adopted: **to decentralise the power of democracy** (UPPSC 2016).
#### 1.1 Confusable Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Democratic decentralisation** | Power passed to elected local bodies |
| Federalism | Union–State division of power |
| Delegation | Revocable at will |
| Direct democracy | Citizens decide without representatives |
### 2. ATTRIBUTES OF LOCAL SELF-GOVERNMENT
| Is a feature | Is NOT a feature |
|---|---|
| Elected by and **accountable to local people** | **Sovereignty** |
| **Defined local area and functions** | **Legislative supremacy** |
| **Own funds** / power to tax | **Judicial powers** (Nyaya Panchayats are separate) |
| **Autonomy within State law** | Central control |
!TRAP Sovereignty and legislative supremacy belong to Parliament and State legislatures, never to local bodies.
### 3. COLONIAL ROOTS
| Viceroy | Resolution | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| **Lord Mayo** | **1870** (14 December) | **Financial decentralisation**; fixed grants to provinces; aim mainly fiscal relief |
| **Lord Ripon** | **18 May 1882** | **Resolution on Local Self-Government**; local bodies as instruments of **political and popular education**; elected non-official majorities and non-official chairmen |
- **Ripon = "Father of Local Self-Government in India"**.
- India's **municipal model** comes from the **British** pattern (Ripon's boards).
### 4. COMMITTEES – RECOMMENDATIONS AND PRECONDITIONS
| Committee | Key point / precondition |
|---|---|
| **Balwant Rai Mehta (1957)** | **Three-tier PRI**; **genuine transfer of power** and **adequate resources** (not constitutional status); NDC accepted it **January 1958** |
| **Ashok Mehta (1977-78)** | **Compulsory taxation powers**, **no supersession** (or elections within **6 months**), social audit, **district as the first point** of decentralisation |
| **Dantwala (1978)** | **Block-level** planning |
| **Hanumantha Rao (1984)** | **District** planning, coordinated by the **Collector** |
| **G.V.K. Rao (1985)** | Development bureaucratised – "**grass without roots**"; restore the **Zila Parishad's** role |
| **L.M. Singhvi** | **Constitutional status** for PRIs |
| Committee on Policy and Programmes (1988) | Constitutional status, **5-year term**, direct elections, **State Finance and Election Commissions** |
#### 4.1 Mehta Three Tiers
| Tier | Level |
|---|---|
| **Zila Parishad** | District |
| **Panchayat Samiti** | Block |
| **Gram Panchayat** | Village |
- **Whose cooperation**: Mehta Committee held **local public participation** indispensable for development.
!PAIR Dantwala 1978 | Block || Hanumantha Rao 1984 | District || Ashok Mehta | District as first point
### 5. OBJECTIVES OF PANCHAYATI RAJ
- **Fundamental objects**: **people's participation in development** and **democratic decentralisation** (UPSC 2015).
- After the **73rd Amendment**, needed: decentralisation of **decision-making**, of **administrative powers**, and of **the system** as a whole – **not judicial powers** (BPSC 2022).
!TRAP **Political accountability** and **financial mobilisation** are not the keyed fundamental objects.
### 6. FIRST ROLLOUTS
| FIRST | Fact |
|---|---|
| **First State** | **Rajasthan – Nagaur, 2 October 1959** |
| Next | **Andhra Pradesh – 1 November 1959** |
- AP's **13 Zilla Parishads** are reorganised into **28** (one per district) after their terms ended on 24 Sep 2026.
### 7. GRAM SWARAJ AND ARTICLE 40
- **Gram Swaraj**: Gandhi's self-reliant **village republic**.
- **Article 40 (DPSP)**: the **constitutional root** of Gram Swaraj – State to organise village panchayats.
- **Rashtriya Gram Swaraj Abhiyan** (Ministry of **Panchayati Raj**), from **2018-19**: **trains PRI members**; revamped for 1 Apr 2022 – 31 Mar 2026; funding **60:40**, **NE and hill States 90:10**.
### 8. DECENTRALISED PLANNING
- **District Planning Committee**: constitutional coordinating body under **Article 243ZD**.
- **Kerala People's Plan Campaign**: launched **17 August 1996**; about **35-40%** of plan funds devolved to local bodies (not 60-70%); turned **30** in Aug 2026.
- **T.M. Thomas Isaac** proposed "**People's Plan Campaign 2.0**" – student internships and an online portal (25 Aug 2026).
- **Economic Survey 2025-26**: PRI own-source revenue, **e-GramSwaraj**, **SabhaSaar** (**AI tool** for writing **Gram Sabha minutes**); low Gram Sabha participation (1 Jul 2026).
- J&K DDCs' term ended 24 Feb 2026 with no fresh polls.
### 9. GLOBAL MODELS
| Model | Feature |
