## U0014 | DECLINE OF THE IVC – COMPETING THEORIES; LATE HARAPPAN CULTURES
### 1. DECLINE THEORIES
- Consensus: **multi-causal and gradual** (about **200 years**, varying by region); sudden catastrophe rejected.
| Theory | Proponent / study |
|---|---|
| **Aryan invasion** (1947, rejected) | **Mortimer Wheeler**; also Gordon Childe, Stuart Piggott |
| **Floods** | John Marshall, E. Mackay, S.R. Rao |
| **Shift of Indus course** | **H.T. Lambrick** |
| **Ghaggar-Hakra drying** | **D.P. Agrawal and Sood** |
| Climate / drought | Aurel Stein; **IIT Kharagpur** – low rainfall for **900 years** |
| **Ecological imbalance** | **W.A. Fairservis** |
| Trade decline | Fall in Mesopotamian demand, c. **1900 BCE** |
!TRAP M.R. Sahni appears under floods and tectonic uplift; Lambrick = Indus course, not floods.
#### 1.1 Evidence for each theory
- Floods: **Chanhudaro** flooded several times; shifting Indus → **Mohenjodaro** abandoned.
- Rivers: Ghaggar-Hakra (densest cluster of sites) dried – tectonic shifts, less rain.
- Ecology: **deforestation, soil salinity, overgrazing**, exhausted copper and timber, failing drains. Malaria at Mohenjodaro.
### 2. WHEELER'S INVASION THEORY AND ITS REJECTION
- Wheeler cited **huddled skeletons** at Mohenjodaro and Indra as **Purandara** (fort-breaker).
- Rejected: no archaeological proof; skeletons = **multiple burials of different periods**; **Rig Veda came after** the decline.
- **Rakhigarhi** female DNA: no **Steppe Pastoralist** gene → suggests indigenous continuity (does not prove no movement).
### 3. CLIMATE EVIDENCE – 4.2 KA EVENT
- **Deoria Tal** (Uttarakhand) pollen (PIB, **30 Jul 2026**): oak-to-pine ratio rises → monsoon weakened sharply about **4,200 years ago** → less water in Indus and Ghaggar-Hakra → drought, eastward migration.
- Same aridity linked to fall of **Akkad** (Mesopotamia).
!NUM 900 years = IIT Kharagpur low rainfall; 4,200 years ago = 4.2 ka event; 200 years = pace of decline
### 4. LATE HARAPPAN PHASE – FEATURES
- c. **1900–1300 BCE**; post-urban **de-urbanisation**; villages, smaller rural sites.
- **Chalcolithic**: stone and copper tools; **no metal objects needing complicated casting** (APPSC 2024).
- Seals, script, weights, bricks lose uniformity; regional pottery; long-distance trade weakens.
- Shift **east** (Ganga-Yamuna Doab) and **south** (Gujarat, Deccan).

### 6. OCP, COPPER HOARDS AND CONTINUITY
- **Copper Hoards** found with **OCP** in the Doab → commonly linked.
- **Bhagwanpura**: 13-roomed house with Late Harappan pottery and **Painted Grey Ware** (overlap).
- Continuity view: Ghaggar-Hakra = Rigvedic **Sarasvati**; fire altars, pipal, Mother Goddess, linga-yoni, ritual bathing, burials continue.
- Discontinuity: **iron, horse, chariot** absent in Mature Harappan.
