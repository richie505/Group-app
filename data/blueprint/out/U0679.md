## U0679 | CLASSIFICATION OF BASIC INDUSTRIES – AGRO, MINERAL, FOREST, FUEL AND MANPOWER-BASED
### 1. CLASSIFICATION BY RAW MATERIAL
| Class | Features | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Agro-based** | Farm produce; rural, seasonal, labour-intensive | Cotton textile, jute, sugar, tea, dairy |
| **Mineral-based** | Capital-intensive; near mines or ports | Iron and steel, aluminium, cement, copper smelting, fertiliser |
| **Forest-based** | Timber + non-timber forest produce | Paper and pulp, furniture, plywood, matchsticks, lac, resin, bamboo products, **rayon** |
| **Animal-based** | Animal produce | Dairy, leather, wool |
| **Marine-based** | Sea produce | Seafood and **coastal aquaculture processing** |
- Agro-based units sit near raw material (perishable, weight-losing). **Largest forest-based industry = pulp and paper.** **Rayon** (artificial silk) is based on wood pulp.
### 2. FUEL-BASED AND MANPOWER-BASED INDUSTRIES
| Class | Basis | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Fuel-based** (energy-based) | Coal, petroleum, natural gas, nuclear | Thermal power, **oil refining, petrochemicals** |
| **Manpower-based** (labour-intensive) | High human labour | **Handloom**, handicrafts, leather, garments |
- Maximum coal is used in **energy (power) production**.
- **Thermal power** is the largest industrial consumer of water (~**88%**); engineering ~5%, paper ~2.3%, textiles ~2%.

### 5. BY SIZE – MSMEs
- **Ministry of MSME (2007)** = merger of Ministry of Small Scale Industries + Ministry of Agro and Rural Industries.
- **2020 definition:** one composite test of **investment + turnover**; no manufacturing/services difference.
- MSMEs work as **ancillary units** to large industries.
| Class (from 1 Apr 2025) | Investment up to | Turnover up to |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | ₹2.5 crore | ₹10 crore |
| Small | ₹25 crore | ₹100 crore |
| Medium | ₹125 crore | ₹500 crore |
### 6. BASIC VS CONSUMER; HEAVY VS LIGHT
| Use-based group | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Basic / primary goods** | Steel, cement, coal |
| Capital / intermediate | Machinery / chemicals |
| Consumer durables / non-durables | Appliances / food |
- **IIP use-based classes:** primary, capital, intermediate, infrastructure/construction, consumer durables, consumer non-durables (**no "export goods"**).
| Term | Meaning / examples |
|---|---|
| **Heavy** | Bulky, capital-intensive: iron and steel, HEC Ranchi |
| **Light** | Lighter inputs, consumer goods: electronics assembly, garments |
| **Footloose** | Diamond polishing, software: need **skilled labour + internet**, not raw material |
| **Sunrise / sunset** | AI, green hydrogen, EVs, semiconductors / legacy print, thermal coal equipment |
- **Quinary** sector = top decision-making, **"gold collar"**.

### 8. PAPER AND WORLD LEADERS
- India ~**5%** of world paper; use **16 kg**/person/year vs world **57 kg**.
- Raw material: recycled fibre 71-75%, wood pulp ~21%; **poplar** is a pulp wood.
- Paper is **capital-intensive**; leaders China, USA, Japan, Germany, Finland, Sweden, Canada; top exporters **Finland, Sweden, Canada**.
- **China** = largest furniture exporter; **rayon** led by China, India, Indonesia.
- Canada = largest soft timber and wood pulp exporter (when asked); **USA** now top wood pulp producer (FAO 2025). Pulp and paper "most developed": key gave **Mexico**.
