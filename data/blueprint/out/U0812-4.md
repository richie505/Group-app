### 4. POLICING: AI4AP, TASK DRIVES AND WOMEN'S SAFETY
#### 4.1 AI4AP Police project
- Launched by **AP Police**; **pilot in Chittoor, Guntur and Annamayya** districts (February 2026); statewide expansion within **six months**.
- Origin: **AI4 Andhra Police Hackathon (June 2025)**, India's **first police-specific AI hackathon**; **200+ innovators**.
- Integrated with **CCTNS**; a **Police Large Language Model (LLM)**; faster investigations, better conviction rates; **AI to assist, not replace**, investigators.
- **Knowledge partner: 4sightAI** (CEO Surya Kotha); **technology partner: Sparity** (MoU).
- Launched by **DGP Harish Kumar Gupta** at **Mangalagiri Police HQ**.
- **Eight AI modules**: complaint reception; digital evidence; social media monitoring; petition review; telecom/banking notices; investigation co-pilot (BNS/POCSO/IT law); **OCR for paper records**; news tracking.
- **Petition AI**: review time **30–45 min → 5–10 min**. Others: **Cognitive Net AI** (call data, bank statements), **Docs-to-Data**, **Social Intelligence AI**, **News-360 AI**.
!TRAP AI4AP modules do not include facial-recognition training; the tool assists, it does not replace investigators.

| Removed (5) | Still pending (Group of Ministers, within two months) |

| **Private patta lands** | |

!TRAP Conditional patta lands were NOT removed; a Group of Ministers is to decide on them.
#### 5.2 Other land reforms
- **2026 declared the "Year of Land Reforms"** in AP.
- **Single-document sufficiency**: old revenue registers, assignment records, Record of Holdings, registration documents, **DKT pattas**.
- Technology: **resurvey, digitisation, auto-mutation** of pattadar passbooks, **AI and Blockchain**.
- **Collectors empowered to cancel fake/double registrations**.
- **21.8 lakh new pattadar passbooks** distributed **1–9 January 2026**.
- Background: **Section 22-A** lets the State **prohibit registration** of certain lands to prevent illegal transfers.

#### 7.1 AIDS-free AP by 2030

| Indicator | Earlier | Now |
|---|---|---|
| HIV positivity | **2.34%** (2015–16) | **0.58%** (2024–25) |

- **59 ART centres**; free ART worth **₹35,000–40,000 per patient per year**.
- **15 mobile ICTC vans** (Integrated Counselling and Testing Centres) for remote areas.
- Rising risk group: **IT employees**; patients registered in **2004** at the **Guntur ART centre** are still alive.
#### 7.2 Universal health screening and Sanjeevani

- Sanjeevani screening of **66 lakh high-risk individuals** and **5 lakh hostel students**; **health profiles of 70 lakh students** up to Class XII.
- Update (27 Aug 2026): screening of the 66 lakh high-risk people and 5 lakh hostel students runs from **August 2026**; Sanjeevani goes statewide from **1 January 2027** after the **Kuppam** pilot.
#### 7.3 5G phones for Anganwadi workers
