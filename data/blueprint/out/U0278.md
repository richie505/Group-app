## U0278 | PREAMBLE – TEXT, KEYWORDS, 42ND AMENDMENT AND ITS STATUS
### 1. MAKING OF THE PREAMBLE – OBJECTIVES RESOLUTION
#### 1.1 From Resolution to Preamble
| Step | Fact |
|---|---|
| Basis | **Objectives Resolution** of **Jawaharlal Nehru** |
| Moved | **13 December 1946** |
| Adopted | **22 January 1947** (unanimously) |
| Role | Laid down the philosophy and guiding principles |
| Result | Its **modified version became the Preamble** |
| Enactment sequence | Preamble enacted **after the rest of the Constitution**, so that it matched the text |
- **Revised draft** taken up in the Assembly on **14 November 1949**; the formal **third reading** ran **17–26 November 1949**.
- Date in the Preamble: **26 November 1949** = **adoption**, not commencement (**26 January 1950**).
- **Constitution Day**: **26 November**, observed **since 2015**.
!TRAP Third reading year is 1949, not 1948. Date in the Preamble is the adoption date, not 26 January 1950.
#### 1.2 Sources of the Preamble's Ideals
| Element | Source |
|---|---|
| **Idea of a preamble**; "We, the people" | **USA** Constitution |
| **Language** of the Preamble | **Australia** |
| **Liberty, Equality, Fraternity**; Republic | **French Revolution (1789)** |
| **Justice – social, economic, political** | **Russian Revolution (1917)** |
| Fraternity (possible source) | **Article 1 of the UDHR (1948)** |
!PAIR Justice | Russian Revolution | 1917 || Liberty, Equality, Fraternity | French Revolution | 1789
### 2. PREAMBLE TEXT – ORDER OF WORDS AND DATE
#### 2.1 Nature of the State
- "**We, the People of India**" – opens the Preamble; shows **people's sovereignty**; the Constitution derives its authority from the people.
- People resolved to constitute India into a **Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic** (that exact sequence).
- **42nd Amendment** put **Socialist and Secular between 'Sovereign' and 'Democratic'**.
- Enacting clause: "IN OUR CONSTITUENT ASSEMBLY this **twenty-sixth day of November, 1949**, do HEREBY **ADOPT, ENACT AND GIVE TO OURSELVES** THIS CONSTITUTION."
- **Main source of political power**: the **People**; sovereignty is with them.
#### 2.2 Original 1949 Text vs Now
| Item | 1949 / 26 Jan 1950 | Now (after 1976) |
|---|---|---|
| Description of India | **"Sovereign Democratic Republic"** | **"Sovereign Socialist Secular Democratic Republic"** |
| Fraternity clause | "**unity of the Nation**" | "**unity and integrity of the Nation**" |
| Fraternity | **Present in original** | Unchanged |
| Words absent in 1949 | **Socialist, Secular, Integrity** | Added |
#### 2.3 Words NOT in the Preamble at All
- **Federal / federation**
- **Fundamental Duties**
- **Welfare state** / **Public Welfare**
- **Adult franchise**
- **Religious Justice**
- **"People's"**
- **"Freedom"** (it uses Liberty, Equality, Justice, Fraternity)
- **Liberty "of action"**, **equality "of rights"**
- **Political Liberty** and **Economic liberty** as separate categories
- **Gram Swarajya**
!TRAP "Federal" is nowhere in the Preamble, and "Freedom" is not used – only "Liberty".
### 3. KEYWORDS – MEANING
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sovereign** | Supreme authority; **internally and externally free**; own foreign policy; free to frame own policies |
| **Socialist** | Aims at **reducing inequality**; **democratic socialism** – a **mixed economy** (public + private sectors); "a blend of Marxism and Gandhism, **leaning towards Gandhian socialism**" |
| **Secular** | **Equal respect for all religions**; **no official/State religion**; not against religion |
| **Democratic** | Government of, by and for the people; representative parliamentary democracy covering **political, social and economic democracy** |
| **Republic** | **Elected head of State**, not hereditary; public offices open to all |
- 'Secular' before 1976 appeared only in **Article 25(2)(a)** (secular activities linked with religious practice); linked to **Arts. 25–28**.
- **Communist State** is NOT the philosophy of the Constitution; a socialist state is a goal.
- India is a **democratic polity, sovereign state, democratic society and welfare state** (welfare state via **DPSP**).
!TRAP APPSC G1 2024: "'Democratic' is a mix of British Fabian Socialism and American Populism" – NOT valid (answer: none of the statements valid).
### 4. OBJECTIVES SECURED FOR ALL CITIZENS
#### 4.1 Kinds of Each Objective
| Objective | Of / Kinds |
|---|---|
| **Justice** | **Social, economic and political** (three types) |
| **Liberty** | **Thought, expression, belief, faith and worship** (only these) |
| **Equality** | **Status and opportunity** |
| **Fraternity** | Assuring **dignity of the individual** and **unity and integrity of the Nation** |
- Preamble secures to all: Justice (social, economic, political), Liberty of thought and expression, Equality of opportunity, Dignity of the Individual – **all of these**.
#### 4.2 Meaning of the Objectives
- **Political justice**: equal political rights and access to office.
- **Liberty**: **absence of restraints** on individual activity **plus opportunities to develop personality**; not freedom from every restraint.
- **Equality**: three dimensions – **civic, political, economic**.
- **Fraternity**: sense of **brotherhood**, promoted by **single citizenship**.
### 5. IS THE PREAMBLE PART OF THE CONSTITUTION? – CASES
| Case | Year | Held |
|---|---|---|
| **Berubari Union** (President's reference) | **1960** | **Not a part** of the Constitution; "**key to the minds of the makers**"; aid to interpretation where an article is ambiguous |
| **Golaknath** | **1967** | Dealt with **amending Fundamental Rights**; did not settle the Preamble's status (distractor) |
| **Kesavananda Bharati** | **1973** | Preamble **is a part**; **amendable under Art. 368**, but basic structure cannot be destroyed; **overruled Berubari** |
| **Minerva Mills** | **1980** | Struck down 42nd Amendment clauses (Art. 368 clauses **(4) and (5)**) |
| **S.R. Bommai** | **1994** | Reaffirmed **secularism as basic structure**; Preamble an **integral part** |
| **LIC of India** | **1995** | **Reaffirmed** Preamble is an integral part |
| **Dr Balram Singh v. Union of India** | **25 Nov 2024** | **Upheld 'Socialist' and 'Secular'**; rejected challenges to 42nd Amendment insertions |
- **Berubari** first declared the Preamble **not a part** of the Constitution.
- **Kesavananda Bharati**: **13-judge bench** gave the **Basic Structure doctrine (1973)**; Preamble seen as part of the basic structure.
!KEY APPSC GS 2025: "Preamble is an integral part – which case?" = Kesavananda Bharati. Distractors: Golaknath, Minerva Mills, Gopalan.
### 6. STATUS AND LEGAL EFFECT
- Reflects the **philosophy, ideals and objectives** of the Constitution; the **objective of the Constitution** is reflected in it.
- **Non-justiciable**: cannot be enforced in a court of law.
- **Does not confer any power** and does not create any right by itself.
- **Neither a source of power** to the legislature **nor a limit** on powers expressly granted.
- Part of the Constitution, but **no legal effect independently** of other parts (Kesavananda Bharati).
- Used as an **aid to interpretation** of ambiguous provisions (Berubari).
#### 6.1 Status at a Glance
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Part of the Constitution? | **Yes** (Kesavananda Bharati, 1973; reaffirmed LIC 1995) |
| Enforceable in court? | **No** – non-justiciable |
| Source of power? | **No** |
| Limit on express powers? | **No** |
| Aid to interpretation? | **Yes**, where a provision is ambiguous |
| Amendable? | **Yes**, under **Art. 368**, but not its basic features |
| Amended how many times? | **Once** – 42nd Amendment, 1976 |
| Enacted when? | **After** the rest of the Constitution was adopted |
- Status of India on **26 January 1950**: "**A Sovereign Democratic Republic**"; the Constitution was adopted on **26 November 1949** and came **into force on 26 January 1950**.
- The Preamble **declares India Secular** ('Secular' added by the 42nd Amendment, 1976).
!PAIR Berubari 1960 | Not a part | Key to makers' minds || Kesavananda 1973 | Part | Amendable, basic structure safe
### 7. AMENDABILITY AND SECULARISM AS BASIC STRUCTURE
| Case | Year | Holding |
|---|---|---|
| Kesavananda Bharati | 1973 | Amendable under **Article 368**; **basic features cannot be destroyed** |
| **Waman Rao** | **1981** | Basic-structure review applies to amendments made **after 24 Apr 1973** |
| S.R. Bommai | 1994 | Secularism basic structure → a State government acting against it could be dismissed |
- **Secularism** was part of the basic structure **even before** the word was inserted in 1976.
- **Kesavananda Bharati came before** the 1976 amendment.
- Amendments so far: **only one** (42nd, 1976).
### 8. 42ND AMENDMENT ACT, 1976
#### 8.1 Words Added
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Words added | **Socialist, Secular, Integrity** |
| Change | "Unity of the Nation" → "**Unity and Integrity of the Nation**" |
| Change | "Sovereign Democratic Republic" → "**Sovereign Socialist Secular Democratic Republic**" (held valid) |
| Context | **Emergency (1975–77)**, **Indira Gandhi** |
| Based on | **Swaran Singh Committee** |
| Nickname | **"Mini Constitution"** (sweeping changes) |
- **Integrity** was not in the Preamble in **1975**; added **1976**.
- **Not added** by the 42nd: **Fraternity** (original), **Sovereign** (original), Gram Swarajya.
- 'Secular' / 'Secularism' and 'Socialist' added by the **42nd** – not 43rd, 44th, 45th, 52nd or 86th.
#### 8.2 Other Changes
- Added **Fundamental Duties**, new DPSPs.
- **Curtailed judicial review** and tried to remove it in some matters – did **not abolish** it fully.
#### 8.3 Minerva Mills (1980)
- Struck down clauses giving Parliament **unlimited amending power** and **excluding judicial review**.
- Struck down clauses giving **DPSPs full priority over Fundamental Rights**; restored the **balance of Parts III and IV**, strengthening basic structure.
!TRAP "1976 Second Amendment" is WRONG – it is the 42nd Amendment.
#### 8.4 Debates
- Words inserted during the Emergency **without a fresh popular mandate**.
- Counter-view: secularism always inherent (Arts. 25–28).
- Political debate on the words: **Jul 2025**; SC had upheld them in **Nov 2024** (Balram Singh).
