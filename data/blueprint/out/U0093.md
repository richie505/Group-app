## U0093 | KAKATIYAS – ORIGINS, BETA I, RUDRADEVA, GANAPATIDEVA; KAKATIYA–YADAVA–HOYSALA RELATIONS
### 1. ORIGIN, OVERLORDS AND RELIGION
- **Second dynasty after the Satavahanas** to unify all the Telugu-speaking regions.
- Ruled the **eastern Deccan** (12th–14th c.) from **Warangal**; Seunas held the western Deccan, Eastern Gangas held Odisha.
- **First mention:** **Magallu grant (956)** of Eastern Chalukya **Danarnava**, through **Kakartya Gundana (Gundyana)**.
#### 1.1 Progenitor, Overlords and Phases
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Progenitor | **Kakara Gundana** (Magallu grant) or **Venna Bhupati** (Bayyaram inscription, which traces the line from **Durjaya**) |
| Overlords | **Rashtrakutas** first (APPSC G2 2005) → then **Kalyani Chalukyas** |
| Phases | **Samanta** (feudatory) **956–1162** → **sovereign 1163–1323** |
| Early base | **Hanamkonda Vishaya** (modern Karimnagar, Warangal, Khammam) |
| Feudatory title | **Mahamandaleswara** (under the Kalyani Chalukyas) |
| Emblem | **Garuda** (Rashtrakuta phase) → **Varaha** (Kalyani Chalukya phase), changed by **Prola I** |
!TRAP Overlord question: answer Rashtrakutas. If Rashtrakutas is not an option, choose Eastern Chalukyas (the Magallu grant is an Eastern Chalukya record).
#### 1.2 Religion and Clan Deity
- Early Kakatiyas = **Jains** (APPSC G2 2011) → later **Shaivas**.
- Clan deity **Kakatamma (Kakati Devi)** = the Jain **Kushmandini** absorbed into Hinduism; **"Kakati" = gourd**.
- **Beta II** was initiated into Saivism by **Rameswara Pandita** of the **Mallikarjuna matha, Srisailam** (APPSC 2025); gave him **Vaijanapalli**.
- **Mahadeva's** guru: **Dhruveshwara Pandita**.
- **Ganapatideva's** Raja Guru: **Visvesvara Sivacharya**, head of the **Golaki Matha**.
!KEY Saiva gurus: Rameswara Pandita → Beta II; Dhruveshwara Pandita → Mahadeva; Visvesvara Sivacharya → Ganapatideva.

### 4. CAPITALS – HANAMKONDA TO ORUGALLU
| Step | Ruler | Capital |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Prola I** | Made **Anumakonda (Hanamkonda)** capital |
| 2 | **Prola II** | Developed Hanamkonda |
| 3 | **Rudradeva** | **Shifted** capital to **Orugallu (Warangal)**; began the **Warangal fort** (APPSC G2 2019) |
| 4 | **Ganapatideva** | Completed Orugallu, moved the court fully; name **Andhranagari** |
| 5 | After 1323 | Renamed **Sultanpur** |
- **Orugallu** = ancient name of Warangal (**"single rock"**).
!TRAP "Who shifted the capital?" → **Rudradeva** (he partly shifted; Ganapatideva completed it).
### 5. GANAPATIDEVA (1199–1262)
#### 5.1 Release and Accession
- Held captive as a boy by Yadava **Jaitugi**; **Recherla Rudra** crushed revolts and installed him.
- Jaitugi released him in **1199** because Hoysala **Veera Ballala II** was pushing into the **Krishna–Tungabhadra** region.
- First inscription: **Manthena, 26 December 1199**.
- Married **Narama and Perama**, daughters of **Pinnachodi**; made their brother **Jayapa** **Gajasahani** (chief of elephant forces).
#### 5.2 Velanati Chodas, Nellore Chodas and Kalingas
- **1201 on:** coastal campaign; general **Mutyalachaundaraya** took **Divi**.
- **1210:** with **Choda Tikka** of Nellore killed **Prithviswara** → **Velanati kingdom ended**.
- **Nellore:** restored **Manumasiddhi II** to the throne; poet **Tikkana** came as envoy to seek his help.
- Tikkana dedicated his **Nirvachanottara Ramayana** to **Manumasiddhi** (not Ganapatideva).
- **1257:** with **Akkana and Bayyana** (enemies of Manumasiddhi) killed **Mummadi Rajendra Chola** = final end of the Velanati Chodas.
- **Pithapuram inscription** of Velanati **Prithvisvara** names **Kolleru Lake** (APPSC G2 2017).
- Fought the **Eastern Gangas** for control of **southern Kalinga and Vengi**.
#### 5.3 Defeat
- **1263, Battle of Muttukur (Muthukuru):** **Pandyas** defeated Ganapatideva; **Nellore fell** to the Pandyas (APPSC G2 2017).
#### 5.4 Other Deeds
- **Telugu replaced Sanskrit** in inscriptions from his reign (not Rudradeva's).
- Ramappa temple, **Lakkavaram** and **Ghanapur** tanks, Ganapavaram villages.
### 6. SUCCESSION AFTER GANAPATIDEVA
| Person | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Rudramamba (Rudrama Devi)** | No son → raised as a son (**putrika**); married Nidadavolu Eastern Chalukya prince **Veerabhadra**; ruled **1262–1289**; trained by mother **Kupama Devi** |
| **Ganapamba** | Married the **Kota** chief |
| **Mailamba, Kundamamba** | Ganapatideva's **sisters**; married sons of **Natavadi Buddharaja** |
- Rudrama defeated the **Pandyas**, **Eastern Gangas** (Odisha) and **Seunas of Devagiri** – not the Western Chalukyas (declined long before).
- **Marco Polo** visited the Kakatiya port **Motupalli ("Mutfili")** c. **1287–1290**, in Rudrama's reign, and praised the ruling queen.
!TRAP Mailamba = Ganapatideva's sister (Bayyaram tank inscription), not his daughter.
### 7. KAKATIYA–YADAVA–HOYSALA AND OTHER WARS
| Opponent | Episode |
|---|---|
| **Yadavas (Sevunas)** | Conflict began under **Prola II**; **Jaitugi killed Rudradeva**, held Ganapati; **Mahadeva died** in the revenge war; **Rudramadevi repulsed Yadava Mahadeva** (Bidar fort inscription) |
| **Hoysalas** | Veera Ballala II's advance → Jaitugi freed Ganapati; Prataparudra's commander **Pedarudru** defeated **Ballala III after 1316** |
| **Velanati Chodas** | Killed Prola II; Rudradeva avenged him, made **Vellanki Gangadhara** viceroy of Sabbinadu; ended 1210/1257 |
| **Kalachuri confederacy** | **Rudradeva** defeated Mailagi, Medaraja (Polavasa), Dommaraja (Nagumuru), **1159** |
| Telugu Chodas, **Tailapa III** | Defeated by Rudradeva |
| **Kalingas (Eastern Gangas)** | Ganapatideva and Rudrama fought them |
| **Pandyas** | Beat Ganapatideva at Muttukur, **1263** |
#### 7.1 Fall of Warangal
- **1310:** **Malik Kafur** besieged Warangal; Prataparudra became a tributary.
- **1323:** **Ulugh Khan** took Warangal → **Sultanpur**; Prataparudra, taken captive, **died jumping into the Narmada** on the way to Delhi (Musunuri **Vilasa** record).
- After 1323, Devagiri's vizier **Malik Burhanuddin** was given charge of Andhra.
### 8. FEUDATORIES AND ADMINISTRATION
| Family | Member and title / work |
|---|---|
| **Recherla** | **Recherla Rudra** – saved the kingdom during Ganapati's captivity; **Kakatiya Rajya Bharadhureya**; built **Ramappa temple and tank, Palampet, 1213** |
| **Kayastha** | **Prasadaditya** – **Kakatiya Rajya Sthapanacharya**, **Raya Pitamahanka** |
| **Viriyala** | **Kamasani** made Garuda Beta a Chalukya vassal (**Gudur inscription**); **Viriyala Suri** backed Rudrama |
| **Malyala** | **Malyala Chauda Senani** built **Chouda Samudram** |
| Natavadi | Buddharaja's sons married Ganapati's sisters |
- **Nayankara system:** organised under **Prataparudra II** – military-feudal land grants (nayakas held land for keeping troops).
- Feudatory order: Kakatiyas were **Mahamandaleswaras** before 1163.
- Tank building was the key to Kakatiya farming and welfare policy.

### 11. LEGACY – LINK TO VIJAYANAGARA
- One tradition says **Harihara and Bukka** (Sangama brothers) were **treasury officers of Prataparudra II** and fled to **Kampili** after the fall of Warangal in 1323; others link them to Kampiladeva or the Hoysalas.
- The Kakatiyas were the **first to rule all Telugu-speakers**; their Nayankara system was carried on by Vijayanagara.
