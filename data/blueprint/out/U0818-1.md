### 1. BORDER AND RURAL DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMMES
#### 1.1 Vibrant Villages Programme (VVP-I and VVP-II)
- Launched **2023**; border villages are called the nation's **"first villages"**.
- Nodal ministry: **Ministry of Home Affairs**; a **High-Powered Committee under the Cabinet Secretary** can relax guidelines.
| Feature | VVP-I | VVP-II |
|---|---|---|
| Type | **Centrally Sponsored Scheme** | **Central Sector Scheme** (100% central funding) |
| Focus | **Northern border** villages | **Other international land borders** |
| Launched at | **Kibithoo**, Anjaw district, Arunachal Pradesh | **Nathanpur**, Cachar, Assam (2026) |
| Outlay | ₹4,800 crore | ₹6,839 crore |
| Period | 2022-23 to 2026-27 | 2024-25 to 2028-29 (approved 2025) |
| Villages | **662** | **1,954** |
| Coverage | Arunachal Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Sikkim, Uttarakhand, Ladakh - 46 blocks, 19 districts | **15 States + 2 UTs** (J&K, Ladakh) |
- Combined outlay **₹11,639 crore**; VVP-I announced in **Budget 2022-23**.
- VVP-II skips **only blocks already covered under VVP-I**, not whole States.
- Roads under **PMGSY-IV** (Ministry of Rural Development); VVP-I uses a **hub-and-spoke** livelihood model.
- **ITBP** buys milk, vegetables and eggs from vibrant villages in Arunachal; reverse migration seen in Kurung Kumey, Dibang Valley, Shi-Yomi.
- **Viksit Vibrant Village Programme** (5 Jun 2026): youth volunteers of **MY Bharat** (Ministry of Youth Affairs) live in border villages of Ladakh, HP, Uttarakhand.
- **Border Area Development Programme (BADP)**: began **1986-87 on the western border**; now in sunset phase.
- Longest land border of India: **Bangladesh**; Kibithoo is linked to the **1962 war**.

#### 3.4 Food fortification and hidden hunger
- **Hidden hunger** = deficiency of micronutrients (iron, iodine, zinc, folic acid, vitamins A, B12, D).
- **Global Nutrition Report 2026**: ~**2.6 billion** people cannot afford a healthy diet (a separate finding).
- **WHO** (Sep 2026): India has about **one-third of global undernutrition**.
- Global: 100+ countries iodise salt (prevented ~750 million goitre cases in 25 years); cereal fortified in 86 countries; oil in 30+.
- India biofortified varieties (2014-2024): **152** - wheat 53, millets 26, maize 24, oilseeds 21, rice 14, pulses 9, amaranth 5.
- Examples: iron rice (Philippines), **iron pearl millet (India)**, iron beans (Rwanda).
| Year | Rice fortification event |
|---|---|
| 2018 | **Anemia Mukt Bharat** |
| 2019 | Pilot - iron, folic acid, vitamin B12 |
| March 2024 | All custom-milled rice in PDS, ICDS, PM-POSHAN, PMGKAY fortified |
| Till Dec 2028 | Extension - ₹17,082 crore |
| Feb 2026 | Temporarily discontinued **under PMGKAY only** (IIT Kharagpur report); PDS, ICDS, MDM not discontinued |
- PM-POSHAN uses **Double Fortified Salt**, oil with vitamins A and D, fortified rice.

### 4. HEALTH MISSIONS
#### 4.1 TB Mukt Bharat - JOSH Teams
- **JOSH = Joint Squad for Health**: launched nationally **8 October 2026**, Bharat Mandapam, by Health Minister **J.P. Nadda**.
- Phase 1: **55 districts in 11 States**; team = Class-I officers + MY Bharat volunteers + NCC cadets.
- AI chatbot **"Khushi Avatar"**.
- **67,933 panchayats in 697 districts** declared TB Mukt in 2025; **46,118 Gram Panchayats certified TB-free** (certification count, 15 Oct 2026).
- **PM TB Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan**: launched **9 September 2022**; **BPaLM** regimen for drug-resistant TB.
- New TB case rate 2015-2024: **India -21% vs global -12%**.
- 28.1 crore tests; 20 lakh+ got TB Preventive Treatment; 5.7 lakh new Ni-kshay Mitras; handheld X-ray screening up 34-fold.
- Background: *Mycobacterium tuberculosis*; **BCG** vaccine; **World TB Day 24 March** (Koch, 1882); **NTEP** (formerly RNTCP); India target 2025 vs SDG 2030.
- **MY Bharat** launched 31 October 2023; **NCC** est. 1948, motto "**Unity and Discipline**".
#### 4.2 Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY (eight years)
- Launched **23 September 2018**; 8 years on 23 Sep 2026.
- **₹5 lakh per family per year** (family floater, not per individual); 60 crore+ people; 1,961 procedures, 27 specialties.
- **World's largest government-funded health assurance scheme**; targets rural and poor families identified through **SECC**.
- Implemented by **National Health Authority**, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.
- ₹9,500 crore in Budget 2026-27; **48.54 crore Ayushman Cards** (23 Sep 2026).
- Cashless, paperless cover for **secondary and tertiary hospitalisation**.
#### 4.3 Hepatitis - NVHCP
- **National Viral Hepatitis Control Programme**: launched **2018**, MoHFW; prevention, screening, diagnosis, treatment; free services through district hospitals.
- Target: eliminate **Hepatitis B and C by 2030**, aligned with **SDG 3.3**.
- Hepatitis affects the liver; **Hepatitis B has a vaccine; Hepatitis C has none** (treated with antivirals).
#### 4.4 Research bodies and Ayush
- **ICMR-CHAMP** (Centre for High Altitude Medicine and Public Health Research): foundation stone by J.P. Nadda, **12 October 2026**, **Keylong, Lahaul and Spiti, HP**; altitude illnesses **AMS, HAPE, HACE**.
- **ICMR**: set up **1911 as IRFA**, renamed ICMR **1949**; under Department of Health Research.
- **Ayurvarta** digital discussion series: launched **1 September 2026** by **All India Institute of Ayurveda, New Delhi**.
- **Ayurveda Day** now fixed on **23 September** (earlier on Dhanteras); Ministry of Ayush.

### 6. LITERACY AND EDUCATION
#### 6.1 ULLAS and fully literate States/UTs
- **ULLAS** = Understanding of Lifelong Learning for All in Society (**New India Literacy Programme / Nav Bharat Saksharta**): **CSS**, Ministry of Education, **2022-23 to 2026-27**; adults **15+**; target **5 crore**; slogan "**Jan Jan Sakshar**"; outlay **₹1,037.90 crore**; app in 27 languages; test = **FLNAT**.
- **95% literacy** = "fully literate".
- **Ladakh**: first administrative unit (UT) declared fully functionally literate under ULLAS (**24 June 2024**).
- **Mizoram**: first fully literate **State** under ULLAS (**May 2025**).
- **Uttarakhand**: the 6th, 10 July 2026.
- **Lakshadweep**: declared **11 October 2026** by Administrator **Praful Patel** the first UT with **100% literacy** - all **1,113** identified illiterate adults passed FLNAT.
- 10 States/UTs declared fully literate (2024-2026): Ladakh, Mizoram, Goa, Tripura, Himachal Pradesh, Chandigarh, A&N Islands, Sikkim, Uttarakhand, J&K.
- Lakshadweep: capital **Kavaratti**; UT since 1 November 1956; smallest UT by area. **International Literacy Day: 8 September**.
