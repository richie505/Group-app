## U0438 | BORDER MANAGEMENT, INTERNAL SECURITY AND EXTERNAL LINKAGES; CROSS-BORDER TERRORISM
### 1. BORDER MANAGEMENT – SET-UP AND FORCES
#### 1.1 Administrative Set-up
- **Department of Border Management** (2004) – **Ministry of Home Affairs**; MHA also controls all **CAPFs**.
- **Ministry of Defence** controls the **Army** (guards **LoC** and **LAC**) and the **Coast Guard**.
- CAPFs guard **six** international borders.
#### 1.2 Force → Border → Raised
| Force | Border / role | Raised |
|---|---|---|
| **BSF** | **Pakistan and Bangladesh**; HQ Delhi; 193 bns (incl. 3 NDRF), 7 artillery regiments | **1 Dec 1965** (not 1962) |
| **ITBP** | **China**, 3,488 km, **Karakoram Pass to Jachep La**; posts at **9,000–18,800 ft**; 64 bns | **1962** |
| **SSB** | **Nepal (2001)** and **Bhutan (2004)**; 73 bns; newest border force | **1963** (Special Service Bureau) |
| **Assam Rifles** | **Myanmar** (not Bangladesh); **oldest** paramilitary | **1835** |
| **Coast Guard** | Territorial waters; under **MoD** | **1977** |
- **CRPF** began **27 July 1939** as the **Crown Representative's Police**; 88th Raising Day 2026.
- Coast Guard vessels: **Vikram, Samarth, Vishwast, Rajshree** Classes.
- **BSF jurisdiction** in Punjab and West Bengal: **15 km → 50 km**; states objected – **Police and Public Order** are in **List II**.

- **Greyhounds:** AP's anti-Naxal force, raised **1989**; base **Anandapuram (Visakhapatnam)**.
- Cut Naxal activity in **Alluri Sitharama Raju** and **Parvathipuram Manyam** districts; broke red corridors on the **Andhra-Odisha Border (AOB)**.
