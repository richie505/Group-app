## U0045 | KUSHANAS – KUJULA KADPHISES, KANISHKA, ERA OF 78 CE, FOURTH BUDDHIST COUNCIL, KUSHANA COINAGE
### 1. ORIGIN AND SIGNIFICANCE
- Kushanas = **Yueh-chi (Yuezhi)** from north-west China and Central Asia; Chinese texts call Kanishka's people the **Yue-chi**.
- **Silk Road middlemen** linking **Han China, Rome and India**.
- Trade led to the **second wave of urbanisation**: Mathura, Taxila, Ujjain, Shravasti.
- Manusmriti called Shakas, Kushans, Hunas and Pratiharas who did not follow priestly customs **"Fallen Kshatriyas"**.
### 2. KUSHANA RULERS
| Ruler | Contribution | Title / note |
|---|---|---|
| **Kujula Kadphises** (Kadphises I) | United the **five Yuezhi clans**; took **Kabul valley and Kashmir**; issued **copper** coins | Kushan ruler (APPSC key) |
| Vima Taktu | Between Kujula and Vima (Rabatak list) | — |
| **Vima Kadphises** (Kadphises II) | Extended empire to **Mathura and Varanasi**; **first large-scale gold**; **Shiva** devotee; **father of Kanishka** | **Maheshvara, Sarvaloka Ishvara** ("Lord of the Whole World") |
| **Kanishka** | Zenith; Fourth Buddhist Council; coronation **78 AD**; **Buddha on coins** | Devaputra and three other titles |
| **Huvishka** | Successor of Kanishka; **Mathura** a major centre | — |
| **Vasudeva I** | **Last great Kushana**; Indian name, Shiva on coins | — |
- **Rabatak genealogy**: Kujula Kadphises → Vima Taktu → Vima Kadphises → Kanishka.

### 3. KANISHKA – CAPITALS, EXTENT, INSCRIPTIONS, CHINA
| Place | Role |
|---|---|
| **Purushapura (Peshawar)** | Main / winter capital |
| **Mathura** | Another capital |
| Surkh Kotal | Provincial centre |
- **Rabatak inscription**: found **1993** near **Surkh Kotal, Afghanistan**; **Bactrian language, Greek script**; names Kanishka; four cities usually listed: **Saketa, Kaushambi, Pataliputra, Sri Champa** (some readings add **Ujjain** and **Kundina**).
- **Sarnath image inscription**: Bodhisattva image dedicated by **Friar Bala** in **year 3** of Kanishka (**81 AD**).
- **China**: Kanishka asked for a **Han princess**, was refused; the Kushanas marched against Chinese general **Pan Chao (Ban Chao)** in **86 AD** with **70,000** troops and **lost** to a smaller Chinese force.
!TRAP **Shravasti** is NOT named in the Rabatak inscription.

### 5. KANISHKA'S COURT SCHOLARS
| Scholar | Work / role |
|---|---|
| **Ashvaghosha** (of Pataliputra) | Court poet (poet laureate); **Buddhacharita, Saundarananda** (Kavya), Sariputraprakarana |
| **Vasumitra** | President of the Council; Mahavibhasha |
| **Nagarjuna** | **Madhyamika**; "Einstein of India"; **Suhrllekha**, a letter to a Satavahana king |
| **Parshva** | Buddhist scholar; urged the Council |
| **Charaka** | Court **physician**; Charaka Samhita |
| Mathara | Scholar in the court list |
- **I-tsing** saw students in India memorising Nagarjuna's **Suhrllekha**.
- **AP angle**: Nagarjuna is tied to Andhra (**Sriparvata**, Satavahana court).
!TRAP NOT in Kanishka's court: **Patanjali** (Shunga period, Mahabhashya), **Vishakhadatta**, **Kalidasa** (Gupta), **Kamban**. Buddhacharita = Ashvaghosha, not Nagarjuna.
### 6. MAHAYANA AND BUDDHA IMAGES
- Shift from **Pali-Prakrit Hinayana** to **Sanskrit Mahayana**; Mahayana arose under Kanishka.
- Mahayana treated the **Buddha as God** and worshipped him through **idols**; Hinayana saw him as a great man.
- Spread to **Central Asia, China, Korea, Japan**.
- **First Buddha images**: 1st c. CE, in Gandhara and Mathura styles.
| School | Style |
|---|---|
| **Gandhara** (north-west) | Greek style; **standing** Buddha in pleated robes |
| **Mathura** (Ganga valley) | Indigenous; **seated** Buddha with a **halo** |
| **Amaravati** (Andhra) | **White limestone** narrative panels |
- Stupas at **Zehanpora (Kashmir)** show the valley's Gandhara Buddhist heritage (2026).
!KEY Hinayana vs Mahayana: the basic difference is worship of the Buddha as God.
### 7. KUSHANA COINAGE
- **Indo-Greeks** (e.g. Menander) issued the **first** gold coins in India, but rarely.
- **Vima Kadphises**: **first Kushana** to issue gold and the first to issue it on a **large scale**, for regular use.
- **Kujula Kadphises**: copper; **Kanishka**: gold and copper with the **Buddha**.
- **Gold dinara**: **Roman** standard, about **8 g (120 grains)**, **22-23 carat**.
- Copper and lead coins plentiful for daily trade; Kushanas issued the largest number of copper coins.
- **30+ deities** (Greek, Iranian, Indian): Helios, Mithra, Nana, Shiva with Nandi, Buddha.
- **Portrait**: Kanishka standing in **peaked helmet, long coat and boots**, sacrificing at an **altar**.
- **Yaudheya** copper coins (Kushana period): **six-headed Kartikeya with a peacock**.
!PAIR Copper | Kujula Kadphises || Large-scale gold | Vima Kadphises || Buddha on coins | Kanishka
### 8. ITEMS INTRODUCED BY SHAKAS AND KUSHANAS
- **Dress**: **trousers, heavy long coat, helmet, boots** (APPSC G1 2024 key; **turban excluded**).
- Other lists also add turbans, saddles and armour: follow the APPSC key.
- **Military**: better **cavalry**; wider use of **saddles and reins**.
### 9. ERA OF 78 CE
- Usually credited to **Kanishka** (his coronation, 78 AD); some give **Gautamiputra Satakarni** the **Salivahana era**.
- Called **Saka era** because the **Western Kshatrapas (Sakas)** used it in records and coins; also **Salivahana Saka**.
- Began **135 years** after the Vikrama era (57 BCE).
- Became India's **national calendar in 1957**.
