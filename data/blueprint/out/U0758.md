## U0758 | GREEN HYDROGEN – NATIONAL GREEN HYDROGEN MISSION, ELECTROLYSERS, APPLICATIONS
### 1. HYDROGEN – BASIC FACTS
- **Hydrogen** = most common element in the Universe (about **3/4 of matter**; helium most of the rest).
- **Highest fuel value** (calorific value, kJ/kg); fuel with **minimum air pollution** – burning gives **only water** (coal, kerosene, diesel give CO2 and harmful gases).
- Burns in air at about **4% to 75%** by volume.
- **Ammonia (NH3)** = nitrogen + hydrogen.
- Exam-key facts: chemical elements on Earth ~**250**; diamond = purest carbon; sand = silicon + oxygen; marble = calcium, carbon, oxygen; sugar = carbon, hydrogen, oxygen.
### 2. COLOURS OF HYDROGEN
| Colour | Production route | CO2 |
|---|---|---|
| **Green** | **Electrolysis of water with renewable power** (solar, wind, hydro) | **Zero** – only zero-carbon colour |
| **Grey** | **Steam methane reforming (SMR)** of natural gas | High; ~**95%** of production; cheapest |
| **Blue** | SMR + **carbon capture and storage (CCS)** | Low; transitional |
| **Pink / purple** | Electrolysis with **nuclear** power | Near-zero |
| **Turquoise** | **Methane pyrolysis** (no oxygen) → solid carbon | Near-zero |
| **Brown / black** | Coal gasification without CCS | Very high – dirtiest |
| **White** | Naturally occurring geological hydrogen | – |
- **SMR:** methane + steam at **700–1000°C**, **nickel catalyst** → hydrogen + CO (syngas).
- Cost (Jul 2026): grey **$1.5–2/kg** vs green **~$4/kg**.
!TRAP Pink = nuclear electrolysis; turquoise = methane pyrolysis; blue = fossil + CCS.

#### 3.2 Targets for 2030
- **5 MMT/year** green hydrogen (up to 10 MMT with exports); **125 GW** added RE.
- **60–100 GW electrolysers** (not 200 GW); **6 lakh jobs**; **₹8 lakh crore+** investment; **50 MMT/yr GHG** cut; ₹1 lakh crore+ lower fossil imports.
- Goals: **energy independence 2047**, **net zero 2070**.
#### 3.3 Progress (Aug 2026)
- **15 companies** awarded **3,000 MW** electrolyser manufacturing capacity.
- Green ammonia **7,24,000 TPA** at **₹53.27/kg** (SECI) – among world's lowest.
- 20,000 MTPA green H2 awarded for IOCL, BPCL, HPCL refineries.
- **Jul 2026:** long-term green ammonia and green methanol export deals with **Japanese** firms.

### 5. ELECTROLYSERS
- **Electrolyser** = device that uses **electricity to split water** into hydrogen and oxygen.
| Type | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Alkaline** | Liquid electrolyte of concentrated KOH/NaOH; oldest, mature route |
| **PEM** | Solid **polymer membrane**; compact; **quick response → suits variable RE** |
| **Solid oxide** | **High-temperature steam**; most efficient |
| **AEM** (anion exchange membrane) | Pure water or dilute alkali; can use non-platinum catalysts (nickel, cobalt, iron) |
- Concern: **Chinese dominance** of electrolyser supply.
### 6. FUEL CELLS
- **Fuel cell** = electrochemical device: **H2 + O2 → electricity + water**; high efficiency; **zero emissions** at point of use; hydrogen is not burnt.
#### 6.1 PEM Fuel Cell Steps
1. **Anode:** H2 → 2H+ + 2e-.
2. Electrons flow through the **external circuit** → electricity.
3. **Protons cross the membrane**.
4. **Cathode:** 1/2 O2 + 2H+ + 2e- → H2O + heat.
- **Exhaust:** only **water vapour and warm air**.
### 7. STORAGE AND TRANSPORT
| Method | Fact |
|---|---|
| Compression | High-pressure **Type-IV** composite cylinders (roof-mounted on hydrogen train) |
| Liquefaction | **−253°C** (cryogenic); density **0.071 g/ml** → bulky tanks |
| Metal hydrides | Hydrogen absorbed in alloys, released on heating |
| Chemical carriers | **Ammonia, methanol, LOHC** (not CO2) |
- Water need: **~9 litres purified water per kg** – limit in dry solar states.
### 8. APPLICATIONS
- **Industry:** refining (desulphurisation), **ammonia for fertilisers**, methanol, **steel by DRI** (hydrogen replaces **coking coal**; 4 steel pilots ~₹106 cr).
- **Fuel uses:** fuel-cell vehicles (buses, trucks, trains, ships), **rocket fuel**, **H-CNG** (blending in CNG / city gas).
- **Power:** fuel cells, backup, long-duration storage, grid balancing.
- **Green ammonia** = nitrogen + green hydrogen; fertiliser, fuel, marine fuel; leads adoption.
- Green hydrogen uses: **direct IC fuel, blending with natural gas, fuel cells** (all three).
- Decarbonises **fertilizer plants, oil refineries, steel plants** (all three) – **hard-to-abate** sectors where direct electrification is difficult.
!KEY Ammonia for fertilisers = industrial use; fuel-cell vehicles, rocket fuel, CNG blending = fuel uses.
