## U0260 | LIBRARY MOVEMENT IN ANDHRA – ORIGIN, GROWTH AND ROLE IN AWAKENING
### 1. IDEA AND SIGNIFICANCE
- "**Air is available freely to all. Knowledge too should be available likewise.**" – **Chilakamarti Lakshmi Narasimham**.
- Under colonial curbs, libraries were **legal centres of political education**.
- They built the **Telugu identity** behind the separate Andhra statehood demand.
- Editors (Andhra Patrika, Krishna Patrika) publicised and funded the movement.

### 3. RISE OF THE ORGANISED MOVEMENT
1. By **1913**: about **125 libraries** in Andhra.
2. Call for a conference by **Ayyanki** and **Suri Venkata Narasimha** through **Andhra Patrika**.
3. **First Andhra library conference**, **Vijayawada, 10 April 1913** – at Ramamohan Library, meeting in the Edward Town Hall.
4. **200 delegates** from **60 libraries** (Andhra districts, Bellary and the Nizam's State).
5. Presided by **Chilakamarti Lakshmi Narasimham**.
6. **1914**: Andhra Desa Library Association founded.
7. **1915**: *Grandhalaya Sarvasvam* begins.

### 5. GRANDHALAYA SARVASVAM AND LIBRARY JOURNALS
- ***Grandhalaya Sarvasvam*** (**1915**) = **first Telugu journal on library science**, published by ADLA; associated with **Ayyanki Venkata Ramanayya**.
- Closed, then **revived in January 1948** as the Telugu-English quarterly ***Andhra Granthalayam***; editor **P. Rajasekharam**.
- Supporting press: Kasinathuni's **Andhra Patrika (1908)** carried the movement's calls.

!TRAP 1918 boat tour = Ayyadevara Kaleswara Rao; Bank Canal boat library (1935–42) = Pathuri Nagabhushanam.
### 7. ALL INDIA PUBLIC LIBRARY CONFERENCE
- The **All India Public Library Conference** was held at **Madras in 1919**, with Andhra leaders in the lead.
- By its decision the **All India Public Library Association** was set up (**1919**).
- Shows the Andhra movement taking its model to the national level.
### 8. LIBRARY LAWS

### 9. HYDERABAD STATE AND TELANGANA LIBRARY MOVEMENT
- Early Telugu libraries of Hyderabad State – **SKDABN (1901)**, **Vignana Chandrika Mandali (1906)**, **Andhra Samvardhani (1905)** – came from the same revival.
- The **Nizam's State** sent delegates to the 1913 Vijayawada conference.
- Libraries were the base for Telugu awakening → **Andhra Jana Sangham** → **Andhra Mahasabha** (first session at **Jogipet**).
