### 2. LORENZ CURVE AND GINI COEFFICIENT
- Lorenz: **X-axis** = cumulative % of population (poorest first); **Y-axis** = cumulative % of income.
- **45° line = perfect equality**; the farther the curve bends away, the greater the inequality. Visual only, no single number.
- **Gini** = area between Lorenz curve and equality line ÷ whole area under the equality line.
- Range **0** (perfect equality) to **1** (one person has everything); Gini index = value × 100; **higher = more unequal**.
- India's consumption Gini (World Bank, Spring 2025): **28.8 (2011-12) → 25.5 (2022-23)**; income Gini (World Inequality Database) rose **52 (2004) → 62 (2023)**.
### 3. KUZNETS CURVES
| Curve | Plots | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| **Kuznets hypothesis** | Inequality vs per capita income: first rises, then falls with growth | **Inverted U** |
| **Environmental Kuznets Curve** | Degradation/pollution vs per capita income | **Inverted U** |
- EKC message: "the solution to pollution is **economic growth**".
### 4. INCOME VS WEALTH VS CONSUMPTION
- NSSO/NSO household inequality indicator: **MPCE** (monthly per capita **consumption** expenditure), not income. Poverty level in India is also based on household consumption expenditure.
| Inequality | Data source | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Consumption | **NSS/HCES (MoSPI)**, every five years | Looks lower than other measures |
| Income | No official survey: **PLFS** wages, tax data, **WID** | Top 10% median = **13 times** bottom 10% (2023-24) |
| Wealth | **AIDIS** (All India Debt and Investment Survey) | Top 10% hold about **77%** |
| Land | World Inequality Lab (Apr 2026) | About **46%** rural households landless |
!TRAP Consumption data may **understate top-end incomes** (7 May 2026 analysis of HCES 2023-24).
### 5. RURAL-URBAN AND REGIONAL INEQUALITY
| Survey | Rural MPCE | Urban MPCE | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| NSS 2011-12 | **₹1,430** | **₹2,630** | about **₹1,200** (84%) |
| HCES 2022-23 | ₹3,773 | ₹6,459 | about 71% |
| HCES 2023-24 | **₹4,122** | **₹6,996** | about **70%** |
- Inter-State: **Bihar** has the lowest per capita NSDP (1980-81 and 2023-24); **Goa** highest (2023-24, constant prices).
- AP: district MPI headcount **2.42% (West Godavari) to 12.84% (Kurnool)** is the intra-State inequality measure; **Rayalaseema and North Coastal** lag.
### 6. MEASURES TO REDUCE INEQUALITY
| Direct (redistribute assets) | Indirect (taxes) |
|---|---|
| **Ceiling on land holdings**, land reforms, tenancy laws | Estate duty, wealth tax, capital gains tax, progressive income tax |
- **Wealth tax** abolished from **1 April 2016** (low yield, high compliance cost); **estate duty** abolished **1985**.
- **Art. 38(2)**: minimise inequalities in income; **Art. 39(c)**: prevent concentration of wealth.
