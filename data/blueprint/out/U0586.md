## U0586 | EXPRESSWAYS AND INDUSTRIAL CORRIDORS UNDER THE ACT; VIZAG-CHENNAI INDUSTRIAL CORRIDOR
### 1. ACT COMMITMENTS
- Thirteenth Schedule: GoI to **examine, within six months**, a **Vizag-Chennai Industrial Corridor** on the lines of **DMIC**.
- GoI to **"take steps"** for rapid **rail and road** links from the new AP capital to **Hyderabad** and other Telangana cities - no deadline.
- **AP-ICDA Act, 2017**: creates AP Industrial Corridor Development Authority; parks notified under **Section 3**.
### 2. CORRIDORS THROUGH AP
| Corridor | Support / fact |
|---|---|
| **VCIC** (~800 km) | **ADB**; first phase of **ECEC**; modelled on DMIC; port-led |
| **CBIC** (Chennai-Bengaluru) | **Japan (JICA)** |
| **HBIC** (Hyderabad-Bengaluru) | Third AP corridor; Orvakal node |
| DMIC (compare) | Japan, MoU **2006**; runs on Western DFC |
- **ECEC**: **Kolkata to Tuticorin** = India's **first coastal economic corridor**.
!TRAP VCIC = ADB (not JICA); CBIC = JICA. ECEC ends at Tuticorin (not Kanyakumari).
