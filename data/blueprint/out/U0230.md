## U0230 | GANDHIAN PHILOSOPHY – TRUTH, AHIMSA, SATYAGRAHA, MEANS AND ENDS
### 1. TRUTH AND AHIMSA
- **Truth is the end; non-violence (ahimsa) is the means** – two sides of one coin, inseparable.
- Truth = reality; described as **existence, consciousness and bliss**.
- **"God is Truth"** = earlier form; **"Truth is God"** = later form (truth given the central place).
- Ahimsa in **thought, word and deed**; a quality of the **strong (brave)**, not the weak.
- Between **cowardice and violence**, Gandhi would advise **violence**.
- **Family planning**: self-control is the best means.
!TRAP "God is Truth" came first; "Truth is God" later.
### 2. SATYAGRAHA
- **Satya + agraha** = insistence on truth; **"soul force"** in place of physical force.
- Born **1906 in the Transvaal**; early English name **passive resistance** (not "weak" resistance).
- Aim is **not to defeat the enemy**; **suffering** is its driving force.
- **First step: fasting** ("Fire Arrow"); **last step: strike**.
| Struggle | Year | Form / "first" |
|---|---|---|
| Champaran | 1917 | First civil disobedience (Salt March 1930 also civil disobedience) |
| Ahmedabad | 1918 | First hunger strike |
| Kheda | 1918 | First non-cooperation (NCM 1920-22) |
| Rowlatt Satyagraha | 1919 | **Hartal**; first all-India mass movement |
| NCM, Swadeshi | — | Boycott, picketing |
- Rules: **never insult the opponent**; bear assaults **without retaliation**; **submit to arrest**; obey jail discipline.
!TRAP First mass movement by Gandhi = Champaran; first all-India = Rowlatt (some call NCM 1920 the first nationwide).
### 3. MEANS, ENDS AND POLITICS
| Quotation | Point |
|---|---|
| "**Wrong means never take us to right ends**" | Means-ends purity |
| "Means are after all everything. As the means so the end" | Means decide the end |
| "What is **morally wrong** can never be **politically right**" | Ethics in politics |
| "Politics bereft of religion is a **death-trap**" | Religion = morality of truth and non-violence |
| "Real Swaraj ... capacity of all to **resist authority** when abused" | Swaraj and authority |
| **Cruellest violence** | Persistence of **poverty** |
| **Gandhi's Talisman** | Recall the face of the poorest and weakest |
- Politics = activity for **public welfare**; based on morality, religion, humanity; **no place for authority** – aim a stateless society.
- Key suggestion: **decentralisation** – village panchayats.
### 4. SWARAJ
- Not only political freedom: **self-rule, self-control** and capacity of the masses to govern themselves without coercion.
- Linked principles: **swadeshi, ahimsa, satyagraha, decentralisation, education for all**.
### 5. INFLUENCES
- **Jainism** (Gujarat; Jain thinker Raychandbhai), **Tolstoy** (The Kingdom of God is Within You), **Ruskin** (Unto This Last), **Thoreau** (Civil Disobedience) and the **Sermon on the Mount** (essence of Christ's teaching for him).
### 6. GANDHISM COMPARED
| Point | Gandhism | Marxism |
|---|---|---|
| Final goal | **Stateless society** | **Stateless society** (common ground) |
| Method | Non-violence, change of heart | Class struggle, revolution |
| Property | **Trusteeship** | Abolition of private property |
| Basis | Moral / spiritual | Economic determinism |
- **Philosophical anarchist**: "enlightened anarchy"; State = **violence in concentrated form**.
- Preamble **socialism** = blend of Marxism and Gandhism, with a **Gandhian tilt**.
- **Class dictatorship** is NOT Gandhian; **heavy industrialisation** = Nehru, not Gandhi (Nehru shared his social principles, differed on economics).
- Core values: truth, non-violence, compassion, simplicity, **Sarvodaya**, means-ends purity.
- **1 Sep 2026**: Gandhian non-violence linked to the **Baltic Way** and Singing Revolution (Baltic re-independence, 1991).
