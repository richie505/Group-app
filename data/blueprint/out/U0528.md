| **1 Sep 1956** | **LIC** (LIC Act 1956; 240+ insurers merged) |

!NUM Order: **RBI (1949) → SBI (1955) → LIC (1956) → 14 banks (1969)**. Earliest of BR Act 1949, First Plan 1951, SBI 1955 = **BR Act**.
- **C. D. Deshmukh**: first Indian RBI Governor; Finance Minister 1950-56.
### 4. BANK NATIONALISATION
#### 4.1 Two Rounds
| Basis | 1969 | 1980 |
|---|---|---|
| Date | **19 July 1969** | **15 April 1980** |
| Banks | **14** | **6** |
| Deposits | **₹50 crore** and above | **₹200 crore** and above |
| Law | Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, **1969** | – |
| PM | **Indira Gandhi** | **Indira Gandhi** |
- **1969 – 14 banks**: Allahabad (now Indian Bank), BoB, BoI, BoM, Central, Canara, **Dena (now BoB)**, Indian Bank, IOB, PNB, **Syndicate (now Canara)**, UCO, Union, United Bank of India (now PNB). The 1969 banks held about 85% of deposits.
- **1980 – 6 banks**: **Andhra Bank, Corporation Bank** (both now Union Bank), **Oriental Bank of Commerce** (now PNB), **Punjab & Sind Bank**, **Vijaya Bank** (now BoB), **New Bank of India**.
!TRAP **Syndicate Bank** was in the **1969** round, not 1980. **ICICI Bank** was never nationalised (formed 1955 as a DFI by World Bank, GoI and Indian industry).
#### 4.2 Reasons and Results
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Reasons | Industrial houses controlled banks; agriculture and small units neglected |
| Motives | **Social control of credit**; shift credit to agriculture and small industry |
| Results | **Branch expansion** + growth of **priority-sector lending** |
- Indira Gandhi's note "**Stray Thoughts on Bank Nationalisation**"; 50th anniversary 19 July 2019.
### 5. BANKING STRUCTURE
#### 5.1 Organised vs Unorganised
| Sector | Who |
|---|---|
| **Organised** | RBI; commercial banks (public, private, foreign, RRBs, SFBs, payments banks, local area banks); cooperative banks; DFIs (NABARD, Exim) |
| **Unorganised** | Indigenous bankers, moneylenders |
- Five ownership groups: **SBI, nationalised banks, RRBs, foreign banks, private banks**.
- **Scheduled banks**: in the **Second Schedule, RBI Act 1934**; paid-up capital and reserves **≥ ₹5 lakh**; may borrow from RBI.
- **Cooperative banks**: State Cooperative Societies Acts + RBI under BR Act; **one member, one vote**.
- **NABARD** = apex bank for agricultural credit.
- **NBFCs** cannot take demand deposits or issue cheques; not DICGC-insured.
- **DICGC (1978)**: merger of Deposit Insurance Corporation (1962) and Credit Guarantee Corporation of India.
#### 5.2 Foreign Banks
- Operate as **branches** or **wholly owned subsidiaries (WOS)** with **near national treatment** (RBI framework, Nov 2013); WOS minimum capital **₹500 crore**.
- Most branches: **Standard Chartered (UK, 100)**; HSBC 27, Deutsche Bank 17, Citibank 14.
### 6. DIFFERENTIATED BANKS – SFBs AND PAYMENTS BANKS
#### 6.1 Origin
- **Nachiket Mor Committee (2013)**: differentiated (niche) licences → **SFBs and payments banks**. Licence types: universal + differentiated; commercial banks now licensed **on tap**.
- Payments banks: licensed under **Sec 22, BR Act 1949**; public limited companies under the **Companies Act, 2013**.
#### 6.2 Comparison
| Feature | Small finance bank | Payments bank |
|---|---|---|
| Deposits | All types | **Demand deposits only**; max **₹2 lakh** per customer (earlier ₹1 lakh) |
| Lending | Yes: **75% of ANBC to PSL**; **50%** of loans ≤ **₹25 lakh** | **No lending, no credit cards** |
| Minimum capital | **₹100 crore** (2014 guidelines) | ₹100 crore paid-up voting equity |
| Other | Can sell MFs and insurance; **on tap since 2019** | **75%** of demand deposits in SLR G-secs/T-bills up to 1 year; CRAR **15%** |
| Purpose | Small units, small/marginal farmers, unorganised sector | Payments, remittances for migrants and low-income households |
- **SFB → universal bank** (RBI, April 2024): voluntary transition; **5 years** of satisfactory track record; minimum net worth **₹1,000 crore**; no change of promoters; **AU SFB** got approval (2025).
- RBI **cancelled Paytm Payments Bank's** licence (harmful to depositors and public).
!KEY **75%** = SFB PSL share = RRB PSL share = payments bank share of demand deposits in SLR securities.
### 7. PRIORITY SECTOR LENDING
| Target (domestic banks) | Share of ANBC |
|---|---|
| Total PSL | **40%** |
| Agriculture | **18%** (small and marginal farmers 10%) |
| Weaker sections | **12%** |
| Micro enterprises | **7.5%** |
| SFBs / RRBs | **75%** |
- **PSLCs** (2016): banks with surplus sell certificates to banks with shortfalls; traded on RBI's **e-Kuber**.
- **Collateral-free MSE loans** up to **₹20 lakh** (and all PMEGP units); banks may raise to **₹25 lakh** (Feb 2026).
- Credit to farmers = an **asset** of a bank.

### 9. LEAD BANK SCHEME AND SLBC
- Began **December 1969** (same year as 14-bank nationalisation); origin in **D. R. Gadgil Study Group** (report October 1969).
- Aim: individual banks **adopt districts** for intensive development; lead bank coordinates **district credit planning**; functions performed by a bank designated for the purpose.
- **Service Area Approach (April 1989)**: each rural/semi-urban branch serves **15–25 villages**.
| Forum | Level |
|---|---|
| Lead District Manager | District |
| **SLBC** | State |
- RBI draft revision of the scheme (Feb 2026) to strengthen SLBCs and LDM offices.
- **AP**: lead bank / **SLBC convener = Union Bank of India**; 236th SLBC (16 Sep 2026): CM Naidu asked banks to lend more to MSMEs, tenant farmers, PM Surya Ghar.
- Mega Credit Outreach Program in AP disbursed **₹3,216 crore** (18 Jul 2026).
