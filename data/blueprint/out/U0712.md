## U0712 | ICT AND GOVERNANCE – NeGP, DIGITAL INDIA PILLARS, E-KRANTI
### 1. E-GOVERNANCE CONCEPTS
| Model | Example |
|---|---|
| G2C | Citizen services (certificates, DigiLocker) |
| **G2B** | **MCA portal, GSTN** |
| **G2G** | **NIC** solutions for departments |
| **G2E** | Online HR tools for employees |
- **Four pillars**: **People, Process, Technology, Resources** (MPPSC 2024).
- **Gartner's four phases**: **Information → Interaction (2nd) → Transaction → Transformation** (MPPSC 2025).
- **Benefits**: lower cost of government, more citizen input (UPPSC 2024), **better transparency**. Challenges: digital divide, shared devices in rural areas, data privacy.
!TRAP E-governance does NOT reduce transparency.
### 2. IMPLEMENTING BODIES → ROLE
| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| **NIC** (**1976**) | Base of India's digitisation; G2G solutions; built **e-Taal** (dashboard of e-transaction statistics) |
| **MeitY** | Coordinates Digital India; **UIDAI** (statutory) is under MeitY |
| **DeitY (now MeitY) + DARPG** | Drafted NeGP |
| **NeGD** | MeitY's programme-management wing under **Digital India Corporation** |
| **CSC e-Governance Services India Ltd** | SPV and implementing agency of CSCs |
| **NIXI + MeitY** | **BhashaNet** on Universal Acceptance Day, **21 Mar 2024** |
- APPSC 2017 key gave **NITI Aayog** for the drive to make 100% of G2C transactions digital.
### 3. NATIONAL E-GOVERNANCE PLAN (NeGP), 2006
- Approved **18 May 2006** with **27 MMPs** and **10 components**; later **31 MMPs**.
#### 3.1 Three-tier Core Infrastructure
| Tier | Component | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Front end | **CSCs** | Village ICT kiosks run by local entrepreneurs |
| Network | **SWAN** | State HQ to block/taluka links |
| Back end | **SDC** | State data centres |
- Other pieces: **MSDG (2011)** for mobile services; **Meghraj = GI Cloud**; **india.gov.in** = National Portal.
#### 3.2 MMP Categories
| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Central | **MCA21**, **Passport Seva**, **Income Tax**, UID, Pensions, Posts |
| State | Land Records, Treasuries, **e-District**, **e-Panchayat**, **CCTNS** |
| Integrated | **eCourts**, **e-Procurement**, CSC, India Portal, **e-Biz** |
!NUM 27 MMPs (2006) → 31 (NeGP expanded) → 44 (e-Kranti)

### 5. DIGITAL INDIA – LAUNCH AND VISION
- Launched by PM Modi on **1 July 2015**; coordinated by **MeitY** (APPSC 2025).
- **Umbrella programme** across ministries; PPP preferred; aim: **digitally empowered society and knowledge economy**; services to citizens electronically.
| Vision area | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Digital infrastructure as a utility** | High-speed internet, cradle-to-grave digital identity, CSC access, cloud locker |
| **Governance and services on demand** | Real-time online services, cashless transactions, GIS |
| **Digital empowerment of citizens** | **Universal digital literacy**, **documents on cloud**, **services in Indian languages** |
- **NOT part / not its aims**: **issue of digital currency** (APPSC 2024); building India's own internet companies "like China"; a framework for overseas MNCs collecting Big Data.
!TRAP 15 Aug 2014 (Independence Day speech) and 25 Sep 2014 (Make in India) are not Digital India's launch.

### 7. E-KRANTI (NeGP 2.0)
- Approved **2015**; **44 MMPs**; vision: "**Transforming e-Governance for Transforming Governance**".
- APPSC 2017: electronic delivery of all services = **e-Kranti**.
- Principles: **transformation not translation**; **integrated not individual** services; **cloud by default**, **mobile first** (not paper-first).

### 9. INDIA STACK, DPI AND EDUCATION PLATFORMS
- **India Stack**: open, interoperable public rails; layers: **identity = Aadhaar**, **payments = UPI**, **data = DEPA / Account Aggregator** (consent-based).
| Platform | Role |
|---|---|
| **UPI / BHIM** | Live in **11 countries**; linked with Singapore's **PayNow (2023)** |
| **DigiLocker** | Cloud wallet; 56.06 crore users; 10 MB, extendable to 1 GB |
| **Bhashini** | **National Language Translation Mission** (Jul 2022); 260 open-source AI models; joined with NIC tools such as **CollabFiles** |
| **BhashaNet** | **Universal Acceptance** portal of NIXI and MeitY |
| **SWAYAM** | Free **MOOCs** for **higher education** (IITs, IIMs); **NPTEL** is a national coordinator |
| **DIKSHA** | **School** e-content; QR-coded textbooks; teacher training |
| **PM eVIDYA** | **One Class, One TV Channel** (DTH, radio, podcasts); joins DIKSHA and SWAYAM |
!PAIR SWAYAM | higher education | NPTEL coordinator || DIKSHA | school | QR textbooks
### 10. CONNECTIVITY, INDICES AND IMPACT
- Rural gaps: **GP fibre via BharatNet** and **4G in villages**; **PM-WANI** public Wi-Fi.
- **AP Fibernet** (APSFL, Govt of AP): triple play (**IPTV, internet, telephone**) over GPON; launched **27 Dec 2017**.
- AP: Singapore delegation studied AP's **RTGS Data Lake and Command Centre** (Jul 2026).
- **UN EGDI 2024**: India **97th** (105th in 2022). DARPG launched **NeSDA 2025** portal (Jun 2026).
- **PMGDISHA (2017)**: rural digital literacy; **one person per household**.
- UPI volume up 13,000-fold in 10 years (Aug 2026).
- DPI raised the digital economy to about **12–14% of GDP**.
