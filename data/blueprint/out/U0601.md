## U0601 | MAJOR LANDFORMS – FLUVIAL LANDFORMS AND RIVER PROFILES
### 1. LANDFORMS AND THEIR AGENTS
| Landform | Formed by | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Mountains | Folding, faulting, volcanism, erosion | Himalayas, Andes, Alps, Rockies, Aravallis |
| Plains | Deposition, erosion, marine processes | Indo-Gangetic Plain, Great Plains |
| Valleys | River, glacier, tectonic faulting, karst solution | V-shaped, U-shaped, rift, hanging, blind |
| Glacial | Glacial erosion and deposition | Cirques, arêtes, horns, U-shaped and hanging valleys, moraines |
| Desert | Wind erosion and deposition | Dunes, yardangs, mushroom rocks, playas, loess |
| Karst | Solution of limestone | Sinkholes, dolines, caves, stalactites, stalagmites, blind valleys |
| Coastal | Waves, currents, tides | Cliffs, caves, arches, stacks, beaches, spits, lagoons |
- Young fold mountains: **Alps, Andes, Rockies**; old: **Appalachians, Urals, Pennines, Aravallis**.
- **Alluvial soils of AP delta regions** are formed by **river deposition**.
### 2. RIVER PROFILE – UPPER, MIDDLE, LOWER COURSE
| Course (stage) | Dominant work | Typical features |
|---|---|---|
| Upper (youth) | **Vertical erosion**, steep gradient | V-shaped valleys, gorges, **canyons**, waterfalls, rapids, potholes |
| Middle (maturity) | **Lateral erosion + transport** | Wider valleys, meanders, floodplains begin |
| Lower (old age) | **Deposition**, gentle gradient | Broad floodplains, levees, oxbow lakes, braided channels, deltas/estuaries |
- Middle course does side erosion and transport too; vertical erosion is not its only work.
!KEY APPSC 2022: A **canyon** forms in the **upper course**; floodplain, alluvial fan and delta do not.
### 3. PROFILE TERMS – GRADE, BASE LEVEL, CAPTURE
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Graded profile** | Smooth **concave** long profile; erosion and deposition roughly balanced |
| **Base level** | Lowest level a river can erode to; **sea level = ultimate**; lakes and dams = local |
| **Stream capture (river piracy)** | Headward erosion breaks a nearby divide and takes a neighbour's upper waters |
| **Peneplain** | Near-featureless gentle plain worn by rivers almost to base level |
### 4. RIVER-PLAIN TERMS AND PLAIN TYPES
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Doab** | Land between two confluent rivers (Persian "do-ab" = two waters); e.g. **Ganga-Yamuna doab**, **Bist Doab** (Beas-Sutlej) |
| **Bhabar** | Narrow pebble belt along the **Shiwalik foothills**; streams drop load and vanish underground |
| Terai | South of Bhabar; water re-emerges, wet marshy belt |
| **Bhangar** | **Older alluvium** above flood level; contains **kankar** (lime nodules) |
| **Khadar** | **Newer alluvium** of the floodplain; renewed every flood, more fertile |
| **Prayag** | A sacred river **confluence** |
- **Panch Prayag** on the Alaknanda (downstream order): **Vishnuprayag** (Dhauliganga), **Nandprayag** (Nandakini), **Karnaprayag** (Pindar), **Rudraprayag** (Mandakini), **Devprayag** (Bhagirathi; the river is called **Ganga** from here).
| Plain type | Formed by |
|---|---|
| Alluvial plain | River deposition of alluvium |
| Flood plain | Fine silt dropped when river overflows |
| **Lacustrine plain** | Lake filled with sediment, then drained; e.g. **Kashmir Valley** (Karewas) |
| Peneplain | Long river erosion to near base level |
!TRAP Bhabar vs Terai = relief and drainage; Bhangar vs Khadar = age of alluvium. Khadar is newer.
### 5. EROSIONAL LANDFORMS
| Landform | Formation |
|---|---|
| **V-shaped valley** | Rills → gullies → valleys by vertical erosion |
| Waterfall | Drop over a resistant rock band or fault step |
| **Potholes** | Eddy-whirled fragments drill circular holes; large one below a waterfall = **plunge pool** |
| **Incised (entrenched) meanders** | Uplift **rejuvenates** the river; meanders cut deep into hard rock |
| River terraces | Stream cuts down into its own floodplain; **paired** = same height both banks; **unpaired** = one bank, slow uplift |
### 6. GORGE VS CANYON
| Basis | Gorge | Canyon |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Deep, narrow, steep-sided valley | Much deeper gorge |
| Width | Almost same at top and bottom | **Wider at top** |
| Rock | Hard rocks | **Horizontally bedded sedimentary** |
| Example | Chisapani Gorge | **Grand Canyon** (Colorado River) |
- Both are forms of the V-shaped valley; **canyon is a variant of a gorge**, not the reverse.
- **Grand Canyon**: carved by the **Colorado** in **Arizona, USA**; about **446 km** long, over **1.8 km** deep.
- **Dihang gorge**: Brahmaputra enters India (Arunachal); Yarlung Tsangpo Grand Canyon near **Namcha Barwa**.
- **Gandikota canyon**: **Pennar (Penna) River, AP** – "**Grand Canyon of India**".
!TRAP APPSC 2018: "Gorges and canyons are V-shaped valleys" = correct; "gorges are extended forms of canyons" = WRONG.
### 7. RAVINES AND BADLANDS
- Gully = water-worn channel of semi-arid areas; rills merge and deepen into gullies; dense gully network = **badland topography**.
- India's classic badlands: **Chambal ravines** (central India); also **Chos of Hoshiarpur** (Punjab).
!KEY APPSC 2022: Badland topography = **Chambal basin** (not Rann of Kutch, Godavari delta, western coastal plain).
### 8. DEPOSITIONAL LANDFORMS
| Landform | How it forms |
|---|---|
| **Alluvial fan** | Coarse load dropped at the **break of slope** as a fan |
| **Natural levee** | Raised sandy banks along the channel |
| Floodplain | Fine alluvium dropped when the river overflows |
| **Braided channel** | Sand bars split the channel into many strands with riverine islands |
| **Oxbow lake** | Meander loop cut off; stagnant water left |
- Meander: **outer bend concave, erosional** (cliff); **inner slip-off side convex, depositional**.
!TRAP APPSC 2023: Depositional work of Himalayan rivers = braided channels, alluvial fans, natural levees; **terraces excluded** (formed by down-cutting).
### 9. DELTAS AND ESTUARIES
#### 9.1 Delta vs Estuary
| Basis | Delta | Estuary |
|---|---|---|
| Process | Deposition | Erosion / tidal flushing |
| Mouth | Many distributaries | Single funnel opening |
| Example | Ganga-Brahmaputra, Godavari | **Narmada, Tapi**, Western Ghats rivers |
- **Estuary**: partly closed river mouth with free connection to the sea; an **ecotone**. **Tapti forms an estuary**.
- West-flowing Deccan rivers form estuaries: **steep gradient, hard rock, short courses**.
- Conditions for a delta: slow flow, heavy sediment load, shallow sea, **weak tides and currents**. Strong tides remove sediment.
#### 9.2 Delta Types
| Type | Shape | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Arcuate** (fan) | Most common, arc | **Nile**, Ganga |
| **Bird's foot** | Long branching distributaries | **Mississippi** |
| Cuspate | Tooth-shaped | **Tiber** (Italy) |
| Estuarine | Drowned funnel mouth | Narmada, Tapi |
- **World's greatest delta**: **Ganga-Brahmaputra** (with Meghna; Sundarbans) – **world's largest mangrove forest**; about **two-thirds in Bangladesh**.
- **Sinking deltas (Apr 2026)**: 7 deltas (Ganga-Brahmaputra, Nile, Mekong, Yangtze, Amazon, Irrawaddy, Mississippi) = about **57%** of sinking delta area; causes: **groundwater extraction + less silt**.
#### 9.3 AP Coast
- Coastal plain ~950 km long; built by **Godavari** (delta = **Konaseema**) and **Krishna** deltas.
- Minor deltas: **Vamsadhara** (Srikakulam), **Penna** (near Nellore).
- **Kolleru**: freshwater lake between Godavari and Krishna deltas; WLS **308.55 sq km** (2024-25).
- **Pulicat**: saltwater lagoon on the **AP–Tamil Nadu border**.
### 10. DRAINAGE PATTERNS
| Pattern | Control | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Dendritic** | Uniform rock; tree-like | Northern plains, Assam Valley, South India, Sindhu, Mahanadi, Godavari |
| **Trellis** | Folded rocks; tributaries join at right angles | **Appalachians**, Singhbhum |
| **Radial** | Outward from a high | **Amarkantak** (Narmada, Son, Mahanadi) |
| **Centripetal** | Into a central depression | **Loktak Lake**, Manipur |
| Rectangular | Joints and faults; right-angle bends | Joint-controlled plateaus |
| Annular | Rings on an eroded dome | Structural domes |
!PAIR Amarkantak | Radial | outward || Loktak | Centripetal | inward
