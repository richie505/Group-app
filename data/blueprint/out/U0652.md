## U0652 | INDIA - SCHEDULED CASTE POPULATION: DISTRIBUTION AND CONCENTRATION
### 1. SC POPULATION, CENSUS 2011
- SCs **20.14 crore** = **16.6%** of India (2001: 16.66 crore = **16.2%**); about 20% more than in 2001.
- **Census 2027** (Q.10 "SC/ST/Caste") = **first enumeration of all castes** in independent India, not only SCs/STs.
!TRAP SC share 2001 = 16.2%, 2011 = 16.6% - not 17.5%.
### 2. RURAL-URBAN DISTRIBUTION
- About **76% rural**, 24% urban.
- SCs = **18.5% of rural** vs **12.6% of urban** population.
- SCs more rural than the general population; **STs even more rural (89.97%)**.
### 3. STATES - NUMBER VS SHARE
| Measure (2011) | State |
|---|---|
| Highest number | **Uttar Pradesh** (about 4.1 crore, ~20% of India's SCs); then West Bengal, Bihar, Tamil Nadu |
| Highest share | **Punjab 31.9%**; then Himachal Pradesh 25.2%, West Bengal 23.5% |
| Lowest share (with SCs) | **Mizoram ~0.1%**, Meghalaya ~0.6% |
- PYQ keys: number = UP (UPPSC 2014, BPSC 2011); share = Punjab (UPPSC 2006).
!KEY Largest number = UP; largest share = Punjab.
### 4. NO NOTIFIED SC POPULATION
- **Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep** (APPSC 2022).
- Reason: overwhelmingly **tribal**; SC status comes from **untouchability in the Hindu caste order**, ST status from tribal traits.
- Small but non-zero: Mizoram (~0.1%), Meghalaya (~0.6%); **Manipur has notified SCs**.
!TRAP Mizoram is negligible, not zero - pick the option Nagaland, Lakshadweep, A&N, Arunachal.
### 5. CONCENTRATION PATTERN
- Highest SC-share district: **Koch Bihar (West Bengal)**, about 50%.
- Dense in **fertile alluvial plains and coastal deltas** (Punjab, Ganga plain, West Bengal) - settled farming used landless labour.
- Sparse in hill, forest and north-eastern tribal districts.
### 6. ANDHRA PRADESH
- AP SCs **84.69 lakh** = **17.08%** of AP (APPSC-G2 2017) = **4.21%** of India's SCs.
- Growth: 32.73 lakh (1971) → 84.69 lakh (2011).
### 7. CONSTITUTION AND LEGAL BASIS
| Provision | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Article 341** | **President** notifies SCs for each State/UT; a State cannot change the list |
| Article 46 | DPSP: promote educational and economic interests of weaker sections, esp. SCs/STs |
| **Article 330 / 332** | Reserved seats in Lok Sabha / State Assemblies |
| Lok Sabha SC seats | **84** of 543 (raised from 79 by 2008 delimitation on Census 2001) |
| Coverage | SCs in **31 States/UTs**; **1,241 groups** |
| Religion rule | Hindus; **Sikhs added 1956**; **Buddhists 1990**; Jains not included |
| **83rd Amendment (2000)** | No SC panchayat reservation in **Arunachal Pradesh** (wholly tribal) |
### 8. SOCIO-ECONOMIC PROFILE
| Indicator | SC | National |
|---|---|---|
| Literacy 2011 | **66.1%** (54.7% in 2001) | about 73% |
| Occupation | Marginal farmers, landless labourers | Upper castes linked to land |
| Unemployment 2017-18 | 5.5% | 5% |
| IMR 2018 | **44** | 32 |
- Punjab has one of the highest SC enrolment shares.
