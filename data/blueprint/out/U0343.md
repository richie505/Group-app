## U0343 | UNION PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION AND STATE PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSIONS – COMPOSITION, FUNCTIONS, REMOVAL
### 1. CONSTITUTIONAL STATUS – PART XIV
- UPSC is an **independent constitutional body**, directly created by the Constitution (**Article 315**).
- UPSC is the **central recruiting agency** of India.
- Provisions: **Part XIV**, **Articles 315–323**.
- UPSC has **nothing to do with the State PSCs**; each works independently.
#### 1.1 Articles 315–323 – Number Game
| Article | Content |
|---|---|
| 315 | PSCs for the Union and the States (incl. **JSPSC**) |
| 316 | Appointment and term of members |
| 317 | **Removal and suspension** of members |
| 318 | Regulations on **conditions of service** |
| 319 | **Bar on holding office** after ceasing to be a member |
| 320 | **Functions** of PSCs |
| 321 | Power to **extend functions** by law |
| 322 | **Expenses** charged on the **Consolidated Fund** |
| 323 | **Reports** – UPSC to President, SPSC to Governor |
- **Art. 315(4)**: UPSC may **serve a State's needs** on the **request of the Governor** with the **approval of the President**.
!KEY Article 322 = expenses of PSCs are charged on the Consolidated Fund (not voted).
### 2. ORIGIN AND HISTORY OF UPSC
#### 2.1 Acts and Commissions → Year
| Year | Step / development |
|---|---|
| 1864 | **Satyendranath Tagore** – first Indian to clear the ICS |
| 1919 | **Government of India Act, 1919** provided for a Public Service Commission |
| 1922 | ICS exams also held in India (Allahabad) – **Montagu-Chelmsford** reforms |
| 1924 | **Lee Commission** recommended a PSC |
| 1 Oct 1926 | First **Public Service Commission** set up; first chairman **Sir Ross Barker** |
| 1935 | **GoI Act, 1935**: **Federal PSC** + provincial PSCs |
| 26 Jan 1950 | Federal PSC became **UPSC** (members continued under Art. 378) |
- UPSC headquarters: **Dholpur House, New Delhi**.
- **Roze Millian Bethew** – first woman Chairman of UPSC (1992–1996).
!TRAP The PSC was recommended in 1924 (Lee Commission) but set up only on 1 October 1926.
### 3. COMPOSITION AND APPOINTMENT
#### 3.1 UPSC vs SPSC vs JSPSC
| Point | UPSC | SPSC | JSPSC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Appointed by | **President** | **Governor** (Art. 316(1)) | **President** |
| Strength fixed by | President (usually 9–11 incl. Chairman) | Governor | President |
| Term | 6 years or age **65** | 6 years or age **62** | 6 years or age **62** |
| Resigns to | President | Governor | **President** |
| Removed by | President | **President** (not Governor) | President |
| Report to | President | Governor | each concerned Governor |
- The Constitution does **not fix** the number of members.
- **At least half** the members must have held office under GoI/State Govt for **10 years**.
- **Acting Chairman**: appointed by the President when the Chairman's office is vacant or he is absent.
- **No second term** in the same office (no reappointment).
- **Madhya Pradesh PSC**: maximum five members; Art. 318 – rules on conditions of service.
!TRAP APPSC 2019: SPSC Chairman is appointed by the **Governor** but removed only by the **President**.
### 4. REMOVAL AND SUSPENSION – ARTICLE 317
#### 4.1 Grounds
1. Adjudged **insolvent**.
2. **Paid employment** outside the duties of office.
3. **Infirmity** of mind or body (in the President's opinion).
4. **Misbehaviour** – only after reference to the **Supreme Court**.
- SC inquiry: removal only if the SC upholds the cause; its **advice is binding**; **Parliament has no role**.
- **Suspension**: President (Governor for SPSC) may suspend the member during the SC inquiry.
- **Art. 317(4)** misbehaviour includes being **interested in a government contract** or sharing in its profit.
- UPSC Chairman removed by the President – **Art. 317(1)**, on misbehaviour, after SC reference.
- SPSC member removed by the **President** after the Supreme Court enquiry.
!KEY Removal process is like no impeachment: the President removes; the Supreme Court inquires; Parliament plays no part.
### 5. INDEPENDENCE SAFEGUARDS
- Removal only in the manner and on the grounds stated in the Constitution (**security of tenure**).
- **Conditions of service** cannot be varied to the member's disadvantage after appointment.
- **Expenses** charged on the **Consolidated Fund** (Art. 322).
- **Post-retirement bar** (Art. 319); **no reappointment** to the same office.
#### 5.1 Post-retirement – Article 319
| Office | May later hold |
|---|---|
| UPSC Chairman | **Nothing** under GoI or any State |
| UPSC member | UPSC Chairman or SPSC Chairman only |
| SPSC Chairman | UPSC Chairman/member; nothing else under a State |
| SPSC member | UPSC Chairman/member, or Chairman of the same or another SPSC |
- A former SPSC member can get **no other government employment** (e.g. under the Government of India).
### 6. FUNCTIONS – ARTICLE 320
#### 6.1 Consulted On
- Conducts **examinations** for **All-India, Central and Group-A services** (SPSC: State services).
- **Methods of recruitment**.
- Principles of **appointment, promotion and transfer**; suitability of candidates.
- **Disciplinary matters** affecting civil servants.
- Claims for **legal expenses** and **pension** for injuries on duty.
- **Temporary appointments of more than one year**.
- Extension of service / re-employment.
#### 6.2 NOT Consulted (Full List)
- **Reservations** of posts for backward classes, SCs and STs (**manner of reservation**).
- Claims of SCs/STs to services and posts.
- **Temporary posts of less than one year**.
- **Group B/C** posts and the **highest diplomatic posts**.
- **Classification, pay and training** of services.
- SPSC is **not consulted on transfers** of civil servants.
- President (Governor for SPSC) may exclude posts by **regulations**, laid before Parliament/Legislature for **14 days**.
!TRAP APPSC-G1 2022: "Manner of reservation" is NOT a UPSC function – reservation policy is set by the government.
#### 6.3 Sources of Functions
- From all four: the **Constitution**, **Parliamentary laws**, executive **rules and orders**, and **conventions**.
### 7. EXTENSION AND NATURE OF ADVICE
- **Art. 321**: functions widened only by an **Act of Parliament** (State Legislature for SPSC) – may cover **local bodies, corporate bodies, public institutions**.
- Advice is only **advisory**; consultation is **directory, not mandatory**.
- Action taken without consulting UPSC is **not invalid** for that reason alone.
- **Selection confers no right to the post** (Supreme Court).
- **CVC** (1964, statutory 2003) is also consulted on disciplinary matters; UPSC has the edge.
- SPSC is the "**watchdog of the merit system**".
### 8. ANNUAL REPORT AND NON-ACCEPTANCE OF ADVICE – ARTICLE 323
| Body | Report to | Laid before |
|---|---|---|
| UPSC | President | **both Houses** with a memorandum on non-accepted advice |
| SPSC | Governor | State Legislature with a similar memorandum |
| JSPSC | **each concerned Governor** | his State Legislature |
- **Non-acceptance**: every case needs approval of the **Appointments Committee of the Cabinet**; a ministry cannot reject UPSC advice on its own.
### 9. JOINT STATE PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION (JSPSC)
- Created by an **Act of Parliament** on the request of **two or more State Legislatures** (resolutions) – **Art. 315(2)**.
- **Statutory**, not constitutional, body. Example: **Punjab and Haryana** (once).
- Chairman + members **appointed, suspended and removed by the President**; resign to the **President**.
- Number and service conditions fixed by the **President**; term 6 years or 62.
!PAIR Constitutional body | UPSC, SPSC | Art. 315 || Statutory body | JSPSC | Act of Parliament
### 10. APPSC AND COMMITTEES
#### 10.1 APPSC
- Came into existence on **1 November 1956** with the formation of Andhra Pradesh (APPSC 2023 key).
- Merged the **Andhra PSC (1953)** and the **Hyderabad PSC**.
- After **2014** bifurcation: APPSC continues for residuary AP; Telangana got **TSPSC**; HQ moved to **Vijayawada**.
- Strength: **Chairman + 9 Members** (enhanced in 1994); Chairman and members appointed by the **Governor of AP**, removed by the **President**.
- Term 6 years / age **62**. Functions: State service recruitment + **lower judicial service** exams (**Art. 234**).
#### 10.2 Committees on Civil Services Examination
| Committee | Year | Known for |
|---|---|---|
| **Kothari** | 1976 | Three-stage exam: prelims, mains, interview |
| **Satish Chandra** | 1989 | Added the **essay** paper |
| **Y.K. Alagh** | 2001 | Review of the exam; aptitude test idea |
| **B.S. Baswan** | 2015–16 | Age limit and pattern review |
#### 10.3 Other Bodies (Distractors)
- **Finance Commission** (Art. 280): formed by the President every five years; **five members** incl. Chairman; qualifications set by Parliament (**FC Act, 1951**); first Chairman **K.C. Neogy**.
- **22 members (15 LS + 7 RS)** = **PAC**, not the FC.
- **Lokpal Selection Committee**: PM (chair), Lok Sabha Speaker, Leader of Opposition, CJI or nominated judge, eminent jurist – **NOT the Rajya Sabha Chairman**.
