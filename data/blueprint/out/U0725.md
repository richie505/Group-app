## U0725 | LAUNCH VEHICLES - SLV-3, ASLV, PSLV, GSLV MK-II, LVM3, SSLV
### 1. SOUNDING ROCKETS AND TERLS THUMBA
- **TERLS** (Thumba Equatorial Rocket Launching Station), near **Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala**; work began 1962.
- Chosen because the **geomagnetic (magnetic) equator** passes through it - suits sounding-rocket experiments.
- First launch: American **Nike-Apache**, **21 Nov 1963** - start of the Indian space programme.
- Early imported rockets: **M-100** (Russia), **Centaure** (France); indigenous rockets from **1965**.
- **Rohini Sounding Rocket (RSR)** programme consolidated in **1975**.
- RH rockets: atmospheric research + testing/qualifying subsystems for larger launch vehicles.
- Number = **diameter in mm** (**RH-200, RH-300, RH-560**); three operational versions: payload **8-100 kg**, apogee **80-475 km**.
- **RH-200**: **200th consecutive successful launch** from Thumba, **Nov 2022**.
!TRAP Thumba = magnetic equator (sounding rockets); Sriharikota = east coast (orbital launches).
### 2. CHRONOLOGY AND FIRST PAYLOADS
- Order: **SLV-3 → ASLV → PSLV → GSLV → LVM3 → SSLV**.
| Vehicle | First flight | First (successful) payload |
|---|---|---|
| SLV-3 | **10 Aug 1979** (E1, failed) | **18 Jul 1980** (E2): **Rohini RS-1** - first indigenous launch success |
| ASLV | **24 Mar 1987** (failed; again 1988) | First success **1992**; SROSS series |
| PSLV | **20 Sep 1993** (D1, failed) | **15 Oct 1994** (D2): **IRS-P2** |
| GSLV | **18 Apr 2001** (D1) | **GSAT-1** (1,536 kg) |
| LVM3 | **18 Dec 2014** (suborbital) | **CARE** (suborbital experiment); 2017 D1: **GSAT-19** |
| SSLV | Aug 2022 (D1, failed) | EOS-02 not placed in orbit; D2 (2023), D3 (2024) succeeded |
- PSLV = third-generation vehicle; GSLV = fourth-generation.
### 3. FULL FORMS, STAGES AND PROPELLANTS
| Vehicle | Full form | Stages |
|---|---|---|
| SLV-3 | Satellite Launch Vehicle-3 | **4, all-solid**; 17 t, 22 m |
| ASLV | Augmented SLV | **5, all-solid** |
| PSLV | **Polar** SLV | **4: solid-liquid-solid-liquid**; 2nd stage **Vikas**; **no cryogenic stage** |
| GSLV Mk-II | **Geosynchronous** SLV | **3: solid - liquid - cryogenic (CE-7.5)** + **4 liquid strap-ons** |
| LVM3 (ex-GSLV Mk-III, "Bahubali") | Launch Vehicle Mark-3 | **2 S200 solid strap-ons + L110 liquid core + C25 cryogenic** (fully indigenous) |
| SSLV | **Small** SLV | **3 solid stages + liquid Velocity Trimming Module (VTM)** for precise orbit insertion |
- PSLV: **first Indian vehicle with liquid stages**; ISRO's "**workhorse**".
- GSLV: one solid motor stage + one **earth-storable liquid** stage + one **cryogenic** stage (APPSC G2 2025).
- GSLV Mk-II has **four liquid strap-ons**, not two solid.
!TRAP PSLV = 4 stages (S-L-S-L); GSLV = 3 (solid-liquid-cryo); SSLV = 3 solid + VTM.

### 5. ORBIT TERMS AND LAUNCH PHYSICS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **LEO** | Low Earth Orbit (up to ~2,000 km) |
| **SSO** | Sun-synchronous, near-polar; passes a place at the same local solar time (same imaging conditions) - PSLV's speciality |
| **GTO** | Geosynchronous Transfer Orbit: elliptical, **apogee near 36,000 km** - GSLV, LVM3 |
| **GEO** | GSO over the equator; fixed in the sky |
| Apogee / perigee | Farthest / nearest point of an orbit |
| Strap-on booster | Extra motors fixed to the core for lift-off thrust |
- **Eastward** launches gain Earth's rotational speed and save fuel; sites **nearer the equator** gain most.
- Sriharikota on the **east coast**: rockets fly over the sea, away from people.
- Polar/SSO launches go **southward**; from Sriharikota a **dog-leg around Sri Lanka** is needed - hence **Kulasekarapattinam** (Tamil Nadu) for SSLV (foundation **27 Aug 2025**).
- All orbital launches, incl. private **Agnibaan**, are from **Sriharikota** (AP); Chandrayaan-1 was launched from AP.
### 6. CRYOGENIC AND SEMI-CRYOGENIC ENGINES
- Early GSLVs used **Russian** cryogenic engines; technology transfer refused in the **1990s**, so India built its own.
- First indigenous cryogenic success: **GSLV-D5 (2014)**.
| Engine / type | Propellant | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| **CE-7.5** (cryogenic) | **LH2 + LOX** | GSLV Mk-II upper stage |
| **CE-20** (cryogenic, ~200 kN) | **LH2 + LOX** | **C25** stage of LVM3 |
| **SCE-200** (semi-cryogenic) | **Refined kerosene + LOX** | LVM3 upgrade, NGLV |
| Vikas (earth-storable) | **UH25 + N2O4** | PSLV 2nd, GSLV 2nd stage |
| Solid | **HTPB** | GSLV 1st stage |
- Engine testing: **IPRC Mahendragiri (Tamil Nadu)**; **LPSC Valiamala** does design, not testing.

### 8. RECORDS AND LANDMARKS
- **100th SHAR launch**: GSLV-F15/NVS-02, **29 Jan 2025** (17th GSLV flight); **105th** PSLV-C62; **107th** GSLV-F17 (19th GSLV flight).
- **Heaviest payload from Indian soil**: BlueBird Block-2, 6,100 kg, LVM3-M6; also the largest commercial communication satellite in LEO (**223 m2** array). LVM3 has flown **6** missions.
- Older record: **INSAT-4CR**, 2,130 kg, GSLV-F04, 2007.
- PSLV setbacks: **PSLV-C61/EOS-09** (18 May 2025) and **PSLV-C62** (64th PSLV flight) both failed at the **third stage**; no ISRO launches in Q1 2026.
- Heavy-lift "**game changer**": LVM3 ended dependence on foreign launchers for **4-tonne** satellites to GTO.
- **49% automatic FDI** in launch vehicles and spaceports (Jul 2026). ISSAR 2025: 315 launches worldwide, 4,651 objects placed in orbit.
### 9. SSLV
- Design goal: **500 kg to 500 km LEO**, low cost, **launch-on-demand** for small satellites.
- **SSLV-D1** (Aug 2022) failed to reach the intended orbit; **D2 (2023)** and **D3 (2024)** succeeded.
- **HAL** won the **Rs 511 crore** Transfer of Technology - **first full ToT** of an ISRO launch vehicle to industry.
!TRAP "SSLV has two solid stages" is WRONG - it has three (APPSC G1 2022).

### 11. REUSABLE AND FUTURE VEHICLES
- **RLV-TD / Pushpak**: autonomous landing tests at **Chitradurga** - **RLV-LEX (2 Apr 2023)**, **LEX-02 (22 Mar 2024)**, **LEX-03 (23 Jun 2024)**; a **landing** experiment, not an engine test.
- **NGLV**: ~**90 m** tall, ~**1,000 t** lift-off mass; needs the **Third Launch Pad** (~4 years); for **Bharatiya Antariksh Station by 2035** and **crewed lunar landing by 2040** (not 2030).
- **HLVM3**: human-rated LVM3 for **Gaganyaan** - crew to **400 km** LEO for **3 days**; first Integrated Air Drop Test **24 Aug 2025**.
- **SOLVE**: sub-orbital test vehicle solid motor ground-tested at SHAR (Jul 2026).
- Foreign reusables: **SpaceX Falcon 9** - first orbital-class booster landing **21 Dec 2015**; **Blue Origin New Shepard** - **suborbital** reusable, landed Nov 2015.
