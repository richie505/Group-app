### 1. STATE SYMBOLS - RESIDUAL AP VS TELANGANA
| Symbol | Residual AP | Telangana (contrast) |
|---|---|---|
| Bird | **Rose-ringed Parakeet** (Rama Chiluka) | **Palapitta** (Indian Roller) |
| Flower | **Jasmine** (Malle Puvvu) | Tangedu |
| Tree | **Neem** (Vepa) | Jammi Chettu |
| Animal | **Blackbuck** (Krishna Jinka) | Jinka (deer) |
- Telangana's animal is the **Jinka**, not the Indian Roller; the **Indian Roller (Palapitta)** is Telangana's bird.
- **Jammi Chettu** is Telangana's tree, not AP's.
!TRAP Red Sanders is endemic to **Seshachalam** but is NOT the State tree. AP State tree = **Neem**.
