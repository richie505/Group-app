### 1. UNESCO PUSH FOR LEPAKSHI AND GANDIKOTA
#### 1.1 The demand
- AP urged the Centre (meeting with the **Union Tourism and Culture Minister**, New Delhi) to back **UNESCO World Heritage** status for **Lepakshi** and **Gandikota**.
- **Lepakshi** (temple architecture, **murals**, sculpture) is **already on the UNESCO Tentative List**; **Gandikota** (**fort and gorge landscape**) is **not yet**.
- Concern also raised over **Amaravati Buddhist sculptures** kept in **London museums**.
!TRAP Lepakshi = already on the Tentative List; Gandikota = not yet.

### 2. INSCRIPTIONS IN NEWS
| Find | Place | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| **Pune copper plates** | **Guntur** | **669 CE**, first regnal year of Eastern Chalukya **Vishnuvardhana II**; name **Kunturu > Gomturu > Gunturu** |
| **Odia inscription** | **Tekkali, Srikakulam** | **Gajapati Kapilendra Deva** ("Pratapa Kapileswara") |
| **3 inscriptions (1554)** | **Sadasivakona, Seshachalam forest** (Tirupati district) | **Sadasiva Deva Raya** (Vijayanagara) |
| **Reddy-era inscription** | **Kondaveedu** (Palnadu district) | Two **Shiva temples** built under **Anavema Reddy** |
#### 2.1 Guntur's antiquity
- Highlighted by **Dr. K. Munirathnam Reddy**, **ASI Director of Epigraphy**.
- **10th-century Ederu copper plates** use the name **"Gomturu"** (in use 10th-14th century).
#### 2.2 Tekkali Odia inscription (Gajapati era)
- Found **under a Tulasi Choura** at **Tekkali royal palace**; script and language **Odia**; line 4 has **"Bijeraije"**.
- Earlier record: **Annual Report on Indian Epigraphy 1926-27**; date likely **15th century** (palaeography), exact year not known; estampage taken.
- **Kapilendra Deva's** kingdom: from **Ganga** (north) to **Kaveri** (south). Tekkali = a former **Odia zamindari of Ganjam**.
#### 2.3 Sadasivakona inscriptions
- Found by the **ASI epigraphy team** led by **K. Munirathnam Reddy** (Director of Epigraphy, **ASI, Mysuru**), after a survey in the **Nallamala Hills**.
- **3 rare inscriptions**, dated **1554**, in **Telugu, Tamil, Kannada**.
- Main record: **Sadasiva Raya**'s visit for a **sacred bath** and **royal donations**; a **Siva temple and a mutt** built at **Papavinasam**; copied as **estampages** (impressions).
- Related: **Gudimallam Siva shrine** - **2nd century BCE**.
- **ASI**: est. **1861**; **DG Yadubir Singh Rawat**. **Vijayanagara Empire**: est. **1336** by **Harihara I and Bukka Raya I**.
#### 2.4 Kondaveedu Reddy inscription
- 14th-century Reddy-period inscription on a **Shiva temple doorway pillar**; on **Rudrapadam (Adam Shafi Hill)**; **Kondaveedu Fort** = 14th-century hill fort.
- **Reddy Kingdom**: est. **1325** by **Prolaya Vema Reddy**; **first capital Addanki** (not Kondaveedu), later Kondaveedu; promoted Telugu culture and literature.
- **Vasantotsavam** = most popular festival under the **Reddi kings**.

### 3. HERITAGE SITES, CAVES AND MUSEUMS
#### 3.1 Kotadibba, Gottiprolu - Monument of National Importance
- **Kotadibba**, **Gottiprolu village**, **Tirupati district**: declared a **Monument of National Importance**; about **24.22 acres** (Survey Nos. 235, 131/1); protected under **AMASR Act, 1958**.
- **ASI excavations (2019)**: **massive brick fortification wall**, large **residential complex**, **Vishnu idol**, pottery.
#### 3.2 Undavalli Caves and the Telugu Mahotsavam
- **Two-day Telugu Mahotsavam** at **Undavalli Caves, Guntur district** (not Krishna) by the **AP State Creativity and Culture Samiti**, to promote Telugu language, literature and arts.
- **First held** (closing event) at Undavalli Caves in **July 2026**; made an **annual event**.
- Undavalli: **4th-5th century AD** rock-cut **sandstone** caves on a hill beside the **Krishna**; Gupta period; linked to **Vishnukundina** kings (**420-620 AD**).
- Largest cave **four storeys**; **2nd floor**: huge **Vishnu in Ananthasayana** posture carved from a **single granite stone**; temples to the **Trimurti**.
- **Originally a Buddhist vihara** - Buddhist sculptures on the first floor.
| Other rock-cut caves | State |
|---|---|
| Ajanta, Ellora, Elephanta | Maharashtra |
| Badami | Karnataka |
| Udayagiri | Madhya Pradesh |
#### 3.3 Kakinada palm-leaf manuscript museum
- **Ministry of Culture** released **₹5 crore** under the **Museum Grant Scheme** for a new building of the **Andhra Sahitya Parishad Government Museum and Research Institute, Kakinada**.
- Total support proposed **₹10 crore**; funding **80:20 (Centre:State)** - not 60:40; **ground-plus-four** building; work from early August.
- About **5,000 palm-leaf manuscripts** (17th-19th centuries): **Ayurveda, mathematics, astrology, Telugu and Sanskrit literature, classical music**.
- **500 manuscripts chemically treated in 2021**; collection handed over by Andhra Sahitya Parishad in the **1970s**.
- Also holds Buddhist pillars, sculptures, megalithic tools, copper and bronze plates, coins.

### 9. WILDLIFE, AGRI-SCIENCE, MINERALS AND INDUSTRY
#### 9.1 Rare pangolin rescued at Kamalapuram (Kadapa)
- **Kamalapuram** municipal area, **Kadapa district**; local name **"alugu"**; released in the **Mydukur range**; likely came via the **Kundu River** basin.
- **8 species**: African - **Black-bellied, White-bellied, Giant Ground, Temminck's Ground**; Asian - **Indian, Chinese, Sunda, Philippine**.
- Pangolins are **mammals**; **Indian Pangolin** range: **India, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh**.
| Species | Status |
|---|---|
| **Indian Pangolin** | IUCN **Endangered** |
| **Chinese Pangolin** | IUCN **Critically Endangered** |
| Both (found in India) | **Schedule I, WPA 1972** |
| All 8 species | **CITES Appendix I** |
#### 9.2 IIOPR-Patanjali Foods MoU
- **ICAR-Indian Institute of Oil Palm Research (IIOPR)**, **Pedavegi** near **Eluru**, **Eluru district**, signed an MoU with **Patanjali Foods** to use its **rapid diagnostic technology** for early detection of **Ganoderma** in oil palm before symptoms appear.
- **Ganoderma** = fungus causing **Basal Stem Rot** of oil palm (not citrus canker or late blight).
#### 9.3 Minerals and industry
- AP holds **92% of India's baryte (BaSO4)** reserves (Ministry of Mines); **Mangampeta** (Tirupati district) - one of the **world's largest** deposits; used as a **weighting agent in drilling fluids**. AP also has mica, calcite, kyanite, granite.
- **Bharat Forge**: **India's first private marine gas turbine facility**, in AP.
