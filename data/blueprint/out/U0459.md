## U0459 | DISTRIBUTIVE JUSTICE AND INCLUSIVE GROWTH
### 1. CONCEPT AND THREE PILLARS
- **Inclusive growth** = **broad-based, shared, pro-poor** growth that raises people's participation; benefits shared by all sections (comprehensive, shared and pro-poor: **all of these**).
- Covers both **pace and pattern** of growth (who benefits), plus gender and regional balance.
- **Three pillars**: **Market Economics, Empowerment, Pragmatism** (APPSC-GS 2025).
- Framing: "Sabka Saath Sabka Vikas aur Sabka Vishwas". OECD/G20 theme: Promoting Strong (Sustainable) and Inclusive Growth.
!TRAP Distractors: "Antyodaya, Empowerment, Poverty and Unemployment"; "Market Economics, Poverty and Literacy". Do not mix with WEF IDI pillars.
### 2. DISTRIBUTIVE JUSTICE – CONSTITUTION
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **38** | Social order with social, economic, political justice; **38(2)** (**44th Amendment, 1978**): minimise inequalities in income, status, facilities, opportunities |
| **39(b)** | Ownership and control of **material resources** distributed to **subserve the common good** |
| **39(c)** | Economic system must not cause **concentration of wealth** and means of production to the common detriment |
| **31C** | **25th Amendment, 1971**: protects laws giving effect to 39(b) and 39(c) |
!TRAP 31C = 25th (1971); 38(2) = 44th (1978).
### 3. BHAGWATI–SEN DEBATE
| | **Jagdish Bhagwati** (with Arvind Panagariya) | **Amartya Sen** (with Jean Dreze) |
|---|---|---|
| Priority | Rapid market-led **growth first**; funds social spending later | **Social infrastructure first** (health, education, nutrition) |
| Book (2013) | **Why Growth Matters** | **An Uncertain Glory** |
- Sen: growth alone does not remove deprivation; for backward countries **primary education and health** get top priority (APPSC-GS 2007). Kerala Model.

### 5. INCLUSIVE GROWTH IN THE PLANS
| Plan | Theme |
|---|---|
| **11th (2007–12)** | "**Towards Faster and More Inclusive Growth**" – **first** to make inclusive growth the objective; target 9% (10% by end), achieved ~8%; investment 32% → 37% |
| **12th (2012–17)** | "**Faster, Sustainable and More Inclusive Growth**" – sustainable focus; **last** Five Year Plan; reduce poverty, regional equality, SC/ST/OBC living conditions |
- 11th Plan components: agricultural growth, employment and poverty reduction, reducing regional disparities, equitable growth – not sole dependence on urban services.
### 6. INSTRUMENTS
- Requirements: infrastructure (widens access), **agricultural revival** (rural people), education (capabilities), health, jobs, **credit**, SHGs (financial participation), MSMEs (employment, entrepreneurship), **RTE**. SHGs, MSMEs, RTE Act aid inclusive growth – **all three**.
- **DAY-NRLM**: **10.05 crore** women in **90.09 lakh** SHGs; Lakhpati Didi target 3 crore.
- **MSMEs**: **31.1%** of GDP, **35.4%** of manufacturing output, **48.58%** of exports.
- **VB-G RAM G** replaced MGNREGA: **100 → 125 days**, from 1 Jul 2026; AP got ₹2,545.50 crore (Jul 2026).
- **Aspirational Districts** (Jan 2018, 49 KPIs, 5 themes); **Aspirational Blocks** (Jan 2023, 500).
- **Inclusive governance**: effective District Planning Committees, more public-health spending, equal access to government facilities – **not** NBFCs doing banking.
!TRAP Inclusive growth is NOT expected from high national-income growth alone; target segments = marginal farmers, landless labourers, SC/ST – not semi-urban persons (UPPSC 2016). Helps: education reform, vocational training; not urban-only focus or social-spending cuts.
#### 6.1 AP Instruments
- **AP Rural Inclusive Growth Project**: five components.
- **Navaratnalu** (2019–24) included Amma Vodi and YSR Rythu Bharosa; successors under **Super Six**: **Talliki Vandanam** (₹15,000 per school child), **Annadata Sukhibhava** (₹20,000 per farmer), **Deepam-2** (3 free LPG cylinders), **Stree Shakti** free bus for women (15 Aug 2025), Aadabidda Nidhi (₹1,500 a month), youth unemployment support (₹3,000 a month).
### 7. MEASURING INCLUSION
- Growth measures: GDP, industrial output, employment. Inclusion: **HDI, poverty rates, literacy, access to basic services**, gender equality.
- World Bank: extreme poverty ($3/day) **27.1% (2011-12) → 5.3% (2022-23)**; $4.20 line 57.7% → **23.9%**. World Inequality Database: inequality **rising**.
- **WEF Inclusive Development Index** pillars: growth and development; inclusion; intergenerational equity and sustainability. **IDI 2018**: India **62nd of 74** emerging economies (China 26th, Pakistan 47th).
