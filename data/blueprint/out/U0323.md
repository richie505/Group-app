## U0323 | CENTRE-STATE ADMINISTRATIVE RELATIONS – ARTICLES 256-263, ALL INDIA SERVICES, DIRECTIONS TO STATES
### 1. ADMINISTRATIVE RELATIONS – ARTICLES AT A GLANCE
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **256** | State executive power must ensure **compliance with Parliament's laws**; Union may **direct** |
| **257** | State executive must **not impede** the Union; directions on **means of communication** of national or military importance and **protection of railways** |
| **258 / 258A** | Entrustment of functions (both ways) |
| **261** | **Full faith and credit** to public acts, records, judicial proceedings |
| **262** | Inter-State **water disputes** |
| **263** | **Inter-State Council** |
| **365** | Non-compliance with Union directions → President may hold the State **cannot be carried on** per the Constitution → **Art. 356** (PYQ 2017, 2018) |
- During a **National Emergency**, the Union can direct States on **any matter**.
!TRAP Art. 257 directions cover communications and railways, NOT public order.
### 2. DELEGATION OF FUNCTIONS – ARTICLES 258 AND 258A
| Article | Who entrusts | To whom | Consent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **258(1)** | **President** | State govt or its officers | **With State consent** |
| **258(2)** | **Parliament by law** | State govt or its officers | **Without consent** |
| **258A** (**7th Amendment, 1956**) | **Governor** | Union govt or its officers | **With Centre's consent** (APPSC-GS 2023) |
- A 258(1) notification is a legislative act, specific or general; purpose: avoid **rigidity and deadlock**.
!TRAP 258(1) = President, not Parliament. 258A = State entrusts to the Union.
### 3. ALL INDIA SERVICES – ARTICLE 312
#### 3.1 Creation and Existing Services
- **Art. 312:** Parliament may create a new AIS after a **Rajya Sabha** resolution by **2/3 of members present and voting**; it expressly mentions an **All India Judicial Service**.
- Existing AIS: **IAS, IPS, IFoS** (**Indian Forest Service, 1966**); governed by the **All India Services Act, 1951**.
- **ICS → IAS** in 1947; **Sardar Patel** called AIS the "**Steel Frame**" of India.
- **UPSC** replaced the **Federal PSC** in **1950**.
- Council of States' exclusive powers: create a new AIS (**Art. 312**); authorise State List laws (**Art. 249**).
#### 3.2 Recruitment and Control
- Recruited by **UPSC** on an all-India basis; **trained by the Centre**; serve in **State cadres**.
- **Appointment** by the **President**, on UPSC recommendation.
- **Dismissal/removal only by the Union Government** (PYQ 2017, 2019; APPSC-G1 2019).
#### 3.3 Federal Critique
- AIS seen as against the **federal principle** and **State autonomy** – Central rules govern them, States lack full control (APPSC-G2 2025: A and R both true, R explains A).
- **IAS (Cadre) Rules, 1954** amendments: **central deputation** without the consent of the **State or the officer**; Centre's order final after a set time; hurts State bureaucracy's morale.
#### 3.4 Classify the Service
| Service | Category | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| **IAS, IPS, IFoS (Forest)** | **All India Services** | Common to Union and States (PYQ 2007) |
| **IFS (Foreign)** | **Central Service** | Union subjects only |
| **IRS (Revenue)** | **Central Service** | Union subjects only |
| SPSC-recruited services | State Services | State subjects |
!PAIR IFoS | Indian Forest Service | All India || IFS | Indian Foreign Service | Central
### 4. ARMED AND CENTRAL FORCES IN STATES
- **Art. 355:** Union's duty to protect States; it may deploy armed/central forces **with or without State consent**.
- Basis: **Union Entry 2A** (**42nd Amendment**).
- States object: **public order** and **police** are **State Entries 1 and 2**.
- **CAPF dispute (Mar 2026):** Centre has not implemented the **2025 SC order** (Organised Group A status for CAPF officers, less IPS deputation); a **Central Armed Forces (General Administration) Bill** is expected.
### 5. PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSIONS AND TRIBUNALS
- **Art. 315:** UPSC; an SPSC for each State; a **JSPSC** for 2+ States if their **legislatures so resolve**.
- **JSPSC** – created by an **Act of Parliament**; members appointed by the **President**.
- **Art. 323A** (**42nd Amendment, 1976**): administrative tribunals for Union and States, individually or jointly; **CAT** (1985 Act) – **New Delhi + 18 benches**, **69 members** (35 judicial + 34 administrative); CAT conference Sep 2025.
!TRAP JSPSC members are appointed by the President, not the Governors.

### 7. INTER-STATE COUNCIL – ARTICLE 263
- Art. 263 is an **enabling** provision: the **President** establishes the Council by order.
- Set up on **28 May 1990** by **Presidential order**, on the **Sarkaria Commission's** recommendation.
| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Chairman | **Prime Minister** |
| Members | **CMs of all States**, UT Administrators/CMs, **six Union Cabinet Ministers** nominated by the PM |
| Decision method | **Consensus**; recommendations **not binding** |
| Standing Committee | Chaired by the **Union Home Minister** |
| Secretariat | Inter-State Council Secretariat (also serves Zonal Councils) |
- Bifurcation disputes: ISC + **Apex Council** under the **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014** suggested.
### 8. ZONAL COUNCILS AND NORTH-EASTERN COUNCIL
#### 8.1 Zonal Councils
- **Statutory** bodies under **Part III** of the **States Reorganisation Act, 1956** (passed Nov 1956; **14 States, 6 UTs**) – not constitutional.
- **5 councils:** Northern, Central, Eastern, Western, Southern; proposed by **Nehru** (1956).
- **Chairman:** **Union Home Minister** (all five); **Vice-chairman:** CMs by **annual rotation**.
- Members: **CM + two other ministers** of each State; Administrators/LGs of UTs.
- Advisers (**no vote**): NITI Aayog representative, **Chief Secretaries**, Development Commissioners.
- **Deliberative and advisory**; advice **not binding**.
- **Southern Zonal Council:** **Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala, Telangana, Puducherry** (not Goa).
- **31st Southern Zonal Council: 20 Aug 2026**, near **Mamallapuram**; chaired by **Amit Shah**; vice-chairman TN CM **C. Joseph Vijay**.
#### 8.2 North-Eastern Council
- Separate **NEC Act, 1971**; **Sikkim added 2002**; chairman since **2018**: **Union Home Minister** ex-officio.
!KEY Zonal Councils = statutory (SRA 1956); NEC = separate Act (1971).
### 9. COOPERATION DEVICES AND NATURE OF BODIES
| Type | Bodies |
|---|---|
| **Constitutional** | **Inter-State Council (263)**, **Finance Commission (280)**, water-dispute provision (**262**), inter-State trade authority (**307**) |
| **Statutory** | **Zonal Councils** (SRA 1956), **NEC** (1971), **water tribunals** (ISRWD Act 1956), JSPSC |
| **Extra-constitutional** | **NDC**, **Planning Commission**, Central Council of Health, Central Council for Local Government, **Governors' Conference (President)**, **CMs' Conference (PM)**, Chief Secretaries' Conference |
- **Art. 261:** full faith and credit to public acts, records and judicial proceedings of the Union and every State (APPSC-GS 2023); **261(3):** final civil judgments executable anywhere in India.
- **Integrated judiciary:** HC judges appointed by the President consulting the CJI and Governor; a common HC for 2+ States possible.
- **Grants:** **Art. 275** – ST welfare and Scheduled Areas; **Art. 282** – discretionary grants.
!TRAP Governors' Conference is chaired by the President; CMs' Conference by the PM.
### 10. INTER-STATE WATER DISPUTES – ARTICLE 262
- **Art. 262:** Parliament provides by law for adjudication of inter-State river disputes → **Inter-State River Water Disputes Act, 1956**.
| Tribunal | Constituted | AP angle |
|---|---|---|
| **Godavari** | **1969** (award 1980) | Maharashtra, AP, Karnataka, MP, Odisha |
| **Krishna-I** (**R.S. Bachawat**) | **1969** | Maharashtra, AP, Karnataka |
| **Krishna-II** | **2004** (report 30 Dec 2010) | AP a party |
| **Vansadhara** | **24 Feb 2010** | **AP and Odisha** |
- **Mullaperiyar** dam (1895) is on the **Periyar** (not Godavari), in **Kerala**, operated by **Tamil Nadu**; SC Supervisory Committee involves the **National Dam Safety Authority** (Dam Safety Act, 2021).
- **Mahanadi:** Odisha vs Chhattisgarh back in focus (Aug 2026).
