## U0501 | MINIMUM SUPPORT PRICE – MECHANISM, CACP, CROPS, PROCUREMENT, CRITICISM; PM-AASHA
### 1. MSP – CONCEPT AND AUTHORITY
- **MSP** = government-announced **minimum (floor) price** for selected crops, declared **before** each kharif/rabi sowing.
- Purpose: protect farmers against a **sharp fall** in prices and **distress sales**; if market price < MSP, agencies buy the stock offered; builds **PDS** buffer stocks (food security).
- **CACP recommends**; **Government of India – CCEA approves/fixes**.
- Legal status: **administrative decision**, not a legal right; no statute.
- Marketing policy tools: **PDS, MSP, buffer stock**.
!TRAP An old APPSC G2 2017 key says CACP "sets" MSP; in fact CACP only recommends and CCEA decides.
### 2. CACP
- **Agricultural Prices Commission (APC)**: **January 1965** → renamed **CACP in 1985**.
- **Attached office** of the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare; **not statutory**.
- Composition: Chairman + Member Secretary + 1 official + 2 non-official members.
- Main objective: **incentivise farmers** → modern technology → productivity → production. Also stable prices, fair farm income, consumer interest (not maximum prices).
- **Factors**: cost of production, demand and supply, domestic and **international prices**, **inter-crop price parity**, terms of trade agriculture vs non-agriculture, minimum 50% margin over cost, effect on consumers.
- Also recommends **FRP for sugarcane**.
### 3. COST CONCEPTS
| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A2** | Actual paid-out costs (seeds, fertilisers, hired labour, fuel, irrigation) |
| **FL** | Imputed **family labour** |
| **A2+FL** | Paid-out cost + family labour (leaves out own-land rent, interest on fixed capital) |
| **C2** | A2 + FL + **land rent** + **interest on fixed capital** (fullest cost) |
- Present policy: MSP at least **1.5 × (A2+FL)** = 50% margin over A2+FL, **not C2**.
- **Swaminathan Commission (NCF, 2004-06)**: **C2 + 50%**; SKM says paddy at C2+50% should be **₹3,243/q** (2026-27) vs ₹2,441 fixed.
### 4. CROPS COVERED
- **22 mandated crops** + **sugarcane FRP** = 23. Split: **14 kharif + 6 rabi + 2 commercial** (jute, copra).
| Group | No. | Crops |
|---|---|---|
| Cereals | 7 | Paddy, wheat, barley, jowar, maize, ragi, bajra |
| Pulses | 5 | Gram, tur, moong, urad, lentil |
| Oilseeds | 7 | Groundnut, rapeseed-mustard, soybean, sunflower, sesamum, safflower, nigerseed |
| Commercial | 3 | Copra, cotton, raw jute |
- **Not covered**: fruits, vegetables, onion, potato, most spices, **areca nut, coffee, turmeric** (under MIS instead).
- **Niger** (Guizotia abyssinica): MSP, **kharif**, tribal cooking oil – all correct (UPSC 2023); oil content 37-47%.
- **Cotton**: MSP for medium and long staple; nodal agency **CCI** (Ministry of Textiles).
- Tobacco, cotton, sugarcane, oilseeds = **cash crops**; rice, millets, pulses = food crops.

### 6. SUGARCANE PRICING
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **FRP** | Minimum mills must pay; **CCEA on CACP advice**; linked to **recovery rate** |
| **SMP** | Replaced by FRP from **2009-10** (Order amended 22.10.2009) |
| **SAP** | Fixed by **States** (UP, Punjab, Haryana), usually above FRP; not by CACP |
- Legal basis: **Sugarcane (Control) Order, 1966** under Essential Commodities Act, 1955. FRP hike ₹10/q for 2026-27.
### 7. PROCUREMENT
| Crop | Procurement |
|---|---|
| **Wheat, paddy** | **FCI** + State agencies – effectively **open-ended** |
| Coarse grains | **State agencies** with prior GoI approval |
| Pulses, oilseeds, copra | Limited PSS, cap **25%** of production |
| Cotton | **CCI** |
- **Decentralised Procurement (1997)**: States procure, store, distribute; Centre repays economic cost minus CIP.
- Wheat procurement RMS 2026-27 above **35 MT**; Punjab 12.1 MT.
- Rice price factors: MSP, government trading, stockpiling, consumer subsidies.
- **AP**: State agencies procure paddy for FCI's Central Pool; **DCMS** societies buy at MSP; **APMARKFED** apex cooperative; DBT within 48 hours.
### 8. PM-AASHA AND PRICE SUPPORT BEYOND MSP
- **Pradhan Mantri Annadata Aay SanraksHan Abhiyan**, **September 2018**, Ministry of Agriculture; aim: MSP reaches farmers incl. small and marginal (farmer welfare).
- Outlay **₹35,000 crore** to 2025-26 (APPSC 2023).
| Component | Nature |
|---|---|
| **PSS** | **Physical procurement** of pulses, oilseeds, copra by **NAFED, NCCF**; registered farmers; 25% cap |
| **PDPS** | Pays **MSP minus market price**; no buying; mainly oilseeds |
| **PPSS** | 2018 pilot; private stockists bought oilseeds at MSP |
| **MIS** | Perishables not under MSP; trigger **≥10%** price fall or production rise; loss **50:50 (75:25 NE)** |
- 2026 components: PSS, PDPS, MIS. PSS 2024-25: 61.87 LMT, >₹39,000 crore, 26.56 lakh farmers.
- **Pulses mission**: 100% MSP buying of **tur, urad, masoor** for **4 years**.
- **Price Stabilisation Fund (2014-15)**: buffers of **onion, potato, pulses**.
- **Kerala**: first State with floor prices for **16 vegetables (Nov 2020)**.
- AP maize sold at ₹1,400-1,600 vs ₹2,400 MSP (May 2026).
### 9. PHASES OF AGRICULTURAL DEVELOPMENT AND POLICY SINCE 1991
| Phase | Hallmark |
|---|---|
| Pre-Green Revolution (1950-65) | Land reforms, community development, irrigation |
| Green Revolution (mid-1960s) | HYV wheat, rice; **APC and FCI 1965**; "Wheat Revolution" stamp July 1968 |
| Price-support phase | MSP, FCI buffer stocks, PDS at issue price |
| Post-1991 | Liberalisation, **WTO 1995**, e-NAM, FPOs, PM-KISAN |
- **WTO Agreement on Agriculture** (1995): pillars – market access, domestic support, export subsidies. **Amber box** (trade-distorting, e.g. MSP price support) capped by **de minimis 10%** of production value for developing countries (5% developed); **green box** (research, infrastructure) unlimited; **blue box** production-limiting payments.
- **Peace clause (Bali, 2013)** protects public stockholding for food security.
- **Bt cotton** approved **2002** (GM crop).
### 10. CRITICISM AND THE LEGAL-GUARANTEE DEBATE
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Crop bias** | Procurement mostly paddy, wheat |
| **Regional bias** | Punjab, Haryana, Telangana, AP, Chhattisgarh; **eastern and NE** farmers gain little |
| **Shanta Kumar HLC (2015)** | Only **~6%** of farmers sell at MSP |
- Legal MSP: farmer unions want binding procurement; economists fear trade distortion, **monoculture**, WTO problems – prefer **PDPS-type** payments.
- SC notice on plea for MSP ≥ weighted average cost (14 Apr 2026). **ES 2025-26**: use procurement savings for crop diversification.
