## U0568 | AGRICULTURAL MARKETING IN ANDHRA PRADESH – MARKET YARDS, RYTHU BAZAARS AND E-MARKETING
### 1. WHY REGULATED MARKETING
- Farmers selling alone face a **bargaining imbalance** with traders: forced sales, false weights, unrecorded prices, middlemen margins.
- AP answers this with three layers: **regulated market yards** (by law), **Rythu Bazars** (direct sale scheme) and **e-marketing** (e-NAM), backed by storage, pledge loans and price intervention.
- Current relevance: AP agriculture minister sought central help for procurement, storage and cold chain (22 Jul 2026).
### 2. AP AGRICULTURAL PRODUCE AND LIVESTOCK MARKETS ACT
#### 2.1 Acts and rules
| Act / rule | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **AP (Agricultural Produce and Livestock) Markets Act** | **1966** | **Act No. 16 of 1966**; covers **crops, livestock and livestock products** |
| AP (AP&L) Markets Rules | **1969** | Made under **Section 33** of the Act; govern **licensing** (issue, fees, turnover, period) |
| Three central farm laws | 2020 | **Repealed November 2021** → 1966 AMC framework remains operative |
| Model APMC Act | — | **Single market fee**, **registration in place of licensing**, farmers' and consumers' markets |
#### 2.2 Mechanisms of the 1966 Act
- **Open auction**, **standard weights**, **recorded prices**, **licensed buyers**.
- The Act **allows** licensed buyers; it does not end them.
!TRAP Act **1966** (No. 16); Rules **1969** under **Section 33**. Not 1969 Act.
### 3. AGRICULTURAL MARKET COMMITTEES AND MARKET YARDS
#### 3.1 How a regulated market is set up
1. Government declares a **notified area**.
2. A **Market Committee** is constituted.
3. The Market Committee establishes the **notified market** (market yard).
- Supervision at district level: **District Agricultural Trade and Marketing Officer (DATMO)**.
- **Adoni** market yard (Kurnool district) = **second largest** market yard in AP.
#### 3.2 Market fee
- Market fee in regulated yards: **1%**.
| Market fee collected | Amount |
|---|---|
| 2024-25 (to Dec 2024) | **Rs 433.39 crore** |
| 2025-26 (to Dec 2025) | **Rs 429.77 crore** |
### 4. RYTHU BAZAARS
#### 4.1 Key facts
- Concept: **farmer sells directly to the consumer**, no middlemen.
- Started by AP government in **1999**; functioning from **January 1999**.
- Run day-to-day by an **Estate Officer**; under the **Revenue Department**.
- **Outside the purview** of Agricultural Market Committees.
- District coordinating officer: **Joint Collector** (not the District Collector).
- Pricing: prices fixed at about **25%** relative to open-market prices – about 25% **above wholesale** and 25% **below local retail**.
- Number: **124** functioning (2024-25); **129** functioning (2025-26).
- Role in price intervention: the State's **quickest market-intervention channel**; government procures **perishables** through them to steady prices.
- **Ryot Bazar** scheme first came in **Punjab**; AP was the **second** State.
!TRAP APPSC 2025 key: "District Collector is the coordinating officer" = **wrong**; the Joint Collector coordinates, the Estate Officer manages.
#### 4.2 Market yard vs Rythu Bazar
| Feature | Regulated market yard | Rythu Bazar |
|---|---|---|
| Basis | AP (AP&L) Markets Act, 1966 | **Executive scheme, 1999** |
| Managed by | Agricultural Market Committee | **Estate Officer** |
| Department | Agricultural Marketing | **Revenue** |
| Seller | Farmers sell to **licensed traders** | Farmer sells **directly** to consumer |
| Fee | Market fee **1%** | No middleman margin |
!PAIR Market yard | 1966 Act | AMC + licensed traders || Rythu Bazar | 1999 scheme | Estate Officer + Revenue Dept
### 5. E-NAM AND E-MARKETING
- **e-NAM** (electronic National Agriculture Market): 1,656 mandis in 23 States and 4 UTs; **Andhra Pradesh: 33 mandis** (Mar 2026; 22 in Dec 2019).
- Each integrated AP mandi received free software and **Rs 30 lakh** assistance.
- **39 FPOs** integrated with e-NAM in AP.
- Assessment: e-NAM mainly helped **price discovery**; not yet fully working for **payments** or **inter-mandi trade**.
### 6. MARKET INTERVENTION AND PRICE STABILISATION
- **Price Stabilisation Fund** (Market Intervention Fund): **Rs 3,000 crore** corpus in the **2019-20** budget for intervention in **agricultural and horticultural crops**, to secure a just price.
- 2019: groundnut (peanut) farmers of seven districts compensated from the fund.
- Rythu Bazars act as the quickest outlet for procured perishables.
### 7. STORAGE, WAREHOUSING AND POST-HARVEST INFRASTRUCTURE
#### 7.1 APSWC
- **AP State Warehousing Corporation**: set up **August 1958** under the Warehousing Corporations Act; share capital **50:50** between **GoAP and CWC**.
#### 7.2 Market committee godowns
| Indicator | 2024-25 | 2025-26 |
|---|---|---|
| Godowns (total) | **1,052**; 9,65,492 MT | 1,052; **9.65 lakh MT** |
| Operational | 626; 5,65,508 MT | **643**; **5,92,163 MT** |
- Main after-harvest gap: **limited operational storage** and need for more **cold storage**.
- **AMI** (national): 49,796 warehouses sanctioned; 982.94 lakh MT capacity (2026).
#### 7.3 Storage and pledge schemes
| Scheme | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)** | Farm-gate infrastructure at **Rythu Bharosa Kendras**; turns PACS into multipurpose facility centres |
| **Raitu Bandhu** (AMC pledge loan) | Farmer **pledges produce** in market-yard godowns and borrows on it; avoids forced sales |
### 8. FPOS AND COLLECTIVE MARKETING
- Central scheme: **10,000 FPOs**; budget **Rs 6,860 crore**.
- FPOs pool produce to bargain collectively and sell through e-NAM; **39 FPOs** on e-NAM in AP.
#### 8.1 Which channel solves which problem
| Problem of the farmer | Channel / scheme |
|---|---|
| Middlemen margin on vegetables | **Rythu Bazar** (direct sale) |
| False weights, unrecorded prices | **Regulated market yard** (1966 Act: open auction, standard weights) |
| Forced sale right after harvest | **Raitu Bandhu** pledge loan; godowns |
| Price crash in a crop | **Price Stabilisation Fund**; procurement through Rythu Bazars |
| Small, scattered lots | **FPOs** (collective marketing) |
| Limited buyers in one mandi | **e-NAM** (online price discovery) |
| Weak farm-gate storage | **AIF** at Rythu Bharosa Kendras; APSWC warehouses |
!KEY AP angle: Rythu Bazars (1999) are a repeat PYQ topic – always check Estate Officer vs Joint Collector vs District Collector.
