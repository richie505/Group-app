## U0280 | UNION AND ITS TERRITORY – ARTICLES 1-4, NEW STATES AND CESSION
### 1. ARTICLES 1–4 – THE UNION AND ITS TERRITORY
#### 1.1 What Each Article Says
| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| **1** | "**India, that is Bharat, shall be a Union of States**"; States and their territories listed in the **First Schedule** |
| **2** | Admission or establishment of **new States** (from outside India) |
| **3** | Formation of new States; alteration of **areas, boundaries or names** of existing States |
| **4** | Laws under Arts. 2 and 3 amend the First and Fourth Schedules; **not deemed an amendment under Article 368** |
- India is a **"Union"**: not the result of an agreement among States; **no State has a right to secede**.
- "**An indestructible Union of destructible States**".
- A **new State** created → the **First Schedule must be amended** (it lists States and UTs).
#### 1.2 Union of India vs Territory of India
| Union of India | Territory of India (Art. 1(3)) |
|---|---|
| **States only** – the members of the federal system | **States + Union Territories + territories that may be acquired** |
| Narrower concept | Wider concept |
- As a sovereign State, India can **acquire foreign territory** by **cession, treaty or conquest** – e.g. **Puducherry, Goa** (acquired territories).
- Acquired territory becomes part of the Territory of India; its people become citizens by **incorporation of territory**.
!TRAP 'Union of India' does not include UTs; 'Territory of India' does.
!KEY Part I (Arts. 1–4) = Union and its Territory; First Schedule = names and territories of States and UTs.
### 2. ARTICLE 3 PROCEDURE AND ARTICLE 4
#### 2.1 Steps Under Article 3
1. Bill introduced in Parliament only on the **recommendation of the President**.
2. President **refers the Bill to the State legislature concerned** to express its views within a specified period.
3. Parliament is **not bound** by those views.
4. Passed by a **simple majority** in the ordinary legislative process.
#### 2.2 Article 4
- A law under Article 2 or 3 is **not an amendment for the purposes of Article 368** – hence simple majority.
- **Renaming Kerala → 'Keralam'**: Union Cabinet approved; **Article 3 procedure** started.
#### 2.3 What Parliament Can Do Under Article 3
- Form a new State by **separating territory** from a State, **uniting** two or more States or parts, or uniting territory with a part of a State.
- **Increase or diminish** the area of a State; alter its **boundaries** or **name**.
- **Cannot** cede Indian territory to a foreign country (Berubari, 1960) – that needs an **Art. 368** amendment.
!TRAP Article 3 laws need only a simple majority – not the special majority of Article 368.

### 4. LINGUISTIC REORGANISATION – ANDHRA TO 1956
#### 4.1 Andhra State Act, 1953
- **Telugu districts** separated from **Madras State**.
- **Andhra State (1953)** = **first linguistic State**, formed on **1 October 1953** after **Potti Sriramulu's** death (**15 December 1952**).
#### 4.2 States Reorganisation Act, 1956
- Came into force **1 November 1956**; created **14 States + 6 UTs**.
- **Andhra State + Telangana region of Hyderabad = Andhra Pradesh**.
- **Kerala** formed; **Zonal Councils** set up.
- UTs replaced the **Part C and Part D States** – **7th Amendment, 1956**.
### 5. NEW STATES – AMENDMENT AND CHRONOLOGY
#### 5.1 State → Year → Instrument
| Year | State | Act / Amendment |

| **1953** | **Andhra State** | Andhra State Act – first linguistic State |

| **1966** | **Haryana** (from Punjab) | Punjab Reorganisation Act |
| **1971** | **Himachal Pradesh** | — |
| **1972** | **Manipur, Tripura, Meghalaya** | Meghalaya first **autonomous** within Assam (**22nd Amendment, 1969**) |
| **1975** | **Sikkim – 22nd State** | **35th (1974)** associate State; **36th (1975)** full State |
| **1987** | **Mizoram** (**20 Feb 1987**) | **State of Mizoram Act, 1986** + **53rd Amendment** |
| **1987** | **Arunachal Pradesh** | **55th Amendment** |
| **1987** | **Goa** (**30 May 1987**) | **56th Amendment** (Art. 371I) |
| **2014** | **Telangana** | AP Reorganisation Act – **newest State** |
!NUM Order: Andhra (1953) → Nagaland (1963) → Haryana (1966) → Sikkim (1975) → Goa (1987) → Telangana (2014).
#### 5.2 UT Entries by Amendment
- **Goa, Daman and Diu** → **12th Amendment, 1962**.
- **Pondicherry** → **14th Amendment, 1962**.
#### 5.3 Sikkim and Arunachal Facts
- Sikkim: **associate State 1974** (35th fixed the terms of association); **full State 1975** after a **referendum**; **monarchy abolished**; **36th** made it the **22nd State**.
- Sikkim Assembly: **not less than 32 members** (36th Amendment).
- Arunachal Pradesh: capital **Itanagar**, named after **Ita Fort ('fort of bricks')**; two National Parks – **Namdapha, Mouling**.
- **Goa** is a **State**, not a UT.
- Sikkim: **Government of Sikkim Act, 1974** set up its first elected government; the **35th Amendment** inserted **Art. 2A** and the **Tenth Schedule** (terms of association); the **36th** omitted them.
#### 5.4 Pattern of State Formation
| Basis | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Language** | Andhra State (1953), SRA 1956 States, Haryana (1966) |
| **Tribal / ethnic identity** | Nagaland (1963), Meghalaya (1972), Mizoram (1987) |
| **UT upgraded to State** | Himachal Pradesh (1971), Manipur, Tripura (1972), Arunachal (1987), Goa (1987) |
| **Merger of a protectorate** | Sikkim (1975) |
| **Regional / development demand** | Telangana (2014) |
### 6. J&K REORGANISATION ACT, 2019
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Presidential order under Art. 370 | **5 August 2019** |
| Act took effect | **31 October 2019** (**Sardar Patel's birth anniversary**) |
| UTs created | **J&K (with legislature)** and **Ladakh (without legislature)** |
| Special status | **First State downgraded to a UT** |
| First LG of UT of J&K | **Girish Chandra Murmu** |
| 2nd LG of J&K | **Manoj Sinha** (7 August 2020) |
| First LG of UT of Ladakh | **Radha Krishna Mathur** (assumed charge 31 October) |
- J&K and Ladakh have **separate LGs**.
- **Sep 2026**: MHA proposed a new **Chapter 'K' under Article 371** for Ladakh (Art. 371 now has chapters A–J covering 12 States).
- Demand to **restore J&K statehood** continues.
!TRAP The J&K Act took effect on 31 Oct **2019**, not 2020. "29 States and 7 UTs" was valid only before Aug 2019.
### 7. UNION TERRITORIES – NUMBER AND ADMINISTRATION
#### 7.1 Current Count (2026)
- **28 States and 8 UTs** (APPSC 2021 key).
- 8 UTs: **Andaman and Nicobar, Chandigarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, J&K, NCT of Delhi, Ladakh, Lakshadweep, Puducherry**.
- **DNH + Daman and Diu** merged, effective **26 Jan 2020**.
- **Dadra and Nagar Haveli** was under **Portuguese** rule (not French) until **1954**.
#### 7.2 Administration
- **Art. 239**: President administers each UT through an **administrator**.
- **Art. 239(2)**: Governor of an **adjoining State** may be administrator – e.g. **Punjab Governor for Chandigarh**.
- **Delhi**: a UT (First Schedule); **National Capital Region**; special status by the **69th Amendment, 1991**.

### 8. CESSION OF TERRITORY
#### 8.1 Cases and Agreements
| Instance | Year | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Berubari reference** | **1960** | Ceding Berubari (West Bengal) to Pakistan under the **Nehru–Noon Agreement (1958)**; SC: **Art. 3 does not cover cession** → needs Art. 368 amendment → **9th Amendment, 1960** |
| **SC position** | **1969** | **Boundary settlement ≠ cession**; done by **executive action**, no amendment |
| **India–Bangladesh LBA** | **1974** (2011 protocol) | **111 Indian enclaves to Bangladesh, 51 to India**; **100th Amendment, 2015**; amended First Schedule entries of **Assam, West Bengal, Meghalaya, Tripura** |
| **Katchatheevu** | **1974–76** | To **Sri Lanka** by agreements, **no constitutional amendment**; challenge pending in SC since **2012** |
!TRAP Cession = Art. 368 amendment (9th, 100th); boundary dispute = executive action, no amendment.
### 9. ANDHRA PRADESH REORGANISATION ACT, 2014
#### 9.1 Basis and Key Provisions
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Constitutional basis | **Articles 3 and 4** |
| Appointed day | **2 June 2014** – Telangana formed |
| Common capital | **Hyderabad** for **10 years**; ended **2 June 2024** |
| **Section 8** | **Governor's special responsibility** for security of residents of the common capital |
| **Section 95** | Existing **admission quotas** in higher, technical and medical education continue |
| **Ninth Schedule** of the Act | **Companies and corporations** – assets and liabilities apportioned (Sec. 53) |
| **Tenth Schedule** of the Act | **Institutions** (about 50 in Hyderabad); host State to keep serving the other State (Sec. 75) |
- **AP journey**: Andhra State **1953** → Andhra Pradesh **1956** → bifurcation **2014** → **Amaravati made sole capital (2026)** by amending **Sec. 5** of the 2014 Act.
