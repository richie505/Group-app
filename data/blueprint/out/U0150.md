## U0150 | SIKHISM - THE TEN GURUS, GURU GRANTH SAHIB, THE KHALSA; BANDA BAHADUR AND RANJIT SINGH
### 1. FOUNDER AND BASIC TERMS
- **Guru Nanak** (**1469-1539**) founded Sikhism; born **1469 at Talwandi (Nankana Sahib)** near the **Ravi**, Khatri family; died **22 September 1539**.
- Teachings: **Nirguna** devotion, **Ek Onkar** (God is one), **Naam Simran**, **Sangat**; rejected rituals of both religions.
- Chose **Bhai Lehna (Guru Angad)** as successor before death (UPPSC 2023).
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sikh** | Disciple (Sanskrit **shishya**) |
| **Khalsa** | "Pure" |
| **Panj Pyare** | Five volunteers baptised with sacred nectar |
| **Anand Karaj** | Sikh marriage: four circumambulations of the Granth while **Lavan** hymns are sung |
| **Dal Khalsa** | Joint force of the Sikh misls |
| **Adalat-i-Ala** | Ranjit Singh's high court at Lahore |
#### 1.1 Kabir (often paired with Nanak)
- Disciple of **Ramananda** (not Guru Nanak); raised by weavers Niru and Nima; followers **Kabirpanthis**; work **Bijak**.
- One God by many names (Ram, Rahim, Allah, Hari); condemned rituals of both faiths; Hindu-Muslim unity.
### 2. THE TEN GURUS IN ORDER
| No. | Guru | Contribution / event |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Nanak Dev** | Founder; Ik Onkar; appointed Angad |
| 2 | **Angad Dev** | Started and standardised **Gurmukhi** script |
| 3 | **Amar Das** | **22 Manjis** (Manji system); strengthened **langar** |
| 4 | **Ram Das** | Founded **Ramdaspur (Amritsar)**; **500 bighas** of land from **Akbar**; dug the holy tank |
| 5 | **Arjan Dev** (1581-1606) | Compiled **Adi Granth (1604)**; built **Harmandir Sahib**; **first martyr Guru** |
| 6 | **Hargobind** | **Militarised** the Panth; released from **Gwalior Fort (1619)** with **52 prisoners** (**Bandi Chhor Divas**) |
| 7 | **Har Rai** | Birth marked by Gurpurab in Magh |
| 8 | **Har Krishan** | Child Guru |
| 9 | **Tegh Bahadur** | Born **Tyag Mal** in Amritsar; founded **Anandpur Sahib (Chak-Nanki)**; **116 hymns**; **"Hind-di-Chadar"**; executed by **Aurangzeb**, Delhi, **1675** |
| 10 | **Gobind Singh** | Born **1666 at Patna**; **Khalsa 1699**; Five Ks; finalised Guru Granth Sahib; died **1708** |
- After Gobind Singh, Guruship passed to the scripture: **Guru Granth Sahib = eternal Guru**.
### 3. GURU GRANTH SAHIB
- **Compiled** as the Adi Granth by **Guru Arjan (5th)**; **finalised** by **Guru Gobind Singh (10th)**; script **Gurmukhi**.
- APPSC 2019: the Sikh scripture = **Adigranth**.
- Contains teachings of **six Gurus** and non-Sikh saints: **Kabir**, Sufi **Sheikh (Baba) Farid**, **Namdev**, **Ravidas/Raidas** (**41 shabdas**), also **Jaydev**.
!TRAP Compiled by Arjan (5th); finalised by Gobind Singh (10th); 116 hymns = Tegh Bahadur; 41 shabdas = Ravidas.
### 4. THE KHALSA
- Founded by **Guru Gobind Singh** on **Baisakhi, 13 April 1699**, at **Takht Keshgarh Sahib, Anandpur**; Panj Pyare baptised with sacred nectar.
- Made Sikhs a military order; surnames **Singh / Kaur**.
- **Five Ks**: **Kesh** (hair), **Kangha** (comb), **Kara/Kada** (bangle), **Kachera/Kachha** (undergarment), **Kirpan** (sword).
!TRAP Kirtan is NOT one of the Five Ks.
### 5. GURUS AND MUGHAL EMPERORS
| Guru | Emperor | Conflict |
|---|---|---|
| Ram Das | Akbar | Friendly - land grant |
| **Arjan Dev** | **Jahangir** | Executed **1606**, charged with supporting rebel **Prince Khusrau** |
| Hargobind | Jahangir | Held in Gwalior Fort, freed 1619 |
| **Tegh Bahadur** | **Aurangzeb** | Executed at Delhi, **1675** |
| **Gobind Singh** | **Aurangzeb**, then **Bahadur Shah** | Khalsa fought Mughals and hill rulers; **Zafarnama** letter accusing the Mughals of betrayal |
- 350th martyrdom year of Guru Tegh Bahadur observed (Jan 2026).

### 7. BANDA BAHADUR
- Original name **Lachhman Dev**; name given by Guru Gobind Singh, who commissioned him at **Nanded (1708)**.
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1709 | Seized **Samana** |
| 1710 | Defeated **Wazir Khan** at **Chappar Chiri**; conquered **Sirhind** → **first sovereign Sikh state**, capital **Lohgarh** |
| 1715 | Eight-month siege of **Gurdas Nangal**; captured |
| 1716 | Executed at **Delhi** by **Farrukhsiyar** for refusing to convert |
- **Coins** minted in the names of **Guru Nanak and Guru Gobind Singh**.
- **Sirhind Fateh Diwas**: 12-14 May.
### 8. SIKH MISLS
- After 1708, Khalsa self-rule between the **Sutlej and Jamuna**.
- After Banda, the Khalsa formed **12 misls**: e.g. **Bhangi, Ahluwalia, Ramgarhia, Kanhaiya, Sukerchakia, Phulkian**; joint force = **Dal Khalsa**.
- **Ranjit Singh** (Sukerchakia) united them into the kingdom of Lahore.
### 9. MAHARAJA RANJIT SINGH
- Born **13 Nov 1780, Gujranwala**; father **Maha Singh**, chief of the **Sukerchakia misl**, died when he was 12; council of regency 1792-1797. Called **"Lion of Punjab"**.
- **Zaman Shah** invaded Punjab 1798; Ranjit Singh returned his cannons that fell in the Jhelum.
- **1799**: captured **Lahore**, made it capital (APPSC 2018).
- **Treaty of Amritsar, 25 April 1809**, with **Lord Minto** (JPSC 2023): **Sutlej** the boundary; stopped his expansion south.
- **1813**: got the **Kohinoor** from Afghan ruler **Shah Shuja** (Nadir Shah had taken it from Delhi in **1739**).
- Secular Punjabi state; "God intended me to look upon all religions with one eye..."; high court **Adalat-i-Ala**.
- Died **1839** → chaos. Successors: **Kharak Singh** (1839), Nau Nihal Singh, Sher Singh, **Duleep (Dalip) Singh** - minor, regent **Rani Jindan**; last king of the Sikh empire (**1843-1849**); died in **Paris, 22 October 1893**.

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- First martyr Guru: Arjan Dev. Last human Guru: Gobind Singh.
- First sovereign Sikh state: Banda Bahadur (Sirhind 1710, capital Lohgarh).
- Last Sikh ruler: Duleep Singh.
