### 2. ARTICLE 17 AND UNTOUCHABILITY
#### 2.1 Provision
- "Untouchability" is **abolished**; its practice **in any form** is forbidden; enforcing any disability from it is an **offence**.
- In the **Right to Equality** group; a "statement of principle" and basis of SC/ST rights.
- **Art. 35(a)(ii):** **only Parliament** prescribes punishment for Art. 17 offences.
- **Horizontal:** binds **private persons** too (**PUDR v Union of India, 1982**).
- **Ambedkar** ensured untouchability became a **punishable offence**.
- **Sabarimala reference (Apr 2026):** Justice **B.V. Nagarathna** – Art. 17 cannot apply "for three days a month".
#### 2.2 Definition and Forms
- **Not defined** in the Constitution or any Act. **Mysore HC:** the practice as historically developed; excludes social boycott of a few individuals.
- Forms: **temple-entry denial**, separate **wells**, **two-tumbler system**, residential segregation, forced "polluting" work. Seat reservation is protective, **not** untouchability.
- **Structural violence (Johan Galtung):** harm built into social structures. UPPSC 2019 A-R: untouchability is the worst structural violence (A), practised in the name of religious sanction (R) – both true, R explains A.
- **Manual scavenging:** "the **worst surviving symbol** of untouchability".
- "Scheduled Castes": first used by the **Simon Commission**; given shape in the **GoI Act 1935**; first list **1936**; first post-1950 list: **Constitution (SC) Order, 1950**.
### 3. LAWS ENFORCING ARTICLE 17
| Law | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Untouchability (Offences) Act 1955** | **First** law enforcing Art. 17; in force **1 June 1955**; renamed **Protection of Civil Rights Act** in **1976** |
| **PCR Act** | Punishes denial of temple entry and public facilities, **justifying** untouchability, insult "on the ground of untouchability" (s.7(1)(d)) |
| **SC/ST (PoA) Act 1989** | In force **30 January 1990**; **Rules 1995**: **special courts**, State and district **vigilance and monitoring committees**, **60-day** investigation |
| AP Devadasis (Prohibition of Dedication) Act 1989 | Rehabilitation of **Jogin** women |
| **Manual Scavengers Act 2013** | In force **6 Dec 2013** |
- Conviction for untouchability offences → **disqualification under Sec. 8, RPA 1951**.
- PCR and PoA overseen by the Dept of Social Justice. (NHRC is under the Protection of Human Rights Act.)
### 4. MEASURES AND MANUAL SCAVENGING
#### 4.1 Measures
- Untouchability is abolished through **laws, education and public awareness** – not by providing jobs (UPPSC 1992).
- Economic: **NSFDC** (BPL SCs), **NSKFDC** (sanitation workers), **PM-DAKSH** (skills), **VCF-SC**.
#### 4.2 Manual Scavenging
- **1993 Act:** banned employment of manual scavengers and dry latrines.
- **Rashtriya Garima Abhiyan (2001):** National Campaign for Dignity – end manual scavenging.
- **2013 Act:** bans hazardous cleaning of sewers/septic tanks by **anyone, incl. private contractors**; manual cleaning of insanitary latrines; mandates **mechanisation** and rehabilitation; **does not legalise dry latrines**.
- **SRMS (2007**, revised 2013, 2021) **subsumed in NAMASTE** (MoSJE + MoHUA; NSKFDC implements; goal **zero sanitation deaths**).
- Status (Aug 2026): **90,915** sewer/septic workers + 1.3 lakh waste pickers profiled; **58,000** manual scavengers identified, only 2,652 projects approved.
### 5. SC/ST LISTS – ARTICLES 341 AND 342
- **Art. 341:** **President** specifies SCs for a State/UT **after consulting the Governor** by public notification; later changes **only by Parliament** by law.
- **States have no power:** SC (July 2024) quashed **Bihar's 2015** notification moving **Tanti-Tantwa** from EBC to SC.
- **Art. 342:** ST lists; **ST status is religion-neutral**.
- **Chinthada Anand v State of AP (2026):** a **Christian convert** cannot claim SC status; reconversion needs proof of original SC status, genuine reconversion and community acceptance.
- Count: **1,241** caste groups notified as SCs (Census 2011).
!TRAP Adding castes is **Art. 341**, not 344 (344 = Official Language Commission).

### 8. SUB-CLASSIFICATION OF SCs – AP ANGLE
- **Madiga Dandora (MRPS):** started **1994** by **Manda Krishna Madiga** at **Eedumudi (Prakasam)**, not Karamchedu; alleged **Mala dominance** of the **15%** SC quota.
- **Justice P. Ramachandra Raju Commission (1996):** **A – Relli**, **B – Madiga**, **C – Mala**, **D – Adi-Andhra**.
- **AP SC (Rationalisation of Reservations) Act 2000:** AP **first to sub-categorise by statute** (Punjab 1975 by circular); fell in Chinnaiah.
- **Usha Mehra Commission (2007-08):** amend Art. 341 to let States sub-categorise.
- **Telangana:** **first to implement** after Davinder Singh (**3 groups, April 2025**).

### 9. NCSC AND NCST
- Original **Art. 338**: a **Special Officer** (Commissioner for SCs and STs) reporting to the President.
- **1978**: non-statutory Commission; **1990**: constitutional joint commission; **89th (2003)**: split – **NCSC (338)** + **NCST (338A)**; NCST from **19 Feb 2004**.
- Composition: **Chairperson + Vice-Chairperson + 3 members**; NCST Chair **Cabinet rank**, Vice-Chair **MoS**, members **Secretary** rank; President fixes tenure.
- Civil-court powers; report to the **President**.
- **Art. 338(9):** Union and States must **consult NCSC** on major policy; State SC Commissions are created by **State law**.
- **AP State Commission for SCs and STs:** 2016, by amending Act 9 of 2003; chairman K.S. Jawahar.

### 11. CASTE ATROCITIES AND DATA
#### 11.1 Causes and Incidents
- **NCSC/ST (1990) causes:** land disputes/alienation, bonded labour, indebtedness, **non-payment of minimum wages** – **not religious causes**.
- Other factors: caste hierarchy, dominance, rising Dalit activism, political interference.
- **Social boycott:** NCRB **2017** new head – "forced to leave residence / social boycott" (UP 57 cases).
| Incident | Year | State |
|---|---|---|
| **Karamchedu** | **1985** | **AP** (Prakasam, now Bapatla) – Madiga Dalits killed; began independent Dalit movement |
| **Tsundur** | **1991** | **AP** (Guntur) |
| **Khairlanji** | 2006 | **Maharashtra** (Bhandara) |
| **Hathras** | 2020 | **UP** – monitoring by **Allahabad HC** |
- Chronology: Poona Pact (1932) → Yeola (1935) → Karamchedu (1985) → Tsundur (1991).
#### 11.2 NCRB and Census
- Crimes against SCs 2023: **57,789** (+0.4%); top: **UP (15,130)**, **Rajasthan**, **MP**, Bihar.
- SCs in India: **20.14 crore = 16.6%** (Census 2011); highest share **Punjab**.
- **AP:** **84.69 lakh = 17.08%**; SC literacy 64.47%.
