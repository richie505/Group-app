## U0413 | CHILD WELFARE – ICDS, POSHAN ABHIYAAN, PM POSHAN, CHILD MARRIAGE, MALNUTRITION
### 1. ICDS – INTEGRATED CHILD DEVELOPMENT SERVICES
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Launch | **2 October 1975** |
| Universalised | **2006** |
| Ministry | **Ministry of Women and Child Development (MWCD)** |
| Nature | Centrally sponsored; implemented by States/UTs |
| Current form | **Anganwadi Services** under **Saksham Anganwadi and Poshan 2.0** |
| Beneficiaries | Children **6 months–6 years**, **pregnant women**, **lactating mothers**; adolescent girls via Scheme for Adolescent Girls |
#### 1.1 The Six Services
| Service | Delivered through |
|---|---|
| Supplementary Nutrition (SNP) | Anganwadi Centre |
| Pre-school non-formal education | Anganwadi Centre |
| Nutrition and health education | Anganwadi Centre |
| Immunisation | Health system (NHM) |
| Health check-up | Health system |
| Referral services | Health system |
- **NOT ICDS services**: cash maternity benefit (**PMMVY**, now under Mission Shakti); school meals (**PM POSHAN**).
#### 1.2 Functionaries
| Functionary | Role |
|---|---|
| **Anganwadi Worker (AWW)** | Grassroots, **honorary** ICDS functionary; runs the Anganwadi Centre; nutrition, health, pre-school for under-6 and mothers |
| Anganwadi Helper | Cooks and serves meals; brings children to the centre |
| CDPO | Block-level ICDS officer; trained by **NIPCCD** (National Institute of Public Cooperation and Child Development) |
- **ANM and ASHA belong to NHM**, not ICDS.
- **ICDS at 50 (2025)**: **1.4 million** AWCs; over 100 million served; 101 million on Poshan Tracker.
- **AP**: **55,746 AWCs in 257 ICDS projects** (AP SES 2025-26).
!TRAP G2 Mains 2025: Anganwadi Worker is a functionary of **ICDS** – not ANM/NHM.
### 2. SAKSHAM ANGANWADI AND POSHAN 2.0
- Started **2021**; MWCD; CSS; period 2021-22 to 2025-26.
- **Merged**: Anganwadi Services (SNP), **POSHAN Abhiyaan**, **Scheme for Adolescent Girls** (girls **14–18** in Aspirational Districts and NER). PM POSHAN is **not** merged.
| Category | Centre : State |
|---|---|
| General States | **60:40** |
| NE, Himalayan States, J&K | **90:10** |
| UTs without legislature | **100%** Centre |
- **Three verticals**: nutrition support (incl. adolescent girls); **ECCE (3–6 years)**; Anganwadi infrastructure.
- **2 lakh** AWCs to be upgraded as Saksham Anganwadis at **40,000 a year** (Dec 2025).
- **Co-location** guidelines (3 Sep 2025): over **2.9 lakh** AWCs in school premises.
- Millets at least once a week in supplementary nutrition.
#### 2.1 One Nation One Anganwadi
- **Migrant families** moving to another State keep ICDS benefits for children below 6 and pregnant/lactating women (**APPSC 2023**).
- Services on demand; **child Aadhaar not mandatory**.
### 3. POSHAN ABHIYAAN AND NUTRITION CAMPAIGNS
#### 3.1 POSHAN Abhiyaan
- **Prime Minister's Overarching Scheme for Holistic Nourishment**; earlier **National Nutrition Mission**; flagship of 2018.
- Launched **8 March 2018** at **Jhunjhunu, Rajasthan**.
- Goal: **Suposhit Bharat**; a **Jan Andolan**.
- Targets: reduce **stunting**, undernutrition, **anaemia**, **low birth weight** (and underweight); stunting to **25% by 2022**.
- Objectives: malnutrition awareness among pregnant women and lactating mothers; reduce anaemia in young children, adolescent girls and women.
!PAIR POSHAN Abhiyaan | Jhunjhunu | 2018 || BBBP | Panipat | 2015
#### 3.2 Campaigns and Tools
| Campaign / tool | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Rashtriya Poshan Maah** | **September** (month); **9th** edition 2026 at **Varanasi**, theme "Hamari Anganwadi Hamari Jimmedari"; marks **10 years of PMMVY** |
| **Poshan Pakhwada** | **Fortnight**, March–April; **8th** edition 2026 (April) on **brain development in the first six years**; AP launch at Gajuwaka, Visakhapatnam (14 Apr 2026) |
| **Poshan Tracker** | Real-time digital monitoring of Anganwadi services and growth data |

### 4. EARLY CHILDHOOD CARE AND EDUCATION
- **Article 45**: ECCE for children up to **6**.
- **National ECCE Policy**: **2013**.
- **NEP 2020 foundational stage**: ages **3–8** = 3 years Anganwadi/pre-school + **Classes 1–2**.
- **Bal Vatika** brought under PM POSHAN.
- **AP**: **1,030** Hub Anganwadi Centres with ITC and Pratham; **55,607** AWWs trained under Poshan Bhi Padhai Bhi.
### 5. MID-DAY MEAL TO PM POSHAN
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Started | **15 August 1995** as **NP-NSPE** (National Programme of Nutritional Support to Primary Education) (APPSC 2025) |
| First State | **Tamil Nadu** |
| Renamed | **PM POSHAN** (Pradhan Mantri Poshan Shakti Nirman), **2021** |
| Nature | **Rights-based** CSS under **NFSA 2013** |
| Ministry | **Ministry of Education** (Dept of School Education and Literacy) |
| Tenure / outlay | 2021-22 to 2025-26; ₹1,30,794.90 crore |
| Sharing | **60:40** |
| Coverage | **Bal Vatika + Classes I–VIII**, government and aided schools; one **hot cooked meal** |
| Reach | Over **11.8 crore** children (29 Jun 2026) |
#### 5.1 Nutrition Norms
| Norm | Primary | Upper primary |
|---|---|---|
| Energy + protein | **450 kcal + 12 g** | **700 kcal + 20 g** |
| Cooking cost per child per day | ₹5.45 | ₹8.17 |
#### 5.2 Aims and Features
- Aims: better nutrition; tackle **classroom hunger**; raise **enrolment, attendance, retention** and concentration of disadvantaged children.
- **Tithi Bhojan**: community meals on special days – first by **Gujarat**.
- School nutrition gardens; **mandatory social audit** in every district; extra nutrition in aspirational and high-anaemia districts; FPO/SHG participation.
- **Akshaya Patra Foundation** (Bengaluru, 2000, Madhu Pandit Dasa): **largest NGO** mid-day meal provider.
- **AP – Dokka Seethamma Mid-Day Meal**: extended to **Intermediate (Classes 11–12)** in 2024-25; 5 eggs + 3 chikkis + ragi java a week.
!KEY PM POSHAN = **Ministry of Education**; ICDS / Poshan 2.0 = **MWCD**. Mid-day meal first = Tamil Nadu; Tithi Bhojan first = Gujarat.

### 7. MATERNAL HEALTH SCHEMES

|---|---|---|
| **Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)** | **2005**, under NRHM; **MoHFW**; 100% centrally sponsored | Cash for **institutional delivery** (₹1,400 rural, ₹1,000 urban; ₹500 home delivery for BPL) |

### 8. GIRL CHILD SCHEMES
#### 8.1 Beti Bachao Beti Padhao
- Launched **22 January 2015** at **Panipat, Haryana**.
- **MWCD** nodal, with **Health** and **Education**; **100% central**; all districts; now under **Sambal** (Mission Shakti).
- Aim: reverse falling **Child Sex Ratio**; girl child's survival and education. SRB **918 → 929**.
- Design: **no DBT**, no asset creation; behaviour-change campaign; district funds linked to SRB.
#### 8.2 Other Girl Child Schemes
| Scheme | Year / ministry | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana** | 2015; **Ministry of Finance** | Up to **two girls**; ₹250 to ₹1.5 lakh a year for 15 years; matures at **21**; 8.2% (Q2 FY 2026-27); 4.53 crore+ accounts |
| **Balika Samriddhi Yojana** | 1997 | Cash grant at birth + scholarships, BPL girls |
| **Dhanalakshmi** | 2008 | Pilot conditional cash transfer: birth registration, immunisation, schooling |
### 9. CHILD MARRIAGE
- **Prohibition of Child Marriage Act, 2006**: minimum age **18 (girls) / 21 (boys)**.
- Adult male contracting, conducting or promoting: **RI up to 2 years** + fine up to **₹1 lakh**.
- Child marriage is **voidable** at the option of the party who was a minor.
- **Child Marriage Prohibition Officers** appointed by **State Governments**.
- **PCM (Amendment) Bill, 2021**: women's age to **21**; sent to Standing Committee; **lapsed** with the 17th Lok Sabha. **Jaya Jaitly Task Force (2020)** recommended it.
- **Allahabad HC (1 July 2026)**: PCMA age limits apply to all citizens **irrespective of religion**.
- **NFHS-5**: **23.3%** of women aged 20–24 married before 18; highest **West Bengal**.
| Initiative | Fact |
|---|---|
| **ALIVA** | Data-driven model to end child marriage in **Nayagarh district, Odisha** (APPSC G2 2025) |
| **100-Day Bal Vivah Mukt Bharat** | MWCD, **December 2025** |
### 10. CHILD PROTECTION SCHEMES
- High-risk families → child labour, dropout, delinquency, malnutrition, crime; measures: ICDS, Mission Vatsalya, POSHAN Abhiyaan, National Family Benefit Scheme.
#### 10.1 Mission Vatsalya
- **2021-22**; MWCD; CSS; **subsumed ICPS** (Child Protection Services); objective: child protection and happy childhood aligned to **SDGs** (APPSC G2 2024).
- **₹900 crore** in the 2021-22 Union Budget; outlay **₹10,916 crore** (Centre ₹6,928 crore).
| Component | Norm |
|---|---|
| Statutory bodies | **JJB and CWC in every district** |
| Sponsorship / foster care | **₹4,000** per child per month |
| CCI maintenance | **₹3,000** per month; capacity 50 (25 in NE/hilly) |
| Aftercare | **18–21** (extendable to 23), ₹4,000 per month |
| Helpline | **CHILDLINE 1098** with **ERSS-112** in 36 States/UTs |
- JJ Amendment Act 2021: **District Magistrate** focal point for adoption.
#### 10.2 PM CARES for Children and Palna
- **PM CARES for Children (2021)**: children who lost both parents, surviving parent, legal guardian or adoptive parents to **COVID-19**; corpus **₹10 lakh** at **23**; health insurance + education.
- **Palna (National Creche Scheme)**: **6 months–6 years**; open **whether or not mother is employed**; **17,000** Anganwadi-cum-Creches.
