## U0382 | KINSHIP – DESCENT, LINEAGE, CLAN, KINSHIP TERMINOLOGY; NORTH-SOUTH INDIAN KINSHIP CONTRAST
### 1. KINSHIP – MEANING AND TYPES
- **Kinship**: a system of social relationships based on **blood and marriage**.
- **Radcliffe-Brown**: kinship = a system of dynamic relations regulated by social usage.
- **Marriage**: a socially approved union; regulates sex, legitimises children, creates kinship ties.
| Type | Basis | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Consanguineal** | blood | parent-child, **uncle-nephew** |
| **Affinal** | marriage | husband-wife, **son-in-law and father-in-law**, brothers-in-law |
| **Fictive / ritual** | assumed, not biological | godparenthood, dharam bhai/behen, rakhi ties |
!TRAP A father-son tie by birth is consanguineal, not fictive.
### 2. DEGREES OF KINSHIP
| Degree | Meaning | Number |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | directly related: parents, siblings, spouse | **8** |
| **Secondary** | primary kin of primary kin (father's father, wife's brother) | **33** |
| **Tertiary** | primary kin of secondary kin (father's brother's wife) | **151** |
!NUM 8 – 33 – 151 = primary – secondary – tertiary.
#### 2.1 Family Scenario (APPSC 2025)
- Ravindra-Seema's children: Krishna, Madhavi. Madhavi-Harish's daughter: Jyothi.
- **Krishna–Jyothi** (maternal uncle–niece) = **consanguineous**.
- Ravindra–Seema, Madhavi–Harish, Krishna–Harish = **affinal**.
### 3. DESCENT RULES
| Rule | Meaning | Indian example |
|---|---|---|
| **Patrilineal** | through the father | most of North India; **Kondhs, Santhals, Bhils** |
| **Matrilineal** | through the mother | **Khasi, Garo, Nair** |
| **Bilateral / cognatic** | both sides equal | – |
| **Double (bilineal)** | different attributes through each parent (movable via mother, immovable via father) | – |
| **Parallel** | women → daughters, men → sons | – |
| **Cross** | mother → son, father → daughter | – |
!TRAP Parallel = same-sex line; cross = opposite-sex line.
### 4. DESCENT GROUPS
| Group | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Lineage** | unilineal group from a **known** ancestor; exogamous |
| **Clan** | believes in a common ancestor; link **not demonstrated** |
| **Gotra** | **exogamous Hindu clan**; common male ancestor; same-gotra bar from the **Later Vedic** period |
| **Phratry** | two or more related clans |
| **Moiety** | society divided into **two halves** |
- **Sapinda**: kin within the prohibited degrees of blood relationship for marriage.
- Order (small to large): lineage → clan → phratry → moiety.
### 5. RESIDENCE AND FAMILY TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Patrilocal | wife lives with the husband's family |
| Matrilocal | husband lives with the wife's family |
| **Avunculocal** | residence with the **maternal uncle** |
| Nuclear / Joint family | parents and children / several generations together |
| Patriarchy | male-dominated system |
- **Endogamy** is a rule about the group within which one marries, not a form by number of spouses.
### 6. KINSHIP TERMINOLOGY
| Basis | Types |
|---|---|
| Meaning | **Classificatory** (one word for many kin: "uncle") vs **denotative / isolative / descriptive** (one kin: "father") |
| Structure | elementary, derivative, descriptive |
| Use | terms of **address** vs terms of **reference** |
- **L.H. Morgan** proposed the classificatory-descriptive distinction: *Systems of Consanguinity and Affinity* (**1871**).
- **Kapadia** linked kinship terms to marriage and inheritance rules.
### 7. KINSHIP USAGES
| Usage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Avoidance** | restraint, e.g. daughter-in-law and father-in-law |
| **Joking relationship** | licensed teasing, e.g. a man and his wife's younger sister |
| **Teknonymy** | addressing through a third person, usually a child ("father of my child") |
| **Avunculate** | special authority of the **maternal uncle**; matrilineal societies |
| **Amitate** | special role of the **father's sister**; patrilineal societies |
| **Couvade** | husband imitates his wife's pregnancy taboos (**Khasi, Toda**) |
!PAIR Avunculate | Maternal uncle | Matrilineal || Amitate | Father's sister | Patrilineal
### 8. NORTH-SOUTH KINSHIP CONTRAST
#### 8.1 Iravati Karve
- *Kinship Organization in India* (**1953**): **4 zones – North, Central, South, East**.
- "**Mother of Indian Kinship Studies**"; first to map Indian kinship systematically and highlight North-South differences.
#### 8.2 Contrast Table
| North | South |
|---|---|
| **gotra and village exogamy**; sasan/sapinda bars | **cross-cousin marriage** preferred; parallel cousins barred |
| brides go to higher-status families; bride's side "inferior" | marriage with **elder sister's daughter**, FZD or MBD; symmetry |
| terms by generation; blood vs affinal kin distinct | terms by older/younger |
| bride **breaks** with natal family | **continuity** with natal family |
#### 8.3 Dumont and Others
- **Dumont** on the South: **immediate exchange**; kin clustered in a narrow area; **no sharp consanguine-affine line**; more freedom for women.
- **Pauline Kolenda**: *Regional Variations in Hindu Kinship* (**1968**), built on Karve.
- **AP angle**: cross-cousin and uncle-niece marriage common in Andhra Pradesh – Telugu **menarikam**.
### 9. MATRILINEAL AND RITUAL KINSHIP
- Matrilineal communities: **Nairs** (Kerala), **Khasis** and **Garos** (Meghalaya).
- In matrilineal societies the **maternal uncle** holds authority (avunculate).
- **Ritual kinship**: godparenthood, ritual siblinghood (dharam bhai/behen, rakhi); fictive, not biological.
