### 2. DALIT MOVEMENT – PRECURSORS
- The two **Adi-Andhra** bodies (1919, 1936) are the precursors of the Dalit movement.
- Adi-Andhra leaders were inspired by **Phule's Satyashodhak Samaj (1873)** and **Periyar's Self-Respect Movement**.
- Current relevance: **200th birth anniversary of Jyotirao Phule** commemorated on **11 April 2026**.
### 3. DALIT FLASHPOINTS – KARAMCHEDU AND CHUNDURU
| Event | Date | District |
|---|---|---|
| **Karamchedu massacre** | **17 July 1985** | **Prakasam** (now Bapatla district) |
| **Chunduru massacre** | **6 August 1991** | **Guntur** |
- After Karamchedu, Dalits **broke away from the Congress and Communist parties** and formed **independent Dalit Mahasabhas**.
!PAIR Karamchedu | 1985 | Prakasam || Chunduru | 1991 | Guntur
### 4. DALIT MAHASABHA AND MADIGA DANDORA
#### 4.1 AP Dalit Mahasabha
- Founded **1985**, after Karamchedu; founder **Katti Padmarao**; **Bojja Tarakam** also recognised as a founder.
- Leaders of modern Dalit assertion: Katti Padmarao, **Gaddar**, **Manda Krishna Madiga**, Jupudi Prabhakar Rao, Endluri Sudhakar, **Boyi Bhimanna**.
#### 4.2 Madiga Reservation Porata Samiti (MRPS)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Founded | **7 July 1994** |
| Place | **Eedumudi** village, **Prakasam** district |
| Founder-president | **Manda Krishna Madiga** |
| Popular name | **Madiga Dandora** |
| Demand | **Sub-categorisation of SC reservation** so that Madigas get a fair share |
- Manda Krishna Madiga received the **Padma Shri (2025)**.
- After Mandal, OBC and Dalit movements split over **sub-categorisation**.
### 5. BACKWARD CLASS COMMISSIONS
#### 5.1 National Commissions
| Commission | Set up | Chairman | Key point |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First Backward Classes Commission** | **January 1953** (Art. 340) | **Kaka Kalelkar** | Reported **1955**; little practical effect |
| **Second (Mandal) Commission** | **1 January 1979** | **B.P. Mandal** | Report **31 Dec 1980**; **27%** OBC reservation; implemented **7 Aug 1990** (V.P. Singh) |
#### 5.2 AP Commissions
| Commission | Year | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| **Anantharaman Commission** | **1968–70** | **First AP BC Commission**; grouped BCs into **A–D** |
| **Muralidhar Rao Commission** | Took charge **27 Jan 1982** | Examined whether **minorities** were in the BC list; combined BC, ST and religious-group populations as **29.08%**; led to a big rise in the State's reservation quota |
!TRAP FIRST AP BC Commission = **Anantharaman**, not Muralidhar Rao. First national = **Kaka Kalelkar**, not Mandal.

### 7. LEFT LAND-OCCUPATION CAMPAIGNS – BHU PORATAM
- **Bhu Poratam** ("land struggle"): joint **CPI–CPI(M)** campaign, launched **2007** (not 1997).
- Aim: **occupy and distribute government and surplus land** to the landless.
- Continues the Communist tradition of **tenancy and land campaigns of 1956–69**.
### 8. ANTI-ARRACK MOVEMENT
#### 8.1 Origin and Course
- Began **1992**, **Dubagunta** village, **Nellore** district; led by **rural women**; leader **Vardhineni (Dubagunta) Rosamma**.
- Grew out of **"Aksharajyothi" adult-literacy classes**: lessons led women to discuss men's **arrack drinking, debt, domestic violence** and men missing work.
- **Methods:** women **closed arrack shops** and **stopped arrack auctions** with **broomsticks and chilli powder**. Legislative elections were **NOT** a method.
- Demand: stop the **sale and auction of arrack**.
- One of India's **most successful women-led movements**.
#### 8.2 Prohibition Timeline
| Step | Date |
|---|---|
| Arrack prohibited in AP | **1 October 1993** |
| Total prohibition | **1995** |
| Prohibition lifted | **1997** |
- The 1995 prohibition was later largely abandoned.
#### 8.3 1930s Precursor
- **Kallur Subbarao** led **anti-liquor and Swadeshi movements** in the **Dattamandalas (Rayalaseema)**.
