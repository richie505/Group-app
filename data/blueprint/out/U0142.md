## U0142 | PESHWAS AND THE MARATHA CONFEDERACY; THIRD BATTLE OF PANIPAT (1761); THREE ANGLO-MARATHA WARS
- **Exams:** Group 1 Prelims / Group 2 Screening | **MED**
- **Rows:** A-119; S1-59 Peshwas, the Maratha confederacy and the Third Battle of Panipat
- **PYQ importance:** APPSC 4 questions | other exams 23
### 1. RISE OF THE PESHWA AS VIRTUAL RULER
- **Balaji Vishwanath** made Peshwa by **Shahu, November 1713** – power began moving from the **Chhatrapati at Satara** to the **Peshwa at Poona** (Peshwas' seat, APPSC 2018).
- Shahu owed his throne to Balaji Vishwanath; the office became **hereditary** (Balaji Baji Rao made Peshwa after Baji Rao I's death, 1740); all expansion was led by the Peshwas.
- **Sangola agreement (1750)**, after Shahu's death (1749): Chhatrapati only nominal; Peshwa the real head. From **Balaji Baji Rao's** time the king became a nobody; by 1750 the kingdom was a **confederacy**.
- **Raja Shahu**, grandson of Shivaji – first Maratha chief to offer **nazr** at the dargah of Khwaja Muinuddin Chishti, Ajmer.
### 2. PESHWAS IN ORDER
| Peshwa | Tenure | Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| **Balaji Vishwanath** | 1713-20 | Restored order after Sambhaji; **1719 Mughal grant** (swarajya + chauth, sardeshmukhi of the Deccan) via the Sayyids |
| **Baji Rao I** (his elder son) | 1720-40 | **Malwa, Bundelkhand**; raided **Delhi (1737)**; **Palkhed (1728)**; organised the confederacy |
| **Balaji Baji Rao (Nana Saheb)** | 1740-61 | **Zenith** (Lahore, Peshawar); took **Gujarat** (APPSC 2025); lost Third Panipat; died 1761 |
| **Madhav Rao I** | 1761-72 | **Revival**: beat the Nizam, **Haidar Ali** of Mysore, Rohillas, Rajputs, Jats; died 1772 |
| **Narayan Rao** | 1772-73 | **Murdered** in a struggle with uncle **Raghunath Rao** |
| Raghunath Rao (Raghoba) | 1773-74 | Ousted by the **Barabhai**; sought British help |
| **Madhav Rao II** | 1774-95 | Infant; ruled through **Nana Phadnavis**; confirmed at Salbai; Kharda 1795 |
| **Baji Rao II** | 1796-1818 | **Treaty of Bassein (1802)**; **last Peshwa** |
- Order (UPPSC 2025): Balaji Vishwanath → Baji Rao I → Balaji Baji Rao → Madhav Rao I. UPPSC 2024: Balaji Vishwanath → Balaji Baji Rao → Narayan Rao → Raghunath Rao.
#### 2.1 Baji Rao I
- Most charismatic Maratha leader after Shivaji; ideology **Hindu Pad Padshahi**; "**strike the trunk, the branches will fall**" (strike the Mughal centre).
- Formed the **confederacy** to keep factions together and satisfy older chiefs under **Senapati Dabhade**.
!TRAP **Nana Saheb = Balaji Baji Rao**, not Balaji Vishwanath. **Raghoba** was Madhav Rao I's **uncle**, not brother.
### 3. MARATHA CONFEDERACY – HOUSES AND SEATS
| House | Seat | Founder |
|---|---|---|
| **Peshwa** | **Poona** | Balaji Vishwanath |
| **Bhonsle** | **Nagpur** | Raghuji Bhonsle |
| **Gaekwad** | **Baroda** | Pilaji Gaekwad |
| **Holkar** | **Indore** | **Malhar Rao Holkar** |
| **Scindia (Shinde)** | **Gwalior** | **Ranoji Scindia** |
- **Main weakness**: rivalries and clashing regional interests of semi-independent chiefs.
- Kolhapur – Tarabai's line; Raigad – Shivaji's capital.
- **Pindaris**: irregular looting horsemen with the Maratha armies.
#### 3.1 Administration
- **Saranjam**: land assignments to chiefs for military service – base of the confederacy.
- **Peshwa Daftar** (Pune): state records (diaries, orders, sanads), mostly in **Modi script**; preserved by **Elphinstone** after 1818.
### 4. MADHAV RAO I, NANA PHADNAVIS AND MAHADJI SCINDIA
- **Nana Phadnavis**: "**Maratha Machiavelli**"; led the twelve-member **Barabhai** council; regent for infant Madhav Rao II (1774-95); held the confederacy together in the First war.
- **Mahadji Scindia**: recaptured Delhi and restored **Shah Alam II (1771)** → Marathas became Mughal king-makers; European-style infantry and artillery.
- After Peshwa control weakened (Madhav Rao I died 1772), **Nana Phadnavis died 1800** → Holkar and Scindia fought over Baji Rao II (1801-02) → Bassein.
### 5. BATTLES AND TREATIES WITH THE NIZAM
| Battle / treaty | Year | Peshwa | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Palkhed** → **Treaty of Mungi-Shevgaon** | 1728 | Baji Rao I | Nizam-ul-Mulk beaten; chauth and sardeshmukhi of the Deccan accepted |
| **Bhopal** / Treaty of Doraha Sarai | 1737-38 | Baji Rao I | Nizam ceded **Malwa** |
| Udgir | 1760 | Balaji Baji Rao | Nizam defeated |
| Rakshasbhuvan | 1763 | Madhav Rao I | Nizam defeated |
| **Kharda (Khurda)** | **1795** | Madhav Rao II | Nizam defeated; forced to pay chauth |
- **AP angle**: Nizam blamed his 1795 defeat on **Sir John Shore's neutrality** → **Subsidiary Alliance 1798**. Marathas and the Nizam joined the British in the **Third Mysore War**.
!KEY APPSC 2025: Mungi-Shevgaon = between the **Marathas and the Nizam**.
### 6. THIRD BATTLE OF PANIPAT – 14 JANUARY 1761
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Opponents | **Marathas vs Ahmad Shah Abdali** (APPSC G1 2018) |
| Maratha commanders | **Sadashiv Rao Bhau** (overall) and **Vishwas Rao** (Peshwa's son) – both killed; artillery **Ibrahim Khan Gardi** |
| Peshwa / emperor | Balaji Baji Rao / **Shah Alam II** (not a combatant) |
| Abdali's motive | Marathas drove his son **Timur Shah** out of **Lahore (1758)**; Raghunath Rao reached Lahore-Attock |
| Abdali's allies | **Najib-ud-Daula** (Rohilla, invited Abdali), **Shuja-ud-Daula** of Awadh |
| Maratha allies | Almost none – **Jats (Suraj Mal)** and Rajputs stayed away |
| Eyewitness | **Kashiraj Pandit**, Shuja-ud-Daula's official |
- **Causes of defeat**: no allies in the north (earlier plunder alienated Rajputs, Jats); supply collapse and starvation; heavy baggage and non-combatants.
- **Consequences**: northern ambitions halted; Peshwa died soon after; vacuum helped the Company (Plassey 1757, Wandiwash 1760); Maratha power recovered within a decade.
!TRAP Panipat III was Marathas vs Abdali – not Mughals vs Abdali; Nadir Shah invaded in 1739.
### 7. ANGLO-MARATHA WARS
#### 7.1 First War (1775-82)
- Cause: succession dispute after **Narayan Rao's murder**; **Raghunath Rao** offered the British Salsette, Bassein and Surat-Bharuch revenue.
| Treaty / event | Year | Point |
|---|---|---|
| **Treaty of Surat** | 1775 | Bombay to back Raghunath Rao with **2,500 troops** |
| Treaty of Purandar | 1776 | Calcutta council annulled Surat |
| **Talegaon / Convention of Wadgaon** | 1779 | British defeated; Wadgaon rejected by **Warren Hastings** |
| **Treaty of Salbai** | **1782** | With **Mahadji Scindia**; status quo; peace for 20 years; first Anglo-Maratha peace |
#### 7.2 Second War (1803-05)
- **Treaty of Bassein (1802)**: Baji Rao II accepted **Lord Wellesley's Subsidiary Alliance**.
- **Assaye (23 September 1803)**: Arthur Wellesley beat Scindia and Bhonsle. **Laswari (1 November 1803)**, near Alwar: Lord Lake vs Scindia.
- Treaties: **Deogaon (December 1803)** – Bhonsle ceded **Cuttack, Balasore**; **Surji-Anjangaon (30 December 1803)** – Scindia ceded the Doab, Delhi-Agra region.
#### 7.3 Third War (1817-18)
- Governor-General **Lord Hastings (Marquess of Hastings)**; causes: imperial designs + **Pindari** campaigns. **Nagpur (Bhonsle)** and **Indore (Holkar)** joined.
- Battles: **Khadki (Kirkee), Koregaon, Sitabuldi, Mahidpur**.
- **Peshwaship abolished 1818**; **Baji Rao II** pensioned to **Bithur near Kanpur**; small **Satara** state for Shivaji's descendant.
!TRAP **Warren Hastings = First war** (rejected Wadgaon); **Lord Hastings = Third war**.
