## U0310 | PARLIAMENTARY PRIVILEGES; ANTI-DEFECTION LAW (TENTH SCHEDULE) AND THE SPEAKER
### 1. PARLIAMENTARY PRIVILEGES
| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| **105** / **194** | Privileges, **freedom of speech**, immunity for anything said or voted – Parliament / State Legislatures |
| **122** / **212** | Courts cannot question **proceedings** for procedural irregularity |
- **Not codified** in one law; rest on **conventions** (House of Commons), rules, statutes, court rulings.
- **Notice**: LS **Rule 222** / RS **Rule 187**, usually after **Question Hour**.
- Procedure: Presiding Officer consents → House or **Privileges Committee** recommends → **House decides**. RS committee: **10** members, usually chaired by the **Deputy Chairman**; LS: **15** nominated by Speaker.
- **Contempt** is wider (any act obstructing the House or members); **breach** = breaking a specific privilege.
- Punishments: **admonition, reprimand, imprisonment, suspension/expulsion**.
| Case | Holding |
|---|---|
| **Raja Ram Pal (2007)** | Privilege decisions reviewable only for **gross illegality or unconstitutionality** |
| **Sita Soren (2024)** | **7 judges**: **no immunity for bribery** to speak or vote; overruled **P.V. Narasimha Rao (1998)** |
- **Latest:** Maharashtra Council's Privileges Committee summoned comedian **Kunal Kamra**.
!TRAP Rule 222 = LS; Rule 187 = RS.
### 2. MAKING OF THE ANTI-DEFECTION LAW
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1967 | **"Aaya Ram Gaya Ram"** (Haryana); **Y.B. Chavan Committee** (1967-69) |
| 1973, 1978 | Anti-defection Bills **lapsed** |
| **1979** | **Jammu and Kashmir** – **first State** with its own law |
| 1984 | **AP**: **Nadendla Bhaskara Rao** toppled **NTR**; NTR restored after a month |
| **1985** | **52nd Amendment**: **Tenth Schedule**; amended Arts **101, 102, 190, 191**; assent **15 Feb 1985**, in force **1 March 1985** |
| **2003** | **91st Amendment** tightened it |
- Aim: curb defections "induced by the **lure of office**", ensure party discipline and stable governments.
!KEY 52nd Amendment (1985) = **first constitutional recognition of political parties** (APPSC 2019).
!TRAP Old Sikkim "Tenth Schedule" = **35th Amendment (1974)**, omitted by the **36th (1975)**.
### 3. 91ST AMENDMENT, 2003
| Change | Provision |
|---|---|
| **One-third split** exception deleted | Para 3 |
| Union ministry ≤ **15%** of Lok Sabha | Art. 75(1A) |
| State ministry ≤ 15% of Assembly, **minimum 12** | Art. 164(1A) |
| Disqualified defector barred from Minister / paid political post until re-elected | Arts 75(1B), 164(1B), **361B** |
### 4. COVERAGE AND GROUNDS
- Covers **both Houses of Parliament** and **State Legislatures**, incl. **MLCs**; party, **independent** and **nominated** members.
| Member | Disqualified for | Exception |
|---|---|---|
| Party member | **Voluntarily giving up membership** (from conduct); voting/abstaining against **whip** (Para **2(1)(b)**) | Prior permission, or party **condones within 15 days** |
| **Independent** | Joining **any party** after election | May become a Minister without joining a party |
| **Nominated** | Joining a party **after 6 months** | Within 6 months allowed |
| **Presiding officer** | Joining **another** party | May leave party on election, rejoin after office (Para **5**) |
- Giving up membership is possible **without resigning** – e.g. campaigning for a rival party.
### 5. MERGER VS SPLIT
| Exception | Rule | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Merger (Para 4)** | **Original party** merges + **two-thirds** of legislature party agree | **In force** |
| **Split (Para 3)** | **One-third** break away | **Deleted, 91st (2003)** |
- Criticism: the two-thirds rule can defend **mass defection**.
!TRAP Merger needs **two-thirds**, not one-third.
### 6. SPEAKER AS DECIDING AUTHORITY
| Basis | Defection | General disqualification |
|---|---|---|
| Provisions | Tenth Sch.; Arts **102(2), 191(2)** | Arts 102(1), **103** / 191(1), **192** |
| Decided by | **Speaker / Chairman** as a **tribunal** (Para **6**) | **President / Governor** |
| ECI | **Not consulted** | Opinion **binding** |
- **Para 7** barred courts – struck down (Kihoto).
- **Judicial review only after** the decision: mala fides, perversity, natural justice, constitutional mandate.
- **No time limit** in the Schedule; Speakers stay party members.
!TRAP President/Governor have no role in defection cases; ECI gives no opinion (UPSC 2025).
### 7. KEY CASES
| Case | Holding |
|---|---|
| **Kihoto Hollohan (1992)** | Tenth Schedule **valid** (no free-speech or basic-structure violation); **Para 7** struck down (no State ratification, Art. **368(2)**), **3:2** |
| **Ravi S. Naik (1994)** | Giving up membership ≠ resigning; **inferred from conduct** |
| **G. Viswanathan (1996)** | **Expelled member** still belongs to party; joining another = giving up |
| **Keisham Meghachandra (Jan 2020)** | Decide within **three months**; suggested an **independent tribunal** (not ECI) |
| **Subhash Desai (May 2023)** | **Political party**, not legislature party, appoints **whip** and leader; no "deemed merger" by legislators alone |
| **Karnataka (2019)** | Upheld disqualification of **17** rebels; Speaker cannot bar them from contesting |
- **Telangana:** SC gave the Speaker a final **3 weeks** for the last **2 of 10 BRS** MLAs, warning of contempt (Feb 2026).
- **May 2026:** **Palaniswami** camp (AIADMK) cited Subhash Desai to claim the whip.
- **Jul 2026:** Bench of **P.S. Narasimha** and **Alok Aradhe** on Para 4 "deemed merger": gaps are "**matters for Parliament**".
!NUM Kihoto 1992 = validity + review; Keisham 2020 = 3 months; Desai 2023 = party appoints whip
### 8. WORKING OF THE LAW AND AP ANGLE
- **Resort politics**: legislators sequestered before a floor test.
- **Resignation route**: MLAs resign, lowering the majority mark, re-contest (**Karnataka 2019, MP 2020**).
- **AP 2014-19**: **23 YSRCP MLAs** moved to TDP; petitions never decided.
- **June 2019**: **4 of 6 TDP RS MPs** "merged" with BJP (Para 4); accepted by RS Chairman.
- **Apr 2026**: **7 of 10 AAP** RS MPs (incl. Raghav Chadha) claimed merger with BJP; **20 TMC** LS MPs told Speaker **Om Birla** of merger with the **NPCI** (June 2026).
### 9. REFORM PROPOSALS
| Body | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Dinesh Goswami (1990)** | Disqualify only for giving up membership or defying whip on **confidence votes**; President/Governor on EC advice |
| **Law Commission 170th (1999)** | Delete split and merger; **pre-poll alliances** as parties |
| **NCRWC (2002)** | Defectors barred from office; defector's **vote invalid** |
| **Election Commission** | President/Governor decide on EC's binding advice |
| **Manish Tewari Bill (2010)** | Limit law to **confidence and money votes** |
- Limiting the whip to votes threatening the government lets members **dissent freely** elsewhere.
- Newer: **fixed timelines** for Speakers (Jul 2026); independent tribunal (SC, 2020).
