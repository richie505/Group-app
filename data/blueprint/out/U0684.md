### 4. RAIL NETWORK, NEW LINES AND TUNNELS
- **First railway in AP: Puttur-Renigunta, 1862.** AP network **100% electrified**; works worth ₹92,649 crore under way (3 Feb 2026).
| New line | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Errupalem-Nambur (Amaravati line)** | **57 km**, **₹2,245 crore**; NTR and Guntur districts (AP) + Khammam (Telangana); **3-km Krishna bridge** |
| **Nadikudi-Srikalahasti** | **308 km**; four backward districts **Guntur, Prakasam, Nellore, Chittoor**; **cement and granite** traffic |
| **Kotipalli-Narasapur** | Konaseema coast; in progress (Feb 2026) |
| 3rd/4th lines Rajahmundry-Visakhapatnam | ₹9,889 crore |
- **Cherlopalli-Rapuru tunnel:** **6.6 km**, SPSR Nellore; **India's longest electrified rail tunnel when opened (2019)**; on the **Obulavaripalle-Venkatachalam** line linking **Rayalaseema to Krishnapatnam port**.
- **Port rail links:** Ramayapatnam next to **NH-16 and Thettu** station; Kakinada Gateway has its own line; last-mile rail to **Kopparthy, Orvakal, Krishnapatnam** nodes incomplete.
!TRAP APPSC G2 2025: the "6-year timeline" for the Amaravati line was the WRONG statement.

### 6. COASTLINE AND PORT FRAMEWORK
- **Coastline 1,053.07 km** = **third-longest** (2023-24 remeasurement); older **974 km = second** (APPSC 2022 key).
- **1 major port** (Visakhapatnam) + **17 notified non-major ports** under **APMB**, **3 captive** (Survey 2025-26).
- Major ports = **Union List Entry 27**; non-major (Other Than Major Ports) = **Concurrent List Entry 31**.
- **Dugarajapatnam** (Duggirajupatnam): major port promised in the **13th Schedule** of the AP Reorganisation Act.
- AP has 3 national industrial corridors + NH-16: an **east-coast gateway to South-East Asia**. **Blue economy target: US$ 20 bn by 2035.**

| Port | District | Model / operator | Feature |
|---|---|---|---|

- **Kakinada** = AP's port city: Anchorage Port, Deep Water Port, Fishing Harbour, Ship-Breaking Unit.
- Non-major cargo 2025-26 (to Nov 2025): **80.82 MMT**; Krishnapatnam 41.09, Gangavaram 24.39, Kakinada DWP 12.93; ₹252.21 crore to the State.
- **PYQ 2024 key:** Krishnapatnam-**Nellore**, Gangavaram-**Visakhapatnam**, Ramayapatnam-**Prakasam**, **Bhavanapadu-Srikakulam**.
!TRAP BOOT = Gangavaram; BOST = Krishnapatnam; OMST = Kakinada DWP; DBFOT = Kakinada Gateway.
### 8. VISAKHAPATNAM PORT
- Opened to ocean traffic **1933**; **deepest landlocked and protected** port (APPSC 2025 key); natural + artificial harbour parts.
- Protected by **Dolphin's Nose** (Yarada hills); called the **"Brightest Jewel"**.
- **Outer harbour** for **iron ore exports**; crude oil berth; all-weather, deep-water (this does not explain its location).
- Ranked **third** in port traffic (FY 2022). Helped transport of agricultural products (APPSC 2022 key).
- **Top seafood-export port** (2025-26): **5.12 lakh tonnes**, over **26%** of India's 19.32 lakh tonnes.
!TRAP Deepest port = Visakhapatnam; deepest draft = Gangavaram.

| Greenfield port | District | Capacity / progress (Oct 2025) |
|---|---|---|
| **Ramayapatnam** | **SPSR Nellore** (Survey 2025-26) | 34.04 MTPA; **74.34%** (highest) |

- **Four greenfield ports** (Visakhapatnam is not one): **₹17,067 crore**; add **108 MMTPA**; cut logistics costs **30-50%**; Phase-I operations by **Dec 2026**.
- Port capacity target: **145 MTPA by 2030**, **300 MTPA by 2047**.
- Ramayapatnam was in Prakasam (APPSC 2024 key); its Gudluru area moved to SPSR Nellore.
- **Vodarevu and Nizampatnam** stalled by a **CBI probe**. Future ports: Bheemunipatnam, Kalingapatnam, Narsapur.

#### 10.1 Bhogapuram
- **Alluri Sitarama Raju International Airport**, Bhogapuram, **Vizianagaram**; ~50 km from Visakhapatnam.
- Developer **GMR (GVIAL)**, **PPP-DBFOT**; ~₹5,640 crore; **6 Mppa**, expandable to **40 Mppa**.
- **Inaugurated by PM 1 Aug 2026**; commercial operations **17 Aug 2026** (code VTZ); replaced the INS Dega civil enclave.
- **AP's first greenfield airport since 2014.** Nodal SPV: **APADCL** (formerly BIACL), **100% State-owned**.
- **Proposed:** **Dagadarthi** (Nellore; no-frills PPP, cargo hub), **Kuppam, Srikakulam**, Ongole, Nagarjuna Sagar, Tuni-Annavaram, Tadepalligudem, **Amaravati (4,500 acres)**. Gannavaram is the existing Vijayawada airport.
- **Seaplane** aerodromes planned at ten sites incl. **Prakasam Barrage**.
- **Cochin (1999)** = first airport built by a **public limited company** under PPP.
### 11. INLAND WATERWAYS
- **NW-4** = Kakinada-Puducherry canal system with the Godavari and Krishna (1,095 km).
- **Canals:** **Kakinada, Eluru, Commamur, North and South Buckingham** (**not Krishna Canal**).
| River | NW-4 stretch |
|---|---|
| Godavari | **Bhadrachalam-Dowleswaram** |
| Krishna | **Wazirabad-Prakasam Barrage** |
- **AP's first riverine project: Muktyala-Madipadu** on the Krishna (APPSC G1 2024). IWAI finished Krishna surveys (Mar 2026).
- **Buckingham Canal:** ~796 km; longest commercial navigation canal on the east coast; through **Pulicat Lake**.
- Industrial Policy 2024-29 plans NW-4 for modal shift.
### 12. LOGISTICS, SAGARMALA AND METRO
- **Nexus Integrated Logistics Park:** Chinna Eetivakam (Satyavedu, Tirupati); built through **APMB = AP Maritime Board** (not Medical Board).
- **APLINC** (AP Logistics Infrastructure Corporation) = holding company. **AP Logistics Fund** = AIF, State sponsor up to 20%.
- Bhogapuram aerotropolis: 25,000 t cargo capacity.
- **Sagarmala pillars:** port modernisation, connectivity, port-led industrialisation, coastal community development, coastal shipping/IWT (**DFCs not a pillar**).
- AP asks: Sagarmala roads to Gangavaram + AM/NS captive port; Sagarmala 2.0 funds for fishing harbours (Juvvaladinne 97.98%).
- **Visakhapatnam Metro:** **4 corridors, 76.70 km**; metro-cum-flyover on NH-16. State says Vizag + Vijayawada metros approved (27 Jan 2026); Centre still checking.
- **Vizhinjam** (Kerala, May 2025) = India's first deep-water transshipment and first semi-automated container port.
- Challenges: logistics cost, no dedicated freight rail to clusters, water-short inland nodes, CBI-stalled ports, land delays.
