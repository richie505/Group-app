### 2. SEISMIC WAVES AND SHADOW ZONE
| Basis | P (primary) | S (secondary) | Surface (L) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Type | Longitudinal, push-pull; vibration **parallel** | Transverse, shear; vibration **perpendicular** | Along surface |
| Speed | **Fastest**, arrive first | Slower | Slowest |
| Medium | **Solid, liquid, gas** | **Solids only** | – |
| Damage | Lower | – | **Highest** |
- P and S are **body waves**. Recorded by the **seismograph**. Speed changes with density → reflection and refraction reveal boundaries.
- **P-wave shadow zone: ~105°–145°** from the epicentre; P-waves strongly refracted at the mantle–core boundary.
- **S-waves absent beyond 105°** → bigger shadow; S-waves vanish in the outer core → **outer core is liquid**.
- Each earthquake has its **own** shadow zone, set by its epicentre (CGPSC 2020).
### 3. LAYERS
| Layer | Depth | State | Density (g/cm³) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Crust** | Oceanic ~5 km, continental ~30 km (~70 under mountains) | Solid | 2.8–3.0 |
| **Mantle** | Moho → **2,900 km** | Solid, plastic flow (convection) | 4.6 |
| **Outer core** | 2,900–5,150 km (~2,260 km thick) | **Liquid** iron-nickel | ~10–12 |
| **Inner core** | 5,150–6,371 km (radius ~1,220 km) | **Solid** (immense pressure); P at 11.23 km/s | **13.3–13.6** |
- **Mantle 83% volume, 68% mass**; **core 16% volume, 32% mass**.
- Continental crust: thicker, older, granitic (Si, Al). Oceanic: thinner, younger, **basaltic** (Fe, Mg).
- Compositional: crust, mantle, core. Mechanical: lithosphere, asthenosphere, mesosphere, outer core, inner core.
- The Moon's core is **solid**.

### 5. LITHOSPHERE AND ASTHENOSPHERE
| Basis | Lithosphere | Asthenosphere |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Rigid; crust + uppermost mantle (~100 km) | Weak, plastic, partly molten (~100–200 km) |
| Role | Broken into **plates** | **Main source of magma**; plates glide over it |
- Sequence: lithosphere → asthenosphere → mesosphere → **barysphere** (core).
- Indian plate under Tibet **delaminating** at ~100 km (94 stations, helium isotopes). Stanford: first global map of continental mantle earthquakes (80+ km below Moho).

### 7. MAGNETISM, HEAT AND PRESSURE
- **Geodynamo**: convection of molten iron in the outer core makes the magnetic field, shielding life from solar wind and cosmic rays; flow changes linked to reversals (some up to ~70,000 years). ESA **Swarm**: outer-core flow under the Pacific switched westward to eastward, weakening ~2020.
- Magnetic axis ~**11.5°** to geographic axis.
- Temperature, pressure, density **all rise with depth**; ~**1°C per 32 m** in the upper crust. Earth's density **5.5 g/cm³** (densest planet).
- Geothermal: **Puga Valley** (Ladakh, highest), Manikaran (HP), Tattapani (Chhattisgarh), **Cambay** (Gujarat), **Bakreshwar** (WB).
- Diamonds rise via **kimberlite pipes**; **MP** has ~90% of reserves.
