## U0371 | PUBLIC-PRIVATE PARTNERSHIPS, DISINVESTMENT AND STRATEGIC SALE
### 1. PPP MODELS AND RISK
| Model | Expansion | Risk / feature |
|---|---|---|
| BOT (Toll) | Build-Operate-Transfer | Private finances, collects toll; **bears revenue risk** |
| BOT (Annuity) | Same | Govt pays fixed annuity; **govt bears revenue risk** |
| BOOT | Build-Own-Operate-Transfer | Private owns during concession, then transfers |
| BOO / BOLT | Own, no transfer / Own-Lease-Transfer | Lease to govt, then transfer |
| DBFO(T) | Design-Build-Finance-Operate-(Transfer) | Private also designs and finances |
| EPC | Engineering, Procurement, Construction | **Govt funds all**; private only constructs |
| HAM | Hybrid Annuity Model | Govt **40%** in construction; developer **60%** repaid by semi-annual annuities over ~**15 years**; govt bears traffic risk |
| TOT | Toll-Operate-Transfer | Toll rights on already-built highways |
- HAM = EPC + BOT-Annuity; first outside roads: **Namami Gange STPs**.
- **MoRTH 2026**: HAM net worth **20%** (from 15%); funds may bid for **BOT**.
### 2. ASSET MONETISATION – NMP
- Objective: **public asset owners** use new structures (**InvITs, REITs**) with private capital; brownfield **public** assets only; rights for a **limited period** (lease/licence), assets return; not a sale.
| Feature | NMP 1.0 | NMP 2.0 |
|---|---|---|
| Launched | **Aug 2021, NITI Aayog** | Budget 2025-26 |
| Period | FY22-FY25 | FY26-FY30 |
| Size | ~**₹6 lakh crore** | **₹16.72 lakh crore** (₹5.8 lakh crore private) |
| Sectors | Roads, railways, power and others | **12**: highways **26%**, power 17%, railways 16%, ports 16%, coal 13% |
- Critics: undervaluation; "sale by another name".

### 5. DISINVESTMENT – DIPAM AND METHODS
- **Department of Disinvestment: 10 Dec 1999** → **DIPAM (14 Apr 2016)**, Ministry of Finance; manages Centre's CPSE equity.
| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Minority | Govt keeps **>51%** and control |
| Majority | Control passes; minority stake kept |
| Strategic sale | Shares **+ management control** to a partner |
- Methods: **IPO** (first issue) vs **FPO** (listed PSE); **OFS** (since **2012**); **IPP** (QIBs only); **CPSE ETF, Bharat-22 ETF**.
- 1991: 20% disinvestment announced; till 1999-2000 only minority stakes; **1999-2004** strategic sales.
- **LIC IPO (17 May 2022)**: biggest issue then.
- Trend: revenue fell every year except **2022-23**; **dividends** grew every year.
#### 5.1 New PSE Policy 2021
- Exit **non-strategic** sectors; bare minimum in four strategic groups: **atomic energy, space, defence**; **transport, telecom**; **power, petroleum, coal, minerals**; **banking, insurance, financial services** (not agriculture/textiles/retail).

### 7. AP PPP PROJECTS
- **AP Infrastructure Development Enabling Act, 2001**: PPP framework incl. Swiss-challenge proposals.
- **Krishnapatnam Port** (Nellore) → **Adani Ports (2020)**; **Gangavaram Port** (Visakhapatnam) → Adani Ports (2021).
- **Bhogapuram airport**: **GMR**, DBFOT, 40-year concession.
- **AP PPP Policy-2026** (Cabinet 6 Aug 2026): **₹1,500 crore VGF** + bridge fund.
### 8. PPP ISSUES
- Stalled projects: **aggressive bidding (winner's curse)**, traffic shortfall, land/clearance delays → renegotiation.
- **Swiss challenge**: unsolicited proposal → counter-bids → proponent may match; criticised for information asymmetry.
