## U0739 | MISSILES BEYOND IGMDP – BRAHMOS, NIRBHAY, ASTRA, PRALAY, HYPERSONIC TECHNOLOGY, MISSION SHAKTI (ASAT)
### 1. MISSILE CLASSES – BALLISTIC VS CRUISE
#### 1.1 Ballistic vs Cruise
| Feature | Ballistic missile | Cruise missile |
|---|---|---|
| Power | Rocket-powered only in the **boost phase** | **Air-breathing**, powered throughout |
| Path | High **parabolic** (free-fall) arc | Flies **low**, constant speed, uses aerodynamic lift |
| Examples | Agni, Prithvi, **Pralay**, Shaurya | **BrahMos**, **Nirbhay** |
#### 1.2 Classification by Speed
| Class | Speed | Indian example |
|---|---|---|
| Subsonic | Below Mach 1 | **Nirbhay** |
| Supersonic | Mach 1–5 | **BrahMos** (Mach 2.8–3.0) |
| Hypersonic | Above **Mach 5** | BrahMos-II (planned), LR-AShM, HSTDV |
#### 1.3 Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **HGV** (Hypersonic Glide Vehicle) | Rocket-boosted, then **glides at Mach 5+** and manoeuvres in the upper atmosphere; hard to intercept |
| **MIRV** | One missile, **several warheads**, different targets; first Indian test on **Agni-V** in **Mission Divyastra (11 March 2024)** |
| **FOBS** (Fractional Orbital Bombardment System) | Warhead put into a (partial) **low-Earth orbit**, then de-orbits over the target; can come from any direction |
| **Scramjet** | **Supersonic combustion**, no rotating parts; uses air, does not carry its oxidiser |
| **SFDR** (Solid Fuel Ducted Ramjet) | Uses atmospheric air; demonstrated from **ITR Chandipur**; for long-range air-to-air missiles |
!TRAP Mission Divyastra MIRV used **Agni-V**, not K-4 (K-4 is submarine-launched).
### 2. BRAHMOS – INDIA-RUSSIA SUPERSONIC CRUISE MISSILE
#### 2.1 Partnership and Name
- Joint venture of **India and Russia** (asked in **APPSC G2 Mains 2025**).
- **Inter-Governmental Agreement** signed in **Moscow, 1998** → created **BrahMos Aerospace**.
- Ownership: **DRDO 50.5%** + **NPO Mashinostroyeniya (NPOM), Russia 49.5%**.
- Name: **Brahmaputra** (India) + **Moskva** (Russia) rivers.
#### 2.2 Technical Features
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| First successful test | **12 June 2001, Chandipur, Odisha** |
| Speed class | **Supersonic** cruise, **Mach 2.8–3.0** (~3,400–3,700 km/h); one of the fastest cruise missiles |
| Propulsion | **Solid booster → liquid ramjet** |
| Navigation | Inertial + satellite (GPS/GLONASS); **fire-and-forget** from a Transport Launch Canister |
| Flight profile | Cruises at ~15 km; dives to ~10 m in terminal phase; low radar cross-section |
| Range | **290 km** originally → 290–400 km (extended range); raised towards ~450 km after India joined **MTCR (2016)** |
#### 2.3 Variants and Launch Platforms
- Base BrahMos can be launched from all **four platforms**: land, ship, submarine and air (**Su-30MKI**).
| Variant | Feature |
|---|---|
| Ship-launched | Warships, e.g. **P-17A frigates** |
| Extended range | ~400+ km |
| **BrahMos-NG** | Lighter (~1.5 t vs 2.5 t), for **Tejas**, smaller warships, submarines; ~Mach 3.5 |
| **BrahMos-II** | Planned **hypersonic**, above Mach 5 (up to Mach 7), under development |
#### 2.4 Test Sites and Milestones
- **January 2009** and **29 March 2009**: land-attack Block-II tests at **Pokhran** (Mobile Autonomous Launcher; 290 km).
- **21 March 2010**: fired from moving warship **INS Ranvir** off the Odisha coast (Bay of Bengal).
- **BrahMos Integration and Testing Facility, Lucknow** (UP defence corridor): inaugurated **11 May 2025**; first batch within five months.
- **First reported combat use: Operation Sindoor (May 2025).**
!KEY APPSC G2 2025: "BrahMos is developed by India and ___" = **Russia**.
### 3. NIRBHAY, ITCM AND ASTRA
#### 3.1 Nirbhay and ITCM
- **Nirbhay**: DRDO's first indigenous **subsonic long-range land-attack cruise missile (LR-LACM)**.
- Range **~1,000 km**; **turbojet**; payload ~200–300 kg; conventional or **nuclear** warheads.
- Terrain-hugging at low altitude; inertial + GPS/NavIC navigation.
- Flight-tested **7 November 2017** from **ITR Chandipur**.
- **ITCM** (Indigenous Technology Cruise Missile): Nirbhay successor, **'Manik' small turbofan engine** by **GTRE**.
- Jun 2026: the family is now called **LR-LACM**.
!PAIR Nirbhay | Subsonic | Turbojet || BrahMos | Supersonic | Ramjet
#### 3.2 Astra (BVRAAM)
- India's **first indigenous Beyond Visual Range Air-to-Air Missile** (DRDO); all-weather; **active radar seeker**.
- BVR = engages beyond 20 nautical miles (37 km).
- First successfully tested **9 May 2003 (Balasore)**; ballistic flight tests at **ITR Chandipur**.
| Variant | Facts (10 Jul 2026) |
|---|---|
| Astra Mk-1 | **80–110 km**, Mach 4.5, up to 20 km altitude; on **Su-30MKI**; planned for Tejas Mk-1A and Rafale |
| Astra Mk-2 | ~200 km (2026), **dual-pulse solid motor** |
| Astra Mk-3 **'Gandiva'** | Under development, **SFDR** engine, **350+ km** |
- **23 August 2023**: LCA Tejas (LSP-7) test-fired Astra off **Goa**; **12 March 2025**: fired from an LCA AF Mk1 prototype.

| Missile | Type / role |
|---|---|

| **VL-SRSAM** | Vertical-launch short-range SAM for **Navy ships**, derived from **Astra** |
| **QRSAM** | Quick Reaction SAM, **Army's mid-range IADWS layer** against low-flying threats |
| **VSHORADS** | **Man-portable** (MANPADS), 4th generation, by **RCI**; DAC-approved buy Jul 2026 |
| **MPATGM** | Man-portable, **third-generation, fire-and-forget ATGM**; procurement approved by DAC Jul 2026 |
| Prahaar | ~150 km tactical ballistic missile |

| NASM-SR | First indigenous **helicopter-launched anti-ship** missile, IIR seeker |

| Trishul | Short-range supersonic missile, tested from Chandipur |
!TRAP Shaurya's striking range is **not 900 km**; its speed is not more than Mach 8.
### 5. HYPERSONIC TECHNOLOGY – INDIAN SYSTEMS AND FIRSTS
- Hypersonic = faster than **Mach 5**.
- **HSTDV** (Hypersonic Technology Demonstrator Vehicle): indigenous **scramjet**; flew above **Mach 6**; proved scramjet combustion and thermal protection above **2,000°C**; scramjet combustor run over **12 minutes (Jan 2026)**. It is not a submarine-launched missile.
- **16 November 2024**: first flight-trial of India's **first long-range hypersonic missile**.
- **LR-AShM**: hypersonic **glide anti-ship** missile for Navy coastal batteries; ~1,500 km; **peak Mach 10, average Mach 5** via **"skipping" manoeuvres**; shown at the 77th Republic Day Parade.
- AP angle: ranges along the **Andhra Pradesh coast** used for **LR-AShM validation rounds** (Jan 2026).
!PAIR HSTDV | Scramjet demonstrator | Mach 6+ || LR-AShM | Glide anti-ship missile | Peak Mach 10
### 6. AIR-DEFENCE SHIELDS – BMD, KUSHA, SUDARSHAN CHAKRA
#### 6.1 Ballistic Missile Defence Layers
| BMD phase | Exo-atmospheric | Endo-atmospheric |
|---|---|---|
| Phase-I | **PAD / Pradyumna** (50–80 km) | **AAD / Ashwin** (15–30 km) |
| Phase-II | **AD-1** (IRBM-class threats) | **AD-2** (under development) |
#### 6.2 New Shields
- **Mission Sudarshan Chakra**: multi-layered air and missile defence network; operational maturity by **2035** (Aug 2026).
- **Project Kusha**: DRDO **long-range** (not short-range) air defence; interceptors **M1/M2/M3 ≈ 150 / 250 / 350–400 km**.
- **THAAD** (USA): hit-to-kill interception of short-, medium- and intermediate-range ballistic missiles in the terminal phase; deployed in **South Korea (2017)**.
!TRAP PAD = Pradyumna = exo; AAD = Ashwin = endo.
### 7. MISSION SHAKTI – INDIA'S ASAT TEST
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Date | **27 March 2019** |
| Agencies | Joint **DRDO + ISRO** (**APPSC G1 2022**) |
| Interceptor | DRDO **PDV Mk-II** (from the BMD programme) |
| Target | Live satellite **Microsat-R** (DRDO; launched by **PSLV-C44**, Jan 2019) |
| Orbit | **Low Earth Orbit, ~283 km** |
| Rank | **4th** ASAT nation, after **USA, Russia, China** |
| Debris | Low LEO chosen so debris **decays quickly** |
!TRAP APPSC G1 2022: "Done in the upper atmosphere to ensure no debris" is **WRONG**; "destroyed a live satellite in LEO" is correct.
