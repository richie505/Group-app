## U0241 | INDIVIDUAL SATYAGRAHA, AUGUST OFFER, CRIPPS MISSION
### 1. WORLD WAR II AND THE CONGRESS MINISTRIES
- **Trigger:** Viceroy **Lord Linlithgow** declared India a party to **World War II** (September 1939) **without consulting** Indians.
- **Congress ministries resigned** in **October-November 1939** in protest.
- **22 December 1939:** Muslim League observed the **"Day of Deliverance"**; **B.R. Ambedkar** joined it in **Bombay**.
- **March 1940:** **Ramgarh session** (Bihar), the **53rd** Congress session - offered war help if a **provisional national government** was formed at the Centre.
!TRAP Ramgarh (March 1940) came BEFORE the August Offer (August 1940); the Offer was the reply to Ramgarh.
### 2. AUGUST OFFER, 1940
#### 2.1 Proposals
- Made by Viceroy **Lord Linlithgow** on **8 August 1940**, when Britain was losing in Europe.
| Proposal | Detail |
|---|---|
| Constitution | A representative **constitution-making body after the war** |
| Executive Council | **More Indians** in the Viceroy's Executive Council |
| Advisory body | A **War Advisory Council** |
| Goal | **Dominion Status** |
| Minorities | **Minority veto** on any future constitution |
- **Not included:** immediate transfer of **defence**.
!KEY August Offer = FIRST official acceptance that **Indians would frame their own constitution**.
- The Constituent Assembly demand was first raised by **M.N. Roy (1934)**; adopted by Congress in **1935**.
#### 2.2 Responses
- **Congress rejected** it; **Nehru**: Dominion Status is **"dead as a doornail"**.
- **Muslim League welcomed the minority veto** but said only **partition** would satisfy it.
### 3. INDIVIDUAL SATYAGRAHA, 1940-41
#### 3.1 Launch and Aim
- Launched by **Gandhi** on **17 October 1940**, after the August Offer was rejected.
- Started from **Paunar (Pavnar), Wardha, Maharashtra** (also spelt "Palner").
- **Aim:** assert **freedom of speech** against the war; oppose being dragged into WWII - **not mass mobilisation** (hence "limited" / "individual").
- Gandhi's line: "It is wrong to help the British war effort with **men or money**."
- Chosen individuals made **anti-war speeches** and courted arrest one after another; also called **Individual Civil Disobedience**.
- More than **25,000** satyagrahis were convicted by 1941.
#### 3.2 First Three Satyagrahis
| Order | Satyagrahi |
|---|---|
| First | **Acharya Vinoba Bhave** |
| Second | **Jawaharlal Nehru** |
| Third | **Brahma Dutt** (chosen for moral conviction, not political prominence) |
- Government arrested **Vinoba, Nehru and Patel**.
- **Bihar:** led by **Shyam Narayan Singh** (BPSC 2025).
#### 3.3 Andhra Angle
- **Ongole Municipality** passed a resolution **condemning the arrests** of Vinoba, Nehru and Patel.
### 4. CRIPPS MISSION, 1942 - CONTEXT
- Arrived **March 1942** (Delhi, **22-23 March**); Cripps returned in **mid-April** after talks broke down.
- Headed by **Sir Stafford Cripps**, a **Labour** member of Churchill's **War Cabinet**.
- Sent by British PM **Winston Churchill** (PM 1940-45, 1951-55).
- Pressure from **USA (Franklin Roosevelt)**, **China (Chiang Kai-shek)** and **Australia** over Japan's rise.
- **Japanese advance:** Pearl Harbor (1941); Malaya, Singapore overrun; **Rangoon fell March 1942**.
- **Objective:** secure Indian cooperation in the war.
- Cripps: "Here is a revolution taking place and we should move quickly"; he was also a member of the **Cabinet Mission (1946)**.
### 5. CRIPPS PROPOSALS (DRAFT DECLARATION)
| Proposal | Detail |
|---|---|
| Status | **Indian Union with Dominion Status** after the war, with the **right to secede** from the Commonwealth |
| Constitution | **Indians to frame their own constitution**; body elected by **provincial lower houses** as electoral college (**1/10th** of their size, proportional representation) + **nominees of princely states** |
| Provincial option | Any province/state could **stay outside the Union** and form separate unions - **"the fatal flaw"**, Pakistan conceded in principle |
| Interim | **Defence and army** stay with the British; Governor-General's powers kept |
- Constitution-making body: **yes**; full independence: **no**.
!KEY Cripps = FIRST to allow provincial opt-out - seed of Partition.
### 6. REJECTION AND REMARKS
#### 6.1 Congress
- Negotiators: **Jawaharlal Nehru** and **Maulana Abul Kalam Azad** (Azad was Congress President).
- Objected to: Dominion Status only, **right to secede / provincial opt-out**, princely-state nominees, **no immediate transfer of power**, risk of **Balkanisation**.
#### 6.2 Muslim League
- **Rejected** it: opposed a **single Indian Union**; Pakistan was **not explicitly** granted.
!PAIR Congress | No immediate power | Feared Balkanisation || Muslim League | No explicit Pakistan | Opposed single Union
#### 6.3 Why It Failed
| Cause | Effect |
|---|---|
| Cripps's **"take it or leave it"** attitude; **Churchill's sabotage** | Talks broke down |
| Too radical for Churchill, too conservative for Indians | Rejected by all sides |
- "Mission solely of whites" - true, but **not** the reason for rejection.
#### 6.4 Gandhi's Remark
- Gandhi called the offer a **"post-dated cheque"**; it is popularly quoted as "a post-dated cheque **on a crashing bank**".
### 7. ROAD TO QUIT INDIA AND CHRONOLOGY
- **Result:** failure of Cripps led to the **Quit India Movement** - resolution **8 August 1942**, AICC, **Bombay**.
- Causes: **disappointment with Cripps** and **threat of Japanese attack**; NOT Gandhi's articles provoking violence.
- **AP angle:** rumours of **Japanese bombing of Vizag and Kakinada** during the Cripps Mission; Gandhi used "Quit India" in **Harijan** (April 1942).

### 10. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. First satyagrahi is **Vinoba Bhave**, not Nehru (second).
2. Individual Satyagraha aimed at **free speech**, not mass mobilisation.
3. Cripps was **Labour**, though in Churchill's (Conservative-led) War Cabinet.
4. Cripps offered **Dominion Status after the war**, not immediate independence.
5. **Both** Congress and League rejected Cripps - for opposite reasons.
6. Defence stayed with the British in the interim.
7. Ramgarh came **before** the August Offer.
8. Constituent Assembly demand: **M.N. Roy 1934**, Congress 1935.
9. Quit India was not caused by Gandhi's articles urging violence.
