### 4. REVENUE RECEIPTS
#### 4.1 Meaning
- **Revenue receipts** = income the government **need not pay back**; = tax + non-tax revenue.
- **Corporation tax = tax revenue**; interest, dividends, fees = **non-tax**; **market borrowings = capital receipt**; pensions = revenue expenditure.
- Centre's current account sources: corporation tax, profit of public enterprises, excise duties. Gross tax order (2009 question): customs > corporation tax > income tax > Union excise.
#### 4.2 Size and Composition
| Head (₹ cr) | 2022-23 | 2023-24 | 2024-25 BE | 2026-27 BE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Own tax | 78,026 | 85,922 | 1,09,789 | 1,25,846 |
| Own non-tax | 5,417 | 7,432 | 10,576 | – |
| Share in central taxes | 38,177 | 45,711 | 50,475 | 64,362 |
| Grants-in-aid | 36,149 | 34,702 | 30,334 | 32,458 |
| **Revenue receipts** | 1,57,768 | **1,73,767** | 2,01,174 | **2,34,190 (+19%)** |
- **2026-27**: own resources **59% (₹1,37,320 cr)** + central transfers **41% (₹96,820 cr)**.
- **2024-25 RE**: **₹1,91,142 cr** = own tax ₹94,967 + non-tax ₹7,018 + Centre ₹89,157. Own tax ~**half**; Centre **45–47%**; non-tax **~4%**.
- Order: **Own Tax > Central Transfers > Own Non-Tax**.
- PYQs: **2018-19 receipts ₹1,57,599 cr**; CAG 2015-16 own tax ≈ **45%**; **grants 2016-17 = 23.6%**; grants 2024-25 BE = 15%.

### 6. OWN NON-TAX REVENUE AND MINERALS
#### 6.1 Heads
- Non-tax = receipts not raised by taxing the public: **interest receipts, dividends and profits of PSUs, fees, fines, user charges, royalties**.
- **Profits and dividends of State PSUs** = non-tax revenue retained by the State (in "Others", ₹2,680 cr, 2024-25 RE).
- **User charges** = a **fiscal consolidation tool**.
#### 6.2 Largest Source – Mines and Minerals
| Head (₹ cr) | 2022-23 | 2023-24 | 2024-25 RE |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mines and Minerals** | 3,024 | 3,060 | 3,518 |
| Medical and Health | 177 | 211 | 313 |
| **Interest receipts** | 9 | 21 | **27** |
| Total non-tax | 5,417 | 7,432 | 7,018 |
- **Mines and Minerals highest every year**: 41% (2023-24) → 50% (2024-25 RE) → **37% (2025-26 RE)**, ahead of **Medical and Health 34%**; forests and interest receipts among the smallest.
- PYQ 2016-17 RE: **mines and minerals** ahead of forests, interest receipts, education.
#### 6.3 Minerals of AP
- AP = "**Ratna Garbha**" (womb of gems). Listed: barytes, beach sands, limestone, dolomite, mica, **uranium**, iron ore, gold, diamonds, granite (**platinum not listed**).
| Mineral | Place |
|---|---|
| **Barytes** | **Mangampet (YSR Kadapa)** |
| **Mica** | **Nellore belt** |
| **Limestone** | **Kadapa–Kurnool basins** |
- Mining revenue **₹4,756 cr (2022-23)**. **Royalty**: **ad valorem** on **Average Sale Price** (MMDR Act, 1957). Ad valorem = % of value (specific tax = fixed per unit).
- **MADA v. SAIL (2024)**: **royalty is not a tax**; States may tax mineral-bearing land. Auction revenue (incl. critical minerals) goes to **States**.
- **DMF = non-profit trust, Section 9B**, MMDR Amendment 2015, for mining-affected persons. **Section 9D**: future State levies need a Centre-controlled framework – called unfair to mineral-rich States.
- MMDR amended 2023; India in **Minerals Security Partnership**; not rich in all **30 critical minerals**.
- **Sand**: **Free Sand Policy, 8 July 2024** – buyers pay only operation cost, seigniorage and taxes.

### 8. REVENUE VS CAPITAL EXPENDITURE
- Revenue expenditure creates no asset (salaries, pensions, interest, **welfare/DBT**); capital expenditure creates assets or cuts liabilities.
- **Welfare = revenue expenditure** → recurring liability, widens revenue deficit; ES 2025-26 urges **conditional transfers or sunset clauses**.
#### 8.1 Capital Outlay Trend
| Year | Revenue exp (₹ cr) | Capital outlay (₹ cr) | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2022-23 | 2,01,256 | **7,244** | ~3.4% |
| 2023-24 | 2,12,450 | **23,331** | ~9.9% |
| 2024-25 RE | 2,24,343 | 24,072 | **~9.7%** |
| 2025-26 RE | 2,38,022 | 33,135 | **~11.9%** |
| 2026-27 BE | 2,56,143 | 48,698 | ~15.7% |
- **2016-21**: AP capex **below 15%** of total expenditure; capex shows **commitment to infrastructure**.
- **2024-25 actual ₹16,141 cr vs BE ₹32,713 cr – about 51% short**.
- All States 2014-24: capex **13–20%**, revenue expenditure **80–87%**.
- Capital outlay mainly: **irrigation and flood control, water supply and sanitation, urban development**.
### 9. DEVELOPMENTAL AND COMMITTED EXPENDITURE
#### 9.1 Classification

| **Economic services**: agriculture, rural development, irrigation, energy, industry, transport | **Interest payments, pensions** |

#### 10.3 Benchmarks, Limits and Rank
- **N.K. Singh Committee (2017)**: general government debt **60%** = **Centre 40% + States 20%**; AP ~36% is far above 20%.
- **FRBM (amended 2018)**: General Govt 60%, Central 40% by **31 March 2025**.
- **16th FC**: States' FD **3% of GSDP** (Union 3.5%). **Net Borrowing Ceiling** = 3% of GSDP; over-borrowing adjusted in later years.
- Comparators (2023-24): **AP 34.6%**, Punjab 42.9%, West Bengal 38.3%, Rajasthan 37.9%. All States 29.2% of GDP.
#### 10.4 Debt Division at Bifurcation (APRA 2014)
- **Section 54**: Public Debt and Public Account liabilities **split by population ratio**; loans re-lent for a project in one State pass to **that State**.
- Debt shared by population but AP got a smaller revenue base.

#### 11.2 Off-Budget Borrowings
- = loans by State PSUs/corporations, **repaid from the State budget**, excluded from reported debt; used to **get around FRBM caps**.
| Date | Outstanding |
|---|---|
| **Mar 2023 (CAG 2024)** | **₹1.28 lakh cr, 9.7% of GSDP** |
| **31 Mar 2024** | **₹1,40,470 cr** |
- **APSDC** escrowed **liquor revenue** to borrow from banks; AP High Court questioned tax revenue going to APSDC instead of the **Consolidated Fund**.
- Centre: off-budget borrowings of FY21–22 adjusted against **NBC** of FY23; future ones counted in NBC (e.g. Kerala's **KIIFB**).
- **16th FC**: **discontinue off-budget borrowings and bring them on budget**.
- They **obscure true consolidated debt** and may hurt **sovereign ratings**.
#### 11.3 Guarantees and Funds
- **Guarantees** = **contingent liabilities**, outside outstanding debt, due only **when invoked**.
- **₹1,77,328 cr (10% of GSDP)** in Budget 2026-27; **₹1,54,797 cr (11%)** in 2023-24; sanctioned exposure **₹3.11 lakh cr** (Dec 2025); conditional ₹27,000 cr (₹25,000 cr APSDC).
| Fund | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Consolidated Sinking Fund** | RBI-run; **amortise liabilities**; invested in **GoI securities** |
| **Guarantee Redemption Fund** | **RBI-run**; meet **invoked guarantees** |
