## U0610 | CLIMATE CHANGE – EVIDENCE, CAUSES, GLOBAL WARMING, IPCC ASSESSMENTS, IMPACTS
### 1. EVIDENCE OF WARMING
| Indicator | Record |
|---|---|
| **2024** | **Hottest year** on record, about **1.55°C** above pre-industrial |
| 2025 | 2nd/3rd warmest (~1.43°C); **warmest La Nina year** |
| 2015–2025 | The **11 warmest years** (WMO State of the Global Climate 2025) |
| **India** | Warmed about **0.7°C since 1900** (less than global land, ~1.59°C) |
| **Gangotri Glacier** | Retreated about **1,700 m since 1935**; cause: global warming (**APPSC G2 2025**) |
| Sea level | Up about **11 cm since 1993** |
| Sea ice (2025) | Arctic near record lows; Antarctic 3rd lowest |
!TRAP India has warmed about 0.7°C, NOT over 2°C.
- **Sea-level rise** = thermal expansion + melting ice; higher snow albedo is not a cause (it only reflects more radiation).
### 2. NATURAL CAUSES
| Cause | Meaning / effect |
|---|---|
| **Eccentricity** | Shape of Earth's orbit |
| **Obliquity** | Axial tilt |
| **Precession** | Wobble of the rotational axis |
| Solar output | Satellites since **1978** show **no net increase**: the Sun does not explain recent warming |
| Volcanoes | SO2 + ash form aerosols that reflect sunlight: **short-term cooling** |
- **Milankovitch theory** (Serbian **Milutin Milankovitch**, **1920s**): orbital forcing by eccentricity, tilt, precession. **Solar irradiance is NOT part** of the astronomical theory.
- **Little Ice Age**: about **1300–1850** (glaciers greatest 1500–1850); one exam key used **1650–1870**; much colder winters in Europe and North America than in the 20th century.
- **Great Ice Age** = **Pleistocene** (cooling began in the Pliocene); glaciation of peninsular India = Pleistocene.
### 3. ANTHROPOGENIC CAUSES AND GREENHOUSE GASES
- **Fossil fuels** (CO2), **land-use change** (deforestation removes sinks), **agriculture** (methane, N2O).
- CO2 emissions cause about **three-quarters** of global warming; methane most of the rest.
- **Water vapour** = most abundant greenhouse gas; natural, little affected directly by human emissions, but rises as the world warms (feedback).
| Gas | GWP (100 yr, AR6) | Main sources | Nature |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CO2** | 1 | Fossil fuels, deforestation, cement | Natural + human |
| **Methane (CH4)** | ~**27–30** | **Rice paddies**, livestock (**enteric fermentation**), manure, landfills | Natural + human |
| **Nitrous oxide (N2O)** | ~**273** | **Fertilisers** (largest farm source), manure | Natural + human |
| CFCs / **HFCs** | Very high | Refrigeration, air conditioning, insulation | **Synthetic** |
| **SF6** | ~**25,200** (highest) | Electrical switchgear, magnesium, semiconductors | **Synthetic** |
### 4. SHORT-LIVED CLIMATE POLLUTANTS (SLCPs)
- Stay in the air for **days to a few decades**, far shorter than CO2: cutting them gives fast results.
| SLCP | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Black carbon** | **Soot** (part of particulate matter); strong forcer; diesel, brick kilns, biomass |
| **Methane** | Direct warming + **precursor of tropospheric ozone** |
| **Tropospheric ozone** | **Not emitted directly**; formed by sunlight acting on methane, CO, VOCs, NOx |
| HFCs | Potent man-made gases in cooling |
- **Climate and Clean Air Coalition**: launched by **UNEP and six countries, 16 Feb 2012**.

### 6. IPCC AND OTHER REPORTS – FINDINGS
| Report | Year | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| **SR1.5** (Special Report on 1.5°C) | 2018 | 1.5°C likely **between 2030 and 2052** at current rate (**APPSC GS 2022**); CO2 must fall **~45% from 2010 levels by 2030**, **net zero ~2050** |
| **AR6 Synthesis** | 20 Mar 2023 | Already **1.1°C** (2011–2020); 1.5°C breached in the **early 2030s**; emissions to **peak before 2025**; net zero mid-century |
| **UNEP Emissions Gap 2025** | 2025 | Current policies → **~2.8°C**; full NDCs → **2.3–2.5°C**; cuts of 35% (2°C) / 55% (1.5°C) by 2035 vs 2019; 1.5°C overshoot "very likely" this decade |
| **WMO State of the Global Climate 2025** | **23 Mar 2026** (World Meteorological Day) | Indicators "**flashing red**"; oceans hold over 90% of excess heat |
- **CMIP7** scenarios drop the worst case (**SSP5-8.5**); world near 2.8°C by 2100.
!PAIR SR1.5 | 2018 | 1.5°C between 2030 and 2052 || AR6 | 2023 | breach in early 2030s
#### 6.1 Terms and Recognition
- **Net zero**: human emissions balanced by **removals** (natural sinks or technological capture).
- **Nobel Physics 2021**: **Syukuro Manabe** and **Klaus Hasselmann** for physical modelling of Earth's climate (shared with Giorgio Parisi).
### 7. IMPACTS
| Area | Effect |
|---|---|
| Sea level | Uninhabited Pacific islets **Tebua Tarawa, Abanuea** submerged 1999; Kiribati, Tuvalu at risk |
| **Coral reefs** | **1.2°C** warm-water reef tipping point **already crossed** (UN brief, 24 Jun 2026); 15+ years to recover |
| Amazon | Beyond ~2°C parts may turn **savanna** and become a carbon **source** |
| Oceans, species | Acidity up **30%** since the Industrial Revolution; up to **25% of mountain species** face extinction (AR6) |
| Disease | Warmer, wetter conditions spread **malaria, dengue** |
| Extremes, farming | India 2025: **4,419 deaths**, **17.4 million ha** crops damaged (WMO); Haryana buffalo milk yields hit |
- IPCC: warming raises frequency and severity of glacier melt, sea-level rise, floods, droughts, storms: **all of these**.
- **AP**: heatwaves in **Palnadu, Kurnool, Anantapur**; coastal erosion at **Uppada (Kakinada)**; APSES 2024-25 tracks SDG 13.
- **COP31**: **Turkiye, November 2026**.
### 8. GEOENGINEERING PROPOSALS
| Proposal | Meaning | Risk / note |
|---|---|---|
| **Stratospheric sulphate aerosol injection** | SO2 forms reflective sulphuric-acid aerosols; copies volcanic cooling | **Termination shock** if stopped |
| **Cirrus cloud thinning** | Seeding high cirrus so they thin; more outgoing heat escapes | – |
| Marine cloud brightening | Sea-salt spray as condensation nuclei; brighter clouds | Could weaken a Super El Nino |
!KEY PYQ: cirrus cloud thinning and stratospheric sulphate injection are proposed to **reduce global warming**.
