### 2. SIMON COMMISSION
#### 2.1 Appointment and Members
- Appointed **November 1927** by **Stanley Baldwin's Conservative** government to study the working of the **1919 Act**.
- **Seven members**, all British, headed by **Sir John Simon** (Liberal Party); **Clement Attlee** a member.
- **No Indian member** → boycotted by the Congress and a section of the Muslim League; slogan "**Simon Go Back**".
#### 2.2 Visits
- Came to India in **1928** to consider administrative reform.
- Two visits: **3 February–31 March 1928** and **11 October 1928–13 April 1929**.
#### 2.3 Lala Lajpat Rai
- Led the **Lahore** protest (1928); injured in a **lathi charge** and died of the injuries.
- He had presided over the **first session of AITUC (1920)**; recalled on 1 May 2026 at the Trade Unions Act centenary.
### 3. SIMON BOYCOTT IN ANDHRA
#### 3.1 Madras Procession – Andhra Kesari

- Police firing killed **Parthasarathi**; Prakasam **bared his chest** and dared the police to shoot ("shoot me") → title "**Andhra Kesari**".

### 4. NEHRU REPORT, 1928
#### 4.1 Origin
- Trigger: **Lord Birkenhead's challenge (1927)** that Indians could not agree on a constitution.
- **All Parties Conference** presided by **Dr M.A. Ansari**; its committee was set up at **Bombay, 19 May 1928**.
- Committee chairman **Motilal Nehru**; secretary **Jawaharlal Nehru**; members included **Tej Bahadur Sapru**, **Ali Imam**, **Subhas Chandra Bose**, **M.S. Aney**, **Mangal Singh**, **Shuaib Qureshi**, **G.R. Pradhan**.
- Subject: **constitutional arrangements** for India; it was also India's reply to the Simon Commission.
- Report submitted at **Lucknow, 28 August 1928**.
!TRAP Ansari **presided the conference**; Motilal **chaired the drafting committee**.
#### 4.2 Recommendations
- **Dominion status**; **end of dyarchy**; responsible government.
- **Bicameral** centre + a **Supreme Court**.
- **Linguistic provinces** (including **Andhra**).
- **Joint electorates + reserved seats** for minorities – not separate electorates.
- **Universal adult suffrage**; **fundamental rights**.
#### 4.3 Reactions
- **Muslim League** rejected it; Jinnah's amendments failed at the **Calcutta All Parties Convention (Dec 1928)**.
- **All Parties Muslim Conference** (Aga Khan) rejected it and demanded separate electorates.
- Radicals **Jawaharlal Nehru** and **Subhas Bose** formed the **Independence for India League**, demanding complete independence.
- Congress gave a **one-year ultimatum** → **Purna Swaraj** at **Lahore (Dec 1929)**.
### 5. MUSLIM PROPOSALS – DELHI PROPOSALS AND FOURTEEN POINTS
| Item | Delhi Proposals | Fourteen Points |
|---|---|---|
| Year | **March 1927** | **March 1929** |
| Session | Muslim leaders' meeting | **Muslim League session, Delhi** |
| Electorates | **Drop separate** electorates for joint + reserved seats | **Keep separate** electorates |
| Other demands | **One-third** central seats; **separate Sindh**; reforms in NWFP and Baluchistan; representation by population in Punjab and Bengal | **Federal** constitution; **residuary powers to provinces**; **one-third** Muslim seats at Centre; safeguards for Muslim-majority provinces |
- Fourteen Points were a **protest against the Nehru Report**.
- All Parties Muslim Conference, **Delhi, 31 Dec 1928–1 Jan 1929**: president **Aga Khan**.
!TRAP Delhi Proposals (1927) = drop separate electorates; Fourteen Points (1929) = keep them.

### 7. SIMON COMMISSION REPORT (1930)
- Published in two volumes, **10 and 24 June 1930**.
- **Abolish dyarchy** → full **provincial autonomy** / responsible government in provinces.
- **Keep separate communal electorates**.
- **Separate Sindh** from Bombay and **Burma** from India.
- Create a new **Orissa** province; **ignored Andhra's demand**.
- Its report became the **base of the GoI Act 1935** (passed **2 August 1935**).
!KEY AP angle: neglect of Andhra + Prakasam's "Andhra Kesari" moment → demand for a separate **Andhra Province**.
### 8. ROUND TABLE CONFERENCES, 1930-32
#### 8.1 Comparison
| RTC | Dates | Congress | Notes / outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1st** | **12 Nov 1930–19 Jan 1931** | **Boycotted** (CDM; leaders in jail) | League, Hindu Mahasabha, princes, Ambedkar, **Maulana Mohammad Ali** attended; **federal structure** agreed → **Gandhi-Irwin Pact** |
| **2nd** | **7 Sep–1 Dec 1931** | **Gandhi sole representative** | **Sarojini Naidu**, **Muhammad Iqbal**, Annie Besant, Malviya, Mahadev Desai, Ambedkar attended; **deadlock on minorities** |
| **3rd** | **17 Nov–24 Dec 1932** | **Absent** (Jinnah too) | Only **46 delegates**; **White Paper** → GoI Act 1935 |
#### 8.2 Key Facts
- All three in **London** under PM **Ramsay MacDonald**; aim: discuss the Simon report and a federal constitution.
- Strictly **one conference in three sessions** (1930-32).
- **Gandhi-Irwin Pact, 5 March 1931** → Gandhi agreed to attend the 2nd RTC.
- Gandhi sailed on **S.S. Rajputana**; stayed at **Kingsley Hall**, London; returned **December 1931** empty-handed.
- Did **not** attend the 2nd RTC: **Jawaharlal Nehru**, Bose, Patel, Azad; **Motilal Nehru** and **C.R. Das** had died before 1931.
- **Ambedkar** and **Tej Bahadur Sapru** attended **all three**.
- After return: Gandhi resumed **CDM**, jailed at **Yerwada** → **Communal Award (Aug 1932)**.
!TRAP **Congress attended only the 2nd RTC**; Jinnah was absent from the 3rd.
