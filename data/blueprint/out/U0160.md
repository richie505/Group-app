## U0160 | FIRST CARNATIC WAR – CAUSES, BATTLE OF ST. THOME, TREATY OF AIX-LA-CHAPELLE
### 1. BELLIGERENTS AND CAUSES
- **First Carnatic War (1746-48)**: between the **English and French East India Companies** (APPSC 2018).
- **European link**: a direct extension of the **War of Austrian Succession**.
- **Immediate cause**: the **English navy seized French ships** → France retaliated.
- France used its **Mauritius fleet** to capture **Madras (1746)**.
### 2. CAPTURE OF MADRAS AND THE DUPLEIX DISPUTE
- The French fleet from Mauritius was led by **La Bourdonnais**; Madras fell in **September 1746**.
- **La Bourdonnais** wanted to **ransom** Madras back to the English; **Dupleix** (Governor at Pondicherry) refused to ratify the deal and kept the town.
!TRAP Madras was captured by **La Bourdonnais**, not Dupleix; the two quarrelled over its ransom.
### 3. NAWAB ANWARUDDIN
| Person | Role |
|---|---|
| **Nizam-ul-Mulk** | Sent Anwaruddin as **guardian** of the minor Nawab **Syed Ali** |
| **Anwaruddin** | Had the minor Nawab killed → **Nawab of Arcot (1744)**; asked both Companies not to fight in his territory; marched to support the **English** against the French |
| **Captain Paradis** | Led the small French force at St. Thome |
| **Mahfuz Khan** | Anwaruddin's son; led the Nawab's army at St. Thome |
- Anwaruddin was later **killed at Ambur (1749)** in the **Second** War.
### 4. BATTLE OF ST. THOME / ADYAR (1746)
- **French (Captain Paradis)** vs **Nawab Anwaruddin's army (Mahfuz Khan)**.
- About **930 French** beat about **10,000**.
- Significance: opened the door to **European interference** in Indian politics.
!NUM 930 French vs 10,000 Indian troops – St. Thome, 1746
### 5. LESSONS OF THE WAR
- A **small, trained European force** (infantry + artillery) could beat far larger Indian **cavalry** armies.
- **Naval power** would decide the Anglo-French contest on the Coromandel coast.
- **Political result: nil**, despite **Dupleix's** victories; both sides now eyed Indian politics.
### 6. TREATY OF AIX-LA-CHAPELLE (1748)
- Ended the **War of Austrian Succession** and the **First Carnatic War**.
- **Madras returned to the English** ↔ **Louisbourg** (North America) given back to the **French**.
- Restored the **pre-war position**.
- UPPSC 2020/2025: "the war ended" and "the English got back Madras" are **both correct**.
!PAIR Madras | to English | India || Louisbourg | to French | North America
### 7. CARNATIC WARS AND THEIR TREATIES
| War | Years | Concluding treaty |
|---|---|---|
| First | 1746-48 | **Aix-la-Chapelle (1748)** – restored pre-war position |
| Second | 1749-54 | **Pondicherry (1754-55)** – Godeheu replaced Dupleix; both sides agreed not to interfere in native rulers' disputes |
| Third | 1756-63 | **Paris (1763)** – ended French political power in India |

- Only exchange: Madras for Louisbourg – not an Indian territory.
