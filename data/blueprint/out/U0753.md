## U0753 | APPLICATIONS OF RADIOISOTOPES IN AGRICULTURE, MEDICINE AND INDUSTRY
### 1. MEDICAL RADIOISOTOPES
| Isotope | Emits | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Cobalt-60** | Powerful gamma | **Cancer radiotherapy**; sterilisation |
| **Iodine-131** | Beta + gamma | **Thyroid** diagnosis and treatment (BRIT supplies it as sodium iodide) |
| **Phosphorus-32** | Beta | **Blood cancer (leukaemia)**, polycythaemia; plant phosphorus tracer |
| **Sodium-24** (radio-sodium) | Gamma | **Speed of blood flow**, circulation blocks, clots; pipeline leaks |
| **Arsenic-74** | Positron | Locating **brain tumours** |
| **Technetium-99m** | Gamma | **Most used diagnostic isotope** |
| Iron-59 | – | Iron metabolism |
| **Gold-198** | – | Cancer treatment (not retinal disorders) |
| Carbon-14 | – | Dating organic material |
!TRAP Leukaemia: both P-32 and Co-60 are accepted in keys.
- **Radioimmunoassay (RIA)**: measures tiny amounts of hormones, enzymes, drugs in blood.
### 2. RADIATION THERAPY AND IMAGING
- **Teletherapy** = external beam (**Co-60**); **brachytherapy** = sealed source inside/next to the tumour (**Ir-192, I-125**).
- **Radon** = radioactive **noble gas** used to treat cancer (APPSC G1 2018).
- **Electron beam therapy**: for skin lymphoma; spot treatment and total skin therapy.
- **PET** = positron emitters, mainly **F-18 FDG**; **SPECT** = single gamma emitters, mainly **Tc-99m**.
- **30 MeV Medical Cyclotron, Kolkata**: produces F-18 FDG commercially.
- **Tata Memorial Centre**: Anchor Centre for IAEA's **'Rays of Hope'**.
### 3. RADON AND RADIATION HAZARDS
- Radon: **alpha emitter** from radium decay; long inhalation causes **lung cancer**.
- Sources: soil gas (main), **building materials** (granite, brick, concrete), **uranium mill tailings**.
### 4. UNITS AND SAFETY
| Quantity | Unit |
|---|---|
| Activity | **Becquerel** (1 decay/s) |
| Absorbed dose | **Gray** (1 J/kg) |
| Biological effect | **Sievert** |
- **Half-life** = time for half the nuclei to decay.
- AERB limits: **20 mSv/yr** (5-year average), 30 mSv max in a year, 100 mSv cumulative; **ALARA**.
### 5. INSTITUTIONS
- **BRIT** (Board of Radiation and Isotope Technology): **industrial unit of DAE**, Vashi, Navi Mumbai; works in **isotope technology** (APPSC GS 2012). Supplies radiopharmaceuticals, RIA kits, sealed sources.
- **BARC**: AEET **1954**, renamed **1967**; India's **leading radioisotope producer**. Dhruva = largest research reactor; Apsara = Asia's first; KAMINI = only U-233.
- Isotopes made at **Trombay reactors, Kolkata accelerator and NPCIL plants** (all three).
| Facility | Fact |
|---|---|
| **KRUSHAK** | **Lasalgaon (Nashik)** – onion irradiation |
| **ISOMED 2.0** | Completed **May 2025**; gamma irradiator for healthcare products |
### 6. RADIOMETRIC DATING
| Method | Dates | Half-life |
|---|---|---|
| **Carbon-14** | Once-living remains (fossils, wood, bones, shell) up to ~50,000 years | ~**5,730 years** |
| **Uranium-238 → Lead-206** | Most ancient rocks, **age of the Earth** | ~4.5 billion years |
| **Potassium-40 → Argon-40** | Volcanic rocks, fossil-bearing layers | ~1.25 billion years |
- Radioactive dating = main source of **absolute age** of rocks. **Clair Cameron Patterson** pioneered uranium-lead dating.
### 7. RADIOISOTOPE THERMOELECTRIC GENERATOR (RTG)
- Turns **decay heat into electricity** via thermocouples (**Seebeck effect**); **not a fission reactor**; no moving parts.
- Fuel: **Plutonium-238** (weapons by-product), not Pu-239.
- Missions: **Voyager, Cassini, Curiosity, Perseverance**.
### 8. AGRICULTURE AND FOOD
- **Mutation breeding**: **L. J. Stadler (1928)**, X-rays on **barley**; later gamma and neutron radiation.
- **72** BARC Trombay varieties; **TBM-9 = banana** (with NRCB Trichy); **RTS-43 = sorghum**.
- **Sterile insect technique**: gamma-sterilised males mate with wild females, no viable offspring.
- Nuclear **moisture gauges** for irrigation; BARC tissue culture (banana, pineapple).
- **Food irradiation**: radiolysis of water; **Co-60 gamma, X-rays or electron beams** (not Co-60 only); packed or bulk food; stops sprouting, kills pests. **Radura** = green symbol for irradiated food.
!TRAP Increasing genetic variety of crops is mutation breeding, not food irradiation.
### 9. INDUSTRY
- **Gamma radiography** (NDT of welds, castings): **Ir-192, Co-60**.
- Thickness gauges; **smoke detectors = Americium-241** (alpha emitter).
- **Radiotracers**: pipeline leaks; port sediment tracking cuts desilting frequency.
