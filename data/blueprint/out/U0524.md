## U0524 | RESERVE BANK OF INDIA – ESTABLISHMENT, ORGANISATION AND FUNCTIONS
### 1. ESTABLISHMENT OF RBI
#### 1.1 Basics
- RBI = the **central bank** of India; a **statutory body** under the **RBI Act, 1934**; called the **Banker's Bank**.
- Set up on the recommendation of the **Hilton Young Commission** (Royal Commission on Indian Currency and Finance, **1926**); **Ambedkar's 1925** evidence to it shaped the concept.
#### 1.2 Timeline
| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Hilton Young Commission recommends a central bank | 1926 |
| RBI Act passed | **1934** |
| RBI starts functioning (private shareholders' bank, Central Office **Calcutta**) | **1 April 1935** |
| Central Office moves Calcutta → **Bombay** (permanent) | **1937** |
| Nationalised under RBI (Transfer to Public Ownership) Act, **1948** | **1 January 1949** |
| 85th Foundation Day | 1 April 2020 |
| RBI@90 | 1 April 2024 |
!TRAP Act **1934** → functioning **1935** → nationalised **1949** (Act of 1948). RBI was established in 1935, not 1949; nationalised in 1949, not 1937.
- **Seal**: **tiger and palm tree** (tiger replaced the lion of the East India Company double mohur).
- **Accounting year**: January–December (1935) → **July–June** (from 1940) → **April–March** from **2020-21** (transition year of nine months, July 2020–March 2021).
### 2. ORGANISATION AND GOVERNANCE
#### 2.1 Central Board and Governors
- **Central Board of Directors**: **21** members, appointed by the Government of India under the RBI Act; directors hold office **4 years**.
- **Governor and Deputy Governors**: appointed by the **Government of India** (ACC approves); tenure **up to 5 years**.
- **Four Deputy Governors**: two from within RBI + one commercial banker + one **economist** (heads monetary policy). **Rohit Jain** appointed May 2026 for 3 years, replacing T. Rabi Sankar.
- Headquarters: **Mumbai** ("Mint Street"); offices in 31 locations. Regional representations (4): New Delhi, Chennai, Kolkata, Mumbai.

### 3. CENTRAL BANKING FUNCTIONS
#### 3.1 Functions and Legal Basis
| Function | Legal basis / content |
|---|---|
| **Bank of issue** | **Sec 22**: sole right to issue notes **except the ₹1 note**; exchanges and destroys unfit notes |
| **Banker to Government** | **Sec 20** (Centre), **Sec 21/21A** (States, by agreement); public debt, **Ways and Means Advances**, overdrafts |
| **Banker's bank** | Keeps SCBs' deposits and reserves; **lender of last resort** (emergency liquidity) |
| **Monetary authority** | Price stability, growth in mind; **six-member MPC** sets repo |
| **Custodian of forex** | Manages forex reserves under **FEMA 1999**; represents India at IMF, World Bank |
| **Controller of credit** | Controls money and credit (qualitative and quantitative measures); controls **credit creation** by commercial banks |
| **Fiscal agent and adviser** | Agent of Government for India's **IMF membership**; adviser in monetary and financial matters |
| Regulator | Banks, NBFCs, AIFIs, HFCs (since 2019); payments under **PSS Act, 2007**; regulates **ECBs** |
- **Public Debt Office** functions under RBI; Public Debt Management Cell eases the debt-vs-monetary-policy conflict.
- RBI is banker to **all State Governments except Sikkim** (Sikkim: limited agreement for public debt only).
- **Bank Rate**: rate at which RBI lends to / rediscounts bills of commercial banks. Repo rate is set by RBI.
#### 3.2 What RBI Does NOT Do
- Accept deposits from or **lend to the public**; **mint coins**; issue the **₹1 note**.
- **Credit creation** (done by commercial banks); **regulation of foreign trade**; income tax collection.
- Does not fix the **PLR** (banks' Prime Lending Rate) or savings account rates (deregulated **25 Oct 2011**).
- Does not regulate securities (**SEBI**), insurance (**IRDAI**), pensions (**PFRDA**).
!KEY Monetary policy = RBI (MPC since 2016); **fiscal policy = Ministry of Finance**; inflation target set by **Finance Ministry in consultation with RBI**.
### 4. CURRENCY MANAGEMENT
#### 4.1 Issuer
| Item | Issued by | Signed by | Legal tender |
|---|---|---|---|
| ₹1 note | **Government of India** (Ministry of Finance) | **Finance Secretary** | – |
| All other notes | **RBI** | **RBI Governor** | **Unlimited** |
| Coins | Government of India (RBI only puts them into circulation) | – | **Limited** (issued up to ₹1,000, Coinage Act) |
#### 4.2 Reserve Backing
| System | Rule |
|---|---|
| Proportional Reserve System (till 1956) | 2/5 of backing in gold or sterling; gold at least ₹40 crore |
| Minimum Reserve System (**1956**) | **₹515 crore** = ₹400 crore foreign securities + **₹115 crore gold** |
| Amended **1957** | **₹200 crore** of gold and forex, of which **₹115 crore gold** |
- Beyond that floor RBI can issue currency **without limit**.
- Assets backing notes (**Sec 33**): gold coin, bullion, rupee coin, GoI rupee securities, foreign securities, held in the Issue Department.
#### 4.3 Denominations and Demonetisation
- Highest denomination permitted: **₹10,000** (**Sec 24**); first printed **1938**, demonetised **1946**, revived **1954**, demonetised **1978**.
- Demonetisation power: **Sec 26(2)**.
| Year | Notes |
|---|---|
| 1946 | High-denomination notes |
| 1978 | ₹1,000, ₹5,000, ₹10,000 (HDBN Demonetisation Act) |
| **8 Nov 2016** | ₹500, ₹1,000; SC upheld **4:1** (2023) |
- **₹2,000** note withdrawn **May 2023**, still legal tender.
#### 4.4 Note Features
- **17 languages**: Hindi and English in front; **language panel of 15** at the back.
- **MANI** (Mobile Aided Note Identifier) app for visually impaired (RBI, 2020).

### 6. REGULATORY AND SUPERVISORY ROLE
#### 6.1 Legal Basis
| Act / Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| **BR Act, 1949** | Licensing, branches, management, **amalgamation**, SLR, audit, inspection |
| BR Act **Sec 22(1)** | Licensing of payments banks (e.g. IPPB, 2018) |
| BR Act **Sec 26A** | **DEA Fund** (2014): deposits unclaimed **10+ years**; depositor's right survives |
| RBI Act **Chapter IIIB** | **NBFC** regulation |
- No bank or NBFC may open without an **RBI licence**; on-tap licensing of UCBs to resume after 20+ years; 1,457 UCBs (Aug 2026).
- **Branch expansion**: banks, SFBs, payments banks, LABs, RRBs may open branches without prior approval if **25%** of new branches are in **unbanked rural** areas (Aug 2026).
- **Mergers** approved by RBI; RBI can seek **winding up** of a bank.
#### 6.2 Boards and Committees
| Body | Year | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Board for Financial Supervision** | **1994** | Integrated supervision of banks, FIs, NBFCs |
| **Payments Regulatory Board** | Oct 2025; first meeting Jan 2026 | **Six** members, Governor chairs, 3 Government nominees; replaced **BPSS**; powers from PSS Act 2007 |
| Tarapore Committee | 1997; 2006 | Capital account convertibility |
| **Inter-disciplinary Standing Committee** | – | **Cyber security** (APPSC 2019) |
| RB-IOS 2026 | **1 July 2026** | "**One Nation One Ombudsman**": banks, RRBs, UCBs, NBFCs, payment operators; no cap on amount |
### 7. RBI AUTONOMY – KEY SECTIONS
| Section (RBI Act) | Provision |
|---|---|
| **7** | Centre may give **directions** in public interest **after consulting the Governor**; 2018 consultations → autonomy debate |
| 22 | Sole right of note issue |
| 24 | Highest denomination ₹10,000 |
| 26(2) | Demonetisation |
| **30** | Centre can **supersede the Central Board** |
| **47** | Net profit after provisions → **Central Government** |
- Government appoints Governor, Deputy Governors and Board; MPC + inflation-targeting law (**2016**) gives operational independence.
- **Finance Commission** is formed by the President under **Article 280** (not RBI).
### 8. RBI INCOME AND SURPLUS TRANSFER
- Income: interest on domestic and foreign securities, loans to Governments and banks, **forex gains**, fees (buying/selling G-bonds and foreign currency).
- **Bimal Jalan Committee** (Economic Capital Framework, **2018**): separated realised equity from revaluation balances; **Contingent Risk Buffer** 5.5–6.5% → **4.5–7.5%** (revised 2025).
- Surplus **FY 2025-26: record ₹2.86 lakh crore**, 6.7% above FY25 (₹2.6+ lakh crore).
- Liquidity: surplus about ₹10.32 lakh crore (3 Sep 2026); VRRR absorbed ₹6.02 lakh crore after FCNR(B) inflows of $136.38 bn.
### 9. DEVELOPMENTAL ROLE
- Helped set up **IDBI, NABARD, SIDBI, EXIM Bank, NHB** (ownership later moved to Government).
- Data-governance guidance for banks (Jul 2026).
