### 1. INDIAN SPACE PROGRAMME - GAGANYAAN, ISRO AND NAVIC
#### 1.1 Gaganyaan - India's First Human Spaceflight
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Crew and orbit | **3 astronauts** to **LEO at about 400 km** for **up to 3 days** |
| Launcher | **Human-rated LVM3** (GSLV Mk III) - solid, liquid and cryogenic stages (**not PSLV**) |

#### 2.4 Space Security and Space Law
- **Dual-use satellites** - civilian satellites also usable for military purposes (e.g. GPS). **Orbital dependency** - reliance on foreign satellite infrastructure.
- **Jamming** = **blocking** signals; **Spoofing** = sending **false** signals (e.g. fake GPS positions).
- **KA-SAT cyberattack (2022)** - on the **Viasat** network; disrupted internet across **Europe**.
- **CERT-In space cybersecurity guidelines 2026** - **"secure-by-design"** across the satellite lifecycle.
| Instrument | Key point |
|---|---|
| **Outer Space Treaty, 1967** | Foundation of space law; peaceful use, no WMD in orbit; **states responsible for all national space activities, including private entities**; space = global commons; **silent on cyber operations** |
| Liability Convention, 1972 | Compensation for damage by space objects - **absolute** liability on Earth/aircraft; **fault-based** in outer space |
| IADC, UN COPUOS guidelines | Debris mitigation - **non-binding, voluntary** |
- **Kessler Syndrome** - chain reaction of debris collisions making orbits unusable.
- In LEO only objects **larger than 10 cm** are regularly tracked; **25-year de-orbit rule** (weak compliance monitoring).

### 6. NUCLEAR ENERGY - PFBR, SHANTI ACT AND TARAPUR
#### 6.1 Kalpakkam PFBR Attains Criticality
- **Prototype Fast Breeder Reactor (PFBR), Kalpakkam** (Tamil Nadu) attained criticality - **India enters Stage II** of its three-stage nuclear programme; PM Modi called it historic.
- **500 MW**, **sodium-cooled**; built by **BHAVINI** (Bharatiya Nabhikiya Vidyut Nigam Ltd, under **DAE**); 200+ Indian industries took part.
- **Criticality** = self-sustaining chain reaction, **k = 1** (subcritical k < 1, supercritical k > 1). Chain reaction idea - **Leo Szilard (1933)**.
- **Fast breeder reactor**: produces **more fissile fuel than it consumes**; **fast neutrons, no moderator**; **plutonium-based MOX fuel** (Pu + U); U-238 blanket -> Pu-239; **liquid sodium** coolant (low dissolved oxygen for corrosion resistance).
| Stage (Homi J. Bhabha) | Reactor | Fuel |
|---|---|---|
| I | **PHWRs** | **Natural uranium** -> Pu-239 by-product |
| II | **FBRs** | **Pu-based MOX** |
| III | **Thorium reactors** | **Th-232 -> U-233** |
- Plants: Tarapur, Rajasthan, Kalpakkam, Narora, Kakrapar, Kaiga, Kudankulam. **Kalpakkam has MAPS and IGCAR**.
#### 6.2 SHANTI Act, 2025
- Full form: **Sustainable Harnessing and Advancement of Nuclear Energy for Transforming India**.
- **Replaces** the **Atomic Energy Act, 1962** and the **Civil Liability for Nuclear Damage (CLND) Act, 2010**.
- **Private companies, JVs and foreign entities** can **build, own, operate and decommission** plants - ends the **NPCIL monopoly**.
- **Operator solely liable**; **supplier liability removed**; operator liability graded by capacity; government liability capped.
- Gives **statutory (legal) status to the Atomic Energy Regulatory Board (AERB)**.
- Target: nuclear capacity from about **8,180 MW to 100 GW by 2047** (over twelvefold); investment over **$200 billion**; nuclear share in power about 3%.
- Installed electricity capacity (June 2025): ~476 GW, ~50% non-fossil.
#### 6.3 Tarapur
- **Tarapur Units 1 and 2 (TAPS)**, near Boisar, Palghar - **boiling water reactors (BWRs)**, not PHWRs; commissioned **1969**; **India's first commercial reactors**.
- **AERB approved the restart of Unit-2 on 7 May 2026** for 10 more years (after NPCIL refurbishment); Unit-1 extended Dec 2025 (160 MWe).
- AERB: est. **1983**, Mumbai, Chairman A.K. Balasubramanian; NPCIL: est. **1987**, CMD Bhuvan Chandra Pathak; DAE: est. **1954**.
!TRAP Tarapur 1 and 2 are BWRs (not PHWRs); Stage I of the programme uses PHWRs.

#### 7.2 Quantum and AI Terms
- **Quantum entanglement**: shared quantum state - **measuring one particle's property (momentum, spin) instantly fixes its partner's, at any distance**. Earlier shown mainly in **photons and electrons**; recently in heavier atoms like **Helium**. **Nonlocality** is a quantum feature; classical physics is based on locality.
- **Anthropic's Mythos** - AI model that can autonomously find and exploit software vulnerabilities; not public; shared selectively with critical-infrastructure organisations under **Project Glasswing**.
- **Zero-day vulnerability** - flaw **unknown to developers, no patch** (not already patched).
- **LLMs** use the **transformer** architecture; **temperature** controls randomness; **tokenization** splits text into words/subwords/characters (it does **not** decide the architecture); loss function measures prediction error.
- Other terms: adversarial attacks, data poisoning, **XAI = Explainable AI**, EDR systems, deepfakes.
- **Neuro-Symbolic AI** - hybrid of neural networks (patterns) + symbolic reasoning (rules, knowledge graphs) -> explainable answers, fewer hallucinations. Examples: **PrahelikaAI - IIT Kharagpur** (puzzle-based reasoning tutor in Hindi, Bengali); CREST framework, PAL (Personal Adaptive Learner), C3AN (offline edge AI). Only **47%** of rural schools have functional computers; ~1.4 million schools; 22 scheduled languages.
- **Anthropic study - "Observed Exposure"**: **actual workplace use** of AI (not theoretical capability); data from ~800 US occupations and Claude usage. **Most exposed**: computer programmers, financial analysts, customer service, data analysts, **legal**, office administration. **Least exposed**: **construction, agriculture, protective services, personal care**. LLMs could theoretically do ~94% of computer/maths tasks, actual use ~33%; entry of 22-25-year-olds into exposed jobs down ~14% since 2022; exposed workers 54.4% female, average age ~42.9. **India: AI could cut IT-services revenue by 9-12% over four years** (Nifty IT fell).
