## U0367 | LIBERALISATION, PRIVATISATION AND GLOBALISATION – THE 1991 REFORMS
### 1. THE 1991 CRISIS AND NEW ECONOMIC POLICY
- Trigger: **NRI deposit flight**; forex reserves fell to **$975 million (June 1991)** = about **two weeks of imports**.
- **67 tonnes of gold** pledged with **Bank of England and UBS**.
- **IMF** approached for about **$7 billion** with **structural-adjustment conditions**.
- NEP (LPG) announced on **24 July 1991**: PM **P.V. Narasimha Rao**, FM **Dr. Manmohan Singh**.
#### 1.1 Stabilisation vs Structural Adjustment
| Aspect | Stabilisation | Structural adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| Time frame | Short-term | Medium/long-term |
| Aim | BoP, inflation, fiscal deficit | Efficiency, competitiveness |
| Measures | Devaluation, deficit cuts | Delicensing, trade, financial, tax, PSU reforms |
!TRAP Devaluation = stabilisation; delicensing = structural adjustment.
### 2. LPG TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Liberalisation | Removing controls: licences, quotas, entry curbs |
| Privatisation | Government leaves ownership/management, incl. **outright sale** of PSUs |
| Disinvestment | Sale of **part of PSU equity** to the public; above **51%** private stake transfers control |
| Globalisation | Joining the world economy; the **outcome** (not precondition) of L and P |
- **Navratnas**: nine PSUs given autonomy in **1997**; **Miniratnas: 97**.
- **New PSE Policy for Atmanirbhar Bharat (2021)**: favours **strategic disinvestment**.
### 3. 1991 MEASURES – PART OF LPG OR NOT
| Measure | Part of 1991 LPG? |
|---|---|
| Industrial de-licensing | Yes; licensing kept only for alcohol, cigarettes, hazardous chemicals, industrial explosives, electronic aerospace and defence, drugs |
| FDI automatic route | Yes; up to **51% in 34 priority industries**; later 100% in hotels, infrastructure, software |
| Current account convertibility | Yes, **1994** |
| Capital account convertibility | **No**; never fully adopted (Tarapore) |
| FERA → FEMA | **1999** |
### 4. INDUSTRIAL POLICIES – YEAR AND THRUST
| Year | Thrust |
|---|---|
| 1948 IPR | Mixed economy; four categories |
| 1956 IPR | Schedule A (17 public), **B (12 mixed)**, C (private) |
| 1977 IPS (Janata) | Small/cottage industries; **District Industries Centres** |
| 1980 IPS | Technology upgrading, export orientation, backward areas; MRTP firms **25% expansion** |
| 1991 IPS | De-licensing, MRTP restructured, FDI, PSU autonomy via **MoUs**; did not expand licensing |
### 5. LICENCE RAJ AND MRTP
- **MRTP Act 1969**; relaxed 1980; in 1991 asset limits and pre-entry curbs on expansion, mergers and acquisitions abolished.
- Replaced by the **Competition Act, 2002 (CCI)**.

### 7. TRADE REFORMS AND SEQUENCING
- Rupee devalued about **20% on 1 and 3 July 1991** → market-determined rupee.
- Import licensing abolished except hazardous/environmentally sensitive items.
| Generation | Content |
|---|---|
| First (1991-2000) | Trade and industrial delicensing; tariffs **~125% → ~30%**; FDI; financial reform |
| Second (2000s) | **Factor markets (labour, land, power)**, fiscal rules, disinvestment, governance |
- **Washington Consensus**: **John Williamson, 1989**; 10 prescriptions (fiscal discipline, tax reform, trade and FDI liberalisation, privatisation, deregulation, property rights).

### 10. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS

2. Factor-market reforms are **second-generation**.
3. Capital account convertibility was **not** part of LPG.
4. MRTP 1969 → **Competition Act 2002**.
5. Navratna = **1997**, not 1996.
6. Devaluation is **stabilisation**.
7. FERA → FEMA in **1999**, not 1991.
