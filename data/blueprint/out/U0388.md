## U0388 | SOCIAL STRATIFICATION AND MOBILITY IN INDIA
### 1. STRATIFICATION – MEANING, BASES, FEATURES
- **Stratification**: ranking of groups by unequal **wealth, prestige, power**.
- Bases: **caste, class, gender, age, race, ethnicity**; traditional – caste, varna, tribe, religion; modern – education, occupation, income, **political power, urbanisation**.
- **Tumin**: social, **ancient, universal**, diverse, **consequential** (decides life chances).
- Muslims: **Ashraf, Ajlaf, Arzal**.
!TRAP **Language is NOT** a basis of stratification (APPSC 2019).
### 2. THEORIES
| Approach | Thinker | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| Functionalist | **Davis and Moore** (1945) | Unequal rewards pull scarce talent into key posts |
| Critique | **Melvin Tumin** | Wastes talent; passes inequality on |

### 3. CASTE VS CLASS AND OTHER SYSTEMS
| System | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Caste** | Birth, endogamy, inherited occupation; **closed**; cumulative inequality |
| **Class** | Education, wealth, occupation; **open**; dispersed inequality |
| **Estate** | Medieval Europe – clergy, nobility, commoners |
| **Slavery** | Person as property – Greece, Rome |
- **Varna** = four-fold, theoretical; **Jati** = real, local birth groups; ranking by **ritual purity**.
- Harappa: citadel vs lower town, **no rigid caste**; varna hardened in **Later Vedic** period.
- Caste is vertical (ranking by birth) and horizontal (separate groups).
- **A.W. Green**: caste ideally allows **no mobility up or down**; **Ghurye**: hierarchy, endogamy, restrictions (*Caste and Race in India*).
- **Caste status**: fixed by birth, linked to occupation. Hereditary occupations: **potter, blacksmith, weaver**; also kingship, property; weakened by education, industrialisation, urbanisation, **occupational mobility**.
- Today: conflicts over **power, not ritual**; **Jajmani** weakened; dominance on secular factors.
### 4. SOCIAL MOBILITY
| Type | Meaning / example |
|---|---|
| **Vertical** | Up/down; farmer's son → IAS |
| **Horizontal** | Same level; clerk to equal job |
| **Inter-generational** | Parents → children; labourer's son → engineer |
| **Intra-generational** | Within one lifetime |
| **Structural** | Change in occupational structure |
- **Sorokin**, *Social Mobility* (**1927**): first set out vertical and horizontal mobility.
- **Ascribed** (caste, gender, family) vs **achieved** (IAS officer, doctor, scientist).
- Group mobility: **Sanskritisation** and **dominant caste** (**Srinivas**: numbers + arable land + fairly high ritual rank); **Kshatriyaisation** (**Kulke**) – Marathas, Jats, Yadavs; reproduces hierarchy.
### 5. AGRARIAN CLASSES
- **Daniel Thorner**: by land rights and income – **Maliks** (landlords on rent), **Kisans** (own, family labour), **Mazdoors** (tenants, sharecroppers, landless).
- **Andre Beteille**: *Caste, Class and Power* (**1965**), **Sripuram, Thanjavur**; caste, class, power **diverge**; *Studies in Agrarian Social Structure*.
### 6. MIDDLE CLASS
- **Macaulay's Minute, 1835** → English-educated middle class (**A.R. Desai**, *Social Background of Indian Nationalism*).
- World Bank **$10-50**/day (PPP); NCAER **₹2-10 lakh** (2001-02); PRICE **₹5-30 lakh**.
- **43.2 crore (31%) in 2021 → 61% by 2047**; OECD: world's largest by **2035**.
### 7. MOBILITY ROUTES, DALITS AND OBCs
- **ES 2025-26**: drivers – **health, education, technology, institutions**; poverty **21.9% (2011-12) → 2.3% (2023-24)**.
- Reservation: **Periyar's Self-Respect Movement**; group mobility.
- **Ezhavas** (Kerala): toddy tapping → tailoring.
- Cities weaken caste; migrants keep networks.
- **Dalits**: state policy, reform movements, Sanskritisation/Westernisation.
- **Mandal**: OBCs **over 52%**; **11 indicators = 4 social + 4 economic + 3 educational**.
- **Telangana survey**: **242 castes**; **135 (67%)** above average backwardness.
- **Marathas, Gujjars, Patels** seek OBC status. AP: **Kakatiya** society relatively open (**Velamas, Kammas, Reddys**); **Virashaivism** a "lubricant" of mobility.
