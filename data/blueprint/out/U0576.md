## U0576 | AP SOCIO-ECONOMIC SURVEY – KEY INDICATORS OF THE LATEST EDITION
### 1. THE SURVEY
- Prepared by the **Planning Department**, Govt of AP; by custom **placed before the State Budget** (like the Union Economic Survey). **Not tabled in the Feb 2026 Budget session**.
- **SES 2025-26**: 11 chapters, last = **Data-Driven Governance**; growth figures are **First Advance Estimates (FAE)**.
- **"Progress Monitoring – KPIs and SDGs"** = last chapter of **SES 2024-25**, not of 2025-26.
- **Swarna Andhra @2047** (13 Dec 2024): GSDP target **₹308.11 lakh crore by 2047-48**; three economic regions **Visakhapatnam, Amaravati, Tirupati**.

### 6. AGRICULTURE, PRICES, JOBS
- **Foodgrains 2025-26 (2nd AE): 190.29 lakh t** (kharif 110.00 + rabi 80.29; +17.03%).
- **Fish: ~29% of India** (leading State); 47.28 lakh t up to Dec 2025. **Rayalaseema = Horticulture Hub**.
- **CPI-IW** Apr–Oct 2025: **AP 1.86%**, India 2.82%; Combined CPI index AP 200.6 vs 195.9.
- PLFS 2023-24: **unemployment 4.1%**, **LFPR 47.5%**, **WPR 45.6%**; youth 17.5%. Ages 15–59: 4.4%, LFPR 67.3%.
- Fiscal: revenue deficit 2025-26 RE **₹41,118 cr (2.33% of GSDP)**, fiscal deficit 4.57%; FM expects ~**₹48,000 cr** after 16th FC ended RD grants.
