## U0169 | ANDHRA REGION UNDER THE COMPANY – MACHILIPATNAM, CEDED DISTRICTS, EARLY ADMINISTRATION AND REVENUE
### 1. HOW ANDHRA CAME UNDER THE COMPANY – STAGES
- Today's Andhra took shape in four stages: **Northern Circars (1766)** → **Guntur (1788)** → **Ceded Districts (1800)** → **Nellore–Chittoor (1801)**.
- **Telangana** stayed under the **Nizam**; the coast and Rayalaseema passed to the Company.
#### 1.1 Stages of acquisition
| Region | Year | From whom | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| Machilipatnam and nearby parganas | **1759** | **Salabat Jung** | Grant after the French lost the coast |
| **Northern Circars** (Guntur excepted) | **1765–66** | Mughal **Shah Alam II** (firman 1765); Nizam Ali (lease 1766) | Firman to **Clive**, then lease |
| **Guntur** Circar | **1788** | Nizam | Handed over on payment of **7 lakh** |
| **Ceded Districts** | **1800** | Nizam | In lieu of the **Subsidiary Alliance** subsidy |
| **Carnatic districts** (Nellore, Chittoor) | **1801** | Nawab **Azim-ud-Daula** | **Carnatic Treaty** |
- **Nellore–Chittoor (1801)** was the **last** part of Andhra to come under Company rule; in 1801 Andhra was merged into the **Madras Presidency**.
!KEY Masulipatnam was a Company station, not one of the five Circars.
### 2. NORTHERN CIRCARS AND THE NIZAM
#### 2.1 The five Circars
| Circar | Modern area |
|---|---|
| **Chicacole** | Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam |
| **Rajahmundry** | East and West Godavari |
| **Ellore** | West Godavari / Eluru |
| **Mustafanagar** (Kondapalli) | Krishna |
| **Guntur** (Murtazanagar) | Guntur |
- **Mughal grant:** firman of **Shah Alam II** secured by **Robert Clive**, dated **1765**.
- The Nizam at first refused to accept it; the Company then negotiated a **lease** with him in **1766**.
#### 2.2 Kandregula Jogipantulu – the intermediary
- **Kandregula (Kondregula) Jogipantulu**: famous **dubashi** (interpreter-negotiator) and British envoy at **Nizam Ali's** court.
- Acted between the Nizam and the Company's men at **Machilipatnam** (APPSC 2019).
- The Nizam was at **Rajamahendravaram**; his army at **Gollapudi mutinied** over unpaid pay.
- Jogipantulu used the **Gollapudi mutiny** to get the Circars leased for **15 lakh rupees a year (1766)**.
#### 2.3 Treaties with the Nizam
| Treaty / grant | Year | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| Salabat Jung's grant | 1759 | Machilipatnam and nearby parganas to the British |
| Circar lease (Jogipantulu) | 1766 | **15 lakh** a year |
| **Treaty of Masulipatnam** | **1768** | Company keeps the Circars for a reduced **peshkash of 9 lakh** a year + military help to the Nizam |
| Guntur settlement | **1788** | **7 lakh** paid; Guntur handed over |
| **Treaty of Hyderabad** (Subsidiary Alliance) | **1798** | Nizam keeps a British subsidiary force at his own cost |
| Cession | **1800** | Ceded Districts given because he could not pay the subsidy in cash |
- The **Nizam of Hyderabad** was the **first Indian ruler to accept Wellesley's Subsidiary Alliance** (APPSC 2002).
- **Baji Rao II** accepted it in **1802** (Treaty of Bassein); **Tipu refused**.
!NUM 15 lakh = 1766 lease; 9 lakh = 1768 peshkash; 7 lakh = Guntur 1788
#### 2.4 Guntur Circar – why it came late
- Guntur was **excepted** from the 1766 lease and stayed out until **1788**, when it was handed over against **7 lakh**.
### 3. CEDED DISTRICTS AND RAYALASEEMA
#### 3.1 Region and background
- **Ceded Districts (1800):** **Kadapa (Cuddapah), Kurnool, Anantapur** (now AP) + **Bellary** (now Karnataka) = today's **Rayalaseema** (APPSC GS 2024).
- **Hyder Ali and Tipu Sultan** had taken these lands from Hyderabad.
- **Nizam Ali** regained them as a British ally in the **Mysore wars of 1790–92 and 1799**.
- Under the **Subsidiary Alliance (1798)** he could not pay in cash → ceded them in **1800**.
#### 3.2 Headquarters
- **1800–1807:** a single collectorate under **Munro**, with headquarters at **Anantapur**.
- **1808:** split into two collectorates – **Bellary** and **Cuddapah**.
- APPSC 2011 key: headquarters = **Bellary** (the seat of one of the two districts after 1808).
#### 3.3 Names
| Name | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Dattamandalam / Dattamandalalu** | Old name, "ceded lands"; used until 1928 |
| **Rayalaseema** | Suggested by **Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao** (APPSC G2 2012 key); adopted at the **Andhra Mahasabha, Nandyal, November 1928**, presided over by **S. Radhakrishnan**; the resolution was moved by **Chilukuri Narayana Rao** |
!TRAP Rayalaseema name year = **1928**, not 1927; place = **Nandyal**.
### 4. CARNATIC DISTRICTS, 1801
- Covers **Nellore**, the **Prakasam** area and part of **Chittoor**.
- Earlier under **Aravidu Sriranga Raya III**, then part of the **Carnatic**.
- After the **Second Carnatic War** the British made **Muhammad Ali** Nawab of the Carnatic (secure only after **1763**).
- After Muhammad Ali's death the Company annexed these districts in **1801** by the **Carnatic Treaty** with Nawab **Azim-ud-Daula**.
### 5. ZAMINDARIS AND THE BATTLES
#### 5.1 Zamindaris → family
| Zamindari | Family / feature |
|---|---|
| **Vizianagaram** | **Pusapati** (Kshatriya Raju); Circars zamindari under the Company |
| **Bobbili** | **Velama**; rival of Vizianagaram; destroyed in 1757 |
| **Venkatagiri** (Nellore) | **Velama** – **Velugoti** family |
| **Peddapuram** | **Vatsavayi (Vatsavai)** dynasty – ruled about three centuries (APPSC GS 2022) |
- Heartland: the **Northern Circars**; hereditary families = **Velamas, Reddis, Kshatriya Rajus**.
- **Status:** Vizianagaram, Bobbili, Pithapuram = old estates brought under the **Permanent Settlement (1802)** → zamindars became **fixed-revenue holders**.
- **Gajapati Vijayarama** belonged to the Vizianagaram kingdom (APPSC GS 2022).
#### 5.2 Battle of Bobbili (24 Jan 1757)
- **Vijayaramaraju of Vizianagaram** + the **French under Bussy** destroyed Bobbili.
- **Bobbili Ranga Rao** died in the battle.
- **Tandra Papayya** (Ranga Rao's brother-in-law) later killed Vijayaramaraju to avenge Bobbili.
#### 5.3 Battle of Padmanabham (10 July 1794)
- The British defeated and killed **Chinna Vijayaramaraju** of Vizianagaram.
- His minor son **Narayana Babu** was made zamindar; **George Russell** took charge of the forts (Russell was **not** Governor of Madras).
!PAIR Bobbili | 1757 | Vijayaramaraju + French || Padmanabham | 1794 | Chinna Vijayaramaraju killed by British
### 6. COMPANY OFFICIALS AND THE POLIGARS
#### 6.1 Sir Thomas Munro (1761–1827)
- **Read and Munro** – **Baramahal** experiment, **1792** (now Salem/Krishnagiri, Tamil Nadu).
- **First Principal Collector of the Ceded Districts (1800)**; sub-collectors at **Harpanahalli, Kadapa, Adoni, Kurnool**.
- **Suppressed the palegars (poligars)** of Rayalaseema and abolished revenue **auctions** there.
- Introduced **Ryotwari** (from the Bara Mahals) in the Ceded Districts (**1802**); surveyed the districts **1802–07**.
- Telugu-speaking Collector who needed no interpreters; remembered locally.
- **Governor of Madras 1820–27**; died at **Pattikonda (Kurnool)** on **6 July 1827**.
#### 6.2 Others
| Official | Post / work |
|---|---|
| **Sir Arthur Cotton** | Irrigation engineer; **Dowleswaram anicut across the Godavari (1847–52)**; designed the **Krishna anicut at Vijayawada (1855)**, executed by Captain Charles Orr |
| **V. (Veenam / Veneti) Veeranna** | Andhra officer who helped Cotton build the **Godavari barrage** (APPSC G2 2019 key); gave the delta sure irrigation |
| **C.P. Brown** | **First Telugu-English dictionary**; published **Vemana's verses**; learnt Telugu thoroughly |
| **G.N. Taylor** | Sub-Collector, **Rajahmundry**; Anglo-vernacular schools |
- Most Collectors did not know Telugu and depended on corrupt **dubashis**; **Munro and C.P. Brown** were rare exceptions.
!TRAP Cotton's assistant = **Veeranna**; Brown = **Vemana**; Dowleswaram is on the **Godavari**, not the Krishna.
### 7. EARLY ADMINISTRATION – COUNCILS, COMMITTEE, BOARD, COLLECTOR
#### 7.1 Factories and the Chiefs and Councils
- Before acquisition: Company factories at **Machilipatnam** and **Visakhapatnam**.
- **Provincial Chiefs and Councils** at these stations governed the Circars; the **Chief-in-Council at Masulipatnam** was the main one.
- Abolished in **1794** → replaced by district **Collectors**.
#### 7.2 Committee of Circuit (Circuit Committee)
- Ordered by the Court of Directors in **1775**, drawn from the **Madras Council**.
- Purpose: examine the **revenue** of the Northern Circars and whether zamindars' payments were fair.
- Findings: unfavourable to the zamindars; treated **Vizianagaram** as too powerful; recommended **against the auction (highest-bidder) system** as it hurt both government and ryots.
#### 7.3 Board of Revenue
- **Madras Board of Revenue set up in 1786**; state-level apex of the revenue hierarchy.
- Andhra **Board of Revenue (1953)** formed after Andhra State was created.
| Level | Revenue officer | Court |
|---|---|---|
| State | **Board of Revenue** | **Sadar Adalats** at Madras |
| District | **Collector** | **Zilla Court** (District Judge) |
| Division | Sub-Collector / Revenue Officer | — |
| Taluk | **Tahsildar** | **Munsiff Court** |
| Firka | Deputy Tahsildar / Revenue Inspector | — |
| Village | **Karanam, Munsab** (hereditary) | Panchayat court |
#### 7.4 The Collector
- **1794:** District Collectors replaced the Chief-and-Council system in the Circars.
- **1802:** office of Collector introduced in the North Sarkar region under the new settlement.
- Duties: **land revenue** (main), law and order, **magistrate**.
- **1816:** Collectors lost judicial powers; later some magisterial powers returned under ryotwari; civil justice stayed with the District Judge.
!KEY APPSC G2 2012 key: "Collector's administration in place of the Revenue Board" = **1786**.
!TRAP The 1794 Collector change was **not** caused by the 1802 Permanent Settlement – it came earlier.
### 8. DISTRICTS AND COLLECTORATES
- **1794:** coastal Andhra had **5 collectorates** – Ganjam, Visakhapatnam, Godavari, Krishna (HQ **Machilipatnam**), Nellore.
- **1808:** Ceded Districts split into **Bellary** and **Cuddapah**.
- **By 1856: 8 collectorates** – Ganjam, Visakhapatnam, Godavari, Krishna (Machilipatnam), Nellore, Bellary, Cuddapah, Kurnool.
- **1859:** Rajahmundry, Masulipatam and Guntur were reorganised into **Godavari** and **Kistna** districts.
| District | Year formed |
|---|---|
| **Kurnool** | **1858** |
| **Anantapur** | **1882** |
| **Guntur, East Godavari** | **1904** |
| **Chittoor** (gave Kadapa its present area) | **1911** |
| **West Godavari** | **1925** (not 1911) |
!TRAP Chittoor = 1911; Kurnool = 1858; Anantapur = 1882 – Kadapa's present area dates from 1911.
### 9. REVENUE SYSTEMS AND TAXES
#### 9.1 Zamindari vs Ryotwari
- **Before 1802:** zamindaris given by **auction**.
- **Permanent Settlement:** **Cornwallis, 1793**; fixed demand replaced auctions; applied to Andhra in **1802**.
- British land-tax management in Andhra had **three methods**; the Zamindari system was imposed in the Circars.
| Feature | Zamindari (Permanent Settlement) | Ryotwari | Mahalwari |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where | **Vizianagaram, Bobbili, Pithapuram** (Northern Circars) | **Rayalaseema first (1802)**; later most of Andhra | Nellore |
| Settled with | Zamindar | **Ryot directly** | Village headmen |
| Effect | **Absentee landlordism**, poor tenants | **Over-assessment (often 50%+)**, moneylender debt, land alienation | — |
- **First region of Ryotwari:** **Baramahal (1792)**; in Andhra, the **Ceded Districts (1802)**.
- **1820:** Ryotwari extended across Madras under Governor Munro.
- **Zamindari abolition:** **Madras Estates (Abolition and Conversion into Ryotwari) Act, 1948**; in force **7 Sep 1949**.
- **Zamindari lands:** 9/10 of the estimated revenue to government, 1/10 to the zamindar; agreements for 10 years.
#### 9.2 Revenue terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Moturpha** | Tax on **looms** – a trade/profession tax on weavers, shops, artisans (APPSC G2 2011); **not a salt tax**; opposed by the **Madras Native Association** (1861 petition) |
| **Peshkash / Peshcush** | Fixed tribute/revenue share paid by **zamindars and other intermediaries to the British** (PYQ 2025: true) |
| **Korunu** | Revenue zamindars collected from **farmers** |
| **Haveli lands** | Lands under **direct British control** in coastal Andhra |
| **Sayer** | **Inland transit and customs** duties |
| **Abkari** | **Excise on liquor** and toddy |
!PAIR Peshkash | zamindar | to British || Korunu | farmer | to zamindar
### 10. JUDICIARY AND POLICE
#### 10.1 Court hierarchy
| Level | Court |
|---|---|
| Taluk | **Munsiff court** |
| District | **Zilla court** under a **District Judge** (post created by the **Cornwallis Code, 1793**) |
| Appeal | **Provincial Courts of Appeal** / Circuit courts (powers expanded 1820) |
| Apex (natives) | **Sadar Adalat** (civil) and **Sadar Faujdari Adalat** (criminal), Madras |
| British subjects, Madras town | **Madras Supreme Court (1801)** |
#### 10.2 Munro's judicial reforms
- **Munro = President of the Judicial Commission (1814)**; report **April 1816**, mostly accepted.
- **Madras Regulations of 1816:** **Munsiff courts** and **Panchayat courts** (in every large village and district) as courts of first instance – Indian officials at the base.
- Criticism: slow, costly British procedure displaced village panchayats → more lawsuits and debt.
#### 10.3 Police
- **Cornwallis:** **thanas under darogas** (Indian officers with constables), blamed for abuse of power.
- Madras: village watchmen supplemented by central police under a District Superintendent.
- **Munro (1816):** police supervision moved **from judges to Collectors**.
- **Codes:** CPC **1859**, IPC **1860**, CrPC **1861**; **Madras District Police Act 1859**.
#### 10.4 Latest developments
- **20 Feb 2026:** sericulture in Rayalaseema traced to British promotion in the "Ceded Districts"; silk hubs today **Dharmavaram** and **Madanapalle**.
- **1 May 2026:** Supreme Court (CJI **Surya Kant**) issued notice on a plea for a separate **revenue judicial service** cadre (revenue officers lack legal training), invoking **Article 14**.
- Fertile, educated Circars vs dry, neglected Ceded Districts → later the **Sri Bagh Pact** debate.
