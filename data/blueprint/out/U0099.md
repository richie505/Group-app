## U0099 | REDDI KINGDOMS OF ADDANKI, KONDAVIDU AND RAJAHMUNDRY – PROLAYA VEMA REDDI TO KUMARAGIRI REDDI
### 1. THE REDDI DYNASTY – PERIOD AND FEATURES
- Reddi kingdom: about **1325–1448 CE** – **Kondaveedu line c. 1325–1424**; **Rajamahendravaram line 1402–1448**.
- Called the **"second golden age" of Telugu culture** and literature; peak of Telugu literature before Vijayanagara; kings were themselves authors.
- Lineage: **Panta Vamsam** (also **Deseti kula**); emblem **Bull**; family deity **Mula Guramma**; royal/official language **Sanskrit**.
- One of three **successor states after 1323** (fall of the Kakatiyas): **Reddis, Musunuris, Recherla Velamas**.
- Prolaya Vema had served in **Prataparudra's** court and in the court of **Kapaya Nayaka** (Musunuri leader who freed Andhra from Delhi) – recorded in the Anitalli (Kaluvacheru) inscription.
### 2. CAPITALS AND FORTS
#### 2.1 Capitals
- Sequence: **Addanki → Kondaveedu (Kondavidu) → Rajamahendravaram (Rajahmundry)**.
| Capital / fort | Region | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Addanki** | **Prakasam** | First capital, Prolaya Vema |
| **Kondavidu** | **Guntur** (Guntur–Palnadu, near Chilakaluripet) | Replaced Addanki; later a battleground of Gajapatis and Vijayanagara |
| Rajahmundry | Godavari | Second seat, 1402–1448 |
| **Kondapalli** | **Krishna** | Strategic hilltop fort; credited to Prolaya Vema; later held by the Gajapatis |
- **Who shifted Addanki → Kondavidu?** **Anavota (Anapota) Reddi**, son of Prolaya Vema – by the **Manyapuram (Manyamapura) inscription**. The APPSC G2 2012 key gave **Anavema Reddi**.
!TRAP Capital shift: Anavota per the Manyapuram inscription; APPSC G2 2012 key = Anavema. If only one of them is in the options, pick it.
!TRAP Kondavidu is in the Guntur region – NOT near Eluru (West Godavari) and NOT in East Godavari.
#### 2.2 Forts
- **Prolaya Vema Reddi built 74 forts**, including **Kondaveedu, Kondapalli and Dharanikota**.
### 3. KONDAVIDU REDDI RULERS – ORDER AND CONTRIBUTION
| Ruler | Period | Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| **Prolaya Vema Reddi** (founder) | **1324/1325–1353** | Expelled **Tughluq** forces from coastal Andhra; patron of **Errana**; steps to Patala Ganga (1346) |
| **Anavota (Anapota) Reddi** | 1353/54–1364 | Shifted capital to Kondavidu; **abolished the gold tax**; developed Motupalli; halted the Kalinga Gangas on the Krishna |
| **Anavema Reddi** (greatest) | 1364–1386 | **Most charitable** Reddi king; beat Kalinga Gajapatis; **Veera Siromandapam**, Srisailam |
| **Kumaragiri Reddi** | 1386–1402 | **Vasantarajiyam**; Vasantotsavam; ousted 1402 |
| **Pedakomati Vema Reddi** | 1402–1420 | **Sangeeta Chintamani**; patron of **Srinatha** |
| **Racha Vema Reddi** (last) | 1420–1424 | **Puriti Pannu**; killed by his own people |
- **Order (APPSC 2025):** Prolaya Vema → **Anavota → Anavema → Kumaragiri → Pedakomati Vema** → Racha Vema.
- **Kumaragiri succeeded Anavema.**
#### 3.1 Prolaya Vema Reddi – Founder
- **Founded the Reddy kingdom in 1324** (expelled Tughluq forces c. 1325); Panta clan.
- Brother **Mallareddi (Mallaya Reddy)** took **Motupalli** by defeating Bahmani founder **Hasan Gangu**.
- Land gifts to Brahmanas: title **Nissivu Bhudana Parasurama**.
- Errana's **Harivamsam** dedicated to him.
#### 3.2 Anavema Reddi – Conquests and Officials
- With help of the **Padmanayakas** and **Vijayanagara**, defeated the **Kalinga Gajapatis**.
- Took **Nidadavolu** (gave it to Choda Bhima), **Rajahmundry, Pithapuram, Simhachalam, Konaseema** – **not Kandukur**.
- Minister **Mamidi Peddanamatya**; army chief **Chennamanayaka**.
- Built **Anavemagiri mandapas at Simhachalam** and **Veera Siromandapam at Srisailam** (APPSC G2 2011).
- No literary work is credited to Anavema.
#### 3.3 Kumaragiri Reddi
- Accession opposed by **Pedakomati Vema** → made him **feudatory of Chandolu and heir**.
- Brother-in-law and commander **Kataya Vema** conquered the east up to **Cuttack**.
- **Division:** made **Rajahmundry** a separate division under his son **Anavota**, then **Kataya Vema (1395)**.
- **1402:** Pedakomati seized Kondavidu; Kumaragiri **fled to Rajahmundry** and died there in refuge.
- Expert in **Bharata Natya**; court dancer **Lakumadevi**.
#### 3.4 Pedakomati Vema and Racha Vema
- **1404:** Pedakomati Vema regained territory from Vijayanagara.
- **Racha Vema**, son of **Surambika**, cut the **Jaganobbaganda canals** to feed her tank; levied **Puriti Pannu** and was killed as a tyrant (**Kondaveeti Kaifiyat**).
### 4. RAJAHMUNDRY (RAJAMAHENDRAVARAM) REDDIS AND THE END OF REDDI RULE
#### 4.1 Rulers
| Ruler | Period | Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Anavota Reddi (Kumaragiri's son) | to 1395 | First ruler of the division; died young |
| **Kataya Vema Reddi** | 1395–1414 | Independent after 1402; patron of poets; fought Pedakomati all his reign; **killed at Kakaraparthi (1414)** by **Gajaraju Tippa**, an ally of Pedakomati |
| **Allada Reddi** (regent) | 1414–c. 1420 | Ran the state for Veerabhadra Reddi |
| **Veerabhadra Reddi** | c. 1414–1448 | Nominal king; restored 1423 and 1429 |
| **Vema (Veda) Reddi** | 1423–1448 | Veerabhadra's brother; prime minister and de facto ruler |
- Founders: **Pakanati Reddis** who moved east under Anavota and Anavema.
- **Allada Reddi – opponents:** **Pedakomati Vema** (attacked Rajahmundry 1414; beat Kataya Vema and Deva Raya I's army at **Attili-Kakaraparthi**); **Annadeva** (took parts of **Vengi** with **Velama** support). Ally: **Vijayanagara** (Deva Raya I backed Kataya Vema). Helpers: sons Vema Reddi, Veerabhadra Reddi, Doddareddi.
- Rivalry with Kondavidu ended with Kondavidu's fall (1424); Rajahmundry survived **24 more years** as a Vijayanagara feudatory.
#### 4.2 Queen Anitalli
- **Anitalli (Anithalli)**: daughter of **Kataya Vema**; wife of **Veerabhadra Reddi** (Allada's second son).
- Issued the **Kaluvacheru inscription (1423)** after the recapture of Rajahmundry.
#### 4.3 Gajapati–Vijayanagara Rivalry over Rajahmundry
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **1420** | Gajapati **Ganga Ganapati** took Rajahmundry |
| **1423** | **Veerabhadra and Vema** recovered it |
| **1429** | Kalinga king **Veerabhanu Deva** occupied it; **Deva Raya II** expelled him up to **Simhachalam** and restored the Reddis as feudatories |
| 1435 | **Kapileswara Gajapati** took Cuttack and pressed on the Reddis |
| 1444 | Draksharama inscription: Vijayanagara commander **Mallappa Odayar** came to help |
| **1446–48** | After Deva Raya II's death, **Kapileswara Gajapati annexed Rajahmundry** |
- Deva Raya II: title **Gajabetekara** ("hunter of elephants"), greatest of the Sangama dynasty; hired Muslim soldiers, especially archers (Ferishta).
#### 4.4 End of Reddi Rule
| Line | Last ruler | Ended by |
|---|---|---|
| **Kondavidu** | **Racha Vema Reddi** | **Vijayanagara (Deva Raya II), 1424** |
| **Rajahmundry** | **Veerabhadra Reddi and Vema Reddi** | **Kapileswara Gajapati of Orissa, 1448** |
| Kandukur branch (Mallareddi's line) | – | **Deva Raya I** reduced them to Vijayanagara feudatories |
### 5. WARS BY OPPONENT
| Opponent | Event |
|---|---|
| **Velamas** | **Battle of Bhimavaram** – Reddis expelled the Velamas from **Palnadu** (APPSC G2 2019 key) |
| **Bahmanis** | Mallaya Reddy beat **Hasan Gangu** at Motupalli; **Gajarao Tippa** beat **Firoz Shah's** army at **Kambhammetta (1396)**; Kandukur attack repelled (**1398**) |
| **Vijayanagara** | Kataya Vema beat **Harihara II**; took Tripurantakam and Vinukonda; **Sarpavaram inscription** treaty – Harihara gave daughter **Vijayalakshmi** to Kataya's son |
| **Gajapatis / Kalinga** | Anavota halted Kalinga Gangas; Anavema defeated Gajapatis; Kataya raided to Cuttack; Kapileswara attacked in 1448 |
!TRAP Do not mix the Reddi Battle of Bhimavaram (Velamas expelled from Palnadu) with the 1367-68 Velama victory over Kapaya Nayaka.
### 6. ADMINISTRATION AND TAXES
#### 6.1 Village Administration
- **12 Ayagars** (village servants; Kakatiya pattern): **Karnam** – accountant; **Reddi / Peddakapu** – headman; **Talari** – watchman.
- Paid in **Manyam** land or **Mera** (crop share); Karnam, Reddi and Talari held village judicial and police powers.
- Nayakas held military-territorial charges.
#### 6.2 Taxes
| Tax | Meaning / ruler |
|---|---|
| **Puriti Pannu** | Tax on **childbirth** – **Racha Vema** (APPSC G2 2019; PYQ 2018) |
| **Kanika** | Presents/gifts to king or officials |
| **Pullari** | Cattle-**grazing** tax |
| **Illari** | **House** tax |
| Ari | Land tax |
| Gold tax | **Abolished by Anavota** |
- Inherited Kakatiya taxes: Kanika, Pullari, Pannu, Siddhayam, Sangam, Darshanam, Niruvidi, Ardhayam.
- Temple lands, Brahmin grants and Ayagar lands were **tax-exempt**.
!TRAP Puriti Pannu = Racha Vema (not Kumaragiri or Anavema). One key named Kataya Vema – Racha Vema is the standard answer.
### 7. TRADE – MOTUPALLI PORT
- **Motupalli** = chief Reddi port; captured by Mallaya Reddy; dock developed by **Anavota**.
- **Motupalli Abhaya Sasanam**: issued by **Somaya**, minister of **Anavota**, to encourage trade.
- **Motupalli epigraph**: fixes **tariff rates** on imports/exports. (Famine relief = Mahasthan inscription.)
- **Haravilasam (Srinatha)**: information on **sea-borne trade**, imports and exports (APPSC 2019; PYQ 2018); dedicated to merchant **Avachi Tippayya Setti**.
- **Komatis** ran inland and coastal trade.
- Earlier route: Buddhist text **Vinayavatthu** mentions the **"Andhra Path"** (APPSC 2019).
!TRAP Assertion-Reason: Haravilasam gives sea-trade information (A) and is dedicated to Tippayya Setti (R) – both true, but R is NOT the explanation of A.
### 8. SOCIETY, WOMEN AND FESTIVALS
#### 8.1 Castes
- Warrior-farmer backbone: **Kammas, Kapus, Reddis, Velamas**; traders: **Komatis**; Brahmana **agraharas** with tax-free grants.
#### 8.2 Festivals and Dance
- **Vasantotsavam** (spring festival) – most popular; celebrated grandly by **Kumaragiri**; funded by **Avachi Tippayya Setti** (recorded in Haravilasam).
- Court dancer **Lakumadevi** (APPSC G2 2012) – Bharatanatyam, at Kumaragiri's court.
- **C. Narayana Reddy** wrote **Karpura Vasanta Rayalu** on Kumaragiri.
- **Jalakaranda** instrument – **Dharmasagara inscription** (PYQ G2 2019). **Ghatika Yantra** (water clock) – **Ghatikagara**.
#### 8.3 Women and Tanks
- **Surambika** (Suryambika), queen of **Pedakomati Vema**, dug a tank: **Santana Sagaram** (APPSC GS 2018 key; also Santana Payonidhi) / **Govardhana Samudram** (APPSC G2 2019 key).
- **Devadasis**: temple women trained in music and dance; respected at Shiva temples incl. Pancharamas.
- **Pakala tank** – Ganapatideva's general **Jagadala Mummadi** (Kakatiya). Betha Raju II – Setti, Kete, **Kesari Samudram** (after title Vikrama Kesari).
!TRAP Surambika's tank: if only one of Santana Sagaram or Govardhana Samudram is offered, that is the answer.
### 9. RELIGION AND TEMPLE BUILDING
- Kings: **Pashupata Shaivism**; people: **Veerashaivism** (popular religion); Bhairava and Mailara worship.
- **Ghoderayas** spread Shaivism from **Tripurantakam**; Reddi kings were disciples of Ghoderayacharulu.
- **Vaishnavism**: **Vadagalai** matha at **Ahobilam**; **Kumaragiri and Kataya Vema** = Vaishnava patrons; Reddis moved from Shaivism towards Vaishnavism.
| Work | Builder |
|---|---|
| **Steps (Sopanamargas) to Patala Ganga, Srisailam (1346)** and to **Ahobilam** | **Prolaya Vema** (not Pedakomati) |
| Pilgrim rest-houses at Ahobilam and Srisailam; walls of Ahobilam and **Mahanandi** | Prolaya Vema |
| **Veera Siromandapam**, Srisailam (for Siro-karthanam devotees) | **Anavema** |
| Anavemagiri mandapas, Simhachalam | Anavema |
| Amareswara temple, Amaravati restored | **Vallaya Vemana**, minister of Anavota |
- Pancharamas renovated: **Draksharama Bhimesvara**, **Samarlakota Kumararama**; Bikkavolu temples.

### 11. COURT POETS
#### 11.1 Errana (Errapragada)
- Court poet of **Prolaya Vema** (PYQ G2 2011); dedicated **Harivamsam** to him.
- **Last of the Kavitrayam**; completed the Telugu Mahabharata (**remaining Aranya Parva – Aranya Parva shesham**).
- Titles **Prabandha Parameswara, Sambhudasa**; wrote **Nrusimha (Narasimha) Puranam** (PYQ G2 2017).
#### 11.2 Srinatha (c. 1365–1441)
- Title **Kavi Sarvabhauma**; **Vidyadhikari** (education officer) under **Pedakomati Vema** (PYQ 2002); composed his Sanskrit inscriptions.
- Defeated **Gauda Dindima Bhatta** of **Deva Raya II's** court → **Kanakabhishekam** and **Ganda Penderam**.
- After 1424 joined the Rajahmundry Reddis (**Veerabhadra Reddi**) with **Bendapudi Annamatya's** help; after 1448, poverty in Palnadu.
- Works: **Haravilasam**, **Sringara Naishadham** (from Sriharsha's Naishadhiyacharita; to **Mamidi Singana**), **Kasikhandam**, **Bhimakhandam** (praises **Bhimeswara of Draksharamam**), **Palnati Veera Charitra** (life of Palnadu), Sivaratri Mahatyam, Maruttarat Charitra.
- **Madanavilasabanam is NOT by Srinatha.**
- Opened the **Kavya Yuga**; cited as evidence of **paper** use in the 15th century (G2 2012).
#### 11.3 Other Court Poets
| Poet | Patron | Works / titles |
|---|---|---|
| **Vamana Bhatta Bana** | **Pedakomati Vema** | **Vema Bhupala Charitra** (prose biography of Pedakomati), Nalabhyudayam, Raghunatha Charitam, Hamsa Sandesam, **Usha Parinayam**, **Kanaka Rekha Kalyanam**; Shabda Ratnakaram; titles **Sahitya Chudamani**, Sahitya Samrajya Dhurandhara |
| **Bala Saraswati** | **Anavota** | Composed Anavota's Sanskrit inscriptions |
