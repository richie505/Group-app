### 2. AIRCRAFT – DEVELOPER, ORIGIN, ROLE
#### 2.1 Tejas LCA
- **Designed by ADA** (with DRDO), **built by HAL**; named by **Atal Bihari Vajpayee**.
- **4.5-generation** light multirole supersonic fighter; smallest and lightest of its class; single engine, **compound delta-wing, tailless**; carbon-fibre composites ~45% of airframe.
- Variants: Mk-1 → **Mk-1A** (**Uttam AESA**, EW suite, air-to-air refuelling) → **Mk-2** (**GE F414** engine, canards).
- **25 Sep 2025**: HAL contract for **97 Mk1A** (68 fighters + 29 twin-seaters), ₹62,370 crore, 64%+ indigenous, deliveries from 2027-28; earlier order of **83**.

### 8. RADAR TYPES AND DOPPLER EFFECT
#### 8.1 Doppler Effect
- Apparent change in frequency from relative motion: **approaching → higher** pitch; receding → lower (passing siren).
- Uses: radar speed guns, **medical ultrasound** (blood-flow velocity), **weather radar** (wind speed), SONAR, bats, **satellite tracking**, astronomy (**red shift** moving away, **blue shift** approaching; **Hubble's expanding universe**).
#### 8.2 Radar Types
| Type | Principle / use |
|---|---|
| Doppler | Speed from frequency shift |
| **SAR** | **Imaging at night and through clouds** |
| **AESA** | T/R module on every element; electronic beam, no moving antenna; tracks multiple targets – UTTAM, MF-STAR |
| **VHF anti-stealth** | Long waves pick up stealth aircraft |
| **Over-the-horizon** | **HF waves bounced off the ionosphere** |
#### 8.3 AWACS vs AEW&C
| AWACS | AEW&C (Netra) |
|---|---|
| Larger, long-range surveillance + **full air-battle control** (APPSC 2018) | Smaller jet, early warning + command-and-control |
- **Netra**: **DRDO-CABS Bengaluru**; AESA on **Embraer EMB-145**; 250–500+ km; FOC Jun 2026.

### 11. EXPLOSIVES, STEALTH AND MATERIALS
#### 11.1 Names and Formulas
| Explosive | Other names | Formula / note |
|---|---|---|
| **TNT** | Trinitrotoluene | C₇H₅N₃O₆; shells, bombs, underwater blasting, dye intermediate |
| **TNG** | **Nitroglycerine, Nobel's oil**, glyceryl trinitrate, GTN | C₃H₅(ONO₂)₃; oily colourless liquid |
| **TNP** | **Picric acid** (trinitrophenol) | C₆H₃N₃O₇; explosive, dyes, reagent, old antiseptic |
| **RDX** | **Cyclonite**, Hexogen, T-4 | C₃H₆N₆O₆; more powerful than TNT; core of **C-4** |
| HMX | **Octogen**, High Melting Explosive | TBRL (fine beta-HMX) |
| **CL-20** | HNIW | C₆H₆N₁₂O₁₂; **most powerful non-nuclear explosive**; **HEMRL Pune** |
| **LLM-105** | – | **Insensitive** high explosive |
- **Nitrochloroform is not an explosive.**
#### 11.2 Mixtures
| Mixture | Composition |
|---|---|
| Dynamite | Nitroglycerine in kieselguhr / sawdust |
| **Amatol** | **TNT + ammonium nitrate** – **no nitroglycerine**; a high explosive, not a propellant |
| Blasting gelatin | NG 91–93% + nitrocellulose (quarries) |
| **Cordite** | NG + nitrocellulose **propellant** |
| **Gun cotton** | Cellulose nitrated with HNO₃ + H₂SO₄; inks, leather finish, celluloid |
| **C-4** | **RDX** (not HMX) in a plastic matrix |
#### 11.3 Stealth and Materials
- **Radar cross-section (RCS)**: how "big" a target looks to radar; cut by shaping, **Radar Absorbing Material**, flush weapons (P17A). Stealth objects **can** be detected at specific frequencies.

| Super-alloys, tungsten alloys | Engines, penetrators | DMRL |
