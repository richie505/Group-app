## U0495 | AGRICULTURAL STRATEGY AND POLICY – NATIONAL AGRICULTURE POLICY, DOUBLING FARMERS' INCOME, NATIONAL MISSIONS
### 1. PHASES OF AGRICULTURAL DEVELOPMENT
| Phase | Period | Defining feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Pre-Green Revolution** | **1951–68** (foundations 1950–67) | Land reforms, irrigation, Community Development; **PL-480** imports; droughts 1965–67 |
| **Early Green Revolution** | **1968–81** | HYV seeds (**wheat first**), fertiliser, irrigation – **New Agricultural Strategy** |
| **Later Green Revolution** | **1987–92** | Spread to **rice** and other regions |
| Income phase | 2016 onwards | **Doubling Farmers' Income** shifted focus from **production to income** |
- Foodgrains: **72.3 MT (1965–66)** → record **357.73 MT (2024–25)**.
- **ICAR Drishti (Vision) 2025**: increase in food production.
### 2. NATIONAL COMMISSION ON AGRICULTURE AND NATIONAL COMMISSION ON FARMERS
| Body | Years / Chair | Key recommendations |
|---|---|---|
| **National Commission on Agriculture (NCA)** | **1970–76** (report 1976); chair **Nathu Ram Mirdha** | HYVs, irrigation, credit reform; **coined "social forestry"** |
| **National Commission on Farmers (NCF)** | Constituted **18 Nov 2004**; chair **M.S. Swaminathan**; 5 reports 2004–06 | **MSP at C2 + 50%**; land reforms; women as farmers; universal crop insurance; National Agricultural Risk Fund |
- Government's present MSP policy: **1.5 × A2+FL** (not C2); **22 mandated crops**.
!TRAP Commission wanted **C2 + 50%**; government pays **1.5 × A2+FL**.
### 3. NATIONAL AGRICULTURE POLICY 2000
- Announced **July 2000**; target **over 4% per annum** growth in agriculture.
- Thrusts: **private participation** via **contract farming** and **land leasing**; **price protection**; **national agricultural insurance**; a wide "**Rainbow Revolution**" across all farm sub-sectors.
| Colour | Sector |
|---|---|
| Yellow | Oilseeds |
| Blue | Fisheries |
| Golden | Horticulture / honey |
| **Silver** | **Eggs** |
| **Grey** | **Fertilisers** |
### 4. AGRICULTURAL POLICY SINCE 1991 – LIBERALISATION AND WTO
#### 4.1 Liberalisation Impacts
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Liberalisation after 1991** | **Decline in government investment** in agriculture (APPSC 2023) |
| Input costs | Fertiliser, seed, pesticide up **over 300%** since early 2000s; real farm incomes stagnated |
| **WTO entry 1995** | Import competition rose; subsidies fell as a share of farm income |
| GM crops | **Bt cotton (2002)** = **only GM crop** approved for commercial cultivation |
- **Dunkel Draft** debate: **Sharad Joshi** backed globalisation; **Tikait (BKU)** and **Nanjundaswamy (KRRS)** opposed it.
- **Essential Commodities** is NOT mentioned in the **Farmers' Produce Trade and Commerce Bill 2020**.
- **Budget 2020–21**: **16 action points** for farmers; goal of doubling income by 2022.
#### 4.2 WTO Subsidy Boxes (Agreement on Agriculture)
| Box | Nature | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Green** | **Minimal distortion – permitted** | Decoupled income support, environmental programmes, regional development, research, **farmer training**, pest and disease control, infrastructure, food security |
| **Amber** | **Trade/production-distorting – limited**; reduction commitments | **Price support (MSP)**, production-linked and input subsidies (power, fertiliser, seed, irrigation); **de minimis 5% developed, 10% developing** |
| **Blue** | **Amber-type support with production-limiting conditions** | Direct payments tied to limiting output |
!TRAP MSP = Amber Box; developing-country de minimis = 10%.
#### 4.3 Agricultural Renewal Action Plan
- Proposed by the **NCF (Swaminathan)**; **2006–07** to be the **Year of Agricultural Renewal**.
- Mutually reinforcing packages to bridge the gap between actual and potential yields: **soil health enhancement**, **water harvesting and efficient water use**, **credit and insurance reform**, **technology dissemination**, and **market reform** (starting with production planning).
### 5. DOUBLING FARMERS' INCOME
- **Ashok Dalwai Committee (2016–18)**: **14 volumes**; **base year 2015–16**, **target year 2022**.
- Shift from "**production**" to "**income**": value addition, clusters, **e-NAM/APMC reform**, post-harvest infrastructure, livestock-fisheries integration, land-leasing laws.
#### 5.1 Seven Sources of Income Growth
1. Crop productivity
2. Livestock productivity
3. Resource-use efficiency
4. Cropping intensity
5. Diversification to high-value crops
6. Better price realisation
7. Shift to non-farm jobs
!TRAP Depending only on traditional low-value crops is NOT a source.
### 6. SUSTAINABLE AGRICULTURE AND RAINFED AREAS
- **Meaning**: using land so its **quality remains intact**, meeting present needs without harming future generations (APPSC 2019).
- **Ultimate goal**: **all three pillars** together (APPSC 2018) – **environmental health, economic profitability, social and economic equity**; save natural resources, protect environment, improve human health and safety.
- **Rainfed farming**: **50–60% of net sown area**, about **40% of food production**.
- **National Rainfed Area Authority (NRAA)**: set up **2006**; expert body giving knowledge inputs and coordinating upgrading of dryland/rainfed areas through a **watershed approach**; single platform for sustainable development of rainfed areas.
- **Rainfed Area Development Programme (RADP)**: **2011–12**, by the Department of Agriculture and Cooperation, sub-scheme under **RKVY**.
### 7. NATIONAL MISSIONS
| Mission | Launch | Focus / crops | Key facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NFSM** | **Oct 2007** | Rice, wheat, pulses, coarse/nutri-cereals, commercial crops (cotton, jute, sugarcane) | **Renamed NFSNM** (Food Security and Nutrition) in **FY 2024–25**; Centrally Sponsored |
| **RKVY** | **2007–08** | State-led agri plans | Grants to raise State farm spending; umbrella **PM-RKVY** |
| **NMSA** | **2014–15** | Climate-resilient, rainfed farming | **One of 8 NAPCC missions**; Krishonnati sub-mission from 2018–19; under PM-RKVY since 2022–23 |
| **MIDH** | 2014–15 | Fruits, vegetables, spices, flowers, mushrooms, coconut, cashew, cocoa, bamboo | **60:40** (90:10 NE/Himalayan) |
| **NMEO-Oil Palm** | **Aug 2021** | Oil palm (focus NE, A&N) | ₹11,040 crore |
| **NMEO-Oilseeds** | **3 Oct 2024** | Groundnut, mustard, soybean etc. | **₹10,103 crore**; **39 → 69.7 MT** by 2030–31 |
- Older: **Technology Mission on Oilseeds, Pulses and Maize (1986)** → **NMOOP (2014)**; "**Tilhan Mission**" = oilseed production.
- **PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana (2025–26)**: **Central Sector**, **100 low-performing districts**, **six years**.
- **Pulses mission**: **100% MSP procurement of tur, urad, masoor** for four years via NAFED/NCCF.
- **Agricultural Engineering** is NOT part of the National Agricultural Development Yojana.
#### 7.1 NMSA Components
| Component | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Rainfed Area Development (RAD)** | ICAR Integrated Farming System models; ₹30,000 per family |
| **Soil Health Management** | Soil Health Cards free **every two years** |
| **Per Drop More Crop** | Micro-irrigation subsidy **up to 55%** |
| Agroforestry | – |
!TRAP National Agricultural Market (e-NAM) is NOT an NMSA component. The APPSC 2018 key treated NMSA as begun in 2011–12; it has worked from **2014–15**.
### 8. SCHEMES FOR BETTER PRICE REALISATION
| Scheme | Objective |
|---|---|
| **PM-AASHA (2018)** | Remunerative prices: **PSS** (procurement via **NAFED and NCCF**), **PDPS** (pays MSP-price gap; coverage **40%** of State oilseed output), **MIS (25%)**, Price Stabilisation Fund |
| **e-NAM (April 2016, SFAC)** | Online unified market over APMC mandis; **1,656 mandis**, 23 States + 4 UTs (Mar 2026) |
| **Operation Greens (Budget 2018–19)** | Stabilise **Tomato-Onion-Potato** prices "on the lines of Operation Flood"; widened to **22 perishables** from 2021–22 |
| Krishi UDAN | Air transport of perishables |
- SFAC = **Small Farmers' Agribusiness Consortium**.
### 9. AP AGRICULTURE SUPPORT
#### 9.1 Separate Agriculture Budget
- **AP Agriculture Budget 2026–27**: about **₹53,753 crore** (third time; Minister **K. Atchannaidu**); Agriculture Dept ₹12,153 crore.
- **Union Budget 2026–27**: agriculture **₹1.3 lakh crore** (+2.6%).
#### 9.2 PM-KISAN vs Annadata Sukhibhava
| Feature | PM-KISAN | Annadata Sukhibhava – PM Kisan (AP) |
|---|---|---|
| Start | **1 Dec 2018**, **Central Sector** | 2025–26 |
| Benefit | **₹6,000/yr in 3 × ₹2,000** | **₹20,000/family/yr = AP ₹14,000 + Centre ₹6,000** |
| Covers | Landholding families (cut-off 1 Feb 2019) | **Landholders and tenant farmers** |
| Latest | 23rd instalment to 9.49 crore farmers; total >₹4.47 lakh crore (Aug 2026) | 2025–26: 46.86 lakh families, ₹6,309 crore in 2 instalments; 2026–27 Budget ₹6,600 crore (₹7,260 crore proposed for 51 lakh families) |
- Earlier **YSR Rythu Bharosa**: **₹13,500/family/yr**; included tenant and RoFR farmers.
- **AP leads India in oil palm**: **2.49 lakh ha (48%** of India; earlier 2.27 lakh ha); **22.81 lakh MT** FFB (2024–25).
