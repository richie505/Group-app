## U0658 | MIGRATION – TYPES, STREAMS, CAUSES AND CONSEQUENCES; INTERNAL MIGRATION IN INDIA
### 1. DEFINITIONS AND TYPES
- **Census**: **place of birth** (lifetime) or **last residence**. **PLFS Usual Place of Residence**: **6 months**+.
- **Refugee**: fears persecution (race, religion, nationality, social/political group); cannot return.
| Basis | Types |
|---|---|
| Boundary | **Internal / international** |
| Time | Temporary or **seasonal** / permanent; return |
| Distance / Number | Short or long / individual or mass |
| Cause | Economic, social, political, religious |
| Will | **Voluntary** (weighing push and pull) / **forced** (persecution, development-induced displacement, exploitation) |
### 2. MIGRATION STREAMS
| Stream | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Rural → rural** | **Largest (2011)**; female, **marriage**; intra-state |
| Rural → urban | **Males, employment**, inter-state; drives urbanisation |
| Urban → urban | Developed countries |
| Urban → rural | Smallest; return; **counter-urbanisation** |
- Migration is the main cause of the rural-urban growth gap.
!TRAP UPPSC 2017: "Rural-urban is the largest stream" – FALSE; "rural-rural is mainly female (marriage)" – TRUE.
### 3. CAUSES – PUSH AND PULL
| Push (origin) | Pull (destination) |
|---|---|
| Low farm income, **monsoon failure**, drought, rural unemployment | Jobs, higher wages |
| Poverty, **poor rural infrastructure** | **Education**, health care |
| Conflict, **political instability**, disasters | Security, amenities |
- PLFS 2020-21: female rate **47.9%** – **86.8%** for **marriage**; male **10.7%** – **22.8%** for **employment**.
- Adults most mobile; networks (Keralites, Sikhs).
- Rural-urban: no rural jobs, urban labour demand, unbalanced development.

### 5. DATA – CENSUS 2011, PLFS, SURVEYS
- Census 2011: **455.8 million** (last residence) = **37.6%**; 141.2 million moved in 2001-11; **4.1 crore+** inter-state.
- **Source**: **UP, Bihar** lead. **Destination**: **Delhi, Maharashtra** lead (also TN, Gujarat, AP, Kerala).
- PLFS 2020-21 rate **28.9%**.
- **MoSPI/NSO Migration Survey (Jul 2026–Jun 2027)**: first since **2007-08**; short-term = **15 days–6 months** (earlier 1–6 months).
### 6. CONSEQUENCES
| Dimension | Effect |
|---|---|
| Economic | Remittances, urban labour, **social remittances** (skills, ideas) |
| Demographic | Source left with **ageing + more women** |
| Social | **"Sons of the soil"** hostility ("Bihari bhaiya"); slums |
- COVID lockdown: about **122 million** jobs lost.
### 7. INTERNATIONAL MIGRATION AND DIASPORA
- **GCM** (Global Compact for Safe, Orderly and Regular Migration): **Marrakech, Dec 2018**; UNGA vote **19 Dec 2018**; **non-binding**; **international only**; not all members; first UN intergovernmental agreement on all sides of migration.
- **World Migration Report 2026** – **IOM** (1951); **biennial since 2000**; 13th edition.
- Top share: **UAE**; most migrants: **Europe**; top percentage: **Oceania**.
- India: **largest diaspora (35 million+)**; ~3.5 million in UAE.
- **Top remittance recipient: USD 137 billion (2024)** – only one above USD 100 billion; USD 135 billion FY 2024-25, more from skilled professionals.
### 8. LAWS AND SCHEMES FOR MIGRANTS
- **Inter-State Migrant Workmen (RECS) Act, 1979**: establishments with **5+** migrants; **displacement allowance 50%** of monthly wages + journey allowance; merged into **OSH Code 2020**.
- **OSH Code**: self-migrating workers, annual travel allowance, PDS and social security portability; four Labour Codes (29 laws), **Nov 2025**.
- **e-Shram (2021)**: One-Stop-Solution, 15 schemes, **22 languages (BHASHINI)**.
- **ONORC**: ration portability, all **36** States/UTs.
- **PDOT (Jan 2018)**: MEA, ECR emigrants; 1,72,220 trained. **GKRA (2020)**: returnees.
### 9. MIGRATION FROM ANDHRA PRADESH
- Gulf source: **Anantapur, Kadapa, Chittoor** (domestic workers, unskilled).
- **Konaseema**: ~15,000 to GCC (Dec 2025); **70%+ women**, housemaids.
- Seasonal labour: **Rayalaseema**, north-coastal AP; **Srikakulam fishermen → Gujarat coast**.
- Telugu diaspora: Gulf + USA; **APNRT** – Gulf help desk. CM: **Bhogapuram airport** will end Uttarandhra migration.
