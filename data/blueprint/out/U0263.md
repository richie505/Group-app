## U0263 | FORMATION OF ANDHRA PRADESH 1956 – VISALANDHRA, GENTLEMEN'S AGREEMENT
### 1. VISALANDHRA – MEANING, ROOTS AND FIRSTS
#### 1.1 Meaning
- **Visalandhra** ("**Greater Andhra**") = one state for all Telugu people divided between **Madras** and the **Nizam's Hyderabad** (APPSC GS 2024: the united-Telugu-state movement = Visalandhra Movement).
- In **1950** southern and eastern Andhra were in Madras State; Telangana was part of Hyderabad State.
- Prakasam's slogan: "**Visalandhra nirmanam is the mother's right of all Andhras.**"
#### 1.2 Roots – Year → Event
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1912 | **Unnava Lakshminarayana** and **Jonnavittula Gurunatham** sketched a united Andhra (Telangana + Circars + Ceded districts) |
| 1914 | Second Andhra Mahasabha – **Nyapati Subbarao** proposed one rule for all Telugu areas |
| 1921 | **Andhra Jana Sangham** founded; the Nizam Andhra Mahasabha grew out of it |
| 1936 | Andhra Congress **Swaraj Party manifesto** |
| 1937 | Concept spreads in the Circar districts |
| **1946** | **Communist Party** first demands it; **Puchalapalli Sundarayya** writes ***Visalandhralo Prajarajyam*** ("People's Rule in Greater Andhra") and **coins the term** |
| 1954 | **Madapati Hanumantha Rao**: idea 50 years old, as old as the Andhra Movement |
!KEY First to use the word "Visalandhra" = Sundarayya; first party demand = Communist Party (1946). The movement started from the (Nizam) Andhra Mahasabha milieu and was championed by the Communists.
#### 1.3 Chronology Asked in Papers
- **Dhar Commission (1948) → Visalandhra Mahasabha (1949) → Swami Sitaram's fast (1951) → Andhra State / SRC (1953)**.
### 2. TELANGANA BACKGROUND – MAHASABHA, HYDERABAD STATE, MULKI RULES
#### 2.1 Nizam Andhra Mahasabha
- Grew out of the **Andhra Jana Sangham (1921)**.
- First Andhra Mahasabha: **Jogipet, 1930**, presided by **Suravaram Pratapa Reddy**.
- Vehicle of Telugu cultural and political awakening; later split into moderate and Communist wings.
- **Chanda Railway Scheme agitation (1883)** = beginning of public political consciousness in Hyderabad State.
#### 2.2 Hyderabad State
- **Join Indian Union Movement** launched **7 Aug 1947** under **Swami Ramananda Tirtha** (founder of the **Hyderabad State Congress**); opposed the Nizam declaring independence. Hyderabad integrated **17 Sep 1948** (**Operation Polo**).
- **Part B State** from **1950 to 31 Oct 1956**.
- **Burgula Ramakrishna Rao** headed the **first elected ministry (1952–56)** – also the **last** elected CM of Hyderabad State (APPSC G2 2002, 2011). Speaker at merger: **Kashinatha Rao**.
#### 2.3 Mulki Rules and the 1952 Agitation
- Mulki rules from **1888**; made stricter by the **Nizam's farman of 1919**; only residents of about **15 years** got local jobs.
- **Mulki agitation, 1952** under the Burgula ministry; **police firing near City College, Hyderabad** (3–4 September 1952) killed students.
- Inquiry commission under Justice **Pingali Jaganmohan Reddy** (report 28 Dec 1952) found lack of coordination; no major change in Mulki rules followed.
- Outcome: domicile protection became the central Telangana demand → Mulki clause of the Gentlemen's Agreement.
!NUM Mulki: 1888 → tightened 1919 → agitation 1952 → Agreement domicile 12 years (1956)

#### 3.2 Representations to the SRC
- **8 July 1954**: Hyderabad PCC delegation (Harishchandra Heda, J.V. Narsing Rao, Arige Ramaswamy) asked for a self-sufficient Telangana of 9 districts + Bastar, parts of Chanda (60,000 sq miles, Rs 17 crore revenue).
- **8 July 1954**, Warangal Congress meeting: Hyderabad government had no official opinion; ministers gave personal views.
- Andhras welcomed the SRC; Telugu, Kannada, Marathi areas of Hyderabad agreed to partition.
### 4. PARTIES, PRESS AND COMMUNIST ROLE
#### 4.1 Communist Party
- **Started/championed** the movement (APPSC G2 2012) through songs, poems, books, pamphlets and press.
- Telangana leaders: **Sundarayya**, **Ravi Narayana Reddy**, **Baddam Ella Reddy**, **Arutla Ramachandra Reddy**.
- **Hyderabad State Communist Party** = a branch of the **Andhra Communist Party** (HQ Vijayawada).
- **People's Democratic Front (PDF)** = Communists' electoral front in Hyderabad State, **1952** (not Congress).
- **1952**: big gains in both regions → expected a majority in a united state → intensified campaign; they propagated Visalandhra even before Andhra State (1953).
- Sundarayya later first **General Secretary of CPI(M) (1964)**.
!TRAP APPSC G2 2025: "Communists opposed integrated linguistic states" = the only incorrect statement.

### 6. ARGUMENTS FOR AND AGAINST
| For Visalandhra | Against / Telangana apprehensions |
|---|---|
| **River waters**: integrated Krishna–Godavari planning | **Employment**: weakening of **Mulki rules** |
| **Economic**: Andhra's agriculture and coast + Telangana's minerals | **Education/services**: advanced Coastal Andhra would dominate |
| **Capital**: Hyderabad solves Andhra's capital problem | **Revenue**: Telangana's surplus spent elsewhere |
| **Development**: Telangana backward under the Nizam | **Disparities** would widen |
| **Agrarian** (Communists): push land reforms, weaken feudalism | Telangana had **higher per capita income and revenue** → viable alone |
!TRAP Not a pro-Visalandhra argument: permanent protection of separate Telangana political institutions.
### 7. WANCHOO COMMITTEE, ANDHRA STATE AND SRC
- **Wanchoo Committee** – appointed **7 Feb 1953** after Potti Sriramulu's death; Justice **K.N. Wanchoo**, CJ of Rajasthan HC; identified the **financial and administrative problems** of forming Andhra State (APPSC G2 2012); **March 1953** report fixed its districts.
- **Andhra State** – **1 Oct 1953**, capital **Kurnool**; first CM **Prakasam**, Deputy CM **N. Sanjiva Reddy**. Prakasam lost a no-confidence vote (**Nov 1954**, prohibition) → mid-term polls **1955** → **Bezawada Gopala Reddy** CM.
- **Pataskar Award** – Andhra–Madras border; **Tiruttani** to Tamil Nadu.
- **SRC** – announced **22 Dec 1953**, constituted **29 Dec 1953**; **Fazl Ali, H.N. Kunzru, K.M. Panikkar** (not Harishchandra Heda); report **1955**.
- **Para 386**: Telangana initially a separate **Hyderabad State**; merger after the **1961** elections by **two-thirds** of its legislature. Raichur, Gulbarga → Mysore; Marathwada separated.
- **Andhra Assembly (1955)**: Bezawada Gopala Reddy's **unanimous** resolution – Telangana priority + population-based shares.
- **Hyderabad Assembly**: **103 for**, **29 against**, **15 neutral**.
- **22 Oct 1955**: CMs agreed to merge at Hyderabad House, New Delhi.
### 8. GENTLEMEN'S AGREEMENT, 20 FEBRUARY 1956
#### 8.1 Date, Place, Signatories
- **20 February 1956**, **Hyderabad House, New Delhi**; **8 signatories – 4 Andhra, 4 Telangana**.
- A **moral and political understanding** on the **Sribagh Pact** model, not a constitutional settlement.
| Andhra | Post | Telangana | Post |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bezawada Gopala Reddy** | CM, Andhra State | **Burgula Ramakrishna Rao** | CM, Hyderabad |
| **Neelam Sanjiva Reddy** | Deputy CM | **K.V. Ranga Reddy** | Deputy CM |
| **Gouthu Latchanna** | Minister | **Marri Chenna Reddy** | Minister |
| **Alluri Satyanarayana Raju** | APCC President | **J.V. Narsing Rao** | Hyderabad PCC President |
!TRAP NOT signatories: **Tanguturi Prakasam** (APPSC GS 2018), Komarraju Lakshmana Rao (d. 1923).
#### 8.2 Clauses
| Subject | Safeguard |
|---|---|
| Finance | Common costs shared proportionately; Telangana **surplus spent only in Telangana**; review after **5 years** |
| Education | Telangana students – priority or at least **one-third** of technical seats |
| Services | Recruitment by **population ratio**; proportionate retrenchment |
| Domicile | **12 years'** residence for recruitment in Telangana |
| Land | Sale of agricultural land controlled by the **Regional Council** |
| Urdu | Kept for **5 years**, then reviewed |
| Prohibition | Left to Telangana legislators |
| **Cabinet** | **60:40** (Andhra:Telangana); at least **one Muslim minister** from Telangana |
| Portfolios | At least two of Home, Finance, Revenue, Planning & Development, Commerce & Industry |
| **CM / Deputy CM** | From **different regions** |
| Regional body | Statutory **Regional Council**; working reviewed after **10 years**; disputes to the Centre |
| Party | Separate Telangana PCC till 1962 |
!TRAP Cabinet ratio = **60:40**, not 3:1 (G2 2025).
### 9. REGIONAL COMMITTEE, DEPUTY CM AND LATER LAW
- **Telangana Regional Committee**: provided by the Agreement (GS 2019); **Art 371(1)** (7th Amendment, 1956) + **AP Regional Committee Order, 1958**.
- Members: Telangana MLAs/MPs elected by Telangana legislators; Telangana ministers (except CM) ex officio. Powers: development, irrigation, agriculture, industry, education, health, recruitment, prohibition.
- **Extended five years in 1968**; wound up after the **32nd Amendment (1973)**.
- **Deputy CM**: **Sanjiva Reddy appointed none**, calling it a "**sixth finger**".
- **Violations** (employment, domicile, revenue surplus) → **Jai Telangana agitation, 1969** (CM **Kasu Brahmananda Reddy**). Root flaw: political assurances, not constitutional mandates.
| Law | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **Art 371D** | **32nd Amendment, 1973** (Six-Point Formula) | Local cadres, equal opportunity in jobs and education, AP Administrative Tribunal |
| **Presidential Order** | **1975** | AP Public Employment (Organisation of Local Cadres and Regulation of Direct Recruitment) Order |
| **APRA 2014, Section 97** | 2014 | Art 371D extended to AP or Telangana |
- **May 2026**: zones and multi-zones under the AP Public Employment Order being re-mapped.
!TRAP 371D came by the **32nd**, not 33rd, Amendment.
### 10. FORMATION OF ANDHRA PRADESH, 1 NOVEMBER 1956
- Under the **States Reorganisation Act, 1956**, on a **linguistic basis** (APPSC GS 2023).
- **11 Andhra districts + 9 Telangana districts**; Kannada areas → Mysore, Marathi → Bombay.
- Andhra Legislature at Kurnool backed the merger unanimously; both ministries resigned.
- Name: draft Bill "**Andhra Telangana**"; Communists "Visalandhra"; final "**Andhra Pradesh**" (7th Amendment Bill, 10 Aug 1956).
- **Capital Hyderabad**, replacing **Kurnool** (1953–56); reasons – SRC view and Mahasabha demand.
- **Inaugurated by Nehru**, 1 Nov 1956.

| Deputy Speaker | Kalluri Subbarao |
| Leader of Opposition | **Puchalapalli Sundarayya** |
| Council Chairman (1958) | **Madapati Hanumantha Rao** |

- Ministers: Bezawada Gopala Reddy (Finance), Kasu Brahmananda Reddy (Local Bodies), J.V. Narsing Rao (Irrigation and Electricity).
- Congress: 40 (Hyderabad, 1952) + 119 (Andhra, 1955) = **159**.
- **1957**: elections only in Telangana (**79** seats); **Legislative Council** constituted **1958** (90 members).
- **High Court** at Hyderabad from **5 Nov 1956**; separate AP High Court at **Amaravati** from **1 Jan 2019**.
- **Burgula** became **first Governor of Kerala (1956)**, then Uttar Pradesh.

- NTR reforms: **Dharma Mahamatra** (first holder **N. Vittal**, G2 2017); Rs 2/kg rice (14 Apr 1983); Women's University, Tirupati; equal property rights for daughters; simple majority for no-confidence against ZP vice-chairmen (1983); **Mandal system** (1985).
- **1984 crisis**: Governor **Ram Lal** dismissed NTR (in the US) → Nadendla sworn in 16 Aug → restored 16 Sep 1984 → Indira assassinated 31 Oct → polls 28 Dec 1984 → NTR sworn in 9 Mar 1985 → **52nd Amendment (1985)** anti-defection; a classic misuse of Governor's discretion (Sarkaria debates).
