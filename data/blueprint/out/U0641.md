## U0641 | ANDHRA PRADESH – SOIL TYPES AND THEIR DISTRIBUTION
### 1. AP SOILS BY AREA
- Order by area: **Red > Black > Deltaic alluvium > Laterite**.
| Rank | Soil | Share of AP | Main districts / regions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Red** | **~66%** ("more than 60%") | Rayalaseema (Anantapur, Sri Sathya Sai, Kadapa, Nandyal, Kurnool); coastal uplands |
| 2 | **Black (regur)** | **~25% (one-fourth)** | Kurnool, Nandyal, YSR Kadapa, Guntur, Bapatla, Prakasam, Palnadu, Krishna |
| 3 | **Deltaic alluvium** | **~6%** | Godavari–Krishna deltas, Konaseema, Nellore, Guntur |
| — | Hill / skeletal | minor | heavily eroded tracts |
| Last | **Laterite** | **~1% (smallest)** | coastal pockets and hills |
- Red = **most extensive** soil of AP (APPSC 2025 key; APPSC-GS 2024: "more than 60%").
- Much of AP lies on **granitoid-gneiss** bedrock.
- India comparison: **alluvium** is India's largest soil group (**45.6%**, ~15 lakh sq km); in AP **red dominates** and alluvium is only ~6%. **Karnataka**: mostly red, then black.
- Red soil is the **third-largest** group of India (~10.6%); it **surrounds the black soil** in the south, east and north.
#### 1.1 Soil Categories (2024-25)
- **22** soil categories; **10** predominate and cover **over 88%** of AP.
- Largest: **"Loamy to clayey skeletal deep reddish-brown soils" – 20.42%**.
- Moderately deep calcareous black: **9.57%**; smallest listed: clayey moderately deep wet soils **0.13%**.
### 2. RED SOILS
- Formed by weathering of **old crystalline igneous and metamorphic rocks** – **granite, gneiss, Dharwar schists, mica schists, greenstones** (basalt gives black soil).
- Red colour = **iron oxides, mainly ferric oxide** (oxidised in well-drained soil).
- **Poor** in lime, phosphates, **nitrogen**, humus/organic matter; fairly rich in **potash**; mainly **siliceous**; well drained; **low moisture retention**.
- **Rayalaseema** red soils: **shallow**, low moisture retention, low organic matter. About 66% of AP's farmed regions.
- India: Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, AP, Telangana, Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand; western TN, NE AP, southern Maharashtra, eastern MP.
#### 2.1 Red-Soil Sub-types
| Sub-type | Share of AP |
|---|---|
| **Red earths with loamy subsoil** | **30% (largest)** |
| **Red soils with clay base** | **12%** |
| **Red loamy** | **9%** |
| **Red sandy (chalka)** | **8%** |
- APPSC-GS 2024 accepted all four statements: red soils over 60%, **six sub-groups**, loamy 9%, clay base 12%.
!TRAP Largest red sub-type = **loamy-subsoil red earth (30%)**, not red sandy.
### 3. BLACK SOILS
- AP black soils come from **Deccan Trap basalt** (Prakasam, Palnadu, Guntur, East Godavari) and **crystalline schists and basic gneiss**.
- Fine, **clayey**; **neutral to strongly alkaline**; colour from **iron and titanium** compounds.
- **Rich** in **calcium, potash, magnesium** (also alumina, iron, lime, magnesium carbonate); **poor** in **nitrogen, phosphorus, humus**.
- **Very high water retention**; swells when wet, **deep cracks** when dry → **"self-ploughing"**; needs little irrigation.
- **Deep black** at **Adoni and Alur (Aluru)**; unclassified black in West Godavari, Krishna, Guntur, mid-Kurnool, NW Kadapa, Tadipatri; **Kunderu basin** (Kadapa).
- **Very deep black soils: north-western Deccan Plateau** (APPSC-G2 2025); thinner towards east and south.
- India: Maharashtra (most widespread), MP, Gujarat, Karnataka; **Malwa Plateau** = black lava soil; **not in the Himalaya**.

### 5. DELTAIC ALLUVIUM AND COASTAL SANDS
- Laid as **river-borne silt** by the **Godavari and Krishna**; **renewed with silt each flood** (fresh silt does not harm the soil).
- **Most fertile** and most important soils of AP for production.
- Porous, loamy, high moisture retention; **rich in potash and lime** (and phosphoric acid); **poor in nitrogen and humus**.
- **Krishna delta**: around **Vijayawada–Machilipatnam**; **Godavari delta**: around **Rajahmundry and Kakinada**.
- Also along the **Vamsadhara and Penna**; Krishna–Godavari–Penna–Vamsadhara delta alluvium → **rice and sugarcane**.
- A **belt of coastal sands** lines the shore. **Alluvial sand** has the **minimum water retention**.
- India: northern plains (Punjab, Haryana, UP, Bihar, WB, Assam), Mahanadi–Godavari–Krishna–Cauvery deltas, Narmada–Tapi valleys; sand content falls west to east.
!KEY Most fertile soil of the K-G deltas = **deltaic alluvial**.
### 6. LATERITE SOILS
- Forms under **high temperature and heavy rainfall** with alternate wet and dry seasons.
- **Intense leaching** removes **silica and lime**; leaves **hydrated iron and aluminium oxides**.
- **Acidic**, low fertility, poor in **nitrogen**, organic matter, phosphorus; red to yellowish-red; well drained; not moisture-retentive; made infertile by extra iron.
- AP extent ~**1%**: coastal – **Ellavaram, Peddapuram (Kakinada), Kavali, Kovvur, Gudur, Sullurpeta**; elevated – **Satyavedu, Puttur**; pockets in Nellore, Srikakulam, East Godavari, Anakapalli, Visakhapatnam.
- North Coastal zone is typed by laterite. India: Western and Eastern Ghats, Maharashtra, **Malabar** coast; **not the Thar**.
!TRAP "Laterite forms in semi-arid, low-rainfall regions" = **incorrect** (APPSC 2023/2024).
### 7. SOIL → CROP SUITABILITY
| Soil | Crops in AP |
|---|---|
| **Deltaic alluvial** | **paddy, sugarcane**, tobacco, maize, vegetables |
| **Black** | **cotton, chillies, tobacco, turmeric**, maize, **jowar**, **black gram**, bajra |
| **Red** | **groundnut, castor, millets**, pulses (**red, green, horse gram**) |
| **Laterite** | **mango, cashew, tamarind**, **coconut**, plantation crops |
| Hill (skeletal) | coffee, tea, horticulture (ASR) |
- India: alluvial – wheat, rice, sugarcane, jute; laterite – tea, coffee, rubber, cashew.
- Black suits cotton because of **very high water retention**; black soil is not rich in nitrogen.
- Maharashtra black soil: **sugarcane competes with cotton** as irrigation expands.
- Legumes (**pea, black gram**) enrich soil nitrogen.

### 9. PROBLEM SOILS AND RECLAMATION
| Area | Problem |
|---|---|
| **Deltas** | **salinity, waterlogging** (poor drainage, faulty irrigation) |
| Coast | saline water intrusion; **Uppada (Kakinada)** coastal erosion from sea-level rise and waves |
| **Rayalaseema plateau** | saline soils high in **lime and salt** – unfit for cropping |
| Laterite tracts | **acidic** soils |
| Black soil tracts | **alkaline** tendency |
- **Salinity** lowers yields and breaks soil structure; **alkalinity** (high pH from limestone/basalt or alkaline water) cuts nutrient supply.
- **Reclamation**: **alkaline → gypsum**; **saline → drainage and leaching**.
- **39 watersheds (896 villages)** classed saline (2025-26); **SMWSIPDCL** (Salinity Mitigation & Water Security) company targets it.
- India: **Gujarat** = largest saline area; **UP** = most alkali land; desert soil (Thar, Gujarat) sandy, low humus, often saline; western Rajasthan soils high in calcium.
- AP's red lateritic and black soils have low fertility and salinity problems (true, but not the cause in assertion-reason items).
- **Halophytes** = saline soils; cryophytes – cold; lithophytes – rocks; xerophytes – deserts; hygrophytes – waterlogged; hydrophytes – aquatic.
### 10. EROSION AND DEGRADATION IN RAYALASEEMA
- **Anantapur, Sri Sathya Sai**: drought-induced degradation, **desertification**, wind erosion of topsoil.
- Heavily eroded tracts → **skeletal soils**: Nallamala slopes of **Badvel (Kadapa), Giddalur (Prakasam)**, patch at **Jammalamadugu**.
- Causes: overgrazing, deforestation, erratic rain; heavy chemicals in Guntur chilli and Krishna paddy belts; **Podu** strips topsoil in ASR and Parvathipuram Manyam.
- Sandy soils and steep slopes lose **soil organic carbon**; clay soils hold more.
- Responses: **APCNF** natural farming restores SOC; **Rainfed Area Development** – **9 rainfed districts** (8 Rayalaseema + Prakasam), **Rs 30,000 per farm family**; Kharif 2026 green-manure drive **₹62.20 crore**, 91,918 quintals of sunn hemp, dhaincha, green gram seed.
- Green manures by nitrogen: **cow pea 0.49%** > sunhemp 0.43% > dhaincha 0.42% > guar 0.34%.
### 11. SOIL HEALTH CARD AND SOIL TESTING
- **Soil Health Card Scheme**: launched **19 February 2015** at **Suratgarh** (Rajasthan).
- Since **2022-23** it runs as **"Soil Health & Fertility"** under **RKVY**.
- Cards give **soil-sample analysis** and **village-wise nutrient mapping**.
- **AP coverage**: **1,51,61,346 Soil Health Cards** issued (cumulative, 30 Jan 2025); India: **over 25 crore** cards (Jul 2025).
- AP 2025-26: **School Soil Health Programme** in **50 Kendriya Vidyalayas + 10 State schools**.
### 12. SOIL SCIENCE TERMS
- **Edaphic** = non-living factor linked to soil.
- **Capillary action** best in **clayey** soil (small pores), least in sandy; **clay** holds the most plant-available water.
- Ideal pH for common crops **6–7**; below 7 acidic, above 7 alkaline.
- **Podzol** = coniferous (taiga) belt near 60° N. **Soil geography** studies formation, types, fertility, distribution and use.
- Net sown area: West Bengal ~60%, AP ~40%; most of AP is not laterite.
