## U0535 | SEBI – ESTABLISHMENT, POWERS AND FUNCTIONS; INVESTOR PROTECTION AND MARKET REGULATION
### 1. SEBI – ESTABLISHMENT AND STATUTORY BASIS
#### 1.1 Basic Facts
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Full form | **Securities and Exchange Board of India** |
| Set up (non-statutory) | **12 April 1988** |
| Statutory status | **30 January 1992** under the **SEBI Act, 1992** (Act passed April 1992, in force from 30 Jan 1992) |
| Took over from | **Controller of Capital Issues** |
| Headquarters | **Mumbai** |
| Ministry | **Ministry of Finance** |
| Chairperson | **Tuhin Kanta Pandey** (from 2025; in office Mar 2026) |
| Role | Main regulator of the **capital and securities market** (stock markets) |
- SEBI's **parent law** is the **SEBI Act, 1992**, not the SCRA, 1956.
- **SEBI Act Preamble**: **investor protection**, **development** and **regulation** of the securities market. Bank lending rates are the RBI's job, not SEBI's.
- Government gave SEBI **regulatory powers to prevent capital-market scams** (APPSC 2025).
- Statutory powers came with the 1991-92 economic reforms.
!TRAP 1988 = set up as non-statutory body; 1992 = statutory under the SEBI Act. SEBI was not set up on the Narasimham Committee's advice (that committee came only in 1991).
#### 1.2 Chronology
1. SEBI non-statutory – **1988**
2. SEBI statutory – **1992** (SEBI Act, April 1992)
3. NSE set up – **November 1992**
4. NSE recognised – **April 1993**
5. SAT set up – **1995**
6. FMC merged into SEBI – **2015**
#### 1.3 Board Composition
- **Chairman** + **2** members from the **Finance and Law** ministries + **1** member from the **RBI** + **5** other members.
- Functions: registration of intermediaries, fees, **investor education**, inspection and audit.
#### 1.4 Market Terms
- **Primary market** – issue of **new** securities.
- **Secondary market** – trading of **existing** securities.

### 3. SEBI REGULATORY SCOPE – ENTITIES REGULATED
#### 3.1 Full List
| Regulated by SEBI | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs)** – stock exchanges, clearing corporations, depositories | NSE, BSE; NSDL, CDSL |
| Brokers, merchant bankers, registrars, **investment advisers** | Stock Brokers Regulations 2026 replaced the 1992 rules (Jan 2026) |
| **Mutual funds, AIFs, FPIs** | FPIs through the **SWAFAT-FI** single window (2025) |
| **REITs, InvITs** | 5 listed REITs, AUM above ₹2.25 lakh crore (2026) |
| **Credit rating agencies** | SEBI, **not RBI** |
| Commodity derivatives | Since FMC merger (2015) |
- **Credit rating agencies** in India: **CRISIL, CARE, ICRA**. Order of founding: **CRISIL (1987) → ICRA (1991) → CARE (1993)**.
- UPSC 2022: ICRA is a public limited company; Brickwork is Indian.
!TRAP CCP split: **money market, G-Sec and forex CCPs stay with the RBI**; capital-market CCPs are with SEBI.
#### 3.2 Mutual Fund Milestones
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1964 | **UTI** – India's **first mutual fund**, set up **1 February 1964** under the **UTI Act, 1963** |
| 1987 | Non-UTI **public-sector** mutual funds |
| 1993 | **Private-sector mutual funds** permitted; **first SEBI Mutual Funds Regulations** |
| 1996 | Revised SEBI Mutual Funds Regulations |
- **Largest mutual fund** today (by AUM): **SBI Mutual Fund**; UTI was the largest when the old question was asked.
- Mutual funds are regulated by **SEBI**, which is based in **Mumbai**.
### 4. COMMODITY MARKET REGULATION – FORWARD MARKETS COMMISSION
- **Forward Markets Commission (FMC)**: regulator of **commodity futures** trading; set up **1953**.
- Worked under the **Forward Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1952**.
- **Merged with SEBI on 28 September 2015**; SEBI now regulates commodity derivatives.
- After the merger: brokers, NSE/BSE and FPIs allowed in commodity derivatives; **Category III AIFs** allowed.
!NUM 1952 = FCRA; 1953 = FMC set up; 2015 = FMC merged into SEBI
### 5. SEBI POWERS AND THE SECURITIES APPELLATE TRIBUNAL
#### 5.1 Three Kinds of Power
| Power | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Quasi-legislative** | Framing regulations |
| **Quasi-judicial** | Orders, penalties, **disgorgement** |
| **Investigative** | **Inspection, search and seizure** |
#### 5.2 Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT)
- Set up in **1995** under the **SEBI Act** (Section 15K).
- Composition: **Presiding Officer** + members.
- Hears **appeals against SEBI and stock exchanges**.
- Further appeal: **SAT → Supreme Court**.
- **NSE case**: the Supreme Court disposed of SEBI's appeals against NSE (co-location, dark fibre) after a **₹1,492 crore** settlement (₹1,224 crore + ₹268 crore).
!KEY Appeal chain: SEBI order → SAT → Supreme Court (not High Court).
### 6. INVESTOR PROTECTION TOOLS
| Term | Meaning |

| **SCORES** | SEBI's **online investor complaints redress system** |

- New tools: new UPI address structure for intermediaries and **"SEBI Check"**.
- **Mission Jagrook** launched on SEBI's **38th Foundation Day**.
!TRAP IEPF is under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, not SEBI.
### 7. SCAMS THAT SHAPED REGULATION
| Scam | Reform triggered |
|---|---|
| Harshad Mehta (1992) | Pushed SEBI's statutory powers and screen-based trading (NSE) |
| **Ketan Parekh (2001)** | **Badla ended**; move to **rolling settlement** |
| **Satyam (2009)** – **"India's Enron"**, accounting fraud | Audit and board reforms in **Companies Act 2013**; **NFRA** |
| **Sahara OFCDs** | **Private placement capped at 50 investors**; SEBI power over deemed public issues |

### 9. RECENT SEBI REFORMS
#### 9.1 Market Reforms
- **T+0 (same-day) settlement**: **optional**, alongside **T+1**; India among the first globally. Aims: **liquidity**, lower **settlement and counterparty risk**, efficiency (APPSC G2 2025).
- **BRSR** (Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report): set by **SEBI (not RBI)** for the **top 1,000 listed companies**; **not mandatory for all** listed companies; **replaced the BRR**; **BRSR Core** needs **reasonable assurance** on key ESG metrics.
- **SME IPOs**: surged **87%** (FY23-25); SEBI chief says the SME market is under-scaled and lacks intermediaries.
- **Finfluencers**: stock tips or return claims = advisory work needing **SEBI registration**; regulated entities cannot work with unregistered finfluencers.
- **Verified "tick mark"** for broker apps – SEBI with **Google**.
#### 9.2 Securities Markets Code Bill, 2025
- Introduced in **Lok Sabha, December 2025**.
- Merges **SEBI Act 1992, SCRA 1956, Depositories Act 1996**.
- SEBI board up to **15** members; MIIs get statutory status.
#### 9.3 AP Angle
- **AP MSME Development Corporation + NSE** ran an **SME IPO awareness programme** in **Vijayawada**.
### 10. DEBT MARKET AND INFRASTRUCTURE FINANCING
- **REIT and InvIT Regulations** (SEBI, 2014) let real-estate and infrastructure assets raise money from investors.
- **Municipal bonds**: SEBI regulations for issue and listing of municipal debt securities.
- **Large corporate borrowing framework**: large listed companies must raise a part of fresh borrowing through bonds.
- Lower **bond ticket (face value) size** to draw retail investors.
- **RBI Retail Direct Scheme**: lets retail investors buy **G-Secs** directly from the RBI; G-Secs stay with the RBI, not SEBI.
