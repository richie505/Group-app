## U0750 | HYDROPOWER AND PUMPED STORAGE; LARGE DAMS AND THEIR TRADE-OFFS
### 1. ENERGY CONVERSION IN A HYDRO PLANT
- Sequence: stored water (**potential**) → falling water (**kinetic**) → turbine (**mechanical**) → generator (**electrical**, by **electromagnetic induction**).
- Net: **potential → electrical** (APPSC 2023).
- Mechanical energy = **kinetic + potential**. **Thermal energy** = internal energy of moving molecules, not potential; electric, chemical, nuclear are stored (potential) forms.
| Device / process | Conversion |
|---|---|
| **Dynamo / generator** | Mechanical → electrical (DC via commutator) |
| **Electric motor** | Electrical → mechanical |
| Battery / **dry cell** | Chemical → electrical |
| **Photoelectric / solar cell** | Light → electrical |
| Wind turbine | Kinetic → mechanical/electrical |
| Free fall, pendulum, roller coaster | Potential → kinetic |
| **Friction** | Kinetic → heat |
- Dynamo and motor work on **electromagnetic induction (Faraday's law)**. Wind energy is only **kinetic**. **Photoelectric effect**: **Heinrich Hertz, 1887**. Lightning burning a tree = huge **electric** energy. PYQ key: electrical energy converted into motion – no heat loss.
### 2. TYPES AND CLASSIFICATION
| Type | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Run-of-the-river** | Little or no storage; natural flow; barely changes stream |
| **Storage** | Dam stores water, regulates output |
| **Pumped storage** | Surplus power pumps water up; released at peak |
- **Small hydro ≤25 MW = MNRE**; **large hydro >25 MW = Ministry of Power**.
- Small hydro up to **3 MW** moved to MNRE in **1989**; raised to **25 MW** in **November 1999**.
- **March 2019**: large hydro declared **renewable**.
!TRAP Large hydro is under Ministry of Power yet counted as renewable since March 2019.
### 3. HYDRO POTENTIAL AND DAMS DATA
- Installed: large hydro **51.96 GW**; small hydro **5.18 GW**.
- Potential **over 145 GW**; only about **36%** developed. **Arunachal Pradesh** = largest state potential; Himalayan belt: HP, Uttarakhand, J&K, Arunachal.
- Small hydro potential **21,133 MW** at **7,133 sites**. AP hydro + reverse-pumped potential about **4 GW**.
- India **3rd** in large dams: **6,628** specified dams, ~330 BCM; most in **Maharashtra**, then MP, Gujarat.
- Peninsular east-flowing rivers suit **irrigation** more than power; **waterfalls** aid hydropower.
!TRAP 7,133 = small hydro sites; 6,628 = specified dams.
### 4. LARGE DAMS – SUSTAINABILITY AND TRADE-OFFS
- Hydro = **best source for sustainable development** (UPPSC 2012): uses water without using it up; cheap and clean once built; flood control, irrigation, grid flexibility.
- Weakness: **drought and snowpack** variability.
| Trade-off | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Displacement** | Bhakra Nangal, Sardar Sarovar, Tehri; 18.5 million affected (1950-90) |
| **Seismicity** | Himalayan risk |
| **Sedimentation** | Cuts reservoir capacity |
| Ecology | **Blocks fish migration** (does not improve it) |
- Dam heights (descending): **Tehri > Bhakra > Idukki > Koldam**.

### 7. PUMPED STORAGE
- **Two reservoirs** at different heights: surplus (solar/wind hours) pumps water up; released through turbines at peak.
- "**Giant water battery**" – **fastest-responding large-scale storage**; **long-duration energy storage** (UPSC 2024).
- Support: **no ISTS charges for BESS and PSP until 2028**; **off-stream closed-loop** PSPs need no CEA concurrence.
- CEA NGAP 2035-36: storage **174 GW = 94 GW PSP + 80 GW BESS**; 1,121 GW capacity; peak 459 GW.
| PSP | State | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Tehri PSP** | Uttarakhand | **1,000 MW** (4 × 250); fully commissioned **12 April 2026**; Tehri complex 2,400 MW |
| **Sharavathi PSP** | Karnataka | **2,000 MW**; Talakalale and Gerusoppa dams |
- Debate: PSP "very limited in scope" – environment, water, regulatory hurdles.

### 11. THERMAL ISSUES AND REFORMS
- **FGD scrubbers** trap **SO₂**; old units retired to meet norms. Coal plants: largest CO₂ source, PM2.5, heavy water use.
- **5% biomass co-firing** – **SAMARTH** mission (2021).
- **Revised SHAKTI (May 2025)**: **Window-I** – coal at notified price; **Window-II** – bidding at a premium.
- **Flexible operation**: batteries, pumped storage, cyclical gas operation, hydro optimisation.
