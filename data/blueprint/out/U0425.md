## U0425 | INDIA AND NEPAL, BHUTAN, BANGLADESH, SRI LANKA, MYANMAR, MALDIVES, AFGHANISTAN
### 1. TREATIES WITH NEIGHBOURS
| Treaty | Year | Country | Key point |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Friendship Treaty** | **1949**, revised **2007** | Bhutan | 2007 removed the clause that Bhutan be **"guided" by India** in foreign affairs |
| **Treaty of Peace and Friendship** | **1950** | Nepal | Open border, security cooperation; Nepal seeks revision |
| **Land Boundary Agreement** | **1974**; implemented **2015** (**100th Amendment**) | Bangladesh | **111 Indian enclaves** to Bangladesh; **51 Bangladeshi enclaves** to India |
| **Indo-Sri Lanka Accord** | **1987** | Sri Lanka | Rajiv Gandhi–**J.R. Jayewardene**; merger of Northern and Eastern provinces; 13th Amendment devolution; IPKF |
- **Tin Bigha**: leased to Bangladesh in **1992** for access to **Dahagram-Angarpota** enclaves.
- DTAA-changing protocol with **Sri Lanka**: approved by the Union Cabinet, **2020**.
!TRAP Bhutan treaty 1949; Nepal treaty 1950.

### 3. POLITICAL CHANGES IN NEIGHBOURS
#### 3.1 Nepal
- **First Constituent Assembly** abolished the monarchy — **28 May 2008**; new Constitution **20 September 2015**.
- **Pushpa Kamal Dahal 'Prachanda'** (UCPN-Maoist): **first PM** of the Federal Democratic Republic; **Ram Baran Yadav**: **first President**.
- 2026: new youth-led leadership pushing to revise the 1950 Treaty.
#### 3.2 Others

### 4. INDIA–SRI LANKA AND THE LTTE
- **IPKF (Operation Pawan)** sent under the 1987 Accord; ended up fighting the LTTE; withdrawn on V.P. Singh's order — last troops left **24 March 1990**.
- **LTTE** killed **Rajiv Gandhi (1991)** → India banned it **1992** (the ban was **not** part of the 1987 Accord). Also banned by Sri Lanka, USA and many others.
- **2009**: Sri Lankan Army crushed the LTTE; chief **V. Prabhakaran** killed.
- **Katchatheevu** ceded to Sri Lanka in **1974**; fishermen issue.
