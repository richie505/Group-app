## U0032 | OTHER HETERODOX SECTS – AJIVIKAS (MAKKHALI GOSALA), CHARVAKA / LOKAYATA MATERIALISM
### 1. SIX HETERODOX TEACHERS OF THE BUDDHA'S TIME
| Teacher | Doctrine |
|---|---|
| **Makkhali Gosala** | **Niyativada** – fatalism (Ajivika) |
| **Purana Kassapa** | **Akriyavada** – actions bring neither merit nor demerit |
| **Ajita Kesakambalin** | **Materialist** – nothing survives death |
| **Pakudha Kaccayana** | **Seven eternal**, uncreated elements |
| **Sanjaya Belatthiputta** | **Sceptic** – would neither affirm nor deny ("eel-wriggling") |
| **Nigantha Nataputta** | = **Mahavira** (Jain) |
!TRAP Niyativada = Gosala; Akriyavada = Purana Kassapa; seven elements = Pakudha; Pratityasamutpada = Buddhist.
### 2. AJIVIKAS – DOCTRINE AND LIFE
- **Makkhali Gosala** – usual exam answer for **founder**; most important leader and populariser (**third chief**) (UKPSC 2024).
- **Niyati** (fate): all thoughts and actions **fixed before birth**; **no free will**; **karma cannot change destiny**; order of rebirth cannot be changed.
- "Fate assigns everything, human is incapable" = Ajivikas.
- **Atomic theory** of matter – influenced the later **Vaisheshika**.
- **Extreme austerities**; ascetics called **Sudra sannyasins**; **no caste discrimination**; appealed to warrior, industrial and mercantile classes.
### 3. AJIVIKAS, MAHAVIRA AND THE BUDDHA
- Gosala was **Mahavira's companion for about six years** before breaking away; Jain texts make him Mahavira's disciple.
- **Majjhima Nikaya** records the Buddha's debates with Jains and Ajivikas.
### 4. AJIVIKAS AND RULERS
| Ruler / record | Ajivika link |
|---|---|
| **Bindusara** | Ajivikas popular under him |
| **Ashoka** | **Donated the Barabar caves** (Bihar) – among the **earliest rock-cut caves** in India |
| **7th Pillar Edict (Delhi-Topra)** | Names Ajivikas with **Brahmanas, the Sangha and Nirgranthas** |
!TRAP Barabar caves were given by Ashoka, not Chandragupta (UPSC 2013). "Ashoka was an Ajivika" is false – he was Buddhist.
### 5. CHARVAKA / LOKAYATA
- Other names: **Lokayata** (APPSC GS 2025), **Barhaspatya** (after legendary founder **Brihaspati**).
- **Complete materialism**; highest value = **Kama** (material pleasure).
- **Dehatmavada**: "**The conscious body is the soul**" – no separate soul survives death (CGPSC 2024).
- Only pramana = **perception (pratyaksha)**; inference and testimony (Vedas) rejected; rejects Vedic ritualism and supernaturalism.
- Famous aphorism: "**Yavaj jivet sukham jivet, rinam kritva ghritam pibet**" – live happily while you live; drink ghee even on debt.
!PAIR Charvaka | Perception only | 1 pramana || Nyaya | Perception, inference, comparison, testimony | 4 pramanas
### 6. SOURCES
| Source | Type | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| **Samannaphala Sutta** | **Digha Nikaya** (Buddhist) | **Ajatashatru** reports the six teachers' views to the Buddha |
| **Bhagavati Sutra** | **Jain Anga** | Fullest account of Gosala and his **quarrel with Mahavira** |
| 7th Pillar Edict, Barabar inscriptions | Inscriptions | Ajivikas named; caves gifted |
| **Brihaspati Sutra** | Charvaka original | **Lost** – no surviving texts |
| **Sarvadarshanasangraha** | Madhava (opponent) | Lokayata known mainly via rivals' criticism |
### 7. DECLINE
- Ajivikas survived nearly **2,000 years**, to the **14th c. CE**, in **Karnataka and Tamil Nadu**, then disappeared.
- Causes: **Puranic-Bhakti revival** (incl. under the **Pallavas**) and **loss of royal patronage**.
### 8. HETERODOX VIEWS COMPARED
| School | Karma | Soul | Fate | Vedas |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jainism | Removable by penance | **Eternal jiva in all things** | Effort | Rejected |
| Buddhism | Intention-based | **Anatta** | Effort | Rejected |
| Ajivika | Cannot alter destiny | Accepted | **Niyati – predetermined** | Rejected |
| Charvaka | Does not decide rebirth | Conscious body | – | **Rejected** |
- **Shramana** movements (6th c. BCE: Jainism, Buddhism, Ajivika, Charvaka) arose amid **urbanisation and Magadhan patronage**.
