## U0431 | BRETTON WOODS INSTITUTIONS – IMF (QUOTA, SDR), WORLD BANK GROUP; ADB, AIIB, NDB
### 1. BRETTON WOODS CONFERENCE AND FOUNDING
- **UN Monetary and Financial Conference** = Bretton Woods Conference, **July 1944**, **Mount Washington Hotel, Bretton Woods, New Hampshire, USA**.
- **730 delegates** from **44 allied nations**; plan built on the ideas of **Harry Dexter White** and **John Maynard Keynes**.
- Created: **IMF + IBRD**; agreements also covered GATT.
- Not created: the proposed **International Trade Organization (ITO)** → **GATT (1947)** filled the gap until the **WTO (1995)**.
- System: fixed exchange rates; currencies pegged to the dollar, dollar pegged to gold. **Smithsonian Agreement (1971)** replaced the parities, widened the band to **2.25%**; lasted about **14 months**.
| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| IMF and IBRD founded | **1944** |
| IMF formal existence (29 members) | **27 December 1945** |
| IMF began operations | **1 March 1947** |
| First IMF borrower | **France**, 8 May 1947 |
| SDR created ("paper gold") | 1967-69 |
!TRAP 1944 = founded; 27 Dec 1945 = formal existence; 1 March 1947 = operations. All three are asked.
- **India** = founding member of both **IMF and IBRD**.
### 2. IMF VS WORLD BANK
| Basis | IMF | World Bank |
|---|---|---|
| Aim | **Monetary stability**, technical help | **Development** |
| Lending | **Short-term balance of payments** problems | Projects; **medium and long term** |
| Voting | By quota; **US alone holds a veto** | - |
| HQ | **Washington, D.C.** (not New York) | Washington, D.C. |
- IMF gives loans **only to member countries**; project financing is the World Bank's job.
- **WTO** (Geneva) = multilateral trade negotiations.
### 3. IMF – QUOTA, SDR AND FACILITIES
- **Members: 191** – Liechtenstein became the 191st on 21 Oct 2024.
- **Quotas** decide financial contribution, **borrowing access** and **voting power**; reviewed periodically; they do **not fix exchange rates**.
- **Quota formula**: GDP **50%**, openness **30%**, variability **15%**, reserves **5%**.
- **India**: quota about **2.75%**, votes about **2.63%**, **8th** by quota (reform effective 25 Nov 2024).
- **SDR** basket of **five** currencies since **October 2016**: US dollar, euro, **Chinese renminbi (yuan)**, yen, pound. IMF lends in SDRs.
- **IMFC** (International Monetary and Financial Committee) advises the Board of Governors.
- **Article IV consultations**: IMF's regular review of each member's economy.
- Facilities: **Stand-By Arrangement**, **Extended Fund Facility**, **Rapid Financing Instrument**, **Rapid Credit Facility**, **PRGT**; ESAF on softest terms.
!TRAP SDR basket = five currencies, not four. **Political-risk guarantees** = MIGA, not IMF.
### 4. WORLD BANK GROUP – FIVE INSTITUTIONS
| Institution | Year | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **IBRD** | 1944 | Loans to **middle-income** governments for infrastructure |
| **IFC** | **1956** | **Private-sector** investment, advisory; 184 members; conceived "Doing Business" |
| **IDA** | **1960** | **Interest-free credits**, concessional loans, grants to **poorest**; run by the IBRD (soft loans) |
| **ICSID** | **1966** (not 1976) | Settles **investment disputes**; **India not a member** |
| **MIGA** | **1988** | **Political-risk insurance** and guarantees |
- IBRD and IFC are UN specialised agencies (World Bank Group), like IFAD and IMF.

### 7. NEW DEVELOPMENT BANK (BRICS)
- Idea: **4th BRICS Summit, New Delhi, 2012**.
- Agreement: **6th Summit, Fortaleza, Brazil, 15 July 2014** (**Fortaleza Declaration**); operations **2015**.
- HQ **Shanghai**; regional offices **Johannesburg, São Paulo, GIFT City (India), Moscow**.
- Founders hold **equal votes; no veto**; open to all UN members.
- Presidency rotates; Russia gave up its turn → Brazilian incumbent's term runs to 2030 (Sep 2026).
- **Russia** = 20% shareholder; NDB froze Russia-linked work, **March 2022**.
- Record (Sep 2026): 139 projects, ~$43 bn approved, only ~$20 bn disbursed.
- 16th BRICS Summit: Kazan, Oct 2024. **New Delhi Declaration** (18th, Sep 2026) demanded IMF/WB quota and voting reform; Global North (~15% of population) holds ~60% of votes.
### 8. ASIAN DEVELOPMENT BANK
- Est. **1966** at **UN-ESCAP's** initiative (not UNCTAD); **India a founding member**.
- HQ **Manila, Philippines**; **69 members** (49 Asia-Pacific, 20 non-regional); **Türkiye** newest (2025).
- Largest shareholders: **Japan and USA** (~15.6% each); **India 4th** (~6.3%).
- President traditionally **Japanese**.
- **Nature Solutions Finance Hub** for Asia-Pacific (2023): money for mangroves, forests, ecosystem restoration.
- India is a member of ADB and the **Colombo Plan**; **not** of APEC or OECD.
### 9. AIIB
- HQ **Beijing**; Articles signed **2015**; operations **January 2016**; rating **AAA**.
- **111** approved members (Sep 2026); **China largest** (~26.5% votes), **India second** (~7.6%).
- Scale: ~$69 bn across 350 projects (Sep 2026).
!PAIR Manila | ADB | Japan-led || Beijing | AIIB | China-led; Shanghai = NDB
