## U0243 | ANDHRA IN THE QUIT INDIA MOVEMENT, 1942
### 1. BACKGROUND - INDIVIDUAL SATYAGRAHA, 1940-41
- **Congress ministries resigned** in **November 1939** → limited **Individual Satyagraha**.
- Order: **Vinoba Bhave first**, **Jawaharlal Nehru second**.
### 2. 1942 DATES - NATIONAL FRAME AND ANDHRA
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 14 July 1942 | **Wardha CWC** resolution |
| 23 July 1942 | **CPI ban lifted** |
| **29 July 1942** | **Andhra (Kurnool) Circular** - before the Bombay AICC |
| **8 Aug 1942** | AICC, **Gowalia Tank, Bombay** - "Do or Die" |
| 9 Aug 1942 | Gandhi arrested in Bombay → **Aga Khan Palace, Pune** (with **Sarojini Naidu**); CWC → **Ahmednagar Fort** |
| 12 Aug 1942 | **Tenali** railway station firing - about **nine** dead |
- **Samadhis** of **Kasturba** and **Mahadev Desai**: Aga Khan Palace, Poona.
!TRAP AICC was on **8 August** (not 6 August); Gandhi's paper was **Harijan**, not Young India.
### 3. ANDHRA (KURNOOL) CIRCULAR
- **Author:** **Kala Venkata Rao** (APPSC 2019 key); dated **29 July 1942**.
- **Contents:** directives (action programme) for the coming movement.
- **Fate:** **seized by police at Kurnool** → hence "Kurnool Circular".
- **APCC** met at **Machilipatnam** under **Pattabhi Sitaramayya**.

### 6. STUDENTS AND WOMEN
- **Andhra University** students' protest; **Mamidipudi Venkatarangayya** (History head) - resolution demanding **Gandhi's release**.
- **Chitturi Annapurna Devi** (aged **17**) - arrested at **Guntur** for pamphlets and picketing.
- **Duvvuri Subbamma** - faced the **lathi charge at Peddapuram**.
!PAIR Chitturi Annapurna Devi | Guntur | Arrest || Duvvuri Subbamma | Peddapuram | Lathi charge
### 7. COMMUNISTS VS CONGRESS SOCIALISTS
- **CPI**: ban lifted **23 July 1942** → **backed the British war effort**, **opposed Quit India**, lost public support.
- **Third Communist Conference, Vijayawada, 1943** - first open Communist conference in Andhra; secretary **Chandra Rajeswara Rao**.
- Price: crushing defeats in **1945** (only **Pillalamarri Venkateswara Rao** won from Andhra) and **1946**.
- **Congress Socialists** led the national underground: **JP, Rammanohar Lohia, Aruna Asaf Ali, Usha Mehta** (secret radio).
- **Jun 2025:** Tamil Nadu freedom fighter **R.P.G.V. Govindan Chettiyar** (jailed six months in Individual Satyagraha) honoured under **Azadi Ka Amrit Mahotsav**.
