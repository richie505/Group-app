## U0646 | INDIA - POPULATION DISTRIBUTION AND DENSITY; STATE-WISE AND DISTRICT-WISE PATTERNS
### 1. DENSITY - MEASURES AND CENSUS 2011
#### 1.1 Three Density Measures
| Measure | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Arithmetic** | Total population / **total area** |
| **Physiological** | Total population / **net cultivated area** |
| **Agricultural** | **Agricultural** population / net cultivable area |
!TRAP Physiological = all people per net cultivated area; agricultural = only farm people.
- Density **rises** with natality and immigration; **falls** with mortality and emigration.
#### 1.2 Census 2011 Figures
| Item | Density (per sq km) |
|---|---|
| India | **382** (325 in 2001) |
| Densest state | **Bihar** (over 1,100) |
| Densest UT | **NCT of Delhi** (over 11,000), then Chandigarh |
| Sparsest | **Arunachal Pradesh** (17) |

### 2. FACTORS OF UNEVEN DISTRIBUTION
| Factor | Dense | Sparse |
|---|---|---|
| Water | River valleys | **Thar desert** |
| Soil | Loamy alluvium | Thin, rocky soils |
| Relief | Plains | Hills, mountains |
- Mountain exception: valleys such as **Kashmir** and parts of **Himachal Pradesh** have higher density.
### 3. REGIONS OF HIGH DENSITY
- **Ganga plain** and **Ganges-Brahmaputra delta** (among densest on Earth): fertile alluvium, water, intensive agriculture.
- **Godavari-Krishna** coastal deltas: intensive rice farming.
- **Dhanbad**: densest district of the **Chotanagpur plateau** due to **coal mining and industrialisation** (BCCL based there); red soil is not useful for farming (UPPSC 2007).
### 4. POPULATION-RESOURCE REGIONS
| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Progressive** (resource-developed) | **Ganga-Yamuna Doab**, **Tamil Nadu** (UPPSC 2010) |
| **Problem** | Population pressure on limited or degraded resources |
| **Deficit** | Resources exceed use |
!TRAP Progressive regions are NOT coastal Odisha or Rajasthan.
### 5. BIMARU STATES
- **BI**har, **MA**dhya Pradesh, **R**ajasthan, **U**ttar Pradesh; Hindi word "bimar" = sick.
- Coined by demographer **Ashish Bose**, mid-1980s (**1986**, APPSC-GS 2019).
- Profile: high population growth and weak socio-economic indicators.
- Faster growth than Kerala, TN, Karnataka, AP links them to the **delimitation debate**.
### 6. STATE-WISE POPULATION AND AREA RANKING

| Rank | State | Share |

| 2 | Maharashtra | 9.29% |

- **Bihar**: 3rd in population but **12th in area** (BPSC 2008), hence high density.
- Least populous state: **Sikkim** (6.08 lakh), then Mizoram, Arunachal, Goa, Nagaland.
- Least populous units overall include Lakshadweep, Daman and Diu.
#### 6.2 Residual AP (2024-25)
- **10th** in population (**4.09%**); **7th** by area (**1,62,970 sq km**, ahead of Odisha).
- 28-district reorganisation (2025: **Polavaram, Markapuram**) changed district density extremes.
