## U0118 | PROVINCIAL KINGDOMS – BAHMANI SULTANATE AND MAHMUD GAWAN; BENGAL, GUJARAT, MALWA, JAUNPUR, KASHMIR
### 1. FOUNDING ORDER OF PROVINCIAL KINGDOMS
| Kingdom | Founder | Year / capital |
|---|---|---|
| **Bengal (Ilyas Shahi)** | **Haji Ilyas (Shamsuddin Ilyas Shah)** | Unified Bengal **1342**; capital **Pandua** |
| **Bahmani** | **Alauddin Hasan Bahman Shah (Hasan Gangu, Zafar Khan)** | **1347**; capital **Gulbarga** |
| **Jaunpur (Sharqi)** | **Malik Sarwar (Khwaja Jahan)**, title **Malik-us-Sharq** ("Lord of the East") | **1394** |
| **Malwa** | **Dilawar Khan Ghuri** broke from Delhi | **1401**; Hoshang Shah moved capital **Dhar → Mandu** |
| **Gujarat** | **Zafar Khan (Muzaffar Shah I)** | **1407**; Ahmedabad 1411 |
!NUM 1342 Bengal – 1347 Bahmani – 1394 Sharqi – 1401 Malwa – 1407 Gujarat
### 2. BAHMANI SULTANATE
- Founded **1347** by Hasan Gangu, who **revolted against Muhammad bin Tughlaq**; **first independent Muslim kingdom of the Deccan**.
- First capital **Gulbarga** (named **Hasanabad**); four regions (tarafs): **Gulbarga, Daulatabad, Berar, Bidar**.
- **Muhammad Shah I** built the **Gulbarga Jama Masjid** (fully covered courtyard).
- **Firuz Shah (1397–1422)**: scholar-king; peak strength.
- **Ahmad Shah I** (not Muhammad Shah I) shifted the capital **Gulbarga → Bidar**.
- Last Sultan **Kalimullah**; kingdom ended **1527**.
#### 2.1 Wars with Vijayanagara (AP Angle)
- Main cause of conflict: **Raichur Doab** (between Krishna and Tungabhadra).
- Other disputed zones: **Krishna-Godavari delta**, **Marathwada**.
!TRAP "Who shifted the capital to Bidar?" = **Ahmad Shah I**, not Muhammad Shah I.
### 3. MAHMUD GAWAN
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Origin / office | **Persian (Afaqi)**; **wazir** (Prime Minister) of **Muhammad Shah III**; kingdom at its greatest extent |
| Administrative reform | Divided the **4 tarafs into 8** provinces; forts under **central commanders** – to curb provincial nobles |
| Military | Took **Goa (1472)**; crushed **Telangana revolts** (from 1453) with **Velama** help (not Reddis) |
| Letters | ***Riyaz-ul-Insha*** |
| Madrasa at **Bidar** | **Three storeys**, glazed tiles, huge library; **Persian (Herat/Samarkand)** model |
| End | Framed by rival nobles; **executed 1481** → break-up of the kingdom |
!TRAP Madrasa model = Herat/Samarkand; the **Cordoba** comparison is for the **Gulbarga Jama Masjid**.

### 5. KASHMIR (SHAH MIR DYNASTY)
| Ruler | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Sikandar (1389–1413)** | **"Butshikan"** (idol-breaker); temples destroyed, **jizya imposed**, Brahmins driven out |
| **Zain-ul-Abidin (1420–1470)** | Original name **Shahi Khan** (brother of Ali Shah); **"Bud Shah"** (Great King) and **"Akbar of Kashmir"**; ruled ~50 years |
#### 5.1 Zain-ul-Abidin – Contributions
- **First to abolish jizya** on Hindus; temples rebuilt; Brahmins called back; **banned cow killing**.
- **Mahabharata** and Kalhana's **Rajatarangini** translated into Persian; **Jonaraja** continued the Rajatarangini.
- **Zaina Kadal** bridge (Srinagar); **Zaina Lank** island in **Wular** lake.
- Completed **Jama Masjid, Srinagar**: turrets, **pagoda-like roofs (Buddhist)**, Persian style – all three.
!PAIR Butshikan | Sikandar | imposed jizya || Bud Shah | Zain-ul-Abidin | abolished jizya
### 6. JAUNPUR (SHARQI)
- City **founded by Firoz Shah Tughlaq**, named after **Muhammad bin Tughlaq** (Jauna Khan).
- Called **"Shiraz-i-Hind"** (Shiraz of India / of the East) – centre of learning; peak under **Ibrahim Shah (1402–40)**.
- Last ruler **Hussain Shah**; fell to **Bahlul Lodi, 1479**. Jaunpur school dated 1394–1479.
- Sharqi court supported **Khyal**; Sufi musician **Pir Bodhan** under **Husain Shah Sharqi** (not Ibrahim).
### 7. MALWA, GUJARAT AND BENGAL RULERS
| Ruler | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Hoshang Shah** (Malwa) | Capital to Mandu; **first marble tomb** in India |
| **Mahmud Khalji** (Malwa) | Founded the Khalji line; completed Jami Masjid, Mandu |
| **Ghiyasuddin Khalji** (Malwa) | **Jahaz Mahal**, **Hindola Mahal** |
| **Baz Bahadur** (Malwa) | **Last Sultan**; romance with **Rupmati**; defeated by **Akbar** |
| **Ahmad Shah I** (Gujarat) | Founded **Ahmedabad (1411)** on the **Sabarmati** |
| **Mahmud Begarha** (Gujarat, 1458–1511) | Took **Girnar (Junagadh)** and **Champaner** – the "two forts"; joined Egypt against Portuguese (Chaul 1508, lost at Diu 1509) |
| **Bahadur Shah** (Gujarat, 1526–37) | Annexed Malwa (1531), attacked Chittor (1535), beaten by Humayun, killed by Portuguese at **Diu (1537)** |
| **Hussain Shah (1493–1519)** + son **Nusrat Shah** (Bengal) | Supported **Bengali Mahabharata** translations; **Chaitanya** lived in Hussain Shah's reign |
