## U0113 | SULTANATE PROVINCIAL AND MILITARY ADMINISTRATION – IQTA, DAGH AND HULIYA, ARMY
### 1. IQTA SYSTEM
- **Iqta** = land revenue of a territory assigned to an officer **instead of cash salary**; not ownership.
- Origin: **Caliph's empire** (land to army officers for limited periods); a **Turkish, foreign** institution.
- **First iqta in India**: **Hansi**, by **Muhammad Ghori** to **Qutbuddin Aibak**.
- **Iltutmish** introduced it in the Sultanate as a **systematic institution** (UPPSC 2010); iqtas to Turkish officers.
- Holder: **iqtadar / muqti** (**wali** for a province). Duties: keep **troops**, collect **revenue**, keep **law and order**, send **surplus (fawazil)** to the centre.
- Iqta revenue did **not** go directly to the Sultan; the muqti collected it. Early Turkish administration was mainly **military** for this reason.
- Iqtas were **transferable** until **Firoz** made them **hereditary**. Ancestor of the Mughal **jagir and mansab** system.
!TRAP "Iqta was an ancient indigenous institution" is FALSE (UPSC 2019).
### 2. CHANGES BY SULTAN AND ARMY PAY
| Sultan | Change |
|---|---|
| **Iltutmish** | Organised the system; paid through iqtas |
| **Balban** | Tried to resume old soldiers' iqtas; **khwajas** (auditors) checked muqtis |
| **Alauddin** | First large **standing army paid in cash**; enlarged **khalisa**; took back **Milq, Idrarat, Waqf** lands |
| **Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq** | Inquiry into grants; unlawful grants seized; improved **post** (runners, horsemen) |
| **Firoz** | Iqtas **hereditary**; **revived** the jagir; hurt army efficiency |
!KEY Alauddin = cash pay; Firoz = hereditary iqtas.
### 3. LAND ASSIGNMENT TERMS
| Term | Meaning | Dynasty |
|---|---|---|
| **Iqta** | Revenue assignment | **Delhi Sultans** |
| **Khalisa** | **Crown land**; revenue for court and household | Sultanate |
| **Jagir** | Assignment to a mansabdar | **Mughals** (also Bahmani, Qutb Shahi) |
| **Amaram** | Land to **Amaranayakas** for troops and **kappam** | **Vijayanagara** |
| **Mokasa** | Assignment for military service | **Marathas** |
| **Mahrousa** | **Protected town** (UPPSC 2023) | Sultanate |
| **Inam** | Grants to religious men | Sultanate |
| **Madad-i-Mash** | **Revenue-free** grant to learned, pious, saints | Mughal |
- Sultanate land = **Khalisa and Iqta**, not "Khalsa and Jagirs" (UPPSC 2025).
- **Zabt** = Mughal revenue system; **Maal** = Mughal land revenue; **Zakat** = tax on Muslims; **Tanka** = Iltutmish's silver coin, not a tax.
- Maratha: **Chauth = one-fourth**; **Sardeshmukhi = one-tenth**.

### 5. ARMY, DAGH AND HULIYA
- **Dagh** (branding of horses) and **huliya** (descriptive roll) = **Alauddin Khalji**; stopped **substandard horses** and **proxy soldiers**. **Sher Shah** revived dagh.
- Ranks, lowest to highest: **sar-i-khail → sipah-salar → amir → malik → khan**; Khan commanded **10 maliks**.
- Technology: **iron stirrup**, **mounted archers**, armour, stronger forts; horses imported from **West Asia**.
!TRAP "Balban introduced branding of horses" is FALSE (UPSC 2002).
