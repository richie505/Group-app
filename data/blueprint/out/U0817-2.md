### 4. GST – NINE YEARS, COLLECTIONS AND COUNCIL
#### 4.1 Nine Years of GST (1 July 2026)

- Gross collections: ₹7.4 lakh crore (2017–18) → **₹22.27 lakh crore (2025–26)**.
| Year | Step |

#### 4.2 Constitutional Framework
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **246A** | Concurrent GST law-making power to Parliament and States; Parliament exclusive for inter-State |
| **269A** | GST on inter-State supplies – levied by Union, apportioned |
| **279A** | **GST Council**, constituted by the President; chaired by the **Union Finance Minister** (Nirmala Sitharaman) |
- GST is a **destination-based consumption tax** (not origin-based); implemented by **CBIC** (HQ New Delhi, Ministry of Finance).
- **Outside GST**: alcoholic liquor for human consumption; petroleum crude, petrol, HSD, **natural gas**, ATF (until the Council recommends); Basic Customs Duty, stamp duty, electricity duty, motor vehicle tax.
#### 4.3 Next-Generation GST Reforms
- Approved at the **56th GST Council meeting**; effective **September 2025**.
- Slabs mainly **5% and 18%**; **40%** for luxury and sin goods.
- Registration threshold (goods) ₹20 lakh → **₹40 lakh**; Composition limit **₹1.5 crore**; QRMP – turnover up to ₹5 crore (quarterly returns, monthly payment).
- GST on toys: 12% → **5%**. Hotel rooms above ₹7,500/night attract 18% GST.
