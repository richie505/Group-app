## U0796 | ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS AND DISASTER MANAGEMENT – DM ACT 2005, NDMA AND NDRF, SENDAI FRAMEWORK, DISASTER TYPES AND MITIGATION
### 1. DISASTER TERMS AND THE DM CYCLE
#### 1.1 Key Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Hazard** | Potentially damaging event |
| **Disaster** | When a hazard meets vulnerability and causes serious harm |
| **Vulnerability** | How easily harmed – **physical, social, economic, environmental** |
| **Risk** | Probability of harm; **Risk = Hazard × Vulnerability / Capacity** |
| **Resilience** | Ability to **resist, absorb and recover quickly** |
| **Capacity** | Strengths and resources that reduce risk |
- Risk assessment dimensions: **hazard, vulnerability, exposure, capacity**.
- **Disaster Crunch Model**: vulnerability comes from **socio-economic processes**.
- **DRR works best when it addresses the underlying causes of vulnerability** (APPSC 2024).
#### 1.2 Crisis / DM Cycle – Three Phases
| Phase | Focus | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Pre-crisis** | **Prevention, mitigation, preparedness** | Embankments, mangroves, land-use zoning |
| **During crisis** | **Response** – rescue, relief | Search and rescue |
| **Post-crisis** | **Recovery → rehabilitation → reconstruction** | Rebuilding |
!KEY Embankments, mangroves and zoning = mitigation (pre-disaster), not response.
#### 1.3 Vulnerable Groups
| Basis | Groups |
|---|---|
| Age | Children (esp. unaccompanied), **elderly** |
| Gender | Women – pregnant, lactating, single, widows; transgender persons |
| Disability | Persons with Disabilities (barriers in warning, evacuation, relief) |
| Socio-economic | Poor, SC/ST, informal workers, homeless |
| Location | Coastal areas, **Seismic Zone V**, flood plains, hills |
### 2. CLASSIFICATION OF DISASTERS
#### 2.1 Broad Classes
| Class | Hazards |
|---|---|
| **Geological / geophysical** | Earthquake, tsunami, landslide, **volcanic eruption** |
| Hydrological | Flood, flash flood, avalanche |
| **Meteorological** | Cyclone, tornado, **thunderstorm**, hailstorm |
| **Climatological** | **Drought, heat wave, cold wave, forest fire** (NOT biological) |
| **Biological** | **Epidemics, pandemics (COVID-19), pest attacks** |
| **Man-made** | Industrial, chemical, **nuclear**, fire – human activity, negligence or technological failure (APPSC 2025) |
- **Fire from careless gas-cylinder handling = man-made** (APPSC 2025). A **nuclear explosion is not natural** (CBRN category).
- Man-made subtypes: **technological** (industrial accidents, chemical leaks, dam failure), **transport**, **social or complex** (terrorism, riots, war).
- **Emerging hazards**: **cyber attacks, space weather, AI failures**.
- **Socio-natural hazards**: natural + human factors – floods worsened by deforestation; landslides from unscientific construction.
- **Flooding causes**: melting snow/ice, lack of vegetation (more runoff), dam failures – a **biological disaster does not cause flooding**.
- Nuclear disasters: reactor accidents; radiological: orphan sources, transport accidents, dirty bombs.
#### 2.2 Notified Disasters
- Eligible for **SDRF/NDRF** aid: **14 since August 2026** (earlier 12) – **heatwave and lightning** added on **16th Finance Commission** advice.
- **No man-made disaster** is notified.
- **Ministry of Agriculture** handles **drought, hailstorm, frost, pest attack**.
!TRAP Drought, heat wave, cold wave are climatological, not biological.
!TRAP **Biological disasters** (epidemics, pest attacks) are a **natural hazard class** – an APPSC answer key gave them as man-made.
### 3. INDUSTRIAL AND CHEMICAL DISASTERS
| Fact | Bhopal Gas Tragedy |
|---|---|
| Date | **Night of 2–3 December 1984** |
| Gas | **Methyl isocyanate (MIC)** – not chlorine, CO or ammonia |
| Company | **Union Carbide India Limited** pesticide plant, Bhopal (MP) |
| Type | Man-made chemical hazard; **world's worst industrial disaster** |
- Chemical accident rules: **MSIHC Rules 1989** + **CAEPPR Rules 1996** – Crisis Groups at **Central, State, District, Local** levels.
- LG Polymers, Visakhapatnam (May 2020): styrene vapour.
### 4. DM ACT 2005, POLICY AND PLANS
- **DM Act 2005**: enacted **23 December 2005**; **11 chapters, 79 sections**. Chapter II (Sec 3) = **NDMA**; Sec 42 = **NIDM**; Sec 44 = **NDRF**.
- **National Policy on DM (NPDM)**: approved by Union Cabinet on **22 October 2009**.
- DM Act: shift from **relief-oriented** action to **risk reduction**; gives **PRIs and ULBs** a legal role in local plans, relief and capacity building.
- Act sets up **NDMA**, **NEC**, **SDMA**, **DDMA**, **NIDM**, **NDRF** (force) and funds; administered by **MHA** (**nodal ministry**).
- **NDMA** notified **2006**; **NDRF** raised **2006** (Raising Day **19 January**) with **8 battalions**, now **16 battalions** (drawn from CAPFs).
- **First National Disaster Management Plan: 2016**; revised **2019**. "Resilient India 2047": NDMA push for Ministry DM plans (Jun 2026).
#### 4.1 Funds
| Fund | Use |
|---|---|
| **SDRF** | Primary State fund for immediate relief |
| **NDRF (fund)** | Severe calamities – response, relief, rehabilitation |
| NDMF / SDMF | Mitigation funds |
- **High Level Committee** (Union Home Minister): financial approvals from national funds.
- AP: SDRF/NDRF released Rs 479.01 crore in 2026-27 (Rs 277.63 crore in 2025-26).

### 6. GLOBAL FRAMEWORKS
| Framework | Year / period | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **IDNDR** (International Decade for Natural Disaster Reduction) | **1990–1999** | Proclaimed by UNGA Res. 44/236 (22 Dec 1989), began 1 Jan 1990; first concerted UN effort; prompted India's DM Cell |
| **Yokohama Strategy** | **1994** | 1st WCDRR; first global guidelines; blueprint from IDNDR |
| UNISDR | 1999 | Secretariat for ISDR |
| **Hyogo Framework** | **2005–2015** | 2nd WCDRR (Kobe); **five priorities** |
| **Sendai Framework** | **2015–2030** | Adopted **18 March 2015**, 3rd WCDRR, Sendai |
| PM's 10-Point Agenda | 2016 | India's action plan aligned to Sendai (not a global blueprint) |
| **CDRI** | **2019** | India-led infrastructure-resilience coalition; secretariat **New Delhi** |
- **All three WCDRRs hosted by Japan**; UNISDR coordinated the 2nd and 3rd.
- At Yokohama, UN member states and other states met at the **World Conference on Natural Disaster Reduction**.
- **UNISDR → UNDRR: 1 May 2019**, **Geneva**; led by the SRSG for DRR; **custodian of Sendai** (implementation, monitoring, review); convenes the **Global Platform for DRR**.
- **Post-2015 frameworks** shaping India's DRR: **Sendai, SDGs (2015–2030), Paris Agreement** – NOT Hyogo.
- World Bank: India's disaster loss 1996–2000 ≈ **2.25% of GDP** a year.
#### 6.1 Sendai – Four Priorities
1. **Understanding disaster risk**
2. **Strengthening disaster risk governance** (laws, institutions, policies, coordination)
3. **Investing in DRR for resilience**
4. **Enhancing preparedness** for effective response and **"Build Back Better"** (recovery used to reduce future risk)
- Voluntary, **non-binding**; successor to Hyogo; States have the primary role; all-of-society, multi-hazard, people-centred.
#### 6.2 Sendai – Seven Global Targets (by 2030)
| Target | Aim |
|---|---|
| A | Reduce disaster **mortality** |
| B | Reduce number of **affected people** |
| C | Reduce direct **economic loss** (vs GDP) |
| D | Reduce damage to **critical infrastructure** and **disruption of basic services** |
| E | Increase national and local **DRR strategies – by 2020** |
| F | Enhance **international cooperation** to developing countries |
| G | Access to **multi-hazard early warning** and risk information |
!NUM Sendai = 4 priorities, 7 targets; Hyogo = 5 priorities; Target E deadline = 2020.
### 7. DISASTER TYPES – CRITERIA AND MITIGATION
#### 7.1 Structural vs Non-structural
| Structural (engineering) | Non-structural (policy, awareness) |
|---|---|
| Embankments, dams, check dams | **Building codes** – **IS 1893** (earthquake design), National Building Code |
| Cyclone shelters, **sea walls** | **Flood Plain Zoning**, land-use planning |
| Earthquake-resistant buildings, **retrofitting** | Insurance, awareness, early warning, training |
- Earthquake-resistant design: **door and window openings small and centrally located**.
#### 7.2 Hazard → Mitigation
| Hazard | Criteria / mitigation |
|---|---|
| Cyclone, storm surge | IMD warning, shelters, evacuation, shelter belts, **mangroves** (Coringa, Krishna) |
| Earthquake | **Seismic zoning** (not for cyclones), safe construction |
| Tsunami | **INCOI** warnings; **Tsunami Ready** villages – **24 Odisha villages** (UNESCO-IOC; target 100); especially **hard to mitigate** |
| Landslide | Hazard zonation mapping, early warning |
| Drought | Ministry of Agriculture |
- **UN-HABITAT** mitigation: planting **mangroves** (roots reduce tsunami impact and erosion); India's share of world mangrove cover ≈ **0.03**.
- **ISRO**: **DMS programme** and **NDEM** (GIS database) support all hazards.
- **Volcanoes**: fewest annual fatalities among natural hazards in the USA.
#### 7.3 Heat Waves
- Abnormally high temperatures above normal maximum; season **March–June** (sometimes July).
- **IMD** forecasts (colour-coded alerts); **NDMA** guidelines – "Guidelines for Preparation of Action Plan – Prevention and Management of Heat Wave" (updated **2019**); NDRF responds.
- **Heat Action Plans**: by States and cities with NDMA and IMD support. **Cool roofs**: reflective coatings or white paint.
- Measures: **adjusted working hours = administrative**; **shade structures = structural**; **ORS = public-health/awareness**; cooling centres.
#### 7.4 Cold Waves
- Sharp fall in **minimum** temperature below normal in winter; season **November–March**, peak **December–February**.
- **NDMA Guidelines on Cold Wave and Frost: 2021** (IMD forecasts; yellow, orange, red alerts) – preparedness, vulnerability mapping, awareness.
- **Cold day**: maximum temperature departure **–4.5°C to –6.4°C**; **severe cold day**: below –6.4°C.
- Impacts: hypothermia, frostbite; most vulnerable: homeless, elderly, children, outdoor workers. Measures: warm clothing, night shelters, community kitchens.
### 8. COMMUNITY-BASED DRR AND EARLY WARNING
- **Aapda Mitra** (NDMA): trains **community volunteers as first responders** – search and rescue, first aid (a scheme, not a fund).
- NDMA focus: **Disability-inclusive DRR guidelines (2019)**; **School Safety** (children); **gender mainstreaming** (women's leadership – part of PM's 10-Point Agenda); minimum relief standards.
- **PM's 10-Point Agenda does NOT list** "greater involvement and leadership of youth".
- National Project for Strengthening Community-Based DRR in PRIs: **20 Model GPs on 6 hazard themes**.
- **Social media**: complementary tool, **not a replacement** for official systems.
- **Gender and age share of deaths**: in the **2004 tsunami**, up to **four times as many women as men** died in some areas of Indonesia, Sri Lanka and India; **Cuddalore** (TN): **391 women vs 146 men** – women could often not swim or climb trees, and were at home with children.
- **Mock exercises** by NDMA, NIDM, NDRF and States throughout the year.
- **SACHET**: NDMA alert system on **Common Alerting Protocol**, developed by **C-DOT**.
- **7th AMCDRR**: hosted by India, **New Delhi, 3–5 November 2016** (PM's 10-Point Agenda announced).
- **First Indian Disaster Management Congress**: inaugurated **29 November 2006**.
- **NPDRR** (National Platform for DRR): multi-stakeholder body chaired by the **Union Home Minister**.
- **First National Conference on Coastal DRR and Resilience**: hosted by **NIDM**, **New Delhi, February 2020**.
