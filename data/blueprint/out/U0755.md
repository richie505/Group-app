## U0755 | SOLAR ENERGY – PV VS THERMAL, NATIONAL SOLAR MISSION, SOLAR PARKS, ROOFTOP SOLAR, ISA
### 1. SOLAR TECHNOLOGIES – PV VS THERMAL
#### 1.1 Photovoltaic vs Solar Thermal
| Point | Photovoltaic (PV) | Solar thermal / CSP |
|---|---|---|
| Principle | **Photovoltaic effect**: light → electricity **directly**, using semiconductors (**silicon**) | Mirrors/collectors **concentrate sunlight** to heat a fluid → steam turbine (works on heat) |
| Output | **Direct current (DC)**; **inverter** changes DC to AC | Heat for water, steam, power |
| Forms | Mono/polycrystalline silicon, thin-film | Flat-plate collectors, concentrators, power towers |
| Heat storage | No | **Possible** |
- **Photovoltaic cells = solar cells**; silicon is the most widely used material.
- A **transformer** only changes voltage; it does not convert DC to AC.
- **Artificial satellites** get electricity from **solar cells** (photovoltaic effect).
- **Solar constant** ≈ **1.4 kW/m²** – solar energy per unit surface at one astronomical unit from the Sun.
!TRAP PV = light to electricity directly (DC); CSP = heat to steam turbine. Only CSP can store heat.
#### 1.2 PV Cell Materials
| Type | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Crystalline silicon** (mono, poly) | **Dominant** technology |
| **Thin-film** (CdTe, CIGS, a-Si) | **Cheaper, bendable, less efficient** |
| **Perovskite** | IIT Bombay (MNRE project): 26% cell (world's 2nd highest); 30.2% perovskite-silicon tandem |
#### 1.3 Applications and Efficiency
- **Solar pumps** (PM-KUSUM B and C), solar water heating, floating solar, rooftop systems.
| Source | Capacity utilisation factor (CUF) |
|---|---|
| Solar | about **21%** |
| Wind | **36–40%** |
| Coal | about **85%** |
### 2. SOLAR POTENTIAL OF INDIA
- **Irradiation**: **4–7 kWh/m²/day** over most of India.
- **250–300 clear sunny days** a year → tremendous potential.
!KEY APPSC G1 2024: "India gets 250–300 sunny days" and "India has huge solar potential" – both true, II explains I.
- **NISE estimate**: **748 GW** ground-based potential (assuming **3% of wasteland**); only **~4.5%** used.
- RE-potential share: **Rajasthan 23.70%**, Maharashtra 14.26%, Gujarat 9.10%, **AP 9.10%**, Karnataka 8.59%, MP 8.09%.
- **NISE (National Institute of Solar Energy), Gurugram**: **autonomous body of MNRE**; centre for solar R&D.

#### 5.2 Rooftop Solar
- **PM Surya Ghar**: Central Sector Scheme to FY 2026-27; 30 GW residential rooftop; **Model Solar Village** – ₹1 crore to one village per district; >39.7 lakh systems (22 Jul 2026).
- **AP**: 4,11,915 rooftop systems (4 Aug 2026), among the top four states.
- **SRISTI**: rooftop incentives, **ARUN app**, **Suryamitra** skilling.
- Grid-connected rooftop ~27.88 GW (2026); cost ~₹30,000/kW after subsidy.
#### 5.3 PM Surya Sarovar Yojana (Floating Solar)
- Cabinet approval **31 Jul 2026**; outlay **₹5,070 crore**; **5,000 MW** floating solar by **2030-31**; CFA up to **₹1 crore/MW**; via **SECI**; mandatory 2-hour storage.
#### 5.4 Market Rules
- **Green Energy Open Access Rules, 2022** (Ministry of Power): open-access limit cut from **1 MW to 100 kW**.
- **GTAM** (Green Term Ahead Market): RE-only segment on **IEX**, launched **2020**.
- **IEX** (est. **2008**), regulated by **CERC**; trades DAM, TAM, RE and ESCerts.

### 7. FLOATING SOLAR
- Potential **102.18 GW** on reservoirs; **Maharashtra** leads (~16 GW), **MP** next (~14 GW).
- First national assessment, **"Solar PV Potential of India (Floating Solar)"** – by **NISE** (Jun 2026).
- **Omkareshwar** (on the **Narmada**, **Khandwa, MP**): **278 MW**, **India's largest floating solar**; to reach 600 MW.
- **AP's floating plant**: **NTPC Simhadri** (Visakhapatnam).
- Pluses: saves land, cuts evaporation, water cooling raises efficiency. Minus: **~25% higher** starting cost.
- World: ~9.6 GW floating (2024); 90% in Asia.
### 8. SOLAR MANUFACTURING – ALMM, DCR, PLI, DUTIES
#### 8.1 ALMM
- **Approved List of Models and Manufacturers**: compulsory since **2019**; only listed modules in **government-funded, open-access and net-metering** projects – incl. PM Surya Ghar, PM-KUSUM, SECI tenders (not only private developers).
| ALMM list | Covers | From |
|---|---|---|
| List-I | **Modules** | 2019 |
| List-II (ALCM) | **Cells** | **1 June 2026** |
| List-III | **Ingots and wafers** | **June 2028** |
- Capacity gap: modules ~**190 GW** vs cells only ~**25 GW**.
#### 8.2 DCR, PLI and Duties
- **Domestic Content Requirement (DCR)**: for developing solar production in India; domestic cells/modules compulsory in CPSU, PM-KUSUM and rooftop-subsidy projects.
- **PLI for High-Efficiency Solar PV Modules (2021)**: Tranche-II 39,600 MW integrated capacity.
- Module capacity: 2.3 GW (2014) → 172 GW (2026); module imports fell 65% in FY25.
- **Basic Customs Duty** (from **April 2022**): **modules 40%, cells 25%**.
- **GST** on solar devices cut **12% → 5%** (22 Sep 2025). REEIMS import-monitoring portal from 1 Nov 2025.
- Concerns: **no solar-panel recycling norms**; cell capacity lag; grid **curtailment**.
### 9. INTERNATIONAL SOLAR ALLIANCE (ISA)
#### 9.1 Founding
| Event | Fact |
|---|---|
| Launch | **India and France**, **30 November 2015**, **COP21, Paris** |
| HQ foundation stone | **25 January 2016**, NISE campus, **Gwal Pahari, Gurugram** – PM Modi and President **Hollande** |
| Framework Agreement in force | **6 December 2017** – treaty-based body |
| First summit / founding conference | **11 March 2018, New Delhi** |
| 8th Assembly | New Delhi, **October 2025** (137 nations) |
- **First treaty-based intergovernmental organisation headquartered in India**; HQ **Gurugram (Haryana)**.
- Members: **112** member countries (**not 38**); 116+ signatories. Newest: **Romania** ratified (March 2026).
- Goal: mobilise **$1 trillion** solar investment by **2030**.
#### 9.2 Initiatives
| Initiative | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **OSOWOG** (One Sun One World One Grid) | **Transnational grid** sharing solar power across time zones |
| **STAR-C** | Knowledge, **training** and best-practice centres |
| Common procurement platform | Pools demand to cut costs through scale |
| **Global Solar Facility** | Fund to **de-risk** solar investment in underserved regions (payment guarantees, insurance) |
| 8th Assembly launches | **Global SIDS Initiative**, **ISA Academy**, Global Capability Centre, **SUNRISE** |
#### 9.3 Partner Pacts
- **Asian Development Bank** (March 2018): solar in Asia and the Pacific.
- **MIGA** (World Bank Group) – ISA Solar Facility (Sept 2024): first guarantee programme of the Global Solar Facility, starting in Sub-Saharan Africa.
