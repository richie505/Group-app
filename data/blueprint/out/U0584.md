## U0584 | VIZAG RAILWAY ZONE - PROMISE, STATUS AND ISSUES
### 1. THE ACT COMMITMENT
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Where | **Thirteenth Schedule** (Infrastructure), AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 |
| Wording | **Indian Railways** shall, **within six months** from the appointed day, **examine** establishing a new railway zone in AP and take an expeditious decision |
| Nature | Only a feasibility promise - the Act did **not** itself create the zone |
| Telangana item | Railways to examine a **Rail Coach Factory** |
| Constitutional basis | Railways = Union subject, **Entry 22, List I** |
| How a zone is made | **Executive decision + gazette notification** of the Ministry of Railways |
!TRAP AP = railway zone; Telangana = Rail Coach Factory. "Examine", not "establish"; period = six months.
### 2. SOUTH COAST RAILWAY (SCoR) - TIMELINE
| When | Step |

- Demand pressed for **over four decades**; the 2014 promise was kept only after **about a decade**.
!TRAP 2019 = announced; 4 May 2026 = gazette; 1 June 2026 = operational.
### 3. DIVISIONS, HQ AND STAFFING
- SCoR has **4 divisions**: **Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Guntur, Guntakal**.
- HQ: **Rs 184 crore** zonal headquarters proposed at **Mudasarlova**, Visakhapatnam (May 2026); interim office in a **VMRDA** building.
- Early results: Vijayawada division revenue about **Rs 4,894 crore** in the first four months; **Rs 300 crore** Visakhapatnam station yard remodelling (6 new platforms, total 14).
- South Central Railway shrinks to **three divisions**.
### 4. WALTAIR DIVISION SPLIT
| Part | Goes to |
|---|---|
| Andhra part of Waltair | Truncated **Visakhapatnam division** of SCoR |
| Rest of Waltair | New **Rayagada division** (Odisha) of **East Coast Railway**, beside Khurda Road and Sambalpur |
| ECoR (Bhubaneswar) | Loses Andhra part; keeps K-K line revenue via Rayagada |
!PAIR Waltair - Andhra part | Visakhapatnam division | SCoR || Waltair - rest | Rayagada division | ECoR
### 5. ISSUES - K-K LINE AND ODISHA
- **Kottavalasa-Kirandul (K-K) line** = "**revenue lifeline**" of Waltair; the sore point of the reorganisation.
- **Odisha** claimed the entire line; the Centre favoured moving the whole K-K line to Rayagada.
- AP's case: Waltair earned **more than Vijayawada, Guntur and Guntakal combined**.
- AP offered only Odisha stretches: **Koraput-Kirandul, Kuneru-Theruvali, Koraput-Rayagada**.
- AP benefit: lower freight costs for **VCIC**, better port connectivity, "multimodal logistics hub".
### 6. RAIL PROJECTS IN AP
| Line | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Errupalem-Namburu** (~57 km, broad gauge) | **Amaravati rail link**; ~3 km Krishna bridge; 100 m buffer in Inner Ring Road land pooling |
| **Kotipalli-Narsapur** | New line |
| **Ramayapatnam-Kadapa** | Dedicated **steel plant** line |
| Kottavalasa-Kirandul | Waltair revenue line |
- AP outlay **Rs 10,134 crore (2026-27)** vs average Rs 886 crore in 2009-14.
- As of 1 Apr 2026: **42 projects, 4,768 km, Rs 77,855 crore**; 1,465 km commissioned; network **100% electrified**.
- **74** Amrit Bharat stations; Sullurupeta (near Sriharikota) opened 22 May 2025.
- July 2021: Railways announced **33 projects** (new lines, doubling) in AP.
- Budget 2026-27: **7 high-speed rail "growth connectors"** incl. Hyderabad-Bengaluru, Hyderabad-Chennai, Chennai-Bengaluru.
