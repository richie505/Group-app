#### 1.2 Gandhi's Associates
- **C.F. Andrews** ("Deenbandhu"): Gandhi's close English friend; professor at **St. Stephen's College, Delhi**; lived with Gandhi at **Phoenix Farm**; went with him to the **Round Table Conference**; close to Tagore and Gokhale.
- **1920 Chhattisgarh tour:** invited by **Pandit Sundarlal Sharma**; **Shaukat Ali** accompanied Gandhi.
- **Katherine Mayo** ("Mother India", 1927), **Aldous Huxley, Charles Andrews, William Digby** - all wrote commentaries on the condition of India.

#### 6.3 Central Legislative Assembly
- **Vithalbhai Patel** - **first Indian President (Speaker)** of the Central Legislative Assembly, elected **24 August 1925** after Sir Frederick Whyte; re-elected **1927**.
- Set the convention that the President stays **neutral** in debates; in **1928** secured a **separate office** for the Assembly, independent of the government.
- **August 2025:** All India Speakers' Conference in Delhi marked the **centenary** of his election.
