## U0451 | ECONOMIC PLANNING IN INDIA – ORIGINS, PLANNING COMMISSION, NATIONAL DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL
### 1. PRE-INDEPENDENCE PLANNING EFFORTS
#### 1.1 Year → Effort → Person
| Year | Effort | Key person / idea |
|---|---|---|
| 1931 | **Karachi Resolution** (INC) | Economic freedom; state ownership of key industries |
| 1934 | **Planned Economy for India** | **M. Visvesvaraya** – ten-year plan to **double national income**; shift labour to industry |
| 1938 | **National Planning Committee** (Congress) | Initiated by **Subhas Chandra Bose** (Haripura); chaired by **Jawaharlal Nehru**; final report **1949** |
| 1944 | **Bombay Plan** | Eight industrialists; state-led industrialisation with protection |
| 1944 | **Gandhian Plan** | **Shriman Narayan Agarwal** – decentralised, self-contained villages |
| 1944 | Planning and Development Department | Under **Ardeshir Dalal** (abolished 1946) |
| 1947 | Economic Programme Committee | Under Nehru |
| — | **People's Plan** | **M.N. Roy** (not a Bombay Plan signatory) |
- **Visvesvaraya** was the first to support planning – "**Father of Indian Planning**".
- **Bombay Plan signatories:** J.R.D. Tata, G.D. Birla, Kasturbhai Lalbhai, Purshottamdas Thakurdas, Ardeshir Dalal, John Mathai.
- Bombay Plan demanded protection and state intervention → the licence raj was partly a capitalist demand.
- **Supporters of planning:** industrialists (Bombay Plan) and socialists. Gandhian and Sarvodaya plans offered a **decentralised alternative**.
!TRAP NPC 1938: **Bose initiated, Nehru chaired** – not the other way round.
### 2. PLANNING COMMISSION
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Set up | **15 March 1950**, by a **Cabinet (executive) resolution**; began work 28 March 1950 |
| Status | **Non-constitutional, non-statutory, advisory** |
| Basis | In line with **Article 39** (DPSP) |
| Chairman | **Prime Minister** (ex-officio); first – Jawaharlal Nehru |
| First Deputy Chairman | **Gulzarilal Nanda** |
| Last Deputy Chairman | **M.S. Ahluwalia** |
| Last Plan | **Twelfth (2012-17)** |
| Replaced by | **NITI Aayog (2015)**, also by Cabinet resolution |
- Planning Commission was the **nodal agency for poverty estimation** (national and state).
- M.S. Ahluwalia also headed the **Sugar De-control Committee**.
- **National Planning Council (1965):** expert advisory body attached to the Commission.
### 3. NATIONAL DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL (NDC)
#### 3.1 Set-up and Status
- Set up **August 1952** by an **executive resolution**, on the recommendation of the First Five-Year Plan (draft outline).
- **Neither constitutional nor statutory**; advisory; advice **not binding**; to meet **at least twice a year**.
- **Sarkaria Commission:** give NDC constitutional status under **Article 263** and rename it "**National Economic and Development Council**".
- Now inactive; role passed to **NITI Aayog's Governing Council**; formal abolition pending.
!KEY Chronology: first post-Independence devaluation → First Five Year Plan submitted to Parliament → NDC set up.
#### 3.2 Composition – Who Is and Is NOT a Member
| Members | NOT members |
|---|---|
| **Prime Minister** (Chairman) | **President** |
| Union Cabinet Ministers | Vice-President |
| **Chief Ministers of all states** | Governors |
| UT administrators | **Chairman of the Finance Commission** |
| Members of the Planning Commission | Secretary, Ministry of Planning and Programme Implementation |
- **Secretary:** Secretary of the Planning Commission.
!TRAP The Deputy Chairman of the Planning Commission was **not** the NDC chairman; the PM chaired both.
#### 3.3 Functions
- Secure states' cooperation; mobilise resources; promote common economic policies; balanced development of all regions.
- **Prescribe guidelines for the national plan, including assessment of resources**; **approve the draft Five-Year Plan** (final authority to approve; can modify).
### 4. WHICH BODY DID WHAT
| Task | Body |
|---|---|
| Assess resources; prepare the draft plan | **Planning Commission** |
| Guidelines incl. assessment of resources; approve the draft | **NDC** (apex approving body) |
| Operative document | Union Cabinet, then **Parliament** |
| Budgetary allocation for plan spending | **Finance Ministry** |
| Allocation of central funds to states now | **Union Finance Ministry** (on Finance Commission advice) |
- **Flow:** Commission drafts → NDC approves → Cabinet → Parliament.
#### 4.1 Planning Commission vs Finance Commission
| Basis | Planning Commission | Finance Commission |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Non-constitutional | Constitutional – **Article 280** |
| Dealt with | Planning, resource allocation | **Tax devolution, grants** |
| Old framework | Plan resources | Non-Plan resources |
- Finance Commission decides the **method and formula** for sharing tax proceeds between Centre and States.
### 5. PLANNING AND THE CONSTITUTION
- "**Economic and social planning**" = **Entry 20, List III (Concurrent List)**, Seventh Schedule.
- **Article 39** (DPSP) – source of plan objectives; **Article 40** – village panchayats, village as primary unit.
- **NDC, Planning Commission, Zonal Councils** – none mentioned in the Constitution; **Zonal Councils are statutory**, the other two are not; NITI Governing Council is not statutory.
- **Planning machinery:** Planning Commission (1950), NDC (1952), State Planning Boards, District and Metropolitan Planning Committees.
### 6. EPITHETS AND CRITICISM
- Epithets: "**super cabinet**", "**economic cabinet**", "**parallel cabinet**", "**fifth wheel of the coach**".
- **Nehru (1958)** admitted a feeling that the Commission had become a kind of "super-cabinet".
- Criticism: **centralised decisions**, weakened **state autonomy**; the "common plan" idea of the Advisory Planning Board bred lasting Centre-state tension.
- **NITI Aayog** = think tank; **bottom-up** planning and **cooperative federalism**; not mainly a fund allocator.
### 7. STATE AND AP PLANNING BODIES
- **State Planning Board:** **CM as Chairman** + Vice-Chairman + **6-9 members**; drafts the state plan; CM sends the state draft to the Planning Commission.
- **AP State Planning Commission:** set up during the **Fifth Plan (1974-79)**.
- **AP Planning Department:** Special Chief Secretary **Piyush Kumar** (15 Jul 2026).
- **AP FIRST:** district-level decentralised planning in the **Sixth Plan (1980-85)** – **Rs 1 crore each to 23 districts**; District Planning Councils set up.
### 8. DECENTRALISED PLANNING
| Body | Article | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **District Planning Committee** | **243ZD** | Mandatory; **four-fifths** elected by and from elected members of district panchayat and municipalities (rural-urban ratio) |
| **Metropolitan Planning Committee** | **243ZE** | Metro areas of **10 lakh+**; **two-thirds** elected |
- DPC chairperson sends the draft district plan (from panchayat and municipal plans) to the **State**.
- **74th Amendment, 1992** inserted **Part IXA** (municipalities), which holds DPC and MPC.
- **Balwantrai Mehta (1957):** entrust planning to PRIs; accepted January 1958. **Thungon sub-committee (1988):** **Zila Parishad** the pivot of planning (not Gram Sabha).
- Bottom-up planning is a goal not yet reached; the village is a suitable unit.
- 5 Jun 2026: Rural Development Ministry training **2,000 BDOs** in GIS/MIS for **Viksit Gram Panchayat Plans**.
!TRAP DPCs are in **Part IXA (74th Amendment)**, not "Article 243J".
