## U0078 | PALLAVA ART AND ARCHITECTURE – MANDAGAPATTU INSCRIPTION, MAHABALIPURAM RATHAS, SHORE TEMPLE, KAILASANATHA
### 1. FOUR PHASES OF PALLAVA ARCHITECTURE
| Phase | Ruler | Type | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mahendra** | Mahendravarman I (600–630) | **Rock-cut pillared mandapas** | **Mandagapattu**, Mahendravadi, Dalavanur, Mamandur |
| **Mamalla** | Narasimhavarman I (630–668) | **Monolithic rathas** and mandapas | **Pancha Rathas**, Mahabalipuram |
| **Rajasimha** | Narasimhavarman II | **First structural temples** | **Shore Temple**; **Kailasanatha**, Kanchi |
| **Nandivarman / Aparajita** | Nandivarman II and later | Smaller structural temples | **Vaikuntha Perumal**, Kanchi |
- Evolution: **rock-cut → monolithic rathas → structural** temples → Chola Brihadeshwara.
!TRAP Pancha Rathas = Narasimhavarman I; Shore Temple and Kailasanatha = Narasimhavarman II (Rajasimha).
### 2. MANDAGAPATTU INSCRIPTION
- Ruler: **Mahendravarman I** (NOT Narasimhavarman I).
- Claim: a temple for **Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva** made **without brick, timber, metal or mortar**.
- Marks the **beginning of rock-cut cave temples in South India**.
- First **dvarapalakas** at the entrance – later common in South Indian temples.
!KEY Materials named in the claim: brick, timber, metal, mortar (four).
### 3. MAHABALIPURAM MONUMENTS → PATRON
#### 3.1 Pancha (Pandava) Rathas – Narasimhavarman I (Mamalla)
- **Monolithic**, each carved from **one granite rock**, copying **wooden** and structural forms.
- Five: **Dharmaraja, Bhima, Arjuna, Nakula-Sahadeva, Draupadi**.
| Ratha | Special fact |
|---|---|
| **Dharmaraja** | **Largest**; forerunner of the **Dravida vimana** |
| **Draupadi** | **Smallest**; hut-shaped single cell; no decoration |
| **Nakula-Sahadeva** | **Apsidal** |
- "Seven Ratha" temple, Mahabalipuram (APPSC-GS 2025) = **Narasimhavarman I** (630–668), Mamalla style, title **Mahamalla**.
#### 3.2 Reliefs and Mandapas
| Monument | Patron | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| **Arjuna's Penance / Descent of the Ganga** | Narasimhavarman I | **Bhagiratha's** penance; **largest open-air rock relief in India** |
| **Varaha Mandapa** | Narasimhavarman I | Varaha rescuing **Bhudevi** |
| **Mahishasuramardini Cave** | Narasimhavarman I | **Durga** slaying Mahishasura |
| **Krishna Mandapa** | – | Krishna lifting **Govardhana** |
#### 3.3 Shore Temple
- Built under **Narasimhavarman II (Rajasimha)**; a **structural** temple.
- **Two Shiva shrines + one Vishnu shrine**.
- Survivor of the "**Seven Pagodas**" (six others believed lost to the sea).
### 4. KANCHI TEMPLES
| Temple | Builder | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Kailasanatha (Rajasimheshvara)** | **Narasimhavarman II (Rajasimha)**, 8th c.; finished by **Mahendravarman III** | **Sandstone**; **oldest surviving Pallava structural temple**; rich Shiva sculptures; model for **Chola** temples and for **Virupaksha, Pattadakal**; carries **Vikramaditya II's Kannada inscription** |
| **Vaikuntha Perumal** | **Nandivarman II** Pallavamalla | **Vishnu**; **storeyed sanctum**; panels on **Pallava history** |
### 5. UNESCO – MAHABALIPURAM VS AJANTA
| Point | Mahabalipuram | Ajanta |
|---|---|---|
| UNESCO | **1984** | **1983** (among India's first) |
| Faith / patron | Hindu; **Pallavas** | Buddhist; Satavahana and Vakataka phases |
| Form | Open-air reliefs, monolithic rathas, structural temple | Cliff complex: **25 viharas + 4 chaityas**; **Jataka** paintings |
| Other | 7th-century Pallava **port** city | Found 1819 by John Smith |
- Only Mahabalipuram is Pallava; Ajanta is **not** Pallava.
### 6. SCULPTURE AND PAINTING
- **Sittannavasal** (Pudukkottai district, TN): **Jain** cave with **lotus pond** murals. Once linked to Mahendravarman I; an inscription records a **9th-century Pandya** renovation.
- **Panamalai** (Talagirishvara temple): **Rajasimha**-period painting of **Parvati**.
### 7. PALLAVA-STYLE ROCK-CUT CAVES IN ANDHRA
| Cave | Location | Attribution | Key fact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Undavalli** | Guntur district, **Krishna** banks | **Vishnukundins** (APPSC G2 2025) | **Multi-storeyed**; monolithic reclining **Anantasayana Vishnu** (Anantapadmanabha) |
| **Mogalrajapuram** | **Vijayawada** | Vishnukundins | **Shiva and Vishnu** shrines; early ardhamandapa + garbhagriha |
| **Bhairavakona** | Prakasam district, on the Nellore border (APPSC keys: **Nellore**) | Vishnukundins; look like early Pallava caves | **8 caves**; centre of **Shaivism** (not Jain) |
- **Vishnukundins** started Hindu cave temples in Telugu lands, imitating the **Pallava** cave style.
- Undavalli: originally a Jain abode → Buddhist → Hindu; described as a **4th–5th century** monolithic complex on the Krishna.
!TRAP Undavalli "built by the Cholas" = WRONG.
- Pallava rathas gave the **vimana + ardhamandapa** to early Andhra temples.

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- First rock-cut temple in South India: Mandagapattu.
- First structural temples: Rajasimha phase.
- Oldest surviving Pallava structural temple: Kailasanatha.
- Largest ratha: Dharmaraja; smallest: Draupadi.
- Largest open-air rock relief: Arjuna's Penance.
- Builders: Brihadisvara = **Rajaraja Chola I**; Gommateshwara = **Chamundaraya**; Barabar caves = **Ashoka**.
