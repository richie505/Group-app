## U0797 | PREPAREDNESS AND RESPONSE – SURVEILLANCE, EPIDEMIC DISEASES ACT, NATIONAL HEALTH EMERGENCY FRAMEWORK
### 1. EPIDEMICS AS BIOLOGICAL DISASTERS – THE DM CYCLE
- **Epidemics count as biological disasters.**
| Measure | Phase |
|---|---|
| **Vaccine distribution before an epidemic** | **Mitigation** (PYQ) |
| Sanitation, vector control | Mitigation |
| Surveillance, stockpiles, hospital readiness, **mock drills** | **Preparedness** |
| **Isolation, quarantine, contact tracing**, treatment, lockdown | **Response** |
| Restoring services, **rehabilitation**, economic relief | **Recovery** |
- **National Disaster Management Plan (2016)**: India's first; covers prevention, mitigation, preparedness, response, recovery; matches **Sendai (2015-30), SDGs, Paris Agreement**.
!TRAP Vaccinating before an outbreak = mitigation, not response.

### 3. LEGAL BASIS OF EPIDEMIC POWERS
#### 3.1 Epidemic Diseases Act, 1897
- Legal framework for biological disasters; used in **COVID-19**. States notify diseases (e.g. **20 states** made human **rabies** notifiable, Jan 2026).
- Notifiable cases: **urgent within 24 hours**; routine within **3 days**.
- **2020 amendment** (ordinance **April**, Act **September 2020**): violence against health workers **cognizable and non-bailable**; **3 months to 5 years** jail + **Rs 50,000 to Rs 2 lakh** fine; grievous hurt **6 months to 7 years**.
- Criticism: **colonial**; **no definition of "dangerous epidemic disease"**; no rights safeguards.
#### 3.2 Other Laws
| Law / instrument | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **Disaster Management Act** | 2005 | **March 2020 national lockdown** ordered by **NDMA and MHA** under it; MHA Empowered Groups ran response |
| **Draft Public Health (Prevention, Control and Management of Epidemics, Bio-Terrorism and Disasters) Bill** | **2017** | Meant to replace 1897 Act; **never enacted** |
### 4. DISEASE SURVEILLANCE
| Programme | Launch | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **IDSP** | **2004** under NHM (MoHFW) | **Central Surveillance Unit (Delhi)** + State and District Surveillance Units |

### 8. INTERNATIONAL HEALTH REGULATIONS 2005
- **IHR (2005)**: **legally binding** WHO instrument; **in force since 2007**.
- India's obligations: build **core surveillance and response capacities**; **report a possible PHEIC to WHO within 24 hours**; name a **National IHR Focal Point**.
- **2024 amendments**: higher **"pandemic emergency"** tier above PHEIC. May 2026 Ebola outbreak = PHEIC only.
- **WHO Pandemic Agreement**: adopted **20 May 2025**.
- **BRICS New Delhi Declaration** (Sep 2026): sub-working group on an integrated early-warning system for infectious diseases.
