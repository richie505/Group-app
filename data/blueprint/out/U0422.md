## U0422 | PHASES OF INDIAN FOREIGN POLICY
### 1. SIX PHASES (JAISHANKAR)
| Phase | Label | Features |
|---|---|---|
| 1946–62 | **Optimistic non-alignment** | Idealism, Panchsheel, NAM, Commonwealth, anti-imperialism |
| 1962–71 | **Realism and recovery** | 1965 war, Tashkent (1966); no NPT (1968) |
| 1971–91 | **Regional assertion** | Leading power in South Asia |
| 1991–98 | **Safeguarding strategic autonomy** | USSR collapse, 1991 crisis |
| 1998–2013 | **Balancing power** | Pokhran-II, NFU, Indo-US deal (NSG waiver 2008) |
| 2014 onwards | **Energetic engagement** | Multi-alignment |
- Idealism → realism; non-alignment → multi-alignment; the constant is **strategic autonomy**.
### 2. NEHRUVIAN ERA
- NAM, Panchsheel, Commonwealth membership.
- **Korean War**: India headed the **Neutral Nations Repatriation Commission** for POWs — **Gen. K.S. Thimayya**.
- Kashmir taken to the UN (Jan 1948); the **1962 war** with China showed the **limits of idealism**.
### 3. 1971–1991: REGIONAL ASSERTION
| Event | Date | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Indo-Soviet Treaty** of Peace, Friendship and Cooperation | **9 Aug 1971** | 20 years; checked US-China-Pakistan axis |
| Bangladesh liberation | **16 Dec 1971** | Pakistan army surrendered in Dhaka (APPSC 2018) |
| **Simla Agreement** | **2 July 1972** | India–Pakistan; **bilateralism**, no third party |
| **Pokhran-I** | **18 May 1974** | "Peaceful nuclear explosion" |
| Katchatheevu ceded to Sri Lanka | 1974 | — |
| **Farakka Accord** | 1977 | Ganga water, India–Bangladesh |
| **IPKF** in Sri Lanka | 1987–90 | India-Sri Lanka Accord 1987 (Rajiv Gandhi–J.R. Jayewardene) |
!KEY Chronology: Tashkent (1966) → Indo-Soviet Treaty (1971) → Simla (1972) → Farakka (1977) → Lahore (1999) → 123 Agreement (2008).
### 4. POST-COLD WAR (1991–98)
- **Look East Policy** 1991–92, **P.V. Narasimha Rao**; closer to ASEAN; ties with **Israel**.
- **LPG reforms**: drawing FDI and markets became foreign-policy goals; strategic ties with the US in the "unipolar moment".
- **Gujral Doctrine (1996)**: goodwill to neighbours without asking anything in return.
### 5. POST-1998
- **Pokhran-II (Operation Shakti), 1998**: India called itself a nuclear-weapon state (not NPT-recognised, not NSG member).
- **No First Use** announced by the **Vajpayee** government (UKPSC 2021); formal doctrine 2003.
- Sanctions: US (**Glenn Amendment**), Japan; followed by **Jaswant Singh–Strobe Talbott** talks.
- **Lahore Declaration, Feb 1999** with Delhi–Lahore **bus diplomacy**.
- **Kargil 1999**: first conflict after both tested nuclear weapons; US pressure made Pakistan withdraw.
| Indo-US civil nuclear deal | Date |
|---|---|
| **Hyde Act** | 2006 |
| **NSG waiver** | **6 Sept 2008** |
| **123 Agreement** signed | Oct 2008 |
### 6. CONTEMPORARY MULTI-ALIGNMENT
- Since 2008 world moves to multipolarity → **issue-based alignment**: US (security, technology), Russia (defence, energy).
| Platform | Key fact |
|---|---|
| **Quad** | India, US, Japan, Australia; formed **2007**, revived **2017** |
| **SCO** | Full member **2017** |
| **BRICS** | Founding member; India chaired **2026**: **New Delhi Declaration** backed UN reform, opposed one-sided sanctions (Sep 2026) |
| **Act East** | Upgraded from Look East, **2014** |
| **Neighbourhood First** | 2014 |
| **G20 Presidency** | Dec 2022–Nov 2023; "One Earth, One Family, One Future"; **African Union** made permanent member |
- **India-UK CETA** signed July 2025 (PM's UK visit).
- First **Voice of Global South Summit**: **January 2023**.

### 8. TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Strategic autonomy** | Judge each issue on merits without military blocs; **≠ neutrality** |
| **Multi-alignment** | Working with rival powers at once (Quad + BRICS/SCO) |
| **Issue-based coalitions** | Partners by issue, not fixed alliances |
| **Global South voice** | Reformed multilateralism, climate finance, DPI |
