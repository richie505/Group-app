## U0492 | AGRICULTURAL INPUTS – SEEDS, FERTILISERS AND SUBSIDY REGIME, PESTICIDES, MECHANISATION, EXTENSION SERVICES
### 1. GREEN REVOLUTION – TECHNOLOGY AND TIMELINE
#### 1.1 Scientific Core and Origins
- Scientific core: **HYV seeds** (APPSC 2024); package also fertilisers, irrigation, pesticides, mechanisation = **seed-fertiliser-water technology** (late 1960s).
- **Wheat**: Borlaug's **Mexican dwarf wheats** → **Kalyan Sona, Sonalika**; 1966: **18,000 t** Mexican wheat imported and crossed with local varieties in Punjab; wheat output rose 5 → 17 MT.
- **Rice**: **IR-8** from **IRRI, Philippines**.
- **Punjab** = "Granary of India", pioneer State.
#### 1.2 Timeline
- **IADP 1960-61**.
- Droughts **1965-66 and 1966-67**: foodgrains fell to **72.3 and 74.2 MT**; dependence on **PL-480**.
- HYV programme full-fledged from **1966**, during Annual Plans (1966-69) → agri growth 4.16%.
- First KVK **1974**; **MSSRF 1988**.
- APPSC 2023 order: **IADP → end of first Green Revolution period → MSSRF** (II, I, III).
| Phase | Years |
|---|---|
| Pre-Green Revolution | 1951-68 |
| Early Green Revolution | 1968-81 |
| Later Green Revolution | 1987-92 |
#### 1.3 Second Green Revolution
- Focus: **pulses, oilseeds, millets (Shree Anna)**, precision farming (drones, AI), **micro-irrigation**, integrated nutrient and pest management, climate-resilient varieties; **not more chemical pesticides**.

### 3. FERTILISER SUBSIDY REGIME
#### 3.1 Urea and Subsidy Size
- **Urea** = most subsidised; **statutory price control**; kept cheap → nitrogen overuse; **46% nitrogen**, dissolves easily.
- Fertiliser subsidy = **second largest after food**.
- Budget 2026-27: **Rs 1.71 lakh crore** (RE 2025-26: Rs 1.86 lakh crore); may reach ~**Rs 2.41 lakh crore** as the **West Asia crisis** doubled urea prices (Dec 2025-Apr 2026).
- India: **2nd largest consumer, 3rd largest producer** of fertilisers.
- National Investment Policy for Urea-2026: adds **10 MT** urea capacity.
#### 3.2 Reforms
| Reform | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **NBS** | **2010** | Fixed per-kg subsidy on **N, P, K and S** announced yearly; **22 grades** of decontrolled P&K; **makers set MRPs** |
| **Neem-coated urea** | Notified **May 2015**; all domestic urea from **1 Sep 2015**, imported from **1 Dec 2015** | Slows N release; stops diversion of farm urea to industry |
| **DBT in fertilisers** | Pilot **October 2016** | Subsidy to **companies** after sale at **PoS** devices |
| **PM-PRANAM** | **28 June 2023** | States keep **50% of subsidy saved** by cutting chemical fertiliser use |
#### 3.3 Nano Fertilisers
- **IFFCO Nano Urea**: **500 ml bottle = one 45 kg bag**; foliar spray; ~**80%** absorption.
- **Nano DAP**: 8% N, 16% P; IFFCO; April 2023.
#### 3.4 Nutrient Balance
- **N:P:K = 10.9 : 4.1 : 1** (2023-24) vs ideal **4:2:1** – urea-centred subsidy distorts the ratio; ES suggests acre-based support.
!TRAP NBS covers P&K (and S) with fixed subsidy; urea stays under price control.
### 4. SOIL HEALTH CARD SCHEME
- Launched **19 February 2015** at **Suratgarh (Rajasthan)**; part of **NMSA** (Soil Health Management).
- Card issued **free every two years**; gives soil nutrient status and fertiliser advice.
- Impact: fertiliser use cut **8-10%**; yields up **5-6%**.
### 5. SEEDS, VARIETIES AND GM CROPS
#### 5.1 Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Seed Replacement Rate** | % of a crop's area sown with certified/quality seed |
| **Varietal Replacement Rate** | % of area under varieties released in last **3-5 years** |
| **Seed Village** | Farmers given foundation seed → produce certified seed locally |
#### 5.2 Variety Releases
- ICAR **2019-21: 562** new HYVs (PYQ key).
- **3,236 HYVs since 2014** vs 3,969 in 1969-2014; NARS: 2,996 climate-resilient varieties.
- Jan 2026: **184 varieties of 25 crops** (122 cereals; 24 cotton incl. 22 Bt cotton).
- **Genome-edited rice (May 2025)**: **DRR Dhan 100 Kamala** (from **Samba Mahsuri**) + **Pusa DST Rice 1** (from **MTU1010**); SDN-1 edits carry no foreign DNA → outside GMO rules.
#### 5.3 Acts and Regulators
| Act / body | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Seeds Act 1966** | Quality of notified seeds |
| **PPV&FR Act 2001** | Protects breeders and farmers; PPV&FR Authority; GURT (terminator) seeds banned |
| **GEAC** | Approves GM crops; Rules 1989 under **EPA 1986**; MoEFCC |
- PPV&FR: farmers may save, use, exchange and **sell farm-saved seed only unbranded**.
- **GM mustard DMH-11**: GEAC cleared 2022; under Supreme Court scrutiny. **Bt cotton** is GM, not a green manure.
### 6. PESTICIDES AND IPM
| Law / event | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Insecticides Act 1968 + Rules 1971** | **Central Insecticides Board** and **Registration Committee** |
| Draft **Pesticides Management Bill 2025** | Replaces them; higher penalties; promotes biological pesticides (comments by 4 Feb 2026) |
| **Endosulfan** | Banned by **Supreme Court, 2011** |
| **Aldrin** (banned) | Killed **mugger crocodiles in Kota** |
- India still uses at least **66** pesticides banned elsewhere.
- **Neem-based** products = **bio-pesticides**.
- **IPM**: biological, cultural and need-based chemical control.
### 7. ORGANIC AND NATURAL FARMING
| Scheme | Year | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **NPOP** | 2001 | Commerce Ministry / **APEDA** certification |
| **PGS-India** | – | Alternative certification route |
| **PKVY** | **2015** | Cluster of **50 farmers / 50 acres**; certified by **PGS-India or NPOP** (not only NPOP) |
| **National Mission on Natural Farming** | Nov 2024 | Stand-alone mission |
| **NMSA** | – | One of **8 NAPCC** missions; merged into **PM-RKVY from 2022-23** |
- NMSA components: Rainfed Area Development, Soil Health Management, Agroforestry ("Har Medh Par Ped").
- **Vermicompost**: earthworms turn organic waste into manure. **Glyricidia** = green leaf manure.
- Green manure crops: **sunn hemp, dhaincha, green gram**.
- Organic farming: compost, green manure, bone meal; crop rotation, companion planting.

### 9. MECHANISATION AND DRONES
- **SMAM** (2014-15 to 2025-26): subsidises machines, **custom hiring centres**, hi-tech hubs, farm machinery banks.
- Mechanisation level: India **40-45%**.
- **Namo Drone Didi**: drones to **women SHGs**; outlay **Rs 1,261 crore** (2023-24 to 2025-26); target **15,000** drones; **80%** central assistance up to Rs 8 lakh; ~500 distributed nationally.

### 12. FARM SUBSIDIES VS PUBLIC INVESTMENT
| Input subsidies (current transfers) | Public investment (capital formation) |
|---|---|
| Fertiliser, power, irrigation water, seed, credit (interest subvention) | Irrigation projects/canals, cold storage, rural roads, research, PACS computerisation, education and training |
- **MSP** = price support, **not** an input subsidy.
- **Land revenue** is not part of farm running-cost capital; seeds, fertilisers, water are.
- Debate: subsidies helped small farmers adopt HYVs but mostly benefit prosperous regions and the fertiliser industry.
