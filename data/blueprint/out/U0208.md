### 2. GHADAR PARTY
#### 2.1 Basic facts
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Founded | **21 April 1913**, **USA**, by Indians living in the **US and Canada**; first meeting in **Oregon** |
| Grew out of | **Pacific Coast Hindustan Association**, San Francisco |
| Headquarters | **Yugantar Ashram, San Francisco** |
| First president (chairman) | **Sohan Singh Bhakna** (called Sarabha his "General") |
| Moving spirit / secretary | **Lala Har Dayal** |
| Other founders | **Kartar Singh Sarabha, Bhagwan Singh, Baba Harnam Singh (Tundilat), Pt. Kanshi Ram** |
| Members | Mainly **Punjabi Sikh** immigrants (peasants, ex-soldiers) on the **West coast** of America |
| Meaning | "Ghadar" = **mutiny / rebellion** |
| Paper | **Ghadar** – a weekly from San Francisco; **Kartar Singh Sarabha** edited the **Punjabi** edition; **Ghadar di Goonj** = its poetry |
#### 2.2 Members vs non-members
- **Associated:** Har Dayal, Sohan Singh Bhakna, Kartar Singh Sarabha, **Rash Behari Bose** (closely linked with the Ghadar rising).
- **NOT associated:** **Khudiram Bose**, **Barindra Kumar Ghosh**, **Jogesh Chandra Chatterjee** (UPSC 2022 trap).
#### 2.3 The wartime uprising (1915)
- Cause of the Ghadar revolution: outbreak of the **First World War (1914)**.
- Pan-India revolt in the British Indian Army planned for **21 February 1915** in **Punjab**; **betrayed by informers** and crushed.
- **45 leaders hanged** under the **Defence of India Act**.
- **First Lahore Conspiracy Case:** **Kartar Singh Sarabha** executed **16 November 1915**, aged **19**, with **Vishnu Ganesh Pingle**; Sarabha was born **24 May 1896**, Sarabha (Ludhiana).
### 3. LALA HAR DAYAL – CAREER ABROAD
- **London's India House circle** → founded the **Ghadar Party (1913)** → **arrested in USA, 1914** → Switzerland / Germany, joined the **Berlin** effort.
- Standard pairing: **Ghadar** (paper) – **Har Dayal**; leader and organiser of the Ghadar Party.
### 4. KOMAGATA MARU (1914)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| What | A **Japanese ship** |
| Chartered by | **Baba Gurdit Singh**, Punjabi businessman |
| Passengers | **376** Indians (Sikh/Muslim) |
| Route | **Hong Kong** → Shanghai, Moji, Yokohama → **Vancouver, Canada** |
| Why refused | Canada's racist immigration laws – the "**continuous journey**" rule |
| Return | Forced back to **Calcutta** |
| **Budge Budge** (near Calcutta) | Police firing on **29 September 1914** killed **22** |
| Impact | Radicalised immigrants, sped up the Ghadar movement; fury in Punjab |
- **2016:** Canada's PM **Justin Trudeau** apologised in the **House of Commons** (recalled 2 May 2026).
!TRAP Organiser = **Gurdit Singh** (sometimes misprinted "Gurdip Singh").
### 5. MADAME BHIKAJI CAMA
- From a rich **Parsi** family; led revolutionary work from **Europe and America**.
- Founded the **Paris Indian Society (1905)**.
- **22 August 1907:** hoisted the **first Indian national flag** at the **International Socialist Congress, Stuttgart (Germany)** – **first woman** to unfurl the tricolour.
- Journal: **Bande Mataram** from **Paris** (Geneva).
!TRAP Two Bande Matarams: **Calcutta (1906)** = **Bipin Chandra Pal** (with Aurobindo); **abroad** = **Bhikaji Cama**.
### 6. BERLIN COMMITTEE AND KABUL GOVERNMENT
- **Berlin Committee (1915):** Virendranath Chattopadhyaya, Bhupendranath Dutta; sought help of **Germany and Turkey** (not France or Russia) to invade India.
- **Provisional (Republican) Government of India, Kabul (1915)**, during **WWI** (the **Home Rule Movement** period):
| Post | Person |
|---|---|
| **President** | **Raja Mahendra Pratap** |
| **Prime Minister** | **Maulana Barkatullah** |
| Member | **Obaidullah Sindhi** |
- Backers: **Germany and Turkey**, with help from the **Afghan Crown Prince**.
!TRAP Mahendra Pratap = **President**; Barkatullah = **Prime Minister**.
### 7. MADAN LAL DHINGRA
- India House member, with **Savarkar** and **Hardayal**.
- **1 July 1909, London:** shot **Sir William Curzon Wyllie** (political ADC at the India Office) and **Cowasji Lalkaka**.
- **Hanged at Pentonville Prison, London (1909)**.
- **Dhingra** and **Udham Singh** = both hanged for killing British officers in England.
