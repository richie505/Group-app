## U0577 | A.P. REORGANISATION ACT 2014 – STRUCTURE, SCHEDULES AND KEY PROVISIONS
### 1. THE ACT AT A GLANCE
- Full name: **Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014** (Act **No. 6 of 2014**).
- Divided the old State of Andhra Pradesh into **2 States**: **Telangana** and residual **Andhra Pradesh**.
- Structure: **12 Parts**, **108 sections** and **13 Schedules**.
- Telangana became the **29th State** of India.
!KEY Structure = 12 Parts + 108 sections + 13 Schedules.
### 2. TIMELINE OF ENACTMENT
| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Bill introduced in Lok Sabha | **13 February 2014** |
| Passed by Lok Sabha (voice vote) | **18 February 2014** |
| Passed by Rajya Sabha (voice vote, no amendment) | **20 February 2014** |
| President's assent; Gazette **No. 6**, **65th year of the Republic** | **1 March 2014** |
| Appointed day (territorial split) | **2 June 2014** |
- President at the time of assent: **Pranab Mukherjee**.
!TRAP "The Act came into effect on 2 March 2014" is WRONG. Enacted on **1 March 2014**; the split took effect on **2 June 2014**.
### 3. KEY SECTIONS
#### 3.1 Territory, Capital and Governor
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| 3 | Telangana formed from **10 districts**; the Khammam villages in the **Polavaram** submergence area stayed with AP (Polavaram Ordinance amended Sec 3) |
| 4 | Residual State of Andhra Pradesh |
| **5** | **Hyderabad** = common capital for **up to 10 years**; afterwards capital of Telangana, with a new capital for AP |
| 7 | **Common Governor** for both States |
| **8** | Governor's **special responsibility** for security of life, liberty and property in the common capital |
| 10 | Amends the **First Schedule** of the Constitution; Telangana = **29th State** |
- Under Section 8 the common Governor is helped by **two advisors appointed by the Centre**.
- **Greyhound Training Centre, Hyderabad**: common training centre for a **maximum of three years**.
#### 3.2 Finance and Development
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| **46** | **13th Finance Commission** award split by **population ratio** and other parameters; grants and a **special development package** for backward areas, especially **Rayalaseema and North Coastal AP** |
| 50 / 51 | Recovery of **tax arrears** and **loans/advances** by location (AP seeks amendment) |
| 59 | Apportionment of **pension liability** (Eighth Schedule) |
| **93** | Centre to take the **Thirteenth Schedule** measures within **10 years** |
| **94** | 94(1) fiscal measures incl. **tax incentives**; 94(2) **backward-area** infrastructure; 94(3) special support for the **new capital** (Raj Bhavan, High Court, Secretariat, Assembly, Council); 94(4) **denotify degraded forest land** for the capital |
| 95 | **Equal opportunities** for quality higher education |
#### 3.3 Water Resources (Sections 84-91)
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| **84** | **Apex Council**: Union **Water Resources Minister** (chair) + both Chief Ministers (3 members) |
| **85** | **GRMB** (HQ in **Telangana**) and **KRMB** (HQ in **Andhra Pradesh**) within **60 days**; security by **CISF** |
| **89** | **KWDT** (Krishna Water Disputes Tribunal) term extended for project-wise allocation |
| **90** | **Polavaram** declared a **national project**; Telangana's consent deemed given |
!PAIR Water sections: 84 | Apex Council | Minister chairs || 85 | GRMB and KRMB | CISF security
!TRAP 89 = KWDT; 90 = Polavaram. GRMB HQ = Telangana; KRMB HQ = AP (do not swap).
### 4. SEAT ALLOCATION
| House | Andhra Pradesh | Telangana | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rajya Sabha | **11** | **7** | 18 |
| Lok Sabha | **25** | **17** | 42 |
| Legislative Assembly | **175** | **119** | 294 |
| Legislative Council | not more than **50** | **40** | – |
- **Section 26**: delimitation can raise the AP Assembly from **175 to 225**, subject to **Article 170**.
- **Section 18**: each Governor could nominate **1 Anglo-Indian MLA** (**Article 333**); ended by the **104th Amendment**.
- **Section 13 / First Schedule of the Act**: allotment of **sitting Rajya Sabha members**.
!NUM 11 + 7 = 18 RS; 25 + 17 = 42 LS; 175 + 119 = 294 Assembly; 175 → 225 promised.
### 5. AMENDMENTS TO THE CONSTITUTION AND OTHER LAWS
| Law / Article amended | Change |
|---|---|
| **First Schedule** of Constitution | New State added (names and territories of States and UTs) – Sec 10 |
| **Fourth Schedule** of Constitution | Rajya Sabha seats: AP 18 → 11; new entry "Telangana – 7" |
| **Art 168(1)(a)** | Telangana added to States with a **Legislative Council** (Sec 96) |
| **Art 371D** | Now reads "**Andhra Pradesh or Telangana**" (Sec 97) |
| **RP Act, 1950** | **First Schedule** amended for **Lok Sabha seats** |
| **RP Act, 1951** | **Section 15A** amended for Telangana Council (Sec 98) |
- Other laws amended: **Delimitation Order 2008**, **SC and ST Orders 1950**, **States Reorganisation Act 1956** (Sec 15).
!KEY APPSC 2021: Constitution Schedules amended = **First and Fourth**.

#### 6.2 Schedule XI (Water)
- Priority order: **drinking water > irrigation > power**.
- No new project on Krishna or Godavari without **Apex Council** sanction.
#### 6.3 Schedule XII (Power)
- **APGENCO** units divided by location.
- **APERC** = joint regulator for up to **six months**; **SLDC** shared for up to **two years**.
### 7. SCHEDULE XIII – CENTRAL COMMITMENTS
#### 7.1 Education
- **IIT, NIT, IIM, IISER**, Central University, **Petroleum University**, Agricultural University, **IIIT**, **AIIMS-type** hospital, **Tribal University**, **NIDM**.
- NOT listed: **Indian Institute of Science** (IISc).
#### 7.2 Infrastructure
- **Duggirajupatnam** port.
- **6-month feasibility studies**: **SAIL steel plant** in **YSR district** (not Kurnool); **refinery**; **Vizag-Chennai Industrial Corridor** (modelled on **DMIC**); **new railway zone**.
!TRAP SAIL steel plant study = **YSR district (Kadapa)**, not Kurnool.
### 8. HIGH COURT BIFURCATION
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| **30** | **High Court at Hyderabad** common to both States until a separate AP High Court is set up under **Article 214**; judges' salaries shared by **population ratio** |
| 105 | Disputes over transfer of pending cases → decided by the **Hyderabad High Court**; its decision is **final** |
- The Act **named no place** for AP's High Court.
- **26 December 2018**: Presidential order under **Article 214** and the Act constituted a separate High Court for AP.
- **1 January 2019**: **High Court of Andhra Pradesh** began functioning with its principal seat at **Amaravati**; the Hyderabad court became the High Court for Telangana.
### 9. CAPITAL QUESTION – LATEST DEVELOPMENTS
- **3 March 2022**: AP High Court order on the capital (Amaravati).
- **28 March 2026**: AP Assembly resolution to insert "**at Amaravati**" in **Section 5(2)**.
- **April 2026**: Parliament passed the **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Act, 2026** (29 March and 1 April 2026).
!KEY 2026 amendment = **first time Parliament named a State's capital by law**.
