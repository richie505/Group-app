## U0534 | PRIMARY AND SECONDARY MARKETS – STOCK EXCHANGES, INDICES, IPO PROCESS, DERIVATIVES
### 1. PRIMARY VS SECONDARY MARKET
| Basis | Primary (new issue) market | Secondary market |
|---|---|---|
| Deals in | **New securities** | **Existing securities** |
| Transactions | Company → investors | **Between investors** |
| Examples | **IPOs, FPOs, rights issues**, private placement | **Stock-exchange trading**; gilt-edged trading of existing securities |
| Money goes to | The issuer | The selling investor |
- **Offer for sale (OFS)**: proceeds go to **selling shareholders**; only a **fresh issue** brings money to the firm.
- Promoters/PE/VC took 84% of OFS proceeds in CY26 (to early July); India ~30% of global IPO listings.
- A **stock exchange** is an organised market for securities, part of the capital market.
### 2. IPO PROCESS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Book building** | Bids within a **price band (floor to cap)**; final price set from bids |
| **Anchor investors** | **QIBs** bidding **before the issue opens**, with **lock-in** |
| **SME IPOs** | Separate platforms: **BSE SME, NSE Emerge** |
| **Listing** | Within **T+3 days** of issue closing |
| Large-company float | Very large firms may offer only **2.75%** (earlier 5%) (SEBI, Sep 2025) |
- **Stag**: buys IPO shares to sell soon after listing.
### 3. STOCK EXCHANGES
| Exchange | Facts |
|---|---|
| **BSE** | Brokers moved to **Dalal Street 1874**; set up **1875**; **Asia's oldest**; first recognised under **SCRA on 31 Aug 1957**; promoted by the Native Share and Stock Brokers' Association; promoted **CDSL** |
| **NSE** | Set up **November 1992**, Mumbai, on the **Manohar J. Pherwani Committee**; recognised **April 1993**; trading from **1994**; first segment **Wholesale Debt Market**, then Cash Market; **first fully automated electronic** exchange; promoters **IDBI, SBI, IFCI, LIC, GIC, IDFC** |
| **MSE** | Mumbai; began 2008 as MCX-SX, renamed 2015 |
| **India INX** (BSE) | **GIFT City IFSC, 30 Dec 2016** – **India's first international exchange** |
| **NSE IX** | GIFT City; NSEIX Global Access lets Indians buy US stocks |
- **NSE** had the **highest turnover in 2000-01** (₹8.39 lakh crore) and is still the largest by volume.
- **IFSC exchanges** (India INX, NSE IX) are regulated by **IFSCA, not SEBI**.
!TRAP BSE = Asia's oldest (1875); NSE = first fully automated (1992); India INX = first international exchange (2016).
### 4. STOCK INDICES
| Index | Exchange | Stocks | Method | Launch / base |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SENSEX** | BSE | **30 blue chips** | **Free-float market cap** | Published 1 Jan 1986; **base 1978-79 = 100** |
| **NIFTY 50** | NSE | **50** (13 sectors) | Free-float market cap | Launched **22 April 1996**; base 3 Nov 1995 = 1000 |
| **DOLLEX-30** | BSE | 30 | Dollar-linked Sensex | 25 July 2001 |
| **GREENEX** | BSE | **25** (20 at launch) | Top green companies by **GHG emissions** (carbon efficiency) | **22 Feb 2012** |
- A rise in Sensex = rise in prices of the **selected 30 companies**, **not all BSE shares**.
- NIFTY is managed by NSE Indices Ltd (formerly IISL), an NSE subsidiary.
- Foreign: **NIKKEI 225** = Tokyo Stock Exchange; **S&P 500** = 500 large US companies, market-cap weighted.
- **Not indices**: **Moody's** (rating agency) (APPSC 2019); **SAPs** (World Bank/IMF policies) are not share-market terms.
- India fell to **7th** in global market capitalisation (overtaken by South Korea). Per a PYQ, **China**'s market-cap exceeded its GDP.
### 5. MARKET PARTICIPANTS AND TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Bull** | Expects prices to **rise**; bull market = rising |
| **Bear** | Expects prices to **fall**; sells to buy back lower |
| **Stag** | Buys IPO shares to sell on listing |
| Lame Duck | A share-market term (with Bull, Bear, Stag) |
| **Blue chip** | Huge, well-known firms with steady earnings and dividends |
| **Short selling** | Sell borrowed shares, buy back lower |
| **Beta** | **Systematic risk** of a security vs the market (beta 1 = moves with market) |
| **Circuit breaker** | **Trading halt** when an index or stock moves past limits |
| **Sweat equity** | Shares to employees/directors for know-how or services, often at a discount |
| **STT** | Securities Transaction Tax on domestic exchange trades |
- A **broker is not a speculator**; speculators include Bulls and Bears.
- **Depositories**: **NSDL** (**first**, Mumbai, 6 Nov 1996) and **CDSL** (promoted by BSE) hold shares in **demat** form.
- **Settlement**: **T+1** (one trading day after the trade), optional **T+0**.
### 6. INVESTOR CLAIMS AND INSTRUMENTS
| Holder / instrument | Claim |
|---|---|
| **Shareholders** | **Owners**; dividends; **residual claim** |
| **Debenture/bondholders** | **Creditors** (borrowed capital, fixed interest); **paid before shareholders** |
| **Mutual funds** | Pooled money priced at **NAV**; **arbitrage funds** keep **at least 65%** in equity |
- **Equity crowdfunding** is not permitted: SEBI has called online platforms offering it unauthorised (2016).
### 7. DERIVATIVES
| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Futures** | **Obligation** to buy/sell at a fixed future price |
| **Options** | **Right, not obligation**: **call = right to buy**, **put = right to sell** |
| **Swaps** | Exchange of cash flows (fixed vs floating interest) |
| **Commodity derivatives** | **FMC merged into SEBI (2015)**; NCDEX **RAINMUMBAI** = India's first weather derivative; NSE electricity futures |
- F&O market designed by the **L.C. Gupta Committee (1998)** and **J.R. Varma Committee (2002)**.
- India's options market is **among the world's largest by contracts**: Indian exchanges handled about **84% of equity options** traded worldwide (Q1 2024).
- SEBI: **9 in 10 option traders lost money**.
- **Budget 2026-27 STT**: futures **0.02% → 0.05%**; options **0.10% → 0.15%**; aim: **curb speculation** (Nifty fell 1.96%).
- **Commodity Transaction Tax (CTT)**: Budget **2013-14**; **0.01%**.
### 8. SEBI – FUNCTIONS
- Regulates **stock exchanges**, brokers, depositories (CDSL), **mutual funds**, **credit rating agencies**; regulates primary and secondary markets; prevents capital-market scams.
- **Government securities** are under the **RBI**, not SEBI.
