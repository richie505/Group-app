## U0647 | INDIA - POPULATION GROWTH: DECADAL TRENDS, GROWTH RATE, CENSUS 2011 HIGHLIGHTS
### 1. CENSUS IN INDIA - HISTORY, LAW AND ORGANISATION
#### 1.1 FIRSTs
| FIRST | Fact |
|---|---|
| **First census, 1872** | **Non-synchronous**, under **Lord Mayo** |
| **First synchronous (complete) census, 1881** | Under **Lord Ripon**; decennial ever since |
| Census 2011 | **15th** census since 1872; **7th** after Independence |
| Census 2011 motto | "**Our Census, Our Future**"; reference date **1 March 2011** |
| Census 2027 | **First digital census** (mobile-app based, self-enumeration) |
| Census 2027 | **First caste count** in Independent India beyond SC/ST |
!TRAP 1872 = first census (Mayo, non-synchronous); 1881 = first synchronous, modern, comprehensive census (Ripon).
#### 1.2 Legal Basis and Custodian
- **Census Act, 1948**.
- Census = **Union List Entry 69** (Article 246).
- Conducted by the **Registrar General and Census Commissioner of India**, under the **Ministry of Home Affairs**.
#### 1.3 Amendments Tied to the Census
| Amendment | Provision |
|---|---|
| **42nd** | Delimitation seat freeze on 1971 census |
| **84th** | Freeze extended till the **first census after 2026** |
| **106th, 2023 (Art. 334A)** | **Women's reservation**, linked to census and delimitation |
### 2. CENSUS 2027 - THE UPCOMING CENSUS
#### 2.1 Delay and Features
- Census 2021 postponed (**COVID-19**); Census 2027 comes **16 years** after 2011.
- **4 June 2025**: census with **caste enumeration** announced, to finish by 28 Feb 2027.
- **40 questions** notified on 14 Aug 2026, **13 new** (e.g. digital literacy, bank accounts); Census 2011 had **29**.
- Caste question is **open-ended**.
#### 2.2 Timeline
| Phase | Timing |
|---|---|
| I - House Listing and Housing Census (HLO) | **1 April - 30 September 2026** |
| II - Population Enumeration (PE) | **February 2027**; reference **00:00 hrs, 1 March 2027** |
| Snow-bound areas (Ladakh, J&K, HP, Uttarakhand) | 17 Aug - 30 Sep 2026; reference **1 October 2026** |
- Aug-Sep 2026: PE underway in snow-bound areas; debate on caste method.
#### 2.3 Census Town (unchanged since 1981)
- Population **at least 5,000**; density **at least 400 per sq km**; **at least 75% of male workers** outside agriculture.
- 17 Sep 2026: **RGI retains** the 1981 definition for Census 2027, though **MoHUA** calls it outdated (male bias, uniform thresholds).

### 4. DECADAL TRENDS - PHASES OF GROWTH
#### 4.1 Four Phases
| Phase | Period | Features |
|---|---|---|
| I - **Stagnant / stationary** | **1901-1921** | High birth and death rates; **negative** growth in 1911-21 (**-0.31%**) |
| II - **Steady growth** | **1921-1951** | Mortality fell (health, sanitation, transport); birth rate stayed high |
| III - **Population explosion** | **1951-1981** | Rapid fall in mortality, high fertility; annual growth ~**2.2%**; migration from **Tibet, Bangladesh, Nepal, Pakistan** |
| IV - **High but declining** | **Post-1981** (1981-2011) | Birth rate falls: later marriage, **female education** |
- Some notes give the explosion phase as 1961-71, the decade of peak growth.
#### 4.2 Year of the Great Divide
- **1921** = "**Year of the Great Divide**": the **only** census period with a fall (influenza pandemic, famines); population has grown every decade since.
- Annual compound growth: ~**0.19%** (1891-1921) to ~**2.15%** later.
!KEY Only decline = 1911-21; peak decadal growth = **24.80% (1961-71)**.
#### 4.3 Census Figures
| Census | Population | Decadal growth |
|---|---|---|
| 1951 | 36.11 crore | 13.31% |
| 1971 | 54.82 crore | **24.80%** (peak) |
| 2001 | 102.87 crore | 21.54% |
| 2011 | **121.09 crore** | **17.7%** |
- India's growth fell from about **24% (1981-91)** to **21.54% (1991-2001)**.
### 5. CENSUS 2011 HIGHLIGHTS
#### 5.1 Totals
- Population **121.09 crore** (1,21,08,54,977, final): **62.33 crore males**, **58.76 crore females**.
- **Rural 68.9%** (83.38 crore); **urban 31.1%** (**37.71 crore**).
#### 5.2 Growth
- Decadal growth 2001-11 = **17.7%** (provisional 17.64%).
- **Sharpest fall since Independence**: down **3.9 points** from 21.54% (1991-2001).
- **Average annual exponential growth rate = 1.64%** (APPSC-G2 2017).
#### 5.3 Children (0-6) and STs
- 0-6 population **1,644.15 lakh** = about **13.6%** (provisional 13.1%, down from 16% in 2001).
- **First absolute fall** in 0-6 children in decades.
- Largest 0-6 populations: **1 UP, 2 Bihar, 3 Maharashtra, 4 MP** (APPSC key).
- ST population **10.43 crore (8.6%)**; ST decadal growth **23.66%** vs 17.69% overall.

### 7. COMPONENTS OF GROWTH
| Component | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Natural increase** | Births - deaths |
| **Migration** | In-migrants - out-migrants |
| **Actual growth** | Natural increase + net migration |
- 1951-81 growth was boosted by **migration** from Tibet, Bangladesh, Nepal, Pakistan.

### 9. POPULATION DENSITY
#### 9.1 Definition and Trend
- **Density** = persons per sq km = total population / total area; a crude measure of the human-land relationship.
- **Physiological** = total population / net cultivated area; **agricultural** = agricultural population / net cultivable area.
- India: **117 (1951) to 382 (2011)** (APPSC-GS 2023); rose in all states and UTs 1991-2011.
