## U0552 | ECONOMIC SURVEY - STRUCTURE AND KEY THEMES OF THE LATEST SURVEY
### 1. WHO PREPARES IT AND HOW IT IS PRESENTED
- Published every year by the **Ministry of Finance** - its main yearly document.
- Prepared by the **Economic Division, Department of Economic Affairs (DEA)**, under the guidance of the **Chief Economic Adviser (CEA)**.
- Presented in **both Houses** during the Budget Session, **before the Union Budget**.
- **First Survey: 1950-51**; presented **separately from the Budget since 1964**.
- Presenting it is only a **convention**, not a constitutional duty; **Article 112** concerns the **Annual Financial Statement** (Budget).
!TRAP Survey = convention; Budget (AFS) = Article 112 obligation.
#### 1.1 Format
- **Two volumes** in the Subramanian years; **single volume** + statistical appendix since **2021-22**.
- **ES 2025-26**: **17 chapters**; new chapters on **Artificial Intelligence** and **urbanisation**.
- **AP Socio-Economic Survey**: by the **Planning Department**; tabled in both Houses with the State Budget (2025-26 edition came out after the Budget session). **UN ESCAP** Economic Survey (HQ **Bangkok**) is a separate Asia-Pacific report.

### 5. ESTIMATION METHODS AND PYQ FINDINGS
- **ES 2016-17**: **satellite imagery** used to estimate the **property-tax gap of Bengaluru** (collection vs potential).
- **ES 2016-17**: most women in households **without toilets** had to **travel long distances** for a toilet.
- **Bare Necessities Index** built from **NSO 69th (2012)** and **76th (2018)** rounds.
- **ES 2022-23**: projected **6.5%** real growth for 2023-24; after the FY23 Budget the geopolitical conflict worsened supply disruptions (fuel and food). Response: **cut fuel excise** and **raise food/fertiliser subsidy** - not raising fuel taxes or cutting subsidies.
- **ES 2024-25**: **services** showed robust growth in high-frequency indicators in FY25.
- Centre's gross tax revenue growth highest in **2016-17 (17.9%)**; 2015-16 16.9%, 2017-18 11.8%, 2013-14 9.9%, 2014-15 9.3%.
### 6. ECONOMIC SURVEY 2025-26 - THEME AND DATA
- Theme: **"Building Strategic Indispensability"** and entrepreneurial policy making amid geopolitical friction. **Chapter 1**: "State of the Economy: **Pushing the Growth Frontier**".
| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Real GDP FY26 (First Advance Estimates) | **7.4%**; Q3 nowcast 7% |
| FY27 projection | **6.8% - 7.2%** |
| Potential growth | **7.0%** (from 6.5% in ES 2022-23) |
| Sectors FY26 | Agriculture **3.1%** < Manufacturing **7.0%** < Services **9.1%** ("stabilising anchor") |
| CPI inflation | 1.7% (Apr-Dec FY26) |
| CAD | **0.8% of GDP** (H1 FY26); forex cover over 11 months of imports |
- High-frequency indicators: automobile sales, UPI transactions, air passenger traffic.
#### 6.1 Fiscal and Monetary Response
- Fiscal: FD **4.8%** in FY25 (vs 4.9% budgeted); FY26 target 4.4%. **S&P upgrade BBB- to BBB** - first major upgrade in about two decades.
- Concerns: **State revenue deficits** and **unconditional cash transfers**.
- Monetary: **repo cut 125 bps** since Feb 2025; bank **GNPA 2.2%**, a multi-decade low.
#### 6.2 Other Themes
- **Climate chapter**: **adaptation over mitigation**; scarce funds not to be diverted from health, agriculture, poverty; civilisational collapse "unlikely".
- Conclusion: **Sreya vs Preya** from the **Katha Upanishad**.
- **Statistical reforms**: National Accounts and **IIP rebased to 2022-23** (Feb 2026); CAPI; **ASISSE**; Household Income Survey.
- Scenario: "Systemic Shock Cascade" (10-20%) worse than 2008.
- **Reforms of 2025**: GST overhaul, **100% FDI in insurance**, **nuclear power** opened to private sector, **four Labour Codes** notified, **QCOs** put on hold. Complete abolition of GST was NOT one.
### 7. RECURRING FRAMEWORKS
- **Deregulation**: ES 2023-24 "letting go"; ES 2024-25 central theme; ES 2025-26 from "**regulation and control**" to "**enabling**", **trust-based compliance** replacing inspection, "**process reforms more important than policy reforms**".
- **Viksit Bharat @2047**: infrastructure as growth pillar; public capex **₹12.2 lakh crore** proposed for 2026-27.
- **Ease of Living**: Budget 2026-27 pillar "Ease of Doing Business and Ease of Living"; ES 2020-21 Bare Necessities Index measured it.
- Sep 2026: CII survey - half of firms say macro growth is not fully reaching them; confidence score 66 (Q2 FY27).
