## U0378 | GLOBALISATION AND SOCIETY – CULTURE, MIGRATION, LABOUR, INEQUALITY; THE ANTI-GLOBALISATION CRITIQUE
### 1. MEANING AND CULTURAL-CHANGE TERMS
- **Globalisation**: free flow of goods, services, capital, technology, ideas and people across borders.
- **Economic Survey 2025-26**: "hyper-globalisation" has given way to **"geostrategic globalisation"**.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Diffusion** | Spread of cultural traits from one society to another |
| **Acculturation** | Mutual exchange; **both change, each stays distinct** |
| **Assimilation** | Minority absorbed into dominant culture; **loses identity** |
| **Glocalisation** | Global products adapted to **local** tastes |
| **Homogenisation** | Convergence to one (often Western) pattern |
| **Hybridisation** | Blend of global and local elements |
| **McDonaldisation** | Fast-food principles – efficiency, calculability, predictability, control |
!TRAP Acculturation ≠ assimilation.

### 3. MIGRATION
- **World Migration Report 2026** (**IOM**, biennial since 2000): **304 million** migrants (**3.7%**); **India = largest diaspora**; India-UAE corridor **5th**, India-US **6th**.
- **India = top remittance recipient**: about **$129 billion in 2024** (World Bank), ahead of Mexico and China.
- **Gulf labour** = backbone of India's remittance economy; remittances and services exports keep the **CAD steady** despite the trade deficit.
- **AP**: East and West Godavari, **Kadapa** major Gulf-emigration districts; **APNRTS** monitored about **10 lakh** AP people in the Gulf (Mar 2026).
- **PLFS 2020-21**: migrant share **28.9%**; rural migrants **48% of women vs 5.9% of men**.
- MEA: **Migration and Mobility Partnerships** (e.g. Moldova); **Pre-Departure Orientation Training** (Jan 2018) under **Pravasi Kaushal Vikas Yojana**.
- **MoSPI** migration survey from July 2026.
### 4. LABOUR
| Concept | Scholar |
|---|---|
| **Informal sector** (coined 1971-73) | **Keith Hart** |
| **Footloose**, circular, casual labour (*Footloose Labour*, 1996) | **Jan Breman** |
- **Gig workers** (NITI Aayog 2022): **77 lakh** (2020-21) → **2.35 crore** by 2029-30 (**4.1%** of workforce). **Fairwork India**: gaps in fair pay and representation.
### 5. INEQUALITY
- **World Inequality Report 2026**: India's top **1% ~40%**, top **10% ~65%** of wealth.
- **Oxfam India, "Survival of the Richest" (Jan 2023)**: top 1% held **40.5%** of wealth (2021); bottom 50% about **3%**.
### 6. IMPACT ON GROUPS
| Group | Effect |
|---|---|
| Consumers / youth culture | More choice, global brands, more media; risk of "mindless aping" of the West – but **not full end** of local culture |
| Youth | **29%** of tertiary-educated unemployed (2023-24) |
| Farmers | Feared cheap imports under WTO; debt-linked suicides in **Maharashtra and AP** |
| Small growers | **98%** of coffee growers small; face Brazil, Vietnam |
| Women | Self-employed women nearly **tripled** since 2017-18 |
| Artisans, domestic firms | Squeezed by MNCs → layoffs; skilled move to MNCs, unskilled left behind |
!TRAP Fulfilling **export obligations** is NOT a bad effect of MNCs.
### 7. ANTI-GLOBALISATION CRITIQUE
- **Vandana Shiva**: **Navdanya**, seed sovereignty, against biopiracy.
- **Joseph Stiglitz**: *Globalization and Its Discontents* (**2002**); criticised **IMF** conditions.
- **Seattle, 1999**: protests against the WTO Ministerial.
- **World Social Forum**: first **Porto Alegre, Brazil, 2001** ("Another world is possible"); **Mumbai 2004**.
- **Sharad Joshi** (Shetkari Sanghatana) **supported** globalisation; **Nanjundaswamy** and **Mahendra Singh Tikait** (BKU) **opposed** the Dunkel Draft, GATT, WTO.
- Criticism: widens rich-poor gaps; may become **"commercial imperialism"**.
