## U0736 | SPACE DEBRIS, SPACE SITUATIONAL AWARENESS AND INTERNATIONAL SPACE LAW
### 1. SPACE DEBRIS
- **Space debris:** all **non-functional man-made objects**, incl. fragments, in orbit or re-entering.
- Sources: defunct satellites, spent stages, **ASAT tests**; **main source = fragmentation** from collisions and explosions.
- Over **1.2 million objects > 1 cm** (ESA 2025).
- **Kessler Syndrome** (Donald Kessler, NASA, 1978): debris density → collisions → more fragments → orbits unusable.
- **MMOD** (micrometeoroids and orbital debris) = top risk in **LEO**, not GEO.
- **Mission Shakti (27 Mar 2019):** **DRDO** kinetic-kill ASAT test; destroyed **Microsat-R** at ~**300 km**.

### 3. MITIGATION
- **Debris Free Space Missions (DFSM) by 2030** – pledged at the **2024 IADC** meeting.
- **POEM-4** (SpaDeX/**PSLV-C60**): controlled re-entry into the **Indian Ocean, 4 Apr 2025**; inflatable sails → re-entry in months, not decades.
- **PSLV-C62** (Jan 2026) planned a PS4 de-boost.
- **Active Debris Removal:** servicer with **robotic arm or net** for a defunct Indian rocket body; tested at **URSC**.
- **2026 re-entry rules:** warn aircraft and ships **at least 45 days** before re-entry.
- Gaps: no international duty-of-care standard; differing licence rules push operators to lax regimes.
### 4. SPACE TREATIES
| Instrument | Core rule | India |
|---|---|---|
| **Outer Space Treaty 1967** | **No sovereignty**; Art. VI national responsibility incl. private actors; **Art. VII liability**; Art. IX due regard/environment | Party (**ratified 1982**) |
| Rescue Agreement 1968 | Return of astronauts | Party |
| **Liability Convention 1972** | **Absolute** liability on Earth; **fault** liability in space | Party |
| Registration Convention 1975 | Register objects with UN | Party |
| **Moon Agreement 1979** | Common heritage | **Signed, not ratified** |
!TRAP Moon Agreement is the only one India signed but did NOT ratify.
- Treaties (1960s–70s) do not cover cumulative debris or mega-constellations; India has **no Space Activities Act** on liability.
