### 2. FOOD TECHNOLOGY - ADDITIVES, PRESERVATION, FORTIFICATION
#### 2.1 Additives vs Adulterants
- **Food additives** = added **intentionally** to improve food (preservatives, colours, emulsifiers, sweeteners, flavour enhancers). **Adulteration** = adding inferior/harmful substances; causes poisoning, allergies, nutritional deficiency.
- Sweeteners: aspartame, stevia; fructose = natural sugar. **MSG (Ajinomoto)** = flavour enhancer, **umami**.
#### 2.2 Preservatives
| Preservative | Used in |
|---|---|
| **Sodium benzoate (E211)** | Soft drinks, pickles, sauces (acidic foods) |
| Sorbic acid / potassium sorbate | Cheese, pickles; against yeasts, moulds |
| Sodium metabisulphite | Jams, sauces |
| SO2 | Dried fruits, winemaking |
| Propionates | Stored grains, cheese |
| Vinegar (acetic acid) | Pickles; made by fermenting sugarcane juice |
| Boric acid | Old, no longer used (toxic) |
#### 2.3 Irradiation, Fortification, Cold Chain
- **Irradiation**: ionising (gamma) radiation kills organisms without much nutrient loss; potato/onion shelf life **8 months** without cold storage. **DAE: 25 irradiators in PPP mode**.
- **PM Kisan Sampada Yojana** (MoFPI) Cold Chain scheme: 16 irradiation units approved, 9 operational (Aug 2025).
- **Fortification**: iodised salt; rice with iron, folic acid, B12; oil/milk with A and D; FSSAI logo **"+F"**.
- **Jul 2026**: food-safety reforms - Food Safety on Wheels, blockchain batch tracing, "One Nation, One Regulator" ends dual **BIS/AGMARK** certification.
- Kitchen: colas contain **phosphoric acid** and **caffeine**; lemon = **citric acid**; ant bite = **formic acid** (stronger than acetic acid).

- Sources: vit C - **amla** (richest), chilli (vegetables), papaya; vit A - carrot, **mango** (fruits), **Golden Rice** (beta carotene); vit E - **wheat germ oil**; milk is poor in vit C and A. Vit C not stored; B12 and K made by gut bacteria; vit A stored in liver.
- Scientists: **Casimir Funk** coined "vitamine"; **James Lind** - citrus cures scurvy; Hopkins - accessory food factors; McCollum and Davis - vit A (1913); Suzuki - rice bran extract.
- Nutrients: carbohydrate = main energy; **fat = 9.3 kcal/g**; protein ~4 kcal/g. Highest protein grain **soybean**; groundnut rich (~25% protein); highest biological value **egg**; iodine **seaweed**; alpha-linolenic acid **linseed**; carbohydrate **rice**.
- Proteins: **casein** (milk), **gluten** (wheat), **keratin** (hair, nails), collagen, fibroin (silk); oxytocin is NOT a protein. **Kwashiorkor** (oedema) and **marasmus** = protein deficiency. Nursing mother 65 g/day.
- Amino acids: 20 (10 essential); not stored. Protein digestion starts in stomach (pepsin), then trypsin.
### 4. HUMAN BODY - GLANDS AND KIDNEY
| Gland | Hormone / fact |
|---|---|
| Pituitary (master) | GH, TSH, ACTH, ADH; oxytocin released here, made in hypothalamus |
| Pineal | **Melatonin** - biological clock |
| Thyroid (largest endocrine) | T3, T4 (iodine), calcitonin; deficiency: cretinism |
| Parathyroid | PTH - blood calcium |
| Adrenal (on kidney) | Adrenaline, cortisol |
| Pancreas | Endocrine + exocrine; insulin from **Islets of Langerhans** |
| Kidney / placenta | Erythropoietin / chorionic gonadotropin |
- **Urea made in the liver**, filtered by kidney; urine ~1.5 L/day, pH ~6.0; albumin in urine = kidney failure (Bright's disease). Normal BP **120/80 mmHg**.
- **Dialysis** when kidneys fail: haemodialysis (machine filter) vs **peritoneal** (peritoneum). Lithotripsy breaks stones with ultrasound.
### 5. HEALTH TECHNOLOGIES - IMAGING AND TELEMEDICINE
| Method | Principle |
|---|---|

- **Telemedicine**: fibre backbone + satellite; **eSanjeevani** = national telemedicine. **Chitra heart valve** - **SCTIMST, Thiruvananthapuram**. Wearables give real-time data.
