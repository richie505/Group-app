### 2. CHOOSING THE NEXT UN SECRETARY-GENERAL
#### 2.1 Step-by-step Process
1. **UNSC straw polls** – the first was held in **August 2026**.
2. A candidate needs at least **9 "Encourage"** votes out of **15**, and **no "Discourage"** vote from a **permanent member (P5)**.
3. The **UNSC recommends** the candidate.
4. The **UNGA appoints** – **Article 97** of the **UN Charter**.
5. Term: **5 years**, from **1 January 2027**.
| Post / process | Rule |
|---|---|
| UN Secretary-General | **5-year** term; successor's term starts **1 January 2027** |
| Appointing authority | **UNGA**, on the **UNSC's recommendation** (Art. 97) |
| Straw poll rule | **9 of 15** "Encourage" + **no P5 "Discourage"** |
!TRAP The **UNGA appoints** the Secretary-General; the UNSC only **recommends**.
