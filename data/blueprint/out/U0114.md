## U0114 | SULTANATE ECONOMY – KHARAJ, JIZYA, ZAKAT, KHAMS; AGRICULTURE, TOWNS, TRADE AND CRAFT
### 1. SHARIA TAXES
| Tax | Nature | Payer |
|---|---|---|
| **Kharaj** | Land tax, usually **1/3** (1/10 to 1/2) | **Non-Muslim** cultivators |
| **Ushr** | Land tax at **1/10** | **Muslim** landholders |
| **Jizya** | Poll tax; Firoz extended it to **Brahmins** | Non-Muslims |
| **Zakat** | Religious tax, about **2.5%** of wealth | **Muslims only** |
| **Khams** | **1/5 of war booty** and treasure-troves (APPSC 2012) | State's share |
- Khams: **Alauddin and MBT took 4/5**; **Firoz restored 1/5**.
- **Firoz** ended illegal cesses; kept only **kharaj, jizya, zakat, khams**.
- **Not sharia**: ghari, charai, haq-i-sharb (cesses), taccavi (loans), iqta/inam (assignments).
### 2. LAND REVENUE AND CESSES
- Peasants paid **1/3**; **Alauddin raised it to 1/2**.
| Cess | Nature | Sultan |
|---|---|---|
| **Ghari** | **House tax** (first charged) | **Alauddin** |
| **Charai** | **Grazing tax** on milch cattle | **Alauddin** |
| **Haq-i-sharb** | **Irrigation tax, 10%** of yield, on ulema's advice (first charged) | **Firoz** |
- Land categories: **khalisa** (crown land), **iqta** (assigned to officials in place of salary), **inam** (religious grants).
- Alauddin began a **Public Distribution System**; price-control markets served **army needs**.
### 3. AGRICULTURE AND IRRIGATION
- Crops: wheat, barley, gram, **rice**, sugarcane, cotton, oilseeds; Ibn Battuta saw **two harvests** (rabi, kharif); rice sown **three times** a year.
- **Not grown**: **maize** (brought later by the **Portuguese**) and **tobacco** (UPPSC 2011).
- **Araghatta** = water-wheel with **earthen pots** on its rim (Ar = spokes, Ghata = pot); **animal-driven**, lifts water from **open wells**; in **Panchatantra** and **Rajatarangini**. Turks spread the **Persian wheel (saqiya)**.
- **First canal-builder** (14th c.): **Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq** (UPPSC 2017); **most canals**: **Firoz**, longest **Yamuna → Hisar**.
- **MBT**: **Diwan-i-Kohi** and **taccavi (sondhar)** loans.
- **AP angle**: Kakatiya **Maagani** (irrigated) vs **Metta** (rain-fed); land tax **Ari**; Bahmani kharaj 1/3.
### 4. COINS
| Coin | Metal | Sultan |
|---|---|---|
| **Tanka** | **Silver, 175 grains** | **Iltutmish** |
| **Jital** | **Copper** | **Iltutmish** |
| **Shashgani** | **Silver**, 6 jitals | Tughlaq |
| **Dinar** | **Gold**, popular after **South Indian loot** | **Alauddin** |
| **Token** | **Copper, brass** (c. 1329-30), at par with tanka | **MBT**; failed by **forgery** |
| Adha, bikh | 1/2, 1/4 jital | Firoz |
| Bahloli | Copper | Bahlol Lodi |
- Iltutmish: **first regular, standard currency**; Caliph's name on coins.
- **Ghori**: gold coins with **Lakshmi** (Gahadavala type); Kalma on others. **Dehliwal** = pre-Sultanate Tomara/Chauhan coin.
- **Sher Shah**: **Rupiya** 178 grains (**173 pure silver**), copper **Daam** 380 grains; **1:64**; pure gold, silver, copper; basis of later currency. Revenue 1/4-1/3 by survey, model for **Todar Mal**.
### 5. TOWNS → FOUNDER

- **Daulatabad (Devagiri)** = MBT; **Nagarkot** = Firoz's **1,300 Sanskrit manuscripts**.
### 6. CRAFTS AND TRADE
- **Karkhanas**: Firoz ran about **36** (luxury goods). Turkish gains: **paper** (record-keeping), glass, **spinning wheel**, **Persian wheel**.
- Crafts grown: **textiles, carpets, leather, metal-work** (not printing, tea, rubber).
- Imports: **horses, textiles, glassware** from West Asia; **silk, porcelain** from China.
| Group | Role |
|---|---|
| **Multanis, Khurasanis** | **Overseas** trade, finance |
| **Gujarati, Marwari, Bohra** | **Inland** trade |
| **Banjaras** | **Bulk grain** on oxen |
- **Ulaq** (horse post, every 4 miles) brought **Khorasan fruit** to Delhi (APPSC 2017); **dawa** = foot post.
- **Amir Khusrau**: Warangal's **diamonds** drew Delhi's invasions; Ibn Battuta: Malabar **pepper** trade.
### 7. SOCIAL CLASSES
- **Ulema**: keepers of religious law. **Peasants**: revenue burden, collected by **khuts and muqaddams**. **Slaves**: slave markets (Ibn Battuta). Caste continued; **sati, purdah** spread.
