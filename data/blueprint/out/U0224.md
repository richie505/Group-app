## U0224 | DEPRESSED CLASSES MOVEMENT, COMMUNAL AWARD AND THE POONA PACT 1932
### 1. OVERVIEW
- Exam: **Group 1 Prelims** | Tier: **MED** | Row: A-184
- PYQ importance: **APPSC 3 questions** | other exams 22 | last APPSC year 2016
- Chain of 1932: **2nd RTC fails** over minority representation → **Communal Award** → **Gandhi's fast** → **Poona Pact**.
### 2. COMMUNAL AWARD, 1932
#### 2.1 Announcement
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Date | **16 August 1932** |
| Announced by | British PM **Ramsay MacDonald** |
| Context | **2nd Round Table Conference** failed over minority representation |
| Groups | Indians divided into **11 groups** |
| Women | **3% of seats** in all provinces **except NWFP** |
#### 2.2 Communities Given Separate Electorates (full list)
- Muslims
- Sikhs
- Indian Christians
- Europeans
- Anglo-Indians
- **Depressed Classes** – declared a **minority** and given a separate electorate **for the first time**
- Depressed classes got a **double vote**: one in a separate electorate + one in the general electorate.
- Under the Award the depressed classes were to get **71 seats** in the provincial legislatures.
!TRAP Depressed classes had a **double vote**, not a single vote.
!KEY A/R (UPPSC 2017): the Award gave each minority seats elected by separate electorate – both true, R explains A.
### 3. GANDHI'S FAST AND THE AMBEDKAR-GANDHI DEBATE
| Point | Gandhi | Ambedkar |
|---|---|---|
| Separate electorate for Dalits | Opposed: it would **permanently divide Hindu society** | Supported the separate electorate the Award gave |
| Action | **Fast unto death from 20 September 1932**, **Yerwada Jail, Poona** | Signed the Poona Pact, accepting a joint electorate |
!TRAP Award = **16 Aug 1932** (MacDonald); fast = **20 Sept 1932** (Yerwada).
### 4. THE POONA PACT, 24 SEPTEMBER 1932
#### 4.1 Signatories and Terms
- Signed on **24 September 1932** in Poona.
- Signed by **B.R. Ambedkar** for the depressed classes and **Madan Mohan Malaviya** for the caste Hindus.
- **Separate electorate dropped**; **joint electorate with reserved seats**.
#### 4.2 Award vs Pact
| Feature | Communal Award | Poona Pact |
|---|---|---|
| Electorate for depressed classes | Separate (+ double vote) | **Joint**, with reserved seats |
| Reserved seats (provincial) | **71** | **148** |
| Basis | British decision | Agreement among Indians |
- The Pact's reserved seats in a joint electorate became the **model for SC political reservation** under the Constitution.
- Forerunner: the **Rajah-Moonje Pact (February 1932)** between **M.C. Rajah** and **B.S. Moonje** (Hindu Mahasabha) on reserved seats in joint electorates; Ambedkar opposed it.
!NUM 71 seats under the Award vs 148 under the Pact.

### 7. DEPRESSED CLASSES IN ANDHRA – ADI-ANDHRA
- **Bhagya Reddy Varma** led the depressed classes in Hyderabad State.
- At the **1917 Vijayawada** conference he argued the depressed classes were the **original inhabitants**, to be called **Adi-Hindu**, not "Panchama"; the conference adopted the name **Adi-Andhra Mahajana Sabha**.
- **Arige Ramaswamy** worked with him to organise the Adi-Andhra movement among the Malas.
- He received the title "**Varma**" from the **Arya Samaj**.

### 9. "SCHEDULED CASTES" – MILESTONES
- **1930**: Simon Commission report first used the term "**Scheduled Castes**".
- **1935**: term fixed by the **Government of India Act, 1935**.
- **1936**: first **SC list**.
- Latest (Apr 2026): the Social Justice Ministry began a 2-year celebration of **Jyotiba Phule's 200th birth anniversary**.

### 12. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS

4. Seats: **148** (Pact) > **71** (Award).
5. Depressed classes got a **double vote** under the Award.
6. Women's 3% seats excluded the **NWFP**.
7. First president of the Anti-Untouchability League = **G.D. Birla**.
8. **Mooknayak** and **Bahishkrit Bharat** = Ambedkar; **Harijan** = Gandhi.
