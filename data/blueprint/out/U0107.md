## U0107 | KHALJI DYNASTY - JALALUDDIN, ALAUDDIN'S CONQUESTS, MALIK KAFUR'S DECCAN CAMPAIGNS
### 1. DELHI SULTANS → REIGN PERIOD
| Sultan | Reign |
|---|---|
| **Jalaluddin Khalji** | **1290-1296** |
| **Alauddin Khalji** | **1296-1316** |
| **Muhammad bin Tughlaq** | **1325-1351** |
| **Khizr Khan** (Sayyid) | **1414-1421** |
| **Bahlol Lodi** | **1451-1489** (not 1491) |
- Khalji dynasty: **1290-1320**. Alauddin's reign = "**imperial period**" of the Sultanate.
!TRAP Alauddin's son Khizr Khan (set aside 1316) is NOT the Sayyid Sultan Khizr Khan (1414-1421).
### 2. JALALUDDIN KHALJI (1290-1296)
- Khalji from **Turkestan**; had fought the Mongols. **Crowned himself at Kilokhari, 1290**, aged about **70**.
- **First Sultan with a benevolent attitude to Hindus**; lenient to rebels such as **Malik Chhajju**.
- Mongols: defeated a Mongol invasion; converted and settled Mongols = "**New Mussalmans**"; married his daughter to **Ulugh Khan**, a descendant of Chengiz Khan.
- End: nephew and son-in-law **Alauddin** raided **Devagiri (1294-96)** = **first Turkish invasion of the south**; Alauddin **murdered Jalaluddin at Kara (1296)**.
### 3. ALAUDDIN KHALJI (1296-1316)
#### 3.1 Name, Posts, Titles
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ali Gurshasp** | Earlier name |
| **Amir-i-Tuzuk** | **Master of ceremonies** (post under Jalaluddin) |
| **Ariz-i-Mumalik** | **Army minister** (post under Jalaluddin) |
| **Sikandar-i-Sani** | "**Second Alexander**", on his coins |
!TRAP Ariz-i-Mumalik = army minister, not minister of law.
#### 3.2 Religious Ambitions and the Shariat
- Wished to be a **world conqueror** and to **found a new religion**; dropped both on the advice of the **Kotwal of Delhi Ala-ul-Mulk** (Barani's uncle); Ulemas opposed.
- **Barani** records his talk with **Qazi Mughisuddin of Bayana**: told his acts violated the Shariat, Alauddin replied he acted for the **interest of the state**.
- Barani separated **Shariat** from **Zawabit** (state laws made by the ruler).
#### 3.3 Nobles
- **Ulugh Khan, Nusrat Khan, Zafar Khan, Ain-ul-Mulk Multani, Malik Kafur, Ghazi Malik**. Court poet: **Amir Khusrau**.
### 4. CONQUESTS IN ORDER
| Year | Kingdom | Ruler defeated | Commander / Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1299** | **Gujarat** (Vaghela) | **Rai Karan** (Karnadeva II) | **Ulugh Khan** and **Nusrat Khan**; queen **Kamala Devi** captured; **Malik Kafur** acquired |
| c. 1299 | Jaisalmer | | Before Ranthambore (UPPSC 2025) |
| **1301** | **Ranthambore** | **Hammir Deva** Chauhan (sheltered New Mussalmans) | **Nusrat Khan killed**; Alauddin led in person; jauhar described by Khusrau |
| **1303** | **Chittor** (Mewar) | **Rana Ratan Singh** | Renamed **Khizrabad** after prince Khizr Khan |
| **1305** | **Malwa** | **Mahlak Deva** | **Ain-ul-Mulk**; Ujjain, Mandu, Dhar, Chanderi |
| c. 1308-11 | **Siwana**, **Jalore** | **Shital Deva** (Siwana) | Rajputana completed by c. 1311 |
- Policy: **north annexed**; **south** kings kept thrones and **paid tribute**.
- Order: **Gujarat - Ranthambore - Chittor - Malwa - Devagiri - Warangal - Dwarasamudra - Madurai** (UPPSC 2022: Gujarat, Ranthambore, Chittor, Warangal).
!TRAP Gujarat = 1299 (APPSC 2023 key), not 1297.
### 5. MALIK KAFUR AND THE DECCAN CAMPAIGNS
#### 5.1 Origin and Epithet
- Taken by **Nusrat Khan** in the Gujarat campaign; called "**Hazar Dinari**" (bought for a thousand dinars).
#### 5.2 Campaigns → Kingdom + Ruler
| Year | Kingdom | Ruler | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1307-08** | **Devagiri** (Yadava) | **Ramachandra** | Tribute agreed |
| **1309-10** | **Warangal** (Kakatiya) | **Prataparudra II** | **Treaty** and annual tribute |
| **1310-11** | **Dwarasamudra** (Hoysala) | **Vira Ballala III** | Submitted |
| **1311** | **Madurai** (Pandya) | Pandya princes | **Kakatiya troops aided** Kafur |
- Aim: **wealth and tribute**, not annexation.
- **Koh-i-Noor tradition**: a later popular tradition says Kafur obtained it from Prataparudra at Warangal; no contemporary record.
#### 5.3 AP Angle: Kakatiya-Delhi Relations
- **1303**: Kakatiya army (**Prataparudra II**) defeated Alauddin's army at **Upparapalli**, Warangal; Delhi army led by **Fakhruddin Juna** and Malik Chhajju.
- Treaty 1309-10 → help against Pandyas 1311 → **tribute stopped after 1316**.
- Invasions of Warangal: Telugu sources **8**, Muslim sources **5**.
### 6. MONGOL INVASIONS
| Period | Fact |
|---|---|
| **1221** | **Chengiz Khan** (Temujin) reached the Indus under **Iltutmish**, chasing **Jalaluddin Mangbarni** = first Mongol threat |
| Jalaluddin | Defeated a Mongol invasion; New Mussalmans |
| **1299** | **Battle of Kili**: Mongols under **Qutlugh Khwaja**; **Zafar Khan killed** |
| **1303** | **Siege of Delhi** under **Targhi**; Alauddin sheltered in **Siri** fort |
| After **1306** | **Ravi** = Sultanate-Mongol boundary; Kafur and Ghazi Malik stopped Mongols under **Kabak** |
| **1320** | **Ghazi Malik** chased the Mongols **29 times** |
| **1398** | **Timur** invaded Delhi (Nasiruddin Mahmud Tughlaq) |
- Defence: repaired and built forts on the north-west frontier (completing Balban's policy); **Siri** as new capital; **large standing army** paid by military, revenue and **market reforms**; Ghazi Malik governor of **Punjab**.
!TRAP "First Mongol invasion was under Jalaluddin" is FALSE (UPSC 2022).
### 7. CHITTOR AND THE PADMINI LEGEND
- **1303**: Alauddin defeated **Rana Ratan Singh**; **Rani Padmini** and Rajput women performed **jauhar**.
| Book | Author | Year / Language |
|---|---|---|
| **Padmavat** | **Malik Muhammad Jayasi** | **1540**, **Awadhi** |
- Debate: written about two centuries later; many scholars treat the Padmini motive as legend.
### 8. SUCCESSORS AND END OF THE KHALJIS
1. Alauddin dies **1316**; **Kafur** sets aside Khizr Khan, enthrones minor **Shihabuddin Umar**; Kafur murdered.
2. **Qutbuddin Mubarak Shah** (1316-20): **Khusrau Khan** subdued Warangal again (**third invasion, 1318**).
3. **Khusrau Khan** (Nasiruddin Khusrau Shah) murdered Mubarak.
4. **Ghazi Malik** killed him (**1320**) → **Tughlaq dynasty** (Ghiyasuddin Tughluq).

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- First Turkish invasion of the south: Alauddin's Devagiri raid (1294-96).
- First Sultan benevolent to Hindus: Jalaluddin.
- Last Khalji Sultan: Mubarak Shah (Khusrau Khan a usurper).
