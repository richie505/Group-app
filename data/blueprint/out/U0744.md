## U0744 | INDIA'S ENERGY RESOURCES AND IMPORT DEPENDENCE – CRUDE OIL, COAL, GAS IMPORT SHARE; ENERGY SECURITY
### 1. CLASSIFICATION OF ENERGY RESOURCES
| Basis | Types |
|---|---|
| Renewal | **Renewable** (solar, wind, hydro, biomass, tidal, geothermal) vs **non-renewable** (coal, oil, gas, nuclear fuels) |
| Use history | **Conventional** (fossil fuels, hydro, thermal, biomass/wood) vs **non-conventional** (solar, tidal, geothermal, nuclear, ocean thermal) |
| Origin | **Not derived from the Sun**: **geothermal** (Earth's interior heat), **tidal** (movement of ocean water), **nuclear** (splitting heavy nuclei) |
!TRAP "Conventional" means long-established use, **not** non-renewable: hydro and biomass are conventional **and** renewable.
### 2. DOMESTIC RESERVES – NUMBER GAME
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| India's share of world | **~17% of population** vs only **0.4% oil**, **0.6% gas**, **7% coal** reserves |
| **Coal** | World's **fifth-largest reserves**; **third-largest producer** |
| Sedimentary basins | **26**, only **7** producing |

!TRAP Coal: **fifth**-largest reserves but **third**-largest producer.

### 4. OIL CONSUMPTION CHALLENGES
#### 4.1 Core Challenge
!KEY APPSC G2 Mains 2025: core challenge in managing oil consumption = **supply-chain disruptions and fluctuating global oil prices**.
| Angle | Issue |
|---|---|
| **Price** | Global volatility feeds **domestic inflation** |
| **Fiscal / external** | Oil = biggest driver of **trade deficit / CAD** |
| **Supply route** | Chokepoints like the **Strait of Hormuz** |
#### 4.2 The 2026 Hormuz Shock
1. **Strait of Hormuz** (between **Iran and Oman**) effectively closed by Iran, **early March 2026**.
2. It carries ~**15 million bbl/day** (~**34%** of world crude trade) and **a fifth of global LNG**.
3. India's crude basket **$82.04/bbl** (July 2026) vs $70.95 a year earlier; Brent ~$91.84 (Aug 2026).
4. Result: wider **trade deficit**, **inflation**, **LPG shortage**, volatile LNG prices.
5. Refiners ordered under the LPG control order to maximise yield: LPG output **35 → 54 TMT/day** in five days.
6. Response: proposed **41 MT strategic fuel programme** (Aug 2026).
### 5. ENERGY IMPORT BILL
- Crude import bill **FY 2024-25: $137.0 billion** (up 3%).
- **July 2026: $13.7 billion, up 41%** year-on-year (PPAC); volume 21.4 MMT (+13.3%); product exports +10% to 5.5 MMT.
- Higher oil prices → wider **trade deficit** and higher **inflation**.
### 6. STRATEGIC PETROLEUM RESERVES
#### 6.1 Phase I (underground rock caverns)
| Site | State | Capacity |
|---|---|---|
| **Visakhapatnam** | **Andhra Pradesh** | **1.33 MMT** |
| **Mangaluru** | Karnataka | **1.5 MMT** |
| **Padur** | Karnataka | **2.5 MMT** |
- Actual stock ~3.37 MT (64% utilisation) → only **~5 days** of cover; **IEA norm = 90 days** (not 60).
#### 6.2 Phase II and New Plan
- Phase II approved: **Chandikhol (Odisha)**, **Padur (Karnataka)**; proposed **Bikaner (Rajasthan)**, **Rajkot (Gujarat)** (Mar 2026).
- Proposed decade-long plan (Aug 2026): **41 MT = 28 MT crude + 9 MT LNG + 4 MT LPG**; cover ~two months crude, 56 days LNG, six weeks LPG.
- Gaps: **no underground gas storage**; total oil stock ~74 days.
!TRAP **Padur** is in both Phase I and Phase II.
### 7. POLICIES – HYDROCARBON VISION 2025 AND UPSTREAM
#### 7.1 Hydrocarbon Vision 2025
- Long-term hydrocarbon plan **adopted in 2000**; linked in exams with **storage of petroleum products** and long-term energy security (UPPSC 2000).
- Focus: **self-reliance** through **indigenous production** and **equity oil abroad**; appraisal of all sedimentary basins; **free market and competition** (no ban on private participation).
#### 7.2 Upstream and Diversification
- **HELP**: **single licence for all hydrocarbons**; **OALP** for upstream exploration and production.
- **One Nation One Gas Grid**; domestic E&P plus overseas acreage.
- **ONGC Videsh (OVL)**: overseas arm of ONGC; buys **equity oil and gas abroad**.
- 2026: refiners + **Shipping Corporation of India** JV ($1.5–2 bn, 59 ships).
- **TAPI** pipeline: **Turkmenistan–Afghanistan–Pakistan–India**; company TPCL (2014); stalled.
- **IPI** ("peace pipeline"): **Iran–Pakistan–India**; India moved away (2007–08) towards TAPI.
### 8. IMPORT-SUBSTITUTION FUELS
#### 8.1 Methanol Economy (NITI Aayog)
- Aims (**APPSC G1 2024: all three correct**): (i) **cut oil import bill**, (ii) **cut GHG emissions**, (iii) convert **coal reserves and municipal solid waste** into methanol.
| Term | Meaning / use |
|---|---|
| **Methanol** | Low-carbon, hydrogen-carrier fuel; from biomass, high-ash coal, agri-residue, CO₂ from thermal plants, natural gas |
| **DME** (Di-Methyl Ether) | **Two methanol molecules minus one water molecule**; replaces **propane in LPG** and diesel in slow-RPM engines |
- Methanol replaces petrol/diesel in transport, DG sets, boilers, and partly **LPG and kerosene in cooking**.
| Blend | Benefit |
|---|---|
| **M15** (15% methanol in petrol) | At least **15% cut** in petrol/crude imports; 20% cut in PM, NOx, SOx |
| **20% DME in LPG** | Saves **₹6,000 crore/yr**; ₹50–100 per cylinder |
#### 8.2 Ethanol Blended Petrol (EBP)
| Year | Step |
|---|---|
| **January 2003** | **5% EBP** in **9 sugarcane States + 4 UTs** (incl. Andhra Pradesh) |
| 2013-14 | Blending ~1.53% |
| **ESY 2025-26** | **E20 achieved** (target advanced from **2030**) |
| **1 Apr 2026** | E20 with minimum **95 RON** mandated |
- Farmer payments over ₹1,29,032 crore.
#### 8.3 Compressed Biogas Obligation (CBO) in City Gas Distribution
| Year | Share of CNG/PNG |
|---|---|
| FY 2025-26 / 2026-27 / 2027-28 | **1% / 3% / 4%** |
| From 2028-29 | **5%** |
- Aim: **replace LNG imports**.
### 9. DEMAND-SIDE MEASURES AND PARTNERSHIPS
#### 9.1 EVs and Clean Energy
- **PM E-DRIVE**: from **1 Oct 2024**, outlay **₹10,900 crore**, **replaced FAME-II**; extended to **March 2028**.
- NITI's **India Electric Mobility Index** (4 Aug 2025) and **e-Amrit** portal.
- **500 GW non-fossil by 2030** and **Green Hydrogen Mission** = import-substitution tools; the 41 MT fuel system is a disruption response.
- Debate: renewables not yet cutting fossil imports; RE takes years to become firm power while shocks hit at once.
#### 9.2 Energy Partnerships
- **IEA**: India an **association country** since **March 2017**; sought **full membership** (Oct 2023).
- **Global Biofuels Alliance**: launched **9 Sep 2023** at the **G20 New Delhi** summit; core founders **India, USA, Brazil**; secretariat in **New Delhi**.
### 10. CRITICAL MINERALS SECURITY
- **National Critical Mineral Mission**: Cabinet approval **29 Jan 2025**; **₹34,300 crore** over seven years (₹16,300 crore government + ₹18,000 crore PSUs), to 2030-31.
- **KABIL** (2019): JV of **NALCO, HCL and MECL** under the Ministry of Mines.
- **Argentina**: KABIL's five **lithium** blocks in **Catamarca** (agreement **15 Jan 2024**, ~₹200 crore).
