### 10. KHELO INDIA, FUNDING AND GOVERNANCE
#### 10.1 Restructured Khelo India Scheme 2026–31
- **Union Cabinet** approved for **2026–27 to 2030–31** – outlay **₹36,441 crore** = **₹29,054 crore** + **₹7,387 crore** for **Assistance to NSFs**; State share **₹565 crore**; **60:40** for general States.
- New component **"Emerging Khelo India Athletes"** – ~**10-fold** expansion of athlete development.
- **National Coach Accreditation Board** to standardise coaching.
- History: Khelo India began as a **Central Sector Scheme (2016-17)**; the Cabinet approved the **revamped** Khelo India programme on **20 September 2017** (₹1,756 crore for 2017-18 to 2019-20), **merging Rajiv Gandhi Khel Abhiyan, Urban Sports Infrastructure Scheme, National Sports Talent Search Scheme**; the **Khelo India Games** began in **2018**; Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports.

#### 10.3 NCAB, Funding and Budget
- **National Coach Accreditation Board**: announced by **Dr Mansukh Mandaviya** at the **63rd SAI Governing Body** meeting; based on a task force led by **Pullela Gopichand**; apex body for coaching standards; **two-stage talent scouting** and **National Talent Repository**; proposed members – **IOA President, Sports Secretary, an eminent personality, a pedagogy expert**.

- **TOPS**: **September 2014**, revamped **April 2018**; April 2026 – **51 core, 52 para core, 130 development** athletes.
- **KIRTI**: talent ID for ages **9–18**; goal – **top 10 by 2036, top 5 by 2047**.
- Upcoming: Asian Games 2026 **Aichi–Nagoya**; Olympics 2028 **Los Angeles**; CWG 2030 **Ahmedabad**; Olympics 2032 **Brisbane**; India preparing a **2036 bid** (Mar 2026).
