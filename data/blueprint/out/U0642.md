## U0642 | ANDHRA PRADESH – ROCKS AND MINERALS: BARYTES, LIMESTONE, MICA, COAL, OIL AND GAS (KG BASIN)
### 1. AP – "RATNA GARBHA"
- AP is called **"Ratna Garbha"** – the **treasure house of minerals**.
- Leading producer of **barytes, limestone, mica, granite**.
#### 1.1 AP's Share and Rank in India
| Mineral | AP's share / rank |
|---|---|
| **Barytes** | **India's top producer**; **nearly one-quarter of world output** (APPSC 2024 key) |
| **Mica** | **first** in India – **reserves 40.5% (~41%)**, **production 70.2%**; Rajasthan 2nd |
| **Limestone** | among top producers (**Rajasthan > MP > AP**, ~50% together); largest **reserves = Karnataka (25–28%)**, IMYB 2022 |
| **Monazite** | **AP first** in production |
| **Diamonds** | **18.23 lakh carats** of India's **319.22 lakh** (older inventory) |
| **Heavy-mineral beach sand** | **~327 Mt of India's 1,078 Mt (~30.4%)** (older inventory) |
| **Asbestos** | found (Kadapa, Pulivendula), but AP is **not** India's leading producer; known for heat resistance |
- Mica reserves of India: **AP 41%, Rajasthan 28%, Odisha 17%, Bihar 2%**; Jharkhand belt = Koderma, Hazaribagh; Rajasthan = Jaipur–Udaipur.
- World mica (2022): **China first, Madagascar second**; India led mica-sheet output in 2001.
!TRAP Largest limestone **reserves** = **Karnataka**, not AP.

### 4. BARYTES, MICA AND MANGANESE
- **Barytes**: Mangampet (Kadapa) – one of the world's largest; AP gives most of India's barytes. Policy: beneficiation plants for low-grade iron ore and **CDW-grade barytes**.
- **Mica**: Nellore famous for **mica blocks for electronics** (APPSC 2025 key); "electrical mica"; mica declared a **minor mineral in 2015**.
- **Manganese**: **Visakhapatnam district** = **world's No. 1 manganese producer until 1929**; today Vizianagaram and Srikakulam; planned **manganese processing clusters in Vizianagaram**.
### 5. GOLD – SWARNAGIRI (JONNAGIRI)
- **India's first (and largest) private-sector gold mine after Independence** (APPSC-G2 2025 key).
- **Tuggali mandal, Kurnool**; renamed Swarnagiri from Jonnagiri (G.O. Ms. No. 181).
- **GeoMysore Services**; **30-year lease**; **597.820 ha**; **open-pit**; ~**1,000 kg gold a year**; bars "Made in Jonnagiri"; opened **Jun–Jul 2026**.
- **Ramagiri (Anantapur)** = old gold field; gold-bearing quartz in Anantapur, Chittoor, Kurnool.
- Next auctions: Boksampalli, Javakula, Chigurugunta, Ramagiri; six **Javakula** blocks under G2 exploration after two failed auctions.
- India's **oldest gold field = Kolar, Karnataka**.
### 6. URANIUM, BEACH SANDS AND RARE EARTHS
- **Tummalapalle (YSR Kadapa)**, Cuddapah basin: principal AP uranium deposit; **low-grade ore**; mine and plant working since **2012** (3,000 t ore a day); 2011 Lok Sabha estimate **63,269 t U3O8**.
- Other uranium mines: **Jaduguda, Narwapahar (Jharkhand)**. **Radium** is extracted from **pitchblende**.
- **Beach sands**: ilmenite, rutile, zircon, garnet, sillimanite, **monazite → thorium**; from **Srikakulam coast to Krishna coast** (not only Nellore); **Bhavanapadu (Srikakulam)**; monazite in **Bheemunipatnam** rocks.
- **Kurnool is not** a beach-sand district.
- **NGRI** found **rare earth elements in syenite rocks of Anantapur** (2023).
- Critical minerals focus: titanium, lithium, gallium; CM ordered statewide mineral mapping with value addition via **APMDC** (2026).

### 8. OIL AND GAS – KRISHNA-GODAVARI BASIN
- AP's gas basin = **Krishna-Godavari (KG) basin** (APPSC 2025 key); **Barmer–Sanchore** is in Rajasthan, not KG.
| Field / unit | Operator / fact |
|---|---|
| **KG-D6** (deepwater) | **Reliance Industries** discovery (with BP); large gas reserves |
| **Ravva** | shallow offshore KG, block PKGM-1; discovered by **ONGC (1987)**; **Cairn** operator since 1996 |
| **Deen Dayal West** (KG-OSN-2001/3) | **GSPC** block; **ONGC** bought 80% and operatorship (**2017**) |
| Onshore KG fields | **ONGC** |
| **Tatipaka** | **ONGC mini-refinery, Konaseema** |
| **HPCL Visakhapatnam** | AP's **main refinery** |
- Petroleum elsewhere: Digboi/Amguri (Assam).
- **Underground coal gasification**: Reliance near **Recherla and Chintalapudi, Eluru district** – gas, hydrogen, ammonia, urea; over Rs 2 lakh crore (Aug 2026).
- National: gas share **~6.7% → 15% by 2030**; **One Nation One Gas Grid**; **JHBDPL = Pradhan Mantri Urja Ganga**; pipelines over 32,000 km.
!KEY Tatipaka = ONGC refinery; KG-D6 = Reliance.
### 9. WATER, MARINE AND FOREST RESOURCES
- **Water**: major basins **Godavari, Krishna, Pennar**; north – **Vamsadhara and Nagavali**.
- **Marine**: coastline **1,053.07 km** (Dec 2024 remeasurement; **third** after Gujarat and Tamil Nadu); older figure **974 km**, then second after Gujarat. Plans: **port-based mineral beneficiation** plants.
- **Forest**: cover **30,084.96 sq km = 23.59%** of AP; **red sanders** endemic to **Seshachalam–Palakonda–Lankamalla** hills.
- **GCC** (Girijan Cooperative Corporation), HQ **Visakhapatnam**: buys minor forest produce (**tamarind, honey, gum karaya, adda leaves**) from tribals at fair prices.
- Debate: **bauxite mining in Araku** vs Eastern Ghats ecology, PVTG/tribal rights, FCA, FRA.
