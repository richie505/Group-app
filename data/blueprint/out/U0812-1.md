#### 3.3 Calendar Reasoning (asked with World Habitat Day)
- If the **third Monday is the 17th**, the first Monday is the 3rd, so the **1st is a Saturday**; in a 31-day month **Saturday, Sunday and Monday** occur five times (Thursday only four times).
- **October 2026** begins on a **Thursday**: so **Thursday, Friday and Saturday** occur five times; first Monday = **5 Oct**, third Monday = **19 Oct**.

### 6. HEALTH SECTOR INITIATIVES
#### 6.1 AP Human Organ Transplantation Amendment Act 2026
- Approved by the **Governor** – **AP Act No. 25 of 2026**; based on **Supreme Court directions** and **Jeevandan** board proposals.
- Covers **tissues** (skin, cornea, heart valves, bone marrow).
- **Grandparents and grandchildren** added as **near relatives**.
- **Swap transplantation** recognised.
- Parent law **THOTA** (Transplantation of Human Organs and Tissues Act): enacted **1994**, amended **2011**.
- **Jeevandan** = AP's **deceased organ donation** programme.
#### 6.2 Trauma Registry for Golden Hour Care
- [21 Oct 2026] AP launched a **Trauma Registry** to ensure timely treatment of accident victims in the **first hour (Golden Hour)**.
- **Pre-Hospital Notification (PHN)** system: **108 and 104** ambulance staff alert hospitals in advance.
- In line with **Supreme Court** directions and central guidelines.
- **Good Samaritan** protection: **Section 134A** of the **Motor Vehicles Act, 1988**.
#### 6.3 Food Safety – Ban on Artificial Paneer
- [29 Oct 2026] Gazette notification bans **manufacture, storage, transport and sale** of **artificial paneer** statewide for **one year**.
- Inspections found fake paneer made from **vegetable fats and oils**.
- **FSSAI**: set up under the **Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006**; HQ **New Delhi**; under the **Union Health Ministry**.

- **Pulicat** = **brackish-water lake** shared by **AP and Tamil Nadu**.
