## U0004 | PRE-HISTORIC CULTURES OF ANDHRA – PALAEOLITHIC, MESOLITHIC, NEOLITHIC AND MEGALITHIC SITES
### 1. STAGES IN ANDHRA (OLDEST FIRST)
1. **Lower Palaeolithic** – handaxe, cleaver, chopper.
2. **Middle Palaeolithic** – flakes, scrapers, borers, points.
3. **Upper Palaeolithic** – blades, bone tools (Kurnool caves, Jwalapuram).
4. **Mesolithic = Microlithic** – microliths.
5. **Neolithic–Chalcolithic** – ash mounds; copper tools, painted pottery.
6. **Megalithic = Iron Age** – black-and-red ware; grave goods.
- Discoverer of AP prehistory: **Robert Bruce Foote**; he also found tools at **Pallavaram** near Chennai.
### 2. TOOLS BY STAGE AND RAW MATERIAL
| Stage | Tools | Raw material |
|---|---|---|
| Lower Palaeolithic | **Handaxe, cleaver, chopper** | **Quartzite** (Kadapa–Kurnool); **limestone** (north of the Krishna) |
| Middle Palaeolithic | **Flakes**, scrapers, borers, points | Chert, jasper, chalcedony, quartz |
| Upper Palaeolithic | **Blades, bone tools** | Silica-rich stone, bone |
| Mesolithic | **Microliths** (arrowheads, blades, composite tools) | Chert, jasper; weapons of **dolomite** |
| Chalcolithic | Copper tools | Copper |
- Microliths were made by **flaking and chipping**.
!TRAP Middle Palaeolithic is the **flake** stage; microliths are Mesolithic. Do not call the Middle Palaeolithic the "small stone-tool stage".

#### 3.1 Sites and Districts
| Site | District / setting | Find |

### 4. MESOLITHIC / MICROLITHIC CENTRES
- **Giddaluru (Prakasam)** = key **microlithic implement centre** of AP (G2 2019, GS 2018).
- Others: **Nagarjunakonda** (Krishna), **Kondapur**, **Khammam**.
- Andhra microliths resemble **South Indian and Caspian** microliths.

### 6. ROCK PAINTINGS AND CAVE ART
- **Ketavaram** (Kurnool): rock paintings; **Borra Caves** (Visakhapatnam).
- **Kurnool caves**: prehistoric cave sites with bone tools.
- Most often painted animal: **deer**. **Petroglyphs** are carved, not painted.
### 7. MEGALITHIC PHASE
#### 7.1 Culture
- **Iron Age = Megalithic culture**; typical pottery **black-and-red ware**.
- **Grave goods**: pottery, beads, **iron implements**, gold ornaments (Brahmagiri) – **not glass mirrors**.
- Stone-circle graves are called "**Rakshasa Gullu**" (also Pandava Gullu).
- **Habitation sites**: **Nagarjunakonda** (centre), Jonnawada, Boodigapalli, Gudivada, Peddavadu.
- Jun 2026: **Mayiladumparai** AMS dating puts the South Indian Iron Age at c. **2100 BCE**, earlier than the Gangetic Iron Age.
#### 7.2 Burial Types and District Concentration
| Type | Form | District concentration |
|---|---|---|
| **Cist** | Stone-slab box | Common in AP; "many cist burials in Visakhapatnam" is keyed **incorrect** (G2 2025) |
| **Dolmen** | Table-like chamber | **Krishna, Chittoor**, East Godavari |
| **Sarcophagus** | Terracotta coffin | **Chittoor and Anantapuramu** |
| **Menhir** | Upright stone | **Krishna** |
| **Anthropomorphic** | Human-shaped stone | **Tirupati** – largest collection in India |

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- First discoverer of AP prehistory: Robert Bruce Foote.
- Largest anthropomorphic collection: Tirupati.
- Only Indian Upper Palaeolithic bone-tool sites: Kurnool caves, Muchatla Chintamani Gavi.
