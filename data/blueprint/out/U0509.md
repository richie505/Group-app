## U0509 | INDUSTRIAL LICENSING, MRTP TO COMPETITION ACT; DISINVESTMENT AND PSE POLICY
### 1. REGULATORY ACTS – CHRONOLOGY
| Act | Year | Replaced by |
|---|---|---|
| **IDRA** | 1951 | Licensing; Development Councils |
| **MRTP** | 1969 (in force 1970) | **Competition Act, 2002** |
| **FERA** | 1973 | **FEMA, 1999** |
| **SICA** (BIFR) | 1985 | **IBC, 2016** |
- Order: **IDRA 1951 → MRTP 1969 → FERA 1973 → SICA 1985 → FEMA 1999 → Competition Act 2002 → IBC 2016**.
### 2. INDUSTRIAL LICENSING
- **IDRA 1951:** licence for new or expanded units; amended 1953.
- **Hazari Report (1967)** and **Dutt Committee (1969)** showed licences favoured **big business houses** → MRTP.
- Compulsory licensing: **18 (1991) → 5 now** – **alcohol, cigarettes, hazardous chemicals, electronic aerospace and defence equipment, industrial explosives**. Leather delicensed 1993.
- After 1991, **APIIC** turned facilitator.
### 3. MRTP ACT, 1969
- Purpose: control **private monopoly** and **concentration of economic power** (APPSC 2019); recommended by the **Dutt Committee**.
| Year | MRTP threshold |
|---|---|
| Original | Assets ₹20 crore+ or 33% market share |
| 1984-85 | Share 25%; assets ₹100 crore |
| **NIP 1991** | **Asset limit scrapped** – no prior approval for expansion, mergers, takeovers |
- Replaced by **Competition Act, 2002** (APPSC 2018); MRTP Commission → **CCI**; focus from curbing monopoly to **fair competition**.
### 4. COMPETITION ACT, 2002 AND CCI
- Recommended by **S.V.S. Raghavan Committee (2000)**; commenced **31 Mar 2003**.
- **CCI** set up **14 Oct 2003**; Chairperson + **2-6 members**; **Ministry of Corporate Affairs**; aim "level playing field". Chairpersons: Ashok Chawla; **Ravneet Kaur** (May 2023).
| Section | Covers |
|---|---|
| **3** | Anti-competitive agreements – cartels, bid-rigging, price-fixing |
| **4** | Abuse of dominance – unfair pricing, denial of market access |
| **5-6** | Combinations – M&A above thresholds; **deemed approved** if CCI misses deadline |
- Cases: **Google ₹1,337 crore (2022)**; **Meta/WhatsApp ₹213.14 crore (2024)** – NCLAT (2025) upheld fine, set aside data-sharing ban.
- **NCLAT** hears CCI appeals (from 26 May 2017).
### 5. KEY TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Disinvestment** | Reducing government equity (APPSC 2019); control may stay |
| **Privatisation** | Ownership + management control to private sector |
| **Strategic sale** | Block of shares (usually **50%+**) with **management control** |
| **Liberalisation** | Removing licences, quotas, controls (no sale) |
| **Monetisation** | **Lease** of brownfield assets; ownership stays |
| Minority / majority disinvestment | Govt keeps >51% and control / control transferred |
| **Complete privatisation** | 100% sold – **most comprehensive mode** |
#### 5.1 Asset Monetisation
- Objective (G1 Prelims 2024): owners use **new financial structures to unlock value** of existing assets.
- **NMP 1.0:** Aug 2021, ₹6 lakh crore, FY22-25.
- **NMP 2.0:** launched **23 Feb 2026**, **₹16.72 lakh crore**, FY26-30, 12 sectors.
- **NLMC** (3 June 2022, under DPE): monetises surplus CPSE land, incl. loss-making units.
- AP PPP ports: **Krishnapatnam, Gangavaram**.
!PAIR Monetisation | lease | ownership stays || Disinvestment | equity sale | permanent

### 9. PSE RESTRUCTURING AND NEW PSE POLICY 2021
- **NRF (1992):** VRS, safety net for workers.
- **BIFR** dissolved **1 Dec 2016** → NCLT/NCLAT; **IBC** enacted **28 May 2016** (T.K. Viswanathan BLRC 2015); IBBI regulator.
- **NCLT:** s.408, Companies Act 2013, set up 1 June 2016; **NCLAT:** s.410.
- **New PSE Policy 2021:** **4 strategic sectors** (incl. banking, insurance, financial services), **1-4 CPSEs** each; non-strategic → privatise, merge or close.
- ES 2025-26: stake in listed CPSEs may fall to **26%**.
- **Criticism:** **"selling the family silver"**, **undervaluation**, lost dividends, security risks.
