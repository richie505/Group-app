## U0546 | PUBLIC EXPENDITURE – REVENUE AND CAPITAL, DEVELOPMENTAL AND NON-DEVELOPMENTAL, TRENDS; SUBSIDIES
### 1. PLAN vs NON-PLAN EXPENDITURE
- Plan/non-plan split **ended with Budget 2017-18** (end of 12th Plan); announced in Budget Speech 2016-17; replaced by **revenue vs capital**.
- **C. Rangarajan Committee (2011)**: split was "dysfunctional", an obstacle to **outcome-based budgeting**.
- Non-plan items: defence, interest, loans to States/UTs/PSUs.
### 2. DEVELOPMENTAL vs NON-DEVELOPMENTAL
| Developmental | Non-developmental |
|---|---|
| **Education**, sports, art, culture | **Defence**, border roads |
| **Medical and public health** | **Interest payments** |
| **Irrigation** and flood control | **Pensions** |
| Rural development, power, railways | Administrative and fiscal services |
### 3. REVENUE vs CAPITAL EXPENDITURE
| Revenue (no asset) | Capital (asset / liability change) |
|---|---|
| **Salaries, pensions, interest** | Roads, airports, irrigation, buildings |
| **Subsidies** (food, fertiliser) | **Defence equipment** (capital outlay) |
| **Grants to States** (even if assets made) | **Loans and advances** to States, PSUs |
| **Maintenance** of existing assets | **Repayment of loans** |
- Test: changes govt's **assets or liabilities** = capital; rest = revenue.
- Capital expenditure buys, creates, upgrades or **extends life** of assets.
- **Revenue deficit** = revenue expenditure − revenue receipts.
- **Golden Rule**: borrow only for asset creation.
!TRAP Grants to States = revenue; loans to States = capital. Food and fertiliser subsidies = revenue (APPSC G2 2008).
### 4. LAWS OF EXPENDITURE GROWTH
| Law | Statement |
|---|---|
| **Wagner's law** (Adolph Wagner) | Spending rises **faster than national income** as economy industrialises = **law of increasing state activity** |
| **Peacock-Wiseman** (1961, UK data) | Rises **in steps**; wars/crises raise tolerable tax = **displacement effect**; no fall-back |
### 5. UNION EXPENDITURE TRENDS (BE 2026-27)
- **Centre + States** spending ≈ **28% of GDP** (ES 2016-17; APPSC G2 2017).
- Total **₹53,47,315 crore = 13.6% of GDP** (+7.7%); revenue exp. ₹41.25 lakh crore (10.5%).
- Revenue share in total: **88% (2014-15) → ~77%**.
- **Largest item of Union revenue expenditure = interest**: **₹14,03,972 crore** = 26% of total, **40% of revenue receipts** (37% in 2013-14; 42% in 2020-21).
- **Committed expenditure** (salaries, pensions, interest) = **65.3%** of revenue receipts.

#### 5.2 States
- States 2014-24: spending **16.15-17.49% of GSDP**; revenue exp. **80-87%** of total; committed + subsidies 51.76% of revenue exp. (FY24).
- 9 of 18 large States budget revenue deficits for 2026-27.
### 6. CAPITAL EXPENDITURE PUSH
- Capex 2026-27: **₹12,21,821 crore** (+11.5%, 3.1% of GDP) = capital outlay **₹9,43,042 crore** + loans **₹2,78,780 crore**.
- **Effective capex** = capex + **grants-in-aid for capital assets**: **₹17.15 lakh crore (4.4% of GDP)**; grants ₹4.93 lakh crore; 101.1% of fiscal deficit.
- **SASCI**: **50-year interest-free** loans to States for capex; **₹1.85 lakh crore** (2026-27).
### 7. SUBSIDIES
- 2026-27: **₹4,54,773 crore**; **food ₹2,27,629 crore + fertiliser ₹1,70,799 crore = 87%**.
- Major subsidies (food, fertiliser, petroleum) **₹4.11 lakh crore = 1.0% of GDP**; Q1 2026-27 up 37.4% (crude, West Asia); overshoot ≈ ₹50,000 crore.
#### 7.1 Explicit vs Hidden
| Explicit | Hidden (implicit) |
|---|---|
| Budget line: food, fertiliser, LPG, export incentives, interest subvention | Under-pricing of public services: **higher education in public universities**, health, irrigation water |
#### 7.2 Mechanisms
- **NBS**: from **1 April 2010**; fixed subsidy per kg of **N, P, K, S**; **22 grades** of decontrolled P&K (DAP, MOP, SSP). **Urea outside NBS**.
- **Oil Pool Account** (APM) closed **1 April 2002** (FM Yashwant Sinha); under-recoveries later paid through **oil bonds**.
- **16th FC**: well-targeted subsidies, **exclusion criteria, sunset clauses**, no off-budget financing.

### 9. AP ANGLE
- Order: **Revenue expenditure > Capital outlay > Loans and advances**; revenue ≈ **83%** of 2026-27 BE.
- 2025-26 RE: revenue ₹2,38,022 crore; capital outlay ₹33,135 crore (2024-25: ₹16,141 crore).
- 2026-27 BE: capital outlay **₹48,698 crore (+47%)**; revenue ₹2,56,143 crore.
- Revenue items: rice and power subsidy, welfare pensions, housing.
