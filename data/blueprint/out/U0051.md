## U0051 | IKSHVAKUS – POLITICAL HISTORY, VASISHTHIPUTRA CHAMTAMULA AND VIRAPURUSHADATTA
### 1. THE IKSHVAKUS – AN OVERVIEW
- **Ikshvakus of Vijayapuri (Nagarjunakonda)** = **successors of the Satavahanas** in the **3rd century CE**.
- They were first **feudatories (lords) under the Satavahanas** and became free after the Satavahana decline.
- Duration: about **75 years** (c. **AD 225–300**).
- Number of kings: **Puranas list 7**; **inscriptions confirm 4**.
- Symbol: **Lion**; royal language: **Prakrit**.
- A **bridge dynasty**: took the **Amaravati school to its peak**, built the **first temples**, and moved Andhra towards a **farming (agrarian), Puranic order**.
- They did **not** replace Buddhism with Jainism.
### 2. POSITION IN THE SEQUENCE OF ANDHRA DYNASTIES
| Order | Dynasty | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Satavahanas** | Ikshvakus were their feudatories |
| 2 | **Ikshvakus** | Vijayapuri, 3rd c. CE |
| 3 | **Brihatpalayanas** | Capital **Pithunda** near Machilipatnam; **Kondamudi** plates of **Jayavarma** (near Tenali) |
| 4 | **Shalankayanas** | Vengi |
| 5 | **Vishnukundins** | – |
| 6 | **Pallavas** | Took over the Krishna–Guntur region |
| 7 | **Eastern Chalukyas** | Vengi |
!TRAP Immediate successors of the Satavahanas in the Krishna valley = Ikshvakus, not the Pallavas or Vishnukundins.
### 3. ORIGIN DEBATE
| Theory | Proponent |
|---|---|
| **Telugu/Andhra people** | **Gopalachari** (G2 2011 key) |
| **North Indian** | **Rapson, Buhler** |
| **Kannada** | **Vogel** |
| **Kshatriya** (from Kshatriya yajnas) or mixed Kshatriya-Brahmin | **D.R. Bhandarkar** |
| Name from **ikshu** (sugarcane) | **B.S.L. Hanumantha Rao** |
- **Andhra origin proof**: southern names + the southern custom of marrying the **maternal uncle's daughter** (cross-cousin marriage).
- Tradition links the name to the **Solar/Rama (Ayodhya)** line and later Shakya descent, but this is **not claimed in their own inscriptions**.
!TRAP G2 2025 key: "Their inscriptions call them descendants of the Ikshvakus of Ayodhya" = **incorrect** statement.
### 4. CAPITAL, SITES AND PORTS
| Place | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Vijayapuri** | Capital = modern **Nagarjunakonda**, in the **Nagarjuna Sagar valley**, now **Palnadu district**; **not at Amaravati** |
| **Nagarjunakonda** | Excavated by **R. Subrahmanyam**; **amphitheatre** (possibly built with Roman help), **Ashvamedha platform**, many **Roman coins** |
| **Motupalli, Ghantasala** | **Ports** |
- Nagarjunakonda (Palnadu) is the main **Ikshvaku heritage site** of AP.
- **Phanigiri** (Suryapet, Telangana): pot of **3,730 lead coins** of the Ikshvaku period found in **2024**.
### 5. RULERS – PERIOD AND CONTRIBUTION
| Ruler | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Vasishthiputra (Sri) Chamtamula / Santamula / Shrisantmula** | **Founder** at Vijayapuri; first a Satavahana feudatory; Vedic yajnas; made the **intelligentsia (scholars) partners in agricultural development** (G2 2012) by gifts of **land, ploughs, bullocks and cows** to Brahmins |
| **Mathariputra Virapurushadatta** | **Golden age of Buddhism**; queens great donors; **Alluru** and **Nagarjunakonda** inscriptions; title **Sri Parvatadhipati** |
| **Ehuvala Chamtamula** | Son of Virapurushadatta and **Bhatti Mahadevi**; ruled about **24 years**; **first Sanskrit inscriptions** in the Telugu lands; **Gummadidurru** inscription |
| **Rudrapurushadatta** | **Last ruler**; issued the **Gurajala (Gurazala)** inscription |
#### 5.1 Vasishthiputra Chamtamula – Vedic Sacrifices
- **Ashvamedha, Vajapeya, Agnishtoma, Agnihotra** and **Rajasuya**.
- Evidence: the **Ashvamedha platform** found at **Nagarjunakonda**.
- Titles **Gosatasahasra** and **Halasatasahasra** = giver of lakhs of cows and ploughs.
#### 5.2 Virapurushadatta – Buddhism
- The Ikshvaku period = **golden age of Buddhism in Andhra**; Nagarjunakonda (Vijayapuri) a great Buddhist centre.
- The king was Vedic, but **his queens** were important Buddhist donors.
#### 5.3 Ehuvala Chamtamula and Rudrapurushadatta
- Ehuvala was named after his **grandfather** (Chamtamula), a common Ikshvaku naming custom.
- Under him **Sanskrit** began to replace Prakrit in records, and Brahmanical temples rose beside Buddhist monuments.
- **Rudrapurushadatta**, the last king, is known from the **Gurajala** record; after him the Krishna valley passed to new powers.
!KEY Founder performs Vedic sacrifices; his son's age is the Buddhist golden age; grandson writes the first Sanskrit inscriptions.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sri Parvatiyas** (Sri Parvatiya Andhras) | Puranic name of the dynasty |
| **Andhrabhrityas** | Puranic name of the dynasty in general – not a title of Ehuvala Chamtamula |
| **Gosatasahasra, Halasatasahasra** | Titles of Chamtamula |
| **Sri Parvatadhipati** | Title of **Virapurushadatta** |
!TRAP An APPSC Group-2 key accepted "Puranas call them **Andhra Bhrityas**" and marked the "Sri Parvathiyas" statement incorrect; the UPPCS 2020 key accepted "**Shriparvatiya** in the Puranas". Follow the exam's key.
!PAIR Name or title: Sri Parvatiyas | Puranic name | Whole dynasty || Sri Parvatadhipati | Title | Virapurushadatta only
### 7. MATRIMONIAL ALLIANCES
- **Virapurushadatta** married **Rudrabhattarika (Rudradhara Bhattarika)**, princess of **Ujjain**, daughter of the **Saka** ruler → tie with the **Sakas / Western Kshatrapas** (G2 2025).
- **Cross-cousin marriage** (maternal uncle's daughter) was practised.
### 8. INSCRIPTIONS ON SUCCESSION AND REPLACEMENT
| Inscription | Issuer | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Alluru** | Virapurushadatta | Donations to many communities and institutions |
| **Nagarjunakonda** | Virapurushadatta and queens | Buddhist gifts; ayaka-pillar records |
| **Jaggayyapeta** | Virapurushadatta | Ikshvaku rule in the lower Krishna |
| **Gummadidurru** | Ehuvala Chamtamula | Later Ikshvaku rule |
| **Gurajala** | Rudrapurushadatta | Last Ikshvaku king |
| **Manchikallu** | Pallava **Simhavarman** | Earliest Pallava record; found near **Vijayapuri** → Pallava conquest that ended the Ikshvakus (end of 3rd c. CE) |
| **Mayidavolu plates** | **Sivaskandavarman** (as crown prince, from Kanchi) | Grant concerning **Dhanyakataka** → Pallava control of the Krishna–Guntur region |
#### 8.1 Who Ended the Ikshvakus?
- **D.C. Sircar**: the Manchikallu findspot near Vijayapuri shows **Simhavarman** helped bring down the Ikshvakus around the end of the 3rd or start of the 4th century CE.
- **T.V. Mahalingam**: Simhavarman had no royal title in that record; **Sivaskandavarman I** completed the takeover.
- An **Abhira** inscription at the Ikshvaku capital is read by some as an Abhira occupation of the kingdom.
- By the **mid-4th century** the **Pallavas** held the former Ikshvaku territory.
- Ikshvaku rule ended after clashes with the **Abhiras, Traikutakas and Brihatpalayanas**, with the **Pallavas** finally taking the region.
### 9. IKSHVAKU FIRSTS AND LASTS
- The Ikshvakus stand between the **Satavahana trade economy** and the **early medieval temple-and-agrahara order** – this is why examiners call them a **bridge dynasty**.
- **Earliest Hindu temples** in Andhra: **Pushpabhadraswami**, **Ashtabhuja Narayana** at Nagarjunakonda.
- **First dated inscriptions naming sculptors**.
- **First Sanskrit inscription** in the Telugu lands: **Ehuvala Chamtamula**.
- Last ruler: **Rudrapurushadatta**.
