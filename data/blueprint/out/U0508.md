## U0508 | INDUSTRIAL SICKNESS – MEANING, CAUSES, CONSEQUENCES AND REMEDIES
### 1. WHO IS A SICK UNIT?
| Test | Sick if |
|---|---|
| **SICA 1985** (original) | Registered **≥7 years**; accumulated losses **≥ net worth**; cash losses |
| **1993 amendment** | Age cut to **5 years**; cash-loss test dropped |
| **RBI** (micro and small) | Any borrowal account **NPA > 3 months**, or **50% net-worth erosion** |
| RBI 2016 framework | Early stress: **SMA-0/1/2** |
### 2. CAUSES
| Internal | External |
|---|---|
| Poor planning, diversion of funds, faulty appraisal | **Competition**, recession (demand) |
| Poor **working-capital** management, excess debt | Delayed, costly credit |
| **Obsolete technology**, low productivity | Power shortage, transport |
| – | Sudden **EXIM**, tax, environment policy changes |
- MSMEs: **delayed payments** by large buyers and government = major trigger.
### 3. CONSEQUENCES AND SECTORS
- **NPAs, locked capital, unemployment, revenue loss to the exchequer** (NOT higher revenue).
- State took over sick private **textile and engineering** units mainly **to protect jobs** → lowered PSU returns.
- Most sickness: **textiles (cotton mills), jute, sugar, engineering**.
### 4. INSTITUTIONS – IRCI TO BIFR
- **1971:** Industrial Reconstruction Corporation of India (**IRCI**) → **IRBI** (1985) – first rehabilitation institution.
- **1985:** **SICA**, after the **Tiwari Committee** (1984-85).
- **January 1987:** **BIFR** set up; appeals to **AAIFR**; under Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance.
- BIFR functions: **early detection, revival of viable units, closure of unviable units**; covered **public and private** companies.
!KEY "BIFR deals with?" → revival of sick industries (not stock markets or SSI promotion).
### 5. BIFR FAILURE AND THE IBC
- Failed due to **procedural delays, legal loopholes** (a SICA reference froze recovery), weak revival – NOT "rapid resolution".
- BIFR and AAIFR dissolved **1 December 2016**.
| Old regime | Successor |
|---|---|
| SICA 1985 | **IBC 2016** |
| BIFR | **NCLT** (adjudicating authority) |
| AAIFR | **NCLAT** (appellate) |
| Debtor-in-possession | Creditor-in-control; regulator **IBBI** |
- Shift strengthened **creditors**; SICA had protected defaulting promoters.
### 6. NATIONAL RENEWAL FUND AND OTHER MEASURES
- **NRF (1992):** social safety net – funded **VRS, retraining, redeployment** of displaced workers; aim = **safeguard workers**; wound up **2000**.
- **"Golden Handshake"** = **Voluntary Retirement Scheme**, used by public and private firms.
- **Liberalised Margin Money Scheme (June 1987):** reduced SSI sickness.
- **RBI:** 2016 MSME revival framework; **one-time restructuring** of MSME loans without downgrade (**2019**, extended 2020-21).
- Today: companies → IBC (NCLT); delayed payments → **MSME Samadhaan**; MSME Bill (Rajya Sabha, Aug 2026) – CPSEs settle invoices on **TReDS**.
- **Udyog Bandhu** (now Invest UP) – UP body helping industrial units.
