## U0120 | FOUNDATION OF VIJAYANAGARA – HARIHARA AND BUKKA, VIDYARANYA; THE SANGAMA DYNASTY
### 1. FOUNDING OF VIJAYANAGARA
- Founded **1336** by brothers **Harihara I** and **Bukka I**, sons of **Sangama** → **Sangama dynasty** (family claimed **Yadava** (lunar) descent; also placed in the Kuruba clan).
- **Vijayanagara = "City of Victory"**, on the **southern bank of the Tungabhadra** (tributary of the Krishna); ruins at **Hampi**.
- **Coronation**: Harihara I crowned **18 April 1336** at the **Virupaksha** temple, after **Vidyaranya** of Sringeri reconverted the brothers to Hinduism.
- **Founding legend**: a **hare turned on the royal hunting dogs** → site chosen as a place of valour.
- Harihara I ruled as agent of the deity (Virupaksha) owning all land south of the Krishna.
- **Emblem: Varaha (boar)**; Sangamas **Shaiva**, the later three dynasties **Vaishnava**.
- Largest Hindu kingdom of medieval South India.
#### 1.1 Founding FIRSTs
| FIRST | Fact |
|---|---|
| First seat | **Anegondi** – set up "in the name of **Sri Virupaksha**" |
| First record calling Harihara I king | **Nellore inscription** – dates his reign 1336–1355 |
| City completed | **1343** |
| First foreign traveller | **Ibn Battuta** (Harihara I's reign) |
- **Harihara I shifted the capital** from **Anegondi → Vijayanagara**.
!NUM 1336 = founding + coronation (18 April); 1343 = city completed; 1346 = Sringeri visit after Hoysala victory
### 2. ORIGIN THEORIES OF HARIHARA AND BUKKA
- **Robert Sewell** recorded **seven** local founding traditions.
| Theory | What it says | Backed by |
|---|---|---|
| **Kakatiya service** | Sangama a **Kakatiya chief**; brothers served **Prataparudra** of Warangal (treasurers); after **1323** taken to Delhi and converted | **APPSC G2 2005 key** |
| **Kampili** | Governed **Kampili**; captured by **Muhammad bin Tughlaq**, converted in Delhi, sent back as Sultanate governors | – |
| **Delhi service** | Sent by Tughlaq to crush Deccan rebels (Musunuri Prolaya Nayaka, Prolaya Vema); defeated, they met Vidyaranya | – |
| **Kannada / Hoysala** | Officers of **Veera Ballala III** | **Father Heras, Rice, S.K. Ayyangar, H. Krishna Sastri, P.B. Desai** |
| **Andhra** | Brothers were Andhras | **N. Venkataramanaiah, Sewell, Nilakanta Sastri** |
#### 2.1 The Delhi Legend – Sequence
Fall of Warangal → conversion in Delhi → sent against Deccan rebels → defeat → met **Vidyaranya** near **Pampa** → return to Hinduism → founded Anegondi in the name of Virupaksha.
!KEY APPSC treats the **Kakatiya link** as the correct origin (G2 2005).
!TRAP *Kampili and Vijayanagara* is by **Nelaturi Venkataramanaiah**, not Sewell.

### 4. FOUR DYNASTIES OF VIJAYANAGARA
| Order | Dynasty | Span | Founder | Origin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sangama** | **1336–1485** | **Harihara I and Bukka I** | **Andhra** |
| 2 | **Saluva** | **1485–1505** | **Saluva Narasimha** – first usurpation | Karnataka (Kalyani) |
| 3 | **Tuluva** | **1505–1570** | **Vira (Veera) Narasimha** (APPSC G2 2002 key) | Karnataka (Tuluva) |
| 4 | **Aravidu** | after 1570 | **Tirumala Raya** | **Andhra** (Araviti, Kurnool) |
- **Sangama** = **longest-ruling** dynasty; greatest ruler **Devaraya II**; last ruler **Praudha Raya**.
- Aravidu capitals: **Penukonda, Chandragiri, Vellore**.
- **Tuluva Narasa Nayaka** = father of Vira Narasimha; Vira Narasimha crowned **1505** after executing **Immadi Narasimha**.
- After Talikota: **Hampi → Penukonda → Chandragiri**.
!KEY Order S-S-T-A = Sangama – Saluva – Tuluva – Aravidu; Andhra in origin: only **Sangama and Aravidu**.
!TRAP Odd one out: **Vishnukundins** – an older Andhra dynasty, not Vijayanagara.
#### 4.1 Ruler → Dynasty
| Ruler | Dynasty | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| Bukkaraya I, Devaraya II | Sangama | – |
| Saluva Narasimha | Saluva | First usurper (1485) |
| **Krishnadevaraya** (1509–29) | **Tuluva** | Zenith; wrote ***Amuktamalyada***; learnt the **veena**; Purandara Dasa (1484–1564) contemporary |
| Achyutaraya | Tuluva | Nuniz visited |
| **Sadasivaraya** | Tuluva | Talikota (1565) under regent Rama Raya; **removed the marriage tax** |
### 5. SANGAMA RULERS – HARIHARA I TO DEVARAYA I
| Ruler | Achievements |
|---|---|
| **Harihara I (1336–56)** | Annexed **Hoysala (1346)**, **Kadambas (1347)**; title **Purva-Paschima Samudradhipati** |
| **Bukka I (1356–77)** | Took **Udayagiri (1343)**; completed the city; **Madurai** conquest; reconciled **Jains and Vaishnavas (1368)** (Shravanabelagola inscription; APPSC G2 2003); **embassy to Ming China (1374)**; title **Vedamarga Pratishthapaka** |
| **Harihara II (1377–1404)** | **Longest-reigning** Sangama king; titles **Rajavyasa, Rajavalmiki**; **first to attack Sri Lanka**; took **Goa and Belgaum (1380)**; ruled Kanara, Mysore, Kanchi, Trichinopoly; minister **Irugappa** built **Ganigitti Jain temple**, wrote *Navaratnamala* |
| **Devaraya I (1406–22)** | **Tungabhadra dam**, canals; **first to appoint Muslims** in the army (APPSC key); **Nicolo Conti** visited; **Motupalli Abhaya Sasanam** |
- **Apr 2025**: Devaraya I's **coronation copper plates** (Sanskrit, Kannada, Nagari) unveiled in **Bengaluru** – **Vamana seal**, not the usual Varaha.
!TRAP Harihara II = longest-reigning king; Sangama = longest-ruling dynasty; Devaraya II = greatest.
#### 5.1 Bukka I – Southern Conquest and Embassy
- **Kumara Kampana** (Kamparaya), son of Bukka I, overthrew **Sambhuvaraya** and destroyed the **Madurai Sultanate** (completed c. 1365–66).
- General **Saluva Mangu** killed the Sultan → title **"Parapakshi Saluva"**.
- Literary record: ***Madhura Vijayam*** (Sanskrit) by **Gangadevi**, Kampana's wife.
- Telugu poet **Nachana Somana** (*Uttara Harivamsam*) – patronised by the **first two Sangama kings**; granted Penchikaladinne agrahara.
### 6. DEVARAYA II (1422–46)
- **Army**: hired **10,000 Muslim soldiers** (APPSC G2 2011); gave them **jagirs and a mosque**; kept a **Quran before his throne** so they could salute it; improved **cavalry and archery**.
- **Titles**: **Gajabetekara** (elephant hunter; Gajabetekara coins), **Praudha Devaraya**, Ilanti Raikonda.
- **Deeds**: took **Kondaveedu (1424)**; **Draksharama inscription (1444)** – pushed back the **Gajapatis**, sent Mallappa Odayar to help Rajahmundry.
- **Culture**: wrote ***Mahanataka Sudhanidhi*** (Sanskrit); literary hall **Mutyala Sala**; honoured **Srinatha**.
- **Visitor**: **Abdur Razzaq**, Persian envoy of **Shah Rukh**, **1443** – called Vijayanagara rich and grand; open **diamond bazaars**, about **300 ports**, **taxation of prostitution**.
!PAIR Gajabetekara | Devaraya II | elephant hunter || Purva-Paschima Samudradhipati | Harihara I | lord of eastern and western seas
!TRAP **Mutyala Sala** = Devaraya II; **Bhuvana Vijayam** = Krishnadevaraya. Dakshina Samudradhipati = Lakkanna Dandanayaka.
### 7. SCHOLARS UNDER THE SANGAMAS
| Scholar | Work / role |
|---|---|
| **Sayana** | ***Vedartha Prakasha*** (Vedic commentary); brother of Vidyaranya; **minister of Harihara II** |
| **Madhava-Vidyaranya** | **Advaita** head of **Sringeri**; inspired the founding; *Vidyaranya Kalajnana* |
| **Madhava** (minister) | General who conquered **Goa**; title **"Bhuvanaikavira"** |
| **Gangadevi** | ***Madhura Vijayam***; wife of Kumara Kampana; daughter-in-law of Bukka I; **disciple of Agastya** |
| **Nachana Somana** | *Uttara Harivamsam* |
- **Vedic commentaries** by Sayana and Madhava **begun under Bukka I** (APPSC G2 2008 key), **continued under Harihara II**.
- *Madhura Vijayam* theme: defeat of **Champaraya** of Tondaimandalam → Kanchi → conquest of Madurai.
### 8. SALUVA NARASIMHA AND ANDHRA CAMPAIGNS
- **Lord of Chandragiri**; clan from Kalyana; ancestor Saluva Mangu.
- **1485**: removed the weak **Praudha Raya** with help of **Tuluva Narasa Nayaka** → **first usurpation**; ruled about five years.
- **Annamacharya (1408–1503)** – his contemporary, supported in his court (APPSC 2011).
| Court work | Author |
|---|---|
| ***Saluvabhyudayam*** | **Rajanatha Dindima** |
| ***Jaimini Bharatam*** | **Pillalamarri Pinaveerabhadra** (dedicated to him) |
#### 8.1 Sangama Campaigns in Andhra
- **Bukka I**: **Srisailam** and **Dupadu** (near Markapur); Udayagiri (1343).
- **Harihara II**: pushed to the Krishna with the **Reddis**; gave a daughter to Kataya Vema's son.
- **Devaraya I**: fought the **Kandukuru Reddis**; allied with the **Velamas** and Kataya Vema; took southern Kondaveedu with **Motupalli**.
- **Devaraya II**: Kondaveedu (1424).
- **Vinukonda Vallabharaya**, governor of Vinukonda under Devaraya II, wrote ***Kridabhiramam*** – describes **Kakatiya Warangal**; character **Machaladevi** (courtesan).
### 9. VIJAYANAGARA-BAHMANI CONFLICTS
- **Raichur Doab (Raichur Antarvedi)** – fertile land between **Krishna and Tungabhadra** – **main cause** (APPSC G2 2000, 2003); aim = territory.
- Bahmani kingdom founded **1347** in Harihara I's reign; conflict over the Doab began under **Bukka I**; wars lasted two centuries.
| War | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **Mudgal (1358)** | Taken by **Bukka I**; **first Indian war with artillery** |
| Goa, Belgaum (**1380**) | Taken by Harihara II's forces |
| **War of the Goldsmith's Daughter** | **Devaraya I vs Firuz Shah**, over **Nehal of Mudgal**; Devaraya gave his daughter and **Bankapur** as dowry |
| Devaraya II | Two wars over Mudgal, Raichur, Bijapur |
| **Goa lost (1472)** | To the Bahmanis |
| **Raichur (1520)** | Retaken by Krishnadevaraya (Gobburi war) with Portuguese help; **Gulbarga 1523** |
| **Talikota (1565)** | Deccan sultanates defeated Rama Raya |
!NUM Mudgal 1358 → Goa lost 1472 → Gulbarga 1523 → Talikota 1565
