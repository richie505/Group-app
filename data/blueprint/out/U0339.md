### 7. CLASSIFICATION AND RECOGNITION
- **Recognition**: **Election Symbols (Reservation and Allotment) Order, 1968**. Recognised parties → **reserved symbols**; RUPPs and independents → **free symbols** (184 in May 2025).
- **State party** (any one): 6% votes + 2 Assembly seats; 6% in LS poll + 1 LS seat; 1 in every 25 LS seats; **8% votes alone** (not 10%).
- **National status**: **AAP in 2023**; **NPP** = first national party from the **North-East**.
- **RUPPs**: 2,800+, only ~750 contested 2024; non-compliant RUPPs delisted after State CEOs verify.
- Satirical "Cockroach Janta Party" put the Symbols Order in news (Jun 2026).
### 8. LEGAL FRAMEWORK AND FUNDING
| Law / case | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Sec. 29A, RPA 1951** | Apply within **30 days** of formation, with party constitution; pledge allegiance to Constitution, **socialism, secularism, democracy**, national integrity |
| **Tenth Schedule (52nd Amendment, 1985)** | Parties entered the Constitution (not in original) |
| **INC v Institute of Social Welfare (2002)** | ECI **cannot deregister** a party except fraud, or party declared unlawful |
| **ADR v UoI (15 Feb 2024)** | **Electoral bonds struck down** |
| **Sec. 12, Income-tax Act 2025** (earlier Sec. 13A, 1961) | Parties tax-exempt |
- Donors above **₹20,000** disclosed annually (contribution report); donations above **₹2,000** only by cheque/bank transfer.
