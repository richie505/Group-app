### 3. PANCHSHEEL 1954
- Preamble of the **India–Tibet Region of China Agreement on Trade and Intercourse**, **29 April 1954** (Nehru–**Zhou Enlai**).
- Five principles: respect for **territorial integrity and sovereignty**; **non-aggression**; **non-interference**; **equality and mutual benefit**; **peaceful co-existence**.
!TRAP **Non-alignment is NOT** a Panchsheel principle (UPSC 1997).
- Burma adopted it later in **1954**; Bandung Ten Principles **1955**; **UNGA 1957**: principle of international law; Belgrade **1961**: core NAM principle.
- Don't confuse: **Tribal Panchsheel (1952)** — Nehru with **Verrier Elwin**, tribal policy.
- **Sep 2025:** Modi–Xi meeting: China stressed Panchsheel.
### 4. NON-ALIGNED MOVEMENT
- **Bandung Conference, April 1955, Indonesia**: 29 Afro-Asian countries; **Ten Principles** (included Panchsheel).
- Founded at **1st Belgrade Summit, 1961 (Yugoslavia)**. Initiated by **Nehru** (UPPSC 2014).
- Non-alignment **≠ neutrality**: judging each issue on merits.
!PAIR Bandung | 1955 | Indonesia, Ten Principles || Belgrade | 1961 | Yugoslavia, NAM founded

### 6. CORE PRINCIPLES
- **Anti-colonialism, anti-apartheid**.
- **Disarmament**; opposed the **NPT (1968)** as discriminatory.
- **Peaceful settlement**; UNSC reform through **"reformed multilateralism"**.
- **Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam** ("world is one family"): G20 2023 theme **"One Earth, One Family, One Future"**.
### 7. INSTITUTIONS AND NUCLEAR DOCTRINE
- **CCS**: PM + Finance, External Affairs, Home, **Defence**; NSA, Cabinet Secretary, service chiefs **invitees**.
- **NSC (1998)**: NSA is its secretary; first NSA **Brajesh Mishra**.
- **Standing Committee on External Affairs**: scrutinises MEA.
- **ICWA**: 1943, **Tej Bahadur Sapru**, Sapru House; **Vice-President** ex officio President; **ICWA Act 2001**.
- **Nuclear doctrine (4 January 2003, CCS)**: **credible minimum deterrence**, **No First Use**, retaliation only. **Nuclear Command Authority** = **Political Council** (chaired by **PM**; sole body to authorise use) + **Executive Council** (chaired by **NSA**).
