## U0385 | TRIBE – DEFINITION, CLASSIFICATION, DISTRIBUTION AND CHARACTERISTICS
### 1. MEANING OF TRIBE
- The word **'tribe'** comes from the Latin **'tribus'**.
- **Marshall Sahlins**: a tribe is a **segmental** body of equal, **unspecialised multi-family groups, clans or bands**.
- **Animism** = belief in and worship of **nature spirits**.
- **Totemism** = a clan's sacred (mystic) bond with an **animal or plant totem**, which is revered as a group symbol and **not killed**; it goes with **clan exogamy**.
- **Shifting cultivation** = slash-and-burn farming; called **Jhum** in North-East India.
### 2. SCHEDULED TRIBES – CONSTITUTION AND CRITERIA
#### 2.1 Articles
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Art. 366(25)** | Defines STs as communities **"deemed under Article 342"** (the term took shape under the GoI Act 1935 and the Constitution) |
| **Art. 342(1)** | **President** notifies STs after consulting the **Governor**; first order = **Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order, 1950** |
| **Art. 342(2)** | Only **Parliament, by law**, can include or exclude a community |
- ST lists are **State/UT-specific**, not one list for all India.
!TRAP 342(1) = President notifies; 342(2) = only Parliament amends the list.
#### 2.2 Criteria (NOT in the Constitution)
- Drawn from **Census 1931**, **Kalelkar Committee (1955)**, **Lokur Committee (1965)** and **Chanda Committee (1969)**.
- Lokur-type tests: primitive traits, distinct culture, geographical isolation, shyness of contact, backwardness.
#### 2.3 Number of Notified STs
- **705** individual ethnic groups are notified as STs, in **30 States/UTs** (Census 2011 count).
- **Andhra Pradesh: 34** notified STs.
- Lists are revised by later amendment orders after the **SC and ST Orders (Amendment) Act, 1976**.

### 5. PARTICULARLY VULNERABLE TRIBAL GROUPS (PVTGs)
#### 5.1 Origin and Numbers
- **Dhebar Commission (1960-61)** flagged the least-developed groups.
- **PTG** category created in **1973**; renamed **PVTGs** in **2006**.
- **75 PVTGs** in **18 States + 1 UT (Andaman and Nicobar)**.
- **Odisha 13** (highest); **AP with Telangana 12**; Bihar 9.
- Population about 27.68 lakh (2001); **Sahariyas** = largest PVTG; **Sentinelese** and Andamanese = smallest.
#### 5.2 AP PVTGs
- Joint list for "Andhra Pradesh and Telangana" = **12**: **Bodo Gadaba, Bondo Poroja, Chenchu, Dongaria Khond, Gutob Gadaba, Khond Poroja, Kolam, Konda Reddi, Konda Savara, Kutia Khond, Parengi Poroja, Thoti**.
- Older spellings: Bondo Poraja, Parangiperja, Kuttiya Kondhs, Kondareddis, Konda Savaras.
- **Kolam and Thoti** are on the Telangana side; the list was never split after 2014. Residual AP = **10** groups.
#### 5.3 PM-JANMAN and Rights
- **PM-JANMAN** (PM Janjati Adivasi Nyaya Maha Abhiyan), **Ministry of Tribal Affairs**; launched **15 Nov 2023** (Janjatiya Gaurav Divas); covers **all 75 PVTGs**.
- Budget 2023 announced **₹15,000 crore**; approved outlay **₹24,104 crore** (Centre ₹15,336 crore, States ₹8,768 crore) for **2023-24 to 2025-26**.
- Housing, water, health, roads, livelihood.
- **Bharia (Madhya Pradesh)** = first PVTG with **habitat rights** under **FRA 2006, Sec. 3(1)(e)**.
- Census 2027: Tribal Affairs Ministry sought separate PVTG enumeration.
### 6. DENOTIFIED AND NOMADIC TRIBES
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **1871** | **Criminal Tribes Act** branded ~200 communities "hereditary/born criminals" (amended 1924) |
| **1949** | **All India Criminal Tribes Inquiry Committee** (**Ayyangar Committee**) |
| **31 Aug 1952** | Act repealed → **"Denotified"** tribes; replaced by State **Habitual Offenders Acts** |
| **2008** | **Renke Commission** |
| **2017** | **Idate Commission** |
| **30 Jan 2026** | RGI agreed to enumerate DNTs in **Census 2027**; communities demand a "separate column" |
- **Nomadic tribes** = no permanent settlement (pastoralists, hunters, entertainers). DNTs were last counted in **Census 1931**.
- Lists are state-specific: **Banchhada** (MP, Rajasthan), once a "criminal tribe", is listed as **SC**.
!TRAP Renke (2008) came before Idate (2017).

### 9. TRIBAL FIRSTS AND NEWS
- **Verrier Elwin** – first anthropologist to study Indian tribes extensively.
- **Tilka Manjhi** – first tribal freedom fighter against the British.
- **Indira Gandhi National Tribal University** (Amarkantak, 2007) – first tribal university.
- **Droupadi Murmu** – first ST (and first tribal woman) President.
- **Ministry of Tribal Affairs** – set up **1999**; National Tribal Policy first drafted **2006**.
- **Shompen** – voted for the first time in the **2024 Lok Sabha** election (19 April 2024, A&N seat; 7 members); protected by the **A&N (Protection of Aboriginal Tribes) Regulation, 1956**; Great Nicobar project dispute raises FRA Sec. 3(1)(e) habitat rights.
