## U0156 | FRENCH IN INDIA – COLBERT, COMPAGNIE DES INDES, PONDICHERRY, DUPLEIX AND HIS POLICY
### 1. FRENCH EAST INDIA COMPANY
- Founded **1664** on the recommendation of **Jean-Baptiste Colbert**, **Finance Minister** of **Louis XIV**.
- Name: **Compagnie des Indes Orientales** ("Compagnie Française pour le Commerce des Indes Orientales").
- Charter: **50-year monopoly** in the **Indian and Pacific Oceans** + rights over **Madagascar**.
- **State-owned**: the French state was the largest shareholder and paid most costs; dependent on **subsidies** → vulnerable.
- **Last European company to arrive** in India.
!TRAP Founder = **Colbert** (minister), not Louis XIV himself and not Dupleix.
### 2. FRENCH SETTLEMENTS – ORDER OF FOUNDING
| Settlement | Year | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Surat** | **1668** | **First (earliest) French factory**, under **François Caron** |
| **Masulipatnam** | **1669** | Caron + **Marcara**; permission of **Abdullah Qutb Shah** |
| **Pondicherry** | **1673-74** | **François Martin** |
| **Chandernagore** | 1673 / 1690 | Permission of **Shaista Khan** (1673); settled 1690 |
| **Yanam** | **1723** | **Godavari delta**; re-occupied 1731; sovereignty confirmed 1750 |
| **Mahe** | **1725** | **Malabar** coast |
| **Karaikal** | **1739** | Coromandel coast |
!KEY APPSC-G1 key order: **Surat → Pondicherry → Mahe → Karaikal → Yanam** (Yanam formally ceded c.1750).
!TRAP **Mahe** is on the Malabar coast; **Chandernagore** is in Bengal, not Malabar.
### 3. PONDICHERRY
#### 3.1 Founding
- **1673**: land grant by **Sher Khan Lodi** (Bijapur governor of **Valikondapuram**) to **François Martin** (director at Masulipatnam).
- **1674**: Pondicherry founded; **Fort St. Louis** built – French **headquarters** in India; became the most important French settlement.
#### 3.2 Changes of Control
| Year | Change |
|---|---|
| **1693** | Captured by the **Dutch** (with English support) |
| **1697** | Restored by the **Treaty of Ryswick** (APPSC-G1 2018 key); Dutch stayed two more years |
| **1761** | Taken by the **British** after Wandiwash |
| **1763** | Restored by the **Treaty of Paris** |
!PAIR Ryswick 1697 | restored from Dutch | Pondicherry || Paris 1763 | restored from British | Pondicherry
### 4. FRENCH GOVERNORS IN ORDER
| Governor | Key point |
|---|---|
| **François Martin** | **First French governor**; builder of Pondicherry; died **31 Dec 1706** |
| **Pierre Benoît Dumas** | Governor **1735-41**; got the right to **mint rupees** at Pondicherry (1736) |
| **Dupleix** (1742-54) | **Peak of French power** |
| **Godeheu** (1754) | **Replaced Dupleix**; recalled at the start of the Third Carnatic War |
| **Count de Lally** | Lost at **Wandiwash (1760)** to **Eyre Coote** |
### 5. DUPLEIX AND HIS POLICY OF INTERVENTION
- **Governor-General of French India (1742-54)**; **first European to interfere in Indian princes' quarrels** to gain territory.
- First used **trained Indian sepoys** and strategic alliances.
- After **Nizam-ul-Mulk's death (1748)** he used two succession disputes:
| Region | Dispute |
|---|---|
| **Hyderabad** | **Nasir Jang vs Muzaffar Jang** |
| **Carnatic** | **Anwaruddin vs Chanda Sahib** |
- **Battle of Ambur (1749)**: Dupleix + Muzaffar Jang + Chanda Sahib killed **Anwaruddin** → Muzaffar Jang **Subedar**, Chanda Sahib **Nawab**.
- Titles and jagir: Dupleix made **Nawab of the Mughal territory south of the Krishna**; jagir **Valapadu**.
- **Treaty of Pondicherry (1754)**: neither side to meddle in princes' quarrels → **Dupleix recalled**.
#### 5.1 Bussy
- Escorted **Muzaffar Jang**; after his murder at **Rayachoti**, made **Salabat Jang** Subedar of the Deccan; stayed seven years in Hyderabad.
### 6. FRENCH IN ANDHRA
#### 6.1 Machilipatnam and Yanam
- **First French trade centre in Andhra** = **Machilipatnam (1669)**, permission of **Abdullah Qutb Shah** (APPSC G2 2002; APPSC-GS 2025).
- **Yanam** = **only French territory in Andhra** (APPSC-G2 2025); under French control until **1954**; now part of the **UT of Puducherry**.
#### 6.2 Northern Circars Grant (1753)
- Given by **Salabat Jang** to pay for **Bussy's troops** – high point of French influence.
- Circars: **Mustafanagar (Kondapalli), Eluru, Rajahmundry, Chicacole (Srikakulam)**.
!TRAP **Nellore** was NOT among the Circars given to the French.
#### 6.3 Battles in Andhra
| Battle | Alignment |
|---|---|
| **Tumnapalem (1756)** | Bussy + Vizianagaram's **Vijayaramaraju** vs **Jafar Ali** |
| **Bobbili (24 Jan 1757)** | **Vijayaramaraju + French (Bussy)** vs **Bobbili Ranga Rao** – Ranga Rao killed |
- **Tandra Papayya** (Ranga Rao's brother-in-law) later killed Vijayaramaraju.
!KEY APPSC G2 2019: at Bobbili, Vijayaramaraju took French help.
#### 6.4 Loss of the Circars
- **1758-59**: after **Chendurthi** and the fall of Machilipatnam to **Colonel Forde (1759)**, Salabat Jang switched to the British.
- Gave them **Machilipatnam, Nizampatnam, Vakal Mannar** and part of Kondapalli; **1759 treaty = first British-Hyderabad agreement**.
- Ended French power north of the Krishna, except in **Guntur**.
- Other Circars British in **1768**; **Guntur in 1788**.
### 7. LATER POSSESSIONS AND MERGER WITH INDIA
- After **Wandiwash** + **Treaty of Paris (1763)**: French kept only **trading enclaves** – **Pondicherry, Karaikal, Mahe, Yanam, Chandernagore**.
| Enclave | Merger |
|---|---|
| **Chandernagore** | Joined India by **plebiscite, 1950** |
| Pondicherry, Karaikal, Mahe, Yanam | **De facto 1954**, **de jure 1962** → **UT of Puducherry** |
- French lost mainly for **lack of naval supremacy** and **steady state support**; they were the only serious European rival to the British.
