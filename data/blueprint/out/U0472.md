## U0472 | POPULATION POLICY OF INDIA – NPP 2000, FAMILY WELFARE PROGRAMME, AGEING AND MIGRATION ISSUES
### 1. FAMILY PLANNING PROGRAMME – MILESTONES
- **India = first country** in the world to officially launch a **national family planning programme, in 1952** (APPSC-GS key).
| Phase | Approach |
|---|---|
| 1950s (1st-2nd Plans) | **Clinic-based** approach |
| **1976** | **First National Population Policy** (16 April); marriage age raised to **21/18**; **42nd Amendment** put population control in the **Concurrent List**; coercive sterilisation in the 1976-77 Emergency |
| **1977** | Renamed **Family Welfare Programme** after Emergency forced sterilisations |
| **1996** | **Target-free approach** (not 1986) |
| 1997 | **Reproductive and Child Health (RCH)** |
| **2000** | **NPP 2000** declared **15 February 2000** |
!TRAP 1952 = first programme; 1976 = first policy; 1977 = renamed Family Welfare; 2000 = NPP.
### 2. NATIONAL POPULATION POLICY 2000
#### 2.1 Approach
- **Target-free, voluntary, informed choice**; promotes the **two-child norm**.
- Delivery through **government, industry and NGOs**.
#### 2.2 Objectives
| Objective | Target |
|---|---|
| **Immediate** | Meet **unmet need for contraception**, health infrastructure and personnel, integrated **RCH** services |
| **Medium-term** | **TFR 2.1 by 2010** |
| **Long-term** | **Stable population by 2045** – later modified to **2070** |
- **Current target year for population stability: 2070.**
- TFR 2.1 actually reached only around **NFHS-5 (2019-21)**.
#### 2.3 10-Year Action Plan
- Included: **SHG involvement**, free compulsory education for **6-14 years**, compulsory **registration of marriages and pregnancies**.
- NOT included: a special reward for men adopting the terminal method.
!TRAP Stabilisation year in NPP 2000 is 2045 (not 2046); revised target 2070.

### 4. FAMILY WELFARE SCHEMES
| Scheme | Objective / target group |
|---|---|
| **Janani Suraksha Yojana** (April **2005**, NRHM) | **Safe motherhood**; cash incentive for **institutional delivery** to cut **maternal and neonatal mortality** among poor pregnant women (APPSC 2018) |
| **Mission Parivar Vikas** | **TFR 2.1 by 2025** in **7 high-fertility States**; outreach teams in **146 districts** |
| **PMMVY** (2.0) | Maternity benefit for **pregnant and lactating women**; conditional aid via **DBT** for the **first live birth** |
| ASHA birth-spacing scheme | 2-year gap after marriage before first child; 3-year spacing after first |
- JSY **replaced the National Maternity Benefit Scheme**; it is **centrally sponsored** (not a State Health Department intervention).
- **PMMVY** was earlier called **Indira Gandhi Matritva Sahyog Yojana**.
- Mission Parivar Vikas States: **MP, UP, Bihar, Assam, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Rajasthan** (**not Odisha**).
- **Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana** (senior citizens, 2017) – started in **Mumbai**.
!PAIR NPP 2000 | TFR 2.1 | by 2010 || Mission Parivar Vikas | TFR 2.1 | by 2025
### 5. BARRIERS TO FAMILY PLANNING
- **Couple Protection Rate**: % of eligible couples effectively protected against childbirth by an approved contraceptive method.
- Low rural impact: **social orthodoxy and psychological factors** (APPSC-G2 2008).
- Other barriers: **son preference**, children as **old-age insurance**, **early marriage**, **low female literacy**.
- **Reluctance for sterilisation** (men especially); distrust after 1976-77 coercion; female sterilisation **37.9%** (NFHS-5).
### 6. SEAT FREEZE AND POPULATION INCENTIVES
| Amendment / Article | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **42nd** | 1976 | Froze Lok Sabha and Assembly seats on **1971 Census until 2000** |
| **84th** | 2001 | Extended the freeze until the **first Census after 2026**; readjustment on 1991 Census |
| **87th** | 2003 | Boundary readjustment on **2001 Census**; seat numbers unchanged |
| **Art. 82, 170** | – | Seats allotted on population |
- Delimitation fear: southern States could lose **8-10 Lok Sabha seats**; Finance Commission population weights (15-25% of devolution criteria).
- **State two-child norms**: **Assam** – Small Family Norms rules (2019) bar parents of more than two children from direct government recruitment, effective **1 Jan 2021**; **UP** – draft Population (Control, Stabilisation and Welfare) Bill, **July 2021**, never passed.
### 7. MIGRATION
- Census measures: **place of birth** and **place of last residence**; **141.2 million** moved during 2001-2011.
- **Largest stream: rural → rural**, mostly **women**, driven by **marriage**; rural → urban is NOT the largest.
- Reasons: **women – marriage; men – work**; Gulf migration (Kerala).
- Inter-State female migration by last residence (2011): **Urban-Urban > Rural-Urban > Rural-Rural > Urban-Rural**.
| Theory | Proponent |
|---|---|
| **Laws of Migration** (1885) | **E.G. Ravenstein** |
| **Push-pull with intervening obstacles** (1966) | **Everett Lee** |
| Expected-income rural-urban model | **Michael Todaro** |
### 8. AGEING
#### 8.1 Data
- Elderly 60+: **9.7%** (SRS 2024; Kerala 15%); about **100 mn (2011) → 230 mn (2036)**, about 1 in 7 Indians.
- Ageing could cut GDP growth by **70 basis points** a year over 2025-50 (IMF).
- Nearly **70% of elderly women** lack a formal pension vs about **40%** of elderly men.
#### 8.2 Policy and Law
| Policy / Act | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **National Policy on Older Persons** (Ministry of Social Justice) | **1999** | **14 areas of intervention**, including health care and nutrition |
| **Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act** | **2007** | Maintenance = **legal duty of children and relatives** |
| **NPHCE** | 2010-11 | Health care for **60+** |
| National Council for Senior Citizens | – | Highest advisory body on elderly policy |
### 9. ANDHRA PRADESH – POPULATION MANAGEMENT POLICY 2026
- Draft policy (**March 2026**): shift from **population control → population management**.
- **₹25,000** incentive for a second or third child; **removes the two-child norm for local body elections**.
| Pillar | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Matrutva** | Maternal health |
| **Shakti** | Women's work participation |
| **Kshema** | Elderly care |
| **Naipunyam** | Skills |
| **Sanjeevani** | Digital health |
- Health Minister **Satya Kumar Yadav** warned of **"shrinconomics"**; AP TFR **1.50**.
