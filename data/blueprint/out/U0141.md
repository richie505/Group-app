## U0141 | RISE OF THE MARATHAS – SHIVAJI'S CAREER, ASHTAPRADHAN, REVENUE SYSTEM, CHAUTH AND SARDESHMUKHI
- **Exams:** Group 1 Prelims / Group 2 Screening | **HIGH**
- **Rows:** A-118 Rise of the Marathas; S1-57 Shivaji – career, conquests and the Ashtapradhan; S1-58 Shivaji's revenue system, chauth and sardeshmukhi; rise of the Maratha Empire
- **PYQ importance:** APPSC 5 questions | other exams 28
### 1. CAUSES OF THE MARATHA RISE
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Geography** | **Sahyadri** hills + hill forts suited guerrilla war |
| **Bhakti saints** | **Tukaram** and **Ramdas** gave spiritual and regional unity |
| **Deccan Sultanate experience** | Marathas served in the armies and revenue posts of **Bijapur and Ahmadnagar** (Shahaji himself); Ashtapradhan modelled on Sultanate councils |
| **Mughal weakness** | Aurangzeb's **27-year Deccan campaign** drained (did not strengthen) the Mughal treasury; succession war after 1707 left a vacuum |
### 2. SHIVAJI'S EARLY LIFE AND THE PEOPLE AROUND HIM
- Born at **Shivneri fort**, **19 February 1630** (date accepted by the Maharashtra government; APPSC 2018). Historian Jadunath Sarkar preferred **1627**, so some keys pair "1627, 1674" (birth, Chhatrapati title).
| Person | Role |
|---|---|
| **Shahaji Bhonsle** | Father; Maratha general |
| **Jijabai** | Mother; with Dadaji Kondadev shaped his early life |
| **Dadaji Kondadev** | Keeper of the **Pune jagir** and his **tutor** |
| **Samarth Ramdas** | **Guru** by tradition; wrote the **Dasbodh** (Advaita text) and the poem **Shivastuti** |
| **Tukaram** | Saint contemporary; shaped the spiritual base |
| **Akkanna** | **Sar Lashkar** of Golconda |
| **Madanna** | **Mir Jumla** of Golconda |
| Abul Hasan Tana Shah | Golconda ruler; treaty with Shivaji (1677) |
| Venkoji / Ekoji | Step-brother; lost Vellore to Shivaji |
- **Torna (1646)**, taken from Bijapur at age **16** = his **first fort**.
- **Raigad** taken from the **Mores of Javli (1656)**; later capital and coronation site.
!TRAP Shivneri = birth (1630); Torna = first fort (1646); Raigad = coronation (1674); Purandar = treaty fort (1665).
### 3. CAREER AND CONFLICTS – CHRONOLOGY
| Year | Episode | Opponent | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1659** | **Afzal Khan** | **Bijapur** general | Killed with a **wagh nakh** at the foot of **Pratapgad** |
| 1660 | Siege of Panhala | Bijapur | Shivaji broke out after months |
| **1663** | **Shaista Khan** | Mughal noble, Aurangzeb's maternal uncle | **Night raid at Pune**; Khan lost his fingers and retreated |
| **1664, 1670** | **Surat** sacked twice | Mughal port | Nearly **one crore rupees**; religious places spared |
| **1665** | **Treaty of Purandar** | Mughals under **Raja Jai Singh** (Mirza Raja of Amber), with **Diler Khan** | Ceded forts |
| **1666** | **Agra** visit | Mughal court | Seated behind lesser nobles; house arrest at **Jaipur Bhawan**; escaped with **Sambhaji** in **baskets of sweets and fruit** |
| **1672** | **Salher** | Mughals | Mughals beaten in **open battle** |
| **1674** | **Coronation** | – | Raigad |
| **1677** | Hyderabad visit; **Karnataka campaign** | – | Treaty with Golconda |
| **1680** | Death | – | – |
#### 3.1 Treaty of Purandar (11 June 1665)
- With the Mughals under **Jai Singh**, who besieged **Purandar fort** where Shivaji kept his family and treasure.
- Ceded **23 of 35 forts**; son **Sambhaji** sent to the Mughal court; Shivaji to help the Mughals against **Bijapur**.
!KEY APPSC 2011: "1665 – Raja Jai Singh" is the correct pairing. Purandar was a treaty with the **Mughals**, not part of the conflicts with Bijapur.
#### 3.2 Karnataka Campaign and the Golconda Alliance (AP angle)
- **1672** attack on Golconda: took **2 lakh Honnu**.
- **7 March 1677**: visited **Hyderabad**; treaty with **Abul Hasan Tana Shah** through **Akkanna and Madanna** – Golconda to give **money for the southern conquests** and **share the spoils**.
- Conquests (1677): **Ballari, Gingee (Jinji), Vellore** (from Venkoji/Ekoji). Shivaji **did not share the spoils**.
### 4. CORONATION (1674), TITLES AND ERA
- **Raigad, 6 June 1674**, Vedic rites; priest **Gaga Bhatt of Banaras (Varanasi)**.
- Title **"Shri Raja Shiva Chhatrapati"**; also **Haindava Dharmoddharak** (not "Hindavi Sultan").
- New era **Rajyabhisheka Shaka** – asserted sovereignty against the Mughals and Sultanates.
### 5. ASHTAPRADHAN – COUNCIL OF EIGHT MINISTERS
| Minister | Persian / other title | Portfolio |
|---|---|---|
| **Peshwa** | Mukhya Pradhan | Prime Minister, general administration |
| **Amatya** | **Majumdar** | **Finance**: treasury, accounts, revenue |
| **Sachiv** | **Shurnavis (Surnis)** | **Royal correspondence**, records, home affairs |
| **Mantri** | **Waqia-navis (Waknis)** | **Intelligence**, king's diary, royal household |
| **Senapati** | **Sar-i-Naubat** | **Military commander** |
| **Sumant** | **Dabir** | **Foreign affairs**; diplomacy, treaties; **master of ceremonies** who received envoys |
| **Nyayadhish** | – | **Justice** (chief justice) |
| **Panditrao** | Danadhyaksha | **Religious affairs** and charitable grants |
- Council of eight by **1674**; each headed a department; **each reported directly to the Chhatrapati**.
- Posts **not hereditary**; ministers could be moved or removed.
- All except **Panditrao and Nyayadhish** had to lead military campaigns.
- Advisory only: Shivaji could accept or reject advice; ministers did not work as a cabinet. "Each minister held independent charge" – keyed **false**.
- Later the **Peshwa** post became hereditary and supreme.
- Keyed answer for officers listed in the Ashtapradhan: **Mazumdar, Waknis, Surnis** (Dabir left out).
!KEY APPSC G1 2024: **Dabir = master of ceremonies** (he handled foreign affairs, not treasury and revenue). APPSC G2 2023: **Sar-i-Naubat = military commander**.
!TRAP Sachiv = correspondence; Sumant = foreign affairs; Mantri = intelligence.
### 6. CHAUTH AND SARDESHMUKHI
| Levy | Rate | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| **Chauth** | **25% (one-fourth)** of land revenue | **Protection money** – against third-party invasion or to be spared Maratha raids; from lands **outside direct Maratha rule** (provinces called **Mughalai**) |
| **Sardeshmukhi** | **Additional 10%** | The king's **hereditary right** as **Sardeshmukh** (chief headman) of the Deccan |
- Collected from Mughal and Deccan Sultanate territory; funded military growth and political control **without the cost of direct governance** – a regional state became a pan-Indian power.
- Recognition: **Mughals in 1719**; the **Nizam at Palkhed**; Nizam forced to pay chauth again after the **1795** war → pushed him into the **Subsidiary Alliance (1798)**.
!KEY UPPSC 2018: "Maratha revenue claim for protection" = **Chauth**.
### 7. REVENUE SYSTEM AND ADMINISTRATIVE UNITS
#### 7.1 Land Revenue Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ryotwari-type settlement | State dealt **directly with the cultivator** |
| **Kathi** | **Measuring rod**; **Annaji Datto's** survey |
| **Jirayat** | Dry land |
| **Bagayat** | Irrigated land |
| **Saranjam** | Service land grants – **abolished by Shivaji**, restored under **Shahu and the Peshwas** |
- **Jagirs abolished**: officials paid in **cash**.
- **Deshmukhs**: hereditary rights of deshmukhs and deshpandes (watandars) **curbed**; big deshmukhs **opposed Shivaji** because they did not back an independent Maratha state and wanted to stay under Bijapur (UPPSC 2024: A and R true, R explains A).
#### 7.2 Units and Officials
| Unit | Official |
|---|---|
| **Prant** (province) | Subedar / Sarsubedar |
| **Pargana, Tarf** | **Deshmukh, Deshpande** |
| **Mauza** (village) | **Patil** (head), **Kulkarni** (accountant) |
### 8. COINS, LANGUAGE AND RECORDS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Hon (Shivrai hon)** | **Gold** coin, probably issued for the 1674 coronation |
| **Shivrai** | **Copper** coin |
| Shivrai rupaya | **Silver** coin with "Sri Raja Shiv" in Devanagari |
| **Rajvyavahar Kosh** | Replaced **Persian** official terms with **Sanskrit** ones |
| **Modi script** | Cursive running script for **Marathi** records (letters, court proceedings, land records) |
| **Ganimi Kava** | Guerrilla hit-and-run warfare in the Sahyadri terrain |
- Official language: **Marathi and Sanskrit** replaced Persian (Hindavi Swarajya).
### 9. MILITARY ORGANISATION – FORTS, ARMY AND NAVY
#### 9.1 Forts
- **Over 300 forts**; each under **three officers of equal rank**: **Havaldar** (garrison commander), **Sabnis** (records), **Sarnaubat/Sarnobat** (military officer).
| Fort type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Giri Durg** (hill) | Raigad, Pratapgad, Torna, Rajgad, Sinhagad, Panhala |
| **Bhu Durg** (land) | Vishalgad |
| **Jal Durg** (sea) | **Sindhudurg, Vijaydurg**, Khanderi, Suvarnadurg |
#### 9.2 Army and Navy
- **Bargirs** = cavalry given horses and equipment by the **state**; **Shiledars** = kept **their own horses**.
- **Mavali** infantry (hill troops); standing army paid in cash – loyalty direct to the state.
- Navy: sea forts **Sindhudurg, Kolaba, Vijaydurg** guarded the **Konkan**; Shivaji called the **Father of the Indian Navy**.
- **UNESCO 2025**: **Maratha Military Landscapes of India** inscribed as World Heritage.
!TRAP Bargirs = state horses; Shiledars = own horses.
### 10. SUCCESSORS OF SHIVAJI
| Ruler | Period | Key steps |
|---|---|---|
| **Sambhaji** | 1680-1689 | Resisted the Mughals; captured and **executed by Aurangzeb (1689)** → conflict became a people's war |
| **Rajaram** | 1689-1700 | Moved to **Gingee (Jinji)**, drew Aurangzeb south; **created the post of Pratinidhi** |
| **Tarabai** (regent for **Shivaji II**) | 1700-1707 | Rajaram's widow; led the struggle **from 1700**; took Singhgarh, Raigarh, Satara; led the Marathas at Aurangzeb's death (1707); her line settled at **Kolhapur** by the **Treaty of Warna (1731)** |
| **Shahu** | 1707-1749 | Sambhaji's son; **released by Bahadur Shah I (1707)**; beat Tarabai at **Khed (1707)**; ruled from **Satara**; made **Balaji Vishwanath Peshwa (1713)** |
- Order (UPPSC 2005): **Sambhaji → Rajaram → Shivaji II (Tarabai) → Shahu**.
- **Aurangzeb died in the Deccan (1707)** without subduing the Marathas; Tarabai's raids broke Mughal finances.
### 11. EXPANSION UNDER THE PESHWAS
- **Balaji Vishwanath** (1713-1720): real power moved from the Chhatrapati at Satara to the Peshwa.
- **1719 Mughal grants** (Farrukhsiyar, through the Sayyids): recognised **Shahu**, confirmed **Swarajya** lands, gave **chauth and sardeshmukhi of the six Deccan subas**.
- **Baji Rao I** (1720-1740): major expansion in North India.
- Peak extent: **Attock to Cuttack**; Gulf of Khambhat to beyond the Kaveri.
- Comparison: Mughal mansabdari rested on **jagirs**; Shivaji paid in **cash**. Ashtapradhan advisory vs Mughal wazir system hierarchical.
- Removal of a "Maratha Empire" map from a revised NCERT textbook led to a PIL in the Bombay High Court (20 May 2026).
