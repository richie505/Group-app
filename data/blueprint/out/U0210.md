## U0210 | REVOLUTIONARY NATIONALISM – SECOND PHASE: HSRA, BHAGAT SINGH, CHANDRASHEKHAR AZAD, SURYA SEN, KAKORI AND LAHORE CASES
### 1. HRA AND HSRA
#### 1.1 Comparison
| Feature | HRA | HSRA |
|---|---|---|
| Year, place | **October 1924, Kanpur** | **September 1928, Feroz Shah Kotla, Delhi** |
| Founders / leader | **Ram Prasad Bismil, Jogesh Chandra Chatterjee, Sachindra Nath Sanyal** | Reorganised under **Chandrashekhar Azad** after **Bismil's** death; **Bhagat Singh** a founder leader |
| Members | **Ashfaqullah Khan, Roshan Singh, Rajendra Lahiri, Azad** | **Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Rajguru, B.K. Dutt, Bejoy (Vijay) Kumar Sinha, Shiv Verma, Jaidev Kapur, Bhagwati Charan Vohra** |
| Goal | **Republic by armed revolt** | Added **socialism**: "**Federal Republic of United States of India**" |
| Most active | 1924–25 | **1928–1931** |
- **Azad** (not Bhagat Singh) reorganised and **renamed Sanyal's HRA as the HSRA**.
- HSRA-era method: **mass propaganda** – hunger strikes, courtroom proceedings, leaflets.
!KEY APPSC GS 2018: HSRA founder key = **Bhagat Singh**. APPSC G1 2024: HRA founders = **Sanyal, Chatterjee, Bismil**.
!TRAP Bhagat Singh was **not** an HRA founder; in 1925 the body was still the HRA (HSRA name dates from **1928**).
!TRAP **Shiv Verma** was **not** an HRA member; he joined the **HSRA**.
### 2. NAUJAWAN BHARAT SABHA AND OTHER BODIES
- **Naujawan Bharat Sabha:** **Lahore, March 1926**; founded by **Bhagat Singh**, with **Sukhdev** and **Bhagwati Charan Vohra**.
- Aim: rouse **youth, peasants and workers**; **oppose communalism**; spread Marxist ideas.
- Early members included **Chhabil Das** and **Yashpal**; **Ambika Chakravarti** was **NOT** an early member (he was a Chittagong raider).

| **Kuka Movement** | **Guru Ram Singh** |

### 3. KAKORI TRAIN ACTION (9 AUGUST 1925)
- HRA looted the **government treasury** on a train at **Kakori** near **Lucknow** (Lucknow–Saharanpur section) to fund revolutionary work.
- **43 arrested**; government advocate (public prosecutor): **Jagat Narain Mulla**.
- **Azad** = the **sole** Kakori revolutionary who **escaped arrest**.
#### 3.1 Hanged (December 1927)
| Revolutionary | Jail |
|---|---|
| **Ram Prasad Bismil** | **Gorakhpur** |
| **Ashfaqullah Khan** | **Faizabad** |
| **Roshan Singh** | **Allahabad (Naini)** |
| **Rajendra Lahiri** | **Gonda** |
- **Bismil** refused milk before execution: "Now, I shall take my **mother's milk** only."
- **Jogesh Chatterjee** got life imprisonment.
!TRAP **Non-participants:** **Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Rajguru** and **Surya Sen** had no part in Kakori.
### 4. ASSASSINATIONS AND ATTEMPTS
| Action | Target | Reason / detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Calcutta, 12 January 1924** | **Charles Tegart**, Police Commissioner | **Gopinath Saha** shot **Ernest Day** by mistake |
| **Lahore, December 1928** | ASP **J.P. Saunders** (mistaken for SP **James A. Scott**) | To avenge **Lala Lajpat Rai**, fatally hurt in the anti-Simon lathi-charge (Oct 1928); shot by **Bhagat Singh and Rajguru** (**Rajguru fired first**); **Azad** covered the retreat, killing head constable **Chanan Singh** |
| **London, Caxton Hall, 13 March 1940** | Sir **Michael O'Dwyer** | **Udham Singh** – revenge for **Jallianwala Bagh** (O'Dwyer was the Punjab Lt. Governor) |

### 5. CENTRAL ASSEMBLY BOMB (8 APRIL 1929)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Place | **Central Legislative Assembly, Delhi** |
| Participants | **Bhagat Singh** and **Batukeshwar Dutt** |
| Bombs | **Non-lethal smoke bombs** and leaflets |
| Bills protested | **Public Safety Bill** and **Trade Disputes Bill** (curbing civil liberties and workers) |
| Slogan | **"Inquilab Zindabad"** |
| Leaflet | "**To make the deaf hear**" |
| Strategy | Courted arrest to use the **court as a platform** |
- **Inquilab Zindabad** ("Long Live Revolution"): written by **Maulana Hasrat Mohani**; Bhagat Singh made it popular.
- **Batukeshwar Dutt:** **life transportation** in the Assembly bomb case (not hanged).
### 6. LAHORE CONSPIRACY CASE (1929–1931)
- Origin: **Saunders killing (1928)** + bomb-making; trial **1929–30** before a **special tribunal** set up by the **Governor-General**.
- This was the "**second**" Lahore Conspiracy Case; the **first (1915)** was the **Ghadar** case.
- **Death sentence: October 1930**.
#### 6.1 Sentences
| Sentence | Revolutionaries |
|---|---|
| **Hanged, 23 March 1931**, Lahore jail | **Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Rajguru** |
| **Transportation for life** | **Kishori Lal, Mahabir Singh, Bejoy Kumar Sinha, Shiv Verma, Gaya Prasad, Jaidev Kapur, Kamalnath Tewari** |
- Cremated at **Hussainiwala** (**Ferozpur district**, banks of the **Sutlej**), now their memorial.
#### 6.2 Lahore jail hunger strike (1929)
- For **political-prisoner status**: **Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Batukeshwar Dutt, Jatin Das**.
- **Jatin Das** – arrested **14 June 1929**; made bombs for Bhagat Singh; earlier in Non-Cooperation (1921); **died after 63 days** of hunger strike (**September 1929**).
!TRAP Lahore case hanged = Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Rajguru – **not Bismil or Roshan Singh** (Kakori).

### 9. CHITTAGONG ARMOURY RAID (18 APRIL 1930)
- Leader: **Surya Sen ("Masterda")**, Chittagong, Bengal (now Bangladesh); group: **Indian Republican Army**.
- Associates: **Ganesh Ghosh, Lokenath Bal, Ananta Singh, Ambika Chakravarty**.
- Aim: seize British arms, cut communications; raided the armoury of police and auxiliary forces; **cut telegraph lines**, blocked railways; fought at **Jalalabad Hills**.
- **Surya Sen hanged – 1934**.

### 10. SIGNIFICANCE, CHRONOLOGY AND AP ANGLE
- Shift from **individual heroism** to **socialist ideology** and **mass propaganda**.
- **Order:** Tegart attempt (1924) → HRA (1924) → **Kakori (1925)** → Naujawan Bharat Sabha (1926) → **HSRA, Saunders (1928)** → **Assembly bomb (April 1929)** → **Chittagong (April 1930)** → executions (1931) → Surya Sen hanged (1934) → O'Dwyer shot (1940).
| Person | Action |
|---|---|
| Batukeshwar Dutt | Central Assembly bombing |
| Bismil | Kakori (HRA) |
| Surya Sen / Kalpana Dutt | Chittagong raid |
- **AP angle:** Andhra Salt Satyagraha prisoners, jailed with Lahore/Meerut convicts, turned **communist**; **Krovi Lingaraju** inspired youth with Alluri and Bhagat Singh; **Hindustan Socialist Republican Army**, Madras (**10 March 1933**) – **Kanuru Ramanatha Choudary**.

4. Saunders was **mistaken for Scott**; Gopinath Saha hit **Ernest Day**, not Tegart.

6. **B.K. Dutt** got life, was not hanged.

9. Udham Singh killed **O'Dwyer** (not General Dyer) at **Caxton Hall, 1940**.
