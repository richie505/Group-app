## U0124 | VIJAYANAGARA ART AND ARCHITECTURE - HAMPI, GOPURAMS, KALYANA MANDAPA, VITTHALA TEMPLE, PAINTING
### 1. THE VIJAYANAGARA STYLE
- **Percy Brown**: Vijayanagara architecture = "**matured state of the Dravidian tradition**"; the last great phase of Dravidian temple building.
#### 1.1 Features
| Feature | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Raya gopuram** | Towering gateway that **dwarfs the central shrine**; set in massive enclosures |
| **Kalyana mandapa** | Pillared hall for the **divine marriage** |
| **Amman shrine** | Separate **goddess** shrine (e.g. Hazara Rama) |
| **Musical pillars** | **Vittala Ranga mandapa – 56 pillars** (not 108) that give notes when tapped |
| **Sahasra Stambha Mandapa** | Thousand-pillared halls with carved **composite pillars** |
| **Ekapaadam** | Hoysala-type single pillar carved in 3–4 sections |
#### 1.2 What Was New vs Earlier Andhra Styles
- **Secular buildings inside temple premises**; **pillared corridors** added to older temples; Indo-Islamic influence in palaces.
- Spread in Andhra through courts at **Kondavidu, Udayagiri, Chandragiri, Vinukonda**.
### 2. HAMPI – UNESCO AND CURRENCY
- Capital on the **southern bank of the Tungabhadra**; Vijayanagara district, **Karnataka**.
- "**Group of Monuments at Hampi**" = UNESCO World Heritage Site, **1986**.
- **Vittala stone chariot** on the reverse of the **₹50 note** (Mahatma Gandhi New series).
- "**Athens of Vijayanagara**" = area around the Virupaksha temple.
### 3. HAMPI TEMPLES
| Temple | Popular name / deity | Patron and facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Virupaksha** | **Pampapati**; Shiva | **First and largest** temple of Hampi; family deity; 7th-century origins; enlarged by **Lakkana Dandesha** under **Devaraya II**; **Krishnadevaraya** added central pillared hall and **eastern gopuram (1510)** |
| **Vitthala** | **Saptaswara Devalayam** (Vaishnava) | Begun under Devaraya II, expanded by **Krishnadevaraya**, continued under Achyutaraya; **56 "SaReGaMa" musical pillars**; **stone chariot (Ekashila Ratham)** |
| **Hazara Rama** | **Ramachandra temple** (APPSC 2008) | **Royal chapel** in the royal centre; built by **Krishnadevaraya** (APPSC 2025); Ramayana friezes; four Hoysala-style pillars |
| **Krishna** (Krishnalayam) | **Bala Krishna** | Idol brought from **Udayagiri** fort by Krishnadevaraya |
| **Achyutaraya temple** | **Tiruvengalanatha** (Vishnu) | **1534**, Achyutaraya's reign; set up by his officer **Hiriya Tirumalaraja** |
- Krishnadevaraya's other Hampi works: **Ugra Narasimha**, **Hanuman** statue, monolithic **Ganesha**.
### 4. SECULAR STRUCTURES AND INDO-ISLAMIC INFLUENCE
| Structure | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Lotus Mahal** (Chitrangi / Padma Mahal) | Lotus-shaped with domes; **Indo-Islamic**; credited to Krishnadevaraya |
| **Queen's Bath** (Maharani Snanavatika) | Near the Lotus Mahal, in the **zenana** area |
| **Elephant stables** | In the zenana area |
| **Mahanavami (Dasara) Dibba** | Built by **Krishnadevaraya** for Dasara |
| **Huzur Daftar** (Diwan Khana) | Council chamber; **Indo-Persian**; **40 pillars** |
| Stepped tank | Symmetrical stone tank in the royal enclosure |
!TRAP Lotus Mahal = Indo-Islamic; Huzur Daftar = Indo-Persian, 40 pillars.
### 5. LEPAKSHI – VEERABHADRA TEMPLE
#### 5.1 Location and Builders
- **Veerabhadra (Papanaseswara)** temple on **Kurma Shailam** hill, near **Hindupur**, **Sri Sathya Sai district** (formerly Anantapur).
- Built **c. 1530–40** by the Nayaka brothers **Virupanna and Veeranna** of **Penukonda** (governors), officers of **Achyutaraya** – not Harihara and Bukka.
- Shrines: **Shiva, Veerabhadra and Vishnu**, each with a mukha mandapa.
#### 5.2 Marvels
| Feature | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Hanging pillar** | Lepakshi (APPSC 2019) |
| **Monolithic Nandi** | "Large stone bull of the Vijayanagara period"; largest monolithic Nandi in India (APPSC G2 2017) |
| **Naga linga** | Linga under a **seven-hooded serpent**, carved from a **single boulder** |
| Ceilings | Richly carved and painted |
#### 5.3 Latest
- 3 Apr 2026: AP sought **₹103.05 crore** for Lepakshi tourism.
- 7 May 2026: TTD approved a new **Kalyana mandapam** at Lepakshi.
- 8 Jun 2026: State launch of **Yogandhra-2026** at Lepakshi.
### 6. PAINTING
- **Lepakshi murals**: finest Vijayanagara-school frescoes; famous for "**pictorial inscriptions**" (APPSC G2 2019).
  - Ceilings: **Ramayana and Mahabharata**.
  - Walls: Shiva themes – **Parvati Parinayamu**, **Tripurasura Samharam**, Shiva Tandavam, Bhikshatana, Dakshinamurti.
- **Hampi Virupaksha murals**: **Dashavatara**, **Girijakalyana**.
- Technique: mineral pigments on lime plaster; bold outlines, flat colours, **elongated figures, energetic movement**.
- Standard survey: **Stella Kramrisch**, **A Survey of Painting in the Deccan**.
