## U0543 | FINANCE COMMISSIONS – MANDATE AND KEY RECOMMENDATIONS OF THE 13TH, 14TH, 15TH AND 16TH
### 1. MANDATE (ARTICLE 280)
- Constituted by the **President** every five years; **Chairman + 4 members**.
- **Art. 280(3)** duties: (a) sharing **net proceeds** of taxes Centre-States and among States; (b) principles of **grants-in-aid** (Art. 275); (bb)/(c) measures to augment State Consolidated Funds for **panchayats and municipalities** (added **1992**, based on **State Finance Commission** reports).
- Recommendations are **advisory**; Art. 282 discretionary grants are **not** an FC role.
- **FC (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1951**: Chairman = experience in **public affairs**; members from judiciary, finance/accounts, administration, economics.

- **11th FC**: **National Disaster Reserve Fund** (supplements National Calamity Contingency Fund).
