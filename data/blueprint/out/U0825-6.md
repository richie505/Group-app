### 1. WORLD PRESS FREEDOM INDEX 2026 – KEY FACTS
- Headline: press freedom at its **lowest level in decades** – global press freedom at a **25-year low**; India **157th of 180**.
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Publisher | **Reporters Without Borders (RSF)** |
| RSF established | **1985** |
| RSF headquarters | **Paris, France** |
| Countries covered | **180** |
| India 2026 | **157th** |
| India 2025 | 151st |
| Change | **Fell 6 places** (151 → 157) |
| India's category | **"Very serious"** |
| Rank 1 | **Norway** |
| Last | **Eritrea** |
- Top five include **Norway (1st)**, the **Netherlands**, **Estonia** and **Sweden**.
- **Iran** is in the bottom five.
- RSF cited a **severe decline in India's legal indicator**.
!KEY WPFI 2026: India 157th of 180, down 6 places from 151st (2025); Norway 1st; Eritrea last.
### 2. INDICATORS AND METHOD
#### 2.1 The Five Indicators
| No. | Indicator |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Political context** |
| 2 | **Legal framework** |
| 3 | **Economic context** |
| 4 | **Socio-cultural context** |
| 5 | **Safety of journalists** |
!TRAP "Number of newspapers" or "circulation" is NOT one of the five WPFI indicators.
#### 2.2 Categories (best to worst)
- Good – Satisfactory – Problematic – **Difficult** – **Very serious**.
- For the **first time** in the Index's history, **more than 50%** of countries are in the **"Difficult" or "Very serious"** categories.
### 3. WHY INDIA FELL
1. **Violence against journalists**.
2. **Arrests and legal pressure** on journalists.
3. **Media ownership concentration**.
4. **Political influence** over media.
5. **Online harassment**.
- Result: rank **151 → 157**.
- The **Union Government rejected/dismissed the rankings as biased**.
### 4. GLOBAL TRENDS AND CONSTITUTIONAL BACKGROUND
- About **100 countries declined** in WPFI 2026.
- Press freedom is **not written separately** in the Constitution; it is **implicit in Article 19(1)(a)** – freedom of speech and expression.
!PAIR Press freedom | Article 19(1)(a) | Implicit right || Reasonable restrictions | Article 19(2) | On the State
