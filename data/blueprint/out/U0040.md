## U0040 | DECLINE OF THE MAURYAN EMPIRE – COMPETING EXPLANATIONS
### 1. THEORIES OF DECLINE → HISTORIAN
| Theory | Historian | Core argument |
|---|---|---|
| **Brahmanical reaction** | **H.P. Shastri** | Pro-Buddhist policy and ban on sacrifices angered Brahmins; Sunga coup = Brahmin revolt |
| **Ashoka's pacifism** | **H.C. Raychaudhuri** | Non-violence after Kalinga dulled the army's spirit |
| **Financial crisis** | **D.D. Kosambi** | Army, bureaucracy and Dhamma spending emptied the treasury |
| **Over-centralisation**, no national consciousness | **Romila Thapar** | Rule depended on the king; weak successors → collapse |
| **Oppressive provincial rule** | Divyavadana evidence | Revolts at **Taxila** against wicked officials (Bindusara's and Ashoka's reigns) |
- No single theory is accepted; historians stress **structural weakness and weak successors** over religious reaction.
#### 1.1 Evidence for the Financial Crisis
- Heavy spending on **Dhamma missions** and gifts to the **Sangha**.
- **Debasement of later punch-marked silver coins** (more alloy).
#### 1.2 Against the Brahmanical-Reaction Theory
- Pushyamitra was himself a **Mauryan general**; **Sanchi and Bharhut** grew under Sunga rule.
!TRAP Shastri = Brahmanical; Raychaudhuri = pacifism; Kosambi = finance; Thapar = centralisation.
### 2. LATER MAURYAS AND DIVISION OF THE EMPIRE
- Usual list: **Kunala, Dasharatha, Samprati, Shalishuka, Devavarman, Shatadhanvan, Brihadratha**.
| Part | Rulers |
|---|---|
| Western (north-west) | **Kunala**, later **Samprati**; replaced by Indo-Greeks by **c. 180 BC** |
| Eastern (Pataliputra) | **Dasharatha** … **Brihadratha** (last) |
- **Samprati**: Ashoka's grandson, **Jain** patron, ruled c. 230–215 BCE.
### 3. FOREIGN THREATS AND BREAKAWAY REGIONS
| Threat / region | Power |
|---|---|
| North-west | Bactrian (Indo-Greek) **Demetrius**, later **Menander** |
| Hindu Kush, c. **206 BC** | **Antiochus III** (Seleucid); tribute from **Subhagasena** |
| Great Wall of China (c. 220 BC) | Turned Scythian tribes west and south towards India |
| Andhra / Deccan | **Satavahanas**, earlier Mauryan feudatories |
| Vidarbha | **Yajnasena** (Malavikagnimitram) |
- Neglect of the **north-west frontier**; Indo-Greek raids drained resources.
- **Menander = Milinda**; capital **Sagala (Sialkot)**; dialogue with monk **Nagasena** in the **Milindapanha**.
!TRAP Indo-Greek pressure was a cause; a permanent alliance with them was NOT.
### 4. END OF THE DYNASTY AND THE SUNGAS
- **Brihadratha**, 7th king after Ashoka, killed by his **senani Pushyamitra Sunga** at a **military review** – a military coup (APPSC 2023).
- Sunga dynasty founded in Magadha **c. 185 BCE**; Sungas = **immediate successors** of the Mauryas (APPSC G2 2003).
- Pushyamitra: **Brahmana, Bharadvaja gotra**; held the Ganga heartland against Demetrius and Menander.
- Rituals: **two Ashvamedhas** – **Ayodhya inscription of Dhanadeva**.
- Buddhism debate: **Divyavadana** calls him a persecutor; yet Sanchi and Bharhut grew.
- Core: Magadha, Malwa-Vidisha, Narmada valley.
- **Malavikagnimitram** (Kalidasa): love story of the **son of Pushyamitra**; grandson **Vasumitra** defeated a Yavana army on the **Sindhu**.
### 5. KANVAS
- Founder **Vasudeva Kanva**, Brahmana minister, killed the last Sunga **Devabhuti**.
- Four kings (c. 73–28 BC): **Vasudeva, Bhumimitra, Narayana, Susharman**.
- **Susharman**, last Kanva, overthrown by the **Satavahanas (Andhras)**, not Menander.
- Order in Magadha: **Mauryas → Sungas → Kanvas** → Satavahana conquest.
### 6. KHARAVELA AND THE SATAVAHANAS
- **Hathigumpha inscription** (2nd c. BC): year-by-year record of **Kharavela**; at **Udayagiri** (not Khandagiri), near Bhubaneswar.
- Udayagiri **18** caves, Khandagiri **15**; natural and artificial.
- Kharavela: **Jaina**, **Chedi (Mahameghavahana)** line; raided Magadha and recovered the **Jina image** taken by the Nandas; sent an army west **ignoring Satakarni**.
- Satavahanas: founder **Simuka (Srimukha)**; Puranas call them **Andhra-bhrityas**; first to unite the Krishna-Godavari region.
!TRAP Rudradaman – Nanaghat = wrong pair; **Nanaghat = Naganika**.

- End of the first pan-Indian empire → regional kingdoms and Brahmanical revival.
