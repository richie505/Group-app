## U0747 | COAL SECTOR – RESERVES, PRODUCTION, WASHING, COMMERCIAL MINING, GASIFICATION
### 1. COAL TYPES BY CARBON AND AGE
| Type | Carbon | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Anthracite** | 86–97% | **Highest carbon**; hardest, purest, little smoke, highest calorific value |
| **Bituminous** | 45–86% | **Most widely used**; includes **coking** grade |
| Sub-bituminous | 35–45% | Between bituminous and lignite; power plants |
| **Lignite** (brown coal) | 25–35% | **Neyveli** (TN), **Palana** (Rajasthan), **Lakhimpur** (Assam) |
| Peat | Lowest | First stage of wood to coal |
- Order: **anthracite > bituminous > sub-bituminous > lignite > peat**.
- **Gondwana** coal (250–300 million years): **98% of reserves**, 99% of production, holds coking coal. **Tertiary**: younger, NE India and J&K, about **2%**, includes lignite.
!TRAP 60–80% is a bituminous figure, not lignite. Gondwana coal is not Tertiary lignite.

### 3. PRODUCTION AND PRODUCERS
- **2024-25: 1,047.50 MT** – first year above **1 billion tonnes**. 2025-26 target 1,157 MT.
- Coal meets about **55%** of commercial energy needs; about **70%** of power generation.
- **Coal India Ltd**: world's largest coal producer; **7** subsidiaries; **CMPDI** = planning arm.
- **SCCL**: **51% Telangana, 49% GoI** – not a CIL subsidiary.
### 4. COMMERCIAL MINING AND LAWS
- Supreme Court (2014) cancelled **204** coal blocks → **Coal Mines (Special Provisions) Act, 2015** for their auction.
- **MMDR** amended March 2021 for sale of captive coal; another amendment declared **coking coal a Critical and Strategic Mineral** → Centre auctions leases.
- **SHAKTI (2017)**: replaced NCDP-2007; linkages by **auction or tariff-based bidding**.
- **Revenue-sharing commercial auctions** began **2020**; **13th round** opened **21 Aug 2025**; **14th round** agreements (Reliance, Axis Energy) – first with **UCG clauses**.
### 5. PROBLEMS, WASHING AND IMPORTS
- Indian coal: **high ash (35–45%)**, **low calorific value**, **high ash-fusion temperature**, **low sulphur (~0.5%)**.
- Problems: poor quality, **movement bottlenecks**, **low washery use**, rising coking-coal imports, administrative issues.
- Imports: coking coal from **Australia**; non-coking from **Indonesia, South Africa**. Steel industry imports coking coal (India has non-coking coal).
### 6. GASIFICATION, CBM AND UCG
- **Gasification**: coal + oxygen + steam at high temperature → **syngas (CO + H₂ + CH₄ + CO₂)**; runs **IGCC** plants.
- Products: SNG, methanol, **ethanol**, **urea**/ammonia, hydrogen, DME, ammonium nitrate. Ammonia is only downstream.
- **CBM** = "**sweet gas**"; free in joints, adsorbed in micropores.
- **UCG**: in-situ; MoEFCC panel refused to waive the **300 m minimum depth**.
!TRAP UPSC 2025: ethanol and urea can be made; nitroglycerine cannot.
### 7. GASIFICATION MISSION AND PROJECTS
- **National Coal Gasification Mission: 100 MT by 2030**.
- **Surface Coal/Lignite Gasification scheme** (13 May 2026): **₹37,500 crore**; up to **20%** of plant cost, cap **₹5,000 crore**/project; **no bidders** – bid windows every two months, not withdrawn.
- Commercial blocks: **50% revenue-share rebate** if at least **10%** output is gasified.
- **JSPL Angul**: only commercial-scale gasification plant. **Talcher**: first gasification fertiliser plant.
