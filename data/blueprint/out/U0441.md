### 2. MUDRA AND ENTERPRISE CREDIT
#### 2.1 Basics
- **MUDRA** = Micro Units Development and Refinance Agency; **PM MUDRA Yojana** launched **April 2015**.
- Nodal ministry: **Finance** (not MSME); refinance through **SIDBI**.
- Aim: **"fund the unfunded"** – bring small entrepreneurs into the formal financial system; loans to **non-corporate, non-farm micro enterprises**; SC/ST enterprises get priority.
#### 2.2 Loan Categories
| Category | Loan size |
|---|---|
| **Shishu** | Up to **₹50,000** |
| **Kishor** | ₹50,000 – **₹5 lakh** |
| **Tarun** | ₹5 lakh – **₹10 lakh** |
| **Tarun Plus** (from 24 Oct 2024; Budget 2024-25) | ₹10 lakh – **₹20 lakh**, only for those who repaid a Tarun loan |
- MUDRA loans are **collateral-free** up to **₹10 lakh** (all three original slabs; APPSC 2022 key). Since Oct 2024, guarantee cover under **CGFMU** extends to ₹20 lakh.
!TRAP Order Shishu → Kishor → Tarun = small → large.
### 3. INDUSTRY AND MSME PROGRAMMES
| Scheme | Features |
|---|---|
| **Make in India** (2014) | Make India a global manufacturing hub |
| **PLI** | **Central Sector**; **14 sectors** (electronics, pharma, telecom, auto components); now works with **National Manufacturing Mission** (2025-26) |
| **Startup India** (2016) | **Fund of Funds ₹10,000 crore** managed by **SIDBI**; **3-year tax holiday**; exit in **90 days** |
| **Stand-Up India** (2016) | Bank loans to **SC/ST and women** entrepreneurs |
| **National Logistics Policy** (2022) | Cut logistics cost via integrated digital systems |
| **Solar Charkha Mission** (2018, MSME, via **KVIC**) | Solar-powered spinning and weaving clusters for rural artisans |
| **ZED** (**Zero Defect Zero Effect**) | MSME certification for quality and low environmental impact; levels **Bronze, Silver, Gold** |
### 4. REGION-SPECIFIC MISSIONS
- **PM-DevINE**: announced in **Union Budget 2022-23** to close development gaps in the **North East**; Ministry of **DoNER**; **100% central funding**; covers all eight NE States.
- Solar Charkha and ZED (above) are the sector-specific MSME missions.

!TRAP UJALA = LED bulbs; UDAY = DISCOM debt – both Power, 2015.
#### 6.2 Green Hydrogen Mission
- Outlay **₹19,744 crore** to 2029-30.
- 2030 targets: **5 MMTA** green hydrogen, 125 GW RE, ₹8 lakh crore investment, 6 lakh jobs.
- Hydrogen hubs: **Kandla, Paradip, Tuticorin**.
- MNRE's Scheme for New and Novel Uses of Hydrogen funds startup pilots, implemented by **NISE + BIRAC** ("BIRAC sole agency" = FALSE).
#### 6.3 PM-KUSUM Components
| Component | Target |
|---|---|
| A | **10 GW** decentralised solar plants |
| B | **17.5 lakh** standalone solar pumps |
| C | Solarise **10 lakh** grid-connected pumps |
#### 6.4 Energy Data
- About **25 lakh** pumps solarised under PM-KUSUM, 13.94 lakh in FY26 (Aug 2026).
- PM Surya Ghar: 8.7 GW added (2026); AP ranks in the top four in rooftop solar; AP's PM Surya Ghar drive (Aug 2026).
- Renewable capacity: about **40 GW (2015) → about 260 GW (2025)**; installed power capacity **509.74 GW**, renewables near 50% (2025-26).
### 7. DIGITAL PROGRAMMES
#### 7.1 Aadhaar and Digital India
- **Aadhaar**: **12-digit** number (APPSC 2023); started **2009**; **UIDAI** works under the **Aadhaar Act, 2016** (amended **2019**), not NFSA.
- **Digital India** (**1 July 2015**): coordinated by **MeitY**; **nine pillars** – from **Broadband Highways** to **Early Harvest** (incl. universal mobile access, public internet access).
- Central Sector sub-schemes: e-Governance, National Knowledge Network, cyber security, IndiaAI Mission.
#### 7.2 Other Digital Programmes
| Programme | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **BharatNet** | Optical-fibre broadband to **all gram panchayats**; began as **NOFN** |
| **India Semiconductor Mission** (2021) | Incentives for **fabs, display and ATMP** units; MeitY |
| **IndiaAI Mission** | Approved **March 2024**, about **₹10,372 crore**; compute, datasets, AI innovation |
| **ONDC** | Open Network for Digital Commerce; promoted by **DPIIT**; 20 crore buyers, 5 lakh sellers (15 Aug 2026) |
| **PM-WANI** (2020) | Public Wi-Fi through **PDOAs**; **DoT** |
- DPI backbone: BharatNet, 5G, UPI, Aadhaar, DigiYatra, FASTag; nationwide 5G (2025-26); Skill India Digital Hub 20 million+ youth.
- Rural gap: weak fibre upkeep and power cuts at GP level (15 Aug 2026).
