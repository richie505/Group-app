### 2. RISAT SERIES – RADAR SURVEILLANCE
- **RISAT = Radar Imaging Satellite**; carries **Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR)**.
- Images **day and night** and **through clouds** (all-weather) – used by the armed forces for border surveillance.
#### 2.1 Satellite, launcher and band
| Satellite | Year | Launcher | Key fact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RISAT-2** | **20 Apr 2009** | **PSLV-C12** | **X-band**, **Israeli-built** radar reconnaissance; border monitoring, anti-terror, floods; co-passenger **ANUSAT** |
| **RISAT-1** | 2012 | **PSLV-C19** | **C-band (5.35 GHz)** SAR microwave remote sensing; **India's first fully indigenous** radar imaging satellite |
| **RISAT-2B** | May 2019 | **PSLV-C46** | X-band; 2019 **collision avoidance manoeuvre** against a defunct US satellite using **NETRA** data |
| **RISAT-2BR1** | Dec 2019 | **PSLV-C48** | **50th PSLV flight**; with 9 foreign commercial satellites; agriculture, forestry, disaster management |
| **EOS-01 (RISAT-2BR2)** | Nov 2020 | PSLV-C49 | Renamed under the new **EOS** naming |
| **EOS-09 (RISAT-1B)** | 2025 | **PSLV-C61 – failed** | C-band SAR, **1,696 kg**; lost after a **pressure drop in the third-stage** solid motor |
!TRAP RISAT-2 (2009) flew BEFORE RISAT-1 (2012). RISAT-2 = X-band, Israeli; RISAT-1 = C-band, indigenous.
#### 2.2 Latest developments
- **12 Jan 2026:** **PSLV-C62/EOS-N1** failed after a **roll-rate disturbance at the end of the third stage** – two consecutive PSLV failures (C61, C62) hit surveillance satellites.
- **7 Sep 2026:** **EOS-05** – India's first earth-imaging satellite meant for **geosynchronous orbit (~36,000 km)**; floods, cyclones, fires, security events.

### 4. PRIVATE SURVEILLANCE SATELLITE – TSAT-1A
- **TSAT-1A**: built by **Tata Advanced Systems Ltd (TASL)** with **Satellogic**; assembled at TASL's **Vemagal (Karnataka)** facility.
- **India's first private-sector-built sub-metre optical** (military-grade imaging) satellite.
- Launched **7 Apr 2024** on a **SpaceX Falcon 9** (Bandwagon-1 mission), USA.
!TRAP TSAT-1A went up on SpaceX Falcon 9, not on PSLV.
### 5. EDUSAT (GSAT-3) – EDUCATION SATELLITE
- **GSAT-3 = EDUSAT**: **India's first satellite dedicated to education**.
- Launched **20 September 2004** on **GSLV-F01** from SHAR, **Sriharikota**.
- **Two-way satellite communication** to classrooms; users: schools, colleges, distance-education networks (**virtual classrooms**).
!KEY EDUSAT = GSAT-3 = GSLV-F01 = 20 Sep 2004.
### 6. STUDENT AND ACADEMIC SATELLITES
| Satellite | Built by | Launch / FIRST |
|---|---|---|
| **ANUSAT** | **Anna University**, Chennai | 2009, **PSLV-C12** (with RISAT-2); experimental communication; **first Indian university satellite** |
| **StudSat** | Consortium of **7 engineering colleges of Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh** (undivided) | 2010, PSLV-C15; **India's first pico-satellite** |
| **Jugnu** | **IIT Kanpur** | 2011, **PSLV-C18**; nano-satellite |
| **SRMSAT** | **SRM University**, Chennai | 2011, **PSLV-C18** |
| **SATYABAMASAT** | **Sathyabama University**, Chennai | **22 June 2016, PSLV-C34** with **Cartosat-2C**; micro experimental; **greenhouse-gas** data |
| **Kalamsat-V2** | **Space Kidz India** | Jan 2019, PSLV-C44; **first to use PS4 stage as orbital platform**; lightest |
| **AzaadiSAT** | Space Kidz India with **750 girl students** of government schools | **Aug 2022, SSLV-D1** – **failed** to reach orbit |
| **WESAT** | **LBS Institute of Technology for Women**, Thiruvananthapuram | **First satellite fully built by women students**; PSLV-C58 **POEM-3** (Jan 2024); solar UV |
- **StudSat, ANUSAT, Jugnu** = Indian student-built nano-satellites.
!TRAP **GSAT-1** is NOT a student satellite – a full-scale ISRO communication satellite, first GSLV payload (2001).
!PAIR AzaadiSAT | girl students | failed (SSLV-D1) || WESAT | women students | flew (POEM-3)
- **AP angle:** StudSat consortium included AP colleges; all these satellites flew from Sriharikota.
### 7. FOREIGN CO-PASSENGERS ON PSLV
- **PSLV-C37 (15 Feb 2017, PSLV's 39th flight):** record **104 satellites** in one launch – **714 kg Cartosat-2 series** + **2 ISRO nanosats** + **101 foreign nanosats from 6 countries** (103 co-passengers); 1,378 kg total; 506 km SSO.
- **Over 400 foreign satellites** launched on Indian rockets (1 Jan 2026).
- **NSIL:** 141 satellites, incl. 138 international/customer (July 2026).
- **PSLV-C62** (64th PSLV flight; 105th Sriharikota launch; 9th dedicated NSIL mission) carried **15 co-passengers** from Indian and foreign start-ups and academic institutions, incl. a **KID re-entry capsule**.
