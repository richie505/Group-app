### 6. ELECTRONICS AND SEMICONDUCTORS
- **India Semiconductor Mission**: 2021; **Semicon 2.0** cleared by Cabinet (16 Jul 2026).
- **Dholera** = fab (not Kia); **Sanand** = Micron ATMP; **Jagiroad** (Assam) = Tata ₹27,000-crore plant (Mar 2024).
- Electronics PLI grew out of **National Policy on Electronics 2019** (4–6% incentive). EMC 2.0: greenfield 50%, brownfield 75% of cost.
- Mobile phones: production ~US$75 bn, exports ~US$30 bn; mobile scheme ~60,000 jobs (16 Jul 2026).
- AP electronics (2024-29): US$50 bn production target, 5 lakh jobs.
### 7. IT AND SOFTWARE
- IT is **footloose**: pulled by engineering talent and tech ecosystem, not raw materials.
- Clusters: Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Noida. **STPI** set up **1991**.
- **Bengaluru = "Silicon Valley of India"**; **Karnataka = "Silicon State"**; **Hyderabad = "Cyberabad"**.
- **Karnataka** = largest software exporter (over a third of India's software exports).
### 8. PHARMACEUTICALS
- India = "**Pharmacy of the World**": **3rd by volume**, **11th by export value**.
- Hubs: Hyderabad (bulk drugs), Ahmedabad-Vadodara, **Parawada Pharma City** (Visakhapatnam).
- **Bulk Drug Parks**: AP, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh; 26 critical raw materials commercialised.
