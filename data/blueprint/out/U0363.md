### 2. CITIZENS' CHARTER
#### 2.1 Purpose and Principles
- A **written commitment** on service standards; main purpose = **time-bound service delivery and grievance redress** (APPSC 2025 key).
- **Six principles**: **quality, choice, standards, value, accountability, transparency**.
- **Participation is NOT** one of the six principles.
#### 2.2 Basic Elements
- Clear **service standards**; **information** to citizens; **grievance redress** mechanism; contact details of officers.
- **What it is not**: a legally enforceable right – charters in India are **non-justiciable**.
#### 2.3 Adoption in India
- Adopted **1997**; **DARPG** coordinates formulation; nodal officers in every ministry; website **goicharters.nic.in**.
- **Citizens Charter Bill, 2011** (time-bound delivery + grievance redress) never became law; **lapsed** with dissolution of Lok Sabha in **2014**.
- **Second ARC** recommended **statutory status** for charters.
!NUM UK 1991 → India 1997 → Bill 2011 → lapsed 2014
### 3. SEVOTTAM MODEL
- **Sevottam = Seva + Uttam** = excellence in service.
- Suggested in the **Second ARC's 12th Report**; promoted by **DARPG**; implemented from **2009**.
| Module | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1. **Citizens' Charter** | Publish service standards |
| 2. **Public Grievance Redress** | Receive, track and resolve complaints |
| 3. **Service Delivery Capability** | Build organisational capacity |
- Certification: Sevottam-compliant certification follows **IS 15700:2005**.
!KEY Sevottam = 3 modules; standard IS 15700:2005.
### 4. SECOND ARC 12TH REPORT (2009)
- Title: **"Citizen Centric Administration – The Heart of Governance"**; Second ARC set up **2005**, chairman **Veerappa Moily**.
- Key recommendations: **Sevottam**; charters with **statutory backing**; strong grievance redress; **social audits** institutionalised in all major welfare schemes (beyond MGNREGA); e-governance; **right to public services** laws.
### 5. RIGHT TO SERVICES ACTS
- Notified services within a **fixed time**; **appeals** against delay; **penalties** on officials.
- They **do not abolish** other grievance mechanisms.
- Order of enactment (UKPSC 2025 key): **Punjab (earliest) → Assam → Gujarat → Maharashtra**.
- **AP**: **AP Public Services Delivery Guarantee Act, 2017** (Act 6 of 2018), in force from **18 Oct 2017**; time-bound delivery of notified services with appeal and penalty.
### 6. CPGRAMS
- **Centralised Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System**; launched **2007** by **DARPG**.
- DARPG is under the **Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions** (not Home Affairs).
- Access **24x7** via web, app and **UMANG**; grievances also through **5.1 lakh CSCs**.
- **Disposal time limit**: DARPG circular of **23 Aug 2024** cut the maximum from **30 days to 21 days**.
- **GRAI** (Grievance Redressal Assessment & Index): **11 indicators, 4 dimensions** – **efficiency, feedback, domain, organisational commitment** (**transparency is NOT** one).
- Pensioners: separate platform **CPENGRAMS**.
- Pendency: over **71,000** grievances pending on CPGRAMS (Sep 2025).
!TRAP Disposal limit 30 → 21 days (23 Aug 2024).
### 7. AP GRIEVANCE PLATFORMS
| Platform | Period / government | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Prajavani** | Earlier AP initiative | Web-based online receipt, reply and tracking of grievances at **District Collectorates** (APPSC 2018 key) |
| **Meekosam** ("For You") | **2015** (TDP government) | **Single-window** portal to lodge grievances/suggestions and track status; data analytics added (Jan 2026) |
| **Spandana** | **2019** (YSRCP government) | **One-stop** grievance redressal platform (APPSC 2021 key) |
| People First | – | Citizen feedback system; a distractor in APPSC 2018 |
!TRAP APPSC 2018 keyed **Prajavani**; APPSC 2021 keyed **Spandana**.
### 8. SOCIAL AUDIT
- **MGNREGA 2005, Section 17**: **Gram Sabha** conducts social audit of all works.
- Social audit = process to raise **accountability** in MGNREGA; communities check funds and works (APPSC 2018).
- **AP SSAAT** (Society for Social Audit, Accountability and Transparency): set up **2009** as an independent society.
### 9. LOKPAL, LOKAYUKTA AND OMBUDSMAN – COMPARED
| Mechanism | Deals with | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| **CPGRAMS / state portals** | Service-delivery grievances | Executive, time-bound disposal |
| **Lokpal / Lokayukta** | **Corruption** allegations against public functionaries | Statutory (**Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act 2013**) |
| **Classic ombudsman** | **Maladministration** | Independent officer of the **legislature** |
| **Social audit** | Scheme works and funds | Community/Gram Sabha |
- Criticism: charters **lack legal force**; grievance **pendency**.
!PAIR Lokpal | Corruption | Statutory || CPGRAMS | Service grievances | Executive
