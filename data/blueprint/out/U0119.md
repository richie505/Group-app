## U0119 | THE FIVE DECCAN SULTANATES – BIJAPUR, GOLCONDA, AHMEDNAGAR, BIDAR, BERAR; GOLCONDA AND ANDHRA
### 1. THE FIVE DECCAN SULTANATES
- The **Bahmani** kingdom split into **five** successor states.
| Dynasty | Capital | Founder, year | End |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Adil Shahi** | **Bijapur** | **Yusuf Adil Shah**, **1489** (first to break away) | **Aurangzeb, 1686** |
| **Nizam Shahi** | **Ahmednagar** | **Malik Ahmad** (Ahmad Nizam Shah), 1490 – founded Ahmadnagar city | **Shah Jahan, 1633** |
| **Imad Shahi** | **Berar** (capital **Ellichpur / Achalpur**) | **Fathullah Imad-ul-Mulk**, 1490 | Annexed by **Ahmadnagar, 1574** |
| **Barid Shahi** | **Bidar** | **Amir Ali Barid** – "**cunning fox of the Deccan**" | Bijapur, 1619 |
| **Qutb Shahi** | **Golconda**, later Hyderabad | **Sultan Quli (Quli Qutb Shah)**, **1518** | **Aurangzeb, 1687** |
!KEY Bijapur = first to break away (1489); Golconda = last to break away (1518) and last to fall (1687).
!TRAP "Berar was founded by" = **Imad Shahis** (APPSC G2 2005).
### 2. QUTB SHAHI DYNASTY – BASICS AND RULERS
- **1518–1687**, **7 rulers**, **Shia**; **Qara Qoyunlu (Black Sheep) Turkmen** from **Hamadan**; official language **Persian**.
| Ruler | Key contribution |
|---|---|
| **Sultan Quli Qutb-ul-Mulk (1518–43)** | Bahmani **tarafdar of Telangana** from 1496; title Qutb-ul-Mulk named the dynasty; independent 1518; ruled **31 years** (counted from 1512); defeated by **Krishnadevaraya** at Goni (1517) / Battle of Golconda; strengthened **Golconda fort**, built Golconda Jama Masjid; **killed by son Jamsheed** |
| **Jamsheed (1543–50)** | Took throne by patricide; won Battle of Koulan over Bidar (1544); created **cabinet – Wakil, Peshwa, Mir Jumla** |
| **Ibrahim Qutb Shah (1550–80)** | **7 years exile at Vijayanagara**; title **Malkibharama** (from subjects for tolerant rule); chief Telugu patron; led **Talikota (1565)** alliance; **Hussain Sagar (1562)**; **first Qutb Shahi coins**; built **high walls of Golconda**; Golconda called "Second Egypt" |
| **Muhammad Quli (1580–1612)** | Founded **Hyderabad (1591)**; **Charminar**, **Dar-ul-Shifa (1595)**, Jama Masjid (1598); Dakhni poet **"Maani"** (*Kulliyat-e-Quli Qutb Shah*); let **Dutch (1605)** and **English (1611)** trade at Machilipatnam |
| **Muhammad Qutb Shah (1612–26)** | Nephew and son-in-law of Muhammad Quli; began **Mecca Masjid (1614)**; pen name **Zillullah** |
| **Abdullah Qutb Shah (1626–72)** | **Longest reign**; with mother **Hayat Bakshi Begum**; **Inqiyad Nama (1636)** – accepted Mughal suzerainty; **Bangaru Farmana (1636)** to the English; **Kshetrayya**; **Tavernier** visited; Urdu peaked |
| **Abul Hasan Tana Shah (1672–87)** | **Last** ruler; relied on **Madanna-Akkanna**; **treaty with Shivaji (1677)**; renamed Peshwa as **Diwan** |
!PAIR Maani | Muhammad Quli | Dakhni poet || Zillullah | Muhammad Qutb Shah | Mecca Masjid
!TRAP Coinage does not explain the title Malkibharama – it came from the subjects for tolerant rule.
### 3. ANDHRA UNDER GOLCONDA – CONQUESTS AND SOCIETY
- **Sultan Quli** took **Rachakonda, Devarakonda, Warangal** (Shitab Khan), **Rajahmundry**, Eluru, Kovilakonda.
- **Ibrahim** gained **Kondavidu** and Machilipatnam after 1565; annexed coastal Andhra (1570–78).
- Qutb Shahis united the Telugu lands for the **third time** (after **Satavahanas** and **Kakatiyas**).
- Hindus in high office: **Murari Rao, Jagadeva Rao, Madanna**.
- Muharram celebrated in Andhra as **Peerla Panduga**.
- **Bonalu** began at **Jagadamba Mahankali temple**, Golconda fort; roots in Kakatiya and Qutb Shahi periods.
### 4. QUTB SHAHI ADMINISTRATION
- Hierarchy: **Sultan → Taraf → Sarkar → Pargana → Village**.
| Official | Duty |
|---|---|
| **Peshwa / Wakil** | Prime minister; renamed **Diwan** under Abul Hasan (held by Madanna) |
| **Mir Jumla** (Jumlat-ul-Mulk) | **Finance minister** |
| Ain-ul-Mulk | Military department |
| **Tarafdar** | Governor of a **Taraf**: law and order, revenue, troops |
| **Faujdar** | Head of a **Sarkar** |
| **Tahsildar / Deshmukh** | Head of a **Pargana** |
| **Mukaddam** / Kulkarni | Village head / accountant |
| **Kotwal** | City police; also ran the mint |
| **Mahasibs / Mustasibs** | **Public morals** (like Mughal Muhtasib) |
| **Shahbandar** | **Chief port officer**, customs |
| Majumdar / Dabir | Auditor / secretary |
| **Kayastha** | **Scribes** (APPSC G2 2012) |
- Abdullah: **6 Tarafs**; Abul Hasan: **37 Sarkars**, **517 Parganas**.
- Justice: Sultan → **Shariat Panch** (chief justice) → **Qazi** → **Pargana Majlis**.
- **Sar Lashkar** = army chief (Akkanna).
!NUM 6 Tarafs – 37 Sarkars – 517 Parganas = Tarafdar – Faujdar – Tahsildar
### 5. REVENUE
- **Main source: land revenue**, collected by **tax farming** – rights auctioned to the highest bidder.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Mustajirs** | Tax farmers (highest bidders, mostly Hindus); could be harsh |
| **Haveli** | Crown lands |
| **Dasabandhu** lands | Paid only **one-tenth** |
- Revenue farming example: **Machilipatnam** leased for **1,80,000 pagodas** a year.
- Other sources: trade taxes, port customs, taxes on courtesans and professions (military service did not replace taxes).
- **State monopolies: salt, betel leaf, tobacco**.
### 6. MADANNA AND AKKANNA
| Angle | Madanna | Akkanna |
|---|---|---|
| Office | **Mir Jumla / Diwan** (prime minister) | **Sar Lashkar** (army chief) |
| Titles | **Surya Prakasa Rao**, Alampana | – |
- **Pingali Brahmin** brothers from **Hanamkonda**, under Tana Shah.
- Nephews: **Podili Linganna** – governor of the Karnatak; **Kancherla Gopanna (Bhakta Ramadasu)** – tahsildar of **Palvancha/Bhadrachalam**.
- **Murdered 1686**, just before Golconda fell (1687).
### 7. HYDERABAD, GOLCONDA AND BIJAPUR MONUMENTS
- Capital shifted from Golconda (**1592**); planned by **Peshwa Mir Momin Astarabadi**.
| Monument | Founder / feature |
|---|---|
| **Hyderabad** | **Muhammad Quli, 1591**; original name **Bhagyanagar** (legend: Bhagmati) |
| **Charminar** | **Muhammad Quli, 1591** (foundation 8 Feb 1591); end of a **plague**; **Char Kaman** gateways |
| **Mecca Masjid** | Begun **1614** by **Muhammad Qutb Shah**; completed by **Aurangzeb (1692–93)** |
| **Golconda fort** | Old names **Mankal / Mangalavaram**; handclap at gate heard at **Bala Hisar**; piped water |
| **Qutb Shahi tombs** (Ibrahim Bagh) | Onion domes on square bases |
| **Hussain Sagar** | Named after **Hussain Shah Wali**; under Ibrahim (1562); UNWTO "**Heart of the World**" (2012) |
| **Gol Gumbaz** (Bijapur) | Tomb of **Muhammad Adil Shah** (17th c.); **whispering gallery**; one of the world's largest domes; mosque in its complex |
| **Ibrahim Rauza** | Tomb of **Ibrahim Adil Shah II** |
| **Malik-e-Maidan** (Bijapur) | A **cannon** |
!TRAP Mecca Masjid begun by Muhammad Qutb Shah, completed by Aurangzeb; Charminar = Muhammad Quli.
### 8. DIAMONDS AND MACHILIPATNAM TRADE
| Mine | Region |
|---|---|
| **Kollur** | On the **Krishna** (Guntur / Palnadu) |
| Paritala | Krishna |
- Diamonds traded at **Moti Darwaza**, polished at **Karwan**.
- **Kohinoor**: from **Kollur** (APPSC G2 2011), found **1656**; given to **Shah Jahan** by **Mir Jumla** (minister of Abdullah); **Tavernier only recorded it**.
- **Tavernier**: **French** jeweller; visited the mines under **Abdullah Qutb Shah** (APPSC G2 2019); wrote *Six Travels*.
| Company | Machilipatnam factory |
|---|---|
| **Dutch** | **1605** – first European factory in Andhra |
| **English** | **1611** – **Captain Hippon**, ship **Globe**; permission of **Muhammad Quli** |
- Machilipatnam famous for **muslin** and **Kalamkari**.
- Latest: Machilipatnam port works revive the old **Gilakaladindi** port (Golconda-era hub); **Golconda Blue** diamond (**23.24 ct**, fancy vivid blue, pear-shaped) in news **May 2025**.
!TRAP English arrival = **Muhammad Quli** (APPSC 2025); if he is not an option, choose Ibrahim (older G2 2008 key).

### 10. BIJAPUR AND AHMEDNAGAR
#### 10.1 Ibrahim Adil Shah II (Bijapur, 1580–1627)
- Called **"Jagadguru"** for his mixed-faith outlook; supported Hindu scholars.
- ***Kitab-i-Nauras*** (Book of Nine Rasas) in **Dakhni** – songs to **Saraswati, Ganesha** and Muslim saints; **59 songs, 17 couplets**.
- Founded **Nauraspur**; **Firishta** dedicated his work to him; married **Chand Sultana** (Muhammad Quli's sister).
#### 10.2 Chand Bibi and Malik Ambar (Ahmednagar)
- **Chand Bibi**: daughter of **Hussain Nizam Shah I**; married **Ali Adil Shah** → anti-Vijayanagara alliance; defended Ahmadnagar against Mughals (1595–96); died when it fell (**1600**).
- **Malik Ambar**: **Abyssinian** regent; fought **Jahangir**; revenue on **Todar Mal's lines** – **survey, fixed assessment, no revenue farming**; founded **Khirki → Aurangabad**; son Fath Khan made peace.
!TRAP **Chand Sultana** (wife of Ibrahim Adil Shah II) is NOT **Chand Bibi** (wife of Ali Adil Shah).
### 11. MUGHAL ANNEXATION AND FALL OF GOLCONDA
- **Talikota (1565)**: four of the five – **all except Berar** – defeated Vijayanagara.
| Emperor | Action |
|---|---|
| **Akbar** | Ahmadnagar city (**1600**); Asirgarh/Khandesh (1601) |
| **Shah Jahan** | Ended **Nizam Shahis (1633)** – last ruler Husain Shah (Nizam Shah III) jailed at **Gwalior**; **1636** ahdnamas with Bijapur and Golconda; Mahawat Khan took Daulatabad (Fateh Khan bribed 10.5 lakh) |
| **Aurangzeb** | Attacked Golconda (1656) as subedar; annexed **Bijapur (1686)**, **Golconda (1687)** |
- **21 September 1687**: traitor **Abdullah Khan Pani** opened the gates; **Abdur Razzaq Lari** honoured for the defence.
- **Abul Hasan** imprisoned at Bidar, then **Daulatabad**; died **1699**; buried at **Khuldabad**.
- Golconda became the **21st Mughal Suba**.
!NUM 1600 Ahmadnagar city – 1633 Nizam Shahi – 1686 Bijapur – 1687 Golconda
