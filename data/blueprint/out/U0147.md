## U0147 | SUFISM - DOCTRINES, KHANQAH, SAMA; SILSILAS - CHISHTI, SUHRAWARDI, QADIRI, NAQSHBANDI
### 1. MEANING, ORIGIN AND FIRSTS
- **Sufism** = Islamic mysticism. "Sufi" from **suf** (coarse wool); also **safa** (purity) or Greek **sophia**.
- Sufis accepted the **Quran and the Prophet** but absorbed **Vedanta, Yoga and Neo-Platonism**. Sufism gave Islam a tolerant, vernacular face; khanqahs were welfare centres.
- **Ulema** = scholars of Islamic religious law; not part of Sufism and generally did not sanction music.
- First prominent Sufi in India: **Ali Hujwiri (Data Ganj Bakhsh)**, author of **Kashf-ul-Mahjub** (love and knowledge of God, fana).
- Most popular order: **Chishti**. Last great Chishti of Delhi: **Nasiruddin Chiragh-i-Dehlavi**.
### 2. SUFI TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Khanqah** | Sufi hospice where disciples lived and trained; gave food and shelter to the poor |
| **Sama** | Musical-spiritual assembly to produce ecstasy; **raqs** = ritual dance |
| **Pir / Murshid / Shaikh** | Master, authorised guide |
| **Murid** | Disciple in full submission; the bond = **Piri-Muridi** |
| **Wali / Auliya** | Friend(s) of God, saint(s); Auliya is a title, not an order |
| **Dargah** | Shrine at a saint's tomb |
| **Urs** | Saint's death anniversary = union with God (e.g. Urs of Moinuddin Chishti at Ajmer) |
| **Fana / Baqa** | Annihilation of the self in God / subsistence in God |
| **Tariqa / Silsila** | Path / order |
| **Malfuzat / Maktubat / Tazkiras** | Conversations / letters / biographies |
- **SufiKalam** (devotional music) is typical of **Kashmir**.
!TRAP Malfuzat = conversations; Maktubat = letters; Tazkiras = biographies (not anti-Shia tracts).
### 3. DOCTRINES AND STAGES
| Doctrine | Meaning | Linked to |
|---|---|---|
| **Wahdat-ul-Wujud** | Unity of Being: **Haq** (Creator) and **Khalq** (creation) are one | **Ibn Arabi**; Qadiris and Chishtis |
| **Wahdat-ush-Shuhud** | Unity of Appearance/Witness: creation only reflects God | **Shaikh Ahmad Sirhindi** (Naqshbandi) |
- **10 maqamat (stages)**: first **Tauba** (repentance) → Wara, Zuhd, Faqr, Sabr, Shukr, Khauf, Raja, Tawakkul → last **Riza** (submission).
### 4. PRACTICES AND YOGIC BORROWINGS
- **Zikr** (remembrance of God), meditation, fasting, asceticism, sama, solitude or retreat (**chilla**).
- **Amritakund**, a Hatha-yogic text, translated into Arabic and Persian (13th c.); after it **breath control** became key in almost every order **except the Naqshbandis**.
- **Alberuni** compared Sufi ideas with **Patanjali's Yoga Sutra** and the **Gita**.
### 5. BA-SHARA VS BE-SHARA
!PAIR Ba-shara | Follow Sharia | Chishti, Suhrawardi, Qadiri, Naqshbandi, Firdausi || Be-shara | Outside Sharia | Wandering Qalandars, Madaris
### 6. SILSILAS - FOUNDERS, SAINTS, REGIONS
| Silsila | Founder / in India | Major saints | Region | Stance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Chishti** | **Abu Ishaq Shami** (of Syria) with disciple Abu Ahmad Abdal at **Chisht (Afghanistan)**; in India **Moinuddin Chishti** | Bakhtiyar Kaki, Baba Farid, Nizamuddin, Nasiruddin, Burhanuddin Gharib, Gesudaraz, Hamiduddin of Nagaur | Delhi-Ajmer; later Deccan | Liberal; aloof from court; sama, qawwali |
| **Suhrawardi** | **Shihabuddin Suhrawardi** (Baghdad); in India **Bahauddin Zakariya** | Sadruddin Arif, Ruknuddin Abul Fath, Jalaluddin Surkh, Jalaluddin Tabrizi, Qazi Hamiduddin Nagori | **Sindh, Multan, Punjab** | Close to rulers; royal service |
| **Firdausi** | Branch of the Suhrawardi order; **Sharfuddin Yahya Maneri** | — | **Bihar** (founded there) | Ba-shara |
| **Qadiri** | **Abdul Qadir Jilani** (Baghdad) | **Miyan Mir**, Muhibullah Shah, **Dara Shikoh** | Punjab, Sindh | Backed Wahdat-ul-Wujud |
| **Naqshbandi** | **Bahauddin Naqshband** (Central Asia, 14th c.); in India **Khwaja Baqi Billah** (late 16th c.) | **Ahmad Sirhindi**, Shah Waliullah, Shah Abdul Aziz, **Khwaja Khawand** (Kashmir centre) | Delhi, North India | Most orthodox; against music |
| **Shattari** | **Shah Abdullah Shattari** (15th c.) | **Shah Muhammad Ghaus** of Gwalior | Mandu, Gwalior | Liberal; yogic practices |
- **Chishti** name comes from **Chisht, a town in Afghanistan**, not a village in Ajmer.
- **Moinuddin Chishti** came with **Muhammad Ghori's** army (**1192**); first Chishti saint in India, earliest Sufi at **Ajmer**; held sama a way to come close to God.
- **Muhammad Ghaus**: saw Krishna as an Auliya; close to **Humayun**, **Tansen**; wrote **Jawahir-e-Khamsa**.
!TRAP Bahauddin Naqshband never came to India (revered by Babur); Baqi Billah brought the order.
!TRAP Abdul Qadir Jilani is NOT a Chishti; he founded the Qadiri order (RPSC 2016).
### 7. ATTITUDE TO STATE, SAMA AND ORTHODOXY
| Silsila | State | Sama | Orthodoxy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chishti | Early phase avoided court and state grants; later Deccan Chishtis took grants | Encouraged | Liberal; used local languages |
| Suhrawardi | Accepted wealth, posts; **Bahauddin Zakariya** backed **Iltutmish** against **Qabacha** → title **Shaikh-ul-Islam** | Sama and raqs | Pragmatic |
| Naqshbandi | — | **Opposed** sama and tomb visits | **Most orthodox** (JPSC 2016); condemned innovations (bid'at) |
- **Ahmad Sirhindi** - **Mujaddid Alf-i-Sani** (reviver of the second Islamic millennium): criticised **Akbar's Sulh-i-Kul** and Shia influence; **jailed by Jahangir**.
- **Nizamuddin Auliya** saw the reign of **seven** or more Sultans but **never visited their court**.

- Baba Farid's disciples: **Nizamuddin Auliya** and **Alauddin Sabir (Kaliyari)**.
- **Amir Khusrau**, disciple of Nizamuddin, introduced **Qaul**, the opening hymn of **Qawwali**.
!PAIR Ali Hujwiri | Data Ganj Bakhsh | First prominent Sufi || Ahmad Sirhindi | Mujaddid Alf-i-Sani | Naqshbandi
!TRAP Nizamuddin lived at Ajodhan and Delhi, not Multan. Two Hamiduddins: Hamiduddin of Nagaur = Chishti (Moinuddin's disciple); Qazi Hamiduddin Nagori = Suhrawardi.
