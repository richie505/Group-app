## U0620 | WORLD – CONTINENTS, MAJOR MOUNTAIN SYSTEMS, PLATEAUS, PLAINS AND DESERTS
### 1. CONTINENTS
#### 1.1 Continents by Area
- Descending: **Asia > Africa > North America > South America > Antarctica > Europe > Australia**.
| Continent | Area (sq km) | Share of land | Countries |
|---|---|---|---|
| Asia | **44,568,500** (largest) | 29.8% | 48 |
| Africa | 30,065,000 | 20.3% | **54 (most)** |
| North America | 24,473,000 | 16.2% | 23 |
| South America | — | 11.8% | 12 |
| Antarctica | — | 9.2% | — |
| Europe | 9,948,000 | 6.8% | 49 |
| Australia / Oceania | — | 5.9% | 14 |
- Two smallest continents: **Australia** (smallest) and **Europe**. **Antarctica is bigger than Europe and Australia**.
- **Australia**: maximum per-capita land (World Bank). **Europe**: highest percentage of plains.
- **Antarctica**: **highest mean elevation** (about **2,300 m**) due to an ice sheet over 98% of it; Asia 923 m, North America 760 m, Africa 585 m, South America 554 m.
#### 1.2 Gondwana and Antarctica
- **Gondwana**: Antarctica, South America, Africa, Madagascar, Australia, Arabian Peninsula, Peninsular India – **North America was NOT** part of it.
- Canada's **RADARSAT-1**: first detailed, complete map of Antarctica (**1997**).
- **Taylor Valley** (Antarctica): discovered by **Robert Falcon Scott (1903)**, "Valley of the Dead".
### 2. COUNTRIES – AREA, BORDERS AND COASTS
#### 2.1 Largest Countries
1. **Russia** 2. Canada 3. **USA** 4. China 5. Brazil 6. Australia 7. **India**
- India: about **2.4%** of world land; first by population (2023). **France** is not larger than India.
- Ascending: Argentina (2,796,427 sq km) < India (3,287,263) < Australia (7,692,024) < Brazil (8,510,417).
#### 2.2 Borders
- **Canada–USA**: longest international border (**8,891 km**); a large stretch follows the **49th Parallel**. Russia–Kazakhstan 6,846 km; Chile–Argentina 5,308 km.
- **Alaska**: bought by the USA from the Russian Empire (treaty **30 March 1867**).
- **Galapagos Islands**: volcanic, part of **Ecuador** (not Peru).
- **Luxembourg**: landlocked, only sovereign Grand Duchy; borders Belgium, Germany, France (Sep 2026).
- **Namibia** (capital Windhoek): borders Angola, Zambia, Botswana, South Africa and the Atlantic (Aug 2026).
#### 2.3 South America
- Pacific coast: **Chile, Peru, Ecuador, Colombia** – NOT Venezuela (Caribbean/Atlantic side).
- Landlocked: **Bolivia and Paraguay**; Peru, Suriname, Uruguay have coastlines. **North America has no landlocked country**.
- **Tropic of Capricorn**: Brazil, Paraguay, Argentina, Chile, Australia, Madagascar, Mozambique, South Africa, Botswana, Namibia, French Polynesia, Tonga – **NOT Bolivia**.

### 3. MOUNTAINS – DEFINITIONS, TYPES AND LONGEST RANGES
- **Mountain**: steep elevation above **600 m**; **plains**: generally below **200 m**, mostly river-formed and thickly populated.
| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Young fold (Alpine) | Himalayas, **Alps, Andes, Rockies, Atlas** |
| Old fold | **Appalachians** (N. America), **Urals** |
| Block mountain | **Sierra Nevada** (California) |
#### 3.1 Longest Ranges
| Rank | Range | Length | Location | Highest peak |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Andes** | ~7,000 km (7,000–8,900) | Western (Pacific) South America | Aconcagua 6,961 m |
| 2 | Rockies | ~4,800 km | Canada to USA (New Mexico) | Mount Elbert 4,401 m |
| 3 | Great Dividing Range | ~3,500 km | Eastern Australia | Kosciuszko 2,228 m |
| 4 | Himalayas | ~2,400 km | India, Nepal, Bhutan, China, Pakistan | Everest 8,849 m |
- Longest continental range = **Andes**; highest = **Himalayas**.
- Oldest ranges: **Appalachians** (N. America), **Urals** (Europe-Asia); oldest in India: **Aravalli**, highest point **Gurushikhar 1,722 m**.
!TRAP Andes are in South America, not North America. Great Dividing Range is in Australia, not Africa.

### 5. HIGHEST PEAK OF EACH CONTINENT
| Continent | Peak | Country |
|---|---|---|
| Asia | **Everest** (8,849 m) | Himalayas |
| South America | **Aconcagua** (6,961 m) – highest outside Asia; non-volcanic | Argentina |
| North America | **Denali** (Mount McKinley) | USA (Alaska) |
| Africa | **Kilimanjaro** (5,895 m) | Tanzania |
| Europe | **Elbrus** (5,642 m) | Russia (Caucasus) |
| Antarctica | **Vinson Massif** | — |
| Australia / Oceania | **Kosciuszko** (~2,228 m) / Puncak Jaya | Australia / Indonesia |
- **Kilimanjaro**: dormant volcano; highest **free-standing** mountain; cones **Kibo, Mawenzi, Shira**; highest point **Uhuru** (formerly Kaiser-Wilhelm-Spitze).
- Vesuvius (near Naples) and Etna (Sicily): active volcanoes.
- **Seven Summits**: first completed by **Richard Bass (1985)**; Bass list has Kosciuszko, Messner list has Puncak Jaya.
!TRAP Mont Blanc is the highest of the Alps / Western Europe only; Europe's highest is Elbrus.
!KEY An APPSC key once gave Mt. Bruce as Australia's highest peak; Mount Kosciuszko is generally taken as mainland Australia's highest.
### 6. PLATEAUS
| Type | Nature | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Intermontane** | Enclosed by mountains | **Tibetan, Pamir, Bolivian (Altiplano), Mongolian**, Peru, Colombia |
| **Piedmont** | At mountain foot | Piedmont (USA), **Patagonia** (foot of the Andes) |
| **Continental** | Broad elevated surfaces | Peninsular, **African, Arabian**, Australian |
| **Volcanic / lava** | Lava accumulation | **Deccan** (one of the oldest), **Columbia** (NW USA) |
| **Fluvial** | River deposition | **Panna, Bhander, Rewa, Rohtas** (Vindhyan region) |
- **Tibetan**: highest and largest plateau; average **4,500 m**; 2.5 million sq km.
- **Pamir**: "**Roof of the World**"; southern border of Central Asia, meeting point of many ranges.
- **Meseta**: Iberian Peninsula (Spain). **Anatolian**: Turkiye, between Pontic and Taurus ranges.
- **Colorado**: USA, cut by the Grand Canyon; largest of Colorado (150,580 sq miles), Columbia (105,633), Coconino (5,000), Aquarius (900).
- **Patagonia**: "storehouse of minerals" of South America. **East African Plateau**: Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda.
- **Tsaidam Basin**: between Kunlun and Altyn-Tagh, Qinghai (China); rich in minerals and salt.
### 7. PLAINS AND BASINS
- **Great Plains**: east of the Rockies (USA, Canada). **North European Plain**.
- **West Siberian Plain**: between the **Urals** (west) and **Yenisei** (east).
- **Indo-Gangetic Plain**: **6,52,000 sq km**; over 2,400 km long, 70–500 km wide; aggradational; **youngest** physiographic division of India.
- **Great Basin** (USA): between the Rockies and the Sierra Nevada; Columbia Plateau north, Mojave and Sonoran south.
- APPSC key: upland between the **Brooks Range and Alaska Range** = "Great Basin" (Yukon-Tanana Upland area).
- **Great Artesian Basin** (Australia): one of the largest underground water reservoirs (~1.7 million sq km), near the Great Dividing Range.
