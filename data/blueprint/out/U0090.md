## U0090 | CHOLA ART – BRIHADESHWARA TEMPLE, DRAVIDA STYLE, CHOLA BRONZES (NATARAJA), TAMIL LITERATURE
### 1. DRAVIDA STYLE AND TEMPLE TERMS
- Shift: rock-cut caves → massive **structural temples**; peak of Dravida style, continued under Kalyani Chalukyas and Hoysalas.
- "The **Pallavas laid the foundation**, the **Cholas built the cathedral**" of Dravidian art.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Garbhagriha** | Womb-house with principal icon |
| **Vimana** | Pyramidal tower over garbhagriha |
| **Shikhara** | Dome-like crown of the vimana (monolithic at Thanjavur) |
| **Antarala** | Vestibule between sanctum and mandapa |
| **Mandapa** | Pillared hall |
| **Jagati** / **Prakara** | Raised platform / enclosure walls |
| **Gopuram** | Many-storeyed entrance gateway; origins in Pallava structures (BPSC 2017) |
| Style | Tower | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Nagara** (north, from c. 5th c.) | Curvilinear shikhara | Kandariya Mahadeva, Lingaraj, Jagannath, Konark (Sun temple), Bhitargaon, Osian |
| **Dravida** | Pyramidal vimana; prakara, gopurams | Kailasanatha, Brihadeeswara |
| **Vesara** | Dravida tiers + Nagara profile; started by **Badami Chalukyas** | Hoysalas, Rashtrakutas |
!TRAP **Teli temple, Gwalior** = Dravida pinnacle, Nagara ornament (UPPSC 2016).
#### 1.1 Temples by Place
- Kailasanathar, Kanchi = **Rajasimha** style (Narasimhavarman II); Mahabalipuram Rathas = **Mamalla** style (Pallavas).
- **Dilwara** = Mount Abu (marble, not Morena); **Chausath Yogini**, Morena = circular; Virupaksha = Hampi.
- **Dashavatara** = Devgarh (Gupta), not Khajuraho (Chandelas).
- **Jagannath, Puri**: founded by **Anantavarman Chodaganga**; Devadasis (**Maharis**) in the news.
- AP: **Kapoteswara**, Chejarla (Buddhist, later Shaiva); **Draksharama Bhimeswara** = Chalukya-Chola style; Padmakshi (Hanamkonda) = Kakatiya.
### 2. CHOLA TEMPLES → BUILDER
| Order | Temple | Builder | Feature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early | Koranganatha, Srinivasanallur | Early Chola | Early structural temple |
| 1 | **Brihadeeswara**, Thanjavur (**Dakshina Meru**) | **Rajaraja I**, 1010 | Granite vimana **216 ft**; capstone ~**80 tonnes** |
| 2 | **Gangaikondacholapuram** | **Rajendra I** | Vimana **180 ft**, concave curvilinear |
| 3 | **Airavatesvara**, Darasuram | **Rajaraja II** | Horse-drawn stone chariot; **Musical Steps** |
| 4 | **Kampaharesvara**, Tribhuvanam | **Kulottunga III** | Late Chola |
- **Chidambaram** Nataraja: enlarged by Cholas; **Chit Sabha**, **108 dance poses** (APPSC 2018: Chidambaram, Thanjavur, Gangaikondacholapuram = Cholas).
- Order (UPPSC 2018): **Seven Pagodas → Shore Temple** (Pallava) → Brihadeeswara → Gangaikondacholapuram.

!TRAP Chidambaram is NOT in the group.
### 3. BRONZES – NATARAJA
- **Lost-wax (cire perdue)**, perfected by Cholas; Nataraja = greatest creation of South Indian sthapatis.
| Element | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Four hands** (Ananda Tandava) | Standard form (UPPSC 2021) |
| **Damaru** (upper right) | Creation – first sound |
| **Agni** (upper left) | Destruction |
| **Abhaya** (lower right) | Protection |
| Lower left | Points to raised left foot – liberation |
| **Apasmara** under right foot | Ignorance |
| Man's earring right, woman's left | Male-female fusion |
| **Prabhamandala** | Cosmic cycle |
### 4. FRESCOES AND SCULPTURE
- Brihadeeswara frescoes (dark ambulatory): Shiva as **Tripurantaka**; **Rajaraja I with guru Karuvur Thevar**.
- **Narthamalai**: Shiva-Parvati; murals at Gangaikondacholapuram; **Dakshinamurthy** panels finest.
### 5. TEMPLE LIFE AND INSCRIPTIONS
- Shaivism emphasised; temples hosted music, dance, festivals.
- Rajaraja I's Thanjavur inscriptions: about **400 temple dancing women**, musicians, staff paid by grants.
- Uttiramerur (**Parantaka I**, not Rajaraja I) = **Kudavolai** pot-ballot.

### 7. TRADE AND GUILDS
- Cholas = known for **maritime trade with South-East Asia**; China embassies 1015, 1033.
- Srivijaya friendly (Nagapattinam vihara) → conflict over China route (1025).
- **Nagapattinam** = chief port; **Kaveripattinam** = early port-capital (not Nagapattinam).
- Arabs: west coast horses and spices; **Anjuvannam** = foreign merchants; Manigramam, Ainnurruvar, Nanadesis = guilds; **Nagarams** = market centres; **Erivirappattanas** = protected towns; **Kasu** = gold coin.
