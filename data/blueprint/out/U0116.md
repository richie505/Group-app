## U0116 | INDO-ISLAMIC ARCHITECTURE – ARCUATE STYLE, QUTB MINAR, ALAI DARWAZA, TUGHLAQABAD, PROVINCIAL STYLES
### 1. ARCUATE STYLE – TERMS AND FEATURES
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Arcuate** | Arch, dome and vault – brought by the Turks; **true arch** held by a **keystone** |
| **Trabeate** | Indian style: lintel, pillar, corbel – merged with arcuate |
| **Surkhi** | Brick dust in the **lime mortar** used for rubble masonry (ancient India used dry-stone masonry) |
| **Mihrab / Minbar** | Prayer niche / pulpit |
| **Jali** | Pierced stone screen |
| **Arabesque** | Floral and geometric vine patterns |
- Decoration moved away from **human and animal figures** to geometric patterns, arabesque and **Quranic calligraphy**.
!TRAP Arcuate = arch and dome (Turkish); trabeate = lintel and beam (Indian).
### 2. ARCHITECTURAL FIRSTS
| First | Building |
|---|---|
| First **Indo-Islamic tomb** | **Sultan Ghari / Sultan Garhi** (1231), built by **Iltutmish** for his son **Nasiruddin Mahmud** |
| First **true arch** | **Tomb of Balban**, Mehrauli |
| First **true dome**; first building fully on Islamic principles by Indian craftsmen | **Alai Darwaza** (1311) |
| **Horse-shoe arch** first introduced | **Alai Darwaza** (Alauddin Khalji) |
| First **octagonal tomb** | Khan-i-Jahan Tilangani's tomb (Firoz Tughlaq's reign) |
| **Double domes** on tombs (pioneers) | **Lodis** |
| First full Mughal double dome | **Humayun's Tomb** |
| Earliest Indo-Islamic building / first major Sultanate mosque | **Quwwat-ul-Islam** |
!KEY Sultan Garhi – Balban's tomb – Alai Darwaza = first tomb – first true arch – first true dome.
#### 2.1 Other Oldest / Earliest
- **Gudimallam** Shiva Linga – earliest Shiva Linga; **Lad Khan Temple, Aihole** – one of the earliest temples.
- **Gupta period** – earliest freestanding temples (Kailasanatha, Kanchi is NOT the earliest).
- **Lingaraja** – one of the oldest temples in Bhubaneswar.
- **Sadak-e-Azam** of Sher Shah = **Grand Trunk Road**, one of Asia's oldest major roads.
### 3. DYNASTY → STYLE FEATURES
| Dynasty | Features | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Khalji** | Horse-shoe arch with **lotus-bud (spearhead) fringe**; red sandstone, marble inlay | Alai Darwaza |
| **Tughlaq** | **Sloping (battered) walls**, pointed domes, false arches; plain, massive, fort-like (Mongol threat, poor finances – "crisis period"); Syrian/Byzantine influence | Tughlaqabad, Ghiyasuddin's tomb |
| **Sayyid and Lodi** | **Octagonal tombs**, **double dome**; Hindu-style gateways, plaster ornament, external **drip-stones** | Sikandar Lodi's tomb, Bara Gumbad, Shisha Gumbad (Lodi Garden) |
| **Sharqi** | **Propylon** screen, no minarets | Atala Masjid |
!TRAP Lodis pioneered the double dome; Humayun's Tomb only "introduced" the full double dome in Mughal architecture.

### 5. QUTB MINAR AND QUTB COMPLEX
#### 5.1 Storey → Builder
| Storey | Builder |
|---|---|
| 1st | **Qutbuddin Aibak** – began it, built only the first storey |
| 2nd–4th | **Iltutmish** – completed it |
| Top | **Firoz Shah Tughlaq** replaced the lightning-damaged top storey with **two storeys** (marble and sandstone) → **five storeys** |
| Repairs | Firoz Tughlaq; also **Sikandar Lodi** |
- Height about **72.5 m (238 ft)**; tapering; bands of Quranic calligraphy.
- Named after Sufi saint **Khwaja Qutbuddin Bakhtiyar Kaki** (commonly believed; debated).
!TRAP "Qutb Minar is 180 ft high" is FALSE – it is about 238 ft.
#### 5.2 UNESCO Site
- "**Qutb Minar and its Monuments, Delhi**" – World Heritage Site, **1993**.
- Components: Qutb Minar, **Quwwat-ul-Islam**, **Alai Darwaza**, Alai Minar, **Iron Pillar**, **Iltutmish's tomb**, Alauddin's tomb and madrasa, **Imam Zamin's tomb**.
!TRAP **Tughlaqabad Fort is NOT** part of the Qutb complex WHS.
- **Iron Pillar**: **Gupta** period; king "Chandra" = **Chandragupta II**; known for **rust resistance**; in the Quwwat-ul-Islam court.
### 6. PROVINCIAL STYLES
| Region / dynasty | Features / monuments |
|---|---|
| **Bengal** | Brick, **curved roofs**; **Adina Mosque, Pandua** (not Gujarat); **Eklakhi tomb**, **Dakhil Darwaza**, **Chota Sona Masjid** |
| **Jaunpur (Sharqi)** | **Atala Masjid** – massive propylon screen, no minarets; Lal Darwaza Mosque |
| **Malwa** | **Jami Masjid, Mandu** |
| **Gujarat** | Strong **Hindu-Jain carving**; Jama Masjid, Ahmedabad |
| **Bahmani** | **Gulbarga Jama Masjid** – fully covered courtyard, large dome; **Mahmud Gawan Madrasa, Bidar** – three storeys, glazed tiles; **Ashtur tombs** – tile mosaic, painted ceilings; **Chand Minar, Daulatabad (1435)** – Alauddin Ahmad Shah |
| **Bijapur (Adil Shahi)** | **Gol Gumbaz** – 17th-c. tomb of **Muhammad Adil Shah**; one of the largest domes, second only to **St. Peter's Basilica** |
| **Hyderabad (Qutb Shahi)** | **Charminar** |
!TRAP Atala Masjid = Jaunpur; Adina Mosque = Pandua (Bengal); Gol Gumbaz = Bijapur.
### 7. QUTB SHAHI HYDERABAD AND GOLCONDA (AP/TELANGANA ANGLE)
| Place | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Hyderabad** | Founded **1591**; capital moved from Golconda in **1592**; planned on **Isfahan** by **Mir Momin Astarabadi** |
| **Charminar** | Marks the **end of a plague**; four arches, four minarets; **Persian arches + Deccani stucco** |
| Sultan Quli's Juma Masjid, Golconda | Its minarets said to have inspired Charminar |
| **Golconda Fort** | **Acoustics** – handclap at gate heard at **Bala Hisar** (~1 km); **piped water** |
| **Qutb Shahi Tombs** | **Ibrahim Bagh**; **seven rulers**; onion domes on square bases |
| **Golconda Jami Masjid** | Gateway in **Nastaliq** (Yazdani); **Naskh** for Quranic verses around the mihrab |
- Qutb Shahi style = **Indo-Saracenic** mix of Persian and Hindu traditions; Sultanate synthesis led to Mughal architecture.
### 8. LATEST DEVELOPMENT
- **May 2025**: **Rajon ki Baoli** (stepwell of the masons), **Mehrauli Archaeological Park** – four-tiered, built **c. 1506** by **Daulat Khan Lodi** under **Sikandar Lodi** – conserved by **ASI** with **World Monuments Fund India** and TCS Foundation.
