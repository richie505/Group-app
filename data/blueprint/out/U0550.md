## U0550 | FRBM ACT - TARGETS, ESCAPE CLAUSE, N.K. SINGH COMMITTEE; FISCAL CONSOLIDATION PATH
### 1. FRBM ACT, 2003 - ORIGIN AND OBJECTIVES
- **Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management Act** - introduced by FM **Yashwant Sinha**; enacted **August 2003**; Rules notified 2004, in force **5 July 2004**.
- Aim: **fiscal discipline and prudence** by cutting **fiscal deficit, revenue deficit and public debt**. It concerns both FD and RD.
- Objectives: **inter-generational equity**, long-run macro stability, eliminate RD, cut FD, debt management, **transparency**, balanced budget over time.
- First Budget of India: **James Wilson, 1860**; of free India: **R.K. Shanmukham Chetty, 1947**.
!TRAP Fixing the **repo rate** is NOT an FRBM objective.
#### 1.1 Original Targets
| Target | Detail |
|---|---|
| Fiscal deficit | **3% of GDP by 31 March 2008** |
| Revenue deficit | **Eliminated by 31 March 2009** |
| Annual cut (Rules) | FD **0.3%**, RD **0.5%** of GDP a year |
| Guarantees | Extra guarantees on Consolidated Fund ≤ **0.5% of GDP** a year |
- **Tax-GDP** is not a legal target; it is one of four indicators in the MTFP Statement.
### 2. WHAT IT PRESCRIBES AND DOES NOT
| Prescribes | Does NOT |
|---|---|
| Deficit limits | **Penal provisions** for missing targets |
| Statements laid with the Budget | Make the **Centre answerable for States** |
| Disclosures (borrowings, contingent liabilities) | Apply to States - they have **own Acts** |
!TRAP "FRBM does NOT provide penal provisions" - the favourite APPSC trap (Group 2, 2017).
### 3. STATEMENTS LAID IN PARLIAMENT (SECTION 3)
| Statement | Content |
|---|---|
| **Macro-Economic Framework** | Growth, inflation, external balance, assumptions, risks |
| **Medium-Term Fiscal Policy** | **3-year rolling targets** for RD, FD, tax-GDP, total outstanding liabilities |
| **Fiscal Policy Strategy** | Tax, spending, borrowing strategy |
| **Medium-Term Expenditure Framework** | 3-year expenditure ceilings; added **2012**, laid after the Budget |
- Rolling targets **suspended since 2021-22** (post-pandemic flexibility). Budget 2026-27 laid the MEFS and a combined MTFPS-cum-FPSS.
### 4. KELKAR TASK FORCE, 2004
- **Task Force on Implementation of the FRBM Act**, chaired by **Vijay Kelkar**.
- Meet targets through **tax reform**: wider base + **comprehensive GST**.
- Kelkar later headed the **13th Finance Commission** (2010-15).
### 5. N.K. SINGH FRBM REVIEW COMMITTEE
- Announced Budget **2016-17**; formed **May 2016**; report **January 2017**; reflected in Budget **2017-18**.
- Chair **N.K. Singh**; members included **Sumit Bose, Urjit Patel, Rathin Roy**.
| Recommendation | Value |
|---|---|
| Principal anchor | **Debt-to-GDP**; FD = operational target |
| General govt debt | **60% by 2023** = Centre **40%** + States **20%** |
| FD | **3%** for the next three years |
| RD | **0.8%** by 2023 |
| Institution | Independent, **advisory Fiscal Council** (no binding powers) |
| Escape clause | Deviation up to 0.5% of GDP |
!PAIR Kelkar | 2004 | Implement via GST || N.K. Singh | 2016-17 | Review, debt anchor
### 6. AMENDMENTS AND SUSPENSION EPISODES
| Year | Change |
|---|---|
| 2004 | Rules notified; RD deadline moved to 2009 |
| **2008-09** | Targets **suspended** after the **global financial crisis** (stimulus) |
| **2012** | **ERD** target and **MTEF Statement** added |
| 2015 | Dates deferred (FD 3% by 2017-18) |
| **2018** | **Debt anchor**: general govt 60%, Centre 40% by **31 March 2025**; FD 3% by **31 March 2021**; RD/ERD targets dropped; **escape and buoyancy clauses** |
| **2020-21** | **COVID**: escape clause used; FD **9.2%**; no amendment |
- Status: FRBM Act, 2003 (amended 2018). Government's deviation statement admitted missing the 3% FD and 40% Central debt targets.
### 7. ESCAPE CLAUSE AND BUOYANCY CLAUSE
- **Escape grounds**: national security, **war**, **national calamity**, **agricultural collapse**, **structural reforms**, sharp output fall - real growth **3 percentage points below** the average of the previous four quarters. NOT a routine tax shortfall.
- **Limit**: deviation **≤ 0.5% of GDP** in a year; government must place a justification and a path back before Parliament.
- **Buoyancy clause** (2018): sharp rise in growth requires FD cut of **at least 0.25%** of GDP - never an automatic increase.
!TRAP Escape = up to 0.5% deviation; buoyancy = at least 0.25% cut.
### 8. FISCAL CONSOLIDATION PATH
| Year | FD (% GDP) |
|---|---|
| 2020-21 | **9.2** (pandemic peak) |
| 2024-25 | 4.8 |
| 2025-26 | **4.4** - "below 4.5%" target (set FY 2021-22) met |
| 2026-27 BE | **4.3** |
- **Halving commitment** (9.2% in five years) met at 4.4% - a commitment, **not a legislative target**.
- **New debt rule (Budget 2026-27, 1 Feb 2026)**: FD set yearly to bring Central debt to **50 ± 1% by 31 March 2031** (55.6% BE 2026-27); criticised as above FRBM's **40%**.
- FY27 FD: 26.8% of target by July (1 Sep 2026).
- ES 2025-26 (30 Jan 2026): delay strict FRBM-type targets to keep flexibility.
- **16th FC**: Centre FD **3.5% by 2030-31**; States **3% of GSDP**; State fiscal rules "**effectively unenforced**"; wider deficit definition including **off-budget borrowing** - Union deferred amending the law (11 Mar 2026).
- Criticism: frequent escape-clause use, deferrals, no penalty, no fiscal council yet.
### 9. STATE FISCAL LAWS AND AP
- States passed Fiscal Responsibility Laws after the **12th FC** tied debt relief to them (**Debt Consolidation and Relief Facility**); Union FRBM does not apply automatically.
- **AP FRBM Act, 2005** (Act 34 of 2005; assent 25 Oct 2005): **eliminate RD**, **FD 3% of GSDP**; amended in 2010, 2011, 2020, 2021 - the 2020 amendment raised RD limits.
- AP FRBM limits control AP's **open-market borrowing**.
- AP BE 2026-27: **FD 3.84%**, RD 1.11% - above the 3% norm (15 Feb 2026). Slippage: FD 5.05% vs 4% ceiling; borrowings ₹81,071 crore (7 Mar 2026).
| Year (15th FC) | States' net borrowing limit (% GSDP) |
|---|---|
| 2021-22 | 4 |
| 2022-23 | 3.5 |
| 2023-24 to 2025-26 | 3 + **0.5** for power-sector reforms |
