## U0401 | CUSTODIAL VIOLENCE, ENCOUNTER DEATHS, PRISON REFORMS, DEATH PENALTY DEBATE
### 1. CUSTODIAL VIOLENCE – CASES
| Case | Principle |
|---|---|
| **Nilabati Behera (1993)** | Compensation (Arts 32/226) for custodial death; **no sovereign immunity** |
| **Joginder Kumar (1994)** | Arrest must be justified; friend/relative informed |
| **D.K. Basu (1997)** | **Arrest memo**, officer ID, informing kin, medical check |
| **Arnesh Kumar (2014)** | **No automatic arrest** for offences up to **7 years** (CrPC s.41, now BNSS s.35) |
- **CrPC s.176(1A)**: mandatory **judicial inquiry** in custodial deaths (now **BNSS s.196**). **BNSS s.218**: sanction before cognisance against public servants delays prosecution.
- **2,253** police-custody deaths, only **3 convictions** (1999-2023). Most: **Bihar**, then Rajasthan, UP.
- **AP:** **Gade Sai Krishna** custodial death, **Krishnalanka** police, **Vijayawada**. **7 Apr 2026:** a T.N. court sentenced nine policemen to death for custodial murder.
!TRAP 1993-1994-1997-2014 = Nilabati - Joginder - D.K. Basu - Arnesh.
### 2. ENCOUNTER DEATHS
- **PUCL v Maharashtra (2014)**: **16-point** guidelines: record tip-off, **FIR**, independent probe by **CID**, **magisterial inquiry**, inform **NHRC/SHRC**, forensic check; **no out-of-turn promotion or gallantry award** until cleared.
- **NHRC norms:** serious violations; mandatory reporting, post-mortem **videography**, independent investigation.
### 3. POLICE REFORMS
- **Prakash Singh (2006)**: **7 directives**: State Security Commission; **DGP from UPSC panel, 2-year tenure** (not five); 2-year field tenure; investigation separate from law and order; Police Establishment Board; National Security Commission; **Police Complaints Authorities** (State and district).
- **State PCA**: headed by a **retired judge**; custodial death, rape, grievous hurt.
- **Police Act 1861** still in force.
### 4. PRISON LAWS AND PRISONERS' RIGHTS
| Law | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Prisons Act 1894** | Colonial; with State jail manuals; **BPR&D** advisory |
| Seventh Schedule | Prisons = **State List, Entry 4** |
| **Model Prisons Act 2023** | **MHA** guide to replace 1894 Act; **not binding** |
| **BNSS s.479** | Undertrial freed after **half** max term (**one-third** first-timers) |
- **Parole/furlough**: not absolute rights; **each State has its own rules** (UPSC 2021).
- **Aug 2025:** SC ordered release of prisoners held beyond sentence.

### 6. TORTURE CONVENTION
- **UNCAT:** India **signed 1997, not ratified**; no anti-torture law (Apr 2026).
- **Prevention of Torture Bill 2010** passed Lok Sabha, **lapsed**.
### 7. DEATH PENALTY AND MERCY
| Case | Holding |
|---|---|
| **Jagmohan Singh (1973)** | Death penalty **constitutional** |
| **Bachan Singh (1980)** | "**Rarest of rare**"; life the rule |
| **Machhi Singh (1983)** | Categories; aggravating vs mitigating |
| **Epuru Sudhakar (2006)** | Pardon open to **limited judicial review** |
| **Shatrughan Chauhan (2014)** | **Delay** in mercy → commute; **14 days** before execution; solitary confinement illegal |
- Path: Sessions Court → **HC confirmation** → SC (**Arts 132, 134, 136**) → mercy.
- **Art. 72**: President **can pardon**; **Art. 161**: Governor **cannot**, may commute/remit/suspend.
- Murder: **BNS s.103**. **Amnesty**: **China** biggest executioner; **23 countries** executed in 2017.
