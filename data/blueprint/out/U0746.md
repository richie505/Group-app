## U0746 | NEW INITIATIVES, PROGRAMMES AND ACHIEVEMENTS IN INDIA'S RENEWABLE ENERGY SECTOR
### 1. MNRE – HISTORY, MISSION AND FIRSTS
#### 1.1 Evolution
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1981 | **Commission for Additional Sources of Energy (CASE)** set up |
| 1982 | **Department of Non-Conventional Energy Sources (DNES)** in the **Ministry of Energy** |
| 1992 | Separate **Ministry of Non-Conventional Energy Sources (MNES)** |
| 2006 | Renamed **Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE)** |
- **India = first country in the world** to set up a ministry for non-conventional energy sources (early 1980s).
!TRAP DNES (1982) was in the Ministry of **Energy**, not Science and Technology. Order: CASE 1981 → DNES 1982 → MNES 1992 → MNRE 2006.
#### 1.2 Mission
- Nodal ministry for all new and renewable energy; mission covers **energy security**, bigger **share of clean power**, **availability and access**, **affordability** and **equity**.
### 2. INSTITUTIONS UNDER MNRE
| Institution | Type | Location / role |
|---|---|---|
| **NISE** | Autonomous | **Gurugram** (Haryana) – solar R&D, technical focal point |
| **NIWE** | Autonomous | **Chennai** (Tamil Nadu) – wind R&D; nodal for wind roadmap |

### 3. IREDA – MoUs AND FINANCING
- **PSU + NBFC** under MNRE; set up **1987**.
- **MoU with IIT Bhubaneswar** for **innovation and research in renewable energy** and start-up support – signed at the **100 Cube Start-up Conclave** (Feb 2024).
- **Performance MoU with MNRE** for 2025-26: revenue target **₹8,200 crore**.
- Other institute links: **IIT Tirupati** – AP solar Centre of Excellence with **UNSW** (31 Jul 2026); **IIT Roorkee DHRE** – small hydro.
!KEY APPSC 2024: IREDA's MoU for RE innovation and research = **IIT Bhubaneswar**.
### 4. NATIONAL CLEAN ENERGY FUND (NCEF)
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Created | **2010-11** |
| Source | **Clean energy cess on coal** (also lignite and peat), domestic **and imported** |
| Rate | **₹50/tonne → ₹400/tonne**; later called Clean Environment Cess |
| Purpose | Fund **research and innovative projects in clean energy technologies** – promote RE and **reduce carbon emissions** |
| Status | Cess **subsumed into GST Compensation Cess (July 2017)**, ending NCEF's dedicated inflow |
!KEY APPSC-G2 2025: primary goal of NCEF = promote renewable energy technologies and reduce carbon emissions.
### 5. DISTRIBUTED ENERGY RESOURCES
- DERs = small, decentralised units near the point of use: **rooftop PV, battery storage, fuel cells, biomass generators** (all four).
| Resource | Programme / feature |
|---|---|
| Rooftop PV | **Phase-II Grid-Connected Rooftop Solar Programme** (target **40,000 MW**) subsumed in **PM Surya Ghar** |
| Battery storage | 25% of new utility-scale solar paired with batteries (IRENA 2025); **PM Surya Sarovar** projects must add **minimum 2-hour BESS** (1 Aug 2026) |
| Fuel cells | Green-hydrogen fuel cells **more efficient than combustion engines** |
| Biomass / biogas | National Biogas and Manure Management Programme (family plants); Biomass Power and Bagasse Co-generation Programme; bio-power ~11.75 GW (Aug 2026) |
| Off-grid tribal | New Solar Power Scheme under **PM JANMAN + DA JGUA** → 4,919 households electrified in 2025 |
### 6. NEW SCHEMES AND MARKET RULES – OBJECTIVE
| Scheme / rule | Objective |
|---|---|
| **PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana** | Rooftop solar for **1 crore** households |
| **PM-KUSUM** | Solar pumps and decentralised solar plants for farmers |
| **Green Energy Open Access Rules, 2022** | Notified by **Ministry of Power** on **6 June 2022**; open-access limit cut from **1 MW to 100 kW** (no limit for captive users) |
| **Green Term Ahead Market (G-TAM)** | Power-exchange segment since **August 2020**; sub-segments **Solar, Non-Solar, Hydro**; purchases count towards **RPO** |
!TRAP Rooftop Solar Phase-II is not a separate live scheme – it was merged into PM Surya Ghar.

### 11. ACHIEVEMENTS AND AP ANGLE
#### 11.1 Capacity and Ranks
- Installed capacity (15 Aug 2026): ~**552 GW**; non-fossil **over 54%**; solar 164.59 GW; wind 58.14 GW (earlier 535 GW, Mar 2026).
- Non-fossil share **51.93% (Dec 2025)** – Panchamrit goal of **50% non-fossil capacity by 2030 met five years early** (Nov 2025). Target: **500 GW** non-fossil by 2030.
- Global (Aug 2026): **3rd** in total RE capacity; **4th** in wind; 2nd-largest solar growth market in 2025; **overtook Japan** as **3rd-largest solar** producer.
- Wind targets: **100 GW by 2030**, **155 GW by 2035** (Jun 2026). Solar module capacity: 172 GW (Mar 2026).
- Debate: **coal** still ~**55%** of primary energy and over 70% of generation (Aug 2026).
#### 11.2 Andhra Pradesh
- **Tata Renewable**: **800 MW hybrid** project (₹5,750 crore) – not the 5,000 MW SECI floating solar.
- **Solar CoE**: IIT Tirupati + UNSW (31 Jul 2026).
