## U0709 | BLOCKCHAIN, DISTRIBUTED LEDGER, CRYPTOCURRENCY AND CBDC (E-RUPEE)
### 1. BLOCKCHAIN AND DISTRIBUTED LEDGER – TERMS
- **Blockchain**: a **time-stamped** series of **immutable records** managed by a cluster of computers **not owned by any single entity** (APPSC 2018).
- Records are **visible to stakeholders** without risk of alteration; database copies sit with the participants (UPSC 2020).
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Distributed ledger** | Shared ledger copied across many **nodes**; one "single source of truth"; no central validator |
| **Block** | Transaction details + **timestamp** + **hash of the previous block** (not private keys) |
| **Immutability** | Data nearly impossible to change without consensus; no central authority changes it |
| **Consensus** | Rule by which nodes agree on valid transactions |
### 2. TYPES AND CONSENSUS MECHANISMS
| Public / permissionless | Private / permissioned |
|---|---|
| Anyone can join, read and validate (**Bitcoin**) | Only authorised entities join and validate (**AP DigiVerify**) |
| Proof of Work (PoW) | Proof of Stake (PoS) |
|---|---|
| Miners race to solve puzzles; **far more energy** | Validators picked by **coins staked**; lower energy |
| Bitcoin still uses PoW | Ethereum since **The Merge (September 2022)** |
!TRAP Bitcoin = public + PoW; Ethereum = PoS since Sep 2022; AP DigiVerify = permissioned.
### 3. CRYPTOCURRENCY
- **Cryptocurrency**: encrypted (cryptographic) **peer-to-peer** fund transfers without a central bank.
- **Bitcoin** (**2009**): first cryptocurrency, by pseudonymous **Satoshi Nakamoto**; decentralised; **mining** validates transactions.
- Bitcoins are **not tracked by central banks**; anyone with a Bitcoin address can send to and receive from any other address.
- India: crypto is **not legal tender**; **VDA service providers** brought under **PMLA** in **March 2023**.
- **VDA tax** (Finance Act 2022): **30%** on gains (Section 115BBH, from 1 Apr 2022); **1% TDS** on transfers (Section 194S, from 1 Jul 2022); losses cannot be set off.
### 4. NFTS, SMART CONTRACTS, DEFI – WEB 3.0 TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Stablecoins** | Pegged to fiat, commodities or crypto |
| **NFT** | Non-Fungible Token; unique, not exchangeable at equivalency; can represent physical assets |
| **Smart contract** | Self-executing code; cuts intermediaries |
| **DeFi** | Lending/trading via smart contracts without banks |
| **Tokenisation** | Ownership rights in an asset → blockchain token |
### 5. NATIONAL STRATEGY AND PLATFORMS
| Initiative | Body / year | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **National Strategy on Blockchain** | **MeitY (Dec 2021)** | National strategy |
| Distributed CoE in Blockchain | MeitY; C-DAC, IDRBT Hyderabad, VJTI Mumbai | Property pilot in **Shamshabad (Telangana, not AP)**; Proof-of-Existence for certificates |
| **Vishvasya** | MeitY (2024) | **Blockchain-as-a-Service** stack across **NIC data centres**; builds on National Blockchain Framework |

- **Uses**: land records, certificates, supply chain, e-voting, health records, e-Notary, farm insurance.
### 6. CBDC – E-RUPEE
- **CBDC** = **legal tender** in digital form, issued by the **RBI**; a **liability of the RBI** (concept note, Oct 2022).
| Pilot | Start | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Wholesale (e₹-W) | **1 Nov 2022** | Settlement of secondary-market government securities; 9 banks |
| Retail (e₹-R) | **1 Dec 2022** | Closed user group; Mumbai, New Delhi, Bengaluru, Bhubaneswar |
!PAIR e-rupee | RBI money | legal tender || UPI | moves bank-account money | crypto: no issuer, not legal tender
### 7. AP BLOCKCHAIN PILOTS
- **Mee Bhoomi-Blockchain**: pilot launched by CM Naidu on **23 Jul 2026**; unifies **Revenue, Survey, Registration**; statewide from November.
- **Mee Bhoomi-Mee Hakku** (10 Apr 2026): tamper-proof **pattadar passbooks**.
- **AP DigiVerify**: permissioned-blockchain PaaS for tamper-proof certificate verification.
- 1 Jan 2026: SC suggested blockchain for land records.
