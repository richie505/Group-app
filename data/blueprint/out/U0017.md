## U0017 | EARLY VEDIC POLITY – RAJAN, SABHA, SAMITI, VIDATHA, GANA; TRIBAL CHIEFDOM
### 1. NATURE OF EARLY VEDIC POLITY
- **Tribal polity** (APPSC 2018 key) – not a republic or autocracy; **kinship-based**, **no formal bureaucracy**, **no fixed territory**.
- **Jana** (tribe) = largest unit; mentioned **275 times** in the Rigveda. The term **Janapada is absent** from the Rigveda.
- Order (largest → smallest): **Jana → Vis** (clan, under Vispati) **→ Grama** (village, Gramani) **→ Kula** (family, Kulapa).
- **Rig Veda** = only source for the period.
#### 1.1 Early vs Later Vedic
| Feature | Early Vedic | Later Vedic |
|---|---|---|
| Unit | Jana (tribe) | **Janapada / Rashtra** (territory) |
| Chief | Elective traces, limited | **Hereditary** territorial king |
| Assemblies | Sabha, Samiti, Vidatha powerful | Vidatha **vanished**; Sabha, Samiti under chiefs and nobles; **women excluded** |
| Revenue | Bali (voluntary) | Regular taxes; **ratnins** |
| Rituals | Simple | **Rajasuya, Vajapeya, Ashvamedha** |
### 2. VEDIC ASSEMBLIES
| Assembly | Composition | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **Sabha** | **Elders, elites**, the learned; women (**Sabhavati**) | Advisory, social, **judicial** |
| **Samiti** | **Common people** (folk assembly) | Tribal business, legislation, **choice of the chief** |
| **Vidatha** | Whole tribe; **earliest**; women attended | Secular, religious, economic, military |
| **Gana** | Troop of tribesmen | Military and tribal functions |
| Assembly | Mentions in Rigveda |
|---|---|
| **Vidatha** | **122** (most) |
| Samiti | 9 |
| Sabha | **8** |
- Sabha and Samiti = **checks on the king's autocracy**; early "democratic" elements.
- **Atharvaveda** calls Sabha and Samiti the **two (twin) daughters of Prajapati** and treats them as separate; it mentions women in the Samiti.
!TRAP Judicial assembly = **Sabha**; earliest / most mentioned = **Vidatha**; "daughters of Prajapati" = **Atharvaveda**, not Rigveda.
#### 2.1 Women in assemblies
- Women attended the **Sabha** (Sabhavati) and the **Vidatha**; excluded in Later Vedic times.
!KEY Vidatha = earliest assembly and the first to disappear in the Later Vedic period.
### 3. THE RAJAN
- Tribal chief: **protector of the tribe and its cattle**, not ruler of a fixed territory.
- Titles: **Gopati** (lord of cattle), Gopa, **Gopajanasya**. Queen = **Mahisi**.
- Selection: tended towards **hereditary** but not strictly; **traces of election by the Samiti**. Older PYQ keys: hereditary monarchy.
- Duties: protect tribe and cattle, lead in war, keep order, settle disputes – with advice of assemblies and purohita.
- Kingship **not divine**; rare exception: **Trasadasyu** (Puru king) called **ardhadeva** (demi-god).
### 4. OFFICIALS
| Official | Role |
|---|---|
| **Purohita** | Chief priest and adviser |
| **Senani** | Army commander |
| **Gramani** | Village head; military leader of the grama |
| **Kulapa / Kulapati** | Head of the family |
| **Vrajapati** | Officer of **pasture land** |
| **Jivagribha / Ugra** | **Police** officials |
| **Spasa** | **Spy** |
| Madhyamasi | Mediator |
!TRAP Vrajapati = pasture land, not village; Gramani = village head; Spasa = spy.
### 5. ARMY, WARFARE AND REVENUE
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Gavishti** | "**Search for cows**" – war = cattle raids |
| **Sena** | Temporary militia; **no standing army** |
| **Sardha, Vrata, Gana** | Army units |
| **Bali** | **Voluntary offering**; **no regular tax** |
- Weapons of **bronze**, not iron; **coat of mail and helmet** used; horse and **spoked-wheel chariot** known (UPSC 2017).
- Wars: intra-Aryan and against Dasas/Dasyus.
### 6. TRIBES AND THE BATTLE OF TEN KINGS
- **Panchajana** (eponymous founders of clans): **Anu, Druhyu, Yadu, Turvasa (Turvasu), Puru**. **Kikat** is not among them.
- **Dasarajna**: intra-Aryan, fought on the bank of the **Parushni (Ravi)**; Bharata king **Sudas** won.
- Bharata chief **Divodasa** defeated **Sambara**.
### 7. RIGVEDIC RIVERS
| Vedic | Modern |

| Asikni | **Chenab** |
| Vipas | **Beas** |
| Sutudri (Shutudri) | **Sutlej** |

- **Sindhu** = **most mentioned**; called **Hiranyayi** (gold); ends in the Arabian Sea.
- **Sarasvati** = holiest: **Matetama** (best of mothers), **Devitama** (best of goddesses), **Naditama** (best of rivers).
- **Yamuna** mentioned thrice; **Ganga** once.
- **Sapta Sindhu**: Sindhu, Vitasta, Asikni, Parushni, Vipas, Sutudri, Sarasvati. **Samudra** = a collection of water, not necessarily the sea.
!TRAP UPSC 2026: Vitasta = Jhelum (not Chenab); Asikni = Chenab; Yavyavati is not the Beas.
### 8. ARYAN, BOGHAZKOI AND AP ANGLE
- **Aryan** = a **speech group**, not a race. Original home: **Central Asia – Max Muller**; **Arctic – Tilak**; **Sapta Sindhu – A.C. Das**; **Tibet – Dayananda Saraswati**.
- **Boghazkoi** inscription (Asia Minor, modern **Turkey**, c. 14th century BCE): treaty of **Hittites** and **Mitanni** naming **Indra, Mitra, Varuna, Nasatyas** → Vedic gods in West Asia; supports movement from Central Asia towards Iran and India.
- **AP**: 'Andhra' first appears in the **Aitareya Brahmana** (**Sunahshepa** story), not the Rig Veda.

- Earliest assembly and first to vanish: Vidatha.
- King called ardhadeva (demi-god): Trasadasyu.
