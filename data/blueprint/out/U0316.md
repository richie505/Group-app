## U0316 | LEGISLATURE OF ANDHRA PRADESH – STRUCTURE AND FUNCTIONING
### 1. SEAT OF THE AP LEGISLATURE
| Period | Seat |
|---|---|
| Before 1956 | **Andhra State** Assembly met at **Kurnool** |
| 1956 to the move to Amaravati | **Hyderabad** (later the common capital) |
| Since **2017** | **Amaravati** – interim building at **Velagapudi** |
- New Assembly building at Amaravati designed by **Norman Foster + Partners** – under construction (Aug 2026).
- **Repeal Act, Nov 2021**: withdrew the three-capitals law and the CRDA repeal; **Amaravati restored**.
### 2. STRUCTURE IN 1956 AND THE ASSEMBLY'S STRENGTH
- **1956**: AP Legislature **unicameral**, Assembly of **301** members (APPSC-GS 2022 key).
- First meeting of the AP Legislative Assembly: **3 December 1956**.
- **1958**: Legislative Council created – bicameral.
#### 2.1 Strength Now
| Item | Number |
|---|---|
| Assembly in 1956 | **301** |
| AP Assembly now | **175** elected (Sec. 17, AP Reorganisation Act; APPSC-GS 2019 key) |
| Telangana Assembly | **119** |
| Reserved seats in AP | **29 SC + 7 ST** |
| Lok Sabha seats from AP | **25** |
- **Anglo-Indian** nominee (Sec. 18 / Art. 333) ended with the **104th Amendment (2020)**.
!NUM 301 (1956) → 175 (AP now) + 119 (Telangana); 29 SC + 7 ST reserved.

### 4. AP LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLIES BY NUMBER
- Residual AP **continues the numbering**: **14th (2014), 15th (2019), 16th (2024)**.
- 1956 Assembly: Congress held a large majority.
- **4th Assembly (1967)**: the most independents ever – **68** of 294 members.
- **B.V. Subba Reddy** left Congress in 1955 and won as an **independent**; later Speaker from 1962.
| Assembly | Result |
|---|---|
| **16th (2024)** | **NDA 164 of 175** – TDP 135, JSP 21, BJP 8 |
| Opposition | **YSRCP 11** – below one-tenth, so **no official Opposition** status |
### 5. AP LEGISLATIVE COUNCIL – YEAR → EVENT
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **1958** | Council created in united AP |
| **1985** | **Abolished** (NTR government) |
| **2007** | **Revived** under the **AP Legislative Council Act, 2005** |
| 2014 | AP Reorganisation Act Sec. 22(1): AP Council **not more than 50**; Telangana **40**, "in accordance with Art. 169" |
| **2015** | **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Act** – raised AP Council from **50 to 58** (APPSC-GS 2021 key) |
| **Jan 2020** | Assembly resolution to **abolish** the Council again |
| **Nov 2021** | Resolution **withdrawn**; the Council continues |
- Present strength: still **58** (Jan 2026).
!TRAP Act of **2005**, revival in **2007** – do not mix the two years.
### 6. COUNCIL OF 58 – COMPOSITION BY ELECTORATE

- **AP-Telangana sharing**: existing MLCs allotted between the two States under the **Fourth Schedule** (Sec. 22(2)).
- **Sec. 25**: Chairman of the united Council continued as AP Council Chairman; **Deputy Chairman went to Telangana**.
### 7. AP REORGANISATION ACT 2014 – LEGISLATURE PROVISIONS
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| **17** | Assembly seats: **AP 175, Telangana 119** |
| **18** | One Anglo-Indian nominee each (Art. 333) – lapsed |
| **19** | Sitting MLAs continue for allotted constituencies |
| **20** | Five-year term under Art. 172(1) |
| **21** | AP **Speaker continues**; **Deputy Speaker to Telangana** |
| **22** | Councils: AP 50 (now 58), Telangana 40 |
| **24** | Council constituencies orders: AP (2006) amended; Telangana Order 2014 |
| **25** | Council Chairman continues in AP; Deputy Chairman to Telangana |
| **26** | Raise Assembly seats to **225 (AP)** and **153 (Telangana)** at next delimitation, subject to Art. 170 |
- **Sec. 26 status (Apr 2026)**: no increase yet – **Art. 170(3)** freezes seats until the **first Census after 2026**; an earlier increase needs a constitutional amendment.
- **Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill, 2026**: fresh delimitation, Lok Sabha to **850**; **negatived** in the Lok Sabha (Apr 2026) – the **84th Amendment** freeze stays.
!TRAP Sec. 21 sends the Deputy Speaker, Sec. 25 the Deputy Chairman, to Telangana.

### 9. COMMITTEES OF THE AP LEGISLATURE
| Committee | Note |
|---|---|
| **Estimates** | Financial committee – ensures grants are properly used |
| **Public Accounts (PAC)** | Financial committee; chair to the **Opposition** by convention |
| **Public Undertakings** | Exists **in addition to** (not instead of) Estimates and PAC |
- Members **elected from the House**; chairpersons named by the **Speaker** (not the Governor); work like parliamentary committees.
