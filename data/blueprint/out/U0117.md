## U0117 | SULTANATE LITERATURE AND CULTURE – AMIR KHUSRAU, BARANI, IBN BATTUTA, MUSIC AND SUFI INFLUENCE

#### 1.1 Ibn Battuta
- Came to Delhi **1333**; made **qazi of Delhi** by MBT; sent as **envoy to China**.
- Rihla records the **postal system** and espionage, **sati**, slave markets, and the Malabar pepper trade.
!TRAP **Tughluqnama** is by **Amir Khusrau**, NOT Ibn Battuta (UPPSC 2019 wrong pair). Ibn Battuta's work = **Rihla**.

#### 2.1 Chronological Order of Texts
**Kitab-ul-Hind** (Al-Biruni, c. 1030) → **Prithviraj Raso** (Chand Bardai, late 12th c.) → **Tabaqat-i-Nasiri** (Minhaj, c. 1260) → **Fatawa-i-Jahandari** (Barani, 14th c.).
### 3. AMIR KHUSRAU
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Real name | **Abul Hasan Yaminuddin Khusrau**; born at **Patiyali** |
| Title | **Tuti-i-Hind** (Parrot of India) – from patron **Alauddin Khalji** |
| Guru | Disciple of **Nizamuddin Auliya** (Chishti) |
| Sultans served | About **seven**, from Balban's time to Ghiyasuddin Tughluq; **poet laureate under Ghiyasuddin** |
| Roles | Poet, historian, musician |
| Language | Scholar of Hindavi and Persian; **first to use Urdu (emerging Hindavi)** for poetry; Hindavi riddles → **Rekhta/Urdu**; **Father of Khari Boli** |
| Persian style | Founded **Sabak-i-Hindi** (Indian style of Persian poetry); first Persian poet to depict the Indian environment |
- Courts linked: Nasiruddin Mahmud, **Balban**, Kaiqubad, Kayumars, **Jalaluddin**, **Alauddin**, Shihabuddin Umar, **Mubarak Shah**, **Ghiyasuddin Tughluq**, **Muhammad bin Tughluq**.
- Said "**Na Lafze Hindiwast aj Farsi Kam**" (Hindi words are no less than Persian).
!TRAP Khusrau was **NOT** associated with the **Bhakti** movement.

| Work | Subject |
|---|---|

!KEY All four – Khazain-ul-Futuh, Nuh Sipihr, Miftah-ul-Futuh, Qiran-us-Sadain – are by Amir Khusrau.

### 4. ZIAUDDIN BARANI
- **Nadim** (companion) of **Muhammad bin Tughlaq**; wrote under **Firoz Tughlaq**.
- **Jahandari** (kingship): **Persian model**, based on **awe and force**.
- Sultans governed through **zawabit** (state laws) → state **not truly Islamic**.
- High office only for the **high-born**; opposed promoting low-born men.
### 5. NIZAMUDDIN AULIYA AND SUFI CULTURE
- Saw the reign of **seven** (more than seven) Sultans; **never visited** their court.
- Refused to meet **Alauddin**; when **Jalaluddin** tried to meet him through Khusrau, he moved to **Ajodhan**.
- ***Fawaid-ul-Fuad*** = conversations (malfuzat) of Nizamuddin, by **Amir Hasan Sijzi**.
### 6. MUSIC
#### 6.1 Khusrau's Contribution
- **Sitar** – Irani **tamboora** + Indian **veena** (example of Hindu-Muslim harmony); **tabla** (from pakhawaj) also credited.
- Creator of **qawwali**; started **qaul**, qalbana, **tarana**, khayal.
- Ragas: **Aiman (Yaman), Zilaf, Sazgiri**.
#### 6.2 Sufi Terms and Instruments
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sama** | Devotional music for ecstasy; spread by the **Chishtis** |
| **Qawwali** | Institutionalised in Chishti khanqahs with Indian ragas |
- Turks brought instruments like the **rabab**; Bahmanis brought rebab and oud to the Deccan.
!TRAP **Sarangi** = answer for "NOT of Indo-Islamic origin" (MPPSC key).

### 9. URDU AND REGIONAL LANGUAGES
- **Persian** = elite language of administration; Indo-Persian historiography (Minhaj, Barani, Afif, Isami) began.
- **Hindavi** grew into **Urdu** in camps and bazaars; Deccan form = **Dakhni**.
| Qutb Shahi language | Use |
|---|---|
| Persian | Court |
| **Telugu** | People, revenue records |
| **Dakhni Urdu** | Camps, bazaars |
- **First Telugu farmans**: **Abdullah Qutb Shah** and **Abul Hasan Tana Shah**.
- **Addanki Gangadhara**: first to dedicate a Telugu work (***Tapati Samvaranopakhyanam***) to a Muslim Sultan – **Ibrahim Qutb Shah**.
- Golconda = Persian literary centre under Mir Momin Astarabadi.
- **Apr 2026** (India-Iran ties): Persian contributions = sitar, santur, qawwali, ghazal and Sufi traditions.
