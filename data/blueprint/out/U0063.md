## U0063 | CHANDRAGUPTA II VIKRAMADITYA, KUMARAGUPTA, SKANDAGUPTA; THE HUNAS AND GUPTA DECLINE
### 1. CHRONOLOGY OF GUPTA RULERS
| Order | Ruler | Period / note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sri Gupta** | c. 240–280 CE; founder (dynasty set up c. 275 AD in one key); title **Maharaja** |
| 2 | Ghatotkacha | c. 280–319 CE |
| 3 | **Chandragupta I** | 319–335 CE; **Maharajadhiraja**; started the **Gupta Era (319–20 CE)** |
| 4 | **Samudragupta** | c. 335–375 CE; Prayag Prashasti; **Napoleon of India** (V.A. Smith, *Early History of India*); first **Parama Bhagawat** |
| 5 | **Ramagupta** | Brief, disputed reign |
| 6 | **Chandragupta II** | **c. 380–415 CE** |
| 7 | **Kumaragupta I** | c. 415–455 CE |
| 8 | **Skandagupta** | c. 455–467 CE; last great Gupta |
| 9 | Purugupta, Kumaragupta II, Budhagupta, **Narasimhagupta (Baladitya)**, Kumaragupta III | Later kings of the main line |
| 10 | **Vishnugupta** | c. 540–550 CE; **last ruler** |
- Exam key: Gupta dynasty ruled **319–500 AD**.
!KEY APPSC G1 2018: **Samudragupta → Chandragupta II → Kumaragupta I → Skandagupta**.
!TRAP Distractor rulers: **Bindusara** extended Mauryan power into the Deccan; **Chandragupta Maurya** founded the Mauryan empire; **Harsha** patronised Banabhatta.
### 2. CHANDRAGUPTA II VIKRAMADITYA
#### 2.1 Ramagupta Episode and Accession
- Source: **Devichandraguptam** of **Vishakhadatta**.
- Elder brother **Ramagupta** (son of Samudragupta) could not resist the Sakas and agreed to surrender queen **Dhruvadevi** to a Saka king; Chandragupta II killed the Saka king, then Ramagupta, and married Dhruvadevi (BPSC 2022).
#### 2.2 Titles and Names
- **Vikramaditya** (linked with the Saka victory), **Sakari** (Saka-conqueror), **Simhavikrama** (lion-slayer coins; his father = tiger-slayer coins).
- **Devagupta** (Sanchi and Vakataka inscriptions), **Devaraja** (Sanchi), **Devashri**.
#### 2.3 Saka Conquest
- Defeated **Rudrasimha III**, the **last Saka** ruler – took **western Malwa and Gujarat** and the western sea ports.
- Made **Ujjain** his **second capital**.
- Evidence: **Udayagiri cave** inscription of his minister **Virasena**; and his **silver coins** (Rupak) on the Saka model, about **30–33 grains** – the **strongest evidence**.
- **First Gupta ruler to issue silver coins.**
!TRAP The Saka-model coins were **silver**, not gold. Harishena was Samudragupta's poet, not Chandragupta II's minister.
#### 2.4 Marriage Alliances
| Alliance | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Naga** | Married **Kuberanaga** |
| **Vakataka** | Daughter **Prabhavati Gupta** married **Rudrasena II**; ruled as **regent** after his death – Gupta influence in the Deccan without annexation |
| Dhruvadevi | Married after killing Ramagupta |
#### 2.5 Court and Fa-Hien
- **Navaratnas** (tradition): **Kalidasa, Amarasimha, Varahamihira**, Dhanvantari, Vararuchi, Shanku, Ghatakarpara, Kshapanaka, Vetalabhatta.
- **Fa-Hien (Faxian)** came via the Silk Route and Central Asia, c. **399–412 CE** (about ten years), also Sri Lanka; **did not name the king**. First of three Chinese pilgrims – the others **Xuan Zang** and **I-Qing** (7th c.).
!TRAP Fa-Hien was a pilgrim, **not** one of the Navaratnas.
### 3. KUMARAGUPTA I (c. 415–455)
- Titles: **Shakraditya, Mahendraditya**.
- Founded **Nalanda** (c. 450 AD). An APPSC G1 key gave Asoka, but Kumaragupta I is the usual answer.
- Nalanda: identified by **Alexander Cunningham** (1861); ASI excavations from **1915–16**; destroyed by **Bakhtiyar Khalji** (end of 12th c.).
- Coins: gold type of king **feeding a peacock + Kartikeya**; Ashvamedha.
- Records: **Bilsad** (Gupta year 96 = 415 CE, earliest); **Damodarpur** copper plates (guilds acting as banks); **Mandsaur** prashasti by **Vatsabhatti** (silk-weavers' Sun Temple, Bandhuvarman).
- Threats: **Pushyamitras** and the first Huna attack late in his reign.
### 4. SKANDAGUPTA (c. 455–467)
- Repulsed the **Hunas twice**; title **Vikramaditya**; "Gupta ruler who defeated the Hunas" (UPPSC 2015).
- **Bhitari pillar** (Saidpur, Ghazipur, UP): victories over the **Pushyamitras and Hunas** (UPPSC 2014).
- **Junagadh:** governor Parnadatta repaired the Sudarshana lake.
- Gold coins: **bow and arrow, Garuda standard, Lakshmi**.
- Central control weakened; governors became **hereditary feudatories**.
!PAIR Kumaragupta I | Kartikeya + peacock | Nalanda || Skandagupta | Garuda + Lakshmi | Hunas repulsed
### 5. HUNA INVASIONS
- Hunas = **Hephthalites / White Huns** from Central Asia via the **Oxus** valley; took **Gandhara** (mid-5th c.).
| Wave / leader | Gupta reign | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| First attack | Late **Kumaragupta I** | Checked by **prince Skandagupta**; he beat them again as king (Guptas = dynasty most troubled by Hunas) |
| **Toramana** (second invasion) | Weak successors (late 5th c.) | Conquered up to **Eran**; by 485 CE Hunas held Punjab, Rajasthan, Kashmir, eastern Malwa, central India |
| **Mihirakula** (Toramana's son, c. 515) | Narasimhagupta | Tyrant, iconoclast, **persecuted Buddhists**; defeated by **Yashodharman** (and Narasimhagupta Baladitya, Maukharis) |
!TRAP Mihirakula was defeated by **Yashodharman**, not Skandagupta.
#### 5.1 Yashodharman of Mandasor
- **Aulikara** king / Gupta feudatory of Malwa; defeated Mihirakula in **528 CE**.
- Victory pillars at **Sondani** (near Mandasor), c. **532 CE**; Sondani inscription composed by **Vasula Kavi**.
- His rise **weakened** Gupta authority; it did not revive the empire.
### 6. INSCRIPTIONS ON HUNAS AND SUCCESSORS
| Inscription | Records |
|---|---|
| **Bhitari pillar** | Skandagupta vs Hunas |
| **Eran boar (Varaha)** | Toramana's first regnal year – Huna rule over Eran |
| **Eran, 510 CE** | Goparaja (Bhanugupta's general) – earliest sati record |
| **Mandasor / Sondani pillars** | Yashodharman's defeat of Mihirakula |
| **Gwalior** | Mihirakula's territory, including Gwalior |
| **Maukhari copper seal** | **Sarvavarman**; found at **Asirgarh** (MP), not Gaya; genealogy from **Harivarman** |
| Chausath Yogini temple (Morena, 1323 CE) | Kachchhapaghata **Devapala** built the temple |
- Banabhatta's **Harshacharita** also mentions the Hunas.
### 7. CAUSES OF GUPTA DECLINE
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| **External** | Repeated Huna invasions drained treasury and army; Pushyamitras |
| **Weak successors** | After Skandagupta |
| **Feudatory** | Land grants and hereditary governors created feudatories who broke away (Yashodharman; Valabhi, Bengal) |
| **Economic** | Land grants cut revenue – no professional army; loss of western India cut trade and **Roman silk** income; silk weavers migrated from Gujarat to Malwa (**AD 473**) |
| **Religious policy** | Later Guptas' leaning to Buddhism listed as a cause in some questions |
- Guptas donated the most villages to temples and Brahmins.
#### 7.1 Coinage Debasement
- Later Gupta gold coins = **less gold, more alloy**; coins slowly replaced by **payments in land**.
- No Gupta coins or inscriptions in **western Malwa and Saurashtra** after Skandagupta – loss of control.
!TRAP The **Arab invasion was not** a cause (UKPSC 2012). Decline led to regional kingdoms and a feudal order, not a return to Mauryan centralisation.
### 8. SUCCESSOR POWERS AND REGIONAL KINGDOMS
#### 8.1 North India before Harsha
| Power | Seat |
|---|---|
| **Later Guptas** | **Magadha** (mid-6th c. to c. 675 CE; not the imperial line) |
| **Maukharis** | **Kannauj** (Bihar–UP) |
| **Maitrakas** | **Valabhi** (Gujarat) – Dharapatta, Guhasena |
| **Pushyabhutis** | **Thanesar** (Haryana), later Kannauj |
| **Aulikaras** | **Mandsaur** (Yashodharman) |
| Gaudas | Bengal |
!KEY UPSC 2021: Guptas of Magadha, Pushyabhutis of Thanesar, Maukharis of Kannauj, Maitrakas of Valabhi – **not** Paramaras of Malwa or Yadavas of Devagiri (both later).
#### 8.2 Deccan and South
| Dynasty | Region | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Vakatakas** | Vidarbha, northern Maharashtra | Brahmins; Gupta contemporaries; **Harishena** patronised **Ajanta**; Prabhavati Gupta queen |
| **Kadambas** | **Banavasi** (Karnataka) | Founded by **Mayurasharma** (not Nalanda) |
| **Vishnukundins** | **Andhra** | **Madhava Varma II** (440–495 CE), greatest – over 100 battles, 11 Ashvamedhas; Indrabhattaraka Varma defeated by Maukhari **Ishanavarma** (Haraha inscription) |

### 11. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. **Saka-model coins** = silver, Chandragupta II.
2. **Mihirakula** defeated by **Yashodharman**; Hunas repulsed by **Skandagupta**.
3. **Toramana** led the second invasion; Eran boar inscription.
4. **Nalanda** = Kumaragupta I, not Ashoka or Mayurasharma.
5. **Fa-Hien** never named the king and was not a Navaratna.
6. **Arab invasion** was not a cause of decline.
7. **Paramaras and Yadavas** were not post-Gupta powers before Harsha.
8. **Maukhari seal** = Sarvavarman, Asirgarh.
