## U0506 | MSMEs – DEFINITION, CONTRIBUTION, PROBLEMS, CREDIT AND SUPPORT SCHEMES
### 1. MSME DEFINITION – EVOLUTION
#### 1.1 Timeline
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1950 | Small-scale unit = investment up to **₹5 lakh** (APPSC 2022) |
| 1991 | Industrial Policy raised small-unit limit **₹35 lakh → ₹60 lakh**; ancillary **₹45 lakh → ₹75 lakh** |
| 12 May 2005 | SME Development Bill introduced |
| **2 Oct 2006** | **MSMED Act, 2006** in force – separate manufacturing and services limits |
| 13 May 2020 | Revised definition announced (Atmanirbhar Bharat package) |
| **1 June 2020** | **Composite** investment + turnover criterion notified (effective 1 July 2020) |
| 1 April 2025 | Limits raised (Budget 2025-26) |
| Aug 2026 | MSMED (Amendment) Bill passed by Rajya Sabha |
#### 1.2 Original MSMED Act 2006 Limits (investment only)
| Category | Manufacturing (plant and machinery) | Services (equipment) |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | up to ₹25 lakh | up to ₹10 lakh |
| Small | up to **₹5 crore** | up to **₹2 crore** |
| Medium | up to ₹10 crore | up to ₹5 crore |
- PYQ key: small-scale manufacturing = P&M **above ₹5 crore, not more than ₹10 crore** (i.e. medium band boundary in 2006 terms).
!TRAP Composite criterion notified **1 June 2020**, NOT 2010. Latest classification year = **2020**.
### 2. CURRENT DEFINITION – COMPOSITE CRITERIA
- Basis: **investment in plant and machinery/equipment AND annual turnover** (APPSC 2024); manufacturing/services distinction removed.
| Category | 2020 (investment / turnover) | From 1 April 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | ₹1 cr / ₹5 cr | **₹2.5 cr / ₹10 cr** |
| Small | ₹10 cr / ₹50 cr | **₹25 cr / ₹100 cr** |
| Medium | ₹50 cr / ₹250 cr | **₹125 cr / ₹500 cr** |
- Memory aid (2020): investment **1-10-50**, turnover **5-50-250**. Example: ₹8 cr investment, ₹40 cr turnover = **small** (2020 limits).
- AP follows the same national definition (AP Survey 2024-25 still quotes the 2020 limits).
- Older PYQ key gave a micro turnover ceiling of **₹15 crore**; in force: ₹5 crore (2020), ₹10 crore (2025).
- **Nature and size of an industry** is judged by capital investment, labour force, power consumption (turnover also relevant).
### 3. MSMED ACT, 2006 – KEY PROVISIONS
- **Chapter V (delayed payments):** buyer must pay an MSE within **45 days**; else **compound interest at 3 times the RBI bank rate** (Sec. 16).
- Disputes go to the **MSE Facilitation Council**.
- **MSMED (Amendment) Bill 2026:** every **CPSE must settle MSME invoices on TReDS**; national digital registration platform.
#### 3.1 Public Procurement Policy for MSEs Order, 2012
- Central ministries and CPSEs must buy **25%** of annual procurement from MSEs (raised from **20% in 2018**).
- Sub-targets: **4% SC/ST-owned**, **3% women-owned** MSEs.
- Achieved FY26 (to 1 Dec 2025): **44.76%**. GeM: ₹18.4 lakh crore GMV, 11 lakh+ MSEs (Aug 2026).
!NUM 45 days = payment limit | 3x bank rate = penalty interest | 25% / 4% / 3% = procurement targets
### 4. SSI RESERVATION AND COMMITTEES
#### 4.1 Reservation of Items
- Began **1967 with 47 items** → peak about **873 (1984)** → last **20 items dereserved in 2015**.
- **Abid Hussain Committee (1997)** recommended **abolishing reservation** and wide deregulation.
#### 4.2 Committees – Chronology
| Year | Committee | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| **1955** | **Karve** (Planning Commission) | Village and small industries in the **Second Plan**; avoid technological unemployment, decentralised development |
| 1992 | **P.R. Nayak** (RBI) | **Working-capital** credit to SSIs (20% of turnover) |
| 1997 | **Abid Hussain** | Small and medium industries – deregulation, competitiveness |
| 2001 | S.P. Gupta | Credit access for SSI |
| 2010 | PM's Task Force on MSMEs | Integrated credit, technology, infrastructure |
| 2019 | **U.K. Sinha** (RBI) | MSME finance, stressed-asset fund, timely payments |
!TRAP Karve = **1955**, not 1995. Order: Karve → Nayak → Abid Hussain → S.P. Gupta → U.K. Sinha.
### 5. ROLE AND CONTRIBUTION
#### 5.1 Role of Small and Cottage Industries
- **Labour-intensive**, need little capital → large employment.
- **Disperse industry** to rural and semi-urban areas; reduce **concentration of economic power** and regional imbalance.
- Act as **ancillaries** to large industry; priority since the **First Plan**.
- Small-scale sector = about **40% of manufacturing gross turnover** (since 1992).
#### 5.2 Latest Contribution (Rajya Sabha, 4 Aug 2026)
| Indicator | Figure |
|---|---|
| Share in GDP | **31%** |
| Share in manufacturing output | **36%** |
| Share in exports | **41%** |
| Enterprises | 6.5 crore+ |
| Outstanding MSME credit | ₹38.35 lakh crore (₹10 lakh crore in 2014-15) |
- Udyam: **6.63 crore** registered (1 July 2020 – 31 July 2025), incl. 2.72 crore informal micro units; **micro = 95.88%**; 46.7 lakh+ women-owned (Aug 2025).
!KEY Latest = 31 – 36 – 41 = GDP – manufacturing – exports.
### 6. PROBLEMS OF MSMEs
| Problem | Detail |
|---|---|
| Credit gap | Costly/scarce credit; **shortage of working capital** = main SSI problem |
| Delayed payments | **MSME Samadhaan (2017)** – 2.56 lakh+ cases, ₹55,244 crore |
| Technology | No latest technology; poor quality |
| Marketing | Weak marketing and exports |
| Others | Power shortage, labour problems, poor managerial skills, **industrial sickness**, compliance burden, skill gap |
- **IPR 1948** listed handicaps: **raw materials, finance, marketing, technology**.
!TRAP **Strikes and lockouts** are a problem of large-scale industries, NOT SSIs. Excess cheap credit is NOT an MSME problem.
### 7. SUPPORT INSTITUTIONS
| Institution | Year / Act | HQ / note | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SIDBI** | **2 April 1990**, SIDBI Act 1989 (APPSC 2019) | **Lucknow** | Apex bank for MSME finance and promotion |
| **SFCs** | SFC Act, **1951** | State level | Term loans to small industry |
| **KVIC** | KVIC Act **1956**; set up **April 1957** | **Mumbai** | Khadi and village industries; runs PMEGP |
| Ministry of MSME | **2007** | – | SSI + Agro and Rural Industries ministries merged |
- **SIDBI:** implements **PM SVANidhi**; co-founded **CGTMSE** with Ministry of MSME; tied up with **SVC Co-operative Bank (July 2022)** (APPSC 2021); runs **Udyam Abhilasha**.
- **KVIC** replaced the **All India Khadi and Village Industries Board (1953, First Plan)**; statutory, under Ministry of MSME; covers khadi, village and coir industries.
- **Udyami helpline:** Ministry of MSME call centre, opened by Dr Manmohan Singh.
### 8. CREDIT GUARANTEE AND PAYMENT TOOLS
| Tool | Key fact |
|---|---|
| **CGTMSE** – Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises | **2000**; Ministry of MSME + SIDBI; **collateral-free** guarantee; corpus **Govt : SIDBI = 4:1** |
| **PMMY (MUDRA)** | Collateral-free up to **₹20 lakh**: Shishu, Kishor, Tarun, **Tarun Plus** |
| **ECLGS** | Covid-era (2020) emergency credit guarantee; **ECLGS 5.0 (May 2026)** – 100% guarantee for units hit by West Asia crisis |
| **TReDS** (2017) | **RBI-regulated** online invoice discounting – **RXIL, M1xchange, Invoicemart** |
| MCGS-MSME | Modified Mar 2026 to include services |
- **RBI (Feb 2026):** no collateral for MSE loans up to **₹20 lakh**; banks may go up to ₹25 lakh.
- **Budget 2026-27:** all CPSE purchases from MSMEs on TReDS; CGTMSE guarantees for TReDS discounting; GeM-TReDS link; receivables as asset-backed securities.
### 9. SUPPORT AND ENTREPRENEURSHIP SCHEMES
#### 9.1 Entrepreneurship Schemes
| Scheme | Key fact |
|---|---|
| **PMEGP (2008)** | = **PMRY + REGP**; credit-linked margin-money subsidy for new micro units; run by **KVIC** (not NABARD); women up to 35% subsidy |
| **Stand-Up India (April 2016)** | **Greenfield** units of **SC/ST or women**; non-individual units need **51%** shareholding and control |
| **Udyam Abhilasha (2021)** | **SIDBI** awareness campaign in aspirational districts |
| **Udyam Registration (2020)** | Free, paperless, self-declaration; linked to GeM, income tax, GST, TReDS |
| **Startup India** | DPIIT recognition: entity up to 10 years old; revised turnover limit ₹200 crore (earlier ₹100 crore); deep-tech: 20 years, ₹300 crore |
| **PM Vishwakarma** | Artisans in **18 trades**; ₹500/day training stipend; collateral-free credit up to **₹3 lakh at 5%** |
- **Sequence:** Ministry of MSME (2007) → PMEGP (2008) → Stand-Up India (2016) → Udyam (2020).
#### 9.2 Other Support Schemes
- **RAMP:** **World Bank-supported**; strengthens Centre-State MSME institutions, market access, credit.
- **Self-Reliant India Fund (2021):** equity to MSMEs through daughter funds.
- **MSE-CDP:** cluster development – grants for **common facility centres** (testing labs, training centres, raw-material depots, effluent treatment).
- **Budget 2026-27 "Champion MSMEs":** ₹10,000 crore **SME Growth Fund**, ₹2,000 crore SRI Fund top-up, "Corporate Mitras".
