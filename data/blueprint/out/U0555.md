## U0555 | BIFURCATION AND AP'S NATURAL RESOURCE ENDOWMENT
### 1. LAND, POPULATION AND COAST
| Item | Residual AP | Telangana |
|---|---|---|
| Area | **1,60,205 → 1,62,970 sq km** (7 Khammam mandals added) | 1,12,077 sq km |
| Population (2011) | **4.96 crore** | 3.52 crore |
| APRA ratio | **58.32** | 41.68 |
| Coast and ports | **Entire coastline, all ports** | Landlocked |
- Coastline **1,053.07 km** (2023-24), **3rd** after Gujarat, Tamil Nadu (older: 974 km).
- **One major port (Visakhapatnam)**; **17** non-major ports, **Krishnapatnam** most cargo; **4** greenfield (Ramayapatnam most advanced).
- ~**29%** of India's fish; ~**75%** of cultured-shrimp exports.
### 2. MINERALS AND ENERGY
| Mineral | Where in AP |
|---|---|
| **Barytes** | **Mangampet (YSR Kadapa)** – India's largest deposit |
| **Uranium** | **Tummalapalle (YSR Kadapa)** |
| Limestone | Kurnool, Kadapa, Guntur, Palnadu, NTR; AP **3rd** after Rajasthan, MP |
| Beach sand (ilmenite, rutile, zircon, garnet, monazite) | Srikakulam, Visakhapatnam, Kakinada coast |
| Bauxite | Araku, Chintapalli, Paderu |
- **Coal mainly to Telangana** (SCCL: 51% TG, 49% GoI); **Twelfth Schedule** keeps SCCL linkages for AP plants.
- Onshore oil/gas royalty: **producing State**.
- **CGS power** split by **five years' consumption** → AP ≈ **46%**.
- Srisailam, Nagarjuna Sagar: **left bank** Telangana, **right bank** AP. June 2021: AP protested TG generating **below 834 feet** at Srisailam.
### 3. WATER AND AGRICULTURAL ENDOWMENT
- **Schedule XI**: tribunal allocations unchanged; priority **drinking water > irrigation > power**.
- Krishna: KWDT-I **811 TMC**, ad hoc **AP 512 : TG 299 (66 : 34)**, not 58:42.
- **Polavaram** = national project (**Sec. 90**), TG consent deemed; **960 MW**, **7.20 lakh acres**, **80 TMC** to Krishna basin.
- Godavari, Krishna, Penna **deltas**; Rayalaseema rain-shadow; **6 agro-climatic zones**.
### 4. FOREST COVER (ISFR 2023)
- **ISFR**: biennial, **FSI Dehradun**; forest cover = canopy **≥10%**, any ownership.
| Measure | Figure |
|---|---|
| Forest area incl. scrub | **38,436.58 sq km = 23.59%** (scrub 8,351.62) |
| **Forest + tree cover** | **21.74%** |
| **Recorded Forest Area** | **37,258 sq km**, **22.86%** (ISFR 2021); **8th** |
| Protected areas | **3 NPs, 13 WLS, 1 Tiger Reserve** |
- Old districts: most cover **Visakhapatnam**; largest area **YSR Kadapa**; least **Krishna**.
- Policy 1988 aim **33%** (66% hills).
!TRAP RFA rank is 8th (ISFR 2019, 2021), not 5th.
### 5. FOREST TYPES AND RED SANDERS
- Mostly **dry deciduous**.
| Type | Where |
|---|---|
| Mangrove (**Coringa**) | Godavari and Krishna mouths |
| Moist deciduous | North-coastal hills |
| Dry deciduous | Kadapa-Kurnool |
| Thorn-scrub | Interior Rayalaseema |
- **Nallamala** largest forest; **NSTR** India's largest tiger reserve.
- **Red sanders**: **endemic, southern Eastern Ghats**; IUCN **Endangered** (2021); **CITES Appendix II**. Ranges: **Seshachalam, Veligonda, Lankamala, Nallamala, Talakona**.
### 6. FOREST LAWS AND SCHEMES
| Law / scheme | Key point |
|---|---|
| **AP Forest Act 1967** | **S.3** power to reserve; **S.4** preliminary notice; **S.15** final notification; **S.28** protected forest **ceases** to be protected |
| FCA 1980 | Renamed **Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam** from **29 Dec 2023** (amended, not repealed) |
| **PESA 1996** | Gram Sabha controls MFP |
| **FRA 2006** | MFP ownership, nistar, PVTG habitat; Gram Sabha clears **public utilities** (not mining) |
| **Nagar Van Yojana** (Central) | **61** Nagar Vans |
| **Nagaravanam** (State) | **37** city forests + 7 Temple Eco-parks |
| **Green India Mission** | 2011, NAPCC; **5 mha new + 5 mha quality** (not 10 mha new) |
| **CAMPA** (MoEFCC) | Compensatory levies for afforestation |
- **MFP**: **GCC** (Girijan Cooperative Corporation), HQ **Visakhapatnam**, registered **1956**, named GCC **1970**; buys MFP, runs **MSP for MFP**. **Beedi leaf** nationalised **1971** (AP MFP (Regulation of Trade) Act); forest department collects and sells.
- **JFM** via **Vana Samrakshana Samithis** from **1992**; **Community Forest Management** from **2002**.
- **FRA in AP** (31 Aug 2026): **2,90,874 claims**, **2,27,300 individual titles**.
