## U0789 | AIR POLLUTION – SOURCES, POLLUTANTS, NAAQS, AQI, NCAP, STUBBLE BURNING AND ACID RAIN
### 1. SOURCES OF AIR POLLUTION
#### 1.1 Natural vs Anthropogenic
| Basis | Examples |
|---|---|
| Natural | **Forest fires** (APPSC 2025), **volcanic eruptions**, dust storms; volcanoes = natural SOx; **lightning** = natural NOx |
| Anthropogenic | Vehicles, power plants, industry, biomass burning, agriculture (ammonia, methane), waste burning |
- Volcanic eruptions emit all four: pyroclastic debris, ash and dust, nitrogen compounds, sulphur compounds.
- Main source in **Indian cities: automobile emissions** (APPSC 2025). Main pollutants in metros: **CO and SO2**.
- NCR: **transport** = largest primary source, **23% of winter load** (CAQM synthesis report, Jan 2026). Delhi is the city most often in the news.
- Vehicle/fuel combustion products: CO, NOx, VOCs, PM, unburnt hydrocarbons, lead.
- **Steel industry** emits SOx, NOx, CO and CO2 (all four).
- **Crop residue burning** releases CO, methane, ozone, SO2 (also NOx, CO2, black carbon, VOCs).
- Tree for preventing dust pollution: **Sita Ashok**. Sleeping under a tree at night is harmful – it releases **CO2**.
#### 1.2 Fuel → Emissions
| Fuel | Emissions |
|---|---|
| Coal | High CO2, SO2, NOx, PM, ash – most polluting |
| Petrol / diesel | CO2, CO, NOx, unburnt HC, PM; **diesel gives more PM** than petrol |
| LPG / CNG | Less CO, HC, PM; still emit CO2; LPG burns without smoke |
| Biogas | Low; mainly CO2, traces of H2S when untreated |
| **Hydrogen** | **Cleanest fuel**; only water – no carbon, CO, SO2 or particles |
| Wood, cow-dung | Smoke, CO, fine particles – indoor pollution |
### 2. PRIMARY VS SECONDARY POLLUTANTS AND SMOG
| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Primary** (emitted directly) | CO, SO2, NOx, PM, **lead** from vehicles |
| **Secondary** (formed in air) | **O3, PAN**, photochemical smog, **ammonium sulphate**, ammonium nitrate |
| Smog | Formed from | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| **Classical / London** | SO2, smoke, particulates + fog | **Cold, humid** |
| **Photochemical / Los Angeles** (summer smog) | NOx + VOCs + sunlight → **ozone, PAN** (peroxyacetyl nitrate) | **Warm, sunny**, traffic-heavy |
- Photochemical smog **always contains ozone**; nitrogen oxides are the key input. Smog is a **secondary** pollutant.
- **Asian Brown Cloud** (2002): soot, **black carbon**, sulphate aerosols over South Asia and Indian Ocean, dry season (**January–March**); from biomass and fossil-fuel burning.
!TRAP London smog = cold + SO2; Los Angeles smog = warm + sunlight. Ground-level ozone is not emitted directly.
### 3. POLLUTANTS → SOURCE AND HEALTH EFFECT
| Pollutant | Source | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **CO** | Incomplete combustion; vehicle exhaust (main gas), cigarettes | Forms **carboxyhaemoglobin**; affinity for haemoglobin **200–250 times** oxygen → hypoxia; "most dangerous" |
| SO2 | Fossil fuels, coal power, smelters, diesel | Eye/airway irritation; acid rain; secondary PM2.5 |
| NOx / NO2 | Vehicles, power plants, boilers | Respiratory illness; acid rain, smog |
| PM2.5 / PM10 | Combustion, construction, road dust, biomass | Heart/lung disease; **PM2.5 crosses placenta** – pre-term birth, low birth weight (AIIMS, 3 June 2026) |
| Ground O3 | Photochemical (secondary) | Asthma, crop damage |
| **Lead** | Leaded petrol, smelters | **Brain damage**; high blood pressure, kidney damage |
| **H2S** | Decaying matter (main), sewage, refineries, landfills, volcanoes | **Rotten-egg smell**; toxic |
| Ammonia | Fertilisers + livestock (**77–80%**) | Secondary particle formation |
| **Magnetite particles** | Combustion, brake wear | Found in brain tissue; possible link to **Alzheimer's** |
| Fly ash | **Coal thermal plants** | – |
| Cadmium / PAHs | Metal smelting / incomplete burning | – |
- Cigarette smoke: **CO and benzene** (also HCN, arsenic, formaldehyde). Inorganic gaseous pollutants: HCN, H2S.
- Gases: CO2, methane = greenhouse; **stratospheric ozone** = UV shield, depleted by **CFCs**.
- Secondary PM2.5: **ammonium sulphate** ≈ one-third of India's PM2.5; ammonium nitrate up to 50%.
- IIT Delhi: curbing power-plant SO2 could prevent ~**1.24 lakh deaths** a year.
- **RSPM** = particles **< 5 µm** (generally taken as PM2.5).
!KEY "Vehicle gas that cuts the blood's oxygen-carrying capacity" = carbon monoxide.
### 4. INDOOR POLLUTION AND BIOINDICATORS
- Indoor sources: dust, smoke from solid fuels, mould, pollen, pesticides, **radon**, CO, **mosquito coils**; **maximum chemical pollution from burning coal**. Not only biological agents.
- Health: COPD, lung cancer, heart disease; **women and children** suffer most.
- Intercity buses: cabin air 2–3 times WHO limits (IntrCity study, 15 Mar 2026).
| Bioindicator | Indicates |
|---|---|
| **Lichens** | **SO2**; disappear / do not grow where SO2 is high |
| **Mosses** | Heavy-metal deposition |
### 5. STANDARDS – NAAQS AND WHO AQG 2021
#### 5.1 NAAQS
- Notified 1982; revised 1994 and **2009**; **12 pollutants**: PM10, PM2.5, SO2, NO2, CO, O3, NH3, Pb + **benzene, benzo(a)pyrene, arsenic, nickel**.
- **Ozone first included in 2009**.
| SO2 limit | Value |
|---|---|
| 24-hour | **80 µg/m³** |
| Annual, urban | 50 µg/m³ |
| Annual, ecologically sensitive | 20 µg/m³ |
#### 5.2 WHO AQG 2021 vs India
| Pollutant | WHO 2021 | India NAAQS |
|---|---|---|
| PM2.5 annual | **5 µg/m³** | **40 µg/m³** (8 times laxer) |
| PM2.5 24-hour | 15 µg/m³ | 60 µg/m³ |
| PM10 | 15 annual; 45 (24-hour) | 60 annual |
| NO2 annual | 10 µg/m³ | – |
#### 5.3 Health Burden and Rankings
- WHO: ~**7 million** deaths a year worldwide; fossil-fuel air pollution ~**1.72 million** premature deaths in India (2022).
- NCR winter PM2.5: nearly **35 times** the WHO limit.
- **IQAir World Air Quality Report 2025** (24 Mar 2026): **India 6th** most polluted country (5th in 2024); **Pakistan 1st**; most polluted city **Loni (UP)**; Delhi 4th and most polluted capital; only **14%** of cities met the WHO PM2.5 guideline.
- IQAir 2021: most polluted city **Bhiwadi**.
### 6. AQI AND MONITORING NETWORKS
#### 6.1 National Air Quality Index
- Launched **6 April 2015** by the PM under **Swachh Bharat**, developed at **IIT Kanpur**; first for 10 cities; run by **CPCB**.
- **8 pollutants**, scale **0–500**: PM10, PM2.5, NO2, SO2, CO, O3, NH3, Pb (**benzene and CO2 not included**). Among CO2, CO, NO2, SO2, methane – only **CO, NO2, SO2** count.
- Motto: **"One Number – One Colour – One Description"**; hourly from CAAQMS.
| AQI | Category | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| 0–50 | Good | Dark green |
| 51–100 | Satisfactory | Light green |
| 101–200 | Moderately Polluted | Yellow |
| 201–300 | Poor | Orange |
| 301–400 | Very Poor | Red |
| 401–500 | Severe | Maroon |
#### 6.2 Networks
| Network | Feature |
|---|---|
| **NAMP** | CPCB, **1984**; regularly monitors **SO2, NO2, PM10, PM2.5** (SPM) at all locations; **1,646 stations in 603 cities** (March 2026) |
| **CAAQMS** | PM2.5, PM10, SO2, NOx, CO, O3, NH3, benzene; data via **SAMEER** app (CPCB) |
| **SAFAR** | **MoES** through **IITM Pune**; unveiled 2018 by Dr Harsh Vardhan (Chandni Chowk); **SAFAR-AIR** app; 400 m forecast for Delhi tied to GRAP |
| Precipitation chemistry | **IMD**, under WMO **Global Atmosphere Watch / BAPMoN** |
- GAW has **33 global stations**; India has none among them.
!TRAP SAMEER = CPCB app; SAFAR-AIR = MoES app.
### 7. NATIONAL CLEAN AIR PROGRAMME (NCAP)
#### 7.1 Launch and Targets
- Launched **January 2019** by **MoEFCC**; coordinated by **CPCB with SPCBs** (not CPCB alone); funded via CSS "Control of Pollution". Legal base: **Air Act, 1981**.
- PAN-India **time-bound national strategy**; expands monitoring.
- Original target: **20–30% PM cut by 2024**, base **2017**.
- Revised (Sept 2022): **up to 40% PM10 cut** or meeting NAAQS (60 µg/m³) by **2025-26**, base **2017-18**.
- **130 cities in 24 States/UTs**. **Non-attainment city** = failed NAAQS for **five years** (2014–18 data); Million Plus cities also covered.
- Tools: City/State Action Plans; Steering, Monitoring and Implementation Committees.
- **PRANA** = Portal for Regulation of Air-pollution in Non-Attainment cities; launched **7 September 2021**.
!KEY APPSC 2025: GoI initiative to control industrial emissions = **NCAP** (Namami Gange, NRCP, NLCP are water programmes).
#### 7.2 Results and Rankings
- 2024-25 vs 2017-18: **103 cities** cut PM10; only **22 of 130** met PM10 NAAQS.
- **Varanasi**: highest % PM10 cut, 2017-18 to 2023-24 (APPSC 2024).
- **Swachh Vayu Survekshan**: annual since 2022-23; **Indore** first in SVS 2023 and SVS 2025 (million-plus); Amravati, Dewas (smaller cities).
- NGT (May 2026): **86%** of NCAP spend went to road dust.
- Critique: only 4% of chronically polluted towns on ₹10,500 crore; only 38 cities met annual targets; 37 of 131 finished source apportionment; PM10 the wrong yardstick vs PM2.5.
- **Swachh Vayu Diwas** (Clean Air for Blue Skies Day): **7 September**.

### 8. CONTROL MEASURES – VEHICLES, FUELS, INDUSTRY
#### 8.1 Vehicles
- **Catalytic converter**: **platinum, palladium, rhodium**; turns CO, HC, NOx into CO2, H2O, N2; needs **unleaded petrol**.
- Fuel-efficiency features: radial tyres, multipoint fuel injection, catalytic converter (**not streamlined body**). Fuel injection gives complete combustion.
| Norm | Fact |
|---|---|
| BS-III | 1 April 2005 in NCR + 10 cities |
| **BS-VI** | Nationwide **1 April 2020**; **BS-V skipped**; cuts NOx 70%, PM 80%, sulphur 80%; Stage II adds Real Driving Emission tests |
- Also: **PUC**, **RFID** pollution charges on Delhi-bound trucks, **vehicle scrappage policy (2021)**, **FAME-II** EV subsidies; public transport, **cycling (saves fuel – APPSC 2025)**, walking.
#### 8.2 Cleaner Fuels
| Fuel / programme | Feature |
|---|---|
| **CNG** | Mainly **methane** |
| Ethanol Blending | All new vehicles **E20**-compatible; ~95% pre-2020 vehicles not fully compatible |
| **Methanol Economy** | NITI Aayog |
| **SATAT** | Compressed biogas |
| National Green Hydrogen Mission | Cleanest fuel |
#### 8.3 Industrial Devices
| Device | Removes |
|---|---|
| **Electrostatic precipitator** | Fine particulates (fly ash) |
| Bag filter | Dust by fabric filtration |
| **Cyclone separator** | Coarse particles by centrifugal force |
| Wet scrubber | Gases (SO2) and particles |
| **FGD** | Up to **95% SO2** from flue gas |
- FGD mandated **2015**; only **39 of 537** coal plants fitted it; mandate **relaxed (2025)** citing costs – while India is the top SO2 emitter.
- **OCEMS** mandatory for red-category industries in Delhi-NCR; ban on pet coke and furnace oil.
### 9. STUBBLE BURNING, CAQM AND GRAP
- Stubble burning: **October–November**, **Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh**.
- In-situ: machinery subsidy (Happy Seeder), CSS on crop residue machinery (2018), **Pusa bio-decomposer (IARI)** – free to farmers (Delhi Govt), turns stubble into compost.
- Bihar suspended DBT registration of 1,807 farmers (2025-26).
- **CAQM**: Ordinance 2020 → **CAQM Act, 2021**; statutory; can shut violators; chairperson ranks as Secretary/Chief Secretary, 3 years or age 70. **Coal banned** in Delhi-NCR from **1 January 2023**. Jan 2026: SC (CJI Surya Kant) said causes still unclear.
| GRAP stage | AQI |
|---|---|
| I | Poor (201–300) |
| II | Very Poor (301–400) |
| III | Severe (401–450) |
| IV | Severe+ (above 450) |
- GRAP enforced by **CAQM**; supported by MoES 400 m forecasts.
- **VAYU/WAYU** roadside purifier: CSIR-**NEERI** with IIT Bombay; Delhi (ITO, Mukarba Chowk), Sept 2018.
- **Cloud seeding / artificial rain**: **silver iodide** (main), potassium iodide, **dry ice (solid CO2)**, common salt.
### 10. ACID RAIN – CAUSES AND CHEMISTRY
- Precipitation with **pH below 5.6**; typical **4.2–4.4**; each pH step = tenfold acidity. Normal rain ~5.6 (carbonic acid).
- Term: **Robert Angus Smith, 1872** (Manchester coal burning).
- Causes: **SO2 and NOx** (APPSC 2018: SO2 and NO2); main single factor **SO2**; main source industries (fossil fuels). **Not** CO, O3, CH4, CFCs or hydrogen.
| Gas | Anthropogenic | Natural |
|---|---|---|
| SOx | Coal power plants, sulphide-ore smelting, H2SO4 plants, refineries | Volcanoes, oceans, decay |
| NOx | Vehicles, power plants, biomass burning | Lightning, forest fires |
- Reaction: SO2 + NOx + O2 + water + photo-oxidants → **H2SO4 (~two-thirds, maximum)** + **HNO3 (~one-third)**.
- **Wet deposition** (rain, snow, fog) vs **dry deposition** (gases/particles settle) – about **half** returns dry.
!TRAP A-R item: CO2, SO2, NO2 dissolve in rain and gases pollute air – both true, but not cause-and-effect. Coal plants emit carbon oxides too, but acid rain is due to SO2 and NOx.
- Gold dissolves in nitric acid (aqua regia mix) – asked with acid rain.
### 11. ACID RAIN – EFFECTS, TAJ MAHAL, CONTROL, TREATIES
| Target | Effect |
|---|---|
| Lakes, fish | Lower pH; aluminium released |
| Forests, soil | Leaches **calcium, magnesium**; toxic aluminium |
| Buildings | Corrodes limestone, marble, metals |
| Health | Heavy metals into drinking water |
- **Marble cancer**: yellowing, lustreless marble (CaCO3) of the **Taj Mahal**; main threat **SO2**, notably **Mathura refinery** (APPSC 2018).
- **Taj Trapezium Zone** ~**10,400 sq km**: protects Taj, **Agra Fort, Fatehpur Sikri** (not Sanchi); Agra, Firozabad, Mathura, Hathras, Etah + Bharatpur.
- **M.C. Mehta v. Union of India (1996)**: SC barred coal/coke in TTZ → natural gas or relocation.
- Regions: **Scandinavia** (Norway, Sweden – most acid rain among options) from UK/central Europe – transboundary; industrial China; **India = largest SO2 emitter (2022), over 20%**.
- Control: **FGD scrubbers**, low-NOx burners, low-sulphur fuels (IMO ship caps), catalytic converters; **liming** = remedial, not preventive; US Clean Air Act cut SO2 >90%.
| Instrument | Year | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **LRTAP** (UNECE, Geneva) | **1979** | First binding treaty on transboundary air pollution |
| **Helsinki** Protocol | 1985 | **30% sulphur** cut |
| **Sofia** Protocol | 1988 | Freeze **NOx** |
| Gothenburg Protocol | 1999 | SO2, NOx, NH3, VOCs |
