## U0650 | INDIA - LITERACY: LEVELS, GENDER AND REGIONAL GAPS, TRENDS
### 1. CENSUS DEFINITION OF LITERACY
- **Literate** = a person aged **7 and above** who can **both read and write with understanding** in any language.
- One who can only read is **not literate**.
- Censuses **1951-1971** counted age **5+**; age **7+** from **1981**.
- **PLFS** uses the same 7+ definition but is a **sample survey** with different samples: not directly comparable with Census.
- Full literacy (ULLAS) = **95%+** literacy among persons aged **15+**.
- **Census 2027** adds a **digital literacy** question.
!TRAP Reading only = not literate; age limit is 7, not 5 or 6.
### 2. NATIONAL TREND SINCE 1951
#### 2.1 Decadal improvement
| Census | 1951 | 1961 | 1971 | 1981 | 1991 | 2001 | 2011 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Literacy % | **18.33** | 28.30 | 34.45 | 43.57 | 52.21 | 64.83 | **74.04** |
- Ascending order: 1951 < 1961 < 1981 < 2001.
- **Largest decadal gain: 1990s**; growth slowed in 2001-11 (India and AP).
- Male literacy about 27% → 82% in 60 years; women: less than 1 in 10 literate (1951) → 2 in 3 (2011).
#### 2.2 Census 2011 - provisional vs final
| India 2011 | Total | Male | Female |
|---|---|---|---|
| Provisional | **74.04%** (APPSC 2023) | 82.14% | 65.46% |
| Final | 73.0% (72.98%) | 80.9% (80.88%) | 64.6% (64.63%) |
- Both versions appear in questions; 74.04% is the most-quoted figure.
### 3. GENDER, RURAL-URBAN AND SOCIAL-GROUP GAPS
#### 3.1 Gender gap
- National gap 2011: about **16 points**; widest in **Rajasthan (about 28 points)**.
- PLFS 2023-24: gap about **12.6 points** (87.2 vs 74.6).
- Causes: distance to schools and safety, early marriage, dowry, son preference, poverty (**not** "universities are in cities").
#### 3.2 Rural-urban

|---|---|---|
| Census 2011 (provisional) | 68.9% | 85.0% |
| PLFS 2023-24 | 77.5% | 88.9% |
- Largest deficit: **rural women**.

- **Akshara Andhra (2025):** AP programme for **100% literacy**, about 25 lakh illiterate adults, universal literacy target 2027.
### 7. ADULT LITERACY PROGRAMMES IN ORDER
| Programme | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Farmers' Functional Literacy Programme | 1967-68 | Early adult education |
| **NAEP** | **2 Oct 1978** | Age **15-35** |
| **National Literacy Mission** | **5 May 1988** | TLC = its main mass-campaign strategy |
| Total Literacy Campaign | 1989-90 | **Ernakulam** first totally literate district (1990); **Kottayam** first fully literate town (1989) |
| **Saakshar Bharat** | **8 Sep 2009** (International Literacy Day) | Adults 15+, **focus on women**; planned up to March 2012 |
| **ULLAS** (NILP) | 2022 | FY 2022-23 to 2026-27 |
- **Velland (Kerala)** = India's first fully **computer-literate** panchayat.
!TRAP NAEP = 15-35; Saakshar Bharat and ULLAS = 15+.
### 8. ULLAS - NEW INDIA LITERACY PROGRAMME
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Full form | **Understanding of Lifelong Learning for All in Society** |
| Other names | Nav Bharat Saaksharta Karyakram / New India Literacy Programme |
| Type | Centrally Sponsored, Ministry of Education, volunteer-based; Budget 2021-22 |
| Target | Non-literates aged **15+** |
| Five components | FLN, critical life skills, basic education, vocational skills, continuing education |
- Fully literate sequence: **Ladakh** (first UT, 2024) → **Mizoram** (first State, 2025) → Goa, Tripura, Himachal Pradesh, Sikkim → **Uttarakhand 6th (Jul 2026**, 98%+).
### 9. SCHOOL EDUCATION POLICY AND SCHEMES
#### 9.1 NEP 2020
- First education policy of the 21st century; replaced **NPE 1986** after 34 years. **Ramamurti Committee (1990)** reviewed NPE.
| Stage | Foundational (5) | Preparatory (3) | Middle (3) | Secondary (4) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ages | 3-8 | 8-11 | 11-14 | 14-18 |
- **5+3+3+4** (APPSC 2025) = 12 years school + 3 pre-school.
- Targets: **100% school GER by 2030**; **50% higher-education GER by 2035**; 2 crore out-of-school children back; **100% youth and adult literacy by 2030** (not 2025).
- **NIPUN Bharat:** foundational literacy by Grade 3; deadline moved from 2025 to **2026-27**.
#### 9.2 Schemes
| Scheme | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **Operation Blackboard** | **1987** (under NPE 1986) | Basic facilities for primary schools in backward areas; two rooms, two teachers, teaching aids (APPSC 2025) |
| **Samagra Shiksha** | 2018 | Pre-school to Class 12; subsumes **SSA, RMSA and Teacher Education**; supports NEP and RTE 2009; covers RPwD Act 2016 |
| **Mana Badi Nadu-Nedu (AP)** | **14 Nov 2019** | Nine facilities: toilets, drinking water, furniture, paint, repairs, lights and fans, green chalkboards, **English labs**, compound walls |
!TRAP English labs = Nadu-Nedu, not Operation Blackboard.
### 10. EDUCATIONAL ATTAINMENT
- **FLN Index** (Institute for Competitiveness for **EAC-PM**): 2nd edition - **Punjab** highest (APPSC 2022); 2021 - **West Bengal** first among larger States in primary education.
- **AISHE 2023-24** (released 8 Jul 2026): GER about **30**, female GER **31.2**, GPI 1.08, enrolment 4.50 crore.
- Female higher-education GER 28.5 → **30.2** (2021-23), above male GER; gender parity at all school levels.
- **UDISE+ 2024-25** dropout: preparatory **2.3%**, middle **3.5%**, secondary **8.2%** (down from 3.7, 5.2, 10.9).
- NFHS-5: women's literacy **71.5%**; women with 10+ years of schooling **41%** (NFHS-4 35.7%).
- **International Literacy Day: 8 September** (UNESCO, since 1966, first observed 1967); 2026 theme "Literacy for people, the planet and prosperity".
