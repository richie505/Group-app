## U0420 | CONSUMER RIGHTS – CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT 2019, CCPA, E-COMMERCE RULES
### 1. CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT, 2019
- **Replaced** the Consumer Protection Act, **1986**.
- **Six rights:** safety, information, choice, to be heard, redressal, **consumer awareness** (no right to private ownership).
- **Consumer** includes **online/electronic** purchases for personal use; buyer for **resale/business** is left out.
- New: **CCPA**, **product liability**, e-commerce rules, penalties for adulterated goods, **mediation cells**, e-filing.
- File where the complainant **lives or works**; complaint **deemed admitted** if not decided on within **21 days**.
- **Filing fee:** **nil** up to **Rs 5 lakh**; Rs 200 to Rs 7,500 above that.
- Consumers may get **food samples tested**; a **legal heir** of a deceased consumer may complain; complaint lies on any fault or failure.
### 2. CONSUMER COMMISSIONS
| Commission | Jurisdiction (2021 Rules) | Appeal |
|---|---|---|
| **District** | Up to **Rs 50 lakh** | To State in **45 days** |
| **State** | **Rs 50 lakh – Rs 2 crore** | To National in **30 days** |
| **National (NCDRC, 1988)** | Above **Rs 2 crore** | To Supreme Court in **30 days** |
- Jurisdiction by **value of consideration paid**, not compensation claimed.
- Called **Consumer Forums** under the 1986 Act; **quasi-judicial**, not civil courts.
- **e-Daakhil** online filing now merged into **e-Jagriti**.
!TRAP Original 2019 slabs (Rs 1 crore / Rs 10 crore) were revised in 2021. A Rs 40 lakh complaint goes to the District Commission.
### 3. CENTRAL CONSUMER PROTECTION AUTHORITY
- Executive agency; in force from **24 July 2020**.
- Core: **false or misleading advertisements**; inquire, order **recalls and refunds**, file **class-action** complaints, impose penalties.
| Section 21 | First | Repeat |
|---|---|---|
| Penalty | Up to **Rs 10 lakh** | Up to **Rs 50 lakh** |
| **Endorser** ban | Up to **1 year** | Up to **3 years** |
- Fined **Meta Rs 10 lakh** (walkie-talkie listings).
- **Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954** bans objectionable drug ads.
### 4. E-COMMERCE RULES
- **E-Commerce Rules 2020**: **marketplace** entity = platform only; **inventory** entity = owns goods; grievance officer acknowledges in **48 hours**, redresses in **one month**.
- **Dark Patterns Guidelines 2023**: **13** dark patterns (false urgency, basket sneaking).
- **Amendment Rules 2026**: notified **9 Sept 2026**, effective **1 January 2027**; country of origin; prior price = lowest in **30 days**; sponsored listings disclosed; annual self-audit.
### 5. HELPLINE, CAMPAIGNS, MEDIATION
- **National Consumer Helpline: 1915** (toll-free); e-commerce firms are partners; a helpline, not a court.
- **Jago Grahak Jago**; mascot **"Jagriti"**.
- **Mediation cells** attached to District, State and National Commissions (not High Courts).

### 7. INTERNATIONAL AND DAYS

- **World Consumer Rights Day**: **15 March** (J.F. Kennedy's 1962 speech).

- AP: **1st** in consumer justice, India Justice Report 2026.
