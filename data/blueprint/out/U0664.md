## U0664 | ANDHRA PRADESH – SC AND ST POPULATION; MAJOR TRIBAL GROUPS AND PVTGS
### 1. SCHEDULED TRIBES – CONSTITUTIONAL BASIS AND NUMBERS
#### 1.1 Constitutional Basis
- **Article 342**: the **President** notifies STs after consulting the **Governor**; only **Parliament** can amend the list.
- **Article 366(25)**: defines Scheduled Tribes.
#### 1.2 Notified STs
| Item | Number |
|---|---|
| SC and ST Orders (Amendment) Act, **1976** – undivided AP | **33** STs (APPSC-G1 2022) |
| Area-restricted among them | **5** – Gowdu, Mali, Nayak, Thoti, Valmiki |
| Present AP | **34** ST communities |
| India – Census 2011 | **698**; over 700 notified under Art. 342 |
| State with most notified tribes | **Odisha (62)** |
| India's largest tribe | **Bhil** |
- Gowdu, Nayak and Valmiki are STs only in the **Agency Tracts** (Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam, East and West Godavari districts of the time).
- **Feb 2024**: Porja and Savara sub-groups added to AP's ST list (Bondo Porja, Khond Porja, Konda Savara).
!TRAP 33 = STs under the 1976 Act; 34 = present AP; 698 = Census 2011 India count.

#### 4.2 Godavari Valley
- **Koya** – Godavari basin (Chintoor, Polavaram, Eluru, ASR); call themselves **Koyi Toor**; once soldiers of feudal lords.
- **Konda Reddi** – Godavari river hills, **Rampa** (East and West Godavari); **PVTG**; podu.
#### 4.3 Southern Forests and Plains
| Tribe | Area |
|---|---|
| **Chenchu** | **Nallamala** forests around Srisailam, Ahobilam |
| **Yanadi** | **Nellore** plains, Chittoor; original home near **Pulicat Lake** |
| **Yerukula** | Almost every plains district (Korava in TN, Korcha in Karnataka) |
| **Sugali** (Lambadi, Banjara) | Rayalaseema and central Andhra; settlements = **thandas** |
| Nakkala (Kurvikaran) | Plains and commercial areas |
- **Chenchus** = "those who live under trees"; the "**aboriginal tribe**" of AP (APPSC-GS 2025); myth of **Ahobilam Narasimha** marrying **Chenchita**; 47,315 Chenchus in Nallamala (ITDA, Jul 2026).
!TRAP Not AP tribes: **Munda, Santhal, Bhil** (APPSC 2018, 2025).
### 5. PVTGS OF AP
- **12 PVTGs** (list issued for AP and Telangana together); **Odisha 13** (most); India **75** (18 States + 1 UT); Bihar 9.
| Cluster | PVTGs | Habitat |
|---|---|---|
| Gadaba / Poraja | **Bodo Gadaba, Gutob Gadaba, Bondo Poraja, Khond Poraja, Parengi Poraja** | Araku Valley / Visakhapatnam agency |
| Khond | **Dongria Khond, Kutia Khond** | North coastal Khond belt |
| Hill / forest | **Chenchu, Konda Reddi, Konda Savara** | Nallamala; Godavari hills; Srikakulam hills |
| Telangana side | **Kolam, Thoti** | – |
- Criteria: **pre-agricultural technology**, low literacy, economic backwardness, **stagnant population** (not rapid growth).
- **1973 Dhebar Commission** created PTG → renamed **PVTG in 2006**.
- 2024 ST Order amendment added 7 PVTG communities to ST lists – 4 in Odisha, 3 in AP; total stays 75.
!TRAP Yerukula is NOT a PVTG; Konda Reddi, Thoti, Bodo Gadaba are.
### 6. LIVELIHOODS
| Tribe | Occupation |
|---|---|
| Chenchu | Hunting, food gathering; semi-nomadic |
| Konda Reddi, Koya, Porja, Savara | **Podu** and hill agriculture |
| Yanadi | Fishing, **rice**, **herbal medicine** |
| Yerukula | **Basket** and rope making, **fortune-telling**, pig-rearing |
| Lambada | Once traders/transporters; now agriculture and animal husbandry |
| Kolam | Basket weaving |
- **Podu** = slash-and-burn; jowar/maize, then left; causes erosion and forest fires; in **ASR and Parvathipuram Manyam** (not Nallamala).
- **GCC** (Girijan Cooperative Corporation, **1956**): buys **MFP**, runs interior **PDS depots**, gives credit; trademark "**GCC Araku Valley Coffee**"; Rs 250.76 crore into tribal economy (2024-25); 16 petrol bunks.
- **Araku coffee** ≈ **1 lakh acres**; Van Dhan Vikas Kendras 415 sanctioned (258 working); annatto at Maredumilli on about 10,000 acres.
- **NSTR closure** (Jul 2026) cuts Chenchus off from forest produce.

### 8. LAWS AND INSTITUTIONS
| Year | Law / event |
|---|---|
| 1956 | GCC |
| 1959 / 1970 | **1/70 Regulation** – bars land transfer from tribals to non-tribals and between non-tribals in Scheduled Areas |
| **1997** | **Samatha** – no leasing of Scheduled Area land to non-tribals or private miners |
| 2020 | SC struck down **G.O. Ms. 3** (100% ST teacher quota) |

### 9. MOVEMENTS AND ISSUES
- **Bauxite**: **Araku, Paderu, Chintapalli** agency; PVTG rights; CPI(M) says surveys bypass PESA Gram Sabhas and TAC (Jul 2026).
- **Polavaram**: about **276 villages** submerged (ASR, Eluru); opposed by **Koya and Kondh** groups.
- Projects (pumped storage, bauxite, Polavaram) clash with **1/70 Regulation, PESA, Forest Rights Act**.
| Revolt | Year |
|---|---|
| Konda Reddi rising | 1802–03 |
| Rampa rising | 1879 |
| Rampa rebellion – **Alluri Sitarama Raju** (forest laws, muttadari) | **1922–24** |
