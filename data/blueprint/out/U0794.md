## U0794 | CARBON EMISSIONS, CARBON FOOTPRINT, CARBON SEQUESTRATION, CARBON CREDITS AND CARBON TRADING
### 1. CARBON EMISSIONS – SOURCES AND EMITTERS
- Sources: **fossil-fuel burning** – petrol and diesel in automobiles; **coal** in thermal power (gives CO2, NOx, SO2). Photosynthesis removes CO2; respiration and decay release it.
- Top emitters (fossil CO2, Global Carbon Budget 2025): **China (~31.8%) > USA (~12.7%) > India (~8.3%, 3rd)**.
- India per capita: about **2 tonnes** CO2 vs world ~4.7 t – **lowest in the G20**.
| India sector | Share of GHG emissions |
|---|---|
| Cement | ~6% |
| Aluminium | ~2.8% |
| Waste | 2.56% (2020, BUR-4) |
- **NITI Aayog** decarbonisation roadmaps (cement, aluminium, MSMEs): round-the-clock RE, nuclear, CCUS.
- Coal plants co-fire **biomass 5% (FY 2024-25) → 7% (FY 2025-26)**, watched by **SAMARTH** mission.
- **Carbon bombs**: fossil projects that could emit **over 1 Gt CO2** in their lifetime; India: **Gevra (Chhattisgarh)**, **Rajmahal (Jharkhand)** coal mines.
- **2035 NDC**: **47%** emission-intensity cut vs 2005; **60%** non-fossil power capacity; extra sink of 3.5–4.0 bn t CO2e.
### 2. FOOTPRINT CONCEPTS AND SCOPES
| Footprint | Measures |
|---|---|
| **Carbon footprint** | Total GHGs produced, directly and indirectly, to support human activities (t CO2e/yr) (APPSC-GS 2025) |
| **Ecological footprint** | **Land and water** needed to produce what we consume and absorb our waste (APPSC-GS 2018) |
| **Water footprint** | Freshwater used – **blue, green, grey** water |
| Scope | Emissions |
|---|---|
| **Scope 1** | Direct, from owned sources (e.g. diesel generator) |
| **Scope 2** | Purchased electricity or heat |
| **Scope 3** | Rest of value chain (manufacture, transport of inputs) |
- **Common Carbon Metric** (supported by **UNEP**): carbon footprint of **building operations**.
!TRAP Carbon footprint = GHGs; Ecological = land + water; Water = blue, green, grey.
### 3. CARBON SINKS AND SEQUESTRATION
- **Natural sinks**: forests, soil, oceans (chlor-alkali plants are NOT).
- **Blue carbon**: carbon captured by oceans and coastal ecosystems – **mangroves, seagrass, salt marshes**.
- **Tropical peatlands**: most carbon **per hectare** of any terrestrial ecosystem.
- **Carbon fertilisation**: more plant growth because higher CO2 raises photosynthesis; ~**70%** of Earth's recent greening.
- **Bhutan**: **carbon-negative** country. Net zero = emissions balanced by removals.
- Forest carbon stock (**ISFR 2023**, Dec 2024): **7,285.5 million tonnes**, up 81.5 Mt over 2021; total sink ~30.43 bn t CO2e, 2.29 bn t above 2005.
#### 3.1 REDD+
- **REDD+** = Reducing Emissions from Deforestation and Forest Degradation; India takes part; forest conservation can earn tradable credits.
- **UN-REDD** (started **2008**; UNDP, FAO, UNEP) contributes to biodiversity protection, forest resilience and poverty reduction (all three).
#### 3.2 IPCC Carbon Pools (5)
1. Above-ground biomass
2. Below-ground biomass
3. Dead wood
4. Litter
5. Soil organic carbon
!TRAP Atmospheric oxygen is NOT a carbon pool.
### 4. CARBON CAPTURE AND REMOVAL
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CCS** | Capture at stationary point source → **transport → storage** (3 steps); store only |
| **CCUS** | Capture, then **use or store**; supports coal gasification, hydrogen |
| **Carbon sequestration** | Capturing and storing CO2 – **geological** (saline aquifers, depleted oil/gas), **ocean**, **terrestrial** (forests, soil) |
| **Direct Air Capture** | CO2 straight from **ambient air**, anywhere (unlike point source); a sequestration method; CO2 useful for plastics, food processing |
| **Enhanced rock weathering** | Crushed silicate rock (**basalt**) on farmland binds CO2 as carbonate |
| **Ocean alkalinity enhancement** | Adding **lime**/alkaline minerals to seawater |
| **BECCS** | Bioenergy + CCS → **net-negative** emissions |
- **Geological storage sites**: abandoned/uneconomic **coal seams**, **depleted oil and gas reservoirs**, **deep saline formations** – all three.
- CO2-removal activities: basalt on farmland, lime in ocean, pumping industrial CO2 underground – **all three**.
- **APPSC-GS 2024**: "captures and stores CO2 from industrial processes" = **carbon sequestration** (carbon capture also accepted).
- **DST**: five **CCU testbeds** in the **cement** sector – first-of-its-kind research cluster.
- **NET Power** (La Porte, Texas) **Allam-Fetvedt cycle**: burns gas in oxygen with **supercritical CO2** as working fluid; captures nearly all CO2.
### 5. CARBON MONITORING
- **National Carbon Project**: under **ISRO Geosphere-Biosphere Programme** (APPSC-GS 2022 – statements I and III).
- Goals: assess **carbon pools, fluxes and net carbon balance** of India's terrestrial biomes; remote-sensing spatial databases. NOT space technology for sequestration.
| CO2-observing satellite | Agency |
|---|---|

| **GOSAT** | Japan |
### 6. CARBON CREDITS AND MARKETS
- **1 carbon credit = 1 tonne CO2e** avoided or removed.
- **Kyoto Protocol (1997)**: set **caps on GHG emissions**; countries set internal quotas for 'operators'; **CDM** issues **CERs** (Certified Emission Reductions).
- Credit prices set by **supply and demand** – NOT fixed by UNEP or any international body.
| Voluntary standard | Run by |
|---|---|
| **Verified Carbon Standard (VCS)** | **Verra** |
| **Gold Standard** | Founded by **WWF** |
#### 6.1 Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS)
- Notified **June 2023** under **Sec. 14AA, Energy Conservation Act 2001** (NOT EP Act); run by **BEE**; trades **Indian Carbon Credits**; India's **first mandatory compliance** market.
- **Oct 2025**: intensity targets for **aluminium, cement, chlor-alkali, pulp & paper**; 282 entities.
- 7 sectors, ~477 Mt CO2e. Carbon pricing covers **29%** of global GHGs.
- **Net Zero Portal** (MoEFCC, Sep 2026): voluntary net-zero disclosure.
- **Budget 2026-27 carbon credit programme**: follows DST roadmap – industrial, not farmer-only.
### 7. CARBON PRICING – TAX AND CBAM
- **New Zealand**: first to **propose** a tax on agricultural (livestock) emissions (APPSC-G1 2024); also cited as first to propose a carbon tax for its people (Sweden, Finland, Netherlands, Norway already had carbon tax).
- **Denmark**: first to **levy** a livestock carbon tax, **from 2030**.
- **EU CBAM**: carbon levy on imports to stop **carbon leakage** (emissions moving abroad). Transitional **2023**; **definitive 1 Jan 2026**. Covers iron and steel, aluminium, cement, fertilisers, hydrogen, electricity. Certificates priced on **EU ETS**; revenue stays in Europe.
- **Article 9**: carbon price already paid (e.g. CCTS) can be deducted.
- **UK CBAM**: from **1 Jan 2027**; Sep 2026 UK recognised **CCTS** as qualifying carbon price → lower liability for Indian exports.
