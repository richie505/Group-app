## U0137 | MUGHAL SOCIETY – NOBILITY, PEASANTRY, POSITION OF WOMEN, URBAN CLASSES
### 1. NOBILITY (UMARA)
- **Umara** = top mansabdars; wealthy but **wholly dependent on the emperor's favour**.
- Cosmopolitan body: **Turanis** (Central Asians), **Iranis** (Persians), **Afghans**, **Hindustanis** (Indian Muslims), **Rajputs**, later **Deccanis**.
| Reign | Shift in nobility |
|---|---|
| Babur–Humayun | **Turanis and Iranis** dominated |
| Akbar | Added **Rajputs and Hindustanis** to balance them |
| Aurangzeb | **Deccanis and Marathas** swelled it after the Deccan wars; Hindu share about **one-third** (M. Athar Ali) |
!TRAP Turanis = Central Asians; Iranis = Persians; Hindustanis = Indian Muslims.
### 2. PEASANTRY
| Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Khud-kasht** | Resident peasant cultivating land in **own village** |
| **Pahi-kasht** | Non-resident cultivator of land in **another village** |
| **Muzarian** | **Tenants** cultivating land held by others |
| **Raiyats** | Peasants – backbone of the economy; bore the land revenue |
### 3. COURT CUSTOMS AND FESTIVALS
| Custom | Origin / note |
|---|---|
| **Jharokha darshan** | Emperor showed himself daily at a balcony; begun by **Akbar**, ended by **Aurangzeb** |
| **Tuladan** | Weighing the emperor against gold and goods on his birthday, given to the poor – **Hindu custom** adopted by Akbar |
| **Sijda / paibos** | Prostration / kissing the feet – introduced by **Akbar** (not Shah Jahan) |
| **Nauroz** | **Persian New Year**; made a court festival by **Akbar**; abolished by Aurangzeb |
- **AP angle**: Qutb Shahi Golconda shared **Nauroz** and **Muharram (Peerla Panduga)**.
### 4. POSITION OF WOMEN
| Royal woman | Role |
|---|---|
| **Gulbadan Begum** | Babur's daughter; wrote the **Humayun-nama** |
| **Nur Jahan** | Married Jahangir **1611**; Padshah Begum; name on coins and farmans; built **Itimad-ud-Daula's tomb (Agra)** |
| **Jahanara** | Shah Jahan's daughter, Padshah Begum; built **Jama Masjid, Agra** and a sarai at **Chandni Chowk** |
| **Zeb-un-Nissa** | Aurangzeb's daughter; poet with pen name **Makhfi** |
!TRAP Jahanara = Jama Masjid, Agra; Shah Jahan = Jama Masjid, Delhi and Agra Fort Moti Masjid.
- **Purdah**: Muslim women strictly veiled, carried in covered palanquins; Hindu women in the Deccan moved freely (**John Fryer**, *A New Account of East India and Persia*).
- **Social evils**: **sati, child marriage, polygamy** (Bernier, Tavernier); Akbar tried to stop **forced sati**.
- **Property**: Muslim women – **inheritance shares + mehr** under sharia; Hindu women – **stridhana**.
### 5. URBAN CLASSES
- Not merchants and soldiers only: **nagarseths, Marwaris, Bohras, Chettis** + **artisans** (guilds, state karkhanas or private).
- Professionals: **hakims**, teachers, lawyers, painters, musicians.
- **Bernier**: no real **middle class** in Delhi – very rich or very poor.
- **Firangi Mahal (Lucknow)** – madrasa of Muslim jurisprudence (UPPSC 2016); **Mulla Nizamuddin** (Sihali) founded the **Dars-e-Nizami**.
### 6. SLAVERY AND DOMESTIC SERVANTS
- Akbar **stopped enslavement of prisoners of war (1562)**, yet noble households stayed full of **slaves and large domestic staffs**.
- **Pelsaert** (Dutch factor) contrasted nobles' luxury with the misery of servants and workmen.
