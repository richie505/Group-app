### 2. DOWRY PROHIBITION ACT, 1961
#### 2.1 Definition and Sections
- **Section 2 – Dowry:** property or valuable security given or agreed to be given, directly or indirectly, **before, at or any time after marriage**, in connection with the marriage.
- **Dower / mahr** under Muslim personal law is **excluded**.
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| s.3 | Giving/taking/abetting: **not less than 5 years** + fine ≥ ₹15,000 or dowry value |
| s.4 | Demanding dowry: **6 months to 2 years** + fine up to ₹10,000 |
| s.8A | **Burden of proof on the accused** |
| s.8B | **Dowry Prohibition Officers** appointed by the **State Government** |
- Offences are **cognisable, non-bailable and non-compoundable**.
#### 2.2 Amendments
- Amended in **1984** and **1986**; 1986 brought **dowry death** (IPC 304B) and its presumption (Evidence Act 113B).
!TRAP Five years is the **minimum** under s.3, not "up to five years".
- Anti-dowry laws fail due to **poor enforcement, faulty FIRs**, deaths shown as accidents or suicides. About **20 dowry deaths a day** (6,000+ a year); **UP, Bihar, MP, Rajasthan** lead.
### 3. DOMESTIC VIOLENCE ACT, 2005 (PWDVA)
- In force **26 October 2006**; a **civil law** (criminal laws may apply to linked acts).
- **Section 3:** physical, sexual, **verbal/emotional** and **economic** abuse.
- **Protection Officers** appointed by the **State Government**; registered **Service Providers** assist; relief by Magistrate.
| Relief | Section |
|---|---|
| Right to reside | 17 |
| Protection order | **18** |
| Residence order | **19** |
| Monetary relief | **20** |
| Temporary custody | **21** |
| Compensation order | **22** |
- **AP:** Protection Officers = **DWCDA Project Directors and RDOs**; **55 NGOs** are Service Providers; **51,581** cases, **₹9 crore** relief to 2,207 victims (AP SES 2025-26).
!TRAP PWDVA is **civil**, not criminal.
### 4. SEXUAL HARASSMENT AT WORKPLACE – VISHAKA AND POSH ACT 2013
#### 4.1 Background
- **Vishaka v. State of Rajasthan (1997):** SC laid down guidelines on sexual harassment at the workplace until a law was made → **POSH Act, 2013**.
- Full name: Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (**Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal**) Act, 2013 (APPSC 2018).
#### 4.2 Committees and Time Limits
| Committee | Where | Composition |
|---|---|---|
| Internal Committee (IC) | Every workplace with **10 or more** employees | **Senior woman employee** as Presiding Officer; 2+ employees; **1 NGO member**; half women |
| Local Committee (LC) | District; set up by the **District Officer** | Workplaces with **fewer than 10**, or complaint against the employer |
| Step | Time limit |
|---|---|
| Complaint | Within **3 months** (extendable by 3) |
| Inquiry | Within **90 days** |
| Employer action | Within **60 days** |
- **SHe-Box 2.0** (MWCD, Mission Shakti): single-window online complaint and tracking of ICs/LCs.
- **AP:** **26 LCs** and **10,437 ICs**; **86 of 88** cases resolved (AP SES 2025-26).

### 7. ACID ATTACKS – LAXMI CASE
- **Laxmi v. Union of India (2013):** acid sold over the counter only against **photo ID** and stated purpose; seller keeps a **register**; **no sale to under-18s**; minimum compensation **₹3 lakh**.
- **RPwD Act 2016** lists acid-attack injury as a **disability**.
### 8. TRAFFICKING – CONSTITUTION, LAWS AND REPORTS
#### 8.1 Constitution and Laws
- **Article 23** prohibits **traffic in human beings, begar** and forced labour; **Article 24** bars children below 14 in factories, mines and hazardous work.
- **ITPA 1956** does **not** punish prostitution itself; punishes **brothel-keeping, soliciting, living on earnings**, detaining persons.
- **CLA 2013** definition: sex trafficking, forced/bonded labour, child trafficking, slavery, **organ removal**; covers all forms **irrespective of consent**.
- **Trafficking of Persons Bill 2018:** passed Lok Sabha (26 July 2018), lapsed; **2021 draft** followed.
#### 8.2 Reports
- **UNODC:** high origin – **Bangladesh, India, Nepal, Pakistan**; high destination – **India and Pakistan**.
