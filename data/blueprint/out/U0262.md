## U0262 | STATES REORGANISATION COMMISSION (FAZL ALI) AND THE STATES REORGANISATION ACT 1956
### 1. BACKGROUND – ANDHRA MOVEMENT MILESTONES
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1918 | **B.N. Sharma** moved the linguistic reorganisation resolution in the Central Legislative Assembly |
| 1930 | First **Nizam Andhra Mahasabha**, **Jogipet** (Suravaram Pratapa Reddy) |
| **16 Nov 1937** | **Sri Bagh Pact**, Madras – irrigation priority, university centres, equal legislative representation for **Rayalaseema** (not merger of Telangana) |
| 1937 | Visalandhra idea starts in the **Circar districts** |
| 1949 | **Visalandhra Mahasabha** formed |
- **Tanguturi Prakasam** – "**Andhra Kesari**" (1928): bared his chest to police guns during the **Simon Commission** boycott in Madras.
- **Orissa (1936)** = first province on linguistic lines.

| **JVP Committee** | 1948–49 | **Nehru, Patel, Pattabhi Sitaramayya**; rejected language as the immediate basis; advised **postponement** |

### 3. ANDHRA STATE 1953
- **Potti Sriramulu** (Gandhian) fasted from **19 Oct 1952** in Madras; died **15 Dec 1952** after **58 days** → unrest → Nehru announced Andhra State on **19 Dec 1952**.
- **Andhra State** formed **1 October 1953** from Madras State = **first linguistic state** of independent India.
- Capital **Kurnool** (Assembly vote 80 to 79 over Vijayawada); first CM **Tanguturi Prakasam**.
- **Oct 1953**: Nehru called the Visalandhra demand "**Andhra imperialism**".
| Item | Andhra State | Andhra Pradesh |
|---|---|---|
| Formed | 1 Oct 1953 | 1 Nov 1956 |
| Capital | Kurnool | Hyderabad |
| First CM | Tanguturi Prakasam | **N. Sanjiva Reddy** |
- **For** linguistic states: government in the people's language, cultural identity, easier administration (APPSC 2024). **Against**: fear for unity after Partition, regionalism, border and minority disputes.
### 4. SRC – APPOINTMENT AND TERMS
- **Trigger**: creation of Andhra (1953) set off similar demands everywhere.
- **Announced 22 December 1953** by Nehru in the Lok Sabha (G2 2017 key); constituted by Home Ministry Resolution **29 December 1953**.
| Member | Position |
|---|---|
| **Syed Fazl Ali** (Chairman) | Governor of **Odisha** |
| **H.N. Kunzru** | Member |
| **K.M. Panikkar** | Member |
!TRAP NOT members: **K.N. Wanchoo**, **Pattabhi Sitaramayya** (JVP), **Tej Bahadur Sapru**. "August 1953" or "1954" for the SRC = wrong.
#### 4.1 Principles
- Rejected "**one language, one state**"; language **important but not the only factor**.
- Other factors: **national unity and security**, **administrative efficiency**, **economic and financial viability**.
- Could split same-language areas into more than one state.
### 5. SRC REPORT 1955
- **1,52,250** memorandums; report **30 Sep 1955**; made public **9 Oct 1955**.
- Recommended **16 states + 3 UTs**; abolish **Part A/B/C** classification.
| Region | SRC recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Vidarbha** | Separate state |
| **Bombay** | Bilingual (Gujarati + Marathi) |
| **Punjabi Suba** | Not accepted |
| **Telugu districts** | Separate **Hyderabad State** |
- **Tenneti Viswanatham** called the report **self-contradictory**.
### 6. SRC ON HYDERABAD / TELANGANA
- **Chapter 5** = Hyderabad; **Chapter 6** = Andhra; **paras 369–374** = case for Visalandhra.
- **Raichur, Gulbarga → Mysore**; **Marathwada → Bombay**.
- Rejected keeping Hyderabad undivided for the 200-year "**Deccani culture**"; **para 368**: 45.4% Urdu speakers, but **no Union Territory** for the twin cities.
- **Para 386**: Telangana to function initially as a separate state, **Hyderabad State**, capital Hyderabad.
- Review: merger **after the 1961 general elections** if **two-thirds** of elected Telangana legislators agree.
!NUM Para 386 | after 1961 | two-thirds majority
| For Visalandhra (paras 369–374) | Against immediate merger |
|---|---|
| Large state (~3.2 crore), rich in minerals | Advanced Coastal Andhra would dominate services |
| Hyderabad solves Andhra's **capital problem** | **Mulki rules** and local jobs at risk |
| Unified **Krishna–Godavari** waters and power | Telangana's **revenue surplus** diverted |
| Telangana's food grains; lower costs | Regional disparities widen |
### 7. REACTIONS AND THE CENTRE'S DECISION
- **Andhra**: CM **Bezawada Gopala Reddy's** resolution (Telangana priority, population-based shares) passed **unanimously** (1955).
- **Communists** consistently backed integrated linguistic states and Visalandhra.
- **Telangana**: Hyderabad Assembly vote – **103 for**, **29 against**, **15 neutral**; CM **Burgula Ramakrishna Rao** worked to allay fears on Congress High Command's direction.
- **22 Oct 1955**: Andhra and Hyderabad CMs agreed to merge (Hyderabad House, Delhi).
- **Nov 1955**: Congress sub-committee – **Nehru, G.B. Pant, Maulana Azad** (Azad kept opposing).
- **20 Feb 1956**: **Gentlemen's Agreement**, Hyderabad House, New Delhi – **Telangana Regional Council**, surplus revenue spent in Telangana, **Mulki rules** retained.
- Centre overrode the wait-till-1961 formula by political agreement.
- **Nehru (1956)**: merger a "**matrimonial alliance**" with a provision for **divorce** – later cited by separate-Telangana advocates.
!TRAP "Communists opposed linguistic states" = incorrect (PYQ 2025).
### 8. STATES REORGANISATION ACT 1956
- In force **1 November 1956**: **14 states + 6 UTs**.
- Telangana + Andhra State = **Andhra Pradesh** – **first state formed under the SR Act** (not Mysore).
- Also bilingual Bombay, enlarged Madras, Mysore, Kerala.
- **APRA 2014** amended **Section 15** of the SR Act to read "Andhra Pradesh and Telangana".
#### 8.1 Zonal Councils
- **Five** – Northern, Central, Eastern, Western, Southern; statutory (Part III, Sections 15–22).
- Chairman: **Union Home Minister** (common to all); Vice-Chairman: a member-state CM by annual rotation.
- Members: CM + two ministers of each state; two members from UTs. Purpose: inter-state cooperation.
### 9. SEVENTH CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT 1956
- Bill introduced in Lok Sabha **10 Aug 1956**.
- Abolished **Part A/B/C/D** → **states and Union Territories**; abolished **Rajpramukh**.
- Inserted **Art 350A** (mother-tongue instruction at primary stage) and **Art 350B** (Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities).
!PAIR SRC 1955 | 16 states | 3 UTs || SR Act 1956 | 14 states | 6 UTs
### 10. LATER BIFURCATIONS AND DEBATES
- **1 May 1960**: bilingual Bombay split into **Gujarat** and **Maharashtra**.
- **Punjab Reorganisation Act, 1966** (after Shah Commission): **Haryana** from **1 Nov 1966**; hill areas to Himachal Pradesh.
- **Belgaum** dispute (Maharashtra–Karnataka) continues.
- Departure from para 386 = core grievance of Telangana movements (**1969, 2001–14**).
- **Jan 2026**: K.T. Rama Rao cited the Fazl Ali recommendation against the 1956 merger; **Feb 2026**: Private Member's Bill for a Permanent Framework for State Reorganisation.
