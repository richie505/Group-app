## U0381 | MARRIAGE – FORMS, RULES OF ENDOGAMY AND EXOGAMY, CHANGING TRENDS AND LEGISLATION
### 1. FORMS OF MARRIAGE
#### 1.1 General Forms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Monogamy** | one husband, one wife |
| **Polygyny** | one man, many wives; **sororal polygyny** = marriage to sisters |
| **Polyandry** | one woman, many husbands; **fraternal polyandry** = marriage to brothers |
| Group marriage | several men with several women |
| **Levirate** | widow marries her **husband's brother** (Gond, Munda) |
| **Sororate** | widower marries his **wife's sister** |
| Child marriage / widow remarriage | below legal age / widow marries again |
!TRAP Levirate = husband's brother; Sororate = wife's sister.
### 2. VEDIC AND CUSTOMARY UNIONS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Anuloma** (hypergamy) | higher-varna man + lower-varna woman; **tolerated** |
| **Pratiloma** (hypogamy) | lower-varna man + higher-varna woman; **condemned** |
| **Sambandham** | consensual, informal union of **Namboothiris and Nairs** under Kerala's matrilineal system |
| **Niyoga** | wife lives with a designated man to have a child when the husband is **impotent or dead** |
- **Later Vedic** changes: child marriage began; **Swayamvara** declined; widow remarriage discouraged; same-gotra marriage banned.
!KEY APPSC 2025: Sambandham and Niyoga asked as meaning-type questions.
### 3. EIGHT FORMS IN MANU SMRITI
- Manu Smriti lists **8** forms: **4 approved (Prashasta)**, **4 disapproved (Aprashasta)**.
| Form | Meaning | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Brahma** | daughter given to a learned, virtuous groom; no dowry or bride price | Approved; **highest** |
| **Daiva** | daughter given to a **priest** officiating at a sacrifice | Approved |
| **Arsha** | after symbolic gifts (**cow and bull**) from the groom; sages | Approved |
| **Prajapatya** | duty, family welfare, children | Approved |
| **Gandharva** | love and consent, no parental approval (Shakuntala–Dushyanta) | Disapproved but **conditionally accepted** |
| **Asura** | **purchase** of bride; groom's family pays bride price | Disapproved |
| **Rakshasa** | forceful **abduction**; linked with Kshatriyas; seen in epics | Disapproved |
| **Paisacha** | deceit, intoxication, force, no consent | **Most condemned** |
!TRAP Gandharva is listed among the disapproved forms although it is conditionally accepted.
- **Satyashodhak marriage** (started by **Savitribai Phule**): no dowry, no Brahmin priest.
### 4. RULES OF MATE SELECTION
#### 4.1 Endogamy and Exogamy
| Rule | Meaning | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Endogamy** | within caste, tribe, religion (varna, caste, sub-caste) | keeps caste boundaries, group identity, controls property passing |
| **Exogamy** | outside own group; bars same **gotra** | – |
| Hypergamy / Hypogamy | into a higher / lower caste | – |
- **Ghurye**: endogamy is the **defining feature of caste**.
- **North India**: caste endogamy + gotra exogamy + **village exogamy**.
- **Irawati Karve** analysed sapinda and gotra.
#### 4.2 Sapinda and Prohibited Degrees
- **HMA Sec. 3(f)** sapinda: **3 generations** on the mother's side, **5** on the father's.
- **Sec. 5(iv)** bars prohibited degrees; **Sec. 5(v)** bars sapinda – both subject to **custom**.
- The **Delhi High Court upheld Sec. 5(v)** (sapinda bar), subject to custom.
!NUM Sapinda: 3 (mother's side) / 5 (father's side).
### 5. TRIBAL WAYS OF ACQUIRING A MATE
- Ways: probation, capture, trial, purchase, service, exchange, mutual consent, intrusion.
| Way | Tribe / example |
|---|---|
| **Capture** | **Naga**, Ho, Bhil, Gond |
| **Service** (groom works at bride's home) | **Gonds** |
| **Exchange** | **Atta Satta** in Rajasthan; criticised by the Rajasthan High Court |
| Purchase (bride price) | parallels Asura form |
#### 5.1 Ho Tribe – Own Terms
| Ho term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Andi** | traditional **arranged** marriage, with **bride price** (gonong); most esteemed |
| **Diku Andi** | later form **borrowed from Hindus** ("diku" = outsider) |
| **Oportipi** | marriage by **capture** |
| **Raji-khushi** | **elopement** by mutual consent (love) |
| **Anader** | **intrusion** – girl forces herself into the lover's home |
- Ho: **marry outside the paternal clan**; maternal cousin marriage common; **elopement and abduction** traditionally common.
### 6. MARRIAGE LAWS
#### 6.1 Acts – Year and Provisions
| Act | Key point |
|---|---|
| **Special (Civil) Marriage Act 1872** | the **Native Marriage Act**; monogamous civil marriage; ages **14 (girl), 18 (boy)**; first special marriage law |
| Indian Divorce Act 1869 + Christian Marriage Act 1872 | adultery, bigamy, conversion; mutual consent added 2001 |
| Age of Consent Act 1891 | early measure against child marriage, not a restraint law |
| **Sharda (Sarda) Act 1929** | **first child marriage restraint law** |
| Anand Marriage Act 1909 | Sikh; registration added **2012** |
| **Parsi Marriage and Divorce Act 1936** | "Parsi" = Parsi Zoroastrian; registration compulsory |
| Dissolution of Muslim Marriage Act 1939 | wife's grounds |
| **Special Marriage Act 1954** | inter-religious civil marriage; ages **21/18**; divorce **Sec. 27** |
| **Hindu Marriage Act 1955** | first codified Hindu law after Independence; Hindus, Buddhists, Jains, Sikhs; **STs only if notified** |
| Foreign Marriage Act 1969 | – |
| **PCMA 2006** | 18 (women)/21 (men); marriage **voidable** at child's option |
| Muslim Women (Protection of Rights on Marriage) Act **2019** | instant triple talaq void and illegal; up to **3 years'** jail + fine |
!TRAP SMA 1872 = 14/18; SMA 1954 = 21/18.
#### 6.2 Goa and Uniform Civil Code
- **Goa**: only State with a Common Civil Code – **Portuguese Civil Code 1867**, brought in **1870**.
- **Uttarakhand UCC** in force since **Jan 2025**; live-in registration mandatory.
- 2026: Amit Shah said UCC will cover all 21 NDA States by 2029; Assam, Gujarat, MP UCC Bills await Presidential assent.

### 8. DIVORCE AND MARRIAGE OFFENCES
#### 8.1 Hindu Law
- **Sec. 13** grounds: adultery, cruelty, desertion, conversion, unsoundness of mind, renunciation, not heard of for **7 years**.
- **Irretrievable breakdown** is **not** an HMA ground; SC grants relief under **Art. 142**.
#### 8.2 Muslim Law
- Wife's routes: **khula** (mutual consent); 1939 Act grounds: no maintenance for **2 years**, imprisonment of **7+ years**, cruelty.
- 2019 Act: subsistence allowance and custody of minor children for the wife.
#### 8.3 Offences (IPC → BNS)
- IPC Chapter XX (493–498) = **BNS 81–84**.
- **Bigamy**: IPC **494** = **BNS 82**.
- **Assam Prohibition of Polygamy Bill 2025**: cognizable, non-bailable; up to **7 years'** RI.
#### 8.4 Family Courts
- **Family Courts Act 1984**: **953** courts (31 Jul 2025).

### 10. CHANGING TRENDS AND CHILD MARRIAGE
#### 10.1 Trends
- **Patricia Uberoi**: companionate marriage, rising age at marriage, serial monogamy, more divorce.
- **Inter-caste** marriages only about **5%**; honour killings where caste hierarchy is challenged; **Dr Ambedkar Scheme**: Rs 2.5 lakh to inter-caste couples.
- **Nepal**: **first South Asian** nation to register a same-sex marriage.
#### 10.2 Child Marriage
- **NFHS-6**: **20.1%** of women aged 20–24 married before 18 (rural 23.3%).
- **PCM (Amendment) Bill 2025**: women's age to **21**; Central Reporting Portal.
- **Bal Vivah Mukt Bharat**: cut child marriage **10% by 2026**; eliminate by **2030**.
- AP: adolescent-education programme in **9,675 schools** covers child marriage.
