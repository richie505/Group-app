### 5. SHORT-LIVED CLIMATE POLLUTANTS AND THE OZONE TREATIES
#### 5.1 SLCPs
- SLCPs cause up to **45% of present warming**; IPCC: rapid cuts can avoid up to **0.6°C by 2050**.
- Also called **super pollutants** / **Short-Lived Climate Forcers**.
- Major warming SLCPs: **black carbon, methane, tropospheric ozone, HFCs**; **sulphate aerosols cool**.
- **Black carbon is not a greenhouse gas**: a particle that absorbs radiation.
- **Tropospheric ozone is not directly emitted**: formed from methane, NOx, VOCs in sunlight.
- **Methane**: about **80 times** CO2's warming over **20 years**; second-largest contributor after CO2.
- Stratospheric ozone **~10-50 km**; tropospheric ozone **surface to 10 km**.
- Ozone causes loss of about **110 million tonnes** of staple crops a year; air pollution about **7 million** premature deaths a year globally.
- India: air pollution caused **1.67 million deaths in 2019** (**17.8%**); loss **US$36.8 billion** (**1.36% of GDP**).
- India's methane (2016, **Third BUR**): **Agriculture 73.96%**, Waste 14.46%, Energy 10.62%, Industry 0.96%.
- India's measures: **PMUY** (over **10.5 crore** LPG connections), **GOBARdhan**, **SRI**, **DSR**, **alternate wetting and drying**.
!TRAP India has NOT joined the Global Methane Pledge; it calls farm methane "survival emissions".
#### 5.2 International Instruments
| Instrument | Fact |
|---|---|

| **Montreal Protocol** | Adopted **16 Sep 1987**, in force **1 Jan 1989**; ozone-depleting substances; Ozone Day **16 September** |

### 6. CLIMATE NEGOTIATIONS AND CLIMATE REPORTS
- **[19 Oct 2026] COP31**: 31st Conference of the Parties, **9-20 Nov 2026**, **Expo Center, Antalya, Turkiye**; **Turkiye** host country, **Australia** to lead negotiations.
- **UNFCCC**: adopted **1992** (Rio Earth Summit), in force **21 Mar 1994**; secretariat **Bonn, Germany**.
- **Paris Agreement**: adopted at **COP21 (2015)**, in force **4 Nov 2016**; aims **well below 2°C** while pursuing **1.5°C**; **NDCs** = national climate commitments under it.
- **[25 Oct 2026] UNEP 17th Emissions Gap Report 2026 "The Point of Return"**:
- Global GHG emissions **58.4 Gt CO2e in 2025**, down only **0.1 Gt** from 2024.
- **Temporary overshoot of 1.5°C** unavoidable without rapid cuts; low-overshoot pathway **1.7-1.8°C**, with about **50% chance** of returning to 1.5°C by 2100.
- Needs cuts of **18% by 2030, 45% by 2035, 69% by 2040** (vs 2025).
- New NDCs (about **two-thirds** of countries) cut only **5-8% by 2030** and **11-15% by 2035**.
- **[29 Oct 2026] UNFCCC NDC report**: even with full NDC implementation, emissions fall only **9% by 2035** from **2019** levels; commitments insufficient for 1.5°C; strong cuts could limit peak warming to about **1.8°C**.
- **[18 Oct 2026] Tuvalu**: rising sea levels may make parts uninhabitable by the end of the century; formerly **Ellice Islands**; Polynesian country between **Australia and Hawaii**; about **26 sq km**, **world's fourth-smallest country**; **nine islands** (**four reef islands, five atolls**) incl. Nanumanga, Niutao, Niulakita; **Kiribati** to north, **Fiji** to south; capital **Funafuti**; **no rivers**; revenue from **copra, postage stamps, fishing licences**; currency **Australian Dollar**.

### 9. DISASTERS, CYCLONES AND NATURAL PHENOMENA
#### 9.1 Nepal Disaster, 26 Aug 2026
1. **Rock-ice collapse** near the **Nepal-China border**; material plunged about **1 km**.
2. Initially read as a **4.4-magnitude quake**; **USGS** said the signal came from the collapse (energy ~**magnitude 5.2**).
3. A **rock-ice collapse-triggered debris flood**, **not a conventional GLOF**; flash floods on the **Bhotekoshi-Trishuli** rivers.
4. Toll (NDRRMA, **8 September 2026**): **1,357 dead**, **5,326** unaccounted for – it rose from a few hundred in the first days; **93,000+** need assistance; Kailash Mansarovar pilgrims missing.
   Source of the flood: an **ice-rock avalanche** from the northern slopes of **Mt Langtang Lirung** into the **Lhende River**, which feeds the **Bhotekoshi**.
5. Nepal lost about **475 MW** hydropower; **13 hydropower projects** damaged.
6. China: **Gyirong Port (Tibet)** buried (upgraded at **$175.8 million**).
7. India: rivers **Gandak, Kosi, Ganga**; **Bihar and UP** alert; **Valmikinagar Barrage** watched (not Farakka); Maharajganj, Kushinagar; India sent **47.5 tonnes** of relief.
#### 9.2 India's Himalayan Hazard Measures
- **National GLOF Risk Mitigation Programme**: **₹150 crore**; **Arunachal Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Sikkim, Uttarakhand**.
- **NDMA Guidelines on GLOFs: 2020**; **Dam Safety Act: 2021**.
- **National Landslide Risk Mitigation Project**: **₹1,000 crore**, **15 States**.

#### 9.3 Cyclones
- **[26 Oct 2026] Cyclone Arnab**: name proposed by **Bangladesh**; formed in the **Bay of Bengal**; 'Arnab' = **ocean/sea** in Bengali; intensified into a **severe cyclonic storm** and was expected to cross the **Andhra Pradesh** coast near **Yanam** on **28 Oct 2026** (AP angle).
- Naming: **WMO/ESCAP Panel on Tropical Cyclones** (**13 members**, **13 names each**; current list adopted **April 2020**); **RSMC New Delhi (IMD)** names North Indian Ocean cyclones; a name is assigned at **34 knots** (~**63 km/h**), **sequentially**, not by landfall country.
- Terms: **hurricane** (North Atlantic, NE/Central North Pacific), **typhoon** (NW Pacific), **'willy-willy'** (historical Australian term).
- **[02 Nov 2026] Cyclone 'Murasu'**: India-proposed; **Tamil** word for a **large war drum** used to announce wars, victories and royal orders (public alertness); previous cyclone **Arnab** (Bangladesh); next names **Akvan (Iran), Kani (Maldives), Elgaman (Oman), Seil (Oman), Sahab (Pakistan)**.
- Other Indian names: **Aag, Vyom, Jhar, Probaho, Neer, Prabhanjan, Ghurni, Ambud, Jaladhi, Vega**.
- **Panel members (13)**: Bangladesh, India, Iran, Maldives, Myanmar, Oman, Pakistan, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Sri Lanka, Thailand, UAE, Yemen.
- **WMO** est. **1950**, HQ **Geneva**; **IMD** est. **1875**, under **Ministry of Earth Sciences** (Dr. Jitendra Singh).
