## U0219 | KISAN MOVEMENTS IN ANDHRA – MUNAGALA, CHALLAPALLI AND OTHER STRUGGLES
### 1. KISAN MOVEMENT IN ANDHRA – OVERVIEW
- Demands: **MSP**, **minimum wages** and **zamindari abolition**.
- Ranga's **1929 memorandum to the Whitley Commission** asked for **minimum wages**.
- Early bodies: **Krishna District Rythu Sangham (1915)**; Guntur and Godavari sanghams (1923).
### 2. PIONEERS AND LEADERS – TITLES
| Leader | Title / role |
|---|---|
| **N.G. Ranga** (Gogineni Ranganayakulu) | **Rythu Bandhu / Rythu Bandhava**, **"Father of the Indian Peasant Movement"**, **"Acharya"**; founder of the Andhra Kisan movement (G2 2019, 2011 keys); **Padma Vibhushan 1991** |
| **Gottipati Brahmaiah** | **"Rythu Pedda"** (leader of farmers); pioneer of the **Zamindari Ryot Movement**; **APCC Secretary**; author of the **Munagala Award**; led the 1931 Mopidevi no-tax resolution |
| **Kommareddi Satyanarayana (Murthy)** | Formed **village peasant associations (1935)**; **led** the **1937 Rythu Yatra** |
| **Parvataneni Veeriah Chowdary** | Led the **1933 Ryot agitation** of Andhra (GS 2024 key) |
| **Pulleti Shyamasundara Rao, Gouthu Latchanna** | Srikakulam kisan leaders; Pulleti **died in jail** after the Mandasa protest |
| **Swami Sahajanand Saraswati** | **First AIKS President**; founded the **Bihar Provincial Kisan Sabha (1929)** |
- Brahmaiah: boycotted the Simon Commission; later **Padma Bhushan (1982)** and Chairman, AP Legislative Council (1964-68).
!TRAP Ranga got the **Padma Vibhushan (1991)**, not the Bharat Ratna. The Munagala Award is Brahmaiah's, not a book of Ranga.

### 4. ANDHRA PROVINCIAL RYOTS ASSOCIATION (1928)
- Also called **Andhra Rashtra Rythu Sangham**, founded **1928**.
- Founders: **N.G. Ranga, Narasimha Devara Satyanarayana, Maganti Bapineedu**.
- First President: **Ranga**; first General Secretary: **B.V. Ratnam**.
- **Merged into AIKS in 1936**.
- **Munagala Zamindari Rythu Sangham (1930)** – Immadi Papayya, Kodati Venkata Narasimha Rao and others.
### 5. ALL INDIA KISAN SABHA (AIKS)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Founded | **April 1936, Lucknow**, at the INC session (first named **All India Kisan Congress**) |
| First President | **Swami Sahajanand Saraswati** |
| General Secretary | **N.G. Ranga** |
| Addressed by | **Jawaharlal Nehru** |
| Nature | **Peasant wing of the Congress**; demanded zamindari abolition and **debt cancellation** |
| Palasa session | **March 1940**, presided by Sahajanand; Ranga, confined to Nidubrolu, defied the order and was jailed |
| WWII | Communist-led AIKS backed the **"People's War"** line |
| 1943 | Ranga left → **Andhra Rashtra Kisan Congress** |
- Sahajanand's papers: **Lok Sangraha**; Hindi weekly **Hunkar (Patna)**.
- Before his death (**1950**) he formed the **All India United Kisan Sabha** – demand: **nationalisation of land and waterways**.
- Oudh Kisan Sabha (1920) = **Nehru and Baba Ramchandra**.
- AIKS still active: protested police action at **Hansi, Haryana (Aug 2026)**.
!TRAP AIKS = **1936** (GS 2024), not 1940.

### 7. KISAN MARCHES – RYTHU RAKSHANA YATRA
- **Ichchapuram → Madras** Rythu Rakshana Yatra.
- **Launched by Ranga on 3 July 1937**; **led on the ground by Kommareddy Satyanarayana**.
- Compared to the **Dandi March**.
- Ended with a memorandum to **Speaker Bulusu Sambamurthy** (Prakasam was then Revenue Minister).
- Ranga's 1,500-mile march is also dated **1938-39** as a cycle rally against the Madras Land Settlement Act.
!TRAP Ranga **launched** the 1937 yatra; Kommareddi **led** it.
### 8. MUNAGALA PARAGANA STRUGGLE (1930-39)
- Location: **Nandigama taluk, Krishna district** then; now **Suryapet/Nalgonda** (back to Nalgonda 1959).
- Zamindar: **Nayani Venkata Ranga Rao** – patron of writers but a harsh landlord.
- **Kalukova Satyagraha (1938)** – **Nanduri Prasada Rao**; over a paddy wage of **80 seers per acre**; the **Sub-Collector** made the zamindar pay.
- **Brahmaiah Award (15 Jan 1939)** – settlement covering **142 villages**.
- **Munagala Day: 11 June 1939**; agitation **withdrawn 19 June 1939** after Prakasam and Ranga promised action.
### 9. CHALLAPALLI ZAMINDARI STRUGGLE (1930-47)
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **June 1930** | Potharlanka, Ghantasala and Movva farmers revolted (**Gorrepati Venkata Subbayya**) |
| 1939 | Zamindar grabbed **600 acres of temple land** |
| **1942** | Led by **Chandra Rajeswara Rao** and **Peta Chapanayya** (both jailed) |
| **1946** | Firing over **Kishkindapalem** land killed **4** |
| **July 1947** | Farmers occupied the zamindar's **surplus land** |
#### 9.1 Munagala vs Challapalli
| Point | Munagala | Challapalli |
|---|---|---|
| District | Krishna then (now Nalgonda) | Krishna |
| Key event | Kalukova Satyagraha 1938 | June 1930 revolt |
| End | Brahmaiah Award 1939 | Land occupied 1947 |
- Latest: Union Cabinet extended **PM-KISAN to 2030-31** (Aug 2026); outlay **Rs 3.15 lakh crore**; **Rs 6,000 a year in three instalments**.
