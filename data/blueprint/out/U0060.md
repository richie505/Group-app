## U0060 | SANGAM KINGDOMS – CHERAS, CHOLAS, PANDYAS; KARIKALA, SENGUTTUVAN, NEDUNJELIYAN
### 1. THE MUVENDAR – THREE KINGDOMS
- **Muvendar** = three crowned kings: **Chera, Chola, Pandya**. Polity = hereditary monarchy + subordinate chieftains.
| Kingdom | Capital | Port | Emblem | River | Territory |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Chera** | **Vanji (Karur / Karuvur)** | **Muziris (Mushiri)**, **Tondi** | **Bow** | **Periyar** | Central/north Kerala + Kongu |
| **Chola** | **Uraiyur → Puhar** | **Kaveripattinam (Puhar)** | **Tiger** | **Kaveri** | Kaveri delta (Tiruchi) up to southern Andhra |
| **Pandya** | **Madurai** | **Korkai** (pearl fishery) | **Fish** | **Vaigai** | Madurai, Tirunelveli (southern TN) |
- **Ma'bar** = Arab name for the Pandya kingdom.
- **Odd one out:** **Pallavas** were NOT a Sangam dynasty (rose from the 4th c. CE, UPPSC 2010); **Kadambas** are not mentioned in Sangam literature.
!TRAP Pandya lifeline river = Vaigai. Chola capital shifted Uraiyur → Puhar.
### 2. PORTS AND ROMAN TRADE
| Port | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Naura, Tondi, Muziris, Nelisanda** | **Western coast**, mainly Chera |
| **Korkai** | Pandya, **Gulf of Mannar** near Tuticorin (not Arabian Sea); linked Pandyas with Rome |
| **Puhar** (Poompuhar, Kaveri Poompattinam) | Main Chola east-coast port, early Chola capital, Kaveri mouth |
| **Uraiyur** | Chola **cotton** centre; in the **Periplus** |
| **Arikamedu** | Indo-Roman trading station near Puducherry; Roman objects |
- **Cheras** = first to trade with Rome; said to have built a **temple of Augustus** near Muziris.
- A **Pandya** king sent an **ambassador to Rome** in **26 BC**.
!TRAP Korkai and Puhar are NOT western-coast ports.
### 3. POLITY TERMS AND CHIEFTAINS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ko, Kon, Mannan, Vendan** | **King** (RPSC 2010) |
| **Aimperunkulu** | Five-member advisory body: ministers, priests, commanders, envoys, spies |
| **Avai** | Royal court (with poets) |
| **Velir** | Minor hill/forest chieftains (Pari, Athiyaman, Ay) |
| **Enadi** | Title of army commanders |
- **Kadai ezhu vallals** (seven great patrons): **Pari, Kari, Ori, Pegan, Ay, Athiyaman, Nalli**.
!TRAP Nalli is a vallal, not an Aimperunkulu minister; Nedunjeliyan is not a vallal.
### 4. RULERS
| Ruler | Kingdom | Achievement / title |
|---|---|---|
| **Karikala** | **Chola** | Won **Venni** and **Vahaipparandalai**; cleared forest land; subject of **Pattinappalai**; **Kallanai** |
| **Udiyanjeral** (Perum Sorru Udhiyan Cheralathan) | **Chera** | **First historical king** of the Sangam period; legend: fed the fighters of the **Mahabharata** war |
| **Imayavaramban Nedunjeral Adan** | Chera | Title = boundary up to the **Himalayas**; carved the Chera bow emblem on the Himalayas; praised in the second ten of **Padirruppattu** |
| **Senguttuvan** ("**Red Chera**", 2nd c. CE) | **Chera** | Founded the **Pattini cult**; brother **Ilango Adigal** wrote Silappadikaram |
| **Talaiyalanganattu Cheruvenra Nedunjeliyan** | **Pandya** | Victor of **Talaiyalanganam** |
| **Arya Padai Kadantha Nedunjeliyan** | Pandya | **Executed Kovalan** |
- **Renati, Pottapi and Nellore Chodas** (Telugu) claimed descent from **Karikala**.
!TRAP "Udiyanjeral – Chola" is the WRONG pair (UPSC 2026): he is a Chera.
### 5. BATTLES
| Battle | Victor | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Venni** | **Karikala** | Defeated Cheras, Pandyas + **11 chieftains** |
| Vahaipparandalai | Karikala | **9 chieftains** submitted |
| **Talaiyalanganam** (Tanjore) | **Nedunjeliyan** (Pandya) | Control of the Tamil country |
### 6. KALLANAI AND PATTINI CULT
- **Kallanai (Grand Anicut)** across the **Kaveri**, built by **Karikala**, with irrigation canals.
- **15 Aug 2026:** remains of a Sangam-period water structure found in the **Kollidam** river.
- **Pattini cult:** founded by **Senguttuvan**; **Kannagi** as the ideal wife; idol stone brought from the **Himalayas**; consecration attended by Sri Lankan king **Gajabahu** (not Augustus).
### 7. DECLINE AND LATER LINKS
- Pandya power fell under the **Kalabhra invasion**.
- **18 May 2026:** **Leiden copper plates** (Chola grants to the Chulamanivarma vihara, Nagapattinam) – seal: Chola **tiger** + Pandya **two fish** + Chera **bow** = Chola victory over both.
- **Ennayiram and Tribhuvani** (South Arcot) = Vedic colleges of the later Chola period.
