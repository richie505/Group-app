## U0560 | NEW CHALLENGES TO INDUSTRY AND COMMERCE AFTER BIFURCATION – LOSS OF INDUSTRIAL BASE, RELOCATION
### 1. WHAT RESIDUAL AP LOST
| Sector | Lost to Telangana | What AP kept / started |
|---|---|---|
| IT/ITeS | **HITEC City** and the **Financial District** (IT export engine) | New IT parks at Visakhapatnam, Tirupati, Vijayawada |
| Pharma | **Genome Valley** and the Hyderabad pharma belt with R&D | Only a **bulk-drug base at Visakhapatnam** |
| MSMEs | Ancillary units tied to Hyderabad industry | Broken supply chains |
- AP got about **58% of population and debt** but only about **46% of revenue** (loss of Hyderabad's revenue base).
### 2. INDUSTRIAL GVA SHARE
- Just after bifurcation: residual AP about **60% agriculture-dependent**; **manufacturing about 10.5% of GSDP**.
- 2025-26 GVA (current prices): services **43%**, agriculture **33%**, industry **24%**; manufacturing **10.84%**.
!TRAP The "10.5% of GSDP" figure is **manufacturing only**, not the whole industry sector (which adds mining, construction, utilities).
- Advantage: coastline **974 km** (older figure), **1,053 km** after remeasurement, supporting a **port-led EXIM strategy**.
### 3. APRA 2014 – ASSETS, INSTITUTIONS AND RELOCATION
| Provision | Subject |
|---|---|
| **Schedule IX** | **91 companies and corporations** (Sheela Bhide Committee) |
| **Schedule X** | **142 institutions**, mostly in Hyderabad |
| Section 53 | Undertakings split by location; HQ assets by population |
| Section 5 | Hyderabad common capital up to 10 years (**till 2024**) |
| Section 5(3) | Inserted by **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Act, 2026**: **Amaravati sole capital** |
- Government moved from Hyderabad to **Velagapudi (Amaravati)**; e.g. APSWAN HQ to Velagapudi Secretariat in **2017**.
- State Cabinet resolution on sole capital: 27 Mar 2026. Ends the 2019-24 three-capitals uncertainty.
!NUM Schedule IX = 91 companies; Schedule X = 142 institutions
### 4. SECTION 94 INCENTIVES
- **94(1)**: fiscal measures including tax incentives; **94(2)**: backward-area infrastructure.
- **7 backward districts**: 4 Rayalaseema + 3 North Coastal.
- New units get **15% additional depreciation** and **15% investment allowance**.
- Criticism: incentives and grants fell short; Special Category Status denied.
### 5. NEW INDUSTRIAL CLUSTERS
| Cluster | Location | Sector |
|---|---|---|
| **Sri City** | Tirupati district | Multi-product SEZ; auto, electronics, food processing |
| Visakhapatnam | North Coastal | Bulk drugs, IT parks |
| Rayalaseema hub | Near Tirupati and beyond | **Kia, JSW, Royal Enfield**, Space City |
- Industrial Development Policy **4.0 (2024-29)**; SIPB cleared **87 proposals, Rs 34,000 crore** (Jun 2026); six BHAVYA parks proposed (Aug 2026).
### 6. COMMERCE AND RELOCATION CHALLENGES
- **Corporate and bank regional HQs stayed in Hyderabad**: AP left without a **commercial nerve centre** or metro base.
- Investment slowdown after 2014: **capital uncertainty, no ready administrative set-up, agitations** (not lack of coastline).
