## U0488 | HUMAN CAPITAL – EDUCATION AND HEALTH EXPENDITURE; NEP 2020; AYUSHMAN BHARAT AND HEALTH FINANCING
### 1. HUMAN CAPITAL FORMATION
- **Human capital** = stock of **skills, knowledge and health** in people (also experience).
- Formed through investment in **education, health, on-the-job training, migration and information**.
- **Skill development** builds human capital; **irrigation has no direct impact** on human capital formation.
- Central to **inclusive growth**.
- **Economic Survey 2025-26** (ch. 11, "Education and Health: What Works and What's Next"): shift from **access → quality**, learning outcomes and **preventive care**; flags **obesity and digital addiction** as new concerns.
- Indicators: **MMR 88** per lakh live births; **stunting 29.3%**, **wasting 19%**.
!KEY PYQ: **Kerala** has a low infant mortality rate because of adequate basic health and educational facilities.
- **Kalp Yojana** (UP Govt, 1998): primary education.
### 2. HEALTH FINANCING – NHP 2017 AND NATIONAL HEALTH ACCOUNTS
#### 2.1 National Health Policy 2017
- Public health spending **2.5% of GDP by 2025**.
- Shift from curative to **preventive and primary care**; goal **universal health coverage** (not limiting services to tertiary hospitals).
- **Seven priority areas**; **Annapurna Scheme is NOT** among them.
#### 2.2 National Health Accounts and Latest Data
- **NHA 2019-20** estimates released **April 2023** by **Dr V.K. Paul, Member (Health), NITI Aayog**.
| Indicator (176th Report, Parl. Standing Committee on Health, Sep 2026) | Figure |
|---|---|
| Govt health expenditure | **1.43% of GDP** (target 2.5%) |
| Household OOPE | **43.4%** of total health expenditure (2022-23) |
| Health share of government spending | **6.12%** (2021-22) → **4.89%** (2022-23) |
| Medicines | ~21% of current health expenditure |
#### 2.3 Missing Middle and Private Care
- **Missing middle**: **40 crore+** Indians outside full subsidy cover who cannot pay for private care.
- Private sector: >60% of inpatient and ~70% of outpatient care.
- Childbirth OOPE: private Rs 37,630 vs public Rs 2,299.
!TRAP Health target **2.5%** of GDP (NHP 2017) vs education target **6%** (NEP 2020); actual health spend **1.43%**.
### 3. AYUSHMAN BHARAT PM-JAY
#### 3.1 Basics
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Launch | **23 September 2018, Ranchi (Jharkhand)** |
| Cover | **Rs 5 lakh per family per year** |
| Base | ~**12 crore families / 55 crore people** (bottom 40%) |
| Identification | **SECC 2011** deprivation criteria; States may widen at own cost |
| Run by | **National Health Authority** |
| Funding | Centre-State **60:40**; **100% Centre** in UTs without legislature |
| Pre/post-hospitalisation | **3 days / 15 days** |
#### 3.2 Expansions and Scale
- **2024**: ASHA, Anganwadi workers and helpers and their families.
- **Senior citizens 70+** (announced **29 October 2024**): **Ayushman Vay Vandana card**, Rs 5 lakh for all aged 70+ regardless of income; those in PM-JAY families get a **top-up of Rs 5 lakh** for themselves.
- 86.51 lakh senior cards by 28 Oct 2025.
- With expansions: **15.14 crore families** (2026).
- 35 States/UTs; **Odisha and Delhi** latest to join (April 2025).
- 28 Oct 2025: **42 crore+** Ayushman cards; 33,000+ hospitals (17,685 public, 15,380 private).
- Women hold ~49% of cards and ~48% of admissions.
#### 3.3 Ayushman Arogya Mandir
- Formerly **Health and Wellness Centres**; comprehensive primary care; target 1.5 lakh, now **1.85 lakh+**.
- Primary care meets **80-90%** of health needs.
- AB School Health and Wellness Programme: MoHFW + MoE.
### 4. PM-ABHIM AND AYUSHMAN BHARAT DIGITAL MISSION
| Item | PM-ABHIM | ABDM |
|---|---|---|
| Launch | **October 2021, Varanasi** | **September 2021**; announced as NDHM on **15 Aug 2020** |
| Aim | Pandemic-ready health infrastructure | Interoperable digital health ecosystem |
| Parts | District critical-care blocks, Integrated Public Health Labs, BSL-3 labs, surveillance; **Rs 64,180 crore** over 6 years | **Four registries**: ABHA, HPR, HFR, Drug Registry |
| Type | **Centrally Sponsored** with central parts | **Central Sector**, run by **National Health Authority** |
- PM-ABHIM: World Bank **US$ 1 billion** loan (July 2022).
- **ABHA**: **14-digit** health ID linking lifelong records; **voluntary, consent-based**.
- ABHA crosses **90 crore**; NMC directed all medical colleges to link records with ABHA (Mar 2026).
!TRAP PM-JAY launched at **Ranchi (2018)**; PM-ABHIM at **Varanasi (2021)**. The four-registry architecture is ABDM, not PM-ABHIM.

### 7. NEP 2020
#### 7.1 Origin and Structure
- Union Cabinet approval **29 July 2020**; drafting committee **K. Kasturirangan**.
| Stage | Ages |
|---|---|
| Foundational | **3-8** |
| Preparatory | **8-11** |
| Middle | **11-14** |
| Secondary | **14-18** |
- New structure **5+3+3+4**; vocational education joined with mainstream **from Grade 6**.
- **Uttarakhand**: first State to start implementing NEP.
#### 7.2 Targets
- **GER 100%** from pre-school to secondary **by 2030**.
- Public education spending **6% of GDP**.
#### 7.3 Linked Schemes
| Scheme | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Samagra Shiksha** | Integrated school education, pre-school to Class 12 (Budget 2018-19) |
| **ULLAS** | Adult literacy (New India Literacy Programme) |
| **DIKSHA** | Digital e-content platform |
| **NIPUN Bharat** | Foundational literacy and numeracy |
| **PM POSHAN** | School meals, Bal Vatika to Class 8 |
| PM-SHRI | Model schools |
| Vidya Pravesh | School readiness |
| PARAKH | Assessment |
- **Bharat Nirman is NOT** NEP-linked.
#### 7.4 Curriculum and Other Education Facts
- **NCF** framed by **NCERT**: Foundational Stage (**2022**); School Education (**2023**).
- ~27% of population aged 3-18 (ES 2025-26).
- **RTE Act 2009** (effective **1 April 2010**): free and compulsory education, ages **6-14**.
- **Saakshar Bharat** (Sept 2009, restructured National Literacy Mission): **female literacy**.
- **Unnat Bharat Abhiyan** (MoE): links higher education institutions with rural communities.
### 8. ANDHRA PRADESH – HEALTH
- **Family Doctor (Family Physician) programme**: launched **6 April 2023**; doctors visit **YSR Village Health Clinics** (10,032), preventive care for rural residents; patients needing advanced care referred to **Aarogyasri** network hospitals (now **Dr NTR Vaidya Seva**).
- **Village health clinics** at village secretariat level.
- **5.04 crore ABHA** accounts; private colleges told to treat patients via ABHA.
- **Sanjeevani** digital health programme from **Aug 2026**: health profile + ABHA for every citizen; integrates PHCs, e-Aushadhi, 108, 104, **telemedicine**, AI.
- Budget 2026-27: 104 services Rs 128 crore.
