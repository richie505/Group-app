## U0138 | MUGHAL ARCHITECTURE – FATEHPUR SIKRI, HUMAYUN'S TOMB, TAJ MAHAL, RED FORT; GARDEN AND TOMB TRADITIONS
### 1. MUGHAL STYLE – AN OVERVIEW
- Zenith of **Indo-Islamic architecture**: a blend of **Persian, Timurid and Indian** elements.
- Hallmarks: red sandstone + white marble, **charbagh**, **double dome**, **pietra dura**, calligraphy, arabesque, **jali** (lattice) work.
- **Akbar** built in **red sandstone**; **Shah Jahan** in **white marble** – his reign is the **"Golden Age"** of Mughal architecture.
!KEY Red sandstone (Akbar) → transition (Jahangir, Itimad-ud-Daula) → white marble (Shah Jahan, Taj).

### 3. TOMB TRADITION – LODI TO MUGHAL
| Stage | Tomb | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| Lodi | **Sikandar Lodi's tomb** – built by **Ibrahim Lodi** | Earlier double dome |
| Sur | **Sher Shah's tomb, Sasaram (Bihar)** – he built his own; set in a tank; finished under Islam Shah | **Transition** Lodi → Mughal |
| Akbar | **Humayun's tomb** | **First garden tomb**; first full Persian **double dome** in Mughal architecture; red sandstone with white marble inlay |
| Jahangir | **Itimad-ud-Daula ("Baby Taj")** | **First** white marble + **pietra dura**; left bank of the Yamuna; for Nur Jahan's father Mirza Ghiyas Beg |
| Shah Jahan | **Taj Mahal** | Climax |
| Aurangzeb | **Bibi ka Maqbara ("Taj of the Deccan", "Second Taj")** | **Last major imitation** in the garden-tomb tradition |
- **Humayun's tomb**: built under **Haji Begum (Bega Begum)**, Humayun's widow; architects **Mirak Mirza Ghiyas** and son **Sayyid Muhammad**; Persian + Indian blend.
- **Humayun-nama** – written by **Gulbadan Begum**, Humayun's sister.
- **Bibi ka Maqbara** – tomb of **Rabia-ud-Daurani (Dilras Banu Begum)**, Aurangzeb's wife, built by son **Azam Shah**; architect **Ata-ullah**, son of Ustad Ahmad Lahori.
!PAIR Baby Taj | Itimad-ud-Daula | Agra || Taj of the Deccan | Bibi ka Maqbara | Aurangabad

### 5. FATEHPUR SIKRI
- Planned capital of **Akbar (1571–85)**, ~37 km from Agra, built in honour of **Sheikh Salim Chishti** (son of Shaikh Bahauddin, title **Shaikh-ul-Hind**); Prince **Salim** named after him. Mainly **red sandstone**; UNESCO site.
| Building | Purpose / feature |
|---|---|
| **Buland Darwaza** | "Gate of Magnificence"; marks **victory over Gujarat** (annexed 1572–73) – **not** Jahangir's birth; southern entrance to the Jami Masjid |
| **Panch Mahal** | **Five storeys**, pillared; Persian **badgir** (wind-catcher); resembles a **Buddhist monastery**; ground floor **84 pillars** |
| **Ibadat Khana** | **House of Worship (1575)** – debates among scholars of different religions |
| **Jodha Bai's palace** | Hindu **trabeate** (beam-lintel) + Islamic **arcuate** (arch-dome) |
| **Diwan-i-Khas** | Private audience; single central carved pillar |
| **Salim Chishti's tomb** | White marble, in the Jami Masjid courtyard |
| **Jama Masjid** | Religious core; called '**Shani-Fatehpur**' |
| **Birbal ki Kothi** | Gujarat and Bengal styles |
- Also at Fatehpur Sikri: **Diwan-i-Aam**. **Akbari Mahal is NOT at Fatehpur Sikri** (it is in Agra Fort).
- Style: Persian, Indian, Central Asian; introduced the **Tudor (four-centred) arch**; continued at **Jahangir Mahal, Orchha (MP)**.
### 6. TAJ MAHAL
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| For | **Mumtaz Mahal** (Arjumand Banu Begum) |
| Place | Agra, right bank of the **Yamuna** |
| Built | **1632–48** (whole complex finished 1653) |
| Chief architect | **Ustad Ahmad Lahori** |
| Marble | **Makrana, Rajasthan** (APPSC GS 2016) |
| Material | Mainly **white marble** (not red sandstone) |
| Layout | Tomb at the **river end** of its charbagh, not the centre |
| Design ancestry | **Humayun's tomb** (garden tomb, double dome) |
| UNESCO | 1983; among the **New Seven Wonders (2007)** |
| Historian | View on style |
|---|---|
| **V.A. Smith** | Combination of **European and Asiatic genius** (APPSC G1 2022) |
| **James Fergusson, Percy Brown** | Product of the **Indo-Persian** tradition |
- **Pollution threat**: "marble cancer" from **sulphur dioxide**; Supreme Court created the **Taj Trapezium Zone (TTZ)** in **M.C. Mehta case (1996)** against industrial pollution.
- Latest (21 Jul 2026): **Allahabad HC** issued notices to the Centre and ASI on a petition against an Agra court's refusal to permit a Taj survey.

### 8. ARCHITECTURAL FEATURES
| Feature | Meaning | First / best seen |
|---|---|---|
| **Double dome** | Inner and outer shells | **Humayun's tomb** (first Mughal) |
| **Pietra dura** | Floral inlay of **semi-precious stones** | Began under **Jahangir**; first at **Itimad-ud-Daula**; peak at the Taj |
| **Charbagh** | **Four-quartered garden** | Introduced by **Babur** (Ram Bagh) |
| Persian–Indian synthesis | Arch-dome + trabeate, chhatris | Fatehpur Sikri, Akbar's tomb |
| **Jali** | Lattice work | Humayun's tomb |
- Contrast: **Bahmani Jama Masjid, Gulbarga** has a **fully covered courtyard**, unlike Mughal open-court mosques (and not Charminar).
