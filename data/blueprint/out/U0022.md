## U0022 | VEDIC LITERATURE – VEDAS, BRAHMANAS, ARANYAKAS, UPANISHADS, VEDANGAS, SUTRAS
### 1. LAYERS OF VEDIC LITERATURE
- Order: **Samhitas → Brahmanas → Aranyakas → Upanishads**; all = **Shruti** ("heard"), **apaurusheya**.
- **Samhitas + Brahmanas = Karma-kanda**; **Aranyakas + Upanishads = Jnana-kanda**.
- **Brahmanas**: prose ritual commentaries (karma-marga). **Aranyakas**: "forest texts", last parts of Brahmanas; bridge karma-marga → jnana-marga (not domestic law books).
- **Veda Vyasa (Krishna Dvaipayana)** classified the Vedas.
### 2. THE FOUR VEDAS
| Veda | Content | Priest | Upaveda |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rigveda** | Hymns; **oldest** | **Hotri** | Ayurveda |
| **Yajurveda** | Sacrificial formulas; rituals of yajnas | **Adhvaryu** | Dhanurveda |
| **Samaveda** | Rig hymns set to melody; **shortest** | **Udgatri** | Gandharvaveda |
| **Atharvaveda** | Spells, charms, healing; **latest** | **Brahman** | Shilpaveda (architecture) |
- **Samaveda**: about **1,549** verses, all but about **75** from the Rigveda; root of Indian music.
- **Atharvaveda**: earliest description of charms; **Ayurveda** ("Science of Life") first appears here; **kshetra-patni** ("mistress of the field"); **Sabha and Samiti** as separate institutions, "daughters of **Prajapati**"; mentions Pashupati.
- **Trayi** = Rig, Yajur, Sama; Atharvaveda excluded.
!TRAP Shilpa Veda = architecture, not astronomy. Hotri-Udgatri-Adhvaryu-Brahman = Rig-Sama-Yajur-Atharva.
### 3. RIGVEDA – STRUCTURE
- **10 mandalas, 1,028 hymns** (1,017 + 11 Valakhilya); similar to the Iranian **Avesta**; "First Testament of Mankind".
- Hymns = prayers to nature deities for cattle, progeny, victory.
| Mandala | Content |
|---|---|
| II-VII | **Family books**, oldest core (Gritsamada, Vishvamitra, Vamadeva, Atri, Bharadvaja, Vasishtha) |
| III | **Gayatri Mantra** (Vishvamitra; deity **Savitri**) |
| VI / VII | Bharadvaja / Vasishtha; VII = **Battle of Ten Kings** |
| IX | Wholly **Soma** (Soma Pavamana, 114 hymns) |
| X | **Purusha Sukta** (four varnas), **Nasadiya Sukta** (creation) |
- Gayatri meaning: "May the Almighty illuminate our intellect to lead us along the righteous path."
- Word **gotra** occurs in the Rigveda meaning **cow shelter / herd of cows**.
### 4. SHAKHAS AND RECENSIONS
| Branch | Nature | Samhitas | Brahmana |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shukla (White) Yajur** | Mantras / verse only | **Vajasaneyi** (Madhyandina, Kanva) | **Shatapatha** |
| **Krishna (Black) Yajur** | Mantras + prose | **Taittiriya, Maitrayani, Kathaka**, Kapishthala | Taittiriya |
- Rigveda: Shakala. Atharvaveda: **Paippalada, Shaunaka**.
### 5. VEDA → BRAHMANA, ARANYAKA, UPANISHAD
| Veda | Brahmanas | Aranyakas | Upanishads |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rig | **Aitareya, Kaushitaki** | Aitareya, Kaushitaki | Aitareya, Kaushitaki |
| Sama | **Panchavimsha (Tandya), Jaiminiya**, Shadvimsha, Chandogya | Jaiminiya | Chandogya, Kena |
| Yajur | **Taittiriya, Shatapatha** | Taittiriya, Brihadaranyaka | Brihadaranyaka, Isha, Katha, Taittiriya, Shvetashvatara |
| Atharva | **Gopatha** (only one) | **None** | Mundaka, Mandukya, Prashna |
- **Shatapatha**: largest Brahmana; eastward spread Kuru-Panchala → Videha; **Great Flood**; **first reference to money-lending** (kusidin).
- **Purusha Medha** = Panchavimsha Brahmana.
### 6. UPANISHADS AND VEDANTA
- **108**, about **13 principal**; c. 800-600 BCE; **anti-ritualistic**; Atman = Brahman; karma, samsara, moksha.
- **Vedanta** = "end of the Vedas"; **Prasthanatrayi** = Upanishads, Brahma Sutra (**Badarayana**), Bhagavad Gita (Bhagavata **not** included).
| Saying / dialogue | Source |
|---|---|
| **Satyameva Jayate** | **Mundaka** |
| **Asato ma sadgamaya, Tamaso ma jyotirgamaya** | **Brihadaranyaka** |
| Yajnavalkya with **Gargi** and Maitreyi; first statement of **samsara** | Brihadaranyaka |
| **Tat tvam asi**; **Satyakama Jabala** | **Chandogya** |
| **Nachiketa–Yama** (12-year-old boy on death) | **Katha** |
| **Trimurti** | Maitrayani |
| Upanishad meaning "white horse" | Taittiriya |
- **Satyameva Jayate** adopted as national motto on **26 January 1950**; written below the National Emblem.
- Upanishads = Shruti; **Puranas = Smriti**.
!TRAP Satyameva Jayate = Mundaka, not Mandukya.
### 7. VEDANGAS AND SUTRAS
| Vedanga | Subject |
|---|---|
| **Shiksha** | Phonetics |
| **Kalpa** | Rituals (Sutras) |
| **Vyakarana** | Grammar |
| **Nirukta** | Etymology |
| **Chandas** | Metrics |
| **Jyotisha** | Astronomy |
| Sutra | Subject |
|---|---|
| **Shrauta** | Public, large sacrifices |
| **Grihya** | Domestic rites |
| **Dharma** | Law and social practice |
| **Sulba** | Measurement and construction of altars |
- Sulba authors: **Baudhayana, Apastamba, Katyayana** (and Manava). **Baudhayana theorem** = sides of a right-angled triangle, early **Pythagoras theorem**.
- One answer key held that Vedic Aryans were **not skilled in astronomy**.

### 9. EPICS, PURANAS AND SMRITIS
- **Mahabharata** (Vyasa): original **Jaya / Jayasamhita** (8,800 shlokas); also **Satasahasri Samhita**; now ~**1 lakh** verses, **18 books**; **Harivamsa** appended; older than Ramayana.
- **Ramayana** (Valmiki): **24,000** verses; five stages from the 5th century BCE.
- **Puranas** five marks: **Sarga, Pratisarga** (creation, renovation), Vamsa, Manvantara, Vamshanucharita.
- **Yajnavalkya Smriti** → commentary **Mitakshara** (**Vijnaneshwara**).
- In classical Sanskrit, **Arya** = a noble man. **Agastya** = sage who Aryanised South India.
### 10. ANDHRAS IN EARLY TEXTS
| Text | Reference |
|---|---|
| **Aitareya Brahmana** (of Rigveda) | **First** use of "Andhra"; **Sunahshepa** story |
| **Rigveda** | Does **NOT** mention Andhras |
| Mahabharata | Andhras sided with **Kauravas** |
| Megasthenes' **Indica** | **30 fortified towns**, large army |
| Ashoka's **Rock Edict XIII** | Andhras among peoples of the empire |
| Jain texts | **Andhakaratta** |
- Sunahshepa: **Vishvamitra** cursed his 50 sons to become Andhras, Pundras, Pulindas and others.
- AP: Eastern Chalukya inscriptions (Cheepurupalli, Nidamarru) praise Vedic scholars; Mar 2026 Vijayawada discourse recalled Vyasa as classifier of Vedas.

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- Oldest Veda: Rigveda; latest: Atharvaveda.
- First "Andhra": Aitareya Brahmana.
- Only Veda without an Aranyaka: Atharvaveda.
