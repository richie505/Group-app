## U0047 | SATAVAHANAS – ORIGIN, SIMUKA, GAUTAMIPUTRA SATAKARNI, NASIK PRASASTI, VASISHTHIPUTRA PULUMAVI
### 1. THE DYNASTY – NAMES, FIRSTS AND SEQUENCE
- **First historical ruling dynasty of the Deccan**; oldest Deccan dynasty (c. 200 BC – 200 AD); a **bridge between north and south India**; queens had a public voice.
- **First (major) dynasty to rule Andhra**; **first kings to issue inscriptions in Andhra itself** (Ashoka's were earlier in time).
- Began as **feudatories of the Mauryas**; became free in the Deccan after Mauryan decline. Andhras won freedom from the Mauryas under the Satavahanas; first king **Srimukha (Simuka)**.
- **Puranic names**: **Andhras / Andhrabhrityas** (= "servants of the Andhras", or Andhras who had been feudatories).
- Seen as **Brahmins**: the Nasik inscription calls Gautamiputra **"Ekabrahmana"**.
- **First to make tax-free land grants** to Buddhists and Brahmanas.
#### 1.1 Position in the Dynastic Sequence
| Stage | Dynasties |
|---|---|
| Magadha | **Mauryas → Sungas → Kanvas → Satavahanas** (Simuka ended the Kanvas; a Satavahana king defeated **Kanva Susarma**) |
| Successors | **Ikshvakus** (Andhra), **Abhiras** (Maharashtra), **Vakatakas** (Vidarbha), Chutus, Pallavas |
| Later in the Deccan | **Chalukyas**, then **Rashtrakutas** |
- **Ikshvaku Virapurushadatta**: his reign = **golden age of Buddhism** at Nagarjunakonda; Upasika **Bodhisri** contributed to Buddhism.

### 3. SOURCES – PURANAS, LITERATURE, FOREIGN ACCOUNTS
#### 3.1 Puranic Accounts
| Purana | Kings | Years | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Matsya** | **30** | **456** (271 BCE–174 CE) | **Longest**, most systematic list; compiled in **Yajnasri**'s reign; basis of political history |
| **Vayu / Vishnu** | **17** | **272** | Not treated as standard |
- Rulers **1-23 = early Satavahanas**; **24-30 = later Satavahanas**.
- **Long chronology**: 271 BC – 174 AD (Simuka c. 230-207 BCE). **Short chronology**: c. 1st c. BCE – 3rd c. CE.
- **Last king per the Puranas: Pulomavi III** (30th).
#### 3.2 Literary Works
| Work | Author / language / note |
|---|---|
| **Kathasaritsagara** | **Somadeva Suri**, Sanskrit, from Brihatkatha |
| **Brihatkatha** | **Gunadhya**, **Paisachi** |
| **Katantra** | **Sharvavarman** (Kundala Satakarni's time) |
| **Leelavati Parinayam** | **Kutuhala** |
| **Gathasaptasati** | **Hala**; Prakrit, **700 verses** on rural life and love |
- Works on **early Satavahana history**: **Kathasaritsagara, Pratishthanapura-kalpa, Dvatrimsatputtalika**; also Jain and **Dipavamsa** traditions.
- **Chulakalinga Jataka** and **Hathigumpha** record Kalinga contact: **Kharavela** sent an army west ignoring his contemporary **Satakarni I**.
!TRAP **Gathasaptasati does NOT narrate** early Satavahana history (G2 2017).

### 4. RULERS – PERIOD – CONTRIBUTION
| No. | Ruler | Period | Contribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | **Simuka (Srimukha)** | c. 230-207 BCE | **Founder** (per Puranas); founder of administration; overthrew the Kanvas; power in north Maharashtra, upper Godavari |
| 2nd | **Kanha (Krishna)** | — | **First Satavahana inscription** (Nasik); **Dharma Mahamatra** |
| 3rd | **Satakarni I** | c. 180 BCE | Greatest early ruler; **Ashvamedha**; **first silver coins**; expanded into Central India; Kharavela's contemporary |
| 6th | **Satakarni II** | — | **Longest reign – 56 years**; **Sanchi torana** |
| 17th | **Hala** | 5 years | **Gathasaptasati**; **Kavivatsala** |
| 23rd | **Gautamiputra Satakarni** | c. 106-130 CE | **Greatest of all**; last of the early Satavahanas |
| 24th | **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi** | c. 130-159 CE | Nasik Prasasti issued; Amaravati inscription; capital to Amaravati; **ship coins** |
| — | **Vasishthiputra (Sivasri) Satakarni** | Later | Married **Rudradaman's daughter** |
| 27th | **Yajna Sri Satakarni** | c. 170-199 CE | **Greatest of the later rulers**; won back territory; **ship coins** (maritime revival); patron of **Nagarjuna** |
| — | **Chanda Satakarni** | Later | **Kodavali** inscription |
| 30th | **Pulomavi III** | — | **Last** king; defeated by Ikshvaku **Srisantamula**; last days at **Myakadoni (Bellary)** |
- **Sanchi south torana** names **Siri Satakarni** (Satakarni I/II), not Gautamiputra.
- **Amaravati stupa**: enlarged under the Satavahanas; **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi** is the standard patron; some keys name Vasishthiputra Satakarni or Srimukha (stone railing around the Mahachaitya).
!TRAP Gautamiputra = **23rd** king; "28th" is an error. Founder **Simuka**; longest **Satakarni II**; last **Pulomavi III**.
### 5. GAUTAMIPUTRA SATAKARNI AND THE KSHAHARATA CONFLICT
| Side | Rulers / records |
|---|---|
| Satavahana | **Gautamiputra Satakarni** ("destroyer of the Sakas") |
| Kshaharata (Western Kshatrapa) | **Nahapana**; son-in-law **Ushavadatta (Rishabhadatta)** – Nasik and Karle records; minister **Ayama** – **Junnar** record |
- Defeated **Nahapana** and regained **Malwa, Saurashtra, Konkan, Nasik** (APPSC 2000).
- **Jogalthambi hoard** (Nasik district): Nahapana's **silver coins restruck** by Gautamiputra; confirms the Prasasti.
- Ushavadatta's Nasik record: **2,000 karshapanas** in a **weavers' guild** at **12%** interest.
- Gautamiputra re-granted land formerly held by Ushavadatta to monks.
#### 5.1 Later Satavahana–Kardamaka Relations
- Kardamaka rivals: **Chashtana** and **Rudradaman**.
- **Junagadh inscription** (Sanskrit, c. 150 CE): Rudradaman **twice defeated Satakarni**, lord of Dakshinapatha, but **spared** him as a near relative.
- **Kanheri** inscription: queen of Vasishthiputra Satakarni = **daughter of Rudradaman**.
- After Rudradaman's attacks, **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi** shifted the centre east to **Dhanyakataka (Amaravati)** and extended rule into **coastal Andhra**.
- **Yajnasri Satakarni** recovered lost territory from the Western Kshatrapas.
!KEY Salivahana era (AD 78) credit to Gautamiputra does not fit his c. 106-130 CE dates.
### 6. NASIK PRASASTI
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Issuer | **Gautami Balasri**, mother of Gautamiputra |
| Reign | **19th regnal year** of her grandson **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi** |
| Language / script | **Prakrit prose, Brahmi** |
| Content | Gautamiputra's expeditions, victories, empire, titles |
| Praise | "Destroyer of **Sakas, Yavanas and Pahlavas**" |
- Main source for Gautamiputra's reign; a family eulogy, so conquest claims may be exaggerated.
- Gifts with Balasri to monks on **Trirashmi hill**: a village and **100 nivartanas** of his own land; Balasri called wife of a **Rajarshi**.
#### 6.1 Social and Religious Claims
- Classes the line as **Brahmana**; also "**crusher of Kshatriya pride**".
- **Protector of the varna system**: **Varnasankarya-nirodhaka** – stopped intercaste marriage among the four varnas; patron of Brahmanism.
#### 6.2 Regions Named
| Region | Modern area |
|---|---|
| Asika, Asaka (Asmaka), Mulaka | Godavari valley, Paithan area |
| Suratha, Kukura | Saurashtra; western Malwa-Rajasthan |
| **Aparanta** | **North Konkan** |
| **Anupa** | **Narmada valley**, capital **Mahishmati** |
| **Vidarbha** | **Berar** |
| **Akara / Avanti** | **East Malwa** (Vidisha) / **West Malwa** (Ujjain) |
#### 6.3 Inscriptions on Gautamiputra
| Site | Relevance |
|---|---|
| **Nasik** | **Military victories** (G2 2008) |
| Naneghat | Satakarni I and Naganika – not Gautamiputra |
| Junnar, Karle | Kshaharata records – distractors |

### 8. QUEENS AND METRONYMICS
| Queen | Role |
|---|---|
| **Devi Naganika** | Wife of **Satakarni I**; daughter of **Maharathi Tranakayiro**; **regent** for her minor son(s); issued **Naneghat**; co-performed **Ashvamedha and Rajasuya** |
| **Gautami Balasri** | Mother of Gautamiputra; **Nasik Prasasti**; built a Nasik cave for **Bhadrayaniya** monks |
| **Lilavati** | Sri Lankan princess, queen of **Hala** (distractor) |
- **Metronymics**: kings put their **mother's (gotra) name** before their own – Gautamiputra, Vasishthiputra, Halamiputra.
- **Begin with Gautamiputra Satakarni** (G2 2008).
- Significance: **high status of royal women**, respect for the mother; but **succession stayed patriarchal**.

### 10. CAPITALS AND AP CENTRES
| Centre | Role / location |
|---|---|
| **Pratishthana (Paithan)** | **Principal western capital**, on the **Godavari**, Maharashtra |
| **Dhanyakataka / Amaravati (Dharanikota)** | Eastern capital after Rudradaman's attacks; Buddhist centre |
| **Srikakulam** (Krishna dt) | Traditional early Andhra capital |
| **Kotilingala** (Karimnagar, Telangana) | Claimed early political centre |
| **Mahishmati** | Capital of subordinate **Anupa** |
- First capital is often thought to be Amaravati, but the real capital was **Paithan**.
- **AP sites**: Amaravati, Bhattiprolu, Ghantasala, Jaggayyapeta, Nagarjunakonda.
- Shift to Dhanyakataka made the **Krishna valley** the dynasty's centre.
### 11. COINS AND ECONOMY
- **Satakarni I**: first silver coins.
- **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi** and **Yajna Sri**: **ship-motif** coins = maritime power.
- **Jogalthambi**: restruck Nahapana coins = proof of victory.
- NextGen CA (Jun 2026) recap: Simuka c. 230-207 BCE; ship coins of Vasishthiputra Pulumavi; Nasik Prashasti and Nahapana restriking.
