## U0809 | HEALTH FINANCING IN INDIA – PUBLIC EXPENDITURE, OUT-OF-POCKET SPENDING AND INSURANCE
### 1. NATIONAL HEALTH ACCOUNTS
- Prepared by **NHATS** under **NHSRC** (MoHFW); framework **SHA 2011** + NHA Guidelines 2016; **first NHA for 2013-14**.
| Indicator (NHA 2022-23, 10th edition) | 2013-14 | 2022-23 |
|---|---|---|
| **GHE, % of GDP** | 1.15% | **1.43%** (1.48% on new GDP base) |
| **Government share of THE** | **28.6%** | **43.7%** |
| **OOPE share of THE** | **64.2%** | **43.4%** |
| Social security share of THE | 6% | 9.9% |
- **COVID peak**: GHE **1.84% of GDP** (2021-22); **OOPE fell** to **39.4%** of THE.
- Centre + States spent about **1.3% of GDP** in 2019-20 (Economic Survey 2021-22).
!TRAP OOPE FELL during COVID, it did not rise; GHE was highest then.
### 2. TARGETS AND FINANCE COMMISSION GRANTS
- **NHP 2017**: GHE **2.5% of GDP by 2025**; Centre's share **40%** of public spending – **target missed** (Centre now ~0.29% of GDP).
- **15th Finance Commission**: **₹70,051 crore** health grants **via local governments** (2021–26) – diagnostic infrastructure for SCs/PHCs, **urban HWCs**, **buildings for building-less SCs/PHCs/CHCs** (not only urban HWCs).
### 3. OUT-OF-POCKET EXPENDITURE
- OOPE: **64.2% → 43.4%** of THE (2013-14 to 2022-23); still about **half of current health expenditure**.
- **Outpatient care, diagnostics, medicines = over 50% of OOPE** – usually not insured.
- Private care costlier: childbirth ₹37,630 vs ₹2,299.
- **"Missing middle"**: over **40 crore** Indians without financial protection; term flagged by **NITI Aayog** (parliamentary panel, Sep 2026).
### 4. PM-JAY
- Cover **₹5 lakh per family per year**.
- **Oct 2024**: extended to **all citizens aged 70+, irrespective of income** (**Ayushman Vay Vandana card**); 70+ members of already covered families get an extra **₹5 lakh top-up**.
### 5. INSURANCE LANDSCAPE
- Government-financed insurance up **2.5-fold** (2017-18 to 2025, NSS 80th round).
- NFHS-6: households with an insured member **41.0% → 60.2%**.
- Criticism: **insurance-led models raise coverage without increasing care**; fix = more spending on **primary and outpatient care**.
### 6. CUTTING MEDICINE COSTS
| Body / scheme | Provision |
|---|---|
| **NPPA (1997)** | **Ceiling prices** of scheduled (NLEM) drugs under **DPCO 2013** |
| **NLEM 2022** | **26 drugs deleted** |
| **Jan Aushadhi Kendras** | **17,990** opened (Dec 2025); target 25,000 by Mar 2027 |
| Free Drugs Service Initiative (NHM) | Free generics; **DVDMS** = procurement and inventory |
| **Clinical Establishments Act 2010** | Adopted in only ~**11 States and 6 UTs** |
- Private pharmacies dominate; outpatient medicine bills about **6 times** Jan Aushadhi.
### 7. ANDHRA PRADESH HEALTH ASSURANCE
- **Dr. NTR Vaidya Seva** (formerly **YSR Aarogyasri**): **cashless** health assurance for economically vulnerable families; in **convergence with PM-JAY** (portability, wider empanelment).
- **Universal Health Policy (Cabinet, Sep 2025)**: up to **₹25 lakh per family per year**; all **3,257 procedures**; claims up to ₹2.5 lakh via insurers, ₹2.5–25 lakh via **NTR Vaidya Seva Trust**; **EHS** employees excluded.
- AP Budget 2024-25: Health ₹18,732 crore (+24%); **₹4,000 crore** to NTR Vaidya Seva Trust.
- State scheme + **EHS + RSBY + ESIS + CGHS** = **70%** of AP households (2026).
