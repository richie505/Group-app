## U0429 | UNITED NATIONS – PRINCIPAL ORGANS, UNGA, UNSC AND REFORM; INDIA'S CANDIDATURE
### 1. UN CHARTER AND MEMBERSHIP
- Signed by **50 nations** at **San Francisco**, **26 June 1945**; **Poland** ratified on 16 Oct 1945 as the **51st** original member.
- In force **24 October 1945** = **UN Day** (81st anniversary in 2026).
- Members: **193**.
- **Six official languages**: Arabic, Chinese, English, French, Russian, Spanish. **Working languages**: English and French.
- **Amendment** (e.g. UNSC reform): **two-thirds** of members, incl. ratification by **all P5**.
#### 1.1 Key Articles
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| Art. 1 | **Purposes** – peace and security, friendly relations, cooperation, harmonising action |
| Art. 2 | **Sovereign equality** |
| Art. 18 | UNGA "important questions" = two-thirds majority |
| Art. 23 | No **immediate re-election** of a retiring non-permanent UNSC member |
| Art. 27 | **Veto** of the P5 |
!TRAP **Hindi is NOT** an official UN language. Purposes = Art. 1; sovereign equality = Art. 2.
### 2. PRINCIPAL ORGANS
| Organ | Composition / mandate |
|---|---|
| General Assembly | All members, one vote each |
| Security Council | 15 members; **only body whose decisions bind members** |
| ECOSOC | **54** members, **3-year** terms; economic, social, SDG coordination |
| Trusteeship Council | **Inactive since 1994**, after **Palau** became self-governing |
| ICJ | Principal judicial organ; **The Hague** |
| Secretariat | Headed by the Secretary-General |
!TRAP **Amnesty International** is an NGO, not part of the UN. GA, ICJ and Trusteeship Council are principal organs.
#### 2.1 ECOSOC Functional Commissions
- **Commission on the Status of Women** (1946) – CSW-70 held 2026.
- **Commission on Sustainable Development** – reviewed **Agenda 21** and the **Rio Declaration**; replaced by the **High-Level Political Forum** in **2013** (APPSC 2018).
- Others: Statistical Commission, Commission on Narcotic Drugs.
### 3. SECURITY COUNCIL
- **P5**: China, France, Russia, UK, USA – veto (Art. 27); only P5 have veto.
- **10 non-permanent** members: elected by UNGA, **two-thirds secret ballot**, for **2 years**; no immediate re-election.
- **Germany is NOT** a permanent member.
- **April 2024**: full UN membership for **Palestine** – **vetoed by the USA**; UK and Switzerland abstained (BPSC 2024).
### 4. GENERAL ASSEMBLY
- One member, one vote; important questions need two-thirds (Art. 18).
- **Credentials Committee**: **9** members appointed each session.
- **Uniting for Peace (Res. 377), 1950**: lets UNGA act when a veto blocks the UNSC; first used in the **1956 Suez Crisis** to send peacekeepers.
- **Emergency special sessions**: e.g. the **10th** – resolution ES-10/23 (10 May 2024) upgraded Palestine's rights as an observer State, without vote.
- **Non-member observer States** (2): **Holy See** (since 1964) and **State of Palestine** (2012, res. 67/19).
- **81st UNGA President** (2026-27): **Khalilur Rahman** (Bangladesh), defeating Cyprus.
- **Vijaya Lakshmi Pandit** – President of the UNGA.
- **22 May 2026** climate resolution: India **abstained** (141-8-28), citing the ICJ's July 2025 opinion.
!TRAP Uniting for Peace was adopted in **1950**, not 1956 (1956 = first use).

### 6. UNSC REFORM
| Group | Members / demand |
|---|---|
| **G4** | **Brazil, Germany, India, Japan** – permanent seats, plus permanent seats for Africa |
| **Uniting for Consensus** ("Coffee Club") | Led by **Italy**; includes **Pakistan**; **opposes new permanent seats** |
| **L.69** | Developing countries of Asia, Africa, Latin America, Pacific; **India a member** |
| **Ezulwini Consensus** (2005) | African Union: **2 permanent seats with veto + 5 non-permanent** |
- **IGN** (Inter-Governmental Negotiations): informal **UNGA** process (not a UNSC body), set up **2008**; annual "**Elements Paper**" maps agreements and disagreements.
- India's PR criticised the 2026 Elements Paper and demands **text-based** negotiations (June 2026).
- **China** opposes India's permanent seat; China is **not in the G4** – already permanent (APPSC-G1 2012).

### 9. INTERNATIONAL COURT OF JUSTICE
- **15 judges**, **9-year** terms; elected by **UNGA and UNSC voting separately**.
- Indian judges: **B.N. Rau**, **Nagendra Singh** (ICJ President **1985-88**), **R.S. Pathak**, **Dalveer Bhandari**.
- **Dalveer Bhandari**: elected 27 April 2012 (remaining term to Feb 2018); re-elected from 6 Feb 2018 for nine years.
- **Kulbhushan Jadhav case (2019)**: Pakistan breached the **Vienna Convention on Consular Relations**; review of conviction ordered.
- **July 2025**: unanimous **advisory opinion on climate** obligations.
!NUM 15 = UNSC members and ICJ judges; 54 = ECOSOC; 9 = ICJ term and Credentials Committee
