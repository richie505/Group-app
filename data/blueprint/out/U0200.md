## U0200 | FOUNDATION OF THE INDIAN NATIONAL CONGRESS 1885 – A.O. HUME, THE SAFETY-VALVE THEORY
### 1. CAUSES OF THE RISE OF NATIONALISM
- **Western education**, the **press**, a **new middle class**, **socio-religious reform** and **economic exploitation** (India = raw-material supplier + captive market).
- NOT a cause: hereditary feudal privileges.
- **Lytton (1876-80)**: lavish **Delhi Durbar 1877** amid the famine; **Vernacular Press Act 1878**; **Arms Act 1878**; **Second Afghan War (1878-80)**.
- **Ilbert Bill (1883, Ripon)**: let Indian judges try Europeans → European **"White Mutiny"** forced dilution → exposed racial discrimination (APPSC 2018).
!TRAP Lytton = Press Act + Arms Act (1878); **Ripon = Ilbert Bill (1883)**.

### 3. A.O. HUME AND THE BRITISH FOUNDERS
- **Retired ICS officer**; clashed with Lytton → demoted 1879, retired 1882, settled at **Shimla**.
- Took the idea of local self-government from **Ripon**; closer to **Bombay-Poona** groups than Calcutta leaders.
- Formed the **Indian (National) Union**; became the INC's **first General Secretary**.
- Title: **"Father of Indian Ornithology"**.
- Other British founders: **William Wedderburn, Justice John Jardine**.
### 4. FOUNDING OF THE INC, 1885
#### 4.1 Origins and Viceroy
- Private meeting of **17 people** after the **Theosophical Convention at Madras (Dec 1884)**.
- **Viceroy: Lord Dufferin (1884-88)** (UPPSC 2025) – not Lytton.
- **Dufferin's attitude**: at first backed Hume's annual-forum idea → soon cooled → mocked the Congress as a **"microscopic minority" (1888)**. No proof he proposed the INC.
- Name **"Indian National Congress"** credited to **Dadabhai Naoroji**.
#### 4.2 First Session
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Dates | **28-31 December 1885** |
| Venue | **Gokuldas Tejpal Sanskrit College, Bombay** |
| Venue shift | From **Poona**, due to **cholera** (not plague) |
| Delegates | **72** – 54 Hindus, 2 Muslims, plus Jains and Parsis; mainly lawyers, journalists, teachers |
| President | **W.C. Bonnerjee** |
| General Secretary | **A.O. Hume** |
#### 4.3 Aims and Demands
- Aims: **national unity**, ending **caste, sectarian and regional biases**, framing public demands.
- **Social issues** were kept off the Congress platform at first.
- Four demands: **simultaneous ICS exams** in England and India; **elected councils**; **against the annexation of Upper Burma**; **cut in military spending**. NOT complete provincial autonomy.
- Early demand: **separation of Executive and Judiciary**.
- **2nd session, Calcutta 1886**: **436 delegates** (some notes give 408, with 21 from Andhra); Provincial Congress Committees set up.
!NUM 72 delegates (1885) vs 436 (1886)
### 5. SAFETY-VALVE THEORY
- **Claim**: Hume and Dufferin created the INC as a safe outlet for educated Indians' anger, to prevent another **1857**.
- Basis: **seven volumes of secret reports** Hume read at Simla in **1878**.
| Person | View |
|---|---|
| **William Wedderburn** | **First mention** – biography of Hume (**1913**) |
| **Lala Lajpat Rai** | **First user** – **Young India (1916)**, against the Moderates |
| **R.P. Dutt** (Marxist) | **"Conspiracy" theory** – INC made to head off a popular uprising |
| **Bipan Chandra** | Critic – a **"colonial myth"**; Hume a **"lightning conductor"**; wrote **India's Struggle for Independence** |
| **G.K. Gokhale** | No Indian could have started it (lightning-conductor view also credited to him) |
- **Lightning conductor**: Indian leaders used a Briton (Hume) to shield the infant body from official hostility.
!TRAP Wedderburn (1913) = first mention; Lajpat Rai (1916) = first user; Bipan Chandra = lightning conductor.
### 6. DADABHAI NAOROJI
- **"Grand Old Man of India"**; INC president **three times**: **Calcutta 1886, Lahore 1893, Calcutta 1906**.
- APPSC-GS 2025: "thrice President, called its grandfather" → **Naoroji**.
- Founded the **East India Association (London, 1866)**.
- **First Indian elected to the British Parliament** – **Finsbury Central, 1892** (Liberal).
- Book **Poverty and Un-British Rule in India (1901)** – **drain theory**.
- **Calcutta 1906**: **Swaraj** declared the goal.

### 11. ANDHRA AND THE INC
- **Andhra delegates, 1885**: **P. Anandacharlu (Chittoor)**, **P. Rangaiah Naidu** (Madras Mahajana Sabha), **Nyapathi Subbarao (Rajahmundry)**, **Gutti Kesava Pillai (Gooty)**, **Venkata Subbarayudu (Machilipatnam)**.
- **P. Anandacharlu**: **first Andhra/Telugu INC president**, **Nagpur 1891** (APPSC G2 2008 key).
- **Madras 1914**: **256 Andhra delegates**, no working-committee seat → case for a separate **Andhra Congress circle**; **Nyapati Subba Rao** = its first president.
- **Kakinada 1923**: **Ponaka Kanakamma** – **first Telugu woman at an INC session** – led the women's wing.
- **Bezawada (Vijayawada) AICC meeting, 31 Mar-1 Apr 1921**: Gandhi hoisted the **Pingali Venkayya** tricolour.
- **Krishna District Association (1892)** followed the INC's suggestion.
- **Kurnool Circular** – **Quit India Movement, 1942** (not Civil Disobedience).
- **Pattabhi Sitaramaiah** wrote the **History of the Indian National Congress**.
- **Govinda Rao Nanal** – first president, Hyderabad State Congress (ban lifted 1946); **Mulla Abdul Qayyum** – first Muslim in Hyderabad to join the INC.
- **Congress(I)**: Indira Gandhi, **1 January 1978**; **1983**: **TDP** broke continuous Congress rule in AP.
