## U0056 | VISHNUKUNDIN SOCIETY, RELIGION, ADMINISTRATION AND COINAGE
### 1. ADMINISTRATION
| Officer / term | Function |
|---|---|
| Rahasyadhikari (Antaranga) | Secret adviser |
| **Gulmika** | Military officer over **border areas** |
| **Akshapataladhikrita** | Keeper of **records** |
| **Rajjuka** | Measured land with **ropes** |
| Phaladara | Measured grain, fixed the king's share |
| **Bhaga** | Tax of **one-sixth** of produce |
| **Vishti** | **Forced labour** (Telugu **vetti**; Tummalagudem plates) |
- **King = highest judge**. **Amrabad** = biggest fort in the Telugu country (walls ~200 km).
- **Official language: Sanskrit**; Telugu entered records ("Vijaya rajya samvatsarambul", Chikkulla plates).
### 2. COINAGE AND TRADE
- Metal: **iron with a thin copper coating** (APPSC GS 2018 key); found by **Rayaprolu Subrahmanyam**; **16 types**.
- Symbols: **lion, Nandi, ship, conch, trishula**.
- **Ship-marked coins** + MV II's title **Trisamudradhipati** = importance of **sea trade**.
- Roman gold coin of **Constantine** at Kudali Sangameswaram near Alampur; **Fa-hien**: cowrie shells (**gavvalu**) used as money.
!TRAP Copper-coated iron coins = Vishnukundins, NOT Satavahanas or Ikshvakus; not gold/silver.
### 3. RELIGION
- Vedic revival: Madhava Varma II performed **11 Ashvamedhas, 16 Rajasuyas, 1 Purushamedha**.
- **Family deity: Sriparvataswami** (Srisaila Mallikarjuna); kings styled "**Sriparvataswami-padanudhyata**".
- **Ganapati** worship began: **Dantamukha Swami** installed at **Velpuru** in MV II's **33rd year**.
### 4. BUDDHISM AND JAINISM
| Vihara / grant | Donor |
|---|---|
| **Paramabhattarika Mahadevi Vihara**, Indrapalanagara | **Queen of Govinda Varma I**; he granted Penkapura and Enmadala |
| **Govinda Vihara**, Chaitanyapuri (Hyderabad) | **Govinda Varma I** (Prakrit inscription) |
| Village Irundhera to the same vihara | **Vikramendra Varma II** (Tummalagudem-2) |
- **Madhava Varma II** = "**destroyer of Buddhism**"; Undavalli, Mogalrajapuram, Indrakeeladri, Bhairavakona became Hindu shrines.
- **Jainism declined** as **Kapalika Shaivism** rose; Jain shrines such as Srisailam converted; **Kumarilabhatta** attacked Jainism.
### 5. AGRAHARAS AND SOCIETY
| Grant | Donee |
|---|---|
| **Tundi plates** (Vikramendra II) | Scholar **Shivavarma** (Tundi village) |
| **Ipur plates** | **Agnisarma, Easwarasarma** |
| **Mutukali grant** | **Indravarma, Agnivarma** |
- Agraharas = centres of Vedic learning. **Feudalisation view:** tax-free **Brahmadeyas** with ruling rights created a **landed intermediary class**.
- Society: **varna rules more rigid**; women's status fell; **child marriage and sati** increased.
- Features: **Vedic-Puranic revival**, temple + agrahara economy, **first Hindu cave temples** in Andhra.
### 6. EDUCATION – GHATIKAS
- **Ghatikas** = higher colleges of **Sanskrit and Vedic** learning; **Sanskrit** was the teaching language.
- **First mention** in Andhra: **Indrabhattaraka Varma's** inscriptions; special Ghatikas at **Amaravati and Vengi**.
- **14 vidyas** = 4 Vedas + 6 Vedangas + Mimamsa + Nyaya + Dharmashastra + Puranas.
### 7. LITERATURE AND SCHOLAR-KINGS
- **Janasraya Chhandovichiti** – Sanskrit work on **prosody**; credited to **Madhava Varma II** ("Janasraya" = refuge of the people), or poet **Gunaswami** in his name.
| King | Title |
|---|---|
| Govinda Varma | **Shadabhigna** |
| Madhava Varma II | **Vidyadvijaguru**, Janasraya |
| Vikramendra Varma I | **Mahakavi** (Indrapalanagara plates); no surviving work |
