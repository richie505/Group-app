## U0092 | YADAVAS OF DEVAGIRI – BHILLAMA, SINGHANA, HEMADRI AND THE HEMADPANTI STYLE
### 1. THE DYNASTY AND CAPITAL
- Also called the **Seuna Yadavas**; first **feudatories of the Western (Kalyani) Chalukyas**.
- Ruled western India and the Deccan, about **1187–1317 AD**.
- Mother tongue **Marathi**; Sanskrit = language of scholarship; the Yadavas **encouraged Marathi**.
- Capital **Devagiri**, founded by **Bhillama V** (c. **1187**) – not by Shankaradeva.
- Later name: **Daulatabad**, given by **Muhammad bin Tughlaq** (1327) when he shifted his capital there.
### 2. RULERS AND ACHIEVEMENTS
| Ruler | Achievement |
|---|---|
| **Bhillama V** | Threw off Kalyani Chalukya rule; founded Devagiri; beaten by Hoysala **Veera Ballala II** |
| **Jaitugi (Jaitrapala)** | Killed Kakatiya **Rudradeva**; held **Ganapatideva** captive, released him (**1199**), married his daughter **Somaladevi** to him |
| **Singhana II** (c. 1210–1246) | **Greatest** Yadava; title **Teluguraya Sthapanacharya** (installer of the Telugu king) |
| Krishna (1246–1261) | Fought the Hoysalas |
| **Mahadeva** (1261–1271) | Defeated by the Hoysalas; attack on Kakatiyas **repulsed by Rudramadevi** |
| **Ramachandra** (1271–1311) | Ruler at **Alauddin Khalji's** invasion; became a feudatory |
| Shankaradeva (1311–1313) | Died in Malik Kafur's invasion |
| **Harapaladeva** (1313–1317) | **Last** ruler; killed in Mubarak Khalji's invasion |
!TRAP Bhillama V = founder; Singhana II = greatest; Harapaladeva = last.
### 3. SINGHANA II AND THE KAKATIYAS (AP ANGLE)
- Defeated the **Hoysalas**.
- Released Ganapatideva as a counterweight to Veera Ballala's push into the **Krishna–Tungabhadra** region.
- His son **Sarangapanideva** later served at **Rudramadevi's** court.
- Court scholars: **Sarngadeva** (music) and astronomer **Changadeva**.
- **Paithan copper plate** of Ramachandra confirms Jaitugi killed Rudradeva and held Ganapatideva.
- **Bidar fort inscription** records Rudramadevi's victory over Mahadeva.
### 4. SCHOLARS AND WORKS
| Person | Role | Work |
|---|---|---|
| **Sarngadeva** | Court musician of Singhana II | **Sangita Ratnakara** – base text of both **Hindustani and Carnatic** music |
| **Changadeva** | Grandson of **Bhaskaracharya** | School at **Patan** to teach Bhaskara's works |
| **Hemadri** | Minister and scholar | **Chaturvarga Chintamani** (Sanskrit encyclopaedia) |
| **Dnyaneshwar** | Bhakti saint | **Dnyaneshwari** – Marathi commentary on the **Gita**, **1290, Nevasa**, Ramachandra's reign |
| **Chakradhar Swami** | Founder | **Mahanubhava** sect |
| **Mhaimbhat** | Mahanubhava author | **Lilacharitra** = **first Marathi prose** |
- Later Bhakti saints Namdev, Tukaram, Ramdas carried Upanishad and Gita ideas into Marathi.
!TRAP Mahanubhava sect = Chakradhar Swami, not Dnyaneshwar.
### 5. HEMADRI AND THE HEMADPANTI STYLE
- Office: **Shrikaranadhipa** (head of secretariat/records) under **Mahadeva and Ramachandra**.
- Chaturvarga Chintamani covers dharma, rituals, charity, pilgrimage, vratas (APPSC G2 2000).
- Claimed Jaitugi captured and sacrificed Kakatiya Rudra.
- **Hemadpanti style**: local **black basalt**; dressed stones **interlocked without mortar** (**dry masonry**).
### 6. FALL TO DELHI
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **1296** | **First Muslim invasion of the South**: **Alauddin Khalji** defeats Ramachandra; Yadavas become vassals; promised **Ellichpur** revenue yearly but did not send it |
| 1307–08 | **Malik Kafur**: Ramachandra submits, taken to Delhi |
| 1313 | Shankaradeva dies in Kafur's invasion |
| 1317–18 | Harapaladeva killed by **Mubarak Khalji**; kingdom ends |
| After 1323 | Malik Burhanuddin of Devagiri given Andhra; Devagiri = Sultanate's Deccan base |
- Devagiri = **first southern kingdom to fall** to Delhi; opened the road to Warangal, Dwarasamudra, Madurai.
