## U0062 | GUPTA EMPIRE – SOURCES, CHANDRAGUPTA I, SAMUDRAGUPTA AND THE ALLAHABAD PRASASTI (HARISHENA)
### 1. SOURCES OF GUPTA HISTORY
#### 1.1 Inscriptions → Ruler and Composer
| Inscription | Ruler | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Allahabad Pillar (Prayaga Prashasti)** | **Samudragupta** | Composed by **Harisena**; 33-line Sanskrit eulogy in Brahmi; conquests, southern expedition; names **Mahendra** as king of **Kosala** |
| **Mehrauli Iron Pillar** | King "**Chandra**" = **Chandragupta II** | In the yard of Delhi's Quwwat-ul-Islam mosque; a **Vishnu Dhvaja** on the hill **Vishnupada**; conquests in the north-west and Bengal |
| **Udayagiri cave** (near Vidisha) | Chandragupta II | Minister **Virasena**; conquests; Varaha image |
| **Mandsaur (Dashpur)** | **Kumaragupta I** | Feudatory **Bandhuvarman**; composed by **Vatsabhatti**; silk-weavers' guild (Tantuwayas) from Lata/Gujarat built a **Sun Temple** |
| **Bhitari Pillar** (Saidpur tehsil, Ghazipur, UP) | **Skandagupta** | War with and defeat of the **Hunas** |
| **Junagadh (Girnar)** | Skandagupta | Governor **Parnadatta** repaired the **Sudarshana lake** |
| **Eran** (Sagar, MP) | Bhanugupta, **510 CE** | **First inscriptional evidence of sati** – wife of commander **Goparaja**; governors called Lokapala |
!TRAP Samudragupta's conquests are in the **Allahabad Pillar**, not Mehrauli, Junagadh or Nasik (APPSC 2025).
#### 1.2 Literary Sources
| Work | Author | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| **Mudrarakshasa** | **Vishakhadatta** | Political drama on Chanakya and Chandragupta Maurya |
| **Devichandraguptam** | **Vishakhadatta** | Ramagupta – Dhruvadevi – Chandragupta II story |
| **Puranas** (e.g. Vayu) | – | Early Gupta territory |
| Allahabad Prashasti | **Harisena** (court poet) | Samudragupta's achievements |
!TRAP Both Mudrarakshasa and Devichandraguptam are by Vishakhadatta.
#### 1.3 Coins
- Gupta coins are **mostly gold** (dinaras); they show rulers, deities and rituals – evidence of titles, religion and prosperity.
### 2. ORIGIN OF THE GUPTAS
- Origin is **uncertain and debated**; they rose from modest beginnings.
- **Caste:** originally **Vaishyas**, later treated by brahmanas as **Kshatriyas**; a **Brahmana** origin is argued from the **Dharana gotra** of Prabhavatigupta (Junagadh does not settle it).
- **Original home:** Sri Gupta ruled a small part of **North Bengal and South Bihar**; early territory = **Magadha, Prayag and eastern UP**.
### 3. CHRONOLOGY OF GUPTA RULERS
| Order | Ruler | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sri Gupta** (c. 240–280) | Founder of the dynasty; "Maharaja" |
| 2 | **Ghatotkacha** (c. 280–319) | "Maharaja" |
| 3 | **Chandragupta I** (c. 319–335) | Real founder of the empire |
| 4 | **Samudragupta** | Greatest Gupta ruler |
| 5 | **Ramagupta** | Killed by Chandragupta II (Devichandraguptam) |
| 6 | **Chandragupta II** | Vikramaditya |
| 7 | **Kumaragupta I** | Mahendraditya |
| 8 | **Skandagupta** | Repulsed the Hunas |
!KEY APPSC G1 key: the start of the Gupta line is given to **Sri Gupta** (founder of the dynasty); Chandragupta I is the **real founder of the empire**.
### 4. CHANDRAGUPTA I (c. 319–335 CE)
- First important king; capital **Pataliputra**; ruled South Bihar, eastern UP (**Saketa, Prayaga**), Bengal.
- **Marriage:** **Kumaradevi**, a **Lichchhavi** princess (Nepal) – gave legitimacy, prestige and power.
- Started the **Gupta Era (Gupta Samvat)** in **319–320 CE** = date of his accession.
- **First Gupta ruler to issue coins** – gold dinaras of the **king-queen type** (Chandragupta I + Kumaradevi), in the joint names of king, queen and Lichchhavis. No coins of Sri Gupta or Ghatotkacha are known.
- **First** to take the title **Maharajadhiraja**.
!TRAP Kumaradevi was the queen of **Chandragupta I**, not Ghatotkacha. Chandragupta I did **not** defeat the Sakas or issue silver coins – that was Chandragupta II (G1 2024 statement I false).
### 5. SAMUDRAGUPTA – CAMPAIGNS
#### 5.1 Four-fold Policy (Prashasti)
| Group | Treatment | Regions |
|---|---|---|
| **North (Aryavarta)** | Rulers slain, kingdoms **annexed** | Ganga–Yamuna region |
| **Atavika rajyas** | Subdued | Forest kingdoms of central India |
| **Frontier kings** | Paid **tribute** | Assam, Nepal, Bengal |
| **South (Dakshinapatha)** | **Defeated and reinstated** as tributaries | 12 kings between Ganga and Kaveri |
| **Distant kings** | Accepted suzerainty, homage | Kushanas, Sakas, Sri Lanka, SE Asia |
- Southern policy: **grahana-moksha-anugraha** (capture, release, favour) → **no permanent annexation** in the south (APPSC G1 2024 key).
#### 5.2 Twelve Southern Kings → Kingdom
| King | Kingdom (present area) |
|---|---|
| **Mahendra** | **Kosala** (Mahanadi region, Odisha) |
| Vyaghraraja | Mahakantara |
| **Mantaraja** | Kurala / Kaurala |
| **Mahendragiri** | **Pishtapura** (Pithapuram, Kakinada) |
| **Swamidatta** | **Kottura** |
| **Damana** | **Erandapalle** (Srikakulam) |
| **Vishnugopa** | **Kanchi** (Pallava) |
| **Nilaraja** | **Avamukta** (Guntur) |
| **Hastivarman** (Salankayana) | **Vengi** (Pedavegi, Eluru) |
| **Ugrasena** | **Palakka** (Nellore area) |
| **Kubera** | **Devarashtra** (S. Kota, Vizianagaram) |
| **Dhananjaya** | **Kusthalapura** (Arcot) |
!TRAP The Pallava king is **Vishnugopa**, not "Vishnugupta". Pishtapura's king is **Mahendragiri**; Kosala's is **Mahendra**.
#### 5.3 AP Angle
- The southern campaign hit **coastal Andhra**: Pishtapura, Vengi, Avamukta, Devarashtra, Erandapalle, Palakka.
- The Allahabad Pillar is the **chief source for 4th-century Andhra** kings.
- A Samudragupta-period record is found at **Sankaram** (Visakhapatnam).
- Empire stretched from **Nepal and Punjab to Kanchi**; influence reached **Simhala**.
### 6. ALLAHABAD PRASASTI AND HARISHENA
- Language: **Sanskrit**, **champu** style (prose + verse), **Brahmi** script (APPSC GS 2025).
- Composer **Harisena** held at once: **Kumaramatya, Sandhivigrahika, Mahadandanayaka**.
- Pillar: an **Ashokan** pillar from **Kaushambi**; **Akbar** shifted it to the Allahabad (Prayagraj) fort.
#### 6.1 Four Inscriptions on the Same Pillar
| Inscription | Fact |
|---|---|
| Ashoka's edicts | Brahmi; message of peace and dhamma |
| **Queen's Edict** | Queen **Karuvaki** |
| Samudragupta's prashasti | By Harisena; glorifies war |
| **Jahangir's** record | **Persian** |
!TRAP Harisena of the prashasti is **not** the later Jain author Harisena of the **Brihatkatha Kosha**.
### 7. SAMUDRAGUPTA – COINS, TITLES, FOREIGN CONTACTS
#### 7.1 Coin Types
| Coin type | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Lyrist (veena-player)** | Him playing the veena – basis of **Kaviraja** |
| **Ashvamedha** | Issued after the horse sacrifice celebrating his conquests |
| **Archer** | King with bow |
!TRAP King-queen type = **Chandragupta I**, not Samudragupta.
#### 7.2 Foreign Contacts
- **Meghavarman**, Buddhist king of **Sri Lanka**, sent a mission to Samudragupta for permission to build a **monastery and rest house at Bodh Gaya** – recorded in a **Chinese source** (BPSC 2023).
- **Fa-Hien** travelled in **Chandragupta II's** empire (UPSC 2025) but never names the king.
### 8. CHANDRAGUPTA II
- Married **Dhruvadevi** after killing **Ramagupta**; also married **Kuberanaga**, a **Naga** princess.
- Gave his daughter **Prabhavatigupta** in marriage to the **Vakataka** prince **Rudrasena II** – an ally for the Saka war.
- **Saka conquest:** defeated the Western Kshatrapas (Gujarat, Saurashtra, Malwa) – title **Sakari**; issued **silver coins** modelled on Saka coins; Udayagiri record of Virasena.
- **Ujjain** became his second capital.

### 12. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. **Allahabad Pillar** = Samudragupta; **Mehrauli** = Chandragupta II.
2. **Vishnugopa** of Kanchi, not Vishnugupta.
3. Southern kings were **reinstated**, not annexed.
4. **Saka victory and silver coins** = Chandragupta II, not Chandragupta I.
5. **Kumaradevi** = queen of Chandragupta I, mother of Samudragupta.
6. **King-queen coin** = Chandragupta I; **lyrist, Ashvamedha, archer** = Samudragupta.
7. **Napoleon of India** was coined by **V.A. Smith**.
8. **Mudrarakshasa and Devichandraguptam** – both Vishakhadatta.
9. **Meghavarman** → Samudragupta (Bodh Gaya); **Fa-Hien** → Chandragupta II.
10. **Mahendra** = Kosala; **Mahendragiri** = Pishtapura; **Hastivarman** = Vengi.
