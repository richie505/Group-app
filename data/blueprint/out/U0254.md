## U0254 | CONSTITUENT ASSEMBLY – COMPOSITION, OBJECTIVES RESOLUTION, COMMITTEES, WORKING AND ADOPTION
### 1. ORIGIN OF THE IDEA
| Year | Development |
|---|---|
| 1895 | **Swaraj Bill** – early idea of a constitution; some sources credit **Tilak** |
| 1934 | **M.N. Roy** – first put forward the idea of a Constituent Assembly (first formal proposal) |
| 1935 | Became an **official INC demand** |
| 1936 | Stated officially at the **Faizpur** Congress session |
| 1938 | **Nehru**: constitution by an Assembly elected on **adult franchise** |
| 1940 | Accepted in principle – **August Offer** |
| 1946 | Set up under the **Cabinet Mission Plan** |
!TRAP APPSC-GS 2019 framed it as "first in 1935"; the key is still **M.N. Roy**. 1934 Roy; 1935 INC; 1940 August Offer.
### 2. COMPOSITION – CABINET MISSION SCHEME
- **Provincial seats** split among **General, Muslim, Sikh** roughly in proportion to population.
- Each community's members elected by its own members in the provincial assembly by **proportional representation – single transferable vote**.
- **Princely states**: members **nominated by rulers**; seats initially unfilled.
- **Not members**: **Gandhi and Jinnah**.
- **15 women** members (e.g. Durgabai Deshmukh, Sarojini Naidu, Hansa Mehta).
#### 2.1 Numbers
- Total strength **389**; fell to **299** after League members from areas that went to Pakistan withdrew.
!NUM 211 = attended first sitting; 389 = original strength; 299 = after partition; 15 = women.

### 6. STATUS AND CRITICISMS
#### 6.1 Indian Independence Act, 1947
- Made it a **fully sovereign body** with two roles:
| Role | Chaired by |
|---|---|
| Constitution-making body | **Rajendra Prasad** |
| Dominion Legislature (first Parliament) | **G.V. Mavalankar** |
- Continued as the **Provisional Parliament till 1952**.
#### 6.2 Criticisms
- **Not elected on adult franchise** – indirect election under the **1935 Act's** restricted franchise (tax, property, education).
- **Congress dominance** ("Congress Assembly Party"); Hindu and lawyer dominance.
- **Socialist Party** National Executive (1946) – demanded **dissolution and re-election by universal adult suffrage**; leaders **Jayaprakash Narayan, Ram Manohar Lohia** (RPSC 2024).
### 7. ADOPTION AND COMMENCEMENT
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Adopted | **26 November 1949** |
| Came into force | **26 January 1950** |
| Time taken | **2 years, 11 months, 18 days** |
| Sessions | **11** |
| Last meeting | 24 January 1950 (members signed the Constitution) |
- **26 January** chosen to honour **Purna Swaraj Day** – Congress (Lahore session, 1929) set 26 January 1930 as the day of complete independence.
- **Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas)** = 26 November; notified in **2015** (earlier National Law Day).
### 8. MEMBERS FROM THE ANDHRA REGION
| Member | Role |
|---|---|
| **B. Pattabhi Sitaramayya** | Chaired the **House Committee**; INC President 1948 (Jaipur); APPSC key treats him as the first Telugu INC President |
| **Durgabai Deshmukh** | Woman member; founded the **Andhra Mahila Sabha** |
| **N.G. Ranga** | Peasant leader; spoke on decentralisation, emergency powers, bicameralism, an Andhra Province |
| **M. Ananthasayanam Ayyangar** | Pressed for an independent judiciary; later Deputy Speaker and **Speaker of Lok Sabha** |
- **Andhra demand**: Nehru accepted it in the Assembly on **27 Nov 1947**; dropped from the **Draft Constitution (21 Feb 1948)**; **Dhar Commission** named **17 June 1948**.
### 9. NATIONAL SYMBOLS
| Symbol | Fact |
|---|---|
| **National Flag** | Adopted **22 July 1947**; Ashoka Chakra replaced the charkha |
| **National Anthem / Song** | Adopted **24 January 1950** |
| **Vande Mataram** | Bankim Chandra Chatterji – composed **1875**, in **Anandamath (1882)**; first sung by **Tagore**, INC **1896** |
| **Jana Gana Mana** | Tagore; Bengali, first of five stanzas of a **Brahmo hymn**; first sung **Calcutta INC, 27 Dec 1911** |

- **3 Aug 2026**: PM tributes on Venkayya's **150th birth anniversary**.
- **Oct 2025**: 150 years of Vande Mataram celebrated nationally.
- **23 Aug 2026**: 2026 amendment to the Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act reportedly extends Anthem protection to the National Song.
