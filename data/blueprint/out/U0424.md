## U0424 | INDIA-CHINA RELATIONS – BOUNDARY, LAC, TRADE AND NEIGHBOURHOOD
### 1. RECOGNITION AND BOUNDARY LINES
- India recognised the **PRC** on **30 December 1949**; ties from **1 April 1950** — **first non-socialist-bloc** country to do so.
| Line | Origin | Position |
|---|---|---|
| **Johnson Line** | **1865**, W.H. Johnson | Aksai Chin in Ladakh — **India's claim** |
| **Macartney-MacDonald Line** | **1899** | Aksai Chin under China — China's preferred |
| **McMahon Line** | **Simla Convention 1914**, Henry McMahon | Eastern Tibet-India boundary; China rejects |
- China occupies ~**38,000 sq km** in Aksai Chin; claims ~**90,000 sq km** in the north-east (most of **Arunachal**, "South Tibet").
- **Shaksgam Valley** (Trans-Karakoram Tract): ceded by Pakistan to China, **1963 Sino-Pakistan Boundary Agreement** (after the war, not a cause); fresh dispute **January 2026**.
- Arunachal: China denied visas to Arunachal members of India's delegation (Beijing women's conference, **1995**); the **USA** reaffirmed Arunachal as part of India.

### 3. 1962 WAR
- **20 October – 21 November 1962**; ended in China's **unilateral ceasefire**.
- Causes: unsettled boundary, **Tibet** and Dalai Lama's asylum (**1959**), India's **Forward Policy**. Sectors: Aksai Chin and **NEFA**.
- **Henderson Brooks-Bhagat Report (1963)**: India's military failure.
- Blow to NAM idealism; India sought Western military aid.

- India's line: relations cannot be normal until the border is peaceful.
### 5. LAC STANDOFFS
| Standoff | Year | Place |
|---|---|---|
| **Sumdorong Chu** | 1986–87 | Tawang |
| **Depsang** | 2013 | Ladakh |
| **Doklam** | 2017 | India-**Bhutan**-China tri-junction |
| **Galwan** | **15 June 2020** | Eastern Ladakh; **20 Indian soldiers killed** |
| **Yangtse** | **9 Dec 2022** | Tawang |
- Disengagement: **Pangong Tso** (2021); **Gogra-Hot Springs** (2022); Galwan; **Depsang and Demchok** — **October 2024** understanding, backed at the **Modi–Xi Kazan** meeting.
- 2025–26: PLA deployment fell but **10 combined arms brigades** remain; **Mudh-Nyoma** airbase (~13,000 ft, BRO) operational.
### 6. TRADE IMBALANCE
- Trade deficit about **$100 billion** (Jul 2026); dependence: machinery, **APIs**, electronics, **solar**.
- **Strategic Economic Dialogue**: 2010; six rounds (2011–2019).
- **App bans (2020)**: **Section 69A, IT Act**.
- **Press Note 3 (2020)**: FDI from land-border countries needs **government approval** (not a ban).
### 7. NEIGHBOURHOOD COMPETITION AND MILITARY TIES
- **BRI**: Xi, **2013** ("21st Century Silk Road"/OBOR). **CPEC**: India objects — passes through **PoJK**.
| Port | Country |
|---|---|
| Hambantota | Sri Lanka |
| Gwadar | Pakistan |
| Chittagong | Bangladesh |
| Kyaukphyu | Myanmar |
- **Hand-in-Hand**: India-China anti-terror army exercise; first **2007, Kunming**.
- China-Pakistan exercises: **Warrior** (army), **Shaheen** (air force), **Sea Guardian** (navy; first January 2020, Arabian Sea).
### 8. WATER, TIBET AND FORUMS
- **Yarlung Tsangpo** (Brahmaputra): Chinese **mega dam**; India raised concern Aug 2025.
- **Expert Level Mechanism (2006)**; flood-season data MoUs for **Brahmaputra and Sutlej**.
- **Kailash Mansarovar Yatra** via **Lipulekh** and **Nathu La** (border trade reopened **2006**).
- **AIIB**: HQ **Beijing**; India a **founding member**. **18th BRICS Summit**: New Delhi, Sep 2026.
