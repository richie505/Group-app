## U0617 | DISASTER MANAGEMENT FRAMEWORK – DM ACT 2005, NDMA, NDRF, EARLY WARNING SYSTEMS
### 1. KEY TERMS – HAZARD, DISASTER, VULNERABILITY, RISK
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Hazard** | Potentially damaging event (e.g. an earthquake in an empty desert = hazard, not disaster) |
| **Disaster** | Hazard meeting a vulnerable population: **Hazard × Vulnerability**; **Sec. 2(d)** DM Act – catastrophe causing loss beyond the **affected community's** coping capacity (not the State Government's) |
| **Risk** | Likelihood of loss; rises with hazard, exposure, vulnerability; **falls with coping capacity** |
| **Mitigation** (DM Act) | Measures to reduce the risk, impact or effects of a disaster or threatening disaster situation |
#### 1.1 Types of Vulnerability
| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| **Physical** | Weak houses, floodplain settlements |
| **Social** | Age, gender, caste, disability discrimination |
| **Economic** | Poverty, daily-wage workers, small farmers |
| **Environmental** | **Mangrove loss** (raises cyclone impact), deforestation → landslides |
| **Systemic / institutional** | Weak governance, poor coordination, weak early warning |
- **Community vulnerability factors**: kutcha housing, farm-labour dependence, thin health infrastructure, weak early warning; **high crop-insurance coverage reduces** vulnerability.
- Hazard types: **geological** (earthquakes, landslides, volcanoes, tsunamis); **hydro-meteorological** (cyclones, floods, droughts, cloudbursts, heat waves); **biological** (epidemics, pest attacks).
- **Natural vs man-made**: natural disasters cause most damage in **developing countries**; **Bhopal gas tragedy (2–3 December 1984, Methyl Isocyanate)** was **man-made**; mangroves reduce cyclone impact. People killed by natural disasters in India, 1980–2010: **1,43,039**.

#### 2.1 High Powered Committee (1999)
- Constituted **August 1999**; first chairman **J.C. Pant**; reported **2001**; theme **"Building a Culture of Prevention"**.
- At first natural disasters only, later **widened to man-made**; classified disasters into **five broad categories**; recommended a **separate Department of DM** in GoI.
- **Uttarakhand**: **first State** with a separate **Ministry of Disaster Management**.
#### 2.2 Shift in Approach
| Basis | Pre-2005 | Post-DM Act 2005 |
|---|---|---|
| Approach | **3 Rs** – rescue, relief, rehabilitation | **3 Ps** – prevention, preparedness, proofing (mitigation) |
| Nature | Relief-centric, reactive | Holistic, proactive, continuous cycle; **Build Back Better** |
### 3. DISASTER MANAGEMENT ACT, 2005
- **Act No. 53 of 2005**; Presidential assent **23 December 2005**; in force progressively from 2006; **whole of India**; **11 Chapters, 79 Sections**.
- Enacted after the **2004 tsunami** exposed gaps; legal and institutional framework at national, state and district levels.
- **MHA** = nodal ministry (except drought, pest attack, epidemics); **primary responsibility lies with State Governments**; Centre supports.

#### 3.2 DM (Amendment) Act, 2025
- **Urban Disaster Management Authorities (UDMA)** in Municipal Corporation cities and State capitals, **except Delhi and Chandigarh**; headed by the **Municipal Commissioner** (not the Mayor).
- **Statutory status** to the **NCMC** and **High Level Committee**; national and state **disaster databases**; definitions of risk, resilience, recovery, evacuation; aligned with Sendai; mandatory SDRF in every state; stronger NDMA powers (minimum relief standards, audits).
- **June 2026 – "Resilient India 2047"**: NDMA asks Union ministries for hazard-specific plans (**s.35**) and Ministry DM Plans (**s.37**).

#### 5.2 NDRF – National Disaster Response Force
- Raised **2006** under **s.44** (2005 = year of the Act); **Raising Day 19 January**; **world's largest dedicated response force**.
- Under **MHA**; superintendence of **NDMA**; **Director General** from senior IPS cadre; HQ **New Delhi**.
- **Motto: "Aapada Seva Sadaiv Sarvatra"** ("Disaster Service Always Everywhere").
- **16 battalions** (from an initial 8; **12** at the time of the APPSC 2018 key); ~1,149 personnel each, >18,000 in all; **18 search-and-rescue teams** per battalion (engineers, technicians, dog squads, medics).
- Parent forces (on deputation): **BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB, Assam Rifles**.
- Skills: **CSSR** (Collapsed Structure Search and Rescue), flood and mountain rescue, **MFR** (Medical First Response), **CBRN** response; natural and man-made disasters; teams pre-positioned by vulnerability profile.
- **AP**: **10th Battalion**, regional HQ for AP, Telangana and Karnataka at **Vijayawada**. NDRF budget estimate 2021-22: **₹1,209 crore**.
#### 5.3 SDRF – State Disaster Response Force
- Raised by States/UTs; first responder in the state for the first 24–48 hours; **trained by NDRF/NDRF Academy**; battalion model.
- Drawn from **State Police, Armed Police, Fire Services, Home Guards, Civil Defence** (NDRF draws on central forces).
- Not yet set up in all states → strain on NDRF in simultaneous disasters.
### 6. DISASTER FUNDS AND NOTIFIED DISASTERS
| Fund | Section | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **State Disaster Response Fund** | **48** | **First fund** for **immediate relief** to victims of notified disasters; run by SEC (Chief Secretary); primary fund for **drought relief** |
| **National Disaster Response Fund** | **46** | Central fund; **tops up SDRF** in severe calamities (not the first fund) |
| **NDMF / SDMF** | 47 | **Mitigation**, long-term risk reduction (not relief); operationalised after the **15th Finance Commission**; ₹4,645 crore approved for 9 states incl. AP |
| SDRF share (Centre : State) | — | **General 75:25**; **NE/Himalayan 90:10** |
- SDRF is **not for individual compensation or long-term rebuilding**. Up to **10%** (not 25%) may go to **state-specific disasters**.
- **Finance Commission** recommends calamities and amounts; **MHA notifies**.
- Notified list: cyclone, drought, earthquake, fire, flood, tsunami, hailstorm, landslide, avalanche, cloudburst, pest attack, frost and cold wave; **no man-made calamity**.
- **Aug 2026**: MHA added **heatwaves and lightning** – list **12 → 14** for **2026–31**, on the **16th Finance Commission's** recommendation.
- Drought support: **PMFBY** – crop insurance; **MGNREGA** – employment.
- AP: ₹277.63 crore (2025-26) + ₹479.01 crore (2026-27) released under SDRF/NDRF.
### 7. POLICY, PLANS AND INTERNATIONAL FRAMEWORKS
#### 7.1 National Policy on DM, 2009 and NDMP
- **NPDM**: approved by Union Cabinet **22 Oct 2009**; prepared by NDMA under the DM Act; vision **"safe and disaster-resilient India"**; covers prevention, mitigation, preparedness, response, community participation, **mainstreaming DRR**.
- **NOT an objective**: "promoting preparedness only after a disaster occurs" or relying only on post-disaster relief.
- **NDMP 2016**: India's first national plan, all phases of the cycle, aligned with Sendai; time frames **short (5-yr), medium (10-yr), long (15-yr)**; revised 2019.
- Mainstreaming DRR is part of the **PM's 10-Point Agenda**.
#### 7.2 International Frameworks
| Framework | Period / year | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| **IDNDR** | 1990s | UN decade; India set up DM Cell |
| **Yokohama Strategy** | 1994 | Mid-decade review |
| **Hyogo Framework** | **2005–2015** | Adopted at Kobe, Jan 2005; building resilience |
| **Sendai Framework** | **2015–2030** | Replaced Hyogo; **4 priorities** – understand risk (first), strengthen risk governance, invest in DRR, enhance preparedness and **"Build Back Better"**; **7 global targets** (mortality, affected people, economic loss … to **multi-hazard early warning** access), 38 indicators; goal: universal early warning by 2030 |
| **CDRI** | 2019 | Launched by India at the **UN Climate Action Summit, New York**; secretariat **New Delhi** |
### 8. DISASTER MANAGEMENT CYCLE
| Phase | Stage | Measures |
|---|---|---|
| **Prevention** | Pre | Proactive, long-term: **land-use zoning**, regulating deforestation, control of industrial siting, GIS hazard zonation |
| **Mitigation** | Pre | **Most cost-effective**; lowers likelihood of disaster: **quake-resistant buildings, building codes**, embankments, afforestation, hazard maps, **retrofitting**, insurance; **vulnerability analysis and risk assessment** |
| **Preparedness** | Pre | "First critical step": **early warning (CAP-SACHET)**, **mock drills**, stockpiling, **Aapda Mitra**, Emergency Operation Centres, contingency plans, evacuation routes |
| **Response** | During | Time-critical: search and rescue, evacuation, first aid, shelter, food, restoring critical services |
| **Recovery / Reconstruction** | Post | Restore normal life; **damage assessment, compensation, psycho-social care**, livelihoods, **Build Back Better** |
- The cycle is **continuous** – lessons from recovery feed prevention; phases overlap.
- During: early warning → evacuation → rescue → shelters → emergency medical/water. After: **relief → restoration → reconstruction → resilient rebuilding**.
- **Structural mitigation** for earthquakes = building codes; cyclones = **coastal tree planting**. **Non-structural mitigation** = **community awareness and education** (public education).
- **Early warning** is a crucial component of mitigation (timely information).
- Terms: **HAZOP** study – checks adequacy of safety measures; **MAT** – Mitigation Assessment Team; **hazard mapping** – mapping areas at risk.
- **First responder**: the **community**. Urban response is shared by civic bodies, fire brigade, police; **district administration** in charge (Collector pivotal).
!TRAP "Planning Mitigation" is NOT a type of mitigation; "Non planning required" is NOT a hazard-specific control activity; **recovery and reconstruction are NOT pre-disaster**.
### 9. SAFETY DRILLS AND AWARENESS
| Hazard | Safest action |
|---|---|
| **Earthquake (indoors)** | **"Drop! Cover! Hold"** under a strong table; away from windows, balconies, stairs (APPSC 2025) |
| **Cyclone** | Move to a cyclone shelter or safe building; never go to sea or **stand under trees** |
| Flood | Move to higher ground; avoid flowing water |
| **Tsunami** | Sudden **sea recession** → move **inland/uphill** at once |
- Tools: mock drills, Aapda Mitra volunteers, **National School Safety Programme**, tsunami escape-route signage.
- **Tsunami Ready villages**: India targets **100**; **12 indicators**; renewed every **4 years**; verified by the National Tsunami Ready Recognition Board (MoES, chaired by Director, INCOIS).
- **CBDRR through PRIs**: **₹507.37 crore**; **MoPR + NDMA**; **20 states, 81 districts**.
- **IMD colour code**: **Green** all well – **Yellow** be aware – **Orange** be prepared – **Red** take action.
- **Subhash Chandra Bose Aapda Prabandhan Puraskar 2020**: Uttarakhand's Disaster Mitigation & Management Centre.

### 11. TECHNOLOGY – GIS AND REMOTE SENSING
- **GIS** stores, analyses, overlays and presents **spatial data**; maps disaster-prone areas and vulnerable people; plans camps, routes, resources; maps flood damage.
- **APPSC 2025**: in floods, GIS **analyses flood patterns and topography** for proactive action (it builds nothing, pays nothing).
- GIS **cannot** give details of individual victims and exact locations, nor fully resolve social issues.
| Phase | GIS use |
|---|---|
| Prevention / mitigation | Hazard zonation, vulnerability and risk mapping |
| Preparedness | Evacuation routes, resource mapping, early warning |
| Response | Real-time damage assessment |
| Recovery | Build Back Better planning |
| Hazard | GIS use / programme |
|---|---|
| Flood | Inundation mapping, floodplain zoning |
| Cyclone | Track monitoring, **storm surge modelling** |
| Landslide | Susceptibility mapping (GSI **NLSM**) |
| Drought | **NADAMS** |
| Tsunami | Inundation modelling (INCOIS) |
| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| Remote sensing | Synoptic, real-time view; inundation and damage mapping (not "hills management") |
| **Google Earth Engine** | Cloud platform for rapid flood mapping |
| **INSAT-3D/3DR** | Track cyclones |
| **NDEM** | GIS repository for the DM cycle, by **ISRO** |
| **DEM** | Digital Elevation Model – digital terrain |
| GSI **Bhukosh** | Landslide maps of 4.3 lakh sq km |
- **Landslide Hazard Zonation** (APPSC 2025): divides hill areas into homogeneous zones by degree of susceptibility, hazard or risk; maps are **probabilistic**.
- Earth observation and communication satellites detect and deliver early warnings; social media and apps increasingly used.
### 12. APSDMA AND AP CYCLONES
- **APSDMA**: apex statutory DM body of AP, chaired by the **Chief Minister**, under the **AP DM Rules 2007** (APPSC 2025); pre-disaster planning + post-disaster restoration.
- Runs the **State Emergency Operations Centre** at **Kunchanapalli**; coordinates districts; alert systems **Vajrapath, Varuna, Red Message**.
- AP moved from relief-centric to **mitigation-centric**; a national leader in DRR. Cyclone measures: IMD warnings, cyclone shelters, evacuation, shelter belts, mangroves.
| Cyclone | Year | Landfall |
|---|---|---|
| **Hudhud** | Oct 2014 | **Visakhapatnam** |
| **Titli** | Oct 2018 | Near **Palasa, Srikakulam** |
| **Michaung** | Dec 2023 | Near **Bapatla** |
- AP faces about **13–14** Bay of Bengal low-pressure systems a year, mostly from the northern Bay → Godavari-Krishna floods.
