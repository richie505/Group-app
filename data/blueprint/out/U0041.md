## U0041 | EARLY HISTORIC ANDHRA – ANDHRAS IN LITERATURE, MAURYAN CONTACT, EARLY BUDDHIST CENTRES
### 1. ANDHRA BEFORE THE SATAVAHANAS
- Andhra was already a **distinct region and people** before the Satavahanas – proved by the **Aitareya Brahmana**, Buddhist texts, **Megasthenes' Indica** and **Ashoka's 13th Rock Edict**.
- The **Mauryan phase** made Buddhism **institutional** in Andhra.
### 2. VEDIC, EPIC AND PURANIC REFERENCES
| Source | Reference |
|---|---|
| **Aitareya Brahmana** | **First / earliest mention** of the Andhras – **Sunahshepa** story: **Vishwamitra** cursed his **50 sons** to become **Andhras, Pundras, Pulindas** |
| **Rigveda** | **No mention** of the Andhras |
| **Mahabharata** | Andhras fought on the **Kaurava** side |
| **Vayu Purana** | Places the Andhra people in the **Deccan** |
!TRAP First mention = **Aitareya Brahmana**; Rigveda = none.
### 3. BUDDHIST AND JAIN TEXTS ON ANDHRA
#### 3.1 Names for Andhra → Text
| Name | Text |
|---|---|
| **Andhapatha / Andhra Path** | **Vinayavatthu** (APPSC G2 2019) |
| **Andhra Padha / Andhrapatha** | **Bhimasena Jataka** – demarcation of the Andhra region |
| **Andhakarattha** | Pali texts – on the banks of the **Godavari**, near where Bavari lived |
| **Andhakas** | **Kathavatthu** – a Buddhist school (Third Council) |
- Pali commentaries treat the Andhakas as a people distinct from the Damilas.
#### 3.2 Other Texts
| Text | Reference |
|---|---|
| **Suttanipata (Parayana-vagga)** | **Bavari** sent **16 disciples** to the Buddha |
| **Mahavamsa** | Sri Lankan Buddhist chronicle useful for Andhra |
| **Indica** (Megasthenes) | **First foreign source** to mention the Andhras |
| **13th Major Rock Edict** | Andhras among peoples following Ashoka's **Dhamma** |
!KEY "Which text mentions Andhra Path?" – **Vinayavatthu**, not Suttanipata or the Jatakas.

### 5. JANAPADAS AND TRADE
| Janapada | Capital | River |
|---|---|---|
| **Assaka (Asmaka)** | **Bodhan (Podana)** (APPSC G2 2012) | **Godavari** |
| **Mulaka** | **Pratishthana** | Neighbour of Assaka |
- Andhra lay on the **Dakshinapatha** trade route.
- Andhra merchant guilds used **Kalingapatnam**, later **Masulipatnam**.
### 6. BAVARI LEGEND
- **Bavari**: Brahmin teacher from **Kosala**; settled on the **Godavari** in **Assaka** territory.
- Sent **16 disciples** to the Buddha (Suttanipata, Parayana-vagga).
- Significance: Buddhist ideas reached the **Godavari region before the Mauryan expansion**.
### 7. MEGASTHENES ON THE ANDHRAS
- Greek ambassador of **Seleucus** at the Mauryan court.
- **First to mention forts in Andhra** (APPSC G2 2002): **30 walled towns** (forts) with walls and towers; numerous villages; Andhras lived in towns.
- Army supplied to the Andhra king: **1,00,000 infantry, 2,000 cavalry, 1,000 elephants** (report preserved by **Pliny**).
!NUM 30 forts | 1,00,000 foot | 2,000 horse | 1,000 elephants
### 8. ASHOKAN EDICTS AND MAURYAN CONTACT
- Andhra came under **Ashoka after the Kalinga War**.
| Site | District | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Erragudi** | Kurnool (now **Nandyal**) | Prakrit, Brahmi; **teachers must inculcate Dhamma (religious thought) in pupils** (APPSC G2 2008); appoints **Rajukas** for justice and welfare |
| **Rajula Mandagiri** | Kurnool | Ashokan **minor** edict (not a pillar edict) |
| **Amaravati** | – | **Fragment of an Ashokan pillar edict** |
- **Mahadeva Bhikshu**: by Andhra tradition sent by Ashoka to **Amaravati** – **first Buddhist teacher in Andhra**; enshrined the Buddha's **bone relics**.
### 9. BUDDHIST PLACE NAMES → MODERN SITES
| Ancient name | Modern identification |
|---|---|
| **Siddharthaka chaitya** | **Guntupalli**, Eluru district (APPSC G2 2008) |
| **Dhanyakataka / Andhranagari** | **Amaravati-Dharanikota** on the **Krishna** |
| **Sriparvata / Vijayapuri** | **Nagarjunakonda** |
| **Andhapura / Andhakapura** | Across the Telavaha (Serivanija Jataka) |
- **P.C. Ray** identified the river at Amaravati-Dharanikota as the **Krishna**.
- **Dhanyakataka**: Buddhist centre of the **Mahasanghikas**; capital of the **later Satavahanas**; Buddhism existed here **before the Mauryan period**.
!TRAP Siddharthaka chaitya = **Guntupalli**, not Gudiwada.
### 10. EXCAVATED EARLY HISTORIC SITES IN AP
| Site | Nature of settlement |
|---|---|
| **Gudiwada Dibba** (Krishna) | Early historic mound; unbroken occupation **Mauryan to Satavahana** |
| **Adurru** (East Godavari) | **Mahasanghika** Buddhist centre |
| **Gottiprolu** (Nellore region) | ASI dig: large settlement in a **massive brick wall**, 2nd–1st c. BCE; **trade centre** near an ancient seaport; Pallava-period **Vishnu** sculpture; imitation amphorae |
| **Vaddamanu** (Guntur, near Amaravati) | **Jain stupa** with label inscriptions of Jain teachers; excavated 1981–85 |
- Gottiprolu was later declared a **Monument of National Importance** (July 2026).
- In news: **Vaishya Tekri** (Ujjain; 23 Aug 2026) – digging to resume, possible Mauryan stupa larger than Sanchi; **Devnimori** (Gujarat, Kshatrapa) Buddha relics sent to Colombo (Jun 2026).
