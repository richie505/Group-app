## U0231 | GANDHIAN SOCIAL AND ECONOMIC THOUGHT – TRUSTEESHIP, SARVODAYA, SWADESHI, VILLAGE REPUBLIC, BREAD LABOUR
### 1. KEY TERMS
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sarvodaya** | Welfare / uplift of all, "progress for all", reaching the **last person**; term used by **M.K. Gandhi** |
| **Trusteeship** | The rich hold wealth **in trust** for society; class harmony, not class struggle |
| **Bread labour** | Everyone must earn bread by **physical labour** |
| **Swadeshi + khadi** | Rural employment, self-reliance; bridge between towns and villages |
| **Gandhian Innovation** | Produce **more from less input for more people** |
- **Gandhian Innovation** was coined by **Prof. Prahlad and R.A. Mashelkar**.
### 2. BOOKS BEHIND THE IDEAS
| Book | Author | Gandhi's link |
|---|---|---|
| **Unto This Last** | **John Ruskin** | Gandhi's Gujarati retelling titled **"Sarvodaya" (1908)**; dignity of labour; a source of bread labour |
| **The Kingdom of God is Within You** | **Leo Tolstoy** | Love and non-violence; **no Gandhi translation** |
| **Civil Disobedience** | **H.D. Thoreau** | Right to break unjust laws – **not** bread labour |
| **Constructive Programme** | Gandhi | Booklet, **1941** |
- Message from Unto This Last (read in South Africa): **"The good of the individual is contained in the good of all."**
- It shaped his ideas on social equality, labour and welfare of all.
!TRAP Sarvodaya paraphrase = **Ruskin**, not Tolstoy or Carlyle (APPSC G1 2022).
### 3. BREAD LABOUR – SOURCE OF THE IDEA
- Sources: **Tolstoy** (who took the term from **T.M. Bondaref**) + **Ruskin's Unto This Last**.
- Gandhi linked it to the **Gita's yajna**.
!KEY Thoreau gave civil disobedience, not bread labour.
### 4. GANDHIAN ECONOMY
- **Non-violent economy**: centralisation leads to exploitation and inequality → **decentralise**.
- Needs **decentralisation and self-reliance**; **production for need (consumption), not profit**.
- Priority to **agriculture and village / cottage industries** over heavy industry.
- Against mechanisation that displaces labour in labour-surplus India.
- Each country's economy should be defined by its **climate, land and the nature of its citizens** → hence Gandhi **did not oppose mechanisation in the USA**.
!TRAP "Gandhi did not favour mechanisation in the USA" is the **incorrect** statement (UPPSC 2013).
### 5. TRUSTEESHIP
- Idea linked to **South Africa, 1903** (law firm at Johannesburg).
- Applied to both **capitalists and zamindars** (e.g. Ahmedabad mill owners).
- **Ahmedabad Mill Strike 1918**: Gandhi's fast; **35% wage rise**.
- **Textile Labour Association (1920)**: arbitration and non-violence.
- A **"Practical Trusteeship Formula"** was drafted by co-workers **Narhari Parikh and Kishorelal Mashruwala** while Gandhi was detained at the **Aga Khan Palace**, and refined by **M.L. Dantwala**; it proposed a minimum living wage and a ceiling on income.
- **30 Jun 2026**: Gandhian trusteeship cited for owners funding fire-safety systems.
### 6. SARVODAYA MOVEMENT – PERSONS
| Person | Contribution |
|---|---|
| Gandhi | Used the term Sarvodaya; popularised **charkha and khadi** |
| **Vinoba Bhave** | Made Sarvodaya the cornerstone; organised **Sarvodaya Samaj**; **Bhoodan** began at **Pochampalli** (now Telangana), **1951** – first land gift **18 April 1951**; landlords asked to gift at least **one-sixth** of land |
| **Jayaprakash Narayan** | **Sarvodaya Plan (1950)** |
| **Shriman Narayan Aggarwal** | **Gandhian Plan**; also **Gandhian Constitution for Free India (1946)**, with Gandhi's foreword |
| **Ambedkar** | Dissent: village = seat of caste domination, "a **sink of localism**, a den of ignorance and communalism"; preferred the **individual** as the unit |
| **Tummala Sitaramamurthy** | Wrote **Sarvodaya Ganam** (inspired by Vinoba); translated Gandhi's autobiography into **Telugu** |
| **Prakasam** | Khadi Development Centre, **Guravareddypalem (Guntur)** got **Rs 10,000** from the AISA |
!PAIR Sarvodaya Plan | 1950 | JP || Bhoodan | 1951 | Vinoba
- **Sarvodaya Day: 30 January**.

### 8. RURAL RECONSTRUCTION AND GRAM SWARAJ
- **Constructive programme** items: khadi, prohibition, removal of untouchability, national schools, Hindu-Muslim unity, village panchayats.
- Gandhi's rural development approach = **Samagra Gramseva** (APPSC 2018).
- Compromise in the Constitution: **Article 40** (DPSP, non-justiciable) – organise **village panchayats** as units of self-government.
| Article | Gandhian Directive Principle |
|---|---|
| **40** | Village panchayats |
| **43** | Cottage industries |
| **47** | Prohibition of intoxicating drinks |
!TRAP 40 – 43 – 47 = panchayats – cottage industries – prohibition.
