## U0026 | RISE OF MAGADHA – HARYANKAS, SHISHUNAGAS, NANDAS AND CAUSES OF SUPREMACY
### 1. MAGADHA – OVERVIEW AND EARLY HISTORY
- Magadha was the **most powerful** and **richest Mahajanapada** (6th–4th c. BCE); it became the base of the **Mauryan Empire**.
- Long rivalry with **Kosala, Anga, Kashi** and the **Vajjis**.
- **Puranas**: first dynasty of Magadha = **Brihadratha**; **Jarasandha** ruled from **Girivraj** (Sabha Parva, Mahabharata).
- First capital: **Girivraj / Rajgriha** (modern **Rajgir**), **not Vaishali**.
### 2. DYNASTIES IN ORDER
| Order | Dynasty | Founder | Capital |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Haryanka** | **Bimbisara** | Rajgriha, then Pataliputra (Udayin) |
| 2 | **Shishunaga** | **Shishunaga** (chosen by the people) | **Vaishali**; Pataliputra again (Kalashoka) |
| 3 | **Nanda** | **Mahapadma Nanda** | Pataliputra; fell c. **321 BCE** to Chandragupta |
### 3. CAUSES OF MAGADHAN SUPREMACY
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Iron ore** nearby | Weapons and tools; more output and taxes |
| **Alluvial soil**, ample rainfall | Agriculture surplus |
| Rivers **Ganga, Son** (navigable) | Transport and trade |
| **Elephants** from the east | Military power |
| Safe capitals | Rajgriha (hills), Pataliputra (water-fort) |
| **Unorthodox society** | Little Vedic hold; ambitious rulers, able ministers |
### 4. HARYANKA RULERS
#### 4.1 Bimbisara (544–492 BCE)
- **Founder of the Haryanka dynasty**; **real (actual) founder** of the Magadhan Empire; first great ruler in Buddhist texts.
- Policy: **marriage alliances**, friendship and conquest – **conquered Anga** (capital **Champa**, modern Munger–Bhagalpur; Magadha lay to its west).
- **Contemporary of the Buddha** and **Mahavira**.
- Royal physician **Jivaka (Jeevaka)**, also a contemporary of the Buddha; sent by Bimbisara to treat **Pradyota (Chanda-pradyota) of Avanti** for **jaundice**.
#### 4.2 Bimbisara's Queens
| Queen | Kingdom / clan | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Kosaladevi (Mahakoshala)** | Kosala | Brought **Kashi** as dowry (revenue) |
| **Chellana** | **Lichchhavi (Vaishali)** | **Mother of Ajatashatru** |
| Khema | Madra | – |
- Gains: Kashi revenue and security on the western and northern flanks.
!TRAP The Kosala marriage was Bimbisara's, not Ajatashatru's. Chellana (not Kosaladevi) was Ajatashatru's mother.
#### 4.3 Ajatashatru (492–460 BCE)
- Other name **Kunika**; son of Bimbisara; **killed his father**.
- Took **Kashi**; war with **Prasenjit of Kosala**, ended with Ajatashatru marrying Prasenjit's daughter **Vajira**.
- Fortified **Pataligrama** on the Ganga during the Vajji war; it grew into **Pataliputra**.
- **Defeated and annexed the Vajji** confederacy (capital Vaishali, ruled by Lichchhavis) after **16 years**; minister **Vassakara** split the Lichchhavi chiefs.
- War engines: **mahashilakantaka** (stone-throwing engine) and **rathamusala** (chariot with mace/blades).
- Patron of the **First Buddhist Council** at Rajgriha.
#### 4.4 Udayin (460–444 BCE)
- Son of Ajatashatru; **first chose Pataliputra** as capital (shift from Rajgriha).
- Probably killed at the urging of **Palaka of Avanti**; the people then chose Shishunaga.
!TRAP Udayin of Magadha is not **Udayan of Vatsa**.
#### 4.5 Patricide Chain (Buddhist texts)
- **Bimbisara → Ajatashatru → Udayin**: each killed by his son.
### 5. SHISHUNAGAS
- **Shishunaga**: chosen by the people; **destroyed Avanti**; capital **Vaishali**.
- **Kalashoka**: capital back to **Pataliputra**; **Second Buddhist Council**.
- **Avanti**: capital **Ujjayini**; **Chanda-pradyota**, son of Pulika, contemporary of the Buddha and Bimbisara (Mahavagga, Puranas).
### 6. NANDAS
- **First non-Kshatriya dynasty**; among **India's first empire-builders**.
- **Mahapadma Nanda**: titles **Ekarat** (sole sovereign) and **Sarvakshatrantaka** (destroyer of Kshatriyas).
- **Dhana Nanda**: Greek names **Agrammes / Xandrames**; ruled at **Alexander's invasion**; overthrown by Chandragupta.
- Greek accounts: huge army and wealth. Extent: **Kalinga and the Deccan**.
- **Hathigumpha inscription** of **Kharavela**: a **Nandaraja** built an **irrigation canal in Kalinga** (Mahapadmananda).
!PAIR Ekarat / Sarvakshatrantaka | Mahapadma | Titles || Agrammes / Xandrames | Dhana Nanda | Greek names
### 7. CAPITALS AND PLACES
| Capital / place | Who, why |
|---|---|
| **Girivraja / Rajgriha** | Bimbisara; **five hills**, near-impregnable |
| Pataliputra (fort) | **Ajatashatru** at Pataligrama |
| Pataliputra (capital) | **Udayin** |
| **Vaishali** | Shishunaga |
| Pataliputra again | Kalashoka |
- **Pataliputra** (modern **Patna**): confluence of **Ganga and Son** (Gandak nearby); Ganga to the north, Son to the west – a **water-fort**.
- Remains of Pataliputra: **Kumrahar**, **Bulandibagh**.
- **Kaushambi** was **not** a Magadha capital (Vatsa).
- **Vaishali**: called the **first republic** of the world (6th c. BC), set up by the **Lichchhavis**; named after King **Vishal**; **birthplace of Mahavira**; Buddhist pilgrimage site.
- Rajgriha: also **Mahavira's first sermon**.
!TRAP Pataliputra fort = Ajatashatru; capital shift = Udayin.
### 8. BUDDHIST COUNCILS

- First Council met just after the Buddha's **Mahaparinirvana**.

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- First non-Kshatriya dynasty: Nandas.
- First capital: Girivraj; first to choose Pataliputra: Udayin.
- Not a contemporary of Bimbisara, Prasenjit and the Buddha: **Milinda (Menander)**.
- **Prasenjit** = Kosala ruler, contemporary of the Buddha.
