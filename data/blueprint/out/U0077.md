## U0077 | PALLAVAS – SIMHAVISHNU, MAHENDRAVARMAN I, NARASIMHAVARMAN I MAMALLA, NANDIVARMAN II
### 1. ORIGIN, TERRITORY AND CAPITAL
#### 1.1 Territory
- Pallava territory: **Tondaimandalam** (APPSC-GS 2023); capital **Kanchipuram**.
#### 1.2 Origin Theories
| Theory | Basis |
|---|---|
| **Parthian (Pahlava)** | Foreign origin from the name Pahlava |
| **Andhra** | Earliest **Prakrit** charters from the **Krishna-Guntur** region; early Pallavas **replaced the Ikshvakus** there |
| **Local Tamil** | Native of Tondaimandalam |
- The Andhra theory rests on two facts: the **earliest charters** are in **Prakrit** and come from the **Krishna-Guntur** area, and the early Pallavas **took over Ikshvaku territory** there before ruling from Kanchi.
!TRAP Origin theories are Parthian, Andhra and local Tamil – NOT Scythian.
### 2. EARLY PALLAVA CHARTERS AND ANDHRA LINKS
#### 2.1 Charter → Ruler
| Charter | Ruler | Point |
|---|---|---|
| **Mayidavolu (Maidavolu) plates** | **Sivaskandavarman** (as **yuvaraja**) | **Prakrit**; among the earliest Pallava records; early Pallava rule in the Andhra **Krishna delta** |
| **Hirahadagalli plates** | Sivaskandavarman | Prakrit; he performed **Agnishtoma, Vajapeya, Ashvamedha** |
| **Manchikallu inscription** | **Simhavarman** | Pallava **conquest of Vijayapuri** after defeating the **Ikshvakus** |
| **Kudumiamalai** | Mahendravarman I | **Music** inscription credited to his patronage |
#### 2.2 End of the Ikshvakus
- Pallava **Simhavarman** defeated the **last Ikshvaku Rudrapurushadatta** → Pallava rule in the **Krishna valley**.
- The Pallavas caused the **downfall of the Ikshvakus** by conquering their territory.
- **Renati Chodas** = early Pallava feudatories.
- Loss later recorded: Krishna-Godavari lands of Mahendravarman I went to **Pulakesin II** (**Maruturu, Kopparam** inscriptions).
!KEY Manchikallu inscription of Simhavarman = proof that the Pallavas replaced the Ikshvakus of Vijayapuri.
!PAIR Mayidavolu | Sivaskandavarman | Prakrit plates || Manchikallu | Simhavarman | Conquest of Vijayapuri

### 4. MAHENDRAVARMAN I (600–630)
#### 4.1 Titles and Personality
- Son of **Simhavishnu**; many-sided – **painting, music, literature**.
- Titles: **Mattavilasa, Vichitrachitta, Gunabhara, Chitrakarapuli, Lalitankura**.
#### 4.2 Mattavilasa Prahasana
- Written by **Mahendravarman I** (APPSC-G1 2012).
- **Sanskrit** comic play (**farce**) mocking **Kapalikas and Buddhist monks**; shows social and religious life.
#### 4.3 Religious Conversion
- Began as a **Jain** → turned to **Shaivism** by Nayanar **Appar (Tirunavukkarasar)**, NOT Sambandar.
#### 4.4 Cave Temples
- Great builder of cave temples; **start of rock-cut temples in South India**.
- **Mandagapattu** cave temple – built **without brick, timber, metal or mortar**.
### 5. NARASIMHAVARMAN I MAMALLA (630–668)
#### 5.1 Titles
- **Mamalla / Mahamalla** ("great wrestler") and **Vatapikonda**; **Mamallapuram (Mahabalipuram)** named after him.
- Vichitrachitta belonged to his father Mahendravarman I, not to him.
#### 5.2 Wars with the Chalukyas
- Killed **Pulakesin II** at **Manimangala (642 CE)**; general **Paranjoti** sacked **Vatapi**.
#### 5.3 Naval Expeditions
- **Two naval expeditions to Sri Lanka (Ceylon)** to reinstate ally prince **Manavarma**.
#### 5.4 Architecture and Religion
- **Monolithic rathas** (Panch Ratha) at **Mahabalipuram**; a firm **Shaivite**.
- **Hiuen Tsang** visited Kanchi **c. 640 CE**; praised Narasimhavarman with Harsha and Pulakesin II.
### 6. NANDIVARMAN II PALLAVAMALLA (731–795)
- Accession: became king as a **boy of 12**, **chosen by ministers and nobles** from a **collateral line** after **Paramesvaravarman II** died (c. 731).
- Title: **Pallavamalla**.
- Setback: lost **Kanchi** temporarily to Chalukya **Vikramaditya II**.
- Religion: ardent **Vaishnavite**; built **Vaikuntha Perumal temple, Kanchi**; contemporary of **Tirumangai Alvar**.
!TRAP Appar → Mahendravarman I (Shaiva); Tirumangai → Nandivarman II (Vaishnava).
### 7. KANCHI AS A CENTRE OF LEARNING
#### 7.1 Book → Author
| Book | Author | Court / note |
|---|---|---|
| Mattavilasa Prahasana | Mahendravarman I | Sanskrit farce |
| **Kiratarjuniya** | **Bharavi** | **Simhavishnu's** court |
| **Dashakumaracharita, Kavyadarsha** | **Dandin** | Pallava court; called Vengi "**Andhra Nagaram**" |
- Kanchi drew scholars to the Pallava court: **Bharavi** under Simhavishnu, **Dandin** later, and the king himself (**Mahendravarman I**) wrote a Sanskrit play.
- **Hiuen Tsang** visited Kanchi **c. 640 CE**, during the reign of Narasimhavarman I.
- **Kudumiamalai** music inscription shows royal interest in music.
#### 7.2 Ghatika and the Mayurasharman Episode
- **Mayurasharman**, a Brahmin student at the **Kanchi Ghatika**, was insulted by a **Pallava horseman** → took up arms → founded the **Kadamba** kingdom (**Talagunda inscription**).
### 8. LATER PALLAVAS AND END
- Later rulers: **Dantivarman, Nandivarman III, Nripatunga, Aparajita**.
- **Aditya I Chola** killed **Aparajita** (c. **897 CE**) → end of the dynasty.
