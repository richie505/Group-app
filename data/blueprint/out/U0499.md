## U0499 | AGRICULTURAL CREDIT – INSTITUTIONAL STRUCTURE, NABARD, KCC, PRIORITY SECTOR LENDING, INTEREST SUBVENTION
### 1. INSTITUTIONAL STRUCTURE OF AGRICULTURAL CREDIT
- Agricultural credit flows through **commercial banks, cooperatives and RRBs** (multi-agency approach).
- Share of **non-institutional** (moneylender) credit in rural credit: about **90% (1950)** to **23.4% (2021-22)**.
#### 1.1 Tiers and Who Lends Directly
| Tier / body | Level | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| **PACS** | Village | Short-term, **direct to farmers** |
| **DCCB** | District | Short-term, **finances PACS** |
| **StCB** (e.g. APCOB) | State apex | Short-term, refinanced by NABARD |
| **Land Development Banks** (SCARDB/PCARDB) | State/primary | **Long-term** (land development, wells, machinery) |
| **RRBs** | District/region | Direct to small farmers, labourers, artisans |
| **Scheduled commercial banks** | – | Direct lending; rural branches 41,464 (2014) to **56,193 (July 2025)** |
- Short-term cooperative chain: **StCB (state) – DCCB (district) – PACS (village)**.
- Cooperative credit structure: State Cooperative Banks, Cooperative Land Development Banks, PACS.
- **Land Development Banks** (now **Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Banks**): part of the **long-term** cooperative structure; quasi-commercial; first at **Jhang, Punjab, 1920**.
- **Direct credit** to rural households: RRBs and Land Development Banks; **NABARD only refinances**.
- **SIDBI** = main institution for **MSMEs**, not agriculture.
- 11 small finance banks operate.
!TRAP Not a source of direct finance to farmers = NABARD (refinance). In the exam key, "not a source of direct finance" was **State Bank of India** in one option set; read options carefully.
### 2. REGIONAL RURAL BANKS
- First RRB: **Prathama Grameen Bank, Moradabad (UP)**, **2 October 1975**; **5 RRBs** in 1975.
- Originating committee: **Working Group on Rural Banks (1975), M. Narasimham**; RRBs part of the multi-agency approach.
- Sponsored by **Scheduled Commercial Banks**, with **Central and State Government** contributions; **regulated by NABARD**, supported by the sponsor bank.
- Serve rural and semi-urban areas; mobilise rural resources; lend mainly to **small and marginal farmers, agricultural labourers, rural artisans** (not large industry); supplement commercial banks.
- RRBs **do not take over agricultural refinance** functions.
- **Bhandari Committee (1994)**: RRB restructuring; identified **49 RRBs**; more powers to RRB Boards.
- **One State, One RRB**: **26 RRBs** amalgamated, effective **1 May 2025** (fourth phase); **28 RRBs** remain.
- RRBs and SFBs face a higher PSL target (**75%**) than commercial banks.
### 3. NABARD
#### 3.1 Establishment
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Full form | **National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development** (traps: Research, Bureau, Board) |
| Recommended by | **CRAFICARD (1979-81), B. Sivaraman** |
| Act | **NABARD Act, 1981** (Act 61 of 1981 of Parliament) |
| Established | **12 July 1982**, **Sixth Five Year Plan** |
| Headquarters | **Mumbai** |
| Initial corpus | Rs 100 crore |
| Ownership | RBI sold its stake in **2019**; fully owned by **Government of India** |
#### 3.2 Bodies Merged
- RBI's **Agricultural Credit Department (ACD)**, **Rural Planning and Credit Cell (RPCC)**, **Agricultural Refinance and Development Corporation (ARDC)**. NOT **IFCI**.
#### 3.3 Role
- **Apex development finance institution** for agriculture and **rural development** (not agriculture alone); premier institution for agricultural credit (APPSC 2019); **coordinates rural financing system** (APPSC 2024).
- **Primary role: refinance** (APPSC 2025): refinances **SCBs, RRBs, State Cooperative Banks, State Land Development Banks**; also NBFC-MFIs. Does **not** refinance **EXIM Bank**.
- Does not lend to farmers directly; lends directly to **States** through RIDF.
- Supervises **RRBs and cooperative banks**; launched **SHG-Bank Linkage**.
- Refinance 2024-25: total **₹3,25,497.81 crore**; direct refinance to cooperatives ₹37,217 crore.
#### 3.4 NABARD Funds
| Fund | Year / size | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **RIDF** | ₹42,714 crore | Loans to States for rural roads, bridges, irrigation; funded by banks' **PSL shortfall** deposits |
| LTIF (under PMKSY) | Rs 1,441 crore | Irrigation |
| Micro Irrigation Fund | – | Micro-irrigation |
| Warehouse Infrastructure Fund | **2013-14, ₹5,000 crore** | Warehousing |
| Food Processing Fund | **2014-15, ₹2,000 crore** | Food processing |
!KEY NABARD = apex rural finance institution because it mainly provides **refinance**; RIDF is administered by NABARD.
### 4. NABARD CREDIT PLANNING
- **Potential Linked Credit Plans (PLPs)**: district plans roll up into the **State Focus Paper**, the base for the State's Annual Credit Plan.
- NABARD March 2022 credit plan for AP (2022-23): increase of **10%**.
- AP **2026-27** plan: **Rs 5.11 lakh crore** (+~20%); agriculture Rs 2.55 lakh crore; crop loans Rs 1.66 lakh crore.
### 5. KISAN CREDIT CARD
#### 5.1 Basics
- Launched **August 1998** (1998-99) as a **NABARD model scheme** on the **R.V. Gupta Committee** recommendation; NOT 2001.
- Aim: adequate and timely credit for cultivation needs.
- Issuers: **commercial banks, RRBs, cooperative banks, small finance banks, PACS**; **not payment banks or local area banks**.
- Purposes: crop cultivation, post-harvest and marketing loans, **household consumption**, working capital for farm-asset maintenance, investment credit for allied activities.
- Short-term KCC credit does **not** cover purchase of combine harvesters, tractors, mini trucks.
- Extended to allied and non-farm activities in **2004**; **animal husbandry and fisheries from 2019** (with interest subvention); electronic KCCs per **T.M. Bhasin Working Group**.
#### 5.2 Limits and Data
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Collateral-free | Up to **₹2 lakh**; ₹3 lakh with hypothecation tie-up |
| Flexi-KCC | ₹10,000-50,000 for marginal farmers |
| Operative KCCs (31 March 2025) | **7.72 crore**; outstanding ₹10.20 lakh crore |
| RBI KCC Directions (19 June 2026) | Tenure **6 years** (from 5) for loans from **1 January 2027** |
| Crop loan period | Short crops 12 months; long crops up to 18 months |
- **Pashu Kisan Credit Card (2020)**: **Haryana**; first cards to 101 animal farmers in Bhiwani (6 December).
- **AP Pashu KCC (Apr 2026)**: up to ₹1.60 lakh without collateral at 7%; 4% on prompt repayment.
### 6. INTEREST SUBVENTION – MISS AND KISAN RIN PORTAL
| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| Lending rate on short-term KCC loans | **7%** |
| Subvention to lenders | **1.5%** |
| Prompt repayment incentive | **3%** |
| Effective rate for prompt repayers | **4%** |
- **Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (MISS)**: Central Sector scheme run through RBI and NABARD; limit **₹5 lakh (Aug 2026)**, earlier Rs 3 lakh.
- Post-harvest loans: 7% for up to **6 months** against **WDRA negotiable warehouse receipts** (small and marginal farmers).
- Subsidy since inception ₹1.87 lakh crore; ~5.9 crore beneficiaries (Feb 2025).
- **Kisan Rin Portal (September 2023)**: joins KCC data with interest-subvention claims; before it, ISS/PRI claims were manual to RBI and NABARD. Flagged ₹1,080.88 crore duplicate/excess claims of Rs 37,506.53 crore (2025-26).
!NUM 7 – 3 = 4% effective; lender gets 1.5%.
### 7. PRIORITY SECTOR LENDING
#### 7.1 Targets
| Category | % of ANBC |
|---|---|
| Overall (domestic banks) | **40%** |
| Overall (RRBs and SFBs) | **75%** |
| Agriculture | **18%** |
| Small and marginal farmers | **10%** |
| Non-corporate farmers | **14%** |
- Applies to SCBs, RRBs, SFBs, Local Area Banks, primary UCBs.
- **Categories**: agriculture, MSME, export credit, education, housing, social infrastructure, renewable energy, others; NOT luxury consumer goods.
#### 7.2 Shortfall and PSLCs
- **Shortfall** goes to NABARD's **RIDF** and similar funds.
- **PSLCs**: a short bank buys certificates from a surplus bank; the lending duty stays.
- **Narasimham Committee (1991)**: phase out directed credit; narrower PSL of **10%**.
- EAC-PM paper (May 2026): 7-8% of districts get ~45-46% of PSL credit; review the 40% target.
!PAIR 40% | 18% | 10% || Overall | Agriculture | Small and marginal
### 8. AGRICULTURAL CREDIT TRENDS
- Agency order of institutional credit: **commercial banks > cooperative banks > RRBs**.
- **Largest source**: commercial banks (about **three-fourth** in 2021-22); 2010-11: ₹3,45,877 crore of ₹4,68,291 crore (**73.86%**); cooperatives 16.68%.
- **Least**: foreign private banks.
- **After 1991 reforms**: notable **rise** in credit flow to agriculture (APPSC 2023).
- Ground Level Credit **₹28.69 lakh crore (FY25)** vs target ₹27.50 lakh crore (104%); quadrupled FY14-FY24 (CAGR 13.06%); NABARD projects >₹32.5 lakh crore in FY26.
- Skew: ~60% short-term; **South** gets 48% of credit with 17% of cropped area; East 8.2%, Central 13.7%.

### 10. ANDHRA PRADESH AGRICULTURAL CREDIT
- Three-tier cooperative structure; older data (2024-25): 442 branches, **2,042 PACS**; PACS being made **Multi-Service Centres** with NABARD support.
- **ACP 2025-26**: agri target **₹3,06,000 crore** (+13.27%); disbursed ₹2,96,764 crore (**97%**); ACP 2024-25 ₹2,64,000 crore.
- AP PSL (2025-26): **63.28%** of ANBC; agriculture 42.11%.
- **Tenant credit**: ₹1,490 crore under ACP 2025-26; APCOB ₹125.90 crore to 14,425 tenants (2024-25); channels **JLGs, RMGs, individual CCRC loans**.
- **SHG-Bank Linkage**: AP ranks **1st**; ~36% of national disbursement; recovery **99.99%**.
- 236th SLBC (Sept 2026): CM pushed lagging banks to meet lending targets.
