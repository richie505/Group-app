## U0055 | VISHNUKUNDINS – POLITICAL HISTORY, MADHAVA VARMA I AND II
### 1. POST-IKSHVAKU ANDHRA – DYNASTY SUCCESSION
#### 1.1 Correct Order
- After the **Ikshvakus** (capital **Vijayapuri**), Andhra split among small dynasties before the Vishnukundins united it.
- **Correct order:** **Ikshvakus → Brihatphalayanas → Salankayanas → Vishnukundins → Eastern Chalukyas**.
- The region **south of the Krishna** went to the **Pallavas** after the Ikshvakus.
| Order | Dynasty | Period | Key facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ikshvakus | c. 225–325 | Capital **Vijayapuri**; ended by the Pallavas (c. 301) |
| 2 | **Brihatphalayanas** | c. 275–325 | Former Ikshvaku feudatories north of the Krishna; name from a gotra; only known king **Jayavarma** (Brahmanical, "**Maheshwara-pada-parigrahita**") |
| 3 | **Salankayanas** | c. 300–420 | Capital **Vengi (Pedavegi, Eluru district)**; followed **Vaishnavism**; plates of Tripurantakam, Denduluru, Eluru; conquered by Madhava Varma II |
| 4 | **Vishnukundins** | c. **420–624** | Rose after **Samudragupta's southern campaign (358 CE)** weakened the Pallavas |
| 5 | Eastern Chalukyas | from 624 | Founded by **Kubja Vishnuvardhana** at Vengi |
- **Anandagotrikas:** Kandara kings, capital **Kandarapuram** (Chejerla inscription); former Ikshvaku feudatories. **Atti Varma (Attivarman)** of the Ananda gotra performed **Hiranyagarbha danam** (Gorantla plates).
!TRAP Vijayapuri is the Ikshvaku capital, NOT the Salankayana capital (Vengi/Pedavegi).
!KEY APPSC G2 2008 order question: Ikshvakus → Brihatphalayanas → Salankayanas → Vishnukundins. Salankayanas = Vaishnavism (G2 2011).
#### 1.2 Significance
- Vishnukundins are the **link between the Ikshvaku age and the Eastern Chalukyas**, who took over their **agrahara system** and administration.
- Their inscriptions and capitals lie across **both Telugu states** – Chikkulla, Polamuru (Godavari) and Tummalagudem (Telangana).
### 2. ORIGIN AND ORIGINAL HOME
#### 2.1 Origin Debate
- **Majority view:** **Brahmins of the Vishnukundina gotra**.
| Historian | Origin theory |
|---|---|
| **B.V. Krishna Rao** | Brahmins |
| **K. Gopalachari** | **Agnikula Kshatriyas** |
| **N. Venkataramanayya** | Brahmins who adopted Kshatriya ways |
| Others | Shudras raised through the Hiranyagarbha rite |
#### 2.2 Original Home
- **Telangana** – early records and forts at **Amrabad, Keesaragutta, Indrapalanagara**.
- First ruled Telangana **north of the Krishna**, later spread into **coastal Andhra**.
- **Keesaragutta** may mark their start as **Vakataka feudatories**.
!PAIR Gopalachari | Agnikula Kshatriya | minority view || Krishna Rao | Brahmins | majority view
### 3. CAPITALS AND THEIR SHIFTS
| Capital | Where / which ruler |
|---|---|
| **Amarapura (Amarapuri)** | **First capital**; kings called themselves "**Amarapurishulu**". Identified with Munnanuru (Amrabad, Nagarkurnool), where yaga-vedikas were found; B.S.L. Hanumantha Rao says Velpuru (Guntur) |
| **Indrapalanagara** (Sukrapuram) | Built by founder **Indravarma** (Tummalagudem inscription); at **Tummalagudem** (Yadadri-Bhuvanagiri/Nalgonda, Telangana); capital of **Madhava Varma I and Govinda Varma** |
| **Bezawada (Vijayawada)** | Capital for a time after they gained the **Krishna region**; Indrakeeladri and Mogalrajapuram caves carved here |
| **Denduluru (Dendulurapura)** | Near Vengi/Pedavegi, Eluru (West Godavari); **Madhava Varma II** moved the capital here from Amarapuri because of **constant Pallava wars**, and fortified Amarapuri |
!TRAP Indrapalanagara is at Tummalagudem in Telangana, NOT near Denduluru/Eluru.
!KEY APPSC G2 2025: Capital shifted Amarapuri → Denduluru = **Madhava Varma II**. An older Group-2 key credited **Vikramendravarma** with shifting the capital from Amarapuri (Amaravati region) to Denduluru (West Godavari) – read the options carefully.
### 4. RULERS – CHRONOLOGICAL ORDER AND CONTRIBUTION
| # | Ruler | Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Indravarma** | **Founder**; **Vakataka vassal**; built **Indrapalanagara**; Ramatirtham plates |
| 2 | **Madhava Varma I** | Ruled **22 years**; married a **Vakataka princess**; excavated caves at **Undavalli, Bhairavakona, Mogalrajapuram** |
| 3 | **Govinda Varma I** | Expanded into Krishna-Godavari region and both sides of **Sriparvata**; queen **Paramabhattarika** built a vihara; **Govinda Vihara** at Chaitanyapuri; title **Shadabhigna** |
| 4 | **Madhava Varma II** | **Greatest** ruler; **golden age**; **Vedic revival**; capital to Denduluru; Velpuru pillar |
| 5 | **Vikramendra Varma I** | Son of MV II by the Vakataka princess; "**Mahakavi**", Parama Saugata |
| 6 | **Indrabhattaraka Varma** | **Ghatikas**; defeated by Maukhari **Ishanavarma**; killed in the **Kalinga war** |
| 7 | **Vikramendra Varma II** | **Chikkulla, Tundi, Tummalagudem-2** plates; put down revolts |
| 8 | **Manchana Bhattaraka** | **Last** ruler; lost to Pulakeshin II, 624 CE |
- Some lists place **Deva Varma** and **Madhava Varma III** after Madhava Varma II; ruler numbering differs between historians.
!TRAP Founder = **Indravarma**, NOT Govinda Varma.
!PAIR Madhava Varma I | caves | Undavalli, Mogalrajapuram || Madhava Varma II | sacrifices | 11 Ashvamedhas
### 5. MADHAVA VARMA II – SACRIFICES, CONQUESTS, JUSTICE
#### 5.1 Sacrifices
- **11 Ashvamedhas**, **16 Rajasuyas**, **1 Purushamedha**, Hiranyagarbha, Agnistoma; about **1,000 yagas** in all.
#### 5.2 Conquests (100+ battles)
| Opponent | Result |
|---|---|
| **Salankayanas** (Vaishnava) | Took **Vengi** (Pedavegi, Eluru district) |
| **Pallavas** | Took **Karma-rashtra** up to the **Gundlakamma** |
| **Vakatakas** | Up to the Godavari coast; married a daughter of **Prithvisena II** |
| **Anandagotrikas** | Won the title **Trikuta-Malayadhipati** |
- **Khanapur plates (Satara, Maharashtra)** record his marriage to a daughter of Vakataka **Prithvisena II**.
#### 5.3 Justice Legend
- **Bezawada inscription (12th century):** he **sentenced his own son to death** after the prince's chariot killed a poor woman's son.
!NUM 11 Ashvamedhas | 16 Rajasuyas | 1 Purushamedha | 100+ battles | ~1,000 yagas
### 6. TITLES – RULER-WISE
| Title | Ruler |
|---|---|
| **Janasraya** | **Madhava Varma II** (G2 2025 key) |
| **Trikuta-Malayadhipati** | **Madhava Varma II** – after defeating the Anandagotrikas; **Trikuta = Kotappakonda (Guntur)** |
| Trisamudradhipati, Devadhideva, Parameshti, Parama Brahmanya | Madhava Varma II |
| **Shadabhigna** (master of six sciences) | **Govinda Varma** |
| **Mahakavi**, Parama Saugata | **Vikramendra Varma I** |
| **Satyasraya**, Paramamaheshwara, Chaturdhayaka Yuddha Veera, Ghatika-vapta-punyasanchaya | **Indrabhattaraka Varma** |
| **Sakala Bhuvana Rakshabharanaikashraya** | **Vikramendra Varma II** |
!TRAP Janasraya is sometimes also given to Vikramendra I; the APPSC key is Madhava Varma II. Satyasraya here = Indrabhattaraka, not a Chalukya king.
### 7. INSCRIPTIONS – RULER-WISE
| Inscription | Ruler | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Chikkulla plates** (Tuni, East Godavari) | **Vikramendra Varma II** | Telugu phrase "**Vijaya rajya samvatsarambul**"; Vakataka marriage ties |
| **Ipur plates** (2), Tenali | **Madhava Varma I** | Lakshmi and conch symbols |
| **Polamuru plates**, Ramachandrapuram | Madhava Varma (IV in some numbering) | Describes divine ordeals |
| **Tummalagudem plates** | **Govinda Varma I** (Tummalagudem-2: Vikramendra II) | Grant to Paramabhattarika's vihara; genealogy; **first Sanskrit inscription in Telangana** |
| **Khanapur plates** (Satara) | **Madhava Varma II** | Marriage to Prithvisena II's daughter |
| **Velpuru pillar** | Madhava Varma II | Ganapati installed in his 33rd year |
| Ramatirtham | Indravarma | – |
| Chaitanyapuri | Govinda Varma | In **Prakrit** |
- Total: **21 inscriptions = 16 copper-plate + 5 stone**.
!TRAP Not all 21 inscriptions are Sanskrit – Govinda Varma's Chaitanyapuri record is in Prakrit.
### 8. WARS AND DECLINE
- **Indrabhattaraka Varma** was defeated by Maukhari **Ishanavarma**, whose son married his daughter (**Jaunpur inscription**).
- He was **killed in the Kalinga war** by **Prithvimula** of the Ranadurjayas and a Kalinga king.
- **End:** **Pulakeshin II** (Badami Chalukya) defeated **Manchana Bhattaraka** at **Kunala (Kolleru)**, **624 CE**.
- His brother **Kubja Vishnuvardhana** founded the **Eastern Chalukyas of Vengi**.
!PAIR Ishanavarma | Maukhari | defeated Indrabhattaraka || Pulakeshin II | Badami Chalukya | ended dynasty, 624

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- First capital: Amarapura; founder: Indravarma.
- Last ruler: Manchana Bhattaraka.
- First Sanskrit inscription in Telangana: Tummalagudem plates (Govinda Varma I).
- Only known Brihatphalayana king: Jayavarma.

### 11. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. Founder = **Indravarma**, not Govinda Varma.
2. Janasraya and Trikuta-Malayadhipati = **Madhava Varma II**.
3. Shadabhigna = Govinda Varma; Mahakavi = Vikramendra I; Satyasraya = Indrabhattaraka.
4. Chikkulla plates (Telugu phrase) = **Vikramendra Varma II**, not Madhava Varma.
5. Indrapalanagara = **Tummalagudem, Telangana**, not Eluru.
6. Vijayapuri = Ikshvaku capital; Vengi = Salankayana capital.
7. Salankayanas = **Vaishnava**; Brihatphalayana Jayavarma = Shaiva (Maheshwara).
8. Dynasty ended by **Pulakeshin II** (624), not the Pallavas.
9. Khanapur plates are in **Satara (Maharashtra)**, not Andhra.
10. Order: Ikshvakus → Brihatphalayanas → Salankayanas → Vishnukundins.
