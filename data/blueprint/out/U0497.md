## U0497 | LAND REFORMS – ABOLITION OF INTERMEDIARIES, TENANCY REFORM, CEILING LAWS, CONSOLIDATION; OUTCOMES
### 1. COMPONENTS AND AIMS OF LAND REFORMS
| Measure | Meaning / outcome |
|---|---|
| **Abolition of intermediaries** | Removing middlemen – the **actual cultivator** has direct contact with the **State** |
| **Tenancy reform** | **Rent** cap **1/4 to 1/6** of produce; **security of tenure**; **ownership** to tenants; failed through "**voluntary surrenders**" and evictions |
| **Land ceiling** | **Legal maximum** land an individual/family may hold; surplus redistributed to the **landless** |
| **Consolidation** | Pooling scattered plots |
- Steps for a helpful agrarian structure: abolition of intermediaries, tenancy reforms, ceiling on holdings.
- Aims: reduce **inequality** (ceilings free surplus land for the landless), agrarian restructuring, agricultural growth; major aim – agricultural land to **all the landless**.
- First intermediary-abolition Act: **Madras Estates (Abolition and Conversion into Ryotwari) Act, 1948**.
!TRAP **Multiple cropping** is NOT a part of land reforms.
### 2. LAND CEILING LAWS
| Feature | Pre-1972 laws | 1972 national guidelines |
|---|---|---|
| Unit | **Individual** (fuelled **benami** transfers to relatives) | **Family** |
| Ceiling | High, varied by State | **10–18 acres** two-crop irrigated; **27** single-crop; **54** dry |
- Two rounds: **1950s and early 1970s**; about 21 States had ceiling laws (2020).
- **Outcome**: **less than 2%** of cultivable area redistributed – due to exemptions for **plantations, orchards, "mechanised" farms**.
### 3. TENANCY REFORM AND OPERATION BARGA
- **Operation Barga** (**West Bengal**, **Left Front**, **1978**): **recording names of sharecroppers (bargadars)** (APPSC 2019); heritable rights and **75%** share of produce.
- Legal backing: Bengal Land Holding Revenue Act, 1979.
!KEY Rent cap 1/4 to 1/6 of produce; Bhoodan appeal 1/6th of land; bargadar share 75%.
### 4. CONSOLIDATION, BHOODAN AND COOPERATIVE FARMING
#### 4.1 Consolidation
- Succeeded in **Punjab, Haryana, western UP**; little progress in southern and eastern States.
#### 4.2 Bhoodan-Gramdan
- **Acharya Vinoba Bhave**, **Pochampally** (Nalgonda, Telangana), **18 April 1951**, amid the **Telangana armed struggle**; led to **Gramdan**.
- Appeal to donate **1/6th** of land; over **40 lakh acres** pledged, much barren; called the "**Bloodless Revolution**".
#### 4.3 Cooperative and Joint Farming
- **Kumarappa Committee (1949)** recommended cooperative farming → **Nagpur Resolution (1959)** made it a party goal; **failed** – captured by rich peasants to evade ceilings.
- **Charan Singh** did not approve cooperative farming.
- **Joint cooperative farming features**: land **pooled voluntarily**; produce shared per land/labour contributed.
!TRAP **Compulsory membership** is NOT a feature (APPSC G2 2017).
### 5. CONSTITUTIONAL SUPPORT
| Amendment / case | Land-reform change |
|---|---|
| **1st (1951)** | Inserted **Arts. 31A, 31B** and **Ninth Schedule** (after Kameshwar Singh v. Bihar) |
| **4th (1955)** | Restated Art. 31(2); widened 31A (zamindari abolition, planning, mineral control) |
| **17th (1964)** | Land under personal cultivation within ceiling acquired only at **market value**; **widened "estate"**; challenged in **Golaknath (1967)** |
| **25th (1971)** | Inserted **Art. 31C** |
| 34th (1974) / 66th (1990) | More land laws incl. AP Acts placed in Ninth Schedule |
| **75th (1993)** | **Art. 323B clause (h)** – "rent and tenancy rights" tribunals |
| **I.R. Coelho (2007)** | Ninth Schedule laws added **after 24 April 1973** (Kesavananda Bharati) open to **Basic Structure** review |
### 6. AP LAND LAWS – ABOLITION AND TENANCY
| Law | Key provisions |
|---|---|
| **Madras Estates (Abolition and Conversion into Ryotwari) Act, 1948** | Abolished **zamindari and inamdari** in Andhra area; recommended by **Prakasam Committee (1938)** |
| **Hyderabad Jagir Abolition Regulation, 1949** | Jagirs and **Sarf-e-khas** lands became **Diwani (ryotwari)** after Police Action |
| **Hyderabad (Telangana Area) Tenancy Act, 1950** | **Protected tenants** (6 years' cultivation) got heritable rights; owner on paying **60%** of market price in **20 instalments** |
| **AP (Andhra Area) Tenancy Act, 1956** | **Subba Rao Committee**; written leases; rent cap **30% wet / 25% dry**; minimum 6-year lease |
### 7. AP CEILING AND LAND PROTECTION LAWS
| Law | Key provisions |
|---|---|
| Ceiling Act, 1961 | **Individual** unit; 27–324 acres; surplus under 15,000 acres |
| **AP Land Reforms (Ceiling on Agricultural Holdings) Act, 1973** | Bill under CM **P.V. Narasimha Rao**; **Ninth Schedule**; in force **1 January 1975** |
| **AP Assigned Lands (Prohibition of Transfers) Act, 1977** | Land assigned to landless poor **cannot be sold or transferred** |
| **AP Land Grabbing (Prohibition) Act, 1982** | Punishes grabbers of government and weaker-section lands |
- **1973 Act**: unit = **family of 5**; **husband's and wife's land clubbed**; each major son = separate unit; +1/5 ceiling per extra member.
- Ceiling **10–27 acres wet, 35–54 acres dry**; **benami** transfers after **30 Jan 1970** ignored; **Land Tribunal** headed by the **RDO**.
- Exemptions: plantations, orchards, religious/charitable trusts, cooperatives, Bhoodan lands.
- **Phase II**: pushed by **Srikakulam Naxalite unrest** and **1966–69 droughts**; **20 lakh acres** surplus estimated vs only **5 lakh** taken over.
- **Indira Prabha** (Y.S. Rajasekhara Reddy): development of barren lands allotted to landless poor.
- **Feb 2026**: AP proposes to decriminalise two offences under the 1973 Act (among 42 offences).
!PAIR 1961 Act | Individual | AP || 1973 Act | Family of 5 | AP
### 8. KONERU RANGA RAO COMMITTEE AND NATIONAL COUNCIL
- **Koneru Ranga Rao Committee (AP Land Committee, 2006)**: set up by the **Y.S. Rajasekhara Reddy** government; to resolve **land-related problems in AP** (APPSC 2021).
- **Focus**: **assigned lands**, confirmation of land rights, **tenancy regulation**, **land records** – **NOT corporate farming** (APPSC G2 2017).
- **National Council for Land Reforms (2008)**: chaired by the **Prime Minister**; committee on "State Agrarian Relations and Unfinished Task in Land Reforms".
- **Apr 2026**: land still skewed – about **28.9%** of land with 0–1 ha farmers; leasing reform and record digitisation urged.
### 9. AGRICULTURAL LAND LEASING
- **Model Agricultural Land Leasing Act, 2016**: **NITI Aayog** (**T. Haque** committee); legalises leasing, tenants get credit/insurance, owner's title protected.
- **First State to implement**: **Uttarakhand** (APPSC 2018).
- AP: **CCRC Act 2019** (11-month cards); proposed AP New Tenancy Act 2024 → tenant entitlement cards.
