## U0418 | MINORITY RIGHTS – ARTICLES 29 AND 30, NCM, SACHAR COMMITTEE, SCHEMES
### 1. MEANING OF MINORITY AND NOTIFIED COMMUNITIES
#### 1.1 Meaning
- **"Minority" is not defined** in the Constitution; the word is used in **Articles 29, 30, 350A and 350B**.
- The Constitution recognises only **religious and linguistic minorities** (Articles 29 and 30 protect both).
- **NCM Act, Section 2(c):** minority = any community **notified by the Central Government**; critics say it gives the Centre **"unbridled power"**, uneasy with T.M.A. Pai.
- **Unit for status = the State**, not all India (**T.M.A. Pai, 2002**): status decided by population in the State concerned.
- States can name their own minorities; **11 States** had declared Jains a minority before 2014.
- **Minority rights are not absolute**: the State may regulate standards but not take away management.
- Religious minorities → NCM and Ministry of Minority Affairs; linguistic minorities → identified State-wise, **Special Officer (Art. 350B)**; not notified under the NCM Act.

### 2. CONSTITUTIONAL ARTICLES
#### 2.1 Articles 29 and 30
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **29(1)** | **Any section of citizens** with a distinct language, script or culture may conserve it |
| **29(2)** | No denial of admission to State-maintained/aided institutions on grounds only of **religion, race, caste or language** |
| **30(1)** | **All minorities**, religious or linguistic, may **establish and administer** educational institutions of their choice ("Charter of Education Rights"); madrasas run under it |
| **30(1A)** | **44th Amendment**: compensation protects minority institutions on compulsory acquisition of property |
| **30(2)** | State shall not discriminate in **aid** on ground of minority management; may regulate standards without destroying minority character |
- Art. 29 is wider (all citizens, not confined to minorities); applies only to citizens. Art. 30 = **only minorities**.
- Political promises to conserve a section's language are **not a "corrupt practice"** under the **RPA, 1951**.
- Related: **Art. 16(2)** – no discrimination in public employment (religion, race, caste, sex, descent, place of birth); **Art. 19** – freedom; **Art. 26** – manage religious affairs.
!TRAP 29(2) does not list **sex** or **place of birth**.
#### 2.2 Linguistic Minority Articles
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **347** | President may recognise a language spoken by a substantial part of a State's population |
| **350** | Representation for grievances in any language used in the Union/State |
| **350A** | **Mother-tongue** instruction at the **primary stage** |
| **350B** | **Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities**, appointed by and reports to the **President**; reports laid before Parliament and sent to States |
- 350A and 350B added by **Section 21, 7th Amendment, 1956** (States reorganisation). There is no Special Officer for every State.
- **Commissioner for Linguistic Minorities:** office set up **1957**; HQ **Prayagraj**; 10-point programme with States.

### 4. NATIONAL COMMISSION FOR MINORITIES
#### 4.1 Evolution
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1978 | Non-statutory Minorities Commission (**MHA resolution**) |
| **1992** | **NCM Act** (in force 17 May 1993) → statutory; renamed NCM |
| 1993 | First statutory Commission; first chairman **Justice Mohammed Sardar Ali Khan** |
| 1995 | Amendment adds **Vice-Chairperson** → 7 members |
| 2006 | **Ministry of Minority Affairs** (Haj added Oct 2016) |
#### 4.2 Composition and Powers
- **Chairperson + Vice-Chairperson + 5 Members = 7**; tenure 3 years; **five incl. Chairperson** must be from minority communities.
- Nominated by the **Central Government**, not the President.
- **Statutory, not constitutional.**
- Functions: evaluate development, monitor safeguards, examine complaints, recommend measures (not conducting elections to minority institutions).
- Powers of a **civil court** while inquiring: summon witnesses, call for records.
- **July 2026:** **Harjit Singh Grewal** Chairperson; Munawari Begum Vice-Chairperson; only **4 of 7** posts filled.
#### 4.3 Other Bodies

| **State Minorities Commissions** | Statutory in **19** States/UTs incl. **Andhra Pradesh**, Telangana, Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, West Bengal, UP, Bihar, Punjab |

### 8. BC, DNT SCHEMES, CREDIT AND PM VISHWAKARMA
#### 8.1 Scholarships (MoSJE)
- **PM-YASASVI** (2021): OBC/EBC/DNT students, income below Rs 2.5 lakh; **60:40**; **30%** slots for girls; portal run by **NTA**.
- **SHREYAS**: higher education; funds 8,571 SC students at IITs and IIMs.
#### 8.2 DNTs
- **Criminal Tribes Act 1871** (first used in north India, later Bengal) → **repealed 1952**; Census 2027 will count DNTs.
- **Renke Commission** (report 2008) – first on DNTs; **Idate Commission** (2014-17): about **1,200** DNTs in SC/ST/OBC lists; **267-268** never classified.
- **SEED** (2022): **Rs 200 crore**; income below Rs 2.5 lakh; coaching, health insurance, livelihoods, housing; portal run by **Department of Social Justice**, not NTA; only Rs 69.3 crore spent – States did not issue DNT certificates.

#### 8.4 PM Vishwakarma
- Launched **17 September 2023** (Vishwakarma Jayanti); artisans in **18 trades**.

- Nodal: **Ministry of MSME** (with MSDE and DFS).
!TRAP PM Vishwakarma is **not a fishermen's scheme**.
### 9. WAQF ADMINISTRATION
- Order: **Waqf Act 1954 → Waqf Act 1995 → Amendment 2013 → Amendment 2025**.
- **Central Waqf Council:** statutory, **1964** (Sec 8A of 1954 Act; now Sec 9 of 1995 Act); advises Centre on State Waqf Boards.
- **2013 Amendment:** Council can direct State Boards; bars sale, gift, mortgage, exchange, transfer of waqf property.
- **Waqf (Amendment) Bill 2024:** Lok Sabha **8 August 2024**; aim transparency, accountability, stopping misuse.
- **2025 Act** (assent 5 April 2025; 1995 Act renamed **UMEED Act**): **waqf by user** only for properties registered before the law; **non-Muslim members** (at least two per Board); central portal within 6 months – **UMEED portal** (June 2025); **Limitation Act** applies; appeal to HC within 90 days.
- **SC interim order (Sep 2025):** stayed the **5-years-practising-Islam** condition and Collector's power; capped non-Muslims at **3** (State Board) / **4** (Central Council); accepted the Limitation Act change.
