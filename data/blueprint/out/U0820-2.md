### 4. 52nd G7 SUMMIT – ÉVIAN-LES-BAINS
#### 4.1 Summit
- **52nd G7 Summit**, **Évian-les-Bains, France** (not Paris), **15-17 June 2026**, at the invitation of President **Macron**.
- Theme (outreach): "**Working Together to Address Major International Challenges**"; French priorities: solidarity through new partnerships and reducing global imbalances.
- **India invited for the 13th time**, **7th consecutive year**; invitees India, China, South Korea, Kenya, Brazil.
- **India's proposals:** **IMPACT** – **International Mobilisation Partnership for Accelerating Connectivity and Trade** (G7 capital + India's talent + Global South ownership; Africa, Latin America, Pacific); **Global Skills Partnership**.
- India's concern: **Strait of Hormuz** disruption – LPG, LNG, diesel, fertilisers.
- **Outcome:** **Declaration on Securing Supply Chains for Critical Minerals** (via **PGII**); also debt relief, Strait of Hormuz, cancer research, minors' online safety.

#### 4.3 Background
- **G7**: informal; **no treaty, no permanent secretariat**; **EU** participates as a **non-enumerated member**.
- PYQ: **G20 Common Framework** – with the **Paris Club**, for **Low Income Countries'** debt; G20 members include Argentina, Mexico, South Africa, Turkey.
### 5. INDIA-FRANCE – NICE OUTCOMES AND INNOVATION ROADMAP 2030
- **India-France Innovation Roadmap 2030** approved by Modi and Macron at **Nice, 14 June 2026**; **13 outcomes**; new **Economic Security Dialogue**; **Joint India-France AI Task Force**.
- Builds on the **Horizon 2047** roadmap and **India-France Year of Innovation 2026**; based on **Viksit Bharat 2047 + France 2030**.
- Ties elevated to **Special Global Strategic Partnership** on **17 February 2026**; Strategic Partnership since **1998**.
#### 5.1 Pillars
- **Trusted AI:** India-France Declaration on AI (2025); **AI Action Summit – France 2025**; **AI Impact Summit – India 2026**; child online safety; **DEPA** (Data Empowerment and Protection Architecture).
- **Students:** France targets **30,000 Indian students by 2030**; **France – first country with Mutual Recognition of Qualifications with India (2018)**.
- **Institutions:** **CEFIPRA** (Indo-French Centre for Promotion of Advanced Research), **IFIN** (India-France Innovation Network), **FIC-LSH** (life sciences for health), InnoXchange Bridge.
- **Aeronautics campus: Kanpur** (with **MSDE**).
- **Space:** Bengaluru Space Expo (7-9 Sept) and International Space Summit, **Paris** (9-10 Sept).
- **Health data:** **ICMR** with France's **Health Data Hub**.
- **ATL Bridge**: launched at **India Innovates 2026, Nice** – **Atal Innovation Mission (NITI Aayog)** + **Dassault Systèmes Foundation** (AIM since 2016).
- **Safran-SatSure MoU**: **AI geospatial intelligence** (France-India Year of Innovation).
- PYQ: **ISA** launched at **COP21 (2015)**; not all UN members are members.
