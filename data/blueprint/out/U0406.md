## U0406 | WOMEN'S WELFARE SCHEMES – BETI BACHAO BETI PADHAO, MISSION SHAKTI, PMMVY, UJJWALA
### 1. MWCD UMBRELLA MISSIONS
- From **1 April 2022**, MWCD schemes are grouped under **three** umbrella missions (APPSC 2021 key: "Three").
| Mission | Coverage |
|---|---|
| **Mission Shakti** | Women: **Sambal** (safety) + **Samarthya** (empowerment) |
| **Mission Vatsalya** | Child protection, foster care, juvenile justice homes; subsumed ICPS; CSS 2021-22 to 2025-26, **₹10,916 crore**; aligned to **JJ Act 2015**; **Childline 1098** integrated with **112** |
| **Saksham Anganwadi & Poshan 2.0** | Ages **0-6**; Anganwadi Services, Poshan Abhiyaan, **Scheme for Adolescent Girls**; **Poshan Tracker**; **Poshan Bhi Padhai Bhi** (ECCE) |
- **ICDS** links the Ministries of Health and WCD: health, nutrition, early childhood care.
!TRAP LPG in women's names = **Ujjwala** (Petroleum), not Mission Vatsalya.
### 2. MISSION SHAKTI – SAMBAL AND SAMARTHYA
#### 2.1 Components
| Sub-scheme | Component | Purpose |

### 3. BETI BACHAO BETI PADHAO (BBBP)
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Launch | **22 January 2015**, **Panipat (Haryana)** |
| Nodal ministry | **Women and Child Development** |
| Joint ministries | **Health & Family Welfare** + **Education** (then HRD) |
| Not involved | **Social Justice & Empowerment** (APPSC 2022) |
| Funding | **100% central**; initial ₹100 crore |
| Coverage | **100 gender-critical districts** (low CSR) → now **all districts** |
| Vertical | **Sambal** of Mission Shakti |
- **Objectives:** arrest the decline in **Child Sex Ratio**; prevent gender-biased sex selection; ensure girls' survival, protection and education – **not legal aid** (APPSC G1 2022).
- BBBP is a PM flagship with Make in India and Digital India (Antyodaya Yojana is not one).
!TRAP BBBP began in **2015**, not "since 2006".

### 4. MATERNAL SCHEMES
| Scheme | Year | Purpose / benefit |
|---|---|---|
| **JSY** | 2005 | Cash for **institutional delivery** (NHM, 100% central) |
| **IGMSY** | 2010 | **Pilot** maternity benefit; forerunner of PMMVY |
| **PMSMA** | 2016 | **Free antenatal check-up on the 9th of every month** (APPSC 2022) |
| **PMMVY** | Announced **31 Dec 2016**; from **1 Jan 2017** | **Partial wage-loss compensation**, **first living child**: **₹5,000** (earlier 3 instalments: 1,000 + 2,000 + 2,000); in sync with NFSA 2013 |
| **PMMVY 2.0** | 2022 (Mission Shakti) | ₹5,000 first child; **₹6,000 for second child if a girl**; **regular government employees excluded** |
- PMMVY nodal: **MWCD**; completed nine years in 2026. MMR fell 130 → 88.
!TRAP **PMSMA** = check-up on the 9th; **PMMVY** = cash for the first child.
### 5. PRADHAN MANTRI UJJWALA YOJANA
- **Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas**; launched **1 May 2016**, **Ballia (UP)**.
- **Free LPG connections** in **women's names** for **BPL households** (APPSC 2025); ends indoor smoke, improves health.
- **Ujjwala 2.0 (August 2021, Mahoba):** **free first refill + hotplate**; **migrants self-declare address**.
- Reach: over **10.5 crore** households (2026).
!TRAP **Ujjawala (2007)** = anti-trafficking (now Shakti Sadan); **Ujjwala (2016)** = LPG.
### 6. ECONOMIC EMPOWERMENT
| Scheme | Ministry | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Rashtriya Mahila Kosh** | **MWCD** | **1993**, **registered society**; lends to **NGOs/MFIs** which on-lend micro-credit to poor women |
| **Mahila e-Haat** | MWCD | **2016** online marketing platform for women entrepreneurs and SHGs; run by **RMK** |
| **Stand-Up India** | **Finance** (2016) | ₹10 lakh-₹1 crore for **greenfield** enterprises; at least **one SC/ST and one woman** borrower per branch; 51% holding for firms; SIDBI handholding |
| **DAY-NRLM** | **Rural Development** | **2011 as Aajeevika**, renamed 2016; ~10 crore women in 90 lakh SHGs |
| **Lakhpati Didi** | Rural Development | SHG member earning **at least ₹1 lakh a year** |
| Mahila Samman Savings Certificate | Finance / India Post | **2 years**, ₹1,000-₹2 lakh, **7.5%** |
!TRAP Stand-Up India funds **new** enterprises, not existing ones.
