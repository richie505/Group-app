## U0692 | SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY AND INNOVATION POLICY - 1958 TO STIP 2020
### 1. POLICY MILESTONES
| Policy | PM | Core thrust |
|---|---|---|
| **Scientific Policy Resolution 1958** (first) | **Nehru** | "Foster, promote and sustain" science - pure, applied, educational; national labs |
| **Technology Policy Statement 1983** | **Indira Gandhi** | Technological competence, **self-reliance**, less import dependence |
| **S&T Policy 2003** | **Vajpayee** | Science and technology together; R&D investment |
| **STI Policy 2013** | **Manmohan Singh** | Added **Innovation**; "STI for the People" |
| **Draft STIP 2020** (fifth) | **Modi** | Decentralised, bottom-up; Atmanirbhar Bharat, open science, equity |
!KEY 1958 - 1983 - 2003 - 2013 - 2020 = Nehru - Indira - Vajpayee - Manmohan - Modi.
### 2. OBJECTIVES OF EACH POLICY
- **STP 2003**: launched **3 January 2003**; research, innovation, technology transfer, **optimal use of existing resources** (all four listed objectives correct).
- **STI 2013**: India in **top five** scientific powers by 2020; publications share **3.5% to over 7%**; "small idea-small money", **Risky Idea Fund**, Fund for Innovations for Social Inclusion; ~10 high-impact sectors.
- **Draft STIP 2020**: **top three** scientific superpowers in the coming decade; double FTE researchers, GERD and private GERD share every 5 years; **One Nation, One Subscription**; **STI Development Bank**; **RIEF**; National Research Foundation with private and CSR money.
!TRAP **Atal Innovation Mission** is NOT a STIP 2020 outcome - set up by **NITI Aayog in 2016**. Top five by 2020 = STI 2013 (not STP 2003).
- **ONOS** approved Nov 2024, about **Rs 6,000 crore** (not Rs 60,000 crore).
### 3. GERD TARGETS AND R&D SPENDING
| Policy | GERD target |
|---|---|
| SPR 1958, TPS 1983 | No numeric target |
| STP 2003 | **2% of GDP** by end of **Tenth Plan** |
| STI 2013 | 2%, private matching public |
| STIP 2020 | Double GERD every 5 years |
- Reality: **0.84% of GDP in FY 2023-24**, a 15-year high (earlier ~0.64-0.65%). The 2% goal has been missed under every policy.
- **24 May 2026** recommendations: GERD 2% in 4-5 years via private sector; **NISPG**; single fellowship **"Vigyan Nidhi"**.
### 4. IMPLEMENTATION AND ADVISORY BODIES
| Body | Fact |
|---|---|
| **PSA** | Office 1999; first PSA **A.P.J. Abdul Kalam** |
| **PM-STIAC** | 2018; chaired by PSA |
| DST / DSIR / DBT | 1971 / 1985 (parent of CSIR, 1942) / 1986 |
| **ANRF** | **ANRF Act 2023**; **PM = President**; S&T and Education Ministers = Vice-Presidents; subsumed SERB |
- ANRF: **Rs 50,000 crore (2023-28)**, ~Rs 36,000 crore from non-government sources.
- **RDI Fund**: **Rs 1 lakh crore** for private R&D, approved **1 July 2025**, via ANRF; DST nodal.
- **NITI Aayog**: nodal for GII rank; **India Innovation Index**; hosts AIM.
### 5. DECADE OF INNOVATION

- **National Technology Day - 11 May**: **Pokhran-II** (1998); declared 1999.
- **GII 2025**: India **38th of 139** (39th in 2024).
