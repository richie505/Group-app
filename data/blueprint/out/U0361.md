## U0361 | GOVERNANCE AND GOOD GOVERNANCE – MEANING, DIMENSIONS, WORLD BANK/UNDP INDICATORS
### 1. MEANING OF GOVERNANCE – DEFINITIONS BY BODY
#### 1.1 Definitions
| Body | Definition / stress |
|---|---|
| **World Bank** | "The manner in which **power is exercised** in the management of a country's **economic and social resources for development**"; stresses accountability, transparency, rule of law |
| **UNDP (1997)** | Exercise of **economic, political and administrative authority** to manage a country's affairs **at all levels**; good governance is participatory, transparent, accountable, responsive |
| **UNESCAP** | **Efficiency, effectiveness, equity, sustainability** |
- The term **"good governance"** was first brought into development use by the **World Bank** in its **1989 report on Sub-Saharan Africa**.
- **Governance is broader than government**: it involves the **state + market + civil society**.
- **Democracy** helps development by encouraging **citizen participation and accountability**.
!TRAP World Bank 1989 = first "good governance"; UNDP 1997 = later definition of governance.
### 2. FEATURES OF GOOD GOVERNANCE – UN/UNDP
#### 2.1 The 8 Attributes (full list)
1. **Consensus oriented**
2. **Participatory**
3. **Rule of law**
4. **Effective and efficient**
5. **Accountable**
6. **Transparent**
7. **Responsive**
8. **Equitable and inclusive**
- UNDP's **three most critical** features: **transparency, accountability, responsiveness**.
#### 2.2 What is NOT a Feature
- **Religiosity** and **emotionality**.
- **Red tapism**.
- **Administrative centralisation**, secrecy, discretion, majoritarian rule.
!TRAP "Which is NOT a feature" – pick centralisation, secrecy, red tapism, religiosity or emotionality as the odd one out.

| Pillar | Meaning |

### 4. WORLD BANK WORLDWIDE GOVERNANCE INDICATORS (WGI)
- Since **1996**; covers **200+ countries**; **DARPG** is India's nodal department.
#### 4.1 Six Dimensions
| No. | Dimension |
|---|---|
| 1 | Voice and Accountability |
| 2 | Political Stability and Absence of Violence/Terrorism |
| 3 | Government Effectiveness |
| 4 | Regulatory Quality |
| 5 | Rule of Law |
| 6 | Control of Corruption |
- **HDI is NOT** a WGI dimension.
- **India (WGI 2023)**: moderate government effectiveness and rule of law; **low political stability** compared with global averages.
- Criticism: **WGI and V-Dem** are perception-based – they depend on **expert opinion**.
### 5. GOOD GOVERNANCE DAY AND GOOD GOVERNANCE INDEX (DARPG)
#### 5.1 Good Governance Day
- **25 December** – birthday of **Atal Bihari Vajpayee**; announced and first observed in **2014**.
#### 5.2 Good Governance Index (GGI)
- Launched by **DARPG** on **Good Governance Day 2019**; uniform tool to rank **States, UTs and districts**; revised **every two years**.
- **10 sectors**: agriculture and allied; commerce and industry; human resource development; public health; public infrastructure and utilities; economic governance; social welfare and development; judicial and public security; environment; citizen-centric governance.
- **Foreign affairs is NOT** a GGI sector.
- **GGI 2021** (25 Dec 2021; 58 indicators): **Gujarat** 1st in the composite ranking, followed by Maharashtra and Goa; **Andhra Pradesh 1st in agriculture and allied sectors**.
#### 5.3 WGI vs GGI
| Feature | World Bank WGI | DARPG GGI |
|---|---|---|
| Since | 1996 | 2019 |
| Units | 6 dimensions | 10 sectors |
| Coverage | 200+ countries | States, UTs, districts |
!NUM WGI = 6 dimensions (1996); GGI = 10 sectors (2019); UN attributes = 8

### 8. GOVERNANCE INSTITUTES AND DARPG TOOLS
#### 8.1 Centre for Good Governance (CGG), Andhra Pradesh
- Set up by the Government of (undivided) **AP in 2001**, in **Hyderabad**.
- Collaboration: **DFID (UK)** and the **World Bank**.
- Mandate: governance-reform **think tank** – administrative reforms, e-governance, research, training.
#### 8.2 NCGG and DARPG Tools
- **NCGG**: autonomous institute set up by upgrading the **National Institute of Administrative Research (LBSNAA)**; head office **New Delhi**, branch **Mussoorie**.
- DARPG tools: **NeSDA (2019)**, Citizen's Charter portal, **CPGRAMS**, State Collaboration Initiatives scheme (revised 2015-16).
#### 8.3 AP Angle
- **Mana Mitra WhatsApp Governance**: **500+ services**, target 1,000; **Rs 100 crore** for RTGS in the 2026-27 Budget.
!KEY CGG = Hyderabad, 2001, DFID + World Bank; NCGG = New Delhi (branch Mussoorie).
