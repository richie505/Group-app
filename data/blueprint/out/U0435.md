## U0435 | EXPORT CONTROL AND SECURITY REGIMES – NPT, CTBT, NSG, MTCR, WASSENAAR, AUSTRALIA GROUP, FATF, IAEA
### 1. EXPORT CONTROL REGIMES COMPARED
#### 1.1 Year, Members and What is Controlled
| Regime | Year | Members | Controls |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NSG** | **1975** | **48** | Nuclear materials and technology |
| **MTCR** | **1987** | **35** | Missiles and drones – **500 kg payload over 300 km** |
| **Wassenaar Arrangement** | 1996, **Vienna** | **42** | **Conventional arms** and **dual-use goods** |
| **Australia Group** | 1985 | – | **Chemical and biological** weapon precursors |
| **Hague Code of Conduct** | **2002** | – | Transparency on **ballistic-missile** proliferation |
!NUM MTCR = 500 kg / 300 km; NSG = 48; Wassenaar = 42, Vienna
#### 1.2 Outsiders
- **China and Pakistan** are outside both **Wassenaar and MTCR**; **North Korea** is outside MTCR.
- North Korea admitted exporting missiles to **Pakistan and Iran** and asked the US for **compensation** to stop their further spread.

!TRAP India is in MTCR, Wassenaar and Australia Group, but **not in the NSG**.
### 3. NUCLEAR SUPPLIERS GROUP
- Formed **1975**, known as the **"London Club"**, in reply to India's **1974 Pokhran-I** test.
- **Voluntary, consensus-based**; **48 members**; **no permanent secretariat**.
- **2008 India-specific waiver** (India-US Civil Nuclear Deal): allows civil nuclear trade, import of uranium and reactors.
- The waiver does **not** make India an NPT member.
- India's entry is blocked by the **consensus rule**; **Australia backs** India's NSG bid. **Rafael Grossi** chaired the NSG in 2016.
### 4. NPT AND CTBT
#### 4.1 NPT
- **Signed 1968**, **in force 1970**, **extended indefinitely in 1995**.
- Recognises **five Nuclear Weapon States** – those that **developed and tested before 1 January 1967**: **US, Russia, UK, France, China** – the same as the **UNSC P5**.
- Of China, France, India, Israel, Pakistan: **only China and France** are NPT-recognised NWS.
- **North Korea left the NPT in 2003**.
- **Review Conferences** every **5 years**; the consensus rule lets single states block outcomes.
- India rejects the NPT as discriminatory – **"nuclear apartheid"**; wants a **time-bound disarmament plan** before signing.
- Criticism: NPT lets recognised powers modernise without disarmament deadlines.
#### 4.2 CTBT
- Adopted **1996**; bans **all nuclear explosions**; **not in force**.
- India has **not signed**: no time-bound disarmament promise; it still allows **subcritical tests**.
### 5. ARMS-CONTROL TREATIES AND NUCLEAR DEALS
| Treaty | Parties | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **JCPOA (2015**, not 2010) | Iran with P5+1 (US, UK, France, Russia, China, Germany) and EU | **US left 2018**; Iran dropped all limits **5 Jan 2020** |
| **New START** (signed **April 2010**) | **USA and Russia** (not China) | Russia paused it **2023**; its expiry (2026) removed all binding limits |
| **INF Treaty (1987)** | **USA and USSR** | **US left 2019** |
| **CCW (1980)** | UN convention on inhumane conventional weapons | India is party to **all five protocols** |
- **START** = **Strategic Arms Reduction Treaty**. New START is a bilateral treaty limiting **deployed strategic nuclear warheads**; signed by **Barack Obama**; the last nuclear arms-control pact left between the US and Russia.
- **US + Russia** hold about **90%** of global arsenals.
- **Iran:** US and Israel struck its nuclear sites **June 2025**; Iran's Parliament weighed **NPT exit** (Mar 2026).
### 6. IAEA AND INDIA'S SAFEGUARDS
- **IAEA:** HQ **Vienna**, established **1957**; India a **founding member (1957)**; verifies that civil nuclear material is not diverted to weapons.
- DG **Rafael Grossi** voiced worry over strikes on Iran's nuclear sites (Apr 2026).
| Agreement | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| NSG India-specific waiver | **2008** | Civil nuclear trade |
| **India-Specific Safeguards Agreement** | **2009** | Covers **civilian** facilities, excludes military |
| **Additional Protocol** | **in force 2014** | IAEA safeguards |
| India-Canada uranium | 2026 | **10,000 tonnes (2027-2035)** for PHWRs, under IAEA safeguards |
| India-Australia uranium | July 2026 | Administrative Arrangement; Australia holds over **one-third** of world uranium |
!KEY Waiver **2008** → Safeguards Agreement **2009** → Additional Protocol **2014**.
### 7. CHEMICAL AND BIOLOGICAL WEAPONS CONVENTIONS
| Feature | CWC | BWC |
|---|---|---|
| Year | Signed **1993**, in force **1997** | Adopted **1972**, in force **1975** |
| Verification body | **OPCW** | **None** |
| India | Party; declared and **destroyed stockpile by 2009** | Party |
- **OPCW:** HQ **The Hague**; implements the CWC, monitors the chemical industry, inspects destruction; **Nobel Peace Prize 2013**; **not an EU body**.
- OPCW organs: **Conference of States Parties**, **Executive Council**, **Technical Secretariat**.
### 8. FATF
- Set up **1989 by G7**; secretariat at **Paris (OECD)**.
- **Grey list** = jurisdictions under **increased monitoring** (not by itself a call for action).
- **Black list** = **high-risk jurisdictions subject to a call for action** – Iran, North Korea, Myanmar (unchanged at the Oct 2026 plenary).
- Oct 2026: grey list fell from 22 to **18** – DRC, Monaco, Bulgaria, Côte d'Ivoire removed.
- **Pakistan:** grey list **June 2018 – October 2022** for failing its **27-point action plan** on terror financing.
- **India:** member since **2010**; **mutual evaluation adopted June 2024** → **"regular follow-up"**; also in the **APG** (Asia/Pacific Group on Money Laundering, a FATF-style regional body).
### 9. TPNW, FMCT AND NUCLEAR-WEAPON-FREE ZONES
- **TPNW:** first binding ban on nuclear weapons; **in force 2021**; no nuclear-armed or NATO support. **India did not sign** – says it adds nothing to customary international law.
- **FMCT:** **India supports** negotiation at the **Conference on Disarmament** – universal, verifiable, non-discriminatory, not capping existing stocks.
!TRAP "India does not support FMCT negotiations" is **false**.
