## U0069 | TRIPARTITE STRUGGLE FOR KANNAUJ – PALAS, PRATIHARAS AND RASHTRAKUTAS
### 1. WHY KANNAUJ?
- Old names: **Mahodaya**, **Mahodaya Shri** ("Nagar Mahodaya Shri", in Mihira Bhoja's time), Kanyakubja.
- After Harsha, Kannauj = **symbol of imperial sovereignty**: its holder claimed to be **Samrat of North India** (like Magadha in the Gupta period).
- Economic: **Gangetic-Doab** farm wealth; **Arabian Sea trade via Gujarat**, avoiding the Arab-held Indus delta.
- Period: **8th–9th century CE**. The struggle was for Kannauj, **not Ujjain**.
### 2. THE THREE CONTENDERS
| Dynasty | Base | Rulers in the struggle |
|---|---|---|
| **Gurjara-Pratiharas** | Rajasthan-Gujarat / Avanti, later Kannauj | Vatsaraja, **Nagabhata II**, Mihira Bhoja, Mahendrapala I |
| **Palas** | Bengal and Bihar | **Dharmapala**, Devapala, Mahipala I |
| **Rashtrakutas** | Deccan, capital **Manyakheta (Malkhed)** | **Dhruva**, **Govinda III**, Amoghavarsha, **Indra III** |
!TRAP NOT part of it: **Cholas**, Pallavas, Kalyani Chalukyas, Chauhans, Gahadavalas.
- Chronology: Pallava Mahendravarman I → Pala Gopala → Pratihara Bhoja → Chola Parantaka I.
### 3. PHASES AND BATTLES
| Phase | Events |
|---|---|
| I (c. 780–800) | **Vatsaraja** takes Kannauj, defeats Dharmapala; **Dhruva** defeats both, takes loot and returns south |
| II (early 9th c.) | **Dharmapala** removes Pratihara nominee **Indrayudha**, installs puppet **Chakrayudha** |
| III | **Nagabhata II** ousts Chakrayudha and defeats Dharmapala at **Monghyr**; **Govinda III** defeats both, then retreats |
| IV (late 9th c.) | **Mihira Bhoja**, Mahendrapala I hold Kannauj; Himalayas to Narmada |
| c. 916 | **Indra III** sacks Kannauj, defeating Pratihara **Mahipala I** |
!TRAP Indrayudha = removed; Chakrayudha = Dharmapala's puppet. Nagabhata II was a Pratihara, not a Pala.
### 4. RASHTRAKUTAS IN THE NORTH
| Ruler (crossed the Vindhyas) | Defeated |
|---|---|
| **Dhruva** (780–793) | **Vatsaraja** and Dharmapala (not Nagabhata I or II) |
| **Govinda III** (Dhruva's successor) | **Nagabhata II** (and Dharmapala) – Himalayan campaign |
| **Indra III** | Mahipala I (c. 916) |
- AP angle: Dhruva also defeated **Vishnuvardhana IV** of Vengi.
### 5. SOURCES
| Source | Dynasty | Tells us |
|---|---|---|
| **Sanjan plates** (871 CE, Amoghavarsha I) + Radhanpur plates | Rashtrakuta | Govinda III's Himalayan campaign: "his horses drank the icy waters of Himalayan streams" |
| **Gwalior prashasti** (Mihira Bhoja) | Pratihara | Nagabhata II beat Chakrayudha and Dharmapala |
| **Khalimpur plates** (Dharmapala) | Pala | **Election of Gopala** to end **Matsya Nyaya** (law of the jungle); Chakrayudha installed |
- Dharmapala: title **Param Saugat** (devout Buddhist); founded **Vikramashila**.
### 6. OUTCOME
1. Rashtrakuta raids gave loot and prestige, no lasting land.
2. **Pratiharas** finally held Kannauj (Bhoja, Mahendrapala I); no power kept it long.
3. All three exhausted; Pratiharas declined after Indra III's raid.
4. Rise of Rajput states: **Chandelas**, Chauhans – power shifted to regional kingdoms.
| King | Dynasty |
|---|---|
| Nannuka | Chandela (founder) |
| Jayashakti | Chandela (not Paramara) |
| Bhoja (of Dhar) | Paramara (not Rashtrakuta) |
- Chandelas began as Pratihara feudatories (early 9th c.); fought Pratiharas, Palas and Kalachuris of Chedi.

### 9. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS
!BOX VERY IMPORTANT
1. **Cholas** were not a party.
2. Dhruva beat **Vatsaraja**; Govinda III beat **Nagabhata II**.
3. Sanjan plates describe **Govinda III**, not Dhruva.
4. **Sanjan** = Rashtrakuta; **Gwalior** = Pratihara; **Khalimpur** = Pala.
5. Mahodaya = **Kannauj**, not Ujjain.
6. Pratiharas, not Palas, finally held Kannauj.
7. Indra III defeated **Mahipala I** (Pratihara).
8. Bhoja of the pairs list = **Paramara**.
