## U0256 | ANDHRA MOVEMENT, POTTI SRIRAMULU'S FAST AND THE FORMATION OF ANDHRA STATE, 1953
### 1. CAUSES OF THE ANDHRA MOVEMENT
#### 1.1 Grievances in Madras Presidency
- Telugus about **40%** (2 crore) of Madras Presidency (c. 1910), lumped as "Madrasis"; official language of the Presidency = **Tamil**.
| Post | Telugu | Non-Telugu |
|---|---|---|
| Deputy Collectors | 21 | 39 |
| District Munsifs | 30 | 93 |
| High State jobs | 8 | 56 |
| District Judges | 0 | 19 |
- **Education (1915)**: 8 of 31 colleges, 163 of 583 secondary schools; **no engineering, medical or law college** in Andhra.
- **Revenue grievance**: surplus revenue of the Andhra **deltas** allegedly spent on Tamil areas.
- Early demands: separate **province**, **university**, agricultural and medical colleges, a **port at Visakhapatnam**, Telugu in administration.
#### 1.2 Regional enthusiasm
| Region | Stand |
|---|---|
| **Krishna, Guntur** (Circars) | Keenest supporters |
| Rayalaseema, Nellore, Ganjam, Visakhapatnam | Lukewarm; saw a Circars/"Brahmin" movement |
#### 1.3 Bengal and Bihar precedent
- **Partition of Bengal 1905**, annulled 1911; **Bihar** separation announced at the **Delhi Durbar (12 Dec 1911)**; **Bihar and Orissa** province from **1 April 1912** → provinces could be redrawn on language lines.
#### 1.4 Press and Early Writings
| Paper / writing | Founder / editor | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Krishna Patrika** | **Konda Venkatappayya** (with Dasu Narayana Rao), **1 Feb 1902**; later editor **Mutnuri Krishna Rao** | Voice of Telugu districts |
| **Andhra Patrika** | **Kasinathuni Nageswara Rao**, **Bombay, 9 Sep 1908**; daily 1914, moved to Madras | Popularised the word "**Andhra**" |
| **Deshabhimani** (Guntur) | Editorials by **Seshagirirao** | Anglo-Telugu; only daily of British Andhra; "no protection for Telugu culture without its own government" (APPSC 2017); called a political meeting **4 Feb 1910** |
| "**On the present position of the Telugu people**" | **Jonnavittula Gurunatham** | 6 articles in **The Hindu, 1911** |
| Andhra Advocate (weekly) | Vizag district meeting, 1913 | Propaganda |
| Janmabhumi | – | 25 Aug 1920: movement can serve **national unity** |
| Swarajya (Vijayawada) | **Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao** | – |
| Desamata (Rajahmundry) | **Chilakamarti Lakshmi Narasimham** | – |
| Andhra Kesari (1904, Rajahmundry) | **Chilukuri Veerabhadra Rao** | Also wrote **Andhrula Charitra** |
- Andhra Patrika writer: Tamils separating from Telugus would **not harm national unity**.
- **Kavitva Tatva Vicharamu** = C.R. Reddy.

#### 2.2 Bapatla 1913 – basic facts
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|

### 5. CONGRESS AND THE LINGUISTIC PRINCIPLE
#### 5.1 Andhras in the early Congress
- **P. Anandacharlu** – **first Telugu INC President**, **Nagpur 1891**; **Pattabhi Sitaramayya** – **Jaipur 1948** (lost **Tripuri 1939** to Bose); N. Sanjiva Reddy – 1960-62.
- First annual INC session in Andhra: **Kakinada 1923** – **Maulana Mohammed Ali**; AICC at Bezawada 1921 (Tilak Swaraj Fund).
- 1914 Madras Congress: **256** Andhra delegates, none on the Working Committee; 1915 provincial conference refused; **Lucknow 1916** no decision (Pattabhi pushed it with Tilak's support).
#### 5.2 Andhra Congress Committee, 1918
- Approved at **Calcutta 1917** with **Tilak's** support; formed **22 Jan 1918**.
- First president **Nyapati Subba Rao**; first secretary **Konda Venkatappayya**.
#### 5.3 Nagpur 1920, Nehru Report and after
- **Nagpur 1920** (C. Vijayaraghavachariar): **PCCs reorganised on linguistic lines**; goal Swaraj by peaceful and legitimate means.
- **Nehru Report 1928** – linguistic provinces.
- 1938 Wardha CWC – assured Andhra, Karnataka, Kerala deputation of linguistic redistribution; **1945-46 election manifesto** – units on a linguistic and cultural basis.
- Congress in office in Madras took up the demand from **1946**; **1948** – Congress rejected linguistic reorganisation for the time being.
#### 5.4 Resolutions, Deputations and Opponents
| Year | Step |
|---|---|
| 1917 | **B.N. Sarma** moved the separate-province resolution |
| **20 Dec 1917** | **Nyapati Subba Rao's** delegation met **Montagu**, Madras |

| July 1918 | **Montagu-Chelmsford Report** – no Andhra province |

### 6. ANDHRA UNIVERSITY AND TELUGU IDENTITY
| Step | Date / fact |
|---|---|

### 9. COMMISSIONS AND COMMITTEES AFTER 1947
- **27 Nov 1947**: Nehru backed linguistic states in the Assembly; Andhra dropped from the **Draft Constitution, 21 Feb 1948** (Tamil, Kannada objections); Centre slow, fearing for unity (Partition, Kashmir, princely states).
| Body | Appointed by / date | Members | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dhar (Dar) Commission** (Linguistic Provinces Commission) | **President of the Constituent Assembly**, **17 June 1948** | **Justice S.K. Dhar**; **Dr. Panna Lal, Jagat Narayan Lal** | Report **10 Dec 1948**: **administrative convenience, not language**; linguistic states a **threat to national unity** |
| **JVP Committee** | **Congress, Jaipur session, Dec 1948** | **Nehru, Patel, Pattabhi Sitaramayya** | Report **April 1949**: postpone; **Andhra if Madras claim given up**; "if public opinion is insistent and overwhelming, we as democrats have to submit" |
| **Partition Committee** | Madras govt, **Dec 1949** | Chair **Kumaraswamy Raja**; Andhra: Prakasam, Kala Venkatrao, B. Gopala Reddy, N. Sanjiva Reddy | Divide assets and liabilities; report **4 Jan 1950**; state by **26 Jan 1950** – failed over Madras |
| **Wanchoo inquiry** | Centre, **7 Feb 1953** | **Justice K.N. Wanchoo** (CJ, Rajasthan HC) | Implications of Andhra State |
- Nehru promised a committee on his **1948 Visakhapatnam** visit.
- **CWC**: Andhra without Madras city.
!TRAP **N. Sanjiva Reddy was not a JVP member** (APPSC 2021). Dhar = appointed by Constituent Assembly; JVP = by Congress.
### 10. FASTS FOR ANDHRA STATE
#### 10.1 Swami Sitaram, 1951
- **Gollapudi Sitarama Sastri**, a Gandhian; fast **15 Aug-20 Sep 1951, 35 days**.
- Demands: Andhra State, Madras city's status, interim arrangement (not Telangana merger).
- **Ended on the appeal of Vinoba Bhave**; no gain.
- Context: April 1951 APCC election – **Sanjiva Reddy beat N.G. Ranga by 5 votes** → Prakasam and Ranga formed the **Kisan Mazdoor Praja Party**.
#### 10.2 Potti Sriramulu – life
- Born **16 March 1901, Madras**; Arya Vysya; ancestral village **Gajula Dinne, Nellore**.
- About **3 years at Sabarmati Ashram**; Salt Satyagraha, Quit India; khadi, prohibition, **Harijan uplift**.
| Earlier fast | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 1946, Nellore Venugopala Swamy temple | Harijan entry |
| 1948, **Wardha, 29 days** | Harijan Day, ₹1 crore; Madras Harijan Welfare Day on the **30th** of every month |
| 1949, 28 days | Gandhi Smarak Din |
- Fasted three times (1946-48) in Nellore for Harijan temple entry; **1952 = only fast for statehood**.
#### 10.3 The death fast, 1952
| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Fast began | **19 Oct 1952**, Fort St. George gate, then **Bulusu Sambamurthy's house, Mylapore, Madras** |
| Death | **15 Dec 1952**, **58th day** → **Amarajeevi** ("Andhra Martyra Martanda") |
| Nehru's announcement in Parliament | **19 Dec 1952** – Andhra from undisputed Telugu areas, **excluding Madras city** |
- Scope: Telugu districts of **Coastal Andhra and Rayalaseema** (APPSC 2019).
- Conditions: immediate announcement, unity of Andhra leaders, Madras a Chief Commissioner's State.
- Aftermath: riots, police firing – **seven** died; Tenneti Viswanatham resigned.
- Commemoration: **Sri Potti Sriramulu Nellore District (2008**, Y.S. Rajasekhara Reddy); Potti Sreeramulu Telugu University; **58-ft "Statue of Sacrifice"**, Amaravati – unveiled **16 Mar 2026** (125th birth anniversary) by CM Chandrababu Naidu.
!NUM 35 days = Swami Sitaram (1951, ended); 58 days = Potti Sriramulu (1952, died)
### 11. ANDHRA STATE, 1 OCTOBER 1953
#### 11.1 Act and formation
- Nehru, **25 Mar 1953**: "no question" of Madras as capital. Bill introduced **10 Aug 1953**; assent **14 Sept 1953**.
- **1 October 1953** – **first linguistic state**, carved from Madras State; capital **Kurnool**, **inaugurated by Nehru**.
- **11 districts**: Coastal (7) Srikakulam, Visakhapatnam, East and West Godavari, Krishna, Guntur, Nellore; Rayalaseema (4) Chittoor, Kadapa, Anantapur, Kurnool. (No Vizianagaram, Prakasam districts.)
- **Bellary** (Justice L.S. Misra): Bellary taluk → Mysore; **Rayadurg → Anantapur**; **Adoni, Alur → Kurnool**.
#### 11.2 Capital debate
| Site | Backed by |
|---|---|
| **Kurnool** | Congress, Prakasam's group, Rayalaseema leaders (Sri Bagh) |
| **Tirupati** | **Krishikar Lok Party** (N.G. Ranga) |
| Vijayawada / Guntur | **Communists** (P. Sundarayya) |
- Madras Assembly vote: **Kurnool 79 – Vijayawada 53** (five non-Andhra votes; Prakasam did not vote).
#### 11.3 First government and High Court
| Office | Andhra State | Andhra Pradesh (1956) |
|---|---|---|
| CM | **T. Prakasam** (1 Oct 1953); **Bezawada Gopala Reddy** (1955-56, last) | **N. Sanjiva Reddy** |
| Deputy CM | **N. Sanjiva Reddy** | **K.V. Ranga Reddy** |
| Speaker | **Nallapati Venkatramaiah** | **Ayyadevara Kaleswara Rao** |
| Governor | **C.M. Trivedi** (only Governor) | C.M. Trivedi (1 Nov 1956); 2nd **Bhimsen Sachar** |
| High Court | **Guntur, 5 July 1954**; CJ **Koka Subba Rao** | – |
- Budget **1955-56** – **Bezawada Gopala Reddy** (APPSC GS 2021); Assembly = 1952 Madras Assembly members; **no Legislative Council** (AP bicameral 1958).
- Prakasam's ministry fell **15 Nov 1954** (no-confidence; prohibition row) → President's rule till **28 Mar 1955**.
- **1955 mid-term poll (11 Feb 1955)**: United Congress front (Congress + Krishikar Lok Party + PSP) vs Communists; Congress **119** seats, CPI 15 → Gopala Reddy CM.
- Hyderabad State: **B. Ramakrishna Rao** – first elected CM (1952); **Govinda Rao Nanal** – first Hyderabad State Congress president; **Mulla Abdul Qayyum** – first Hyderabad Muslim to join INC.
