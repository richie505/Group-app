## U0805 | RECENT TRENDS IN INDIA'S DISEASE BURDEN – COMMUNICABLE AND NON-COMMUNICABLE DISEASES
### 1. BURDEN OF DISEASE – KEY TERMS
#### 1.1 Dual and Triple Burden
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Dual burden of disease** | **Rising rates of both infectious and non-communicable diseases**; lasting communicable diseases (TB, malaria, dengue, diarrhoeal diseases) exist alongside rising NCDs (diabetes, cardiovascular disease) |
| **Triple burden** | Adds **injuries** (road accidents, trauma) to CDs + NCDs |
| **Dual burden of malnutrition** | **Undernutrition + obesity** (adult overweight) |
| **DALY** | **YLL** (years of life lost to premature death) + **YLD** (years lived with disability) |
- DALY does **not** add years gained through treatment.
!KEY Dual burden = CDs + NCDs; add injuries = triple burden; undernutrition + obesity = dual burden of malnutrition.
#### 1.2 Who Measures the Burden
- **India State-Level Disease Burden Initiative** = **ICMR + PHFI + IHME**; part of the **Global Burden of Disease (GBD)** study.
### 2. EPIDEMIOLOGICAL TRANSITION
#### 2.1 Omran's Three Stages
| Stage | Feature |
|---|---|
| 1. **Age of Pestilence and Famine** (first) | High deaths from infections and famine |
| 2. **Age of Receding Pandemics** | Infectious deaths fall as sanitation and vaccination improve |
| 3. **Age of Degenerative and Man-Made Diseases** | NCDs dominate |
- India's path: a "**linear shift**" from infectious diseases to NCDs.
#### 2.2 State-wise Transition
- The "**Nations within a nation**" study (Lancet, 2017; data 1990–2016) placed states in **4 Epidemiological Transition Level (ETL) groups**: low, lower-middle, higher-middle and high.
- ETL is based on the ratio of communicable-disease DALYs to NCD + injury DALYs; a **low ratio = high ETL** (more advanced).
- India as a whole: NCD + injury DALYs overtook communicable DALYs in **2003**; the gap between state groups was **24 years**.
- **High ETL**: **Kerala, Goa, Tamil Nadu, Punjab**. Karnataka and Delhi are **higher-middle**.
- **EAG and North-East** states still carry a much higher infectious-disease burden.
!TRAP High ETL means the transition is MORE advanced (NCDs dominate), not a higher infectious burden.
### 3. KEY DATA – MORTALITY AND HEALTH GAINS
| Indicator | Fact |
|---|---|
| NCD share of all deaths in India | **57%** (Economic Survey 2025-26) |
| Leading cause of death in India | **Cardiovascular diseases** |
| World's single biggest killer (WHO) | **Ischaemic heart disease** |
| Life expectancy | **49.7** (1973) → **70.3 years** (2023) |
| MMR since 1990 | Fallen **86%** |
| Under-five mortality since 1990 | Fallen **78%** |
- Most countries cut NCD deaths in 2001–2019, but India's probability of dying from an NCD **rose** – especially **women over 40** and **men over 55** (Lancet).
!NUM 57% = NCD share of deaths; 86% = MMR fall; 78% = U5MR fall; 70.3 = life expectancy 2023.
### 4. COMMUNICABLE VS LIFESTYLE DISEASES
#### 4.1 Classification
| Communicable (pathogen-caused) | Lifestyle / NCD |
|---|---|
| Malaria, dengue, TB, typhoid, cholera, chikungunya, JE, Nipah | Diabetes, hypertension, obesity, heart disease, stroke, cancer, COPD |
- **Malaria** is communicable (Plasmodium, spread by mosquitoes) – **NOT a lifestyle disease** (odd-one-out PYQ).
- **Coronary artery disease** – result of **plaque buildup** in the arteries.
- **"Big four" NCDs**: **cardiovascular diseases, cancers, chronic respiratory diseases, diabetes**.
#### 4.2 Vectors and Spread
| Disease | Vector / route |
|---|---|
| Dengue, chikungunya, Zika | **Aedes** |
| Japanese encephalitis | **Culex** |
| Meningococcal meningitis | **Person to person by respiratory droplets**, not mosquitoes; vomiting, neck stiffness |
### 5. EMERGING AND RE-EMERGING INFECTIONS
- TB, dengue, Nipah, Zika and avian influenza persist alongside NCDs – the core of the dual burden.
- ICMR initiatives: **India TB Research Consortium**; **MERA** (malaria elimination).
- **Aug 2026**: H1N1 spike reported.
- **2026**: ICMR **wastewater surveillance** expanded from **5 to 50 cities**; tracks **10 viruses** including avian influenza, plus AMR.
- Vaccination registration platform: **U-WIN**.
### 6. LIFESTYLE RISK FACTORS AND FATS
#### 6.1 Risk Factors
- Main NCD risk factors: **unhealthy diet, physical inactivity, tobacco, alcohol**; also urbanisation and ageing.
- **Trans fats**: bakery products made with **hydrogenated fats** contain trans fats; excess trans fats raise NCD risk – both statements correct (PYQ).
- **Obesity**: excess energy intake + low physical activity → obesity and heart disease; sedentary life + energy-dense food → degenerative diseases – both correct (PYQ).
- Overweight/obese (**NFHS-5**, 2019-21): **24% women, 23% men**.
- **Blood pressure generally increases with age.**
#### 6.2 Good and Bad Fats
- **Trans fat** raises **LDL** and **lowers HDL** ("good cholesterol"); high HDL reduces heart-disease risk.
- **FSSAI** caps trans fat at **2%** in fats and oils.
- Excess **saturated fat** raises blood lipids and cholesterol.
- Cook in **oil rather than vanaspati ghee**: oils hold unsaturated fats (mono- and polyunsaturated) that lower cholesterol and CVD risk.
- **Sunflower oil**: suits heart patients; low saturated fat; more **vitamin E** than any other vegetable oil.
- **Eskimos (Inuit)**: little heart disease despite a high meat/fat diet – rich in **omega-3** from fish and marine mammals.
- **Linseed/flaxseed**: richest source of **alpha-linolenic acid (omega-3)**; stops fat buildup in blood vessels (also canola, soyabean, walnut oil).
#### 6.3 Fatty Acids and Sources
| Fatty acid | Source |
|---|---|
| **α-Linolenic (omega-3)** | **Linseed oil** |
| Linoleic | Peanut oil, chicken fat, olive oil |
| Arachidonic | Liver, brain, glandular organs |

| Palmitoleic | Macadamia nuts |

#### 7.2 Cancer
- Cases: **1.41 million (2022)** → projected **2.46 million (2045)**.
- Cancer is **not nationally notifiable**; **17 States** have notified it, **Telangana** the most recent (Jul 2026).
### 8. MENTAL HEALTH AND ROAD INJURIES
- **Mental disorders** (GBD, 2017): **197.3 million** Indians – about **1 in 7**; most common: **depressive (45.7 mn)** and **anxiety disorders (44.9 mn)**.
- **Tele-MANAS**: 24x7 tele-mental-health support in **all 36 States/UTs** (Jun 2026).
- **Road injuries** (MoRTH, 2022): **4,61,312 accidents**, **1,68,491 deaths**; two-wheeler users the largest group of victims.
- **SDG 3** target: **halve road-traffic deaths**.
### 9. PROGRAMMES, DECLARATIONS AND TARGETS
| Programme / declaration | Provision |
|---|---|
| **NP-NCD** (formerly **NPCDCS, 2010**) | NCD clinics at district hospitals and CHCs; **everyone above 30** screened; funding **60:40** (90:10 NE/hilly states) |
| **Ayushman Arogya Mandirs** | Screen hypertension, diabetes, oral/breast/cervical cancer |
| **SDG 3** | Cut premature NCD deaths by **one-third**; promote mental health; halve road deaths |
| **UNGA declaration on NCDs and mental health** | 2030 targets; oral health, lung health, childhood cancer; air pollution, lead; first time **digital harms**; stronger regulation of **tobacco, trans fats, e-cigarettes** |
| **FSSAI** | Trans fat ≤ 2% |
- Growing NCD burden → health system must shift from **curative care to screening and prevention**.
