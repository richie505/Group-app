## U0161 | SECOND CARNATIC WAR – ANWARUDDIN, CHANDA SAHIB, MUHAMMAD ALI; ROBERT CLIVE AT ARCOT
### 1. TRIGGER AND RIVAL CLAIMANTS
- **Second Carnatic War (1749-54)**: fought over **Indian succession disputes**, not a European war. Backing Indian claimants brought territory and revenue.
- Trigger: **death of Nizam-ul-Mulk (1748)** + **release of Chanda Sahib** (son-in-law of Dost Ali) from **Maratha** captivity.
| Throne | French-backed | English-backed |
|---|---|---|
| **Hyderabad** (Deccan subahdari) | **Muzaffar Jang** (Nizam-ul-Mulk's daughter's son) | **Nasir Jang** (Nizam-ul-Mulk's son) |
| **Carnatic** (Arcot) | **Chanda Sahib** | **Anwaruddin**, then his son **Muhammad Ali** |
!TRAP Muzaffar Jang = **grandson** (daughter's son); Nasir Jang = **son** of Nizam-ul-Mulk.
### 2. BATTLE OF AMBUR (1749)
- **First major battle** of the Second War: **Muzaffar Jang + Chanda Sahib + the French (Dupleix)** vs **Anwaruddin**.
- Anwaruddin **killed**; **Chanda Sahib** became Nawab of the Carnatic; Muzaffar Jang claimed the Deccan; **Muhammad Ali fled to Trichinopoly** (Tiruchirappalli).
- Ambur = **1749**, not 1752 (CGPSC 2023).
### 3. EVENTS IN THE DECCAN
| Event | Year | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Nasir Jang captures Muzaffar Jang | – | English-backed side ahead first |
| **Murder of Nasir Jang** | – | Killed by **Himmat Khan**, Nawab of **Kurnool**, in a **Dupleix plot**; Muzaffar Jang freed → Subahdar |
| **Rayachoti** | **1751** | Muzaffar Jang killed by the Nawabs of **Kadapa, Kurnool, Nayanur**; **Bussy** at once installed **Salabat Jang** (Nizam-ul-Mulk's **4th son**) |
### 4. REWARDS TO DUPLEIX AND THE ANDHRA CONNECTION
- **Muzaffar Jang**, as Nizam, made **Dupleix Governor (Nawab) of Mughal territory south of the Krishna** (APPSC 2021).
- **Jagir: Valapadu**; near Pondicherry: **Villianur, Bahur**.
- **Bussy** stayed at Hyderabad and got the **Northern Circars (1753)** from **Salabat Jang** for the upkeep of French troops.
- AP angle: the Andhra coast – **Machilipatnam, Diviseema, Northern Circars** – was the **main French territorial gain**.
!KEY Dupleix's title = governor **south of the Krishna** (APPSC 2021).
### 5. ROBERT CLIVE AT ARCOT
- Chanda Sahib and the French were besieging **Muhammad Ali at Trichinopoly**.
- **Clive** proposed a **diversionary attack on Arcot**, the Carnatic capital.
- **1751**: Clive took Arcot with about **210 men** and held it about **53 days** against **Raja Sahib** (Chanda Sahib's son).
- Clive then beat Raja Sahib at **Arni** and **Kaveripak**.
- **June 1752**: **Clive and Stringer Lawrence** relieved Trichinopoly; French commander **Law surrendered**; **Chanda Sahib** surrendered and was **executed**; **Muhammad Ali** became Nawab.
- English allies: **Morari Rao** (Maratha chief), **Mysore**, **Tanjore**.
!NUM Arcot 1751: about 210 men, about 53 days
### 6. DUPLEIX'S RECALL (1754)
- Reasons: French Company alarmed at the **heavy cost** and failures of interference, mainly the **defeat at Trichinopoly**.
- Successor: **Godeheu** (1754), who made peace with the English.
- Dupleix **won the Deccan** (Hyderabad) but **lost the Carnatic** (Arcot, Trichinopoly).
- Dupleix = **first European** to join Indian princes' quarrels for territory and to use a European army at Indian courts at Indian kings' cost; the English later copied these tactics.
### 7. TREATY OF PONDICHERRY (1754-55)
- Signed by **Godeheu** with English Governor **Thomas Saunders** (December 1754).
- Both Companies agreed **not to interfere** in the internal quarrels of Indian rulers.
- **Muhammad Ali** recognised as Nawab of the Carnatic.
