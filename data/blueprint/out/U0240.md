## U0240 | GANDHI-IRWIN PACT, ROUND TABLE CONFERENCES, COMMUNAL AWARD, POONA PACT; RESUMPTION AND WITHDRAWAL
### 1. BACKGROUND – FROM DANDI TO THE FIRST RTC
- **CDM** began with the **Dandi March (12 March – 6 April 1930)**; Viceroy **Lord Irwin**.
- The **Simon Commission** (arrived 3 Feb 1928) and the Lahore **Purna Swaraj** (Dec 1929) set the stage; the British offered **Round Table Conferences** in **London** to frame a new constitution.
- Lahore (1929) had already said there was **no benefit from the RTC**, so Congress stayed away from the first one.
!KEY Viceroy sequence: Irwin (1926-31) – Pact and first phase; Willingdon (1931-36) – repression of the second phase.
### 2. FIRST ROUND TABLE CONFERENCE (1930-31)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Dates | **12 November 1930 – 19 January 1931** |
| Venue | **London**; inaugurated by **King George V** |
| Chairman | British PM **Ramsay MacDonald** |
| Agenda | Constitutional reform for India (Simon Commission report) |
| Congress | **Boycotted** – CDM in progress, leaders in jail |
| Who attended | Princes, Muslim League, Liberals, Hindu Mahasabha, depressed classes (Ambedkar) and other groups |
| Outcome | Little result without Congress; British wanted Congress at the next round |
!TRAP The First RTC (1930) was held WITHOUT Congress; Gandhi attended only the Second.
### 3. GANDHI-IRWIN PACT (DELHI PACT), 1931
#### 3.1 Date and Mediators
- Signed **5 March 1931** at Delhi (not March 1930); the CWC authorised Gandhi.
- **Mediators: Tej Bahadur Sapru and M.R. Jayakar** (also V.S. Srinivasa Sastri).
- Sarojini Naidu called Gandhi and Irwin the **"Two Mahatmas"**.
#### 3.2 Terms
| Irwin agreed | Irwin refused | Gandhi / Congress agreed |
|---|---|---|
| **Release of non-violent political prisoners** | **Public inquiry into police excesses** | **Suspend the CDM** |
| Coastal villagers may **make salt for own use** | **Commuting death sentences of Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Rajguru** | **Attend the 2nd RTC** |
| Withdraw ordinances; remit fines; return unsold confiscated lands | – | – |
#### 3.3 Criticism
- **No commutation** for Bhagat Singh and comrades (executed **23 March 1931**).
- **No police inquiry**; youth disillusioned.
- Irwin's biographer **Alan Campbell Johnson** called Gandhi's gains **"Consolation Prizes"**.
- Yet the Pact was **ratified at Karachi** and Congress was treated as an equal party for the first time.
!TRAP Gandhi-Irwin Pact = 1931. Dating it to March 1930 is WRONG.
### 4. KARACHI SESSION, MARCH 1931
#### 4.1 Session
- Dates: **29-31 March 1931**; **President: Vallabhbhai Patel**.
- Held **days after the execution of Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev and Rajguru** – Congress **admired their bravery but rejected political violence**.
- **Endorsed** (did not oppose) the **Gandhi-Irwin (Delhi) Pact**; **reiterated Purna Swaraj** (= Complete Independence); chose Gandhi for the 2nd RTC.
#### 4.2 Resolution on Fundamental Rights and National Economic Programme
- **Drafted by Jawaharlal Nehru** (with M.N. Roy's help); passed at the session Patel presided.
| Fundamental Rights (political) | Economic Programme |
|---|---|
| **Civil liberties** – free speech, press, assembly | **Cuts in rent and revenue** |
| Equality before law | **Debt relief** for peasants |
| **Universal adult franchise** | **State control of key industries** |
| **Religious neutrality** of the State | **Labour welfare**, worker protection |
| **Free primary education** | Economic justice as a duty of the State |
- It **foreshadowed Part III (Fundamental Rights) and Part IV (DPSP)** of the Constitution.
!PAIR Karachi 1931 | President | Vallabhbhai Patel || Karachi 1931 | Drafter | Jawaharlal Nehru
### 5. SECOND ROUND TABLE CONFERENCE (1931)
#### 5.1 Facts
- **London, 7 September – 1 December 1931**; held in the backdrop of the Gandhi-Irwin Pact.
- **Gandhi = sole Congress representative**; the **only RTC he attended** (1931, not 1932).
#### 5.2 Participants
| Participant | Represented / role |
|---|---|
| **Gandhi** | Sole Congress representative |
| **Sarojini Naidu** | **Indian women** |
| **Muhammad Iqbal** | Muslim delegate |
| **Madan Mohan Malaviya** | Attended |
| **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar** | Depressed classes |
| **M.A. Jinnah, Aga Khan** | Muslims |
| **V.V. Jogayya Pantulu** | Andhra – went to assist Gandhi; handed him the **separate-Andhra resolution** |
| **Ramsay MacDonald** | British PM; on deadlock threatened a **unilateral award** |
- Non-participant: **Jawaharlal Nehru** did not go – Gandhi alone represented Congress.
#### 5.3 Deadlock
- Failed over **separate electorates for minorities and depressed classes**.
- MacDonald threatened a **one-sided award** → the **Communal Award (1932)**.
- **AP angle:** Andhra leaders used RTC-2 to press for a **separate Andhra province**.
### 6. RESUMPTION OF CDM AND REPRESSION, 1932-33
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **Jan 1932** | Gandhi returned **empty-handed** from RTC-2; Viceroy **Lord Willingdon struck first**: Gandhi arrested, **Congress banned**, ordinances, harsh repression |
| **15 Jan 1932** | **Firing on satyagrahis at Berhampur** |
| 1932 | Second phase of CDM (1932-34) – failed under repression |
| **8 May 1933** | **Gandhi released** |
| **14 July 1933** | **Mass CDM suspended** → **individual satyagraha** instead |
| **April 1934** | **CDM formally withdrawn** |
### 7. COMMUNAL AWARD AND POONA PACT, 1932
#### 7.1 Communal Award
- Announced **16 August 1932** by British PM **Ramsay MacDonald**.
- Gave **separate electorates** to Muslims, Sikhs, Christians and others, and also to the **depressed classes**.
#### 7.2 Gandhi's Yerwada Fast
- Gandhi, in **Yerwada Jail, Poona**, opposed separate electorates for depressed classes (it would split Hindu society).
- **Fast unto death began 20 September 1932**; broken after the Pact (26 Sept 1932).
#### 7.3 Poona Pact
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Date | **24 September 1932** |
| Place | **Yerwada Jail, Poona** |
| Signed by | **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar** (depressed classes) and **Madan Mohan Malaviya** (caste Hindus); Gandhi himself did not sign |
| Main change | **Separate electorates dropped** → **reserved seats in joint electorates** |
| Seats for depressed classes | **148** in provincial legislatures (vs **71** under the Award) |
| Legacy | Root of **reserved seats – Articles 330 and 332** |
- **Harijan Sevak Sangh** (first named Anti-Untouchability League) founded **30 September 1932**; first president **G.D. Birla**.
!NUM 71 seats under the Communal Award → 148 seats under the Poona Pact
!TRAP Poona Pact signatories = Ambedkar and Malaviya, not Gandhi and Ambedkar.
### 8. THIRD ROUND TABLE CONFERENCE AND OUTCOME
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Dates | **17 November – 24 December 1932** |
| Congress | **Did not attend**; Gandhi absent |
| Attendance | Poor; Labour Party also stayed away |
| Outcome | **White Paper (March 1933)** → Joint Select Committee → **Government of India Act, 1935** |
#### 8.1 All Three RTCs Compared
| RTC | Year | Congress | Key point |
|---|---|---|---|
| First | 1930-31 | Boycotted | Opened by King George V |
| Second | 1931 | **Gandhi only** | Deadlock on minorities; Sarojini Naidu, Iqbal attended |
| Third | 1932 | Absent | Led to White Paper and 1935 Act |
### 9. WITHDRAWAL OF CDM AND COUNCIL-ENTRY REVIVAL
#### 9.1 Why the CDM Ended
1. **RTC-2 deadlock**.
2. Gandhi arrested; **ordinances and harsh repression** under Willingdon.
3. **Mass movement suspended (14 July 1933)** – individual satyagraha.
4. **Formally withdrawn (April 1934)** → the **Swarajist line revived**.
#### 9.2 Council Entry Revived
- In **1934** Congress returned to council entry and **contested the Central Assembly elections**.
- Gandhi turned to constructive work, especially **"Service to Harijans"**, his top priority.
!KEY CDM: suspended 14 July 1933 → withdrawn April 1934 → 1934 Central Assembly elections.
#### 9.3 Two Phases of the CDM Compared
| Point | First phase (1930-31) | Second phase (1932-34) |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Dandi March, 12 March 1930 | January 1932, after RTC-2 |
| Viceroy | Lord Irwin | Lord Willingdon |
| Government response | Arrests, but talks followed | Struck first: Congress banned, ordinances |
| End | Gandhi-Irwin Pact, 5 March 1931 | Suspended July 1933; withdrawn April 1934 |
| Result | Congress invited to the 2nd RTC | Movement crushed; council entry revived |
#### 9.4 Significance
- The Pact and the 2nd RTC showed the British dealing with Congress **as an equal party**.
- The Karachi resolution gave the movement a **social and economic programme** for the first time.
- The Poona Pact settled the depressed classes' representation through **joint electorates with reserved seats**, a system later carried into the Constitution.
- The failure of the second phase pushed Congress back to **legislatures (1934)**.

### 13. HIGH-PROBABILITY MCQ TRAPS

2. **Karachi: Patel presided, Nehru drafted** – not the reverse.
3. Gandhi-Irwin Pact = **5 March 1931**; mediators **Sapru and Jayakar**.
4. Irwin **refused** to commute Bhagat Singh's sentence and refused a police inquiry.
5. **Sarojini Naidu** at RTC-2 represented **Indian women**.
6. Communal Award (**16 Aug 1932**) came before the Poona Pact (**24 Sept 1932**).
7. Poona Pact signed by **Ambedkar and Malaviya**; 148 seats; root of **Arts. 330, 332**.
8. Repression Viceroy = **Willingdon**, not Irwin.
9. Mass CDM suspended **14 July 1933**; formally withdrawn **April 1934**.
10. Karachi resolution foreshadowed **Parts III and IV** of the Constitution.
