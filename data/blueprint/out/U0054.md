## U0054 | IKSHVAKU ART, ARCHITECTURE AND LITERATURE
### 1. NAGARJUNAKONDA SCULPTURE – AMARAVATI SCHOOL
- Nagarjunakonda = **main centre** of the **Amaravati School**; its **final, most mature phase** with **Mahayana** themes – Buddha's life and **Jataka** stories.
- Early Ikshvaku carving (less stylisation, **low relief**) = the "**last flicker**" of the Satavahana Amaravati tradition.
| Sculpture / feature | Significance |
|---|---|
| **Mandhata** (Chakravarti Mandhata), **Jaggayyapeta** | Most important Ikshvaku sculpture; **first depiction of a Chakravarti** |
| **Sati panel** | **First depiction of sati** in Indian art |
| Five **Aparamahavinaseliya** sculptures | Buddhist sculptures of that sect |
| **Sculptors' names carved** | Start of the "**Sculptor's Style**" – e.g. **Badantacharya** at the Mahavihara |
### 2. STUPA AND TEMPLE PLANS
| Stupa type | Built over |
|---|---|
| **Dhatugarbha** | **Bodily relics** (e.g. the Maha Chaitya) |
| **Paribhogika** | **Objects used by monks** (alms bowls) |
| **Uddeshika** | **Nothing**; purely commemorative |
- Major stupas: **wheel-shaped (spoked) plan** + **ayaka platforms** in the four directions, **five pillars** each. Ayaka platforms belong to **Buddhist stupas**.
- Temples follow the **Agamic** plan: **garbhagudi, antarala, mantapa, dhvajastambha, prakara**.
- Ikshvakus laid the base of **Dravida temple architecture**; built the **first structural Hindu temples**.
### 3. SECULAR STRUCTURES AT VIJAYAPURI
| Structure | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Rajaprasadam** and prakaras | Palace complex; compound walls |
| **Kreeda Vedi** (amphitheatre) | Sports arena, thought built with **Roman help**; **no parallel elsewhere in India** |
| **Acoustic building** | Built on rules of sound; the site's **most unique find** |
| **Kummakundam** | Water tanks / bathing pools |
| **Yeleswaram** | Town credited to commander **Elisri**, who also built the Sarvadevalaya |
### 4. LANGUAGE OF INSCRIPTIONS
- Early inscriptions in **Prakrit** – the court language (G2 key).
- **Ehuvala Santamula** brought **Sanskrit** into inscriptions; Sanskrit replaced Prakrit from the **4th c. CE**.
| Inscription | Language |
|---|---|
| **Swarna Devalayam** (Golden Temple), Nagarjunakonda, his **11th** regnal year | **First Sanskrit inscription of Andhra and South India** |
| **Elisri's Sarvadeva** inscription | Sanskrit |
| Ehuvala's **Gummadidurru** record (Guntur) | **Prakrit** |
- Telugu words: **Bapi, Remmanaka, Adavi, Ehuvala, Anikki** (not Dhanyakataka).

### 7. NAGARJUNA SAGAR PROJECT AND RELOCATION
- **Nehru** laid the foundation stone on **10 December 1955** (G2 2012); valley submerged after **1960**.
- **Salvage archaeology**: ASI dismantled and rebuilt monuments on higher ground at **Anupu** (Krishna bank) and **Nagarjunakonda island** – "**transplanted monuments**".
- Nagarjunakonda has **India's only island museum**.
- **2 Apr 2026**: Palnadu Collector reviewed the museum and **Swadesh Darshan** works.
!TRAP Discovered 1926 (Ranganatha Saraswati) ≠ Nagarjuna Sagar foundation 1955.

#### FIRST / LAST / SPECIAL FACTS
- First Sanskrit inscription in South India; first structural Hindu temples; first sculptor signatures – all **Ikshvaku**.
- **Amaravati** = largest stupa in AP (G2 2019); **Bhattiprolu** = oldest stupa in Andhra.
