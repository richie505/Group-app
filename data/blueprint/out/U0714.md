## U0714 | E-GOVERNANCE PROGRAMMES AND SERVICES – UMANG, DIGILOCKER, E-SIGN, E-HOSPITAL, CSCs, AP INITIATIVES
### 1. E-GOVERNANCE CONCEPTS
#### 1.1 Gartner Four-Phase Model
1. **Information** – putting information online.
2. **Interaction** – two-way communication.
3. **Transaction** – complete services online.
4. **Transformation** – the **final** phase; government processes redesigned.
#### 1.2 Models
| Model | Example |
|---|---|
| G2C / G2B / G2G | Services through **SDC + SWAN + CSC** |
| G2E | **CEMS** (employee management) |
| **G2P** | **DBT** (LPG subsidy, pensions) |
| Open Government Data | **data.gov.in** |
- Role of technology in governance: **enhance citizen engagement and improve service delivery** (APPSC 2025); brings transparency, reliability and efficiency.
- Digital India vision area: digital infrastructure as a **core utility**.
### 2. NeGP 2006 AND E-KRANTI
#### 2.1 National e-Governance Plan
- Framed by **DeitY + DARPG**; approved **18 May 2006**.
- **27 MMPs** (**9 Central, 11 State, 7 Integrated**) + **10 components**; later expanded to **31 MMPs**.
- **Vision**: make all government services **accessible to the common man in his locality** through **common service delivery outlets**, with **transparency, reliability and efficiency at affordable cost**.
- **Integrated MMPs**: **e-Courts** (judiciary ICT, since **2007**), **e-Procurement**, National Portal (**india.gov.in**). State MMP example: **e-Panchayat**.
#### 2.2 e-Kranti (NeGP 2.0)
| Item | NeGP (1.0) | e-Kranti (NeGP 2.0) |
|---|---|---|
| MMPs | 27 → 31 | **44** |
| Place | Stand-alone national plan | **A pillar of Digital India** |
| Features | Service outlets | Integrated (cloud, mobile, geospatial), outcome-oriented, fast and agile |
!NUM 27 MMPs (9 + 11 + 7) → 31 → 44 under e-Kranti
!TRAP APPSC 2025 asked the number of MMPs listed by NeGP 2006: the answer is 27, not 31 or 44.
### 3. NeGP CORE INFRASTRUCTURE
| Pillar | Full form | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **SWAN** | **State Wide Area Network** | Converged **backbone** for data, voice, video across the state |
| **SDC** | **State Data Centre** | Hosts state e-gov applications and data on common secure IT infrastructure |
| **CSC** | **Common Service Centre** | **Village-level front-end** delivery outlet |
- Together they enable seamless G2G, G2C, G2B services.
- **MeghRaj (GI Cloud)** now provides government cloud hosting.
!TRAP Core infrastructure = SDC + SWAN + CSC; NIIF is not part of it.
### 4. COMMON SERVICE CENTRES AND AP DELIVERY OUTLETS
#### 4.1 Common Service Centres
- SPV and implementing agency: **CSC e-Governance Services India Ltd**.
- Run by **Village Level Entrepreneurs (VLEs)**; reach down to the **Gram Panchayat** level.
- **400+ services**: government, financial, Aadhaar, welfare, education, telemedicine, travel, utility bills.
- **APAAR** (Automated Permanent Academic Account Registry): launched **Feb 2024** by Ministry of Education **through CSCs**.
#### 4.2 MeeSeva (AP)
- **MeeSeva** = "**At your service**" (Telugu); launched **2011** in undivided AP.
- **Single-window** service delivery through MeeSeva centres and authorised agents (**about 11,079** in Jan 2023).
- Predecessor: **e-Seva (2001)** = **India's first integrated citizen service centres** (bill payments and citizen services at one centre).
#### 4.3 Gram/Ward Sachivalayam (2019)
- **10,000+ secretariats**; **500+ services**; **SPANDANA** grievance portal.

### 6. DIGILOCKER, E-SIGN AND SECURE MESSAGING
#### 6.1 DigiLocker
- **MeitY** cloud locker (**Feb 2015**); 56.06 crore users, 970.70 crore documents.
- Driving licence and vehicle RC linked with MoRTH (Sept 2016).
- **Legal validity**: DigiLocker-issued documents = originals under **IT Act, 2000** + **Digital Locker Rules, 2016** (not RTI Act).
#### 6.2 e-Sign
- **Aadhaar eKYC-based** signing of e-documents; based on **Public Key Infrastructure (PKI)**, not blockchain.
- Legal backing: **IT Act, 2000**; C-DAC's **e-Hastakshar** among issuers; 110.26 crore e-Signs issued (29.07 crore by C-DAC).
#### 6.3 Secure Messaging and E-mail
- **GIMS** = **Government Instant Messaging System**: India's in-house alternative to WhatsApp/Telegram for government (APPSC 2018); released as the **Sandes** app; built by **NIC**.
- **NIC Mail**: official government e-mail; integrated with **CollabFiles** office suite, e-Sign, **Bhashini**, Parichay single sign-on.
### 7. GRIEVANCE AND SECTORAL PORTALS

- **SAMADHAN DIDI** (2026): AI voice chatbot built with Bhashini, linked to CPGRAMS; all **22 Eighth Schedule languages**.
- **RTI Online**: **DoPT** portal for RTI applications and appeals to central public authorities.

### 10. AP DIGITAL GOVERNANCE
- **AP chain**: **e-Seva (2001) → MeeSeva (2011) → e-Pragati → RTGS → Mana Mitra (2025)**.
#### 10.1 CARD and BhuSeva
- **CARD** = **Computer-aided Administration of Registration Department**: computerised document registration, **1998**.
- **BhuSeva**: integrated land-services platform launched **20 Nov 2018** at Amaravati with the **Bhudaar** portal; every land holding gets an **11-digit Bhudaar** number.
#### 10.2 e-Pragati
- AP's **whole-of-government** e-governance programme; all systems interconnected; AP's full e-governance project for service delivery.
- **Core datasets**: **People Hub, Land Hub, Entity Hub, GIS Hub, IoT Hub** (not Revenue Hub).
- Feb 2026: 45 more services integrated → "faceless, paperless and cashless" governance.
#### 10.3 Real Time Governance Society (RTGS)
- Tools: **Data Lake, Data Lens, Command & Control Centre**; real-time dashboards; minister in charge Nara Lokesh.
#### 10.4 Mana Mitra (WhatsApp Governance)
- **AI-powered** conversational governance on **WhatsApp**; launched **30 January 2025** (APPSC 2025).
- **500+ services**; Outcome Budget target up to 1,000 services (Feb 2026).
#### 10.5 AP FIRSTs
- **e-Seva (2001)**: India's first integrated citizen service centres.
- **Rajiv Internet Village** (RAJiv): launched Aug 2004; rural internet kiosks run by women's self-help groups.
- BhuSeva/Bhudaar: described at launch as the first such land portal in the country (2018).
