## U0668 | SECTORS OF THE ECONOMY – PRIMARY, SECONDARY, TERTIARY AND QUATERNARY; GLOBAL PATTERNS
### 1. SECTOR CLASSIFICATION – ACTIVITY TO SECTOR
| Sector | Nature | Activities |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | Direct use of nature | Agriculture, forestry, fishing, mining and quarrying |
| **Secondary** | Processing / manufacturing | **Cement manufacturing**, construction |
| **Tertiary** | Services | **Transport**, trade, banking |
| **Quaternary** | Knowledge | **Research and development**, IT, education, consulting |
| **Quinary** | Top decisions | **Top decision-making**: government, top executives ("gold collar") |
!KEY APPSC 2018: **Transport = tertiary** sector.
!TRAP **Mining** is primary by nature, but GVA reporting groups it under the broad **Industry** sector (weight in revised IIP 11.05%).
### 2. TYPES OF PRIMARY ACTIVITY
| Type | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Oldest** | Depend directly on nature | **Hunting and gathering** |
| **Extractive** | Take what nature provides | Mining, capture fishing, hunting, lumbering |
| **Genetic** | Breed plants and animals | **Agriculture**, **animal husbandry**, fish farming |
- **Stock farming** = breeding of animals (keeping livestock).
- **Animal husbandry** = scientific management of livestock: feeding, breeding, disease control.
!TRAP Animal husbandry is a **genetic** industry, not extractive. Genetic industry = Agriculture (UPPSC 2016).
### 3. COLLAR-COLOUR TERMS
| Collar | Workers |
|---|---|
| **Red** | Primary-sector **outdoor** workers |
| **Blue** | **Manual**, factory and construction workers |
| **White** | Office and professional service workers |
| **Pink** | **Nursing, teaching, clerical** – service jobs long held mostly by women |
| **Green** | Environment and **renewable energy** jobs |
| **Gold** | Quinary: top decision-makers and experts |
- 2025: **UAE** overtook Saudi Arabia as top destination for Indian blue-collar workers.
### 4. SECTORAL SHARES – INDIA AND AP
| Economy | Primary | Secondary | Tertiary |
|---|---|---|---|
| India GVA 2024-25 (base 2022-23) | **21.4%** | **25.8%** | **52.9%** |
| AP GVA 2025-26 (current prices) | **33%** | 24% | 43% |
| AP employment (PLFS 2023-24) | **45.6%** | 22.8% | 31.6% |
- **Services lead in GVA; agriculture leads in jobs** (India and AP). Agriculture employs about **46.1%** of India's workers (Aug 2026).
- AP more agrarian than India: agriculture ~33% vs ~21%, led by **livestock and fisheries**.
- **New GDP series (Feb 2026)**: base year **2022-23**; GDP levels **3-4% lower**; manufacturing **14.7%**; FY26 growth **7.6%**.
- **Revised IIP (June 2026)** added a **water supply and waste management** sector.
### 5. STRUCTURAL TRANSFORMATION
- **Fisher-Clark thesis** – **Allan Fisher (1935)** and **Colin Clark (1940)**: as income rises, labour and output move **primary → secondary → tertiary**.
- **Premature deindustrialisation**: India skipped the mass factory-job stage before services expanded; only **2.9%** of global manufacturing value added.
- AP: services-led; manufacturing only ~**11% of GSDP** – the "**missing middle**" targeted by **Industrial Policy 4.0 (2024-29)**.
- Counterpoint: organised manufacturing jobs rose 6% in FY24 (about 10 lakh).
### 6. GLOBAL PATTERNS
| Feature | Developed countries | Developing countries |
|---|---|---|
| Services share of GDP | ~**70-80%** | Growing faster than industry |
| Employment | Mostly services | **Primary** still large |
| Quaternary/quinary | **Growing**, not shrinking | Small |
### 7. ORGANISED VS UNORGANISED
| Organised | Unorganised |
|---|---|
| Registered; regular terms; labour laws (PF, minimum wages, leave) | Small, scattered units; irregular pay; **no social security** |
- **Public vs private** is a separate split, by **ownership**.
- **e-Shram**: 31 crore+ unorganised workers, **54% women** (Jan 2026).
- AP informal employment: **62.3% rural** vs **45.6% urban**.
!TRAP 45.6% = AP agriculture employment share AND AP urban informal employment.
