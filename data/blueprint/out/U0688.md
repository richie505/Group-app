## U0688 | ENERGY RESOURCES - COAL, PETROLEUM, GAS, HYDEL, NUCLEAR, RENEWABLE; INDIA AND AP
### 1. CLASSIFICATION OF ENERGY SOURCES
| Class | Sources | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| **Renewable** (replenished naturally, short period) | Solar, wind, hydro, geothermal, tidal, wave, biomass, biogas | Basis of the **energy transition** |
| **Non-renewable** (finite) | **Fossil fuels** (coal, petroleum, natural gas) + **nuclear fuel** (uranium, thorium) | Fossil fuels formed over millions of years; burning gives CO2 |
- **Uranium** = non-renewable nuclear fuel, **NOT a fossil fuel**. **Wood** is not a fossil fuel. Natural gas is **not renewable**.
- First source of fossil-fuel energy = **the Sun** (energy stored in buried organisms).
- **Petroleum**: non-renewable, from ancient marine organisms; gives **plastic toothbrushes and gasoline**.
- **Petroleum products**: petrol, diesel, kerosene, **LPG, naphtha, bitumen**, petrochemical feedstock. **Anthracite** is coal, not a petroleum product.
- **Commercial** sources: power, coal, oil, gas, hydro-electricity, uranium. **Biogas = non-commercial**.
- Non-conventional energy = renewable energy; biomass = renewable organic material.
- APPSC 2025 keys: solar = not a fossil fuel; wind = renewable; **solar + wind = energy-transition sources**; thermal plants burn fossil fuels; solar plants use radiation, not coal.
- Fossil fuels: ~**75% of global GHGs, ~90% of CO2**; subsidies $1.8 trillion (2024). Largest SO2 source: fossil-fuel power plants (73%).
- **First oil crisis: 1973** (OPEC raised oil price four times). Photovoltaic = solar to electricity; National Solar Mission under **NAPCC**.
- Earth's resources include water, minerals, fish stocks, fossil fuels; sewage treatment plants = sustainable technology for water pollution.
#### 1.1 Fuels and Composition
| Fuel | Composition |
|---|---|
| Natural gas / **CNG** | Mainly **methane** (70-90%), ethane |
| **LPG** | **Propane + butane** |
| **Biogas** | Methane 50-75%, CO2 25-50%; **anaerobic** digestion; gives power + manure |
| **Water gas** | **CO + H2** |
| **Producer gas** | **CO + H2 + N2** |
| Coal gas | H2, methane, CO |
| Petrol / Kerosene / Diesel | C5-C10 / **C10-C16** / C15-C18 (fractional distillation of crude) |
| Hydrogen | Electrolysis or steam reforming |
- **Green hydrogen** = RE-powered electrolysis; **blue hydrogen** = gas + CCS.

### 3. COAL
#### 3.1 Grades
| Grade | Carbon | Occurrence |
|---|---|---|

#### 3.2 Gondwana vs Tertiary
| Type | Age | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Gondwana** | **250-300 mn yr** | **Damodar valley** (largest), Mahanadi, Godavari, Son |
| **Tertiary** | **15-60 mn yr** | **North-East** (Assam, Meghalaya); lignite: TN, Rajasthan, Gujarat |
- **Jharkhand** = largest coal reserves, **no major lignite**. **Tamil Nadu** = largest lignite (48.65% of output 2024-25; Gujarat 29.46%, Rajasthan 21.88%); India's lignite **47.36 bn t** (1 Apr 2023).
- Neyveli: first lignite-fired TPS in South-East Asia.

#### 3.4 Mining, PSUs, Rank
- **Opencast** = main method. **Rat-hole mining** (Meghalaya): narrow pits up to 400 ft, simple tools, **small family groups** (APPSC 2025 key: "mining by the family members"); **banned by NGT 2014**.
- **CIL** = world's largest coal producer; **SCCL** = Telangana:Centre **51:49**; **NLC India** = Neyveli lignite.
- **Coal Controller's Organisation** (1916): coal statistics; statistical authority under Collection of Statistics Act 2008.
- Revised **SHAKTI** (May 2025): coal through two windows.
- World: **China** largest producer (51.78%) and consumer; India **2nd producer (11.11%) and consumer, 5th in reserves**; reserves (end 2023): USA, Russia, China, Australia, India.
- AP: coal mining was a main activity before colonial rule; after bifurcation AP **lost its coal mines** - higher transport cost.

#### 4.3 Reserves, SPR and Gas Grid
- **SPR** (ISPRL): **Padur, Visakhapatnam, Mangaluru** (~5.33 MMT, ~9.5 days); Phase-II **Chandikhol** + Padur; **Ankleshwar not selected**.

- Hormuz closure (March 2026): India turned to coal and gas, LNG and LPG from other suppliers.

### 6. HYDEL POWER
- Most potential in **Himalayan basins** (Brahmaputra, Indus, Ganga): snow-fed perennial flow, steep slopes - suit large dams and run-of-the-river. Peninsular rivers seasonal.
- **Arunachal Pradesh** = highest hydel potential.
- **Pumped storage** stores daytime solar power for night peaks; off-stream closed-loop PSPs no longer need CEA approval.
- First hydropower station: **Sidrapong, Darjeeling (1897)**; **Shivasamudram** (Kaveri, Karnataka) second oldest. **NHPC** set up 1975.

#### 7.2 Three-Stage Programme (Bhabha, 1954)
| Stage | Reactor / fuel |
|---|---|
| I | **PHWR** - natural uranium + heavy water |
| II | **Fast breeder** - plutonium; breeds more fuel than it uses |
| III | **Thorium** (AHWR) |
- **Uranium**: **Jaduguda, Narwapahar** (Singhbhum, Jharkhand), **Tummalapalle** (YSR Kadapa, AP); ore low-grade; ~70,000 t. World: **Australia** largest reserves; **Kazakhstan** top producer (~40%).
- **Thorium**: monazite and ilmenite beach sands (Kerala, TN, AP, Odisha); ~3.6 lakh t = ~25% of world, 3rd largest. Budget 2026-27: **nil duty on monazite**.
