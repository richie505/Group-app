## U0229 | GANDHI – EARLY LIFE, SOUTH AFRICAN PHASE, MAKING OF SATYAGRAHA; HIND SWARAJ
### 1. EARLY LIFE AND EDUCATION
- Born **2 October 1869** at **Porbandar** (Gujarat); father **Karamchand Gandhi** (Diwan of Porbandar), mother **Putali Bai**.
- Early education at **Rajkot**; married **Kasturba** at the age of 13.
- Studied at **Samaldas College, Bhavnagar**, then law at the **Inner Temple, London (1888-1891)**; called to the Bar as a **barrister**.
- Legal practice at **Rajkot** and **Bombay** did not succeed.
- **1893**: went to South Africa for **Dada Abdullah's** law case.
!TRAP Gandhi studied law at the **Inner Temple**, not Lincoln's Inn or Middle Temple; his Indian college was Samaldas College, Bhavnagar.
### 2. SOUTH AFRICA (1893-1914) – THE LABORATORY OF SATYAGRAHA
#### 2.1 Events in Order
- Duration: **1893-1914** (about **21 years**).
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 7 June 1893 | Thrown out of a first-class compartment at **Pietermaritzburg** station (on the way to Pretoria for Dada Abdullah's case) |
| 1894 | Founded the **Natal Indian Congress** |
| Boer War | Served the wounded (ambulance work) |
| June 1903 | **Indian Opinion** first issue, Durban |
| 1903 | Law firm at **Johannesburg**; idea of **Trusteeship** linked to this year |
| 1904 | Read Ruskin's **Unto This Last** on a train → founded **Phoenix Settlement** near Durban |
| 1906 | **First satyagraha**: against the **Asiatic Registration Ordinance ("Black Act")**, **Transvaal**; stretcher-bearers in the Zulu Rebellion |
| 1907-08 | Burning of registration certificates; **first jail term 1908** |
| 1910 | **Tolstoy Farm** near Johannesburg, with **Hermann Kallenbach** |
| 1913 | **Great March** against the **£3 poll tax** and invalidation of non-Christian (Indian) marriages; miners and plantation (indentured) workers joined |
| 1914 | **Indian Relief Act**: poll tax abolished, Indian marriages recognised; Gandhi left for India |
#### 2.2 Seva Dharma and the Method
- Adopted **'Seva Dharma'** in South Africa: worked as a **compounder** in **Rustomji's charity hospital** and served the Boer War wounded.
- Best known there for **passive resistance**, which he renamed **Satyagraha**.
- South Africa = the **'laboratory'** where satyagraha was invented (**1906**).
- The South African struggles **prepared Gandhi to lead the national movement** (not the militancy of Indians there).
!KEY First satyagraha = **Transvaal 1906** (Black Act); first satyagraha in India = **Champaran 1917**.
### 3. SETTLEMENTS AND ASHRAMS
| Ashram / settlement | Place | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phoenix Settlement** | Near **Durban** | **1904** | His first settlement; community living, manual labour; inspired by Unto This Last |
| **Tolstoy Farm** | Near **Johannesburg** | **1910** | Cooperative settlement for satyagrahis and families; **Hermann Kallenbach**; the INC had **no role** |
| **Kochrab Ashram** | Ahmedabad | **1915** | First ashram in India |
| **Sabarmati Ashram** | Ahmedabad | **1917** | Also called **Satyagraha Ashram**; **Potti Sriramulu** worked here |
| **Sevagram (Wardha)** | Wardha | **1936** | Later major Gandhi centre |
| **Sadaqat Ashram** | Patna | — | Gandhi's Bihar work |
- **Order:** Phoenix (1904) → Tolstoy Farm (1910) → Kochrab (1915) → Sabarmati (1917) → Sevagram (1936).
- Gandhi called **Yerawada Jail** his **"Mandir"**.
!PAIR Phoenix | Durban | 1904 || Tolstoy Farm | Johannesburg | 1910
!KEY AP angle: **Potti Sriramulu** worked at **Sabarmati Ashram** (about 3 years) – asked in APPSC 2017.
### 4. INFLUENCES – BOOK AND AUTHOR
| Book / source | Author | What Gandhi took |
|---|---|---|
| **Unto This Last** | **John Ruskin** | Dignity of labour, **Sarvodaya**; "the good of the individual is contained in the good of all" |
| **Civil Disobedience** | **H.D. Thoreau** | Right to break unjust laws |
| **The Kingdom of God is Within You** | **Leo Tolstoy** | Non-violent resistance; Tolstoy Farm named after him |
| **Bhagavad Gita** | — | **Nishkama karma** (action without attachment to fruits) |
| **Jainism** | — | Five vows: ahimsa, satya, asteya, aparigraha, brahmacharya |
- Copy of Unto This Last was given by **Henry Polak** in **1904**; it influenced Gandhi most.
- Gandhian thought was influenced by **Ruskin, Thoreau and Tolstoy** – "All the above".
- Philosophical pillars: ahimsa, satya, asteya, aparigraha, sarvodaya.
!TRAP Jain vows: first **four from Parshvanatha**; **brahmacharya added by Mahavira**.
### 5. HIND SWARAJ (1909)
- Written in **1909**, in **Gujarati**, aboard the ship **Kildonan Castle** (London → South Africa).
- Themes: **swaraj as self-rule**, self-reliance, rural life; **against industrialisation** and modern civilisation.
- Called the British Parliament **"sterile and prostitute"**.
- **Banned** by the **Bombay Government (1910)**.
- **Translated into English by Gandhi himself** (to avoid the ban).
!TRAP Hind Swaraj: written 1909 in Gujarati; banned 1910; English version by Gandhi himself, not by a disciple.

### 7. RETURN TO INDIA
- Landed at **Bombay** on **9 January 1915** → observed as **Pravasi Bharatiya Divas**.
- **Gopal Krishna Gokhale**, his **political guru**, advised him to spend **one year studying India** before any movement.
- Received the **Kaisar-i-Hind gold medal** in **1915** for ambulance work in South Africa.
#### 7.1 First Satyagrahas in India
| Satyagraha | Issue | FIRST |
|---|---|---|
| **Champaran 1917** | Indigo | First **civil disobedience** |
| **Ahmedabad 1918** | Mill wages | First **hunger strike** |
| **Kheda 1918** | Revenue remission | First **non-cooperation** |
!TRAP C – A – K = civil disobedience – hunger strike – non-cooperation.
