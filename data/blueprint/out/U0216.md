## U0216 | PROMINENT ANDHRA LEADERS – KONDA VENKATAPPAYYA, PATTABHI SITARAMAYYA, TANGUTURI PRAKASAM, BULUSU SAMBAMURTHY
### 1. THE FOUR LEADERS – OVERVIEW
- These four led Andhra through the **NCM, CDM, Simon boycott, Sribagh Pact and statehood**.

|---|---|---|
| **Konda Venkatappayya** | **Desabhakta** | Dictator of the Andhra Salt Satyagraha; Father of the Andhra State Movement |

### 4. KONDA VENKATAPPAYYA
#### 4.1 Andhra Mahasabha and Early Roles (Chronology)
| Year | Role |
|---|---|
| **1902** | Co-founded **Krishna Patrika** with **Dasu Narayana Rao**; its **first editor** |
| **1907** | President, **Swadeshi meeting at Tenali** |
| **1910** | President, **National College, Machilipatnam** |
| **1912** | Secretary, **Nidadavolu standing committee** |
| **1913** | **Secretary**, **first Andhra Mahasabha, Bapatla** |
| **1917** | **President**, **fifth Andhra Mahasabha, Nellore** |
!TRAP Konda = Secretary at Bapatla (1913) but President at Nellore (1917).
#### 4.2 Congress Offices and CDM
- **First president, Andhra Congress Circle**; **APCC president 1918-23**; **AICC Secretary (1923)**.
- **Calcutta special session (1920)**: **opposed** the Non-Cooperation resolution (passed **1,886-884**).
- **CDM**: appointed **Dictator of the Andhra Salt Satyagraha / Civil Disobedience** (APPSC G2 2008).
- **Jailed 1930, 1932 and 1942**.
- **MLC** from **Guntur-Tenali, 1937**.
- Book: ***Andhrudyamam***.
- **Pedanandipadu (1922)**: Gandhi's message to suspend the no-tax campaign was sent to him.
### 5. TANGUTURI PRAKASAM
#### 5.1 Simon Commission Protest
- Simon Commission appointed **Nov 1927**, arrived **3 Feb 1928**.
- Prakasam led the **boycott in Madras (Feb 1928)**; he bared his chest to police guns → **"Andhra Kesari"**.
#### 5.2 Newspapers
| Paper | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Swarajya** (1921) | **English daily** (APPSC 2011 key) |
| **Grama Swarajya** | Separate **weekly** |
!TRAP Prakasam's Swarajya ≠ Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao's separate "Swarajya" magazine.
#### 5.3 Posts and Premiership
| Year | Post |
|---|---|
| **1903** | **Chairman, Rajahmundry Municipality** (council member from 1900) |
| — | **President of the Andhra Congress Committee 18 times** |
| **1937** | **Revenue Minister**, Rajaji ministry |
| **1946** | **Premier (Chief Minister) of composite Madras** |
| **1951** | Left Congress with **N.G. Ranga** → **Kisan Mazdoor Praja Party** (later Praja Socialist Party) |
| **1953** | **First CM of Andhra State** |
- **Sribagh Pact, 16 Nov 1937**: coastal signatory.
- **Bezawada Gopal Reddy**: **2nd and last CM of Andhra State (28 Mar 1955 – 31 Oct 1956)**.
- **Prakasam district** is named after him.
#### 5.4 Books
- ***Naa Jeevita Yatra*** – four-part autobiography: **three parts by Prakasam**, the **fourth by Tenneti Viswanatham**.
- *Chenna Nagaramu Andhra Nagaramae*; ***Law Times***; *Monetary Systems of the World*; *Indian Monetary Systems*.
### 6. PATTABHI SITARAMAYYA
#### 6.1 Congress Role
- **Tripuri (1939)**: **Gandhi's candidate**, **defeated by Subhas Chandra Bose**; Gandhi called it **"my own defeat"**.
- **Presided the Jaipur session (1948)**.
- **JVP Committee** (**Nehru, Patel, Pattabhi**) – first **rejected linguistic states**.
- Wrote articles in **The Hindu** on the need for linguistic states.
- **APCC President (1937)**.
#### 6.2 Posts after 1947
- **Governor of Madhya Pradesh (1952-57)**; Rajya Sabha member.

### 8. PINGALI VENKAYYA AND THE NATIONAL FLAG
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Early career | Soldier in the British Army in **South Africa** during the **Anglo-Boer War** |
| Congress sessions | Attended the **22nd INC session, Calcutta (1906)**, where the Calcutta flag was hoisted – his flag interest took shape |
| Book | **1916** book on national-flag designs – ***Bharatha Desaniki Oka Jatiya Patakam*** (A National Flag for India), about **30 designs** |
| Flag shown to Gandhi | **Vijayawada AICC, 31 Mar – 1 Apr 1921**; venue named **"Gandhi Nagar"** (APPSC G2 2019) |
| Result | His tricolour was later adopted, with minor changes, as the **National Flag** |
| Titles | Patti / Japan / Diamond Venkayya |
