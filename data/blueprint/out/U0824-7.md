|---|---|---|---|

!TRAP **US$300 bn = NCQG (COP29 Baku)**; **US$1.3 trn = Baku-Belém Roadmap (COP30)**.
#### 3.2 Blocs and Terms
- **BASIC**: **Brazil, South Africa, India, China**.
- **Loss and Damage Fund**: operationalised/started at **COP28 (Dubai)**.
- **Carbon-negative countries**: **Bhutan, Suriname, Panama**.
- "**35 by 35**" (COP31): electricity to be **35% of final energy by 2035**.
