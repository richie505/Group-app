## U0774 | BIODIVERSITY – LEVELS, VALUES, MEASUREMENT; INDIA AS A MEGADIVERSE COUNTRY
### 1. MEANING, DEFINITION AND LEVELS
- Term **"biodiversity"** coined by **Walter G. Rosen (1985)**; **popularised by E.O. Wilson**.
- **CBD (1992) definition**: variability among living organisms from **all sources** (terrestrial, marine, other aquatic) and the **ecological complexes** they belong to – **within species, between species and of ecosystems**.
| Level | Meaning / example |
|---|---|
| **Genetic** | Variation **within a species** – basic source; all rice varieties are **Oryza sativa** but differ in size, aroma, nutrients |
| **Species** | Variety of species in an area (**richness + abundance**) |
| **Ecosystem** | Variety of ecosystems; **one ecosystem cannot replace another** (boreal conifers ≠ tropical deciduous) |
- Chinese, Americans, Indians and Black Africans = the **same species, Homo sapiens**.
- Butterflies, fish and frogs all have **poisonous species**; **Monarch butterfly** is toxic from **cardenolides** of **milkweed** eaten as caterpillar.
### 2. MEASURING BIODIVERSITY
| Measure | Scale |
|---|---|
| **Alpha (α)** | **Within** one habitat – local; mean species diversity there |
| **Beta (β)** | **Between** ecosystems – species **turnover** along a gradient |
| **Gamma (γ)** | **Regional** total across a landscape |
| **Species richness** | Number of species |
| **Evenness** | Relative abundance; **low = few species dominate** |
- Terms alpha, beta, gamma by **R.H. Whittaker**.
- Example: Forest A (2 tigers, 6 deer, 7 rabbits) vs B (1, 6, 8) – same richness; **A more even**.
- Indices: **Shannon-Wiener** and **Simpson**.
- **Functional diversity**: range of ecological roles – functional richness, evenness, divergence.
!TRAP Alpha = within; Beta = between; Gamma = whole region.
### 3. SPECIES ESTIMATES AND RICHNESS PATTERNS
- Described: about **1.5 million** living species (+300,000 fossil). Totals: **Wilson (1992) 10–50 million**; **Robert May ~7 million**.
- **Over 70%** of recorded species are **animals** (insects richest); **plants ~22%**.
- **Latitudinal gradient**: richest in **tropics**, falls towards poles; **ants ~10 species near North Pole vs ~2,000 at equator**.
- Richest: **tropical rainforests** (land), **coral reefs** (sea) – not taiga, desert or temperate forest.
- Tropics richest: **long undisturbed evolutionary time (no glaciation)**, **stable climate**, **more solar energy → productivity**; moderate disturbance and habitat variety also help.
- Most butterflies in tropics – **they cannot tolerate low temperature** (A/R: R explains A).
- **Himalayas** rich: **meeting point of biogeographical zones** (Palearctic + Indo-Malayan); ~25% flora, 30% fauna.
- **Species-area relationship** (**Humboldt**, log S = log C + Z log A): richness rises with area, up to a limit.
### 4. VALUES OF BIODIVERSITY
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Consumptive** | Used locally, **not sold**: fuelwood, fodder, wild food |
| **Productive** | **Sold commercially**: timber, fibre, medicines, genetic material |
| **Ecosystem services** | Clean air, water, pollination, climate |
| Social / cultural | Sacred groves, indigenous knowledge |
| Aesthetic | Beauty, ecotourism |
| **Option** | **Unknown future uses** – new drugs, crop genes |
- Most significant aspect: **maintenance of the ecosystem**. Role in SD (APPSC 2025): **supports ecosystem services**.
- **Rivet popper hypothesis** (**Paul Ehrlich**): ecosystem = aeroplane, species = rivets; losing a key rivet (**keystone species**) may collapse it.
- **TEEB**: G8+5 initiative, **2007**; "make nature's values visible"; hosted by **UNEP** (not IMF/WEF); led by **Pavan Sukhdev**.
- **Dasgupta Review (2021)**: **Partha Dasgupta** for **UK Treasury**; nature as an asset; GDP ignores nature's depreciation.
### 5. INDIA AS A MEGADIVERSE COUNTRY
- **17 megadiverse countries** (36 = hotspots): Australia, Brazil, China, Colombia, DR Congo, Ecuador, **India**, Indonesia, Madagascar, Malaysia, Mexico, Papua New Guinea, Peru, Philippines, South Africa, USA, Venezuela.
- Criteria (**Conservation International, 1998**): **≥5,000 endemic plants** + **border marine ecosystems**.
- **LMMC**: negotiating bloc, **Cancun Declaration 2002**; India a member.
| Item | India |
|---|---|
| Land / species / people | **2.4% / 7–8% / ~18%** |
| Global fauna | ~**7.5%**; over **30%** of animals endemic |
| Ranks | **10th** plant-rich; **11th** endemic higher vertebrates; **6th** crop origin |
| Hotspots | **4 of 36** |
- **2025 additions** (BSI + ZSI, 1 Jul 2026): **709 animal species = 483 new + 226 records**; **353 plant taxa**; fauna now **1,05,953**; top: Kerala 98, West Bengal 76.
- Night frog **Mewasingi**: **Kozhikode, Kerala** (2019).
### 6. BIOGEOGRAPHIC ZONES OF INDIA
- **Rodgers and Panwar (WII, 1988)**: **2 realms** (Palearctic + Indo-Malayan), **5 biomes** (tropical humid forests, tropical dry/deciduous forests, warm deserts and semi-deserts, **coniferous forests**, **alpine meadows**), **10 zones**, **25 provinces**.
| Zone | Note |
|---|---|
| Trans-Himalaya | Cold desert; only ~5.7% of land |
| Himalaya | **Most faunal species** (30,377) |
| Western Ghats | High endemism |
| **Deccan Peninsula** | **Largest (~42%)**; **5 provinces**; AP lies here |
- Deccan provinces (APPSC 2024): **Central Highlands, Chotanagpur, Eastern Highlands, Central Plateau, Deccan South**.
### 7. ZOOGEOGRAPHY – NATURAL RANGE
| Animal | Range |
|---|---|
| **Asiatic lion** | **Gir – only India** |
| One-horned rhino | Kaziranga; also Nepal |
| **Nilgiri tahr**, lion-tailed macaque | **Western Ghats only** (Eravikulam) |
| **Hoolock gibbon** | NE India south of Brahmaputra – **India's only ape** |
| Saltwater crocodile | **Bhitarkanika, Sundarbans** |
| **Double-humped (Bactrian) camel** | **Nubra Valley, Ladakh** – only place in India (cold desert) |
| **Red panda** | **Eastern Himalaya**: Sikkim, Arunachal, Darjeeling–Kalimpong (WB) – not Western Himalaya |
| **Bengal slow loris** | **NE India** forests (Assam, Arunachal) |
| **Indian pangolin** / **Chinese pangolin** | Indian: across India south of the Himalaya (not NE); Chinese: eastern terai and **NE**; both **Schedule I** |
| Gharial | Chambal |
- **Marsupials do not occur naturally in India** (UPSC 2023); "thrive in montane grasslands" is false; pouched young, ~270 species.
### 8. AGRO-BIODIVERSITY AND BIOPIRACY
- **N.I. Vavilov**: **8 centres** of origin; **Hindustan (Indo-Burma)**: rice, sugarcane, mango, pigeon pea, brinjal, cucumber, black pepper, citrus.
- India: 811 crops + 902 crop wild relatives. **Nokrek** (Garo Hills) = **Citrus Gene Sanctuary**; **Borjuli** (Assam, BHS 2026) = **Oryza rufipogon**, wild ancestor of rice.
| Case | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **Turmeric** (US) | Revoked **1997** (CSIR) |
| **Neem** (EPO; W.R. Grace) | Revoked **2000** |
| **Basmati** (RiceTec) | Most claims withdrawn |
| **Hoodia** (San people) | Benefit-sharing deal |
- **Bioprospecting** = search for valuable genes; **biopiracy** = patenting without consent. **TKDL** (**2001**, CSIR + AYUSH) – **prior art**. ABS: CBD 1992 → **Nagoya 2010**; **BD Act 2002** (amended 2023). NBA: ₹266 crore; Red Sanders 45%.
### 9. AP BIODIVERSITY
- **Eastern Ghats**: Mahanadi to Nilgiris; highest peak **Arma Konda (1,680 m, ASR district)**; **Mahendra Girulu** in Srikakulam; **not a global hotspot**.
- **Seshachalam BR (2010)**: **Red Sanders** (endemic, southern Eastern Ghats), **Cycas beddomei**, **Golden Gecko** (micro-endemic). Seshachalam–Nallamala: tigers, elephants, **Chenchus**.
- **Jerdon's courser** (Critically Endangered, endemic): **sparse scrub forest with bare ground** in **Sri Lankamalleswara WLS (Kadapa)** and Sri Penusila Narasimha WLS (Nellore); **rediscovered 1986** after being thought extinct – the sanctuary was declared to protect it.
- **Slender loris** (grey slender loris): **dry Eastern Ghats** scrub with acacia, e.g. **Sri Venkateswara NP** (Tirupati).
- AP: **second-largest mangrove** ecosystem.
- **APSBB**: PBRs, **~13,000 BMCs**; **5% of Red Sanders profits** to farmers; Marine ABS framework; OECMs for 30x30.
