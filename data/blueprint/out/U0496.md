## U0496 | AGRARIAN CRISIS – CAUSES, FARMER DISTRESS AND SUICIDES, LOAN WAIVERS, SMALL-HOLDING VIABILITY
### 1. CAUSES OF AGRARIAN CRISIS
| Cause | Data point |
|---|---|
| Small holdings | **86%** small and marginal |
| Rain-fed | ~50% NSA; groundwater = **64%** of irrigation; **17%** blocks over-exploited |
| Post-harvest | Cold-chain deficit **30%** → up to **40%** fruit/vegetable loss |
| Soil | NPK **10.9:4.1:1** vs ideal **4:2:1** |
- **Debt cycle**: input cost → borrowing → crop failure → new borrowing → asset sale → **debt trap**.
- **Rural poverty** slows agricultural development.
### 2. SMALL HOLDINGS AND VIABILITY
| Census | Small + marginal holdings | Their area |
|---|---|---|
| 2010–11 | ~**85%** | ~**44%** (APPSC 2017) |
| **2015–16** | **86.2%** | **47.3%** |
- 2015–16: semi-medium + medium **13.2%** holdings, **43.6%** area.
- Remedies: **10,000 FPOs (2020, ₹6,865 crore)**, **Custom Hiring Centres**, cluster and **joint farming**.
- **Bullock capitalists**: **Lloyd and Susanne Rudolph** (1987).
### 3. INDEBTEDNESS AND INFORMAL CREDIT
- **NSS 77th Round SAS (2019)**: ~half indebted; debt **₹74,121**; income **wages → crops → livestock**.
- Most indebted: **AP 93.2%**, **Telangana 91.7%**; ~50% of AP farmers use informal credit.
- **NSSO 70th Round (2013)**: **Rajasthan** = most agricultural households; **Kerala** >60% non-farm income.
- **Guntur study (NABARD)**: tenants **₹2,07,444** vs owners **₹76,306**; informal **51.5%** (tenants ~60%).
- **Expert Group on Agricultural Indebtedness (2007)**: **R. Radhakrishna**.
- **KCC gap**: 7.72 crore KCCs vs 146 million holdings.
- **AP Microfinance (Regulation of Money Lending) Act, 2011**; MFI limits: **3 lenders**, cap **₹2 lakh**.
### 4. FARMER SUICIDES – NCRB ADSI
- NCRB **ADSI**; concentrated in **rain-fed belts**.
- **ADSI 2015**: biggest cause = **bankruptcy/indebtedness** (APPSC 2017).
- **ADSI 2023**: farm sector **10,786**; **Maharashtra 38.5%**, Karnataka 22.5%, **AP 8.6%**.
### 5. NATIONAL COMMISSION ON FARMERS
- Constituted **2004**, chair **M.S. Swaminathan**.
- **MSP = C2 + 50%**; C2 = A2 + imputed family labour + rent/interest on owned land and capital.
- Also: low-cost credit, land reforms, women and tenant rights, universal crop insurance, **National Agricultural Risk Fund**.
### 6. LOAN WAIVERS
| Waiver | Facts |
|---|---|
| **ADWDRS 2008** | Small/marginal: **full waiver**; others: **25% OTS** on paying 75% |
| **AP Debt Redemption 2014** (Rythu Runa Mafi) | **Rythu Sadhikara Samstha**; ceiling **₹1.5 lakh/family**; horticulture ₹10,000/acre |
- Critique: **moral hazard**, hurts **credit culture** (RBI).
### 7. CROP INSURANCE
| PMFBY (2016) | Fact |
|---|---|
| Replaced | **NAIS (1999), MNAIS (2010)** – not WBCIS |
| Premium | **Kharif 2%, Rabi 1.5%, commercial 5%** |
| Voluntary | From **2020** |
| Tech | **YES-TECH** (yield), **WINDS** (weather), DigiClaim |
- "One Nation, One Crop, One Premium".
- **CCIS 1985** → NAIS 1999–2000; **WBCIS** first **Karnataka (2007)**; **Varsha Bima** (AICIL) **2004**.
- **RWBCIS**: pays when weather deviates from triggers.
- **AP**: **free insurance (2019–24)**, State paid all, CCRC holders covered; 22 crops (2022); voluntary from **Rabi 2024–25**; Kharif 2026 – all crops, Collectors accountable.
