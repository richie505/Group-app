## U0496 | AGRARIAN CRISIS – CAUSES, FARMER DISTRESS AND SUICIDES, LOAN WAIVERS, SMALL-HOLDING VIABILITY
### 1. CAUSES OF AGRARIAN CRISIS
| Cause | Data point |
|---|---|
| Small holdings | **86%** small and marginal |
| Rain-fed | ~50% NSA; groundwater = **64%** of irrigation; **17%** blocks over-exploited |
| Middlemen | Farmers get ~**35%** of retail price; cold-chain deficit **30%** → up to **40%** fruit/vegetable loss |
| Inputs | Up **300%+** since early 2000s |
| Soil | NPK **10.9:4.1:1** vs ideal **4:2:1** |
| Climate | Yields fall ~**8% per 1°C** |
- **Debt cycle**: high input cost → borrowing → crop failure/low price → cannot repay → new borrowing → asset sale → **debt trap**.
- **Rural poverty** = real reason for slow agricultural development (UPPSC 2016).
### 2. SMALL HOLDINGS AND VIABILITY
| Census | Small + marginal holdings | Their area |
|---|---|---|
| 2010–11 | ~**85%** | ~**44%** (APPSC 2017) |
| **2015–16** | **86.2%** | **47.3%** |
- 2015–16: semi-medium + medium **13.2%** holdings, **43.6%** area; average **1.08 ha** (2.28 ha in 1971). AP average **0.86 ha** (11th Census, provisional).
- Remedies: **10,000 FPO scheme (2020, Central Sector, ₹6,865 crore)**, **Custom Hiring Centres**, cluster and **cooperative/joint farming**.
- **Bullock capitalists**: middle farmers – term of **Lloyd and Susanne Rudolph**, *In Pursuit of Lakshmi* (1987).
### 3. INDEBTEDNESS AND INFORMAL CREDIT
- **NSS 77th Round SAS (2019)**: ~half of households indebted; debt **₹74,121**; income **₹10,218/month**; largest source **wages → crops → livestock**.
- Most indebted: **AP 93.2%**, **Telangana 91.7%**. AP informal credit ~50% vs national ~31%.
- **NSSO 70th Round (2013)**: **Rajasthan** = highest share of agricultural households; **Kerala** >60% non-farm income.
- **Guntur study (NABARD, 2023)**: 88% in debt; tenants **₹2,07,444** vs owners **₹76,306**; informal **51.5%** (tenants ~60%).
- **Expert Group on Agricultural Indebtedness (2007)**: **R. Radhakrishna**.
- **KCC gap**: 7.72 crore operative KCCs vs 146 million holdings.
- **AP Microfinance Institutions (Regulation of Money Lending) Act, 2011** after the 2010 crisis; MFI limits: **3 lenders**, household cap **₹2 lakh**.
### 4. FARMER SUICIDES – NCRB ADSI
- Source: NCRB "**Accidental Deaths and Suicides in India**"; concentrated in **rain-fed belts**.
- **ADSI 2015**: biggest cause = **bankruptcy/indebtedness** (APPSC 2017).
- **ADSI 2023**: farm sector **10,786** (6.3%); **Maharashtra 38.5%**, Karnataka 22.5%, **AP 8.6%** (3rd in labourer suicides). Latest: **10,546** (6.2%) = 4,633 farmers + 5,913 labourers.
### 5. NATIONAL COMMISSION ON FARMERS
- Constituted **18 Nov 2004**, chair **M.S. Swaminathan**; to address farmer suicides.
- **MSP = C2 + 50%**; C2 = A2 + imputed family labour + rent/interest on owned land and capital.
- Also: low-cost credit, land reforms, rights for women and tenants, universal crop insurance, **National Agricultural Risk Fund**; led to **National Policy for Farmers 2007**.
### 6. LOAN WAIVERS
| Waiver | Facts |
|---|---|
| **ADWDRS 2008** | Small/marginal: **full waiver**; others: **25% OTS** on paying 75%; ~₹52,000 crore |
| **AP Debt Redemption 2014** (Rythu Runa Mafi) | **Rythu Sadhikara Samstha**; ceiling **₹1.5 lakh/family**; horticulture ₹10,000/acre (max ₹50,000) |
- Critique: **moral hazard**, harms **credit culture** (RBI), excludes informal and tenant loans.
### 7. CROP INSURANCE
| PMFBY (2016) | Fact |
|---|---|
| Replaced | **NAIS (1999), MNAIS (2010)** – not WBCIS |
| Premium | **Kharif 2%, Rabi 1.5%, commercial 5%** |
| Voluntary | From **2020** |
| Tech | **YES-TECH** (yield), **WINDS** (weather), DigiClaim |
- "One Nation, One Crop, One Premium"; pre-sowing to post-harvest; 4.19 crore insured (2024–25).
- **CCIS** 1 Apr **1985** → NAIS from Rabi 1999–2000; **WBCIS** first in **Karnataka (Kharif 2007)**; **Varsha Bima** (AICIL) **2004** SW monsoon.
- **RWBCIS**: pays when weather deviates from triggers.
- **AP**: **Dr YSR Free Crop Insurance (2019–24)**, State paid all, CCRC holders covered; YSR scheme 2022 = 22 crops; voluntary from **Rabi 2024–25** (State and Centre share equally); Kharif 2026 – every crop insured, Collectors accountable.
### 8. INCOME SUPPORT AND AP TENANT FARMERS
| Scheme | State |
|---|---|
| **PM-KISAN** | Centre, from **Feb 2019**; 3 × ₹2,000 |
| **Annadata Sukhibhava** | **AP** – ₹20,000 incl. PM-KISAN; tenants covered |
| **KALIA** | **Odisha** |
| **Rythu Bandhu/Bharosa** | **Telangana** – ₹12,000/acre; Rythu Bima |
- **PM Kisan Maandhan**: pension for small farmers.
- **2011 crop holiday**: **Konaseema** paddy farmers; committee chair **Mohan Kanda**. **Koneru Ranga Rao** = AP Land Committee.
| Law | Instrument |
|---|---|
| **AP Land Licensed Cultivators Act, 2011** | **Loan Eligibility Cards** |
| **AP Crop Cultivator Rights Act, 2019** | **CCRC** from **2 Oct 2019**, valid **11 months**; owner-cultivator agreement; no ownership transfer |
- CCRC gives credit, insurance, benefits; 8.24 lakh active, ₹3,768 crore (2025–26). Hurdle: **landowner's signature**; tenants missing from e-crop lose relief. AP pioneered **RMGs, JLGs**.
