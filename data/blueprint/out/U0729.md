## U0729 | EARTH OBSERVATION – IRS, CARTOSAT, RESOURCESAT, OCEANSAT, RISAT, EOS
### 1. REMOTE SENSING – DEFINITION AND COMPONENTS
- **Remote sensing**: information about the Earth **without physical contact**, by measuring **reflected and emitted electromagnetic radiation** from a distance (satellite or aircraft).
- It uses **electromagnetic waves** (part of the EM spectrum).
#### 1.1 Components (stages)
1. Energy source / illumination
2. Radiation through the atmosphere
3. Interaction with the target
4. Recording by the sensor
5. Transmission, reception and processing
6. Interpretation and analysis
7. Application
!TRAP APPSC 2022: "Interaction of Energy with **Minimisation**" is NOT a component; nor is physical sampling.
### 2. SENSORS, IMAGING TYPES AND RESOLUTION
#### 2.1 Passive vs active
| Type | Energy | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Passive** | Reflected sunlight or emitted heat | IRS, Resourcesat, Oceansat, visible cameras, multispectral imagers, infrared radiometers |
| **Active** | Sends its own pulse, reads the echo | **RISAT SAR** (microwave), **laser/LiDAR** |
!KEY APPSC 2023: **Laser = active sensor**; heat of volcanic eruptions is captured by **infrared-sensitive** sensors.
#### 2.2 Optical vs SAR vs hyperspectral vs thermal
| Imaging | Feature |
|---|---|
| Optical (visible/NIR) | Reflected light; needs **daylight and clear sky** |
| **SAR** (microwave) | Pierces **clouds and darkness**; all-weather, day-night |
| **Hyperspectral** | Hundreds of narrow bands → identifies materials/chemicals |
| **Thermal infrared** | Emitted heat → volcanoes, forest fires, urban heat |
!TRAP APPSC 2021 key gave **Visible** as the band for cloud-covered disaster areas; in principle SAR (microwave) sees through cloud. Follow the key in that exact question.
#### 2.3 Resolution types
- Four: **spatial, spectral, temporal, radiometric**.
!TRAP APPSC 2025: "**Chemical** resolution" does not exist.
### 3. REMOTE-SENSING TECHNIQUES → WHAT THEY MAP
| Technique | Maps |
|---|---|
| **InSAR** | Ground deformation – subsidence, earthquake/volcano uplift |
| **LiDAR** | Laser ranging – elevation, 3D terrain, **canopy height** |
| **NDVI** | Vegetation health from **red vs near-infrared** reflectance |
| **Thermal infrared** | Volcanic heat, forest fires, urban heat |
| **Hyperspectral** | Materials and chemicals |
- **MODIS** = Moderate Resolution Imaging Spectro-radiometer; detected **52,785 forest fires** (Nov 2020 – Jun 2021).
### 4. ORBITS FOR EARTH OBSERVATION
| Orbit | Altitude | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **LEO** (Low Earth Orbit) | 200–2,000 km | **Earth observation**, ISS, Starlink-type constellations |
| **MEO** | 2,000–30,000 km | **Navigation** (GPS) |
| **GEO** (geostationary) | **35,786 km** over the equator | **Communication, weather**; appears fixed |
| **GSO** (geosynchronous) | Same height, inclined | Traces a **figure-8**; NavIC's 4 inclined satellites |
| **SSPO** (sun-synchronous polar) | ~**500–900 km** | Polar imaging |
- **Sun-synchronous**: near-polar; passes each place at the **same local solar time** → even lighting; so most EO satellites use it.
- **Geostationary** = geosynchronous, **circular**, in the **plane of the equator**, zero inclination; fixed over one longitude; also called **Clarke's orbit**; used for telecom relay.
- GEO and GSO period = one **sidereal day, 23 h 56 min 4.09 s**; only geostationary looks fixed.
- Trade-off: **LEO** = finer detail, periodic revisit; **geosynchronous** imagers = coarser detail, very frequent viewing (EOS-05: targets every 5 min, all India every 30 min).
### 5. IRS PROGRAMME – ORIGINS AND FIRSTS
- IRS began **1988** with IRS-1A → **world's largest civilian remote-sensing constellation**.
| FIRST / early satellite | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Rohini** (1980) | On **SLV-3**; series of four, RS-D1 on 31 May 1981 |
| **Bhaskara I** (1979) | First experimental EO satellite (with Bhaskara-2) |
| **IRS-1A** | **17 Mar 1988**, **Baikonur**, Soviet **Vostok** – first operational remote-sensing satellite |
| IRS-1B (1991), 1C (1995) | Also from Baikonur on Soviet rockets |
| **IRS-P2** (1994) | First IRS on **PSLV**, Sriharikota |
- **SLV-3** is a launch vehicle, **not a satellite**.
- IRS uses: crop productivity, groundwater, mineral exploration (**not** telecom or traffic studies).

### 8. RECENT EO MISSIONS – EOS-05, NISAR, LOSSES
- **EOS-05**: **GSLV-F17**, Sriharikota, **4 Sep 2026**, 2,367 kg; visible to long-wave IR; floods, cyclones, forest fires, crops, security (not submarine communication). India's first **geosynchronous land-imaging** satellite (earlier GEO "eyes" were weather satellites).
- **NISAR** (NASA-ISRO SAR): **GSLV-F16, 30 Jul 2025**; **NASA L-band + ISRO S-band**; world's first dual-frequency radar satellite; **12 m** antenna reflector.

### 10. NAVIGATION – NAVIC, GNSS AND GAGAN
- **IRNSS** = Indian Regional Navigation Satellite System; **NavIC** = Navigation with Indian Constellation.
- **7 satellites: 3 GEO + 4 GSO**; India + **1,500 km**; **L5 and S** bands.
- **NVS-01** (2023) – 2nd generation; **NVS-02**: GSLV-F15, **29 Jan 2025**, **100th Sriharikota launch**; never reached circular orbit – a **loose pyro connector** (panel, 26 Feb 2026).
- **13 Mar 2026**: IRNSS-1F's last rubidium clock stopped (3 days after its 10-year life) → only **3** PNT satellites (**1B, 1I, NVS-01**); a fix needs **4**.
- First NavIC-based device: **Elena Geo Systems**.
| System | Owner | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| GPS | USA (~30 satellites, MEO) | Global |
| **GLONASS** | **Russia** (not UK) | Global |
| **Galileo** | **European Union** (not USA) | Global |
| **BeiDou** | **China** | Global |
| **QZSS (Michibiki)** | **Japan** | Regional |
| NavIC | India | Regional |
- India: **only developing country** with its own independent regional system; **Japan** also has its own.
- **GAGAN** = GPS Aided GEO Augmented Navigation; **ISRO + Airports Authority of India**; ground stations send corrections; **civil aviation precision landing** (not an NRSC EO use).
### 11. NRSC PLATFORMS, EO IN NEWS AND AP
- Chain: collection → ground station → processing at **NRSC, Hyderabad** (nodal) → ministries, states, disaster agencies.

- Uses: groundwater mapping, wastelands, wetlands, **FASAL** crop forecasting, drought, NUIS, **INFFRAS** (forest fire response and assessment system), SIS-DP.
| In news | Agency / satellite |
|---|---|
