## U0795 | RECENT PROJECTS, ACTS AND INITIATIVES TO REDUCE ENVIRONMENTAL POLLUTION IN INDIA
### 1. ALTERNATIVES TO CONVENTIONAL POLLUTION CONTROL
| Tool | Type | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Green technology** | Alternative | Renewables, efficient kilns, **green cement (Portland limestone cement)**, alternative fuels |
| **CCS** | Alternative | Captures CO2 from **point sources** (e.g. cement plants); largely at **research stage** |
| **Bioremediation** | Alternative | Microbes/plants clean water and soil; eco-friendly, **cost-effective but slower**; results vary with local conditions |
| **Incineration** | **Conventional** | NOT an alternative (PYQ) |
!TRAP Incineration = conventional; green tech, CCS, bioremediation = alternatives.
#### 1.1 CCUS Roadmap
- **DST R&D Roadmap for CCUS** (Dec 2025): pilots 2025–30 → industrial integration 2030–35 → commercial scale 2035–45. DST approved five CCUS test beds (May 2025).
- Pilot: **NTPC Vindhyachal** captures ~20 t CO2/day. Budget 2026 proposed **₹20,000 crore** for CCUS.
### 2. RECENT LEGAL CHANGES
| Measure | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Act** | 2023 | Decriminalised minor offences under **Air Act and EP Act** – jail replaced by **monetary penalties** |
| Water Act amendment | 2024 | Decriminalised likewise |
| Environmental compensation | Aug 2025 | **SC upheld** PCB power under **Sec 33A Water Act** and **Sec 31A Air Act** |
| **Environmental (Protection) Fund Rules** | 2026 | Penalties go to Fund under **Sec 16 EPA**; shared **75% States/UTs : 25% Centre**; audited by **CAG** |
| Consent to Operate | Jan 2026 | Valid till cancelled |
- Shift from **command-and-control** to **trust-based, technology-enabled** regulation.
- Revised industry categories: new **Blue** category (e.g. **compressed biogas**); compulsory **33% green belt** replaced by pollution-potential norms.

### 4. CLEAN-ENERGY SCHEMES
| Scheme | Launch | Objective |
|---|---|---|
| **PM Ujjwala Yojana** | 2016 | Free LPG to poor households – less indoor smoke |
| **PM-KUSUM** | 2019 | Farm **solar pumps** and solar plants |
| **PM E-DRIVE** | 2024 | EV and charging incentives; **successor to FAME** |
| **PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana** | Feb 2024 | Rooftop solar for **1 crore** homes; up to **300 units** free a month; ₹75,021 crore |
- **UNEP-CCAC 'Hidden Assets'** (Sep 2026): PMUY a model clean-cooking programme; clean cooking/heating = South Asia's top solution to 2035; every **$1 → ~$15**.
### 5. ECO-LABELS AND GREEN CREDITS
| Label | By / year | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Ecomark** | **1991**, Government of India (environment ministry) | Voluntary label for environment-friendly products meeting Indian standards |
| **Ecomark Rules** | **26 Sep 2024**; replaced 1991 scheme | Correct labelling; stop **misleading green claims** |
| **Blue Flag** | **FEE (Foundation for Environmental Education), Denmark** | Beach eco-label; **8 Indian beaches** recommended in **2020** |
| **PRAMAAN** | – | Voluntary third-party **forest and wood** certification |
- Ecomark categories: **soaps and detergents, paper, plastics, cosmetics, aerosols** – NOT **drugs and antibiotics**.
- AP: **Rushikonda** (Visakhapatnam) – only AP Blue Flag beach (2020); status restored **March 2025**.
- **Green Credit Programme**: rules notified **Oct 2023**; administrator **ICFRE**; eight activities, **tree plantation** first operational.
!TRAP Blue Flag is by FEE, Denmark – not UNEP, MoEFCC or IUCN.
