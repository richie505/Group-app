### 6. YIELD PER HECTARE AND REGIONAL VARIATION
#### 6.1 Crop Yields vs Benchmarks
| Crop | India | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| **Rice** | about **2,929 kg/ha** | **China about 7,100** – India less than half |
| **Cotton** | about **440 kg/ha** | Global ~770; **Kapas Kanti** target **755** |
| Oilseeds | 1,398 kg/ha | – |
| Soybean | below 2 t/ha in many rainfed districts | – |
| **Groundnut** | **above global average** (exception) | – |
#### 6.2 High and Low Productivity States
- **Punjab**: **highest rice yield** (canal + tubewell irrigation despite low rainfall) and **highest wheat yield** (about 50 q/ha).
- **AP, Telangana, TN**: above national rice average.
- **Odisha, UP, Bihar** (eastern rice states): below the national average.
- **Pulses yields**: highest in **MP and Gujarat** (black/semi-arid soils, high SRR).
- **Highest average cotton yield**: **West Bengal**. **Black (Regur) soil** is best for cotton; covers most of the Deccan Plateau.
### 7. YIELD CONCEPTS, CAUSES OF GAPS AND PATHWAYS
#### 7.1 Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Cereal yield** | **kg per hectare of harvested land under cereals** |
| **Yield gap vs world** | Cereals, maize, soybean, pulses below global averages; exception **groundnut** (semi-arid, better varieties) |
| **TFP** | **Output growth not explained by growth in inputs** (land, labour, capital, fertiliser) |
#### 7.2 Causes and Determinants
| Factor | Effect / figure |
|---|---|
| **Irrigation** | Rice ~**67%** irrigated vs pulses ~**26%** |
| Inputs | Skewed NPK, low seed replacement (SRR), low mechanisation |
| **Post-harvest** | **~30% cold-chain losses** |
| Holding size | Small holdings limit mechanisation |
| **Climate** | ~**8% yield fall per 1°C** (or 20% rainfall deficit); wheat 6–10%; 1°C wipes ~**4 years** of rice/wheat gains |
| **El Niño (pulses)** | Acreage −2 to −9%; yield −5 to −25%; output −6 to −30% |
!TRAP Universal cold-chain access is NOT a cause of the yield gap; the lack of it is.
#### 7.3 Pathways
- **Seed**: SRR/VRR and **genome editing**.
- **Nutrition**: **Soil Health Card**.
- **Mechanisation**: **Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs)**.
### 8. TOTAL FACTOR PRODUCTIVITY
- TFP = output growth beyond input growth.
- **Drivers**: research and new varieties, irrigation, rural roads and markets, extension, education.
- Debate: growth comes **more from area expansion than yield**; growth now led by livestock/fisheries while crop growth (3.5%) lags.
### 9. AREA TRENDS – STAGNANT OR DECLINING CROPS
- **Cotton**: area down **18 lakh ha**; output **290.91 lakh bales (2025–26)** from about 350; Punjab cotton area down **37.5% to 80,000 ha** (2026); India became a **net importer of raw cotton**.
- **Pulses and oilseeds**: stagnant yields; **soybean area in MP fell 10%** (2023 to 2024).
- **Telangana**: cultivated area **1.31 crore acres (2014) → 2.2 crore acres (FY23)** via Kaleshwaram and Mission Kakatiya.
### 10. CROP MISSIONS, POLICIES AND COMMITTEES
| Mission | Launch | Target crops / area |
|---|---|---|
| **Technology Mission on Oilseeds** | **1986** | Oilseeds; pulses, maize added later; **Yellow Revolution (Sam Pitroda)** |
| **NFSM** | **Oct 2007 (2007–08)** | Rice, wheat, pulses, coarse cereals; later commercial crops, nutri-cereals; **renamed NFSNM in FY25**; credit is NOT an objective |
| **BGREI** (RKVY) | **2010–11** | Rice in **Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Odisha, eastern UP, West Bengal** |
| **INSIMP** (RKVY) | 2011–12 | **Millets** |
| **NMOOP** | **2014** | Oilseeds + oil palm |
| NMEO-Oil Palm / NMEO-Oilseeds | 2021 / 2024 | ~70 MT oilseeds by 2030–31 |
| **Mission for Aatmanirbharta in Pulses** | 2025–26 to 2030–31, ₹11,440 crore | **350 lakh t** from 310 lakh ha; **100% MSP procurement of tur, urad, masoor** |
| **PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana** | 2025–26 to 2030–31 | **100 low-productivity districts** |
- "**Tilhan Mission**" (**Feb 2020**): **oilseed production**.
- **National Commission on Agriculture (NCA)**: set up **1970** (chair N.R. Mirdha); final report **1976** (15 parts, incl. rural employment).
- **Doubling Farmers' Income Committee**: **Ashok Dalwai** (not Swaminathan); set up **2016**, report **2018**.
### 11. GREEN REVOLUTION OUTCOMES
- Benefited **wheat** first, then rice; regions **Punjab, Haryana, western UP**; widened **regional disparity** – eastern rice states still lag (hence BGREI).
