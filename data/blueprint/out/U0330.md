## U0330 | SARKARIA COMMISSION (1983) – RECOMMENDATIONS ON LEGISLATIVE, ADMINISTRATIVE AND FINANCIAL RELATIONS
### 1. COMMISSION AT A GLANCE
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Set up by | **Central Government**, **June 1983** |
| Chairman | Justice **Rajinder Singh Sarkaria**, **retired Supreme Court judge** (APPSC 2023) |
| Members | **B. Sivaraman**, **Dr S.R. Sen** (3 in all) |
| Objective | Examine the **relationship and balance of power** between Centre and States; suggest changes **within the existing constitutional framework** (APPSC 2018) |
| Worked | 1983-87 |
| Report | **January 1988**; **247 recommendations** |
- Subject = **Centre-State relations** – not police reform, State reorganisation or electoral reform (APPSC 2019).
!TRAP Report year is 1988; the Commission worked from 1983 to 1987.
### 2. RECOMMENDATIONS ON THE GOVERNOR
- An **eminent person** in some walk of life (APPSC 2017).
- **From outside the State**; not closely involved in local politics.
- **Not an active politician** in the recent past.
- **Chief Minister** to be effectively **consulted** in the appointment.
- **Secure 5-year term**, cut short only for **extremely compelling reasons**.
- Appointment strictly **non-partisan**; decide on **Bills within a specified period**.
!TRAP NOT recommended: Governor "should have **travelled extensively** in the State" (APPSC-G2 2017).
### 3. RECOMMENDATIONS ON ARTICLE 356
1. Use **very sparingly**, only in rare cases, as a **last resort**, after a **warning** to the State.
2. Doubt over majority → **floor test** in the Assembly, not the Governor's own view.
3. **Hung Assembly** → invite parties in a **set order of preference** (later endorsed by Punchhi, 2010).
- Courts relied on Sarkaria in **S.R. Bommai (1994)** and **Rameshwar Prasad (2006)**.
- Court quoted Sarkaria: Governors recommend President's Rule to "promote the political interests of the party in power at the Centre" (May 2026).
### 4. LEGISLATIVE RELATIONS
- **Concurrent List:** Centre to consult States through an **institutionalised process** of prior consultation before legislating.
- **Residuary powers (Art. 248):** **taxation stays with Parliament**; other residuary matters to the **Concurrent List**.
!PAIR Residuary – Sarkaria | Tax with Parliament, rest Concurrent | Centre 1983 || Residuary – Rajamannar | All to States | TN 1969
### 5. ADMINISTRATIVE RELATIONS
- **All-India Services:** **strengthen** them and **create more** such services – opposite of Rajamannar (abolish).
- **Central armed forces:** Centre may deploy in a State **even without the State's request**; consulting the State first is desirable.
### 6. FINANCIAL RELATIONS
- **Cesses and surcharges:** kept to a **minimum**, **time-bound**, then merged into the **shareable pool**.
- **Finance Commission and Planning Commission:** present division of functions found **reasonable**; to continue.
- **NDC** to be renamed **National Economic and Development Council**.
### 7. INSTITUTIONS SET UP AND IMPLEMENTATION
- **Permanent Inter-State Council** under **Art. 263**, proposed name "**Inter-Governmental Council**".
- ISC set up **28 May 1990** – the **main implemented** recommendation; ISC Secretariat set up **1991**.
- Status (Lok Sabha reply, 2013): of **247**, **180** accepted/implemented, **65 not accepted**, **2** under implementation.
!KEY Inter-State Council (1990) = Sarkaria's legacy, a permanent intergovernmental forum.
