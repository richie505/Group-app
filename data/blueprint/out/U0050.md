## U0050 | SATAVAHANA RELIGIOUS CONDITIONS – BUDDHISM, BRAHMANISM AND ROYAL PATRONAGE
### 1. THE BASIC RULE – KINGS VEDIC, QUEENS BUDDHIST
- Satavahana **kings followed the Vedic religion**; **queens patronised Buddhism** (G2 2008). Royal women supported both Buddhism and Jainism.
- **Religious pluralism**: the dynasty performed the **Ashvamedha** and also supported **Amaravati** and **Nagarjunakonda**.
- Buddhism depended more on **public support than royal support**.
- **Prakrit** (people's language) helped Buddhism become a mass movement.
| Patron | Religion |
|---|---|
| Satavahana rulers | **Vedic religion** |
| Satavahana queens | **Buddhism** |
| Pallavas | Rise of Brahmanism, downfall of Buddhism |
| Reddy period | **Veerasaivism** – the popular religion |
| Acharya Dignaga (as listed) | Brahmanism |
### 2. BRAHMANISM – VEDIC SACRIFICES
#### 2.1 Naneghat Inscription
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Issuer | **Devi Naganika**, wife of **Satakarni I** |
| Her father | Maharathi **Tranakayiro** (Amgiya family) |
| Language | **Prakrit** |
| Yajnas | Satakarni I – **two Ashvamedhas + one Rajasuya** |
| Titles of Satakarni I | **Apratihatachakra**, **Dakshinapathapati** |
| Deities invoked | Indra, Vasudeva, Sankarshana, Surya, Chandra, Yama, Varuna |
- **Vedic gods**: Indra, Yama, Varuna. **Bhagavata deities**: Vasudeva, Sankarshana.
- A queen performing such rites was unusual; she was effectively ruling as guardian of her son.
#### 2.2 Other Vedic Evidence
- Yajna-performing kings: **Satakarni I, Satakarni II, Gautamiputra, Yajna Sri**.
- Names **Vedasri** and **Yajnasri** themselves show Vedic faith.
- **Nasik inscription** calls Gautamiputra **Agama nilaya** (abode of traditional learning).
!TRAP Apratihatachakra / Dakshinapathapati = Satakarni I (Naneghat); Agama nilaya = Gautamiputra (Nasik).
### 3. BHAGAVATA RELIGION AND SHIVA WORSHIP
#### 3.1 Bhagavata Religion
- Originator **Krishna (Vasudeva)**, a **Vrishni (Satvat)** descendant from **Mathura**; son of **Devaki**, disciple of **Ghor Angirasa** (Chhandogya Upanishad).
- Centred at **Mathura**; reached Andhra under **Krishna** (the 2nd Satavahana king); called the "**Vasudeva religion**" by Hala's time.
- Gautamiputra's inscriptions carry **Bhagavata symbols**.
#### 3.2 Gudimallam
- **Parasurameswara temple, Gudimallam** (Yerpedu mandal; Chittoor, now **Tirupati district**) = **oldest Shiva temple**; has the **oldest Shivalinga** of the Satavahana period (G2 2008).
!TRAP Gudimallam distractors: Thondawada, Renigunta, Jogulamba.
### 4. BUDDHISM IN ANDHRA – ARRIVAL AND SECTS
- Buddhism reached Andhra in the **6th century BC**; local form called "**Andhra Buddhism**"; doctrine **Pratitya Samutpada**.
- Tibetan tradition: Buddha preached at Amaravati at the request of king **Subendran**.
- **Mahadeva Bhikshu** = first Buddhist acharya in Andhra; founded the **Chaityaka** sect (worship of chaityas).
| Sect | Centre / note |
|---|---|
| **Chaityaka** (Chaityakavada) | Founder Mahadeva Bhikshu |
| **Mahasanghika** | **Dhanyakataka**; followers called Mahasanghis / Chaityakas |
| **Purvasaila, Aparasaila** | Hills around Dhanyakataka |
| **Rajagirika** | **Sriparvatam** (G2 2012 key) |
| **Bhadrayaniya** | **Nasik** |
- **Sriparvata = Nagarjunakonda**.
- **Sri Parvataswamy** identified with the **Buddha** by **Kielhorn** (G2 2019 key). The Vishnukundins' family deity was also Sriparvataswami.
!TRAP Another APPSC key credits **N. Venkataramanayya** with describing Sri Parvataswamy as the Buddha; the G2 2019 key gave **Kielhorn**.
### 5. ROYAL AND QUEENS' GIFTS TO THE SANGHA
| Patron | Gift |
|---|---|
| **Gautami Balasri** | Cave vihara at **Nasik** for the **Bhadrayaniya** sect (for her son's recovery) |
| **Gautamiputra Satakarni** | Village at **Tirasmri** and 100 nivartanas to monks; toranas of Sanchi |
| **Pulumavi II** | Village to **Mahasanghika** monks at **Karle** |
| **Yajna Sri Satakarni** | **Sriparvata vihara / Mahachaitya** for Nagarjuna (**1,500 rooms**) |
| **Krishna** (2nd king) | **Nasik cave** for shramanas; appointed a **Dharma Mahamatra** |
- **Nasik, Karle and Kanheri** caves are in **Maharashtra**.
### 6. DONORS BY CATEGORY
- **Queens** were key patrons; kings gave villages; **Yavana merchants** funded caves at Karle, Bhaja, Nasik, Junnar and Kanheri.
- **Guild banking**: guilds at **Nasik, Junnar, Kanheri** held money on deposit and paid **interest to monks** – e.g. **Ushavadata's** deposit with the **Govardhana weavers**.
- **Alluru inscription**: of the Ikshvaku **Virapurushadatta**; records the emperor's donations to many communities and institutions (G2 2017).
!TRAP Alluru distractors: Karle, Veerapuram, Ramagiri.
### 7. ACHARYA NAGARJUNA
| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| Works | **Suhrullekha** ("friendly letter"), **Ratnavali**, Mulamadhyamakakarika |
| Philosophy | **Madhyamika / Sunyavada** (Soonya Vaadam) school of Mahayana |
| Patron | **Yajna Sri Satakarni** (close friend; Suhrullekha addressed to him) |
| Place | **Sriparvata (Nagarjunakonda)** |
- **Lilavati Parinayam**: a Satavahana king supported him. **Kathasaritsagara**: Nagarjuna was killed by a Satavahana prince.
### 8. JAINISM – KUNDAKUNDACHARYA
- **Kundakundacharya** preached **Syadvada** (G2 2008) and wrote **Samayasara**.
