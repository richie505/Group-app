## U0057 | VISHNUKUNDIN ART AND ARCHITECTURE – UNDAVALLI, MOGALRAJAPURAM AND BHAIRAVAKONDA CAVES
### 1. CAVE TEMPLES – DYNASTY, DEITY, LOCATION
- **Vishnukundins** = **first Hindu cave temples in the Telugu states**.
- **Guptas** = earliest Hindu cave temples in India (**Udayagiri, Madhya Pradesh**).
- **Madhava Varma I** is credited with Undavalli, Bhairavakonda and Mogalrajapuram.
| Cave | Dynasty | Deity / sect | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Undavalli** | **Vishnukundins** (G2 2025) | **Ananta Padmanabha Swamy** (Vishnu); earlier Jain, Buddhist | Tadepalli mandal, **Guntur** district; **Krishna** bank near **Vijayawada** (Amaravati capital area) |
| **Mogalrajapuram** | **Vishnukundins** | **Trimurti, Durga, Nataraja**; mainly Shaiva | **Vijayawada** (NTR district) |
| **Bhairavakonda** | Vishnukundin (MV I); Pallava per some historians | **Shiva**, **Kapalika** sect | **Nellore** (exam key, G2 2012); Nallamala hills on the Nellore–Prakasam border |
| **Akkanna-Madanna** | Vishnukundins | Shaiva | **Indrakeeladri**, Vijayawada, on the Krishna |
| Bojjannakonda | – | Buddhist | Visakhapatnam |
| Keesaragutta, Chaitanyapuri | Vishnukundin sites | – | Hyderabad |
- Brahmanical caves of Mogalrajapuram and Undavalli = **Vishnukundins** (G2 2019); Hindu, mainly **Vaishnavite** support.
!TRAP Ikshvakus, Salankayanas, Pallavas, Cholas are distractors for Undavalli/Mogalrajapuram.
!KEY APPSC GS 2019: Undavalli is near **Vijayawada** on the **Krishna** (not Godavari); revenue district Guntur.
### 2. UNDAVALLI CAVES
#### 2.1 Structure and Sculpture
- **Four storeys**, **three main caves**; rock-cut multi-storeyed cave.
- **Carved top-down**: upper levels finely detailed, ground floor left **crude and unfinished**.
- Centrepiece: **monolithic reclining Vishnu** (Ananta Padmanabha Swamy), about **5 m (16 ft)**.
- Date: **4th–5th c. CE**; regional form of the **Gupta rock-cut style**; shows **Pallava influence**.
#### 2.2 Changing Religious Use
1. **Jain abode** →
2. Used by **Buddhist monks** (a Buddha image was replaced by Vishnu) →
3. Made a **Hindu temple**.
!TRAP "Built by the Cholas as a Hindu temple, later Buddhist" is FALSE – the order was Jain → Buddhist → Hindu.
#### 2.3 In News
- AP government's two-day **Telugu Mahotsavam** at Undavalli Caves, **25–26 July 2026**; Minister **Kandula Durgesh** said it will become an annual festival in every region.
### 3. MOGALRAJAPURAM CAVES
| Cave | Feature |
|---|---|
| Caves 1–3 | Sanctums behind a **pillared mandapa**, for the **Trimurti** (Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva) |
| Cave 4 | **Durga** cave; **Ardhanarishvara** on the back wall |
| Cave 5 (**largest**) | **Shiva Tandava Nataraja** |
- **Five caves** in all; Trimurti figures on the **kapota** (eave) above pillar capitals.
### 4. BHAIRAVAKONDA CAVES
- **Eight rock-cut caves**, all dedicated to **Shiva** (G2 2012).
- Centre of **Shaivism** (G2 2008) – specifically the **Kapalika** sect.
- **Attribution debate:** Madhava Varma I (Vishnukundin) vs **Pallava period (7th–8th c.)**; closely resembles early Pallava caves of **Mahabalipuram**.
- Pillars: **lion-based** with **kumbha (pot) capitals**.
!NUM 4 storeys = Undavalli | 5 caves = Mogalrajapuram | 8 caves = Bhairavakonda
!TRAP Bhairavakonda caves: 8, not 4, 9 or 12; Shaiva, not Buddhist, Jain or Vaishnava.
### 5. ARCHITECTURE FEATURES
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pillared mandapa (ardhamandapa)** | Hall in front of rock-cut **garbhagriha** cells – usual Vishnukundin plan |
| **Kumbha capitals** | Pot capitals on lion-based pillars (Bhairavakonda) |
| **Kapota** | Decorated eave above capitals (Mogalrajapuram) |
| **Dwarapalas** | Door guardians flanking shrine entrances |
- Style: mix of **Ikshvaku and Vakataka** traditions with **Pallava** borrowings.
- Structural temples: **brick**, with garbhagriha, antarala-mandapa, mukhamandapa (**Keesaragutta**).
### 6. VISHNUKUNDIN VS PALLAVA CAVES
| Point | Vishnukundin caves | Pallava (Mahendravarman I) caves |
|---|---|---|
| Date | **4th–6th c.** | **7th c. CE** |
| Plan | Mandapa + garbhagriha; storeyed at Undavalli | Mandapa + garbhagriha |
| Pillars | Lion pedestals, pot capitals | **Lion-based (simha-mukha)** pillars |
| Influence | Gupta-Vakataka base | Later used by **Telugu Chodas** and **Eastern Chalukyas** |
!PAIR Vishnukundin | 4th–6th c. | Undavalli || Mahendravarman I | 7th c. | lion pillars
