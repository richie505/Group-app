## U0756 | WIND ENERGY – POTENTIAL, INSTALLED CAPACITY, OFFSHORE WIND POLICY, HYBRID WIND-SOLAR POLICY
### 1. HOW A WIND TURBINE MAKES POWER
- Energy conversion: wind's **kinetic energy** → **rotational (mechanical) energy** of blades/rotor → **electrical energy** in the generator.
- Turbine power varies with the **cube of wind speed** (and with swept area).
- **Betz limit**: at most **16/27 (about 59.3%)** of wind's kinetic energy can be captured.
#### 1.1 HAWT vs VAWT
| Feature | HAWT | VAWT |
|---|---|---|
| Axis | **Parallel to the wind** (horizontal) | **Vertical** |
| Design | 3-blade; **needs yaw control** | **Darrieus, Savonius** |
| Wind direction | Must face the wind | **Takes wind from any direction** |
| Use | Standard utility turbine | Small sites; **lower efficiency** |
!TRAP Darrieus and Savonius are VAWT designs, not HAWT.
#### 1.2 Capacity Factor and Turbine Classes
- **Capacity Utilisation Factor (CUF)** = actual output ÷ output at full rated power over the period.
| Source | CUF in India |
|---|---|
| Wind | about **36–40%** |
| Solar | about **21%** |
| Coal | about **85%** |
- **Turbine classes (IEC I, II, III)**: set by site wind speed – Class I for high-wind, Class III for low-wind sites.
### 2. ONSHORE VS OFFSHORE WIND
| Point | Onshore | Offshore |
|---|---|---|
| Location | On land | Sea / large lakes |
| Winds | Variable | **Stronger, steadier** → higher capacity factor |
| Cost | **Cheaper**, easy upkeep; cheapest new electricity globally (costs fell 4% in 2025) | **Costly**, harder to maintain |
| Issues | Land conflict | **Rusting, undersea cables, navigation, marine ecology** |
- **Floating turbines** are used in deep water.

- Functions: wind R&D, **wind resource assessment**, **Wind Atlas**, identifying potential sites, testing and certification.
- **Nodal agency** for offshore wind (not SECI).

### 7. OFFSHORE WIND POLICY
- **National Offshore Wind Energy Policy, 2015**: framework for offshore farms in India's **EEZ**; **nodal ministry MNRE**, **nodal agency NIWE** (not SECI).
- Identified zones: off **Gujarat** coast and **Tamil Nadu** coast (incl. **Gulf of Mannar**).
- **VGF scheme for offshore wind (2024)**: **₹7,453 crore** for the first **1 GW** – **500 MW each off Gujarat and Tamil Nadu**.
| Partner | Cooperation |
|---|---|
| UK | **India-UK Offshore Wind Taskforce**, Feb 2026 (Vision 2035) |
| Denmark | 2019 agreement; MoU renewed May 2025 |
- Slow offshore take-off: high cost and port/grid infrastructure.
### 8. WIND-SOLAR HYBRID POLICY AND ROUND-THE-CLOCK POWER
- **National Wind-Solar Hybrid Policy, 2018**: large grid-connected wind-solar PV hybrids – wind turbines and solar PV at the **same point of grid connection**; better land and transmission use, less variability.
- **Hybrid parks**: e.g. **Ramagiri** (Ananthapuramu, AP) hybrid solar-wind park.
- **Round-the-clock (RTC) tenders**: first by **SECI in 2020** – RE bundled with thermal; at least **51%** of annual energy from renewables (incl. storage).
- **ISTS charges waived** for projects commissioned by **30 June 2025**.
- **Intra-State GEC-I** (from 2015): **AP**, Gujarat, HP, Karnataka, MP, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu (**Odisha not included**).
### 9. REPOWERING POLICY
- **Repowering** = replacing old, less efficient turbines with **larger modern turbines at the same site** to raise output.
- **National Repowering Policy, 2016** → revised as **Repowering and Life Extension Policy, 2023**.
- Other supports: accelerated depreciation, GBI, **Wind RPO trajectory to 2030**.
!NUM POLICY ORDER: Offshore policy 2015 → Repowering 2016 → Hybrid 2018 → Repowering and Life Extension 2023 → Offshore VGF 2024
### 10. AP WIND ENERGY
- AP wind cluster in **Rayalaseema**: **Ananthapuramu, Kurnool, YSR Kadapa**.
- Capacity: **4,397.67 MW** (2025-26; up from 3,636.48 MW in 2024-25) – AP's **second-largest RE source after solar**; 4,461 MW, **6th in India** (Jun 2026).
- **Suzlon** = **largest wind developer in AP** (over **1,700 MW**); S144 blade unit at Hulikera, Kanekal mandal, Anantapur opened 25 Aug 2026.
- CEA: AP wind to reach 8,257 MW; 4,200 MW more needed for RPO up to 2035-36.
- AP is in Intra-State GEC-I; Ramagiri hybrid park.
### 11. ISSUES AND DEVELOPMENTS
- Grid oscillations from RE variability – one from Rajasthan was felt at Kudankulam (28 Feb 2026).
- Ageing turbines, curtailment, slow offshore growth.
- El Nino and a weak monsoon expected to dent wind and hydro output (Jul 2026).
- Industry seeks PLI for large forgings and export incentives (Aug 2026).
