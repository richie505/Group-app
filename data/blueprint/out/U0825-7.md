### 1. HOW INDEX QUESTIONS ARE ASKED
- **Rank + total countries + publisher** are asked together; always quote the **edition year**.
- A rank without its edition is a trap: many indices are quoted for two or three years in articles.
!KEY Exam rule: learn every index as a triple – Publisher | India's rank of total | Edition year.

### 3. DEVELOPMENT AND HUNGER FRAMEWORKS – HOW THEY ARE BUILT
#### 3.1 Human Development Index
- Designed by **Mahbub ul Haq** with **Amartya Sen**; HDR by **UNDP since 1990**.
- Income measure: **GNI per capita (PPP)**.
- Dimensions combined by **geometric mean**.
#### 3.2 Global Hunger Index
- Publisher: **Concern Worldwide + Welthungerhilfe**; **4 indicators**, **0–100 scale** (0 = best).
| Indicator | Weight |
|---|---|
| **Undernourishment** | **1/3** |
| **Under-five mortality** | **1/3** |
| Child stunting | 1/6 |
| Child wasting | 1/6 |
!TRAP GHI weights: 1/3 + 1/3 (undernourishment, under-5 mortality) and 1/6 + 1/6 (stunting, wasting).
#### 3.3 Human Capital Index (World Bank)
- Components: **survival**, **quality-adjusted schooling**, **health**.

### 5. DEMOCRACY INDICES AND THE DEBATE ON RANKINGS
- **V-Dem 2026**: India **106th of 179** on the Electoral Democracy Index – classed an **"electoral autocracy"**; score lowest since 1975.
- India **contests perception-based indices**:
  - **V-Dem** – relies on **expert opinion**.
  - **EPI** – **ignores per-capita emissions**.
- Newest releases: Global Gender Gap 2026 (India 131st, 17 Sep 2026), V-Dem 2026 (106th), EPI 2026 (176th).
