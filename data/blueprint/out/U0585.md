### 3. KADAPA STEEL PLANT
- Act: SAIL to examine within six months an integrated steel plant in **YSR district**.
- SAIL's feasibility report (**2 Dec 2014**): **prima facie not financially viable**; a Task Force (SAIL, RINL, NMDC, MECON, MSTC) followed on 19 Oct 2016.
- The State formed its own SPV **AP High Grade Steels Ltd** (Nov 2019) for a 3 MTPA plant.
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 2004-09 (YSR era) | Land allotted to **Brahmani Steels**; stalled |
| **Dec 2018** | Foundation stone by CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu** at **M. Kambala Dinne, Mylavaram mandal** |
| 2019 and 2023 | Stones laid again by CM **Y.S. Jagan Mohan Reddy** |
| **3 Jul 2026** | **JSW Rayalaseema Integrated Steel Plant** works launched at **Sunnapurallapalli and Peddandluru**, Jammalamadugu constituency |
- JSW plant: **Rs 16,350 crore** in two phases (Rs 4,500 crore + Rs 11,850 crore); 1,100 acres; **scrap-based Electric Arc Furnace** green steel; water **2 tmc ft/year from Gandikota** (15 km pipeline); target 2028.
!TRAP 2018 stone = M. Kambala Dinne; 2026 JSW plant = Sunnapurallapalli.
### 4. DUGARAJAPATNAM PORT
- **Only port named** in the Schedule for a new major port by GoI.
- Dropped as non-feasible: a 2016 consultant study (AECOM) found it **financially and technically not viable**; risk to **Pulicat Lake** and security of **Satish Dhawan Space Centre (Sriharikota)**.
- Replacement: State built **Ramayapatnam** on its own.
- Revival (2026):
  - G.O. (2 Jan 2026): **APMB** to set up greenfield port + **mega shipbuilding cluster**, **Tirupati district**, under the Centre's **Shipbuilding Development Scheme (SbDS)**.
  - SPV: **National Shipbuilding and Heavy Industries Park Andhra Pradesh Ltd** (2 Mar 2026) = **50:50 JV of VPA and APMB**; ~3,500 acres; cluster Rs 30,000 crore.
  - One of **five** national cluster sites (others: **TN, Gujarat, Odisha, Maharashtra**).
!TRAP Act port ("shall develop") = Duggirajupatnam; built substitute = Ramayapatnam.

### 6. REFINERY-CUM-PETROCHEMICAL COMPLEX
- **BPCL** complex near Ramayapatnam port, between **Prakasam and Nellore**; villages **Chevuru and Ravuru**.
- Cost ~**Rs 1.45 lakh crore** (earlier ~Rs 1 lakh crore); **9 MTPA**; ~5.4 MT Euro-6 fuels.
- **Kakinada**: GAIL-HPCL signed an MoU (Jan 2017) for a ~Rs 32,901 crore complex; the Centre said a **viability gap funding** of Rs 5,615 crore was needed and asked the State to pay it; AP refused.
- Kakinada petrochemical MoUs cleared by the State pollution board; Kakinada lies in the **Visakhapatnam-Kakinada PCPIR**.
### 7. METRO RAIL
- Agency: **Andhra Pradesh Metro Rail Corporation Ltd (APMRCL)**.
- Visakhapatnam metro: **4 corridors, 76.70 km, Rs 11,498 crore**; Phase-1 needs 99.11 acres (only 7.57 private); Rs 600 crore sought; double-decker metro-cum-road on NH-16 surveyed.
- Jan 2026 Governor's address: Visakhapatnam and Vijayawada metros approved by the **State**.
### 8. CURRENT DEBATE
- 20 May 2026: Y.S. Sharmila - promises worth ~Rs 5 lakh crore unfulfilled (Kadapa steel, Dugarajapatnam, metro).
- Kadapa steel + Ramayapatnam port + BPCL refinery, linked by a new rail line = southern AP industrial cluster.
