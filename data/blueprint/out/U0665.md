- District answers change between the 13, 26 (2022) and 28 (2025) district maps.
### 2. OFFICIAL LANGUAGES OF AP
- First official language: **Telugu** (AP Official Languages Act, **1966**).
- **Urdu** = **second official language** – AP Official Languages (Amendment) Bill, **2022**, extended it to **all districts**.
- Earlier, Urdu was second official language in only **15 districts** of joint AP.
!TRAP Urdu is the second official language of AP in **all** districts since 2022, not only in Kurnool or Guntur.
### 3. RELIGIOUS COMPOSITION (2011)

|---|---|
| Hindus | about **90.9%** |
| Muslims | about **7.3%** |
| Christians | about **1.4%** |
- **Kurnool** district: about **16.5% Muslim** – a major Muslim concentration of Rayalaseema.
- Linguistic minority is judged at the **State level** (**TMA Pai case**).
### 4. TELUGU AND INDIA'S LANGUAGES
- Telugu = **most widely spoken Dravidian language**.
- **4th** in India in **Census 2011** (about **6.7%**), after **Hindi, Bengali, Marathi**.
- India 2011: **121 languages**, **270 mother tongues**; Hindi **43.6%**, Bengali **8%**.
- Telugu is one of the **original 14** languages of the **Eighth Schedule**.
!TRAP Telugu = **4th** in 2011, not 3rd (its 2001 position).
### 5. LINGUISTIC POCKETS AND BORDER DISTRICTS
| District / place | Borders | Language |
|---|---|---|
| **Srikakulam** | Odisha | **Odia** |
| **Chittoor / Tirupati** | Tamil Nadu | **Tamil** |
| **Anantapur** | Karnataka | **Kannada** |
| **Kurnool** | Karnataka | **Kannada** (not Urdu) |
| Yanam (Puducherry) | next to East Godavari | Telugu enclave |
- Tribal dialects **Savara, Gadaba, Koya** in the Eastern Ghats – documented by **CTUAP** (Central Tribal University of AP).
- **Bellary** district (1953): Telugu areas to Andhra, Kannada areas to Mysore.
