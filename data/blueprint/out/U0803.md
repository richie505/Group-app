## U0803 | NANOTECHNOLOGY – NATURE, SCOPE, APPLICATIONS, NANO MISSION, HEALTH AND ENVIRONMENTAL CONCERNS
### 1. NANOSCALE – MEANING AND PROPERTIES
- **1 nm = 10^-9 m** (one billionth of a metre); **0.1-1 nm** = atomic scale.
- **Nanoparticle size: 1-100 nm** (APPSC 2018) – at least one dimension **less than 10^-7 m**.
- New properties come from **quantum effects** and a very high **surface-area-to-volume ratio** → more reactive, different optical/electrical behaviour.
- Most important property of nanomaterials (RAS 2013 key): **friction**.
!KEY Nanoparticle = 1 to 100 nm, i.e. 10^-9 to 10^-7 m.

### 3. CARBON ALLOTROPES AND NANOMATERIALS
| Allotrope | Bonding / structure | Key property |
|---|---|---|
| **Diamond** | sp³; **tetrahedral** 3D network, each C bonded to four | **Hardest natural substance** (Mohs 10); **electrical insulator**; excellent **thermal conductor**; transparent, high refractive index |
| **Graphite** | sp² layers held by **weak van der Waals forces** | **Soft, slippery** (layers slide); opaque, black; good conductor; **lubricant** for heavy machines |
| **Fullerene C60** | sp² hollow cage; **12 pentagons, 20 hexagons**, 60 C atoms | "**Buckyball**"; semiconductor |
| **Graphene** | **Single-atom-thick** sp² layer; **2D hexagonal honeycomb** | **Strongest** material; excellent conductor, flexible, transparent |
| **Carbon nanotubes** | **Rolled graphene** cylinders (1D tubes) | Very strong; **carry drugs and antigens** |
| Amorphous carbon | Coal, charcoal, coke, lampblack | Activated charcoal – high surface area |
- Diamond: least compressible and best heat conductor among natural materials; insulator (no free electrons) – two separate facts.
- **Wurtzite boron nitride** is believed harder than diamond.
- **Graphene** = pure **carbon** (not silicon), extracted from graphite.
- **Fullerene** = carbon allotrope (**not a fluorine compound**).

### 4. TOOLS AND SYNTHESIS
| Tool / route | Fact |
|---|---|
| **STM** | Images atoms on **conducting** surfaces only |
| **AFM** (1986) | Works on **non-conductors** too |
| **Top-down** | Cut bulk down – **lithography, milling, etching** |
| **Bottom-up** | Build from atoms – **self-assembly**, CVD, sol-gel |
!PAIR Microscopes: STM | 1981 | Conductors only || AFM | 1986 | Non-conductors too
### 5. GENERATIONS OF NANOPRODUCTS (M.C. ROCO)
| Generation | Nature | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Passive** nanostructures | **Aerosols**, colloids, polymers, ceramics, coatings, nanocomposites |
| **II** | **Active** nanostructures | **Targeted drugs**, sensors, 3-D transistors, amplifiers |
| **III** | **Systems of nanosystems** | **Robotics, 3-D networking**, nanoelectronics, nanomedicine |
| **IV** | **Molecular nanosystems** | **Molecular manufacturing** |
!KEY Order: Aerosol → Targeted drugs → 3D networking → Molecular manufacturing.
### 6. NANO DEVICES – NANOGENERATORS AND NANOSENSORS
- **NCL Pune nanogenerator**: electricity when pressure is applied → **piezoelectric effect** (APPSC 2018, 2019).
- **Quartz crystal in watches** = piezoelectric effect (charge from mechanical stress).
- **Gold-nanoparticle** lateral-flow kits (Transasia, Mylab): detect **malaria, dengue, typhoid** in 15 minutes.
- **Graphene** bendable **wearable sensors** (IISc + C-DAC).
!TRAP The NCL nanogenerator is piezoelectric – NOT photoelectric or thermoelectric.

### 9. NANO FERTILISERS
- **Urea** = most popular fertiliser for **foliar application** (high solubility, quick leaf absorption).
| Product | Facts |
|---|---|
| **Nano Urea (IFFCO)** | Liquid, **20-50 nm**, **foliar spray**; N-use efficiency **~70% vs 30-35%**; cuts urea use up to **~25%**; FCO specs 4% (24 Feb 2021), 8% (2 Mar 2023), 16% (15 Apr 2024); sales 11.31 crore bottles (30 Sep 2025) |
| **Nano DAP** | Notified under **FCO on 2 March 2023**; **seed treatment and foliar** use; sales 3.19 crore bottles (30 Sep 2025) |
- Nano urea **does not fully replace** conventional urea; criticised for **quick rollout without long-term studies**.

### 11. CONCERNS AND REGULATION
- **Nanotoxicity**: particles enter cells and organs → **oxidative stress, inflammation**, long-term toxicity, **persistence and bioaccumulation**; unclear food-chain entry.
| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Natural** | **Volcanic ash, sea spray, smoke** |
| **Engineered** | **Nano-silver**, CNTs, TiO2 |
- **No dedicated nano law**; frameworks: **DGFASLI** guidelines (occupational), **FSSAI 2022** guidelines, **SCOMET** list (dual-use).
- **Guidelines for Evaluation of Nano-based Agri-input and Food Products** (2020): by **DBT**, Agriculture Ministry and **FSSAI**.
- Concern of a "**nano-divide**".
