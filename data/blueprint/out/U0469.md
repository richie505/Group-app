## U0469 | TRENDS IN INDIA'S NATIONAL INCOME, PER CAPITA INCOME AND SECTORAL SHARES; INTER-STATE GSDP COMPARISON
### 1. KEY TERMS AND STRUCTURAL SHIFT
#### 1.1 Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **"Hindu rate of growth"** | About **3.5%** a year during **1950-80** |
| **"Miracle economies"** | **7%+** growth for a decade or more |
| Income class | India is still **lower-middle income** (World Bank, **GNI per capita, Atlas method**; classes updated every **1 July**) |
| World Bank income groups | Low, lower-middle, upper-middle, high |
| Low income economy (World Development Report 1994) | Per capita GNP **US $725 or less** |
- **GDP does NOT measure income distribution**; dollar rankings move with exchange rates and base revisions.
#### 1.2 Sectoral Shift – Then and Now
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Largest contributor at Independence | **Agriculture** (APPSC 2023) |
| Industry share, 1950-51 | About **13%** |
| Industry share, 2006-07 | APPSC key: **20-25%** |
| Agriculture share by 2017-18 | About **17%** (APPSC 2019) |
| Largest now | **Services – over 50%** of GDP |
| Order now | **Services > Industry > Agriculture** |
| Services' share of GVA growth | About **69.0%** (2011-12 to 2015-16); about **72.5%** (2017-18) |
!KEY Largest source of national income: by sector = services; at Independence = agriculture.
### 2. LARGEST SOURCE OF NATIONAL INCOME – INSTITUTIONAL SECTORS
| Institutional sector | Share of GVA |
|---|---|
| **Household** (largely unorganised) | **45.5%** (2011-12); **44.8%** (2014-15) |
| Private corporate | **33.9%**, then **35.9%** (2014-15) |
- Largest institutional sector = **household sector** (largely unorganised).
- **Private sector far exceeds the public sector.**
!TRAP Largest institutional sector is the household sector, not the private corporate or public sector.
### 3. GROWTH RECORD BY PERIOD
#### 3.1 Long-run Record
- **1950-51**: GDP growth **3.6%** (APPSC-GS 2021).
- Trend is **not a steady rise**; contractions in **1979-80** (drought + oil shock) and **2020-21** (COVID-19), followed by a **sharp rebound in 2021-22**.
- **Since 1991**: nominal GDP at market prices grew **almost 12 times** (APPSC 2018).
- Economic Survey 2020-21 (IMF estimate): real growth **11.5%** for 2021-22 and **6.8%** for 2022-23.
#### 3.2 Real GDP Growth – Recent Years
| Year | Real GDP growth |
|---|---|
| 2022-23 | 7.2% (older series) |
| 2023-24 | First printed **8.2%** (2024, APPSC-G2 2025 key); later 9.2% (old series); **7.2%** (new series) |
| 2024-25 | **7.1%** (new series; old 6.5%) |
| 2025-26 | FAE **7.4%** → SAE **7.6%** (Feb 2026); Q3 7.8%; Provisional Estimates (5 Jun 2026) **7.7%** |
| Q1 2026-27 | Real GDP **7.8%** (RBI projected 7.0%); real GVA **8.2%**; nominal GDP **10.3%**; manufacturing 9.2%; tertiary GVA 10.0% |
#### 3.3 New GDP Series (2026)
- **Reduced nominal GDP and per capita income**; raised FY26 growth to **7.6%**.
- Cut economy size by a cumulative **₹43 lakh crore** over four years since 2022-23; Opposition questioned the deflator.
- Raised agriculture GVA by 3.8-5.9%; cut trade by 36%.
- Sector growth FY 2025-26: agriculture **3.1%** (4.6% the year before).
!TRAP 2023-24 growth in the APPSC key = 8.2% (first printed estimate); later series changed it.
### 4. SIZE OF THE ECONOMY AND PER CAPITA INCOME
#### 4.1 Size
- 2025-26: about **₹345 lakh crore ≈ USD 3.9 trillion** (new series); earlier estimate ₹357 lakh crore (about USD 4 trillion).
#### 4.2 Per Capita Income
- **Per capita income = National income ÷ Population.**
- Per capita national income 2025-26 (FAE, Jan 2026, current prices): **₹2,47,487**, up **₹16,025** over the previous year.
- At **current prices** PCI rises faster than at **constant prices** (constant removes inflation).
- Reasons for slow PCI growth: population growth, low productivity in agriculture, large unorganised sector.
- The 2026 new series **lowered PCI**.
### 5. INDIA'S GLOBAL RANK AND TARGETS
#### 5.1 Rank
| Measure | India's rank |
|---|---|
| **Nominal GDP** | **4th** – overtook **Japan** in 2025 (USD **4.18 trillion**); above India: USA, China, Germany |
| **PPP GDP** | **3rd** – after **China and USA** (ICP 2021; World Bank 2024; APPSC 2025); 2nd in Asia-Pacific |
- **Second largest economy** after USA = **China** (Japan was 2nd in 2005).
- **ICP**: run by **UNSD**; World Bank implements; **NSO** nodal agency in India; ICP 2024 in progress (APPSC-GS 2025).
- IMF WEO April 2025: India's nominal GDP 2025 about **USD 4.187 trillion**.
#### 5.2 Targets
- Projected **3rd largest by 2028**; **upper-middle income by 2030**; new-series cut pushes milestones further away.
- **USD 5 trillion**: IMF (Nov 2025) now expects it in **FY29 (2028-29)**, a year later than earlier; weak rupee is the main reason.
- **Viksit Bharat @2047**: developed nation by the 100th year of Independence.
### 6. ECONOMIC SURVEY AND GROWTH DRIVERS
- **Economic Survey 2025-26** (tabled **29 Jan 2026**; CEA **V. Anantha Nageswaran**): FY27 growth **6.8-7.2%**; potential growth raised to **7%**; FY27 called a **"year of adjustment"**.
- Economic Survey: retail inflation **1.7%** (Apr-Dec 2025, decade low); bank GNPA **2.2%**, multi-decade low.
- **Drivers of outlook (N1)**: private consumption (**PFCE**), public **capex** (3.1% of GDP in FY26; up 28% YoY in Apr-Nov 2025) and private investment, services and travel indicators, monsoon and rabi prospects.

### 10. INTER-STATE COMPARISON AND ANDHRA PRADESH
#### 10.1 States
- Crossed **upper-middle-income threshold (USD 4,636)**: **Karnataka, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Delhi** (**not Kerala**); India USD 2,760.
- Largest state economies by GSDP (2024-25): **Maharashtra** 1st, **Tamil Nadu** 2nd, then Karnataka and Gujarat; Maharashtra had the slowest growth of the big four since 2021-22; Karnataka grew fastest (about 65%).
- **Divergence**: Gini of State PCI rose **0.230 (1994-95) → 0.261 (2025-26)**; growth since 1994-95: middle-income States **36.7x**, richest 28.3x, poorest 26.6x.
- **16th Finance Commission**: new **contribution criterion = square root of GSDP**; keeps per capita GSDP **income-distance** criterion; devolution stays **41%**.
#### 10.2 EAC-PM Report (Sep 2024)
- "**Relative Economic Performance of Indian States: 1960-61 to 2023-24**" (Sanjeev Sanyal, Aakanksha Arora).
- Share of national GDP 2023-24: **Telangana 4.9%** (up from 3.8% in 2010-11); **Andhra Pradesh about 4.7%**, broadly flat after bifurcation.
- Relative PCI 2023-24: Telangana **193.6%**, AP **131.6%** of national average; top: Delhi, Telangana, Karnataka, Haryana.
#### 10.3 Andhra Pradesh vs India
| Item | AP | India |
|---|---|---|
| GSDP 2025-26 (current) | **₹17.62 lakh crore** (growth 10.75%) | ₹357.14 lakh crore (8.0%) |
| Constant-price growth 2024-25 (FAE) | 9.24% | 6.4% |
| Rank by GSDP | **8th** | – |
- AP's PCI about **1.34 times** India's (2025-26 FAE); AP's GSDP growth has outpaced the national average.
#### 10.4 Swarna Andhra @2047
- Vision unveiled Dec 2024 (CM N. Chandrababu Naidu): GSDP **USD 2.4 trillion** (about ₹308 lakh crore) by **2047**; per capita income about **USD 42,000** (₹55 lakh); target growth **15%** a year; built on **Ten Guiding Principles (P4 Padi Sutralu)**.
