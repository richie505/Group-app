#### 1.2 National Space Day
- Marks **Chandrayaan-3's Vikram lander** landing at **Shiv Shakti Point** (23 August 2023).
- **First observed 2024** (first anniversary of the landing).
- **2nd National Space Day (2025)** theme: **"Aryabhatta to Gaganyaan: Ancient Wisdom to Infinite Possibilities"**.
- The **3rd** was held in **2026**.
#### 1.3 Ayurveda Day – New Fixed Date
- From **2025**, Ayurveda Day is fixed on **23 September** (autumnal equinox).
- Earlier it was observed on **Dhanvantari Jayanti / Dhanteras** (a moving date).
!KEY APPSC 2025 asked the new fixed date of Ayurveda Day (23 September) and National Consumer Day (24 December).
