## U0328 | ISSUES AND CHALLENGES IN INDIAN FEDERALISM – GOVERNOR'S ROLE, GST COUNCIL, CENTRAL AGENCIES, ALL INDIA SERVICES
### 1. FACTORS AND PHASES OF CENTRE-STATE RELATIONS
- Shaping factors: **constitutional provisions**, **planning**, **party politics**, **judicial interpretation**.
| Phase | Features |
|---|---|
| 1950s – early 1960s | **Nehru era**; Congress at Centre and States; calm except new-State demands; States relied on grants |
| Mid-1960s onwards | Congress lost many States; opposition State governments; **autonomy demands** grew |
| Since 1990s | **Coalition era**; greater say for States → **cooperative federalism** |
- **S.R. Bommai (1994):** Art. 356 action subject to **judicial review**.
- **2015:** Planning Commission replaced by **NITI Aayog**.

### 4. KEY RECOMMENDATIONS
| Body | Recommendations |
|---|---|
| ARC | **Inter-State Council under Art. 263**; **non-partisan Governors**; more delegation and finances to States |
| Rajamannar | **Delete Art. 356**; ISC under Art. 263; **abolish Planning Commission**; **abolish AIS** |
| Sarkaria | **Permanent Inter-Governmental Council** (ISC); Art. 356 **sparingly, last resort**; Governor an **eminent outsider**, detached, not recently in politics, **CM consulted**; floor test; consult States on Concurrent List laws; bills decided in a set period; time-bound cesses; **strengthen AIS** |
| NCRWC | **Assent within ~4 months**; hung-assembly order (pre-poll alliance, largest party, post-poll coalition); Art. 356 safeguards; All India Judicial Service |
| Punchhi | **Fixed tenure** for Governors; **impeachment-like removal** (APPSC-G1 2024); no misuse of discretion; time-bound disposal of bills; regular PM-CM meetings; **localised emergency** |
- **Residuary powers (Sarkaria):** taxation stays with Parliament; other residuary matters to the **Concurrent List**.

### 5. INSTITUTIONS CRITICISED AS AGAINST FEDERAL PRINCIPLES
| Institution | Criticism |
|---|---|
| **Planning Commission** | Extra-constitutional; made States "**suppliants for aid**" (Rajamannar) |
| **Governor** | Appointed by the Centre; seen as central interference |
| **Arts 256, 257, 356, 365** | Tools of Union overreach |
| **Cesses and CSS** | Cesses (~18% of gross tax revenue) are not shared |
| **All India Services** | Restrict State autonomy and patronage |
### 6. ALL INDIA SERVICES AND STATE AUTONOMY
- **Art. 312:** AIS = **IAS, IPS, Indian Forest Service**.
- New AIS: **Rajya Sabha** resolution by **two-thirds present and voting** (not Lok Sabha).
- Recruited by **UPSC**, serve the States; **ultimate control with Centre, immediate control with States**; uniform pay scales.
- Against: violates federal principle (APPSC-G2 2025: A and R true, R explains A).
- For: high standards, uniformity, **Centre-State coordination**.
### 7. CENTRAL AGENCIES
| Agency | Act | State consent |
|---|---|---|
| **CBI** | **DSPE Act, 1946** | **Section 6** – needs State consent |
| **ED** | **PMLA 2002 + FEMA 1999** | Not needed; enforces nationwide |
| **NIA** | **NIA Act, 2008** (after 26/11) | Not needed – concurrent jurisdiction over scheduled offences anywhere |
- If **general consent** is withdrawn, CBI needs consent **case by case**; pending probes not affected.
- States that withdrew general consent (Lok Sabha reply, Dec 2023): Punjab, Jharkhand, Kerala, Rajasthan, Chhattisgarh, West Bengal, Mizoram, Telangana, Meghalaya, Tamil Nadu.
- **Chhattisgarh** challenged the NIA Act in the Supreme Court (Jan 2020).
!TRAP Only the CBI needs State consent; NIA and ED do not.
### 8. GST COUNCIL AND FISCAL CENTRALISATION
- **Art. 279A**; vote weight: **Centre 1/3**, **States 2/3**; decisions need **3/4 majority** of weighted votes; nearly all decisions by consensus.
- **Mohit Minerals (May 2022):** GST Council recommendations are **not binding** on Union and States (persuasive value).
- GST **subsumed State taxes**; cesses and surcharges stay **outside the divisible pool** – divisible pool ~**81%** of gross tax (2025-26, as of 12 Feb 2026).
- **16th FC keeps 41%** (2026-31); States want **50%**; other proposals: bring off-budget borrowing into accounts, strengthen **SFCs** (2026).
- Tamil Nadu Assembly's unanimous resolution for fair tax devolution (Aug 2026).
### 9. GOVERNOR-STATE CONFLICTS
- Bill assent delays (Tamil Nadu, Kerala).
- **State of Tamil Nadu v Governor (April 2025):** SC set timelines for assent and "deemed assent".
- **Presidential Reference (Art. 143), opinion 20 Nov 2025:** 5-judge bench – **no court-imposed timelines** for Governor (Art. 200) or President (Art. 201); **deemed assent alien** to the Constitution.
- **Kurian Joseph Committee** (TN, Part I, 16 Feb 2026) seeks a "**structural reset**".
### 10. INTER-STATE COUNCIL AS A REFORM FORUM
- Set up **1990** under **Art. 263** on Sarkaria's advice; recommendatory; **PM chairs**; **Home Minister** chairs Standing Committee.
- Members: CMs, UT administrators, Governors of States under PR, **6 Union Cabinet ministers**.
- **11th meeting – 16 July 2016** (last); revival urged (Feb 2026).
### 11. SPECIAL CATEGORY STATUS AND ART. 371
- SCS: **not in the Constitution**; granted by the **NDC** using **Gadgil formula** criteria (hilly terrain, low density/tribal share, border location, backwardness, weak finances).
- Benefit: **90%** Central funding of plan spending; **11 States** granted SCS (e.g. Sikkim, Himachal Pradesh, J&K); **Bihar never granted**.
- **14th FC:** devolution raised to **42%** + revenue deficit grants; **no distinction** between special and general category States – Centre's main reason to deny SCS.
- **AP:** PM's assurance **20 Feb 2014**; got a **special assistance package (2016)** instead; end of revenue deficit grants is a grievance.

- Ladakh offered Art. 371 safeguards instead of the Sixth Schedule (2026); **FNTA** gives eastern Nagaland autonomy over **46 subjects** without affecting Art. 371A (2026).
- **131st Amendment Bill 2026** (Lok Sabha expansion) defeated – sharpened the North-South debate (May 2026).
