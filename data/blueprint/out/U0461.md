## U0461 | RELATED UNDP INDICES – IHDI, GII, GDI, MPI
### 1. YEAR INTRODUCED – FIRSTS
| Index | Introduced | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **GDI** (Gender-related Development Index) | **HDR 1995** | Gender inequalities in HDI achievements |
| **GEM** (Gender Empowerment Measure) | **HDR 1995** | Gender inequalities in empowerment |
| **Capability Poverty Measure** | **HDR 1996** | Three indicators |
| **Human Poverty Index (HPI)** | **HDR 1997** | "Human poverty"; complemented HDI |
| **MPI, GII, IHDI** | **HDR 2010** | **MPI replaced HPI**; GII fixed GDI/GEM weaknesses |
| Gender Social Norms Index | **5 March 2020** (first) | Social beliefs blocking gender equality (76 countries) |
!NUM 1995 – 1996 – 1997 – 2010 = GDI/GEM – CPM – HPI – MPI/GII/IHDI
### 2. HUMAN POVERTY INDEX
- Deprivation in **longevity, knowledge, decent standard of living**; indicates **standard of living** (APPSC-GS 2018 key).
- **HPI-1** for developing, **HPI-2** for developed countries; replaced by **MPI in HDR 2010**.
### 3. CAPABILITY POVERTY MEASURE (HDR 1996)
- Lack of three basic capabilities (well-fed and healthy, healthy reproduction, educated). Indicators:
1. **Children under 5 underweight** (child malnutrition)
2. **Births unattended** by trained health personnel
3. **Female illiteracy**
!TRAP Male labour-force participation is NOT a CPM indicator.
### 4. GENDER INEQUALITY INDEX AND GDI
#### 4.1 GII (HDR 2010)
| Dimension | Indicators |
|---|---|
| **Reproductive health** | **Maternal mortality ratio**, **adolescent birth rate** |
| **Empowerment** | Women's share of parliamentary seats; adults 25+ with some secondary education |
| **Labour market** | Female and male labour force participation (15+) |
- **Higher GII = greater inequality**. India (HDR 2025): **102nd of 193**, value **0.403** (up from 108).
#### 4.2 GDI and IHDI
- **GDI** = ratio of female HDI to male HDI (health, education, earned income); **GDI = 1 = parity**; countries in **5 groups** by distance from parity. India (HDR 2025): **0.874**, **Group 5**.
- **IHDI** = HDI discounted for inequality in each dimension; **HDI − IHDI = loss** (in %). IHDI = HDI with no inequality, otherwise always lower.
!TRAP "0 = equal, 1 = worst" describes the GII, not the GDI.
### 5. MPI DESIGN
- Proposed (**2010**) by **Sabina Alkire and Maria Emma Santos**; developed by **OPHI with UNDP**, presented in HDR 2010; method **Alkire-Foster**.
- **MPI = Headcount ratio (H) × Intensity (A)** – hence small values (India 0.066); not a sum, not a geometric mean (that is HDI), not a $1-a-day measure.
- **MPI-poor** = deprived in at least **one-third** of weighted indicators. Uses household-survey micro-data.
- Most suitable index for the **intensity of poverty** in India.
#### 5.1 Global MPI – 3 Dimensions, 10 Indicators
| Dimension | Indicators | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| **Health** | Nutrition, child mortality | 1/6 each |
| **Education** | Years of schooling, school attendance | 1/6 each |
| **Standard of living** | Electricity, drinking water, sanitation, housing, cooking fuel, assets | 1/18 each |
#### 5.2 National MPI – 3 Dimensions, 12 Indicators
| Dimension | Indicators |
|---|---|
| Health (3) | Nutrition, child and adolescent mortality, **maternal health** |
| Education (2) | Years of schooling, school attendance |
| Standard of living (7) | Cooking fuel, sanitation, drinking water, electricity, housing, assets, **bank account** |
- Years of schooling is NOT a standard-of-living indicator.
- Social indicators of poverty: illiteracy, lack of healthcare, jobs, malnutrition, safe drinking water, sanitation – **not** size of house or fewer means of transport.
!TRAP Global 10 vs National 12. Saying the National MPI does not use Alkire-Foster is wrong. The 2010 MPI does NOT measure only extreme poverty below $1 a day (UPPSC 2025).
### 6. GLOBAL VS NATIONAL MPI
| Feature | Global MPI | National MPI |
|---|---|---|
| Publisher | **UNDP + OPHI** (Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative), since 2010 | **NITI Aayog** (with UNDP, OPHI) (APPSC-G2 2025) |
| Method | Alkire-Foster | Same |
| Indicators | 10 | 12 |
- National baseline report: **Nov 2021** (NFHS-4); monitored under **GIRG** (Global Indices for Reforms and Growth).
