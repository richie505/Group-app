## U0058 | ANDHRA SOCIETY AND CULTURE IN THE EARLY PERIOD – VARNA, WOMEN, EDUCATION AND FESTIVALS
### 1. VARNA AND CASTE
- Early Andhra society was shaped by the **Vedic varna system**; the **Satavahanas** played a crucial role (APPSC GS 2024). The fourfold system existed and the Satavahanas were **Brahmins**; kings upheld **varna-dharma**.
- **Nanaghat inscription:** Brahmins trained in Vedic learning and ritual; Brahmins got **tax-free villages**.
- **Kshatriya claims by assimilation:** **Greeks, Sakas, Pahlavas, Kushanas** (also Kardhamakas, Western Kshatrapas) took Indian names and faiths – e.g. Nahapana's daughter **Dakshamitra**, son-in-law **Rishabhadatta**, Rudradaman.
- **Vishnukundins** followed varnasramadharma; **farmers = "the backbone"**, got land grants; Vaisyas traded; Sudras (carpenters, sculptors, weavers) had a recognised place.
#### 1.1 Occupational Sub-castes
| Term | Occupation |
|---|---|
| **Halikas** | **Farmers** (emerging agricultural group) |
| **Sethis** | Merchants |
| **Selavaddikas** | **Stone sculptors / masons** |
| **Swarnakaras** | Goldsmiths |
- Later terms: **Samayamu** = caste associations (**Kakatiya** period); **Kayasthas** = record-keepers.
### 2. MARRIAGE CUSTOMS
- Marriage = **sacred**; **polygamy** among upper classes; widows had to observe **celibacy**.
| Type | Union | Children |
|---|---|---|
| **Anuloma** | Higher-caste man + lower-caste woman | **Ugra** |
| **Viloma (Pratiloma)** | Lower-caste man + higher-caste woman | **Chandala** |
- **Kathasaritsagara** mentions inter-caste marriages – caste not fully rigid.
| King | Married |
|---|---|
| **Hala** | Sri Lankan princess **Lilavati** |
| Satakarni I | **Naganika**, daughter of Maharathi **Tranakayira** (joined Satavahanas with Maharathis) |
| **Vasishthiputra Satakarni** | Daughter of Saka **Rudradaman** |
### 3. STATUS OF WOMEN
- **Patriarchal joint family**, yet women had **considerable respect and freedom** – joined men in festivals, assemblies, public life.
- **Matronymics:** Gautamiputra Satakarni, Vasishthiputra Pulumavi. Titles from husbands: **Mahasenapatni, Mahabhojaki, Maharathi**.
- Female poets in the Gathasaptasati: **Madhavi, Revati, Anupamalabdha, Anulakshmi**.
- **Sati:** a **Nagarjunakonda sculpture** and Greek writer **Strabo**.
| Woman | Dynasty | Patronage |
|---|---|---|
| **Gautami Balasri** | Satavahana | Cave-vihara at **Nasik** (not a Gathasaptasati poet) |
| **Bodhisiri** | **Ikshvaku** | **Greatest woman patron of Buddhism**; shrines, viharas at Nagarjunakonda |
| **Chantisiri** | **Ikshvaku** | Sister of Vasishthiputra Chantamula; built the **Mahachaitya**, Nagarjunakonda |
!TRAP Bodhisiri and Chantisiri are Ikshvaku, NOT Satavahana.
### 4. EDUCATION
| Centre | Nature |
|---|---|
| **Viharas** | Buddhist monastic learning (Nagarjunakonda a leading centre) |
| **Ghatikasthanas** | **Vedic colleges** (Vishnukundins) |
| **Agraharas** | Land grants to Vedic Brahmins (Agnisarma, Easwarasarma) |
- **Indrabhattaraka Varma** – "**Ghatika Vapta punyasancharya**"; credited with founding ghatikas.
### 5. RELIGION AND FOLK CULTS
- **Amaravati reliefs:** worship of **nagas, yakshas and sacred trees** beside Buddhist symbols.
- **Ikshvaku kings** – Vedic sacrifices (Chantamula: Asvamedha, Vajapeya); **queens** – Buddhism; Brahmanical shrines such as **Pushpabhadraswami** at Nagarjunakonda.
- Vishnukundins: family deity **Sriparvataswamy**; titles Paramamaheswara, Parama Brahmanya.
### 6. FESTIVALS, AMUSEMENTS, DRESS
- **Gathasaptasati (Gaha Sattasai)** by **Hala**: ~**700 verses**, **Maharashtri Prakrit**; love and country verses; begins with a prayer to **Shiva (Pasupati)**; **main literary source** of Satavahana society (APPSC 2019).
- "**Maiputa**" used by dancers = **Kusuma Lepanam** (APPSC 2019).
- **Amphitheatre at Nagarjunakonda (Vijayapuri)** – for performances and games, not Vedic sacrifices.
- Reliefs: **men** – lower cloth and **turban**; **women** – heavy **anklets, bangles, girdles, necklaces**.
