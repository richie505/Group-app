## U0804 | HEALTH AND ENVIRONMENT – VECTOR-BORNE AND WATER-BORNE DISEASES, AIR POLLUTION, AMR, ONE HEALTH
### 1. VECTOR-BORNE DISEASES
- Vector-borne = spread by a **vector** (mosquito, flea, sandfly, mite).
- **Only female mosquitoes suck blood** (bigger wings); males feed on plant juices. **Culex, Anopheles, Aedes** are found worldwide.
#### 1.1 Vector → Disease
| Vector | Disease (pathogen) |
|---|---|
| **Female Anopheles** | **Malaria** (*Plasmodium*, protozoan) |
| **Aedes aegypti** | **Dengue, chikungunya, Zika, yellow fever** |
| **Aedes albopictus** (tiger / forest mosquito) | Dengue, chikungunya, yellow fever – **NOT JE** |
| **Culex** (*C. quinquefasciatus*) | **Filariasis**, **Japanese encephalitis** |
| **Haemagogus** | **Forest yellow fever** |
| **Sandfly** (*Phlebotomus argentipes*) | **Kala-azar** (*Leishmania donovani*) |
| **Tsetse fly** (*Glossina*) | **Sleeping sickness** (*Trypanosoma brucei*) |
| **Chigger mite** | **Scrub typhus** (*Orientia tsutsugamushi*) |
| **Rat flea** | **Plague** (*Yersinia pestis*) |
| **Ticks** (monkeys) | **Kyasanur Forest Disease** |
| **Cyclops** (water flea) | Carries **Guinea worm** larvae |
| **Snail** (intermediate host) | **Schistosomiasis** |
| Leafhopper / whitefly | Rice tungro / okra yellow vein mosaic |
!TRAP Not spread by mosquitoes: kala-azar, sleeping sickness, scrub typhus, plague (APPSC G2 2025). Mosquito-borne: Zika, West Nile, chikungunya, dengue, malaria, JE, filariasis.
#### 1.2 Disease Details
| Disease | Pathogen | Affects |
|---|---|---|
| **Malaria** | Protozoan *Plasmodium* | **Liver first, then RBCs**; **enlarged spleen** |
| **Dengue** ("breakbone fever") | Flavivirus | **Platelets fall** → internal bleeding (APPSC 2023) |
| **Chikungunya** | **Alphavirus** (viral, not bacterial) | Severe joint pain |
| **Zika** | Zika virus; also blood, sexual spread | Fever, rash, joint pain |
| **JE** | **JEV** (flavivirus) | **Brain**; **pigs amplify**, pigs + wild birds = reservoirs |
| **Kala-azar** | *Leishmania* | **Liver, spleen, bone marrow** |
| **Sleeping sickness** | *T. b. rhodesiense, T. b. gambiense* | Brain (late stage); tropical Africa |
| **Filariasis** | Nematode ***Wuchereria bancrofti*** | **Lymph vessels → elephantiasis**; fever, swelling of lower limbs |
- Keeping **pigs away** from settlements helps end JE.
- Malaria vs dengue differ in **mosquito**: female Anopheles vs female Aedes aegypti.
#### 1.3 Plasmodium
- Five human species: ***P. falciparum, vivax, malariae, ovale, knowlesi***.
- ***P. falciparum*** = **most lethal, malignant/cerebral malaria**, no relapse.
- ***P. vivax*** = **~65%** of India's cases; **relapsing** (liver **hypnozoites**).
- Vaccine is hard: several species with **different antigens**.
- **Arteether** from ***Artemisia annua***.

| Disease | Cause | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Amoebiasis** | *Entamoeba histolytica* | **Large intestine** |
| **Ascariasis** | Nematode *Ascaris lumbricoides* | Swallowing eggs; **small intestine** |
| **Fascioliasis** | Flatworm *Fasciola hepatica* (sheep liver fluke) | **Liver, bile ducts**; ruminants; raw water plants; snail host |
| **Taeniasis** | *Taenia solium* (pork), *T. saginata* (beef) | Eggs → **neurocysticercosis** (brain cysts) |
| **Guinea worm** | Nematode *Dracunculus medinensis* (not annelid) | Drinking water with Cyclops |
| **Schistosomiasis** | Flatworm *Schistosoma* | Urinary tract, intestine; unsafe water |
| **Athlete's foot** | *Trichophyton, Epidermophyton, Microsporum* | Skin between toes; floors, towels |
| **Mucormycosis** | Mucormycetes | Nose, sinuses, eyes, brain; **not zoonotic** |
| **Ergotism** | *Claviceps purpurea* on rye | Nervous, vascular, endocrine |

- **Tapeworm = hermaphrodite**; roundworm = separate sexes.
- Ringworm – dermatophytes (**griseofulvin**); candidiasis – *Candida*; aspergillosis – *Aspergillus*; **Amanita** – alpha-amanitin.
- **Epidemic dropsy**: mustard oil adulterated with ***Argemone mexicana*** (sanguinarine).
- Fungal (per keys): baldness, seborrheic dermatitis; **colour blindness = genetic**.
- Plant: *Puccinia* – rust of wheat; *Ustilago* – smut.
### 3. WATER-BORNE DISEASES
- **Faeco-oral** group: **cholera, typhoid, hepatitis A and E, acute diarrhoea, dysentery, polio**.
| Disease | Pathogen | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Typhoid** | *Salmonella Typhi* | **Widal test**; intestine |
| **Cholera** | *Vibrio cholerae* | Toxin → dehydration; **ORS**; small intestine |
| **Hepatitis A, E** | Viruses | Jaundice |
| **Acute diarrhoea** | Rotavirus, E. coli, Shigella | Leading child killer |
| **Polio** | Poliovirus | **Spinal cord**, paralysis |
- ~**5,000 children die daily** worldwide from preventable water- and sanitation-related disease.
- **Floods**: cholera, typhoid, hepatitis, **leptospirosis**.
- Shallow hand-pump water: cholera, typhoid, jaundice – **not fluorosis**.
- Unsafe-water diseases: **malaria, acute diarrhoea, schistosomiasis**; **kwashiorkor NOT**.
- Remedies: **Jal Jeevan Mission, Swachh Bharat, AMRUT**.

| Viral | Fact |

- **Diabetes** cannot be controlled by vaccination.

### 6. VECTOR CONTROL AND ELIMINATION
- **NVBDCP (now NCVBDC)**: six diseases – **malaria, JE, dengue, chikungunya, kala-azar, LF** (**not TB**).
- **Elimination**: malaria, LF, kala-azar; dengue, chikungunya, JE only controlled.
- **National Framework for Malaria Elimination (2016-2030)**: **malaria-free India by 2030**.
- **MERA India** (Malaria Elimination Research Alliance): **ICMR**, **24 April 2019**; steered by **ICMR-NIMR, New Delhi**.
- WHO malaria-free in South Asia: **Maldives (2015), Sri Lanka (2016)**.
| Bio-control | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| ***Gambusia affinis*** (mosquito fish), guppy | **Larvivorous** fish |
| **Bti** | **Bacterial bio-larvicide** |
| **Wolbachia** | Bacterium; **cytoplasmic incompatibility** + **pathogen blocking**; no GM, no pesticide |
- Wolbachia: in about **50%** of insect species; **naturally absent in Aedes aegypti and Anopheles**; blocks dengue, chikungunya, Zika.
- Verily **'Debug'** (2 Jun 2026): Wolbachia male *Culex quinquefasciatus* released.
### 7. AIR POLLUTION AND HEALTH
- Main target: **respiratory system** (APPSC 2023); also heart disease, stroke, lung cancer.
- **WHO**: **4.2 million** premature deaths from **ambient** air pollution (**2019**).
- **State of Global Air 2020**: air pollution killed **116,000+ Indian infants** within a month of birth (2019).
- **DALY = YLL + YLD**.
- **Noise**: hearing loss, hypertension, anxiety, sleep disorders.
### 8. CLIMATE-SENSITIVE DISEASES
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Floods | Cholera, hepatitis outbreaks |
| **Heatwaves** | Dehydration, **heatstroke**, cardiovascular stress |
| **Warming** | **Expands range** of dengue and malaria vectors |
- **3.6 billion+** people in highly climate-vulnerable areas.
- **WHO**: ~**2.5 lakh extra deaths a year** (2030-2050): **child undernutrition 95,000, malaria 60,000, diarrhoea 48,000, heat (elderly) 38,000**.
- **58% (218 of 375)** human infectious diseases aggravated by climate hazards (2022).
- Dasra 'Under the Weather' (10 Apr 2026): **~40% of districts** at high extreme-weather risk.
- **Malaria and dengue** = outbreak-prone and climate-sensitive.
### 9. ANTIBIOTICS AND ANTIMICROBIAL RESISTANCE
#### 9.1 Antibiotics
- **Fleming**: **penicillin, 1928** (*Penicillium notatum*); **Waksman** coined "**antibiotic**" (**1942**), found **streptomycin** (first anti-TB drug).
- Mostly from **bacteria (Streptomyces)**; mostly natural or semi-synthetic; **sulfa drugs synthetic**.
- **Chloramphenicol** = broad-spectrum (typhoid); Penicillin G narrow; Cibazole = short-acting sulpha.
- Analgesics – pain; antiseptics – stop microbes; aspirin, paracetamol – analgesic, antipyretic; chloroquine – antimalarial.
- **Acetobacter** – vinegar; **Bacillus** – several antibiotics.
- **Monoclonal antibodies**: man-made proteins binding specific antigens.
#### 9.2 How AMR Builds
- Causes: **incorrect dose**, **incomplete course**, **overuse**, **self-medication**, **livestock use**, unregulated veterinary drugs, **horizontal gene transfer**, mutation, poor sanitation, pharma effluents.
- **DBT Scoping Report (Nov 2017)**: MDR from **incorrect doses**, **livestock use** – **not genetics**.
- Severity: **MDR → XDR → TDR**.
- **Biofilm**: microbes in a self-made **EPS matrix** on **implants, catheters, tissues, food surfaces**; blocks antibiotics → **chronic infection**, food spoilage.

### 10. ONE HEALTH, SURVEILLANCE AND IMMUNISATION
- **One Health** = **human, animal, environmental** health together; **biodiversity loss** raises zoonotic risk.
- **National One Health Mission** (**PM-STIAC**): rabies, Nipah, avian flu, AMR.
- **National Institute of One Health, Nagpur**.
| Programme / Act | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Epidemic Diseases Act, 1897** | **Bubonic plague, Bombay**; **4 sections**; amended **2020** to protect health workers |
| **IDSP** (2004) | **MoHFW / NCDC**; **IHIP** digital platform |
| **UIP** (1985) | **6 diseases** at launch, **now 12** |
| **Mission Indradhanush** | **Dec 2014**; **>90% full immunisation** of children and pregnant women; IMI 5.0 |
| **U-WIN** | Portal registering every UIP vaccination |
- UIP 12: diphtheria, pertussis, tetanus, polio, measles, rubella, childhood TB, hepatitis B, Hib, rotavirus, pneumococcal pneumonia, JE.
- **BCG**: prevents TB; at birth, up to **12 months**.
