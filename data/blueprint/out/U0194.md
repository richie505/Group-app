### 2. KUKA (NAMDHARI) MOVEMENT
#### 2.1 Origin and Practices
- Region: **Punjab**; centre under Ram Singh at **Bhaini (Ludhiana)**, not Nankana Sahib.
- Wore **white clothes**, ate **no meat**, carried a **lathi**.
- Rejected idol worship, worship of tombs, trees and snakes, and **Brahmin-led rituals**; revived **Guru Gobind Singh's Khalsa** ceremonies.
#### 2.2 Boycott Methods
- Boycotted **British goods, English education, law courts and the postal system**.
- **Satguru Ram Singh** = **first to use non-cooperation and boycott** as political weapons (APPSC 2025).
- Significance: anticipated the **Swadeshi** and **non-cooperation** methods.
#### 2.3 Suppression
- After the **1872 Malerkotla** clash, dozens of Kukas were **blown from cannons**.
- Ram Singh **deported to Rangoon (Burma)**.
!KEY Kuka and Wahabi both began as **religious revolts** and became **political movements** against British rule.
### 3. NIRANKARI MOVEMENT
- Founder **Baba Dayal Das** (1855).
- Teaching: worship of God as **Nirankar (formless)**; no idol worship.
- Issued **hukamnamas** on correct practice; own worship places and priests.
- **Cooperated with the British**; became a **separate group within Sikhism**.
#### 3.1 Namdhari vs Nirankari
| Feature | Namdhari (Kuka) | Nirankari |
|---|---|---|
| Belief | Khalsa ceremonies; no idols | God as Nirankar |
| Practice | White clothes, no meat, lathi | Hukamnamas; own priests |
| British | **Boycott**: goods, courts, post | **Cooperated** |
| Fate | Malerkotla 1872; Ram Singh to Rangoon | Separate group in Sikhism |
### 4. SINGH SABHA MOVEMENT
- **Amritsar Singh Sabha – 1873**; more radical **Lahore Singh Sabha – 1879**.
- Aims: reform Sikh society, remove un-Sikh practices, promote **Gurmukhi/Punjabi** literature and education.
- **Khalsa College, Amritsar – 1892** (foundation stone).
- The Akali movement grew out of it.
### 5. AKALI (GURDWARA REFORM) MOVEMENT
- Aim: free gurdwaras from corrupt **Udasi mahants**; leader **Kartar Singh Jhabbar**.
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1920 | **SGPC** formed; movement begins |
| 1921 | **Nankana Sahib massacre** |
| 1922 | **Guru-ka-Bagh** morcha |
| 1925 | **Sikh Gurdwaras Act** – gurdwaras under the SGPC |
- Later became a political party, the **Akali Dal**.
!TRAP 1920 = SGPC; 1921 = Nankana Sahib; 1922 = Guru-ka-Bagh.
### 6. PARSI REFORM
- **Rahnumai Mazdayasnan Sabha** (1851): **women's (girls') education**, reform of marriage customs; challenged Parsi orthodoxy.
- **Naoroji Furdonji** – first president.
- **Rast Goftar** ("Truth-teller") – its organ, **edited by Dadabhai Naoroji**.
- **Behramji M. Malabari** – greatest Parsi social reformer of the 19th century; campaign against **child marriage** → **Age of Consent Act, 1891**.
!PAIR Rast Goftar | Parsi organ | Dadabhai Naoroji || Age of Consent Act 1891 | Child marriage | Behramji Malabari
### 7. OTHER BODIES
- **Radhasoami Satsang**: inner realisation through the **guru**.
- **Dev Samaj**: eternity of the soul, supremacy of the guru, good deeds, abstaining from **alcohol and meat**.
- **Bharat Dharma Mahamandal**: united orthodox Hindu leaders to preserve **Sanatan Dharma**.
- **Dharma Sabha** (1830): **opposed abolition of sati**.
