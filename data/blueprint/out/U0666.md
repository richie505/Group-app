### 2. URBAN GROWTH AND TREND
- Urban share: **18.35% (1971)** → 22.14% (1981) → 24.13% (2001) → **29.47% (2011)**.
- 2001–11: urban **109.56 → 146.10 lakh ≈ 33%**; rural only **≈ 1.5%** (344.41 → 349.67 lakh); total growth 9.21%.
- **Peri-urban** expansion: the **16th Finance Commission "Urbanisation Premium"** rewards merging peri-urban villages into ULBs.

### 6. AMARAVATI CAPITAL REGION
- **APCRDA** = **Andhra Pradesh Capital Region Development Authority**; set up under the **APCRDA Act, 2014**; plans the Amaravati capital region (Guntur, Palnadu, Bapatla).
- **Foundation stone: 22 Oct 2015** by PM **Narendra Modi** at **Uddandarayunipalem** (Guntur district), with CM Chandrababu Naidu; soil from Parliament and Yamuna water used.
- On the **southern bank of the Krishna**; capital area Vijayawada–Guntur–Tenali–Mangalagiri chosen for its central location.
- Physical progress only **6.84%** (Survey 2025-26); land-pooling grievances.
- **Jul 2026**: MoU with **UN-Habitat** → Centre of Excellence for Urban Affairs at Amaravati.

### 9. MIGRATION
- About **39.55 lakh** people of AP live away from their native places; about **half** are temporary/seasonal migrants.
- **Gulf migration**: **East Godavari** (Konaseema), **West Godavari**, **Kadapa** (YSR) and Rayachoti–Rajampet areas; supported by **APNRTS**.
- Rayalaseema migrants also go to **Bengaluru** and **Hyderabad**; **Uttarandhra** is a historic out-migration region.
### 10. URBAN PROBLEMS
- **Slums**: undivided AP had about **1.02 crore** slum dwellers (Census 2011), **second after Maharashtra**.
- **Urban flooding**: **Vijayawada – Budameru** rivulet; flash floods in Visakhapatnam and Vijayawada from **lost wetlands, blocked drains**, unplanned growth, concretisation.
- AI flood forecasting: RTGS + APSDMA + IIT Gandhinagar.
- Water supply **2,048 MLD** across 123 ULBs; sewage treated **613 MLD**; 94.62% wards with door-to-door collection.
- Complexes producing over 100 kg waste a day must process it themselves (Aug 2026).
