## U0106 | SLAVE / MAMLUK DYNASTY - AIBAK, ILTUTMISH, RAZIA, BALBAN
### 1. DYNASTY NAMES AND PERIOD
- **Mamluk** = Arabic "**owned**": an owned **military slave** (not a domestic slave). Also called **Slave dynasty**; ruled **1206-1290**.
- First Muslim dynasty to rule north India from **Delhi**.
| Line | Period | Founder |
|---|---|---|
| **Qutbi** | 1206-1211 | **Qutbuddin Aibak** |
| **First Ilbari** | 1211-1266 | **Iltutmish** |
| **Second Ilbari** | 1266-1290 | **Balban** |
#### 1.1 Order of Rulers
- Aibak → Aram Shah → **Iltutmish** (1210-36) → Ruknuddin Firuz (1236) → **Razia** (1236-40) → Bahram Shah (1240-42) → Alauddin Masud (1242-46) → **Nasiruddin Mahmud** (1246-66) → **Balban** (1266-86) → Kaiqubad → Kaimurs.
#### 1.2 Sultanate Dynasties
| Dynasty | Period |
|---|---|
| Mamluk | 1206-1290 |
| Khalji | 1290-1320 |
| Tughlaq | 1320-1414 |
| Sayyid | 1414-1451 |
| Lodi | 1451-1526 |
!TRAP Odd one out: Ruknuddin Firuz, Alauddin Masud, Nasiruddin Mahmud ARE Mamluks; Jalaluddin Khalji and the Tughlaqs are NOT.
### 2. QUTBUDDIN AIBAK (1206-1210)
- Born in **Turkestan**; sold first to the **Qazi of Nishapur**, then slave of **Muhammad Ghori** (APPSC G1 2004).
- Rose to **Amir-i-Akhur** (Master of Stables), then general; after **Tarain II (1192)** Ghori's representative in India.
- Title: **Lakh Bakhsh** ("giver of lakhs"). Capital: **Lahore**.
- Monuments begun: **Qutb Minar**, **Quwwat-ul-Islam** mosque (first mosque in Delhi), **Adhai Din ka Jhonpra** (Ajmer).
- Fought **Tajuddin Yildiz** for Ghurid lands after 1206.
- Death: **1210** at Lahore, fell from his horse playing **chaugan (polo)**; buried near **Anarkali Bazaar**, Lahore. Polo was brought to India by the Turks.
### 3. ILTUTMISH (1210-1236)
#### 3.1 Rivals, Capital, Legitimacy
- Rivals: **Yildiz** (defeated at **Tarain, 1216**) and **Nasiruddin Qabacha** (fought for Lahore).
- Moved capital from **Lahore to Delhi**.
- **Investiture ("mansur")** from the **Abbasid Caliph** of Baghdad, **1229** - first Caliphal investiture.
#### 3.2 Institutions and Coins
| Institution | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Turkan-i-Chahalgani** | "The Forty" / **Chalisa**: **Turkish military elite** |
| **Iqta** | Revenue assignments to officers (**muqtis**) instead of cash salaries |
| **Silver tanka**, **copper jital** | Regular, purely Arabic coinage |
- First Sultan to bring in **regular coins** and make **Delhi** capital.
!TRAP The Forty were Turkish military nobles, not Persian revenue clerks.
#### 3.3 Mongol Threat and Monuments
- Refused shelter to **Jalaluddin Mangbarni** of Khwarazm → kept **Chengiz Khan** out of India.
- Completed the **Qutb Minar** (dedicated to **Khwaja Qutbuddin Bakhtiyar Kaki**).
- **Sultan Ghari** tomb → Iltutmish called "**Father of tomb building**".
### 4. RAZIA SULTAN (1236-1240)
- **First and only woman ruler** of the Delhi Sultanate.
- **Discarded purdah**, wore **male attire**, personally **led the army**.
- Opposition: Turkish nobles resented her favour to **Yaqut**, an **Abyssinian**.
- **1240**: **Altuniya**, governor of **Bhatinda (Sirhind)**, revolted → Yaqut killed, Razia imprisoned.
- End: Razia **married Altuniya**, marched on Delhi, was defeated and killed (1240).
- Razia made Balban **Amir-i-Shikar**.
### 5. NASIRUDDIN MAHMUD AND THE RISE OF BALBAN
- Nasiruddin Mahmud (1246-66) **immediately preceded** Balban; **Tabaqat-i-Nasiri** named after him.
- Balban was his **principal minister (naib)** and leader of the Forty; title **Ulugh Khan**.
- Briefly displaced by **Imaduddin Raihan**, then returned.
- Balban's early life: born c. **1205** as **Bahauddin**; captured by Mongols, bought by Iltutmish → **water-carrier** → member of the Forty → Amir-i-Shikar.
### 6. BALBAN (1266-1286)
- Took the regnal title **Ghiyasuddin Balban** on accession (1266).
#### 6.1 Theory of Kingship and Court
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Zil-i-Ilahi** | Sultan = **shadow of God** |
| **Sijda** | **Prostration** (greeting lying on the ground) |
| **Paibos** | **Kissing the feet** |
| **Nauroz** | **Persian festival** brought in by Balban |
- Court copied **Iranian kings**; strict etiquette.
#### 6.2 Blood and Iron, Army
- **Blood and iron** policy; **broke the power of the Forty** by promoting **junior Turks**.
- Created military department **Diwan-i-Arz**, separating it from **Diwan-i-Wizarat** (finance).
- **Outposts and forts** against the Mongols.
!TRAP Iltutmish created the Forty; Balban broke its power.
### 7. END OF THE DYNASTY AND RISE OF THE KHALJIS
- Balban (d. 1286) → grandson **Kaiqubad** (murdered; body thrown into the **Yamuna**) → infant **Kaimurs**.
- **Jalaluddin Firoz Khalji** crowned himself at **Kilokhari (1290)** → end of Mamluk rule, start of the **Khalji dynasty**.
