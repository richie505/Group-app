### 2. 18th BRICS SUMMIT – INDIA'S 2026 CHAIRSHIP
#### 2.1 Key Facts
- **18th BRICS Summit: New Delhi, 12-13 September 2026**, at **Bharat Mandapam**; India's **4th Chairship** (from **1 January 2026**).
- Concluded **13 Sep 2026**; India handed the chair to **China (2027)**.
- **Theme:** "**Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability**" – the BRICS acronym recast by Modi at Rio, where India explained BRICS as "Building Resilience and Innovation for Cooperation and Sustainability"; acronym **RICS** = Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation, Sustainability.
- **Logo:** **lotus** (national flower) with a "**Namaste**" motif.
- **Members (11):** Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa, Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, Saudi Arabia, UAE, **Indonesia (Jan 2025)**. **Saudi Arabia** has not formally confirmed full membership and attended New Delhi as an **invited country**, so the **full members are counted as 10** (10 Sep 2026).
- **Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, UAE** joined **2024**.
- **Partners (10, 2025):** Belarus, Bolivia, Cuba, Kazakhstan, Malaysia, Nigeria, Thailand, Uganda, Uzbekistan, **Vietnam (10th partner, 2025)**.
- BRICS share: **49.5% population**, **~40% GDP**, **26% trade**.
- **NDB:** HQ **Shanghai**; President **Dilma Rousseff**.
#### 2.2 New Delhi Declaration – Outcomes
- Adopted **unanimously** (Sep 2026), despite differences among Iran, Saudi Arabia, UAE over West Asia.
- Opposes **unilateral wars**; promotes **local currencies**; governance reform.
- **UNSC reform** (China, Russia back India, Brazil); **IMF/WB reform**; rejects **unilateral sanctions** and **carbon tariffs (CBAM)**; a **Pahalgam** paragraph.
- **First BRICS declaration since 2022 with no reference to Ukraine** (18 Sep 2026).
- **NIMHANS** to coordinate BRICS mental-health centres (16 Sep 2026).
- Welcomed the **BRICS Photovoltaic Industry Action Plan** (adopted **Jaipur**, Aug 2026) (15 Sep 2026).

| Feature | Details |

#### 3.2 Outcomes
- **28 documents** adopted, headed by the **Bishkek Declaration** on the SCO's 25th anniversary; **4 MoUs**, **20+ decisions**; a protocol amending the SCO Charter.
- 4 MoUs on **climate, nuclear safety, energy security, drug control**; anti-synthetic-drug measures till **2030**; **SCO Green Technology Corridor**.
- Approved an **SCO-CSTO-CIS roadmap** (2 Sep 2026); condemned U.S.-Israel attacks on Iran; terrorism "in all forms".
- **Lahore: SCO Tourism and Cultural Capital 2026-27**; Kyrgyzstan named a mountain "**SCO Peak**".
- **India's proposal of English as an official SCO language** accepted (traditionally **Russian and Chinese**).
- India to host the **first SCO Civilisation Dialogue Forum** later in 2026; also Young Scientist Forum, Young Authors' Conclave.
- Work towards an **SCO Development Bank** welcomed.
- India's three pillars: **Security, Connectivity, Opportunity**; PM Modi – terror financing, recruitment, radicalisation, safe havens.
- Called for an **inclusive government in Afghanistan**; affirmed right to peaceful nuclear energy; just solution to the Palestinian issue; cooperation on regional data centres and AI.
- **Lahore**: capital of Pakistan's Punjab, on the **Ravi**; **Lahore Fort and Shalimar Gardens** – UNESCO **1981**.
#### 3.3 Background
- **1996: Shanghai Five** – China, Russia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan.
- **2001**: SCO founded in **Shanghai** with **Uzbekistan**; **2017: India and Pakistan**; **2023: Iran**; **July 2024: Belarus (10th member)**.
- **Secretariat: Beijing**; **RATS: Tashkent**; observers **Afghanistan, Mongolia**; languages Chinese and Russian.

### 8. BILATERAL MEETINGS AND DISPUTES
#### 8.1 Indus Waters Treaty and the PCA
- India **rejected** the **Permanent Court of Arbitration** ruling on the Indus Waters Treaty – called it an "illegally constituted arbitral tribunal"; did not participate.
- Treaty **in abeyance** since the **Pahalgam** terror attack (**22 April 2025**); India announced abeyance from **23 April 2025** – "blood and water cannot flow together", till Pakistan ends cross-border terrorism.
- **IWT**: signed **19 September 1960, Karachi**; brokered by the **World Bank**; **Jawaharlal Nehru and Ayub Khan**.
- **Eastern rivers (Ravi, Beas, Sutlej) – India**; **Western rivers (Indus, Jhelum, Chenab) – Pakistan**, with limited Indian use.
- Indus rises on the **Tibetan Plateau**, drains into the **Arabian Sea**.
- **PCA**: est. **1899**, Peace Palace, **The Hague** – distinct from the **ICJ**.
#### 8.2 India-China Corps Commander Talks
