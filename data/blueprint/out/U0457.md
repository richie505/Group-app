## U0457 | NITI AAYOG INITIATIVES – SDG INDIA INDEX, ASPIRATIONAL DISTRICTS, SECTORAL INDICES, ATAL INNOVATION MISSION
### 1. SDG BASICS AND NODAL BODIES
#### 1.1 The SDGs
- **17 SDGs**, **169 targets**; adopted in **2015** under the **UN 2030 Agenda**; succeeded the **MDGs (2000–15)**.
- Adopted under the 2030 Agenda by UN member states – not by "198" member states (distractor).
- **India's success is key to global SDGs**: SDG indicators are population-based and India is about **one-sixth** of the world total (R explains A).

!TRAP SDG 14 = Life Below Water; SDG 15 = Life on Land. Do not swap them.
#### 1.3 India's Nodal Bodies and Partners
- **NITI Aayog** = **nodal institution** for SDG implementation; oversees the SDGs and leads **SDG localisation**.
- **MoSPI** = frames the **National Indicator Framework (NIF)**, **277 indicators** (2026), and monitors through it.
- **April 2022**: NITI Aayog and **UNICEF India** – Statement of Intent on SDGs, focus on **children**.
- **August 2023**: NITI Aayog **MoU with UNDP** on fast-tracking SDGs.
!KEY Indices make **competitive federalism** measurable: measurable State comparisons link national goals to district results (R explains A).

### 3. SDG INDIA INDEX – EDITIONS, DESIGN, CATEGORIES
#### 3.1 Purpose
- Main tool for **localising and monitoring SDGs**; promotes **competitive federalism**.
#### 3.2 Editions
| Edition | Year / release | India score | Key facts |

- **113 indicators** aligned to MoSPI's NIF.

### 6. ATAL INNOVATION MISSION (AIM)
- Set up **2016** (formed **16 January 2016**) under **NITI Aayog** (parent body).
- Aim: promote a culture of **innovation and entrepreneurship**.
| Component | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Atal Tinkering Labs (ATLs)** | In **schools**, students of **grades 6–12**; tools like 3D printing, electronics, robotics, IoT and sensors |
| **Atal Incubation Centres (AICs)** | **Business incubators** for start-ups, set up with AIM support at academic and non-academic institutions |
| Atal New India Challenges | Product innovation for national needs |
| Atal Community Innovation Centres | Innovation in underserved areas |
| Mentor India Campaign | Mentors for ATLs and AICs |
- Cabinet approved continuation as **AIM 2.0** under NITI Aayog with an enhanced budget.
!TRAP ATL = schools; AIC = start-ups. AIM sits in NITI Aayog, not the Ministry of Education.
### 7. ASPIRATIONAL DISTRICTS AND BLOCKS
#### 7.1 Aspirational Districts Programme (ADP)
- Launched **January 2018**; transforms **112** backward districts.
- Strategy: **Convergence, Collaboration, Competition**; districts ranked monthly by **delta ranking** (incremental progress) on the **Champions of Change** dashboard.
| Theme | Weight |
|---|---|
| **Health and Nutrition** | **30%** |
| **Education** | **30%** |
| Agriculture and Water Resources | 20% |
| Financial Inclusion and Skill Development | 10% |
| Basic Infrastructure | 10% |
- **49 indicators** (81 data points).
#### 7.2 AP Districts in ADP
- Original three: **Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam, YSR Kadapa**.
- After the 2022 reorganisation: **Alluri Sitharama Raju** (from Visakhapatnam) and **Parvathipuram Manyam** (from Vizianagaram) replaced the parent districts; YSR Kadapa continues.
- **Vizianagaram** topped the **baseline ranking** (48.13%); in the first delta ranking Vizianagaram was 4th and YSR Kadapa 5th.
- **Feb 2025**: YSR Kadapa chosen among best-performing aspirational districts (**₹3 crore** award).
- **Jul 2026**: **ASR district** national first in ADP **basic infrastructure**.
#### 7.3 Aspirational Blocks Programme (ABP)
- Launched **7 January 2023** (2nd National Conference of Chief Secretaries).
- **500 blocks** in 27 States and 4 UTs; **40 key performance indicators**, monitored quarterly.
- Sectors: Health and Nutrition, Education, Agriculture and Allied, Social Development, Basic Infrastructure.
!PAIR ADP | Jan 2018 | 112 districts, 49 indicators || ABP | 7 Jan 2023 | 500 blocks, 40 indicators
