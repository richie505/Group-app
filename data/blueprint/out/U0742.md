## U0742 | DEFENCE INDIGENISATION – MAKE IN INDIA IN DEFENCE, DAP 2020, iDEX, DEFENCE CORRIDORS, FDI IN DEFENCE
### 1. POLICY MILESTONES
| Year | Step |
|---|---|
| **May 2001** | Defence opened to **100% Indian private** participation + **FDI up to 26%**, both under licensing |
| **2016** | **DPP 2016** first added **Buy (Indian-IDDM)** (Dhirendra Singh committee) |
| **2017** | **Strategic Partnership (SP) model** added: Indian private firms + foreign OEMs build major platforms in India |
| **April 2018** | **iDEX** launched by the PM |
| **2020** | **DAP 2020**: IDDM top priority; **offsets dropped** in G2G and single-vendor deals |
| **2021** | 200-year-old **OFB dissolved** → **41 factories** into **7 DPSUs** |
| **2025** | MoD's **'Year of Reforms'**; DAP 2020 review for JVs and ToT |
| **DPM 2025** | Released **23 Oct 2025**, effective **1 Nov 2025**; revenue procurement ~₹1 lakh crore (capital buys stay under DAP 2020) |
- The 7 DPSUs: Munitions India, AVNL, AWEIL, **Troop Comforts**, Yantra India, India Optel, **Gliders India**.
!TRAP DPP 2016 = IDDM added; DPP 2017 = SP model; DAP 2020 = offsets dropped.
### 2. DAP 2020 – PROCUREMENT CATEGORIES
| Priority | Category |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Buy (Indian-IDDM)** – Indigenously Designed, Developed and Manufactured; **most preferred** |
| 2 | Buy (Indian) |
| 3 | Buy and Make (Indian) |
| 4 | **Buy (Global – Manufacture in India)** |
| 5 | **Buy (Global)** – lowest |
!TRAP DAP 2020 has **5** buy categories: **Buy (Global – Manufacture in India)** replaced the old **Buy and Make** category.
### 3. INDIGENISATION LISTS, iDEX AND FUNDS
#### 3.1 Lists and Portal
- **Five Positive Indigenisation Lists**: bar imports of listed items after set dates.
- **SRIJAN**: indigenisation portal; DPSUs and Services list import items Indian industry can make.
#### 3.2 iDEX Ecosystem
| Scheme | Fact |
|---|---|
| **iDEX** (Innovations for Defence Excellence) | Launched **April 2018**; MSMEs, start-ups, academia; **676** start-ups by Mar 2026; outlay ₹498.78 crore |
| **DIO** (Defence Innovation Organisation) | Runs iDEX; not-for-profit set up by **HAL and BEL** (not DRDO) |
| **ADITI** | Under iDEX; funds deep-tech: **AI, quantum, hypersonics, directed energy** (PYQ 2024) |
| **DISC** (Defence India Startup Challenge) | Problem statements set by the **Services** |
!TRAP iDEX "aims at an ecosystem to foster innovation in industry and trade" is **not correct** – it is for **defence**.
#### 3.3 Technology Development Fund and VIMARSH
- **TDF**: MoD programme **executed by DRDO** under Make in India; grants **up to ₹50 crore per project**, esp. MSMEs and start-ups.
- TDF project: **green propulsion system** (1N green monopropellant thruster) by Bengaluru start-up **Bellatrix Aerospace**; proved in orbit on **PSLV-C58** (POEM), announced **1 Feb 2024**.
- **VIMARSH 2026** (Sep): funding, incubation, test-facility access, source-code sharing; **9 ToT agreements to 13 partners**.

### 8. FDI, PRODUCTION AND EXPORTS
#### 8.1 FDI
- **74% automatic route**; **above 74% up to 100% via government route** (where modern technology access is involved).
- Cumulative FDI May 2001 – June 2025: only **₹5,730.55 crore**; **576** licences to private firms since 2013-14.
#### 8.2 Data
| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Production FY 2024-25 | ₹1,51,339 crore |
| Production FY 2025-26 | **Record ₹1.78 lakh crore** |
| Exports | **₹686 crore (2013-14) → ₹23,622 crore (2024-25)**, ~34-fold |
| **DPEPP 2020** targets (2025) | **₹1.75 lakh crore** production, **₹35,000 crore** exports (not ₹50,000 crore) |
| R&D FY 2026-27 | **₹29,100 crore** |
- Buyers: **Philippines (BrahMos)**, **Indonesia (Astra, Jul 2026)**, UAE in talks.
- Limits: India still **outside top 25** arms exporters; imports **jet engines and defence chips**; low FDI; small R&D share.
!TRAP ₹1.75 lakh crore = DPEPP target; ₹1.78 lakh crore = actual FY 2025-26.
