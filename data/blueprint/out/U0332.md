### 3. FIRST ADMINISTRATIVE REFORMS COMMISSION (1966)
- Set up a **study team on Centre-State relations** headed by **M.C. Setalvad**; the ARC based its own report (1969) on it.
- Key recommendation: an **Inter-State Council under Article 263**, with the PM, Central ministers and Chief Ministers – the ARC **asked for it in 1969**.
- It made **22 recommendations** on Centre-State relations; **no action** was taken on them at the time.
- Also recommended deploying central armed forces in States "either on their request or otherwise".
### 4. COMMITTEE OF GOVERNORS – BHAGWAN SAHAY (1970-71)
- Governor = **constitutional head of the State** and a **link with the Centre**.
- Keeps **watch** on the State administration and **reports** to the Centre.
### 5. NCRWC – VENKATACHALIAH COMMISSION
- Also called the **Constitutional Review Commission** (February 2000); reviewed the Constitution's **first 50 years**, including **social justice**.
- **Centre-State chapter:** greater **fiscal autonomy for States**; **safeguards against misuse of Art. 356**; **stronger third tier** (local bodies).
- **Judiciary:** **All India Judicial Service** and a **National Judicial Commission**.
### 6. SECOND ADMINISTRATIVE REFORMS COMMISSION
- Constituted **2005**; **15 reports, 2006-2009**; called the **"Bible" of administrative reforms**.
- Key recommendations: **Code of Ethics**, **Right to Public Services** laws, **Department of E-Governance**, **financial autonomy for PRIs and ULBs**.
- Federal matters: **cooperative federalism**, restrained use of emergency powers, better Centre-State coordination.
- Judiciary: proposed a **National Judicial Council** (2007-08).

### 7. NATIONAL INTEGRATION AND COMMUNAL INQUIRIES
| Body | Head | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| **National Integration Council** | **Prime Minister** | Fights communalism, casteism, regionalism |
| **Sri Krishna Commission** | **B.N. Srikrishna** | **1992-93 Mumbai riots** |
| **Liberhan Commission** | **Justice M.S. Liberhan** | **1992 Babri Masjid demolition** |
- Both inquiry commissions laid guidelines for **police accountability**, **riot prevention** and impartial district administration.
### 8. PARLIAMENTARY AND OTHER FOLLOW-UP
- Centre-State questions (police, education, Inter-State Council) usually go before the **Parliamentary Standing Committee on Home Affairs**.
- **Standing Committee on Rural Development** (Lok Sabha) reports on **devolution to Panchayats** (2009, 2011; action-taken report 2019): Panchayats are a **State subject**, so devolution of **functions, finances and functionaries** is left to each State and **varies across States**.
- The **Standing Committee of the Inter-State Council** (set up 1996, chaired by the **Union Home Minister**) is a different body; it finished discussing all **273 Punchhi recommendations in May 2018**.
### 9. OUTCOMES AND CURRENT
- Only recommendation that became an institution: **Inter-State Council, 1990**, under **Art. 263**, on **Sarkaria's** advice.
- **Chronology:** First ARC study team (1969) → Sarkaria (1983-88) → Inter-State Council (1990) → Punchhi (2007-10).
- Kurian Joseph Committee said federalism needs "a structural reset comparable in ambition to the economic reforms of **1991**"; its report got little attention compared with Sarkaria (2 Sep 2026).
