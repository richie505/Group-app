### 2. RURAL WATER – MGNREGA SCHEDULE I AND JAL JEEVAN MISSION
#### 2.1 MGNREGA Schedule I Amended (Water Works)
- **Ministry of Rural Development** amended **Schedule I of MGNREGA, 2005** – minimum spending on **water works** by groundwater stress.
- Basis: **CGWB Dynamic Ground Water Resources Assessment** (not Census of India).
| Block category (groundwater extraction) | Minimum spend on water works |
|---|---|
| **Over-exploited (>100%)** and **Critical (90–100%)** | **65%** |
| **Semi-critical (70–90%)** | **40%** |
| **Safe (≤70%)** | **30%** |
- **CGWB 2024**: over-exploited **11.13%**, critical **3.05%**, semi-critical **10.54%**, safe **73.39%**.
- India: **largest groundwater user** – about **25% of global extraction**.
!NUM 65% – 40% – 30% = least water-works spend in over-exploited/critical, semi-critical and safe blocks
#### 2.2 Jal Jeevan Mission – AP Angle
- **JJM**: launched **2019** – **Har Ghar Jal**; original target: tap water to **every rural household by 2024**.
- **AP coverage**: **70,51,976** rural households = **73.82%**; at launch (2019) only **32.18%** (about 30.74 lakh).
| AP District | Coverage |
|---|---|
| **YSR** | **100%** (3,29,890 households) |

### 3. IMMUNISATION – UIP, MISSION INDRADHANUSH, ZERO-DOSE CHILDREN
#### 3.1 Universal Immunisation Programme
- **UIP**: **world's largest** – **2.6 crore infants** and **2.9 crore pregnant women** a year; **12 diseases** (11 nationwide + 1 endemic).
- **U5MR**: **45 → 31** per 1,000 (2014–2021, SRS).
- **Mission Indradhanush**: **2014**; **IMI 2017**; by 2023, **12 phases** – **5.46 crore children**, **1.32 crore pregnant women**.
- Digital: **U-WIN** (pregnant women and children **up to 16 years**), **CO-WIN**, **eVIN**, **NCCMIS**, **SAFE-VAC**.
- **COVID vaccination**: from **16 January 2021**; **220+ crore** doses by **January 2023**; **Vaccine Maitri**.
- New: **IMI 5.0 (2023)**; **Zero MR Elimination Campaign (2025)**.
#### 3.2 Immunisation Milestones
| Milestone | Year |
|---|---|
| **Expanded Programme on Immunization (WHO)** | **1974** – 4 billion children, 154 million deaths averted |
| **Universal Immunisation Programme** | **1985** |
| **Polio-free** | **2011** – last case **13 January 2011** (West Bengal); removed from endemic list **2012**; WHO certified the region **27 March 2014** |
| Mission Indradhanush | **2014** |
| **Maternal & neonatal tetanus** eliminated | **2015** |
| **Yaws-free** | **2016** |

!TRAP India's last polio case was in **2011**, not 2015.
#### 3.3 Zero-Dose Children – Lancet Study
- **Global Burden of Disease Vaccine Coverage Collaborators**, **The Lancet**, **25 June 2025**.
- **India: 1.44 million** zero-dose children in 2023 – **second-highest after Nigeria**.
- Global: **15.7 million** zero-dose in 2023; over half in **eight countries**; South Asia **13%**.
- Measles coverage fell in **100 of 204** countries (2010–2019); **21 of 36** high-income countries saw dips.
- **Zero-dose** = no dose of **DTP** vaccine by **age one**.
- **IA2030** goal: **halve zero-dose children by 2030**. Platforms: Co-WIN, U-WIN.
#### 3.4 Maldives – World's First Triple Elimination of MTCT
- **WHO** validated **Maldives** as the **first country** to eliminate **mother-to-child transmission** of **HIV, syphilis and hepatitis B**.
- **Zero** infants born with HIV or syphilis in **2022 and 2023**; health spending **over 10% of GDP**.
- **WHO SEA Region (2024)**: **23,000+** pregnant women with syphilis; **42 million** pregnant women with hepatitis B.
### 4. HEALTH SYSTEM – UHC, PMJAY, HEALTH WORKERS AND DRUGS
#### 4.1 Universal Health Care and PMJAY
- **Bhore Committee (1946)**: defined **UHC** – care to all **irrespective of ability to pay**.
- **PMJAY (2018, Ayushman Bharat)**: **₹5 lakh per household per year**, in-patient care.
- 2023–24: PMJAY covered **58.8 crore** people, budget about **₹12,000 crore**; SHIPs about ₹16,000 crore; total about ₹28,000 crore.
- **Private hospitals** get **two-thirds** of PMJAY spending.
- Utilisation: about **80%** nominal coverage but only **35%** of insured patients used insurance (2022–23).
- Pending dues over **₹12,000 crore**; **609** hospitals opted out; **NHA** flagged **3,200** hospitals for fraud.
- Public health spending: **1.3% of GDP** (2022, World Bank) vs world average **6.1%**.
- **UHC models**: **Canada, Thailand**. **National Health Policy 2017**: public health spending target **2.5% of GDP**.
#### 4.2 Anshakalin Stri Parichars (ASPs) – Maharashtra
- Women community health workers in rural **Maharashtra**; ASPs **predate ASHAs**.
- Wage stuck at **₹3,000 a month since 2016**; State promised **₹6,000 a month by December 2025**.
- **2023**: **Nagpur labour court** said ASPs deserve **Minimum Wages Act** protection – implementation left to the State (it did **not** mandate parity with MPWs).
- **ASHA**: introduced under **NRHM (2005)** – classified as **volunteers**, paid by incentives.
#### 4.3 Drug Safety Deficit
- **GMP** introduced only in **2005**; post-marketing surveillance and annual inspections mandatory only in **2015**.
- **CDSCO** is **not independent** – reports to **DGHS → Health Ministry**; States also license.
- **WHO**: **1 in 10** drugs in developing countries fake/substandard.
- **Branded generics**: **87%** of Indian market.
- Pharma sector: **$50 billion → $130 billion by 2030**; supplies **40% of US generics**, **50–60% of world's vaccines**.
- Cough syrup deaths: **Gambia, Uzbekistan**. Jaipur study: 2 of 8 online pharmacies sold without prescription. Retail chains: quality +5%, prices −2%.
#### 4.4 Jan Aushadhi Stores in All GGHs (AP)
- **AP Health Department**: Jan Aushadhi stores in **all Government General Hospitals (GGHs)**; District and Area Hospitals only at **feasibility** stage.
- Review of **16 GGHs**: 23 private medical stores; margins **50%–600%**; **Nandyal GGH had none**; stocked only 30–40% essential generics.
- **PM-BJAP**: profit margin capped at **15%**; software by **Department of Pharmaceuticals**.
- **Branded drugs** only with **prior approval** when **no generic** exists.
- **Emergency preparedness directive** (India–Pakistan tensions): signed by **G. Veerapandian**, Commissioner of Health & FW and Mission Director, NHM – stocks of medicines, oxygen, blood, trauma kits; all leaves cancelled; district nodal officers; 24x7 control rooms; **CBRN** (Chemical, Biological, Radiological, Nuclear) preparedness.
!TRAP CDSCO is **not** an independent regulator; ASPs (Maharashtra) are **older** than ASHAs.
### 5. LIFESTYLE DISEASES, NUTRITION AND FOOD SECURITY
#### 5.1 ICMR-INDIAB 2025 – High Carbs, Low Protein
- Survey of **1,21,077 adults** across **36 States/UTs**.
- **Carbohydrates: 62%** of daily calories – white rice, milled grains, added sugars.
- **Diabetes**: over **100 million** adults (**ICMR, 2023**).
- Child obesity (**Lancet**): over **12 million** children grossly overweight in 2022 vs **0.4 million** in 1990.
- Measures: **International Year of Millets 2023**; **CBSE "sugar boards"** in schools; **FSSAI Front-of-Pack Labelling (FoPL)** proposals.
#### 5.2 Heart Disease and Stroke
- About **50%** of heart-attack patients are **under 40** (hospital data 2020–2023); 50% of poor heart health is lifestyle-induced.
- **Health of the Nation 2025**: only **25%** of Indians could identify heart symptoms (2024).
- **Stroke**: about **1.8 million** new cases a year; only **1 in 5** survivors get structured rehab (under 20%).
- **WHO (2023)**: stroke rehab should start within **24–48 hours**.
- 2019: **6.99 lakh** stroke deaths – **7.4%** of all deaths. Costs: acute ₹2–8 lakh; rehab ₹10,000–30,000 a month.
- Campaign **FAST** = **Face, Arms, Speech, Time**; programme **NPCDCS**.
#### 5.3 NFSA Ration Card Verification
- About **1.17 crore** ration-card holders flagged **potentially ineligible** under **NFSA, 2013** – Centre's **first such list**.
- By **Department of Food and Public Distribution** – inter-ministerial data matching; tool: **Rightful Targeting Dashboard** (API-based).
- States/UTs to verify and remove by **30 September 2025**; earlier **1.34 crore** bogus cards deleted (2021–2023).
- **Deletion is the job of States/UTs**; the Centre only gives data.
| Database | Category | Flagged |
|---|---|---|
| **Income Tax Dept** | **Taxpayers** | **94.71 lakh** |
| **MoRTH** | **Four-wheeler owners** | **17.51 lakh** |
| **MCA** | **Company directors** | **5.31 lakh** |
| CBDT, CBIC, PM-Kisan | Overlaps | Being verified |
- **NFSA 2013**: covers **67%** of population – **75% rural, 50% urban**; prices **rice ₹3/kg, wheat ₹2/kg, coarse grains ₹1/kg** via TPDS; made **free** under **PM Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana**.
- Ineligible: taxpayers, government employees, households with income **≥ ₹1 lakh**, four-wheeler owners, company directors.
#### 5.4 'Thali' Framework
- About **50% rural** and **20% urban** households **cannot afford two thali meals** a day.
- Big gap in **pulses** consumption between poorest and richest; **PDS subsidy favours cereals**.
### 6. LITERACY AND DIGITAL LITERACY FIRSTS
#### 6.1 Himachal Pradesh – Fully Literate State
- Literacy **99.30%** (benchmark **95%**) – announced by CM **Sukhvinder Singh Sukhu** on **International Literacy Day 2025** at **Puran Sakshar Himachal Samaroh and ULLAS Mela, Shimla**.
- Programme: **ULLAS – New India Literacy Programme**; criterion **≥95% literacy among age 15+**, including **digital and financial literacy**.
- History: about **7%** at Independence → **99.3%** in 2025; HP **1st in student-teacher ratio**.
- HP is the **4th** fully literate State after **Mizoram** (first, **2024**), **Tripura**, **Goa**; **Ladakh** first fully literate **UT (2024)**.
#### 6.2 Kerala – First Fully Digitally Literate State (Digi Kerala)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Date | **21 August 2025** |
| Programme | **Digi Kerala**, modelled on **Digi Pullampara** |
| Certified | **21.87 lakh** (of 21.88 lakh identified); **1.51 crore** people in **83.45 lakh** households surveyed |
| Volunteers | **2.57 lakh**; master trainers trained at **KILA** (Kerala Institute of Local Administration) |
| Evaluator | **Economics & Statistics Department** (third party) |
| Inclusivity | **15,221 trainees above 90 years**; over 13 lakh women, 8 lakh men, **1,644** transgender persons |
| Focus | **Smartphone-based** literacy (not computer) |
- **National Digital Literacy Mission** is limited to **60 years** of age.
- **Pullampara model**: Pullampara panchayat, **Thiruvananthapuram**, started **2021**; **3,917** digitally illiterate identified, **3,300** trained; **15 tasks in 3 modules**, pass = **6 of 15**; success **96.18%**; volunteers – **NSS, Kudumbashree, SC/ST promoters, library council**; **September 2022** – first fully digitally literate panchayat in Kerala.
- **Digi Kerala 2.0** (CM **Pinarayi Vijayan**): cybersecurity literacy, media literacy, service access; **KFON** – free internet to **14,000 BPL** families, 74,203 commercial connections; **K-SMART** – digitisation of all LSG services.
#### 6.3 Other Firsts
- **Bharat Taxi** – **first cooperative ride-hailing platform**.

#### 7.3 Displaced Gutti Koya Tribals
- **Gutti Koya (Gond)** tribals displaced from **Chhattisgarh** by anti-Maoist operations (**Strategic Hamlet Programme**, since 2005); living on forest lands in AP and Telangana for about two decades.
- **ST in Chhattisgarh** but **not ST in AP/Telangana**.
- Strategic hamleting moved about **50,000 Gonds** to roadside camps; strategy borrowed from the **US war in Vietnam**; earlier used in **Mizoram (1960s)**.
- **FRA 2006, Section 3.1(m)**: tribals displaced before cut-off **13 December 2005** get **alternative forest land**.
- No national IDP policy; contrast **Bru Rehabilitation Plan, 2019** (Bru settled in **Tripura**); **NCST** ordered surveys; suggested alignment with **UN Guiding Principles on Internal Displacement**.
### 8. DISABILITY – UDID, CENSUS 2027, LEGAL FRAMEWORK
#### 8.1 UDID Coverage Below 40%
- **Less than 40%** of PwDs have a UDID card; over **11 lakh** applications pending, 60%+ for over 6 months.
- Above 50% coverage: **Tamil Nadu, Meghalaya, Odisha, Karnataka**; **West Bengal only 6%**.
- Highest delays: **Himachal Pradesh, Ladakh, Mizoram** (80%+ pending over 6 months).
- UDID sub-scheme funding **reduced 2019–2023**; only **1 crore** UDID cards issued since **2015**.
- Department: **DEPwD**; UDID covers **21 disabilities**.
- **ADIP** scheme – aids (**wheelchairs, prostheses, hearing aids, Braille slates**); it is not the 21-disability coverage.
#### 8.2 Census 2027 and Disability Data
- **Census 2027**: India's **first digital Census**; first since **2011** (17 years).
| Source | Prevalence |
|---|---|
| **Census 2011** | **26.8 million – 2.21%** |
| **NFHS-5** | **4.52%** |
- Economic loss about **₹4.5 lakh crore a year (4% of GDP)**.
- Employment: **23.8%** disabled (15–59) employed vs 50% others (NSS 2018); private sector **0.4%** (ILO 2021).
- Education: **46%** illiterate (2011); 22.5 lakh disabled children enrolled (UDISE+ 2021-22).
- Census 2011 flaw: **medical yes/no** questions in **8 categories**; suggested **Washington Group – Short Set (WG-SS)**, aligned with the **21 categories** of RPwD Act, 2016; India has **not adopted WG-SS in all surveys**.
- Global: **Canada 22%** (Accessible Canada Act); **Australia 18.3%** (NDIS); **UK 19%**; **China 6.34%** (2020). Indian local surveys: **Salem (2025)**, **Kerala (2014–15)** – 22 categories.

### 9. LABOUR, EPFO, SKILLS, MoUs AND DEMOGRAPHIC DIVIDEND
#### 9.1 Draft Shram Shakti Niti 2025 (National Labour and Employment Policy)
- Released by the **Union Labour Ministry** for **public consultation**.
- **Universal, portable social security accounts** integrating **EPFO, ESIC, PMJAY, e-SHRAM**, State welfare boards.
- **Female LFPR target: 35% by 2030**.
- **OSH Code** with risk-based, **AI-enabled inspections**; green jobs; **'One Nation Integrated Workforce'**.
| Phase | Period |
|---|---|
| **I** | **2025–27** – institutional setup, pilots |
| **II** | **2027–30** – nationwide social security accounts, district Employment Facilitation Cells |
| **III** | **Beyond 2030** – **paperless governance** |
- Monitoring: **Labour & Employment Policy Evaluation Index (LPEI)**; **Annual National Labour Report to Parliament**.
#### 9.2 EPFO Withdrawals and New Norms
- About half of members have **under ₹20,000** at final settlement; 75% under ₹50,000; 87% under ₹1 lakh.
- **EPFO**: **30 crore+** accounts, about **7 crore active**; corpus **over ₹26 lakh crore**; wage ceiling **₹15,000 a month**.
- **95%** of final settlements in 2024–25 were premature; of **52.95 lakh**, **24.21 lakh (46%)** rejoined employment.
- **Para 69(2), EPF Scheme 1952**: full withdrawal after **two months** of unemployment.
- Illness claims 2024–25: **3.25 crore** worth ₹52,633.92 crore (+55%).
- **Reforms**: withdrawal categories **13 → 3** (essential needs, housing, special circumstances); **25% minimum balance**; **education up to 10 times**, **marriage up to 5 times**; premature final settlement wait **2 → 12 months**; **75%** withdrawable immediately after job loss.
- **EPS**: pension eligibility needs **10 years** of pensionable service.

#### 9.4 Demographic Dividend and Shikshit Bharat
- Working-age (15–64): about **two-thirds** of population; **800 million+ below 35**; window till the **2040s**; 60%+ population below 35.
- **Job-ready graduates: only 42–43%** (not 80%); engineering graduates 40–50% unemployed/underemployed.
- **93%** of high school students know only 6–7 careers vs 20,000+ paths.
- AI by 2030 (global): **170 million** jobs created, **92 million** displaced.
- **Skill India target: 400 million by 2022 – missed**.
- Schemes: NEP 2020, NCrF, NAPS 2.0, NATS, NSDM, PMKVY, **PM-DAKSH**, PM Vishwakarma, JSS, **Atal Tinkering Labs**, Start-up India, **Stand-up India** (Digital India is **not** in this list).
- R&D spending about **0.6–0.7% of GDP** vs over 2% China; recommended **1.5%**. **ASER** shows weak foundational literacy and numeracy.
#### 9.5 Ageing – HelpAge India Study
- Elderly (60+): **10% now → about 20% by 2050**; life expectancy about 50 (1970s) → 70+.
- Study **"Understanding Intergenerational Dynamics & Perceptions on Ageing"** – **5,700** respondents, **10 cities**.
- Youth see elders as wise (**51%**) but lonely (**56%**); loneliness concern – youth **69%**, elders **68%**; **78%** youth think elders lack digital interest vs **71%** elders cite youth impatience; **88%** youth expect to live with family in old age.
- Schemes cited: **NPS Vatsalya**, **AB PM-JAY**.

### 10. SCHOOL EDUCATION – CBSE TWO BOARD EXAMS AND RTE QUOTA (AP)
#### 10.1 CBSE Class 10 – Two Board Exams from 2026–27
| Feature | First exam | Second exam |
|---|---|---|
| Month | **Mid-February** | **May** |
| Status | **Mandatory** | **Optional** – improvement in **up to three subjects** (Science, Maths, Social Science, two languages) |
| Results | By **April** | By **June** |
- Aligned with **NEP 2020**; **better of the two scores** counts.
- **Essential Repeat** (failed/absent in **3+ subjects**) **cannot** take the second exam; **Compartment** (1–2 subjects) can use it.
- Flexibility for sports persons and snow-bound areas (e.g. Ladakh).
#### 10.2 25% RTE Quota in AP Private Schools (2025–26)
- **Section 12(1)(c), RTE Act, 2009**: **25%** of **Class 1** seats in **private unaided** schools for **disadvantaged/EWS** children, in AP from **2025–26**.
- Applications **28 April – 15 May** via School Education Dept website; also Village Secretariat, MRC.
- Age: **5 years** completed by **31 March** (IB/CBSE/ICSE) or by **1 June** (State syllabus).
- **RTE Act**: free and compulsory education for **6–14** years; implements **Article 21-A**; **no detention till Class 8**; **no capitation fees**; **School Management Committees**.
