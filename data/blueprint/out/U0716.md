## U0716 | GOVERNMENT SCHEMES PROMOTING ICT – BHARATNET, PMGDISHA, MEGHRAJ, DIGITAL VILLAGE
### 1. BHARATNET
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 25 Oct 2011 | **NOFN** approved for **2.5 lakh** gram panchayats |
| 25 Feb 2012 | **BBNL** (SPV) registered |
| Feb 2023 | BBNL **merged with BSNL** |
| 22 Feb 2026 | AP MoC (Amaravati) with Digital Bharat Nidhi Administrator: **13,426** village panchayats; Centre Rs 2,432 crore |
- Laid by CPSUs **BSNL, RailTel, PGCIL**; target **100 Mbps per GP**, scalable to **1 Gbps**.
| Phase | Focus |
|---|---|
| I and II | Fibre to GPs via CPSUs and states |
| III / **Amended BharatNet** | **Rs 1.39 lakh crore**; **PPP**, bigger role for states, revenue sharing, last mile, 5G links |
- AP: state SPV = AP BharatNet Infrastructure Ltd; 1,692 Phase-I GPs upgraded + 11,254 Phase-II villages + 480 new GPs.
- **APSWAN** (AP State Wide Area Network): initial capacity **2 Mbps**. **AP FiberNet** reached **4,000+ tribal hamlets** (Eastern Ghats).
### 2. RURAL CONNECTIVITY FUNDS AND ACCESS
- **USOF**: **non-lapsable** fund for telecom in commercially unviable rural areas; funded NOFN.
- Renamed **Digital Bharat Nidhi** under the **Telecommunications Act 2023**; 5% of collections to the Telecom Technology Development Fund.
- **LWE Phase I**: USOF-funded 2G sites by BSNL; **2,343** sites radiating, approved for upgrade to **4G**.
- **PM-WANI**: public Wi-Fi, **December 2020**; 3.15 lakh hotspots.
### 3. DIGITAL LITERACY – PMGDISHA AND SCHOOLS
- **NDLM** and **DISHA** (Digital Saksharta Abhiyan) came before PMGDISHA.
- **PMGDISHA**: launched by PM Modi at **Gandhinagar**; **6 crore rural households**, one person each; age **14-60**.
- Closed **31 March 2024**: 7.35 crore registered, 6.39 crore trained, 4.78 crore certified.
| Scheme | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Vidya Vahini** | Computer education in primary government schools |
| **ICT@Schools** | Computer labs in government schools |
| **e-Pathshala** | MHRD + NCERT (Nov 2015); e-textbooks |
| E&ICT Academy II | MeitY; 1,35,000 faculty via 14 agencies |
### 4. MEGHRAJ – GI CLOUD
- Started by **DeitY**; common pool of cloud infrastructure and apps on demand.
| Feature | Content |
|---|---|
| **5** characteristics | On-demand self-service, ubiquitous network access, metered use, elasticity, resource pooling |
| **3** service models | IaaS, PaaS, SaaS |
| **4** deployment models | Public, private, hybrid, community |
- Architecture: national and state clouds + **e-Gov App Store**; **MeitY empanels** private Cloud Service Providers.
!TRAP Not a characteristic: dedicated hardware per user. Not a model: "satellite cloud".
