## U0762 | RENEWABLE ENERGY IN ANDHRA PRADESH – POTENTIAL, INSTALLED CAPACITY AND STATE POLICY
### 1. SOLAR PARK CONCEPTS
- **Solar Park Scheme** (MNRE, **12 December 2014**): CFA **₹25 lakh per park** for DPR + up to **₹20 lakh/MW or 30%** of cost.
- **Ultra Mega Solar Park = 500 MW or more** at one location (APPSC 2019).
- **Floating solar:** India ~0.7 GW installed vs **102 GW** potential; largest = **Omkareshwar** (278 MW, Narmada, **Madhya Pradesh**).
- **Largest solar park of India (2023): Bhadla** Solar Park; **Shakti Sthala** = expected largest solar park in Karnataka.
!TRAP Ultra Mega threshold is 500 MW, not 1,000 MW.

### 3. NEW INVESTMENTS AND WIND
- **SAEL 600 MW** AC solar, **Kadapa** (₹3,000 crore; 2,400 acres): **first project commissioned under ICEP-2024**, in 11 months (May 2026).
- **Tata Renewable 800 MW hybrid** (₹5,750 crore): **400 MW solar at Pathikonda (Kurnool) + 400 MW wind at Kanekallu (Anantapur)** (Jul 2026).
- Wind: major **2025 wind partnership in Anantapur** (APPSC 2025).
### 4. STATE ENERGY POLICIES
| Policy | Key Facts |
|---|---|
| **AP Green Hydrogen and Green Ammonia Policy (2023)** | Green fuels from RE; nodal **NREDCAP** |
| **Integrated Clean Energy Policy (ICEP) 2024** | **160 GW** new RE by 2029 (**78.5 GW solar, 35 GW wind**); **22 GW pumped storage**, **25 GWh BESS**; **1.5 MTPA green hydrogen**, 3 GW electrolysers; ~₹10 lakh crore investment; supports biofuels and green hydrogen export; **net zero by 2047** |
- **Land policy (Apr 2026):** **50,000 acres of barren land** opened for solar leases to avoid diversion of farmland.
### 5. INSTALLED CAPACITY AND MIX
- AP RE capacity **10,639.75 MW** = **solar 5,614.52 MW (~53%) + wind 4,397.67 MW (~41%)**.
- AP added **3,865.63 MW** RE in 2025-26, nearly four times 2024-25 (1,085.15 MW).
- India solar **162.15 GW** vs AP **5.6 GW**.
- **State ranking (31 Mar 2026):** AP total RE incl. large hydro **15,980 MW** (~5.8% of India's 274.7 GW) = **6th** after Gujarat, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka.
- AP is also **6th in solar (7,494.8 MW)** and **6th in wind (4,415.8 MW)**; large hydro 3,290 MW.
!TRAP AP is 6th, not in the top five; Gujarat leads total RE and wind, Rajasthan leads solar.
- After **bifurcation AP lost its coal mines** → long coal haulage, poor coal quality.
