### 4. CHABAHAR
- **Sistan-Baluchestan**, Gulf of Oman; access to **Afghanistan and Central Asia bypassing Pakistan**.
- **Shahid Beheshti** terminal, **IPGL**; contract **May 2024, 10 years** (not 20).
- US sanctions main issue; US strikes (Jul 2026) – terminal not damaged.
### 5. ENERGY SECURITY
- Imports ~**88%** crude, ~**50%** gas (2026); ~50% crude via **Hormuz**.
- **Third-largest** energy consumer; four A's: availability, accessibility, affordability, **acceptability**.
- **TAPI** = Turkmenistan-Afghanistan-Pakistan-India; **IPI** = **"Peace Pipeline"** (~2,775 km).
- **SPR:** Visakhapatnam, Mangaluru, Padur (5.33 MMT); **9.5 days** vs IEA's 90. **UAE** – 4th crude supplier, only one storing oil in SPR.
