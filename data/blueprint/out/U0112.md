## U0112 | SULTANATE CENTRAL ADMINISTRATION – SULTAN, WAZIR, DIWANS, NOBILITY, THEORY OF KINGSHIP
### 1. THE SULTAN AND THE STATE
- Sultans saw themselves as the **Caliph's representatives**; not every Sultan got investiture.
- Sultan = **supreme commander** and **highest court of appeal**; supreme in military, legal and political matters.
- **Succession: no fixed rule**; acceptance by **nobles and ulema**, or **military strength**.
- **Naib**: ranked next to the Sultan; in practice controlled all departments.
### 2. LEGAL BASIS AND NATURE OF THE STATE
| Element | Content |
|---|---|
| **Sharia** | **Civil matters of Muslims** |
| **Zawabit** | **Royal decrees** that supplemented the sharia |
| Criminal law | Followed the **Sultan's rules** |
| Hindus | Kept their **own personal law** |
- Government = mix of **Perso-Arabic, Turko-Mongol and Indian** elements (JPSC 2021).
| Perso-Arabic | Turko-Mongol |
|---|---|
| Title **Sultan**; Caliph's nominal authority; **sijda, paibos, Nauroz** | Army in **decimal units** (khan, malik, amir, sipahsalar); **mounted archers** |
- Essentially a **military, centralised** state.
### 3. THEORY OF KINGSHIP
| Sultan | Theory / claim |
|---|---|
| **Iltutmish** | Legitimacy through **caliphal investiture** |
| **Balban** | **Zil-i-Ilahi** (Shadow of God) + **Niyabat-i-Khudai** (deputy of God); **sijda, paibos, Nauroz**; **blood and iron**; no low-born men in office |
| **Alauddin** | State interest above ulema; "**kingship knows no kinship**"; **Sikandar-i-Sani** (Alexander the Second) |
| **MBT** | **Rationalist absolutism**; appointed non-Turks and low-born men; coin legend "**Al Sultan Zilli Allah**" |
- **Balban**: **first** Muslim ruler with a theory like the **divine right of kings**; Sultan = God's representative on earth, ranked **next to the Paigambar**; term **Zillillah first used** by him.
- Balban's real name **Baha-ud-din**; title **Ulugh Khan** from **Nasiruddin Mahmud**; he did **not** introduce the iqtadari system.
!TRAP Din-i-Ilahi = Akbar; Tuti-i-Hind = Amir Khusrau. Neither is a Sultanate kingship term.
### 4. RELATIONS WITH THE CALIPHATE
| Sultan | Relation |
|---|---|
| **Iltutmish** | **Mansur / khillat** of investiture from the **Abbasid Caliph of Baghdad (1229)**; Sultanate part of **Dar-ul-Islam**; Caliph's name on coins |
| **Balban** | "**Helper of a Caliph**" in the **Garhmukteshwar** mosque inscription |
| **Alauddin Khalji** | Titles **Yamin-ul-Khilafat Nasir-i-Amir-ul-Mominin** on coins, **without investiture** (UPPSC 2023) |
| **Mubarak Shah Khalji** | **Proclaimed himself Caliph**; titles Al-Imam, Khilafatullah |
| **MBT** | Sought investiture from the **Abbasid Caliph in Egypt** |
| Firoz | Respected the Caliph |
!PAIR Baghdad Caliph | Iltutmish | 1229 || Egypt Caliph | MBT | investiture
- Coins: **Muhammad Ghori** = Goddess **Lakshmi** with the **Kalma**; **Mahmud of Ghazni** = silver coins in **Arabic and Devanagari Sanskrit**.
### 5. CENTRAL DEPARTMENTS (DIWANS)
| Diwan | Head | Function | Sultan |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Diwan-i-Wizarat** | **Wazir** | **Finance and revenue**; helped by Auditor-General and Accountant-General | From the start |
| **Diwan-i-Arz** | **Ariz-i-Mumalik** | **Army / defence**: recruitment, inspection, pay | **Balban** |
| **Diwan-i-Insha** | **Dabir-i-Khas** | **Royal correspondence**, decrees | Early |
| **Diwan-i-Risalat** | **Sadr-us-Sudur** | **Religious affairs**, endowments, grants to mosques and madrasas | Early |
| **Diwan-i-Qaza** | **Qazi-ul-Quzat** | **Justice** | — |
| **Diwan-i-Mustakhraj** | — | **Recovering arrears** from revenue officials | **Alauddin** |
| **Diwan-i-Riyasat** | Naib-i-Riyasat | **Market control** | **Alauddin** |
| **Diwan-i-Kohi** (Amir-i-Kohi) | — | **Agriculture**; extend cultivation, **planned rotation of crops** | **MBT** |
| **Diwan-i-Bandagan** | — | **Slaves** | **Firoz** |
| **Diwan-i-Khairat** | — | **Charity** | **Firoz** |
- Chief Sadr was also the **Chief Qazi**.
- Wizarat was inspired by **Persia**; **heyday under the Tughlaqs (Firoz)**, with **Khan-i-Jahan Maqbul** running the government.
- Firoz: most slaves (about **1,80,000**), paid **10 to 100 tankas**; banned cruel punishments (**amputation, molten lead, iron nails**).
!TRAP "Alauddin first set up a separate Ariz department" is FALSE; it was Balban.
### 6. CENTRAL OFFICIALS → DUTY
| Official | Duty |
|---|---|
| **Ariz-i-Mumalik** | Head of the army department |
| **Sadr-us-Sudur** | Religious affairs, endowments |
| **Qazi-ul-Quzat** | **Chief Justice** |
| **Barid-i-Mumalik** | **News and intelligence** |
| **Wakil-i-Dar** | **Court and royal household** |
| **Khareetadar** | **Despatcher of royal decrees** |
| **Dabir-i-Khas** | Royal correspondence |
- **Mir Bakshi** did **not** exist under the Sultans; it is **Mughal** (UPSC 2019).
### 7. WAZIRS AND KEY NOBLES
| Noble | Sultan served | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Nizam-ul-Mulk Junaidi** | **Iltutmish** (not MBT) | Wazir |
| **Imad-ul-Mulk** | **Balban** | Commander of Diwan-i-Arz, **freed from the Wazir's financial control** |
| **Malik Kafur** | **Alauddin** | **Naib Malik** |
| **Ain-ul-Mulk Multani** | Alauddin (won **Malwa, 1305**); also MBT | Noble |
| **Khan-i-Jahan Maqbul** | **Firoz Tughlaq** | Wazir; heyday of wizarat |
- Maqbul: a **Telugu Brahmin convert**; a Malik Maqbul is recorded at **Warangal** after 1323 (AP angle).
### 8. NOBILITY OVER TIME
#### 8.1 The Chalisa (Turkan-i-Chahalgani)
- **Iltutmish** created **the Forty**: **Turkish slave-nobles**; Turkish monopoly of office.
- They made and unmade Sultans after him, including over **Razia**.
- **Balban**, himself one of the Forty, **destroyed** their power.
- After Balban, nobles set aside his nominee **Kai Khusrau**, raised **Kaiqubad**; **Jalaluddin Khalji** took power in **1290**.
#### 8.2 Later Dynasties
| Dynasty | Change |
|---|---|
| **Khalji revolution (1290)** | High office opened to **non-Turks and Indian Muslims**; Alauddin crushed Balbani and Jalali nobles, banned noble gatherings, spied on nobles |
| **Tughlaq** | MBT promoted **foreigners and low-born**; Firoz made **iqtas hereditary** |
| **Lodi** | **Afghan tribal nobility**; Sultan = **first among equals** |
### 9. IQTA AND LOCAL OFFICIALS
- **Iqta**: revenue assignment; the system was organised by **Iltutmish**; its holder (governor) = **muqti / wali**.
- **Firoz Tughlaq** made iqtas **hereditary** (earlier they were not).
| Unit | Official | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Iqta** (province) | **Muqti / Wali** | Governor |
| **Shiq** | **Shiqdar** | In charge of a shiq |
| **Pargana** | **Amil** | **Revenue collection** (UPSC 2019) |
| **Village** | **Muqaddam / Chaudhari** | **Village headman**; chief rural land-revenue official (not an accountant) |
| Village | **Patwari** | Village **accountant** |
| — | **Khut** | Hereditary rural landholder; **Alauddin abolished the khut's cess** |
### 10. MILITARY
- **Balban** set up **Diwan-i-Arz** for a **large standing army** against the **Mongols**; head **Ariz-i-Mamalik** (recruitment, inspection, pay).
- Army in **decimal units**; **horse-based warfare** with mounted archers.
- **AP angle**: the Delhi model passed to the **Bahmanis** (Majlis-i-Malakat, Wakil-us-Saltanat, Amir-i-Jumla) and **Qutb Shahis** (Peshwa, **Mir Jumla = finance**, **Ain-ul-Mulk = army**, Dabir's Diwan-i-Insha). Sultanate diwans prefigured the Mughal **Diwan, Mir Bakshi, Sadr-us-Sudur**.

- First separate army department: Balban.
- Only Sultan to proclaim himself Caliph: Mubarak Shah Khalji.
