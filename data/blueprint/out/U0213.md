## U0213 | REVOLUTIONARY LITERATURE IN TELUGU – SRI SRI, ABHYUDAYA MOVEMENT
### 1. PATH OF TELUGU POETRY
- Telugu poetry turned from the **nationalist** struggle to the **class struggle**.
1. **Nationalist poetry** (Gurajada, Rayaprolu, Garimella)
2. **Abhyudaya (progressive)** – 1930s-40s, organised by **Arasam (1943)**
3. **Sri Sri's Shashtipurti, Vizag (Feb 1970)** – the spark
4. **Viplava (revolutionary)** – **1970, Virasam**, after the **Srikakulam** struggle
!KEY Abhyudaya = progressive (Arasam); Viplava = revolutionary (Virasam).
#### 1.1 Three Phases Compared
| Feature | Bhava Kavitvam | Abhyudaya | Viplava |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meaning | Romantic poetry | **Progressive** poetry | **Revolutionary** poetry |
| Period | Before Abhyudaya | **1930s-40s** | **From 1970** |
| Leading figure | Rayaprolu Subbarao | **Sri Sri** | **Sri Sri** (Virasam president) |
| Body | — | **Arasam (1943)** | **Virasam (1970)** |
| Theme | Love, nature, personal emotion | Poor, workers, social injustice, class | Armed change, class war, Srikakulam |
| Key text | Andhravali | **Mahaprasthanam** | **Maro Prasthanam**, Jhamjha |
| Form | Freed from rigid prosody | **Free verse** in spoken idiom | Songs, free verse |
- Progressive (Abhyudaya) writers called the romanticism of Bhava Kavitvam **"escapist"**.
- Abhyudaya sought **gradual change** by bringing social consciousness into literature; Viplava called for **revolution**.
- Progressive Telugu writing had appeared by **1933** (Jayabheri), ten years before Arasam was organised.
### 2. WRITERS' AND CULTURAL BODIES
#### 2.1 Comparison Table
| Body | Year / place | Founder / note |
|---|---|---|
| **AIPWA** (All India Progressive Writers' Association) | **1936, Lucknow** | Founding session presided by **Premchand** |
| **Arasam** (Abhyudaya Rachayitala Sangham) | **1943**; first conference at **Tenali** | Telugu Progressive Writers' Association; first conference presided by **Tapi Dharma Rao** |
| **Praja Natya Mandali** | **1943** | Communist cultural front; Andhra wing of the **Indian People's Theatre Association** |
| **Virasam** (Viplava Rachayitala Sangham) | **July 1970, Hyderabad** | Revolutionary Writers' Association; **Founding President Sri Sri** |
| **Jana Natya Mandali** | **1971** | Grew from **Gaddar** and B. Narsinga Rao's **Art Lovers (1970)** |
#### 2.2 AIPWA and Arasam
- **Arasam = Telugu counterpart of the AIPWA** (1936, Lucknow).
- Through the **AIPWA and the AISF**, communists spread anti-British revolutionary ideas.
- Arasam **organised the Abhyudaya trend that Sri Sri's poems started**.
#### 2.3 Virasam (1970)
- Spark: Sri Sri's **Shashtipurti** (60th birthday) in **Vizag, Feb 1970**.
- Poetry collections: **Jhamjha (1970)**, **Le**, **Viplavam Vardhillali**.
- Publications **banned**: **Le** and **March**.
- Vice-President (1970): **Kodavatiganti Kutumba Rao**.
!TRAP Calling Virasam the "Progressive Writers' Association" is wrong – Arasam is the progressive body; Virasam is the revolutionary body.
### 3. SRI SRI – PERSON
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Real name | **Srirangam Srinivasa Rao** |
| Born | **2 Jan 1910, Vizag** |
| Title | **Mahakavi** (Maha Kavi) – from the fame of *Mahaprasthanam* |
| Influences | **Marx and Hegel** |
| Early body | **Sahiti Samiti** (Vizag), co-founded with **Puripanda Appalaswamy** |
| Award | **National Award for Best Lyricist** – **"Telugu Veera Levara"** (film *Alluri Seetharama Raju*, **1974**); called the first National Award for a Telugu lyricist |
| Later organisation | **Founding President of Virasam (1970)** |
### 4. SRI SRI – WORKS
| Work | Note |
|---|---|
| ***Mahaprasthanam*** | About **41 poems**, written **1933-47**, published **1950**; first poem ***Jayabheri* (1933)**; dedicated to **Kompella Janardhana Rao**; preface by **Chalam**; founding text of **Abhyudaya kavitvam** |
| ***Khadga Srushti*** | Need for radical, **armed change** |
| ***Maro Prasthanam*** | His revolutionary (**post-1970**) phase |
| ***Siprali*** | **Sirisirimuvva satakam** plus **limericks** |
| ***Anantham*** | **Autobiography** |
- **Chronology**: Jayabheri (1933) → Mahaprasthanam published (1950) → Virasam phase (1970).
- Debate: traditionalists said his **free verse destroyed the "sweetness" of Telugu**; supporters say he made Telugu poetry modern.
- **6 Jan 2026**: the CM cited Sri Sri among the greats of Telugu literature at the World Telugu Mahasabhalu.
### 5. ABHYUDAYA WRITERS → WORKS
| Writer | Works / role |
|---|---|
| **Arudra** (Bhagavatula Sadasiva Sankara Sastri, **Sri Sri's nephew**) | ***Tvamevaham*** (1948-49, on the **Telangana struggle**), ***Cinevali***, ***Samagra Andhra Sahityam***; translated *Veera Telangana Viplava Geethalu* |

### 8. PRAJA NATYA MANDALI AND PROGRESSIVE SONGS
- **Communist cultural front**: spread ideas through **drama, songs and folk forms**.
- Main folk form: **Burrakatha**.
- **Garikapati Rajarao** and Koduri Atchaiah led its district workshops teaching folk forms.
- Activities were **suspended by 1948**.
- Play ***Maa Bhoomi*** – **Sunkara Satyanarayana and Vasireddy Bhaskara Rao**, staged by Praja Natya Mandali.
- **Jana Natya Mandali (1971)** carried revolutionary songs (Gaddar) into the Viplava phase.
| Feature | Praja Natya Mandali | Jana Natya Mandali |
|---|---|---|
| Year | 1943 | 1971 |
| Phase | Abhyudaya / communist cultural front | Viplava / revolutionary |
| Origin | Andhra wing of IPTA | Art Lovers (1970) of Gaddar and B. Narsinga Rao |
| Forms | Burrakatha, drama, songs | Revolutionary songs, folk forms |
| Known work | *Maa Bhoomi* | Gaddar's songs |
