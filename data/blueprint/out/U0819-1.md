### 3. NEW BODIES AND UNION CABINET DECISIONS
#### 3.1 Integrated Transport and Logistics Authority (ITLA)
- Approved by the **Union Cabinet on 6 October 2026** as an **SPV** for **research, planning, appraisal and monitoring** in transport and logistics.
- Prepares the **National Transport Master Plan** with a horizon of **10+ years** (roads, rail, ports, aviation, waterways, coastal, urban, logistics).
- **Technical appraisal** of central projects of **₹500 crore or more** (financial appraisal stays with existing mechanisms).
- Related: **PM Gati Shakti NMP** - launched **October 2021**, **GIS-based**.
#### 3.2 Digital Communications Commission (DCC) abolished
- DCC: top telecom decision body for **37 years**; earlier the **Telecom Commission** (est. **1989**), renamed **DCC in 2018**; under **DoT, Ministry of Communications**.
- Projects of **₹500-1,000 crore** that DCC examined now go to the **Expenditure Finance Committee** (Finance Ministry).
- Unchanged: **Telecom Secretary** approves up to **₹100 crore**; **Telecom Minister** up to **₹500 crore**.
#### 3.3 RoDTEP extended
- **RoDTEP** = Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products; extended to **31 December 2026**.
- In force from **1 January 2021**; replaced **MEIS** (WTO objections); **WTO-compliant**.
- **Notified** by the **Department of Commerce through DGFT**; **implemented** by the **Department of Revenue through CBIC**.
!TRAP RoDTEP: DGFT notifies, CBIC implements - not the other way round.
#### 3.4 Mission Rangeen Machhli-2031
- Strategic action plan for **ornamental fisheries**, released by the **Vice-President at Agatti, Lakshadweep** (Lakshadweep capital **Kavaratti**).
- **Department of Fisheries**; linked with **PMMSY**.
- National standards for **broodstock, breeding, hatcheries, quarantine**; traceability and certification for **captive-bred fish** (to stop illegal coral-reef collection).
- Clusters with **air-cargo links**; training for **coastal fishers, youth and women SHGs**.

### 4. REGULATORY, PRESS AND STATE COMMISSIONS
#### 4.1 Press Council of India (PCI)
- **[17 Oct 2026]** **15th PCI reconstituted**: **MIB nominated 13 journalists** (**six editors, seven working journalists**) under **Sections 5 and 6 of the Press Council Act, 1978**.
- **Chairperson**: former SC judge **Justice Ranjana Prakash Desai**, since **24 April 2026**, for **three years**.
- PCI: **statutory, autonomous, quasi-judicial**; first set up **1966** under the **Press Council Act, 1965**; **HQ New Delhi**.
- **Chairperson + 28 members**: **13 journalists**, **6 newspaper owners**, **5 MPs (3 LS + 2 RS)**, **3 from UGC, Bar Council and Sahitya Akademi**, **1 news agency**.
- Chairperson chosen by the **RS Chairman, LS Speaker and one PCI member**; tenure **three years**.
- Can **warn, admonish, censure** but has **no criminal penalties**.
- **National Press Day: 16 November**; press freedom under **Article 19(1)(a)**.

### 6. REPORTS AND NATIONAL DATA
#### 6.1 NITI Aayog report on the care economy
- **[27 Oct 2026]** India's **60+ population**: **149 million (2022)**, **194 million (2031)**, **347 million (2050, about 20.8%)**.
- Funds for training **1.5 lakh multi-skilled caregivers**; family caregivers about **10%** of the population.
- Recommends: **National Care Council**; **National Care Qualification Framework** with regional training centres; **National Caregiver Registry and portal**; specialised training (**dementia, palliative, mental health**); caregiver welfare; formalising the care economy for **women's employment**.
- **NPHCE** launched **2010-11**; **International Day of Older Persons: 1 October**.
#### 6.2 Foodgrain target 2026-27
- Announced by **Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan**, **29 September 2026**.
- Target **373.93 MT** - **slightly lower** than the record **376.56 MT (2025-26)**.
- **Rabi 2026-27 target: 177.72 MT** (vs 171.14-174.51 MT in 2025-26).
- **Kharif**: sown with the **southwest monsoon**, harvested in autumn; main cereal **rice**. **Rabi**: sown in winter, harvested in spring; main cereal **wheat**. **Zaid**: between Rabi and Kharif.
!TRAP The 2026-27 target (373.93 MT) is LOWER than the 2025-26 record (376.56 MT).
#### 6.3 NCRB data (latest edition)
- **NCRB** (HQ **New Delhi**) released **Crime in India 2024** in **May 2026**: the first full-year data under the **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023**; total cognisable crime down about **6%**; cybercrime up about **17.9%**.
- Companion **ADSI** report: **1,70,746 suicides** in 2024.

### 9. SOCIAL SECTOR: ELDERLY, WOMEN, DRUGS, PANCHAYATS
#### 9.1 Kerala Elderly Care Bill
- **Kerala**: **first State** with a dedicated **comprehensive elderly care bill** - protection from **abuse, neglect, illegal property occupation**; **mandatory registration of old-age homes**; **jail and fines**.
- **MWPSC Act 2007**: senior citizen = **60 years or above**; **Section 23** - property transfer can be declared **void**; **Article 41**; International Day of Older Persons **1 October**.
#### 9.2 Kerala Police "Speak Up" task force
- Constituted **8 October 2026**; special officer **IG Ajitha Begum**; against domestic violence.
- **PWDVA 2005** covers **physical, sexual, verbal, emotional and economic** abuse; **NCW** est. **1992**; emergency number **ERSS 112**.
#### 9.3 Drug-free India by 2029
- **Amit Shah**, high-level meeting at **Panaji, Goa**: action plan for **drug-free India by 2029**, linked to **Viksit Bharat 2047**.
- Target **whole networks and supply chains**, not just small offenders; **four-tier NCORD** (Narco-Coordination Centre) strengthened.
- **NCB**: **1986**, **MHA**; **NDPS Act, 1985**; **Article 47 (DPSP)** - prohibition of intoxicants except medicinal.
- **Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan**: **2020**, **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment**; International Day Against Drug Abuse: **26 June**.
#### 9.4 Panchayat-level climate action
- Climate plans to be part of the **Gram Panchayat Development Plan (GPDP)** - no parallel bureaucracy; initiatives also in **Uttar Pradesh and Karnataka**.
- **Bela Gram Panchayat (Maharashtra)**: called **India's first net-zero panchayat**; **90,000+ trees**, solar panels, waste segregation.
- **73rd Amendment** - **Part IX**; **Article 243G** - powers to Panchayats for **economic development and social justice**.
- **Eleventh Schedule**: agriculture, minor irrigation, social forestry, drinking water, **non-conventional energy** etc.
- **PESA Act, 1996**: Gram Sabha protects community resources in **Scheduled Areas**.
- **NAPCC: 2008, eight national missions**; **SAPCCs** at State level.
