## U0582 | PROJECTS OF EXTERNAL ASSISTANCE IN ANDHRA PRADESH – WORLD BANK, ADB, JICA AND OTHER FUNDED PROJECTS
### 1. EXTERNALLY AIDED PROJECTS (EAPs) – THE BASICS
- An **Externally Aided Project (EAP)** is a State project financed by a **multilateral or bilateral lender** (World Bank, ADB, JICA, AIIB, NDB, KfW, UNDP), routed through the Centre.
- In AP, EAPs cover **agriculture and irrigation**, **roads**, **urban water**, **power transmission**, **health**, **poverty** and the **Amaravati capital**.
- **Domestic lenders** such as **HUDCO** and **NABARD** are **not** external assistance.
!KEY Multilateral = World Bank, ADB, AIIB, NDB; bilateral = JICA (Japan), KfW (Germany); UN agency = UNDP.

### 3. WORLD BANK PROJECTS IN AP
#### 3.1 Climate-Resilient Agriculture – APIIATP
- Aim: **climate resilience and productivity** in **565 minor-tank villages** across **22 districts**.
- **Concluded 31 October 2025** (SES 2025-26).
#### 3.2 Rural Inclusive Growth Project
- Approved by the World Bank board on **19 December 2014**; World Bank financing **$75 million** of a **$107 million** cost.
- **Five components**, the first being **value chain development**; others include **human development** and **project implementation support**.
- Aim: raise **agricultural incomes** of selected poor households and improve access to human development services and social entitlements.
- Implementing agency: **SERP** (Project Management Unit headed by SERP's CEO).
#### 3.3 Roads, Health and Disaster Recovery
- **AP Road Sector Project**: repair/strengthening of **850 km** of **MDRs and State highways**, climate-resilient (February 2026).
- **AP Health Systems Strengthening**: World Bank.
- **AP Disaster Recovery Project**: after **Cyclone Hudhud (2014)**.

### 5. ADB PROJECTS
- **Visakhapatnam-Chennai Industrial Corridor (VCIC)**: **ADB**; first phase of the East Coast Economic Corridor idea; progress **remains slow**.
- **Amaravati** Phase-I: ADB co-lender with the World Bank ($788.8 million).
- **APCRDA** implements Amaravati works, **not VCIC**.
### 6. NDB, KfW, AIIB AND JICA PROJECTS
#### 6.1 New Development Bank – Roads
- Road projects worth **₹6,400 crore**; financing **70:30** (NDB : State).
- **Phase-I**: **₹3,014 crore** for **1,243 km**.
- **Phase-II** (proposed): double-lane links from **district HQ to mandal HQ**:
  - **APRBRP** (Roads & Bridges Reconstruction): **539.45 km** of State highways.
  - **APMCRCIP** (Mandal & Rural Connectivity): **728.59 km** of MDR/ODR.
- Implementing agency: **R&B** department.
#### 6.2 KfW (Germany)
- **Green Energy Corridor Phase-I**: **KfW 40% : NCEF 40% : APTRANSCO 20%**; ₹1,379.75 crore, 8 packages.
- GEC status: **4 of 8** packages done (APSES 2024-25) → **fully completed by December 2025** (SES 2025-26).
- **APCNF (Zero Budget Natural Farming)**: KfW loan of **EUR 90 million** + EUR 1 million grant; **₹711 crore** loan pact signed **January 2020**; results-based; implemented by **Rythu Sadhikara Samstha**.
#### 6.3 AIIB – Urban Water
- **Urban water supply and sewerage** projects; part of **₹19,561 crore** mobilised with **AMRUT 2.0** and others.
- **Online Works Monitoring System**: tracks **800+** projects in **123 ULBs**, incl. AIIB works (July 2026).
#### 6.4 JICA – Irrigation
- **APILIP-II** (AP Irrigation and Livelihood Project Phase-II).

### 8. EAP REPAYMENT UNDER SPECIAL ASSISTANCE
- **Special Assistance Measure** (Union Cabinet, **15 March 2017**), given **in lieu of Special Category Status**.
- Centre repays **loans and interest** of EAPs signed in **2015-16 to 2019-20**; Centre bears **90%** of EAP cost.
- Centre reported disbursing **₹7,798 crore** of loans for **17 EAPs** in AP; largest: **VCIC (₹1,859 crore)**, **Health Systems Strengthening (₹935 crore)**.
!TRAP 90% = Centre's EAP share under the package; **90:10** = SCS CSS funding pattern.
