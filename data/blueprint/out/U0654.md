## U0654 | INDIA - RURAL AND URBAN COMPONENTS; LEVEL OF URBANISATION AND ITS REGIONAL PATTERN
### 1. CENSUS DEFINITION OF URBAN
| Type | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Statutory town** | Any place with a **municipality, corporation, cantonment board or notified town area committee**, declared by State law |
| **Census town** | Population **5,000+**; **75%+** of male main workers non-agricultural; density **400+ per sq km** |
- 2011 towns **7,935** = **4,041 statutory** + **3,894 census towns**.
- Census towns **1,362 (2001) → 3,894 (2011)** - shows reclassification as a major component.
### 2. RURAL COMPONENT - VILLAGES
- Villages **6,40,867**; inhabited **5,97,608 (about 93.2%)**.
- Rural population **about 83.3 crore** (2011).
- MPPSC 2010: "about how many villages" - answer "None of these"; true figure about 6.4 lakh.
!KEY 2001-11: **first time since Independence** urban India added more people than rural India.
### 3. LEVEL OF URBANISATION
| Measure (2011) | Fact |
|---|---|
| India urban share | **31.16%** (27.8% in 2001) |
| Most urbanised State | **Goa ~62.2%** |
| Least urbanised State | **Himachal Pradesh ~10%** (highest rural share, about 90%) |
| Most urbanised UT | **Delhi 97.5%**, then Chandigarh 97.25% |
#### 3.1 Other estimates
- **EAC-PM** satellite data: about **63% in 2015** vs Census 31%.
- **World Bank** (2010) "urban-like": **55.3%**.
- **16th Finance Commission**: **41% by 2031**; urban bodies' share of local grants **36% → 45%**.
- Budget 2026-27: **Rs 5,000 crore per City Economic Region** over five years.
- Debate: the binary rural-urban definition leaves fast-urbanising areas under panchayats with weak finances.
### 4. COMPONENTS OF URBAN GROWTH
1. **Natural increase** - similar in rural and urban areas.
2. **Rural-to-urban migration** - explains most of the rural-urban gap.
3. **Reclassification** - villages turn into towns / new towns form.
4. **Expansion of town boundaries** over villages.
!TRAP "Decline" is not a component; migration, not natural increase, explains the gap.
### 5. DRIVERS OF URBANISATION
| Factor | Examples |
|---|---|
| Pull | Industrialisation and jobs, trade and commerce, better health and education, transport links |
| Push | Failed monsoon → rural unemployment → migration; poverty, landlessness |
- **Rakesh Mohan**: three processes - **metropolitanisation, commercialisation, rural (subsistence) urbanisation**.
### 6. REGIONAL PATTERN
| Level | Regions / States |
|---|---|
| More urbanised | **South and west**: Goa, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Maharashtra, Gujarat; NE exception **Mizoram** |
| Low | Middle and lower Ganga plains, Telangana, western Rajasthan, NE hills |
#### 6.1 Andhra Pradesh districts
| District | Urbanisation |
|---|---|
| **Visakhapatnam** (highest) | **47.45%** |
| Krishna (second) | 40.81% |
| **Srikakulam** (lowest) | **16.16%** |
### 7. RURAL-URBAN CONTINUUM
- **PURA** (Providing Urban Amenities to Rural Areas): concept of **A.P.J. Abdul Kalam**; announced by PM Vajpayee, **2003**; pilot in 7 clusters (2004-05); restructured 2012.
- **Shyama Prasad Mukherji Rurban Mission**: successor to PURA; launched **21 Feb 2016 at Kurubhat, Rajnandgaon (Chhattisgarh)**; **300 Rurban clusters**.
