## U0258 | ROLE OF THE PRESS AND NEWSPAPERS IN THE ANDHRA MOVEMENT
### 1. TELUGU PRESS – FIRSTS AND "ONLY"
| First / only | Paper |
|---|---|
| **First Telugu newspaper / magazine** | **Satyadoota** (Satya Duta), **1835, Bellary** – Christian missionaries (Bellary Christian Society) |
| First Telugu newspaper **printed in Andhra** | **Hitavadi** (1841), **Edmund Park** |
| First Telugu paper started by a **Muslim** | **Vartamana Tarangini** – **Syed Rahamatullah** |
| First **political** journal / weekly in Telugu | **Andhra Prakasika** (1885) |
| **Only Telugu daily** in British Andhra | **Desabhimani** |
| First fully **socialist** Telugu journal | **Navasakthi** |
| First **communist** Telugu journal | **Prajasakthi** |
| First Telugu journal after **independence** | **Jaya Bharat** |
| Introduced the **editorial column** | **Andhra Bhasa Sanjivani** – **Kokkonda Venkataratnam** |
!TRAP Satyadoota = first Telugu paper (Bellary); Hitavadi = first **printed in Andhra**.

### 7. PRESS LAWS AND PROSECUTIONS
- **Vernacular Press Act, 1878** ("**Gagging Act**") – repealed by **Lord Ripon, 1882**.
- **Swarajya**: Gadicherla (with Bodi Narayana Rao) convicted of **sedition (Section 124A IPC)** over an article of **26 March 1908**; sentenced to **3 years** – first Telugu editor in Madras Presidency imprisoned in the Vandemataram movement (1908-1911); revolutionary editorials such as "**Veerabali**".
### 8. PRESS AND THE SEPARATE-ANDHRA DEMAND
- Telugu papers demanded a separate state from **1911**.
| Paper | Statement |
|---|---|
| **The Hindu** | **Jonnavittula Gurunatham** – **6 articles, 1911**; "**Are Telugu people backward?**" (**15 Apr 1911**): civil services **56 non-Andhras : 8 Andhras**; judiciary **19 : 0** |
| **Krishna Patrika** | **30 Nov 1912**: "strengthening (building) the nation begins with the **Andhra movement**"; **19 Jul 1913** cartoon: **Rayalaseema as an iceberg** blocking the "Andhra Movement" ship; 11 Apr 1914 editorial |
| **Desabhimani** | Urged Telugu papers to write on Andhra affairs instead of world politics; Telugu culture needs its own government |
| **Janmabhumi** | **25 Aug 1920**: Andhra movement strengthens **national unity** |
- Supporters of a separate Andhra: **Andhra Patrika, Krishna Patrika, Desabhimani, Desamata, Darbar**.
- Andhra Patrika popularised the word "**Andhra**".

### 9. SIGNIFICANCE
- Press spread the national movement and built support for the separate-Andhra demand.
- Newspapers gave rise to **reading rooms and libraries** – influence beyond reporting politics.
