## U0491 | WATER RESOURCES AND RIVER-LINKING; WATER USE EFFICIENCY AND COMMAND AREA DEVELOPMENT
### 1. WATER STRESS AND STATE FIRSTS
| Per capita water per year | Status |
|---|---|
| Below **1,700 m³** | **Water-stressed** |
| Below **1,000 m³** | **Water-scarce** |
- India is already **below 1,700 m³** (water-stressed).
- **Meghalaya**: first State to approve a **draft State Water Policy** (APPSC 2019).
- **Tamil Nadu**: first to make **rooftop rainwater harvesting compulsory** for all buildings.
- AP: **APWALTA 2002** (rainwater harvesting on plots 200 sq m+).
- Water = **Entry 17, State List**; inter-State rivers = **Entry 56, Union List**.
### 2. WATER-USE EFFICIENCY
| Method | Efficiency |
|---|---|
| **Drip** | **~90%** (highest) |
| Sprinkler | ~70-80% |
| Flood/basin/canal surface | **30-50%** (lowest) |
- **Micro-irrigation**: water straight to the **root zone**; cuts evaporation and runoff.
- **DSR** (Direct Seeded Rice), like **SRI**: saves up to **50%** water.
### 3. POLICIES, BODIES AND MISSIONS
| Body / policy | Year | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **NWRC** | – | Chaired by **Prime Minister**; CMs members; adopts NWP |
| National Water Policy | **1987 → 2002 → 2012** | NWP-2012 in force |
| **Ministry of Jal Shakti** | **May 2019** | MoWR, RD&GR + Drinking Water and Sanitation merged |

- **NWP-2012**: national water framework law, Water Regulatory Authority in each State, **recycling and reuse**, **micro-irrigation incentives**, **community participation**; **no single Union authority replacing State institutions**.
- **Annual Ground Water Quality Report 2024**: released **31 Dec 2024** by **C.R. Patil**; by **CGWB**; first with a standard operating procedure for sampling.
### 4. COMMAND AREA DEVELOPMENT
- **CAD Programme 1974-75**: narrow the gap between irrigation potential **created and used**; **AP CADA** August 1974.
- Components: **field channels, drains, land levelling, Water Users' Associations** (not coal-mine reclamation).
- **CADWM** under **PMKSY-Har Khet Ko Pani**; **no new approvals after 2021-22**; 27 LTIF projects complete, 22.21 lakh ha CCA developed.
- **M-CADWM** from 2025-26: Rs 1,600 crore; underground piped irrigation, SCADA/IoT.
### 5. NATIONAL RIVER LINKING PROJECT
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| **National Perspective Plan** | **1980** |
| **NWDA** | Set up **1982** (not 1992); carries out NPP |
| Links | **16 Peninsular + 14 Himalayan = 30** |
| Progress | PFRs all 30; FRs 26; DPRs 14 |
- **Arthur Cotton**: first proposed (19th century); **K.L. Rao**: **Ganga-Cauvery** link; **Suresh Prabhu**: 2002 Task Force.
#### 5.1 Priority Links
- **Ken-Betwa**: **first NPP link implemented**; MP-UP; MoA March 2021; Rs 44,605 crore; **Daudhan Dam inside Panna Tiger Reserve**.
| Godavari-Cauvery link | From | To |
|---|---|---|
| Godavari-Krishna | **Inchampalli** | Nagarjunasagar |
| Krishna-Pennar | Nagarjunasagar | **Somasila** |
| Pennar-Cauvery | Somasila | **Grand Anicut** |
- **Pattiseema lift** (2014-19): **Polavaram → Prakasam barrage**; Godavari water to Krishna delta; saves Srisailam water for Rayalaseema.
- Polavaram diverts 80 TMC to Krishna basin → feeds **HNSS and GNSS**.
- Criticism: "surplus" basins unreliable under **climate change**; displacement; ecological loss.
