## U0143 | BHAKTI MOVEMENT – ORIGINS, ALVARS AND NAYANARS, NIRGUNA AND SAGUNA STREAMS, SOCIAL BASE
- **Exams:** Group 1 Prelims / Group 2 Screening | **HIGH**
- **Rows:** A-120 Bhakti Movement – origins, Alvars and Nayanars, Nirguna and Saguna streams, social base; S1-53 Bhakti Movement – origin, streams, principal saints
- **PYQ importance:** APPSC 7 questions | other exams 60
### 1. MEANING, ROOTS AND ORIGIN
- **Bhakti** from the root **"bhaj"** – to share or participate.
- Roots: **Upanishads, Bhagavad Gita, Bhagavata Purana** (not the Arthashastra).
- **Gita Chapter 12 = Bhakti Yoga**: both Saguna and Nirguna valid; Saguna upasana easier for ordinary people.
- **Bhagavata Purana**: first to put bhakti at the centre of religion; **ninefold (navadha) bhakti** – shravana, kirtana, smarana, padasevana, archana, vandana, dasya, sakhya, atmanivedana. Most scholars date it c. 9th-10th c.
- Began in the **Tamil South** with the **Alvars and Nayanars (6th-9th c.)**; reached the north by the **15th c.**; overall span **6th-18th c.**
- Revival in the **15th-16th c.** (Kabir, Tulsi, Nanak, Sur, Mirabai); a line traces its revival to **Adi Shankaracharya's Advaitavada (8th c.)** against Buddhism's influence.
- Movement **started by the Alvar saints** (with the Shaiva Nayanars).
#### 1.1 Why It Arose
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Reaction | Against ritualism, priestly dominance and caste |
| Hard paths | **Gyana marga and karma marga** too hard for ordinary people |
| Debate | Older historians: reaction to **Islam**; modern scholars: **indigenous** Alvar-Nayanar roots – Islam shaped only the later egalitarianism |
- Core beliefs: **monotheism**; **guru essential**; salvation through devotion.
### 2. ALVARS AND NAYANARS
| Feature | Alvars | Nayanars |
|---|---|---|
| Devotees of | **Vishnu** | **Shiva** |
| Number | **12** | **63** |
| Canon | **Nalayira Divya Prabandham** (4,000 hymns), compiled by **Nathamuni** – **"Tamil Veda"** | **Tirumurai** (12 books), compiled by **Nambi Andar Nambi** – **"Fifth Veda"** (JPSC 2021) |
| Women | **Andal** – only female Alvar | **Karaikkal Ammaiyar** – one of 3 women among the 63; only female Nayanar whose poems survive; c. 6th c. |
| Notable | **Poigai, Bhoothath, Pey** = first three Alvars; **Nammalvar** – foremost Alvar, **Tiruvaymoli** (1,102 verses; 1,352 of the 4,000 hymns) | **Appar, Sambandar, Sundarar** (Tevaram) + Manikkavachakar = the Samayacharyas; Sambandar bested Jain monks in debate |
- Both came from **all castes, high and low**, and questioned Brahmin dominance.
#### 2.1 Sacred Texts → Tradition
| Text | Tradition | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Nalayira Divya Prabandham** | Alvars (Sri Vaishnavism) | 4,000 hymns; Nathamuni |
| **Tevaram** | Nayanars | Hymns of Appar, Sambandar, Sundarar; first seven books of the Tirumurai |
| **Tiruvasagam** | Nayanars | **Manikkavachakar** |
| **Tirumurai** | Shaiva canon | 12 books; Nambi Andar Nambi |
| **Periya Puranam** | Shaiva hagiography | **Sekkizhar** under **Kulottunga II**; lives of the 63 Nayanars; **12th Tirumurai** |
!TRAP "Tamil Veda" = Nalayira Divya Prabandham; "Fifth Veda" = Tirumurai. Alvars = 12 (Vishnu); Nayanars = 63 (Shiva).
### 3. PHILOSOPHICAL SCHOOLS → FOUNDER
| School | Founder | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Advaita** (Kevala) | **Shankaracharya** | 8th c. |
| **Vishishtadvaita** (qualified non-dualism) | **Ramanuja** | 11th c.; Vaishnavism |
| **Dvaita** (dualism) | **Madhvacharya** | Critiqued Advaita and Vishishtadvaita |
| **Dvaitadvaita** (Bhedabheda) | **Nimbarka** | Kishangarh rulers backed the Nimbarka sect |
| **Shuddhadvaita / Pushtimarg** | **Vallabhacharya** (c. 1479-1531) | **Telugu Brahmin**; visited **Krishnadevaraya's** court; guru of **Surdas** |
| Achintya Bhedabheda | **Chaitanya** | Gaudiya Vaishnavism |
### 4. NIRGUNA AND SAGUNA STREAMS
| Stream | Idea of God | Saints |
|---|---|---|
| **Nirguna** | Formless, attributeless; rejects idols (e.g. the Sikh **Nirankar**) | **Kabir** (APPSC GS 2024), **Guru Nanak**, **Dadu Dayal**, **Ravidas (Raidas)** |
| **Saguna – Ramamargi** | Rama | **Tulsidas** |
| **Saguna – Krishnamargi** | Krishna | **Surdas, Mirabai, Chaitanya, Vallabha** |
- Both streams drew on the Upanishadic **Advaita**; movement basically **monotheistic**.
- Nirguna saints rejected idol worship, ritual, pilgrimage, fasting, caste; **Saguna saints kept idol worship**.
- **Dadu Panth** – founded by **Dadu Dayal in Rajasthan**, in Kabir's tradition (not by Kabir).
- **Raskhan** (Sayyid Ibrahim, also Sujan Raskhan) – 16th-c. Muslim poet devoted to **Krishna** (not Rama); **Prem Vatika (c. 1614)** in Braj.
!KEY APPSC GS 2024: Nirguna saint = **Kabir**.
### 5. NATHPANTHIS, SIDDHAS AND YOGIS
- Spread devotional religion in **Northern India** (BPSC 2023).
- Leading figures: **Gorakhnath, Matsyendranath**.
- Practices: **hatha yoga**, meditation, **renunciation**; criticised **ritualism, caste and orthodox religion**.
- Used the vernacular; influenced **Kabir's nirguna** idiom.
### 6. KABIR, GURU NANAK AND THE SIKH GURUS
#### 6.1 Kabir
- Disciple of **Ramananda**; a **weaver**; raised by Muslim weaver couple **Niru and Nima**; called the greatest saint of the cult.
- **One God, many names** (Ram, Rahim, Allah, Hari); condemned ritual on both sides.
- **Bijak** = sacred book of his followers.
#### 6.2 Guru Nanak and Successors
- Nirguna; rejected caste discrimination; stressed virtue over caste; **Guru ka Langar** (common dining) began at **Kartarpur**.
- Successor **Guru Angad** (Lehna): standardised the **Gurmukhi** script; systematised the langar.
- **Adi Granth (Guru Granth Sahib)** compiled by **Guru Arjan Dev**, completed and installed in the Harmandir Sahib, **Amritsar, 1604**; scribe **Bhai Gurdas**; includes **Kabir** and other Bhagats.
- **Guru Gobind Singh (1706)** added Guru Tegh Bahadur's hymns and declared it the Guru.

### 8. MAHARASHTRA DHARMA – VARKARI SECT
- Centred on **Vithoba (Vitthal)** at **Pandharpur**; annual pilgrimage on **Ekadashi**.
- **Jnaneshwar** – credited with founding the sect; **Jnaneshwari** (Marathi commentary on the Gita), Amritanubhava.
- **Namdev** – **most influential** saint of the Maharashtra tradition; his bhakti showed Islamic influence.
- **Eknath** – Eknathi Bhagavata; **Tukaram** – 17th c., thousands of **abhangas**; also **Narhari**.
### 9. VIRASHAIVA / LINGAYAT MOVEMENT
- **Basavanna (Basaveshwara)**: head of the treasury at **Kalyana** under Kalachuri king **Bijjala**; **Anubhava Mantapa** – assembly of sharanas of every caste, men and women, presided by **Allama Prabhu**.
- **Vachanas**: short Kannada devotional sayings signed **Kudalasangamadeva**.
- **Akkamahadevi** – 12th-c. woman saint of Karnataka.
- **AP angle**: Veerashaivism reached Andhra through **Mallikarjuna Panditaradhya** and **Palkuriki Somanatha**.
### 10. SOCIAL BASE – GURUS, DISCIPLES, CASTE AND WOMEN
#### 10.1 Guru → Disciple
| Guru | Disciple(s) |
|---|---|
| Ramanuja | Ramananda (in his tradition) |
| **Ramananda** | **Kabir** (weaver), **Raidas/Ravidas** (shoemaker), **Dhanna** (Jat cultivator), **Sena** (barber) |
| **Ravidas** | **Mirabai** |
| **Vallabhacharya** | **Surdas** |
| **Charandas** (Charandasi Sampradaya) | **Daya Bai** (RPSC 2018) – Dayabodh, Vinay Malika |
- **Ramananda** – forerunner who brought bhakti to the north; **first medieval reformer to openly denounce caste**; admitted disciples of all castes.
- **Chronological order**: Ramananda → Kabir → Nanak → Chaitanya → Tulsidas.
#### 10.2 Women and Anti-Caste Voices
- Women saints: **Andal, Karaikkal Ammaiyar, Akkamahadevi, Mirabai, Daya Bai**; **Sahajobai** – Sahaj-Prakash (guru bhakti, Nirgun).
- Ganesh Dev Leela – wrongly matched with Gangabai.
- **Palnati Brahmanayudu** (AP): set up **Chapakudu** (common dining for all castes); Veeravaishnavism in Palnadu; treated Mala devotee **Kannama Dasu** as his son.
- **Vemana** and **Potuluri Veerabrahmam** condemned caste.
- Earlier challenges: **Buddha** (birth does not decide spiritual worth); **Mahavira** (Kshatriya of Kundagrama; first disciple Jamali).
- **Impact**: brought Hindus and Muslims closer; weakened caste chains but **did not end caste** or achieve lasting Hindu-Muslim unity.
- **15 July 2026**: Ravidassia community renewed its demand for a separate "Ravidassia religion" category in the Census.
