# TOPIC 58: Sep–4 Nov 2026: International - treaties, agreements and developments in international organisations
# PAGE: New Current Affairs 15 Oct - 4 Nov 2026

[1] 16 Oct 2026 - IIT Hyderabad and Japan's Mie University MoUs for semiconductor research: student and faculty exchanges, joint research, one or two semesters at the partner university; focus on semiconductors and digital technologies; Mie region a semiconductor hub. IIT Hyderabad est. 2008 at Kandi, Sangareddy, Telangana; Mie University est. 1949, Tsu City, Mie Prefecture. India Semiconductor Mission launched 2021 under MeitY; Semicon India Programme ₹76,000 crore; silicon = most used semiconductor. Japan: capital Tokyo, currency Yen.

[2] 17 Oct 2026 - India-Japan Joint Crediting Mechanism (JCM) Implementation Guidelines launched in New Delhi, marking a shift to project implementation. JCM = Japan-initiated bilateral, project-based mechanism for low-carbon technologies, with credits for verified emission reductions; launched by Japan in 2013, first partner Mongolia; key principle MRV (Measurement, Reporting and Verification); enabled under Article 6 of the Paris Agreement. Paris Agreement: adopted 2015 at COP21, in force 2016, under UNFCCC; goal well below 2 °C, pursue 1.5 °C.

[3] 17 Oct 2026 - Bharat Forge and Pratt & Whitney Canada long-term aerospace engine component supply agreement (October 2026); components made at BFL's aerospace facility in India; aims at diversified, resilient global aerospace supply chains. Bharat Forge est. 1961, HQ Pune; Pratt & Whitney Canada = subsidiary of US-based RTX Corporation, formed 2020 by merger of Raytheon and United Technologies' aerospace businesses.

[4] 20 Oct 2026 - India approves joining the Southern Indian Ocean Fisheries Agreement (SIOFA): Union Government approved accession; agreement signed 7 July 2006 in Rome, in force June 2012; aims at conservation and sustainable use of fisheries in the Southern Indian Ocean; covers fish, molluscs, crustaceans but excludes highly migratory species in Annex I of UNCLOS; bodies: Meeting of the Parties (at least once a year), Scientific Committee, Secretariat; opens opportunities for Indian fishing vessels.

[5] 21 Oct 2026 - India-New Zealand FTA comes into force: signed 27 April 2026, in force 20 October 2026 (Piyush Goyal); 100% elimination of duties on Indian goods to NZ, benefiting textiles, leather, footwear, garments; FDI of US$20 billion (about ₹1.90 lakh crore) expected over 15 years; bilateral trade target from US$2.4 billion to NZ$7 billion by 2030; Goyal flagged off JCB India mini excavator exports; DGFT set Tariff Rate Quotas for apples, kiwi fruit and Manuka honey. NZ: capital Wellington, currency NZ Dollar.

[6] 21 Oct 2026 - Pakistan to chair the World Bank and IMF Boards of Governors in 2027: Board of Governors = highest decision-making body; each member appoints a Governor and an Alternate; no executive authority over daily operations. IMF and World Bank born at the Bretton Woods Conference, 1944, both HQ Washington DC; IMF MD Kristalina Georgieva; World Bank President Ajay Banga. Pakistan capital Islamabad.

[7] 22 Oct 2026 - Three Indian sites added to the UNESCO World Heritage Tentative List (India's total 81): Baratang Karst-Mud Volcano Complex, Andaman and Nicobar Islands (limestone caves, karst, active mud volcanoes); Andaman Subduction Volcanoes, Andaman Sea (Barren and Narcondam Islands); Sirpur Cultural and Archaeological Landscape, Chhattisgarh (Hindu and Buddhist temples, monasteries). Tentative listing = preliminary step before nomination; India has 45 World Heritage Sites; UNESCO est. 1945, HQ Paris; World Heritage Convention adopted 1972, ratified by India 1977; India's first sites (1983): Ajanta, Ellora, Agra Fort, Taj Mahal; Barren Island = India's only historically active volcano; ASI est. 1861, HQ New Delhi.

[8] 22 Oct 2026 (added 4 September 2026) - Four cultural sites added to the Tentative List (total then 73): Rangpur-Sivasagar Historic Ensemble, Assam (Ahom Kingdom; Rang Ghar, Talatal Ghar, large water tanks); Colonial Penal Settlements of the Andaman Islands (incl. Cellular Jail); Nicobarese Cultural Continuity in the Central Nicobar Islands; Shekhawati Painted Haveli Towns, Rajasthan (merchants' havelis with murals). ICOMOS advises UNESCO on cultural sites, IUCN on natural sites.

[9] 22 Oct 2026 - Five heritage sites/proposals added (total then 78): Bagh Cave Paintings (Buddhist murals); Group of Temples of the Chambal Valley; Udayagiri Caves (Gupta period); Ginnorgarh (tribal heritage); "Sites of Satyagraha" - a serial transnational nomination linking India and South Africa; Union Culture and Tourism Minister Gajendra Singh Shekhawat.

[10] 23 Oct 2026 - India approves accession to SIOFA: long-term conservation and sustainable use of fishery resources in the Southern Indian Ocean; signed Rome, 7 July 2006, in force June 2012; lets Indian vessels fish there under international rules; excludes highly migratory species (Annex I, UNCLOS); bodies: Meeting of the Parties (governing, at least annually), Scientific Committee, Secretariat.

[11] 24 Oct 2026 - 'Badr' defence cooperation MoU signed 21 October 2026 by Türkiye, Qatar, Indonesia and Pakistan; 'Badr' = "Full Moon"; covers joint military production, military technology, training, R&D; capitals Ankara, Doha, Jakarta, Islamabad; Türkiye joined NATO in 1952.

[12] 29 Oct 2026 - India re-elected Vice-Chair of the UNESCO International Convention Against Doping in Sport (Vice-Chair of the COP Bureau): Convention adopted 19 Oct 2005 at UNESCO's 33rd General Conference, in force 1 Feb 2007; Union Minister for Youth Affairs and Sports chaired the 3rd Formal Meeting of the COP10 Bureau and 4th Formal Meeting of the Fund Approval Committee (2026-27) in New Delhi; only legally binding international treaty dedicated to preventing doping in sport; 192 States Parties - UNESCO's second most widely ratified convention; India ratified 7 Nov 2007; COP = supreme governing body, meets every two years. UNESCO est. 16 Nov 1945, HQ Paris; WADA est. 1999, HQ Montreal; NADA India est. 2005, HQ New Delhi; National Anti-Doping Act, 2022 = India's legal framework.

[13] 31 Oct 2026 - MYAS, Ministry of Education and UNESCO MoU on clean sport values in schools: signed 28 Oct 2026 at the Clean Sport Conclave organised by NADA India, New Delhi; aligned with Khelo Bharat Niti 2025; anti-doping awareness and fair play via classroom education; in presence of Sports Minister Dr. Mansukh Mandaviya, Union Minister Pralhad Joshi and Dr. Tim Curtis (Director, UNESCO Regional Office for South Asia).

[14] 31 Oct 2026 - India and Mexico launch negotiations for a Preferential Trade Agreement (PTA): Terms of Reference signed during Commerce Secretary Rajesh Agrawal's visit to Mexico City; areas: market access, Rules of Origin, customs, trade facilitation, dispute settlement, SPS, TBT, trade remedies; priority sectors: automobiles, chemicals, electronics, pharma, agri-food, energy, critical minerals, medical devices, advanced manufacturing; diplomatic relations since 1950; Mexico: capital Mexico City, currency Peso.

[15] 2 Nov 2026 - ADB approves $139.55 million loan for Puducherry: for the Puducherry Integrated Urban Livability Improvement Project (PIULIP); ADB's first project in Puducherry UT; climate-resilient water supply, sewerage, sanitation, public spaces and municipal governance. ADB est. 19 Dec 1966, HQ Mandaluyong, Metro Manila, President Masato Kanda; India a founding member. Puducherry UT = Puducherry, Karaikal, Mahe and Yanam.

[16] 3 Nov 2026 - UN Transitional Assistance Mission in Somalia (UNTMIS) concludes: ended operations on 31 Oct 2026, closing over three decades of UN political missions in Somalia; a Special Political Mission under UN DPPA; responsibilities transferred to the Federal Government of Somalia and the UN Country Team; UNTMIS created 2024 by UNSC Resolution 2753, replacing UNSOM (2013, Resolution 2102); UN Secretary-General António Guterres; Somalia: capital Mogadishu, currency Somali Shilling, Horn of Africa.
