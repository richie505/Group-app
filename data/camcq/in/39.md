# TOPIC 39: Sep–4 Nov 2026: National - new central schemes, missions and their features
# PAGE: New Current Affairs 15 Oct - 4 Nov 2026

[1] 15 Oct 2026 - PM Gati Shakti National Master Plan completes five years: launched by PM Modi on 13 October 2021; GIS-based digital planning platform with satellite imagery; connects 58 Central Ministries and all 36 States/UTs; aims to cut logistics costs and avoid duplication; Cabinet approved Integrated Transport and Logistics Authority (ITLA) to appraise central infrastructure projects of ₹500 crore or more; nodal DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce and Industry; National Logistics Policy launched 17 September 2022; seven sectors - Railways, Roads, Ports, Waterways, Airports, Mass Transport, Logistics Infrastructure.

[2] 16 Oct 2026 - Lakshadweep first UT with 100% literacy under ULLAS: declared 11 Oct 2026 by Administrator Praful Patel; all 1,113 identified illiterate adults passed FLNAT; doorstep teaching and assessment (details in 6.1).

[3] 17 Oct 2026 - Rural works raised to 375 under VB-G RAM G Act 2025 (Viksit Bharat - Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act): Ministry of Rural Development notified works raised from 266 to 375 under four pillars (41% increase); planned bottom-up via Gram Sabha-approved Viksit Gram Panchayat Plans (VGPP); digital monitoring; aligned with Viksit Bharat @2047; Article 243(b) defines Gram Sabha; 73rd Amendment 1992 - Part IX (Arts 243-243O), 11th Schedule with 29 subjects; Article 243G devolution; National Panchayati Raj Day 24 April.

[4] 21 Oct 2026 - Cabinet approves ₹53,428 crore Pradhan Mantri Samajik Sahayata Yojana (PMSSY): NSAP renamed PMSSY and continued 2026-27 to 2030-31; NSAP launched 15 August 1995, fully centrally funded, non-contributory, Department of Rural Development; supports BPL elderly, widows, disabled and families losing the breadwinner; new names - PM Vriddhavastha Pension Yojana (IGNOAPS), PM Vidhwa Pension Yojana (IGNWPS), PM Divyangjan Pension Yojana (IGNDPS), PM Parivar Labh Yojana (NFBS); Article 41.

[5] 22 Oct 2026 - Shilp Samagam Mela-2026, Guwahati, Assam (21-28 Oct 2026): organised by Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment; market access for SC, OBC, Safai Mitra and Denotified Tribe (DNT) artisans; follows the Mysuru edition (ended 19 Oct 2026); launched 2022, over 1,000 artisans in first 14 editions; DNTs were "Criminal Tribes" under British rule; Assam capital Dispur.

[6] 22 Oct 2026 - IIT Hyderabad launches India's first "Goodness" academic credit course: "Goodness: Character, Responsibility and Human Flourishing" (LA2270); one-credit elective for all UG (B.Tech, B.Des) students, no prerequisites; character, responsibility, ethics, well-being; IITH est. 2008 at Kandi, Sangareddy, Telangana; IITs are Institutes of National Importance under the Institutes of Technology Act, 1961; NEP 2020 stresses ethical values.

[7] 23 Oct 2026 - UDAN completes 10 years: launched 21 October 2016 (Ude Desh ka Aam Nagrik); 1.71 crore+ passengers; 687 routes, 95 airports, heliports and water aerodromes; operational airports 74 (2014) to 166 (2026); India third-largest domestic aviation market; Modified UDAN approved by Cabinet 25 March 2026 with ₹28,840 crore for 10 years, launched by PM at Jodhpur Airport on 4 July 2026; Ministry of Civil Aviation through AAI (est. 1995); first UDAN flight Shimla-Delhi, 27 April 2017; uses Viability Gap Funding.

[8] 25 Oct 2026 - Guidelines for ₹84,084 crore Samudra Manthan Scheme: Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas; deepwater (over 400 m) and ultra-deepwater (over 1,500 m) oil and gas exploration; till FY 2030-31 (FY 2026-27 preparatory; first phase 2027-28 to 2030-31); support for 56 exploratory and 4 stratigraphic wells; govt funds up to 50% of drilling cost, max ₹675 crore per well; only PSUs and PSU-led consortia eligible; total for 60 wells up to ₹43,200 crore; 40% released after 40% of depth drilled, 60% after completion; main offshore regions Mumbai Offshore and Krishna-Godavari Basin.

[9] 26 Oct 2026 - Ladakh declared India's first 100% organic UT by Lt Governor V. K. Saxena on 25 October 2026: sale and use of chemical fertilisers banned from July 2026; organic fertilisers distributed before sowing; crops apricot, Himalayan berries, buckwheat (kuttu), barley; Sikkim first fully organic State (2016); Ladakh UT since 31 October 2019 (J&K Reorganisation Act, 2019); districts Leh and Kargil; PKVY launched 2015. (Reported again 28 Oct 2026.)

[10] 27 Oct 2026 - Kerala plans India's first cooperative airline "Co-Keralam": approved 26 Oct 2026, announced by CM V. D. Satheesan; through Kerala Civil Aviation Development Cooperative Society; led by C. N. Vijayakrishnan (Chairman, MVR Cancer Centre and Research Institute); to start from Kochi with two leased 72-seat aircraft; fares ₹3,500-7,000; Ministry of Cooperation est. 6 July 2021; DGCA is the civil aviation safety regulator.

[11] 27 Oct 2026 - IIT Ropar introduces India's first thesis-free PhD: PhD for products, patents, technology transfer or startups; a product earning ₹10 lakh revenue may qualify (Associate Dean Sarang); promotes applied research, curbs data manipulation and plagiarism; China has similar models; IIT Ropar est. 2008, Rupnagar, Punjab; patents generally valid 20 years.

[12] 28 Oct 2026 - Component II of RELIEF Scheme extended to 31 March 2027: Resilience & Logistics Intervention for Export Facilitation, launched 19 March 2026 under Export Promotion Mission; support and risk cover for exporters hit by disruptions on Red Sea, Persian Gulf and West Asian routes; formulated by Department of Commerce, implemented by ECGC Limited (est. 1957, HQ Mumbai) via a digital portal.

[13] 28 Oct 2026 - IIT Mandi "Science on Wheels" in Himachal Pradesh (launched 17 October 2026): experiential science for rural school students; partners Plaksha University and IDYM Foundation (Igniting Dreams of Young Minds), supported by DST (est. 1971); inaugurated by Director Prof. Laxmidhar Behera at IIT Mandi Campus School; to cover 50 schools in Mandi, Kullu, Bilaspur, Hamirpur, Kangra; earlier reached 7,500+ students in 70 schools in Chhattisgarh and Punjab; IIT Mandi est. 2009 at Kamand.

[14] 29 Oct 2026 - Cabinet approves Khelo India Sports Goods Manufacturing Scheme: outlay ₹1,856.67 crore, 2026 to FY 2033-34; announced in Union Budget 2026-27; technology adoption, R&D, skilling, quality certification, market expansion; Sports Goods Manufacturing Zones (SGMZs) using BHAVYA Scheme infrastructure and NICDC industrial parks; aligned with Khelo Bharat Niti 2025 (national sports policy), Make in India, Atmanirbhar Bharat; major hubs Meerut (UP) and Jalandhar (Punjab).

[15] 29 Oct 2026 - Cabinet approves Pradhan Mantri Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana (PM VGSY): CSS, 2026-27 to 2030-31 (16th Finance Commission period); outlay ₹7,395.55 crore (Centre ₹4,869.48 crore, States ₹2,526.07 crore); about 32 lakh elected representatives and functionaries across 2.55 lakh+ Panchayats; about 2.55 lakh GPDPs a year via eGramSwaraj; each awareness campaign to reach at least 1 crore people; at least 2.55 lakh online audit plans a year via AuditOnline for own-source revenue (OSR) mobilisation; no permanent jobs (contractual staff allowed); nodal Ministry of Panchayati Raj; 73rd Amendment 1992 in force 24 April 1993; Part IX (Articles 243-243O).

[16] 29 Oct 2026 - Cabinet approves Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme (CMPS): ₹10,000 crore up to FY 2039-40; announced in Union Budget 2026-27; globally competitive container manufacturing, fewer imports; Greenfield and Brownfield projects; Interest Subvention Assistance ₹859 crore (3% a year); Operational Expenditure Assistance ₹8,891 crore; Testing and Certification Assistance ₹150 crore incl. Dynamic Longitudinal Impact Testing; Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways.

[17] 30 Oct 2026 - "Punah Bharan" app for groundwater recharge planning: launched by the Union Jal Shakti Minister; developed by BISAG-N with the National Water Mission; finds recharge sites within a 3-km radius and recommends structures; web portal gives village-level Artificial Recharge Structure (ARS) zonation maps and GIS tools; NWM is one of eight NAPCC missions (2008); BISAG-N under MeitY, HQ Gandhinagar.

[18] 31 Oct 2026 - 24th PM-KISAN instalment to 9.34 crore farmers: over ₹18,690 crore; total since inception over ₹4.64 lakh crore; ₹6,000 a year in three instalments of ₹2,000; Aadhaar-linked accounts, DBT, e-KYC; proposed to continue till FY 2030-31 with ₹3.15 lakh crore; launched 24 February 2019, effective from 1 December 2018; Central Sector Scheme, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare.

[19] 1 Nov 2026 - ₹325 crore Bharat Bovine Breeding and Research Centre, Gujarat: inaugurated by PM Modi at Bhildi, Banaskantha; set up by Banas Dairy on 127 acres with NDDB technical guidance; under Rashtriya Gokul Mission; IVF and embryo transfer, about 25,000 embryos a year; breeds Kankrej, Gir, Sahiwal, Tharparkar; NDDB est. 1965, HQ Anand; RGM launched December 2014; Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying (Minister Rajiv Ranjan Singh); India = world's largest milk producer.

[20] 4 Nov 2026 - NITI Aayog and TEXMiN (IIT-ISM Dhanbad) sign a Statement of Intent on critical minerals: TEXMiN = Technology Translation Research Park supported by DST; indigenous technology and stable critical-mineral supply chains; aligned with National Critical Mineral Mission, Atmanirbhar Bharat, Viksit Bharat; NITI Aayog est. 1 January 2015, chaired by PM; IIT (ISM) Dhanbad est. 1926 as Indian School of Mines, IIT status 2016; NCMM approved January 2025 with ₹16,300 crore expenditure and ₹18,000 crore expected investment; Mines Minister G. Kishan Reddy.

[21] 4 Nov 2026 - MoPR and CESL MoU for EV infrastructure in Panchayats: EV charging and battery-swapping on Panchayat-owned land; exchanged by MoPR Joint Secretary Mukta Shekhar and CESL Director Akhilesh Kumar Dixit at the National Workshop on People's Plan Campaign 2026-27, New Delhi, before Minister Rajiv Ranjan Singh (Lalan Singh); CESL est. 2020, wholly owned subsidiary of EESL (PSU JV under Ministry of Power); PPC = "Sabki Yojana Sabka Vikas" for GPDPs.
