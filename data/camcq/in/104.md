# TOPIC 104: Sep–4 Nov 2026: Books and authors; important days and their themes
# PAGE: New Current Affairs – 15 October to 4 November 2026

[1] [15 Oct 2026] International Day of Rural Women – recognises rural women's contribution to agriculture, food security and rural development; proclaimed by UNGA in 2007 (Resolution 62/136), first observed 15 October 2008; UN Women est. 2010, operations from 2011, HQ New York; SDG 5 gender equality, SDG 2 zero hunger; CEDAW adopted 1979, in force 1981; International Women's Day – 8 March.

[2] [16 Oct 2026] World Food Day – marks founding of FAO on 16 October 1945; 2026 theme "Innovate today. Nourish tomorrow."; first observed 1981 after the 1979 FAO Conference decision; FAO HQ Rome, founded at Quebec City, DG Qu Dongyu (China); Rome-based agencies FAO, IFAD (1977) and WFP (1961, Nobel Peace Prize 2020); SDG 2 Zero Hunger by 2030.

[3] [17 Oct 2026] International Day of the Intangible Cultural Heritage – no official 2026 theme; proclaimed at the 42nd UNESCO General Conference, November 2023; first observed 17 October 2024, so 2026 is the third edition; linked to the UNESCO 2003 Convention for the Safeguarding of ICH (adopted 17 October 2003, in force 20 April 2006, India ratified 2005); UNESCO est. 16 November 1945, HQ Paris; Sangeet Natak Akademi safeguards performing arts in India.

[4] [17 Oct 2026] International Day for the Eradication of Poverty – 2026–2028 theme "United for Peace and Human Rights"; 2026 special focus "Safe Homes for All"; first gathering 17 October 1987 at Trocadero Square, Paris, initiated by Joseph Wresinski (founder of ATD Fourth World); proclaimed by UNGA in 1992 (Resolution 47/196); SDG 1 No Poverty, SDG 11; World Bank revised extreme poverty line to USD 3.00 per day (2021 PPP) in 2025; Article 25 of UDHR 1948 – adequate standard of living; World Day of Social Justice – 20 February.

[5] [18 Oct 2026] Breast Cancer Awareness Month (1–31 October) – 2026 theme "Every story is unique, every journey matters."; first launched October 1985 by the American Cancer Society and the pharmaceutical division of Imperial Chemical Industries; known as Pink October; Pink Ribbon symbol since the early 1990s; breast cancer is the most commonly diagnosed cancer among women (WHO); World Cancer Day – 4 February, led by UICC; WHO est. 7 April 1948, HQ Geneva.

[6] [19 Oct 2026] International Infection Prevention Week (19–25 October) – theme "Generations of Infection Prevention"; tagline "Past. Present. Protected."; organised by APIC (Association for Professionals in Infection Control and Epidemiology, est. 1972, HQ Arlington, Virginia, USA); launched 1986; focus on hand hygiene, healthcare-associated infections, AMR and patient safety; World Hand Hygiene Day – 5 May; World AMR Awareness Week – 18–24 November.

[7] [25 Oct 2026] 14th International Lead Poisoning Prevention Week (18–24 October 2026) – theme "From Detection to Prevention"; aims to end lead paint and protect children; supported by the Global Alliance to Eliminate Lead Paint set up by UNEP and WHO in 2011; first week observed 2012; UNEP est. 1972, HQ Nairobi; WHO est. 7 April 1948, HQ Geneva.

[8] [27 Oct 2026] Vigilance Awareness Week (26 October – 1 November) – theme "Probity for Prosperity"; under the Central Vigilance Commission; held around 31 October, Sardar Patel's birth anniversary (National Unity Day); CVC est. 1964 on the Santhanam Committee recommendation, statutory via the CVC Act, 2003, HQ New Delhi; week observed since 2000.

[9] [27 Oct 2026] Global Media and Information Literacy Week (24–31 October) – led by UNESCO; theme "We All Play a Part – Media and Information Literacy for the People"; launched 2012 by UNESCO, UNESCO MIL Alliance (formerly GAPMIL), UNAOC and MILID University Network; proclaimed by UNGA in 2021.

[10] [28 Oct 2026] UN Disarmament Week (24–30 October) – called for by the UNGA at the first Special Session on Disarmament in 1978 (Resolution A/RES/S-10/2); begins on UN Day, 24 October; Resolution 50/72 B of 12 December 1995 encouraged public awareness; coordinated by UNODA (est. 1998, HQ New York); UN est. 24 October 1945; NPT in force 1970 – India not a signatory.

[11] [31 Oct 2026] World Cities Day – observed 31 October, first in 2014; proclaimed by UNGA Resolution 68/239; organised by UN-Habitat; theme "Regenerating the City: Adequate Housing for All"; global host city Fuzhou, China (31 Oct – 2 Nov 2026); linked to SDG 11; related campaign Urban October; World Cities Report 2026 (UN-Habitat) themed "The Global Housing Crisis: Pathways to Action".

[12] [01 Nov 2026] BRICS Sustainable Lifestyle Week (1–7 November 2026) – promotes public health and sustainable lifestyles; awareness programmes in government hospitals, medical colleges, district and mandal levels; India holds the BRICS Chairmanship in 2026; first BRIC Summit 2009, Yekaterinburg; South Africa joined 2010; Mission LiFE launched by PM Modi on 20 October 2022; Health Minister J.P. Nadda.

[13] [02 Nov 2026] Commemorative postage stamps for Breast Cancer Awareness Month – Department of Posts released a souvenir sheet with two stamps of ₹25 and ₹50 plus picture postcards; unveiled by DG Postal Services Manisha Sinha and Health Secretary Punya Salila Srivastava at Dak Bhawan, New Delhi; pink ribbon is the symbol; Communications Minister Jyotiraditya Scindia; India Post est. 1854.
