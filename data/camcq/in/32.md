# PAGE: New Current Affairs 15 Oct - 4 Nov 2026

[1] 18 Oct 2026 - Paddy area down 15.56 lakh ha in 2026-27 Kharif (Union Agriculture Department): biggest falls Telangana 4.26 lakh ha, Karnataka 4.23, Uttar Pradesh 1.84, Madhya Pradesh 1.75, Andhra Pradesh 1.70; marginal rise in Odisha, Assam, Bihar, Gujarat; other crops net area down 11.81 lakh ha, but pulses up 2.57 lakh ha, oilseeds 1.11 lakh ha, millets 77,000 ha. Kharif is sown June-July with the Southwest Monsoon and harvested September-October.

[2] 18 Oct 2026 - SEBI investor awareness programme "Samajh Se Investing Simple" under Project Jagruk, launched 5 Oct 2026 at Panjab University, Chandigarh, during World Investor Week (5-11 Oct 2026); promotes financial literacy and protection from securities fraud. SEBI: est. 1988, statutory 1992 (SEBI Act 1992), HQ Mumbai, Chairman Tuhin Kanta Pandey.

[3] 19 Oct 2026 - Real GDP growth estimated at 7.3% in Q2 (July-September) 2026-27: Finance Ministry's Monthly Economic Review for September 2026; driven by domestic demand and investment; from a nowcasting model using high-frequency indicators, first presented in the Economic Survey 2026. Real GDP = GDP adjusted for inflation.

[4] 20 Oct 2026 - New GST Standing Committee: 12 States (5 large, 4 medium, 3 small); permanent members: Karnataka, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu; 7 seats rotate every two years; aims at better GST administration and enforcement.

[5] 20 Oct 2026 - Fitch BMI (Business Monitor International, a division of Fitch Solutions) raised India's FY2026-27 growth forecast from 6.8% to 7.5%; FY2027-28 cut from 6.5% to 6.3%; risks - crude oil prices and El Nino; Fitch Group HQ New York.

[6] 20 Oct 2026 (reported again 22 Oct) - India's largest phenol-acetone facility inaugurated at Haldia, West Bengal (Purba Medinipur district): investment ₹6,000 crore; built by Adperma Private Limited, wholly owned subsidiary of Haldia Petrochemicals Limited (HPL); first phenol plant on India's eastern coast; India imported about 50% (3.2-3.7 lakh tonnes) of its phenol; consumption over 6 lakh tonnes a year. Phenol (C6H5OH) - plastics, resins, pharmaceuticals; acetone (C3H6O) - industrial solvent; both made together by the cumene process.

[7] 26 Oct 2026 - PM inaugurated the RBI Greenfield Data Centre and Enterprise Computing and Cyber Security Training Institute at Info Valley-II, Jatni, Khordha district (near Bhubaneswar), Odisha, on 25 Oct 2026: cost ₹169 crore, 18.55 acres; backup and disaster-recovery centre for RBI's main data centre in Navi Mumbai, securing UPI, NEFT, RTGS; training in cybersecurity, AI, quantum computing; foundation laid 22 March 2023 by then Governor Shaktikanta Das; attended by Nirmala Sitharaman, Governor Sanjay Malhotra, Odisha Governor Braj Mohan Ram, CM Mohan Charan Majhi.

[8] 26 Oct 2026 - India targets 30 million tonnes fish production in five years (from 19.7 million tonnes); announced by Union Fisheries Secretary Dr. Abhilaksh Likhi; marine exports target ₹1.25 lakh crore (from ₹70,000 crore). PMMSY launched 2020; NFDB est. 2006, HQ Hyderabad; World Fisheries Day 21 November.

[9] 27 Oct 2026 - National Strategic Trade Command Centre (NSTCC) inaugurated at Vanijya Bhawan, New Delhi by Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal; developed by the Department of Commerce with DGFT as a data analytics and visualisation facility for real-time trade monitoring; initially tracks IEC, Advance Authorisation, EPCG, Certificates of Origin and SCOMET applications. SCOMET = Special Chemicals, Organisms, Materials, Equipment and Technologies.

Setty; aims to strengthen cybersecurity, threat intelligence and resilience in the financial sector.
