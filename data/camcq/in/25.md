# TOPIC 25: Sep–4 Nov 2026: National - polity and governance: bills, acts, ordinances, landmark judgments
# PAGE: New Current Affairs 15 Oct - 4 Nov 2026

[1] [16 Oct 2026] Kerala Elderly Care Bill: Kerala Assembly introduced India's first comprehensive Elderly Care Bill - Kerala first State with a dedicated comprehensive bill for elderly welfare; protects against abuse, neglect, exploitation and illegal occupation of property; mandatory registration and regulation of old-age homes; imprisonment and fines. Central law - Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act 2007 (senior citizen 60+; Section 23 property transfer may be declared void); Article 41 public assistance in old age; International Day of Older Persons 1 October; Kerala capital Thiruvananthapuram, formed 1 November 1956.

[2] [18 Oct 2026] Cancer medicines: Union Government approved a 30% cap on MRP-based trade margins for non-scheduled anti-cancer medicines; Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers estimates prices could fall up to 70%, saving about Rs 2,500 crore a year. NPPA est. 1997 under the Department of Pharmaceuticals, HQ New Delhi, regulates prices under DPCO 2013; scheduled medicines are in Schedule I of DPCO, linked to NLEM.

[3] [19 Oct 2026] CAFE-III norms: Corporate Average Fuel Economy-III announced by the Ministry of Power - 16.7% improvement in car fuel efficiency by 2032; in force 1 April 2027 to 31 March 2032; under Central Motor Vehicles Rules 1989 for M1 category (up to eight passengers plus driver); standard tightened from 3.996 to 3.3273 litres per 100 km; reference mass raised from 1,082 kg to 1,229 kg; incentive multipliers - BEVs and REEVs 3x, PHEVs 2.5x, strong hybrids 1.6x.

[4] [20 Oct 2026] FSSAI infant food sugar ban (draft): Draft Food Safety and Standards (Foods for Infant Nutrition) Amendment Regulations 2026 published in the Gazette on 16 October 2026; proposes prohibiting sucrose and fructose in foods for infants under six months; still a draft - the 2020 Regulations remain in force. FSSAI under the Food Safety and Standards Act 2006, HQ New Delhi, under the Health Ministry.

[5] [21 Oct 2026] ECI Special Summary Revision: SSR of electoral rolls in 17 States and UTs, qualifying date 1 January 2027; these reportedly skipped annual revision in 2026; aims to enrol new voters, correct details and remove dead/duplicate entries. Article 324 - ECI control over rolls; Section 21(2), RP Act 1950; ECI est. 25 January 1950; Article 326 adult suffrage; 61st Amendment 1988 lowered voting age 21 to 18; four qualifying dates 1 January, 1 April, 1 July, 1 October; National Voters' Day 25 January.

[6] [21 Oct 2026] National Conference on E-Governance Reforms-2026: organised by DARPG and the Himachal Pradesh government in Dharamshala, 16-17 October 2026; focus on digital transformation and citizen-centric services. DARPG under the Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions; NeGP approved 2006; Digital India launched 1 July 2015; CPGRAMS online grievance platform; Himachal capital Shimla, statehood 25 January 1971.

[7] [26 Oct 2026] Uttarakhand SDRF: became India's first ISO-certified disaster response force - ISO Quality Management System (QMS) certification. ISO est. 1947, HQ Geneva; ISO 9001 = QMS standard; NDRF est. 2006 under the Disaster Management Act, 2005; Uttarakhand winter capital Dehradun, summer capital Gairsain.

[8] [29 Oct 2026] Telangana El Niño committees: District Collectors directed to form mandal-level coordination committees chaired by Mandal Parishad Development Officers (MPDOs); focus on preventive measures, rural employment, fodder and drinking water; members from Panchayati Raj, Irrigation, Rural Water Supply, Agriculture, Animal Husbandry, Horticulture; meet weekly and report to Collectors. El Niño = warm phase of ENSO with above-normal SST in the central and eastern equatorial Pacific, often linked to a weaker southwest monsoon.

[9] [01 Nov 2026] KG Basin gas dispute arbitrator: SC appointed Justice Ranjana Prakash Desai as sole arbitrator in the dispute between the Centre and Reliance Industries, BP Exploration (Alpha) and Niko; bench of CJI Surya Kant, Justice Joymalya Bagchi and Justice Vipul M. Pancholi; two party-nominated experts to assist. Alleged extraction of gas migrating from adjoining ONGC fields into the KG-D6 block; Petroleum Ministry demand notice of $1.47 billion (about ₹14,000 crore) on 4 Nov 2016 for 338.332 million MMBtu over seven years; Delhi HC Division Bench overturned the Single Judge ruling; on 20 May SC rejected a request to suspend proceedings. Stakes: Reliance 60%, BP 30%, Niko 10%; ONGC est. 1956, Maharatna; Petroleum Minister Hardeep Singh Puri; Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996; Article 142.

[10] [02 Nov 2026] Telangana judicial officers' retirement age - ordinance: ordinance of 31 Oct 2026 raises the age from 61 to 62 for the district judiciary, retrospective from 31 Mar 2026; covers Telangana State Higher Judicial Service and Judicial Service; promulgated by Governor Shiv Pratap Shukla under Article 213(1), amending Section 3(1A) of the Telangana Public Employment (Regulation of Age of Superannuation) Act, 1984; follows SC interim orders of 22 Jul 2026 and the Telangana HC Full Court recommendation of 19 Aug 2026; retired officers may be reappointed till 62. Article 233 District Judges; 235 HC control over subordinate courts; 217 HC judges retire at 62; 124 SC judges at 65.

[11] [03 Nov 2026] Conference of PAC Chairpersons: inaugurated in Thiruvananthapuram by Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla; themes Audit, Accountability and Good Governance. PAC first set up in 1921 after the Montagu-Chelmsford Reforms; 22 members (15 Lok Sabha, 7 Rajya Sabha), one-year term, no Union Ministers; Chairperson appointed by the Speaker, traditionally from the opposition; examines CAG reports (Article 148).

[12] [03 Nov 2026] Green crackers in Delhi-NCR: SC permitted CSIR-NEERI-approved green crackers on 7-8 Nov 2026, 6-7 AM and 8-10 PM; banned within 300 m of hospitals, nursing homes, old-age homes and silence zones; series crackers and barium-based crackers prohibited. CSIR-NEERI est. 1958, HQ Nagpur; Article 21 includes the right to a healthy environment.

[13] [04 Nov 2026] Bihar Silt Management Policy: Bihar = first State with a dedicated Silt Management Policy, announced by CM Samrat Choudhary on 3 Nov 2026; Bihar Water Source Restoration and Silt Management Policy-2026 approved 15 September; aims to restore river carrying capacity, reduce floods and use dredged silt; covers Ganga, Kosi, Bagmati, Kamala-Balan and Gandak; MoRTH (Nitin Gadkari) to use river silt in highway construction; Kosi = "Sorrow of Bihar"; a Joint Committee monitors the 1996 India-Bangladesh Ganga Water Sharing Treaty.

[14] [04 Nov 2026] Bharatiya Vayuyan Niyam-2026: notified by the Civil Aviation Ministry; replaces the nearly 90-year-old Aircraft Rules, 1937; framed under the Bharatiya Vayuyan Adhiniyam, 2024; in force from 1 Jan 2027; covers registration, airworthiness, design, maintenance, flight operations, licensing of pilots, engineers and ATCs, airfares and airport management. The Adhiniyam 2024 replaced the Aircraft Act 1934, in force 1 Jan 2025; Civil Aviation Minister Kinjarapu Rammohan Naidu; DGCA regulator, BCAS security; ICAO est. 1944, HQ Montreal.
