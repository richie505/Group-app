=== National Health Mission – ASHA and Mother-Child Schemes
- **NRHM launched 2005** (accessible, affordable, quality care for rural India); **NUHM 2013**; **NHM** (NRHM + NUHM) approved 1 May 2013.
- **ASHA = Accredited Social Health Activist** – village-level community health worker who, with the ANM, links households to the health system (not "Association" or "Assistant"). AP has 42,060 ASHAs (2026).
- Funding: Centre:State **60:40**; North-Eastern and Himalayan States **90:10**.
| Component | What to remember |
|---|---|
| JSY (Janani Suraksha Yojana) | **Cash** for institutional delivery; 100% centrally sponsored |
| **JSSK** (Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram) | **Free ("zero expense") delivery incl. C-section, drugs, diagnostics, blood, diet and transport** for pregnant women and sick newborns at public facilities |
| RKSK | Adolescents **10–19 years** |
| **Anaemia Mukt Bharat** (2018) | 6x6x6 strategy; **iron deficiency anaemia** is India's major nutritional public health problem |
| **Kilkari** / Mobile Academy | **72 free weekly audio messages** to pregnant women and mothers (2nd trimester till child is one); Mobile Academy = audio course for ASHAs |
- **"Public" in public health** = community-wide, municipal, unrestricted (not private or exclusive).
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy APPSC 18 p.4 (NHM 2013); NextGen Environment APPSC Part 8.2 p.10 (ASHAs); Rocket Biology p.35 (iron → anaemia); app notes

=== Immunisation – UIP, Mission Indradhanush and eVIN
- **Mission Indradhanush (Dec 2014)**: catch-up **immunisation of children** (and pregnant women) under UIP; reaches **zero-dose and partly immunised** children; target **90% full immunisation**.
- **eVIN** (Electronic Vaccine Intelligence Network, MoHFW + UNDP): indigenous system that **digitises vaccine stocks and monitors cold-chain temperature through a smartphone app**. **U-WIN**: digital registry of routine immunisation.
| Age (UIP) | Vaccines |
|---|---|
| Birth | BCG, OPV-0, Hepatitis B birth dose |
| 6, 10, 14 weeks | OPV 1–3, Pentavalent 1–3, Rotavirus, fIPV; PCV (6 and 14 weeks) |
| **9–12 months** | **MR-1**, JE-1, PCV booster, Vitamin A |
| 16–24 months | MR-2, DPT booster-1, OPV booster, JE-2 |
| 5–6 years | **DPT booster-2** |
| 10 and 16 years | **Td** |
- Childhood vaccines vs disease: **BCG – TB; OPV – polio; DPT – diphtheria, pertussis, tetanus; MMR – measles, mumps, rubella**; hepatitis B; TT booster. There is **no "cancer vaccine"** in the child schedule.
> Wrong pairs in PYQs: Td at 5–6 years; OPV-1 at 16–24 months; DPT booster-2 at 6 weeks.

=== Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission and Digital Health
- **ABDM**: launched **September 2021**; implemented by the **National Health Authority (NHA)**; consent-based, interoperable digital health ecosystem.
| Type | Components |
|---|---|
| Registries | **ABHA (Health ID, 14 digits)**, **Healthcare Professionals Registry**, Health Facility Registry, Drug Registry |
| Gateways | Health Information Consent Manager, UHI, National Health Claim Exchange (NHCX) |
| Testing | **Sandbox** |
- **Arogya Mitra is NOT an ABDM component** – it is the PM-JAY hospital facilitator (the app's notes record an APPSC 2025 key of "Sand Box", which is wrong: Sandbox is ABDM's testing environment).
- **Digital Health Incentive Scheme** (NHA, Dec 2022): in effect from **1 January 2023**.
- **First ABDM Hackathon** (Round 1 – Unified Health Interface): **Pune**, 14–17 July 2022.
- **Andhra Pradesh = first State to link the electronic health records of one crore people** to the ABDM database.
- ABHA accounts: **over 90 crore** (30 May 2026); nearly half held by women. NMC (Mar 2026): medical colleges to link ABHA IDs for all OPD, IPD and emergency patients.
- Benefit of digital records: **better sharing and use of patient information** (not less privacy or more paperwork). eSanjeevani = telemedicine.
| Public health technology – chronology | Year |
|---|---|
| **Vaccines** (Jenner, smallpox) | 1796 |
| **X-ray** (Röntgen) | 1895 |
| **Electron microscope** | 1931 |
| **Electronic health records** | 1960s onward |

=== Ayushman Bharat – Pillars, PM-JAY, NHA and PM-ABHIM
- **Ayushman Bharat** = the **health** sector flagship (2018) with four pillars:
| Pillar | Role |
|---|---|
| Ayushman Arogya Mandir (earlier AB-HWCs) | Comprehensive primary care; target **1,50,000**; meets 80–90% of health needs |
| **PM-JAY** | Hospitalisation cover **₹5 lakh per family per year** |
| ABDM | Digital backbone |
| **PM-ABHIM** | Health infrastructure – launched by **PM Narendra Modi** at **Varanasi, 25 Oct 2021**; ₹64,180 crore (2021-22 to 2025-26) |
- **National Health Authority** (runs PM-JAY and ABDM): Governing Board **chaired by the Union Health Minister**.
- AAM services: NCD screening (hypertension, diabetes, oral/breast/cervical cancer), mental health, ENT, eye, oral, geriatric, palliative and trauma care.
- PM-ABHIM: critical care blocks, Integrated Public Health Labs, BSL-3 labs; in AP 10 Critical Care Blocks and 12 IPHLs inaugurated from **Vijayawada** (Sep 2026).
~~ Sources: NextGen Environment APPSC Part 8.2 p.10-11 (PM-ABHIM, AAM, PM-JAY); app notes

=== WHO and Global Health
- **WHO**: constitution in force **7 April 1948** → **World Health Day = 7 April**; HQ **Geneva**.
- **Director-General: Dr Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus** (since 2017; second term ends 15 Aug 2027). Earlier: Gro Harlem Brundtland, Lee Jong-wook, Margaret Chan.
- Membership: **166 (April 1991)** → 193 (2009) → 194 (2013).
- WHO **measles-free**: **Sri Lanka** and Maldives (2020).
- World Health Day 2026 theme: "Together for health. Stand with science."

=== ICMR, AYUSH and Health Research Bodies
- **ICMR**: began **1911** as IRFA, renamed **1949**. **Strategic Plan / Vision 2030** (Oct 2017): 5 pillars – capacity building, data management, **leveraging traditional medicine**, evidence to policy, research for programme implementation; **aligned with NHP 2017** – so all three statements of the APPSC 2023 PYQ are correct.
- ICMR initiatives: MERA India, India TB Research Consortium, India Hypertension Control Initiative (not "India Food Security Consortium").
- **ICMR-NARFBR, Hyderabad** (National Animal Resource Facility for Biomedical Research): inaugurated by **Health Minister Mansukh Mandaviya, 17 Dec 2022**.
- **Ministry of AYUSH**: formed **9 Nov 2014** (earlier Department of Indian Systems of Medicine and **Homoeopathy**, 1995 – renamed AYUSH 2003); a separate ministry, not under Home Affairs; **Sowa-Rigpa** (Tibetan medicine) added – AYUSH → AYUSH(S).

=== National Health Policies – Timeline
| Policy | Feature |
|---|---|
| **NHP 1983** | First policy; preventive and promotive care, health volunteers, referral system |
| NHP 2002 | Primary care approach; more public spending + larger private/NGO role |
