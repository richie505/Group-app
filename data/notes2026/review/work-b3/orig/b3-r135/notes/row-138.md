=== World Bank Projects in AP
- **AP Economic Restructuring Project (1998)**: AP became the **first Indian State** to negotiate an **independent World Bank loan**; it included reform of district primary education in **14** educationally disadvantaged districts.
- **APRIGP**: approved **19 December 2014**; **$75 million** of $107 million; **five components** (first: **value chain development**); covers the **150 most backward mandals**; implemented by **SERP**.
- **SERP** also runs the **Indradhanussu** total-immunisation drive and a **retail chain** concept to market SHG products and FMCG goods.
- **APIIATP**: climate resilience in **565 minor-tank villages**, **22 districts**; concluded **31 October 2025**.
- AP Road Sector Project: **850 km** of MDRs and State highways; **AP Disaster Recovery Project** after **Cyclone Hudhud (2014)**.

=== Amaravati, ADB, NDB, KfW, AIIB
| Lender | Loan | Approved |
|---|---|---|
| World Bank (Amaravati Integrated Urban Development Programme) | **$800 million** | **19 December 2024** |
| ADB (results-based loan) | **$788.8 million** | December 2024 |
- Both disbursed in **Japanese yen**; Budget 2026-27: ₹432.09 crore for Amaravati through **IBRD**.

- **AIIB:** urban water and sewerage (Online Works Monitoring, **123 ULBs**); **US$160 million** (2017) for **AP 24x7 Power for All**, AIIB's **first project in India**.

=== EAP Repayment under Special Assistance
- **Special Assistance Measure** (Union Cabinet, **15 March 2017**), in lieu of SCS: Centre repays loans and interest of EAPs signed **2015-16 to 2019-20**, bearing **90%** of the cost.
- Centre disbursed **₹7,798 crore** for **17 EAPs**; largest **VCIC**, then Health Systems Strengthening.
