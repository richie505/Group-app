=== Thirteenth Schedule – Infrastructure Commitments
| Commitment (Act) | Location | Status |
|---|---|---|
| GoI "**shall develop**" a new **major port**, Phase I by end-2018 | **Duggirajupatnam** (Dugarajapatnam) | Shelved by **NITI Aayog** as unviable; State built **Ramayapatnam** instead |
| **SAIL** to examine an integrated steel plant within six months | **YSR (Kadapa)** district (+ Khammam, Telangana) | Private JSW plant, works launched 3 Jul 2026 |
| IOC or HPCL to examine a greenfield refinery + petrochemical complex | AP | BPCL refinery near Ramayapatnam |
| Examine VCIC on the lines of the **Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor** | Visakhapatnam-Chennai | ADB-funded |
| Expand airports to international standard | Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Tirupati | Bhogapuram airport opened 1 Aug 2026 |
| Examine a new railway zone | SCoR, HQ Visakhapatnam | Operational 1 Jun 2026 |
| Metro rail feasibility | **Visakhapatnam** and **Vijayawada-Guntur-Tenali** (no Kurnool) | Before the Centre |
> Only the port carries "shall develop"; steel, refinery, zone, VCIC only "examine". Act named SAIL – plant is JSW; Act named IOC/HPCL – refinery is BPCL.

=== Kadapa Steel Plant
- Act: **SAIL** to examine within six months an integrated steel plant in **YSR district**; SAIL's report (2 Dec 2014): **prima facie not financially viable**.
- **December 2018**: foundation stone by CM N. Chandrababu Naidu at **M. Kambala Dinne, Mylavaram mandal**.
- **December 2019**: AP signed an MoU with **NMDC** for **iron ore** supply; State SPV **AP High Grade Steels Ltd** (Nov 2019).
- **3 Jul 2026**: JSW Rayalaseema Integrated Steel Plant at **Sunnapurallapalli and Peddandluru**; **₹16,350 crore**, scrap-based **EAF** green steel; water **2 tmc ft/year from Gandikota**.

=== Ports – Duggirajupatnam and Ramayapatnam
- **Duggirajupatnam**: only port named for a new major port by GoI; dropped as unviable (risk to **Pulicat Lake**, security of **Sriharikota**).
- Revival (2026): greenfield port + shipbuilding cluster in **Tirupati district**; SPV = **50:50 JV of VPA and APMB**; one of **five** national cluster sites.
| Ramayapatnam | Fact |
|---|---|
| District | **Prakasam** (earlier SPS Nellore, **Ulavapadu** mandal) |
| Location | 93 km north of **Krishnapatnam** |
| Developer | **Ramayapatnam Port Development Corporation Ltd** (State SPV) |
| Phase-I | ₹4,929.39 crore; **34.04 MTPA** |
| Progress | **74.34%** (Oct 2025), highest of four greenfield ports |
- **Captive ports** in AP's port list: **Meghavaram** and **Nakkapalli**; Ramayapatnam and Narsapur are non-captive (commercial) ports.

=== Refinery and Metro Rail
- **BPCL** refinery-cum-petrochemical complex near Ramayapatnam, between **Prakasam and Nellore** (villages **Chevuru and Ravuru**); **9 MTPA**; about **₹1.45 lakh crore**.
- **Kakinada** GAIL-HPCL complex: Centre sought **₹5,615 crore** viability gap funding from AP; Kakinada lies in the Visakhapatnam-Kakinada **PCPIR**.
- Metro: **AP Metro Rail Corporation Ltd**; Visakhapatnam metro **4 corridors, 76.70 km**; Visakhapatnam and Vijayawada metros approved by the **State** (Jan 2026).
- Southern AP cluster: Kadapa steel + BPCL refinery + **Ramayapatnam** port.
