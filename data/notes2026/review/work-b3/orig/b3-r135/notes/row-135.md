=== Principles of Division – Item → Basis → Section
# The core rule
- **Act:** AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 – President's assent **1 March 2014**; appointed day **2 June 2014**.
- **Core rule:** **assets by location**, **liabilities (debts) by population ratio**.
- **Population ratio** (Census 2011): **AP : Telangana = 58.32 : 41.68** (≈ **58 : 42**).
- **Result:** AP got about **46% of revenue** but inherited about **58% of debt** – major assets built in the united State stand in **Hyderabad**, which went to Telangana; so the location rule is unfair to AP.
- **Financial division:** **Part VI**, "Apportionment of Assets and Liabilities", **Sections 47-67** (the money items are **Sections 47-64**, Sec 64 = residuary; 76-83 = services); disputes settled by mutual agreement, failing which by **Central order on the advice of the CAG** (Sec 47(4)).
# Item → basis → section
| Item | Basis | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Award of the **13th Finance Commission** | **Population ratio** and other parameters (Centre apportions) | 46(1) |
| Land, stores, goods **inside** a State | **Location** | 48 |
| Stores, goods, property **outside** the existing State (e.g. AP Bhavan) | **Population ratio** | 48 |
| **Treasury and bank cash balances** | **Population ratio**, by **adjusting credit balances in the books of the RBI** – no transfer of cash between treasuries | 49 |
| Arrears of property tax / land revenue | State where the **property** lies | 50 |
| Arrears of **any other tax** | State of the **place of assessment** | 50 |
| Loans and advances to a body or person **inside** the State (incl. **deferred taxes** of industrial units) | State where the **area / unit is located** | 51(1) |
| Loans and advances to a person or institution **outside** the State | Right to recover belongs to **Andhra Pradesh**; sums recovered shared by population ratio | 51(2) |
| Securities against **Seventh Schedule** funds (List of Funds in the Public Account) | **Population ratio** | 52 |
| **Calamity Relief Fund** securities | **Area** ratio (the only area-based item) | 52 |
| Commercial / industrial undertakings (State PSUs) | **Operational units by location**; **headquarters by population ratio** | 53 |
| **Public debt** and Public Account liabilities | **Population ratio** (re-lent loans follow the area of the borrower) | 54 |
| Floating loans | Exclusive purpose → that State; rest by population ratio | 55 |
| Refund of property tax collected in excess | State where property lies | 56 |
| Refund of **other taxes** collected in excess | **Population ratio** (58.32 : 41.68) | 56 |
| **Provident Fund** | State to which the employee is **permanently allotted** | 58 |
| **Pensions** | As in the **Eighth Schedule** (population ratio) | 59 |
| Salaries of judges of the **common High Court** | **Population ratio** | 30(2) |
| **CM Relief Fund** (and other common funds) | **Population ratio** | – |
> Population ratio is used most often to divide **assets** held in common (cash, securities, HQ, outside property); the summary rule still reads "assets by location, debts by population".
> Matching trap: **Arrears of taxes = Sec 50** (correct pair); treasury = 49 (not 53), PF = 58 (not 59), public debt = 54 (not 58).

=== Schedule IX and Schedule X Institutions
| Feature | Schedule IX | Schedule X |
|---|---|---|
| What it lists | **Government companies and corporations** | **State institutions** whose facilities continue for both States |
| Count | **89** in the Act as enacted (91 commonly cited after additions) | **107** in the Act; **142** after **35** were added by notification GSR 369(E) of **7 May 2015** |
| Examples | **APSFC**, **APGENCO, APTRANSCO, SCCL**, **Sports Authority of AP (SAAP)** | **APSCHE**, **Shilparamam Arts, Crafts and Cultural Society**, training institutes |
| Governing section | **Sec 68** (continue to function) + **Sec 53** (manner of division) | **Sec 75** (continuance of facilities) |
| Division rule | Operational units by **location**; headquarters by **population ratio** | Assets by location; cash by population (APSCHE ruling) |
| Time limit | – | Terms for continuing facilities agreed within **one year** |
| Committee | **Sheela Bhide Committee** | – |
- **APSFC** (AP State Financial Corporation): Schedule IX; divided under **Section 70** – shareholder-approved scheme → Centre's sanction; if not approved, referred to a **High Court judge**, whose scheme is final.
- **Section 74**: set-off and carry-forward of PSU losses under the Income-tax Act.
- **Shilparamam** = Arts, Crafts and Heritage villages showing handicrafts of artisans (Schedule X).
- **SAAP** = **Sports Authority of Andhra Pradesh** (Schedule IX).
- **Section 66**: Centre may order allocation of benefits/liabilities; AP uses it for institutions in no schedule.
# Supreme Court and Home Ministry
| Case / Body | Ruling / Rule |
|---|---|
| **APSCHE** (Schedule X), Supreme Court **2016** | Division under **Section 47** (Sec 75 only keeps facilities going); assets by location, **cash and bank balances by population ratio** |
| Union Home Ministry order on APSCHE | Assets belong to the State of **location**; cash deposits/balances shared **52 : 48**; the MHA then **applied the same formula to other Schedule X institutions** |
| APSRTC **hospital and Kalyana Mandapam**, Hyderabad | Treated as **not part of APSRTC headquarters** (HQ read narrowly) → go by location |
| AP Bhavan, New Delhi | Outside the State → population ratio; Telangana claims it as Nizam's property |
| AP plea, Supreme Court, **December 2022** | Quick division of **Schedule IX and Schedule X** assets; AP's plea (advocate Mahfooz Ahsan Nazki, 2023) said non-division benefits **Telangana** and about **91% of assets lie in Hyderabad** |
> APSCHE = Schedule X; APSFC and SAAP = Schedule IX; Shilparamam = Schedule X.

=== Committees and Employee Apportionment – Part VIII
| Committee | Chair | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| **Sheela Bhide Committee** | Sheela Bhide (retd IAS) | Assets, liabilities and employees of **Schedule IX** companies and corporations; set up by the **undivided AP government** |
| **Kamalanathan Committee** | C.R. Kamalanathan | Allocation of **State-level employees** |
| **Pratyush Sinha Committee** | Pratyush Sinha | Allocation of **All India Services** officers (IAS/IPS/IFoS), under **Sec 80** |
| **Justice D.M. Dharmadhikari Committee** | One-man, set up by the **Supreme Court** (28 Nov 2018) | Division of **power-utility employees** |
- Sheela Bhide Committee recommended division of most Schedule IX bodies (89 of 91 reported); Telangana opposed some; its term expired Aug 2015 and the Centre revived it.
- **Part VIII**, "**Provisions as to Services**" (**Sections 76-83**): AIS and State employees in local, district, zonal, multi-zonal and State cadres.
- **Section 76**: two separate **State cadres** of **IAS, IPS, IFoS** (creates the Telangana State cadre).
- **Section 77**: State employees; does **not** apply to All India Services.
- **Section 80**: **Advisory Committees** within **30 days** of enactment; allocation by the Centre on their recommendation.
- **Section 82**: PSU staff stay for **one year**; the **corporate body itself** decides the distribution.
- **Section 83**: APPSC continues for AP; Telangana to constitute its own PSC.
- Non-opters allotted on **native (domicile) status**; seniority and pension disputes under **Sec 59 / Eighth Schedule**; DoPT one-member **Deepak Khandekar** committee (March 2024) on AIS cases.
- Comparison: the **1953 Andhra State** partition committee was chaired by **T.T. Krishnamachari**.

=== Twelfth Schedule – Power and Coal
- **Twelfth Schedule** = principles for **coal, oil and gas, and power** (Sec 92).
- **Coal:** **SCCL** equity **51% Telangana, 49% Government of India** – AP holds **no** equity; **existing coal linkages continue** without change; new linkages under the New Coal Distribution Policy.
- **Power:** **APGENCO units divided by geographical location** of the plants; existing **PPAs continue**.
- **Central Generating Stations:** power shared on the **actual energy consumption of the DISCOMs in the last 5 years**.
- Deficit State gets **first right of refusal** over the other's surplus power for **10 years**; APERC joint for 6 months, SLDC common for 2 years.
- AP got about **46% of power** for **58% of population**.
> The amicable division ratio is **AP : Telangana = 58 : 42** – a statement giving it as "Telangana and AP … 58 : 42 respectively" is wrong.
~~ Sources: AP Reorganisation Act 2014 (apra.txt, Twelfth Schedule); NextGen AP State Reorganisation Act notes Part 2 p.14-16

=== Access to Higher Education – Part XI
- **Section 95**, **Part XI** (Access to Higher Education): **equal opportunities for quality higher education**.
- Existing **Article 371D** admission quotas in all government/private, aided/unaided institutions of higher, technical and medical education continue for **10 years**, with the **common admission process**.
- **Section 97** amended **Article 371D** to read "**Andhra Pradesh or Telangana**".
> 10 years = common admission (Sec 95) = surplus-power right = common capital Hyderabad.

=== Unresolved Division – Status
- **CAG (2019):** **₹1,51,349.67 crore under capital heads** and ₹28,099.68 crore of loans and advances still **not apportioned** between AP and Telangana (still pending after 7 years).
- **Common institutions:** Telangana claims about **₹408 crore** from AP for running the High Court, Raj Bhavan etc. in Hyderabad (2022); the exact sum AP undertook is not established.
- Over **140** corporations/institutions worth more than **₹39,000 crore** undivided; AP wants **population ratio**, Telangana insists on **location**.
- **Unapportioned public debt** of over ₹24,000 crore on AP's books cuts its **FRBM borrowing room**.
- **Southern Zonal Council** directed AP to pay Telangana its share of a central subsidy credited to AP.
- Criticism: schedules not exhaustive; "**headquarters**" undefined → litigation.
