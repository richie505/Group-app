=== Backward Districts Package – Sections 46 and 94
- **Section 46(2)**: Centre may make **appropriate grants** and give a **special development package** to **backward areas** of AP; **46(3)**: adequate incentives **in particular for Rayalaseema and north coastal regions**.
- **Section 94(2)**: Centre to support backward-area programmes (physical and social infrastructure).
| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Districts | **7** = 4 Rayalaseema + 3 North Coastal |
| Rayalaseema | Anantapur, Kurnool, YSR Kadapa, Chittoor |
| North Coastal | Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam |
| Amount | **₹50 crore per district per year** = ₹350 crore a year (2016-17) |
| Model | **KBK** (Odisha) and **Bundelkhand** packages |
| Status | First allocations made; disbursements stalled |
> Section options 43-46: the package is **Section 46** (46(2)-(3)); not 45, 47 or 49.

=== BRGF and the Aspirational Districts Programme
- **BRGF** (Ministry of Panchayati Raj, 2006-07): objectives – **bridge critical gaps in local infrastructure** and development, **strengthen panchayat and municipality governance**, give **professional support** to local bodies for planning, improve their performance; all three are objectives.
- BRGF covered **250** districts; **13** in undivided AP (9 Telangana + Anantapur, Chittoor, Kadapa, Vizianagaram; not Srikakulam or Hyderabad); **delinked from central support in 2015-16**, folded into the Rashtriya Gram Swaraj Abhiyan (RGSA).
- **Aspirational Districts Programme**: **NITI Aayog**, launched by PM Modi in **January 2018**; **112** districts; built on **Convergence, Collaboration, Competition** (not compensation).
- **Convergence** = effective use of funds and services of different departments.
- Visakhapatnam Economic Region (**10 districts**, 13 Nov 2025) is not part of ADP.

=== Tax Incentives and Revenue Compensation
- **Section 94(1)**: fiscal measures incl. tax incentives (notified 2015): **35% additional depreciation** + **15% investment allowance** for units set up **1 Apr 2015 – 31 Mar 2020** in backward districts of **both AP and Telangana**.
- SCS (refused) would have given a **90% central share in CSS** and tax exemptions.
- **101st Amendment, 2016**: GST compensation for revenue loss.
- 2014-15 resource-gap grant: **₹3,979.50 crore**; **16th FC** ended RD grants for **all States** (AP loss up to ₹38,000 crore); AP cites the PM's **Rajya Sabha** assurances.
- **Polavaram**: Centre limits liability to **old price levels**.

=== Rayalaseema
- Now **8 districts**: Kurnool, Nandyal, Anantapur, Sri Sathya Sai, YSR, Annamayya, Tirupati, Chittoor.
- **Low population density** due to recurring **droughts and famines**.
- **43%** of horticulture area, **71%** of micro-irrigation; six AP districts in India's top 10 for micro-irrigation = five Rayalaseema + **Prakasam**.
- **Mega Seed Park**: **Kurnool** district (Tangadancha).
- **Sri Bagh Pact (16 Nov 1937)**: irrigation priority to Rayalaseema for 10 years.
| Project | Fact |
|---|---|
| Godavari-Penna link Ph I | **73 TMC** Godavari flood water to the Pennar, **five-stage** lift |
| Veligonda | **Krishna to Gundlakamma**; 71.5% complete |
| Polavaram-Nallamala Sagar link | Godavari surplus to Rayalaseema |

=== North Coastal Andhra (Uttarandhra)
- "**Kalinga Andhra**", 6 districts: Alluri Sitharama Raju, Anakapalli, Parvathipuram Manyam, Visakhapatnam, Vizianagaram, Srikakulam.
- Tribes: **Savara, Jatapu** (Srikakulam side); Gadaba, Bagata, Kotia, Porja, Khond (Araku agency).
- **Lowest literacy: ASR (44.67%)**; new **Polavaram** district (HQ **Rampachodavaram**) has the lowest urbanisation and density.
- Srikakulam, Vizianagaram: no anchor industries → **distress migration**; ASR **1st** in ADP **Basic Infrastructure** (March 2026).
- Projects: Nagavali-Champavathi link via **Thotapalli barrage**; **Bhogapuram airport** (Vizianagaram).
