=== Meaning, Indicators and Causes of Sickness
| Test | Sick if |
|---|---|
| **SICA 1985 (original)** | Registered **≥ 7 years**; **accumulated losses ≥ net worth** at year-end; cash losses in that and the preceding year |
| **1993 amendment** | Age cut to **5 years**; cash-loss test dropped |
| Companies Act (2002 amendment) | Accumulated losses ≥ 50% of average net worth of the preceding 4 years |
| RBI (micro and small) | Any borrowal account **NPA > 3 months**, or **50%** net-worth erosion |
| RBI 2016 framework | Early stress: **SMA-0, SMA-1, SMA-2** |
- **Reserves** = accumulated (retained) profits; **net worth** = share capital + reserves – accumulated losses, so sickness = losses wiping out net worth.
- **Immediate indicators** (financial symptoms): **drop in profitability**, **shrinking of market credit**, **labour unrest** at the unit; a **decline in market share** is a slower market trend, not an immediate signal (SSC 2005 key).
- **Types:** **sickness at birth** (project non-viable from the outset), genuine, induced, temporary.
| Internal causes | External causes |
|---|---|
| **Management inefficiency**, poor planning, diversion of funds, faulty appraisal | Competition, recession (demand) |
| **Defective capital structure**, inability to raise finance, poor working-capital management | Delayed, costly credit |
| Obsolete technology (internal **production** cause), low productivity | **Power shortage**, transport |
| – | Sudden **EXIM**, tax, environment policy changes |
- MSMEs: **delayed payments** by large buyers and government = major trigger.
# Consequences and sectors
- **NPAs, locked capital, unemployment, revenue loss to the exchequer** (NOT higher revenue).
- State took over sick private **textile and engineering** units mainly **to protect jobs** → lowered PSU returns.
- Most sickness: **textiles (cotton mills), jute, sugar, engineering** (not software services).

=== Institutions – IRCI to BIFR, SICA to IBC
| Year | Step |
|---|---|
| **1971** | Industrial Reconstruction Corporation of India (**IRCI**) – first rehabilitation body |
| 1985 | IRCI → **IRBI** (Industrial Reconstruction Bank of India) |
| 1997 | IRBI → **IIBI** (Industrial Investment Bank of India) |
| **1985** | **SICA** enacted, after the **Tiwari Committee** (1984-85) |
| **January 1987** | **BIFR** set up (started functioning 1987); appeals to **AAIFR** |
| **30 Aug 2001** | Sick Industrial Companies (Special Provisions) **Repeal Bill** introduced in the Lok Sabha; Repeal Act 2003 |
| **1 Dec 2016** | **BIFR and AAIFR dissolved**; cases → NCLT/NCLAT |
- **BIFR** = **reconstruction and financing of sick units**: early detection, revival of viable units, closure of unviable ones; covered **public and private** companies; under the **Ministry of Finance** (Department of Financial Services).
- BIFR failed due to **procedural delays, legal loopholes** (a SICA reference froze recovery) – NOT "rapid resolution".
| Old regime | Successor |
|---|---|
| SICA 1985 | **IBC 2016** |
| BIFR | **NCLT** (adjudicating authority) |
| AAIFR | **NCLAT** (appellate) |
| Debtor-in-possession | Creditor-in-control; regulator IBBI |
- Shift strengthened **creditors**; SICA had protected defaulting promoters.
- Rehabilitation needs **effective coordination among stakeholders** (banks, FIs, management, workers, government).
| Committee | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Tiwari (1984-85) | SICA 1985, BIFR |
| **Omkar Goswami (1993)** | Industrial sickness and corporate restructuring |
| Kohli (1999-2000) | Sick SSI units |
| Eradi (1999) | Insolvency → NCLT/NCLAT |
| T.K. Viswanathan (BLRC, 2015) | IBC 2016 |

=== National Renewal Fund, VRS and Other Remedies
- **National Renewal Fund (Feb 1992):** a **social safety net** – funded **VRS, retraining and redeployment** of workers displaced by restructuring; aim = **social security of workers**, not capital for modernising industries; wound up **2000**.
- **"Golden Handshake"** = **Voluntary Retirement Scheme** – a generous payment for leaving service early; used by public and private firms.
- **Liberalised Margin Money Scheme (June 1987):** to reduce SSI sickness.
- **RBI:** 2016 MSME revival framework; **one-time restructuring** of MSME loans without downgrade (**2019**, extended 2020-21).
- Today: companies → IBC (NCLT); delayed payments → **MSME Samadhaan**; MSME Bill (Rajya Sabha, Aug 2026) – CPSEs settle MSME invoices on **TReDS**.
- **Udyog Bandhu** (now Invest UP) – UP body **helping industrial units solve time-bound problems** (single window), not a labour or finance agency.
