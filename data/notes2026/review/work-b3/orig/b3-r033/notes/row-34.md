=== Status Approaches – UPS, UPSS, CWS, CDS
| Approach | Reference period / unit | Counted employed if | Captures |
|---|---|---|---|
| **UPS** (Usual Principal Status) | **365 days** | Worked the major time (**183 days or more**) | **Chronic** (long-term) unemployment |
| **UPSS** (Principal + Subsidiary) | 365 days | Also subsidiary work for at least **30 days** | Broadest worker count |
| **CWS** (Current Weekly Status) | **7 days** | Worked at least **1 hour on any day** | **Chronic + seasonal** |
| **CDS** (Current Daily Status) | Each day of the reference week; unit = **half day** | **4 hours or more** = full day; 1-4 hours = half day | Daily / intermittent; **highest rate** |
- Persons **unemployed for a major part of the year** = **usual status** unemployment; chronic unemployment is measured with **usual status** data, and the weekly status also captures it (so "both US and CWS").
- **CDS gives the highest rate** – it also counts the days of unemployment of persons recorded as employed under CWS.
- NSS uses **three reference periods** (year, week, day); **"Usual / Current Monthly Status" is NOT an NSSO approach**.
- **Census:** **main worker = worked 183 days or more** in the year; marginal worker = less than 183 days. The 183-day line is not an NSS approach.
> UPPSC 2005, 2016, 2019 and APPSC Group-I: chronic = usual status; "monthly" approach is the odd one out.

=== Labour Force Indicators – LFPR, WPR, UR
| Indicator | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Labour force** | **Employed + unemployed** (seeking or available for work); workforce = employed only |
| **LFPR** | Labour force (employed + those willing and available) ÷ **working-age** population (per 100 / 1000) |
| **WPR** (Worker Population Ratio) | **Employed persons as a percentage of the total population** |
| **UR** (Unemployment Rate) | Unemployed ÷ **labour force** × 100 |
| **PU** (Proportion Unemployed) | Unemployed ÷ population |
| Identity | **WPR = LFPR × (1 − UR)** |
- **Students, homemakers** and others not seeking work are **outside** the labour force.
- Casual worker = non-regular employment; fixed-term worker = employed for a predetermined period.
- NCERT: about **35 of every 100** persons are workers (Census WPR); PLFS 2022-23 WPR (15+, usual status) **56.0%**.
> "Percentage of employed persons in the population" = **WPR** (APPSC 2023 Degree Lecturers, RAS 2024).

=== Agencies and Sources of Employment Data
| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| **NSSO (now NSO)** | **Collects and publishes** unemployment data – quinquennial Employment-Unemployment rounds (**27th to 68th**); PLFS since 2017 |
| CSO | Official statistics; merged with NSSO into **NSO (2019)** |
| **Labour Bureau** (**1920**, Ministry of Labour and Employment) | EUS and **Quarterly Employment Survey**; labour statistics incl. **industrial disputes, closures, retrenchments and lay-offs** in factories |
| **CMIE** | Private; monthly **CPHS**, larger sample, different method, higher UR |
| DGE&T / Employment Exchanges | Job-seeker registration |
| Census of India | Main / marginal workers every 10 years |
- **Before NITI Aayog** (and still), unemployment data came mainly from the **NSSO**, not the CSO, the Planning or Finance Commission, the UNO or a "Rangarajan / Lakdawala formula".
- APPSC flashcard wording: unemployment data "collected by **CSO and NSSO**" (the NSSO was then under the CSO's ministry).

=== Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS)
- Conducted by the **National Statistical Office (NSO), MoSPI** – NSO's **standard method** for measuring unemployment since 2017.
- Designed by the **Standing Committee on Labour Force Statistics (SCLFS)**, set up on the advice of the **National Statistical Commission**.
- **Launched April 2017**; **first annual report (2017-18) released May 2019**; replaced the quinquennial NSSO rounds (last = **68th, 2011-12**).
| Feature | Original | From January 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | – | **Monthly all-India CWS** estimates (LFPR, WPR, UR) |
| Quarterly | **Urban only** (CWS) | Extended to rural |
| Annual | Rural + urban, UPSS and CWS | Calendar year (Jan-Dec) |
| Sample | – | **Raised** (not reduced) to about **2,72,304 households** |
- **Criticism:** CWS (1 hour a week = employed) **masks underemployment** and misses **circular migration**; CMIE and PLFS diverge (monthly, larger sample, different method).
