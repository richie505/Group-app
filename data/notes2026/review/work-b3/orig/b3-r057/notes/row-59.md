=== Malnutrition – Terms, Indicators and Burden
| Indicator | Measure | Type |
|---|---|---|
| **Stunting** | **Low height-for-age** | Chronic |
| **Wasting** | Low weight-for-height | **Acute** |
| Underweight | Low weight-for-age | Composite |
- **Undernutrition** = **protein-energy malnutrition + micronutrient deficiency + stunting + wasting** (i, ii, iii, v); overweight and **obesity** are **overnutrition**. Both together in one population = **double burden of malnutrition**.
- Malnutrition results from **all** of: inadequate diet, disease/infection, poverty and food insecurity. Biggest factor: **poor dietary habits and food insecurity**; **open defecation and unsafe water** add through **disease-linked loss of nutrients** – it is multi-causal, not diet alone.
- **Both rich and poor are malnourished** – the rich eat a wrong diet, the poor a poor diet (A and R true, R explains A).
- **FAO estimate (c. 2012): about 245 million undernourished Indians** (SOFI 2022 revised series: 247.8 million in 2004-06 → **224.3 million in 2019-21**; SOFI 2025: 12.0% of the population).
| Indicator (under 5) | NFHS-5 | NFHS-6 (2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Stunting | 35.5% | **29.3%** |
| Severe wasting | 7.7% | 5.2% |
| Underweight | 32.1% | 31.8% (barely moved) |
| Breastfed within 1 hour | 41.8% | 50.1% |
| Complementary feeding (6-8 months) | 45.9% | 59.5% |
- **Anaemia (NFHS-5):** children under 5 about **67%**, women about **57%**. **AP (NFHS-5):** stunting **31.2%**, wasting 16.1%, underweight 29.6%.
| Burden | State |
|---|---|
| Highest share of malnourished children (MoSPI 2011, ~60%) | **Madhya Pradesh** – "**Ethiopia of India**" |
| Stunting (Poshan Tracker, July 2025) | **Uttar Pradesh** (48.8%) |
| Wasting | **Bihar** (9.3%) |
| Underweight | Madhya Pradesh |
- **Anaemia** (mainly **iron** deficiency) is a leading cause of **maternal mortality**, rising in AP. FSSAI: no HFSS food sale within **50 m of schools**.

=== ICDS and National Nutrition Programmes
| Programme | Launch | Ministry |
|---|---|---|
| National Nutritional Anaemia Prophylaxis Programme | **1970** | Health |
| **ICDS** | **2 October 1975** | **Women and Child Development** |
| **NIDDCP** (renamed National Goitre Control Programme, 1962) | **1992** | Health and FW |
| NP-NSPE / Mid-Day Meal | **15 Aug 1995** (idea first in **Tamil Nadu**) | Education |
| National Programme for Prevention and Control of **Fluorosis** | 2008-09 | Health and FW |
| **MAA** (Mothers' Absolute Affection) – **breastfeeding** | **2016** | **Health and FW** |
| Anaemia Mukt Bharat | 2018 | Health and FW |
| PM POSHAN (renamed MDM) | 2021 | Education |
- APPSC pairs: "NIDDCP – 1999" and "Fluorosis – 2000" are **wrong**; **MAA – 2016** and **NNAPP – 1970** are **correct** (both 2 and 4).
- **ICDS services:** supplementary nutrition, **pre-school non-formal education**, primary healthcare, **immunisation**, health check-up, referral, **nutrition and health education**. **Not:** **family planning**, free books, school dress.
- National Nutrition Programmes include NIDDCP, National Iron Plus Initiative, Prevention and Control of Fluorosis – **not the National Oral Health Programme**.
- **1000 Days approach** = conception to age two – **the nutritional foundation of the child**.
- **National Nutrition Strategy (NITI Aayog, 2017)** envisages **Kuposhan Mukt Bharat**.

=== PM POSHAN (Mid-Day Meal)
- **NP-NSPE** (National Programme of Nutritional Support to Primary Education) launched **15 August 1995** as a Centrally Sponsored Scheme in 2,408 blocks (started in UP the same year); renamed **PM POSHAN in 2021**.
- Covers **Bal Vatika and classes I-VIII** in government and aided schools; financed and managed by the **Ministry of HRD (now Education)**; Centre-State **60:40** (non-NER States/UTs).
- Cooking cost for Bal Vatika and primary raised to **₹6.78** per child per day.
- Organised by **school management, PRIs and SHGs**; a **contractor** does not initiate or organise it. Partner NGO: **Akshay Patra Foundation** (2000, Bengaluru) – mid-day meals for school students.
~~ Sources: app notes 3:59 sub 7; Ramesh Singh Indian Economy part 04 p.165 (MDMS)

=== POSHAN Abhiyaan and Anaemia Mukt Bharat
- **POSHAN Abhiyaan (2018):** goal **Suposhit Bharat**; pillars **convergence, technology, Jan Andolan** (people's movement); targets stunting, underweight, anaemia and low birth weight – **not adult obesity**.
- **Rashtriya Poshan Maah = September**; **Poshan Pakhwada** (April, two weeks; 2025 edition) – to **improve nutrition**, especially of women and children.
- **Saksham Anganwadi and Poshan 2.0** (2021-22 to 2025-26): **2 lakh** Saksham AWCs; **POSHAN Tracker** = real-time monitoring of Anganwadi services.
- **Anaemia Mukt Bharat:** **IFA tablets, deworming**, outreach for **6 target groups**; **WIFS** for adolescents **10-19**; **delayed cord clamping** campaign; **periodic deworming**; addresses **non-nutritional causes** (malaria, haemoglobinopathies, fluorosis). It does **not** give prophylactic **calcium** → **only three** of the four UPSC 2023 statements are correct.

=== Food Fortification, GHI and SOFI
| Fortified rice phase | Year | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| I | 2021-22 | **ICDS and PM POSHAN** |
| II | 2022-23 | **291** Aspirational and high-burden districts under TPDS |
| III | 2023-24 | All TPDS districts |
- FSSAI logo **"+F"**; **double-fortified salt = iodine + iron**.
- **GHI 2025:** India **102nd of 123**, score 25.8 – "**serious**"; indicators: undernourishment, child stunting, child wasting, child mortality (not adult obesity). Government objection: **child-only indicators cannot measure hunger of the whole population**.
