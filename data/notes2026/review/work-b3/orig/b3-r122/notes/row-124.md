=== Rythu Bazars
- **Concept:** **direct marketing between farmers and consumers**, no middlemen → **better price to farmers and fair price to consumers**.
- **Started 1999**; first Rythu Bazars **functioning from January 1999**.
- **Ryot Bazar idea first in Punjab (Apni Mandi)**; **AP was the second** State.
- Run day-to-day by an **Estate Officer**; district coordinating officer = **Joint Collector** (not the District Collector); outside the purview of Agricultural Market Committees.
- **Pricing:** about **25% above wholesale** and **25% below local retail** prices.
- **Number:** 124 functioning, 28 under construction (2024-25); 129 (2025-26).
- **Price intervention:** the State's quickest channel – government procures **perishables** through them when prices swing.
| Feature | Regulated market yard | Rythu Bazar |
|---|---|---|
| Basis | AP (AP&L) Markets Act, 1966 | Executive scheme, 1999 |
| Managed by | Agricultural Market Committee | Estate Officer |
| Seller | Farmers sell to licensed traders | Farmer sells directly to consumer |
> Trap (APPSC): "District Collector is the coordinating officer" is the wrong statement – it is the Joint Collector.

=== Market Yards, Market Committees and the 1966 Act
- Farmers selling alone face **bargaining imbalance**: forced sales, false weights, unrecorded prices, middlemen margins.
| Act / rule | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| AP (Agricultural Produce and Livestock) Markets Act | 1966 | Act **No. 16** of 1966; crops, livestock and products |
| AP (AP&L) Markets Rules | 1969 | Under **Section 33** of the Act; licensing |
| Three central farm laws | 2020 | Repealed Nov 2021 → 1966 framework **remains operative** |
| Model APMC Act | 2003 | **Single market fee**, **registration** in place of licensing |
- **1966 Act mechanisms:** open auction, standard weights, recorded prices, **licensed buyers**.
- **Setting up:** notified area → Market Committee → notified market (yard). District supervision: **DATMO**.
- **Market fee: 1%**; Rs 433.39 crore (2024-25 to Dec 2024). AMCs pay **25%** of income (10% before **Oct 2015**) into the **Central Market Fund**.
- **Adoni** = second largest market yard; **Guntur Mirchi Yard** = Asia's largest chilli market; **Agri Export Zone for red chillies** in Guntur.
- **Pulpwood trading (Feb 2016):** **Subabul, Eucalyptus and Casuarina** sold to paper mills through market committees – **not bamboo**.
~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.95 (market fee); app notes 3:124 sub 1-2

=== Cooperative Marketing – MARKFED and Price Stabilisation
- **AP MARKFED** (AP State Cooperative Marketing Federation Ltd) = **apex organisation of the Cooperative Marketing Societies**; supplies inputs, cattle feed, fertilisers; works through RSKs and PACS.
- **13 DCMSs** procure produce at minimum administered prices, also on behalf of MARKFED.
- **Price Stabilisation Fund:** **Rs 3,000 crore** (2019-20); **MARKFED** played the key role; protects MSP for **turmeric, minor millets, sweet orange, banana, onion**; 2019 – groundnut farmers of 7 districts compensated.
~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.64-65; app notes 3:124 sub 5

=== Pledge Loans, Storage and Cold Storage
- **Rythu Bandhu Pathakam** (Raitu Bandhu): **pledge loan** scheme – farmer stores produce in **market-committee godowns** and borrows against it (hypothecation); avoids distress sale.
- **Capital Intensive Subsidy Scheme for cold storages:** introduced **July 1999**.
- **APSWC:** set up **August 1958**; share capital **50:50 GoAP and CWC**.
| Market committee godowns | 2024-25 | 2025-26 |
|---|---|---|
| Total | 1,052; 9.65 lakh MT | 1,052 |
| Operational | 626 | **643** |
- Gap: limited operational storage, need for **cold storage**. **AIF**: farm-gate infrastructure at **RBKs/RSKs**.

=== e-Marketing – e-NAM and eFarmarket
- **e-NAM:** 1,656 mandis in 23 States and 4 UTs; **AP 33 markets**; each AP mandi got free software + **Rs 30 lakh**; 39-52 FPOs registered.
- **Mostly intra-mandi:** most e-NAM trades are within one mandi; AP: only **11 mandis** in inter-mandi trade (Rs 13.98 crore, Dec 2024). Helped **price discovery**, not yet payments or inter-mandi trade.
- **Model APLM Act 2017:** makes the **whole State a single market**, private markets, e-trading, unified licence – i.e. pushes **inter-mandi and inter-State** trade, not intra-mandi.
- **eFarmarket:** AP = **first State** with an e-marketing platform linking its farmers to traders across India (APFEVCL SPV, 2021). **CM APP** (Continuous Monitoring of Agricultural Prices and Procurement): farm-gate prices collected **daily from every RBK** – AP the only State.

=== FPOs and Collective Marketing
- **Central Sector Scheme for 10,000 FPOs** (budget Rs 6,860 crore); implementing agencies **SFAC, NABARD, NCDC, NAFED**; AP: 455 FPOs registered.
- **Farmer Producer Companies** get financial assistance from **NABARD and SFAC** (equity grant, credit guarantee).
| Problem of the farmer | Channel |
|---|---|
| Middlemen margin on vegetables | Rythu Bazar |
| False weights, unrecorded prices | Regulated market yard |
| Forced sale after harvest | Rythu Bandhu pledge loan |
| Price crash | Price Stabilisation Fund |
| Small, scattered lots | FPOs |
| Limited buyers in one mandi | e-NAM |
