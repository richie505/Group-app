=== Cooperative Credit Structure – Three Tiers in AP
| Tier | Institution in AP | Number / fact |
|---|---|---|
| State (apex) | **APCOB** – AP State Cooperative Bank | 18 branches |
| District | **DCCBs** – District Cooperative Central Banks | **13** DCCBs; 442 branches (2024-25), 436 (2025-26) |
| Village | **PACS** – Primary Agricultural Cooperative Credit Societies | 2,042 (APSES 2024-25); 2,024 (2025-26) |
- **Short-term cooperative credit is three-tiered**: PACS (village) → DCCB (district) → State Cooperative Bank (apex).
- **DCCB = federation of the PACS of a district**: it works as their **integrated unit**, financing and supervising them.
- **DCCB funds:** **entry fee, share capital, deposits and loans** (borrowings from APCOB/NABARD) – all of these.
- **Long-term credit:** nationally by **ARDBs** (Land Development / Cooperative and Rural Development Banks).
- **AP's integration:** the **Single Window** system (**1987**, on the Mohan Kanda recommendations) put short- and long-term loans under one cooperative umbrella; AP integrated the two from **1986**; primary agricultural development banks abolished in **1987**.
- **APCCADB merged into APCOB** from **30 April 1994** (Single Window Act, 1994); **APCOB** now refinances DCCBs for long-term credit.
> Traps: DCCB (not APCOB, not an Agricultural Development Bank) is the district integrated unit of PACS; three tiers for short-term credit.

=== Cooperative Laws and Principles
| Act / policy | Year | Key provision |
|---|---|---|
| **Cooperative Credit Societies Act** | **1904** | **Started the cooperative movement** in India – and in Andhra (Madras Presidency); cooperatives' entry into rural finance |
| Cooperative Societies Act | 1912 | Allowed **non-credit** societies; federations |
| AP Cooperative Societies Act | 1964 | Main State law; AP Cooperative Tribunal under **Section 75** |
| AP MACS Act | 1995 | Self-reliant, member-run societies; **no government share capital**, little State control |
| National Cooperative Policy | 2002 | – |
| 97th Amendment | 2011 | Art. 19(1)(c), Art. 43B (DPSP), Part IXB |
- **Cooperative Principles (1995):** adopted by the **International Cooperative Alliance (ICA) Congress** at Manchester (Statement on the Cooperative Identity, seven principles).
- **AP Cooperative Tribunal:** headed by a **judicial officer**; hears **appeals** under the 1964 Act; **original jurisdiction** under the MACS Act, 1995. Tribunals set up at Visakhapatnam and Warangal in 2003.
- Supreme Court struck down Part IXB for **State** cooperatives; it survives only for **multi-State** societies.
- **Nehru:** three pillars of democracy = **cooperatives, panchayats and schools**.
> Traps: 1904 started the movement (credit only); 1912 allowed non-credit societies; Tribunal = Section 75 of the 1964 Act.

=== PACS – Functions and Computerisation
- **Credit:** crop and short-term loans; **inputs** (fertilisers, pesticides, seeds, cattle feed).
- **Non-credit:** paddy and maize procurement, seed processing, PDS, insurance, drinking water, warehousing, petrol bunks, Jan Aushadhi Kendras, CSCs.
- **Model bye-laws:** **25+** non-credit activities; **NABARD** supports PACS as **Multi-Service Centres**.
- **Computerisation (CSS, Ministry of Cooperation):** **2,037** AP PACS; PACS software integrated with **B@ncs24** of DCCBs (6,000+ touch points); 1,553 PACS completed pre-migration; ERP nationally about 80,000 PACS (Jul 2026).
- **Who runs it:** **NABARD** = national-level implementing agency; in AP the project runs through the Cooperation Department and **APCOB** (State nodal / facilitating agency) with the DCCBs.
- **1,246 of 1,490** PACS (assigned to fertilizer companies) became **PM Kisan Samruddhi Kendras**.
- **PACS as FPOs:** PACS in **207 blocks** eligible for **NCDC** equity grants; **APCOB = CBBO**.
- **AIF through PACS:** NABARD loan Rs 736.03 crore; 695 godowns, 3.98 lakh MT; farm-gate infrastructure at RSKs; PACS as Multi-Purpose Facility Centres.

=== Ministry of Cooperation Initiatives
- Set up **2021**; vision "**Sahkar se Samriddhi**".
- **Grain storage plan** ("world's largest grain storage programme in cooperatives"): piloted **31 May 2023**, storage at **PACS** via AIF, AMI; by Jul 2026, godowns in **313** AP PACS, 1.80 LMT.
- Sep 2026: AMI subsidy **25% → 33.33%**; margin money **20% → 10%**; with 3% AIF subvention PACS pay about 1%.
- **Tribhuvan Sahakari University** (IRMA, Anand): India's **first** national cooperative university. **NUCFDC**: umbrella body of **urban cooperative banks**.
- 2 lakh new societies planned; **White Revolution 2.0**: **50%** more milk procurement.
