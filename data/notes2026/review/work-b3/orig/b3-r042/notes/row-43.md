=== Swachh Bharat Mission and Swachh Survekshan
# SBM basics
- Launched **2 October 2014** (**Gandhi Jayanti**) – October 2014; goal **ODF and Clean India by 2 October 2019** (Gandhi's **150th** birth anniversary) – the only one of Jan Dhan, BBBP, Janshree Bima and SBM with that deadline.
- Two components: **Rural (Grameen)** and **Urban (Shahari)**; SBM-Gramin also targeted an ODF rural India by **2 October 2019**.
- Focus on **collective behavioural change** of the whole community; open defecation is part of the usual early-morning walk, crop check and socialising for many (mainly rural India).
- **Swachh Yug** (2016): to make **villages along the Ganga ODF**.
- **SBM-U 2.0** launched **1 October 2021**: garbage-free cities.
# Swachh Survekshan
- **2017 assessment areas**: waste collection, sweeping and transportation; processing and disposal; ODF; **capacity building (SBM e-learning portal)**; **IEC and behaviour change** – **not "level of micro particles"** in the air.
| Garbage Free City star | Prerequisite certification |
|---|---|
| 1 Star | ODF |
| 3 Star | ODF+ |
| 5 Star | ODF++ |
| 7 Star | Water+ |
- **Water+ (Aug 2021)**: AP became the first State with more than one Water+ city – **three: Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Tirupati**.
- GFC vision: no garbage or litter visible in the city at any time of day.
- **Indore**: cleanest city (10 lakh+), year after year; **Swachh Survekshan 2024-25 (July 2025)**: Indore top for the **8th year in a row**; new **Super Swachh League** of 23 cities.
- Super Swachh League, 10 lakh+ group: **Indore, Surat, Navi Mumbai, Vijayawada (AP)**.
- Ranked "cleanest big city" (10 lakh+) in 2024-25: **Ahmedabad**, then Bhopal, Lucknow.
- Visakhapatnam: SafaiMitra Surakshit Shehar award (sanitation worker safety).
- **Swachh Vayu Survekshan** (MoEFCC, under the **National Clean Air Programme**): ranks cities on clean-air actions; million-plus top city **Indore** (2023, announced by Bhupender Yadav; also 2025); **2026** (5th edition): **Lucknow** 1st, Indore 2nd, Jabalpur 3rd.

=== Smart Cities Mission
# Selection
- Launched **25 June 2015** by MoHUA; **100 cities** – a holistic city rejuvenation programme via competitive challenge.
- Two-stage; rounds: Round 1 (Jan 2016) 20, fast track 13, Round 2 27, **Round 3 (June 2017) 30** (incl. **Amaravati**), **Round 4 (Jan 2018) 9** + Shillong (June 2018) = 100.
- City shares among States: **equal weight to urban population and number of statutory towns** (not districts).
- **Uttar Pradesh: 10 smart cities** – Lucknow, Kanpur, Agra, Varanasi, Prayagraj, Aligarh, Jhansi, Moradabad, Bareilly, Saharanpur.
- **Logo text**: "Smart City – **MISSION TRANSFORM-NATION**".
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Area-Based Development | Retrofitting/redevelopment of at least 50 acres |
| Pan-City | Tech solutions; Integrated Command and Control Centres |
| SPV | One per city; company under Companies Act, 2013; 50:50 equity of State/UT and ULB |
| CITIIS 2.0 (2023) | 18 smart cities; integrated waste management; partners AFD, KfW, EU, NIUA |
# Financing
- Centre **Rs 48,000 crore** – **Rs 100 crore per city per year, about Rs 500 crore per city** over the mission – matched by State/ULB.
- Other sources: ULB own funds, Finance Commission grants, municipal bonds, PPP, loans, convergence.
- **Water and sewage financed through property tax**.
- Progress (11 Jul 2025): Rs 47,459 crore (**99%**) of central share claimed; mission extended to **2025-26**.
- **BRICS Smart Cities Conference**: **Jaipur, 17-19 August 2016** (MEA with ORF).

|---|---|

| CLSS – Credit Linked Subsidy | Central Sector |
| AHP – Affordable Housing in Partnership | CSS |

- PMAY-U 2.0 (2024): **1 crore more** urban houses; verticals BLC, AHP, Affordable Rental Housing, Interest Subsidy Scheme; **ISSR not in 2.0**; 1.25 crore+ houses sanctioned under PMAY-U and 2.0.

- **Rajiv Awas Yojana (RAY)**: mission of **"Slum-free India / slum-free cities"** – not free houses or interest-free loans.

- **VAMBAY** (Valmiki Ambedkar Awas Yojana, **Dec 2001**): housing for **urban slum dwellers below the poverty line without adequate shelter**; merged into JNNURM's **BSUP**; rural counterpart **Indira Awaas Yojana**.
- **PMAY-NTR Nagar** (AP, 2017): **1.93 lakh** houses for the urban poor (built by L&T, Shapoorji Pallonji and others); **APTIDCO** is the State nodal agency for PMAY-U (TIDCO houses).

=== JNNURM and Earlier Urban Schemes
- Announced in Budget 2005-06; launched **3 December 2005**; a **7-year** **urban renewal programme**.
- Urban infrastructure and basic services in **63 mission cities** – mainly the **million-plus cities** (over 60), State capitals and heritage cities; succeeded by AMRUT and Smart Cities (2015).
- Objectives: urban infrastructure (water supply, sewerage, solid waste, roads, **urban transport**, renewal of old city/**heritage areas**), houses and basic services for the poor; **urban electrification was NOT an objective**; not rural housing or employment.
- Components: **UIG** (Urban Infrastructure and Governance) + **BSUP** (Basic Services to Urban Poor); smaller towns via **UIDSSMT** + **IHSDP** (Integrated Housing and Slum Development Programme).
- Reforms: **25% of municipal budget** for the urban poor; **25% of developed land** for EWS/LIG; **7-point charter** – tenure, housing, water, sanitation, education, health, social security.
- **ILCS** (Integrated Low Cost Sanitation), **1980-81**: end dry latrines and manual scavenging; funded on **sharing basis – Centre 75%, State 15%, beneficiary 10%**.

=== AMRUT and AMRUT 2.0
- Launched **25 June 2015**; revamps **urban infrastructure** (basic services) – initially **500 cities** (15 merged → 485) covering about **60% of urban population** (so "400 cities" is wrong).
- Mission cities: all ULBs with **1 lakh+** population; other State/UT capitals; all **HRIDAY cities**; cities on stems of main rivers; hill-State, island and tourist cities. **Dausa was NOT** a Rajasthan mission city (till March 2024); Jaipur, Ajmer, Alwar, Tonk qualified.
- Outlay: **Rs 1 lakh crore**, incl. Rs 50,000 crore central (2015-16 to 2019-20); delivered through **State Annual Action Plans**.
- **Thrust areas**: **water supply**; **sewerage and septage**; **storm-water drains to reduce flooding**; **public / non-motorised urban transport**; **green spaces and parks with recreation centres for children**; capacity building. NOT: high-rise buildings, Wi-Fi, uninterrupted power, crop procurement.
- **AMRUT 2.0** (1 Oct 2021 to 2025-26): **universal water supply in all statutory towns**; 100% sewerage/septage in the 500 AMRUT cities; Rs 2.79 lakh crore projects sanctioned.

=== HRIDAY – Heritage Cities
- **National Heritage City Development and Augmentation Yojana**, started **21 January 2015** by **MoHUA**: urban planning, economic development and heritage conservation.
- **12 cities**: Ajmer, **Amaravati (AP)**, Amritsar, Badami, Dwarka, **Gaya**, Kanchipuram, Mathura, **Puri**, Varanasi, Velankanni, **Warangal**.

=== Andhra Pradesh Angle
- **Four Smart Cities**: **Visakhapatnam, Kakinada, Tirupati, Amaravati** (not Guntur or Vijayawada); Amaravati came in Round 3 (30 cities, June 2017).
- **Kakinada**: second most populous city of (old) East Godavari, near the 82.5°E IST meridian, a Smart City.
- Apr 2026: Vizag, Kakinada and Tirupati reported progress under the extension; Vizag – integrated command centre + intelligent traffic system.
- **Swachh Andhra** (2024-25): waste segregation **96.07%**; 6,890 TPD; 104 STPs (690.90 MLD). AP's **Black Spot Monitoring and Management App** tracks **garbage vulnerable points** and sanitation issues.
- Urban funding (Jul 2026): ~**Rs 19,561 crore** mobilised (AMRUT 2.0, AIIB, Urban Challenge Fund, UIDF, SBM 2.0).
- **Anna Canteens**: meals at **Rs 5** for the urban poor; reopened **Aug 2024**.
- Urban-affairs MoU with **UN-Habitat** (not UNESCO), Jul 2026; parliamentary panel wants a High-Level Expert Committee on urban infrastructure to 2047.
- **APTIDCO**: State nodal agency for PMAY-Urban (TIDCO houses).
