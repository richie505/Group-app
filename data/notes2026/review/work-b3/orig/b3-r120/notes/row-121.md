=== Six Agro-climatic Zones and Their Districts
- Residual AP has **six agro-climatic zones** (SES 2024-25 Table 5.2): regions defined by climate, soil, topography – they decide the best cropping systems.
| # | Zone | Districts (13 old districts) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **North Coastal** | Srikakulam, **Vizianagaram**, Visakhapatnam |
| 2 | **Godavari** | East Godavari, West Godavari |
| 3 | **Krishna** | Krishna, Guntur, **Prakasam** |
| 4 | **Southern** | **Chittoor, YSR Kadapa, SPS Nellore** |
| 5 | **Scarce Rainfall** | Kurnool, **Anantapuramu** |
| 6 | **High Altitude and Tribal Areas** | Hill and tribal parts of Srikakulam, Visakhapatnam, East Godavari |
- **No "Eastern Zone"** and **no "Low Altitude" zone**: the sixth is **High** Altitude and Tribal Areas.
- **Chittoor** is Southern, not North Coastal or Scarce Rainfall; **Nellore** is Southern, not Krishna or Scarce Rainfall; Kurnool is Scarce Rainfall, not Southern.
- Undivided AP (NARP): **7 zones** (incl. North and Southern Telangana; Krishna-Godavari as one zone).
- Not the six **Article 371-D** zones (administrative zones for public employment).
- Zones are still listed against the **13 old districts**; AP now has **28 districts** (Aug 2026).
> Exam angle: number of zones (6), match zone ↔ district, odd-one-out zone name.
~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.50 (Table 5.2); app notes 3:121

=== Rainfall, Drought and Cloud Seeding
| Zone | Annual rainfall |
|---|---|
| North Coastal | 1,000-1,100 mm |
| Krishna-Godavari | 800-1,100 mm |
| Southern | 700-1,100 mm |
| Scarce Rainfall | 500-750 mm (lowest) |
| High Altitude and Tribal | above 1,400 mm (highest) |
- **Average rainfall** of (undivided) AP: about **890-940 mm** (Group-I 2003 key: 890 mm). June-Dec 2024: **960.3 mm** vs normal **860.0 mm** (+11.7%).
- **Drought-prone**: Scarce Rainfall Zone (Kurnool, Anantapuramu). Highest rainfall: North Coastal + tribal highlands.
- **Cloud seeding:** first artificial rain experiment in AP at **Atmakur (Anantapur)**; the 2004 programme used **silver iodide** and **sodium chloride**.
- **Kharif 2026**: all 28 districts in deficit; worst hit Annamayya, Prakasam, Chittoor, Markapuram, Visakhapatnam (Sep 2026).
~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.8; app notes 3:121; general knowledge for the 2003 average (not checked)

=== Soils, Crop Suitability and Research Stations
| Zone | Soils / setting | Crops | RARS |
|---|---|---|---|
| North Coastal | High rainfall, laterite soils | Plantation crops, paddy | Anakapalle |
| Godavari | Fertile deltaic alluvium | "Rice bowl", aquaculture hub | Maruteru (not Rajahmundry) |
| Krishna | Deltaic and coastal plains | Paddy, chillies, cotton | Lam (Guntur) |
| Southern | Varied: plains to hills | Mixed dryland and irrigated | Tirupati |
| Scarce Rainfall | Red and black soils | Groundnut, dryland crops | Nandyal |
| High Altitude and Tribal | Hill soils | Coffee, plantation crops | Chintapalle |
- **West Godavari**: first in rice production in recent years (keyed) and largest paddy area in 2016-17 (keyed); East Godavari close behind.
- AP lies in the **Southern cotton zone** with Telangana and Karnataka.
- Zones decide region-specific interventions; AP's farm-income disparities follow the six zones.
- District agricultural contingency plans: **ICAR-CRIDA**; **PMDS** under natural farming uses leftover soil moisture in drought-prone areas.

=== Agro-climatic and Agro-ecological Zoning of India
| Classification | Number |
|---|---|
| **Planning Commission agro-climatic zones** (1988, for the **8th Plan**) | **15** |
| NBSS&LUP agro-ecological regions / sub-regions | 20 / 60 |
| **Sengupta and Sdasyuk (1968)** | 4 macro, 11 meso, **60 micro** regions |
| Karnataka agro-climatic zones | 10 |
| AP agro-climatic zones | 6 |
- Planning Commission zones are based on soil, climate, rainfall (basis for state-wise agricultural roadmaps, Apr 2026); **agro-ecological zone** = agro-climatic zone + overlaid landform.
- Sengupta and Sdasyuk: macro = climate, meso = physiography, micro = crop combinations.
