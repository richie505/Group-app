=== Vertical Devolution – States' Share
- **Vertical devolution** = sharing of **net tax proceeds between the Union and the States**; **horizontal devolution** = allocation of the States' share **among the States**.
| FC | States' share |
|---|---|
| 10th | **29%** of gross Central taxes (Alternative Scheme of Devolution) |
| 11th | **29.5%** |
| 12th | **30.5%** |
| 13th | **32%** |
| 14th | **42%** (biggest-ever rise, +10 points) |
| 15th | **41%** (J&K became a UT) |
| 16th | **41% retained** |
- **80th Amendment (2000)**, effective **1 April 1996**: gave effect to the **10th FC** scheme; made **all central taxes shareable**.
- **14th FC basis for the vertical share:** the spirit of the constitutional provisions, fiscal-space concerns of the Centre and States, and clarity on functional and expenditure responsibilities – **not** differing aspirations of State populations.
- **16th FC:** **18 of 28 States** sought **50%** – rejected because of the Union's fiscal position (defence, infrastructure); capping cesses "neither permissible nor desirable".
- 42 → 41 → 41: the one-point cut came at the **15th FC**, not the 16th.
> Exam angle: share-by-number (29, 29.5, 30.5, 32, 42, 41, 41) is asked in nearly every State PSC paper.

=== Horizontal Devolution Criteria
| Criterion | 13th | 14th | 15th | 16th |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Income / fiscal capacity distance | **47.5** | **50** | **45** | 42.5 |
| Population 1971 | 25 | 17.5 | – | – |
| Population 2011 | – | 10 | 15 | **17.5** |
| Area | 10 | 15 | 15 | **10** |
| Forest cover / forest and ecology | – | **7.5 (first time)** | 10 | 10 |
| Fiscal discipline | 17.5 | – | – | – |
| Demographic performance | – | – | 12.5 | 10 |
| Tax and fiscal effort | – | – | **2.5** | **dropped** |
| Contribution to GDP | – | – | – | **10 (new)** |
- **Highest weight** in every recent FC = income (fiscal capacity) distance.
- 16th FC: GDP weight criticised for rewarding **size, not need**; a shift **from entitlement to performance**.
- **Equity vs efficiency:** most FCs relied on income distance and population; critics say fiscal capacity is not uniform, so devolution does not equalise by itself.
- Eight States (mostly NE + West Bengal) lose in both devolution and grants under the 16th FC.

=== Channels of Transfer and Grants
| Channel | Article | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| Tax devolution | **270** | **Untied** |
| FC grants-in-aid | **275** | Statutory, charged on the Consolidated Fund of India |
| Discretionary grants | **282** | Mostly tied, via Union ministries; route for most CSS |
| Centrally Sponsored Schemes | 282 | Designed by the Centre; **matching State share** |
- **Art. 123** (ordinance) is not a transfer channel. Art. 275 grants: revenue deficit, local bodies, disasters, tribal welfare – **not** for every CSS.
- **CSS rationalisation (2015-16, Sub-Group of CMs):** **Core of the Core, Core and Optional** schemes.
- **CSS 2026-27:** **₹5,48,798 crore** (+30.6%); 16th FC tied CSS to **measurable outcomes** and proposed a **high-powered committee** to close inefficient schemes.
- **15th FC grants:** revenue-deficit, **sector-specific, State-specific** (total **₹10.1 lakh crore**); **₹4,800 crore** for **educational outcomes** (2022-23 to 2025-26).
- **16th FC grants:** total **₹9.47 lakh crore**; grants' share in FC transfers **19.4% → 8.3%**; only **local-body (₹7.91 lakh crore)** and **disaster** grants – **revenue-deficit, sector- and State-specific grants discontinued**.
- **Disaster:** SDRF/SDMF corpus **₹2,04,401 crore** (Union about ₹1.55 lakh crore); cost-share **90:10** NE/Himalayan, **75:25** others; **heatwave and lightning** to be notified disasters.
- **Who releases central funds:** the **Union Finance Ministry**; NITI Aayog has no fund-allocation role.

=== Divisible Pool and Cesses
- Pool = Art. 270 taxes (corporation tax, income tax, CGST, the Centre's IGST share) **minus cesses, surcharges and cost of collection**.
- Pool as share of gross tax: **89.2% (FC-13) → 82.1% (FC-14) → 78.3% (FC-15)**; about **81%** in 2025-26 (89.1% in 2014-15, 74-80% in 2020-24).
- **"41% illusion":** cesses and surcharges are outside the pool, so the stated share overstates the States' real share of gross tax.
- **16th FC:** annual **CAG-certified** disclosure of net tax proceeds.

=== Transfers and Outcomes; Borrowing; GST Compensation
- **Economic Survey 2016-17:** no **positive** relationship between **redistributive resource transfers** and per capita consumption, GSDP growth or governance; **suggestive negative** relationship, notably with **own tax effort** → link transfers to fiscal and governance effort.
- **ES 2025-26:** transfers rose sharply; **SASCI** 50-year interest-free capex loans prevented **pro-cyclical** compression of State capex – 2.17% (FY22) → **2.37%** of GDP (FY25).
- **Budget 2026-27:** devolution **₹15,26,255 crore**; total transfers ₹26,20,769 crore; capex loans ₹1,85,000 crore.
- **Borrowing:** State fiscal deficit **3% of GSDP** (16th FC); extra **0.5% of GSDP** for **power-sector reforms**; extra borrowing equal to **NPS/UPS** contributions.
- **GST compensation:** 5 years **July 2017 – June 2022**; cess continued to repay COVID loans; unused balance **50:50** Centre-States, States' half by **SGST revenue**.

=== Gadgil and Gadgil-Mukherjee Formulae
| Criterion | Gadgil (1969) | Gadgil-Mukherjee (1991) |
|---|---|---|
| Population | **60%** | **60%** |
| Per capita income | 10% | **25%** |
| Tax effort | 10% | 7.5% |
| Irrigation and power projects | 10% | – |
| Special problems | 10% | 7.5% |
- Endorsed by the **NDC (1968-69)**; used from the **4th Plan (1969)**.
- First **Special Category States**: **Assam, J&K, Nagaland** → **90% grant, 10% loan**; general States **30:70**.
- Formula plan transfers ended when **NITI Aayog** replaced the Planning Commission (**1 January 2015**).
