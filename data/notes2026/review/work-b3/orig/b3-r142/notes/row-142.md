=== VCIC, ECEC and the Act's Commitment
# What the Act says (Thirteenth Schedule)
- **Item 5:** GoI shall, **within six months** of the appointed day, examine the feasibility of a **Vizag-Chennai Industrial Corridor** "along the lines of the **Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor** (DMIC)" and take an expeditious decision (asked: Asst. Architectural Surveyor).
- Distractors: Delhi-Kolkata, Delhi-Raipur, Mumbai-Kolkata corridors – none is in the Act.
- Same Schedule: GoI to **"take steps"** for rapid **rail and road** connectivity from the new AP capital to **Hyderabad** and other Telangana cities – **no deadline**.
- **AP-ICDA Act, 2017**: creates the AP Industrial Corridor Development Authority; industrial parks notified under **Section 3**.
# VCIC at a glance
| Feature | Fact |
|---|---|
| Length | About **800 km**, along NH-16 on the AP coast |
| Parent corridor | First phase of the **East Coast Economic Corridor (ECEC)** = India's **first coastal economic corridor** (planned 2,500 km, **Kolkata to Tuticorin**) |
| Districts | Traverses **nine** (old) districts of AP (asked: DAO Mains) |
| Purpose | Gateway to **ASEAN** and East Asian markets – Act East Policy, Make in India |
| Funding partner | **ADB** (not JICA); modelled on DMIC; port-led |
- **"VCIC is a key part of"** → ECEC + ASEAN link + nine AP districts = **"All of these"** (G2 Mains 2019).
- AP is implementing **three** corridors: **VCIC, CBIC and HBIC** (Hyderabad-Bengaluru).
- Key initiative of the residual State quoted as attracting about **$17 billion** and **20,000 jobs** = **VCIC** (JL 2020 key; figure not verified). ADB: corridor industrial output to rise fourfold to about **$64 billion a year**.
# Nodes
| Stage | Nodes |
|---|---|
| ADB's **four** identified nodes (conceptual plan, 2016) | **Visakhapatnam** (anchor), **Kakinada**, **Gannavaram-Kankipadu**, **Srikalahasti-Yerpedu** (nine clusters in all) |
| Long-term phased nodes | **Donakonda** and **Machilipatnam** |
| Nodes now being developed (SES 2024-25) | **Vizag, Chittoor (Srikalahasti-Yerpedu), Kopparthy** – 33,000 acres, Rs 5,000 crore |
- **"How many potential nodes on VCIC?"** → **4** (Panchayat Secretary Mains).
- Vizag node: **Bulk Drug Park 2,002 acres** + steel cluster (Phase-1 2,200 acres); **Rambilli + Nakkapalli** clusters (Visakhapatnam) ≈ **49%** of corridor output.
- Chittoor node: Srikalahasti-Yerpedu start-up area **2,628 acres** (Rs 463 crore).
- **Kopparthy** (YSR Kadapa): Phase-1 **2,596 acres**, trunk infra ~Rs 1,264 crore via **NICDIT** (not JICA); **CCEA approved 28 Aug 2024**.
- **Orvakal** is an **HBIC** node, not VCIC.
> Exam angle: "on the lines of DMIC", ECEC first phase, nine districts, four nodes; do not confuse ADB (VCIC) with JICA (CBIC).

=== CBIC, HBIC, DMIC and the National Programme
# Corridors compared
| Corridor | Partner | AP node(s) |
|---|---|---|
| **DMIC** (Delhi-Mumbai) | **Japan** – MoU 2006; runs on the **Western DFC** | – |
| **CBIC** (Chennai-Bengaluru, ~560 km to Chitradurga) | **Japan (JICA)** | **Krishnapatnam** |
| **HBIC** (Hyderabad-Bengaluru) | NICDIT | **Orvakal** |
| **VCIC** | **ADB** | Vizag, Chittoor, Kopparthy |
- **Corridor developed with Japan** (State PSC): among Chennai-Vizag, Mumbai-Bengaluru, **Delhi-Mumbai**, Amritsar-Kolkata → **DMIC** (CBIC is also JICA-assisted but was not an option).
# CBIC nodes
- JICA's Comprehensive Integrated Master Plan for CBIC: master planning of **three** nodes – **Ponneri** (Tamil Nadu), **Tumakuru** (Karnataka), **Krishnapatnam** (AP) (G2 Prelims 2019 asked "how many"; printed key "five" is wrong).
- **Kuppam is NOT a CBIC node** (Panchayat Secretary Mains) – it is only a town along the route.
- **Krishnapatnam Industrial Area (KRIS City)**: **Tirupati district** (since 2022), CBIC; Krishnapatnam North Node **10,834 acres**, start-up area 2,500 acres, Rs 2,139 crore via **NICDIT**.
- **Orvakal** (Kurnool, HBIC): **2,621 acres** Phase-1, Rs 1,781 crore; CCEA approved **28 Aug 2024**.
# National bodies and the Budget
| Body | Fact |
|---|---|
| **NICDC** | **Corporation**, formerly **DMICDC**, Jan 2008; Commerce & Industry via **DPIIT**; runs NICDP and BHAVYA |
| **NICDIT** | **Trust**; funds trunk infra at **Kopparthy, Krishnapatnam, Orvakal**; Rs 3,000 crore BE 2026-27 |
| **NICDP** | **11** industrial corridors; Aug 2024: 12 more node projects approved |
| **BHAVYA** | 2026-27 to 2031-32; **Rs 33,660 crore**; 100 parks; **six** in AP |
- **Union Budget 2024-25**: funds promised for **Kopparthi** (VCIC) and **Orvakal** (HBIC) nodes (G2 PYQ).
> Exam angle: JICA = CBIC and DMIC; ADB = VCIC; CBIC nodes Ponneri-Tumakuru-Krishnapatnam; Kopparthi + Orvakal in Budget 2024-25.

=== APIIC, Industrial Parks, Clusters and SEZs
# APIIC
- **APIIC = Andhra Pradesh Industrial Infrastructure Corporation** (asked twice, 2023) – "Corporation", not "Company"; not "Inclusive"/"Indigenous".
- Incorporated **26 Sep 1973** (Polytechnic Lecturers; asked "year" → **1973**).
- **Nodal agency** for industrial infrastructure – IT, biotech, apparel parks and **SEZs**; PIU of VCIC; develops nodes under NICDP.
