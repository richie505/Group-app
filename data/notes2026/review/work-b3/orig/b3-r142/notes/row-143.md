=== AP and SCS – the 2014 Assurance and the Controversy
# The assurance
- **20 Feb 2014**: PM **Manmohan Singh** in the **Rajya Sabha** (not Lok Sabha, not a joint sitting) announced SCS for AP for **five years** – "a solemn assurance".
- **Not written into the AP Reorganisation Act, 2014** – so the Centre treated it as non-binding. Exam keys still answer "duration of special status as per the Act" = **five years** (it is really the PM's assurance).
- After bifurcation, citing the **revenue deficit**, CM **Chandrababu Naidu**'s main demand to GoI = **grant Special Category Status** (JL 2020).
# Timeline
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 2015 | 14th FC award used to deny SCS |
| Sep 2016 | **Special Assistance Package** instead |
| 2018 | **TDP left the NDA**; no-confidence motion in the **Lok Sabha** (July), defeated |
| 2019 | SCS an "emotional referendum"; **YSRCP** called the package a "betrayal" |
| 2026 | Demand revived (Lok Sabha debate on the AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Bill) |
- AP's argument: a promise on the floor of Parliament carries constitutional weight.
- SCS = status with automatic **90:10**; special package = **project-linked**, no automatic 90:10.
> Exam angle: Rajya Sabha, 20 Feb 2014, five years; TDP exit 2018 in the Lok Sabha.

=== Origin, Criteria and Benefits of SCS
# Origin
- SCS introduced in **1969**, usually credited to the **5th Finance Commission**; the **Gadgil Formula** (1968-69) approved by the **National Development Council (NDC)** gave SCS States a reserved first share of Normal Central Assistance – do not swap FC and NDC.
- **Not in the Constitution** – an executive, **Planning Commission-era** classification; later **Gadgil-Mukherjee Formula (1991)**.
- **First three:** **J&K, Assam, Nagaland**; **11** SCS States in all, **last = Uttarakhand**.
# Criteria (NDC)
- **Hilly and difficult terrain**; **low population density and/or sizeable tribal population**; **strategic location along international borders**; **economic and infrastructural backwardness**; **non-viable state finances**.
- NOT criteria: high population density, advanced infrastructure, high per-capita income.
- **Raghuram Rajan Committee (2013)**: proposed a **Multi-Dimensional Index (MDI)** of state backwardness (10 sub-indicators) to replace SCS-style allocation – States scoring ≥0.6 "least developed" (SI 2019).
# Benefits
| Benefit | SCS State | General State |
|---|---|---|
| CSS funding | Automatic **90:10** | Normal |
| Central loans | **Concessional** interest | Normal |
| Industry | Tax breaks and incentives | None special |
| Unspent funds | **Do not lapse** – carried forward | Lapse |
| Consumer Welfare Fund seed money | **90:10** | 75:25 |
| PMGSY habitations | **250+** | 500+ (plains) |
| GST registration threshold | Lower (**Rs 10 lakh**) | Rs 20 lakh |
- Rationale: a larger share of central funds to cover the gap from geographic disadvantage and backwardness.
# SCS States
| Status | States |
|---|---|
| Granted (11) | Assam, Nagaland, J&K, Himachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Tripura, Sikkim, Arunachal Pradesh, Mizoram, Uttarakhand |
| Asked, never granted | **Bihar, Odisha, Andhra Pradesh** |
| Not SCS | **Telangana** |
- Centre's fear: SCS for AP would bring identical demands from **Bihar and Odisha**.

=== Finance Commissions and the 42% Argument
| FC | Chair | Period | States' share | AP's share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13th | Vijay Kelkar | 2010-15 | **32%** | – |
| **14th** | **Y.V. Reddy** | **2015-20** | **42%** | **4.305%** |
| 15th | N.K. Singh | 2020-21; 2021-26 | 41% | 4.111% (2020-21), 4.047% (2021-26) |
| 16th | Arvind Panagariya | 1 Apr 2026 - 31 Mar 2031 | 41% | **4.217%** |
- 14th FC accepted **24 Feb 2015**; the **32% → 42%** jump applied from **2015-16** (Polytechnic Lecturers 2020) – the largest increase ever.
- 14th FC made **no distinction** between general and special category States; Centre: higher devolution **subsumed** SCS benefits.
- AP: 42% went to all States alike; it ignored AP's **asymmetric loss of Hyderabad**.
- 16th FC added a **"Contribution to GDP"** criterion (**10%** weight) and ended revenue deficit grants.
> Exam angle: 32 → 42 → 41 → 41; 14th FC from 2015-16; Y.V. Reddy.
