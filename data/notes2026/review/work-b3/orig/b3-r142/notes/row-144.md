=== The Package – Announcement, Period and Amounts Released
# Announcement
- Announced **September 2016** by Union FM **Arun Jaitley**, in place of SCS; also called **Special Assistance Measure (SAM)** or "Special Package".
- Union Cabinet approved it on **15 March 2017**.
- Period: **2015-16 to 2019-20** = five years, the promised SCS period (SCS assurance: **Manmohan Singh**, Rajya Sabha, 2014).
# Money released
| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| **Special Assistance 2016-17** (announced 19 Aug 2016) | **Rs 1,976.50 crore** = Rs 1,176.50 cr resource gap + **Rs 350 cr** seven backward districts + **Rs 450 cr** capital (G1 2017) |
| Resource-gap / revenue deficit grant for 2014-15 | **Rs 3,979.50 crore** released by 2016 |
| Backward districts (to 2016-17) | Rs 1,050 crore at Rs 50 crore per district a year |
| Capital city | **Rs 2,500 crore** (incl. Rs 1,000 crore from MoUD for Guntur and Vijayawada drainage) |
| Total central assistance to Aug 2016 | Rs 8,379.50 crore (Rs 4,403 cr in 2014-15, Rs 2,000 cr in 2015-16) |
| SAM total (as stated in notes) | Rs 24,350 crore (incl. Rs 3,979 crore RD grant; rest infrastructure) |
- **Trap:** Rs 1,976.50 crore (not Rs 4,058.04 crore) is the 2016-17 special assistance; Rs 1,176.50 crore is only its resource-gap part.
- **NITI Aayog report (1 Dec 2015)** on developmental support to AP: **Rs 2,100 crore** for the **7 backward districts** at **Rs 300 crore per district**.

=== Components, the EAP Route and SCS vs Package
# Components and Act sections
| Component | Content |
|---|---|
| Special assistance | Money equal to the extra central share AP would get as an SCS State (**90%**) – via **Externally Aided Projects** |
| Polavaram (**Sec 90**) | National project; Centre bears **100%** of the irrigation-component cost from **1 Apr 2014** |
| Tax (**Sec 94(1)**) | Industrial incentives; promised 10-year tax holiday delayed, diluted or merged into GST |
| Backward areas (**Sec 46(2)**) | **Rayalaseema and North Coastal** – 7 districts (4 + 3); releases stalled |
| Revenue deficit (Sec 46) | Bridge the 2014-15 resource gap |
| Capital | **Rs 2,500 crore** promised |
# The EAP route
- AP borrows from **World Bank, ADB** etc.; the **Centre repays loan and interest** of EAPs signed and disbursed in **2015-16 to 2019-20** – i.e. **all EAP loans of the period are repaid by the Centre** (APPSC Asst. Director).
- Limit: the **SCS-equivalent 90%** share; no open grant; funds tied to **approved EAPs**.
- AP's criticism: long approval cycles, heavy paperwork – **red tape and delays**; Jan 2018: CM asked for the money as a grant via **NABARD**.
# SCS vs package
| Benefit | SCS | Special Assistance Package |
|---|---|---|
| CSS funding | Automatic 90:10 grant | Equivalent amount only via EAP repayments |
| Tax for industry | Yes | Only weaker Sec 94(1) incentives |
| Use of funds | **All schemes** | Tied to approved EAPs |
| Duration | Promised 5 years | 2015-16 to 2019-20 |
> Exam angle: Centre repays EAP loans; Sec 90 = Polavaram, Sec 94(1) = tax, Sec 46(2) = backward areas.

=== Centre's Stand, AP's Stand and Present Measures
# Centre's stand
- **14th FC** removed the general-special distinction and raised devolution to **42%** – Centre said this **covered SCS benefits**.
- **"Constitutional and technical constraints"** on SCS for a new State; **domino fear**: identical demands from **Bihar and Odisha**.
# AP's stand over time
| Period | Stand |
|---|---|
| TDP govt (NDA ally), 2014-18 | Balanced alliance vs SCS demand |
| YSRCP | Package a "betrayal"; SCS its 2019 campaign plank |
| AP govt 2024-26 (NDA ally) | Seeks project money (Amaravati, Polavaram, SASCI) – did not leave NDA |
- **Aug 2026**: AP FM **Payyavula Keshav** – loss up to **Rs 38,000 crore** as the **16th FC dropped revenue deficit grants**; AP revenue deficit **Rs 16,000 crore (2014-19)** → projected **Rs 48,000 crore (2025-26)**.
# Measures since 2024
| Measure | Amount |
|---|---|
| Amaravati (2024-25 onward) | **Rs 15,000 crore** |
| Polavaram | **Rs 12,157 crore** |
| SASCI loans | Rs 4,090 crore (2023-24); Rs 7,900 crore (2024-25); 2025-26: Rs 6,544 crore availed to 12 Feb 2026 + Rs 1,438 crore due (~Rs 7,982 crore); up to **Rs 8,226 crore** (2026-27) |
| Rayalaseema Global Horticulture Hub (Aug 2026 nod) | Centre **Rs 10,000 crore** + State Rs 30,000 crore |
- **SASCI** (Scheme for Special Assistance to States for Capital Investment): **Ministry of Finance**, since **Oct 2020**; **50-year interest-free** loans for **capital works only**; above the net borrowing ceiling, **outside the 3% of GSDP** limit; not for revenue spending or EAP repayment.
