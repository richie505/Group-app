=== NABARD – Establishment, Role, Refinance and RIDF
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Full form | **National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development** (traps: Nation, Real, Net, Research) |
| Recommended by | **CRAFICARD (1979-81), B. Sivaraman** |
| Act | **NABARD Act, 1981** (Act 61 of 1981 of Parliament) |
| Established | **12 July 1982** (Sixth Five Year Plan) |
| Headquarters | **Mumbai**; initial corpus Rs 100 crore |
| Ownership | **RBI sold its stake in 2019** → fully owned by the Government of India |
- **Merged/replaced at birth:** RBI's **Agricultural Credit Department (ACD)**, **Rural Planning and Credit Cell (RPCC)** and the **Agricultural Refinance and Development Corporation (ARDC)** – NOT IFCI, IDBI or SIDBI.
- **Apex development finance institution** for **agriculture AND rural development** (not agriculture alone; also cottage, small and village industries) – the **apex bank for rural / agricultural credit** and the **only body catering to all types of rural credit**; set up as an apex body to **coordinate all institutions in the rural financing system**.
- **Primary role = refinance**: refinances **Scheduled Commercial Banks, RRBs, State Cooperative Banks, State Land Development Banks** – **not EXIM Bank**; does not lend directly to farmers (lends to **States** via RIDF). Refinance only for agriculture and rural development (not export or industrial finance).
- **Supervises RRBs and State/District cooperative banks**; launched **SHG-Bank Linkage (1992)**.
- **Credit planning:** NABARD prepares district-wise **Potential Linked Credit Plans (PLPs)** → roll up into the **State Focus Paper** → base of the State's **Annual Credit Plan**. AP 2026-27 plan: **Rs 5.11 lakh crore**.
- **NABARD Award for Rural Innovations** instituted in **2012** to mark NABARD's **30 years**.
| NABARD fund | Year / size | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **RIDF** (Rural Infrastructure Development Fund) | **1995-96**; funded by banks' **PSL shortfall deposits** | **Loans to States** for rural roads, bridges, godowns, irrigation, fisheries, forests |
| LTIF (PMKSY) | – | Irrigation |
| Warehouse Infrastructure Fund | 2013-14, **Rs 5,000 crore** | Warehousing |
| Food Processing Fund | **2014-15, Rs 2,000 crore** | Food processing |

=== Cooperative Credit Structure and Land Development Banks
| Tier / body | Level | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| **PACS** | Village | Short-term, direct to farmers |
| **DCCB** | District | Short-term; main function **finances PACS** |
| **State Cooperative Bank** (e.g. APCOB) | State apex | Short-term; refinanced by NABARD |
| **Land Development Banks** (now **Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Banks**) | State/primary | **Long-term** – land development, wells, machinery |
| **RRBs** | Region | Direct to small farmers, labourers, artisans |
- Short-term cooperative chain: **StCB → DCCB → PACS** (3-tier); long-term: **LDBs / CARDBs**. LDBs are part of the **Cooperative Credit Structure** (quasi-commercial), not commercial banks, IDBI or FCI.
- **First Land Development Bank: Jhang (Punjab), 1920.**
- Cooperative banks work on the **"No Profit No Loss"** model and "one member one vote"; they lend beyond agriculture; **NABARD is not a cooperative bank**.
- **Direct credit to rural households:** **RRBs and Land Development Banks**; **NABARD only refinances** (UPSC 2013: 1 and 3).
- UPSC 2020: **Scheduled Commercial Banks**, not DCCBs, deliver the most short-term farm credit; financing PACS is a key DCCB function.
- Institutional sources: **cooperative societies/banks, commercial banks, RRBs**, government (**taccavi** loans); non-institutional: moneylenders, relatives, traders.
- **SIDBI** = main institution for **MSMEs**, not agriculture.

=== Regional Rural Banks
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Recommended by | **Working Group on Rural Banks (1975), M. Narasimham** – RRBs as part of the **multi-agency approach** |
| Set up | **Ordinance 26 Sept 1975 → RRB Act 1976**; **5 RRBs on 2 October 1975** (Gandhi Jayanti) |
| First RRB | **Prathama (Grameen) Bank, Moradabad (UP)**, sponsored by Syndicate Bank |
| Sponsor | A **Scheduled Commercial Bank** (not RBI, not NABARD) |
| Share capital | **Centre 50% : Sponsor bank 35% : State Government 15%** |
| Regulation | **NABARD** supervises; sponsor bank supports |
- Functions: banking in rural and semi-urban areas; mobilise rural resources; loans mainly to **small and marginal farmers, agricultural labourers, rural artisans** (not large industry); **supplement Scheduled Commercial Banks**; participate in institutional farm credit. RRBs did **not take over agricultural refinance** (that is NABARD's).
- **Bhandari Committee (1994):** **RRB restructuring**; identified 49 RRBs; more powers to RRB Boards.
- **One State, One RRB:** 26 RRBs amalgamated, effective **1 May 2025** → **28 RRBs**.
- RRBs and SFBs have a **75%** PSL target.

=== Kisan Credit Card
- Launched **August 1998 (1998-99)** – not 2001 – as a **NABARD model scheme** (with RBI), on the **R.V. Gupta Committee** recommendation; aim: **adequate and timely credit** for cultivation needs.
- **Issuers:** **commercial banks, RRBs, cooperative banks/credit societies (PACS)**, small finance banks – not NABARD, EXIM Bank, payment banks or local area banks.
- **Short-term purposes (UPSC 2020: 1, 3, 4):** crop cultivation, **post-harvest expenses**, marketing loans, **consumption requirements** of farm households, **working capital for maintenance of farm assets**; also investment credit for allied activities → KCC covers **consumption and investment credit**.
- **Not** short-term KCC credit: **combine harvesters, tractors, mini trucks**, family house, village cold storage.
- Extended to allied/non-farm activities **2004**; **animal husbandry and fisheries 2019**; electronic KCC per **T.M. Bhasin** Working Group.
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Collateral-free | Up to Rs 2 lakh; **Rs 3 lakh** with hypothecation tie-up |
| Operative KCCs (31 March 2025) | **7.72 crore** |
| RBI KCC Directions (19 June 2026) | Tenure **6 years** for loans from 1 January 2027 |
- **Pashu Kisan Credit Card:** **Haryana (2020)**; first cards to 101 animal farmers at Bhiwani (6 December).

=== Sources and Shares of Agricultural Credit
- **Largest source:** **commercial banks** – about **three-fourths** of institutional farm credit (2010-11: 73.86%; cooperatives 16.68%).
- **Agency order:** **commercial banks > cooperative banks > RRBs** (3, 1, 2); **least: foreign private banks**.
- After the **1991 reforms**, credit flow to **agriculture** rose notably.
- **Rural branches** of SCBs incl. RRBs ≈ **37%** of all branches (RBI, 30 June 2012).
- Non-institutional share in rural credit: ~90% (1950) → **23.4%** (2021-22).
- **Ground Level Credit FY25 Rs 28.69 lakh crore** vs target **Rs 27.50 lakh crore**; **South** gets ~48% of credit with ~17% of cropped area.

=== Types of Credit and Rural Credit Terms
| Credit | Duration | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Short-term** (crop loans) | **Up to 15 months** | Seeds, fertiliser, wages |
| **Medium-term** | **15 months – 5 years** | Bullocks, pumps, implements |
| **Long-term** | **Over 5 years** | Land development, wells, machinery (LDBs) |
- **Taccavi (taqavi)** = government **agricultural loans** to cultivators (Muhammad bin Tughluq's taqavi written off by Firoz Shah).
- **Bank-linked SHG** lending is the "**modern**" rural lending channel (cooperatives, RRBs, commercial banks = conventional).
- **All India Rural Credit Review Committee (1969)** recommended **entry of commercial banks** into rural credit (multi-agency approach).

=== Interest Subvention and Kisan Rin Portal
| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| Lending rate on short-term crop/KCC loans | **7%** |
| Subvention to lenders | **1.5%** |
| **Prompt repayment incentive** | **3%** |
| Effective rate for prompt repayers | **4%** |
- **Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (MISS):** **Central Sector**, run through **RBI and NABARD**; limit Rs 3 lakh → **Rs 5 lakh (Aug 2026)**.
- Post-harvest loans at 7% for up to **6 months** against **WDRA negotiable warehouse receipts**.
- **Kisan Rin Portal (September 2023):** joins **KCC data with interest-subvention claims**; before it, banks filed ISS/PRI claims **manually with RBI and NABARD**.

=== Priority Sector Lending
| Category | % of ANBC |
|---|---|
| Overall (domestic banks) | **40%** |
| Overall (RRBs and SFBs) | **75%** |
| Agriculture | **18%** |
| Small and marginal farmers | **10%** |
| Non-corporate farmers | **14%** |
- PSL covers **agriculture, micro and small enterprises, weaker sections**, export credit, education, housing, social infrastructure, renewable energy – NOT luxury consumer goods.
- **Shortfall** → NABARD's **RIDF**; **PSLCs** let a short bank meet targets by buying certificates.
- **Narasimham Committee (1991):** phase out directed credit; narrower PSL of **10%**.

=== Andhra Pradesh – Agricultural Credit
- Three-tier cooperatives; PACS being made **Multi-Service Centres** with **NABARD** support.
- ACP 2025-26: agri target Rs 3,06,000 crore; **97%** disbursed. AP PSL 2025-26: **63.28%** of ANBC.
- **Tenant credit:** JLGs, RMGs and **CCRC** loans (AP Crop Cultivators Rights Act 2019).
- **SHG-Bank Linkage:** AP **1st**; ~**36%** of national disbursement; recovery 99.99%.
| Scheme (loans repaid within a year) | Subsidy | Loan size |
|---|---|---|
| Vaddi Leni Runalu | **4%** | Up to Rs 1 lakh |
| Pavala Vaddi | **1%** | Rs 1-3 lakh |
- **Indira Jala Prabha (Oct 2011):** **borewell irrigation** on SC/ST farmers' lands, funded by NABARD **RIDF** with MGNREGS.
