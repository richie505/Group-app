| Wage employment | Self-employment |
|---|---|
| RMP (1960), CSRE (1971), FFW (1977), NREP (1980), RLEGP (1983) | IRDP (1978 pilot / 2 Oct 1980), TRYSEM (15 Aug 1979), DWCRA (1982) |

- **Order:** TRYSEM 1979 → RLEGP 1983 → JRY 1989 → EAS 1993; IRDP 1980 → DWCRA 1982 → JRY 1989 → EAS 1993 → SGRY 2001 → NREGA 2006; PMRY 1993 → REGP 1995 → PMGY 2000 → VAMBAY 2001; IRDP → SGSY 1999 → NRLM 2011 → DDU-GKY 2014.
- **1970s motto:** rural development programmes of the 1970s aimed at **creating wage employment and income-generating assets for the rural poor**.
- **JRY objectives:** employment for the rural unemployed and underemployed **and** strengthening the rural economic and social structure ("all of the above").
- **JGSY** was implemented **entirely at the level of the Village Panchayat** (Gram Panchayat).
- **SGRY** (2001) fully absorbed **JGSY and EAS** – not Samagra Awaas Yojana or SGSY.
- **SGSY (April 1999)** merged **IRDP, TRYSEM, DWCRA, SITRA, GKY and the Million Wells Scheme (MWS)**; it organised the assisted poor into **Self Help Groups** to lift them above the poverty line; restructured as **NRLM (Aajeevika), 2011**.
- **PMRY** = **self-employment, not wage employment**; implemented through **District Industries Centres (DICs)** with bank loans.
- **SJSRY (1 Dec 1997)** = urban **employment-generating** scheme; subsumed **NRY, UBSP and PMIUPEP**; it did **not** include PMRY.
- Not employment-related: **RMSA** (March 2009, secondary education); MGNREGA, NRLM and **STEP** (training for women) are.
> APPSC asks "not a wage programme" (PMRY), "JRY restructured in 1999" (JGSY), PMRY agency (DIC), chronology codes (UPPSC 2024, BPSC 2015) and SGRY mergers (APPSC 2003).

=== Food for Work and NFFWP
- **Food for Work Programme:** launched **April 1977** by the **Janata government** to use surplus foodgrain stocks; wages paid in **food grains** (in part or full, the rest in cash); renamed **NREP in October 1980**.
- **First wage programme among exam options:** the **Crash (printed "Cash") Scheme for Rural Employment, 1971** – earlier than Food for Work (1977), NREP (1980) and JRY (1989).
- **National Food for Work Programme (NFFWP):** **14 November 2004**, by the UPA government, in the **150 most backward districts**, during the **Tenth Plan (2002-07)**; an interim step before NREGA.
- **NFFWP + SGRY** were subsumed into NREGA in its districts from **2006**.
- The Planning Commission was **not** a constitutional body (it was set up by a cabinet resolution).
> APPSC 2004: 150 districts; Group-II 2017: 1977; CDS 2008: FFW in the Tenth Plan.

=== MGNREGA 2005 – Enactment, Phases and Name
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Idea | First proposed in **1991 by P.V. Narasimha Rao**; promised in the UPA's **National Common Minimum Programme (2004)** – 100 days of work a year |
| Passed | Parliament **23 Aug 2005** |
| Assent | **5 Sept 2005** (Act No. 42 of 2005) |
| Notified / enacted | **7 Sept 2005** |
| Launch | **2 Feb 2006** in **200** backward districts |
| Phase 2 | **130** more districts in 2007-08 |
| Whole country | **1 April 2008** (all **625** districts) |
| Renaming | **"Mahatma Gandhi" added 2 Oct 2009** (MGNREGA) |
| Repealed | Replaced by the **VB-G RAM G Act** from **1 July 2026** |
- **Enacted 2005, scheme launched 2006**: "MGNREGA launched" = **2006**; "Act enacted / passed" = **2005**.
- The 100-day programme for rural folk = **REGA / NREGA**, not DRDA, SGSY or IAY.
- **Largest rural employment programme** in India; landmark of the **Manmohan Singh** government with **RTI 2005** and **NFSA 2013** (NEP 2020 is not).
> 7 Sept 2005 is the date asked as "enacted"; 7 Nov 2005 is a distractor. 625 = 2008 coverage, not the 2006 launch.

=== MGNREGA – Entitlement, Eligibility and Objectives
- **Objective:** "**enhancing livelihood security** in rural areas by providing **at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year** to every **household** whose **adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work**".
- **Unit = household**, not each adult: "100 days to every adult" is wrong; **150 days** is wrong for the normal guarantee.
- **Eligible:** adult members of **any rural household** – not only SC/ST, BPL or backward communities.
- **Unskilled** manual work only – "100 days of **skilled** labour" is not an objective.
- Other aims: **durable assets** (roads, canals, ponds, wells), rural income, **women's empowerment**, inclusive growth.
- Work within **5 km**; minimum wages; **legal entitlement** (statutory right, **not a Fundamental Right**); anchored in **Article 41** (DPSP).
- The Act covers **all rural areas of the country** in notified phases – **not "only selected States"**.
- **Drought-hit areas:** an extra **50 days** – **150 days** in a year in areas declared drought-affected (as in Feb 2019).
- **Features of MGNREGS:** guaranteed wage employment, **rights-based**, rural wage employment; **nutritious food is not** a feature.
- **SDG link:** NITI Aayog's **SDG India Index 2023-24** tracks MGNREGA under **SDG 1 (No Poverty)** – 99.7% of those who demanded work were offered it; Goal 1 score **72**.
- **World Development Report 2014** (World Bank): "**stellar example of rural development**"; safety net in agrarian distress and **COVID-19**.
> UPSC 2011 (any household), APPSC Group-II 2017 (household, 100 days), CDS 2020 (skilled labour is not an objective), APPSC Group-I 2023 (nutritious food is not a feature).

=== MGNREGA – Funds, Works, Monitoring and Social Audit
- **Women:** at least **one-third** of beneficiaries (Schedule II) – not one-half; actual share **about 56% of person-days (2016-17)**.
- **Work within 15 days** of application, else **unemployment allowance** (**1/4 of wage for the first 30 days, 1/2 thereafter**); wages within **15 days** of work; unemployment allowance is a **transfer payment**.
- **Funds:** Centre and State money is pooled in the **State Employment Guarantee Fund** (Section 21); the material component and the **14th Finance Commission** grants go directly to **Gram Panchayat** accounts.
- **Agriculture and allied works:** about **two-thirds (≈68%)** of expenditure in **2016-17**, up from ≈48% in 2013-14.
- **Permissible works:** water conservation, **percolation tanks**, **fish ponds**, **compost pits**, **cattle sheds**, land development, rural connectivity – all four in the APPSC 2017 question are permissible.
- **Convergence:** MGNREGS is the textbook example – 90/95 person-days for **PMAY-G** houses, works with agriculture, horticulture and watershed schemes.
- **Social audit:** **Section 17** – the **Gram Sabha** conducts regular social audits; **NREGA was the first Act** to make social audit a means of continuous public vigilance; a village social auditor must **not be a political person**.
- **Monitoring:** **GeoMGNREGA** geo-tags assets on ISRO's **Bhuvan mobile platform**; **NREGASoft** MIS; in AP fund flow was tracked on **RAGA soft** (TCS).
- **Newsletter** of MoRD for MGNREGA in the APPSC key: **Saaransh** (key not verified).
- **CAPART** and **NIRDPR (Hyderabad)**: autonomous bodies under the Ministry of Rural Development.
> 15 days twice: allowance if no work within 15 days of application; wages within 15 days of work.

=== VB-G RAM G Act, 2025 – Replacing MGNREGA
- **Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025**: passed in **December 2025** (18 Dec); in force from **1 July 2026**; nodal **Ministry of Rural Development**.
| Aspect | MGNREGA 2005 | VB-G RAM G 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Guarantee | 100 days/household | **125 days**/household, operated over **305 days** |
| Seasonal pause | None | States may pause up to **60 days** (sowing / harvest) |
| Funding | Mostly Central, demand-driven | **60:40**; **90:10** NE and Himalayan States; normative allocation |
| Wages | Within 15 days | Floor **₹300/day** (2026-27), indexed to CPI-AL |
| Works | Many categories | **4 priority areas:** water security, core rural infrastructure, livelihood infrastructure, extreme-weather mitigation; 375 works (Oct 2026) |
| Admin ceiling | 6% | **9%** |
- Job cards → **Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Cards**; Viksit Gram Panchayat Plans; biometric attendance; unemployment allowance kept (15 days).
- **AP ranked first** in the first 45 days (2.10 crore person-days); State-Level Council chaired by the CM.

=== Other Employment and Skilling Schemes
| Scheme | Ministry / agency | Target / feature |
|---|---|---|
| **PMEGP (2008)** | MSME; run by **KVIC** | PMRY + REGP; margin money 15-35%; cap ₹50 lakh (mfg) / ₹20 lakh (service) |
| **DDU-GKY (2014)** | Rural Development | **Placement-linked** skilling of **poor rural youth aged 15-35** (upper limit relaxed for vulnerable groups); skilling arm of NRLM |
| **Himayat** | Rural Development | Placement-linked skilling for **youth of J&K** |
| PM-SVANidhi | Housing & Urban Affairs | Collateral-free working-capital loans to street vendors |
| PM Vishwakarma | MSME | 18 traditional artisan trades |
| **Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyan (2020)** | Rural Development | 125-day campaign for **returnee migrants**, 116 districts of 6 States |
| **National Career Service (2015)** | **Labour & Employment** (not DoPT) | Job-matching portal for **all** job seekers, not only uneducated youth |
| Recognition of Prior Learning | Skill Development (PMKVY) | Certifies existing skills against NSQF |
| PM-Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana | Labour & Employment | Formal-job incentive, launched 15 Aug 2025 |
- **DDU-GKY** is for rural poor youth in general – **not exclusively for rural girls**; aims to turn the demographic surplus into a **dividend**; State share in a project can be partly in kind (APPSC key: **10%**, not verified).
- **Indira Gandhi Urban Employment Guarantee Scheme** (Rajasthan, 2022): **100 days**, raised to **125 days from April 2023**; work is due within **15 days**, not 30.
- PMEGP met its target of **4.02 lakh** micro-enterprises; 80% rural, 40% women.
- **Atmanirbhar Bharat (2020):** Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyan, PM-SVANidhi and extra MGNREGS funds were the employment legs of the COVID packages.
> CDS 2018: "exclusively for rural girls" is the wrong statement; UPSC 2017: NCS – neither statement correct.
