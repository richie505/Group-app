=== Insurance – Milestones, Companies and Regulator
| Act / body | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| LIC Act | **1 September 1956** | LIC formed; life insurance nationalised (over 240 insurers merged) |
| GIC | **1972** | General insurers nationalised; **National, New India, Oriental, United India** became GIC subsidiaries |
| Malhotra Committee | 1993-94 | Open insurance to private companies + independent regulator (not the Gadgil Committee) |
| IRDA Act | 1999 | Set up **IRDAI** – statutory regulator, HQ **Hyderabad**; set up **April 2000** |
| Agriculture Insurance Company of India (AIC) | 2002 | Implements crop insurance (RKBY/NAIS) in AP and other States; before it the **GIC** ran crop insurance |
| PMFBY | 2016 | Replaced NAIS (Rashtriya Krishi Bima Yojana) and MNAIS |
| Sabka Bima Sabki Raksha (Amendment of Insurance Laws) Act | Passed 17 Dec 2025 | **FDI cap 74% → 100%** |
- **LIC is not the oldest insurer** (Oriental Life 1818; National Insurance 1906); **National Insurance** was nationalised in 1972 and made a GIC subsidiary; **United India** HQ = **Chennai** (UPSC 2006 → B and C).
- The **2025 Act** amends the Insurance Act 1938, LIC Act 1956 and IRDA Act 1999 and creates a Policyholders' Education and Protection Fund.
- IRDAI vision: **"Insurance for All by 2047"**; FY25 premium income **Rs 11.9 lakh crore**; **health** passed **motor** as the top non-life segment.
- IRDAI panel on a standard **cyber liability** insurance product (Oct 2020): chaired by **P. Umesh**.
- Only about **5%** of Indians had insurance cover in the 2000s (APPSC G1 2008 key).
- AP enrolment under PMFBY, 2016-17: about **15 lakh** farmers (APPSC ASO 2017 key).
~~ Sources: Rocket Indian Economy #30 p.44 (LIC 1 Sept 1956); NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC 04 p.32 (IRDA Act 1999); GIC Re history (gicre.in); aicofindia.com (AIC 2002, RKBY); app notes 3:4 sub 0

=== Insurance Terms and Principles
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Insurance penetration | **Total premium as % of GDP** – not "insured persons per 100 people" (APPSC G2 2017 → none of these) |
| Insurance density | **Premium per capita** (USD 97 in FY25) |
| Principle of indemnity | Restores the insured to the **pre-loss position**, no profit; applies to fire, marine, motor, health – **not life** insurance |
| Actuary | Insurance expert who judges risk and estimates the premium |
| Cyber insurance | Data breach, hacking, ransomware, identity theft |
| Aviation hull | Physical damage to the aircraft itself |
| P&I | Shipowners' third-party liabilities; first sovereign-backed cover: **Bharat Maritime Insurance Pool** (Jul 2026) |
~~ Sources: Economic Survey 2025-26 Summary - Part 1 p.33-34 (penetration, density); NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC 01 p.33; app notes 3:4 sub 1

=== Tourism
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| ITDC | October **1966**; prime mover of the **tourism and hotel industry** |
| Swadesh Darshan | **2014-15**; integrated thematic circuits; revamped as Swadesh Darshan 2.0 |
| PRASHAD | 2014-15; Pilgrimage Rejuvenation and Spiritual, Heritage Augmentation Drive |
| TTCI → TTDI | Published by the **World Economic Forum** (TTDI from 2021); India **39th** of 119 (2024) |
| NIDHI | **National Integrated Database of Hospitality Industry** (Tourism Ministry) |
| Vision 2047 | **USD 1 trillion** tourism economy, 100 million foreign tourist arrivals |
| National Strategy for Sustainable Tourism | Ministry of Tourism, **June 2022**; Responsible Traveller campaign |
- Top State in **foreign** tourist visits: **Maharashtra** (2010, 2023, 2024); top in **domestic** visits: **Uttar Pradesh**.
- Foreign tourist spending in India = **export of services** = **invisible export** (foreign visitors at the **XIX Commonwealth Games**, UPSC 2011).
- Invisible exports: IT/software, tourism, banking, insurance, financial services, advertising.
- **Sustainable tourism** = managing tourism and the environment while maintaining cultural integrity and ecological processes.
- Economic Survey 2023-24: NIDHI, the 2047 vision, rising hotel occupancy and an expanding **Services PMI** – **all** four statements valid (APPSC G1).

=== Health Services
| NHA edition | Government health spending |
|---|---|
| 2018-19 | 1.28% of GDP |
| Latest (Sep 2026) | **1.43%** of GDP vs **NHP 2017 target 2.5%** |
- Health share of total government (Centre + States) spending: 6.12% (2021-22) → **4.89%** (2022-23).
- **PM-JAY (2018)**: **Rs 5 lakh** per family; with State schemes covers ~**80%** of citizens.
- **Aarogyasri:** launched **1 April 2007** in undivided AP by the **Y.S. Rajasekhara Reddy** government; named after **Rajiv Gandhi**; **cashless** cover for catastrophic illness of **BPL** families; continued as Telangana's flagship scheme after 2014.
- State + central schemes cover **70%** of AP households (2026).

=== Para-banking
- Legal basis: **Section 6, Banking Regulation Act, 1949**, with **RBI** approval; run **departmentally or through subsidiaries** (subsidiary route: **Section 19(1)**).
- Para-banking = **credit cards, insurance** (as **corporate agent** or **joint venture**), **factoring**, equipment leasing, mutual funds – **not** "credit to paramilitary personnel" or issuing currency.
- Bank credit-deposit ratio: 62-year high of 82.6% in Q1 FY27 (Jul 2026).
