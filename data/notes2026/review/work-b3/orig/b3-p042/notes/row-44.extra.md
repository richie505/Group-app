=== Higher Education – Institutions, Funding and Rankings
- **First distance education:** **Delhi University** – School of Correspondence Courses, **1962**; IGNOU came in 1985.
- **NIOS** (National Institute of Open Schooling) conducts **Secondary and Senior Secondary** examinations through open schooling.
- **First IIT:** **IIT Kharagpur** (1951, Hijli); then Bombay 1958, Madras 1959, Kanpur 1959, Delhi 1961.
- **NIRF 2020 (National Ranking) overall:** 1st **IIT Madras**, 2nd **IISc Bangalore**, 3rd IIT Delhi.
- **HEFA** = **Higher Education Financing Agency** (2017; JV of **Canara Bank** and the Education Ministry) – loans for infrastructure in IITs, NITs and other central institutions.
- **RUSA** (Rashtriya Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan, **2013**): CSS for State higher education under the **Ministry of Education** (then MHRD).
- **PPP route:** the Prime Minister's statement – higher education to be expanded through **Public-Private Partnership** (not outright privatisation or foreign providers).
- **Budget 2012-13:** education allocation raised ~18% to **Rs 61,427 crore** (school Rs 45,969 crore, higher Rs 15,458 crore).
- **Budget 2017-18:** new **AIIMS in Jharkhand and Gujarat**.

=== Learning Theories, Learning Curve and Behaviour Therapy
- **Gagne's hierarchy of learning:** signal (conditioned-reflex) → stimulus-response → chaining → verbal association → discrimination → concept → rule → **problem-solving (highest)**.
- **Guthrie's contiguity theory:** we forget **not by disuse but by other (new) learning** – interference.
- **Plateau** in the learning curve: a period of no visible progress when the learner is **consolidating previous learning and preparing for further learning** – not the final stage.
- **Bandura** – social (observational) learning theory, **Bobo doll** experiment (1961); rats/pigeons = Skinner, dog = Pavlov.
- **Assimilated learning:** a group learns something and **its behaviour and attitudes change** as a result (printed key).
- **Behaviour therapy:** counselling based on **learning theories and principles** (conditioning).
- **Motives** are not observed directly – they are **inferences from behaviour**.
- **Classification / sorting** by similarities and differences develops **cognitive** skills (Piaget).
- **Word mapping errors:** **under-extension** = defining a word too narrowly ('dog' only for the family pet); **over-extension** = using it too broadly.

=== Teaching Aids, Micro-teaching and Evaluation
- **Projected aids:** need a projector – **epidiascope**, slides, filmstrips, films, OHP. **Non-projected aids:** charts, pictures, graphs, models, pictographs, **globe, diorama, blackboard**.
- **Micro-teaching** (Stanford, **1963**): a **skill-based** teacher-training technique – one skill practised in a short, scaled-down lesson with feedback.
- **A good test** must have **validity, reliability and objectivity** (all three).
- **Test-retest reliability:** the **same test given twice** to the same group over a time gap; parallel forms = two equivalent tests; split-half = one test divided.
- **Diagnostic test:** finds specific weaknesses (reading, maths) so that **remedial instruction** or level placement can follow; achievement test measures what was learnt.
- **Shanghai (maths mastery) technique:** one concept per lesson, interactive whole-class teaching of an **all-ability group**, topic covered in depth, class moves on only when all have mastered it; **piloted in the UK** – all four statements correct.
- **The World of Learning** (Europa/Routledge annual): directory of **universities, colleges, libraries, museums, research institutes and learned societies** worldwide.
