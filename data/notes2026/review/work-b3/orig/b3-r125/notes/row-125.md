=== Industrial Development Policies – Editions, Targets and Incentives
# Editions → period
| Policy | Period | What is asked |
|---|---|---|
| Industrial Development Policy (IDP) | **2015-20** | With Single Desk Policy, **MSME Policy 2015-20** and sector policies (aerospace, textiles, food) |
| IDP | **2020-23** | **10 thrust areas**; micro unit = plant and machinery **≤ Rs 1 crore**, turnover **≤ Rs 5 crore**; **mega = 2,000 jobs**; **30 skill development centres**; **2 skill universities** (one for AI, robotics, VR, Industry 4.0); immersion programmes with international agencies; **not applicable within Vijayawada Municipal Corporation limits** |
| IDP | **2023-27** | In force **1 April 2023 – 31 March 2027**; aims to make AP an **"unchallenged" global investment destination**; **33%** of land in new industrial parks for MSMEs |
| IDP **(4.0)** | **2024-29** | Notified **26 Oct 2024**, **G.O.Ms.68**; flagship for Sub-Large, Large, Mega, Ultra-Mega units |
- **IDP 2020-23 incentives:** **SGST reimbursement for medium, large and mega units for 5 years, linked to employment**; **16.2%** of APIIC plots for **SC** entrepreneurs; APIIC 25% land rebate capped at **Rs 20 lakh**.
- **IDP 2023-27 aims (G1 2024):** a **minimum 30%** (not 25%) industry share in GSDP; port-led growth; APIIC land allotment and fast-track clearances in **21 days** (not 2 weeks); Single Desk Portal and **YSR AP One** for end-to-end support → statements I, II and V of the 2024 question are wrong.
- **The (4.0) 2024-29 suite (all 26 Oct 2024):** IDP (4.0) **G.O.68**, MSME & Entrepreneur Development (4.0) **G.O.69**, Electronics Manufacturing (4.0) **G.O.70**, Food Processing (4.0) **G.O.71**; also Private Industrial Parks ("Plug and Play"), Sustainable Electric Mobility, Textile & Garments 2024-29, IT & GCC Policy (4.0).
# IDP (4.0) targets and sectors
| Target (2024-29) | Value |
|---|---|
| Manufacturing GVA | Rs 3.4 → **7.3 lakh crore** |
| Investment | **Rs 30 lakh crore** |
| FDI | At least **USD 10 billion** |
| Jobs | **5 lakh** first-time manufacturing jobs |
| Industrial parks | **175+** |
| Exports | USD 20 → **40 bn** |
- **Motto:** **cut the cost of production and raise the Speed of Doing Business**.
- **10 Sustenance** sectors (automobile, engineering, chemicals, petrochemicals, food processing, pharma, textiles, metals …) and **10 Propelling** sectors (aerospace-defence-drones, EV and battery, toys, medical devices, **blue economy**, furniture, biotech, green energy, semiconductors, ports-logistics). Textiles = sustenance, not propelling.
- **P4** = Public-Private-People Partnership; "**One Family, One Entrepreneur**" by **2030**.
- **Investment Facilitation Cell:** liaison officers, handholding, approval tracking, resolution of delays.
# IDP (4.0) categories and incentives (FCI = **Fixed** Capital Investment, land excluded)
| Category | Threshold | Investment subsidy |
|---|---|---|
| Sub-Large | **Rs 51-500 crore** | 12% (+5% women/SC/ST/BC/specially-abled); 5 instalments |
| Large | Rs 501-1,000 crore | Up to **12%** (**6%** if only 50% capacity installed); **7 instalments** |
| Mega | Rs 1,001-5,000 crore **or 2,000+ jobs** | Up to **15%**, tailor-made |
| Ultra-Mega | **Above Rs 5,000 crore** | Up to 15%, tailor-made |
- **Early Bird:** **30%** for the first **200** projects with CFE in 18 months; **40%** for PLI sectors with CFE in 24 months.
| Standard incentive | Rate |
|---|---|
| Net SGST | **100% for 5 years** from commercial production |
| Stamp and transfer duty | **100%** reimbursement on purchase/lease of land |
| Land-conversion fee | **100%** reimbursement |
| Fixed power cost | **Rs 1 per unit for 2 years** |
| De-carbonisation | Up to **6% of FCI** |
| PLI top-up | **10% of GoI PLI**, cap 5% of FCI |
| Local procurement | **1% of export turnover, 3 years** |
| Employment Creation Subsidy | Up to **10% of FCI** (more than 5 workers) |
- **Not a standard incentive (G2 2025):** "15% capital subsidy on **Foreign** capital investment".
- **Escrow Guarantee** = promised subsidies paid on time. **Pavala vaddi:** State repays interest above **3%** a year → unit pays **25 paise per Rs 100 per month**.
- **Local jobs:** AP Employment of Local Candidates in Industries/Factories Act, **2019** – **75%** for locals.
> Number traps: mega = 2,000 jobs; SGST 5 years; 30 skill centres; 16.2% SC plots; 33% MSME land; IDP 2023-27 ends 2027; (4.0) = 2024-29.

=== Single Desk Portal, APIIC, APEDB and Other Promotion Bodies
# Single window → Single Desk
| Item | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| AP Industrial Single Window Clearance Act | **2002** | Statutory backing; **deemed approval** if a department misses its deadline |
| **Single Desk Portal (SDP)** | **April 2015** | Under the **Single Desk Policy 2015-20**; one-stop **G2B** platform for clearing **investment proposals** |
| AP Omnibus (Speed of Doing Business) Bill | 2026 | Amends the 2002 Act; passed Aug 2026 |
- **SDP:** **93+ clearances** from **19 departments**; **pre-establishment and pre-operation approvals within 21 days**; online application, payment, tracking, certificate – **no physical visits** to regulators. Since 2015: 1,20,634 applications, 1,17,954 approved (to 1 Jan 2025).
- **Single Desk integrates all departments on one platform** → investors need not approach each department (A and R true, R explains A).
- **National:** **e-Biz** portal (Ministry of Commerce, **28 Jan 2013**) = **single window for approach to government services** (G2B). PARIVESH 3.0 = environmental clearances; E-Samudra = maritime administration.
- BRAP (DPIIT) reform areas include the **single window system**.

=== AP Industrial Base, Investments and Rankings
# Profile
- **Endowments:** coastline **1,053.07 km – 3rd longest** (after Gujarat and Tamil Nadu; revised Dec 2025, earlier "974 km, 2nd"); **six operational ports**, minerals, farm base, surplus power. A national surplus of coal is **not** an AP strength.
- **Manufacturing ≈ 11% of GSDP** (APSES 2024-25); manufacturing share **22.11% in 1987-88** (combined AP); large and medium industries grew **18.83%** a year in 1957-2006.
- **Rank:** AP **7th in industrial output** (RBI Handbook of Statistics on Indian States 2020-21).
- **Corporate investment (RBI study, Sept 2011, for 2010-11):** **Chhattisgarh 1st, AP 2nd**, Gujarat 3rd.
- **Sunrise sector** identified by AP: **agro-food processing**.
- After bifurcation the MSME profile shifted to **capital-intensive** units. APEDB: AP = **5.8%** of India's exports (2020-21).
- National frame (2026): IIP base **2022-23**; core index **nine** industries (**iron ore** added), weight **32.88%**.

# Major units
- **Visakhapatnam Steel Plant (RINL):** India's **first shore-based** integrated steel plant; **USSR** collaboration; dedicated **1 August 1992**.
- **Pharma:** Jawaharlal Nehru Pharma City, **Parawada** (Anakapalli) – bulk drugs/APIs. **Cement:** **26** operational plants (2014-15), in **YSR Kadapa, Kurnool, Guntur**.
- **Minerals:** **Mangampet** (YSR Kadapa) = world's single largest **barytes** deposit, mined by **APMDC**; Aug 2026: NGRI found **lithium and gallium** at **Tummalapalle**.
- **ONGC U-field Onshore Deepwater Block** (KG basin): **Rs 2,917 crore**, dedicated by PM Modi in **Nov 2022**.
- **Employment in large and mega projects:** highest in **Visakhapatnam** district (G2 2017).

=== MSME Policy 2015-20, Sectoral Policies and MSME (4.0)
# MSME Policy 2015-20
- Targets: new jobs for **at least 3.5 lakh people** and new investment of **at least Rs 15,000 crore by 2020**; MSME Revival Fund **Rs 100 crore** (2015-16).
- **25% rebate in land cost, limited to Rs 10 lakh**, in industrial estates/parks.
- AP Budget 2017-18: **Rs 126 crore** for rejuvenation of MSME units.
- **M-Parks Policy:** **200 MSME parks by 2023**, about **30,000 MSMEs**, **Rs 45,000 crore** investment.
# Sectoral policies
- **Aerospace and Defence Manufacturing Policy 2015-20:** at least **5,000 highly skilled jobs** and **Rs 20,000 crore** investment by 2020, plus 4 anchor units – two separate targets (A and R both true, R does not explain A).
- **Textile, Apparel and Garments Policy 2018-23:** capital subsidy caps – **100% of FCI** (garmenting, made-ups, integrated, processing, technical textiles), **75%** (weaving, knitting), **50%** (spinning, ginning) → "all of the above"; policy never got operational guidelines and was replaced by (4.0) 2024-29.
- **Textile (4.0) 2024-29:** 2 lakh+ jobs. **Food Processing (4.0):** Large Rs 50-100 crore, **Mega above Rs 100 crore**. **Electronics (4.0):** clusters at Kopparthy, Tirupati, **Sri City**. **Tourism (Mar 2026):** 5-10% FCI subsidy up to Rs 40 crore.
# MSME & Entrepreneur Development Policy (4.0) 2024-29
- **G.O.Ms.69**; target **Rs 50,000 crore**; **175+ MSME parks – one per assembly constituency**; **Rs 500 crore** corpus.
| Capital subsidy | General | Special (women, SC/ST/BC, minority, PwD) |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | 25%, cap Rs 25 lakh | 45%, cap Rs 45 lakh |
| Small | 25%, cap Rs 1.5 cr | **45%, cap Rs 4.5 cr** |
| Medium | 25%, cap Rs 7 cr | 35%, cap Rs 7 cr |
- **Profile (IDP 4.0 baseline):** about **9 lakh** Udyam MSMEs (Feb 2024; **3.23%** of India); **79% services**; MSMEs ≈ **33.5% of GSDP**. "**28** exclusive MSME parks" is **not** the policy figure.
| MSME limits (investment / turnover) | 2020 | From 1 April 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | Rs 1 cr / Rs 5 cr | Rs 2.5 cr / Rs 10 cr |
| Small | Rs 10 cr / Rs 50 cr | **Rs 25 cr** / Rs 100 cr |
| Medium | Rs 50 cr / Rs 250 cr | **Rs 125 cr / Rs 500 cr** |
- **India:** MSMEs ≈ **30% of GDP, 46% of exports**; women own about **20%**; Stand Up India and MUDRA (Tarun) for women entrepreneurs.
- **Credit and relief:** **APIIC-Union Bank** MoU (**July 2022**) – loans in **39 industrial parks**; **ReSTART (May 2020)** – COVID relief, mainly cleared **incentive arrears**; **MSME Samadhaan** – delayed payments, **45-day** rule.
- **Clusters:** MSE-CDP (SIA = APIIC); AP Cluster Development Programme 2026-30 – **45 clusters**, up to **80%** of cost. **ODOP:** **99** products in 26 districts (Raja Rani dolls – Annamayya).
- **Large and mega units (APSES 2024-25):** **1,074** operational, Rs 3.01 lakh crore, **5.33 lakh** jobs; SIPB FY25: **8** projects, Rs 2,45,275 crore. Jun 2026: Cabinet ratified 87 SIPB projects, **Rs 34,000 crore**.
