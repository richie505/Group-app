=== Amaravati Capital – APCRDA, Foundation and Building
# APCRDA and the capital city
- **APCRDA** = AP Capital Region Development Authority, under the **APCRDA Act, 2014** (authority set up **2014**); HQ **Rayapudi**.
- **Amaravati** = a **greenfield capital** on the **Krishna**, between Vijayawada and Guntur, in **Thullur** mandal; "India's first smart capital"; **water-centric**, 51% green/blue space.
| Unit | Area |
|---|---|
| **Capital city** (notified) | **217.23 sq km**, 29 villages |
| Capital region | 8,352.69 sq km (expanded Aug 2026, from 7,068.20) |
- **Master plan:** **Surbana Jurong (Singapore)**. **Amaravati Development Partner** = Singapore consortium (**Ascendas-Singbridge, Sembcorp**) with **Amaravati Development Corporation** – **no Japanese** partner.
- **Land pooling:** farmers sent to **Singapore** to learn best practice; Phase I **93.1% of 37,921 acres**, **70,550** returnable plots; LPS 2.0 about 16,666 acres in 7 villages (Apr 2026); **6,212.50 acres** for the **Inner Ring Road** (Sep 2026).
- **"My Brick – My Amaravati"** (2015): donors bought e-bricks at **Rs 10 per brick**.
- **Interim Legislative building** (Velagapudi): completed in **192 days** (AP Budget 2017-18).
- **Justice City:** India's first, planned in Amaravati (High Court, judicial academy, legal institutions).
- **Works and funds:** 96 works, ₹57,592.71 crore; World Bank + ADB ₹15,000 crore; HUDCO ₹11,000 crore; Amaravati Sangamam at Sakhamuru.

=== Urban Bodies, Smart Cities and Urban Schemes
# Development authorities and corporations
| Body | Fact |
|---|---|
| VUDA → **VMRDA** | Visakhapatnam (Urban → Metropolitan Region) Development Authority |
| TUDA | Tirupati Urban Development Authority |
| GUDA (2017) | Rajamahendravaram–Kakinada |
| **Special Development Authority** (2011) | For the **petroleum** sector (PCPIR, Visakhapatnam-Kakinada) |
| **AP Urban Greening and Beautification Corporation** (2015) | Greening and beautifying cities under the **Swachh Andhra** mission |
- **1996 urban reform:** urban land used as a resource by **streamlining the levy of development charges**.
- **CLAP** = **Clean Andhra Pradesh** (Jagananna Swachh Sankalpam); "OWMS – Online water management services" is not the expansion.
# Smart Cities Mission
- AP's **4 smart cities**: **Visakhapatnam, Kakinada** (first round, Jan 2016), **Tirupati**, **Amaravati** (third round, June 2017); not Vijayawada or Guntur.
- AP Budget **2022-23: Rs 1,000 crore** for the Smart Cities Mission.
# Urbanisation and schemes
- **Census 2011** (residuary AP): urban **29.5%** (India 31.2%); most urbanised district **Visakhapatnam**, then Krishna; largest UA **Visakhapatnam**.
- **AMRUT** (water, sewerage): 1.0 – 174 projects; **2.0 – 506 projects**. PMAY-U 2.0 = pucca houses. YSR Jagananna Colonies under **Navaratnalu – Pedalandariki Illu**.
- **City Economic Regions (2026):** three – Visakhapatnam (10 districts, NITI Aayog pilot, US$750-800 bn by 2047), Amaravati, Tirupati. Criticism: land-pooling farmers' grievances.
