=== Four Labour Codes and Labour Laws
- **29** central laws → **4 Codes**; sections 1,232 → 480. Labour = **Concurrent List**.
- In force **21 November 2025**; draft rules **Dec 2025**; Central Rules May 2026.
| Code | Laws | Key laws / provisions |
|---|---|---|
| Wages, 2019 | 4 | Minimum Wages 1948, Payment of Wages 1936, Payment of Bonus 1965, Equal Remuneration 1976; universal minimum wage; Centre's national floor wage; no gender discrimination; 8-hour day |
| Industrial Relations, 2020 | 3 | Trade Unions 1926, Standing Orders 1946, Industrial Disputes 1947; lay-off permission 100 → 300 |
| Social Security, 2020 | 9 | EPF, ESI, Employees' Compensation; 26-week maternity; gig workers |
| OSH, 2020 | 13 | Factories Act, Contract Labour Act 1970 |
- Contract Labour Act → **OSH Code**, NOT Wages Code (4 laws, not 29).
- **National floor wage:** **Anoop Satpathy Committee (2019)** recommended a national minimum wage of **₹375/day**
- **Occupier (Factories Act 1948, Sec 2(n)):** person with **ultimate control** over a factory; in a company a **director** must be named
- **2018 Standing Orders Rules**: **fixed-term employment, all sectors**; no termination notice for temporary workmen.
- Gains (ES 2025-26): formalisation 60.4% → 75.5%; unions: anti-worker.
| Law | Year |
|---|---|
| Industrial Disputes Act | **1947** |
| Minimum Wages Act | **1948** |
| Factories Act | **1948** |
- **Wages (Code on Wages, Sec. 2(y))** include **basic pay, dearness allowance, retaining allowance**; **exclude overtime allowance**, HRA, bonus.
- UPPSC 2021: Code on Wages merged **Minimum Wages, Payment of Bonus, Equal Remuneration** (+ Payment of Wages) – **not Contract Labour** → 'I, II and IV'.

=== Reform Generations and the Reform Agenda
| First (1991-2000) | Second (2000-01 on) | Third (Tenth Plan) |
|---|---|---|
| Private-sector promotion (**de-licensing**, de-reservation, MRTP limits abolished), **tariff cuts** and QR removal, public-sector reform, external sector, financial sector, tax reforms | **Factor-market** reforms (APM dismantling – petroleum, sugar, fertiliser, drugs), **public-sector** reforms, **reforms in government and public institutions**, **legal-sector** reforms, critical areas (infrastructure, agriculture, health, education) | Fully functional **PRIs** |
- **'Legal system reforms' ARE a second-generation head** (Ramesh Singh); 'oil sector reforms' is not a separate head (UPPSC 2009 key said legal – disputed).
- **'Greater rigour in labour laws'** is NOT an economic reform (reforms = SEZs, less SSI reservation, removal of entry barriers).
- **CII 10-point agenda** for economic revival (June 2012; again 2013).
- Factor markets: **land** - DILRMP, ULPIN, NGDRS; **labour** - four Codes; **capital** - IBC, capital-market reform. Next-generation pillars start with land, labour.

=== GST as Structural Reform
- **101st Amendment Act, 2016** (122nd Bill); in force **1 July 2017**; **Kelkar** Committee; **Canadian dual model**; **Assam** ratified first.
| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| 246A | Power to levy GST |
| 269A | IGST on inter-State supply |
| 279A | GST Council |
| 366(12A) | Definition |
| 286 | No State tax on supplies outside State / import-export |
- Destination-based; input tax credit ends cascading; subsumed **State VAT**, Central excise, service tax, CST.
- **GST 2.0** (Aug 2025): rate rationalisation, **40% slab**: sin/luxury goods.
- **EoDB gain:** one tax replaced many Central and State indirect taxes; ended cascading and **checkpost delays** → better **Ease of Doing Business**.

=== IBC 2016
- CIRP **180 days** + one **90-day** extension; outer limit **330 days** (2019 amendment). **NCLT** adjudicates; **IBBI** regulates.
- **Objectives:** consolidate insolvency laws for **companies, partnership firms and individuals**; resolution in a **time-bound manner** for **maximisation of value of assets**; promote **entrepreneurship and availability of credit**; balance the interests of all stakeholders; set up the **IBBI**.
