=== Levy, Collection and Appropriation of Taxes
| Article | Levied by | Collected / kept by |
|---|---|---|
| 268 | Union (stamp duties, excise on **medicinal and toilet preparations**) | Collected and kept by **States** |
| 269 | Union (inter-State sale/consignment of goods) | Collected by Union, **assigned to States** |
| 269A | Union (GST on inter-State trade) | Apportioned as Parliament provides on GST Council advice |
| 270 | Union (all other Union List taxes) | **Shared** with States as the Finance Commission recommends |
| 271 | Union (**surcharge**) | Wholly for the **Union** |
- **Corporation tax** (Union List Entry 85): **before the 80th Amendment (2000)** it belonged **exclusively to the Union**; now it is in the shared (divisible) pool like income tax and customs.
- **Taxes on transactions in stock exchanges and futures markets** (Union List): **levied and collected by the Union** – not collected by the States.
- **State taxes**: **agricultural income tax** (State List **Entry 46**) – only States can tax agriculture; **State excise on alcoholic liquor** (a **State** revenue); entertainment tax; **taxes on professions/trades ("business tax")** (Art. 276, ceiling **₹2,500**).
- **Petroleum products** are outside GST, so States still levy and can **raise VAT/sales tax** on them; telephone services and income tax are Union subjects.
> Exam angle: who levies / collects / keeps – corporation tax, STT-type taxes, agricultural income, alcohol excise, petroleum VAT.

=== Grants – Article 275 vs Article 282

|---|---|---|
| Nature | Statutory | Discretionary |
| Recommended by | **Finance Commission** | Executive (formerly Planning Commission) |
| Charged on | Consolidated Fund of India | Voted |

=== Divisible Pool, Cesses and Surcharges
- **Divisible pool** = all Union taxes **except cesses and surcharges** (and Art. 268/269 taxes), net of collection charges – set up by the **80th Amendment (2000)** following the 10th FC.
- Cesses and surcharges per ₹100 of Central tax: **₹13.5 (2021-22, decade high)** → ₹11 (2025-26 est.).
- Headline devolution **41%**; effective only **30-32%** of gross tax revenue.
- **16th FC "grand bargain"**: States take a **smaller share of a larger pool** if most cesses merge into regular taxes.
~~ Sources: Ramesh Singh Indian Economy 10th ed. Part 5 p.215; app notes 3:98

=== Vertical Fiscal Imbalance
- Cause: the Union holds the buoyant bases (**corporation tax, income tax, most of GST**) while States carry heavy spending.
- Worsened by **cesses/surcharges outside the pool**, loss of indirect taxes to GST, CSS conditions, weak State revenue. Transfers 2026-27: ₹25.44 lakh crore.
