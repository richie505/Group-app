=== Human Development and the HDR – Origin, UNDP, Concept and Pillars
- **UNDP** was the first to focus on the **human aspect of development**: first **Human Development Report (HDR), 1990**, which contained the first **HDI**; HDRs released most years since.
- The HDR **compares countries on educational levels, health status and per capita income** ("Human Development Report" – not a population, education or quality report).
- **Mahbub ul Haq** introduced the concept and **created the HDI (1990)**; **Amartya Sen** worked with him on the first HDRs (team incl. Inge Kaul, Paul Streeten, Frances Stewart). Sen = **capability approach**; **Martha Nussbaum** extended it with a list of central capabilities.
- Human development = enlarging people's **choices, freedoms and opportunities**; people, not growth alone, are the criteria; income is a **means**, not the end.
- **Four pillars of human development:** **Equity, Sustainability, Productivity, Empowerment** (CDS 2020) – not "inclusion" or "gender".
| Approach to human development | Idea |
|---|---|
| Income | Higher income = higher human development (oldest) |
| Welfare | People as passive beneficiaries of welfare spending |
| **Minimum / basic needs** (ILO) | Health, education, food, water, sanitation, housing – India's **Minimum Needs Programme** (5th Plan) = **investment in human (resources) approach** |
| Capabilities | Amartya Sen |
- **HDR 2016** ("Human Development for Everyone"): **universalism** is the key to human development.
- UNDP identifies two categories of poverty: **income poverty** and **human poverty** – both.
- **Same GNI, different HDI** shows the role of **national policy choices** (Malaysia richer than Chile, but Chile ~7 years more life expectancy and 3 more years of expected schooling).
| Basis | GDP / per capita income | Human development |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Primarily **economic** measure | Broader, people-centred |
| Dimensions | One-dimensional | Multidimensional |
- **Countries covered:** HDR 2018 update **189**; HDR 2021/22 191; HDR 2023-24 and 2025 **193**.

=== HDI Dimensions, Indicators and Methodology
| Dimension | Indicator | Min | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long and healthy life | **Life expectancy at birth** | 20 | 85 |
| Knowledge | **Expected years of schooling** | 0 | 18 |
| Knowledge | **Mean years of schooling** (adults 25+) | 0 | 15 |
| Decent standard of living | **GNI per capita (PPP $)**, log | $100 | $75,000 |
- HDI = composite of **life expectancy, educational attainment and per capita income**; base = **health, education, standard of living**. Before 2010 the income indicator was per capita **GDP**.
- **NOT in the HDI:** **ownership of basic assets**, **access to banking**, **employment**, poverty, nutritional status, quality of life, environment, urbanisation, human security, gender, inequality (that is the **IHDI**).
- Method: dimension index = (actual − min)/(max − min); education index = **arithmetic mean** of its two sub-indices; income uses the **logarithm** (diminishing returns); **HDI = geometric mean** of the three indices (**since 2010**), equal weights.
- **PPP dollars** allow fair comparison across price levels.
| Category | HDI value |
|---|---|
| Very high | 0.800 and above |
| High | 0.700–0.799 |
| **Medium** | **0.550–0.699** |
| Low | below 0.550 |
- India **0.685** (HDR 2025, for 2023) = **Medium**, just below 0.700.

- HDR 2025: Sri Lanka and Maldives above India; Bangladesh tied at 130; India **lowest among the five original BRICS**.

=== Human Development in Indian States – NHDR and State HDRs
| Report | Year | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **First State HDR** | **1995** | **Madhya Pradesh**; released by **Amartya Sen** in Delhi; HDI for the State and all districts |
| **First NHDR** | **April 2001** | **Planning Commission**; **Kerala** top, Punjab 2nd, UP 13th, Bihar 15th (descending: Kerala, Punjab, UP, Bihar) |
| Second NHDR (India Human Development Report) | 2011 | Planning Commission / IAMR; Kerala 1st |
| First AP HDR | 2007 (released 2008) | **CESS, Hyderabad**; sponsored by Planning Commission + UNDP |
- The Indian Human Development Report gives, for each sample village, **education, health and infrastructure-and-amenities** indices – **no unemployment index**.
- **Kerala** consistently tops ("Kerala Model"); Bihar, UP, MP at the bottom (Bihar 0.650 vs India 0.685, 2023).
- **HDI and SDG ranking** of States are **positively related** because SDG targets rest largely on **education and health** (UPPSC 2019: A and R true, R explains A).
~~ Sources: Planning Commission NHDR 2001 (planningcommission.nic.in); UNDP India, MP HDR 1995; app notes 3:16 sub 8
