=== New Current Affairs: 5–14 Nov 2026
# Defence exercises
- **IMNEX-26 (AP angle):** **8th India–Myanmar Naval Exercise** at **Visakhapatnam**, **10–14 Nov 2026**; Myanmar Navy ship **UMS King Sin Phyu Shin**; Indian ships **INS Rana** and **INS Sumedha**; opening onboard INS Rana; Harbour and Sea phases; aligned with **MAHASAGAR**.
- **RAHAT MITRA 2026:** **first** India–Myanmar **bilateral Tri-Services HADR** exercise, **Chennai**, **5–6 Nov 2026**; theme "**Together for Humanity, Stronger through Partnership**"; **300+ personnel**; under the aegis of the **Indian Navy**; linked to **Neighbourhood First** and MAHASAGAR.
- **Tiger Triumph 2026 concluded (AP angle):** the **5th** India–US tri-service **HADR** exercise closed at **Visakhapatnam** on **5 Nov 2026** (PIB: held **31 Oct–5 Nov**); phases **STAFFEX** and **Tabletop Exercise (TTX)**; stress on **combined amphibious operations**; first edition **2019**.
- **Naseem Al Bahr ("Sea Breeze"):** **15th** edition of the **India–Oman** naval exercise; **INS Surat** and Oman's **Al-Mu'azzar**; harbour phase on **EOD, mine countermeasures, hydrography**; sea phase **VBSS** and cross-deck drills. *(news 9–11 Nov 2026)*
- **Mitra Shakti 2026:** **12th** India–**Sri Lanka** Army exercise, **13–26 Nov 2026**, **Army Training School, Maduru Oya**; **170**-member Indian contingent mainly from the **Rajputana Rifles** (+ IAF); **counter-terrorism in a semi-urban setting** under a **UN mandate**; annual, venue alternates between the two countries.
- **Sea Vigil-26:** **5th** pan-India **coastal defence** exercise coordinated by the **Indian Navy**, **11–19 Nov 2026**: **CDSRE** phase (11–17 Nov) + **Sea phase** (18–19 Nov); whole coastline and **EEZ**, all coastal States/UTs; with Coast Guard, Coastal Security Police, Customs; conceived in **2018** after **26/11**; precursor to the Navy's **TROPEX**.
- **CARAT Bangladesh 2026** (**US–Bangladesh** naval exercise, **2–6 Nov 2026**): US mine-countermeasures ship **USS Warrior (MCM-10)** at **Chattogram**. (**Tiger Lightning** = Bangladesh Army–US Army Pacific exercise.)
- **Coalition of the Willing – Exercise Koalicja (Poland):** first drill of the **UK–France-led** coalition for Ukraine, about **2,800 soldiers from 11 countries** in south-eastern **Poland**; coalition formed **March 2025**, **35 nations**; its military arm **Multinational Force–Ukraine (MNF-U)** set up **January 2026**, joint HQ in **Paris** (to rotate to the UK); **not a NATO or peacekeeping force**. *(news 10 Nov 2026)*
# Defence deals
- **INS Betwa refit (11 Nov 2026):** MoD–**Cochin Shipyard Ltd** contract of **₹498.58 crore** for **Normal Refit and Re-powering** of the **Brahmaputra-class frigate INS Betwa** – **steam propulsion replaced by diesel engines**, upgraded weapons; **50+ MSMEs**; signed in the presence of Defence Secretary **Rajesh Kumar Singh**.
# Visits and diplomacy
- **PM Modi to Manila, Philippines (16–17 Nov 2026)** at the invitation of President **Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr.**: **23rd ASEAN–India Summit** and **21st East Asia Summit** on **17 Nov**; the **Philippines = ASEAN Chair 2026**; **2026 = ASEAN–India Year of Maritime Cooperation**; **2027** = **35 years of Dialogue Relations** and 5 years of the **Comprehensive Strategic Partnership**; **ASEAN–India Plan of Action 2026–2030** (adopted at the 22nd Summit, Kuala Lumpur, 26 Oct 2025).
- **India–Guyana:** EAM **S. Jaishankar** met President **Irfaan Ali** on the sidelines of **CHOGM 2026 (Antigua and Barbuda)**: a **US$500 million Line of Credit** (roads, health, defence) and three agreements – MSE projects, **Quick Impact Projects**, **Digital Public Infrastructure** (a UPI-like system). *(news 5 Nov 2026)*
- **India–France (Paris, 9–10 Nov 2026):** Foreign Secretary **Vikram Misri** and **Martin Briens** co-chaired the **3rd India–France Strategic Space Dialogue** and the **inaugural Economic Security and Critical Technologies Dialogue** (set up during PM Modi's France visit, June 2026); reviewed the **Horizon 2047 Roadmap**; aim to **double bilateral trade in five years**.
- **India–Egypt:** Commerce Minister **Piyush Goyal** in Egypt **15–16 Nov 2026**; **7th Joint Trade Committee** in **Cairo** (now **ministerial level**) with Egypt's **Mohamed Farid Saleh**; trade **US$5.04 billion in Apr–Sep 2026 (+64%)**; FY 2025-26 exports **US$3.87 bn**, imports **US$2.60 bn**; **SCZONE** (Suez Canal Economic Zone) on the agenda.
- **India–Bhutan (background to the 108 stupas):** **Treaty of Friendship 1949, revised February 2007**; India committed **₹10,000 crore** to Bhutan's **13th Five-Year Plan (July 2024–June 2029)**; five joint hydro projects (**Chukha, Kurichhu, Tala, Mangdechhu, Punatsangchhu-II**) = **3,156 MW**, about **90%** of Bhutan's installed capacity; trade **US$1,744.44 million (2024-25)** ≈ two-thirds of Bhutan's foreign trade.
- **Russia–Ukraine:** PM Modi spoke with **Zelenskyy** (call reported 9 Nov 2026) – India's "**human-centric approach**", dialogue and diplomacy; EAM Jaishankar spoke with **Sergey Lavrov**; Zelenskyy said India was ready to **mediate food corridors**. Ukraine's three deep-water ports near **Odesa** – **Odesa, Chornomorsk, Pivdennyi** – handle about **90%** of its farm exports.
- **Black Sea:** six littoral states – **Bulgaria, Georgia, Romania, Russia, Turkey (Türkiye), Ukraine**; **three are NATO members (Türkiye, Bulgaria, Romania)**, two are NATO partners (Ukraine, Georgia); only **Russia** is unaffiliated.
- **Paraguay:** Congress **impeached President Santiago Peña** after the **Ueno Bank** collapse (placed in resolution by the Central Bank, **3 Nov 2026**); Vice-President **Pedro Alliana** (Colorado Party) sworn in; Paraguay is **landlocked** (Argentina, Brazil, Bolivia), capital **Asunción**.
- **G7 Foreign Ministers – Paris (10–11 Nov 2026):** G7 (informal; formed after the **1973 oil crisis**; no secretariat) – **52nd summit 2026: France**, **53rd 2027: USA**.

Chandrababu Naidu**; deals up to **₹11 lakh crore** expected; held annually since **1995**.
- **South Korean auto-component firms – ₹1,614 crore in AP:** **Hwasin Automotive** (₹850 crore, **Sri Sathya Sai** district, for **Kia**), **Seoyon E-Hwa** (₹514.17 crore, 918 jobs), **Iyamant India** (₹250 crore, **Kurnool**). *(news 3 Nov 2026)*
