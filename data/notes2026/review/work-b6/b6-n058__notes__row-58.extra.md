=== New Current Affairs: 5–14 Nov 2026
# Treaties and bilateral agreements
- **Ganga Water Treaty (India–Bangladesh)** expires on **12 December 2026**: a **30-year** treaty signed on **12 Dec 1996** by PM **H.D. Deve Gowda** and Bangladesh PM **Sheikh Hasina** (her first term); shares the Ganga's dry-season flow at the **Farakka Barrage**; at **70,000 cusecs or less** each side gets **50%**. *(news 4 Nov 2026)*
- **Haj Agreement 2027 (India–Saudi Arabia):** signed in **Jeddah** after the **9 Nov 2026** meeting of Minority Affairs Minister **Kiren Rijiju** and Saudi Minister of Hajj and Umrah **Tawfiq bin Fawzan Al-Rabiah**; India's pilgrim quota **1,75,025**; Rijiju also attended the **6th Hajj Conference and Exhibition** (8 Nov). Haj is one of the **five pillars of Islam** (Shahadah, Salat, Zakat, Sawm, Hajj). *(news 10–11 Nov 2026)*
- **Horizon Europe:** India and the EU **concluded negotiations** for India's **association** with **Horizon Europe**, the EU's main **research and innovation** funding programme (**2021–2027**); about **23 countries** are already associated; three pillars: **Excellent Science** (European Research Council, Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions), **Global Challenges** and **Innovative Europe** (European Innovation Council); proposed successor **2028–2034** with **€175 billion**. *(news 9 Nov 2026)*
- **7th India–EU Strategic Partnership Review Meeting (New Delhi, 9 Nov 2026):** co-chaired by **Sibi George** (Secretary West, MEA) and **Matti Maasikas** (EEAS); reviewed the **Joint Comprehensive Strategic Agenda** of the **16th India–EU Summit (New Delhi, 27 Jan 2026)**; the India–EU **FTA is not yet signed**; also Investment Protection Agreement, GI Agreement, **TTC**, **IMEC**, **Global Gateway**; 8th SPRM in **Brussels** next year.
# Climate diplomacy – COP31 and reports
- **COP31 (UNFCCC):** **Antalya, Türkiye**, **9–20 Nov 2026** – **first COP hosted by Türkiye**; COP31 President **Murat Kurum** (Türkiye's Environment Minister), while **Australia leads the negotiations** (a split presidency); the presidency calls it a "**COP of Implementation**". COP30 = **Belém, Brazil (2025)**.
- **India at COP31:** opening statement by MoEFCC Secretary **Narendra Bhooshan**: equity and **CBDR-RC**, adaptation and **just transition**; public finance from developed countries under **Article 9.1 of the Paris Agreement**; India aligned with **G77 and China, LMDC and BASIC**.
- **UNFCCC basics:** adopted **9 May 1992** (UN HQ, New York), opened at the **Rio Earth Summit (1992)**, in force **21 March 1994**; **198 Parties**; India ratified **1 Nov 1993**.
- **"35-by-35" Global Electrification Pledge:** a **voluntary, non-binding** COP31 initiative of the **Turkish presidency** (introduced at the **Bonn** conference, June 2026): raise electricity's share of global final energy use from about **20–23% to 35% by 2035**; **India has not yet decided** whether to join. (COP28 Dubai pledge: **triple renewables to 11,000 GW by 2030**, double energy-efficiency gains to over 4% a year.)
- **WMO State of the Climate 2026** (released on COP31's opening day): Jan–Sep 2026 average **1.47 °C above 1850–1900**; 2026 to be among the **four warmest years**; **El Niño** strengthening since August 2026; CO₂ **426.8 ppm (2025)**, **53% above 1750 (278 ppm)**; methane **1,948 ppb** (+167%); oceans absorb **~90%** of excess heat; 2026 on track for the **10th straight year of record ocean heat**.
# UN bodies and international organisations
- **MINURSO** (UN Mission for the Referendum in Western Sahara) **ceased operations on 1 Nov 2026** after **35 years** (UN came in with the **1991 ceasefire**): its mandate lapsed when **Russia vetoed** a US-backed renewal that favoured **Morocco's autonomy plan**; a skeleton team is closing **13 sites**. *(news 8 Nov 2026)*
- **UNESCO Memory of the World – Asia-Pacific Regional Register (Nov 2026):** manuscripts of the **Mahabharata (Vyasa)**, **Rajatarangini (Kalhana)** and **Arthashastra (Kautilya)** added; already on it (2024): **Ramcharitmanas (Tulsidas)**, **Panchatantra (Vishnu Sharma)**, **Sahrdayaloka-Locana** (Anandavardhana, Abhinavagupta). The MoW programme was launched in **1992**.
- **First BIMSTEC Health Ministers' Meeting** (**Colombo**, 11 Nov 2026; Senior Officials met 10 Nov): India led by **J.P. Nadda**; ended with a **Joint Ministerial Statement**; India–Sri Lanka MoU for **100 more ambulances** and **50 Health ATMs (medical kiosks)**; India has **1.85 lakh+ Ayushman Arogya Mandirs**.
- **BOB-NOR (Bay of Bengal Network of Ocean Researchers for Fisheries):** launched by **India, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh and the Maldives** at a meeting hosted by **ICAR-CMFRI (Kochi)** – climate-informed fisheries forecasting from satellite, ocean and catch data. **BOBP-IGO** (set up **2003**) has the same four members; Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar and Thailand are cooperating non-contracting parties. *(news 5–6 Nov 2026)*
- **ICAPP (International Conference of Asian Political Parties):** its **13th General Assembly in Seoul** unanimously adopted the **Seoul Declaration** – sovereignty, territorial integrity, respect for the **UN Charter**, concern at unilateral actions; ICAPP launched in **Manila, September 2000**; General Assembly **biennial**; Indian parties (BJP, INC) take part.
- **World Bank International Debt Report 2026:** developing countries' external debt **+5.5%** to a record **US$9.4 trillion (2025)**; they paid **$436 billion more** in debt service than they received in new financing (2022–2025); debt of **IDA**-eligible countries **+8.1% to $1.3 trillion**; **India's external debt $763.5 billion (2025)** vs $717.4 billion (2024), about **+6.4%**.
- **FATF plenary (late Oct 2026)** agreed to step up action on evolving financial-crime threats. FATF: set up **1989 by the G7**; **40 members** (38 jurisdictions + **European Commission** and **GCC**); **India a member since 2010**; secretariat at the **OECD, Paris**; **black list** = Iran, North Korea, Myanmar; India elected **FATF Vice-President for the first time (June 2026)** – **Vivek Aggarwal**, July 2026–June 2027.
- **AP angle – BRICS Sustainable Lifestyle Week (1–7 Nov 2026):** observed in **Andhra Pradesh** under **India's BRICS Presidency (2026)**, with daily themes from **Mission LiFE** (Lifestyle for Environment).
> ⚠ Trap: COP31 is in **Antalya, Türkiye** (not Australia) – Australia only leads the negotiations; the Ganga treaty (1996, 30 years) expires in **Dec 2026**, not 2030; India is **not yet** associated with Horizon Europe – only the negotiations are concluded; MINURSO ended because of a **Russian veto**.
