=== New Current Affairs: 5–14 Nov 2026
# Hockey – Men's Asian Champions Trophy 2026 (Mohali)
- **[05 Nov 2026] India beat Japan 4–1** in the final at the **Olympian Balbir Singh Senior International Hockey Stadium, Mohali (Punjab)**; Japan led from the **3rd minute** (Kazumasa Matsumoto); India scored through **Shilanand Lakra, Dilpreet Singh, Jugraj Singh** (penalty corner) and **Vivek Sagar Prasad**; captain **Harmanpreet Singh**.
- India's **record 6th** Asian Champions Trophy title (2011, 2016, 2018 shared, 2023, 2024, 2026) and a **hat-trick of consecutive titles**; **unbeaten – 7 wins in 7 matches**; beat **South Korea 8–1** in the semi-final; **Malaysia** won **bronze** (beat South Korea); six teams – India, Malaysia, Japan, South Korea, China, Bangladesh.
# Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games (31 Oct – 13 Nov) – India
- **Tanvi Patri** (15, **Odisha**) – **first Indian woman** to reach the **badminton semi-finals** at the YOG (beat Thailand's Kungkaew Kakanik), then won **silver** in girls' singles; earlier only **H.S. Prannoy, Lakshya Sen and Aditya Joshi** (all men) had reached a YOG badminton semi-final.
- **Khushbu Rajakbhai Darjada** – **silver**, girls' **63 kg judo**: the **first Siddi medallist** and the **second Indian judoka** with a YOG medal (after **Thangjam Tababi Devi**, silver, Buenos Aires 2018). **Siddis** – community of **African (East African) origin**, ST in parts of **Gujarat since 1956**; one of Gujarat's **five PVTGs**; also in Karnataka, Maharashtra, Goa, Daman and Diu.
- Other medals (to 11 Nov): **Taniya** – silver, women's **75 kg beach wrestling** (India's **first YOG wrestling medal**); **Dhinidhi Desinghu** – **two bronzes** (100 m and 200 m freestyle; India's first YOG swimming medal); **Tirthank Pegu** – bronze, men's 200 m butterfly; **Rajkumari Lanchenbi Chanu** – bronze, **wushu** (changquan); **Shourya Ambure** – bronze, 100 m hurdles → **0 G, 3 S, 5 B = 8 medals**.
- Boxer **Gunjan** reached the **women's 48 kg final** (beat Lily Bassett, GB, 4–1; final vs **Bouthaina Bensalah, Algeria**) – medal assured; result not yet confirmed here.
# Chess – India's 100th Grandmaster
- **[08 Nov 2026] Anuj Shrivatri** (22, **Katni, Madhya Pradesh**) became **India's 100th GM** by winning the **7th ASEAN Plus Individual Open Chess Championship, Ozamiz City (Philippines)** – the title carried a direct GM title; **first-ever GM from Madhya Pradesh**.
- **99th GM – V.S. Rathanvel** (July 2026); India's **first GM – Viswanathan Anand (1988)**. FIDE awards the GM title for a **2500 rating + three GM norms**.
# Shooting, Badminton and Football
- **ISSF World Championship 2026, Doha (Qatar):** India won the **women's 25 m pistol team gold** (**Esha Singh, Manu Bhaker, Divya Thadigol Subbaraju**, 1761) – India's **second gold** of the event; also **men's 50 m rifle 3 positions team bronze** (Aishwary Pratap Singh Tomar, Akhil Sheoran, Niraj Kumar) and **women's 10 m air pistol team silver** (report of 11 Nov 2026).
- **[08 Nov 2026] Korea Open 2026 (BWF Super 500):** women's singles – **An Se-young** (South Korea, world No. 1) beat **Akane Yamaguchi** (Japan) – her **11th** title of 2026; men's singles – **Victor Lai (Canada)**. Korea Open: BWF Super Series 2007, **Super 500** from 2018.
- **Hylo Open (Saarbrücken):** Sindhu beat **Line Hojmark Kjaersfeldt (Denmark)** in the final; the Hylo Open was formerly the **BMW Badminton Cup**, held since **1988**.
- **[05 Nov 2026] Cristiano Ronaldo** (41) **retired from international football** after **23 years** with Portugal – debut **2003**, **234 caps**, **146 goals** (both **world records** for men's internationals); won **Euro 2016** (captain) and the **UEFA Nations League 2019 and 2025**; club **Al Nassr** (Saudi Arabia).
# Andhra Pradesh Angle
- **5th Asian Para Games (Aichi-Nagoya):** India's **97 para-athletes** won **114 medals (30 G, 35 S, 49 B)**; **PM Modi felicitated** the contingent (7 Nov 2026). The Games evolved from the **FESPIC Games (1975)**.
- **AP medallists:** **Valasanaini Ravanni** (19, visually impaired, tribal hamlet near **Paderu, Alluri Sitharama Raju district**) – **two bronzes** (women's **400 m and 1,500 m**); **Ravi Karthik Budigina** (**Jangareddygudem, Eluru**) – **silver in men's 100 m freestyle swimming** – **India's first swimming medal** at the Games – plus two more medals; **Ravi Rongali** (Anakapalli) – bronze, **shot put**; **M.V. Murali Krishna** (Visakhapatnam) – coach of the para-badminton team.
- **Inaugural Commonwealth Kabaddi Championship 2026** was held in **Ahmedabad** – **13 men's and 10 women's teams**; captains **Surjeet Singh** (men) and **Nidhi Sharma** (women).
- **AISSNG 2026** (All India Sainik Schools National Games), **Bijapur (Karnataka)**, by the **Sainik Schools Society (MoD)**: **Sainik School Amaravatinagar** won the junior boys' **volleyball**.
- **K. Sri Sarayu** (VP Siddhartha Public School, **Vijayawada**) – **silver**, U-17 sports aerobics, **CBSE National Aerobics Championship 2026–27, Pune** (27–31 Oct).
