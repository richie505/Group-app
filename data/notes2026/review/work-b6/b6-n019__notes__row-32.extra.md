=== New Current Affairs: 5–14 Nov 2026
# CPI inflation - October 2026 (released 12 Nov 2026)
- **CPI inflation, Oct 2026: 6.09%** (provisional; **NSO, MoSPI**, **12 Nov 2026**; base **2024=100**) - **above the RBI's 6% upper tolerance limit**; first breach of the band in the new series.
- **Rural 6.63%**, **urban 5.41%**; **Sep 2026 (final): 5.54%** (rural 6.08%, urban 4.88%).
- **Food inflation (CFPI): 8.94%** (rural **9.09%**, urban **8.68%**), up from **7.74%** in Sep.
- Divisions: **Food and beverages 8.51%** (weight **36.75%**); highest - **Personal care, social protection and misc. 10.14%**, **Restaurants and accommodation 10.06%**; lowest - **Health 2.05%**.
- Items: highest **Onion 116.22%**, Garlic 60.74%, Silver jewellery 52.39%; lowest **Potato -14.24%**, Cauliflower -10.57%.

# Cabinet decisions - 11 Nov 2026
- **Stand-Up India extended** for **5 years, to 31 March 2031**, with a **₹4,111 crore credit guarantee corpus**; aim - at least **2.5 lakh more entrepreneurs**.
  - For **SC, ST and women** entrepreneurs setting up **greenfield** enterprises: bank loans **above ₹10 lakh up to ₹1 crore** (up to **85% of project cost**); **at least one SC/ST and one woman borrower per bank branch**.
  - Guarantee **85% of the loan**; guarantee fee **0.5%**; launched **April 2016**; over **2.75 lakh** loans so far.
- **Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU)** continued to **31 March 2031** with a **₹5,000 crore corpus** - to back about **₹12.6 lakh crore of PM MUDRA Yojana loans** over five years (16th Finance Commission period).
  - Guarantee fee cut **1% → 0.75%**; trustee **NCGTC**; CGFMU launched **January 2016**; PMMY: **60.36 crore loans worth ₹43.34 lakh crore** sanctioned (Sep 2026).
- **Nutrient Based Subsidy (NBS), Rabi 2026-27**: about **₹62,370 crore** subsidy on **P&K fertilisers** (DAP, NPKS) for **1 Oct 2026 - 31 Mar 2027**; **DAP stays at ₹1,350 per 50 kg bag**.
> ⚠ Trap: Stand-Up India loans are **₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore** (MUDRA loans are smaller, up to ₹20 lakh); CGFMU backs **MUDRA**, not Stand-Up India.
# Industry, schemes and companies in news
- **[9 Nov 2026] Semicon 2.0 web portal** launched by **Ashwini Vaishnaw** (MeitY). Semicon 2.0: Cabinet approval **15 July 2026**, outlay **₹1,27,500 crore**, **six pillars** - chip design; machines and materials; more fabs; ATMP/OSAT; R&D; talent.
  - **India Semiconductor Mission 1.0**: approved **December 2021**, **₹76,000 crore**, up to **50%** fiscal support; **10 projects, ₹1.60 lakh crore, 6 States** (Dec 2025).
- **[9 Nov 2026] Chemicals sector**: India **6th-largest chemicals producer in the world, 4th in Asia**; **2nd-largest** maker/exporter of **dyes**; **3rd-largest exporter of agrochemicals**; ~USD 220 bn now.
  - **BHAVYA Rasayan** (Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojana - Rasayan, July 2026): **three chemical parks**, each **≥ 8 sq km (2,000 acres)**; Centre up to **₹1,000 crore per park** (State min **₹500 crore**); outlay **₹3,030 crore**.
- **[11 Nov 2026] PLI for Medical Devices (PLI-MD)**: fresh applications invited; scheme of **2020-21**, **extended to 2035-36**, ~**₹2,700 crore** available; incentive **5% of incremental sales**; **Department of Pharmaceuticals**.
- **[12 Nov 2026] Kutch Copper Ltd** (Adani Enterprises) - **first Indian copper producer** to get **Veramark Assurance** (formerly **Copper Mark**) ESG certification.
- **[9 Nov 2026] National Entrepreneurship Day** - **MSDE** set up **9 Nov 2014**; **2.62 lakh DPIIT-recognised start-ups** (Nov 2026), **25 lakh+ jobs**, **1.07 lakh** with a woman director; India **3rd-largest** start-up ecosystem; **SISFS** corpus **₹945 crore** (2021); **Fund of Funds for Startups** managed by **SIDBI**.
# Jobs, debt and data reports
- **[11 Nov 2026] ICRIER "The Participation Paradox"** (PLFS + Time Use Survey): working women **104 million → 201 million** (2017-18 to 2025); **female WPR 22% → 38%**; a **rural woman earns ~45%** of a rural man's pay (**53%** earlier); **Time Use Survey 2024**: women spend **3.7 hours more per day** on domestic work.
- **[2 Nov 2026] Decent work**: unemployment rate **6.1% (2017-18) → 3.2% (2023-24)**; **~90% of the workforce informal** (**India Employment Report 2024, ILO-IHD**); decent work = **SDG 8**.
- **[7 Nov 2026] World Bank International Debt Report 2026**: developing countries' external debt **+5.5% to a record USD 9.4 trillion (2025)**; **India's external debt USD 763.5 billion (2025)** vs USD 717.4 billion (2024), **~6.4%** growth.
> ⚠ Trap: the record **USD 9.4 trillion** is all developing countries' external debt; India's is **USD 763.5 billion**.
