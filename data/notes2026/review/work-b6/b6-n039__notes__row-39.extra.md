=== New Current Affairs: 5–14 Nov 2026
# Andhra Pradesh – schemes and initiatives
- **Vidya Vahini** portal: launched by **APSCHE** on **National Education Day, 11 November 2026** (Maulana Azad's birth anniversary); built under the **APSCHE LMS–MOOCs** initiative.
- Vidya Vahini first courses: **Introduction to AI**, **Applications of AI** and **Quantum Technology** – each **four-credit**, six-quadrant, **40 modules**.
- **APSCHE**: statutory body, est. **20 May 1988** under the **APSCHE Act, 1988**, on the **NPE 1986** recommendation; the **first State Council of Higher Education** of its kind in India.
- **Lift irrigation revival** (Nov 2026): CM Naidu ordered restoration of **789 defunct lift irrigation schemes** at **₹632.07 crore** before the next Kharif; **Jalaharathi Corporation** to monitor and maintain them.
- **Mana Mitra**: AP's **WhatsApp governance** platform (number **95523 00009**), launched **30 January 2025** by **Nara Lokesh** at Undavalli; **161 services** at launch, **200 by March 2025**; the **Centre praised it in cyclone management** (Cyclone Murasu, Nov 2026).
- **MSME Net-Zero roadmap**: AP **MSME Development Corporation** with the **Union MSME Ministry** and **EESL** (Ministry of Power); covers HT-connected units above **100 kVA**; launched at the MSME Growth Enclave, Vijayawada (25 May 2026).
# Social justice, defence and fisheries
- **Vayo Samman Rashtriya Abhiyan** (Nov 2026): **National Intergenerational Bonding Campaign** launched by **Dr Virendra Kumar**, **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment**, under the **Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY, 2021)**.
- Vayo Samman uses the **'5C' model** – **Connect, Converse, Co-create, Contribute, Continue**.
- **Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana** (a component of AVYAY): **1 April 2017**, **Central Sector**; assisted-living devices for senior citizens with age-related disabilities.
- **Agnipath change** (Nov 2026): the **75% of Agniveers** not retained after **4 years** get an **extra 2 years** and a chance to join the regular forces; Seva Nidhi during the first four years is **50:50** (Agniveer:Government); for exits after the extra two years the Government pays **70%** of the corpus.
- **Soreng (Sikkim)** = India's **first notified Organic Fisheries Cluster under PMMSY**; Fisheries Minister **Rajiv Ranjan Singh** also launched an **SOP for organic certification of fisheries in the North-East** and projects of ₹3.75 crore (Biofloc, RAS).
- **Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan** (MoSJE, **15 August 2020**, flagship of **NAPDDR**): reached **35.84 crore people** (14.33 crore youth, 11.32 crore women) as on 10 Nov 2026.
- **Punjab 'Pendu Khed Maidan'**: 1,000 more model village sports grounds as an anti-drug step – **1,764** done against a target of **3,100**.
# Union Cabinet, 4 November 2026
- **Fast Track Special Courts (FTSC)**: **Centrally Sponsored Scheme** continued for **five years, 1 April 2026 – 31 March 2031**; began **October 2019**; **Ministry of Law and Justice**; time-bound trial of **rape and POCSO Act, 2012** cases.
- FTSC outlay **₹3,788.04 crore** = Central share **₹2,353.15 crore** (from the **Nirbhaya Fund**) + State/UT share ₹1,434.89 crore; **Nirbhaya Fund**: 2013, **non-lapsable**, Ministry of Finance.
- **VGF for grid-scale energy storage**: **₹14,500 crore**, FY 2026-27 to 2030-31, for **92 GWh** = **60 GWh pumped hydro + 30 GWh battery (BESS) + 2 GWh other technologies**; BESS must have **40% domestic content**; investment expected ~₹97,600 crore.
- **VISTAR** = **Viksit Bharat Initiative for Statistical Transformation, Analytics & Research**: **Central Sector Scheme**, **₹4,371.10 crore**, 2026-27 to 2030-31, **MoSPI**; national surveys **43 → 59**; funds the **8th Economic Census**.
# Union Cabinet, 11 November 2026
- **Stand-Up India** continued till **31 March 2031**, with a **credit guarantee corpus of ₹4,111 crore**; target **2.5 lakh more** entrepreneurs; over **2.75 lakh** loans since **April 2016**.
- Stand-Up India: bank loans **above ₹10 lakh up to ₹1 crore** (up to 85% of project cost) for a **greenfield** enterprise – at least **one SC/ST and one woman borrower per bank branch**; credit guarantee **85%**, fee **0.50%**; launched by the **Ministry of Finance (2016)**.
- Same meeting: **CGFMU** (Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units) continued till 31 March 2031 (corpus **₹5,000 crore**); **P&K fertiliser subsidy** for **Rabi 2026-27**: **₹62,370 crore**.
- **Yuge Yugeen Bharat Museum**: **₹3,230 crore**, in the **North and South Block** complex, New Delhi – planned as the museum with the **world's largest display area**; announced by PM on **18 May 2023**; Ministry of Culture.
# Electronics, pharma and solar schemes
- **Semicon 2.0** (India Semiconductor Mission 2.0): Cabinet **15 July 2026**, **MeitY**, **₹1,27,500 crore**, **six pillars** (chip design; machines and materials; more fabs; ATMP/OSAT; R&D; talent); web application portal launched by **Ashwini Vaishnaw** (Nov 2026).
- **ISM 1.0**: Cabinet **December 2021**, **₹76,000 crore**, fiscal support **up to 50%**; 10 projects worth ₹1.60 lakh crore in 6 States (Dec 2025).
- **PLI for Medical Devices (PLI-MD)**: fresh applications invited (Nov 2026); launched **2020-21**, **extended up to 2035-36**; **Department of Pharmaceuticals** (Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers); about **₹2,700 crore** available; incentive **5% of incremental sales**.
- **PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana** (**February 2024**, **MNRE**): rooftop solar for **1 crore households by FY 2026-27**; subsidy ₹30,000–60,000 (1–2 kW), ₹60,000–78,000 (2–3 kW), **₹78,000 max (above 3 kW)**; pace to reach **1 lakh rooftops every 5 days** (Nov 2026).
- **Model Solar Village** (component of PM Surya Ghar): **one per district**; a revenue village with population **above 5,000** (2,000 in special category States).
> ⚠ Trap: Stand-Up India = SC/ST **and** women, loans **₹10 lakh–₹1 crore** (not MUDRA's up to ₹20 lakh); FTSC central share comes from the **Nirbhaya Fund**; VISTAR is under **MoSPI**, Semicon 2.0 under **MeitY**.
