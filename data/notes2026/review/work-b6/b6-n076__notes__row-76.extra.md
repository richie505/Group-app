=== New Current Affairs: 5–14 Nov 2026
# COP31 Opens in Antalya (9 Nov 2026)
- **[09 Nov 2026] COP31 opened** at the expo centre in **Antalya, Türkiye** (runs to **20 Nov**); **COP31 President: Murat Kurum**, Türkiye's **Minister of Environment**; Türkiye hosts the COP **for the first time**; **Australia** leads the negotiations (**Chris Bowen**, "President of negotiations"); the hosts call it the "**implementation COP**"; **COP30** was at **Belém, Brazil (2025)**.
- **[10 Nov 2026] India's opening statement** was delivered by **Narendra Bhooshan, Secretary, MoEFCC** (Minister **Bhupender Yadav** joins in the second week): India upheld **equity** and **CBDR-RC** (Common But Differentiated Responsibilities and Respective Capabilities), called the **UNFCCC and Paris Agreement** "the most inclusive platforms", and said **public finance from developed countries** remains central for developing countries and adaptation.
- **35-by-35 Global Electrification Pledge:** introduced by the **Turkish COP31 Presidency** at the **Bonn Climate Conference (June 2026)**; aims to raise electricity's share of **global final energy consumption** from about **20–23%** to **35% by 2035**; **voluntary, non-binding**; **India had not yet decided** to join (4 Nov); compare the **COP28 (Dubai)** pledge to **triple renewable capacity to 11,000 GW** and **double energy-efficiency gains (2% → 4%/yr) by 2030**.
- **UNFCCC:** adopted **9 May 1992** (New York; opened at the Rio Earth Summit), in force **21 March 1994**; **198 Parties**; **India ratified 1 November 1993**.
# WMO State of the Climate 2026 (9 Nov 2026)
- Released by the **WMO** on the **opening day of COP31**: global mean temperature **Jan–Sep 2026 = 1.47°C** above the **1850–1900** average; 2026 likely **among the four warmest years** on record (above 2025 and 2023, slightly below **2024**).
- A strong **El Niño** has been intensifying since **August 2026** and may become **one of the strongest on record by December 2026**.
- **CO2 426.8 ppm (2025)**, **53% above** 278 ppm of **1750**; **methane 1,948 ppb**, **167% above** 1750 (729 ppb); oceans absorb about **90% of excess heat**; 2026 on track for the **tenth consecutive year of record ocean warmth**.
# Global Carbon Budget 2026 (9–10 Nov 2026)
- **21st** annual **Global Carbon Budget** of the **Global Carbon Project** (140+ scientists; preprint in *Earth System Science Data*): **fossil CO2 emissions 38.2 billion tonnes in 2026, down 0.4%** (oil −1.9%, gas −0.3%, **coal +0.6%**); **total CO2** incl. land-use **43.3 Gt** (42.8 Gt in 2025); land-use emissions **5.1 Gt**.
- **Countries (fossil CO2, 2026):** **China −0.9%** (about **32%** of the world total), **USA −1.3%**, **EU −1.5%**, **India +4.4%** (above its decade average of 3.5%).
- **Remaining carbon budget for 1.5°C: about 150 Gt CO2** – **less than four years** of current emissions; atmospheric **CO2 427.7 ppm in 2026**, about **54% above** pre-industrial.
# Antarctic Ozone Hole and Cyclones
- **[07 Nov 2026] The 2026 Antarctic ozone hole is the 7th largest on record:** it forms in the **stratosphere**; the **polar vortex** isolates cold Antarctic air; **polar stratospheric clouds** provide surfaces for ozone-destroying reactions.
- **[06 Nov 2026] Cyclone Arnab attribution study (AP):** by **Imperial College London's Grantham Institute** using the **Imperial College Storm Model (IRIS)**: human-induced climate change raised Arnab's **economic damage by about 33%** (about **25%** of the damage attributable); **max wind +5%**, **eyewall rainfall +11%**; the likelihood of such a cyclone rose **19%**; present climate taken as **~1.3°C** above pre-industrial.
- **Arnab's landfall:** crossed on **28 Oct 2026** near **Narsapur** (about **20 km ENE of Narsapur**, **40 km SW of Yanam**), peak sustained winds up to **202 kmph**.
- **[02 Nov 2026] Cyclone Murasu:** a deep depression over the **Southwest Bay of Bengal** became Cyclonic Storm **Murasu on 1 Nov 2026**, forecast to cross between **Nellore and Narsapur** (AP); its rain damaged the **Polavaram Right Main Canal** lining (Eluru district). Cyclones need **SST above 27°C** and a **Coriolis force**, so they **do not form near the equator**.
- **[10 Nov 2026] Rayalaseema drought (Kharif 2026):** **Kurnool** worst hit (**1.85 lakh farmers**, **1.68 lakh ha**), then Nandyal (1.64 lakh ha) and YSR Kadapa (1.49 lakh ha); **meteorological drought** = rainfall **25% or more below normal**; other types: agricultural, hydrological, socio-economic.
# Species, Protected Areas and Days
- **[03 Nov 2026] International Day for Biosphere Reserves – 3 November:** proclaimed by **UNESCO in November 2021**, first observed **3 Nov 2022** (end of **50 years of the MAB Programme**); India has **18 notified Biosphere Reserves**, of which **13** are in UNESCO's **World Network of Biosphere Reserves**; India = one of **17 megadiverse countries**, **7–8% of species** on **2.4% of land**.
- **[03 Nov 2026] *Gegeneophis chandgadi*:** a new **caecilian** (**legless amphibian**), family **Grandisoniidae**, found at **Buzawade village, Chandgad taluka, Kolhapur (Maharashtra)**; caecilians live underground and are mistaken for earthworms or blind snakes; India has **458 amphibian** species incl. **41 caecilians**; genus *Gegeneophis* has **12** species (**11 endemic to the Western Ghats**).
- **[03 Nov 2026] Cheetah strays into Rajasthan:** a **Botswana-born male** from **Kuno National Park (MP)** crossed into the **Baran forests, Rajasthan**; MP's cheetah sites: **Kuno** (first), **Gandhi Sagar WLS (Mandsaur)**, **Veerangana Durgavati Tiger Reserve**; African cheetah **IUCN Vulnerable**, **Asiatic cheetah (Iran) Critically Endangered**; in **CAMI (2014)**; fastest land mammal (**~103 km/h**).
- **[04 Nov 2026] Asian Openbill Storks at Punyakshetram (AP):** thousands arrived late (linked to **El Niño**) at **Punyakshetram village, Rajanagaram mandal, East Godavari** – a traditional nesting site; family **Ciconiidae**; feeds mainly on **freshwater snails**; **IUCN Least Concern**; **Schedule II, Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972**.
- **[06 Nov 2026] BOB-NOR (Bay of Bengal Ocean Research Network):** launched by **India, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh and the Maldives** at a meeting hosted by **ICAR-CMFRI**; climate-informed fisheries management (sea temperature, monsoon, salinity, productivity); **BOBP-IGO** (est. **2003**) members: **Bangladesh, India, Maldives, Sri Lanka**.
- **[03 Nov 2026] BRICS Sustainable Lifestyle Week, 1–7 Nov 2026:** observed in **Andhra Pradesh** under **India's BRICS Presidency (2026)**, around the **seven themes of Mission LiFE** (healthy lifestyles, saving energy, conserving water, sustainable food, reducing single-use plastic, e-waste, general waste).
- **[04 Nov 2026] Green firecrackers (CSIR-NEERI):** emit about **30% less particulate matter** (or 20% less PM + 10% less gases); three types – **SWAS** (Safe Water Releaser), **STAR** (Safe Thermite Cracker), **SAFAL** (Safe Minimal Aluminium); the **Supreme Court** allowed them in Delhi for two days at Deepavali.
