=== Industrial Revolution and Imperialism – Background
- **Industrial Revolution:** began in **Britain (England)** in the late **18th century** – the world's first industrial nation (coal and cotton textiles first, railways next); spread later to France, Germany, the USA and Russia.
- **Manufacturing systems → workers:** **factory** system – **operatives**; **guild** system – **apprentices**; **domestic** system – **manual workers** (family craftsmen); **putting-out** system – **business entrepreneurs** (merchant-capitalists).
- **"The White Man's Burden" (1899):** poem by **Rudyard Kipling** (author of *The Jungle Book*) – the imperial idea that Europeans had a duty to "civilise" colonised peoples.
> Industrial Revolution in Britain = ruin of Indian handicrafts (sub-section on deindustrialisation).
