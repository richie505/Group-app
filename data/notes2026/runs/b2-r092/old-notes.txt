## SUB 1 | Prevention of Corruption Act 1988
[92:1:0] - Origin: Santhanam Committee's push for tougher bribery laws → consolidated as **PCA 1988**.
[92:1:1] - **Public servant (Sec 2(c))**: any government or **local-authority** employee, any **Judge**, anyone whose office requires a **public duty** – not only permanent Union employees.
[92:1:2] # PC (Amendment) Act 2018
[92:1:3] TABLE head: Change | Detail
  [92:1:3.0] Purpose | Align with UNCAC
  [92:1:3.1] Bribe-giving | Made a direct offence, up to 7 years; a coerced bribe-giver is protected if he reports within 7 days (not 30)
  [92:1:3.2] Commercial organisations | Liable for bribes offered on their behalf; offence framed around "undue advantage"
  [92:1:3.3] Sec 17A | Government's prior approval before inquiry/investigation into official decisions
  [92:1:3.4] Punishment | Bribe-taking public servant: 3 to 7 years + fine
  [92:1:3.5] Trial | Within 2 years, extendable to max 4 years
## SUB 2 | Sanction for Prosecution
[92:2:0] TABLE head: Stage | Provision
  [92:2:0.0] Before inquiry/investigation | Sec 17A PCA – government approval
  [92:2:0.1] Before court takes cognizance | Sec 19 PCA – prior sanction; covers retired public servants since 2018
  [92:2:0.2] General protection | Sec 218 BNSS (earlier Sec 197 CrPC; BNSS from 1 July 2024)
[92:2:1] - **Vineet Narain (1997)** and **Subramanian Swamy (2014)** struck down prior-sanction rules.
[92:2:2] - **CPIL v UoI (Jan 2026)**: **split verdict** on 17A – **Justice Nagarathna** struck it down (Art. 14); **Justice Viswanathan** upheld it if approval rests on a binding **Lokpal/Lokayukta** opinion; pending before a larger Bench.
[92:2:3] - Debate: "sanction paralysis" vs protecting honest officers from a "play-it-safe syndrome".
## SUB 3 | Whistle Blowers Protection Act 2014
[92:3:0] - **Whistle-blower**: **any public servant or other person, including an NGO**, making a **public interest disclosure** to the Competent Authority (APPSC 2019 key) on corruption, wilful misuse of power or a criminal offence.
[92:3:1] TABLE head: Complained against | Competent authority
  [92:3:1.0] Central employees | CVC
  [92:3:1.1] State employees | State Vigilance Commission
  [92:3:1.2] Union Minister | Prime Minister
  [92:3:1.3] State Minister | Chief Minister
  [92:3:1.4] MPs | Rajya Sabha Chairman / Lok Sabha Speaker
  [92:3:1.5] MLAs | Presiding officer of the House
[92:3:2] - **Identity** given to the authority and kept **confidential**; protection against **victimisation**.
[92:3:3] - Complaint within **7 years**; **no anonymous** complaints.
[92:3:4] - Penalties: revealing identity = up to **3 years + ₹50,000**; knowingly false complaint = up to **2 years + ₹30,000**.
[92:3:5] - **SPG** excluded from the rules.
[92:3:6] - The Act exists but is **not operationalised**.
[92:3:7] The Bill passed the Lok Sabha in **2011**, but the Act is of **2014**.
[92:3:8] # Amendment Bill 2015
[92:3:9] - Passed by **Lok Sabha, May 2015**; would bar disclosures in **RTI Sec 8(1)-type** categories (sovereignty, security, Official Secrets, Cabinet papers).
[92:3:10] - **Lapsed in 2019** with the 16th Lok Sabha.

## SUB 5 | Lokpal and Lokayuktas
[92:5:0] # Lokpal (2013 Act)
[92:5:1] - **Chairperson + up to 8 members**; **50% judicial**; at least 50% from **SC, ST, OBC, minorities, women**.
[92:5:2] - **Selection committee**: **PM** (chair), **Speaker**, **LoP**, **CJI** or nominated SC judge, an **eminent jurist**; appointed by the **President**.
[92:5:3] - **First Lokpal**: **Justice Pinaki Chandra Ghose** (March 2019); **Justice A.M. Khanwilkar** chairperson from **Feb 2024**.
[92:5:4] - The Act obliged every State to set up a **Lokayukta within one year**.
[92:5:5] # AP Lokayukta
[92:5:6] - **AP Lokayukta and Upa-Lokayukta Act, 1983**; Lokayukta = **higher functionaries**, Upa-Lokayukta = the rest.
[92:5:7] - **2024 amendment**: retired HC Chief Justice or Judge, on recommendation of a **selection committee chaired by the CM**; term **5 years**.
[92:5:8] - **25 Aug 2026**: **Justice T. Mallikarjuna Rao** sworn in; oath by Governor **S. Abdul Nazeer**.
