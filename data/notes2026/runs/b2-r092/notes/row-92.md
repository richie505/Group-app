=== Money Laundering, Benami Property and Offshore Leaks
# PMLA 2002
- **Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002**: India's principal anti-money-laundering law; **in force 1 July 2005** (with its Rules).
- **Section 3:** money laundering = concealing, possessing, acquiring, using, projecting or claiming **"proceeds of crime"** as untainted property; punishment **3 to 7 years** RI (up to 10 in some cases) + fine.
- **Director of the Enforcement Directorate** can **attach property** believed to be proceeds of crime (APPSC Group 1).
- Machinery: **Adjudicating Authority**, **Appellate Tribunal** and **Special Courts** for the trial of money-laundering offences.
- **Schedule:** **Part A** = listed offences (IPC/BNS, NDPS, etc.) with no money limit; Part B = Customs false declaration; **Part C = offences with cross-border implications** (so "Part A = trans-border crimes" is **wrong**).
- **"Proceeds of crime" definition (Sec 2(1)(u)) amended by the Finance Act, 2015** (w.e.f. 14 May 2015): added property taken or held **outside the country** → equivalent value within India (APPSC AEE 2016); widened again in **2018** and **2019** (Explanation).
- **Vijay Madanlal Choudhary (2022):** SC upheld the main PMLA provisions; ED works under the **Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance** (ED set up 1956).
# Benami and fugitive offenders
| Law | Key point |
|---|---|
| Benami Transactions (Prohibition) Act, 1988 | **Sec 3(2): up to 3 years' imprisonment, or fine, or both** for entering into a benami transaction |
| Benami (Amendment) Act, 2016 | Prohibition of Benami Property Transactions Act; bans proxy holdings; **confiscation** + RI **1 to 7 years** (not ten) |
| Fugitive Economic Offenders Act, 2018 | Warrant + offence of **₹100 crore** or more + flight from India; **Special Court** (on ED application) may confiscate **even before conviction**; e.g. Vijay Mallya, Nirav Modi |
- **Ganpati Dealcom (23 Aug 2022):** SC held **Sec 3(2)** (3-year punishment) **unconstitutional** and the 2016 Act **prospective** (APSLPRB SI 2023). **⚠ Recalled in Oct 2024** when the SC allowed the Union's review petition.

=== UN Conventions against Corruption and Organised Crime
| Feature | UNCAC | UNTOC (Palermo) |
|---|---|---|
| Adopted | **2003** (Merida) | 2000 |
| Signature | **only legally binding universal anti-corruption instrument** | Organised crime, money laundering, extradition |
| Special chapter | **Chapter V – asset recovery** (return of assets to rightful owners) | — |

=== Prevention of Corruption Act and Sanction for Prosecution
- **PCA 1988**: consolidated anti-bribery law after the **Santhanam Committee**'s push.
- **Public servant (Sec 2(c))**: any government or **local-authority** employee, any **Judge**, anyone whose office requires a **public duty** – not only permanent Union employees.
# PC (Amendment) Act 2018
| Change | Detail |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Align with **UNCAC** |
| Bribe-giving | Made a direct offence, up to 7 years; a coerced bribe-giver is protected if he reports within **7 days** (not 30) |
| Commercial organisations | Liable for bribes offered on their behalf; offence framed around "undue advantage" |
| Punishment | Bribe-taking public servant: **3 to 7 years** + fine |
| Trial | Within **2 years**, extendable to max **4 years** |
# Sanction stages
| Stage | Provision |
|---|---|
| Before inquiry/investigation | **Sec 17A PCA** – government approval (official decisions) |
| Before court takes cognizance | **Sec 19 PCA** – prior sanction; covers retired public servants since 2018 |
| General protection | **Sec 218 BNSS** (earlier Sec 197 CrPC; BNSS from 1 July 2024) |
- **Subramanian Swamy v Manmohan Singh (31 Jan 2012)**: SC upheld a **private citizen's right to seek sanction** for prosecuting a public servant (A. Raja, 2G) and faulted delay by the PMO (APPSC JAO/AE).
- **Vineet Narain (1997)** (Single Directive) and **Subramanian Swamy v Director CBI (2014)** (Sec 6A DSPE) struck down prior-sanction rules.
- **CPIL v UoI (Jan 2026)**: **split verdict** on 17A – **Justice Nagarathna** struck it down (Art. 14); **Justice Viswanathan** upheld it with approval vested in an independent body (**Lokpal/Lokayukta**); pending before a larger Bench.

=== Whistle Blowers Protection Act 2014
- **Whistle-blower**: **any public servant or other person, including an NGO**, making a **public interest disclosure** on corruption, wilful misuse of power or a criminal offence to the **Competent Authority** (CVC by default).
| Complained against | Competent authority |
|---|---|
| Central employees | **CVC** |
| State employees | State Vigilance Commission |
| Union Minister | **Prime Minister** |
| State Minister | Chief Minister |
| MPs | Rajya Sabha Chairman / Lok Sabha Speaker |
| MLAs | Presiding officer of the House |
- **Identity** kept **confidential**; protection against **victimisation**; **no anonymous** complaints; complaint within **7 years**.
- Penalties: revealing identity = up to **3 years + ₹50,000**; knowingly false complaint = up to **2 years + ₹30,000**.
- **SPG** excluded. Bill passed the Lok Sabha in 2011; **Act of 2014**, still **not brought into force** (CVC uses the 2004 PIDPI resolution).
- **Amendment Bill 2015** (Lok Sabha, May 2015) would bar disclosures in **RTI Sec 8(1)-type** categories; **lapsed in 2019** with the 16th Lok Sabha.

=== Lokpal and Lokayuktas
- **Lokpal (2013 Act)**: **Chairperson + up to 8 members**; **50% judicial**; at least 50% from **SC, ST, OBC, minorities, women**.
- **Selection committee**: **PM** (chair), **Speaker**, **LoP**, **CJI** or nominated SC judge, an **eminent jurist** (not the Cabinet Secretary).
- **First Lokpal**: **Justice Pinaki Chandra Ghose** (March 2019); **Justice A.M. Khanwilkar** chairperson from **Feb 2024**.
- The Act obliged every State to set up a **Lokayukta within one year**.
- **AP Lokayukta and Upa-Lokayukta Act, 1983**; Lokayukta = **higher functionaries**, Upa-Lokayukta = the rest; **2024 amendment**: retired HC CJ or Judge, chosen by a committee **chaired by the CM**, term **5 years**.
- **Aug 2026**: **Justice Tallapragada Mallikarjuna Rao** selected and sworn in (25 Aug) as AP Lokayukta; oath by Governor **S. Abdul Nazeer**.
