## SUB 4 | E-governance – Meaning, Models and Types of Interaction
[4:0] # Meaning and significance
[4:1] - E-governance = use of ICT to deliver government services, information and transactions; aim **"faceless, paperless, cashless"**.
[4:2] - Significance: **citizen engagement**, better **service delivery**, fewer face-to-face contacts, **audit trails** (e-governance **strengthens** them – "removal of audit trails" is not a benefit).
[4:3] # Arie Halachmi – five models
[4:4] TABLE head: Model | Meaning
  [4:4.0] Broadcasting / wider dissemination | Government information put in the public domain
  [4:4.1] Critical flow | Critical information to a targeted audience (e.g. corruption data → ombudsman)
  [4:4.2] Comparative analysis | Learning from best practices elsewhere
  [4:4.3] E-advocacy / mobilisation and lobbying | Online civil-society networks influence decisions
  [4:4.4] Interactive service | Two-way G2C services (filing, payments, feedback)
[4:5] # Types of interaction
[4:6] TABLE head: Type | Meaning | Examples
  [4:6.0] G2C | Government to Citizen | Online certificates; UMANG, MeeSeva, eSeva
  [4:6.1] G2B | Government to Business | MCA portal, GSTN, e-Procurement
  [4:6.2] G2G | Between departments/governments | NIC solutions, SWAN, e-Office
  [4:6.3] G2E | Government to Employees | HR and administrative tools

## SUB 5 | NeGP, e-Kranti and Digital India
[5:0] # NeGP (2006)
[5:1] - Formulated by **DeitY and DARPG**; approved **18 May 2006** with **27 Mission Mode Projects** and 10 components.
[5:2] - **2011**: 4 MMPs added – **Health, Education, PDS, Posts** → **31**.
[5:3] - Core infrastructure: **SWAN**, **State Data Centres**, **CSCs** (UMANG is not core NeGP infrastructure).
[5:4] - MMPs: **Central, State, Integrated**.
[5:5] # e-Kranti (NeGP 2.0)
[5:6] - Approved by the Union Cabinet **25 March 2015**; a **pillar of Digital India**; vision **"Transforming e-Governance for Transforming Governance"**; **44 MMPs** = **15 Central, 17 State, 12 Integrated**.
[5:7] # Digital India
[5:8] - Launched **1 July 2015**; ministry **MeitY**; **nine pillars**: Broadband Highways; Universal Access to Mobile Connectivity; Public Internet Access Programme; e-Governance; **e-Kranti**; Information for All; Electronics Manufacturing; IT for Jobs; Early Harvest Programmes (a satellite launch programme is not a pillar).
