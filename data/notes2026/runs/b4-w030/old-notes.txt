## SUB 4 | Watershed Management and Water Schemes
[30:4:0] - **Watershed development** = soil and moisture conservation of a whole catchment: **earthworks, soil conservation and tree planting** to conserve moisture and raise groundwater recharge – its hallmark in semi-arid India (UPSC 1994).
[30:4:1] - **IWMP** (2009-10): run by the **Department of Land Resources** (MoRD); **merged DPAP, DDP and IWDP**; key objective – **agricultural productivity and water conservation**.
[30:4:2] - IWMP/IWDP benefits: **prevents soil run-off, rainwater harvesting, groundwater recharge, regenerates vegetation** – NOT linking perennial and seasonal rivers.
[30:4:3] - **DPAP and DDP** = Department of Land Resources (MoRD), not Agriculture or Environment; **NWDPRA** = **Ministry of Agriculture**, not Rural Development (so none of the UPSC 2014 pairs is correct).
[30:4:4] - IWMP merged into **PMKSY (2015)** as the **Watershed Development Component**; CAD&WM is a PMKSY component.
[30:4:5] - **Hermann Bacher** (Swiss priest, WOTR) – **Father of community-led watershed development** in India.
[30:4:6] - **Neeranchal** – World Bank-assisted watershed project; **Watershed Development Fund** – NABARD.
[30:4:7] TABLE head: Scheme | Key facts
  [30:4:7.0] Hariyali (27 Jan 2003) | Vajpayee; Centrally sponsored watershed and wasteland development through Panchayati Raj Institutions – for the rural population, not urban
  [30:4:7.1] PMKSY (2015-16) | AIBP = faster completion of major/medium projects; Har Khet Ko Pani = CAD&WM, minor irrigation, RRR of water bodies
  [30:4:7.2] Jal Shakti Abhiyan (2019) | Water conservation and water security campaign; Phase 1: 1 Jul–15 Sep 2019; Phase 2: 1 Oct–30 Nov 2019 for states of the NE (retreating) monsoon – AP, Karnataka, Puducherry, TN
  [30:4:7.3] Atal Bhujal Yojana | First community-led CSS on demand-side groundwater management
  [30:4:7.4] Namami Gange (2014); NRCP (1995) | Ganga main stem; other rivers

## SUB 5 | Forest Resources and Forest Movements
[30:5:0] - **Forests are renewable**; that they improve the environment is true but does not explain why they are renewable.
[30:5:1] - **Forest dieback:** a stand of trees losing health and dying **without an obvious cause**.
[30:5:2] - **FAO (Global Forest Resources Assessment 2010):** net forest loss fell from **8.3 million ha/yr (1990s)** to **5.2 million ha/yr (2000–2010)**.
[30:5:3] - Highest share of area under forest among China, India, Indonesia, Japan: **Japan** (about two-thirds).
[30:5:4] TABLE head: Movement | Facts
  [30:5:4.0] Chipko | Himalayas (Garhwal, 1973)
  [30:5:4.1] Appiko | Karnataka – Salkani forest, Sirsi, Uttara Kannada, 8 Sep 1983; modelled on Chipko; aims – save the forests of the Western Ghats, restore greenery to denuded areas, rational use of forest resources (NOT cleaning rivers)
[30:5:5] - **NFP 1988:** 33% of the country under forest, **two-thirds in hills**; formally introduced JFM (Centre's guidelines 1990; forerunner **Arabari, West Bengal, 1972**).
[30:5:6] - **Forest Rights Act 2006:** Gram Sabhas manage community forest resources. **National Agroforestry Policy 2014** – India's first.
[30:5:7] - Terms: silviculture (raising and tending trees), agroforestry (trees with crops or livestock), jhum (NE shifting cultivation; shorter fallow → degradation and erosion).
[30:5:8] - **ISFR 2023:** largest forest area – **Madhya Pradesh** (77,073 sq km).
[30:5:9] - World: **Amazon** – largest continuous rainforest (Congo second); **taiga** – largest terrestrial biome; Bhutan – Constitution keeps ≥ 60% under forest; Canada – largest softwood and pulp exporter; dense forests lie near the equator.

[30:5:12] - **Form quotient** = ratio of a tree's **mid-diameter** to its **diameter at breast height** (dbh, 1.37 m) – a forest-mensuration term.

[30:7:3] # Land Use
[30:7:4] - **Nine-fold classification:** forests; non-agricultural uses; barren and unculturable; permanent pastures; miscellaneous tree crops; culturable wasteland; current fallow; other fallow; net sown area ("seasonal plantation reserve" is not one).
[30:7:5] - Fallow ladder: current fallow ≤ 1 yr; other fallow 1–5 yrs; culturable wasteland > 5 yrs. Highest net-sown share: Punjab and Haryana.
[30:7:6] - **India's land use (approx.):** net sown area **~46%**, forests **~23%**, other areas ~31% (2022-23: 45.9 / 23.5 / 30.6).
[30:7:7] - **Trends (NCERT):** forest, non-agricultural use, current fallow and net sown area have **increased**; **barren and wasteland, pastures and tree crops**, culturable waste and other fallow have **declined**; non-agricultural use has risen fastest. Forest area has **not** declined.
[30:7:8] - AP (undivided, 2006-07): current fallow about **6.02 lakh ha** (printed key; figure not verified).
[30:7:9] - **Wasteland:** degraded land restorable with reasonable effort; mapped by satellite (IRS) National Wastelands Monitoring.
[30:7:10] - Land degradation: erosion, waterlogging, salinisation, nutrient loss, falling water tables; UNCCD (**1994**) – desertification in arid, semi-arid and dry sub-humid areas.
[30:7:11] - Milestones: Club of Rome (Apr 1968), Limits to Growth (1972), Brundtland "Our Common Future" (1987).

[30:7:14] - A **resource** must have **utility** and be technologically accessible, economically feasible and culturally acceptable – **quantity is not** a necessary characteristic.

  [30:9:0.1] 2015 | Auction mandatory; District Mineral Foundation (Sec 9B); NMET
  [30:9:0.2] 2023 | Centre auctions critical and strategic minerals (lithium); exploration licence
  [30:9:0.3] 2026 (14 Aug) | States may tax mineral rights / lands only within limits set by the Centre
[30:9:1] - **DMF:** non-profit trust in every mining district; purpose – **protect the interests and benefit of persons and areas affected by mining** (it neither promotes exploration nor issues licences); funded by miners (30% of royalty for old leases, 10% for auctioned leases), not the Consolidated Fund; runs PMKKKY.
[30:9:2] - **MADA v. SAIL (2024, nine judges):** royalty is not a tax; States tax mineral rights (Entry 50) and mineral land (Entry 49).
[30:9:3] - India: diamonds – Panna (MP); chromite – Sukinda (Odisha); gold – Jonnagiri (Kurnool, AP); Peninsular plateau = mineral heartland.
[30:9:4] - **Deep sea:** India first to sign an **ISA** contract for **polymetallic sulphides** on the **Carlsberg Ridge** (2025); sulphides – Cu, Zn, Pb, Fe, Ag, Au; nodules – Mn, Co, Ni, Cu. ISA – Jamaica; ITLOS – Hamburg.
