=== Countries – Area, Borders, Coastlines and Landlocked States
# Largest Countries by Area
- **Order:** 1. **Russia** 2. **Canada** 3. **USA** 4. China 5. Brazil 6. Australia 7. **India** (about **2.4%** of world land; first by population). So "Russia, Canada, USA, China" is the correct descending order.
- **USA vs China:** by total area (land + water) the **USA is third**, China fourth – the answer most books and keys give; by land area alone China is slightly larger.
- **France** (and Argentina) are smaller than India. Ascending: **Argentina (27.96 lakh sq km) < India (32.87 lakh) < Australia (76.9 lakh) < Brazil (85.1 lakh)**.
- **Largest country without land borders:** **Australia**; among Japan, Philippines, New Zealand, Cuba it is **Japan**.
# Borders
- **Canada–USA:** **longest international border** (**8,891 km**, incl. Alaska–Canada); much of it follows the **49th Parallel**. Next: Russia–Kazakhstan (6,846 km), Chile–Argentina (5,308 km, along the Andes).
- **Most land neighbours:** **Russia** and **China**, **14 each**; India 7, longest border with **Bangladesh**.
- **Israel:** **Lebanon, Syria, Jordan, Egypt** (and the West Bank, Gaza).
- **Myanmar** (besides India, China): **Bangladesh, Laos, Thailand** – not Vietnam, Cambodia or Malaysia.
- **Afghanistan:** **Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan** in the north, China, Pakistan, Iran (and India's PoK claim) – **not Kyrgyzstan, Azerbaijan or Russia**.
- **Ukraine:** Belarus, Russia, Moldova, **Romania, Hungary**, Slovakia, Poland – not Bulgaria, Czech Republic, Latvia, Lithuania.
- **Lithuania:** Latvia, Belarus, Poland, Russia (Kaliningrad) – **not Ukraine**.
- **Panama:** **Costa Rica, Colombia**, Caribbean Sea and **Pacific** – **not Venezuela**.
- **Indonesia** land borders: **Malaysia** (Borneo), **Timor-Leste**, **Papua New Guinea** – **not Brunei** (Brunei touches only Malaysia).
- **Zambia:** DR Congo, Tanzania, Malawi, Mozambique, Zimbabwe, Namibia, Angola and a **~150 m border with Botswana at Kazungula** (books treat Kazungula as a "quadripoint" and say Zambia does not border Botswana).
- **Namibia** (capital Windhoek): Angola, Zambia, Botswana, South Africa and the Atlantic – not Zimbabwe.
- **Luxembourg:** landlocked, the only sovereign Grand Duchy; borders **Belgium, Germany, France**.
- **Germany–Poland boundary:** strictly the **Oder–Neisse Line** (1945; recognised 1990); GK books list it as the "**Hindenburg Line**" (really a WWI German defence line in France).
# Coastlines
- **Longest coastline:** **Canada** (~2,02,080 km, Arctic archipelago, three oceans), then Norway, Indonesia.
- **Pacific coast in South America:** **Chile, Peru, Ecuador, Colombia** – **not Venezuela** (Caribbean side).
# Landlocked Countries
| Region | Landlocked | Trap (not landlocked) |
|---|---|---|
| South America | **Bolivia, Paraguay** | Peru, Suriname, Uruguay |
| Africa | **Chad**, Mali, Niger, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Botswana, Lesotho … (16) | Angola, Kenya, Senegal |
| Europe | **Hungary**, **Switzerland**, Austria, Luxembourg, Czech Rep., Serbia … (14) | Belgium, Romania, Ukraine |
| Asia | **Afghanistan**, Nepal, Bhutan, **Laos** (only one in SE Asia), Mongolia, Central Asian states, Armenia, Azerbaijan | Thailand, Malaysia, Cambodia |
- **North America has no landlocked country.**
- **Doubly landlocked:** **Uzbekistan** and Liechtenstein.
- **Azerbaijan** touches only the Caspian (a lake), so it is landlocked strictly; books and keys still single it out as "not landlocked" against Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Uzbekistan.
# Other Country Facts
- **Alaska:** part of the **USA**, bought from the Russian Empire (treaty **30 March 1867**).
- **Galapagos Islands:** volcanic, part of **Ecuador** (not Peru).
- **Tropic of Capricorn:** Brazil, Paraguay, Argentina, Chile, Australia, Namibia, Botswana, South Africa, Mozambique, Madagascar – **NOT Bolivia**.
- **Puerto Rico** (US territory): **most densely populated** among Latin American options.
- **Great Britain** = England, Wales, Scotland (**not Northern Ireland**; UK = GB + Northern Ireland); **England ~54%** of the UK's area (less than 60%).

=== Continents – Area, Order, Elevation and Antarctica
# Continents by Area
- Descending: **Asia > Africa > North America > South America > Antarctica > Europe > Australia**.
| Continent | Area (sq km) | Share of land | Countries |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Asia** | **44.57 million (~44 million; largest)** | **29.8%** | 48–49 |
| **Africa** | 30.07 million | **20.3%** | **54 (most)** |
| North America | 24.47 million | **16.2%** | 23 |
| South America | 17.84 million | **11.8%** | 12 |
| Antarctica | ~14 million | 9.2% | – |
| Europe | 9.95 million | **6.8%** | 49–51 |
| Australia / Oceania | ~8.5 million | 5.9% | 14 |
- Two smallest: **Australia** (smallest) and **Europe**; **Antarctica is bigger than both**. Among Europe, Africa, N. and S. America, **Africa** is the biggest.
- **Asia:** largest and **most populous** continent. **Africa:** "**Cradle of Mankind**" (earliest human fossils); has the **largest number of people living in extreme poverty** (about 7 in 10 of the world's extreme poor, 2023).
- **Australia:** maximum **per-capita land**. **Europe:** highest **percentage of plains**.
- **South America:** "**Continent of Birds**"; nearest continent to **Antarctica** (across the Drake Passage).
- **Oceania:** Australia, New Zealand, Papua New Guinea – Melanesia, Micronesia, Polynesia; **Indonesia is part of Asia**.
- **Oceans:** Atlantic separates the Americas from **Europe and Africa** (not Asia); Indian Ocean – Asia, Africa, Australia; Southern Ocean encircles Antarctica.
# Gondwana and Antarctica
- **Gondwana:** South America, Africa, Madagascar, Antarctica, Australia, Arabia, **Peninsular India** – **not North America or Europe** (Laurasia).
- **Antarctica:** **highest mean elevation** (~**2,300 m**, ice sheet over 98%); Asia 923 m, North America 760 m.
- Canada's **RADARSAT-1** made the first detailed, complete map of Antarctica (**1997**).
- **Taylor Valley** (Antarctica): discovered by **Robert Falcon Scott (1903)**, "Valley of the Dead".
- **Indian stations:** **Dakshin Gangotri** (1983, first; abandoned 1990), **Maitri** (1989), **Bharati** (2012, the **third**), run by NCPOR Goa.
> Order-of-area questions repeat (UPPCS ×4, IAS 2005 ×2); Antarctica mean elevation asked twice by UPSC 1995.

=== Longest Ranges, Mountain Types and Highest Peaks
# Longest Ranges
| Rank | Range | Length | Location | Highest peak |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Andes** | **~7,000 km** (to 8,900 km) | South America (7 countries) | Aconcagua 6,961 m |
| 2 | **Rockies** | ~4,800 km | Canada–USA | Mount Elbert 4,401 m |
| 3 | Great Dividing Range | ~3,500 km | **Eastern Australia** (not Africa) | Kosciuszko 2,228 m |
| 4 | Himalayas | ~2,400–2,500 km | India, Nepal, Bhutan, China, Pakistan | Everest 8,849 m |
- **Longest = Andes**; highest = Himalayas. Strict descending order: **Andes > Rockies > Great Dividing Range > Himalayas**.
- **Mountain** = steep elevation above **600 m**; **plains** generally lie below **200 m**.
# Types
| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Young fold (Alpine, Tertiary) | **Himalayas, Alps, Andes, Rockies, Atlas** |
| Old fold / residual | **Appalachians**, Urals, Aravallis, Pennines |
| Block (fault) | **Sierra Nevada**, Vosges, Black Forest |
| Volcanic | Kilimanjaro, Fuji, Etna |
- **Appalachians** are the odd one out among Alps, Andes, Himalayas (old fold). **Aravalli:** oldest fold range of India; highest **Gurushikhar 1,722 m** (Mount Abu).

=== Plateaus
| Type | Nature | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Intermontane** | Enclosed by mountains | **Tibetan**, Pamir, **Bolivian (Altiplano)**, Mongolian, Peru, Colombia |
| **Piedmont** | At the foot of mountains | Piedmont (USA), **Patagonia** |
| Continental | Broad uplifted surface | Peninsular India, African, Arabian, Australian |
| **Volcanic / lava** | Lava accumulation | **Deccan** (one of the oldest), **Columbia** (NW USA) |
| **Fluvial** | River deposition | **Panna, Bhander, Rewa, Rohtas** (Vindhyan region) |
- **Tibetan:** highest and largest plateau; average **4,500 m**; ~2.5 million sq km. **Pamir:** "**Roof of the World**".
- **Meseta:** **Iberian Peninsula** (Spain). **Anatolian:** Turkiye, between the **Pontic and Taurus** ranges.
- **Colorado:** USA, cut by the **Grand Canyon**. **Columbia** is in the USA, not Iberia.
- **Patagonia:** "storehouse of minerals" of South America. **East African Plateau:** **Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda**. **Abyssinian** (Ethiopian) plateau is in **Africa**, not Arabia.
- **Guiana Highlands:** **northern South America** (Venezuela, Guyana, Brazil), not Africa.

=== Plains, Basins and Regional Names
- **Great Plains:** **east of the Rockies** (USA, Canada). **West Siberian Plain:** between the **Urals** and the **Yenisei**.
- **Indo-Gangetic Plain:** **6,52,000 sq km**; aggradational; **youngest** physiographic division of India.
- **Great Basin** (USA): between the **Rockies and the Sierra Nevada**; Mojave and Sonoran to the south.
- **Great Artesian Basin** (Australia): one of the largest underground water reservoirs, west of the **Great Dividing Range**.
- **Okavango Basin:** Botswana (not Patagonia).
