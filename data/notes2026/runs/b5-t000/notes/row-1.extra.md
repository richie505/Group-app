=== Everyday Chemistry – Atoms, Reactions, Acids, Metals and Organic Compounds
# Chemical reactions
- **Rusting** (hydrated iron oxide) is a **chemical change** – the nail's **weight increases**; **tarnishing of silver** (Ag2S) is **corrosion**; freezing, dissolving salt, a glowing filament are physical changes.
- **Acid + metal → salt + hydrogen**; **acid + base → salt + water** (neutralisation); C + O2 → CO2 is **exothermic**.
- **Signs of a chemical reaction:** gas evolved, colour change, heat change – **evaporation** is only a change of state (physical).
- **Limestone:** CaCO3 → CaO + **CO2** on heating – CO2 **puts out a flame**; **quicklime + water → slaked lime Ca(OH)2 + much heat**; acid (HCl) + egg shell → CO2 – turns **lime water milky**.
- **Propane combustion:** C3H8 + **5 O2** → 3 CO2 + 4 H2O; **Haber:** N2 + 3 H2 ⇌ 2 NH3 (X = 1, Y = 3, Z = 2).
- **Combustion** needs fuel, oxygen and ignition temperature – **not water**; **magnesium** in crackers – bright light from **oxidation** to MgO.
- **Displacement:** iron nail in copper sulphate gets a **brown copper** coat (solution pale green); iron + dilute HCl → **iron chloride + hydrogen**; **silver bromide** turns **grey** in sunlight (photolysis).
- **Physical change:** dissolving salt in water; burning wax/wood and curdling milk are chemical.
- **Ignition temperature:** a substance catches fire only once heated to it – at 100 °C, substances igniting at 95 °C and 98 °C burn, those at 105 °C and 108 °C do not.
# Atoms and the periodic table
- **Helium:** noble gas, Group 18, period 1 – **top right corner** of the periodic table; inert, so safe in balloons; **hydrogen** is lighter but **inflammable** (least safe).
- **Atom:** electrons held round the nucleus by the **electrostatic (Coulomb) force**; nucleons held by the **strong nuclear force**; **protium (¹H)** has atomic number = mass number = 1.
- **Periodic table:** metals on the **left**, metalloids on the zigzag, **non-metals on the right**; **Group IA = alkali metals** (Li, Na, K…); **potassium** is soft enough to **cut with a knife**.
- **Spectral (emission) line:** electron **jumps from a higher to a lower orbit**, emitting a photon.
- **Organic compound** must contain **carbon**; **ammonia** = N + H (NH3); **~94** elements occur naturally (118 known – "about 100"); **hydrogen** = most common element in the **Universe**.
- **Transition (d-block) metals** form **coloured compounds** (d–d transitions).
# Organic chemistry
- **Alcohols as reducing agents:** they are themselves **oxidised to aldehydes or ketones**.
- **Hydrogenation** of unsaturated hydrocarbons/oils uses a **nickel** catalyst (vanaspati); **Wurtz reaction** – alkyl halides + **sodium** in dry ether → higher alkanes; **vulcanisation** of rubber with **sulphur** (Goodyear, 1839).
- **Perspex** (PMMA) – monomer methyl methacrylate made with **methyl alcohol**; **tear gas CN** = **α-chloroacetophenone**.
- **Named reactions:** **Etard** – aromatic methyl → aldehyde (chromyl chloride); **Stephen** – nitrile → aldehyde (SnCl2/HCl); Gattermann–Koch = formylation (CO + HCl).
- **Functional groups:** alcohols – **–OH (hydroxyl)**; **esters** – fruity smell; smallest **ketone** = acetone (**3 C**); **C2H6O** = ethanol; alcohol + conc. H2SO4 at 170 °C → **alkene** (dehydration); ethanol + alkaline KMnO4 → **acid**, acid + ethanol → **ester**.
- **Alkaloids:** **nicotine** – tobacco; morphine – opium poppy; reserpine – Rauwolfia; **benzene** (crude oil, petrol) – carcinogen causing **leukaemia**.
# Metals
- **Gold** = most **malleable** and ductile metal (silver next); **aluminium** = most used metal in nearly pure form (foil, cans, conductors).
- **Thermite:** **iron oxide + aluminium** – welds rails; **galvanisation** = **zinc** coating; **silver** = best electrical conductor; **lead** = toxic metal of car batteries.
- **Electrolytic refining of copper:** impure anode dissolves; **pure copper deposits at the cathode**; excess **lithium in LiCl → pink** (F-centres; K in KCl violet, Na in NaCl yellow).
- **Smelting** = extracting metal from ore by reduction at high temperature; **blast furnace:** coke → CO, limestone → CaO + CO2, CO reduces Fe2O3 (no FeSO4 decomposition); **seasoning** with oil stops **cast iron** rusting.
# Soaps, water and household chemistry
- **Saponification** = fat/oil + strong base (NaOH) → **soap + glycerol**; the soap's **hydrocarbon tail** grips oily dirt while the ionic head faces water (micelles).
- **Alum** purifies water by **coagulation** of suspended mud (Al³⁺ neutralises the charge); **rain water** = purest natural water (natural distillation).
- **Safety match:** **red phosphorus** on the **striking strip**; head = potassium chlorate + antimony trisulphide (most keys say "head = phosphorus" – old strike-anywhere matches).
- **Melamine** – unbreakable crockery; **tincture of iodine** – antiseptic; enzymes = biocatalysts; **DNA fingerprinting** – identifying criminals; **borax** dissolves shellac for water-based varnish.
# Salts and their uses
- **Salts:** sodium carbonate – washing soda, glass; sodium benzoate – food preservative; calcium carbonate – flux in metal extraction; calcium phosphate – fertiliser.
- **Sodium thiosulphate** – antidote for **cyanide**; **normal saline** = **0.9% NaCl** (isotonic); **gunpowder** = sulphur + charcoal + **potassium nitrate**.
- **Common names:** **alum** = potassium aluminium sulphate dodecahydrate; **aspirin** = acetylsalicylic acid; chalk = CaCO3; **barium nitrate** – oxidiser giving **green** flame in fireworks (strontium red, copper blue).
# Acids and bases
- **All acids contain hydrogen** (H⁺ in water); **pH = "potential of hydrogen"**; **NaOH + HCl → NaCl + H2O** = neutralisation, a **double displacement**.
- **Acids** conduct electricity in water, taste sour, change indicators and **react with active metals** (H2); **litmus:** **purple** in neutral, **blue → red** in acid; **ammonia** turns red litmus blue (**weak base**).
# Carbon and non-metals
- **Graphite** = **inorganic** solid lubricant (lanolin, mineral oil, PTFE are organic).
- **Sulphur** = common **fungicide**; **bromine** = only **non-metal liquid** at room temperature (mercury is a metal).
- **Noble gases in air:** argon, neon, helium… – **radon** is the one usually said to be absent (strictly, traces near the ground); **dry ice** = **solid CO2** (sublimes at −78.5 °C) – vaccine transport.
# Mixtures and colloids
- **Aerosol** = tiny liquid droplets (or solid particles) dispersed in **air/gas** – fog, mist, sprays; **sea water** = a mixture (table salt, sugar, lime are compounds).
- **Solubility in water (25 °C):** **glucose ~90 g/100 mL** > ammonium chloride ~38 > table salt ~36 > alum ~14.
- **Butter** = water-in-fat **emulsion** (NCERT lists butter as a gel – liquid in solid); vinegar, sugar solution and honey are solutions.
# Laboratory
- **Titration** uses a **burette**; pipette transfers a fixed volume; **dry litmus + dry HCl gas → no change** (no ionisation without water); **turmeric** is a natural acid–base indicator (red-brown in base).
# Industrial processes
- **Haber process:** N2 + H2 → **ammonia** (iron catalyst, high T and P); **Contact process:** SO2 → SO3 over **V2O5** → **sulphuric acid**.

=== Human Physiology – Organs, Blood, Nerves, Senses and First Aid
# Medicine and first aid
- **Diuretics:** furosemide (loop), mannitol (osmotic), acetazolamide (carbonic-anhydrase inhibitor) – all increase urine.
- **Catgut** (collagen from animal gut) = classic **absorbable suture**; silk and nylon are non-absorbable.
- **Burns:** **1st degree** – epidermis only, redness and pain; 2nd – partial thickness, blisters; 3rd – full thickness.
- **Heat stroke:** body cooling fails and core temperature **rises quickly** (> 40 °C); give **lemon water or buttermilk** (fluids and salts).
- **Wounds:** **abscess** = cavity with **pus**; hematoma = collected blood; **contusion (bruise)** = classic **closed wound**; **root canal therapy** saves an abscessed tooth.
- **Dosage forms:** syrup = liquid; tablets, capsules, boluses = solid; suppositories and enemas are rectal – **ecbolics** (oxytocics) are injected.
- **Burn first aid:** cool the part in **cold running water**; do not pull off stuck cloth or apply butter.
- **Capsule shells** = **gelatin**; **Dettol** = **chloroxylenol** + α-terpineol; **KOH mount** clears skin scrapings for fungi/mites; **sulphur ointment** controls **mange**.
- **Antipyretics** (paracetamol) lower fever; analgesics relieve pain; **bronchoscopy** examines airways – not a nervous-system test (EEG, myelography are).
- **Anorexia nervosa, bulimia** = **eating disorders**.
# Digestion
- **Digestion:** pancreas – digestive enzymes **and** insulin; **small intestine** – most absorption; **gall bladder stores bile** (made by the liver).
- **Enzymes:** **lipase** – fat; **amylase** – starch; **trypsin** – protein; **lactase** – lactose; **oesophagus** links pharynx to stomach.
- **Digestive system** = breaks food down and **absorbs nutrients**.
- **Saliva** pH ~**6.7**, **salivary amylase (ptyalin)** digests starch; **lipase** digests fats; HCl from **gastric glands**.
- **Fat digestion:** bile (made in the **liver**, stored in gall bladder) emulsifies, lipase completes it in the **small intestine**; digested fats go into **lacteals** – carried by **lymph**.
- **Alimentary canal** = mouth → **anus**; **small intestine** = longest part (6–7 m), starts with the **duodenum**; **stomach** = cardiac, fundic, body, pyloric parts (serosa is a wall layer).
# Heart and blood
- **Resting heart rate** of an adult = **60–100 beats/min** (~72).
- **Sphygmomanometer** measures **blood pressure**.
- **WBCs** (with platelets) are **~1%** of blood volume (buffy coat).
- **Blood pH 7.35–7.45** (slightly alkaline); **hypernatraemia** = excess **sodium**; **4 heart valves** (tricuspid, pulmonary, mitral, aortic).
- **Vessels:** **capillaries** = narrowest (5–10 µm, one cell thick); **aorta** = largest artery; **arteries** carry blood from the heart to the body; **pulmonary artery** carries CO2-rich blood to the lungs; **renal** – kidney, **hepatic** – liver, **coronary** – heart.
- **Blood cells:** **erythrocytes = RBC** (formed in **red bone marrow**), **leucocytes = WBC** (colourless, no haemoglobin), **thrombocytes = platelets**.
# Brain and nerves
- **Right cerebral hemisphere** – **spatial abilities**, face recognition; left – language.
- **Adult brain** ≈ 1.3–1.4 kg (~**3 pounds**); **axon** = the long fibre carrying impulses away from the cell body (dendrites short, branched); **Nissl's granules** are in the neuron cell body (not excretory); **trigeminal (V)** = largest cranial nerve, sensory + motor.
- **Synapse** = junction (gap) between two neurons.
- **Neurons** send and receive information; **medulla oblongata** – involuntary actions (BP, salivation, vomiting, **breathing**).
# Skeleton and muscles
- **Axial skeleton** = skull, vertebral column, ribs, sternum; **femur** = longest and strongest bone (tibia = lower leg).
- **Ossification** = deposition of calcium and minerals on cartilage → bone.
- **Tooth enamel** = hardest substance in the body – **calcium phosphate** (hydroxyapatite); **calcium + phosphorus** give bones their strength; **milk teeth** fall from **6–8 years** (most keys; strictly ~6 to 12).
- **Skeletal (striated) muscle** = **voluntary**; smooth and cardiac – involuntary.
# Teeth, eye and senses
- **Adult dentition (32):** **8 incisors, 4 canines, 8 premolars, 12 molars**.
- **Eye:** visual acuity **20/200** = **legally blind**; **ciliary muscles** change the lens curvature (accommodation); one eye only → loss of depth – a **two-dimensional** world.
- **Iris** gives the eye its colour – pigment made by **melanocyte cells**.
# Kidney and excretion
- **Excretory system:** nephron – **Bowman's capsule**, glomerulus, **Henle's loop**; ureters – **not** alveoli (lungs), colon or Schwann cells (myelin of nerves).
- **Kidneys** filter waste and excess water from the blood.
# Respiration
- **Lungs:** gas exchange in the **alveoli**; total lung capacity ≈ **6 L** (vital capacity ~4.5–4.8 L).
- **Alveoli** spread out ≈ a **tennis court** (~70 m²); **sneezing** = a reflex of the **respiratory system**.
# Body composition
- **Human body by mass:** **oxygen ~65%** > carbon > hydrogen > nitrogen > calcium.
- **Anatomy** = structure (physical arrangement) of body parts; **physiology** = how they function.
- **Trace elements:** official key treats **arsenic** as having a functional requirement (its essentiality in humans is unproven).
# Immunity and disease
- **Innate immunity** (first line): skin, mucus, **tears** (lysozyme) flushing dust from the eye.
- **Pneumonia** bacteria: **Haemophilus influenzae**, Streptococcus pneumoniae (Lactobacillus, S. thermophilus – curd).
- **Tears** contain **lysozyme** (antibacterial); ptyalin in saliva.
# Reproduction
- **Vas deferens** = **male** sperm duct – not female (female: ovary, fallopian tube with fimbriae, uterus, endometrium, cervix).
# Glands and hormones
- **Goitre** = **enlargement of the thyroid gland**, mostly from iodine deficiency.

=== Plants – Structure, Transport, Photosynthesis, Pollination and Useful Plants
# Medicinal and economic plants
- **Ashwagandha** = ***Withania somnifera*** (Solanaceae); *W. coagulans* = paneer dodi; *Solanum nigrum* = black nightshade.
- **Pseudocereals** (seeds used like grain, not grasses): **buckwheat** (kuttu, Polygonaceae), amaranth, quinoa.
- **Botanical names:** fennel *Foeniculum vulgare*; ginger *Zingiber officinale*; garlic *Allium sativum*; touch-me-not *Mimosa pudica*.
- **Medicinal uses:** **sarpagandha** – nervous disorders, hypertension (reserpine); **isabgol** – chronic constipation; **arjuna** – heart; **sandal** – diuretic, refrigerant.
- **Garlic** – edible **bulb** (cloves); **potato** "eyes" = **axillary buds** (tuber is a stem); **apple** – edible part is the fleshy **thalamus**, not the mesocarp.
- **Cissus quadrangularis** (hadjod, "bone-setter") – herb for **bone healing**.
- **Rauvolfia serpentina** (sarpagandha) – traditional **snake-bite** antidote; **fenugreek** seeds help **diabetics**.
- **Pitcher plant** – insectivorous (eats mosquitoes); **Rauwolfia** drug comes from the **root** of sarpagandha; **national tree** = banyan, ***Ficus benghalensis***.
# Plant types and parts
- **Annuals (seasonal plants)** complete their life cycle in one season; biennials two; perennials many years; **gymnosperms** = **naked-seeded** (pine, cycas).
- **Agrostology** = study of **grasses**.
- **Plant kingdom:** algae (**Thallophyta** – Spirogyra, Ulothrix, Cladophora, Chara – no roots, stem, leaves), bryophytes, pteridophytes, gymnosperms, angiosperms – **annelids are animals**; **angiosperms** bear flowers, seeds enclosed in ovary, usually have vessels.
- **Endodermis** of dicot stem = **starch sheath**; **Sachs:** three tissue systems – **dermal, vascular, fundamental**.
- **Banana** is a giant **herb** (pseudostem), not a tree.
- **Edible parts:** **potato** – modified **stem** (tuber); **carrot** – modified **root**; **apple** – swollen **thalamus**; **cabbage** – **vegetative bud**.
# Flowers, pollination and seeds
- **Pollination:** bright, scented, nectar-rich flowers → **insects (entomophily)**; **anemophily = wind**; hydrophily = water; ornithophily = birds.
- After fertilisation the **ovary → fruit** and the ovules → seeds.
- **Sporopollenin** (pollen exine) = most resistant organic material: withstands high temperature, strong acids and alkalis; no enzyme degrades it – so pollen fossilises well.
- Repeated **self-pollination** → inbreeding depression, **weak seeds**; cross-pollination gives vigour.
- **Winged seeds** (wind dispersal): **drumstick, maple**; hooks – Xanthium, Urena; explosive – castor, balsam.
- **Placentation:** **axile** – Hibiscus (Malvaceae); marginal – legumes; **phyllotaxy** = arrangement of leaves on the stem; **orchids** – "**dust seeds**"; **ornithophily** = bird pollination.
- **Vallisneria:** the **whole male flower** floats to the female flower (water pollination).
# Leaf, photosynthesis and respiration
- **Leaf:** **stomata** – pores for gas exchange and transpiration; **guard cells** open and close them; **chloroplasts** hold chlorophyll; enzymes = biocatalysts.
- **Photosynthesis:** green plants **make their own food** (glucose) from CO2 + water with sunlight and chlorophyll; light absorbed by chlorophyll, CO2 **reduced** (not oxidised); the O2 released comes from **water** (photolysis); **oxygen is a product, not a requirement**.
- **Stomata** = pores for **gas exchange**, opened and closed by **guard cells** through **potassium ions** moving in and out.
- **Inputs of photosynthesis:** **chlorophyll**, light, CO2, water (O2 and glucose are products).
- **C4 types:** **Panicum** – PEP-CK; maize, sugarcane – NADP-ME; Amaranthus – NAD-ME.
- **Chlorophyll a** = **blue-green**; chlorophyll b = yellow-green.
# Water, roots and transport
- **Root pressure** is **lowest in summer** (heavy transpiration) and highest in the rainy season.
- **Xylem** carries **water and minerals upward** from roots to leaves; **phloem** carries food (**translocation**); a **vascular bundle** holds both; vessel **end walls and pit pores** stop cavitation spreading.
- **Transpiration** = evaporation at the **leaf–air** surface; the tension **pulls water up** (not loss at the root surface).

=== Everyday Physics – Motion, Gravity, Fluids, Heat and States of Matter
# Heat and states of matter
- **Boiling points:** mercury ~357 °C > water 100 °C > benzene 80 °C > ethanol 78 °C (mercury boils last).
- **Conduction:** iron from the fridge feels **colder** than wood at the same temperature – a good conductor draws heat from the hand faster.
- **Changes of state:** **sublimation** = solid → gas; **deposition** = gas → solid.
- **Convection** – air-conditioners and heaters circulate air (cool air sinks, warm air rises).
- **Specific heat** = heat to raise **1 g by 1 °C**.
- **Water expands ~9% on freezing** – a corked glass bottle full of water **breaks** in the freezer.
- **Supercooling** = cooling a liquid **below its freezing point** without it solidifying.
- **Water has a high specific heat** – a watermelon stays cool longer than an apple; kerosene ≈ **0.5 cal/g °C** (water 1); **1 calorie ≈ 4.2 J** (4.186).
- **Dissolved salt raises the boiling point and lowers the freezing point** (colligative): salt in boiling water stops the boil until it reaches the new, higher boiling point; **rock salt melts ice** on roads.
- **Pressure lowers the melting point of ice** (regelation) – ice skating; **melting ice stays at 0 °C** (latent heat of fusion); **triple point of water = 0.01 °C** (273.16 K, ~611.657 Pa).
- **Mothballs (naphthalene)** work by **sublimation**.
- **Anomalous expansion:** water contracts from 0 °C to **4 °C (maximum density)** and expands above it.
- **Sound-proofing:** rockwool, wood, laminated glass – **not iron sheets**; **copper-bottomed pans** – copper conducts heat better than steel; a **small LPG burner** saves gas.
# Motion, force and gravity
- **Free fall:** without air resistance all bodies fall with the same g – brick and feather land **together** (Galileo; Apollo 15).
- **Friction** acts in brakes, striking a match, walking – **not** in planets' revolution (gravity in near-vacuum).
- A ball released from an orbiting satellite keeps its orbital speed and **revolves in the same orbit**.
- **Motion** = change of position with time (strictly, change of position = displacement); **Newton's 1st law** (inertia) – rest or uniform motion unless an unbalanced force acts; 2nd F = ma; 3rd action–reaction.
- **g decreases with depth** below the surface (and with height) – weight **decreases slightly** in a deep mine.
- **Displacement** = shortest path between initial and final position (distance = actual path); at **maximum height** the velocity of a body thrown up is **0**.
- **Friction** slows a moving object; **writing with a pencil** = friction (not gravity); gravity – planets, rolling rock, tides.
- **Levers** are of **3 classes** (fulcrum, load or effort in the middle).
- **Conservation of momentum:** no external force → momentum cannot change (a man on frictionless ice can move by whistling – printed key: both true, R not the explanation).
# Pressure, fluids and gases
- **Boyle's law:** V ∝ 1/P at constant T – an air bubble **grows** as it rises in a lake; **Charles's law:** V ∝ T at constant P.
- **Bernoulli:** fast air blown between two sheets lowers the pressure there → the papers **come closer**.
- **Surface tension** lets insects (water striders) walk on water.
- **Floating:** stones in a boat displace their weight of water; thrown in, only their volume – the **lake level falls**.
- **Density of water** ≈ **1 g/cm³** (1000 kg/m³), highest at 4 °C.
- **Charles's law in daily life:** a balloon in **hot water swells** (air expands on heating).

=== Cell Biology, Biomolecules, Genetics and Respiration
# Respiration
- **Aerobic respiration** needs **oxygen** → CO2 + water + energy; anaerobic in muscle → lactic acid; in yeast → ethanol + CO2.
- **Respiration** is **continuous** (day and night) and **exothermic**; **yeast fermentation** = **anaerobic respiration**; **sweating** (evaporation) is **endothermic**; composting is exothermic.
- **Glycolysis** (1st stage of glucose oxidation) → **pyruvic acid**; ATP made via the electron transport chain = **oxidative phosphorylation** (photophosphorylation in photosynthesis).
# Genetics and chromosomes
- **Humans:** 46 chromosomes = **23 pairs** (22 autosome pairs + 1 sex pair).
- **Genetic code:** **triplet codon**, nearly universal; **AUG** = start (methionine); **stop** = UAA, UAG, UGA.
- **44 autosomes** + **XY sex chromosomes** (XX female, XY male; ZW in birds); genes pass through **both sperm and egg**.
- **Variation** = differences among individuals of a species; the **limit of growth** is fixed by **internal (genetic, hormonal) factors**.
- **DNA content** changes over the cell cycle: G1, telophase = 2C; G2, prophase = 4C; **somatic : gamete chromosomes = 2 : 1** (2n : n).
# Cell, osmosis and diffusion
- **Egg shell** = ~95% **calcium carbonate**; dissolved in acid, the egg's membrane is semi-permeable – the egg **shrinks** in salt water (hypertonic), swells in plain water.
- **Cell organelles:** **nucleus** = control centre; **mitochondria** = **powerhouse** (ATP) – removing them **stops energy metabolism**; chloroplasts make food; nucleolus makes ribosomes.
- **Plant cells only:** **cellulose cell wall** (both have vacuoles, membrane, Golgi); **ribosomes** – protein synthesis; **cell membrane** – regulates passage.
# Biomolecules
- **Cell composition (% of mass):** water 70–90; **proteins 10–15**; **nucleic acids 5–7**; **carbohydrates 3**; **lipids 2**.
- **ATP → ADP** releases ~**30.5 kJ/mol** (7.3 kcal); **cellulose** = structural carbohydrate of plant cell walls; **chlorophyll's** central ion = **magnesium**.
- **ATP = adenosine triphosphate** – the cell's **"energy molecule"** / energy currency.
- **Cellulose** = **homopolymer** of β-glucose and the **most abundant organic compound** on Earth.
# Reproduction
- **Asexual reproduction:** **binary fission** – Amoeba; **budding** – yeast, Hydra; **regeneration** – Planaria; **spores** – Rhizopus.
- **Amoeba** – binary fission, but **not all unicellular organisms are only asexual** (Paramecium conjugation, Chlamydomonas, yeast).
# Evolution and origin of life
- **Adaptive radiation** (one species → many forms for different niches) – printed key "adaptation"; **biogeny sequence:** coacervates → first cells (mycoplasma-like) → heterotrophs → aerobes.
# Microbes and sterilisation
- **Hot-air oven** sterilises glassware at **160 °C for 60 min** (170 °C/30 min, 180 °C/20 min).

# Engineering and maths (off-syllabus)

=== Animal Kingdom – Classification, Anatomy and Veterinary Facts
# Classification
- **Aschelminthes (roundworms):** body **circular in cross-section** (hence the name); organ-system level; pseudocoelomate.
- **Phylum features:** **Porifera** – ostia, osculum, choanocytes, **cellular level**; **Cnidaria** – **polyp and medusa**, **tissue level**; **Ctenophora** – comb plates, **bioluminescence**; **Platyhelminthes** – **organ level**; **Arthropoda** – jointed appendages; **Echinodermata** – **water vascular system**.
- **Chordates vs non-chordates:** all chordates are bilateral, many non-chordates radial – so **bilateral symmetry** is the feature **not common** to both (printed key).
- **Marsupial:** **koala** (pouch); **Clarias** = catfish (Pisces), not amphibian (Bufo, Rana, Hyla are); **emu** has **no crop**; **centipedes** – first legs → poison claws (**forcipules**).
# Anatomy of animals
- **Skull:** mammals and amphibians **dicondylic**; reptiles and birds **monocondylic**.
- **Beaks:** insect-eaters **long and sharp**; seed-eaters short and strong.
- **Grasshopper (insects)** breathe through **tracheae** (spiracles).
- **Protozoa** (Amoeba) take food by **phagocytosis** and digest it with **enzymes in food vacuoles**.
- **Birds** have **pneumatic (air-filled) bones**.
- **Cockroach (insects):** **open circulation**, **tracheal respiration**; **cutaneous respiration** – **frog and earthworm**.

- **Dog breeds:** **Chihuahua – Mexico** (not China).
- **Snake venom:** **krait** (and cobra) – **neurotoxic**, neuroparalysis; Russell's and saw-scaled vipers – haemotoxic; pythons non-venomous.

=== Everyday Physics – Light, Sound, Electricity and Magnetism
# Light and colour
- **Visible spectrum (VIBGYOR):** wavelength rises from violet to **red** (orange > yellow > indigo > violet); an opaque object shows the colour it **reflects**.
- **TV remote** = **infrared** LED.
- **Refraction** = change of direction (bending) of light passing into another medium: tank looks **shallower**; **prism dispersion** (each colour refracted differently); **twinkling of stars** and **Sun seen ~2 min before sunrise/after sunset** = **atmospheric refraction**.
- **Scattering:** **blue sky**, **red Sun at sunrise/sunset**; the **rainbow** is **dispersion**, not scattering; a **pinhole camera's** smallest aperture is limited by **diffraction**.
- **Grass looks green** – it **reflects green**; **red** has the longest visible wavelength (~620–750 nm).
- **Dispersion** = splitting of white light into seven colours by a prism.
- **Primary colours of light:** red, green, blue – **yellow is not** one.
# Electricity
- **Rectifier:** AC → DC (inverter: DC → AC).
- **Indian supply: 230 V, 50 Hz** – a **110 V heater** draws ~4× its power and **burns out** within seconds/minutes.
- **Resistivity** depends on the material and temperature, **not on shape** (resistance does); insulators – practically **infinite** resistance; a **semiconductor's resistance decreases** on heating (metal's increases).
- **Short circuit:** resistance ≈ 0 → **current increases heavily**; a **fuse** melts on heavy current.
- **Galvanometer:** detects current and its **direction**; null detector in bridges; more sensitive, not more accurate, than an ammeter.
- **Transformer** works on **mutual induction**.
# Sound
- **Sound** is a **longitudinal** mechanical wave: **speed rises with temperature** (~0.6 m/s per °C in air); **solids** carry both longitudinal and transverse waves; **cannot travel in vacuum** (ultrasound too).
- **Loudness** depends on **amplitude**; **pitch** on frequency.
- **Echo:** reflected sound must arrive ≥ 0.1 s later → reflector at least **17.2 m** away (344 m/s); **women's voices** have **higher pitch** (shorter, thinner cords).
- **Frequency** depends only on the **source** (same in every medium); speed and wavelength change with the medium.
# Magnetism
- **Magnetic shielding:** put the instrument inside **soft iron of high permeability**.

=== Instruments, Units, Scientists and Inventions
# Scientists and discoveries

- **1 acre = 4,046.86 m²** (~0.4047 ha); 1 hectare = 10,000 m².
