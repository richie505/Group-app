=== Branches of Science and the "-ologies"
# Kinds of science
| Type | Fields |
|---|---|
| Natural | **Physics, chemistry, biology, earth science, space science** |
| Formal | **Mathematics, logic, statistics** |
| Applied | Engineering, medicine, agriculture |
- **Interdisciplinary:** **biotechnology** = biology + engineering; **geoinformatics** (geospatial) = geography + **information technology** (GIS, remote sensing, GPS); **data science** = statistics + computing; **cyber-physical systems** = computing + physical systems via sensors.
- **Genetic engineering** (rDNA technology) is a branch of **biology** (biotechnology), not of electronics or civil/mechanical engineering (APRO 2008).
- **ESSO** (Earth System Science Organisation, **2007**) works under the **MoES**: ocean, atmospheric and climate, geoscience, polar and cryosphere science.
# Study of … (most-asked angle)
| Term | Study of |
|---|---|
| **Ethology** | **Animal behaviour** (Lorenz, Tinbergen, von Frisch – Nobel 1973) |
| Etymology | Origin and history of words (**etymological dictionary** = history and origin of words) |
| **Entomology** | **Insects** (UK UDA/LDA 2003 ×2) |
| Ichthyology / Malacology / Parasitology | Fish / molluscs / parasites |
| **Rheology** | Flow and deformation of matter – **soft solids and flowing matter** |
| **Axiology** | **Nature of values** (ethics, aesthetics) |
| **Environmental psychology** | Interaction between the **physical world and human behaviour** |
| Biometrics | Identity from physical / behavioural traits (see Technology Terms) |
| Euphenics | Improving the phenotype |
| Biocenosis | A community of interacting organisms |
| Astrobiology | Origin and possibility of **life in the universe** (not bacteria – that is bacteriology) |
| Anatomy | Identification and description of **body structures** |
| Biotechnology | Use of living cells to make or modify products (e.g. **GM foods**) |
- **Palaeogeography:** physical geography of past geological ages; it is part of **historical geography** (exam key: "historical geography"). **Palaeography** = ancient writing; biogeography = distribution of living things.
- **Apograph:** a **copy or transcript of a manuscript** (the original = autograph).
> ⚠ Trap: ethology (behaviour) vs etymology (words) vs entomology (insects); astrobiology is not "study of bacteria" (EO Gr-III 2021: only B and C correct).

=== Scientific Method and Research Methods
# Steps of the scientific method
- **Order:** **observation → question → hypothesis → experiment → analysis → conclusion**, then replication / peer review.
- **Modern method of acquiring knowledge = the scientific method**; authority, expert opinion and personal experience are traditional sources (HWO).
- **Theory** = well-tested explanation of **why**; **law** = what always happens, often mathematical (Newton's laws).
- **⚠ Trap:** a theory does **not** "become" a law.
# Research designs
| Design | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pilot study** | Small study **preceding the main study** to check its viability |
| **Action research** | Practitioners (teachers) study **their own classroom problems** to improve practice – Stephen Corey (1953) |
| Field experiment | Hypothesis tested in a natural setting |
| Clinical / case study | In-depth study of one individual |
> ⚠ Trap: "studying a classroom problem" = **action research**, not field experiment.
~~ Sources: app notes 5:0 sub 2; structural-learning.com (action research, Corey 1953); general knowledge (pilot study)

=== Psychology – Methods and Theories of Behaviour
# Methods of studying behaviour
- **Objective:** **observation**, experiment, tests.
- **Subjective:** **introspection**, interview, dream analysis (depend on the person's own report).
- **Simplest form of cognition: sensation** → perception → concept.
# Theories
| Theory | Proponent |
|---|---|
| **Anarchic (multifactor) theory** of intelligence | **Thorndike** |
| Two-factor theory | Spearman |
| **Achievement motivation** | **McClelland** (with Atkinson) |
| Need hierarchy | Maslow – **peak experience at self-actualisation** |
> Statement angle: Monarchic / unitary theory = intelligence as one power – not Thorndike.

=== Technology Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Digital embossing** | Raised touch printing; **no plates, chemicals or solvents, no pollutants**; **braille maps**; introduced in India by **NATMO (attached office of DST)**, Jan 2022 (APPSC G1 2022: I, II and III) |
| **Biometrics** | Identification by unique **physical** (fingerprint, iris, face) or **behavioural** (voice, gait, signature) traits |
| **Cyber (cyborg) insects** | Live insects with **micro-systems implanted in their bodies** to steer them and carry sensors (DARPA HI-MEMS) |
| 3D printing | **Additive manufacturing**, layer by layer from a digital model; uses: confectionery, bionic ears, automotive, reconstructive surgery |
| RFID | Radio waves, **no line of sight**; **FASTag = passive RFID** (powered by toll reader) |
| NFC | 13.56 MHz, **~4 cm**; **metro smart card = passive NFC** |
| ICOLC | **International Coalition of Library Consortia** |
> ⚠ Trap: cyber insects are not software viruses or "explosive insects" (AEE 2011 printed key wrong).

=== Science vs Technology; Characteristics of Science
# Science vs technology
| Basis | Science | Technology |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Know-why | **Know-how** |
| Output | **Laws, theories** | **Products, processes** |
# Research and innovation
| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Basic research** | **Curiosity-driven** |
| **Applied research** | Solves a set problem |
| Translational | Takes lab results to use |
| **Invention** | First creation of a product or process |
| **Innovation** | Makes the invention useful in market or society |
- **Science:** **empirical, testable (falsifiable), reproducible, systematic, self-correcting**.
- **Pseudoscience:** claims scientific status but **cannot be tested**.
- **J.V. Narlikar:** **astrology** is not a science (UPPSC 2018); **astronomy** (celestial bodies), **cosmology** and **nanotechnology (1-100 nm)** are.
