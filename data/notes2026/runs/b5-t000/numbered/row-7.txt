## SUB 0 | INSPIRE – DST Flagship (2008)
[0:0] # Basics
[0:1] - **INSPIRE** = **Innovation in Science Pursuit for Inspired Research**; flagship scheme of the **Department of Science and Technology (DST)**, launched **2008**, to draw young talent into science.
[0:2] - **Not** a Ministry of Education scheme and **not** a residential programme for Classes VI–X (the residential part is the INSPIRE science camp; MANAK is an award, not a course).
[0:3] # Components
[0:4] TABLE head: Component | Full form | Target
  [0:4.0] SEATS | Scheme for Early Attraction of Talents for Science | School students (MANAK awards, INSPIRE camps)
  [0:4.1] MANAK | Million Minds Augmenting National Aspiration and Knowledge | Classes VI–X, aged 10–15; ₹10,000 each
  [0:4.2] SHE | Scholarship for Higher Education | Top-ranked UG/PG students in natural and basic sciences
  [0:4.3] AORC | Assured Opportunity for Research Careers – INSPIRE Fellowship (PhD) + INSPIRE Faculty | Doctoral and post-doctoral researchers
[0:5] - **2025 data:** MANAK **8.54 lakh** ideas, **46,926** students given ₹10,000; INSPIRE Fellowship **653** doctoral fellowships, **65% women**.
[0:6] > ⚠ Trap: SHE = **Scholarship for Higher Education**, NOT "Support for Her Education" – it is not a women's scheme (APPSC FRO 2022). Statement angle: "separate components SEATS, SHE, AORC" ✓ and "DST flagship" ✓; "residential for VI–X" ✗ and "launched by Ministry of Education" ✗ → **B & C only** (TREI-RB 2023).

[1:0] TABLE head: Scheme | Body | Target group
  [1:0.0] WISE-KIRAN (Knowledge Involvement in Research Advancement through Nurturing; WISE = Women in Science and Engineering) | DST | Women scientists, esp. after a career break; WISE-PhD, WISE-PDF, WIDUSHI
  [1:0.1] WIDUSHI | DST (under WISE-KIRAN) | Senior women scientists, interdisciplinary S&T
  [1:0.2] Vigyan Jyoti | DST, with Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti | Meritorious girls, Classes IX–XII
  [1:0.3] CURIE | DST | 22 women PG colleges – research facilities
  [1:0.4] GATI (Gender Advancement for Transforming Institutions) | DST | Institutions – gender-equity charter, modelled on UK's Athena SWAN
  [1:0.5] SERB-POWER | SERB (now ANRF) | Women researchers in academic/R&D institutions
  [1:0.6] BioCARe | DBT | Women biotech scientists after a career break

[1:2] - **WEST (Women in Engineering, Science & Technology):** launched **September 2022** by the **Office of the Principal Scientific Adviser** as a new initiative **of I-STEM**; helps women with STEM backgrounds, including after a career break.
[1:3] - **I-STEM** (Indian Science, Technology and Engineering facilities Map) = a **national** web portal for sharing R&D facilities (PM-STIAC mission) – **not international**.

## SUB 3 | Atal Innovation Mission (AIM)
[3:0] - **AIM** = flagship of **NITI Aayog**, set up **2016** (announced Budget 2015) to promote innovation and entrepreneurship across the country – not DST, Labour or Skill Development (UPSC 2019; APPSC Polytechnic Lecturers 2018).
[3:1] TABLE head: Component | Focus
  [3:1.0] Atal Tinkering Labs (ATLs) | Schools, grades 6–12: IoT, 3D printing, robotics; 10,000 ATLs in 700+ districts
  [3:1.1] Atal Incubation Centres (AICs) | Start-ups in universities/institutions
  [3:1.2] Atal Community Innovation Centres (ACICs) | Rural and under-served areas
  [3:1.3] ARISE | MSMEs
  [3:1.4] Atal New India Challenges | Product innovation for national challenges
[3:2] - **AIM 2.0 (2024):** **₹2,750 crore** till **March 2028**; **LIPI** – vernacular innovation centres in the 22 scheduled languages.
[3:3] - **Budget 2026-27:** ATL funding **₹500 crore → ₹3,200 crore**. **Jun 2026:** **Tripura** first State Innovation Mission under AIM 2.0; **India-France ATL Bridge** launched at **Nice**.
[3:4] > ⚠ Trap: "Sardar Patel / Mahatma Innovation Mission" do not exist; Kaushal Navin Lakshay is a distractor.
[3:5] ~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 1 p.41 (NITI Aayog – AIM); app notes 5:7 sub 7

## SUB 6 | Andhra Pradesh Innovation Ecosystem
[6:0] - **Andhra Pradesh Innovation Society (APIS):** set up **2015**; nodal agency for start-ups under the **ITE&C Department**, headed by a CEO; began at **Sunrise Towers, Innovation Valley, Rushikonda, Visakhapatnam** (exam answer for "headquarters"); its office is now at **Mangalagiri** (Amaravati region).
[6:1] - AP was the **first State** with an **Innovation and Startup Policy (2014-2020)**; AP has **590 ATLs** (2024-25).
[6:2] - **Ratan Tata Innovation Hub (RTIH):** set up by APIS as a Section-8 company; inaugurated **20 Aug 2025**; central hub at **Mangalagiri** with spokes at Visakhapatnam, Rajahmundry, Vijayawada-Guntur, Tirupati, Anantapur.
[6:3] - **IIDT (International Institute of Digital Technologies):** **Tirupati**.
[6:4] > ⚠ Outdated angle: "APIS HQ = Visakhapatnam" is the old answer; current office Mangalagiri.
