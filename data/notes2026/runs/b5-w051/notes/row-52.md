=== iDEX, ADITI, TDF and Indigenisation Lists
# iDEX ecosystem
| Scheme | Fact |
|---|---|
| **iDEX** (Innovations for Defence Excellence) | **Launched by the PM, April 2018**; ecosystem for innovation in **defence and aerospace** (not "industry and trade"), engaging **MSMEs, start-ups, individual innovators, R&D institutes and academia** |
| **DIO** (Defence Innovation Organisation) | **Funds and manages iDEX**; a **not-for-profit** (Sec. 8) company set up by **HAL and BEL** (not DRDO) |
| **ADITI** | Launched **Mar 2024** under iDEX to fund innovation in **critical and strategic (deep-tech) defence technology**: AI, quantum, hypersonics, directed energy |
| DISC (Defence India Startup Challenge) | Problem statements set by the **Services** |
> APPSC FRO 2022 "NOT correct": iDEX for "industry and trade" – it is for **defence**.
# TDF, lists and portal
- **TDF (Technology Development Fund):** MoD programme **executed by DRDO**; grants **up to Rs 50 crore per project**, esp. MSMEs and start-ups.
- TDF project: **green propulsion** thruster by Bengaluru start-up **Bellatrix Aerospace**, proved in orbit on **PSLV-C58** (Feb 2024).
- **Five Positive Indigenisation Lists:** bar imports of listed items after set dates. **SRIJAN:** indigenisation portal.
- **VIMARSH 2026** (Sep): DRDO–industry meet; funding, incubation, test facilities, source-code sharing for **MSMEs and start-ups**.
