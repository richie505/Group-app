=== DERs, Net Metering, RPO and Electricity Planning
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Distributed energy resources | Small, decentralised units near the point of use: **battery storage, biomass generators, fuel cells, rooftop solar PV – all four** (UPSC 2024) |
| Net metering | **Billing mechanism** that credits rooftop-**solar** owners for power fed to the grid |
| Prosumer | Consumer who also produces/stores power and sells the surplus |
| RPO | **DISCOMs and open-access/captive users** must buy a share of RE; fixed by **SERCs** (Electricity Act 2003) |
- **Draft National Electricity Plan (CEA, Dec 2016):** **no coal-based capacity addition needed in 2017-2022** beyond ~50 GW under construction (final plan 2018: 6,440 MW).
- **ISTS charge waiver:** 100% for solar/wind commissioned by **30 June 2025**; then graded.
- **Tariff Policy** 2006, revised **2016**. Draft National Electricity Policy 2026: **100 GW pumped storage by 2047**, Time-of-Day tariffs.
- EC Act 2001: emission-intensity targets for **208** industrial units feed the **domestic carbon market** (Jun 2026).

=== Energy Transition Targets – Panchamrit, NDC, Net Zero
# Panchamrit – COP26, Glasgow (2021)
- 1. **500 GW** non-fossil capacity by 2030
- 2. **50%** of energy requirement from renewables by 2030 (not 60%)
- 3. Cut projected cumulative emissions by **1 billion tonnes** by 2030
- 4. Cut carbon intensity by **45%** by 2030
- 5. **Net zero by 2070** (not 2050/2060)
| Commitment | Intensity cut vs 2005 | Non-fossil capacity |
|---|---|---|
| Updated NDC (2022) | 45% by 2030 | 50% by 2030 |
| New NDC (2031-35) | **47% by 2035** | 60% by 2035 |
- **Long-Term Low Emission Development Strategy:** submitted at **COP27 (2022)**, not COP26.
- 2026: non-fossil **52.27%** of capacity; RE crossed 200 GW.
- **Energy independence** goal = **2047**; net zero = **2070**.

# Integrated Energy Policy
- Meet energy needs of all at **least cost** till **2031-32**; **market-determined / trade-parity pricing**; **subsidies targeted to the poor**.
- Pricing reforms: petrol deregulated **June 2010**, diesel **October 2014**; LPG subsidy by DBT (**PAHAL**, 2015).
# IESS 2047 and the National Energy Policy
- IESS: open-access scenario tool; **Business-As-Usual** and **Ambitious** scenarios.
- **National Energy Policy aim** (APPSC FSO 2025): **energy access for all while ensuring energy security, affordability and sustainability**. Four objectives: **access at affordable prices; improved security and independence; greater sustainability; economic growth** (not greater import dependence).
- Fossil fuels = nearly **90%** of commercial primary energy supply.
- NITI "Scenarios Towards Viksit Bharat and Net Zero" (Feb 2026): nuclear ~100 GW by 2047, **200–300 GW by 2070**.

=== Energy Finance – NCEF, IREDA, Green Bonds
- **National Clean Energy Fund:** financed by a **clean energy cess on coal** (Budget 2010-11, ₹50/tonne → **₹400/tonne** by 2016-17); after GST (2017) the cess became part of the **GST Compensation Cess**.
- **IREDA:** set up **1987** under **MNRE** (not Ministry of Power); listed Nov 2023; **Navratna April 2024**.
- **Sovereign green bonds:** first issued **January 2023**. Net-zero finance need: **over $20 trillion** by 2070.
- Stranded gas-based capacity: about **25 GW**.
