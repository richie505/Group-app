## SUB 2 | Radar, Doppler Effect and Stealth
[51:2:0] # RADAR and the Doppler effect
[51:2:1] - **RADAR** (Radio Detection And Ranging): **detects and fixes the position** (range, direction, speed) of objects with radio waves.
[51:2:2] - **Doppler effect:** apparent change in frequency from relative motion – source **approaching → higher** pitch; **receding → lower** (a train moving away sounds lower).
[51:2:3] - **Uses:** **police speed radar guns**, **satellite tracking**, medical ultrasound (blood-flow velocity), **Doppler Weather Radar** (cyclones, **heavy rainfall and thunderstorms**), **expanding universe** (red shift = moving away, Hubble).
[51:2:4] TABLE head: Radar type | Principle / use
  [51:2:4.0] Doppler | Speed from frequency shift
  [51:2:4.1] SAR | Imaging at night and through clouds
  [51:2:4.2] AESA | T/R module on every element; electronic beam, no moving antenna; tracks multiple targets – Uttam, MF-STAR
  [51:2:4.3] VHF anti-stealth | Long waves pick up stealth aircraft
  [51:2:4.4] Over-the-horizon | HF waves bounced off the ionosphere
[51:2:5] - **Uttam AESA** (LRDE): solid-state active phased-array fire-control radar, wide-band RF front end, adaptable to fighters (Tejas Mk-1A); "**not capable of tracking multiple targets**" is the false statement.

[51:2:10] # Stealth
[51:2:11] - **Stealth technology:** **absorbing / deflecting radar waves** – very small **radar cross-section (RCS)** + **Radar Absorbing Material**; not "invisible to the eye".
[51:2:12] - Stealth objects **can** be detected at specific (low, VHF/UHF) frequencies; metamaterials are used to **reduce**, not increase, scattering (UPSC 2026: **1 and 2 only**).
[51:2:13] - **IIT Kharagpur:** low-cost **radar-absorbing metamaterial** to cloak soldiers and equipment from enemy radar.
[51:2:14] ~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 6 p.99-101 (radar types, AESA, Swathi, AWACS vs AEW&C); app notes 5:51 sub 7, 8, 10
[51:2:15] - **AWACS** (Airborne Warning and Control System) = an **air-based long-range radar surveillance and control system** for air defence, called the **"eye in the sky"** (IAF: Il-76 Phalcon).

## SUB 4 | Tejas and Other Indigenous Aircraft
[51:4:0] # Tejas LCA
[51:4:1] - **What:** India's indigenous **Light Combat Aircraft (LCA)**, a single-engine, **compound delta-wing, tailless** multirole **supersonic** fighter (Mach 1.6+); smallest and lightest supersonic multirole fighter of its class; digital **fly-by-wire** – India's **first indigenous fly-by-wire aircraft**; over 40% composites.
[51:4:2] - **Who:** **designed by ADA** (Aeronautical Development Agency, DRDO framework), **built by HAL**; named by **Atal Bihari Vajpayee**; HAL handed the first two to the IAF in 2016 (No. 45 Sqn "Flying Daggers").
[51:4:3] - **Variants:** Mk-1 (4th gen) → **Mk-1A** (4.5 gen; **Uttam AESA**, EW suite, air-to-air refuelling) → **Mk-2** (**GE F414** engine, canards); naval LCA and trainer.
[51:4:4] - **Orders:** **25 Sep 2025** HAL contract for **97 Mk-1A** (68 fighters + 29 twin-seaters), Rs 62,370 crore, 64%+ indigenous; earlier order of **83**.
[51:4:5] - **"First indigenous fighter":** exam keys say **Tejas**; strictly Tejas is India's **second** indigenous jet fighter – the first was **HAL HF-24 Marut** (1961).
[51:4:6] - **Distractors:** Akash = missile, Vikrant = carrier, Arjun = tank, Garud/Surya Kiran (aerobatic team)/Sukhoi are not the LCA.

  [53:0:1.0] Energy (work) | joule (J) – SI unit | kWh is a commercial unit of energy, not power
  [53:0:1.1] Power | watt (W) | rate of using energy; kilowatt, HP are power units
  [53:0:1.2] 1 unit of electricity | 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J | = 1,000 W × 3,600 s
  [53:0:1.3] Current / voltage / charge | ampere / volt / coulomb | distractors for "unit of power"
[53:0:2] - **Formula:** units (kWh) = watts × hours ÷ 1,000.
[53:0:3]   - 100 W × 4 h = 400 Wh = **0.4 unit**; 5 bulbs × 100 W × 20 h = **10 units**; 100 W × 10 h = 1 unit; 60 W × 5 h × 30 days = 9 units.
[53:0:4] - **Energy crisis:** the danger that fossil fuels such as coal and petroleum run out.

## SUB 1 | Demand, Mix, Per-capita Use and HDI
[53:1:0] # Demand and mix
[53:1:1] - India = **third-largest energy consumer** and **second-largest coal consumer**; demand to **triple by 2047**.
[53:1:2] - **Coal/thermal** gives the **largest share of power generation**: ~**55% of the energy mix**, **74%+ of generation** (APPSC EO: "maximum share" = **thermal power**).
[53:1:3] - After fossil fuels, the second source of supply is **solar** (UPPSC 2021).
[53:1:4] - **Non-fossil capacity:** 32% (2014) → **51% (2025)**; the 50% target was crossed five years early.
[53:1:5] - **Sustainable choice:** of coal, oil/gas, hydro and atomic energy, **hydroelectricity** is the renewable option that does not use up its resource (UPPCS 2012).
[53:1:6] # Per-capita consumption and HDI
[53:1:7] - **Per-capita electricity:** **957 kWh (FY14) → 1,460 kWh (FY25)**; about **one-fifth of China's**, one-ninth of the USA's – lower than China's.
[53:1:8] - **Per-capita energy:** about **one-third of the world average**; low, with a large **urban-rural** gap.
[53:1:9] - **HDI link** (APPSC Degree Lecturers 2017): a **certain minimum per-capita energy consumption is needed for a minimally acceptable level of well-being** – hence the strong correlation.
[53:1:10] # Sectors and drivers
[53:1:11] - **Industry** uses ~**50%** of electricity and stays the largest consumer by 2040; **cooking** energy demand falls.
[53:1:12] - Household electrification ~**99%**; **Saubhagya** connected ~**26 million** households.
[53:1:13] - Drivers of peak: **EVs, farm pumps, appliances, cooling, data centres, telecom**; peak **183 GW (Dec 2020) → 250+ GW (Apr 2026)**, ~37% rise.

[53:2:0] - **Shortage trend:** **10–15%** earlier → **4.2% (FY14)** → **0.03% (2025)** → **zero by Nov 2025** (first zero-shortage month).
[53:2:1] - **Record peak:** **270.8 GW (May 2026)**, about **90 GW** above 2019.
[53:2:2] - **25 Apr 2026:** no shortage in solar hours; about **2% deficit in non-solar hours** → the **evening peak (6–11 p.m.)** drives the **storage** push.
[53:2:3] - **Delhi's response** to the evening peak: **Time-of-Day tariffs and smart meters**.
[53:2:4] - **Regional imbalances:** poor **storage** and slow **transmission** strand renewable power; heatwaves stress grids.
[53:2:5] - **AT&C losses:** **22.62% (FY14) → 15.04% (FY25)**.

## SUB 3 | Hydrocarbon Vision 2025 and Upstream Policy
[54:3:0] - **Hydrocarbon Vision 2025:** long-term hydrocarbon plan **adopted in 2000**; exam keys link it with **storage of petroleum products** (UPPCS 2000) – it calls for adequate strategic storage of crude and products, within a wider aim of energy security.
[54:3:1]   - Focus: **self-reliance** through **indigenous production** and **equity oil abroad**; appraisal of all sedimentary basins; **free market and competition** – it does **not** ban private participation.
[54:3:2] - **HELP:** a **single licence for all hydrocarbons**; **OALP** = **Open Acreage Licensing Policy** – companies choose and bid for blocks.
[54:3:3] - **ONGC Videsh (OVL):** overseas arm of ONGC; acquires **equity oil and gas abroad** (not LPG retail).
[54:3:4] - **One Nation One Gas Grid**; refiners + Shipping Corporation of India JV (2026).
[54:3:5] - **TAPI** (Turkmenistan–Afghanistan–Pakistan–India) stalled; **IPI** (Iran–Pakistan–India) dropped by India 2007-08.
