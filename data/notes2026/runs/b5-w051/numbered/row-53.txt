## SUB 0 | Energy Units and Basic Terms
[0:0] # Units – the most-asked angle
[0:1] TABLE head: Quantity | Unit | Note
  [0:1.0] Energy (work) | joule (J) – SI unit | kWh is a commercial unit of energy, not power
  [0:1.1] Power | watt (W) | rate of using energy; kilowatt, HP are power units
  [0:1.2] 1 unit of electricity | 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J | = 1,000 W × 3,600 s
  [0:1.3] Current / voltage / charge | ampere / volt / coulomb | distractors for "unit of power"
[0:2] - **Formula:** units (kWh) = watts × hours ÷ 1,000.
[0:3]   - 100 W × 4 h = 400 Wh = **0.4 unit**; 5 bulbs × 100 W × 20 h = **10 units**; 100 W × 10 h = 1 unit; 60 W × 5 h × 30 days = 9 units.
[0:4] - **Energy crisis:** the danger that fossil fuels such as coal and petroleum run out.
[0:5] # Grid and energy terms
[0:6] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [0:6.0] Energy intensity | Energy used per unit of GDP; a falling value = growth decoupling from energy use
  [0:6.1] Peak demand | Highest power drawn in a 15-minute interval
  [0:6.2] Energy shortage / peak shortage | Unmet energy in MU / unmet peak in MW
  [0:6.3] Energy security | Four A's: availability, accessibility, affordability, acceptability (not accumulation)
  [0:6.4] Primary vs final energy | Coal, petroleum and natural gas are primary sources that are also used directly as final energy
[0:7] > Units PYQs (UPPCS 2017, UP Lower 2009, NDA 2018, SSC 2017, BPSC): always convert W × h → kWh; "kWh is a unit of energy".

## SUB 1 | Demand, Mix, Per-capita Use and HDI
[1:0] # Demand and mix
[1:1] - India = **third-largest energy consumer** and **second-largest coal consumer**; demand to **triple by 2047**.
[1:2] - **Coal/thermal** gives the **largest share of power generation**: ~**55% of the energy mix**, **74%+ of generation** (APPSC EO: "maximum share" = **thermal power**).
[1:3] - After fossil fuels, the second source of supply is **solar** (UPPSC 2021).
[1:4] - **Non-fossil capacity:** 32% (2014) → **51% (2025)**; the 50% target was crossed five years early.
[1:5] - **Sustainable choice:** of coal, oil/gas, hydro and atomic energy, **hydroelectricity** is the renewable option that does not use up its resource (UPPCS 2012).
[1:6] # Per-capita consumption and HDI
[1:7] - **Per-capita electricity:** **957 kWh (FY14) → 1,460 kWh (FY25)**; about **one-fifth of China's**, one-ninth of the USA's – lower than China's.
[1:8] - **Per-capita energy:** about **one-third of the world average**; low, with a large **urban-rural** gap.
[1:9] - **HDI link** (APPSC Degree Lecturers 2017): a **certain minimum per-capita energy consumption is needed for a minimally acceptable level of well-being** – hence the strong correlation.
[1:10] # Sectors and drivers
[1:11] - **Industry** uses ~**50%** of electricity and stays the largest consumer by 2040; **cooking** energy demand falls.
[1:12] - Household electrification ~**99%**; **Saubhagya** connected ~**26 million** households.
[1:13] - Drivers of peak: **EVs, farm pumps, appliances, cooling, data centres, telecom**; peak **183 GW (Dec 2020) → 250+ GW (Apr 2026)**, ~37% rise.

## SUB 2 | Peak Demand and Deficit
[2:0] - **Shortage trend:** **10–15%** earlier → **4.2% (FY14)** → **0.03% (2025)** → **zero by Nov 2025** (first zero-shortage month).
[2:1] - **Record peak:** **270.8 GW (May 2026)**, about **90 GW** above 2019.
[2:2] - **25 Apr 2026:** no shortage in solar hours; about **2% deficit in non-solar hours** → the **evening peak (6–11 p.m.)** drives the **storage** push.
[2:3] - **Delhi's response** to the evening peak: **Time-of-Day tariffs and smart meters**.
[2:4] - **Regional imbalances:** poor **storage** and slow **transmission** strand renewable power; heatwaves stress grids.
[2:5] - **AT&C losses:** **22.62% (FY14) → 15.04% (FY25)**.
