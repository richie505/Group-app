## SUB 0 | Ghadar Party (1913) – Founding, Leaders and the 1915 Rising
[0:0] # Basic facts
[0:1] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [0:1.0] Founded | 1913, in the USA (North America) by Indian immigrants of the US and Canada; the Hindi Association formed at Portland (Oregon) in May 1913 became the Hindustan Ghadar Party
  [0:1.1] Headquarters | Yugantar Ashram, San Francisco (not Berlin, Singapore, Tashkent or Vancouver)
  [0:1.2] First president (chairman) | Baba Sohan Singh Bhakna
  [0:1.3] General secretary / moving spirit | Lala Har Dayal – the leader and organiser; came to the US in 1911 (lectured at Stanford)
  [0:1.4] Treasurer | Pandit Kanshi Ram
  [0:1.5] Members | Mainly Punjabi Sikh peasants and ex-soldiers settled on the Pacific coast of North America
  [0:1.6] Meaning of "Ghadar" | Revolt / mutiny
  [0:1.7] Paper Ghadar | A weekly, for free circulation; first issue 1 November 1913, in Urdu (Gurmukhi edition a month later); masthead "Angrezi Raj ka Dushman"; poems collected as *Ghadar ki Goonj*
[0:2] - **Founder:** books credit **Lala Har Dayal** (organiser, secretary) together with **Sohan Singh Bhakna** (founder-president).
[0:3] - **Programme:** assassinate officials, publish revolutionary literature, work among Indian troops abroad, procure arms, and bring about a **simultaneous revolt in all British colonies**; ideology strongly **secular**.
[0:4] # Leaders – who was and who was not
[0:5] - **Ghadarites:** Lala Har Dayal, Sohan Singh Bhakna, **Ramchandra**, Bhagwan Singh, **Kartar Singh Sarabha** (sub-editor of *Ghadar*), **Barkatullah**, **Bhai Parmanand**, **Rash Behari Bose** (led the 1915 rising).
[0:6] - **NOT Ghadarites:** **Surya Sen** (Chittagong, 1930), **Khudiram Bose** (Muzaffarpur bomb, 1908), Barindra Kumar Ghosh, Jogesh Chandra Chatterjee.
[0:7] - **Pre-Ghadar work in North America:** Ramnath Puri, **G.D. Kumar**, **Taraknath Das**, Sohan Singh Bhakna.
[0:8] TABLE head: Body | Place
  [0:8.0] Swadesh Sevak Home (G.D. Kumar, 1909) | Vancouver
  [0:8.1] United India House (Taraknath Das and G.D. Kumar, 1910) | Seattle
  [0:8.2] Yugantar Ashram (Ghadar HQ) | San Francisco
  [0:8.3] INA / Azad Hind Fauj (1940s) | Singapore, later Rangoon
[0:9] # The rising of 1915
[0:10] - **Triggers (1914):** Har Dayal's arrest (March 1914), the **Komagata Maru** incident and, above all, the **outbreak of the First World War** – "England's difficulty, India's opportunity".
[0:11] - **21 February 1915:** date fixed for an armed revolt in the **Ferozepur, Lahore and Rawalpindi** garrisons; foiled by **treachery**.
[0:12] - **Defence of India Act (March 1915)** passed mainly to crush the Ghadar; **45 hanged** (Spectrum; PMF gives 42).
[0:13] - **Lahore Conspiracy Case (1915):** against the Ghadarites – **Kartar Singh Sarabha** and **Vishnu Ganesh Pingle** executed (16 Nov 1915); Bhai Parmanand arrested; Sohan Singh Bhakna transported for life.
[0:14] - **Singapore mutiny (15 February 1915):** 5th Light Infantry, inspired by the Ghadar.
[0:15] > Trap: Ghadar = **USA / North America, 1913, San Francisco**; first president **Bhakna**, leader and organiser **Har Dayal**; the 1915 rising was set off by the **First World War**.

## SUB 1 | India House, London and Revolutionaries in Europe
[1:0] # Shyamji Krishnavarma – London, 1905
[1:1] TABLE head: Body | Founder | Place, year
  [1:1.0] Indian Home Rule Society | Shyamji Krishnavarma | London, February 1905
  [1:1.1] India House (hostel for Indian students) | Shyamji Krishnavarma | London, 1905
  [1:1.2] *The Indian Sociologist* (journal) | Shyamji Krishnavarma | London, 1905
  [1:1.3] Paris Indian Society | Madame Bhikaji Cama (with S.R. Rana) | Paris, 1905
[1:2] - **India House circle:** **Savarkar**, **Har Dayal**, **Madan Lal Dhingra**; Savarkar led it after Krishnavarma left for Paris (1907); it broke up after 1909.
[1:3] - Not founders of India House: Rash Behari Bose, Taraknath Das, Barkatullah, Virendranath Chattopadhyay, Har Dayal; Annie Besant, Tilak and Gandhi did **not** start the Indian Home Rule Society (Besant and Tilak had the 1916 Home Rule Leagues).
[1:4] # Madan Lal Dhingra (1909)
[1:5] - **1 July 1909, London:** shot **Sir William Curzon Wyllie** (political ADC at the India Office) and **Cowasji Lalkaka**.
[1:6] - **Hanged at Pentonville Prison, London (1909)** – not life imprisonment.
[1:7] - **Hanged for killing British officers in England:** **Madan Lal Dhingra** (1909) and **Udham Singh** (Michael O'Dwyer, 1940).
[1:8] # Madame Bhikaji Cama
[1:9] - Born to a rich **Parsi** family of Bombay (1861); went to London in 1902; worked as **Dadabhai Naoroji's private secretary**; then based in **Paris** (and Geneva).
[1:10] - **22 August 1907:** unfurled the Indian flag at the **International Socialist Congress, Stuttgart (Germany)** – the **first woman** (first person) to hoist an Indian flag abroad; not Paris, London or Geneva.
[1:11] - Brought out **Bande Mataram** from Paris; popularly called the "**Mother of the Indian Revolution**".
[1:12] - **Prominent revolutionaries abroad:** **Bhikaji Cama, M. Barkatullah, V.V.S. Iyer, M.N. Roy** (UPSC 1994).
[1:13] > Statement trap (IAS 2006): Cama's flag = **Stuttgart**, not Paris ✗; Naoroji's private secretary ✓; Parsi ✓ → "II and III".
[1:14] ~~ Sources: Spectrum p.319-320, p.324-325; PMF Modern History Part 4 p.75-76, p.107; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhikaiji_Cama; app notes 1:207 (sub 0, 4, 6)
## SUB 2 | Komagata Maru (1914)
[2:0] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [2:0.0] What | A Japanese steamship carrying Indian would-be immigrants to Canada (not a party, a Chinese village or a leader)
  [2:0.1] Chartered by | Baba Gurdit Singh, a Punjabi contractor based in Singapore (misprinted "Gurdip Singh" in UPPCS 2014)
  [2:0.2] Passengers | 376 (mostly Sikhs, also Muslims and Hindus)
  [2:0.3] Route | Left Hong Kong (4 April 1914) → Shanghai, Moji, Yokohama → Vancouver (arrived 23 May 1914)
  [2:0.4] Why refused | Canada's "continuous journey" rule
  [2:0.5] Return | Forced back; reached Budge Budge near Calcutta – police firing on 29 September 1914 (Spectrum: 22 died; PMF: 18)
  [2:0.6] Impact | Fury in Punjab; spurred the Ghadar rising
[2:1] - **2016:** Canadian PM **Justin Trudeau** apologised in the House of Commons.
[2:2] > Sequence: **Komagata Maru (1914) → Gandhi's return (9 January 1915) → Tilak's Home Rule League (April 1916)**.
[2:3] ~~ Sources: Spectrum p.321; PMF Modern History Part 4 p.89; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Komagata_Maru_incident; app notes 1:207 (sub 3)
## SUB 3 | Berlin Committee and the Provisional Government at Kabul (1915)
[3:0] - **Berlin Committee for Indian Independence (1915):** **Virendranath Chattopadhyaya**, **Bhupendranath Dutta**, Lala Har Dayal, with the German foreign office ("Zimmerman Plan"); sought help of **Germany and Turkey**.
[3:1] - Missions went to **Baghdad, Persia, Turkey and Kabul**.
[3:2] TABLE head: Provisional Government of India, Kabul (Afghanistan), 1 December 1915 | Person
  [3:2.0] President | Raja Mahendra Pratap
  [3:2.1] Prime Minister | Maulana Barkatullah
  [3:2.2] Member | Obaidullah Sindhi
[3:3] - Set up during the **First World War**, with German and Turkish backing and the help of the Afghan crown prince **Amanullah**; it was a **republican government abroad** – not Bose's (Azad Hind, 1943).
[3:4] - **Period:** December 1915 – the wartime phase of the Ghadar and Berlin efforts, just before the Home Rule Leagues (1916); keys put it in the "Home Rule Movement" period.
[3:5] ~~ Sources: Spectrum p.322-323; PMF Modern History Part 4 p.76-77; NextGen Indian History 06 p.78; app notes 1:207 (sub 5)
