## SUB 1 | Article 280 – Mandate, Composition and Nature
[1:0] - **Article 280:** the **President** constitutes a Finance Commission **every fifth year or earlier**; **Chairman + 4 members**; members are eligible for re-appointment.
[1:1] - **Qualifications:** fixed by **Parliament** – **Finance Commission (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1951**: Chairman = experience in **public affairs**; members from: a High Court judge (or one qualified), finance and accounts of government, financial matters and administration, economics.
[1:2] TABLE head: Clause | Duty (recommendation to the President)
  [1:2.0] 280(3)(a) | Distribution of net proceeds of taxes between the Union and the States (vertical) and among the States (horizontal)
  [1:2.1] 280(3)(b) | Principles of grants-in-aid to States out of the Consolidated Fund of India (Art. 275)
  [1:2.2] 280(3)(bb), (c) | Measures to augment a State's Consolidated Fund for panchayats and municipalities, on State Finance Commission advice – added 1992 (73rd/74th Amendments)
  [1:2.3] 280(3)(d) | Any other matter referred by the President in the interests of sound finance
[1:3] - **Art. 281:** the President lays the report and an **explanatory memorandum** on action taken before **each House**.
[1:4] - **Nature:** **quasi-judicial**; recommendations **advisory**, not binding (Rajamannar: should not be turned down without compelling reasons); **"balancing wheel of fiscal federalism"**.
[1:5] - **Not permanent:** it is constituted afresh each time and its **tenure is fixed by the President's order** (it reports in about two years) – not a five-year or permanent body.
[1:6] - **Not its job:** preparing the Budget, assessing income tax, controlling expenditure, **Art. 282 discretionary grants**, GST sharing (GST Council), devolving State taxes such as trade/sales tax.
[1:7] > Exam angle: "primary function = distribute revenue between Centre and States"; "constituted by the President under Art. 280".

## SUB 2 | Thirteenth and Fourteenth Finance Commissions
[2:0] TABLE head: Item | 13th FC (Kelkar, 2010-15) | 14th FC (Y.V. Reddy, 2015-20)
  [2:0.0] States' share | 32% (from 30.5%) | 42% (+10 points, largest-ever rise)
  [2:0.1] Highest-weight criterion | Fiscal capacity distance 47.5% | Income distance 50%
  [2:0.2] Other criteria | Population 1971 25%, area 10%, fiscal discipline 17.5% | Population 1971 17.5%, population 2011 10%, area 15%, forest cover 7.5% (first time)
[2:1] - **14th FC approach:** **tax devolution, not grants,** as the primary route ("compositional shift"); took a State's **entire revenue expenditure** – **ended the Plan/non-Plan distinction**; **no distinction** between general and **Special Category** States; **no sector-specific grants**; States to share more of CSS costs.
[2:2] - **14th FC fiscal roadmap:** Centre's fiscal deficit **3% of GDP** (2016-17 to 2019-20); States anchored at **3% of GSDP**; States **excluded from NSSF** loans from **1 April 2015**.
[2:3] - **14th FC local bodies:** **₹2,87,436 crore** (2015-20), of which gram panchayats **₹2,00,292 crore**; distributed **90% population (2011), 10% area**.
[2:4] - Government accepted the 14th FC report on **24 Feb 2015**.
[2:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 10 p.8-9; NextGen Polity full p.431-432; NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 03 p.1; app notes 3:99 sub 4
## SUB 3 | Fifteenth Finance Commission
[3:0] - Constituted **27 Nov 2017**, chairman **N.K. Singh**; two reports – **2020-21** (interim) and **2021-22 to 2025-26**.
[3:1] - **States' share 41%** (one point less than 42%, because **J&K became a Union Territory**).
[3:2] TABLE head: Criterion | Weight
  [3:2.0] Income distance | 45%
  [3:2.1] Population (2011) | 15%
  [3:2.2] Area | 15%
  [3:2.3] Forest and ecology | 10%
  [3:2.4] Demographic performance | 12.5%
  [3:2.5] Tax effort | 2.5% (reintroduced to reward fiscal performance)
[3:3] - Not a criterion: **income tax collected in a State**.
[3:4] - **Grants:** revenue-deficit, **sector-specific**, **State-specific**, local-body and disaster grants (about **₹10.1 lakh crore**); **₹4,800 crore** (₹1,200 crore a year, **2022-23 to 2025-26**) to improve **educational outcomes**; **₹45,000 crore** performance incentive for **agricultural reforms**; rural local bodies **₹2.36 lakh crore**.
[3:5] - **Terms of reference rows:** mandated to use **Census 2011** population → **southern States** objected; ToR also covered disaster financing and a **non-lapsable fund for defence** (and internal security).
