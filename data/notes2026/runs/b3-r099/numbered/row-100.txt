## SUB 0 | Vertical Devolution – States' Share
[0:0] - **Vertical devolution** = sharing of **net tax proceeds between the Union and the States**; **horizontal devolution** = allocation of the States' share **among the States**.
[0:1] TABLE head: FC | States' share
  [0:1.0] 10th | 29% of gross Central taxes (Alternative Scheme of Devolution)
  [0:1.1] 11th | 29.5%
  [0:1.2] 12th | 30.5%
  [0:1.3] 13th | 32%
  [0:1.4] 14th | 42% (biggest-ever rise, +10 points)
  [0:1.5] 15th | 41% (J&K became a UT)
  [0:1.6] 16th | 41% retained
[0:2] - **80th Amendment (2000)**, effective **1 April 1996**: gave effect to the **10th FC** scheme; made **all central taxes shareable**.
[0:3] - **14th FC basis for the vertical share:** the spirit of the constitutional provisions, fiscal-space concerns of the Centre and States, and clarity on functional and expenditure responsibilities – **not** differing aspirations of State populations.
[0:4] - **16th FC:** **18 of 28 States** sought **50%** – rejected because of the Union's fiscal position (defence, infrastructure); capping cesses "neither permissible nor desirable".
[0:5] - 42 → 41 → 41: the one-point cut came at the **15th FC**, not the 16th.
[0:6] > Exam angle: share-by-number (29, 29.5, 30.5, 32, 42, 41, 41) is asked in nearly every State PSC paper.

[2:1] - **Art. 123** (ordinance) is not a transfer channel. Art. 275 grants: revenue deficit, local bodies, disasters, tribal welfare – **not** for every CSS.
[2:2] - **CSS rationalisation (2015-16, Sub-Group of CMs):** **Core of the Core, Core and Optional** schemes.

[2:4] - **15th FC grants:** revenue-deficit, **sector-specific, State-specific** (total **₹10.1 lakh crore**); **₹4,800 crore** for **educational outcomes** (2022-23 to 2025-26).

[2:6] - **Disaster:** SDRF/SDMF corpus **₹2,04,401 crore** (Union about ₹1.55 lakh crore); cost-share **90:10** NE/Himalayan, **75:25** others; **heatwave and lightning** to be notified disasters.
[2:7] - **Who releases central funds:** the **Union Finance Ministry**; NITI Aayog has no fund-allocation role.

## SUB 3 | Divisible Pool and Cesses
[3:0] - Pool = Art. 270 taxes (corporation tax, income tax, CGST, the Centre's IGST share) **minus cesses, surcharges and cost of collection**.
[3:1] - Pool as share of gross tax: **89.2% (FC-13) → 82.1% (FC-14) → 78.3% (FC-15)**; about **81%** in 2025-26 (89.1% in 2014-15, 74-80% in 2020-24).
[3:2] - **"41% illusion":** cesses and surcharges are outside the pool, so the stated share overstates the States' real share of gross tax.
[3:3] - **16th FC:** annual **CAG-certified** disclosure of net tax proceeds.
[3:4] ~~ Sources: Praveen CA360 Feb 2026 p.121; PMF LENS Feb 2026 Part 1 p.11; app notes 3:100 sub 3
## SUB 4 | Transfers and Outcomes; Borrowing; GST Compensation
[4:0] - **Economic Survey 2016-17:** no **positive** relationship between **redistributive resource transfers** and per capita consumption, GSDP growth or governance; **suggestive negative** relationship, notably with **own tax effort** → link transfers to fiscal and governance effort.
[4:1] - **ES 2025-26:** transfers rose sharply; **SASCI** 50-year interest-free capex loans prevented **pro-cyclical** compression of State capex – 2.17% (FY22) → **2.37%** of GDP (FY25).
[4:2] - **Budget 2026-27:** devolution **₹15,26,255 crore**; total transfers ₹26,20,769 crore; capex loans ₹1,85,000 crore.
[4:3] - **Borrowing:** State fiscal deficit **3% of GSDP** (16th FC); extra **0.5% of GSDP** for **power-sector reforms**; extra borrowing equal to **NPS/UPS** contributions.
[4:4] - **GST compensation:** 5 years **July 2017 – June 2022**; cess continued to repay COVID loans; unused balance **50:50** Centre-States, States' half by **SGST revenue**.
