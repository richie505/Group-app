## SUB 0 | Largest Items and Trends of Union Expenditure
[0:0] - **Largest item of Union revenue expenditure = interest payments** – larger than defence, subsidies, pensions or grants to States; it was also the **largest non-plan item** (1991-92 onwards). BE 2026-27: **₹14,03,972 crore** = about **26% of total spending** and **40% of revenue receipts** (37% in 2013-14; 42% in 2020-21).
[0:1] - **Revenue expenditure** is the largest block of the Budget (bigger than plan, non-plan or capital expenditure): **88% of total (2014-15) → about 77%** (2026-27).
[0:2] - **Centre + States** spending ≈ **28% of GDP** (Economic Survey 2016-17).
[0:3] - Total Union spending BE 2026-27 **₹53,47,315 crore = 13.6% of GDP** (+7.7%); revenue expenditure ₹41.25 lakh crore (10.5% of GDP).
[0:4] - **Committed expenditure** (salaries, pensions, interest) = **65.3%** of revenue receipts.
[0:5] - **Why spending grows:** urbanisation, interest payments, subsidies and defence – all of them.

  [0:6.0] States' share of taxes | 18% | 22%
  [0:6.1] Interest | 20% | 20%
  [0:6.2] Central Sector Schemes | 17% | 17%
  [0:6.3] Defence | 8% | 11%
  [0:6.4] Major subsidies | 7% (not 9%) | 6%
[0:7] - Then CSS 8%, FC transfers 7%, pensions 2% (2026-27).
[0:8] > Exam angle: "largest item of (revenue / non-plan / total) expenditure" = **interest payments** – asked by UPPCS, CGPSC and APPSC almost every year.

## SUB 1 | Revenue vs Capital Expenditure
[1:0] TABLE head: Revenue (no asset) | Capital (asset / liability change)
  [1:0.0] Salaries, pensions, interest | Roads, airports, irrigation, buildings
  [1:0.1] Subsidies (food, fertiliser) | Defence equipment (capital outlay)
  [1:0.2] Grants to States (even if assets are made) | Loans and advances to States, PSUs
  [1:0.3] Maintenance of existing assets | Repayment of loans
[1:1] - **Test:** changes the government's **assets or liabilities** = capital; the rest = revenue. Capital expenditure buys, creates, upgrades or **extends the life** of assets.
[1:2] - **Revenue deficit** = revenue expenditure − revenue receipts.
[1:3] - **Golden Rule:** borrow only for **asset creation** (capital expenditure).
[1:4] - Firms too: **acquiring new technology = capital expenditure**; debt vs equity is a way of **financing**, not a type of expenditure.
[1:5] - **Plan expenditure** had **both revenue and capital** parts.
[1:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC 04 p.10; NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 10 p.8; app notes 3:102 sub 2
## SUB 2 | Plan vs Non-Plan Expenditure
[2:0] - Plan/non-plan split **ended with Budget 2017-18** (end of 12th Plan), announced in the Budget Speech 2016-17; replaced by **revenue vs capital**. **Andhra Pradesh** also dropped it from its **2017-18** Budget.
[2:1] - **C. Rangarajan High Level Expert Committee on Efficient Management of Public Expenditure (Planning Commission, 2011):** the split was **"dysfunctional"**, an obstacle to **outcome-based budgeting** → remove it.
[2:2] - **Non-plan items:** **interest payments (largest)**, defence, **subsidies** (food, fertiliser), salaries, pensions, loans to States/UTs/PSUs.
[2:3] - Plan = asset-creating, productive; non-plan = consumptive, committed. **Maintenance of completed Plan assets** was shifted to the **non-plan (non-developmental)** side.
[2:4] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC 04 p.10, p.40; NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 02 p.11; app notes 3:102 sub 0

## SUB 4 | Subsidies
[4:0] - **2026-27:** **₹4,54,773 crore**; **food ₹2,27,629 crore (largest) + fertiliser ₹1,70,799 crore = 87%**.
[4:1] - Major subsidies (food, fertiliser, petroleum) **₹4.11 lakh crore = 1.0% of GDP**; Q1 2026-27 up 37.4% (crude, West Asia).
[4:2] - **2002-03:** Central subsidies about **1.6% of GDP** (never above 2% since 1991).
[4:3] TABLE head: Explicit | Hidden (implicit)
  [4:3.0] Budget line: food, fertiliser, LPG, export incentives, interest subvention | Under-pricing of public services: higher education in public universities, health, irrigation water
[4:4] - **Oil Pool Account** under the **Administered Pricing Mechanism** closed **1 April 2002** (FM **Yashwant Sinha**); under-recoveries later paid through **oil bonds**.
[4:5] - **NBS:** from **1 April 2010**; fixed subsidy per kg of **N, P, K, S**; **22 grades** of decontrolled P&K; **urea outside NBS**.
[4:6] - **16th FC:** well-targeted subsidies, **exclusion criteria, sunset clauses**, no off-budget financing.
[4:7] - **Rationalising subsidies** and cutting revenue expenditure reduce the deficit.

## SUB 8 | State and Andhra Pradesh Expenditure
[8:0] - **RBI "State Finances: A Study of Budgets" (2018):** States' capital outlay was highest on **rural development**, then water supply and sanitation, urban development, education.
[8:1] - States 2014-24: spending **16.15-17.49% of GSDP**; revenue expenditure **80-87%** of total; 9 of 18 large States budget revenue deficits for 2026-27.
