## SUB 1 | Constitutional Provisions and State Finance Commissions
[1:0] TABLE head: Article | Body | Provision
  [1:0.0] 243H | Panchayats | State Legislature may authorise taxes, tolls, fees; assign State taxes; give grants
  [1:0.1] 243-I | Panchayats | Governor constitutes an SFC every five years
  [1:0.2] 243X | Municipalities | Same enabling power
  [1:0.3] 243Y | Municipalities | The same SFC (243-I) reviews municipal finances – no separate SFC
  [1:0.4] 280(3)(bb), (c) | Both | Central FC suggests measures to augment the State fund on SFC recommendations
[1:1] - Clause 280(3)(bb)/(c) was added by the **73rd and 74th Amendments (1992)** – not the 80th (2000), which changed tax sharing.
[1:2] - **SFC functions:** review the **financial position** of panchayats and municipalities; recommend the **distribution of State revenues between the State and local bodies**, and among rural and urban bodies; grants-in-aid. It **determines its own procedure**.
[1:3] - **"May" = enabling** – the State is not forced to impose taxes.
[1:4] - **Eleventh Schedule** (Art. 243G): **29** subjects; **Twelfth Schedule** (Art. 243W): **18** subjects → **funds, functions, functionaries (3Fs)** rarely move together.

## SUB 3 | Panchayat Revenue
[3:0] - **Property (house) tax** = major own revenue of panchayats; they levy only **State-authorised** taxes: **local fairs**, house tax, fees, tolls.
[3:1] - Sources: **FC grants**, **Central Cooperative Banks**, **CSS** allocations, **SFC** allocations, **loans from the State**. **NABARD** is not a direct panchayat source; **sales tax, land revenue** are State levies.
[3:2] - **Grant dependence:** limited taxing power → panchayats depend substantially on **grants** (FC, SFC, CSS funds).
[3:3] - **Matching grant:** the panchayat raises about **10%** as its matching share.
[3:4] - **BRGF:** States had to pass Central funds to PRIs within **15 days**.
[3:5] - Gram sabhas spend only **4%** of meeting time on revenue (13% on local issues).
[3:6] ~~ Sources: app notes 3:101 sub 2

[4:4] - **SVAMITVA** drone maps → panchayat property-tax baseline; **AMRUT 2.0** rewards property-tax reform and bonds.

## SUB 6 | Challenges of Local Finance
[6:0] - **IIHS** (80 ULBs, 2012-17): transfers ≈ **40%** of ULB revenue.
[6:1] - India's intergovernmental transfers to local bodies ≈ **0.5% of GDP** vs global **2-5%**.
[6:2] - No **accrual accounting**, weak balance sheets → no bond market access.
[6:3] - SFCs **not constituted regularly**; State governments accept and release only part of SFC awards.
