=== Headquarters, Founding Years and Types of UN Bodies

| Body | HQ | Founded |
|---|---|---|
| ILO | **Geneva** (not Rome, Washington or Manila) | **1919** (Treaty of Versailles) – oldest of ILO/FAO/IMF/WHO; UN specialised agency 1946 |

| WMO | **Geneva** | 1950 |
| ITU | Geneva | **1865** – oldest specialised agency |
| WIPO | Geneva | 1967 |

| UNESCO | **Paris** (not Geneva) | Constitution signed **16 Nov 1945** (in force 4 Nov 1946); India a founding member |
| FAO | **Rome** | **16 October 1945** |
| WFP | **Rome** (not Geneva, New York or Brussels) | 1961 |
| IFAD | Rome | 1977 |
| UPU | **Bern(e)** | **1874** |
| IMO | London | 1948 |
| ICAO | **Montreal** | 1947 |
| UNIDO | **Vienna** | 1966 |
| IAEA | Vienna | 1957 |
| UNEP | **Nairobi, Kenya** | **1972**, after the Stockholm Conference |
| UN-Habitat | Nairobi | 1978 |
| UNICEF | **New York** (not Geneva) | **11 December 1946** |
| UNDP, UNFPA, UN Women | New York | 1965 / 1969 / 2010 |
| IMF, World Bank | Washington, D.C. (not Paris) | 1944 |
- **Rome food trio**: FAO, WFP, IFAD. **Nairobi**: UNEP and UN-Habitat. **Vienna**: UNIDO and IAEA.
- **Not UN bodies**: **ISO** (International Organisation for Standardisation) – HQ **Geneva** (not Rome); **ISO 9000 = quality management**, **ISO 14000 = environmental management** (UPSC 2005).
# Types of UN bodies
| Category | Bodies | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| Specialised agencies | WHO, ILO, UNESCO, FAO, **IFAD**, ITU, UPU, WIPO, IMO, ICAO, UNIDO, WMO, **IMF**, World Bank (**IBRD, IDA, IFC**) | Autonomous, own treaty and budget, linked to **ECOSOC** |
| Funds and programmes | UNICEF, UNDP, UNEP, UNFPA, UN-Habitat, WFP | Created by the General Assembly; voluntary funding |
| Other entities | UNHCR (an Office of the GA), UN Women | Refugees; gender |
| Related organisations | IAEA, WTO, OPCW | Not specialised agencies |
- UPSC 2001: **IBRD, IFC, IFAD and IMF are all UN specialised agencies** (ICSID and MIGA are not).
> Trap: IFAD is both a specialised agency and an international financial institution for the rural poor; UNICEF, UNDP and WFP are programmes, not agencies.

=== UNESCO – Lists, Prizes and Membership
- **Members**: **194** Member States + 12 Associate Members (2025-26). **Palestine** became the **195th** member in **October 2011** (South Sudan the 194th, days earlier); the US announced its withdrawal again in July 2025.
- **Executive Board**: **58** members; India re-elected in **November 2021** for **four years** (2021-25).
- **First woman Director-General**: **Irina Bokova** (Bulgaria), 2009. Now **Khaled El-Enany** (Egypt) – from **15 Nov 2025**; first Arab DG.
- Programmes: **Information for All Programme (IFAP)**; **Man and the Biosphere (MAB)**; Memory of the World (1992); leads **SDG-4** (Education 2030).
- **Kalinga Prize** for the popularisation of science – awarded by **UNESCO** (since 1952).
- **Delors Commission** report to UNESCO (1996): "**Learning: The Treasure Within**" (four pillars of learning).
- **World Capital of Architecture** (UNESCO + UIA): **Rio de Janeiro, 2020** – the first.

=== Mandates, Programmes and Reports
# Who does what
| Body | Mandate / programme |
|---|---|
| **UNICEF** | **Children's** rights and well-being; **first UN body to hold and transact in cryptocurrency** (UNICEF Cryptocurrency Fund, **October 2019**); **Danny Kaye Humanitarian Award** – **Priyanka Chopra** (Dec 2019) |
| **FAO** | Food and agriculture; launched the "**Freedom from Hunger**" campaign (**1960**) |
| **WFP** | World's largest humanitarian agency against hunger; funded entirely by voluntary contributions; India's **five-year rice supply** deal (2026) |
| IFAD | Both a specialised agency and an international financial institution for the rural poor |
| UNHCR | Refugees |
| UNEP | Climate, biodiversity, pollution |
| UNDP | Development and poverty; co-builds the **Global MPI** with **OPHI** |
| UNFPA | Population; State of World Population |
| **UN Women** | Gender equality; proposed partnering **Kerala's Gender Park** (Kozhikode) as a South Asia hub (2020) |
| GEF (1992) | Financial mechanism run by **UNDP, UNEP and the World Bank** |
| **WHO** | **Global Centre for Traditional Medicine – Jamnagar, Gujarat** (agreement with the **Ministry of AYUSH**, March 2022; first such centre) |
# ILO
- **Tripartite** (governments, employers, workers) – in the International Labour Conference and the **Governing Body** (not ECOSOC). Indian industrialist **Y.K. Modi** was elected to the **ILO Governing Body** (2011, employers' group).
- Conventions: India has ratified **C29, C105** (forced labour), **C100, C111** (equality), **C138, C182** (child labour, **2017**); **not** ratified **C87, C98** (freedom of association, collective bargaining) and **C189** (Domestic Workers, adopted **2011**).
- Child labour (ILO-UNICEF): **160 million** (2020, released 2021); about **138 million** (2024, released 2025).
