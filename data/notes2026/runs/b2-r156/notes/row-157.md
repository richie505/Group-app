=== Bretton Woods Conference and the IMF
# Bretton Woods (1944)
- **United Nations Monetary and Financial Conference** = **Bretton Woods Conference**, **July 1944**, Mount Washington Hotel, **Bretton Woods, New Hampshire, USA** – **44 allied nations**, 730 delegates; plans of **Harry Dexter White** (US, adopted) and **John Maynard Keynes** (UK).
- Created the "Bretton Woods twins": **IMF + IBRD (World Bank)**, both HQ **Washington, D.C.**; the planned **ITO** was never born → **GATT (1947)** filled the gap until the **WTO (1995)**.
- Fixed exchange rates (dollar pegged to gold); **Smithsonian Agreement (1971)** widened the band to **2.25%**, lasted about **14 months**.
| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| IMF and IBRD founded (conceived) | July 1944 |
| IMF Articles in force – formal existence (29 members) | **27 December 1945** |
| IMF began operations | 1 March 1947 |
| First IMF borrower | France, 8 May 1947 |
| SDR created ("paper gold") | 1967-69 (first allocation 1970) |
- **India** = founding member of both the IMF and the IBRD.
# IMF – functions and lending
- **IMF** = **International Monetary Fund** – monetary stability; **short-term balance-of-payments (BoP)** support; technical assistance; **not** project/development finance (that is the World Bank's job).
- Lends **only to member countries** (UPSC 2011, MPPSC 2017) – not to "any country", not only to developed ones, not to central banks as such.
- Facilities: **Stand-By Arrangement**, **Extended Fund Facility**, **Rapid Financing Instrument (RFI)** and **Rapid Credit Facility (RCF)** (urgent BoP needs; RCF concessional for low-income members), PRGT. **Political-risk guarantees** = MIGA, not IMF.
- **1991 BoP crisis**: India borrowed from the **IMF** (SDR 3.56 billion) – with the World Bank about **$7 bn** in all → New Economic Policy.
# Quotas, votes, SDR
- **Members: 191** – Liechtenstein the 191st (21 Oct 2024); **189** in January 2020 (Nauru 190th, 2021).
- **Quotas** (in SDRs) decide financial contribution, **borrowing access** and **voting power**; do **not** fix exchange rates. Formula: GDP **50%**, openness **30%**, variability **15%**, reserves **5%**.
- Major decisions need **85%** of votes → the **US** (~16.5%) alone holds a **veto**. **India 8th** by quota (~2.75%).
- **SDR** basket of **five** currencies since **1 October 2016**: US dollar, euro, **Chinese renminbi (yuan)** (added 2016), yen, pound.
- **IMFC** advises the Board of Governors; **Article IV consultations** = regular review of each member's economy.
> Trap: 27 Dec 1945 = formal existence; 1 March 1947 = operations; 1944 = founding conference. HQ = Washington, not New York.

=== World Bank Group
- **IBRD** = the original "**World Bank**" (1944; began 1946); HQ **Washington, D.C.** (not New York, Manila, Geneva, Vienna or Brussels); gives **medium- and long-term** loans; **India a founding member**; first loan to India 1949 (Railways).
| Institution | Year | Function |
|---|---|---|
| IBRD | 1944 | Loans to middle-income governments for infrastructure, reconstruction and development |
| IFC | 1956 | **Private-sector** investment; conceived "Doing Business" |
| **IDA** | **1960** | "**Soft window**": interest-free credits, concessional (**soft**) loans and grants to the poorest; an **affiliate of the World Bank**, **administered by the IBRD** (same staff) |
| ICSID | 1966 (not 1976) | Settles investment disputes; **India not a member** |
| MIGA | 1988 | Political-risk insurance and guarantees (India joined 1994) |
- IBRD, IDA and IFC are UN specialised agencies; ICSID and MIGA are not.
- **Match (UPSC 1999)**: WTO – multilateral trade negotiations; **IDA – soft loans**; **IMF – short-term BoP loans**; **IBRD – lending for reconstruction and development**.
- **Aid India Consortium** (World Bank-chaired donor group, 1958) → renamed **India Development Forum**.
- World Bank funds: **BioCarbon Fund Initiative for Sustainable Forest Landscapes (ISFL)** – managed by the **World Bank**; **Bangladesh Road Safety Project** – **$358 million** (June 2023; Gazipur-Elenga N4, Natore-Nawabganj N6 highways).

=== NDB, ADB and AIIB
# New Development Bank (BRICS)
- Idea: **4th BRICS Summit, New Delhi, 2012**. Agreement: **6th Summit, Fortaleza, Brazil, 15 July 2014** – the **Fortaleza Declaration** (BRICS, not ASEAN/OECD/WTO); operations 2015.
- HQ **Shanghai, China**; regional offices Johannesburg, São Paulo, **GIFT City** (India), Moscow. First President **K.V. Kamath**.
- Founders hold **equal votes; no veto**; open to all UN members. New members: Bangladesh, UAE (2021), **Egypt** (2023), Algeria (2025).
- **Russia** = 20% shareholder; NDB froze Russia-linked work, March 2022. 16th BRICS Summit: **Kazan**, Oct 2024; **New Delhi Declaration** (18th, 2026): Global North (~15% of people) holds ~**60%** of IMF/WB votes.
# Asian Development Bank
- Est. **1966** at **UN-ESCAP's** initiative (not UNCTAD); HQ **Manila, Philippines**; **India a founding member** (31 founders); **69 members**; **Türkiye** newest (2025).
- Largest shareholders **Japan and USA** (~15.6% each); **India 4th** (~6.3%); President traditionally **Japanese**.
- Launched the **Nature Solutions Finance Hub for Asia and the Pacific** (2023).
- India is a member of **ADB** and the **Colombo Plan**; **not** of **APEC** or the **OECD** (UPSC 2008).
# AIIB
- China-proposed; HQ **Beijing**; Articles signed **2015**; operations **January 2016**.
- **Founding members** include **India, China and the UK**; the **USA** (and Japan) stayed out.
- **111** approved members; **China largest** (~26.5% votes), **India second** (~7.6%).
> Trap: Manila = ADB (Japan-led); Beijing = AIIB (China-led); Shanghai = NDB (BRICS, equal votes).
