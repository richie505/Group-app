## SUB 0 | Bretton Woods Conference and Founding
[157:0:0] - **UN Monetary and Financial Conference** = Bretton Woods Conference, **July 1944**, **Mount Washington Hotel, Bretton Woods, New Hampshire, USA**.
[157:0:1] - **730 delegates** from **44 allied nations**; plan built on the ideas of **Harry Dexter White** and **John Maynard Keynes**.
[157:0:2] - Created: **IMF + IBRD**; agreements also covered GATT.
[157:0:3] - Not created: the proposed **International Trade Organization (ITO)** → **GATT (1947)** filled the gap until the **WTO (1995)**.
[157:0:4] - System: fixed exchange rates; currencies pegged to the dollar, dollar pegged to gold. **Smithsonian Agreement (1971)** replaced the parities, widened the band to **2.25%**; lasted about **14 months**.

  [157:0:5.0] IMF and IBRD founded | 1944

## SUB 2 | Imf – Quota, Sdr and Facilities
[157:2:0] - **Members: 191** – Liechtenstein became the 191st on 21 Oct 2024.

[157:2:5] - **IMFC** (International Monetary and Financial Committee) advises the Board of Governors.
[157:2:6] - **Article IV consultations**: IMF's regular review of each member's economy.
[157:2:7] - Facilities: **Stand-By Arrangement**, **Extended Fund Facility**, **Rapid Financing Instrument**, **Rapid Credit Facility**, **PRGT**; ESAF on softest terms.

=== NOTES 2:158 | World Trade Organization – Structure, Agreements (AoA, Trips, Trims, Gats), Dispute Settlement, India's Issues
## SUB 0 | Wto Basics
[158:0:0] - Formed **1 January 1995** under the **Marrakesh Agreement**, after the **Uruguay Round**; replaced the **GATT** (1947) system (GATT survives as a WTO agreement).
[158:0:1] - HQ **Geneva** (not Vienna); **one-country-one-vote**, consensus – gives developing countries bargaining room.
[158:0:2] - Members **166** (March 2026); latest: **Comoros** and **Timor-Leste** (2024).
[158:0:3] - **GATT Article XXIV**: FTAs allowed as MFN exceptions if they cover "substantially all trade".
[158:0:4] - DG: **Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala** – second term 1 Sept 2025 – 31 Aug 2029. **Pascal Lamy** – former DG; French; earlier EU Trade Commissioner.
[158:0:5] Marrakesh = WTO agreement; Uruguay = round that created it; Geneva = HQ.
