## 5. Key settlements
- **1991 gold**: the gold was pledged with the **Bank of England and the Bank of Japan** (46.91 t, July 1991, ~$405 mn) – not with the IMF or World Bank; printed key missing → Bank of Japan.
- **Largest grant source 2005-06**: Economic Survey 2006-07 Table 8.3(A) – UK Rs 1,371.5 crore ≫ Japan 56.4, World Bank (IBRD + IDA) 43.0, ADB 1.1, all UN bodies 252.8 → no printed option is right; "United Kingdom" added.
- **Palestine = 195th UNESCO member** (31 Oct 2011; South Sudan 194th) – kept.
- **ASEAN-India InnoTech Summit 2019 = Davao City** (DOST Philippines) – kept.
