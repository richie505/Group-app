=== Gram Panchayat at Work – Sarpanch, Panchayat Secretary, Meetings
# Who does what in a Gram Panchayat (AP Panchayat Raj Act, 1994)
| Function | Answer |
|---|---|
| Convenes Gram Panchayat meetings | **Panchayat Secretary** – with the approval of, or on the direction of, the **Sarpanch** (s.31); the Sarpanch **directs** that the meeting be held |
| Presides over GP meetings | **Sarpanch**; in his absence the **Upa-Sarpanch** |
| Implements resolutions of the GP and its committees | **Panchayat Secretary** (the executive authority) |
| Collects taxes; calculates house tax of a new building | **Panchayat Secretary** |
| Written permission for any advertisement in the village (s.64) | **Panchayat Secretary** (executive authority) |
| Expels a convicted bye-law offender from a public market (s.107) | **Panchayat Secretary** (executive authority) |
| Marriage Officer of the gram panchayat (appointed by the Governor) | **Panchayat Secretary** |
| Bans sewage on public roads, sanitation orders; cremation/burial of destitute bodies | **Gram Panchayat** (the body) |
| Scrutinises the records of the GP | **Sarpanch** |
| Forms Joint Action Committees for joint works and settles disputes between panchayats | **Sarpanch** |
| Reports epidemics, atrocities on SCs, prepares the draft BPL list | **Panchayat Secretary** |
| Reports births/bodies found in hospitals, jails, choultries, open places | the **officer in charge / the person who first sees it** |
- **Sarpanch:** **executive head** and political head of the GP; **directly elected**; **controls** the Panchayat Secretary.
- **Panchayat Secretary:** the **Government's representative** (functionary) in each panchayat; **executive authority**; works under the **administrative control of the Gram Panchayat** and is its **immediate subordinate** (all officers and employees are **subordinate to the Gram Panchayat**, s.36(5)).
- **Not** a Secretary's function: **school supervision** (his work is village administration, community welfare and community development).
- **Appeal:** an order of the Panchayat Secretary granting, refusing, suspending or revoking a **licence or permission** → appeal to the **Gram Panchayat** (s.128).
- **Obstruction:** wrongfully restraining the Secretary or his delegate from entering any place to exercise lawful powers is **an offence under the Indian Penal Code** (now the **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023**).
- **Annual report:** the Secretary prepares an **Administration Report** on the panchayat's governance each year, submitted by **May**.
- **Other Acts:** the competent authority under the **Minimum Wages Act, 1948** can notify the Panchayat Secretary as an **Inspector**.
- **Dissolution:** if a GP abuses its powers, the **State Government** (through the administration) can dissolve it.
- **Body corporate:** every Gram Panchayat (and every Mandal Praja Parishad) is a **body corporate** with perpetual succession and a common seal.
- **Disqualification:** contractors for the panchayat, its employees and honorary magistrates are disqualified; a person who served the last term as a **ward member can contest**.
# Obligatory vs discretionary functions
| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Obligatory | Sanitation, drinking water, drains, street lighting, village roads, **burial grounds** |
| Discretionary | Tree planting, **libraries**, markets, mother-child welfare homes, modern farming practices |
- **Municipal Corporation:** **water supply** is an **obligatory** function; relief to destitutes, fairs and exhibitions and roadside tree planting are **discretionary**.
- GP **property protection:** the 10% open-space land of layouts and parks is a **Category A** property to be protected; district **GP Properties Protection Wing** committees are supervised at the Commissionerate by the **Vigilance (Encroachment) Wing**.
> Exam angle: "convenes / implements / collects / permits" = **Secretary**; "presides / directs / scrutinises / controls" = **Sarpanch**; "the body that orders" = **Gram Panchayat**.

=== Gram Panchayat Finances – Taxes, Accounts and Funds
# Taxes (s.60)
- **Compulsory taxes:** **House tax** (main own revenue, levied every year) and **Kolagaram / Katarusum** – tax on village produce sold in the village **by weight, measurement or number** (i.e. by weight and by volume).
- **Optional taxes:** a **vehicle tax** and a **tax on agricultural land for a specific purpose**.
- **Land cess:** 2 naya paise in the rupee on annual rental value; a fee for a specific purpose over and above taxes on land is a **cess**.
- **Surcharge on land:** with the prescribed authority's prior approval, up to **25 naya paise in the rupee** (s.60(5)).
- **Betterment charges:** **Rs 50 per sq m** in GPs under a municipal master plan, at mandal headquarters, or with 10,000+ population.
- **Gated Community:** minimum site area **1000 sq m** (AP key).
- **Not** a source of GP funds: **fees on services to SHGs** (taxes, Central grants and loans are).
# Accounts
- **Model Accounting System (MAS)** – the framework prepared by the **Central Government** (MoPR with the **C&AG**, 2009); PRIs keep accounts on a **cash basis**.
- MAS classifies transactions on a **three-tier structure**: **Function (Major Head), Programme (Minor Head), Object (Object Head)**; "weekly upgradation of accounts" is **not** a MAS feature.
- Book-keeping: **double entry** (PRIASoft); some keys say "both single and double entry".
- **PRIASoft** – MoPR's web-based **accounting software for PRIs**, for accountability and transparency; the most popular accounting application in panchayats.
- Fiscal year ends **31 March**; salaries paid through the **Acquittance Register**; durable goods first entered in the **Stock Register**; approved bills stamped **"Paid and cancelled"**.
- Working balance: at least **5%** of estimated receipts; surplus money invested for at most **three years**; contractor's **EMD 2.5%** of the estimate; **10%** of GP funds for welfare activities.
- **Surcharge certificate** – enforceable like a **civil court decree**.
- **Zilla Parishad markets:** income shared **35 : 65** among PRIs.
- **Internal audit** of Zilla Praja Parishads – inspected by the **Commissioner, Panchayat Raj**.
- **m-FMS pilot** (Government of India) in AP – **East Godavari** district.
# Devolution and State Finance Commissions
- **SFC** (243I/243Y) – formed by the **Governor** (not the SEC) every five years; Part VI of the 1994 Act. **4th SFC** report Oct 2019; **5th SFC** constituted 20 March 2023 for **2025-30**.
- **3rd SFC** per-capita grant **ZP : MP : GP = Rs 8 : 16 : 8**; the **4th SFC** (Gummadi Nancharaiah, report Oct 2019) recommended raising these grants to **Rs 25 : 50 : 25** (GP Rs 8 → 25, MP Rs 16 → 50, ZP Rs 8 → 25).
- **14th and 15th FC** grants favoured **drinking water and sanitation**. **16th FC (2026-31):** AP rural local bodies **Rs 16,627 crore** (AP Budget 2026-27); only **duly constituted** (elected) bodies get grants.
- **Main own revenue of a GP – property (house) tax**; intermediate and district tiers rely on grants.
- **Atmanirbhar Panchayat Program:** GP own revenue **≥ Rs 50 lakh** (Block ≥ Rs 1 crore).
- **3Fs – Funds, Functions, Functionaries** – still not fully devolved.
| Devolution Index (MoPR-TISS 2015-16) component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Transfer of **Finances** | 50 |
| Transfer of Functionaries | 15 |
| **Autonomy of PRIs** | **15** |
| Transfer of Functions | 10 |

=== Evolution of Panchayati Raj in AP – Acts and Committees
# Order among States
- **Balwant Rai Mehta Committee (1957)** – accepted by the **NDC, January 1958**; three tiers – **Gram Panchayat, Panchayat Samiti, Zilla Parishad** (no Mandal Parishad); **Panchayat Samiti** the executive body; the **Zilla Parishad approves the Samiti's budget**.
- **Rajasthan** first – **Nehru** inaugurated it on **2 October 1959** at **Nagaur**; **Andhra Pradesh** second – **1 November 1959**.
- **Kasu Brahmananda Reddy** – Minister for Local Bodies in N. Sanjiva Reddy's cabinet.
# AP laws
| Law / reform | Year |
|---|---|
| AP Panchayat Samithis and Zilla Parishads Act (first AP law) | **1959** (amended 1961: standing committees **5 → 7**) |
| AP Gram Panchayats Act – integrated Andhra and Telangana panchayats; provided for the **Gram Sabha** | **1964** |
| Mandal system (NTR Government, **Act 31 of 1986**): Panchayat Samithis abolished → **Mandal Praja Parishads**; ZPs → Zilla Praja Parishads | **1986** |
| AP Panchayat Raj Act (**Act 13 of 1994**) – incorporates the 73rd Amendment | **21 April 1994** |
| AP Panchayat Raj (Amendment) Act for Scheduled Areas (PESA) | **1998** |
- **1104 mandals** were formed in united AP after the Samithis were abolished.
- 1994 Act: **278 sections**; Part II Gram Panchayat; III Mandal Parishads; IV Zilla Parishads; V elections; VI Finance Commission; **s.40** – functional committees at GP level (agriculture, public health, water supply, sanitation, education …).
- **Revenue village** (s.3): a local area recognised as a village in the revenue accounts, after excluding municipal, cantonment and similar areas.
- The 1986 Mandal system drew on **Ashok Mehta**'s "Mandal Panchayat".
# AP committees on Panchayati Raj
| Committee | Key recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Vengala Rao** (AP) | **No party symbols** in panchayat polls; **reduce the importance of the Sarpanch** |
| **C. Narasimham** (AP) | **Direct election of the Sarpanch**; model AP Panchayat Raj on **Maharashtra** |
| **Purushottam Pai** (AP) | **Categorisation of Blocks** |
| **B.P.R. Vithal** (AP) | **Taluk Panchayat Samithis** for 80,000–1,00,000 population |
| Ashok Mehta (1977-78) | Two tiers (ZP + Mandal Panchayat); party participation |
| G.V.K. Rao (1985) | PRIs "grass without roots" |
| L.M. Singhvi (1986) | Constitutional recognition; Gram Sabha as direct democracy |
| Thungon (1988) | ZP as pivot; supersession max **six months**; constitutional recognition |
- **N.T. Rama Rao** – Chief Minister three times between 1983 and 1994 (1983, 1984, 1985) and again in 1994; brought the Mandal system.

=== Three Tiers, Elections and the AP State Election Commission
| Tier | Members | Head and mode | Term / age |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gram Panchayat | Ward members, directly elected | **Sarpanch, directly elected** | **5 years**; 21 |
| Mandal Parishad | MPTC members, directly elected | MPP President, indirect from MPTCs | 5 years; 21 |
| Zilla Parishad | ZPTC members, directly elected | **ZP Chairperson, indirectly elected** from ZPTCs | 5 years; 21 |
- AP's intermediate tier = **Mandal Parishad**, not Panchayat Samiti; **13 Zilla Praja Parishads → 28** after district reorganisation.
- **Article 243C:** village chairperson's mode left to the State; intermediate and district chairpersons indirect.
- **Members:** a GP of population **up to 300** has **5 members including the Sarpanch**. **Mandal Parishad permanent invitees – five** (Collector, Sarpanches, ZPTC member, ZP Chairperson, Agricultural Market Committee President; no vote); one minority member co-opted.
- **Zilla Praja Parishad:** **7 standing committees** (1994 Act).
- **Non-party polls:** **Gram Panchayat** (Sarpanch and ward) polls are **without party symbols** – no party competition at GP level; MPTC/ZPTC polls are on party symbols.
- **Two-child norm:** s.19(3) – a person with **more than two children** was disqualified; removed by the AP Panchayat Raj and Municipal Laws (Amendment) Acts, **Nov 2024**.
- GP governing bodies' tenure (2013 batch) ended on **01.08.2018**.
# State Election Commission
- **Art. 243K** (panchayats) + **243ZA** (municipalities); conducts all local polls – **not** co-operative polls.
- **Commissioner:** appointed by the **Governor** for **five years**; **status of a High Court Judge**; removed only like a HC judge.
- Original AP SEC formed **17 June 1994**; for residuary AP the Governor constituted the SEC on **30 January 2016**.
- **Anil Chandra Punetha** – SEC (appointed Apr 2026).
- **Nigha** – AP app (March 2020) for citizens to report poll irregularities (money, liquor) in local body elections.
# Reservation and recent polls
- SC/ST by population, **by rotation**; women **at least one-third**.
- **AP Cabinet, 18 Aug 2026:** **34% BC** in rural bodies; Mandal Parishad Presidents stay indirect.
- **Unanimous-election incentives (Jan 2021):** Rs 5 lakh (< 2,000), 10 lakh, 15 lakh, 20 lakh (> 10,000); 2021 panchayat polls in **four phases**.
- **Sarpanch honorarium** – Rs **3,000** a month (APPSC key).

=== Gram Sabha, MGNREGA and Community Organisations
- **Gram Sabha** (Art. 243A; defined in **Art. 243(b)**) – all persons registered in the electoral rolls of the panchayat area; convened and presided over by the Sarpanch (or Upa-Sarpanch).
- **Article 243G** – powers, authority and responsibilities of Panchayats: plans for **economic development and social justice** – the main objective of PRIs.
- **Requisition:** the Sarpanch must call a Gram Sabha on a written request of **10% of members or 50 members**.
- **MGNREGA works** are identified by the **Gram Panchayat on the recommendation of the Gram Sabha and Ward Sabhas**.
- **MGNREGA social audit** in AP culminates in an **open meeting (public hearing) at mandal level on the 7th day**.
- **Community-based organisations** for PR purposes: **SHGs, Village Organisations, Mandal Samakhyas** – **not political parties**; SHGs must **encourage members to attend Gram Sabhas**.
- **Gram Samvaad** – app giving single-window access to rural-development programme information at GP level.
- **Jamabandi** – annual check (audit) of village revenue accounts.

=== Urban Local Bodies in AP
- **AP SES 2025-26:** **123 ULBs = 17 Municipal Corporations + 76 Municipalities + 30 Nagar Panchayats** (16 corporations at the time of the older APPSC paper).
| Law | Key fact |
|---|---|
| AP Municipalities Act, 1965 | In force **2 April 1965**; Municipalities and Nagar Panchayats |
| AP Municipal Corporations Act, 1994 | Corporations; Hyderabad Municipal Corporations Act, 1955 as parent law |
- Municipal grades by **annual income**: Special, Selection, Grade I, II, III.
- **Nagar Panchayat** – transitional area (243Q); classified by population, density, revenue, % non-farm employment.
- **Article 243W** – State Legislature may endow Municipalities with powers and authority.
- **33.33% BC quota** in ULBs under the **1994 and 1965 Acts** (not the PR Act).
- **AP Municipal Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2024** – Minister **P. Narayana**; passed **without debate**; **removed the two-child norm** for municipal polls, to address the falling fertility rate (CM N. Chandrababu Naidu) – both statements correct.
- **AP Municipal Laws (Third Amendment) Bill 2026** – **Uniform Mutation Fee**.
- Mayor so far **indirect**; **AP Cabinet (18 Aug 2026)** approved **direct election** of Mayors and Chairpersons.
- Corporation = Council (Mayor) + **Standing Committees** + Commissioner; **GVMC** has zonal commissioners.
- First Municipal Corporation – **Madras (1688)**; 74th Amendment – Part IX-A, **243P–243ZG**, 18 subjects.
- **APCRDA** (Act **2014**); **VMRDA** (2018); UDAs are **not under ULBs**.
- **AIIB**-aided AP Urban Water Supply Project – 50 ULBs.
