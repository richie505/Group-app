=== Centre for Good Governance (CGG), Andhra Pradesh and Governance Institutes
- **CGG** – set up by the Government of (undivided) **AP in 2001**, in **Hyderabad**, in collaboration with **DFID (UK) and the World Bank**; a governance-reform **think tank**.
- **NCGG** – autonomous institute, upgraded from the National Institute of Administrative Research (LBSNAA); head office **New Delhi**, branch **Mussoorie**.
- DARPG tools: **NeSDA (2019)**, Citizen's Charter portal, **CPGRAMS**; **National Awards for e-Governance** – a **DARPG** scheme.
- **Mana Mitra WhatsApp Governance** (AP): **500+ services**; **Rs 100 crore** for RTGS in 2026-27.

=== Features of Good Governance – UN/UNDP
- The **8 attributes:** **consensus oriented, participatory, rule of law, effective and efficient, accountable, transparent, responsive, equitable and inclusive**.
- UNDP's **three most critical**: transparency, accountability, responsiveness.
- **NOT** features: **religiosity and emotionality**, **red tapism**, administrative **centralisation**, secrecy.
- **Centralisation** may **reduce efficiency** of service delivery for lack of local adaptation to diverse contexts.
| Pillar | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ethos | Service |
| Ethics | Honesty and integrity |
| Equity | Empathy for weaker sections |
| Efficiency | Speedy delivery using ICT |
- Tools: **people's participation** (most basic), RTI, Sevottam, Citizen's Charters, e-governance.
> Exam angle: "which is NOT a feature" – red tapism, religiosity, centralisation.

=== Meaning of Governance – Definitions and Origin
| Body | Definition / stress |
|---|---|
| World Bank | "The manner in which power is exercised in the management of a country's economic and social resources for development" |
| UNDP (1997) | Exercise of economic, political and administrative authority to manage a country's affairs at all levels |
| UNESCAP | Efficiency, effectiveness, equity, sustainability |
- **"Good governance"** was first brought into development discourse by the **World Bank** (1989 report on Sub-Saharan Africa).
- **Governance is broader than government**: **state + market + civil society**.
- **Democracy** helps development by encouraging **citizen participation and accountability**.
- **Aristotle**: "A good citizen makes a good state and a bad citizen a bad state".
~~ Sources: app notes 2:87; general knowledge (Aristotle)

=== Good Governance Day and the Good Governance Index
- **Good Governance Day – 25 December**, birthday of **Atal Bihari Vajpayee**; first observed in **2014**.
- **GGI** – launched by **DARPG** on Good Governance Day 2019; ranks States, UTs, districts; revised **every two years**; **10 sectors** (agriculture, commerce and industry, HRD, public health, public infrastructure, economic governance, social welfare, judicial and public security, environment, citizen-centric governance); **foreign affairs is NOT** a sector.
- **GGI 2021** (25 Dec 2021; **58 indicators**): **Gujarat** 1st, then Maharashtra, Goa; **AP 1st in agriculture and allied**.
~~ Sources: app notes 2:87

=== Governance Indices – World Bank, UNDP and Others
- **World Bank WGI** – since **1996**, 200+ countries; **DARPG** is India's nodal department; **six dimensions**: Voice and Accountability; Political Stability and Absence of Violence; Government Effectiveness; Regulatory Quality; Rule of Law; Control of Corruption. **HDI is NOT** a WGI dimension.
- **UNDP Human Development Index** – **three basic dimensions**: long and healthy life, knowledge (education), decent standard of living.
| Index | Publisher | Latest |
|---|---|---|
| Good Governance Index; GRAI | DARPG | GRAI June 2026 |
| **Public Affairs Index** | Public Affairs Centre, Bengaluru | **PAI 2018: Kerala** best-governed large State |
| BRAP rankings | DPIIT (not NITI) | – |
| SDG India Index; National MPI | NITI Aayog | – |
| Investment Friendliness Index 2026 | NITI Aayog | Gujarat 1st |
| V-Dem | University of Gothenburg | India 106th of 179 (2026) |
| Responsible Nations Index | WIF, JNU, IIM Mumbai | India 16th of 154 (2026) |
- **WGI and V-Dem** are perception-based (expert opinion).
- **India (WGI 2023):** moderate government effectiveness and rule of law; **low political stability**.
