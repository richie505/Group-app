=== Social Policy – Social Justice, Social Legislation, Social Protection
# Social justice
- **Social justice** – ending social and economic inequalities (Art. 38, 46). **John Rawls**: equal access to liberties, rights and opportunities **and** care for the interests of the deprived and disadvantaged.
- Lets the courts **uphold** laws protecting weaker sections, **reservations** for backward classes and **affirmative action** – **not** "unquestionable laws" for some people.
- Critics list **compulsory military service** and **state interference in private life** as negative repercussions of the **social justice** idea (state-driven equality).
- **Utilitarianism** ("greatest happiness of the greatest number") – founded by **Jeremy Bentham**; the classical basis of welfare policy.
- **World Day of Social Justice – 20 February**; 2019 theme: "**If you want Peace & Development, Work for Social Justice**".

# Term → Meaning
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Social policy | State's overall framework for social well-being |
| Social welfare | Targeted services for vulnerable – disabled, destitute children, widows |
| Social defence | Rehabilitation in deviant/vulnerable situations (drugs, beggary, juveniles) |
| **Social protection** | Policies and programmes that reduce poverty and vulnerability and **improve capacity to manage risks such as disability and old age** |
- **Welfare** = short-term, consumption; **development** = long-term, production.
- **RSBY** (Rashtriya Swasthya Bima Yojana) – health insurance, a **social protection programme** (NRHM, BRGF, SJSRY are not).
- Social development programmes: Mid-Day Meal, SSA, Rural Health Mission; NOT Look East Policy. **General health services for all** is NOT a targeted welfare service.
# Social legislation
- Aim: protect the vulnerable; minimum floors for dignified life; "**freedom of contract → fairness in contract**" – the aims question answer is "**all of the above**".

=== Policy Cycle – Formulation, Implementation, Monitoring, Evaluation
| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1. Agenda setting | Problem identified; media, advocacy, experts push it |
| 2. Formulation | Research, stakeholder consultation, **cost-benefit analysis**, impact assessment |
| 3. Adoption | Option formally chosen – **policy making is the major function of the Cabinet** (Centre and States) |
| 4. Implementation | Planning, resources, coordination |
| 5. Monitoring and evaluation | Data test whether goals are met; feedback |
| 6. Termination / revision | Failing policy ended or changed; policy learning goes back to agenda setting |
- **Monitoring** – keeping a **careful watch on specific events or activities** that form the main components of a programme, while it runs.
- **Formative evaluation** (internal) – judges a programme's worth **while its activities are forming / in progress**; **summative** – at the end, on outcomes.
- **Data analysis** – critical examination of the assembled and grouped data to study the characteristics of the object under study.
- **Communication and transparency** run through **every stage**.
- **DMEO** – apex M&E office under **NITI Aayog**; evaluates Central schemes; with AP's **ATRs** it marks the shift from announcement-based to **outcome-based governance**.
- **Gender budgeting** and **Outcome Budget** – both **2005-06**; **DBT** from **1 January 2013** (JAM – Jan Dhan, Aadhaar, Mobile).
- **Social audit** – **MGNREGA s.17**, by the **Gram Sabha**; AP statewide social audits **2006**, independent **SSAAT 2009**.
- **17 Sep 2026:** NITI Aayog (with WRI India) – second **India Electric Mobility Index**.
- AP example: GoM under **Kollu Ravindra** framing a new excise policy; AP **Action Taken Reports** on **seven white papers** (from 22 Jun 2026).

=== Welfare Ministries and the Evolution of the Welfare Approach
- **Ministry of Women and Social Welfare** – already in place by **31 Dec 1984** (MoS M. Chandra Sekhar, to Sep 1985); split in **1985** into the **Department of Women and Child Development** (under MHRD) and the Ministry of Welfare.
- **AP Social Welfare Fund** (aid to registered voluntary organisations in social welfare) – **1975**.
- **Ministry of Welfare (1985)** → renamed **Social Justice and Empowerment (1998)**; **Tribal Affairs** split off **1999**; **Minority Affairs** **29 January 2006**; **WCD** a separate ministry 2006.
| Ministry | Deals with |
|---|---|
| MoSJE | SC, OBC welfare; senior citizens; transgender welfare; Disabilities department |
| Tribal Affairs | STs, PESA, FRA, tribal education |
| Minority Affairs | Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Parsis, Jains (not Hindus) |
| WCD | Women, children, ICDS, Poshan |
- **Protection of Civil Rights Rules, 1977** – Central Ministries: **Home Affairs, Social Justice and Empowerment, Tribal Affairs**.
| Phase | Approach |
|---|---|
| 1950s–70s | State-led welfare; Central Social Welfare Board 1953 |
| 6th Plan | Women: welfare → development |
| 8th–9th Plans | Empowerment |
| 2000s | Rights-based laws |
| Now | "New welfarism" – targeted, tech-driven DBT |
- **ES 2025-26:** social protection covers **64.3%** (22% in 2016); spending **4.0% of GDP**.

=== Meaning and Definitions of Public Policy
- **Public policy** – planned state action to tackle public issues; the **bridge between political intent and administrative action**.
- Indian bases: Constitution, laws, **judicial interpretation** (e.g. **Right to Food** read into **Article 21**), NITI Aayog agendas, Budget.
| Thinker | Definition / idea |
|---|---|
| Thomas Dye | "Whatever governments choose to do or not to do" |
| David Easton | "Authoritative allocation of values for the whole society"; systems model |
| Harold Lasswell | Founder of the **policy sciences** |
| James Anderson | "Purposive course of action" by actors dealing with a problem |
