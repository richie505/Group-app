## SUB 0 | Social Policy – Social Justice, Social Legislation, Social Protection
[0:0] # Social justice
[0:1] - **Social justice** – ending social and economic inequalities (Art. 38, 46). **John Rawls**: equal access to liberties, rights and opportunities **and** care for the interests of the deprived and disadvantaged.
[0:2] - Lets the courts **uphold** laws protecting weaker sections, **reservations** for backward classes and **affirmative action** – **not** "unquestionable laws" for some people.
[0:3] - Critics list **compulsory military service** and **state interference in private life** as negative repercussions of the **social justice** idea (state-driven equality).
[0:4] - **Utilitarianism** ("greatest happiness of the greatest number") – founded by **Jeremy Bentham**; the classical basis of welfare policy.
[0:5] - **World Day of Social Justice – 20 February**; 2019 theme: "**If you want Peace & Development, Work for Social Justice**".
[0:6] # Welfare state and DPSPs

  [0:7.0] 38 | Social order of justice; reduce inequalities
  [0:7.1] 39 | Livelihood, equal pay, no concentration of wealth
  [0:7.2] 41 | Right to work, education, public assistance
  [0:7.3] 42 | Just and humane work conditions; maternity relief
  [0:7.4] 43 | Living wage for workers
  [0:7.5] 46 | Interests of SCs, STs, weaker sections
  [0:7.6] 47 | Nutrition, living standards, public health
  [0:7.7] Term | Meaning
  [0:7.8] Social policy | State's overall framework for social well-being
  [0:7.9] Social welfare | Targeted services for vulnerable – disabled, destitute children, widows
  [0:7.10] Social defence | Rehabilitation in deviant/vulnerable situations (drugs, beggary, juveniles)
  [0:7.11] Social protection | Policies and programmes that reduce poverty and vulnerability and improve capacity to manage risks such as disability and old age
[0:8] - **Welfare** = short-term, consumption; **development** = long-term, production.
[0:9] - **RSBY** (Rashtriya Swasthya Bima Yojana) – health insurance, a **social protection programme** (NRHM, BRGF, SJSRY are not).
[0:10] - Social development programmes: Mid-Day Meal, SSA, Rural Health Mission; NOT Look East Policy. **General health services for all** is NOT a targeted welfare service.

## SUB 1 | Policy Cycle – Formulation, Implementation, Monitoring, Evaluation
[1:0] TABLE head: Stage | What happens
  [1:0.0] 1. Agenda setting | Problem identified; media, advocacy, experts push it
  [1:0.1] 2. Formulation | Research, stakeholder consultation, cost-benefit analysis, impact assessment
  [1:0.2] 3. Adoption | Option formally chosen – policy making is the major function of the Cabinet (Centre and States)
  [1:0.3] 4. Implementation | Planning, resources, coordination
  [1:0.4] 5. Monitoring and evaluation | Data test whether goals are met; feedback
  [1:0.5] 6. Termination / revision | Failing policy ended or changed; policy learning goes back to agenda setting
[1:1] - **Monitoring** – keeping a **careful watch on specific events or activities** that form the main components of a programme, while it runs.
[1:2] - **Formative evaluation** (internal) – judges a programme's worth **while its activities are forming / in progress**; **summative** – at the end, on outcomes.
[1:3] - **Data analysis** – critical examination of the assembled and grouped data to study the characteristics of the object under study.
[1:4] - **Communication and transparency** run through **every stage**.
[1:5] - **DMEO** – apex M&E office under **NITI Aayog**; evaluates Central schemes.
[1:6] - **Gender budgeting** and **Outcome Budget** – both **2005-06**; **DBT** from **1 January 2013** (JAM – Jan Dhan, Aadhaar, Mobile).
[1:7] - **Social audit** – **MGNREGA s.17**, by the **Gram Sabha**; AP statewide social audits **2006**, independent **SSAAT 2009**.
[1:8] - **17 Sep 2026:** NITI Aayog (with WRI India) – second **India Electric Mobility Index**.
[1:9] - AP example: GoM under **Kollu Ravindra** framing a new excise policy; AP **Action Taken Reports** on **seven white papers** (from 22 Jun 2026).

## SUB 3 | Meaning and Definitions of Public Policy
[3:0] - **Public policy** – planned state action to tackle public issues; the **bridge between political intent and administrative action**.
[3:1] - Indian bases: Constitution, laws, **judicial interpretation** (e.g. **Right to Food** read into **Article 21**), NITI Aayog agendas, Budget.
