## SUB 1 | Features of Good Governance – UN/UNDP
[1:0] - The **8 attributes:** **consensus oriented, participatory, rule of law, effective and efficient, accountable, transparent, responsive, equitable and inclusive**.
[1:1] - UNDP's **three most critical**: transparency, accountability, responsiveness.
[1:2] - **NOT** features: **religiosity and emotionality**, **red tapism**, administrative **centralisation**, secrecy.
[1:3] - **Centralisation** may **reduce efficiency** of service delivery for lack of local adaptation to diverse contexts.
[1:4] TABLE head: Pillar | Meaning
  [1:4.0] Ethos | Service
  [1:4.1] Ethics | Honesty and integrity
  [1:4.2] Equity | Empathy for weaker sections
  [1:4.3] Efficiency | Speedy delivery using ICT
[1:5] - Tools: **people's participation** (most basic), RTI, Sevottam, Citizen's Charters, e-governance.
[1:6] > Exam angle: "which is NOT a feature" – red tapism, religiosity, centralisation.
[1:7] ~~ Sources: app notes 2:87; Jharkhand PCS 2016, 2021 keys
## SUB 2 | Meaning of Governance – Definitions and Origin
[2:0] TABLE head: Body | Definition / stress
  [2:0.0] World Bank | "The manner in which power is exercised in the management of a country's economic and social resources for development"
  [2:0.1] UNDP (1997) | Exercise of economic, political and administrative authority to manage a country's affairs at all levels
  [2:0.2] UNESCAP | Efficiency, effectiveness, equity, sustainability
[2:1] - **"Good governance"** was first brought into development discourse by the **World Bank** (1989 report on Sub-Saharan Africa).
[2:2] - **Governance is broader than government**: **state + market + civil society**.
[2:3] - **Democracy** helps development by encouraging **citizen participation and accountability**.
[2:4] - **Aristotle**: "A good citizen makes a good state and a bad citizen a bad state".
[2:5] ~~ Sources: app notes 2:87; general knowledge (Aristotle)
## SUB 3 | Good Governance Day and the Good Governance Index
[3:0] - **Good Governance Day – 25 December**, birthday of **Atal Bihari Vajpayee**; first observed in **2014**.
[3:1] - **GGI** – launched by **DARPG** on Good Governance Day 2019; ranks States, UTs, districts; **10 sectors** (agriculture, commerce and industry, HRD, public health, public infrastructure, economic governance, social welfare, judicial and public security, environment, citizen-centric governance); **foreign affairs is NOT** a sector.
[3:2] - **GGI 2021** (25 Dec 2021; **58 indicators**): **Gujarat** 1st, then Maharashtra, Goa; **AP 1st in agriculture and allied**.
[3:3] ~~ Sources: app notes 2:87
## SUB 4 | Governance Indices – World Bank, UNDP and Others
[4:0] - **World Bank WGI** – since **1996**, 200+ countries; **DARPG** is India's nodal department; **six dimensions**: Voice and Accountability; Political Stability and Absence of Violence; Government Effectiveness; Regulatory Quality; Rule of Law; Control of Corruption. **HDI is NOT** a WGI dimension.
[4:1] - **UNDP Human Development Index** – **three basic dimensions**: long and healthy life, knowledge (education), decent standard of living.
[4:2] TABLE head: Index | Publisher | Latest
  [4:2.0] Good Governance Index; GRAI | DARPG | GRAI June 2026
  [4:2.1] Public Affairs Index | Public Affairs Centre, Bengaluru | PAI 2018: Kerala best-governed large State
  [4:2.2] BRAP rankings | DPIIT (not NITI) | –
  [4:2.3] SDG India Index; National MPI | NITI Aayog | –
  [4:2.4] Investment Friendliness Index 2026 | NITI Aayog | Gujarat 1st
  [4:2.5] V-Dem | University of Gothenburg | India 106th of 179 (2026)
  [4:2.6] Responsible Nations Index | WIF, JNU, IIM Mumbai | India 16th of 154 (2026)
[4:3] - **WGI and V-Dem** are perception-based (expert opinion).
[4:4] - **India (WGI 2023):** moderate government effectiveness and rule of law; **low political stability**.
[4:5] ~~ Sources: Ramesh Singh, Indian Economy (src/economy/ramesh-singh-indian-economy-03.txt) p.55 (HDI); app notes 2:87
