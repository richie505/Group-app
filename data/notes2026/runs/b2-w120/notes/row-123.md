=== MGNREGA and the VB-G RAM G Act
| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| NREGA passed by Parliament | **August 2005** (assent September 2005) |
| In force | **2 February 2006**, launched in **Anantapur** district (AP); all districts from 1 April 2008 |
| Renamed MGNREGA | **2 October 2009** |
| VB-G RAM G Act, 2025 | Passed 18 Dec 2025; **in force 1 July 2026, replacing MGNREGA** |
# Provisions (MGNREGA)
- **Legal right** to at least **100 days** of unskilled wage work in a **financial year** per **rural household**; **adult members of any rural household** willing to do unskilled manual work are eligible (not only SC/ST/BPL).
- Aim: guarantee the **"Right to Work"**; livelihood security, durable assets, **strengthening the livelihood resource base of the poor**, **proactive social inclusion**, **strengthening PRIs** – not higher education.
- Work within **5 km**; no work within **15 days** of demand → **unemployment allowance**.
- At least **one-third** of workers must be **women** (not half).
- **Gram Sabha:** recommends works, monitors them, holds **social audits**; **Gram Panchayat** registers households, **receives work applications and issues dated receipts**.
- Follows the **Directive Principles** (Art. 41); the right to work is **not** in Part III (Fundamental Rights).
# VB-G RAM G Act, 2025 (now)
- **Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin)**; Ministry of Rural Development.
- **125 days** per rural household per year; wage floor **₹300/day** (2026-27); funding **60:40** (NE/Himalayan 90:10).
- **AP ranked first** in implementation (2.10 crore person-days in the first 45 days).

=== Poverty and Employment Programmes – Chronology
| Year | Programme | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| 1972 | **Maharashtra EGS** | **First** employment guarantee scheme (state level) |
| 1978-79 / 2 Oct 1980 | **IRDP** | Self-employment through subsidy and credit for rural BPL |
| 15 Aug 1979 | TRYSEM | Training rural youth for self-employment; component of IRDP |
| 1980 | **NREP** | Rural wage employment (replaced Food for Work) |
| 1982-83 | DWCRA | Sub-scheme of IRDP so that benefits reach rural women |
| 1989 | **JRY** | Merged **NREP and RLEGP**; wage employment |
| **1 April 1999** | **SGSY** | Merged IRDP, TRYSEM, DWCRA (and others); SHG-based self-employment |
| 2005 / 2006 | NREGA | Legal guarantee of wage work |
| 2008 | PMEGP | Merged PMRY and REGP; credit-linked subsidy through **KVIC** |
| 2011 | **NRLM "Aajeevika"** (DAY-NRLM from 2016) | **Restructured SGSY**; SHG livelihoods |
- **Order:** IRDP → NREP → JRY → SGSY → NRLM. DAY-NRLM restructured **SGSY**, NOT JRY.

=== Social Exclusion
- **Meaning:** a **process** that shuts people out of **facilities, benefits and opportunities** that others enjoy; it is **involuntary and systematic** (NCERT).
- Term coined by **René Lenoir** (France, **1974**).
| Basis | Example |
|---|---|
| Caste | Untouchability against SCs |
| Gender | Denial of property, work, mobility |
| Race / tribe | Adivasis in remote areas, racial discrimination |
| Disability | Lack of access to schools, jobs; **segregated special schools** |
| Religion / region | Minorities; backward, remote areas |
- **All of caste, gender, race and tribe** are bases of exclusion (TSPSC 2018).
- **Practices leading to exclusion:** **untouchability**, **segregation** (e.g. a **special school for the blind** instead of inclusive schooling), denied access to **land, water and jobs**. MGNREGA wage work is a remedy, not a practice of exclusion.
- **Results:** hurts **human-capital development**, **widens inequalities**, drives **social unrest and crime** – it does **not** bridge historical divisions (TSPSC 2023).
# Constitutional remedies
- **Reservations** for SCs, STs, OBCs aim at **social justice and equal opportunity**.
- **Art. 46 (DPSP):** promotion of **educational and economic interests** of SCs, STs and other weaker sections.
- **10 Aug 2026:** the Union **opposes a creamy layer for SCs/STs**, since income alone does not end caste-based exclusion.

=== Poverty – Concepts, Causes and Indicators
| Concept | Meaning | Measured by |
|---|---|---|
| Absolute poverty | Spending below the minimum for basic needs | A fixed poverty line (MPCE cut-off) |
| Relative poverty | Being poor **compared with others in the same society** | Inequality / share of income |
| Multidimensional poverty | Deprivation in health, education, living standards | National MPI (NITI Aayog) |
- India's official poverty line is **consumption-based (MPCE)**, not income-based.
- **MPCE 2011-12 (NSS 68th round, MMRP):** about **₹1,430 rural** and **₹2,630 urban** – a gap of about **₹1,200** (urban ~84% higher).
- **Causes (NextGen):** **illiteracy and lack of quality education** (main cause for rural and tribal poor), unemployment, disguised unemployment in agriculture, discrimination, health costs, inequality.
- **Social indicators:** illiteracy, malnutrition, poor health care, lack of safe drinking water and sanitation; **size of the house is NOT** one.
- **Culture of poverty:** **Oscar Lewis (1959)** – fatalism and poverty passed down generations.

=== Measurement – Agencies, Surveys and the National MPI
| Body / survey | Role |
|---|---|
| **NITI Aayog** (earlier Planning Commission) | **Nodal agency** for poverty estimation; uses NSSO data |
| NSSO (now NSO, MoSPI) | HCES about **every 5 years**; MPCE sets the line |
| **PLFS** (NSO) | The standard for unemployment data |
| **National MPI** | **NITI Aayog + UNDP + OPHI** |
- **PLFS status:** usual status = reference **year**; **CWS = 7 days**; **CDS** = each day of the reference week.
- **National MPI:** **3 equally weighted dimensions** (health, education, standard of living), **12 indicators**; standard-of-living indicators: cooking fuel, sanitation, drinking water, housing, electricity, assets, **bank accounts** (nutrition is a **health** indicator).

=== Unemployment – Types and Data
| Type | Meaning | Predominant area |
|---|---|---|
| Disguised | More workers than needed; extra workers add near-zero output | **Agriculture** (family farms) |
| Seasonal | Work only in some months | **Agriculture** |
| Structural | Mismatch between skills and jobs | Whole economy |
| Cyclical | Fall in labour demand in a slowdown | Industry and services |
| Frictional | Temporary, while changing or searching for jobs | Urban labour market |
| Educated | Joblessness among the educated | **Urban areas** |
- Disguised and seasonal = rural/agriculture; educated and frictional = urban.

=== Poverty by Social Group; Informal Labour
- **Scheduled Tribes** have the **highest poverty**, followed by **Scheduled Castes** (ST > SC > others); cause: **discrimination and social exclusion**, landlessness, remoteness.
- Poverty, unemployment and exclusion **reinforce each other**; responses: MGNREGA/VB-G RAM G, SHG self-employment (SGSY, DAY-NRLM), protective measures for SCs/STs.
