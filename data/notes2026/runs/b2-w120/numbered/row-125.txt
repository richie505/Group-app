## SUB 1 | Article 21 Expanded by the Courts
[1:0] - **A.K. Gopalan (1950):** narrow – protection only against **executive** action. **Maneka Gandhi (1978):** procedure must be **fair, just and reasonable** – protection against **arbitrary executive and legislative action**; life includes **living with human dignity**.
[1:1] - **Art. 21 statements (NDA 2014) – all true:** available to **citizens and aliens**; covers arbitrary executive and legislative action; includes **human dignity**; can be taken away only **according to procedure established by law**.
[1:2] - **Rights read into Art. 21 (Laxmikanth list):** human dignity; **decent environment incl. pollution-free water and air**; livelihood; privacy; shelter; **health**; free education up to 14; legal aid; speedy trial; against custodial harassment; **information** (also from 19(1)(a)); and more.

## SUB 2 | UDHR in the Preamble, DPSPs and Duties; Treaties in Indian Law
[2:0] - **UDHR is reflected in the Preamble** (justice, liberty, equality, dignity), **DPSPs** (work, living wage, education, health) and **Fundamental Duties** (UDHR Art. 29: duties to the community) – **all three** (UPSC 2020).

## SUB 3 | Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993 – Key Sections
[3:0] - **Ordinance** promulgated by the President under **Art. 123** on **28 Sept 1993**; Act **deemed in force from 28 Sept 1993** (assent **Jan 1994**), extends to the whole of India; **NHRC constituted 12 Oct 1993**.
[3:1] - Three-tier set-up: **NHRC** (national) → **SHRC** (state) → **Human Rights Courts** (district).
[3:2] TABLE head: Section | Provision
  [3:2.0] 2(1)(a) | Armed forces = naval, military and air forces + other armed forces of the Union; armed forces of the State (state armed police) NOT included
  [3:2.1] 2(1)(d) | Human rights = rights relating to life, liberty, equality and dignity guaranteed by the Constitution or embodied in the International Covenants and enforceable by Indian courts
  [3:2.2] 2(1)(f) | International Covenants = ICCPR + ICESCR
  [3:2.3] 2(1)(m) | Public servant = as in Sec. 21 IPC (now Sec. 2(28) BNS, from 1 July 2024)
  [3:2.4] 3 / 21 | NHRC / SHRC
  [3:2.5] 13 | Powers of a civil court (CPC 1908) in inquiries; deemed civil court for offences under IPC 175, 178, 179, 180, 228
  [3:2.6] 30 | Human Rights Court: a Court of Session in each district, notified by the State Govt with the HC Chief Justice's concurrence
  [3:2.7] 31 | Special Public Prosecutor: an advocate of at least 7 years' practice
  [3:2.8] 39 | Members and officers deemed public servants
[3:3] - **Rocket's "Section 2(m) = armed forces" is a slip:** armed forces = 2(1)(a); 2(1)(m) = public servant.
[3:4] - Proceedings are judicial proceedings for IPC **193** (false evidence) and **228** (insulting a public servant in a judicial proceeding).
