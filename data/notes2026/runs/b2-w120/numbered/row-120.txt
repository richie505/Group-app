## SUB 2 | AP Reorganisation Act, 2014
[2:0] - **Appointed day: 2 June 2014** – AP and Telangana became separate States (Telangana = 29th State); **Hyderabad** common capital for up to **10 years**.
[2:1] - **Sec. 46(2)-(3):** backward-areas package; **Sec. 94:** tax incentives.
[2:2] - **7 backward districts** = 4 Rayalaseema + 3 North Andhra; grant of **₹50 crore per district per year**.
[2:3] - **SCS:** PM promised in Rajya Sabha (**Feb 2014**); **14th Finance Commission** ended the general/special distinction → "**special assistance measure**"; demand still a political issue.
[2:4] ~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. Part 1 l.4683; app notes 2:120 sub 4
## SUB 3 | Mulki Rules, Six Point Formula and Movements
[3:0] - **Jai Andhra movement (1972):** reaction to the Supreme Court **upholding Mulki rules**; sought separate Andhra; started in Coastal Andhra and Rayalaseema.
[3:1] - **Six Point Formula (21 Sept 1973)** → **32nd Amendment, 1973** inserted **Article 371D** – the **special provision for Andhra Pradesh** (zonal system of local cadres, education and employment) and **371E**; ended the Regional Committee (Art. 371(1)).
[3:2] - **8-Point Formula (1969):** set up a high-level committee under a judge and a special committee on Telangana surpluses.
[3:3] - **Telangana movements:** 1969 agitation; 2009-14 phase → Telangana State 2014.
[3:4] - **⚠ Trap:** Capital-location committee came under the **2014 Act**, not the 8-Point Formula.
[3:5] ~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. Part 2 l.18556 (Art. 371D); app notes 2:120 sub 2

## SUB 5 | Historical Agreements
[5:0] - **Sri Bagh Pact (16 Nov 1937):** first Coastal-Rayalaseema accord; drafted by **Pattabhi Sitaramayya**; Rayalaseema to choose **either the capital or the High Court**; aimed to raise Rayalaseema's farm and economic level to coastal districts. Andhra State capital → **Kurnool** (1953).
[5:1] - **Gentlemen's Agreement – 1956** (not 1969): reused the Sri Bagh template for Telangana safeguards.
[5:2] - Such pacts institutionalised sub-regional fault lines; poor implementation fed agitations.
[5:3] ~~ Sources: app notes 2:120 sub 1
