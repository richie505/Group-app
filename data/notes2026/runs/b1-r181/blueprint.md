## 3. Trends and notes

- Leader → centre recall dominates (about a third of all PYQs); Bihar/Kunwar Singh alone is asked 11 times, mostly by UPPCS/BPSC.
- Historiography is asked as quotation → person (Majumdar, Savarkar, Outram-Taylor, Aitchison, Coupland) – never as an essay-type judgement in prelims.
- APPSC's own angle is the Andhra/Hyderabad response (Dandasena, Subba Reddy, Turrebaz Khan, Peer Saheb, Vizag "Mohammad Patnam"); fuller treatment belongs to row 187.
- Printed-key problems found: Satara missing from the options; Uttarakhand 2025 code wrong (Mangal Pandey was hanged on 8 April, not 29 March); Bakht Khan's arrival (1 July, not 3 July); Tantia Tope hanged 18 April 1859 (19 April printed); PMF prints 15 April and its practice key for the Azamgarh Proclamation ("Hindu-Muslim divide") contradicts its own text.
- Heavy in the old notes but never asked: the long list of non-1857 books (Gandhi, Bose, Vajpayee …), Subsidiary Alliance year table, Uda Devi / Azizun, White Mutiny, RIN revolt link – kept short only where own MCQs need them.
