=== Backward Classes Commissions – Kalelkar, Mandal, Rohini
# The three commissions (all under Article 340)
| Feature | First – Kaka Kalelkar | Second – B.P. Mandal | Justice G. Rohini |
|---|---|---|---|
| Set up | **29 January 1953** by the President (**Nehru** Govt); opened 18 March 1953 | Decided **1978** (Janata Govt of **Morarji Desai**), commission constituted **1979**; through the **Ministry of Home Affairs** | **October 2017**, Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment |
| Report | **30 March 1955** | **1980** | **31 July 2023** to the President |
| Castes | **2,399** backward castes (837 "most backward") | **3,743** backward castes | ~3,000 Central-list OBC castes examined |
| Key point | **First** to use **social and educational** criteria; recommended a caste-based census and reservations | **11 indicators** (4 social, 3 educational, 4 economic); OBCs = **52%**; **27%** quota | **Sub-categorisation** of OBCs into **3-4 sub-groups** |
| Outcome | **Not implemented** (rejected 1961) | Implemented by the **V.P. Singh** (National Front) Govt, **August 1990**; 27% central posts from 1993 | Report not public |
- **First Backward Classes Commission** chairman = **Kaka Kalelkar** (D.B. = Dattatreya Balkrishna Kalelkar), 1953 – asked 7 times; distractors B.P. Mandal, B.R. Ambedkar, Jagjivan Ram.
- **Second Backward Classes Commission** (report 1980) chairman = **B.P. Mandal** (**Bindheshwari Prasad Mandal**, an MP) – the "Mandal Commission"; set up by the **Janata** Government under **Morarji Desai** (not Janata Dal, not V.P. Singh, who only implemented it).
- **Year set up:** NextGen and most keys say **1978** (the Janata Govt's decision, December 1978); Laxmikanth says "in 1979" (formal constitution). An exam offering only 1978 → **1978**.
- **Mandal criteria** (examples): castes **considered socially backward by others**; castes **mainly dependent on manual labour**; castes where **women's work participation is at least 25% above the State average** – "stimulus stigma or impact" is **not** a criterion.
- Mandal **mandate**: identify the socially and educationally backward; dealt with **reservation in government employment** → basis of the **27% OBC quota**.
- **Rohini findings:** ~**97%** of benefits taken by **25%** of sub-castes; ~**1,000** communities with zero representation; 14 extensions. It examined **OBCs**, not STs.
# Other bodies
| Body | Fact |
|---|---|
| Justice **R.N. (Ram Nandan) Prasad** Expert Committee | **First laid down the creamy-layer criteria** (report 1993, accepted → DoPT OM, September 1993) |
| M. Veerappa Moily Oversight Committee (2006) | OBC quota through **expansion of seats** (~54%, phased) so general seats did not shrink |

> Trap: Mandal was set up through **Home Affairs**, Rohini through **Social Justice**; Kalelkar's report was never implemented, Mandal's was (1990).

=== Constitutional Basis – Articles 15, 16, 335, 338, 340
| Article | Content | Added by |
|---|---|---|
| 15(4) | Special provisions for SEBCs, SCs, STs | 1st CAA, 1951 |
| 15(5) | Reservation in admission incl. private aided/unaided; **minority institutions excluded** | 93rd CAA, 2005 |
| 15(6) / 16(6) | **EWS** in education / posts | 103rd CAA, 2019 |
| **16(4)** | Reservation of **posts** for **backward classes** | **Original Constitution** |
| **16(4A)** | Reservation in **promotion** for **SCs/STs** | 77th CAA, 1995 |
| 16(4B) | Backlog vacancies as a separate class | 81st CAA, 2000 |
| 335 proviso | Relaxed marks for SCs/STs in promotion | 82nd CAA, 2000 |
| 338B | National Commission for Backward Classes | 102nd CAA, 2018 |
| 342A | SEBC lists | 102nd CAA, 2018 |
| 366(26C) | Defines SEBCs by reference to Art. 342A | 102nd CAA, 2018 |
| **340** | **President appoints a commission** to investigate the conditions of backward classes and recommend remedies | Original |
| 341 / 342 | SC / ST lists | Original |
- **"Backward Classes" is NOT defined** in the Constitution (APPSC G2: the "defined" statement is the wrong one).
- Correct term: **Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (SEBC)** – not "Socially and Economically".
- **Art. 16(4)** backward classes are **mainly social**; they need not be both socially and educationally backward (Indra Sawhney) – identified mainly by **caste and social backwardness**.
- **Art. 338(10)** (before 2018): references to SCs included such backward classes as the **President** specified – BC complaints went to the SC Commission.
- For the CEI Act, OBCs are the classes "so determined by the **Central Government**".
- **Protective discrimination** = e.g. **reservation of posts for STs**; SHG aid or minority scholarships are welfare schemes; one-region-only jobs would be plain discrimination.
- **First reservation in India:** **Kolhapur State, 1902**, under **Shahu Maharaj** (for non-Brahmins and backward classes).
> Number game: Art. 340 = commission (Kalelkar, Mandal, Rohini all under it); 338B = NCBC; 342A = SEBC list; NHRC is statutory, not constitutional.

=== Landmark Cases – Indra Sawhney to Janhit Abhiyan
# Indra Sawhney v. Union of India (1992, "Mandal case")
- **Caste** can be the main criterion of backwardness (overruled Balaji on this); **upheld 27%** OBC quota.
- Total reservation **not above 50%** except in **extraordinary cases**; applied **year by year**; **carry-forward** allowed within 50%.
- **Creamy layer** excluded from OBCs (the concept originates here).
- Reservation only at **initial appointment, not promotion** → answered by the **77th** Amendment.
- Directed a **permanent statutory body** for OBC lists → **NCBC** (1993).
| Case | Year | Issue decided |
|---|---|---|
| State of Madras v. Champakam Dorairajan | 1951 | Caste-based quota struck down → 1st Amendment, Art. 15(4) |
| M.R. Balaji v. State of Mysore | 1962 | Reservation generally within 50%; caste not the sole test |
| P.A. Inamdar | 2005 | No quota in private unaided institutions → undone by 93rd |
| M. Nagaraj | 2006 | Upheld 77th/81st/82nd/85th; promotion quota needs data on **backwardness, inadequacy and efficiency** |
| **Ashoka Kumar Thakur v. UoI** | 2008 | Upheld the **93rd Amendment** and **27% OBC quota** in central educational institutions (CEI Act); creamy layer applies to OBCs |
| Ram Singh v. UoI | 2015 | **Jats** struck out of the Central OBC list |
| Jarnail Singh | 2018 | Creamy layer can apply to SC/ST promotions |
| Jaishri Patil (Maratha case) | 5 May 2021 | Maratha SEBC quota struck down (breach of 50%); only Centre identifies SEBCs → reversed by 105th |
| Janhit Abhiyan | 7 Nov 2022 | EWS upheld |
| Union of India v. Rohith Nathan | 11 March 2026 | Parental salary alone cannot place a candidate in the creamy layer |
- **OBC quota timeline:** 27% central jobs from **1993**; **CEI (Reservation in Admission) Act, 2006** – 27% OBC seats, in force **2007**.

=== Creamy Layer and EWS Reservation
# Creamy layer
- **Status-based exclusions**: children of constitutional post-holders, **Class-I officers**, parents **both Class-II** officers, senior armed-forces officers, large landowners, high earners – **not all OBC children** are excluded.
| Year | Income limit (per annum) |
|---|---|
| 1993 | ₹1 lakh |
| 2004 | ₹2.5 lakh |
| 2008 | ₹4.5 lakh |
| 2013 | ₹6 lakh |
| 2017 | **₹8 lakh** (still current) |
- **SC/ST creamy layer debate:** the Union Government opposed it (August 2026) – SC/ST status flows from **Arts. 341 and 342**.
# EWS – 103rd Amendment (2019)
- **Up to 10%** for economically weaker sections **not covered** by SC/ST/OBC quotas; **over and above** existing reservation (outside the 50% cap).
- In jobs and education, including **private** institutions; **enabling, not mandatory** for States.
- Criteria: family income **below ₹8 lakh**; agricultural land below **5 acres**; flat below **1,000 sq ft**.
- **First State to implement** the 10% EWS quota: **Gujarat**, from **14 January 2019**.
- **Janhit Abhiyan v. Union of India (2022):** upheld **3:2** by a **five-judge** bench; dissent: **CJI U.U. Lalit** and **Justice S. Ravindra Bhat**.

=== Andhra Pradesh – BC Groups and Muslim Quota
| Group | Who | Quota |
|---|---|---|
| BC-A | Aboriginal tribes, vimukta jatis, nomadic groups | 7% |
| BC-B | Vocational / occupational groups | 10% |
| BC-C | SC converts to Christianity | 1% |
| BC-D | Other classes | 7% |
| BC-E | Socially and educationally backward **Muslims** (2007 Act) | **4%** |
- Combined BC-A to BC-E = **29%**.
- **Muslim quota:** AP was the State that **proposed/ordered Muslim reservation**: **5%** (2004, 2005) struck down by the AP HC; **2007 Act – 4%** struck down by the AP HC in 2010, but the **Supreme Court's interim order** lets it continue pending a Constitution Bench.
- **Sukhdev case** (AP HC): caste alone cannot be the basis → **112 classes** listed on poverty, residence and occupation.
- **Anantharaman Commission**: **first AP BC Commission**, report **1970**; framed the **A-D grouping** and a **25%** quota. **Muralidhara Rao Commission**: took charge 27 Jan 1982; reviewed BC reservation and inclusion of minorities.
- **Kapus**: Justice **Manjunatha Commission (2016)** → 2017 Bill giving **5% as BC-F**; never approved by the Centre.
- **Local bodies:** **NTR** introduced a **20%** BC quota in **1984**; raised to 34%; cut to **24.15%** under GOs **176, 559, 560**. Supreme Court **triple test**: dedicated commission + local-body-wise proportion + total within **50%** (no NCBC approval needed).
