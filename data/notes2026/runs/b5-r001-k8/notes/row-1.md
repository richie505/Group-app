- **Cyanocobalamin** = the synthetic, cobalt-containing form of **B12**.
# Functions asked
- **Vitamin D** = the **"sunshine vitamin"** (made in skin under sunlight); needed for **bones and teeth** (calcium absorption).
- **Vitamin C** = collagen formation (bones, teeth, skin, wound healing), iron absorption, antioxidant; **not stored**; the vitamin most **destroyed by heating**; lost by **oxidation** when cut fruit is exposed to air.
- **Vitamin K** = blood clotting; made by gut bacteria (with **B12**). Vitamin A is stored in the liver.
- **Heart disease risk:** high BP, raised glucose, raised lipids – **not** vitamin A deficiency.

=== Proteins, Amino Acids and Malnutrition
- **Proteins** = **body-building** nutrients (growth and repair; most important for growing children and athletes building muscle) – ~**4 kcal/g**; **amino acids** are their **building blocks** (20 common, ~9–10 essential; not stored).
- **Essential amino acids** (from food): histidine, **isoleucine**, leucine, lysine, **methionine**, phenylalanine, **threonine**, **tryptophan**, valine. **Non-essential:** **tyrosine**, alanine, aspartic acid, cysteine, glycine, **proline** (an amino acid).
- **Egg** = **highest biological value** – contains **all essential amino acids** in the right proportion.
| Protein | Found in |
|---|---|
| **Casein** (~80% of milk protein) | Milk (primary group); whey proteins the rest |
| **Gluten** | Wheat |
| **Zein** | Maize |
| **Keratin** | Hair, nails, horn |
| **Myosin** (with actin) | Muscle |
| Albumin | Egg white, blood |
| Collagen / fibroin | Connective tissue / silk |
- "**Only proteins**" pair: **keratin and myosin** (fructose, maltose and sucrose are sugars).
- **Lactoderm** = the sticky **protein film (skin) on heated milk**.
# Protein-energy malnutrition
| Disease | Cause | Sign |
|---|---|---|
| **Kwashiorkor** | **Protein** deficiency (calories adequate), children over 1 | **Oedema**, swollen belly, skin and hair changes |
| **Marasmus** | Severe lack of **both protein and calories**, infants | Extreme wasting, no oedema |
| **Marasmic kwashiorkor** | Deficiency of **both proteins and calories** with oedema | Wasting + oedema |
- Not PEM: scurvy (C), goitre (iodine), mumps (virus), achondroplasia (genetic dwarfism), rickets (D).
- **Protein per 100 g (increasing):** egg white (~11 g) < fish (~20 g) < Bengal gram dal (~21 g) < groundnut (~25 g) < **skimmed milk powder (~35 g)**; soybean is the **highest-protein grain** (~40 g).
- Nursing mother needs **65 g** protein a day.
- **Pepsin** (stomach) breaks proteins into **peptides**; trypsin works later in the small intestine.

=== Carbohydrates, Sugars, Fats and Food Groups
| Nutrient | Role | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Carbohydrate** | **Main energy-giving** food | Rice – highest carbohydrate share; **rice's major nutrient = carbohydrate** |
| **Fat** | Stored energy | ~**9.3 kcal/g** (most per gram); built from **fatty acids** + glycerol |
| **Protein** | Body-building | ~4 kcal/g |
| Vitamins, minerals | Regulatory, **micronutrients** | Protein, carbohydrate and fat = **macronutrients** (also for livestock) |
- **Starch** = a **polysaccharide** (glucose polymer); the **iodine test** turns starch **blue-black**.
- **Fructose** = **fruit sugar**, the **sweetest natural sugar**; gives fruits their sweet taste.
- **Honey:** mainly **fructose** (~38%) > glucose (~31%); sucrose and maltose minor; honey is not a colloid.
- **Relative sweetness (descending): fructose > sucrose > glucose > maltose** (> lactose).
- **Invert sugar** = glucose + fructose from **hydrolysis of sucrose**; **sweeter than sucrose** because of its free fructose (the hydrolysis is how it is made, not why it is sweet).
- **Sucrose** = glucose + fructose (disaccharide); maltose, lactose = disaccharides; glucose, fructose, ribose = monosaccharides.
- **Soybean** is rich in **all three** – protein (~40%), fat (~20%) and carbohydrate.
- Plants make the essential foods **carbohydrates, fats and proteins**; **nucleic acids** are genetic material, not a "food".
- **Oatmeal** = a **cereal-grain** product; sago and arrowroot are starches from stems/roots, soya flour is from a legume.

=== Food Preservation, Ripening, Adulterants and Toxins
# Preservatives and additives
| Preservative | Used in |
|---|---|
| **Sodium benzoate** / **benzoic acid** (E211) | Soft drinks, pickles, sauces (acidic foods) |

- **Food additives** = added **intentionally** to improve food (preservatives, colours, emulsifiers, sweeteners, flavour enhancers). **Adulteration** = adding inferior/harmful substances; causes poisoning, allergies, nutritional deficiency.
- **MSG (Ajinomoto)** = flavour enhancer, **umami**. Aspartame and stevia are sweeteners; fructose is a natural sugar, not an artificial sweetener.
- **Fortification:** iodised salt; rice with iron, folic acid, B12; oil/milk with A and D; FSSAI logo **"+F"**.

- **Jul 2026:** food-safety reforms – Food Safety on Wheels, blockchain batch tracing, "One Nation, One Regulator" ends dual **BIS/AGMARK** certification.
# Storage and freezing
- **Cold storage** works because low temperature **lowers the rate of respiration** of fruits.
- **IQF = Individually Quick Frozen** – each piece frozen separately and fast.
- **Rancidity** = **oxidation** of fats and oils (so check the manufacture date of fried foods); nitrogen-flushed chip packets prevent it.
- **Blackheart of potato** = **oxygen deficiency** in storage (an abiotic disorder).
# Ripening
- **Ethylene** = the gaseous plant hormone for **fruit ripening** (and the gas used for artificial ripening).
- **Calcium carbide** releases **acetylene**, which acts like ethylene – the chemical (illegally) used to ripen mangoes.

=== Human Body, Disorders and Health Technology
# Genetic and blood disorders
- **Thalassemia:** autosomal recessive; **reduced rate of synthesis of one of the globin chains** (α or β) of haemoglobin → anaemia (not X-linked, not a clotting defect, not phenylalanine build-up = PKU).
- **Haemophilia:** **X-linked recessive** defect of a **clotting factor** – a cut keeps bleeding.
- **Viruses** contain **nucleic acid** (DNA or RNA) in a protein coat; no enzymes for metabolism of their own.
- **Scorpion venom** toxins are **proteins** (peptides).
# Glands and digestion
| Gland | Hormone / fact |
|---|---|
| Pituitary (master) | At the base of the brain; GH, TSH, ACTH, ADH; oxytocin released here, made in hypothalamus |
| Pineal | In the brain (epithalamus); **melatonin** – biological clock; not on the kidney |
| **Thyroid** (largest endocrine) | **Thyroxine (T4, T3) contains iodine**; calcitonin; deficiency: cretinism, goitre |
| Thymus | Behind the sternum; trains **T cells** |
| Parathyroid | Back of thyroid; PTH – blood calcium |
| Adrenal (above kidneys) | Adrenaline, cortisol, aldosterone |
| Pancreas | Insulin (beta cells) lowers blood sugar; glucagon (alpha) raises it |
| Testes | Sperm and **testosterone** |
| Kidney | **Erythropoietin** (RBC production) |
- **Plant hormone** among options = **cytokinin** (cell division); thyroxine, oestrogen, testosterone are animal hormones.
- Type 1 diabetes = **low insulin**; type 2 = **insulin resistance**.
- **Stomach HCl:** from **parietal (oxyntic) cells**; pH ~1.5–3.5; activates **pepsinogen → pepsin**.
- **Cattle:** **pepsin** is made in the **abomasum** (the "true stomach"); rumen, reticulum and omasum make no gastric juice.
- **Blood:** **neutrophils** = most abundant granulocytes; Landsteiner – ABO groups (1901); **O negative = universal donor**, AB = universal recipient; natural pacemaker = **SA node** (right atrium).
- **Liver:** largest gland; **urea is made in the liver** (not the kidney); **regrows** after partial removal → living-donor transplants.
# Kidney and dialysis
- Urine ~1.5 L/day, **pH ~6.0**; normal BP **120/80 mmHg**.
- **Dialysis** removes wastes and excess water, but **cannot reabsorb nutrients** (glucose, amino acids) as the kidney tubules do; haemodialysis (machine) vs **peritoneal** dialysis (peritoneum as filter).
