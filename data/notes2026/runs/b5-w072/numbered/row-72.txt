## SUB 0 | Solar Parks – Concepts and the Largest Parks
[0:0] - **Solar Park Scheme** (MNRE, **12 December 2014**): at least 25 parks / ultra mega projects; CFA **₹25 lakh per park** for DPR + up to **₹20 lakh/MW or 30%** of cost.
[0:1] - **Ultra Mega Solar Park = 500 MW or more** at one location – not 1,000 MW.
[0:2] - **Largest solar park of India (2023):** **Bhadla Solar Park, Rajasthan** (~2,245 MW) – ahead of Pavagada (Karnataka, ~2,050 MW), Kurnool and Ananthapuram (APPSC DEO 2023; APPSC FBO 2025).
[0:3] - **Kurnool Ultra Mega Solar Park (1,000 MW):** once the **world's largest single-location solar park** (2017).
[0:4] - **Floating solar:** India ~0.7 GW vs **102 GW** potential; largest = **Omkareshwar** (278 MW, Narmada, **Madhya Pradesh**).
