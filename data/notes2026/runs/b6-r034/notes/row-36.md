=== GST 2.0 – 56th GST Council (September 2025)
# The meeting and the new structure
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Meeting | 56th GST Council, 3 September 2025; a 10.5-hour meeting |
| Chair | Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman |
| Revenue Secretary | Arvind Shrivastava |
| State members named | Bihar Deputy CM Samrat Choudhary, UP FM Suresh Khanna |
| Decision | Unanimous |
| New slabs | 0 / 5 / 18 / 40%; 12% and 28% abolished (some special rates below 5%) |
| Effective from | 22 September 2025 |
| Name given by PM Modi | "GST Bachat Utsav" |
| 40% slab (sin/demerit goods) | Tobacco, cigarettes, paan masala, sugary drinks, online real-money gaming |
| Nil GST | Individual health and life insurance, reinsurance, life-saving (cancer) drugs |
- Earlier structure: GST introduced **2017**; slabs 5, 12, 18, 28% plus compensation cess (with 0%).
- **Compensation cess** largely ended; it continues on **tobacco** until the loans are repaid.
- Aim: correct the **inverted duty structure**.
- Revenue impact: about **₹48,000 crore**; with the income-tax reforms, revenue foregone about **₹1 lakh crore**.
- Q1 nominal GDP grew **8.8%** against 10.1% projected; direct taxes **-4.3%**.
> **28% → 40%** for some goods shows the **merger of the compensation cess**, not a real hike.
# GoM on Rate Rationalisation (before the Council)
- GoM chaired by **Bihar Deputy CM Samrat Choudhary**; it **accepted the Centre's proposal**: keep 5% and 18%, remove 12% and 28%.
- **99%** of 12% items → 5%; **90%** of 28% items → 18%. Compensation cess to be abolished.
- Final say: the **GST Council** (chaired by the Union Finance Minister); it works by **consensus – voting is rare**.
- Average effective GST rate: **~11.5% → ~10%**.
- Revenue loss: ₹60,000–1,00,000 crore a year (0.2–0.3% of GDP); FY2025-26 about ₹45,000 crore.
- **18% slab** gives about **70%** of GST revenue – kept unchanged.
- More affected: manufacturing/service States (**Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu**; the 2018 rate cut caused a **3–4% monthly dip**). Less affected: agrarian States (**Bihar, UP, North-East**).
- Old compensation: **5 years from July 2017** through the compensation cess – **ended June 2022**.
- Maximum Indian exports (2018) went to the **European Union**.

=== Rupee, Forex and External Sector
# Rupee at record low
- **All-time low: ₹89.41 per USD on 21 November 2025**; rebounded to ₹89.22–89.23 (24–25 November).
- Causes: record **goods trade deficit ~USD 42 billion (October 2025)**; exports fell ~12%; **imports record ~USD 76 billion** (gold); **FPI selling**; **Japanese yen carry-trade unwind**; doubt over US trade deal.
- RBI extended **export proceeds realisation from 9 to 15 months**.
- **Forex reserves ~USD 690 billion**; net spot sales (Jan–Sep 2025) ~USD 20.1 billion; forward sales USD 53.4 bn (Aug) → 59.4 bn (Sep).
- RBI approach: **managed float** – curbs volatility, **does not target a level**.
- Forex reserve components: **FCA (largest)**, Gold, SDRs, Reserve Tranche Position (RTP); **held by RBI, owned by Government of India**.

# Payments (UPI) and cyber fraud
- **NPCI** (Global Fintech Fest, Mumbai, **October 2025**): UPI payments by **biometric authentication** (fingerprint/face) instead of PIN; **Aadhaar face authentication**; **UPI Lite on smart glasses**; cash withdrawal via **Micro ATMs**.

# Andhra Pradesh angle
- **US 26% tariff on aqua products** (President Donald Trump): AP shipments to US **halted within 24 hours**; competitor **Ecuador** faced only **10%**.
- India's seafood exports 2023-24: **US largest – 2,97,571 MT (34.53%)**; **AP ~33%** of India's seafood exports. AP exporters seek a **free trade agreement with the US**.
