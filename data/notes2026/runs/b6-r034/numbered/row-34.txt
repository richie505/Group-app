## SUB 1 | Energy Security – LPG, LNG, SPR, Kerosene, Ethanol
[1:0] # LPG Shortage and Storage Gap
[1:1] - India **second-largest LPG consumer**; ~3 million t/month (~80,000 t/day); 85%+ used by households.
[1:2] - Imports ~20 million t/year (tripled 2011–12 to 2024–25); dependency 47% (2015) → **~60%**; ~90% of imports via Hormuz → ~54% of supply vulnerable.
[1:3] - **33 crore** domestic connections (33.3 crore households); **~10 crore added after 2017 under PMUY**.
[1:4] - **Underground LPG caverns: 1.4 lakh t – less than two days** of consumption; MoPNG: no more caverns planned.
[1:5] - Response: LPG and CNG on priority list under Essential Commodities Act; refineries maximised LPG (domestic output +40%; share 40% → 55%); booking interval urban 25 days, rural 45 days; ~60 lakh households can shift to PNG; **US deal 2.2 million t LPG/year** (~45 days shipping); IEA release of ~400 million barrels.
[1:6] - NRAI warned ~60% of restaurants could shut; IEA flagged lack of storage.
[1:7] - Gas: ~50% imported, 55–60% of it from West Asia → ~30% of LNG affected; PNG/CNG prioritised over industry.
[1:8] - Storage options: depleted gas fields – **KG Basin, Cambay, Mumbai Offshore** (**Deccan Traps not** among them).
[1:9] - **Ethyl mercaptan** (sulphur compound) is added to LPG for leak detection.

[3:8] # Rupee Management
[3:9] - RBI curbs excess volatility; **no exchange-rate target**; India – **managed float**.
[3:10] - RBI **defends the rupee by selling dollars** (absorbs rupees); **RBI buying dollars expands money supply** (sterilisation may follow).
[3:11] - **Higher FDI inflows → rupee appreciates**. Weak rupee → **imported inflation**, higher external debt burden; export gain limited by import content and price-taker status.
[3:12] - Forex reserves = **foreign currency assets, gold, SDRs, reserve tranche in IMF**.
[3:13] - **Dornbusch Overshooting Model**: exchange rate overreacts in the short run (sticky prices).
[3:14] - **Impossible Trinity** (Mundell-Fleming): cannot have fixed exchange rate + free capital mobility + independent monetary policy together.
[3:15] - Expansionary policy does not stop a rupee slide; **Masala Bonds**, easier ECB help. CAD financing hard due to **negative net FDI** and volatile FPI.
[3:16] - **2013 Taper Tantrum**: rupee fell; RBI tightened short-term rates, oil-dollar swap; gold import duty raised.
[3:17] - **Fowler Committee (1898)**: Gold Exchange Standard – rupee pegged at **1s 4d** (15 rupees = £1).
