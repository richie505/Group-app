[0:7] > **28% → 40%** for some goods shows the **merger of the compensation cess**, not a real hike.
[0:8] # GoM on Rate Rationalisation (before the Council)
[0:9] - GoM chaired by **Bihar Deputy CM Samrat Choudhary**; it **accepted the Centre's proposal**: keep 5% and 18%, remove 12% and 28%.
[0:10] - **99%** of 12% items → 5%; **90%** of 28% items → 18%. Compensation cess to be abolished.
[0:11] - Final say: the **GST Council** (chaired by the Union Finance Minister); it works by **consensus – voting is rare**.
[0:12] - Average effective GST rate: **~11.5% → ~10%**.
[0:13] - Revenue loss: ₹60,000–1,00,000 crore a year (0.2–0.3% of GDP); FY2025-26 about ₹45,000 crore.
[0:14] - **18% slab** gives about **70%** of GST revenue – kept unchanged.
[0:15] - More affected: manufacturing/service States (**Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu**; the 2018 rate cut caused a **3–4% monthly dip**). Less affected: agrarian States (**Bihar, UP, North-East**).
[0:16] - Old compensation: **5 years from July 2017** through the compensation cess – **ended June 2022**.
[0:17] - Maximum Indian exports (2018) went to the **European Union**.
