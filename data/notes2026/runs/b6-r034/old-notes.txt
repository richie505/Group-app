[34:3:8] # Rupee Management
[34:3:9] - RBI curbs excess volatility; **no exchange-rate target**; India – **managed float**.
[34:3:10] - RBI **defends the rupee by selling dollars** (absorbs rupees); **RBI buying dollars expands money supply** (sterilisation may follow).
[34:3:11] - **Higher FDI inflows → rupee appreciates**. Weak rupee → **imported inflation**, higher external debt burden; export gain limited by import content and price-taker status.
[34:3:12] - Forex reserves = **foreign currency assets, gold, SDRs, reserve tranche in IMF**.
[34:3:13] - **Dornbusch Overshooting Model**: exchange rate overreacts in the short run (sticky prices).
[34:3:14] - **Impossible Trinity** (Mundell-Fleming): cannot have fixed exchange rate + free capital mobility + independent monetary policy together.
[34:3:15] - Expansionary policy does not stop a rupee slide; **Masala Bonds**, easier ECB help. CAD financing hard due to **negative net FDI** and volatile FPI.
[34:3:16] - **2013 Taper Tantrum**: rupee fell; RBI tightened short-term rates, oil-dollar swap; gold import duty raised.
[34:3:17] - **Fowler Committee (1898)**: Gold Exchange Standard – rupee pegged at **1s 4d** (15 rupees = £1).
