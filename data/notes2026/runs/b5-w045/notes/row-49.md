=== BrahMos – India-Russia Supersonic Cruise Missile
# Partnership and name
- **Joint venture of India and Russia:** **DRDO 50.5%** + **NPO Mashinostroyeniya (NPOM), Russia 49.5%** = **BrahMos Aerospace**, created by an Inter-Governmental Agreement signed in **Moscow, 1998** (not USA, France, Israel, Japan, China).
- **Name:** **Brahmaputra** (India) + **Moskva** (Russia) rivers – "BRAHMOS", not Brahmastra.
- **Production:** missiles are integrated at the **BrahMos Integration Complex, Kanchanbagh, Hyderabad** (first production unit; later Thiruvananthapuram, and **Lucknow** – BrahMos Integration and Testing Facility, inaugurated **11 May 2025**). Not Bangalore, Mumbai, Ludhiana or Dehradun.
# Type and technical features
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Type | **Supersonic cruise missile** (not ballistic, not ICBM); one of the world's fastest cruise missiles |
| Speed | **Mach 2.8–3.0** (~3,400–3,700 km/h) |
| Role | Essentially **anti-ship**, also land-attack; not anti-aircraft, not anti-tank |
| Propulsion | Solid booster → **liquid ramjet** |
| Navigation | Inertial + satellite; fire-and-forget from a Transport Launch Canister |
| Range | **290 km** originally → **400–450 km** after India joined **MTCR (2016)**; BrahMos-ER **450–800 km**. Never 1,200 km |
| Platforms | **Land, ship, submarine and air (Su-30MKI)** – not "only submarines"; in service with **Army, Navy and Air Force** |
- **Variants:** BrahMos-ER (extended range); **BrahMos-NG** – lighter (~1.5 t vs 2.5 t), for Tejas, smaller warships, submarines, ~Mach 3.5; **BrahMos-II** – planned **hypersonic** (above Mach 5, up to Mach 7–8), scramjet.

# Exports
- **Philippines** – first export customer: **US$375 million** deal (**January 2022**) for **three batteries** of shore-based anti-ship BrahMos; first batch delivered **April 2024**. Export range about **290 km** (so "490 km" is false).
- **UAE**: BrahMos and Akashteer only in **early-stage talks** (23 Jun 2026) – nothing delivered.

=== Astra and Nirbhay
# Astra (BVRAAM)
- India's **first indigenous Beyond Visual Range Air-to-Air Missile** (DRDO); all-weather, day-night; **active radar seeker**. So Astra = **air-to-air** (not surface-to-surface, surface-to-air or air-to-surface).
- BVR = beyond about 20 nautical miles (37 km).
- First successfully tested **9 May 2003, Balasore**; ballistic flight test at ITR **Chandipur, 20 May 2011**.
| Variant | Facts |
|---|---|
| Astra Mk-1 | **80–110 km** (max ~110 km head-on), Mach 4.5, up to 20 km altitude; on **Su-30MKI**, Tejas |
| Astra Mk-2 | ~160–200 km, dual-pulse solid motor |
| Astra Mk-3 'Gandiva' | Under development, **SFDR** engine, 300–350+ km |
- **23 August 2023:** LCA **Tejas (LSP-7)** test-fired Astra off **Goa**; **12 March 2025:** fired from an LCA AF Mk1 prototype.
- **Export:** **Indonesia** – Astra Mk-1 deal Jul 2026, first-ever Astra export.
# Nirbhay and ITCM
- **Nirbhay:** India's first indigenous **long-range subsonic cruise missile** (land-attack, LR-LACM) – **not supersonic**.
- Range **~1,000 km**; **turbojet**; about **Mach 0.8**; conventional or **nuclear** warheads; **terrain-hugging**; surface-to-surface; seen as India's counter to Pakistan's **Babur**.
- Flight-tested **7 November 2017** from **ITR Chandipur**.
- **ITCM** (Indigenous Technology Cruise Missile): Nirbhay successor, **'Manik' small turbofan** by **GTRE**; Jun 2026: family now called **LR-LACM**.
> Astra is asked as "air-to-air / first BVR" ×8; Nirbhay as "subsonic" (the wrong statement is always "supersonic").

|---|---|

=== Missile Classes, FOBS and Hypersonic Technology
# Ballistic vs cruise
| Feature | Ballistic missile | Cruise missile |
|---|---|---|
| Power | **Rocket-powered** only in the boost phase | **Air-breathing (jet)**, powered throughout |
| Path | High parabolic (free-fall) arc | Flies low, constant speed, aerodynamic lift |
| Examples | Agni, Prithvi, Pralay, Shaurya | BrahMos, Nirbhay |
- UPSC 2023: "ballistic = jet-propelled subsonic; cruise = rocket only at start" and "Agni-V cruise, BrahMos ICBM" are **both wrong** (neither).
| Class | Speed | Indian example |
|---|---|---|
| Subsonic | Below Mach 1 | Nirbhay |
| Supersonic | Mach 1–5 | BrahMos (Mach 2.8–3.0) |
| Hypersonic | Above **Mach 5** | BrahMos-II (planned), LR-AShM, HSTDV |
# Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **FOBS** (Fractional Orbital Bombardment System) | Warhead put into a (fractional) low Earth orbit, then **de-orbits over the target**; can come from any direction (e.g. over the South Pole); Soviet Cold War concept |
| HGV (Hypersonic Glide Vehicle) | Rocket-boosted, then glides at Mach 5+ and manoeuvres in the upper atmosphere; hard to intercept |
| MIRV | One missile, several warheads, different targets; first Indian test on **Agni-V** in **Mission Divyastra** (11 March 2024), not K-4 |
| Scramjet | **Supersonic combustion**, no rotating parts; uses air, does not carry its oxidiser |
| SFDR (Solid Fuel Ducted Ramjet) | Uses atmospheric air; demonstrated from ITR Chandipur; for **long-range air-to-air missiles** |
# Indian hypersonic systems
- **HSTDV:** indigenous **scramjet** demonstrator; flew above **Mach 6**; scramjet combustion and thermal protection above **2,000°C**; combustor run over 12 minutes (Jan 2026). Not a submarine-launched missile.
- **16 November 2024:** first flight-trial of India's first long-range hypersonic missile.
- **LR-AShM:** hypersonic **glide anti-ship** missile for Navy coastal batteries; **~1,500 km**; **peak Mach 10**, average Mach 5 via "skipping"; at the 77th Republic Day Parade; **AP coast ranges** used for validation rounds (Jan 2026).
