=== Formation, Motto, Vision and Mission
- **Formed 1958** by amalgamating the **Technical Development Establishments (TDEs)** of the Indian Army, the **Directorate of Technical Development & Production (DTDP)** and the **Defence Science Organisation (DSO)** (not AEET Trombay).
- **DRDO = Defence Research and Development Organisation**; R&D wing of the **Ministry of Defence**; HQ **DRDO Bhawan, New Delhi**.
- **Motto: "Balasya Mulam Vigyanam"** – the source of strength is science (**not** Tejasvinavadhitamastu, Jagratam Aharnisham or Vayam Rakshamah).
- **Growth:** started with **10 establishments**; now about **41 laboratories and 5 DRDO Young Scientist Laboratories** (DRDO website; many books still say **about 52** labs, the pre-merger figure).
- **Vision:** empower the nation with state-of-the-art indigenous **defence and security** technologies (older wording: make India prosperous through a world-class S&T base and give the Services a decisive edge with internationally competitive systems).
- **Mission (three points):** (1) design, develop and lead to production **state-of-the-art sensors, weapon systems, platforms** and allied equipment; (2) **technological solutions** to optimise **combat effectiveness** and the **well-being of troops**; (3) **infrastructure, quality manpower and a strong indigenous technology base**. "Promote international collaboration" is **not** in the mission.
- **Achievements:** Agni and Prithvi missiles, LCA **Tejas**, **Pinaka** MBRL, **Akash** air defence, radars and EW systems.

=== Academic Centres and Young Scientist Labs
- **JCBCAT** (Jagadish Chandra Bose Centre for Advanced Technology) – **Jadavpur University, Kolkata**, set up with **DRDO** (not ISRO, NASA, BARC or BEL).
- **DRDO Young Scientist Laboratories (DYSLs):** **5** labs (dedicated **2 Jan 2020**) in five futuristic areas – AI, quantum technology, cognitive, asymmetric technologies, smart materials (still 5 as of Oct 2024).
- **DIA-CoEs:** **15** DRDO Industry-Academia Centres of Excellence at IITs and IISc; **DIAT Pune** – DRDO-backed deemed university.
- **Agnishodh** – the **Indian Army's** research cell at **IIT Madras**.
- **College of Military Engineering (CME)** – **Dapodi, Pune**, an Army institution affiliated to **JNU** (not DRDO).

=== TDF, Technology Transfer and Budget
- **TDF:** MoD programme run by DRDO under **Make in India**; grants up to **₹50 crore** to MSMEs and start-ups.
- Since **2020** royalty-free patent access; **2,100+** free ToTs; DRDO test facilities open to private industry.
- **Aug 2026:** ToT of **Astra, Rudram, Nag, VSHORADS** to private industry approved; **Agni and K-series** stay in state production.
- DRDO tested **Gaganyaan crew-escape parachutes** for ISRO.
- **Budget 2026-27:** **₹29,100.25 crore** (2025-26: ₹26,816.82 crore).
