# Mission Sudarshan Chakra
- Announced **Independence Day 2025**; networked national shield (air defence, cybersecurity, surveillance), **Vision 2035**.
- UPSC 2026: aims at **air defence, BMD and aerial offensive capability**; **rapid, precise, powerful responses** (strategic autonomy); cover **all important public places** by a nationwide shield **by 2035** – **all three correct**.

- "Sudarshan Chakra" = IAF's name for the S-400 **and** the Vision 2035 mission.

=== Nuclear Doctrine, No First Use and Command
# Timeline
- **Draft doctrine:** **National Security Advisory Board (NSAB)**, **August 1999**.
- **No First Use** came from the **Vajpayee government after Pokhran-II** (not Indira Gandhi after Pokhran-I).
- Formally adopted by the **CCS** on **4 January 2003**.
# Key tenets
- **Credible minimum deterrence** backed by an assured **second-strike capability** (survivable SSBNs) – not a bigger arsenal or first use.
- **No First Use:** only in **retaliation against a nuclear attack on Indian territory or Indian forces**.
- **Massive retaliation** causing unacceptable damage.
- **No use against non-nuclear-weapon states**; nuclear option kept against a **major biological or chemical attack**.
- **Unilateral moratorium** on nuclear tests (since 1998) – so a doctrine question "NFU, moratorium, minimum credible deterrence" = **all of the above**.
- India kept the bomb option open and would **not open its strategic nuclear plants to inspection** (UPPCS 1994: A true, R "India will not produce any bomb" false).
# Nuclear Command Authority
| Body | Chaired by | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Political Council | **Prime Minister** | **Only body that can authorise use** |
| Executive Council | **National Security Advisor** | Advises; carries out directions |
| Strategic Forces Command | Tri-service (set up **2003**) | **Manages and operates** the nuclear arsenal |
- **CCS** oversees civilian political control; it does not manage the forces. Aug 2026: Agni user trials held "under the aegis of SFC".

=== Nuclear Triad – Land, Air, Sea
| Leg | Platforms |
|---|---|
| Land | Prithvi and **Agni** series (canisterised, road- and rail-mobile); Agni-V (5,000+ km, solid, three-stage; first test **19 April 2012**) |
| Air | **Jaguar and Mirage 2000** (free-fall bombs) |
| Sea | K-15 / K-4 SLBMs from Arihant-class SSBNs; earlier **Dhanush** from ships (**INS Subhadra**, INS Rajput) |
- **SSBN = most survivable leg** – concealment gives sure **second-strike** capability, which backs NFU. Next step: **continuous at-sea deterrence**.
- India has a fully operational triad, with **USA, Russia, China** (Apr 2026). US triad = **Minuteman III** ICBMs + bombers + Ohio/Columbia-class SSBNs.
- Agni-4 user trial 6 Aug 2026: 4,000 km, two-stage solid, road-mobile. SIPRI (Jun 2026): **12** Indian warheads "operationally deployed", linked to **SSBN patrols and canisterised Agni**.

=== Nuclear Weapon Technology
| Fission (atomic) weapon | Fusion (thermonuclear / hydrogen) weapon |
|---|---|
| Heavy nucleus (U-235 / Pu-239) splits in a neutron chain reaction | Light nuclei (hydrogen isotopes) fuse |
| Example: Pokhran-I | A **fission "primary"** triggers the fusion; tested in Pokhran-II |
- **Krytron** = high-speed gas-filled switch that fires a nuclear bomb's detonators (the "trigger"; not a MOSFET, solid-state relay or solenoid).
| Grade | U-235 share |
|---|---|
| Natural uranium | **~0.7%** |
| Reactor-grade LEU | 3–5% |
| HEU | 20%+ |
| Weapons-grade | ~90% |
