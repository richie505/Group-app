## 3. Trends
- APPSC asks the *advance estimate printed in that year's Socio-Economic Survey* (2016-17 AE 11.61 / 11.18; 2021-22 AE 11.43; 2023-24 AE 1.69 / 8.02 / 5.21). Later revisions change these figures, so the notes give both the PYQ-era figure and the revised one (SES 2024-25 series).
- Sector-share questions keep returning to the same three triples (2016-17, 2021-22, 2024-25) and to "largest sector = services, largest sub-sector at current prices = livestock".
- Vision 2029 (2016-19 papers) is now giving way to Swarna Andhra @2047 / P4 (2025 papers).
- Row 110 draws few AP PYQs; most "per capita income" PYQs in the pool are all-India or inter-State (moved to row 25).
- Row 111 PYQs are geography-flavoured (minerals, forests, tiger reserve, Forest Act) rather than "bifurcation endowment" proper.

## 4. Heavy in the old notes but never asked
- Long 2025-26 sub-sector growth tables, Q2 2025-26 review, debt/capital-outlay figures, Swarna Andhra KPI tables, Gini of State PCI, 16th FC weights, Nagar Van / Nagaravanam counts, FRA title counts – kept short, for the app's own MCQs.
- Old notes' "average agricultural growth since bifurcation about 17%" was wrong (constant-price average 2014-15 to 2017-18 = 11.3%, SES 2024-25 Table 2.2) and is corrected; "China now highest BRICS PCI" was wrong (World Bank 2024: Russia) and is corrected; "Gujarat > Maharashtra" for the UPSC 2001 per capita NSDP order was dropped.
