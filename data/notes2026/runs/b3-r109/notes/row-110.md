- AP's PCI has been **above India's every year since 2014-15**; 2025-26 gap ≈ **₹75,000** (AP ≈ **1.34 times** India).
- **All-India PCI 2023-24 (PE) ₹1,84,205 is lower than AP's 2023-24 PCI** – both statements of the 2025 PYQ are correct.

- **Telangana vs AP PCI:** 6% higher (2004-05) → **1.3 times (2013-14)** → **1.35 times (2024-25)**; pre-1956 Telangana had higher PCI and revenue (SRC).

- India's first national estimate (National Income Committee, 1951): **₹225** for 1948-49. India = **lower-middle-income economy** (~$2,760).

=== District and Regional Disparities
# Inter-district disparity
- **Inter-district disparity in PCI is high**: Visakhapatnam / Krishna have roughly twice the PCI of Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Anantapur.
- **Best district in GDDP (current prices), 2017-18 (13 old districts): East Godavari** (Krishna close behind).
- **2023-24 GDDP:** highest **Visakhapatnam** ₹1,14,859 cr; lowest Parvathipuram Manyam. **Per capita GDDP:** highest **Visakhapatnam ₹5,45,588**; lowest **Srikakulam ₹1,59,029**.
- **Visakhapatnam ranked 1st in PCI (FY 2025-26)**; drivers: Vizag Port, RINL, Hindustan Shipyard, HPCL, IT parks, **Eastern Naval Command**.
- **District Domestic Product** is compiled by the **State Directorate of Economics and Statistics** (not NSO).
- **Highest number of illiterates** (Vision document): **Kurnool**.
- **Post offices, March 2016:** keyed **Anantapuramu** (G2 Mains 2017); the app's earlier notes said **Chittoor** – not verified. Latest: Prakasam (post offices); Visakhapatnam (bank branches, mobile connections).

=== AP's Position Among States
# PCI and GSDP ranks
- **2022-23 (MoSPI):** AP's PCI (~₹2.2 lakh) is **below Haryana's**, above Punjab, Rajasthan, West Bengal; **Telangana (₹3.54 lakh)** is the richest major State, then Karnataka, Haryana, Tamil Nadu.
- **2017-18 PCI rank:** app MCQ keys **5th**; the 2019 G2 PYQ (4th/5th/6th/7th) is left unkeyed – the rank depends on which States are counted.
- **Per capita NSDP 2023-24 (ES 2025-26):** **Sikkim** highest (₹5,87,743), **Goa** second (₹5,85,953), **Bihar** lowest (₹62,201).
- **EAC-PM working paper (Sept 2024):** share of national GDP 2023-24 **AP 4.7%, Telangana 4.9%**; five southern States **over 30%**; Telangana's economy = **96.5%** of AP's (2024-25); metro Hyderabad **47.5%** of Telangana's output.
- Largest State economy = **Maharashtra**. Gini of State PCI **0.230 (1994-95) → 0.261 (2025-26)** = divergence.
- **16th Finance Commission:** **42.5%** weight to **income distance** → AP's above-average PCI **lowers** its share.

=== Per Capita Income – Concept
- **PCI = NSDP ÷ population** (net); **standard of living** is shown by PCI.
- PCI **rises only when income grows faster than population**; it **can fall in a developing country even as national income rises** when **population grows faster than income** (A and R true, R explains A).
- PCI is an **average**: it **hides inequality** and cost-of-living differences.
- Per capita GSDP and PCI are **not interchangeable** terms.
