# Oil, gas, coal and power
- **DGH:** AP's natural resource = **oil and natural gas** of the **Krishna-Godavari basin** (~52,000 sq km), hydrocarbon resource base about **1,130 million metric tonnes**.
- **Coal went mainly to Telangana** (SCCL: **Telangana 51%, GoI 49%**); the **Twelfth Schedule** keeps SCCL linkages for AP plants unchanged.
- Onshore oil and gas royalty → **the producing State**.
- **CGS power** split by **five years' consumption** (AP ≈ 46%), not the population ratio.
- Srisailam and Nagarjuna Sagar: **left bank → Telangana**, right bank → AP; June 2021 dispute over generation below **834 feet** at **Srisailam**.

=== Forests, Protected Areas and Forest Law
# Forest cover (ISFR, FSI Dehradun, biennial)
- **ISFR** is **biennial**, published by the **Forest Survey of India, Dehradun**.
- **ISFR 2021: AP's forest cover rose by 647 sq km over 2019** – the largest increase among States.
- ISFR 2023: **forest + tree cover 21.74%** of area; forest area incl. scrub 38,436.58 sq km (**23.59%**, includes 8,351.62 sq km scrub); **Recorded Forest Area 37,258 sq km – 8th** (ISFR 2021); forest = canopy **≥10%**.
- Old districts: most cover **Visakhapatnam**; largest area **YSR Kadapa**; least **Krishna**. National Forest Policy **1988**: 33% (66% in hills).
# Protected areas and forest types
- **13 Wildlife Sanctuaries, 1 Tiger Reserve, 3 National Parks**; the tiger reserve = **Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam (NSTR)**, India's largest, in **Nallamala** (AP's largest forest).
- Mostly **tropical dry deciduous**; **mangroves** (Coringa) at the Godavari and Krishna mouths; moist deciduous in the north-coastal hills.
- **Red Sanders:** **Chittoor and YSR Kadapa** (Seshachalam, Veligonda, Lankamala, **Nallamala**, Talakona); endemic to the southern Eastern Ghats; IUCN **Endangered** (2021).
# Laws, schemes and minor forest produce
| Law / scheme | Key point |
|---|---|
| **AP Forest Act, 1967** | **S.3** power to reserve; S.15 final notification; **S.28 power to declare a protected forest to cease to be protected** |
| FCA 1980 | Renamed Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam from **29 Dec 2023** |
| PESA 1996 / FRA 2006 | Gram Sabha controls MFP / recommends diversion for public utilities |
| Green India Mission (2011, NAPCC) | 5 mha new + 5 mha better forest (not 10 mha new) |
| Nagar Van Yojana / Nagaravanam | **61** Nagar Vans / **37** city forests |
| CAMPA | Spends compensatory levies on afforestation |
- **Minor Forest Produce:** collected and sold mainly by the **Girijan Cooperative Corporation (GCC)**, HQ **Visakhapatnam**; runs MSP for MFP; **beedi leaf nationalised 1971**.
- **JFM** (Vana Samrakshana Samithis) from **1992**; Community Forest Management from **2002**; **FRA titles 2,27,300** individual (31 Aug 2026).
- **Neeru-Chettu:** making AP **drought-free** through water conservation and tree planting.
