## SUB 3 | Per Capita Income – Concept
[3:0] - **PCI = NSDP ÷ population** (net); **standard of living** is shown by PCI.
[3:1] - PCI **rises only when income grows faster than population**; it **can fall in a developing country even as national income rises** when **population grows faster than income** (A and R true, R explains A).
[3:2] - PCI is an **average**: it **hides inequality** and cost-of-living differences.
[3:3] - Per capita GSDP and PCI are **not interchangeable** terms.
