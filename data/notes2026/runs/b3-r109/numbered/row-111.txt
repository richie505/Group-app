## SUB 1 | Forests, Protected Areas and Forest Law
[1:0] # Forest cover (ISFR, FSI Dehradun, biennial)
[1:1] - **ISFR 2021: AP's forest cover rose by 647 sq km over 2019** – the largest increase among States.
[1:2] - ISFR 2023: **forest + tree cover 21.74%** of area; forest area incl. scrub 38,436.58 sq km (**23.59%**, includes 8,351.62 sq km scrub); **Recorded Forest Area 37,258 sq km – 8th** (ISFR 2021); forest = canopy **≥10%**.
[1:3] - Old districts: most cover **Visakhapatnam**; largest area **YSR Kadapa**; least **Krishna**. National Forest Policy **1988**: 33% (66% in hills).
[1:4] # Protected areas and forest types
[1:5] - **13 Wildlife Sanctuaries, 1 Tiger Reserve, 3 National Parks**; the tiger reserve = **Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam (NSTR)**, India's largest, in **Nallamala** (AP's largest forest).
[1:6] - Mostly **tropical dry deciduous**; **mangroves** (Coringa) at the Godavari and Krishna mouths; moist deciduous in the north-coastal hills.
[1:7] - **Red Sanders:** **Chittoor and YSR Kadapa** (Seshachalam, Veligonda, Lankamala, **Nallamala**, Talakona); endemic to the southern Eastern Ghats; IUCN **Endangered** (2021).
[1:8] # Laws, schemes and minor forest produce

[1:11] - **JFM** (Vana Samrakshana Samithis) from **1992**; Community Forest Management from **2002**; **FRA titles 2,27,300** individual (31 Aug 2026).
[1:12] - **Neeru-Chettu:** making AP **drought-free** through water conservation and tree planting.
