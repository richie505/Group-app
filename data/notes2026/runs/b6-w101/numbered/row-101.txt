## SUB 1 | Athletics, Endurance and Transplant Games
[1:0] # Viswabharath Allamsetti – first Telugu to finish UTMB
[1:1] - Born in **Guntur**, lives in **France** – **first Telugu runner to complete the Ultra Trail du Mont Blanc (UTMB)**.
[1:2] - Time: **44 hours**.
[1:3] - Race: **176.5 km loop** around the **Mont Blanc massif**, elevation gain over **10,000 m**; passes through **France, Italy, Switzerland**; ends at **Chamonix, France**; 2025 edition began **29 August**.

## SUB 6 | Cricket – National Titles of the Period
[6:0] # ICC Women's ODI World Cup 2025
[6:1] - **India** won its **first-ever Women's ODI World Cup**, beating **South Africa by 52 runs** in the final at **DY Patil Stadium, Navi Mumbai**, on **2 November 2025**.
[6:2] - India had lost the finals of 2005 and 2017. **Shafali Verma** and **Deepti Sharma** starred; South Africa captain **Laura Wolvaardt** scored a century in the final.
[6:3] # IPL 2025
[6:4] - **Royal Challengers Bengaluru** won their **maiden IPL title**, beating **Punjab Kings by 6 runs** at **Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad** (3 June 2025).
[6:5] - RCB 190/9; PBKS 184/7. Captain **Rajat Patidar**; Virat Kohli's first IPL title.
[6:6] # Asia Cup 2025 (T20)
[6:7] - **India beat Pakistan by 5 wickets** in the final, **Dubai**, 28 September 2025 – India's **9th** Asia Cup title.
[6:8] - Player of the Match (final) **Tilak Varma**; Player of the Tournament **Abhishek Sharma**.

## SUB 8 | Hosting Rights – 2030 Commonwealth Games
[8:0] - **Ahmedabad** awarded the **2030 Commonwealth Games** at the Commonwealth Sport **General Assembly, Glasgow**, **26 November 2025**; chosen over **Abuja (Nigeria)**.
[8:1] - 2030 is the **centenary** edition (first Games: **Hamilton, Canada, 1930**).
[8:2] - India's **second** CWG as host, after **Delhi 2010**.
[8:3] - Other hosts in this period: Commonwealth Weightlifting Championships 2025 – **Ahmedabad**; FIDE World Cup 2025 – **Goa**; World Transplant Games – **Dresden**.
