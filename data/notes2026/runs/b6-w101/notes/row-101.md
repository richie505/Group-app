=== Weightlifting – Andhra Pradesh in Focus
# Juhita Guna – Commonwealth gold
- **Juhita Guna** (14), from **Rajamahendravaram**, Andhra Pradesh – **gold in the 69 kg (Youth girls)** category at the **Commonwealth Weightlifting Championships 2025, Ahmedabad** (28 August 2025).
- Lifts: 76 kg snatch + 92 kg clean and jerk = **168 kg** total.
- Her inspiration: **Karnam Malleswari**.

=== Athletics, Endurance and Transplant Games
# Viswabharath Allamsetti – first Telugu to finish UTMB
- Born in **Guntur**, lives in **France** – **first Telugu runner to complete the Ultra Trail du Mont Blanc (UTMB)**.
- Time: **44 hours**.
- Race: **176.5 km loop** around the **Mont Blanc massif**, elevation gain over **10,000 m**; passes through **France, Italy, Switzerland**; ends at **Chamonix, France**; 2025 edition began **29 August**.
# Athletics 2025
- **Akani Simbine (South Africa)** – in **April 2025** became the only sprinter to run a **sub-10 s 100 m in 11 consecutive years**, passing **Usain Bolt** (Jamaica).
- **2025 athletics calendar (earliest to latest):** **20th National Youth Athletics Championships, Patna** (10–12 March) → **28th National Federation Senior Athletics Championships, Kochi** (April) → **Indian Grand Prix 2, Thiruvananthapuram** (May) = order 2-3-1.

=== Cricket – National Titles of the Period
# ICC Women's ODI World Cup 2025
- **India** won its **first-ever Women's ODI World Cup**, beating **South Africa by 52 runs** in the final at **DY Patil Stadium, Navi Mumbai**, on **2 November 2025**.
- India had lost the finals of 2005 and 2017. **Shafali Verma** and **Deepti Sharma** starred; South Africa captain **Laura Wolvaardt** scored a century in the final.
# IPL 2025
- **Royal Challengers Bengaluru** won their **maiden IPL title**, beating **Punjab Kings by 6 runs** at **Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad** (3 June 2025).
- RCB 190/9; PBKS 184/7. Captain **Rajat Patidar**; Virat Kohli's first IPL title.
# Asia Cup 2025 (T20)
- **India beat Pakistan by 5 wickets** in the final, **Dubai**, 28 September 2025 – India's **9th** Asia Cup title.
- Player of the Match (final) **Tilak Varma**; Player of the Tournament **Abhishek Sharma**.

=== Hosting Rights – 2030 Commonwealth Games
- **Ahmedabad** awarded the **2030 Commonwealth Games** at the Commonwealth Sport **General Assembly, Glasgow**, **26 November 2025**; chosen over **Abuja (Nigeria)**.
- 2030 is the **centenary** edition (first Games: **Hamilton, Canada, 1930**).
- India's **second** CWG as host, after **Delhi 2010**.
- Other hosts in this period: Commonwealth Weightlifting Championships 2025 – **Ahmedabad**; FIDE World Cup 2025 – **Goa**; World Transplant Games – **Dresden**.
- **LA 2028 cricket venue:** announced on **15 April 2025** – a temporary stadium at the **Fairgrounds (Fairplex), Pomona**, Southern California; **six-team T20** events for both men and women.
