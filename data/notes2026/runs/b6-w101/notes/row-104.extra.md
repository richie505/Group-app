- **[01 Nov 2026] States' Formation Day:** the PM greeted the **eight States** formed on 1 November; **Andhra Pradesh – 1 November 1956** (States Reorganisation). Basis – **Article 3** (Parliament forms new States, alters boundaries/names); **Article 1** – India a "**Union of States**"; **Article 2** – admission of new States from outside.
- **[03 Nov 2026] Biosphere Reserves Day:** marks the end of **50 years of UNESCO's MAB Programme**; India has **18 notified Biosphere Reserves**, **13** in UNESCO's World Network.
- **[05 Nov 2026] World Tsunami Awareness Day:** designated by the **UNGA in 2015 (A/RES/70/203)**; 2026 commemoration at **UNHQ, New York**; about **78% of tsunamis (1900–2015)** occurred in the **Pacific**.
- **[07 Nov 2026] 150 years of Vande Mataram:** the **Ministry of Railways** ended its **year-long celebration**; composed by **Bankim Chandra Chatterjee**, first published in the journal **Bangadarshan (7 Nov 1875)**, later in **Anandamath (1882)**; first sung by **Tagore** at the **1896 Calcutta INC session**; first a political slogan on **7 August 1905** (**Swadeshi Movement**); **National Song – 24 January 1950**.
- **[09 Nov 2026] National Legal Services Day:** **NALSA** – statutory body under the **Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987**; **CJI = Patron-in-Chief**, a serving/retired SC judge = Executive Chairman; constitutional basis **Article 39A** (equal justice and free legal aid).
- **[09 Nov 2026] National Entrepreneurship Day:** India – **3rd largest start-up ecosystem**; from under **500** start-ups (2014) to **2.62 lakh DPIIT-recognised start-ups** (Nov 2026), **25 lakh+ jobs**, **1.07 lakh+** with at least one woman director; **Fund of Funds for Startups** – DPIIT, managed by **SIDBI**; **SISFS** corpus **₹945 crore** (2021).
- **[10 Nov 2026] World Science Day:** the 2026 observance is the Day's **25th edition** (proclaimed by UNESCO in 2001, first observed 2002).
- **[11 Nov 2026] Maulana Abul Kalam Azad:** born **11 Nov 1888, Mecca**; real name **Abul Kalam Ghulam Muhiyuddin**; Urdu weekly **Al-Hilal (1912)**; **INC President 1940 (Ramgarh) – 1946**; **first Education Minister (1947–58)** – first **IIT (1951)**, **UGC (1953)**; Gandhi called him "**Emperor of Learning**"; **Bharat Ratna 1992** (posthumous); National Education Day since **2008**.
- **[14 Nov 2026] World Diabetes Day:** marks the birthday of **Sir Frederick Banting**, who co-discovered **insulin** with **Charles Best (1921)**; led by **IDF** and **WHO**; WHO: **over 40%** of people with diabetes are **undiagnosed**.
- **[14 Nov 2026] Children's Day:** India marks **Jawaharlal Nehru's** birthday (**14 Nov 1889**, Allahabad) – "Chacha Nehru"; the UN's **Universal Children's Day is 20 November**.

# Andhra Pradesh Angle – C.P. Brown and Events
- **[10 Nov 2026] C.P. Brown's birth anniversary:** CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu** honoured his Telugu legacy. **Charles Philip Brown** – born **10 Nov 1798, Calcutta**; **Madras Civil Service (1817)**; Deputy Collector, **Kadapa (1820)**, later Machilipatnam, Rajahmundry, Guntur; "**Father of Modern Telugu Literature**", "**Restless Pandit**"; **A Dictionary, Telugu and English (1852)**; edited the poems of **Yogi Vemana**; **C.P. Brown Library – Kadapa**; died **12 Dec 1884, London**.
- **19th Pravasi Bharatiya Divas Convention:** **Kolkata, 7–9 January 2027**; theme "**Return to Roots: Reigniting the Diaspora Connect with Bharat**" (2025: "Diaspora's contribution to a Viksit Bharat"); PBD on **9 January** marks **Gandhi's return from South Africa (1915)**; convention **biennial since 2015**; organised by **MEA**.
- **Yuge Yugeen Bharat Museum** (Cabinet, Nov 2026, **₹3,230 crore**): in the **North and South Block** buildings, New Delhi – to have the **world's largest display area**; announced by the PM on **18 May 2023** (International Museum Day).
- **Bihar Museum, Patna** – ranked **1st in Asia, 9th globally** in "**World's 100 Best Museums 2026**" by **Worth a Journey** (Munich); designed by **Fumihiko Maki** (Pritzker winner) with Opolis Architects; fully open **2017**. India's largest museum – **Indian Museum, Kolkata (1814, Asiatic Society)**.
