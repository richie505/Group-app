=== Science Days, IISF and Vigyanika
| Day | Date | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| **National Science Day** | **28 February** | **Raman Effect** (C.V. Raman, 1928); declared 1986 |
| **National Technology Day** | **11 May** | **Pokhran-II** (1998); declared 1999 |

- Science Day themes: 2015 "Science for Nation Building"; **2016 "Scientific Issues for Development of the Nation"** (DST release: "Make in India: S&T driven Innovations") – **not** "Science for Sustainable Development"; 2026 "Women in Science: Catalyzing Viksit Bharat".

=== Vigyan Prasar, Outreach Bodies and the National Innovation Foundation
# Science communication
- **Vigyan Prasar**: autonomous body of **DST**, **1989**, New Delhi (not HRD).
- **DD Science** (one-hour slot on DD National) + **India Science** (Vigyan Prasar's internet/OTT channel, not NCSM) – launched together **15 January 2019**.
- **Vigyan Dhara**: DST umbrella scheme merging 3 schemes – capacity building; R&D; innovation and technology deployment (Aug 2024).
| Channel | Run by |
|---|---|
| Gyan Darshan | 26 Jan 2000; MHRD with **IGNOU** and Prasar Bharati |
| Gyan Vani / Vyas | FM counterpart / UGC-CEC channel |
| **SWAYAM Prabha** | Free DTH, Ministry of Education, 2017, **32 channels** |

- **ICSSR** (social science) is **not an S&T body**, unlike DST, CSIR, DAE.
- India's **first dinosaurium**: **B.M. Birla Science Centre, Hyderabad**.
# National Innovation Foundation
- Grew from the **Honey Bee Network** (Prof. **Anil K. Gupta**, SRISTI, IIM Ahmedabad); **NIF-India, March 2000**: **autonomous body of DST**, **Gandhinagar**.
- Mandate: scout, support, spread **grassroots innovations** and traditional knowledge – **not** advanced research with foreign institutes (UPSC 2015: "a only").
- IGNITE, Biennial Grassroots Innovation Awards, **Festival of Innovation (FOIN)** at **Rashtrapati Bhavan**.
