## SUB 1 | Gravitational Waves, LIGO-India and Mega-Science Projects
[1:0] # Gravitational waves
[1:1] - Predicted by **Einstein (1916)**; ripples in space-time from **merging black holes**, neutron-star collisions.
[1:2] - **LIGO** = **Laser Interferometer Gravitational-Wave Observatory** (USA); first detection **14 September 2015** (announced 11 Feb 2016); **Nobel 2017**: Weiss, Barish, Thorne.
[1:3] - **Second detection** announced **June 2016**: two **black holes** merging ~**1.4 billion light-years** away (not white dwarfs).
[1:4] - **eLISA** (evolved Laser Interferometer Space Antenna) = space mission to **detect gravitational waves** (not neutrinos).
[1:5] - Black-hole merger observations (UPSC 2019) = **gravitational waves detected**, not Higgs bosons.
[1:6] # LIGO-India
[1:7] - At **Aundh, Hingoli**, Maharashtra; **DAE + DST** with US **NSF**.
[1:8] - Lead institutions: **IPR Gandhinagar, IUCAA Pune, RRCAT Indore** (not IIST or "Kepler's Observatory").
[1:9] - Consortium = **IndIGO** (Indian Initiative in Gravitational-wave Observations) – the APPSC G2 2017 key said "IndLIGO", which is not the name.
[1:10] # Other projects and telescopes

  [1:11.0] ITER | Cadarache, France | ITER-India, IPR Gandhinagar (leads both LIGO-India and ITER-India)

  [1:11.2] Square Kilometre Array | South Africa, Australia | NCRA-TIFR, Pune

[1:13] - **Sampurnanand Telescope** (104 cm) at **ARIES, Nainital** (Uttarakhand – **not Ladakh**): **50 years** (1972-2022); comets, star-forming regions, clusters.
[1:14] - **IIA Bengaluru**: VELC payload of Aditya-L1, Himalayan Chandra Telescope (2 m, Hanle), Kodaikanal Solar Observatory.

## SUB 2 | ICAR – Origin, Structure, Heads and News
[2:0] - **ICAR** = **Indian Council of Agricultural Research**; set up **16 July 1929** (ICAR Day = **16 July**) as a **society** (Societies Registration Act 1860) on the **Royal Commission on Agriculture** report; HQ New Delhi.
[2:1] - Under **DARE** (Dec 1973), Ministry of Agriculture; **113 institutes** (72 research institutes).
[2:2] - **Director General** = Secretary DARE: **Dr S. Ayyappan** (2010-16, the answer in 2015) → **Dr Mangi Lal Jat** (as of 2026).
[2:3] - **KVKs**: district level, **100% centrally funded**, under **11 ATARIs**; first KVK **Puducherry, 1974** (Mohan Singh Mehta committee, 1973). Sep 2026: skill centres in all KVKs by Dec 2028.
[2:4] # In the news
[2:5] - **13 Dec 2019**: ICAR–**NABARD** MoU (sustainable agriculture, climate-resilient farming).
[2:6] - **Sardar Patel Outstanding ICAR Institution Award 2021** (large institute): **ICAR-NAARM, Hyderabad**.
[2:7] - **562** high-yielding field-crop varieties released 2019-2021 (APPSC AE 2022 key).
[2:8] - **Lumpi-ProVacInd**: lumpy skin disease vaccine developed by **two ICAR institutes**.

[4:10] - Mandates: ISRO – space for development; DRDO – defence systems; DAE – nuclear power, radioisotopes; ICAR – agriculture; ICMR – biomedical research. ICMR under DHR, CSIR under DSIR.
[4:11] - Other HQs: Indian Bureau of Mines Nagpur; CIMFR Dhanbad; NATMO Kolkata; **MIDHANI Hyderabad**.
