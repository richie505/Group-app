=== New Current Affairs: 5–14 Nov 2026
# Obituaries
- **[10 Nov 2026] Sanjit "Bunker" Roy** (81), founder of **Barefoot College**, died at his home in **Tilonia, Rajasthan**; born **1945**; studied at **The Doon School** (with Rajiv Gandhi); moved by the **1966 Bihar famine**; set up the **Social Work and Research Centre (SWRC), Tilonia, in 1972** – known as Barefoot College (rural solar energy, water, education, "barefoot" solar engineers); **Padma Shri 1986**, **Tyler Prize 2004**, **TIME 100 (2010)**, Clinton Global Citizen Award 2013; wife – activist **Aruna Roy**.
- **Dr Ashok Kumar Mahapatra** (died early Nov 2026) – also the **founding Director of AIIMS Bhubaneswar** and head of Neurosurgery, AIIMS New Delhi; led the team that separated the **conjoined twins Jaga and Balia (AIIMS, 2017)**.
# Sports Persons in News
- **[05 Nov 2026] Cristiano Ronaldo** (41) retired from **international football** after **23 years** (debut 2003): **234 caps, 146 goals** – most by any player; **Euro 2016** winning captain; club **Al Nassr**.
- **[08 Nov 2026] Anuj Shrivatri** (22, Katni) – **India's 100th chess Grandmaster**, **first from Madhya Pradesh**.
# Honours to Indians and AP Persons
- **[09 Nov 2026] Dr Devi Shetty** (founder-chairman, **Narayana Health**) – conferred **Chevalier de l'Ordre national du Mérite** (Knight of the National Order of Merit) by **France** (Consulate General, **Bengaluru**) for support to the French community during **Covid-19**; the Order was set up on **3 Dec 1963** by **Charles de Gaulle** – France's **second-highest** national order after the **Legion of Honour**; earlier Indian recipients **Sunil Kant Munjal (2025)**, **Swati Piramal (2006)**.
- **[11 Nov 2026] K. Ram Mohan Naidu** (Union **Civil Aviation Minister**, MP from **Srikakulam, AP**) – named among **Purdue University's "38 by 38" Achievers** (College of Engineering, USA; award instituted **2024**, for 38 alumni by the age of **38**, inspired by **Neil Armstrong**, a Purdue alumnus who walked on the Moon at 38); he did his **BSEE at Purdue (2008)**.
- **[10 Nov 2026] J.T. Amareswar** (V.R. Siddhartha Engineering College, **Vijayawada**) – **Best Mechanical Engineering Student 2026**, by **ISTE, AP Section** (ceremony 7 Nov 2026).
- **[04 Nov 2026] National Medical Commission:** Chairperson **Dr Abhijat Chandrakant Sheth**; NMC – statutory, **NMC Act 2019**, set up **2020** (replaced MCI); **33 members** (Chairperson, **10 ex officio**, **22 part-time**) under **Section 4(1)**.
