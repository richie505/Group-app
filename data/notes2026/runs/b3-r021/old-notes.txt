=== NOTES 3:21 | India's Environmental Policy Framework – Nep, Eia, Polluter Pays and Precautionary Principles
## SUB 0 | Environmental Principles
[21:0:0] TABLE head: Principle | Meaning
  [21:0:0.0] Precautionary | Act/stop the activity even without full scientific certainty; burden of proof on the actor to show it is harmless (APPSC 2024, 2025)
  [21:0:0.1] Polluter Pays | Polluter bears cost of remediation + compensation (APPSC 2025)
  [21:0:0.2] Public trust | State holds natural resources in trust for the public
  [21:0:0.3] Absolute liability | Hazardous industries – no exceptions
## SUB 1 | Cases -> Doctrine
[21:1:0] TABLE head: Case | Year | Doctrine
  [21:1:0.0] Ratlam Municipality | 1980 | Municipal duty on public nuisance; health, sanitation
  [21:1:0.1] M.C. Mehta (Oleum gas, Delhi) | 1986 | Absolute liability
  [21:1:0.2] Subhash Kumar v Bihar | 1991 | Pollution-free water and air in Art. 21
  [21:1:0.3] T.N. Godavarman | 1995 | "Forest" regardless of ownership
  [21:1:0.4] Vellore Citizens | 1996 | Precautionary + Polluter Pays; tanneries, Palar (TN)
  [21:1:0.5] Bichhri | 1996 | Polluter Pays; Rajasthan chemical units
  [21:1:0.6] M.C. Mehta v Kamal Nath | 1997 | Public trust
  [21:1:0.7] Taj Trapezium | 1997 | Mandated CNG
[21:1:1] Vellore = both principles; Bichhri = Polluter Pays only; Kamal Nath = public trust; Oleum gas = absolute liability.
## SUB 2 | Constitutional Provisions
[21:2:0] - **Article 48A** (DPSP) = duty of the **State**; **Article 51A(g)** (FD) = duty of the **citizen**.
[21:2:1] - **Article 21** read to include a clean environment (Subhash Kumar, 1991).
## SUB 3 | Acts and Bodies
[21:3:0] TABLE head: Act / body | Year | Purpose
  [21:3:0.0] Wildlife (Protection) Act | 1972 | Wildlife, habitats; regulates hunting
  [21:3:0.1] Water Act; CPCB | 1974 | CPCB formed under Water Act; later Air Act too
  [21:3:0.2] Forest (Conservation) Act | 1980 | Forests
  [21:3:0.3] Air Act | 1981 | Amended 1987 (stricter penalties)
  [21:3:0.4] Environment (Protection) Act | 1986 | Umbrella law; Sec. 3; EIA and CRZ under it

[21:3:1] - SPCB compensation powers: **Sec. 33A Water Act**, **Sec. 31A Air Act** (SC, Aug 2025).
## SUB 4 | National Environment Policy 2006
[21:4:0] - Core idea: **conservation and livelihood security go together**.
[21:4:1] - Objectives: conserve critical resources; **inter-generational equity**; livelihood security for poor tribal communities; integrate environment into development plans; efficient resource use.
[21:4:2] - Principles: **precautionary + polluter pays + public trust**.
[21:4:3] NOT an NEP objective: ending all development in ecologically sensitive areas.
[21:4:4] - **Year:** The National Environment Policy was adopted in **2006**.

## SUB 6 | National Green Tribunal
[21:6:0] - **NGT Act 2010**; in force **18 Oct 2010**; replaced the National Environment Appellate Authority.
[21:6:1] - **Principal Bench: Delhi**; zonal: **Bhopal, Pune, Kolkata, Chennai**.
[21:6:2] - Civil cases on substantial environmental questions under **7 Acts** in Schedule I.
## SUB 7 | Napcc 2008 – 8 Missions
[21:7:0] Solar; Enhanced Energy Efficiency; Sustainable Habitat; Water; Himalayan Ecosystem; **Green India**; **Sustainable Agriculture**; Strategic Knowledge for Climate Change. Climate change and human health is also listed (2026).
[21:7:1] **Coastal Security** is NOT a NAPCC mission.
[21:7:2] - **NAPCC:** The National Action Plan on Climate Change was released on **30 June 2008** with **eight** national missions.
## SUB 8 | Eco-mark and Ap Institutions
[21:8:0] - **Eco-Mark (1991)**: environment-friendly products; logo **earthen pot**; certified by **BIS**, not CPCB.
[21:8:1] - Products: soaps, detergents, paper, plastics, cosmetics, aerosols, food; **not drugs/antibiotics**.
[21:8:2] - **APPCB**: constituted **24 Jan 1976** under the Water Act; air role **1981**; gives consents. **White** = practically non-polluting; **Green** = low pollution (Jul 2026).
[21:8:3] - **EPTRI** (Hyderabad): environment training and research.
