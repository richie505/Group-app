=== Hydrogen as a Fuel – Basic Facts
- **Cleanest fuel:** burning hydrogen gives **only water** – least / minimum air pollution (coal, kerosene, diesel, petrol give CO2 and harmful gases; CNG and power alcohol still give CO2).
- **Highest fuel (calorific) value:** about **150,000 kJ/kg** – above natural gas, gasoline, charcoal.
- Burns in air at about **4% to 75%** by volume.
- **Rocket fuel:** liquid hydrogen (**dihydrogen**, H2) with liquid oxygen in cryogenic stages – not hydrogen peroxide, heavy water or CFCs.
- **Storage for vehicles:** hydrogen absorbed at low temperature by a **metal hydride** (e.g. palladium hydride) and released by exhaust heat.
- Hydrogen is also the **most abundant element in the Universe** (~3/4 of matter) and the fuel of the Sun's fusion reactions.
> Least pollution / highest fuel value / burning gives water – the classic hydrogen-fuel PYQ.

=== Applications of Green Hydrogen
- **Uses of green hydrogen (UPSC 2023 – all three correct):** **directly as a fuel in internal combustion** engines (H2-ICE), **blended with natural gas** for heat or power, and in **hydrogen fuel cells** to run vehicles.
- **Decarbonises fertiliser plants (ammonia), oil refineries and steel plants** (UPSC 2023 – all three) – **hard-to-abate** sectors where direct electrification is difficult.
- **Industry:** refining (**desulphurisation**), **ammonia for fertilisers**, methanol, **steel by DRI** (hydrogen replaces **coking coal**).
- **Fuel uses:** fuel-cell vehicles (buses, trucks, trains, ships), rocket fuel, **H-CNG** (hydrogen blended with CNG).
- **Green ammonia** = nitrogen + green hydrogen; fertiliser, fuel, marine fuel.
- **India's first green hydrogen blending project:** **NTPC + Gujarat Gas Ltd**, **Kawas, Surat** (Jan 2023), blending into the PNG network; PNGRB allowed 5% blending at start, scaling to 20%.

=== Colours of Hydrogen
| Colour | Production route | CO2 |
|---|---|---|
| **Green** | **Electrolysis of water with renewable power** | Zero – only zero-carbon colour |
| **Grey** | **Steam methane reforming (SMR)** of natural gas | High; ~95% of production; cheapest |
| Blue | SMR + carbon capture and storage (CCS) | Low |
| Pink / purple | Electrolysis with **nuclear** power | Near-zero |
| Turquoise | **Methane pyrolysis** → solid carbon | Near-zero |
| Brown / black | **Coal gasification** without CCS | Very high |
| White | Naturally occurring geological hydrogen | – |
- **SMR:** methane + steam at **700–1000°C**, **nickel catalyst** → hydrogen + CO.
- Cost (Jul 2026): grey **$1.5–2/kg** vs green **~$4/kg**.
- "Green" means the **production route (renewable energy)** – not sea water, not coal, not a green-coloured gas.

=== Fuel Cells
- **Fuel cell** = electrochemical device: **H2 + O2 → electricity (DC) + water + heat**; no combustion; efficiency up to ~55%.
- With pure hydrogen the only by-products are **heat and water**; FCEV exhaust = **water vapour** (and warm air) – not ammonia, nitrogen, oxygen or hydrogen peroxide.
- Fuel cells power buildings **and** small devices (laptops); they give **DC, not AC** (UPSC 2015: only statement 1 correct).
- A fuel cell combines hydrogen with **oxygen**, not carbon dioxide.
# PEM fuel cell steps
- 1. **Anode:** H2 → 2H+ + 2e-; electrons flow through the external circuit.
- 2. **Protons cross the membrane**; **cathode:** 1/2 O2 + 2H+ + 2e- → H2O + heat.

=== National Green Hydrogen Mission
- Approved by **Union Cabinet, 4 January 2023**; nodal ministry **MNRE**; outlay **₹19,744 crore** up to **2029-30**.
| Component | Role | Outlay |
|---|---|---|
| **SIGHT** | Incentives for **electrolyser manufacturing** + **green H2 production** | ₹17,490 cr |
| Pilot projects | Steel, mobility, shipping, storage, biomass hydrogen | ₹1,466 cr |
| **SHIP** (Strategic Hydrogen Innovation Partnership) | PPP for R&D | ₹400 cr |
| Green Hydrogen Hubs | Production-use-export clusters; **4 Hydrogen Valley Innovation Clusters** | – |
- **2030 targets:** **5 MMT/year** green hydrogen; **125 GW** added RE; **60–100 GW electrolysers** (not 200 GW); **6 lakh jobs**; ₹8 lakh crore investment; **50 MMT/yr GHG** cut.
- Goals: **energy independence 2047**, **net zero 2070**.
- Progress: **15 companies**, 3,000 MW electrolyser manufacturing; green ammonia 7,24,000 TPA at **₹53.27/kg** (SECI); **Japanese** firms signed green ammonia/methanol export deals (Jul 2026).
- **Hub ports:** **Deendayal (Kandla), Gujarat**; **V.O. Chidambaranar (Tuticorin), Tamil Nadu** (₹35 crore bunkering pilot); Paradip, Odisha.
- Chronology: rooftop solar programme (2014) → **NGHM (Jan 2023)** → PM Internship Scheme (Oct 2024).

=== Electrolysers, Standard, Certification and Storage
- **Electrolyser** = uses **electricity to split water** into hydrogen and oxygen.
| Type | Feature |
|---|---|
| Alkaline | Liquid KOH/NaOH electrolyte; oldest, mature |
| **PEM** | Solid polymer membrane; compact; quick response suits variable RE |
| **Solid oxide** | High-temperature steam; most efficient |
| AEM | Can use **non-platinum** catalysts (nickel, cobalt, iron) |
- Concern: **Chinese dominance** of electrolyser supply; dependence on imported electrolysers.
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Green Hydrogen Standard (MNRE, Aug 2023) | Well-to-gate **≤ 2 kg CO2-eq per kg H2** (12-month average) |
| GHCI certification scheme | **April 2025** |
| Certification nodal authority | **Bureau of Energy Efficiency** |
| Storage method | Fact |
|---|---|
| Compression | High-pressure composite cylinders |
| Liquefaction | **−253°C** (cryogenic) |
| **Metal hydrides** | Solid-state storage; absorbed in alloys, released on heating |
| Chemical carriers | **Ammonia, methanol, LOHC** (not CO2) |
- Water need: **~9 litres** purified water per kg hydrogen.

=== Andhra Pradesh – Green Hydrogen Policy and Valley
- **AP Green Hydrogen and Green Ammonia Policy (2023):** target **0.5 MTPA green hydrogen or 2.0 MTPA green ammonia** in five years (not 1 MTPA); nodal agency **NREDCAP**; RE may be sourced via open access, captive, **third-party sale and power exchange** (allowed, not prohibited); 100% SGST reimbursement for 5 years.
- **AP Integrated Clean Energy Policy 2024**: green hydrogen export; net zero 2047.
- **Green Hydrogen Valley Declaration, Amaravati, 19 July 2025:** AP as India's green hydrogen capital; goal **1.5 MMTPA by 2030** + 5 GW electrolyser manufacturing; nodal **SRM University-AP**; valley cluster at Kakinada.
| Project | Place |
|---|---|
| NTPC Green Energy hub | **Pudimadaka** (Anakapalli) |
| AM Green green ammonia | **Kakinada** |
| NTPC Simhadri | Seawater-to-hydrogen blending trials (Jan 2026) |
- Amaravati is not a Paradip green ammonia export terminal.
