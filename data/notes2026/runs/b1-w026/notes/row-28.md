=== Places of the Buddha's Life – Lumbini, Bodh Gaya, Sarnath, Kushinagar
| Event | Place | Today / marker |
|---|---|---|
| **Birth** | **Lumbini** grove (near Kapilavastu) | **Rumindei / Rummindei, Nepal**; **Ashoka's** inscribed pillar records "here the Buddha Shakyamuni was born" |
| Great Renunciation | **Kapilavastu** | – |
| **Enlightenment** | **Uruvela = Bodh Gaya**, under the Bodhi (pipal) tree, on the **Niranjana (Lilajan)** | Bihar; **Mahabodhi Temple** |
| **First sermon** | **Deer Park (Mrigadava), Sarnath = Rishipatana**, near Varanasi | UP; Ashoka's Lion Capital (State Emblem) |
| **Mahaparinirvana** (death) | **Kusinara / Kushinagar (Kasia)**, capital of the **Mallas** | Deoria–Kushinagar district, UP; **Mahaparinirvana Temple** |
- **Match (APPSC ASO, CDS 2011):** birth – **Lumbini**, enlightenment – **Bodh Gaya**, first sermon – **Sarnath**, death – **Kushinagar**; Rajgriha is the spare distractor.
- **Kushinagar:** the **Malla republic**, not the Lichchhavis or Sakyas; the Buddha died there at **80** under sal trees.
- **Mahaparinirvana Temple, Kushinagar:** reclining dying Buddha of **Chunar sandstone**, unearthed **1876–77**.
- **Niranjana river:** only **Bodh Gaya** of the sacred places stands on it (not Kushinagar, Lumbini, Rishipatana).
- **Rummindei pillar** marks the **birth** (Ashoka visited in his 20th regnal year); the king whose inscription names Lumbini = **Ashoka**.
- **"Birthplace of Buddhism":** APPSC G2 2011 calls **Sarnath** the "birthplace of Buddhism" – the **Dharma** and **Sangha** began there with the first sermon; Lumbini is only the Buddha's birthplace.
- **Ashtamahasthanas** (eight great sites): **7 in India**, Lumbini (Nepal) the eighth.
# Bodh Gaya facts
- **Mahabodhi Temple:** UNESCO World Heritage Site since **2002**.
- Original Bodhi tree destroyed by **Shashanka** (7th c., per **Hiuen Tsang**; earlier Pushyamitra Sunga by legend); the present tree was planted under **Alexander Cunningham** (1881) and is called the **fifth generation** (Bihar Tourism) – some count it the sixth, so BPSC dropped the question.
- **Maha Bodhi Society (1891):** **Anagarika Dharmapala**, to restore Bodh Gaya to Buddhists.
> Lumbini = birth; Bodh Gaya = enlightenment (Niranjana); Sarnath = first sermon; Kushinagar = death (Mallas).

=== Core Teachings – Nirvana, Karma, Anatta, Middle Path
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Nirvana (nibbana)** | **Main goal**; "**extinction of the flame of desire**"; end of suffering and of rebirth |
| **Middle Path (Madhyama Marga)** | Avoids both **self-indulgence and self-mortification** – the path to nirvana, not the goal |
| **Anicca** | **Impermanence** – "the world is unstable and transient" |
| **Anatta / Anatmavada** | **No permanent self (soul)** – a Buddhist doctrine, not Sankhya, Vedanta or Jain |
| **Dukkha** | Suffering |
| **Karma** | Intention-based morality; present state from past actions; **rebirth** till nirvana |
| Raft simile | Teaching as a raft – upaya (skilful means), not dogma |
- **Three marks:** Anicca, Anatta, Dukkha.
- **Beliefs (UPPSC 1992):** karma ✓, rebirth ✓, nirvana ✓; **God neither affirmed nor denied** – belief in God ✗; belief in God not needed for the Sangha.
- **Rejected Vedic authority**, animal sacrifice and ritual; stressed **purity of conduct / a moral life** over philosophical problems (the seed of later Buddhist philosophy lies in his own teachings – CGPSC 2023: both true).
- **Caste (UPPSC 1998):** Buddhism **did not abolish caste and class** as social facts, **challenged the Brahmins' top rank** (Kshatriyas listed first), and texts call some **jatis and crafts (hina-sippa) low** – exam key: all three true (PMF instead says the Buddha condemned varna).
- **Statements on the Buddha:** born in (the Sakya state of) **Kapilavastu** – strictly at Lumbini near it; enlightened at **Bodh Gaya**; **discarded the Vedic religion**; preached the **noble truths** – key: all four.
> Nirvana = goal; Middle Path = means; Anatta = no soul; God neither affirmed nor denied.
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 1 p.65-66; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.45-46; old app notes row 28 (sub 6)

=== Life Story – Dates, Renunciation, Epithets and Texts
- **Born 563 BC** (traditional; PMF "566–563 BCE"), Vaishakha Purnima; **died 483 BCE** at 80 (NextGen: c. 486 BC).
- **Four sights:** old man, sick man, corpse, ascetic → **Mahabhinishkramana** (Great Renunciation = **departure from home**) at **29**, with charioteer **Channa** and horse **Kanthaka**.
- **Six years** of austerities with **five ascetics**; tempted by **Mara**; **enlightenment at 35** on the 49th day of meditation.
- **Last words:** **"appamadena sampadetha"** – "all composite things decay; **strive diligently**".

|---|---|

| **"Light of Asia"** | **Buddha** – **Edwin Arnold's** poem (1879, subtitle "The Great Renunciation"), based on the **Lalitavistara** |
| **Jina, Kevalin, Nigantha Nataputta, Nayaputta** | **Mahavira** |
| **Prachhanna Buddha** ("crypto-Buddhist") | **Shankaracharya**, not the Buddha |
- **UPSC 2024:** Shakyamuni ✓, Tathagata ✓, Nayaputta ✗ → "2 and 3 only".
# Texts on his life
- **Buddhacharita** (Ashvaghosha, Sanskrit) = the **life history of the Buddha**; **Jatakas** = stories of his **previous births** (in the Sutta Pitaka); **Tripitakas** = the canon.
> 29 – 35 – 80 = Renunciation – Enlightenment – Parinirvana.

=== Sangha, Triratna and Monastic Terms
- **Triratna** (three jewels / refuges) = **Buddha, Dhamma, Sangha** – not the Tripitaka, not Sheel-Samadhi-Sangha; not Trivarga or Trimurti.
- **Match (APPSC G2 Mains 2025):** Jainism – **Triratna**; Buddhism – **Ashtanga Margam**; Saivism – **Linga**; Animism – **Nature worship**.
- **Ajivika – Niyati; Charvaka – materialism; Jainism – Anekantavada; Buddhism – Ashtangika Marga.**
# Admission
- Open to all **above 15**, **caste no bar**; barred: **criminals, lepers, those with infectious diseases**; slaves, debtors, children needed permission.
- **Women** admitted at **Vaishali** at the request of **Ananda** and **Mahapajapati Gotami** – the **first nun** (not Shravasti).
- **Sanghamitta** took the Bhikkhuni Sangha to **Sri Lanka**; it lasted till the **Chola invasion of 1017 AD**.

=== First Sermon – Dhammachakkappavattana
- **Name:** **Dhammachakra Pravartana / Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta** = "setting in motion the **Wheel of Dharma**" – the **first sermon at Sarnath** (not his "sight", norms or rituals).
- **Theme:** the **Four Noble Truths** and the **Eightfold Path** – so the Eightfold Path "forms the theme of" the **Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta** (UPSC 1998), not the Dipavamsa, Divyavadana or Mahaparinibbana Sutta.
  - **Mahaparinibbana Sutta** = his last journey and death; **Brahmajala Sutta** = first sutta of the Digha Nikaya.
- **Audience:** the **five former companions** – **Kondanna (Kaundinya), Bhaddiya, Vappa, Mahanama, Assaji (Ashvajit)** – who became **arhats**.
- **Vesak / Buddha Purnima** (Vaisakha full moon) marks **birth, enlightenment and Mahaparinirvana** – **not the first sermon**.
> Dhammachakra = first sermon = Sarnath = Four Truths + Eightfold Path.

=== Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path
| Truth | Content |
|---|---|
| 1. **Dukkha** | The world is full of **suffering** ("sabbam dukkham") |
| 2. **Samudaya** | Suffering has a **cause** – craving (tanha), ignorance, attachment |
| 3. **Nirodha** | **Cessation** of suffering = Nirvana |
| 4. **Magga** | The **path** to cessation = the **Eightfold Path** |
- **Order:** suffering → cause → cessation → path = **A-D-B-C / I-IV-II-III** (UPPSC 2006).
- **Buddha and Mirabai** both held the **world full of sorrow** (first Noble Truth).
# Eightfold Path (Ashtangika Marga)
| Group | Components |
|---|---|
| **Prajna** (wisdom) | Right View (samma-ditthi), Right Intention (**sankappa**) |
| **Sila** (ethics) | Right Speech, Right Action, Right Livelihood |
| **Samadhi** (mental discipline) | Right Effort, **Right Mindfulness**, Right Concentration |
- Called the **Middle Path** because it **avoids self-indulgence as well as self-mortification** (APSC 2017).

=== Pratityasamutpada – Dependent Origination
- **Pratityasamutpada (Paticca-samuppada):** every phenomenon arises from a cause; a **12-link** chain (**12 nidanas**, bhavachakra); removing ignorance ends the chain.
| Order | Links |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | **Avidya** (ignorance) → **Samskara** (impressions) → Vijnana (consciousness) → Namarupa |
| 5–8 | Shadayatana (six senses) → Sparsha (contact) → Vedana (feeling) → Trishna (craving) |
| 9–12 | Upadana (clinging) → Bhava (becoming) → Jati (birth) → **Jara-marana** (old age–death) |
- **Cause of Samskara = Avidya** (CGPSC 2024) – the first link and root of suffering; Vijnana comes after Samskara.
