=== Jain Councils and the Canon
# The two councils
| Council | Place, date | President | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First** | **Pataliputra**, c. **300 BCE** (Chandragupta Maurya's reign) | **Sthulabhadra** | Teachings arranged into **12 Angas**; boycotted by Bhadrabahu's returning monks → first split |
| **Second** | **Vallabhi (Valabhi)**, Gujarat, **512 CE** (Maitraka patronage) | **Devardhi Kshamashramana** | Canon **finally compiled and written down** in Ardhamagadhi; Upangas added; 12th Anga lost |
- **First council:** **Pataliputra** (UKPSC 2021; UPPCS 2004) – not Vaishali, Mathura, Ujjain, Vallabhi, Abu or Pava.
- **Svetambara Agama finally edited:** **Valabhi (Balabhi)** (UPPCS 2008).
- **CGPSC 2020:** "Svetambara canon includes 12 Angas" ✓; "Angas compiled at Vallabhi" ✗ (compiled at Pataliputra; Vallabhi only recompiled them) → **I true, II false**.
- **Andhra:** a **Jain conference at Venkatathatipuri** (Satavahana era), presided over by **Arhabali** – not one of the two councils.
# The canon
| Term | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Agamas / Siddhanta** | **Sacred books of the Jains** (APPSC G2 2011), compiled by the Ganadharas |
| **14 Purvas** | **Earliest** scriptures, preached by all Tirthankaras; held in the 12th Anga (Drishtivada); now lost |
| **12 Angas** | Main body of the canon – Acharanga, Sutrakritanga, Bhagavati … |
| Ang-bahya | **12 Upangas, 10 Prakirnas**, 6 Chhedasutras, 4 Mulasutras |
| Language | **Ardhamagadhi** Prakrit (UPPSC 2006) – not Pali (Buddhist) or Sanskrit |
- **Earliest holy book:** the **Fourteen Purvas** (State PSC) – not the 12 Angas or Upangas.
- **Acharanga Sutra:** code of conduct for monks; **Bhagavati Sutra:** doctrines.
- **Digambaras** hold that the Purvas and original Angas were **all lost** and reject the Svetambara canon; their chief texts are the Satkhandagama and Kasaya-pahuda.
- **Not Jain:** **Therigatha** – verses of Buddhist nuns in the Pali Khuddaka Nikaya (UPSC 1996); Acharanga, Sutrakritanga and Brihatkalpa are early Jain texts.

=== Spread of Jainism and Royal Patrons
# Spread
- **South:** during a **12-year famine** in Magadha (Chandragupta Maurya's reign) **Bhadrabahu** led monks with Chandragupta to **Shravanabelagola** – he is credited with taking Jainism south (CDS 2008), not Sthulabhadra, Sudharma or Indrabhuti.
- **Kalinga:** under **Kharavela** (c. 1st century BCE); his **Hathigumpha** inscription records Jain patronage.
- **Early Christian era centres:** **Mathura and Ujjain**.
  - **APPSC G1 2004:** the centre that grew under **Vajramuni** (1st–2nd c. CE) is keyed **Kausali** (official key; not confirmed in the notes).
- **Later:** Karnataka (Gangas, Kadambas, Rashtrakutas), Tamil country, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Malwa; it survives in **Gujarat, Rajasthan, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu**, mainly among **traders**.
- **UPSC 2004:** Kharavela's patronage (1st c. BCE) ✓; "spread south under Sthulabhadra" ✗; "Bhadrabahu's followers = Svetambaras" ✗; "early Jains worshipped images, unlike Buddhists" ✗ (initially neither worshipped images).

=== Jainism in Andhra and Telangana
# Royal patronage and centres
- **Early Kakatiyas** patronised **Jainism** (APPSC; Junior Asst) before turning to Shaivism; Andhra Jainism was mainly **Digambara** → "Jainism of the Digambara sect". Source: **Siddeshwara Charitra**.
- **Potla Cheruvu (Patancheru)** was a popular **Jain** centre in the **Eastern Chalukya** period (APPSC G2 2017 ×2).
- **Jain kshetras of Telangana:** Bodhan, Vemulawada, Potlacheruvu, Anmakonda (Hanamkonda), Kolanupaka.
| Centre | Note |
|---|---|
| Nedumba Vasati, **Bejawada** | Built by **Ayyana Mahadevi**, wife of Eastern Chalukya Vishnuvardhana |
| Kathikabharana Jinalaya, **Dharmavaram** | **Durgaraja** |
| **Ramatirtham** (Vizianagaram) | Hill centre; Ugradityacharya wrote Kalyanakaraka |
| Kadalalaya Basadi | **Mailama**, wife of Kakatiya minister Betana (1117 CE) |
| Aryavatam, Kolanupaka, **Munugodu** | Other Jain centres |
- Under the Eastern Chalukyas, **Shudras** took up Jainism for social advancement; a Jain monk fled **Rajaraja Narendra's** persecution to Hanamkonda (**Orugallu Kaifiyat**).
- **Vijayanagara:** **Devaraya II** built a Jinalaya in the Pan Supari Bazar.
- Jainism held rocky **Rayalaseema**; **Buddhism** held the Krishna–Godavari delta.
# Decline
- **Serious blow:** the **Virashaiva (Lingayat)** movement (APPSC G1) – Kalamukhas and Vira Shaivas persecuted Jains (Brahmaya at Govur and Potlacheru; **Ganapatideva** at Anmakonda).
- **Karnataka:** **Basavanna's** 12th-century Lingayat movement; **Tamil country:** the **Alvars and Nayanars**.

| Work | Author / note |

| Samayasara, Pravachanasara | Kundakundacharya (Saraswati gachcha at **Konakondla**) |

| Adipurana (Mahapurana) | **Jinasena** |
| Kalyanakaraka (medicine) | **Ugradityacharya** |

- **UPSC 2022 ×2:** Jain texts = **Parishishtaparvan and Trishashtilakshana Mahapurana** (2 and 4 only); **Nettipakarana and Avadanashataka are Buddhist**.
# Language
- **Early Jains discarded Sanskrit** and preached in **Prakrit**; the Agamas are in **Ardhamagadhi**.
- **Apabhramsha:** the Jains composed its **earliest important works** and its **first grammar** – so "they never used Apabhramsa for the earliest important works" is the **incorrect** statement (APPSC G1).
- Jain writers enriched **Prakrit, Apabhramsa, Kannada and Telugu**.
# Social base and decline
- **Traders and merchants** formed the social base (war and agriculture were barred); stressed **action / conduct** over birth.
- **Decline:** lack of **missionary zeal**, loss of royal patronage, sectarian split, severe austerity, Bhakti revival.

=== Digambara–Svetambara Split and Sub-sects
# The split
- **Story:** after the 12-year famine, **Bhadrabahu's** monks returned from the south, still naked and strict; those who stayed under **Sthulabhadra** wore **white** → **Digambaras** (sky-clad) and **Svetambaras** (white-clad), c. 300 BCE.
- **First founder of the Svetambara creed:** **Sthulabhadra** (RAS 1999) – Bhadrabahu led the Digambaras.
| Aspect | Digambara | Svetambara |
|---|---|---|
| Leader | **Bhadrabahu** | **Sthulabhadra** |
| Dress | Sky-clad monks | White robes |
| Women | Must be reborn as men for moksha | Can attain liberation |
| Mahavira | Never married | Married Yashoda |
| Monk's items | Broom and water pot | **14** items incl. clothes |
| Canon | Original Angas lost | Keep the 12 Angas |
# Sub-sects
| Sub-sect | Branch | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Sthanakvasi** | **Svetambara** | **Non-idolatrous**, prayer halls (sthanakas); **Lonka Shah** – a **Jain** sect (UPSC 2018) |
| **Terapanthi** | Svetambara (from the Sthanakvasis) | **Acharya Bhikshu**, 18th c. |
| Murtipujaka | Svetambara | Idol-worshipping original Svetambaras |
| **Yapaniya** | Grew from the **Digambaras** | Bridge sect; some Svetambara views; nude images; active in Karnataka and Andhra – a **Jain** sect (UPPCS 2010) |
| Bisapantha, Terapantha, Taranapantha | Digambara | Taranapanthis worship books, not idols |
