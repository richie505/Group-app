[90:0:2] # Medicine 2026 - Optogenetics
[90:0:3] - Protein used: **Channelrhodopsin-2 (ChR-2)** from the green alga **Chlamydomonas** (its eyespot) - a "**molecular light switch**" that opens in about **0.2 ms**.
[90:0:4] - Alga species: **Chlamydomonas reinhardtii**, a **unicellular green alga**; field - **neuroscience**.
[90:0:5] - **Hegemann** studied Chlamydomonas (early 1990s); **Nagel** expressed ChR-1/ChR-2 in **frog egg cells**; their joint paper - **2003**.
[90:0:6] - **Deisseroth** put ChR-2 into **rat neurons (2005)**, coined the name "**optogenetics**" (**2006**), and controlled **mouse whiskers (2007)**.
[90:0:7] - **Francis Crick** (1970s) first proposed using light to control neurons.
