=== Regulating Act 1773 – Background, Provisions, Supreme Court
# Before 1773
- **1764** Buxar → **1765** Diwani of Bengal, Bihar, Orissa granted by **Shah Alam II**.
- **1767:** first Parliamentary intervention – an Act obliged the Company to pay the British treasury **£400,000 a year**; dividend raised to/capped at **10%**.
- **1772:** Select Committee (**Burgoyne**) and Secret Committee; the Company sought a £1 million loan → **Regulating Act, 1773**.
# Provisions
- **First step** by the British Parliament to **control and regulate** the Company; recognised its political and administrative functions; foundation of central administration.
- **Governor of Bengal → Governor-General of Bengal** (**Warren Hastings**), with a **council of four** (Francis, Clavering, Monson, Barwell); majority decisions, GG only a **casting vote** (no veto) → Hastings outvoted.
- **Bombay and Madras subordinated** to Bengal.
- **Directors to submit** to the British Government all communications on the **civil and military affairs of Bengal** and the **revenues of India**.
- **Supreme Court at Calcutta (Fort William), 1774:** **1 Chief Justice (Sir Elijah Impey) + 3 judges** – India's **first Supreme Court**, set up by the **Regulating Act** (not Pitt's India Act, the 1858, 1909 or 1919 Acts, nor the Charter Act 1853 or GoI Act 1935); its jurisdiction vis-à-vis the Council was **left vague**. Later Supreme Courts: **Madras (1801)** – serving the Madras Presidency incl. Andhra – and Bombay (1823).
- Did **not** abolish the Court of Directors; did not create the Board of Control (1784).
- **⚠ Trap statements:** "Board of Control of six commissioners" = Pitt's Act 1784; "officers to disclose property within two months of joining" = Pitt's Act 1784; "Supreme Court's powers clearly defined" = false.

=== Act of Settlement 1781, Pitt's India Act 1784 and Act of 1786
# Amending Act 1781 (Act of Settlement)
- GG, Council and servants **exempt from the Supreme Court** for official acts; **revenue** matters excluded; Court to apply **personal laws of Hindus and Muslims**.
# Pitt's India Act 1784
- **Board of Control** created (**six commissioners**, incl. Chancellor of the Exchequer and a Secretary of State) for **political** affairs; **Court of Directors** kept **commercial** affairs → **double government** (ended by the GoI Act 1858).
- Company territories first called **"British possessions in India"**; GG's council cut to **three**; Pitt's Act came under **Warren Hastings**.
# Act of 1786
- Passed to meet **Lord Cornwallis's** conditions: the GG could **override his Council** (reject its decision) in special cases and also be **Commander-in-Chief** – not the Regulating Act, Pitt's Act, the Charter Act 1793 or the Act of 1813.
- **Charter Act 1793** extended the override to all future GGs and presidency Governors.
- **Order:** 1773 → 1781 → 1784 → 1786 → 1793.
# Landmarks vs non-landmarks
- Landmarks of constitutional development: Regulating Act 1773, Charter Act 1833, GoI Act 1919 …; the **Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955** (renamed from the Untouchability (Offences) Act in 1976) is **post-independence**, not a British-era landmark.
