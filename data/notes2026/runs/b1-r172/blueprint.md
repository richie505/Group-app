## 4. Key corrections and traps found
- **First census = 1872 (Mayo)**; NextGen Indian History p.3 prints 1871 – a slip.
- **NextGen also prints** VPA "1876" and First Factory Act "1882" – both wrong (1878 and 1881, Spectrum p.851).
- **Calcutta Supreme Court** came from the **Regulating Act 1773**, not Pitt's India Act (printed key contradicted its own explanation) – option added.
- **ASI:** exam keys say Curzon; strictly Canning 1861 – keyed Curzon with a ⚠ Disputed line.
- **Old explanation for Gateway of India** said the stone was laid in 1911 – it was laid on 31 March 1913 (key 1913 stands).
