## SUB 1 | Babur – Origin, Name and Titles
[1:0] - **Name:** **Zahir-ud-din Muhammad** (APPSC G1); "**Babur**" = **tiger** – he took the name Babur (CGPSC 2003); not Abdul Raheem, Umar Sheikh (his father) or Daulat Khan.
[1:1] - **Born 14 Feb 1483** at **Farghana (Ferghana)**, Central Asia (CDS 2001) – not Khiva, Khorasan or Seistan; ruler of Farghana at 12 (1494).
[1:2] - **Descent:** **Timur** (father's side) and **Chingiz Khan** (mother's side).
[1:3] - **Mughals were Turks** – **Chagatai Turks**, named after **Chagatai, second son of Chingiz Khan** (APPSC G1 2002; UDA 2010) – not Mongols, Afghans, Persians or Arabs; they called themselves **Timurids**.
[1:4] - **Title Padshah:** first adopted at **Kabul** (taken **1504**; title in 1507), dropping the ancestral "Mirza" (UPPCS 2015) – not Ferghana, Delhi or Samarkand (held only 8 months, 1501).
[1:5] - **Founder of Mughal rule in India:** **Babur** (1526) (SSC 2017).
[1:6] - **Contemporaries:** **Krishnadevaraya** of Vijayanagara (praised by Babur as the most powerful ruler in India), Sultan Quli of Golconda, Rana Sanga; the Mughals **did not interfere in the Deccan** in Babur's time.
[1:7] ~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval 04 p.80 (Zahiruddin Muhammad; Chagatai Turks), p.82 (Farghana 1494, Kabul 1504); NextGen Indian History - APPSC 04 p.20 (born in Farghana); app notes 1:127 (sub 0)
## SUB 2 | Babur's Empire, Coins and Buildings
[2:0] - **Empire at his death:** **Kabul**, **Punjab** and modern **Uttar Pradesh** (Delhi, Agra, up to Bihar's border) – **not modern Rajasthan** or Bengal → **I, II and III** (UKPSC 2003 ×2); **Badakhshan** given to **Humayun**; ruled through **jagirs** and **paraganas**; no administrative system (Mansabdari = **Akbar**).
[2:1] - **Death:** **Agra, December 1530**; first buried at Agra, later moved to **Bagh-e-Babur, Kabul** (SSC 2009) – Kabul is the trap.
[2:2] - **Coin:** silver **Shahrukhi** (Timurid style) – Babur (UPRO 2017); also copper coins.
[2:3] - **Babri Masjid, Ayodhya (1528)** – built by **Mir Baqi**, Babur's commander (UPPSC 1992) – not Babur himself.
[2:4] - **Buildings and gardens:** **Kabuli Bagh mosque** (Panipat), Sambhal mosque; **charbagh** gardens – **Ram Bagh (Aram Bagh), Agra** = first Mughal garden.
[2:5] ~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval 04 p.84 (died in Agra 1530, Bagh-e Babur; charbagh; Kabuli Bagh), p.85 (Babri Masjid – Mir Baqi); app notes 1:127 (sub 5, 7)
## SUB 3 | Tuzuk-i-Baburi (Baburnama)
[3:0] - Babur's memoirs, written in **Turki (Chagatai Turkish)** (State PSC) – not Persian, Arabic or Urdu.
[3:1] - **A–R (IAS 1993):** "Babur wrote his memoirs in Turki" ✓; "Turki was the official language of the Mughal court" ✗ – the court language was **Persian** → **A true, R false**.
[3:2] - **Translations:** Persian (1589) under Akbar by **Abdur Rahim Khan-i-Khanan**; English first by **Leyden and Erskine** (1826; "Leyden and Ruskin" in the UPRO 2017 options), later **Annette Beveridge** (from the Turki).
[3:3] - **India in the Baburnama:** **five Muslim and two Hindu** powers – the Hindu ones **Vijayanagara and Mewar** (UPPCS 1995) – not Orissa, Gujarat or Kashmir.
[3:4] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC 04 p.20 ("Tuzuk-i-Baburi" in Turkish), APPSC 05 p.19 (Baburnama in Turkish); PMF Ancient & Medieval 04 p.84-85; app notes 1:127 (sub 6)
