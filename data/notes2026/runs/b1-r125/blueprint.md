## 3. Repeats and trends
- **Most repeated:** Talikota year 1565 (6), Penukonda (5), Khanwa / Rana Sanga (7), Kuchipudi = AP (3+2 matches), Etikoppaka (3), Rath Yatra (2), Kshetrayya (2), Kuravanji (2), Vasantotsavam (2), Raja Wodeyar–Venkata II (2), Babur's empire (2), Khanwa A–R (2).
- **Key traps found:** Kshetrayya sang for Tirumala Nayaka, Vijayaraghava Nayaka **and** Abdullah Qutb Shah (NextGen: 1500 padams to Abdullah) – the printed key "Vijayaraghava" was too narrow; the same Kuravanji question is keyed two ways (Rukmini Arundale / Jakka Radhika); "NOT traditional AP culture" keys bamboo work although Bidri, Cherial and Nirmal are Telangana crafts after 2014; Babur did not introduce gunpowder or (strictly) cannons – the Bahmanis used them first.
- **Name of the 1565 battle:** NextGen credits "Rakshasa-Tangadi" to Nilakanta Sastri and "Bannihatti" to H.K. Sherwani; the app notes (and own MCQ f755e14d3f2e9) credit Rakshasa-Tangadi to Fr. Heras – both kept in the notes.
- Row 125 PYQs are mostly **other states'** culture (Rajasthan, UP, NE); AP-only angles are crafts/GI and a few festivals. The old notes' "Number game", "APPSC PYQ table" and "traps" lists were dropped; the old AP folk-dance table is asked only by the own MCQs (kept short).
