  [4:0.1] Rest of Coastal Andhra (12) | Kakinada, Konaseema, East and West Godavari, Eluru, NTR, Krishna, Guntur, Palnadu, Bapatla, Prakasam, SPSR Nellore
  [4:0.2] Rayalaseema (8) – inland south/south-west | Kurnool, Nandyal, Anantapur, Sri Sathya Sai, YSR Kadapa, Annamayya, Tirupati, Chittoor
[4:1] - Coastal Andhra: deltas, most farm output, highest population density. Rayalaseema: Deccan plateau, drought-prone, very low density; borders TN, Karnataka, Telangana. Palnadu, Bapatla and NTR are coastal.
[4:2] - **Physiographic divisions:** coastal plains (~950 km long, "Rice Bowl"), Eastern Ghats hill region, western (Rayalaseema) plateau.
[4:3] - **Rayalaseema plateau** is made of **gneissic** (granite-gneiss) Archaean rock; red soils.
[4:4] - **Oldest rocks:** Archaean **Dharwar** rocks (>2,500 million years) of Anantapur, Chittoor, Kadapa – e.g. the schist belt round **Kuppam**.
[4:5] TABLE head: Landmark | Fact

  [4:5.2] Billa Surgam | Kurnool, near Betamcherla; prehistoric
  [4:5.3] Gandikota | YSR Kadapa – Penna gorge, "Grand Canyon of India"
[4:6] - AP called the **"Kohinoor of India"** (Kohinoor diamond from Kollur, Krishna valley). Firsts: first linguistic State; Asia's first rubber dam (**Janjavathi**, Nagavali tributary); first private gold mine **Jonnagiri** (Kurnool).
