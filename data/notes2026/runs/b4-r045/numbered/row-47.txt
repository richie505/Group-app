## SUB 0 | Eastern Ghats – Character, Sections, Rocks and Limits
[0:0] # Character
[0:1] - Lie between the **western side of the Coastal Plain** and the **eastern side of the Deccan Plateau** – the **major hill system of AP**; AP = eastern part of the **Deccan plateau** + a large part of the **Eastern Ghats**.
[0:2] - **Discontinuous, eroded, residual hills** – not a continuous range, not young fold or volcanic mountains, no snow; cut by the **Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri**; **older and lower** than the Western Ghats (the Sahyadri are the Western Ghats).
[0:3] - In AP the **Godavari and Krishna** split the Ghats into two sections:
[0:4] TABLE head: Section | Name
  [0:4.0] North AP | Eastern Ranges
  [0:4.1] South AP | Kadapa Ranges
[0:5] - Geologically the old residual hills form the **Eastern Ghats Mobile Belt**.
[0:6] - Rocks: **khondalite and charnockite** (Archaean); **khondalite hills = highest bauxite-bearing mountains** → AP's **bauxite** lies in the **Eastern Ghats** (erstwhile Visakhapatnam and East Godavari: Ananthagiri, Araku, Galikonda, Chintapalli belt).
[0:7] - Rivers rising in the Eastern Ghats: **Vamsadhara** (biggest), **Nagavali**, Sabari, Machkund (UPSC 2021: Nagavali and Vamsadhara – not Brahmani or Subarnarekha).
[0:8] # Limits beyond AP
[0:9] - The Eastern Ghats run from the **Mahanadi valley** (Odisha) **to the Nilgiris**, where they **join the Western Ghats**; Mahendragiri is only the northern start in AP.
[0:10] - Eastern Ghats hills outside AP: **Garhjat** (Odisha), **Javadi** (Vellore–Tiruvannamalai, TN), **Shevaroy** (Yercaud, **Salem, Tamil Nadu**), **Kalvarayan** (TN), Kolli Malai (TN), Biligirirangan (Karnataka). **Tirumala** hills are also Eastern Ghats.
[0:11] - **North → south:** **Nallamalai – Javadi – Nilgiri – Anaimalai** (UPSC 2005); Kaimur – Nallamalai – Seshachalam – Biligirirangan.
[0:12] - **Salher** (Maharashtra) is a **Western Ghats** peak, not Eastern Ghats.
[0:13] > Most asked: "discontinuous and eroded"; Eastern Ranges (north) / Kadapa Ranges (south); Mahanadi to Nilgiris.
[0:14] ~~ Sources: Rocket AP Geography p.19, p.21-22, p.24-25; NextGen AP Geography 02 p.1-2; NextGen Indian Geography 05 p.13-14; app notes 4:47 (sub 6)

[1:2] - **Seshachalam = Eastern Ghats**, not the Nilgiri, Cardamom, Annamalai or Palani hills (Western Ghats).
[1:3] - **Long mountain pass (>300 km)** from the Seshachalam north to the Nallamala: Venkatagiri–Badvel–Porumamilla–Giddalur–Markapur; **Ganjikunta pass** links the two.

[1:5] - Velikonda, Nallamala, Seshachalam, Palakonda are AP ranges; **Javadi, Sirumalai, Karanthamalai** are in Tamil Nadu.

## SUB 2 | Coastline – Length, Rank and End Points
[2:0] # Length and rank (revised)
[2:1] - **India's coastline revised: 7,516.6 km → 11,098.81 km** (PIB, 4 Dec 2025; GIS retracing at 1:250,000 scale, not physical change).
[2:2] TABLE head: State | Revised coastline
  [2:2.0] Gujarat | 2,340.62 km (1st)
  [2:2.1] Tamil Nadu | 1,068.69 km (2nd)
  [2:2.2] Andhra Pradesh | 1,053.07 km (3rd) – Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways recalculation, 2025
[2:3] - **Old figure:** **974 km, 2nd longest** after Gujarat – the key in every paper before 2025.
[2:4] - AP is still the longest coast **on the east coast** in older questions; in the revised figures Tamil Nadu edges it.
[2:5] # End points and districts
[2:6] - AP coast runs along the Bay of Bengal (**eastern boundary**) from **Ichchapuram (Srikakulam)** in the north to **Tada** (Tirupati; earlier Nellore district) at Pulicat in the south – i.e. **Srikakulam to Nellore** in the 13-district map.
[2:7] - Longest district coast: **Srikakulam (~193 km)**; shortest: West Godavari (~23 km); 12 districts touch the coast (2022).
[2:8] - Beaches: **Yarada – Visakhapatnam**, **Manginapudi – Krishna (Machilipatnam)**, **Suryalanka – Bapatla**, **Mypadu – Nellore**.
[2:9] - Erosion (NCCR, Aug 2026): 38% of the east coast eroding; **Uppada (Kakinada)** worst.

## SUB 4 | Pulicat, Kolleru and Sriharikota
[4:0] TABLE head: Feature | Pulicat | Kolleru
  [4:0.0] Type | Brackish (saltwater) lagoon – India's 2nd largest after Chilika | Freshwater lake – AP's largest
  [4:0.1] Place | Southern tip of AP (Tirupati; earlier Nellore), shared with Tamil Nadu; on the Coromandel Coast – not north-eastern AP | Between the Godavari and Krishna deltas (Eluru, Krishna)
  [4:0.2] Links | Cut off from the Bay by Sriharikota island; Buckingham Canal; Arani river | Fed by Tammileru, Budameru; drains to the sea through Upputeru
  [4:0.3] Birds | Greater flamingos | Painted storks, grey pelicans; Ramsar site 2002
[4:1] - **Sriharikota:** barrier island **near Pulicat Lake**, in **Andhra Pradesh** (Nellore district when asked, now Tirupati), partly in **Sullurpeta** and partly in **Tada** mandal; **separates Pulicat from the Bay**; SDSC-SHAR – chosen 1969, operational 1971.
[4:2] - Coastal bird sites: Nelapattu (Tirupati), Uppalapadu (Guntur), Coringa WLS (Kakinada, Godavari mangroves), Krishna WLS, Kondakarla Ava, Rollapadu (Nandyal, dry grassland).
[4:3] - Threats: aquaculture encroachment (Kolleru); ISRO launches, thermal plants, sand mining (Pulicat) – not Himalayan glacial erosion. **Uppalapadu** (Guntur): spot-billed storks/pelicans, black-headed ibises; **Rollapadu**: Great Indian Bustard, **lesser florican**; Kondakarla Ava: freshwater lake near Anakapalle.
[4:4] - Waddepally and Laknavaram are Telangana lakes; Sur Sagar is in Gujarat.

## SUB 5 | Coastal Plains and Deltas
[5:0] - **Coastal plain:** from **Mahendragiri/Srikakulam to Pulicat/Nellore**, ~950 km long, ~60 km wide (narrow in the far north and south), up to 150 m; part of the **emergent Eastern Coastal Plains**.
[5:1] - AP falls in the **Eastern Ghats and Coastal region** physiographic division; order east → west: coastal plains – Eastern Ghats – western plateau.
[5:2] TABLE head: Coast | Where
  [5:2.0] Northern Circars | AP coast north of the Krishna (Odisha to Krishna) – the AP coastal plain in most keys
  [5:2.1] Coromandel | South of the Krishna to Kanyakumari – mainly Tamil Nadu, but includes the southern AP coast
[5:3] - **Deltas:** **Godavari** (Konaseema; central delta ~150 km wide) and **Krishna** form the fertile alluvial **"Rice Bowl"** (Krishna–Godavari delta), on the **Eastern Coastal Plains**; the **Penna** forms a small delta in Nellore.
[5:4] - Yarada hills / **Dolphin's Nose** (335 m): a rocky headland with **sea cliffs** on the Visakhapatnam coast, shielding Visakhapatnam Port.
[5:5] - **Krishna delta:** Krishna, Guntur, Bapatla; **Godavari delta (Konaseema):** Konaseema, East/West Godavari, Kakinada – **coconut and banana**. The **Penna** meets the sea at **Utukuru (Vutukuru)**, Nellore – not at Pulicat.
[5:6] - **Yanam** (Puducherry, ~30 sq km) is an enclave in the Godavari delta.
[5:7] - Mangroves: Coringa (Godavari estuary), **Krishna WLS (Krishna estuary)**; India's 2nd largest mangrove cover is **Gujarat** (ISFR 2023), not AP.
[5:8] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP Geography 01 p.23-27; Rocket AP Geography p.26-27; NextGen Indian Geography 06 p.2; PMF Indian Physical Geography 02 p.8; Rocket Indian Geography p.122 (Coromandel: Krishna delta–Kanyakumari); app notes 4:47 (subs 2-4)
