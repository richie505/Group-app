# Mandals and revenue divisions
- **After the 2014 bifurcation:** **50 revenue divisions** (13 districts); **Chittoor** had the most mandals (66), ahead of Anantapur.
- **Now (28 districts):** about **81 revenue divisions** and **688 mandals**; YSR Kadapa has the most mandals (40).
- Mandal counts: **West Godavari 19** (re-formed 2022, western Godavari delta, HQ **Bhimavaram**); **Parvathipuram Manyam 15**; Polavaram 11 at formation.
- Telangana: **33 districts**; revenue divisions **73** (c. 2020), **74** now.

=== Area and Rank
- **Area: 1,62,970 sq km** (often printed 1,62,975) = **4.96%** of India; 1,60,205 sq km = area left in 2014 before the 7 mandals were added; undivided AP ~2,75,000 sq km.
- **Rank by area:** **8th** in NextGen, Rocket, the AP Socio-Economic Survey and most keys (counting J&K as a State, as in 2014); strictly **7th** among today's States since J&K became a UT (31 Oct 2019) – after Rajasthan, MP, Maharashtra, UP, Gujarat, Karnataka.

=== Regions, Physiography and Rocks
| Region (2022 set of 26) | Districts |
|---|---|
| Uttarandhra ("Kalinga Andhra", 6) | ASR, Anakapalli, Parvathipuram Manyam, Visakhapatnam, Vizianagaram, Srikakulam |
| Rest of Coastal Andhra (12) | Kakinada, Konaseema, East and West Godavari, Eluru, NTR, Krishna, Guntur, Palnadu, Bapatla, Prakasam, SPSR Nellore |
| **Rayalaseema (8)** – inland **south/south-west** | Kurnool, Nandyal, **Anantapur**, Sri Sathya Sai, YSR Kadapa, Annamayya, Tirupati, **Chittoor** |
- Coastal Andhra: deltas, most farm output, highest population density. Rayalaseema: Deccan plateau, drought-prone, very low density; borders TN, Karnataka, Telangana. Palnadu, Bapatla and NTR are coastal.
- **Physiographic divisions:** coastal plains (~950 km long, "Rice Bowl"), Eastern Ghats hill region, western (Rayalaseema) plateau.
- Western (Rayalaseema) plateau lies mostly at **150–600 m**; Mahendragiri belongs to the Eastern Ghats hill region (Srikakulam), not this plateau.
- **Rayalaseema plateau** is made of **gneissic** (granite-gneiss) Archaean rock; red soils.
- **Oldest rocks:** Archaean **Dharwar** rocks (>2,500 million years) of Anantapur, Chittoor, Kadapa – e.g. the schist belt round **Kuppam**.
| Landmark | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Borra Caves** | **Ananthagiri hills**, ASR; limestone karst; Gosthani river; found by William King, 1807 |
| Belum Caves | Nandyal (earlier Kurnool); 2nd-longest cave of the subcontinent |
| Billa Surgam | Kurnool, near Betamcherla; prehistoric |
| Gandikota | YSR Kadapa – Penna gorge, "Grand Canyon of India" |
- AP called the **"Kohinoor of India"** (Kohinoor diamond from Kollur, Krishna valley). Firsts: first linguistic State; Asia's first rubber dam (**Janjavathi**, Nagavali tributary); first private gold mine **Jonnagiri** (Kurnool).
