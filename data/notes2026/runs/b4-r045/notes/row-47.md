=== Eastern Ghats – Character, Sections, Rocks and Limits
# Character
- Lie between the **western side of the Coastal Plain** and the **eastern side of the Deccan Plateau** – the **major hill system of AP**; AP = eastern part of the **Deccan plateau** + a large part of the **Eastern Ghats**.
- **Discontinuous, eroded, residual hills** – not a continuous range, not young fold or volcanic mountains, no snow; cut by the **Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri**; **older and lower** than the Western Ghats (the Sahyadri are the Western Ghats).
- In AP the **Godavari and Krishna** split the Ghats into two sections:
| Section | Name |
|---|---|
| North AP | **Eastern Ranges** |
| South AP | **Kadapa Ranges** |
- Geologically the old residual hills form the **Eastern Ghats Mobile Belt**.
- Rocks: **khondalite and charnockite** (Archaean); **khondalite hills = highest bauxite-bearing mountains** → AP's **bauxite** lies in the **Eastern Ghats** (erstwhile Visakhapatnam and East Godavari: Ananthagiri, Araku, Galikonda, Chintapalli belt).
- Rivers rising in the Eastern Ghats: **Vamsadhara** (biggest), **Nagavali**, Sabari, Machkund (UPSC 2021: Nagavali and Vamsadhara – not Brahmani or Subarnarekha).
# Limits beyond AP
- The Eastern Ghats run from the **Mahanadi valley** (Odisha) **to the Nilgiris**, where they **join the Western Ghats**; Mahendragiri is only the northern start in AP.
- Eastern Ghats hills outside AP: **Garhjat** (Odisha), **Javadi** (Vellore–Tiruvannamalai, TN), **Shevaroy** (Yercaud, **Salem, Tamil Nadu**), **Kalvarayan** (TN), Kolli Malai (TN), Biligirirangan (Karnataka). **Tirumala** hills are also Eastern Ghats.
- **North → south:** **Nallamalai – Javadi – Nilgiri – Anaimalai** (UPSC 2005); Kaimur – Nallamalai – Seshachalam – Biligirirangan.
- **Salher** (Maharashtra) is a **Western Ghats** peak, not Eastern Ghats.
> Most asked: "discontinuous and eroded"; Eastern Ranges (north) / Kadapa Ranges (south); Mahanadi to Nilgiris.

=== Eastern Ghats Ranges in AP – Seshachalam, Nallamala, Velikonda, Mahendragiri
| Range (local name) | District(s) | Key feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Mahendragiri (Mahendra Girulu)** | **Srikakulam**, into Odisha | Local name of the Ghats in Srikakulam |
| Papikondalu | Godavari districts | Godavari gorge; average height 915 m |
| **Nallamala** | Kurnool, Nandyal, Prakasam, Palnadu (also Telangana) | **Between the Krishna and the Penna**; biggest forest of AP; Srisailam; Chenchus; NSTR |
| Erramala | Kurnool–Nandyal | Water divide: Krishna (north) / Penna (south) |
| **Seshachalam** | **YSR Kadapa and Chittoor/Tirupati** | **Tirumala–Tirupati** hills; **Red Sanders (red sandalwood) endemic** – smuggled for export; **Seshachalam Biosphere Reserve** (2010) |
| Palakonda | YSR Kadapa–Tirupati | Southern extent |
| **Velikonda (Veliconda)** | SPSR Nellore–Prakasam | **South-eastern** AP; easternmost southern range |
| Simhachalam, Yarada | Visakhapatnam | Northern extent; coastal hills |
| Anantagiri | ASR | Source of the Gosthani; Borra Caves |
- **Tirupati / the Venkateswara temple** lie on the **Seshachalam** range (Eastern Ghats) – not Nallamala, Palakonda or Velikonda in the books; some keys (UPPCS 2018) give "Nallamala".
- **Seshachalam = Eastern Ghats**, not the Nilgiri, Cardamom, Annamalai or Palani hills (Western Ghats).
- **Long mountain pass (>300 km)** from the Seshachalam north to the Nallamala: Venkatagiri–Badvel–Porumamilla–Giddalur–Markapur; **Ganjikunta pass** links the two.
- **Horsley Hills** (Annamayya, Madanapalle; ~1,265 m) = locally **Enugu Mallamma Konda (Enugula Mallamma hills)**.
- Velikonda, Nallamala, Seshachalam, Palakonda are AP ranges; **Javadi, Sirumalai, Karanthamalai** are in Tamil Nadu.

=== Coastline – Length, Rank and End Points
# Length and rank (revised)
- **India's coastline revised: 7,516.6 km → 11,098.81 km** (PIB, 4 Dec 2025; GIS retracing at 1:250,000 scale, not physical change).
| State | Revised coastline |
|---|---|
| Gujarat | 2,340.62 km (1st) |
| **Tamil Nadu** | **1,068.69 km (2nd)** |
| **Andhra Pradesh** | **1,053.07 km (3rd)** – Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways recalculation, 2025 |
- **Old figure:** **974 km, 2nd longest** after Gujarat – the key in every paper before 2025.
- AP is still the longest coast **on the east coast** in older questions; in the revised figures Tamil Nadu edges it.
# End points and districts
- AP coast runs along the Bay of Bengal (**eastern boundary**) from **Ichchapuram (Srikakulam)** in the north to **Tada** (Tirupati; earlier Nellore district) at Pulicat in the south – i.e. **Srikakulam to Nellore** in the 13-district map.
- Longest district coast: **Srikakulam (~193 km)**; shortest: West Godavari (~23 km); 12 districts touch the coast (2022).
- Beaches: **Yarada – Visakhapatnam**, **Manginapudi – Krishna (Machilipatnam)**, **Suryalanka – Bapatla**, **Mypadu – Nellore**.
- Erosion (NCCR, Aug 2026): 38% of the east coast eroding; **Uppada (Kakinada)** worst.

=== Pulicat, Kolleru and Sriharikota
| Feature | Pulicat | Kolleru |
|---|---|---|
| Type | **Brackish (saltwater) lagoon** – India's **2nd largest** after Chilika | **Freshwater** lake – AP's largest |
| Place | Southern tip of AP (Tirupati; earlier Nellore), **shared with Tamil Nadu**; on the **Coromandel Coast** – **not** north-eastern AP | Between the Godavari and Krishna deltas (Eluru, Krishna) |
| Links | Cut off from the Bay by **Sriharikota** island; Buckingham Canal; Arani river | Fed by Tammileru, Budameru; drains to the sea through **Upputeru** |
| Birds | Greater flamingos | Painted storks, grey pelicans; Ramsar site 2002 |
- **Sriharikota:** barrier island **near Pulicat Lake**, in **Andhra Pradesh** (Nellore district when asked, now Tirupati), partly in **Sullurpeta** and partly in **Tada** mandal; **separates Pulicat from the Bay**; SDSC-SHAR – chosen 1969, operational 1971.
- Coastal bird sites: Nelapattu (Tirupati), Uppalapadu (Guntur), Coringa WLS (Kakinada, Godavari mangroves), Krishna WLS, Kondakarla Ava, Rollapadu (Nandyal, dry grassland).
- Threats: aquaculture encroachment (Kolleru); ISRO launches, thermal plants, sand mining (Pulicat) – not Himalayan glacial erosion. **Uppalapadu** (Guntur): spot-billed storks/pelicans, black-headed ibises; **Rollapadu**: Great Indian Bustard, **lesser florican**; Kondakarla Ava: freshwater lake near Anakapalle.
- Waddepally and Laknavaram are Telangana lakes; Sur Sagar is in Gujarat.

=== Coastal Plains and Deltas
- **Coastal plain:** from **Mahendragiri/Srikakulam to Pulicat/Nellore**, ~950 km long, ~60 km wide (narrow in the far north and south), up to 150 m; part of the **emergent Eastern Coastal Plains**.
- AP falls in the **Eastern Ghats and Coastal region** physiographic division; order east → west: coastal plains – Eastern Ghats – western plateau.
| Coast | Where |
|---|---|
| **Northern Circars** | AP coast **north of the Krishna** (Odisha to Krishna) – the AP coastal plain in most keys |
| **Coromandel** | **South of the Krishna** to Kanyakumari – mainly Tamil Nadu, but includes the southern AP coast |
- **Deltas:** **Godavari** (Konaseema; central delta ~150 km wide) and **Krishna** form the fertile alluvial **"Rice Bowl"** (Krishna–Godavari delta), on the **Eastern Coastal Plains**; the **Penna** forms a small delta in Nellore.
- Yarada hills / **Dolphin's Nose** (335 m): a rocky headland with **sea cliffs** on the Visakhapatnam coast, shielding Visakhapatnam Port.
- **Krishna delta:** Krishna, Guntur, Bapatla; **Godavari delta (Konaseema):** Konaseema, East/West Godavari, Kakinada – **coconut and banana**. The **Penna** meets the sea at **Utukuru (Vutukuru)**, Nellore – not at Pulicat.
- **Yanam** (Puducherry, ~30 sq km) is an enclave in the Godavari delta.
- Mangroves: Coringa (Godavari estuary), **Krishna WLS (Krishna estuary)**; India's 2nd largest mangrove cover is **Gujarat** (ISFR 2023), not AP.
