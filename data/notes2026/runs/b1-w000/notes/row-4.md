=== Mehrgarh – Earliest Farming and Settled Life
- **Mehrgarh** (Kachhi plain, Baluchistan, near the **Bolan Pass**; c. **7000 BCE**): **earliest agriculture** (wheat, barley), domestication and **settled life** in the subcontinent; excavated by **J.-F. Jarrige** (1974).
- Seven periods (I–VII) give remains **from the Stone Age (Neolithic) to the Harappan** period – unlike Amri, Kot Diji or Kalibangan.
| Period | Evidence |
|---|---|
| I (c. 7000 BCE) | **Aceramic** Neolithic; mud-brick houses; barley, wheat; sheep, goat, cattle |
| II | **Pottery** and **cotton** (c. 5000 BCE, oldest cotton) begin |
| III onward | **Copper** working, wheel-made painted pottery, beads |
- **Burials:** under or near the houses, flexed, with grave goods, sometimes with **goats**.
- Neolithic Baluchistan and Indus plains: crops **wheat, barley, dates, cotton**; main animals **sheep and goats**.
| Early village | Area | Excavator |
|---|---|---|
| Kili Gul Muhammad | Quetta valley | W. A. Fairservis |
| Damb Sadaat | Quetta valley (Early Harappan phase) | W. A. Fairservis |
| Rana Ghundai | Zhob-Loralai valley | E. J. Ross |
> Mehrgarh is a **Neolithic** site, not a Harappan city; "earliest settled life" and "earliest agriculture" = Mehrgarh (not Kili Gul Muhammad, Quetta or Kalat).

=== Early Harappan Cultures – Amri, Kot Diji, Sothi, Kalibangan-I
- Early (Pre-) Harappan phase **3300–2600 BCE** = **Regionalisation Era** (Hakra phase); copper, the wheel and the **plough** were already known.
| Culture / site | Area | Feature | Excavator |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amri | Sindh | Transitional pre- to Mature culture; **rhinoceros** remains; later Jhangar | N. G. Majumdar (1929), later J.-M. Casal |
| Kot Diji | Sindh | Fortified, **destroyed by fire**; many stone implements | Ghurye; F. A. Khan (1955) |
| Sothi-Siswal | Rajasthan–Haryana (Ghaggar) | Pre-Harappan pottery | A. Ghosh identified Sothi |
| Hakra | Cholistan (Ghaggar-Hakra) – not Gujarat | – | – |
| Banawali | Fatehabad, Haryana | Pre- and Mature phases | R. S. Bisht (1974) |
| Mitathal | Bhiwani, Haryana | Pre-, Mature and Late phases | Suraj Bhan (1968) |
- **Amalananda Ghosh** (1953) was the **first to see the likeness between a pre-Harappan culture (Sothi) and the Mature Harappan**.
- Pottery was regional (Amri, Kot Diji, Sothi-Siswal, Hakra wares), not uniform; Early Harappans used the potter's wheel.
# Kalibangan-I
- **Earliest ploughed field** with **criss-cross furrows** = **mixed cropping**; mud-brick fortification; pottery in **six fabrics**.
- Excavated **1961–69** by **B. B. Lal and B. K. Thapar**. The ploughed field is pre-Harappan; the fire altars belong to the Mature phase.

=== Origin Debate and Excavators
- **W. A. Fairservis** wrote "**The Roots of Ancient India**" (1971) on the prehistoric village cultures of Baluchistan.
| View on Harappan origin | Scholar |
|---|---|
| Indigenous growth | John Marshall |
| Migration from **Sumer** | **E. J. H. Mackay** |
| Diffusion of ideas from West Asia | **Mortimer Wheeler** |
| Local evolution from the Mehrgarh Neolithic | Current consensus |
- Kalibangan and Kot Diji foreshadow Mature buildings and pottery → urbanisation was **gradual and local**, not imported from Mesopotamia.
- Unsolved: the **gap between Neolithic Mehrgarh and the urban phase**.
- 23 Jun 2026: **Rakhigarhi** (~550 ha) shows unbroken Early to Mature occupation; Haryana protected **Tighrana and Mitathal** (**Bhiwani**).
