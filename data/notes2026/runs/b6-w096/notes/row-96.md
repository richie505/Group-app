=== Padma Awards and Bharat Ratna
# The Ladder
| Award | Rank | Given for |
|---|---|---|
| Bharat Ratna | 1st – highest civilian award | Exceptional service of the highest order |
| Padma Vibhushan | 2nd | Exceptional and distinguished service |
| Padma Bhushan | 3rd | Distinguished service of a high order |
| Padma Shri | 4th | Distinguished service in any field |
- Instituted **1954**; Padma lists announced on **Republic Day**; conferred by the **President** at **Rashtrapati Bhavan** (March–April). Bharat Ratna is **not** a gallantry award (**Param Vir Chakra** is military) and the Nobel is not Indian.
- **Balaji Raghavan case (1996)**: Padma awards are **not titles under Article 18**; not to be used as prefix or suffix.
- Counts: **2009 – 93 Padma Shri**; **2025 – 139** (7 / 19 / 113); **2026 – 131** (5 / 13 / 113, announced 25 Jan 2026, two duo cases counted as one each). Second investiture 2026: **Ganatantra Mandap**, Rashtrapati Bhavan.
