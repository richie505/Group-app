=== NOTES 1:149 | Sikhism - the Ten Gurus, Guru Granth Sahib, the Khalsa; Banda Bahadur and Ranjit Singh
## SUB 0 | Founder and Basic Terms
[149:0:0] - **Guru Nanak** (**1469-1539**) founded Sikhism; born **1469 at Talwandi (Nankana Sahib)** near the **Ravi**, Khatri family; died **22 September 1539**.
[149:0:1] - Teachings: **Nirguna** devotion, **Ek Onkar** (God is one), **Naam Simran**, **Sangat**; rejected rituals of both religions.
[149:0:2] - Chose **Bhai Lehna (Guru Angad)** as successor before death (UPPSC 2023).
[149:0:3] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [149:0:3.0] Sikh | Disciple (Sanskrit shishya)
  [149:0:3.1] Khalsa | "Pure"
  [149:0:3.2] Panj Pyare | Five volunteers baptised with sacred nectar
  [149:0:3.3] Anand Karaj | Sikh marriage: four circumambulations of the Granth while Lavan hymns are sung
  [149:0:3.4] Dal Khalsa | Joint force of the Sikh misls
  [149:0:3.5] Adalat-i-Ala | Ranjit Singh's high court at Lahore
[149:0:4] # Kabir (often paired with Nanak)
[149:0:5] - Disciple of **Ramananda** (not Guru Nanak); raised by weavers Niru and Nima; followers **Kabirpanthis**; work **Bijak**.
[149:0:6] - One God by many names (Ram, Rahim, Allah, Hari); condemned rituals of both faiths; Hindu-Muslim unity.
## SUB 1 | The Ten Gurus in Order

  [149:1:0.0] 1 | Nanak Dev | Founder; Ik Onkar; appointed Angad
  [149:1:0.1] 2 | Angad Dev | Started and standardised Gurmukhi script
  [149:1:0.2] 3 | Amar Das | 22 Manjis (Manji system); strengthened langar
  [149:1:0.3] 4 | Ram Das | Founded Ramdaspur (Amritsar); 500 bighas of land from Akbar; dug the holy tank
  [149:1:0.4] 5 | Arjan Dev (1581-1606) | Compiled Adi Granth (1604); built Harmandir Sahib; first martyr Guru
  [149:1:0.5] 6 | Hargobind | Militarised the Panth; released from Gwalior Fort (1619) with 52 prisoners (Bandi Chhor Divas)

  [149:1:0.9] 10 | Gobind Singh | Born 1666 at Patna; Khalsa 1699; Five Ks; finalised Guru Granth Sahib; died 1708
[149:1:1] - After Gobind Singh, Guruship passed to the scripture: **Guru Granth Sahib = eternal Guru**.
## SUB 2 | Guru Granth Sahib
[149:2:0] - **Compiled** as the Adi Granth by **Guru Arjan (5th)**; **finalised** by **Guru Gobind Singh (10th)**; script **Gurmukhi**.
[149:2:1] - APPSC 2019: the Sikh scripture = **Adigranth**.
[149:2:2] - Contains teachings of **six Gurus** and non-Sikh saints: **Kabir**, Sufi **Sheikh (Baba) Farid**, **Namdev**, **Ravidas/Raidas** (**41 shabdas**), also **Jaydev**.
[149:2:3] Compiled by Arjan (5th); finalised by Gobind Singh (10th); 116 hymns = Tegh Bahadur; 41 shabdas = Ravidas.
## SUB 3 | The Khalsa
[149:3:0] - Founded by **Guru Gobind Singh** on **Baisakhi, 13 April 1699**, at **Takht Keshgarh Sahib, Anandpur**; Panj Pyare baptised with sacred nectar.
[149:3:1] - Made Sikhs a military order; surnames **Singh / Kaur**.
[149:3:2] - **Five Ks**: **Kesh** (hair), **Kangha** (comb), **Kara/Kada** (bangle), **Kachera/Kachha** (undergarment), **Kirpan** (sword).
[149:3:3] Kirtan is NOT one of the Five Ks.
## SUB 4 | Gurus and Mughal Emperors
[149:4:0] TABLE head: Guru | Emperor | Conflict
  [149:4:0.0] Ram Das | Akbar | Friendly - land grant
  [149:4:0.1] Arjan Dev | Jahangir | Executed 1606, charged with supporting rebel Prince Khusrau
  [149:4:0.2] Hargobind | Jahangir | Held in Gwalior Fort, freed 1619
  [149:4:0.3] Tegh Bahadur | Aurangzeb | Executed at Delhi, 1675
  [149:4:0.4] Gobind Singh | Aurangzeb, then Bahadur Shah | Khalsa fought Mughals and hill rulers; Zafarnama letter accusing the Mughals of betrayal
[149:4:1] - 350th martyrdom year of Guru Tegh Bahadur observed (Jan 2026).

## SUB 6 | Banda Bahadur
[149:6:0] - Original name **Lachhman Dev**; name given by Guru Gobind Singh, who commissioned him at **Nanded (1708)**.
[149:6:1] TABLE head: Year | Event
  [149:6:1.0] 1709 | Seized Samana
  [149:6:1.1] 1710 | Defeated Wazir Khan at Chappar Chiri; conquered Sirhind → first sovereign Sikh state, capital Lohgarh
  [149:6:1.2] 1715 | Eight-month siege of Gurdas Nangal; captured
  [149:6:1.3] 1716 | Executed at Delhi by Farrukhsiyar for refusing to convert
[149:6:2] - **Coins** minted in the names of **Guru Nanak and Guru Gobind Singh**.
[149:6:3] - **Sirhind Fateh Diwas**: 12-14 May.
## SUB 7 | Sikh Misls
[149:7:0] - After 1708, Khalsa self-rule between the **Sutlej and Jamuna**.
[149:7:1] - After Banda, the Khalsa formed **12 misls**: e.g. **Bhangi, Ahluwalia, Ramgarhia, Kanhaiya, Sukerchakia, Phulkian**; joint force = **Dal Khalsa**.
[149:7:2] - **Ranjit Singh** (Sukerchakia) united them into the kingdom of Lahore.
## SUB 8 | Maharaja Ranjit Singh
[149:8:0] - Born **13 Nov 1780, Gujranwala**; father **Maha Singh**, chief of the **Sukerchakia misl**, died when he was 12; council of regency 1792-1797. Called **"Lion of Punjab"**.
[149:8:1] - **Zaman Shah** invaded Punjab 1798; Ranjit Singh returned his cannons that fell in the Jhelum.
[149:8:2] - **1799**: captured **Lahore**, made it capital (APPSC 2018).
[149:8:3] - **Treaty of Amritsar, 25 April 1809**, with **Lord Minto** (JPSC 2023): **Sutlej** the boundary; stopped his expansion south.
[149:8:4] - **1813**: got the **Kohinoor** from Afghan ruler **Shah Shuja** (Nadir Shah had taken it from Delhi in **1739**).
[149:8:5] - Secular Punjabi state; "God intended me to look upon all religions with one eye..."; high court **Adalat-i-Ala**.
[149:8:6] - Died **1839** → chaos. Successors: **Kharak Singh** (1839), Nau Nihal Singh, Sher Singh, **Duleep (Dalip) Singh** - minor, regent **Rani Jindan**; last king of the Sikh empire (**1843-1849**); died in **Paris, 22 October 1893**.
