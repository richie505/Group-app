[9:1] - **Farm to Home**: farmers to consumers via FPOs, linked with **Rythu App, Digi Rythu Bazaar App and Rythu Bazaars** (not Rythu Bazaars alone).
[9:2] - **Eastern Ghats seed network**: **Gasaba village, Peda Bayalu mandal, Alluri Sitharama Raju district**; **Soil and Seed Information Centre, Kiloguda** – 2001 with 45 varieties → **360+** now.
[9:3] - **Araku Kouni**: AP's first **tribal millet menu** – recipes of **Alluri Sitarama Raju** district; first in **Haritha restaurants (APTDC) in ASR district**; documented by **Indian Culinary Institute, Tirupati**.
[9:4] # Livestock
[9:5] - **FMD vaccination**: **16 March – 29 April 2026** under NADCP; free, door-to-door; calves above **four months**. FMD is a **viral** disease.
[9:6] - **Heat-stress advisory**: **clean drinking water through the day**, **community water troughs** in villages, sheds **east–west**; **crossbred / high-yield animals are more vulnerable** than indigenous breeds. **India** has the world's largest cattle population.
[9:7] - **Pashu Samvardhaka Margadarshini**: **livestock e-magazine** launched by Minister **K. Atchannaidu**.
