## SUB 0 | Forest Cover and Recorded Forest Area – ISFR Data
[0:0] # Recorded (notified) forest area
[0:1] - **Recorded Forest Area (RFA): 37,258 sq km = 22.86%** of AP – **8th in India** (ISFR 2019, 2021, 2023; AP Forest Department, May 2024). "5th" is the usual wrong option.
[0:2] - Legal classes: Reserved **85.8%**, Protected **13.6%**, Unclassed **0.6%**; notified forest per APSES 2024-25: 37,221.11 sq km (22.84%); revised 37,421.11 sq km (~22.96%) in 2025-26.
[0:3] - Land-use statistics show **36.88 lakh ha (22.63%)** of AP as forest land – the figure some keys give as "area covered by forests".

## SUB 1 | Forest Types and Natural Vegetation
[1:0] # Types by rainfall
[1:1] TABLE head: Forest type | Rainfall | Where

  [1:1.1] Dry deciduous (largest) | 75–100 cm | erstwhile Kadapa, Kurnool; Seshachalam–Nallamala belt
  [1:1.2] Short thorn | < 70 cm | Kadapa, Kurnool, Chittoor, Anantapur
  [1:1.3] Tidal / mangrove ("Koringa forests") | coastal | mouths of the Godavari and Krishna – Coringa, Krishna WLS; coastal alluvial belt of Krishna district
[1:2] - **AP forests are mainly tropical deciduous**; most of the natural vegetation is **dry deciduous**.
[1:3] - **Seshachalam hills = dry deciduous** (not wet evergreen, desert or mangrove); **Papikonda NP = moist deciduous**.
[1:4] - **ISFR 2019 forest types:** **Southern Dry Mixed Deciduous** is the largest (15,128 sq km), then Dry Deciduous Scrub (12,551); Mangroves and Littoral 418 sq km.
[1:5] - **Coringa:** mangrove forest where the **Godavari** meets the Bay of Bengal backwaters; tidal forests were taken up on a large scale in the **6th Five-Year Plan**.
[1:6] - **Biggest forest: Nallamala** (dry deciduous; Gundlakamma forest zone part of it).
[1:7] - **Biotic provinces:** **Deccan Plateau** is AP's largest (about 53% of forests), then Central Plateau, Eastern Highlands, East Coastal Plains.
[1:8] - Forest extent: north **Simhachalam hills**; south **Palakonda and Nallamala**; west **Balaghat**.
[1:9] # Mangroves
[1:10] - Godavari–Krishna estuaries: called India's **2nd-largest mangrove ecosystem** (APSES) – true only on the **east coast**; by ISFR area AP is behind West Bengal, Gujarat and A&N.
[1:11] - ISFR 2023: AP mangrove cover **+13.01 sq km**; **MISHTI** launched **5 June 2023**.

## SUB 2 | Tree Species, Forest Produce and Plantations
[2:0] # Species
[2:1] - **Red sanders (*Pterocarpus santalinus*)**: **endemic** to AP's **Seshachalam, Palakonda and Veligonda** ranges (southern Eastern Ghats); a **dry deciduous** tree, **not** a tropical rainforest tree; **greatest economic value**; IUCN **Endangered**, WPA **Schedule IV**, **CITES Appendix II**; thermal-imaging drones against smuggling (Feb 2026).
[2:2] - **Axlewood** = ***Anogeissus latifolia*** (dry deciduous).
[2:3] - **Eucalyptus** = family **Myrtaceae**; *Cylindrocladium* causes **dieback/blight of *Eucalyptus tereticornis***.
[2:4] - Teak – dry deciduous; **bamboo, sandalwood** – northern Eastern Ghats moist hill forests.
[2:5] - **Thimmamma Marrimanu:** giant **banyan** at Kadiri (erstwhile Anantapur, now **Sri Sathya Sai** district), about 70 km from Horsley Hills; **Guinness record 1989** as the **largest banyan (canopy) in the world**; about 550 years old; canopy usually quoted as "about 5–6 acres" (Guinness: about 4.7 acres).
[2:6] # Revenue and agencies
[2:7] - **Forest produce revenue:** older AP keys name **bamboo** as the top earner; red sanders auctions have earned more in recent years. Forest revenue ₹32.81 crore (2023-24), ₹19.63 crore (2024-25 to Dec); NTFP over ₹500 crore a year.
[2:8] - **GCC** (HQ Visakhapatnam) buys minor forest produce; **TRIFED** (1987, Multi-State Co-op Societies Act 1984; Tribal Affairs) – MSP for MFP; **APFDC** – export agent for **red sanders and beedi leaf**; **CAMPA** – compensatory afforestation through an Annual Plan of Operation.
[2:9] - Wood banks: teak **Kapavaram**, red sanders **Tirupati**, bamboo Rajamahendravaram; craft wood banks (Kondapalli, Etikoppaka, Udayagiri, Bobbili …) give timber to artisans at **no profit**.

[2:13] # Trees outside forests and programmes
[2:14] - **TOFI = Trees Outside Forests in India**: **MoEFCC + USAID**, launched **8 Sep 2022**; AP one of **7** States; AP Forest Department launch **Nov 2022**.
[2:15] - **ISFR 2019 rural TOF in AP:** **mango (*Mangifera indica*)** is the most numerous species (about 28–29%).
[2:16] - **Vanam-Manam**: green cover outside reserved forests – **5.29 crore** seedlings (2024-25 to Dec).
[2:17] - Van Adhiniyam 1980: 26,924 ha diverted to 2017-18; 2026 changes allow commercial plantations on degraded forest land (33% goal). **16th FC** counts open forests with density weights. **Rekapalli revolt (1879)** against British forest laws.
