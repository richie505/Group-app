## SUB 1 | Koeppen, Trewartha and Thornthwaite – Climatic Regions of India
[1:0] TABLE head: Koeppen code | Meaning | Area
  [1:0.0] Amw | monsoon, short dry season | Western coastal region south of Goa
  [1:0.1] As | monsoon, dry summer | Coromandel coast (Tamil Nadu)
  [1:0.2] Aw | tropical savanna (wet and dry) | most of the Peninsula; AP
  [1:0.3] BShw | semi-arid steppe | rain-shadow belts, parts of Rajasthan, Gujarat, Haryana
  [1:0.4] BWhw | hot desert | western Rajasthan
  [1:0.5] Cwg | monsoon with dry winter (humid sub-tropical) | Ganga plain, North-East India incl. North Bengal
  [1:0.6] Dfc | cold humid winter, short summer | Arunachal Pradesh
  [1:0.7] E | polar / tundra | High Himalaya, Ladakh
[1:1] - Trewartha (modified Koeppen, temperature–rainfall based): Am – tropical rainforest of the west coast and North-East; Aw – Peninsula; BS/BW – Rajasthan, rain-shadow Deccan; **Caw (Ganga plain) = Koeppen Cwg**; H (Himalaya) ≈ Dfc/E.
[1:2] - Thornthwaite: **moisture index and potential evapotranspiration**; perhumid (Western Ghats–west coast, North-East) > humid > sub-humid > **semi-arid (rain-shadow interior Deccan)** > **arid (western Rajasthan and Kutch)**.
[1:3] - Arid regions cover about one-third of the Earth.

## SUB 2 | Climate Change Action – NAPCC and State Plans
[2:0] - **NAPCC**: released **30 June 2008**; **8 missions** – Solar, Enhanced Energy Efficiency, Sustainable Habitat, Water, Himalayan Ecosystem, Green India, Sustainable Agriculture, Strategic Knowledge; **no nuclear mission**.
[2:1] - SAPCCs: **Odisha first (2010)**; shift from mitigation to **district-level adaptation** (2020 on); **Jharkhand Action Plan on Climate Change: 2014** (now revising as SAPCC 2.0).
[2:2] - **AP SAPCC 2025-30**: 8 energy-sector adaptation measures; **1 lakh EV charging stations**.
[2:3] - DST vulnerability report (2021): 8 highly vulnerable States – **Jharkhand (most)**, Mizoram, Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Assam, Bihar, Arunachal Pradesh, West Bengal; AP is not among them. Adaptation spending **5.6% of GDP** (FY22).
[2:4] ~~ Sources: napccindia.moef.gov.in/sapcc/state/jharkhand (SAPCC 2014); app notes 4:54 (sub 5)
## SUB 3 | Climate Change Signals and Impacts on AP
[3:0] - **2024** = India's warmest year since **1901** (IMD anomaly **+0.65°C**; 2025: +0.28°C); **446** heatwave days.
[3:1] - WMO: 2025 about **1.43°C** above pre-industrial; 2015–2025 the 11 warmest years.
[3:2] - 2026: IMD forecast a below-normal monsoon for most of AP, citing **El Nino**.
[3:3] - Coastal erosion at **Uppada** and the Krishna coast: **rising seas and cyclonic waves**; **Godavari delta sinking** – land subsidence exceeds regional sea-level rise.
[3:4] - Coastline **1,053.07 km** (third longest), **28 districts**; earlier 974 km, 26 districts.
