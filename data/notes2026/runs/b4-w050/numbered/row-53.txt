## SUB 1 | AP's Share and Rank in India
[1:0] - AP = **"Ratna Garbha"** – the treasure house of minerals.
[1:1] - **IMYB (2017 state review):** AP is the **sole producer of apatite** in India; leading producer of **barytes**, ball clay, dolomite, garnet, laterite, **limestone**, quartz, silica sand, vermiculite.
[1:2] - AP's share of India's **resources**: **92% barytes**, 40% calcite, **41% mica**, **31% kyanite** and garnet, 19% titanium minerals, 16% bauxite, 15% dolomite, 13% sillimanite, 12% vermiculite and limestone.
[1:3] TABLE head: Mineral | AP's share / rank
  [1:3.0] Barytes | India's top producer; ~92–93% of India's reserves; nearly one-quarter of world output; a minor mineral since 2015
  [1:3.1] Mica | First in India – reserves 40.5% (~41%), production 70.2%; Rajasthan 2nd; Jharkhand, Rajasthan also produce, Goa does not
  [1:3.2] Heavy-mineral beach sands | ~327 Mt of India's 1,078 Mt = 30.4%; AP first in monazite
  [1:3.3] Limestone | production: Rajasthan > MP > AP (third); largest reserves: Karnataka
  [1:3.4] Diamonds (NMI) | 18,22,955 carats (18.23 lakh) of India's 319.22 lakh
  [1:3.5] Kyanite | ~30–31% of India (NMI 2020); tin reserves are in Chhattisgarh, not AP
  [1:3.6] Gold (IMYB 2021, 1.4.2020) | 47.17 tonnes resources; Chigargunta–Bisanattam (Chittoor, Kolar schist belt) reports handed to AP after the MMDR Amendment 2015
  [1:3.7] Asbestos | AP produces chrysotile (Kadapa) but holds only a few % of reserves – Rajasthan 59%, Karnataka 36% of India's asbestos resources
[1:4] - Barytes production share: keys give **92%** – strictly the IMYB 92% is AP's share of **resources**; nearly all of India's barytes output comes from Mangampet.
[1:5] - After beach sands, **bauxite** has the largest share of AP's major-mineral resources.
[1:6] - **APMDC** (AP Mineral Development Corporation) incorporated **1961**.
[1:7] - World mica (2022): **China first**, Madagascar second.

## SUB 3 | Gold, Uranium and Beach Sands
[3:0] # Gold
[3:1] - **Swarnagiri (Jonnagiri)** – **India's first (and largest) private-sector gold mine** after Independence; **Tuggali mandal, Kurnool**; **GeoMysore Services**, **30-year lease**; ~1,000 kg a year; opened 2026.
[3:2] - **Ramagiri (Anantapur)** – old gold field in the Dharwar greenstone belt; gold-bearing quartz in Anantapur, Chittoor, Kurnool.
[3:3] - India's **oldest gold field = Kolar** (Karnataka).
[3:4] # Uranium
[3:5] - **Tummalapalle (YSR Kadapa)**, **Kadapa basin**: principal AP uranium deposit, **low-grade** ore; **2011**: huge reserves confirmed – among the world's top 20; mine and plant working since **2012**.
[3:6] - Jaduguda, Narwapahar = Jharkhand; **radium** is extracted from **pitchblende**.
[3:7] # Beach sands and rare earths
[3:8] - Ilmenite, rutile, zircon, garnet, **sillimanite**, **monazite → thorium**; along the coast from **Srikakulam to Nellore** (Visakhapatnam, Vizianagaram, Godavari, Krishna, Guntur, Prakasam); **Bhavanapadu (Srikakulam)**; monazite at Bheemunipatnam.
[3:9] - **Kurnool** is inland – **no beach sands**.
[3:10] - NGRI found rare earth elements in syenite rocks of **Anantapur** (2023); critical-mineral mapping through **APMDC** (2026).
