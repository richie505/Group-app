=== The Dictator and the Organisation in Andhra
- **Konda Venkatappayya** (had met Gandhi with an Andhra team; "Father of the Andhra State Movement") was appointed **Dictator for Andhra** by the **Andhra Congress Committee** to conduct the **Salt Satyagraha / CDM** – the first leader of the CDM in Andhra (APPSC G2 2000, 2008, 2017) – not Prakasam, Pattabhi, Gadicherla or N.G. Ranga.
- **Each district had its own dictator, a war council and a military-style camp (sibiram)** – the camps served as salt satyagraha headquarters (idea credited to **Pattabhi Sitaramayya**).
| Camp | Place |
|---|---|
| Guntur camp | Guntur |
| **Pallepadu Ashram** | **Nellore** |
| Bellary camp | Rayalaseema |
| **Vedavanam** (T. Prakasam's house) | **Madras** – the centre of Salt Satyagraha activity in Madras (not Krishna district) |
| Eluru National School | West Godavari – volunteers joined (May 1930) |
- **Programme:** towns – **picketing foreign-cloth shops**; villages – **making salt, cutting toddy palms, picketing toddy and arrack shops, raiding salt depots**; main target the **salt tax**. Later echo: women's anti-arrack agitation at **Dubagunta (Nellore), 1991**.
- Spectrum: district salt marches in East and West Godavari, Krishna and Guntur; merchants funded the Congress; but mass support of the NCM level was missing.

=== Kakinada Bomb Case, Repression and End
- **Kakinada Bomb Case:** circle inspector **Mustafa Ali** brutally attacked the satyagrahis of the **Seethanagaram (Sita) Ashram**; in revenge **Prativadi Bhayankarachari**, founder of the revolutionary "**Ujjivana Bharata Samithi / Sammelan**" in the **Godavari district**, threw a bomb at him at Kakinada (failed); he was sentenced (14 years) and sent to the **Andaman Cellular Jail**; he wrote "**Andaman Jeevitam**". NextGen heads the case "1930" (the year of the ashram raid); the app's MCQ dates the bomb case **1933**.
- Repression: **Venneti Satyanarayana** lathi-charged at **Rajahmundry**; **15 January 1932** police firing at **Berhampore** (Willingdon's repression).
- **Viceroys:** **Irwin** – Dandi March 1930, Pact 1931; **Willingdon** – repression of 1932-34.
