## SUB 1 | E-pragati, RTGS and Grievance Platforms
[119:1:0] - **e-Pragati**: **whole-of-government** State Enterprise Architecture; developed under **CM N. Chandrababu Naidu** (2014-19); **14 packages / 72 projects / 33 departments**; core hubs **People, Land, Entity, GIS, IoT** (**no Finance Hub**); goal **faceless, paperless, cashless**.
[119:1:1] - Campaign aims: AP Fibre Grid to every gram panchayat; State Data Centre hosting all data and apps; **People Hub** database of citizens for welfare delivery. **Computerising land registration is NOT an e-Pragati aim** (that was CARD, 1998).
[119:1:2] - **RTGS** (Real Time Governance Society, **2017**): real-time monitoring, analytics; Command and Control Centre; **AWARE 2.0** disaster early warning; **AP DigiVerify** blockchain certificates; AI4PGRS.
[119:1:3] - **State Data Lake**: single source of truth for **40+ departments**; concerns **privacy, algorithmic bias**. **Mana Mitra**: **865 services** (SES 2025-26).
[119:1:4] - **Grievance platforms:** **Prajavani** → **Meekosam** (online forum, 2014-19) → **Spandana** (2019-24) → **PGRS**.

## SUB 2 | IT Policies and Definitions
[119:2:0] - **AP IT Policy 2021-24**: approved 30 June 2021; in force **1 April 2021 – 31 March 2024**; **55,000 jobs** aim.
[119:2:1]   - **Local procurement:** IT needs **up to Rs 1 crore** procured **exclusively** from IT companies/start-ups **operating in AP**, provided quality is comparable.
[119:2:2]   - **Entry-level IT job**: CTC **up to Rs 5 lakh**; high-end above Rs 10 lakh; an **MNC** has facilities in at least one country other than home.
[119:2:3] - **IT & GCC Policy 4.0 (2024-29)**: in force **12 Dec 2024**; GCC = a multinational's **own offshore unit**; co-working spaces in Tirupati, Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada.
[119:2:4] - **LIFT Policy 4.0** = **Land Incentive for Tech Hubs** (land for GCCs and IT infra developers).
[119:2:5] - **Innovation and Startup Policy 4.0**: by **ITE&C Department**; **20,000 startups**, **1 lakh jobs**; implemented by **AP Innovation Society**.
[119:2:6] - **Data Center Policy 4.0**: up to **1 GW**. **Drone Policy 4.0**: 100 companies, 25,000 pilots, **15,000 direct** jobs.
[119:2:7] - Legal: IT Act 2000 (MeitY; last amended **2008**); **AP IT (Electronic Service Delivery) Rules 2011** (electronic delivery mandatory); AP Cyber Security Policy 2017 → APCSOC (2018).
[119:2:8] > Exam angle: policy clauses (local procurement, CTC definitions) – asked in Polytechnic/NTRUHS papers.

[119:2:10] - **FinTech Valley Vizag:** **₹100 crore fund of funds** for fintech start-ups announced on **25 January 2017** (CM N. Chandrababu Naidu).
[119:2:11] - **IT Policy 2021-24 for foreign investment:** **quick, time-bound approval of investment proposals** (digital libraries and concept cities serve other aims).

## SUB 4 | Connectivity – Fiber Grid, Digital Village, APSFL
[119:4:0] - **AP Fiber Grid Phase-I**: **24,000 km** **IP-MPLS ring** (not GPON), all **13 districts**; **NOC at Visakhapatnam**.

=== NOTES 3:121 | Agro-climatic Zones of Andhra Pradesh and Their Cropping Suitability
## SUB 0 | Six Agro-climatic Zones and Their Districts
[121:0:0] - Residual AP has **six agro-climatic zones** (SES 2024-25 Table 5.2): regions defined by climate, soil, topography – they decide the best cropping systems.
[121:0:1] TABLE head: # | Zone | Districts (13 old districts)
  [121:0:1.0] 1 | North Coastal | Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam
  [121:0:1.1] 2 | Godavari | East Godavari, West Godavari
  [121:0:1.2] 3 | Krishna | Krishna, Guntur, Prakasam
  [121:0:1.3] 4 | Southern | Chittoor, YSR Kadapa, SPS Nellore
  [121:0:1.4] 5 | Scarce Rainfall | Kurnool, Anantapuramu
  [121:0:1.5] 6 | High Altitude and Tribal Areas | Hill and tribal parts of Srikakulam, Visakhapatnam, East Godavari
[121:0:2] - **No "Eastern Zone"** and **no "Low Altitude" zone**: the sixth is **High** Altitude and Tribal Areas.
[121:0:3] - **Chittoor** is Southern, not North Coastal or Scarce Rainfall; **Nellore** is Southern, not Krishna or Scarce Rainfall; Kurnool is Scarce Rainfall, not Southern.
[121:0:4] - Undivided AP (NARP): **7 zones** (incl. North and Southern Telangana; Krishna-Godavari as one zone).
[121:0:5] - Not the six **Article 371-D** zones (administrative zones for public employment).
[121:0:6] - Zones are still listed against the **13 old districts**; AP now has **28 districts** (Aug 2026).
[121:0:7] > Exam angle: number of zones (6), match zone ↔ district, odd-one-out zone name.

## SUB 1 | Rainfall, Drought and Cloud Seeding
[121:1:0] TABLE head: Zone | Annual rainfall
  [121:1:0.0] North Coastal | 1,000-1,100 mm
  [121:1:0.1] Krishna-Godavari | 800-1,100 mm
  [121:1:0.2] Southern | 700-1,100 mm
  [121:1:0.3] Scarce Rainfall | 500-750 mm (lowest)
  [121:1:0.4] High Altitude and Tribal | above 1,400 mm (highest)
[121:1:1] - **Average rainfall** of (undivided) AP: about **890-940 mm** (Group-I 2003 key: 890 mm). June-Dec 2024: **960.3 mm** vs normal **860.0 mm** (+11.7%).
[121:1:2] - **Drought-prone**: Scarce Rainfall Zone (Kurnool, Anantapuramu). Highest rainfall: North Coastal + tribal highlands.
[121:1:3] - **Cloud seeding:** first artificial rain experiment in AP at **Atmakur (Anantapur)**; the 2004 programme used **silver iodide** and **sodium chloride**.

## SUB 0 | Cooperative Credit Structure – Three Tiers in AP
[122:0:0] TABLE head: Tier | Institution in AP | Number / fact
  [122:0:0.0] State (apex) | APCOB – AP State Cooperative Bank | 18 branches
  [122:0:0.1] District | DCCBs – District Cooperative Central Banks | 13 DCCBs; 442 branches (2024-25), 436 (2025-26)
  [122:0:0.2] Village | PACS – Primary Agricultural Cooperative Credit Societies | 2,042 (APSES 2024-25); 2,024 (2025-26)
[122:0:1] - **Short-term cooperative credit is three-tiered**: PACS (village) → DCCB (district) → State Cooperative Bank (apex).
[122:0:2] - **DCCB = federation of the PACS of a district**: it works as their **integrated unit**, financing and supervising them.
[122:0:3] - **DCCB funds:** **entry fee, share capital, deposits and loans** (borrowings from APCOB/NABARD) – all of these.
[122:0:4] - **Long-term credit:** nationally by **ARDBs** (Land Development / Cooperative and Rural Development Banks).
[122:0:5] - **AP's integration:** the **Single Window** system (**1987**, on the Mohan Kanda recommendations) put short- and long-term loans under one cooperative umbrella; AP integrated the two from **1986**; primary agricultural development banks abolished in **1987**.
[122:0:6] - **APCCADB merged into APCOB** from **30 April 1994** (Single Window Act, 1994); **APCOB** now refinances DCCBs for long-term credit.
[122:0:7] > Traps: DCCB (not APCOB, not an Agricultural Development Bank) is the district integrated unit of PACS; three tiers for short-term credit.
