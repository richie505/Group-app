## SUB 0 | Bahmani Kingdom – Founder and Capitals
[0:0] - **Founded:** **1347** (14th century) by **Alauddin Hasan Bahman Shah** – **Hasan Gangu / Zafar Khan** (UPPSC 1995, 2005; UKPSC 2002; State PSC) – not Firuz Shah, Mahmud Gawan, Malik Ambar or Mujahid Shah.
[0:1] - **First capital:** **Gulbarga**, renamed **Hasanabad** (CGPSC 2014) – not Bidar or Daulatabad.
[0:2] - **Four regions:** **Gulbarga, Daulatabad, Berar, Bidar** (Golconda was not one).
[0:3] - **Andhra link:** **Kapaya Nayaka** helped Hasan Gangu found it.
[0:4] - **Bidar:** **Ahmad Shah I** shifted the capital from Gulbarga to Bidar (APPSC G2 2003).
[0:5] - **Mahmud Gawan:** minister of **Muhammad III**; kingdom largest under him; **executed in 1481** – the kingdom then broke into five sultanates.
[0:6] - **⚠ Dates:** **1336** = Vijayanagara; **1347** = Bahmani.
[0:7] ~~ Sources: NextGen qutabshahis p.1; NextGen post kakatiyas p.4; app notes 1:100 (sub 0)
## SUB 1 | Krishnadevaraya and Prataparudra Gajapati
[1:0] - **Defeated Gajapati:** **Prataparudra Gajapati** (APPSC PYQ) – Kalinga campaign **1513–1519**.
[1:1] - **Order of campaign:** 1. **Udayagiri** (Nellore) – fell after an **18-month siege**; 2. Kandukuru, Vinukonda, Addanki, **Kondaveedu**; 3. **Kondapalli**, Bejawada; 4. march to **Cuttack**.
[1:2] - **Victory pillars:** at **Potnuru (Potnur)** and **Simhachalam** (APPSC G2-B 1991) – not Penugonda, Lepakshi or Hampi.
[1:3] - **Peace (c. 1518–19):** Prataparudra's daughter **Annapurna Devi** (Bhadradevi / Tukkadevi) married Krishnadevaraya; **lands north of the Krishna returned** to the Gajapati.
[1:4] - **⚠ Not a term:** **Cuttack and the Gajapati heartland were NOT annexed**.
[1:5] - **Earlier:** **Saluva Narasimha** drove the Gajapatis out of south-eastern Andhra by **c. 1470** and occupied land up to the Godavari.
[1:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen vijayanagara kingdom p.19-20; app notes 1:100 (sub 5, 7)
## SUB 2 | Gajapati Rulers in Andhra
[2:0] - **Title:** **Gajapati = "Lord of Elephants"** – taken by the Suryavamsi rulers of Orissa (UPPSC 2016); capital **Cuttack**; rule **c. 1434–1541**.
[2:1] - **Order of Andhra campaigns:** **Kapilendra → Purushottama → Prataparudra**.
[2:2] TABLE head: Ruler | Andhra campaign
  [2:2.0] Kapilendra (Kapileswara) – founder | Attacked Tiran Andhra (1448) (Penugonda inscription); annexed Rajamahendravaram after 1446, ending the Rajahmundry Reddis; took Kondaveedu c. 1450
  [2:2.1] Hamvira (Kapileswara's son) | Defeated the Bahmani army attacking Devarakonda; 1471 – Bahmani puppet
  [2:2.2] Purushottama | Won the throne after a struggle; refused to help Muhammad III
  [2:2.3] Prataparudra | Lost Udayagiri, Kondaveedu, Kondapalli to Krishnadevaraya
[2:3] # Key Places
[2:4] TABLE head: Place | Note
  [2:4.0] Rajamahendravaram | Reddis had been helped by Vijayanagara commander Mallappa Odayar (Draksharama inscription, 1444)
  [2:4.1] Kondaveedu | Under Vijayanagara till 1449 → fell to the Gajapatis c. 1450
  [2:4.2] Udayagiri (Nellore) | Major Gajapati stronghold in southern Andhra
