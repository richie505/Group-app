=== Appointment, Tenure, Removal and Status of the CEC and ECs
# Appointment
- **Art. 324(2):** the CEC and other ECs are appointed by the **President** (subject to any law of Parliament) – **not** elected by Parliament, not appointed by the PM, the Home Minister or the CJI.
- **Anoop Baranwal v. Union of India (2 March 2023, Constitution Bench):** until Parliament made a law, appointment on the advice of **PM + Leader of Opposition (or leader of the largest opposition party) in Lok Sabha + CJI**; it also said it is **desirable** that **other ECs** be removable only on the grounds of an SC judge – it said nothing about **Regional Commissioners**.
- **CEC and Other ECs (Appointment, Conditions of Service and Term of Office) Act, 2023** (Act 49 of 2023; replaced the **1991 Act**):
| Stage | Composition / rule |
|---|---|
| Search Committee | Headed by the **Law Minister** + two Secretary-rank members; shortlists **five** names |
| Selection Committee | **PM (chair)**, LoP / largest opposition party leader in Lok Sabha, a **Union Cabinet Minister nominated by the PM** (replaces the CJI); may pick names **outside** the shortlist |
| Appointment | **President**, on the Selection Committee's recommendation |
- Criticism: gives the government a **2:1** majority; challenged by ADR and **Jaya Thakur (Jaya Thakur v. Union of India)** – pending as of 2026.
# Committees that recommended a three-member ECI
- **Dinesh Goswami Committee on Electoral Reforms (1990)** – appointed by the V.P. Singh Government, chaired by Law Minister Dinesh Goswami; first main recommendation: **"Election Commission to be a three-member body"**, CEC appointed in consultation with the **CJI and LoP**.
- **Tarkunde Committee (1974-75)** – **unofficial**, set up by **Jayaprakash Narayan**; also proposed a multi-member Commission chosen by a PM-LoP-CJI panel. **Rajamannar** and **Sarkaria** dealt with Centre-State relations.
# Tenure, salary and status
| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Term | **6 years or age 65, whichever is earlier** (1991 Act; 2023 Act s.9 – **no reappointment**); may resign by writing to the President |
| Powers and pay | CEC and ECs have **equal powers** and **equal salary** = a **Judge of the Supreme Court**; differences decided by **majority** |
| Status | The CEC's status is that of a **Supreme Court judge** (pay, removal) – **not** that of a Governor or a Speaker |
| Service conditions | Cannot be varied to the CEC's disadvantage after appointment (Art. 324(5)) |
- **Not** "5 years or 62" and **not** "equal powers but unequal salaries" (both wrong – UPSC 2002).
# Removal (Art. 324(5))
| Office | How removed |
|---|---|
| **CEC** | Only **in like manner and on like grounds as a Supreme Court judge** – by the President on an address of **both Houses** passed by **special majority** on **proved misbehaviour or incapacity**; motion needs **100 Lok Sabha / 50 Rajya Sabha** signatures (Judges Inquiry Act) |
| **Election Commissioners and Regional Commissioners** | By the **President on the recommendation of the CEC** – so the CEC's security of tenure is **not** available to them |
| **State Election Commissioner** | Like a **High Court judge** (Art. 243K(2)) |
# Gaps the Constitution leaves
- **No qualifications** and **no term** prescribed for members; **retiring ECs are not debarred** from further government appointment (contrast **CAG – Art. 148(4)**, **UPSC Chairman – Art. 319**). (NextGen's "ineligible for office of profit after retirement" is **not** a constitutional bar.)
- ECI expenses are **not charged** on the Consolidated Fund (a long-standing demand); it depends on the Law, Home and DoPT Ministries.

=== Constitutional Status and Composition – Single-Member to Multi-Member
# Status
- A **permanent, independent constitutional body** set up directly by the Constitution – **Part XV ("Elections"), Articles 324-329**; basis **Art. 324**; an **all-India body common to the Centre and the States**.
- Art. 324 is a **"reservoir of power"** (Mohinder Singh Gill, 1978) – used only where **no law holds the field**.
- Seat **Nirvachan Sadan**, New Delhi; training arm **IIIDEM**, New Delhi.
# Composition (Art. 324(2)-(4))
- **CEC + such number of ECs as the President fixes**; today **CEC + 2 ECs = a three-member body** (APPSC G2 2025 – "three member body" and "constitutional body" are true; "appointed by the CJI" is false).
- The CEC acts as **chairman**; the President may appoint **Regional Commissioners** after **consulting the Election Commission**.
- All members have **equal powers**; decisions by **majority** – upheld in **T.N. Seshan v. Union of India (1995)**.
# Single-member to multi-member
| Period | Status |
|---|---|
| 1950 – 15 Oct 1989 | **Single-member** – CEC only (**originally one**) |
| **16 Oct 1989** | Two ECs added – **first three-member Commission** (work grew after the **61st Amendment** cut the voting age 21 → 18) |
| **Jan 1990** | Posts abolished – single-member again |
| **1 Oct 1993 onwards** | Multi-member (CEC + 2 ECs) **continuously** |
- So the ECI was **not** multi-member "since inception"; "made multi-member / three-member" in a 1987-1990 option set = **1989**.

=== Powers and Functions of the ECI
# Three kinds – administrative, advisory, quasi-judicial
- Superintendence, direction and control of **electoral rolls** and **conduct of elections** to **Parliament, State Legislatures, President and Vice-President** (Art. 324(1)); decides **every aspect from the announcement of elections to the declaration of results** (APPSC G1 2023).
- **Officers on election duty work under the ECI's control**, not the government's; staff preparing rolls are deemed on **deputation** to the ECI. It can issue guidelines and order **transfers/suspension** – it does **not** order the government to **dismiss** officials.
- Delimits constituencies on the basis of the **Delimitation Commission Acts**; prepares and revises **electoral rolls** – **Special Intensive Revision** under **Art. 324 + Section 21, RPA 1950**.
- Notifies **dates and schedules** (the Home Ministry has no such role), scrutinises nominations, fixes **expenditure limits**, can **cancel polls** for booth capturing or rigging.
- **Recognises parties and allots symbols** (**Election Symbols (Reservation and Allotment) Order, 1968**) and acts as a court on **splits/mergers and symbol disputes** – not the Speaker, PM or President.
- **Model Code of Conduct** – not a statute; rests on **Art. 324**; applies **from the announcement of the schedule** (**Harbans Singh Jalal, 1997**); **Part VII** = party in power.
- Advises the **President on MPs' disqualification (Art. 103)** – the **President decides after obtaining the ECI's opinion and acts on it** – and the **Governor on MLAs' (Art. 192)**.
- Advises the President whether elections can be held in a State under **President's Rule** (for extension beyond one year) – it does **not recommend** President's Rule.
# What it does NOT do
- **Election disputes:** decided by **courts** – election petitions under **RPA 1951** go to the **High Court** (appeal to SC); President/VP disputes to the **Supreme Court (Art. 71)**. The ECI **cannot** give the final verdict on disputes and does not try petitions.
- **Anti-defection (Tenth Schedule)** → **Speaker/Chairman**.
- Delimitation of J&K (2022) was by a Commission under **Justice Ranjana Desai**; **Assam** was delimited by the **ECI itself** (effective Aug 2023).

=== Elections Conducted by the ECI and by the State Election Commission
| Under the ECI (Art. 324) | Not under the ECI |
|---|---|
| **Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha** | **Panchayats and Municipalities** → **State Election Commission** (Arts. **243K** – panchayats, **243ZA** – municipalities) |
| **State Assemblies and Councils** | **Speaker, Deputy Speaker** – chosen by the House (Art. 93; States Art. 178) |
| **President and Vice-President** | **Deputy Chairman, RS** (Art. 89(2)); **Chairman of a Legislative Council** (Art. 182) |
- So "the ECI administers elections to Panchayats and Municipalities" is **false**; co-operative societies and banks are not SEC elections either.
| Basis | ECI | SEC | Chief Electoral Officer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source | Art. 324, Part XV | Arts. 243K, 243ZA (73rd/74th Amendments, 1992) | Sec. 13A, RPA 1950 – **not** a constitutional authority |
| Appointed by | President | **Governor** | ECI, in consultation with the State Government |
| Removal | Like an **SC judge** | Like a **High Court judge** (243K(2)) | By the ECI |
- **District:** the **Collector = District Election Officer / District Returning Officer**; appoints **Returning and Presiding Officers**.
- **AP:** State Election Commissioner **Anil Chandra Punetha** (local-body polls).
- Other facts for app MCQs: emergency provisions are **Part XVIII**; the federal-to-unitary switch in an Emergency is a **unique feature** of the Constitution (Laxmikanth), **not** borrowed from Weimar (Weimar = suspension of FRs).

=== Election Disputes and Polling-Day Situations
- **Art. 329(a):** laws on delimitation and allotment of seats **cannot be questioned in court**.
- **Art. 329(b):** an election to Parliament/State Legislature can be challenged **only by an election petition** under **RPA 1951** – tried by the **High Court** (original jurisdiction), appeal to the **Supreme Court**.
- **Art. 71:** President/VP election disputes → **Supreme Court**. The ECI **cannot review a declared result**.
| RPA 1951 section | Situation | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 57 | Riot, open violence, natural calamity | **Presiding or Returning Officer** adjourns; resumes with ECI approval; **only those who had not voted** may vote |
| 58 | EVM/ballot box unlawfully taken, destroyed or tampered | ECI declares the poll **void**, fixes a fresh poll |
| 58A | **Booth capturing** | Adjournment or countermand |
