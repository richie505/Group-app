=== NOTES 2:62 | Election Commission of India – Composition, Appointment (2023 Act), Powers and Functions
## SUB 0 | Constitutional Status
[62:0:0] - A **permanent, independent, constitutional body** (APPSC GS 2024), **common to the Centre and the States**.
[62:0:1] - **Part XV, Articles 324-329**, titled **"Elections"**; constitutional basis **Article 324**.
[62:0:2] - **Art. 324(1):** vests the **superintendence, direction and control** of elections and **electoral rolls** for **Parliament, State Legislatures, President and Vice-President** in the ECI.
[62:0:3] - Established **25 January 1950** → observed as **National Voters' Day since 2011**.
[62:0:4] - Seat: **Nirvachan Sadan**, New Delhi; training arm: **IIIDEM**, New Delhi.
[62:0:5] - Art. 324 is a **"reservoir of power"** (Mohinder Singh Gill, 1978) – usable only where **no law holds the field**; it cannot normally override an existing law.
## SUB 1 | Composition and Decision Rule
[62:1:0] - **One CEC + as many ECs as the President fixes**; now **CEC + 2 ECs** = **three-member body** (APPSC G2 2025); CEC presides.
[62:1:1] - **Equal powers**, **equal salaries and allowances**; differences settled by **majority**.
[62:1:2] - Appointed by the **President** – **Art. 324(2)**, subject to a law of Parliament.
[62:1:3] - **Regional Commissioners** may be appointed by the President after consulting the EC.
[62:1:4] - PYQ key: **status of the CEC = Governor of a State**; salary = **Supreme Court judge**.
[62:1:5] # Single-Member to Multi-Member
[62:1:6] TABLE head: Period | Status
  [62:1:6.0] 1950 – Oct 1989 | Single-member (CEC only)
  [62:1:6.1] 16 Oct 1989 | Two ECs added – first three-member Commission, after the 61st Amendment lowered voting age 21 → 18; served only briefly
  [62:1:6.2] 1990 | Single-member again
  [62:1:6.3] 1 Oct 1993 onwards | Multi-member continuously; majority rule upheld in T.N. Seshan v. Union of India (1995)
## SUB 2 | Appointment – the 2023 Act and Anoop Baranwal
[62:2:0] # The Road to the Act
[62:2:1] TABLE head: Year | Event
  [62:2:1.0] 1975 | Tarkunde Committee, set up by Jayaprakash Narayan – PM + LoP + CJI panel; three-member Commission
  [62:2:1.1] Before 2023 | President appointed on the PM's advice alone
  [62:2:1.2] 2023 | Anoop Baranwal v. Union of India – Constitution Bench: until Parliament makes a law, CEC and ECs chosen by PM + LoP (or leader of largest opposition party) in Lok Sabha + CJI
  [62:2:1.3] 2023 | CEC and Other ECs (Appointment, Conditions of Service and Term of Office) Act, 2023 (Act No. 49 of 2023) – replaced the 1991 Act; CJI replaced by a Union Cabinet Minister
[62:2:2] # Procedure under the 2023 Act
[62:2:3] TABLE head: Stage | Composition / rule
  [62:2:3.0] Search Committee | Headed by the Law Minister + two Secretary-rank members; shortlists five names
  [62:2:3.1] Selection Committee | PM (chair), LoP (or largest opposition party leader) in Lok Sabha, a Union Cabinet Minister nominated by the PM; may pick names outside the shortlist
  [62:2:3.2] Appointment | President, on the Selection Committee's recommendation
[62:2:4] - Criticism: gives the government a **2:1 majority**; challenged by **ADR** and **Jaya Thakur** (Jaya Thakur v. Union of India, 2024 – pending as of May 2026).
[62:2:5] - **May 2026:** SC said the **CJI's place was only temporary**, until a law; questioned the lack of "one absolutely neutral person"; asked whether the LoP was "ornamental".
[62:2:6] - **March 2026:** **CJI Surya Kant** moved the petitions to a Bench without a future CJI.
[62:2:7] Anoop Baranwal panel had the **CJI**; the 2023 Act put a **Union Cabinet Minister** in his place.
## SUB 3 | Tenure, Salary, Removal and Gaps
[62:3:0] TABLE head: Item | Rule
  [62:3:0.0] Term | 6 years or age 65, whichever earlier; no reappointment (2023 Act)
  [62:3:0.1] Salary | = Supreme Court judge; service conditions cannot be varied to their disadvantage
  [62:3:0.2] CEC removal | Like a Supreme Court judge – Art. 324(5) (Sec. 11(2) of the 2023 Act repeats it); proven misbehaviour or incapacity; special majority in both Houses; motion needs 100 Lok Sabha or 50 Rajya Sabha signatures
  [62:3:0.3] ECs and Regional Commissioners | Removed by the President only on the CEC's recommendation (Sec. 11)
  [62:3:0.4] State Election Commissioner | Like a High Court judge (Art. 243K(2))
[62:3:1] # Constitutional Gaps (Listed Flaws)
[62:3:2] - **No qualifications** and **no term** prescribed for members in the Constitution.
[62:3:3] - **No bar on post-retirement office** for retiring ECs (APPSC G1 2024).
[62:3:4] - Contrast: **CAG (Art. 148(4))** and **UPSC Chairman (Art. 319)** have constitutional bars.
[62:3:5] - ECI depends on **DoPT, Law and Home Ministries**; demand to charge its expenses on the **Consolidated Fund of India**; no power over inner-party democracy or party finances.
[62:3:6] "The Constitution bars the CEC from post-retirement office" = FALSE.

## SUB 5 | Powers and Functions
[62:5:0] - ECI decides **every step** from announcement of schedule to **declaration of results** (APPSC G1 2022).
[62:5:1] # Administrative
[62:5:2] - Prepares and revises **electoral rolls**; **Special Intensive Revision (SIR)** under **Art. 324 + Section 21, RPA 1950**.
[62:5:3] - Notifies schedules, scrutinises nominations, regulates expenses.
[62:5:4] - Decides territorial constituencies under the **Delimitation Commission Act**. **J&K delimitation (2022)** – Commission under **Justice Ranjana Desai**; **Assam** delimited by the **ECI itself** (effective Aug 2023).
[62:5:5] - **Model Code of Conduct** – **not a statute**; rests on **Art. 324**; applies **from the day the schedule is announced** (**Harbans Singh Jalal, 1997**). Part VII = conduct of the party in power.
[62:5:6] # Quasi-Judicial and Advisory
[62:5:7] - **Recognises parties and allots symbols** under the **Election Symbols (Reservation and Allotment) Order, 1968** (issued under RPA 1951 + Art. 324); settles symbol disputes.
[62:5:8] - Advises the **President on MPs' disqualification (Art. 103)** and the **Governor on MLAs' (Art. 192)**.
[62:5:9] - **Anti-defection (Tenth Schedule)** → **Speaker/Chairman**, not the ECI.
[62:5:10] - **Recommending President's Rule** is **not** an ECI function.
[62:5:11] # Voter Facilitation
[62:5:12] TABLE head: App / campaign | Purpose
  [62:5:12.0] SVEEP | Voter awareness and education for inclusive, accessible elections
  [62:5:12.1] cVIGIL | Citizens report MCC and expenditure violations with time-stamped evidence; status within 100 minutes
  [62:5:12.2] Saksham-ECI | Registration and polling help for persons with disabilities; developed with the Department of Empowerment of PwDs
  [62:5:12.3] Voter Helpline | Search name in roll, submit forms, complaints; toll-free 1950
[62:5:13] - **Home voting:** voters **above 85** and PwDs with **40% benchmark disability**.
[62:5:14] # VVPAT
[62:5:15] - Prints a paper slip of the vote so the voter can verify it.
[62:5:16] - **8 April 2019:** SC raised verification from one to **five randomly selected EVMs-VVPATs per Assembly segment**.
[62:5:17] - **26 April 2024 (ADR v. ECI):** **100% VVPAT verification rejected**; **symbol loading units sealed** and kept in the strong room **45 days** after results; verification request within **7 days** at the candidate's cost.

## SUB 7 | Eci vs State Election Commission and Chief Electoral Officer
[62:7:0] TABLE head: Basis | ECI | SEC | Chief Electoral Officer
  [62:7:0.0] Basis | Art. 324, Part XV | Arts. 243K, 243ZA, Parts IX, IX-A (73rd/74th Amendments, 1992) | Sec. 13A, RPA 1950 – not a constitutional authority
  [62:7:0.1] Elections | Parliament, legislatures, President, VP | Panchayats (all three tiers), municipalities | LS and Assembly polls, under ECI control
  [62:7:0.2] Appointed by | President | Governor | ECI, in consultation with the State Government, from senior State officers
  [62:7:0.3] Removal | Like SC judge | Like HC judge | By the ECI
[62:7:1] - SEC prepares rolls and conducts local polls (Arts. 243K(1), 243ZA(1)); **243K = panchayats, 243ZA = municipalities**.
[62:7:2] - **Decision to hold panchayat elections** = **State Government**; panchayat is **entry 5 of the State List**.
[62:7:3] - Constitutional authorities: **SEC 243K**, **SFC 243I**, **District Panchayat 243B**.
[62:7:4] - District: **Collector = District Election Officer**; appoints **Returning and Presiding Officers**.
[62:7:5] - **Rajasthan SEC** constituted **July 1994** (not December), one-member; its secretary is also the CEO.
[62:7:6] - **AP:** SEC **Anil Chandra Punetha** for local-body polls; **ASR district** – 100% SIR digitalisation.
[62:7:7] MPPSC 2019 key gave **Art. 243K** for urban-body polls; textbook article for municipalities is **243ZA**.
## SUB 8 | Election Disputes and Poll Disruption
[62:8:0] - **Art. 329(a):** delimitation and seat allotment laws **cannot be questioned in court**.
[62:8:1] - **Art. 329(b):** Parliament/Assembly election challenged **only by an election petition** under **RPA 1951** – tried by the **High Court**, appeal to the **Supreme Court**.
[62:8:2] - **Art. 71:** President/VP disputes → **Supreme Court**.
[62:8:3] - ECI **cannot review a declared result** and **does not hear election petitions**.
[62:8:4] # Polling-Day Situations (RPA 1951)
[62:8:5] TABLE head: Section | Situation | Action
  [62:8:5.0] 57 | Riot, open violence, natural calamity or other sufficient cause | Presiding or Returning Officer adjourns the poll; adjourned poll resumes with ECI approval; only those who had not voted may vote
  [62:8:5.1] 58 | EVM/ballot box unlawfully taken, destroyed, lost, damaged or tampered | ECI declares poll void, fixes a fresh poll
  [62:8:5.2] 58A | Booth capturing | Adjournment or countermand of election
