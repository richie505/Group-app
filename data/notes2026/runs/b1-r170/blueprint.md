## 4. Key corrections and traps settled
- **Sugauli:** the old notes said the Company got "Ranikhet and Nainital (not Kathmandu, not Shimla)". Spectrum p.158 lists **Shimla, Mussoorie and Nainital** as hill-station sites gained, and Drishti's 2025 key gives 2, 3 and 4 – rekeyed, with a ⚠ Disputed line.
- **Widow Remarriage Act 1856:** NextGen and Spectrum say Dalhousie; it was passed after Canning took over – Dalhousie keyed, both noted.
- **Bentinck and slavery:** the Charter Act 1833 only ordered abolition; Act V of 1843 (Ellenborough) abolished it – the State PSC key (1, 2, 4) kept with ⚠, while the NDA item (slavery not Bentinck's) stays.
