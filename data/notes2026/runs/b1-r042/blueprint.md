## 4. Keys that needed settling
- **ya439ab40535 (APPSC ASO):** "Indo-Greek ruler … issued coins depicting Indian deities" – printed Menander I → **Agathocles** (Balarama and Vasudeva-Krishna at Ai-Khanoum, PMF Ancient 02 p.148); Menander's coins carry Buddhist symbols, not deities.
- **y9819dfb2532 (APPSC G1):** dress items introduced by Shakas and Kushanas – printed and old app key II, III, IV, V (turban excluded) → **I-V, all five** (PMF Ancient 03 p.20 and R.S. Sharma both list the turban first). The old notes' "turban excluded – follow the APPSC key" trap is withdrawn. The official 2024 key could not be retrieved (question paper OCR'd; key PDF not reachable).
- **ya1514c1fe46 (Endowment 2022):** Sthapathi, unkeyed → **"the master who makes sculptures"** (sthapati = chief architect-sculptor; shilpi = carver).
- **yb7fb3ed7d53 (Welfare 2017):** lotus carvings, unkeyed → **Amaravati**, matching the twin bank PYQ.
- Kept with a note: Kanishka/78 AD (modern view c. 127 CE), Badami Saka 465 = Vikrama 601 (strictly 600), oldest Buddha sculpture = Mathura (priority debated), Surkhi = Kushans (Rocket; later Sultanate use is the trap).
