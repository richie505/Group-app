## SUB 2 | C.V. Raman and National Science Day
[2:0] - Born **7 November 1888**; worked at **IACS, Kolkata**; **Raman Effect** discovered **28 February 1928** → **National Science Day, 28 February** (marks the discovery, not his birthday or a satellite launch).
[2:1] - **Raman Effect:** when light passes through a **transparent** material (any transparent medium – solid, liquid or gas), a small fraction is **scattered with a change in wavelength**.
[2:2] - **Raman spectroscopy** identifies molecular structure – the robust tool to **characterise graphene**.
[2:3] - **Sky is blue** because of **scattering** (Rayleigh scattering of shorter wavelengths) – not absorption, reflection or dispersion.
[2:4] - Discovered the Effect at **IACS, Kolkata**; first Indian director of **IISc**; first to study the harmonic nature of the **tabla and mridangam**. He did **not** found the Saha Institute.
[2:5] - **Honours:** knighted **1929**, **Nobel Physics 1930** (first Asian science Nobel), **Bharat Ratna 1954**; "Father of Indian Physics".
[2:6] - Founded the **Indian Academy of Sciences (1934)** and the **Raman Research Institute, Bengaluru (1948)** – now an autonomous institute under **DST**.
[2:7] > ⚠ Trap: 28 Feb = Science Day (Raman); 22 Dec = Mathematics Day (Ramanujan); 11 May = Technology Day.
[2:8] ~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 1 p.36 (Raman Effect, Raman spectroscopy); Rocket Physics p.6, p.9, p.27 (blue sky – Rayleigh scattering); app notes 5:9 sub 1
## SUB 3 | Homi J. Bhabha and the Nuclear Institutions
[3:0] - **Father of India's Nuclear / Atomic Energy Programme** (also "Father of Indian Nuclear Science"); designed the **three-stage** programme (thorium).
[3:1] - Founded **TIFR, Mumbai (1945)**; first chairman of the **Atomic Energy Commission (1948)**; set up **AEET Trombay (1954)**, renamed **BARC** in **1967** after his death (Mont Blanc air crash, **1966**); Apsara reactor 1956.
[3:2] - **Five-Year Plan angle:** exam keys place TIFR and the Atomic Energy Commission under the **Second Plan (1956-61)** (AEC reconstituted with full powers in 1958); strictly TIFR dates from 1945 and the AEC from 1948.
[3:3] - **Homi Bhabha Award** – for **nuclear energy**. **Bhabha Kavach** – BARC's lightweight bullet-proof jacket (2018) for the forces.
[3:4] - **Bhabha scattering** (electron-positron); **cascade theory** of cosmic-ray showers; **Adams Prize 1942**, **FRS 1941**, **Padma Bhushan 1954**.
[3:5] - Three-stage programme is **thorium-based** (not uranium-based); Bhabha was **President of the first UN Conference on the Peaceful Uses of Atomic Energy, Geneva, 1955**.
[3:6] - BARC was **not** founded as BARC in 1945: TIFR = 1945, AEET = 1954, BARC name = 1967.
[3:7] > ⚠ Trap: not Sarabhai (space), Raja Ramanna, Satish Dhawan or Bhatnagar.
[3:8] ~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 1 p.37; PMF S&T Part 4 p.61 (TIFR 1945, AEC 1948, AEET 1954; it says renamed 1966, Part 5 BARC entry says 1967); Rocket Physics p.9, p.22; web: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five-Year_Plans_of_India and study-guide summaries (Second Plan); app notes 5:9 sub 6
## SUB 4 | J.C. Bose – Plants Have Life
[4:0] - **Jagadish Chandra Bose** showed that **plants have life** and respond to stimuli (light, heat, electricity); invented the **Crescograph**.
[4:1] - Pioneer of **radio / microwave** research (coherer, galena detector) → researched in **both physics and biology**.
[4:2] - Founded **Bose Institute, Kolkata (1917)**; **FRS 1920**; books *Response in the Living and Non-Living* (1902), *The Nervous Mechanism of Plants*; Bengali science fiction *Niruddesher Kahini*.
[4:3] > ⚠ Trap: J.C. **Bose** (plants, radio) ≠ **S.N. Bose** (bosons) ≠ J.C. Ghosh (chemist).

## SUB 5 | Ramanujan, S.N. Bose and Meghnad Saha
[5:0] - **Srinivasa Ramanujan** (1887–1920): born 22 Dec 1887, Erode; worked with **G.H. Hardy** at Cambridge; first Indian **Fellow of Trinity College, Cambridge** (1918) and FRS; **1729** = Hardy-Ramanujan number; **22 Dec = National Mathematics Day**; biopic **"The Man Who Knew Infinity"** (2015, from Robert Kanigel's book).
[5:1] - **Ramanujan's work:** number theory, partitions, infinite series, **mock theta functions**; **2012 = National Mathematical Year** (125th birth anniversary).
[5:2] - **S.N. Bose:** paper **"Planck's Law and the Hypothesis of Light Quanta" (1924)**, sent to **Einstein**, who **translated it into German** → **Bose-Einstein statistics** (quantum statistics); particles called **bosons** – name given by **Paul Dirac**; **Padma Vibhushan 1954**, **FRS 1958**; Tagore dedicated *Visva-Parichay* to him.
[5:3] - **Meghnad Saha:** **thermal ionisation** theory (1920-21) → **Saha ionisation equation** for stellar spectra; Damodar Valley plan; founded **NASI (1930)**; **Saha Institute of Nuclear Physics** is in **Kolkata**; elected to the **Lok Sabha 1952**; devised an instrument to measure the **weight and pressure of solar rays**; FRS 1927.
[5:4] ~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 1 p.35-36; app notes 5:9 sub 3-5
