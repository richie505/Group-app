=== Indian Supercomputers and Computing Firsts
- India's supercomputer family is **PARAM**, designed and assembled by **C-DAC, Pune** (C-DAC set up 1988) – not Shubham, SX-2, Tejas or EKA.
- **PARAM 8000 (1991)** = India's **first** (first indigenously developed) supercomputer; C-DAC under **Vijay Bhatkar**, the **father of Indian supercomputing**.
| Machine | Developer / site |
|---|---|
| PARAM 8000 | C-DAC (1991) |
| **Anupam** | **BARC** – from 1991, parallel processing |
| Flosolver | NAL Bengaluru |
| **PARAM Padma** | C-DAC (2003) – **first Indian system on the TOP500** list (June 2003, rank 171) |
| SAGA-220 | ISRO (2011) – about 220 TFLOPS |
| SahasraT (Cray XC40) | SERC, **IISc** – India's fastest in 2015-16 (ahead of Aaditya, IITM Pune) |
| **PARAM Siddhi-AI** | C-DAC (2020) – ranked **62nd/63rd** on TOP500 (Nov 2020), India's highest then |
| Pratyush / Mihir (weather) | IITM Pune / NCMRWF Noida |
- **Computing firsts:** first analog computer in India designed by **Samarendra Kumar Mitra** (ISI Kolkata, 1953); **TIFRAC** (1960) = first indigenous digital computer; exam keys name **"Siddharth"** as "the first computer made in India".
> ⚠ Trap: PARAM 8000 is the first; PARAM Shivay is only the first NSM machine; PARAM Padma, not Siddhi-AI, was the first in the TOP500.

=== National Supercomputing Mission (NSM, 2015) – Systems and Hosts
- Run jointly by **DST and MeitY** (2015); implemented by **C-DAC Pune + IISc Bengaluru**; machines linked as the **National Supercomputing Grid** over the **National Knowledge Network**; **40 systems** planned.
- Indigenous stack: **Rudra** server board + **Trinetra** interconnect + open-source (AlmaLinux) software.
| System | Host |
|---|---|
| PARAM Shivay (2019) | **IIT (BHU) Varanasi** – first NSM machine |
| PARAM Brahma | IISER Pune |
| **PARAM Ganga** | **IIT Roorkee** (March 2022; built by C-DAC) |
| **PARAM Porul** | **NIT Tiruchirappalli** (May 2022) |
| PARAM Pravega | IISc Bengaluru |
| PARAM Ananta | IIT Gandhinagar |
| **PARAM Kamrupa** | **IIT Guwahati** – opened by President Droupadi Murmu, Oct 2022 |
| PARAM Shakti | IIT Kharagpur; 3.1 PF Rudra-based system at IIT Madras (Jan 2026) |
| PARAM Rudra | GMRT Pune, IUAC Delhi, S.N. Bose Centre Kolkata |
| PARAM Pragya | IIT Delhi (Sonipat), AI system, Aug 2026 |
| **AIRAWAT** (AI supercomputer) | **C-DAC Pune**, under the national AI programme – **75th** in TOP500 at **ISC 2023** (Germany, May 2023) |

=== National Quantum Mission (NQM)
- **Qubit** = basic unit of **quantum information**; by **superposition** it can be 0 and 1 together (bit/byte are classical).
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Approved | Union Cabinet, **19 April 2023** |
| Outlay | **₹6,003.65 crore** |
| Period | **2023-24 to 2030-31** (8 years) |
| Nodal | **DST** |
- Targets: computers of **50–1000 physical qubits**; satellite QKD over **2000 km**; magnetometers and atomic clocks.
| T-Hub | Host |
|---|---|
| Quantum Computing | IISc Bengaluru |
| Quantum Communication | IIT Madras (with C-DOT) |
| Quantum Sensing and Metrology | IIT Bombay |
| Quantum Materials and Devices | IIT Delhi |
- Start-ups backed: **QNu Labs** (QKD), QpiAI. **14 April = World Quantum Day**.

=== Deep Ocean Mission
- Nodal **Ministry of Earth Sciences**; Cabinet **June 2021**, **₹4,077 crore** for five years; six components.
- **Samudrayaan** = sub-project (India's first crewed deep-ocean mission); **Matsya 6000** = crewed submersible built by **NIOT Chennai**, **3 people to 6,000 m**; **Varaha** = deep-sea mining machine trialled at 5,270 m.
- **Components:** deep-sea mining and manned submersible; ocean climate change advisory services; deep-sea biodiversity; deep ocean survey; **energy and freshwater from the ocean** (OTEC-powered desalination); **Advanced Marine Station for Ocean Biology**.

=== BIO-E3, ANRF and Other Missions
- **Bio-E3 = Biotechnology for Economy, Environment and Employment**; Cabinet **24 Aug 2024**; steered by **DBT**; biomanufacturing hubs, Bio-AI hubs, biofoundries; bioeconomy **over US$165 billion** (2024); Budget 2026-27 **Biopharma SHAKTI** ₹10,000 crore over 5 years.
- **ANRF:** created by the **ANRF Act, 2023**; **subsumed SERB**; **Prime Minister** = ex officio President of its Governing Board; custodian of the **₹1 lakh crore RDI Fund** via a **two-tier Special Purpose Fund**.

=== Andhra Pradesh Quantum Push – Amaravati Quantum Valley
- **Andhra Pradesh** = **first State** with its own **quantum computing policy**; foundation stone at **Uddandarayunipalem**.
- Incentive: **100% exemption from stamp duty and registration fees** for tech firms; plan to train 4.5 million people in AI and quantum.
- **14 April 2026** (World Quantum Day): India's first **Quantum Reference Facility** test beds – **1S at SRM University-AP**, **1Q at Medha Towers, Gannavaram**.
- MoUs (Feb 2026): **IBM to train 1 lakh youth**; **NIELIT Quantum and AI campus** classes from Sept 2026 at **Acharya Nagarjuna University, Guntur**.
- PMF: AP announced India's first **133-qubit** quantum computer at Amaravati (April 2026).
