## SUB 0 | Kuka (Namdhari) Movement – Punjab, Ram Singh, First Boycott
[0:0] - **Region: Punjab** (not U.P., Malabar, Bihar, Bengal or Maharashtra); founded **1840** by **Bhagat Jawahar Mal (Sian Sahib)** in western Punjab (Spectrum) – he **started** the Kuka revolt.
[0:1] - **Baba (Satguru) Ram Singh** – disciple of Balak Singh; **organised** it and founded the **Namdhari** sect (**1857**, **Bhaini, Ludhiana**) – not Guru Ram Das, Nanak or Gobind Singh, not Nankana Sahib.
[0:2] - Practices: **white clothes**, no meat, alcohol or drugs, a **lathi**; against idol, tomb, tree and snake worship and **Brahmin-led rituals**; widow remarriage, intermarriage.
[0:3] - **Boycott** of British **goods, English education, law courts and the postal system** → **Ram Singh = first to use non-cooperation and boycott (Swadeshi) as a political weapon** (APPSC 2025) – before Tilak, Pal or Gandhi.
[0:4] - Suppression: **Malerkotla clash, 1872** – Kukas blown from cannons; Ram Singh **deported to Rangoon (Burma)**; Kuka movement under Viceroy Northbrook.
[0:5] - Kuka and Wahabi both began as religious revolts and became anti-British political movements.
[0:6] > Kuka = Punjab; begun by Jawahar Mal (1840); Ram Singh, Bhaini, boycott; Malerkotla 1872.

## SUB 1 | Singh Sabha, Nirankari and the Akali Movement
[1:0] - **Singh Sabha:** **Amritsar, 1873**; more radical **Lahore Singh Sabha, 1879**; aims – modern Western education for Sikhs through **Khalsa schools**, **Gurmukhi/Punjabi** literature, remove un-Sikh practices, counter missionaries.
[1:1] - **Khalsa College, Amritsar – 1892** (foundation stone) by Singh Sabha leaders – not 1894, 1896 or 1898.
[1:2] - **Nirankari:** **Baba Dayal Das** (1855); God as **Nirankar** (formless); issued **hukamnamas**; own priests; **cooperated with the British** and became a separate group in Sikhism.
[1:3] TABLE head: Akali step | Year
  [1:3.0] SGPC formed; Akali (Gurdwara Reform) Movement begins | 1920
  [1:3.1] Nankana Sahib massacre | 1921
  [1:3.2] Guru-ka-Bagh morcha | 1922
  [1:3.3] Sikh Gurdwaras Act – gurdwaras under the SGPC | 1922, amended 1925
[1:4] - **Akali movement** = offshoot of the Singh Sabha; freed gurdwaras from corrupt **Udasi mahants**; leader **Kartar Singh Jhabbar**; later the **Akali Dal**. It is a **Sikh** movement – "Akali Movement – Tamilians" is the **wrong pair** (Rahnumai – Parsis, Aligarh – Muslims, Arya Samaj – Hindus are right).
[1:5] > Singh Sabha 1873 Amritsar → Khalsa College 1892 → Akali / SGPC 1920 → Act 1925.
[1:6] ~~ Sources: Spectrum Modern India (Ahir 2019) file p.264-265; old app notes row 193 (Lahore 1879, Khalsa College 1892, Nirankari, Akali dates)
## SUB 2 | Parsi Reform – Rahnumai Mazdayasnan Sabha, 1851
[2:0] - **Rahnumai Mazdayasnan Sabha** (Religious Reform Association), **Bombay, 1851** – to reform the **Parsis** (not Sikhs, Muslims or Anglo-Indians).
[2:1] - Founders: **Naoroji Furdonji** (first president), **Dadabhai Naoroji**, **K.R. Cama**, **S.S. Bengalee**.
[2:2] - Organ: **Rast Goftar** ("Truth-Teller"), edited by **Dadabhai Naoroji** – not Malabari.
[2:3] - Work: restore Zoroastrian purity; **women's education**, removal of purdah, higher marriage age, legal status and uniform inheritance laws for Parsi women.
[2:4] - **Behramji M. Malabari** – child-marriage campaign → **Age of Consent Act, 1891**; Seva Sadan (1908).
[2:5] > Rahnumai = Parsis, Bombay 1851, Furdonji, Rast Goftar.
[2:6] ~~ Sources: Spectrum Modern India (Ahir 2019) file p.256, p.264; PMF Modern History Part 3 p.190, p.200; old app notes row 193
## SUB 3 | Other Hindu Bodies – Radhasoami, Dev Samaj, Dharma Sabha, Bharat Dharma Mahamandal
[3:0] TABLE head: Body | Founder | Place, year
  [3:0.0] Radhasoami Satsang | Shiv Dayal Singh (Khatri) – "Soamiji Maharaj", also called Shiv Dayal Sahab | Agra, 1861 (not Haridas Swami, Agnihotri, Shraddhanand)
  [3:0.1] Dev Samaj | Shiv Narayan Agnihotri (ex-Brahmo) | Lahore, 1887; text Deva Shastra
  [3:0.2] Dharma Sabha | Radhakant Deb | Calcutta, 1830 – orthodox; opposed the abolition of sati
  [3:0.3] Bharat Dharma Mahamandal | Pandit Din Dayalu Sharma | Haridwar, 1887; combined 1902, HQ Varanasi – orthodox Hindus

[3:1] - **Radhasoami:** one God, the **guru** and satsang; inner spiritual life.
[3:2] - **Dev Samaj:** eternity of the soul, supremacy of the guru, good action; no bribes, alcohol or meat; against child marriage.
[3:3] - **Bengali leader who opposed reform and supported orthodoxy = Radhakant Deb** (yet he favoured Western education, even for girls).
[3:4] > Radhasoami = Agra 1861; Dev Samaj = Lahore 1887; Dharma Sabha = Calcutta 1830.
