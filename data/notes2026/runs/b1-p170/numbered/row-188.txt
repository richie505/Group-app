## SUB 1 | Brahmo Samaj, 1828 – Founding and Beliefs
[1:0] - **Founded:** **Brahmo Sabha**, **20 August 1828**, Calcutta, by **Raja Rammohan Roy**; later called the **Brahmo Samaj**. First secretary Tarachand Chakravarti.
[1:1] - **Year traps:** 1829 = sati ban; **1843** = Debendranath joined; correct founding year = **1828** (option added where missing).
[1:2] - **Monotheism:** worship of **one formless, eternal God**; denounced **polytheism and idol worship**; no images in Samaj halls; no faith in **avatars**.
[1:3] - **Reason + Upanishads:** rested on reason and the Vedas/Upanishads but **rejected the infallibility of the Vedas** – no scripture above reason and conscience.
[1:4] - **No priestly class** needed to interpret texts; criticised **caste**; took in the best of Western/other religious thought.
[1:5] - **Correct statements:** opposed idolatry ✔; denied need for priests ✔; "Vedas infallible" ✘ (IAS 2012); "accepted need of priestly class" ✘ (SI 2023: a, b, c).
[1:6] - **Nature:** a **monotheistic reformist** movement within Hinduism (not polytheistic, not political); **reformist**, while the **Arya Samaj** is **revivalist**.
[1:7] - **Significance:** condemned sati, purdah, child marriage, polygamy; backed widow remarriage and women's education; influence hardly went beyond **Bengal**.
[1:8] - **Opponent:** **Radhakant Deb's Dharma Sabha (1830)** – orthodox, against the sati ban.
[1:9] - **Brahmo Dharma Gitanjali:** collection of devotional songs sung in Samaj prayers.
[1:10] > Brahmo = one God + reason; no idols, no avatars, no priests, no infallible scripture.

## SUB 2 | Debendranath Tagore, Keshab Chandra Sen and the Splits
[2:0] TABLE head: Year | Event
  [2:0.0] 1839 | Debendranath Tagore founds the Tattvabodhini Sabha (organ Tattvabodhini Patrika, Bengali) to spread Roy's ideas
  [2:0.1] 1843 (Spectrum 1842) | Debendranath joins and revitalises the Brahmo Samaj
  [2:0.2] 1857-58 | Keshab Chandra Sen joins; Debendranath makes him acharya
  [2:0.3] 1865-66 | Keshab dismissed; forms the Brahmo Samaj of India (Bharatvarshiya) in 1866; Debendranath's body = Adi Brahmo Samaj
  [2:0.4] 1870 | Keshab founds the Indian Reform Association
  [2:0.5] 1872 | Native (Civil / Brahmo) Marriage Act – after Keshab's petition; legal Brahmo/inter-caste marriage; minimum age 14 (bride), 18 (groom)
  [2:0.6] 1878 | Keshab marries his minor daughter to the Maharaja of Cooch Behar → Sadharan Brahmo Samaj (Ananda Mohan Bose, Shibchandra Deb, Umesh Chandra Datta / Shivnath Shastri)
  [2:0.7] 1881 | Keshab's Naba Bidhan (New Dispensation)
[2:1] - **Debendranath Tagore** (1817-1905), 'Maharishi', father of Rabindranath; also first **secretary of the British Indian Association (1851)**; did NOT found the Atmiya Sabha or the Brahmo Samaj.
[2:2] - **Keshab** spread the Samaj to U.P., Punjab, **Bombay** and **Madras** – his visits inspired the **Prarthana Samaj** (1867) and the **Veda Samaj** (Madras, 1864); under him the Samaj campaigned for **women's education** (IAS 2001 III ✔).
[2:3] - **Dev Samaj:** **Shiv Narayan Agnihotri**, an ex-Brahmo, Lahore **1887**.
[2:4] > Atmiya 1815 → Brahmo 1828 → Tattvabodhini 1839 → split 1866 → Indian Reform Assn 1870 → Marriage Act 1872 → Sadharan 1878.
