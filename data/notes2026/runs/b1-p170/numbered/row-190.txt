## SUB 0 | Arya Samaj, 1875 – Founder and Founding
[0:0] - **Founder:** **Swami Dayananda Saraswati** (Mool Shankar, 1824-1883), born in a **Brahmin** family in **Morvi state (Tankara), Gujarat**; guru **Swami Virajananda** of Mathura; died at Ajmer, 1883.
[0:1] - **First Arya Samaj:** **Bombay, 1875** (10 April); headquarters later at **Lahore** (1877); strongest base Punjab.
[0:2] - Founder = **Dayananda** – not Vivekananda, Gokhale, Ramakrishna, Ranade, V.R. Shinde, Chandavarkar or Keshab.
[0:3] - **Wrong date trap:** "Arya Samaj founded in 1835" ✘ (IAS 2001).
[0:4] - **Current linkage:** **International Arya Mahasammelan 2025**, New Delhi, **30 Oct-2 Nov 2025** – 150 years of the Arya Samaj and Dayananda's **200th birth anniversary**.
[0:5] > Arya Samaj = Dayananda, Bombay 1875, HQ Lahore.

## SUB 1 | Doctrines, Satyarth Prakash and Titles
[1:0] - **'Back to the Vedas':** Vedas = **infallible**, "India's Rock of Ages"; a revival of Vedic learning and purity, not of Vedic times; rejected the **Puranas**.
[1:1] - **Attacked:** idolatry, polytheism, caste rigidity and untouchability, child marriage, priestly ritual; caste by **merit/occupation, not birth** (CDS 2009: all three statements ✔).
[1:2] - **Supported:** widow remarriage, inter-caste marriage, women's education; minimum marriage age 25 (boys) and 16 (girls); **ten principles**.
[1:3] - **Satyarth Prakash** ('The Light of Truth', 1875, **Hindi**, revised 1882) – Dayananda's main work and the **Arya Samaj's** sacred text.
[1:4] - **Revivalist** in form (cf. reformist Brahmo/Prarthana Samaj).
[1:5] # Titles and slogans
[1:6] TABLE head: Said / title | Person
  [1:6.0] 'India for the Indians' | Dayananda Saraswati (political message)
  [1:6.1] Martin Luther of India | Dayananda Saraswati
  [1:6.2] First to use the term 'Swaraj' | Dayananda (as usually given)
  [1:6.3] Prophet of Indian Nationalism | not Dayananda – Aurobindo
[1:7] > Dayananda: Back to the Vedas, Satyarth Prakash, India for the Indians, Martin Luther of India.

## SUB 2 | Shuddhi, DAV Schools, the 1893 Split and Social Base
[2:0] - **Shuddhi** (purification): started by the **Arya Samaj** (Dayananda) to **reconvert** to Hinduism those who had become Muslims or Christians; also tried to raise untouchables; aggressive campaign of the 1920s (Shraddhanand) sharpened communalism.
[2:1] - **DAV:** **Dayananda Anglo-Vedic School/College, Lahore, 1886** (after Dayananda's death) – **Lala Hansraj** first headmaster/founder; Western-style education.
[2:2] - **1893 split:** **College Party** – Lala Hansraj, Lala Lal Chand, **Lala Lajpat Rai** (kept DAV); **Mahatma / Gurukul Party** – Guru Datta Vidyarthi and **Munshi Ram (Swami Shraddhanand)**, who set up the **Gurukul** (Gujranwala 1900, **Kangri, Haridwar 1902**).
[2:3] - **Swami Shraddhanand** spoke on Hindu-Muslim unity from the pulpit of the **Jama Masjid, Delhi, 4 April 1919** (Rowlatt satyagraha).
[2:4] - **Impact (A-R):** gave Hindus **self-respect and self-confidence**, which **undermined the myth of white superiority** – R explains A.
[2:5] - **Social base:** **Punjab and western U.P.**, mainly the **trading castes**; wider than the Brahmo Samaj, whose influence hardly went beyond Bengal; famous for social service in calamities.
[2:6] - **Lala Lajpat Rai:** Arya Samaj leader, ran the paper **The People** (not "unconnected"); **Al Hilal** (1912) = **Maulana Abul Kalam Azad**.
[2:7] - **Hyderabad, 1938-39:** Arya Samaj satyagraha against the **Nizam's curbs on religious and civil rights**, alongside the State Congress satyagraha.
[2:8] > Shuddhi = Arya Samaj; DAV Lahore 1886 = Hansraj; Gurukul Kangri 1902 = Shraddhanand; split 1893.
