=== Swami Vivekananda – Life, Chicago 1893, Ideas, Books and Sayings
# Life
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Birth name | **Narendranath Datta** |
| Born | **Calcutta, 12 January 1863** (Spectrum prints 1862); died 1902 |
| Guru | **Ramakrishna Paramahamsa** (= Gadadhar Chattopadhyay) of Dakshineswar |
| National Youth Day | His birthday, **12 January** – declared by the Government of India in **1984**, first observed **1985** (12 August is the UN's *International* Youth Day) |
| Western disciple | **Sister Nivedita = Margaret Elizabeth Noble** (Agnes Gonxha Bojaxhiu = Mother Teresa; Madeleine Slade = Mirabehn) |
| Vedanta Societies | **USA and Britain** – Vedanta Society of New York (1894) |
| Titles | **Best representative / preacher of Neo-Hinduism** (later 19th century) – not Ramakrishna, Bankim or Rammohan; "Cyclonic Hindu"; called "Prophet of Indian Nationalism" in many MCQ banks; Bose: "spiritual father of the modern nationalist movement" (Bengal) |
# Chicago and after – order of events
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Dec 1892 | Meditated on the Kanyakumari rock |
| Feb 1893 | Spoke at **Mahboob College, Secunderabad** |
| **11 Sep 1893** | **World's Parliament of Religions, Chicago** – opened with "**Sisters and Brothers of America**"; represented Hinduism (not 1863, 1872, 1881, 1890, 1892, 1894, 1895, 1897, 1899, 1901) |
| 10 Nov 1896 | First lecture on **Practical Vedanta, London** |
| 1897 | Returned via **Colombo, Ramnad, Madras** → *Lectures from Colombo to Almora*; toured Andhra (organised by Nyapathi Subba Rao) |
| 1 May 1897 | Ramakrishna Mission |
| 1898 | Belur Math |
- **Others at Chicago 1893:** Brahmo Samaj – **P.C. Mozoomdar**; Jainism – **Virchand Gandhi**; Buddhism (Ceylon) – **Anagarika Dharmapala**. Of Dayananda, Gandhi and Rammohan Roy, none attended (Roy d. 1833, Dayananda d. 1883).
- Keynote at Chicago: a balance of the **spiritualism of the East and the materialism of the West**; Hinduism "the mother of religions".
# Ideas
- **Practical Vedanta:** Vedanta to be lived in daily life; serving the poor is serving God – **"Daridra Narayana"** (God manifest in the poor); "service of jiva is worship of Shiva". Not Dayananda, Rammohan or Gandhi.
- Followed **Vedanta, "a fully rational system"**; criticised Indians for **losing touch with the outside world** ("stagnant and mummified"); **condemned the caste system**, ritual and superstition – but did **not** call the Veda infallible (that is Dayananda) → CDS 2011: **I, II and III**.
- **Vedanta the religion of all**; revived the best Hindu traditions; admired the **status of women in the West** → CDS 2009: **I, II and III**.
- **"Our religion is in the kitchen"** – his attack on touch-me-not orthodoxy ("Our God is the cooking-pot").
- Humanitarian relief = a **monastic duty**; knowledge without action is useless.

=== Ramakrishna Mission, 1897 – Founder, Year, Work
- **Founded 1 May 1897 by Swami Vivekananda**, at Balaram Bose's house, **Baghbazar, Calcutta**, in his guru's name – not by Ramakrishna (died 1886), Vidyasagar, Dayananda, Rammohan Roy, Gokhale, Shyamji Krishna Varma, M.N. Das Gupta or Ranganathananda.
- **Year = 1897** – not 1861, 1879, 1886, 1891, 1892, 1893, 1898 or 1899. "Founded the Mission in **1879**" = the INCORRECT statement.
- **HQ: Belur Math** on the Hooghly, Howrah (land bought **1898**); Math and Mission are twin bodies, legally and financially separate.
- Motto: **"Atmano Mokshartham Jagad Hitaya Cha"** – for one's own liberation and the welfare of the world.
- **Two wings:** (1) a **monastic order** of renunciation and practical spirituality; (2) **lay disciples** – preaching, philanthropy, humanitarian work.
- **Work:** schools, hospitals, orphanages, **famine and disaster relief**; social and spiritual – **non-political, non-sectarian, not proselytising**; unlike the Arya Samaj it accepts image worship.
- **Current linkage (1 Feb 2026):** RKM's **Vivekananda Human Excellence Centre, Vijayawada**, inaugurated by RKM President Swami Gautamananda.
> RK Mission = Vivekananda, 1 May 1897, Calcutta; HQ Belur Math.

=== Annie Besant – Career, Central Hindu College, BHU and Writings
# Milestones
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1885 | Joined the **Fabian Society** (British gradualist socialism) – the Fabian among Hume, M.M. Dutt and R. Palme Dutt |
| 1889 | Joined the Theosophical Society |
| 1893 | Came to India |
| **1898** | **Central Hindu College (School), Benaras** – 7 July 1898 |
| **1907** | **President of the Theosophical Society** after Olcott's death |
| 1914 | *Commonweal* (weekly, Jan) and *New India* (Madras Standard renamed, Aug) |
| 1916 | **Home Rule League** (1 Sep 1916); National (BT) College, Madanapalle |
| **1917** | **First woman President of the INC – Calcutta session** |
- Besant flashcard statements (TS President 1907 ✔, first woman INC President 1917 ✔, Central Hindu College 1898 ✔) → **all three correct**.
- Besant did **not found** the Theosophical Society (Blavatsky and Olcott did, 1875); she **was** one of the founders of BHU (her Central Hindu College was its nucleus) and **believed in the transmigration of the soul** (karma and reincarnation).
- Pairs: **Annie Besant – New India** ✔ (also Commonweal); Hind Swaraj – Gandhi; India Wins Freedom – Azad; Tilak – Kesari/Mahratta.
- **At the Feet of the Master** (1910) – **Jiddu Krishnamurti** ("Alcyone"), Besant's adopted ward, written at about 14 – not Besant, Radhakrishnan or C.R. Reddy.
- *Isis Unveiled*, *The Secret Doctrine* – **H.P. Blavatsky**; *My Master* – Vivekananda (not Besant, not Olcott).

=== Theosophical Society, 1875 – Founders, Adyar, Beliefs
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Founded | **New York, November 1875** – not London, Paris or Adyar (same year as the Arya Samaj) |
| Founders | **Madame H.P. Blavatsky** (Russian) and **Col. H.S. Olcott** (American), with William Q. Judge – **not** Annie Besant, Hume, Rammohan, Gandhi, Vivekananda |
| Came to India | **1879** |
| HQ | **Adyar, near Madras, from 1882** – not Madanapalle, Pondicherry, Kanchipuram |
| Success in India | Mainly due to **Annie Besant** (President 1907-33) |
| Telugu name | **Divya Gnana Samajam** |
- **Beliefs:** **universal brotherhood** of humanity without distinction of race, creed, sex, caste or colour; study of ancient religions; **karma, reincarnation, transmigration of the soul**; drew on Upanishads, Samkhya, Yoga, Vedanta; opposed child marriage and caste discrimination.
- A Western-led **revivalist** movement allied with the Hindu renaissance; gave Indians self-respect – and led straight into the **Home Rule** movement.
- **A-R trap:** HQ at Adyar ✔, but it was **not** founded at Madras ✘ → A true, R false.
- Wrong statement (MPPSC 1995): "**Gandhi was born in Gandhinagar**" ✘ – Porbandar, 2 Oct 1869; Besant a Theosophist, Adyar HQ, Dayananda–Arya Samaj are all correct.
> TS = Blavatsky + Olcott, New York 1875 → Adyar 1882; Besant President 1907.

- **Besant (National) Theosophical College, Madanapalle** – founded by **Annie Besant**, May **1916** (NextGen; PMF gives 19 July 1915) as part of her **National Education** scheme; first principal **James H. Cousins** – not Jiddu Krishnamurti (born at Madanapalle), Konda Venkatappayya or Mutnuri Krishna Rao.

- UPSC 2021 wrong options: Pingali Venkayya did not design the flag there; Pattabhi did not lead Quit India from there; Blavatsky and Olcott set up the HQ at **Adyar**, not Madanapalle.

=== Ramakrishna Paramahamsa (1836-1886)
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Original name | **Gadadhar Chattopadhyay** |
| Role | Mystic and priest of the **Kali temple, Dakshineswar**, near Calcutta |
| Guru | **Totapuri** (Vedanta) |
| Wife | **Sarada Devi (Saradamani / Shardamani)**, married 1859 – the "Holy Mother" – not Rammohan Roy's wife, Vivekananda's mother or Keshab's daughter |
| Chief disciple | **Swami Vivekananda** |
- **Teachings:** "**As many faiths, so many paths**"; **Krishna, Hari, Ram, Christ, Allah** = different names of one God; salvation through **renunciation, meditation and bhakti**; "service of man is service of God". Wrote no books.
- Laid the foundation of the **Ramakrishna Math** with his young monks; did **not** found the Mission.
> Gadadhar = Ramakrishna; Narendranath = Vivekananda; Saradamani = Ramakrishna's wife.
