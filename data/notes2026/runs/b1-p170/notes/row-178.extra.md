=== Educational Thinkers – Pedagogy Basics
- **John Dewey:** philosophy of **experimentalism** (pragmatism / instrumentalism) – **learning by doing**, ideas tested in experience.
- **Pestalozzi:** the **first to integrate psychology with educational theory** – aimed to "psychologise education" (child's mental development; head, heart and hand).
- **Society and the curriculum:** different socio-economic strata with different class interests **set up mutually incompatible tensions in the educational programme**.
