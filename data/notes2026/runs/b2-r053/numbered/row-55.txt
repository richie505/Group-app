## SUB 0 | Rajamannar Committee – Appointment and Purpose
[0:0] TABLE head: Item | Detail
  [0:0.0] Formal name | Centre-State Relations Inquiry Committee
  [0:0.1] Appointed by | Tamil Nadu – DMK government of M. Karunanidhi (not the Centre)
  [0:0.2] Year | 1969 (22 Sept); report 1971
  [0:0.3] Chairman | Dr P.V. Rajamannar, former Chief Justice of the Madras High Court
  [0:0.4] Members | Three in all – Rajamannar, A. Lakshmanaswami Mudaliar, P. Chandra Reddy
[0:1] - **First** committee on Centre-State relations appointed by a **State**.
[0:2] - Purpose: to **study the Centre-State relations** as a whole and suggest amendments to secure **utmost autonomy to the States** (not Tamil Nadu's relations with the Centre or with other southern States, nor UT-State relations).
[0:3] - Took forward **C.N. Annadurai's 1967** view that the Union need not control **education and health**; it was to reduce, not strengthen, the Centre's control over them.
[0:4] - P.V. Rajamannar also chaired the **4th Finance Commission**.
[0:5] ~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.15 p.237-238; app notes 2:55
## SUB 1 | Rajamannar Recommendations
[1:0] TABLE head: Subject | Rajamannar recommended
  [1:0.0] Inter-State Council | Set up immediately under Art. 263; no decision affecting States without consulting it; every Bill affecting States to be referred to it first
  [1:0.1] Article 356 | Complete deletion (with 357 and 365); if kept, only for a complete breakdown
  [1:0.2] All-India Services | Abolish IAS, IPS (and IFoS) – to reduce central administrative control
  [1:0.3] Planning Commission | Abolish – extra-constitutional; alone decided grants to States; replace with a statutory body
  [1:0.4] Finance Commission | Make permanent; revitalise with wider revenue devolution
  [1:0.5] Governor | Delete "pleasure of the Governor" clause for the State ministry – not strengthen the Governor system
[1:1] - Ending IAS and IPS = **Rajamannar** (UPSC 1993) – not the Dhebar, Kalelkar or Kher commissions, nor Sarkaria.
[1:2] - Passage clue: "made a call for **immediate constitution of the Inter-State Council**, every Bill affecting States referred to it first, Art. 356 only in rare cases" = **Rajamannar**.
[1:3] - Passage clue: "greater autonomy in legislative and fiscal matters, critical analysis of the '**strong Centre**' concept, States' competence to amend Central laws, mandatory consultation" = **Rajamannar** (Sarkaria **defended** a strong Centre).
[1:4] # Findings
[1:5] - Despite the federal form, States had become "**administrative units of the Centre**"; causes – special central powers, **one-party rule**, weak State finances, **central planning**.
[1:6] - Overreach Articles: **256, 257, 365** (not 280, 282).
[1:7] - The Centre's control over States in the **planning process** has been termed "**municipal relations**" (UPSC 1994) – not legislative, administrative or financial control.
[1:8] - Relations depend on constitutional provisions, conventions and practices, judicial interpretation and mechanisms for dialogue.
[1:9] # Seventh Schedule and residuary powers
[1:10] - Transfer subjects (e.g. **stock exchanges, oil fields, trade unions, industrial disputes**, inland waterways) from Union/Concurrent Lists to the **State List** – **not foreign affairs**.
[1:11] - **Residuary powers → States** (Sarkaria: residuary taxation with Parliament, other residuary to the Concurrent List).
[1:12] ~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.15 p.237-238; NextGen Polity - APPSC Exams p.313; app notes 2:55
## SUB 2 | Aftermath and Influence
[2:0] - **Centre ignored** the report; no action.
[2:1] - **April 1974:** TN Assembly adopted a **State autonomy resolution** based on it.
[2:2] - DMK slogan: "**autonomy for the States, federalism at the Centre**".
[2:3] - Base for **Anandpur Sahib (1973)** and the **West Bengal memorandum (1977)**.
[2:4] - The ISC (1990) came through **Sarkaria**, not Rajamannar.
[2:5] - **Kurian Joseph Committee** – appointed by Tamil Nadu in **2025** (Part I, 16 Feb 2026): State (1969) → Centre (1983, 2007) → State (2025).
[2:6] ~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.15 p.238; app notes 2:55
