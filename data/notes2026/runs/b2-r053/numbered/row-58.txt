## SUB 1 | First Administrative Reforms Commission (1966)
[1:0] - Six-member; set up a **study team on Centre-State relations** under **M.C. Setalvad**; report **1969** with **22 recommendations**.
[1:1] - Asked for an **Inter-State Council under Article 263** (1969); Governors with long public life and **non-partisan** attitude; maximum delegation and more resources to States.
[1:2] - Deploy central armed forces in States "**either on their request or otherwise**".
[1:3] - Also: **no political interference** in administration (civil-service neutrality).
[1:4] - **No action** was taken on its recommendations.
[1:5] ~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.15 p.237; app notes 2:58
## SUB 2 | Sarkaria and the Inter-Governmental Council
[2:0] - Sarkaria Commission **appointed 1983** (report 1988).
[2:1] - Recommended a **permanent Inter-State Council called the Inter-Governmental Council** under **Art. 263** – the only recommendation that became an institution: **Inter-State Council, 1990**.
[2:2] - Sarkaria = first to recommend **cooperative federalism** as the guiding principle (not Rajamannar, NCRWC or Punchhi).
[2:3] - **Chronology:** First ARC (1969) → Sarkaria (1983-88) → ISC (1990) → Punchhi (2007-10).
[2:4] - **Standing Committee of the ISC** (1996) is chaired by the **Union Home Minister**; it finished discussing all **273 Punchhi** recommendations in **May 2018**.
[2:5] ~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.16 p.253-254; app notes 2:58
## SUB 3 | NCRWC – Venkatachaliah Commission
[3:0] - **Constitutional Review Commission**, set up **February 2000** (Vajpayee govt), chaired by **M.N. Venkatachaliah**; reviewed the first 50 years incl. **social justice**; report **2002**.
[3:1] - **Centre-State chapter:** greater **fiscal autonomy for States**; **safeguards against misuse of Art. 356** (not deletion); Governor appointed after consulting the CM; **Inter-State Trade and Commerce Commission**.
[3:2] - **Judiciary:** **All India Judicial Service** and a **National Judicial Commission**.
[3:3] - **Elections:** nearly **two-thirds (~70%)** of elected representatives win with **less than 50%** of votes cast; examine a **50% + 1** rule.
[3:4] ~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.90 p.866-874; app notes 2:58
## SUB 4 | Second Administrative Reforms Commission
[4:0] - Constituted **31 August 2005** as a **Commission of Inquiry**, chairman **Veerappa Moily** (V. Ramachandran from 2009); **15 reports, 2006-2009**; the "**Bible**" of administrative reforms.
[4:1] - Key recommendations: **Code of Ethics**, **Right to Public Services** laws, **Department of E-Governance**, financial autonomy for PRIs and ULBs; a **National Judicial Council**.
[4:2] - **AFSPA:** its **5th report (Public Order, 2007)** recommended **repeal of AFSPA** (after the Jeevan Reddy Committee, 2005) – earlier than Punchhi (2010).
[4:3] TABLE head: Report | Relevance
  [4:3.0] 5th – Public Order | Police, AFSPA
  [4:3.1] 6th – Local Governance | Third tier
  [4:3.2] 7th – Capacity Building for Conflict Resolution | Inter-state and regional conflicts
  [4:3.3] 15th – State and District Administration | State-level governance
[4:4] - **NCRWC = National Judicial Commission**; **2nd ARC = National Judicial Council**.
[4:5] ~~ Sources: 2nd ARC 5th Report (darpg.gov.in); PRS; app notes 2:58
