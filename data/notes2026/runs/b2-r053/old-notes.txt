=== NOTES 2:53 | Inter-state Relations – Inter-state Council, Zonal Councils, River-water Disputes (article 262), Full Faith and Credit
## SUB 0 | Articles at A Glance
[53:0:0] TABLE head: Article | Content
  [53:0:0.0] 261 | Full faith and credit to public acts, records and judicial proceedings
  [53:0:0.1] 262(1) | Parliament may provide for adjudication of inter-State river water disputes
  [53:0:0.2] 262(2) | Parliament may bar the Supreme Court and all other courts
  [53:0:0.3] 263 | President may establish an Inter-State Council
  [53:0:0.4] 131 | SC original jurisdiction – excludes water disputes
  [53:0:0.5] 301 | Freedom of trade, commerce and intercourse throughout India
  [53:0:0.6] 302 / 303 | Parliament may restrict in public interest / no discrimination
  [53:0:0.7] 304(b) | State bill restricting inter-State trade needs President's previous sanction
  [53:0:0.8] 306 | Part B States – repealed
  [53:0:0.9] 307 | Authority for Arts 301-304 (e.g. Inter-State Commerce Commission)
[53:0:1] Art. 262 = river water disputes; Art. 263 = Inter-State Council; Art. 131 excludes water disputes.
## SUB 1 | Full Faith and Credit – Article 261
[53:1:0] - **261(1):** public acts, records and judicial proceedings of the **Union and every State** get **full faith and credit** throughout India.
[53:1:1] - **261(2):** manner of proof and their effect – decided by **Parliament** by law.
[53:1:2] - **261(3):** **final judgments or orders of civil courts** can be **executed anywhere** in India according to law.
[53:1:3] - Modelled on the **US** Full Faith and Credit Clause.
## SUB 2 | Inter-state Trade and Commerce – Arts 301-307
[53:2:0] - **Art. 301:** freedom of trade, commerce and intercourse throughout India.
[53:2:1] - **Art. 302:** Parliament may impose restrictions in **public interest**.
[53:2:2] - **Art. 304(b):** State may impose reasonable restrictions, but the bill needs the **previous sanction of the President** (PYQ 2018).
[53:2:3] - **Art. 306** (Part B States) repealed; **Art. 307** – authority for Arts 301-304.
[53:2:4] - **Art. 305:** saves **existing laws** and laws providing for **State monopolies** from Arts 301 and 303.
## SUB 3 | Inter-state Council (art. 263)
[53:3:0] # Origin and Nature
[53:3:1] - **Established by the President** – Presidential order of **28 May 1990** (APPSC-G1 2017).
[53:3:2] - Recommended by the **Sarkaria Commission**: a permanent body called the "**Inter-Governmental Council**" (RPSC 2023).
[53:3:3] - Earlier backers: **ARC (1966-69)** and **Rajamannar Committee (1969)**.
[53:3:4] - Sarkaria also proposed renaming the NDC as **National Economic and Development Council** under Art. 263.
[53:3:5] - **Constitutional, recommendatory** body – not judicial; purpose: investigate and discuss subjects of common interest (Union-States, among States) and recommend policy coordination.
[53:3:6] - Decides all questions by **consensus**; meetings **in camera**; to meet at least **3 times a year**, but has met only about 11 times since 1990.
[53:3:7] # Composition
[53:3:8] TABLE head: Post | Holder
  [53:3:8.0] Chairman | Prime Minister
  [53:3:8.1] Members | CMs of all States and of UTs with legislatures
  [53:3:8.2] Members | Administrators of UTs without assemblies
  [53:3:8.3] Members | 6 Union Cabinet ministers nominated by the PM
  [53:3:8.4] Members (only then) | Governors of States under President's Rule
  [53:3:8.5] Standing Committee chair | Union Home Minister
  [53:3:8.6] Secretariat | Inter-State Council Secretariat (MHA)
[53:3:9] - Other Art. 263 councils: **Central Council of Health**, Central Council of Local Self-Government, **Transport Development Council**.
[53:3:10] ISC chairman = PM; Standing Committee = Union Home Minister.
## SUB 4 | Zonal Councils
[53:4:0] - Statutory bodies under **Sections 15-22 of the States Reorganisation Act, 1956**; idea of **PM Nehru** (1956); set up 1957.
[53:4:1] - **Five** zonal councils: Northern, Central, Eastern, Western, Southern.
[53:4:2] - **Sikkim:** in the **Eastern** Zonal Council; joined the **North Eastern Council** in 2002 ⚠ APPSC key (2004) gave Central zone.
[53:4:3] - **Chairman:** **Union Home Minister** (each council).
[53:4:4] - **Vice-Chairman:** a **CM by annual rotation**.
[53:4:5] - Members: CMs, **2 other ministers per State**, UT Administrators/LGs.
[53:4:6] - **Deliberative and advisory**; advice **not binding**; criticised as only recommendatory and needing more resources.
[53:4:7] # Southern Zonal Council
[53:4:8] - Members: **Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala, Telangana, Puducherry**.
[53:4:9] - 29th meeting at **Tirupati**, chaired by Amit Shah.
[53:4:10] - **27th meeting (2016):** chaired by Union Home Minister **Rajnath Singh**.
[53:4:11] - **31st meeting – 20 Aug 2026, Tamil Nadu**: Amit Shah urged early settlement of southern water disputes and river-linking. Telangana to raise Karnataka's unauthorised Krishna projects and seek national status for **Kaleshwaram** and **Palamuru-Rangareddy** (Aug 2026).
[53:4:12] Zonal Council chairman = Union Home Minister, not the PM; vice-chairman = CM by rotation.
## SUB 5 | North Eastern Council
[53:5:0] - Set up under the **North Eastern Council Act, 1971** (statutory).
[53:5:1] - **2002 amendment:** **Sikkim** added as the **8th** member State; NEC became a **regional planning body**; President nominates **3** members.
[53:5:2] - Members: **Governors and CMs** of the 8 NE States.
[53:5:3] - Chairman: Union Home Minister; Vice-Chairman: Minister of DoNER.

## SUB 2 | Recommendations on the Governor
[57:2:0] # Appointment and Tenure
[57:2:1] - **Fixed tenure of 5 years**, to shield the Governor from Central pressure.
[57:2:2] - Should be an **eminent person** not in **active politics, even at the local level**, for **a couple of years** before appointment.

[57:2:4] # Removal – Article 156
[57:2:5] - Delete the words **"during the pleasure of the President"** from **Article 156**.
[57:2:6] - Removal only by **impeachment by the State Legislature**, on the lines of the **President's impeachment**.
[57:2:7] # Universities and Bills
[57:2:8] - Governors should **not be Chancellors** of universities or hold other **statutory roles**, so the office stays out of clashes with State governments.
[57:2:9] - A constitutional amendment giving the Governor **six months** to decide on **Bills** passed by the Assembly.
[57:2:10] Periodic **"rotation of Governors"** and **"abolish the office of Governor"** are distractors – Punchhi recommended neither.
## SUB 3 | Articles 355 and 356 – Emergency Provisions
[57:3:0] - **Amend Articles 355 and 356**; do not leave them as they are.
[57:3:1] - **Article 356** to be used **sparingly**, only as a **last resort**, never in a **partisan** way.
[57:3:2] - **"Localised emergency":** the Centre takes over a specific troubled area – a **district or part of a district** – for a **limited period**, instead of a whole State.
[57:3:3] "The Commission said there is no need to amend Articles 355 and 356" = WRONG (APPSC G2 2017).
[57:3:4] - **Article 357:** laws made by **Parliament** (or the President) for a State under President's Rule **continue in force after the Proclamation ends**, until **altered, repealed or amended** by the State Legislature.

## SUB 0 | Types of Federalism
[59:0:0] TABLE head: Type | Meaning
  [59:0:0.0] Cooperative federalism | Centre and States work together on a common platform; NITI's version is bottom-up, also called "marble-cake federalism"
  [59:0:0.1] Competitive federalism | States compete with each other and with the Centre to draw investment and improve outcomes
  [59:0:0.2] Competitive sub-federalism | Competition among local bodies / districts (APPSC G1 2017), e.g. Aspirational Districts Programme rankings
[59:0:1] - Cooperative federalism was **first suggested by the Sarkaria Commission** (APPSC G1 2017); put into practice by **NITI Aayog (2015)**.

[59:0:3] - Competitive federalism tool: better policies and services; driver: **NITI's performance rankings**.
