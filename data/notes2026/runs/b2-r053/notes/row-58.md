=== First Administrative Reforms Commission (1966)
- Six-member; set up a **study team on Centre-State relations** under **M.C. Setalvad**; report **1969** with **22 recommendations**.
- Asked for an **Inter-State Council under Article 263** (1969); Governors with long public life and **non-partisan** attitude; maximum delegation and more resources to States.
- Deploy central armed forces in States "**either on their request or otherwise**".
- Also: **no political interference** in administration (civil-service neutrality).
- **No action** was taken on its recommendations.
~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.15 p.237; app notes 2:58

=== Sarkaria and the Inter-Governmental Council
- Sarkaria Commission **appointed 1983** (report 1988).
- Recommended a **permanent Inter-State Council called the Inter-Governmental Council** under **Art. 263** – the only recommendation that became an institution: **Inter-State Council, 1990**.
- Sarkaria = first to recommend **cooperative federalism** as the guiding principle (not Rajamannar, NCRWC or Punchhi).
- **Chronology:** First ARC (1969) → Sarkaria (1983-88) → ISC (1990) → Punchhi (2007-10).
- **Standing Committee of the ISC** (1996) is chaired by the **Union Home Minister**; it finished discussing all **273 Punchhi** recommendations in **May 2018**.
~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.16 p.253-254; app notes 2:58

=== NCRWC – Venkatachaliah Commission
- **Constitutional Review Commission**, set up **February 2000** (Vajpayee govt), chaired by **M.N. Venkatachaliah**; reviewed the first 50 years incl. **social justice**; report **2002**.
- **Centre-State chapter:** greater **fiscal autonomy for States**; **safeguards against misuse of Art. 356** (not deletion); Governor appointed after consulting the CM; **Inter-State Trade and Commerce Commission**.
- **Judiciary:** **All India Judicial Service** and a **National Judicial Commission**.
- **Elections:** nearly **two-thirds (~70%)** of elected representatives win with **less than 50%** of votes cast; examine a **50% + 1** rule.
~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.90 p.866-874; app notes 2:58

=== Second Administrative Reforms Commission
- Constituted **31 August 2005** as a **Commission of Inquiry**, chairman **Veerappa Moily** (V. Ramachandran from 2009); **15 reports, 2006-2009**; the "**Bible**" of administrative reforms.
- Key recommendations: **Code of Ethics**, **Right to Public Services** laws, **Department of E-Governance**, financial autonomy for PRIs and ULBs; a **National Judicial Council**.
- **AFSPA:** its **5th report (Public Order, 2007)** recommended **repeal of AFSPA** (after the Jeevan Reddy Committee, 2005) – earlier than Punchhi (2010).
| Report | Relevance |
|---|---|
| 5th – Public Order | Police, AFSPA |
| **6th – Local Governance** | Third tier |
| **7th – Capacity Building for Conflict Resolution** | Inter-state and regional conflicts |
| 15th – State and District Administration | State-level governance |
- **NCRWC = National Judicial Commission**; **2nd ARC = National Judicial Council**.

=== Committee of Governors, NIC and Inquiry Commissions
- **Bhagwan Sahay** Committee (1970-71): Governor = **constitutional head of the State** and a **link with the Centre**; keeps watch and **reports to the Centre**.
| Body | Head | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| **National Integration Council** | **Prime Minister** (Manmohan Singh in 2011; now Narendra Modi) | Communalism, casteism, regionalism |
| Sri Krishna Commission | B.N. Srikrishna | **1992-93 Mumbai riots** |
| Liberhan Commission | Justice **M.S. Liberhan** | **Babri Masjid demolition** (1992) |
- Centre-State questions go to the **Parliamentary Standing Committee on Home Affairs**; the Standing Committee on Rural Development notes Panchayats are a **State subject** and devolution varies across States.
- **Kurian Joseph Committee** (Tamil Nadu, 2025): federalism needs a reset comparable to the reforms of **1991**.
