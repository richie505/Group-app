## 3. Repeats and traps settled
- **Inter-State Council:** set up by the **President** under Art. 263 on **Sarkaria's** advice (1990). The Rajamannar Committee only urged it. Chaired by the **PM**; its Standing Committee by the **Home Minister** (Amit Shah, Nov 2024).
- **Constitutional status:** ISC = constitutional; Zonal Councils, NEC and river tribunals = statutory; NDC, Governors' Conference and NSC = executive. That makes the UPSC 1995 answer "NDC and Governors' Conference" only (the "I, II, III" version gets an added option plus a dispute line).
- **North Eastern Council:** 8 States since 2002 (Sikkim added). Its members are the Governors and CMs plus 3 nominated by the President.
- **AFSPA "first recommendation":** the 2nd ARC (2007, 5th report), not Punchhi (2010). Flagged as disputed.
- **Sarkaria report year:** 1988. The BPSC key's 1987 is shown as a dispute line.
