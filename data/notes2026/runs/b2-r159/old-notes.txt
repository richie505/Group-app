## SUB 3 | Bimstec
[159:3:0] # Name and Growth
[159:3:1] - Full form: **Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation**.
[159:3:2] - Began 1997 as **BIST-EC** (Bangladesh, India, Sri Lanka, Thailand); Myanmar joined 1997; **Nepal and Bhutan joined 2004**.
[159:3:3] - Sectors **cut from 14 to 7** (not expanded); each has a lead country. India leads **security** (counter-terrorism, disaster management, energy).
