=== BRICS
# Origin and expansion
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 2001 | Term "BRIC" coined by **Jim O'Neill** (Goldman Sachs) |
| 2006 | BRIC formalised (foreign ministers) |
| 2009 | **First summit, 16 June, Yekaterinburg (Russia)** – Brazil, Russia, India, China |
| 2010-11 | **South Africa** invited Dec 2010; first attended at **Sanya, China (2011)** |
| June 2022 | **Iran** and Argentina applied to join |
| Aug 2023 | 15th Summit, Johannesburg: six invited – **Argentina**, Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, Saudi Arabia, UAE; **Argentina declined (Dec 2023)** |
| 1 Jan 2024 | Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, UAE joined |
| 2025 | **Indonesia** joined |
- Members now **10**; **Saudi Arabia's** status ambiguous (listed by some sources, yet to formally join, 14 Sep 2026).
- **BRIC in 2010:** China's GDP was more than the combined GDP of Brazil, Russia and India; but India + Brazil together outnumber China's population (UPSC 2010: A only).
- India is a **founder**; India **avoids de-dollarisation**. Debate: "anti-West" (Russia, China, Iran) vs "non-West" (India, Brazil, South Africa).

=== G20
# Origin
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Planned at | G7 Cologne summit, June 1999 |
| Set up | 26 Sep 1999 as a forum of **finance ministers and central bank governors** (after the Asian Financial Crisis) |
| First meeting | **Berlin, Dec 1999** |
| First leaders' summit | **Washington D.C., 2008** (global financial crisis) |
- **Members:** **19 countries** – Argentina, Australia, Brazil, Canada, China, France, Germany, India, Indonesia, Italy, Japan, **Mexico**, Russia, Saudi Arabia, **South Africa**, South Korea, **Turkey**, UK, US – + **EU** + **African Union** (permanent member since **2023**). **Not members:** Malaysia, New Zealand, Iran, Vietnam, Singapore.
- **No permanent secretariat**; annual rotating presidency. **Troika:** previous + current + next presidencies; 2022 = **Indonesia, Italy, India**.
- About **85% of world GDP, 75% of trade, two-thirds of population**.

# India's presidency (2022-23)
- India took over from **Indonesia on 1 December 2022** – India's **maiden presidency**; first time India convened the Leaders' Summit (Sep 2023); **43 Heads of Delegations** – the largest ever in the G20.
- Theme **"Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam – One Earth, One Family, One Future"**; **"Data for Development"** an integral part of it.
- **New Delhi Declaration (2023):** **African Union** admitted as permanent member; **Global Biofuels Alliance** launched.
- **Brisbane-eThekwini Goal** (revised, G20 Development Ministers 2025): cut gender gap in labour participation by **25% by 2030** from 2012.
> Exam angle: host city by year; India's theme vs Indonesia's theme; members (Argentina, Mexico, South Africa, Turkey).

=== G7 / G8
- **Members (7):** US, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Canada, Japan. The **EU attends** meetings. No treaty or secretariat.
- **Group of Six 1975** → Canada joined **1976** → **Russia** joined 1997-98 → **G8** (France, Germany, Italy, Japan, UK, US, Canada, Russia); suspended **2014 after Crimea** → back to G7.
- **Not members** (distractors): **Russia**, **Netherlands**, **New Zealand**, India, China.
| G7 summit | Venue |
|---|---|
| 1995 (21st) | **Halifax, Canada** |
| 2016 (42nd) | **Ise-Shima, Japan** |
| 2022 | Germany – **PGII** launched (counter to China's BRI) |
| 2025 (51st) | Kananaskis, Canada |
| June 2026 | France – PM Modi met President Trump |
- **India:** regular **outreach invitee**, not a member (2025 = sixth consecutive invitation).
> Exam angle: "not a G7 member" → Russia / Netherlands / New Zealand; Halifax = G7.

| BRICS | 10 | Founder |

- **Indonesia** is in BRICS (2025) and MIKTA, but **not** in the Quad or IBSA.
# IBSA and BASIC
- IBSA founded **2003, Brasilia Declaration**; **IBSA Trust Fund** (2004, run by **UNOSSC**); **IBSAMAR** naval exercise.
- **5th IBSA Summit – Pretoria, 18 October 2011**; Leaders' meetings: Rio de Janeiro (Nov 2024), **Johannesburg (Nov 2025)** – UNSC expansion "not an option but an imperative".
- India's proposals (Nov 2025): **IBSA Fund for Climate-Resilient Agriculture**, **IBSA Digital Innovation Alliance** (UPI, CoWIN), NSA-level dialogue.
- **BASIC** formed **2009**, before **COP15 Copenhagen**; stand: **CBDR-RC**; climate finance from developed countries.
# Quad
- Origin: **Tsunami Core Group** after the **2004 Indian Ocean tsunami**; first meeting **2007**; **revived 2017**.
- First leaders' summit – **virtual, March 2021**; first in-person – **Washington, Sep 2021**; Dec 2024 statement marked 20 years.
- Initiatives: **Quad Vaccine Partnership**, **Critical and Emerging Technologies group**, **IPMDA** (2022).
# Trans-Pacific Partnership
- **12 Pacific Rim nations**; deal concluded **October 2015**, signed **4 February 2016** (Auckland); about **40% of world GDP**; **India was not** a member; the US left in 2017 → **CPTPP** (2018).
# Colombo Security Conclave
- Began **2011** as India-Sri Lanka-**Maldives** maritime trilateral; renamed CSC **2020**; secretariat **Colombo**.
- **Members:** India, Sri Lanka, Maldives, **Mauritius**, **Bangladesh**; **Seychelles – observer**.
- **Five pillars:** maritime safety and security; counter-terrorism and radicalisation; **trafficking and organised crime**; cyber security and critical infrastructure; **HADR**.

=== Share of the World Economy
| Grouping | Share |
|---|---|
| G20 | about 85% of GDP, 75%+ of trade, two-thirds of population |
| Expanded BRICS | 40%+ of GDP (PPP), about 50% of population, about 26% of trade |
| BRICS in 2011 | 20% of GDP but only 11% of IMF votes |
| G7 | about 43% of nominal GDP (2023); about 30% in PPP (2024) |
- Country shares (around 2019-20, nominal): **Japan ~6%** of world GDP, China ~16%, India ~3%, Canada ~2%.
- BRICS = **46% of global trade** is a wrong statement; "first summit 2009 in Russia" is correct.
- Firsts: G20 first leaders' summit Washington 2008; first BRIC summit Yekaterinburg 2009; AU became the G20's second regional member after the EU.
