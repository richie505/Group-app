## SUB 1 | G20
[1:0] # Origin
[1:1] TABLE head: Item | Fact
  [1:1.0] Planned at | G7 Cologne summit, June 1999
  [1:1.1] Set up | 26 Sep 1999 as a forum of finance ministers and central bank governors (after the Asian Financial Crisis)
  [1:1.2] First meeting | Berlin, Dec 1999
  [1:1.3] First leaders' summit | Washington D.C., 2008 (global financial crisis)
[1:2] - **Members:** **19 countries** – Argentina, Australia, Brazil, Canada, China, France, Germany, India, Indonesia, Italy, Japan, **Mexico**, Russia, Saudi Arabia, **South Africa**, South Korea, **Turkey**, UK, US – + **EU** + **African Union** (permanent member since **2023**). **Not members:** Malaysia, New Zealand, Iran, Vietnam, Singapore.
[1:3] - **No permanent secretariat**; annual rotating presidency. **Troika:** previous + current + next presidencies; 2022 = **Indonesia, Italy, India**.
[1:4] - About **85% of world GDP, 75% of trade, two-thirds of population**.
[1:5] # Summits
[1:6] TABLE head: Year | Venue
  [1:6.0] 2018 | Buenos Aires, Argentina
  [1:6.1] 2019 | Osaka, Japan
  [1:6.2] 2020 | Riyadh (15th, virtual) – Saudi Arabia took the chair from Japan (Dec 2019)
  [1:6.3] 2022 | Bali, Indonesia – theme "Recover Together, Recover Stronger"
  [1:6.4] 2023 | 18th, New Delhi (Bharat Mandapam, 9-10 Sep)
  [1:6.5] 2024 | Rio de Janeiro
  [1:6.6] 2025 | Johannesburg, South Africa – first G20 summit in Africa
[1:7] # India's presidency (2022-23)
[1:8] - India took over from **Indonesia on 1 December 2022** – India's **maiden presidency**; first time India convened the Leaders' Summit (Sep 2023); **43 Heads of Delegations** – the largest ever in the G20.
[1:9] - Theme **"Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam – One Earth, One Family, One Future"**; **"Data for Development"** an integral part of it.
[1:10] - **New Delhi Declaration (2023):** **African Union** admitted as permanent member; **Global Biofuels Alliance** launched.
[1:11] - **Brisbane-eThekwini Goal** (revised, G20 Development Ministers 2025): cut gender gap in labour participation by **25% by 2030** from 2012.
[1:12] > Exam angle: host city by year; India's theme vs Indonesia's theme; members (Argentina, Mexico, South Africa, Turkey).

## SUB 2 | G7 / G8
[2:0] - **Members (7):** US, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Canada, Japan. The **EU attends** meetings. No treaty or secretariat.
[2:1] - **Group of Six 1975** → Canada joined **1976** → **Russia** joined 1997-98 → **G8** (France, Germany, Italy, Japan, UK, US, Canada, Russia); suspended **2014 after Crimea** → back to G7.
[2:2] - **Not members** (distractors): **Russia**, **Netherlands**, **New Zealand**, India, China.
[2:3] TABLE head: G7 summit | Venue
  [2:3.0] 1995 (21st) | Halifax, Canada
  [2:3.1] 2016 (42nd) | Ise-Shima, Japan
  [2:3.2] 2022 | Germany – PGII launched (counter to China's BRI)
  [2:3.3] 2025 (51st) | Kananaskis, Canada
  [2:3.4] June 2026 | France – PM Modi met President Trump
[2:4] - **India:** regular **outreach invitee**, not a member (2025 = sixth consecutive invitation).
[2:5] - Economy shares (around 2019-20, nominal): **Japan ~6%** of world GDP, China ~16%, India ~3%; G7 together about 43% of nominal GDP (2023), about 30% in PPP.
[2:6] > Exam angle: "not a G7 member" → Russia / Netherlands / New Zealand; Halifax = G7.

[3:3] # IBSA and BASIC
[3:4] - IBSA founded **2003, Brasilia Declaration**; **IBSA Trust Fund** (2004, run by **UNOSSC**); **IBSAMAR** naval exercise.
[3:5] - **5th IBSA Summit – Pretoria, 18 October 2011**; Leaders' meetings: Rio de Janeiro (Nov 2024), **Johannesburg (Nov 2025)** – UNSC expansion "not an option but an imperative".
[3:6] - India's proposals (Nov 2025): **IBSA Fund for Climate-Resilient Agriculture**, **IBSA Digital Innovation Alliance** (UPI, CoWIN), NSA-level dialogue.
[3:7] - **BASIC** formed **2009**, before **COP15 Copenhagen**; stand: **CBDR-RC**; climate finance from developed countries.
[3:8] # Quad
[3:9] - Origin: **Tsunami Core Group** after the **2004 Indian Ocean tsunami**; first meeting **2007**; **revived 2017**.
[3:10] - First leaders' summit – **virtual, March 2021**; first in-person – **Washington, Sep 2021**; Dec 2024 statement marked 20 years.
[3:11] - Initiatives: **Quad Vaccine Partnership**, **Critical and Emerging Technologies group**, **IPMDA** (2022).
[3:12] # Trans-Pacific Partnership
[3:13] - **12 Pacific Rim nations**; deal concluded **October 2015**, signed **4 February 2016** (Auckland); about **40% of world GDP**; **India was not** a member; the US left in 2017 → **CPTPP** (2018).
[3:14] # Colombo Security Conclave
[3:15] - Began **2011** as India-Sri Lanka-**Maldives** maritime trilateral; renamed CSC **2020**; secretariat **Colombo**.
[3:16] - **Members:** India, Sri Lanka, Maldives, **Mauritius**, **Bangladesh**; **Seychelles – observer**.
[3:17] - **Five pillars:** maritime safety and security; counter-terrorism and radicalisation; **trafficking and organised crime**; cyber security and critical infrastructure; **HADR**.
