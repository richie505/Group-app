## 5. Key corrections worth knowing
- **BIMSTEC Feb 2020** conference (drug trafficking) was hosted by **India** (New Delhi), not Bangladesh.
- **India-Portugal maritime transport and ports agreement** was signed **14 Feb 2020**, not 14 Jan 2020.
- The 2019 **ABU Media Summit on Climate Action** was in **Kathmandu** (ABU calls it the 5th edition; the question says 8th).
- **G7 non-member (APPSC 2012):** three printed options are non-members; the book's answer "Netherlands and New Zealand" (Russia was still in the G8 in 2012) is added as an option.
