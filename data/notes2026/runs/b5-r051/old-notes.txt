## SUB 7 | Radar Types and Doppler Effect
[51:7:0] # Doppler Effect
[51:7:1] - Apparent change in frequency from relative motion: **approaching → higher** pitch; receding → lower (passing siren).
[51:7:2] - Uses: radar speed guns, **medical ultrasound** (blood-flow velocity), **weather radar** (wind speed), SONAR, bats, **satellite tracking**, astronomy (**red shift** moving away, **blue shift** approaching; **Hubble's expanding universe**).
[51:7:3] # Radar Types
[51:7:4] TABLE head: Type | Principle / use
  [51:7:4.0] Doppler | Speed from frequency shift
  [51:7:4.1] SAR | Imaging at night and through clouds
  [51:7:4.2] AESA | T/R module on every element; electronic beam, no moving antenna; tracks multiple targets – UTTAM, MF-STAR
  [51:7:4.3] VHF anti-stealth | Long waves pick up stealth aircraft
  [51:7:4.4] Over-the-horizon | HF waves bounced off the ionosphere
