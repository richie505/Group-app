- **"First / oldest":** first indigenous carrier, first carrier (1961 Vikrant), first SSBN, first indigenous fighter, oldest shipyard, first private-yard OPV, first solar-panel warship.

- **Match / pairs:** training institutes (CDS 2014), Do-228 / IL-76 / C-17 (UPSC 2025, twice), Aero India / iDEX pairs.
- **Chronology:** 2022 defence events (TSPSC 2023).
- **Assertion-reason:** Vikrant and Aatmanirbhar Bharat.
- **Definition / science:** Doppler effect, RADAR, stealth, explosives' other names.
- **Current linkage:** Navy operational demonstration at Visakhapatnam (2022), Tavasya (2025), Vagir (2023), hydrogen ferry (2024), DefExpo and Aero India editions, JIMEX-24.

## 6. Key issues settled
- **INS Shakti** (UPPCS 2010): printed "destroyer" is impossible – INS Shakti is a foreign-built fleet tanker; option added.
- **First indigenous fighter**: keys say Tejas; strictly HF-24 Marut (PMF) – disputed, Tejas kept.
- **2022 chronology**: INAS 325 commissioned 31 May 2022 (PIB), so B, A, C, D.
- **JIMEX-18** at Visakhapatnam, not Chennai → (a), (b), (d). **V-409** built by Bharati Defence. **Varuna** = France. **Aero India 2019 / iDEX** pairs both correct.
