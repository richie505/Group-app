## SUB 0 | Tejas and Other Indigenous Aircraft
[0:0] # Tejas LCA
[0:1] - **What:** India's indigenous **Light Combat Aircraft (LCA)**, a single-engine, **compound delta-wing, tailless** multirole **supersonic** fighter (Mach 1.6+); smallest and lightest supersonic multirole fighter of its class; digital **fly-by-wire** – India's **first indigenous fly-by-wire aircraft**; over 40% composites.
[0:2] - **Who:** **designed by ADA** (Aeronautical Development Agency, DRDO framework), **built by HAL**; named by **Atal Bihari Vajpayee**; HAL handed the first two to the IAF in 2016 (No. 45 Sqn "Flying Daggers").
[0:3] - **Variants:** Mk-1 (4th gen) → **Mk-1A** (4.5 gen; **Uttam AESA**, EW suite, air-to-air refuelling) → **Mk-2** (**GE F414** engine, canards); naval LCA and trainer.
[0:4] - **Orders:** **25 Sep 2025** HAL contract for **97 Mk-1A** (68 fighters + 29 twin-seaters), Rs 62,370 crore, 64%+ indigenous; earlier order of **83**.
[0:5] - **"First indigenous fighter":** exam keys say **Tejas**; strictly Tejas is India's **second** indigenous jet fighter – the first was **HAL HF-24 Marut** (1961).
[0:6] - **Distractors:** Akash = missile, Vikrant = carrier, Arjun = tank, Garud/Surya Kiran (aerobatic team)/Sukhoi are not the LCA.

## SUB 4 | Radar, Doppler Effect and Stealth
[4:0] # RADAR and the Doppler effect
[4:1] - **RADAR** (Radio Detection And Ranging): **detects and fixes the position** (range, direction, speed) of objects with radio waves.
[4:2] - **Doppler effect:** apparent change in frequency from relative motion – source **approaching → higher** pitch; **receding → lower** (a train moving away sounds lower).
[4:3] - **Uses:** **police speed radar guns**, **satellite tracking**, medical ultrasound (blood-flow velocity), **Doppler Weather Radar** (cyclones, **heavy rainfall and thunderstorms**), **expanding universe** (red shift = moving away, Hubble).
[4:4] TABLE head: Radar type | Principle / use
  [4:4.0] Doppler | Speed from frequency shift
  [4:4.1] SAR | Imaging at night and through clouds
  [4:4.2] AESA | T/R module on every element; electronic beam, no moving antenna; tracks multiple targets – Uttam, MF-STAR
  [4:4.3] VHF anti-stealth | Long waves pick up stealth aircraft
  [4:4.4] Over-the-horizon | HF waves bounced off the ionosphere
[4:5] - **Uttam AESA** (LRDE): solid-state active phased-array fire-control radar, wide-band RF front end, adaptable to fighters (Tejas Mk-1A); "**not capable of tracking multiple targets**" is the false statement.
[4:6] # Indigenous radars
[4:7] TABLE head: Radar | Role
  [4:7.0] Swathi | Weapon-locating radar (guns, mortars, rockets); BEL; exported to Armenia
  [4:7.1] Surya | VHF radar that tracks stealth aircraft
  [4:7.2] Swordfish | BMD long-range tracking (Green Pine based)
  [4:7.3] Arudhra | IAF medium-power 4D surveillance radar
  [4:7.4] Ashwini | Low-level lightweight AESA radar (cruise missiles, drones)
  [4:7.5] Rajendra | Fire-control radar of Akash
  [4:7.6] INDRA | Low-level surveillance (Indian Doppler Radar)
[4:8] # AWACS vs AEW&C
[4:9] TABLE head: AWACS | AEW&C (Netra)
  [4:9.0] Large aircraft (Il-76 Phalcon), long range, full battle management | Smaller jet (Embraer EMB-145), early warning + limited control; DRDO-CABS Bengaluru
[4:10] # Stealth
[4:11] - **Stealth technology:** **absorbing / deflecting radar waves** – very small **radar cross-section (RCS)** + **Radar Absorbing Material**; not "invisible to the eye".
[4:12] - Stealth objects **can** be detected at specific (low, VHF/UHF) frequencies; metamaterials are used to **reduce**, not increase, scattering (UPSC 2026: **1 and 2 only**).
[4:13] - **IIT Kharagpur:** low-cost **radar-absorbing metamaterial** to cloak soldiers and equipment from enemy radar.
