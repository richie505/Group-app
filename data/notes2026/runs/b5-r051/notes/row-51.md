=== Tejas and Other Indigenous Aircraft
# Tejas LCA
- **What:** India's indigenous **Light Combat Aircraft (LCA)**, a single-engine, **compound delta-wing, tailless** multirole **supersonic** fighter (Mach 1.6+); smallest and lightest supersonic multirole fighter of its class; digital **fly-by-wire** – India's **first indigenous fly-by-wire aircraft**; over 40% composites.
- **Who:** **designed by ADA** (Aeronautical Development Agency, DRDO framework), **built by HAL**; named by **Atal Bihari Vajpayee**; HAL handed the first two to the IAF in 2016 (No. 45 Sqn "Flying Daggers").
- **Variants:** Mk-1 (4th gen) → **Mk-1A** (4.5 gen; **Uttam AESA**, EW suite, air-to-air refuelling) → **Mk-2** (**GE F414** engine, canards); naval LCA and trainer.
- **Orders:** **25 Sep 2025** HAL contract for **97 Mk-1A** (68 fighters + 29 twin-seaters), Rs 62,370 crore, 64%+ indigenous; earlier order of **83**.
- **"First indigenous fighter":** exam keys say **Tejas**; strictly Tejas is India's **second** indigenous jet fighter – the first was **HAL HF-24 Marut** (1961).
- **Distractors:** Akash = missile, Vikrant = carrier, Arjun = tank, Garud/Surya Kiran (aerobatic team)/Sukhoi are not the LCA.

=== Radar, Doppler Effect and Stealth
# RADAR and the Doppler effect
- **RADAR** (Radio Detection And Ranging): **detects and fixes the position** (range, direction, speed) of objects with radio waves.
- **Doppler effect:** apparent change in frequency from relative motion – source **approaching → higher** pitch; **receding → lower** (a train moving away sounds lower).
- **Uses:** **police speed radar guns**, **satellite tracking**, medical ultrasound (blood-flow velocity), **Doppler Weather Radar** (cyclones, **heavy rainfall and thunderstorms**), **expanding universe** (red shift = moving away, Hubble).
| Radar type | Principle / use |
|---|---|
| Doppler | Speed from frequency shift |
| SAR | Imaging at night and through clouds |
| **AESA** | T/R module on every element; electronic beam, no moving antenna; **tracks multiple targets** – Uttam, MF-STAR |
| VHF anti-stealth | Long waves pick up stealth aircraft |
| Over-the-horizon | HF waves bounced off the ionosphere |
- **Uttam AESA** (LRDE): solid-state active phased-array fire-control radar, wide-band RF front end, adaptable to fighters (Tejas Mk-1A); "**not capable of tracking multiple targets**" is the false statement.

# Stealth
- **Stealth technology:** **absorbing / deflecting radar waves** – very small **radar cross-section (RCS)** + **Radar Absorbing Material**; not "invisible to the eye".
- Stealth objects **can** be detected at specific (low, VHF/UHF) frequencies; metamaterials are used to **reduce**, not increase, scattering (UPSC 2026: **1 and 2 only**).
- **IIT Kharagpur:** low-cost **radar-absorbing metamaterial** to cloak soldiers and equipment from enemy radar.
