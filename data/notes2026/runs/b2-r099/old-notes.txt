## SUB 1 | Reserve Bank of India
[99:1:0] # Functions
[99:1:1] - **Monetary authority**; banker and debt manager to government.
[99:1:2] - Issues currency **except the ₹1 note and coins** (issued by the **Ministry of Finance**).
[99:1:3] - Regulator and supervisor of **banks and NBFCs**.
[99:1:4] - **Forex manager** under **FEMA 1999**.
[99:1:5] - **Public Debt Management Cell**: set up to reduce RBI's conflict of interest between monetary policy and debt management.
[99:1:6] # Recent RBI Actions
[99:1:7] - **May 2025**: regulation-making framework – transparency, consultation, periodic review.
[99:1:8] - **2026-27**: **17 Upper-Layer NBFCs** named, including **Tata Sons**; must list within **3 years**.
## SUB 2 | Monetary Policy Committee and Inflation Targeting
[99:2:0] # MPC Composition
[99:2:1] TABLE head: Feature | Fact
  [99:2:1.0] Basis | Statutory, under the RBI Act, 1934
  [99:2:1.1] Size | 6 members
  [99:2:1.2] From RBI | 3 – Governor (chair, casting vote) + a Deputy Governor + one officer
  [99:2:1.3] External | 3, appointed by the Centre for 4 years
  [99:2:1.4] Job | Fixes the repo rate
[99:2:2] # Inflation-Targeting Mandate
[99:2:3] - Recommended by the **Urjit Patel Committee (2014)**; adopted **2016**.
[99:2:4] - Target **4% CPI-Combined ± 2%** (CPI, not WPI); nominal anchor headline CPI (base year 2024).
[99:2:5] - **Price stability** is primary; growth secondary.
[99:2:6] - **Sec. 45ZA**: Centre, in consultation with RBI, sets the target **every 5 years**.
[99:2:7] - **Sec. 45ZN**: band breached for **3 consecutive quarters** → RBI reports to the Centre with reasons, remedies and time to restore.
## SUB 3 | Sebi – Mandate and Powers
[99:3:0] - Regulates the **securities market, stock exchanges, listed companies, intermediaries** and **mutual funds**.
[99:3:1] - Replaced the **Controller of Capital Issues**.
[99:3:2] - **Quasi-judicial** powers: investigates, summons, passes orders and penalties.
[99:3:3] # Appeal Route
[99:3:4] - 1. SEBI order
[99:3:5] - 2. **Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT)**
[99:3:6] - 3. **Supreme Court**
[99:3:7] - IRDAI and PFRDA orders are also appealable to SAT **since 2014**.
[99:3:8] - **Sep 2025**: **SWAGAT-FI** – single-window framework for trusted **FPIs and FVCIs**, with the India Market Access Portal.
## SUB 4 | Irdai – Insurance Regulator
[99:4:0] # Basis and Origin
[99:4:1] - Statutory body under the **IRDA Act, 1999**; apex regulator of **insurance and reinsurance** companies.
[99:4:2] - Recommended by the **Malhotra Committee** (set up 1993, reported 1994) – private entry into insurance.
[99:4:3] - **Section 114A, Insurance Act 1938**: IRDAI's regulation-making power.
[99:4:4] - **Feb 2025**: seven-member expert panel under **Dinesh Khara** set up to review IRDAI regulations.
[99:4:5] "Which is NOT correct about IRDAI": it was recommended by **Malhotra**, not the **Gadgil** Committee.
[99:4:6] # Insurance FDI Cap

  [99:4:7.1] 2015 | 49%
  [99:4:7.2] 2021 | 74%
  [99:4:7.3] 2025-26 | 100%
[99:4:8] - **Sabka Bima Sabki Raksha (Amendment of Insurance Laws) Act, 2025**, passed **17 Dec 2025**: up to 100% FDI; added **Sec. 16A** to the IRDA Act – **Policyholders' Education and Protection Fund**.
[99:4:9] # Recent IRDAI Actions
[99:4:10] - **Ind AS 117 and Ind AS 109** replace IGAAP for insurers; IRDAI enforces adoption.
[99:4:11] - **D-SIIs, FY 2025-26**: **LIC, GIC Re, New India Assurance**.
[99:4:12] HDFC Bank is a **D-SIB** (bank), not a D-SII (insurer).
## SUB 5 | Pfrda, Ibbi and Ifsca

[99:5:1] - **IBBI**: regulator under the IBC 2016.
[99:5:2] - **BIFR** (SICA 1985): dissolved **1 Dec 2016**; cases moved to **NCLT/NCLAT** under the IBC.

## SUB 8 | Fsdc and Fslrc
[99:8:0] # Financial Stability and Development Council
[99:8:1] - Set up **2010**; **non-statutory** apex body; chaired by the **Union Finance Minister** (not RBI Governor). Sub-committee chaired by the RBI Governor.
[99:8:2] - Roles: financial stability, **inter-regulatory coordination**, macro-prudential supervision of large conglomerates, financial literacy and inclusion.
[99:8:3] # Financial Sector Legislative Reforms Commission
[99:8:4] - Chair **Justice B.N. Srikrishna**; set up 2011, report **2013**.
[99:8:5] - Proposals: single **Indian Financial Code**; **Unified Financial Agency** (merging SEBI, FMC, IRDA, PFRDA); RBI keeps banking and payments; Financial Redress Agency; **Public Debt Management Agency**; Resolution Corporation; SAT expanded into a Financial Sector Appellate Tribunal.
[99:8:6] FSLRC did NOT propose a separate constitutional court for financial disputes.
[99:8:7] # Current Debate
[99:8:8] - **Economic Survey 2025-26** ("Refining the Regulatory Touch"): RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA moving to **anticipatory regulation**.
[99:8:9] - Criticism: overlapping mandates; a decade of inflation targeting with mixed evidence on output and jobs.
[99:8:10] - AP angle: IRDAI HQ Hyderabad was in the shared AP–Telangana capital until 2024.

=== NOTES 2:101 | Quasi-judicial Bodies – National Green Tribunal, Nclt/nclat, Cat, Sat, Consumer Commissions
## SUB 0 | Constitutional Basis – Articles 323A and 323B
[101:0:0] - **Part XIVA** (Articles **323A and 323B**) was inserted by the **42nd Amendment, 1976**, in force from **1 March 1977**.
[101:0:1] TABLE head: Feature | Article 323A | Article 323B
  [101:0:1.0] Subject | Administrative tribunals – public service matters | Other matters
  [101:0:1.1] Who can create | Parliament only | Parliament and State Legislatures
  [101:0:1.2] Example | CAT, State Administrative Tribunals | Tax, land reform, rent tribunals
[101:0:2] # Full List of Article 323B Subjects
[101:0:3] - 1. Taxation
[101:0:4] - 2. Foreign exchange
[101:0:5] - 3. Industrial and labour disputes
[101:0:6] - 4. Land reforms
[101:0:7] - 5. Ceiling on urban property
[101:0:8] - 6. Elections to Parliament / State Legislature (except Articles 329 and 329A matters)
[101:0:9] - 7. Foodstuffs
[101:0:10] - 8. Rent and tenancy rights
## SUB 1 | National Green Tribunal
[101:1:0] # Basic Facts
[101:1:1] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [101:1:1.0] Parent Act | National Green Tribunal Act, 2010
  [101:1:1.1] Operational from | 18 October 2010
  [101:1:1.2] Principal bench | New Delhi
  [101:1:1.3] Zonal benches | Bhopal (Central), Pune (West), Kolkata (East), Chennai (South)
  [101:1:1.4] Civil jurisdiction | 7 Acts in Schedule I
  [101:1:1.5] Disposal mandate | Within 6 months of filing
  [101:1:1.6] Appeal | Directly to the Supreme Court within 90 days
[101:1:2] - Schedule I includes the **Water Act, 1974** and the **Water Cess Act, 1977**.
[101:1:3] - Funded by **MoEFCC**; criticism: NGT has **no IT division**.
[101:1:4] # Landmark NGT Orders
[101:1:5] TABLE head: Order | Issue
  [101:1:5.0] Ban on rat-hole mining, Meghalaya (2014) | Unscientific, unsafe coal mining (APPSC 2024)
  [101:1:5.1] Ban on diesel vehicles older than 10 years, Delhi-NCR (2015) | Air pollution
  [101:1:5.2] Art of Living fined (2016) | World Culture Festival damaged the Yamuna floodplain
  [101:1:5.3] BRO to plant at least 10,000 trees in Uttarakhand within two months (Aug 2023) | Tree felling for road works
[101:1:6] - **Constitutional link:** the NGT Act's preamble says the right to a healthy environment is part of the **right to life under Article 21**; it also cites the **Rio de Janeiro (1992)** Earth Summit.
[101:1:7] - **Composition (Sec. 4):** a full-time **Chairperson**, **10 to 20 Judicial Members** and **10 to 20 Expert Members**; term **5 years**, no reappointment.
[101:1:8] - **Schedule I (7 Acts):** Water Act 1974, Water Cess Act 1977, Forest (Conservation) Act 1980, Air Act 1981, Environment (Protection) Act 1986, Public Liability Insurance Act 1991, Biological Diversity Act 2002. The **Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972** is **not** included.
## SUB 2 | Nclt and Nclat
[101:2:0] - **NCLT** – Sec. **408**, **NCLAT** – Sec. **410** of the **Companies Act, 2013**; both from **1 June 2016**.
[101:2:1] - NCLT is the **adjudicating authority** for companies under the **IBC**; IBC appeals go to NCLAT (from 1 Dec 2016) under **Sec. 61** within **30 days** (+15 days on cause).
[101:2:2] - Companies Act appeals to NCLAT: within **45 days** (Sec. 421).
[101:2:3] - **BIFR** cases moved to NCLT/NCLAT on its dissolution (1 Dec 2016).
[101:2:4] TABLE head: NCLAT hears appeals from | Since
  [101:2:4.0] NCLT | 2016
  [101:2:4.1] IBBI | —
  [101:2:4.2] CCI | 26 May 2017 (replaced COMPAT)
  [101:2:4.3] NFRA | —
[101:2:5] - Further appeal: **Supreme Court**.
## SUB 3 | Central Administrative Tribunal
[101:3:0] - Set up under the **Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985**, made under **Article 323A(1)**.
[101:3:1] - Jurisdiction: recruitment and service matters of **Central government employees**.
[101:3:2] - **L. Chandra Kumar v. Union of India (1997)**: clauses 323A(2)(d) and 323B(3)(d) excluding High Courts were struck down; judicial review under **Articles 226/227** is **basic structure**.
[101:3:3] - Appeal route after 1997: CAT order → **Division Bench of the High Court** → Supreme Court.
## SUB 4 | Other Appellate Tribunals

  [101:4:0.2] APTEL (24 Aug 2004) | CERC, SERCs, PNGRB | Supreme Court
[101:4:1] SAT does **not** hear CCI appeals; CCI appeals go to **NCLAT**.
[101:4:2] - **SAT – full form:** SAT is the **Securities Appellate Tribunal**, set up under the **SEBI Act, 1992**; it hears appeals against orders of SEBI, IRDAI and PFRDA.

## SUB 6 | Tribunals Reforms Act 2021 and Madras Bar Association
[101:6:0] - **Tribunals Reforms Act, 2021** dissolved several appellate bodies and moved work to **High Courts** / commercial courts:
[101:6:1]   - Appellate Tribunal under the **Cinematograph Act, 1952** (film certification) → High Court
[101:6:2]   - **Airport Appellate Tribunal** (AAI Act)
[101:6:3]   - **Authority for Advance Rulings** (Customs Act) → High Court
[101:6:4]   - Appellate Boards under **Trade Marks, Copyright, Patents** Acts (IPAB) → High Court / Commercial Court
[101:6:5] - On abolition, members ceased office; pending cases shifted to courts.
[101:6:6] - **19 Nov 2025 – Madras Bar Association v. Union of India** (CJI **B.R. Gavai**): struck down key provisions that re-enacted rules already quashed; Centre to set up a **National Tribunals Commission within four months**.
[101:6:7] - Principles of **Madras Bar Association-4 (2020)** and **-5 (2021)** continue to govern appointment, tenure and service of members.
