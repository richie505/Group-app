## SUB 0 | IRDAI – Insurance Regulator and Insurance Reforms
[0:0] - **IRDAI** = statutory body under the **IRDA Act, 1999**; set up **April 2000**; apex regulator of **insurance and reinsurance** (not SEBI, RBI, GIC or DFHI). HQ **Hyderabad**.
[0:1] - Recommended by the **Malhotra Committee** (R.N. Malhotra; set up **1993**, reported **1994**) – private entry into insurance and an independent regulator. Not the Gadgil, Dandekar, Khusro or Rangarajan Committee.
[0:2] - **Section 114A, Insurance Act 1938** = IRDAI's regulation-making power.
[0:3] - **Feb 2025**: seven-member expert panel under **Dinesh Khara** (ex-SBI chairman) set up to review IRDAI regulations (not Shaktikanta Das, Debasish Panda or Urjit Patel).

  [0:4.4] Dandekar / Khusro | Poverty line / agricultural credit

## SUB 2 | SEBI and Market Regulation
[2:0] - **SEBI** set up **12 April 1988** by resolution (non-statutory); **statutory on 12 April 1992** (SEBI Act, 1992). Replaced the **Controller of Capital Issues**.
[2:1] - Regulates the **securities market, stock exchanges, listed companies, intermediaries** (brokers, merchant banks, VC funds) and **mutual funds**.
[2:2] - **Quasi-judicial** powers: investigates, summons, orders, penalties. Appeal: SEBI → **Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT)** → **Supreme Court** (not NCLAT); IRDAI and PFRDA orders also go to SAT (since 2014).
[2:3] - **Forward Markets Commission** (commodities regulator, 1953) **merged into SEBI, Sep 2015** – commodity exchanges now under SEBI.
[2:4] - **World's first diamond futures** contracts began on India's **ICEX (Indian Commodity Exchange, Mumbai)** on **28 August 2017** – answer "**India**", not Belgium, South Africa or USA.
[2:5] - **Sep 2025**: **SWAGAT-FI** – single-window framework for trusted **FPIs and FVCIs**.
[2:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy P1 - APPSC 04 p.32; Rocket Indian Economy p.14; app notes 2:99 (SEBI); ICEX diamond futures – app explanation, general knowledge (not checked)
## SUB 3 | RBI, the MPC and Payment Codes
[3:0] - RBI: **monetary authority**, banker and debt manager to government, regulator of **banks and NBFCs**, **forex manager** under **FEMA 1999**. Issues currency **except the ₹1 note and coins** (**Ministry of Finance**).
[3:1] - **Public Debt Management Cell**: to reduce RBI's conflict of interest between monetary policy and debt management.
[3:2] - **IFSC (Indian Financial System Code)**: **11 characters** – first **4 letters** = bank code, 5th = **0**, last **6** = branch code; assigned by RBI to each branch for NEFT/RTGS.
[3:3] - **2026-27**: **17 Upper-Layer NBFCs** named (incl. **Tata Sons**); must list within **3 years**.
[3:4] TABLE head: MPC feature | Fact
  [3:4.0] Basis | Statutory, RBI Act 1934 (amended 2016)
  [3:4.1] Members | 6 – 3 RBI (Governor chairs, casting vote) + 3 external for 4 years
  [3:4.2] Target | 4% CPI-Combined ± 2% (not WPI); price stability primary; base year of CPI 2024
  [3:4.3] Origin | Urjit Patel Committee (2014); adopted 2016
  [3:4.4] Sec. 45ZA | Centre (with RBI) sets target every 5 years
  [3:4.5] Sec. 45ZN | Band breached 3 consecutive quarters → RBI reports reasons to Centre

## SUB 4 | IBBI, IBC, PFRDA and the Companies Act 2013
[4:0] - **Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016**: President's assent **28 May 2016** (not September); **IBBI** established **1 Oct 2016** (Sec. 188).
[4:1] - **IBBI Board (Sec. 189)**: Chairperson + **3 ex officio** Central Government officers + **1 RBI nominee** + **5 other** members (at least 3 whole-time) – **not 13 members**.
[4:2] - **BIFR** (SICA 1985) dissolved **1 Dec 2016**; cases moved to **NCLT/NCLAT**.
[4:3] - **PFRDA**: pension funds and **NPS**; **NPS e-Shramik** for informal workers.
[4:4] - **Companies Act, 2013**: **CSR** (Sec. 135), **One Person Company** (new entrepreneurs), e-filing/**MCA21** (IT use); shift from government approval to **self-regulation with disclosure** – so "the Act **regulates** the corporate sector" is the statement keyed as not correct (CDS 2015).
[4:5] ~~ Sources: ibbi.gov.in/en/about (IBBI est. 1 Oct 2016); web search: IBC assent 28.05.2016 and IBBI Sec. 189 composition (ibbi.gov.in publication); examrobot.com CDS-II 2015 Q101
## SUB 5 | FSDC and FSLRC
[5:0] - **FSDC** (Financial Stability and Development Council), **2010**: **non-statutory** apex body (executive order), **not an organ of NITI Aayog**; chaired by the **Union Finance Minister** (not RBI Governor); sub-committee chaired by the **RBI Governor**. Proposed by the **Raghuram Rajan Committee (2008)**.
[5:1] - Roles: financial stability, **inter-regulatory coordination**, **macro-prudential supervision**, financial literacy and inclusion – not fixing the repo rate.
[5:2] - **FSLRC**: **Justice B.N. Srikrishna**; set up 2011, report **2013** – **Indian Financial Code**; **Unified Financial Agency** (SEBI + FMC + IRDA + **PFRDA**); Financial Redress Agency; Public Debt Management Agency; SAT → Financial Sector Appellate Tribunal. No constitutional court for financial disputes.
[5:3] - **Economic Survey 2025-26**: RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA moving to **anticipatory regulation**.
[5:4] ~~ Sources: Rocket Indian Economy p.87 (FSDC – chairman Union Finance Minister); app notes 2:99 (FSDC, FSLRC)
