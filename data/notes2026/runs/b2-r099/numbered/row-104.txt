## SUB 0 | Meaning of Globalisation and Cultural Change
[0:0] - **Globalisation**: free flow of **goods, services, capital (investment), technology, ideas and people** across borders – "worldwide interconnectedness"; flows of ideas, capital, commodities and people.
[0:1] - Intended outcomes: reduced trade barriers, free flow of capital and labour – **not** the flow of **classified military technology**.
[0:2] - Essence (UPSC Astt. Comm. 2017): the coming together of **worldwide social relations bypassing territories** and **thinking transterritorially about global risks** – not mere inter-state relations or suprastate governance.
[0:3] - **Global village** (Marshall McLuhan) rests on **transport and communication** development.
[0:4] - Culture and tradition are said to disappear under **globalisation** (homogenisation), though local culture does not fully end.
[0:5] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [0:5.0] Diffusion | Spread of cultural traits from one society to another
  [0:5.1] Acculturation | Mutual exchange; both change, each stays distinct
  [0:5.2] Assimilation | Minority absorbed; loses identity
  [0:5.3] Glocalisation | Global products adapted to local tastes
  [0:5.4] Homogenisation | Convergence to one (Western) pattern
  [0:5.5] Hybridisation | Blend of global and local
  [0:5.6] McDonaldisation | Efficiency, calculability, predictability, control
[0:6] - **Economic Survey 2025-26**: "hyper-globalisation" → **"geostrategic globalisation"**.

## SUB 1 | Anti-globalisation Critique and Social Forums
[1:0] - **Vandana Shiva**: founder of **Navdanya** – seed, food, water, land and knowledge sovereignty; against biopiracy.
[1:1] - **Seattle, 1999**: protests at the WTO Ministerial. **World Social Forum**: first **Porto Alegre, Brazil, 2001**; **Mumbai 2004**.
[1:2] - **Asian Social Forum**: **Hyderabad, 2-7 January 2003**. **European Social Forum**: first held in **Florence, November 2002**.
[1:3] - **Samir Amin** (Egyptian economist) directed the **Third World Forum, Dakar (Senegal)** from 1980; chaired the World Forum for Alternatives.
[1:4] - **Joseph Stiglitz**, *Globalization and Its Discontents* (**2002**) – criticised **IMF** conditions. **Sharad Joshi** supported globalisation; **Tikait**, **Nanjundaswamy** opposed it.
[1:5] - Criticism: widens rich-poor gaps; "commercial imperialism"; left and right critiques.

## SUB 2 | Labour and Migration
[2:0] TABLE head: Concept | Scholar
  [2:0.0] Informal sector (1971-73) | Keith Hart
  [2:0.1] Footloose labour – migrant, circular, casual (*Footloose Labour*, 1996) | Jan Breman
[2:1] - **Gig workers** (NITI Aayog 2022): **77 lakh** (2020-21) → **2.35 crore** by 2029-30.
[2:2] - **World Migration Report 2026** (**IOM**, biennial since 2000): **304 million** migrants (**3.7%**); **India = largest diaspora**; India-UAE corridor **5th**, India-US **6th**.
[2:3] - **India = top remittance recipient**, about **$129 billion (2024)**; Gulf labour = backbone of the **remittance economy**; remittances keep the CAD steady despite the **trade deficit**.
[2:4] - AP: **East and West Godavari, Kadapa** – Gulf emigration; **APNRTS** monitored about **10 lakh** in the Gulf (Mar 2026). **PLFS 2020-21**: migrants **28.9%**; rural migrants 48% of women vs 5.9% of men. **MEA**: Migration and Mobility Partnerships; Pre-Departure Orientation Training.

## SUB 3 | MNCs and Impact on Groups
[3:0] - Negative effects of MNCs: **large dividend remittances**, **crowding out domestic saving and investment**, **influence in key sectors**, layoffs of artisans and domestic firms. **Fulfilling export obligations** is **not** a negative effect.
[3:1] TABLE head: Group | Effect
  [3:1.0] Consumers / youth | More choice, global brands, cultural fusion; risk of mindless aping
  [3:1.1] Farmers | WTO-era debt-linked suicides – Maharashtra and AP
  [3:1.2] Small coffee growers | 98% small; face Brazil, Vietnam
  [3:1.3] Women | Self-employed women nearly tripled since 2017-18
  [3:1.4] Youth | 29% of tertiary-educated unemployed (2023-24)
[3:2] ~~ Sources: NCERT World History part 36 p.16 (farmers, local businesses vs MNCs); app notes 2:104 (impact on groups)
