## SUB 0 | Pressure Groups – Meaning, Types and Techniques
[0:0] - **Pressure group**: an **organised group** that seeks to influence public policy **without contesting elections or seeking office**; also called **interest groups, lobby groups**; labelled the **"Anonymous Empire"**.
[0:1] - Test: seeks to form the government → political party. **Members of a trade union** = pressure group; members of the Lok Sabha, a panchayat or the Cabinet are not (MPPSC 2017). The **Election Commission** is a constitutional authority, not a pressure group.
[0:2] - **Almond and Powell**: **institutional, associational, non-associational, anomic**.
[0:3] TABLE head: Type | Indian examples
  [0:3.0] Business – most powerful and largest | FICCI, CII, ASSOCHAM – work for commerce and industry interests
  [0:3.1] Trade union | AITUC, INTUC, CITU, BMS, HMS
  [0:3.2] Agrarian | Bharatiya Kisan Union, All India Kisan Sabha (oldest), Shetkari Sanghatana (Maharashtra); Swabhimani Shetkari Sanghatana = farmers' union, Maharashtra, founder Raju Shetti
  [0:3.3] Professional | Indian Medical Association, Bar associations
  [0:3.4] Women / feminist, caste, religious, student | Feminist groups are organised social (interest) groups pressing for women's rights

## SUB 1 | Media, Press Freedom and the Press Council of India
[1:0] - **Fourth Estate** = **the Press** (media) – watchdog and **agenda-setter**: media sets the public agenda **because** it selectively filters news (A and R true, R explains A).
[1:1] - **Freedom of the press** is not named in the Constitution; it is read into **Article 19(1)(a)** (Art. 19), not Arts. 14, 21 or 32.
[1:2] - **Press Council of India**: first set up **1966** (Press Council Act, 1965); now under the **Press Council Act, 1978**; **statutory, quasi-judicial**; **Chairman + 28 members**; **print media only**; can warn, admonish, censure – no punitive powers. Chairman by custom a **retired SC judge**, chosen by the RS Chairman, LS Speaker and a PCI member.

  [1:3.0] Markandey Katju | 2011-2014
  [1:3.1] Justice C.K. (Chandramauli Kumar) Prasad | 2014-2022
  [1:3.2] Justice Ranjana Prakash Desai | 17 Jun 2022 – Dec 2025; again from 24 April 2026 (three years) – current, as of Nov 2026
[1:4] - Problems: paid news, fake news, concentrated ownership. RSF index (May 2026): India **"very serious"**.

## SUB 2 | SHGs and Women's Microfinance – DWCRA, SERP, Velugu
[2:0] - SHGs in AP are popularly called **DWCRA groups**; a **self-help group** has **10 to 20 members** (NABARD / national norm).
[2:1] - **Velugu** (AP District Poverty Initiatives Project) was launched in **2000** to organise and federate women's SHGs; run by **SERP** (autonomous society of the Rural Development Dept); renamed **Indira Kranthi Patham** (Velugu + DWCRA merged) under Congress; Velugu name restored after 2014.
[2:2] - **SAPAP** (1995, UNDP): base for Velugu. **Pavala Vaddi** (2004) for SHGs. **Mana Kirana**: general stores run by DWCRA groups supplying quality goods.
[2:3] - **MEPMA** (urban): G.O. Ms 414 of 4 Jun 2007, launched **1 Sep 2007**; SHG → Slum Level Federation → Town Level Federation.

## SUB 4 | NGOs, CBOs and Social Service
[4:0] - NGOs are to be treated as **partners of government in development** (National Policy on the Voluntary Sector, **2007**, Planning Commission: partnership, not subordination). The APPSC key printed "**people's representatives**".
[4:1] - **Social service** is provided by **both government and private individuals**, profit and non-profit organisations.
[4:2] - **Community Based Organisations (CBOs)**: non-profit groups that **extend social services locally**.
[4:3] - Civil society = the **"third sector"** beside State and market; shift from welfare delivery to **rights, advocacy and watchdog** work.
[4:4] TABLE head: Legal form | Law
  [4:4.0] Society | Societies Registration Act, 1860
  [4:4.1] Private trust | Indian Trusts Act, 1882
  [4:4.2] Public charitable trust | State laws (e.g. Bombay Public Trusts Act, 1950)
  [4:4.3] Not-for-profit company | Section 8, Companies Act, 2013
[4:5] - **Bhagavatula Charitable Trust** (Yellamanchili, Visakhapatnam) – founded by **B.V. Parameswara Rao**.
[4:6] - **Amaravati farmers' agitation**: Dec 2019 – June 2024 (**1,631 days**), led by **Amaravati Parirakshana Samithi**.
