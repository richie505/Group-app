=== IRDAI – Insurance Regulator and Insurance Reforms
- **IRDAI** = statutory body under the **IRDA Act, 1999**; set up **April 2000**; apex regulator of **insurance and reinsurance** (not SEBI, RBI, GIC or DFHI). HQ **Hyderabad**.
- Recommended by the **Malhotra Committee** (R.N. Malhotra; set up **1993**, reported **1994**) – private entry into insurance and an independent regulator. Not the Gadgil, Dandekar, Khusro or Rangarajan Committee.
- **Section 114A, Insurance Act 1938** = IRDAI's regulation-making power.
- **Feb 2025**: seven-member expert panel under **Dinesh Khara** (ex-SBI chairman) set up to review IRDAI regulations (not Shaktikanta Das, Debasish Panda or Urjit Patel).
| Committee | Subject |
|---|---|
| **Malhotra (1993-94)** | **Insurance** sector reforms – not civil services |
| Hota (2004) | Civil service reforms |
| Alagh (2001) | Civil services examination |
| Surinder Nath (2003) | Civil services – appraisal and postings |
| Dandekar / Khusro | Poverty line / agricultural credit |

=== SEBI and Market Regulation
- **SEBI** set up **12 April 1988** by resolution (non-statutory); **statutory on 12 April 1992** (SEBI Act, 1992). Replaced the **Controller of Capital Issues**.
- Regulates the **securities market, stock exchanges, listed companies, intermediaries** (brokers, merchant banks, VC funds) and **mutual funds**.
- **Quasi-judicial** powers: investigates, summons, orders, penalties. Appeal: SEBI → **Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT)** → **Supreme Court** (not NCLAT); IRDAI and PFRDA orders also go to SAT (since 2014).
- **Forward Markets Commission** (commodities regulator, 1953) **merged into SEBI, Sep 2015** – commodity exchanges now under SEBI.
- **World's first diamond futures** contracts began on India's **ICEX (Indian Commodity Exchange, Mumbai)** on **28 August 2017** – answer "**India**", not Belgium, South Africa or USA.
- **Sep 2025**: **SWAGAT-FI** – single-window framework for trusted **FPIs and FVCIs**.

=== RBI, the MPC and Payment Codes
- RBI: **monetary authority**, banker and debt manager to government, regulator of **banks and NBFCs**, **forex manager** under **FEMA 1999**. Issues currency **except the ₹1 note and coins** (**Ministry of Finance**).
- **Public Debt Management Cell**: to reduce RBI's conflict of interest between monetary policy and debt management.
- **IFSC (Indian Financial System Code)**: **11 characters** – first **4 letters** = bank code, 5th = **0**, last **6** = branch code; assigned by RBI to each branch for NEFT/RTGS.
- **2026-27**: **17 Upper-Layer NBFCs** named (incl. **Tata Sons**); must list within **3 years**.
| MPC feature | Fact |
|---|---|
| Basis | Statutory, RBI Act 1934 (amended 2016) |
| Members | **6** – 3 RBI (Governor chairs, casting vote) + **3 external** for **4 years** |
| Target | **4% CPI-Combined ± 2%** (not WPI); price stability primary; base year of CPI **2024** |
| Origin | **Urjit Patel Committee (2014)**; adopted **2016** |
| Sec. 45ZA | Centre (with RBI) sets target **every 5 years** |
| Sec. 45ZN | Band breached **3 consecutive quarters** → RBI reports reasons to Centre |

=== IBBI, IBC, PFRDA and the Companies Act 2013
- **Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016**: President's assent **28 May 2016** (not September); **IBBI** established **1 Oct 2016** (Sec. 188).
- **IBBI Board (Sec. 189)**: Chairperson + **3 ex officio** Central Government officers + **1 RBI nominee** + **5 other** members (at least 3 whole-time) – **not 13 members**.
- **BIFR** (SICA 1985) dissolved **1 Dec 2016**; cases moved to **NCLT/NCLAT**.
- **PFRDA**: pension funds and **NPS**; **NPS e-Shramik** for informal workers.
- **Companies Act, 2013**: **CSR** (Sec. 135), **One Person Company** (new entrepreneurs), e-filing/**MCA21** (IT use); shift from government approval to **self-regulation with disclosure** – so "the Act **regulates** the corporate sector" is the statement keyed as not correct (CDS 2015).

=== FSDC and FSLRC
- **FSDC** (Financial Stability and Development Council), **2010**: **non-statutory** apex body (executive order), **not an organ of NITI Aayog**; chaired by the **Union Finance Minister** (not RBI Governor); sub-committee chaired by the **RBI Governor**. Proposed by the **Raghuram Rajan Committee (2008)**.
- Roles: financial stability, **inter-regulatory coordination**, **macro-prudential supervision**, financial literacy and inclusion – not fixing the repo rate.
- **FSLRC**: **Justice B.N. Srikrishna**; set up 2011, report **2013** – **Indian Financial Code**; **Unified Financial Agency** (SEBI + FMC + IRDA + **PFRDA**); Financial Redress Agency; Public Debt Management Agency; SAT → Financial Sector Appellate Tribunal. No constitutional court for financial disputes.
- **Economic Survey 2025-26**: RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA moving to **anticipatory regulation**.
