=== Central Departments – Kandachara, Athavane and the Council
# Departments
- **Rayavachakam** records **nine departments**, each headed by a **Samprati**.
| Department | Function |
|---|---|
| **Kandachara** | **Military** – standing army, headed by the **Senapati / Dalavayi** |
| **Athavane / Attavana** | **Revenue** / finance |
| **Bhandara (Sri Bhandara)** | **Treasury** |
| **Dharmasana** | **Judicial**; Pradvivaka = judge |
- **Kandachara dealt with military affairs / defence** – asked ×7 (APPSC G2 2019; G2 Mains; SI 2016; AP History PYQ ×3; G2 PYQ). Not revenue, finance, land survey (Bhoomi Sisthu), police, justice or religious institutions; **Athavana** = revenue, **Dandaniti** / **Mansabdari** are distractors.
# Officers and the council
| Officer | Role |
|---|---|
| **Mahapradhani / Prime Minister** | Chief minister presiding over the council of pradhanis; also called **Sarva Sirah Pradhani, Sabhanayaka, Tantranayaka**; e.g. **Saluva Timmarusu** |
| **Dalavayi** | Commander-in-chief |
| Rayasams | Secretaries (Kondamarasu, Ayyamarasu) |
| Karanikas | Scribes |
- **President of the imperial council:** the **Prime Minister** (AMVI 2009) – "Sabhanayaka" is only another name of the same office; the king was advised by, not president of, the council.

=== Amara-Nayankara System and the Palegars
# Amaram and Amaranayakas
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Amaram** | Land granted to **military chiefs** instead of cash salary; the Rayas owned all land |
| **Amaranayakas** | Holders; also Nayaks, **Palaiyagars, Poligars** |
| **Mahamandaleshwara** | Their title; more autonomy than Durgadannayakas |
- **Nayakas were:** **military chiefs controlling particular territories** (CDS 2001) – not central ministers, cavalry soldiers or temple priests.
- **Amaranayakas were NOT** "wise men who advised the rulers" – they were **military commanders**, **collected taxes**, and their **contingents** fought for the king.
- **Administrative system (G1 2007):** the **Amaranayaka** (Amara-Nayankara) system – a military governor (Nayaka) given amarams for salary and army expenses.
- Within the amaram: **collected taxes**, kept **law and order**, administered their territory (**palaiyam**).
- **Obligations:** tribute (**Kappam**); a quota of troops, horses, elephants; attend court at **Mahanavami** with gifts; maintain forts, temples, irrigation.
# Kakatiya nayankara vs Vijayanagara amara-nayankara
| Point | Kakatiya nayankara | Vijayanagara amara-nayankara |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | **Prataparudra II** | Grew out of the Kakatiya system |
| Scale | **75 or 77** nayakas | **About 200** nayakas |
| Outcome | Discontent among older feudatories | Semi-independent, then independent **Nayaka states** after 1565 (**Madurai, Tanjore, Keladi**) |
# Palegars (Poligars)
- **Origin:** **amara-nayakas** → Palegars when the centre weakened after **Talikota (1565)**; hereditary; **forerunners of the zamindars**.
- **Who were they:** **territorial administrative and military governors** (UPPSC 2015) – not ordinary zamindars, Mahajans or traders.
- **Duties under the Nayakas:** administer palaiyams, collect taxes, maintain law and order – **NOT building health and education centres**.
- **Thomas Munro**, with **Campbell**, crushed the Rayalaseema Palegars and then introduced the **Ryotwari** system.

=== Revenue and Taxes
- **Chief feature of the financial system:** **land revenue** (BPSC 1999) – usually **1/6** of produce, **1/4 or 1/3** on fertile land; land-tax documents = **Adhuvanatantram**.
# Land classes
| Term | Land |
|---|---|
| Nanja | Wet |
| Punja | Dry |
| **Bagayati** | **Garden** |
| Banjara | Waste |
- **Neerarambamu / Magani** (irrigated): tax in **grain**, higher; **Kaadarambamu / Metta** (dry): cash, lower.
# Tax terms
| Tax | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Navidadere** / Kondaju | **Barbers'** tax |
| **Maneyanna / Manevana** / Illari Pannu | **House** tax |
| **Melivamsam** | Plough tax (G1 match) |
| **Tudimeya** | Marriage tax (G1 match) |
| **Meruka** | Tax on **sale and purchase** (APPSC G2 2011) |
| Putrodaya Kanuka | Birth of a son |
| Kalyana Kanika | Wedding tax on both parties |
| Siddhaya | Fixed levy: Pinje (cotton ginners), Eidipi (toddy tappers), Jati (professions) |
- **G1 match:** Navidadere–barbers, Melivamsam–ploughs, Manevana–houses, Tudimeya–marriage = **a-II, b-III, c-IV, d-I**.
- **Meruka ≠ Mera:** Mera = the Ayagars' harvest share; Merulu = the Talari's grain gifts; Meruka = sale–purchase tax (not Magani/Metta land or export–import).
- **Marriage tax:** first remitted by **Saluva Narasimha**; **totally withdrawn by Krishnadevaraya** (Rocket; NextGen previous papers – APPSC G1 2010). ⚠ Some keys give Sadasiva Raya; Sadasiva's inscriptions (Rama Raya's regency) record the **barbers'** tax remission (at Kandoja's request).
- Market (**santhalu**) tax rights auctioned to **Sunkandarlu**.

=== Justice and Police
- **Pradvivaka:** adviser to the king in the **judiciary**; judge who explained legal texts (APPSC G2 2019; G2 PYQ) – not military, revenue or espionage.
- **Authoritative law book:** **Parasara Madhaviyam** – Madhavacharya's commentary on the Parashara Smriti (with Yajnavalkya) – not Amuktamalyada or the Arthashastra.
- **Himsodbhava** = criminal; **Dhanodbhava** = civil.
- The **Raya** was the supreme judge; ministers like **Timmarusu** ("Dharma Pratipalaka") also tried cases.
- **Punishments:** treason – **death**; theft – **mutilation**; Brahmins – **life imprisonment** instead of death.
# Police
| Level | Police (Kavali system) |
|---|---|
| Village | **Talari** |
| Sthala | **Kavali Daru** |
| Towns | Peta Kavalidaru |
- **Padikaval:** the system in which **police rights were sold** to individuals (AP History PYQ) – not Athavana or Kandachara.

=== Provincial and Village Administration – the Ayagars
# Provincial units
| Unit | Head |
|---|---|
| **Rajya / Mandala** (province) – the **largest** unit | Prince (Sangama princes titled **Odeyar**) or **Durga Dannayaka** |
| Nadu / Seema (district) | **Nadagauda; Parupatyagar** |
| Sthala / Kampana | Reddi, Gauda, Sthalakaranam |
| Grama (village) | **12 Ayagars** |
- **Biggest unit:** **Mandalam** (RPSC 2012) – not Kottam, Nadu or Kurram.
- **Parupatyagar:** the king's officer of the nadu/sima who **collected revenue** and **repaired and maintained forts** (AP History PYQ).
- About **17 rajyams** under **Achyutaraya**. Governors could **mint coins** and levy or remit taxes, and were **frequently transferred**.
| Andhra Rajya | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Udayagiri** | Most important; **Paka and Muliki** districts |
| **Chandragiri** | **Saluva Narasimha** was its governor before 1485 |
| **Penukonda** | Tirumala's headquarters after Talikota |
| Gooty (Gutti) | Fort raised to rajya status |
# Village – the Ayagars
- **12 hereditary Ayagars** per village: Karanam (accountant), Reddi (head), Talari (watchman), Purohita, plus artisans – Kamsali, Kummari, Vadrangi, **Kase (mason / house-builder)**, Kammari, Chakali, Mangali.
- **Pay:** no cash salary – tax-free **Manyam** lands and a harvest share **Mera**.
| Officer | Duties |
|---|---|
| **Karanam** | Accounts, land and crop registers |
| **Reddi** | Village head (**Gauda / Gowda** in Karnataka) and revenue collector |
| **Talari** | Watchman; collected **Merulu** |
| **Begari** | Supervised forced labour (vetti) |
- **Village headman:** **Gowda** (APPSC Jr Asst key) – the Karnataka name of the Andhra **Reddi**; Chowdhary and Parupatyadar are wrong.
- **Brahmins as Karanams:** **Harihara I** followed the **Kakatiya** practice (APPSC Jr Asst) – the Sangamas inherited Kakatiya village administration.

=== Army, Forts and the Nature of the State
# Army
- Two divisions: the **Siddha (standing) army** under the **Dalavayi** (via Kandachara), paid every **4 months**, and the **Amaranayaka** contingents.
- **Bukka I** = first to use **cannon**; **Saluva Narasimha** introduced **Samugaris** (gunmen).
- **Horses** (Arabian, Persian) imported through **Goa, Honnavar, Bhatkal**.
- **Four kinds of forts:** **Giri** (hill), **Sthala**, **Jala**, **Vana** – under **Durga Dannayakas**.
- **Against sieges:** Vijayanagara enclosed **large agricultural tracts within the fortified area** (APPSC G1 2017; NCERT Themes II ch. 7) – granaries were the usual practice elsewhere.
# Nature of the state
- **King:** supreme head and fountain of justice, acting within **Dharma** and with **his ministers'** counsel.
- **Nilakanta Sastri:** "**military administration**"; **Burton Stein:** "**segmentary state**".
# Political maxims in Amuktamalyada
| Area | Maxim |
|---|---|
| Revenue | Bulk of revenue to the **army** |
| Trade | Improve harbours; encourage import of horses, elephants, gems; treat **foreign merchants generously** |
| Welfare | Build tanks; **win over forest tribes** rather than only crush them |
| Forts | **Brahmins** should command forts |
- It refers to the king's **campaigns** and gives political advice, but gives **no genealogy from Bukka**.
