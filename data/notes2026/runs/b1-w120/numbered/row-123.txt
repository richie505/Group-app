## SUB 0 | Hampi – the Capital, its Ruins and its Plan
[0:0] - **Ruins of Vijayanagara = Hampi** (SI 2011; UPPCS 2008; ×5) – not Bijapur, Golconda, Ahmadnagar or Baroda (sultanate / other capitals).
[0:1] - On the **southern bank of the Tungabhadra** (Jharkhand PCS 2023) – not Narmada, Godavari or Bhima; in **Vijayanagara district, Karnataka** (SSC 2019).
[0:2] - "**Group of Monuments at Hampi**" = UNESCO World Heritage Site, **1986**; the **Vittala stone chariot** is on the reverse of the **₹50 note**.
[0:3] - **"Athens of Vijayanagara"** = the **valley area around the Virupaksha temple** (app notes; APPSC) – not Nellore-Chittoor, Bangalore-Kolar-Chittoor or the Pandya country.
[0:4] - **Fields inside the fortifications:** so the city would have **food supply even if invaders blockaded it for months** (NCERT; Paes) – not irrigation, revenue accounts or royal granaries.
[0:5] - **Why so many temples:** to **legitimise royal authority** by association with the deities – the kings ruled on behalf of **Virupaksha** (NCERT).
[0:6] - **Raw material:** the **Deccan plateau** gave easy local access to **stone (granite)** – that made temples like Lepakshi possible.
[0:7] ~~ Sources: NextGen Vijayanagara Kingdom p.1, p.8; NCERT Class 12 Themes in Indian History II ch.7 (fortifications enclosing fields; temples and legitimacy); app notes 1:123 (sub 1, 8)
## SUB 1 | Hampi Temples – Virupaksha, Vitthala, Hazara Rama
[1:0] TABLE head: Temple | Popular name / deity | Builder and facts
  [1:0.0] Virupaksha | Pampapati; Shiva – the kingdom's deity | First and largest temple of Hampi; 7th-century core; enlarged by Lakkana Dandesha under Devaraya II; Krishnadevaraya added the central hall and eastern gopuram (1510); ceiling paintings incl. Vidyaranya in procession
  [1:0.1] Vitthala (Vithalalayam) | Saptaswara Devalayam; Vishnu (Vithoba) | Begun under Devaraya II, expanded by Krishnadevaraya, continued under Achyutaraya; 56 musical ("SaReGaMa") pillars; stone chariot
  [1:0.2] Hazara Rama | Ramachandra temple (APPSC G2 2008) – royal chapel | Krishnadevaraya (NextGen; Rocket; MPPSC 2014) – many art histories date its core to Devaraya I/II; Ramayana friezes
  [1:0.3] Krishna | Bala Krishna | Krishnadevaraya – idol brought from Udayagiri
  [1:0.4] Achyutaraya | Tiruvengalanatha | 1534, set up by Hiriya Tirumalaraja
[1:1] - **Musical pillars:** **Vittala temple, Hampi** (UPSC 2007) – not Belur, Bhadrachalam or Srirangam.
[1:2] - **Virupaksha is at Hampi** (UPSC 2009) – "Virupaksha – Thiruvananthapuram" is the **wrong pair** (APPSC G1 2019 ×2); Kandariya Mahadeva – Khajuraho, Lingaraja – Bhubaneswar, Mahabodhi – Bodh Gaya, Dilwara – Mount Abu, Brihadeswara – Thanjavur are right.
[1:3] - **Kings ruled on behalf of Virupaksha** (RPSC 2012) – not Nataraja, Murugan, Venkatesa, Pampadevi or Vishnu.
[1:4] - **Vidyaranya's portrait:** painted on the ceiling of the **Ranga mandapa of the Virupaksha temple, Hampi** (Vidyaranya in a palanquin procession).
[1:5] - Krishnadevaraya's other Hampi works: **Ugra Narasimha**, Hanuman statue, monolithic Ganesha.
[1:6] - Distractor temples: **Kailasa, Ellora – Rashtrakuta Krishna I**; Hoysaleswara, Halebidu; Chennakesava, Belur – Vishnuvardhana.

## SUB 2 | The Vijayanagara Style – Raya Gopurams, Kalyana Mandapas, Secular Buildings
[2:0] - **Raya gopurams:** towering gateways that dwarf the central shrine – built by **Krishnadevaraya** at Hampi, Tiruvannamalai, Chidambaram, Srirangam, Tirupati (SSC 2001) – not Vidyaranya, Harihara or Rajaraja; he also built the southern Rajagopuram of the **Ekambaranatha temple, Kanchipuram (1509)**.
[2:1] - **Kalyana mandapas** (pillared hall for the divine marriage) = a notable feature of **Vijayanagara** temples (UPSC 2019) – not Chalukya, Chandela or Rashtrakuta.
[2:2] TABLE head: Feature | Meaning
  [2:2.0] Amman shrine | Separate goddess shrine
  [2:2.1] Sahasra Stambha Mandapa | Thousand-pillared hall with composite pillars
  [2:2.2] Ekapaadam | Hoysala-type single pillar in 3-4 sections
[2:3] - **Percy Brown:** the "**matured state of the Dravidian tradition**"; new: **secular buildings in temple premises**, pillared corridors added to older temples.
[2:4] - **Indo-Saracenic / Indo-Islamic building:** **Lotus Mahal** (Chitrangi / Padma Mahal), built by Krishnadevaraya "in Indo-Arabic style" (NextGen) – not the Vitthala or Hazara Rama temples.
[2:5] - Queen's Bath near the Lotus Mahal; **Mahanavami Dibba** (Krishnadevaraya); **Huzur Daftar** – Indo-Persian council chamber with **40 pillars**.
[2:6] - **Music:** **Sangita Suryodayam** by **Bandaru Lakshminarayana** (court musician of Krishnadevaraya) – not Krishnadevaraya, Rama Raya or Ramabhadramba; the **veena** gained prominence.
[2:7] ~~ Sources: NextGen Vijayanagara Kingdom p.5 (Lotus Mahal in Indo-Arabic style), p.33 (Sahasra Stambha Mandapas); Wikipedia "Ekambareswarar Temple" (southern gopuram, Krishnadevaraya 1509, inscriptions); app notes 1:123 (sub 0, 3, 9)
## SUB 3 | Lepakshi – Veerabhadra Temple, Nandi, Hanging Pillar, Paintings
[3:0] - **Veerabhadra (Papanaseswara) temple** on **Kurma Shailam**, near **Hindupur**, **Sri Sathya Sai district** (formerly Anantapur) – kingdom: **Vijayanagara**.
[3:1] - Built **c. 1530-40** by the Penukonda governors **Virupanna and Veeranna**, officers of **Achyuta Deva Raya** – "Penugonda Virupanna's Veerabhadreswara temple" = **Lepakshi** (not Tadipatri, Hampi or Kadiri).
[3:2] - **Three shrines:** **Shiva, Vishnu and Veerabhadra** (APPSC ASO) – not Jagannath, Kedarnath or Somnath; **Virabhadra = the fierce form of Shiva**.
[3:3] - **Hanging pillar** = Lepakshi (APPSC 2019).
[3:4] - **Huge stone bull (monolithic Nandi)** of the Vijayanagara period = **Lepakshi** – largest monolithic Nandi in India; not Tadipatri, Kadiri or Penugonda.
[3:5] - **Naga linga:** a linga under a **seven-hooded** serpent from a single boulder.
[3:6] - **Paintings:** the finest **Vijayanagara-school** murals in Andhra are at **Lepakshi** (×9) – not Srisailam, Tirupati, Tadipatri, Simhachalam, Ahobilam, Pushpagiri, Draksharama, Vontimitta, Kondapalli or Kondavidu.
[3:7]   - Ceilings: **Ramayana and Mahabharata**; walls: **Parvati Parinayamu**, **Tripurasura Samharam**; "pictorial inscriptions".
[3:8]   - Hampi Virupaksha murals: **Dashavatara**, **Girijakalyana**; style – mineral colours on lime plaster, bold outlines, **elongated, energetic figures**; survey – **Stella Kramrisch**.
[3:9] - **Match (APPSC JA):** largest Nandi – Lepakshi; Lord of the Seven Hills – Tirumala; saviour of Prahlada – Ahobilam; Jyotirlinga – Srisailam.
[3:10] - Latest: 8 Jun 2026 – state launch of **Yogandhra-2026** at Lepakshi.
[3:11] ~~ Sources: NextGen previous papers p.10 ("A famous huge stone bull of the Vijayanagar period is located at Lepakshi"), p.15, p.36, p.40; Rocket AP History sheets #6, #24; app notes 1:123 (sub 4, 5)
## SUB 4 | Tadipatri and Other Andhra Temples
[4:0] - **Tadipatri (Anantapur):** art critics rate its **gopurams above those of Hampi's Vitthala temple** (APPSC G2 2019 ×3) – not Ahobilam, Srisailam, Lepakshi or Chandragiri.
[4:1] - **Chintala Venkataramana** (Vishnu) and **Bugga Ramalingeswara** (Shiva) temples = **Tadipatri** (APPSC G1 1990-91, 1994) – patrons the **Pemmasani Nayakas** (Pemmasani Timmanayudu); high-relief sculpture (**Ubattu Shilpalu**) and the painting of **Vatapatra Shayi**.
[4:2] TABLE head: Temple | Place / note
  [4:2.0] Kodandarama, Ontimitta (Vontimitta) | Kadapa; AP's official Sri Rama Navami temple after Bhadrachalam went to Telangana (2014)
  [4:2.1] Yaganti (Uma Maheswara) | Kurnool – now Nandyal district; Agastya, Venkateswara and Veera Brahmam caves; the "growing" Nandi
  [4:2.2] Ahobilam / Kadiri | Narasimha
  [4:2.3] Srikalahasti | Vayu linga; gali gopuram collapsed 2010
  [4:2.4] Srisailam | Mallikarjuna; four gateways; mukha mandapam – Harihara II; sculptured prakaram – Shanti Lingayya
  [4:2.5] Tirumala | Kalyana mandapam and bronze statues of the king with his queens – Krishnadevaraya; steps and Govindaraja temple – Anantaraju
[4:3] - **Ekambaranatha, Kanchipuram:** Pallava origin, but the great southern **Rajagopuram (1509)** is **Krishnadevaraya's** – the builder the APPSC question expects.
[4:4] - Latest (5 Mar 2026): Achyutaraya's **1534** inscriptions on a 50-ft pillar of the **Somapalayam Chennakesava** temple, Annamayya district.

## SUB 5 | Forts, Cities and Later Capitals
[5:0] - **Gooty (Gutti) fort:** wall inscriptions from the **7th century** (APPSC FRO 2023 ×2); a **Bukka I** record calls it the foremost of forts; Aliya Rama Raya's base.
[5:1] - **Penukonda fort** = **Vijayanagara** kingdom; first **Aravidu capital (1570)** → **Chandragiri** (Venkatapati Raya II; Raja Mahal) → **Vellore (1600)**.
[5:2] - **Nagalapura (Nagalapuram)** = city founded by **Krishnadevaraya** for his mother Nagala Devi (UPPCS 2016) – not Warangal, Udayagiri or Chandragiri.
[5:3] - Udayagiri (Nellore) – Panchaloha cannon; Gandikota – idols looted by **Mir Jumla** (1657).
[5:4] ~~ Sources: anantapur.ap.gov.in (Gooty fort); NextGen Vijayanagara Kingdom p.5; app notes 1:123 (sub 8)
