## 4. Corrections to the old notes
- MSME Policy 2015-20 target = **3.5 lakh** jobs and Rs 15,000 crore (policy PDF), not "5 lakh".
- Pepper Motion: Rs 4,640 crore unit at **Punganur** (Nov 2023 approval); no "earlier Badvel plan".
- Swachh Survekshan 2022: Vijayawada = **cleanest capital** (not "among cleanest big cities"); Pulivendula and Salur categories were swapped in G1 2023 statements III and IV.
- IDP 2023-27 aims at a **minimum 30%** industry share and 21-day land allotment (Nexdigm summary).
- Coastline: **1,053.07 km, 3rd** (PIB, Dec 2025), not 974 km, 2nd.
- RBI corporate-investment study (Sept 2011) placed AP 2nd, not 3rd (key kept with dispute).
