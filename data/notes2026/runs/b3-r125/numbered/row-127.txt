## SUB 0 | Amaravati Capital – APCRDA, Foundation and Building
[0:0] # APCRDA and the capital city
[0:1] - **APCRDA** = AP Capital Region Development Authority, under the **APCRDA Act, 2014** (authority set up **2014**); HQ **Rayapudi**.
[0:2] - **Amaravati** = a **greenfield capital** on the **Krishna**, between Vijayawada and Guntur, in **Thullur** mandal; "India's first smart capital"; **water-centric**, 51% green/blue space.
[0:3] TABLE head: Unit | Area
  [0:3.0] Capital city (notified) | 217.23 sq km, 29 villages
  [0:3.1] Capital region | 8,352.69 sq km (expanded Aug 2026, from 7,068.20)
[0:4] - **Master plan:** **Surbana Jurong (Singapore)**. **Amaravati Development Partner** = Singapore consortium (**Ascendas-Singbridge, Sembcorp**) with **Amaravati Development Corporation** – **no Japanese** partner.
[0:5] - **Land pooling:** farmers sent to **Singapore** to learn best practice; Phase I **93.1% of 37,921 acres**, **70,550** returnable plots; LPS 2.0 about 16,666 acres in 7 villages (Apr 2026); **6,212.50 acres** for the **Inner Ring Road** (Sep 2026).
[0:6] - **"My Brick – My Amaravati"** (2015): donors bought e-bricks at **Rs 10 per brick**.
[0:7] - **Interim Legislative building** (Velagapudi): completed in **192 days** (AP Budget 2017-18).
[0:8] - **Justice City:** India's first, planned in Amaravati (High Court, judicial academy, legal institutions).
[0:9] - **Works and funds:** 96 works, ₹57,592.71 crore; World Bank + ADB ₹15,000 crore; HUDCO ₹11,000 crore; Amaravati Sangamam at Sakhamuru.
