## SUB 1 | Health Insurance and Health Schemes
[1:0] # Aarogyasri → Dr NTR Vaidya Seva
[1:1] - **Origin:** **Rajiv Aarogyasri** (**April 2007**, BPL families) → **Dr YSR Aarogyasri** → **Dr NTR Vaidya Seva**.
[1:2] - **2014-19 (NTR Vaidya Seva):** cover **up to Rs 2.5 lakh per BPL family** on a **floater** basis.
[1:3] - **Now:** **BPL families and families with income up to Rs 5 lakh**; cover **up to Rs 25 lakh per family per year**, **no limit for cancer**; **3,257 procedures**; **1.43 crore** families benefited. Budget 2026-27: Rs 4,000 crore to the Trust.
[1:4] - **Aarogya Raksha** (**7 April 2017**): for families **above the poverty line** (outside Aarogyasri), premium **Rs 1,200** per member a year, cover Rs 2 lakh.
[1:5] - **YSR Aarogya Aasara:** **post-operative sustenance allowance** during recovery.
[1:6] - **Universal Health Policy (2026):** up to **Rs 2.5 lakh per citizen** through insurers.
