=== Rival Claimants and the Battle of Ambur (1749)
- Trigger: death of **Nizam-ul-Mulk (1748)** and release of **Chanda Sahib** (son-in-law of Dost Ali) from **Maratha** captivity.
| Throne | French-backed | English-backed |
|---|---|---|
| Hyderabad (Deccan) | **Muzaffar Jang** (Nizam-ul-Mulk's daughter's son) | **Nasir Jang** (son) |
| Carnatic (Arcot) | **Chanda Sahib** | **Anwaruddin**, then **Muhammad Ali** |
- **Battle of Ambur (1749, near Vellore)** = first major battle: **Muzaffar Jang + Chanda Sahib + the French** defeated and **killed Anwaruddin**; Chanda Sahib became Nawab; Muhammad Ali fled to **Trichinopoly**.
- CGPSC 2023: "fought in **1752**" is **not true** (it was 1749); the alliance and Anwaruddin's defeat are true → only (i).
- Nasir Jang killed by **Himmat Khan** (Nawab of Kurnool) in a Dupleix plot; Muzaffar Jang killed at **Rayachoti (1751)** → **Bussy** installed **Salabat Jang** (fourth son).
~~ Sources: Spectrum Modern India p.77-78 (book p.45-46); NextGen Modern India L2 Part 1 p.12-13; old app notes row 160

=== Dupleix's Gains – South of the Krishna and the Northern Circars
- **Muzaffar Jang**, after becoming Nizam, appointed **Dupleix** governor of all Mughal territory **south of the River Krishna** – not Pierre Dulivier, François Martin or Bellanger.
- Jagir **Valapadu**; Masulipatnam and areas near Pondicherry ceded; **Bussy** stationed at Hyderabad, got the **Northern Circars (1753)** from **Salabat Jang**.
- Main French territorial gains were on the **Andhra coast** (Machilipatnam, Northern Circars) – unlike the First War, which was a European extension.
- Dupleix **won the Deccan but lost the Carnatic**; recalled **1754** for the heavy cost and failures.
~~ Sources: Spectrum Modern India p.78-80 (book p.46-48); NextGen Modern India Part 1 p.4; old app notes row 160

=== Robert Clive at Arcot and the Treaty of Pondicherry
- **1751**: to relieve **Muhammad Ali** at Trichinopoly, **Clive** made a diversionary raid and took **Arcot** with about **210 men**; held it about **53 days** against **Raja Sahib**; then won at **Arni** and **Kaveripak**.
- Allies: **Morari Rao** (Maratha), Mysore, Tanjore; **June 1752**: **Clive and Stringer Lawrence** relieved Trichinopoly; French commander **Law** surrendered; **Chanda Sahib** surrendered and was **executed**.
- **Treaty of Pondicherry (1754-55)**: **Godeheu** with English Governor **Thomas Saunders**; neither Company to interfere in Indian princes' quarrels; **Muhammad Ali** recognised as Nawab.
