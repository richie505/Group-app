## SUB 0 | Rival Claimants and the Battle of Ambur (1749)
[0:0] - Trigger: death of **Nizam-ul-Mulk (1748)** and release of **Chanda Sahib** (son-in-law of Dost Ali) from **Maratha** captivity.
[0:1] TABLE head: Throne | French-backed | English-backed
  [0:1.0] Hyderabad (Deccan) | Muzaffar Jang (Nizam-ul-Mulk's daughter's son) | Nasir Jang (son)
  [0:1.1] Carnatic (Arcot) | Chanda Sahib | Anwaruddin, then Muhammad Ali
[0:2] - **Battle of Ambur (1749, near Vellore)** = first major battle: **Muzaffar Jang + Chanda Sahib + the French** defeated and **killed Anwaruddin**; Chanda Sahib became Nawab; Muhammad Ali fled to **Trichinopoly**.
[0:3] - CGPSC 2023: "fought in **1752**" is **not true** (it was 1749); the alliance and Anwaruddin's defeat are true → only (i).
[0:4] - Nasir Jang killed by **Himmat Khan** (Nawab of Kurnool) in a Dupleix plot; Muzaffar Jang killed at **Rayachoti (1751)** → **Bussy** installed **Salabat Jang** (fourth son).

## SUB 1 | Dupleix's Gains – South of the Krishna and the Northern Circars
[1:0] - **Muzaffar Jang**, after becoming Nizam, appointed **Dupleix** governor of all Mughal territory **south of the River Krishna** – not Pierre Dulivier, François Martin or Bellanger.
[1:1] - Jagir **Valapadu**; Masulipatnam and areas near Pondicherry ceded; **Bussy** stationed at Hyderabad, got the **Northern Circars (1753)** from **Salabat Jang**.
[1:2] - Main French territorial gains were on the **Andhra coast** (Machilipatnam, Northern Circars) – unlike the First War, which was a European extension.
[1:3] - Dupleix **won the Deccan but lost the Carnatic**; recalled **1754** for the heavy cost and failures.
[1:4] ~~ Sources: Spectrum Modern India p.78-80 (book p.46-48); NextGen Modern India Part 1 p.4; old app notes row 160
## SUB 2 | Robert Clive at Arcot and the Treaty of Pondicherry
[2:0] - **1751**: to relieve **Muhammad Ali** at Trichinopoly, **Clive** made a diversionary raid and took **Arcot** with about **210 men**; held it about **53 days** against **Raja Sahib**; then won at **Arni** and **Kaveripak**.
[2:1] - Allies: **Morari Rao** (Maratha), Mysore, Tanjore; **June 1752**: **Clive and Stringer Lawrence** relieved Trichinopoly; French commander **Law** surrendered; **Chanda Sahib** surrendered and was **executed**.
[2:2] - **Treaty of Pondicherry (1754-55)**: **Godeheu** with English Governor **Thomas Saunders**; neither Company to interfere in Indian princes' quarrels; **Muhammad Ali** recognised as Nawab.
[2:3] ~~ Sources: Spectrum Modern India p.78, 80-81 (book p.46, 48-49); old app notes row 160
