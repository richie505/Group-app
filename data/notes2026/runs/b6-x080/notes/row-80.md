# Thirstwaves
- **Thirstwave** = **three or more consecutive days of high atmospheric evaporative demand** (Kukal and Hobbins, *Earth's Future*, March 2025).
- Measured with **short-crop evapotranspiration (ET₀)** – a theoretical **12 cm grass crop**; drivers: temperature, low humidity, wind, solar radiation (not temperature alone, not rainfall).
| Aspect | Heatwave | Thirstwave |
|---|---|---|
| Based on | High air temperature | High evaporative demand |
| Main impact | Human health | **Crop water stress** |
