## SUB 2 | SHANTI Act, 2025 – Nuclear Sector Opened
[2:0] # The Act (Winter Session, December 2025)
[2:1] - **Sustainable Harnessing and Advancement of Nuclear Energy for Transforming India (SHANTI) Act, 2025**.
[2:2] - **Replaces** the **Atomic Energy Act, 1962** and the **Civil Liability for Nuclear Damage (CLND) Act, 2010**.
[2:3] - **Ends the NPCIL monopoly** – private and foreign firms may **build, own, operate** plants and **manufacture equipment**.
[2:4] - **Kept for the Centre:** enrichment beyond the prescribed limit, **spent fuel management**, heavy water, reprocessing.
[2:5] - **AERB** (set up **1983**) gets **statutory status**, accountable to Parliament.
[2:6] - **Section 39:** Centre may declare nuclear information "restricted" – overrides the **RTI Act**.
[2:7] - Appeals go to the **Appellate Tribunal for Electricity**; Nuclear Damage Claims Commission handles claims.

  [2:8.0] Liability cap | 300 million SDR (~₹3,900 crore); operator ₹100–3,000 crore by reactor size
  [2:8.1] Supplier liability | Eliminated (no right of recourse)
  [2:8.2] Above the cap | Nuclear Liability Fund
[2:9] # Background and targets
