=== Sports Administration – 3% Sports Quota
- Notified **20 April 2025**: **3% horizontal reservation** for meritorious sportspersons in **direct recruitment** (departments, local bodies, PSUs, police, excise, forest) and professional-course admissions.
- Under the **AP Sports Policy 2024–29**; valid **five years or until a new policy**.
- **65 sports** in two disciplines; verification by **SAAP** (Sports Authority of AP).
- Selection **without a written examination** – merit list of a **State Level Committee**, recruitment via **APPSC** and other boards.
- Policy features: **one ground per village**; **Sports City in Amaravati** (no "sports capital at Kakinada"); aligned with **Swarna Andhra Sports Vision 2047**.

=== Heritage, Classical Telugu and NSU Tirupati
# Erra Matti Dibbalu (Red Sand Hills)
- On the **Visakhapatnam–Bheemunipatnam Beach Road**; added to the **UNESCO World Heritage Tentative List** – **one of seven Indian sites** (2025); one of India's **34 National Geo-heritage Sites**.
- Formed in the **Last Glacial Maximum (~18,500 years ago)**; red colour from **ferrous minerals reacting with moisture**.
# Classical Telugu – CESCT, Nellore
- Telugu declared **classical language in 2008**; CESCT launched in **Mysuru (2018)**, shifted to **Nellore in 2019** at the instance of then VP **M. Venkaiah Naidu**; functions under **CIIL, Mysuru**.
- Work: restored 3 palm-leaf manuscripts; published Dr **Emani Sivanagireddy's "Mana Telugu Vari Sasanalu"**; translating **"Kreedabhiramamu"**.
# NSU Tirupati
- **IKS Centre** sanctioned at **NSU, Tirupati** under the **IKS Division, Ministry of Education** (not Culture); **8 IKS centres** sanctioned in all.
- **"Sri Ramayana Sankharavam"** – legal conclave of the **Valmiki Research Centre** (NSU), **20 September 2025**, **Vizianagaram campus**.
# 17th-century inscription, Prakasam
- Found at **Anantararayunipalli village, Komarolu Mandal**; records a land grant (Ganupulapalli village and lands at Komaravoli) for services to **Lord Hanumanta**.

=== Environment, Science and Other State Initiatives
# Striped dolphins off Visakhapatnam
- Pod of **striped dolphins (*Stenella coeruleoalba*)** – filmed by a fisherman, confirmed by the **East Coast Conservation Team (ECCT)**; IUCN **Least Concern**.
- Commonly seen in AP waters: Indian Ocean humpback (*Sousa plumbea*), spinner, spotted dolphins – the pod was **not** humpback.
- **All cetaceans are in Schedule I** (not II) of the WPA 1972; network **MMRCNI**.
# Swachh Ratham and circular economy
- **Swachh Ratham** pilot: villagers exchange **dry waste for groceries** of equal value; waste **sold to recyclers** (not biogas); pilot in seven panchayats of Guntur Rural mandal, with drivers of Material Delivery Units repurposing their vehicles.
- CM target: cut **chemical fertiliser use by 4 lakh tonnes** next year.
# Chittoor mango covers
- **Biodegradable iron-wire mesh** cover over each mango; ₹2 in the market, given at **₹1 per cover** (subsidy); **1.92 crore covers** over 2,000 acres; protected mangoes fetched **double the price**.
- Chittoor varieties: **Totapuri, Benishan, Mallika, Alphonso** (not Banganapalle); diseases: powdery mildew, anthracnose.
# Defence, space and skills
- **DRDO:** est. **1958**, Ministry of Defence; **Agni, Prithvi, Akash, Nag**; **Mission Shakti** (first ASAT test); **no major DRDO lab is headquartered in AP**, though AP has test facilities and collaborations.
- **SHAR, Sriharikota** – ISRO launch centre; **named after Satish Dhawan in 2002**; **two launch pads**; near-equator launches save fuel.
- **Unnati Foundation** with **APSSDC** – vocational training centre in **Kakinada**.
