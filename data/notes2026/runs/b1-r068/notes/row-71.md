=== Rulers and Their Achievements – Kailasa Is Krishna I
| Ruler | Reign | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Dantidurga** | 753–756 | Founder; Hiranyagarbha |
| **Krishna I** | 756–773 | Monolithic **Kailasa (Kailashanatha)** temple, Ellora **Cave 16** |
| **Dhruva** | 780–793 | **First to go north**: beat Vatsaraja and Dharmapala; Ganga–Yamuna on his banner; subdued Eastern Chalukyas and Gangas |
| **Govinda III** | 793–814 | Beat **Nagabhata II** and Dharmapala; reached the **Himalayas** (Sanjan and Radhanpur plates) |
| **Amoghavarsha I** | 814–878 | Built **Manyakheta**; wrote **Kavirajamarga**; **Jain** patron |
| **Indra III** | 10th c. | Looted Kannauj c. 916, defeating Pratihara Mahipala I |
| **Krishna III** | mid-10th c. | Beat the Cholas at **Takkolam** (949); patron of Ponna |
- **Kailasa temple = Krishna I** (G1 1990-91; RAS 1999; CGPSC 2015; Uttarakhand 2021) – not Govinda II, Krishna II, Krishna III, Dantidurga, Govinda III, Amoghavarsha or Indra III.
- **Wrongly matched (G1 Prelims 2024):** "**Krishna III** – rock-cut Shiva temple at Ellora" ✗. Correct pairs: Amoghavarsha – first (available) Kannada book; Dantidurga – founder; **Vallabharaja** – contemporary of Al-Masudi.
- **Order:** **Dantidurga → Krishna I → Dhruva → Govinda III → Amoghavarsha I**.
- **Amoghavarsha I** was born in **800 CE** at **Shribhavan** near the Narmada, **in a military camp** (not Manyakheta), as father Govinda III returned from the north (UPPSC 2013); a disciple of the Jain **Jinasena**.

=== Pampa and Kannada Literature
# Pampa – the Adikavi
- **Vikramarjuna Vijayam (Pampa Bharata)** in **Kannada** – by the **Telugu-origin** poet **Pampa (Pampana)** (G2 2012; APPSC PYQ); not Rattaka, Narayana Bhattu or Bhimakavi.
- **Patron:** Vemulavada Chalukya **Arikesari II (Immadi Arikesari)**, a Rashtrakuta feudatory; Arjuna is modelled on him – Pampa was his contemporary (G1 1991).
- **Mahabharata in regional languages (IAS 1997):** **Pampa – Kannada** ✓; Sarala Dasa – Odia, Kasirama – Bengali, Tikkana – Telugu.
- Pampa = Jain, brother of **Jinavallabha**; also wrote the Kannada **Adipurana** (life of **Rishabhadeva**).
# Ratnatraya and patrons
| Poet | Patron | Work |
|---|---|---|
| **Pampa** | **Arikesari II** (Vemulavada) | Vikramarjuna Vijaya, Adipurana |
| **Ponna** | **Krishna III** | Santipurana |
| **Ranna** | Western Chalukya **Tailapa II** | **Gadayuddha** |
- **Pampa, Ponna and Ranna** = the 'three gems' (**Ratnatraya**) of **Kannada** poetry (Sachivalayam).
- **Vemulavada Chalukyas** (Karimnagar, Rashtrakuta feudatories): strong **Jain** patrons – Pampa, **Somadeva Suri**; Jinavallabha's **Kurkyala** inscription has early **Telugu** verses.

=== Dantidurga – Founder
- **Founder (UPSC 2006; Polytechnic Lecturers 2012):** **Dantidurga** (753), a former **Chalukya feudatory** – not Amoghavarsha, Dhruva, Krishna, Pulakesin I, Vindhyasakti (Vakataka) or Vikramaditya II.
- **Independence:** the kingdom became independent under **Dantidurga** (Tribal Welfare Officer), who overthrew the Badami Chalukya **Kirtivarman II**.
- **Hiranyagarbha** ritual at **Ujjayini (Ujjain)** – to be "reborn" a Kshatriya (UPPSC 2015) – not Mayurasharma, Harishchandra or Harsha.
- **Seat:** **Elapura (Ellora)** region; his inscription is in Ellora's **Dashavatara cave** (Cave 15).
- Contemporary of Eastern Chalukya **Vijayaditya I**. Dynasty span: c. 753–973 CE.
- **⚠ Trap:** Dantidurga did NOT build Manyakheta – Amoghavarsha I did.
~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 3 p.109; NextGen Rocket Indian History Part 2 p.124; NextGen Eastern Chalukyas p.12; app notes 1:71 (sub 0)

=== Capital, Religion and Titles
- **Capital:** **Manyakheta (Malkhed**, Kalaburagi, **Karnataka)**, built by **Amoghavarsha I** (G1 1983-84) – not Vijayapuri, Badami or Bhattiprolu; early seat **Elapura (Ellora)**.
- **Religion:** patronised **Shaiva, Vaishnava, Shakta** faiths but leaned to **Jainism** (UDA/LDA 2010: answer **Jain**); about a third of the Deccan was Jain.
- **Kailasa** = Shaiva, **Dravidian** style.

=== Battle of Takkolam (949 CE)
- **Cholas vs Rashtrakutas** (UDA/LDA 2001; UPPSC 2003) – not Chalukyas, Hoysalas or Pandyas.
- **Krishna III** with the **Western Gangas** and **Vaidumbas** defeated the Cholas of **Parantaka I**.
- Chola prince **Rajaditya** (son of Parantaka I) led and was **killed**; Krishna III took the title conqueror of Kanchi and Tanjore.
~~ Sources: NextGen Rocket Indian History Part 1 p.69; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 3 p.110; app notes 1:71 (sub 6)

=== Vengi Wars and the End of the Dynasty
| Rashtrakuta | Eastern Chalukya | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Dhruva** (through Vemulavada Arikesari I) | **Vishnuvardhana IV** | Dhruva won; Vishnuvardhana gave daughter **Shila Mahadevi** to Dhruva |
| Rashtrakuta forces | **Vijayaditya II** | **108 battles**; he built **108 Shiva temples**; linked with naming **Vijayawada** |
| Amoghavarsha I | Eastern Chalukyas | Won at **Vingavalli** |
| Krishna II | **Gunaga Vijayaditya III** | Gunaga won |
- **Length:** the Rashtrakuta–Vengi wars went on for about **200 years**.
- **End:** Paramara **Siyaka II** defeated **Khottiga** and sacked Manyakheta (972); **Tailapa II** (Kalyani Chalukya) overthrew the line c. **973**.
~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 3 p.110; app notes 1:71 (sub 5, 9)
