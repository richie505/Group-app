=== Dynasty, Capital and Mother Goddess
- **Badami (Vatapi) Chalukyas:** **c. 543–753 CE**; ruled the **Deccan and the South**, **NOT North India** (Uttarakhand 2010: "dynasty that did not rule North India" = Chalukyas).
- **Capital = Vatapi** (UPPSC 1991 ×2) = modern **Badami, Bagalkot district, Karnataka** – not Shravasti, **Kanchi** (Pallava) or Kannauj; sandstone hills around Agastya lake. Before Vatapi, **Aihole (Ayyavole)** was the seat.
- **Vatapi fortified by Pulakesin I** – **Badami cliff inscription, 543 CE**.
- **Mother goddess:** the early Chalukyas called themselves **Hariti-putras** and worshipped **Hariti** (G2 Mains 2019; APPSC PYQ) – not Kushmandini, Padmakshi or Kanakadurga.

=== Pulakesin II – the Greatest Chalukya
# Greatest ruler
- **Pulakesin II (610–642)** = the greatest ruler of the **Chalukyas of Vatapi** (UPPSC 1991; SSC 2013) – not Kalyani Chalukyas, Pallavas or Cholas; not Vikramaditya, Mangalesha or Pulakesin I.
- **Hiuen Tsang** visited his kingdom and praised him (APPSC FRO 2012) – not Pulakesin I or III; Madhava Varma was a Vishnukundin.
# Events
| Year (CE) | Event |
|---|---|
| 617 | **Satara inscription**: brother **Kubja Vishnuvardhana** viceroy of Vengi |
| 618–19 | Stopped **Harsha** on the **Narmada** |
| **624** | Vengi independent = Eastern Chalukyas (capital **Pithapuram**) |
| c. 626 | Embassy with Persian king **Khusrau II** |
| 634–35 | Aihole inscription |
| 642 | Pallava **Narasimhavarman I** destroyed Vatapi → title **Vatapikonda** |
- **Eastern campaign:** **Kosala & Kalinga** → Vengi → defeated the **Durjayas** → then the Pallava **Mahendravarman I**.
# Titles and foreign accounts
| Ruler | Title |
|---|---|
| Kushanas | **Devaputra** |
| **Pulakesin II** | **Dakshinapatheshwara** |
| Harshavardhana | **Sakalottarapathanatha** |
| Samudragupta | **Kaviraja** |
- Match (UPRO 2023): **A-i, B-iii, C-ii, D-iv**.
- **Paramesvara** after beating **Harsha**; also Satyashraya, Prithvivallabha.
- China–India relations under the Chalukyas: **Ma Twan-lin** (Matwalin). **Lohnara inscription (630 CE)** does NOT list Harsha; Aihole does.
- First **gold coins** of South India credited to Pulakesin II.

=== Aihole Inscription of Ravikirti
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Author | **Ravikirti**, **Jain** court poet of Pulakesin II |
| Language / script | **Sanskrit** prasasti, **Southern Brahmi** |
| Date | **634–35 CE** (Saka era) |
| Place | **Meguti Jain temple**, Aihole |
| Poets named | **Kalidasa and Bharavi** (Ravikirti claimed fame like theirs) |
- **Kalidasa is mentioned in** the **Aihole inscription of Pulakesin II** (IAS 1994; UPPSC 2013) – not the Allahabad pillar, Alapadu grant, Hanumakonda, Gwalior, Karamdanda or Mathura pillar inscriptions.
- **Victories:** **Harsha** (Narmada), **Kadambas** (Banavasi), Gangas, Alupas, Mauryas of Konkan, Latas, Malavas, Gurjaras, **Kosala, Kalinga, Pishtapura** (**Pithapuram**), **Pallavas**.
- Kirtivarman I = "**night of doom**" to the **Nalas, Mauryas and Kadambas**.
~~ Sources: NextGen Rocket Indian History Part 1 p.11; NextGen Rocket Indian History Part 2 p.121; app notes 1:73 (sub 4)

=== Rulers and Succession
| Ruler | Reign | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Pulakesin I** | 543–566 | **Real founder**; **Ashvamedha**; walled Vatapi |
| **Kirtivarman I** | 566–597 | Took **Kadambas, Gangas, Mauryas of Konkan**; Bahusuvarna-Agnishtoma (**Mahakuta pillar**) |
| **Mangalesha** | 597–609 | Regent; **earliest rock-cut caves at Badami** |
| **Pulakesin II** | 610–642 | Zenith |
| **Vikramaditya I** | 655–680 | Revived power after the Pallava occupation of Vatapi |
| **Vikramaditya II** | 733–746 | Took **Kanchi** thrice but spared it; queen **Lokamahadevi** built the **Virupaksha** temple, Pattadakal |
| **Kirtivarman II** | 746–753 | **Last**; overthrown by Rashtrakuta **Dantidurga** |
- Other sacrifices: **Vajapeya, Hiranyagarbha** – claim to **Kshatriya** status.
- **Succession war:** Kirtivarman I left minor sons → brother **Mangalesha** ruled as **regent**; Pulakesin II killed him at **Elapattu-Simbige** (Bana territory, c. 609–10).
~~ Sources: NextGen Rocket Indian History Part 2 p.121; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 3 p.107-108; app notes 1:73 (sub 1, 2)
