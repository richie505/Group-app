## SUB 0 | Chalukya–Harsha Conflict – Battle of the Narmada
[0:0] - **Victor:** **Pulakesin II** (Badami Chalukya) stopped **Harsha's** southward march on the **Narmada** (UPSC 2003 ×2; UPPSC 2016) – not Pulakesin I, Vikramaditya I/II or Kirtivarman II.
[0:1] - **Title Paramesvara** after defeating Harsha = **Pulakesin II** (APPSC G1) – not Pulakesin I or the Pallava Mahendravarmans.
[0:2] - **Date:** **618 CE** (some books 630–634); before the **Aihole inscription** (634) recorded it.
[0:3] - **Sources:** **Aihole inscription + Hiuen Tsang** (NOT the Kailasanatha inscription).
[0:4] - **Titles:** Paramesvara and **Dakshinapatheshwara** ("lord of the south") vs Harsha's **Uttarapatheshwara**; Harsha accepted the Narmada as his southern border.
[0:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History APPSC Part 3 p.21; NextGen Rocket Indian History Part 2 p.48, p.119, p.121; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 3 p.107; app notes 1:74 (sub 0)
## SUB 1 | Chalukya–Pallava Rivalry
[1:0] # Four rounds
[1:1] TABLE head: Round | Chalukya | Pallava | Result
  [1:1.0] 1 | Pulakesin II | Mahendravarman I | Advanced to Kanchi; won the Krishna–Godavari land (Maruturu, Kopparam)
  [1:1.1] 2 | Pulakesin II | Narasimhavarman I | Pallava victory; Vatapi taken (642), Pulakesin II killed
  [1:1.2] 3 | Vikramaditya I | Narasimhavarman I / Paramesvaravarman I | Vatapi recovered (c. 655)
  [1:1.3] 4 | Vikramaditya II | Nandivarman II | Kanchi taken three times, spared
[1:2] - Pallava occupation of Vatapi: **642 to c. 655** (about **13 years**).
[1:3] - **End:** Badami Chalukyas replaced by the **Rashtrakutas (Dantidurga)**, NOT the Cholas; Nandivarman II did not overthrow the Rashtrakutas.
[1:4] # Battles
[1:5] TABLE head: Battle | Victor
  [1:5.0] Pullalur (near Kanchi, c. 618–19) | Pulakesin II over Mahendravarman I (Chalukya records; Pallava records claim it)
  [1:5.1] Manimangala (c. 642) | Narasimhavarman I over Pulakesin II (also Pariyala, Suramara)
  [1:5.2] Vatapi (642) | Narasimhavarman I; sacked by general Paranjoti
[1:6] - **Battle where Pulakesin II conquered Mahendravarman I = Pullalur** (SI 2016) – not Vatapi, Kolleru or Aihole.
[1:7] # Titles
[1:8] TABLE head: Title | Meaning | Ruler
  [1:8.0] Paramesvara, Dakshinapatheshwara | Lord of the south | Pulakesin II
  [1:8.1] Vatapikonda | Conqueror of Vatapi | Narasimhavarman I (Pallava; also Mamalla)
  [1:8.2] Kanchiyamkonda | Who took Kanchi | Vikramaditya II (Chalukya)
[1:9] # Chronology (SI 2023)

## SUB 2 | Women in Chalukya Administration
[2:0] - **Dynasty that often gave women high administrative posts = Chalukyas** (UPPSC 2007) – not Cholas, Palas or Senas.
[2:1] TABLE head: Woman | Role
  [2:1.0] Vijayabhattarika (Vijaya-mahadevi) | Queen of Chandraditya (son of Pulakesin II); two copper plates / land grants in her own name; poetess; administered the kingdom after his death
  [2:1.1] Kumkumdevi | Sister of Vikramaditya I; village granted to a Jain monastery
  [2:1.2] Lokamahadevi, Trailokyamahadevi | Queens of Vikramaditya II; Virupaksha and Mallikarjuna temples, Pattadakal
[2:2] - Comparison: Kalyani Chalukya princess **Akkadevi** governed **Kisukad**; Eastern Chalukya queens (Ayyana Mahadevi, Chamakamba) = temple builders, not governors.
[2:3] ~~ Sources: NextGen Rocket Indian History Part 2 p.121; app notes 1:74 (sub 6)
## SUB 3 | Chalukya Occupation of Kanchi
[3:0] - **Vikramaditya II** entered Kanchi **c. 740 CE** (**Nandivarman II's** reign); **no plunder**.
[3:1] - Gave gold to the **Rajasimheshvara (Kailasanatha)** temple; left a **Kannada inscription** on a pillar.
[3:2] - **Lokamahadevi's Virupaksha temple, Pattadakal** copies the **Kailasanatha** of Kanchi.
[3:3] - **Kanchi** – Pallava capital, temple city and seat of learning, the "**Kashi (Benares) of the South**".
[3:4] ~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 3 p.108, p.135; app notes 1:74 (sub 4)
## SUB 4 | Chalukya Administration and the Andhra Angle
[4:0] - Units: **rashtra** (province) → **vishaya** (district, under the **vishayapati**) → **grama** (village, under the gramakuta).
[4:1] - **Vatapi and Aihole** = urban and trade centres.
[4:2] - **Agraharas:** Brahmin land grants run by **mahajanas**.
[4:3] - Feudatories: **Rashtrakutas**, Alupas, Gangas, Sendrakas; **Renati Chodas** (Rayalaseema) – feudatories of the early Pallavas, then the **Badami Chalukyas**.
[4:4] - **AP angle:** the wars led to the rise in Rayalaseema of the **Renati Chodas, Banas, Vaidumbas**; the **Pitikayagulla** inscription (8th c., early Telugu script) shows **post-Badami Chalukya influence**.
[4:5] ~~ Sources: app notes 1:74 (sub 5)
