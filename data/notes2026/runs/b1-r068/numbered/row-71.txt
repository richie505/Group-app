## SUB 1 | Pampa and Kannada Literature
[1:0] # Pampa – the Adikavi
[1:1] - **Vikramarjuna Vijayam (Pampa Bharata)** in **Kannada** – by the **Telugu-origin** poet **Pampa (Pampana)** (G2 2012; APPSC PYQ); not Rattaka, Narayana Bhattu or Bhimakavi.
[1:2] - **Patron:** Vemulavada Chalukya **Arikesari II (Immadi Arikesari)**, a Rashtrakuta feudatory; Arjuna is modelled on him – Pampa was his contemporary (G1 1991).
[1:3] - **Mahabharata in regional languages (IAS 1997):** **Pampa – Kannada** ✓; Sarala Dasa – Odia, Kasirama – Bengali, Tikkana – Telugu.
[1:4] - Pampa = Jain, brother of **Jinavallabha**; also wrote the Kannada **Adipurana** (life of **Rishabhadeva**).
[1:5] # Ratnatraya and patrons
[1:6] TABLE head: Poet | Patron | Work
  [1:6.0] Pampa | Arikesari II (Vemulavada) | Vikramarjuna Vijaya, Adipurana
  [1:6.1] Ponna | Krishna III | Santipurana
  [1:6.2] Ranna | Western Chalukya Tailapa II | Gadayuddha
[1:7] - **Pampa, Ponna and Ranna** = the 'three gems' (**Ratnatraya**) of **Kannada** poetry (Sachivalayam).
[1:8] - **Vemulavada Chalukyas** (Karimnagar, Rashtrakuta feudatories): strong **Jain** patrons – Pampa, **Somadeva Suri**; Jinavallabha's **Kurkyala** inscription has early **Telugu** verses.

## SUB 2 | Dantidurga – Founder
[2:0] - **Founder (UPSC 2006; Polytechnic Lecturers 2012):** **Dantidurga** (753), a former **Chalukya feudatory** – not Amoghavarsha, Dhruva, Krishna, Pulakesin I, Vindhyasakti (Vakataka) or Vikramaditya II.
[2:1] - **Independence:** the kingdom became independent under **Dantidurga** (Tribal Welfare Officer), who overthrew the Badami Chalukya **Kirtivarman II**.
[2:2] - **Hiranyagarbha** ritual at **Ujjayini (Ujjain)** – to be "reborn" a Kshatriya (UPPSC 2015) – not Mayurasharma, Harishchandra or Harsha.
[2:3] - **Seat:** **Elapura (Ellora)** region; his inscription is in Ellora's **Dashavatara cave** (Cave 15).
[2:4] - Contemporary of Eastern Chalukya **Vijayaditya I**. Dynasty span: c. 753–973 CE.
[2:5] - **⚠ Trap:** Dantidurga did NOT build Manyakheta – Amoghavarsha I did.
[2:6] ~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 3 p.109; NextGen Rocket Indian History Part 2 p.124; NextGen Eastern Chalukyas p.12; app notes 1:71 (sub 0)
## SUB 3 | Capital, Religion and Titles
[3:0] - **Capital:** **Manyakheta (Malkhed**, Kalaburagi, **Karnataka)**, built by **Amoghavarsha I** (G1 1983-84) – not Vijayapuri, Badami or Bhattiprolu; early seat **Elapura (Ellora)**.
[3:1] - **Religion:** patronised **Shaiva, Vaishnava, Shakta** faiths but leaned to **Jainism** (UDA/LDA 2010: answer **Jain**); about a third of the Deccan was Jain.
[3:2] - **Kailasa** = Shaiva, **Dravidian** style.

## SUB 4 | Battle of Takkolam (949 CE)
[4:0] - **Cholas vs Rashtrakutas** (UDA/LDA 2001; UPPSC 2003) – not Chalukyas, Hoysalas or Pandyas.
[4:1] - **Krishna III** with the **Western Gangas** and **Vaidumbas** defeated the Cholas of **Parantaka I**.
[4:2] - Chola prince **Rajaditya** (son of Parantaka I) led and was **killed**; Krishna III took the title conqueror of Kanchi and Tanjore.
[4:3] ~~ Sources: NextGen Rocket Indian History Part 1 p.69; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 3 p.110; app notes 1:71 (sub 6)
