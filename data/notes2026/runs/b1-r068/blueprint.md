## 3. Repeats and traps
- **Repeated (3+ times):** Vikramashila = Dharmapala (×10); Kailasa = Krishna I / Rashtrakutas (×12 across rows 71-72); Ellora caves facts (×9); Harsha stopped by Pulakesin II (×4); Nalanda = Kumaragupta I / Gupta (×3); Pampa – Vikramarjuna Vijaya (×5); Tripartite powers (×5); Dantidurga founder (×4).
- **Keys settled against the printed or old key:**
  - **Mangalapura:** G2 2019 printed key "Kopparam" is wrong – NextGen Previous Papers p.13, Rocket AP History and NextGen AP History Part 6 all say the **Marturu** inscription calls Mangalagiri Mangalapura (the old notes already had this right).
  - **Rashtrakuta religion (CDS 2007):** printed "Saivism" → **Jainism**, matching the UDA/LDA 2010 key and the examrobot UPSC key; Saivism is mentioned as the kings' own early faith.
  - **Ellora chronology (G1 2023):** no option is right. Dumar Lena (Cave 29) is among the earliest, Caves 14 and 15 came before 16, so the order is IV, I, III, II (added as an option). The old notes also had a wrong order (Ravan ki Khai first).
  - **Battle of Manneru:** an unkeyed AP item (Velanati Chodas with Kulottunga II against the Kalyani Chalukyas) was keyed from web sources and moved to row 83.
- **Source conflicts found:** Rocket and PMF credit Elephanta to the Rashtrakutas, while PMF Art & Culture dates it to the 5th–6th century (Kalachuri), so it gets a ⚠ dispute line. NextGen Eastern Chalukyas makes Pampa a contemporary of Vishnuvardhana IV, which is chronologically impossible; the notes follow Pampa – Arikesari II. Harsha's defeat is dated 618 (most books) or 630–634 (Rocket).
