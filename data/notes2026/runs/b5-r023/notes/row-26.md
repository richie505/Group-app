=== BharatNet and Rural Connectivity
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **25 Oct 2011** | **NOFN** approved for **2.5 lakh gram panchayats** |
| **25 Feb 2012** | **BBNL** (SPV) registered |
| Feb 2023 | BBNL merged with **BSNL** |
| 22 Feb 2026 | AP MoC with the **Digital Bharat Nidhi Administrator**: **13,426** village panchayats |
- **BBNL mission:** broadband up to **100 Mbps per GP**, scalable to **1 Gbps**; laid using fibre of **BSNL, RailTel, PGCIL**; funded by USOF; GPON technology.
- **Phase III / Amended BharatNet:** **Rs 1.39 lakh crore**; **PPP**; AP SPV = **AP BharatNet Infrastructure Ltd**.
- **APSWAN** = **Andhra Pradesh State Wide Area Network**; **AP FiberNet** reached 4,000+ tribal hamlets (**Eastern Ghats**).
- **USOF:** **non-lapsable** fund for commercially unviable rural telecom; renamed **Digital Bharat Nidhi** (Telecommunications Act 2023). LWE Phase I 2G sites set up by **BSNL**.
- **PM-WANI:** **public Wi-Fi**, December 2020.
~~ Sources: NextGen S&T Group 2 Part 3 p.7-8; app notes 5:26 sub 0-1

- **PMGDISHA:** launched at **Gandhinagar**; **rural**; **6 crore households**, one person each; age **14-60**; closed 31 March 2024 with **4.78 crore** certified.

=== MeghRaj – GI Cloud
- **MeghRaj** = **GI Cloud**, initiated by **DeitY**; common pool of cloud infrastructure and apps on demand.
| Feature | Content |
|---|---|
| **5** characteristics | On-demand self-service, ubiquitous network access, metered use, elasticity, resource pooling |
| 3 service models | IaaS, PaaS, SaaS |
| 4 deployment models | Public, private, hybrid, community (no "satellite cloud") |
- Architecture: national and state clouds + **e-Gov App Store**; **MeitY** empanels private Cloud Service Providers.
~~ Sources: NextGen S&T Group 2 Part 3 p.20-21; app notes 5:26 sub 3
