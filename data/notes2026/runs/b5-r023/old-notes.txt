## SUB 1 | Npit 2012 – Vision and Objectives
[23:1:0] - **Two goals**: bring **ICT within reach of all**; make India the **Global Hub and Destination for IT and ITES Services by 2020**.
[23:1:1] - Context (2011–12): IT = **USD 100 bn** industry; **~80%** of revenue from **exports**; **six hubs** (Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Pune, NCR) ≈ **90%** of industry.
[23:1:2] - Aimed to **spread IT beyond the six hubs** to Tier II and Tier III cities (not concentrate).
[23:1:3] - Objectives: jobs; ICT in education, health, skills, financial inclusion, governance; e-literacy for all.

[24:0:0] # Gartner Four-Phase Model
[24:0:1] - 1. **Information** – putting information online.
[24:0:2] - 2. **Interaction** – two-way communication.
[24:0:3] - 3. **Transaction** – complete services online.
[24:0:4] - 4. **Transformation** – the **final** phase; government processes redesigned.

  [24:0:6.0] G2C / G2B / G2G | Services through SDC + SWAN + CSC

  [24:0:6.3] Open Government Data | data.gov.in

## SUB 1 | NeGP 2006 and E-kranti
[24:1:0] # National e-Governance Plan
[24:1:1] - Framed by **DeitY + DARPG**; approved **18 May 2006**.
[24:1:2] - **27 MMPs** (**9 Central, 11 State, 7 Integrated**) + **10 components**; later expanded to **31 MMPs**.
[24:1:3] - **Vision**: make all government services **accessible to the common man in his locality** through **common service delivery outlets**, with **transparency, reliability and efficiency at affordable cost**.
[24:1:4] - **Integrated MMPs**: **e-Courts** (judiciary ICT, since **2007**), **e-Procurement**, National Portal (**india.gov.in**). State MMP example: **e-Panchayat**.
[24:1:5] # e-Kranti (NeGP 2.0)
[24:1:6] TABLE head: Item | NeGP (1.0) | e-Kranti (NeGP 2.0)
  [24:1:6.0] MMPs | 27 → 31 | 44
  [24:1:6.1] Place | Stand-alone national plan | A pillar of Digital India
  [24:1:6.2] Features | Service outlets | Integrated (cloud, mobile, geospatial), outcome-oriented, fast and agile
[24:1:7] 27 MMPs (9 + 11 + 7) → 31 → 44 under e-Kranti
[24:1:8] APPSC 2025 asked the number of MMPs listed by NeGP 2006: the answer is 27, not 31 or 44.

## SUB 3 | Common Service Centres and Ap Delivery Outlets

[24:3:1] - SPV and implementing agency: **CSC e-Governance Services India Ltd**.
[24:3:2] - Run by **Village Level Entrepreneurs (VLEs)**; reach down to the **Gram Panchayat** level.

[24:3:4] - **APAAR** (Automated Permanent Academic Account Registry): launched **Feb 2024** by Ministry of Education **through CSCs**.

[24:3:8] - Predecessor: **e-Seva (2001)** = **India's first integrated citizen service centres** (bill payments and citizen services at one centre).

[24:3:10] - **10,000+ secretariats**; **500+ services**; **SPANDANA** grievance portal.

## SUB 1 | Aadhaar – Firsts, Format and Scale
[25:1:0] # Firsts

[25:1:2] - UIDAI had planned to issue the first set of unique identity numbers from February 2011; the first number actually came in September 2010.
[25:1:3] # Format and Biometrics
[25:1:4] - **12-digit random number** issued by **UIDAI**; free, lifelong, one enrolment per person.
[25:1:5] - Does not show caste, religion or geography.
[25:1:6] - Biometrics: **10 fingerprints, 2 iris scans, face photograph**.
