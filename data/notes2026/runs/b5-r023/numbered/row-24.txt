## SUB 0 | Andhra Pradesh Digital Governance
[0:0] # Service-delivery chain
[0:1] - **AP chain:** **e-Seva (2001) → MeeSeva (2011) → e-Pragati → RTGS → Mana Mitra (2025)**.
[0:2] - **e-Seva (2001):** **India's first integrated citizen service centres** (bill payments and certificates at one centre).
[0:3] - **MeeSeva** = "**At your service**" (Telugu): launched **4 November 2011** (Chittoor) in undivided AP – **single-window** delivery through MeeSeva centres and authorised agents (**about 11,079** in Jan 2023).
[0:4] - **Legal backing:** **AP IT (Electronic Service Delivery) Rules, 2011** (G.O.Ms.No.10, 18 Oct 2011) – legal sanctity to **digitally signed certificates**; departments to migrate to electronic delivery.
[0:5] - **Gram/Ward Sachivalayam:** launched **2 October 2019** to bring governance to the doorstep; **10,000+** secretariats, **500+** services, **SPANDANA** grievance portal.
[0:6] # e-Pragati and RTGS
[0:7] - **e-Pragati:** **whole-of-government** programme – all e-governance systems **interconnected and integrated**, free flow of information; the digital route to **'Sunrise AP 2022'**.
[0:8] - **Core datasets:** **People Hub, Land Hub, Entity Hub, GIS Hub, IoT Hub** (no Revenue Hub).
[0:9] - **RTGS (Real Time Governance Society):** **Data Lake, Data Lens, Command & Control Centre**.
[0:10] - **Mana Mitra (WhatsApp Governance):** AI-powered, launched **30 January 2025**; **500+** services.
[0:11] # Land, registration and sectoral systems
[0:12] TABLE head: System | Fact
  [0:12.0] CARD (1998) | Computer-aided Administration of Registration Department – computerised document registration
  [0:12.1] BhuSeva + Bhudaar | Land records online; launched 20 Nov 2018; each holding gets an 11-digit Bhudaar number
  [0:12.2] e-Seed (NIC-AP) | Aadhaar-enabled, webland-linked biometric distribution of subsidised groundnut seed – from Kharif 2016 (Anantapur)
  [0:12.3] CFST (Transport) | Online learner licence, tax tokens, transfer of ownership – not utility bills
  [0:12.4] Rajiv Internet Village | Aug 2004; rural kiosks run by women's self-help groups
[0:13] - **Not an AP project:** **e-Mitra** = Rajasthan.
[0:14] > Exam angle: MeeSeva year (2011) ×2, e-Pragati ×2, BhuSeva/Bhudaar ×2, "NOT an AP project" (e-Mitra).

## SUB 1 | NeGP 2006 and e-Kranti
[1:0] # National e-Governance Plan
[1:1] - **E-Governance** = use of computers/ICT in governance to deliver services – the IT-based system of governance.
[1:2] - Framed by **DeitY + DARPG**; approved **18 May 2006**.
[1:3] - **27 MMPs** (**9 Central, 11 State, 7 Integrated**) + **10 components**; later **31 MMPs**.
[1:4] - **Vision:** all government services **accessible to the common man in his locality** through **common service delivery outlets**, with **efficiency, transparency and reliability at affordable costs**.
[1:5] - **Integrated MMPs:** **e-Courts** (judiciary ICT, since **2007**), **e-Procurement**, National Portal. **State MMP:** **e-Panchayat**.
[1:6] - **MMP** = **Mission Mode Project**.
[1:7] TABLE head: Item | NeGP (1.0) | e-Kranti (NeGP 2.0)
  [1:7.0] MMPs | 27 → 31 | 44
  [1:7.1] Place | Stand-alone national plan | A pillar of Digital India
[1:8] > APPSC asked: approval year (2006), vision (locality, affordable cost), MMP count (27), e-Courts (2007).

## SUB 6 | DigiLocker, e-Sign and Secure Messaging
[6:0] - **DigiLocker:** **MeitY**, **February 2015** – secure personal space to **store and share digital documents**, linked to **Aadhaar**; DL and RC linked with MoRTH (Sept 2016); **56.06 crore** users.
[6:1] - **Legal validity:** documents = originals under **IT Act, 2000** + **Digital Locker Rules, 2016** (not the RTI Act).
[6:2] - **e-Sign:** **Aadhaar eKYC-based**; **PKI**, not blockchain; legal backing **IT Act, 2000**; C-DAC's **e-Hastakshar** among issuers.
[6:3] - **GIMS** = **Government Instant Messaging System** – government's WhatsApp alternative, released as **Sandes**; built by **NIC**.
[6:4] - **NIC Mail:** integrated with **CollabFiles** office suite, e-Sign, Bhashini.
[6:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen S&T Group 2 Part 4 p.4; app notes 5:24 sub 5
