## SUB 0 | Four Vedas – Content, Priests, Trayi
[0:0] TABLE head: Veda | Content | Priest | Upaveda
  [0:0.0] Rigveda | Hymns (prayers); oldest / earliest | Hotri (Hota) | Ayurveda
  [0:0.1] Yajurveda | Sacrificial formulas; rites and rituals of yajnas (verse + prose) | Adhvaryu | Dhanurveda
  [0:0.2] Samaveda | Rigvedic hymns set to melody; shortest; origin of Indian music | Udgatri | Gandharvaveda
  [0:0.3] Atharvaveda | Magical charms, spells, healing; latest | Brahman | Shilpaveda (architecture) / Arthashastra
[0:1] - **Samaveda** is **compiled from the Rigveda**: about 1,549 verses (1,875 with repeats), all but about **75** from the Rigveda (mostly Mandalas VIII and IX).
[0:2] - **Atharvaveda**: earliest charms and spells; **Ayurveda ("Science of Life") first appears** here (its prime origin; PMF lists Ayurveda as Upaveda of the Rigveda); term **kshetra-patni ("mistress of the field")** originates here (UPSC 2026); Sabha and Samiti = "twin daughters of **Prajapati**"; mentions Pashupati; recensions **Paippalada, Shaunaka**; reflects non-Aryan beliefs.
[0:3] - **Trayi / Vedatrayi** = **Rig, Yajur, Sama**; Atharvaveda excluded (recognised later).
[0:4] - **Yajurveda** has two branches, **Krishna (Black)** and **Shukla (White)**; first mentions Rajasuya and Vajapeya.
[0:5] - **Upavedas:** Ayurveda – medicine; Dhanurveda – warfare; Gandharvaveda – music; **Shilpaveda – architecture** (not astronomy).
[0:6] - Vedic people were skilled in **astronomy** (Vedanga Jyotisha), **mathematics** (Sulbasutra geometry) and **medicine** (Atharvaveda) – **not chemistry**.
[0:7] > Hotri-Adhvaryu-Udgatri-Brahman = Rig-Yajur-Sama-Atharva; Trayi excludes Atharva.

## SUB 1 | Layers of Vedic Literature – Shruti
[1:0] - Order: **Samhitas → Brahmanas → Aranyakas → Upanishads**; Vedangas, Sutras, Smritis come later.
[1:1] - All are **Shruti** ("heard"), **apaurusheya** (not of human origin); Smriti = remembered (Dharmashastras, epics, Puranas).
[1:2] - **Samhitas + Brahmanas = Karma-kanda**; **Aranyakas + Upanishads = Jnana-kanda**.
[1:3] - **Brahmanas**: prose commentaries that **explain the Vedic hymns** and the meaning of sacrifices (karma-marga; "liturgies").
[1:4] - **Aranyakas**: "**forest texts**", concluding parts of the Brahmanas; symbolic / secret meaning of ritual; bridge karma-marga → jnana-marga. **Atharvaveda has no Aranyaka**.
[1:5] - **Veda Vyasa (Krishna Dvaipayana)** compiled and classified the Vedas. Veda from *vid* = to know.
[1:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 1 p.21-24; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 1 p.143, p.149, p.152; old app notes 1:21
## SUB 2 | Rigveda – Mandalas, Hymns, Gayatri
[2:0] - **10 mandalas, 1,028 hymns (suktas)** = 1,017 + 11 Valakhilya; 10,552 mantras; a **collection of hymns** to nature deities; similar to the Iranian **Avesta**; "First Testament of Mankind".
[2:1] TABLE head: Mandala | Content
  [2:1.0] II–VII | Family books, oldest core (Gritsamada, Vishvamitra, Vamadeva, Atri, Bharadvaja, Vasishtha)
  [2:1.1] III | Gayatri Mantra – composed by Vishvamitra, deity Savitri
  [2:1.2] VII | Battle of Ten Kings (Vasishtha)
  [2:1.3] IX | Wholly Soma (Soma Pavamana, 114 hymns)
  [2:1.4] X | Purusha Sukta (four varnas, origin of caste – first use of "varna" for the four orders), Nasadiya Sukta (creation), Surya Sukta (marriage, X.85)
[2:2] - Word **gotra** first occurs in the **Rigveda**, meaning cow shelter / herd of cows.
[2:3] - **Memory of the World (2007):** **30 Rigveda manuscripts** at the **Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute, Pune** on UNESCO's register; Vedic chanting = UNESCO intangible heritage (2008).
[2:4] - **Script:** the Rigveda was composed and transmitted **orally**; exam keys give **Brahmi** as its basic script (surviving manuscripts are much later, in Devanagari / Sharada).
[2:5] > Gayatri = Mandala III, Vishvamitra; Soma = Mandala IX; Purusha Sukta = Mandala X.
[2:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 1 p.22-23, p.29; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 1 p.145-147; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.1; old app notes 1:21

## SUB 4 | Upanishads and Vedanta
[4:0] - **108** (Muktika list), about **13 principal**; c. 800–600 BCE; **anti-ritualistic**; **Atman = Brahman**; karma, samsara, moksha; called **Vedanta** ("end of the Vedas").
[4:1] - **Prasthanatrayi** = **Upanishads, Brahma Sutra** (Badarayana), **Bhagavad Gita**; the **Bhagavata is not** included. **Shankaracharya** wrote bhashyas on these three – **not on the Rigveda**.
[4:2] TABLE head: Saying / story | Upanishad
  [4:2.0] Satyameva Jayate (national motto, adopted 26 Jan 1950, below the State Emblem) | Mundaka
  [4:2.1] Asato ma sadgamaya; Yajnavalkya with Gargi and Maitreyi; first statement of transmigration | Brihadaranyaka
  [4:2.2] Tat tvam asi; Satyakama Jabala (son of the unmarried Jabala); Uddalaka–Shvetaketu; Krishna, son of Devaki (first mention) | Chandogya
  [4:2.3] Nachiketa–Yama dialogue on death | Katha (Kathopanishad)
  [4:2.4] Trimurti | Maitrayani
  [4:2.5] Name means "white horse"; introduces bhakti | Shvetashvatara
  [4:2.6] Taittiriya Upanishad | Krishna Yajurveda
[4:3] > Satyameva Jayate = Mundaka (not Mandukya); Jabala Upanishad is a different, later text.
[4:4] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 1 p.24-25; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 1 p.149-152; old app notes 1:21

## SUB 6 | Epics, Puranas and Smritis
[6:0] # Mahabharata
[6:1] - By **Vyasa**; original **Jaya / Jayasamhita** (8,800 shlokas) → Bharata → **Shatasahasri Samhita** (~1 lakh verses), **18 books (parvas)**, **Harivamsa** appended; older than the Ramayana; Bhagavad Gita (part of it) in **Sanskrit**.
[6:2] - **Vaisampayana** told it to **Janamejaya**; **Ugrashrava Sauti** narrated it to Shaunaka's sages at **Naimisharanya**.
[6:3] - War at Kurukshetra for **18 days**; Arjuna's grandfather = **Vichitravirya** (Pandu's nominal father); Karna spent Indra's **Vasavi Shakti** on **Ghatotkacha**.
[6:4] # Ramayana
[6:5] - By **Valmiki**: **24,000** verses, 7 kandas, five stages from the 5th century BCE; Rama first met Hanuman in the **Kishkindha Kanda**.
[6:6] - **Janaka**'s brother = **Kushadhvaja** (king of Sankashya); Janaka's family priest-guru = **Shatananda**, son of sage **Gautama** (the Upanishadic Janaka learnt from Yajnavalkya).
[6:7] - **Samudra manthan:** serpent **Vasuki** was the churning rope.
[6:8] # Puranas and Smritis
[6:9] - **18 Mahapuranas**; five marks (**pancha-lakshana**): **Sarga, Pratisarga**, Vamsa, Manvantara, Vamshanucharita. Complete **Dasavatara** narration: **Bhagavata (Maha-bhagavatam)**.
[6:10] - Yugas: **Krita (Satya) → Treta → Dvapara → Kali**.
[6:11] - **Manu** = first law-giver (Manusmriti; disapproves **widow remarriage**); **Mitakshara** on the Yajnavalkya Smriti = **Vijnaneshwara**.
[6:12] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 1 p.34; old app notes 1:21; Valmiki Ramayana Bala Kanda (Shatananda, Kushadhvaja)
