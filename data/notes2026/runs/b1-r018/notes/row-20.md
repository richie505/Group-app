=== Varna Order, Untouchability and Guilds
- Society stratified in the order **Brahmana, Kshatriya, Vaishya, Shudra** = the **Varna system** (varna came to rest on **birth**; spread of jatis is post-Vedic).
| Varna | Role |
|---|---|
| **Brahmana** | Priests, teachers; **most powerful** |
| Kshatriya (**Rajanya**) | Rulers, warriors |
| **Vaishya** | Farmers, herders, traders, artisans; only tribute-payers |
| Shudra | Service; no upanayana, no Vedas, no Sabha |
- First statement of the four varnas: **Purusha Sukta, Rigveda 10th Mandala**; **Dvija** (twice-born) = upper three, entitled to upanayana.
- **Aitareya Brahmana**: Shudra = "**servant of another**", could be expelled or beaten at will.
- Outside the varna order: **Vratyas, Nishadas**.
- Varna + ashrama = **Varnashrama Dharma**.
# Untouchability
- **Chhandogya Upanishad**: the **Chandala** as a person of despised birth – an early sign only; untouchability was absent in the Rigvedic age.
- Untouchability took **clear, formal shape in the Dharmashastra phase** (early Christian era), not in the Rigvedic or Later Vedic phase.
# Guilds and money-lending
- **Sarthavaha** (caravan leader, head of a trade guild / Srenipramukh) = **Vaishya**; guild rules = **Samay, Srenidharma**; Vaishya guilds (gana) headed by a **Shreshthin**.
- First reference to **money-lending**: **Shatapatha Brahmana**; usurer = **kusidin**.

=== Ashramas, Purusharthas and Pancha Mahayajnas
| Ashrama | Duty |
|---|---|
| Brahmacharya | Student; Vedic study |
| **Grihastha** | Householder; daily **Pancha Mahayajnas** |
| Vanaprastha | Gradual withdrawal (forest) |
| Sannyasa | Complete renunciation; added last |
- **Four ashramas** are described; they are the custom that **emerged in the post-Vedic (Later Vedic) period** – the varnas go back to the Rigveda's Purusha Sukta and Indra-Surya-Rudra-Maruts are Rigvedic gods.
- Only the first three were established in Later Vedic times: first three in the **Chhandogya Upanishad**; all four first in the **Jabala Upanishad** (PMF: early Dharmasutras).
- **Pancha Mahayajnas:** **Brahma** (Vedic study), **Deva** (gods), **Pitri** (ancestors), **Bhuta** (living beings), **Nri / Atithi** (hospitality to guests).
- **Purusharthas:** Dharma (duty), Artha (wealth), Kama (desire) = **trivarga**; **Moksha** (liberation) added later under Upanishadic ideas.

=== Gotra and Kinship
- **Gotra** literally = **cow-pen** (word first in the **Rigveda**, meaning cow shelter); as a **clan descended from a common male ancestor** it was established in **Later Vedic (post-Vedic) times**; the Atharvaveda mentions gotra in the lineage sense.
- **Gotra exogamy**: no marriage within the same gotra (with caste endogamy and **sapinda** rules).
- **Kula** = family; **Vamsha** = lineage; **Gotra** = clan; **Kosa = treasury (odd one out)**.
- **Irawati Karve**: *Kinship Organization in India* (1953) – four kinship zones.

=== Samskaras, Upanayana and Education
- **Samskaras = 16** (shodasha): 3 before birth (Garbhadhana, Pumsavana, Simantonnayana), Jatakarma, Chudakarma (tonsure, 3rd year) … Upanayana, Vivaha, Antyeshti.
- **Upanayana** = sacred-thread initiation into Vedic study for the **upper three varnas**, in the boy's **8th year**; full description in the **Shatapatha Brahmana**; **Shudras and women excluded**.
| Varna | Upanayana metre |
|---|---|
| Brahmana | **Gayatri** |
| Kshatriya | **Trishtubh** |
| Vaishya | **Jagati** |
- **Gurukula**: the pupil **lived with the guru**.
| Teacher | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Acharya | Initiated the pupil; taught the whole Veda free |
| **Upadhyaya** | Taught Veda / Vedangas **for a livelihood** |
> Kshatriya = Trishtubh; Upadhyaya paid, Acharya free.

=== Forms of Marriage
| Form | Nature |
|---|---|
| Brahma | Duly dowered daughter given to a Veda-learned man of good conduct |
| **Daiva** | Daughter given to the **officiating priest** as fee |
| **Arsha** | Groom gives a **token cow and bull** |
| **Prajapatya** | No dowry or bride price; couple jointly perform dharma |
| **Asura** | **Marriage by purchase** (bride price) |
| **Gandharva** | **Mutual love / consent** |
| **Rakshasa** | Marriage by **capture** |
| **Paishacha** | Seduction of a sleeping or drunk girl; **lowest** |
- **8 forms** listed by Manu and Kautilya; first four approved.
- **Anuloma** = higher-varna man + lower-varna woman; **Pratiloma** = lower-varna man + higher-varna woman (disapproved).
- **Satavahana Andhra (AP angle):** Gautamiputra Satakarni claimed to protect varnashrama dharma; anuloma offspring = **Ugra**, pratiloma offspring = **Chandala**.
> Asura = purchase; Rakshasa = capture; Gandharva = love.

=== Status of Women
- **Declined**: lost upanayana and political rights (barred from the **Sabha**), excluded from most rituals, could not choose husbands; child marriage appears; symbolic widow self-immolation (sati as a practice is later).
| Text | Statement |
|---|---|
| **Aitareya Brahmana** | Daughter = source of misery; son = protector |
| **Shatapatha Brahmana** | Wife is half her husband |
| Maitrayani Samhita | Liquor, dice and women = three evils |
| **Brihadaranyaka Upanishad** | **Gargi** and **Maitreyi** debated with **Yajnavalkya** in Janaka's court |
- Learned women of Vedic literature: **Gargi, Maitreyi** (Upanishadic); Apala, Ghosha, Lopamudra, Vishvavara (Rigvedic hymn-composers).
