## SUB 4 | Spectrum and the Telecommunications Act 2023
[20:4:0] - **Telecommunications Act 2023**: licensing → **authorisation**; **First Schedule**: terrestrial spectrum **auctioned**, satellite spectrum **assigned administratively**.
[20:4:1] - **TRAI (Feb 2026)**: auction all spectrum across **nine bands**; uniform **35%** cap; new-entrant net worth **Rs 100 cr → Rs 50 cr** per service area.
[20:4:2] - **Supreme Court (14 Feb 2026, Aircel case)**: telcos **do not own spectrum**; IBC cannot rework spectrum rights.
## SUB 5 | Satellite Communication – Geo vs Leo
[20:5:0] TABLE head:  | GEO | LEO
  [20:5:0.0] Altitude | ~35,786 km; fixed over one spot | Below ~2,000 km
  [20:5:0.1] Latency | Higher | Low
  [20:5:0.2] Examples | GSAT series | Starlink, Eutelsat OneWeb, Amazon Kuiper
[20:5:1] - **Starlink** (8,000+ LEO satellites): **GMPCS** licence in India in **2025**; OneWeb (Bharti) earlier got a letter of intent.
[20:5:2] - **GMPCS** = Global Mobile Personal Communication by Satellite (DoT licence); conditions: geofencing, no cross-border spillover, lawful surveillance, terminals **made in India within 5 years**.
[20:5:3] - **ITU**: orbital slots first-come, first-served; de-orbit within **25 years** of mission end.
## SUB 6 | Optical Fibre
[20:6:0] - Works on **total internal reflection (TIR)**; signals travel as **pulses of light**; thin strand of glass (silica) or plastic.
[20:6:1] - **Fastest** medium vs copper, radio, satellite (APPSC 2025); main use: communication; very low power, free from radio-frequency interference.
[20:6:2] - **Single-mode** = long distances; **multi-mode** = short runs.
[20:6:3] - **TIR**: denser → rarer medium, angle of incidence > critical angle; refractive index **Diamond > Glass > Water > Air**. Uses: **endoscope**, glowing **glass beads** on traffic signals.
[20:6:4] # Undersea Cables and National Fibre
[20:6:5] - Undersea network ~**1.5 million km**; carries ~**99%** of international traffic; cheaper, lower latency than satellites.
[20:6:6] - **CANI** (Chennai–Andaman and Nicobar Islands): ~2,300 km; inaugurated **10 Aug 2020**; by BSNL; links Port Blair and seven other islands.
[20:6:7] - **NOFN**: all **2,50,000 Gram Panchayats**; funded by **USOF (not NIIF)**; GPON technology.
[20:6:8] - PM Gati Shakti NMP (2026): ~13.5 lakh route km OFC; 8.26 lakh towers; **PM-WANI 3.15 lakh** hotspots.
[20:6:9] - **BharatNet:** NOFN was later renamed **BharatNet**.
## SUB 7 | Electromagnetic Waves in Communication
[20:7:0] TABLE head: Wave | Wavelength | Use
  [20:7:0.0] Radio | > 1 m (longest) | Radio, TV, mobile, Wi-Fi, MRI
  [20:7:0.1] Microwave | 1 mm–1 m (300 MHz–300 GHz) | Satellite, radar, 5G, point-to-point links
  [20:7:0.2] Infrared | 700 nm–1 mm | Night vision, TV remotes, muscle-pain therapy
  [20:7:0.3] Visible | ~400–700 nm | Li-Fi
  [20:7:0.4] UV / X-rays / Gamma | 10–400 / 0.01–10 / < 0.01 nm | Ionising; not for communication
[20:7:1] - **Energy rises** Radio < Microwave < IR < Visible < UV < X-ray < Gamma; photon energy ∝ frequency (200–280 nm UV highest among options).
[20:7:2] - **Decreasing frequency**: Gamma > X-rays > UV > Radio.
[20:7:3] - Visible: violet shortest, **red longest** (least scattering, used in signals); blue highest energy among colours.
[20:7:4] - **Wavelength** = distance between two successive crests or troughs.
[20:7:5] - **UHF** (300 MHz–3 GHz) travels by **space waves**.
[20:7:6] - **Supersonic waves are not electromagnetic**; radio waves are not bent by electric/magnetic fields.
[20:7:7] - **Sonic boom:** shock-wave bang from an object faster than sound (**supersonic aircraft**) ⚠ APPSC key (2002) gave laser rays.
[20:7:8] - Dish TV lost in rain: raindrops absorb and scatter energy.
[20:7:9] Common TV remote = infrared; one exam key marked radio waves.
## SUB 8 | Li-fi, Wi-fi and Alternative Access
[20:8:0] TABLE head: Li-Fi (Light Fidelity) | Wi-Fi
  [20:8:0.0] Visible light from LEDs; term by Harald Haas (2011) | Radio waves
  [20:8:0.1] More bandwidth, efficiency, security | Longer range; wireless LANs
  [20:8:0.2] Does not pass through walls | Passes through walls
[20:8:1] - **VLC**: ~375–780 nm; not long-range.
[20:8:2] - **Wi-Fi 6 = 802.11ax; Wi-Fi 7 = 802.11be**; Wi-Fi uses 2.4 or 5 GHz.
[20:8:3] - **2.4–2.5 GHz ISM band** (licence-free): Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, cordless phones, microwave ovens.
[20:8:4] - **Bluetooth**: 2.4 GHz; developed **1994** by **Jaap Haartsen**. **NFC**: a few cm.
[20:8:5] - **RFID**: radio tags read wirelessly (Kavach track tags).
[20:8:6] - **FSO**: data by laser beams without fibre; world's first 2 Tbit/s FSO link with small terminals (2026).
[20:8:7] - **Project Loon** (Google/Alphabet): stratospheric helium balloons for internet; shut **January 2021**.
[20:8:8] - **Mumbai Central**: first station with Google free public Wi-Fi (**Jan 2016**).
[20:8:9] - India **49th** in Network Readiness Index 2024 (from 60th).
[20:8:10] - **CCTV** = **Closed-Circuit Television**: cameras send signals to a limited set of monitors, not broadcast openly to the public; used mainly for surveillance and security.

[21:2:0] - Devices networked to **communicate without human intervention**.

[21:2:2] # Three-Layer Architecture
[21:2:3] - 1. **Perception layer**: sensors collect data.
[21:2:4] - 2. **Network layer**: transmits data.
[21:2:5] - 3. **Application layer**: user services.
[21:2:6] Layer order: perception → network → application (not network first).
