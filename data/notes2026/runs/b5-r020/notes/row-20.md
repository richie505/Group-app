=== Electromagnetic Spectrum – Order, Uses and Wave Basics
# Order of the spectrum
| Wave | Wavelength | Frequency | Main uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Radio** | **> 1 m (longest)** | < 3 × 10⁸ Hz (lowest) | AM/FM radio, TV, mobile, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, MRI; **satellite links** (radio family) |
| **Microwave** | **1 mm – 1 m** | 300 MHz – 300 GHz | **Satellite communication, radar**, 5G, point-to-point **telecom** links, ovens |
| **Infrared** | 700 nm – 1 mm | | **TV/AC remotes**, **night vision**, **physiotherapy (muscle pain, paralysis)**, heaters |
| **Visible** | **~400–700 nm** (violet shortest, **red longest**) | | Vision, **Li-Fi/VLC**, optical fibre (some) |
| **Ultraviolet** | 10–400 nm | | **Sterilisation** (UV lamps, semen straws, water), **forgery detection** on currency, vitamin D; **sunscreens block UV** |
| **X-rays** | 0.01–10 nm | | Radiography, CT, baggage scans, cracks in metals |
| **Gamma** | **< 0.01 nm (shortest)** | **highest** | Radiotherapy, sterilising instruments |
- **Energy and frequency rise** Radio < Microwave < IR < Visible < UV < **X-ray** < **Gamma**; photon energy E = hν, so shorter wavelength = more energy (200–280 nm UV beats 280–400 nm; **blue** beats green, yellow, red).
- **Decreasing frequency:** **Gamma > X-rays > UV > Radio**. Longest wavelength: **radio**; smallest among X-ray/visible/IR/microwave: **X-rays**; among radio/visible/IR/UV: **UV**.
- Visible light range: **400–700 nm** (also given as **390–780 nm**; VLC uses 375–780 nm).
- **Visible > X-ray in wavelength; X-ray photons > UV photons > visible photons in energy.**
- **X-rays** are uncharged EM waves: **not deflected** by electric or magnetic fields; high penetration; wavelength far below visible.
- **Red** is used in danger/traffic signals: **longest wavelength**, least scattered, seen from far.
- **TV remote:** common remotes use **infrared** (some use radio). **Telecommunication** links: **microwaves**; **radar**: **microwaves**.
# Wave basics and sound
- **Wavelength** = distance between **two successive crests or troughs**.
- EM waves are transverse and need no medium; **sound** is mechanical and **longitudinal** (radio, X-ray, gamma are EM).
- **Supersonic waves are not electromagnetic.** **Sonic boom** = shock-wave bang from an object faster than sound (**supersonic aircraft**).
- Sound travels **faster in steel (~5,000–6,000 m/s) than in air (~343 m/s)** – a listener at the rail hears a blow twice.
> Exam angle: "which has the highest energy / longest wavelength / decreasing frequency" – about 25 PYQs (UPSC, UPPSC, RAS, NDA, CDS, SSC).

=== Optical Fibre and Total Internal Reflection
# Total internal reflection (TIR)
- Conditions: light goes from **denser → rarer** medium **and** angle of incidence **> critical angle**; light is reflected **with no loss of intensity**.
- Refractive index: **Diamond (~2.42) > sapphire (~1.77) > dense flint glass > glass (~1.5) > fused quartz > water > air** – so TIR is possible **diamond → glass**, **diamond → air**, never air → water/glass.
- Higher refractive index = **slower light** (among glass, water, air, vacuum: slowest in **glass**).
| Phenomenon / device | Principle |
|---|---|
| **Optical fibre** | **TIR** (successive TIRs let light follow a bent, zig-zag path) |
| **Endoscope** | Bundle of optical fibres, **TIR** |
| **Brilliance of diamond** | **TIR** (critical angle ≈ 24.4°) |
| **Mirage** (desert) | **TIR** in hot, rarer air near the ground |
| Glass beads on traffic signs | **TIR** (A and R both true, R explains) |
| Crack in glass seen obliquely | Looks **silvery** (TIR) |
| **Rainbow** | **Dispersion + (total) internal reflection** + refraction |
| Stick bent in water | **Refraction** (not scattering) |
| Sea looks blue | **Reflection of sky + scattering** of light by water |
# Optical fibre communication
- Thin strand of **glass (silica)** or plastic; **core (higher n) + cladding (lower n)**; signal travels as **pulses of light (light waves)**.
- **Fastest** medium vs copper, radio, satellite; main use **communication** (telecom, internet); very **low power**, **free from radio-frequency interference**.
- In India **Sterlite (Sterlite Tech)** makes optical fibre (not Reliance). **Single-mode** = long distances; **multi-mode** = short runs.
# Undersea cables and national fibre
- Undersea cables ~**1.5 million km**, carry ~**99%** of international traffic.
- **CANI** (Chennai–Andaman and Nicobar Islands), ~2,300 km, BSNL, inaugurated **10 Aug 2020**.
- **NOFN** (approved 25 Oct 2011) → **BharatNet**: all **2,50,000 Gram Panchayats**, funded by **USOF** (now Digital Bharat Nidhi), **not NIIF**.
- PM Gati Shakti NMP (2026): ~**13.5 lakh** route km OFC; **PM-WANI 3.15 lakh** hotspots.
> Exam angle: "optical fibre works on…" = TIR – asked about 15 times.

=== Mobile Generations, GSM Family and LTE
# Generations
| Generation | Core technology / feature |
|---|---|
| 1G | Analog, **FDMA** |
| 2G | Digital; **GSM** (TDMA) and **CDMA**; SMS (G = **Generation**) |
| 2.5G / 2.75G | **GPRS / EDGE** (GSM data) |
| 3G | **CDMA-based** (WCDMA/CDMA2000) |
| 4G | **LTE = Long Term Evolution**, OFDMA; **VoLTE** = voice carried over the LTE data network |
| 5G | **NR = New Radio**; latency as low as **1 ms** |
| 6G | **Early 2030s**; "omnipresent wireless intelligence" |
- India's cellular technologies: **GSM and CDMA**.
- **GSM family:** GPRS, EDGE, LTE (UPSC 2010: II, III, IV); **DSL is wired** (telephone line), not GSM.
- UPSC 2019: LTE is **4G** (not "3G"), and VoLTE is not "voice-only" → **neither** statement correct.
- **WLL** = Wireless Local Loop; TRAI prefers **C-V2X** for vehicle communication.

=== 5G in India and the 6G Roadmap
# 5G
- **World's first 5G:** **South Korea, April 2019** (commercial launch 3–5 April 2019).
- **India:** launched by the PM on **1 Oct 2022** at **India Mobile Congress**; **Hyderabad** among the first cities (with Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Bengaluru, Varanasi…).
- **Indigenous 5G Test Bed** (DoT grant, 2018): multi-institute project led by IIT Madras at five locations (IIT Madras, IIT Delhi, IIT Hyderabad, IIT Kanpur, IISc) – true, but it does not explain the launch date.
- **Jio** 5G partners: Meta, Google, Microsoft, Intel, Qualcomm; Jio + Google: affordable 5G smartphones.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| sub-6 GHz | Wider coverage, better penetration |
| **mmWave** | Very high data rates, **short range** |
| **Network slicing** | **One physical network split into several virtual networks** |
| 5G Standalone (SA) | **No 4G core** → lower latency |
| **VoNR** | Voice over New Radio: voice on the 5G network itself (replaces 2G/3G/VoLTE voice) |
- 5G enables **IoT, edge computing, network slicing**, AR/VR, remote surgery.
- **5G & Beyond Hackathon 2023:** announced by **DoT (not DST)** in June 2023; open to individuals, students, start-ups, academia; **100 winners share Rs 1 crore** (Rs 1 lakh each).
- Open-RAN 5G: **Airtel–Tata**; **TTDF** (2022) gets **5% of Digital Bharat Nidhi**; **C-DOT** set up **1984**.
- **AP:** **IIITDM Kurnool** ranked **1st among 100 5G Use Case Labs** (DoT gradation Jan–Jun 2026, all four categories).
# 6G
- **Bharat 6G Vision** (PM, **March 2023**): India to lead 6G design, development and deployment by **2030**; **Bharat 6G Alliance** launched **2023**.

=== Wireless Access – Li-Fi, Wi-Fi, WiMAX, Bluetooth
| Li-Fi (Light Fidelity) | Wi-Fi |
|---|---|
| **Visible light** from LEDs; term by **Harald Haas (2011)** | Radio waves (2.4 / 5 GHz) |
| Faster, more bandwidth, secure, no EM interference | **Longer range** |
| Does **not** pass through walls | Passes through walls |
- **VLC** (UPSC 2020): 375–780 nm, **faster than Bluetooth**, **no EM interference**, but **short-range** (not long-range) → **1, 3, 4**.
- **WiMAX** = World Wide Interoperability for **Microwave Access** (IEEE 802.16), **communication technology**, mainly **2–3.5 GHz**.
- **Bluetooth:** short-range **radio waves**, 2.4 GHz ISM band; secure **Personal Area Networks**; developed **1994** by **Jaap Haartsen**; wireless link between equipment.
- **2.4 GHz ISM band** (licence-free): Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, cordless phones, microwave ovens – **not FM radio**.
- **Wi-Fi 6 = 802.11ax; Wi-Fi 7 = 802.11be.**
- **Short-range devices** (UPSC 2022): **CCTV, RFID and WLAN** – all three. **CCTV** = Closed-Circuit Television. **RFID**: radio tags (Kavach track tags). **FSO**: data by laser beams through the air.
- **Project Loon** (Google/Alphabet): stratospheric helium **balloons** for internet = **wireless communication**; shut Jan 2021.
- **Mumbai Central:** first station with Google free public Wi-Fi (**Jan 2016**). India **49th** in Network Readiness Index 2024 (from 60th).

=== Satellite Communication and Radio Propagation
- Satellite communication works through **transponders** (receive uplink, amplify, retransmit downlink) and uses **microwaves** (radio family; SSC keys say "radio waves").
| Mode | Bands | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ground wave | LF/MF | AM radio |
| **Sky wave** (ionosphere reflects) | **HF 3–30 MHz** | **Shortwave radio – very distant stations** |
| **Space wave** (line of sight) | **VHF, UHF 300 MHz–3 GHz**, microwaves | **UHF = cellular phones** (also TV, GPS); satellites |
- Dish TV fails in rain: raindrops **absorb** and **scatter** the energy; a **small antenna** cannot pick up the weakened signal.
| | GEO | LEO |
|---|---|---|
| Altitude | **~35,786 km** | Below ~**2,000 km** |
| Latency | Higher | Low |
| Examples | **GSAT** | **Starlink, Eutelsat OneWeb (Bharti), Amazon Kuiper** |
- **Starlink** (8,000+ satellites): **GMPCS** (Global Mobile Personal Communication by Satellite) licence **2025**; user terminals **made in India within 5 years**.
- **Telecommunications Act 2023:** licensing → **authorisation**; terrestrial spectrum **auctioned**, satellite spectrum **assigned administratively**. ITU: de-orbit within **25 years**.
- **SREESAT-1** (SNIST Hyderabad students, 2019): **balloon-based** environment monitor (temperature, pressure, humidity, UV/IR) – not a drone or Wi-Fi system.

=== Telecom Bodies, Spectrum and EMF Limits
| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| **TRAI (1997)** | Regulator of telecom and broadcasting |
| **TDSAT (2000 amendment)** | Settles disputes; **hears appeals against TRAI** |
| **C-DOT (1984)** | Government telecom R&D centre |
| **TEC** | DoT's technical arm: standards, equipment testing (**not** USOF collection) |
| **DoT (1985)** | Created when Posts and Telecom split; **MTNL and VSNL 1986** |
| **ITU WTDC** | World Telecommunication Development Conference, **every four years** |
- **EMF limits for towers:** India (from 1 Sept 2012) = **one-tenth of ICNIRP**: **~0.92 W/m² at 1800 MHz** (ICNIRP ~9.2 W/m²). The APPSC key 9.2 W/m² is the ICNIRP value.
- **TRAI (Feb 2026):** auction across nine bands; uniform **35%** cap; new-entrant net worth **Rs 100 cr → Rs 50 cr**. **SC (Aircel, Feb 2026):** telcos **do not own spectrum**.
- **Opensignal 4G speeds:** **Airtel** led 4G **download** speed (2018–20 reports); **Vodafone (Vi)** led **upload** speed.
