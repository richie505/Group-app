=== Internet of Things – Concept, Layers and Devices
- **IoT = Internet of Things:** physical devices with sensors, software and connectivity that **communicate without human intervention** – smart homes, connected appliances, smart mirror, fridge that reorders groceries, car that reroutes (UPSC 2018 scenario).
- **Four pillars:** **device, data, analytics, connectivity**.
# Three-layer architecture
| Layer | Role |
|---|---|
| **Perception** | Sensors collect data at the physical level |
| **Network** | **Transmits** data – the **wireless connection** (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular, RFID) |
| **Application** | User services |
- Order from the physical level up: **Perception → Network → Application**.
| Use | Examples |
|---|---|
| Smart city | Traffic and air-quality monitoring, waste collection |
| Smart agriculture | Precision farming, soil monitoring |
| Wearables | Fitness trackers, health monitors |
| **IoMT** | **Internet of Medical Things**: medical devices linked to health IT systems |
| **IIoT** | Industrial Internet of Things |
# IoT devices and RFID in news
- **AmbiTag** (IIT **Ropar**, AWaDH hub with ScratchNest, 2021): **USB-shaped IoT** data logger recording **ambient temperature** of perishables, vaccines, blood, organs (−40 to +80 °C, 90 days) – **not** IISc.
- **RFID** = **Radio Frequency Identification**: tags + readers over **radio waves**; **active** tags have their own battery and longer range, **passive** tags are powered by the **reader's signal**.
- **RFID Access Control System:** **Paradip Port** was first among major ports (Nov 2016), with **enhanced maritime security** features, as per **Ministry of Shipping** directives – so "Mumbai Port was first" is false.

=== Metaverse, AI Tools and Facial Recognition
- **Metaverse:** a shared immersive **virtual world for people's interaction**; "an **interoperable network of 3D virtual worlds** accessed simultaneously by millions, with **property rights over virtual items**" (UPSC 2024); built on AR and VR.
- **Kiyaverse** (Kiya.ai, June 2022): **India's first banking metaverse**.
- **Facial recognition:** **NAFRS** (National Automated Facial Recognition System) is being built by **NCRB** (MHA), **not NIC**; main concern is **privacy/accuracy**, not "cannot identify in real time" → APPSC 2023: **neither** statement correct.
- **ASTR** (AI and Facial Recognition powered Solution for Telecom SIM Subscriber Verification): developed by the **Department of Telecommunications** to catch SIMs taken on fake documents.
- **Geofencing:** virtual geographic boundary that triggers an action on a device; credited to **Michael Dimino** (Getting, Gladys West, Easton = GPS pioneers).

=== Cloud Computing, Big Data and Edge
- **Cloud computing:** **storing and accessing data and programs over the internet** instead of the computer's hard drive.
| Service model | What is provided |
|---|---|
| IaaS | Infrastructure (computing, storage) |
| **PaaS** | Platform to build and run applications |
| **SaaS** | **Ready-to-use software** – e.g. **Outlook, Hotmail, Yahoo! Mail**; users can **customise the interface** and change data fields; access from **mobile devices** (UPSC 2022: **all three**) |
- **MeghRaj = GI Cloud** (MeitY); NIC national cloud **2014**; empanelment models: Public Cloud, Virtual Private Cloud, **Government Community Cloud**.
- **Big data Vs:** original 3 (2001) **Volume, Velocity, Variety**; later **Veracity** (quality, trustworthiness) and **Value** (usefulness).
- **Edge computing:** processing near the source → low latency; **fog** sits between edge and cloud. Reliance pledged a nationwide edge network (India AI Summit 2026).
- **AP State Data Lake:** links **RCH health with POSHAN**, and **housing with land pattas** to plug leakages.
~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 5 p.67-68, p.100-101; app notes 5:21 sub 5-7

=== Blockchain
- **Features:** decentralised **distributed ledger**, **immutable**, **transparent** (records are not controlled by one body), smart contracts.
- **NFTs** = unique digital assets as tokens; real-world asset tokens (land, bonds).
- **Governance uses:** **land records**, pharma and agri supply chains, **e-Notary (e-Sign)**, certificates, e-voting.

=== 5G, 6G and the Bharat Telecom Stack
- 5G: latency as low as **1 millisecond**; ITU/3GPP use cases **eMBB, URLLC, mMTC** (GPRS is not one).
- **Network slicing** splits one physical network into virtual networks (SDN/NFV) – it does **not merge** them.
- **Bharat Telecom Stack:** **C-DOT** core + **Tejas Networks** radio + **TCS** integration; BSNL; launched by PM **27 Sep 2025**.
- **IIITDM Kurnool** topped DoT's 5G labs ranking (July 2026); Airtel 'Priority Postpaid' raised a **net neutrality** debate.
- **Wi-Fi 7:** IEEE **802.11be**, Multi-Link Operation, up to **46 Gbps**. **6G:** early 2030s; Bharat 6G Alliance **2023**.
