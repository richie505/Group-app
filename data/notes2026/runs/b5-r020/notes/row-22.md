=== Digital India – Vision Areas, Nine Pillars and e-Kranti
- Launched by PM Modi on **1 July 2015**; implemented/coordinated by **MeitY**; umbrella programme to make India a **digitally empowered society and knowledge economy**.
# Three vision areas
| Vision area | Includes |
|---|---|
| **Digital infrastructure as a core utility to every citizen** | High-speed internet, Aadhaar identity, CSC access, cloud locker; **connect villages, Wi-Fi in schools, public places, tourist centres** |
| **Governance and services on demand** | Online/mobile services, single window, cashless transactions |
| **Digital empowerment of citizens** | Universal digital literacy, documents on the cloud, services in Indian languages |
- **NOT a key area / not an aim:** **issue of digital currency**; "digital application for export promotion"; "digital commerce and trade"; forming India's own internet companies "like China"; a framework for overseas MNCs to build data centres (UPSC 2018: **3 only**).
# Nine pillars
| No. | Pillar | Flagship / data |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Broadband Highways | **BharatNet**: 2,18,347 GPs (Mar 2025) |
| 2 | Universal Access to Mobile Connectivity | 4G in villages |

| 4 | e-Governance – reforming government through technology | e-Office |

| 6 | **Information for All** | **Open Government Data** platform |
| 7 | Electronics Manufacturing | Target **net zero imports**; Rs 2.13 lakh cr (2020-21) → **Rs 5.25 lakh cr** (2024-25) |
| 8 | IT for Jobs | IT skills |
| 9 | **Early Harvest Programmes** | **Biometric attendance, Wi-Fi in universities** |
# e-Kranti (NeGP 2.0)
- Approved **2015**; **44 MMPs** (not 31); vision "**Transforming e-Governance for Transforming Governance**".
- APPSC G1 2017: mission for **electronic delivery of all government services** = **e-Kranti**.
- Principles: transformation not translation; integrated services; **cloud by default**, **mobile first** (not paper-first).
- Sister missions: **Make in India (25 Sep 2014)**, **Skill India (15 Jul 2015)**.
- Rural gaps: **GP fibre via BharatNet** and **4G in villages**.

=== E-governance – Concepts, Models and Pillars
- **E-governance** = use of ICT for **technology-driven governance**, aiming to **engage, enable and empower citizens**; improves **transparency**, cuts cost.
- **Four pillars:** **People, Process, Technology, Resources** (MPPSC 2024).
- **Gartner's four phases:** **Information → Interaction (2nd) → Transaction → Transformation (last)** (MPPSC 2025).
| Model | Example |
|---|---|
| G2C | Certificates, DigiLocker |
| **G2B** | **MCA portal (MCA21)**, GSTN |
| **G2G** | **NIC** solutions for departments |
| G2E | Online HR tools |
- **MCA21** (Corporate Affairs) = **e-governance** project for companies (UPSC 2007).

- **NeGP** approved **18 May 2006**: **27 MMPs + 10 components** → 31 → **44 (e-Kranti)**; vision: services accessible to the common man **in his locality**.
| Tier | Component |
|---|---|
| Front end | **CSCs** |
| Network | **SWAN** |
| Back end | **SDC** |
