=== Ghadar Party (1913) – Founding, Leaders and the 1915 Rising
# Basic facts
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Founded** | **1913**, in the **USA** (North America) by Indian immigrants of the US and Canada; the Hindi Association formed at **Portland (Oregon)** in May 1913 became the **Hindustan Ghadar Party** |
| **Headquarters** | **Yugantar Ashram, San Francisco** (not Berlin, Singapore, Tashkent or Vancouver) |
| **First president (chairman)** | **Baba Sohan Singh Bhakna** |
| **General secretary / moving spirit** | **Lala Har Dayal** – the leader and organiser; came to the US in **1911** (lectured at Stanford) |
| Treasurer | **Pandit Kanshi Ram** |
| Members | Mainly **Punjabi Sikh peasants and ex-soldiers** settled on the **Pacific coast** of North America |
| Meaning of "Ghadar" | **Revolt / mutiny** |
| Paper **Ghadar** | A **weekly**, for free circulation; first issue **1 November 1913**, in **Urdu** (Gurmukhi edition a month later); masthead "Angrezi Raj ka Dushman"; poems collected as *Ghadar ki Goonj* |
- **Founder:** books credit **Lala Har Dayal** (organiser, secretary) together with **Sohan Singh Bhakna** (founder-president).
- **Programme:** assassinate officials, publish revolutionary literature, work among Indian troops abroad, procure arms, and bring about a **simultaneous revolt in all British colonies**; ideology strongly **secular**.
# Leaders – who was and who was not
- **Ghadarites:** Lala Har Dayal, Sohan Singh Bhakna, **Ramchandra**, Bhagwan Singh, **Kartar Singh Sarabha** (sub-editor of *Ghadar*), **Barkatullah**, **Bhai Parmanand**, **Rash Behari Bose** (led the 1915 rising).
- **NOT Ghadarites:** **Surya Sen** (Chittagong, 1930), **Khudiram Bose** (Muzaffarpur bomb, 1908), Barindra Kumar Ghosh, Jogesh Chandra Chatterjee.
- **Pre-Ghadar work in North America:** Ramnath Puri, **G.D. Kumar**, **Taraknath Das**, Sohan Singh Bhakna.
| Body | Place |
|---|---|
| **Swadesh Sevak Home** (G.D. Kumar, 1909) | **Vancouver** |
| **United India House** (Taraknath Das and G.D. Kumar, 1910) | **Seattle** |
| **Yugantar Ashram** (Ghadar HQ) | **San Francisco** |
| INA / Azad Hind Fauj (1940s) | Singapore, later **Rangoon** |
# The rising of 1915
- **Triggers (1914):** Har Dayal's arrest (March 1914), the **Komagata Maru** incident and, above all, the **outbreak of the First World War** – "England's difficulty, India's opportunity".
- **21 February 1915:** date fixed for an armed revolt in the **Ferozepur, Lahore and Rawalpindi** garrisons; foiled by **treachery**.
- **Defence of India Act (March 1915)** passed mainly to crush the Ghadar; **45 hanged** (Spectrum; PMF gives 42).
- **Lahore Conspiracy Case (1915):** against the Ghadarites – **Kartar Singh Sarabha** and **Vishnu Ganesh Pingle** executed (16 Nov 1915); Bhai Parmanand arrested; Sohan Singh Bhakna transported for life.
- **Singapore mutiny (15 February 1915):** 5th Light Infantry, inspired by the Ghadar.
> Trap: Ghadar = **USA / North America, 1913, San Francisco**; first president **Bhakna**, leader and organiser **Har Dayal**; the 1915 rising was set off by the **First World War**.

=== India House, London and Revolutionaries in Europe
# Shyamji Krishnavarma – London, 1905
| Body | Founder | Place, year |
|---|---|---|
| **Indian Home Rule Society** | **Shyamji Krishnavarma** | **London, February 1905** |
| **India House** (hostel for Indian students) | **Shyamji Krishnavarma** | **London, 1905** |
| *The Indian Sociologist* (journal) | **Shyamji Krishnavarma** | London, 1905 |
| **Paris Indian Society** | **Madame Bhikaji Cama** (with S.R. Rana) | Paris, 1905 |
- **India House circle:** **Savarkar**, **Har Dayal**, **Madan Lal Dhingra**; Savarkar led it after Krishnavarma left for Paris (1907); it broke up after 1909.
- Not founders of India House: Rash Behari Bose, Taraknath Das, Barkatullah, Virendranath Chattopadhyay, Har Dayal; Annie Besant, Tilak and Gandhi did **not** start the Indian Home Rule Society (Besant and Tilak had the 1916 Home Rule Leagues).
# Madan Lal Dhingra (1909)
- **1 July 1909, London:** shot **Sir William Curzon Wyllie** (political ADC at the India Office) and **Cowasji Lalkaka**.
- **Hanged at Pentonville Prison, London (17 August 1909)** – not life imprisonment.
- **Hanged for killing British officers in England:** **Madan Lal Dhingra** (1909) and **Udham Singh** (Michael O'Dwyer, 1940).
# Madame Bhikaji Cama
- Born to a rich **Parsi** family of Bombay (1861); went to London in 1902; worked as **Dadabhai Naoroji's private secretary**; then based in **Paris** (and Geneva).
- **22 August 1907:** unfurled the Indian flag at the **International Socialist Congress, Stuttgart (Germany)** – the **first woman** (first person) to hoist an Indian flag abroad; not Paris, London or Geneva.
- Brought out **Bande Mataram** from Paris; called the "**Mother of the Indian Revolution**".
- **Prominent revolutionaries abroad:** **Bhikaji Cama, M. Barkatullah, V.V.S. Iyer, M.N. Roy** (UPSC 1994).
> Statement trap (IAS 2006): Cama's flag = **Stuttgart**, not Paris ✗; Naoroji's private secretary ✓; Parsi ✓ → "II and III".

=== Komagata Maru (1914)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| What | A **Japanese steamship** carrying Indian would-be immigrants to **Canada** (not a party, a Chinese village or a leader) |
| Chartered by | **Baba Gurdit Singh**, a Punjabi contractor based in Singapore (misprinted "Gurdip Singh" in UPPCS 2014) |
| Passengers | **376** (mostly Sikhs, also Muslims and Hindus) |
| Route | Left **Hong Kong (4 April 1914)** → Shanghai, Moji, Yokohama → **Vancouver** (arrived 23 May 1914) |
| Why refused | Canada's "**continuous journey**" rule |
| Return | Forced back; reached **Budge Budge near Calcutta** – police firing on **29 September 1914** (Spectrum: 22 died; PMF: 18) |
| Impact | Fury in Punjab; spurred the Ghadar rising |
- **2016:** Canadian PM **Justin Trudeau** apologised in the House of Commons.
> Sequence: **Komagata Maru (1914) → Gandhi's return (9 January 1915) → Tilak's Home Rule League (April 1916)**.
~~ Sources: Spectrum p.321; PMF Modern History Part 4 p.89; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Komagata_Maru_incident; app notes 1:207 (sub 3)

=== Berlin Committee and the Provisional Government at Kabul (1915)
- **Berlin Committee for Indian Independence (1915):** **Virendranath Chattopadhyaya**, **Bhupendranath Dutta**, Lala Har Dayal, with the German foreign office ("Zimmerman Plan"); sought help of **Germany and Turkey**.
- Missions went to **Baghdad, Persia, Turkey and Kabul**.
| Provisional Government of India, **Kabul (Afghanistan)**, 1 December 1915 | Person |
|---|---|
| **President** | **Raja Mahendra Pratap** |
| **Prime Minister** | **Maulana Barkatullah** |
| Member | Obaidullah Sindhi |
- Set up during the **First World War**, with German and Turkish backing and the help of the Afghan crown prince **Amanullah**; it was a **republican government abroad** – not Bose's (Azad Hind, 1943).
- **Period:** December 1915 – the wartime phase of the Ghadar and Berlin efforts, just before the Home Rule Leagues (1916); keys put it in the "Home Rule Movement" period.

=== Andhra Link – Darsi Chenchaiah
- **Darsi (Dasari) Chenchaiah** of **Kanigiri** – the **only Telugu member of the Ghadar Party**; worked for it in America (1912-15); arrested on suspicion; autobiography **Nenu Naa Desam**; later a communist.
- Distractors: Chandra Rajeswara Rao, Jonnalagadda Ramalingaiah, Siva Varma.
- **Sohan Singh Bhakna** attended the **Palasa Kisan Sabha (March 1940)**.
