=== World Economic Forum – Davos Meetings and Initiatives
# The WEF and its venue
- **WEF:** non-profit foundation founded **1971 by Klaus Schwab**; based at **Cologny (Geneva)**; **Annual Meeting** every January at **Davos(-Klosters), Switzerland**.
- **"Summer Davos"** (Annual Meeting of the New Champions) is held in **China** – June 2026 at **Dalian**.
| Annual Meeting | Venue / date | Theme or focus |
|---|---|---|
| 2016 | Davos, Switzerland, January | Mastering the Fourth Industrial Revolution |
| 2017 | Davos | **Responsive and Responsible Leadership** (Xi Jinping attended) |
| 2023 | Davos, **16–20 January** | **Cooperation in a Fragmented World** (IMF's Gita Gopinath only a panellist, not chair) |
| 2025 | Davos | **AI governance and climate change** ("Collaboration for the Intelligent Age") |
| 2026 | Davos, January | Canada PM Carney: "rupture in the world order", call for middle powers; **Davos Compact on AMR** launched |
> "Where was the WEF meeting held?" – always **Switzerland (Davos)**, whatever the year; Summer Davos is the China trap.
# WEF initiatives asked
- **The Great Reset** (June 2020): WEF's initiative for **post-COVID recovery of the global economy** – not World Bank, OECD or UNCTAD.
- **100 Million Farmers**: WEF multistakeholder platform for **net-zero, nature-positive, resilient food and water systems** by 2030 (UPSC 2024).
- **Board of Peace** (WEF Davos, Jan 2026): **27 members**; launched by the **US President** for **Gaza reconstruction**; **India only an observer (no vote)**.

=== G20 – Structure, Summits, India 2023, Johannesburg 2025
# Structure
- Established **1999**; first Leaders' Summit **2008**; members: **19 countries + EU + African Union**.
- Works through **Finance track + Sherpa track**.
- Presidency rotates annually among **five regional groups**; **Troika = past + present + next** presidencies.

=== G7, NATO, Quad and Bilateral Summits
# G7 and NATO
- **G7 members:** USA, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, **Canada** (EU attends); **Netherlands** and **New Zealand** are **not** members.
- **Russia** joined in **1997** (Denver "Summit of the Eight", creating the **G8**; formally a full member **1998**, Birmingham – many books: 1998); suspended in **2014** after Crimea.
- **36th NATO Summit**: **Ankara, Türkiye, 7–8 July 2026**; defence spending **5% of GDP by 2035** = **3.5% core defence** + related spending.
- NATO founded 1949; newest members **Finland (2023)** and **Sweden (2024)**.
