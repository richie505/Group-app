| Feature | Location | Status |
|---|---|---|

| **Narcondam** | Andaman Sea, ~150 km NE of Barren | **Dormant** (PMF: probably extinct); no recorded eruption; Narcondam Hornbill |
| **Baratang** | Middle Andaman | **Mud volcanoes** (and limestone caves); activity 2 Oct 2025 |
| Diglipur | North Andaman | Mud volcano: mud, water, methane |

| Dhosi Hill, Tosham | Haryana | Extinct volcanic hills |

- **India's two volcanic islands:** **Barren and Narcondam** (State PSC).

- **Mud volcanoes** erupt mud, water and gases (methane, CO₂, nitrogen), **not lava**; driven by tectonic pressure on the **Andaman Arc subduction zone**. APPSC 2021: mud volcanoes = **Baratang**; Barren = magmatic.
- **No volcanoes in the Himalaya or the Peninsula** today.

=== Types of Volcanoes – Activity and Form
| By activity | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Active** | Erupts fairly frequently | Barren Island, **Stromboli**, Etna, **Mauna Loa** |
| **Dormant** | Quiet for long, **capable of erupting again** | Narcondam, Kilimanjaro, Mauna Kea |
| **Extinct** | No eruption expected | Dhinodhar, Mount Kenya |
# By form
| Form | Shape | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Shield** | Broad, gentle slopes of fluid basalt | **Mauna Loa**, Hayli Gubbi |
| **Composite / strato** | Steep cone of alternating lava and ash; explosive | Fuji, Vesuvius, St. Helens, Fuego, Taftan |
| **Cinder cone** | Small steep cone of ash, cinders, scoria around one vent | Cerro Negro (Nicaragua) |
| **Complex** | Several vents, overlapping cones | Taal, Dukono |
| **Caldera** | Cauldron-shaped hollow after the chamber empties and the summit collapses | – |
- Crater = funnel-shaped mouth at the top of a cone; caldera = much larger collapse hollow.

=== Magma, Lava and Volcanic Materials
- **Magma** = molten rock **below** the surface (~600–1,200°C, in a magma chamber); **lava** = magma at the surface. "Molten material inside the Earth" = magma → "None of the above" when only lava, basalt, obsidian are offered (UKPSC 2006).
- Solidified magma/lava → **igneous rocks**.
| Acidic (felsic) lava | Basic (mafic) lava |
|---|---|
| **Silica-rich**, viscous; **solidifies faster**, does not flow far | Silica-poor, fluid, flows far |
| Explosive eruptions; steep cones, stratovolcanoes | Quiet outpourings; shield volcanoes, lava plateaus (basalt) |
- **More silica → faster solidification** (APPSC Dental Asst Surgeons).
- **Products of eruptions (UPSC 2024 → all four):** pyroclastic debris, ash and dust, **nitrogen** and **sulphur** compounds; gases = **water vapour (most abundant)** > CO₂ > SO₂. Pyroclasts: ash, lapilli, bombs; pyroclastic flows and **lahars** (mudflows) come from stratovolcanoes.
- SO₂ aerosols scatter sunlight and **cool the Earth for years**; ash falls back within weeks.
- NDA 2016: eruptions are **accompanied by earthquakes** and **emit water vapour and dust** – both true, but II does **not explain** I.

=== Global Distribution of Volcanoes
| Belt | Volcanoes |
|---|---|
| **Circum-Pacific "Ring of Fire"** | **Most eruptions and earthquakes** – Fuji, St. Helens, Cotopaxi, Tungurahua, Fuego, Mayon, Pinatubo, Krakatau, Whakaari, Popocatepetl |
| Mid-Atlantic belt | Iceland – Hekla |
| Mediterranean belt | Vesuvius, Etna, **Stromboli** |
| Intraplate hotspots | Hawaii (Mauna Loa) |
| East African Rift | Kilimanjaro, Afar triple junction |
- **No extrusive volcanoes in the Himalayas** (CDS 2018): continent–continent collision yields no magma; Alaska, Rockies, Andes lie on subduction margins.
- Stromboli = Mediterranean, **not** East African Rift.

=== Volcanic Landforms, Hot Springs and Geysers
- **Volcanic cone = basic (constructional) landform** (UKPSC 2016, asked twice); residual mountain, monadnock, erosional waterfall are erosional forms.
| Intrusive form | Shape / position |
|---|---|
| Batholith | Largest, deep dome-like mass; exposed only after erosion |
| Laccolith | Dome-shaped body fed by a pipe-like conduit from below |
| Lopolith | Saucer-shaped, sagging in the centre |
| Phacolith | Wavy mass at the base of synclines or crest of anticlines |
| Sill / sheet | Near-horizontal along rock layers (thin = sheet) |
| Dyke | Near-vertical wall across layers; feeders of the Deccan Traps |
- **Basaltic lava plateaus** (Deccan) weather into fertile **black soil**.
- **Geysers** = hot springs that erupt periodically. **Yellowstone National Park, USA** (Wyoming; 1872) has the most geysers (500+), including **Old Faithful** (asked twice).
| Hot spring | State |
|---|---|
| Manikaran | Himachal Pradesh (Kullu valley) |
| Tattapani | Chhattisgarh |
| Taptapani | Odisha |
| Vajreshwari | Maharashtra |
| Puga Valley, Chumathang | **Ladakh** (not Himachal) |
- **Puga:** ONGC drilled India's **first and deepest geothermal wells** (to 1,000 m) for a 1 MW pilot. GSI lists **340** geothermal sites.
