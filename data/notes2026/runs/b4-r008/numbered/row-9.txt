## SUB 3 | Magma, Lava and Volcanic Materials
[3:0] - **Magma** = molten rock **below** the surface (~600–1,200°C, in a magma chamber); **lava** = magma at the surface. "Molten material inside the Earth" = magma → "None of the above" when only lava, basalt, obsidian are offered (UKPSC 2006).
[3:1] - Solidified magma/lava → **igneous rocks**.
[3:2] TABLE head: Acidic (felsic) lava | Basic (mafic) lava
  [3:2.0] Silica-rich, viscous; solidifies faster, does not flow far | Silica-poor, fluid, flows far
  [3:2.1] Explosive eruptions; steep cones, stratovolcanoes | Quiet outpourings; shield volcanoes, lava plateaus (basalt)
[3:3] - **More silica → faster solidification** (APPSC Dental Asst Surgeons).
[3:4] - **Products of eruptions (UPSC 2024 → all four):** pyroclastic debris, ash and dust, **nitrogen** and **sulphur** compounds; gases = **water vapour (most abundant)** > CO₂ > SO₂. Pyroclasts: ash, lapilli, bombs; pyroclastic flows and **lahars** (mudflows) come from stratovolcanoes.
[3:5] - SO₂ aerosols scatter sunlight and **cool the Earth for years**; ash falls back within weeks.
[3:6] - NDA 2016: eruptions are **accompanied by earthquakes** and **emit water vapour and dust** – both true, but II does **not explain** I.
[3:7] # Monitoring instruments (APPSC 2018)
[3:8] TABLE head: Instrument | Measures
  [3:8.0] Tiltmeter | Ground tilt over faults and volcanoes
  [3:8.1] Creepmeter | Fault slip
  [3:8.2] Saffir–Simpson scale | Hurricane intensity
  [3:8.3] Anemometer | Wind speed (wind vane gives direction) – the wrong pair

## SUB 5 | Volcanic Landforms, Hot Springs and Geysers
[5:0] - **Volcanic cone = basic (constructional) landform** (UKPSC 2016, asked twice); residual mountain, monadnock, erosional waterfall are erosional forms.
[5:1] TABLE head: Intrusive form | Shape / position
  [5:1.0] Batholith | Largest, deep dome-like mass; exposed only after erosion
  [5:1.1] Laccolith | Dome-shaped body fed by a pipe-like conduit from below
  [5:1.2] Lopolith | Saucer-shaped, sagging in the centre
  [5:1.3] Phacolith | Wavy mass at the base of synclines or crest of anticlines
  [5:1.4] Sill / sheet | Near-horizontal along rock layers (thin = sheet)
  [5:1.5] Dyke | Near-vertical wall across layers; feeders of the Deccan Traps
[5:2] - **Basaltic lava plateaus** (Deccan) weather into fertile **black soil**.
[5:3] - **Geysers** = hot springs that erupt periodically. **Yellowstone National Park, USA** (Wyoming; 1872) has the most geysers (500+), including **Old Faithful** (asked twice).
