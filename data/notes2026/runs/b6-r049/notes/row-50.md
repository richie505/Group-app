# 14th Finance Commission (2015–20)
- Constituted **2 January 2013**; chair **Y.V. Reddy**; members Sushma Nath, M. Govinda Rao, Abhijit Sen, Sudipto Mundle, A.N. Jha.
- Raised States' share of the divisible pool **32% → 42%** – the largest increase ever; accepted 24 February 2015.
- Horizontal formula: **income distance 50%** – the largest weight (the only measure of fiscal capacity).
- **Largest horizontal share: Uttar Pradesh (17.959%)** – the most populous State; AP **4.305%**.
> Exam angle: APPSC Group 2 asked the 14th FC chairman (**Y.V. Reddy**, not Kelkar/N.K. Singh/Gokarn) and the State with the largest share (**Uttar Pradesh**).

=== Finance Commission – Nature and the 16th FC
# Nature of the Body
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Nature | Constitutional, quasi-judicial body |
| Article | Article 280 |
| Appointed by | The President, every 5 years |
| Report made public | When tabled in Parliament (Article 281) |
- **Vertical devolution** = States' share in central taxes; **horizontal devolution** = distribution among States.
> The Finance Commission is **constitutional** (Art. 280) – unlike the Pay Commission or Law Commission, which are set up by executive order.

=== Vertical Devolution and the Divisible Pool
# States' Share
- States' share: **41% of the divisible pool** – **retained** as under the **15th FC**.
- Share **not increased** – the Commission cited the **Union's tight fiscal position**.
# Divisible Pool
- 1. **Gross tax revenue**
- 2. minus **cesses, surcharges** and **cost of collection**
- 3. = **divisible pool**
- 4. **41%** of it goes to the States
- **Shrinking pool**: **89.1%** of gross tax revenue in **2014–15** → about **74–80%** in **2020–24**, due to rising cesses and surcharges.
> 41% = retained (same as 15th FC); 42% = 14th FC (Y.V. Reddy); 32% = before the 14th FC.
- Other 16th FC recommendations: Centre's fiscal deficit **3.5% of GDP by 2030–31**; States **3% of GSDP**; local-body grants about **₹7.91 lakh crore**; **revenue-deficit grants dropped**.
~~ Sources: Rocket CA 2026 Part 01 p.12 (41% retained; FD targets; grants) and p.24; app notes 6:50 (sub 1)

=== Horizontal Devolution Criteria (16th FC)
| Criterion | 16th FC weight | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Income distance | 42.5% | Reduced |
| Population | 17.5% | Increased |
| Demographic performance | 10% | – |
| Area | 10% | Reduced |
| Forest and ecology | 10% | Retained |
| Contribution to GDP | 10% | New parameter |
- A **new parameter** (contribution to GDP) was introduced – it addresses the **southern States' population-control concern**.
- **Gainers**: **Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Telangana**.
- **Andhra Pradesh** had pressed for greater weight to **capital needs** (Feb 2026).
> Area weight was **reduced**; forest and ecology was **retained** at 10%; population was **increased** to 17.5%.

# RBI – State Finances: A Study of Budgets of 2025–26
- Released by the **RBI** in **January 2026**; theme: **demographic transition** and its effect on State finances.
