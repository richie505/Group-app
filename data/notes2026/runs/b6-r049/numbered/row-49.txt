## SUB 1 | Pension Schemes – OPS, NPS and UPS
[1:0] # Comparison
[1:1] TABLE head: Feature | OPS | NPS | UPS
  [1:1.0] Nature | Non-contributory, defined benefit | Contributory, defined contribution | Hybrid – contributory, partially guaranteed (assured payout)
  [1:1.1] Pension | 50% of last basic + DA | Market annuity (market-linked) | 50% of average basic of last 12 months (for 25 years' service)
  [1:1.2] Employee share | None | 10% | 10%
  [1:1.3] Govt share | 100% | 14% | 18.5%
  [1:1.4] Minimum pension | ₹9,000 | None | ₹10,000 (10+ years' service)
  [1:1.5] Family pension | 50–60% | Annuity-based | 60%
  [1:1.6] Period | Till 31 December 2003 | From 1 January 2004 (except armed forces) | From 1 April 2025
[1:2] - NPS regulator: **PFRDA** (PFRDA Act 2013); NPS: **60%** of corpus withdrawn tax-free, **40%** to annuity.
[1:3] - NPS for government employees began in **2004** (not 2005).
[1:4] # Unified Pension Scheme (UPS)
[1:5] - Announced **2024**; from the **T.V. Somanathan Committee**; indexed to **CPI-IW**; an option **under NPS** with assured payout; **effective 1 April 2025**.
[1:6] - Lump sum: **1/10th of monthly pay per 6 months'** service; employee contribution required; **choice irreversible**.
[1:7] - Rules: **CCS (Pension) Rules, 2021**; CCS (Extraordinary Pension) Rules, 2023.
[1:8] - Tax: same **Income Tax Act, 1961** benefits as **NPS** (not OPS).
[1:9] - Uptake: **31,555** employees as of 20 July 2025; last date to enrol **30 September 2025**.
[1:10] - **One-time, one-way switch UPS → NPS** allowed by the Finance Ministry – **not to OPS**; not later than **1 year before superannuation** or **3 months before deemed retirement (VRS)**; irreversible.
[1:11] - States back to OPS: **Rajasthan, Chhattisgarh, Punjab, Himachal Pradesh**.
[1:12] > ₹10,000 minimum = **UPS**, not OPS (OPS minimum = ₹9,000). UPS employee share = **10%**, not 14% (14% = govt share in NPS).
[1:13] - **M.S. Sahoo Committee** (PFRDA, **13 January 2026**, 15 members): guidelines for **assured payouts under NPS**.
