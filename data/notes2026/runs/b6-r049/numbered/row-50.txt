## SUB 2 | Vertical Devolution and the Divisible Pool
[2:0] # States' Share
[2:1] - States' share: **41% of the divisible pool** – **retained** as under the **15th FC**.
[2:2] - Share **not increased** – the Commission cited the **Union's tight fiscal position**.
[2:3] # Divisible Pool
[2:4] - 1. **Gross tax revenue**
[2:5] - 2. minus **cesses, surcharges** and **cost of collection**
[2:6] - 3. = **divisible pool**
[2:7] - 4. **41%** of it goes to the States
[2:8] - **Shrinking pool**: **89.1%** of gross tax revenue in **2014–15** → about **74–80%** in **2020–24**, due to rising cesses and surcharges.
[2:9] > 41% = retained (same as 15th FC); 42% = 14th FC (Y.V. Reddy); 32% = before the 14th FC.
[2:10] - Other 16th FC recommendations: Centre's fiscal deficit **3.5% of GDP by 2030–31**; States **3% of GSDP**; local-body grants about **₹7.91 lakh crore**; **revenue-deficit grants dropped**.
[2:11] ~~ Sources: Rocket CA 2026 Part 01 p.12 (41% retained; FD targets; grants) and p.24; app notes 6:50 (sub 1)
