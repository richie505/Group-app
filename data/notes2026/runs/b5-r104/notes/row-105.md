=== Andhra Pradesh Measures
| Measure | Year | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Green tax** | **Sept 2019** | AP govt (CM Jagan Mohan Reddy, 26 Sep 2019) decided to impose a green tax on **industrial houses** to check pollution, with a ban on single-use plastic |
| **APPCB** | est. **1976** (Water Act 1974) | **Implements environmental laws and rules in AP**; siting guidelines; SOPs for effluents, marine outfalls, hazardous waste; computerised **risk-based** inspections |
| BRAP | 2025-26 | Digital single-window clearances |
| AP National Green Corps | 2002-03 | Eco-clubs of school children |
- **Rushikonda** (Visakhapatnam): AP's only Blue Flag beach (2020); status restored **March 2025**.
- Mandaram Resource Centre (Chittoor district): demonstration centre for alternative agriculture and sustainability with volunteers – the parent project could not be verified (PYQ unscored).
> Exam angle: AP green tax (Sept 2019), body that enforces environmental laws in AP = APPCB.

=== Eco-labels, Blue Flag and Green Credits
| Label | By / year | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Ecomark** | **1991**, Government of India (environment ministry, with BIS standards) | Voluntary label for environment-friendly consumer products |
| Ecomark Rules | 26 Sep 2024; replaced the 1991 scheme | Correct labelling; **stop misleading green claims** |
| **Blue Flag** | **FEE (Foundation for Environmental Education), Denmark** | Beach eco-label (33 criteria) |
| PRAMAAN | – | Voluntary third-party certification for sustainable forestry and agroforestry |
- **Ecomark categories:** soaps and detergents, paper, plastics, **cosmetics, aerosols** – **NOT drugs and antibiotics**.
- **Blue Flag in India:** July 2019 – MoEFCC shortlisted **12 beaches** to apply (incl. Rushikonda, AP); **8** recommended in 2020 and certified; BEAMS programme; Blue Flag is by FEE, not UNEP, MoEFCC or IUCN.
- **Green Credit Programme:** rules notified **Oct 2023**; administrator **ICFRE**; eight activities, **tree plantation** first operational.
> Exam angle: product not under Ecomark, number of beaches set to apply.
~~ Sources: Shankar Environment Part 21 p.11 (Ecomark); NextGen Environment APPSC 12 p.1 (Ecomark PYQ); gktoday.in, 16 Jul 2019 (12 beaches); app notes 5:105 sub 4

=== Air-quality Technologies and Citizen Initiatives
- **WAYU** (Wind Augmentation and Air Purifying Unit): indigenous purifier for traffic junctions – filters PM and removes gases (NO2, SO2, CO, O3) with activated carbon; developed by **CSIR-NEERI** with IIT Bombay, DST-funded – **Ministry of Science and Technology**.
- **Paryavaran Mitra** (Delhi govt, **7 July 2022**, Environment Minister Gopal Rai): citizen-volunteer campaign; registration by missed call.
- Air: NCAP, CAQM, GRAP, **BS-VI from 1 April 2020**; **NAAQS for 12 pollutants**.
- Clean-up biotech: Oilzapper (oil-spill bioremediation).
> Exam angle: ministry behind WAYU, Paryavaran Mitra launch year.

=== Recent Legal Changes
| Measure | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Act | **2023** | Decriminalised minor offences under Air Act and EP Act – jail replaced by monetary penalties |
| Water Act amendment | **2024** | Decriminalised likewise |
| Environmental compensation | Aug 2025 | SC upheld PCB power under **Sec 33A Water Act** and **Sec 31A Air Act** |
| Environmental (Protection) Fund Rules | 2026 | Penalties go to Fund under **Sec 16 EPA**; shared **75% States/UTs : 25% Centre**; audited by **CAG** |
| Consent to Operate | Jan 2026 | Valid **till cancelled** |
- Shift from **command-and-control** to **trust-based, technology-enabled** regulation.
- Revised industry categories: new **Blue** category (e.g. compressed biogas); compulsory **33% green belt** replaced by pollution-potential norms.

=== Alternatives to Conventional Pollution Control and CCUS
| Tool | Type | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Green technology | Alternative | Renewables, efficient kilns, **green cement (Portland limestone cement)** |
| CCS | Alternative | Captures CO2 from **point sources** (e.g. cement plants); largely at research stage |
| Bioremediation | Alternative | Microbes/plants clean water and soil; **cost-effective but slower** |
| Incineration | **Conventional** | Not an alternative |
- **DST R&D Roadmap for CCUS** (Dec 2025): pilots 2025–30 → industrial integration 2030–35 → **commercial scale 2035–45**. DST approved five CCUS test beds (May 2025).
- **NTPC Vindhyachal** pilot captures ~**20 t CO2/day**. Budget 2026 proposed **₹20,000 crore** (over five years) for CCUS.
