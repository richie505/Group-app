## SUB 2 | Air-quality Technologies and Citizen Initiatives
[2:0] - **WAYU** (Wind Augmentation and Air Purifying Unit): indigenous purifier for traffic junctions – filters PM and removes gases (NO2, SO2, CO, O3) with activated carbon; developed by **CSIR-NEERI** with IIT Bombay, DST-funded – **Ministry of Science and Technology**.
[2:1] - **Paryavaran Mitra** (Delhi govt, **7 July 2022**, Environment Minister Gopal Rai): citizen-volunteer campaign; registration by missed call.
[2:2] - Air: NCAP, CAQM, GRAP, **BS-VI from 1 April 2020**; **NAAQS for 12 pollutants**.
[2:3] - Clean-up biotech: Oilzapper (oil-spill bioremediation).
