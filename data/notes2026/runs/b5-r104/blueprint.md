## Trends
- **Definitions dominate row 104.** Blue carbon, carbon footprint and ecological footprint, sequestration and carbon fertilisation are asked as straight recall, and UPSC statement questions (REDD+, FCPF, DAC, basalt/lime) are repeated by state PSCs.
- **APPSC adds "first" and AP items:** Phayeng, Paryavaran Bhawan, Cochin airport, the AP share of forest carbon, the AP green tax and APPCB.
- **Disputed items:** the coal-power "recent step" question (printed key supercritical technology, though the Dec 2015 emission limits are just as valid), and "technology that captures and stores CO2" (key carbon sequestration; strictly CCS). The Mandaram Resource Centre item could not be verified and stays unscored.
- **Row 105 is thin on PYQs (7).** Its own-MCQ content (Jan Vishwas, EP Fund Rules 2026, PARIVESH, schemes, CCUS roadmap) is kept as short tables.
