=== Make in India – Launch, Pillars and Sectors
# Launch
- **Launched:** **25 September 2014** by PM Modi (MPPSC 2020; CDS 2019 – not 2018, not 2012/2016); nodal **Ministry of Commerce and Industry (DPIIT)**, not Heavy Industries or S&T (SSC 2019).
- **Aim:** encourage multinational and domestic companies to manufacture in India; make India a **global manufacturing hub** and **foster innovation**; focus on job creation and skills in **25 key sectors**.
- **Logo:** a **lion** made of cogwheels (UPPSC RO 2014). Links: "Vocal for Local", "Make in India for the World".
- **Central approach:** **attracting FDI and global firms** to manufacture in India; **streamlining regulatory processes** to attract foreign investment and foster innovation – not higher tariffs, subsidies to old sectors or centralised planning (APPSC ASO).
- **Targets (via NMP 2011):** manufacturing **25% of GDP** and **100 million additional jobs** – by 2022, later restated **by 2025** (APPSC JA: target for 2025 = **25%**).
# Four Pillars
| Pillar | Covers |
|---|---|
| **New Processes** | Ease of doing business, single window, e-Biz, faster IPR registration |
| **New Infrastructure** | Industrial corridors and smart cities, world-class infrastructure |
| **New Sectors** | FDI opened in defence, insurance, medical devices, construction, railway infrastructure |
| **New Mindset** | Government as **facilitator, not regulator** |
- The initiative "built on four pillars – New Processes, New Infrastructure, New Sectors, New Mindset" and integrating India into global supply chains = **Make in India** (not Start-up, Digital or Skill India).
- **Strategic components:** skill development, world-class infrastructure, manufacturing hub for domestic and foreign firms – **liberal finance to rural women for new enterprises is NOT one** (APPSC JA).
- **Not part of Make in India (Degree Lecturers 2017 key):** "facilitating easy exit for investors" – EoDB, 100% FDI in new sectors such as defence, and corridors/smart cities are parts. (Faster exit norms belong to the Start-up India action plan.)
# Sectors
| Phase | Sectors |
|---|---|
| Original (2014) | **25 sectors** (APPSC AEE 2016) |
| Make in India 2.0 – Economic Survey 2017-18 | **10 champion sectors** for renewed focus (APPSC AMVI flashcard: **10**) |
| Champion services sectors (Cabinet, Feb 2018) | **12** |
| Make in India 2.0 (now) | **27** = 15 manufacturing (DPIIT) + 12 services (Dept of Commerce) |
- Champion sectors include capital goods, auto, defence and aerospace, pharma, ESDM, leather, textiles, food processing, renewable energy, shipping, railways.
- **Public Procurement (Preference to Make in India) Order:** preference to local suppliers in government purchase.
> Most-asked: launch date (Sept 2014), 25 sectors, four pillars, lion logo, nodal ministry. Trap: "10" champion sectors (ES 2017-18) vs 12 champion services vs 27 sectors now.

=== National Manufacturing Policy 2011 and NIMZs
# NMP 2011
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Released | **4 November 2011** (approved by Cabinet 25 Oct 2011) – "introduced in **2011**" |
| Share of manufacturing in GDP | **16% → 25%** within a decade, i.e. **by 2022** |
| Growth target | **12-14%** per annum over the medium term |
| Jobs | **100 million** additional jobs (by 2022) |
| Competitiveness | enhance **global competitiveness**, domestic value addition and technological depth |
| Infrastructure | on **PPP basis**; skilling of rural youth; sector-, location- and technology-neutral |
- **Objectives (RAS 2013):** 12-14% growth, 100 million jobs, global competitiveness = **1, 2 and 3**; "manufacturing exports to rise 25% a year" is NOT an objective – 25% is the GDP-share target.
- **Present share:** manufacturing still about **16.3-17.5%** of GVA → **25% target missed**.
- **National Manufacturing Mission (Budget 2025-26):** 25% share and **143 million jobs by 2035**.
# NIMZ – National Investment and Manufacturing Zones
- **NIMZ** = large **integrated industrial township** – zoned land use, clean and energy-efficient technology, social infrastructure, skill facilities; the main instrument of NMP 2011.
- NIMZs differ from SEZs in **size, infrastructure planning, governance, exit policy and fiscal incentives**.
- **India's first NIMZ was proposed in Andhra Pradesh** (UPSC 2016). In-principle approval for **Chittoor and Prakasam** (14 Aug 2013); **Prakasam** got final approval (2016).
# National Capital Goods Policy 2016
- Aims to **raise domestic capital goods production**, deepen technology across sub-sectors, a **technology development fund** – **increasing imports of capital goods is NOT a feature** (OPSC 2018).
> NMP year asked 4 times (2011; 4 Nov 2011); target year 2022; NIMZ = AP (Chittoor and Prakasam).

=== Ease of Doing Business – World Bank Report and Reforms
- **Doing Business report:** **World Bank Group** (conceptualised with **IFC**); mainly **guides foreign investors**; **10 parameters** – starting a business, construction permits, getting electricity, registering property, getting credit, protecting minority investors, paying taxes, trading across borders, enforcing contracts, resolving insolvency.

| Edition | India's rank |
|---|---|
| DB 2015 | 142 |
| DB 2018 | **100** – jump of 30 |
| DB 2020 | **63** (last edition) |
- **Discontinued 2021** (data irregularities). Successor **B-READY** – 3 pillars: regulatory framework, public services, operational efficiency.
- Weakest indicators: **enforcing contracts, registering property**.

| Reform | EoDB area |
|---|---|
| **GST (2017)**, faceless assessment, e-filing | Paying taxes, unified market |
| SPICe+ (2020), MCA21 V3 | Starting a business |
| IBC 2016 | Resolving insolvency |
| Online building-plan approvals; land records (DILRMP) | Construction permits; registering property |
- **GST** is the reform credited with improving EoDB (APPSC G2) – "removal of all foreign ownership restrictions", nationalisation of banks and PSU expansion are NOT EoDB reforms.
- **Platforms:** **e-Biz** (Mission Mode Project under NeGP); **National Single Window System** (FIF portal merged Aug 2022); **Invest India** (2009, not-for-profit JV of DIPP, FICCI, CII, NASSCOM and States).
- **Jan Vishwas 2.0 Bill:** 16 Acts, 355 provisions, **288 decriminalised**. Four **labour codes** → formalisation and consolidation, yet **80%+** workers informal. IMD Competitiveness rank **41** (2025).
- **Industrial-development initiatives:** Make in India, Ease of Doing Business, Start-up India – **Digital India is NOT one** (digital infrastructure and services).
> Statement PYQ (APPSC ASO): Make in India = manufacturing hub ✓; Skill India = employability ✓; "success depends solely on the Centre" ✗ → 1 and 3.

=== PLI and Sectoral Schemes
- **PLI (2020):** **14 sectors**, **₹1.97 lakh crore**; incentive on **incremental sales** of goods made in India; sectors include white goods, solar PV modules, specialty steel, textiles, drones – **NOT coal mining equipment**.
- Results (to Sept 2025): investment >₹2.0 lakh crore; **12.60 lakh jobs**.
- **PLI-Auto:** **₹25,938 crore**; **≥50% domestic value addition**.
- **PM E-DRIVE:** **Ministry of Heavy Industries**, 29 Sept 2024, **₹10,900 crore**; replaced **FAME-II**.
- **Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme (FY27-FY31):** **₹62,500 crore**; replaces **PLI for Large Scale Electronics Manufacturing**; up to **1.5%** extra for domestic sourcing.
- **ECMS:** outlay raised to **₹40,000 crore** (Budget 2026-27).

=== Electronics and Semiconductors
- **India = 2nd-largest mobile phone producer** in the world, **after China** (Indian Cellular Association data for 2017; still 2nd in 2023 and 2025-26).
- Mobile output **₹5.45 lakh crore (2024-25)**; **99.2%** of phones used are made in India; **smartphones = largest export category (2025)**.
- **India Semiconductor Mission:** under **Digital India Corporation (MeitY)**; **₹76,000 crore** (2021); **up to 50%** capital assistance; ISM 1.0 = **12 projects**, most in **Gujarat**.

=== SEZ Basics for this Row
- **SEZ Act, 2005** with SEZ Rules in force **10 February 2006**; set up by **Central Govt, State Govt or any person**.
- **Sec 10AA:** **100%** tax exemption on export income for 5 years, **50%** next 5, 50% of ploughed-back profit for 5 more; **sunset** – units operational by **30 June 2020**.
- **DESH** = Development of Enterprise and Service Hubs (proposed successor framework).
