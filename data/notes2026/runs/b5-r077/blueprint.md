## Trends
- **NGT is the core of the topic** (16 of 35 PYQs): the date, the purpose and the HQ repeat in near-identical form across APPSC, UPSC and other state exams. Statement questions turn on two points: the NGT is not bound by the CPC, and it has five sittings, not six.
- APPSC asks **AP-specific institutions** (APPCB offices and labs, APEMC, river water class) and **MoEFCC schemes** (ENVIS, GSDP), material the old notes covered thinly.
- Recent APPSC Mains (2024-25) brought **case law** (Noble M. Paikada) and the **EIA vs LCA** comparison.
- **Outdated risk:** "Who is the NGT Chairperson" (key Swatanter Kumar → now Justice Prakash Shrivastava, 2026).

EIA stages, the 1994/2006/2020 notifications, public-hearing rules, the July 2026 appraisal amendment and the Vanashakti saga. These are now kept compact for the own MCQs. The old notes' "Number game", "PYQ list" and "Traps" pages are dropped; their facts are merged into the content sub-sections.
