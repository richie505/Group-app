=== Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB)
# Set-up and status
- Constituted in **September 1974** under the **Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974** as the Central Board for the Prevention and Control of Water Pollution; renamed **CPCB** in **1988**; the **oldest national pollution-control body**.
- **Air functions** came from the **Air Act 1981**; it gives technical services to the MoEFCC under the EP Act 1986.
- **Statutory body** under the **MoEFCC** (not an executive-order body); HQ **New Delhi**.
- **Chairman:** a **full-time Chairman nominated by the Central Government** (not the President, PM, Vice-President or Minister); Amandeep Garg (Sep 2026).
- Role: **enforces environmental standards** under the EP Act framework; sets standards, monitors, advises the Centre; **nodal agency** for standards and monitoring of waste residues with SPCBs.

| National **AQI** | Launched **2015** |

=== SPCBs and the Andhra Pradesh Pollution Control Board
# SPCBs
- Created under the **Water Act 1974** (with the CPCB); chairperson nominated by the **State Government**; inspect, analyse samples, enforce standards, **grant consents**.
- **Consent to Establish** before setting up; **Consent to Operate** before **starting production or discharge**.
# APPCB
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Set up | **1976** by the Government of AP as the State Board for Prevention and Control of Water Pollution (**Water Act 1974**); later renamed APPCB |
| Offices | Head office **Vijayawada**; **3 Zonal and 13 Regional** offices |
| Laws enforced | Water, Air and EP Acts + waste rules (not the Water Act alone) |
| Lab instruments | UV-Visible spectrometer, specific ion analyser, **Atomic Absorption Spectrophotometer** (heavy metals such as lead, cadmium) – **Davis Instruments Airlinks is NOT** listed |
| River water quality | APPCB rates AP river water **Class 'C'** (usable for drinking only after conventional treatment and disinfection; high coliform – e.g. Nagavali, Vamsadhara) |
# Other AP environment bodies
- **AP Environment Management Corporation (APEMC/APEMCL):** cabinet approval **Nov 2019**; **collection, transport, storage, treatment and disposal of industrial and other waste**, with the APPCB.
- **AP SEIAA:** clears **Category B** projects under EIA 2006.
- **AP State Biodiversity Board:** biodiversity parks on flood plains of **5 polluted rivers**.

=== National Biodiversity Authority and the Biological Diversity Act 2002
- **Biological Diversity Act 2002** (follows the **CBD**): conservation, sustainable use, **access and benefit sharing**; amended **2023**; BD Rules 2024.
- **NBA:** statutory, autonomous body set up in **2003** to implement the Act; HQ **Chennai** (not Bengaluru).
- **Three tiers:** **NBA** (national) → **State Biodiversity Boards** → **Biodiversity Management Committees** (local).
- **People's Biodiversity Register (PBR):** a **record of biodiversity and related knowledge at the local level**, prepared by the BMC with local people.
- **Biopiracy:** foreigners need **NBA approval** to access bio-resources or traditional knowledge; NBA approval **precedes IPR**.
- NBA handles biodiversity, **not NCAP**.
