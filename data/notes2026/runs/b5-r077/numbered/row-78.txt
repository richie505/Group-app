## SUB 1 | SPCBs and the Andhra Pradesh Pollution Control Board
[1:0] # SPCBs
[1:1] - Created under the **Water Act 1974** (with the CPCB); chairperson nominated by the **State Government**; inspect, analyse samples, enforce standards, **grant consents**.
[1:2] - **Consent to Establish** before setting up; **Consent to Operate** before **starting production or discharge**.
[1:3] # APPCB
[1:4] TABLE head: Item | Fact
  [1:4.0] Set up | 1976 by the Government of AP as the State Board for Prevention and Control of Water Pollution (Water Act 1974); later renamed APPCB
  [1:4.1] Offices | Head office Vijayawada; 3 Zonal and 13 Regional offices
  [1:4.2] Laws enforced | Water, Air and EP Acts + waste rules (not the Water Act alone)
  [1:4.3] Lab instruments | UV-Visible spectrometer, specific ion analyser, Atomic Absorption Spectrophotometer (heavy metals such as lead, cadmium) – Davis Instruments Airlinks is NOT listed
  [1:4.4] River water quality | APPCB rates AP river water Class 'C' (usable for drinking only after conventional treatment and disinfection; high coliform – e.g. Nagavali, Vamsadhara)
[1:5] # Other AP environment bodies
[1:6] - **AP Environment Management Corporation (APEMC/APEMCL):** cabinet approval **Nov 2019**; **collection, transport, storage, treatment and disposal of industrial and other waste**, with the APPCB.
[1:7] - **AP SEIAA:** clears **Category B** projects under EIA 2006.
[1:8] - **AP State Biodiversity Board:** biodiversity parks on flood plains of **5 polluted rivers**.

## SUB 2 | National Biodiversity Authority and the Biological Diversity Act 2002
[2:0] - **Biological Diversity Act 2002** (follows the **CBD**): conservation, sustainable use, **access and benefit sharing**; amended **2023**; BD Rules 2024.
[2:1] - **NBA:** statutory, autonomous body set up in **2003** to implement the Act; HQ **Chennai** (not Bengaluru).
[2:2] - **Three tiers:** **NBA** (national) → **State Biodiversity Boards** → **Biodiversity Management Committees** (local).
[2:3] - **People's Biodiversity Register (PBR):** a **record of biodiversity and related knowledge at the local level**, prepared by the BMC with local people.
[2:4] - **Biopiracy:** foreigners need **NBA approval** to access bio-resources or traditional knowledge; NBA approval **precedes IPR**.
[2:5] - NBA handles biodiversity, **not NCAP**.
[2:6] ~~ Sources: Shankar Environment Part 21 p.16-17; NextGen Rocket Environment p.40, p.66; PMF Environment Part 6 p.94; app notes 5:78 sub 6
## SUB 3 | Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB)
[3:0] # Set-up and status
[3:1] - Constituted in **September 1974** under the **Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974** as the Central Board for the Prevention and Control of Water Pollution; renamed **CPCB** in **1988**; the **oldest national pollution-control body**.
[3:2] - **Air functions** came from the **Air Act 1981**; it gives technical services to the MoEFCC under the EP Act 1986.
[3:3] - **Statutory body** under the **MoEFCC** (not an executive-order body); HQ **New Delhi**.
[3:4] - **Chairman:** a **full-time Chairman nominated by the Central Government** (not the President, PM, Vice-President or Minister); Amandeep Garg (Sep 2026).
[3:5] - Role: **enforces environmental standards** under the EP Act framework; sets standards, monitors, advises the Centre; **nodal agency** for standards and monitoring of waste residues with SPCBs.
[3:6] # Functions and programmes
[3:7] TABLE head: Item | Detail
  [3:7.0] Principal functions | Cleanliness of streams and wells (water); improve air quality
  [3:7.1] NAMP | National Air Monitoring Programme – 1,646 stations in 603 cities (March 2026)
  [3:7.2] National AQI | Launched 2015
  [3:7.3] RTWQMS | Real-time water quality of the Ganga and Yamuna
  [3:7.4] NCAP | Tracked on the PRANA portal
