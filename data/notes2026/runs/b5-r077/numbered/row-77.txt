## SUB 0 | National Green Tribunal – Set-up, Purpose and Structure
[0:0] # Set-up (most asked)
[0:1] TABLE head: Event | Date
  [0:1.0] NGT Act passed | 2 June 2010
  [0:1.1] NGT established / Act in force | 18 October 2010 (falls in the 11th Five Year Plan, 2007-12)
  [0:1.2] Started functioning | July 2011
[0:2] - **Full form:** **National Green Tribunal**; created by the **NGT Act 2010** – a **statutory (not constitutional)** body; constitutional anchor **Article 21** (right to a healthy environment), not Article 28.
[0:3] - **Purpose:** **effective and expeditious disposal of cases relating to environmental protection and conservation of forests and other natural resources**, enforcing environmental legal rights and giving **relief and compensation** – i.e. it **handles environmental and pollution-control cases**; monitoring pollution and setting emission/water standards are **CPCB/SPCB** jobs.
[0:4] - India is the **third country** with a specialised environmental tribunal, after **Australia and New Zealand**; NGT reduces the litigation burden on higher courts.
[0:5] - **Procedure:** **not bound by the CPC 1908** (or the Evidence Act); follows **natural justice**; powers of a civil court; applies sustainable development, precautionary and **polluter-pays** principles and **absolute liability** for accidents.
[0:6] - **Time limits:** disposal within **6 months**; appeal to the **Supreme Court within 90 days**.
[0:7] # Composition and Chairpersons
[0:8] - **Members:** Chairperson + **not less than 10 and up to 20** full-time **judicial** members and as many **expert** members (not "5 to 10").
[0:9] - **Chairperson:** must be **a judge of the Supreme Court or a Chief Justice of a High Court** (serving or retired) – an ordinary HC judge does not qualify.

## SUB 2 | EIA – Meaning, Origin and Related Tools
[2:0] # What EIA is
[2:1] - **EIA:** systematic evaluation of the likely environmental impacts of a **proposed project before it is implemented** (studying the environmental effects of a project – not a census, pollution check or plantation); linked with **environmental clearance (EC)**; governed by the **EP Act 1986**.
[2:2] - **Global origin:** **NEPA, USA, 1969**.
[2:3] - **India:** **1976-77** – Planning Commission asked the **DST** to examine **river-valley projects** (first sector); **1978** – EIA first used; until 1994 EC was only an administrative decision.
[2:4] - **R2 Code of Practices is NOT EIA** – it is for **responsible electronics recycling**.
[2:5] # EIA vs related tools
[2:6] TABLE head: Tool | Focus
  [2:6.0] EIA | A specific project, before it starts
  [2:6.1] LCA (Life-Cycle Assessment) | A product or process across its whole life – cradle to grave
  [2:6.2] SEA (Strategic Environmental Assessment) | Policies, plans, programmes – upstream of projects (e.g. a transport policy)
  [2:6.3] Cumulative Impact Assessment | Combined effect of several projects in one area
  [2:6.4] Environmental audit | Compliance of a running unit, after the fact
[2:7] - **Cleaner production:** cuts waste and pollution **at the source** (process change, input substitution) – preventive, not end-of-pipe.
[2:8] - **Green chemistry:** **12 principles – Paul Anastas and John Warner, 1998**.
[2:9] - **Sector-wide EIAs** strengthen **strategic and cumulative** assessment.
[2:10] > Exam angle: EIA = project before start; LCA = product life cycle (APPSC 2025).
