=== NOTES 5:77 | Environmental Impact Assessment and the National Green Tribunal
## SUB 0 | Eia – Meaning and History
[77:0:0] - **EIA**: identifies, predicts and evaluates the likely environmental impacts of a proposed project **before it starts**; linked with environmental clearance (EC).
[77:0:1] - Global origin: **NEPA, USA, 1969**.
[77:0:2] - India: **1976-77** – Planning Commission asked **DST** to examine **river-valley projects** (first sector); **1978** – EIA first used; till 1994, EC was only an administrative decision.
[77:0:3] TABLE head: Year | Milestone
  [77:0:3.0] 27 Jan 1994 | First EIA Notification under EPA
  [77:0:3.1] 1997 | Public hearing made mandatory
  [77:0:3.2] 14 Sep 2006 | EIA Notification 2006 replaced 1994
  [77:0:3.3] 2011 | Amendment on river valley projects
  [77:0:3.4] 2018 | PARIVESH portal
  [77:0:3.5] 2020 | Draft notification – not finalised
[77:0:4] R2 Code of Practices is NOT EIA – it is for responsible electronics recycling.
## SUB 1 | Eia vs Related Tools
[77:1:0] TABLE head: Tool | Focus
  [77:1:0.0] EIA | Specific project before it starts
  [77:1:0.1] LCA | Product/process across its life – cradle to grave
  [77:1:0.2] SEA | Policies, plans, programmes – upstream of projects
  [77:1:0.3] Cumulative Impact Assessment | Combined effect of several projects in one area
  [77:1:0.4] Environmental audit | Compliance of a running unit, after the fact
[77:1:1] - **Cleaner production**: preventive – cuts waste at source (process change, input substitution), not end-of-pipe; APPCB encourages it.
[77:1:2] - **Green chemistry**: designs out hazardous substances; **12 principles – Paul Anastas and John Warner, 1998** (prevent waste, atom economy, safer solvents, renewable feedstocks, design for degradation).
[77:1:3] - Both shrink impacts at design stage, leaving fewer residual impacts for EIA/LCA.
[77:1:4] APPSC 2025: EIA = project; LCA = product life cycle.
## SUB 2 | Eia Process
[77:2:0] TABLE head: Stage | What happens
  [77:2:0.0] Screening (first) | Is EIA needed; which category
  [77:2:0.1] Scoping | Key issues, Terms of Reference
  [77:2:0.2] Baseline data | Existing environment surveyed
  [77:2:0.3] Impact prediction | Alternatives (location, technology) compared; mitigation, EMP, risk assessment
  [77:2:0.4] Public consultation | Hearing + written responses
  [77:2:0.5] Appraisal | Expert committee reviews EIA report
  [77:2:0.6] Decision | EC with conditions or rejection
  [77:2:0.7] Monitoring | EMP implementation checked
## SUB 3 | Eia Notifications 1994, 2006 and Draft 2020
[77:3:0] - **1994**: Schedule I projects needed MoEF clearance; two-stage (site + final) clearance for pithead thermal and valley projects.
[77:3:1] - **2006**: basis shifted from **investment size to impact potential**; EC compulsory for **39 categories**.
[77:3:2] TABLE head: Category | Appraisal | Clearance | Note
  [77:3:2.0] A | EAC (central) | MoEFCC | No screening; full EIA
  [77:3:2.1] B1 | SEAC | SEIAA | EIA needed
  [77:3:2.2] B2 | SEAC | SEIAA | No EIA
[77:3:3] - **Draft EIA 2020** contested provisions: **post-facto EC** on penalty; hearing notice **30 → 20 days**; more projects in B2/exempt; yearly (not half-yearly) compliance reports; violations reportable **only by violator or government**.
## SUB 4 | Public Hearing
[77:4:0] - Conducted by the **SPCB**, **District Collector** presiding; notice at least **30 days**; completed within **45 days**.
[77:4:1] - Exempt: **defence, national-security, strategic** projects (status decided by the **Central Government** – criticised as arbitrary, Jan 2026); **B2** projects; units in notified **industrial estates**.
[77:4:2] - APPCB hearing on a Water Aerodrome at **Prakasam Barrage** – draft EIA called error-ridden (May 2026).
## SUB 5 | Clearance Bodies and Parivesh
[77:5:0] - **PARIVESH** (2018): single-window online portal for environment, forest, wildlife and CRZ clearances.
[77:5:1] - **July 2026 amendment**: SEAC delay over **120 days** escalates via PARIVESH to new **SCEIA**; **SAEIA** stands in for SEIAA (tenure 6 + 6 months); Centre may remove EAC/SEAC/SEIAA members mid-term for delays; Category B no longer defaults to central EAC.
[77:5:2] - Members: **3-4 years**, max two terms, **age limit 70**. Concern: bureaucrats replace scientists.
## SUB 6 | Post-facto Clearance Cases
[77:6:0] TABLE head: Case | Holding
  [77:6:0.0] Common Cause (2017) | Odisha mining beyond EC – pay 100% of illegal mineral value
  [77:6:0.1] Alembic v. Rohit Prajapati (2020) | Ex post facto EC contrary to EIA regime; compensation instead of closure
  [77:6:0.2] Noble M. Paikada (2024) | 2020 exemption for ordinary earth in linear projects – blanket exemption not upheld (APPSC 2024)
  [77:6:0.3] Vanashakti (May 2025) | Retrospective EC a "gross illegality"
  [77:6:0.4] Recall (Nov 2025) | 2:1 majority recalled it
  [77:6:0.5] Final (29 July 2026) | CJI Surya Kant bench: 2021 OM nullified prospectively; earlier ECs survive; must meet Articles 14 and 21; SC keeps Article 142 power
[77:6:1] - S.P. Muthuraman (2025) also held retrospective ECs illegal.
[77:6:2] Prior EC is the rule; post-facto EC only a narrow exception.
[77:6:3] - **Alembic (2020):** the Supreme Court also quashed the **2002 MoEF circular** that had allowed ex post facto clearance.
[77:6:4] - **Vanashakti recall:** the May 2025 ruling had struck down the **2017 Notification** and the **2021 Office Memorandum**; the November 2025 recall (2:1) noted about **Rs 20,000 crore** of projects at stake and relied on the **D. Swamy** and **Pahwa Plastics** judgments.
## SUB 7 | National Green Tribunal
[77:7:0] - **NGT Act 2010**; in force **18 October 2010**; specialised environmental tribunal.
[77:7:1] - Principal seat **New Delhi**; other sittings **Bhopal, Pune, Kolkata, Chennai**.
[77:7:2] - Appeal to the **Supreme Court within 90 days**; aims to dispose of cases within **6 months**.
[77:7:3] - NGT is a **judicial tribunal**; **CPCB** is a **statutory regulator** (Water Act 1974) – NGT can direct it.
[77:7:4] - Predecessors: **National Environment Tribunal Act 1995** (hazardous-substance accidents) and **National Environment Appellate Authority Act 1997** (appeals against clearances) – both **repealed by Section 38** of NGT Act; pending NEAA cases moved to NGT.
[77:7:5] # Landmark NGT Orders
[77:7:6] TABLE head: Order | Direction
  [77:7:6.0] Rat-hole mining, Meghalaya (2014) | Banned
  [77:7:6.1] BRO, Uttarakhand (Aug 2023) | Plant 10,000 trees in one month
  [77:7:6.2] Maharashtra, West Bengal (2022) | Fines for waste mismanagement
  [77:7:6.3] LG Polymers styrene leak, Visakhapatnam (May 2020) | Suo motu; Rs 50 crore interim deposit
  [77:7:6.4] Great Nicobar | High-Powered Committee; EIA criticised for thin baseline data
  [77:7:6.5] Suo motu (15 Jan 2026) | Rajasthan, MP, UP – sewage in drinking water
[77:7:7] - **Objective of NGT:** effective and speedy disposal of cases on environmental protection and conservation of forests and other natural resources, including relief and compensation for damage.
## SUB 8 | Ap Projects and Criticism
[77:8:0] - **Polavaram**: submergence in **Odisha and Chhattisgarh**; **Telangana** sought EIA review in SC (APPSC 2018).
[77:8:1] - **Amaravati**: NGT (**17 Nov 2017**) refused to set aside the 2015 EC; held it **Category B**; added conditions with supervisory and implementation committees.
[77:8:2] - Criticism: EIA by **proponent-paid consultants**; **no central baseline data bank**; weak emergency plans; exempted hearings; post-facto ECs becoming "the design". Fix: **independent EIA authority**; sector-wide (strategic, cumulative) EIAs.
[77:8:3] - **NGT on AP projects (2 Dec 2021):** ordered the AP government to pay about **Rs 243 crore** environmental compensation to the **APPCB** for violations in **Polavaram** (about Rs 120 crore), Pattiseema, Purushottapatnam and Chintalapudi projects; AP appealed in the Supreme Court.
