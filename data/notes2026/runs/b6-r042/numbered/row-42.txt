## SUB 0 | BIS – IS 19445:2025 Bomb Disposal Standard and SHINE
[0:0] # IS 19445:2025 (UPSC CSE Prelims 2026 PYQ)
[0:1] - **What:** India's **first national standard for bomb disposal systems** – "Bomb Disposal Systems – Performance Evaluation and Requirements"; released by Union Consumer Affairs Minister **Pralhad Joshi** around **National Consumer Day, December 2025**.
[0:2] - **Covers:** bomb blankets, baskets and inhibitors – tested against **blast loads and splinter effects**.
[0:3] - **Aim:** uniform testing and procurement → **better interoperability** of equipment across the Armed Forces, CAPFs and State Police.
[0:4] - **Developed by:** **BIS**, at the request of the **MHA** and **TBRL (DRDO)**; TBRL convened the expert panel (Arms and Ammunition for Civilian Use Sectional Committee) – **no foreign (Russian) collaborator**.
[0:5] - **Status:** a **voluntary** standard (mandatory only through a Quality Control Order under the BIS Act, 2016).
[0:6] > Statement trap: "developed with the 30th Central Scientific Research Institute, Russia" is false – answer **1 and 2 only**.
[0:7] # SHINE Scheme – BIS
[0:8] - Launched at the **79th Foundation Day** of the **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)**, New Delhi.
[0:9] - **SHINE = Standards Help Inform & Nurture Empowered Women** – women at the centre of the **quality ecosystem**, via **NGOs and SHGs**.

## SUB 4 | Diseases in News and Adolescent Mental Health
[4:0] # Kyasanur Forest Disease (monkey fever)
[4:1] - News: a **29-year-old man in Karnataka** died of KFD (**January 2026**).
[4:2] - **Tick-borne viral haemorrhagic** disease; first identified **1957**, Kyasanur Forest, Karnataka.
[4:3] - Virus: family Flaviviridae; vector – **hard tick Haemaphysalis spinigera**.
[4:4] - **No human-to-human transmission**; season Oct–Nov, peaks **January–April**.
[4:5] - Fatality **5–10%** (not 20–30%); **no antiviral**; **vaccine available**.
[4:6] # Scabies (WHO)
[4:7] - Contagious **parasitic** skin disease caused by the mite **Sarcoptes scabiei** – **not viral**; itching worse at night.
[4:8] # Adolescent mental health

[4:10] - **National Mental Health Survey:** **7–10%** of adolescents have diagnosable conditions (**not 20%**); 5–7% school children have ADHD.
