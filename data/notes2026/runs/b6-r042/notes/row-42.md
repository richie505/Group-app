=== BIS – IS 19445:2025 Bomb Disposal Standard and SHINE
# IS 19445:2025 (UPSC CSE Prelims 2026 PYQ)
- **What:** India's **first national standard for bomb disposal systems** – "Bomb Disposal Systems – Performance Evaluation and Requirements"; released by Union Consumer Affairs Minister **Pralhad Joshi** around **National Consumer Day, December 2025**.
- **Covers:** bomb blankets, baskets and inhibitors – tested against **blast loads and splinter effects**.
- **Aim:** uniform testing and procurement → **better interoperability** of equipment across the Armed Forces, CAPFs and State Police.
- **Developed by:** **BIS**, at the request of the **MHA** and **TBRL (DRDO)**; TBRL convened the expert panel (Arms and Ammunition for Civilian Use Sectional Committee) – **no foreign (Russian) collaborator**.
- **Status:** a **voluntary** standard (mandatory only through a Quality Control Order under the BIS Act, 2016).
> Statement trap: "developed with the 30th Central Scientific Research Institute, Russia" is false – answer **1 and 2 only**.
# SHINE Scheme – BIS
- Launched at the **79th Foundation Day** of the **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)**, New Delhi.
- **SHINE = Standards Help Inform & Nurture Empowered Women** – women at the centre of the **quality ecosystem**, via **NGOs and SHGs**.

# MGNREGA vs VB-G RAM G
| Feature | MGNREGA | VB-G RAM G |
|---|---|---|
| Guaranteed days | 100 (up to 150 in special cases) | **125** – **Section 5(1)** |
| Wage funding | **Centre 100%** | **60:40**; **90:10** for NE and Himalayan States; 100% Centre for UTs without legislature |
| Allocation | **Bottom-up labour budget** from the Gram Panchayat | **Normative** – set by the Centre |
| Agriculture pause | None | **60 days** in peak sowing/harvest |
| Cards | Job cards valid 5 years | **Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Cards** valid **3 years** |
| Penalty (Section 27) | ₹1,000 | **₹10,000** |
- **Viksit Gram Panchayat Plans:** four areas – **water security**, **core rural infrastructure**, **livelihood infrastructure**, **extreme weather (climate) mitigation**; integrated with **PM Gati Shakti**.
- **Special cards:** single women, PwDs, elderly, released bonded labourers, **PVTGs**, transgender persons.
- **MGNREGA eligibility:** **adult members of any rural household**; average employment about **50 days per household**.

=== Health Programmes – HPV Vaccination and NAP-AMR 2.0
# Free nationwide HPV vaccination for girls
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Launch | **28 February 2026**, **Ajmer (Rajasthan)**; all 36 States/UTs |
| Ministry | **Union Health Ministry** |
| Target | Girls aged **14**; free and voluntary |
| Vaccine | **Gardasil** (quadrivalent) – HPV 16 & 18 (cervical cancer) + HPV 6 & 11 (warts) |
| Dose | **Single dose** (optional second dose after 3–5 years) |
| Procured via | **Gavi, the Vaccine Alliance** |
| Tracked on | **U-WIN** (not CoWIN) |
| Recommended by | **NTAGI** |
| Where given | Only government facilities – Ayushman Arogya Mandirs, CHCs, hospitals, medical colleges |
- Indigenous **Cervavac** is **not yet WHO-approved** for the programme.
- **Cervical cancer:** **2nd most common** cancer among Indian women; about 80,000 cases and 42,000+ deaths a year; HPV 16/18 cause over 80%.
- Vaccine **does not replace screening**: Pap smear from 21 years; HPV DNA test after 30.
- **WHO 90–70–90 targets by 2030:** **90%** girls vaccinated by age **15**; 70% women screened (at 35 and 45); 90% treated.
# National Action Plan on AMR 2.0 (2025–29)
- **NAP-AMR 2.0** for **2025–29**; earlier **NAP-AMR 1.0** in **2017**.
- Approach: **One Health** – human, animal and environmental health interlinked.
- Governance: inter-sectoral supervision under **NITI Aayog** via a **Coordination & Monitoring Committee**; State AMR Cells.
- Only 7 States had NAP 1.0 State plans: Kerala, MP, Delhi, **Andhra Pradesh**, Gujarat, Sikkim, Punjab.
- **Kerala:** **PROUD and AMRITH** initiatives.
- India: **highest MDR-TB burden**; **4th largest** (not largest) consumer of animal antibiotics; **Colistin banned in animal feed**.
- **Musi River (Hyderabad):** antibiotic levels about **1,000×** safe limits; newborns: **50,000+ deaths a year** from resistant sepsis.

=== Diseases in News and Adolescent Mental Health
# Kyasanur Forest Disease (monkey fever)
- News: a **29-year-old man in Karnataka** died of KFD (**January 2026**).
- **Tick-borne viral haemorrhagic** disease; first identified **1957**, Kyasanur Forest, Karnataka.
- Virus: family Flaviviridae; vector – **hard tick Haemaphysalis spinigera**.
- **No human-to-human transmission**; season Oct–Nov, peaks **January–April**.
- Fatality **5–10%** (not 20–30%); **no antiviral**; **vaccine available**.
# Scabies (WHO)
- Contagious **parasitic** skin disease caused by the mite **Sarcoptes scabiei** – **not viral**; itching worse at night.
# Adolescent mental health
- Trigger: deaths of three adolescent girls in Ghaziabad.
- **National Mental Health Survey:** **7–10%** of adolescents have diagnosable conditions (**not 20%**); 5–7% school children have ADHD.
- India has fewer than 10,000 psychiatrists for 1.4 billion+ people.
- **WHO guidelines (2019)** warned **against** excessive screen exposure.
- Response: Economic Survey 2025–26, NMHP, Ayushman Bharat HWCs, Tele-MANAS (**TKDL is not** a mental-health measure).
- Social-media regulation models: **Australia, France, South Korea**.
