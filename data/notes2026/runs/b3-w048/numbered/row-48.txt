## SUB 0 | Fertilisers – Urea, NBS, Neem Coating, Nano and DBT
[0:0] # Urea and the subsidy regime
[0:1] - **Urea:** white, highly water-soluble, **46% nitrogen** (closest exam option: 47%); loses N quickly by **volatilisation and leaching**, hence slow-release forms (**urea briquettes**, neem coating).
[0:2] - **Urea = most subsidised fertiliser**; stays under **statutory price control** (fixed MRP); **not** covered by NBS. Budget 2026-27: urea subsidy Rs 1,16,805 crore vs NBS Rs 54,000 crore.
[0:3] - **Nutrient Based Subsidy (NBS), 2010:** the policy now followed for **P&K fertilisers** – a **fixed per-kg subsidy on N, P, K and S**, announced yearly; covers **22 grades** of decontrolled P&K; **manufacturers set the MRPs**.
[0:4] - Fertiliser subsidy = **second largest subsidy after food**; Budget 2026-27 **Rs 1.71 lakh crore** (RE 2025-26: Rs 1.86 lakh crore).
[0:5] - India: **2nd largest consumer, 3rd largest producer** of fertilisers. National Investment Policy for Urea-2026 adds **10 MT** urea capacity.
[0:6] TABLE head: Reform | Year | Provision
  [0:6.0] NBS | 2010 | Fixed per-nutrient subsidy on P&K (and S)
  [0:6.1] Neem-coated urea | Notified May 2015; all domestic urea from 1 Sep 2015, imported from 1 Dec 2015 | Neem coating slows the release/dissolution of urea (nitrogen) in soil and stops diversion of farm urea to industry
  [0:6.2] DBT in fertilisers | Pilot from October 2016 | Subsidy paid to fertiliser companies after sale at PoS devices (Aadhaar-linked), not to farmers
  [0:6.3] PM-PRANAM | Approved 28 June 2023 | States keep 50% of the subsidy saved by cutting chemical fertiliser use
  [0:6.4] Nano DAP (liquid) | Launched by Amit Shah, April 2023 | IFFCO; 8% N, 16% P
[0:7] - **IFFCO Nano Urea:** one **500 ml bottle = one 45 kg bag**; foliar spray.
[0:8] # Balance and efficiency
[0:9] - **N:P:K = 10.9 : 4.1 : 1 (2023-24)** vs ideal **4:2:1** – the urea-centred subsidy **distorts the NPK ratio**.
[0:10] - **Balanced fertiliser use** = nutrients in the right proportion as per soil test → **corrects all deficiencies** (not just "adds NPK").
[0:11] - **Efficient fertiliser use** = **more crop per kg of fertiliser**; the grain response per kg NPK fell from 13.4 kg (1970) to 3.7 kg (2005).
[0:12] - **Indiscriminate use of urea** is the main human disturbance of the **nitrogen cycle** (nitrate leaching, N2O emission).
[0:13] # Fertiliser types and use by State
[0:14] - Nitrogenous: **ammonium sulphate**, ammonium nitrate, **urea**; phosphatic: **ammonium phosphate (DAP/MAP)**, superphosphate.
[0:15] - **Largest consumer by quantity: Uttar Pradesh**; in 2009 the top two were **UP and (undivided) Andhra Pradesh**; Punjab is high per hectare.

## SUB 2 | Seeds – Seed Village, SRR, GM Crops and Seed Systems
[2:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [2:0.0] Seed Replacement Rate | % of a crop's area sown with certified/quality seed (not farm-saved seed)
  [2:0.1] Varietal Replacement Rate | % of area under varieties released in the last 3-5 years
  [2:0.2] Seed Village | Farmers given foundation seed and trained in quality seed production → produce certified seed locally and make it available to others at the right time and affordable cost
[2:1] - **Seed Village Programme:** ensures timely supply of quality certified seed of notified varieties at farmers' places at affordable prices and quick multiplication of new varieties in the mandal/district; **6.85 lakh seed villages** under the Sub-Mission on Seeds and Planting Materials (ES 2025-26).
[2:2] - **SRR constraint (UPSC 2014):** a **demand-supply gap in quality seed of low-value, high-volume crops**; India **has** a National Seed Policy (2002) and private firms **do** supply vegetable/horticulture seed.
[2:3] - Seed accounts for **20-25%** of productivity; problems: high cost of hybrids for small farmers, shortage of quality seed, unresolved GM seed issues.
[2:4] - **SATHI portal** (Seed Traceability, Authentication and Holistic Inventory): **Phase 1 launched 19 April 2023 by Narendra Singh Tomar** (Union Agriculture Minister).
[2:5] - **Indian Seed Congress 2017** (Kolkata) theme: **"Seed of Joy"**.
[2:6] - ICAR **2019-21: 562** new HYVs; **3,236 HYVs since 2014**; Jan 2026: **184 varieties of 25 crops**.
[2:7] - **Genome-edited rice (May 2025):** **DRR Dhan 100 Kamala** (from **Samba Mahsuri**) + **Pusa DST Rice 1** (from **MTU 1010**); SDN-1 edits **carry no foreign DNA** → outside GMO rules.
[2:8] TABLE head: Act / body | Provision
  [2:8.0] Seeds Act 1966 | Quality of notified seeds
  [2:8.1] PPV&FR Act 2001 | Farmers may save, use, exchange and sell farm-saved seed only unbranded
  [2:8.2] GEAC | Approves GM crops; Rules 1989 under EPA 1986; MoEFCC; cleared GM mustard DMH-11 in 2022
[2:9] - **GM crops with built-in toxins (Bt):** risk of pest resistance and of **encouraging more use of agricultural chemicals**; **Bt cotton** is GM, not a green manure.

## SUB 4 | Soil Health Card, Manures and Organic Farming
[4:0] - **Soil Health Card Scheme:** launched **19 February 2015, Suratgarh (Rajasthan)**; part of NMSA (Soil Health Management); card shows nutrient status and fertiliser advice.
[4:1] - **Interval:** the 2015 scheme said a card **once in every 3 years** (AEE 2016 key); in practice cards are now issued **free every two years** (cycles 2015-17, 2017-19).
[4:2] - Impact: fertiliser use cut **8-10%**; over **25 crore** cards issued.
[4:3] - **Glyricidia = green leaf manure**; green manure crops: **sunn hemp, dhaincha, green gram** (Bt cotton is GM, not a green manure). **Vermicompost:** earthworms turn organic waste into manure.
[4:4] - **Organic farming = eco-friendly:** compost, green manure, bone meal; crop rotation, companion planting (HYV cultivation, glass houses and shifting cultivation are not).

  [4:5.3] NMSA | – | Merged into PM-RKVY from 2022-23; agroforestry "Har Medh Par Ped"

## SUB 5 | Mechanisation and Pesticides
[5:0] - Private investment in agriculture goes mostly to **labour-saving mechanisation** – a response to **rising wages and a tighter labour market** (migration, non-farm jobs).
[5:1] - Mechanisation level: India **40-45%** (below 50% vs >90% in developed countries).
[5:2] - **Zero Till Seed-cum-Fertiliser Drill:** developed at **G.B. Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar**.
[5:3] - **Cage wheel:** fitted to tractors/power tillers for puddling in **wetland (paddy)** fields.
[5:4] - **SMAM** (Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanisation): subsidises machines, **custom hiring centres**, farm machinery banks; 25,689 CHCs (2014-15 to 2025-26).
[5:5] - **Namo Drone Didi:** drones to **women SHGs**; **Rs 1,261 crore** (2023-24 to 2025-26); target **15,000** drones; **80%** central assistance up to Rs 8 lakh.
[5:6] # Pesticides
[5:7] - **Chemical pesticides:** advantage = **high efficacy**, quick action; drawbacks = residues, harm to friendly insects, resistance.
[5:8] - **Biological control** (natural enemies, bio-pesticides such as **neem-based** products): **low cost**, eco-friendly.
[5:9] - **IPM:** biological, cultural and need-based chemical control.
[5:10] TABLE head: Law / event | Provision
  [5:10.0] Insecticides Act 1968 + Rules 1971 | Central Insecticides Board and Registration Committee
  [5:10.1] Draft Pesticides Management Bill 2025 | Replaces the 1968 Act
  [5:10.2] Endosulfan | Banned by Supreme Court, 2011
  [5:10.3] Aldrin (banned) | Killed mugger crocodiles in Kota
[5:11] - India still uses at least **66** pesticides banned elsewhere.

## SUB 7 | Andhra Pradesh – Inputs and Extension
[7:0] - **Tenant farmers' cards:** **Loan Eligibility Cards / Certificates of Cultivation (COC)** earlier; now **Crop Cultivator Rights Cards (CCRCs)** under the **AP Crop Cultivators Rights Act 2019** (11-month agreement; **9,13,283** CCRCs by 3 Jan 2025) – give access to bank credit, insurance, input subsidy.
[7:1] - **D-Krishi:** Aadhaar-enabled **seed** distribution system of AP.
[7:2] - **ATMA in AP:** all **26 districts**, **60:40** funding; disseminates latest technologies and fills adoption gaps through **bottom-up planning (SREP)**.
[7:3] - **APCNF:** run by **Rythu Sadhikara Samstha (RySS)**; **jeevamrutham, ghanajeevamrutham**; **10.20 lakh farmers** (2025-26); **Gulbenkian Prize for Humanity 2024**.
[7:4] - **Rythu Seva Kendras** (formerly Rythu Bharosa Kendras): village one-stop centres for inputs, soil testing, e-Panta, CCRCs, insurance; **8,489** functional after rationalisation; buildings under **MGNREGS**.
[7:5] - Fertiliser consumption **39.39 LMT** (2024-25); 2015-16 actual use **80.78%** of allotment; pesticide use **2,650 MT → 1,716 MT** (2014-15 → 2024-25).
