=== Seals and Sealings – Uses and Value As A Source
- The seal is the **most distinctive artefact** of the IVC (APPSC FRO; APPSC GS 2024) – not megaliths, DNA or royal palaces.
- With the script unread, **seals** are the **main source** of knowledge about the Harappan people (RAS 1996); broadly, the culture is known only from **archaeological excavations** (UPPSC 1993) – no inscriptions read, no religious books.
- A **sealing** is the clay impression of a seal pressed on the knot of a package; an intact sealing proved the goods were untouched and named the sender.
- **Uses:** facilitating **long-distance communication** and trade (APPSC G1 → **seals and sealings**), authentication, **ownership**, amulets – **not coins**.
- **Uniform seals and weights** = main proof of **commercial and economic development** and regulated trade (SSC CPO 2002).

=== Seals – Material and Shape
- Mostly **steatite (soapstone)** – easy to carve; also agate, chert, copper, faience, terracotta, ivory.
- Standard seal: **square** plaque about 2 × 2 inches of steatite.
| Shape | Feature / site |
|---|---|
| Square | Animal motif + inscription |
| Rectangular | Inscription only |
| Mesopotamian-type cylinder | Rare; Mohenjodaro, Kalibangan |
| Button seal, Persian Gulf-type | Lothal |
> Cylinder seals are Mesopotamian-type and rare; the normal Harappan seal is square steatite.

=== Weights and Measures
- Weights mostly **chert** (also limestone, steatite), **cubical** (not spherical), no markings.
| Denomination | System |
|---|---|
| Lower | **Binary**: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 |
| Higher | **Decimal** multiples: 160, 320, 640, 1600, 3200 |
- Unit weight about **13.63 g**; "16" ratio (16 annas = 1 rupee tradition).
- Same weights over about **8,00,000 sq km** → **central regulation** of trade; Dilmun weights followed this standard.
| Scale | Site | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| **Ivory scale** | **Lothal** (APPSC G2 2011) | Smallest division ~1.70 mm |
| Shell scale | Mohenjodaro | ~6.7 mm |
| Copper scale | Harappa | – |
- Foot ≈ 37.6 cm; cubit 51.8–53.6 cm. Ivory weight balance at Mohenjodaro and Lothal.
> Ivory = Lothal; shell = Mohenjodaro; copper = Harappa.

=== Harappan Script – Features
| Feature | Fact |
|---|---|
| Nature | Mostly **pictographic** (not alphabetic); fish = commonest sign |
| Direction | **Right to left**; some **boustrophedon** (Kalibangan) |
| Number of signs | About 375–400 (estimates 62 to 676) |
| Average / longest inscription | About **5** / **26** signs |
| Corpus | About **5,000** inscribed objects (≈3,500 seals) |
| Found on | Seals, tablets, pottery, copper tools, bone rods, jewellery, a signboard |
| Status | **Undeciphered** |
- First noticed 1853; seals with unknown symbols reported by **J. Fleet, 1912**.
- Unread because **no bilingual text** (like the **Rosetta Stone**) has been found and the language is unknown.
- **Dholavira signboard:** **ten large signs** near the **North Gate** – largest Harappan letters; Dholavira dug by **R.S. Bisht, 1990**.
