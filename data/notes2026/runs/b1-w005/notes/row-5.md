=== Discovery and Excavators
- **1826**: **Charles Masson** described the Harappa ruins (1826-38). **1853, 1856**: **Alexander Cunningham** saw pottery, tools and a seal but missed their value.
- **1857**: Harappan bricks used as ballast for the **Karachi–Lahore** railway. **1912**: **J. Fleet** published Harappan seals.
- **Sir John Marshall** (DG, ASI 1902–28) directed the 1921-22 campaign; announced the civilisation in 1924; coined "Indus Valley Civilisation".
- The two Indian discoverers: **Daya Ram Sahni** and **Rakhaldas Banerji**.

=== Basic Facts – Bronze Age, Area, Rivers, Non-Aryan Character
- **Proto-historic** (script undeciphered), **Bronze Age**; India's **first urbanisation**.
- Area about **1.3 million sq km** – larger than Egypt and Mesopotamia; population ~5 million; matured in **Sindh and Punjab** and spread **south and east**.
- Highest concentration of sites on the **Ghaggar–Hakra** system (about 250 sites, 6 cities).
- **Rivers:** Sindhu, **Chenab, Jhelum**, Ravi, Sutlej, Ghaggar – up to the **Hindon** (Alamgirpur); **not the Ganga**.
- **Main sites:** Mohenjodaro, Harappa, Ropar, Kalibangan; roads, houses and drains were planned.
- **Metals:** copper, bronze, gold, **silver** (first use of silver in India), lead, tin – but **no iron**.
- **Non-Aryan:** Harappan culture was **urban**; the Rigvedic Aryans were **pastoral/rural**.
| Period | Economy |
|---|---|
| Indus Valley | Urban |
| Rigvedic | Pastoral |
| Later Vedic | Agrarian |
- **Not Harappan:** **Sohgaura** (Gorakhpur – Mauryan copper plate), **Pataliputra** (Magadha capital), Mehrgarh (Neolithic), Burzahom (Neolithic pit dwellings).
- Assertion–reason: Mohenjodaro and Harappa are "dead" cities **and** were found by excavation – both true, but R does not explain A.

=== Names, Meluhha and Contemporary Civilisations
| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|

| Dilmun | Bahrain (intermediate station) |
| Magan (Makan) | Makran coast / Oman |

| Kalibangan | "Black bangles" |

- **Harappan Civilisation** is preferred: named by Marshall after **Harappa**, the first site found; the culture went beyond the Indus valley. "Sindhu-Sarasvati" stresses the Ghaggar-Hakra sites.
- **Bronze Age river civilisations:** Mesopotamia – Tigris and Euphrates; **Egypt – Nile**; China – Hwang Ho; Harappa – Indus.
- **First proper writing:** **Sumerian cuneiform** (Mesopotamia); Egypt – hieroglyphics; the Indus script is undeciphered.
- **Makli Necropolis** (Thatta, Sindh) – one of the world's largest necropolises (UNESCO) – is not Mohenjodaro.

=== Largest Sites, UNESCO and Recent Developments
- **Rakhigarhi** (Hisar, Haryana; ~550 ha) = **largest Harappan site in India**; **Dholavira** second; **Mohenjodaro** largest in Pakistan. Five largest: Harappa, Mohenjodaro, Ganweriwala, Rakhigarhi, Dholavira.
- **Dholavira:** UNESCO WHS **2021** – India's **40th**, Gujarat's 4th, the **first IVC site in India**; absence of encroachment helped.
- **Mohenjodaro:** UNESCO WHS since **1980**. **Rakhigarhi:** on the UNESCO **Tentative List** (Jun 2026).
- **Union Budget 2020-21:** **five** iconic archaeological sites – **Rakhigarhi, Hastinapur, Shivsagar, Dholavira, Adichanallur**.
- Jun 2026: Haryana protected **Tighrana and Mitathal** (Bhiwani) under the **Haryana Ancient and Historical Monuments Act, 1964**.
- 23 Jun 2026: ASI transferred Rakhigarhi's **56 skeletons** to the **Anthropological Survey of India**; a 4,500-year-old skeleton gave the ancient DNA study.

=== Chronology and Phases
| Phase | Dates | Features |
|---|---|---|
| Early Harappan | 3300–2600 BCE | Kot Diji, Amri, Sothi; villages |
| Mature Harappan | 2600–1900 BCE | Planned cities, 1:2:4 bricks, script |
| Late Harappan | 1900–1300 BCE | De-urbanisation; OCP, Cemetery-H |
- **John Marshall:** 3250–2750 BCE by **Mesopotamian cross-dating**; **D. P. Agrawal** (radiocarbon): **2500–1750 BCE**.
- **Dholavira:** inhabited c. **3000–1800 BCE** in **seven stages** (R. S. Bisht); UNESCO: 3rd to mid-2nd millennium BCE.
