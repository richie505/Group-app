# 5th Asian Para Games 2026 - AP athletes
- **5th Asian Para Games**: **Aichi-Nagoya, Japan**, **18-24 October 2026**; organised by the **Asian Paralympic Committee (APC)**; held **every four years**.
- **[7 Nov 2026] PM Narendra Modi felicitated** the Indian contingent: **97 para-athletes** won **114 medals** (**30 gold, 35 silver, 49 bronze**).
- Background: grew out of the **FESPIC Games (from 1975)**; **first Asian Para Games: Guangzhou, China, 2010**.

| **M.V. Murali Krishna** | **Visakhapatnam** | **Coach**, Indian **para-badminton** team |

# Swachh Survekshan 2025-26 - six awards for AP
- **[11 Nov 2026]** **President Droupadi Murmu** presented the **Swachh Survekshan awards** at **Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi**; AP Municipal Minister **Dr P. Narayana** received AP's awards.
- **AP won 6 awards** - **Greater Visakhapatnam (GVMC), Vijayawada, Guntur, Tirupati, Rajamahendravaram** Municipal Corporations and **Kuppam Municipality**.
- **Vijayawada**: among the **top three Clean Cities of the Super Swachh League** (with **Navi Mumbai** and **Surat**).
- **Tirupati**: **Super Swachh League** award for the **fourth straight year**; **Tirupati (Mentor City) - Kuppam (Mentee City)** among the **top three Swachh Shehar Jodis**.
- **Kuppam**: **Swachh City** in towns **below 20,000 population**; **Rajamahendravaram**: **Promising Swachh Shehar**.
- Survey: **10th edition** (2025-26), theme **"Swachhata Ki Nayi Pehel - Badhayein Haath, Karein Safai Saath"**; launched **2016** by **MoHUA** under **SBM-Urban**.
# Rankings in which AP leads
- **[10 Nov 2026] Access (In)Equality Index (AEI) 2026**: **AP 2nd** (score **0.66**) after **Goa (0.68)**; **Sikkim 3rd**; AP a **"Front Runner"** State.
  - AP **1st** in the **socio-economic security** dimension (**0.69**, Goa 0.50) - though AP is **13th in per capita income**.
  - Index by **Centre for New Economic Studies (CNES), O.P. Jindal Global University**; started **2021**; **5 dimensions** (basic infrastructure, healthcare, education, socio-economic security, access to justice), **60 indicators**, **23 categories**, equal weights.
- **[10 Nov 2026] Ease of Vehicle Ownership Index (EVOI)**: **AP 1st** for smooth **used-vehicle ownership transfer**, then **Kerala, Tamil Nadu**, Delhi 4th; **Uttar Pradesh last**; by **IndiaTech.org** with **Consumer VOICE**.
- **[4 Nov 2026] National SARAS Mela 2026 sales**: total business **₹12.84 crore**; **AP 1st** with **₹2.99 crore (~23%)**, **UP 2nd (₹1.22 crore)**; venue **Punnami Ghat, Vijayawada**; the **first National SARAS Mela was in 1999 (Delhi)**.
- **[4 Nov 2026] QS World University Rankings: Asia 2027**: **SRM University-AP, Amaravati** - **1st among AP universities** (govt and private); top 100 in Southern Asia. QS Asia rankings since **2009** (Quacquarelli Symonds, UK).
> ⚠ Trap: AEI 2026 topper is **Goa**, not AP (AP 2nd); in EVOI AP is **1st**.
# Heritage in news
- **Tirumala inscriptions of Achyutadevaraya** (ASI, **2 and 5 Nov 2026**), both in **Tamil**, on the **third prakaram** of the **Sri Venkateswara temple**:
  - **South wall** (2 Nov): endowment for **Perarulalayyan** (an **Ekangi Srivaishnava**); deposit of **675 panam** in the **bhandaram** (treasury); **1 panam = 0.36 g of gold**; mentions **Brahmotsavam**, **Uri-adi** (Krishna Janmashtami) and **Adhyayanotsavam**; villages called **Tiruvitaiyata-urkal**.
  - **North wall** (5 Nov): grant to **Dalavay Timmarasayyar**, son of Pradhani Somarasayyar, a military commander at **Chandragiri fort**; **150 rekhai-pon** and a deposit of **15,000 narpanam**.
- **Achyutadevaraya**: **Tuluva dynasty**, ruled **1529-1542**; succeeded his half-brother **Krishnadevaraya**; crowned first at **Tirupati**, then at Vijayanagara; court poet **Rajanatha Dindima** wrote **Achyutarayabhyudayam**.
- **[7 Nov 2026] Motupalli** ("Marco Polo's port of call"), **Bapatla district**; old name **Desiyakkondapattana**; Kakatiya **Ganapatideva's Abhaya Shasanam** (charter of security to merchants) **1244-45 CE**; **Anavota Reddy's** inscription **1358 CE**; Marco Polo's **"Mutfili"** (identified by **Henry Yule**), ruled by a queen identified with **Rudrama Devi**.
- **Dutch in Andhra** (7 Nov 2026): **VOC founded 1602**; first important Dutch factory in Andhra at **Masulipatnam (1605)**; also **Bheemunipatnam**, **Nizampatnam (Pettapoli)**, **Palakollu**, **Draksharamam**, **Jagannathapuram**.
- **[10 Nov 2026] C.P. Brown birth anniversary** commemorated by **CM N. Chandrababu Naidu**: born **10 Nov 1798, Calcutta**; Madras Civil Service **1817**; Deputy Collector **Kadapa (1820)**; **"A Dictionary, Telugu and English" (1852)**; collected and published **Vemana's** poems; called the **Father of Modern Telugu Literature** and **"Restless Pandit"**; **C.P. Brown Library, Kadapa**; died **12 Dec 1884, London**.
> ⚠ Trap: both Tirumala inscriptions are in **Tamil**, not Telugu or Kannada; Abhaya Shasanam at Motupalli is **Kakatiya (Ganapatideva)**, not Reddy.
