=== National Highways, Expressways and Railway Zone
# Highways
- NH projects worth **more than ₹1.40 lakh crore** under way, to finish **by 2029** (30 Jan 2026); **64 NH projects** worth over **₹1 lakh crore** (May 2026).
- **Buttaigudem-Kovvur**: two-laning, **45 km**, **₹840 crore** (Aug 2026).
- **Port link**: **5-km four-lane road** from **Kakinada deep-sea port** to **NH-216**.
- **13 July 2023:** **Nitin Gadkari** laid foundation stones for **three NH projects** (87 km, about ₹2,900 crore) at **Tirupati** – incl. the **Thamminapatnam–Narikellapalle** section (16 km, ₹610 crore; extension of the dedicated port road from **Eupuru to Krishnapatnam Port**, NH-516W/NH-67), Naidupeta–Turpu Kanupur (NH-71) and Chillakuru Cross–Krishnapatnam Port South Gate.
- **Greenfield expressways:** **Raipur-Visakhapatnam** and **Nagpur-Vijayawada**; other corridor: **Kharagpur-Visakhapatnam-Amaravati**.
# South Coast Railway zone
- Gazette notification (May 2026); **headquarters Visakhapatnam**; operational from **1 June 2026**; India's **18th railway zone**.
- Four divisions: **Guntakal, Guntur, Vijayawada** and **Visakhapatnam** (renamed Waltair division).
- **Vijayawada railway station:** **IGBC** (Indian Green Building Council) Green Railway Station **Platinum** rating – not **LEED**.
- **Origin:** **AP Reorganisation Act 2014, Thirteenth Schedule** (Infrastructure, item 8) – Indian Railways to **examine** a new railway zone for AP within six months; zone announced **27 Feb 2019**; **PM Narendra Modi** laid the foundation stone of the SCoR HQ (about ₹149 crore, Mudasarlova, **Visakhapatnam**) on **8 Jan 2025**.

=== Power Distribution Reforms – RDSS and Farm-power DBT
- **RDSS** (Revamped Distribution Sector Scheme): launched **2021**, outlay **₹3.03 lakh crore**.
- About **₹10,000 crore** for **rural power networks** (17 Sep 2026).
- **State Distribution Reforms Committee** (chaired by the **Chief Secretary**) cleared **₹522 crore** of **loss-reduction works** for **Godavari Pushkaralu 2027**.
- **DBT of free-power subsidy (agricultural consumers):** piloted in **Srikakulam district** from **September 2020** – meters on farm pump sets; the subsidy is credited to farmers' accounts and paid on to the DISCOM (**APEPDCL**); statewide rollout planned from 1 April 2021.
