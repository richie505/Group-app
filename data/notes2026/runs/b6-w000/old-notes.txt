## SUB 0 | P4 Zero Poverty, NTR Bharosa Pensions and TTD Insurance
[0:0:0] # P4 – Public-Private-People Partnership (APPSC FSO PYQ)
[0:0:1] - **P4 = Public-Private-People Partnership**; launched by CM Naidu and Dy CM Pawan Kalyan at **Velagapudi, Amaravati** on **Ugadi, 30 March 2025** (a Sunday) – not Independence Day, Republic Day or Diwali.
[0:0:2] - Rolled out statewide from **19 August 2025**.
[0:0:3] - **Model:** the **top 10%** of society adopt the **bottom 20%**; mentors/donors = **Margadarsis**, beneficiary families = **Bangaru Kutumbams** (golden families); voluntary help (aid, mentoring), not a cash-transfer scheme.
[0:0:4] - About **19.16 lakh** Bangaru families identified.
[0:0:5] - **First anniversary (30 Mar 2026, Tirupati):** **1.06 lakh Margadarsis** had adopted 10.3 lakh families; goal restated as a **poverty-free society by 2047** (earlier: zero poverty by 2029).

[0:0:7] - **NTR Bharosa** = AP's social security pensions; about **₹76,000 crore** disbursed by October 2026.
[0:0:8] - **October 2026:** **₹2,692.34 crore** released; door-to-door distribution from 1 October 2026.
