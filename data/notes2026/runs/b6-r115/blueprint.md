## 3. Angles
- **"Who is the present / new …?"** – about 60% of the PYQs. Almost all are time-bound: 51 carry an `outdated` line with today's holder. Rule: key the holder **at the exam date**, learn the current holder from the "Holder now" columns.
- **Person → country / field / organisation** (identity): about 30% (obituaries, founders, "belongs to which country").
- **Match the following** (heads ↔ posts, PMs ↔ countries, CMs ↔ States): 9 PYQs (2016 service chiefs, 2018 NDRF/DCGI/ICHR/VSSC, 2019 BSF/CSIR/ITBP, 2022 IMF ED/DRDO, Feb 2020 PMs).
- **Number / ordinal:** Nth term (Gayoom, Netanyahu 6th), 23rd CAS (Browne), 44th US President, 104th Archbishop, 14th Israeli PM.
- **Statements / NOT true:** Kuldip Nayar, Moynihan.
- **Current linkage:** the old PYQs sit next to the 2026 holder of the same post (CAS Dixit 29th, COAS Dhiraj Seth 31st, CNS Swaminathan 27th, CBFC Vempati, CBSE Bhandari, UK PM Burnham).
- **Repeats:** EAC-PM chairman (3), UGC chairman (2), Defence Minister Sitharaman (2), Tata Sons Chandrasekaran (2), Paul Boateng (2), Qaboos of Oman (2), Marrero Cruz (2), Forbes Most Powerful Women (5), Sanna Marin (2), Mauritius (3).

## 4. Heavy in the old notes but never asked
- UN Secretary-General straw-poll rules, CEC selection panel, Lord's honours board, Forbes billionaires 2026 – asked only in the app's own MCQs; kept short.
- Long "Number game" and "MCQ traps" lists repeated table facts – dropped.

## 5. Settled conflicts
- **K.M. Mani vs Ganpatrao Deshmukh (50 years as legislator):** Mani completed 50 years on **15 March 2017** (IANS); Deshmukh's golden jubilee was **2012** – the old notes and one own MCQ said 2017 for Deshmukh (corrected).
- **Sudarsan Pattnaik:** the old notes said Berlin **2010**; his World Champion title at Berlin was **2008** (2010 was a People's Choice prize).
- **Navy chief "now":** earlier explanations said Dinesh K. Tripathi; since 31 May 2026 it is **Krishna Swaminathan** (Praveen CA-360 May 2026 p.72).
