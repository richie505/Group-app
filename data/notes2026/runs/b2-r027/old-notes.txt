=== NOTES 2:27 | Vice-president; Prime Minister and the Union Council of Ministers – Articles 74, 75, 77, 78
## SUB 0 | Vice-president – Office and Election
[27:0:0] # Office (Articles 63, 64, 79, 89)
[27:0:1] - **Article 63**: **second-highest office**; modelled on the **US Vice-President**.
[27:0:2] - **Articles 64 and 89(1)**: VP is **ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha**; has only a **casting vote** in a tie.
[27:0:3] - VP is **not a member of either House** – RS is chaired by a non-member.
[27:0:4] - **Article 79**: Parliament = **President + Council of States + House of the People**; the VP is **not** a part of Parliament.
[27:0:5] - Does not preside while a resolution for **his own removal** is under consideration.
[27:0:6] - Ex officio **President of ICWA** (Indian Council of World Affairs).
[27:0:7] # Election (Articles 66, 71)
[27:0:8] - Elected (not appointed) by **members of both Houses** – **elected and nominated MPs** (788 when full); **State Legislatures have no role**.
[27:0:9] - **PR-STV**, **secret ballot**; Presidential and Vice-Presidential Elections Act, 1952.
[27:0:10] - **Article 71**: all disputes decided by the **Supreme Court** – final; acts done before a void election is declared remain valid.
[27:0:11] - **39th Amendment (1975)** removed the SC's power over election disputes of President, VP, PM and Speaker; **44th Amendment (1978)** restored it.
[27:0:12] State Legislative Assemblies vote in electing the **President, Rajya Sabha members and Legislative Council members** – not the VP.
## SUB 1 | Vice-president – Qualifications, Term, Removal, Acting President
[27:1:0] # Qualifications and term (Article 67)
[27:1:1] - Citizen; **35 years**; qualified for election to the **Rajya Sabha** (not Lok Sabha); no office of profit.
[27:1:2] - Term **5 years**; continues till successor takes charge (Art 67(c)); **re-election any number of times**.
[27:1:3] - Oath by the **President** (Art 69).
[27:1:4] # Removal (Article 67(b))
[27:1:5] - Resolution moved **only in the Rajya Sabha** by a **majority of all the then members**, **agreed to by the Lok Sabha**; **14 days' notice**; **no grounds** stated; no impeachment.
[27:1:6] TABLE head: Point | President | Vice-President
  [27:1:6.0] Qualified to be member of | Lok Sabha | Rajya Sabha
  [27:1:6.1] Resigns to | Vice-President (Art 56(1)(a)) | President (Art 67(a))
  [27:1:6.2] Removal | Impeachment (Art 61), two-thirds, 14 days' notice | RS resolution + LS agrees
  [27:1:6.3] Vacancy filled | Within 6 months (Art 62) | "As soon as possible" – no deadline (Art 68)
[27:1:7] - On the President's resignation the VP immediately informs the **Speaker** (Art 56(2)).
[27:1:8] # Acting President (Article 65)
[27:1:9] - On vacancy (death, resignation, removal) or the President's inability; **maximum 6 months**.
[27:1:10] - While acting: **ceases to do Chairman's duties** – the **Deputy Chairman** presides; draws the **President's salary**, not the Chairman's.

## SUB 3 | Prime Minister – Appointment, Oath, Membership
[27:3:0] # Appointment (Article 75(1))
[27:3:1] - **Appointed by the President**; by **convention** the leader of the **LS majority**. Other Ministers on the **PM's advice**; PM has **full discretion** in choosing them. ⚠ APPSC key (2002) gave "PM is elected by the Parliament"; under Art 75(1) the President appoints the PM.
[27:3:2] - Oath by the **President** (Art 75(4)).
[27:3:3] - May be from **either House**; need not be an MP when appointed – must become one within **six months** (Art 75(5)).
[27:3:4] - **UK** PM must belong to the House of Commons; **Indian** PM – either House.
[27:3:5] - **Article 53(1)**: executive power in the **President** (nominal, de jure); real executive = **PM** (de facto). The President "**represents the nation but does not lead the nation**".
[27:3:6] - Art 54 = President's election; 75 = PM and Ministers; 155 = Governor; 164 = CM and State Ministers.
[27:3:7] # Tenure and responsibility
[27:3:8] - **No fixed tenure**; formally at the President's pleasure, in practice while he has the **Lok Sabha's confidence**.
[27:3:9] - PM resigning on losing majority (e.g. **Budget defeated** in LS) is a **convention**, not written in the Constitution.
## SUB 4 | Prime Minister – Powers, Functions, Bodies Chaired
[27:4:0] - Selects Ministers, **allocates portfolios**, can ask any Minister to resign; advises on **summoning/prorogation** and **dissolution of LS** – not on the CJI (collegium).
[27:4:1] - **Article 78** (not 74) – PM's duties to the President (see section 6).
[27:4:2] - Administering ministers' oaths is **not** an Article 78 duty.

[27:4:4] - **Gandhi Peace Prize** (started **1995**, Gandhi's 125th birth anniversary): jury of **five** incl. **PM, CJI, Leader of Opposition in LS** – not the President.

## SUB 6 | Council of Ministers – Responsibility and Non-members
[27:6:0] # Collective and individual responsibility
[27:6:1] - **Article 75(3)**: Council **collectively responsible to the House of the People** only – even if the PM or Ministers are RS members.
[27:6:2] - **Individual responsibility**: Ministers hold office at the **President's pleasure** (Art 75(2)) – not legally the PM's; President removes a Minister only on PM's advice.
[27:6:3] # Non-member Ministers (Article 75(5))
[27:6:4] - Any person (incl. PM) may be a Minister; a non-member for **six consecutive months** ceases to be one.
[27:6:5] - **S.R. Chaudhuri v. State of Punjab (2001)**: a non-member **cannot be re-appointed repeatedly** without getting elected.
[27:6:6] # Ministers in Parliament (Article 88)
[27:6:7] - A Minister may **speak** in both Houses and **joint sittings**, but **votes only** in his own House (an RS Minister or RS PM cannot vote in the Lok Sabha).
[27:6:8] - **24 Jul 2026**: MoS **Ravneet Singh Bittu's** resignation accepted "under **Article 75(2)**" after his RS term ended.
[27:6:9] - Giriraj Singh (Begusarai) – Union **Textiles** Minister, 18th LS.
## SUB 7 | Size of the Council of Ministers – 91ST Amendment, 2003
[27:7:0] - Before 2003 the number of Ministers was **not in the Constitution**.
[27:7:1] - **Article 75(1A)**: Ministers incl. PM **not more than 15%** of LS total strength (543 → max **81**).
[27:7:2] - **Article 164(1A)** (States): 15% of Assembly incl. CM; **minimum 12**. West Bengal (294): 15% = 44.1 → **max 44**.
[27:7:3] - Aims: limit ministry size; strengthen **anti-defection law**.
[27:7:4] The APPSC key gave the "97th Amendment" for limiting cabinet size; the 97th Amendment (2011) dealt with **cooperative societies** – the size cap is the **91st (2003)**.
[27:7:5] - **130th Constitution Amendment Bill, 2025**: PM/CM/Minister detained **30 consecutive days** for an offence punishable with **5+ years** is removed on the **31st day**; touches **Articles 75, 164, 239AA**.

[27:8:0] # Council vs Cabinet

[27:8:2] - **Cabinet Ministers** head major ministries, attend Cabinet; **MoS (independent charge)** own ministry; **MoS** attached; **Deputy Ministers** only assist.
[27:8:3] - The Constitution **does not classify** Ministers into ranks – **convention** and PM's choice.
[27:8:4] - Word **"Cabinet"** not in the original Constitution; inserted in **Article 352(3)** by the **44th Amendment, 1978** (Cabinet's written advice for Emergency).
