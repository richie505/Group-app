=== Right to Privacy – Article 21 and Puttaswamy
# Justice K.S. Puttaswamy (Retd) v Union of India
- **Decided 24 Aug 2017** by a **9-judge** bench, **unanimous**: right to privacy is **intrinsic to life and personal liberty under Article 21** and part of the freedoms of **Part III** – a **Fundamental Right**.
- So **Article 21** secures **life, personal liberty and (read in) privacy**; not Art. 14, 15, 17, 19, 20, 22, 23, 24, 29, and not the DPSPs or any amendment.
- Petition filed by **Justice K.S. Puttaswamy (Retd)**, a former Karnataka High Court judge, against Aadhaar.
- Overruled **M.P. Sharma (1954)** and **Kharak Singh (1962)** insofar as they denied privacy.
- **Three-fold test:** **legality** (a law is needed; a public aim alone is not enough), **legitimate aim**, **proportionality**.
| Case | Point |
|---|---|
| **Gobind v State of MP (1975)** | Privacy part of personal liberty |
| **PUCL v UoI (1997)** | Safeguards on **telephone interception** |
| Puttaswamy (2017) | Privacy = Fundamental Right (Art. 21 + Part III) |
> Asked 6 times (APPSC AEE 2023, Welfare Officers, Group-2; UPSC 2018 ×2, 2021): the answer is always **Article 21 / Part III**.

=== GDPR and the Right to Be Forgotten
- **GDPR** (General Data Protection Regulation) – **European Union**: adopted **April 2016**, applied from **25 May 2018**; replaced the **1995** Data Protection Directive; explicit consent before processing.
- GDPR: administrative fines, member states may add criminal penalties; DPDP Act: **financial penalties only**.
- **Right to Be Forgotten:** began in **Google Spain v AEPD (2014)**; = **Right to Erasure, GDPR Art. 17** (DPDP: erasure Sec. 12). In India it limits **name-based searches** and **does not erase judicial records**.
- **Data portability:** explicit GDPR right (Art. 20); no statutory right under the DPDP Act.

=== DPDP Act 2023 – Scope and Terms
- Covers only **digital** personal data; excludes **non-personal** data; also applies to **foreign entities** offering goods/services in India.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Data Principal** | Individual whose data is processed; for a child (under 18), the **parent/guardian** |
| **Data Fiduciary** | Decides purpose and means (GDPR: **Data Controller**) |
| **Significant Data Fiduciary** | **DPO in India**, **independent auditor**, **impact assessment** (no consent exemption) |
| **Consent Manager** | **Registered entity** through which consent is given, managed, withdrawn |
~~ Sources: app notes 5:32 sub 3

=== Rights, Duties and Children
- **Rights:** information/access, correction, **erasure (Sec. 12)**, grievance redress, withdrawal of consent, **nomination** (no right to impose criminal penalties).
- **Duties:** no false information, impersonation or **frivolous complaints**.
- **Children:** **verifiable parental consent below 18**; **no tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted ads**.
~~ Sources: app notes 5:32 sub 4

=== Exemptions and RTI
- **Sec. 7:** **legitimate uses** (State processing without consent).
- **Sec. 17:** Centre may exempt notified **State agencies** (sovereignty, security, public order); research, archiving, statistics; **journalism – no exemption**.
- **Sec. 44(3)** amended **RTI Sec. 8(1)(j)**: personal information may be refused; **public-interest override removed**; challenge referred to a **Constitution Bench** (Aug 2026).

=== DPDP Rules 2025, Localisation and Surveillance
- **DPDP Rules:** draft **3 Jan 2025**; final notified **14 Nov 2025** – Board provisions at once; **Consent Manager Nov 2026**; full compliance **May 2027** (18 months).
- Rules require clear, specific, informed consent; plain-language notices; purpose limitation; data minimisation; retention controls.
- **Localisation:** **RBI (April 2018)** – payment-system data stored **only in India**. DPDP cross-border transfer = **blacklist** (allowed unless the Centre restricts); GDPR = **whitelist / adequacy**.
- First national standards for **cloud, data centres, ethical AI** focus on bias, data sovereignty, privacy.
- **Pegasus:** SC (27 Oct 2021, CJI N.V. Ramana) set up a technical committee overseen by **Justice R.V. Raveendran** (retd SC judge); malware on **5 of 29** phones, not clearly Pegasus.
- **AP:** AP Police **AI4AP** pilot (Chittoor, Guntur, Annamayya) raises DPDP issues.
