## SUB 0 | IT Act 2000 – Purpose, Sections and Shreya Singhal
[0:0] # Purpose
[0:1] - **Information Technology Act, 2000** = India's **first cyber law**: **legal recognition** to transactions by **electronic data interchange** ("**electronic commerce**") and electronic records; framework against computer crimes.
[0:2] - Concepts (Sec. 2): **electronic record, digital signature, certifying authority** (all three).
[0:3] - **Special law**: may override the **IPC** where an offence is fully covered; **Controller of Certifying Authorities (CCA)** licenses CAs, which issue Digital Signature Certificates.
[0:4] - **2008 amendment** (legal framework for cybercrime today): technology-neutral "**electronic signature**"; added sexually explicit material, **video voyeurism**, data leakage by intermediaries, e-commerce fraud; inserted 70A, 70B.
[0:5] # Sections
[0:6] TABLE head: Sec. | Subject
  [0:6.0] 4 / 5 | Recognition of e-records / e-signatures
  [0:6.1] 43 / 43A | Unauthorised access / compensation for failure to protect data
  [0:6.2] 66 | Hacking-type offences
  [0:6.3] 66A | Offensive messages – struck down (2015)
  [0:6.4] 66C / 66D | Identity theft / cheating by personation
  [0:6.5] 66E / 66F | Privacy / cyber terrorism (life)
  [0:6.6] 67 / 67A | Obscene / sexually explicit
  [0:6.7] 69 / 69A | Interception / blocking
  [0:6.8] 70 / 70A / 70B | Protected systems / NCIIPC / CERT-In
  [0:6.9] 79 / 79A | Safe harbour / Centre notifies Examiners of Electronic Evidence
  [0:6.10] 87 | Rule-making (IT Rules 2021)
[0:7] # Shreya Singhal v. Union of India (24 March 2015)
[0:8] - **66A struck down** as violating **Art. 19(1)(a)** (not saved by 19(2)); **69A upheld**; **79 read down** – "actual knowledge" = court order or government notice.
[0:9] - **IT Rules 2021**: no due diligence = loss of **safe harbour**; takedown within **three hours** (27 Mar 2026).

## SUB 1 | Digital Signature and PKI
[1:0] - **Digital signature** = an **electronic method of signing** an electronic document that **ensures the original content is unchanged**; gives **authentication, integrity, non-repudiation** – **not secrecy**; asymmetric crypto + hash; **private key signs**, public key verifies.
[1:1] - A record that **identifies the issuing Certifying Authority** = **digital certificate**, not the signature; proof of identity to access a server = certificate/login, not the signature.
[1:2] - **Public Key Infrastructure (PKI)** = **digital security infrastructure**: creates, manages and revokes certificates.
[1:3] > UPSC 2019 key: **only statement 3**; UPSC 2020: PKI = digital security infrastructure.
[1:4] ~~ Sources: app notes 5:30 sub 3
## SUB 2 | CERT-In and Cyber Swachhta Kendra
[2:0] - **CERT-In**: operational since **January 2004**, under **MeitY**; national incident response centre; statutory via **70B** (2008); **CVE Numbering Authority**.
[2:1] - **Directions, 28 Apr 2022**: service providers, intermediaries, data centres, body corporates, **government** and **VASPs** report incidents within **6 hours**; logs **180 days**; VPN providers keep data **5 years**.
[2:2] - **Cyber Swachhta Kendra** = **Botnet Cleaning and Malware Analysis Centre** run by CERT-In – helps users **detect and remove malware and botnets**; free **USB Pratirodh, AppSamvid, M-Kavach**.
[2:3] - **NCCC** (National Cyber Coordination Centre): **situational awareness** of threats; **Cyber Surakshit Bharat** (2018, MeitY) trains **CISOs**.
[2:4] ~~ Sources: NextGen S&T Group 2 Part 3 p.7 (CERT-In since Jan 2004, MeitY); app notes 5:30 sub 4
