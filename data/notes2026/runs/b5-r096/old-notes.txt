## SUB 1 | Panchamrit and Net Zero 2070
[96:1:0] - Announced by the PM at **COP26, Glasgow, 1 Nov 2021**. Hook: **500-50-1-45-2070**.
[96:1:1] TABLE head: # | Pledge | By
  [96:1:1.0] 1 | 500 GW non-fossil capacity | 2030
  [96:1:1.1] 2 | 50% energy from renewables | 2030
  [96:1:1.2] 3 | Cut projected emissions by 1 billion t (not by 2050) | 2030
  [96:1:1.3] 4 | 45% intensity cut from 2005 | 2030
  [96:1:1.4] 5 | Net zero | 2070
[96:1:2] - **Net zero** = residual emissions **balanced by removals** (forests, carbon capture); not zero gross emissions. India's year **2070** (APPSC 2024), later than developed 2050; defended by **CBDR-RC** (India supports it) and development needs.
[96:1:3] - **LT-LEDS** at **COP27 (Nov 2022)**; 7 transitions: low-carbon electricity, transport, urban adaptation and efficient buildings, decoupling industry, **CO2 removal**, forest cover, low-carbon finance. **Not**: fossil-fuel power expansion.
[96:1:4] - **Net Zero Portal (Sep 2026)**: **voluntary** corporate disclosure (Scope 1, 2; Scope 3 voluntary) + NAPCC Dashboard.
[96:1:5] - **IEA "Net-Zero by 2050"** roadmap: May 2021.
## SUB 2 | Energy Transition and Carbon Sink
[96:2:0] - Non-fossil **51.5%** of capacity (262.74 of 509.64 GW, Nov 2025); **generation only ~25%** (storage shortage).
[96:2:1] - **3rd** in RE capacity, after China and USA (IRENA 2026). Solar 157.05 GW, wind 56.81 GW (May 2026).
[96:2:2] - **Green Hydrogen Mission**: **₹19,744 crore**; **5 MMT/yr by 2030**. **Nuclear Mission**: **5 indigenous SMRs by 2033**.
[96:2:3] - **Pumped storage**: **258 GW** identified; **7 GW** operational; >17 GW by 2030. First standalone BESS: 180 MW (Gujarat).
[96:2:4] - **AP**: **Pinnapuram 1,680 MW** integrated RE + pumped storage project.
[96:2:5] - **Green India Mission**: key sink programme; **5 Mha new + 5 Mha improved**; MISHTI: 540 sq km mangroves. Forest and tree cover **~24.6%** (2021).
[96:2:6] - India has **not signed** the Global Methane Pledge.
## SUB 3 | Mission LiFE
[96:3:0] - **LiFE = Lifestyle for Environment** (APPSC 2024).
[96:3:1] - Idea at **COP26 (2021)**, not COP25; Global Movement **5 June 2022**; Mission launched **Oct 2022, Kevadia**, with the **UN Secretary-General**.
[96:3:2] - **P3** = Pro-Planet People; **75 actions under 7 themes** (save energy, save water, no single-use plastic, sustainable food, reduce waste, e-waste, healthy lifestyles). Aligned: **UJALA** (LED), **PAT**.

=== NOTES 5:98 | Environmental Pollution – Types, Sources and Impacts
## SUB 0 | Types of Pollution – Source and Impact
[98:0:0] TABLE head: Pollution type | Major primary source (APPSC 2025 key) | Key impacts
  [98:0:0.0] Air | Industrial emissions (SO2, NOx, PM); vehicle exhaust | Respiratory and heart disease; acid rain; poor visibility
  [98:0:0.1] Water | Sewage discharge; industrial effluents | Water-borne diseases, eutrophication, fish kills
  [98:0:0.2] Soil | Chemical fertilisers and pesticides; industrial waste | Loss of fertility, toxic crops, soil fauna imbalance
[98:0:1] - Air pollution deaths: **~7 million a year (WHO)**; household air pollution ~3.8 million.
[98:0:2] - Highest chemical pollution from industrial effluents: **leather industry** (water and soil).
[98:0:3] - Survey (Yale, 2024-25): **53%** Indians faced water pollution, **52%** air pollution.
[98:0:4] APPSC 2025: soil pollution's main source = fertilisers and pesticides; water pollution's = sewage.
## SUB 1 | Classification of Pollutants
[98:1:0] TABLE head: Basis | Types | Examples
  [98:1:0.0] Form | Primary (as emitted) vs secondary (formed by reaction in air) | Plastics vs ozone, PAN (peroxyacyl nitrate)
  [98:1:0.1] Existence in nature | Quantitative vs qualitative | CO2 vs insecticides, herbicides
  [98:1:0.2] Natural disposal | Biodegradable vs non-biodegradable | Sewage, heat vs plastics, DDT
  [98:1:0.3] Source geometry | Point / line / area / diffuse (non-point) | Chimney; highway; mining area; farm runoff
[98:1:1] - **Xenobiotics**: man-made chemicals foreign to living systems (pesticides, **PCBs**, plastics); resist degradation.
[98:1:2] Ozone and PAN are secondary pollutants; plastics are primary. Farm runoff is non-point (diffuse).
## SUB 2 | Pollutant → Effect and Matched Pairs
[98:2:0] TABLE head: Pollutant | Effect
  [98:2:0.0] SO2 + NOx | Acid rain (H2SO4, HNO3)

  [98:2:0.4] N2O | Greenhouse gas

## SUB 3 | Biomagnification and Bioaccumulation
[98:3:0] - **Bioaccumulation**: build-up of a persistent pollutant inside one organism over time.
[98:3:1] - **Biomagnification**: rise in its concentration at each higher **trophic level** of the food chain.
[98:3:2] - Typical pollutants: **DDT** and **mercury** (methyl mercury in fish – Minamata).
[98:3:3] - Top consumers (fish-eating birds, humans) carry the highest load.
