## SUB 2 | Types of Pollution – Sources and Impacts
[2:0] TABLE head: Pollution type | Major source | Key impacts
  [2:0.0] Air | Industrial emissions; vehicle exhaust (main city source); fossil-fuel combustion | Respiratory and heart disease; acid rain
  [2:0.1] Water | Sewage; industrial effluents | Water-borne diseases, eutrophication, fish kills
  [2:0.2] Soil | Chemical fertilisers and pesticides; industrial waste; mining | Loss of fertility, toxic crops
  [2:0.3] Noise | Construction activities, traffic | Hearing loss, stress
[2:1] - APPSC G2 Mains 2025 match: **air – industrial emissions, water – sewage, soil – chemical fertilisers, noise – construction**.
[2:2] - **Natural** causes of air pollution: forest fires, volcanoes, dust storms, **pollen**, spores. **Pollen grains are not anthropogenic**; pesticide residues, sewer gases and coal/oil power stations are.
[2:3] - **Fertilisers**: indiscriminate use pollutes **soil, water and air** (ammonia) → "all of these". Agro-chemicals cause **water pollution** (runoff), not better fertility.
[2:4] - **Nitrogen pollution** → loss of biodiversity, pollution of rivers and seas, **ozone depletion** (N2O) → all three.
[2:5] - **Mineral extraction** → biodiversity loss, erosion and water pollution (all). Pollution disturbs the **balance of nature, geochemical cycles and flora** (all).
[2:6] - **Recycling** paper, plastic and metals cuts the waste dumped on land → reduces **soil (land) pollution** (also eases air and water).
[2:7] - **Sewage treatment plants** = sustainable technology against water pollution; **fossil fuels** → air pollution + GHG → both statements correct.
[2:8] - Highest chemical pollution from industrial effluents: **leather industry** (tanneries – chromium; water and soil).
[2:9] - Control measures (NTRUHS): poisonous gases – **catalytic converters**; green tax – **old vehicles**; untreated water – **coagulation**; plastic waste – **Plastic Waste Management Amendment Rules 2021**.
[2:10] - Air pollution deaths: **~7 million a year (WHO)**. Survey (Yale, 2024-25): 53% of Indians faced water pollution, 52% air pollution.
[2:11] ~~ Sources: NextGen Environment APPSC 09 p.12, p.14; NextGen Environment APPSC 10 p.11-12; Rocket Environment p.21-22; app notes 5:98 sub 0
## SUB 3 | Classification of Pollutants – Biodegradable or Not
[3:0] TABLE head: Basis | Types | Examples
  [3:0.0] Natural disposal | Biodegradable (decomposed by microbes, generally organic) vs non-biodegradable (generally inorganic; bioaccumulate) | Sewage, heat, natural rubber, wool, leather, jute vs plastics, polythene, DDT, heavy metals (mercury), glass, silver foil, asbestos
  [3:0.1] Form | Primary (as emitted) vs secondary (formed by reaction) | Plastics vs ozone, PAN
  [3:0.2] Existence in nature | Quantitative vs qualitative | CO2 vs insecticides, herbicides
  [3:0.3] Source geometry | Point / line / area / diffuse (non-point) | Chimney; highway; mining area; farm runoff
[3:1] - **DDT** = **non-biodegradable** pollutant (persistent, biomagnifies) – not biodegradable.
[3:2] - Industry producing the **most non-biodegradable waste**: **thermal power plants** (fly ash).
[3:3] - **Xenobiotics**: man-made chemicals foreign to living systems (pesticides, **PCBs**, plastics); resist degradation.

## SUB 5 | Pollutant → Effect Pairs and Biomagnification
[5:0] TABLE head: Pollutant | Effect
  [5:0.0] SO2 + NO2 (acid rain) | Marble cancer – acids react with the calcium carbonate of the Taj Mahal's marble
  [5:0.1] Chlorinated hydrocarbons (DDT) | Biomagnification
  [5:0.2] Methane, CO2, N2O | Greenhouse effect
  [5:0.3] Nickel, asbestos | Lung cancer
  [5:0.4] CFCs / Freon | Ozone depletion
  [5:0.5] SF6 | Extremely potent greenhouse gas (absorbs infrared, lasts thousands of years)
[5:1] - APPSC G2 Mains 2025 match: marble cancer – **III**, biomagnification – **IV**, greenhouse – **I**, lung cancer – **II** → **III IV I II**.
[5:2] - **Bioaccumulation**: build-up inside one organism; **biomagnification**: rise at each higher **trophic level** – highest in **top consumers** (fish-eating birds, humans). Typical: **DDT** and **mercury** (Minamata).
