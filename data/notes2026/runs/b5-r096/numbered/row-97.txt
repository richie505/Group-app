## SUB 0 | NAPCC – Release, Scope and Number of Missions
[0:0] - **NAPCC** released on **30 June 2008** by PM **Dr Manmohan Singh** (APPSC AEE 2023) – India's **first** National Action Plan on Climate Change and first overarching domestic climate policy.
[0:1] - Prepared under the **PM's Council on Climate Change** (formed **2007**).
[0:2] - Scope: **both mitigation and adaptation** (APPSC G2 Mains 2025) – not only GHG emissions, biodiversity or air pollution.
[0:3] - Core: **8 National Missions** (APPSC G1) – "How many missions?" = **8**, even though the Economic Survey 2025-26 counts nine.
[0:4] - **Four** of the eight missions focus on adaptation.
[0:5] - NAPCC is the domestic vehicle for India's **NDCs**; NDCs are delivered through the **National Adaptation Plan + national missions + SAPCCs**.
[0:6] - Adaptation spending: **3.7% of GDP (FY16) → 5.6% (FY22)**.
[0:7] - Climate change is treated as the defining global political issue of the present generation (APPSC 2010 asks it loosely).
[0:8] - CoP30: **Belem, Brazil**; India's National Statement on 17 Nov 2025. Net zero target: **2070**.
[0:9] ~~ Sources: Rocket Environment p.4; Shankar Environment Part 17 p.16; app notes 5:97 sub 0
## SUB 1 | The Eight National Missions – and What Is NOT One
[1:0] TABLE head: Mission | Nodal ministry | Launch / note
  [1:0.0] National Solar Mission (JNNSM) | MNRE | Launched 11 Jan 2010 (APPSC Draughtsman: Jan 2010); 100 GW by 2022
  [1:0.1] Enhanced Energy Efficiency (NMEEE) | Power / BEE | Includes PAT
  [1:0.2] Sustainable Habitat (NMSH) | MoHUA | Buildings, urban transport, waste – including power from waste
  [1:0.3] National Water Mission | Jal Shakti | Set up 30 June 2008
  [1:0.4] Sustaining the Himalayan Ecosystem (NMSHE) | DST | 12 thematic areas
  [1:0.5] Green India (GIM) – afforestation | MoEFCC | Approved by CCEA 20 Feb 2014
  [1:0.6] Sustainable Agriculture (NMSA) | Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare | Operational from 2014-15
  [1:0.7] Strategic Knowledge (NMSKCC) | DST | 4 thematic areas; research networks
[1:1] - Two missions are under **DST** (NMSHE and NMSKCC), not Jal Shakti.
[1:2] - **NMSKCC** builds S&T capacity: Centres of Excellence, **State Climate Change Centres**, Global Technology Watch Groups.
[1:3] - **NOT one of the eight:** **nuclear / atomic power** (UPPSC 2016, UP UDA 2010), **National Mission for Sustaining the Coastal and Marine Ecosystem** (APPSC Poly 2018), wind, green hydrogen, **National Clean Air Programme**, biomass (SAMARTH), schemes such as Swachh Bharat, Ujjwala, AMRUT.
[1:4] - "Waste to energy" is **not** a separate mission but falls within the **Sustainable Habitat** mission (power from urban waste), so the "not included" answer is **nuclear**.
[1:5] > Most-asked angle: the odd one out – **nuclear** or **coastal and marine**.

## SUB 2 | Mission Objectives – Water, Green India, PAT, NMSA
[2:0] # National Water Mission
[2:1] - Goals: public water database, focus on **over-exploited areas**, **20% gain in water-use efficiency** (basin-level IWRM).
[2:2] - IAS 2012: if fully implemented, urban water needs partly met by **recycling waste water** and coastal cities' needs met through **desalination / ocean-water technologies** → **I and II only**. Linking Himalayan and peninsular rivers and fully reimbursing farmers' bore-well costs are **not** NWM goals.
[2:3] - State Specific Action Plans: **₹50 lakh** for major States.
[2:4] # Green India Mission
[2:5] - Aim: **protect, restore and enhance forest cover** and respond to climate change through **both adaptation and mitigation** (IAS 2016: only statement III) – not green accounting, not a second green revolution.
[2:6] - **3 sub-missions**: forest quality; forest/tree cover with ecosystem restoration; **incomes of forest-dependent people** (not thermal power). Also covers biodiversity, water, biomass, mangroves, wetlands.
[2:7] - Implemented in States through State plans – e.g. **Jharkhand and Madhya Pradesh** (JPSC 2016: both); works in **convergence with MGNREGA** and CAMPA (the "merged with MGNREGA" flashcard).
[2:8] - Targets: **5 Mha new + 5 Mha improved**; **50-60 Mt** extra annual sequestration by 2020; additional sink of **2.5-3.0 bn t CO2e** by 2030.
[2:9] - **CAG audit (Aug 2026)**, 16 States/UTs, 2015-16 to 2024-25:
[2:10] TABLE head: Component | Achieved | Target | Shortfall
  [2:10.0] Quality improvement | 0.11384 Mha | 1.4 Mha | 91.87%
  [2:10.1] Cover increase | 0.03409 Mha | 1.4 Mha | 97.57%
[2:11] # PAT and NMSA
[2:12] - **PAT (Perform, Achieve and Trade)**: market mechanism under NMEEE; energy-intensive units beating targets earn **tradable Energy Saving Certificates** (not carbon credits).
[2:13] - **NMSA**: Green Revolution-Krishonnati (2018-19) → **PM-RKVY** from 2022-23. Components: Rainfed Area Development, On-Farm Water Management, Soil Health Management, Sub-Mission on Agroforestry. Up to **55% micro-irrigation subsidy**; **Soil Health Cards** free every **two years**.
[2:14] ~~ Sources: Shankar Environment Part 17 p.17-18; Rocket Environment p.14; app notes 5:97 sub 3

## SUB 4 | State Action Plans and Andhra Pradesh Climate Action
[4:0] - **34 SAPCCs** (28 States + 6 UTs) approved and operational.
[4:1] TABLE head: Phase | Nature
  [4:1.0] 2010-15 | Broad, mitigation-centric plans
  [4:1.1] 2020 onwards | District-specific, risk-based adaptation plans tied to social schemes and local institutions
[4:2] - Support: State Climate Change Centres (**NMSKCC**); vulnerability assessments; CCAP. Weakness: finance – fix proposed: **climate budgeting** via State Finance Departments.
[4:3] - **Jharkhand Action Plan on Climate Change (2014)**: **Seraikela-Kharsawan** most sensitive district (JPSC 2016). **Tamil Nadu**: Governing Council on Climate Change headed by the **CM**.
[4:4] # Andhra Pradesh
[4:5] - **Exclusive Climate Change Cell: November 2022** (APPSC FRO 2022); now the **AP State Climate Change Cell** under the Dept of **Environment, Forests and Science & Technology**.
[4:6] - Revised **AP SAPCC 2025-30**; **Aug 2026**: the Cell prescribed **eight energy-sector adaptation measures**. Vulnerable sectors: **water, agriculture, food security, health** (heatwaves, droughts, floods, cyclones). Green cover: **37% by 2030 → 50% by 2047**.
[4:7] TABLE head: Organisation | Fact
  [4:7.0] FFFAP – Fridays For Future Andhra Pradesh | Climate-awareness group; joined the first global climate strike in 2019 (APPSC 2022-23 ×3)
  [4:7.1] AP National Green Corps | 2002-03 (NGC nationally 2001-02); 4,350 Eco-Clubs; 1.63 lakh students
[4:8] - **Eco-Clubs (National Green Corps)** are a component of MoEFCC's central sector scheme **Environment Education, Awareness and Training (EEAT)** (FBO 2019), launched 1983-84.
