=== Pollution Disasters – Bhopal, Minamata, LG Polymers
| Event | Year | Place | Pollutant / cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| Los Angeles smog | 1943 | Los Angeles, USA | **Photochemical** smog: sunlight on NOx and hydrocarbons from vehicles |
| London smog | 5-11 Dec 1952 | London | **Classical** smog: SO2 + water vapour at low temperature; ~4,000 killed |
| **Minamata disease** | Officially identified **1956**; first patient's symptoms **1953** (Kyushu) | Minamata, Japan | **Methyl mercury** in **Chisso Corporation** wastewater, biomagnified in **fish** – **mercury poisoning** (water pollution) |
| **Bhopal gas tragedy** | **2-3 Dec 1984** | Bhopal, MP | **Methyl isocyanate (MIC)** from the **Union Carbide** pesticide plant; over 2,500 (officially 3,000+) deaths |
| Chernobyl | 26 April 1986 | Ukraine (then **USSR**) | Radioactive leak from a reactor |
| Fukushima | 2011 | Japan | Hydrogen explosion and radioactive leak after earthquake-tsunami |
| **LG Polymers** | **7 May 2020** | R.R. Venkatapuram, **Visakhapatnam** (AP) | **Styrene** vapour leak – the most recent worst man-made industrial disaster in AP |
- **Man-made disasters** come from human action, negligence or technology; **industrial disasters** (Bhopal, LG Polymers) are mostly **toxic gas** leaks.
- MIC spellings: **Methyl Iso Cyanate** – not "Methyl Iso Cyanide", not ethyl/carbide isocyanate, not hydrogen cyanide or NO2.
- Other metal diseases: **cadmium → itai-itai**; **arsenic → black-foot disease**; nitrate → blue-baby syndrome; fluoride → fluorosis.
> Most-asked angle (≈13 PYQs): Bhopal = **MIC, Dec 1984**; Minamata = **mercury**.

=== Types of Pollution – Sources and Impacts
| Pollution type | Major source | Key impacts |
|---|---|---|
| **Air** | **Industrial emissions**; **vehicle exhaust** (main city source); fossil-fuel combustion | Respiratory and heart disease; acid rain |
| **Water** | **Sewage**; industrial effluents | Water-borne diseases, eutrophication, fish kills |
| **Soil** | **Chemical fertilisers** and pesticides; industrial waste; mining | Loss of fertility, toxic crops |
| **Noise** | **Construction** activities, traffic | Hearing loss, stress |
- APPSC G2 Mains 2025 match: **air – industrial emissions, water – sewage, soil – chemical fertilisers, noise – construction**.
- **Natural** causes of air pollution: forest fires, volcanoes, dust storms, **pollen**, spores. **Pollen grains are not anthropogenic**; pesticide residues, sewer gases and coal/oil power stations are.
- **Fertilisers**: indiscriminate use pollutes **soil, water and air** (ammonia) → "all of these". Agro-chemicals cause **water pollution** (runoff), not better fertility.
- **Nitrogen pollution** → loss of biodiversity, pollution of rivers and seas, **ozone depletion** (N2O) → all three.
- **Mineral extraction** → biodiversity loss, erosion and water pollution (all). Pollution disturbs the **balance of nature, geochemical cycles and flora** (all).
- **Recycling** paper, plastic and metals cuts the waste dumped on land → reduces **soil (land) pollution** (also eases air and water).
- **Sewage treatment plants** = sustainable technology against water pollution; **fossil fuels** → air pollution + GHG → both statements correct.
- Highest chemical pollution from industrial effluents: **leather industry** (tanneries – chromium; water and soil).
- Control measures (NTRUHS): poisonous gases – **catalytic converters**; green tax – **old vehicles**; untreated water – **coagulation**; plastic waste – **Plastic Waste Management Amendment Rules 2021**.
- Air pollution deaths: **~7 million a year (WHO)**. Survey (Yale, 2024-25): 53% of Indians faced water pollution, 52% air pollution.

=== Classification of Pollutants – Biodegradable or Not
| Basis | Types | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Natural disposal | **Biodegradable** (decomposed by microbes, generally organic) vs **non-biodegradable** (generally inorganic; bioaccumulate) | **Sewage**, heat, **natural rubber**, wool, leather, jute vs **plastics, polythene, DDT**, heavy metals (mercury), glass, **silver foil**, asbestos |
| Form | **Primary** (as emitted) vs **secondary** (formed by reaction) | Plastics vs **ozone, PAN** |
| Existence in nature | **Quantitative** vs **qualitative** | CO2 vs insecticides, herbicides |
| Source geometry | Point / line / area / diffuse (non-point) | Chimney; highway; mining area; farm runoff |
- **DDT** = **non-biodegradable** pollutant (persistent, biomagnifies) – not biodegradable.
- Industry producing the **most non-biodegradable waste**: **thermal power plants** (fly ash).
- **Xenobiotics**: man-made chemicals foreign to living systems (pesticides, **PCBs**, plastics); resist degradation.
~~ Sources: NextGen Environment APPSC 09 p.13; NextGen Environment APPSC 08 p.6; app notes 5:98 sub 1

=== Toxic Pollutants – Heavy Metals and Their Sources
| Pollutant | Source / effect |
|---|---|
| **Mercury** | **Coal-based thermal power plants**, **gold mining** (amalgamation), pesticides, **dental amalgam**, **fluorescent lamps**, smelters; synthetic idol paints (with cadmium, lead) |
| **Lead** | **Motor vehicles** – pollutes big-city air; smelters; **paints** (paint-industry workers) → brain damage; household/decorative paints capped at **90 ppm** |
| Coal ash | Soil pollution; arsenic, mercury, lead, cadmium, chromium |
| Brominated flame retardants | Electronics, plastics, foam; persistent, endocrine-disrupting; released from **e-waste** |
| Ammonia (NCR) | 77-80% from **fertilisers + livestock** |
| Open burning (Bhogi bonfires) | Tyres, plastics, flex, e-waste → dioxins, furans, CO, SO2, NOx, lead, cadmium, mercury (APPCB advisory, 11 Jan 2026) |
- UPSC 2023 mercury statements: gold mining and coal plants are mercury sources; "no known safe level of exposure" is disputed between answer keys (PMF: true → **all three**; Vajiram and others: false → **only two**).

=== Pollutant → Effect Pairs and Biomagnification
| Pollutant | Effect |
|---|---|
| **SO2 + NO2** (acid rain) | **Marble cancer** – acids react with the **calcium carbonate** of the Taj Mahal's marble |
| **Chlorinated hydrocarbons** (DDT) | **Biomagnification** |
| **Methane**, CO2, N2O | **Greenhouse effect** |
| **Nickel**, asbestos | **Lung cancer** |
| CFCs / Freon | Ozone depletion |
| SF6 | Extremely potent greenhouse gas (absorbs infrared, lasts thousands of years) |
- APPSC G2 Mains 2025 match: marble cancer – **III**, biomagnification – **IV**, greenhouse – **I**, lung cancer – **II** → **III IV I II**.
- **Bioaccumulation**: build-up inside one organism; **biomagnification**: rise at each higher **trophic level** – highest in **top consumers** (fish-eating birds, humans). Typical: **DDT** and **mercury** (Minamata).

# CEPI (Comprehensive Environmental Pollution Index)
- Developed by **CPCB with IIT Delhi (2009)**; scores clusters 0-100; factors: **pollutant, pathway, receptor**.
| CEPI score | Category |
|---|---|
| 70 or above | **Critically polluted** |
| 60 to below 70 | Severely polluted |
- 2009: **43 of 88** clusters critical. AP: **Visakhapatnam** assessed; **Patancheru-Bollaram** (now **Telangana**) critical.
# Red / Orange / Green / White (CPCB, 2016)
- Pollution Index from **water pollution, air pollution and hazardous waste**: **Red ≥60**, Orange 41-59, Green 21-40, **White ≤20** (practically non-polluting). 2025 revision added **Blue**.
