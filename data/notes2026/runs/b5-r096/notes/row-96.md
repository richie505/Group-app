=== Climate Indices and Coalitions – CCPI, C40, Biofuels Alliance
# Climate Change Performance Index (CCPI)
- Published by **Germanwatch, NewClimate Institute and Climate Action Network (CAN) International**; annually **since 2005**, released at the COP; 59 countries + EU (≈92% of emissions); weights: GHG 40%, renewables 20%, energy use 20%, climate policy 20%.
- Top three ranks are left **empty** (no country does enough).
| CCPI edition (released) | India's rank |
|---|---|
| **2017** (Nov 2016, COP22 Marrakech; 58 countries) | **20th** |
| 2018 (Nov 2017) | 14th |
| **2019** (Dec 2018, COP24 Katowice) | **11th** |
| **2020** (10 Dec 2019, COP25 Madrid) | **9th** – first time in the top 10 |
| 2021 | 10th |
| 2024 | **7th** |
- "India's rank **in 2019**" (JL 2020) = the index **released in 2019** = **CCPI 2020 → 9th**; the CCPI **2019** edition (released Dec 2018) had India **11th**.
- CCPI 2017, industrialised nations: among Australia, Canada, Japan and the **UK**, only the **UK** was among the better performers (Australia, Canada, Japan ranked low).

=== Panchamrit and Net Zero 2070
- Announced by the PM at **COP26, Glasgow, 1 Nov 2021**. Hook: **500-50-1-45-2070**.
| # | Pledge | By |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **500 GW** non-fossil energy capacity (not 250 GW) | 2030 |
| 2 | **50%** of energy requirements from renewables (not 60%) | 2030 |
| 3 | Cut projected (cumulative) carbon emissions by **1 billion tonnes** | 2030 |
| 4 | Cut carbon (emissions) intensity of the economy by **45%** (from 2005) | 2030 |
| 5 | **Net zero** (not 2045 or 2050) | **2070** |
- TSPSC JL 2023: true = **II, III and IV** (I says 250 GW; V says 2045). COP26 "NOT true": "60% of energy from renewables" (it is 50%).
- **Net zero** = residual emissions **balanced by removals** (forests, carbon capture) – **not** zero gross emissions, and **not** a fixed 2050 date for every country (UPPCS 2021: only statement 2 correct). India's year **2070** is later than developed countries' 2050, defended by **CBDR-RC** and development needs.
- **LT-LEDS** presented at **COP27 (Nov 2022)**; 7 transitions: low-carbon electricity, transport, urban adaptation and efficient buildings, decoupling industry, **CO2 removal**, forest cover, low-carbon finance. **Not**: expansion of fossil-fuel power.
- **India's Net Zero Portal** and **NAPCC Dashboard** (MoEFCC, **16 Sep 2026**): **voluntary** registration and disclosure of net-zero targets (Scope 1, 2; Scope 3 voluntary).
- **IEA "Net-Zero by 2050"** roadmap: **May 2021**.

=== India's NDCs – INDC 2015 to NDC 3.0 (base year 2005)
- **INDC** = each country's own pledged post-2020 **climate-change action plan**, submitted before COP21 Paris (IAS 2016) – not refugees, AIIB or SDGs.
| Commitment | INDC 2015 (2030) | Updated NDC, Aug 2022 (2030) | NDC 3.0 (2035) | Progress |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Emissions-intensity cut of GDP | **33-35%** | **45%** | **47%** | 36% by 2020 |
| Non-fossil share of **installed capacity** | ~40% | 50% | **60%** | Crossed 50% **June 2025**; 54.18% on 30 June 2026 |
| Extra carbon sink | 2.5-3 bn t CO2e | Unchanged | 3.5-4.0 bn t | 2.29 bn t added |
- **Base year 2005**; Aug 2022 target **45%** (APPSC G-IV 2022). NDCs prepared by **MoEFCC**, approved by the **Union Cabinet**.
| Version | Date | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| INDC | **Oct 2015**, before COP21 | Eight goals, three quantitative; 4 of 8 NAPCC missions on adaptation |
| Updated NDC | **Aug 2022** | Took in two Panchamrit pledges (**45%** intensity, **50%** non-fossil); added **Mission LiFE** |
| NDC 3.0 (2031-35) | Cabinet **Mar 2026**; submitted **24 Apr 2026** | LiFE embedded; adaptation (coasts, farms, Himalaya); low-cost finance |
- Updated NDC did **not remove adaptation**. By 31 Dec 2025, India and **Argentina** were the only G20 members without a 2035 NDC.
> Traps: 33-35% (not 30-35%); the target is **installed capacity**, not generation.

=== Energy Transition and Carbon Sink
- An effective **positive measure** against climate change = **expansion of carbon sinks through afforestation** (AP Panchayat Secretary) – not subsidising electricity or removing all fuel subsidies.
- Non-fossil **51.5%** of capacity (Nov 2025); **generation share only ~25%** (storage shortage).
- **3rd** in renewable capacity, after China and the USA (IRENA 2026, 250.50 GW end-2025).
- **Green Hydrogen Mission**: **₹19,744 crore**; **5 MMT/yr by 2030**. **Nuclear Energy Mission**: **5 indigenous SMRs by 2033**.
- **Pumped storage**: **258 GW** identified; **7 GW** operational; >17 GW more by 2030. First standalone BESS: 180 MW (**Gujarat**). AP: **Pinnapuram 1,680 MW** integrated RE + pumped storage.
- Carbon-sink programmes: **Green India Mission** (**5 Mha new + 5 Mha improved**); **MISHTI** (~540 sq km of **mangroves**). Forest and tree cover **~24.6%** (2021).
- India has **not signed** the Global Methane Pledge.

=== Mission LiFE
- **LiFE = Lifestyle for Environment** – an **India-led global mass movement** to nudge **individual and community action** to protect the environment (TSPSC 2023).
- Idea first put forward at **COP26 (2021)**; LiFE Global Movement **5 June 2022**; Mission launched **20 Oct 2022** at **Kevadia (Ekta Nagar), Gujarat**, by the **PM** with the **UN Secretary-General** – not 5 June 2021, not Maharashtra, not the President.
- **P3** = **Pro-Planet People**; **75 LiFE actions under 7 themes** (save energy, save water, say no to single-use plastic, sustainable food, reduce waste, e-waste, healthy lifestyles). Aligned schemes: **UJALA** (LED bulbs), **PAT** (industrial efficiency).

=== Carbon Markets – CCTS and Green Credit Programme
| | CCTS | Green Credit Programme |
|---|---|---|
| Launch | Notified **June 2023** | Oct 2023 |
| Law | **Sec 14AA, Energy Conservation Act 2001** (amended 2022) | EPA 1986 |
| Run by | **BEE** (Ministry of Power) | ICFRE, Dehradun |
| Nature | India's **first mandatory compliance** carbon market | Incentive for **voluntary** environmental actions |
- CCTS = **compliance** mechanism (energy-intensive sectors get GHG Emission Intensity targets; beating them earns tradeable **Carbon Credit Certificates**) + **offset** mechanism (voluntary). **Intensity-based, not cap-based**; supports the **45%** intensity target.
- **282 obligated entities** (Oct 2025: aluminium, cement, chlor-alkali, pulp and paper). **UK CBAM** recognises CCTS as a carbon price from **1 Jan 2027**.
- GCP afforestation can be exchanged against **compensatory afforestation** obligations.
- **Surat ETS**: particulate matter cut **20-30%**. **Khasi Hills REDD+** (Meghalaya): India's first **Plan Vivo**-certified REDD+ project.
