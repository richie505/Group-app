=== NAPCC – Release, Scope and Number of Missions
- **NAPCC** released on **30 June 2008** by PM **Dr Manmohan Singh** (APPSC AEE 2023) – India's **first** National Action Plan on Climate Change and first overarching domestic climate policy.
- Prepared under the **PM's Council on Climate Change** (formed **2007**).
- Scope: **both mitigation and adaptation** (APPSC G2 Mains 2025) – not only GHG emissions, biodiversity or air pollution.
- Core: **8 National Missions** (APPSC G1) – "How many missions?" = **8**, even though the Economic Survey 2025-26 counts nine.
- **Four** of the eight missions focus on adaptation.
- NAPCC is the domestic vehicle for India's **NDCs**; NDCs are delivered through the **National Adaptation Plan + national missions + SAPCCs**.
- Adaptation spending: **3.7% of GDP (FY16) → 5.6% (FY22)**.
- Climate change is treated as the defining global political issue of the present generation (APPSC 2010 asks it loosely).
- CoP30: **Belem, Brazil**; India's National Statement on 17 Nov 2025. Net zero target: **2070**.

=== The Eight National Missions – and What Is NOT One
| Mission | Nodal ministry | Launch / note |
|---|---|---|
| **National Solar Mission** (JNNSM) | MNRE | Launched **11 Jan 2010** (APPSC Draughtsman: Jan 2010); **100 GW by 2022** |
| Enhanced Energy Efficiency (NMEEE) | Power / BEE | Includes **PAT** |
| Sustainable Habitat (NMSH) | MoHUA | Buildings, urban transport, waste – including **power from waste** |
| **National Water Mission** | Jal Shakti | Set up 30 June 2008 |
| Sustaining the Himalayan Ecosystem (NMSHE) | DST | 12 thematic areas |
| **Green India** (GIM) – afforestation | MoEFCC | Approved by CCEA **20 Feb 2014** |
| Sustainable Agriculture (NMSA) | Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare | Operational from 2014-15 |
| Strategic Knowledge (NMSKCC) | DST | 4 thematic areas; research networks |
- Two missions are under **DST** (NMSHE and NMSKCC), not Jal Shakti.
- **NMSKCC** builds S&T capacity: Centres of Excellence, **State Climate Change Centres**, Global Technology Watch Groups.
- **NOT one of the eight:** **nuclear / atomic power** (UPPSC 2016, UP UDA 2010), **National Mission for Sustaining the Coastal and Marine Ecosystem** (APPSC Poly 2018), wind, green hydrogen, **National Clean Air Programme**, biomass (SAMARTH), schemes such as Swachh Bharat, Ujjwala, AMRUT.
- "Waste to energy" is **not** a separate mission but falls within the **Sustainable Habitat** mission (power from urban waste), so the "not included" answer is **nuclear**.
> Most-asked angle: the odd one out – **nuclear** or **coastal and marine**.

=== Mission Objectives – Water, Green India, PAT, NMSA
# National Water Mission
- Goals: public water database, focus on **over-exploited areas**, **20% gain in water-use efficiency** (basin-level IWRM).
- IAS 2012: if fully implemented, urban water needs partly met by **recycling waste water** and coastal cities' needs met through **desalination / ocean-water technologies** → **I and II only**. Linking Himalayan and peninsular rivers and fully reimbursing farmers' bore-well costs are **not** NWM goals.
- State Specific Action Plans: **₹50 lakh** for major States.
# Green India Mission
- Aim: **protect, restore and enhance forest cover** and respond to climate change through **both adaptation and mitigation** (IAS 2016: only statement III) – not green accounting, not a second green revolution.
- **3 sub-missions**: forest quality; forest/tree cover with ecosystem restoration; **incomes of forest-dependent people** (not thermal power). Also covers biodiversity, water, biomass, mangroves, wetlands.
- Implemented in States through State plans – e.g. **Jharkhand and Madhya Pradesh** (JPSC 2016: both); works in **convergence with MGNREGA** and CAMPA (the "merged with MGNREGA" flashcard).
- Targets: **5 Mha new + 5 Mha improved**; **50-60 Mt** extra annual sequestration by 2020; additional sink of **2.5-3.0 bn t CO2e** by 2030.
- **CAG audit (Aug 2026)**, 16 States/UTs, 2015-16 to 2024-25:
| Component | Achieved | Target | Shortfall |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quality improvement | 0.11384 Mha | 1.4 Mha | 91.87% |
| Cover increase | 0.03409 Mha | 1.4 Mha | **97.57%** |
# PAT and NMSA
- **PAT (Perform, Achieve and Trade)**: market mechanism under NMEEE; energy-intensive units beating targets earn **tradable Energy Saving Certificates** (not carbon credits).
- **NMSA**: Green Revolution-Krishonnati (2018-19) → **PM-RKVY** from 2022-23. Components: Rainfed Area Development, On-Farm Water Management, Soil Health Management, Sub-Mission on Agroforestry. Up to **55% micro-irrigation subsidy**; **Soil Health Cards** free every **two years**.

=== State Action Plans and Andhra Pradesh Climate Action
- **34 SAPCCs** (28 States + 6 UTs) approved and operational.
| Phase | Nature |
|---|---|
| 2010-15 | Broad, mitigation-centric plans |
| 2020 onwards | District-specific, risk-based adaptation plans tied to social schemes and local institutions |
- Support: State Climate Change Centres (**NMSKCC**); vulnerability assessments; CCAP. Weakness: finance – fix proposed: **climate budgeting** via State Finance Departments.
- **Jharkhand Action Plan on Climate Change (2014)**: **Seraikela-Kharsawan** most sensitive district (JPSC 2016). **Tamil Nadu**: Governing Council on Climate Change headed by the **CM**.
# Andhra Pradesh
- **Exclusive Climate Change Cell: November 2022** (APPSC FRO 2022); now the **AP State Climate Change Cell** under the Dept of **Environment, Forests and Science & Technology**.
- Revised **AP SAPCC 2025-30**; **Aug 2026**: the Cell prescribed **eight energy-sector adaptation measures**. Vulnerable sectors: **water, agriculture, food security, health** (heatwaves, droughts, floods, cyclones). Green cover: **37% by 2030 → 50% by 2047**.
| Organisation | Fact |
|---|---|
| **FFFAP – Fridays For Future Andhra Pradesh** | Climate-awareness group; joined the **first global climate strike in 2019** (APPSC 2022-23 ×3) |
| AP National Green Corps | **2002-03** (NGC nationally 2001-02); 4,350 Eco-Clubs; 1.63 lakh students |
- **Eco-Clubs (National Green Corps)** are a component of MoEFCC's central sector scheme **Environment Education, Awareness and Training (EEAT)** (FBO 2019), launched 1983-84.
