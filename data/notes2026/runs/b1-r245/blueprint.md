## 3. Repeats and trends
- **APPSC's own angle is Andhra and Patel commemoration:** Pingali Venkayya (6 APPSC items across 1990–2022) and National Unity Day / Statue of Unity (7 APPSC items 2018–2019).
- **Duplicates:** Bharat Kokila (APPSC G2 = APPSC 2018), first I&B Minister (same pair), Statue of Unity inauguration (APPSC 2019 ×2), India for Indians (BPSC 2019 = State PSC), Golden Threshold (UPSC 2009 ×2).
- **Other states' PSCs dominate the book-author and title angles** (UPPCS, MPPSC, SSC) – fixed, repeatable recall.

## 4. Heavy in the old notes but never asked
