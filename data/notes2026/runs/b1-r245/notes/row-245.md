=== Andhra Contributors and the National Flag
# Pingali Venkayya and the tricolour
- **Pingali Venkayya:** a **Telugu** from **Andhra Pradesh** (born **Bhatlapenumarru**, Krishna district) who designed the flag later adopted, with changes, as the **National Flag** (APPSC G1 1990, 1994) – not Harisarvottama Rao, Ramananda Tirtha or Duggirala.
- **1906:** at the **Calcutta AICC session under Dadabhai Naoroji** he was inspired to design a national flag (APPSC FRO 2022).
- **1916:** wrote **"A National Flag for India"**.
- **1921:** presented his flag model to Gandhi at the **Vijayawada AICC session** (APPSC G2 2019 and G2) – not 1920, 1923 or 1925.
- **22 July 1947:** the Constituent Assembly adopted the present flag; the **Ashoka Chakra (24 spokes, from the Sarnath Lion Capital)** replaced the charkha.
- **Flag Code: length : width = 3 : 2** (APPSC Technical Assistant).
- **Pairs (APPSC Forest SO):** proportion 3:2, adopted 22 July 1947 and designed by Pingali Venkayya are **all correct** – no pair is wrong.

=== Capitalists and Artists in the National Movement
- **Jamnalal Bajaj:** **cotton merchant, banker, Congressman**, Gandhi's "fifth son" (IAS 1993); a capitalist who was **AICC treasurer** for years and was **jailed in 1923 and 1930** (State PSC) – not G.D. Birla, J.R.D. Tata or Walchand Hirachand; set up the Satyagraha Ashram at Wardha.
- **Jamini Roy:** **painter** (MPPSC 1991) – a pupil of Abanindranath Tagore, known for folk (Kalighat, patua) style.
- **Vishnu Chinchalkar:** a **painter (chitrakar)** of Indore, Madhya Pradesh (MPPSC 2018).
- **Rabindranath Tagore as painter:** began painting at about **60** and emerged as a painter with his **1930 exhibitions** (Paris, London) at about **70** (UPUDA 2001); a Bengal-school painter of bold black lines.
