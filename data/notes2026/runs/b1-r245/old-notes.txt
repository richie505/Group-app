[246:0:2] # Bardoli Satyagraha, 1928
[246:0:3] - Place: **Bardoli taluk, Surat district**, Gujarat (Bombay Presidency); begun by the **Mehta brothers (Kalyanji, Kunwarji)** and **Dayalji Desai**.
[246:0:4] - Issue: revenue raised about **30%** despite floods, famine.
[246:0:5] - Method: **no-tax campaign** + village intelligence network.
[246:0:6] - Women: **Mithuben Petit, Bhaktiba, Maniben Patel**.
[246:0:7] - Outcome: raised assessment stopped, fresh survey ordered; **Maxwell-Broomfield inquiry** cut the increase to about **6%**.
[246:0:8] Bardoli was led by Patel, not Gandhi. Champaran, Ahmedabad Mill Strike and Dandi were led by Gandhi.
