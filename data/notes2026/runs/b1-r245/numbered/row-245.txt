## SUB 4 | Andhra Contributors and the National Flag
[4:0] # Pingali Venkayya and the tricolour
[4:1] - **Pingali Venkayya:** a **Telugu** from **Andhra Pradesh** (born **Bhatlapenumarru**, Krishna district) who designed the flag later adopted, with changes, as the **National Flag** (APPSC G1 1990, 1994) – not Harisarvottama Rao, Ramananda Tirtha or Duggirala.
[4:2] - **1906:** at the **Calcutta AICC session under Dadabhai Naoroji** he was inspired to design a national flag (APPSC FRO 2022).
[4:3] - **1916:** wrote **"A National Flag for India"**.
[4:4] - **1921:** presented his flag model to Gandhi at the **Vijayawada AICC session** (APPSC G2 2019 and G2) – not 1920, 1923 or 1925.
[4:5] - **22 July 1947:** the Constituent Assembly adopted the present flag; the **Ashoka Chakra (24 spokes, from the Sarnath Lion Capital)** replaced the charkha.
[4:6] - **Flag Code: length : width = 3 : 2** (APPSC Technical Assistant).
[4:7] - **Pairs (APPSC Forest SO):** proportion 3:2, adopted 22 July 1947 and designed by Pingali Venkayya are **all correct** – no pair is wrong.
