=== Bharat Stage Timeline and Supreme Court Deadlines
- **First vehicle emission norms:** **1991 (petrol), 1992 (diesel)** – before the Euro-based system.
- **Mashelkar Committee → National Auto Fuel Policy (2003) → Euro norms** (roadmap for BS stages).
- **Euro-based norms under the Bharat Stage name from 2000** (four-wheelers, APPSC Asst. Chemist); BS-I was also called **"India 2000"**.
- **Euro I** came into force in Europe in **1992**; India generally lagged Europe by about **5 years**.
- **Who notifies:** tailpipe standards notified under the **Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989** by **MoRTH**; CPCB/MoEFCC set the pollution side (Shankar credits CPCB under MoEFCC with implementation).
| Stage | Euro equivalent | First applied | Nationwide |
|---|---|---|---|
| BS-I ("India 2000") | Euro I | – | **2000** |
| BS-II | Euro II | **Delhi, 1 April 2000** | **2005** |
| BS-III | Euro III | NCR + 10 cities incl. **Hyderabad-Secunderabad**, 1 April 2005 | **2010** |
| BS-IV | Euro IV | **13 cities, 2010** | **1 April 2017** |
| BS-V | Euro 5 | **Skipped** | – |
| BS-VI | **Euro 6** | – | **1 April 2020** |
| BS-VI Phase 2 | – | **Real Driving Emissions (RDE)** + OBD Stage II-A | **1 April 2023** (OBD II-B from 1 April 2025) |
| BS-VII | Euro 7 | Planned 2026-27 | – |
- **Supreme Court, 29 March 2017 (M.C. Mehta case):** **no sale or registration of BS-III (and below) vehicles from 1 April 2017**.
- **Supreme Court, 2018:** **no sale or registration of BS-IV vehicles from 1 April 2020** (APPSC Polytechnic Lecturers).
- **Leapfrog decision 2016:** BS-IV straight to BS-VI – urgent **Delhi-NCR** air pollution, close the gap with Europe in **one step**; BS-V is the only stage skipped.
- **BS-VI statements (APPSC NTRUHS):** BS-IV and BS-VI are based on **Euro 4 and Euro 6**; **RDE introduced for the first time**; **On-Board Diagnostics (OBD) is mandatory** – "OBD not necessary" is the **INCORRECT** statement.

=== Fuel Sulphur, Pollutant Limits and Control Technologies
| Stage | Diesel sulphur | Petrol sulphur |
|---|---|---|
| BS-I | ~2,500 ppm | – |
| BS-II | **500 ppm** | 500 ppm |
| BS-III | **350 ppm** | 150 ppm |
| BS-IV | **50 ppm** | 50 ppm |
| BS-VI | **10 ppm** (−80% vs BS-IV) | 10 ppm |
- Euro II ultra-low sulphur diesel: **0.05%** or less = **500 ppm**.
- Euro / BS norms limit **CO, hydrocarbons, NOx, PM**; Euro norms also cover **lead** ("all of the above" with CO, HC, lead).
- **BS-VI limits:** NOx **60 mg/km petrol, 80 mg/km diesel**; diesel PM **4.5 mg/km**; NOx down ~70%, PM ~80%.
- Refineries had to supply **10 ppm fuel nationwide**; sulphur cut 500 → 10 ppm in about **15 years**.
| Technology | Function |
|---|---|
| Catalytic converter (BS-I era) | CO → CO2 |
| Multi-point fuel injection (BS-II) | Replaced carburettors |
| **SCR** | Diesel NOx → N2 + water using urea **AdBlue** |
| **DPF** | Traps soot, periodically oxidises it |
| **OBD-II** | Monitors emissions, alerts driver |
| Electronic Fuel Injection | Two-wheelers |

=== Vehicular Pollutants and Health Effects
| Pollutant | Effect |
|---|---|
| **CO** | Forms **carboxyhaemoglobin**; cuts oxygen supply |
| NOx | Respiratory illness; forms ground-level ozone and acid rain |
| PM2.5 | Deep lungs/bloodstream; heart and lung disease |
| **Benzene** | Carcinogen – **leukaemia** |
| **Lead** | **Neurotoxin**; leaded petrol phased out by **2000** |
- **Photochemical smog** needs NOx + hydrocarbons + **sunlight** (cannot form without sunlight).
- **Chernobyl** = nuclear (radioactive) pollution. **Nanoparticles** pollute water, soil and the food chain.
- **Bioremediation** uses **microorganisms**.
~~ Sources: PMF Environment Part 04 p.5, p.43; app notes 5:100 sub 4

=== Allied Measures – PUC, E20, CAFE, Scrappage
- **PUC** certificate mandatory under the **CMV Rules, 1989** (generally 6 months; 12 months for BS-IV/BS-VI vehicles).
- **E20:** notified **17 Feb 2026**; all States/UTs from **1 April 2026**, minimum **RON 95** (not 91); replaced the 2021 E10 notification; concerns – mileage, older vehicles.
- **CAFE III** third draft (16 July 2026): cars from **April 2027**; credits for ethanol, CBG, biofuels; targets about **21% relaxed** (not stricter) vs the Sept 2025 proposal.
- **Vehicle Scrappage Policy (2021):** Automated Testing Stations + **Registered Vehicle Scrapping Facilities**.
