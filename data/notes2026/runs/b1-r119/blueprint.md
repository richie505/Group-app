## 3. Repeats and trends
- **Most repeated:** founders Harihara and Bukka (8 incl. year/first ruler), Gangadevi's Madhura Vijayam (4) and her guru Agastya (2), Bukka I and the Jains (4), Devaraya II's 10,000 Muslims (3), Gajabetekara (3), Abdur Razzaq (3), Sewell (5), dynasty order/first dynasty (7).
- **Key traps found:** "Sangama = Kakatiya chief" (printed key; the inscriptions make him a Yadava); "first to enlist Muslims" is Devaraya I, the 10,000 are Devaraya II (old explanation had it backwards); Sayana's commentaries keyed Bukka I in one copy and Harihara II in another; the Hazara Rama temple keyed Devaraya II (books: Krishnadevaraya; scholarship: Devaraya I); "conflict began" (Harihara I) vs "war began" (Bukka I).
- The old notes were heavy on **Saluva Narasimha's court, Andhra campaigns, origin-theory historians and a "number game"** – asked 0-2 times as PYQs here; kept short for the own MCQs.
