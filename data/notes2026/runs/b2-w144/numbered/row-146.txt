## SUB 2 | Consumer Protection Act 2019 – Rights, Consumer and Complaints
[2:0] - **Replaced** the Consumer Protection Act, **1986** (2019 Act: assent 9 August 2019). Like the old Act, a **three-tier** consumer dispute redressal machinery.
[2:1] - **Six rights:** safety, information, choice, to be heard, redressal, **consumer awareness** (no "right to private ownership").
[2:2] - **Consumer** includes **online/electronic** purchases for personal use; a buyer for **resale or commercial purpose** is excluded. **IMA v. V.P. Shantha (1995)**: paid medical services are a **"service"**.
[2:3] - New in 2019: **CCPA**, **product liability**, e-commerce rules, **mediation cells** (attached to District, State and National Commissions, not High Courts), e-filing.
[2:4] - File where the complainant **lives or works**; admissibility decided within **21 days** or deemed admitted.
[2:5] - **Fees:** **nil up to Rs 5 lakh**; a small fee above that – so "no fee is required in any Consumer Forum" is **wrong**.
[2:6] - Consumers may get **food samples tested**; the **legal heir** of a deceased consumer may complain (UPSC 2012: statements 1 and 3 correct).
[2:7] > Exam angle: statement questions on fee, samples, legal heir; the resale buyer is not a consumer.
[2:8] ~~ Sources: Laxmikanth Indian Polity 8th ed. Part 2 p.78-83 (Consumer Commissions); app notes (old-notes.txt row 146); UPSC 2012 key

[4:0] - **E-Commerce Rules 2020**: **marketplace** entity = platform only; **inventory** entity = owns the goods; grievance officer acknowledges in **48 hours**, redresses in **one month**.
