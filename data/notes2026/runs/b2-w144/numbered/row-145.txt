## SUB 1 | Bonded Labour – Article 23, the 1976 Act and Rehabilitation
[1:0] - **Article 23** bans **begar** and forced labour → **Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Ordinance, 25 Oct 1975** → **Act of 1976**. **District Vigilance Committees** identify and free bonded labourers.

## SUB 2 | The Four Labour Codes and the Factories Act
[2:0] TABLE head: Code | Year | Main laws subsumed
  [2:0.0] Code on Wages | 2019 | Minimum Wages Act 1948, Payment of Wages Act 1936, Payment of Bonus Act 1965, Equal Remuneration Act 1976 (not the Contract Labour Act or the Industrial Disputes Act)
  [2:0.1] Industrial Relations Code | 2020 | Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Trade Unions Act, Standing Orders Act
  [2:0.2] Code on Social Security | 2020 | EPF, ESI, Maternity Benefit, Gratuity, Unorganised Workers' Social Security Act 2008
  [2:0.3] OSH Code | 2020 | 13 laws incl. Factories Act 1948, Contract Labour Act, Inter-State Migrant Workmen Act 1979
[2:1] - Together they replaced **29** central labour laws; all four in force from **21 November 2025**. Code on Wages renames the Inspector **Inspector-cum-Facilitator**. Critique: **Indian Labour Conference** not held since **2015**.
[2:2] - **IR Code thresholds:** prior permission for layoff/retrenchment/closure – **300** workers (earlier 100); Standing Orders – **300**; contract labour provisions 20 → **50**; no strike within **14 days** of notice (notice 60 days before); **fixed-term workers** – gratuity after **1 year**.
[2:3] - **Factories Act 1948:** minimum age **14**; Inspectors appointed by the **State Government**; every **District Magistrate** is an Inspector for his district; if no manager is named, the **occupier** is deemed manager.
[2:4] ~~ Sources: Economic Survey 2025-26 (29 laws into four Codes); app notes (old-notes.txt row 145); UPPCS 2021 key
## SUB 3 | Unorganised Sector – 2008 Act, NCEUS, e-Shram and Shram Suvidha
[3:0] # Unorganised Workers' Social Security Act, 2008
[3:1] - Set up the **National Social Security Board**; schemes in **Schedule I** – IGNOAPS, National Family Benefit Scheme, **Janani Suraksha Yojana**, Janashree Bima Yojana, AABY, RSBY (APY is not in it). Provides for **life and disability cover, health and maternity benefits, old-age protection** (health care, pension, insurance).
[3:2] - Merged into the **Code on Social Security, 2020**.
[3:3] - **NCEUS** (2004, **Arjun Sengupta**): **77%** living on **under Rs 20 a day**. **Over 90%** of India's workers are informal.
[3:4] # e-Shram
[3:5] - Launched **26 August 2021** (Ministry of Labour); India's first **Aadhaar-seeded National Database of Unorganised Workers**; age **16-59**; not income-tax payers, not EPFO/ESIC members; **12-digit UAN**.
[3:6] - **One-Stop Solution (2024)** links **15** central schemes; over **31 crore** registered (54% women, Jan 2026); **31.78 crore**, **AP 88.8 lakh** (July 2026).
[3:7] # Shram Suvidha
[3:8] - **LIN (Labour Identification Number)** is issued to **establishments** (not individual workers) on the **Shram Suvidha Portal** (2014) for unified labour compliance.
[3:9] ~~ Sources: Economic Survey 2025-26 Summary Part 2 p.33; app notes (old-notes.txt row 145)
