## SUB 1 | 73rd/74th Amendments – Women in Local Bodies
[1:0] # Article 243D (panchayats) / 243T (municipalities)
[1:1] - **243D(2):** not less than **one-third of SC/ST seats** for SC/ST women (not 1/4, not 50%).
[1:2] - **243D(3):** not less than **one-third of total seats** in every Panchayat; rotated.
[1:3] - **243D(4):** not less than one-third of **Chairperson** offices (Pradhan, Pramukh, Adhyaksha).
[1:4] - **243D(5) sunset:** SC/ST reservation tied to **Art. 334** (to 2030); **women's reservation has no sunset**.
[1:5] - **243T** gives the same for municipalities; ST seats in municipalities = **243T(1)**. **BC reservation** is left to States (optional).
[1:6] - Tenure **5 years** from first meeting (**243E**); minimum age to contest **21** (243F, 243V). **73rd in force 24 April 1993**.
[1:7] # States at 50%
[1:8] - One-third is a **floor, not a ceiling**. **Bihar** first with **50% (2006)**; MP, Uttarakhand, HP followed.
[1:9] - **MoPR (April 2026): 21 States and 2 UTs** have 50% for women (about 20 when APPSC asked); **Haryana** moved to 50% in **2020**. Women are **49.75%** of 25 lakh+ elected panchayat representatives.
[1:10] - Earlier one-third-only States (APPSC flashcard): **UP, Haryana (till 2020), Goa, J&K**. **UP Panchayat Laws (Amendment) Act 1994:** **one-third** for women.
[1:11] - **Andhra Pradesh:** **50%** in panchayats and municipalities. AP 2026: 34% BC (rural) and 33.33% (urban) approved.
[1:12] - **Nagaland (2017):** violent protests against **33% reservation for women in urban local bodies** (claimed to violate Art. 371A).

## SUB 3 | 106th Amendment and the Women's Reservation Bill
[3:0] - **Constitution (106th Amendment) Act 2023 = Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam:** **one-third** of Lok Sabha, State Assembly and Delhi Assembly seats for women, incl. one-third of SC/ST seats; **no OBC sub-quota**; **not** Rajya Sabha or Councils.
[3:1] - Before it, Arts 330-334 had **no provision for women**, so a **constitutional amendment** was needed (party ticket quotas need none).
[3:2] TABLE head: Article | Content
  [3:2.0] 330A / 332A / 239AA(2)(ba) | Lok Sabha / State Assemblies / Delhi Assembly
  [3:2.1] 334A | Starts after the first Census after the Act + delimitation (2027 Census); 15 years, extendable; seats rotate after each delimitation
[3:3] - **Assent 28 September 2023**; brought into force **16 April 2026**; stated in force for **J&K and Puducherry** Assemblies from 17 April 2026 (app notes).
[3:4] # Bill history
[3:5] TABLE head: Year | Bill | Fate
  [3:5.0] 1996 | 81st Amendment Bill (Deve Gowda) | Lapsed
  [3:5.1] 2008 | 108th Bill (Rajya Sabha) | Referred to the Law and Justice Standing Committee (chair E.M. Sudarsana Natchiappan; report Dec 2009 signed by Jayanti Natarajan); passed RS 9 March 2010; lapsed 2014
  [3:5.2] 2023 | 128th Bill | Passed 21 Sept 2023 → 106th Amendment
  [3:5.3] 2026 | 131st Bill (Lok Sabha 550 → 850, delimitation on 2011 Census) | Defeated 17 April 2026: 298-230, short of 352 (Art. 368)
[3:6] - Women's groups demand removing the **Census-delimitation condition**.
[3:7] - **Arts 325-326** = equal franchise; **Arts 84/173** = women can contest.
