## SUB 1 | UNESCO – Lists, Prizes and Membership
[1:0] - **Members**: **194** Member States + 12 Associate Members (2025-26). **Palestine** became the **195th** member in **October 2011** (South Sudan the 194th, days earlier); the US announced its withdrawal again in July 2025.
[1:1] - **Executive Board**: **58** members; India re-elected in **November 2021** for **four years** (2021-25).
[1:2] - **First woman Director-General**: **Irina Bokova** (Bulgaria), 2009. Now **Khaled El-Enany** (Egypt) – from **15 Nov 2025**; first Arab DG.
[1:3] - Programmes: **Information for All Programme (IFAP)**; **Man and the Biosphere (MAB)**; Memory of the World (1992); leads **SDG-4** (Education 2030).
[1:4] - **Kalinga Prize** for the popularisation of science – awarded by **UNESCO** (since 1952).
[1:5] - **Delors Commission** report to UNESCO (1996): "**Learning: The Treasure Within**" (four pillars of learning).
[1:6] - **World Capital of Architecture** (UNESCO + UIA): **Rio de Janeiro, 2020** – the first.
