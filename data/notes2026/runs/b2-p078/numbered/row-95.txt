## SUB 0 | Contractualisation and Outsourcing
[0:0] - **Outsourcing:** hiring outside sources (legal, IT, advertising, security) for regular work once done in-house; an outcome of globalisation, spread by IT – the method that "became popular in the recent past" for staffing jobs in both public and private sectors (APPSC G1).
[0:1]   - Distractors: contract appointments (a form of it, narrower), emergency recruitment, on-line recruitment (a channel, not a method of employing).
[0:2] - **Contract labour:** **20% (1999) → 40.7%** of manufacturing workforce (2022-23, ASI); such firms **31% lower productivity** (Aug 2025).
[0:3] - Weaker direct employer relation → weaker unions; **Industrial Relations Code 2020**: 10% or 100 workers to form a union.
[0:4] # AP angle
[0:5] - **APCOS** (2020): recruits outsourced staff and **pays directly**, cutting contractor middlemen.
[0:6] - **APSRTC:** about 8,000 outsourced staff paid via contractors; wage, PF, ESI irregularities (28 Jul 2026).
[0:7] - **Civil Services Board:** curbs political interference in postings.
[0:8] - Upper age limit for non-uniform direct recruitment raised **42 → 44** till 30 Sep 2027 (G.O. Ms. No. 187).
[0:9] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC Part 3 p.41 (outsourcing); app notes 2:95 (sub 4)
## SUB 1 | Downsizing the State and Pay Commissions
[1:0] - **Downsizing, disinvestment, privatisation** shrink the State; **FDI promotion** is a growth/external-sector measure, **not** a cut in State size.
[1:1] - Keyed set: downsizing, disinvestment, privatising higher education (UPSC 2010).
[1:2] - **Disinvestment:** sale of part of PSU equity to the public; need not transfer ownership or management. **Privatisation:** withdrawal or outright sale.
[1:3] - **Economic Survey 2025-26:** redefine "Government Company" so the State may hold **less than 51%**.
[1:4] # Pay Commissions and staff strength

## SUB 2 | New Public Management
[2:0] - Private-sector techniques in government (Phase 4 of Indian public administration after 1991); term popularised by **Christopher Hood (1991)**.
[2:1] - **Hood's doctrines:** hands-on professional management, explicit standards and performance measures, **output controls**, competition.
[2:2] - **Features:** performance auditing, **contracting out non-core services**, citizen as customer; it does **not** reject performance measures.
[2:3] - **Pioneers:** **UK (Thatcher), New Zealand, Australia, USA**.

## SUB 4 | Critiques of NPM and Citizen as Customer
[4:0] TABLE head: Thinker | Work | Idea
  [4:0.0] Denhardt & Denhardt | New Public Service (2000) | "Serve, not steer"; citizenship, public interest
  [4:0.1] Mark Moore | Creating Public Value (1995) | Value includes legitimacy and trust, not only efficiency
[4:1] - Both reject citizen → customer and State → service seller.
[4:2] - **Citizen-as-customer instruments:** Citizens' Charters, service standards, grievance redressal.
[4:3] - **Criticism:** citizen has **rights, not just purchasing power**; equity and accountability suffer.

## SUB 5 | Lateral Entry and Performance Management
[5:0] - **Normal route:** JS/Director posts by tenure deputation (**Central Staffing Scheme, DoPT**).
[5:1] - **Second ARC:** lateral entry at **Joint Secretary** level; performance-based appraisal and promotion; performance incentives.
[5:2] - **NITI Aayog Action Agenda:** wider entry at middle/senior levels; longer, stable tenures.
[5:3] - First lateral JS joined **2019**; **Aug 2024**: UPSC told to cancel advertisement for **45** posts.
[5:4] - **Debate:** domain expertise vs no reservation in single posts, conflict of interest, demotivated career officers.
[5:5] # Performance tools
[5:6] - **RFD (2009)**, PMES, Cabinet Secretariat: objectives, success indicators, targets = **Key Result Areas**.
[5:7] - **Referral Board** reviews adverse ACR remarks; **360-degree** multi-source feedback.
[5:8] - **Good Governance Index:** uniform framework for States/UTs. **NeSDA** (2019; 2025 portal June 2026). **GRAI:** **11 indicators, 4 dimensions** on CPGRAMS.
[5:9] - India **97th** in UN EGDI 2024.
