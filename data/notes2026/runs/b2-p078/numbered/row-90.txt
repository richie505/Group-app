## SUB 1 | Preamble, Objectives and Nature of the Right
[1:0] - Preamble terms: **transparency of information**, **revelation of information**, **informed citizenry**, **paramountcy of the democratic ideal** (all four).
[1:1] - Objectives: transparency and accountability, **answerability**, **containing corruption**, empowering citizens, making democracy work for the people – **NOT** "empowering women and weaker sections".
[1:2] - RTI **mandates a timely response** to citizens' requests for government information (not NeGP, Aadhaar or RTE).
[1:3] - Nature: a **statutory (legal) right** under the 2005 Act; the **right to know** is also read into **Article 19(1)(a)** (State of U.P. v. Raj Narain, 1975) – hence "both" a legal and a fundamental right in most keys.
[1:4] - **Namit Sharma v. Union of India (2012)** – RTI case on Information Commissions.
[1:5] - Impact: raised the **sense of responsibility in bureaucracy**, but "miles to go" (both true, R does not explain A); the Act is **misused by both** applicants and public authorities.
[1:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Rocket Polity Part 1 p.54 (legal right; Namit Sharma 2012); app's current notes 2:90 sub 1, 11

## SUB 5 | Information Commissions and Penalties
[5:0] TABLE head: Item | CIC | SIC
  [5:0.0] Composition | Chief IC + up to 10 ICs | State Chief IC + up to 10 SICs
  [5:0.1] Appointed by | President | Governor
  [5:0.2] Committee | PM (chair), LoP in Lok Sabha, Union Cabinet Minister nominated by PM | CM (chair), LoP in the Assembly, State Cabinet Minister nominated by the CM
  [5:0.3] Removal for misbehaviour | President, after Supreme Court inquiry | Governor, after Supreme Court inquiry
[5:1] - **No Chief Justice** (CJI or High Court CJ) on either committee.
[5:2] - Eligibility: eminence in law, S&T, social service, management, **journalism**, mass media, administration; **cannot be an MP or a member of any State Legislature**, hold office of profit or be linked to a party.
[5:3] - Statutory bodies with **civil-court powers**; term now **prescribed by the Centre (3 years)** or 65 (RTI Amendment Act 2019; earlier 5 years).
[5:4] - **Section 20 penalty**: imposed by the **CIC or SIC** (not courts) – **₹250 per day, max ₹25,000**; burden of proof on the PIO; Commission can also recommend disciplinary action.
[5:5] - **AP SIC**: **Vajji (Vajja) Srinivasa Rao** appointed Chief Information Commissioner (Jan 2026) with four ICs; 2026 inspection of 258 offices found Section 4(1)(b) violations.
[5:6] - Before 2019: CIC's salary = **Chief Election Commissioner**, IC = Election Commissioner; State CIC = Election Commissioner, State IC = **Chief Secretary**; term was **5 years or 65**.
[5:7] - Contrast **CVC**: CVC + up to 2 Vigilance Commissioners; **4 years or 65**.
