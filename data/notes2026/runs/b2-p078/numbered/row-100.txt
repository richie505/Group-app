## SUB 0 | Competition Commission of India
[0:0] - Statutory body under the **Competition Act, 2002**, as amended by the **Competition (Amendment) Act, 2007**; **Ministry of Corporate Affairs**.
[0:1] - The Act **replaced the MRTP Act, 1969** and its **MRTP Commission** – shift from curbing monopolies to **promoting competition**.
[0:2] - **Established:** notified **14 October 2003**, but legal challenges delayed it; **fully constituted / functional in March 2009** (MCA: "established in March 2009").
[0:3] - **Function:** to **ensure fair competition in markets**, prevent practices with adverse effect on competition, protect consumers – not registering competitors or monitoring capital markets.
[0:4] - **Composition:** **Chairperson + 2 to 6 other members** appointed by the **Central Government** (original 2002 Act: up to 10) – so "chairperson and five members" is wrong.
[0:5] TABLE head: CCI area | What it does
  [0:5.0] Anti-competitive agreements | Cartels, bid-rigging
  [0:5.1] Abuse of dominant position | e.g. WhatsApp 2021 privacy policy – Meta fined ₹213.14 crore
  [0:5.2] Combinations | Mergers and acquisitions; ₹2,000 crore deal-value threshold for start-up "killer acquisitions"
[0:6] - Appeals: **NCLAT from 26 May 2017** (earlier **COMPAT**) → Supreme Court. CCI's **Digital Markets Division**; proposed **Digital Competition Bill** for **Systemically Significant Digital Enterprises**.
[0:7] - Market forms: many sellers of differentiated brands (e.g. **toothpaste**) = **monopolistic competition**; identical product = perfect competition.

## SUB 1 | TRAI and TDSAT
[1:0] - **TRAI** = **Telecom Regulatory Authority of India**, set up **20 February 1997** under the **TRAI Act, 1997**; regulates **tariffs, interconnection, quality of service**.
[1:1] - **TDSAT** (May 2000), created by the **TRAI (Amendment) Act, 2000**, to **separate adjudication** from regulation; also hears appeals under the **IT Act, 2000** and the **DPDP Act**. No separate "TDSAT Act".
[1:2] - **TRAI MyCall** app revamped for call-quality rating (Aug 2026), after **DND** and **MySpeed**.
[1:3] ~~ Sources: app notes 2:100 (TRAI, TDSAT)
## SUB 2 | FSSAI – Food Regulator
[2:0] - **FSSAI**: under the **Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006**; set up **2008**; administered by the **Ministry of Health and Family Welfare** (not DGHS). It is the agency that enforces food safety laws (not FDA, WHO or FAO).
[2:1] - The Act consolidated **eight** laws and orders incl. the **Prevention of Food Adulteration Act, 1954**.
[2:2] - From **1 July 2019** (Packaging Regulations, 2018): **no recycled plastics or newspaper** to wrap food.
[2:3] - **Eat Right India** (2018). **2026 reforms**: perpetual validity of licences; registration up to **₹1.5 crore** turnover, State licence ₹1.5-50 crore, Central licence above **₹50 crore**.
[2:4] ~~ Sources: app notes 2:100 (FSSAI)

## SUB 4 | Energy and Airport Regulators – CERC, SERCs, PNGRB, AERA
[4:0] - **CERC** = inter-State electricity; regulator of carbon trading under the **Carbon Credit Trading Scheme**. **SERCs** = intra-State tariffs. **APTEL** hears appeals from CERC, SERCs and PNGRB.
[4:1] - **APERC** constituted **1999** under the **AP Electricity Reform Act, 1998**; under the AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 it stayed joint regulator for **not more than six months**.
[4:2] - **PNGRB** regulates **City Gas Distribution** and authorises pipelines; not electricity. **Jul 2026**: **10 Geographical Areas** in AP approved for CGD; **East Godavari** has the most connections.
[4:3] - **Kakinada–Srikakulam natural gas pipeline** (about 271 km): authorised by PNGRB (16 Jul 2014) to **AP Gas Distribution Corporation (APGDC)**; APGDC later surrendered it and PNGRB **terminated the authorisation on 1 Dec 2023**.
[4:4] - **AERA** regulates **aeronautical charges** at major airports.

## SUB 5 | Data Protection Board of India
[5:0] - **DPDP Act, 2023** (Aug 2023): consent-based processing. **DPBI**: adjudicatory body, **4 members** (DPDP Rules 2025), appointed by the Centre on a Selection Committee's advice.
[5:1] - Penalty up to **₹250 crore**, **no imprisonment**; appeal to **TDSAT within 60 days** → Supreme Court.
[5:2] - DPDP Act amended **RTI Sec. 8(1)(j)** (wider personal-information exemption).
[5:3] ~~ Sources: app notes 2:100 (Data Protection Board)
## SUB 6 | Education, Film and Other Regulators
[6:0] - **Education** is in the **Concurrent List** (moved from the State List by the **42nd Amendment, 1976**) – regulated by both Union and States; UGC, AICTE, NCTE are central regulators.
[6:1] - **NCTE and NAAC** signed the MoU (2002) for **accreditation of teacher education institutions**.
[6:2] - **CBFC** (Central Board of Film Certification), under the **Ministry of I&B** (Cinematograph Act, 1952), regulates **public exhibition of films**.
[6:3] - **Viksit Bharat Shiksha Adhishthan (VBSA) Bill, 2025** (HECI idea): introduced in **Lok Sabha, Dec 2025** → **Joint Committee**; not passed. Would repeal **UGC Act 1956, AICTE Act 1987, NCTE Act 1993** (not the NMC Act 2019); three councils – **Regulatory, Accreditation, Standards** (no **Funding Council**).
