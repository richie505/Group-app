## SUB 0 | Fifth Schedule – Scheduled Areas, Governor and Tribes Advisory Council
[0:0] - **Article 244(1), Part X:** the **Fifth Schedule** governs the administration and control of **Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes** in any State **other than Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura and Mizoram**.
[0:1] - **Ten States** have Scheduled Areas: **Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Rajasthan**.
[0:2] - STs reside in what the Constitution calls **Scheduled Areas** (Fifth Schedule); the purpose of both Fifth and Sixth Schedules is to **protect the interests of Scheduled Tribes**.
[0:3] # Declaration
[0:4] - **President** declares an area a Scheduled Area **by Order**; can increase, decrease, **alter boundaries**, rescind – in consultation with the Governor (not the Governor, Parliament or Assembly).
[0:5] - **Criteria** (not in the Constitution): **preponderance of tribals**, compactness and reasonable size, viable administrative unit (**largest unit the district, lowest a cluster of villages in a block**), **economic backwardness** – not industrial development.
[0:6] # Executive power
[0:7] - The **State's executive power extends** to Scheduled Areas – the State does not lose it, and no local body takes over (UPSC 2025: neither statement correct).
[0:8] - The **Union** can only **give directions** to the State on their administration – it does not take over the area.
[0:9] - The **Governor** reports to the **President**, **annually or whenever required** – not the Chief Minister to the Home Ministry (UPSC 2023: only two statements correct).
[0:10] # Tribes Advisory Council (Para 4)
[0:11] - Compulsory in every State **with Scheduled Areas**; the President may direct one in a State having STs but no Scheduled Areas.
[0:12] - **Not more than 20 members** ("up to 20"), about **three-fourths** of them **ST members of the State Assembly**; advises the Governor on ST welfare – no legislative or judicial power.
[0:13] # Laws and regulations (Para 5)
[0:14] - The **Governor** may direct that **any Act of Parliament or the State Legislature shall not apply**, or apply with modifications, to a Scheduled Area (after consulting the TAC).
[0:15] - The **Governor** makes **regulations for peace and good government**: may **prohibit or restrict transfer of land** by or among STs (so declaring an area Scheduled **prevents tribal land passing to non-tribals**), regulate land allotment and **money-lending**; may repeal or amend Acts – all need the **President's assent**.
[0:16] - **Parliament** may amend the Fifth and Sixth Schedules by **ordinary law** (not an Art. 368 amendment).
[0:17] > APPSC G2 "all correct except": the false one is "Governor is **not** authorised to direct that an Act shall not apply".

## SUB 1 | Sixth Schedule – Tribal Areas and Autonomous District Councils
[1:0] - **Articles 244(2) and 275(1):** **Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram** only – NOT Nagaland (371A), **Manipur** (371C), Arunachal or Sikkim.
[1:1] - **Not** applicable wherever STs exceed 40% – the four States are named; the areas stay **within the State's executive authority** (and State Legislature's, subject to the Schedule).
[1:2] - **Ten autonomous districts:** Assam 3, Meghalaya 3, Tripura 1, Mizoram 3.
[1:3] - The **Governor** (not the President) organises and re-organises autonomous districts, alters areas, names and boundaries; where there are different tribes he can divide a district into **autonomous regions**.
[1:4] - **Autonomous region** = a unit **within an autonomous district**, with its own **Regional Council**.
[1:5] - **District Council: 30 members – 26 elected** by adult franchise, **4 nominated** by the Governor; elected members 5 years (not 24 + 6).
[1:6] - **Powers:** laws on land, forests, canal water, shifting cultivation, village administration, **inheritance**, marriage and divorce, social customs (Governor's assent); village courts – **High Court jurisdiction specified by the Governor**; land revenue and taxes.
[1:7] - **Application of laws:** Acts do not apply or apply with modifications – direction by the **Governor in Assam** (Acts of Parliament and State); in Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram by the **President** for Acts of Parliament.
[1:8] - **Article 244A:** Parliament may form an **autonomous State within Assam** comprising certain tribal areas, with a local legislature or council of ministers or both.
[1:9] - **Income Tax Act s.10(26):** STs residing in specified Sixth Schedule areas are exempt on certain incomes.
[1:10] > APPSC G2 Mains 2025 (40%-rule / outside State authority / only President reorganises / no High Court): **none** is correct.

## SUB 2 | PESA Act, 1996
[2:0] - **Why:** **Art. 243M** keeps Part IX out of Scheduled and Tribal Areas; **243M(4)(b)** lets Parliament extend it by an **ordinary law** – done by the **Provisions of the Panchayats (Extension to the Scheduled Areas) Act, 1996** (PESA / Extension Act).
[2:1] - **Passed 1996**; in force **24 December 1996**; based on the **Dilip Singh Bhuria Committee** (1995).
[2:2] - Extends the **73rd Amendment (Part IX)** to **Fifth Schedule (Scheduled) Areas** only – not to Sixth Schedule Tribal Areas, not J&K.
[2:3] - **Ten PESA States** = the ten Fifth Schedule States; **not** in Assam, Meghalaya, **Tamil Nadu** (RAS 2016).
[2:4] - **Objectives:** extend Part IX with modifications; **self-rule** for tribals; **Gram Sabha the nucleus**; administration consistent with **traditional practices**; safeguard traditions and customs; free tribals from exploitation; stop higher Panchayats taking over lower-level powers – so "**all of the above**".
[2:5] - **NOT an objective:** creating **autonomous regions** (that is the Sixth Schedule) – UPSC 2013.
[2:6] # Gram Sabha powers
[2:7] TABLE head: Subject | Power
  [2:7.0] Plans and projects | Approves before the village Panchayat takes them up; identifies beneficiaries
  [2:7.1] Use of funds | Village Panchayat must get a certificate of utilisation of funds from the Gram Sabha (mandatory)
  [2:7.2] Traditions | Safeguards customs, cultural identity, community resources, customary dispute resolution
  [2:7.3] Land acquisition / resettlement | Consultation (not consent)
  [2:7.4] Minor minerals | Recommendation mandatory for prospecting licence / mining lease – minor minerals only, not "any mineral"
  [2:7.5] Minor forest produce | Ownership
  [2:7.6] Tribal land | Prevent alienation; restore unlawfully alienated land
  [2:7.7] Liquor, money-lending, village markets | Prohibit / regulate; control; manage village markets
[2:8] - **UPSC 2012:** stop land transfer + MFP ownership correct, "recommendation for **any** mineral" wrong → **1 and 2 only**.
[2:9] - **Reservation:** STs at least **one-half** of seats; **all chairpersons ST**; State may nominate unrepresented tribes (≤ one-tenth).
[2:10] - **Gram Sabha meeting:** presided over by an **ST member elected by the Gram Sabha** (not the Sarpanch), for one year; else the oldest ST woman present.
[2:11] - Gram Sabha may be at **habitation** level (para, majra, tola); conflicting State laws lapse **one year** after assent.
[2:12] - **Zila Panchayat:** minor water bodies, social-sector institutions, Tribal Sub-Plans.
[2:13] - **Implementation gaps:** minor-mineral and MFP provisions least implemented; **40%+** PESA representatives could not explain a Gram Sabha (MoPR, Dec 2025); Tana Bhagats – no Gram Sabha consent for Bharatmala; **Odisha** – only draft rules (Apr 2026).

## SUB 3 | Andhra Pradesh – Scheduled Areas, PESA and ITDAs
[3:0] - **AP PESA law:** **AP Panchayat Raj (Amendment) Act, 1998 (Act 7 of 1998)** amended the AP PR Act 1994 – AP implemented PESA from **1998**; **AP PESA Rules, 2011** (G.O.Ms.No.66, PR&RD, 24 Mar 2011).
[3:1] - **AP PESA Rules, 2011:** the **Gram (village) Panchayat** concerned acts as the **market committee** for village markets/shandies in Scheduled Areas.
[3:2] - **PESA districts (13-district map):** **5** – Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, **Visakhapatnam**, East Godavari, West Godavari; since **April 2022** the agency tracts lie mainly in **Alluri Sitharama Raju** (carved from Visakhapatnam) and **Parvathipuram Manyam** districts.
[3:3] - **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014:** **s.29 + its Sixth Schedule** amend the **Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order, 1950** for AP (the Act's own Sixth Schedule, not the Constitution's).
[3:4] - **Agency tracts** of the **Eastern Ghats**; **34** ST communities; **2,038** PESA Gram Sabhas.
[3:5] - **7 ITDAs, 76 mandals:** **Paderu, Rampachodavaram, Chintoor** (Alluri Sitharama Raju; Chintoor – Godavari Koya belt); **Seethampeta, Parvathipuram** (Parvathipuram Manyam); **KR Puram** (Eluru); **Srisailam** (Chenchus, Nallamala).
[3:6] - **Alluri Sitharama Raju** district: tribal-majority, with **PVTGs**; **Araku** – ST Lok Sabha seat; **Tribal Medical College, Paderu** (2024-25, 50 seats).
[3:7] - **PESA Mahotsav** (30 years of PESA) – **Visakhapatnam**, Dec 2025.

## SUB 4 | Land, Forest Rights and Landmark Cases
[4:0] - **Samatha v. State of AP (1997, SC):** transfer/lease of **tribal land in Scheduled Areas to private parties for mining** is **null and void** – under the **Fifth Schedule**.
[4:1] - **AP Scheduled Areas Land Transfer Regulation 1 of 1959**, amended by **Regulation 1 of 1970** – bars transfers between tribals and non-tribals and among non-tribals.
[4:2] - **Forest Rights Act, 2006:** the **Gram Sabha** is the authority to **initiate the process** of determining the nature and extent of individual and community forest rights; MFP ownership; diversion for public utilities needs Gram Sabha recommendation; OTFDs – **three generations (75 years)** before 2005.
[4:3] - **Niyamgiri (2013):** Gram Sabhas decided whether bauxite mining affected their **religious and forest rights** – rejected **Vedanta**.
