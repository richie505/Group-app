## SUB 0 | NSAP and Old-age Pensions
[0:0] # National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP)
[0:1] - Launched **15 August 1995** (first national social assistance programme); ministry **Rural Development (MoRD)**, not Social Justice; rests on **Articles 41 and 42**.
[0:2] - Cabinet approved continuation **FY 2026-27 to 2030-31** (outlay ₹53,428 crore); renamed **Pradhan Mantri Samajik Sahayata Yojana** (sub-schemes e.g. PM Vriddhavastha Pension Yojana, PM Divyangjan Pension Yojana) (2026).
[0:3] - Five components:
[0:4]   - 1. **IGNOAPS** – old-age pension (BPL, 60+): Rs 200/month (60-79), Rs 500 (80+); Centre's share frozen at Rs 200 since 2007; about 2.2 crore beneficiaries (June 2026).
[0:5]   - 2. **IGNWPS** – widow pension (BPL widows 40-79).
[0:6]   - 3. **IGNDPS** – disability pension (80% or more disability).
[0:7]   - 4. **NFBS** – lump sum on death of the primary breadwinner (Rs 20,000).
[0:8]   - 5. **Annapurna** – **10 kg free food grains** a month to seniors eligible for but not getting IGNOAPS.
[0:9] # State schemes
[0:10] TABLE head: Scheme | State | Feature
  [0:10.0] Mukhyamantri Vriddhajan Pension Yojana | Bihar (2019) | Universal: all aged 60+ with no other pension; Rs 400 (60-79), Rs 500 (80+); no income bar
  [0:10.1] NTR Bharosa Pension (earlier YSR Pension Kanuka) | Andhra Pradesh | Old-age pension raised from Rs 3,000 to Rs 4,000/month (G.O. 43, 13 June 2024); minimum age 60, but 50 for STs (incl. PVTGs)
[0:11] - AP: 7 physiotherapy centres under the National Action Plan for Senior Citizens treated 87,240 patients (APSES 2024-25).

## SUB 2 | Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007
[2:0] - Enacted in **2007**; nodal ministry **MoSJE**; brought into force **state by state** from **29 December 2007**.
[2:1] - Creates a **legal obligation** on adult **children (biological, adopted, step)** and **legal heirs** to maintain parents and senior citizens who cannot maintain themselves.
[2:2] - The Act **punishes children for neglecting old parents**: **abandonment** of a senior citizen – up to **3 months** jail and/or **Rs 5,000** fine.
[2:3] - **Old age homes:** at least **one per district**.
[2:4] TABLE head: Section | Provision
  [2:4.0] Section 20 | Medical care of senior citizens; basis of the NPHCE
  [2:4.1] Section 23 | Property transfer made on condition of care can be declared void by the Tribunal if the transferee neglects the parent
  [2:4.2] Feature | Detail
  [2:4.3] Maintenance Tribunal | Sub-division level; in AP headed by RDO / Sub-Collector
  [2:4.4] Appellate Tribunal | District level; in AP headed by the District Collector
  [2:4.5] Maximum maintenance | Rs 10,000 per month
  [2:4.6] Time limit | Case decided within 90 days
[2:5] - **2019 Amendment Bill:** proposed **removing the Rs 10,000 cap** and covering **in-laws and grandchildren**; it **lapsed** (not passed).
[2:6] - **First State law:** **Himachal Pradesh** Maintenance of Parents and Dependents Act, **2001**.

## SUB 4 | Elder Line, Ageing Data and Elder Abuse
[4:0] - **Elder Line 14567** – National Helpline for Senior Citizens; **MoSJE**; component of **AVYAY**; technical support from **Tata Trusts** and **NSE Foundation**.
[4:1] - **LASI** (Longitudinal Ageing Study in India), Wave 1 **2017-18**, by **IIPS, Mumbai**, under MoHFW; about **72,250** persons aged **45+** and their spouses.
[4:2] - **India Ageing Report 2023** – by **UNFPA** with **IIPS** (not HelpAge India): elderly to **exceed children (0-14) by 2046**; feminisation and ruralisation of ageing.
[4:3] - **AP elderly share:** about **12.4%** of AP's population aged **60+** in **2021** (*Elderly in India 2021*, NSO); India **10.1%**; Kerala 16.5%.
[4:4] - **HelpAge India:** elderly-care services; releases **elder-abuse surveys**.
[4:5] - **Shyam Saran Negi** (Kalpa, Kinnaur, HP) – independent India's **first voter** (Oct 1951).
