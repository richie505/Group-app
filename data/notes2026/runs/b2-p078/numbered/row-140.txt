  [1:1.0] 1981 | UN International Year of Disabled Persons

[1:2] - Order: UNCRPD adopted → India ratified → RPwD enacted → in force.
[1:3] # RPwD Act 2016 – key provisions
[1:4] - **First rights-based** disability law; **replaced the 1995 Act**; meets UNCRPD obligations; bars discrimination in government employment.
[1:5] - The 2016 law is named **"The Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act"** – the 1995 law was "Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act".
[1:6] - **Sec. 2(s)**: PwD = long-term physical, mental, intellectual or sensory impairment which, with barriers, blocks full participation.
[1:7] TABLE head: Provision | Value
  [1:7.0] Specified disabilities | 21 (from 7), e.g. autism, acid attack victims, sickle cell disease; Centre may add
  [1:7.1] Benchmark disability | At least 40%
  [1:7.2] Government jobs | 4%
  [1:7.3] Higher education | 5% seats
  [1:7.4] Free education | Benchmark-disability children 6–18
  [1:7.5] Monitoring | Chief Commissioner for PwDs, Sec. 75(1)
[1:8] - Survivors of **forcible acid ingestion** recognised as acid attack victims (SC, 5 May 2026).
[1:9] > Trap: passed **2016** (not 2014/2018), in force 19 April 2017; 4% jobs vs 5% higher education; 7 → **21** (not 17 or 15).

## SUB 2 | Types of Disability – Learning Disability and Blindness
[2:0] - **Learning disability** = difficulty in a specific academic skill despite normal intelligence: **dyslexia** (reading), dysgraphia (writing), dyscalculia (maths).
[2:1] - **Dyslexia** is the example specific to learning disability; **autism spectrum disorder**, **ADHD** and **mental retardation (intellectual disability)** are separate developmental conditions, not specific learning disabilities.
[2:2] - RPwD Act 2016 lists **specific learning disabilities** among its 21 disabilities, separately from autism and intellectual disability.
[2:3] - **New definition of blindness (April 2017, MoHFW, WHO criteria):** a person is blind if unable to **count fingers from 3 metres** (the 1976 definition used **6 metres**); programme renamed National Programme for Control of Blindness and Visual Impairment.
[2:4] > Trap: dyslexia = learning disability; ADHD and autism are not "specific learning disabilities"; blindness now 3 m, not 6 m.
[2:5] ~~ Sources: https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/archive/nation/india-changes-definition-of-blindness-394675; general knowledge, not checked (learning-disability types); app notes 2:140 sub 2

## SUB 6 | Data and Andhra Pradesh – SADAREM
[6:0] - **Census 2011**: **2.68 crore** PwDs = **2.21%** (about **2.2%**) of the population (2001: 2.13%); largest: **movement 20.3%**, hearing 18.9%, seeing 18.8%. WHO estimate ~16% → under-reporting.
[6:1] - **Census 2027**: adds acid attack, neurological and blood disorders; autism and dwarfism left out.
[6:2] - **SADAREM** (Software for Assessment of Disability for Access, Rehabilitation and Empowerment), Andhra Pradesh: **centralised database** that assesses **disabled persons** and issues digitally signed permanent disability certificates; 3,14,211 certificates in 2024-25.
[6:3] - **AP**: **11.04 lakh** PwDs (Census 2011); largest type orthopaedic.
[6:4] - **Divyang Shakti** (AP): **100% free APSRTC travel** from **18 March 2026** for 40%+ disability; escort 50%; SADAREM certification.
[6:5] - AP pension **₹6,000** a month; 52,263 assistive devices (11 Jun 2026).
[6:6] - **AP Differently Abled and Senior Citizens Assistance Corporation**: aids, appliances, economic rehabilitation.
[6:7] - AP Samagra Shiksha: **Best State** in implementing RPwD Act (2024-25); ₹3,500 per CwSN; 679 Bhavitha Centres; 9 DDRCs, 70 NGOs under DDRS.
[6:8] > Trap: SADAREM = AP disability database (not minorities/women/children); UDID = the national one.
