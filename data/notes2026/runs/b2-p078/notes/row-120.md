=== Capital City Debate
| Capital (2020 plan) | City |
|---|---|
| Executive | Visakhapatnam |
| Legislative | Amaravati |
| Judicial | Kurnool |
- **2020:** **AP Decentralisation and Inclusive Development of All Regions Act, 2020** + **APCRDA (Repeal) Act, 2020**.
- **Nov 2021:** Repeal Bill introduced in the AP Assembly; both Acts **withdrawn (2021)**.
- **31 Jan 2023:** CM Y.S. Jagan Mohan Reddy declared **Visakhapatnam** the capital.
- **2026:** the Lok Sabha passed the **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Bill, 2026**, formally declaring **Amaravati the (sole) capital** after the 10-year joint-capital period ended.
- **Amaravati:** planned to be built in **three phases**; **M. Swarajyamma** is cited as the first lady to donate **₹1 crore** for its construction (APPSC PYQ).
- **Jan 2026:** coalition govt committed to finishing Amaravati in three years; seeks a central law to protect it. **1 Feb 2026:** CII AP sought a special package for Amaravati.
- YSRCP view: Amaravati spending hurts north coastal and drought-prone Rayalaseema.
- Compare: **Kalyana Karnataka** – **Art. 371J** (since 2013); disparities continue.

- **Srikrishna Committee (Feb 2010):** set up **on the question of Telangana State**.
# Srikrishna Committee findings
- **First option:** keep AP **as it is (status quo)** – found least workable; the six options ranged from status quo to bifurcation.
- **Nature of the demand:** a desire for **greater democracy and empowerment** – **greater political space and power** within a political unit.
- **Per capita income growth 2000-01 to 2007-08:** highest in **Telangana excluding Hyderabad (60.3%)**; 63% with Hyderabad; Rayalaseema 58%; Coastal Andhra 54.1%.
- **Urbanisation 1961-2001:** highest growth in **Telangana** (Hyderabad-led).
- Telangana (excluding Hyderabad) on par with or a shade lower than Coastal Andhra on most indicators; far better once Hyderabad is included.

=== AP Reorganisation Act, 2014
- **Appointed day: 2 June 2014** – AP and Telangana became separate States (Telangana = 29th State); **Hyderabad** common capital for up to **10 years**.
- **Sec. 46(2)-(3):** backward-areas package; **Sec. 94:** tax incentives.
- **7 backward districts** = 4 Rayalaseema + 3 North Andhra; grant of **₹50 crore per district per year**.
- **SCS:** PM promised in Rajya Sabha (**Feb 2014**); **14th Finance Commission** ended the general/special distinction → "**special assistance measure**"; demand still a political issue.

=== Mulki Rules, Six Point Formula and Movements
- **Jai Andhra movement (1972):** reaction to the Supreme Court **upholding Mulki rules**; sought separate Andhra; started in Coastal Andhra and Rayalaseema.
- **Six Point Formula (21 Sept 1973)** → **32nd Amendment, 1973** inserted **Article 371D** – the **special provision for Andhra Pradesh** (zonal system of local cadres, education and employment) and **371E**; ended the Regional Committee (Art. 371(1)).
- **8-Point Formula (1969):** set up a high-level committee under a judge and a special committee on Telangana surpluses.
- **Telangana movements:** 1969 agitation; 2009-14 phase → Telangana State 2014.
- **⚠ Trap:** Capital-location committee came under the **2014 Act**, not the 8-Point Formula.

=== Historical Agreements
- **Sri Bagh Pact (16 Nov 1937):** first Coastal-Rayalaseema accord; drafted by **Pattabhi Sitaramayya**; Rayalaseema to choose **either the capital or the High Court**; aimed to raise Rayalaseema's farm and economic level to coastal districts. Andhra State capital → **Kurnool** (1953).
- **Gentlemen's Agreement – 1956** (not 1969): reused the Sri Bagh template for Telangana safeguards.
- Such pacts institutionalised sub-regional fault lines; poor implementation fed agitations.
