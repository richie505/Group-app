=== P. Anandacharlu and Andhra at the Early Congress
# P. Anandacharlu (Panapakkam Anandacharyulu, 1843-1907)
- **First Telugu (Andhra) President of the INC** – **7th session, Nagpur, 1891** (not Kakinada, Meerut or Lucknow; not 1889, 1890 or 1892).
- **Secretary of the Madras Mahajana Sabha (1884)**; attended the **first INC session (1885)**.
- **⚠ Trap:** Pattabhi Sitaramayya, Konda Venkatappayya, Nyapathi Subbarao and Kaleswara Rao never were the first Telugu INC president.
# Andhra at the first sessions
- **Bombay 1885 (72 delegates):** Andhra delegates **P. Anandacharlu**, **P. Rangaiah Naidu**, **Nyapathi Subbarao (Rajahmundry)**, **Gutti Kesava Pillai (Gooty)**, **Venkata Subbarayudu (Machilipatnam)**.
- **Demands of the first session:** **simultaneous ICS exams** in England and India; **elected legislative councils**; **cut in military expenditure**; **opposition to the annexation of Upper Burma**.
- **Calcutta 1886:** presided by **Naoroji**; **21 Andhra members** out of 436.
- Madras session **1914**: **256 Andhra delegates**.
> NextGen's "Modern India Part 1" p.11 wrongly puts Anandacharlu at the 1886 session; the session list settles **Nagpur 1891**.

=== Associations – Madras Sabhas and the Krishna District Association
# Madras associations
| Association | Year | Founder / office-bearers |
|---|---|---|
| **Madras Native Association** | **26 Feb 1852** | **Gazula Lakshmi Narasu Chetty** (founder, first president) |
| **Madras Mahajana Sabha** | **16 May 1884** | **P. Rangaiah Naidu** (president), **P. Anandacharlu** (secretary); with G. Subramania Iyer, M. Veeraraghavachari |
# Krishna District Association (1892)
- **Formed in 1892** – the **first district association in the Madras Presidency** (APPSC 2021), set up **on the lines suggested at the first INC session**, to fight British rule on social and public issues.
- **Organiser: Ramaswamy Gupta** (not Gajula Lakshmi Narasu, Mutnuri Krishna Rao or Kokkonda Venkataratnam).
- **First meeting (conference): Guntur, July 1892**; **second: Machilipatnam, 1893**.
- **⚠ Key fixed:** one APPSC Group 2 (2012) key gives **Machilipatnam** for the first meeting – wrong; every NextGen source and Rocket give **Guntur**.
- **1894:** Krishna ryots met at **Eluru** against higher water rates; the 1894 session was presided by **Nyapathi Subbarao**.
- Its **1899 resolution** led to **Krishna Patrika (1902)**.

=== Early Telugu Press, 1835-1905
| Paper | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Satyadoota** (1835) | **First Telugu journal** – published by the **Christian Association of Bellary** |
| **Andhra Prakasika** (1885) | **First political journal/weekly in Andhra**; Telugu news weekly from **Madras**; editor **A.C. (A.P.) Parthasarathi Naidu**; supported the Congress |
| **Krishna Patrika** (2 Feb 1902) | **Machilipatnam**; started by **Konda Venkatappayya** with **Dasu Narayana Rao**; later edited by **Mutnuri Krishna Rao** |
| **Vyjayanti**; People's Magazine | **Nyapathi Subbarao** (monthly; editor) |
| Deshabhimani, Sasilekha, Rasikollasini, Gowtami, Vivekavardhani | Among about **20 Telugu journals by 1905** |
- **Role:** journals **criticised administrative failures** and spread **nationalism and democracy**.
> First Telugu journal = **Satyadoota**; first political journal = **Andhra Prakasika** (not Krishna Patrika, Deshabhimani or Golconda Patrika).

=== Andhra Moderate Leaders and Local Politics to 1905
# Leaders
| Person | Role |
|---|---|
| **Nyapathi Subbarao** | Rajahmundry lawyer; titles **Rajabahadur, Padyavinoda**; founded **Vyjayanti**, the **Godavari District Association** and the Hindu Samaj, Rajahmundry; Congress General Secretary; later **first president of the Andhra PCC** and of the **2nd Andhra Conference (Vijayawada, 1914)** |
| **Mocherla Ramachandra Rao** | "**Gokhale of South India**" |
| **G. Subramania Iyer** | "**Father of South India**" (not Telugu) |
| **Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao** | Later (**1919, 7th Andhra Mahasabha, Guntur**) gave a data-led critique of the drain from Andhra to Madras |
| Others | V.V. Jogaiah, Gade Raghava Rao, A.P. Patro, B.N. Sarma |
- Andhra Moderates **repeated the INC's economic critique** (drain of wealth) and its constitutional demands.
- **After 1908**, with the Extremists crushed, **Konda Venkatappayya and B.N. Sarma** came to the fore.
# Order of events
- Madras Native Association (**1852**) → Madras Mahajana Sabha (**1884**) → first INC (**1885**) → Anandacharlu presides (**1891**) → Krishna District Association (**1892**) → Krishna Patrika (**1902**).
# Local politics and 1905
- **Guntur district** carved out of Krishna in **1904**; Konda Venkatappayya moved there.
- **1905:** real political awakening came with **Vandemataram/Swadeshi**; Andhras opposed both the partition of Bengal and the **transfer of Ganjam** from Madras – opposing Ganjam's transfer is called the "**first victory**" of the Andhra movement.
