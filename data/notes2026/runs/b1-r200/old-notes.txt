## SUB 2 | Dadabhai Naoroji
[200:2:0] - INC president **three times (not four)**: **Calcutta 1886, Lahore 1893, Calcutta 1906**.
[200:2:1] - **Calcutta 1906** – first demand for **Swaraj** as the goal (APPSC 2025).
[200:2:2] - **First Indian member of the British Parliament** – House of Commons, **1892**, **Liberal Party**, **Central Finsbury**.
[200:2:3] - Founded the **London Indian Society (1865)** and **East India Association (1866, London)**.
[200:2:4] - Backed the Indian Parliamentary Committee (1893); served on the **Welby Commission (1895)**.
[200:2:5] - Wrote **Poverty and Un-British Rule in India (1901)** – called British rule "**Un-British**"; **estimated India's national income**.

## SUB 3 | G.k. Gokhale (1866-1915)
[200:3:0] - **Political guru of Gandhi**; Gandhi returned to India (**1915**) on Gokhale's advice.
[200:3:1] - Gandhi wrote **"Gokhale – My Political Guru"** (first ed. Ahmedabad, Feb 1955).
[200:3:2] - **M.G. Ranade** = Gokhale's political and intellectual guru.
[200:3:3] - President, **21st INC session, Banaras 1905** – condemned the **partition of Bengal**, backed **Swadeshi**.
[200:3:4] - **Refused a knighthood** and a seat in the **Council of the Secretary of State for India**.
[200:3:5] - Constitutional methods; pushed social and educational reform.
[200:3:6] Guru chain: **Ranade → Gokhale → Gandhi**.

## SUB 5 | Economic Critique – Drain of Wealth
[200:5:0] - **Naoroji, R.C. Dutt and Dinshaw Wacha** showed Britain drained India's wealth through:
[200:5:1] - 1. **Home Charges**
[200:5:2] - 2. **Salaries and pensions**
[200:5:3] - 3. **Unequal trade** + British monopoly
[200:5:4] - 4. India's wealth transferred to Britain
[200:5:5] - **Greatest Moderate contribution** – broke the myth of British benevolence; later used by the Extremists and Gandhi.
[200:5:6] - **Economic critics of colonialism**: Naoroji, **G. Subramania Iyer**, R.C. Dutt (all three).
[200:5:7] **Sir Syed Ahmed Khan rejected** Naoroji's Drain Theory; saw British rule as modernising.

## SUB 6 | Demands of the Moderates
[200:6:0] TABLE head: Area | Demands
  [200:6:0.0] Constitutional | Larger councils, elected members, control over the budget; "No taxation without representation"

  [200:6:0.3] Civil rights | Free speech, press, association; protested Tilak's arrest and deportation without trial of the Natu brothers
## SUB 7 | Methods and Limitations
[200:7:0] # Beliefs and Methods
[200:7:1] - British rule seen as "providential"; British just but ill-informed → **faith in British justice and fair play**.
[200:7:2] - **3 Ps: Prayers, Petitions, Protests**; resolutions, meetings, pamphlets – **strictly constitutional agitation**.
[200:7:3] - Two-pronged: build opinion in India + persuade the British public.
[200:7:4] - **Distrusted mass action**; no mass civil disobedience.
[200:7:5] # Limitations
[200:7:6] - Annual session lasted only about **three days**; **no machinery** between sessions.
[200:7:7] - **Narrow urban, educated base**.
[200:7:8] - **Partition of Bengal (1905)** exposed their "**naive faith**" in British justice.
[200:7:9] - Moderates **supported social reform** (e.g. **Age of Consent Bill**); **Tilak opposed** it.

## SUB 9 | Achievements
[200:9:0] # Indian Councils Act, 1892
[200:9:1] - Passed under Viceroy **Lansdowne** (20 June 1892).
[200:9:2] - **More non-official members**, filled by **recommendation** of municipal/district boards, universities, chambers of commerce – **indirect election**; the word "election" avoided.
[200:9:3] - Members could **discuss the budget** but **not vote or amend** it; could **ask questions** (no supplementaries).
[200:9:4] - Weak: **no direct vote**, **official majority** kept; INC found it inadequate.
[200:9:5] # Other Gains
[200:9:6] - **Welby Commission (1895)** – on Indian expenditure.
[200:9:7] - Pan-Indian **national identity**; **economic critique**; training in **democratic politics** and civil liberties – NOT complete independence.
[200:9:8] - Moderates **backed Europe's democratic and scientific culture** and **sharply criticised the economic side** of imperialism (both true).
[200:9:9] - Goal: **self-government within the Empire**.
