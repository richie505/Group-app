## SUB 1 | Anaemia Control and Fortification
[1:0] - **Food fortification:** adding selected nutrients not normally present in a food (iron, folic acid, vitamin B12, iodine, vitamin A); fortified rice via PDS, ICDS, PM POSHAN.
[1:1] - **Iron + folic acid (IFA)** tablets prevent anaemia.
[1:2] - Old anaemia control programme (NNAPP): pregnant women got **100 mg elemental iron + 500 mcg folic acid daily for 100 days**; now (Anaemia Mukt Bharat) **60 mg iron + 500 mcg folic acid daily for 180 days** from the 4th month.
[1:3] - **Anaemia Mukt Bharat (2018), 6x6x6:** prophylactic **IFA** (not calcium) for six age groups, **deworming**, behaviour-change campaign incl. **delayed cord clamping**, testing and treatment, fortified food, **non-nutritional causes** (malaria, haemoglobinopathies, fluorosis) in endemic pockets.
[1:4] - AP: **180 red IFA tablets** to pregnant women; AP ranked **1st** for IFA supplementation; **Thalli Bidda Express** – free transport for high-risk and severely anaemic women.
[1:5] ~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.218-219; app notes 5:118 (sub 8)

## SUB 4 | Nutrition Indicators and Malnutrition
[4:0] TABLE head: Indicator | Measure
  [4:0.0] Stunting | Low height-for-age
  [4:0.1] Wasting | Low weight-for-height
  [4:0.2] Underweight | Low weight-for-age
[4:1] - **BMI** = weight in kg ÷ (height in m)².
[4:2] TABLE head: India | NFHS-5 | NFHS-6
  [4:2.0] Stunting | 35.5% | 29.3%
  [4:2.1] Underweight | 32.1% | 31.8%
  [4:2.2] Anaemia (children 6–59 months / women) | 67.1% / 57.0% | —
[4:3] - **AP:** stunting 31.2% (NFHS-5) → 25% (NFHS-6); NFHS-6: Madhya Pradesh high wasting; Meghalaya stunting decline.
[4:4] - Underweight highest among **STs (44.1%)**, then SCs. **SOFI 2025:** India's wasting **18.7%** – world's highest.
[4:5] - **Main cause of malnutrition in India: poor dietary habits and food insecurity**; poverty → energy-dense, nutrient-poor food; women with 10+ years of schooling 41%.
[4:6] - WHO "malnutrition in all its forms": undernutrition + micronutrient deficiency + overweight/obesity; triple burden (SOFI 2025) adds micronutrient deficiency/anaemia.
[4:7] - **Colostrum** (first milk) is rich in antibodies (IgA) – **anti-infective**.
[4:8] - **Calcium:** cheese and sardines with bones are very rich per 100 g; milk is the usual daily-value staple (APPSC printed key: raw cow milk).
