## SUB 2 | Public vs Private Care and Challenges
[2:0] - **Public system** largely **curative**, with limited preventive, promotive and rehabilitative care (UPSC 2023 Statement-I, true).
[2:1] - **States are primarily responsible** for organising health services (health is a State subject) – Statement-II, also true but **not the explanation** of Statement-I.
[2:2] - **SDG 3 challenges:** costly, variable private care; unreliable public care; poor **rural access**; neglected **preventive care** – Centre-State coordination being "compulsory" is not a challenge.
[2:3] TABLE head: Indicator | Figure
  [2:3.0] NFHS-3 | Private sector main source of care for 70% of urban households
  [2:3.1] Private outpatient share | About 70%
  [2:3.2] Average hospitalisation (Aug 2026) | Rs 50,508 private vs Rs 6,631 government
  [2:3.3] Urban skew | 70% of infrastructure for 30% of population
[2:4] - Concerns: high **out-of-pocket expenditure** (**39.4%** of total health expenditure, NHA 2022-23), "missing middle" of over 40 crore people; private sector dominates secondary–quaternary care in cities.

## SUB 3 | Human Resources
[3:0] - Doctor-population ratio about **1:834** (NMC-registered allopathic doctors) vs WHO norm 1:1000 – but doctors cluster in cities, so real rural availability is far lower.
