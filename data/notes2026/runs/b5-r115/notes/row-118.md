=== Deficiency Diseases – Vitamins and Minerals
| Deficiency | Disease |
|---|---|
| Vitamin A | **Night blindness** (xerophthalmia) |
| Vitamin B1 (thiamine) | **Beri-beri** |
| Vitamin B3 (niacin) | **Pellagra** – not vitamin C |
| Vitamin B12 | **Pernicious anaemia** |
| Vitamin C | **Scurvy** |
| Vitamin D (+ calcium) | **Rickets** – weak, soft bones in the young (humans and animals) |
| Vitamin E | Sterility, muscular weakness – not night blindness |
| Vitamin K | Excessive **bleeding** |
| **Iron** | **Anaemia** – the main cause of anaemia (not calcium, zinc or magnesium) |
| **Iodine** | **Goitre**, cretinism; thyroid malfunction |
| Protein | Kwashiorkor; energy + protein – marasmus |
- **Goitre:** enlarged thyroid (thicker neck); iodine is given to goitre patients; common in **hilly areas** because the soil and food there are iodine-deficient (A and R true, R explains A).
> 18 PYQs: iodine–goitre (×7) and iron–anaemia (×4) are the most repeated pairs.

=== Anaemia Control and Fortification
- **Food fortification:** adding selected nutrients not normally present in a food (iron, folic acid, vitamin B12, iodine, vitamin A); fortified rice via PDS, ICDS, PM POSHAN.
- **Iron + folic acid (IFA)** tablets prevent anaemia.
- Old anaemia control programme (NNAPP): pregnant women got **100 mg elemental iron + 500 mcg folic acid daily for 100 days**; now (Anaemia Mukt Bharat) **60 mg iron + 500 mcg folic acid daily for 180 days** from the 4th month.
- **Anaemia Mukt Bharat (2018), 6x6x6:** prophylactic **IFA** (not calcium) for six age groups, **deworming**, behaviour-change campaign incl. **delayed cord clamping**, testing and treatment, fortified food, **non-nutritional causes** (malaria, haemoglobinopathies, fluorosis) in endemic pockets.
- AP: **180 red IFA tablets** to pregnant women; AP ranked **1st** for IFA supplementation; **Thalli Bidda Express** – free transport for high-risk and severely anaemic women.

=== Nutrition Indicators and Malnutrition
| Indicator | Measure |
|---|---|
| **Stunting** | Low **height-for-age** |
| Wasting | Low weight-for-height |
| Underweight | Low weight-for-age |
- **BMI** = weight in kg ÷ (height in m)².
| India | NFHS-5 | NFHS-6 |
|---|---|---|
| Stunting | 35.5% | 29.3% |
| Underweight | 32.1% | 31.8% |
| Anaemia (children 6–59 months / women) | 67.1% / 57.0% | — |
- **AP:** stunting 31.2% (NFHS-5) → 25% (NFHS-6); NFHS-6: Madhya Pradesh high wasting; Meghalaya stunting decline.
- Underweight highest among **STs (44.1%)**, then SCs. **SOFI 2025:** India's wasting **18.7%** – world's highest.
- **Main cause of malnutrition in India: poor dietary habits and food insecurity**; poverty → energy-dense, nutrient-poor food; women with 10+ years of schooling 41%.
- WHO "malnutrition in all its forms": undernutrition + micronutrient deficiency + overweight/obesity; triple burden (SOFI 2025) adds micronutrient deficiency/anaemia.
- **Colostrum** (first milk) is rich in antibodies (IgA) – **anti-infective**.
- **Calcium:** cheese and sardines with bones are very rich per 100 g; milk is the usual daily-value staple (APPSC printed key: raw cow milk).
