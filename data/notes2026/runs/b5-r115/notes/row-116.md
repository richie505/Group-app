| Toxoid | Inactivated bacterial toxin |
- **Convalescent plasma therapy:** uses **neutralising antibodies** from the blood of recovered COVID-19 patients to treat patients (passive immunity).

=== Vaccine Pioneers and Classic Vaccines
| Scientist | Associated with |
|---|---|
| **Edward Jenner** (1749–1823) | **Father of immunology**; **smallpox vaccine 1796** – the first vaccine and first attempt at vaccination; proposed the immunisation concept |
| **Jonas Salk** | **First successful polio vaccine** (1952); Albert Sabin – oral polio vaccine |
| **Louis Pasteur** | Rabies vaccine, germ theory; **live attenuated fowl cholera vaccine** (1879-80) |
| Robert Koch | Bacteriology, TB bacterium – NOT father of immunology |
| Elie Metchnikoff | Phagocytosis, cellular immunity |
- **DPT** = diphtheria + pertussis + tetanus – a **combined vaccine**; MMR = measles, mumps, rubella; **BCG** – TB, just after birth.
- Smallpox – **variola virus**; **rabies – a virus** (lyssavirus), spread by dog/bat bites.
- **Tetanus** – bacterium *Clostridium tetani* (contaminated wounds), not *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* (TB).
- **Biojector:** a **painless needle-free injector** (used for ZyCoV-D).
- **World Immunization Week:** **24–30 April** (last week of April).
- WHO malaria vaccines: RTS,S/AS01 (Mosquirix), R21/Matrix-M; TAK-003 (Qdenga) – India's first approved dengue vaccine (CDSCO, Jul 2026).

=== SARS-CoV-2 and COVID-19 Basics
- Virus = **SARS-CoV-2**; disease = **COVID-19 ("Coronavirus Disease 2019")** – the WHO name for the disease caused by the **2019 novel coronavirus (2019-nCoV)**; it caused the COVID-19 global pause (not SARS-CoV-1 or MERS).
- First identified: **Wuhan, Hubei, China, December 2019**; MoHFW screened flights for **novel coronavirus (nCoV)** illness in January 2020.
- **SARS** is caused by a **coronavirus**; **MERS, SARS and COVID-19** are all coronavirus diseases.
- Enters human cells via the **ACE2 receptor**; reservoir bats, **pangolin** suspected intermediate host.
- **Viruses** are acellular – no cells and no metabolic enzymes of their own (unlike lichen, bacteria, algae).

| **Delta** | **India** |

=== Lessons from COVID-19
- Exposed **health infrastructure and oxygen gaps**, the **migrant crisis**, dependence on imported supplies.
- **IIT Bombay** converted a nitrogen generator into an oxygen generator (second wave).
- Digital tools: CoWIN, U-WIN, ABDM, **eSanjeevani**; strategy test-track-treat-vaccinate with a **three-tier** facility arrangement.
- TB still the top single-agent killer: **1.25 million** deaths worldwide in 2023; India's TB incidence 237 (2015) → 187 per lakh (2024).
