=== Dietary Fats, Cholesterol and Heart Health
# Good and bad fats
- **Trans fat:** raises **LDL** and **lowers HDL** ("good cholesterol"), so it is bad for health (UPPSC 2012); excess trans fat raises cardiovascular risk.
- **Bakery products** made with **hydrogenated fats** contain trans fats, and excess trans fats raise the risk of NCDs – **both statements correct** (APPSC Fisheries 2023).
- **WHO REPLACE plan:** eliminate industrially produced trans fats from the global food supply **by 2023** (target date; many countries still pending). **FSSAI** caps trans fat at **2%** in fats and oils.
- **Saturated fat** excess raises blood lipids and cholesterol; **HDL** removes bad cholesterol – high HDL lowers heart-disease risk.
- **Triglyceride** = ester of glycerol + three fatty acids = a **fat**; the main constituent of body fat.
- **Most coronary heart disease patients** have elevated **cholesterol** (LDL).
# Oils and fatty acids
- Cook in **oil rather than vanaspati ghee**: oils hold **unsaturated fats** (mono- and polyunsaturated) that lower cholesterol (Uttarakhand 2006).
- **Sunflower oil:** suitable for **heart patients**; good-quality edible oil **because** it is rich in unsaturated fatty acids (A and R both true, R explains A); more vitamin E than other vegetable oils.
- **Highest PUFA among common oils:** **safflower oil** (about 75% linoleic acid) > sunflower > soybean; **coconut oil** is mostly saturated.
- **Fish** is healthier than other meat because it contains **polyunsaturated fatty acids** (omega-3) that cut LDL and heart risk.
- **Eskimos (Inuit):** little heart disease despite a high meat/fat diet – food rich in **omega-3** from fish and marine mammals.
- **Linseed = flaxseed:** richest source of **alpha-linolenic acid (18-carbon omega-3)**; not oats, moth or mung bean.
- **Essential fatty acids** cannot be made by the body and must be in the diet; their absence causes deficiency disease – but body cells **can** make most other fatty acids.
| Fatty acid | Source |
|---|---|
| **α-Linolenic (omega-3)** | **Linseed oil** |
| Linoleic | Peanut oil, chicken fat, olive oil |
| Arachidonic | Liver, brain, glandular organs |
| Oleic | Olive oil, canola oil |
| Lauric | Coconut / palm-kernel oil, breast milk |
| Palmitic | Butter, palm oil, meat |
| Stearic | Meat, cocoa butter |
| **Butyric** | **Butter** |
- **Obesity:** excess energy intake + low physical activity → obesity and heart disease; NFHS-5 overweight/obese: **24% women, 23% men**.

=== Major NCDs – Heart, Diabetes, Cancer, Lungs and Brain
# Heart and blood vessels
- **Coronary artery disease** = result of **plaque buildup** in the arteries supplying the heart (APPSC G2 2018; not "coronary veins disease").
- **Atherosclerosis** (hardening and narrowing of arteries by plaque) is a disease of the **circulatory system**.
- **Blood pressure generally increases with age.**
- Leading cause of death in **India = cardiovascular diseases**; world's single biggest killer (WHO) = **ischaemic heart disease**.
# Diabetes
- **Normal fasting blood sugar** (non-diabetic): about **70–100 mg/dL** – nearest option **60–100 mg/dL**.
- **HbA1c (glycosylated haemoglobin):** average blood glucose over 2–3 months → used to manage **diabetes**.
- **BGR-34:** CSIR's Ayurvedic anti-**diabetes** drug (NBRI + CIMAP, 2016).
- **First State diabetes registry:** **Goa** (June 2011).
| ICMR-INDIAB (Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology, June 2023) | Prevalence | People |
|---|---|---|
| Diabetes | **11.4%** | ~**101 million** |
| Prediabetes | 15.3% | ~136 million |
| Hypertension | 35.5% | ~315 million |
# Cancer
- **Cancer** = **abnormal (uncontrolled) growth of cells**; non-contagious; can spread (metastasis).
- **Oncogenes** (mutated growth genes) are associated with **cancer**; first oncogene (SRC) found 1970.
- **Blood cancer = leukaemia** (abnormal WBC production in bone marrow); haemophilia is a clotting disorder.
- Cases **1.41 million (2022)** → **2.46 million (2045)**; cancer is **not nationally notifiable**, **17 States** have notified it, **Telangana** latest (Jul 2026).
- **Free HPV vaccination** campaign for **14-year-old girls** (cervical cancer) launched **28 Feb 2026** from Ajmer.
# Lungs and brain
- **COPD = Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease**; World COPD Day in November.
- **Alzheimer's disease:** degenerative disorder of the **brain**, the leading cause of **dementia** (memory loss, confusion).
- **PSE = Photosensitive Epileptic Seizures** – triggered by flashing lights or regular patterns.
- **"Big four" NCDs:** cardiovascular diseases, cancers, chronic respiratory diseases, diabetes.

=== Dual Burden and Classification of Diseases
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Dual burden of disease** | Rising rates of both **infectious and non-communicable** diseases (TB, malaria, dengue alongside diabetes, CVD) |
| Triple burden | Adds **injuries** (road accidents) to CDs + NCDs |
| Dual burden of malnutrition | Undernutrition + obesity |
| DALY | YLL (years of life lost) + YLD (years lived with disability) – not years gained by treatment |
- **Genetic disorders** (haemophilia, sickle-cell, Down syndrome) are **non-communicable**; **club foot** is a **birth defect**.
- **Malaria** is communicable (Plasmodium, Anopheles) – **NOT a lifestyle disease**; typhoid and chikungunya are also communicable.
- **Lifestyle risk factors:** unhealthy diet, physical inactivity, **tobacco**, alcohol.
| Disease | Vector / spread |
|---|---|
| Dengue, chikungunya, Zika | **Aedes** |
| Japanese encephalitis | **Culex** |
| Meningococcal meningitis | Person to person by respiratory droplets |
- **India State-Level Disease Burden Initiative** = **ICMR + PHFI + IHME**; part of the **Global Burden of Disease (GBD)** study.
- Emerging/re-emerging: TB, dengue, Nipah, avian influenza; ICMR **MERA** (malaria elimination); **wastewater surveillance 5 → 50 cities** (2026), tracks **10 viruses**; vaccination platform **U-WIN**.

=== Road Injuries and Mental Health
- **AP Road Safety Policy** was declared in **2015** (target: 15% annual cut in fatalities), not 2017; AP is **8th** in road-accident deaths; 2024 (Jan–Nov): 7,477 deaths in 16,898 accidents.
- **Rural areas** had **53.8%** of India's road accidents in 2015 (MoRTH).
- **MoRTH 2022:** 4,61,312 accidents, **1,68,491 deaths**; **two-wheeler users** the largest group of victims. **NCRB ADSI 2024:** road deaths **1,75,142** (~37.4% of accidental deaths).
- **SDG 3:** **halve road-traffic deaths**.
- **Mental disorders (GBD 2017):** **197.3 million** Indians – about **1 in 7**; most common **depressive (45.7 mn)** then anxiety disorders.
- **Tele-MANAS:** 24x7 tele-mental-health support in all 36 States/UTs.

=== Epidemiological Transition and Key Data
- **Omran's stages:** 1. **Age of Pestilence and Famine** (first) → 2. Age of Receding Pandemics → 3. **Age of Degenerative and Man-Made Diseases** (last).
- **"Nations within a nation"** (Lancet 2017; data 1990–2016): **4 ETL groups**; ETL = ratio of communicable DALYs to NCD + injury DALYs; **low ratio = high ETL** (more advanced).
- **High ETL:** **Kerala**, Goa, Tamil Nadu, Punjab; **Karnataka** and Delhi higher-middle; NCD + injury DALYs overtook communicable DALYs for India in **2003**.
| Indicator | Fact |
|---|---|
| NCD share of deaths | **57%** (Economic Survey 2025-26) |
| MMR since 1990 | Fallen **86%** |
| Under-five mortality since 1990 | Fallen **78%** |
- India's probability of dying from an NCD **rose** in 2001–2019 – **women over 40, men over 55** (Lancet).
