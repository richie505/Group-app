=== NOTES 4:34 | India – Physiographic Division: the Himalayas (ranges, Regional Divisions, Passes)
## SUB 0 | Dimensions, Origin and Structure
[34:0:0] # Dimensions
[34:0:1] TABLE head: Dimension | Figure
  [34:0:1.0] Length | 2,414 km – Nanga Parbat (west) to Namcha Barwa (east) (about 2,400 km)
  [34:0:1.1] Width | 500 km in Kashmir to 200 km in Arunachal
  [34:0:1.2] Depth | 240–320 km
  [34:0:1.3] Area | About 5 lakh sq km (not 15 lakh)
[34:0:2] # Origin
[34:0:3] - Raised from **Tethys** sediments during the **Tertiary** (Eocene to Pliocene, ~60–7 million years ago).
[34:0:4] - **Indian plate** collided with **Eurasia** about **4–5 crore years** ago; the Himalayas are **still rising**.
[34:0:5] - **Trans-Himalayas** (Tibetan Himalaya) were formed **before** the Himalayas.
[34:0:6] # Structure (APPSC Group 1 2024: statements I, II, III true)
[34:0:7] - **Wider in Kashmir**, narrower in the North-East.
[34:0:8] - Folds of the **Himadri are asymmetrical**; core of **granite**.
[34:0:9] - **Steep slope faces the plains** of India; **gentle slope faces Tibet**.
[34:0:10] - **Significance**: India's northern boundary and climatic barrier; Shiwalik debris built the **Indo-Gangetic Plain**.
[34:0:11] - Relief share: **plains** form the largest part of India's area – about **43%** (APPSC 2024).

## SUB 2 | Parallel Ranges – North to South
[34:2:0] # Sequence and Features
[34:2:1] TABLE head: N → S | Range | Key facts
  [34:2:1.0] 1 | Karakoram | Trans-Himalaya; K2; Siachen, Rimo, Baltoro glaciers
  [34:2:1.1] 2 | Ladakh | South of Karakoram; continues into Tibet as the Kailash Range (not the Zaskar)
  [34:2:1.2] 3 | Zaskar | Trans-Himalaya; Kashmir Valley separates Pir Panjal from it
  [34:2:1.3] 4 | Himadri (Greater) | Northernmost of the three, oldest, loftiest, most continuous; average 6,000 m
  [34:2:1.4] 5 | Himachal (Lesser / Middle) | Pir Panjal, Dhauladhar, Mussoorie, Mahabharat (Nepal); average width 50 km, 3,700–4,500 m; hill stations Shimla, Nainital, Mussoorie
  [34:2:1.5] 6 | Shiwalik (Outer) | Youngest, narrowest, outermost; foothills
[34:2:2] - Himadri: highest point **Everest** (~8,850 m).
[34:2:3] - **Pir Panjal**: Lesser Himalaya; about **300 km**, **Jhelum–Beas** sector; runs south-east to north-west through HP and J&K.
[34:2:4] - **Mahabharat Range**: Lesser Himalaya in central Nepal, south of the main range.
[34:2:5] - **Ladakh Plateau** (Trans-Himalaya): **highest plateau in India**; **Aksai Chin** (~38,000 sq km, Chinese-occupied Indian territory) is part of it.
[34:2:6] - Trans-Himalaya is also called the **Tibet Himalaya** (most parts in Tibet).
[34:2:7] # Shiwalik
[34:2:8] - Formed in the **Cenozoic** (Pliocene, about 2–5 million years ago) from **loose river sediments**; simple **Jura-type** folds.
[34:2:9] - Width **10–50 km**; height **900–1,100 m**.
[34:2:10] - Shiwalik Hills are part of the Himalaya (outermost range).
[34:2:11] # Comparison of the Three Parallel Ranges
[34:2:12] TABLE head: Feature | Himadri (Greater) | Himachal (Lesser / Middle) | Shiwalik (Outer)
  [34:2:12.0] Position | Northernmost | Middle | Southernmost, outermost
  [34:2:12.1] Age | Oldest | Intermediate | Youngest (Pliocene)
  [34:2:12.2] Height | Average 6,000 m; loftiest peaks | 3,700–4,500 m | 900–1,100 m
  [34:2:12.3] Width | Most continuous range | Average 50 km | 10–50 km, narrowest
  [34:2:12.4] Composition | Granite core; asymmetrical folds | Folded ranges, valleys | Loose river sediments
  [34:2:12.5] Examples | Everest, Kanchenjunga, Nanda Devi | Pir Panjal, Dhauladhar, Mussoorie, Mahabharat | Foothills; duns behind them
  [34:2:12.6] Features | Glaciers (Gangotri) | Hill stations Shimla, Nainital, Mussoorie | Debris built the plains
[34:2:13] APPSC 2025: the northernmost of the three parallel ranges is the Himadri, not the Shiwalik.
[34:2:14] # Sequence Questions
[34:2:15] - N → S: **Karakoram, Himadri, Himachal, Shiwalik**; also Karakoram, Ladakh, Zanskar, Shiwalik.
[34:2:16] - S → N (west): **Shiwalik – Lesser Himalaya – Great Himalaya**; Lesser Himalaya lies between them.
[34:2:17] - Northward: **Pir Panjal, Zanskar, Ladakh, Karakoram** (Pir Panjal Middle Himalaya; rest Trans-Himalaya).
[34:2:18] - S → N: **Dhauladhar, Zanskar, Ladakh, Karakoram**.
[34:2:19] - Hills S → N: **Satmala** (Maharashtra), **Kaimur** (MP–UP–Bihar), **Naga** (Indo-Myanmar), **Pir Panjal** (J&K–HP).
