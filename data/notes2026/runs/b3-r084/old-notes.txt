[84:3:2] - **1969 – 14 banks**: Allahabad (now Indian Bank), BoB, BoI, BoM, Central, Canara, **Dena (now BoB)**, Indian Bank, IOB, PNB, **Syndicate (now Canara)**, UCO, Union, United Bank of India (now PNB). The 1969 banks held about 85% of deposits.
[84:3:3] - **1980 – 6 banks**: **Andhra Bank, Corporation Bank** (both now Union Bank), **Oriental Bank of Commerce** (now PNB), **Punjab & Sind Bank**, **Vijaya Bank** (now BoB), **New Bank of India**.

## SUB 4 | Banking Structure
[84:4:0] # Organised vs Unorganised
[84:4:1] TABLE head: Sector | Who
  [84:4:1.0] Organised | RBI; commercial banks (public, private, foreign, RRBs, SFBs, payments banks, local area banks); cooperative banks; DFIs (NABARD, Exim)
  [84:4:1.1] Unorganised | Indigenous bankers, moneylenders
[84:4:2] - Five ownership groups: **SBI, nationalised banks, RRBs, foreign banks, private banks**.
[84:4:3] - **Scheduled banks**: in the **Second Schedule, RBI Act 1934**; paid-up capital and reserves **≥ ₹5 lakh**; may borrow from RBI.
[84:4:4] - **Cooperative banks**: State Cooperative Societies Acts + RBI under BR Act; **one member, one vote**.
[84:4:5] - **NABARD** = apex bank for agricultural credit.
[84:4:6] - **NBFCs** cannot take demand deposits or issue cheques; not DICGC-insured.
[84:4:7] - **DICGC (1978)**: merger of Deposit Insurance Corporation (1962) and Credit Guarantee Corporation of India.
[84:4:8] # Foreign Banks
[84:4:9] - Operate as **branches** or **wholly owned subsidiaries (WOS)** with **near national treatment** (RBI framework, Nov 2013); WOS minimum capital **₹500 crore**.
[84:4:10] - Most branches: **Standard Chartered (UK, 100)**; HSBC 27, Deutsche Bank 17, Citibank 14.
[84:4:11] - **Syndicated loan:** one loan to a **single borrower** funded by a **group (syndicate) of lenders**; a **lead bank** (lead arranger) arranges and manages it.
## SUB 5 | Differentiated Banks – SFBs and Payments Banks
[84:5:0] # Origin
[84:5:1] - **Nachiket Mor Committee (2013)**: differentiated (niche) licences → **SFBs and payments banks**. Licence types: universal + differentiated; commercial banks now licensed **on tap**.
[84:5:2] - Payments banks: licensed under **Sec 22, BR Act 1949**; public limited companies under the **Companies Act, 2013**.
[84:5:3] # Comparison
[84:5:4] TABLE head: Feature | Small finance bank | Payments bank
  [84:5:4.0] Deposits | All types | Demand deposits only; max ₹2 lakh per customer (earlier ₹1 lakh)
  [84:5:4.1] Lending | Yes: 75% of ANBC to PSL; 50% of loans ≤ ₹25 lakh | No lending, no credit cards
  [84:5:4.2] Minimum capital | ₹100 crore (2014 guidelines) | ₹100 crore paid-up voting equity
  [84:5:4.3] Other | Can sell MFs and insurance; on tap since 2019 | 75% of demand deposits in SLR G-secs/T-bills up to 1 year; CRAR 15%
  [84:5:4.4] Purpose | Small units, small/marginal farmers, unorganised sector | Payments, remittances for migrants and low-income households
[84:5:5] - **SFB → universal bank** (RBI, April 2024): voluntary transition; **5 years** of satisfactory track record; minimum net worth **₹1,000 crore**; no change of promoters; **AU SFB** got approval (2025).
[84:5:6] - RBI **cancelled Paytm Payments Bank's** licence (harmful to depositors and public).
[84:5:7] **75%** = SFB PSL share = RRB PSL share = payments bank share of demand deposits in SLR securities.

## SUB 8 | Lead Bank Scheme and Slbc
[84:8:0] - Began **December 1969** (same year as 14-bank nationalisation); origin in **D. R. Gadgil Study Group** (report October 1969) and the **Nariman Committee**.
[84:8:1] - Aim: individual banks **adopt districts** for intensive development; lead bank coordinates **district credit planning**; functions performed by a bank designated for the purpose.
[84:8:2] - **Service Area Approach (April 1989)**: each rural/semi-urban branch serves **15–25 villages**.
[84:8:3] TABLE head: Forum | Level
  [84:8:3.0] Lead District Manager | District
  [84:8:3.1] SLBC | State
[84:8:4] - RBI draft revision of the scheme (Feb 2026) to strengthen SLBCs and LDM offices.
[84:8:5] - **AP**: lead bank / **SLBC convener = Union Bank of India**; 236th SLBC (16 Sep 2026): CM Naidu asked banks to lend more to MSMEs, tenant farmers, PM Surya Ghar.
