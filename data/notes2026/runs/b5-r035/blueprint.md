## 6. Settled conflicts
- **NGLV payload:** old notes "20,000 kg to LEO, 2.5× LVM3" → Cabinet approval 18 Sep 2024: **30 t to LEO, ~3× LVM3** (IANS report; PMF S&T Part 2 p.9). Own MCQ f6c07367afb03 gets an added option; n56cb369e8d4 explanation corrected.
- **GSAT-8 date:** APPSC G1 2012 key "21 April 2011"; ISRO page: **21 May 2011** → keyed d with a dispute line.
- **PMF S&T Part 2 p.2** calls GSAT-24 "the first commercial mission of LVM3". Wrong: GSAT-24 flew on Ariane-5, and LVM3-M2/OneWeb was LVM3's first commercial mission (ISRO LVM3-M2 page). Not used.
