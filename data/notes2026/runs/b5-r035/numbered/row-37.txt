## SUB 0 | Geostationary Orbit – Physics of a Communication Satellite
[0:0] TABLE head: Orbit | Fact
  [0:0.0] Low Earth | 160–2,000 km; ISS ~400 km
  [0:0.1] Geostationary (GEO) | About 36,000 km (35,786 km) above the equator = orbit radius ~42,000 km (42,164 km from Earth's centre); period 24 hours (1 day)
  [0:0.2] Polar | Passes over the poles
  [0:0.3] Elliptical | Apogee and perigee differ
  [0:0.4] Circular | Centripetal force equals gravitational force
[0:1] - **Geostationary:** revolves **in the same direction as the Earth rotates** (west to east) with the same period, so it appears fixed over one point; it stays at a **fixed height** that does **not depend on its mass**.
[0:2] - **What keeps it in orbit:** the **centripetal force**, supplied by the **Earth's gravitational pull on the satellite** – not a rocket engine, not the Sun.
[0:3] - **No energy needed** to keep orbiting: in near-vacuum there is no drag; solar panels only power the instruments.
[0:4] - **Global TV:** **geostationary satellites** relay TV across the world (three cover nearly the whole globe); ground and space waves are short-range.
[0:5] - **Uplink / downlink:** sending data **up to the satellite / network** = **uplink**; receiving from it = downlink.
[0:6] - Launcher → elliptical **GTO** → satellite's own motor raises it to GEO.
[0:7] ~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 1 p.64-66; app notes 5:37 sub 0
## SUB 1 | INSAT System
[1:0] # Firsts
[1:1] TABLE head: Year | Milestone
  [1:1.0] 19 Jun 1981 | APPLE (Ariane Passenger PayLoad Experiment) – first experimental geostationary comsat, on Ariane-1 from Kourou, French Guiana
  [1:1.1] 1982 | INSAT-1A – first INSAT (INSAT-1 series built abroad by Ford Aerospace)
  [1:1.2] 1983 | INSAT system commissioned; INSAT-1B made it operational
  [1:1.3] 1990 | INSAT-1D – linked India by TV from ~36,000 km
  [1:1.4] 10 Jul 1992 | INSAT-2A, Kourou – first of the fully indigenous INSAT-2 series
  [1:1.5] 12 Sep 2002 | METSAT (PSLV-C4), renamed KALPANA-1 after Kalpana Chawla – India's first exclusive weather satellite
[1:2] # Design and management
[1:3] - **INSAT = Indian National Satellite** system: **multipurpose** geostationary system – telecom, TV, **meteorology**, disaster warning, search-and-rescue, tele-education, telemedicine (**not astronomy**); asked as "**Meteorological Satellite**" (not resource, military or polar).
[1:4] - Joint venture of **DoS, DoT, IMD, AIR, Doordarshan**; coordinated by **ICC**; **Master Control Facility, Hassan** (Karnataka).
[1:5] - **Cyclones** are tracked through **INSAT** (INSAT-3D/3DR imagers); INSAT-3D and 3DR sharply cut cyclone deaths on the east coast, incl. AP. **INSAT-1C** (1988) has no disaster-management role; RISAT-2B, INSAT-3D, INSAT-3DR do.
[1:6] # Key INSATs
[1:7] TABLE head: Satellite | Launch | Note
  [1:7.0] INSAT-3C | Jan 2002, Ariane-4, Kourou | Communication
  [1:7.1] INSAT-3A | 10 Apr 2003, Kourou | Multipurpose – broadcasting, mobile, meteorology
  [1:7.2] INSAT-3E | Sep 2003, Kourou (French Guiana) | Communication
  [1:7.3] INSAT-4A | 22 Dec 2005, Ariane-5, Kourou | First of INSAT-4 (3,081 kg)
  [1:7.4] INSAT-4C | 10 Jul 2006, GSLV-F02 – exploded 60 s after lift-off | 2,168 kg; replaced by INSAT-4CR (Sep 2007, GSLV-F04)
[1:8] - **Launched abroad:** till 2002 **all INSATs** went up from abroad (USA, Kourou); later INSAT-4CR, INSAT-3DR, INSAT-3DS flew on GSLV from Sriharikota.
[1:9] - **Launch sites:** **Kourou** (French Guiana, Ariane – Europe's spaceport); **Cape Canaveral** on the Atlantic coast of **Florida** (US); Baikonur (Kazakhstan, Russia).
[1:10] - **NSIL** took 10 in-orbit comsats (2022); **19** operational comsats (2026).

[2:3] - **China:** **Shijian-20**, its heaviest satellite, went up on **Long March 5** (Dec 2019).

## SUB 4 | Transponder Bands and Applications
[4:0] TABLE head: Band | Use
  [4:0.0] C / extended C | Telecom, TV distribution
  [4:0.1] Ku | DTH, VSAT
  [4:0.2] Ka | High-throughput broadband (GSAT-11, GSAT-N2)
  [4:0.3] S | Mobile satellite services, DWS downlink
[4:1] - **MF band is NOT used**. High-throughput: GSAT-19, -29, -11, N2; support **BharatNet**.
[4:2] - **DTH**, **VSAT**, **DSNG** (news gathering), **MSS** (mobile satellite services), GRAMSAT, telemedicine.
[4:3] - **Swayam Prabha**: **GSAT-15**, 40 free DTH education channels.
[4:4] - **DWS**: Cyclone Warning Centre **Chennai**; uplink **C**, downlink **S** to addressable coastal receivers.
[4:5] - Search and rescue: **COSPAS-SARSAT**.
[4:6] - **AP**: SDSC-SHAR gives APSDMA cyclone-track data; RTGS–SHAR MoU (**Jun 2025**); **satellite phones** in emergencies; the AP government signed an MoU with **Malaysia** (MEASAT Satellite Systems) for **Ku-band** services on **MEASAT-1 and MEASAT-2**.
