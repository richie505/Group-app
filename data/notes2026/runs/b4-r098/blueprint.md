## 3. Repeats and trends
- Beach-to-town matching is APPSC's favourite (G2 2023, G2 Prelims 2024, ADE): know every coastal district's beaches.
- Older papers use undivided districts (Visakhapatnam for Lambasingi/Araku, Kurnool for Ahobilam/Belum, Chittoor for Tirupati) – keyed to the district at the time with an outdated line.
- WHS counts and "latest inscription" questions recur every year: 44 (2025) → 45 (Sarnath, 2026).
