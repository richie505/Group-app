## SUB 0 | GST – Nature and Objectives
[0:0] - **GST (Goods and Services Tax)**: an **indirect tax**; **single, comprehensive, multi-stage, destination-based** tax levied on **every value addition** – a **value-added consumption tax**, **not a gross value tax**.
[0:1] - **Taxable event: supply** of goods and services (not manufacture, not sale).
[0:2] - **Destination principle**: tax accrues to the State where goods/services are **consumed**, **not where produced**.
[0:3] - Constitutional definition: **Article 366(12A)** – a tax on supply of goods or services except **alcoholic liquor for human consumption**.
[0:4] - In force **1 July 2017**; **101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016**; biggest indirect-tax reform since Independence – a unified framework for many indirect taxes.
[0:5] - Objectives: **"one nation, one tax"**; **end cascading ("tax on tax")** through input tax credit; **one national market** (replaces multiple taxes of multiple authorities); transparency in **indirect** taxes; designed to be **non-inflationary** (A-R: avoids tax-on-tax → correct explanation).
[0:6] - Not an advantage: GST does not "drastically cut the current account deficit" or make India "overtake China" (UPSC 2017: only I).
[0:7] - GST involves **both Centre and States** (dual GST) – "only at the central level" is false.
[0:8] > Exam angle: statements on nature – destination-based (4 PYQs), replaces indirect taxes, not direct taxes, not gross value.
[0:9] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 09 p.4; NextGen Rocket Sheets Indian Economy p.19-20; Ramesh Singh Indian Economy 10th ed. Part 5 p.204-206

## SUB 3 | GST Council
[3:0] - **Constitutional body** under **Article 279A**; **never statutory** (APPSC G1 2024: "statutory, later constitutional" is false).
[3:1] - **Chairperson**: **Union Finance Minister** (first: Arun Jaitley). **Members**: Union **MoS Revenue/Finance** (only a member); Finance/Taxation Minister of each State and UT with legislature; **Vice-Chairperson** chosen by State members.
[3:2] TABLE head: Rule | Value
  [3:2.0] Quorum | One-half of total members
  [3:2.1] Centre's weight | 1/3 of votes cast
  [3:2.2] All States together | 2/3 of votes cast – each State has an equal vote
  [3:2.3] Decision | 3/4 majority of weighted votes of members present and voting
[3:3] - Recommends **tax rates, exempted goods, threshold limits**, model laws, special rates, date for petroleum – the **Council recommends**, the Centre and States **notify** rates (rates are not fixed by Parliament, EC or the Finance Commission).
[3:4] - **Disputes**: the Council itself sets up a dispute mechanism (not the PAC); proceedings are **not invalid** due to a vacancy or defect in appointment.
[3:5] - **Mohit Minerals (2022)**: recommendations **persuasive, not binding**; simultaneous power under 246A; "flexible federation".
[3:6] > Exam angle: chair = Union FM (5 PYQs); statements on status, voting and functions.
[3:7] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 09 p.5; NextGen Rocket Sheets Indian Economy p.19-21; app notes 3:96
## SUB 4 | Taxes Subsumed vs Not Subsumed
[4:0] TABLE head: Central taxes subsumed | State taxes subsumed
  [4:0.0] Central Excise Duty, Additional Excise Duties, excise on medicinal and toilet preparations | State VAT / Sales Tax, Central Sales Tax
  [4:0.1] Service Tax | Purchase Tax, Luxury Tax, Entry Tax (all forms), Octroi
  [4:0.2] CVD and SAD on imports | Entertainment Tax (except by local bodies)
  [4:0.3] Central cesses and surcharges on supply | Taxes on advertisements, lotteries, betting, gambling
[4:1] - GST replaced **indirect** taxes only – conventionally **8 Central + 9 State** taxes (subsumed **Central as well as State** taxes) – **not** income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax or property tax.
[4:2] - **Not subsumed**: **Basic Customs Duty**, **stamp duty**, electricity duty, motor vehicle tax, property tax, **State excise on alcoholic liquor**.

## SUB 7 | Rates and Rate-change Procedure
[7:0] - 2017: **four slabs 5, 12, 18, 28%** + exempt + compensation cess; **GST 2.0** (56th Council meeting, effective **22 Sep 2025**): **5% (merit)**, **18% (standard)**, **40% (sin / ultra-luxury)**.
[7:1] - Rates are changed by **government notification on GST Council advice**, within **statutory ceilings** (**CGST 20%**, IGST 40%) – not by amending the Acts.
[7:2] - Item rates in PYQs: **sugar 5%**; **lottery** uniform **28%** (from March 2020; 40% under GST 2.0); **music books** 12% → **nil** (31st meeting, Dec 2018).

## SUB 8 | GST Mechanisms – ITC, Anti-profiteering, Composition
[8:0] TABLE head: Mechanism | Meaning
  [8:0.0] Input Tax Credit (ITC) | Credit of tax paid on inputs by a registered person, set off against output tax; avoids double taxation/cascading; not available for exempt or non-taxable supplies, or under composition
  [8:0.1] Reverse Charge (RCM) | Liability moves from supplier to recipient
  [8:0.2] Anti-profiteering (Sec 171, CGST Act) | Rate-cut / ITC benefit must reach consumers (NAA)
  [8:0.3] Composition scheme | Goods turnover up to ₹1.5 crore (1%), services ₹50 lakh (6%); no ITC
  [8:0.4] Inverted duty structure | ITC refund allowed
[8:1] - **Anti-profiteering chain**: **NAA → CCI (Dec 2022) → GSTAT Principal Bench (from 1 Oct 2024)**.

## SUB 9 | GSTN, Saksham, E-way Bill and E-invoicing
[9:0] - **GSTN**: IT backbone for GST; set up (2013) as a **not-for-profit, non-government company** (Centre 24.5%, States 24.5%, private institutions **51%**); made **fully government-owned** (decision **2018**). **GSTIN** = **15** digits.
[9:1] - **Project Saksham**: CBEC/CBIC IT project for the **new indirect tax (GST) network** (approved Sept 2016).
[9:2] - **E-way bill**: goods **above ₹50,000**; valid **1 day per 200 km**. **E-invoicing** curbs fake invoices, checks ITC.
[9:3] - Budget 2026-27: automated registration within **3 working days** for low-risk applicants.
[9:4] ~~ Sources: app notes 3:96; general knowledge (Saksham, not checked)
