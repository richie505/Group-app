## SUB 3 | Divisible Pool, Cesses and Surcharges
[3:0] - **Divisible pool** = all Union taxes **except cesses and surcharges** (and Art. 268/269 taxes), net of collection charges – set up by the **80th Amendment (2000)** following the 10th FC.
[3:1] - Cesses and surcharges per ₹100 of Central tax: **₹13.5 (2021-22, decade high)** → ₹11 (2025-26 est.).
[3:2] - Headline devolution **41%**; effective only **30-32%** of gross tax revenue.
[3:3] - **16th FC "grand bargain"**: States take a **smaller share of a larger pool** if most cesses merge into regular taxes.
[3:4] ~~ Sources: Ramesh Singh Indian Economy 10th ed. Part 5 p.215; app notes 3:98
## SUB 4 | Vertical Fiscal Imbalance
[4:0] - Cause: the Union holds the buoyant bases (**corporation tax, income tax, most of GST**) while States carry heavy spending.
[4:1] - Worsened by **cesses/surcharges outside the pool**, loss of indirect taxes to GST, CSS conditions, weak State revenue. Transfers 2026-27: ₹25.44 lakh crore.
