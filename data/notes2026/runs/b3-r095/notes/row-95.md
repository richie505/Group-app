| Committee | Year | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Nicholas Kaldor** | 1956 | First suggested **expenditure tax**; also **wealth, gift, capital gains** taxes |
| **Wanchoo** (Direct Taxes Enquiry) | 1971 | **Black money**, tax evasion |
| **K.N. Raj** | 1972 | **Taxation of agricultural wealth and income** – proposed the **Agricultural Holding Tax** |
| **Raja J. Chelliah** (Tax Reforms Committee) | 1991-93 | **Lower rates**, broader base, **minimum incentives/exemptions**; basis of the 1992 wealth-tax revision; "father of Indian tax reforms" |
| **Vijay Kelkar Task Forces** (direct and indirect taxes) | Set up Sept 2002, report Dec 2002 | Abolish **rebates under Section 88** (also 80L, Sec 10 interest); abolish **wealth tax** and the **standard deduction**; exempt dividends and capital gains on listed equity; exemption limit **₹1 lakh**, **₹1.5 lakh for senior citizens and widows** |
| Kelkar Task Force on FRBM | 2004 | First proposed a national **GST** |
| Parthasarathi Shome | 2012 | GAAR review; drop retrospective tax |
| Justice R.V. Easwar | 2015-16 | Simplifying the Income-tax Act |
| T.R. Rustagi (Rastogi) | 2006-07 | Review of **service tax** circulars (CBEC; report March 2007) – not CBDT restructuring, as some lists say |
| Task force on a new direct tax law | Nov 2017 | Convener **Arbind Modi**; later headed by **Akhilesh Ranjan** (report 2019) |

> Exam angle: committee ↔ subject items – Raj = agricultural taxation (5 PYQs), Kelkar = Section 88 rebates, Kaldor = expenditure tax, Chelliah = lower rates/fewer incentives.

=== Income Tax – History, Budgets, Slabs and Deductions
- **Income tax** first introduced **1860** by **James Wilson**, the first **Finance Member** (Act assent **24 July 1860**); a **progressive direct tax**; the **Income-tax Act, 1961** took effect **1 April 1962**.
- Budget **1997-98** (FM **P. Chidambaram**, "dream budget"): cut almost **every tax**; income-tax rates **10, 20, 30%** (from 15, 30, 40%); corporate tax 40% → 35%.
- **One-by-six scheme**: return filing compulsory for anyone showing any one of six lifestyle signs; the **telephone connection** criterion was dropped in Budget **2002-03**.
| Budget | Exemption / slab fact |
|---|---|
| 2005-06 | Senior citizens exempt up to **₹1.5 lakh** |
| 2012-13 | ₹2 lakh general; **₹2.5 lakh senior citizens (60-80)**; ₹5 lakh very senior (80+) |
| 2020-21 (first new optional regime) | 0-2.5 lakh nil; **2.5-5 lakh 5%**; **5-7.5 lakh 10%**; 7.5-10 lakh 15%; 10-12.5 lakh 20%; 12.5-15 lakh 25%; above 15 lakh 30% |
| 2023-24 | Up to 3 lakh nil; 3-6 lakh 5%; 6-9 lakh 10%; 9-12 lakh 15%; **12-15 lakh 20%**; above 15 lakh 30%; top surcharge (above ₹2 crore) cut 37% → 25% |
| 2025-26 (unchanged in 2026-27) | 0-4 lakh nil; 4-8 lakh **5%**; 8-12 lakh 10%; **12-16 lakh 15%**; 16-20 lakh 20%; **20-24 lakh 25%**; **above 24 lakh 30%** |
- **New regime (Section 115BAC)**: **default, exemption-free**; old regime with deductions stays optional. **Section 87A rebate**: income up to **₹12 lakh** tax-free; **₹12.75 lakh** for salaried after the **standard deduction of ₹75,000**.
- Old regime: senior citizens ₹3 lakh, very senior ₹5 lakh exemption limit.
- **Deductions**: interest on a **housing loan** (Sec 24(b)) is deductible; interest on an **education loan** is also deductible (**Sec 80E**); **Section 80C** covers NSC (and its accrued interest), PPF, EPF, life insurance, tuition fees of **two** children, home-loan **principal**.
> Exam angle: budget-specific slab questions – key the slab of the budget asked about; the 12-15 lakh slab was 20% (2023-24) but 12-16 lakh is 15% now.

=== Minimum Alternate Tax and Corporate Tax Cuts
- **MAT** taxes **"zero-tax companies"** – companies showing **book profits** but paying little or no regular tax because of exemptions and deductions.
| Milestone | Fact |
|---|---|
| First introduced | Budget **1987-88** (announced 1986-87; **Finance Act 1987**, Sec 115J; effective AY 1988-89); rate 15.75% |
| Withdrawn / reintroduced | Withdrawn **1990**; **reintroduced in Budget 1996-97** (Sec 115JA, from AY 1997-98) – the year most books and keys give as "introduced" |
| Before 2026 | 15% of book profits |
| Budget 2026-27 | **14%**, a **final tax**; no new MAT credit after 1 April 2026; old credit set off up to **1/4** of liability (new regime only) |
- **Taxation Laws (Amendment) Act, 2019** (Sept 2019 ordinance): domestic companies **30% → 22%** (Sec **115BAA**, effective ~**25.17%**); new manufacturing companies after 1 Oct 2019 **15%** (Sec **115BAB**, ~**17.01%**); **no MAT** for those opting in.
- Taxes on the **corporate sector**: corporation tax on company profit, MAT, Fringe Benefit Tax (2005-09). **Capital gains tax** is the usual "not corporate" answer (a levy on an investor's gain), though companies too pay it.
> Exam angle: MAT = zero-tax companies (2 PYQs); "introduced in" → **1996-97** in the options (strictly 1987-88).

=== Special Direct Taxes – Introduced and Status
| Tax | Introduced | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Estate duty | 1953 | Abolished 1985 |
| **Wealth tax** | **1957** (Wealth Tax Act; Kaldor) | Abolished 2015 |
| **Expenditure tax** | **1958** – FM T.T. Krishnamachari, on **Kaldor's** advice | Abolished (1962; revived briefly 1964-66) |
| Gift tax | April 1958 | Abolished **October 1998** |
| STT | **1 October 2004** | In force |
| DDT | – | Abolished from 1 April 2020; dividends taxed in **shareholders'** hands |
| **Angel tax** | **Budget 2012** – on share premium of unlisted start-ups above fair value (Sec 56(2)(viib)) | Abolished in Budget **2024-25** |
- **Capital gains**: arise on **transfer** of a capital asset; tax on sale of **inherited property** = **capital gains tax** (no inheritance tax in India).
- Short-term asset: held **up to 24 months** (general rule); **LTCG 12.5%** (as of Aug 2026); **indexation** uses the **Cost Inflation Index** (CBDT, base **2001-02**).
- **Rural agricultural land** is **not a capital asset**; agricultural income is tax-free (State subject).
- **Poll tax** (Community Charge, 1989-90) – the unpopular tax linked to **Margaret Thatcher's** downfall (1990).

=== Black Money and Disclosure Schemes
- Main worry from black money: **loss of revenue** to the Exchequer through **tax evasion** (UPSC 2021).
| Scheme | Key fact |
|---|---|
| VDIS 1997 | Voluntary disclosure of black income |
| IDS 2016 | 1 June – 30 Sept 2016; **45%** levy |
| **PMGKY 2016** (**Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Yojana**) | After demonetisation; **50%** levy; declarations **17 Dec 2016 – 31 March 2017** |
| Taxation Laws (Second Amendment) Act, 2016 | Tax on undisclosed income/unexplained deposits (Sec 115BBE) raised to **60%** (+25% surcharge, cess, penalty) |
| **Operation Clean Money** (Jan 2017) | **Income Tax Department**; e-verification of post-demonetisation deposits – about **18 lakh** persons/accounts with suspicious cash deposits flagged |
| Vivad se Vishwas | Direct Tax Vivad se Vishwas Act, **2020**; second scheme 2024 – settle pending disputes |
| FAST-DS 2026 | Foreign Assets of Small Taxpayers – Disclosure Scheme (Budget 2026-27): 30% tax + equal amount = **60%**; limit **₹1 crore**, or **₹5 crore** for previously taxed but unreported assets (₹1 lakh fee); file till 31 Dec 2026; immunity under the **Black Money Act, 2015** |

=== Cross-border and Digital Taxation
- **BEPS** (OECD/G20): **15 actions** to curb **tax avoidance by multinational companies** shifting profits to low-tax places (UPSC 2016).
- **OECD Pillar Two**: **15% global minimum** corporate tax for **large MNEs** (not all companies).
- **Equalisation levy**: **6%** on online advertisement services by non-residents (Finance Act **2016**, a **separate levy, not part of the Income Tax Act**, so **no DTAA credit**; abolished from 1 April 2025); **2%** on e-commerce (2020, dropped 2024).
- **Vodafone** (SC, 2012) → **Finance Act 2012** taxed **indirect transfers retrospectively**; **Taxation Laws (Amendment) Act, 2021** withdrew such demands (Vodafone, Cairn).

=== Tax Administration and PAN
- **CBDT**: statutory under the **Central Boards of Revenue Act, 1963**; in the **Department of Revenue** (not Expenditure).
- **Faceless assessment + Taxpayers' Charter**: **13 August 2020**; **faceless appeals** September 2020; **Project Insight** (2017, working 2019) – AI and data analytics; **AIS** shows all reported transactions; **e-Sahyog** – online check of mismatches.
- **PAN** (e.g. AFZPK7190K): 10 characters – 5 letters, 4 numbers, a letter; **4th character** = holder type (**P = individual/person**, C = company, H = HUF, F = firm); shows name, father's name, DOB – **not proof of address**.
- **TCS on LRS** for education and medical cut **5% → 2%** (2026-27).

=== Direct Taxes Code and Income-tax Act, 2025
- **Direct Taxes Code** idea: one law to replace the Income-tax Act 1961 (and Wealth Tax Act) – it relates to **income tax** and other direct taxes; DTC Bills 2009/2010 lapsed.
- **Income-tax Act, 2025** replaced the 1961 Act **w.e.f. 1 April 2026** (not 2025); a single **Tax Year** replaces Assessment Year and Previous Year.
