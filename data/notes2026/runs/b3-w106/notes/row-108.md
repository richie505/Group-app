=== Who Prepares the Survey and How It Is Presented
- Published every year by the **Ministry of Finance** – prepared by the **Economic Division, Department of Economic Affairs**, under the **Chief Economic Adviser (CEA)**; not RBI, NITI Aayog, Planning Commission, NDC or ISI.
- Presented in **both Houses** during the Budget Session, **before the Union Budget** (a day or a few days earlier); a **convention**, not a constitutional duty (Art. 112 is the Budget).
- **First Survey 1950-51**; separate from the Budget since **1964**; single volume + statistical appendix since 2021-22.
- **First Economic Adviser** after Independence: **J.J. Anjaria**.
| CEA | Surveys |
|---|---|
| Arvind Subramanian | 2014-15 to 2017-18 |
| K.V. Subramanian | 2018-19 to 2020-21 |
| Sanjeev Sanyal (Principal Economic Adviser) | 2021-22 |
| **V. Anantha Nageswaran** | 2022-23 to 2025-26 (term to 31 March 2027) |
- **AP Socio-Economic Survey** – by the **Planning Department**, tabled with the State Budget; **UN ESCAP** Survey (HQ **Bangkok**) is separate.

=== Economic Survey 2025-26 – Theme and Data
- Theme **"Building Strategic Indispensability"**; Chapter 1 "State of the Economy: **Pushing the Growth Frontier**"; **17 chapters** (new: **Artificial Intelligence**, **urbanisation**).
| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Real GDP FY26 (First Advance Estimates) | **7.4%** |
| FY27 projection | **6.8% – 7.2%** |
| Potential growth | **7.0%** |
| Sectors FY26 | Agriculture 3.1%, manufacturing 7.0%, **services 9.1%** ("stabilising anchor") |
| CPI inflation | **1.7%** (Apr-Dec FY26) |
| Bank GNPA | **2.2%** (multi-decade low) |
| Repo cut since Feb 2025 | **125 bps** |
- Fiscal: FD **4.8%** in FY25 vs 4.9% budgeted; **S&P upgrade BBB- to BBB**; concerns – **State revenue deficits and unconditional cash transfers**.
- External: low CAD and forex cover over 11 months of imports seen as **an external strength**.
- Climate chapter: **adaptation over mitigation**; conclusion **Sreya vs Preya** (**Katha Upanishad**).
- Statistics: National Accounts and IIP **rebased to 2022-23**; reforms of 2025 – GST overhaul, 100% FDI in insurance, nuclear power opened, Labour Codes (not abolition of GST).
- Deregulation line: ES 2023-24 "**letting go**", ES 2024-25 central theme, ES 2025-26 from "regulation and control" to "**enabling**", **trust-based compliance** in place of inspection; public capex **₹12.2 lakh crore** for 2026-27.
- **Earlier frameworks:** Twin Balance Sheet hidden NPAs exposed by the **RBI Asset Quality Review (2015)**; Chakravyuha and Twin Balance Sheet answered by the **IBC**; Bare Necessities Index (ES 2020-21) at **State level**, from **NSO 69th and 76th rounds**, excludes primary education; Centre's gross tax growth highest in **2016-17 (17.9%)**.
