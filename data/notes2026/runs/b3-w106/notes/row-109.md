=== GSDP Size, Growth Rate and AP's Rank
# Concepts
- **GSDP = GVA + product taxes – product subsidies**; State income = value of all goods and services produced within the State, counted once.
- Compiled by the **Directorate of Economics & Statistics (DES), AP** (not NSO) at current and constant **2011-12** prices; **3 sectors, 17 sub-sectors** (agriculture 5, industry 4 incl. **construction**, services **8**).
- **Estimate cycle:** AE/FAE → FRE → SRE → TRE; figures are revised (e.g. 2024-25 real growth 9.24% FAE → 8.27% FRE).
- **India's new GDP series** (MoSPI, 27 Feb 2026) has base **2022-23**; AP tables still use 2011-12.

=== Bifurcation's Impact and AP in India
# What bifurcation did to the economy
- AP bifurcated on **2 June 2014** (AP Reorganisation Act, 2014); residual AP had **13 districts**.
- **Hyderabad alone ≈ 48%** of undivided AP's total revenue of **₹70,548 crore (2012-13)**.
- **APRA, 2014 gives AP no share of Hyderabad's tax revenue**: taxes go to the State where the property is or where the tax is assessed (Sec. 50).
- Loss of Hyderabad's revenue base → AP a **chronic revenue-deficit State**; capital and major institutions stayed with Telangana → new capital **Amaravati**.
- Hyderabad-centred services and industry went to Telangana → **agriculture's share rose**; new IT parks at **Visakhapatnam, Tirupati, Vijayawada**.
- **Assertion-reason (2017):** better governance and service delivery was stressed (R true), but there was **no turnaround in fiscal performance** (A false) – keyed "A false, R true".
- **1999-2006:** AP's **primary sector ranked 2nd among States by compound growth rate**.
