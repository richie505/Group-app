=== NOTES 2:31 | Presiding Officers – Speaker, Deputy Speaker, Chairman; Secretariat; Sessions, Adjournment, Prorogation, Dissolution
## SUB 0 | Presiding Officers at A Glance
[31:0:0] TABLE head: Office | Chosen | Resigns to | Removal
  [31:0:0.0] Speaker | LS from its members (Art 93); date by the President | Deputy Speaker (Art 94(b)) | Effective majority, 14 days' notice (Art 94(c))
  [31:0:0.1] Deputy Speaker | LS from its members; date by the Speaker (Rule 8(1)) | Speaker | Same
  [31:0:0.2] Chairman (RS) | VP ex officio (Arts 64, 89(1)) | – | Only by removal as VP
  [31:0:0.3] Deputy Chairman | RS from its members (Art 89(2)) | Chairman | RS effective majority, 14 days' notice (Art 90(c))
[31:0:1] - Resignations go **laterally**, not upward to the President or PM.
[31:0:2] - **No separate oath** for Speaker, Deputy Speaker or Deputy Chairman – only the oath as an **MP**.
[31:0:3] - **14 days' notice**: removal of the **President, Speaker, Deputy Speaker, Deputy Chairman**. The **Governor** is removed by the President at will, without notice.
[31:0:4] - Salaries of Speaker and Deputy Speaker: **charged on the Consolidated Fund** (**Art 97**).
## SUB 1 | Speaker – Election, Office, Removal
[31:1:0] # Election and status
[31:1:1] - Speaker's provisions: **Arts 93–96** (election, vacation, removal), **100** (casting vote), **110** (Money Bill), **118** (joint sitting rules).
[31:1:2] - **Art 93**: chosen by the House from **among its members** – must already be an **elected MP**; the six-month grace is only for **Ministers**.
[31:1:3] - **LS Secretariat** works directly under the Speaker's control.
[31:1:4] # Vacation and dissolution
[31:1:5] - Vacates on ceasing to be a member (Art 94).
[31:1:6] - On **dissolution**, the Speaker **does not vacate** – continues till **just before the first meeting** of the new LS; the **Deputy Speaker vacates at once**. States: **Art 179** same rule for the State Speaker.
[31:1:7] - Removal only by an LS resolution passed by a **majority of all the then members** (not simple, not two-thirds).
[31:1:8] # Own removal resolution (Article 96)
[31:1:9] TABLE head: Situation | Presides | Speaks | Votes first instance | Casting vote
  [31:1:9.0] Normal sitting | Yes | Yes | No | Yes (Art 100(1))
  [31:1:9.1] Own removal resolution | No, even if present | Yes | Yes | No
[31:1:10] - **Chairman (RS)** during his own removal: cannot preside and **cannot vote**, but may speak.
[31:1:11] # Pro-Tem Speaker (Article 95(1))
[31:1:12] TABLE head: Basis | Regular Speaker | Pro-Tem Speaker
  [31:1:12.0] Article | 93 | 95(1)
  [31:1:12.1] Chosen by | Elected by LS | Appointed and sworn in by the President – usually the senior-most member (list sent to the Minister of Parliamentary Affairs)
  [31:1:12.2] Role | Presides; enforces rules | Administers oath to new MPs; holds the Speaker's election
  [31:1:12.3] Tenure | Through dissolution | Ends when the regular Speaker is elected
[31:1:13] - **Article 96:** the Speaker (or Deputy Speaker) shall not preside while a resolution for his removal is under consideration; he may speak and vote in the first instance, but has no casting vote.
## SUB 2 | Speaker's Powers
[31:2:0] TABLE head: Power | Article / basis
  [31:2:0.0] Casting vote only in a tie | Art 100(1)
  [31:2:0.1] Decides whether a Bill is a Money Bill – final | Art 110(3)
  [31:2:0.2] Certificate endorsed on every Money Bill sent to RS and the President | Art 110(4)
  [31:2:0.3] Presides over joint sitting (summoned by President, Art 108) | Art 118(4)
  [31:2:0.4] Anti-defection decisions | Tenth Schedule – subject to judicial review (Kihoto Hollohan, 1992)
  [31:2:0.5] Adjourns/suspends sitting without quorum (one-tenth) | Art 100(3)
  [31:2:0.6] Recognises Leader of Opposition – party needs one-tenth of House strength; LoP statutory since the 1977 Act | Speaker's direction
  [31:2:0.7] Fixes Deputy Speaker's election date | Rule 8(1)
  [31:2:0.8] Rulings protected from courts | Art 122
[31:2:1] - Money Bill decision cannot be questioned by any House or the President; the RS cannot overrule it.
[31:2:2] - Resigned Minister makes a statement in LS with the **Speaker's permission**; cannot be forced to.
[31:2:3] President appoints the Pro-Tem Speaker; **adjournment sine die**, Tenth Schedule decisions and the Deputy Speaker's election date are the **Speaker's**.
## SUB 3 | Deputy Speaker and Panel of Chairpersons
[31:3:0] # Deputy Speaker
[31:3:1] - Elected by LS from its members (Art 93); date fixed by the Speaker, told to members by the **Secretary-General**.
[31:3:2] - **From the Opposition** – only a **convention**, not constitutional.
[31:3:3] - **Art 95**: does the Speaker's work when the office is vacant or he is absent.
[31:3:4] - In the Chair: same powers as the Speaker – **no appeal to the Speaker** against his ruling; **not subordinate** to the Speaker.
[31:3:5] - Out of the Chair: an ordinary member – debates, moves motions, **votes in the first instance**.
[31:3:6] - Own removal: cannot preside; may speak and vote in first instance; no casting vote.
[31:3:7] - Automatically **chairman of any committee** he is appointed to.
[31:3:8] - Joint sitting order (**Art 118(4)**): **Speaker → Deputy Speaker → RS Deputy Chairman**.
[31:3:9] - LS Deputy Speaker post **vacant since June 2019** – through the 17th and 18th Lok Sabhas; also vacant in **8 State Assemblies** (Art 178), Jharkhand for **21 years** (Jun 2026).
[31:3:10] # Panels
[31:3:11] - **Panel of Chairpersons**: nominated by the **Speaker**, up to **ten**; a member presides when both Speaker and Deputy Speaker are **absent**, **not when the Speaker's post is vacant**.
[31:3:12] - RS: **Panel of Vice-Chairpersons** nominated by the Chairman.
## SUB 4 | Chairman and Deputy Chairman of the Rajya Sabha
[31:4:0] - **VP** is ex-officio Chairman; has a **casting vote** only; is not a member of RS.
[31:4:1] - When the VP **acts as President**, he draws the President's salary and the **Deputy Chairman** does the Chairman's work (**Art 91**).
[31:4:2] - **Jan 2026 (Justice Varma case)**: SC upheld the Deputy Chairman's power under **Art 91** to decide a judge-removal motion when the Chairman's office was vacant.

## SUB 6 | Sessions – Article 85
[31:6:0] - **Art 85(1)**: President summons each House at such time and place as he thinks fit; the Houses may be summoned **separately**.
[31:6:1] - **Maximum gap six months** – from the **last sitting of one session** to the **first sitting of the next** → at least **two sessions a year** (APPSC G2 2017).
[31:6:2] - **Not in the Constitution**: number of sessions (Budget, Monsoon, Winter are a **convention**; Budget, Feb–May, longest) or **minimum sitting days** (not 60, 90 or 100).
[31:6:3] - **Session**: first sitting → prorogation or dissolution. **Recess**: prorogation → next session.
[31:6:4] - **Quorum**: **one-tenth** of total members, **including the presiding officer** – **Art 100(3)** (casting vote is 100(1)).
[31:6:5] - States: **Art 174** – same six-month rule; State Assemblies averaged only **24 sittings in 2025**.
## SUB 7 | Ending A Sitting or Session
[31:7:0] TABLE head: Device | By whom | Ends | Effect on business
  [31:7:0.0] Adjournment | Presiding Officer | A sitting, for hours/days/weeks | Session unaffected; business stays
  [31:7:0.1] Adjournment sine die | Presiding Officer | Sitting, no date fixed | Precedes prorogation; bills continue (APPSC 2025)
  [31:7:0.2] Prorogation – Art 85(2)(a) | President on CoM advice (Governor in States) | The session, not the House | Bills do not lapse; pending notices lapse, except notices to introduce bills
  [31:7:0.3] Dissolution – Art 85(2)(b) | President – LS only | Life of the House | Pending business lapses (subject to Art 107); RS is permanent
[31:7:1] - Sequence: adjournment sine die → prorogation → recess → new session.

## SUB 9 | Parliament Secretariat – Article 98
[31:9:0] - **Art 98(1)**: **separate secretarial staff** for each House; **common posts** may be created; Parliament may regulate recruitment by law.
[31:9:1] - **Art 98(3)**: until such law, rules by the **President after consulting the Speaker/Chairman**; appointment orders given by the Speaker.
[31:9:2] - Head: **Secretary-General** of each House – appointed by the **presiding officer**, not the Union Government; rank of **Cabinet Secretary**.
[31:9:3] - **Independent of the executive**: LS Secretariat under the Speaker, RS Secretariat under the Chairman.
