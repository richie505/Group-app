## SUB 0 | Union Executive – Article 53 and the President
[0:0] # Article 53
[0:1] - **Art. 52:** there shall be a President of India.
[0:2] - **Art. 53(1):** the **executive power of the Union is vested in the President** and is exercised by him **either directly or through officers subordinate to him** (Ministers included) in accordance with the Constitution – "only through officers" is wrong.
[0:3] - **Art. 53(2):** **Supreme Command** of the Defence Forces vests in the President, its exercise **regulated by law**.
[0:4] - **Art. 53(3)(b):** nothing in Art. 53 **prevents Parliament from conferring by law functions on authorities other than the President**.
[0:5] - **Appointing the PM** (Art. 75) is an **executive power** of the President.
[0:6] # Nominal vs real executive
[0:7] - President = **nominal / constitutional head**; **Head of State**, not Head of Government; **First Citizen**; **Rank 1** in the Order of Precedence; part of Parliament (**Art. 79**).
[0:8] - **Real executive:** the **Council of Ministers** headed by the **PM** (**Art. 74(1)**), collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha – the feature that shows this is **parliamentary democracy** (not federalism or adult franchise).
[0:9] - **Art. 77:** all executive action is taken **in the President's name**.
[0:10] - **Art. 299:** all **contracts** made in exercise of the Union's executive power are **expressed to be made by the President** (for a State, by the Governor).
[0:11] > A-R angle: "President is the executive power of the Union" (A) is explained by Art. 53(1) (R) – both true, R explains A.

## SUB 1 | Union Territories – Administration, Delhi and Lieutenant Governors
[1:0] # Article 239 and Delhi
[1:1] - **Art. 239:** every UT is administered by the **President**, acting through an **Administrator / Lieutenant Governor** appointed by him – so "UTs are administered by the President".
[1:2] - **Delhi** is a **Union Territory** in the First Schedule; the **69th Amendment, 1991** made it the **National Capital Territory** – **Art. 239AA**: Legislative Assembly + Council of Ministers.
[1:3] - **Outside the Delhi Assembly's power:** **public order, police, land** (State List entries 1, 2, 18) – not public health or industries.
[1:4] - **Police Complaints Authorities:** in **March 2010** the **MHA** ordered PCAs for the **Union Territories** (following Prakash Singh, 2006); States set up their own.
[1:5] - **Ladakh (2026):** **Art. 371-like safeguards** instead of the Sixth Schedule (Jul); a **sui generis** elected body (Sep).

## SUB 2 | State Executive and the Extent of Executive Power
[2:0] TABLE head: Article | Level | Provision
  [2:0.0] 73 | Union | Matters on which Parliament can legislate + treaty/agreement rights
  [2:0.1] 154 | State | Executive power vests in the Governor
  [2:0.2] 162 | State | Matters within the State Legislature's competence, within the State – heading "Extent of executive power of State"
  [2:0.3] 163(1) | State | CoM advises the Governor, except in discretionary matters
  [2:0.4] 371A / 371H | State | Governor's special responsibility for law and order: Nagaland / Arunachal Pradesh
[2:1] - **Art. 154** vests power in the Governor (Arts 153 = office of Governor, 155 = appointment, 156 = term); real power lies with the Council of Ministers.
[2:2] - **Art. 161 remission:** Governor bound by **State Cabinet advice** (SC, Apr 2026).

## SUB 3 | Concurrent List – Who Executes?
[3:0] - **Default:** executive power on Concurrent subjects stays with the **States**.
[3:1] - **Exception:** the Centre executes where the **Constitution or a Parliamentary law** expressly says so (provisos to **Arts 73 and 162**).

## SUB 4 | Entrustment, Directions and Compliance
[4:0] TABLE head: Article | Direction | Condition
  [4:0.0] 258(1) | Union → State | President, with the State's consent
  [4:0.1] 258(2) | Union → State | Parliament by law, no consent needed
  [4:0.2] 258A | State → Union | Governor entrusts State functions with the Union's consent (7th Amendment, 1956)
  [4:0.3] 256 | States | Ensure compliance with Union laws and existing laws; Union may direct
  [4:0.4] 257(1) | States | State executive power must not impede or prejudice the Union's executive power
  [4:0.5] 257(2), (3) | Directions | Means of communication of national/military importance; protection of railways
  [4:0.6] 365 | Non-compliance | President may hold the State cannot be run per the Constitution → opens Art. 356
[4:1] - **⚠ Trap:** "compliance with Union laws" is **Art. 256**, not 257; Art. 258A is **not** about river valley agreements.
