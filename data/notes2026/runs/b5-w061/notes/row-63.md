=== Radioisotopes in Medicine and Cancer Therapy
| Isotope | Emits | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Cobalt-60** | Powerful **gamma** | Cancer **radiotherapy** (teletherapy, internal neoplasms); sterilising surgical instruments |
| **Iodine-131** | Beta + gamma | **Thyroid** diagnosis and treatment (BRIT supplies it as sodium iodide) |
| **Phosphorus-32** | Beta | **Blood cancer (leukaemia)**, polycythaemia; fertiliser tracer |
| **Sodium-24** (radio-sodium) | Gamma | **Speed of blood flow**, circulation blocks, clots; pipeline leaks |
| **Arsenic-74** | Positron | **Detecting/locating (brain) tumours** |
| Technetium-99m | Gamma | Most used diagnostic isotope (SPECT) |
| Iron-59 | – | Iron metabolism |
| **Gold-198** | – | **Cancer** treatment (not retinal disorders) |
- **Radon** = radioactive **noble gas** used to treat cancer (not xenon, krypton, argon, helium, neon).
- **Teletherapy** = external beam (**Co-60**); **brachytherapy** = sealed source inside/next to the tumour (Ir-192, I-125).
- **Electron beam therapy:** radiation therapy for **certain cancers** (e.g. skin lymphoma).
- **CyberKnife:** robotic, image-guided system giving a precise radiation dose with sub-millimetre accuracy – it **treats**, it does **not map the spread** of a tumour.
- **LINAC = Linear Accelerator** (radiotherapy machine).
- **PET** = positron emitters, mainly **F-18 FDG** (made at the 30 MeV Medical Cyclotron, Kolkata); **SPECT** = **Tc-99m**.
- **Radioimmunoassay (RIA):** measures tiny amounts of hormones, enzymes, drugs in blood.
- **Tata Memorial Centre:** Anchor Centre for IAEA's **'Rays of Hope'**.
> Leukaemia: **P-32** is the usual key; some keys also accept Co-60 (external radiotherapy).

=== Radiometric Dating
| Method | Dates | Half-life |
|---|---|---|
| **Carbon-14** (₆C¹⁴) | Once-living remains – **fossils, wood, bones, shells**, trees – up to **~50,000 years** | **~5,730 years** |
| **Uranium-238 → Lead-206** | **Most ancient rocks/mountains**, **age of the Earth** | ~4.5 billion years |
| Potassium-40 → Argon-40 | Volcanic rocks, fossil-bearing layers | ~1.25 billion years |
- **Radioactive dating** measures the **age of rocks** (absolute age) – not their composition, colour or weight.
- **Clair Cameron Patterson** pioneered uranium-lead dating of the Earth.
- Radiocarbon, U-Pb and K-Ar all date soil/minerals/rocks or remains; **mining** is not a dating technique.
- Carbon dating is the standard answer for "**age of organic matter / fossils of animals and trees**"; very old fossils and rocks need uranium methods.

=== Radiation Basics, Units, Shielding and Hazards
| Radiation | Nature | Stopped by |
|---|---|---|
| Alpha | Helium nucleus (+) | Paper, skin |
| Beta | Fast electron (−) | Thin aluminium |
| **Gamma** | **Electromagnetic, no charge** | Thick **lead** or concrete |
| **X-rays** | **Electromagnetic** | Lead aprons |
- **X-rays and gamma rays** are electromagnetic; alpha, beta and cathode rays are particles.
- **CT scanning uses X-rays**.
- **Lead** shields against nuclear radiation (dense; absorbs gamma and X-rays).
| Quantity | Unit |
|---|---|
| **Activity** | **Becquerel** (1 decay/s) – SI unit of radioactivity |
| Absorbed dose | Gray (1 J/kg) |
| Biological effect | Sievert |
- **Half-life** = time for half the nuclei to decay.
- **Dosimeter** records the radiation dose received by a person; Geiger counter detects/counts radiation.
- **Radon:** **alpha emitter** from radium/uranium decay; from soil, **granite, brick, concrete**; accumulates indoors – the **most important indoor air pollutant** in such questions; long inhalation causes **lung cancer** (second to smoking).

=== Institutions and Isotope Production
- **BRIT** (Board of Radiation and Isotope Technology): **industrial unit of DAE**, Vashi, Navi Mumbai; works in **isotope technology** (not railway wagons, IT or road transport).
- **BARC**: AEET **1954**, renamed **1967**; India's **leading radioisotope producer**. Dhruva = largest research reactor; Apsara = Asia's first; KAMINI = only U-233-fuelled reactor.
- Isotopes are made at **Trombay reactors, the Kolkata accelerator and NPCIL plants**; India supplies Co-60 and I-131 to 40+ countries.
- **Cyclone-30:** a **particle accelerator (cyclotron) that produces radioisotopes** – not a cyclone centre or a reactor.

=== Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generator (RTG)
- Turns **decay heat into electricity** via thermocouples – the **Seebeck effect** (thermocouple principle); **not a fission reactor**; no moving parts.
- Fuel: **Plutonium-238** (by-product of weapons production).
- Used on **Voyager, Cassini, Curiosity, Perseverance, New Horizons**.

=== Agriculture, Food and Industry
- **Mutation breeding**: **L. J. Stadler (1928)**, X-rays on **barley**; later gamma rays. **72** BARC Trombay varieties; **TBM-9 = banana**; **RTS-43 = sorghum**.
- **Sterile insect technique**: radiation-sterilised males mate with wild females – no viable offspring.
- **P-32 / N-15** trace fertiliser uptake; nuclear **moisture gauges** guide irrigation.
- **Food irradiation**: radiolysis of water; **Co-60 gamma, X-rays or electron beams**; packed or bulk food; stops sprouting, kills pests; **Radura** = irradiated-food symbol. Raising crop variety = mutation breeding, not food irradiation.
- **Industry**: gamma radiography (**Ir-192, Co-60**) for welds; **smoke detectors = Americium-241** (alpha); thickness gauges; **Na-24** tracer for pipeline leaks; port sediment tracers **reduce** desilting; Co-60 sterilises medical products.
