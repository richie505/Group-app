## SUB 0 | Six Orthodox Schools – Founders, Texts and Pairs
[0:0] TABLE head: School (darshana) | Founder | Key text | Core idea
  [0:0.0] Samkhya (oldest) | Kapila | Samkhya Sutra; Samkhya Karika (Ishvarakrishna) | Dualism of Purusha and Prakriti; Satkaryavada
  [0:0.1] Yoga | Patanjali | Yoga Sutra | Ashtanga (eight-limbed) discipline; Samkhya metaphysics
  [0:0.2] Nyaya | Gautama (Akshapada) | Nyaya Sutra | Logic; four pramanas; 16 padarthas
  [0:0.3] Vaisheshika | Kanada | Vaisheshika Sutra | Atomism (paramanu); 9 dravyas
  [0:0.4] Purva Mimamsa | Jaimini | Mimamsa Sutra | Vedic ritual and duty (karma)
  [0:0.5] Uttara Mimamsa (Vedanta) | Badarayana (Vyasa) | Brahma Sutra | Brahman the only reality; knowledge of Brahman
[0:1] - **Three pairs:** **Samkhya–Yoga, Nyaya–Vaisheshika, Mimamsa–Vedanta**.
[0:2] - **Oldest darshana = Samkhya** (APPSC 2018 flashcard). Kapila is its **earliest exponent**.
[0:3] - **Uttara Mimamsa = Badarayana = Vyasa** (APPSC G1 2002) – not Kapila, Patanjali or Gautama; an old APPSC key giving Kapila is wrong.
[0:4] - **Not among the six:** **Smriti** (a class of texts), **Lokayata** (Charvaka materialism), **Kapalika** (a Shaiva sect) (UPSC 2014), Charvaka, Ajivika.
[0:5] - **Fully articulated / perfected in the Gupta age** (SSC CPO 2003); their sutras are older.
[0:6] - **Navya-Nyaya** ("new logic") was founded by **Gangesha Upadhyaya** of Mithila (Tattvachintamani, 14th c.); Raghunatha Shiromani came later.
[0:7] - **Kapila and the sons of Sagara:** in the Ramayana and Puranas the sage **Kapila** burnt the 60,000 sons of King Sagara to ashes; Bhagiratha brought the Ganga down to redeem them.
[0:8] > Founder matches: Kapila–Samkhya, Patanjali–Yoga, Gautama–Nyaya, Kanada–Vaisheshika, Jaimini–Purva Mimamsa, Badarayana–Uttara Mimamsa.
[0:9] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 1 p.25, p.36-37; PMF Art & Culture Part 5 p.97-102; NextGen Indian History Part 3 (Guptas); Rocket Indian History Part 1 p.121; Britannica 'Nyaya'
## SUB 1 | Mimamsa – Jaimini, Kumarila Bhatta, Karma and Apurva
[1:0] - **Founder:** **Jaimini** (Mimamsa Sutra). Other exponents: **Kumarila Bhatta** and **Prabhakara**.
[1:1] - **Kumarila Bhatta** = **Purva Mimamsa** (APPSC G2 2012, 2019; AP History PYQs) – not Uttara Mimamsa, Advaita, Dvaita, Yogachara or Jnana marga. Works: **Slokavartika**, Tantravartika. He **defended Vedic ritual (karma-kanda)** and attacked Buddhist doctrines.
[1:2] - Also called **Karma-Mimamsa**: it centres on ritual action → the **theory of karma** belongs to Mimamsa (UPPSC 1997).
[1:3] - **Liberation through karma** – correct performance of Vedic rites and duties (UPSC 2005).
[1:4] - The **Vedas are eternal** and contain eternal truth (CGPSC 2013).
[1:5] - **Apurva** = the **unseen force** that links a ritual act (karma) with its later result.
[1:6] - **Purva Mimamsa = Karmakanda** (ritual part of the Vedas); **Uttara Mimamsa = Jnanakanda** (knowledge part).
[1:7] ~~ Sources: PMF Art & Culture Part 5 p.100-101; Rocket Indian History Part 1 p.121; NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 1 p.37; old app notes row 22
## SUB 2 | Rigvedic Gods – Indra, Agni, Varuna, Soma
[2:0] TABLE head: God | Domain | Exam fact
  [2:0.0] Indra | Lightning, storm, war | ~250 hymns (most); ranked first; Purandara (fort-breaker); most popular god; wife Shachi (Indrani)
  [2:0.1] Agni | Fire | ~200 hymns (second); messenger between gods and men; first hymn of the Rigveda (1.1)
  [2:0.2] Varuna | Water, cosmic order | Ranked third; upholder of Rita; ethically the highest
  [2:0.3] Soma | Plant and ritual drink | Whole 9th Mandala; god of plants; Haoma of the Avesta
  [2:0.4] Surya | Sun | Aspects: Savitri (Gayatri), Mitra, Pushan, Vishnu
  [2:0.5] Pushan | Paths, herdsmen, cattle | Solar aspect; god of the Shudras in later Vedic times
  [2:0.6] Brihaspati | Prayer | Priest (purohita) and preceptor of the gods
  [2:0.7] Dyaus | Sky | Celestial father, like Zeus
  [2:0.8] Ushas | Dawn | Female
  [2:0.9] Aditi | Mother of the gods | Female; not Prithvi (Prithvi = fertility goddess)
  [2:0.10] Aranyani | Forest | Female
  [2:0.11] Rudra | Disease | Archer god of the mountains; later merged with Shiva
  [2:0.12] Yama | Death | –
  [2:0.13] Vishnu | Aspect of Surya | Only 3 hymns; rose later
[2:1] - **War-god** = Indra (UPPSC 2011, Uttarakhand 2016). When Indra is not offered, the most hymns = **Agni** (UPPSC 2010).
[2:2] - **Priest of the gods** = **Brihaspati** in most keys; Agni is also hailed as purohita in Rigveda 1.1.
[2:3] - **Later Vedic:** **Prajapati** became supreme creator; **Vishnu** rose as preserver; **Rudra** grew; **Indra and Agni lost importance**.
[2:4] > Indra 250 > Agni 200 > Varuna third; 9th Mandala = Soma; Aditi (not Prithvi) = mother of gods.
[2:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 1 p.28, p.34-36; Britannica 'Brihaspati', 'Indra'; old app notes row 22
## SUB 3 | Vedanta Schools – Shankara, Ramanuja, Madhva
[3:0] TABLE head: School | Teacher | Meaning | Path to mukti
  [3:0.0] Advaita | Adi Shankaracharya (8th c., Kaladi, Kerala) | Non-dualism: Brahman alone is real, Atman = Brahman, world = Maya | Jnana (knowledge)
  [3:0.1] Vishishtadvaita | Ramanujacharya (11th c., Sriperumbudur; Srirangam) | Qualified non-dualism: souls and world real, inseparable from Vishnu | Bhakti and divine grace
  [3:0.2] Dvaita | Madhvacharya | Dualism | Bhakti
  [3:0.3] Dvaitadvaita | Nimbarka | Dualistic non-dualism | –
  [3:0.4] Shuddhadvaita | Vallabhacharya | Pure non-dualism | –
[3:1] - **Shankara:** founded **four mathas** – **Sringeri** (south), **Puri** (east), **Dwarka** (west), **Badrinath / Jyotirmath (Joshimath)** (north); not Prayag, Kanchi or Varanasi. Called a "**crypto-Buddhist**" (Buddhist in disguise). Also Dashanami order and Panchayatana worship.
[3:2] - **Ramanuja:** first great promoter of the **Bhakti** movement; Sri Vaishnava.
[3:3] - **Bhagavad Gita:** gives **equal importance to Jnana, Karma and Bhakti** as means of liberation (UPPSC 2005).
[3:4] - **Swami Vivekananda's Neo-Vedanta** reworked Advaita.
[3:5] - Other teachers: **Lakulisha** – Pashupata Shaivism; **Nagarjuna** – Madhyamika (Shunyavada).
[3:6] > Advaita = Shankara = non-dualism = jnana; Vishishtadvaita = Ramanuja = bhakti; Dvaita = Madhva.
[3:7] ~~ Sources: PMF Art & Culture Part 5 p.98, p.105-110; Britannica 'Bhagavadgita'; old app notes row 22
## SUB 4 | Doctrines of the Schools – Samkhya, Nyaya, Vaisheshika
[4:0] TABLE head: Doctrine | School
  [4:0.0] Satkaryavada (effect pre-exists in the cause) | Samkhya (MPPSC 2025)
  [4:0.1] World formed by the blending of nature (Prakriti) | Samkhya
  [4:0.2] Atom as the basis of the world | Vaisheshika (Kanada)
  [4:0.3] Pramanas – perception, inference, comparison, testimony | Nyaya
  [4:0.4] Apurva; theory of karma | Mimamsa
  [4:0.5] Niyati (predetermined fate) | Ajivika (Makkhali Gosala)
  [4:0.6] Materialism | Lokayata / Charvaka
  [4:0.7] Syadvada | Jaina
  [4:0.8] Shunyavada / Madhyamika | Nagarjuna
[4:1] - **Samkhya:** **accepts rebirth**; liberation by **self-knowledge** (discriminating Purusha from Prakriti), not by an outside agent → UPSC 2013 "only 2". Kapila taught that man's life is shaped by the **forces of nature, not a divine agency** (CGPSC 2020: both true).
[4:2] - **Nyaya:** "nyaya" = logic or decision; **16 padarthas**. **Vaisheshika:** 9 dravyas; beginning of physics in India.
[4:3] - **APPSC G1 2023 match:** Samkhya – blending of nature; Vaisheshika – atom; Lokayata – materialism; Ajivika – predetermined fate.
[4:4] ~~ Sources: PMF Art & Culture Part 5 p.99-104; NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 1 p.37-38; Rocket Indian History Part 1 p.121; old app notes row 22
## SUB 5 | Astika vs Nastika; Charvaka (Lokayata)
[5:0] - **Test:** **belief in the authority of the Vedas** – astika accept it, nastika reject it (UPPSC 2005); not belief in God, rebirth or heaven.
[5:1] - **Astika:** the six darshanas. **Nastika:** **Charvaka, Buddhism (Bauddha), Jainism, Ajivika**.
[5:2] - **Charvaka = Lokayata** ("of the common people"); credited to **Brihaspati**; **materialism**; **perception** the only valid knowledge; no soul, rebirth or afterlife.
[5:3] - **Ajita Kesakambali** – early materialist of the Buddha's time.
[5:4] > Odd-one-out lists slip a nastika school (Charvaka, Bauddha, Ajivika) among the six.
[5:5] ~~ Sources: PMF Art & Culture Part 5 p.97, p.105; old app notes rows 22-23
## SUB 6 | Upanishadic Philosophy
[6:0] TABLE head: Concept | Meaning
  [6:0.0] Brahman | Ultimate reality (cosmic principle)
  [6:0.1] Atman | The self – not body or mind
  [6:0.2] Karma | Links action with consequence
  [6:0.3] Samsara | Rebirth – transmigration first in the Brihadaranyaka
  [6:0.4] Moksha | Liberation – not a Vedic Samhita term; first discussed in the Upanishads
[6:1] - **Main theme = philosophy** (UPPSC 2004, UP Lower 1998); anti-ritualistic; **Atman is Brahman**.
[6:2] - Criticised **mechanical sacrifice**; stressed meditation and the guru-disciple relationship; **ignorance** = cause of sorrow.
[6:3] - **Katha (Katha) Upanishad:** **Nachiketa** questions **Yama** on death and the Atman (UPSC 1997).
[6:4] - Basic approach is universal – it does not reject other schools or scriptures.
[6:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 1 p.25, p.29; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.2; old app notes row 22

## SUB 8 | Nature of Vedic Religion – Rita, Dharma, Sacrifice
[8:0] - **Nature worship**; male gods dominate; worship for material gains; **sacrifice (yajna) central**, in the open air – **no temples, no idols** (UPPSC 1993: "sacrifice and rituals").
[8:1] - **Henotheism** (term of **Max Müller**): each god praised in turn as supreme.
[8:2] - **Rita** = cosmic and moral order, upheld by **Varuna**; **Dharma** = obligation and duty to oneself and others, grown from Rita → UPSC 2011 "both".
[8:3] - Iranian parallels: **Mitra–Mithra, Rita–Asha, Soma–Haoma**.
[8:4] TABLE head: Ritual / term | Associated with
  [8:4.0] Hotri (Rig), Adhvaryu (Yajur), Udgatri (Sama) | Priests
  [8:4.1] Ceremonial chanting | Samaveda
  [8:4.2] Agnistoma | Regular Soma sacrifice
  [8:4.3] Rajasuya, Vajapeya, Ashvamedha | Royal sacrifices
  [8:4.4] Grihya Sutras | Domestic rites
[8:5] - **Sanskaras: 16** = 3 before birth, 12 during life, **1 after death**.
[8:6] - **Later Vedic:** elaborate, costly sacrifices; Prajapati supreme; Indra and Agni decline; priests grow powerful.
