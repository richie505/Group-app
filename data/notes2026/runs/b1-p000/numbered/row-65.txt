## SUB 0 | Bhagavatism and Vaishnavism
[0:0] # Origin
[0:1] - **First worshippers of Vasudeva-Krishna:** the **Bhagavatas** (Vasudevakas) (UPPCS 1997) – not Vedic Aryans, Tamils or Abhiras; Vasudeva worship is known from **Panini's** age.
[0:2] - **Originator:** **Krishna (Vasudeva)** of the **Vrishni (Satvata)** clan, son of Devaki, pupil of Ghora Angirasa (Chhandogya Upanishad) (RAS 1993) – not Janaka, Yajnavalkya or Surdas.
[0:3] - **Earliest epigraphic evidence:** the **Garuda pillar at Besnagar** (Vidisha, c. 2nd c. BCE), set up to Vasudeva by **Heliodorus**, envoy of the Indo-Greek **Antialcidas** at the court of the Sunga **Bhagabhadra** (UKPSC 2012 ×2) – the Prayag prashasti, Nasik and Ayodhya records are later.
[0:4] - **Doctrine:** **bhakti**, **ahimsa**, the **avatara** theory – **ten incarnations**; open to women, Vaishyas, Shudras and foreigners.
[0:5] # Gupta patronage
[0:6] - **Greatest contribution to the Bhagavata sect:** the **Guptas** (State PSC) – virtually the state religion; rulers styled **Parama Bhagavata** (first: **Samudragupta**); dynastic emblem **Garuda**.
[0:7] - **Udayagiri (MP):** colossal **Varaha** panel carved under **Chandragupta II**.
[0:8] # Avatars and attributes
[0:9] - **Varaha (boar):** the incarnation that **saved the Earth from the sea**, lifting her on his tusks after killing **Hiranyaksha** (UP UDA/LDA 2010) – not Matsya, Kurma or Narasimha.
[0:10] - **Dashavatara:** Matsya, Kurma, Varaha, Narasimha, Vamana, Parashurama, Rama, Krishna, Buddha, Kalki.
[0:11] - **Kaustubha (Kausthubham):** the gem on **Vishnu's chest**, from the churning of the ocean (APPSC Endowment 2022).
[0:12] - **Vaishnava symbols:** chakra, shankha, Garuda; **AP angle:** Salankayanas were Vaishnavas (title Parama Mahesvara); Pedavegi inscription of Nandivarman II – first AP record of Vishnu temples.
[0:13] ~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval 03 p.71-72; NextGen Indian History APPSC 03 p.9; app notes 1:65 (sub 1)
## SUB 1 | Saivism, Shaktism and the Puranas
[1:0] # Shaiva sects
[1:1] TABLE head: Sect | Key facts
  [1:1.0] Pashupata | Oldest Shaiva sect; founder Lakulisha
  [1:1.1] Kalamukha | Followers of Kalanana (disciple of Lakulisha); extremist; Simha Parishat in Andhra
  [1:1.2] Kapalika | Skull-bearing Vamacharas; Bhairava worship; Srisailam a Kapalika kshetra; Bhairavakonda caves
  [1:1.3] Mattamayura | A Shaiva sect known from Chedi (Kalachuri) inscriptions (IAS 1996)
  [1:1.4] Nathpanth / Lingayats | Matsyendranath, Gorakhnath / Basava
[1:2] - **Kapalikas emerged from Misra Saivam** (APPSC G2) – NextGen's APPSC fact list; NextGen's AP history also calls them the Vamacharas of the Kalamukhas. Not Pashupata Saivam, Vaishnavam or Shuddha Saivam in the APPSC key.
[1:3] - **Worshippers of Kapalini:** the **Kapalikas** (APPSC Endowment 2022).
[1:4] - **⚠ Not a Shaiva sect:** Ajivika (Makkhali Gosala), Mayamata (a text on architecture), Isanasivagurudeva Paddhati (a ritual manual).
[1:5] - **Gudimallam** (Tirupati district): earliest known Shiva linga; Gupta **ekamukhalinga** = linga with one face.
[1:6] # Shakti
[1:7] - **Ardhanarishvara:** half Shiva, half Parvati = the **union of God with his Shakti** (UPPSC 1997).
[1:8] - **Mother goddesses:** Durga, Amba, Kali, Chandi; **Tantrism** spread from the **5th century**, open to women and Shudras.
[1:9] - **Kalika Purana:** an **upapurana** of **Shaktism**, likely composed in **Assam** (MPPSC 2018 ×2) – not Vaishnava, Buddhist or Jain.
[1:10] # Puranas
[1:11] - **18 Mahapuranas**; took their final shape in the **Gupta period**; five topics (**pancha-lakshana**): creation, re-creation, genealogies, manvantaras, dynastic history.
[1:12] - **Surya worship:** the silk-weavers' guild built a **Sun temple at Mandsaur** under **Kumaragupta I**; Skandagupta's **Indore** plate endows a lamp in a Sun temple.
[1:13] - **Kakatiya link:** the **Kalamukha and Pashupata** sects later flourished under the Kakatiyas.

## SUB 2 | Gupta Temple Architecture – Deogarh, Bhitargaon and the Panchayatana
[2:0] - **Earliest free-standing Hindu temples** survive from the **Gupta** age: square **garbhagriha**, pillared mandapa, raised **jagati**.
[2:1] TABLE head: Stage | Examples | Features
  [2:1.0] First | Sanchi Temple 17, Tigawa | Flat roof, square sanctum, shallow portico
  [2:1.1] Second | Nachna (Parvati), Bhumara (Shiva) | Covered ambulatory, raised platform; Bhumara has two flanking shrines
  [2:1.2] Third | Deogarh (Dashavatara, Vishnu), Bhitargaon | Low square shikhara; Panchayatana plan
[2:2] - **Dashavatara temple, Deogarh:** in **Lalitpur** district near **Jhansi** (UP); **Gupta** period (APPSC G2 2000) – not Pallava, Chola or Vijayanagara; Vishnu, ten avatars; earliest **Panchayatana** temple of North India.
[2:3] - **Panchayatana:** a **style of temple construction** – main shrine plus **four subsidiary shrines** at the corners (UPSC 2014 ×2) – not a village assembly, sect or official.
[2:4] - **Deogarh and Garhwa:** the Deogarh temple near Jhansi and the sculptures of **Garhwa** near Allahabad (Prayagraj) are remains of **Gupta art** (State PSC).
[2:5] - **Bhitargaon** (near Kanpur): **brick and terracotta** Hindu temple – one of the oldest terracotta temples; not a Buddhist chaitya.
[2:6] - **Gupta temple sites:** Deogarh, **Bhitargaon**, **Bhitari** (Ghazipur – Skandagupta's pillar and excavated Gupta Vishnu temples), Nachna, Bhumara, Tigawa, Sanchi, Eran – **Rajgir** is not listed among Gupta temple sites (SI 2018).

## SUB 4 | Buddhism, Jainism and Religious Tolerance
[4:0] - **Buddhism:** lost royal patronage, overshadowed by Bhagavatism; **Nalanda** founded by **Kumaragupta I**; persecuted by the Huna **Mihirakula**.
[4:1] TABLE head: Scholar | Contribution
  [4:1.0] Vasubandhu | Abhidharmakosa
  [4:1.1] Dinnaga | Founder of Buddhist logic
  [4:1.2] Buddhaghosa | Visuddhimagga
[4:2] - **Jainism:** **Vallabhi council** (5th c.) under **Devarddhi Kshamashramana** wrote down the **Svetambara** canon; **Mathura** a centre of Jain art; **Kahaum** pillar (Skandagupta) records Jain images.
[4:3] - **Tolerance:** Samudragupta let **Meghavarman** of Sri Lanka build a monastery at Bodh Gaya; **Amrakardava**, Chandragupta II's officer, made a grant at Sanchi.
[4:4] - **Purusharthas:** dharma, artha, kama, moksha – the first three = **trivarga**; devotional Hinduism replaced sacrificial Brahmanism.
[4:5] - **Match:** Jainism – Triratna, Agamas, Mahavira; Buddhism – Ashtanga Margam; Saivism – Linga; Ajivika – Makkhali Gosala; Lingayat – Basava.
[4:6] ~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval 03 p.72; NextGen Indian History APPSC 03 p.3-4, p.9; app notes 1:65 (sub 0, 5, 6, 7, 8)
