## SUB 0 | Gunaga Vijayaditya III (848–892) – Greatest King, Titles, Pandaranga, Kandukuru
[0:0] # The greatest Eastern Chalukya king
[0:1] - **Gunaga Vijayaditya = Vijayaditya III**, the **greatest king** of the Vengi Chalukyas (G2 2011 ×2) – not Danarnava, Chalukya Bhima I or Rajaraja Narendra; ruled **43 years**; "Gunaga" for his skill in mathematics.
[0:2] - **Military:** defeated the **Rashtrakuta Krishna II** and the **Kalinga king Devendravarma**; expanded the kingdom; issued grand **copper-plate** records with royal titles (ATWO).
[0:3] # Titles
[0:4] - **Tripura Martya Maheswara** (G2 2012, Mains 2017; G1, G2 PYQ) – not Vishnuvardhana I/III, Vijayaditya I, Kubja Vishnuvardhana, Rajaraja Narendra or Vijayaditya II.
[0:5]   - Assumed to commemorate his victory over **Nellore, Kiranapura and Achalapura** (G2 2012).
[0:6]   - **⚠ Settled:** the printed key "Kiranapuram, Achalapuram and **Kandukur**" is wrong – NextGen and Rocket give **Nellore, Kiranapura and Achalapura**.
[0:7] - Other titles: **Gunaka-Nallata, Parachakra Rama, Vallabha**, Vira Makaradhwaja, Ranaranga Sudraka, Dakshinadhipati (Sachivalayam: the set = **Vijayaditya III**, not Chalukya Bhima, Vishnuvardhana IV or Amoghavarsha).
[0:8] # Pandaranga (Panduranga), his commander
[0:9] - **Pandaranga = commander of Gunaga Vijayaditya** (G1 1983-84, 1987; Welfare Officers 2017; APPSC PYQ) – i.e. of the **Vengi (Eastern) Chalukyas**, not the Ananda Gotrikas, Pallavas or Badami Chalukyas.
[0:10] - One of **four Brahmin generals** (Pandaranga, Vinayaditya Sarma, Kadiyaraju, Rajaditya); subdued the **Boyas**, built **Pandurangapuram** and a Maheswara temple on **Pulicat lake**.
[0:11] - Issued the **Addanki inscription** – the **first Telugu verse (padya) inscription**, in **Taruvoja** metre.
[0:12] # Kandukuru "on the lines of Bezawada"
[0:13] - **Vijayaditya III (Gunaga Vijayaditya)** developed **Kandukuru** like **Bezawada** (G2 2008 ×2, 2019; Mains; APPSC PYQ) – Pandaranga made it a strong fort after conquering the Boya kingdoms.
[0:14]   - **⚠ Settled:** one G2 2008 printed key gave **Vijayaditya II** – wrong; Vijayaditya II is the 108-temples king.

## SUB 1 | Nannaya and Telugu – Nandampudi, Narayana Bhattu and the First Telugu Inscriptions
[1:0] # Nannaya
[1:1] - **Nannaya Bhattu** (Adi Kavi), **court poet of Rajaraja Narendra** (G2 1993, 2011 ×2) – Rajaraja Narendra **patronised** him, not Bhima II, Vijayaditya, Vishnuvardhana or Ammaraja; Pampa and Nannechoda are distractors.
[1:2] - Began the **Andhra Mahabharatamu** (c. 1045–65; **Adi, Sabha** and part of **Aranya** parvas) – the **earliest surviving Telugu literary work**, in **Champu** (verse + prose) style; the court poet, **not the king**, began it.
[1:3] - **Telugu literature owes its origin to the (Eastern) Chalukyas** (APPSC JA) – not Cholas, Cheras or Pandyas; the Vengi court turned Telugu into a literary language.
[1:4] - Titles: **Adikavi, Vagadheeshwara, Shabda Shasanudu**; grammar **Andhra Shabda Chintamani**.
[1:5] - **A–R (ATWO):** A (earliest surviving Telugu work) and R (translated in the 11th century at Rajaraja Narendra's request) are both true; the key takes R as the explanation of A.
[1:6] # Narayana Bhattu and the Nandampudi grant
[1:7] - **Narayana Bhattu helped Nannaya** (JA 2012); he was a scholar sent to Vengi as the **Kalyani Chalukya** (Someshvara I) representative; title **Ashtabhasha Kavisekhara**.
[1:8] - **Evidence: the Nandampudi grant (copper-plate inscription) of Rajaraja Narendra** (G2 Prelims 2019; Mains 2017; G2 PYQ; APPSC PYQ ×2) – not Andhra Kavi Tarangini, Kollipaka, Pedda Cherukuru, Maruturu, Kupama or Mangallu.
[1:9] - **Donee of Nandampudi village = Narayana Bhattu** (G2 Mains 2017; G2 PYQ); **Nannaya composed** the grant – Nannaya, Jina Vallabha and Pavuluri Mallana were not the donee.
[1:10] - **Korumelli** plates of Rajaraja Narendra – same scribe as Nandampudi.

## SUB 2 | Capitals and Cities – Rajamahendravaram, Vengi, Pithapuram, Bezawada
[2:0] # Rajamahendravaram (Rajahmundry)
[2:1] - **Built by Ammaraja I (Vishnuvardhana VI, 921–927)**, title **Rajamahendra**, after which the city is named (APPSC PYQ ×3; JA 2012; G2 2008); he shifted the capital from Vengi there – "**cultural capital of the Andhras**".
[2:2]   - **⚠ Settled:** one G2 2008 printed key gave **Rajaraja Narendra** – he only **ruled from** Rajahmundry and **made it famous**; NextGen and its previous-paper key: "**Ammaraja ruler built Rajahmundry**".
[2:3] - **Dynasty:** Rajamahendri was built in the time of the **Eastern Chalukyas** (G2 2001, 2002; unknown 2002 "founded by the Eastern Chalukyas") – not Vishnukundins, Kalingas or Vijayanagara.
[2:4] - **Statement (VRO):** A – Rajamahendravaram built by **Ammaraja Vishnuvardhana I** ✓; B – **Rajahmundry district** created in the **Madras Presidency in 1823** ✓ (split into Godavari and Krishna in 1859).

[3:6] # Jain patronage
[3:7] - **Ayyana Mahadevi**, queen of **Kubja Vishnuvardhana**, built the Jain **Nedumba (Nadumbi) Vasati / Nedumbasadi** at **Vijayawada** (G2 PYQ; APPSC PYQ) – recorded in the **Musinikonda grant**; not Mylama, Upasika Bodhisiri or Devi Naganika; not Gunaga Vijayaditya, Jatachoda Bhima or Jayasimha Vallabha.
[3:8] - **Vimaladitya** = the Eastern Chalukya king who **embraced Jainism** (guru **Trikalayogi Siddhanti**; Ramatirtha cave) (JA 2012) – not Gunaga Vijayaditya, Bhima I or Rajaraja Narendra.

[3:10] - **Dynasty that most propagated Jainism in Telugu land = the Vemulawada Chalukyas** (Welfare Officers 2017 ×2) – patrons of **Pampa** and Somadeva Suri; Baddega built the Subhadhama Jinalaya – not the Eastern, Badami or Kalyani Chalukyas.

## SUB 4 | Founder, Dynasty Outline and the Chola Connection
[4:0] # Founder
[4:1] - **Kubja Vishnuvardhana**, **brother of Pulakeshin II**, founded the **Chalukyas of Vengi** (UPPSC 2003) – not Bhanuvardhan, Vijayaditya I or Pulakeshin IV.
[4:2] - **Pulakeshin II** took Vengi after defeating the **Vishnukundins** (c. 616); Kubja **viceroy 617**, **independent 624** (JL 2006) – Kopparam plates (**631**) of Pulakeshin II call him **Yuvaraja**; Chipurupalli plates (**Vizianagaram**) of his **18th regnal year**.
[4:3] - Titles: **Vishamasiddhi** ("conqueror of difficult places / forts") (APPSC PYQ) – not Jayasimha I, Vijayaditya I/II; also **Makaradhwaja**, Kamadeva, Parama Bhagavata.
[4:4] # Identity
[4:5] - Branch of the **Badami Chalukyas**; initially **subordinates of Badami**; ruled **624–1075** (over four centuries); emblem **boar (Varaha)**; official language **Sanskrit** (most of ~100 grants); matronymic **Hariti-putra** (like the Satavahanas, Ikshvakus, Kadambas); seal sign manual **Tribhuvanankusa**.
[4:6] - **NOT true (ATWO):** they were **not annexed by the Rashtrakutas** – they survived the long Rashtrakuta wars and merged into the **Chola** line.
[4:7] - **Contemporaries in the south: the Pallavas** (Jayasimha I clashed with them) – not Ikshvakus (earlier), Mughals or Palas.
[4:8] - **Land measures:** **nivartana**, kharidika, kharis = units of **area** (land) (APPSC JA).

[4:11] - Succession war: **Rajaraja Narendra** (son of Kundavai, backed by **Rajendra I**) vs **Vijayaditya VII** (son of Melama, backed by Kalyani Jayasimha II); **Rajadhiraja I** won at **Dharanikota**, burnt **Kollipaka**, was killed at **Koppam** by **Ahavamalla Someshwara**.
[4:12] - After **973** the main rival was the **Kalyani Chalukyas**.
[4:13] # Chola connection and the end
[4:14] - The **Cholas kept close political and matrimonial ties with the Chalukyas of Vengi** (G1 Re-exam 2010; G1 PYQ) – not the Pandyas or the Badami/Kalyani Chalukyas.
[4:15] - **Rajendra Chalukya = Kulottunga I** (married **Madhurantaki**, daughter of **Rajendra II**) united the two lines – first Chalukya-Chola king; the **Chalukya-Chola era / Mandalika Yugam** (1076–c.1210, Velanati Chodas) followed.
[4:16] - Andhra order: **Eastern Chalukyas → Kakatiyas → Reddis → Vijayanagara**; **Kesari Samudra** (Kesamudram) lake = Kakatiya **Prola I**.

## SUB 5 | Vijayaditya II (Narendra Mrigaraja) – the 108 Shiva Temples
[5:0] - **Title Narendra Mrigaraja ("lion among kings") = Vijayaditya II** (APPSC PYQ; Welfare Officers 2017) – not Vishnuvardhana IV, Gunaga Vijayaditya or Chalukya Bhima; also Chalukya Rama, Vikramadhavala.
[5:1] - **108 Shiva temples – the Narendreswara / Narendra Mrigeswara-alayas = Vijayaditya II** (G2 2008 ×2, 2012, 2019; G2 PYQ; APPSC PYQ) – built at the sites of his **108 battles in 12 years** with the Rashtrakutas (Govinda III, Amoghavarsha), **as penance**; recorded in the **Sataluru** inscription.
[5:2]   - **⚠ Settled:** one G2 printed key gave **Vijayaditya I**; the king is **Vijayaditya II** (when he is missing from the options, the correct answer is added).
[5:3]   - Not Kubja Vishnuvardhana, Vishnuvardhana III/IV, Gunaga Vijayaditya or Chalukya Bhima (Bhima I = 360 battles).
[5:4] - He overthrew his brother **Bhima Salki** and took the throne (c. 808/818).
