=== Golden Age of Buddhism – Virapurushadatta
- **Golden age of Buddhism in Andhra** = the **Ikshvaku period** (APPSC PYQ) – not the Satavahana, Vishnukundin or Chalukya period.
- **Greatest patronage to Buddhism** in the Ikshvaku kingdom: reign of **Virapurushadatta** (G2 2008 ×2; APPSC PYQ) – not Sri Chantamula, Ehuvala Chantamula or Rudrapurushadatta.
- **Women patronised Buddhism** most under the Ikshvakus (APPSC PYQ); queens, princesses and rich lay women funded stupas and viharas.
- **Upasika Bodhisri** served Buddhism in the reign of **Virapurushadatta** (her Chula-Dhammagiri record is dated in his **14th** regnal year) (G2 2012).
- **⚠ Trap:** a G2 2012 answer grid printed **Ehuvala Chantamula** for Bodhisri – wrong; NextGen, Rocket and her own inscription place her under Virapurushadatta.
- **Trampled Shivalinga:** a Nagarjunakonda sculpture shows **Mathariputra Virapurushadatta** trampling a Shivalinga – a sign of his turn to Buddhism (APPSC PYQ).
| King | Religious policy |
|---|---|
| Vasishthiputra Sri Santamula (founder) | Vedic: Ashvamedha, Rajasuya, Vajapeya, Agnistoma; worshipped **Virupakshapati** (Shiva) and **Mahasena** (Skanda); agraharas to northern Brahmins |
| **Virapurushadatta** | **Only** Ikshvaku king to adopt Buddhism (earlier Vedic; did not wholly drop Brahmanical ties); "**Ashoka of the South**", **Sri Parvatadhipati**; rebuilt the Maha Chaitya under Acharya **Bhadanta Ananda**; set up the **Sriparvata University** |
| Ehuvala Santamula | Revived Brahmanism; first Hindu temples; first Sanskrit inscriptions; still built a vihara for the **Bahushrutiyas** in his mother's name |
| Rudrapurushadatta (last) | Vedic; deity Mahasena; custom of **chhaya-stambhas** (shadow pillars) near graves began |
- **Puranic Shaivism, Vaishnavism and Shakti worship** all thrived; the Ikshvakus were the **last royal Buddhist zenith** in Andhra.
- **Kings** (all except Virapurushadatta) were Vedic; kings were **not** the chief patrons of stupas and viharas.

=== Deities – Hariti and the Dynastic Gods
- **Hariti (Harati)** = **tutelary deity** of the **Ikshvakus** of Vijayapuri (G2 2012; APPSC PYQ) – the protector of **children**; women offered bangles for progeny.
- Installation of her image = beginning of **Shakti worship**; Hariti was also a mother goddess of the Early Chalukyas.
| Deity | Dynasty / group |
|---|---|
| **Hariti** | **Ikshvakus** (also Early Chalukyas) |
| **Sriparvataswamy** | **Vishnukundins** – family deity (identified with **Srisaila Mallikarjuna**) |
| **Chitraratha Swamy** (Sun) | **Salankayanas** – family deity |
| Bappa Bhattaraka | Salankayanas – "regarding the **father as God**" |
| Vishnu | Salankayana **Nandivarman II** – land grant in the Pedavegi inscription |
| Kakati | Kakatiyas |
| Virupaksha | Vijayanagara kings (Hampi) |
| Vasavi | Arya Vysya community – seat **Penugonda** |
- **Sri Parvataswamy** as tutelary deity = **Vishnukundins** (G2 1993) – not the Ikshvakus, Salankayanas or Anandagotrikas.
- **⚠ Trap:** Hariti = Ikshvaku tutelary deity; Virupakshapati and Mahasena = gods the Ikshvaku kings worshipped; Kakati = Kakatiyas.

=== Monasteries, Sects and Foreign Links
| Monastery / donor | Sect |
|---|---|
| Maha Chaitya (Chamtisri, Harmyasri) | **Aparamahavinaseliya** (Aparasaila) – Nagarjunakonda its main centre |
| Vihara of Bhatti Mahadevi (by Ehuvala) | **Bahushrutiya** |
| Kodabalisri's vihara | Mahishasaka |
| Bodhisri's **Sinhala Vihara (Chuladhammagiri)** | **Mahaviharavasin** (Theravada) – Sri Lankan monks |
| Purvasailiya | **Amaravati** |
| Aparasailiya | **Nagarjunakonda** |
- Mahasanghika branches / related sects: Purvasailiya, Aparasailiya, Bahushrutiya, Rajagirika, Siddharthika – **Mahaviharavasin is not one**.
- **Acharya Bhadanta Ananda** (Aparamahavinaseliya) knew the **Digha and Majjhima Nikayas** by heart.
# Maha Chaitya and Bodhisri
- A **Dhatugarbha** stupa over the Buddha's bodily relics: "**Samma Sambudhasa dhatuvara parigahita**".
- Earlier **Paravata Mahachaitya** built by **Yajnasri Satakarni** for **Acharya Nagarjuna**; rebuilt by **Chamtisri** with **9 ayaka pillars**.
- **Bodhisri's** works: stone mandapa at the eastern gateway of the Maha Chaitya at **Kantakasala (Ghantasala)**; **seven viharas at Papili**.
# Foreign links
- Bodhisri's inscription names lands that received the Dhamma: **Kashmir, Gandhara, China, Chilata, Tosali, Aparanta, Vanga, Vanavasi, Yavana, Damila, Palura** (not Magadha).
- **Ceylonese monks** made Nagarjunakonda their **Hinayana** base; **Mahadeva Bhikshu** took Buddhism from the **Pallava-bhoga** region to Sri Lanka (Mahavamsa).
- **Suhrllekha:** the Chinese pilgrim **Itsing** (I-tsing, in India 671–695 CE) saw Indian students memorising Nagarjuna's *Suhrllekha*, a letter to **Yajnasri Satakarni** (G2 2012) – not Fa-hien or Hiuen Tsang.
- **Hiuen Tsang (7th c.)** found the **Sriparvata vihara neglected**.

=== Brahmanical Temples at Nagarjunakonda
- The Ikshvakus built **India's first structural Hindu temples**: **9 Shaiva + 1 Vaishnava** at Nagarjunakonda.
- Temples found in excavations: **Harati, Mahajana, Kumara Swami, Pushpabhadra Swami, Ashtabhuja Narayana Swami**; also Nandikeshwara and Navagraha.
| Temple | Builder / deity |
|---|---|
| **Pushpabhadraswami** | Started by **Virapurushadatta**, completed by **Ehuvala Santamula**; Shiva; **Gajaprishtha** (elephant-back) plan |
| **Sarvadevadhivasam** | Commander **Elisiri** (of Ehuvala); for **Kumara Swami (Kartikeya)**; Sanskrit record |
| **Ashtabhuja Narayanaswami** | **Shivasena**, commander of the Abhira king **Vasusena**; **first Vaishnava temple** in Andhra |
- Inscriptions of Vasishthiputra Santamula were found at **Rentala** and Kesanapalli near Nagarjunakonda.
~~ Sources: NextGen Ikshvakus p.2, p.13-14, p.19; Rocket AP History sheet #4 (temples), #7; app notes 1:52 (sub 7)

=== Acharya Nagarjuna and Sriparvata
- **Nagarjuna** = **Madhyamika** philosopher; **Sriparvata = Nagarjunakonda / Vijayapuri**, named after him.
- **Yajnasri Satakarni** built viharas and chaityas at Sriparvata for him; **Virapurushadatta** set up the **Sriparvata University**.
- Sriparvata = "**Dakshina Gaya**", sacred to **Mahayana**, while **Bodh Gaya** is sacred to **Hinayana**.
- **Sri Parvataswamy = Buddha:** identified by **N. Venkataramanayya** (G2 2019); do not confuse with **Sriparvataswami**, the Vishnukundin family deity (Srisaila Mallikarjuna).
- **In news (May 2025):** Miss World contestants visited **Buddhavanam** next to Nagarjunakonda (Buddhist circuit).
