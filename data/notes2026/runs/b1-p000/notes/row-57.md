=== Ikshvaku Women Patrons – Bodhisiri and Chantisiri
# The women donors of Nagarjunakonda
| Woman | Dynasty | Patronage |
|---|---|---|
| **Bodhisiri (Bodhisri)** | Ikshvaku (Virapurushadatta's time) | **Upasika** (lay devotee); built **viharas and chaityas** at Nagarjunakonda; sent monks to **Kashmir and Tibet**; daughter of **Revanta of Amaravati** |
| **Chantisiri (Santisiri, Chamtisiri)** | Ikshvaku | Sister of **Vasishthiputra Chamtamula**; built the **Mahachaitya at Nagarjunakonda** over the Buddha's relics |
| Adavi Chantisiri | Ikshvaku | Daughter of Chamtamula; another royal donor |
| Gautami Balasri | Satavahana | Funded the **cave-vihara at Nasik**; not a Gathasaptasati poet |
- **Woman who gave the greatest service to Buddhism in the Ikshvaku period:** **Bodhisiri** (G2 2012, G2 2019, APPSC PYQ).
- **Mahachaitya at Nagarjunakonda:** **Chantisiri** (APPSC Tribal Welfare Officer).
- **⚠ Trap:** Bodhisiri and Chantisiri are **Ikshvaku**, not Satavahana; **Rudramadevi** (Kakatiya) and Vasavadatta are distractors; "Bapisiri" is a made-up option.

=== Gathasaptasati – the Chief Source of Satavahana Society
# Hala's anthology
- **Author:** Satavahana king **Hala**; about **700 verses (gathas)** in **Maharashtri Prakrit** on love, village and family life.
- **Chief literary source** of Satavahana social and cultural history (G2 Prelims 2019; APPSC PYQ) – not the Aitareya Brahmana, Dharmasutras or Puranas.
- **Opening:** begins with a prayer to **Shiva (Pasupati)** and ends with a prayer to **Gauri** (APPSC PYQ); Saiva names Pashupati, Rudra, Gauri, Ganapati appear.
- **Women poets in it:** **Madhavi, Revati, Anupamalabdha, Anulakshmi**.
- **Telugu words in it:** atta, addam, potta, pilla, padi.
> Exam angle: "most important source of Satavahana society" = **Gathasaptasati of Hala**; opening prayer = **Shiva**.
~~ Sources: NextGen Satavahanas p.1-2, p.14, p.23-26; app notes 1:57 (sub 5)

=== Dress, Cosmetics and Amusements
# Dancers and dress
- **Foundation cream of Satavahana dancers:** **Aradalam** – in Telugu "**maipoota / Maiputa**" (body coating) (G2 Mains 2019 and G2 2019).
  - **⚠ Settled:** "Maiputa = Kusuma Lepanam" (old app key) is wrong – Maiputa is the Telugu word for the foundation cream, which NextGen and Rocket name **Aradalam**; Kusuma Lepanam, Mukhateji and Meni-kanti are distractors.
- **Reliefs (Amaravati, Nagarjunakonda):** **men** – lower cloth and **turban**; **women** – heavy **anklets, bangles, girdles, necklaces**.
- **Amphitheatre at Nagarjunakonda (Vijayapuri):** for **performances and games**, not Vedic sacrifices.
~~ Sources: NextGen AP History Previous Papers p.13; Rocket AP History (Foundation cream = Aradalam); app notes 1:57 (sub 5)

=== Varna and Caste
# Varna in early Andhra
- **Caste of the Satavahanas:** **Brahmins** – Gautamiputra Satakarni is called **Ekabrahmana** (Eka Brahmin) and **Agama Nilaya** in the Nasik prasasti; kings upheld **varna-dharma**.
- **Nanaghat inscription:** Brahmins trained in Vedic learning and ritual; Brahmins got **tax-free villages**.
- **Kshatriya status by assimilation:** **Greeks, Sakas, Pahlavas, Kushanas** took Indian names and faiths – e.g. Nahapana's daughter **Dakshamitra**, son-in-law Rishabhadatta.
- **Vishnukundins:** followed varnasramadharma; **farmers = "the backbone"**, got land grants.
| Term | Occupation |
|---|---|
| **Halikas** | Farmers |
| Sethis | Merchants |
| **Selavaddikas** | Stone sculptors / masons |
| Swarnakaras | Goldsmiths |
- **Samayamu** = caste associations of the **Kakatiya** period (later term).
~~ Sources: NextGen Satavahanas p.15, p.25; app notes 1:57 (sub 0)

=== Position of Women and Marriage
# Women
- **Patriarchal joint family**, yet women had **respect and freedom** – joined men in festivals and gatherings and **donated generously** to viharas and chaityas.
- **Matronymics:** kings bore their mothers' names – **Gautamiputra** Satakarni, **Vasishthiputra** Pulumavi.
- **Titles from husbands:** **Mahasenapatni, Mahabhojaki, Maharathi**.
- **Sati:** a **Nagarjunakonda sculpture** and the Greek writer **Strabo**.
- **Polygamy** was practised in the **upper classes**; widows observed **celibacy**; marriage was **sacred**.
  - **⚠ Settled:** the PYQ "what inscriptions tell us about women" has three true options (prominence, lavish donations, matronymics) and no "all of these" – left unkeyed; "no polygamy among princes" is false.

=== Education and Popular Religion
# Education
| Centre | Nature |
|---|---|
| **Viharas** | Buddhist monastic learning (Nagarjunakonda a leading centre) |
| **Ghatikasthanas** | **Vedic colleges** (Vishnukundins) |
| **Agraharas** | **Land grants to Vedic Brahmins** |
- **Indrabhattaraka Varma** (Vishnukundin) – "**Ghatika Vapta punyasancharya**"; credited with founding ghatikas.
# Religion and folk cults
- **Amaravati reliefs:** worship of **nagas, yakshas and sacred trees** beside Buddhist symbols.
- **Ikshvaku kings** performed **Vedic sacrifices** (**Vasishthiputra Chamtamula**: Asvamedha, Vajapeya); **queens** patronised Buddhism; Brahmanical shrines such as **Pushpabhadraswami** stood at Nagarjunakonda.
- **Vishnukundins:** family deity **Sriparvataswamy**; titles **Paramamaheswara, Parama Brahmanya**.
~~ Sources: NextGen Vishnukundins; NextGen Ikshvakus p.10; app notes 1:57 (sub 3, 4)
