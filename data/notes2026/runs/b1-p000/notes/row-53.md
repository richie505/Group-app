=== Discovery, Excavation and the Nagarjuna Sagar Dam
| Year | Person / event |
|---|---|
| **1926** | Site **discovered** by **A.R. Saraswati** (A. Rangaswami Sarasvati, Telugu Assistant, Madras epigraphy office) |
| 1927–31 | Excavated by **A.H. Longhurst** (ASI) |
| 1938 | Re-excavated by **T.N. Ramachandran** |
| 1954–60 | Large **rescue excavation** before submergence; **R. Subrahmanyam** among the excavators; palace complex and brick Brahmanical temples |
- **Discoverer of Nagarjunakonda (Nagarjuna Hill):** **A.R. Saraswati** (Welfare Officers 2017 ×2; APPSC PYQ) – Longhurst and Ramachandran excavated later; R. Subrahmanyam led the 1950s dig.
- **Nagarjuna Sagar Project:** **Nehru** laid the foundation stone on **10 December 1955** at Nandikonda (construction 1955–67); the valley was submerged after **1960**.
- **⚠ Trap:** 29 Dec 1955, 23 Mar 1955, 1 Nov 1955 and 29 Dec 1956 are all wrong dates (AEE 2011 offered none of 10 Dec 1955).
- **Salvage archaeology:** ASI dismantled and rebuilt monuments on higher ground at **Anupu** (Krishna bank) and on **Nagarjunakonda island** – "**transplanted monuments**"; India's only **island museum**.

=== Language of the Inscriptions
- **Prakrit** = court language and language of the Ikshvaku inscriptions, including the Nagarjunakonda records of gifts to the great monastery (NTR Univ. JA; APPSC PYQ) – not Sanskrit, Telugu, Tamil or Paisachi.
- **Ehuvala Santamula** brought **Sanskrit** into inscriptions; Sanskrit replaced Prakrit from the **4th c. CE**.
| Inscription | Language |
|---|---|
| **Swarna Devalayam** (Golden Temple), Nagarjunakonda, Ehuvala's **11th** regnal year | **First Sanskrit inscription** of Andhra and South India |
| Elisri's Sarvadeva inscription | Sanskrit |
| Ehuvala's Gummadidurru record (Guntur) | Prakrit |
- Telugu words in the records: **Bapi, Remmanaka, Adavi, Ehuvala, Anikki** (not Dhanyakataka).
~~ Sources: NextGen Ikshvakus p.5, p.9, p.14, p.19; Rocket AP History sheet #11 (Prakrit – Ikshvaku); app notes 1:53 (sub 3)

=== Sculpture – the Amaravati School at Nagarjunakonda
- Nagarjunakonda = **main centre** of the **Amaravati School** in its **final, most mature phase**; **Mahayana** themes – Buddha's life and **Jataka** stories.
- Early Ikshvaku carving (low relief) = the "**last flicker**" of the Satavahana Amaravati tradition.
| Sculpture / feature | Significance |
|---|---|
| **Mandhata** (Chakravarti Mandhata), **Jaggayyapeta** | Most important Ikshvaku sculpture; first depiction of a Chakravarti |
| Sati panel (Nagarjunakonda) | First depiction of **sati** in Indian art |
| Sculptors' names carved | Start of the "**Sculptor's Style**" – e.g. **Badantacharya** at the Mahavihara |
- **Largest stupa in AP** = **Amaravati**; oldest stupa in Andhra = **Bhattiprolu**.
~~ Sources: NextGen Ikshvakus p.3, p.7, p.18; Rocket AP History sheet "Art" (Mandhata – Jaggayyapet); app notes 1:53 (sub 0)

=== Stupas, Temple Plans and Secular Buildings
| Stupa type | Built over |
|---|---|
| **Dhatugarbha** | Bodily relics (e.g. the Maha Chaitya) |
| **Paribhogika** | Objects used by monks (alms bowls) |
| **Uddeshika** | Nothing – purely commemorative |
- Major stupas: **wheel-shaped (spoked) plan** + **ayaka platforms** in the four directions, **five pillars** each; ayaka platforms belong to **Buddhist stupas**.
- Temples follow the **Agamic** plan: garbhagudi, antarala, mantapa, dhvajastambha, prakara; the Ikshvakus laid the base of **Dravida** temple architecture.
| Structure | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Rajaprasadam and prakaras | **Palace complex**; compound walls |
| **Kreeda Vedi** (amphitheatre) | Sports arena, thought built with **Roman help**; no parallel elsewhere in India |
| **Acoustic building** | Built on rules of sound; the site's most unique find |
| Kummakundam | **Water tanks / bathing pools** |
| Yeleswaram | Town credited to commander **Elisri**, who also built the Sarvadevalaya |
~~ Sources: NextGen Ikshvakus p.18-19; app notes 1:53 (sub 1, 2)
