=== Mahabharata – Characters, Epithets and Episodes
# Narrators and scribe
- **Ganesha** wrote the Mahabharata down as **Vyasa** dictated it (tradition, Adi Parva).
- **Sanjaya**, given divine sight by Vyasa, narrated the war to the blind king **Dhritarashtra** (the Bhagavad Gita is his report).
- **Vishnu Sahasranama** was first recited by **Bhishma** to Yudhishthira (Anushasana Parva).
# Who's who
| Person | Remember |
|---|---|
| Bhishma (Devavrata) | Son of King **Shantanu** and the river Ganga |
| Karna | **Radheya** (after foster mother Radha); led the Kaurava army for **two days** (16th–17th) after Drona |
| Arjuna | **Gudakesha** ("conqueror of sleep") |
| Sahadeva | Youngest Pandava; killed **Shakuni** on the 18th day |
| Nakula | Conch **Sughosha** (Sahadeva – Manipushpaka; Krishna – Panchajanya; Arjuna – Devadatta; Bhima – Paundra) |
- **Dushta chatushtaya** (the four wicked): **Duryodhana, Dussasana, Karna, Shakuni** – Sisupala is not one of them.
> Endowment-exam angle: epithet → hero, weapon/conch → owner, "who killed whom".
~~ Sources: Mahabharata (BORI Critical Edition) Adi, Karna, Shalya and Anushasana Parvas; Bhagavad Gita 1.14–1.18 (conches); old app notes 1:21

=== Bhagavata Purana, Sages and Puranic Stories
- **Shuka** (son of Vyasa) narrated the **Bhagavata Purana** to King **Parikshit**; Suta later retold it to Shaunaka's sages.
- **Ekavimsati** (21) in the Bhagavatam = the **21 avataras** of Vishnu listed in its first skandha.
- **Durvasa** = son of sage **Atri** (one of the Saptarishis) and **Anasuya** (with Dattatreya and Chandra).
- **Vaikhanasa** = a **maharshi** to whom the Vaikhanasa Agama of temple worship is traced.
- **Dhruva** = son of King **Uttanapada** and Suniti (Vishnu and Bhagavata Puranas).
- **Samudra manthan** of the Kshira Sagara: Mount **Mandara** = churning rod (serpent Vasuki = rope).
- **Gurus:** **Shukracharya** (son of Bhrigu) = guru of the asuras/rakshasas; **Brihaspati** = guru of the devas.
- **Vasishtha's cow** = **Nandini** (daughter of Kamadhenu) in most books and keys; the Valmiki Ramayana calls her **Shabala**.
~~ Sources: Bhagavata Purana 1.3 (avataras), 1.4 and 4.8 (Dhruva), 8.6–8.7 (churning); Vishnu Purana 1.11; Mahabharata Adi Parva (Nandini); old app notes 1:21
