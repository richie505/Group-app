## SUB 0 | Institutional Beginnings
[33:0:0] # INCOSPAR, ISRO, Department of Space
[33:0:1] TABLE head: Body | Year | Fact
  [33:0:1.0] INCOSPAR (Indian National Committee for Space Research) | 1962 | Under the Department of Atomic Energy; set up with Homi Bhabha, chaired by Vikram Sarabhai
  [33:0:1.1] ISRO | 15 Aug 1969 | Superseded INCOSPAR
  [33:0:1.2] Department of Space + Space Commission | 1972 | ISRO brought under DoS
[33:0:2] INCOSPAR (1962) came first; ISRO (1969) replaced it; DoS and Space Commission came in 1972.
[33:0:3] # TERLS, Thumba
[33:0:4] - **Thumba Equatorial Rocket Launching Station**, Thumba (**Kerala**); chosen because it lies close to the **magnetic equator** (study of the equatorial electrojet).
[33:0:5] - First sounding rocket: **Nike-Apache** (American-built), **21 Nov 1963**.
[33:0:6] - **RH-75**: first **Indian-made** sounding rocket, **20 Nov 1967**.
[33:0:7] - Satellite Telecommunication Earth Station, **Ahmedabad**: **1 Jan 1967**.
## SUB 1 | Early Satellites and Experiments
[33:1:0] TABLE head: Date | Satellite / experiment | Vehicle / site
  [33:1:0.0] 1 Aug 1975-76 | SITE: first use of space technology for education; with NASA, ATS-6 satellite; about 2,400 villages, nearly 5 million people | NASA ATS-6
  [33:1:0.1] 19 Apr 1975 | Aryabhata: first Indian satellite; 360 kg; India entered the space age | Soviet Kosmos-3M, Kapustin Yar (USSR)
  [33:1:0.2] 1977-79 | STEP (Satellite Telecommunication Experiments Project) | Franco-German Symphonie satellite
  [33:1:0.3] 1979-81 | Bhaskara I (1979) and II: experimental earth observation | Soviet Intercosmos rocket, Baikonur
  [33:1:0.4] 18 Jul 1980 | Rohini: first satellite on an Indian rocket; India 6th nation | SLV-3, Sriharikota (AP)
  [33:1:0.5] 19 Jun 1981 | APPLE: first experimental geostationary communication satellite | Ariane-1, Kourou, French Guiana
  [33:1:0.6] 1983 | INSAT-1B | US Space Shuttle Challenger
  [33:1:0.7] 1988 | IRS-1A: first operational remote sensing | Soviet Vostok, Baikonur
[33:1:1] - Aryabhata was conceived by Sarabhai, launched after his death; satellite team led by **U.R. Rao**.
[33:1:2] - Chronology: **SITE (1975) → STEP (1977) → APPLE (1981)**; satellites **Aryabhata → Bhaskara → Rohini → APPLE**.
[33:1:3] NASA's ATS-6 was used for SITE, not to launch APPLE. APPLE went up on Ariane-1.
