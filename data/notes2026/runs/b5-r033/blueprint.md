## 3. Angles
- **Factual recall** dominates (about 80%): first satellite, years, locations, full forms.
- **Dated "current holder"**: ISRO chairman (2008, 2010, 2017, 2018 papers) and TRAI chairman. Key the holder when the paper was set and tag it outdated.
- **Match / pairs**: centre–city (RAS 2018, Sachivalayam), head–organisation (Degree Lecturers 2011).
- **Statements**: Chandrayaan-1 and -3, PSLV-C53, PRL exoplanet. Each turns on one wrong date, site or agency (Thumba instead of Sriharikota, Antrix instead of NSIL).
- **NOT-type**: IIRS disciplines and equipment (angiography, LINAC).
- **Chronology**: SITE → STEP → APPLE → IGMDP.
- **Repeats**: Aryabhata ×9, NRSC Hyderabad ×5, IIRS Dehradun ×4, Sriharikota district ×4, IIRS former name ×2, Sarabhai father ×2, ISTRAC MoU ×2.
