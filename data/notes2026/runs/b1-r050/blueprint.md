## 6. Corrections to the old notes
- **G2 Mains 2025 Ikshvaku question:** the old notes said the keyed incorrect statement was "Puranas call them Sri
  Parvathiyas" (key: Andhra Bhrityas). The official final key marks the **Ayodhya-descent** statement incorrect; the
  Sri Parvatiya name is true (UPPCS 2020, NextGen).
- **Denduluru:** old notes said the 2025 key was Vikramendravarma only; the final key accepted **both** Vikramendravarma
  and Madhavavarma; the notes now give Madhava Varma II (NextGen) with the dispute stated.
- **Bodhisri's reign:** Virapurushadatta (14th year), not Ehuvala; the G2 2012 PYQ printed Ehuvala.
- **Damodaravarma** did not perform the Hiranyagarbha (Attivarma did); he was a Buddhist.
- **Nagarjunakonda discoverer** spelled A.R. Saraswati (A. Rangaswami Sarasvati), not "A. Ranganatha".
