## SUB 0 | Ghatikas – Education
[0:0] - **Ghatikas** = **educational institutions** – higher colleges of **Sanskrit and Vedic** learning (G2 1993; NTR Univ. JA) – not ports, tanks, dance centres, viharas or bathing ghats.
[0:1] - **First mention** in Andhra: **Indrabhattaraka Varma's** inscriptions (Ghatika-vapta-punyasanchaya); special Ghatikas at **Amaravati and Vengi**.
[0:2] - **Medium of instruction:** **Sanskrit** – also in the colleges attached to the large temples of South India (G1 PYQ).
[0:3] - **Pallava** educational institutions were also called **Ghatikas** (G2 2012) – e.g. the Kanchi ghatika; not Mathas, Vidyapithas or Gacchas.
[0:4] - **14 vidyas** = 4 Vedas + 6 Vedangas + Mimamsa + Nyaya + Dharmashastra + Puranas.
[0:5] - **Agraharas** to Vedic Brahmins were centres of learning.
[0:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Vishnukundis p.7, p.12, p.15; app notes 1:55 (sub 5)
## SUB 1 | Religion
[1:0] - **Vedic revival:** Madhava Varma II performed **11 Ashvamedhas, 16 Rajasuyas, 1 Purushamedha**.
[1:1] - **Family deity: Sriparvataswami** (Srisaila Mallikarjuna); kings styled "Sriparvataswami-padanudhyata"; titles Paramamaheswara, Parama Brahmanya.
[1:2] - **Ganapati** worship began: **Dantamukha Swami** installed at **Velpuru** in MV II's **33rd** year.
[1:3] TABLE head: Vihara / grant | Donor
  [1:3.0] Paramabhattarika Mahadevi Vihara, Indrapalanagara | Queen of Govinda Varma I; he granted Penkapura and Enmadala
  [1:3.1] Govinda Vihara, Chaitanyapuri (Hyderabad) | Govinda Varma I
  [1:3.2] Village Irundhera to the same vihara | Vikramendra Varma II (Tummalagudem-2)
[1:4] - **Madhava Varma II** = "**destroyer of Buddhism**".
[1:5] - **Jainism declined** as **Kapalika Shaivism** rose; Jain shrines (e.g. Srisailam) converted; **Kumarilabhatta** attacked Jainism.
[1:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Vishnukundis p.6-7, p.9, p.11-12, p.14; app notes 1:55 (sub 2, 3)

## SUB 3 | Coinage and Trade
[3:0] - Metal: **iron with a thin copper coating**; found by **Rayaprolu Subrahmanyam**; **16 types**; symbols **lion**, Nandi, ship, conch.
[3:1] - **Ship-marked coins** + MV II's title **Trisamudradhipati** = importance of **sea trade**; ports Motupalli and Koduru.
[3:2] - **Roman gold coin of Constantine** at **Kudali Sangameswaram** near Alampur; **Fa-hien**: cowrie shells (**gavvalu**) used as money.
[3:3] - **⚠ Trap:** not gold or silver coins.
[3:4] ~~ Sources: NextGen Vishnukundis p.5, p.14; app notes 1:55 (sub 1)
## SUB 4 | Agraharas, Society and Literature
[4:0] TABLE head: Grant | Donee
  [4:0.0] Tundi plates (Vikramendra II) | Scholar Shivavarma
  [4:0.1] Ipur plates | Agnisarma, Easwarasarma
  [4:0.2] Mutukali (Mruthukali) grant | Indravarma, Agnivarma
[4:1] - **Feudalisation view:** tax-free **Brahmadeyas** with ruling rights created a **landed intermediary class**.
[4:2] - Society: **varna rules more rigid**; women's status fell; **child marriage and sati** increased; Vedic-Puranic revival, temple + agrahara economy, first Hindu cave temples – Brahmanical learning flourished.
[4:3] - **Janasraya Chhandovichiti:** Sanskrit work on **prosody**, credited to **Madhava Varma II** or poet **Gunaswami** in his name.
[4:4] - Scholar-kings: Govinda Varma **Shadabhigna**; Madhava Varma II **Vidyadvijaguru**; Vikramendra Varma I **Mahakavi** (Indrapalanagara plates).
[4:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen Vishnukundis p.7, p.12-15; app notes 1:55 (sub 4, 6)
