## SUB 0 | Golden Age of Buddhism – Virapurushadatta
[0:0] - **Golden age of Buddhism in Andhra** = the **Ikshvaku period** (APPSC PYQ) – not the Satavahana, Vishnukundin or Chalukya period.
[0:1] - **Greatest patronage to Buddhism** in the Ikshvaku kingdom: reign of **Virapurushadatta** (G2 2008 ×2; APPSC PYQ) – not Sri Chantamula, Ehuvala Chantamula or Rudrapurushadatta.
[0:2] - **Women patronised Buddhism** most under the Ikshvakus (APPSC PYQ); queens, princesses and rich lay women funded stupas and viharas.
[0:3] - **Upasika Bodhisri** served Buddhism in the reign of **Virapurushadatta** (her Chula-Dhammagiri record is dated in his **14th** regnal year) (G2 2012).
[0:4] - **⚠ Trap:** a G2 2012 answer grid printed **Ehuvala Chantamula** for Bodhisri – wrong; NextGen, Rocket and her own inscription place her under Virapurushadatta.
[0:5] - **Trampled Shivalinga:** a Nagarjunakonda sculpture shows **Mathariputra Virapurushadatta** trampling a Shivalinga – a sign of his turn to Buddhism (APPSC PYQ).
[0:6] TABLE head: King | Religious policy
  [0:6.0] Vasishthiputra Sri Santamula (founder) | Vedic: Ashvamedha, Rajasuya, Vajapeya, Agnistoma; worshipped Virupakshapati (Shiva) and Mahasena (Skanda); agraharas to northern Brahmins
  [0:6.1] Virapurushadatta | Only Ikshvaku king to adopt Buddhism (earlier Vedic; did not wholly drop Brahmanical ties); "Ashoka of the South", Sri Parvatadhipati; rebuilt the Maha Chaitya under Acharya Bhadanta Ananda; set up the Sriparvata University
  [0:6.2] Ehuvala Santamula | Revived Brahmanism; first Hindu temples; first Sanskrit inscriptions; still built a vihara for the Bahushrutiyas in his mother's name
  [0:6.3] Rudrapurushadatta (last) | Vedic; deity Mahasena; custom of chhaya-stambhas (shadow pillars) near graves began
[0:7] - **Puranic Shaivism, Vaishnavism and Shakti worship** all thrived; the Ikshvakus were the **last royal Buddhist zenith** in Andhra.
[0:8] - **Kings** (all except Virapurushadatta) were Vedic; kings were **not** the chief patrons of stupas and viharas.

## SUB 1 | Deities – Hariti and the Dynastic Gods
[1:0] - **Hariti (Harati)** = **tutelary deity** of the **Ikshvakus** of Vijayapuri (G2 2012; APPSC PYQ) – the protector of **children**; women offered bangles for progeny.
[1:1] - Installation of her image = beginning of **Shakti worship**; Hariti was also a mother goddess of the Early Chalukyas.
[1:2] TABLE head: Deity | Dynasty / group
  [1:2.0] Hariti | Ikshvakus (also Early Chalukyas)
  [1:2.1] Sriparvataswamy | Vishnukundins – family deity (identified with Srisaila Mallikarjuna)
  [1:2.2] Chitraratha Swamy (Sun) | Salankayanas – family deity
  [1:2.3] Bappa Bhattaraka | Salankayanas – "regarding the father as God"
  [1:2.4] Vishnu | Salankayana Nandivarman II – land grant in the Pedavegi inscription
  [1:2.5] Kakati | Kakatiyas
  [1:2.6] Virupaksha | Vijayanagara kings (Hampi)
  [1:2.7] Vasavi | Arya Vysya community – seat Penugonda
[1:3] - **Sri Parvataswamy** as tutelary deity = **Vishnukundins** (G2 1993) – not the Ikshvakus, Salankayanas or Anandagotrikas.
[1:4] - **⚠ Trap:** Hariti = Ikshvaku tutelary deity; Virupakshapati and Mahasena = gods the Ikshvaku kings worshipped; Kakati = Kakatiyas.
[1:5] ~~ Sources: Rocket AP History sheet #7 (Gods); NextGen Ikshvakus p.2, p.10; NextGen Vishnukundis p.10; NextGen Previous Papers p.27; app notes 1:52 (sub 2, 8)
## SUB 2 | Monasteries, Sects and Foreign Links
[2:0] TABLE head: Monastery / donor | Sect
  [2:0.0] Maha Chaitya (Chamtisri, Harmyasri) | Aparamahavinaseliya (Aparasaila) – Nagarjunakonda its main centre
  [2:0.1] Vihara of Bhatti Mahadevi (by Ehuvala) | Bahushrutiya
  [2:0.2] Kodabalisri's vihara | Mahishasaka
  [2:0.3] Bodhisri's Sinhala Vihara (Chuladhammagiri) | Mahaviharavasin (Theravada) – Sri Lankan monks
  [2:0.4] Purvasailiya | Amaravati
  [2:0.5] Aparasailiya | Nagarjunakonda
[2:1] - Mahasanghika branches / related sects: Purvasailiya, Aparasailiya, Bahushrutiya, Rajagirika, Siddharthika – **Mahaviharavasin is not one**.
[2:2] - **Acharya Bhadanta Ananda** (Aparamahavinaseliya) knew the **Digha and Majjhima Nikayas** by heart.
[2:3] # Maha Chaitya and Bodhisri
[2:4] - A **Dhatugarbha** stupa over the Buddha's bodily relics: "**Samma Sambudhasa dhatuvara parigahita**".
[2:5] - Earlier **Paravata Mahachaitya** built by **Yajnasri Satakarni** for **Acharya Nagarjuna**; rebuilt by **Chamtisri** with **9 ayaka pillars**.
[2:6] - **Bodhisri's** works: stone mandapa at the eastern gateway of the Maha Chaitya at **Kantakasala (Ghantasala)**; **seven viharas at Papili**.
[2:7] # Foreign links
[2:8] - Bodhisri's inscription names lands that received the Dhamma: **Kashmir, Gandhara, China, Chilata, Tosali, Aparanta, Vanga, Vanavasi, Yavana, Damila, Palura** (not Magadha).
[2:9] - **Ceylonese monks** made Nagarjunakonda their **Hinayana** base; **Mahadeva Bhikshu** took Buddhism from the **Pallava-bhoga** region to Sri Lanka (Mahavamsa).
[2:10] - **Suhrllekha:** the Chinese pilgrim **Itsing** (I-tsing, in India 671–695 CE) saw Indian students memorising Nagarjuna's *Suhrllekha*, a letter to **Yajnasri Satakarni** (G2 2012) – not Fa-hien or Hiuen Tsang.
[2:11] - **Hiuen Tsang (7th c.)** found the **Sriparvata vihara neglected**.
[2:12] ~~ Sources: NextGen Ikshvakus p.2, p.5-6, p.12-14; Rocket AP History sheet #23 (Itsing); app notes 1:52 (sub 3, 4, 5)
## SUB 3 | Brahmanical Temples at Nagarjunakonda
[3:0] - The Ikshvakus built **India's first structural Hindu temples**: **9 Shaiva + 1 Vaishnava** at Nagarjunakonda.
[3:1] - Temples found in excavations: **Harati, Mahajana, Kumara Swami, Pushpabhadra Swami, Ashtabhuja Narayana Swami**; also Nandikeshwara and Navagraha.
[3:2] TABLE head: Temple | Builder / deity
  [3:2.0] Pushpabhadraswami | Started by Virapurushadatta, completed by Ehuvala Santamula; Shiva; Gajaprishtha (elephant-back) plan
  [3:2.1] Sarvadevadhivasam | Commander Elisiri (of Ehuvala); for Kumara Swami (Kartikeya); Sanskrit record
  [3:2.2] Ashtabhuja Narayanaswami | Shivasena, commander of the Abhira king Vasusena; first Vaishnava temple in Andhra
[3:3] - Inscriptions of Vasishthiputra Santamula were found at **Rentala** and Kesanapalli near Nagarjunakonda.
[3:4] ~~ Sources: NextGen Ikshvakus p.2, p.13-14, p.19; Rocket AP History sheet #4 (temples), #7; app notes 1:52 (sub 7)
## SUB 4 | Acharya Nagarjuna and Sriparvata
[4:0] - **Nagarjuna** = **Madhyamika** philosopher; **Sriparvata = Nagarjunakonda / Vijayapuri**, named after him.
[4:1] - **Yajnasri Satakarni** built viharas and chaityas at Sriparvata for him; **Virapurushadatta** set up the **Sriparvata University**.
[4:2] - Sriparvata = "**Dakshina Gaya**", sacred to **Mahayana**, while **Bodh Gaya** is sacred to **Hinayana**.
[4:3] - **Sri Parvataswamy = Buddha:** identified by **N. Venkataramanayya** (G2 2019); do not confuse with **Sriparvataswami**, the Vishnukundin family deity (Srisaila Mallikarjuna).
[4:4] - **In news (May 2025):** Miss World contestants visited **Buddhavanam** next to Nagarjunakonda (Buddhist circuit).
[4:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen Ikshvakus p.13; Rocket AP History sheet #7; NextGen Previous Papers p.13; app notes 1:52 (sub 6, 9)
