## SUB 0 | Salankayanas of Vengi
[0:0] TABLE head: Item | Fact
  [0:0.0] Capital | Vengi (Vengipuram) = Pedavegi, near Eluru (G2 2011 ×3; APPSC PYQ) – not Vijayapuri, Amaravati or Pithunda
  [0:0.1] Name | A gotra name; Salankayana = Nandi / the holy bull (G2 2011 ×2)
  [0:0.2] Emblem | Bull (Nandi) – the dynasty called "Nandi" in ancient Andhra (Sainik Welfare)
  [0:0.3] Founder | Vijaya Devavarma (performed an Ashvamedha, declared independence)
  [0:0.4] Greatest king | Hastivarma – defeated by Samudragupta (Allahabad Pillar)
  [0:0.5] Family deity | Chitraratha Swamy (= Sun); kings were "Chitrarathaswami-padabhakta" (MPDO 1997 ×2; APPSC PYQ)
  [0:0.6] Religion | Saivism (Parama Maheswara) (G2 2011 ×2); later kings also Vaishnava (Parama Bhagavata)
  [0:0.7] Inscriptions | 8: 3 Prakrit, 5 Sanskrit; Sanskrit replaced Prakrit as official language from their time
[0:1] - **Bappa Bhattaraka:** they called themselves devotees of Bappa Bhattaraka – "regarding the **father as God**".
[0:2] - **Pedavegi inscription** of **Nandivarman II**: land grant to Vishnu – first mention of **Vishnu temples** in Andhra; he also issued the Kolleru inscription.
[0:3] - **⚠ Trap:** Chitraratha = Salankayanas; Sriparvataswamy = Vishnukundins; Hariti = Ikshvakus.
[0:4] # Overseas links
[0:5] - **Colonisation abroad in Indo-China and Malaysia (Welfare Officers 2017):** the **Salankayanas** – Ptolemy's "Salankenoi" held the coast west of the Godavari, and in their period Andhra Buddhism spread to **Burma and Cambodia**.
[0:6] - **⚠ Low-certainty fact:** the sources state the Burma–Cambodia spread, not "colonisation" in so many words; Vishnukundins, Brihatphalayanas and Anandagotras are the distractors.
[0:7] - **End:** **Madhava Varma II** (Vishnukundin) conquered Vengi from the Salankayanas.

## SUB 1 | Order of Post-Ikshvaku Dynasties; Brihatphalayanas and Anandagotrikas
[1:0] - **Correct order:** **Ikshvakus → Brihatphalayanas → Salankayanas → Vishnukundins → Eastern Chalukyas** (G2 2008 ×2: a, d, c, b).
[1:1] - After the Ikshvakus, the land **south of the Krishna** went to the **Pallavas**, the land **north of the Krishna** to the **Brihatphalayanas**.
[1:2] TABLE head: Order | Dynasty | Period | Key facts
  [1:2.0] 1 | Ikshvakus | c. 225–325 | Capital Vijayapuri; ended by the Pallavas
  [1:2.1] 2 | Brihatphalayanas | c. 275–325 | Ikshvaku feudatories (Nagarjunakonda inscription); only known king Jayavarma
  [1:2.2] 3 | Salankayanas | c. 300–420 | Capital Vengi (Pedavegi)
  [1:2.3] 4 | Vishnukundins | c. 420–624 | Rose after Samudragupta's southern campaign (c. 358 CE) weakened the Pallavas
  [1:2.4] 5 | Eastern Chalukyas | from 624 | Founded by Kubja Vishnuvardhana at Vengi
[1:3] # Brihatphalayanas
[1:4] - **Kondamudi copper plates** of **Jayavarma** (found near Tenali): the **only** record of the dynasty and the **first copper plate found in Andhra** (APPSC PYQ) – not Ikshvaku, Ananda gotra or Satavahana.
[1:5] - Issued in his **10th** regnal year from **Koduru (Kuduru)**, near Ghantasala / Gudur near Machilipatnam; grant of **Panthur** village to eight Brahmins; text **Prakrit**, seal Sanskrit.
[1:6] - Jayavarma was **Vedic / Shaiva** ("Maheshwara-pada-parigrahita").
[1:7] # Anandagotrikas (Kandara kings)
[1:8] - Capital **Kandarapuram** (Chejerla inscription); former Ikshvaku feudatories; kings **Kandara, Attivarma, Damodaravarma**.
[1:9] - **Hiranyagarbha danam:** **Attivarma (Attivarman)** – Gorantla plates (APPSC PYQ); not Kandara or Damodara.
[1:10] - **Damodaravarma** issued the **Mattepadu plates** and leaned to **Buddhism** ("meditating at the feet of Samyak-Sambuddha").

## SUB 2 | Vishnukundin Rulers and Titles
[2:0] - **Founder:** **Indravarma** – a Vakataka vassal; built **Indrapalanagara**; Ramatirtham plates.

  [2:1.2] 3 | Govinda Varma I | Expanded into the Krishna–Godavari region and both sides of Sriparvata; title Shadabhigna; queen Paramabhattarika's vihara
  [2:1.3] 4 | Madhava Varma II | Greatest ruler; golden age; Vedic revival; conquered the Salankayanas

  [2:1.7] 8 | Manchana Bhattaraka | Last ruler; lost to Pulakeshin II, 624 CE
[2:2] - **Greatest Vishnukundin:** **Madhava Varma II** (G2 1993) – not Vikramendravarma I, Indravarma or "Madhavavarma IV".
[2:3] - **Mahakavi:** **Vikramendra Varma I** (NTR Univ. JA) – not Govinda Varma I, Madhava Varma I or Indravarma I.
[2:4] - Some lists place Deva Varma and Madhava Varma III after MV II; numbering differs between historians.
[2:5] # Madhava Varma II
[2:6] - **Sacrifices:** **11 Ashvamedhas**, **16 Rajasuyas**, **1 Purushamedha**, Hiranyagarbha, Agnistoma.
[2:7] - **Conquests:** Salankayanas (took Vengi); Pallavas (Karma-rashtra up to the Gundlakamma); Vakatakas (married a daughter of **Prithvisena II** – Khanapur plates, Satara); Anandagotrikas (title **Trikuta-Malayadhipati**, Trikuta = **Kotappakonda**).
[2:8] - **Justice legend:** a 12th-c. **Bezawada** inscription – he sentenced **his own son** to death after the prince's chariot killed a poor woman's son.

## SUB 5 | Wars and Decline
[5:0] - **Indrabhattaraka Varma** was defeated by Maukhari **Ishanavarma**, whose son married his daughter (**Jaunpur** inscription); he was **killed in the Kalinga war** by **Prithvimula** of the **Ranadurjayas**.
[5:1] - **End:** **Pulakeshin II** (Badami Chalukya) defeated **Manchana Bhattaraka** at **Kunala (Kolleru)**, **624 CE**; his brother **Kubja Vishnuvardhana** founded the **Eastern Chalukyas of Vengi**.
[5:2] - **Link role:** the Vishnukundins are the bridge between the **Ikshvaku age and the Eastern Chalukyas**, who took over their agrahara system and administration.
[5:3] ~~ Sources: NextGen Vishnukundis p.4, p.12-13; app notes 1:54 (sub 7)
