## SUB 0 | Royal Women Donors
[0:0] - **Dual pattern:** kings were **Vedic**; **royal women** were the main patrons of Buddhism at Nagarjunakonda, giving **without male intervention** – a trend of women endowing Buddhism while the men patronised Hindu cults (Tribal Welfare Officer PYQ: statements 1 and 2 true, "women took no part in donations" false).
[0:1] - **Nagarjunakonda inscriptions** record gifts by the Ikshvaku family both to **Hindu temples** and to **Buddhist monks**.
[0:2] - **Santisri (Chamtisri) and Harmyasri** = **sisters of Vasishthiputra Santamula (Chantamula I)** (Welfare Officers 2017; APPSC PYQ) – not of Virapurushadatta or Ehuvala.

  [0:3.0] Chamtisri (Santisri) | Sister of Santamula; paternal aunt of Virapurushadatta | First woman donor; rebuilt the Maha Chaitya in Virapurushadatta's 6th regnal year; 9 ayaka pillars; 170 dinari-masakas
  [0:3.1] Harmyasri | Sister of Santamula | Pillar for her mother at the Aparamahavinaseliya chaitya; daughter Chathasri also gave
  [0:3.2] Adavi Chamtisri | Sister of Virapurushadatta (daughter of Santamula) | Ayaka pillar, Maha Chaitya
  [0:3.3] Bapisri, Chatisri | Queens of Virapurushadatta (his aunt's daughters) | Pillars at the Mahachaitya
  [0:3.4] Rudradhara Bhattarika | Queen; daughter of Rudrasena of Ujjain | Ayaka pillar
  [0:3.5] Kodabalisri | Daughter of Virapurushadatta | Vihara for Mahishasaka monks
  [0:3.6] Bhatti Mahadevi | Chief queen; mother of Ehuvala | Vihara for the Bahushrutiya

## SUB 1 | Agrarian Economy and Agraharas
[1:0] - **Sri Chantamula (Vasishthiputra Santamula)** tried to make the **intelligentsia partners in agricultural development** (G2 2012) – gave **gold, cows, ploughs and land**; brought **Brahmins from the north** and gave them **agraharas**.
[1:1] - **Agraharas** = grants of land and its royal income, mostly to **Brahmins** (JA Panchayat Raj 2012; NTR Univ. JA) – not to Shudras, Kshatriyas, leaders, goldsmiths or widows.
[1:2] - He performed **Ashvamedha, Vajapeya, Agnistoma** – altar found at **Nagarjunakonda**.
[1:3] - **Revenue split four ways:** administration + army; temples + festivals; poets + scholars; gifts to religious bodies; some on **tanks and canals** – not on overseas colonies.
[1:4] - **Shift:** from the Satavahana **trade economy** to an **agrarian, agrahara-based** early medieval order.
[1:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen Ikshvakus p.1, p.4, p.15; NextGen Previous Papers p.28; app notes 1:51 (sub 2)

## SUB 3 | Society
[3:0] - **Shudras** were the majority; Brahmins rose through **Brahmadeya** grants (exempt from **18 taxes**) – a new **landlord class**.
[3:1] - **Cross-cousin marriage** began with the Ikshvakus; alliances with the **Kshatrapas of Ujjain** and the **Chutus** (Kodabalisri married Vishnurudra Sivalananda Satakarni).
[3:2] - Records show **matronymics** (Vasishthiputra, Mathariputra), naming after the **grandfather** (Ehuvala Santamula) and **sati** (Satisahagamanam).
[3:3] ~~ Sources: NextGen Ikshvakus p.3, p.16, p.19; app notes 1:51 (sub 5)
