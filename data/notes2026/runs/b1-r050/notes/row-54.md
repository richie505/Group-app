=== Salankayanas of Vengi
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Capital | **Vengi (Vengipuram)** = **Pedavegi**, near **Eluru** (G2 2011 ×3; APPSC PYQ) – not Vijayapuri, Amaravati or Pithunda |
| Name | A **gotra** name; Salankayana = **Nandi** / the holy bull (G2 2011 ×2) |
| Emblem | **Bull (Nandi)** – the dynasty called "**Nandi**" in ancient Andhra (Sainik Welfare) |
| Founder | **Vijaya Devavarma** (performed an Ashvamedha, declared independence) |
| Greatest king | **Hastivarma** – defeated by **Samudragupta** (Allahabad Pillar) |
| Family deity | **Chitraratha Swamy** (= Sun); kings were "Chitrarathaswami-padabhakta" (MPDO 1997 ×2; APPSC PYQ) |
| Religion | **Saivism** (Parama Maheswara) (G2 2011 ×2); later kings also Vaishnava (Parama Bhagavata) |
| Inscriptions | **8**: **3 Prakrit, 5 Sanskrit**; Sanskrit replaced Prakrit as official language from their time |
- **Bappa Bhattaraka:** they called themselves devotees of Bappa Bhattaraka – "regarding the **father as God**".
- **Pedavegi inscription** of **Nandivarman II**: land grant to Vishnu – first mention of **Vishnu temples** in Andhra; he also issued the Kolleru inscription.
- **⚠ Trap:** Chitraratha = Salankayanas; Sriparvataswamy = Vishnukundins; Hariti = Ikshvakus.
# Overseas links
- **Colonisation abroad in Indo-China and Malaysia (Welfare Officers 2017):** the **Salankayanas** – Ptolemy's "Salankenoi" held the coast west of the Godavari, and in their period Andhra Buddhism spread to **Burma and Cambodia**.
- **⚠ Low-certainty fact:** the sources state the Burma–Cambodia spread, not "colonisation" in so many words; Vishnukundins, Brihatphalayanas and Anandagotras are the distractors.
- **End:** **Madhava Varma II** (Vishnukundin) conquered Vengi from the Salankayanas.

=== Order of Post-Ikshvaku Dynasties; Brihatphalayanas and Anandagotrikas
- **Correct order:** **Ikshvakus → Brihatphalayanas → Salankayanas → Vishnukundins → Eastern Chalukyas** (G2 2008 ×2: a, d, c, b).
- After the Ikshvakus, the land **south of the Krishna** went to the **Pallavas**, the land **north of the Krishna** to the **Brihatphalayanas**.
| Order | Dynasty | Period | Key facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ikshvakus | c. 225–325 | Capital Vijayapuri; ended by the Pallavas |
| 2 | Brihatphalayanas | c. 275–325 | Ikshvaku feudatories (Nagarjunakonda inscription); only known king **Jayavarma** |
| 3 | Salankayanas | c. 300–420 | Capital Vengi (Pedavegi) |
| 4 | Vishnukundins | c. 420–624 | Rose after Samudragupta's southern campaign (c. 358 CE) weakened the Pallavas |
| 5 | Eastern Chalukyas | from 624 | Founded by Kubja Vishnuvardhana at Vengi |
# Brihatphalayanas
- **Kondamudi copper plates** of **Jayavarma** (found near Tenali): the **only** record of the dynasty and the **first copper plate found in Andhra** (APPSC PYQ) – not Ikshvaku, Ananda gotra or Satavahana.
- Issued in his **10th** regnal year from **Koduru (Kuduru)**, near Ghantasala / Gudur near Machilipatnam; grant of **Panthur** village to eight Brahmins; text **Prakrit**, seal Sanskrit.
- Jayavarma was **Vedic / Shaiva** ("Maheshwara-pada-parigrahita").
# Anandagotrikas (Kandara kings)
- Capital **Kandarapuram** (Chejerla inscription); former Ikshvaku feudatories; kings **Kandara, Attivarma, Damodaravarma**.
- **Hiranyagarbha danam:** **Attivarma (Attivarman)** – Gorantla plates (APPSC PYQ); not Kandara or Damodara.
- **Damodaravarma** issued the **Mattepadu plates** and leaned to **Buddhism** ("meditating at the feet of Samyak-Sambuddha").

=== Vishnukundin Rulers and Titles
- **Founder:** **Indravarma** – a Vakataka vassal; built **Indrapalanagara**; Ramatirtham plates.

|---|---|---|

| 3 | Govinda Varma I | Expanded into the Krishna–Godavari region and both sides of Sriparvata; title **Shadabhigna**; queen Paramabhattarika's vihara |
| 4 | **Madhava Varma II** | **Greatest** ruler; golden age; Vedic revival; conquered the Salankayanas |

| 6 | Indrabhattaraka Varma | **Ghatikas**; title Satyasraya; defeated by Maukhari Ishanavarma |
| 7 | Vikramendra Varma II | Chikkulla, Tundi, Tummalagudem-2 plates; put down revolts |

- **Greatest Vishnukundin:** **Madhava Varma II** (G2 1993) – not Vikramendravarma I, Indravarma or "Madhavavarma IV".
- **Mahakavi:** **Vikramendra Varma I** (NTR Univ. JA) – not Govinda Varma I, Madhava Varma I or Indravarma I.
- Some lists place Deva Varma and Madhava Varma III after MV II; numbering differs between historians.
# Madhava Varma II
- **Sacrifices:** **11 Ashvamedhas**, **16 Rajasuyas**, **1 Purushamedha**, Hiranyagarbha, Agnistoma.
- **Conquests:** Salankayanas (took Vengi); Pallavas (Karma-rashtra up to the Gundlakamma); Vakatakas (married a daughter of **Prithvisena II** – Khanapur plates, Satara); Anandagotrikas (title **Trikuta-Malayadhipati**, Trikuta = **Kotappakonda**).
- **Justice legend:** a 12th-c. **Bezawada** inscription – he sentenced **his own son** to death after the prince's chariot killed a poor woman's son.

=== Capitals and Original Home
| Capital | Where / which ruler |
|---|---|
| **Amarapura (Amarapuri)** | First capital; Munnanuru (**Amrabad**, Nagarkurnool) |
| **Indrapalanagara** (Sukrapuram) | Built by **Indravarma**; at **Tummalagudem** (Nalgonda/Yadadri, Telangana) |
| Bezawada (Vijayawada) | Capital for a time after they gained the Krishna region |
| **Denduluru** (Dendulurapura) | Near Vengi/Pedavegi, Eluru; capital moved here from Amarapuri |
- **Amarapuri → Denduluru:** **Madhava Varma II**, to check constant **Pallava** invasions (NextGen) (G2 Mains 2025 ×2).
- **⚠ Disputed key:** the G2 Mains 2025 **final key accepted both** "Vikramendravarma" and "Madhavavarma"; NextGen and most texts credit Madhava Varma II, while Vikramendravarma II's Chikkulla plates were issued from Denduluru.
- **Origin:** most historians – **Brahmins** of the Vishnukundina gotra (B.V. Krishna Rao); K. Gopalachari – Agnikula Kshatriyas; N. Venkataramanayya – Brahmins with Kshatriya ways.
- **Original home:** **Telangana** – Amrabad, **Keesaragutta** (start as **Vakataka** feudatories), Indrapalanagara; the name is linked with **Vinukonda**.

=== Wars and Decline
- **Indrabhattaraka Varma** was defeated by Maukhari **Ishanavarma**, whose son married his daughter (**Jaunpur** inscription); he was **killed in the Kalinga war** by **Prithvimula** of the **Ranadurjayas**.
- **End:** **Pulakeshin II** (Badami Chalukya) defeated **Manchana Bhattaraka** at **Kunala (Kolleru)**, **624 CE**; his brother **Kubja Vishnuvardhana** founded the **Eastern Chalukyas of Vengi**.
- **Link role:** the Vishnukundins are the bridge between the **Ikshvaku age and the Eastern Chalukyas**, who took over their agrahara system and administration.
