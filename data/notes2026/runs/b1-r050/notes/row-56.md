=== Undavalli and Mogalrajapuram – Which Dynasty
- **Undavalli, Mogalrajapuram and Vijayawada (Indrakeeladri) cave temples** = **Vishnukundins** (G2 2019; G2 Mains 2025; Welfare Officers 2017 ×2; APPSC PYQ ×3) – not Ikshvakus, Salankayanas, Pallavas, Rashtrakutas, Cholas, Chalukyas, Kakatiyas or Reddi kings.
- **Brahmanical (Hindu) caves** of Mogalrajapuram and Undavalli are assigned to the Vishnukundins; **Madhava Varma I** is credited with Undavalli, Bhairavakonda and Mogalrajapuram.
- **Vishnukundins** = **first Hindu cave temples in the Telugu states**; **Guptas** = earliest Hindu cave temples in India (**Udayagiri**, Madhya Pradesh).

|---|---|---|---|

=== Undavalli Caves
- **District:** **Guntur** (Endowment EO 2022) – not Krishna, NTR or Prakasam; the caves face **Vijayawada** across the Krishna (Boilers 2019: "near Vijayawada").
- **Four storeys**, **three main caves**; carved **top-down** – upper levels finely finished, ground floor **crude and unfinished**.
- Centrepiece: **monolithic reclining Vishnu** (**Ananta Padmanabha Swamy**), about 5 m; a Buddha in padmasana was later turned into Vishnu; **Purnakumbha** motif.
- **Changing use:** **Jain abode → Buddhist monks → Hindu temple**.
- **⚠ Trap:** on the **Krishna**, not the Godavari; "built by the Cholas as a Hindu temple, later Buddhist" is false.
- **In news:** AP's two-day **Telugu Mahotsavam** at Undavalli Caves, **25–26 July 2026**.
~~ Sources: NextGen Vishnukundis p.15; app notes 1:56 (sub 1)

=== Mogalrajapuram Caves
| Cave | Feature |
|---|---|
| Caves 1–3 | Sanctums behind a pillared mandapa for the **Trimurti** |
| **Cave 4** | **Durga** cave; **Ardhanarishvara** on the back wall |
| Cave 5 (largest) | **Shiva Tandava Nataraja** |
- **Five caves** in all, in Vijayawada (NTR district); Trimurti figures on the **kapota** (eave) above the pillar capitals.
~~ Sources: NextGen Vishnukundis p.15; app notes 1:56 (sub 2)

=== Bhairavakonda Caves
- **Eight rock-cut caves**, all dedicated to **Shiva** (G2 2012) – not 4, 9 or 12; a centre of **Shaivism** (Kapalika sect).
- **Location:** listed under **Nellore** district in NextGen/Rocket and the G2 2012 stem; on the ground in the **Nallamala hills** on the Nellore–Prakasam border.
- **Attribution debate:** Madhava Varma I (Vishnukundin) vs **Pallava period (7th–8th c.)**; closely resembles the early Pallava caves of **Mahabalipuram**.
- Pillars: **lion-based** with **kumbha (pot) capitals**.
> Number angle: 4 storeys = Undavalli | 5 caves = Mogalrajapuram | 8 caves = Bhairavakonda.
~~ Sources: NextGen Vishnukundis p.9, p.15; Rocket AP History sheet #4, "Art" (Bhairavakonda – eight caves); NextGen Previous Papers p.28; app notes 1:56 (sub 3)

=== Features and the Pallava Comparison
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pillared mandapa | Hall in front of rock-cut garbhagriha cells – the usual Vishnukundin plan |
| Kumbha capitals | Pot capitals on lion-based pillars (Bhairavakonda) |
| **Kapota** | **Decorated eave above capitals** (Mogalrajapuram) |
| Dwarapalas | Door guardians |
- Style: mix of **Ikshvaku and Vakataka** traditions with **Pallava** borrowings; dated **4th–6th c. CE**.
- **Brick structural temples** with garbhagriha, antarala-mandapa, mukhamandapa at **Keesaragutta**.
- **Lost-wax (cire perdue):** early multi-armed metal images credited to the **Vishnukundin** age.
- **Pallava caves** of **Mahendravarman I** (7th c. CE) – lion-based pillars – later taken up by the **Telugu Chodas and Eastern Chalukyas**.
- **⚠ Trap:** the storeyed cave is **Undavalli**, not Bhairavakonda.
~~ Sources: NextGen Vishnukundis p.8, p.15; app notes 1:56 (sub 4, 5)
