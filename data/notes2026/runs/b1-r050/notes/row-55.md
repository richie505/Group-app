=== Ghatikas – Education
- **Ghatikas** = **educational institutions** – higher colleges of **Sanskrit and Vedic** learning (G2 1993; NTR Univ. JA) – not ports, tanks, dance centres, viharas or bathing ghats.
- **First mention** in Andhra: **Indrabhattaraka Varma's** inscriptions (Ghatika-vapta-punyasanchaya); special Ghatikas at **Amaravati and Vengi**.
- **Medium of instruction:** **Sanskrit** – also in the colleges attached to the large temples of South India (G1 PYQ).
- **Pallava** educational institutions were also called **Ghatikas** (G2 2012) – e.g. the Kanchi ghatika; not Mathas, Vidyapithas or Gacchas.
- **14 vidyas** = 4 Vedas + 6 Vedangas + Mimamsa + Nyaya + Dharmashastra + Puranas.
- **Agraharas** to Vedic Brahmins were centres of learning.
~~ Sources: NextGen Vishnukundis p.7, p.12, p.15; app notes 1:55 (sub 5)

=== Religion
- **Vedic revival:** Madhava Varma II performed **11 Ashvamedhas, 16 Rajasuyas, 1 Purushamedha**.
- **Family deity: Sriparvataswami** (Srisaila Mallikarjuna); kings styled "Sriparvataswami-padanudhyata"; titles Paramamaheswara, Parama Brahmanya.
- **Ganapati** worship began: **Dantamukha Swami** installed at **Velpuru** in MV II's **33rd** year.
| Vihara / grant | Donor |
|---|---|
| Paramabhattarika Mahadevi Vihara, Indrapalanagara | Queen of **Govinda Varma I**; he granted Penkapura and Enmadala |
| Govinda Vihara, Chaitanyapuri (Hyderabad) | **Govinda Varma I** |
| Village Irundhera to the same vihara | **Vikramendra Varma II** (Tummalagudem-2) |
- **Madhava Varma II** = "**destroyer of Buddhism**".
- **Jainism declined** as **Kapalika Shaivism** rose; Jain shrines (e.g. Srisailam) converted; **Kumarilabhatta** attacked Jainism.

| Bhaga | Tax of **one-sixth** of produce |

- **King = highest judge**; **official language: Sanskrit**; **Amrabad** = biggest fort in the Telugu country (walls ~200 km).
- **Territorial units:** **Rashtras** and **Vishayas** (e.g. Palki Rashtra, Karma Rashtra, Guddadi Vishaya).
- **Army:** fourfold; **Hastikosa** = elephant forces, Virakosa = land forces.

=== Coinage and Trade
- Metal: **iron with a thin copper coating**; found by **Rayaprolu Subrahmanyam**; **16 types**; symbols **lion**, Nandi, ship, conch.
- **Ship-marked coins** + MV II's title **Trisamudradhipati** = importance of **sea trade**; ports Motupalli and Koduru.
- **Roman gold coin of Constantine** at **Kudali Sangameswaram** near Alampur; **Fa-hien**: cowrie shells (**gavvalu**) used as money.
- **⚠ Trap:** not gold or silver coins.
~~ Sources: NextGen Vishnukundis p.5, p.14; app notes 1:55 (sub 1)

=== Agraharas, Society and Literature
| Grant | Donee |
|---|---|
| Tundi plates (Vikramendra II) | Scholar **Shivavarma** |
| Ipur plates | **Agnisarma, Easwarasarma** |
| Mutukali (Mruthukali) grant | **Indravarma, Agnivarma** |
- **Feudalisation view:** tax-free **Brahmadeyas** with ruling rights created a **landed intermediary class**.
- Society: **varna rules more rigid**; women's status fell; **child marriage and sati** increased; Vedic-Puranic revival, temple + agrahara economy, first Hindu cave temples – Brahmanical learning flourished.
- **Janasraya Chhandovichiti:** Sanskrit work on **prosody**, credited to **Madhava Varma II** or poet **Gunaswami** in his name.
- Scholar-kings: Govinda Varma **Shadabhigna**; Madhava Varma II **Vidyadvijaguru**; Vikramendra Varma I **Mahakavi** (Indrapalanagara plates).
~~ Sources: NextGen Vishnukundis p.7, p.12-15; app notes 1:55 (sub 4, 6)
