=== Distribution of Tax Revenues – Articles 268 to 271 and State Taxes
# Who levies, who collects, who keeps (present position, after the 80th and 101st Amendments)
| Article | Levied by | Collected / kept by | Taxes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **268** | **Union** | **States collect and appropriate** | **Stamp duties** on bills of exchange, cheques, promissory notes, insurance policies, share transfers; proceeds **not part of the CFI** |
| **269** | **Union** | Union collects, **assigned to the States** | Taxes on **inter-State sale or purchase of goods (not newspapers)** and on **inter-State consignment**; net proceeds **do not form part of the CFI** |
| 269A | Union | Shared as Parliament provides on **GST Council** advice | **IGST** on inter-State supplies (101st Amendment) |
| **270** | **Union** | **Shared** with States on **Finance Commission** advice (divisible pool) | All Union List taxes – **income tax (non-agricultural)**, **corporation tax**, customs, CGST … – **except** 268, 269, 269A taxes, **surcharges (271)** and **cesses** |
| **271** | Union | **Union only** – States get **no share** | **Surcharges** on 269 and 270 taxes (not on GST) |
| 268A (omitted) | Union | Union + States | Service tax – inserted by **88th (2003)**, omitted by **101st (2016)** |
| 272 (omitted) | Union | Shared by law | Union excise – omitted by the **80th (2000)** |
- **Income tax:** the Union **levies, collects and distributes** it between itself and the States (Art. 270) – it is neither kept wholly by the Union nor given wholly to the States; only the **surcharge** stays with the Union.
- **Before 2000** (old Art. 269) the Union levied and collected but **assigned to the States** taxes on **railway fares and freights**, **stock-exchange and futures-market transactions** (other than stamp duty), estate and succession duty, newspapers and their advertisements; the **80th Amendment** moved these into the **Art. 270 pool**. Only **income tax** was shared from 1950; **corporation tax and customs** became shareable only from **1996** (80th).
- **Art. 268 before GST** also covered **excise on medicinal and toilet preparations containing alcohol** – levied by the Union, **collected and kept by the States**; the 101st Amendment removed it (now under GST).
# Taxes levied, collected and retained by the States (State List, 18 heads)
- **Land revenue**, **agricultural income tax** (Entry 46 – the **Constitution**, not the Finance Commission, assigns it), duties on succession to agricultural land, taxes on **lands and buildings**, mineral rights.
- **State excise on alcoholic liquor** for human consumption, opium and narcotics; taxes on **sale of petroleum crude, diesel, petrol, natural gas, ATF and liquor** (outside GST – levied **by the States**, not the Centre).
- Electricity, goods and passengers by road/inland waterways, **vehicles**, animals and boats, **tolls**, **professions** (Art. 276), capitation, **entertainment tax by local bodies**, stamp duty on other documents.
- **Profession tax (Art. 276(2)):** the **only tax whose total per person is capped by the Constitution** – **₹2,500 a year** (₹250 till the **60th Amendment, 1988**).
# Local bodies
- **Art. 243H / 243X:** the State Legislature may let Panchayats/Municipalities **levy, collect and appropriate** taxes, duties, tolls and fees, and may **assign** to them taxes collected by the State.
- **Assigned revenues:** taxes **assigned to local bodies by statute but collected by the State** and passed on to the Panchayats.
- **Gram Panchayat taxes:** house tax, **tax/fee on fairs and markets ("fair tax")**, water and lighting rates, vehicle and animal fees – **not** sales tax, customs or excise.
> Asked as: which tax is levied by the Union but collected and kept by the States (stamp duty); levied and collected by the Union but assigned (Art. 269); levied by the Centre and shared (income tax, Art. 270); State-only taxes (land revenue, liquor excise); statements on 269/270/271 (APPSC Group-I 2024); profession tax cap.

=== Finance Commission and Devolution – Article 280
# The body
- **Art. 280:** a **quasi-judicial** body constituted by the **President** every **fifth year** or earlier; chairman + 4 members (Finance Commission Act, 1951); **Art. 281:** its report and the action taken are laid before Parliament.
- Called the **"balancing wheel of fiscal federalism"**; its advice is **not binding**.
# What it recommends (Art. 280(3))
- **Distribution of the net proceeds of taxes** between the Union and the States (**vertical**) and **among the States inter se** (**horizontal distribution**).
- **Principles governing grants-in-aid** to the States out of the **Consolidated Fund of India** (Art. 275).
- Measures to augment a State's Consolidated Fund for **Panchayats and Municipalities** (73rd/74th Amendments), and any matter referred by the President.
- It does **not** attract foreign capital, distribute funds among PSUs or audit financial administration (UPSC 2011 – "none of these").
- Tax sharing is **not** fixed by the Finance Ministry, RBI, NABARD, NITI Aayog, the Inter-State Council, NDC or the GST Council; the **CAG certifies the net proceeds** (Art. 279).
# Shares
| FC | Period | States' share of divisible pool |
|---|---|---|
| 10th | 1995-2000 | **Alternative Scheme of Devolution** – 29% of gross Union taxes (80th Amendment) |
| 14th (Y.V. Reddy) | 2015-20 | **42%** – "compositional shift" from grants to devolution |
| 15th (N.K. Singh) | 2020-26 | **41%** |
| 16th (Arvind Panagariya) | 2026-31 | **41%** retained; State fiscal deficit **3% of GSDP**; end **off-budget borrowing** |
- **Compositional shift:** more tax devolution and fewer conditional grants → **increase in the share of unconditional (untied) transfers** to States and more fiscal autonomy (APPSC Group-I 2017).
- **Cesses and surcharges** (~18% of gross tax) stay outside the pool, so effective devolution is about **31-32%**; southern States' **"Chalo Delhi" (2024)** opposed population-based criteria. Tax devolution 2024-25: **₹12.87 lakh crore**.
> Asked as: who recommends distribution of net tax proceeds / principles of grants-in-aid (Finance Commission – 6 PYQs); horizontal distribution; effect of the compositional shift.

=== GST and the GST Council
# Framework
- **101st Amendment Act, 2016** (the **122nd Amendment Bill**) – GST from **1 July 2017**; **destination-based**; inserted **Arts 246A, 269A, 279A**, omitted 268A; replaced about **17** indirect taxes.
- **Subsumed:** Central excise, service tax, CVD/SAD, **State VAT/sales tax**, **entry tax and octroi**, **entertainment tax (except by local bodies)**, luxury, purchase and lottery taxes.
- **Not subsumed:** **land revenue** (not a tax on supply at all), **State excise on alcohol for human consumption** (liquor is outside GST), stamp duty, electricity duty, basic customs; petroleum products zero-rated till the Council decides.
- **Art. 246A:** Parliament and States both make GST laws; **only Parliament** for **inter-State supply**; **Art. 269A:** IGST, revenue to the **destination State**.
# GST Council – Art. 279A
| Feature | Fact |
|---|---|
| Chair | **Union Finance Minister** |
| Members | Union MoS (Revenue/Finance) + every State's Finance Minister |
| Votes | Centre **1/3**, all States together **2/3**; decision by **3/4** of weighted votes |
| Status | **Mohit Minerals (2022):** recommendations **persuasive, not binding** |
- **53rd GST Council (22 June 2024):** monetary limits for departmental appeals – **₹20 lakh GSTAT, ₹1 crore High Court, ₹2 crore Supreme Court** (CBIC Circular 207/1/2024-GST).
- **GST and federalism:** "**pooled sovereignty**" – Centre and States decide **jointly** in the Council as partners (cooperative federalism); critics note the Centre's effective veto. Compensation cess on luxury/sin goods for **5 years (2017-22)**; **GST 2.0** from **September 2025**.
> Asked as: Amendment number (101st – 2 PYQs); Council chair; tax not subsumed; 2024 Council decisions; GST and Centre-State relations.

=== Grants-in-aid, Plan Transfers and Special Category Status
# Statutory vs discretionary grants
| Feature | Statutory (Art. 275) | Discretionary (Art. 282) |
|---|---|---|
| To whom | States **in need** (not every State); ST welfare, Scheduled Areas | Any State – **any public purpose**, even outside legislative competence |
| Recommended by | **Finance Commission** | Earlier the Planning Commission; now Union ministries |
| Charged on CFI | **Yes** | No |
- Statutory grants are on the **Finance Commission's** advice, **not NITI Aayog's**.
# Plan-transfer formulas
| Formula | Year | Weights (%) |
|---|---|---|
| **Gadgil** (D.R. Gadgil) | **1969**, 4th Plan | Population 60, per-capita income 10, tax effort 10, irrigation/power 10, special problems 10 |
| Gadgil-Mukherjee | 1991, 8th Plan | Population 60, per-capita income 25, performance 7.5, special problems 7.5 |
- **Gadgil formula** = allocation of **Central Plan assistance** to States, i.e. **devolution of funds** – not tax division, borrowing, river waters or LoC. Both ended with the **Planning Commission (2015)**.
# Special Category Status (NDC, 1969)
- Benefits: Central assistance as **90% grant : 10% loan**, **excise-duty and tax concessions** for industry – so "all of these".
- Parameters: **hilly terrain**, **low population density / large tribal share**, **strategic border location**, economic backwardness, **non-viable State finances** – not high per-capita income.
- **AP:** post-2014 demand for SCS (never written into APRA 2014).
> Asked as: Article for grants (275/282); Gadgil formula (2 PYQs); SCS benefits.

=== Borrowing – Articles 292 and 293
| Article | Rule |
|---|---|
| 292 | Union borrows on the security of the **CFI**, within or outside India, within limits fixed by **Parliament** by law |
| **293(1)** | A State borrows **only within India**, on the security of its Consolidated Fund, within limits fixed by the State Legislature – **no direct foreign loans** |
| 293(2) | Union may **lend to States** or **guarantee** their loans |
| 293(3) | A State owing the Union needs the **Union's consent** (with conditions) for a fresh loan |
- **Net Borrowing Ceiling** (~3% of GSDP) is imposed via **293(3)** and counts off-budget borrowing; **Kerala v Union of India (2024)** – Art. 131 suit, referred to a **five-judge bench**.
- AP net borrowings **2024-25: ₹60,485.40 crore**.
> Asked as: can a State take foreign loans directly? – **No** (UPPCS 2012).

=== Amendments, Emergencies and Fiscal Disputes
| Amendment | Year | Change |
|---|---|---|
| 60th | 1988 | Profession tax ceiling ₹250 → **₹2,500** |
| **80th** | 2000 (in force **1 April 1996**) | 10th FC Alternative Scheme – **29%** of gross Union taxes; **omitted Art. 272**; all Union taxes shareable |
| 88th | 2003 | Inserted Art. 268A (service tax) |
| **101st** | 2016 | GST – Arts 246A, 269A, 279A; **omitted 268A** |
- 268A and 272 were **not** omitted by the same Amendment (272 – 80th; 268A – 101st).
- **Art. 354:** in a National Emergency the President may **modify revenue distribution**, till the end of the **financial year** in which the Emergency ends.
- **Art. 360:** Union may direct States on **financial propriety**; State **Money/Financial Bills reserved** for the President.
- **Disputes:** Supreme Court's original jurisdiction (**Art. 131**); matters referred to the **Finance Commission are excluded**.
