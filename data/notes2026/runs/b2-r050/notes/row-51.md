=== Union's Duty to Protect States – Article 355 → 365 → 356
- **Art. 355:** "It shall be the **duty of the Union** to protect every State against **external aggression and internal disturbance** and to ensure that the government of every State is **carried on in accordance with the Constitution**" (UPSC 2003 – asked twice).
  - It is the constitutional basis for **Art. 356** and for Union directions to keep a State's governance constitutional.
  - **IMDT Act, 1983** struck down in **Sarbananda Sonowal v Union of India (2005)** for violating the Art. 355 duty.
- **Art. 365:** where a State **fails to comply with Union directions**, it is **lawful for the President to hold** that its government **cannot be carried on per the Constitution** – a ground for **President's Rule (Art. 356)**, not for sending CRPF, a National Emergency or dissolving the Assembly as such.
- **Art. 256 / 257:** Union directions – to ensure compliance with Union laws, and on **means of communication of national or military importance**, protection of railways.
- **UPSC 2023:** the Union's duty against internal disturbance (true); Art. **22(3)** exempts **preventive detention** from the right to counsel (true); under **POTA, 2002** a confession to a senior police officer **was admissible** (s.32), so statement 3 was false → **only two** correct.
> Asked as: which Article imposes the duty to protect States (355 – 3 PYQs); consequence of defying Union directions (Art. 365 → President's Rule).

=== Emergency and Federal Relations – Articles 250, 353, 354
| Sphere | Effect of a National Emergency | Article |
|---|---|---|
| Legislative | Parliament legislates on **State List**; laws end **6 months** after the Emergency | **250** |
| Executive | Union may direct a State on **any matter**; State governments **not suspended** | **353** |
| Financial | President may cut/cancel transfers till the end of that **financial year** | **354** |
- The federation becomes **quasi-unitary**; Lok Sabha term extendable **1 year at a time**.
- Emergency Articles: 352 / 353 / 354 / **355** / 356 / 357 / 358 / 359 / **360** (Financial Emergency).
