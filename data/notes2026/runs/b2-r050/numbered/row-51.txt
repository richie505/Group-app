## SUB 0 | Union's Duty to Protect States – Article 355 → 365 → 356
[0:0] - **Art. 355:** "It shall be the **duty of the Union** to protect every State against **external aggression and internal disturbance** and to ensure that the government of every State is **carried on in accordance with the Constitution**" (UPSC 2003 – asked twice).
[0:1]   - It is the constitutional basis for **Art. 356** and for Union directions to keep a State's governance constitutional.
[0:2]   - **IMDT Act, 1983** struck down in **Sarbananda Sonowal v Union of India (2005)** for violating the Art. 355 duty.
[0:3] - **Art. 365:** where a State **fails to comply with Union directions**, it is **lawful for the President to hold** that its government **cannot be carried on per the Constitution** – a ground for **President's Rule (Art. 356)**, not for sending CRPF, a National Emergency or dissolving the Assembly as such.
[0:4] - **Art. 256 / 257:** Union directions – to ensure compliance with Union laws, and on **means of communication of national or military importance**, protection of railways.
[0:5] - **UPSC 2023:** the Union's duty against internal disturbance (true); Art. **22(3)** exempts **preventive detention** from the right to counsel (true); under **POTA, 2002** a confession to a senior police officer **was admissible** (s.32), so statement 3 was false → **only two** correct.
[0:6] > Asked as: which Article imposes the duty to protect States (355 – 3 PYQs); consequence of defying Union directions (Art. 365 → President's Rule).
