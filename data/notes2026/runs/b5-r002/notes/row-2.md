=== India's S&T Policies – 1958 to STIP 2020
# The five policies
| Policy | Year | PM | Core thrust |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scientific Policy Resolution (SPR)** – first | **1958** | **Nehru** | "Foster, promote and sustain" science – pure, applied, educational; state-led institution building (national labs, IITs) |
| **Technology Policy Statement (TPS)** | **1983** | **Indira Gandhi** | Technological competence and **self-reliance**, less import dependence |
| **Science and Technology Policy (STP)** | **2003** | **Vajpayee** | Science and technology together; innovation, PPP, IPR, more R&D investment |
| **STI Policy** | **2013** | **Manmohan Singh** | First to add **Innovation**; "STI for the People" |
| **Draft STIP 2020** – fifth | **2020** | **Modi** | Decentralised, **bottom-up**, inclusive design; Atmanirbhar Bharat, open science, equity |
- **Order to remember:** 1958 – 1983 – 2003 – 2013 – 2020 = Nehru – Indira – Vajpayee – Manmohan – Modi.
- **⚠ Trap:** first Science Policy Resolution = **1958**, Technology (Policy) Statement = **1983** – "1958 and 1983 respectively" (TREI-RB 2023); not the other way round, not both in one year.
- **SPR 1958** came during the **Second Five Year Plan** (1956-61).

=== Objectives of Each Policy
# STP 2003
- **Launched:** **3 January 2003** (Indian Science Congress); research, innovation, technology transfer, more R&D investment.
- **Objectives include all four** asked by UPPSC 2008: **optimal utilisation of existing physical and knowledge resources**, **development of innovative technologies**, **systems and technologies for mitigation and management of natural hazards**, and **management of intellectual property** → "All the four".
- **GERD target:** **2% of GDP by the end of the Tenth Plan**.
# STI Policy 2013
- India among the **top five** scientific powers by 2020; publications share **3.5% → over 7%**; about **10 high-impact sectors** for direct STI intervention.
- **"Small idea-small money"**, **Risky Idea Fund**, **Fund for Innovations for Social Inclusion**; GERD to **2%** with private spending matching public.
# Draft STIP 2020
- Vision: among the **top three** scientific superpowers in the coming decade; **double FTE researchers, GERD and private GERD share every 5 years**.
- Proposals: **One Nation, One Subscription**, **STI Development Bank** (corpus for long-term strategic areas), **RIEF**, National STI Observatory, open science (INDSTA archive), National Research Foundation with private/CSR (hybrid) funding.
- **⚠ Trap:** **Atal Innovation Mission** is NOT a STIP 2020 outcome – set up by **NITI Aayog in 2016**. Top five by 2020 = STI 2013 (not STP 2003).
- **ONOS** approved Nov 2024, about **Rs 6,000 crore** (not Rs 60,000 crore).

=== GERD Targets and Advisory Bodies
| Policy | GERD target |
|---|---|
| SPR 1958, TPS 1983 | No numeric target |
| STP 2003 | 2% of GDP by end of Tenth Plan |
| STI 2013 | 2%, private matching public |
| STIP 2020 | Double GERD every 5 years |
- **Reality:** **0.84% of GDP in FY 2023-24**, a 15-year high (earlier ~0.64-0.65%); the 2% goal missed under every policy.
- **24 May 2026** recommendations: GERD 2% in 4-5 years via private sector; **NISPG**; single fellowship **"Vigyan Nidhi"**.
| Body | Fact |
|---|---|
| PSA | Office **1999**; first PSA **A.P.J. Abdul Kalam** |
| PM-STIAC | **2018**; chaired by the **PSA** |
| DST / DSIR / DBT | **1971** / **1985** (parent of CSIR, 1942) / **1986** |
| ANRF | ANRF Act 2023; **PM = President**; S&T and Education Ministers = Vice-Presidents; **subsumed SERB** |
- **ANRF:** **Rs 50,000 crore (2023-28)**, about Rs 36,000 crore from non-government sources.
- **RDI Fund:** **Rs 1 lakh crore** for private R&D, approved **1 July 2025**, via ANRF; **DST nodal**.
- **NITI Aayog:** nodal for the GII rank; publishes the **India Innovation Index**; hosts AIM.
- **DRDO** (1958) was formed by merging the **TDE, DTDP and the Defence Science Organisation**.

- **Scientific Social Responsibility (SSR) Guidelines 2022:** released by **DST, Ministry of Science and Technology**, on National Technology Day, **11 May 2022** (with the SRIMAN guidelines); scientists' voluntary engagement with society through anchor institutions.

=== Innovation and Research Concepts
- **Adopters of an innovation (Rogers' diffusion of innovations):** **Innovators → Early Adopters → Early Majority → Late Majority → Laggards** (APPSC G2).
- **Validity of a study:** it measures what it claims to measure; **external validity** = the results can be **generalised** beyond the sample (APPSC G2 answer: "results can be generalised").
- **Decade of Innovation: 2010-2020**; vehicle = **National Innovation Council** under **Sam Pitroda**; inclusive innovation.

=== Andhra Pradesh – S&T Administration
- **APCOST** = AP Council of Science and Technology, under AP's **Environment, Forests, Science and Technology (EFS&T) Department**.
- **State symbols (2018):** notified by the **EFS&T Department**, four years after bifurcation – not General Administration.
- **State Councils of S&T** (such as APCOST): frame state S&T policy, give research grants and popularise science.
- **Jul 2026:** DST-STI Hub at **SPMVV, Tirupati** – sericulture, agriculture, livelihoods for SC/ST women (3 years).
