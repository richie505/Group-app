- Published by **WIPO** (est. **1967**, **Geneva**, UN specialised agency; 1883/1886 Paris/Berne conventions, 1893 = predecessor bureau BIRPI).
- **7 pillars:** 5 input (institutions, human capital and research, infrastructure, market sophistication, business sophistication) + 2 output (knowledge-technology, creative).

=== Patents, Copyright and IPR Law
| Act / policy | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Patents Act, 1970** (amended 2005 for TRIPS) | Protection **20 years** from filing; novel, inventive, industrially applicable |
| **Copyright Act, 1957** | Published book: **lifetime of the author + 60 years** |
| Trade Marks Act, 1999 | 10 years, renewable |
| National IPR Policy, 2016 | **"Creative India; Innovative India"** |
| SIPP 2016 | Free start-up IP filing facilitation |
- **Not patentable in India:** **plants and animals** (except micro-organisms), **plant varieties** and **essentially biological processes** for producing plants or seeds (Sec. 3(j)); plant varieties are protected instead by the **PPV&FR Act, 2001**.
- **IPAB** (Intellectual Property Appellate Board): set up **15 Sept 2003**; heard patent appeals from 2 April 2007; **abolished in April 2021** (Tribunals Reforms Ordinance/Act 2021) – appeals now go to the **High Courts**.
- **CSIR** = the Indian institution with the most patents – it has filed more patents than all individual Indian patentees put together (APPSC G2 2003).
- **Resident vs non-resident filings:** in **January-March 2022**, Indian applicants filed **10,706** applications against **9,090** by foreign applicants – first time in 11 years.

=== GERD and Who Spends on R&D
| Country | GERD (% of GDP) |
|---|---|
| India | **0.84% (FY 2023-24)** – 15-year high, ending a decade-long fall |
| USA | about 3.5% |
| China | about 2.4% |
| Israel | about 6.3% (2023) – world's highest (PMF Lens gives ~5%) |
| South Korea | about 5% (2023) – 2nd |
- **Earlier:** **0.64-0.65%** (2020-21). National target: **2% of GDP**.
- **Total R&D funding:** Rs 1.33 lakh crore (FY20) → **Rs 2.45 lakh crore** (FY24).
- **FY 2023-24:** private industry share **51.8%** – passed public funding **for the first time**; 2020-21 private only **~36%** (USA ~70%, South Korea ~76%).
- **Agency shares of central R&D (2020-21):** **DOS 18.4%**, ICAR 12.4%, DAE 11.4%, CSIR 8.2%, DST 6.8%; **DRDO** long the largest (31.6% in 2017-18).
- **Why private R&D lags:** risk aversion, weak lab-industry links, **shortage of deep-tech capital**.

=== Start-ups and R&D Funding Reforms
- **Startup India – 16 January 2016:** Fund of Funds Rs 10,000 crore, **3-year tax exemption**, exit in **90 days** (not 30), self-certification.
- India = **3rd-largest start-up hub**; **1.59 lakh** DPIIT-recognised start-ups (Sep 2025); unicorns: India **4th with 61** (Hurun Global Unicorn Index, Jun 2026).
- **Support:** **AIM** (Atal Tinkering Labs, incubators); **iDEX** with India-Korea **KIND-X**; **MANAGE, Hyderabad** – agri-start-up incubation.
- **ANRF Act 2023:** funds **basic and applied** research; ~80% of funds went to IITs (Aug 2026 critique).
- **RDI Scheme – 1 July 2025:** **Rs 1 lakh crore**; custodian **ANRF**; private R&D in AI, quantum, biotech, energy.
