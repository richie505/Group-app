## SUB 1 | Patents, Copyright and IPR Law
[1:0] TABLE head: Act / policy | Provision
  [1:0.0] Patents Act, 1970 (amended 2005 for TRIPS) | Protection 20 years from filing; novel, inventive, industrially applicable
  [1:0.1] Copyright Act, 1957 | Published book: lifetime of the author + 60 years
  [1:0.2] Trade Marks Act, 1999 | 10 years, renewable
  [1:0.3] National IPR Policy, 2016 | "Creative India; Innovative India"
  [1:0.4] SIPP 2016 | Free start-up IP filing facilitation
[1:1] - **Not patentable in India:** **plants and animals** (except micro-organisms), **plant varieties** and **essentially biological processes** for producing plants or seeds (Sec. 3(j)); plant varieties are protected instead by the **PPV&FR Act, 2001**.
[1:2] - **IPAB** (Intellectual Property Appellate Board): set up **15 Sept 2003**; heard patent appeals from 2 April 2007; **abolished in April 2021** (Tribunals Reforms Ordinance/Act 2021) – appeals now go to the **High Courts**.
[1:3] - **CSIR** = the Indian institution with the most patents – it has filed more patents than all individual Indian patentees put together (APPSC G2 2003).
[1:4] - **Resident vs non-resident filings:** in **January-March 2022**, Indian applicants filed **10,706** applications against **9,090** by foreign applicants – first time in 11 years.

[4:0] - **Startup India – 16 January 2016:** Fund of Funds Rs 10,000 crore, **3-year tax exemption**, exit in **90 days** (not 30), self-certification.
