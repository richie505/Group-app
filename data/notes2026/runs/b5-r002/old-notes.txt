=== NOTES 5:2 | Science, Technology and Innovation Policy - 1958 to Stip 2020
## SUB 0 | Policy Milestones
[2:0:0] TABLE head: Policy | PM | Core thrust
  [2:0:0.0] Scientific Policy Resolution 1958 (first) | Nehru | "Foster, promote and sustain" science - pure, applied, educational; national labs
  [2:0:0.1] Technology Policy Statement 1983 | Indira Gandhi | Technological competence, self-reliance, less import dependence
  [2:0:0.2] S&T Policy 2003 | Vajpayee | Science and technology together; R&D investment
  [2:0:0.3] STI Policy 2013 | Manmohan Singh | Added Innovation; "STI for the People"
  [2:0:0.4] Draft STIP 2020 (fifth) | Modi | Decentralised, bottom-up; Atmanirbhar Bharat, open science, equity
[2:0:1] 1958 - 1983 - 2003 - 2013 - 2020 = Nehru - Indira - Vajpayee - Manmohan - Modi.
[2:0:2] - **SPR 1958** came during the **Second Five Year Plan** (1956-61).
## SUB 1 | Objectives of Each Policy
[2:1:0] - **STP 2003**: launched **3 January 2003**; research, innovation, technology transfer, **optimal use of existing resources** (all four listed objectives correct).
[2:1:1] - **STI 2013**: India in **top five** scientific powers by 2020; publications share **3.5% to over 7%**; "small idea-small money", **Risky Idea Fund**, Fund for Innovations for Social Inclusion; ~10 high-impact sectors.
[2:1:2] - **Draft STIP 2020**: **top three** scientific superpowers in the coming decade; double FTE researchers, GERD and private GERD share every 5 years; **One Nation, One Subscription**; **STI Development Bank**; **RIEF**; National Research Foundation with private and CSR money.
[2:1:3] **Atal Innovation Mission** is NOT a STIP 2020 outcome - set up by **NITI Aayog in 2016**. Top five by 2020 = STI 2013 (not STP 2003).
[2:1:4] - **ONOS** approved Nov 2024, about **Rs 6,000 crore** (not Rs 60,000 crore).
## SUB 2 | Gerd Targets and R&d Spending
[2:2:0] TABLE head: Policy | GERD target
  [2:2:0.0] SPR 1958, TPS 1983 | No numeric target
  [2:2:0.1] STP 2003 | 2% of GDP by end of Tenth Plan
  [2:2:0.2] STI 2013 | 2%, private matching public
  [2:2:0.3] STIP 2020 | Double GERD every 5 years
[2:2:1] - Reality: **0.84% of GDP in FY 2023-24**, a 15-year high (earlier ~0.64-0.65%). The 2% goal has been missed under every policy.
[2:2:2] - **24 May 2026** recommendations: GERD 2% in 4-5 years via private sector; **NISPG**; single fellowship **"Vigyan Nidhi"**.
## SUB 3 | Implementation and Advisory Bodies
[2:3:0] TABLE head: Body | Fact
  [2:3:0.0] PSA | Office 1999; first PSA A.P.J. Abdul Kalam
  [2:3:0.1] PM-STIAC | 2018; chaired by PSA
  [2:3:0.2] DST / DSIR / DBT | 1971 / 1985 (parent of CSIR, 1942) / 1986
  [2:3:0.3] ANRF | ANRF Act 2023; PM = President; S&T and Education Ministers = Vice-Presidents; subsumed SERB
[2:3:1] - ANRF: **Rs 50,000 crore (2023-28)**, ~Rs 36,000 crore from non-government sources.
[2:3:2] - **RDI Fund**: **Rs 1 lakh crore** for private R&D, approved **1 July 2025**, via ANRF; DST nodal.
[2:3:3] - **NITI Aayog**: nodal for GII rank; **India Innovation Index**; hosts AIM.
## SUB 4 | Decade of Innovation
[2:4:0] - **2010-2020**; vehicle: **National Innovation Council** under **Sam Pitroda**; inclusive innovation.
## SUB 5 | Dst Schemes
[2:5:0] TABLE head: Scheme | Purpose / target
  [2:5:0.0] SATHI | Shared high-end analytical instruments (2020)
  [2:5:0.1] SAIF | Regional instrument facilities
  [2:5:0.2] FIST | Upgrades S&T infrastructure in universities
  [2:5:0.3] INSPIRE | Young talent, school to PhD
  [2:5:0.4] PURSE | University grants based on publications
  [2:5:0.5] Vigyan Jyoti | Meritorious girls of Classes 9-12 into STEM
  [2:5:0.6] WISE-KIRAN | Women in S&T R&D; Women Scientists Scheme for career-break women
  [2:5:0.7] GATI | Gender Advancement for Transforming Institutions
[2:5:1] - **Full forms:** **SATHI** = Sophisticated Analytical and Technical Help Institutes; **PURSE** = Promotion of University Research and Scientific Excellence (both DST).
[2:5:2] - **INSPIRE** = **Innovation in Science Pursuit for Inspired Research**: a continuum from school awards to PhD fellowships and faculty posts.
## SUB 6 | Science Days and Innovation Ranking
[2:6:0] - **National Science Day - 28 February**: **Raman Effect** (1928); 2026 theme "Women in Science: Catalyzing Viksit Bharat".
[2:6:1] - **National Technology Day - 11 May**: **Pokhran-II** (1998); declared 1999.
[2:6:2] - **GII 2025**: India **38th of 139** (39th in 2024).
## SUB 7 | State Level - Apcost
[2:7:0] - **APCOST** = AP Council of Science and Technology, under AP's **Environment, Forests, Science and Technology** department.
[2:7:1] - **Jul 2026**: DST-STI Hub at **SPMVV, Tirupati** - sericulture, agriculture, livelihoods for SC/ST women (3 years).
[2:7:2] - **State Councils of S&T** (such as APCOST): frame state S&T policy, give research grants and popularise science.
