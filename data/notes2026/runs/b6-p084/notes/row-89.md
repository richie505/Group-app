=== Development and Hunger Frameworks
- **HDI**: conceived by **Mahbub ul Haq** with **Amartya Sen**; UNDP HDR since **1990**; income = **GNI per capita (PPP)**; dimensions combined by a **geometric mean**.
- **HDR 2025**: **Sri Lanka** (high human development) is the only offered neighbour well above India (130th, medium); Bangladesh, Nepal, Pakistan rank lower.
- **GHI**: undernourishment and under-5 mortality **1/3** each; stunting and wasting **1/6** each.
- **Human Capital Index (World Bank)**: **survival, quality-adjusted schooling, health** (not GNI).
~~ Sources: Rocket CA 2026 Part 02 p.85 (HDR 2025 India 130); app notes 6:89 (sub 2)
