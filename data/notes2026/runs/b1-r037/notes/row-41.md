=== Dynastic Succession – Mauryas to Satavahanas
| Dynasty | Period | Founder | End |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maurya | c. 321–185 BCE | Chandragupta | **Brihadratha**, the last Maurya, killed by **Pushyamitra** |
| Sunga | c. 185–73 BCE (NextGen: from 187 BC) | **Pushyamitra** | **Devabhuti** killed by his Brahmana minister (Amatya) **Vasudeva Kanva** |
| Kanva | c. 73–28 BCE, 4 kings | Vasudeva Kanva | **Susharman** overthrown by the **Andhras (Satavahanas)** – Simuka |
- **After the Sungas came the Kanvas** (State PSC); both were **Brahmins**.
- **UPSC 2003:** all three statements correct – Brihadratha killed by Pushyamitra; Devabhuti killed by Vasudeva Kanva; the last Kanva deposed by the Andhras.
- **Most powerful dynasty in the south after the Mauryas:** the **Satavahanas** (UPPCS 1993); the Puranas say they defeated the Sungas and Kanvas; **Satakarni I** is said to have defeated Pushyamitra.
- **Magadhan order:** **Haryanka (544–412 BC) → Shishunaga → Nanda (344–321 BC) → Maurya → Sunga → Kanva** (CGPSC 2008: Haryanka, Nanda, Maurya, Sunga); sequence Maurya – Sunga – Kanva – **Kushana**.
| Founder | Replaced | Dynasty ended |
|---|---|---|
| Pushyamitra | Brihadratha | Maurya |
| Vasudeva Kanva | Devabhuti | Sunga |
| Simuka (Sindhuk, Shiprak) | Susharma(n) | Kanva |
| Mahapadma Nanda | last Shishunaga | Shishunaga |
- **Mahapadma Nanda** (Ugrasena): **Sarvakshatrantaka** and Ekarat in the Puranas.

=== Pushyamitra Sunga – Coup, Ashvamedhas and Buddhism
- **Mauryan commander-in-chief (senani)**, a **Brahmin**; killed Brihadratha while showing him the army (Harshacharita) – the last Mauryan king was killed by **Pushyamitra Shunga** (APPSC 2023), not Dattamitra, Vibhuti or Indrabhattarika.
- **Ancestors of the Sungas came from Ujjain** (MPPSC 2018); heartland **Magadha, Malwa–Vidisha, Narmada valley**.
- **Two Ashvamedhas** – recorded in the **Ayodhya inscription of Dhanadeva** (UPPCS 2018) – not Sarnath, Besnagar or Hathigumpha; not three.
- **Wars:** beat the Indo-Greeks (Demetrius), fought the Deccan powers and **Kharavela** of Kalinga.
- **Buddhism:** Pushyamitra **did not contribute to the expansion of Buddhism** (CGPSC 2018) – unlike Ashoka, Kanishka and Harsha; the **Divyavadana** and **Taranatha** call him a persecutor, yet Bharhut and Sanchi were renovated under him (Romila Thapar, Lamotte: exaggerated). Hostility was probably aimed at monasteries allied with the **Indo-Greeks**.
- **Bodhi Tree:** tradition links its destruction to Pushyamitra and later **Shashanka of Gauda**.
- **Society:** feudatories rose – **Bhojas, Maharathis, Rastrikas**; classical **Sanskrit** revived; **Manusmriti** took shape, classing foreigners as **Vratya Kshatriyas**; Brahmanical revival with the rise of Bhagavatism and stone Buddhist art.

=== Literary Sources – Malavikagnimitram, Mahabhashya, Gargi Samhita
| Source | Author | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| **Gargi Samhita** (Yuga Purana) | Garga | Yavana march on Saketa, Panchala, Mathura, Pataliputra |
| **Mahabhashya** | **Patanjali** | Yavana siege of **Saketa** and **Madhyamika**; sacrifices for Pushyamitra |
| **Malavikagnimitram** | **Kalidasa** | Love story of **Agnimitra** (son of Pushyamitra) and Malavika; **Vasumitra** beats the Yavanas on the Sindhu; war with Vidarbha |
| Divyavadana | Buddhist text | Pushyamitra as persecutor |
| Harshacharita | **Banabhatta** | Brihadratha killed at the army review |
- **Yavana invasion under Pushyamitra** is in Gargi Samhita, Mahabhashya and Malavikagnimitram – **NOT Visakhadatta's Mudrarakshasa**, which tells **Chandragupta Maurya's** rise (APPSC G1 2023).
- **Malavikagnimitram:** the book with the love story of the **son of the Sunga founder** (UPSC 2016) – protagonist **Agnimitra** (UPPCS 1998), not Pushyamitra; Kalidasa's **first play**, **5 acts**; not Swapnavasavadatta, Meghaduta or Ratnavali. Mrichchhakatikam is by **Sudraka**.
- **Patanjali** (Mahabhashya) was the **contemporary** and, it is believed, **royal priest** of **Pushyamitra Sunga** (UPPCS 2011) – not of Chandragupta Maurya, Ashoka or Chandragupta I; he explained **Panini's Ashtadhyayi** through **Katyayana's Varttikas**. Panini is earlier (not Pushyamitra's).
| Sunga ruler | Fact |
|---|---|
| Pushyamitra | Founder |
| **Agnimitra** | Son; viceroy at **Vidisha**; fought **Yajnasena** of Vidarbha |
| **Vasumitra** | Grandson; defeated the Yavanas |
| Bhagabhadra | Heliodorus came to his court |
| Devabhuti | Last Sunga |

=== Besnagar (Heliodorus) Pillar
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Donor | **Heliodorus**, a Yavana from **Taxila**, calling himself a **Bhagavata** |
| Envoy of | Indo-Greek king **Antialcidas** |
| At the court of | Sunga king **Bhagabhadra** |
| Site | **Besnagar (Vidisha)**, MP |
| Nature | **Garuda-dhvaja** of **Vasudeva** only (IAS 1998) – not Sankarshana or Pradyumna |
| Language/script | Prakrit / Brahmi |
- **Earliest inscriptional evidence of the Bhagavata religion** (UPPSC Mains 2008) – earlier than the Allahabad, Bhitari or Mehrauli inscriptions; shows a foreigner absorbed into an Indian religion.
- **Not Gupta:** the Besnagar pillar (2nd c. BCE) is the item NOT related to Gupta art, unlike the Sarnath Buddha images, Deogarh temple and the Nalanda copper Buddha.
~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval 03 p.5; PMF Art & Culture 04 p.29; app notes 1:41 (sub 6)

=== Sunga Art – Bharhut and Sanchi
| Site | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Bharhut** (Satna, MP) | **Fine example of Sunga architecture** (RPSC 2023); railings and gateways of early narrative art; inscriptions name guilds (ivory workers, stone carvers) as donors |
| **Sanchi** | Ashoka's brick stupa enlarged and cased in stone; railings (vedika) and toranas; gateway shows **Ashoka's pilgrimage to the Bodhi tree with his two queens** (MPPSC 2022) |
| Bodh Gaya | Early stone railing |
| Mathura | The **Mathura** school of sculpture began under the Sungas |
- **Transition** from wood/ivory to stone; Sanchi's south torana carries an inscription of **Ananda** linking it to **Satakarni II**, so the gateways are often dated Satavahana.
- **Gupta temple architecture did not begin under the Sungas.**
