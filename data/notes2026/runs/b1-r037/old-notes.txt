=== NOTES 1:37 | Mauryan Administration – Central, Provincial, Local; Espionage, Judiciary, Revenue
## SUB 0 | Nature of the Mauryan State
[37:0:0] - Highly centralised "**paternal despotism**" – king heads executive, legislature and judiciary; the **first centralised bureaucratic empire** (census, standardised measures).
[37:0:1] - Checks on the king: **Mantriparishad**, paura-janapada assemblies, public opinion – Kautilya: "**a chariot cannot move on one wheel**".
[37:0:2] - The Arthashastra describes an ideal; how fully it worked is disputed.
## SUB 1 | Saptanga Theory – Seven Limbs in Order
[37:1:0] TABLE head: No. | Element | Meaning
  [37:1:0.0] 1 | Swami | King (comes first)
  [37:1:0.1] 2 | Amatya | Ministers
  [37:1:0.2] 3 | Janapada | Territory and people
  [37:1:0.3] 4 | Durga | Fort
  [37:1:0.4] 5 | Kosha | Treasury
  [37:1:0.5] 6 | Danda / Bala | Army
  [37:1:0.6] 7 | Mitra | Ally – "the ear of the state"
[37:1:1] - Allies help in peace and war; Kautilya separates true from fake allies; natural allies are better than artificial ones.
[37:1:2] **Shatru (enemy)** is NOT one of the standard seven; some lists add it only as an eighth.

## SUB 6 | Megasthenes – City and Army Boards
[37:6:0] - **Agronomoi** = district officers (roads, irrigation, land measurement); **Astynomoi** = city officers, the municipal boards.
[37:6:1] - Pataliputra: **30 members in 6 committees of 5**.
[37:6:2] TABLE head: Committee | Function
  [37:6:2.0] 1 | Crafts and industries
  [37:6:2.1] 2 | Foreigners
  [37:6:2.2] 3 | Births and deaths (census)
  [37:6:2.3] 4 | Trade, prices, weights and measures
  [37:6:2.4] 5 | Sale of manufactured goods
  [37:6:2.5] 6 | Tax on sales (one-tenth), incl. imports and exports – NOT land revenue
[37:6:3] - Army: also **6 boards of 5** – infantry, cavalry, chariots, elephants, navy, transport/commissariat. Chandragupta's army: **6,00,000 foot, 30,000 cavalry**.
[37:6:4] Registration of births and deaths is municipal work unbroken since Mauryan times.

## SUB 8 | Judiciary
[37:8:0] TABLE head: Court | Cases | Presided by
  [37:8:0.0] Dharmasthiya | Civil – marriage, contract, inheritance | 3 dharmasthas
  [37:8:0.1] Kantakashodhana ("removal of thorns") | Criminal | 3 pradeshtris
[37:8:1] - **Pradvivaka**: chief judge / legal adviser (APPSC G2 2019).
[37:8:2] - Ladder: village court → **Sangrahana** → Dronamukha → Janapada → **king** (supreme judge).
[37:8:3] - Punishments: fines, maiming, death; harshness varied by **varna**. Megasthenes: **no written code**.
[37:8:4] - Civic penalty: **Pankodakasannirodhe** – fine for slushing the road (dirt, water logging).
## SUB 9 | Revenue System
[37:9:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [37:9:0.0] Sita | Income from crown land
  [37:9:0.1] Bhaga | King's share of produce, normally 1/6 (Megasthenes: 1/4 to 1/6)
  [37:9:0.2] Bali | Additional levy / tribute
  [37:9:0.3] Pindakara | Tax on groups of villages
  [37:9:0.4] Pranaya | Emergency tax ("gift of affection")
  [37:9:0.5] Vivita | Pasture land dues
  [37:9:0.6] Hiranya | Tax only in cash
  [37:9:0.7] Setu | Gardens, orchards, root crops
  [37:9:0.8] Vana | Forests
[37:9:1] - Land revenue was the main income; Megasthenes: **king owned all land**, officials collected directly.
[37:9:2] - Other income: ferries, mines, pastures, licences, fines. Lumbini: exempt from bali, bhaga cut to **1/8**.
[37:9:3] - **Sales tax evasion: death** (Megasthenes).

## SUB 0 | Economy and State Control
[38:0:0] - **Sita** = crown land farmed directly for the state under the **Sitadhyaksha**, using **slaves and hired labour** (not only free peasants).
[38:0:1] - **Bhaga** (land revenue) = **1/4 to 1/6** (Megasthenes), collected by royal officials; **Kara** = tax on orchards.
[38:0:2] - Rainfall measured in **drona** near granaries (records predate Korea's Cheugugi gauge, 1441 CE).
[38:0:3] - Famine relief: seed, cattle, tools.
[38:0:4] - **State monopoly/control**: mining, forestry, salt, liquor, arms, metallurgy.
[38:0:5] - Interest **15% a year** (Arthashastra); debt relief for students, poor, sick.
[38:0:6] - Pottery: **NBPW** (Northern Black Polished Ware).
## SUB 1 | Guilds, Coins and Currency
[38:1:0] - **Shrenis** (guilds), merchants (shreshthis) and artisans funded early Buddhist centres, including in **Andhra**; Arthashastra counts **shreni troops** among army types.

## SUB 3 | Society – Megasthenes and the Arthashastra
[38:3:0] # Megasthenes' Indica
[38:3:1] - Megasthenes, Greek envoy of **Seleucus**, wrote **Indica** (Greek).
[38:3:2] - **Seven classes** (BPSC 2017): philosophers, farmers, herdsmen/hunters, artisans and traders, soldiers, overseers/spies, councillors and assessors.
[38:3:3] - Hereditary jobs, endogamy; **no slavery**; no written law; two types of philosophers – **Brahmins and Shramanas**.
[38:3:4] # Slavery and Labour
[38:3:5] - Kautilya: **nine categories of slaves (dasas)**; Ashokan inscriptions also mention dasas.
[38:3:6] - Sources: birth, sale, war capture, judicial punishment. **No Arya could be made a slave**.
[38:3:7] - Female slave who bore her master a son → **free**; son gets the master's son's legal status (UPSC 2022).
[38:3:8] - Penalty for selling a pregnant slave without maternity care; freedom by payment.

[38:3:11] # Women, Caste and Learning
[38:3:12] - Divorce and **remarriage** allowed to a wife **deserted** by her husband; also for long stay abroad, physical handicap, mutual enmity. **Widow remarriage** allowed.
[38:3:13] - **Antyavasayins** = five mixed castes outside Aryan society; **Sishtas** = learned men; Shudras could own land; **Chandalas, Shvapakas** grew.
[38:3:14] - Centres of learning: **Taxila** (most famous), Ujjain, Varanasi.

  [39:0:0.1] Ashoka's pacifism | H.C. Raychaudhuri | Non-violence after Kalinga dulled the army's spirit
  [39:0:0.2] Financial crisis | D.D. Kosambi | Army, bureaucracy and Dhamma spending emptied the treasury

[39:0:4] - **Debasement of later punch-marked silver coins** (more alloy).

[39:0:8] - **Heavy taxation:** Kosambi also points to very heavy taxes, raised even on actors and prostitutes, as a sign of the money crisis of the later Mauryas.
