=== Bhagavatism and Vaishnavism
# Origin
- **First worshippers of Vasudeva-Krishna:** the **Bhagavatas** (Vasudevakas) (UPPCS 1997) – not Vedic Aryans, Tamils or Abhiras; Vasudeva worship is known from **Panini's** age.
- **Originator:** **Krishna (Vasudeva)** of the **Vrishni (Satvata)** clan, son of Devaki, pupil of Ghora Angirasa (Chhandogya Upanishad) (RAS 1993) – not Janaka, Yajnavalkya or Surdas.
- **Earliest epigraphic evidence:** the **Garuda pillar at Besnagar** (Vidisha, c. 2nd c. BCE), set up to Vasudeva by **Heliodorus**, envoy of the Indo-Greek **Antialcidas** at the court of the Sunga **Bhagabhadra** (UKPSC 2012 ×2) – the Prayag prashasti, Nasik and Ayodhya records are later.
- **Doctrine:** **bhakti**, **ahimsa**, the **avatara** theory – **ten incarnations**; open to women, Vaishyas, Shudras and foreigners.
# Gupta patronage
- **Greatest contribution to the Bhagavata sect:** the **Guptas** (State PSC) – virtually the state religion; rulers styled **Parama Bhagavata** (first: **Samudragupta**); dynastic emblem **Garuda**.
- **Udayagiri (MP):** colossal **Varaha** panel carved under **Chandragupta II**.
# Avatars and attributes
- **Varaha (boar):** the incarnation that **saved the Earth from the sea**, lifting her on his tusks after killing **Hiranyaksha** (UP UDA/LDA 2010) – not Matsya, Kurma or Narasimha.
- **Dashavatara:** Matsya, Kurma, Varaha, Narasimha, Vamana, Parashurama, Rama, Krishna, Buddha, Kalki.
- **Kaustubha (Kausthubham):** the gem on **Vishnu's chest**, from the churning of the ocean (APPSC Endowment 2022).
- **Vaishnava symbols:** chakra, shankha, Garuda; **AP angle:** Salankayanas were Vaishnavas (title Parama Mahesvara); Pedavegi inscription of Nandivarman II – first AP record of Vishnu temples.

=== Saivism, Shaktism and the Puranas
# Shaiva sects
| Sect | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Pashupata** | Oldest Shaiva sect; founder **Lakulisha** |
| **Kalamukha** | Followers of Kalanana (disciple of Lakulisha); extremist; Simha Parishat in Andhra |
| **Kapalika** | Skull-bearing **Vamacharas**; Bhairava worship; Srisailam a Kapalika kshetra; Bhairavakonda caves |
| **Mattamayura** | A **Shaiva sect** known from **Chedi (Kalachuri)** inscriptions (IAS 1996) |
| Nathpanth / Lingayats | Matsyendranath, Gorakhnath / Basava |
- **Kapalikas emerged from Misra Saivam** (APPSC G2) – NextGen's APPSC fact list; NextGen's AP history also calls them the Vamacharas of the Kalamukhas. Not Pashupata Saivam, Vaishnavam or Shuddha Saivam in the APPSC key.
- **Worshippers of Kapalini:** the **Kapalikas** (APPSC Endowment 2022).
- **⚠ Not a Shaiva sect:** Ajivika (Makkhali Gosala), Mayamata (a text on architecture), Isanasivagurudeva Paddhati (a ritual manual).
- **Gudimallam** (Tirupati district): earliest known Shiva linga; Gupta **ekamukhalinga** = linga with one face.
# Shakti
- **Ardhanarishvara:** half Shiva, half Parvati = the **union of God with his Shakti** (UPPSC 1997).
- **Mother goddesses:** Durga, Amba, Kali, Chandi; **Tantrism** spread from the **5th century**, open to women and Shudras.
- **Kalika Purana:** an **upapurana** of **Shaktism**, likely composed in **Assam** (MPPSC 2018 ×2) – not Vaishnava, Buddhist or Jain.
# Puranas
- **18 Mahapuranas**; took their final shape in the **Gupta period**; five topics (**pancha-lakshana**): creation, re-creation, genealogies, manvantaras, dynastic history.
- **Surya worship:** the silk-weavers' guild built a **Sun temple at Mandsaur** under **Kumaragupta I**; Skandagupta's **Indore** plate endows a lamp in a Sun temple.
- **Kakatiya link:** the **Kalamukha and Pashupata** sects later flourished under the Kakatiyas.

=== Gupta Temple Architecture – Deogarh, Bhitargaon and the Panchayatana
- **Earliest free-standing Hindu temples** survive from the **Gupta** age: square **garbhagriha**, pillared mandapa, raised **jagati**.
| Stage | Examples | Features |
|---|---|---|
| First | **Sanchi Temple 17**, Tigawa | Flat roof, square sanctum, shallow portico |
| Second | **Nachna** (Parvati), **Bhumara** (Shiva) | Covered ambulatory, raised platform; Bhumara has two flanking shrines |
| Third | **Deogarh** (Dashavatara, Vishnu), **Bhitargaon** | Low square **shikhara**; Panchayatana plan |
- **Dashavatara temple, Deogarh:** in **Lalitpur** district near **Jhansi** (UP); **Gupta** period (APPSC G2 2000) – not Pallava, Chola or Vijayanagara; Vishnu, ten avatars; earliest **Panchayatana** temple of North India.
- **Panchayatana:** a **style of temple construction** – main shrine plus **four subsidiary shrines** at the corners (UPSC 2014 ×2) – not a village assembly, sect or official.
- **Deogarh and Garhwa:** the Deogarh temple near Jhansi and the sculptures of **Garhwa** near Allahabad (Prayagraj) are remains of **Gupta art** (State PSC).
- **Bhitargaon** (near Kanpur): **brick and terracotta** Hindu temple – one of the oldest terracotta temples; not a Buddhist chaitya.
- **Gupta temple sites:** Deogarh, **Bhitargaon**, **Bhitari** (Ghazipur – Skandagupta's pillar and excavated Gupta Vishnu temples), Nachna, Bhumara, Tigawa, Sanchi, Eran – **Rajgir** is not listed among Gupta temple sites (SI 2018).

=== Buddhism, Jainism and Religious Tolerance
- **Buddhism:** lost royal patronage, overshadowed by Bhagavatism; **Nalanda** founded by **Kumaragupta I**; persecuted by the Huna **Mihirakula**.
| Scholar | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Vasubandhu** | Abhidharmakosa |
| **Dinnaga** | Founder of Buddhist logic |
| **Buddhaghosa** | Visuddhimagga |
- **Jainism:** **Vallabhi council** (5th c.) under **Devarddhi Kshamashramana** wrote down the **Svetambara** canon; **Mathura** a centre of Jain art; **Kahaum** pillar (Skandagupta) records Jain images.
- **Tolerance:** Samudragupta let **Meghavarman** of Sri Lanka build a monastery at Bodh Gaya; **Amrakardava**, Chandragupta II's officer, made a grant at Sanchi.
- **Purusharthas:** dharma, artha, kama, moksha – the first three = **trivarga**; devotional Hinduism replaced sacrificial Brahmanism.
- **Match:** Jainism – Triratna, Agamas, Mahavira; Buddhism – Ashtanga Margam; Saivism – Linga; Ajivika – Makkhali Gosala; Lingayat – Basava.
