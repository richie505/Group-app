## SUB 0 | Satavahana Inscriptions – Language, Script and Issuers
[0:0] # Language and script – the most-asked fact
[0:1] - **All Satavahana inscriptions:** **Prakrit language, Brahmi script** (G2 2002; APPSC PYQ ×2) – not "Sanskrit language, Prakrit script" or "Brahmi language".
[0:2] - **Royal language:** **Prakrit** – the administrative, business, written and royal language (G2 2011); Sanskrit and Telugu are distractors.
[0:3] - **Script:** **Brahmi** (not Kharosthi, Greek or Aramaic).
[0:4] # Issuer → record
[0:5] TABLE head: Inscription | Dynasty / issuer | What it records
  [0:5.0] Naneghat | Queen Naganika, wife of Satakarni I | Prakrit; Vedic sacrifices; gifts of cows, land, gold to Brahmins; label "Raya Simuka Satavahana" – first Satavahana with the title Rajan
  [0:5.1] Nasik | Gautami Balasri, in the reign of Vasishthiputra Pulumavi | Prakrit prose; victories and titles of Gautamiputra Satakarni: "Ekabrahmana", "Kshatriya-darpa-mana-mardana", destroyer of Shakas, Yavanas, Pahlavas
  [0:5.2] Amaravati | Pulumavi II | First Satavahana inscription in Andhra; word "Nagabu"
  [0:5.3] Myakadoni (Bellary) | Pulumavi III | Fall of the kingdom; officer "Gulmika"; Telugu word "Vepuru"
  [0:5.4] Malkapuram (1261 CE) | Kakatiya, Rudramadevi's reign | Musical instrument "Jalakaranda"
[0:6] - **⚠ Wrong pair (APPSC, G1):** **Rudradaman – Nanaghat**; Rudradaman's record is **Junagadh**. Gautami Balasri – Nasik, Kharavela – Hathigumpha, Ashoka – Maski are right.
[0:7] - Naneghat and Nasik lie in **Maharashtra**.
[0:8] - **Gulmika:** officer over a group of villages – the **village headman** of the Satavahana age (gramani in Mauryan times); also a border/feudatory chief (Myakadoni). The question's "Maski (Macedonian)" means **Myakadoni**.

## SUB 1 | Mauryan and Pre-Satavahana Records – Erragudi, Bhattiprolu, Hathigumpha
[1:0] # Earliest records of Andhra
[1:1] - **Earliest inscriptions on Andhra history:** Ashoka's edicts at **Yerragudi (Erragudi)** and **Rajula Mandagiri** (Kurnool) – **Prakrit** language, Brahmi script (G1 1984, 1987).
[1:2] - **Proof of Mauryan rule in Andhra:** **Yerragudi** inscription (not Maski, which is in Karnataka).
[1:3] - **Erragudi:** Ashoka tells **teachers to inculcate Dhamma in pupils** (Welfare Officers flashcard).
[1:4] # Bhattiprolu (Guntur)
[1:5] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [1:5.0] Date | 3rd c. BC, relic caskets of the oldest stupa in AP
  [1:5.1] King | Raja Kubhiraka (Kubheraka / Kuberaka), a local pre-Satavahana ruler
  [1:5.2] Body named | Nigama-sabha (town council / guild assembly) (G2 2011 ×2)
  [1:5.3] Other | Among the oldest Prakrit inscriptions of Andhra; Roman coins found
[1:6] - **⚠ Trap:** Nanaghat, Erragudi and Amaravati do not mention the Nigama-sabha; Ashoka, Mahadeva and Sudarshana did not rule Bhattiprolu.
[1:7] # Kharavela and the Krishna
[1:8] - **Hathigumpha inscription:** in his 2nd year Kharavela sent an army west, ignoring **Satakarni**, to the **Kannabenna** river (= **Krishna**) and attacked the Mushika city.
[1:9] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP HISTORY Part 1 p.6, p.11, Part 2 p.2-3, Part 6 p.14, Part 7 p.7; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 1 p.42; app notes 1:1 (sub 2)
## SUB 2 | Ikshvaku, Pallava, Brihatphalayana and Salankayana Inscriptions
[2:0] # Issuer and record
[2:1] TABLE head: Inscription | Issuer | Records
  [2:1.0] Nagarjunakonda | Ikshvaku (Veerapurushadatta) | Upasika Bodhisri; Buddhist patronage; Santamula founded the kingdom at Vijayapuri
  [2:1.1] Jaggayyapeta | Ikshvaku, Virapurushadatta's reign | Buddhist donations
  [2:1.2] Alluru | Ikshvaku period | Multiple gifts to different sections and institutions by the ruler (G2 2017) – the Buddhist community got bullock carts
  [2:1.3] Chejerla | Kandararaju | Ikshvaku – Anandagotrika wars
  [2:1.4] Manchikallu | Pallava Simhavarman, near Vijayapuri | Conquest of Vijayapuri after defeating the Ikshvakus (G2 2019)
  [2:1.5] Mayidavolu (c. AD 300) | Pallava Sivaskandavarman | Prakrit copper-plate grant; first inscriptional use of "Andhrapatha"
  [2:1.6] Kondamudi (copper) | Jayavarma, Brihatphalayana, 10th regnal year | Only record of the Brihatphalayanas (Ikshvaku feudatories); capital Koduru; village to Brahmins; Prakrit language, seal and subject in Sanskrit
  [2:1.7] Pedavegi | Nandi Varma II (Salankayana) | Land grant to Vishnu; first to mention Vishnu temples in Andhra
  [2:1.8] Kolleru | Nandi Varma II | –
  [2:1.9] Eluru | Devavarma | "Munyada" = political employee
  [2:1.10] Pithapuram | – | Mentions Kolleru lake
  [2:1.11] Kalamalla | Erikal Mutthuraju Dhananjaya Varma | First inscription in Telugu (not Sanskrit)
  [2:1.12] Allahabad Pillar | Samudragupta | Names Hastivarman of Vengi (Salankayana) among southern kings defeated
[2:2] - **Ikshvaku inscriptions:** **Prakrit** was their official and inscriptional language (Asst. BC Welfare 2017); Sanskrit got patronage only from **Ehuvala Santamula**'s time.
[2:3] - **⚠ Trap:** **Manchikallu and Mayidavolu = Pallavas**, not Ikshvakus (G2 Mains 2025); Karle, Veerapuram and Ramagiri are distractors for Alluru.
[2:4] # Fall of the Ikshvakus
[2:5] - Last Ikshvaku ruler **Rudrapurushadatta** (son of Ehuvala Chamtamula); his Nagarjunakonda pillar (11th year) honours his mother Vammabhatta; the **Phanigiri** pillar is of his 18th year.
[2:6] - Defeated by **Pallava Simhavarman** (Manchikallu), not by the Vishnukundins.
[2:7] - **South of the Krishna → Pallavas**; north → Brihatphalayanas.

## SUB 4 | Coins and Trade Evidence
[4:0] - **Ship coins:** the anchored-ship (ship-mast) coins of **Yajnasri Satakarni** prove brisk **maritime (naval) trade** (APPSC PYQ; Special Qualifying Test 2018); **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi** also issued ship-motif coins.
[4:1]   - **⚠ Trap:** the Periplus, Nasik and Karle records are not the evidence asked for.
[4:2] - Satavahana coins: **lead, potin, copper, bronze** and silver; gold was not their coinage.
[4:3] - **Roman coins** at Andhra sites belong to the **Satavahana** period (APSLPRB 2019) and show **Andhra–Rome trade**.

  [4:4.0] Roman gold | Attirala (Kadapa), Vinukonda (Guntur)
  [4:4.1] Others | Salihundam, Vijayawada, Nagarjunakonda, Nellore, Bhattiprolu

## SUB 5 | Copper-plate Grants and Land Grants
[5:0] - **Donor** = king; **donee** = Brahmins; gift of a **village/land**, often with **tax exemptions**.
[5:1] - **Earliest surviving land grant with fiscal exemptions:** the **Satavahanas** (Naneghat; Gautamiputra's Nasik grants) – not the Guptas (Asst. Tribal Welfare Officer PYQ).
[5:2] - **Vishnukundin** grants measured land in **nivartanas**.
[5:3] - Examples: Mayidavolu (Pallava), Kondamudi (Brihatphalayana).
[5:4] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [5:4.0] nivartana | Land measure (Vishnukundin grants)
  [5:4.1] Nigama-sabha | Town council (Bhattiprolu)
  [5:4.2] Munyada | Political employee (Eluru)
  [5:4.3] Jalakaranda | Musical instrument (Malkapuram)
[5:5] ~~ Sources: Upinder Singh, A History of Ancient and Early Medieval India ch. 8; app notes 1:1 (sub 4)
