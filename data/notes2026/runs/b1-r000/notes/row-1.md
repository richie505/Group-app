=== Satavahana Inscriptions – Language, Script and Issuers
# Language and script – the most-asked fact
- **All Satavahana inscriptions:** **Prakrit language, Brahmi script** (G2 2002; APPSC PYQ ×2) – not "Sanskrit language, Prakrit script" or "Brahmi language".
- **Royal language:** **Prakrit** – the administrative, business, written and royal language (G2 2011); Sanskrit and Telugu are distractors.
- **Script:** **Brahmi** (not Kharosthi, Greek or Aramaic).
# Issuer → record
| Inscription | Dynasty / issuer | What it records |
|---|---|---|
| **Naneghat** | Queen **Naganika**, wife of Satakarni I | Prakrit; Vedic sacrifices; gifts of cows, land, gold to Brahmins; label "**Raya Simuka Satavahana**" – first Satavahana with the title **Rajan** |
| **Nasik** | **Gautami Balasri**, in the reign of Vasishthiputra Pulumavi | Prakrit prose; victories and titles of **Gautamiputra Satakarni**: "**Ekabrahmana**", "**Kshatriya-darpa-mana-mardana**", destroyer of Shakas, Yavanas, Pahlavas |
| Amaravati | Pulumavi II | First Satavahana inscription in Andhra; word "Nagabu" |
| Myakadoni (Bellary) | Pulumavi III | Fall of the kingdom; officer "**Gulmika**"; Telugu word "Vepuru" |
| Malkapuram (1261 CE) | Kakatiya, Rudramadevi's reign | Musical instrument "Jalakaranda" |
- **⚠ Wrong pair (APPSC, G1):** **Rudradaman – Nanaghat**; Rudradaman's record is **Junagadh**. Gautami Balasri – Nasik, Kharavela – Hathigumpha, Ashoka – Maski are right.
- Naneghat and Nasik lie in **Maharashtra**.
- **Gulmika:** officer over a group of villages – the **village headman** of the Satavahana age (gramani in Mauryan times); also a border/feudatory chief (Myakadoni). The question's "Maski (Macedonian)" means **Myakadoni**.

=== Mauryan and Pre-Satavahana Records – Erragudi, Bhattiprolu, Hathigumpha
# Earliest records of Andhra
- **Earliest inscriptions on Andhra history:** Ashoka's edicts at **Yerragudi (Erragudi)** and **Rajula Mandagiri** (Kurnool) – **Prakrit** language, Brahmi script (G1 1984, 1987).
- **Proof of Mauryan rule in Andhra:** **Yerragudi** inscription (not Maski, which is in Karnataka).
- **Erragudi:** Ashoka tells **teachers to inculcate Dhamma in pupils** (Welfare Officers flashcard).
# Bhattiprolu (Guntur)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Date | 3rd c. BC, relic caskets of the **oldest stupa** in AP |
| King | Raja **Kubhiraka** (Kubheraka / Kuberaka), a local pre-Satavahana ruler |
| Body named | **Nigama-sabha** (town council / guild assembly) (G2 2011 ×2) |
| Other | Among the oldest Prakrit inscriptions of Andhra; Roman coins found |
- **⚠ Trap:** Nanaghat, Erragudi and Amaravati do not mention the Nigama-sabha; Ashoka, Mahadeva and Sudarshana did not rule Bhattiprolu.
# Kharavela and the Krishna
- **Hathigumpha inscription:** in his 2nd year Kharavela sent an army west, ignoring **Satakarni**, to the **Kannabenna** river (= **Krishna**) and attacked the Mushika city.

=== Ikshvaku, Pallava, Brihatphalayana and Salankayana Inscriptions
# Issuer and record
| Inscription | Issuer | Records |
|---|---|---|
| Nagarjunakonda | Ikshvaku (Veerapurushadatta) | **Upasika Bodhisri**; Buddhist patronage; Santamula founded the kingdom at Vijayapuri |
| Jaggayyapeta | Ikshvaku, Virapurushadatta's reign | Buddhist donations |
| **Alluru** | Ikshvaku period | **Multiple gifts to different sections and institutions by the ruler** (G2 2017) – the Buddhist community got bullock carts |
| Chejerla | Kandararaju | Ikshvaku – Anandagotrika wars |
| **Manchikallu** | **Pallava Simhavarman**, near Vijayapuri | **Conquest of Vijayapuri** after defeating the Ikshvakus (G2 2019) |
| Mayidavolu (c. AD 300) | Pallava Sivaskandavarman | Prakrit copper-plate grant; first inscriptional use of "Andhrapatha" |
| **Kondamudi** (copper) | **Jayavarma**, **Brihatphalayana**, 10th regnal year | **Only** record of the Brihatphalayanas (Ikshvaku feudatories); capital **Koduru**; village to Brahmins; **Prakrit** language, seal and subject in **Sanskrit** |
| Pedavegi | Nandi Varma II (Salankayana) | Land grant to Vishnu; first to mention Vishnu temples in Andhra |
| Kolleru | Nandi Varma II | – |
| Eluru | Devavarma | "Munyada" = political employee |
| Pithapuram | – | Mentions Kolleru lake |
| Kalamalla | Erikal Mutthuraju Dhananjaya Varma | First inscription in **Telugu** (not Sanskrit) |
| **Allahabad Pillar** | Samudragupta | Names **Hastivarman of Vengi** (Salankayana) among southern kings defeated |
- **Ikshvaku inscriptions:** **Prakrit** was their official and inscriptional language (Asst. BC Welfare 2017); Sanskrit got patronage only from **Ehuvala Santamula**'s time.
- **⚠ Trap:** **Manchikallu and Mayidavolu = Pallavas**, not Ikshvakus (G2 Mains 2025); Karle, Veerapuram and Ramagiri are distractors for Alluru.
# Fall of the Ikshvakus
- Last Ikshvaku ruler **Rudrapurushadatta** (son of Ehuvala Chamtamula); his Nagarjunakonda pillar (11th year) honours his mother Vammabhatta; the **Phanigiri** pillar is of his 18th year.
- Defeated by **Pallava Simhavarman** (Manchikallu), not by the Vishnukundins.
- **South of the Krishna → Pallavas**; north → Brihatphalayanas.

=== Literary Sources for Andhra
| Text | Content revealed |
|---|---|
| **Matsya Purana** | **30 (29 in some counts) Satavahana kings ruled for 456 years** |
| Vishnu Purana | Story of the Satavahanas |
| Bhimasena Jataka | "Andhapatha" (Andhra route); Serivanija Jataka = Andhapura on the **Telavaha** |
| Chulla-Kalinga Jataka | Kharavela defeated by Asmaka king Satakarni |
| Vinayavatthu | "Andhapatha" |
| **Gathasaptasati** (King **Hala**) | **Maharashtri Prakrit**, 700 verses; society; early Telugu words "atta, addam, potta" |
| Pratishtana Pura Kalpa, Dwatrimsatputtalika, Kathasaritsagara | **Early history of the Satavahana family** |
| Kashikhandam and Haravilasam | Both by **Srinatha**; sea trade of the Reddi period |
| **Nritta Ratnavali** | **Jayapa Senani** (Ganapatideva's Gaja Sahini) – **Sanskrit** treatise on dance |
- **Not a source of early Satavahana history:** the **Gathasaptasati** (G2 Mains 2017) – it is a verse anthology.
- **Andhakaratta:** the name of the Andhras in **Buddhist** literature (G2 Mains 2017) – not Jain or Vedic.
  - **⚠ Settled:** the printed key "Jain literature" is wrong (NextGen Previous Papers p.8).
- **Chilukuri Veerabhadra Rao:** wrote **Andhrula Charitramu (The History of the Andhras)**, which carried knowledge of the Andhras' past across Andhra; Kanyasulkam is Gurajada Apparao's.
- Reddi sea-trade question has two keys: Kashikhandam (G2 2011) and Haravilasam (G2 2019) – both by Srinatha.

=== Coins and Trade Evidence
- **Ship coins:** the anchored-ship (ship-mast) coins of **Yajnasri Satakarni** prove brisk **maritime (naval) trade** (APPSC PYQ; Special Qualifying Test 2018); **Vasishthiputra Pulumavi** also issued ship-motif coins.
  - **⚠ Trap:** the Periplus, Nasik and Karle records are not the evidence asked for.
- Satavahana coins: **lead, potin, copper, bronze** and silver; gold was not their coinage.
- **Roman coins** at Andhra sites belong to the **Satavahana** period (APSLPRB 2019) and show **Andhra–Rome trade**.
| Hoard | Find-spots |
|---|---|
| Roman gold | **Attirala (Kadapa)**, **Vinukonda (Guntur)** |
| Others | Salihundam, Vijayawada, Nagarjunakonda, Nellore, Bhattiprolu |
~~ Sources: NextGen Satavahanas p.19, p.21; NextGen AP HISTORY Part 1 p.13, Part 2 p.8; app notes 1:1 (sub 5, 7)

=== Copper-plate Grants and Land Grants
- **Donor** = king; **donee** = Brahmins; gift of a **village/land**, often with **tax exemptions**.
- **Earliest surviving land grant with fiscal exemptions:** the **Satavahanas** (Naneghat; Gautamiputra's Nasik grants) – not the Guptas (Asst. Tribal Welfare Officer PYQ).
- **Vishnukundin** grants measured land in **nivartanas**.
- Examples: Mayidavolu (Pallava), Kondamudi (Brihatphalayana).
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| nivartana | Land measure (Vishnukundin grants) |
| Nigama-sabha | Town council (Bhattiprolu) |
| Munyada | Political employee (Eluru) |
| Jalakaranda | Musical instrument (Malkapuram) |
~~ Sources: Upinder Singh, A History of Ancient and Early Medieval India ch. 8; app notes 1:1 (sub 4)

=== Archaeological Sites and Foreign Accounts on Andhra
- Satavahana history rests on the **Puranas**, **Prakrit inscriptions** and **coins**; foreign accounts must be cross-checked against these.
| Type | Sites |
|---|---|
| Stupa sites | Amaravati, Nagarjunakonda, **Bhattiprolu (oldest)**, Jaggayyapeta, Guntupalli, Salihundam |
| Habitation mounds | Gudiwada Dibba (Mauryan to Satavahana), Kotilingala, **Kondapur (mint)**, Dharanikota |
| Writer | Period | Andhra places / facts |
|---|---|---|
| Megasthenes | 4th c. BCE, Mauryan | Andhras: 30 fortified towns |
| Periplus | c. 60 CE | **Koddura** port; Saka–Satavahana conflict |
| Pliny | 1st c. CE | Roman gold drain to India |
| Ptolemy | 2nd c. CE | Masulia (Machilipatnam), **Ben Ra (Vengi)** |
| Hiuen Tsang | 7th c. CE (visited Dhanakataka, c. **639 CE**) | Buddhist monasteries of Andhra |
- Dharanikota is a habitation mound, not a listed stupa site.
