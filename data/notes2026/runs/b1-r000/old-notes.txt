=== NOTES 1:0 | Sources of Ancient Indian History – Archaeological, Literary, Foreign Accounts; Dating Methods
## SUB 0 | Sources – Types and Reliability
[0:0:0] - Three groups: **archaeological** (inscriptions, coins, monuments, sites), **literary** (religious and secular texts) and **foreign accounts**.
[0:0:1] - **Inscriptions** are the **most reliable** source; foreign accounts only **supplement** Indian sources and fill gaps where Indian texts are silent.
[0:0:2] - Chinese writers called India "**Yin-tu**" (or Thian-tu), a form of Hindu/Sindhu.
