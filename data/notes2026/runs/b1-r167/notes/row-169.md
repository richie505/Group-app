=== Anicuts and Irrigation – Cotton, Veeranna, Orr and Ramapada Sagar
# Dowleswaram (Godavari) anicut
- 1. **Guntur famine (1832-33)** – the trigger (about 40% of Guntur's people died).
- 2. **1844:** Cotton's report to the Board of Revenue for an anicut near Rajahmundry.
- 3. Sanctioned late **1846**; begun **1847**; **completed April 1852** at about **Rs 1.5 lakh** (NextGen L3 p.49 says 1849 – the standard year is **1852**).
- **Veenam Veeranna** – the **Andhra (Indian) official** who managed the labour; made **Rao Bahadur** and Assistant Engineer; name on the anicut wall.
| Anicut | River | Engineer | Years |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dowleswaram** | **Godavari** | **Sir Arthur Cotton**, assisted by **Veenam Veeranna** | 1847-**1852** |
| **Krishna anicut, Bezwada** (now **Prakasam Barrage**) | Krishna | proposed by Col. Henry Atlock (Lake); executed by **Captain (Charles) Orr** on Cotton's plan | 1850-**1855** |
| Sangam anicut | Penna (Nellore) | – | 1882-86 |
- **Captain Orr** = the **English officer** who assisted Cotton (Krishna anicut); Captain Nolt defeated Narasimha Reddy – a distractor.
- **Cotton (1803-1899):** "**pioneer of irrigation in South India**"; knighted **1861**; "Apara Bhagiratha", "Cotton Dora"; on his suggestion Rajahmundry → **Godavari** and Machilipatnam → **Krishna** districts (1859); today's **Sir Arthur Cotton Barrage** replaced the old anicut.
- **Ramapada Sagar(am) at Polavaram:** the "dream-child" of **S.V. (Sonti Venkata) Ramamurthi, ICS**, who named it after the backwaters reaching Lord Rama's feet at Bhadrachalam (1941-42; first survey by **L. Venkatakrishna Iyer**) = forerunner of the **Polavaram** project (APPSC G2 Prelims 2019). Not Cotton or K.L. Rao.
- **Godavari and Krishna deltas** = the "**Rice Bowl of Andhra**". **Feb 2026:** crest-gate telemetry and SCADA installed at the **Prakasam Barrage**.

=== Railways and Communications
- **First railway line in Andhra:** **Puttur–Renigunta**, opened **15 September 1862** (Madras Railway) – asked ×4; later Gooty–Renigunta.
- Railways linked the interior with the **Madras port**; built mainly for military and commercial needs.
- **Buckingham Canal:** salt-water canal through **Pulicat Lake**; extended as famine relief in **1876-78**; now part of **NW-4**.
- **Fort St. George** was built at **Madrasapatnam** (1639-40).

=== District Reorganisation and the Collector
| Year | Change |
|---|---|
| 1802 | Rajahmundry + Kakinada collectorates → Rajahmundry district |
| 1819 | **Ganjam Collector** made Agent to the Governor for the Agency tracts |
| 1859 | Rajahmundry → Godavari, Machilipatnam → Krishna districts |
| 1904 / 1925 | Guntur and East Godavari / West Godavari |
- The **Collector** held revenue, magisterial and police powers; revenue was collected **in cash**; hierarchy Collector → Tahsildar → village officers.
- **Rajahmundry jail:** the old fort was converted into a jail in **1864** (made a Central Jail in 1870).

=== Economic Effects and Famines
- Cash revenue demand → **commercial crops**: **tobacco in Guntur**, indigo, cotton (Ganjam 1865-66 famine followed the cotton boom of the American Civil War).
- **Deindustrialisation:** duty-free British cloth ruined weavers – Visakhapatnam exports ₹7 lakh → ₹1 lakh by the 1830s; Machilipatnam dyed cloth, Eluru carpets, Kurnool blankets declined.
| Famine | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Doji Bara (Skull)** | 1791-92 | Madras region |
| **Guntur (Nandana / Pedda Karuvu)** | 1832-33 | ~1.5 lakh deaths; led to Dowleswaram |
| Ganjam (Orissa) | 1865-66 | after the cotton boom |
| Great Famine | 1876-78 | worst in the Ceded Districts |
