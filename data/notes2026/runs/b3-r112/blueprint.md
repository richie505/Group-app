## 3. Trends
- Row 113-114 questions are almost all **bare-Act recall** of APRA Part IX (Sections 84-91, Eleventh Schedule): who chairs, how many members, which section, where the HQ is, who guards (CISF), who releases water (Tungabhadra Board), what is not a Board function. The rebuilt notes put the section table first.
- Polavaram: Section 90 (×8) dominates; newer questions add NITI Aayog's 2016 "AP executes" twist, the Cabinet's "irrigation component as on 1.4.2014" funding formula, and the PPA CEO (2024).
- Revenue questions are data-bound to the CAG's 2014-15 report (RD ₹13,776 cr; FD 6.10%) and to budget-year figures (2022-23 BE RD ₹17,000 cr, 1.27%).
- Irrigation questions come from the 2016-17 Socio-Economic Survey era (wells as main source, 103.5 lakh acres, priority projects, Neeru-Chettu, NTR Jalasiri, Pattiseema).
- Many off-topic PYQs had been filed here (science, Brexit, state symbols, SHGs) – moved.
