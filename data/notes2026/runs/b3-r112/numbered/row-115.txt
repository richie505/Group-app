## SUB 2 | Pattiseema Lift and Godavari-Krishna Linking
[2:0] - **Pattiseema** = a **lift irrigation scheme** on the Godavari at Pattiseema (West Godavari, now Eluru); **24 pumps**; first water Sept **2015**.
[2:1] - Objective: **link the Godavari and Krishna rivers** – Godavari → **Polavaram Right Main Canal** → Krishna at **Prakasam Barrage** = **first Godavari-Krishna link**.
[2:2] - Beneficiary: **Krishna delta farmers**; ~450 TMC moved.
[2:3] - **Temporary** until Polavaram's gravity flow starts.
[2:4] - The RMC runs about **174 km** – the statement "the canal traverses **100 km** through West Godavari and Krishna" is **false**.
[2:5] TABLE head: Link | Rivers | Status
  [2:5.0] Pattiseema | Godavari → Krishna | Working since 2015
  [2:5.1] Polavaram Right Main Canal | Godavari → Krishna (gravity) | With Polavaram
  [2:5.2] Polavaram-Banakacherla / Nallamala Sagar | Godavari → Krishna → Penna basin (Rayalaseema) | Proposed; 200 TMC
[2:6] - Telangana objects to AP's Godavari links, citing APRA 2014 and the 1980 GWDT award.
[2:7] - **GWDT award (1980)** permits diversion of **80 TMC** Godavari water to the Krishna.
[2:8] - **Polavaram**: first proposed as **"Ramapada Sagaram"** (Sir Sonti Venkataramamurthy); **started 2005**, still a **Jalayagnam** project; May 2026: 88% civil works done.
[2:9] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP Geography - APPSC Exams Part 2 p.16; Rocket AP Geography p.36 (Pattiseema LIS: Godavari → Polavaram RMC → Krishna); old app notes 3:115 sub 3, 4; 174 km canal length general knowledge, not checked

## SUB 4 | Groundwater Regulation and Water Policy
[4:0] - **APWALTA, 2002** (AP Water, Land and Trees Act): checks random borewell digging; the authority (Panchayat Secretary in villages) may require **rainwater harvesting structures on premises of 200 sq m or more**.
[4:1] - **National Water Policy 2012**: **Water Users Associations** to be given **statutory powers** to collect and retain water charges.
[4:2] - **Maharashtra (April 2016)**: banned digging bore wells **deeper than 200 feet** during drought.
[4:3] - **APFAMGS**: farmers collect rainfall and well data, prepare **crop-water budgets**, voluntarily limit pumping (FAO-supported).
[4:4] - 40,000-50,000 new bore wells a year: area irrigated per well falling.
[4:5] - **National Hydrology Project** (2016-17, 100% grant) set up AP's real-time groundwater monitoring.
[4:6] ~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.127 (NHP); old app notes 3:115 sub 8; NWP 2012 and Maharashtra 2016 ban general knowledge, not checked

## SUB 7 | Micro-irrigation and PMKSY
[7:0] - **PMKSY (Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchai Yojana, 2015)** – the scheme formulated to boost irrigation = AIBP + Har Khet Ko Pani + Watershed + Per Drop More Crop.
[7:1] - **APMIP launched 2003**; now run as **RKVY-PDMC**.
[7:2] - 37.90 lakh acres, 13.49 lakh farmers since inception; **75% drip, 25% sprinkler**.
[7:3] - **AP 1st** nationally in micro-irrigation (2024-25); **100% subsidy for SC/ST**.
[7:4] - Per Drop More Crop (2015-16; under RKVY since 2022-23): **55%** small/marginal, **45%** others.
[7:5] - **Micro Irrigation Fund**: Rs 10,000 crore via **NABARD**; AP among eight States.
[7:6] - AIBP (PMKSY): 8 of 99 priority projects in AP; HKKP-RRR: 100 water bodies approved (60% central share).
[7:7] ~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.128 (AIBP, HKKP-RRR); NextGen AP Economy STATIC-1 Part 1 p.15 (APMIP 2003); old app notes 3:115 sub 7
