## SUB 0 | National Project Status – APRA Section 90
[0:0] TABLE head: Sub-section | What it says
  [0:0.0] 90(1) | Polavaram Irrigation Project declared a national project
  [0:0.1] 90(2) | Expedient in the public interest that the Union takes control of its regulation and development for the purposes of irrigation
  [0:0.2] 90(3) | Consent of Telangana deemed to have been given
  [0:0.3] 90(4) | Central Government executes the project and obtains environmental, forest and R&R clearances
[0:1] - **Polavaram is the only project declared national by the Reorganisation Act itself** – not Gundlakamma, Pulichintala or Jeedipalli.
[0:2] - So it is a **National Project**, executed by the Centre **in consultation with AP and Telangana** – not a mere State project.
[0:3] - **NITI Aayog (2016):** suggested **the State Government (AP) executes** Polavaram while the **Centre funds** the irrigation component; since then **GoAP executes it on behalf of GoI** and claims reimbursement.
[0:4] > Exam angle: **Section 90** (not 30, 89 or 91); "for the purposes of **irrigation**" only; by the Act the **Centre** executes, in practice **AP** does (NITI Aayog).

## SUB 2 | Polavaram Ordinance and the Seven Mandals
[2:0] - **Telangana came into existence on 2 June 2014** (appointed day).
[2:1] - **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Ordinance, 2014** = "**Polavaram Ordinance**" – moved the Khammam **submergence villages and mandals to AP (Seemandhra)** to ease **resettlement of those displaced** by Polavaram.
[2:2] - Base rule in the Act: Khammam → Telangana, excluding villages in **G.O.Ms. No. 111 (27 June 2005)**.
[2:3] - The Bill replacing it was passed by the **Lok Sabha on 11 July 2014** (by voice vote) – not June 2016.
[2:4] - **Seven mandals** moved from Khammam to AP:
[2:5] - 1. Kukunoor
[2:6] - 2. Velerupadu
[2:7] - 3. VR Puram (Vararamachandrapuram)
[2:8] - 4. Chintoor
[2:9] - 5. Kunavaram
[2:10] - 6. **Bhadrachalam (except the temple town)**
[2:11] - 7. Burgampadu
[2:12] - Telangana Assembly constituencies left with part of their territory in AP: **Pinapaka, Aswaraopeta and Bhadrachalam**.
[2:13] - **Bhadrachalam town** (with the Sri Rama temple) stayed in **Telangana**; it lies on the bank of the Godavari whose surrounding mandals went to **Andhra** (the "Andhra side").
[2:14] - Effect: AP's area **1,60,205 → 1,62,970 sq km**.
[2:15] > Exam angle: **7 mandals**; temple town of Bhadrachalam stayed in Telangana; Lok Sabha **11 July 2014**.
[2:16] ~~ Sources: AP Reorganisation Act 2014 (apra.txt s.3, Schedules of constituencies: Pinapaka, Aswaraopeta); old app notes 3:114 sub 1, 4; Bhadrachalam side general knowledge, not checked
