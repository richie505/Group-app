## SUB 3 | Constitutional and Legal Base
[3:0] - **Article 262**: Parliament may make law for adjudicating **inter-State river water disputes** (use, distribution or control of waters).
[3:1] - **Article 262(2)**: Parliament may provide that **neither the Supreme Court nor any other court** shall exercise jurisdiction over such disputes.
[3:2] - **Inter-State River Water Disputes (ISRWD) Act, 1956** – enacted under Article 262.
[3:3]   - **Section 11**: bars the Supreme Court and other courts from disputes that may be referred to a tribunal.
[3:4]   - The Supreme Court held (Cauvery case, 1991) that this bar does **not** cover its appeal jurisdiction under **Article 136**.
[3:5]   - **Section 5(1)**: used in **October 2023** to give **KWDT-II further terms of reference**.
[3:6] - Articles 265 (no tax without law) and 268 (duties levied by the Union, collected by States) are tax articles – not water.
[3:7] ~~ Sources: old app notes 3:113 sub 0; distractor articles general knowledge, not checked
