## SUB 1 | Industrial Base and GSDP Share
[1:0] - Before 2014, undivided AP ranked about **5th** among industrialised States (Hyderabad pharma, IT, engineering).
[1:1] - Just after bifurcation: residual AP about **60% agriculture-dependent**; **manufacturing about 10.5% of GSDP**.
[1:2] - **Industry sector excluding IT/ITES ≈ 23% of GSDP (2019-20)**.
[1:3] - 2025-26 GVA (current prices): services **43%**, agriculture **33%**, industry **24%**; manufacturing **10.84%**.
[1:4] - The "10.5% of GSDP" figure is **manufacturing only**, not the whole industry sector (which adds mining, construction, utilities).
[1:5] - Advantage: coastline **1,053.07 km (3rd in India after the 2025 remeasurement)**; old figure **974 km (2nd)** – supports a **port-led EXIM strategy**.

## SUB 3 | Section 94 Incentives
[3:0] - **94(1)**: fiscal measures including tax incentives to promote industrialisation; **94(2)**: backward-area physical and social infrastructure.
[3:1] - **7 backward districts**: 4 Rayalaseema + 3 North Coastal.
[3:2] - New units get **15% additional depreciation** and **15% investment allowance**.
[3:3] - Criticism: incentives and grants fell short; Special Category Status denied.
