=== River Management Boards – KRMB and GRMB (Sections 85-88)
# Constitution and status
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| **85(1)** | **Constitution and functions**: two Boards within **60 days** of the appointed day |
| **85(2)** | HQ: **KRMB in Andhra Pradesh**; **GRMB in Telangana** |
| 85(3) | Autonomous bodies under administrative control of the Centre |
| 85(4) | Chairperson (Centre, Secretary/Additional Secretary rank) + **2 members per State** (technical + administrative) + **1 central expert** |
| 85(5) | Full-time **Member Secretary** (Chief Engineer, CWC) |
| **85(7)** | **CISF** assists in day-to-day management of reservoirs |
| **86** | **Staff** on deputation equally from both States; States fund the Boards |
| **87** | **Jurisdiction** over projects **notified by the Centre** |
| **88** | **Power of the Board to make regulations** |
- **Members apart from the chairman**: 4 State members + 1 expert = **5 under Sec. 85(4)**; counting the full-time Member Secretary = **6** (most keys).
- AP's petition asking the Centre to **notify KRMB's jurisdiction** was under **Section 87**.
- **Jal Shakti** notified both Boards' jurisdiction on **15 July 2021**.
# Functions (Sec. 85(8) and Eleventh Schedule)
- **Regulate supply** of water and power from notified projects per **tribunal awards**; **administer and regulate** notified projects.
- **Release water** per States' shares; **advise both States on disaster, drought and flood management** (Schedule XI para 6).
- **Not** a Board function: **allocating or redetermining** shares (tribunals do that).
- **Schedule XI para 9**: a State that does not implement a Board decision **bears financial and other penalties imposed by the Central Government**.
- **Schedule XI para 7**: no new project on Godavari/Krishna without **Apex Council sanction**, after Board appraisal.

=== Apex Council (Section 84)
| Member | Role |
|---|---|
| **Union Minister of Water Resources** (now Jal Shakti) | **Chairperson** |
| Chief Minister of Andhra Pradesh | Member |
| Chief Minister of Telangana | Member |
- **3 members** in all, including the chairperson.
- **Sec. 84(1)**: constituted by the Centre from the appointed day to **supervise the GRMB and KRMB**.
- **Functions – Sec. 84(3)**:
- 1. **Supervise** GRMB and KRMB.
- 2. **Plan and approve new projects** on Krishna/Godavari after appraisal by the **Boards and CWC** – new projects need **Apex Council sanction**.
- 3. **Settle disputes by negotiation** and mutual agreement.
- 4. **Refer disputes** not covered by KWDT to a **tribunal** under the ISRWD Act, 1956.
- **Management and development of water resources** of the two States = **Apex Council + River Management Boards** (Part IX), not the Governors.
- **Not** its function: allocating shares, dividing assets, employees or capital works.
- Apex Council does **not** include the PM, Home Minister or Governors.

| Tribunal | Chair | Award | Key point |
|---|---|---|---|
| KWDT-I | Justice R.S. Bachawat | 1973, final 1976 | 75% dependability; Maharashtra 560, Karnataka 700, **united AP 800 TMC** |

- **AP's 800 TMC + 11 TMC return flows = 811 TMC**.
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|

=== Constitutional and Legal Base
- **Article 262**: Parliament may make law for adjudicating **inter-State river water disputes** (use, distribution or control of waters).
- **Article 262(2)**: Parliament may provide that **neither the Supreme Court nor any other court** shall exercise jurisdiction over such disputes.
- **Inter-State River Water Disputes (ISRWD) Act, 1956** – enacted under Article 262.
  - **Section 11**: bars the Supreme Court and other courts from disputes that may be referred to a tribunal.
  - The Supreme Court held (Cauvery case, 1991) that this bar does **not** cover its appeal jurisdiction under **Article 136**.
  - **Section 5(1)**: used in **October 2023** to give **KWDT-II further terms of reference**.
- Articles 265 (no tax without law) and 268 (duties levied by the Union, collected by States) are tax articles – not water.

=== Tungabhadra Board
- **Tungabhadra Board**: set up under **Section 66 of the Andhra State Act, 1953**.
- **Sec. 91(1), APRA**: AP and Telangana **replace undivided AP** on the Board.
- **Sec. 91(2)**: the Board **continues to monitor the release of water** to the **High Level Canal, Low Level Canal and Rajolibanda Diversion Scheme (RDS)** – not CWC, CGWB or NWDA.
~~ Sources: AP Reorganisation Act 2014 (apra.txt s.91); old app notes 3:113 sub 6

=== KWDT-II and Section 89
- **Sec. 89, APRA**: term of KWDT extended for:
  - (a) **project-wise allocation** where no tribunal had made one;
  - (b) **operational protocol** for releases in **deficit flows**.
- Section 89 did **not** automatically divide undivided AP's share between AP and Telangana.
- **October 2023**: Centre gave KWDT-II further terms under **Sec. 5(1), ISRWD Act** → divide AP's share between AP and Telangana.
- **Sec. 90** = Polavaram national project (row 114).
