# Minor irrigation
- **Minor irrigation**: ayacut **up to 5,000 acres** (AP); sources include **surface lift schemes, deep tube wells and dug wells** and tanks.
- Undivided AP: about **78,000 tanks** serving about **46.5 lakh acres**; residual AP now **38,862 tanks** with **25.60 lakh acres** registered ayacut.
- Tanks: **Coastal Andhra over 14,000** minor tanks; **Rayalaseema over 6,800** active tanks. Tanks suit the plateau's undulating relief; canals dominate the deltas.
> Exam angle: largest source now **groundwater**; largest river command **Krishna**; potential **106.15 lakh acres** (Dec 2024).

=== Pattiseema Lift and Godavari-Krishna Linking
- **Pattiseema** = a **lift irrigation scheme** on the Godavari at Pattiseema (West Godavari, now Eluru); **24 pumps**; first water Sept **2015**.
- Objective: **link the Godavari and Krishna rivers** – Godavari → **Polavaram Right Main Canal** → Krishna at **Prakasam Barrage** = **first Godavari-Krishna link**.
- Beneficiary: **Krishna delta farmers**; ~450 TMC moved.
- **Temporary** until Polavaram's gravity flow starts.
- The RMC runs about **174 km** – the statement "the canal traverses **100 km** through West Godavari and Krishna" is **false**.
| Link | Rivers | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Pattiseema | Godavari → Krishna | Working since 2015 |
| Polavaram Right Main Canal | Godavari → Krishna (gravity) | With Polavaram |
| Polavaram-Banakacherla / Nallamala Sagar | Godavari → Krishna → Penna basin (Rayalaseema) | Proposed; 200 TMC |
- Telangana objects to AP's Godavari links, citing APRA 2014 and the 1980 GWDT award.
- **GWDT award (1980)** permits diversion of **80 TMC** Godavari water to the Krishna.
- **Polavaram**: first proposed as **"Ramapada Sagaram"** (Sir Sonti Venkataramamurthy); **started 2005**, still a **Jalayagnam** project; May 2026: 88% civil works done.

=== Groundwater Regulation and Water Policy
- **APWALTA, 2002** (AP Water, Land and Trees Act): checks random borewell digging; the authority (Panchayat Secretary in villages) may require **rainwater harvesting structures on premises of 200 sq m or more**.
- **National Water Policy 2012**: **Water Users Associations** to be given **statutory powers** to collect and retain water charges.
- **Maharashtra (April 2016)**: banned digging bore wells **deeper than 200 feet** during drought.
- **APFAMGS**: farmers collect rainfall and well data, prepare **crop-water budgets**, voluntarily limit pumping (FAO-supported).
- 40,000-50,000 new bore wells a year: area irrigated per well falling.
- **National Hydrology Project** (2016-17, 100% grant) set up AP's real-time groundwater monitoring.

=== Micro-irrigation and PMKSY
- **PMKSY (Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchai Yojana, 2015)** – the scheme formulated to boost irrigation = AIBP + Har Khet Ko Pani + Watershed + Per Drop More Crop.
- **APMIP launched 2003**; now run as **RKVY-PDMC**.
- 37.90 lakh acres, 13.49 lakh farmers since inception; **75% drip, 25% sprinkler**.
- **AP 1st** nationally in micro-irrigation (2024-25); **100% subsidy for SC/ST**.
- Per Drop More Crop (2015-16; under RKVY since 2022-23): **55%** small/marginal, **45%** others.
- **Micro Irrigation Fund**: Rs 10,000 crore via **NABARD**; AP among eight States.
- AIBP (PMKSY): 8 of 99 priority projects in AP; HKKP-RRR: 100 water bodies approved (60% central share).
