=== NOTES 3:135 | Division of Assets, Liabilities, Employees and Institutions; Ninth and Tenth Schedule Institutions
## SUB 0 | The Basic Asymmetry
[135:0:0] - AP Reorganisation Act, 2014: assent **1 March 2014**; appointed day **2 June 2014**.
[135:0:1] - Core rule: **assets by location**, **debts by population** (AP : Telangana = **58 : 42**, Census 2011).
[135:0:2] - Result: AP got about **46% of revenue** but inherited about **58% of debt**.
[135:0:3] - Financial division provisions sit in **Sections 47-64**.
## SUB 1 | Principles of Division – Item → Basis → Section
[135:1:0] TABLE head: Item | Basis | Section
  [135:1:0.0] Land, stores, goods inside a State | Location | 48
  [135:1:0.1] Stores, goods and property outside the existing State (e.g. AP Bhavan) | Population ratio | 48
  [135:1:0.2] Treasury and bank cash balances | Population ratio, by adjusting entries in RBI books (no physical transfer) | 49
  [135:1:0.3] Arrears of taxes (non-property) | Place of assessment | 50
  [135:1:0.4] Securities against Seventh Schedule funds | Population ratio | 52
  [135:1:0.5] Commercial undertakings: operational units / headquarters | Location / population ratio | 53
  [135:1:0.6] Refund of taxes other than property taxes | Population ratio | 56
  [135:1:0.7] Provident Fund | State the employee is allotted to | 58
  [135:1:0.8] Pension liability | Eighth Schedule | 59
[135:1:1] **Calamity Relief Fund** was divided by **area ratio**, not population.
## SUB 2 | Seventh Schedule Funds and Power Sharing
[135:2:0] # Seventh Schedule
[135:2:1] - Seventh Schedule = "**List of Funds**" in the **Public Account**: **provident, pension and insurance** funds and others (**APPSC 2022**).
[135:2:2] - Securities held against them divided by **population ratio** (Sec 52).
[135:2:3] # Electricity (Twelfth Schedule)
[135:2:4] - **APGENCO plants** divided by **location** (not population); existing **PPAs continue**.
[135:2:5] - **Central Generating Station** power shared on the **DISCOMs' actual consumption of the last 5 years**.
[135:2:6] - AP got about **46% of power** for **58% of population**.
[135:2:7] - **Surplus power**: the deficit State has the **first right of refusal** over the other State's surplus for **10 years**.
[135:2:8] APPSC-GS 2019 key marked "**Gadgil formula**" for the electricity distribution formula. The Act's rule is the **5-year consumption basis**; if the options force a choice, the key's answer is Gadgil.
## SUB 3 | Schedule Ix vs Schedule X
[135:3:0] TABLE head: Feature | Schedule IX | Schedule X
  [135:3:0.0] What it lists | Government companies and corporations | State institutions whose facilities continue for both States
  [135:3:0.1] Count | 91 | 107 in the Act as enacted; 142 after 35 more were added by notification (7 May 2015)
  [135:3:0.2] Examples | APSFC, Sports Authority of AP | APSCHE, Shilparamam Arts, Crafts and Cultural Society, tribunals
  [135:3:0.3] Governing section | Sec 68 (continue to function) and Sec 53 (division) | Sec 75 (continuance of facilities)
  [135:3:0.4] Division rule | Operational units by location; headquarters by population ratio | Assets by location; cash by population; facilities continue for the other State
  [135:3:0.5] Time limit | – | Terms for continuing facilities fixed within 1 year
  [135:3:0.6] Committee | Sheela Bhide Committee | –
[135:3:1] Schedule X: **107** = the Act's original list (APPSC 2021 answer); **142** = 107 + 35 added in May 2015. Pick 107 when the question is about the Act as passed.
[135:3:2] - Most Schedule X institutions are **in or around Hyderabad**.
[135:3:3] - **Section 66**: some institutions are in **no schedule**; AP uses Sec 66 to bring them into Schedule IX or X.
[135:3:4] - Telangana's stand (10 August 2026): share only **cash balances**; institutions go by **location**.
## SUB 4 | Committees on Apportionment
[135:4:0] TABLE head: Committee | Chair | Subject
  [135:4:0.0] Sheela Bhide Committee | Sheela Bhide (retired IAS) | Assets, liabilities and employees of Schedule IX companies and corporations; set up by the undivided AP government (APPSC 2021)
  [135:4:0.1] Kamalanathan Committee | C.R. Kamalanathan | Allocation of State-level employees
  [135:4:0.2] Pratyush Sinha Committee | Pratyush Sinha | Allocation of All India Services officers (IAS/IPS/IFoS); formed under Sec 80
[135:4:1] - Sheela Bhide Committee recommended division of **89 of the 91** Schedule IX bodies; Telangana opposed its recommendations on **22** of them; the Union Home Ministry sought **legal opinion**.
[135:4:2] - Its term expired in **August 2015**; the Centre later **revived** it.
[135:4:3] - Comparative note: the **1953 Andhra State** partition committee was chaired by **T.T. Krishnamachari** (APHQ 2025).
## SUB 5 | Employee Apportionment – Part Viii
[135:5:0] # Provisions as to Services
[135:5:1] - **Part VIII**, "**Provisions as to Services**" (**Sections 76-83**).
[135:5:2] - **Section 76**: separate **State cadres** of **IAS, IPS, IFoS** under the **1954/1966 Cadre Rules**.
[135:5:3] - **Section 77**: **State employees**; does **not** apply to All India Services.
[135:5:4] - **Advisory Committees**: Centre could set them up within **30 days** of enactment (Kamalanathan for State employees; Pratyush Sinha for AIS).
[135:5:5] - Employees **opt** for a State; **non-opters** allotted by the Centre on **native (domicile) status**.
[135:5:6] - **PSUs and corporations**: staff stayed in place for **1 year**; the corporation itself decided division (Sec 82).
[135:5:7] # Disputes
[135:5:8] - **Seniority and pension** disputes: **Sec 59** and **Eighth Schedule**; backlog in the **AP Reorganisation Tribunal**.
[135:5:9] - **3 January 2024**: Telangana High Court ordered AIS allotment cases reconsidered under the Pratyush Sinha guidelines; DoPT set up a one-member **Deepak Khandekar** committee (**March 2024**).
[135:5:10] - **October 2024**: Centre sent AIS officers serving in Telangana back to AP.
## SUB 6 | Education Provisions
[135:6:0] - **Section 95**, **Part XI** (Access to Higher Education): **equal opportunities** for quality higher education.
[135:6:1] - **Article 371D** admission quotas continue in all higher, technical and medical institutions (government/private, aided/unaided).
[135:6:2] - **Common admission process** to continue for **10 years**.
[135:6:3] - **Section 97**: amended **Article 371D** to cover "**Andhra Pradesh or Telangana**".
[135:6:4] 10 years = common admission (Sec 95) = surplus power right of refusal = common capital.

[135:7:4] SC **2016** = APSCHE ruling; SC **December 2022** = AP's plea on Schedule IX and X assets.

## SUB 0 | Externally Aided Projects (EAPs) – the Basics
[138:0:0] - An **Externally Aided Project (EAP)** is a State project financed by a **multilateral or bilateral lender** (World Bank, ADB, JICA, AIIB, NDB, KfW, UNDP), routed through the Centre.
[138:0:1] - In AP, EAPs cover **agriculture and irrigation**, **roads**, **urban water**, **power transmission**, **health**, **poverty** and the **Amaravati capital**.
[138:0:2] - **Domestic lenders** such as **HUDCO** and **NABARD** are **not** external assistance.
[138:0:3] Multilateral = World Bank, ADB, AIIB, NDB; bilateral = JICA (Japan), KfW (Germany); UN agency = UNDP.
