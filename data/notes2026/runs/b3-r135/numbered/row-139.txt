## SUB 1 | BRGF and the Aspirational Districts Programme
[1:0] - **BRGF** (Ministry of Panchayati Raj, 2006-07): objectives – **bridge critical gaps in local infrastructure** and development, **strengthen panchayat and municipality governance**, give **professional support** to local bodies for planning, improve their performance; all three are objectives.
[1:1] - BRGF covered **250** districts; **13** in undivided AP (9 Telangana + Anantapur, Chittoor, Kadapa, Vizianagaram; not Srikakulam or Hyderabad); **delinked from central support in 2015-16**, folded into the Rashtriya Gram Swaraj Abhiyan (RGSA).
[1:2] - **Aspirational Districts Programme**: **NITI Aayog**, launched by PM Modi in **January 2018**; **112** districts; built on **Convergence, Collaboration, Competition** (not compensation).
[1:3] - **Convergence** = effective use of funds and services of different departments.
[1:4] - Visakhapatnam Economic Region (**10 districts**, 13 Nov 2025) is not part of ADP.
[1:5] ~~ Sources: web page tnrd.tn.gov.in/schemes/cen_brgf_13.html (BRGF aims); app notes 3:139 (old-notes.txt)
## SUB 2 | Tax Incentives and Revenue Compensation
[2:0] - **Section 94(1)**: fiscal measures incl. tax incentives (notified 2015): **35% additional depreciation** + **15% investment allowance** for units set up **1 Apr 2015 – 31 Mar 2020** in backward districts of **both AP and Telangana**.
[2:1] - SCS (refused) would have given a **90% central share in CSS** and tax exemptions.
[2:2] - **101st Amendment, 2016**: GST compensation for revenue loss.
[2:3] - 2014-15 resource-gap grant: **₹3,979.50 crore**; **16th FC** ended RD grants for **all States** (AP loss up to ₹38,000 crore); AP cites the PM's **Rajya Sabha** assurances.
[2:4] - **Polavaram**: Centre limits liability to **old price levels**.

## SUB 3 | Rayalaseema
[3:0] - Now **8 districts**: Kurnool, Nandyal, Anantapur, Sri Sathya Sai, YSR, Annamayya, Tirupati, Chittoor.
[3:1] - **Low population density** due to recurring **droughts and famines**.
[3:2] - **43%** of horticulture area, **71%** of micro-irrigation; six AP districts in India's top 10 for micro-irrigation = five Rayalaseema + **Prakasam**.
[3:3] - **Mega Seed Park**: **Kurnool** district (Tangadancha).
[3:4] - **Sri Bagh Pact (16 Nov 1937)**: irrigation priority to Rayalaseema for 10 years.

## SUB 4 | North Coastal Andhra (Uttarandhra)
[4:0] - "**Kalinga Andhra**", 6 districts: Alluri Sitharama Raju, Anakapalli, Parvathipuram Manyam, Visakhapatnam, Vizianagaram, Srikakulam.
[4:1] - Tribes: **Savara, Jatapu** (Srikakulam side); Gadaba, Bagata, Kotia, Porja, Khond (Araku agency).
[4:2] - **Lowest literacy: ASR (44.67%)**; new **Polavaram** district (HQ **Rampachodavaram**) has the lowest urbanisation and density.
[4:3] - Srikakulam, Vizianagaram: no anchor industries → **distress migration**; ASR **1st** in ADP **Basic Infrastructure** (March 2026).
[4:4] - Projects: Nagavali-Champavathi link via **Thotapalli barrage**; **Bhogapuram airport** (Vizianagaram).
