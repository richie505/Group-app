## SUB 0 | Resource Gap and Revenue Deficit Grant
[0:0] - **Loss of Hyderabad:** with Hyderabad going to Telangana, AP faced a revenue deficit of **more than ₹15,000 crore**.
[0:1] - Mandate: Centre to bridge AP's **resource gap** for the first year, **2014-15**.
[0:2] TABLE head: Figure | Meaning
  [0:2.0] ₹16,078 crore | AP's claimed 2014-15 gap
  [0:2.1] ₹4,117.89 crore | Centre's calculation (standardised accounting, excluding new commitments)
  [0:2.2] ₹3,979.50 crore | Released (2 June 2014 to 2016-17)
[0:3] - The ₹3,979 crore was counted as a revenue deficit grant within the **Special Assistance Measure (SAM, ₹24,350 crore)**.
[0:4] - **14th Finance Commission (2015-20):** post-devolution **Revenue Deficit Grant of ₹22,113 crore** to AP over five years; States' share raised **32% → 42%**; removed the general/special category distinction.
[0:5] - **Special Category Status:** promised in Parliament for **5 years**; denied citing the 14th FC; SAM gives a **90% grant equivalent only for EAPs** (AP says SAM lacks statutory permanence).
[0:6] - **16th FC** discontinued RD grants for **all States** → AP says it lost up to **₹38,000 crore**; revenue deficit about ₹16,000 crore (2014-19) → projected **₹48,000 crore** (2025-26); Centre then sanctioned **₹15,000 crore for Amaravati**.
[0:7] - **SASCI**: 50-year interest-free loans, **outside the 3% of GSDP** fiscal-deficit cap; Centre prefers loans and guarantees, AP wants grants.
[0:8] - **Per capita income gap** after bifurcation: Telangana ₹15,454 vs AP ₹8,979 (figure as quoted in the PYQ, 2015-16).
[0:9] - AP paid **₹32,944 crore** interest in 2024-25.
[0:10] - **White Paper on the Impact of State Reorganisation**: released by CM N. Chandrababu Naidu in **2014**.

## SUB 2 | Disputes, Committees and Power Dues
[2:0] - **Justice D.M. Dharmadhikari Committee**: one-man committee set up by the **Supreme Court** by order of **28 November 2018** to divide **power-utility employees** between AP and Telangana.
[2:1] - **AP's 3-member committee** on division of assets with Telangana: **Y. Ramakrishnudu, K. Atchannaidu, K. Srinivasulu**.
[2:2] - **APGENCO** plants divided by **location**; existing PPAs continue (Twelfth Schedule).
[2:3] - **29 August 2022**: Union Power Ministry ordered Telangana to pay AP **₹6,756.92 crore** (principal ₹3,441.78 crore + surcharge) for power supplied 2 June 2014 – 10 June 2017, within **30 days**.
[2:4] - Telangana counter-claimed **₹12,940 crore**; Telangana High Court set aside the order (19 October 2023); AP appealed to the Supreme Court.
