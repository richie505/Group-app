## SUB 1 | Central Releases and Funding for Amaravati
[1:0] - Direct grants under Sec 94(3): **₹2,500 crore** = **₹1,500 crore** initial (capital buildings) + **₹1,000 crore** later (counted as the 2017-18 special assistance for the capital).
[1:1] - The ₹1,000 crore went to **underground drainage** works in **Vijayawada and Guntur**.
[1:2] - Finance Ministry: balance ₹1,000 crore only after **utilisation certificates (UCs)** for the ₹1,500 crore; AP (2018) said **NITI Aayog** had not rejected its UCs.
[1:3] - Cost gap: AP's estimate about **₹1.2 lakh crore** vs Centre's commitment about ₹27,000 crore.
[1:4] - **Union Budget 2024-25**: **₹15,000 crore** for Amaravati, as **Centre-facilitated multilateral** support (World Bank + ADB, 0.6% + TONA).
