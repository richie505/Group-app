=== The Act Commitment and South Coast Railway
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Where | **Thirteenth Schedule** (Infrastructure), AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 |
| Wording | Indian Railways shall, **within six months** from the appointed day, **examine** establishing a **new railway zone** in AP and take an expeditious decision |
| Telangana item | Railways to examine a **Rail Coach Factory** |
| Constitutional basis | Railways = Union subject, **Entry 22, List I** |
| How a zone is made | Executive decision + gazette notification of the Ministry of Railways |
# Timeline of South Coast Railway
| When | Step |

> AP = railway zone (Schedule 13); Telangana = Rail Coach Factory. "Examine", not "establish".

=== Divisions, Waltair Split and the K-K Line
- SCoR has **4 divisions**: **Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Guntur, Guntakal**; Vijayawada, Guntur and Guntakal came from **South Central Railway**, which shrinks to **three divisions**.
- **South Central Railway** itself began functioning on **2 October 1966**.
| Part | Goes to |
|---|---|
| Andhra part of Waltair | Visakhapatnam division of SCoR |
| Rest of Waltair | New **Rayagada** division of **East Coast Railway** (HQ **Bhubaneswar**) |
- **Kottavalasa-Kirandul (K-K) line** = "revenue lifeline" of Waltair; Odisha claimed it; AP argued Waltair earned more than **Vijayawada, Guntur and Guntakal combined**; AP offered only Odisha stretches.
- A dedicated zone helps **VCIC** by cutting freight costs and improving port connectivity.
