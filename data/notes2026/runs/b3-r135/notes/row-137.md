=== Act Sections and Committees on the Capital
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| **5** | **Hyderabad** = common capital for a period **not exceeding 10 years** (GHMC area); then capital of Telangana and a new capital for AP |
| **6** | Centre's **Expert Committee** to study alternatives for AP's new capital, report within **six months** |
| **94(3)** | **Central Government** to give **special financial support** for essential facilities in the new capital: Raj Bhavan, High Court, Secretariat, Legislative Assembly, Legislative Council and other essential infrastructure |
| **94(4)** | Centre to facilitate the capital by **denotifying degraded forest land**, if required |
| 95 | Not the capital – higher-education quotas (Part XI) |
# Committees on the capital
| Committee | Set up by / Chair | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Expert Committee (Sec 6) | Centre; **K.C. Sivaramakrishnan** | Reported Aug 2014; gave **loose options**, no firm site; opposed a single "super capital" (ecological fears) |
| Capital City Advisory Committee | AP government; **P. Narayana** | Backed the Vijayawada-Guntur region |
| G.N. Rao Committee + BCG study | AP government | Basis of the three-capitals plan (2020) |
- The **AP Cabinet** (not the Centre) selected **Amaravati** on the **Krishna**, between Vijayawada and Guntur.
> APPSC key once marked "Section 95" for the capital-support provision; the Act's provision is **Section 94(3)**.

=== Central Releases and Funding for Amaravati
- Direct grants under Sec 94(3): **₹2,500 crore** = **₹1,500 crore** initial (capital buildings) + **₹1,000 crore** later (counted as the 2017-18 special assistance for the capital).
- The ₹1,000 crore went to **underground drainage** works in **Vijayawada and Guntur**.
- Finance Ministry: balance ₹1,000 crore only after **utilisation certificates (UCs)** for the ₹1,500 crore; AP (2018) said **NITI Aayog** had not rejected its UCs.
- Cost gap: AP's estimate about **₹1.2 lakh crore** vs Centre's commitment about ₹27,000 crore.
- **Union Budget 2024-25**: **₹15,000 crore** for Amaravati, as **Centre-facilitated multilateral** support (World Bank + ADB, 0.6% + TONA).
| Lender | Amount |
|---|---|
| World Bank + ADB | ₹15,000 crore |
| HUDCO | ₹11,000 crore term loan |
| NaBFID | ₹7,500 crore |
| NABARD (NIDA) | ₹7,387.70 crore |
- Dispute: AP wants **non-repayable grants**; Centre gives **loans with sovereign guarantees** (**Article 293(2)**) → repayment burdens may crowd out welfare spending; AP debt projected **₹7.11 lakh crore** (~**36% of GSDP**).

=== Amaravati Project – Timeline
- **APCRDA Act, 2014** in force **30 December 2014**; Land Pooling Scheme Jan 2015; consent for about **33,000 acres** by March 2015; original **29 villages**.
- Pooling farmers exempt from **capital gains tax and stamp duty** on first sale.
- **22 October 2015**: PM **Narendra Modi** laid the foundation stone at **Uddandarayunipalem**.
| Three capitals (2020) | City |
|---|---|
| Legislative | Amaravati |
| Executive | Visakhapatnam |
| Judicial | Kurnool |
- **3 March 2022**: AP High Court struck down the CRDA Repeal Act (2020).
- **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Act, 2026**: Amaravati = **sole and permanent capital**, retrospective from 2 June 2024.
