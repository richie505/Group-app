=== Resource Gap and Revenue Deficit Grant
- **Loss of Hyderabad:** with Hyderabad going to Telangana, AP faced a revenue deficit of **more than ₹15,000 crore**.
- Mandate: Centre to bridge AP's **resource gap** for the first year, **2014-15**.
| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ₹16,078 crore | AP's claimed 2014-15 gap |
| ₹4,117.89 crore | Centre's calculation (standardised accounting, excluding new commitments) |
| ₹3,979.50 crore | Released (2 June 2014 to 2016-17) |
- The ₹3,979 crore was counted as a revenue deficit grant within the **Special Assistance Measure (SAM, ₹24,350 crore)**.
- **14th Finance Commission (2015-20):** post-devolution **Revenue Deficit Grant of ₹22,113 crore** to AP over five years; States' share raised **32% → 42%**; removed the general/special category distinction.
- **Special Category Status:** promised in Parliament for **5 years**; denied citing the 14th FC; SAM gives a **90% grant equivalent only for EAPs** (AP says SAM lacks statutory permanence).
- **16th FC** discontinued RD grants for **all States** → AP says it lost up to **₹38,000 crore**; revenue deficit about ₹16,000 crore (2014-19) → projected **₹48,000 crore** (2025-26); Centre then sanctioned **₹15,000 crore for Amaravati**.
- **SASCI**: 50-year interest-free loans, **outside the 3% of GSDP** fiscal-deficit cap; Centre prefers loans and guarantees, AP wants grants.
- **Per capita income gap** after bifurcation: Telangana ₹15,454 vs AP ₹8,979 (figure as quoted in the PYQ, 2015-16).
- AP paid **₹32,944 crore** interest in 2024-25.
- **White Paper on the Impact of State Reorganisation**: released by CM N. Chandrababu Naidu in **2014**.

=== Tax Arrears, Refunds, Deferred Taxes and Debt
| Item | Basis | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Arrears of property tax / land revenue | State where property lies | 50 |
| Arrears of other taxes | **Place of assessment** | **50** |
| **Deferred taxes** of industrial units (a loan to the unit) | State where the **unit is located** | **51(1)** |
| Refund of property tax collected in excess | State where property lies | 56 |
| Refund of other taxes | **Population ratio 58.32 : 41.68** | 56 |
| Public debt and Public Account | Population ratio; re-lent loans stay with the State of the borrower | 54 |
| Floating loans not for one State | Population ratio | 55 |
| Pensions | Population ratio **58.32 : 41.68** (Eighth Schedule) | 59 |
| Provident Fund | State of **permanent allotment** | 58 |
- **Population ratio** is used most often for dividing common **assets** (cash, securities, HQ, outside property) and for debts; land and undertakings go by location.
- Oddity: arrears follow assessment (mostly **Hyderabad**), refunds follow population → cost to AP about **₹3,900 crore** (14th FC).
- **Section 47(4)**: disputes settled by **Central order on the advice of the CAG** (the CAG only advises).
- AP took **58% of debt** but got **46% of revenue** assets.

=== Disputes, Committees and Power Dues
- **Justice D.M. Dharmadhikari Committee**: one-man committee set up by the **Supreme Court** by order of **28 November 2018** to divide **power-utility employees** between AP and Telangana.
- **AP's 3-member committee** on division of assets with Telangana: **Y. Ramakrishnudu, K. Atchannaidu, K. Srinivasulu**.
- **APGENCO** plants divided by **location**; existing PPAs continue (Twelfth Schedule).
- **29 August 2022**: Union Power Ministry ordered Telangana to pay AP **₹6,756.92 crore** (principal ₹3,441.78 crore + surcharge) for power supplied 2 June 2014 – 10 June 2017, within **30 days**.
- Telangana counter-claimed **₹12,940 crore**; Telangana High Court set aside the order (19 October 2023); AP appealed to the Supreme Court.
