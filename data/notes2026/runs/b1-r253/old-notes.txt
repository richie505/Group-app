## SUB 1 | Composition – Cabinet Mission Scheme
[253:1:0] - **Provincial seats** split among **General, Muslim, Sikh** roughly in proportion to population.
[253:1:1] - Each community's members elected by its own members in the provincial assembly by **proportional representation – single transferable vote**.
[253:1:2] - **Princely states**: members **nominated by rulers**; seats initially unfilled.
[253:1:3] - **Not members**: **Gandhi and Jinnah**.
[253:1:4] - **15 women** members (e.g. Durgabai Deshmukh, Sarojini Naidu, Hansa Mehta).
[253:1:5] # Numbers
[253:1:6] - Total strength **389**; fell to **299** after League members from areas that went to Pakistan withdrew.

## SUB 5 | Status and Criticisms
[253:5:0] # Indian Independence Act, 1947
[253:5:1] - Made it a **fully sovereign body** with two roles:
[253:5:2] TABLE head: Role | Chaired by
  [253:5:2.0] Constitution-making body | Rajendra Prasad
  [253:5:2.1] Dominion Legislature (first Parliament) | G.V. Mavalankar
[253:5:3] - Continued as the **Provisional Parliament till 1952**.
[253:5:4] # Criticisms
[253:5:5] - **Not elected on adult franchise** – indirect election under the **1935 Act's** restricted franchise (tax, property, education).
[253:5:6] - **Congress dominance** ("Congress Assembly Party"); Hindu and lawyer dominance.
[253:5:7] - **Socialist Party** National Executive (1946) – demanded **dissolution and re-election by universal adult suffrage**; leaders **Jayaprakash Narayan, Ram Manohar Lohia** (RPSC 2024).

[254:0:0] - PM **15 Aug 1947** till death **27 May 1964** (interim government + 1952, 1957, 1962 elections); seat **Phulpur**.
[254:0:1] - Books: **The Discovery of India** (written in **Ahmednagar Fort** jail), Glimpses of World History, Letters from a Father to His Daughter, A Bunch of Old Letters (1958, edited letters).
[254:0:2] - Influences: **Fabian socialism**, **British liberalism**, **Gandhi**, **Soviet planning** – not German nationalism.
