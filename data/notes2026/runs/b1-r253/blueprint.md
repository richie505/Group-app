## 5. Keys to watch
- UPPSC Mains 2005 sessions: the printed options (7/9/12/15) drop the exam's "11"; Laxmikanth and NextGen both say 11 sessions – option added. "12" counts the 24 Jan 1950 signing meeting.
- MPPSC 2006 "China occupied Tibet": NCERT (OnlyIAS) says 1950; 1959 is the uprising and the Dalai Lama's flight – option added.
- UP UDA 2001 "Swaraj Party in 1935": the Swaraj Party's demand was May 1934 (Rocket Polity); the option is kept as the only right party.
- UPPCS 2007 chronology assumes Chou En-lai's April 1960 visit (he also came in 1954 and 1956-57).
- Many recall keys (Glimpses, A Bunch of Old Letters, Mundaka, 24 spokes, Krishna Menon, Rabindra Bharati) rest on general knowledge – no library file states them.
