=== Nehru – Profile, Books and Ideas
- PM from **15 Aug 1947** till his death on **27 May 1964**; Lok Sabha seat **Phulpur**.
| Book | Note |
|---|---|
| **Glimpses of World History** | Letters to Indira from prison (1934) |
| **The Discovery of India** | Written in **Ahmednagar Fort** jail (1944) |
| **A Bunch of Old Letters** | **1958** – edited collection of letters written **to and by Nehru** |
| Letters from a Father to His Daughter; An Autobiography | – |
- **Influences:** **Fabian socialism**, **British liberalism**, **Gandhi**, Soviet planning – **not German nationalism** (UKPSC 2006).
- **Nehru vs Gandhi:** both stood for truth, non-violence and against untouchability; Nehru alone favoured **heavy industrialisation**, science and central planning – Gandhi favoured a decentralised village and cottage-industry economy (UPPCS 2006).
- **Quotes:** "**Success** often **comes to those who dare** and act" – Nehru (AP SI 2018); "Everything else can wait, but not agriculture" – Nehru.
- Prison link table: Ahmednagar Fort – Nehru; Yerwada – Gandhi; Mandalay – Tilak; Cellular Jail – Savarkar.
> Not German nationalism; heavy industry = Nehru, not Gandhi; A Bunch of Old Letters = 1958.

=== Chronology of the Nehru Years and the China War
| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| **Air India nationalised** (Air India International + Indian Airlines) | **1953** |
| Panchsheel (Nehru – Zhou Enlai) | 29 Apr 1954 |
| **Imperial Bank → State Bank of India** | **1 July 1955** |
| **Khrushchev and Bulganin visit India** | Nov–Dec **1955** |
| **First elected Communist government** (Kerala, E.M.S. Namboodiripad) | **1957** |
| **Dalai Lama flees to India** | **March 1959** |
| **Zhou Enlai's (Chou En-lai) Delhi visit** for border talks | **April 1960** |
| **Goa liberated** – Operation Vijay | **December 1961** |
| India–China war | Oct–Nov **1962** |
- **China took over / annexed Tibet in 1950**; in 1959 it crushed the Tibetan uprising and the Dalai Lama sought asylum in India (MPPSC 2006 printed 1959–1962 only).
- **1962 war:** causes – **Aksai Chin road**, McMahon Line, the Dalai Lama's asylum, the Forward Policy; **Emergency 26 Oct 1962**; China's unilateral ceasefire **21 Nov 1962**.
- **Defence Minister in 1962 = V.K. Krishna Menon** – resigned after the debacle (CDS 2007); Y.B. Chavan succeeded.
> 1953 Air India → 1955 SBI, Khrushchev → 1957 Kerala → 1959 Dalai Lama → 1960 Zhou → 1961 Goa → 1962 war.

=== Planning – Bodies and Five Year Plans
| Body | Set up | Note |
|---|---|---|
| National Planning Committee | 1938 | Congress, chaired by Nehru |
| **Planning Commission** | **15 Mar 1950** | **Executive resolution**; PM chairman; first Dy Chairman **Gulzarilal Nanda**, last **Montek Singh Ahluwalia** |
| **National Development Council** | **6 Aug 1952** | Final approval of Plans |
| **NITI Aayog** | **1 Jan 2015** | Replaced the Planning Commission; Vice-Chairperson **Ashok Kumar Lahiri** (from May 2026) |
# Five Year Plans
| Plan | Model | Priority / outcome |
|---|---|---|
| First (**1951**-56) | **Harrod-Domar** | Agriculture; growth **3.6%** vs target 2.1% |
| Second (1956-61) | **Mahalanobis** – "Mahalanobis Plan" | **Heavy industry**, socialist pattern of society; food shortages and inflation |
| Third (1961-66) | Sandy-Chakravarty; "**Gadgil Yojana**" | Self-reliance; upset by the 1962 and 1965 wars |
- **Era of Five Year Plans began in 1951** (First Plan from 1 April 1951) (APPSC FRO 2022).
- **Second Plan = Mahalanobis Plan** (NDA 2015) – not Harrod-Domar (First) or the People's Plan (M.N. Roy, 1945).
- **Plan Holiday 1966-69**: three annual plans.
> 1950 Planning Commission; 1951 First Plan; 1952 NDC; Second = Mahalanobis; Third = Gadgil Yojana.

=== Foreign Policy – Panchsheel, Bandung and Non-Alignment
| Event | Year | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral Nations Repatriation Commission (Korea) | 1953 | India chaired; **Gen. K.S. Thimayya** |
| **Panchsheel** | **29 Apr 1954** | Nehru + **Zhou Enlai**, in the agreement on **trade with Tibet**; **Burma** the first other country to adopt it |
| **Bandung** Afro-Asian Conference | **1955** | Sponsored by the **Colombo Powers** (India, Indonesia, Burma, Ceylon, Pakistan) |
| Suez crisis | 1956 | Nehru condemned the **Anglo-French-Israeli** attack on Egypt |
| **NAM** founded | **1961** | **Belgrade** |
- Panchsheel principles: mutual respect for sovereignty, **non-aggression**, non-interference, equality and mutual benefit, peaceful co-existence – **no collective military defence**.
> Panchsheel 1954 → Bandung 1955 → Dalai Lama 1959 → Belgrade NAM 1961.

=== Secularism, the Hindu Code and Language
- Indian secularism: **no State religion**; the State is neutral but **may intervene to reform social practices** (unlike strict Western separation).
- **Art. 30** – minority educational institutions; **Art. 343** – Hindi in Devanagari, English to continue for **15 years**.
- **Ambedkar** – first **Law Minister** (1947); resigned **27 Sep 1951** over the stalled **Hindu Code Bill**.
- **Hindu Marriage Act 1955**; Succession, Minority and Guardianship, Adoptions and Maintenance Acts **1956**.
