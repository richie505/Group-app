=== New Current Affairs: 5–14 Nov 2026
# Defence tests and platforms
- **Pralay user-evaluation trial (5 Nov 2026):** flight-tested at about 11:10 am from **Dr APJ Abdul Kalam Island**, off **Odisha**; all objectives met (tracked by **ITR Chandipur**); MoD says **induction is imminent**. Developed by **Research Centre Imarat (RCI), Hyderabad** with other DRDO labs; produced by **BDL**; **short-range surface-to-surface quasi-ballistic** missile, **solid propellant**, range **150–500 km**, payload **500–1,000 kg**.
- **Amini delivered (6 Nov 2026):** indigenous **Anti-Submarine Warfare Shallow Water Craft (ASW-SWC)** built by **GRSE, Kolkata**; named after **Amini Island (Lakshadweep)**, reviving the name of a **Petya III-class** corvette; **80%+ indigenous**; **waterjet propulsion**; lightweight torpedoes, indigenous rocket launchers, sonar; built to **Indian Register of Shipping** rules. The ASW-SWC programme = **16 ships**: **8 Arnala-class (GRSE)** + **8 Mahe-class (Cochin Shipyard)**.
# Space and astronomy
- **Voyager 1** (NASA, launched **5 Sep 1977**) reaches **one light-day** from Earth (about **26 billion km**) on **18 Nov 2026** – the **first human-made object** to do so; it became the most distant object on **17 Feb 1998** (passing Pioneer 10) and entered **interstellar space in August 2012**. **Voyager 2** = only craft to visit **Uranus and Neptune**. *(news 4 Nov 2026)*
- **NASA's Swift (Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory)**, launched **2004** to study **gamma-ray bursts**, **re-entered on 4 Nov 2026** over **North Sumatra, Indonesia** after **22 years** (design life 2 years; no propulsion; rescue by the **LINK** vehicle was abandoned in August).
- **2026 QQ8:** a ~**1-metre** asteroid captured by Earth's gravity as a temporary **"mini-moon"**, expected to stay until **December 2026**; discovered **20 Aug 2026** by **Krisztián Sárneczky**.
- **Teghaza 001:** a **4.1-billion-year-old Martian meteorite** (pink rock) found in the **Sahara, Mali**, named after the **Teghaza salt plain** – the **oldest piece of Mars** found on Earth; holds several minerals formed from molten rock.
- **Cosmoserve Space "Base 1"** (Hyderabad, 5 Nov 2026): **active space-debris removal and in-orbit servicing** facility at **Gachibowli** (~14,800 sq ft), inaugurated virtually by **Dr Jitendra Singh**; vision "**debris-free space by 2030**"; its **Mission EMBRACE** tested soft robotic petals in space (July 2026); India's space economy about **US$9 billion**, ~**450** space start-ups.
- **COSMOS-Mysuru Planetarium** (inaugurated by **PM Modi**, **13 Nov 2026**): **India's first LED dome planetarium**, Mysuru, Karnataka – with the **Indian Institute of Astrophysics** and the **University of Mysore**.
# Health and biotech
- **WHO Global TB Report 2026 (13 Nov):** India's TB **mortality down 38% since 2015** (global **35%**) – from **28 to 18 per lakh** (2015→2025); **94% treatment success** for drug-susceptible TB; **67,933 panchayats TB-free (2025)**; **96%** of programme funding is domestic.
- **BPaL patent rejected:** the **Indian Patent Office** rejected the **TB Alliance** (US) application for the combination **bedaquiline + pretomanid + linezolid** – a **6-month all-oral WHO regimen for drug-resistant TB**; **BPaLM** adds **moxifloxacin** (success **89%** vs 52–75% for standard regimens); opens the way for **generic competition**. *(news 6 Nov 2026)*
- **Kochon Prize 2026:** the NGO **REACH** (Resource group for Education and Advocacy for Community Health) – **first Indian civil-society non-profit** to win it; shared with Kenya's Centre for Health Solutions and the **Marshall Islands** TB programme; given by Korea's **Kochon Foundation** with the **Stop TB Partnership**.
- **Ebola – AP alert (AP angle):** Andhra Pradesh stepped up **preventive measures** under Union Health Ministry guidelines after outbreaks in **DR Congo** and **Uganda**. Ebola: genus **Orthoebolavirus**, first identified **1976** (South Sudan and DRC), named after the **Ebola River** (near Yambuku, **DRC**); a **viral haemorrhagic fever**, **zoonotic**, reservoir likely **fruit bats**; spreads by **direct contact**, not normally airborne; infectious only **after symptoms**.
- **PJ.2.1:** new **SARS-CoV-2** lineage, first identified in **Ontario, Canada (2 May 2026)**; **25.2%** of Ontario sequences (20 Sep–17 Oct 2026); spike-protein mutations, no unique symptoms; **LP.8.1-adapted vaccine** antibodies still neutralise it.
- **StopBleed:** **nano-biopolymer haemostatic powder** by **Sabir Hussain** (Miracules Medsolutions, **West Bengal**) – stops severe bleeding in **60 seconds**; room-temperature shelf life **3 years**; **CDSCO-approved Class C medical device**; top honour at **Slingshot 2026 (Singapore)**.
- **Anti-cancer drug prices:** **NPPA** capped **trade margins at 30% of MRP** (plus GST) for **137 non-scheduled anti-cancer medicines** – order **17 Oct 2026**, revised MRPs from **9 Nov 2026**; savings about **₹3,541 crore a year**; biggest cut **96.45%**; under **Para 19 of DPCO 2013** (extraordinary powers), the **Trade Margin Rationalisation** route (2019 TMR covered **42** such drugs). NPPA: set up **1997**, under the **Department of Pharmaceuticals**.
# Semiconductors, cyber security, fusion and ocean science
- **Semicon 2.0 portal (6 Nov 2026):** launched by **Ashwini Vaishnaw** for applications under **Semicon 2.0** (₹1,27,500 crore, six pillars); **12 more ECMS approvals** – **₹7,503 crore** investment, **7,127 jobs**, 7 States; ECMS total **118 approvals**, **₹77,051 crore**, **16 States**.
- **CERT-In AI security guidelines:** first cybersecurity **auditing guidelines for AI systems**; reportable AI-related incidents to be disclosed within **six hours**; AI systems in **four risk classes – low, moderate, high, critical**. CERT-In: **2004**, under **MeitY**, **Section 70B of the IT Act, 2000**. *(news 5 Nov 2026)*
- **Tokamak:** magnetic-confinement **fusion** device holding **plasma** in a ring-shaped (toroidal) chamber; no tokamak has yet produced **net energy**; D–T fusion releases **17.6 MeV** (about **80% to the neutron**). India's tokamaks at the **Institute for Plasma Research, Gandhinagar**: **ADITYA** (first, **1989**; rebuilt **ADITYA-U**, 2017) and **SST-1** (superconducting, first plasma **2013**). A **stellarator** twists the field with external coils only. *(news 6 Nov 2026)*
- **INCOIS–Vignan's University–Ananth Technologies MoU (AP angle):** tripartite MoU with **Vignan's University (Guntur)** and Hyderabad's **ATPL** for ocean science, satellite data and the **Blue Economy**, incl. **Potential Fishing Zone (PFZ)** advisories; **INCOIS**: est. **1999**, **Ministry of Earth Sciences**, HQ **Hyderabad**. *(news 3 Nov 2026)*
> ⚠ Trap: Pralay is **quasi-ballistic** (not a cruise missile) and was developed by **RCI Hyderabad**; Voyager 1 – not Voyager 2 – reaches one light-day; the NPPA cap is **30% trade margin**, not a 30% price cut; INCOIS is under **MoES**, not ISRO; ADITYA (1989) – not SST-1 – was India's first tokamak.
