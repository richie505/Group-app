=== All India Services – Article 312
# Creation
- **Art. 312:** Parliament may create a new AIS if the **Rajya Sabha** declares it necessary in the national interest by a resolution supported by **2/3 of members present and voting** – not "2/3 of total members". It expressly mentions an **All India Judicial Service**.
- **Council of States' exclusive powers:** recommend **creation of a new AIS (Art. 312)**; authorise Parliament to legislate on a **State List** subject (**Art. 249**).
- **IAS and IPS** are **deemed to be services created by Parliament** (Art. 312(2)); governed by the **All India Services Act, 1951**; created by **Parliament**, not the UPSC, Home Ministry or courts.
- Existing AIS = **IAS, IPS, Indian Forest Service (IFoS, 1966)** – all three.
- **ICS → IAS** (1947); **Sardar Patel** – "**Father of All India Services**", called them the "**Steel Frame**".
# Recruitment and control
- Recruited by the **UPSC** on an all-India basis; trained by the Centre; serve in **State cadres**.
- **Appointment** by the **President** (on UPSC recommendation) – not Governors, the UPSC itself or SPSCs.
- **Dismissal / removal only by the Union Government**, even while serving a State; officers can be **transferred** and serve both Union and States.
- **Uniform service** across India = a device of **national integration** (not caste or religious associations).
# Classify the service
| Service | Category | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| **IAS, IPS, IFoS (Forest)** | All India Services | Common to Union and States |
| IFS (Foreign), IRS, IA&AS, Indian Economic Service, Indian Statistical Service | Central Services | Union subjects only |
| SPSC-recruited services | State Services | State subjects |
- **⚠ Trap:** **IPS is an AIS, not a Central Service**; Indian Forest Service (AIS) ≠ Indian Foreign Service (Central).
# Federal critique and commissions
- **Main criticism:** AIS are **incompatible with the federal system** / State autonomy – Central rules govern them and States lack full control.
- **Sarkaria Commission:** the AIS should be **further strengthened and more such services created** (e.g. medicine, engineering, education).
- **Rajamannar Committee:** **abolish** the AIS (IAS, IPS, IFoS).
- **IAS (Cadre) Rules, 1954** amendment proposal (2022): **central deputation** without the consent of the **State or the officer**.

=== Directions to States – Articles 256, 257 and 365
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **256** | State executive power must ensure **compliance with Parliament's laws** and existing laws; Union may direct |
| **257(1)** | State executive power **must not impede or prejudice** the Union's executive power |
| 257(2), (3) | Union may direct on **means of communication of national or military importance** and **protection of railways** |
| 258 / 258A | Entrustment of functions (both ways) |
| 261 | Full faith and credit to public acts, records, judicial proceedings |
| 262 | Inter-State water disputes |
| 263 | Inter-State Council |
| **365** | If a State fails to comply with Union directions, the **President may hold** that its government cannot be carried on per the Constitution → **President's Rule (Art. 356)** |
- **Union executive can direct States only on certain matters and occasions** – neither "never" nor "without constraint"; during a **National Emergency** it may direct on **any matter**.
- **⚠ Trap:** Art. 257 directions cover communications and railways, not public order; 355 and 356 are emergency articles.

=== Delegation of Functions – Articles 258 and 258A
| Article | Who entrusts | To whom | Consent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 258(1) | **President** | State govt or its officers | **With State consent** |
| 258(2) | **Parliament by law** | State govt or its officers | **Without consent** |
| **258A** (7th Amendment, 1956) | **Governor** | Union govt or its officers | **With the Centre's consent** |
- Mutual delegation is of **executive** functions only (legislative power is not delegated this way); purpose: avoid **rigidity and deadlock**.
- So the **Governor can delegate State executive functions to the Centre**, but the **President cannot delegate** Union executive functions **without the State's consent**.

=== Inter-State Council and Commissions on Centre-State Relations
# Inter-State Council – Article 263
- **Enabling** provision: the **President** may establish the Council by order.
- Set up on **28 May 1990** by **Presidential order**, on the **Sarkaria Commission's** recommendation (Sarkaria called it the "**Inter-Governmental Council**").
- **Not the first to suggest it:** the **ARC (1969)** and **Rajamannar Committee (1971)** recommended an ISC earlier.
- **Not related:** the **69th Amendment** (Delhi NCT).
| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Chairman | **Prime Minister** |
| Members | CMs of all States, UT Administrators / CMs, **six** Union Cabinet Ministers nominated by the PM |
| Decision method | **Consensus**; recommendations **not binding** |
| Standing Committee | Chaired by the **Union Home Minister** |
| Secretariat | Inter-State Council Secretariat (also serves Zonal Councils) |
- **Bifurcation disputes:** ISC + **Apex Council** under the **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014**.
# Commissions
| Body | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **ARC (1966)** | **Morarji Desai**, then **K. Hanumanthaiya**; M.C. Setalvad study team (1969); urged an **Inter-State Council**, non-partisan Governors |
| **Rajamannar (1969, TN govt; report 1971)** | ISC immediately; residuary powers to States; **omit Arts 356, 357, 365**; abolish AIS |
| **Sarkaria (June 1983, report 1988)** | Justice R.S. Sarkaria, B. Sivaraman, S.R. Sen; **247** recommendations; **permanent ISC**; Art. 356 sparingly; strengthen AIS |
| **Punchhi (2007, report 2010)** | Governor removable by a **State Legislature resolution**, like the **impeachment of the President** |
- **Art. 357(2):** a Parliamentary law made for a State under President's Rule **continues after PR** until the **State Legislature** alters, repeals or **re-enacts** it – so re-enactment is allowed.
- **Art. 249** resolution = **Rajya Sabha only**, not the Lok Sabha.

=== Zonal Councils and North-Eastern Council
# Zonal Councils
- **Statutory** bodies under **Part III (Sections 15-22)** of the **States Reorganisation Act, 1956** (which created **14 States, 6 UTs**) – not constitutional.
- **5 councils:** Northern, Central, **Eastern**, Western, Southern; proposed by **Nehru** (1956).
- **Chairman:** **Union Home Minister** (all five); **Vice-chairman:** CMs by **annual rotation**.
- Members: **CM + two other ministers** of each State; Administrators / LGs of UTs.
- Advisers (**no vote**): NITI Aayog nominee, **Chief Secretaries**, Development Commissioners.
- **Deliberative and advisory**; advice **not binding**.
| Council | States |
|---|---|
| **Eastern** | Bihar, Jharkhand, Odisha, West Bengal (+ **Sikkim**, added after 1975) |
| **Southern** | **Andhra Pradesh**, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala, Telangana, Puducherry (not Goa) |
- **Sikkim:** placed in the **Eastern** zone; added to the **North-Eastern Council in 2002**, and MHA has begun excluding it from the Eastern Zonal Council (recent EZC meetings: 4 States).
- **31st Southern Zonal Council: 20 Aug 2026**, near **Mamallapuram**; chaired by **Amit Shah**; vice-chairman TN CM **C. Joseph Vijay**.
# North-Eastern Council
- Separate **NEC Act, 1971**; **Sikkim added 2002** (8th member); since **2018** the **Union Home Minister** is ex-officio chairman.

=== Armed Forces, Public Service Commissions and Tribunals
- **Art. 355:** Union's duty to protect States; it may deploy armed / central forces **with or without State consent**.
- Basis: **Union Entry 2A** (**42nd Amendment**); States object because **public order** and **police** are **State Entries 1 and 2**.
- **CAPF dispute (Mar 2026):** Centre has not implemented the **2025 SC order** (Organised Group A status for CAPF officers, less IPS deputation); a **Central Armed Forces (General Administration) Bill** is expected.
- **Art. 315:** UPSC; an SPSC for each State; a **JSPSC** for 2+ States if their **legislatures so resolve** – created by an **Act of Parliament**, members appointed by the **President**.
- **UPSC** replaced the **Federal PSC** on **26 Jan 1950**.
- **Art. 323A** (**42nd Amendment, 1976**): administrative tribunals for Union and States; **CAT** (1985) – Chairman + 69 Members (34 judicial, 35 administrative).

=== Inter-State Water Disputes – Article 262
- **Art. 262:** Parliament provides by law for adjudication of inter-State river disputes (and may bar courts) → **Inter-State River Water Disputes Act, 1956**.
| Tribunal | Constituted | AP angle |
|---|---|---|
| Godavari | **1969** (award 1980) | Maharashtra, AP, Karnataka, MP, Odisha |
| Krishna-I (**R.S. Bachawat**) | 1969 | Maharashtra, AP, Karnataka |
| Krishna-II | **2004** (report 30 Dec 2010) | AP a party |
| **Vansadhara** | 24 Feb 2010 | AP and **Odisha** |
- AP = **Southern Zonal Council**; party to **Godavari, Krishna and Vansadhara** tribunals.
- **Mullaperiyar** dam (1895) is on the **Periyar** (not Godavari), in **Kerala**, operated by **Tamil Nadu**; SC Supervisory Committee involves the **National Dam Safety Authority** (Dam Safety Act, 2021).
- **Mahanadi:** Odisha vs Chhattisgarh back in focus (Aug 2026).
