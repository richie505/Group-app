## SUB 0 | All India Services – Article 312
[0:0] # Creation
[0:1] - **Art. 312:** Parliament may create a new AIS if the **Rajya Sabha** declares it necessary in the national interest by a resolution supported by **2/3 of members present and voting** – not "2/3 of total members". It expressly mentions an **All India Judicial Service**.
[0:2] - **Council of States' exclusive powers:** recommend **creation of a new AIS (Art. 312)**; authorise Parliament to legislate on a **State List** subject (**Art. 249**).
[0:3] - **IAS and IPS** are **deemed to be services created by Parliament** (Art. 312(2)); governed by the **All India Services Act, 1951**; created by **Parliament**, not the UPSC, Home Ministry or courts.
[0:4] - Existing AIS = **IAS, IPS, Indian Forest Service (IFoS, 1966)** – all three.
[0:5] - **ICS → IAS** (1947); **Sardar Patel** – "**Father of All India Services**", called them the "**Steel Frame**".
[0:6] # Recruitment and control
[0:7] - Recruited by the **UPSC** on an all-India basis; trained by the Centre; serve in **State cadres**.
[0:8] - **Appointment** by the **President** (on UPSC recommendation) – not Governors, the UPSC itself or SPSCs.
[0:9] - **Dismissal / removal only by the Union Government**, even while serving a State; officers can be **transferred** and serve both Union and States.
[0:10] - **Uniform service** across India = a device of **national integration** (not caste or religious associations).
[0:11] # Classify the service
[0:12] TABLE head: Service | Category | Nature
  [0:12.0] IAS, IPS, IFoS (Forest) | All India Services | Common to Union and States
  [0:12.1] IFS (Foreign), IRS, IA&AS, Indian Economic Service, Indian Statistical Service | Central Services | Union subjects only
  [0:12.2] SPSC-recruited services | State Services | State subjects
[0:13] - **⚠ Trap:** **IPS is an AIS, not a Central Service**; Indian Forest Service (AIS) ≠ Indian Foreign Service (Central).
[0:14] # Federal critique and commissions
[0:15] - **Main criticism:** AIS are **incompatible with the federal system** / State autonomy – Central rules govern them and States lack full control.
[0:16] - **Sarkaria Commission:** the AIS should be **further strengthened and more such services created** (e.g. medicine, engineering, education).
[0:17] - **Rajamannar Committee:** **abolish** the AIS (IAS, IPS, IFoS).
[0:18] - **IAS (Cadre) Rules, 1954** amendment proposal (2022): **central deputation** without the consent of the **State or the officer**.

## SUB 2 | Delegation of Functions – Articles 258 and 258A
[2:0] TABLE head: Article | Who entrusts | To whom | Consent
  [2:0.0] 258(1) | President | State govt or its officers | With State consent
  [2:0.1] 258(2) | Parliament by law | State govt or its officers | Without consent
  [2:0.2] 258A (7th Amendment, 1956) | Governor | Union govt or its officers | With the Centre's consent
[2:1] - Mutual delegation is of **executive** functions only (legislative power is not delegated this way); purpose: avoid **rigidity and deadlock**.
[2:2] - So the **Governor can delegate State executive functions to the Centre**, but the **President cannot delegate** Union executive functions **without the State's consent**.

## SUB 3 | Inter-State Council and Commissions on Centre-State Relations
[3:0] # Inter-State Council – Article 263
[3:1] - **Enabling** provision: the **President** may establish the Council by order.
[3:2] - Set up on **28 May 1990** by **Presidential order**, on the **Sarkaria Commission's** recommendation (Sarkaria called it the "**Inter-Governmental Council**").
[3:3] - **Not the first to suggest it:** the **ARC (1969)** and **Rajamannar Committee (1971)** recommended an ISC earlier.
[3:4] - **Not related:** the **69th Amendment** (Delhi NCT).
[3:5] TABLE head: Feature | Detail
  [3:5.0] Chairman | Prime Minister
  [3:5.1] Members | CMs of all States, UT Administrators / CMs, six Union Cabinet Ministers nominated by the PM
  [3:5.2] Decision method | Consensus; recommendations not binding
  [3:5.3] Standing Committee | Chaired by the Union Home Minister
  [3:5.4] Secretariat | Inter-State Council Secretariat (also serves Zonal Councils)
[3:6] - **Bifurcation disputes:** ISC + **Apex Council** under the **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014**.
[3:7] # Commissions
[3:8] TABLE head: Body | Key facts
  [3:8.0] ARC (1966) | Morarji Desai, then K. Hanumanthaiya; M.C. Setalvad study team (1969); urged an Inter-State Council, non-partisan Governors
  [3:8.1] Rajamannar (1969, TN govt; report 1971) | ISC immediately; residuary powers to States; omit Arts 356, 357, 365; abolish AIS
  [3:8.2] Sarkaria (June 1983, report 1988) | Justice R.S. Sarkaria, B. Sivaraman, S.R. Sen; 247 recommendations; permanent ISC; Art. 356 sparingly; strengthen AIS
  [3:8.3] Punchhi (2007, report 2010) | Governor removable by a State Legislature resolution, like the impeachment of the President
[3:9] - **Art. 357(2):** a Parliamentary law made for a State under President's Rule **continues after PR** until the **State Legislature** alters, repeals or **re-enacts** it – so re-enactment is allowed.
[3:10] - **Art. 249** resolution = **Rajya Sabha only**, not the Lok Sabha.

## SUB 4 | Zonal Councils and North-Eastern Council
[4:0] # Zonal Councils
[4:1] - **Statutory** bodies under **Part III (Sections 15-22)** of the **States Reorganisation Act, 1956** (which created **14 States, 6 UTs**) – not constitutional.
[4:2] - **5 councils:** Northern, Central, **Eastern**, Western, Southern; proposed by **Nehru** (1956).
[4:3] - **Chairman:** **Union Home Minister** (all five); **Vice-chairman:** CMs by **annual rotation**.
[4:4] - Members: **CM + two other ministers** of each State; Administrators / LGs of UTs.
[4:5] - Advisers (**no vote**): NITI Aayog nominee, **Chief Secretaries**, Development Commissioners.
[4:6] - **Deliberative and advisory**; advice **not binding**.
[4:7] TABLE head: Council | States
  [4:7.0] Eastern | Bihar, Jharkhand, Odisha, West Bengal (+ Sikkim, added after 1975)
  [4:7.1] Southern | Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala, Telangana, Puducherry (not Goa)
[4:8] - **Sikkim:** placed in the **Eastern** zone; added to the **North-Eastern Council in 2002**, and MHA has begun excluding it from the Eastern Zonal Council (recent EZC meetings: 4 States).
[4:9] - **31st Southern Zonal Council: 20 Aug 2026**, near **Mamallapuram**; chaired by **Amit Shah**; vice-chairman TN CM **C. Joseph Vijay**.
[4:10] # North-Eastern Council
[4:11] - Separate **NEC Act, 1971**; **Sikkim added 2002** (8th member); since **2018** the **Union Home Minister** is ex-officio chairman.

## SUB 5 | Armed Forces, Public Service Commissions and Tribunals
[5:0] - **Art. 355:** Union's duty to protect States; it may deploy armed / central forces **with or without State consent**.
[5:1] - Basis: **Union Entry 2A** (**42nd Amendment**); States object because **public order** and **police** are **State Entries 1 and 2**.
[5:2] - **CAPF dispute (Mar 2026):** Centre has not implemented the **2025 SC order** (Organised Group A status for CAPF officers, less IPS deputation); a **Central Armed Forces (General Administration) Bill** is expected.
[5:3] - **Art. 315:** UPSC; an SPSC for each State; a **JSPSC** for 2+ States if their **legislatures so resolve** – created by an **Act of Parliament**, members appointed by the **President**.
[5:4] - **UPSC** replaced the **Federal PSC** on **26 Jan 1950**.
[5:5] - **Art. 323A** (**42nd Amendment, 1976**): administrative tribunals for Union and States; **CAT** (1985) – Chairman + 69 Members (34 judicial, 35 administrative).
