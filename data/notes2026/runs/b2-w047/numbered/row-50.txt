## SUB 0 | Distribution of Tax Revenues – Articles 268 to 271 and State Taxes
[0:0] # Who levies, who collects, who keeps (present position, after the 80th and 101st Amendments)
[0:1] TABLE head: Article | Levied by | Collected / kept by | Taxes
  [0:1.0] 268 | Union | States collect and appropriate | Stamp duties on bills of exchange, cheques, promissory notes, insurance policies, share transfers; proceeds not part of the CFI
  [0:1.1] 269 | Union | Union collects, assigned to the States | Taxes on inter-State sale or purchase of goods (not newspapers) and on inter-State consignment; net proceeds do not form part of the CFI
  [0:1.2] 269A | Union | Shared as Parliament provides on GST Council advice | IGST on inter-State supplies (101st Amendment)
  [0:1.3] 270 | Union | Shared with States on Finance Commission advice (divisible pool) | All Union List taxes – income tax (non-agricultural), corporation tax, customs, CGST … – except 268, 269, 269A taxes, surcharges (271) and cesses
  [0:1.4] 271 | Union | Union only – States get no share | Surcharges on 269 and 270 taxes (not on GST)
  [0:1.5] 268A (omitted) | Union | Union + States | Service tax – inserted by 88th (2003), omitted by 101st (2016)
  [0:1.6] 272 (omitted) | Union | Shared by law | Union excise – omitted by the 80th (2000)
[0:2] - **Income tax:** the Union **levies, collects and distributes** it between itself and the States (Art. 270) – it is neither kept wholly by the Union nor given wholly to the States; only the **surcharge** stays with the Union.
[0:3] - **Before 2000** (old Art. 269) the Union levied and collected but **assigned to the States** taxes on **railway fares and freights**, **stock-exchange and futures-market transactions** (other than stamp duty), estate and succession duty, newspapers and their advertisements; the **80th Amendment** moved these into the **Art. 270 pool**. Only **income tax** was shared from 1950; **corporation tax and customs** became shareable only from **1996** (80th).
[0:4] - **Art. 268 before GST** also covered **excise on medicinal and toilet preparations containing alcohol** – levied by the Union, **collected and kept by the States**; the 101st Amendment removed it (now under GST).
[0:5] # Taxes levied, collected and retained by the States (State List, 18 heads)
[0:6] - **Land revenue**, **agricultural income tax** (Entry 46 – the **Constitution**, not the Finance Commission, assigns it), duties on succession to agricultural land, taxes on **lands and buildings**, mineral rights.
[0:7] - **State excise on alcoholic liquor** for human consumption, opium and narcotics; taxes on **sale of petroleum crude, diesel, petrol, natural gas, ATF and liquor** (outside GST – levied **by the States**, not the Centre).
[0:8] - Electricity, goods and passengers by road/inland waterways, **vehicles**, animals and boats, **tolls**, **professions** (Art. 276), capitation, **entertainment tax by local bodies**, stamp duty on other documents.
[0:9] - **Profession tax (Art. 276(2)):** the **only tax whose total per person is capped by the Constitution** – **₹2,500 a year** (₹250 till the **60th Amendment, 1988**).
[0:10] # Local bodies
[0:11] - **Art. 243H / 243X:** the State Legislature may let Panchayats/Municipalities **levy, collect and appropriate** taxes, duties, tolls and fees, and may **assign** to them taxes collected by the State.
[0:12] - **Assigned revenues:** taxes **assigned to local bodies by statute but collected by the State** and passed on to the Panchayats.
[0:13] - **Gram Panchayat taxes:** house tax, **tax/fee on fairs and markets ("fair tax")**, water and lighting rates, vehicle and animal fees – **not** sales tax, customs or excise.

## SUB 1 | Finance Commission and Devolution – Article 280
[1:0] # The body
[1:1] - **Art. 280:** a **quasi-judicial** body constituted by the **President** every **fifth year** or earlier; chairman + 4 members (Finance Commission Act, 1951); **Art. 281:** its report and the action taken are laid before Parliament.
[1:2] - Called the **"balancing wheel of fiscal federalism"**; its advice is **not binding**.
[1:3] # What it recommends (Art. 280(3))
[1:4] - **Distribution of the net proceeds of taxes** between the Union and the States (**vertical**) and **among the States inter se** (**horizontal distribution**).
[1:5] - **Principles governing grants-in-aid** to the States out of the **Consolidated Fund of India** (Art. 275).
[1:6] - Measures to augment a State's Consolidated Fund for **Panchayats and Municipalities** (73rd/74th Amendments), and any matter referred by the President.
[1:7] - It does **not** attract foreign capital, distribute funds among PSUs or audit financial administration (UPSC 2011 – "none of these").
[1:8] - Tax sharing is **not** fixed by the Finance Ministry, RBI, NABARD, NITI Aayog, the Inter-State Council, NDC or the GST Council; the **CAG certifies the net proceeds** (Art. 279).
[1:9] # Shares
[1:10] TABLE head: FC | Period | States' share of divisible pool
  [1:10.0] 10th | 1995-2000 | Alternative Scheme of Devolution – 29% of gross Union taxes (80th Amendment)
  [1:10.1] 14th (Y.V. Reddy) | 2015-20 | 42% – "compositional shift" from grants to devolution
  [1:10.2] 15th (N.K. Singh) | 2020-26 | 41%
  [1:10.3] 16th (Arvind Panagariya) | 2026-31 | 41% retained; State fiscal deficit 3% of GSDP; end off-budget borrowing
[1:11] - **Compositional shift:** more tax devolution and fewer conditional grants → **increase in the share of unconditional (untied) transfers** to States and more fiscal autonomy (APPSC Group-I 2017).
[1:12] - **Cesses and surcharges** (~18% of gross tax) stay outside the pool, so effective devolution is about **31-32%**; southern States' **"Chalo Delhi" (2024)** opposed population-based criteria. Tax devolution 2024-25: **₹12.87 lakh crore**.
[1:13] > Asked as: who recommends distribution of net tax proceeds / principles of grants-in-aid (Finance Commission – 6 PYQs); horizontal distribution; effect of the compositional shift.

## SUB 3 | GST and the GST Council
[3:0] # Framework
[3:1] - **101st Amendment Act, 2016** (the **122nd Amendment Bill**) – GST from **1 July 2017**; **destination-based**; inserted **Arts 246A, 269A, 279A**, omitted 268A; replaced about **17** indirect taxes.
[3:2] - **Subsumed:** Central excise, service tax, CVD/SAD, **State VAT/sales tax**, **entry tax and octroi**, **entertainment tax (except by local bodies)**, luxury, purchase and lottery taxes.
[3:3] - **Not subsumed:** **land revenue** (not a tax on supply at all), **State excise on alcohol for human consumption** (liquor is outside GST), stamp duty, electricity duty, basic customs; petroleum products zero-rated till the Council decides.
[3:4] - **Art. 246A:** Parliament and States both make GST laws; **only Parliament** for **inter-State supply**; **Art. 269A:** IGST, revenue to the **destination State**.
[3:5] # GST Council – Art. 279A
[3:6] TABLE head: Feature | Fact
  [3:6.0] Chair | Union Finance Minister
  [3:6.1] Members | Union MoS (Revenue/Finance) + every State's Finance Minister
  [3:6.2] Votes | Centre 1/3, all States together 2/3; decision by 3/4 of weighted votes
  [3:6.3] Status | Mohit Minerals (2022): recommendations persuasive, not binding
[3:7] - **53rd GST Council (22 June 2024):** monetary limits for departmental appeals – **₹20 lakh GSTAT, ₹1 crore High Court, ₹2 crore Supreme Court** (CBIC Circular 207/1/2024-GST).
[3:8] - **GST and federalism:** "**pooled sovereignty**" – Centre and States decide **jointly** in the Council as partners (cooperative federalism); critics note the Centre's effective veto. Compensation cess on luxury/sin goods for **5 years (2017-22)**; **GST 2.0** from **September 2025**.
[3:9] > Asked as: Amendment number (101st – 2 PYQs); Council chair; tax not subsumed; 2024 Council decisions; GST and Centre-State relations.
