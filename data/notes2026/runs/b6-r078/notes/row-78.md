=== Big Cats – IBCA, Project Cheetah, Lions and Tigers
# International Big Cat Alliance (IBCA)
- Launched by **PM Narendra Modi** on **9 April 2023** – **50 years of Project Tiger**; Union Cabinet approval **February 2024**; treaty-based organisation from **23 January 2025**; HQ/secretariat in **India (not Geneva)**.
- **Seven big cats**: tiger, lion, leopard, snow leopard, cheetah, jaguar, puma (**no rhinoceros**); India has **five**.
- Members: 24 earlier in 2026; **Saudi Arabia joined on 22 May 2026 – 26 members and 5 observers** (observers incl. Kazakhstan, Namibia, Thailand); **Bangladesh** became the **27th** member (July 2026).
- **First IBCA conference**: New Delhi, **1 June 2026** – 400+ scientists, conservationists, policymakers.
- **First IBCA Summit**: hosted by India, **1–3 June 2026, New Delhi**; ~**95 countries**; outcome **"(New) Delhi Declaration"** – **first international declaration exclusively on big cats**; landscape-based and transboundary conservation, habitat connectivity, wildlife crime, finance, science, community participation, One Health.
# Project Tiger and Big Cats
- Project Tiger **1973** (Centrally Sponsored, under **NTCA**, MoEFCC); **58 tiger reserves** (Sep 2026); India holds **~75%** of the world's wild tigers; tiger – **Schedule I, WPA 1972**; core + buffer zones.

# Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026
- In force **1 April 2026**, replacing **SWM Rules 2016**; under **Environment Protection Act, 1986**; also covers rural bodies; goal **zero landfill**, circular economy; digital monitoring (reporting to **CPCB**).
- **Four-way segregation**: **wet, dry, sanitary, special domestic hazardous**.
- **Bulk generators**: more than **100 kg/day** waste, more than **40,000 litres/day** water use, or more than **20,000 sq m** area – must **process on site**.
- Industries raise **RDF** use **5% → 15%** over six years; **biomining/bioremediation** for legacy dumps; **tourist user charges** in hill/island areas.
- Related rules: Plastic Waste Rules 2016, **E-Waste Rules 2022**, Biomedical Waste Rules 2016. MoEFCC est. **1985**.
- **EP Act, 1986** enacted under **Article 253** (implementing international treaties) after the **Stockholm Declaration 1972**.
- **Subsidiarity**: tasks at the **lowest capable level** (decentralisation).
# Varkala – UN "20 Cities Towards Zero Waste"
- **Varkala (Kerala)** in the **first list** of "20 Cities Towards Zero Waste" – led by the **UN Secretary-General's Advisory Board on Zero Waste** (launched **2023**); supported by **UN-Habitat** and **UNEP**.

# Improved Cookstoves and Pollutants
- **Improved Cookstoves (ICS)**: **secondary aeration** → fuller combustion; thermal efficiency: traditional chulha **~10%**, ICS **38–45%**; fuel use cut up to **two-thirds**.
- Cost: household ICS below ₹2,000; commercial up to ₹20,000; eligible for **carbon credits**; reduce **black carbon**.
- ICS **reduce but do not eliminate** indoor pollution.
- **Photochemical smog**: **NOx + VOCs** in sunlight → **ozone, PAN**.
# Rare Earth Mining Pollutes the Mekong
- Unregulated rare earth mining in **Myanmar and Laos** – arsenic, mercury, lead, cadmium in tributaries **Sai and Ruak** → Mekong ("world's kitchen").
- Mekong: longest river of **Southeast Asia**; rises in the Tibetan Plateau as the **Lancang**; flows through China, Myanmar, Laos, Thailand, Cambodia, Vietnam → South China Sea.
- **Mekong Delta (Vietnam)** – "rice bowl" of SE Asia; **Tonle Sap Lake (Cambodia)** – **seasonal flow reversal**.
- **Mekong River Commission (1995)**: **four** full members – **Cambodia, Laos, Thailand, Vietnam**; **China, Myanmar** = dialogue partners.
- Rare earths: **17** elements (lanthanides + scandium + yttrium); **Lanthanum** – optical glass; **Dysprosium** – **NdFeB magnets** (EV motors); **Monazite** has **thorium and uranium**; ionic clay deposits – in-situ leaching; **froth flotation cannot separate individual REEs**.

# Great Indian Bustard – Jumpstart Method
- GIB chick hatched **26 March 2026** at **Naiya grassland, Kutch (Gujarat)** – first successful **jumpstart** use in Gujarat; flew ~18 April, then missing 4–5 days (likely predation); not geotagged.
- **Jumpstart**: wild female's **infertile egg replaced with a fertile egg** from a **Rajasthan captive centre**; wild mother raises the chick. Gujarat has only **three** surviving wild female GIBs.

# Honey Bee Attacks and NBHM
- Bee attack deaths in UP, Gujarat, Maharashtra; **Uttar Pradesh** considering bee attacks as a **state-notified disaster**.
- **Apis dorsata (Giant Rock Bee)** – open hives on cliffs/forts; up to **60,000 bees**; chases intruders at **~35 km/h**; more aggressive in summer; bee dies after a single sting; swatting releases pheromones; risk of anaphylaxis.
- **National Beekeeping and Honey Mission (NBHM)**: Central Sector Scheme – "**Sweet Revolution**"; **₹500 crore**, FY 2020–21 to 2025–26; under Atmanirbhar Bharat; implemented by **National Bee Board** (**Ministry of Agriculture**); 3 Mini Missions; **Madhukranti portal** – registration and traceability.
- Honey exports FY 2023–24: **1.07 lakh MT** (USD 177.55 million); India **second-largest exporter** (from 9th in 2020).
- **Waggle dance** – decoded by **Karl von Frisch** (Nobel).
# Lantana to Biochar – Amrabad Tiger Reserve
- Pilot in **Amrabad TR** converts invasive **Lantana camara** into **biocharcoal ("black gold")** – burned under soil in low oxygen; initial ~3 kg; ~₹7,000/tonne; livelihoods through **JFMCs**.
- Lantana: native to **Central and South America**; spread in the 17th century as an ornamental; among IUCN's **world's worst invasive species**; spreads by **allelopathy**, bird/monkey seed dispersal, deep roots, **resin-coated leaves**; **toxic to livestock**.
# Enforcement and Forest Monitoring
- **Operation Thunder**: wildlife-trafficking crackdown in **134 countries** – **INTERPOL (1923, Lyon)** + **WCO (1952, Brussels)**.
- **Anavaran** portal (**FSI**, since **January 2024**) – satellite + machine learning detection of forest loss; alerts to states **every 15 days**. FSI: **1981**, Dehradun.
- **Allelopathy:** a plant releases chemicals that **suppress the growth of other plants** nearby; Lantana camara spreads partly by allelopathy.

=== Extreme Weather and Climate Terms
# Heatwave (IMD Criteria)
| Criterion | Value |
|---|---|
| Minimum maximum temperature | 40°C plains, 30°C hills |
| Heat wave (departure) | 4.5–6.4°C above normal |
| Severe heat wave (departure) | Over 6.4°C |
| Heat wave (absolute) | 45°C |
| Severe heat wave (absolute) | 47°C |
- **Wet-bulb temperature** above **35°C** sustained can be fatal.
- **Feels-like (apparent) temperature** = temperature + humidity + wind.
# Western Disturbance and ENSO
- IMD forecast a fresh **Western Disturbance** over North India on **11–13 May 2026**.
- Western Disturbance: **east-moving** rain system from beyond **Afghanistan/Iran**; moisture from the **Mediterranean**, Black, Caspian, Arabian seas; moves with the **subtropical westerly jet**; brings **winter rain/snow to north-west India** (not south); helps **wheat**.
- ENSO phases: **El Niño** (warm), **La Niña** (cool), **Neutral** (neither).
- **US Climate Prediction Center (April 2026)**: ENSO-neutral now; El Niño likely in **May–July 2026** – **61%** chance, persisting to end-2026.
- **ECMWF**: **Super El Niño** likely in 2026, from June–August to year-end. Super El Niño = SST rise **above 2.5°C**; past ones **1982–83, 1997–98, 2015–16**; may **weaken the SW monsoon**, reduce Atlantic hurricanes, raise Pacific activity.
# Kashmir's "Death of Winter"
- **Seven consecutive winters** of below-normal snowfall (every winter since 2019–20).
| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Winter precipitation (Dec–Feb 2025–26) | 100.6 mm vs normal 284.9 mm – 65% deficit |
| February precipitation | 14.2 mm vs 130.4 mm – ~89% deficit |
- Impacts: **apples, cherries, walnuts** lack **chilling hours**; pests (aphids, mites); winter tourism (Gulmarg, Pahalgam) hit.
# Earthquake Lights (EQL)
- Luminous atmospheric phenomena **before or during earthquakes**; cause – **ionisation of air** by electrical charges generated under **tectonic stress** in rocks.

=== Protected Areas, Ramsar Sites and Eco-sensitive Zones
# Shekha Jheel – India's 99th Ramsar Site
- **Shekha Jheel Bird Sanctuary**, near **Aligarh (UP)** – **99th Ramsar site**, announced **22 April 2026** by Environment Minister **Bhupender Yadav**; freshwater wetland; 180+ migratory bird species; **Central Asian Flyway**.
- India's Ramsar sites: **26 (2014) → 99 (April 2026)** – **now 103** (Sukhna Wetland and Sauj Jheel added, October 2026).
| Batch | Sites |
|---|---|
| 2026 (to 98) | Patna Bird Sanctuary (UP), Chhari-Dhand (Gujarat) |
| 2025 | Siliserh Lake (Rajasthan), Kopra Reservoir (Chhattisgarh), Goga Beel, Gokul Reservoir, Udaipur Jheel (Bihar), Menar, Khichan (Rajasthan) |
| World Wetlands Day | Sakkarakottai, Thirthangal (TN), Khecheopalri (Sikkim), Udhwa Lake (Jharkhand) |
- Convention signed **1971**; India joined **1 February 1982**; Secretariat **Gland** (hosted by IUCN); first sites – **Chilika and Keoladeo**; most sites – Tamil Nadu, UP; largest – **Sundarbans**; smallest – **Vembannur** (earlier Renuka); India ~1.4 million ha – first in Asia, third globally.

=== Water, Rivers and Fisheries
