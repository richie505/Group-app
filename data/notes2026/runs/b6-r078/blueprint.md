## 4. Corrections and updates made in the notes
- **RDF** co-processing in industries: row 79 said 6% → 15%; corrected to **5% → 15%** over six years (PMF Lens Feb 2026 p.5; Praveen CA-360 April 2026 p.13; row 78 already said 5%).
- Germanwatch index released at COP30 (Nov 2025) is the **Climate Risk Index 2026** (Praveen CA-360 April 2026 p.79). Row 78 called it "CRI 2025", and two app MCQs (n666e8434681, f08b345725b25) still use that label. Their answers (9th; 15th for 2024) are right.
- Ramsar sites: 98 (Feb 2026) → 99 (Shekha Jheel, April 2026) → **now 103** (Sukhna Wetland, Sauj Jheel, Oct 2026; Rocket CA 2026 Part 02 p.84); Ramsar Secretariat = Gland (hosted by IUCN).
- Tiger reserves: "50+" → **58** (Sep 2026; Rocket CA 2026 Part 02 p.137).
- Updated NDC: Cabinet approval on 25 March 2026, **submitted on 24 April 2026** (Rocket CA 2026 Part 02 p.182). 50% non-fossil capacity reached **June 2025**, 54.18% by 30 June 2026 (Part 01 p.161).
- Project Cheetah: 29 cheetahs imported (Namibia 8, South Africa 12, Botswana 9 = 6 F + 3 M, 28 Feb 2026); population 59 on 12 Oct 2026 (Rocket CA 2026 Part 02 p.142-143).
- Rocket CA 2026 mainly covers Jun–Nov 2026. Most Dec 2025–May 2026 environment items appear only in Praveen CA-360, PMF Lens, CARE and the app's own notes, and are cited from those.
