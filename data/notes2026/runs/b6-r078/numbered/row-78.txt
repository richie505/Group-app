## SUB 0 | Big Cats – IBCA, Project Cheetah, Lions and Tigers
[0:0] # International Big Cat Alliance (IBCA)
[0:1] - Launched by **PM Narendra Modi** on **9 April 2023** – **50 years of Project Tiger**; Union Cabinet approval **February 2024**; treaty-based organisation from **23 January 2025**; HQ/secretariat in **India (not Geneva)**.
[0:2] - **Seven big cats**: tiger, lion, leopard, snow leopard, cheetah, jaguar, puma (**no rhinoceros**); India has **five**.
[0:3] - Members: 24 earlier in 2026; **Saudi Arabia joined on 22 May 2026 – 26 members and 5 observers** (observers incl. Kazakhstan, Namibia, Thailand); **Bangladesh** became the **27th** member (July 2026).

[0:5] - **First IBCA Summit**: hosted by India, **1–3 June 2026, New Delhi**; ~**95 countries**; outcome **"(New) Delhi Declaration"** – **first international declaration exclusively on big cats**; landscape-based and transboundary conservation, habitat connectivity, wildlife crime, finance, science, community participation, One Health.

[3:18] - ICS **reduce but do not eliminate** indoor pollution.

## SUB 5 | Climate Negotiations and India's Updated NDC (2031–2035)
[5:0] # Updated NDC – Approval
[5:1] - The **Union Cabinet** approved India's updated **NDC (2031–2035)** on **25 March 2026**, to be communicated to the **UNFCCC**; India **submitted** it on **24 April 2026** (Rocket CA).
[5:2] - India and **Argentina** were the **only G20 countries** without a 2035 NDC by end-2025.
[5:3] - India sets its target in **emissions intensity** (per unit of GDP), **not absolute emissions**.
[5:4] TABLE head: Parameter | 2022 NDC | New NDC (2035)
  [5:4.0] Non-fossil installed power capacity | 50% by 2030 (achieved June 2025, five years early) | 60% by 2035

  [5:4.2] Additional carbon sink | 2.5–3 billion t CO₂e | 3.5–4 billion t CO₂e by 2035
  [5:4.3] Long-term goal | Net zero 2070 | Net zero 2070 (intensity, not absolute)
[5:5] > The 2022 NDC intensity target is **45%** by 2030; the new 2035 target is **47%**. The 2015 NDC said 33–35%.
[5:6] # Progress Figures
[5:7] - Non-fossil share of installed capacity: **~52.6% (52.57%, Feb 2026)**.
[5:8] - Emission intensity already cut **36%** (2005–2020); carbon sink of **2.29 billion t** created by 2021.
[5:9] - Forest cover **~24%** against the goal of **33%**.
[5:10] # Emissions Data (CREA / Carbon Brief)
[5:11] - India's CO₂ emissions rose only **0.7% in 2025** – slowest since **2001** (excluding 2020); growth only **0.5%** in the second half of 2025; coal use in thermal plants down **20%**.
[5:12] - 2025 additions: **47 GW solar**, 6.3 GW wind, 4 GW hydro, 0.6 GW nuclear.
[5:13] - Plans: **100 GW new coal** in 7 years; **$1 trillion** petrochemicals by 2040.
[5:14] - **CREA**: est. **2019**, HQ **Helsinki**.

[7:4] # Western Disturbance and ENSO
[7:5] - IMD forecast a fresh **Western Disturbance** over North India on **11–13 May 2026**.
[7:6] - Western Disturbance: **east-moving** rain system from beyond **Afghanistan/Iran**; moisture from the **Mediterranean**, Black, Caspian, Arabian seas; moves with the **subtropical westerly jet**; brings **winter rain/snow to north-west India** (not south); helps **wheat**.
[7:7] - ENSO phases: **El Niño** (warm), **La Niña** (cool), **Neutral** (neither).
[7:8] - **US Climate Prediction Center (April 2026)**: ENSO-neutral now; El Niño likely in **May–July 2026** – **61%** chance, persisting to end-2026.
[7:9] - **ECMWF**: **Super El Niño** likely in 2026, from June–August to year-end. Super El Niño = SST rise **above 2.5°C**; past ones **1982–83, 1997–98, 2015–16**; may **weaken the SW monsoon**, reduce Atlantic hurricanes, raise Pacific activity.
