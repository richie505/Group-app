=== Union Budget 2026–27 – Basics, Budget History and Fiscal Numbers
# Basics
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Presented by | FM Nirmala Sitharaman – her 9th Budget (most by a woman FM); 6th digital Budget |
| Date | 1 February 2026 |
| Prepared by | Department of Economic Affairs (DEA), Ministry of Finance |
| Special | First Union Budget prepared in Kartavya Bhawan |
| Theme | "Yuva Shakti-driven" Budget built on three Kartavya |
| Constitutional basis | Article 112 – Annual Financial Statement |
- **Three Kartavya (duties)**: (1) accelerate and sustain economic growth; (2) fulfil aspirations and build capacity; (3) **Sabka Sath, Sabka Vikas** – access for every family, community, region and sector.
- **Six intervention areas** (Kartavya 1): manufacturing in 7 strategic/frontier sectors; legacy industries; Champion MSMEs; infrastructure; energy security; **City Economic Regions**.
- **350+ reforms** since Independence Day 2025 – GST simplification, Labour Codes notified, QCO rationalisation.

# Three Macro Worries
- **Weak nominal GDP growth**: ~8% (lowest in years) vs 10.1% assumed in the February 2025 Budget.
- **Weak tax buoyancy**: actual **0.6** vs assumed 1.1 – **tax grows slower than GDP**.
- Weak private corporate investment despite 2019 tax cuts, capex, PLI; FPI outflows, rupee pressure.
- Spending shares (CMIE): Budget size grew 10.4% a year since 2009; roads + railways <4% (2009) → ~11%; **MSME share only 0.24%**; interest payments ~1 in every 4 rupees; Skill Development 0.06% → 0.05%.

=== Banking, Markets, Currency and Crypto
# Banking Laws (Amendment) Act, 2025
- Notified in two stages – **1 August and 1 November 2025**.
- Amends **five Acts**: RBI Act 1934, Banking Regulation Act 1949, **SBI Act 1955**, Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Acts 1970 and 1980.
| Reform | Detail |
|---|---|
| Nominees | Up to four per account; simultaneous (percentage, total 100%) or successive (lockers) |
| 'Substantial interest' | ₹5 lakh (1968) → ₹2 crore |
| Cooperative bank directors | Max tenure 8 → 10 years (not 15); aligns with 97th Amendment |
| PSB audit | PSBs fix auditor remuneration; unclaimed shares/interest to IEPF |
- SBI Act 1955 turned **Imperial Bank of India into SBI**; nationalisation of **14 banks in 1969**, more in **1980**.
- **Banking Regulation (Amendment) Act, 2020** – RBI control over cooperative banks; **1966 amendment** brought Urban Cooperative Banks under the BR Act.
# Bank Initiatives
- **SBI CHAKRA**: Centre of Excellence for financing **8 sunrise sectors** – renewable energy, data centres, e-mobility, battery tech, semiconductors, green hydrogen, decarbonisation, smart infrastructure; ₹100 lakh crore+ potential by 2030; SBI Chairman **C.S. Setty**.
- **Bank of India**: ₹10,000 crore via infrastructure bonds (bids ₹15,300 crore vs base ₹5,000 crore).
- **Bank infra bonds**: RBI minimum maturity **7 years** (typical 10–15); **CRR and SLR exemptions**; reduce asset–liability mismatch.
| Aspect | Infrastructure Bonds | InvITs |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Debt | Trust-based vehicle |
| Returns | Fixed interest | Market-linked |
| Tenure | Fixed | No fixed maturity |
| Regulation | RBI and SEBI | SEBI (InvIT Regulations, 2014) |
- **InvITs** recognised as borrowers under the **SARFAESI Act, 2002**.
# Capital Markets and Savings
- Domestic household savings replacing FPI as main market liquidity source; **FPI holding 15-month low 16.9%** (24.1% in NIFTY 50); **retail ~19%** – highest in 20+ years.
- **2025: 71 mainboard IPOs raised over ₹1 lakh crore**; domestic participation gives RBI more policy autonomy.
- Suggested: SEBI (LODR) 2015 suitability-based selling; **Mutual Fund Sahi Hai**; **National Strategy for Financial Education**.
- Rupee: **full current account convertibility**; capital account only partial.
- **Small savings rates for January–March 2026 unchanged** (announced 31 December 2025; seventh straight quarter): **PPF 7.1%**, NSC 7.7%, POMIS 7.4%, KVP 7.5% (matures in 115 months), **Sukanya Samriddhi 8.2%**, post office savings 4%.
- **NPS Swasthya Pension Scheme (NSPS)**: **PFRDA** pilot under **Regulatory Sandbox**, Multiple Scheme Framework; voluntary – all citizens with an NPS Common Scheme Account; partial withdrawal up to **25%** of own contribution for medical costs; if inpatient costs exceed **70% of corpus**, withdraw up to **100%**; payment directly to hospitals/HBA/TPA.
# Crypto and Digital Rupee
- **Bitcoin** record **~$126,000 (October 2025)** → below $85,000; ~$1 trillion crypto value wiped out; long-term holders sold **800,000 BTC** in a month; **10/10 crash** (100% tariff threat on China); US Fed cut 25 bps on 10 December.
- Bitcoin behaves like a **risk asset, not "digital gold"**; created **2009** by Satoshi Nakamoto; capped at **21 million**; **Bitcoin Cash** – hard fork **2017**; halving every four years (last April 2024); US Bitcoin ETFs January 2024; GENIUS Act (June 2025) on stablecoins.

# Agriculture and Dairy
- **NITI Aayog**: agriculture grew **4.45% a year (2014–15 to 2024–25) – highest ever** (China 4.10%); farmers' income +10.11% a year (2014–15 to **2023–24**); irrigated area 98 → 122 million ha; **KCC 7.75 crore**; ~20% food surplus possible by 2035.
- **Rice**: staple for ~**65%** of Indians; ~**25%** of cropped area; kharif – **>25°C, >100 cm rain**; transplanted paddy **25–27 irrigations**; flooding raises **methane** (anaerobic); solutions – **DSR**, **AWD** (cuts methane), SRI; ICAR **NICRA**; fortified rice under NFSA and PM-POSHAN; ICAR released 184 varieties of 25 crops.
- **Soil Health Card**: 25 crore+ cards; launched **2015**; every **two years**; linked to **National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture**.
- **Agriculture Infrastructure Fund** raised **₹1 lakh crore → ₹2 lakh crore**; Lakhpati Didi target 6 crore by 2030 (3 crore achieved).
- **Milk 2023–24: 239.3 MMT** (~25% of world; largest producer); per capita 459 g/day; dairy 3.5% of GDP.

- **Banas Dairy** (Banaskantha, Gujarat) – **Asia's largest dairy cooperative**; bio-CNG and bio-fertiliser plant; circular model can raise incomes ~20% in five years; **Operation Flood (1970)** – Verghese Kurien, NDDB.
- **Biostimulants**: not fertilisers or insecticides (outside Insecticides Act, 1968); **Schedule VI of FCO 1985** (February 2021); **nine categories**; only 132 approved after 16 June 2025; seaweed extracts 41% of global market; hydrocolloids – **agar, alginate, carrageenan**.
- **Biostimulants – meaning:** substances or micro-organisms that stimulate natural plant processes (nutrient uptake, growth, stress tolerance) independently of their own nutrient content.

=== Energy – Oil, Gas, Ethanol and SAF
# Energy
- **E20 petrol mandatory nationwide from 1 April 2026** – RON 95.
- **MoPNG four energy priorities**: Access, Efficiency, Sustainability, Security (not Affordability).
- **PMUY**: ~10.35 crore beneficiaries (1 Dec 2025); launched **1 May 2016 at Ballia (UP)**; **₹300 subsidy per 14.2 kg cylinder, up to nine refills**; single **Deprivation Declaration** replaced multi-point self-declaration; 25 lakh more connections for FY26.
- **Unified Pipeline Tariff** ("One Nation, One Grid, One Tariff"): from **1 April 2023**; ~90% of pipelines. Gas pipelines 15,340 km (2014) → 25,429 km (June 2025).
- **Ethanol blending 19.24% in ESY 2024–25** (₹1.55 lakh crore forex saved); **SATAT** 130+ CBG plants by Nov 2025; PM JI-VAN – 2G plants at Panipat, Numaligarh.
- **SAF targets (international flights)**: **1% (2027), 2% (2028), 5% (2030)**; **IOCL** – first Indian firm with **ISCC CORSIA** certification (Panipat).
- PNGRB Act 2006; appeals to APTEL (Section 110, Electricity Act 2003); **first independent regulator = TRAI (1997)**, not PNGRB.
- **SAKSHAM 2026** (Samrakshan Kshamata Mahotsav), MoPNG, **2–16 February 2026** – "Conserve Oil and Natural Gas – Towards a Greener Future".
- **NTPC coal-to-SNG**: **Talaipalli, Chhattisgarh**; **coking coal** declared critical and strategic mineral (MMDR Act, ~95% imported).

=== External Sector – Trade, FDI, Aid, Rice and Seafood
- **FDI 2025 (UNCTAD): USD 47 bn (+73%)**; global FDI +14% to USD 1.6 trillion; China −8% to USD 107.5 bn; India **7th** in data-centre investments (top three France, US, South Korea).
- **India–China trade 2025** (China customs): record **USD 155.62 bn**; India's exports USD 19.75 bn; China's exports USD 135.87 bn; **record deficit USD 116.12 bn** (2024: USD 99.21 bn).
- **World Bank Country Partnership Framework**: **USD 8–10 billion a year for five years**; agreed with President **Ajay Banga** and FM Sitharaman; WBG est. 1944.
- **ADB**: additional **$182 million** for **Assam's Brahmaputra** flood and erosion project (original $200 million, Oct 2023); President Masato Kanda.
- **Seafood**: Apr–Oct 2025 exports +16% to $4.87 bn; Apr–Nov $5.75 bn; US duties on shrimp ~60% – yet **US still largest market**; main item **frozen Vannamei shrimp**; India **3rd in fish production, 2nd in aquaculture**; 2024–25 exports record ₹62,408 crore; **MPEDA** – export promotion, **PMMSY** – infrastructure; **ReALCRaft** – online registration and licensing of fishing vessels.
- **Rice trade**: India **overtook China as largest producer** – **150.18 MT vs 145.28 MT** (announced by Shivraj Singh Chouhan); ~40% of global rice trade; exports 198.65 lakh t ($12.95 bn) to 172 countries; **India's share in US rice imports 26.1% (2024)**.
- Trump's **rice "dumping"** claim at a White House farmers' roundtable (with Brooke Rollins, Scott Bessent) – **not dumping**: the US imports premium aromatic rice at high prices.
- **Basmati → mainly West Asia** (Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Iran, UAE); to US 2.74 lakh t ($337.10 m); **non-basmati → mainly West Africa**; LT Foods' **"Royal"** – 55% of North American basmati market.
- **APEDA**: 40th anniversary; est. **13 February 1986** (APEDA Act 1985). **First export of GI Salem Sago** – 0.5 MT to **Canada** (SAGOSERVE).
- **Fishing grounds:** The richest fishing grounds are where warm and cold ocean currents meet (e.g. Grand Banks off Newfoundland, the Kuroshio–Oyashio meeting area off Japan); the mixing brings up nutrients and plankton.
