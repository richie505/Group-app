## SUB 2 | Energy Security – LPG, LNG, SPR, Kerosene, Ethanol
[34:2:0] # LPG Shortage and Storage Gap
[34:2:1] - India **second-largest LPG consumer**; ~3 million t/month (~80,000 t/day); 85%+ used by households.
[34:2:2] - Imports ~20 million t/year (tripled 2011–12 to 2024–25); dependency 47% (2015) → **~60%**; ~90% of imports via Hormuz → ~54% of supply vulnerable.
[34:2:3] - **33 crore** domestic connections (33.3 crore households); **~10 crore added after 2017 under PMUY**.
[34:2:4] - **Underground LPG caverns: 1.4 lakh t – less than two days** of consumption; MoPNG: no more caverns planned.
[34:2:5] - Response: LPG and CNG on priority list under Essential Commodities Act; refineries maximised LPG (domestic output +40%; share 40% → 55%); booking interval urban 25 days, rural 45 days; ~60 lakh households can shift to PNG; **US deal 2.2 million t LPG/year** (~45 days shipping); IEA release of ~400 million barrels.
[34:2:6] - NRAI warned ~60% of restaurants could shut; IEA flagged lack of storage.
[34:2:7] - Gas: ~50% imported, 55–60% of it from West Asia → ~30% of LNG affected; PNG/CNG prioritised over industry.
[34:2:8] - Storage options: depleted gas fields – **KG Basin, Cambay, Mumbai Offshore** (**Deccan Traps not** among them).
[34:2:9] - **Ethyl mercaptan** (sulphur compound) is added to LPG for leak detection.

[34:4:8] # Rupee Management
[34:4:9] - RBI curbs excess volatility; **no exchange-rate target**; India – **managed float**.
[34:4:10] - RBI **defends the rupee by selling dollars** (absorbs rupees); **RBI buying dollars expands money supply** (sterilisation may follow).
[34:4:11] - **Higher FDI inflows → rupee appreciates**. Weak rupee → **imported inflation**, higher external debt burden; export gain limited by import content and price-taker status.
[34:4:12] - Forex reserves = **foreign currency assets, gold, SDRs, reserve tranche in IMF**.
[34:4:13] - **Dornbusch Overshooting Model**: exchange rate overreacts in the short run (sticky prices).
[34:4:14] - **Impossible Trinity** (Mundell-Fleming): cannot have fixed exchange rate + free capital mobility + independent monetary policy together.
[34:4:15] - Expansionary policy does not stop a rupee slide; **Masala Bonds**, easier ECB help. CAD financing hard due to **negative net FDI** and volatile FPI.
[34:4:16] - **2013 Taper Tantrum**: rupee fell; RBI tightened short-term rates, oil-dollar swap; gold import duty raised.
[34:4:17] - **Fowler Committee (1898)**: Gold Exchange Standard – rupee pegged at **1s 4d** (15 rupees = £1).
