## SUB 2 | Characteristics and Classification of Tribes
[2:0] # Meaning and features
[2:1] - **'Tribe'** comes from the **Latin 'tribus'**. **Marshall Sahlins:** a tribe is a **segmental** organisation of equivalent, **unspecialised multi-family groups, clans or bands**.
[2:2] - **Features (NextGen):** **segmentary but egalitarian**; **no mutual interdependence** (unlike caste); **direct access to land**; primitive subsistence technology (**shifting cultivation**, hunting, gathering); **animism**; monogamy with polygamy, **polyandry** among **Toda** and **Khasa**; mostly patrilineal, **matrilineal** among **Khasi, Jaintia, Garo**.
[2:3] - **Animism** = belief in and worship of **nature spirits**; **Totemism** = a clan's sacred bond with an **animal or plant totem**, revered and **not killed**; goes with **clan exogamy**.
[2:4] - **Shifting cultivation** = slash-and-burn; called **Jhum** in North-East India.
[2:5] - **Division of labour** in tribal society rests mainly on **age and sex (gender)**; **age-groups (age-sets)** are a basis of tribal stratification.
[2:6] - **Gender taboos:** among the **Santhal**, women may **not touch the plough** or **thatch (cover) the roof** of a house.
[2:7] - **Kula ring** (Trobriand Islands, Malinowski): ceremonial exchange of **shell necklaces (soulava)** and **armshells/bracelets (mwali)**, moving in opposite directions.
[2:8] - **Change:** loss of languages (e.g. **Bo**, Andamans, extinct 2010), new economic classes among Chotanagpur tribes in Assam tea gardens.

## SUB 3 | Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs)
[3:0] # Origin, criteria, numbers
[3:1] - **Dhebar Commission (1960-61)** flagged the least-developed groups; **PTG** category created **1973**; renamed **PVTGs** in **2006**.
[3:2] - **Criteria:** **pre-agricultural** technology, **low literacy**, economic backwardness, **declining or stagnant population**.
[3:3] - **75 PVTGs** in **18 States + 1 UT (Andaman and Nicobar)** – not 95.
[3:4] - **Odisha 13** (highest), then **AP with Telangana 12**; Bihar 9.
[3:5] - **Sahariyas** = largest PVTG; **Sentinelese** and Andamanese = smallest.
[3:6] - **Irular** (Tamil Nadu) and **Konda Reddi** (AP) are PVTGs.
[3:7] # AP PVTGs
[3:8] - Joint list "Andhra Pradesh and Telangana" = **12**: **Bodo Gadaba, Bondo Poroja, Chenchu, Dongaria Khond, Gutob Gadaba, Khond Poroja, Kolam, Konda Reddi, Konda Savara, Kutia Khond, Parengi Poroja, Thoti**.
[3:9] - **Kolam and Thoti** are on the Telangana side; residual AP = **10**. Older spellings: Bondo Poraja, Parangiperja, Kuttiya Kondhs, Kondareddis.
[3:10] - PVTG population in AP under PM-JANMAN: **4,92,552** (21 Nov 2024) – among the top three States.
[3:11] # PM-JANMAN and rights
[3:12] - **PM-JANMAN**, Ministry of Tribal Affairs; launched **15 Nov 2023** (Janjatiya Gaurav Divas); covers **all 75 PVTGs**; outlay **₹24,104 crore** (Centre ₹15,336 crore, States ₹8,768 crore), 2023-24 to 2025-26 (Budget 2023 announced ₹15,000 crore).
[3:13] - **Bharia (Madhya Pradesh)** = first PVTG with **habitat rights** under **FRA 2006, Sec. 3(1)(e)**.
[3:14] - **Shompen** (Great Nicobar) voted for the first time in the **2024 Lok Sabha** election (**Andaman and Nicobar** seat, 19 April 2024; 7 members); protected by the **A&N (Protection of Aboriginal Tribes) Regulation, 1956**; Great Nicobar project dispute raises FRA habitat rights.
[3:15] ~~ Sources: NextGen Society G2 Part 1 p.44-45; NextGen Society G2 Part 3 p.32; Rocket Indian Society p.76; app notes 2:111 sub 4, 8

[4:0] TABLE head: Year | Event
  [4:0.0] 1871 | Criminal Tribes Act branded about 200 communities "hereditary criminals" (amended 1924)
  [4:0.1] 1949 | All India Criminal Tribes Inquiry Committee (Ayyangar Committee)
  [4:0.2] 31 Aug 1952 | Act repealed → communities called "Denotified" tribes; replaced by State Habitual Offenders Acts
  [4:0.3] 2008 | Renke Commission
  [4:0.4] 2017 | Idate Commission

[4:1] - **De-notified tribes** = tribes **earlier classified as criminal tribes**; last counted in **Census 1931**.
[4:2] - **Nomadic tribes** = no permanent settlement, **frequently move from one place to another** (shepherds, hunters, entertainers); **semi-nomadic** move between fixed places.
[4:3] - Lists are state-specific: **Banchhada** (MP, Rajasthan), once a "criminal tribe", is listed as **SC**.
