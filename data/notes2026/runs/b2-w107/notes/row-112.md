=== Ethnicity – Concept, Identity and Minority
# Concept
- **Ethnic group** = people who identify with each other on a common **language/dialect, history, society, culture or nation**; markers – language, religion, race, region, culture.
- **Consensus approach:** groups **coexist** with separate identities; **conflict approach:** ethnic groups are **interest groups competing unequally** for power and resources (South Africa, Sri Lankan Tamils).
- **Xenocentrism** = preferring another group's culture and **rejecting one's own**; opposite of **ethnocentrism**.
# Views on ethnic identity
| View | Core idea | Scholars |
|---|---|---|
| Primordialist | Identity given by birth; "primordial attachments" | **Clifford Geertz**, Edward Shils |
| Instrumentalist | Elites mobilise identity for power | **Paul Brass**; Rajni Kothari (*Politics in India*); Christophe Jaffrelot (*India's Silent Revolution*) |
| Constructivist | Identity socially made, kept by boundaries | **Fredrik Barth** (*Ethnic Groups and Boundaries*); **Benedict Anderson** (*Imagined Communities*) |
# Minority
| Scholar | Contribution |
|---|---|
| T.K. Oommen | Separates **nation, nationality, ethnicity and citizenship** |
| **Arnold Rose** | Minority = group set apart by **race, nationality, religion or language**, regarded as different by itself and others |
| Giddens | Minority marked by disadvantage, group solidarity, isolation |
- **National minority:** non-dominant citizen group with its own ethnic, religious or linguistic identity; term from **European minority-rights law**.
- Abroad: **Rohingya** – Sunni Muslim minority of **Rakhine State, Myanmar**; China recognises **55** minority groups, **Hui** = largest Muslim group (Uyghurs too); China's "ethnic unity and progress" law (Mar 2026); Sri Lanka Tamil-Muslim common platform (Jul 2026).
