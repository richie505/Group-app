## 3. Repeats and traps
- The same item appears more than once: cotton/groundnut/rice kharif (×3); kharif-area statements from UPSC 2019 (×2); rice-exporter questions from 2019 (×2); rainfed crops from 2012 (×2); NFSM crops (×3); rice–wheat cropping system (×2).
- **The year in the stem matters.** WB was the top rice State and AP the surplus State when those questions were asked. Now UP leads rice (2024-25), and MP has replaced AP among maize leaders. These items carry ⚠ Outdated lines.
- An assertion-reason item says "tropical" cyclones for western disturbances. Western disturbances are temperate (extra-tropical) cyclones. The item is keyed the way the books key it and carries a ⚠ Disputed line.

## 4. Heavy in the old notes but never asked in PYQs
- Agro-climatic zones and ICAR institute locations (old sub 9) are asked only in the app's own MCQs. They are kept short.
- Crop varieties and diseases (old sub 10) are asked once. They are cut to one table.
- The old "Number game", "APPSC PYQ list" and "MCQ traps" sub-sections only repeated other sections. They are dropped, and their facts now sit in the topic sub-sections.
- Detailed NFSM funding, the 2026 monsoon deficit and the soybean, mentha and tobacco State tables are never asked. They are dropped. Commercial crops belong to row 82.
