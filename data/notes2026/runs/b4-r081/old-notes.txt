[81:4:2] # Origin, Inputs, Crops
[81:4:3] - **New Agricultural Strategy, 1965** (drought years 1965-67); **HYV Programme from Kharif 1966** (1966-67).
[81:4:4] - Aim: **foodgrain self-sufficiency** – India moved from **PL-480** wheat imports to self-sufficiency.
[81:4:5] - Inputs: **HYV seeds**, chemical fertilisers, pesticides, assured irrigation, mechanisation, **MSP**, rural electrification, rural roads, marketing.
[81:4:6] - Dwarf wheat: **Lerma Rojo 64-A, Sonora-63, Sonora-64, Mayo-64, S227**; Kalyan Sona; Sonora-64 developed by induced mutation at IARI.
[81:4:7] - Crops most benefited: **wheat** (output 2.5 times; first phase), then **rice** (later). Pulses, oilseeds, coarse cereals lagged.
[81:4:8] - Phases: 1st (mid-1960s-1980s) wheat; 2nd (1980s) spread east and west, growth 3.4% vs 2.3%; **3rd = price policy on MSP and procurement** (PYQ 2017).
[81:4:9] # Regions
[81:4:10] - First stage: **Punjab, Haryana, western UP** (Trans- and Upper Gangetic Plains); AP and TN rice deltas gained via irrigation.
[81:4:11] - Eastern belt (MP, Bihar, Odisha, WB, Assam) lagged.
