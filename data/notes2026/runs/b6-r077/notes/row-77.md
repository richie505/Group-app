=== Climate, Monsoon and Hazards
# El Niño and a Weak Monsoon
- First month of the 2026 monsoon: deficit about **40%**; IMD forecast July rainfall **below 94% of normal**.
- Reservoirs (**166**, CWC): **47.725 BCM** on 2 July vs 78.077 BCM last year.
- Agri exports: CAGR **8.2% (2020-2025)**, about **12%** of core exports; agriculture about **one-fifth of GVA**, **46%** of workforce, **55%** of population linked.
- El Niño drought years: **1972, 1982, 2009, 2015**.
- **El Niño**: warming of the **central-eastern equatorial Pacific**; Spanish for "**Little Boy / Christ Child**"; weakens **trade winds**, the **Humboldt (Peruvian) Current** and **upwelling**.
- ENSO phases: **El Niño, La Niña, Neutral**; a developing El Niño raises the **probability (not certainty)** of a weak monsoon.
- **IMD**: est. **1875**; **Ministry of Earth Sciences**; HQ **New Delhi**; **6 Regional Meteorological Centres** (Mumbai, Chennai, New Delhi, Kolkata, Nagpur, Guwahati); one of **six WMO RSMCs**, names North Indian Ocean cyclones.
> La Niña generally strengthens the Indian monsoon; El Niño weakens it.
# Glacial Lakes and GLOFs
- **Mago Chu Basin, Tawang (Arunachal)**: **4 of 5** glacial lakes expanded **2016-2026**, incl. high and very high risk lakes.
- ICIMOD lake types: **glacial erosion** (cirque, valley lakes); **moraine-dammed** = **most vulnerable to GLOF** (loose debris), e.g. **Samudra Tapu, Gepang Gath (HP)**.
- **Glacial Lake Atlas 2023**: by **NRSC (ISRO)**; **Brahmaputra basin** has the most glacial lakes.
- **Elevation-dependent warming**: high altitudes warm faster.
- Events: **Kedarnath 2013 (Chorabari lake)**; **South Lhonak Lake, Sikkim, 2023**.
- **NDMA GLOF Guidelines 2020**; **CWC** risk indexing; **National GLOF Risk Mitigation Project** (2025): **Arunachal, Himachal, Sikkim, Uttarakhand**.
- **Siachen Glacier**: north of the **Nubra Valley**.
# Flash Floods and Cloudbursts - Assam
- Assam floods first blamed on a **cloudburst in Nagaland**; IMD said it was heavy rainfall, below the cloudburst threshold.
- **IMD cloudburst**: more than **100 mm in one hour** over **20-30 sq km**.
- **Flash flood**: develops in **less than six hours**; not every flash flood is a cloudburst (dam release, GLOF, saturated soil, poor drainage).
- **1°C** warming = about **7%** more atmospheric moisture.
- Flash floods most frequent in the **Brahmaputra** basin, then Ganga and Krishna.
- **NDMA**: **Disaster Management Act 2005** (**23 Dec 2005**); constituted **27 Sep 2006**; chaired by **PM** (PM + 9 members); **SDMA - CM**; **DDMA - District Collector**; supervises **NIDM**; background: **High Powered Committee 1999**, Gujarat earthquake 2001. **NDRF** is under **MHA**.
# Europe's 2026 Heatwave - Omega Block
- **Omega Block**: high pressure between two lows, shaped like **Ω**; stalls the **jet stream** (west to east).
- **Heat dome**: sinking, compressed air under the high; **suppresses clouds and rain**.
- **Europe**: warming at more than **twice** the global rate: the **fastest-warming continent**.
- Terms: **climate whiplash** (rapid swing between extremes); albedo loss from Arctic ice; **tropical night**; **urban heat island**.
# Climate Talks and Indices
- **SB64 Bonn** (UNFCCC mid-year talks): **8-18 Jun 2026**; first **UNFCCC Dialogue on Trade and Climate** (13 Jun) saw developing countries object to climate-linked trade measures; **India** pushed back on new obligations beyond agreed mandates.
- **CCPI 2026** (by **Germanwatch, NewClimate Institute, CAN**; 63 countries + EU; released at **COP30, Belém**): **India 23rd**, down **13** places from 10th; now a **medium performer**; main reason: no coal phase-out timeline.

# Himalayan Forest Fires - Parliamentary Panel
- **Parliamentary Standing Committee on Science and Technology, Environment, Forest and Climate Change** recommended: a **geostationary satellite** for 24x7 fire monitoring; a regional climate-fire study; at least **2 NDRF centres** for Himalayan fires; a **National Pine-Needle Utilisation Policy**.
- Himalayas: about **17% of landmass** but about **57% of forest fire incidents**.
- **Chir pine needles = "Pirul"**: resin-rich, inflammable.
- **FSI vulnerability**: **54.40%** occasional fires; **7.49%** moderately frequent; **2.40%** high; **35.71%** largely unaffected.
- Season **November to June**; peak **March-May**.
- Satellites now used: foreign polar-orbiting **AQUA** and **Suomi-NPP**.
- Types: **ground**, **surface (common in India)**, **crown (rare in India)**.
- Setting forests on fire is punishable under **BNS 2023**; nodal ministry **MoEFCC**; **National Action Plan on Forest Fire** with a **Central Monitoring Committee**.
- Fire lines: **kachha** (undergrowth cleared), **pucca** (clear-felled strips).
# Small River Rejuvenation Framework (NMCG)

=== Biochar, Pesticides and Green Initiatives
# Biochar
- Made by **pyrolysis**: oxygen-depleted heating at **300-700°C**; by-products **syngas** and **bio-oil**.
- **Decomposes very slowly**: stores carbon for **centuries**.
- Benefits: water-holding **+10-25%**; yield **+10-30%**; **1 t biochar sequesters 2.5-3 t CO2e**.
- Carbon credits: **VM0042** methodology, **2-2.8 t CO2e** credits per tonne; **KISAN kiln** by **IIT Kharagpur**.
- India: crop waste **500 MT+/yr**; Punjab-Haryana burn **20 MT+** paddy straw; MSW **62 MT** (50%+ biodegradable).
- Potential: syngas **20-30 MT** (8-13 TWh); bio-oil **24-40 MT** (substitutes 12-19 MT diesel/kerosene).
- **Biochar concrete**: **2-5%** biochar; heat resistance **+20%**; captures ~**115 kg CO2/m³**.
- Trials: **Akola** (maize stalk, black soil); **Kerala** (coconut leaf stalk); abroad: Kenya (rice husk), Thailand, Brazil (**Embrapa**).
- PYQ (2020): biochar in **vertical farming** aids **nitrogen-fixing microbes** and **retains water**.
# Carbosulfan Ban Proposed
- **Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare** proposed a **complete ban** on **carbosulfan** (manufacture, import, transport, distribution, sale, use).
- A highly hazardous **carbamate** insecticide; converts to highly toxic **carbofuran**: harms birds, pollinators, aquatic life.
- After consulting the **Registration Committee** under the **Insecticides Act, 1968**; **Central Insecticides Board and Registration Committee** are under the Ministry of Agriculture.
