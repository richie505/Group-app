=== Online Safety Practices and Helplines
# Recommended vs NOT recommended
| Recommended | NOT recommended |
|---|---|
| **Strong passwords** | **Accepting friend requests from unknown people without caution** |
| Multi-factor / two-factor authentication | Clicking a link or **scanning a QR code to receive money** |
| **Verifying information before sharing** | Paying a "friend" who urgently asks for money without checking |
| Caution with personal/sensitive data; privacy settings | Open, unsecured Wi-Fi |
| **WPA3** Wi-Fi; keep digital footprint low | Sharing OTPs |
- Friend urgently asks for money: verify through **another channel** (ID may be hacked).
- Report cyber offences: **cybercrime.gov.in** and helpline **1930**.

=== Digital Literacy and Computer Literacy Day
- **Computer Literacy Day / World Computer Literacy Day:** **2 December** – started by **NIIT** in **2001** (its 20th anniversary); spreads computer skills, especially among women and children.
- Digital India **Vision Area 3** (Digital Empowerment of Citizens) begins with **Universal Digital Literacy**.
| Scheme | Feature |
|---|---|
| **PMGDISHA** | **One digitally literate person in each rural family**; launched by PM at Gandhinagar |
| **ULLAS** | 2022-27, **Ministry of Education** (not MeitY), adults 15+; digital + financial literacy |
- NSS CAMS 2022-23 (age 15-24): **78.4%** could send e-mail with attachment; 71.2% could copy-paste.
- **Himachal Pradesh** = 5th State/UT with full functional literacy.
- Other days: **21 March** Universal Acceptance Day; **1 July** Digital India anniversary; 10 Dec Human Rights Day.

=== Netiquette and Online Behaviour Terms
# Netiquette
- **Netiquette** = the **Internet's ethical protocol** (net + etiquette); **etiquette** = the customary code of polite behaviour in a social group.
- Core rule: **constructive online dialogue** and **respect privacy**; "**digital freedom** should be accompanied by **digital responsibility**".
| Medium | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Email | Clear subject line, polite greeting, check recipients | Type in **ALL CAPS** (reads as shouting); forward **chain mails** |
| Social media | Verify before sharing, respect privacy, credit sources | Post others' photos without consent (even if credited); troll |
| Online meetings | Join on time, **mute when not speaking** | Talk over others; record without permission |
| Online forums | Stay on topic, civil discourse | Trolling, **spamming** |
# Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Cyber venting** | Using websites to **pour out one's grievances** (e.g. against employer) |
| **Nomophobia** | Irrational fear of being **without one's mobile** (no signal, low battery, no balance) |
| Trolling | Deliberately provocative or abusive content |
| Cyberbullying | Repeated online harassment, esp. of children |
| Doxxing | Publicly sharing someone's private info without consent; **no universal legal definition**; prosecuted under stalking/harassment/cyber laws |
| Cyberstalking | Repeated unwanted monitoring or contact online |
| Digital footprint | Trail of data a person leaves online |
| Filter bubble | Algorithms rank content by **user preference**, isolating users |
# Sections → offence
| Section | Offence |
|---|---|
| IT Act **66E** | Privacy violation |
| IT Act 67 | Obscene material |
| **BNS 78** | Stalking, incl. monitoring electronic communication |

=== Misinformation, Fact-checks and Critical Thinking
| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Misinformation** | False content, **no intent** to deceive |
| **Disinformation** | False content **deliberately** created for political/social/economic goals |
| **Malinformation** | **Genuine** information shared to cause harm (leaked private data) |
- Echo chambers / filter bubbles strengthen fake news; **deepfakes** = AI-made fake audio/video.
- **Critical thinking** = determining the **quality of our beliefs** (evidence, reasoning) – not speed, memory or brainstorming; **NEP 2020** stresses **critical thinking and civic engagement** as an answer to fake news.
# Fact-check timeline
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **Nov 2019** | **PIB Fact Check Unit** set up – debunks fake news about **Government of India, ministries, PSUs** |
| 2021 | IT (Intermediary Guidelines & Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules under **Sec. 87**; SSMIs file **monthly** compliance reports |
| April 2023 | Amendment: intermediary loses safe harbour if content on Central Government business is flagged "fake" by **PIB FCU** |
| March 2024 | FCU notified; **Supreme Court stayed** it |
| Sept 2024 | **Bombay High Court** struck down the 2023 amendment |

=== Digital Citizenship and State Initiatives
- **Responsible digital citizen:** identifies misinformation, **verifies sources**, protects data, respects privacy; keeps a **low digital footprint**.
- Rights side: **online grievance redressal**, e-consultations, open government data (e.g. AP Grama and Ward Sachivalayam system).
- **Digital Detox** = **Karnataka** Government initiative with **AIGDF** (All India Game Developers Forum) on **gaming and social-media** addiction (not Kerala).
