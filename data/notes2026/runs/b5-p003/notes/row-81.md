=== The 17 SDGs – Number, Goal Numbers and Themes
# The 17 goals (most-asked angle)
| SDG | Goal | Key words in the stem |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **No Poverty** | end poverty in all its forms everywhere |
| 2 | **Zero Hunger** | end hunger, **food security**, improved nutrition, **sustainable agriculture** |
| 3 | **Good Health and Well-being** | healthy lives, well-being for all ages |
| 4 | **Quality Education** | inclusive, equitable quality education, **lifelong learning** |
| 5 | **Gender Equality** | empower all women and girls |
| 6 | **Clean Water and Sanitation** | **availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation** |
| 7 | **Affordable and Clean Energy** | affordable, reliable, sustainable, modern energy |
| 8 | **Decent Work and Economic Growth** | jobs, youth employment |
| 9 | Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure | (internet access only a target, 9.c) |
| 10 | **Reduced Inequalities** | within and among countries; safe, orderly migration |
| 11 | Sustainable Cities and Communities | urbanisation |
| 12 | **Responsible Consumption and Production** | |
| 13 | **Climate Action** | urgent action to combat climate change |
| 14 | **Life Below Water** | oceans, seas, marine resources (Blue Economy) |
| 15 | **Life on Land** | **forests**, desertification, land degradation, biodiversity |
| 16 | **Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions** | |
| 17 | **Partnerships for the Goals** | finance, technology, capacity-building, **trade** (means of implementation) |
- **Count:** **17 goals, 169 targets** (232 indicators) – asked in some 15 APPSC papers as "how many SDGs" (11 / 13 / 15 / 21 are distractors).
- **Grouping:** Goals **1–7** extend the MDG concerns; **8–10** inclusiveness, jobs, infrastructure, distribution; the last seven (**11–17**) sustainability, peace and partnership.
# Problem → goal
| Problem or theme | SDG |
|---|---|
| Food insecurity, malnutrition | 2 |
| Inadequate healthcare | 3 |
| Literacy, vocational skills | 4 |

| Unsafe drinking water, open defecation | 6 |

- **Goal 4 targets:** free primary and secondary education; early-childhood care; **affordable technical, vocational and tertiary** education; **Target 4.7** – knowledge for sustainable development, **global citizenship**, **culture of peace** and cultural diversity.
- **Goal 5 targets:** end **all discrimination and violence** against women and girls; end **child marriage** and **FGM**; **recognise unpaid care work**; women's **full participation in leadership**.
# NOT an SDG (NOT-type questions)
- **Not goals:** "Conflict-free outer space", "Life in sky", "Stop migration", "**Internet for all**", "**End racism**", "**Organising international sports tournaments**", **Blue Economy**, **Green Economy**, Digital Inclusion.
- **"Reducing poverty"** is the odd one out when Gender Equality, Zero Hunger and Peace–Justice are listed verbatim: SDG 1 seeks to **end** poverty ("No Poverty").
> ⚠ Trap: the two "life" goals are **Life Below Water (14)** and **Life on Land (15)**; SDG 2 is "Zero Hunger", not "No Hunger".

=== Agenda 2030 – Origin, Adoption and the MDGs
# Adoption
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Proposed at | **Rio+20** (UN Conference on Sustainable Development), Rio de Janeiro, **2012**; idea from **Colombia and Guatemala** |
| Rio+20 outcome | **"The Future We Want"** – called for goals to follow the MDGs |
| Framework | **"Transforming our World: the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development"** (not 2035) |
| Adopted | **25 September 2015**, UN Sustainable Development Summit, **UNGA 70th session**, by all **193** member states (not 198) |
| Period | **2016–2030**, a **15-year** plan; in force **1 January 2016**; target year **2030** |
| Motto | **"Leave no one behind"** – a universal call to action to end poverty and protect the planet |
- **Universal:** binds all countries (MDGs were mainly for developing countries); three dimensions – **economic, social, environmental**.
- **Human-rights basis:** guided by the **UDHR (1948)** and human-rights treaties.
- **USDG vision:** universal access to **clean water, sanitation and healthcare** is essential for **human dignity**.
- **"A Million Voices: The World We Want"** (Sept 2013): post-2015 consultation report of the **UN Development Group (UNDG)**.
# MDGs (Millennium Summit 2000)
| Feature | MDGs | SDGs |
|---|---|---|
| Goals / targets | **8** / 21 | **17** / 169 |
| Period / deadline | 2000–**2015** | 2016–**2030** |
| Scope | Mainly developing countries | Universal |
- **The 8 MDGs:** extreme poverty and hunger; universal primary education; gender equality; child mortality; maternal health; HIV/AIDS, malaria; environmental sustainability; global partnership.
- **Results:** under-5 mortality **90 → 43** per 1,000; India **halved extreme poverty** (MDG target) before 2015.
# Chronology
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1951 | India's First Five Year Plan |
| 2000 | Millennium Declaration – 8 MDGs |
| 2002 | UNGA res. **57/254** → **UN Decade of Education for Sustainable Development 2005–2014** (lead agency **UNESCO**) |
| 2012 | Rio+20 – SDG idea |
| 2015 | Agenda 2030 adopted |
| 2016 | SDGs in force; first SDSN SDG Index |
| 2018 | First SDG India Index (NITI Aayog) |
| 2021 | NER District SDG Index, 1st edition (August) |
> Order: **Rio+20 2012 → Agenda 2030 2015 → in force 2016 → SDG India Index 2018**.

=== SDG Monitoring in India – NITI Aayog, MoSPI and Localisation
# Who does what
| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| **NITI Aayog** | **Nodal agency** to oversee and monitor SDGs; **SDG India Index** (ranks States) |
| **MoSPI** | Develops the **National Indicator Framework (NIF)**; **SDG-NIF Progress Report 2026: 277 indicators** |
| UN SDSN | Sustainable Development Report (ranks countries) |
- **NIF is MoSPI's, not NITI Aayog's.** India's **Voluntary National Review (VNR)** goes to the UN HLPF.
- **Multidimensional poverty (NIF):** **24.85% (2015-16) → 14.96% (2019-21)**.
# Localisation
- **Localised SDGs in Gram Panchayats** (**Ministry of Panchayati Raj**): **nine themes** – poverty-free, healthy, child-friendly, water-sufficient, clean and green, self-sufficient infrastructure, socially just and secure, good governance, **women-friendly village**; "industrially advanced village" is **not** one.
- **NER District SDG Index:** 1st edition **August 2021**.
- **SDG Conclave 2020** ("Partnerships, Cooperation and Development of North Eastern States", Guwahati, 24-26 Feb 2020): **NITI Aayog** with **North Eastern Council, Government of Assam, Tata Trusts, RIS, UNDP** – **not the World Bank**.
> Easy rule: **NITI ranks States, SDSN ranks countries, MoSPI counts indicators.**

=== Global SDG Reports – UN SDG Report and SDSN Index
# UN Sustainable Development Goals Report (UN DESA)
- **SDG Report 2022:** a record **100 million** people forcibly displaced (May 2022); electricity access **91%** (2020) – about **9% (733 million)** still without power.
- **SDG Report 2026:** released by **UN DESA** on **7 July 2026**; 17 SDGs, deadline 2030.
# SDSN Sustainable Development Report (SDG Index)
| Edition | India's rank |
|---|---|
| 2016 (first SDG Index) | **110th of 149** |
| Other earlier editions | 112th (counted from 2015), 109th |
| SDR 2025 | **99th** – first time in top 100; Finland (87) top |
| SDR 2026 (11th edition) | **94th of 169** (score 68.3) – best ever |
- **SDR 2026:** Finland 1st, Sweden, Denmark; India lags on **13 of 17** SDGs, **SDG 2** the biggest concern; still below **Bhutan, Maldives, Nepal, Sri Lanka**; only **17%** of targets on track globally.
