=== NPIT 2012 – Goals, Context and Targets
# Twin goals
- **Goals:** bring the **full power of ICT within the reach of all** India, and make India the **Global Hub and Destination for IT and ITES Services by 2020**.
- **Context (2011-12):** IT = **USD 100 bn** industry, **~80%** of revenue from **exports**; **six hubs** (Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Pune, NCR) ≈ **90%** of the industry and near saturation.
- **Spread, not concentrate:** NPIT aimed to diversify IT into **Tier II and Tier III cities** and rural areas.
- **Objectives:** jobs; ICT in education, health, skills, financial inclusion, governance; Aadhaar-based authentication, PKI, payment gateways; e-governance through the NeGP.
# Targets (all by 2020)
| NPIT 2012 target | Value |
|---|---|
| IT-ITES revenue | **US$ 100 bn → US$ 300 bn** |
| IT exports | **US$ 69 bn → US$ 200 bn** |
| Skilled manpower | **10 million** more trained ICT workers |
| e-literacy | **One e-literate person in every household** |
> ⚠ Trap: NPIT 2012 deadline = **2020** (not 2025); $300 bn = revenue, $200 bn = exports.

=== National Policy on Electronics 2019 and Schemes
- **NPE 2019:** approved **20 February 2019**; India as a global hub for **ESDM**, **including chipsets**.
| NPE 2019 target (by 2025) | Value |
|---|---|
| Electronics manufacturing | **US$ 400 bn** (~₹26 lakh crore) |
| Mobile handsets | **1 billion a year**; 600 million for export |
| Mobile manufacturing / exports | US$ 190 bn / **US$ 110 bn** |
- **Schemes (2020):** **PLI** (large-scale electronics), **SPECS** (**components and semiconductors**), **EMC 2.0** (notified **1 Apr 2020**; **electronics clusters + common facility centres**).
- **MPMS** (Aug 2026): **₹62,500 crore**, FY 2026-27 to 2030-31; production **₹39 lakh crore**, exports ₹15 lakh crore; replaces **PLI (Large Scale Electronics)**.

=== Outcomes and AP IT Policy
- **Digital economy:** **~12-14% of GDP**; projected **~20% by 2029-30**.
- **Electronics:** production **₹13.11 lakh crore** (2025-26); electronics = **3rd-largest export** item.
- **GCCs:** offshore units of MNCs serving their **own parent** (outsourcing vendors serve unrelated clients).
- **AP IT & GCC Policy 4.0 (2024-29):** in force **12 Dec 2024**; AP Data Centre Policy: up to **1 GW**.
- **Visakhapatnam:** **cable landing station**, AI data projects with Anakapalli, **Infosys** campus (~7,000 jobs), IoT CoE.
- **Amaravati:** **Quantum Valley** (C-DAC centre), NIELIT campus.
