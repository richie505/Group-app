=== Mass Communication and Media Basics
- **Mass communication:** **impersonal**, reaches a large scattered audience (**universal access**), passes through **gatekeepers**; feedback is **delayed and indirect** – so **instant feedback is NOT** one of its characteristics.
- **Audio conferencing** = live voice exchange among two or more parties → **two-sided verbal communication** (no non-verbal cues).
- **Sign** (semiotics, Saussure) = anything that **produces meanings** (signifier + signified).
- **Narrative** = a **structured sequence of events in time** (a story told in order).
- **Lifestyle channels** = TV channels on **home, garden and family** (food, travel, décor).
- **Television:** invented by **J.L. Baird** (first working TV, 1926) – dynamite = Nobel, radioactivity = Becquerel, radio = Marconi; the medium credited (printed key) with the biggest role in the **knowledge empowerment** of people in India.
