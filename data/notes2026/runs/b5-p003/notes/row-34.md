=== Sriharikota (SDSC-SHAR)
# Location and history
- **District:** **Nellore** when the APPSC 2019 papers were set; **Tirupati** district now (April 2022 reorganisation).
- Barrier island between **Pulicat Lake (west)** and the **Bay of Bengal (east)**; it divides the lake from the sea (not Chilika, Mahanadi or Godavari mouth).
- Set up **1971** as **Sriharikota High Altitude Range (SHAR)**; renamed **2002** after **Satish Dhawan**.
- Why chosen: **east coast** (eastward launch gains from Earth's rotation), **near the equator**, **sea** for spent stages, **thinly populated**.
# Facilities and launches
| Pad | Vehicles |
|---|---|
| First Launch Pad | PSLV, SSLV |
| Second Launch Pad | GSLV, LVM3; standby for PSLV |
| Third Launch Pad | For NGLV; approved 16 Jan 2025, Rs 3,984.86 crore, 48 months |
- **SPROB** (Solid Propellant Space Booster Plant): casts **S200** boosters of LVM3.
- **Launch View Gallery:** opened **31 March 2019** (PSLV-C45); first phase seats **5,000** visitors.
| Launch | Mission | Date |
|---|---|---|
| **ISRO's 100th space mission** | PSLV-C21, **from Sriharikota** | **Sept 2012** |
| 100th launch from SHAR | GSLV-F15/NVS-02 | 29 Jan 2025 |
- **February 2004:** blast at the solid propellant plant at SHAR, **Sriharikota**.
# Second spaceport – Kulasekarapattinam
- **SSLV Launch Complex**, **Thoothukudi** district, **Tamil Nadu** (approved 2023); **direct southward launch** to polar/sun-synchronous orbit, without the fuel-costly dog-leg around **Sri Lanka**.

=== IIRS and Training Bodies
- **IIRS** (Indian Institute of Remote Sensing), **Dehradun** (not New Delhi, Lucknow, Nagpur); constituent unit of **ISRO, Department of Space**.
- Founded **1966** as the **Indian Photo-interpretation Institute (IPI)** (not 1956, 1986, 1996).
- Disciplines: **remote sensing, photogrammetry, cartography, geo-informatics/GIS** – **not angiography** (medical imaging).
- Field equipment (tree caliper, electronic levelling instrument, snow density meter …) does **not** include a **LINAC** (linear accelerator).
- **Director, Dec 2019:** **Dr Prakash Chauhan**.
- **CSSTEAP**: Centre for Space Science and Technology Education in Asia and the Pacific; **UN-affiliated**; **1995**; hosted at IIRS Dehradun.

=== Ground Network – ISTRAC
- **ISTRAC** (HQ Bengaluru) runs the TTC network tracking launch vehicles through the ascent phase; stations abroad: **Mauritius, Brunei, Biak (Indonesia)**; **Indian Deep Space Network**: **Byalalu**, near Bengaluru.
- **MoU, August 2017:** **ISTRAC/ISRO/Department of Space** with **CSIR-National Physical Laboratory (NPL)** – time and frequency traceability for ISRO's IRNWT timing stations (NavIC); valid 5 years.
- ISRO-ESA Technical Implementing Plan for **ground-tracking support** for Gaganyaan.
