=== Andhra Pradesh Digital Governance
# Service-delivery chain
- **AP chain:** **e-Seva (2001) → MeeSeva (2011) → e-Pragati → RTGS → Mana Mitra (2025)**.
- **e-Seva (2001):** **India's first integrated citizen service centres** (bill payments and certificates at one centre).
- **MeeSeva** = "**At your service**" (Telugu): launched **4 November 2011** (Chittoor) in undivided AP – **single-window** delivery through MeeSeva centres and authorised agents (**about 11,079** in Jan 2023).
- **Legal backing:** **AP IT (Electronic Service Delivery) Rules, 2011** (G.O.Ms.No.10, 18 Oct 2011) – legal sanctity to **digitally signed certificates**; departments to migrate to electronic delivery.
- **Gram/Ward Sachivalayam:** launched **2 October 2019** to bring governance to the doorstep; **10,000+** secretariats, **500+** services, **SPANDANA** grievance portal.
# e-Pragati and RTGS
- **e-Pragati:** **whole-of-government** programme – all e-governance systems **interconnected and integrated**, free flow of information; the digital route to **'Sunrise AP 2022'**.
- **Core datasets:** **People Hub, Land Hub, Entity Hub, GIS Hub, IoT Hub** (no Revenue Hub).
- **RTGS (Real Time Governance Society):** **Data Lake, Data Lens, Command & Control Centre**.
- **Mana Mitra (WhatsApp Governance):** AI-powered, launched **30 January 2025**; **500+** services.

=== NeGP 2006 and e-Kranti
# National e-Governance Plan
- **E-Governance** = use of computers/ICT in governance to deliver services – the IT-based system of governance.
- Framed by **DeitY + DARPG**; approved **18 May 2006**.
- **27 MMPs** (**9 Central, 11 State, 7 Integrated**) + **10 components**; later **31 MMPs**.
- **Vision:** all government services **accessible to the common man in his locality** through **common service delivery outlets**, with **efficiency, transparency and reliability at affordable costs**.
- **Integrated MMPs:** **e-Courts** (judiciary ICT, since **2007**), **e-Procurement**, National Portal. **State MMP:** **e-Panchayat**.
- **MMP** = **Mission Mode Project**.
| Item | NeGP (1.0) | e-Kranti (NeGP 2.0) |
|---|---|---|
| MMPs | 27 → 31 | **44** |
| Place | Stand-alone national plan | **A pillar of Digital India** |
> APPSC asked: approval year (2006), vision (locality, affordable cost), MMP count (27), e-Courts (2007).

=== DigiLocker, e-Sign and Secure Messaging
- **DigiLocker:** **MeitY**, **February 2015** – secure personal space to **store and share digital documents**, linked to **Aadhaar**; DL and RC linked with MoRTH (Sept 2016); **56.06 crore** users.
- **Legal validity:** documents = originals under **IT Act, 2000** + **Digital Locker Rules, 2016** (not the RTI Act).
- **e-Sign:** **Aadhaar eKYC-based**; **PKI**, not blockchain; legal backing **IT Act, 2000**; C-DAC's **e-Hastakshar** among issuers.
- **GIMS** = **Government Instant Messaging System** – government's WhatsApp alternative, released as **Sandes**; built by **NIC**.
- **NIC Mail:** integrated with **CollabFiles** office suite, e-Sign, Bhashini.
