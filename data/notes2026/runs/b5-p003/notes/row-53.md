=== Energy Units and Basic Terms
# Units – the most-asked angle
| Quantity | Unit | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Energy (work) | **joule (J)** – SI unit | kWh is a commercial unit of **energy**, not power |
| Power | **watt (W)** | rate of using energy; kilowatt, HP are power units |
| 1 unit of electricity | **1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J** | = 1,000 W × 3,600 s |
| Current / voltage / charge | ampere / volt / coulomb | distractors for "unit of power" |
- **Formula:** units (kWh) = watts × hours ÷ 1,000.
  - 100 W × 4 h = 400 Wh = **0.4 unit**; 5 bulbs × 100 W × 20 h = **10 units**; 100 W × 10 h = 1 unit; 60 W × 5 h × 30 days = 9 units.
- **Energy crisis:** the danger that fossil fuels such as coal and petroleum run out.
# Grid and energy terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Energy intensity | Energy used per unit of GDP; a **falling** value = growth decoupling from energy use |
| Peak demand | Highest power drawn in a **15-minute** interval |
| Energy shortage / peak shortage | Unmet energy in **MU** / unmet peak in **MW** |
| Energy security | **Four A's**: availability, accessibility, affordability, acceptability (**not accumulation**) |
| Primary vs final energy | Coal, petroleum and natural gas are primary sources that are also used directly as final energy |
> Units PYQs (UPPCS 2017, UP Lower 2009, NDA 2018, SSC 2017, BPSC): always convert W × h → kWh; "kWh is a unit of energy".

=== Demand, Mix, Per-capita Use and HDI
# Demand and mix
- India = **third-largest energy consumer** and **second-largest coal consumer**; demand to **triple by 2047**.
- **Coal/thermal** gives the **largest share of power generation**: ~**55% of the energy mix**, **74%+ of generation** (APPSC EO: "maximum share" = **thermal power**).
- After fossil fuels, the second source of supply is **solar** (UPPSC 2021).
- **Non-fossil capacity:** 32% (2014) → **51% (2025)**; the 50% target was crossed five years early.
- **Sustainable choice:** of coal, oil/gas, hydro and atomic energy, **hydroelectricity** is the renewable option that does not use up its resource (UPPCS 2012).
# Per-capita consumption and HDI
- **Per-capita electricity:** **957 kWh (FY14) → 1,460 kWh (FY25)**; about **one-fifth of China's**, one-ninth of the USA's – lower than China's.
- **Per-capita energy:** about **one-third of the world average**; low, with a large **urban-rural** gap.
- **HDI link** (APPSC Degree Lecturers 2017): a **certain minimum per-capita energy consumption is needed for a minimally acceptable level of well-being** – hence the strong correlation.
# Sectors and drivers
- **Industry** uses ~**50%** of electricity and stays the largest consumer by 2040; **cooking** energy demand falls.
- Household electrification ~**99%**; **Saubhagya** connected ~**26 million** households.
- Drivers of peak: **EVs, farm pumps, appliances, cooling, data centres, telecom**; peak **183 GW (Dec 2020) → 250+ GW (Apr 2026)**, ~37% rise.

=== Peak Demand and Deficit
- **Shortage trend:** **10–15%** earlier → **4.2% (FY14)** → **0.03% (2025)** → **zero by Nov 2025** (first zero-shortage month).
- **Record peak:** **270.8 GW (May 2026)**, about **90 GW** above 2019.
- **25 Apr 2026:** no shortage in solar hours; about **2% deficit in non-solar hours** → the **evening peak (6–11 p.m.)** drives the **storage** push.
- **Delhi's response** to the evening peak: **Time-of-Day tariffs and smart meters**.
- **Regional imbalances:** poor **storage** and slow **transmission** strand renewable power; heatwaves stress grids.
- **AT&C losses:** **22.62% (FY14) → 15.04% (FY25)**.

=== Fuel Gap, NDC Targets and Plans
# Fuel gap
| Fuel | Position |
|---|---|
| Crude oil | ~**88%** imported |
| Natural gas / LNG | ~**50%** imported; **Strait of Hormuz** a risk to LPG (2026 gas crisis) |
| Share of world reserves | oil **0.4%**, gas 0.6%, **coal 7%** (not oil 7%) |
# NDC targets (versus 2005)
| NDC | Emission-intensity cut | Non-fossil capacity |
|---|---|---|
| First NDC (2015) | 33–35% by 2030 | ~40% |
| Updated NDC (Aug 2022) | **45% by 2030** | 50% |
| New NDC 2031–35 | **47% by 2035** | **60%** (not 50%) |
- Emission intensity already **36% below 2005**.
- **BEE** and **NMEEE** (a mission of the **NAPCC**) work under the **Energy Conservation Act 2001** through **ESCOs** (Energy Service Companies).
- **Draft National Electricity Policy 2026** – **Ministry of Power**; replaces the **2005** policy; **100 GW pumped storage by 2047**.
- **COP31 "35 by 35":** electricity = **35%** of final energy by 2035. **Nuclear:** 24×7 baseload, cuts fossil imports.
# Andhra Pradesh
- CEA **Resource Adequacy Plan** (2025-26 to 2035-36): peak **30,927 MW** by 2035-36; ~**5,588 MW** new thermal; **APERC** cleared the first **1,600 MW**.
- Sep 2026 roadmap: **1,500 MW / 6,000 MWh BESS**; central VGF project with **NHPC 500 MW / 1,000 MWh**; **APGENCO** allowed to import coal.
