=== Cryptocurrency and Bitcoin
- **Cryptocurrency:** money transfers secured with **encryption (cryptography)**, peer-to-peer, **without a central bank** (not legal tender, RTGS, NEFT).
- **Bitcoin** (**2009**): first cryptocurrency, by pseudonymous **Satoshi Nakamoto**; decentralised; coins created by **mining** (complex software / computation).
- **UPSC 2016:** Bitcoins are **not tracked by central banks** ✗; anyone with an address can send/receive ✓; payments possible **without either side knowing the other's identity** ✓ → **2 and 3 only**.
- **CDS 2017:** decentralised ✓, generated by complex software ✓, **RBI never recognised it as legal tender** ✗ → **I and II**.
- **Japan:** recognised Bitcoin as a **legal payment method from 1 April 2017**.
- **BuyUcoin:** first Indian platform for **wholesale crypto trading** (April 2019).
- **Not a cryptocurrency:** the **Yen** (Japan's fiat currency).
- India: crypto **not legal tender**; VDA providers under **PMLA (March 2023)**; **30%** tax on VDA gains (**Sec 115BBH**, Finance Act **2022**) + **1% TDS** (**Sec 194S**); losses cannot be set off.
~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 7 p.113-116; NextGen S&T G2 Part 1 p.26; gktoday.in 25 Apr 2019 (BuyUcoin); app notes 5:19 sub 2; general knowledge, not checked (Japan 2017)

=== Blockchain and Distributed Ledger
- **Blockchain:** a **time-stamped series of immutable records** managed by a **cluster of computers not owned by any single entity**; first introduced in **Bitcoin** (Satoshi Nakamoto).
- **UPSC 2020:** public ledger everyone can inspect, no single user controls ✓; data only about cryptocurrency ✗; apps can be built **without anybody's permission** ✓ → **1 and 3 only**.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Distributed ledger | Shared ledger copied across nodes; single source of truth; no central validator |
| Block | Transactions + **timestamp** + **hash of the previous block** (not private keys) |
| Hash / node | Cryptographic fingerprint of a block / a computer holding a ledger copy |
| Immutability | Cannot be altered without consensus – no central authority changes data |
| Consensus | Rule by which nodes agree on valid transactions |
- Stakeholders **hold copies** and changes need **consensus** – so records are visible but not alterable.
- **Uses:** land records, certificates, supply chain, **e-voting**, health records, e-Notary, farm insurance.
~~ Sources: NextGen S&T G2 Part 1 p.26; PMF S&T Part 7 p.107, p.111-112; app notes 5:19 sub 0

=== CBDC – the e-Rupee
- **Digital rupee (e₹)** = India's **CBDC**, **sovereign currency issued by the RBI**, legal tender; a **direct liability on the RBI's balance sheet**; **freely convertible against commercial bank money and cash**; **not "insured against inflation"** → UPSC 2024: **1, 2 and 4**.
| Pilot | Start | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Wholesale (e₹-W) | **1 Nov 2022** | Settlement of **secondary-market government securities** |
| Retail (e₹-R) | **1 Dec 2022** | Closed user group – **Mumbai, New Delhi, Bengaluru, Bhubaneswar** (not Hyderabad) |
- RBI **concept note on CBDC: October 2022**. e-rupee is RBI money; UPI only moves bank-account money.
~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 5 p.105-106; app notes 5:19 sub 5; general knowledge, not checked (convertibility, RBI concept note)

=== Types and Consensus Mechanisms
| Public / permissionless | Private / permissioned |
|---|---|
| Anyone can join, read, validate (**Bitcoin**) | Only authorised entities (**AP DigiVerify**) |
| **Proof of Work** – miners race to solve puzzles; far more energy | **Proof of Stake** – validators chosen by coins staked |
| Bitcoin still PoW | **Ethereum** since **The Merge (Sept 2022)** |
~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 7 p.107; app notes 5:19 sub 1
