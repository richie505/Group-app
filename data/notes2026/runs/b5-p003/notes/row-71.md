=== PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana and Net Metering
- **Launched Feb 2024; MNRE**; Central Sector scheme; outlay **₹75,021 crore**; goal = **rooftop solar on 1 crore residential houses** by **March 2027** – rural and urban (not urban-only).
- **Up to 300 units/month free**; households become **prosumers**.
- **CFA: 60% for the first 2 kW; 40% for extra capacity up to 3 kW** (not a flat 30%); collateral-free loans at about **7%** (repo-linked, not 4%); **100% online** national portal.
- **Capacity building:** MNRE trains people at the grassroots in installation, operation, maintenance and repair; **more than 3 lakh skilled manpower** through fresh skilling and upskilling.
- 30 GW rooftop; **one Model Solar Village per district**; **world's largest domestic rooftop solar scheme**; subsumed Rooftop Solar Phase II.
- Progress (2025): 14.43 lakh systems, 18.14 lakh households.
- **Net metering (UPSC 2016 ×2):** promotes **production and use of solar energy by households/consumers** – surplus fed to the grid; household pays only the difference.
> Statements angle: UPSC/IAS 2025 – all three statements correct; RAS 2024 – only "1 crore houses / 300 units" and "100% online" correct.

=== UDAY and RDSS – Discom Finances
- **UDAY (Ujwal DISCOM Assurance Yojana), Nov 2015, Ministry of Power:** **financial turnaround and revival of DISCOMs** (UPSC 2016).
- States took over **75%** of discom debt (**50% in 2015-16 + 25% in 2016-17**) via **UDAY bonds**; discoms bonded the other 25%.
- UDAY states account for **97%** of total outstanding discom debt (Power Ministry; APPSC G2); bonds actually issued covered ~86% of debt to be taken over (2017).
- **Rajasthan** had the **largest discom debt exposure** under UDAY (~₹70,000+ crore bonded).
| Feature | UDAY | RDSS (2021) |
|---|---|---|
| Aim | Discom debt takeover | **Smart prepaid metering**, loss cut |
| Outlay | – | ₹3,03,758 crore |
| AT&C target | **15% by 2018-19** | **12–15%** |
| ACS-ARR gap | Zero | Zero |

=== Saubhagya, DDUGJY and IPDS – Electrification
- **Saubhagya** (Pradhan Mantri Sahaj Bijli Har Ghar Yojana), **Sept/Oct 2017:** **universal household electrification** – last-mile connections to all un-electrified households in **rural and urban** areas (CDS 2020; APPSC FSO).
  - Not clean cooking fuel (that is **PMUY**), not LPG subsidy.
  - **2.86 crore** willing households electrified (2.82 crore = older figure).
- **DDUGJY (Dec 2014):** rural distribution; **feeder separation** (agri vs non-agri); subsumed **RGGVY**.
- **IPDS (Dec 2014):** **urban** sub-transmission and distribution, metering, IT; subsumed **R-APDRP**.
- Last village electrified: **Leisang (Manipur), 28 April 2018**.

| Saubhagya | 2017 | Power |
| PM-KUSUM | 2019 | **MNRE** (not Power) |
| Surya Nutan | 2022 | **IOCL** indoor solar stove (not EESL) |
| PM Surya Ghar | 2024 | MNRE |

=== PM-KUSUM – Farm Solar
- **PM-KUSUM (2019, MNRE):** primary aim = **subsidies for solar irrigation pumps** and farm solar (APPSC FBO 2025).
| Component | Covers |
|---|---|
| A | Grid-connected solar/RE plants on farm/barren land |
| B | **Standalone solar pumps** |
| C | **Solarisation of grid-connected pumps** |
- B and C subsidy: **30% Centre, 30% State, 40% farmer** (bank loan possible).
- Urban household rooftop solar = PM Surya Ghar, not PM-KUSUM.
~~ Sources: app notes 5:71 sub 3; NextGen S&T Group 2 Part 5 p.55

=== Andhra Pradesh – Free Power and Rooftop Solar
- **Free power for SC/ST households:** the Social Welfare Department paid bills up to **75 units** (APPSC question) → **100 units** (Dec 2018, Jagjeevan Jyothi) → **200 units** for BPL SC/ST since 2019 (now **20.26 lakh** households; ₹10,449.92 crore subsidy, FY 2024-25).
- **SC/ST:** free rooftop solar via **Virtual Net Metering**.
- PM Surya Ghar target in AP: **6 lakh** (6,000 per constituency).
- PM-KUSUM in AP: **2.93 lakh** pumps solarised; RDSS ₹15,083.14 crore.
~~ Sources: thehansindia.com 'Free power up to 100 units to SC, ST households' (6 Dec 2018, via search); app notes 5:71 sub 6

=== Clean Cooking – PMUY
- **PMUY (2016, MoPNG):** deposit-free LPG to poor women; Phase I **8 crore** by **2019**; now **9.5 crore+**.
- **Ujjwala 2.0:** free first refill and stove; migrants by **self-declaration**; **₹1,600** per connection.
- Refill subsidy since **Oct 2023: ₹300 × 9 refills/year**.
