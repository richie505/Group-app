## SUB 1 | Digital Literacy and Computer Literacy Day
[1:0] - **Computer Literacy Day / World Computer Literacy Day:** **2 December** – started by **NIIT** in **2001** (its 20th anniversary); spreads computer skills, especially among women and children.
[1:1] - Digital India **Vision Area 3** (Digital Empowerment of Citizens) begins with **Universal Digital Literacy**.
[1:2] TABLE head: Scheme | Feature
  [1:2.0] PMGDISHA | One digitally literate person in each rural family; launched by PM at Gandhinagar
  [1:2.1] ULLAS | 2022-27, Ministry of Education (not MeitY), adults 15+; digital + financial literacy
[1:3] - NSS CAMS 2022-23 (age 15-24): **78.4%** could send e-mail with attachment; 71.2% could copy-paste.
[1:4] - **Himachal Pradesh** = 5th State/UT with full functional literacy.
[1:5] - Other days: **21 March** Universal Acceptance Day; **1 July** Digital India anniversary; 10 Dec Human Rights Day.

## SUB 2 | Netiquette and Online Behaviour Terms
[2:0] # Netiquette
[2:1] - **Netiquette** = the **Internet's ethical protocol** (net + etiquette); **etiquette** = the customary code of polite behaviour in a social group.
[2:2] - Core rule: **constructive online dialogue** and **respect privacy**; "**digital freedom** should be accompanied by **digital responsibility**".
[2:3] TABLE head: Medium | Do | Don't
  [2:3.0] Email | Clear subject line, polite greeting, check recipients | Type in ALL CAPS (reads as shouting); forward chain mails
  [2:3.1] Social media | Verify before sharing, respect privacy, credit sources | Post others' photos without consent (even if credited); troll
  [2:3.2] Online meetings | Join on time, mute when not speaking | Talk over others; record without permission
  [2:3.3] Online forums | Stay on topic, civil discourse | Trolling, spamming
[2:4] # Terms
[2:5] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [2:5.0] Cyber venting | Using websites to pour out one's grievances (e.g. against employer)
  [2:5.1] Nomophobia | Irrational fear of being without one's mobile (no signal, low battery, no balance)
  [2:5.2] Trolling | Deliberately provocative or abusive content
  [2:5.3] Cyberbullying | Repeated online harassment, esp. of children
  [2:5.4] Doxxing | Publicly sharing someone's private info without consent; no universal legal definition; prosecuted under stalking/harassment/cyber laws
  [2:5.5] Cyberstalking | Repeated unwanted monitoring or contact online
  [2:5.6] Digital footprint | Trail of data a person leaves online
  [2:5.7] Filter bubble | Algorithms rank content by user preference, isolating users
[2:6] # Sections → offence
[2:7] TABLE head: Section | Offence
  [2:7.0] IT Act 66E | Privacy violation
  [2:7.1] IT Act 67 | Obscene material
  [2:7.2] BNS 78 | Stalking, incl. monitoring electronic communication

## SUB 3 | Misinformation, Fact-checks and Critical Thinking
[3:0] TABLE head: Type | Meaning
  [3:0.0] Misinformation | False content, no intent to deceive
  [3:0.1] Disinformation | False content deliberately created for political/social/economic goals
  [3:0.2] Malinformation | Genuine information shared to cause harm (leaked private data)
[3:1] - Echo chambers / filter bubbles strengthen fake news; **deepfakes** = AI-made fake audio/video.
[3:2] - **Critical thinking** = determining the **quality of our beliefs** (evidence, reasoning) – not speed, memory or brainstorming; **NEP 2020** stresses **critical thinking and civic engagement** as an answer to fake news.
[3:3] # Fact-check timeline
[3:4] TABLE head: Date | Event
  [3:4.0] Nov 2019 | PIB Fact Check Unit set up – debunks fake news about Government of India, ministries, PSUs
  [3:4.1] 2021 | IT (Intermediary Guidelines & Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules under Sec. 87; SSMIs file monthly compliance reports
  [3:4.2] April 2023 | Amendment: intermediary loses safe harbour if content on Central Government business is flagged "fake" by PIB FCU
  [3:4.3] March 2024 | FCU notified; Supreme Court stayed it
  [3:4.4] Sept 2024 | Bombay High Court struck down the 2023 amendment
