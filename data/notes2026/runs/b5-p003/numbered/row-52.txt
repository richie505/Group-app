[1:0] # iDEX ecosystem

  [1:1.0] iDEX (Innovations for Defence Excellence) | Launched by the PM, April 2018; ecosystem for innovation in defence and aerospace (not "industry and trade"), engaging MSMEs, start-ups, individual innovators, R&D institutes and academia
