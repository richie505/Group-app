## SUB 1 | Agenda 2030 – Origin, Adoption and the MDGs
[1:0] # Adoption
[1:1] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [1:1.0] Proposed at | Rio+20 (UN Conference on Sustainable Development), Rio de Janeiro, 2012; idea from Colombia and Guatemala
  [1:1.1] Rio+20 outcome | "The Future We Want" – called for goals to follow the MDGs
  [1:1.2] Framework | "Transforming our World: the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development" (not 2035)
  [1:1.3] Adopted | 25 September 2015, UN Sustainable Development Summit, UNGA 70th session, by all 193 member states (not 198)
  [1:1.4] Period | 2016–2030, a 15-year plan; in force 1 January 2016; target year 2030
  [1:1.5] Motto | "Leave no one behind" – a universal call to action to end poverty and protect the planet
[1:2] - **Universal:** binds all countries (MDGs were mainly for developing countries); three dimensions – **economic, social, environmental**.
[1:3] - **Human-rights basis:** guided by the **UDHR (1948)** and human-rights treaties.
[1:4] - **USDG vision:** universal access to **clean water, sanitation and healthcare** is essential for **human dignity**.
[1:5] - **"A Million Voices: The World We Want"** (Sept 2013): post-2015 consultation report of the **UN Development Group (UNDG)**.
[1:6] # MDGs (Millennium Summit 2000)
[1:7] TABLE head: Feature | MDGs | SDGs
  [1:7.0] Goals / targets | 8 / 21 | 17 / 169
  [1:7.1] Period / deadline | 2000–2015 | 2016–2030
  [1:7.2] Scope | Mainly developing countries | Universal
[1:8] - **The 8 MDGs:** extreme poverty and hunger; universal primary education; gender equality; child mortality; maternal health; HIV/AIDS, malaria; environmental sustainability; global partnership.
[1:9] - **Results:** under-5 mortality **90 → 43** per 1,000; India **halved extreme poverty** (MDG target) before 2015.
[1:10] # Chronology
[1:11] TABLE head: Year | Milestone
  [1:11.0] 1951 | India's First Five Year Plan
  [1:11.1] 2000 | Millennium Declaration – 8 MDGs
  [1:11.2] 2002 | UNGA res. 57/254 → UN Decade of Education for Sustainable Development 2005–2014 (lead agency UNESCO)
  [1:11.3] 2012 | Rio+20 – SDG idea
  [1:11.4] 2015 | Agenda 2030 adopted
  [1:11.5] 2016 | SDGs in force; first SDSN SDG Index
  [1:11.6] 2018 | First SDG India Index (NITI Aayog)
  [1:11.7] 2021 | NER District SDG Index, 1st edition (August)
[1:12] > Order: **Rio+20 2012 → Agenda 2030 2015 → in force 2016 → SDG India Index 2018**.

## SUB 4 | SDG Monitoring in India – NITI Aayog, MoSPI and Localisation
[4:0] # Who does what
[4:1] TABLE head: Body | Role
  [4:1.0] NITI Aayog | Nodal agency to oversee and monitor SDGs; SDG India Index (ranks States)
  [4:1.1] MoSPI | Develops the National Indicator Framework (NIF); SDG-NIF Progress Report 2026: 277 indicators
  [4:1.2] UN SDSN | Sustainable Development Report (ranks countries)
[4:2] - **NIF is MoSPI's, not NITI Aayog's.** India's **Voluntary National Review (VNR)** goes to the UN HLPF.
[4:3] - **Multidimensional poverty (NIF):** **24.85% (2015-16) → 14.96% (2019-21)**.
[4:4] # Localisation
[4:5] - **Localised SDGs in Gram Panchayats** (**Ministry of Panchayati Raj**): **nine themes** – poverty-free, healthy, child-friendly, water-sufficient, clean and green, self-sufficient infrastructure, socially just and secure, good governance, **women-friendly village**; "industrially advanced village" is **not** one.
[4:6] - **NER District SDG Index:** 1st edition **August 2021**.
[4:7] - **SDG Conclave 2020** ("Partnerships, Cooperation and Development of North Eastern States", Guwahati, 24-26 Feb 2020): **NITI Aayog** with **North Eastern Council, Government of Assam, Tata Trusts, RIS, UNDP** – **not the World Bank**.
[4:8] > Easy rule: **NITI ranks States, SDSN ranks countries, MoSPI counts indicators.**
