## SUB 0 | Aadhaar and UIDAI
[0:0] # UIDAI
[0:1] - **UIDAI** set up **28 January 2009** as an attached office of the **Planning Commission**; statutory authority under the **Aadhaar Act, 2016**, working under **MeitY** (not Home, Finance or Rural Development).
[0:2] TABLE head: Act / rule / policy | Year | Provision
  [0:2.0] Aadhaar Act | 2016 | UIDAI made statutory under MeitY; targeted delivery of subsidies
  [0:2.1] Amendment | 2019 | Amended the Aadhaar Act
  [0:2.2] Aadhaar Authentication for Good Governance Rules | 2020 | Voluntary authentication by other entities
  [0:2.3] MeitY Policy on Open APIs | 2015 | Led to API Setu
[0:3] # Firsts
[0:4] TABLE head: FIRST | Fact
  [0:4.0] First Aadhaar number | Ranjana Sonawane, Tembhli village, Nandurbar, Maharashtra, 29 September 2010
  [0:4.1] Planned first issue | UIDAI planned the first set of numbers for Aug 2010 – February 2011 (exam key: February 2011)
[0:5] # Format and scale
[0:6] - **12-digit random number**, **free and lifelong**, one enrolment; no caste, religion or geography.
[0:7] - **Biometrics:** **10 fingerprints, 2 iris scans, face photograph**; **dactylography** = study of **fingerprints**.
[0:8] - **Scale:** **142 crore** numbers generated (31 Jul 2025); about 116 crore enrolled by mid-2017 (120 crore crossed only later).
[0:9] - **Uses:** DBT, **Jeevan Pramaan** (**digital life certificate**), e-KYC. Mobile-Aadhaar linking was mandated in 2017 but struck down by the Supreme Court in Sept 2018.
[0:10] - **ABHA** = **14** digits (health ID), Aadhaar = 12.
[0:11] ~~ Sources: NextGen S&T Group 2 Part 4 p.3; app notes 5:25 sub 1-2; general knowledge (dactylography, 2017 enrolment, 2018 judgment)
## SUB 1 | UPI, NPCI Products and Payment Systems
[1:0] # UPI
[1:1] - Launched **April 2016** by **NPCI**; one app, several bank accounts, **VPA**.
[1:2] - **UPI 2.0:** daily limit **Rs 2 lakh**; **UPI Lite:** on-device wallet **without a UPI PIN**.
[1:3] - **Abroad:** **France** = **first country in Europe** to accept UPI (Feb 2024); **PayNow-UPI** link with **Singapore** (2023).
[1:4] TABLE head: Product | Key fact
  [1:4.0] BHIM | NPCI's UPI app; 30 December 2016
  [1:4.1] e-RUPI | One-time, cashless, person- and purpose-specific voucher; NPCI with DFS, MoHFW, NHA; launched by PM Modi, August 2021
  [1:4.2] Vajra | NPCI platform for fast and secure payments (DLT-based clearing)
  [1:4.3] NFS | National Financial Switch – links all ATMs; built by IDRBT (2004), now run by NPCI
[1:5] # Operators
[1:6] TABLE head: System | Operator
  [1:6.0] NEFT, RTGS | RBI
  [1:6.1] IMPS, UPI, AePS, BBPS, NACH, RuPay | NPCI
  [1:6.2] FASTag (NETC) | NPCI with NHAI/IHMCL
  [1:6.3] iPay gateway | IRCTC
[1:7] - **AePS** = cash withdrawal and balance enquiry at micro-ATMs using Aadhaar.
[1:8] - **New Umbrella Entities:** RBI move to end NPCI's monopoly; minimum capital **Rs 500 crore**.
[1:9] - **First digital (mobile) wallet company:** **MobiKwik** (2009).

## SUB 2 | Nodal Bodies, Committees and Digitisation Drive
[2:0] - **After demonetisation (Nov 2016):** **NITI Aayog** coordinated the drive to make **100% Government-Citizen transactions digital**; **Committee of Chief Ministers on Digitisation** headed by **N. Chandrababu Naidu**.
[2:1] - **MeitY** got "Promotion of Digital Transactions" in **February 2017** (taken over from NITI Aayog).
[2:2] - **17 July 2023:** "Promotion of Digital Payments" moved to the **Department of Financial Services (Ministry of Finance)** – the present nodal body.
[2:3] - **Nilekani Committee:** set up by **RBI in January 2019** on deepening digital payments.
[2:4] - **RBI Digital Payments Index:** base **March 2018 = 100**; published **half-yearly**.
[2:5] ~~ Sources: app notes 5:25 sub 5; general knowledge (NITI Aayog 2016, Naidu committee, Nilekani 2019)
## SUB 3 | India Stack and Open Digital Platforms
[3:0] TABLE head: India Stack layer | Component
  [3:0.0] Identity (presence-less) | Aadhaar, e-KYC
  [3:0.1] Cashless | UPI
  [3:0.2] Paperless | eSign, DigiLocker
  [3:0.3] Consent | DEPA / Account Aggregator
[3:1] - India Stack gives **online, paperless, cashless** access and cuts e-KYC cost – through the **identity layer (Aadhaar e-KYC)**, not the payments layer; the original stack had 3 layers, consent was added later.
[3:2] - **Open-source platforms:** **Aarogya Setu, CoWIN, DigiLocker, DIKSHA** – all four (UPSC 2022).
[3:3] - **ONDC (2022):** open e-commerce network that **breaks platform monopolies**.
[3:4] - **Account Aggregator:** consent-based sharing of financial data (not the identity layer).
[3:5] - Digital economy: **12-14% of GDP**.
[3:6] ~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 5 p.77-80; app notes 5:25 sub 0
## SUB 4 | DBT – JAM and Delivery in Andhra Pradesh
[4:0] - **JAM** = Jan Dhan + Aadhaar + Mobile; DBT routed via **CPSMS (now PFMS)** + NPCI's **Aadhaar Payment Bridge**.
[4:1] - **First JAM application:** **PAHAL** (LPG subsidy). **DBT Mission:** now in the **Cabinet Secretariat**.
[4:2] - **Numbers:** **Rs 44 lakh crore** transferred (May 2025); savings **Rs 3.48 lakh crore** (2015 to Mar 2023); **PMJDY 58.77 crore** accounts (Aug 2026), **more than half held by women**.
[4:3] - **AP:** DBT inaugurated at **Gollaprolu, East Godavari**, **6 January 2013** by **Jairam Ramesh**; Aadhaar e-PoS PDS from **Krishna** (May 2015); AP = first state with **e-PoS in 100% of fair price shops**.
[4:4] ~~ Sources: NextGen S&T Group 2 Part 4 p.4; app notes 5:25 sub 7-8
