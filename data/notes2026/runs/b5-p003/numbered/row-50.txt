## SUB 3 | Nuclear Doctrine, No First Use and Command
[3:0] # Timeline
[3:1] - **Draft doctrine:** **National Security Advisory Board (NSAB)**, **August 1999**.
[3:2] - **No First Use** came from the **Vajpayee government after Pokhran-II** (not Indira Gandhi after Pokhran-I).
[3:3] - Formally adopted by the **CCS** on **4 January 2003**.
[3:4] # Key tenets
[3:5] - **Credible minimum deterrence** backed by an assured **second-strike capability** (survivable SSBNs) – not a bigger arsenal or first use.
[3:6] - **No First Use:** only in **retaliation against a nuclear attack on Indian territory or Indian forces**.
[3:7] - **Massive retaliation** causing unacceptable damage.
[3:8] - **No use against non-nuclear-weapon states**; nuclear option kept against a **major biological or chemical attack**.
[3:9] - **Unilateral moratorium** on nuclear tests (since 1998) – so a doctrine question "NFU, moratorium, minimum credible deterrence" = **all of the above**.
[3:10] - India kept the bomb option open and would **not open its strategic nuclear plants to inspection** (UPPCS 1994: A true, R "India will not produce any bomb" false).
[3:11] # Nuclear Command Authority
[3:12] TABLE head: Body | Chaired by | Role
  [3:12.0] Political Council | Prime Minister | Only body that can authorise use
  [3:12.1] Executive Council | National Security Advisor | Advises; carries out directions
  [3:12.2] Strategic Forces Command | Tri-service (set up 2003) | Manages and operates the nuclear arsenal
[3:13] - **CCS** oversees civilian political control; it does not manage the forces. Aug 2026: Agni user trials held "under the aegis of SFC".
[3:14] ~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 6 p.134-137; PMF S&T Part 7 p.1, p.8; app notes 5:50 sub 2

[5:0] TABLE head: Leg | Platforms
  [5:0.0] Land | Prithvi and Agni series (canisterised, road- and rail-mobile); Agni-V (5,000+ km, solid, three-stage; first test 19 April 2012)
