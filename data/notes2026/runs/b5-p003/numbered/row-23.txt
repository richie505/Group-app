## SUB 0 | NPIT 2012 – Goals, Context and Targets
[0:0] # Twin goals
[0:1] - **Goals:** bring the **full power of ICT within the reach of all** India, and make India the **Global Hub and Destination for IT and ITES Services by 2020**.
[0:2] - **Context (2011-12):** IT = **USD 100 bn** industry, **~80%** of revenue from **exports**; **six hubs** (Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Pune, NCR) ≈ **90%** of the industry and near saturation.
[0:3] - **Spread, not concentrate:** NPIT aimed to diversify IT into **Tier II and Tier III cities** and rural areas.
[0:4] - **Objectives:** jobs; ICT in education, health, skills, financial inclusion, governance; Aadhaar-based authentication, PKI, payment gateways; e-governance through the NeGP.
[0:5] # Targets (all by 2020)
[0:6] TABLE head: NPIT 2012 target | Value
  [0:6.0] IT-ITES revenue | US$ 100 bn → US$ 300 bn
  [0:6.1] IT exports | US$ 69 bn → US$ 200 bn
  [0:6.2] Skilled manpower | 10 million more trained ICT workers
  [0:6.3] e-literacy | One e-literate person in every household
[0:7] > ⚠ Trap: NPIT 2012 deadline = **2020** (not 2025); $300 bn = revenue, $200 bn = exports.

## SUB 4 | Outcomes and AP IT Policy
[4:0] - **Digital economy:** **~12-14% of GDP**; projected **~20% by 2029-30**.
[4:1] - **Electronics:** production **₹13.11 lakh crore** (2025-26); electronics = **3rd-largest export** item.
[4:2] - **GCCs:** offshore units of MNCs serving their **own parent** (outsourcing vendors serve unrelated clients).
[4:3] - **AP IT & GCC Policy 4.0 (2024-29):** in force **12 Dec 2024**; AP Data Centre Policy: up to **1 GW**.
[4:4] - **Visakhapatnam:** **cable landing station**, AI data projects with Anakapalli, **Infosys** campus (~7,000 jobs), IoT CoE.
[4:5] - **Amaravati:** **Quantum Valley** (C-DAC centre), NIELIT campus.
