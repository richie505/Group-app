## SUB 1 | Decadal Trends – Phases and the Great Divide
[1:0] TABLE head: Phase | Period | Features
  [1:0.0] I Stagnant / stationary | 1901–1921 | High birth and death rates; negative growth in 1911–21 (−0.31%)
  [1:0.1] II Steady growth | 1921–1951 | Mortality fell (health, sanitation, transport); birth rate stayed high
  [1:0.2] III Population explosion | 1951–1981 | Rapid fall in mortality, high fertility; annual growth ~2.2%; migrants from Tibet, Bangladesh, Nepal, Pakistan
  [1:0.3] IV High but declining | Post-1981 | Birth rate fell – later marriage, female education
[1:1] - **1921 = "Year of the Great Divide"** – after it population grew every decade (1911 is the decade of decline, not the divide).
[1:2] - British period: high birth and death rates, famines and epidemics; annual compound growth about **0.19%** (1891–1921). 1951: literacy **18.33%** (male 27.2, female 8.9), life expectancy about **32 years**.

## SUB 2 | Census 2011 Highlights
[2:0] # Totals and growth
[2:1] - **121.09 crore** (62.33 crore males, 58.76 crore females); **rural 68.84–68.9%**, **urban 31.1–31.16%** (37.71 crore).
[2:2] - Decadal growth **17.64%** (provisional; 17.7% final) – the **sharpest decline since Independence**, **3.9 points** below 21.54%; average annual exponential growth **1.64%**.
[2:3] - About **65%** of Indians were **below 35** (2011); the 2001-11 decade was the first in which slowing fertility dampened net additions.
[2:4] # Children (0–6)
[2:5] - 0–6 population **16.44 crore ≈ 13.6%** (provisional 13.1%, down from 16% in 2001) – the **first absolute fall** in decades.
[2:6] - **Largest 0–6 populations:** **1 Uttar Pradesh, 2 Bihar, 3 Maharashtra, 4 Madhya Pradesh**.
[2:7] # Composition
[2:8] - **ST 10.43 crore (8.6%)**, ST decadal growth 23.66%; **elderly 60+ 8.6%** (Kerala 12.6% highest, Jharkhand lowest).
[2:9] - SRS 2024: 0–14 **24%**, 15–59 **66.4%**, 60+ **9.7%**.
[2:10] - Census 2011 = **15th** census since 1872, **7th** after Independence; motto "**Our Census, Our Future**"; reference date **1 March 2011**.
[2:11] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Geography 15 p.12 (17.64%, sharpest decline), p.17 (child population); OnlyIAS NCERT Human Geography 03 p.15; Ramesh Singh Indian Economy 04 p.86 (youth below 35); NextGen Indian Economy P1 04 p.23; app notes 4:58 (sub 4, 5)

## SUB 5 | Census Organisation and Census 2027
[5:0] # History and law
[5:1] - **1872** first census (non-synchronous, **Lord Mayo**); **1881** first complete, synchronous census (**Lord Ripon**), decennial since.
[5:2] - **Census Act, 1948**; Census = **Union List, Entry 69**.
[5:3] - Conducted by the **Registrar General and Census Commissioner of India**, **Ministry of Home Affairs**: **C. Chandramouli** at Census 2011; now **Mritunjay Kumar Narayan**.
[5:4] TABLE head: Amendment | Provision
  [5:4.0] 42nd | Seat freeze on the 1971 census
  [5:4.1] 84th | Freeze till the first census after 2026
  [5:4.2] 106th (2023, Art. 334A) | Women's reservation tied to census and delimitation
[5:5] # Census 2027
[5:6] - Census 2021 postponed (COVID-19); Census 2027 comes **16 years** after 2011; **first digital** census and **first caste count** beyond SC/ST since Independence (announced **4 June 2025**); caste question **open-ended**.
[5:7] - **40 questions** (13 new); Phase I House Listing **1 April – 30 September 2026**; Population Enumeration Feb 2027, reference **1 March 2027**; snow-bound areas reference **1 October 2026**.
