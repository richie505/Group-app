=== Population Density – Measures and Census 2011 Pattern
# Measures
| Measure | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Arithmetic (crude) density** | **Total population ÷ total area** (persons per sq km) |
| **Physiological density** | Total population ÷ **net cultivated (arable) area** |
| Agricultural density | Agricultural population ÷ net cultivable area |
- Physiological density is the **better index** of pressure on land because it uses arable land, while arithmetic density uses total area – so in the CDS 2009 A–R both are true and **R explains A**.
- Density **rises** with natality and immigration; **falls** with mortality and emigration.
# India
- **117 (1951) → 382 per sq km (2011)**; 325 in 2001. India is among the densest large countries (only Bangladesh and Japan higher among big ones).

=== Factors, Regions of High Density and BIMARU
# Factors
| Factor | Dense | Sparse |
|---|---|---|
| Water | River valleys | **Thar desert** (lack of water) |
| Soil | **Loamy alluvium** | Thin, rocky soils |
| Relief | **Plains** | Hills, mountains (except valleys like **Kashmir**, parts of Himachal) |
# High-density regions
- **Ganga plain and Ganges–Brahmaputra delta**; **Godavari–Krishna deltas** (intensive **rice farming**); Kerala coast.
- **Dhanbad** is the densest district of the **Chotanagpur plateau** because of the **development of mining industries and industrialisation** (coal; BCCL); its red soil is poor for farming, and mere availability of minerals is not the reason (UPPSC 2007).
- Growing **population pressure** hinders government efforts to improve the quality of life (APPSC Group II 2011).
# Population–resource regions
| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Progressive** | Resource-developed: **Ganga–Yamuna Doab, Tamil Nadu** |
| **Problem** | Population pressure on limited or degraded resources |
| **Deficit** | Resources exceed their use |
# BIMARU
- **BI**har, **MA**dhya Pradesh, **R**ajasthan, **U**ttar Pradesh ("bimar" = sick); coined by demographer **Ashish Bose** in **1986** (not Odisha).
- High growth and weak indicators; their faster growth than the southern States feeds the **delimitation debate**.

=== Census Town Criteria
- A **Census Town** needs population **at least 5,000**, density **at least 400 per sq km**, and **at least 75% of male workers** in non-farm work – unchanged since **1981**.
- 17 Sep 2026: MoHUA told a parliamentary panel the criteria are outdated; the **RGI keeps them for Census 2027**.
