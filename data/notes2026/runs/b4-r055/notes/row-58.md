=== Decadal Trends – Phases and the Great Divide
| Phase | Period | Features |
|---|---|---|
| I Stagnant / stationary | **1901–1921** | High birth and death rates; **negative growth in 1911–21 (−0.31%)** |
| II Steady growth | **1921–1951** | Mortality fell (health, sanitation, transport); birth rate stayed high |
| III Population explosion | **1951–1981** | Rapid fall in mortality, high fertility; annual growth ~**2.2%**; migrants from Tibet, Bangladesh, Nepal, Pakistan |
| IV High but declining | Post-1981 | Birth rate fell – **later marriage, female education** |
- **1921 = "Year of the Great Divide"** – after it population grew every decade (1911 is the decade of decline, not the divide).
- British period: high birth and death rates, famines and epidemics; annual compound growth about **0.19%** (1891–1921). 1951: literacy **18.33%** (male 27.2, female 8.9), life expectancy about **32 years**.
| Census | Population | Decadal growth | Annual exponential growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1951 | 36.11 crore | 13.31% | 1.25% |
| 1971 | 54.82 crore | **24.80% (peak, 1961-71)** | 2.20% |
| 1991 | 84.63 crore | 23.85–23.87% | 2.16% |
| 2001 | 102.87 crore | **21.54%** | **1.93% (provisional) / 1.97% (final)** |
| 2011 | 121.09 crore | **17.64% (provisional) / 17.7% (final)** | **1.64%** |

=== Census 2011 Highlights
# Totals and growth
- **121.09 crore** (62.33 crore males, 58.76 crore females); **rural 68.84–68.9%**, **urban 31.1–31.16%** (37.71 crore).
- Decadal growth **17.64%** (provisional; 17.7% final) – the **sharpest decline since Independence**, **3.9 points** below 21.54%; average annual exponential growth **1.64%**.
- About **65%** of Indians were **below 35** (2011); the 2001-11 decade was the first in which slowing fertility dampened net additions.
# Children (0–6)
- 0–6 population **16.44 crore ≈ 13.6%** (provisional 13.1%, down from 16% in 2001) – the **first absolute fall** in decades.
- **Largest 0–6 populations:** **1 Uttar Pradesh, 2 Bihar, 3 Maharashtra, 4 Madhya Pradesh**.
# Composition
- **ST 10.43 crore (8.6%)**, ST decadal growth 23.66%; **elderly 60+ 8.6%** (Kerala 12.6% highest, Jharkhand lowest).
- SRS 2024: 0–14 **24%**, 15–59 **66.4%**, 60+ **9.7%**.
- Census 2011 = **15th** census since 1872, **7th** after Independence; motto "**Our Census, Our Future**"; reference date **1 March 2011**.

=== Census Organisation and Census 2027
# History and law
- **1872** first census (non-synchronous, **Lord Mayo**); **1881** first complete, synchronous census (**Lord Ripon**), decennial since.
- **Census Act, 1948**; Census = **Union List, Entry 69**.
- Conducted by the **Registrar General and Census Commissioner of India**, **Ministry of Home Affairs**: **C. Chandramouli** at Census 2011; now **Mritunjay Kumar Narayan**.
| Amendment | Provision |
|---|---|
| **42nd** | Seat freeze on the 1971 census |
| **84th** | Freeze till the first census after 2026 |
| **106th** (2023, Art. 334A) | Women's reservation tied to census and delimitation |
# Census 2027
- Census 2021 postponed (COVID-19); Census 2027 comes **16 years** after 2011; **first digital** census and **first caste count** beyond SC/ST since Independence (announced **4 June 2025**); caste question **open-ended**.
- **40 questions** (13 new); Phase I House Listing **1 April – 30 September 2026**; Population Enumeration Feb 2027, reference **1 March 2027**; snow-bound areas reference **1 October 2026**.
