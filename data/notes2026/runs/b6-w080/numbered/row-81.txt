## SUB 0 | Ramsar Convention and India's Numbers
[0:0] # The Convention
[0:1] - **Ramsar Convention:** signed/adopted in **1971** at **Ramsar, Iran**; subject: **wetlands of international importance** (conservation and wise use).
[0:2] - **India joined:** became a party on **1 February 1982**; first Indian sites designated in **1981** (Chilika, Keoladeo).
[0:3] > 1971 = Convention signed; 1981 = first Indian sites; 1982 = India became a party.
[0:4] # India's Numbers (as of Oct 2026)
[0:5] TABLE head: Item | Fact
  [0:5.0] Total Ramsar sites | 103 (15 Oct 2026: Sukhna Wetland and Sauj Jheel added)
  [0:5.1] June 2026 | 100 (Surha Tal = 100th), about 14 lakh ha
  [0:5.2] 3 Aug 2026 | 101 (Glaw Lake, Arunachal Pradesh)
  [0:5.3] Rank in Asia | 1st
  [0:5.4] Rank in the world | 3rd (after UK 176, Mexico 144; Aug 2026)
  [0:5.5] Sites till 2014 | 26
  [0:5.6] January 2026 | 98
[0:6] - **Growth:** 26 (till 2014) → 98 (January 2026) → 100 (June 2026) → 101 (Aug 2026) → **103 (Oct 2026)**.
[0:7] # National and Global Wetland Initiatives
[0:8] - **Amrit Dharohar:** announced in the **Union Budget 2023-24** (launched 2023) to promote wise use of Ramsar sites; four components: **Species and Habitat Conservation**, **Nature Tourism**, **Wetlands Livelihoods**, **Wetlands Carbon Assessment**.
[0:9] - **Other support:** National Plan for Conservation of Aquatic Ecosystems (NPCA) and the Wetlands (Conservation and Management) Rules, 2017.
[0:10] - **Ramsar COP15:** **Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe**, **23–31 July 2025** – adopted the **Fifth Strategic Plan (2025–2034)** and the **Victoria Falls Declaration**; **Zimbabwe** took the presidency for **2025–2028** after **China**.
[0:11]   - New **Ramsar Wetland Cities**: **Victoria Falls** (Zimbabwe), Kasane-Kazungula and Shakawe (Botswana).
[0:12] ~~ Sources: Rocket CA 2026 Part 02 p.84, p.146-147 (103 sites; UK 176, Mexico 144); Rocket Environment p.7 (Ramsar, Iran); app notes 6:81 sub 0 and 8
