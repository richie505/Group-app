## SUB 3 | Approach, Objectives and Functions
[3:0] - Guiding principle: **Cooperative federalism** (not competitive, collateral or compulsive) – also called **"marble-cake" federalism** (shared roles, vs layered "layer-cake").
[3:1] - **Bottom-up** approach (vs Planning Commission's **top-down, one-size-fits-all**); bottom-up and top-down are strategies of **information processing**.
[3:2] - State rankings (SDG India Index etc.) → **competitive federalism**; competition among States and cities = **competitive sub-federalism**.
[3:3] # Objectives in the Cabinet resolution
[3:4] - **Shared vision** of national priorities with the States; structured support to States (strong States make a strong nation).
[3:5] - **Credible plans at the village level, aggregated progressively at higher levels** of government.
[3:6] - **Antyodaya**, inclusive and participatory development; focus on **small enterprises** (not large ones); monitoring and evaluation; knowledge and innovation hub.
[3:7] - **Four mandate areas:** knowledge and innovation; NITI for States; driving transformational change; monitoring and evaluation.
[3:8] # NOT a function
[3:9] - **Allocating central funds to States** / **finalising financial resource allocation** – that was the Planning Commission's role (now Finance Ministry / Finance Commission).
[3:10] > Assertion-Reason (APPSC G2 Mains 2025): A (non-constitutional advisory body) and R (village-level plans aggregated upwards) are **both true, but R does not explain A**.
[3:11] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 03 p.8-10; NextGen Rocket Sheets Indian Economy #24 p.34 (cooperative federalism + bottom-up); NITI Aayog Cabinet Resolution 1 Jan 2015; app notes 3:12 sub 3

## SUB 5 | Planning Commission vs NITI Aayog
[5:0] TABLE head: Aspect | Planning Commission | NITI Aayog
  [5:0.0] Set up | 15 March 1950, Cabinet resolution; first chairman Nehru | 1 January 2015, Cabinet resolution
  [5:0.1] Status | Non-constitutional, non-statutory | Same
  [5:0.2] Funds | Allocated plan funds and grants to States | No fund-allocation power
  [5:0.3] Approach | Top-down, one-size-fits-all | Bottom-up, cooperative federalism
  [5:0.4] States' role | NDC and annual plan talks | Every CM on the Governing Council
  [5:0.5] Members | Up to 8 full-time; no part-time | Full-time + up to 2 part-time
  [5:0.6] Top officials | Deputy Chairman + Member-Secretary | Vice-Chairperson + CEO
[5:1] - Constituted in **1950** (not 1947 or 1955); its plans went to the **NDC** for approval.
[5:2] - Statement traps: Finance Commission is **constitutional** (Art. 280), not "non-statutory"; Planning Commission was **not statutory**; **RBI nationalised 1 January 1949** (not 26 Jan 1949) – so "none of the above" is correct.
