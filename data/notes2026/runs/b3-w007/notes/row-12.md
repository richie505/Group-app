=== NITI Aayog – Basics, Date and Status
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Full form | **National Institution for Transforming India** |
| Set up | **1 January 2015** (not 2012, 2014 or 2016; not 26 Jan) |
| Mode | **Union Cabinet resolution** – an **executive order**, not an Act or amendment |
| Replaced | **Planning Commission** (not the Finance, Law or Human Rights Commission) |
| Status | **Non-constitutional and non-statutory**; apex public policy **think tank**; advisory |
| Chairperson | **Prime Minister** (ex officio) – **no full-time Chairman** |
- A **think tank, not a fund allocator** – it does **not** make Five Year Plans.
- "Not a statutory body": **NITI Aayog** – Finance Commission (Art. 280), UPSC and Election Commission are **constitutional**.
- **Zonal Councils** = **statutory** (States Reorganisation Act, 1956), chaired by the **Union Home Minister**.
> Statements asked: "set up in 2015 to replace the Planning Commission" ✓; "constitutional body making Five-Year Plans" ✗.

=== Composition, Governing Council and Regional Councils
| Post | Provision |
|---|---|
| Chairperson | Prime Minister |
| Vice-Chairperson | Appointed by the PM; **Cabinet Minister** rank |
| Full-time members | Number **not fixed**; 3 in 2015; **5** in most books; **7** from June 2026 |
| Part-time members | **Maximum 2**, from universities / research bodies, ex officio, rotational |
| Ex-officio members | **Maximum 4** Union Ministers nominated by the PM |
| CEO | Appointed by the PM, fixed tenure, rank of **Secretary to GoI** |

# Governing Council
- **Members:** PM (chair), **Chief Ministers of all States**, **CMs of Delhi and Puducherry**, **Lieutenant Governors of other UTs** (e.g. Andaman and Nicobar), VC, full-time and ex-officio members, special invitees.
- **Governors of States are NOT members.**
- First constituted **Feb 2015**; took over the work of the **NDC**.
- **Sub-groups of CMs (2015):** rationalisation of **Centrally Sponsored Schemes**, **Skill Development**, **Swachh Bharat**. **Digital payments** was not one of them – a separate **Committee of CMs on Digital Payments** (Nov 2016) was headed by **N. Chandrababu Naidu**.

=== Approach, Objectives and Functions
- Guiding principle: **Cooperative federalism** (not competitive, collateral or compulsive) – also called **"marble-cake" federalism** (shared roles, vs layered "layer-cake").
- **Bottom-up** approach (vs Planning Commission's **top-down, one-size-fits-all**); bottom-up and top-down are strategies of **information processing**.
- State rankings (SDG India Index etc.) → **competitive federalism**; competition among States and cities = **competitive sub-federalism**.
# Objectives in the Cabinet resolution
- **Shared vision** of national priorities with the States; structured support to States (strong States make a strong nation).
- **Credible plans at the village level, aggregated progressively at higher levels** of government.
- **Antyodaya**, inclusive and participatory development; focus on **small enterprises** (not large ones); monitoring and evaluation; knowledge and innovation hub.
- **Four mandate areas:** knowledge and innovation; NITI for States; driving transformational change; monitoring and evaluation.
# NOT a function
- **Allocating central funds to States** / **finalising financial resource allocation** – that was the Planning Commission's role (now Finance Ministry / Finance Commission).
> Assertion-Reason (APPSC G2 Mains 2025): A (non-constitutional advisory body) and R (village-level plans aggregated upwards) are **both true, but R does not explain A**.

=== Planning Commission vs NITI Aayog
| Aspect | Planning Commission | NITI Aayog |
|---|---|---|
| Set up | **15 March 1950**, Cabinet resolution; first chairman **Nehru** | 1 January 2015, Cabinet resolution |
| Status | Non-constitutional, **non-statutory** | Same |
| Funds | Allocated plan funds and grants to States | No fund-allocation power |
| Approach | Top-down, one-size-fits-all | Bottom-up, cooperative federalism |
| States' role | NDC and annual plan talks | Every CM on the Governing Council |
| Members | Up to 8 full-time; no part-time | Full-time + up to 2 part-time |
| Top officials | Deputy Chairman + Member-Secretary | Vice-Chairperson + CEO |
- Constituted in **1950** (not 1947 or 1955); its plans went to the **NDC** for approval.
- Statement traps: Finance Commission is **constitutional** (Art. 280), not "non-statutory"; Planning Commission was **not statutory**; **RBI nationalised 1 January 1949** (not 26 Jan 1949) – so "none of the above" is correct.

=== Andhra Pradesh and NITI Aayog
- AP sought NITI's help for its own policy body: **State Institution for Transformation** (2023, State Support Mission).

- **Visakhapatnam Economic Region** plan made with NITI under the **City and Regional Growth Hubs (G-HUB)** initiative – one of **four** pilot cities; USD **750-800 billion** economy by 2047.
- **NITI Aayog** built, with the AP government, a **dashboard for real-time monitoring of district progress** (Aspirational Districts).
