  [0:0.0] Founder | M.K. Gandhi, 30 September 1932, while in (Yerawada) prison
  [0:0.1] Context | Set up after the Poona Pact (Sept 1932) – from then Gandhi made Harijan upliftment part of his political and social programme (UPSC 2025)
  [0:0.2] Former name | All India Anti-Untouchability League
  [0:0.3] First president | G.D. (Ghanshyam Das) Birla
  [0:0.4] Secretary | Amritlal Thakkar (Thakkar Bapa)
[0:1] - Work: access to **wells, schools, temples**; schools, hostels, cooperatives; **inter-dining, inter-caste marriage** – **separate electorates was NOT its activity**.
[0:2] - **Harijan** weekly: first issue **11 February 1933, Poona**.

## SUB 1 | Nai Talim – Basic Education (Wardha Scheme, 1937)
[1:0] - **Basic education** propounded by **Mahatma Gandhi** (articles in Harijan, 1937); **craft-centred**, vocational; dignity of manual labour; education should be **self-supporting**.
[1:1] - **Wardha (All-India National Education) Conference, October 1937** → scheme drawn up by the **Zakir Hussain Committee**; adopted at **Haripura (Feb 1938)**.
[1:2] - Principles: **free and compulsory**, **7 years (age 7-14)**; **mother tongue** medium; learning through a **productive craft**; self-sufficient villages.
[1:3] - Ultimate aim of education (Gandhi): **self-realisation** – growing into a **divine human being** by realising godliness, not acquisition or mere employment.
[1:4] - **Socially Useful Productive Work (SUPW)** in the curriculum grew from **Gandhi's** idea of education through productive work.
[1:5] ~~ Sources: PMF Modern History Part 5 p.16; PMF Part 3 p.47; Spectrum (Ahir) pdf p.602; old app notes (7-14 years, mother tongue); general knowledge, not checked (aim of education, SUPW)

## SUB 3 | Women and Religion
[3:0] - Opposed **purdah, child marriage, dowry**; **supported remarriage of child widows** – but widow remarriage is not one of "Gandhi's concepts" (those are satyagraha, sarvodaya, trusteeship, Nai Talim).
[3:1] - Women's role in satyagraha: **picketing liquor shops**, salt-making.
[3:2] - **Sarva dharma samabhava** – equal respect for all religions; **ashram prayers** drew on many faiths.
[3:3] - **Hindu-Muslim unity** = bedrock of nationalism; backed the **Khilafat**.
[3:4] ~~ Sources: PMF Modern History Part 4 p.123-124 (religion, Khilafat); old app notes (women, sarva dharma samabhava)
## SUB 4 | Gandhi in Andhra
[4:0] - **Khadi tour (April 1929):** visited **Bezawada**; laid the foundation stone of the **Saraswata Niketanam** library building, **Vetapalem**.
