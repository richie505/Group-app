## SUB 0 | Wage Employment vs Self-employment Programmes
[36:0:0] # Classification
[36:0:1] TABLE head: Wage employment | Self-employment
  [36:0:1.0] NREP, RLEGP, JRY, EAS | IRDP, TRYSEM, DWCRA
  [36:0:1.1] JGSY, SGRY, NFFWP | SGSY, NRLM
  [36:0:1.2] MGNREGS, VB-G RAM G | PMRY (1993), REGP, PMEGP
[36:0:2] - **PMRY** (Prime Minister's Rozgar Yojana, **1993**): self-employment for **educated unemployed youth**; the poverty programme of the reform period; later merged into PMEGP.
[36:0:3] - **PMRY delivery:** implemented through **District Industries Centres (DICs)** with bank loans to educated unemployed youth
[36:0:4] - **PMEGP (2008)** = merger of **PMRY + REGP**.
[36:0:5] PMRY is **not** a wage employment programme (APPSC 2017); JRY, EAS, SGRY are wage programmes.
[36:0:6] # Urban Programmes
[36:0:7] - **SJSRY (1 December 1997)** absorbed **NRY, UBSP** and the PM's Integrated Urban programme; it did **not** include PMRY.
[36:0:8] - **Indira Gandhi Urban Employment Guarantee Scheme** (Rajasthan, **2022**): **100 days**, raised to **125 days in 2023**.
[36:0:9] - Not employment-related: **RMSA** (March 2009, secondary education); MGNREGA and NRLM are.

## SUB 2 | Mgnrega, 2005: the Act
[36:2:0] # Milestones
[36:2:1] TABLE head: Item | Fact
  [36:2:1.0] Name | National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, 2005 (first proposed in 1991 by P.V. Narasimha Rao)
  [36:2:1.1] Passed | 23 Aug 2005; assent 5 Sept 2005; notified 7 Sept 2005
  [36:2:1.2] Launch | 2 Feb 2006 in 200 backward districts
  [36:2:1.3] Phase 2 | 130 more districts in 2007-08
  [36:2:1.4] All districts | 1 April 2008 (625 districts; NREGS extended to the whole country)
  [36:2:1.5] Renaming | "Mahatma Gandhi" added 2 Oct 2009 (MGNREGA)
  [36:2:1.6] Repealed | 1 July 2026: VB-G RAM G Act in force
[36:2:2] 2005 Act - 2006 launch - 2008 all districts - 2009 "Mahatma Gandhi" added
[36:2:3] - **Origin:** UPA's **National Common Minimum Programme (2004)** promised 100 days of work a year, which led to NREGA 2005
[36:2:4] 625 = all-district coverage of 2008, not the launch figure; NFFWP merged in **2006**, not 2009. The correct name is Mahatma Gandhi **National Rural** Employment Guarantee Act.
[36:2:5] # Entitlement and Wages
[36:2:6] - **At least 100 days** of guaranteed wage employment **per financial year**.
[36:2:7] - **Unit = household** (not each adult); for **adult members** who volunteer for **unskilled manual work**; **rural** only.
[36:2:8] - Wages: State-notified wage, **within 15 days** of work; delay brings compensation; **DBT** to Aadhaar-linked accounts.
[36:2:9] - **Ministry of Rural Development**; rights-based, demand-driven, anchored in **Article 41** (right to work, DPSP).
[36:2:10] APPSC 2017: mandate = "100 days to every **household** whose adult members volunteer", not "100 days to every adult".
## SUB 3 | Mgnrega Objective
[36:3:0] - "**Enhancing livelihood security** of rural households" through at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment (APPSC 2023).
[36:3:1] - Target group: rural, **unskilled**, adult volunteers (not urban, not skilled).
[36:3:2] - **Olga Tellis (1985)** linked livelihood with **Article 21**; MGNREGA is the only law built on the right to work.
[36:3:3] - **World Development Report 2014** (World Bank): "**stellar example of rural development**".
[36:3:4] - Largest rural employment programme; safety net in agrarian distress and **COVID-19**.
## SUB 4 | Mgnrega Features and Monitoring
[36:4:0] # Features
[36:4:1] - **Women:** at least **one-third** of beneficiaries; actual share over **50%** of person-days.
[36:4:2] - **Unemployment allowance:** if no work within **15 days** of application: at least **1/4 of wage for first 30 days**, **1/2 thereafter**.
[36:4:3] - **Permissible works:** water conservation, land development, rural connectivity, durable community assets.
[36:4:4] - **Convergence:** 90/95 person-days to **PMAY-G** beneficiaries (AwaasSoft-NREGASoft link).
[36:4:5] - Scale: 5-6 crore households, 200-250 crore person-days a year.
[36:4:6] # Monitoring
[36:4:7] TABLE head: Tool | Use
  [36:4:7.0] Bhuvan mobile platform (ISRO) | Geo-tagging of assets: GeoMGNREGA (APPSC 2018)
  [36:4:7.1] NREGASoft | MIS
  [36:4:7.2] Gram Sabha | Mandatory social audit; plans and monitors works
[36:4:8] - **CAPART** and **NIRDPR (Hyderabad, Telangana)**: autonomous bodies under the Ministry of Rural Development.
[36:4:9] 15 days twice: wages within 15 days of work; allowance if no work within 15 days of application.
## SUB 5 | Mgnrega vs Vb-g Ram G Act, 2025
[36:5:0] - **Viksit Bharat - Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025**: passed by Parliament **18 Dec 2025**; replaced MGNREGA from **1 July 2026**.
[36:5:1] TABLE head: Aspect | MGNREGA 2005 | VB-G RAM G 2025
  [36:5:1.0] Guarantee | 100 days/household | 125 days/household (over 305 days)
  [36:5:1.1] Seasonal pause | None | States may pause up to 60 days (sowing/harvest)
  [36:5:1.2] Funding | Mostly Central, demand-driven | 60:40 (90:10 NE/Himalayan States, UTs); normative allocation
  [36:5:1.3] Wages | Within 15 days | Weekly or within a fortnight; floor ₹300/day
  [36:5:1.4] Works | Many categories | 4 priority areas: water security, core rural infrastructure, livelihood infrastructure, extreme-weather works
  [36:5:1.5] Admin ceiling | 6% | 9%
[36:5:2] - Job cards → **Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Cards**; no contractors; assets on VB-NRIS stack; Central and State Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Councils; unemployment allowance retained.
[36:5:3] - Apr-Jul 2026: only **70 crore** person-days (MGNREGA + VB-G RAM G).
[36:5:4] - 13 May 2026: unions sought rollback, 200 days and ₹700 wage. 21 Aug 2026: Supreme Court bench to examine whether right to work should be on par with Article 21.
[36:5:5] 125 - 60 - 9% = guarantee days - seasonal pause - admin ceiling
