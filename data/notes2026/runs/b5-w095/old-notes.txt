## SUB 1 | Kyoto Protocol, CDM and Carbon Credits
[95:1:0] - **Kyoto Protocol:** adopted **11 Dec 1997** at **Kyoto, Japan (COP3)**; in force **16 Feb 2005**; **first legally binding** treaty with GHG targets; called on **industrialised (Annex I) nations** to cut emissions (~5% below **1990** in 2008-12) – deals with **greenhouse gases / climate change**, not ozone (Montreal), poverty or arms.
[95:1:1] - **Binding on Annex I only**; **USA never ratified**; **Canada withdrew** (announced 12 Dec 2011, effective Dec 2012) – first to leave.
[95:1:2] - **Six gases:** CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, HFCs, PFCs, SF₆; **NF₃** added only for the 2nd period. **Methane** is a more potent GHG than CO₂.
[95:1:3] - **Doha Amendment (2012):** 2nd period **2013-2020**, **18%** below 1990; in force 31 Dec 2020.
[95:1:4] - **India acceded to Kyoto in August 2002** (UNFCCC 1992 → Kyoto 2002; First Five Year Plan 1951 came first).
[95:1:5] TABLE head: Mechanism | Between | Unit
  [95:1:5.0] International Emissions Trading | Annex I ↔ Annex I | Allowances
  [95:1:5.1] CDM (Article 12) | Annex I (Annex B) party runs a project in a developing country | CERs
  [95:1:5.2] Joint Implementation | Annex I project in another Annex I country | ERUs
[95:1:6] - **CDM:** the market-based device under the UNFCCC/Kyoto that lets developing countries get funds and technology from developed countries; it **encourages** (does not prohibit) developed-country investment in developing countries; CDM projects are **not** confined to Annex I countries.
[95:1:7] - **Annex I** is a UNFCCC/Kyoto grouping (not Cartagena); **CERs** come from the CDM (issued by the **CDM Executive Board**), not Nagoya → only "CDM – Kyoto" is correctly matched (UPSC 2016).
[95:1:8] # Carbon credits and markets
[95:1:9] - **Carbon credit concept:** originated from the **Kyoto Protocol (1997)** – not the Rio Earth Summit, Montreal Protocol or G-8; **1 credit = 1 tonne CO₂e**; **carbon trading** belongs to Kyoto, **not Montreal**.
[95:1:10] - **Paris Article 6:** **6.2** bilateral deals (India-Japan **JCM**, Aug 2025); **6.4** UN-run Paris Agreement Crediting Mechanism (credits = **A6.4ERs**), successor to the CDM; rules completed and **fully operational at COP29**; first 6.4 credits: clean-cooking project in **Myanmar**.
[95:1:11] - **GHG Protocol** (WRI + WBCSD): an **international accounting tool** for governments and businesses to quantify and manage GHG emissions – not a UN incentive scheme or REDD+ initiative.
[95:1:12] > Angles: pairs (Annex I / CER / CDM), "carbon credit originated from", CDM "not true" statements, Canada's withdrawal.

[95:3:1] - **First major international agreement on climate change** = the **UNFCCC** (the 1987 Montreal Protocol is on ozone).
[95:3:2] - **Objective:** stabilise **greenhouse-gas** concentrations at a level that prevents **dangerous anthropogenic interference** (GHG mitigation – not just CO₂ or fossil fuels). Two instruments: **Kyoto Protocol** and **Paris Agreement**. **IPCC AR1 (1990)** fed into it.

[95:3:4] - **CBDR-RC:** developed countries, with most **historical emissions**, should lead – **Rio Declaration Principle 7**, UNFCCC Art. 3.1, Paris Art. 2.2.

## SUB 4 | Paris Agreement and NDCs
[95:4:0] - **Goal:** hold warming **well below 2°C** and pursue **1.5°C** above pre-industrial levels (APPSC FBO: **1.5°C**).
[95:4:1] - **Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs):** each country sets its own (not centrally, UN- or capacity-determined); **INDCs** = **Intended NDCs**, the **plans of action to combat climate change** submitted before Paris; NDCs every **5 years**; each must go beyond the last (**ratchet**).
[95:4:2] - **Legal nature (APPSC G2 Mains 2025):** a **legally binding treaty**, but only the **procedure** binds (prepare, communicate, report NDCs); **NDC targets bind no party** – not the **USA, China, India or EU** → "**None of these**".
[95:4:3] TABLE head: Article | Subject
  [95:4:3.0] 6.2 / 6.4 | Carbon markets
  [95:4:3.1] 9.1 | Finance from developed countries
  [95:4:3.2] 13 | Enhanced Transparency Framework; Biennial Transparency Reports (first due end-2024)
  [95:4:3.3] 14 | Global Stocktake every 5 years (first at COP28)
  [95:4:3.4] 28 | Withdrawal: notice after 3 years from entry into force; effect 1 year after notice

## SUB 6 | IPCC – Body and Reports
[95:6:0] - Set up **1988** by **WMO and UNEP**; Secretariat **Geneva**; does **no original research**; each report has a **Summary for Policymakers**; **Nobel Peace Prize 2007** with **Al Gore**. **WMO** (1950; UN agency 1951; HQ **Geneva**) grew out of the **International Meteorological Organization (1873)**.
[95:6:1] - Working Groups: **WG I** physical science; **WG II** impacts, adaptation, vulnerability; **WG III** mitigation.
[95:6:2] - **ARs:** AR1 1990, AR2 1995, AR3 2001, AR4 2007, **AR5 2014**, AR6 2021-23 (Synthesis **20 Mar 2023**); **AR7** began **July 2023** (Special Report on **Climate Change and Cities**, 2027).
[95:6:3] TABLE head: Report | Finding
  [95:6:3.0] SR1.5 (Oct 2018) | Warming likely reaches 1.5°C between 2030 and 2052; net CO₂ must fall ~45% from 2010 by 2030 and reach net zero by 2050; deep, rapid cuts (not gradual)
  [95:6:3.1] SRCCL (Aug 2019) | Land, desertification, food security
  [95:6:3.2] SROCC (Sep 2019) | Marine heatwaves more frequent (doubled since 1982)
  [95:6:3.3] AR6 SYR (2023) | 1.1°C (2011-2020); 1.5°C likely in the early 2030s; 70-85% renewable electricity by 2050; carbon budget ~500 GtCO₂ from 2020

## SUB 7 | India in Climate Negotiations
[95:7:0] - **Per capita:** India's per-capita emissions are far **below** industrialised nations (about 2.9 t CO₂e vs USA 18 t, China 11 t) – in 2012 India was the **lowest per-capita** emitter among the USA, UK, China and India.
[95:7:1] - **BASIC** (4 members: Brazil, South Africa, India, China): India **attended** the 19th BASIC meeting at **Sun City** (so "did not attend" is the untrue statement, JPSC 2016).
[95:7:2] - **Blocs:** **G77 and China** (largest developing-country group); LMDC; **AOSIS** (small islands, push 1.5°C). At **Bonn SB64 (June 2026)** India with G77+China, LMDC, BASIC sought agenda space on **Article 9.1** finance.
[95:7:3] - **COP26:** **Panchamrit**; India pushed **"phase down"** of unabated coal. **Not signed:** Global Methane Pledge, **Glasgow Leaders' Declaration on Forests and Land Use**, COP28 renewables pledge, COP28 health declaration. **COP33 bid** made at **COP28**, withdrawn April 2026.
[95:7:4] - **Net zero 2070** (later than 2050) – development needs and **coal dependence**; wants **clean, affordable technology and finance**; coal measures incl. **wet scrubbers**, ESPs, gasification.
[95:7:5] - **EU CBAM** (iron/steel, aluminium, cement, fertilisers, hydrogen, electricity; definitive 1 Jan 2026): India – **violates CBDR-RC, green protectionism**; 12th BRICS Environment Ministers (New Delhi, Aug 2026): "unilateral, punitive, discriminatory, protectionist".
[95:7:6] - **Platforms:** G20 New Delhi 2023 – **Green Development Pact**, **Global Biofuels Alliance**; **Voice of Global South** Summits since **Jan 2023**; **Mission LiFE** – individual behaviour change; **ISA** – COP21 (2015), India + France, HQ **Gurugram**, first intergovernmental body headquartered in India.
[95:7:7] - **Carbon space** = room for developing countries to grow; sea-level-rise refugees fall outside the **1951 Refugee Convention**.

## SUB 3 | Mission Objectives – Water, Green India, PAT, NMSA
[97:3:0] # National Water Mission
[97:3:1] - Goals: public water database, focus on **over-exploited areas**, **20% gain in water-use efficiency** (basin-level IWRM).

## SUB 2 | Types of Pollution – Sources and Impacts
[98:2:0] TABLE head: Pollution type | Major source | Key impacts
  [98:2:0.0] Air | Industrial emissions; vehicle exhaust (main city source); fossil-fuel combustion | Respiratory and heart disease; acid rain
  [98:2:0.1] Water | Sewage; industrial effluents | Water-borne diseases, eutrophication, fish kills
  [98:2:0.2] Soil | Chemical fertilisers and pesticides; industrial waste; mining | Loss of fertility, toxic crops
  [98:2:0.3] Noise | Construction activities, traffic | Hearing loss, stress
[98:2:1] - APPSC G2 Mains 2025 match: **air – industrial emissions, water – sewage, soil – chemical fertilisers, noise – construction**.
[98:2:2] - **Natural** causes of air pollution: forest fires, volcanoes, dust storms, **pollen**, spores. **Pollen grains are not anthropogenic**; pesticide residues, sewer gases and coal/oil power stations are.
[98:2:3] - **Fertilisers**: indiscriminate use pollutes **soil, water and air** (ammonia) → "all of these". Agro-chemicals cause **water pollution** (runoff), not better fertility.
[98:2:4] - **Nitrogen pollution** → loss of biodiversity, pollution of rivers and seas, **ozone depletion** (N2O) → all three.
[98:2:5] - **Mineral extraction** → biodiversity loss, erosion and water pollution (all). Pollution disturbs the **balance of nature, geochemical cycles and flora** (all).
[98:2:6] - **Recycling** paper, plastic and metals cuts the waste dumped on land → reduces **soil (land) pollution** (also eases air and water).
[98:2:7] - **Sewage treatment plants** = sustainable technology against water pollution; **fossil fuels** → air pollution + GHG → both statements correct.
[98:2:8] - Highest chemical pollution from industrial effluents: **leather industry** (tanneries – chromium; water and soil).
[98:2:9] - Control measures (NTRUHS): poisonous gases – **catalytic converters**; green tax – **old vehicles**; untreated water – **coagulation**; plastic waste – **Plastic Waste Management Amendment Rules 2021**.
[98:2:10] - Air pollution deaths: **~7 million a year (WHO)**. Survey (Yale, 2024-25): 53% of Indians faced water pollution, 52% air pollution.
[98:2:11] ~~ Sources: NextGen Environment APPSC 09 p.12, p.14; NextGen Environment APPSC 10 p.11-12; Rocket Environment p.21-22; app notes 5:98 sub 0
