## SUB 2 | Panchamrit and Net Zero 2070
[2:0] - Announced by the PM at **COP26, Glasgow, 1 Nov 2021**. Hook: **500-50-1-45-2070**.
[2:1] TABLE head: # | Pledge | By
  [2:1.0] 1 | 500 GW non-fossil energy capacity (not 250 GW) | 2030
  [2:1.1] 2 | 50% of energy requirements from renewables (not 60%) | 2030
  [2:1.2] 3 | Cut projected (cumulative) carbon emissions by 1 billion tonnes | 2030
  [2:1.3] 4 | Cut carbon (emissions) intensity of the economy by 45% (from 2005) | 2030
  [2:1.4] 5 | Net zero (not 2045 or 2050) | 2070
[2:2] - TSPSC JL 2023: true = **II, III and IV** (I says 250 GW; V says 2045). COP26 "NOT true": "60% of energy from renewables" (it is 50%).
[2:3] - **Net zero** = residual emissions **balanced by removals** (forests, carbon capture) – **not** zero gross emissions, and **not** a fixed 2050 date for every country (UPPCS 2021: only statement 2 correct). India's year **2070** is later than developed countries' 2050, defended by **CBDR-RC** and development needs.
[2:4] - **LT-LEDS** presented at **COP27 (Nov 2022)**; 7 transitions: low-carbon electricity, transport, urban adaptation and efficient buildings, decoupling industry, **CO2 removal**, forest cover, low-carbon finance. **Not**: expansion of fossil-fuel power.
[2:5] - **India's Net Zero Portal** and **NAPCC Dashboard** (MoEFCC, **16 Sep 2026**): **voluntary** registration and disclosure of net-zero targets (Scope 1, 2; Scope 3 voluntary).
[2:6] - **IEA "Net-Zero by 2050"** roadmap: **May 2021**.

## SUB 4 | Energy Transition and Carbon Sink
[4:0] - An effective **positive measure** against climate change = **expansion of carbon sinks through afforestation** (AP Panchayat Secretary) – not subsidising electricity or removing all fuel subsidies.
[4:1] - Non-fossil **51.5%** of capacity (Nov 2025); **generation share only ~25%** (storage shortage).
[4:2] - **3rd** in renewable capacity, after China and the USA (IRENA 2026, 250.50 GW end-2025).
[4:3] - **Green Hydrogen Mission**: **₹19,744 crore**; **5 MMT/yr by 2030**. **Nuclear Energy Mission**: **5 indigenous SMRs by 2033**.
[4:4] - **Pumped storage**: **258 GW** identified; **7 GW** operational; >17 GW more by 2030. First standalone BESS: 180 MW (**Gujarat**). AP: **Pinnapuram 1,680 MW** integrated RE + pumped storage.
[4:5] - Carbon-sink programmes: **Green India Mission** (**5 Mha new + 5 Mha improved**); **MISHTI** (~540 sq km of **mangroves**). Forest and tree cover **~24.6%** (2021).
[4:6] - India has **not signed** the Global Methane Pledge.
[4:7] ~~ Sources: Rocket CA 2026 Part 01 p.161; Rocket Environment p.14; app notes 5:96 sub 2
## SUB 5 | Mission LiFE
[5:0] - **LiFE = Lifestyle for Environment** – an **India-led global mass movement** to nudge **individual and community action** to protect the environment (TSPSC 2023).
[5:1] - Idea first put forward at **COP26 (2021)**; LiFE Global Movement **5 June 2022**; Mission launched **20 Oct 2022** at **Kevadia (Ekta Nagar), Gujarat**, by the **PM** with the **UN Secretary-General** – not 5 June 2021, not Maharashtra, not the President.
[5:2] - **P3** = **Pro-Planet People**; **75 LiFE actions under 7 themes** (save energy, save water, say no to single-use plastic, sustainable food, reduce waste, e-waste, healthy lifestyles). Aligned schemes: **UJALA** (LED bulbs), **PAT** (industrial efficiency).

## SUB 6 | Carbon Markets – CCTS and Green Credit Programme
[6:0] TABLE head:  | CCTS | Green Credit Programme
  [6:0.0] Launch | Notified June 2023 | Oct 2023
  [6:0.1] Law | Sec 14AA, Energy Conservation Act 2001 (amended 2022) | EPA 1986
  [6:0.2] Run by | BEE (Ministry of Power) | ICFRE, Dehradun
  [6:0.3] Nature | India's first mandatory compliance carbon market | Incentive for voluntary environmental actions
[6:1] - CCTS = **compliance** mechanism (energy-intensive sectors get GHG Emission Intensity targets; beating them earns tradeable **Carbon Credit Certificates**) + **offset** mechanism (voluntary). **Intensity-based, not cap-based**; supports the **45%** intensity target.
[6:2] - **282 obligated entities** (Oct 2025: aluminium, cement, chlor-alkali, pulp and paper). **UK CBAM** recognises CCTS as a carbon price from **1 Jan 2027**.
[6:3] - GCP afforestation can be exchanged against **compensatory afforestation** obligations.
[6:4] - **Surat ETS**: particulate matter cut **20-30%**. **Khasi Hills REDD+** (Meghalaya): India's first **Plan Vivo**-certified REDD+ project.
