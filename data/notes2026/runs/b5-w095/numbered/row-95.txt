## SUB 1 | Kyoto Protocol, CDM and Carbon Credits
[1:0] - **Kyoto Protocol:** adopted **11 Dec 1997** at **Kyoto, Japan (COP3)**; in force **16 Feb 2005**; **first legally binding** treaty with GHG targets; called on **industrialised (Annex I) nations** to cut emissions (~5% below **1990** in 2008-12) – deals with **greenhouse gases / climate change**, not ozone (Montreal), poverty or arms.
[1:1] - **Binding on Annex I only**; **USA never ratified**; **Canada withdrew** (announced 12 Dec 2011, effective Dec 2012) – first to leave.
[1:2] - **Six gases:** CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, HFCs, PFCs, SF₆; **NF₃** added only for the 2nd period. **Methane** is a more potent GHG than CO₂.
[1:3] - **Doha Amendment (2012):** 2nd period **2013-2020**, **18%** below 1990; in force 31 Dec 2020.
[1:4] - **India acceded to Kyoto in August 2002** (UNFCCC 1992 → Kyoto 2002; First Five Year Plan 1951 came first).
[1:5] TABLE head: Mechanism | Between | Unit
  [1:5.0] International Emissions Trading | Annex I ↔ Annex I | Allowances
  [1:5.1] CDM (Article 12) | Annex I (Annex B) party runs a project in a developing country | CERs
  [1:5.2] Joint Implementation | Annex I project in another Annex I country | ERUs
[1:6] - **CDM:** the market-based device under the UNFCCC/Kyoto that lets developing countries get funds and technology from developed countries; it **encourages** (does not prohibit) developed-country investment in developing countries; CDM projects are **not** confined to Annex I countries.
[1:7] - **Annex I** is a UNFCCC/Kyoto grouping (not Cartagena); **CERs** come from the CDM (issued by the **CDM Executive Board**), not Nagoya → only "CDM – Kyoto" is correctly matched (UPSC 2016).
[1:8] # Carbon credits and markets
[1:9] - **Carbon credit concept:** originated from the **Kyoto Protocol (1997)** – not the Rio Earth Summit, Montreal Protocol or G-8; **1 credit = 1 tonne CO₂e**; **carbon trading** belongs to Kyoto, **not Montreal**.
[1:10] - **Paris Article 6:** **6.2** bilateral deals (India-Japan **JCM**, Aug 2025); **6.4** UN-run Paris Agreement Crediting Mechanism (credits = **A6.4ERs**), successor to the CDM; rules completed and **fully operational at COP29**; first 6.4 credits: clean-cooking project in **Myanmar**.
[1:11] - **GHG Protocol** (WRI + WBCSD): an **international accounting tool** for governments and businesses to quantify and manage GHG emissions – not a UN incentive scheme or REDD+ initiative.
[1:12] > Angles: pairs (Annex I / CER / CDM), "carbon credit originated from", CDM "not true" statements, Canada's withdrawal.

[3:1] - **First major international agreement on climate change** = the **UNFCCC** (the 1987 Montreal Protocol is on ozone).
[3:2] - **Objective:** stabilise **greenhouse-gas** concentrations at a level that prevents **dangerous anthropogenic interference** (GHG mitigation – not just CO₂ or fossil fuels). Two instruments: **Kyoto Protocol** and **Paris Agreement**. **IPCC AR1 (1990)** fed into it.

## SUB 4 | Paris Agreement and NDCs
[4:0] - **Goal:** hold warming **well below 2°C** and pursue **1.5°C** above pre-industrial levels (APPSC FBO: **1.5°C**).
[4:1] - **Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs):** each country sets its own (not centrally, UN- or capacity-determined); **INDCs** = **Intended NDCs**, the **plans of action to combat climate change** submitted before Paris; NDCs every **5 years**; each must go beyond the last (**ratchet**).
[4:2] - **Legal nature (APPSC G2 Mains 2025):** a **legally binding treaty**, but only the **procedure** binds (prepare, communicate, report NDCs); **NDC targets bind no party** – not the **USA, China, India or EU** → "**None of these**".
[4:3] TABLE head: Article | Subject
  [4:3.0] 6.2 / 6.4 | Carbon markets

  [4:3.2] 13 | Enhanced Transparency Framework; Biennial Transparency Reports (first due end-2024)

## SUB 6 | IPCC – Body and Reports
[6:0] - Set up **1988** by **WMO and UNEP**; Secretariat **Geneva**; does **no original research**; each report has a **Summary for Policymakers**; **Nobel Peace Prize 2007** with **Al Gore**. **WMO** (1950; UN agency 1951; HQ **Geneva**) grew out of the **International Meteorological Organization (1873)**.
[6:1] - Working Groups: **WG I** physical science; **WG II** impacts, adaptation, vulnerability; **WG III** mitigation.
[6:2] - **ARs:** AR1 1990, AR2 1995, AR3 2001, AR4 2007, **AR5 2014**, AR6 2021-23 (Synthesis **20 Mar 2023**); **AR7** began **July 2023** (Special Report on **Climate Change and Cities**, 2027).
[6:3] TABLE head: Report | Finding
  [6:3.0] SR1.5 (Oct 2018) | Warming likely reaches 1.5°C between 2030 and 2052; net CO₂ must fall ~45% from 2010 by 2030 and reach net zero by 2050; deep, rapid cuts (not gradual)
  [6:3.1] SRCCL (Aug 2019) | Land, desertification, food security
  [6:3.2] SROCC (Sep 2019) | Marine heatwaves more frequent (doubled since 1982)
  [6:3.3] AR6 SYR (2023) | 1.1°C (2011-2020); 1.5°C likely in the early 2030s; 70-85% renewable electricity by 2050; carbon budget ~500 GtCO₂ from 2020
