=== Kyoto Protocol, CDM and Carbon Credits
- **Kyoto Protocol:** adopted **11 Dec 1997** at **Kyoto, Japan (COP3)**; in force **16 Feb 2005**; **first legally binding** treaty with GHG targets; called on **industrialised (Annex I) nations** to cut emissions (~5% below **1990** in 2008-12) – deals with **greenhouse gases / climate change**, not ozone (Montreal), poverty or arms.
- **Binding on Annex I only**; **USA never ratified**; **Canada withdrew** (announced 12 Dec 2011, effective Dec 2012) – first to leave.
- **Six gases:** CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, HFCs, PFCs, SF₆; **NF₃** added only for the 2nd period. **Methane** is a more potent GHG than CO₂.
- **Doha Amendment (2012):** 2nd period **2013-2020**, **18%** below 1990; in force 31 Dec 2020.
- **India acceded to Kyoto in August 2002** (UNFCCC 1992 → Kyoto 2002; First Five Year Plan 1951 came first).
| Mechanism | Between | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| International Emissions Trading | Annex I ↔ Annex I | Allowances |
| **CDM (Article 12)** | Annex I (Annex B) party runs a project **in a developing country** | **CERs** |
| Joint Implementation | Annex I project in another Annex I country | ERUs |
- **CDM:** the market-based device under the UNFCCC/Kyoto that lets developing countries get funds and technology from developed countries; it **encourages** (does not prohibit) developed-country investment in developing countries; CDM projects are **not** confined to Annex I countries.
- **Annex I** is a UNFCCC/Kyoto grouping (not Cartagena); **CERs** come from the CDM (issued by the **CDM Executive Board**), not Nagoya → only "CDM – Kyoto" is correctly matched (UPSC 2016).
# Carbon credits and markets
- **Carbon credit concept:** originated from the **Kyoto Protocol (1997)** – not the Rio Earth Summit, Montreal Protocol or G-8; **1 credit = 1 tonne CO₂e**; **carbon trading** belongs to Kyoto, **not Montreal**.
- **Paris Article 6:** **6.2** bilateral deals (India-Japan **JCM**, Aug 2025); **6.4** UN-run Paris Agreement Crediting Mechanism (credits = **A6.4ERs**), successor to the CDM; rules completed and **fully operational at COP29**; first 6.4 credits: clean-cooking project in **Myanmar**.
- **GHG Protocol** (WRI + WBCSD): an **international accounting tool** for governments and businesses to quantify and manage GHG emissions – not a UN incentive scheme or REDD+ initiative.
> Angles: pairs (Annex I / CER / CDM), "carbon credit originated from", CDM "not true" statements, Canada's withdrawal.

=== UNFCCC and the Family of Environmental Conventions
| Treaty | Adopted | In force | Remember |
|---|---|---|---|
| **UNFCCC** | **9 May 1992, New York**; opened for signature at the **Rio Earth Summit (June 1992)** | **21 March 1994** (after 50 ratifications) | 198 Parties; Secretariat **Bonn** |
| Kyoto Protocol | 11 Dec 1997 | 16 Feb 2005 | First binding targets |
| Paris Agreement | 12 Dec 2015 (signed from **22 April 2016**) | **4 Nov 2016** | India ratified **2 Oct 2016**; meeting of Parties = **CMA** |
- **First major international agreement on climate change** = the **UNFCCC** (the 1987 Montreal Protocol is on ozone).
- **Objective:** stabilise **greenhouse-gas** concentrations at a level that prevents **dangerous anthropogenic interference** (GHG mitigation – not just CO₂ or fossil fuels). Two instruments: **Kyoto Protocol** and **Paris Agreement**. **IPCC AR1 (1990)** fed into it.
| Group | Members | Obligation |
|---|---|---|
| Annex I | Industrialised + economies in transition | Lead emission cuts |
| **Annex II** | OECD members of Annex I | **Provide finance and technology** to developing countries |
| Non-Annex I | Developing countries incl. India | No binding cuts |
- **CBDR-RC:** developed countries, with most **historical emissions**, should lead – **Rio Declaration Principle 7**, UNFCCC Art. 3.1, Paris Art. 2.2.

=== Paris Agreement and NDCs
- **Goal:** hold warming **well below 2°C** and pursue **1.5°C** above pre-industrial levels (APPSC FBO: **1.5°C**).
- **Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs):** each country sets its own (not centrally, UN- or capacity-determined); **INDCs** = **Intended NDCs**, the **plans of action to combat climate change** submitted before Paris; NDCs every **5 years**; each must go beyond the last (**ratchet**).
- **Legal nature (APPSC G2 Mains 2025):** a **legally binding treaty**, but only the **procedure** binds (prepare, communicate, report NDCs); **NDC targets bind no party** – not the **USA, China, India or EU** → "**None of these**".
| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| 6.2 / 6.4 | Carbon markets |
| 9.1 | Finance from developed countries |
| **13** | Enhanced Transparency Framework; Biennial Transparency Reports (first due end-**2024**) |
| **14** | Global Stocktake every **5 years** (first at COP28) |
| **28** | Withdrawal: notice after **3 years** from entry into force; effect **1 year** after notice |

=== IPCC – Body and Reports
- Set up **1988** by **WMO and UNEP**; Secretariat **Geneva**; does **no original research**; each report has a **Summary for Policymakers**; **Nobel Peace Prize 2007** with **Al Gore**. **WMO** (1950; UN agency 1951; HQ **Geneva**) grew out of the **International Meteorological Organization (1873)**.
- Working Groups: **WG I** physical science; **WG II** impacts, adaptation, vulnerability; **WG III** mitigation.
- **ARs:** AR1 1990, AR2 1995, AR3 2001, AR4 2007, **AR5 2014**, AR6 2021-23 (Synthesis **20 Mar 2023**); **AR7** began **July 2023** (Special Report on **Climate Change and Cities**, 2027).
| Report | Finding |
|---|---|
| **SR1.5 (Oct 2018)** | Warming likely reaches **1.5°C between 2030 and 2052**; net CO₂ must fall **~45% from 2010 by 2030** and reach **net zero by 2050**; deep, rapid cuts (not gradual) |
| SRCCL (Aug 2019) | Land, desertification, food security |
| SROCC (Sep 2019) | Marine heatwaves **more frequent** (doubled since 1982) |
| AR6 SYR (2023) | **1.1°C** (2011-2020); 1.5°C likely in the **early 2030s**; **70-85%** renewable electricity by 2050; carbon budget **~500 GtCO₂** from 2020 |
- **AR6 on India:** warming **faster than the global average**; Gangotri retreated ~1,700 m; **black carbon lowers snow albedo**.
- **SSPs** (AR6) replaced the **RCPs** (AR5); **SSP3 = Regional Rivalry**.
> Angles: SR1.5 numbers (2030-2052; net zero by 2050), what each WG does.

=== India in Climate Negotiations
- **Per capita:** India's per-capita emissions are far **below** industrialised nations (about 2.9 t CO₂e vs USA 18 t, China 11 t) – in 2012 India was the **lowest per-capita** emitter among the USA, UK, China and India.
- **BASIC** (4 members: Brazil, South Africa, India, China): India **attended** the 19th BASIC meeting at **Sun City** (so "did not attend" is the untrue statement, JPSC 2016).
- **Blocs:** **G77 and China** (largest developing-country group); LMDC; **AOSIS** (small islands, push 1.5°C). At **Bonn SB64 (June 2026)** India with G77+China, LMDC, BASIC sought agenda space on **Article 9.1** finance.
- **COP26:** **Panchamrit**; India pushed **"phase down"** of unabated coal. **Not signed:** Global Methane Pledge, **Glasgow Leaders' Declaration on Forests and Land Use**, COP28 renewables pledge, COP28 health declaration. **COP33 bid** made at **COP28**, withdrawn April 2026.
- **Net zero 2070** (later than 2050) – development needs and **coal dependence**; wants **clean, affordable technology and finance**; coal measures incl. **wet scrubbers**, ESPs, gasification.
- **EU CBAM** (iron/steel, aluminium, cement, fertilisers, hydrogen, electricity; definitive 1 Jan 2026): India – **violates CBDR-RC, green protectionism**; 12th BRICS Environment Ministers (New Delhi, Aug 2026): "unilateral, punitive, discriminatory, protectionist".
- **Platforms:** G20 New Delhi 2023 – **Green Development Pact**, **Global Biofuels Alliance**; **Voice of Global South** Summits since **Jan 2023**; **Mission LiFE** – individual behaviour change; **ISA** – COP21 (2015), India + France, HQ **Gurugram**, first intergovernmental body headquartered in India.
- **Carbon space** = room for developing countries to grow; sea-level-rise refugees fall outside the **1951 Refugee Convention**.
