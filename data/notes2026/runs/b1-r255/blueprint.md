## 3. Repeats and traps
- **Bapatla, 26 May 1913, B.N. Sarma** – asked 25+ times; Sarma presided yet **opposed** the demand; secretary **Konda Venkatappayya**; the resolution passed only at **Vijayawada 1914**.
- **Session order:** Bapatla 1913 – Vijayawada 1914 – Visakhapatnam 1915 – Kakinada 1916 – Nellore 1917 (APPSC G2 Mains 2025 match = a-III, b-II, c-IV, d-I).
- **First Rayalaseema Mahasabha = Madras, 28 Jan 1934** (one NextGen sheet slips to 1936); 2nd = Kadapa 1935.
- **Sri Bagh = 16 Nov 1937, Kasinathuni Nageswara Rao's house in Madras, Coastal-Rayalaseema** – not Telangana, not 60:40.
- **AU first HQ = Vijayawada (1926)**, not Guntur; Waltair 1930; SVU 1954.
- **Hindu articles = Jonnavittula Gurunatham**, not Sitarama Sastri (APPSC G2 Mains 2017 key wrong).
- **First INC session in Andhra = Kakinada 1923**; Bezawada 1921 was only an AICC meeting.
