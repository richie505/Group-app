## SUB 2 | DBT, PAHAL and Ujjwala
[2:0] - **DBT** piloted **1 Jan 2013** (7 schemes, 20 districts); scaled up in 2015 with JAM; FY 2024-25: **₹6.9 lakh crore, 327 schemes**; the **DBT Mission** sits in the **Cabinet Secretariat**.
[2:1] - **State DBT nodal officer / coordinator:** a **Principal Secretary (Planning/IT/Finance)** or equivalent officer.
[2:2] - **MGNREGS:** largest number of DBT transactions in 2018-19 (about 43 crore by Feb 2019); **PAHAL** = largest cash-transfer scheme by beneficiaries.
[2:3] TABLE head: Scheme | Fact
  [2:3.0] PAHAL (Pratyaksha Hastaantarit Laabh) | DBT of LPG subsidy into the consumer's bank account; 54 districts 15 Nov 2014, national 1 Jan 2015; first JAM application; Guinness record (2015): world's largest cash-transfer programme; Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas
  [2:3.1] PM Ujjwala Yojana | Free LPG connection to women of BPL households – clean cooking fuel to safeguard the health of women and children; 1 May 2016, Ballia (UP); "Swachh Indhan Behtar Jeevan"; ₹8,000 crore for 3 years; 5 crore connections; support ₹1,600 per connection (not ₹2,800); target later raised to 8 crore
[2:4] - Schemes vs inequality: PMJDY, MGNREGA, Stand-Up India reduce inequality; **UDAN** (regional air connectivity) does not.
[2:5] ~~ Sources: Business Standard/IANS 10 May 2019 (top DBT schemes, MGNREGA 43.24 crore transactions; web search); app notes 3:38 sub 2

## SUB 4 | NSAP and Social Security Concepts
[4:0] - **NSAP:** from **15 Aug 1995**; **Ministry of Rural Development**; Core of the Core CSS; fulfils **Article 41**; BPL households; chief aim = **old-age pension to the very poor**.
[4:1] TABLE head: Component | Who | Benefit
  [4:1.0] IGNOAPS | 60-79 / 80+ | ₹200 / ₹500 a month
  [4:1.1] IGNWPS | BPL widows | Monthly pension
  [4:1.2] IGNDPS | BPL, 18-79, 80%+ disability | ₹300 a month
  [4:1.3] NFBS | Family losing breadwinner | Lump sum
  [4:1.4] Annapurna | Needy elderly not on IGNOAPS | 10 kg free foodgrains/month
[4:2] - **NOT in NSAP / not social security:** **Mahila Kisan Sashaktikaran Pariyojana** (DAY-NRLM), **Skill Development Programme**, **SJSRY** (employment generation).
[4:3] - ~3.09 crore beneficiaries (FY 2025-26); May 2026: old-age pension lost nearly **half its real value** to inflation.
[4:4] - **Universal Basic Income** = an unconditional **minimum income support by the Government to every citizen** (Economic Survey 2016-17).
[4:5] - **ILO** = the UN body for social security; about **80% of the world's population** lacked adequate **social security** (ILO).
