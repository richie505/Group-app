## SUB 1 | Food for Work and NFFWP
[1:0] - **Food for Work Programme:** launched **April 1977** by the **Janata government** to use surplus foodgrain stocks; wages paid in **food grains** (in part or full, the rest in cash); renamed **NREP in October 1980**.
[1:1] - **First wage programme among exam options:** the **Crash (printed "Cash") Scheme for Rural Employment, 1971** – earlier than Food for Work (1977), NREP (1980) and JRY (1989).
[1:2] - **National Food for Work Programme (NFFWP):** **14 November 2004**, by the UPA government, in the **150 most backward districts**, during the **Tenth Plan (2002-07)**; an interim step before NREGA.
[1:3] - **NFFWP + SGRY** were subsumed into NREGA in its districts from **2006**.
[1:4] - The Planning Commission was **not** a constitutional body (it was set up by a cabinet resolution).
[1:5] > APPSC 2004: 150 districts; Group-II 2017: 1977; CDS 2008: FFW in the Tenth Plan.
[1:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC 04 p.25; Rocket Sheets Indian Economy p.4; pubadmin.institute "Evolution of employment schemes"; app notes 3:36 sub 1

## SUB 3 | MGNREGA – Entitlement, Eligibility and Objectives
[3:0] - **Objective:** "**enhancing livelihood security** in rural areas by providing **at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year** to every **household** whose **adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work**".
[3:1] - **Unit = household**, not each adult: "100 days to every adult" is wrong; **150 days** is wrong for the normal guarantee.
[3:2] - **Eligible:** adult members of **any rural household** – not only SC/ST, BPL or backward communities.
[3:3] - **Unskilled** manual work only – "100 days of **skilled** labour" is not an objective.
[3:4] - Other aims: **durable assets** (roads, canals, ponds, wells), rural income, **women's empowerment**, inclusive growth.
[3:5] - Work within **5 km**; minimum wages; **legal entitlement** (statutory right, **not a Fundamental Right**); anchored in **Article 41** (DPSP).
[3:6] - The Act covers **all rural areas of the country** in notified phases – **not "only selected States"**.
[3:7] - **Drought-hit areas:** an extra **50 days** – **150 days** in a year in areas declared drought-affected (as in Feb 2019).
[3:8] - **Features of MGNREGS:** guaranteed wage employment, **rights-based**, rural wage employment; **nutritious food is not** a feature.
[3:9] - **SDG link:** NITI Aayog's **SDG India Index 2023-24** tracks MGNREGA under **SDG 1 (No Poverty)** – 99.7% of those who demanded work were offered it; Goal 1 score **72**.
[3:10] - **World Development Report 2014** (World Bank): "**stellar example of rural development**"; safety net in agrarian distress and **COVID-19**.
[3:11] > UPSC 2011 (any household), APPSC Group-II 2017 (household, 100 days), CDS 2020 (skilled labour is not an objective), APPSC Group-I 2023 (nutritious food is not a feature).
[3:12] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC 01 p.57; NREGA 2005 Act text (prsindia.org PDF); drishtiias.com SDG India Index 2023-24 (via web search); app notes 3:36 sub 2-3
## SUB 4 | MGNREGA – Funds, Works, Monitoring and Social Audit
[4:0] - **Women:** at least **one-third** of beneficiaries (Schedule II) – not one-half; actual share **about 56% of person-days (2016-17)**.
[4:1] - **Work within 15 days** of application, else **unemployment allowance** (**1/4 of wage for the first 30 days, 1/2 thereafter**); wages within **15 days** of work; unemployment allowance is a **transfer payment**.
[4:2] - **Funds:** Centre and State money is pooled in the **State Employment Guarantee Fund** (Section 21); the material component and the **14th Finance Commission** grants go directly to **Gram Panchayat** accounts.
[4:3] - **Agriculture and allied works:** about **two-thirds (≈68%)** of expenditure in **2016-17**, up from ≈48% in 2013-14.
[4:4] - **Permissible works:** water conservation, **percolation tanks**, **fish ponds**, **compost pits**, **cattle sheds**, land development, rural connectivity – all four in the APPSC 2017 question are permissible.
[4:5] - **Convergence:** MGNREGS is the textbook example – 90/95 person-days for **PMAY-G** houses, works with agriculture, horticulture and watershed schemes.
[4:6] - **Social audit:** **Section 17** – the **Gram Sabha** conducts regular social audits; **NREGA was the first Act** to make social audit a means of continuous public vigilance; a village social auditor must **not be a political person**.
[4:7] - **Monitoring:** **GeoMGNREGA** geo-tags assets on ISRO's **Bhuvan mobile platform**; **NREGASoft** MIS; in AP fund flow was tracked on **RAGA soft** (TCS).
[4:8] - **Newsletter** of MoRD for MGNREGA in the APPSC key: **Saaransh** (key not verified).
[4:9] - **CAPART** and **NIRDPR (Hyderabad)**: autonomous bodies under the Ministry of Rural Development.
[4:10] > 15 days twice: allowance if no work within 15 days of application; wages within 15 days of work.

## SUB 5 | VB-G RAM G Act, 2025 – Replacing MGNREGA
[5:0] - **Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025**: passed in **December 2025** (18 Dec); in force from **1 July 2026**; nodal **Ministry of Rural Development**.
[5:1] TABLE head: Aspect | MGNREGA 2005 | VB-G RAM G 2025
  [5:1.0] Guarantee | 100 days/household | 125 days/household, operated over 305 days
  [5:1.1] Seasonal pause | None | States may pause up to 60 days (sowing / harvest)
  [5:1.2] Funding | Mostly Central, demand-driven | 60:40; 90:10 NE and Himalayan States; normative allocation
  [5:1.3] Wages | Within 15 days | Floor ₹300/day (2026-27), indexed to CPI-AL
  [5:1.4] Works | Many categories | 4 priority areas: water security, core rural infrastructure, livelihood infrastructure, extreme-weather mitigation; 375 works (Oct 2026)
  [5:1.5] Admin ceiling | 6% | 9%
[5:2] - Job cards → **Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Cards**; Viksit Gram Panchayat Plans; biometric attendance; unemployment allowance kept (15 days).
[5:3] - **AP ranked first** in the first 45 days (2.10 crore person-days); State-Level Council chaired by the CM.
