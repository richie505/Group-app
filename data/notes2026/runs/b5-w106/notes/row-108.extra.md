=== Biotech Innovations and Hubs in News
| Innovation | Institution / place |
|---|---|
| **IndiGau** – India's **first cattle genomic chip** (Aug 2021), for conserving **pure indigenous breeds** (Gir, Kankrej, Sahiwal, Ongole); the government called it the world's **largest** cattle chip (not the second largest) | **NIAB, Hyderabad** (DBT) |
| **Next Generation Sequencing (NGS) facility** (July 2019; up to 30 human genomes a day) | **CSIR-CCMB, Hyderabad** |
| **Implantable bio-artificial pancreas** – a silk scaffold holding insulin-making beta cells (2017; preclinical) | **IIT Guwahati** (Prof. Biman Mandal) |
| **Paper kit** that tests milk freshness and how well it was pasteurised | **IIT Guwahati** |
| First genome sequence of the **Jamun tree** (*Syzygium cumini*) | **IISER Bhopal** |
| **Genome Valley** biotech cluster | Telangana – **Ranga Reddy** district in books (Shamirpet–Turkapally belt, now Medchal-Malkajgiri) |
| AP government's **Biotechnology Hub** (printed key) | **Visakhapatnam** |
- **Durga (Kanupriya Agarwal):** India's first and the world's second **test-tube baby**, born **3 Oct 1978** in Kolkata, created by **Dr Subhash Mukhopadhyay**; his work was officially recognised only later.
- **Artificial womb – EVE (ex-vivo uterine environment) therapy:** an **amniotic-fluid bath + artificial placenta** to prevent severe morbidity in extremely premature infants; tested on **lambs** (Univ. of Western Australia with Tohoku University Hospital, Japan), **not yet on humans**.
- **Optogenetics:** a relatively new field of biotechnology that uses light-sensitive proteins to turn **light into brain activity and brain activity into light**. "Biased inheritance of genes to alter whole populations" is a **gene drive**, not optogenetics.
- **Aerial metagenomics (airborne eDNA):** **collecting DNA samples from the air of a habitat at one go** to detect many species together.
