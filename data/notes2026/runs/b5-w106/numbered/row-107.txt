## SUB 1 | Nodal Ministries and Legal Basis of Epidemic Powers
[1:0] - **Nodal ministry for all disaster management: Ministry of Home Affairs**; **for epidemics / biological disasters: Ministry of Health & Family Welfare** (an APPSC 2018 key gave MHA – wrong).
[1:1] - **COVID lockdown** (March 2020): legal basis = **DM Act 2005** (orders of NDMA/NEC and MHA) **and the Epidemic Diseases Act 1897** – not the Mental Health Care Act 2017 or Enemy Property Act 1968.
[1:2] - **Epidemic Diseases Act 1897**: States notify diseases; **2020 amendment** (ordinance April, Act September 2020) – violence against health workers **cognizable, non-bailable**; **3 months–5 years** jail, **Rs 50,000–2 lakh** fine (grievous hurt 6 months–7 years). Criticised as colonial, no definition of "dangerous epidemic disease".
[1:3] - Draft Public Health Bill 2017 – never enacted. Notifiable diseases: urgent within **24 hours**; 20 States made human rabies notifiable (Jan 2026).
[1:4] TABLE head: Institution | Role
  [1:4.0] MoHFW (DGHS technical arm; DM Cell Dec 2020) | Nodal for epidemics
  [1:4.1] NCDC (formerly NICD) | Runs IDSP and National Public Health EOC
  [1:4.2] NDMA | Biological Disaster Management guidelines 2008
