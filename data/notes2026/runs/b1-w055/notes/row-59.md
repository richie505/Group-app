=== The Three Crowned Kingdoms – Capitals, Ports, Emblems, Rivers
# Muvendar
- **Muvendar** = the three crowned kings: **Chera, Chola, Pandya** – the only ruling dynasties of the Sangam age.
| Kingdom | Capital | Port(s) | Emblem | River | Territory |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Chera** | **Vanji = Karur (Karuvur)** | **Muziris (Mushiri, Muchiri), Tondi** | Bow | **Periyar** | Central/north Kerala + Kongu |
| **Chola** | **Uraiyur → Puhar** | **Puhar (Kaveripattinam)** | **Tiger** | Kaveri | **Kaveri delta**, Tiruchi up to southern Andhra |
| **Pandya** | **Madurai** | **Korkai** (pearl fishery) | **Fish** | **Vaigai** | Madurai, Tirunelveli, Ramnad |
- **Chera capital** = Karur/Vanji, not Kanchipuram or Madurai (State PSC).
- **Pandya lifeline** = **Vaigai** (printed "Vengi" in one PYQ) – not Godavari, Krishna or Tungabhadra.
- **Ma'bar** = Arab writers' name for the **Pandya** kingdom (Coromandel coast) (G2 2000, APPSC PYQ).
- **Odd one out:** **Pallavas** (rose from the **4th c. CE**) and **Kadambas** (4th c., Banavasi) are **not** Sangam dynasties (UPPSC 2010; State PSC).
# Ports by coast
| Coast | Ports |
|---|---|
| **West (mainly Chera)** | **Muziris (Mushiri), Tondi, Naura, Nelisanda** |
| East | **Korkai** (Pandya, Gulf of Mannar), **Puhar** (Chola), **Arikamedu** (near Puducherry) |
- **Chera kingdom parts:** **Musiri and Tondi** (IAS 1996); Korkai is Pandya and Puhar Chola.
- **Korkai, Poompuhar and Muchiri** were all **ports** (UPSC 2023).
- **Uraiyur** (inland Chola capital) was a **cotton** centre, named in the Periplus.
# Why small kingdoms?
- South India grew **small regional kingdoms** rather than large empires because it lacked **vast continuous fertile plains** – its fertile land lies in separate river deltas (IAS 1999).

=== Rulers – Karikala, Udiyanjeral, Senguttuvan, Nedunjeliyan
| Ruler | Kingdom | Achievement / title |
|---|---|---|
| **Karikala (Karikalan)** | **Chola** | Won **Venni** (Cheras, Pandyas + **11** chieftains) and Vahaipparandalai; built the **Kallanai (Grand Anicut)** on the **Kaveri**; subject of **Pattinappalai** |
| **Udiyanjeral** (Perum Sorru Udhiyan Cheralathan) | **Chera** | **First historical king** of the Sangam age; legend: **fed the armies of the Mahabharata war** |
| Imayavaramban Nedunjeral Adan | Chera | Claimed his boundary up to the **Himalayas** |
| **Senguttuvan** ("Red Chera") | Chera | Founded the **Pattini cult** (Kannagi as the **ideal wife**); idol stone from the **Himalayas**; Sri Lankan king **Gajabahu** attended (not Augustus) |
| **Nedunjeliyan** | **Pandya** | Won **Talaiyalanganam** over the Chera, Chola and Velir chiefs → Pandya control of the Tamil country; "Arya Padai Kadantha" Nedunjeliyan **executed Kovalan** |
- **Karikala** = Chola (G2 2000); **Udiyanjeral** = **Chera** (Uttarakhand 2021); "Udiyanjeral – Chola" is the wrong pair (UPSC 2026).
- **Renati, Pottapi and Nellore Chodas** (Telugu) claimed descent from **Karikala**.
- **15 Aug 2026:** remains of a Sangam-period water structure found in the **Kollidam** river.
~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval India Part 3 p.31-34; app notes 1:59 (sub 3, 4, 5)

=== Sangam Kingdoms and Rome
- **Cheras** = the power most associated with **trade with Rome**: Romans kept a settlement at **Muziris** and built a **temple of Augustus** there (State PSC).
- **Pandya embassy to Augustus:** a Pandya king sent an ambassador to Rome (c. **26–20 BCE**) (MPPSC 2005) – not Chola or Chera.
- Pliny called Muziris the "first emporium of India".
~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval India Part 3 p.33-34; Rocket Indian History Part 1 p.89; app notes 1:59 (sub 1)

=== Polity – King, Councils, Chieftains
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ko, Kon, Mannan, Vendan** | **King** (RAS 2010) |
| **Aimperunkulu** | **Five-member** council: ministers, priests, commanders, envoys, spies (not poets) |
| **Avai** | **Royal court** |
| **Velir** | Minor hill and forest **chieftains** |
| **Enadi** | Title of **army commanders** |
- **Kadai ezhu vallals** (seven great patrons): **Pari, Kari, Ori, Pegan, Ay, Athiyaman, Nalli** – Nedunjeliyan is not one.

=== Decline and Later Links
- Pandya and Sangam power fell under the **Kalabhra** invasion (3rd c. CE).
- **18 May 2026:** the **Leiden copper plates** (Chola grants to the Chulamanivarma vihara, Nagapattinam) – seal: Chola **tiger** with the Pandya **two fish** and the Chera **bow**.
- **Ennayiram and Tribhuvani** (South Arcot) = **Vedic colleges** of the **later Chola** period.
