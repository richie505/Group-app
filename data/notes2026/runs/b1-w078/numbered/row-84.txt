## SUB 0 | Mitakshara, Dayabhaga and Medieval Jurists
[0:0] - **Mitakshara:** by **Vijnaneshwara** at the court of **Vikramaditya VI** – a **commentary on the Yajnavalkya Smriti** (G1 Prelims 2019 ×4).
[0:1]   - **⚠ Trap:** "treatise on inheritance" is narrower than the key; inheritance is only its best-known part. Not a commentary on the Arthashastra, a grammar or a mathematics text.
[0:2]   - "Which clearly defines Mitakshara": a **treatise on ancient Hindu law of inheritance** (WBCS 2019) – not astronomy, agriculture or medicine.
[0:3] TABLE head: Point | Mitakshara | Dayabhaga
  [0:3.0] Author | Vijnaneshwara | Jimutavahana (Bengal)
  [0:3.1] Sons' right in ancestral property | By birth (even in the father's lifetime) | Only after the father's death
  [0:3.2] Region | Most of India | Bengal and Assam
[0:4] - **UPSC 2021 statements:** only 2 is correct – "Mitakshara for upper castes, Dayabhaga for lower castes" ✗; "Mitakshara only for male members' property" ✗.

## SUB 1 | Rulers – Tailapa II to Someshvara III
[1:0] - **Also called** the **Western Chalukyas**; ruled c. **973–1189 CE** (some books 1195/1200); **543–753** is **Badami**, not Kalyani.
[1:1] - **Capital:** **Kalyani = Basavakalyan** (Karnataka); branches in order **Badami → Vengi → Kalyani**.
[1:2] TABLE head: Ruler | Achievement
  [1:2.0] Tailapa II (973) | Founder; overthrew the last Rashtrakuta Karka II
  [1:2.1] Satyasraya | Sent Bayyalanambi against Vengi (1006); repulsed by Saktivarma I with Chola help
  [1:2.2] Someshvara I Ahavamalla (1042–68) | Killed Chola Rajadhiraja I at Koppam (1053); title Vengi Purveshwara; granted Hanamkonda to Kakatiya Prola I
  [1:2.3] Vikramaditya VI (1076–1126) | Greatest; ruled 50 years; title Tribhuvanamalla; started the Chalukya-Vikrama era (1076); court of Bilhana and Vijnaneshwara
  [1:2.4] Someshvara III (1126–1138) | Son and successor of Vikramaditya VI; wrote the Manasollasa
[1:3] - **Vikramaditya VI's reign:** **1076–1126 CE** (APPSC PYQ).
[1:4] - **Someshvara III came to the throne after his father Vikramaditya VI** (SCH & Welfare 2019; one version misprints "Somesvara II").
[1:5] - **Chalukya-Vikrama era:** from **1076**, Vikramaditya VI's accession; replaced the **Saka era** in royal records.
[1:6] - **Significance:** checked Chola expansion northwards; Mitakshara shaped Hindu law for a millennium.
[1:7] ~~ Sources: app notes 1:84 (sub 0, 1); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vikramaditya_VI; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Chalukya_Empire

## SUB 3 | Chola-Chalukya Wars and Kalyani Art
[3:0] - **Cause:** rivalry over **Vengi** (coastal Andhra) and the Raichur doab.
[3:1] - **Koppam (1053):** Someshvara I killed Rajadhiraja I.
[3:2] - **Kudalasangamam (c. 1062):** at the **Krishna–Tungabhadra** confluence; the Cholas heavily defeated Someshvara I.
[3:3] - **Gadag (mature Vesara) style:** **chloritic schist (soapstone)**, not granite; decorated doorways and pillars; **star-shaped plans** later a **Hoysala** model.

## SUB 4 | End and Successor Powers
[4:0] - **Kakatiyas as vassals:** first under the **Rashtrakutas**, then the **Kalyani (Western) Chalukyas** until Rudradeva's independence (1163) (G2 2005) – not the Yadavas or Hoysalas.
[4:1] - **Khazipet inscription:** Hanamkonda granted to the Kakatiyas with **hereditary rights**.
[4:2] TABLE head: Power | Capital
  [4:2.0] Hoysalas | Dwarasamudra
  [4:2.1] Yadavas (Seunas) | Devagiri
  [4:2.2] Kakatiyas | Hanamkonda–Warangal (Beta I, Prola I were feudatories)
  [4:2.3] Kalachuris | Kalyani – Bijjala seized the throne c. 1156
[4:3] - **End:** c. **1189 CE**.
