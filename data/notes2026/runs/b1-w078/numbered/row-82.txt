## SUB 0 | Bahmani–Vijayanagara Rivalry – Raichur Doab, Talikota and the Deccan Sultanates
[0:0] # Raichur Doab – the most-asked fact of this row
[0:1] - **Where:** the **Raichur Doab (Raichur Antarvedi)** lies between the **Krishna and Tungabhadra** (G2 Mains 2017; APPSC PYQ).
[0:2] - **Why fought over:** fertile black soil and a strategic position = **main cause of the Bahmani–Vijayanagara wars** from **Harihara I's** time (G2 2003, G2-B 1989, FRO; APPSC PYQ ×3).
[0:3]   - Answer words: "**acquisition of the Raichur Doab**" / "**acquisition of territory**" – not religious discord, tribute or the Krishna-Godavari region.
[0:4] - **Main opponents of Vijayanagara:** the **Bahmanis** (G1 2008), later the Deccan Sultanates.
[0:5] - **⚠ Trap:** Raichur Doab = Krishna–Tungabhadra; the **Krishna–Godavari** doab is the separate coastal tract contested by Reddis, Velamas, Gajapatis, Vijayanagara and the Bahmanis.

## SUB 1 | Delhi Sultanate Invasions and the Fall of the Kakatiyas
[1:0] - **Five invasions under the Kakatiyas:** **1303, 1309, 1317 (1318), 1321, 1323** – the Muslims first invaded Andhra under the **Kakatiyas** (APPSC PYQ).
[1:1] - **First attack on Orugallu: 1303** – Malik Fakhruddin Juna, repulsed at Upparapalli (G2 2012).
[1:2] TABLE head: Year | Commander | Result
  [1:2.0] 1303 | Fakhruddin Juna (Alauddin Khalji) | Repulsed
  [1:2.1] 1309–10 | Malik Kafur | Prataparudra became a tributary
  [1:2.2] 1323 | Ulugh Khan (later Muhammad bin Tughlaq) | Prataparudra captured; Kakatiya rule ended
[1:3] - **Warangal renamed Sultanpur** after 1323 (AP Police Constable 2019).
[1:4] - **1323** = end of Telugu imperial unity until the **Qutb Shahis**; unifiers of all Telugu lands: **Satavahanas → Kakatiyas → Qutb Shahis**.
[1:5] - **Kakatiya temples:** **Ramappa** (Palampet, built by Recherla Rudra) and **Pillalamarri** (Civil Asst. Surgeon 2012).

## SUB 2 | Dynasties in Chronological Order and the Nayaka Kingdoms
[2:0] TABLE head: Order | Dynasty | Start | Founder / marker | Capital
  [2:0.0] 1 | Eastern Chalukyas | 624 | Kubja Vishnuvardhana; Krishna-Godavari doab | Vengi, Rajahmundry
  [2:0.1] 2 | Kakatiyas | Sovereign 1163–1323 | Rudradeva | Hanamkonda → Orugallu
  [2:0.2] 3 | Musunuri Nayakas | 1325–1368 | Prolaya and Kapaya Nayaka | Orugallu
  [2:0.3] 4 | Reddis | 1325 | Prolaya Vemareddy (Pakanadu) | Addanki → Kondavidu
  [2:0.4] 5 | Recherla Velamas | 14th c. | Singama | Rachakonda, Devarakonda
  [2:0.5] 6 | Vijayanagara | 1336 | Harihara I and Bukka | Hampi; later Penugonda, Chandragiri
  [2:0.6] 7 | Bahmanis | 1347 | Hasan Gangu | Gulbarga
  [2:0.7] 8 | Gajapatis | 1435 | Kapilendra | Odisha
[2:1] - **Chronology PYQ (G1 2004, G1):** Kakatiyas → Kondaveeti Reddis → Vijayanagara → Bahmanis → Gajapatis = **c, b, d, a, e**.
[2:2] - **Who took Warangal back from the Muslims (1325–1368):** the **Musunuri Nayaks** – not the Reddis or Recherlas (SCH & Welfare PYQ).
[2:3] - **Nayaka kingdoms:** Musunuris, Reddis and Velamas were **Prataparudra's generals** who rose after 1323; **Anavota Reddy** shifted the Reddi capital from **Addanki to Kondavidu**.
[2:4] - **Golconda fort:** ceded by **Kapaya Nayaka** to Bahmani Muhammad I after his son **Vinayaka** was killed (1361).
[2:5] - **Eastern Chalukya region:** the **Krishna-Godavari doab** (Vengi), not Karnataka, Rayalaseema or Vidarbha (Tribal Welfare PYQ).
[2:6] - **⚠ Trap:** the Reddis came **after** the Kakatiyas.
[2:7] - **Earlier founders (asked with this row):** **Simuka** – Satavahana; **Vasishthiputra Shantamula (Chantamula) I** – Ikshvaku, Krishna valley (APPSC ASO 2008); **Vijaya Deva Varma** – Salankayana; **Pulakesi I** – Chalukya; the Buddhist **Vinayavatthu** mentions **Andhrapatha**.

## SUB 3 | Regional Succession and Successor States
[3:0] TABLE head: Region | Sequence
  [3:0.0] Telangana | Kakatiyas → Delhi Sultanate (1323) → Musunuri Nayakas → Velamas / Bahmanis → Qutb Shahis
  [3:0.1] Coastal Andhra | Eastern Chalukyas → Velanati Chodas → Kakatiyas → Reddis → Gajapatis / Vijayanagara → Qutb Shahis
  [3:0.2] Rayalaseema | Kakatiyas (Kayastha chiefs) → Vijayanagara → Qutb Shahis
[3:1] - **Feudatories that became rulers:** Kakatiyas (Rashtrakutas, then Kalyani Chalukyas → sovereign 1163); **Kota family** (subordinate to the **Kakatiyas**, Dharanikota); **Kayasthas** (revolt under Ambadeva, 1272, with **Pandya** support).
[3:2] - **Successor states (UPSC 2021, official key "2 only"):** the **Mysore** kingdom grew out of the **Vijayanagara** empire ✓.
[3:3]   - The **Arcot (Carnatic) Nawabs** were Mughal appointees dependent on the Nizam, not a state born out of Hyderabad ✗.
[3:4]   - **Rohilkhand** was carved out by **Ali Muhammad Khan Rohilla** from Mughal territory, not from Ahmad Shah Durrani's conquests ✗.
[3:5] - **In news:** new **Seshachalam inscriptions** (16th c.) linked to the Tuluva or early Aravidu period.
