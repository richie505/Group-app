## SUB 1 | Religion – Sects, Agamas and the Chalukya Mother Goddess
[1:0] # Family goddess
[1:1] - **Mother goddess of the early Chalukyas:** **Hariti** – the Chalukyas called themselves **Hariti-putras** of the **Manavya gotra**, nourished by the **Seven Mothers** (Saptamatrikas) (G2 2019).
[1:2]   - **⚠ Settled:** the printed key "Cushmandini" is wrong – **Kushmandini** is a **Jain yakshi**; Rocket and the Chalukya prasastis give **Hariti**.
[1:3] - **Same goddess, other dynasty:** Hariti was also the tutelary deity of the **Ikshvakus**; the metronymic "Hariti-putra" suggests a southern origin of the Eastern Chalukyas.
[1:4] # Shaivism and Vaishnavism
[1:5] - **Shaivism dominant:** sects **Pasupata** (founder **Lakulisha**, oldest, peaceful), **Kalamukha** (extremist) and **Kapalika** (Bhairava worship).
[1:6] - **Trilinga desa:** Draksharama, Kaleshwaram and Srisailam; Srisailam was called a Kapalika kshetra by Bhavabhuti.
[1:7] - **Vaishnava kings:** **Kubja Vishnuvardhana** (Kunti Madhava temple, Pithapuram) and **Danarnava** (Mandavya Narayana temple, Samalkot).
[1:8] - **Jayasimha I** performed the **Ashvamedha**; royal prasastis boast of many Ashvamedhas.
[1:9] - **Jayasimha II** brought the **Koya Kottalu** (Boya Kottas) into the Brahmanical fold.
[1:10] # Agamas and saint traditions

[1:13] # Buddhism
[1:14] - **Declining:** Buddharamas became Shaiva temples (Dhanyakataka → Amareswara); Hiuen Tsang (639) found Buddhist sites decaying; survived at **Amaravati and Nagarjunakonda**.

## SUB 2 | Administration – Offices, Nadus and Taxes
[2:0] # Structure
[2:1] - **State theory:** the **Saptanga** idea (seven limbs); king autocratic, succession to the eldest son; official language **Sanskrit**.
[2:2] - **Ashtadasa Tirthas** = **eighteen administrative offices**.
[2:3] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [2:3.0] Tirthulu | Council of ministers; Samaharta = its chief
  [2:3.1] Vishaya / Mandala | Province under a Vishayapati / Mandaleswara
  [2:3.2] Kottam | Another territorial subdivision
  [2:3.3] Grameyaka / Gramani / Urgavunda | Village head
  [2:3.4] Rattodi (Rattadi) | Chief village officer
  [2:3.5] Vanneyus (Manneyus) | Toll collectors
  [2:3.6] Sthanadhipati | Officer managing temples and their endowments
  [2:3.7] Pradvivaka | King's adviser on judicial matters; verdicts = jayapatras with the royal seal
  [2:3.8] Talari | Village watchman; paid the value of goods if the thief was not caught
[2:4] # Nadus → region

[2:6] - **Capitals:** Pithapuram → **Vengi** (Jayasimha I) → **Rajamahendravaram** (Ammaraja I).
[2:7] # Taxes
[2:8] - **Land tax:** **1/6** of the produce, called **Koru / Aripannu / Siddhaya**; tax-free land = **Sarva Kara Pariharam**.
[2:9] - **Udranga:** in epigraphy the **fixed or principal land tax** (tax on permanent tenants), paired with **uparikara** (minor taxes) – D.C. Sircar.
[2:10]   - **⚠ Settled:** "Udranga = tax on water" (old app key) has no source support; a PYQ offering only tolls / salt / water / shop tax has no correct option.

## SUB 3 | Society, Economy and Guilds
[3:0] # Society
[3:1] - **Varna order** with Brahmin privilege; Brahmins split into **Vaidikas** and **Niyogis** (administration and army).
[3:2] - **Agraharas:** tax-free villages for Brahmins, who performed **Hiranyagarbha** and **Bahusuvarna** rituals.
[3:3] - **Vaisyas = Komatis**; their guilds = **Nakarams**; Reddy, Kamma and Kapu communities grew strong.
[3:4] - **Panchanamvaru:** five artisan groups (blacksmith, goldsmith, brazier, carpenter, sculptor) appear as an organised body.
[3:5] - **Panduranga** (general of Gunaga Vijayaditya) **subdued the Boyas**, built Pandurangapuram on Pulicat lake and issued the Addanki verse inscription.
[3:6] # Guilds and trade
[3:7] - **Penugonda (West Godavari)** = **headquarters of the merchant guilds** of medieval Andhra and main seat of goddess **Vasavi** (APPSC PYQ).
[3:8]   - Bejawada was a trade centre but not the guild headquarters.
[3:9] - **Kakumanu inscription** (Chalukya Bhima I) names a **Setti Shreshthi** (chief merchant).
[3:10] TABLE head: Fact | Ruler
  [3:10.0] Gold coins found in Arakan (Burma) | Saktivarma I (Chalukya Chandra)
  [3:10.1] Trade with the eastern islands | Rajaraja Narendra
  [3:10.2] 72 merchants sent to China | Kulottunga I

## SUB 5 | Education – Ghatikas
[5:0] - **Ghatikas** = advanced **Sanskrit schools** attached to **agraharas or temples**.
[5:1] - **Heads:** **Ghatika Samanyulu**, e.g. **Mandasharma** of the **Asanapura Ghatika**, Kammanadu.
[5:2] - **Subjects:** Vedas, Vedangas, Dharmashastras, Tarka, Vyakarana, Arthashastra, **Dhanurveda**, **Ayurveda**.
[5:3] - **⚠ Trap:** **Hallisaka** (a dance) was **not** a Ghatika subject.
[5:4] - **Jain pupils** began their studies with **"Om Namah Siddham"**.
[5:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen Eastern Chalukyas p.10, p.30; app notes 1:79 (sub 5)
