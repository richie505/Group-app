## SUB 0 | Palnadu War (Palnati Yuddham), 1182
[0:0] - **Where and when:** **Karempudi, 1182 CE** (Rocket); epic **Palnati Veera Charitra** by **Srinatha**; also the folk **Palnati Veerula Katha**.
[0:1] - **Rivals:** all sons of the Palnadu Haihaya **Alugaraju (Anugaraju)** – Nalagamaraju's faction vs the Malideva faction.
[0:2] TABLE head: Person | Role
  [0:2.0] Nalagamaraju | Son of Mailama Devi, daughter of Velanati Gonka II
  [0:2.1] Malideva and brothers | Half-brothers – rival faction, led by Brahmanaidu
  [0:2.2] Nayakuralu Nagamma | Nalagama's minister; Shaivite; built the Veerabhadra temple, Gurajala; her intrigue exiled Malideva and Brahmanaidu
  [0:2.3] Brahmanaidu | Minister of Malideva; Vira Vaishnava reformer
  [0:2.4] Balachandra | Brahmanaidu's son; hero killed in the war
[0:3] - **Cause of the war:** **Nagamma** – the rivalry of Nayakuralu Nagamma and Brahmanaidu (APPSC PYQ); Balachandra was a hero, not the cause.
[0:4] - **Brahmanaidu's faith:** follower of **Ramanujacharya** (Vira Vaishnavism) (APPSC PYQ).
[0:5] - **Dalit temple entry:** **Brahma Naidu** opened the **Chennakesava temple, Macherla** to all castes, including Dalits (APPSC PYQ).
[0:6] - **Chapa Koodu / Dasa Samaj:** his inter-caste dining; his followers became the **Maladasari** (dasa) community.
[0:7] - **Karempudi angle (G2 PYQ):** the **Velamas**, "intruders" into the Reddi-dominated economic life of Palnadu, were driven out in the **Battle of Karempudi**.
[0:8] - **Allies:** **Kota Bhimaraju II** joined Kakatiya **Rudradeva** to back Nalagamaraju.
[0:9] ~~ Sources: Rocket AP History sheet #12, #19; NextGen Kakatiyas (Rudradeva backed Nalagamaraju); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Palnadu; app notes 1:83 (sub 4)
## SUB 1 | Velanati Chodas
[1:0] - **Seat:** **Velanadu** (Bapatla, Tenali, Repalle, Guntur); capital **Chandavolu (Dhanadapuram)**.
[1:1] - **Line:** progenitor **Mallavarma**; founder **Gonka I** (Samasta Senadhipati of Vengi under the Chalukya-Cholas); greatest **Gonka II** (1132-1161).
[1:2] - **Origin:** not Telugu Chodas – ancestors were the **Kirtipura lords of Madhyadesha**; they named sons after their Chalukya-Chola overlords (first: **Rajendra Choda**).
[1:3] - **Pithapuram inscription** of Velanati **Prithvisvara** names **Kolleru Lake** (G2 Mains 2017; G2 PYQ) – not Chilka, Pulicat or Pakala.
[1:4] - **End:** Kakatiya **Ganapatideva** (1201 campaign) killed **Prithvisvara**, with Nellore Choda Tikkana's help, and later the last member **Mummadi Rajendra Choda**.
[1:5] # Marriage alliances
[1:6] - **Mailama (Mailamba)**, daughter of **Gonka II**, was married to the Palnadu Haihaya **Anugaraju**, who ruled Palnadu from **Gurajala** with Velanati support.
[1:7] - The **Kondapadumatis** were also tied to Velanadu by marriage.
[1:8] > Statement angle (NTR Univ. JA): "Rajendra Choda, son of Gonka II, married Mailamba, daughter of Gonkaraja" ✗ (Mailamba was his sister, married to Anugaraju); "Anama, sister of Buddharaja, married the Haihaya chief of Palnadu" – not in the sources → key "neither A nor B".
[1:9] ~~ Sources: NextGen Kakatiyas (Ganapati's Velanati campaign, Prithvisvara); NextGen AP History Previous Papers p.8; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Palnadu; app notes 1:83 (sub 1, 2)
## SUB 2 | Kulottunga I and the Chalukya-Chola Viceroys of Vengi
[2:0] - **Kulottunga I (Rajendra Chalukya)**, son of Rajaraja Narendra and Ammanga Devi, = **first Chalukya-Chola king**: Chola throne **1070**, Vengi **1075** after the death of **Vijayaditya VII**, the last Eastern Chalukya → he **united the Chola and Eastern Chalukya crowns** (G1 1983-84).
[2:1] - **Married** **Madhurantaki**, daughter of the Chola **Rajendra**.
[2:2] - **Deeds:** sent **72 merchants to China**; built **Kulottunga Cholapuram** (Visakhapatnam); conquered **Kalinga by sea** → **Kalingattuparani** by **Jayangondar**.
[2:3] # Viceroys
[2:4] - **Titles taken:** the Eastern Chalukya **Sarvalokasraya, Sri Vishnuvardhana**; coronation at **Jananathapura (Draksharama)**, the provincial capital of Vengi.
[2:5] - **Viceroy crowned at Draksharama:** **Rajaraja Chodaganga** (G2 Mains 2025 key).
[2:6]   - The Chellur plates show Kulottunga's son **Vira Choda** also styled Vishnuvardhana; he is not an option.
[2:7] - **Vikrama Chola** served after Rajaraja Chodaganga and was recalled in **1118** as his father's **co-regent**; the line ended with **Parantaka Chola**.
[2:8] - **After Kulottunga:** Andhra became a web of **nadus** under vassals who accepted **Velanadu**, later **Kakatiya**, overlordship; he raised Velanati, Nellore, Konidena and Pottapi Chodas, Chagis and Kona Haihayas as Mandaleswaras.
[2:9] ~~ Sources: NextGen Eastern Chalukyas p.21-22; NextGen AP History Previous Papers p.46; Rocket AP History sheet #1; SII Vol 1, Chellur plates of Vira Choda (https://www.whatisindia.com/inscriptions/south_indian_inscriptions/volume_1/vira_choda.html); app notes 1:83 (sub 0)
