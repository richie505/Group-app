=== Palnadu War (Palnati Yuddham), 1182
- **Where and when:** **Karempudi, 1182 CE** (Rocket); epic **Palnati Veera Charitra** by **Srinatha**; also the folk **Palnati Veerula Katha**.
- **Rivals:** all sons of the Palnadu Haihaya **Alugaraju (Anugaraju)** – Nalagamaraju's faction vs the Malideva faction.
| Person | Role |
|---|---|
| Nalagamaraju | Son of **Mailama Devi**, daughter of Velanati **Gonka II** |
| Malideva and brothers | Half-brothers – rival faction, led by Brahmanaidu |
| **Nayakuralu Nagamma** | Nalagama's minister; Shaivite; built the Veerabhadra temple, Gurajala; her intrigue exiled Malideva and Brahmanaidu |
| **Brahmanaidu** | Minister of Malideva; Vira Vaishnava reformer |
| Balachandra | Brahmanaidu's son; hero killed in the war |
- **Cause of the war:** **Nagamma** – the rivalry of Nayakuralu Nagamma and Brahmanaidu (APPSC PYQ); Balachandra was a hero, not the cause.
- **Brahmanaidu's faith:** follower of **Ramanujacharya** (Vira Vaishnavism) (APPSC PYQ).
- **Dalit temple entry:** **Brahma Naidu** opened the **Chennakesava temple, Macherla** to all castes, including Dalits (APPSC PYQ).
- **Chapa Koodu / Dasa Samaj:** his inter-caste dining; his followers became the **Maladasari** (dasa) community.
- **Karempudi angle (G2 PYQ):** the **Velamas**, "intruders" into the Reddi-dominated economic life of Palnadu, were driven out in the **Battle of Karempudi**.
- **Allies:** **Kota Bhimaraju II** joined Kakatiya **Rudradeva** to back Nalagamaraju.

=== Velanati Chodas
- **Seat:** **Velanadu** (Bapatla, Tenali, Repalle, Guntur); capital **Chandavolu (Dhanadapuram)**.
- **Line:** progenitor **Mallavarma**; founder **Gonka I** (Samasta Senadhipati of Vengi under the Chalukya-Cholas); greatest **Gonka II** (1132-1161).
- **Origin:** not Telugu Chodas – ancestors were the **Kirtipura lords of Madhyadesha**; they named sons after their Chalukya-Chola overlords (first: **Rajendra Choda**).
- **Pithapuram inscription** of Velanati **Prithvisvara** names **Kolleru Lake** (G2 Mains 2017; G2 PYQ) – not Chilka, Pulicat or Pakala.
- **End:** Kakatiya **Ganapatideva** (1201 campaign) killed **Prithvisvara**, with Nellore Choda Tikkana's help, and later the last member **Mummadi Rajendra Choda**.
# Marriage alliances
- **Mailama (Mailamba)**, daughter of **Gonka II**, was married to the Palnadu Haihaya **Anugaraju**, who ruled Palnadu from **Gurajala** with Velanati support.
- The **Kondapadumatis** were also tied to Velanadu by marriage.
> Statement angle (NTR Univ. JA): "Rajendra Choda, son of Gonka II, married Mailamba, daughter of Gonkaraja" ✗ (Mailamba was his sister, married to Anugaraju); "Anama, sister of Buddharaja, married the Haihaya chief of Palnadu" – not in the sources → key "neither A nor B".

=== Kulottunga I and the Chalukya-Chola Viceroys of Vengi
- **Kulottunga I (Rajendra Chalukya)**, son of Rajaraja Narendra and Ammanga Devi, = **first Chalukya-Chola king**: Chola throne **1070**, Vengi **1075** after the death of **Vijayaditya VII**, the last Eastern Chalukya → he **united the Chola and Eastern Chalukya crowns** (G1 1983-84).
- **Married** **Madhurantaki**, daughter of the Chola **Rajendra**.
- **Deeds:** sent **72 merchants to China**; built **Kulottunga Cholapuram** (Visakhapatnam); conquered **Kalinga by sea** → **Kalingattuparani** by **Jayangondar**.
# Viceroys
- **Titles taken:** the Eastern Chalukya **Sarvalokasraya, Sri Vishnuvardhana**; coronation at **Jananathapura (Draksharama)**, the provincial capital of Vengi.
- **Viceroy crowned at Draksharama:** **Rajaraja Chodaganga** (G2 Mains 2025 key).
  - The Chellur plates show Kulottunga's son **Vira Choda** also styled Vishnuvardhana; he is not an option.
- **Vikrama Chola** served after Rajaraja Chodaganga and was recalled in **1118** as his father's **co-regent**; the line ended with **Parantaka Chola**.
- **After Kulottunga:** Andhra became a web of **nadus** under vassals who accepted **Velanadu**, later **Kakatiya**, overlordship; he raised Velanati, Nellore, Konidena and Pottapi Chodas, Chagis and Kona Haihayas as Mandaleswaras.

|---|---|---|---|
| Kona Haihayas | Konaseema | **Rakuduru** | Progenitor Kirti Varma |
| Palnadu Haihayas | Palnadu | **Gurajala** | Claimed descent from **Kartavirya Arjuna**; founder Betaraju I (Satrasala inscription) |
| Gona Haihayas | South Telangana | **Vardhamanapuram** | – |
| Kota kings | Kammanadu (Amaravati to Tripurantakam) | **Dhanyakatakam** | Founder Betaraju I; Bhimaraju I (1108-27) Kalinga campaigns |
| Chagis | Natavadi and Vijayavati vishayas | **Gudimetta** (also Vinukonda) | Overlords: Chalukya-Cholas → Kakatiyas → Gajapatis |
| Matsyas of Oddadi | Upland Srikakulam–Vizianagaram | **Oddadi** | Claimed descent from a **fish**; emblem **double fish**; founder Satyamartanda married Prabhavati, daughter of **Jayatsena of Utkala** |
| Bana kings | Rayalaseema | **Parivipuram (Hindupur)** | Descent from Bali |
| **Vatsavayis** | Peddapuram | **Peddapuram** | Ruled Peddapuram for about three centuries (APPSC PYQ) |
| **Pusapatis** | Vizianagaram | **Vizianagaram** | Gajapati Vijayarama |
- **Kota overlords:** **Chalukya-Cholas → Velanati Chodas → Kakatiyas**; a Kota chief married **Ganapamba**, daughter of Ganapatideva.
- **Melambika**, Ganapatideva's sister, married into the **Chagi** family.
- **Matsyas:** ruled c. 1200–1470; replaced by the **Pusapati** and **Madgole** zamindaris (Madgole claim Matsya descent).
- **Pusapatis:** Raja **Vijayarama Raju** was killed fighting the East India Company at **Padmanabham (10 July 1794)**; Vizianagaram became a zamindari under the **Permanent Settlement (1802)**.
- **Taruvariyur inscription** praises Nellore Choda **Manumasiddhi I** as a thunderbolt to his enemies.
