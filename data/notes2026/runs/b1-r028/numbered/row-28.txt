## SUB 0 | Places of the Buddha's Life – Lumbini, Bodh Gaya, Sarnath, Kushinagar
[0:0] TABLE head: Event | Place | Today / marker
  [0:0.0] Birth | Lumbini grove (near Kapilavastu) | Rumindei / Rummindei, Nepal; Ashoka's inscribed pillar records "here the Buddha Shakyamuni was born"
  [0:0.1] Great Renunciation | Kapilavastu | –
  [0:0.2] Enlightenment | Uruvela = Bodh Gaya, under the Bodhi (pipal) tree, on the Niranjana (Lilajan) | Bihar; Mahabodhi Temple
  [0:0.3] First sermon | Deer Park (Mrigadava), Sarnath = Rishipatana, near Varanasi | UP; Ashoka's Lion Capital (State Emblem)
  [0:0.4] Mahaparinirvana (death) | Kusinara / Kushinagar (Kasia), capital of the Mallas | Deoria–Kushinagar district, UP; Mahaparinirvana Temple
[0:1] - **Match (APPSC ASO, CDS 2011):** birth – **Lumbini**, enlightenment – **Bodh Gaya**, first sermon – **Sarnath**, death – **Kushinagar**; Rajgriha is the spare distractor.
[0:2] - **Kushinagar:** the **Malla republic**, not the Lichchhavis or Sakyas; the Buddha died there at **80** under sal trees.
[0:3] - **Mahaparinirvana Temple, Kushinagar:** reclining dying Buddha of **Chunar sandstone**, unearthed **1876–77**.
[0:4] - **Niranjana river:** only **Bodh Gaya** of the sacred places stands on it (not Kushinagar, Lumbini, Rishipatana).
[0:5] - **Rummindei pillar** marks the **birth** (Ashoka visited in his 20th regnal year); the king whose inscription names Lumbini = **Ashoka**.
[0:6] - **"Birthplace of Buddhism":** APPSC G2 2011 calls **Sarnath** the "birthplace of Buddhism" – the **Dharma** and **Sangha** began there with the first sermon; Lumbini is only the Buddha's birthplace.
[0:7] - **Ashtamahasthanas** (eight great sites): **7 in India**, Lumbini (Nepal) the eighth.
[0:8] # Bodh Gaya facts
[0:9] - **Mahabodhi Temple:** UNESCO World Heritage Site since **2002**.
[0:10] - Original Bodhi tree destroyed by **Shashanka** (7th c., per **Hiuen Tsang**; earlier Pushyamitra Sunga by legend); the present tree was planted under **Alexander Cunningham** (1881) and is called the **fifth generation** (Bihar Tourism) – some count it the sixth, so BPSC dropped the question.
[0:11] - **Maha Bodhi Society (1891):** **Anagarika Dharmapala**, to restore Bodh Gaya to Buddhists.
[0:12] > Lumbini = birth; Bodh Gaya = enlightenment (Niranjana); Sarnath = first sermon; Kushinagar = death (Mallas).

## SUB 2 | First Sermon – Dhammachakkappavattana
[2:0] - **Name:** **Dhammachakra Pravartana / Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta** = "setting in motion the **Wheel of Dharma**" – the **first sermon at Sarnath** (not his "sight", norms or rituals).
[2:1] - **Theme:** the **Four Noble Truths** and the **Eightfold Path** – so the Eightfold Path "forms the theme of" the **Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta** (UPSC 1998), not the Dipavamsa, Divyavadana or Mahaparinibbana Sutta.
[2:2]   - **Mahaparinibbana Sutta** = his last journey and death; **Brahmajala Sutta** = first sutta of the Digha Nikaya.
[2:3] - **Audience:** the **five former companions** – **Kondanna (Kaundinya), Bhaddiya, Vappa, Mahanama, Assaji (Ashvajit)** – who became **arhats**.
[2:4] - **Vesak / Buddha Purnima** (Vaisakha full moon) marks **birth, enlightenment and Mahaparinirvana** – **not the first sermon**.
[2:5] > Dhammachakra = first sermon = Sarnath = Four Truths + Eightfold Path.
[2:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 1 p.63; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.43; PMF Art & Culture Part 5 p.74; old app notes row 28 (sub 3)
## SUB 3 | Core Teachings – Nirvana, Karma, Anatta, Middle Path
[3:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [3:0.0] Nirvana (nibbana) | Main goal; "extinction of the flame of desire"; end of suffering and of rebirth
  [3:0.1] Middle Path (Madhyama Marga) | Avoids both self-indulgence and self-mortification – the path to nirvana, not the goal
  [3:0.2] Anicca | Impermanence – "the world is unstable and transient"
  [3:0.3] Anatta / Anatmavada | No permanent self (soul) – a Buddhist doctrine, not Sankhya, Vedanta or Jain
  [3:0.4] Dukkha | Suffering
  [3:0.5] Karma | Intention-based morality; present state from past actions; rebirth till nirvana
  [3:0.6] Raft simile | Teaching as a raft – upaya (skilful means), not dogma
[3:1] - **Three marks:** Anicca, Anatta, Dukkha.
[3:2] - **Beliefs (UPPSC 1992):** karma ✓, rebirth ✓, nirvana ✓; **God neither affirmed nor denied** – belief in God ✗; belief in God not needed for the Sangha.
[3:3] - **Rejected Vedic authority**, animal sacrifice and ritual; stressed **purity of conduct / a moral life** over philosophical problems (the seed of later Buddhist philosophy lies in his own teachings – CGPSC 2023: both true).
[3:4] - **Caste (UPPSC 1998):** Buddhism **did not abolish caste and class** as social facts, **challenged the Brahmins' top rank** (Kshatriyas listed first), and texts call some **jatis and crafts (hina-sippa) low** – exam key: all three true (PMF instead says the Buddha condemned varna).
[3:5] - **Statements on the Buddha:** born in (the Sakya state of) **Kapilavastu** – strictly at Lumbini near it; enlightened at **Bodh Gaya**; **discarded the Vedic religion**; preached the **noble truths** – key: all four.
[3:6] > Nirvana = goal; Middle Path = means; Anatta = no soul; God neither affirmed nor denied.
[3:7] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 1 p.65-66; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.45-46; old app notes row 28 (sub 6)
## SUB 4 | Life Story – Dates, Renunciation, Epithets and Texts
[4:0] - **Born 563 BC** (traditional; PMF "566–563 BCE"), Vaishakha Purnima; **died 483 BCE** at 80 (NextGen: c. 486 BC).
[4:1] - **Four sights:** old man, sick man, corpse, ascetic → **Mahabhinishkramana** (Great Renunciation = **departure from home**) at **29**, with charioteer **Channa** and horse **Kanthaka**.
[4:2] - **Six years** of austerities with **five ascetics**; tempted by **Mara**; **enlightenment at 35** on the 49th day of meditation.
[4:3] - **Last words:** **"appamadena sampadetha"** – "all composite things decay; **strive diligently**".
[4:4] # Epithets
[4:5] TABLE head: Epithet | Belongs to
  [4:5.0] Buddha (Awakened), Tathagata ("thus-gone"), Shakyamuni (sage of the Shakyas), Mahamuni | Gautama Buddha
  [4:5.1] "Light of Asia" | Buddha – Edwin Arnold's poem (1879, subtitle "The Great Renunciation"), based on the Lalitavistara
  [4:5.2] Jina, Kevalin, Nigantha Nataputta, Nayaputta | Mahavira
  [4:5.3] Prachhanna Buddha ("crypto-Buddhist") | Shankaracharya, not the Buddha
[4:6] - **UPSC 2024:** Shakyamuni ✓, Tathagata ✓, Nayaputta ✗ → "2 and 3 only".
[4:7] # Texts on his life
[4:8] - **Buddhacharita** (Ashvaghosha, Sanskrit) = the **life history of the Buddha**; **Jatakas** = stories of his **previous births** (in the Sutta Pitaka); **Tripitakas** = the canon.
[4:9] > 29 – 35 – 80 = Renunciation – Enlightenment – Parinirvana.
[4:10] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 1 p.63, p.66; NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 2 p.21 (Ashvaghosha); Rocket Indian History Part 1 p.40 (Buddhacharita – life of Buddha); PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.42-44; old app notes row 28 (sub 1, 5)

## SUB 6 | Sangha, Triratna and Monastic Terms
[6:0] - **Triratna** (three jewels / refuges) = **Buddha, Dhamma, Sangha** – not the Tripitaka, not Sheel-Samadhi-Sangha; not Trivarga or Trimurti.
[6:1] - **Match (APPSC G2 Mains 2025):** Jainism – **Triratna**; Buddhism – **Ashtanga Margam**; Saivism – **Linga**; Animism – **Nature worship**.
[6:2] - **Ajivika – Niyati; Charvaka – materialism; Jainism – Anekantavada; Buddhism – Ashtangika Marga.**
[6:3] # Admission
[6:4] - Open to all **above 15**, **caste no bar**; barred: **criminals, lepers, those with infectious diseases**; slaves, debtors, children needed permission.
[6:5] - **Women** admitted at **Vaishali** at the request of **Ananda** and **Mahapajapati Gotami** – the **first nun** (not Shravasti).
[6:6] - **Sanghamitta** took the Bhikkhuni Sangha to **Sri Lanka**; it lasted till the **Chola invasion of 1017 AD**.
[6:7] # Terms
[6:8] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [6:8.0] Bhikkhu / Bhikkhuni | Monk / nun living on alms
  [6:8.1] Pravrajya | Going forth (head shaved, robes)
  [6:8.2] Upasampada | Full ordination
  [6:8.3] Uposatha | Fortnightly confession and recitation of rules
  [6:8.4] Vassa | 3-month rainy-season retreat
  [6:8.5] Pavarana | Confession of offences by monks at the end of the Vassa (UPSC 2002)
  [6:8.6] Vihara / Chaitya / Stupa | Monks' residence / prayer hall / relic mound
  [6:8.7] Upasaka / Upasika | Lay follower
  [6:8.8] Donors | Anathapindika – Jetavana; Vishakha – Pubbarama; Bimbisara – Venuvana
[6:9] - **AP angle:** sage **Bavari's** disciples from the **Godavari** region brought Buddhism to **Andhra**.
[6:10] > Triratna = Buddha-Dhamma-Sangha; Pavarana = end-of-Vassa confession; nuns admitted at Vaishali.

## SUB 7 | Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path
[7:0] TABLE head: Truth | Content
  [7:0.0] 1. Dukkha | The world is full of suffering ("sabbam dukkham")
  [7:0.1] 2. Samudaya | Suffering has a cause – craving (tanha), ignorance, attachment
  [7:0.2] 3. Nirodha | Cessation of suffering = Nirvana
  [7:0.3] 4. Magga | The path to cessation = the Eightfold Path
[7:1] - **Order:** suffering → cause → cessation → path = **A-D-B-C / I-IV-II-III** (UPPSC 2006).
[7:2] - **Buddha and Mirabai** both held the **world full of sorrow** (first Noble Truth).
[7:3] # Eightfold Path (Ashtangika Marga)
[7:4] TABLE head: Group | Components
  [7:4.0] Prajna (wisdom) | Right View (samma-ditthi), Right Intention (sankappa)
  [7:4.1] Sila (ethics) | Right Speech, Right Action, Right Livelihood
  [7:4.2] Samadhi (mental discipline) | Right Effort, Right Mindfulness, Right Concentration
[7:5] - Called the **Middle Path** because it **avoids self-indulgence as well as self-mortification** (APSC 2017).

## SUB 8 | Pratityasamutpada – Dependent Origination
[8:0] - **Pratityasamutpada (Paticca-samuppada):** every phenomenon arises from a cause; a **12-link** chain (**12 nidanas**, bhavachakra); removing ignorance ends the chain.
[8:1] TABLE head: Order | Links
  [8:1.0] 1–4 | Avidya (ignorance) → Samskara (impressions) → Vijnana (consciousness) → Namarupa
  [8:1.1] 5–8 | Shadayatana (six senses) → Sparsha (contact) → Vedana (feeling) → Trishna (craving)
  [8:1.2] 9–12 | Upadana (clinging) → Bhava (becoming) → Jati (birth) → Jara-marana (old age–death)
[8:2] - **Cause of Samskara = Avidya** (CGPSC 2024) – the first link and root of suffering; Vijnana comes after Samskara.
[8:3] ~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.45; old app notes row 28 (sub 9)
