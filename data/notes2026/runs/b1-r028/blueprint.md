## 5. Source notes
- NextGen Part 1 p.63 lists the first five disciples as "Asvajit, Upali, Mogallana, Sariputra, Ananda" – wrong; the five companions of the first sermon were Kondanna, Bhaddiya, Vappa, Mahanama, Assaji (PMF Part 2 p.43). The notes use PMF.
- NextGen dates the death c. 486 BC; PMF and the exams use 483 BCE.
