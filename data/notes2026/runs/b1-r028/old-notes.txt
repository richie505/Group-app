## SUB 1 | Life of the Buddha
[28:1:0] - Born **563 BC** at **Lumbini**, Vaishakha Purnima.
[28:1:1] - **Four sights**: old man, sick man, corpse, ascetic → **Great Renunciation (Mahabhinishkramana)** at **29**.
[28:1:2] - **Six years** of austerities with **five ascetics**, later abandoned.
[28:1:3] - Tempted by **Mara**; **enlightenment at 35** (c. 528 BCE) under the **Bodhi (Pipal) tree**, **Uruvela (Bodh Gaya)**, on the **Niranjana**.
[28:1:4] - Last rainy season at **Vaishali (Beluva)**, aged 80, with Ananda; food poisoning at **Pava** after **Sukaramaddava** offered by **Chunda**.
[28:1:5] - **Mahaparinirvana** at **Kusinara (Kushinagar)**, in the **Malla republic**, aged **80**, traditionally **483 BCE**.
[28:1:6] - Last words: **"appamadena sampadetha"** – "All composite things decay, strive diligently".
[28:1:7] - Stressed a **moral life** over philosophical problems; later scholars built the philosophy.
[28:1:8] 29 – 35 – 80 = Renunciation – Enlightenment – Parinirvana

## SUB 3 | First Sermon, Mahaparinirvana and Vesak
[28:3:0] - **Dhammachakra Pravartana** ("Turning the Wheel of Dharma") at **Sarnath**, after enlightenment at Bodh Gaya.
[28:3:1] - Audience: **five former companions** – **Kondanna, Bhaddiya, Vappa, Mahanama, Assaji** – who became **Arhats**.
[28:3:2] - Content: **Four Noble Truths** and **Noble Eightfold Path**.
[28:3:3] - **Buddha Purnima / Vesak** (Vaisakha full moon): **birth, enlightenment, Mahaparinirvana** – **not the first sermon**.

## SUB 6 | Core Teachings

## SUB 9 | Pratityasamutpada
[28:9:0] - **Dependent origination**: every phenomenon arises from a cause; a **12-link** chain (**12 nidanas**).
[28:9:1] TABLE head: Order | Links
  [28:9:1.0] 1–4 | Avidya (ignorance) → Samskara → Vijnana (consciousness) → Namarupa
  [28:9:1.1] 5–8 | Shadayatana (six senses) → Sparsha (contact) → Vedana (feeling) → Trishna (craving)
  [28:9:1.2] 9–12 | Upadana (clinging) → Bhava (becoming) → Jati (birth) → Jara-marana (old age–death)
[28:9:2] Cause of Samskara = **Avidya**, root of all suffering.
