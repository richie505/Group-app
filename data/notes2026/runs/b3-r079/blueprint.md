## 5. Corrections to the old notes
- "Per APPSC key, IDBI is a bank under the control of the RBI" → the RBI owned 72.5% of NABARD until 2010; IDBI went to the Government in 1976.
- "Importance reduced most = pension funds" → development banks (the RBI says the DFIs' role diminished; ICICI and IDBI became banks).
- "Number of UCBs March 2012 = 65" → 1,618 (RBI).
- "LIC → IDBI → UTI → SEBI" → UTI (February 1964) came before IDBI (July 1964).
- NextGen gives the EXIM Bank head office as New Delhi; the head office is in **Mumbai** (the notes keep Mumbai).
