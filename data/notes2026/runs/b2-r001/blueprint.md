## 5. Key corrections and flags
- **y11eaec51034** (flashcard, which statements are incorrect): printed "None of the above" → **Only b** – the Purna Swaraj resolution was of Lahore, December 1929; 26 January **1930** was the Independence Day, not 26 January 1929.
- **ycc98f1ce6d3** (fundamental freedoms): printed 7 → **6** – the 44th Amendment (1978) deleted the right to property; tagged outdated (moved to row 10).
- **y4f70360b529** (first Finance Minister): printed Sarojini Naidu → **-1** – it was R.K. Shanmukham Chetty; no option is right (John Mathai was the second).
- **y31f4eab8b29** (committee match): printed A → **-1** – Finance and Staff Committee was chaired by Rajendra Prasad, not Nehru; no option is fully right.
- **yfa48b164117** ("argued the Constitution should be flexible"): printed H.V. Kamath → **-1** – the debates show Nehru and Brajeshwar Prasad arguing for flexibility and Kamath for safeguards against hasty amendment. **The owner should look at this one**; the source of the question was not found.
- **y088bcfb2976** (Bharat Ratna before becoming President): printed Radhakrishnan → **-1** – Abdul Kalam (1997, President 2002) also qualifies (moved to row 25).
