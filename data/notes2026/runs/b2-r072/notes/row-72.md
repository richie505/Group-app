=== Attorney General – Highest Law Officer, Appointment and Qualification (Art. 76)
# The office
- **Attorney General (AG) of India** = the **highest law officer** of the country and the **first law officer / chief legal adviser** of the **Government of India** (Art. 76).
  - Distractors: the **Chief Justice of India** heads the judiciary, the **Law Minister** looks after legal matters in the Cabinet, the **Solicitor General** is only the **second** law officer, the CAG is the auditor.
- **Part V** (The Union, Chapter I – The Executive); the AG is part of the **Union Executive** but **not a member of the Union Cabinet**.
- **Only the AG** (Union) and the **Advocate General** (State) are constitutional law officers; the **Solicitor General and Additional Solicitors General are not mentioned in Art. 76**.
# Appointment and qualification – Art. 76(1)
- Appointed by the **President** (on the advice of the Union Cabinet) – **not** by the PM, the CJI or "after consulting the Vice-President".
- Must be **qualified to be appointed a judge of the Supreme Court** – not merely a High Court judge, "any advocate" or "a retired SC judge".
| SC-judge qualification (Art. 124(3)) = AG qualification |
|---|
| **Citizen of India**, and |
| (a) a **High Court judge for 5 years**, or |
| (b) an **advocate of a High Court for 10 years**, or |
| (c) a **distinguished jurist in the opinion of the President** |
- **Advocate General** (State) needs only the **High Court-judge** qualification (Art. 165(1)).

=== Attorney General – Rights in Parliament and in the Courts
# Parliament – Art. 88
- The AG has the right to **speak and otherwise take part** in the proceedings of **both Houses**, their **joint sitting** and **any parliamentary committee of which he is named a member** – **without being an MP**.
- He **cannot vote** (UPSC 2013: "take part, be a committee member, speak" correct; "vote" wrong).
- **Art. 105(4)**: he enjoys the **privileges and immunities of an MP**.
- **Only the AG** among the listed officers has this right: **not** the Solicitor General, CJI, CEC, CAG, Finance Commission chairman, NHRC chairman or National Security Adviser.
  - Ministers also speak in both Houses (Art. 88), so "AG and SG are the only officers allowed to participate in Parliament" is **wrong** on both counts (UPSC 2022).
# Courts – Art. 76(3)
- **Right of audience in all courts in the territory of India** – not only the Supreme Court, not only High Courts or Sessions courts.
- The President has assigned him to **appear for the Government of India in all Supreme Court cases** in which it is concerned, to represent it in **Art. 143** references, and to appear in any High Court when required.

=== Attorney General – Term, Remuneration, Duties and Limitations
# Term and removal – Art. 76(4)
- **No fixed term** in the Constitution; **no grounds or procedure for removal**; holds office **during the pleasure of the President** – not the PM, not Parliament. So "the President cannot remove the AG" is **wrong**.
- **Resigns** to the **President**; **by convention** (not by the Constitution) he resigns when the Council of Ministers resigns or is replaced (UPSC 2022: "the Constitution requires him to resign with the government" = wrong).
- **Remuneration**: "such remuneration as the **President** may determine" – **not Parliament**, not fixed by the Constitution, not charged on the Consolidated Fund.
# Duties – Art. 76(2)
- 1. **Advise the Government of India** on legal matters **referred by the President**.
- 2. Perform other duties of a legal character **assigned by the President**.
- 3. Discharge functions conferred by the Constitution or any law.
- References reach him through the **Ministry of Law and Justice (Department of Legal Affairs)** – only legal matters of real importance.
- He does **not** preside over joint sittings of Parliament.
# Limitations
- Should **not advise or hold a brief against the Government of India**, nor in cases where he must advise or appear for it.
- Should **not defend accused persons** in criminal prosecutions, or become a company director, **without the permission of the Government of India**.
- **Not a full-time counsel** and **not a government servant**, so **not debarred from private practice**.

=== Advocate General of the State – Article 165
# Office and appointment
- **Highest (first) law officer of the State** and its **official legal adviser** – the State counterpart of the AG (Art. 76). Not the Government Pleader, Standing Counsel or Amicus Curiae.
- **Part VI** (The States). Appointed by the **Governor** (Art. 165(1)) – **not the President** "on the Governor's recommendation", not the CM or Law Minister (UPSC 2009: statement false).
- Qualification: **qualified to be a High Court judge** – citizen + **10 years in a judicial office** or **10 years as a High Court advocate** (no "jurist" route) – not Vidhan Sabha / Lok Sabha membership, not a magistrate.
- **Pleasure of the Governor** (Art. 165(3)) – not the CM or State Cabinet; no fixed term; by convention resigns with the State government.
- **Remuneration determined by the Governor** – not equal to an HC judge, Chief Secretary or State minister (APPSC Group-II Prelims 2017).
# Duties and rights
- **Art. 165(2)**: advise the **State Government** on legal matters referred by the Governor and perform other legal duties assigned.
- **Art. 177**: may speak and take part in **either House of the State Legislature** and its committees – **no vote**; **Art. 194(4)**: privileges of a member of the State Legislature.
- Right of audience: in courts **in the State** (the AG: all courts in India).
- **Not appointed by the Governor:** **High Court judges** (the **President** appoints them, Art. 217); the Governor does appoint the CM, the Advocate General and the SPSC chairman.
- **AP:** the Advocate General of AP is appointed by the **Governor of AP**; **Dammalapati Srinivas** was reappointed on **19 June 2024**. The Advocate General may use the **multi-colour AP State emblem** (G.O. of 14 Nov 2018).

=== Solicitor General and the Office-holders
# Solicitor General (SG) and ASGs
- **SG = second law officer** of India; **assists the AG**; is himself assisted by **Additional Solicitors General**.
- He is a **legal adviser** (tenders advice and appears for the Union) – not an administrative officer, not the President's personal law officer.
- **Statutory, not constitutional** – **Law Officers (Terms and Conditions) Rules, 1987**; appointed by the **Appointments Committee of the Cabinet**; normal term **3 years**.
- **No Art. 88 right** to take part in Parliament.
- **AG and SG** are **ex officio members of the Bar Council of India** (**Advocates Act, 1961**).
