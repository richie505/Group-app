## 4. Key changes and traps found
- **HC strength:** "Who decides the number of Judges in a High Court?" was keyed Parliament – wrong; the **President** (Art. 216); Parliament fixes the SC's strength.
- **Reservation in promotion:** printed key 85th Amendment; reservation in promotion is the **77th (1995)** – option added; the 85th only gave consequential seniority.
- **Dated persons:** the "present" AG/NCW/NHRC/NCST chair keys are right only for the exam year – all tagged ⚠ Outdated with the 2026 holder; FRO 2022 "as of July 2023 Venugopal heads…" and Jharkhand 2021 "current NHRC chairman Dattu" carry a ⚠ Disputed line (both had already left office).
- **NCBC "not constitutional"** and the **NHRC 5-year term** are pre-2018 / pre-2019 facts – keyed for the time, tagged outdated.
- Old explanation said Rekha Sharma "became NCW chairperson in July 2023" – corrected (chair Aug 2018 – Aug 2024).
