## SUB 0 | National Commission for Women (NCW)
[0:0] # Act and status
[0:1] - **Statutory**, not constitutional: **National Commission for Women Act, 1990** (in force 30 Aug 1990); Commission constituted on **31 January 1992**. Nodal ministry: **Women and Child Development**.
[0:2] - Recommended earlier by the **Committee on the Status of Women in India (1974)** and the National Perspective Plan for Women (1988).
[0:3] # Composition (Sec. 3)
[0:4] - **Chairperson + 5 members + a Member-Secretary**, all **nominated by the Central Government** (not the President, not elected).
[0:5] - Members: persons with experience in **law or legislation, trade unionism, management of an industry**, **women's voluntary organisations (incl. women activists)**, **administration, economic development, health, education or social welfare**; at least **one SC and one ST** member.
[0:6] - Member-Secretary: an expert in management, organisational structure or **sociological movement**, or a civil servant.
[0:7] - Term: **3 years** (chairperson and members).
[0:8] # Functions (Sec. 10) and powers
[0:9] - **Section 10** lists the functions: investigate safeguards for women; report to the **Central Government**; review laws and suggest amendments; **look into complaints and take suo motu notice** of deprivation of women's rights; special studies; **promotional and educational research** to ensure **due representation of women** in all spheres; inspect jails and remand homes; fund litigation.
[0:10] - **Powers of a civil court** while investigating; regulates its own procedure; forms committees and co-opts outsiders. It cannot impose or enforce criminal sentences.
[0:11] - **Mahila Jan Sunwai** = public hearing of women's grievances; **Parivarik Mahila Lok Adalat** for family disputes.

## SUB 1 | National Human Rights Commission (NHRC)
[1:0] # Status
[1:1] - **Statutory** (not constitutional) – **Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993**; set up **12 October 1993** (ordinance of 28 Sep 1993); **Paris Principles**; headquarters **New Delhi**. Contrast: State/Union Finance Commissions (Art. 243-I, 280) and the Lok Sabha Secretariat (Art. 98) are constitutional.
[1:2] # Composition and appointment
[1:3] - **Chairperson** (former **CJI or SC judge** – since 2019) + **5 full-time members** (an SC judge, an HC Chief Justice, **3** human-rights experts incl. a woman) + **7 ex officio** members (chairs of NCM, NCSC, NCST, NCW, NCBC, NCPCR, Chief Commissioner for PwD).
[1:4] - Appointed by the **President** on the recommendation of a **6-member committee**: **PM (head)**, **Speaker of the Lok Sabha**, **Deputy Chairman of the Rajya Sabha**, **Leaders of the Opposition in both Houses**, **Union Home Minister**. The **Chairman of the Rajya Sabha (Vice-President) is NOT** on it.
[1:5] - **Removal:** by the President; for misbehaviour or incapacity only after a **Supreme Court inquiry**.
[1:6] TABLE head: Term | Before the 2019 amendment | After PHR (Amendment) Act, 2019
  [1:6.0] Chairperson and members | 5 years or 70 years of age, whichever earlier | 3 years or 70 years; eligible for reappointment
[1:7] # Working
[1:8] - Recommendations **not binding**; government reports action within one month; cannot inquire into a matter **after one year**; civil-court powers.

## SUB 2 | State Human Rights Commission (SHRC)
[2:0] - Under the same **Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993**; constituted by the **State government**; inquires only into **State List and Concurrent List** matters not already before the NHRC or another statutory commission.
[2:1] - **Chairperson** (former Chief Justice **or judge** of a High Court – since 2019) **+ 2 members**.
[2:2] - **Appointed by the Governor** on the recommendation of a committee of the **Chief Minister (head)**, **Speaker**, **State Home Minister** and **Leader of the Opposition in the Assembly** (+ Council Chairman and Council LoP where a Council exists). The **Governor is not a member** of the committee; he only appoints.
[2:3] - **Removed only by the President**, not the Governor. Term **3 years or 70** (5 years before 2019).
[2:4] - **AP SHRC** functions from **Kurnool**. **Human Rights Courts** in every district (with the HC Chief Justice's concurrence).
[2:5] > Asked as: who is not on the SHRC recommending committee (Governor – MPPSC 2020).

## SUB 3 | NCPCR and the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child
[3:0] - **NCPCR**: statutory – **Commissions for Protection of Child Rights Act, 2005**; set up **2007** (Act in force 5 Feb 2007); under **Women and Child Development**; **Chairperson + 6 members** (at least 2 women); 3 years, max two terms; age limit 65 (chair) / 60 (members); State Commissions (SCPCRs) too.
[3:1] - Monitors the **RTE Act 2009** (incl. **Sec. 12(1)(c)** 25% free seats), **POCSO Act 2012** and **JJ Act 2015**.
[3:2] - "Child rights" include those in the **UNCRC**: adopted by the UN on **20 Nov 1989**, **entered into force 2 Sep 1990**, **54 articles**; **India acceded/ratified 11 Dec 1992**; a child = below **18 years**.

## SUB 4 | National Commission for Minorities and National Commission for Safai Karamcharis
[4:0] # NCM
[4:1] - **Minorities Commission** set up by an MHA **resolution, 1978**; statutory under the **NCM Act, 1992**; first statutory Commission **1993**; under the **Ministry of Minority Affairs**.
[4:2] - **Chairperson + Vice-Chairperson (added 1995) + 5 members = 7**, nominated by the **Central Government**; five incl. the chair from minority communities; 3 years.
[4:3] - **Six notified minorities:** Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Parsis (1993) and **Jains (27 Jan 2014)**.
[4:4] - **15 States** (incl. **AP**) have State Minorities Commissions.
[4:5] # NCSK
[4:6] - Constituted **1994** under the **NCSK Act, 1993**; Act lapsed **29 Feb 2004** → **non-statutory** body under the **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment** (not WCD); monitors the **Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers Act, 2013**.
[4:7] - **January 2022:** the Cabinet extended it by **three years** beyond 31 Mar 2022, to **31 March 2025**; latest extension up to **31 March 2028**.
[4:8] - Functions: recommend programmes, **study and evaluate the implementation of schemes**, **investigate complaints** of non-implementation and **take suo motu notice** – it does **not distribute relief amounts**.
[4:9] > Asked as: NCSK extension of Jan 2022 (three years – APPSC); NOT a function of NCSK (distribution of relief – APPSC Welfare Officers).
