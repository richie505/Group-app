=== IMF, World Bank Group and Multilateral Banks
# IMF
- **France** = **first borrower** from the IMF; lends mainly for **balance-of-payments** problems.
- **Rapid Financing Instrument:** quick liquidity **without a full programme** (disasters, pandemics, post-conflict).
- **Structural Adjustment Programmes (1980s-90s):** austerity, privatisation, devaluation.
- **SDR** = "**paper gold**"; basket of **five currencies** – US dollar, euro, **Chinese yuan**, yen, pound.
- India's IMF **voting share about 2.63%** (after the 2010 reforms).
- **BRICS:** about **20% of world GDP** but 11% of IMF votes in **2011**.
