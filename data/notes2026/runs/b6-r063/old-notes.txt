[63:8:9] - India's IMF **quota share about 2.75%**, **voting share about 2.63%** (after the 2010 reforms).
[63:8:10] - **BRICS**: about **20% of world GDP** but **11% of IMF votes** in **2011**; now about **40%** of GDP with barely higher vote share; the **New Delhi Declaration** seeks quota and voting reform; quota-based voting not abolished.
