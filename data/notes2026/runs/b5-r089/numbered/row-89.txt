## SUB 0 | Project Tiger – Launch, First Reserves and Tiger Reserves
[0:0] # Launch
[0:1] - **Project Tiger:** launched **1973** (Centrally Sponsored Scheme) on the advice of a **task force set up by PM Indira Gandhi**; aim = **save the tiger from extinction** by keeping viable populations in their natural habitats (core-buffer strategy).
[0:2] - **First reserve:** **Jim Corbett NP (Uttarakhand)**, under Project Tiger from **1 April 1973**; Corbett = India's first NP (1936, Hailey NP); not Bandhavgarh, Gir or Sundarbans.
[0:3] - **First nine reserves (1973):** Corbett, Kanha, Manas, Palamau, Similipal, Melghat, Bandipur, **Ranthambhore** and Sundarbans – so Ranthambhore became a tiger reserve in **1973** (1955 and 1980 are distractors; Ranthambhore NP itself dates from 1980).
[0:4] - **Launch order of projects:** Project Tiger 1973 → Crocodile project 1975 → Project Elephant 1992 → Indian Rhino Vision 2020 (2005) → Project Snow Leopard 2009 → Project Lion and Project Dolphin 2020 → Project Cheetah 2022.
[0:5] - **Five new national projects (MoEFCC, 2026):** dolphin, Asian elephant, **gharial**, **GIB**, **Olive Ridley** (not snow leopard).
[0:6] - **Tiger = National Animal of India**; the special projects in India's wildlife programme are for **tigers and elephants** (Project Tiger 1973, Project Elephant 1992).

## SUB 1 | Tiger Estimation, NTCA and Global Tiger Efforts
[1:0] # Counting tigers
[1:1] - **Pugmark technique:** old method – footprints used to **estimate the population** of tigers and other wild animals; now only an index of occurrence.
[1:2] - **Now:** **camera trapping** (stripes; **ExtractCompare** software) + **DNA from scats**; census is based on **double sampling** (ground survey + camera traps).
[1:3] - **M-STrIPES** = **Monitoring System for Tigers – Intensive Protection and Ecological Status**: software-based patrolling and monitoring system launched in tiger reserves by **NTCA and WII in 2010** → context = **maintenance of tiger reserves**; double sampling + M-STrIPES are used for **counting tigers**.
[1:4] - **All India Tiger Estimation:** every **4 years** by **NTCA with WII**; 2022 (5th cycle) = **3,682** tigers (upper limit 3,925); India has ~**70-75%** of the world's wild tigers.

[1:6] # NTCA
[1:7] - **NTCA:** statutory body under the **2006 WPA amendment** (Tiger Task Force advice); chaired by the **Union Environment Minister**; administers Project Tiger; **Sec 38V(3):** Tiger Conservation Plan for every reserve.
[1:8] - **MEE 2022:** 51 TRs assessed, **12 "Excellent"**.
[1:9] - **NTCA is also the implementing agency of Project Cheetah** (Kuno NP).
[1:10] # Global efforts
[1:11] - **Global Tiger Forum:** 1993-94, HQ **New Delhi**.
[1:12] - **Tiger Summit 2010:** **St Petersburg (Russia)**, 21-24 Nov 2010 – **TX2** = double wild tigers by **2022** (India achieved it in 2018); began **International Tiger Day, 29 July**.
[1:13] - **CA|TS** = **Conservation Assured | Tiger Standards** (2013): **14** Indian TRs accredited on Tiger Day 2021 (23 now).

## SUB 2 | Vultures and Diclofenac
[2:0] # Why vultures vanished
[2:1] - **Cause:** **diclofenac**, a **painkiller (NSAID)** given to **cattle**; vultures eating the carcasses suffer **kidney failure (visceral gout)** – not viral/bacterial infection, food scarcity or nesting loss; confirmed by **Peregrine Fund** research (May 2003).
[2:2] - **Decline first noticed:** **Keoladeo NP, Bharatpur (1998)**.
[2:3] - **Ban:** veterinary diclofenac banned **May 2006**; **aceclofenac and ketoprofen** banned for animals (2023).
[2:4] - **Meloxicam** = safe alternative; it is a **non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID)** (prednisolone, betamethasone, hydrocortisone are steroids; atropine, alum, ranitidine are not anti-inflammatories).
[2:5] - **9** vulture species in India; **4 resident ones CR**: white-rumped (white-backed), slender-billed, long-billed (Indian), red-headed.
[2:6] # Conservation
[2:7] - **Vulture Recovery Plan / Action Plan 2006**; **Action Plan for Vulture Conservation 2020-25**: **State-level vulture committees**, more breeding centres, Vulture Safe Zones.
[2:8] - **Tamil Nadu** was the first to set up a **State-Level Committee for Vulture Conservation** (GO 19 Oct 2022).
[2:9] - **Jatayu Conservation Breeding Centre, Pinjore (Haryana)** – first (2001); **Asia's first Gyps Vulture Reintroduction Programme (2016): Pinjore** – two Himalayan griffons released.
[2:10] - **SAVE** = **Saving Asia's Vultures from Extinction** (consortium; 2011 Nepal-India programme).
[2:11] - **Coringa WLS (AP)** hosts the CR **white-backed and long-billed vultures**.
[2:12] - **Aug 2026:** world's first release of captive-bred **slender-billed vultures** (5 birds, BNHS centre, **Assam**).

## SUB 3 | Ganges River Dolphin and Project Dolphin
[3:0] - **National Aquatic Animal:** **Ganges River Dolphin** (*Platanista gangetica*), declared **5 October 2009** (not crocodile, Olive Ridley, gharial, coral or jellyfish); **5 October = National Dolphin Day**.
[3:1] - **Dolphins are mammals** (cetaceans), not fish, amphibians or reptiles.
[3:2] - **Status:** IUCN **Endangered** (not Vulnerable); WPA **Schedule I**; CITES App. I; found in **India, Nepal, Bangladesh** (Ganga-Brahmaputra-Meghna, Karnaphuli-Sangu).
[3:3] - **Threats:** **dams and barrages**, pollution incl. **agricultural chemicals**, **fishing-net entanglement**, poaching for oil – **NOT** "more crocodiles".
[3:4] - **Places:** **Vikramshila Gangetic Dolphin Sanctuary – Bhagalpur, Bihar**; **National Dolphin Research Centre – Patna** (India's and Asia's first); **National Chambal Sanctuary** (MP-Rajasthan-UP) = Gangetic dolphin, **mugger** and **gharial**.
[3:5] - **Guwahati city animal:** the **Gangetic dolphin** ("Sihu"), declared mascot by Kamrup Metropolitan district (June 2016) – first Indian city animal.
[3:6] - **Project Dolphin (2020):** riverine **and marine** dolphins and other cetaceans; first national estimate **6,327** dolphins (2025).
[3:7] ~~ Sources: NextGen Environment APPSC 07 p.4; Shankar Environment Part 14 p.19-20; PMF Environment 03 p.98; https://www.downtoearth.org.in/news/wildlife-biodiversity/gangetic-dolphins-declared-as-official-mascot-of-guwahati-54304 (web search); app notes 5:89 sub 3
## SUB 4 | Project Elephant
[4:0] - **Launched February 1992** (the 1991-92 plan year) as a **Centrally Sponsored Scheme** – not 1990.
[4:1] - **Objectives:** protect elephants, habitats and **corridors**; address **man-animal conflict**; **welfare of captive (domesticated) elephants** – so "only wild elephants" is wrong.
[4:2] - **33 Elephant Reserves in 14 States**; merged with Project Tiger in 2023 ("Project Tiger and Elephant Division").
[4:3] - **MIKE** = **Monitoring the Illegal Killing of Elephants** (CITES; South Asia since 2003).
[4:4] - **Biology:** herds led by a **female** (matriarch); gestation up to **22 months**; calves till about 50s (not "40 only"); largest State population = **Karnataka** (not Kerala); tuskless males = **makhnas**.
[4:5] - **Facts:** **National Heritage Animal (2010)**; India has ~**60%** of Asian elephants; population **22,446** (Apr 2026); **Rayala ER** = AP's only elephant reserve.
[4:6] - **Human cost:** elephants and tigers together kill about **one person a day** in India (MoEF data).
[4:7] - **Chronology:** WPA 1972 → Project Tiger 1973 → Project Elephant 1992 → Biological Diversity Act 2002.
[4:8] ~~ Sources: NextGen Environment APPSC 07 p.1-2; Shankar Environment Part 14 p.6-8; PMF Environment 03 p.95-96; https://www.savetheelephants.org/news/one-human-killed-each-day-in-last-3-years-as-conflict-with-wildlife-escalates-india (web search); app notes 5:89 sub 2
## SUB 5 | Project Cheetah, IBCA and Project Lion
[5:0] # Cheetah
[5:1] - **Cheetah** = the large wild cat that became **extinct in India (declared 1952)** – only large mammal lost since Independence; Asiatic cheetah survives only in **Iran**; India brought **African** cheetahs.
[5:2] - **Project Cheetah:** **world's first intercontinental large-carnivore translocation**; restores **open forest and grassland**; site **Kuno NP (Sheopur, MP)**; implementing agency **NTCA** (not AWBI).
[5:3] - **8 Namibian cheetahs (17 Sep 2022) = 5 females + 3 males**; book "The End of a Trail – The Cheetah in India" = **Divyabhanusinh**.
[5:4] TABLE head: Batch | Source | Date

  [5:4.1] 12 | South Africa | 18 Feb 2023
  [5:4.2] 2 females | Botswana | May 2026
[5:5] - **Second site:** **Gandhi Sagar WLS (MP)** – two males moved from Kuno, April 2025.
[5:6] # International Big Cat Alliance
[5:7] - Launched **April 2023, Mysuru**, for **50 years of Project Tiger**; treaty-based (2025); **Secretariat New Delhi** (not Geneva).
[5:8] - **7 big cats:** tiger, lion, leopard, snow leopard, cheetah, jaguar, puma.
[5:9] - Open to **96 range countries** plus non-range countries.
[5:10] - **Cabinet (Feb 2024): ₹150 crore one-time support for five years (2023-24 to 2027-28)** – not two years.
[5:11] # Project Lion
[5:12] - **2020**; Asiatic lions of **Gir**; **891** lions (2025).
[5:13] - **Palpur-Kuno (MP)** was selected as the second home for **Gir lions** (SC order 2013); Gujarat did not act → Kuno went to cheetahs.
[5:14] - **Barda WLS** (Gujarat) proposed instead – criticised as **too close to Gir**.
[5:15] - **2018 Gir lion deaths:** **Canine Distemper Virus**.

## SUB 6 | Great Indian Bustard, CMS and Other Species
[6:0] # Great Indian Bustard
[6:1] - *Ardeotis nigriceps* (Godawan), Rajasthan's state bird; IUCN **Critically Endangered**; main range **Thar (Rajasthan)**; in AP at **Rollapadu**.
[6:2] - **Threat:** collision with **overhead power lines** in solar and wind zones.
[6:3] - **M.K. Ranjitsinh v. Union of India (March 2024):** modified (did not uphold unchanged) the 2021 blanket undergrounding order; recognised a **right against adverse effects of climate change** under **Arts 14 and 21**.
[6:4] - Breeding at **Sam and Ramdevra, Jaisalmer**.
[6:5] # CMS (Bonn Convention)
[6:6] - **COP13: Gandhinagar, Feb 2020**; mascot **Gibi = GIB** (not Amur falcon); **Asian elephant, GIB and Bengal florican** added to **Appendix I** (endangered migratory species) – not the Bengal tiger.
[6:7] - **COP14: Samarkand, Feb 2024**. Amur falcon stopover: **Doyang reservoir**; **Pangti** = "Falcon Capital of the World" (Nagaland).
[6:8] # Other species
[6:9] - **Snow leopard:** **Nepal** was the first country with a **climate-smart snow leopard landscape plan** (Aug 2017); Project Snow Leopard **2009**; snow leopard = national symbol of **Kyrgyzstan** (Dec 2023).
[6:10] - **Naturally found in India:** black-necked crane, flying squirrel, snow leopard – **not** the cheetah (extinct).
[6:11] - **Sloth bear** = the street-performing ("dancing") bear.
[6:12] - **Gharials:** best seen in the **Chambal River** (National Chambal Sanctuary, 1979; India's only **tri-state riverine PA**: MP, Rajasthan, UP).
[6:13] - **Kharai camel (Kutch):** **swims up to 3 km** in seawater and **grazes mangroves**; it is domesticated (statement 3 false); survives in **coastal and dry ecosystems**.
[6:14] - **Crocodile Breeding and Management Project: 1975** (FAO-UNDP); **Baula** (saltwater crocodile) at **Dangamal, Bhitarkanika**; mugger at Ramatirtha (1984).
[6:15] - **Endangered status:** Asian elephant = **Endangered**; Indian crocodiles: gharial **CR**, mugger **VU**, saltwater **LC**.
[6:16] - **Whale shark:** Gujarat protection programme since **2004** (not 2006).
[6:17] - **Indian Rhino Vision 2020:** **2005**, Assam.

## SUB 7 | Marine Turtles, Dugong and Coastal Conservation
[7:0] - **Olive Ridley:** main killer = **mechanised fishing boats/trawler nets** (hence **Turtle Excluder Devices**); **Operation Olivia** = annual **Indian Coast Guard** operation off **Odisha** – nesting season is roughly **November-May** (not August).
[7:1] - **Mass nesting (arribada):** **Rushikulya (Odisha)**; **Srikakulam and Vizianagaram beaches (AP)**; National Marine Turtle Action Plan **2021**.
[7:2] - **Dugong:** a **mammal** (vulnerable), **herbivorous** (seagrass), **Schedule I**; found only in Gulf of Mannar, Palk Bay, Gulf of Kutch and Andaman & Nicobar – **not the entire coast**; first **Dugong Conservation Reserve: Palk Bay, TN (2022)**.
[7:3] - **Hope Spots** (Mission Blue + IUCN): **Lakshadweep** and **Andaman and Nicobar**.
[7:4] - **MISHTI (2023):** mangrove restoration.
