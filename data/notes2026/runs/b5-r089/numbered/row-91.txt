## SUB 0 | Afforestation, Forestry Terms and Their Benefits
[0:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [0:0.0] Afforestation | New forest on denuded, degraded or unproductive land
  [0:0.1] Reforestation | Re-establishing forest cover in existing / depleted forest areas
  [0:0.2] Social forestry | Trees for community needs; term used by the National Commission on Agriculture (1976)
  [0:0.3] Extension forestry | Trees along roadsides, canals and railway strips
  [0:0.4] Agroforestry | Trees + crops + livestock on the same land
[0:1] - **Benefits:** **afforestation controls floods** and **checks soil erosion by running water** (roots bind soil, slow run-off) – deforestation, overgrazing, urbanisation, tilling or removing topsoil worsen them.
[0:2] - **Floods worsened by:** unscientific agriculture, disturbing natural drainage channels, colonising flood-plains and river-beds – **NOT reforestation**.
[0:3] - **Criticism of social forestry:** drifted into **commercial monocultures** (eucalyptus); fix = mixed native species.
[0:4] - **Miyawaki method:** dense, multi-layered **mini-forests of native species in urban areas**; Japanese botanist **Akira Miyawaki**, **1970s**.
[0:5] - **Seed bomb (seed ball):** a ball of **mud/clay, dung and seeds** thrown on land to grow trees.
[0:6] - **Craib's thinning:** heavy early (advance) thinning of plantations, done in a regular crop **in anticipation of suppression** (I.J. Craib, South Africa).
[0:7] - **Wild animals** help natural regeneration through **seed dispersal** (e.g. elephants, hornbills).
[0:8] - **Vanamahotsav:** **K.M. Munshi, 1950**; first week of July.
[0:9] ~~ Sources: Rocket Environment p.18; PMF Environment 06 p.90; PMF Environment 03 p.95; app notes 5:91 sub 0
## SUB 1 | Afforestation Schemes – NAP, NAEB, GIM, Nagar Van
[1:0] TABLE head: Scheme | Fact
  [1:0.0] NAEB | National Afforestation and Eco-development Board, set up by MoEF in August 1992
  [1:0.1] National Afforestation Programme (2002) | NAEB's flagship; decentralised, two-tier: Forest Development Agency (division) + JFMC (village); SFDA added later; now merged with GIM
  [1:0.2] Green India Mission | One of the 8 NAPCC missions; via JFMCs; partly National CAMPA
  [1:0.3] Nagar Van Yojana (2020) | Run by NAEB, funded by National CAMPA; 600 Nagar Vans + 400 Nagar Vatikas (2020-21 to 2026-27)
  [1:0.4] Ek Ped Maa Ke Naam | From 5 June 2024 (World Environment Day)
[1:1] - **Green India Mission goals:** increase forest/tree cover on **5 million ha** and improve the quality of forest cover on **another 5 million ha**; improve ecosystem services; raise forest-based livelihood income of about **3 million households** – "**2 million ha of non-forest land**" is NOT a goal.
[1:2] - **GIM revised 2021-30:** Aravallis, Western Ghats, Himalayas, mangroves; **CAG:** only **0.11 million ha** improved vs **1.4 million ha** target.
[1:3] - **NVY in AP:** **13** projects approved (2020-21 to 2023-24); 61 Nagar Vans sanctioned by 2024-25.
[1:4] - **Nyay Vatika (July 2025):** green space at the President's Bodyguard ground, Delhi, planted by the CJI and SC judges under the **Ek Ped Maa Ke Naam** campaign.
[1:5] - **State schemes:** **Apna Van Apna Dhan – Himachal Pradesh**; Haritha Haram – **Telangana**; Mukhyamantri Vriksharopan Protsahan Yojana – **Chhattisgarh**; AP – Great Green Wall, Nagar Van.
[1:6] - **AP State Forest Development Agency:** federates **22 FDAs**.

## SUB 2 | Joint Forest Management and People's Forest Movements
[2:0] - **JFM:** origin **Arabari, West Bengal (1971-72)**; introduced by **NFP 1988**; **MoEF guidelines 1 June 1990** – JFM started in **1990**.
[2:1] - **AP:** JFM committees = **Vana Samrakshana Samithis (VSS)** – a **government-initiated** community conservation strategy (sacred groves, Arvari Sansad and Chipko are people's initiatives); Tribal Affairs Ministry ordered withdrawal of community titles given to **1,669 VSS**.
[2:2] - **Bishnois – Khejarli (Jodhpur), Rajasthan, 1730:** **Amrita Devi** and 363 Bishnois hugged **khejri** trees to stop felling for the Maharaja's palace.
[2:3] - **Amrita Devi Bishnoi Smriti / Wildlife Protection Award:** for **protection of forests and wildlife**.
[2:4] - **Chipko (1973, Garhwal, Uttarakhand):** **forest conservation** by hugging trees – Gaura Devi, Chandi Prasad Bhatt, Sunderlal Bahuguna.

## SUB 4 | Compensatory Afforestation and CAMPA
[4:0] - **CA:** forest land diverted → **equal non-forest land**, or **twice the degraded forest land** (Central PSUs / no non-forest land); the agency pays **CA cost + NPV** before the project.
[4:1] - **CAMPA:** ad hoc by SC order (2004/2006); statutory via **Compensatory Afforestation Fund Act, 2016**: National and State Funds in the Public Accounts – **non-lapsable, interest-bearing**; split **90% States : 10% National**.
[4:2] - **National CAMPA** headed by the **Union Environment Minister**; exists at **national and State** levels; **people's participation is not mandatory** under the Act (UPSC 2019).
[4:3] - **Uses:** regeneration, wildlife, catchment treatment, village relocation, human-wildlife conflict, fires; **not** private urban housing. **₹66,000+ crore** held (2024), only **30-35%** used.
[4:4] - **FC Rules 2022:** **Accredited CA** = plantations raised **in advance** (land banks); dropped forest-dwellers' **consent** before diversion – disputed by **NCST**.
[4:5] - **Van (S.E.S.) Rules 2023:** afforestation under the **Green Credit Programme** can be swapped for CA.
[4:6] ~~ Sources: PMF Environment 06 p.90-91; Shankar Environment Part 11 p.12; app notes 5:91 sub 2
## SUB 5 | Forest Rights Act 2006
[5:0] - **Nodal ministry:** **Tribal Affairs** (not Environment); cut-off **13 December 2005**.
[5:1] - **Eligible:** forest-dwelling **STs**; **OTFDs** with **3 generations (75 years)** of residence.
[5:2] - **Rights:** individual title on cultivated land up to **4 ha**; **community forest resource** management; **ownership of minor forest produce** – FRA defines MFP to include **bamboo**, tendu, honey, canes.
[5:3] - **Gram Sabha** starts the claim process; District Level Committee decides.
[5:4] - **Critical wildlife habitat:** NP/sanctuary areas kept inviolate, defined in the FRA.
[5:5] - **AP (2022):** **2,12,650** titles (2,10,828 individual, **1,822** community), mostly in **ITDA** areas; **Koya and Kondh** oppose **Polavaram** over land rights and rehabilitation.
[5:6] > UPSC 2019: IFA amendment frees bamboo only on non-forest land (statement 1 wrong); FRA bamboo = MFP and MFP ownership (2 and 3 right).
[5:7] ~~ Sources: PMF Environment 06 p.83-85; app notes 5:91 sub 5
