## Trends
- **Dates repeat in identical form:** Project Tiger 1973, WPA 1972, FCA 1980, Project Elephant 1992 and the national aquatic animal (2009) account for about a quarter of all PYQs.
- **Vultures/diclofenac** is the single most repeated cause-effect question (8 near-identical PYQs across UPSC, UPPSC, CGPSC, APPSC). APPSC adds the angle of meloxicam as an NSAID.
- **APPSC-specific angles:** AP's largest tiger reserve (NSTR), Coringa, the AP forest diversion figures, NVY projects in AP, VSS, and Forest Range/Section Officer forestry items (Craib's thinning, seed bombs, Miyawaki, the GROW report).
- **APPSC Group I (2023-24)** asks statement questions on Project Cheetah and the IBCA, built on traps such as 5 females + 3 males, five years of funding not two, and 96 range countries.
- **Outdated or disputed:** WPA schedules (6 → 4 since 2022); the flying fox as vermin (official 2024 key vs the 2022 amendment); AWBI HQ (Chennai → Ballabhgarh, 2018); the Corbett "renaming" (never notified); Coringa's mangrove rank.
