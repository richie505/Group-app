## SUB 1 | Project Tiger and Ntca
[89:1:0] - Launched **1973** on advice of **Indira Gandhi's task force**, WWF support; began with **9 reserves (18,278 sq km)** incl. **Palamau** and **Manas**; now **58 reserves in 18 States**, 84,488 sq km (~2.56% of India) (Jul 2026).
[89:1:1] - Newest: **Madhav TR, MP (58th, March 2025)**; 57th Ratapani (Dec 2024). **Chilka** is NOT a tiger reserve.
[89:1:2] - **Largest TR: Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam (AP)**, Nallamala, core ~**5,443 sq km**. AP to receive 4 female tigers from Maharashtra and MP (Jul 2026).
[89:1:3] - **NTCA:** statutory via **2006 WPA amendment**; chaired by **Union Environment Minister** (PM chairs NBWL). Tiger Task Force also led to WCCB. **Sec 38V(3)**: Tiger Conservation Plan for every reserve.
[89:1:4] - **MEE 2022:** 51 TRs assessed, 12 "Excellent", average 78%. Other efforts: **CA|TS**, ITHCP (IUCN), IBCA.
[89:1:5] - Projects in tiger corridors need **NBWL Standing Committee** clearance; a 3rd railway line threatens the **Satpura-Melghat** corridor; tigers dispersing outside reserves.
[89:1:6] # Tiger Estimation
[89:1:7] - **All India Tiger Estimation** every **4 years** by **NTCA with WII**; camera-trap survey holds a Guinness record.
[89:1:8] - **Pugmark** method replaced by **camera trapping + ExtractCompare** (stripe patterns).
[89:1:9] - **M-STrIPES** = Monitoring System for Tigers – Intensive Protection and Ecological Status (NTCA-WII, 2010, software-based).
[89:1:10] - **2022 (5th cycle):** **3,682** tigers (upper 3,925); 6.1% annual growth; India ~**70-75%** of world's wild tigers.

[89:1:12] # Global Tiger Efforts
[89:1:13] - **Global Tiger Forum:** 1993, **New Delhi**.
[89:1:14] - **Global Tiger Summit:** **St Petersburg, 21-24 Nov 2010**; adopted **TX2** (double wild tigers by 2022; India met it early); began **International Tiger Day, 29 July**.
[89:1:15] - **CA|TS** = **Conservation Assured | Tiger Standards**; on International Tiger Day **2021**, **14** Indian tiger reserves (e.g. Kaziranga, Manas, Kanha, Panna) got CA|TS accreditation.
## SUB 2 | Project Elephant
[89:2:0] - **Feb 1992**; objectives: protect elephants, habitats and **corridors**, reduce man-elephant conflict, **welfare of captive elephants** (not only wild).
[89:2:1] - **33 Elephant Reserves in 14 States**; overlap TRs, sanctuaries, reserved forests.
[89:2:2] - **MIKE** = Monitoring the Illegal Killing of Elephants (**CITES**).
[89:2:3] - **Biology:** herds led by a **female** (matriarchal); gestation up to **22 months** (longest of any mammal); calf nursed 2-4 years; lifespan 60-70 years; only male Asian elephants have tusks (tuskless = **makhnas**).
[89:2:4] - **National Heritage Animal: 2010**; India has **60%** of Asian elephants; population **22,446** (Apr 2026).
[89:2:5] - **AP:** **Koundinya WLS** (Chittoor, borders TN) is part of **Rayala Elephant Reserve** (AP's only ER; not a Ramsar site); near Brahmagiri-Nilgiri-Eastern Ghats ER.
## SUB 3 | Gangetic Dolphin and Project Dolphin
[89:3:0] - **Ganges River Dolphin** (Platanista gangetica): **National Aquatic Animal, 5 October 2009** (not Olive Ridley, humpback dolphin or gharial; APPSC 2019). **5 October = National Dolphin Day**.
[89:3:1] - IUCN **Endangered**; **Schedule I**; CITES App. I; India, Nepal, Bangladesh (Ganga-Brahmaputra-Meghna, Karnaphuli-Sangu).
[89:3:2] - **Vikramshila Gangetic Dolphin Sanctuary: Bhagalpur, Bihar**; **National Dolphin Research Centre: Patna**.
[89:3:3] - First national survey: **6,327** dolphins; 8,500 km, 28 rivers, 8 States.
[89:3:4] - **Threats:** dams and barrages, pollution, **fishing-net bycatch**, poaching, killing for oil, boat traffic (not "more crocodiles").
[89:3:5] - Jan 2026: Dolphin Rescue Ambulance and Aqua Life Conservation Monitoring Centre (WII, Namami Gange). WB plans a community reserve for it.
## SUB 4 | Great Indian Bustard
[89:4:0] - *Ardeotis nigriceps* (**Godawan**), Rajasthan's state bird; **CR**; fewer than **150**.
[89:4:1] - Range: mainly **Thar (Rajasthan)**; also Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka; **Rollapadu (AP)**.
[89:4:2] - Main threat: **overhead power lines** in solar and wind zones.
[89:4:3] - **M.K. Ranjitsinh v. Union of India (March 2024):** modified the 2021 blanket undergrounding order, set up an expert committee; recognised a **right against adverse effects of climate change** (Arts **14 and 21**) (APPSC 2024).
[89:4:4] - Breeding at **Sam and Ramdevra, Jaisalmer**; on Species Recovery Programme and new national projects.
## SUB 5 | Vultures
[89:5:0] - **9** vulture species; **4 CR**: white-rumped, slender-billed, long-billed, red-headed.
[89:5:1] - Decline noticed at **Keoladeo, 1998**; cause **diclofenac** in cattle carcasses → kidney failure (**visceral gout**); confirmed by **Peregrine Fund** research, May 2003.
[89:5:2] - **Veterinary diclofenac banned 2006**; **aceclofenac and ketoprofen** banned for animals 2023; safe alternative **meloxicam**.
[89:5:3] - **Vulture Recovery Plan 2006**; **Action Plan for Vulture Conservation 2020-25** (extended to 2030): **state-level vulture committees**, more breeding centres, Vulture Safe Zones. **Tamil Nadu** set up its committee (Oct 2022).
[89:5:4] - First breeding centre: **Jatayu, Pinjore (Haryana)**; first portal "The Vulture Network"; **SAVE** = Saving Asia's Vultures from Extinction consortium.
[89:5:5] - **World's first** release of captive-bred **slender-billed vultures**: 5 birds, BNHS Assam centre (Aug 2026); first Nilgiris release failed (Jun 2026).
## SUB 6 | Project Cheetah, Ibca and Project Lion
[89:6:0] - **Cheetah** ("hunting leopard") declared **extinct in India in 1952**; only large mammal lost in recent history. **Asiatic cheetah** survives only in **Iran**; India brought **African** cheetahs.
[89:6:1] - Purpose: restore **open forest and grassland** (APPSC G1 2022). SC first ruled on reintroduction in 2012.
[89:6:2] - **Firsts:** world's first **intercontinental** large-carnivore translocation; the 8 Namibian cheetahs = **5 females + 3 males**.
[89:6:3] TABLE head: Batch | Source | Date
  [89:6:3.0] 8 | Namibia (not Nigeria) | 17 Sep 2022, Kuno
  [89:6:3.1] 12 | South Africa | 18 Feb 2023
  [89:6:3.2] 2 females | Botswana | May 2026
[89:6:4] - **Kuno NP** (Sheopur, MP); ~30 cheetahs incl. India-born cubs; debates on deaths and carrying capacity.
[89:6:5] - **Second site: Gandhi Sagar WLS (Mandsaur, MP)**: two males moved from Kuno, 20 April 2025.
[89:6:6] # International Big Cat Alliance
[89:6:7] - Launched **April 2023, Mysuru**, for 50 years of Project Tiger; treaty-based; **Secretariat New Delhi** (not Geneva).
[89:6:8] - **7 big cats:** tiger, lion, leopard, snow leopard, cheetah, jaguar, puma.
[89:6:9] - Open to **96 range countries** plus non-range countries (APPSC G1 2024); 27 members; first summit **New Delhi, 1-3 June 2026**.
[89:6:10] - **IBCA funding:** Cabinet approved HQ in India with **₹150 crore for five years** (2023-24 to 2027-28).
[89:6:11] # Project Lion
[89:6:12] - **2020**; conserves last free-ranging Asiatic lions (**Gir**); **891** lions.
[89:6:13] - **Palpur-Kuno** (Sheopur) first chosen as second home; SC ordered shifting **2013**; Gujarat did not act → Kuno went to cheetahs.
[89:6:14] - **Barda WLS** (Gujarat) proposed; criticised as too close to Gir.
## SUB 7 | Snow Leopard, Rhino, Crocodiles and Others
[89:7:0] - **Snow leopard:** **718** (first assessment); **national symbol of Kyrgyzstan** (Dec 2023); 12 range countries; in India found in J&K, Ladakh-Himachal-Uttarakhand belt, Sikkim, Arunachal.
[89:7:1] - Naturally found in India: Black-necked Crane, Flying Squirrel, Snow Leopard. Sloth bear = street-performing bear.
[89:7:2] - **Rhinos:** over **4,000**; Assam 2,650 (2018).
[89:7:3] - **National Chambal Sanctuary (1979):** MP-Rajasthan-UP; India's only **tri-state riverine PA**; only viable **gharial** population; SC took suo motu cognisance.
[89:7:4] - Crocodile sub-projects: **Baula at Dangamal, Bhitarkanika**; **mugger at Ramatirtha** (1984).
[89:7:5] - 10 captive-bred **Asian giant tortoises** released in **Ntangki NP, Nagaland**.
[89:7:6] - **Kharai camel (Kutch):** swims up to **3 km** in seawater, grazes mangroves; coastal and dry ecosystems.
[89:7:7] - **Whale shark:** India's largest fish (~50 ft); Gujarat protection programme since **2004** (not 2006).
## SUB 8 | Species Recovery Programme and Funds
[89:8:0] - Under **CSS-Integrated Development of Wildlife Habitats (IDWH)** for **22 CR species**: snow leopard, Asiatic lion, GIB, Gangetic dolphin, dugong, vultures, Hangul, marine turtles. **Gharial and sloth bear** recommended for inclusion (Jun 2026).
[89:8:1] - Recovery Programme for Critically Endangered Species: funded via **National CAMPA Advisory Council**, initially **5 years** (APPSC 2019).
[89:8:2] - **CAMPA:** ad hoc by SC 2006; **Compensatory Afforestation Fund Act, 2016**: **90%** to State Funds, **10%** to National Fund.
[89:8:3] - **Project HANUMAN (AP):** **3 March 2026** (World Wildlife Day), reduces human-wildlife conflict.
## SUB 9 | Migratory Species and Cms
[89:9:0] - **CMS (Bonn Convention):** 1979, under **UNEP**; India a party.
[89:9:1] - **COP13: Gandhinagar, Feb 2020**; mascot **Gibi** (a GIB, not Amur falcon); India's **Asian elephant, GIB and Bengal florican** added to **Appendix I**.
[89:9:2] - **COP14: Samarkand, Uzbekistan, Feb 2024**.
[89:9:3] - **Amur falcon:** breeds in SE Russia and N China → southern Africa; non-stop Arabian Sea crossing; stopover at **Doyang reservoir, Nagaland**; **Pangti** = "Falcon Capital of the World".

## SUB 1 | Constitutional Position
[90:1:0] - **42nd Amendment (1976)** moved forests and wildlife from **State List to Concurrent List**: **Entry 17A** = forests; **Entry 17B** = protection of wild animals and birds.
[90:1:1] - Also inserted **Art 48A** (DPSP, State) and **Art 51A(g)** (Fundamental Duty, citizen).
[90:1:2] - A **State Bill amending WPA** (e.g. Maharashtra 2026 on Sec 12, leopard relocation; proposal to move leopard Schedule I → II, called "regressive") needs **President's assent**, as it changes a central law.
## SUB 2 | Indian Forest Act 1927
[90:2:0] TABLE head: Category | Protection | Chapter
  [90:2:0.0] Reserved forest | Strictest: felling, grazing, hunting barred unless permitted | Ch II, Sec 3-27
  [90:2:0.1] Village forest | A reserved forest (not protected) assigned to a village | Ch III, Sec 28
  [90:2:0.2] Protected forest | Activities allowed unless prohibited | Ch IV, Sec 29-34
  [90:2:0.3] Unclassed | Outside both; least regulated | —
[90:2:1] - Level of protection: **Reserved > Protected > Village** forest.
[90:2:2] - **Sec 26:** acts banned in a reserved forest; trespass punishable (APPSC 2024). **Sec 35:** State may regulate non-government forests.
[90:2:3] - **2017 amendment** (ordinance 2017, Act 2018): **bamboo grown outside forests** removed from the definition of "tree" (**Sec 2(7)**); felled and moved without transit permit; benefits tribals and farmers.
