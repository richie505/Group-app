=== Afforestation, Forestry Terms and Their Benefits
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Afforestation | New forest on denuded, degraded or unproductive land |
| Reforestation | Re-establishing forest cover in existing / depleted forest areas |
| Social forestry | Trees for community needs; term used by the **National Commission on Agriculture (1976)** |
| Extension forestry | Trees along **roadsides, canals and railway strips** |
| Agroforestry | **Trees + crops + livestock** on the same land |
- **Benefits:** **afforestation controls floods** and **checks soil erosion by running water** (roots bind soil, slow run-off) – deforestation, overgrazing, urbanisation, tilling or removing topsoil worsen them.
- **Floods worsened by:** unscientific agriculture, disturbing natural drainage channels, colonising flood-plains and river-beds – **NOT reforestation**.
- **Criticism of social forestry:** drifted into **commercial monocultures** (eucalyptus); fix = mixed native species.
- **Miyawaki method:** dense, multi-layered **mini-forests of native species in urban areas**; Japanese botanist **Akira Miyawaki**, **1970s**.
- **Seed bomb (seed ball):** a ball of **mud/clay, dung and seeds** thrown on land to grow trees.
- **Craib's thinning:** heavy early (advance) thinning of plantations, done in a regular crop **in anticipation of suppression** (I.J. Craib, South Africa).
- **Wild animals** help natural regeneration through **seed dispersal** (e.g. elephants, hornbills).
- **Vanamahotsav:** **K.M. Munshi, 1950**; first week of July.

=== Afforestation Schemes – NAP, NAEB, GIM, Nagar Van
| Scheme | Fact |
|---|---|
| NAEB | **National Afforestation and Eco-development Board**, set up by MoEF in **August 1992** |
| National Afforestation Programme (2002) | NAEB's flagship; **decentralised, two-tier**: Forest Development Agency (division) + **JFMC** (village); SFDA added later; now merged with GIM |
| Green India Mission | One of the **8 NAPCC missions**; via JFMCs; partly National CAMPA |
| Nagar Van Yojana (2020) | Run by **NAEB**, funded by **National CAMPA**; **600 Nagar Vans + 400 Nagar Vatikas** (2020-21 to 2026-27) |
| Ek Ped Maa Ke Naam | From **5 June 2024** (World Environment Day) |
- **Green India Mission goals:** increase forest/tree cover on **5 million ha** and improve the quality of forest cover on **another 5 million ha**; improve ecosystem services; raise forest-based livelihood income of about **3 million households** – "**2 million ha of non-forest land**" is NOT a goal.
- **GIM revised 2021-30:** Aravallis, Western Ghats, Himalayas, mangroves; **CAG:** only **0.11 million ha** improved vs **1.4 million ha** target.
- **NVY in AP:** **13** projects approved (2020-21 to 2023-24); 61 Nagar Vans sanctioned by 2024-25.
- **Nyay Vatika (July 2025):** green space at the President's Bodyguard ground, Delhi, planted by the CJI and SC judges under the **Ek Ped Maa Ke Naam** campaign.
- **State schemes:** **Apna Van Apna Dhan – Himachal Pradesh**; Haritha Haram – **Telangana**; Mukhyamantri Vriksharopan Protsahan Yojana – **Chhattisgarh**; AP – Great Green Wall, Nagar Van.
- **AP State Forest Development Agency:** federates **22 FDAs**.

=== Joint Forest Management and People's Forest Movements
- **JFM:** origin **Arabari, West Bengal (1971-72)**; introduced by **NFP 1988**; **MoEF guidelines 1 June 1990** – JFM started in **1990**.
- **AP:** JFM committees = **Vana Samrakshana Samithis (VSS)** – a **government-initiated** community conservation strategy (sacred groves, Arvari Sansad and Chipko are people's initiatives); Tribal Affairs Ministry ordered withdrawal of community titles given to **1,669 VSS**.
- **Bishnois – Khejarli (Jodhpur), Rajasthan, 1730:** **Amrita Devi** and 363 Bishnois hugged **khejri** trees to stop felling for the Maharaja's palace.
- **Amrita Devi Bishnoi Smriti / Wildlife Protection Award:** for **protection of forests and wildlife**.
- **Chipko (1973, Garhwal, Uttarakhand):** **forest conservation** by hugging trees – Gaura Devi, Chandi Prasad Bhatt, Sunderlal Bahuguna.

=== Compensatory Afforestation and CAMPA
- **CA:** forest land diverted → **equal non-forest land**, or **twice the degraded forest land** (Central PSUs / no non-forest land); the agency pays **CA cost + NPV** before the project.
- **CAMPA:** ad hoc by SC order (2004/2006); statutory via **Compensatory Afforestation Fund Act, 2016**: National and State Funds in the Public Accounts – **non-lapsable, interest-bearing**; split **90% States : 10% National**.
- **National CAMPA** headed by the **Union Environment Minister**; exists at **national and State** levels; **people's participation is not mandatory** under the Act (UPSC 2019).
- **Uses:** regeneration, wildlife, catchment treatment, village relocation, human-wildlife conflict, fires; **not** private urban housing. **₹66,000+ crore** held (2024), only **30-35%** used.
- **FC Rules 2022:** **Accredited CA** = plantations raised **in advance** (land banks); dropped forest-dwellers' **consent** before diversion – disputed by **NCST**.
- **Van (S.E.S.) Rules 2023:** afforestation under the **Green Credit Programme** can be swapped for CA.

=== Forest Rights Act 2006
- **Nodal ministry:** **Tribal Affairs** (not Environment); cut-off **13 December 2005**.
- **Eligible:** forest-dwelling **STs**; **OTFDs** with **3 generations (75 years)** of residence.
- **Rights:** individual title on cultivated land up to **4 ha**; **community forest resource** management; **ownership of minor forest produce** – FRA defines MFP to include **bamboo**, tendu, honey, canes.
- **Gram Sabha** starts the claim process; District Level Committee decides.
- **Critical wildlife habitat:** NP/sanctuary areas kept inviolate, defined in the FRA.
- **AP (2022):** **2,12,650** titles (2,10,828 individual, **1,822** community), mostly in **ITDA** areas; **Koya and Kondh** oppose **Polavaram** over land rights and rehabilitation.
> UPSC 2019: IFA amendment frees bamboo only on non-forest land (statement 1 wrong); FRA bamboo = MFP and MFP ownership (2 and 3 right).
