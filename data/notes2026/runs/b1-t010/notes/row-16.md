=== Vedic Assemblies – Sabha, Samiti, Vidatha, Gana
| Assembly | Nature | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Sabha** | Small, **select council of elders/nobles**; advised the Rajan and had **judicial** functions | Mentioned **8 times** in the Rigveda; women members **Sabhavati**; "narishta" in the Atharvaveda |
| **Samiti** | General **tribal assembly** of the people | Tribal business, legislation, **choice of the chief** |
| **Vidatha** | Oldest assembly – religious, military and distributive | **Most mentioned** (122 times); **first to disappear** in Later Vedic times |
| **Gana** | Tribal group / troop | Also a military unit – not a judicial body or a priest |
- **Daughters of Prajapati:** the **Atharvaveda** calls the **Sabha and Samiti** the two daughters of Prajapati – where they appear as separate institutions (UPPCS 2009; MPPSC 2024).
- **Judicial assembly:** **Sabha** (JPSC 2016).
- **RAS 1994:** the Sabha = a select council (closest: "**council of ministers**"); the "assembly of all the people" is the **Samiti**.
- **Women:** attended the **Sabha and Vidatha**; the Atharvaveda mentions women in the **Samiti**; in the Later Vedic age women were excluded and the Vidatha vanished, while the Sabha and Samiti came under chiefs and nobles.
- **Democratic element:** traces of election of the chief by the Samiti.

=== Nature of the Polity – Rajan and Tribal Chiefdom
- **Tribal polity:** kinship-based **tribal chiefdom** (APPSC SQT 2018) – no formal bureaucracy, **no fixed territory**, no standing army.
- **Hierarchy:** **Jana** (tribe; 275 mentions) → **Vis** (clan, under **Vispati**) → **Grama** (under **Gramani**) → **Kula** (family, under **Kulapa**); **janapada** is absent from the Rigveda.
- **Rajan:** called **Gopati** (lord of cattle) / gopajanasya; protector of the tribe and cattle, **not ruler of a territory**; queen = **Mahisi**.
- **Succession:** **monarchy**; the post tended to be **hereditary** (with traces of choice by the Samiti) → "**descended (hereditary) monarchy**" (RAS 1993).
- **Divinity:** kingship not divine – only the Puru king **Trasadasyu** is called **ardhadeva** (demi-god).
- **Later Vedic contrast:** territorial kingship, **Rajasuya**, **ratnins** and regular taxes.

=== Officials, Army, Warfare and Bali
| Official | Role |
|---|---|
| **Purohita** | Chief priest and adviser (Vasishtha, Vishvamitra) |
| **Senani** | Army commander |
| **Vrajapati** | Officer in charge of **pasture land** (SI 2023) |
| **Gramani** | Head of the village / fighting band |
| **Kulapa** | Head of the family |
| **Spasa** | Spy |
| **Duta** | Messenger |
| **Jivagribha, Ugra** | Police officials |
| **Madhyamasi** | Mediator |
- **Army:** temporary tribal militia (**Sena**) in units **Sardha, Vrata and Gana**; foot soldiers (patti) and chariot-warriors (rathins); **bronze** weapons, **no iron**.
- **Gavishti** = "search for cows" – the word for war.
- **Bali:** a **voluntary offering** to the Rajan, not a regular tax.
