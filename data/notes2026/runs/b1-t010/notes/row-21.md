=== Four Vedas – Content, Priests, Trayi
| Veda | Content | Priest | Upaveda |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rigveda** | Hymns (prayers); **oldest / earliest** | **Hotri (Hota)** | Ayurveda |
| **Yajurveda** | Sacrificial formulas; **rites and rituals of yajnas** (verse + prose) | **Adhvaryu** | Dhanurveda |
| **Samaveda** | Rigvedic hymns set to melody; **shortest**; origin of Indian music | **Udgatri** | Gandharvaveda |
| **Atharvaveda** | **Magical charms, spells**, healing; **latest** | **Brahman** | Shilpaveda (architecture) / Arthashastra |
- **Samaveda** is **compiled from the Rigveda**: about 1,549 verses (1,875 with repeats), all but about **75** from the Rigveda (mostly Mandalas VIII and IX).
- **Atharvaveda**: earliest charms and spells; **Ayurveda ("Science of Life") first appears** here (its prime origin; PMF lists Ayurveda as Upaveda of the Rigveda); term **kshetra-patni ("mistress of the field")** originates here (UPSC 2026); Sabha and Samiti = "twin daughters of **Prajapati**"; mentions Pashupati; recensions **Paippalada, Shaunaka**; reflects non-Aryan beliefs.
- **Trayi / Vedatrayi** = **Rig, Yajur, Sama**; Atharvaveda excluded (recognised later).
- **Yajurveda** has two branches, **Krishna (Black)** and **Shukla (White)**; first mentions Rajasuya and Vajapeya.
- **Upavedas:** Ayurveda – medicine; Dhanurveda – warfare; Gandharvaveda – music; **Shilpaveda – architecture** (not astronomy).
- Vedic people were skilled in **astronomy** (Vedanga Jyotisha), **mathematics** (Sulbasutra geometry) and **medicine** (Atharvaveda) – **not chemistry**.
> Hotri-Adhvaryu-Udgatri-Brahman = Rig-Yajur-Sama-Atharva; Trayi excludes Atharva.

=== Layers of Vedic Literature – Shruti
- Order: **Samhitas → Brahmanas → Aranyakas → Upanishads**; Vedangas, Sutras, Smritis come later.
- All are **Shruti** ("heard"), **apaurusheya** (not of human origin); Smriti = remembered (Dharmashastras, epics, Puranas).
- **Samhitas + Brahmanas = Karma-kanda**; **Aranyakas + Upanishads = Jnana-kanda**.
- **Brahmanas**: prose commentaries that **explain the Vedic hymns** and the meaning of sacrifices (karma-marga; "liturgies").
- **Aranyakas**: "**forest texts**", concluding parts of the Brahmanas; symbolic / secret meaning of ritual; bridge karma-marga → jnana-marga. **Atharvaveda has no Aranyaka**.
- **Veda Vyasa (Krishna Dvaipayana)** compiled and classified the Vedas. Veda from *vid* = to know.

=== Rigveda – Mandalas, Hymns, Gayatri
- **10 mandalas, 1,028 hymns (suktas)** = 1,017 + 11 Valakhilya; 10,552 mantras; a **collection of hymns** to nature deities; similar to the Iranian **Avesta**; "First Testament of Mankind".
| Mandala | Content |
|---|---|
| II–VII | **Family books**, oldest core (Gritsamada, Vishvamitra, Vamadeva, Atri, Bharadvaja, Vasishtha) |
| **III** | **Gayatri Mantra** – composed by **Vishvamitra**, deity **Savitri** |
| VII | Battle of Ten Kings (Vasishtha) |
| **IX** | Wholly **Soma** (Soma Pavamana, 114 hymns) |
| **X** | **Purusha Sukta** (four varnas, origin of caste – first use of "varna" for the four orders), **Nasadiya Sukta** (creation), **Surya Sukta** (marriage, X.85) |
- Word **gotra** first occurs in the **Rigveda**, meaning cow shelter / herd of cows.
- **Memory of the World (2007):** **30 Rigveda manuscripts** at the **Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute, Pune** on UNESCO's register; Vedic chanting = UNESCO intangible heritage (2008).
- **Script:** the Rigveda was composed and transmitted **orally**; exam keys give **Brahmi** as its basic script (surviving manuscripts are much later, in Devanagari / Sharada).
> Gayatri = Mandala III, Vishvamitra; Soma = Mandala IX; Purusha Sukta = Mandala X.

=== Yajurveda Branches and the Brahmanas
| Branch | Nature | Samhitas | Brahmana |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shukla (White)** | Mantras only, clearly arranged | **Vajasaneyi** (Madhyandina, Kanva) | **Shatapatha** |
| **Krishna (Black)** | Mantras + prose mixed | Taittiriya, Maitrayani, Kathaka, Kapishthala | Taittiriya |
| Veda | Brahmanas | Aranyakas | Upanishads |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rig | **Aitareya, Kaushitaki** | Aitareya, Kaushitaki | Aitareya, Kaushitaki |
| Sama | **Panchavimsha (Tandya), Jaiminiya**, Shadvimsha, Chandogya | Jaiminiya, Chandogya | Chandogya, Kena |
| Yajur | **Taittiriya, Shatapatha** | Taittiriya, Brihadaranyaka | Brihadaranyaka, Isha, Katha, **Taittiriya** (Krishna YV), Shvetashvatara |
| Atharva | **Gopatha** (only one) | None | Mundaka, Mandukya, Prashna |
- **Shatapatha Brahmana**: largest; Videgha Madhava's eastward journey; **Great Flood**; **first reference to money-lending** (kusidin); full **upanayana**; "wife is half her husband"; **Purushamedha** (described with the Vajasaneyi Samhita of the Shukla Yajurveda).
- **Aitareya Brahmana**: origin of kingship, ten forms of government, Shudra as servant, first mention of the **Andhras** (Sunahshepa story).
> Gopatha = Atharva; Jaiminiya = Sama; Purushamedha = Shatapatha.

=== Upanishads and Vedanta
- **108** (Muktika list), about **13 principal**; c. 800–600 BCE; **anti-ritualistic**; **Atman = Brahman**; karma, samsara, moksha; called **Vedanta** ("end of the Vedas").
- **Prasthanatrayi** = **Upanishads, Brahma Sutra** (Badarayana), **Bhagavad Gita**; the **Bhagavata is not** included. **Shankaracharya** wrote bhashyas on these three – **not on the Rigveda**.
| Saying / story | Upanishad |
|---|---|
| **Satyameva Jayate** (national motto, adopted 26 Jan 1950, below the State Emblem) | **Mundaka** |
| Asato ma sadgamaya; Yajnavalkya with **Gargi** and **Maitreyi**; first statement of transmigration | **Brihadaranyaka** |
| **Tat tvam asi**; **Satyakama Jabala** (son of the unmarried Jabala); Uddalaka–Shvetaketu; **Krishna, son of Devaki** (first mention) | **Chandogya** |
| **Nachiketa–Yama** dialogue on death | **Katha (Kathopanishad)** |
| Trimurti | Maitrayani |
| Name means "**white horse**"; introduces bhakti | **Shvetashvatara** |
| Taittiriya Upanishad | Krishna Yajurveda |
> Satyameva Jayate = Mundaka (not Mandukya); Jabala Upanishad is a different, later text.

=== Epics, Puranas and Smritis
# Mahabharata
- By **Vyasa**; original **Jaya / Jayasamhita** (8,800 shlokas) → Bharata → **Shatasahasri Samhita** (~1 lakh verses), **18 books (parvas)**, **Harivamsa** appended; older than the Ramayana; Bhagavad Gita (part of it) in **Sanskrit**.
- **Vaisampayana** told it to **Janamejaya**; **Ugrashrava Sauti** narrated it to Shaunaka's sages at **Naimisharanya**.
- War at Kurukshetra for **18 days**; Arjuna's grandfather = **Vichitravirya** (Pandu's nominal father); Karna spent Indra's **Vasavi Shakti** on **Ghatotkacha**.
# Ramayana
- By **Valmiki**: **24,000** verses, 7 kandas, five stages from the 5th century BCE; Rama first met Hanuman in the **Kishkindha Kanda**.
- **Janaka**'s brother = **Kushadhvaja** (king of Sankashya); Janaka's family priest-guru = **Shatananda**, son of sage **Gautama** (the Upanishadic Janaka learnt from Yajnavalkya).
- **Samudra manthan:** serpent **Vasuki** was the churning rope.
# Puranas and Smritis
- **18 Mahapuranas**; five marks (**pancha-lakshana**): **Sarga, Pratisarga**, Vamsa, Manvantara, Vamshanucharita. Complete **Dasavatara** narration: **Bhagavata (Maha-bhagavatam)**.
- Yugas: **Krita (Satya) → Treta → Dvapara → Kali**.
- **Manu** = first law-giver (Manusmriti; disapproves **widow remarriage**); **Mitakshara** on the Yajnavalkya Smriti = **Vijnaneshwara**.
