=== Appointments and Persons in News
# Justice Lisa Gill – Chief Justice, AP High Court
- Appointed by **President Droupadi Murmu**; assumed office **25 April 2026**; sworn in by Governor **S. Abdul Nazeer** at **Lok Bhavan**.
- **First woman Chief Justice** of the AP High Court after the **2019 reorganisation** (AP HC re-established **1 January 2019** at Amaravati).
- Succeeded **Justice Dhiraj Singh Thakur**; transferred from the **Punjab and Haryana High Court**.
- Appointment under **Art 217(1)** on Collegium advice.
- In March 2026, as a puisne judge, she was made **Executive Chairperson, AP State Legal Services Authority** (post of the 2nd senior-most judge, appointed by the Governor; Legal Services Authorities Act **1987**; **Article 39A** – equal justice and free legal aid). After she became CJ, **Justice Ravinath Tilhari** took the post (June 2026).
# High Court – constitutional provisions
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| 215 | HC is a **Court of Record** – can punish for contempt |
| 217 | Appointment by the President; 217(1)(b) + 124(4): removal only for **proved misbehaviour or incapacity** |
| 222 | **Transfer** of HC judges |
| 226 | Writs for Fundamental Rights **and other legal rights** – wider than Art 32 |
- Retirement: **HC 62**, SC 65.
- Removal motion: **100 Lok Sabha** or **50 Rajya Sabha** members; inquiry under the **Judges (Inquiry) Act, 1968**; the Constitution does **not** use the word "impeachment" for judges.

=== Godavari Pushkarams 2027 and AP Tourism
# Pushkarams 2027 preparations
- **Tent cities at 21 temple locations** on the Godavari; largest at **Bridge Lanka, Rajamahendravaram** (Godavari Riverfront Project).
- **50,000 homestay rooms in five years**, starting with **Konaseema**.
- **Environmental Protection Task Force** on river pollution – set up after a review chaired by **Deputy CM Pawan Kalyan**; the Task Force itself is chaired by the **Principal Secretary, Forest and Environment**, with the APPCB Member Secretary as convener.
- Pushkaram: every **12 years**, when **Jupiter enters Leo (Simha Rasi)**; first 12 days **Adi Pushkaram**, last 12 days **Antya Pushkaram**.
# Godavari and AP geography
- Rises at **Trimbakeshwar (Nashik)**; ~1,465 km; **second-longest river of India** after the Ganga.
- Right-bank tributaries: Pravara, Purna, **Manjira**; left-bank: Indravati, Sabari.
- **Dowleswaram Barrage** – **Sir Arthur Cotton**; **Papi Kondalu** gorge through the **Eastern Ghats**.
- **Kondapalli** (Krishna) – **chromite**; Kondapalli Bommalu toys.
- AP rainfall: SW monsoon about **60–65%** (not 85%); Rayalaseema rain-shadow; NE monsoon vital for Nellore, Prakasam.
# AP tourism
- **AP Tourism Policy 2024–29**: B&B and tent cities through **PPP**; 100+ MoUs in 18 months (₹20,000 crore+).
- **"Holiday in Andhra Pradesh"** – **domestic tourism** and destination weddings (Tirupati, Araku, Visakhapatnam coast).
- Coastline **1,053 km** (revised official figure); spiritual circuit – Tirumala, Srisailam, Vijayawada, Arasavalli; festivals – **Araku Utsav, Flamingo Festival, Kondaveedu Utsav**.

# Mee Intiki – Mee Doctor

- Launched **3 March 2026** (World Wildlife Day) by Deputy CM **Pawan Kalyan** at Mangalagiri.
- Rescue centres: **Visakhapatnam, Rajamahendravaram, Tirupati, Bairaluti**; Rapid Response Teams; village **"Vajra" volunteer teams**.
- Compensation: death **₹10 lakh**, injury ₹2 lakh, livestock – **market value**.
# Pattadar passbooks and land registration
- Revised pattadar passbooks with the State Emblem given in **Nandyal district** – part of the **Record of Rights**, a **facilitating document, not conclusive proof of title**; the **AP Land Titling Act was repealed**.
- **Conclusive titling** – State guarantees title; presumptive – it does not.
- **State Emblem of India (Prohibition of Improper Use) Act, 2005**.
- **Settipalle** (Tirupati) – first land registration in nearly five decades; coincides with a ₹1,766 crore township of the **Tirupati Urban Development Authority**.
- Section 22A, Registration Act 1908 – bars registration of certain lands; stamp duty – States; **mutation does not confer title**; DILRMP digitises records; land = **Entry 18, State List**.
