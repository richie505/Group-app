=== Records and Recognitions
# Kuppam – Guinness World Record for E-cycles
- **Kuppam** set a **Guinness World Record** for distributing **5,555 electric cycles within 24 hours** (single day) on **1 February 2026**.
- Initiative: **"Green & Healthy Kuppam"**, led by CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu**.
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Range | **Up to 60 km per charge**; household power or rooftop solar |
| Emission cut | **74.7% lower GHG** – e-cycle **15.2 g/km CO₂** vs petrol scooter **67.9 g/km** |
| Price | Subsidised **₹23,999** (market ₹34,999) |
| Finance | EMI via **AP Grameena Bank**; **no down payment for women** |
| Supplier | **e-Motorad** – assembled at its Kuppam unit |
| Agency | **Kuppam Area Development Authority (KADA)** |
| Technical support | **IIT Kanpur** – **State–Academia–Industry** model |
| Long-term framework | **Swarna Kuppam Vision 2029** |
# Highway Guinness Records – Bengaluru–Vijayawada NH
- **Rajpath Infracon** set **four Guinness World Records** on the **Bengaluru–Vijayawada (via Kadapa)** National Highway.
- **6 January 2026**, near **Bontlapalli (AP)**: **28.89 lane-km** and **10,655 t bituminous concrete** in **24 hours** (two records).
- By **11 January**: **57,500 t** continuous laying (third); **156 lane-km** (26 km × 6 lanes) continuous (fourth).
# Jeevan Daan – Organ Donation Record 2025
- **301 organs from 93 brain-dead donors** in 2025 – **highest since bifurcation**.
- Since 2015: **1,296 organs** in 10 years; **403 donors** (703 kidneys, 316 livers, 181 lungs, 91 hearts).
- Law: **THOTA, 1994** (amended **2011**).
# Kanipakam Temple – ISO Certification
- **Sri Varasiddhi Vinayaka Temple, Kanipakam (Chittoor)** – **ISO certification** by **HIME (Hyderabad)**.
- **Three-star rating for queue management** – **first in AP and Telangana**.
- Under the **AP Endowments Department**; local MLA **Kalikiri Murali Mohan (Puthalapattu)**.

=== Heritage Village and Heritage Finds
# Kangundi – AP's First Heritage Village
- **Kangundi** village **near Kuppam** to be declared **AP's first Heritage Village**.
- Framework: Centre's **Swachh, Suramya and Sanskrutik** framework, part of the **Swarna Kuppam** initiative.
- Location: **tri-junction of AP, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu**.
- Known for **Neolithic cave art**, bouldering rocks, temples of **Chola, Hoysala, Vijayanagara** periods.
- Plans: **Kangundi Rocks Bouldering Gymnasium**; annual **Kangundi Bouldering Festival & Climbing Cup**; **AP Sports Climbing Hub**; Olympic-standard climbing wall.
- Institutions: KADA, AP Tourism, **Indian Mountaineering Foundation**, Mahavatar Babaji TadEkam Foundation.
> Kangundi is under **Swarna Kuppam** (Swachh, Suramya, Sanskrutik) – **not HRIDAY or PRASHAD**.

=== GI Tags and Crafts
# Narsapuram Crochet Lace
- **GI tag: March 2024** (registered by **DPIIT** in the GI Registry); certificate given to the West Godavari Collector at **GI & Beyond 2024**, New Delhi (Nov 2024). Also under **ODOP**.
- Exports **over ₹150 crore** in the current financial year – revival after Covid.
- Districts: **West Godavari** and **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Konaseema**.
- Origin **1844**, brought by **Scottish missionaries**; survived the **1899 famine** and **1929 Great Depression**.
- **About 15,000 artisans, ~60% women** (2,000+ women in the early 20th century).
- Technique: **fine cotton thread** with a **single crochet hook**; markets: UK, US, France, Middle East.
- Support: **International Lace Trade Centre (ILTC), Narsapuram**; **EPCH** (Export Promotion Council for Handicrafts) – statutory body under the **Ministry of Textiles**.

- GI registered **in favour of KVIC** by the Geographical Indications Registry.
