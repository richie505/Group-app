## SUB 0 | MeeSeva and e-Seva – Single-window Citizen Services
[0:0] # Journey
[0:1] - **AP's e-governance legacy:** **N. Chandrababu Naidu's SMART governance** model and the **HITEC City** IT push of 1995-2004 – "**use of IT for development**" was the stated strategy of the **Andhra Pradesh Government** (UPPSC 1998).
[0:2] - **e-Seva** began in **2001**; it was succeeded by **MeeSeva** in **2011** (combined state) as the **single-window G2C** delivery mechanism.
[0:3] - **"MeeSeva"** in Telugu = **"At your service"**; channels: **MeeSeva franchise centres** + **online portal**; also enrolment for Aarogya Raksha.

  [0:5.1] Category B | Statutory services needing notices, hearings or field verification (departmental workflow)

## SUB 1 | e-Pragati, APSWAN and AP FiberNet
[1:0] # e-Pragati – AP State Enterprise Architecture
[1:1] - **e-Pragati** = new name of the **AP State Enterprise Architecture (APSEA)**: comprehensive, **whole-of-government** e-governance – all e-Governance systems interconnected; core datasets **People Hub, Land Hub, Entity Hub, GIS Hub, IoT Hub**.
[1:2] - Introduced under CM **Chandrababu Naidu**; approved by the state cabinet in **2015**.
[1:3] - Outlay **Rs 2,358 crore**: **72 projects** in **14 packages**, over three years; state share about Rs 1,528 crore, rest via **PPP**; covers **33 secretariat departments** and **300+ agencies**; built with **Wipro**.
[1:4] # APSWAN and networks
[1:5] - **APSWAN** (AP State Wide Area Network) = backbone network linking the **state capital (Secretariat), district HQs and mandal HQs** under **NeGP**; operational **Nov 2010**; initial capacity **2 Mbps**.
[1:6] - Upgraded to **APSDWAN** (software-defined) on **1 May 2021**; DHQ–MHQ links 8 Mbps; **APSCAN** (Secretariat campus, Velagapudi, 2016); AP State Data Centre at Pi Data Centers, Mangalagiri.
[1:7] # AP FiberNet
[1:8] - **AP State FiberNet Ltd (APSFL):** incorporated **October 2015** under the Companies Act 2013; under the Energy, Infrastructure & Investment Dept; runs the **AP Fibergrid** – inaugurated by the **President** in **December 2017**.
[1:9] - **Triple-play**: **broadband + TV + telephone**; basic plan **Rs 149** (15 Mbps, ~250 TV channels); reached **4,000+ tribal hamlets** in the Eastern Ghats.
[1:10] > e-Pragati (not Citi Net / e-Jan Seva) = the comprehensive AP e-governance project; APSWAN (not APSLAN/APSMAN) links capital–districts–mandals.

## SUB 3 | RTGS and Mana Mitra
[3:0] # Real Time Governance Society
[3:1] - **RTGS** formed on **6 September 2017**; works directly under the **Chief Minister**; **RTG State Centre** (command centre) inaugurated at the **Secretariat, Velagapudi** on **26 November 2017**.
[3:2] - Dashboards pull data from surveillance cameras, drones, biometric systems and **AWARE** (AP Weather Forecasting and Early Warning Research Centre).
[3:3] - Revived from **2024** as an **AI-driven** governance platform; **Swarna Andhra @2047** pillar "**Effective Governance and AI-based Public Services**"; AP has **59 Chief Data Officers**.
[3:4] # Mana Mitra – WhatsApp governance
[3:5] - Launched **30 January 2025**; run by **RTGS**.
[3:6] TABLE head: Stage | Data
  [3:6.0] Launch (Jan 2025) | 150 services
  [3:6.1] 2025-26 | 865 services
  [3:6.2] Cap per service | 5 user inputs, 2 documents
[3:7] - Services: **rice card eKYC, electricity bills, 1-B Adangal, APSRTC tickets, TTD and temple darshan**.
[3:8] - Temples: **Rs 22.26 crore** to **21 temples**; top earner = **Srikalahasti** (not Tirumala).

## SUB 4 | Rajiv Internet Village (RAJiv)
[4:0] - **RAJiv** launched by CM **Y.S. Rajasekhara Reddy** in **August 2004** to bring **government services closer to rural people** (affordable, transparent).
[4:1] - First internet village: **Chiluvuru** (Duggirala mandal, Guntur district).
[4:2] - Village kiosks run by local entrepreneurs: **utility bill payment**, state **bus tickets**, land records; contract with **Bharat Electronics** (Dec 2004) for **5,550** centres in 14 districts.
