=== MeeSeva and e-Seva – Single-window Citizen Services
# Journey
- **AP's e-governance legacy:** **N. Chandrababu Naidu's SMART governance** model and the **HITEC City** IT push of 1995-2004 – "**use of IT for development**" was the stated strategy of the **Andhra Pradesh Government** (UPPSC 1998).
- **e-Seva** began in **2001**; it was succeeded by **MeeSeva** in **2011** (combined state) as the **single-window G2C** delivery mechanism.
- **"MeeSeva"** in Telugu = **"At your service"**; channels: **MeeSeva franchise centres** + **online portal**; also enrolment for Aarogya Raksha.
# Service categories
| Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Category A** | **Over-the-counter** services issued on the spot (e.g. certified copies) |
| **Category B** | **Statutory** services needing **notices, hearings or field verification** (departmental workflow) |
# Departments inside MeeSeva
| Department | Role |
|---|---|
| **EDS Department** | **Prints MeeSeva certificates** |
| **Education Department** | **Migration certificates** (passed candidates only) |
# Other AP registration and portal services
| AP project | Department / service |
|---|---|
| **CARD** (Computer-aided Administration of Registration Department) | Registration Department: computer-aided **registration of property documents** (not revenue land records) |
| AP Online | Web portal for G2C services |
| Registration Service Kendras (2026) | Property registration on the **Passport Seva Kendra model**; legal powers stay with Sub-Registrar Offices |
- **RSK debate:** effect on **document writers'** livelihoods; State assures SROs are **not being privatised**.
- **Telangana pairs (APPSC Sample Taker):** **T Wallet** – Telangana's official e-wallet, launched **1 June 2017**; **DOST** (Degree Online Services Telangana) – online degree admissions; DOST itself began in **2016**, the **DOST-2021** cycle opened **1 July 2021** (the key reads pair II this way).
> APPSC 2023: MeeSeva = "At your service"; EDS prints certificates; Education Dept issues migration certificates – all pairs correct. Category B = statutory (notices/hearings/field inspection).

=== e-Pragati, APSWAN and AP FiberNet
# e-Pragati – AP State Enterprise Architecture
- **e-Pragati** = new name of the **AP State Enterprise Architecture (APSEA)**: comprehensive, **whole-of-government** e-governance – all e-Governance systems interconnected; core datasets **People Hub, Land Hub, Entity Hub, GIS Hub, IoT Hub**.
- Introduced under CM **Chandrababu Naidu**; approved by the state cabinet in **2015**.
- Outlay **Rs 2,358 crore**: **72 projects** in **14 packages**, over three years; state share about Rs 1,528 crore, rest via **PPP**; covers **33 secretariat departments** and **300+ agencies**; built with **Wipro**.
# APSWAN and networks
- **APSWAN** (AP State Wide Area Network) = backbone network linking the **state capital (Secretariat), district HQs and mandal HQs** under **NeGP**; operational **Nov 2010**; initial capacity **2 Mbps**.
- Upgraded to **APSDWAN** (software-defined) on **1 May 2021**; DHQ–MHQ links 8 Mbps; **APSCAN** (Secretariat campus, Velagapudi, 2016); AP State Data Centre at Pi Data Centers, Mangalagiri.
# AP FiberNet
- **AP State FiberNet Ltd (APSFL):** incorporated **October 2015** under the Companies Act 2013; under the Energy, Infrastructure & Investment Dept; runs the **AP Fibergrid** – inaugurated by the **President** in **December 2017**.
- **Triple-play**: **broadband + TV + telephone**; basic plan **Rs 149** (15 Mbps, ~250 TV channels); reached **4,000+ tribal hamlets** in the Eastern Ghats.
> e-Pragati (not Citi Net / e-Jan Seva) = the comprehensive AP e-governance project; APSWAN (not APSLAN/APSMAN) links capital–districts–mandals.

=== RTGS and Mana Mitra
# Real Time Governance Society
- **RTGS** formed on **6 September 2017**; works directly under the **Chief Minister**; **RTG State Centre** (command centre) inaugurated at the **Secretariat, Velagapudi** on **26 November 2017**.
- Dashboards pull data from surveillance cameras, drones, biometric systems and **AWARE** (AP Weather Forecasting and Early Warning Research Centre).
- Revived from **2024** as an **AI-driven** governance platform; **Swarna Andhra @2047** pillar "**Effective Governance and AI-based Public Services**"; AP has **59 Chief Data Officers**.
# Mana Mitra – WhatsApp governance
- Launched **30 January 2025**; run by **RTGS**.
| Stage | Data |
|---|---|
| Launch (Jan 2025) | 150 services |
| 2025-26 | 865 services |
| Cap per service | 5 user inputs, 2 documents |
- Services: **rice card eKYC, electricity bills, 1-B Adangal, APSRTC tickets, TTD and temple darshan**.
- Temples: **Rs 22.26 crore** to **21 temples**; top earner = **Srikalahasti** (not Tirumala).
~~ Sources: app notes 5:27 sub 3

=== Rajiv Internet Village (RAJiv)
- **RAJiv** launched by CM **Y.S. Rajasekhara Reddy** in **August 2004** to bring **government services closer to rural people** (affordable, transparent).
- First internet village: **Chiluvuru** (Duggirala mandal, Guntur district).
- Village kiosks run by local entrepreneurs: **utility bill payment**, state **bus tickets**, land records; contract with **Bharat Electronics** (Dec 2004) for **5,550** centres in 14 districts.
