=== Aadhaar and UIDAI
# UIDAI
- **UIDAI** set up **28 January 2009** as an attached office of the **Planning Commission**; statutory authority under the **Aadhaar Act, 2016**, working under **MeitY** (not Home, Finance or Rural Development).
| Act / rule / policy | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| Aadhaar Act | **2016** | UIDAI made statutory under MeitY; targeted delivery of subsidies |
| Amendment | 2019 | Amended the Aadhaar Act |
| Aadhaar Authentication for Good Governance Rules | **2020** | Voluntary authentication by other entities |
| MeitY Policy on Open APIs | **2015** | Led to API Setu |
# Firsts
| FIRST | Fact |
|---|---|
| First Aadhaar number | **Ranjana Sonawane**, **Tembhli** village, **Nandurbar**, Maharashtra, **29 September 2010** |
| Planned first issue | UIDAI planned the first set of numbers for Aug 2010 – **February 2011** (exam key: February 2011) |
# Format and scale
- **12-digit random number**, **free and lifelong**, one enrolment; no caste, religion or geography.
- **Biometrics:** **10 fingerprints, 2 iris scans, face photograph**; **dactylography** = study of **fingerprints**.
- **Scale:** **142 crore** numbers generated (31 Jul 2025); about 116 crore enrolled by mid-2017 (120 crore crossed only later).
- **Uses:** DBT, **Jeevan Pramaan** (**digital life certificate**), e-KYC. Mobile-Aadhaar linking was mandated in 2017 but struck down by the Supreme Court in Sept 2018.
- **ABHA** = **14** digits (health ID), Aadhaar = 12.

=== UPI, NPCI Products and Payment Systems
# UPI
- Launched **April 2016** by **NPCI**; one app, several bank accounts, **VPA**.
- **UPI 2.0:** daily limit **Rs 2 lakh**; **UPI Lite:** on-device wallet **without a UPI PIN**.
- **Abroad:** **France** = **first country in Europe** to accept UPI (Feb 2024); **PayNow-UPI** link with **Singapore** (2023).
| Product | Key fact |
|---|---|
| **BHIM** | NPCI's UPI app; **30 December 2016** |
| **e-RUPI** | **One-time**, cashless, person- and purpose-specific **voucher**; NPCI with **DFS, MoHFW, NHA**; launched by **PM Modi**, **August 2021** |
| **Vajra** | NPCI platform for **fast and secure payments** (DLT-based clearing) |
| **NFS** | National Financial Switch – **links all ATMs**; built by **IDRBT** (2004), now run by **NPCI** |
# Operators
| System | Operator |
|---|---|
| **NEFT, RTGS** | **RBI** |
| **IMPS**, UPI, AePS, BBPS, NACH, RuPay | **NPCI** |
| FASTag (NETC) | **NPCI with NHAI/IHMCL** |
| **iPay** gateway | **IRCTC** |
- **AePS** = cash withdrawal and balance enquiry at micro-ATMs using Aadhaar.
- **New Umbrella Entities:** RBI move to end NPCI's monopoly; minimum capital **Rs 500 crore**.
- **First digital (mobile) wallet company:** **MobiKwik** (2009).

=== Nodal Bodies, Committees and Digitisation Drive
- **After demonetisation (Nov 2016):** **NITI Aayog** coordinated the drive to make **100% Government-Citizen transactions digital**; **Committee of Chief Ministers on Digitisation** headed by **N. Chandrababu Naidu**.
- **MeitY** got "Promotion of Digital Transactions" in **February 2017** (taken over from NITI Aayog).
- **17 July 2023:** "Promotion of Digital Payments" moved to the **Department of Financial Services (Ministry of Finance)** – the present nodal body.
- **Nilekani Committee:** set up by **RBI in January 2019** on deepening digital payments.
- **RBI Digital Payments Index:** base **March 2018 = 100**; published **half-yearly**.
~~ Sources: app notes 5:25 sub 5; general knowledge (NITI Aayog 2016, Naidu committee, Nilekani 2019)

=== India Stack and Open Digital Platforms
| India Stack layer | Component |
|---|---|
| Identity (presence-less) | Aadhaar, e-KYC |
| **Cashless** | **UPI** |
| **Paperless** | **eSign, DigiLocker** |
| **Consent** | **DEPA / Account Aggregator** |
- India Stack gives **online, paperless, cashless** access and cuts e-KYC cost – through the **identity layer (Aadhaar e-KYC)**, not the payments layer; the original stack had 3 layers, consent was added later.
- **Open-source platforms:** **Aarogya Setu, CoWIN, DigiLocker, DIKSHA** – all four (UPSC 2022).
- **ONDC (2022):** open e-commerce network that **breaks platform monopolies**.
- **Account Aggregator:** consent-based sharing of financial data (not the identity layer).
- Digital economy: **12-14% of GDP**.
~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 5 p.77-80; app notes 5:25 sub 0

=== DBT – JAM and Delivery in Andhra Pradesh
- **JAM** = Jan Dhan + Aadhaar + Mobile; DBT routed via **CPSMS (now PFMS)** + NPCI's **Aadhaar Payment Bridge**.
- **First JAM application:** **PAHAL** (LPG subsidy). **DBT Mission:** now in the **Cabinet Secretariat**.
- **Numbers:** **Rs 44 lakh crore** transferred (May 2025); savings **Rs 3.48 lakh crore** (2015 to Mar 2023); **PMJDY 58.77 crore** accounts (Aug 2026), **more than half held by women**.
- **AP:** DBT inaugurated at **Gollaprolu, East Godavari**, **6 January 2013** by **Jairam Ramesh**; Aadhaar e-PoS PDS from **Krishna** (May 2015); AP = first state with **e-PoS in 100% of fair price shops**.
