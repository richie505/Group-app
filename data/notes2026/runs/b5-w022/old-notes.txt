=== NOTES 5:22 | Ict and Governance – NeGP, Digital India Pillars, E-kranti
## SUB 0 | Digital India – Vision Areas, Nine Pillars and e-Kranti
[22:0:0] - Launched by PM Modi on **1 July 2015**; implemented/coordinated by **MeitY**; umbrella programme to make India a **digitally empowered society and knowledge economy**.
[22:0:1] # Three vision areas
[22:0:2] TABLE head: Vision area | Includes
  [22:0:2.0] Digital infrastructure as a core utility to every citizen | High-speed internet, Aadhaar identity, CSC access, cloud locker; connect villages, Wi-Fi in schools, public places, tourist centres
  [22:0:2.1] Governance and services on demand | Online/mobile services, single window, cashless transactions
  [22:0:2.2] Digital empowerment of citizens | Universal digital literacy, documents on the cloud, services in Indian languages
[22:0:3] - **NOT a key area / not an aim:** **issue of digital currency**; "digital application for export promotion"; "digital commerce and trade"; forming India's own internet companies "like China"; a framework for overseas MNCs to build data centres (UPSC 2018: **3 only**).
[22:0:4] # Nine pillars
[22:0:5] TABLE head: No. | Pillar | Flagship / data
  [22:0:5.0] 1 | Broadband Highways | BharatNet: 2,18,347 GPs (Mar 2025)
  [22:0:5.1] 2 | Universal Access to Mobile Connectivity | 4G in villages
  [22:0:5.2] 3 | Public Internet Access Programme | 5.97 lakh CSCs (Apr 2025)
  [22:0:5.3] 4 | e-Governance – reforming government through technology | e-Office
  [22:0:5.4] 5 | e-Kranti – electronic delivery of services | 44 MMPs
  [22:0:5.5] 6 | Information for All | Open Government Data platform
  [22:0:5.6] 7 | Electronics Manufacturing | Target net zero imports; Rs 2.13 lakh cr (2020-21) → Rs 5.25 lakh cr (2024-25)
  [22:0:5.7] 8 | IT for Jobs | IT skills
  [22:0:5.8] 9 | Early Harvest Programmes | Biometric attendance, Wi-Fi in universities
[22:0:6] # e-Kranti (NeGP 2.0)
[22:0:7] - Approved **2015**; **44 MMPs** (not 31); vision "**Transforming e-Governance for Transforming Governance**".
[22:0:8] - APPSC G1 2017: mission for **electronic delivery of all government services** = **e-Kranti**.
[22:0:9] - Principles: transformation not translation; integrated services; **cloud by default**, **mobile first** (not paper-first).
[22:0:10] - Sister missions: **Make in India (25 Sep 2014)**, **Skill India (15 Jul 2015)**.
[22:0:11] - Rural gaps: **GP fibre via BharatNet** and **4G in villages**.

## SUB 1 | E-governance – Concepts, Models and Pillars
[22:1:0] - **E-governance** = use of ICT for **technology-driven governance**, aiming to **engage, enable and empower citizens**; improves **transparency**, cuts cost.
[22:1:1] - **Four pillars:** **People, Process, Technology, Resources** (MPPSC 2024).
[22:1:2] - **Gartner's four phases:** **Information → Interaction (2nd) → Transaction → Transformation (last)** (MPPSC 2025).
[22:1:3] TABLE head: Model | Example
  [22:1:3.0] G2C | Certificates, DigiLocker
  [22:1:3.1] G2B | MCA portal (MCA21), GSTN
  [22:1:3.2] G2G | NIC solutions for departments
  [22:1:3.3] G2E | Online HR tools
[22:1:4] - **MCA21** (Corporate Affairs) = **e-governance** project for companies (UPSC 2007).
[22:1:5] # Implementing bodies
[22:1:6] TABLE head: Body | Role
  [22:1:6.0] NIC (1976) | G2G solutions; built e-Taal
  [22:1:6.1] MeitY | Coordinates Digital India; UIDAI is under it
  [22:1:6.2] DeitY (now MeitY) + DARPG | Drafted NeGP
  [22:1:6.3] NeGD | MeitY's programme-management arm under Digital India Corporation; runs capacity building
  [22:1:6.4] CSC e-Governance Services India Ltd | SPV of CSCs
[22:1:7] - **Capacity building Phase III** (Sept 2024): launched by **MeitY** (NeGD with NISG) – DPI, procurement, AI/ML training for officials.
[22:1:8] - **UN EGDI 2024:** India **97th** (105th in 2022).

[25:0:5] # Format and scale
[25:0:6] - **12-digit random number**, **free and lifelong**, one enrolment; no caste, religion or geography.
[25:0:7] - **Biometrics:** **10 fingerprints, 2 iris scans, face photograph**; **dactylography** = study of **fingerprints**.
[25:0:8] - **Scale:** **142 crore** numbers generated (31 Jul 2025); about 116 crore enrolled by mid-2017 (120 crore crossed only later).
[25:0:9] - **Uses:** DBT, **Jeevan Pramaan** (**digital life certificate**), e-KYC. Mobile-Aadhaar linking was mandated in 2017 but struck down by the Supreme Court in Sept 2018.
