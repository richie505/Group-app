## SUB 1 | Who Prepares the Survey and How It Is Presented
[1:0] - Published every year by the **Ministry of Finance** – prepared by the **Economic Division, Department of Economic Affairs**, under the **Chief Economic Adviser (CEA)**; not RBI, NITI Aayog, Planning Commission, NDC or ISI.
[1:1] - Presented in **both Houses** during the Budget Session, **before the Union Budget** (a day or a few days earlier); a **convention**, not a constitutional duty (Art. 112 is the Budget).
[1:2] - **First Survey 1950-51**; separate from the Budget since **1964**; single volume + statistical appendix since 2021-22.
[1:3] - **First Economic Adviser** after Independence: **J.J. Anjaria**.

  [1:4.2] Sanjeev Sanyal (Principal Economic Adviser) | 2021-22
  [1:4.3] V. Anantha Nageswaran | 2022-23 to 2025-26 (term to 31 March 2027)
[1:5] - **AP Socio-Economic Survey** – by the **Planning Department**, tabled with the State Budget; **UN ESCAP** Survey (HQ **Bangkok**) is separate.
