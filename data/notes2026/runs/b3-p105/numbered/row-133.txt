## SUB 0 | Structure of the Act – Parts, Sections, Schedules
[0:0] # The Act at a glance
[0:1] - **Size:** **12 Parts, 108 Sections, 13 Schedules** – Act **No. 6 of 2014** (Gazette of India Extraordinary **No. 6**, 1 March 2014).
[0:2] - **Popular name:** the "**Telangana Act**" (bifurcation Act); made under **Articles 3 and 4**.
[0:3] - **Character:** passed by Parliament although the AP Legislature had rejected the Bill; widely criticised as hurriedly drafted ("arbitrary, does not clearly address many issues").

  [1:0.2] Passed by Rajya Sabha (Upper House) | 20 February 2014

[1:1] - **Parliament passed it in February 2014; the President signed it in March 2014.**

[1:3] - The State was bifurcated in **June 2014**; residuary AP continues the State of 1956 – only Telangana was new.
[1:4] - **Sec 10:** First Schedule of the Constitution amended – new entry "**29. Telangana**" (India now has 28 States after J&K became a UT in 2019).

## SUB 2 | Part II – Telangana, Common Capital, Governor and Police
[2:0] TABLE head: Section | Provision
  [2:0.0] 3 | Formation of Telangana – 10 districts incl. Hyderabad, Medak, Karimnagar, Khammam (minus Polavaram villages); amended by the 2014 Polavaram Ordinance/Act to move 7 Khammam mandals to AP
  [2:0.1] 4 | Residuary AP – the remaining territories (13 districts, e.g. Srikakulam)
  [2:0.2] 5 | Hyderabad common capital for a period not exceeding ten years; then capital of Telangana, AP to get a new capital; common capital = area notified as GHMC
  [2:0.3] 6 | Central expert committee on AP's new capital – report within six months of enactment
  [2:0.4] 7 | Governor of existing AP to be common Governor for a period determined by the President
  [2:0.5] 8 | Governor's special responsibility for security of life, liberty and property of residents of the common capital (law and order, internal security, vital installations, Government buildings)
  [2:0.6] 9 | Central Government to assist both States to raise additional police; Greyhound Training Centre
  [2:0.7] 10 | First Schedule of the Constitution – Telangana as entry 29
[2:1] - **Sec 8(3):** Governor acts in his **individual judgment after consulting the Telangana Council of Ministers**; his decision in his discretion is **final**.
[2:2] - **Sec 8(4):** assisted by **two advisors** appointed by the **Central Government**.
[2:3] - **Sec 9(2):** Centre to maintain the **Greyhound Training Centre, Hyderabad** as a common centre for **three years**, then it goes to Telangana; **9(5):** **Greyhound and OCTOPUS** forces divided **on options from personnel**.
[2:4] - **Hyderabad's status:** it lay in Telangana's territory (not common territory); it was AP's **de jure** capital till 2 June 2024 while AP worked from Amaravati; tax revenue of Hyderabad is **not shared** with AP.
[2:5] - **⚠ Trap:** the common-capital limit is "not exceeding 10 years"; the common Governor's period is set by the President, not by the Act.
[2:6] > Sec 5 capital ≤10 yrs (GHMC); 6 committee 6 months; 7 common Governor; 8 Governor + 2 advisers; 9 police, Greyhounds 3 yrs.
[2:7] ~~ Sources: AP Reorganisation Act 2014 Gazette text pp.2-4, ss.3-10; job b1-r263 notes row-263 (Polavaram ordinance)

[3:6] - AP's 175 seats: **29 SC, 7 ST**; most seats East Godavari, fewest Vizianagaram (9) in 2014.
[3:7] - Constitution Schedules amended by the Act: **First** (States) and **Fourth** (Rajya Sabha seats).

## SUB 4 | Part IV – High Court
[4:0] - **Sec 30:** the **High Court of Judicature at Hyderabad** = **common High Court** for both States till AP's High Court is constituted (named "High Court of Judicature at Hyderabad for the State of Telangana and the State of Andhra Pradesh"); judges' salaries shared by population ratio.
[4:1] - **Sec 31:** a **separate High Court for AP**; the Hyderabad court becomes Telangana's; **31(2): principal seat at such place as the President notifies** – the Act names **no place**.
[4:2] - **Presidential order of 26 December 2018** (Art 214) → **High Court of Andhra Pradesh at Amaravati** functioning from **1 January 2019**.
[4:3] > Sec 30 common HC; Sec 31 separate AP HC; Amaravati, 1 Jan 2019.
[4:4] ~~ Sources: AP Reorganisation Act 2014 Gazette text pp.8-9, ss.30-31; job b1-r263 notes row-263; old app notes 3:133

## SUB 7 | Capital Question After 2014
[7:0] - **Sivaramakrishnan Committee** (Sec 6) on the new capital; AP chose **Amaravati** (2015).
[7:1] - Common capital ended on **2 June 2024**; Hyderabad is now Telangana's capital only.
[7:2] - AP Assembly resolution of **28 March 2026** asked to insert "**at Amaravati**" in Sec 5(2); the **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Act, 2026** named **Amaravati** the sole capital with effect from **2 June 2024** – the first law in which Parliament named a State's capital. AP High Court capital order: 3 March 2022.
