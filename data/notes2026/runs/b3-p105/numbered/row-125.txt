## SUB 3 | MSME Policy 2015-20, Sectoral Policies and MSME (4.0)
[3:0] # MSME Policy 2015-20
[3:1] - Targets: new jobs for **at least 3.5 lakh people** and new investment of **at least Rs 15,000 crore by 2020**; MSME Revival Fund **Rs 100 crore** (2015-16).
[3:2] - **25% rebate in land cost, limited to Rs 10 lakh**, in industrial estates/parks.
[3:3] - AP Budget 2017-18: **Rs 126 crore** for rejuvenation of MSME units.
[3:4] - **M-Parks Policy:** **200 MSME parks by 2023**, about **30,000 MSMEs**, **Rs 45,000 crore** investment.
[3:5] # Sectoral policies
[3:6] - **Aerospace and Defence Manufacturing Policy 2015-20:** at least **5,000 highly skilled jobs** and **Rs 20,000 crore** investment by 2020, plus 4 anchor units – two separate targets (A and R both true, R does not explain A).
[3:7] - **Textile, Apparel and Garments Policy 2018-23:** capital subsidy caps – **100% of FCI** (garmenting, made-ups, integrated, processing, technical textiles), **75%** (weaving, knitting), **50%** (spinning, ginning) → "all of the above"; policy never got operational guidelines and was replaced by (4.0) 2024-29.
[3:8] - **Textile (4.0) 2024-29:** 2 lakh+ jobs. **Food Processing (4.0):** Large Rs 50-100 crore, **Mega above Rs 100 crore**. **Electronics (4.0):** clusters at Kopparthy, Tirupati, **Sri City**. **Tourism (Mar 2026):** 5-10% FCI subsidy up to Rs 40 crore.
[3:9] # MSME & Entrepreneur Development Policy (4.0) 2024-29
[3:10] - **G.O.Ms.69**; target **Rs 50,000 crore**; **175+ MSME parks – one per assembly constituency**; **Rs 500 crore** corpus.
