## SUB 0 | Principles of Division – Item → Basis → Section
[0:0] # The core rule
[0:1] - **Act:** AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 – President's assent **1 March 2014**; appointed day **2 June 2014**.
[0:2] - **Core rule:** **assets by location**, **liabilities (debts) by population ratio**.
[0:3] - **Population ratio** (Census 2011): **AP : Telangana = 58.32 : 41.68** (≈ **58 : 42**).
[0:4] - **Result:** AP got about **46% of revenue** but inherited about **58% of debt** – major assets built in the united State stand in **Hyderabad**, which went to Telangana; so the location rule is unfair to AP.
[0:5] - **Financial division:** **Part VI**, "Apportionment of Assets and Liabilities", **Sections 47-67** (the money items are **Sections 47-64**, Sec 64 = residuary; 76-83 = services); disputes settled by mutual agreement, failing which by **Central order on the advice of the CAG** (Sec 47(4)).
[0:6] # Item → basis → section
[0:7] TABLE head: Item | Basis | Section
  [0:7.0] Award of the 13th Finance Commission | Population ratio and other parameters (Centre apportions) | 46(1)
  [0:7.1] Land, stores, goods inside a State | Location | 48

[0:8] > Population ratio is used most often to divide **assets** held in common (cash, securities, HQ, outside property); the summary rule still reads "assets by location, debts by population".
[0:9] > Matching trap: **Arrears of taxes = Sec 50** (correct pair); treasury = 49 (not 53), PF = 58 (not 59), public debt = 54 (not 58).

[2:0] TABLE head: Committee | Chair | Subject

  [2:0.1] Kamalanathan Committee | C.R. Kamalanathan | Allocation of State-level employees
  [2:0.2] Pratyush Sinha Committee | Pratyush Sinha | Allocation of All India Services officers (IAS/IPS/IFoS), under Sec 80

[2:1] - Sheela Bhide Committee recommended division of most Schedule IX bodies (89 of 91 reported); Telangana opposed some; its term expired Aug 2015 and the Centre revived it.
[2:2] - **Part VIII**, "**Provisions as to Services**" (**Sections 76-83**): AIS and State employees in local, district, zonal, multi-zonal and State cadres.
[2:3] - **Section 76**: two separate **State cadres** of **IAS, IPS, IFoS** (creates the Telangana State cadre).
[2:4] - **Section 77**: State employees; does **not** apply to All India Services.
[2:5] - **Section 80**: **Advisory Committees** within **30 days** of enactment; allocation by the Centre on their recommendation.
[2:6] - **Section 82**: PSU staff stay for **one year**; the **corporate body itself** decides the distribution.
[2:7] - **Section 83**: APPSC continues for AP; Telangana to constitute its own PSC.
[2:8] - Non-opters allotted on **native (domicile) status**; seniority and pension disputes under **Sec 59 / Eighth Schedule**; DoPT one-member **Deepak Khandekar** committee (March 2024) on AIS cases.
[2:9] - Comparison: the **1953 Andhra State** partition committee was chaired by **T.T. Krishnamachari**.

## SUB 3 | Twelfth Schedule – Power and Coal
[3:0] - **Twelfth Schedule** = principles for **coal, oil and gas, and power** (Sec 92).
[3:1] - **Coal:** **SCCL** equity **51% Telangana, 49% Government of India** – AP holds **no** equity; **existing coal linkages continue** without change; new linkages under the New Coal Distribution Policy.
[3:2] - **Power:** **APGENCO units divided by geographical location** of the plants; existing **PPAs continue**.
[3:3] - **Central Generating Stations:** power shared on the **actual energy consumption of the DISCOMs in the last 5 years**.
[3:4] - Deficit State gets **first right of refusal** over the other's surplus power for **10 years**; APERC joint for 6 months, SLDC common for 2 years.
[3:5] - AP got about **46% of power** for **58% of population**.
[3:6] > The amicable division ratio is **AP : Telangana = 58 : 42** – a statement giving it as "Telangana and AP … 58 : 42 respectively" is wrong.
[3:7] ~~ Sources: AP Reorganisation Act 2014 (apra.txt, Twelfth Schedule); NextGen AP State Reorganisation Act notes Part 2 p.14-16
## SUB 4 | Access to Higher Education – Part XI
[4:0] - **Section 95**, **Part XI** (Access to Higher Education): **equal opportunities for quality higher education**.
[4:1] - Existing **Article 371D** admission quotas in all government/private, aided/unaided institutions of higher, technical and medical education continue for **10 years**, with the **common admission process**.
[4:2] - **Section 97** amended **Article 371D** to read "**Andhra Pradesh or Telangana**".
[4:3] > 10 years = common admission (Sec 95) = surplus-power right = common capital Hyderabad.
[4:4] ~~ Sources: AP Reorganisation Act 2014 (apra.txt, Part XI s.95, s.97); NextGen AP State Reorganisation Act notes Part 2 p.9-11
## SUB 5 | Unresolved Division – Status
[5:0] - **CAG (2019):** **₹1,51,349.67 crore under capital heads** and ₹28,099.68 crore of loans and advances still **not apportioned** between AP and Telangana (still pending after 7 years).
[5:1] - **Common institutions:** Telangana claims about **₹408 crore** from AP for running the High Court, Raj Bhavan etc. in Hyderabad (2022); the exact sum AP undertook is not established.
[5:2] - Over **140** corporations/institutions worth more than **₹39,000 crore** undivided; AP wants **population ratio**, Telangana insists on **location**.
[5:3] - **Unapportioned public debt** of over ₹24,000 crore on AP's books cuts its **FRBM borrowing room**.
[5:4] - **Southern Zonal Council** directed AP to pay Telangana its share of a central subsidy credited to AP.
[5:5] - Criticism: schedules not exhaustive; "**headquarters**" undefined → litigation.
