=== National Project Status – APRA Section 90
| Sub-section | What it says |
|---|---|
| **90(1)** | Polavaram Irrigation Project **declared a national project** |
| 90(2) | Expedient in the public interest that the **Union takes control of its regulation and development for the purposes of irrigation** |
| 90(3) | **Consent of Telangana deemed** to have been given |
| 90(4) | **Central Government executes** the project and obtains environmental, forest and R&R clearances |
- **Polavaram is the only project declared national by the Reorganisation Act itself** – not Gundlakamma, Pulichintala or Jeedipalli.
- So it is a **National Project**, executed by the Centre **in consultation with AP and Telangana** – not a mere State project.
- **NITI Aayog (2016):** suggested **the State Government (AP) executes** Polavaram while the **Centre funds** the irrigation component; since then **GoAP executes it on behalf of GoI** and claims reimbursement.
> Exam angle: **Section 90** (not 30, 89 or 91); "for the purposes of **irrigation**" only; by the Act the **Centre** executes, in practice **AP** does (NITI Aayog).

=== Project Features
| Feature | Fact |
|---|---|
| River / site | **Godavari**, 42 km upstream of the Cotton Barrage, near **Ramayyapet village, Polavaram mandal** – earlier **West Godavari** district, now **Eluru** district |
| Name | **Indira Sagar** (Polavaram) – multipurpose terminal reservoir |
| Dam | 2,467.50 m earth-cum-rock fill dam; spillway 1,121.20 m |
| **FRL** | **+150 ft** (45.72 m) |
| Reservoir | Gross 194.6 TMC; utilisation 322.73 TMC |
| Irrigation | **7.20 lakh acres** – East & West Godavari, Visakhapatnam, Krishna |
| Hydel | **960 MW** (12 units × 80 MW) |
| Drinking/industry | **23.44 TMC for Visakhapatnam**; 28.5 lakh people in 540 villages |
| Diversion | **80 TMC** of Godavari water to the **Krishna** – a **river-linking** project |
# Canals
| Canal | Destination |
|---|---|
| **Right Main Canal (RMC)** | **Krishna delta** – carries **80 TMC** of Godavari water to the Krishna above Vijayawada (Pattiseema lift as interim) |
| **Left Main Canal (LMC)** | **East Godavari and Visakhapatnam** (Yeleru, Vizag water supply); LMC to Visakhapatnam targeted Mar 2027 |
- **Polavaram-Nallamala Sagar link** (earlier the **Banakacherla link**): Telangana says it violates **APRA 2014** and the **1980 GWDT award**.
- Split of the 80 TMC (GWDT, 1980): **45 TMC to AP** + **35 TMC** to upper States – **Karnataka 21, Maharashtra 14**.
> Exam angle: **80 TMC** to Krishna; FRL **150 ft**; **960 MW**, **7.20 lakh acres**; site in **West Godavari** (now Eluru).

=== Polavaram Ordinance and the Seven Mandals
- **Telangana came into existence on 2 June 2014** (appointed day).
- **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Ordinance, 2014** = "**Polavaram Ordinance**" – moved the Khammam **submergence villages and mandals to AP (Seemandhra)** to ease **resettlement of those displaced** by Polavaram.
- Base rule in the Act: Khammam → Telangana, excluding villages in **G.O.Ms. No. 111 (27 June 2005)**.
- The Bill replacing it was passed by the **Lok Sabha on 11 July 2014** (by voice vote) – not June 2016.
- **Seven mandals** moved from Khammam to AP:
- 1. Kukunoor
- 2. Velerupadu
- 3. VR Puram (Vararamachandrapuram)
- 4. Chintoor
- 5. Kunavaram
- 6. **Bhadrachalam (except the temple town)**
- 7. Burgampadu
- Telangana Assembly constituencies left with part of their territory in AP: **Pinapaka, Aswaraopeta and Bhadrachalam**.
- **Bhadrachalam town** (with the Sri Rama temple) stayed in **Telangana**; it lies on the bank of the Godavari whose surrounding mandals went to **Andhra** (the "Andhra side").
- Effect: AP's area **1,60,205 → 1,62,970 sq km**.
> Exam angle: **7 mandals**; temple town of Bhadrachalam stayed in Telangana; Lok Sabha **11 July 2014**.

=== Submergence, Inter-State Cases and R&R
| State | Concern |
|---|---|
| **Odisha** | Submergence; protective embankments; **original suit in SC** pending |
| Chhattisgarh | Submergence; protective embankments; SC case pending |
| **Telangana** | Backwater at Bhadrachalam; asked the SC to halt work and first review the project's **EIA** |
- **Maharashtra and Karnataka have not filed cases** against Polavaram (they share the 80 TMC diversion benefit).
- CAG (audit to Mar 2023): settle submergence issues with neighbours with PPA; build protective embankments (due by 2014-15).
- Jul 2026: ~189 villages in the submergence zone on alert during Godavari-Sabari floods.
# R&R
- Displaced: mainly **Koya and Kondh tribals**.
- R&R follows the **RFCTLARR Act, 2013**.
- **Aug 2026: 94% land acquisition, 52% relocation**; R&R colonies targeted Dec 2026.
- **₹1,943 crore** paid in three phases (Mar 2026); house-site value **₹1 lakh → ₹2 lakh** (Cabinet, 1 May 2026).

=== Funding and Cost
- **Cabinet decision (Special Assistance Measure, Sep 2016 / Mar 2017)**: the Centre funds **100% of the remaining cost of the irrigation component only**, **for the period starting from 1.4.2014, to the extent of the cost of the irrigation component on that date** – not drinking water or hydel, and not "as incurred".
- Irrigation component at 2013-14 prices ₹20,398.61 crore; balance as on 1.4.2014 ₹15,667.90 crore (Finance Ministry note).

=== Construction Milestones and Status
- **8 June 2017**: CM N. Chandrababu Naidu laid the foundation stone for the **cofferdam** (and the spill-channel bridge); diaphragm wall foundation was laid in Feb 2017.
- First contemplated **1941** – proposed as **"Ramapada Sagaram"** (Sir Sonti Venkataramamurthy); **GWDT set up 1969**, its **1980 award** cleared Polavaram; works **commenced 2005** under **Jalayagnam**.
| Date | Status |
|---|---|
| Dec 2024 | Overall 53.47%; head works 74.92%, RMC 93%, LMC 73% |
| Dec 2025 | Overall 60.8%; head works 81.61%, RMC 92.79%, LMC 73.40% |
| Aug 2026 | Civil works 90%, overall 77% |
- Done: spillway, radial gates, coffer dams, Gap-III concrete dam, buttress dam.
- **Diaphragm wall (1,372 m)** rebuilt from 18 Jan 2025 in **440 days** (announced 2 Apr 2026), ~₹1,000 crore extra.
- **Foreign expert panel** (6th visit, with CWC) reviewed Gap-1, Gap-2, D-wall (Jan 2026); **CWC cleared spillway safety** (Apr 2026).
- Target moved **Dec 2027 → March 2027**, before the 2027 **Godavari Pushkaralu**.
