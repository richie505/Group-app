=== Education Schemes – Amma Vodi, Thalliki Vandanam, Nadu-Nedu and Others
# Mother-aid schemes
| Scheme | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Jagananna Amma Vodi** (**January 2020**) | **Rs 15,000 a year** to **poor mothers (or guardians)** of school- and college-going children; an **education** scheme of **Andhra Pradesh** (one child per mother) |
| **Thalliki Vandanam** (2025-26, Super Six; replaced Amma Vodi) | **Rs 15,000 a year for every school-going child** (Classes I-XII, govt and private), paid to the mother; Rs 13,000 by DBT + Rs 2,000 for school upkeep |
# School infrastructure and learning
- **Mana Badi Nadu-Nedu** (launched **November 2019**): **school education** – modernising government school infrastructure to make government schools **competitive institutions**; not Amma Vodi.
- **Vidya Kanuka** (2020): free kit (uniforms, shoes, bag, books, dictionary) for Class 1-10. **Badikosta** (2017): **bicycles** to Class IX girls. **Akshara Andhra:** adult literacy. **Andhra Model of Education:** 100% enrolment. **TaRL:** language 21% → 47%, maths 20% → 50%. **LEAP:** responds to **ASER** learning gaps. **Jagananna Gorumudda:** enriched mid-day meal under **PM POSHAN**.
- **Happy Classroom:** **Isha Foundation**, slow learners, about **3,000** schools of **Chittoor**.
- **Zero-enrolment schools (U-DISE):** about **1,000**.

=== Health Insurance and Health Schemes
# Aarogyasri → Dr NTR Vaidya Seva
- **Origin:** **Rajiv Aarogyasri** (**April 2007**, BPL families) → **Dr YSR Aarogyasri** → **Dr NTR Vaidya Seva**.
- **2014-19 (NTR Vaidya Seva):** cover **up to Rs 2.5 lakh per BPL family** on a **floater** basis.
- **Now:** **BPL families and families with income up to Rs 5 lakh**; cover **up to Rs 25 lakh per family per year**, **no limit for cancer**; **3,257 procedures**; **1.43 crore** families benefited. Budget 2026-27: Rs 4,000 crore to the Trust.
- **Aarogya Raksha** (**7 April 2017**): for families **above the poverty line** (outside Aarogyasri), premium **Rs 1,200** per member a year, cover Rs 2 lakh.
- **YSR Aarogya Aasara:** **post-operative sustenance allowance** during recovery.
- **Universal Health Policy (2026):** up to **Rs 2.5 lakh per citizen** through insurers.

=== Health Facilities, Services and Spending
# Network
| Facility | Number |
|---|---|
| Village Health Clinics (village secretariat level) | **10,032** |
| Rural PHCs / urban PHCs | 1,293 / 559 |
| CHCs | 173 |

- **Family Physician / Family Doctor Programme:** **6 April 2023**; PHC Medical Officers visit Village Health Clinics every 15 days – integrated with PHCs. Telemedicine runs in AB-HWCs.
- **New medical colleges (Sept 2023):** **Vizianagaram, Eluru, Machilipatnam, Rajamahendravaram, Nandyal** – first of **17**; **Pulivendula and Adoni later**.
- **ESI:** **seven** new ESI hospitals approved **Feb 2021**, incl. a **100-bed** hospital at **Vizianagaram**.
- **Palasa** (Srikakulam): Kidney Research Centre for the **Uddanam** belt. **Talli Bidda Express:** free drop-home for mothers and newborns.
- **Doctors (2001-02, combined AP):** highest share after Hyderabad – **Visakhapatnam**.
- **Digital health:** ABDM (2021, **Ministry of Health and Family Welfare**); AP **tops** in ABHA coverage; **Sanjeevani** statewide from Aug 2026 – digital health profile + **ABHA ID**.
# Spending
- **Health 2021-22:** **Rs 13,830.44 crore**, up **21.1%**. Feb 2026 budget: health up **9.5%**.
- **Budget 2026-27:** total **Rs 3,32,205 crore**; **School Education Rs 32,308 crore = largest**; education **11.7%** (all-States average 14.5%); Health **Rs 19,306 crore = 4th**; debt service **18 paise** of each rupee.
