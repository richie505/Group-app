=== Special Category Status and the Special Assistance Measure
- **SCS** began in **1969** (Fifth FC era) – first **J&K, Assam, Nagaland**; CSS funded **90:10** (others **60:40**), concessional loans, tax breaks.
- **SCS is not in the APRA 2014** – it was a political assurance (Rajya Sabha, Feb 2014); the **14th FC** removed the special/general distinction in devolution.
- AP, **Bihar and Odisha** still demand SCS (as of January 2026).
- **Special Assistance Measure** approved by the Union Cabinet on **15 March 2017**:
  - **EAPs** signed 2015-16 to 2019-20: Centre repays loan and interest – bears **90%** of EAP cost.
  - **Polavaram:** Centre funds **100% of the remaining cost of the irrigation component** (cost as on 1 April 2014).
- Largest central transfer to AP in 2023-24 RE: **Finance Commission grants**.
> 90:10 = SCS CSS share; 90% = Centre's share of EAP cost.

=== Polavaram and the River Boards
- **Sec 90:** **Polavaram** declared a **national project**; Telangana's consent deemed given (Sec 89 = KWDT term – not Polavaram).
- Executed by the **Government of AP on behalf of GoI** (reimbursement); **CWC** clears designs – spillway blocks cleared April 2026.
- Irrigation **2.91 lakh ha** (East Godavari, Visakhapatnam, West Godavari, Krishna); Union Budget 2026-27: **₹3,320 crore**, storage up to **+41.15 m**; friction = cost revision, land acquisition, R&R.
- **Apex Council (Sec 84):** Union Water Resources Minister (chair) + both CMs; boards within **60 days** (Sec 85): **GRMB HQ in Telangana, KRMB HQ in AP**; Eleventh Schedule – no new project without Apex Council sanction; jurisdictions notified by the **Ministry of Jal Shakti** on 15 July **2021**.
~~ Sources: AP Reorganisation Act 2014 Gazette text ss.84-90, Eleventh Schedule; old app notes 3:134
