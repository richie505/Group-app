## 3. Repeats and trends
- **Most repeated:** Iltutmish (founder, capital, tanka-jital, iqta, Forty) ≈ 14; Balban titles/kingship ≈ 9; MBT (capital, token, Kohi, south, Holi) ≈ 10; Alauddin (market, cash army, half revenue, Sikandar-i-Sani) ≈ 12; Diwan-i-Arz ≈ 5; araghatta 4 (UPSC 2016 and 2025 each in two copies).
- **Key traps found:** Timur's PYQ keyed "Bahlul Lodi" (Timur came in 1398 under the last Tughlaq; option added); the old notes and one app MCQ said the horse post (ulaq) carried Khurasan fruit – Ibn Battuta says the foot post (dawa); NextGen 05 p.12 prints "copper tanka & silver jital" (reversed) and NextGen 03 p.56 "175 grams" (it is 175 grains); NextGen 03 p.61 says Alauddin's demand was one-fifth (it was one-half); Rocket 01 p.97 lists Mir Bakshi under the Khaljis (it is Mughal – UPSC 2019 answer "1 only").
- The old notes were heavy on **number-game / trap lists, the conversion-theory table, Deccan syncretism and Bahmani/Qutb Shahi parallels**; these are never asked in PYQs and are kept short for the own MCQs. PYQ weight is overwhelmingly on **ruler-specific "who did it" facts** (row 110) and **diwan / iqta / tax definitions** (rows 111-113).
