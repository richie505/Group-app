=== The Iqta System
- **Iqta** = the revenue of a territory **assigned** to an officer **in lieu of salary**; not ownership of land. Holder = **iqtadar / muqti** (wali of a province).
- **Origin:** an **Arabic / Caliphate** institution (land revenue to officers for limited periods; described in Nizam-ul-Mulk Tusi's **Siyasatnama**) – a **foreign Turkish import, not an ancient indigenous institution** (UPSC 2019).
- **First iqta in India:** **Hansi**, granted by **Muhammad Ghori** to **Qutbuddin Aibak**.
- **Iltutmish introduced** the iqta as a **systematic institution** of the Sultanate (iqtas to his Turkish officers).
- **Muqti's duties:** collect revenue, **maintain troops** from it, keep law and order, pay his men, and send the **surplus (fawazil)** to the treasury. Revenue was **not deposited directly in the Sultan's treasury**.
- **Fawazil** = the **excess amount** paid by the iqtadars to the exchequer after salary and troops.
- **A-R (UPSC 1998):** early Turkish administration was **essentially military** **because** the country was parcelled out as **iqtas among military leaders** – both true, R explains A.
| Sultan | Change |
|---|---|
| Iltutmish | Organised the system |
| Balban | Tried to resume old soldiers' iqtas; **khwajas** (auditors) checked muqtis |
| Alauddin, MBT | Strict control: income assessed, accountants, **cash pay**; enlarged **khalisa** (took back milk, idrarat, waqf lands) |
| Firoz | Relaxed control; iqtas became **hereditary**; **revived the jagir** – army efficiency fell |
- Iqtas were **transferable** (muqtis rotated every 3-4 years) until Firoz; the iqta is the ancestor of the Mughal **jagir and mansab** system.

=== Army – Cash Pay, Dagh and Huliya, Ranks
- **Alauddin Khalji:** **largest standing army**, recruited and **paid in cash** directly by the state – the **first Sultan to pay soldiers in cash**; raised it to protect the frontier against the **Mongols**.
- **Dagh** (**branding of horses**) and **huliya / chehra** (**descriptive roll** of soldiers) = **Alauddin** – to stop substandard horses and proxy soldiers at the muster; **Sher Shah** revived dagh.
- **Balban** set up the **Diwan-i-Arz** under the **Ariz-i-Mumalik**; the Sultan himself was commander-in-chief.
- **Ranks (decimal):** **sar-i-khail** (10 horsemen) → sipah-salar → amir → malik → **khan** (highest; commanded 10 maliks).
- **Turkish military gains:** **iron stirrup**, mounted archers, armour; horses imported from **West Asia**; war elephants were the **Sultan's monopoly**.

=== Provincial and Local Units
| Unit | Officer |
|---|---|
| **Iqta** (province) | **Muqti / wali / iqtadar** |
| **Shiq** | **Shiqdar** |
| **Pargana** | **Amil** (revenue collection) and chaudhri |
| **Village** | **Muqaddam / chaudhari** (headman), **khot**; **patwari** (accountant) |
- **Amil** = in-charge of **revenue collection** (UPSC 2019, statement 1 true).
- **Muqaddam or Chaudhari** = **village headman** (BPSC key); the **chaudhari** was the leading rural land-revenue official above the village (IAS 2004 key: "Chaudhary").
- **Khuts, muqaddams, chaudharis** collected kharaj for the state; **Alauddin** ended their privileges and cesses.

=== Land Assignments, Post and Intelligence
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Khalisa** | Crown land; revenue for the court and household |
| **Iqta** | Revenue assignment to officials |
| **Inam** | Grants to religious men |
| Mahrousa | Protected town |
| Madad-i-Mash | Mughal revenue-free grant to saints, religious institutions, mashaikhs |
| Jagir / Amaram / Mokasa | Mughal / **Vijayanagara** / **Marathas** |
- Sultanate territory = **Khalisa and Iqta** lands. Maratha **chauth = one-fourth**, **sardeshmukhi = one-tenth**. Tanka = Iltutmish's **silver coin**.
- **Post (Ibn Battuta):** **ulaq** = **horse post**; **dawa** = **foot post** (runners every third of a mile) – the **foot post, quicker than the horse post, was often used to bring the fruits of Khurasan** to India. **Dak chowki** = Sher Shah. **Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq** improved the post with runners and horsemen.
- **Barids** (news-writers) and **munhiyans** (secret spies) sent Alauddin daily market reports.
- **AP angle:** Kakatiya royal bodyguards = **Lenkas**.
