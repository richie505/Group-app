=== Neolithic – First Farming, Pit Dwellings, Dog Burials, Ash Mounds
# First farming
- **Cultivation of cereals began in the Neolithic.**
- **Mehrgarh** (Baluchistan, c. **7000 BCE**): **earliest agriculture** (wheat, barley) and settled life in the subcontinent; of dated options "7000 BC" is the start of farming.
- **Rice:** **Lahuradeva** (UP, c. **6500 BCE**, oldest paddy); **Koldihwa** (Belan valley, UP) – earlier thought oldest; sequence **Neolithic–Chalcolithic–Iron**.

|---|---|---|
| Burzahom | Kashmir (16 km NW of Srinagar) | **Pit dwellings**; **dog buried with master** |
| Gufkral | Kashmir | **Pit dwellings** (with Burzahom) |
| Chirand | Bihar | **Bone and antler tools** |
| Koldihwa | Belan valley, UP | Early rice |
| Sanganakallu | Ballari, Karnataka | **Ash mound** (also Utnur, Piklihal, Kupgal, Budihal) |
| Tekkalakota | Ballari, Karnataka | Hill-dwellers; excavated by **M. S. Nagaraja Rao** |
| Brahmagiri, Maski | Karnataka | Neolithic–megalithic sites |
- **Ash mounds:** burnt dung of cattle pens = seasonal camps of Neolithic herdsmen.
- **Eastern Neolithic** (Birbhanpur, Chirand, Kuchai, Sankarjang): **pointed-butt celts** and **shouldered axes**.
- **Pit dwellings** are NOT at Mehrgarh (mud-brick houses), Pallavaram (Palaeolithic tools) or Balakot (Harappan).

=== Mesolithic – Domestication, Burials and Ganga–Vindhya Sites
# Most-asked facts
- **Animal domestication began in the Mesolithic:** earliest evidence at **Adamgarh** (Narmadapuram, MP) and **Bagor** (Bhilwara, Rajasthan) – not Langhnaj, Birbhanpur or Chopani Mando.
- **Mahadaha** (Pratapgarh, UP): **bone and antler ornaments** (earrings, necklaces), bone implements, double burials.
- **Damdama** (Pratapgarh): 41 graves, five double burials and one **triple burial** (three skeletons in one grave).
- **Sarai Nahar Rai** (Pratapgarh): a grave with **four** skeletons.
- **Lekhahia** (Mirzapur, Vindhyas): **17 human skeletons** from rock shelter 1 – the most from any Vindhyan rock shelter.

> Mesolithic = microlithic stage; tools made by **flaking and chipping**.

=== Bhimbetka and Prehistoric Rock Art
- **Bhimbetka:** foothills of the Vindhyas, **Raisen** district near Bhopal (MP); discovered by **V. S. Wakankar in 1957-58**; **UNESCO World Heritage Site 2003**.
- **Most rock paintings in India** (up to 20 layers in places); Upper Palaeolithic art in about **300** caves and shelters; continuous use from Lower Palaeolithic to medieval times.
- Paintings are **Upper Palaeolithic and Mesolithic** – hunting scenes, animals, geometric patterns; Ajanta, Ellora, Bagh are historic-period cave art, not prehistoric.
- Most commonly painted animal: **deer**. A **petroglyph** is **carved** into rock; a painting is applied on the surface.
- 12 May 2026 study: after contact with the Chalcolithic farmers of **Malwa**, the Bhimbetka style became **static**.
- H. D. Sankalia, S. S. Nigam, R. Pandey, M. S. Vats, V. N. Misra are distractors – the discoverer is **Wakankar**.
~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 1 p.59-61; app notes 1:2 (sub 6)

=== Chalcolithic Cultures – Ahar, Kayatha, Malwa, Jorwe
- **Chalcolithic = Copper Age** (copper + stone tools); **first metal used = copper**.
- The **Chalcolithic** people are credited with the first wide use of **painted pottery** (a few Neolithic sites had some).
| Culture | Sites | Region | Date | Pottery / feature |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ahar-Banas | Ahar, Gilund, Balathal | Mewar, Banas valley (Rajasthan) | c. 2800/2100–1500 BCE | White-painted black-and-red ware; copper smelting |
| Kayatha | Kayatha (Chambal) | MP | 2450–1700 BCE | **Chocolate-slipped ware** |
| Malwa | **Navdatoli** (Narmada), Eran | **Madhya Pradesh** | 1900–1400 BCE | Malwa ware; Navdatoli dug by H. D. Sankalia |
| Jorwe | Jorwe, Nevasa, Daimabad, Inamgaon | Maharashtra (nuclear zone Pravara–Godavari) | 1400–700 BCE | **Painted red ware** (black-on-red) |
- **Contemporaries of the Harappans:** **Ahar-Banas** and Kayatha; Malwa and Jorwe are later. Order of start: Ahar → Kayatha → Malwa → Jorwe.
- **Burials in Maharashtra:** dead buried in urns **under the house floor, north to south**.
- **Daimabad** (Pravara): largest Jorwe site; **bronze chariot**, buffalo, elephant, rhinoceros; **symbolic burial**; southernmost Late Harappan site.
- **Jorwe end:** most sites abandoned gradually after c. 1000 BCE (drought); Inamgaon, Prakash continued (Late Jorwe) – "**suddenly deserted**" is the wrong statement (CDS 2012).

=== Stone Age Framework – Phases, Proto-history, Early Man
- **Order:** Lower → Middle → Upper Palaeolithic → Mesolithic → Neolithic → Chalcolithic → Iron Age (Megalithic).
- **Phases are defined by tool type and technology**; tools varied by region and cultures did **not** evolve uniformly or unilinearly.
- **Three-Age system** (Stone, Bronze, Iron): **C. J. Thomsen** (Copenhagen museum); "Palaeolithic/Neolithic" coined by Lubbock.
- **Proto-history:** writing exists but is undeciphered – the **Indus civilisation** is **proto-historic**.
- **Early man:** **Narmada Man** skull found by **Arun Sonakia, 1982** at **Hathnora** (Narmada valley, MP) – earliest human fossil of India; Bori (Maharashtra) artefacts c. 1.38 million years.
- **Cro-Magnon** = anatomically modern humans of the Upper Palaeolithic – later than Neanderthals and Heidelberg man.

=== Palaeolithic – Tool Techniques and Sites
| Stage | Technique / tools | Sites |
|---|---|---|
| Lower | **Acheulian** = handaxe-cleaver; **Soanian** = chopper-chopping | **Attirampakkam** (TN), Hunsgi and Isampur (Karnataka), Didwana (Rajasthan), Sohan valley (Pakistan Punjab), Belan valley |
| Middle | **Flake** tools; prepared-core **Levallois** technique; scrapers, borers, points | Nevasa, Bhimbetka, Narmada valley; **Sembur** (TN) |
| Upper | Blades, burins, bone tools | Kurnool caves, Jwalapuram, Patne, Baghor |
- **Attirampakkam** is an **Old Stone Age** (Lower Palaeolithic, Acheulian) site – not "Middle Stone Age" (Mesolithic).
- **Levallois** = Middle Palaeolithic prepared-core flake technique (Sembur); "Lacoid/Placoid/Hollois" are not techniques. No Neanderthal fossil has been found in India.
- Middle Palaeolithic = **flake** stage, not the microlithic stage.
~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 1 p.47-50; app notes 1:2 (sub 1)

=== Megaliths – Nature, Iron Age and New Finds
- **Megaliths:** graves with weapons, tools and pottery, marked by **big stones** (mega = large, lith = stone) – **burial sites** away from homes; not microliths, chalcoliths or monoliths.
- Belong to the **Iron Age**, with **black-and-red ware**; c. 1000 BCE – 2nd century BCE (PMF).
- Earliest AMS-dated iron: **Mayiladumparai** (Krishnagiri, TN) – **2172 BCE**.
- **Bihar:** first documentation of a megalithic site (July 2019) in the **Kaimur hills** (Hurmeta menhirs).
- **Kerala:** rock-cut burial chamber at **Panayal** (Kasaragod).
- 21 Jul 2026: **Karivalamvanthanallur** (Tenkasi, TN) passed straight from **Microlithic to Iron/Early Historic**, with no Neolithic or Chalcolithic.
