=== ICDS – Services, Beneficiaries, Functionaries and Funding
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Launch | **2 October 1975** (5th Plan), on an experimental basis in 33 blocks; universalised **2006** |
| Ministry | **Ministry of Women and Child Development** |
| Nature | Centrally sponsored, implemented by States/UTs – **shared funding by Centre and States** (60:40 general States; **supplementary nutrition 50:50**) – not fully Central, State or externally aided |
| Convergence | **Ministry of Health (NHM)** and **Ministry of Women and Child Development** |
| Beneficiaries | Children **0–6 years**, **pregnant women and lactating mothers**; adolescent girls via the Scheme for Adolescent Girls |
| Current form | **Anganwadi Services** under **Saksham Anganwadi and Poshan 2.0** |
| AP start (1975) | **Utnoor** (Adilabad, tribal) and **Kambadur** (Anantapur) projects |
# The six services
| Service | Delivered through |
|---|---|
| **Supplementary Nutrition** (SNP) | **Anganwadi Centre** |
| **Pre-school non-formal education** (children 3–6) | Anganwadi Centre |
| **Nutrition and health education** (women 15–45) | Anganwadi Centre |
| **Immunisation** | Health system (NHM) |
| **Health check-up** | Health system |
| **Referral services** | Health system |
- **Not ICDS services**: **cash maternity benefit** (PMMVY), school meals (PM POSHAN), **100-day job guarantee** (MGNREGA).
- **Non-formal (pre-school) education** beneficiaries: children **3–6**; nutrition and health education for **women 15–45**, pregnant and lactating mothers.
# Functionaries
| Functionary | Role |
|---|---|
| **Anganwadi Worker (AWW)** | **Grassroots, honorary** ICDS functionary; runs the **Anganwadi** (the government child-care centre in rural India) – the main local-level functionary |
| Anganwadi Helper | Cooks and serves meals |
| Lady Supervisor | Supervises a cluster of AWCs |
| **CDPO** | **Block-level** ICDS officer – trained by **NIPCCD** |
- **NIPCCD** (National Institute of Public Cooperation and Child Development) trains ICDS staff – itself trains the **CDPOs**; supervisors and AWWs are trained at middle-level and Anganwadi training centres.
- **ANM and ASHA belong to NHM**, not ICDS; MMU = mobile medical unit.
- **ICDS at 50 (2025)**: about **14 lakh** AWCs. **AP**: 55,746 AWCs in **257 ICDS projects** (AP SES 2025-26).
- **One Nation One Anganwadi**: **migrant families** moving to another State keep ICDS benefits for children below 6 and pregnant/lactating women (APPSC 2023).
- **Mahila Shakti Kendras** (Budget 2017-18): to be set up in **14 lakh Anganwadi Centres**.

=== POSHAN Abhiyaan, Poshan 2.0 and Nutrition Campaigns
# POSHAN Abhiyaan
- **Prime Minister's Overarching Scheme for Holistic Nourishment** (earlier **National Nutrition Mission**) – the **2018 flagship** for holistic nutrition of children, adolescents, pregnant women and lactating mothers; launched **8 March 2018** at **Jhunjhunu, Rajasthan**. Goal: **Suposhit Bharat**, a **Jan Andolan**.
- Targets: reduce **stunting**, undernutrition, **anaemia**, low birth weight; stunting to **25% by 2022** ("Mission 25 by 2022").
- **Objectives (UPSC 2017)**: awareness of malnutrition among pregnant women and lactating mothers; reduce **anaemia** in young children, adolescent girls and women – **not** promoting millets/unpolished rice or poultry eggs.
# Campaigns and tools
| Campaign / tool | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Rashtriya Poshan Maah** | **September**; **5th in 2022** (celebrated through Gram Panchayats as "**Poshan Panchayats**"), **6th in 2023**, **9th in 2026** at **Varanasi** |
| **Poshan Pakhwada** | A **fortnight**, March–April; **8th edition April 2026** on brain development in the first six years |
| Poshan Tracker | Digital monitoring of Anganwadi services – not a panic button (that is **112 India**, MHA – single-number police, medical, fire dispatch) |
| Poshan Bhi Padhai Bhi | ECCE in Anganwadis through play-based pedagogy |
| **Poshan Vatika** | **Nutrition gardens** at Anganwadi centres |
| **Bhartiya Poshan Krishi Kosh** | Announced **November 2019** by the **Ministry of Women and Child Development** (with the Gates Foundation) – a repository of diverse crops by agro-climatic zone |
| **Tikki Mausi** | A **mascot** (Odisha WCD dept with UNICEF, 2019) for child and women's nutrition |
# Saksham Anganwadi and Poshan 2.0 (2021)
- Introduced in **2021** (MWCD, CSS, 2021-22 to 2025-26); merged **Anganwadi Services**, **POSHAN Abhiyaan** and the **Scheme for Adolescent Girls** (girls **14–18** in Aspirational Districts and NER); **PM POSHAN is not merged**. One of MWCD's three umbrella missions.
- Funding: **60:40**; **90:10** NE, Himalayan States, J&K; 100% UTs without legislature.
- Three verticals: nutrition support; **ECCE (3–6 years)**; Anganwadi infrastructure (not school mid-day meals). **2 lakh** AWCs to become Saksham Anganwadis at **40,000 a year**.

=== Mid-Day Meal to PM POSHAN
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|

| First State | **Tamil Nadu** |

=== Early Childhood Care and Education and Child Development
# ECCE
- **Article 45** (after the 86th Amendment, 2002): **early childhood care and education** for all children until they complete **six years**.
- National ECCE Policy **2013**. **NEP 2020 foundational stage**: ages **3–8** = 3 years Anganwadi/pre-school + **Classes 1–2**.
- **NCERT's National Curricular and Pedagogical Framework for ECCE** (children up to 8) is in **two parts** (0–3 and 3–8).
- **NCF for the Foundational Stage (3–8)** – **Panchakosha**: **Sharirik** (physical), **Pranik** (life energy), **Manasik** (mental), **Bauddhik** (intellectual), **Chaitsik** (spiritual) vikas – **Dharmik vikas is not one**.
- **NISHTHA 4.0** = online teacher training for **ECCE** (NISHTHA 1.0–3.0: elementary, secondary, FLN).
- **Montessori** method: curriculum **child-centred**, based on the child's natural abilities and self-paced activity.
# Child development and learning (psychology)
| Concept | Fact |
|---|---|
| **2–6 years** (early childhood) | **Pre-gang age** (Hurlock); 6–12 = gang age |
| **Adolescence** | "**Stress and storm**" – G. **Stanley Hall** |
| **IQ** | Mental age ÷ chronological age × 100 → 10/15 × 100 ≈ **67** |
| **Punishment** | **Presenting a negative stimulus or removing a positive one** |
| Reinforcement | Motivated behaviour should be followed by **reward** |
| Counselling | **Non-directive** = **client-centred** (Carl Rogers); directive = counsellor-centred |
| **Authoritarian parenting** | **Parent strictly controls** all aspects of the child's life; little warmth |
| **Psychomotor domain** (Harrow) | Begins with **reflex movements**; analysis = cognitive, receiving/character = affective |

=== Girl Child and Child Marriage
# Child marriage law
- **Prohibition of Child Marriage Act, 2006**: minimum age **18 (girls) / 21 (boys)** – the minimum marriageable age of girls under law is **18**. Adult male contracting it: RI up to **2 years** + fine up to **₹1 lakh**. Child marriage is **voidable**; petition for annulment within **two years of attaining majority**. Child Marriage Prohibition Officers appointed by **State Governments**.
- **PCM (Amendment) Bill, 2021**: women's age to **21**; annulment window **two → five years** after majority; **overrides any other law, custom or practice**; amends the 2006 Act → statements A, C, D correct. Referred to a Standing Committee; **lapsed** with the 17th Lok Sabha. **Jaya Jaitly Task Force (2020)** recommended it.
- **Allahabad HC (1 July 2026)**: PCMA age limits apply to all citizens **irrespective of religion**. **NFHS-5**: **23.3%** of women 20–24 married before 18; highest **West Bengal**.
- **ALIVA**: data-driven model to end child marriage – **Nayagarh district, Odisha**.

=== Child Protection – Mission Vatsalya, Creches and Balwadis
- **Mission Vatsalya** (MWCD, CSS, **2021-22**): child protection, **happy childhood** and development aligned to the **SDGs** (not Laalana, Bala Vihar or Balsamrudhi); **subsumed ICPS**; outlay **₹10,916 crore**; CHILDLINE 1098 with ERSS-112.
| Component | Norm |
|---|---|
| Sponsorship / foster care | **₹4,000** per child per month |
| Aftercare | **18–21** (extendable to 23) |
- **PM CARES for Children (2021)**: COVID-19 orphans; **₹10 lakh** at **23**. JJ Amendment 2021: **District Magistrate** focal point for adoption.
- **Creches**: first started by the **Central Social Welfare Board** for children **0–5** of working mothers (agricultural, construction work) → Rajiv Gandhi National Creche Scheme → **Palna** (**6 months–6 years**, open whether or not the mother is employed).
- **Balwadi Nutrition Programme**: **supplementary nutrition** to children **3–5 years** in **Balwadis** and/or creches.
- **Green Passage** (Odisha, 2020): **orphan students** – free higher education. **Mission DAKSH** (Bihar): remedial classes to minimise **school dropouts**.
- **National Bravery Award (ICCW)**: **Bharat Award** (announced **January 2017**) posthumously to **Tarh Peeju** (8, Arunachal Pradesh), who died saving two friends from drowning.
