## SUB 1 | POSHAN Abhiyaan, Poshan 2.0 and Nutrition Campaigns
[1:0] # POSHAN Abhiyaan
[1:1] - **Prime Minister's Overarching Scheme for Holistic Nourishment** (earlier **National Nutrition Mission**) – the **2018 flagship** for holistic nutrition of children, adolescents, pregnant women and lactating mothers; launched **8 March 2018** at **Jhunjhunu, Rajasthan**. Goal: **Suposhit Bharat**, a **Jan Andolan**.
[1:2] - Targets: reduce **stunting**, undernutrition, **anaemia**, low birth weight; stunting to **25% by 2022** ("Mission 25 by 2022").
[1:3] - **Objectives (UPSC 2017)**: awareness of malnutrition among pregnant women and lactating mothers; reduce **anaemia** in young children, adolescent girls and women – **not** promoting millets/unpolished rice or poultry eggs.
[1:4] # Campaigns and tools
[1:5] TABLE head: Campaign / tool | Fact
  [1:5.0] Rashtriya Poshan Maah | September; 5th in 2022 (celebrated through Gram Panchayats as "Poshan Panchayats"), 6th in 2023, 9th in 2026 at Varanasi
  [1:5.1] Poshan Pakhwada | A fortnight, March–April; 8th edition April 2026 on brain development in the first six years
  [1:5.2] Poshan Tracker | Digital monitoring of Anganwadi services – not a panic button (that is 112 India, MHA – single-number police, medical, fire dispatch)
  [1:5.3] Poshan Bhi Padhai Bhi | ECCE in Anganwadis through play-based pedagogy
  [1:5.4] Poshan Vatika | Nutrition gardens at Anganwadi centres

[1:6] # Saksham Anganwadi and Poshan 2.0 (2021)
[1:7] - Merged **Anganwadi Services**, **POSHAN Abhiyaan** and the **Scheme for Adolescent Girls** (girls **14–18** in Aspirational Districts and NER); **PM POSHAN is not merged**. One of MWCD's three umbrella missions.

[1:9] - Three verticals: nutrition support; **ECCE (3–6 years)**; Anganwadi infrastructure (not school mid-day meals). **2 lakh** AWCs to become Saksham Anganwadis at **40,000 a year**.

## SUB 3 | Early Childhood Care and Education and Child Development
[3:0] # ECCE
[3:1] - **Article 45** (after the 86th Amendment, 2002): **early childhood care and education** for all children until they complete **six years**.
[3:2] - National ECCE Policy **2013**. **NEP 2020 foundational stage**: ages **3–8** = 3 years Anganwadi/pre-school + **Classes 1–2**.
[3:3] - **NCERT's National Curricular and Pedagogical Framework for ECCE** (children up to 8) is in **two parts** (0–3 and 3–8).
[3:4] - **NCF for the Foundational Stage (3–8)** – **Panchakosha**: **Sharirik** (physical), **Pranik** (life energy), **Manasik** (mental), **Bauddhik** (intellectual), **Chaitsik** (spiritual) vikas – **Dharmik vikas is not one**.
[3:5] - **NISHTHA 4.0** = online teacher training for **ECCE** (NISHTHA 1.0–3.0: elementary, secondary, FLN).
[3:6] - **Montessori** method: curriculum **child-centred**, based on the child's natural abilities and self-paced activity.

## SUB 5 | Girl Child and Child Marriage
[5:0] # Child marriage law
[5:1] - **Prohibition of Child Marriage Act, 2006**: minimum age **18 (girls) / 21 (boys)** – the minimum marriageable age of girls under law is **18**. Adult male contracting it: RI up to **2 years** + fine up to **₹1 lakh**. Child marriage is **voidable**; petition for annulment within **two years of attaining majority**. Child Marriage Prohibition Officers appointed by **State Governments**.
[5:2] - **PCM (Amendment) Bill, 2021**: women's age to **21**; annulment window **two → five years** after majority; **overrides any other law, custom or practice**; amends the 2006 Act → statements A, C, D correct. Referred to a Standing Committee; **lapsed** with the 17th Lok Sabha. **Jaya Jaitly Task Force (2020)** recommended it.
[5:3] - **Allahabad HC (1 July 2026)**: PCMA age limits apply to all citizens **irrespective of religion**. **NFHS-5**: **23.3%** of women 20–24 married before 18; highest **West Bengal**.
[5:4] - **ALIVA**: data-driven model to end child marriage – **Nayagarh district, Odisha**.
