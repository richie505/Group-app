## SUB 0 | Krishna Patrika and Mutnuri Krishna Rao
[0:0] # Krishna Patrika (1902)
[0:1] - **Founded:** **February 1902** (1 Feb per NextGen; 2 Feb per Wikipedia), at **Machilipatnam (Bandar)**, as a **weekly** – in the **20th century**, not the 18th.
[0:2] - **Founders:** **Konda Venkatappayya** and **Dasu Narayana Rao** – not Veeresalingam, Duggirala, Kasinathuni or Mutnuri.
[0:3] - **First editor:** **Konda Venkatappayya** (edited till 1905; NextGen lists Dasu Narayana as first editor).
[0:4] - **Mutnuri Krishna Rao:** sub-editor from 1902, **editor from 1907 to his death in 1945** – the **second and most famous editor**, "**largely responsible for its success**" (FRO; APPSC GS 2024) – not Konda, Dasu Narayana Rao or Piratla Venkateswarlu.
[0:5]   - Made Krishna Patrika a **political weekly** and a **hub for freedom fighters**; called the **doyen of Telugu journalism**.
[0:6]   - Wrote scholarly editorials attacking British government spending; also ran the magazine **Andhra Bharati** for some time.
[0:7] - **Statements:** "Krishna Patrika was founded by Veeresalingam" – wrong; "edited by Mutnuri Krishna Rao" – right.
[0:8] - **Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya:** set up the **Andhra Vidya Peetha Goshti** (Chirala, 1920) – true in the statement questions.
[0:9] > Exam angle: founder Konda Venkatappayya (×7) vs editor Mutnuri (×7) – the core pair of this row.

## SUB 1 | Kasinathuni Nageswara Rao – Andhra Patrika and Bharati
[1:0] - **Life:** **1867–1947**; founded the **Amrutanjan** business and used the wealth for public work.
[1:1] - **Papers:** **Andhra Patrika** (9 Sept **1908**, Bombay; Telugu daily from 1914, Madras) – founder and first editor; **Bharati** (**1924**) – a **literary and cultural monthly** – not 1920-22.
[1:2] - **Publishing house:** **Andhra Granthamala**, named to popularise the word "Andhra".
[1:3] - **Library role:** any group of **ten youths** opening a village library got his paper, books and *Bharati* **free**; finished the **catalogue of printed Telugu books** begun by **Velidanda Hanumantha Rao**.
[1:4] - **Granthalayodyama Trimurthulu:** Kasinathuni + Ayyanki + Gadicherla.
[1:5] - **Sribagh:** his house in Madras; the **Sribagh Pact** was signed there on **16 November 1937**.
[1:6] TABLE head: Title | Person
  [1:6.0] Desoddharaka | Kasinathuni Nageswara Rao
  [1:6.1] Deshabandhu | C.R. Das
  [1:6.2] Deenabandhu | C.F. Andrews
  [1:6.3] Lokmanya | Tilak
  [1:6.4] Doyen of Telugu journalism | Mutnuri Krishna Rao (not Kasinathuni)
[1:7] - **Andhra Patrika** = founded by Kasinathuni; it was not the first political weekly (that was **Andhra Prakasika**).

[2:1] - **Pairs (APPSC):** Krishna Patrika–Konda Venkatappayya, Andhra Patrika–Kasinathuni, Swarajya–Gadicherla – all three correct.
[2:2] - **The Crescent:** English weekly of **Gazulu Lakshminarasu Chetty** – not Mutnuri.
[2:3] - **Kotamraju Punnaiah:** Telugu journalist, editor of **The Sindh Observer, Karachi** from 1920 (about 25 years), hailed as "architect of modern Sindh"; a **Karachi street** bears his name – not C.Y. Chintamani or M. Chalapathi Rau.
[2:4] - **Konda Venkatappayya ("Desabhakta"):** booklet **Andhrodyamamu** (1912) on the neglect of the Telugu areas; also Srusti Vicharam, an autobiography.
[2:5] - **Andhra Jateeya Kalasala (Andhra National College), Machilipatnam:** founded **20 Feb 1910** by **Kopalle Hanumantha Rao** (founder-principal), inaugurated by Konda Venkatappayya (president); later run with **Mutnuri Krishna Rao** and Pattabhi Sitaramayya – not Mutnuri, Gadicherla or Madapati as founder.
[2:6] - **Editors as institution-builders:** Kasinathuni – Amrutanjan, Andhra Granthamala; Gadicherla – Edward Corporation Library, Nandyal; Chilukuri Veerabhadra Rao – Bala Bharati Samithi; Khasa Subba Rao – Swatantra, Swarajya (English).

## SUB 3 | Mutnuri and Bipin Chandra Pal's Tour (1907)
[3:0] - **Organiser:** **Mutnuri Krishna Rao** organised and invited Bipin Chandra Pal's tour of Andhra (**April 1907**) – not T. Prakasam, Konda Venkatappayya or Gadicherla.
[3:1] - **Lectures:** Kakinada (17 Apr 1907); **five lectures at Rajahmundry** (19-24 Apr 1907); Machilipatnam.
[3:2] - **Bala Bharati Samithi** (Feb 1907, Rajahmundry; Chilukuri Veerabhadra Rao, Gante Laxmanna, Unguturi Sriramulu) took part in organising the Rajahmundry leg.
[3:3] - **Rajahmundry College incident (1907):** Gadicherla expelled / blacklisted from government jobs.
[3:4] > Exam angle: Pal's tour = Mutnuri (×3).
