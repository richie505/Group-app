## SUB 0 | Sri Krishnadevaraya Andhra Bhasha Nilayam and the Hyderabad Libraries
[0:0] - **Sri Krishnadevaraya Andhra Bhasha Nilayam (SKDABN):** founded **1901** in **Hyderabad** by **Komarraju (Venkata) Lakshmana Rao** with associates, at the home of **Ravichettu Ranga Rao** – the **first Telugu library** of Hyderabad (Telangana) – not Machilipatnam, Vijayawada, Rajahmundry or **Kovvur 1916**; not Madapati, Adiraju or Burgula.
[0:1] - **Komarraju Lakshmana Rao:** **leader of the library movement** in Hyderabad State – "Father of the Library Movement", "Father of Research of Andhra History", **Andhra Charitra Pitamaha** (not Ravi Narayana Reddy or Kaloji).
[0:2]   - His libraries: Hyderabad (**1901**), **Rajaraja Narendra Andhra Bhasha Nilayam, Hanamkonda (1904)**, Secunderabad (1905).
[0:3] - **Andhra Samvardhani Granthalayam:** **Secunderabad, 1905**.
[0:4] - **Andhra Saraswati Parishad:** Hyderabad, 1922.
[0:5] - Early Telugu libraries of Hyderabad State – SKDABN (1901), Andhra Samvardhani (1905), Vignana Chandrika Mandali (1906) – later the base for the **Andhra Jana Sangham** and the **Andhra Mahasabha** (first session **Jogipet**).
[0:6] - **"Andhra History and Culture":** authorship not established (see the PYQ note) – Komarraju's own work is *Andhrula Charitra*; Suravaram Pratapa Reddy's is *Andhrula Sanghika Charitra*.
[0:7] > Exam angle: SKDABN = Hyderabad, 1901, Komarraju (×12, the most-asked library fact).

## SUB 1 | Vignana Chandrika Mandali and Andhrula Charitramu
[1:0] - **Vignana Chandrika Mandali (Granthamandali / Granthamala):** founded **1906** at **Hyderabad** by **Komarraju (K.V.) Lakshmana Rao** with the support of the **Munagala Zamindar**; co-founders / secretaries included **Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao** and **Ayyadevara Kaleswara Rao** – not Veeresalingam, Burgula, Madapati or Suravaram; some papers say 1905.
[1:1] - **Andhrula Charitramu (History of the Andhras):** published by the Mandali in **1910** (written by **Chilukuri Veerabhadra Rao**) – not Andhra Patrika or Krishna Patrika; it roused interest in Andhra history before the Andhra movement.
[1:2] > Exam angle: 1910 = Andhrula Charitramu (×3); Mandali founder = Komarraju (×4).

## SUB 3 | Andhra Desa Library Association, Conferences, Laws and Innovations
[3:0] # Organised movement
[3:1] - **By 1913:** about **125 libraries**; call for a conference by **Ayyanki** and **Suri Venkata Narasimha** through Andhra Patrika.
[3:2] - **First Andhra library conference:** **Vijayawada, 10 April 1913** – about **200 delegates** from 60 libraries (incl. the Nizam's State); presided by **Chilakamarti Lakshmi Narasimham** ("Air is available freely to all; knowledge too should be available likewise").
[3:3] - **Andhra Desa Library Association (Andhra Desa Grantha Bhandagara Sangham):** **1914, Vijayawada** – the **first (state-level) library association in India**; first president **Mocherla Ramachandra Rao**; secretaries Ayyanki and Nalam Krishnarao.
[3:4] - **Grandhalaya Sarvasvam:** started by the ADLA in **1915** – Telugu **quarterly**, the first Telugu library-science journal; revived Jan 1948 as *Andhra Granthalayam* (editor P. Rajasekharam).
[3:5] - **All India Public Library Conference:** Madras, **1919** → All India Public Library Association (1919).
[3:6] - Libraries were **legal centres of political education** under colonial curbs.
