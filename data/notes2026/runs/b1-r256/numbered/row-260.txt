## SUB 0 | Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao – Rayalaseema, Swarajya and Jail
[0:0] # "Rayalaseema"
[0:1] - **Named the Ceded Districts (Dattamandalam) "Rayalaseema"** at the **1928 Nandyal Andhra Mahasabha** – after the Vijayanagara Rayas – not Sri Krishnadevaraya, Munro, Hyder Ali, Peddana or Bezawada Gopala Reddy.
[0:2] - **Sribagh Pact (1937):** signed it for Rayalaseema.
[0:3] # Journalist and prisoner
[0:4] - **Papers:** **Swarajya (1907, Vijayawada; earlier Sri Venkateswara Patrika)**, Navayugam, Andhra Patrika (editor 1916-18), Matru Seva, Panchayati Rajyam – **not Grandhalaya Sarvasvam** (Ayyanki's journal).
[0:5] - **Jailed for sedition in 1908** (Section 124A, 3 years) for Swarajya writings – the **first Telugu journalist / political prisoner jailed for sedition (treason) in South India** – not Kasinathuni, Challa Seshagiri Rao or Sivalenka Sambhu Prasad.
[0:6] - **Vandemataram:** the student expelled from the **Rajahmundry Teacher Training College (1907)** and blacklisted from government jobs.
[0:7] - **Home Rule:** started the Home Rule League branches in Andhra; its secretary; **linked the library movement to the Home Rule League**.
[0:8] # Library movement
[0:9] - **Granthalayodyama Trimurthulu:** Ayyanki (**built institutions**), **Gadicherla (mobilised people – the "radical mobiliser")**, Kasinathuni (**money and political backing**).
[0:10] - **Edward Corporation Library, Nandyal** (now Victoria Reading Room) – with **Kaderbad Narasinga Rao**.
[0:11] - **President** of the library association in **1946**; **Komma Sitaramayya's** gift bought a building → **Sarvottama Bhavan**, opened **1949** in his honour.
[0:12] > Exam angle: Rayalaseema name (×4); first Telugu journalist jailed for sedition (×2).

## SUB 1 | Ayyanki Venkata Ramanayya and Pathuri Nagabhushanam
[1:0] # Ayyanki (Iyyanki) Venkata Ramanayya
[1:1] - **Started (led) the library movement** – "**Father of the Library Movement**"; said "**Library Movement in Andhra is Swayambhu**"; slogan "Public libraries are temples of knowledge" – not Chilukuri Veerabhadra Rao or Veeresalingam.
[1:2] - **Secretary, Rama Mohana Library**, Vijayawada (1911); founder-secretary of the **ADLA (1914)**; edited ***Grandhalaya Sarvasvam*** (1915).
[1:3] - **Conceived the library pilgrimage** (village tours, 1934-48).
[1:4] - Organised the All India Public Library Conference (Madras, Nov 1919); General Secretary of the All India Public Library Association at its second conference (**Dec 1923**).
[1:5] - Associations he helped found, in order: ADLA (1914) → **All India Public Library Association (1919)** → **Bengal (1925)** → **Madras (1928)** → **Punjab (1929)**; the **Indian Library Association** was formed at Calcutta in **1933**.
[1:6] TABLE head: Title / award | Person
  [1:6.0] Granthalaya Pitamaha (from the Maharaja of Baroda); Saraswati Samrajya Pratishtapanacharya; Granthala Visaradha; first Indian with the Kaula Gold Medal; Padma Shri | Ayyanki
  [1:6.1] Andhra Desa Granthalayodyama Roopasilpi; "University of Humanity", "Walking Library" (from Karunasri – Jandhyala Papayya Sastri); Kala Prapoorna (Andhra University, 1975) | Pathuri Nagabhushanam
  [1:6.2] Library Gandhi | Velaga Venkatappayya – led tours in Bhimavaram, Tadepalligudem, Kovvur and Guntur taluks
[1:7] # Pathuri (Paturi) Nagabhushanam
[1:8] - **Pioneer of the boat library service** – on passenger boats on the **Bank Canal**, **1935-42** (seven years).
[1:9] - Started the **Bala Saraswati Bhandaramu** village library as a schoolboy; as Secretary of the **Guntur Library Association** set up libraries in about **160 villages**.
[1:10] - Member of the drafting committee of the **AP Public Libraries Act (1960)**.
[1:11] # Other helpers
[1:12] - **Madras Public Libraries Bill, 1938:** Vavilala Gopalakrishnayya – not Ayyanki.
[1:13] - **Vavilala Ramaswamy Sastrulu & Sons:** books to the Ramamohan Library and free copies to village libraries; Andhra Granthamala (Kasinathuni) also supplied books.
[1:14] > Exam angle: who started the library movement = Ayyanki; boat library = Pathuri.
