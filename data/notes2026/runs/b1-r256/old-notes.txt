## SUB 2 | Mutnuri Krishna Rao
[258:2:0] - Editor of **Krishna Patrika** from **1907 to 1945** (died 25 June 1945); called the **doyen of Telugu journalism**.
[258:2:1] - Made Krishna Patrika a **political weekly** and a **hub for freedom fighters**.
[258:2:2] - Wrote scholarly editorials attacking British government spending; also ran the magazine **Andhra Bharati** for some time.
[258:2:3] - Invited extremist leader **Bipin Chandra Pal** to Andhra (**April 1907**).
[258:2:4] APPSC GS 2024: Mutnuri was "largely responsible" for the success of Krishna Patrika.
## SUB 3 | Kasinathuni Nageswara Rao
[258:3:0] # Life and Businesses
[258:3:1] - Lived **1867–1947**.
[258:3:2] - Founded the **Amrutanjan** business; used the wealth for public work (Gandhian **trusteeship**).
[258:3:3] - Publishing house **Andhra Granthamala**, named to popularise the word "**Andhra**".
[258:3:4] - Papers: **Andhra Patrika (1908)**, **Bharati (1924)**.
[258:3:5] # Library Movement Role
[258:3:6] - Any group of **ten youths** opening a village library got his newspaper, books and *Bharati* **free**.
[258:3:7] - Finished and published the **catalogue of printed Telugu books** begun by Velidanda Hanumantha Rao.
[258:3:8] - **Granthalayodyama Trimurthulu**: Kasinathuni + **Ayyanki** (Venkata Ramanaiah) + **Gadicherla**.

## SUB 4 | Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao
[258:4:0] - Editor of **Swarajya (1907)**.
[258:4:1] - **Jailed for sedition in 1908** for the article "**Veerabali**"; the **first Telugu journalist / political prisoner** so jailed in South India.
[258:4:2] - **Blacklisted** from government jobs after the **1907 Rajahmundry College incident**.
[258:4:3] - With **Kaderbad Narasinga Rao**, founded the **Edward Corporation Library, Nandyal** (now **Victoria Reading Room**).
[258:4:4] Edward Corporation Library is at Nandyal, not Kurnool.

=== NOTES 1:259 | Library Movement in Andhra – Origin, Growth and Role in Awakening
## SUB 0 | Idea and Significance
[259:0:0] - "**Air is available freely to all. Knowledge too should be available likewise.**" – **Chilakamarti Lakshmi Narasimham**.
[259:0:1] - Under colonial curbs, libraries were **legal centres of political education**.
[259:0:2] - They built the **Telugu identity** behind the separate Andhra statehood demand.
[259:0:3] - Editors (Andhra Patrika, Krishna Patrika) publicised and funded the movement.
