=== Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao – Rayalaseema, Swarajya and Jail
# "Rayalaseema"
- **Named the Ceded Districts (Dattamandalam) "Rayalaseema"** at the **1928 Nandyal Andhra Mahasabha** – after the Vijayanagara Rayas – not Sri Krishnadevaraya, Munro, Hyder Ali, Peddana or Bezawada Gopala Reddy.
- **Sribagh Pact (1937):** signed it for Rayalaseema.
# Journalist and prisoner
- **Papers:** **Swarajya (1907, Vijayawada; earlier Sri Venkateswara Patrika)**, Navayugam, Andhra Patrika (editor 1916-18), Matru Seva, Panchayati Rajyam – **not Grandhalaya Sarvasvam** (Ayyanki's journal).
- **Jailed for sedition in 1908** (Section 124A, 3 years) for Swarajya writings – the **first Telugu journalist / political prisoner jailed for sedition (treason) in South India** – not Kasinathuni, Challa Seshagiri Rao or Sivalenka Sambhu Prasad.
- **Vandemataram:** the student expelled from the **Rajahmundry Teacher Training College (1907)** and blacklisted from government jobs.
- **Home Rule:** started the Home Rule League branches in Andhra; its secretary; **linked the library movement to the Home Rule League**.
# Library movement
- **Granthalayodyama Trimurthulu:** Ayyanki (**built institutions**), **Gadicherla (mobilised people – the "radical mobiliser")**, Kasinathuni (**money and political backing**).
- **Edward Corporation Library, Nandyal** (now Victoria Reading Room) – with **Kaderbad Narasinga Rao**.
- **President** of the library association in **1946**; **Komma Sitaramayya's** gift bought a building → **Sarvottama Bhavan**, opened **1949** in his honour.
> Exam angle: Rayalaseema name (×4); first Telugu journalist jailed for sedition (×2).

=== Ayyanki Venkata Ramanayya and Pathuri Nagabhushanam
# Ayyanki (Iyyanki) Venkata Ramanayya
- **Started (led) the library movement** – "**Father of the Library Movement**"; said "**Library Movement in Andhra is Swayambhu**"; slogan "Public libraries are temples of knowledge" – not Chilukuri Veerabhadra Rao or Veeresalingam.
- **Secretary, Rama Mohana Library**, Vijayawada (1911); founder-secretary of the **ADLA (1914)**; edited ***Grandhalaya Sarvasvam*** (1915).
- **Conceived the library pilgrimage** (village tours, 1934-48).
- Organised the All India Public Library Conference (Madras, Nov 1919); General Secretary of the All India Public Library Association at its second conference (**Dec 1923**).
- Associations he helped found, in order: ADLA (1914) → **All India Public Library Association (1919)** → **Bengal (1925)** → **Madras (1928)** → **Punjab (1929)**; the **Indian Library Association** was formed at Calcutta in **1933**.

|---|---|
| Granthalaya Pitamaha (from the **Maharaja of Baroda**); Saraswati Samrajya Pratishtapanacharya; Granthala Visaradha; **first Indian with the Kaula Gold Medal**; **Padma Shri** | **Ayyanki** |
| **Andhra Desa Granthalayodyama Roopasilpi**; "University of Humanity", "Walking Library" (from **Karunasri – Jandhyala Papayya Sastri**); **Kala Prapoorna** (Andhra University, 1975) | **Pathuri Nagabhushanam** |
| **Library Gandhi** | **Velaga Venkatappayya** – led tours in Bhimavaram, Tadepalligudem, Kovvur and Guntur taluks |
# Pathuri (Paturi) Nagabhushanam
- **Pioneer of the boat library service** – on passenger boats on the **Bank Canal**, **1935-42** (seven years).
- Started the **Bala Saraswati Bhandaramu** village library as a schoolboy; as Secretary of the **Guntur Library Association** set up libraries in about **160 villages**.
- Member of the drafting committee of the **AP Public Libraries Act (1960)**.
# Other helpers
- **Madras Public Libraries Bill, 1938:** Vavilala Gopalakrishnayya – not Ayyanki.
- **Vavilala Ramaswamy Sastrulu & Sons:** books to the Ramamohan Library and free copies to village libraries; Andhra Granthamala (Kasinathuni) also supplied books.
