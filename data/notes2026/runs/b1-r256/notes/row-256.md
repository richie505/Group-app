=== Capital, High Court and the Bellary Taluks
# Capital of Andhra State (1953)
- **Capital:** **Kurnool** – temporary capital of Andhra State from its formation on **1 October 1953** (APPSC JA 2023: "Kurnool became the capital in **1953**").
- **How it was chosen:** Rayalaseema legislators wanted the capital in their region as promised by the **Sribagh Pact**; the communists favoured **Vijayawada**; in the vote of the legislators **Kurnool won by majority**, but only with the help of **five non-Andhra members** of the residual Madras Assembly (C. Rajagopalachari had suggested they should not vote).
  - Counted without the five non-Andhra votes, Vijayawada-Guntur would have had the edge – hence some keys answer "None" to "capital chosen with the support of the majority".
- **Nehru's condition (25 Mar 1953):** capital to be **inside Andhra**, not Madras; temporary capital to be decided **before 1 July 1953**; some offices could stay in Madras for a while.
# High Court
- **Andhra High Court:** set up at **Guntur** in **1954** (first Chief Justice **Koka Subba Rao**) – the compromise for coastal Andhra, while the capital went to Kurnool; not Kurnool, Vijayawada or Anantapur.
- **Interim:** the **Madras High Court** kept jurisdiction until the Guntur court was set up.
# Bellary district split
| Bellary area | Went to |
|---|---|
| **Alur (Aalur), Adoni** | Andhra – **Kurnool district** |
| **Rayadurg** | Andhra – **Anantapur district** |
| Bellary taluk, Siruguppa, Hospet, Sandur, Harpanahalli, Hadagali | Mysore |
- **Justice L.S. Mishra** (Chief Justice, **Hyderabad High Court**): Bellary inquiry (hearings 1-15 May 1953); on his report **Bellary taluk went to Mysore**.
- **Legislature:** members elected to the **Madras Assembly in 1952** formed the Andhra Assembly; **unicameral – no Legislative Council**.
> Exam angle: Kurnool capital / Guntur High Court (×4); Alur-Adoni → Kurnool, Rayadurg → Anantapur.

=== Wanchoo Committee and the Andhra State Act, 1953
# Wanchoo Committee
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Appointed | **December 1952**, after Nehru's statement of **19 December 1952** (following Potti Sriramulu's death) |
| Member | **Justice Kailas Nath (K.N.) Wanchoo**, Chief Justice of the **Rajasthan High Court** – a **single-member** committee |
| Task | To report on the **financial and other implications** of forming Andhra State – identifying the problems of forming the state (boundaries, finances, division of assets, administration); not the capital of India, not the Nizam or peasants |
| Report | **7 February 1953** |
| Centre's decisions on it | Announced by Nehru on **25 March 1953** |
- **NextGen's dates differ:** NextGen gives "appointed 7 Feb 1953, report 25 Mar 1953"; the Lok Sabha record has the report presented on 7 Feb 1953 and the decisions announced on 25 Mar 1953.
- **On Madras:** Wanchoo suggested the Andhra Government work temporarily from **Madras** (not Kurnool) for **3-5 years**; Nehru rejected it – "no question" of Madras as capital.
- **Earlier:** **Partition Committee (1949-50)** under **Kumaraswamy Raja** on asset split; **JVP Committee (April 1949)** – Andhra only if Telugus gave up Madras.
- **Special Officer:** **C.M. Trivedi**, later the first Governor.
# Finances, assets and services
- Assets and administration divided between Andhra and **residual Madras**; the Wanchoo apportionment of **assets and liabilities** was largely reproduced in the Act's schedule.
- The 25 Mar 1953 statement left **services** and **financial implications** to be considered separately later.
- New state "**administratively and economically orphaned**": no developed capital, no commercial centre.
# Andhra State Act, 1953
- **Act 30 of 1953**; **assent of the President: 14 September 1953**; in force **1 October 1953** – not May or June 1953.
- **2014 parallels:** **Kamalanathan** committee – employees; **Sheela Bhide** committee – Schedule IX institutions; **Section 6** expert committee – new capital.
> Exam angle: Wanchoo = financial and other implications (×2); Bill assent 14 Sept 1953.

=== Tungabhadra Project and the Sribagh Pact
| Pact / Act | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| **Sribagh (Sreebagh) Pact** | **1937** | Irrigation priority for **Rayalaseema and Nellore** on the **Krishna, Penna and Tungabhadra** – for **10 years** or as long as needed |
| **Andhra State Act** | **1953** | **Tungabhadra Board** constituted by the President under **Section 66(4)** of this Act to manage the works – not the River Boards Act 1956 or the AP Reorganisation Act 2014 |
- After Bellary's division the Tungabhadra works were shared by **Andhra and Mysore**.
- **26 Jun 2026:** Union Minister **C.R. Patil** opened **33 new gates** of the Tungabhadra dam with the three CMs.
> Exam angle: Sribagh rivers = Krishna, Penna, Tungabhadra (not Godavari or Musi).
