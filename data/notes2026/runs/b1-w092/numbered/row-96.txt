[0:0] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [0:0.0] Builder | Recherla Rudra, general of Ganapatideva (not a Kakatiya king); title Kakatiya Rajya Bharadhureya
  [0:0.1] Year | 1213 CE
  [0:0.2] Place | Palampet, old Warangal district – now Mulugu district (since 2019), Telangana

[0:3] - **Dynasty:** the **Kakatiyas** (ASO 2011).

[0:6] - **Type:** keyed **"Monolithic" (Ekasila)** (G2 2012); strictly a single-shrine (ekakuta) temple – the Trikuta is the Thousand Pillar temple.
[0:7] - **"The brightest gem in the galaxy of medieval temples of the Deccan":** Ramappa at **Palampeta**.

[0:10] - **Madanika** bracket figures in dance poses; **28** elephant-lion (Gajakesari) figures.
[0:11] - **Painted ceilings** with Ramayana–Mahabharata scenes – Ramappa is a site of **Kakatiya paintings**.

## SUB 3 | Jayapa's Nritta Ratnavali and Perini Shiva Tandavam
[3:0] TABLE head: Work | Author | Post / court | Link
  [3:0.0] Nritta Ratnavali (Sanskrit) | Jayapa Senani (Jayana, Jayapa Nayaka) | Gaja Sahini (head of the elephant corps) of Ganapatideva | Ramappa madanika poses follow it
  [3:0.1] Gita Ratnavali, Vadya Ratnavali | Jayapa Senani | same | Music and instruments
  [3:0.2] Sangita Ratnakara | Sarangadeva | Yadavas of Devagiri | General music-dance treatise
[3:1] - **Author:** **Jayapa Senani** (G2-A 1989; Labs 2012) – not Racherla Rudra, Prolaya Nayaka or Ganapatideva.
[3:2] - **Profession:** **Gaja Sahini** (G2 2019; G2 PYQ) – not Asva Sahini, architect or dancer.
[3:3] - **Reign:** **Ganapatideva** (G1 2010; G1 PYQ) – a general and scholar of his court.
[3:4] - **Ramappa dance sculptures:** carved on the principles of **Jayapa's Nritta Ratnavali** (G2 2019 ×2) – not Ranganatha Ramayana or a separate "marga/desi" book; the text itself covers **Marga** (classical) and **Desi** (folk) dance and describes **Perini**.
[3:5] # Perini Shiva Tandavam
[3:6] - **What:** warrior dance of **male warriors before battle**, invoking Shiva, to drum beats; postures carved at Ramappa.
[3:7] - **Revived by:** **Nataraja Ramakrishna** (G2 PYQ; APPSC PYQ) from Nritta Ratnavali and the **Ramappa / Thousand Pillar** sculptures – not Vedantam Satyanarayana Sarma, Yamini Krishnamurthy or Raja-Radha Reddy.
[3:8] ~~ Sources: NextGen Kakatiyas p.31, p.33; NextGen AP History - APPSC 07 p.1; app notes 1:96 (sub 7)

## SUB 6 | Kakatiya Style – Techniques and Features
[6:0] TABLE head: Feature | What it is
  [6:0.0] Stellate plan | Raised star-shaped platform
  [6:0.1] Sandbox foundation | Pits filled with sand, stone chips and earth – absorbs shocks; earthquake-resistant heritage engineering
  [6:0.2] Floating bricks | Light porous bricks in Ramappa's vimana – reduce dead weight of the tower
  [6:0.3] Ekasila | Monolithic carving (Nandi, pillars)
  [6:0.4] Kirtimukha | "Face of glory" motif (not monolithic carving)
  [6:0.5] Madanikas | Bracket figures on outer walls
[6:1] - **Style:** **Chalukyan / Vesara**, influence of the **Western Chalukyas of Kalyani**; **transitional** between early Chalukyan and Vijayanagara.
[6:2] - **Padmakshi temple** (Hanamkonda) was originally **Jain**.
[6:3] ~~ Sources: NextGen Kakatiyas p.33; app notes 1:96 (sub 4, 5)
