## SUB 1 | Temple Components and the Three Orders
[1:0] - **Core:** **garbhagriha** + **mandapa**; **shikhara** (north) or **vimana** (south); axial **vahana with dhvaja pillar**.
[1:1] - **Dwarapalakas:** door-guardian figures **left and right** of the entrance or gopuram (Endowment 2022) – dikpalakas guard the eight directions, kshetrapalakas the site.
[1:2] - **Trikutalaya:** three shrines on one plan (Thousand Pillar temple).
[1:3] TABLE head: Order | Marker | Andhra example
  [1:3.0] Nagara | Curvilinear shikhara | Kalinga variant: Mukhalingam, Simhachalam
  [1:3.1] Dravida | Pyramidal vimana, gopurams | Eastern Chalukya delta temples; Vijayanagara
  [1:3.2] Vesara | Blend | Kakatiya temples
[1:4] - **Pattadakal:** **Papanatha** = Nagara; Virupaksha = Dravida.
[1:5] - **Siddhavatam (Kadapa):** western gopuram in a **1233 CE** Tamil-Grantha inscription of **Chola Rajendra III**.

## SUB 2 | Styles by Dynasty
[2:0] TABLE head: Dynasty | Features | Examples
  [2:0.0] Eastern Chalukya | Dravidian, from Pallava and Badami forms; laterite and limestone | Vijayaditya II – 108 temples; Biccavolu
  [2:0.1] Chalukya-Chola | Model for the Godavari region | Draksharama – Chalukya Bhima I
  [2:0.2] Kakatiya | Vesara; star platforms, sandbox, floating bricks – not wooden superstructures | Ramappa (1213), Thousand Pillar (Shiva, Vishnu, Surya – not Brahma)
