=== NOTES 1:241 | Quit India Movement 1942 - Causes, Course, Underground Activity, Repression, Significance
## SUB 0 | Causes of the Quit India Movement
[241:0:0] TABLE head: Cause | Detail
  [241:0:0.0] Failure of the Cripps Mission (March-April 1942) | No immediate self-government - the main trigger
  [241:0:0.1] Japanese advance in the East | Malaya, Singapore overrun; Rangoon fell March 1942; Japanese at India's eastern frontier; rumours of bombing of Vizag and Kakinada
  [241:0:0.2] Economic grievances | Rising prices, wartime shortages
[241:0:1] - UPSC 2013: Quit India was launched **in response to the Cripps Proposals**.
[241:0:2] - Britain freed India in 1947 because it had become **weak in WWII** (A and R true, R explains A).
[241:0:3] - British PM during Quit India: **Winston Churchill** (1940-45).
[241:0:4] - **C. Rajagopalachari's** pamphlet **"The Way Out"** offered a solution to the constitutional deadlock.
## SUB 1 | The Quit India Resolution
[241:1:0] # Meetings and Dates
[241:1:1] TABLE head: Date | Meeting / Place | Event
  [241:1:1.0] 14 July 1942 | CWC, Wardha | Resolution passed - "Wardha Resolution"; Gandhi empowered to lead a non-violent mass movement
  [241:1:1.1] 7-8 August 1942 | AICC, Bombay, Gowalia Tank Maidan (now August Kranti Ground) | Resolution ratified on 8 August - "August Resolution"
  [241:1:1.2] 9 August 1942 | All India | Movement begins; Operation Zero Hour arrests
[241:1:2] - At Wardha and at Bombay the resolution was **moved by Jawaharlal Nehru** and **seconded/supported by Sardar Patel**.
[241:1:3] - The movement was **primarily launched at Bombay**.
[241:1:4] # Demands of the Resolution
[241:1:5] - **Immediate end of British rule**.
[241:1:6] - A **provisional government** after the British withdrew.
[241:1:7] - **Mass civil disobedience** under Gandhi.
[241:1:8] - NOT in it: continuing British rule until the war ended.
[241:1:9] # Gandhi's Instructions
[241:1:10] TABLE head: Group | Instruction
  [241:1:10.0] Government servants | Stay in service but pledge loyalty to Congress
  [241:1:10.1] Soldiers | Not to fire on Indians
  [241:1:10.2] Students | May leave studies

## SUB 3 | Repression
[241:3:0] # Viceroy and Operations
[241:3:1] - Viceroy: **Lord Linlithgow** (1936-43, **longest-serving Viceroy**); called it **"the most serious rebellion since 1857"** - reason: a huge peasant rising (R explains A).
[241:3:2] - Linlithgow called Gandhi's movements **"Political Blackmail"**.
[241:3:3] - **Operation Zero Hour:** pre-dawn arrest of Congress leaders on **9 August 1942** (the day after the resolution) → movement became **leaderless, spontaneous and decentralised**.
[241:3:4] - **Baldev Sahai** resigned as Advocate General in 1942 in protest against the arrests.
[241:3:5] # Detention Places
[241:3:6] TABLE head: Place | Detention / Event
  [241:3:6.0] Ahmednagar Fort | CWC members held
  [241:3:6.1] Aga Khan Palace, Poona | Gandhi held; 21-day fast, February 1943, against state violence
  [241:3:6.2] Poona (1944) | Kasturba Gandhi died in detention
[241:3:7] - Effect of the fast: **three members of the Viceroy's Executive Council resigned**.
[241:3:8] - Brothers **Gangaram and Kheemdev** were killed in police firing at **Almora**.

  [241:4:0.1] Aruna Asaf Ali | Hoisted the flag at Gowalia Tank; main organiser of the underground network

  [241:4:0.5] Others | Biju Patnaik, Chhotubhai Puranik, Achyut Patwardhan, R.P. Goenka

## SUB 8 | Attitude of Parties
[241:8:0] - **CPI opposed** it - backed the British war effort after **Nazi Germany attacked the USSR**; CPI ban lifted **23 July 1942**; lost heavily in **1945-46 elections**.
[241:8:1] - Did NOT support: **Muslim League, Hindu Mahasabha, CPI, Unionist Party (Punjab), princely states**; **RSS** kept away.
[241:8:2] - **Jinnah/League condemned** it - British and Muslims came closer in hatred of Congress (A and R true, R explains A).
## SUB 9 | Significance
[241:9:0] - **Gandhi's final mass movement**; culmination of the national movement - next need was a way to transfer power (A and R true).

[241:9:2] - Showed independence could not be postponed after the war.
[241:9:3] - The **Struggle-Truce-Struggle** strategy is linked with the Quit India call.
