## SUB 0 | The Andhra (Kurnool) Circular
[0:0] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [0:0.0] Issued by | Andhra Provincial Congress Committee (state Congress leaders)
  [0:0.1] Date | 29 July 1942 – before the Bombay AICC of 8 August
  [0:0.2] Drafted by | Kala Venkata Rao (APPSC G2 2019; G2 Mains) – sent to the CWC through Pattabhi Sitaramayya
  [0:0.3] Name | Police seized a copy in a raid on the Kurnool Congress office → "Kurnool Circular"
  [0:0.4] Movement | Quit India Movement – not Vandemataram, Salt Satyagraha or Non-Cooperation
[0:1] - **⚠ Trap:** author = **Kala Venkata Rao** – not Pattabhi Sitaramayya, Ayyadevara Kaleswara Rao or Tanguturi Prakasam.
[0:2] - **Aim:** "to paralyse all means of communications and machinery of administration".
[0:3] - **Programme:** defy prohibitory orders; lawyers give up practice, students leave colleges; **picketing** salt, foreign trade and industry; cutting toddy trees; **travelling without tickets**, pulling chains; blowing up bridges, cutting telegraph and telephone wires; no taxes except municipal; national flags on government buildings.
[0:4]   - **NOT in it:** **raids on British military camps** (APPSC Royalty Inspector key).
[0:5] - **APCC meeting at Machilipatnam** under **Pattabhi Sitaramayya**.

## SUB 1 | Places and Incidents in Andhra
[1:0] TABLE head: Place | District | Event
  [1:0.0] Tenali | Guntur | 12 Aug 1942: hartal; crowd set fire to the railway station (booking office, North Cabin) and the Madras-Puri passenger train; police firing – about 9 dead (NextGen; local accounts: 3 at the station, 7 martyrs in all)
  [1:0.1] Guntur | Guntur | Firing 13 Aug on students near Hindu College
  [1:0.2] Bhimavaram | West Godavari | Firing 17 Aug; Collectorate attacked
  [1:0.3] Bapatla | Guntur (now Bapatla) | Railway station attacked and burnt
  [1:0.4] Chirala | then Guntur | 12 Aug: 500 students marched on the Sub-Magistrate's court; signal wires cut
  [1:0.5] Tadepalligudem | West Godavari | Post office burnt
  [1:0.6] Kakinada | East Godavari | Lawyers demanded Gandhi's release
[1:1] - **Firing order:** **Tenali 12 → Guntur 13 → Bhimavaram 17 August 1942**.
[1:2] - **Railway station burnt in 1942:** keys give **Tenali** (APPSC G2-A 1984); NextGen's previous-papers list gives **Bapatla** – both stations were attacked.
[1:3] - **⚠ Trap:** the **Tenali Bomb Case (April 1909)** belongs to the **Vandemataram movement**, not Quit India.
[1:4] - Gandhi was held at the **Aga Khan Palace** (not Chowmahalla or Falaknuma) for **21 months** (Aug 1942 – May 1944).

## SUB 3 | Background and the Communists
[3:0] # Before 1942
[3:1] - **Congress ministries resigned** in **October-November 1939** over the war → limited **Individual Satyagraha** (17 Oct 1940): **Vinoba Bhave first**, **Nehru second**; organiser in Andhra **Vavilala Gopalakrishnaiah**.
[3:2] - **Kasturba** and **Mahadev Desai** died in detention; their samadhis are at the Aga Khan Palace.
[3:3] # Communists vs Congress Socialists
[3:4] - **CPI**: ban lifted **23 July 1942** → backed the British war effort ("People's War") and **opposed Quit India**; lost public support.
[3:5] - **Third Communist Conference, Vijayawada, 1943** – the first open Communist conference in Andhra; secretary **Chandra Rajeswara Rao**.
[3:6] - Price paid: heavy defeats in **1945-46** – only **Pillalamarri Venkateswara Rao** won from Andhra in 1945.
[3:7] - **Congress Socialists** led the national underground: **JP, Lohia, Aruna Asaf Ali, Usha Mehta**.
[3:8] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP History Part 19 p.6, p.14; Spectrum pdf p.478, p.483; old app notes 1:242 (sub 0, 6)
