## 3. Repeats and traps
- **"Do or Die" = Gandhi, Quit India** (11 PYQs) – an old APPSC key gave Tilak.
- **8 August 1942 at Gowalia Tank, Bombay** (AICC) vs **14 July, Wardha** (CWC) vs **9 August** (arrests and outbreak); NextGen Part 23 slips to "6 August" and Part 19 to "Young India" – the correct facts are 8 August and Harijan.
- **Usha Mehta = Congress Radio; Lohia = broadcasts; JP = Hazaribagh, Azad Dasta; Aruna = flag at Gowalia Tank.**
- **Kurnool Circular = Kala Venkata Rao**, Quit India, 29 July 1942.
- **Tenali bomb case = 1909, Vandemataram**; the 1942 Tenali event is the station and train fire (one APPSC set keyed it to Quit India).
- **UPSC 2011:** the "not true" option is labour non-participation; two printed keys in this set disagreed.
