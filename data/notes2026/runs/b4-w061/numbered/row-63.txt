## SUB 1 | No Notified SC Population and Concentration
[1:0] - **No notified SCs (2011):** **Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep**.
[1:1] - Reason: overwhelmingly **tribal**; SC status comes from untouchability in the Hindu caste order, ST status from tribal traits.
[1:2] - Small but non-zero: **Mizoram (~0.1%)**, Meghalaya (~0.6%); **Manipur, Sikkim and Tripura have notified SCs** – so any list with Mizoram is wrong.
[1:3] - Highest SC-share district: **Koch Bihar (West Bengal)**, about 50%; lowest: Lawngtlai (Mizoram).
[1:4] - Dense in **fertile alluvial plains and coastal deltas** (Punjab, Ganga plain, West Bengal); sparse in hill, forest and north-eastern tribal districts.
