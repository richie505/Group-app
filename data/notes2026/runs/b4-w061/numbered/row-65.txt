## SUB 1 | Census Definition of Urban and Components of Growth
[1:0] TABLE head: Type | Criteria
  [1:0.0] Statutory town | Any place with a municipality, corporation, cantonment board or notified town area committee, declared by State law
  [1:0.1] Census town | Population 5,000+; 75%+ of male main workers non-agricultural; density 400+ per sq km
[1:1] - 2011 towns **7,935** = **4,041 statutory** + **3,894 census towns**; urban population 37.7 crore (7.9 crore in 1961).
[1:2] - Census towns **1,362 (2001) → 3,894 (2011)** – shows reclassification as a major component.
[1:3] # Components of urban growth
[1:4] - 1. **Natural increase** – similar in rural and urban areas.
[1:5] - 2. **Rural-to-urban migration** – explains most of the rural-urban gap.
[1:6] - 3. **Reclassification** – villages turn into towns / new towns form.
[1:7] - 4. **Expansion of town boundaries** over villages.
[1:8] - "Decline" or contraction is not a component; migration, not natural increase, explains the gap.
[1:9] # Drivers
[1:10] TABLE head: Factor | Examples
  [1:10.0] Pull | Industrialisation and jobs, trade and commerce, better health and education, transport links
  [1:10.1] Push | Failed monsoon → rural unemployment → migration; poverty, landlessness
[1:11] - **Rakesh Mohan**: three processes – **metropolitanisation, commercialisation, rural (subsistence) urbanisation**.
[1:12] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Geography 12 p.12 (p.166); app notes 4:65 sub 0, 3, 4
## SUB 2 | Rural Component – Villages
[2:0] - Villages **6,40,867**; inhabited **5,97,608 (about 93.2%)**.
[2:1] - Rural population **about 83.3 crore** (2011) = 68.8%.
[2:2] - Village size: under 200 persons in NE hill States, western Rajasthan, Rann of Kachchh; up to 17,000 in Kerala and parts of Maharashtra.
[2:3] - 2001-11: **first time since Independence** urban India added more people than rural India.
[2:4] - Bihar and Sikkim are highly rural; only slightly over half of Goa's and Maharashtra's people live in villages.

## SUB 3 | Rural-Urban Continuum – PURA and Rurban Mission
[3:0] - **PURA** (Providing Urban Amenities to Rural Areas): concept of **A.P.J. Abdul Kalam**; announced by PM Vajpayee, **2003**; pilot in 7 clusters (2004-05); restructured 2012.
[3:1] - **Four connectivities of PURA:** **physical** (roads), **electronic** (telecom, internet), **knowledge** (education, training) → leading to **economic** connectivity – **cultural connectivity is not one of them**.
[3:2] - **Shyama Prasad Mukherji Rurban Mission**: successor to PURA; launched **21 Feb 2016 at Kurubhat, Rajnandgaon (Chhattisgarh)**; **300 Rurban clusters**.
