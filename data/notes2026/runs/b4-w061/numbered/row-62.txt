## SUB 1 | Female Work Participation and Gender
[1:0] - Female LFPR (15+): **23.3% (2017-18)** → 30% (2019-20) → **37% (2022-23)** → about **40% (2025)**.
[1:1] - **Claudia Goldin U-curve:** India on the falling half till **2018-19**.
[1:2] - Rise: subsistence farming, unpaid family work, hardship, return migration, **wider survey coverage**.
[1:3] - Low levels: **mechanisation**, more schooling of girls, no flexible hours, distance, safety, family duties.
[1:4] - Women (2011): **55%** of agricultural labourers, 24% of cultivators, only **12.8%** of operational holdings; men's **out-migration** → **feminisation of farm work**.
[1:5] # Gender and work (sociology)
[1:6] - All societies divide household tasks by gender; for women **production and reproduction** are interlinked; much of women's productive work is **unpaid**; men have played a minor role in domestic work (all four statements correct – TSPSC 2023).
[1:7] - **Engels / Marxist view:** women form part of the **reserve army of labour** – drawn into work in booms and pushed out in slumps (UPSC 2000).
