## 3. Notes heavy but never asked
- The old notes' "Number Game", "APPSC Previous Year Questions" and "High-probability MCQ Traps" sub-sections repeated facts already in the topic sub-sections. They were removed, and their "not X" points were folded into the bullets.
- The heavy case-law backgrounds (personality-rights case lists, DNA case table, Chandigarh history, Waqf provisions, cannabis rules) are kept only as far as the app's own MCQs use them. No PYQ has asked any of them.
- Rocket CA 2026 (Parts 01–02) covers the 2026 items: the Assembly elections (Part 01 p.65–66), the Governors (p.42), the digital Census (p.7) and the SC (Number of Judges) Act and other 2026 Acts (Part 02 p.16–19). It has nothing on the Apr–Nov 2025 items in row 29 (Labour Codes, the 130th Bill, the Waqf ruling, DPDP), so those rest on the app notes and Laxmikanth.
