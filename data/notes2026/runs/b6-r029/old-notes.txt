## SUB 0 | Four Labour Codes (in Force 21 November 2025)
[29:0:0] # The Codes
[29:0:1] - From **21 November 2025**, **4 Labour Codes replaced 29 Central labour laws**.
[29:0:2] - Labour is in the **Concurrent List** (List III, **Entries 22–24 and 35**).
[29:0:3] TABLE head: Code | Laws merged | Remember
  [29:0:3.0] Code on Wages, 2019 | 4 | National Floor Wage; max 48 hrs/week
  [29:0:3.1] Code on Social Security, 2020 | 9 (EPF, ESI, gratuity, maternity benefit) | Gig and platform workers defined
  [29:0:3.2] Industrial Relations Code, 2020 | 3 (Trade Unions, Industrial Disputes, Industrial Employment) | Layoff permission 100 → 300
  [29:0:3.3] OSH Code, 2020 | 13 (factories, mines, contract labour, construction) | Factory licence 10 → 20
[29:0:4] # Code on Wages
[29:0:5] - Applies to all employees; **National Floor Wage** – States cannot fix minimum wage below it.
[29:0:6] - Wage = Basic + DA + Retaining Allowance; **deductions capped at 50%**.
[29:0:7] - Hours 8–12/day, **max 48/week**; **overtime at least twice** the normal rate.
[29:0:8] - Payment monthly within 7 days; on resignation/termination within 2 days; wage slips mandatory.
[29:0:9] - **Anoop Satpathy Expert Committee (2019)** – national minimum wage **₹375 per day**.
[29:0:10] # Code on Social Security
[29:0:11] - **First legal definition of gig and platform workers** (this Code, not the Code on Wages); Social Security Fund.
[29:0:12] - Aggregators contribute **1–2% of annual turnover**, capped at 5% of amount payable to gig workers.
[29:0:13] - **Fixed-term employees: gratuity after 1 year** (5 years applies to permanent employees).
[29:0:14] - EPFO: establishments with 20+ employees; ESIC pan-India, mandatory even with 1 worker in hazardous work (earlier 10); inspector-cum-facilitators replace inspectors.
[29:0:15] # Industrial Relations Code
[29:0:16] - Layoff/retrenchment/closure permission threshold **100 → 300** workers (not 500).
[29:0:17] - Merged the **Industrial Disputes Act, 1947** and **Trade Unions Act, 1926**.
[29:0:18] - Strike: notice within 60 days before; no strike within 14 days of notice; mass casual leave by >50% workers = strike.
[29:0:19] - Negotiating union: **51%** membership; council – unions with ≥20%.
[29:0:20] # OSH Code
[29:0:21] - Factory licence: with power **10 → 20** workers; without power **20 → 40**.
[29:0:22] - Contract labour law applies when contractor has **50+** (earlier 20).
[29:0:23] - **Women may work before 6 am / after 7 pm** with consent and safety measures.
[29:0:24] - **Appointment letters** and **annual free health check** mandatory.
[29:0:25] - Safety Committees: ≥500 factory, ≥250 construction, ≥100 mine workers.
[29:0:26] Gig workers are defined in the **Social Security Code**, not the Wages Code; IR layoff threshold is **300**, not 500.
