## SUB 0 | Supreme Court on District Judges, Waqf and Minority Schools (2025)
[0:0] # Rejanish K.V. v K. Deepa – district judges (9 October 2025)
[0:1] - **Ruling:** five-judge Bench led by **CJI B.R. Gavai** – **serving judicial officers can be directly recruited as district judges** (earlier only advocates with 7 years' practice).
[0:2] - **Overruled:** **Satya Narain Singh (1984)** and **Dheeraj Mor v High Court of Delhi (2020)**; **Justice Sundresh** – an absolute bar violates **Article 14**.
[0:3] - **Conditions:** **7 years** of advocacy + judicial service combined; uniform minimum age **35 years**.
[0:4] - **Article 233:** **district judges** are appointed by the **Governor in consultation with the High Court**; other judicial officers (Art 234) – Governor after consulting the **State PSC and High Court** (Arts 233–237, Part VI).
[0:5] - **Criminal courts:** CJM – offences up to 7 years; JM – up to 3 years; a **death sentence by a Sessions Judge needs High Court confirmation**.
[0:6] # Seniority in Higher Judicial Services
[0:7] - **Hearings from 28 October 2025** (five-judge Bench, CJI Gavai): promotees vs direct recruits; **promotees ≈ three-fourths** of the cadre, though direct recruits dominate senior posts.
[0:8] - **All India Judges Association (2002):** **75% promotion** (50% merit-cum-seniority + 25% LDCE), **25% direct recruitment**; 40-point roster (R.K. Sabarwal, 1995).
[0:9] - **May 2025 SC framework:** LDCE 25% restored; **qualifying service 5 → 3 years**; **3 years' practice** for Civil Judge entry.
[0:10] - **District judiciary** handles about **90%** of litigation.
[0:11] - **NALSA:** **Patron-in-Chief = CJI**; **Executive Chairman = second senior-most SC judge**; Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987; Art 39A.

## SUB 3 | Elections – ECI, SIR, ECINET and the Vice-President
[3:0] # Election Commission – what it can and cannot do (APPSC PYQ)
[3:1] - **SVEEP** = **Systematic Voters' Education and Electoral Participation** – the **ECI's flagship voter-awareness programme** (APPSC PYQ answer).
[3:2] - **Symbols:** the ECI recognises parties and allots symbols under the **Election Symbols (Reservation and Allotment) Order, 1968** – a power from Art 324 and statute, not one the Constitution names.
[3:3] - **No power to deregister:** the ECI registers parties under Sec 29A, RPA 1951, but may **not deregister** a party for violating the Act (INC v Institute of Social Welfare, 2002 – only for fraud in registration or similar).
[3:4] - **Seats per State:** allotment of Lok Sabha seats to States is fixed by **Parliament (Arts 81–82) and the Delimitation Commission**, not the ECI.
[3:5] - **ECI composition:** three members; 2023 selection committee **chaired by the PM** (LoP and a Cabinet Minister members); 6 years or 65; advises on disqualification of MPs (**Art 103**) and MLAs (Art 192).
[3:6] # Bihar SIR and pan-India SIR (2025)
[3:7] - **10 July 2025 SC order:** **did not stay** the Bihar SIR; ordered draft-roll publication; directed the ECI to accept **Aadhaar as the 12th valid document** – proof of identity, **not citizenship** (Justices Surya Kant, Joymalya Bagchi).
[3:8] - **Pan-India SIR:** announced **27 October 2025**.
