## 3. Repeats and trends
- No PYQ is repeated. The PYQs are spread over a decade of AP news: MoUs (Tata Trusts 2015), services (IndiGo Singapore 2019), schemes (DBT 2020), NH foundation stones (2023), zones (SCoR) and firsts (Quantum Valley).
- The examiner asks **where** (venue, district) and **who** (airline, developer). The notes therefore give every project with its district and developer.
