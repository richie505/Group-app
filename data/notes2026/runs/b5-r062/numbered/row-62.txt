## SUB 5 | IAEA, Safeguards, NSG and Treaties
[5:0] # IAEA
[5:1] - **IAEA:** set up **1957**; HQ **Vienna, Austria**; autonomous body in the UN system; ~180 members; **India a founding member**; reports non-compliance to the **UN Security Council**.
[5:2] - **Why only some Indian reactors are under IAEA safeguards:** they use **imported uranium**/technology; strategic reactors use domestic uranium.
[5:3] - **2006 separation plan:** civil and military facilities separated; civil reactors under safeguards so NSG members could supply fuel.
[5:4] - **India-Specific Safeguards Agreement:** **2 February 2009**. **Additional Protocol:** signed **2009**, in force **2014**; implication = **civilian reactors come under IAEA safeguards** (wider access, declared civilian facilities only).
[5:5] # NSG and export controls
[5:6] - **NSG:** **1975** ("**London Club**"), response to **Pokhran-I (1974)**; **48 members** (China, New Zealand, Ireland are members; **Iran is not**); consensus; no permanent secretariat.
[5:7] - India applied **2016**; blocked mainly by **China**; membership would give **technology access + a say in rule-making**, NOT NPT membership.
[5:8] - India's regimes: **MTCR 2016 → Wassenaar 2017 → Australia Group 2018**.

## SUB 7 | Nuclear Safety – AERB and Zones
[7:0] - **AERB:** set up **15 November 1983** by **executive order** under the Atomic Energy Act 1962; worked under the **DAE**; **SHANTI Act 2025** gave it **statutory** status.
[7:1] - Powers: licensing plants and fuel-cycle facilities; inspections; suspend/revoke licences; **radiation safety in medical and industrial uses**; waste; transport of radioactive material.
[7:2] - **MDEP:** AERB became the **11th member** of the Multinational Design Evaluation Programme on **4 April 2012**.
[7:3] - **Nuclear safety ranking:** **Australia** ranked first (NTI Nuclear Materials Security Index, 2012).
[7:4] TABLE head: Zone | Rule
  [7:4.0] Exclusion zone | At least 1.5 km radius (AERB siting code; often written "1–1.5 km"); no public habitation
  [7:4.1] Sterilised zone | 1.5–5 km; only natural growth of population
  [7:4.2] Emergency Planning Zone | 16 km; concentric zones for protective actions
[7:5] - **Defence in Depth** (multiple layers); **ALARA** = As Low As Reasonably Achievable.
[7:6] - Dose limits: **20 mSv/year** (5-year average); max 30 mSv in a year.
[7:7] - **Convention on Nuclear Safety:** adopted 1994, in force **1996**; India a party.

## SUB 8 | Liability Law – CLNDA 2010 to SHANTI Act 2025
[8:0] - **CLNDA 2010:** operator liability **no-fault**, capped at **₹1,500 crore**; **Section 17(b)**: operator's recourse against suppliers; Indian Nuclear Insurance Pool.
[8:1] - **SHANTI Act 2025:** assent **20 Dec 2025**; **repealed the Atomic Energy Act 1962 and CLNDA 2010**; private Indian firms and JVs may build and operate; FDI capped at **49%**.
