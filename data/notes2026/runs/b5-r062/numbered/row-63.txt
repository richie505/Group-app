[0:8] - **Tata Memorial Centre:** Anchor Centre for IAEA's **'Rays of Hope'**.

## SUB 1 | Radiometric Dating
[1:0] TABLE head: Method | Dates | Half-life
  [1:0.0] Carbon-14 (₆C¹⁴) | Once-living remains – fossils, wood, bones, shells, trees – up to ~50,000 years | ~5,730 years
  [1:0.1] Uranium-238 → Lead-206 | Most ancient rocks/mountains, age of the Earth | ~4.5 billion years
  [1:0.2] Potassium-40 → Argon-40 | Volcanic rocks, fossil-bearing layers | ~1.25 billion years
[1:1] - **Radioactive dating** measures the **age of rocks** (absolute age) – not their composition, colour or weight.
[1:2] - **Clair Cameron Patterson** pioneered uranium-lead dating of the Earth.
[1:3] - Radiocarbon, U-Pb and K-Ar all date soil/minerals/rocks or remains; **mining** is not a dating technique.
[1:4] - Carbon dating is the standard answer for "**age of organic matter / fossils of animals and trees**"; very old fossils and rocks need uranium methods.
[1:5] ~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 4 p.31 (C-14 dating up to ~50,000 years); Rocket Biology p.6 (oldest rocks/ancient mountains = uranium-lead); app notes 5:63 sub 5
## SUB 2 | Radiation Basics, Units, Shielding and Hazards
[2:0] TABLE head: Radiation | Nature | Stopped by
  [2:0.0] Alpha | Helium nucleus (+) | Paper, skin
  [2:0.1] Beta | Fast electron (−) | Thin aluminium
  [2:0.2] Gamma | Electromagnetic, no charge | Thick lead or concrete
  [2:0.3] X-rays | Electromagnetic | Lead aprons
[2:1] - **X-rays and gamma rays** are electromagnetic; alpha, beta and cathode rays are particles.
[2:2] - **CT scanning uses X-rays**.
[2:3] - **Lead** shields against nuclear radiation (dense; absorbs gamma and X-rays).
[2:4] TABLE head: Quantity | Unit
  [2:4.0] Activity | Becquerel (1 decay/s) – SI unit of radioactivity
  [2:4.1] Absorbed dose | Gray (1 J/kg)
  [2:4.2] Biological effect | Sievert
[2:5] - **Half-life** = time for half the nuclei to decay.
[2:6] - **Dosimeter** records the radiation dose received by a person; Geiger counter detects/counts radiation.
[2:7] - **Radon:** **alpha emitter** from radium/uranium decay; from soil, **granite, brick, concrete**; accumulates indoors – the **most important indoor air pollutant** in such questions; long inhalation causes **lung cancer** (second to smoking).
[2:8] ~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 4 p.29 (alpha, beta, gamma table), p.30 (half-life), p.33 (Radon-222 lung cancer); PMF General Science Part 6 p.52-53 (X-rays in CT scans, lead aprons; gamma rays); PMF General Science Part 7 p.38 (lead as radiation shield); PMF Environment Part 4 p.2, p.69 (indoor radon); app notes 5:63 sub 2-3
## SUB 3 | Institutions and Isotope Production
[3:0] - **BRIT** (Board of Radiation and Isotope Technology): **industrial unit of DAE**, Vashi, Navi Mumbai; works in **isotope technology** (not railway wagons, IT or road transport).
[3:1] - **BARC**: AEET **1954**, renamed **1967**; India's **leading radioisotope producer**. Dhruva = largest research reactor; Apsara = Asia's first; KAMINI = only U-233-fuelled reactor.
[3:2] - Isotopes are made at **Trombay reactors, the Kolkata accelerator and NPCIL plants**; India supplies Co-60 and I-131 to 40+ countries.
[3:3] - **Cyclone-30:** a **particle accelerator (cyclotron) that produces radioisotopes** – not a cyclone centre or a reactor.

## SUB 4 | Agriculture, Food and Industry
[4:0] - **Mutation breeding**: **L. J. Stadler (1928)**, X-rays on **barley**; later gamma rays. **72** BARC Trombay varieties; **TBM-9 = banana**; **RTS-43 = sorghum**.
[4:1] - **Sterile insect technique**: radiation-sterilised males mate with wild females – no viable offspring.
[4:2] - **P-32 / N-15** trace fertiliser uptake; nuclear **moisture gauges** guide irrigation.
[4:3] - **Food irradiation**: radiolysis of water; **Co-60 gamma, X-rays or electron beams**; packed or bulk food; stops sprouting, kills pests; **Radura** = irradiated-food symbol. Raising crop variety = mutation breeding, not food irradiation.
[4:4] - **Industry**: gamma radiography (**Ir-192, Co-60**) for welds; **smoke detectors = Americium-241** (alpha); thickness gauges; **Na-24** tracer for pipeline leaks; port sediment tracers **reduce** desilting; Co-60 sterilises medical products.
[4:5] ~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 4 p.31 (mutation breeding, P-32/N-15, SIT, Na-24 leaks, Ir-192/Co-60 radiography); app notes 5:63 sub 7-8
## SUB 5 | Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generator (RTG)
[5:0] - Turns **decay heat into electricity** via thermocouples – the **Seebeck effect** (thermocouple principle); **not a fission reactor**; no moving parts.
[5:1] - Fuel: **Plutonium-238** (by-product of weapons production).
[5:2] - Used on **Voyager, Cassini, Curiosity, Perseverance, New Horizons**.
[5:3] ~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 4 p.31 (RTG, Pu-238, Voyager, Curiosity, New Horizons); app notes 5:63 sub 6
