=== Nuclear Power Plants – Location, State and Firsts
# Operating plants (NPCIL)
| Plant | State | Units / capacity | Key fact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tarapur** (TAPS) | **Maharashtra** (near Bombay) | 2 x 160 MW **BWR** + 2 x 540 MW PHWR | **India's first nuclear power plant (1969)**; BWRs supplied by the **USA** ("Atoms for Peace"); TAPS 3-4 = first 540 MW PHWRs |
| **Rawatbhata** (RAPS/RAPP), near Kota | **Rajasthan** | RAPS 1-6 + RAPP-7 (700 MW), RAPP-8 next | Oldest PHWR site; RAPS-1 (1972-73) = first PHWR, with **Canada** (CANDU/AECL) |
| **Kalpakkam** (MAPS), near Madras | **Tamil Nadu** | 2 x 220 MW | **First fully indigenous PHWR** (MAPS-1 critical **1983**); also IGCAR (1971) and the PFBR |
| **Narora** (NAPS) | **Uttar Pradesh** | 2 x 220 MW | PHWR (not VVER); in **Seismic Zone IV** |
| **Kakrapar** (KAPS), **Surat** district | **Gujarat** | 4 units, 1,840 MW | **KAPP-3 = first indigenous 700 MW PHWR**, commercial operation **30 June 2023** |
| **Kaiga** (KGS), near river **Kali**, Uttara Kannada | **Karnataka** | 4 x 220 = 880 MW; Units 5-6 begun 2026 | Capacity factor >90%; Kaiga 1-2 began operating in **1999-2000** (newest then) |
| **Kudankulam** (KKNPP), Tirunelveli | **Tamil Nadu** | 2 x 1,000 MW **VVER** = 2,000 MW; Units 3-6 being built | **Largest operating station** (2024); Russia |
- **Fleet mode (2017):** ten 700 MW PHWRs – **Kaiga 5-6** (Karnataka), **Gorakhpur 3-4** (**Haryana**, not UP), **Chutka 1-2** (MP), **Mahi Banswara 1-4** (Rajasthan, by **ASHVINI**, NPCIL–NTPC JV); aim to cut build time from **13 to 8 years**.
- PHWR sizes: **220 → 540 → 700 MW**, all indigenised.
- Seismic zones: **Narora Zone IV**; Kaiga and Tarapur Zone III; Kalpakkam Zone II.
- Pairs: **Shivanasamudra** (Kaveri, 1902) = **hydel**; Kalpakkam = nuclear.
- **SODAR** (sonic weather/wind profiler) is run at nuclear sites – **Kaiga and Kalpakkam** (UPPSC key); Kalpakkam's Doppler SODAR dates from 1990.

=== Civil Nuclear Agreements and the NSG Waiver

=== IAEA, Safeguards, NSG and Treaties
# IAEA
- **IAEA:** set up **1957**; HQ **Vienna, Austria**; autonomous body in the UN system; ~180 members; **India a founding member**; reports non-compliance to the **UN Security Council**.
- **Why only some Indian reactors are under IAEA safeguards:** they use **imported uranium**/technology; strategic reactors use domestic uranium.
- **2006 separation plan:** civil and military facilities separated; civil reactors under safeguards so NSG members could supply fuel.
- **India-Specific Safeguards Agreement:** **2 February 2009**. **Additional Protocol:** signed **2009**, in force **2014**; implication = **civilian reactors come under IAEA safeguards** (wider access, declared civilian facilities only).
# NSG and export controls
- **NSG:** **1975** ("**London Club**"), response to **Pokhran-I (1974)**; **48 members** (China, New Zealand, Ireland are members; **Iran is not**); consensus; no permanent secretariat.
- India applied **2016**; blocked mainly by **China**; membership would give **technology access + a say in rule-making**, NOT NPT membership.
- India's regimes: **MTCR 2016 → Wassenaar 2017 → Australia Group 2018**.

=== Nuclear Safety – AERB and Zones
- **AERB:** set up **15 November 1983** by **executive order** under the Atomic Energy Act 1962; worked under the **DAE**; **SHANTI Act 2025** gave it **statutory** status.
- Powers: licensing plants and fuel-cycle facilities; inspections; suspend/revoke licences; **radiation safety in medical and industrial uses**; waste; transport of radioactive material.
- **MDEP:** AERB became the **11th member** of the Multinational Design Evaluation Programme on **4 April 2012**.
- **Nuclear safety ranking:** **Australia** ranked first (NTI Nuclear Materials Security Index, 2012).
| Zone | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Exclusion zone** | At least **1.5 km** radius (AERB siting code; often written "1–1.5 km"); **no public habitation** |
| Sterilised zone | 1.5–5 km; only natural growth of population |
| Emergency Planning Zone | **16 km**; concentric zones for protective actions |
- **Defence in Depth** (multiple layers); **ALARA** = As Low As Reasonably Achievable.
- Dose limits: **20 mSv/year** (5-year average); max 30 mSv in a year.
- **Convention on Nuclear Safety:** adopted 1994, in force **1996**; India a party.

=== Liability Law – CLNDA 2010 to SHANTI Act 2025
- **CLNDA 2010:** operator liability **no-fault**, capped at **₹1,500 crore**; **Section 17(b)**: operator's recourse against suppliers; Indian Nuclear Insurance Pool.
- **SHANTI Act 2025:** assent **20 Dec 2025**; **repealed the Atomic Energy Act 1962 and CLNDA 2010**; private Indian firms and JVs may build and operate; FDI capped at **49%**.
| Facility | Liability cap |
|---|---|
| Large | ₹3,000 crore |
| Medium | ₹1,500 crore |
| SMR | ₹100 crore |
- Total cap **300 million SDR**; appeals to **APTEL**; Government keeps **enrichment, spent-fuel reprocessing and heavy water**.
- **Aug 2026:** Supreme Court issued notice on a petition challenging the **liability caps**.
