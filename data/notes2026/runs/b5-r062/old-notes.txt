## SUB 6 | Iaea and Safeguards
[62:6:0] - **IAEA**: founded **1957**; HQ **Vienna, Austria**; autonomous body in the UN system; ~180 members; **India a founding member**.
[62:6:1] - Reports safeguards non-compliance to the **UN Security Council**.
[62:6:2] - **2006 separation plan**: civil and military facilities separated; civil reactors under IAEA safeguards so NSG members could supply fuel.
[62:6:3] - **Why some reactors are under safeguards**: they use **imported uranium**; others use domestic fuel.
[62:6:4] - **India-Specific Safeguards Agreement**: signed **2 February 2009**.
[62:6:5] - **Additional Protocol**: signed **2009**, in force **2014**; wider inspection access, only **declared civilian facilities**.
## SUB 7 | Nsg, Export-control Regimes and Treaties
[62:7:0] # Nuclear Suppliers Group
[62:7:1] - Formed **1975** ("**London Club**") in response to **Pokhran-I (1974)**; **48 members**; consensus; **no permanent secretariat**; Iran is not a member.
[62:7:2] - India applied **2016**; blocked mainly by **China** (India not in NPT). Australia reaffirmed support (Jul 2026).
[62:7:3] - Benefits: **technology access + say in rule-making**; NOT automatic NPT membership.
[62:7:4] - India's regimes: **MTCR 2016 → Wassenaar 2017 → Australia Group 2018**; NSG pending.
[62:7:5] # Treaties

  [62:7:6.1] CTBT (1996) | Bans all test explosions | Not signed – no time-bound disarmament; subcritical tests allowed
  [62:7:6.2] PTBT (1963) | Bans tests in air, space, underwater | Signed

## SUB 8 | Liability Law – Clnda 2010 to Shanti Act 2025
[62:8:0] # CLNDA 2010
[62:8:1] - Operator liability **no-fault**, capped at **₹1,500 crore**; Centre pays beyond the cap.
[62:8:2] - **Section 17(b)**: operator's right of recourse against suppliers for defective equipment; **Section 46** kept other civil laws open.
[62:8:3] - Indian Nuclear Insurance Pool; Nuclear Damage Claims Commission; Nuclear Liability Fund.
[62:8:4] # SHANTI Act 2025
[62:8:5] - Introduced in Lok Sabha **15 Dec 2025**; assent **20 Dec 2025**.
[62:8:6] - **Repealed Atomic Energy Act 1962 and CLNDA 2010**; ended NPCIL monopoly – Indian private firms and JVs can build, own, operate; foreign firms excluded, FDI capped at 49%.
[62:8:7] TABLE head: Facility | Liability cap
  [62:8:7.0] Large | ₹3,000 crore
  [62:8:7.1] Medium | ₹1,500 crore
  [62:8:7.2] SMR | ₹100 crore
[62:8:8] - Total cap **300 million SDR** (~₹3,900 crore); excess via **Nuclear Liability Fund**. Appeals: **APTEL**.
[62:8:9] - Only amended **Section 4 of the Patents Act** (peaceful nuclear patents).
[62:8:10] - Government keeps **enrichment, spent-fuel reprocessing, heavy water**.
[62:8:11] - **Aug 2026**: Supreme Court (Bench headed by **CJI Surya Kant**) issued notice on a petition challenging the liability caps.
## SUB 9 | Nuclear Safety – Aerb and Zones
[62:9:0] # AERB
[62:9:1] - Set up **15 November 1983** by **executive order** under the Atomic Energy Act 1962; worked under the **DAE** (the promoter) – the **independence debate**.
[62:9:2] - **NSRA Bill** never enacted; **SHANTI Act 2025** gave the AERB **statutory** status, answerable to Parliament (Jan 2026).
[62:9:3] - Powers: licensing plants and fuel-cycle facilities; inspections; can suspend or revoke licences; **radiation safety in medical and industrial uses**; waste; transport of radioactive material.
[62:9:4] # Zones Around a Reactor
[62:9:5] TABLE head: Zone | Rule
  [62:9:5.0] Exclusion zone | 1–1.5 km radius; no public habitation
  [62:9:5.1] Emergency Planning Zone | 16 km; concentric zones for protective actions
[62:9:6] APPSC GS 2022 key: exclusion zone = 1 km.
[62:9:7] # Safety Practice
[62:9:8] - **Defence in Depth** (multiple independent protective layers); **ALARA** = As Low As Reasonably Achievable.
[62:9:9] - Dose limits: **20 mSv/year** (5-year average); max **30 mSv** in a year; **100 mSv** cumulative.
[62:9:10] - **Post-Fukushima**: 700 MW PHWRs carry advanced safety features; releases <1% of AERB limits (2026).
[62:9:11] - **Convention on Nuclear Safety**: adopted **1994**, in force **1996**; India a party; requires on-site and off-site emergency plans.

## SUB 1 | Radiation Therapy and Imaging
[63:1:0] - **Teletherapy** = external beam (**Co-60**); **brachytherapy** = sealed source inside/next to the tumour (Ir-192, I-125).
[63:1:1] - **Radon** = radioactive **noble gas** used to treat cancer (APPSC G1 2018).
[63:1:2] - **Electron beam therapy**: skin lymphoma (spot or total skin).
[63:1:3] - **PET** = positron emitters, mainly **F-18 FDG**; **SPECT** = single gamma emitters, mainly **Tc-99m**.
[63:1:4] - **30 MeV Medical Cyclotron, Kolkata**: produces F-18 FDG commercially.
[63:1:5] - **Tata Memorial Centre**: Anchor Centre for IAEA's **'Rays of Hope'**.
## SUB 2 | Radon and Radiation Hazards
[63:2:0] - Radon: **alpha emitter** from radium decay; long inhalation causes **lung cancer**.
[63:2:1] - From soil gas, **building materials** (granite, brick, concrete), **uranium mill tailings**.
