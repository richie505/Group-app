## SUB 0 | 97th Amendment, 2011 – Co-operative Societies
[0:0] - Passed in **2011**, notified **12 January 2012** (hence "97th Amendment Act of 2012" in some papers); gave **constitutional status and protection to co-operative societies** – not education, terrorism or Lokpal.
[0:1] TABLE head: Provision | Content
  [0:1.0] Art. 19(1)(c) | Right to form co-operative societies made a Fundamental Right
  [0:1.1] Art. 43B (DPSP) | Promote voluntary formation, autonomous functioning, democratic control, professional management of co-operatives
  [0:1.2] Part IXB | Arts 243ZH–243ZT – a new Part
  [0:1.3] 243ZJ | Board of max 21 directors; one SC/ST seat and two women seats; term 5 years
  [0:1.4] 243ZM | Accounts audited within six months of the close of the financial year
  [0:1.5] 243ZP | Returns filed within six months of the close of every financial year (not two months)
[0:2] - So of "A. Part added by the 97th; B. audit in six months; C. returns in two months; D. six-year term", only **A and B** are correct.
[0:3] - **Union of India v. Rajendra N. Shah (2021)**: Part IXB **struck down for State co-operatives** (no State ratification); it still applies to **multi-State co-operatives**.
[0:4] - The **15% cap on Ministers** is the **91st (2003)**, not the 97th.
[0:5] ~~ Sources: Rocket Polity Part 1 p.41, p.45-48, p.57-58 (src/polity/rocket-polity-01.txt); Constitution of India Arts. 243ZJ, 243ZM, 243ZP

## SUB 2 | 101st Amendment, 2016 – GST
[2:0] - Assent **8 September 2016**; GST from **1 July 2017**.
[2:1] - Inserted **246A** (Parliament and States both make GST laws), **269A** (GST on inter-State supply), **279A** (**GST Council**: chaired by the **Union Finance Minister**; decisions by **three-fourths** weighted votes).
[2:2] - Omitted **Art. 268A** (service tax); added the GST Council to the provisions needing **State ratification** (amended Art. 368).
[2:3] ~~ Sources: Rocket Polity Part 1 p.57 (src/polity/rocket-polity-01.txt); PMF Polity Notes Part 1 p.93

## SUB 4 | 103rd and 104th Amendments, 2019
[4:0] - **103rd**: passed as the **124th Amendment Bill, 2019**; assent **12 January 2019**; inserted **15(6)** and **16(6)** – up to **10%** for EWS (income limit ₹8 lakh, fixed by notification).
[4:1] - **Janhit Abhiyan v. Union of India (7 Nov 2022)**: upheld **3:2**; the 50% ceiling does not apply to EWS.
[4:2] - **104th**: SC/ST seat reservation (Art. 334) extended to **2030**; **Anglo-Indian nomination** to the Lok Sabha and Assemblies ended (**January 2020**).
[4:3] ~~ Sources: Rocket Polity Part 1 p.57 (src/polity/rocket-polity-01.txt); Janhit Abhiyan v. Union of India (2022)
## SUB 5 | 106th Amendment, 2023 – Women's Reservation
[5:0] - **Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam**; **assent 28 September 2023** (so "2023", not 2020-2022).
[5:1] - One-third seats for women in the **Lok Sabha (330A)** and **State Assemblies (332A)**; **334A** on timing; 239AA amended for **Delhi**.
[5:2] - Starts only after **delimitation** on the first Census after commencement; lasts **15 years**, extendable by Parliament by law; **not yet in force** (Nov 2026).
[5:3] - Debates: **no OBC sub-quota**; **southern States** fear a smaller share after delimitation.
[5:4] - Local bodies have had **one-third for women** since **1992** (**243D, 243T**).
[5:5] - **131st Amendment Bill, 2026** (early delimitation on 2011 data, Lok Sabha to 850) was **defeated** in the Lok Sabha on 17 April 2026.
[5:6] ~~ Sources: Praveen CA360 Apr 2026 p.6 (src/current-affairs/praveen-ca360-2026-04-magazine.txt); PMF Lens Apr 2026 (1) p.1.10; Rocket Polity Part 1 p.57
