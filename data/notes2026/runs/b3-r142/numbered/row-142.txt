## SUB 0 | VCIC, ECEC and the Act's Commitment
[0:0] # What the Act says (Thirteenth Schedule)
[0:1] - **Item 5:** GoI shall, **within six months** of the appointed day, examine the feasibility of a **Vizag-Chennai Industrial Corridor** "along the lines of the **Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor** (DMIC)" and take an expeditious decision (asked: Asst. Architectural Surveyor).
[0:2] - Distractors: Delhi-Kolkata, Delhi-Raipur, Mumbai-Kolkata corridors – none is in the Act.
[0:3] - Same Schedule: GoI to **"take steps"** for rapid **rail and road** connectivity from the new AP capital to **Hyderabad** and other Telangana cities – **no deadline**.
[0:4] - **AP-ICDA Act, 2017**: creates the AP Industrial Corridor Development Authority; industrial parks notified under **Section 3**.
[0:5] # VCIC at a glance
[0:6] TABLE head: Feature | Fact
  [0:6.0] Length | About 800 km, along NH-16 on the AP coast
  [0:6.1] Parent corridor | First phase of the East Coast Economic Corridor (ECEC) = India's first coastal economic corridor (planned 2,500 km, Kolkata to Tuticorin)
  [0:6.2] Districts | Traverses nine (old) districts of AP (asked: DAO Mains)
  [0:6.3] Purpose | Gateway to ASEAN and East Asian markets – Act East Policy, Make in India
  [0:6.4] Funding partner | ADB (not JICA); modelled on DMIC; port-led
[0:7] - **"VCIC is a key part of"** → ECEC + ASEAN link + nine AP districts = **"All of these"** (G2 Mains 2019).
[0:8] - AP is implementing **three** corridors: **VCIC, CBIC and HBIC** (Hyderabad-Bengaluru).
[0:9] - Key initiative of the residual State quoted as attracting about **$17 billion** and **20,000 jobs** = **VCIC** (JL 2020 key; figure not verified). ADB: corridor industrial output to rise fourfold to about **$64 billion a year**.
[0:10] # Nodes
[0:11] TABLE head: Stage | Nodes
  [0:11.0] ADB's four identified nodes (conceptual plan, 2016) | Visakhapatnam (anchor), Kakinada, Gannavaram-Kankipadu, Srikalahasti-Yerpedu (nine clusters in all)
  [0:11.1] Long-term phased nodes | Donakonda and Machilipatnam
  [0:11.2] Nodes now being developed (SES 2024-25) | Vizag, Chittoor (Srikalahasti-Yerpedu), Kopparthy – 33,000 acres, Rs 5,000 crore
[0:12] - **"How many potential nodes on VCIC?"** → **4** (Panchayat Secretary Mains).
[0:13] - Vizag node: **Bulk Drug Park 2,002 acres** + steel cluster (Phase-1 2,200 acres); **Rambilli + Nakkapalli** clusters (Visakhapatnam) ≈ **49%** of corridor output.
[0:14] - Chittoor node: Srikalahasti-Yerpedu start-up area **2,628 acres** (Rs 463 crore).
[0:15] - **Kopparthy** (YSR Kadapa): Phase-1 **2,596 acres**, trunk infra ~Rs 1,264 crore via **NICDIT** (not JICA); **CCEA approved 28 Aug 2024**.
[0:16] - **Orvakal** is an **HBIC** node, not VCIC.
[0:17] > Exam angle: "on the lines of DMIC", ECEC first phase, nine districts, four nodes; do not confuse ADB (VCIC) with JICA (CBIC).

  [1:1.1] CBIC (Chennai-Bengaluru, ~560 km to Chitradurga) | Japan (JICA) | Krishnapatnam

[1:3] # CBIC nodes
[1:4] - JICA's Comprehensive Integrated Master Plan for CBIC: master planning of **three** nodes – **Ponneri** (Tamil Nadu), **Tumakuru** (Karnataka), **Krishnapatnam** (AP) (G2 Prelims 2019 asked "how many"; printed key "five" is wrong).
[1:5] - **Kuppam is NOT a CBIC node** (Panchayat Secretary Mains) – it is only a town along the route.
[1:6] - **Krishnapatnam Industrial Area (KRIS City)**: **Tirupati district** (since 2022), CBIC; Krishnapatnam North Node **10,834 acres**, start-up area 2,500 acres, Rs 2,139 crore via **NICDIT**.
[1:7] - **Orvakal** (Kurnool, HBIC): **2,621 acres** Phase-1, Rs 1,781 crore; CCEA approved **28 Aug 2024**.
[1:8] # National bodies and the Budget

  [1:9.2] NICDP | 11 industrial corridors; Aug 2024: 12 more node projects approved
  [1:9.3] BHAVYA | 2026-27 to 2031-32; Rs 33,660 crore; 100 parks; six in AP
