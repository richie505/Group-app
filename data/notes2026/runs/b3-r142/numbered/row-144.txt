## SUB 0 | The Package – Announcement, Period and Amounts Released
[0:0] # Announcement
[0:1] - Announced **September 2016** by Union FM **Arun Jaitley**, in place of SCS; also called **Special Assistance Measure (SAM)** or "Special Package".
[0:2] - Union Cabinet approved it on **15 March 2017**.
[0:3] - Period: **2015-16 to 2019-20** = five years, the promised SCS period (SCS assurance: **Manmohan Singh**, Rajya Sabha, 2014).
[0:4] # Money released
[0:5] TABLE head: Item | Amount
  [0:5.0] Special Assistance 2016-17 (announced 19 Aug 2016) | Rs 1,976.50 crore = Rs 1,176.50 cr resource gap + Rs 350 cr seven backward districts + Rs 450 cr capital (G1 2017)
  [0:5.1] Resource-gap / revenue deficit grant for 2014-15 | Rs 3,979.50 crore released by 2016
  [0:5.2] Backward districts (to 2016-17) | Rs 1,050 crore at Rs 50 crore per district a year
  [0:5.3] Capital city | Rs 2,500 crore (incl. Rs 1,000 crore from MoUD for Guntur and Vijayawada drainage)
  [0:5.4] Total central assistance to Aug 2016 | Rs 8,379.50 crore (Rs 4,403 cr in 2014-15, Rs 2,000 cr in 2015-16)

## SUB 1 | Components, the EAP Route and SCS vs Package
[1:0] # Components and Act sections
[1:1] TABLE head: Component | Content
  [1:1.0] Special assistance | Money equal to the extra central share AP would get as an SCS State (90%) – via Externally Aided Projects
  [1:1.1] Polavaram (Sec 90) | National project; Centre bears 100% of the irrigation-component cost from 1 Apr 2014
  [1:1.2] Tax (Sec 94(1)) | Industrial incentives; promised 10-year tax holiday delayed, diluted or merged into GST
  [1:1.3] Backward areas (Sec 46(2)) | Rayalaseema and North Coastal – 7 districts (4 + 3); releases stalled
  [1:1.4] Revenue deficit (Sec 46) | Bridge the 2014-15 resource gap
  [1:1.5] Capital | Rs 2,500 crore promised
[1:2] # The EAP route
[1:3] - AP borrows from **World Bank, ADB** etc.; the **Centre repays loan and interest** of EAPs signed and disbursed in **2015-16 to 2019-20** – i.e. **all EAP loans of the period are repaid by the Centre** (APPSC Asst. Director).
[1:4] - Limit: the **SCS-equivalent 90%** share; no open grant; funds tied to **approved EAPs**.
[1:5] - AP's criticism: long approval cycles, heavy paperwork – **red tape and delays**; Jan 2018: CM asked for the money as a grant via **NABARD**.
[1:6] # SCS vs package
[1:7] TABLE head: Benefit | SCS | Special Assistance Package
  [1:7.0] CSS funding | Automatic 90:10 grant | Equivalent amount only via EAP repayments
  [1:7.1] Tax for industry | Yes | Only weaker Sec 94(1) incentives
  [1:7.2] Use of funds | All schemes | Tied to approved EAPs
  [1:7.3] Duration | Promised 5 years | 2015-16 to 2019-20
[1:8] > Exam angle: Centre repays EAP loans; Sec 90 = Polavaram, Sec 94(1) = tax, Sec 46(2) = backward areas.

## SUB 2 | Centre's Stand, AP's Stand and Present Measures
[2:0] # Centre's stand
[2:1] - **14th FC** removed the general-special distinction and raised devolution to **42%** – Centre said this **covered SCS benefits**.
[2:2] - **"Constitutional and technical constraints"** on SCS for a new State; **domino fear**: identical demands from **Bihar and Odisha**.
[2:3] # AP's stand over time
[2:4] TABLE head: Period | Stand
  [2:4.0] TDP govt (NDA ally), 2014-18 | Balanced alliance vs SCS demand
  [2:4.1] YSRCP | Package a "betrayal"; SCS its 2019 campaign plank
  [2:4.2] AP govt 2024-26 (NDA ally) | Seeks project money (Amaravati, Polavaram, SASCI) – did not leave NDA
[2:5] - **Aug 2026**: AP FM **Payyavula Keshav** – loss up to **Rs 38,000 crore** as the **16th FC dropped revenue deficit grants**; AP revenue deficit **Rs 16,000 crore (2014-19)** → projected **Rs 48,000 crore (2025-26)**.
