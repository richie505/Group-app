## SUB 0 | National Constitution Day (samvidhan Divas) 2025
[108:0:0] # The Day
[108:0:1] - Observed on **26 November**; 26 November 2025 marked **76 years** since the adoption of the Constitution.
[108:0:2] - Declared in **2015**, the **125th birth anniversary year of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar**.
[108:0:3] - Earlier observed as **Law Day**.
[108:0:4] Constitution Day is **not a public holiday**.
[108:0:5] # Constitution: Key Dates
[108:0:6] TABLE head: Event | Date
  [108:0:6.0] First meeting of the Constituent Assembly | 9 December 1946
  [108:0:6.1] Drafting Committee constituted (Chairman Dr. B.R. Ambedkar) | 29 August 1947
  [108:0:6.2] Draft Constitution introduced | 4 November 1948
  [108:0:6.3] Constitution adopted | 26 November 1949 (→ Samvidhan Divas)
  [108:0:6.4] Constitution came into force | 26 January 1950
[108:0:7] - Time taken to frame the Constitution: **2 years, 11 months, 18 days**.
[108:0:8] The Drafting Committee was set up on **29 August 1947**, not on 26 November 1949 (that is the date of adoption).

## SUB 3 | Swadeshi Idea and National Handloom Day
[108:3:0] # National Handloom Day – 7 August
[108:3:1] - Observed on **7 August** because the **Swadeshi Movement was launched on 7 August 1905**.
[108:3:2] # Evolution of Swadeshi
[108:3:3] TABLE head: Period | Person / event | Idea
  [108:3:3.0] 1822 | Bengali weekly Samachar Chandrika | Use Indian goods for religious/social reasons
  [108:3:3.1] 19th century | Gopal Hari Deshmukh (1823–1892), Rajnarayan Basu (1826–1899) | Locally produced goods
  [108:3:3.2] Mid-19th century | M.G. Ranade (1842–1901) | Prefer domestic goods even if costlier
  [108:3:3.3] Late 19th century | Dadabhai Naoroji – Drain of Wealth; R.C. Dutt – *The Economic History of India* | Economic nationalism, deindustrialisation
  [108:3:3.4] 1905–1911 | Swadeshi Movement | Vande Mataram the rallying cry
  [108:3:3.5] Gandhi | Khadi, charkha | Economic + moral + cultural swadeshi; village self-sufficiency
  [108:3:3.6] Post-1947 | Mixed economy, import substitution | Support to small-scale industry
[108:3:4] Gandhi's swadeshi was **not purely economic** – it was economic, moral and cultural.

[108:4:0] # World Patient Safety Day – 17 September
[108:4:1] - Observed by **WHO** on **17 September** every year.
[108:4:2] - 2025 theme: **"Safe care for every newborn and every child"**.
[108:4:3] - Harm affects **1 in 10** hospitalised patients; **4 in 10** in outpatient care.

## SUB 8 | Ap Inscriptions, Anniversaries and Festivals
[108:8:0] # Srisailam Copper Plates (1420 CE)
[108:8:1] - Date: **Saka 1341, Sarvari, Magha Sudha Trayodasi = 28 January 1420 CE** – **15th century** CE.
[108:8:2] - Format: **three copper leaves**, bilingual – **Sanskrit and Telugu**.
[108:8:3] - Term: **'Swarajya Punarudhharana'** (restoration of independent state) – **first use of 'Swarajya' in a Telugu inscription** (per Dr K. Munirathnam Reddy, Director (Epigraphy), ASI Mysore).
[108:8:4] - Ruler: **Recharla Lingama Nayaka** (Recharla dynasty) – reclaimed areas from the **Turakas**; restored **eight agraharas**.
[108:8:5] - Freedom-movement link: **Bal Gangadhar Tilak** – "Swarajya is my birthright"; **Tanguturi Prakasam** started the newspaper **'Swarajya'** in **three languages**.
[108:8:6] Headlines said "14th century", but 1420 CE is the **15th century**.
[108:8:7] # Vankeswara Temple Inscription, Durgi (1518 CE)
[108:8:8] - Where: **Vankeswara temple**, **Durgi** mandal HQ, **Palnadu district**; the inscription is **sinking**.
[108:8:9] - Date: **22 November 1518 CE**, reign of **Sri Krishnadevaraya** (Vijayanagara).
[108:8:10] - Commissioned by **Timmapa Nayanim Garu**, a **doorkeeper** in the royal court.
[108:8:11] - Inspected by **E. Sivanagireddy**, CEO, **Pleach India Foundation**.
[108:8:12] # Sri Modakondamma Jatara, Paderu
[108:8:13] - Where: **Modakondamma Temple, Paderu**, **Alluri Sitharama Raju district**, in the **Eastern Ghats**.
[108:8:14] - When: annual tribal festival in **May** – 2025 on **11, 12, 13 May**.
[108:8:15] - Deity: **Grama Devatha**, a form of **Goddess Durga**; revered by **Kondhs and Koyas**.
