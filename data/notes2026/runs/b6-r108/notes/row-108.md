=== National Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas) 2025
# The day
- Observed on **26 November**; 26 November 2025 marked **76 years** since the adoption of the Constitution.
- Declared in **2015**, the **125th birth anniversary year of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar**; earlier observed as **Law Day**; **not a public holiday**.
# Constitution: key dates
| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| First meeting of the Constituent Assembly | 9 December 1946 |
| Drafting Committee constituted (Chairman Dr. B.R. Ambedkar) | **29 August 1947** |
| Draft Constitution introduced | **4 November 1948** |
| Constitution adopted | **26 November 1949** (→ Samvidhan Divas) |
| Constitution came into force | 26 January 1950 |
- Time taken to frame the Constitution: **2 years, 11 months, 18 days**.
# Borrowed features (asked with Constitution Day)
| Source | Features borrowed |
|---|---|
| Government of India Act, 1935 | Federal structure, office of Governor, Public Service Commissions, Emergency provisions |
| USA | Fundamental Rights, **Judicial Review**, Impeachment, Vice President |
| Ireland | DPSPs, method of Presidential election, nomination to Rajya Sabha |
| Canada | Strong Centre, **residuary powers** |
| **Australia** | **Concurrent List, freedom of trade** |
| Germany (Weimar) | **Suspension of Fundamental Rights during Emergency** |
| **USSR** | **Fundamental Duties** |
| France | Liberty, Equality, Fraternity |
| South Africa | Amendment procedure, election of Rajya Sabha members |
| **Japan** | **Procedure Established by Law** |
> Drafting Committee 29 Aug 1947 vs adoption 26 Nov 1949 vs enforcement 26 Jan 1950; residuary powers came from Canada, not Ireland.

=== 150 Years of Vande Mataram
# News
- PM Modi marked 150 years of Vande Mataram in the **127th Mann Ki Baat** (**26 October 2025**); the 150th-year celebration runs **7 November 2025 – 7 November 2026**.
# History of the song
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Written by | **Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay**, 1875 |
| First published | Literary journal **Bangadarshan**, 7 November 1875 |
| Novel | Later included in **'Anandamath' (1882)** |
| Language | Sanskritised Bengali |
| First sung | By **Rabindranath Tagore** at the **1896** INC session, Calcutta |
| Swadeshi Movement (1905–1911) | **Rallying cry** of the movement |
| National Song | First two stanzas; declared on **24 January 1950** |

=== Swadeshi Idea and National Handloom Day
# National Handloom Day – 7 August
- Observed on **7 August** because the **Swadeshi Movement was launched on 7 August 1905**.
# Evolution of swadeshi
| Period | Person / event | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| 1822 | Bengali weekly **Samachar Chandrika** | Use Indian goods for religious/social reasons |
| 19th century | Gopal Hari Deshmukh, Rajnarayan Basu | Locally produced goods |
| Mid-19th century | **M.G. Ranade** | Prefer domestic goods **even if costlier** |
| Late 19th century | **Dadabhai Naoroji – Drain of Wealth**; **R.C. Dutt – *The Economic History of India*** | Economic nationalism, deindustrialisation |
| 1905–1911 | Swadeshi Movement | Vande Mataram the rallying cry |
| Gandhi | Khadi, charkha | Economic + moral + cultural swadeshi |
- Gandhi's swadeshi was **not purely economic** – it was economic, moral and cultural.

=== International Days and Their 2025 Themes
# World Patient Safety Day – 17 September
- Observed by **WHO** on **17 September**; 2025 theme **"Safe care for every newborn and every child"**.
- Harm affects **1 in 10** hospitalised patients and **4 in 10** in outpatient care.
- **Patient Advisory Councils (PACs)** are recommended by the **WHO** Global Patient Safety Action Plan; India: National Patient Safety Implementation Framework (2018–2025).
# World Food Day 2025 – 80 years of India–FAO partnership
- India and **FAO** marked **80 years** of partnership; India is a founding member of FAO (**1945**); book "**Sowing Hope, Harvesting Success**" launched.
- India has less than 4% of the world's agricultural land and freshwater; **NFSA (2013)** covers 800 million+ people.

=== Heritage: ASI, AP Inscriptions and Festivals
# Archaeological Survey of India
- Under the Ministry of Culture; governed by the **AMASR Act, 1958**; manages 3,650+ monuments of national importance.
- Founded **1861**; **Alexander Cunningham** was its **first Director-General** ("Father of Indian Archaeology"); strengthened by Lord Curzon.
- PPP successes in conservation: **Elephanta Caves and Dr Bhau Daji Lad Museum, Mumbai**; INTACH and ASI central to heritage management.
# Srisailam copper plates (1420 CE)
- **Saka 1341 = 28 January 1420 CE** – **15th century** (headlines said 14th); three copper leaves, **Sanskrit and Telugu**.
- 'Swarajya Punarudhharana' – first use of 'Swarajya' in a Telugu inscription; ruler **Recharla Lingama Nayaka** reclaimed areas from the Turakas and restored eight agraharas.
- Freedom-movement link: **Tilak** – "Swarajya is my birthright"; **Tanguturi Prakasam** started the newspaper 'Swarajya' in three languages.
# Vankeswara temple inscription, Durgi (1518 CE)
- **Durgi**, **Palnadu district**; 22 November 1518 CE, reign of Sri Krishnadevaraya; commissioned by **Timmapa Nayanim Garu**, a doorkeeper; the inscription is sinking.
# Sri Modakondamma Jatara
- **Modakondamma Temple, Paderu** (Alluri Sitharama Raju district); May (2025: 11–13 May); Grama Devatha, a form of **Goddess Durga**, revered by Kondhs and Koyas.
