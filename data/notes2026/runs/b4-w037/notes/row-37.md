=== East-flowing vs West-flowing Rivers – Deltas, Estuaries and Direction
# Why most Peninsular rivers flow east
- The **Western Ghats** are the main water divide; the Deccan Plateau is **higher in the west and slopes east** to the Bay of Bengal, so most large rivers flow **east**.
- The Peninsular block was **tilted north-west → south-east** (Himalayan upheaval), giving the whole drainage a Bay of Bengal orientation.
- **Narmada and Tapi** are the only major Peninsular rivers flowing **west** – they occupy **trough faults (rift valleys)**.
# Comparison
| Feature | East-flowing (Bay of Bengal) | West-flowing (Arabian Sea) |
|---|---|---|
| Rivers | **Subarnarekha, Brahmani, Baitarani, Mahanadi, Vamsadhara, Nagavali, Godavari, Krishna, Pennar, Palar, Kaveri, Vaigai, Tamraparni** | **Narmada, Tapi, Mahi, Sabarmati, Vaitarna, Mandovi, Kali, Sharavati, Netravati, Periyar, Bharathapuzha, Pamba** |
| Size | Long and large, dendritic tributaries | Short and swift (except Narmada, Tapi) |
| Mouth | **Extensive deltas** | **No deltas – long, deep estuaries** (natural harbours) |
| Use | Irrigation | Hydropower |
- **Why no deltas in the west:** steep Western Ghats slope (rapid flow), **short courses**, **hard rocky terrain**; Narmada and Tapi filled their fault troughs, so there is little alluvium – sediment is carried away by waves.
- **Mahanadi** (delta near **Paradeep**, Odisha), Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri build deltas; **Narmada, Tapi, Mandovi** end in **estuaries** (Narmada estuary ~27 km, south of **Bharuch**).
- **Subarnarekha** forms an **estuary** between the Ganga and Mahanadi deltas.
- About **600** short, swift rivers drain the west coast.
# Classic odd-ones
- **Kali (Kalinadi)** of Karnataka is **west-flowing** (Diggi, Uttara Kannada → Arabian Sea at Karwar) – not east-flowing.
- **Periyar** – west-flowing, reaches the Arabian Sea via **Vembanad**; **Pennar and Palar** flow east to the Bay of Bengal.
- **Krishna** rises ~64 km from the Arabian Sea but flows ~1,400 km east to the **Bay of Bengal**.
- **Tamraparni** flows east into the Gulf of Mannar – not west-flowing; **Brahmani** is east-flowing.
- **Betwa** is a Yamuna tributary (flows north-east); **Rapti** is a Ghaghara tributary (NW → SE) – neither is west-flowing.
- **Tungabhadra, Penganga** flow east (Krishna / Godavari systems).
- **Kerala:** all major rivers flow west, but the **Kabini** (Wayanad) flows **east** to the Kaveri; **MP:** most rivers flow east but **Narmada and Tapi** flow **west** – so "no east-flowing river in Kerala" and "no west-flowing river in MP" are both **false**.
> A–R (UPSC 2004): "West-flowing rivers have no deltas" / "they do not carry any alluvial sediments" → key (a), both true and R explains A; PMF calls this debatable because "do not carry any" is too absolute.
> A–R (CDS 2010): "East-flowing Deccan rivers have deltas" is true, but "because they flow through soft rocks" is false – the Deccan is hard rock.
> A–R (UPPSC 2014): "Western Ghats rivers do not form deltas" / "short courses, high speed over hard rocks" → both true, R explains A.

=== Krishna System
# Course
- Rises near **Mahabaleshwar** (Sahyadri, Maharashtra; ~1,337 m) → Maharashtra (**Sangli**), Karnataka, Telangana, AP → Bay of Bengal at **Hamsaladeevi**.
- **~1,400 km**; **second-longest Peninsular river** and second-largest east-flowing river; basin ~**8%** of India; one of India's largest deltas.
- Splits into two branches at **Puligadda**, **64 km below Vijayawada**; **Diviseema** lies between them.
- Cities: **Sangli, Vijayawada, Srisailam, Kurnool (Tungabhadra), Hyderabad (Musi)**.
# Tributaries
| Bank | Tributaries |
|---|---|
| Right | **Tungabhadra** (largest), **Ghataprabha**, **Malaprabha**, **Koyna**, Yerla, **Varna (Warna)**, **Panchganga**, Dudhganga |
| Left | **Bhima** (largest left-bank; Chandrabhaga at Pandharpur), **Dindi**, **Musi** (Anantagiri hills; Hyderabad), Munneru |
- **Tungabhadra** = **Tunga + Bhadra**, both rising at **Varaha Parvata (Varaha hills), Western Ghats**; 531 km; joins the Krishna at **Sangameswaram** (Kurnool); **Hampi** on its bank; **Hagari (Vedavathi)** is its biggest tributary.
- **Raichur Doab** = wedge between the **Krishna and Tungabhadra**.
- **Not Krishna tributaries:** **Tel** (Mahanadi), **Gomati** (Ganga). **Panchganga** and **Koyna** belong to the Krishna, not the Godavari; Mand, Warna, Tungabhadra are Krishna tributaries.
# Dams and projects
| Project | Place / note |
|---|---|
| **Nagarjuna Sagar** | On the Krishna near **Nandikonda**; multipurpose; then in undivided AP – now on the **Telangana (Nalgonda)–AP (Palnadu) border** |
| **Srisailam** | On the Krishna (not Tungabhadra); HEP; heart of the **Nagarjuna Sagar–Srisailam Tiger Reserve** |
| **Almatti** (**Lal Bahadur Shastri Dam**) | Krishna, Bijapur/Vijayapura, **Karnataka**; main reservoir of the **Upper Krishna Project** |
| Tungabhadra (Pampa Sagar) | Near Hosapete |
- **Krishna Raja Sagara (KRS)** is on the **Kaveri**, not the Krishna, despite the name.
> "Rises in Maharashtra, flows through Karnataka and AP, meets the Bay of Bengal on the Andhra coast" (also "passes Sangli, meets the sea at Hamsaladeevi") = **Krishna**; the Godavari does not enter Karnataka.

- **Western Ghats** = "**Water Tower of Peninsular India**" – sources of the **Godavari, Krishna and Kaveri**.
- **Amarkantak** is where two major rivers rise – the **Son** flows **north** to join the Ganga (Bay of Bengal) and the **Narmada** flows **west** to the Arabian Sea (UPSC 2009). **Damodar** does **not** rise at Amarkantak (Khamarpat hill, Chota Nagpur).
- **Son – "actual" source:** usually given as **Amarkantak** (Sonmuda, ~1.5 km from Narmada Kund); the stricter view places its origin near **Pendra** – the village **Sonbachharwar**, Pendra tehsil (then **Bilaspur district**, now Gaurela-Pendra-Marwahi, Chhattisgarh).
- **Bhedaghat** (marble rocks, Jabalpur), Shahdol and Hoshangabad lie **on the course** of the Narmada, not at its source.

=== Mahanadi, Kaveri, Pennar and Other East-flowing Rivers
# Mahanadi
- Rises at **Sihawa** (Chhattisgarh) → Chhattisgarh and **Odisha** (does **not** pass through AP); **851 km**; basin **1,41,589 sq km** (~4.3% of India); **Cuttack**; **delta in Odisha near Paradeep**; second to the Godavari in water potential and flood capacity.
| Bank | Tributaries |
|---|---|
| Left | **Seonath (Shivnath)**, **Hasdeo** (largest), **Mand**, **Ib** |
| Right | **Ong, Tel, Jonk** |
- **Hirakud Dam** on the Mahanadi, ~15 km from **Sambalpur** (Odisha) – longest earthen dam (main dam 4.8 km). **Kuakhai** (Bhubaneswar) is a delta branch.
- **Tel** = right-bank tributary of the **Mahanadi** (not of the Krishna or any Bihar river).
# Kaveri (Cauvery)
- **Talakaveri, Brahmagiri hills, Kodagu (Karnataka)** → Karnataka, **Kerala** (Kabini catchment), **Tamil Nadu**, Puducherry → Bay of Bengal near **Poompuhar**; ~800 km; basin 81,155 sq km.
- Called **"Dakshina Ganga" / "Ganga of the South"** (the Godavari is also called Dakshin/Vriddha Ganga).
- **Cauvery delta = "South India's granary"** (Thanjavur).
| Bank | Tributaries |
|---|---|
| Left | Harangi, **Hemavathi**, Shimsha, Arkavathi |
| Right | Lakshmantirtha, **Kabini (Kapila)**, Suvarnavati, **Bhavani**, Noyyal, **Amaravati** |
- **Kabini** – Wayanad (Kerala), flows east, joins at **Tirumakudalu Narsipur**.
- Forks thrice: islands **Srirangapatna, Sivasamudram, Srirangam**; dams **KRS (Kannambadi)**, **Mettur**; most exploited river (highest share abstracted for irrigation).
# Pennar and smaller rivers
- **Pennar (Uttara Pinakini)** – Nandi hills (Karnataka) → AP (Anantapur, Kadapa – **Gandikota gorge**, Nellore) → Bay of Bengal; **lifeline of Rayalaseema**; tributaries **Jayamangali**, Kunderu, Sagileru (left), Chitravathi, **Papagni**, Cheyyeru (right).
- **Palar** – Nandi hills → Karnataka, AP (Chittoor), Tamil Nadu. **Vaigai** – Madurai → Palk Bay.
- **Brahmani** (South Koel + Sankh) and **Baitarani** – common delta at **Bhitarkanika** (Odisha). **Subarnarekha** – Ranchi plateau; estuary.
- **Machkund** – Duduma falls; **Machkund HEP** (AP–Odisha) is on the **Machkund** river.
# Hilsa
- **Hilsa (Indian shad)** migrates up the east-coast rivers; in AP the **Godavari's "Pulasa"** (Jul–Sep floods) is the famous catch; the **Narmada estuary (Bharuch)** now lands the largest hilsa catch on the west coast; Hooghly and Mahanadi also have hilsa.

=== Godavari System
- **Dakshin Ganga / Vriddha Ganga** – called so for its **length and the area it covers** and its many tributaries; **longest Peninsular river** (1,465 km), second longest in India; **largest Peninsular basin** (3,12,812 sq km, ~9.5% of India).
- Rises at **Trimbakeshwar (Nashik)** → Maharashtra (Nanded), Telangana, AP → Bay of Bengal; basin also in Chhattisgarh, Odisha, MP, Karnataka, Puducherry (Yanam).
- **Widest/extensive delta** of the Deccan (**Konaseema**); after Rajahmundry splits into **Gautami** (east) and **Vasishta** (west), which divide into **7 branches** (Sapta Godavari).
| Branch | Mouth |
|---|---|
| Gautami | Yanam |
| Vasishta | Antarvedi |
| Vainateya | Komaragiripatnam |
| Tulya, Bharadwaja, Kaushika, Athreya | Bendamoorlanka / Coringa area |
- **2.7 km** wide at Rajahmundry; **Sir Arthur Cotton's anicut** at **Dowleswaram** (1852).
# Tributaries
| Bank | Tributaries |
|---|---|
| Left | **Purna**, **Pranhita** (Penganga + Wardha + Wainganga), **Indravati**, **Sabari**, Kinnerasani |
| Right | **Pravara**, **Manjira (Manjeera)**, **Maner (Manair)** |
- **Pranhita** = **largest tributary** (biggest flow), joins near **Kaleshwaram**; Penganga reaches the Godavari **via the Wardha–Pranhita**, not the Ganga.
- **Indravati** rises in **Kalahandi (Odisha)**, flows through Bastar (Jagdalpur; Chitrakote falls).
- **Sabari** (= **Kolab** in Odisha; Sinkaram hills) is the **last major tributary**, joining near **Kunavaram** above the delta; its tributary **Sileru** (upper reaches **Machkund**) joins it at the **AP–Chhattisgarh–Odisha** tri-junction.
- **Nizam Sagar** is on the **Manjira** (Kamareddy, Telangana).
- **Not Godavari tributaries:** **Koyna, Ghataprabha, Panchganga, Bhima** (Krishna), **Vamsadhara, Pennar**, **Amaravati** (Kaveri).
> The Godavari is **not** perennial everywhere: like other Peninsular rivers it is rain-fed and seasonal in its upper Maharashtra course; Narmada and Tapi (rift-valley rivers) have more sustained flow.

=== Structure and Sequence – Older Drainage, Rift Valleys, Consequent and Superimposed Rivers
- Peninsular drainage is **older** than the Himalayan – **broad, graded, shallow valleys**, **fixed courses**, no meanders, **non-perennial** flow.
- **Consequent rivers** follow the general slope – **most Peninsular rivers**, e.g. **Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri** (Western Ghats → Bay of Bengal). **Subsequent:** Chambal, Sind, Ken, Betwa, Son meet the Yamuna/Ganga at right angles.
- **Superimposed** rivers keep a course developed on a former cover rock – **Chambal**, Damodar, Subarnarekha, Banas.
# Rift valleys
| River | Rift between / note |
|---|---|
| **Narmada** | **Vindhya (north) and Satpura (south)** – divides the **Deccan Tableland from northern India**; west-flowing |
| **Tapi** | **Satpura (north) and Ajanta (south)** |
| **Damodar** | Chota Nagpur – **east-flowing rift valley due to down-warping** (Gondwana trough); called a "**biological desert**" because of heavy industrial pollution |
# Sequences
- East-flowing, **north → south:** **Subarnarekha, Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Pennar, Kaveri, Vaigai** (UPSC 2002) – so south → north = Kaveri, Pennar, Krishna, Godavari, Mahanadi.
- **North → south:** Godavari, Pennar, Kaveri, **Periyar**; **Penganga, Godavari, Bhima, Tungabhadra**.
- Whole India N → S: **Indus – Ganga – Godavari – Krishna** (Ganga – Mahanadi – Godavari – Krishna is also N → S).
- Main systems: **Himalayan** = Indus, Ganga, Brahmaputra (the Indus rises on the northern slopes of the **Kailash range near Mansarovar**); **Peninsular** = Narmada, Tapi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri, Mahanadi.

=== Narmada, Tapi and the West-coast Rivers
# Narmada
- Amarkantak (Maikal) → MP, Maharashtra (border), Gujarat → **Gulf of Khambhat** south of Bharuch; **1,312 km** – **longest west-flowing**; **Jabalpur, Bhedaghat** (marble rocks, **Dhuandhar**), Kapildhara near Amarkantak.
- Tributaries: **Tawa** (longest; Mahadeo hills; joins at Bandra Bhan), Burhner, **Banjar**, Sher, Shakkar, **Dudhi**, Ganjal (left); **Hiran**, Kolar, Orsang (right). **Purna** is **not** a Narmada tributary.
- Projects: **Sardar Sarovar** (Gujarat), **Indira Sagar** (Khandwa, MP – **largest reservoir in India by volume**; Nagarjuna Sagar 2nd), **Omkareshwar** (Khandwa), **Bargi**, Maheshwar. The Narmada Valley plan has **30 major dams** – **29 major projects in MP** plus Sardar Sarovar in Gujarat.
- Riverine islands: **Aliabet** (largest), **Sarfuddin, Dashan, Mehgam, Vegni**.
- Basin: MP, Gujarat, Maharashtra and **Chhattisgarh** (Kabirdham/**Rajnandgaon** area of the Maikal).
# Tapi (Tapti)
- **Multai, Betul (MP)** → MP, Maharashtra, Gujarat → Arabian Sea at **Suvali near Surat**; **724 km**, second-longest west-flowing; "twin / handmaid of the Narmada"; **Purna** (largest tributary), Girna; **Ukai** dam.
# Other west-flowing rivers
| River | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Mahi** | Minda, Vindhyas (Dhar, **MP**) → Banswara (Rajasthan) → Gujarat → **Gulf of Khambhat**; crosses the **Tropic of Cancer twice** |
| **Sabarmati** | Aravallis (Udaipur) → **Ahmedabad** → Gulf of Khambhat; **shortest and smallest** of the major (>20,000 sq km) basins |
| **Vaitarna** | Trimbak hills (Nashik) → Arabian Sea north of Mumbai |
| **Mithi** | Rises at **Vihar Lake**, Mumbai; Powai Lake water joins; Mahim Bay |
| **Periyar** | Longest river of Kerala; **Mullaperiyar** dam; **Idukki/Cheruthoni** dam on a Periyar tributary |
| Sharavati / Kali / Netravati / Mandovi | Jog falls / Diggi, Karwar / Mangaluru / Dudhsagar, Goa |

=== Luni and Inland Drainage
- **Luni** – rises in the Aravallis south-west of **Ajmer** (near Pushkar; called **Sagarmati**), flows west then south-west after **Telwara**, and is **lost in the marshy Rann of Kutch** – does not reach the sea (not the Gulf of Khambhat or Kutch); **endorheic / land-bound**; largest river of the Thar; ~495–511 km.
- Its water is **fresh in the upper course and saline in the lower course** (salt-bearing desert soils).
- Left-bank tributaries **Jawai, Sukri**, Guhiya, Bandi; right bank Jojari.
- Inland drainage: **Luni, Ghaggar**. **Ghaggar** water is **not** used by the Indira Gandhi Canal (which draws on the Sutlej–Beas at Harike).

=== River Linking, Water Disputes and Watershed
- After bifurcation (2014), **Telangana and AP** fight over the **Krishna and Godavari** waters (KRMB, GRMB; Polavaram–Banakacherla / Nallamala Sagar link opposed by **Telangana**).
- **River linking:** first proposed by **Sir Arthur Cotton** (19th c.); **National Perspective Plan 1980** (NWDA) – **30 links = 14 Himalayan + 16 Peninsular**. First link completed: **Godavari–Krishna (Pattiseema), 2015**. **Ken-Betwa** (MP–UP; Daudhan dam in **Panna Tiger Reserve**) is the flagship link; **Par-Tapi-Narmada** – Gujarat and Maharashtra; **Tapti Basin Mega Recharge** – MP–Maharashtra MoU (May 2025). AP priorities: Vamsadhara-Penna intra-linking, Godavari-Krishna.
- **Disputes:** **Inter-State River Water Disputes Act 1956** under **Article 262**; water = **Entry 17, State List**, subject to **Entry 56, Union List**.
| Dispute | Tribunal / outcome |
|---|---|
| Krishna (MH, KA, AP/Telangana) | KWDT-I **Bachawat (1969/1973)**; KWDT-II **Brijesh Kumar (2004)** |
| Kaveri (KA, TN, Kerala, Puducherry) | CWDT 2007; **SC 2018: TN 404.25, KA 284.75 tmcft** |
| Mahadayi/Mandovi | **Goa, Karnataka, Maharashtra** (award 2018) |
- **Watershed** = small catchment (basin = large); **PMKSY-WDC** run by the **Department of Land Resources, Ministry of Rural Development**.
