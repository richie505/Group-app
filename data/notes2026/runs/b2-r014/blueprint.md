## 4. Key decisions
- **yc879609a8d5** (APPSC Panchayat Secretary 2017, "soul of the Constitution"): keyed Right to Constitutional Remedies (Ambedkar). The Preamble is also called the "soul" in some books (Rocket p.37), and the explanation says so.
- **yabb6264f225** (Art. 226 cannot be invoked if …): the printed "all" is wrong because Art. 226 covers "any other purpose". Keyed the alternative and equally efficacious remedy (T.R. Verma).
- **yf6f3fff4c24** (UPPCS 2019 A-R): keyed (d) against the official (a). The SC's jurisdiction over Central laws is not exclusive.
- **y88e2ffaedd8** (Art. 33 "except"): Art. 33(d) covers only the telecom staff of the named forces, so "communication sector" in general is the exception.
