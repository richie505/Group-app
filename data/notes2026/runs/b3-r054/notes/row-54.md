=== Andhra Pradesh Land Records, Registration and Resurvey
# Portals and records
| Platform | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Bhudhaar (Bhudaar)** | Web portal (with Bhuseva) launched by CM N. Chandrababu Naidu on **20 Nov 2018**; gives every land parcel an **11-digit unique number**; people **view land records online** |
| **Meebhoomi** | Earlier portal (13 June 2015) to view Adangal / 1-B records |
| **Webland** | AP's digitised land records platform |
| **Bhu Bharathi** | Computerisation of land records, united AP, **1992**; pilot **Nizamabad** |
| **CARD** | Computer-aided registration: **anywhere registration, auto-mutation**, at Grama Sachivalayams – a registration system, **not a court** |
| **Mee Bhoomi-Blockchain** | Pilot **24 July 2026**, **7 mandals** |
- **Encumbrance Certificate (EC):** issued by the **Sub-Registrar's (Registration) office** where the property documents are registered; shows the charges/transactions on a property.
- **Village secretariat land-record staff:** AP proposed a **Survey Assistant** in each village secretariat for **100% verification of land records**; the post was filled in 2019 as **Village Surveyor (Grade-III)**.
# Resurvey and passbooks
- **Resurvey** (Jagananna Saswatha Bhu Hakku and Bhu Raksha) from **December 2020**: drones, CORS, GIS; **6,901 villages, 81 lakh parcels**.
- **QR-coded pattadar passbooks** (January 2026) carry the **State emblem**: 36 lakh issued; 56 lakh more by March 2027.
- **N-LRSI** (NCAER Land Records and Services Index) grades States on **digitisation and quality of land records**: AP **10th (2020) → 6th (2021)**; **Madhya Pradesh** topped.
- **Karnataka Bhoomi (2000):** first to computerise **RTCs** (Records of Rights, Tenancy and Crops).

=== SVAMITVA – Drone Mapping of Village Abadi
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Full form | **Survey of Villages and Mapping with Improvised Technology in Village Areas** |
| Launch | **24 April 2020** (National Panchayati Raj Day); nationwide 2021 |
| Ministry / type | **Panchayati Raj** (not Mines); **Central Sector** |
| Agencies | **Survey of India** (implementing); **NICSI** (technology) |
| Technology | **Drones + CORS** (Continuously Operating Reference Stations) – an integrated **property validation solution for rural India** |
| Coverage | **Abadi (inhabited village) areas only** – not agricultural land |
| Output | **Property Cards** (Records of Rights, also in DigiLocker): **3.06 crore** in 1.86 lakh villages (Jan 2026; earlier 2.76 crore) |
| Target | 6.62 lakh villages; drone survey done in **3.28 lakh** of 3.44 lakh notified villages (Dec 2025) |
> Exam angle: drones + CORS + property cards = SVAMITVA; ULPIN and DILRMP are land-record programmes, PMGSY is roads.

=== DILRMP, ULPIN and NGDRS
- Began as **NLRMP in 2008** (UPPSC 2016) = merger of **CLR** (Computerisation of Land Records) + **SRA&ULR** (Strengthening of Revenue Administration and Updating of Land Records); renamed **Digital India Land Records Modernisation Programme**.
- **Central Sector, 100% central funding** from **1 April 2016** (earlier centrally sponsored); extended to **2025-26** with two new components – **consent-based Aadhaar seeding** and **computerisation of revenue courts**; mandatory **gender column** in the RoR.
- Components: modern record rooms, survey/resurvey, **digitised cadastral maps**, computerised registration, State data centre (Property Cards for abadi are **SVAMITVA's**, not DILRMP's).
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ULPIN (Bhu-Aadhaar)** | **14-digit alphanumeric** parcel ID from the **geo-coordinates of the parcel's vertices**; 36.67 crore parcels |
| **NGDRS** | "**One Nation One Registration Software**" – common, configurable registration application for sub-registrars and citizens |
| **Land Stack** (National Digital Land Initiative) | Cloud GIS; a **Bhu-Aadhaar** for every parcel |
| Phase (DILRMP 3.0) | 1 (2008-16) digitisation, resurvey; **2 (2016-26) ULPIN, NGDRS, e-Court integration**; 3 (2026-31) GIS Land Stack |
- Progress: **RoR digitised 99.8%**; cadastral maps 97.24%; SROs computerised **95.73%**.

=== Land Titling – Presumptive vs Conclusive
| Presumptive (present) | Conclusive |
|---|---|
| Registration only **records a transaction**; title can be challenged | **State guarantees the title**; cannot be disputed once issued |
| Registration, survey and revenue records kept separately | **Single title register** |
- **Clear titles** → land usable as **collateral**, less litigation.
- **NITI Aayog Model Conclusive Land Titling Act, 2020:** adoption is **each State's choice**.
- **SC (1 January 2026):** **registration of a transfer deed is distinct from title**.
- **AP Land Titling Act, 2022:** presumptive → **conclusive**, State as guarantor; criticised for limiting disputes to the **High Court**; **repealed 2024**.
