=== Grading and Standardisation – AGMARK
- **AGMARK** = **quality** certification mark for **agricultural goods**, under the **Agricultural Produce (Grading and Marking) Act, 1937** (trap: not "Grading and Marketing").
- Issued by the **Directorate of Marketing and Inspection (DMI)**, Ministry of Agriculture – **not FAO, not FCI, not BIS**.
| Laboratory | Place |

| Central Quality Control Laboratory (APPSC) | **Nagpur** |
- **BIS Standard Mark is mandatory for automotive tyres and tubes** (UPSC 2017: only I correct).
- **AGMARKNET** (2000) = market-price portal. **One Nation, One Regulator** = end dual certification under **BIS and AGMARK**.

=== e-NAM – National Agriculture Market
- Launched **April 2016**; "**One Nation, One Market**"; online platform **linking APMC mandis and other yards** – a virtual market backed by physical mandis (does not replace them).
- Lead agency **SFAC**; Central Sector, funded via **Agri-Tech Infrastructure Fund** (2015); up to **Rs 75 lakh per mandi**.
- **Reforms required of States:** **electronic trading / e-auction** for price discovery, **single licence valid across the State**, **single-point levy** of market fee – NOT abolition of market trading or State price regulation.
- **E-auction** = bidding through an **electronic trading portal**.
- **Direct payment** to the farmer's bank account; warehouse trading with **e-NWRs**.
- **Economic Survey 2022-23:** online, competitive, transparent bidding with **1.74 crore farmers and 2.39 lakh traders** = e-NAM. Mar 2026: **1,656 mandis**.
- **AP:** **33 markets** on e-NAM; **11 mandis** in inter-mandi trade (2024-25).
~~ Sources: Ramesh Singh Indian Economy (10th ed.) part 04 p.168; AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.95; app notes 3:56 sub 5

=== APMC Acts, Model Acts and the Farm Laws 2020
- Agricultural markets = **State List**; markets are regulated under **APMC Acts enacted by States** (UPSC 2015) – not the Essential Commodities Act or the Grading and Marking Act.
- AP law: **AP (Agricultural Produce and Livestock) Markets Act, 1966**. Set-up order: **area notified** → Market Committee → market yard.
- Regulated markets ensure **open auction, standard weights, recorded prices, licensed buyers**; ~**2,477** regulated markets; market fee **1%**.
- APMC problems: market/licence fees, commission charges, **cartels of licensed traders**, too few mandis; **price spread** = **consumer price − farmer's price**; NCF: a regulated market within **~5 km**; poor storage → **distress sale**.
- Farmer's share in the consumer rupee (RBI, Oct 2024): tomato ~**33%**, pulses higher (gram ~**75%**).
| Model Act | Key feature |
|---|---|
| **Model APMC Act, 2003** | **Direct sale to contract-farming sponsors**; private markets; farmer-consumer markets |
| **Model APLM Act, 2017** | **Whole State one market area**; single-point fee, unified licence |
| **Model Contract Farming Act, 2018** | Outside APMC; sponsor buys the **entire pre-agreed quantity**; farmer = **weaker party**; registration by **RARC** |

=== Contract Farming, e-Choupal and Small Farmer Large Field
- **Contract farming** = arrangement between farmer producers and agri-business firms to sell produce at a **pre-fixed Price or Quantity or Time or All Three**; buyer often supplies inputs and advice; **no land transfer**. Pioneer: **Punjab (PepsiCo, late 1980s)**.
- **e-Choupal** = **ITC**'s internet-kiosk rural marketing network (2000).
- **Small Farmer Large Field (UPSC 2023):** many marginal farmers in an area **organise into groups and synchronise/harmonise selected operations** – they work as one large field (no land surrender).

=== Cooperative Marketing, NAFED and FPOs
- **NAFED** (2 October 1958) = **apex cooperative marketing body at national level** – agricultural marketing (not FCI); PM-AASHA PSS: **pulses, oilseeds, copra**.
- **TRIFED** (1987) = tribal produce marketing. **AP MARKFED** = apex of Cooperative Marketing Societies in AP; **Rs 3,000 crore Price Stabilisation Fund**.
- **10,000 FPO scheme (2020):** Central Sector, **Rs 6,865 crore**; agencies **SFAC, NABARD, NCDC, NAFED**; one FPO per **block**; CBBO handholding **5 years**; management support Rs 18 lakh over 3 years; equity grant **Rs 2,000 per member** (max Rs 15 lakh); credit guarantee up to **Rs 2 crore**. Aim: bargaining power, scale, lower costs (not land transfer).
- **NCDC** promotes cooperative-sector FPOs through PACS.
- **Producer companies:** introduced by the **Companies (Amendment) Act, 2002** (Part IXA).
- **SFAC "Millets Giveaway"** (IYM 2023): buy directly from FPOs, try ONDC's MyStore, adopt Shree Anna – **not FDI**.
~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.62, p.65; app notes 3:56 sub 7; general knowledge (Producer companies 2002), not checked

=== Operation Greens, Export Schemes and Agri Infrastructure
- **Operation Greens:** **Union Budget 2018-19**, for **TOP (tomato, onion, potato)** price volatility, "**on the lines of Operation Flood**"; promotes FPOs, agri-logistics, processing; Ministry of **Food Processing Industries**.
- **TOP to TOTAL:** Budget **2021-22**, **22** perishables; long-term grant 50% (**70% for FPOs**).
- **Vishesh Krishi (and Gram) Upaj Yojana:** promotes **exports of agricultural produce** by offsetting freight costs.
- **AIF (2020):** **Rs 1 lakh crore**; **3%** subvention on loans up to **Rs 2 crore**; guarantee via **CGTMSE**.
