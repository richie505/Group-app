=== Urban Missions – AMRUT 2.0, Smart Cities Mission, UIDF
- **AMRUT 2.0:** launched **1 October 2021** by the **Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA)** for five years (to 2025-26); urban water security, water supply to all statutory towns, wastewater recycling, a circular water economy.
- **Smart Cities Mission:** launched **25 June 2015** (MoHUA) for **100 cities**; after extensions it **formally ended on 31 March 2025**; legacy – Integrated Command and Control Centres (ICCCs).
- **Urban Infrastructure Development Fund (UIDF):** announced in **Budget 2023-24** for **Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities**; operated / managed by the **National Housing Bank (NHB)** from priority-sector lending shortfall.
