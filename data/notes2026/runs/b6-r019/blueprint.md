## 3. Angles
- **Factual recall** of one-off news items (about 70%), usually from the year before the paper. Most cannot be checked in the library, so they stay on the printed key ("printed key, not verified").
- **"Which of these is from AP"**: the distractors are from Telangana (Jeevanji Deepthi, Pochampally Ikat).
- **NOT / statements**: GI "not registered" items carry a date ("till Nov 2016"), so the registration year decides. Sahitya Akademi statements test the genre and book (short stories vs poetry; criticism).
- **Match**: person and art form; farmer and district.
- **Outdated risk**: State symbols changed in 2018; office-holders (Home Minister, TTD posts) change with each government.
