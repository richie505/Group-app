## SUB 1 | Heritage Village and Heritage Finds
[1:0] # Kangundi – AP's First Heritage Village
[1:1] - **Kangundi** village **near Kuppam** to be declared **AP's first Heritage Village**.
[1:2] - Framework: Centre's **Swachh, Suramya and Sanskrutik** framework, part of the **Swarna Kuppam** initiative.
[1:3] - Location: **tri-junction of AP, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu**.
[1:4] - Known for **Neolithic cave art**, bouldering rocks, temples of **Chola, Hoysala, Vijayanagara** periods.
[1:5] - Plans: **Kangundi Rocks Bouldering Gymnasium**; annual **Kangundi Bouldering Festival & Climbing Cup**; **AP Sports Climbing Hub**; Olympic-standard climbing wall.
[1:6] - Institutions: KADA, AP Tourism, **Indian Mountaineering Foundation**, Mahavatar Babaji TadEkam Foundation.
[1:7] > Kangundi is under **Swarna Kuppam** (Swachh, Suramya, Sanskrutik) – **not HRIDAY or PRASHAD**.

[5:1] - **GI tag: March 2024** (registered by **DPIIT** in the GI Registry); certificate given to the West Godavari Collector at **GI & Beyond 2024**, New Delhi (Nov 2024). Also under **ODOP**.
[5:2] - Exports **over ₹150 crore** in the current financial year – revival after Covid.
[5:3] - Districts: **West Godavari** and **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Konaseema**.
[5:4] - Origin **1844**, brought by **Scottish missionaries**; survived the **1899 famine** and **1929 Great Depression**.
