## SUB 0 | UNESCO Push for Lepakshi and Gandikota
[0:0] # The demand
[0:1] - AP urged the Centre (meeting with the **Union Tourism and Culture Minister**, New Delhi) to back **UNESCO World Heritage** status for **Lepakshi** and **Gandikota**.
[0:2] - **Lepakshi** (temple architecture, **murals**, sculpture) is **already on the UNESCO Tentative List**; **Gandikota** (**fort and gorge landscape**) is **not yet**.
[0:3] - Concern also raised over **Amaravati Buddhist sculptures** kept in **London museums**.

## SUB 1 | Inscriptions in News
[1:0] TABLE head: Find | Place | Key point
  [1:0.0] Pune copper plates | Guntur | 669 CE, first regnal year of Eastern Chalukya Vishnuvardhana II; name Kunturu > Gomturu > Gunturu
  [1:0.1] Odia inscription | Tekkali, Srikakulam | Gajapati Kapilendra Deva ("Pratapa Kapileswara")
  [1:0.2] 3 inscriptions (1554) | Sadasivakona, Seshachalam forest (Tirupati district) | Sadasiva Deva Raya (Vijayanagara)
  [1:0.3] Reddy-era inscription | Kondaveedu (Palnadu district) | Two Shiva temples built under Anavema Reddy
[1:1] # Guntur's antiquity
[1:2] - Highlighted by **Dr. K. Munirathnam Reddy**, **ASI Director of Epigraphy**.
[1:3] - **10th-century Ederu copper plates** use the name **"Gomturu"** (in use 10th-14th century).
[1:4] # Tekkali Odia inscription (Gajapati era)
[1:5] - Found **under a Tulasi Choura** at **Tekkali royal palace**; script and language **Odia**; line 4 has **"Bijeraije"**.
[1:6] - Earlier record: **Annual Report on Indian Epigraphy 1926-27**; date likely **15th century** (palaeography), exact year not known; estampage taken.
[1:7] - **Kapilendra Deva's** kingdom: from **Ganga** (north) to **Kaveri** (south). Tekkali = a former **Odia zamindari of Ganjam**.
[1:8] # Sadasivakona inscriptions
[1:9] - Found by the **ASI epigraphy team** led by **K. Munirathnam Reddy** (Director of Epigraphy, **ASI, Mysuru**), after a survey in the **Nallamala Hills**.
[1:10] - **3 rare inscriptions**, dated **1554**, in **Telugu, Tamil, Kannada**.
[1:11] - Main record: **Sadasiva Raya**'s visit for a **sacred bath** and **royal donations**; a **Siva temple and a mutt** built at **Papavinasam**; copied as **estampages** (impressions).
[1:12] - Related: **Gudimallam Siva shrine** - **2nd century BCE**.
[1:13] - **ASI**: est. **1861**; **DG Yadubir Singh Rawat**. **Vijayanagara Empire**: est. **1336** by **Harihara I and Bukka Raya I**.
[1:14] # Kondaveedu Reddy inscription
[1:15] - 14th-century Reddy-period inscription on a **Shiva temple doorway pillar**; on **Rudrapadam (Adam Shafi Hill)**; **Kondaveedu Fort** = 14th-century hill fort.
[1:16] - **Reddy Kingdom**: est. **1325** by **Prolaya Vema Reddy**; **first capital Addanki** (not Kondaveedu), later Kondaveedu; promoted Telugu culture and literature.
[1:17] - **Vasantotsavam** = most popular festival under the **Reddi kings**.

## SUB 2 | Heritage Sites, Caves and Museums
[2:0] # Kotadibba, Gottiprolu - Monument of National Importance
[2:1] - **Kotadibba**, **Gottiprolu village**, **Tirupati district**: declared a **Monument of National Importance**; about **24.22 acres** (Survey Nos. 235, 131/1); protected under **AMASR Act, 1958**.
[2:2] - **ASI excavations (2019)**: **massive brick fortification wall**, large **residential complex**, **Vishnu idol**, pottery.
[2:3] # Undavalli Caves and the Telugu Mahotsavam
[2:4] - **Two-day Telugu Mahotsavam** at **Undavalli Caves, Guntur district** (not Krishna) by the **AP State Creativity and Culture Samiti**, to promote Telugu language, literature and arts.
[2:5] - **First held** (closing event) at Undavalli Caves in **July 2026**; made an **annual event**.
[2:6] - Undavalli: **4th-5th century AD** rock-cut **sandstone** caves on a hill beside the **Krishna**; Gupta period; linked to **Vishnukundina** kings (**420-620 AD**).
[2:7] - Largest cave **four storeys**; **2nd floor**: huge **Vishnu in Ananthasayana** posture carved from a **single granite stone**; temples to the **Trimurti**.
[2:8] - **Originally a Buddhist vihara** - Buddhist sculptures on the first floor.
[2:9] TABLE head: Other rock-cut caves | State
  [2:9.0] Ajanta, Ellora, Elephanta | Maharashtra
  [2:9.1] Badami | Karnataka
  [2:9.2] Udayagiri | Madhya Pradesh
[2:10] # Kakinada palm-leaf manuscript museum
[2:11] - **Ministry of Culture** released **₹5 crore** under the **Museum Grant Scheme** for a new building of the **Andhra Sahitya Parishad Government Museum and Research Institute, Kakinada**.
[2:12] - Total support proposed **₹10 crore**; funding **80:20 (Centre:State)** - not 60:40; **ground-plus-four** building; work from early August.
[2:13] - About **5,000 palm-leaf manuscripts** (17th-19th centuries): **Ayurveda, mathematics, astrology, Telugu and Sanskrit literature, classical music**.
[2:14] - **500 manuscripts chemically treated in 2021**; collection handed over by Andhra Sahitya Parishad in the **1970s**.
[2:15] - Also holds Buddhist pillars, sculptures, megalithic tools, copper and bronze plates, coins.

## SUB 8 | Wildlife, Agri-science, Minerals and Industry
[8:0] # Rare pangolin rescued at Kamalapuram (Kadapa)
[8:1] - **Kamalapuram** municipal area, **Kadapa district**; local name **"alugu"**; released in the **Mydukur range**; likely came via the **Kundu River** basin.
[8:2] - **8 species**: African - **Black-bellied, White-bellied, Giant Ground, Temminck's Ground**; Asian - **Indian, Chinese, Sunda, Philippine**.
[8:3] - Pangolins are **mammals**; **Indian Pangolin** range: **India, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh**.
[8:4] TABLE head: Species | Status
  [8:4.0] Indian Pangolin | IUCN Endangered
  [8:4.1] Chinese Pangolin | IUCN Critically Endangered
  [8:4.2] Both (found in India) | Schedule I, WPA 1972
  [8:4.3] All 8 species | CITES Appendix I
[8:5] # IIOPR-Patanjali Foods MoU
[8:6] - **ICAR-Indian Institute of Oil Palm Research (IIOPR)**, **Pedavegi** near **Eluru**, **Eluru district**, signed an MoU with **Patanjali Foods** to use its **rapid diagnostic technology** for early detection of **Ganoderma** in oil palm before symptoms appear.
[8:7] - **Ganoderma** = fungus causing **Basal Stem Rot** of oil palm (not citrus canker or late blight).
[8:8] # Minerals and industry
[8:9] - AP holds **92% of India's baryte (BaSO4)** reserves (Ministry of Mines); **Mangampeta** (Tirupati district) - one of the **world's largest** deposits; used as a **weighting agent in drilling fluids**. AP also has mica, calcite, kyanite, granite.
[8:10] - **Bharat Forge**: **India's first private marine gas turbine facility**, in AP.
