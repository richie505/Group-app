## SUB 0 | Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972 – Year, Scope and Key Sections
[0:0] # Year and scope
[0:1] - **Enacted by Parliament in 1972** (assent 9 Sept 1972) – not 1962, 1974, 1982, 1986 or 1990; first comprehensive wildlife law.
[0:2] - **Protects wild animals, birds and plants** and manages their habitats – **not animals only**; it is the Act that **regulates National Parks and Wildlife Sanctuaries** (not EPA 1986, FCA 1980 or BD Act 2002).
[0:3] - **Poaching** = illegal hunting of wild animals – barred by the Act.
[0:4] - **66 sections, 7 chapters**; amended 1982, 1986, 1991, 1993, 2002, 2006, 2013, **2022**.
[0:5] # Key sections
[0:6] TABLE head: Section | Provision
  [0:6.0] 2(1) | "Animal" = amphibians, birds, mammals, reptiles, their young, eggs of birds and reptiles
  [0:6.1] 2(37) | "Wildlife"
  [0:6.2] 9 | Hunting of scheduled animals prohibited, subject to exceptions
  [0:6.3] 11 | Animal dangerous to human life (or crops), diseased or disabled beyond recovery may be captured/killed with the Chief Wildlife Warden's permission
  [0:6.4] 12 | Permits for education, research, scientific management, translocation
  [0:6.5] 38J | Teasing, molesting, injuring or feeding animals in a zoo prohibited
  [0:6.6] 39(1) | Wild animals = State Government property; in a Centre-declared sanctuary/NP = Central Government
  [0:6.7] 38V(3) | Tiger Conservation Plan for every TR (2006)
  [0:6.8] 62 | Centre may notify a non-Schedule I animal as vermin
[0:7] - **Gharial, Indian wild ass, wild buffalo** cannot be hunted by anyone except as the law permits (UPSC 2017: all three).
[0:8] - **UPSC 2022 (official key "1 and 2"):** wild animals are government property; a protected animal gets equal protection inside or outside PAs; mere *apprehension* of danger is not enough to kill it.
[0:9] - **Protected animal may be killed** when it endangers human life **and** when it is diseased – both true, but disease does not explain the danger clause.
[0:10] - **Mining:** barred inside NPs and sanctuaries; SC bars mining within **1 km** of every NP/sanctuary unless a larger ESZ is notified.
[0:11] ~~ Sources: Rocket Environment p.25; NextGen Environment APPSC 06 p.14; PMF Environment 06 p.71-76; app notes 5:90 sub 4
## SUB 1 | WPA Schedules and the 2022 Amendment
[1:0] TABLE head: Before 2022 (6 schedules) | After 2022 (4 schedules)
  [1:0.0] I and II: absolute protection (tiger, rhino) | I: highest protection
  [1:0.1] III and IV: lesser (game species) | II: lower protection
  [1:0.2] V: vermin (common crow, fruit bats, rats, mice) | III: protected plants
  [1:0.3] VI: specified plants – cultivation only with a licence | IV: CITES-listed specimens
[1:1] - **Number of schedules:** **6** in the 1972 Act (the usual key); **4** since the 2022 amendment (in force 1 April 2023).
[1:2] - **Schedule I effect:** a tortoise in Schedule I gets **the same protection as the tiger** (not "extinct in the wild" or "endemic").
[1:3] - **Schedule VI (old):** a **licence is required to cultivate** the plant; a specified plant cannot be picked, uprooted or traded without permission.
[1:4] - **Flying fox** = a **fruit bat** (Pteropus), not a kite, stork or vulture; it does **not** feed on blood. It was **vermin (Schedule V)**; since 2022 it is in **Schedule II**.
[1:5] - **Schedule I examples:** Asiatic lion, tiger, elephant, snow leopard, rhino, Gangetic dolphin, dugong, GIB, **blackbuck**; mugger (Kurnool, Sep 2026) and rock eagle-owl (Dharmavaram, Aug 2026) in news.
[1:6] # 2022 amendment
[1:7] - **Schedules 6 → 4**; vermin schedule deleted (Sec 62 power stays with the **Central Government**); **CITES** provisions (Management and Scientific Authorities); Centre regulates **invasive alien species** (African catfish, Lantana).
[1:8] - Voluntary **surrender of captive animals without compensation**; Chief Wildlife Warden in every State; Centre may notify conservation reserves.
[1:9] - Fines: specially protected animals – minimum **₹25,000** (from ₹10,000); general violations – maximum **₹1 lakh** (from ₹25,000).
[1:10] # Earlier amendments
[1:11] - **2002:** **NBWL** replaced the Indian Board for Wildlife; **Conservation Reserves** (government land, e.g. **Tsarap Chu**) and **Community Reserves** (private/community land).
[1:12] - **2006:** **NTCA and WCCB**.
[1:13] ~~ Sources: PMF Environment 06 p.76-78; Shankar Environment Part 14 p.12; app notes 5:90 sub 5
## SUB 2 | Protected Areas and Community Reserves under WPA
[2:0] - **Five kinds:** wildlife sanctuary, national park, conservation reserve, community reserve, tiger reserve.
[2:1] - **Community Reserve (Sec 36C):** private/community land, declared by the **State Government**; run by a **Community Reserve Management Committee (Sec 36D)** – not the Chief Wildlife Warden.
[2:2] - In a community reserve **hunting is not allowed** and people may **collect non-timber forest produce**, but **traditional agriculture (jhum) is not allowed** → UPSC 2023: "only two" statements correct.
[2:3] - **NP vs sanctuary:** NP – no grazing, no human activity except as the Chief Wildlife Warden permits; sanctuary – regulated grazing.
[2:4] - **India's PAs:** **1,75,169.42 sq km = 5.32%** of land (2026).
[2:5] - **Eco-sensitive zone:** the **National Chambal Sanctuary (MP)** was notified as an ESZ on **2 March 2020**.
[2:6] ~~ Sources: PMF Environment 06 p.71-72; Rocket Environment p.25-26; https://www.gktoday.in/national-chambal-sanctuary-declared-as-eco-sensitive-zone (web search); app notes 5:90 sub 5, 9
## SUB 3 | Forest (Conservation) Act 1980 / Van Adhiniyam
[3:0] - **Enacted 1980** (27 Dec 1980, in force from **25 Oct 1980**) – not 1976, 1978, 1981 or 1988.
[3:1] - **Sec 2:** **prior approval of the Central Government (MoEF)** to de-reserve a forest or use forest land for **non-forest purposes** – not the Collector, State or FSI. FCA = the Act that **regulates forest-land diversion**.
[3:2] - **2023 amendment:** renamed **Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam, 1980** (w.e.f. 29 Dec 2023); covers land notified as forest under IFA 1927 or any law, and land **recorded as forest on or after 25 Oct 1980**; **excludes land converted before 12 Dec 1996**.
[3:3] - **Exemptions:** strategic linear projects within **100 km** of IB/LoC/LAC; zoos, safaris, eco-tourism; no Gram Sabha consent required (a key criticism).
[3:4] - **Compensatory afforestation:** equal non-forest land, else **twice** the degraded forest land + **NPV**.
[3:5] - **T.N. Godavarman (1995 writ; 1996 order):** "forest" = every area recorded as forest **irrespective of ownership**; the 2023 amendment narrows this.
[3:6] - **AP diversions:** **3,474.57 ha** (1 Jan 2015-5 Feb 2019); **149.5687 ha** (1 Apr-31 Dec 2025).
[3:7] ~~ Sources: PMF Environment 06 p.80-83; Rocket Environment p.12, p.18; app notes 5:90 sub 6
## SUB 4 | Indian Forest Act 1927 and Colonial Forest Laws
[4:0] TABLE head: Year | Law | Point
  [4:0.0] 1865 | First Indian Forest Act | Drafted 1865
  [4:0.1] 1878 | Indian Forest Act | Created reserved, protected, village forests
  [4:0.2] 1927 | Indian Forest Act | Consolidating law, still in force
[4:1] - **Degree of protection:** **Reserved > Protected > Village** forest; a **village forest** is a **reserved forest** assigned to a village community.
[4:2] - **Reserved forest (Ch II):** everything banned unless permitted; **Sec 26** = acts prohibited in a reserved forest, incl. punishable **trespass**.
[4:3] - **Protected forest:** activities allowed unless prohibited – people could take **head-loads of wood**, collect produce for their own use and **graze cattle** (entry not barred).
[4:4] - **Unclassed forest:** outside the reserved and protected categories. **Sec 35:** State may regulate non-government forests.
[4:5] - **2017 amendment** (ordinance 2017, Act 2018): **bamboo grown outside forest areas** removed from the definition of "tree" (**Sec 2(7)**) – may be felled and moved without permit. Bamboo **inside** forests is not freed.
[4:6] - **Colonial plantations:** British policy encouraged **monocropping through plantations** (tea, coffee, teak), a cause of environmental degradation.
[4:7] ~~ Sources: PMF Environment 06 p.80, p.84; NextGen Environment APPSC 05 p.11; Rocket Environment p.18; app notes 5:90 sub 0, 2
## SUB 5 | National Forest Policy 1952 and 1988
[5:0] TABLE head: Feature | NFP 1952 | NFP 1988
  [5:0.0] National target | One-third | One-third (33%) of geographical area
  [5:0.1] Hills | 60% | Two-thirds
  [5:0.2] Classes / thrust | National, Protected, Village forests + Tree-lands (National Park not a class) | Ecological stability, people's participation
[5:1] - **NFP 1988 includes:** afforestation and wasteland development; reforestation in existing forests; **wood substitutes and alternative fuels** – NOT wide use of **insecticides and pesticides**. "Two-thirds of the country under forest" is wrong (hills only).
[5:2] - **Ecological balance:** Forest Policy, EPA 1986, Industrial and Educational Policy all relate to it (UPPSC: "all the above").
[5:3] - **Draft NFP 2018**; **NAPCC launched 2008** (not 2018).
[5:4] - **Order:** IFA 1927 → NFP 1952 → WPA 1972 → FCA 1980 → EPA 1986 → NFP 1988 → BD Act 2002 → FRA 2006.

## SUB 7 | Biological Diversity Act 2002 and Which Act Protects What
[7:0] TABLE head: Act | What it does
  [7:0.0] WPA 1972 | Wild animals, birds, plants and habitats
  [7:0.1] EPA 1986 | Umbrella law – protection and improvement of the environment
  [7:0.2] FCA 1980 | Forest-land diversion
  [7:0.3] PCA Act 1960 | Cruelty to animals; parent Act of AWBI
  [7:0.4] BD Act 2002 | CBD, access and benefit sharing
[7:1] - **"Nature (Protection) Act, 2000"**, "Forests Protection Act 1982", "EPA 1996" do **not exist**.
[7:2] - **NGT** cannot hear **WPA 1972** or FRA 2006 cases.
[7:3] - **BD Act 2002:** three tiers – **NBA (Chennai, 2003)**, State Biodiversity Boards, **Biodiversity Management Committees** (prepare **People's Biodiversity Registers**); **Biodiversity Heritage Sites** under **Sec 37** (State Government).
[7:4] - **2023 amendment (in force 1 April 2024):** offences **decriminalised** (civil penalties); exemption for AYUSH practitioners and **BMC-certified** cultivated medicinal plants; NBA approval **180 → 90 days**; foreign entities **still need** prior NBA approval.
[7:5] - **AP (May 2026):** more than **13,500 BMCs**, **12,000 PBRs**.
[7:6] - **Constitution:** 42nd Amendment (1976) moved forests (**Entry 17A**) and wild animals and birds (**Entry 17B**) to the Concurrent List; inserted **Art 48A** (DPSP) and **Art 51A(g)** (Fundamental Duty). A State Bill amending WPA (e.g. Maharashtra 2026, Sec 12; leopard **Schedule I → II**) needs the **President's assent** because it amends a **central law**.
[7:7] ~~ Sources: Rocket Environment p.12, p.40, p.66; NextGen Environment APPSC 06 p.14; app notes 5:90 sub 1, 7, 9
