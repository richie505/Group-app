=== New Current Affairs: 5–14 Nov 2026
# Literary Awards
- **[09–10 Nov 2026] Booker Prize 2026 – M. John Harrison** (British, aged **81**) for ***The End of Everything***, a satirical post-apocalyptic **science-fiction** novel (his **13th** novel); announced in **London**; **oldest winner ever**; the first winner from the **science-fiction** world; jury chair **Mary Beard**.
- **Booker Prize:** for the best sustained work of **fiction written in English**, published in the UK/Ireland; by the **Booker Prize Foundation**; **£50,000** (plus **£2,500** to each of the **six** shortlisted authors); **2025 winner: David Szalay – *Flesh***.
- **Do not confuse:** **International Booker 2026** = ***Taiwan Travelogue*** by **Yáng Shuāng-zǐ**, translated from **Mandarin** by **Lin King** (first Mandarin Chinese winner, 19 May 2026); **International Booker 2025** = ***Heart Lamp*** (**Banu Mushtaq**, Kannada; tr. **Deepa Bhasthi**); from **2027** it becomes the **Bukhman International Booker Prize** (£100,000).
- **[06 Nov 2026] Sharjah Award – Nirmala Lakshman:** her book ***The Tamils: A Portrait of a Community*** won the **Best International Non-Fiction Book** award at the **Sharjah International Book Fair Awards** (UAE); presented by **Sheikha Bodour bint Sultan Al Qasimi**, Chairperson, **Sharjah Book Authority**; Lakshman is an author, journalist and **Director of The Hindu Group**.
# Indians Honoured Abroad
- **[09 Nov 2026] Dr Devi Shetty** (founder-chairman, **Narayana Health**) – France's **Chevalier de l'Ordre national du Mérite** (Knight of the National Order of Merit), conferred by the **Consulate General of France in Bengaluru** for support to the French community during **Covid-19**.
- **Ordre national du Mérite:** founded **3 December 1963** by **Charles de Gaulle**; France's **second-highest** national order after the **Legion of Honour**; earlier Indian Chevaliers: **Sunil Kant Munjal (Nov 2025)**, **Dr Swati Piramal (2006)**.
- **[07 Nov 2026] Kochon Prize 2026 – REACH** (Resource group for Education and Advocacy for Community Health, India) – **first Indian civil-society non-profit** to win it; given by the **Kochon Foundation (South Korea)** with the **Stop TB Partnership** for the fight against **tuberculosis**; shared with the **Centre for Health Solutions (Kenya)** and the **Marshall Islands TB Control Program**.
- **[05 Nov 2026] StopBleed – top honour at Singapore's Slingshot 2026:** a **nano-biopolymer haemostatic powder** by **Sabir Hussain** (**Miracules Medsolutions**, West Bengal) that controls severe bleeding in about **60 seconds**; approved by **CDSCO** as a **Class C medical device**.
- **[11 Nov 2026] Ram Mohan Naidu** (Union **Civil Aviation** Minister, from AP) – among **Purdue University's "38 by 38" Achievers** (instituted **2024** by Purdue's College of Engineering, USA, for 38 alumni under **38**, inspired by alumnus **Neil Armstrong**, who walked on the Moon at 38); he did his **BSEE at Purdue (2008)**.
# Institutional and Heritage Honours
- **[09 Nov 2026] UNESCO Memory of the World – Asia-Pacific Regional Register:** manuscripts of the ***Mahabharata* (Vyasa)**, ***Rajatarangini* (Kalhana)** and ***Arthashastra* (Kautilya)** added; earlier Indian entries include ***Ramcharitmanas*** (Tulsidas), ***Panchatantra*** (Vishnu Sharma) and ***Sahrdayaloka-Locana*** (Anandavardhana, Abhinavagupta); MoW programme launched in **1992**.
- **[04 Nov 2026] Asia-Pacific Broadcasting Union (ABU) Prizes 2026 – Prasar Bharati:** **Akashvani Bengaluru's "Beyond the Call"** – **Audio Public Service Announcement Special Commendation**; **Nongmaithem Nandakumar Singh** – **ABU Broadcast Engineering Excellence Award 2026**; **ABU:** est. **1964**, HQ **Kuala Lumpur**.
- **[05 Nov 2026] ₹250 commemorative coin – Muthuswami Dikshitar (1775–1835), 250th birth anniversary**, released by **Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman**; born at **Thiruvarur (TN)**; mudra/signature **"Guruguha"**; a **veena** player; one of the **Carnatic Trinity** with **Tyagaraja** (Pancharatna Kritis) and **Syama Sastri** (mudra "Syama Krishna").
- **[10 Nov 2026] AP – Best Mechanical Engineering Student Award 2026:** **J.T. Amareswar** (V.R. Siddhartha Engineering College, Vijayawada), conferred by the **ISTE (Indian Society for Technical Education) AP Section** on **7 Nov 2026**.
