[0:1] - **Event:** a **rock-ice collapse** near the **Nepal-China border**; material plunged about **1 km**; first read as a **4.4-magnitude quake**, but **USGS** said the signal came from the collapse (energy ~**magnitude 5.2**).
[0:2] - **Type:** a **rock-ice collapse-triggered debris flood**, **not a conventional GLOF**; an **ice-rock avalanche** from the northern slopes of **Mt Langtang Lirung** choked the **Lhende River (Lhende Khola)**, which feeds the **Bhotekoshi**; flash floods on the **Bhotekoshi-Trishuli** rivers.
[0:3] - **Toll (NDRRMA, 8 Sep 2026):** **1,357 dead**, **5,326** unaccounted for (it rose from a few hundred in the first days); **93,000+** need assistance; Kailash Mansarovar pilgrims missing.

[0:5] - **India:** rivers **Gandak, Kosi, Ganga**; **Bihar and UP** on alert; **Valmikinagar Barrage** watched (not Farakka); Maharajganj, Kushinagar; India sent **47.5 tonnes** of relief.

## SUB 5 | Short-lived Climate Pollutants and the Ozone Treaties
[5:0] # SLCPs
[5:1] - SLCPs cause up to **45% of present warming**; IPCC: rapid cuts can avoid up to **0.6°C by 2050**; also called **super pollutants** / **Short-Lived Climate Forcers**.
[5:2] - Major warming SLCPs: **black carbon, methane, tropospheric ozone, HFCs**; **sulphate aerosols cool**.
