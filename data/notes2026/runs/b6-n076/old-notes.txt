## SUB 6 | Short-lived Climate Pollutants and the Ozone Treaties
[76:6:0] # SLCPs
[76:6:1] - SLCPs cause up to **45% of present warming**; IPCC: rapid cuts can avoid up to **0.6°C by 2050**; also called **super pollutants** / **Short-Lived Climate Forcers**.
[76:6:2] - Major warming SLCPs: **black carbon, methane, tropospheric ozone, HFCs**; **sulphate aerosols cool**.
[76:6:3] - **Black carbon is not a greenhouse gas** (a particle that absorbs radiation); **tropospheric ozone is not directly emitted** (formed from methane, NOx, VOCs in sunlight).
[76:6:4] - **Methane:** about **80 times** CO2's warming over **20 years**; second-largest contributor after CO2.
[76:6:5] - Stratospheric ozone **~10-50 km**; tropospheric ozone **surface to 10 km**.
