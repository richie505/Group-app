## SUB 3 | Law Commission of India
[3:0] # History and Nature
[3:1] - Colonial precursor: **Macaulay's** Law Commission produced the **IPC (1860)**.
[3:2] - First post-independence Law Commission: **1955**, chaired by **M.C. Setalvad**.
[3:3] - Nature: **neither constitutional nor statutory**; an **executive, advisory** body set up by a Government of India order for a **fixed 3-year term**.
[3:4] - Reports to the **Ministry of Law and Justice**, not the President.
[3:5] - **23rd Law Commission**: constituted **September 2024** for 3 years; Chairperson **Justice Dinesh Maheshwari** (former SC judge), took charge **15 April 2025**; term up to **31 August 2027**.
