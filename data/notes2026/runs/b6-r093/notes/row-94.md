# Ramon Magsaysay Award 2025 – Educate Girls

=== Anniversaries – Vithalbhai Patel and Batukeshwar Dutt
# Vithalbhai Patel – centenary as first Indian President of the Central Legislative Assembly
- Centenary marked by the **All India Speakers' Conference** (Delhi Legislative Assembly, **August 2025**); India Post commemorative stamp in 2025; Lok Sabha centenary souvenir 1973.
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 27 Sept 1873 | Born Nadiad, Gujarat – elder brother of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel |
| 1912 | Bombay Legislative Council |
| 1918 | Imperial Legislative Council |
| 1923 | Co-founded Swaraj Party with Motilal Nehru, C.R. Das (opposed suspension of NCM after Chauri Chaura, 1922) |
| 22 August 1925 | First Indian President of the Central Legislative Assembly (Speaker-equivalent) |
| 1928 | Motilal Nehru motion (backed by Lala Lajpat Rai) for a separate Assembly Department |
| 1929 | Independent Legislative Assembly Secretariat created |
| 22 Oct 1933 | Died in Geneva |
- **Refused to stand to receive the Viceroy** – guarding the dignity of the chair.
- After the Bhagat Singh–Dutt bomb (1929), kept "**ward and watch**" security under the President – continued till **2024** when **CISF** took over.
- Later allied with **Subhas Chandra Bose**; his will left his estate for Bose's work – the family won the court case.
# Batukeshwar Dutt – birth anniversary
- Born **18 November 1910, Burdwan, Bengal**; died **20 July 1965**.
- **Central Assembly Bombing, 8 April 1929**, with Bhagat Singh – slogans "**Inquilab Zindabad**", "Down with Imperialism"; bombs **harmless and symbolic**, not meant to kill.
- Convicted in the **Delhi Assembly Bomb Case – 12 June 1929**.
- Jails: Multan, Jhelum, Trichinopoly, Salem, Andamans – 9 years.
- Sequence: in **Salem Jail** at the hangings of Bhagat Singh, Rajguru, Sukhdev (**23 March 1931**) → **released 1938** → **re-arrested in Quit India (1942)** – 4 more years.
- Organisations: **HSRA**, Naujawan Bharat Sabha; met Bhagat Singh in **Kanpur**.
- Later life: settled in **Patna**; six-month nomination to the Bihar Legislative Council; bone cancer – AIIMS.
- Cremated at **Hussainiwala**, beside Bhagat Singh, Rajguru, Sukhdev.

=== Nobel Medicine and Physics 2025
# Physiology or Medicine – peripheral immune tolerance
| Laureate | Discovery |
|---|---|
| Shimon Sakaguchi (1995) | Regulatory T cells (T-regs) – CD4⁺CD25⁺ subset in mice |
| Brunkow & Ramsdell (2001) | Gene FOXP3 – mutations cause IPEX syndrome (humans) and scurfy (mice) |
- **Central tolerance** = deletion of self-reactive T-cells in the **thymus**; **peripheral tolerance** works **outside the thymus**.
- Applications: **autoimmune diseases** (type-1 diabetes, rheumatoid arthritis, lupus), **transplants**, **cancer immunotherapy**.
# Physics – macroscopic quantum tunnelling
- **John Clarke** – born in **Cambridge, UK**; professor at **University of California, Berkeley**. **Michel H. Devoret** – born **France**. **John M. Martinis** – born **US**.
- Applications: **superconducting qubits**, **circuit QED**, quantum amplifiers and sensors.
- **Nuclear fusion in the Sun** is only an **example of tunnelling in nature**, not an application of the prize work.

=== Nobel Chemistry 2025 – Metal-organic Frameworks
| Laureate | Contribution |
|---|---|
| Richard Robson (UK/Australia) | First MOF concept – copper ions with four-armed organic molecules |
| Susumu Kitagawa (Japan, Kyoto University) | Flexible / "soft" MOFs letting gases flow in and out |

- MOFs = **crystalline porous materials** of **metal ions + organic ligands**.

- Uses: **removing PFAS** from water, carbon capture, gas storage, drug delivery, catalysis.
