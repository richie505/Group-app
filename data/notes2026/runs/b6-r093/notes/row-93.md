# Padma Awards – rules and background
- Instituted **1954**; suspended **1978-79** and **1993-97**.
- **No cash prize**; yearly limit **120** awards (excludes **posthumous** and **foreign** awardees).
- 2025 Padma Vibhushan list included **Dr D. Nageshwar Reddy**.
- PYQ facts (2023): **8 Padma Shri** awards went to science and engineering; **S. R. Srinivasa Varadhan** got the **Padma Vibhushan in 2023**.

# Global Teacher Prize 2026 – Rouble (Rubal) Nagi
- Indian teacher **Rouble Nagi** won the **Global Teacher Prize 2026** (10th edition), presented on **5 February 2026** at the **World Governments Summit, Dubai**, by Dubai Crown Prince **Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed**.
- Prize: **$1 million** (~₹9 crore) by the **Varkey Foundation** (founder **Sunny Varkey**, London); prize est. **2014**.
- Founder of **Rouble Nagi Art Foundation** – ~**800 education centres**; murals on slum walls used as classrooms; project **"Living Walls of Learning"**.
