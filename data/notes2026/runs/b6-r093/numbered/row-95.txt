## SUB 2 | Padma Awards – Rules, Order and Past Lists
[2:0] - Instituted **1954**; announced on **Republic Day** (eve, 25 January) through a **Ministry of Home Affairs notification**.
[2:1] - Conferred by the **President** at **Rashtrapati Bhavan** (around March–April).
[2:2] - Annual limit **120** (excluding **posthumous** and **foreigners**).
[2:3] - **Posthumous** award normally not given – allowed only if **death within one year**.
[2:4] - **No cash prize**.
[2:5] TABLE head: Award | Service recognised | Rank among civilian awards
  [2:5.0] Padma Vibhushan | Exceptional and distinguished service | 2nd (after Bharat Ratna)
  [2:5.1] Padma Bhushan | Distinguished service of high order | 3rd
  [2:5.2] Padma Shri | Distinguished service in any field | 4th

[5:3] # Netaji – background
[5:4] - Born **23 January 1897**; died **18 August 1945**, air crash near **Taihoku (Taiwan)**.
[5:5] - **125th birth anniversary** (23 January 2022) – statue near **India Gate** (sculptor **Arun Yogiraj**).
[5:6] - **Forward Bloc** founded **3 May 1939**; **Indian National Army** (1943–45).
[5:7] - **Azad Hind Government:** **21 October 1943, Singapore** – recognised by 9 countries; Bose was PM, Foreign and Defence Minister.
[5:8] - **30 December 1943:** Tricolour hoisted at **Port Blair**.
[5:9] - **6 July 1944:** first to call Gandhi "**Father of the Nation**" (radio, Singapore).
[5:10] - **2018:** Ross Island renamed **Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose Island**.
[5:11] - Azad Hind Fauj originally formed **29 October 1915** by Raja Mahendra Pratap Singh, Rash Behari Bose, Niranjan Singh Gill; INA handed to Bose by **Rash Behari Bose** on **4 July 1943**.
[5:12] - **Bharat Ratna to Netaji:** announced posthumously in **1992**, but withdrawn in **1997** after a Supreme Court case – the only time a Bharat Ratna announcement was cancelled.
