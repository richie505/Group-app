## SUB 5 | Nobel Medicine and Physics 2025
[5:0] # Physiology or Medicine – peripheral immune tolerance
[5:1] TABLE head: Laureate | Discovery
  [5:1.0] Shimon Sakaguchi (1995) | Regulatory T cells (T-regs) – CD4⁺CD25⁺ subset in mice
  [5:1.1] Brunkow & Ramsdell (2001) | Gene FOXP3 – mutations cause IPEX syndrome (humans) and scurfy (mice)
[5:2] - **Central tolerance** = deletion of self-reactive T-cells in the **thymus**; **peripheral tolerance** works **outside the thymus**.
[5:3] - Applications: **autoimmune diseases** (type-1 diabetes, rheumatoid arthritis, lupus), **transplants**, **cancer immunotherapy**.
[5:4] # Physics – macroscopic quantum tunnelling
[5:5] - **John Clarke** – born in **Cambridge, UK**; professor at **University of California, Berkeley**. **Michel H. Devoret** – born **France**. **John M. Martinis** – born **US**.
[5:6] - Applications: **superconducting qubits**, **circuit QED**, quantum amplifiers and sensors.
[5:7] - **Nuclear fusion in the Sun** is only an **example of tunnelling in nature**, not an application of the prize work.
