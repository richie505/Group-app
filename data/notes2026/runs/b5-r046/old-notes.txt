=== NOTES 5:46 | Space Debris, Space Situational Awareness and International Space Law
## SUB 0 | Space Debris
[46:0:0] - **Space debris:** all **non-functional man-made objects**, incl. fragments, in orbit or re-entering.
[46:0:1] - Sources: defunct satellites, spent stages, **ASAT tests**; **main source = fragmentation** from collisions and explosions.
[46:0:2] - Over **1.2 million objects > 1 cm** (ESA 2025).
[46:0:3] - **Kessler Syndrome** (Donald Kessler, NASA, 1978): debris density → collisions → more fragments → orbits unusable.
[46:0:4] - **MMOD** (micrometeoroids and orbital debris) = top risk in **LEO**, not GEO.
[46:0:5] - **Mission Shakti (27 Mar 2019):** **DRDO** kinetic-kill ASAT test; destroyed **Microsat-R** at ~**300 km**.
## SUB 1 | Space Situational Awareness

  [46:1:0.0] Project NETRA (Network for Space Object Tracking and Analysis) | ISRO; indigenous SSA to safeguard Indian space assets; 24–72 hours' warning

  [46:1:0.2] Digantara (Bengaluru) | Private SSA start-up; space-surveillance satellites, debris tracking
[46:1:1] - **ISSAR 2025 (Apr 2026):** **18 collision avoidance manoeuvres** (Cartosat, Resourcesat, GSAT); RISAT-2B avoided a defunct US satellite (2019).
[46:1:2] IS4OM compiles ISSAR; NETRA = tracking network.
[46:1:3] - **IS4OM:** **ISRO System for Safe and Sustainable Space Operations Management**; dedicated to the nation in **2022** by Dr Jitendra Singh. ISSAR 2025 was released by ISRO Chairman **Dr V. Narayanan** on **8 Apr 2026** at SMOPS-2026, Bengaluru.
## SUB 2 | Mitigation
[46:2:0] - **Debris Free Space Missions (DFSM) by 2030** – pledged at the **2024 IADC** meeting.
[46:2:1] - **POEM-4** (SpaDeX/**PSLV-C60**): controlled re-entry into the **Indian Ocean, 4 Apr 2025**; inflatable sails → re-entry in months, not decades.
[46:2:2] - **PSLV-C62** (Jan 2026) planned a PS4 de-boost.
[46:2:3] - **Active Debris Removal:** servicer with **robotic arm or net** for a defunct Indian rocket body; tested at **URSC**.
[46:2:4] - **2026 re-entry rules:** warn aircraft and ships **at least 45 days** before re-entry.
[46:2:5] - Gaps: no international duty-of-care standard; differing licence rules push operators to lax regimes.

[48:0:0] - Launched **1983** under **Dr APJ Abdul Kalam**; nodal lab **DRDL, Hyderabad**, with **RCI and ASL** (Missile Complex).
[48:0:1] TABLE head: Phase | Missiles

[48:0:3] - Spin-offs: long-range tracking, composites, **ring laser gyros**, radomes (not a satellite navigation constellation).
[48:0:4] - Significance: India from missile importer to **missile exporter**; base for Agni-V MIRV, BMD.
