## SUB 0 | Space Situational Awareness – NETRA, IS4OM and Digantara
[0:0] # Space Situational Awareness (SSA)
[0:1] - **SSA:** the capability to **detect, track, monitor and predict** the movement of objects in space (satellites, debris); used for collision avoidance and space security.
[0:2] TABLE head: System | Fact
  [0:2.0] Project NETRA (Network for Space Object Tracking and Analysis) | ISRO's indigenous SSA system; aim = safeguard Indian space assets from debris and collisions; 24–72 hours' warning
  [0:2.1] SSA Control Centre | foundation stone laid by ISRO in August 2019 at Bengaluru (Peenya, ISTRAC campus); the NETRA control centre
  [0:2.2] IS4OM | ISRO System for Safe and Sustainable Space Operations Management; dedicated to the nation in 2022; compiles the annual ISSAR
  [0:2.3] Digantara Research and Technologies | private SSA start-up based in Bengaluru; authorised by IN-SPACe on 24 June 2022 to launch its payloads; space-surveillance satellites, debris tracking
[0:3] - **ISSAR 2025 (8 Apr 2026):** **18 collision avoidance manoeuvres** (CAMs) by Indian satellites in 2025; **RISAT-2B** avoided a defunct US satellite in 2019.
[0:4] > NETRA is asked twice (APPSC JL 2020, DAO 2020): agency **ISRO** (not RBI, ICSSR, ICHR) and aim **safeguard Indian space assets** (not a space station, Gaganyaan or the Vikram lander search).

## SUB 1 | Space Debris and Re-entry
[1:0] # Space debris
[1:1] - **Space debris:** all **non-functional man-made objects**, including fragments, in orbit or re-entering (defunct satellites, spent rocket stages, collision and explosion fragments, paint flakes).
[1:2] - **Main source = fragmentation** from collisions and in-orbit explosions; **ASAT tests** add debris (China 2007; **Mission Shakti**, DRDO, 27 Mar 2019, Microsat-R at ~300 km).
[1:3] - Over **1.2 million objects > 1 cm** (ESA 2025); objects move at ~7–8 km/s in LEO.
[1:4] - **Kessler Syndrome** (Donald Kessler, NASA, **1978**): debris density → collisions → more fragments → a self-sustaining cascade that can make orbits unusable.
[1:5] - **MMOD** (micrometeoroids and orbital debris) is the top risk in **LEO**, not GEO.
[1:6] # Controlled re-entry
[1:7] - **POEM-4** (PSLV Orbital Experimental Module of **PSLV-C60 / SpaDeX**): re-entered and impacted the **Indian Ocean on 4 April 2025** (ISRO: controlled re-entry).
[1:8] - **Mir** (Russian station, ~140 t): de-orbited in a controlled re-entry over the Pacific on **23 March 2001** after ~15 years, having made **86,331 orbits**.

## SUB 2 | Mitigation and Debris-free Missions
[2:0] - **Debris Free Space Missions (DFSM) by 2030** – India's pledge at the **2024 IADC** meeting.
[2:1] - **25-year rule:** LEO satellites should re-enter within 25 years of mission end (NASA, then IADC and UN guidelines); GEO satellites go to a **graveyard orbit**.
[2:2] - **Active Debris Removal:** a servicer with a **robotic arm or net** to capture a defunct Indian rocket body (tested at URSC).
[2:3] - **PSLV-C62** (Jan 2026) planned a PS4 de-boost; **2026 re-entry rules:** warn aircraft and ships **at least 45 days** before re-entry.
[2:4] - **Gaps:** IADC/UN guidelines are non-binding; no international **duty-of-care** standard; lax licensing regimes attract operators.

## SUB 3 | Space Law – Treaties and the Space Activities Bill
[3:0] TABLE head: Instrument | Core rule | India
  [3:0.0] Outer Space Treaty 1967 | No national sovereignty; Art. VI national responsibility incl. private actors; Art. VII liability; Art. IX due regard | Party (ratified 1982)
  [3:0.1] Rescue Agreement 1968 | Return of astronauts | Party
  [3:0.2] Liability Convention 1972 | Absolute liability on Earth; fault liability in space | Party
  [3:0.3] Registration Convention 1975 | Register space objects with the UN | Party
  [3:0.4] Moon Agreement 1979 | Common heritage | Signed, not ratified
[3:1] - The 1960s–70s treaties do not cover cumulative debris or mega-constellations.
[3:2] # Space Activities Bill (draft, 2017)
[3:3] - Aims to **facilitate the overall growth of space activities** in India; a **regulatory body to license** space activities (non-governmental too); protection of **IPR** created through space activity; liability and penalties.
[3:4] - It does **not** create a **military wing of the armed forces in outer space** (the incorrect statement, Sachivalayam PYQ).
[3:5] - India still has **no Space Activities Act** on liability.
