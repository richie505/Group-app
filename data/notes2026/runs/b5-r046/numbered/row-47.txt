## SUB 1 | Formation, Motto, Vision and Mission
[1:0] - **Formed 1958** by amalgamating the **Technical Development Establishments (TDEs)** of the Indian Army, the **Directorate of Technical Development & Production (DTDP)** and the **Defence Science Organisation (DSO)** (not AEET Trombay).
[1:1] - **DRDO = Defence Research and Development Organisation**; R&D wing of the **Ministry of Defence**; HQ **DRDO Bhawan, New Delhi**.
[1:2] - **Motto: "Balasya Mulam Vigyanam"** – the source of strength is science (**not** Tejasvinavadhitamastu, Jagratam Aharnisham or Vayam Rakshamah).
[1:3] - **Growth:** started with **10 establishments**; now about **41 laboratories and 5 DRDO Young Scientist Laboratories** (DRDO website; many books still say **about 52** labs, the pre-merger figure).
[1:4] - **Vision:** empower the nation with state-of-the-art indigenous **defence and security** technologies (older wording: make India prosperous through a world-class S&T base and give the Services a decisive edge with internationally competitive systems).
[1:5] - **Mission (three points):** (1) design, develop and lead to production **state-of-the-art sensors, weapon systems, platforms** and allied equipment; (2) **technological solutions** to optimise **combat effectiveness** and the **well-being of troops**; (3) **infrastructure, quality manpower and a strong indigenous technology base**. "Promote international collaboration" is **not** in the mission.
[1:6] - **Achievements:** Agni and Prithvi missiles, LCA **Tejas**, **Pinaka** MBRL, **Akash** air defence, radars and EW systems.

## SUB 4 | Academic Centres and Young Scientist Labs
[4:0] - **JCBCAT** (Jagadish Chandra Bose Centre for Advanced Technology) – **Jadavpur University, Kolkata**, set up with **DRDO** (not ISRO, NASA, BARC or BEL).

[4:3] - **Agnishodh** – the **Indian Army's** research cell at **IIT Madras**.
[4:4] - **College of Military Engineering (CME)** – **Dapodi, Pune**, an Army institution affiliated to **JNU** (not DRDO).
