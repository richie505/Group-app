## SUB 3 | UNESCO – Sarnath and India's Tentative List
[3:0] # Sarnath – India's 45th World Heritage Site
[3:1] - **Ancient Buddhist Site of Sarnath** inscribed at the **48th World Heritage Committee session, Busan (South Korea), July 2026**.
[3:2] - **India's 45th** WHS; **UP's 4th** (others: **Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Fatehpur Sikri**).
[3:3] - Components: **Chaukhandi Stupa** + archaeological remains; criteria **(iii) and (vi)**.
[3:4] - **Buddha's first sermon** (**Dharmachakra**); ancient name **Rishipattan**.
[3:5] - On the Tentative List since **3 July 1998** (**28 years**).
[3:6] # Tentative List – Additions in September 2026
[3:7] - **4 September 2026** – four sites added, total **73**: **Rangpur–Sivasagar Historic Ensemble** (Assam – Ahom: **Rang Ghar, Talatal Ghar**, historic large water tanks), **Colonial Penal Settlements of the Andaman Islands** (incl. **Cellular Jail**), **Shekhawati Painted Haveli Towns** (Rajasthan – merchants' havelis with murals) and a **Nicobarese** heritage site.
[3:8] - Five more added – total now **78**:

  [3:9.0] Bagh Cave Paintings | Madhya Pradesh | 9 Buddhist rock-cut caves, 4th–6th c. CE; Buddhist murals; Cave 4 "Rang Mahal"; near Baghni River
  [3:9.1] Udaigiri (Udayagiri) Caves | Madhya Pradesh | Gupta patronage, 4th–5th c.; 20 caves; Varaha (Cave 5), Anantasayana Vishnu (Cave 13)
  [3:9.2] Group of Temples of Chambal Valley | Morena, MP | Bateshwar, Padavali, Kakarmath; 8th–11th c.; Nagara style; Gurjara-Pratihara and Kachchhapaghata

[3:13] # Background and Process
[3:14] - **World Heritage Convention 1972**; **196 States Parties** (2024); **India ratified in 1977** (not 1972).
[3:15] - **10 criteria**: (i)–(vi) cultural, (vii)–(x) natural, merged in **2004**.
[3:16] - **ICOMOS** evaluates cultural, **IUCN** natural sites; **21-member** World Heritage Committee.
[3:17] - A site must first be on the **Tentative List** before nomination; tentative listing **does not** give WHS status.
[3:18] - In India, **ASI and INCCU** propose Tentative List sites.
[3:19] - Danger-list examples: **Bamiyan Valley**, **Zabid (Yemen)**, **Bam (Iran)**.
