## SUB 3 | Unesco – Sarnath and India's Tentative List
[104:3:0] # Sarnath – India's 45th World Heritage Site

[104:3:2] - **India's 45th** WHS; **UP's 4th** (others: **Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Fatehpur Sikri**).
[104:3:3] - Components: **Chaukhandi Stupa** + archaeological remains; criteria **(iii) and (vi)**.
[104:3:4] - **Buddha's first sermon** (**Dharmachakra**); ancient name **Rishipattan**.
[104:3:5] - On the Tentative List since **3 July 1998** (**28 years**).

[104:3:13] # Background and Process
[104:3:14] - **World Heritage Convention 1972**; **196 States Parties** (2024); **India ratified in 1977** (not 1972).
[104:3:15] - **10 criteria**: (i)–(vi) cultural, (vii)–(x) natural, merged in **2004**.
[104:3:16] - **ICOMOS** evaluates cultural, **IUCN** natural sites; **21-member** World Heritage Committee.
[104:3:17] - A site must first be on the **Tentative List** before nomination; tentative listing **does not** give WHS status.
[104:3:18] - In India, **ASI and INCCU** propose Tentative List sites.
[104:3:19] - Danger-list examples: **Bamiyan Valley**, **Zabid (Yemen)**, **Bam (Iran)**.
