## 3. Angles
- **Date of a day / week** and **event it commemorates** (Gandhi at AIR Delhi 1947; Montreal Protocol 1987 for Ozone Day; FAO 1945 for Food Day): the dominant PYQ angle.
- **Theme of a given year**: themes change yearly, so each row's tables carry the year's theme; the PYQs ask older years too (2022, 2025).
- **Who inaugurated / where** for conclaves and summits.
- App MCQs add chronology (arrange days in calendar order), NOT-correct statements, assertion-reason (Forest Martyrs Day), founding years (UNGA resolution, first observed) and heritage (Sarnath 45th WHS).
