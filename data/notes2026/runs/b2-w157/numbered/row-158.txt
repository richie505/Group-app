## SUB 0 | WTO Basics, Structure and Ministerial Conferences
[0:0] # Basics
[0:1] - Formed **1 January 1995** under the **Marrakesh Agreement**, after the **Uruguay Round (8th GATT round, 1986-94)**; **successor of GATT (1947)** – GATT survives as the WTO agreement on goods.
[0:2] - Deals with the **global rules of trade between nations**; helps producers, exporters and importers conduct business; frames rules on goods, **services (GATS)** and **intellectual property (TRIPS)** – it does not "distance itself" from IPR (CDS 2014).
[0:3] - HQ **Geneva** (not Montreal, Seattle, The Hague or Vienna); **one-country-one-vote**, consensus – gives developing countries bargaining room.
[0:4] - Members **166** (March 2026); latest **Comoros** and **Timor-Leste** (2024).
[0:5] - **Principles**: MFN and national treatment; **GATT Article XXIV** lets FTAs be MFN exceptions if they cover "substantially all trade"; Special and Differential Treatment (SDT).
[0:6] # Directors-General
[0:7] - **Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala** (Nigeria) – first woman and first African DG (March 2021); second term **1 Sept 2025 – 31 Aug 2029**.
[0:8] - **Pascal Lamy** (**France**, former EU Trade Commissioner) – DG **2005-2013**; then Roberto Azevêdo (2013-20). Arthur **Dunkel** was GATT DG (1980-93).

## SUB 1 | Agreements – TRIPS, TRIMS, GATS, SPS and Others
[1:0] TABLE head: Agreement | Subject
  [1:0.0] GATT | Trade in goods
  [1:0.1] GATS | Trade in services
  [1:0.2] TRIPS | Intellectual property – minimum standards for all IPR (not tariffs, trade facilitation or property taxation)
  [1:0.3] TRIMS | Trade-distorting investment measures (local content)
  [1:0.4] AoA | Market access, domestic support, export subsidies
  [1:0.5] SPS | Food safety, animal and plant health (science-based)
  [1:0.6] TBT | Technical regulations, standards, conformity assessment
  [1:0.7] Anti-dumping | Duties on goods sold below normal value
  [1:0.8] SCM | Subsidies: prohibited, actionable, non-actionable
[1:1] - Phytosanitary rules, technical standards = **non-tariff barriers**. Only new agreements in 30 years: **Trade Facilitation** and **Fisheries Subsidies**. India's **DGTR**: anti-dumping, countervailing, safeguard inquiries.
