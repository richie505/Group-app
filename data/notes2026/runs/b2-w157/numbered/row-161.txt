## SUB 0 | NPT, CTBT and the Nuclear-weapon States
[0:0] # NPT
[0:1] - **Signed 1968**, **in force 1970**, **extended indefinitely in 1995**; Review Conferences every **5 years**.
[0:2] - Recognises **five Nuclear Weapon States** – those that **tested before 1 January 1967**: **USA, Russia, UK, France, China** (= UNSC P5).
[0:3] - Of China, France, India, Israel, Pakistan: **only China and France** are NPT-recognised NWS (UPSC 2015, asked twice).
[0:4] - **North Korea left the NPT in 2003**; it claimed its **first hydrogen-bomb test on 6 January 2016**.
[0:5] - India rejects the NPT as discriminatory – **"nuclear apartheid"**; wants **time-bound, universal, non-discriminatory disarmament** (Rajiv Gandhi Action Plan, 1988).
[0:6] # CTBT
[0:7] - Adopted **1996**; bans **all nuclear explosions**; **not in force** – needs ratification by all **44 Annex-2 States** (signing alone is not enough).
[0:8] - India has **not signed** (no time-bound disarmament commitment; subcritical tests still allowed).
[0:9] - **Iraq signed on 19 August 2008** and ratified on 26 September 2013 – so "Iraq and India have not signed" was already false in 2009.
[0:10] # India's nuclear doctrine
[0:11] - **No First Use**, **credible minimum deterrence**, massive retaliation; Pokhran-II **1998**.
[0:12] > Exam angle: NPT NWS = tested before 1 Jan 1967 (China, France among the options); CTBT entry into force = 44 Annex-2 ratifications.

## SUB 1 | NSG and the Export-control Regimes
[1:0] TABLE head: Regime | Year | Members | Controls | India
  [1:0.0] NSG ("London Club") | 1975 | 48 | Nuclear materials and technology | Not a member – only the 2008 India-specific waiver
  [1:0.1] MTCR | 1987 | 35 | Missiles and drones – 500 kg over 300 km | Member, June 2016
  [1:0.2] Wassenaar Arrangement | 1996, Vienna | 42 | Conventional arms and dual-use goods | 42nd member, December 2017
  [1:0.3] Australia Group | 1985 | – | Chemical and biological weapon precursors | 43rd member, 19 January 2018
  [1:0.4] Hague Code of Conduct | 2002 | – | Transparency on ballistic-missile proliferation | Joined
[1:1] - **NSG:** formed in reply to India's **1974 Pokhran-I** test; **voluntary, consensus-based**, no permanent secretariat; India's entry is blocked by the **consensus rule**.
[1:2] - **Membership gives access to the latest nuclear technologies**; it does **not** make a country an NPT member (UPSC 2018: only statement I correct).
[1:3] - The 2008 waiver (India-US Civil Nuclear Deal) allows civil nuclear trade – import of uranium and reactors – without joining the NPT.
[1:4] - **China and Pakistan** are outside both **Wassenaar and MTCR**; **North Korea** is outside MTCR.
[1:5] - **North Korea** admitted exporting missiles to **Pakistan and Iran** and asked the US for **compensation** to stop their further spread.
[1:6] - India = "responsible nuclear state": entry into **3 of the 4** regimes (MTCR, Wassenaar, Australia Group – not NSG).
[1:7] - **NSG** in a general GS paper usually = **National Security Guard** (MHA counter-terror force, 1984, "Black Cats"); in non-proliferation = Nuclear Suppliers Group.
[1:8] > Exam angle: wrong-pair questions swap years/controls (NSG 1975 nuclear; MTCR 1987 missiles); India is in MTCR, Wassenaar, AG but not NSG.

## SUB 2 | Arms-control Treaties, JCPOA and ICAN
[2:0] TABLE head: Treaty | Parties | Status
  [2:0.0] JCPOA (2015) | Iran with P5+1 (US, UK, France, Russia, China, Germany) and EU | US left 2018; Iran dropped all limits 5 January 2020
  [2:0.1] New START (signed April 2010, Prague) | USA and Russia (not China) | Bilateral strategic nuclear arms reduction; 1,550 deployed warheads; Russia suspended 2023; expired February 2026
  [2:0.2] INF Treaty (1987) | USA and USSR | US left 2019
  [2:0.3] CCW (1980) | UN convention on inhumane conventional weapons | India party to all five protocols
[2:1] - **START** = **Strategic Arms Reduction Treaty**; New START was the last US-Russia nuclear arms-control pact; **US + Russia** hold about **90%** of world arsenals.
[2:2] - **Iran:** US and Israel struck its nuclear sites **June 2025**; Iran's Parliament weighed **NPT exit** (Mar 2026).
[2:3] - **Nobel Peace Prize 2017: ICAN** (International Campaign to Abolish Nuclear Weapons) – for its work behind the **TPNW**.
[2:4] # TPNW, FMCT and nuclear-weapon-free zones
[2:5] - **TPNW:** first binding ban on nuclear weapons; **in force 2021**; **India did not sign** – says it adds nothing to customary international law.
[2:6] - **FMCT:** **India supports** negotiation at the **Conference on Disarmament** ("India does not support FMCT" is false).

[2:8] > Exam angle: New START = USA-Russia bilateral (2010); JCPOA = 2015, Iran's full withdrawal 5 Jan 2020; ICAN = Nobel 2017.

## SUB 3 | FATF
[3:0] - Set up **1989 by the G7**; secretariat at **Paris (OECD)**; standards against **money laundering and terror financing**.
[3:1] - **Grey list** = jurisdictions under **increased monitoring**; **black list** = **high-risk jurisdictions subject to a call for action** – Iran, North Korea, **Myanmar** (unchanged at the Oct 2026 plenary).
[3:2] - **Pakistan:** **grey list** from **June 2018** for deficiencies in **terror financing and money laundering**; kept there (June 2020 too) for failing its **27-point action plan**; removed **October 2022**.
[3:3] - Oct 2026: grey list fell from 22 to **18** – DRC, Monaco, Bulgaria, Côte d'Ivoire removed.
[3:4] - **India:** member since **2010**; mutual evaluation adopted **June 2024** → **"regular follow-up"**; also in the **APG** (Asia/Pacific Group on Money Laundering).
[3:5] > Exam angle: Pakistan = grey (not black) list, 27-point plan, terror financing + money laundering.
