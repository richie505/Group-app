## SUB 0 | Bretton Woods Conference and the IMF
[0:0] # Bretton Woods (1944)
[0:1] - **United Nations Monetary and Financial Conference** = **Bretton Woods Conference**, **July 1944**, Mount Washington Hotel, **Bretton Woods, New Hampshire, USA** – **44 allied nations**, 730 delegates; plans of **Harry Dexter White** (US, adopted) and **John Maynard Keynes** (UK).
[0:2] - Created the "Bretton Woods twins": **IMF + IBRD (World Bank)**, both HQ **Washington, D.C.**; the planned **ITO** was never born → **GATT (1947)** filled the gap until the **WTO (1995)**.
[0:3] - Fixed exchange rates (dollar pegged to gold); **Smithsonian Agreement (1971)** widened the band to **2.25%**, lasted about **14 months**.
[0:4] TABLE head: Event | Date
  [0:4.0] IMF and IBRD founded (conceived) | July 1944
  [0:4.1] IMF Articles in force – formal existence (29 members) | 27 December 1945
  [0:4.2] IMF began operations | 1 March 1947
  [0:4.3] First IMF borrower | France, 8 May 1947
  [0:4.4] SDR created ("paper gold") | 1967-69 (first allocation 1970)
[0:5] - **India** = founding member of both the IMF and the IBRD.
[0:6] # IMF – functions and lending
[0:7] - **IMF** = **International Monetary Fund** – monetary stability; **short-term balance-of-payments (BoP)** support; technical assistance; **not** project/development finance (that is the World Bank's job).
[0:8] - Lends **only to member countries** (UPSC 2011, MPPSC 2017) – not to "any country", not only to developed ones, not to central banks as such.
[0:9] - Facilities: **Stand-By Arrangement**, **Extended Fund Facility**, **Rapid Financing Instrument (RFI)** and **Rapid Credit Facility (RCF)** (urgent BoP needs; RCF concessional for low-income members), PRGT. **Political-risk guarantees** = MIGA, not IMF.
[0:10] - **1991 BoP crisis**: India borrowed from the **IMF** (SDR 3.56 billion) – with the World Bank about **$7 bn** in all → New Economic Policy.
[0:11] # Quotas, votes, SDR
[0:12] - **Members: 191** – Liechtenstein the 191st (21 Oct 2024); **189** in January 2020 (Nauru 190th, 2021).
[0:13] - **Quotas** (in SDRs) decide financial contribution, **borrowing access** and **voting power**; do **not** fix exchange rates. Formula: GDP **50%**, openness **30%**, variability **15%**, reserves **5%**.
[0:14] - Major decisions need **85%** of votes → the **US** (~16.5%) alone holds a **veto**. **India 8th** by quota (~2.75%).
[0:15] - **SDR** basket of **five** currencies since **1 October 2016**: US dollar, euro, **Chinese renminbi (yuan)** (added 2016), yen, pound.
[0:16] - **IMFC** advises the Board of Governors; **Article IV consultations** = regular review of each member's economy.
[0:17] > Trap: 27 Dec 1945 = formal existence; 1 March 1947 = operations; 1944 = founding conference. HQ = Washington, not New York.

## SUB 1 | World Bank Group
[1:0] - **IBRD** = the original "**World Bank**" (1944; began 1946); HQ **Washington, D.C.** (not New York, Manila, Geneva, Vienna or Brussels); gives **medium- and long-term** loans; **India a founding member**; first loan to India 1949 (Railways).
[1:1] TABLE head: Institution | Year | Function
  [1:1.0] IBRD | 1944 | Loans to middle-income governments for infrastructure, reconstruction and development
  [1:1.1] IFC | 1956 | Private-sector investment; conceived "Doing Business"
  [1:1.2] IDA | 1960 | "Soft window": interest-free credits, concessional (soft) loans and grants to the poorest; an affiliate of the World Bank, administered by the IBRD (same staff)
  [1:1.3] ICSID | 1966 (not 1976) | Settles investment disputes; India not a member
  [1:1.4] MIGA | 1988 | Political-risk insurance and guarantees (India joined 1994)
[1:2] - IBRD, IDA and IFC are UN specialised agencies; ICSID and MIGA are not.
[1:3] - **Match (UPSC 1999)**: WTO – multilateral trade negotiations; **IDA – soft loans**; **IMF – short-term BoP loans**; **IBRD – lending for reconstruction and development**.
[1:4] - **Aid India Consortium** (World Bank-chaired donor group, 1958) → renamed **India Development Forum**.
[1:5] - World Bank funds: **BioCarbon Fund Initiative for Sustainable Forest Landscapes (ISFL)** – managed by the **World Bank**; **Bangladesh Road Safety Project** – **$358 million** (June 2023; Gazipur-Elenga N4, Natore-Nawabganj N6 highways).

## SUB 4 | NDB, ADB and AIIB
[4:0] # New Development Bank (BRICS)
[4:1] - Idea: **4th BRICS Summit, New Delhi, 2012**. Agreement: **6th Summit, Fortaleza, Brazil, 15 July 2014** – the **Fortaleza Declaration** (BRICS, not ASEAN/OECD/WTO); operations 2015.
[4:2] - HQ **Shanghai, China**; regional offices Johannesburg, São Paulo, **GIFT City** (India), Moscow. First President **K.V. Kamath**.
[4:3] - Founders hold **equal votes; no veto**; open to all UN members. New members: Bangladesh, UAE (2021), **Egypt** (2023), Algeria (2025).
[4:4] - **Russia** = 20% shareholder; NDB froze Russia-linked work, March 2022. 16th BRICS Summit: **Kazan**, Oct 2024; **New Delhi Declaration** (18th, 2026): Global North (~15% of people) holds ~**60%** of IMF/WB votes.
[4:5] # Asian Development Bank
[4:6] - Est. **1966** at **UN-ESCAP's** initiative (not UNCTAD); HQ **Manila, Philippines**; **India a founding member** (31 founders); **69 members**; **Türkiye** newest (2025).
[4:7] - Largest shareholders **Japan and USA** (~15.6% each); **India 4th** (~6.3%); President traditionally **Japanese**.
[4:8] - Launched the **Nature Solutions Finance Hub for Asia and the Pacific** (2023).
[4:9] - India is a member of **ADB** and the **Colombo Plan**; **not** of **APEC** or the **OECD** (UPSC 2008).
[4:10] # AIIB
[4:11] - China-proposed; HQ **Beijing**; Articles signed **2015**; operations **January 2016**.
[4:12] - **Founding members** include **India, China and the UK**; the **USA** (and Japan) stayed out.
[4:13] - **111** approved members; **China largest** (~26.5% votes), **India second** (~7.6%).
[4:14] > Trap: Manila = ADB (Japan-led); Beijing = AIIB (China-led); Shanghai = NDB (BRICS, equal votes).
