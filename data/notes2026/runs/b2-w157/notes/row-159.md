=== European Union and Council of Europe
# Evolution
| Year | Step |
|---|---|
| 1951 | **Treaty of Paris** – European Coal and Steel Community (6 states) |
| 1957 | **Treaty of Rome** – **EEC** (forerunner of the EU) and Euratom |
| 1986 | **Single European Act** |
| 1992 | **Maastricht Treaty** (signed 7 Feb 1992, in force 1993) – **created the EU** |
| 1999 | **Euro** launched (1 January 1999) only as a **unit of account**; notes and coins **in circulation from 1 January 2002** |
| 2007 | Bulgaria and Romania join – **27 members** |
| 2009 | **Lisbon Treaty** in force (Article 50 exit clause) |
| 2013 | **Croatia** joins (1 July 2013) – latest member; **28** |
| 2020 | **UK leaves, 31 January 2020** – back to **27** |
- The EU grew out of the **European Community**; EU citizens hold EU citizenship in addition to national citizenship; **Switzerland is not** a member.
- The EU has its **own flag, anthem and currency** but **no Constitution** (the 2004 Constitutional Treaty failed); its combined armed forces are the second largest in the world (NCERT).
- The **Preamble of the proposed EU Constitution** was drafted by **Valéry Giscard d'Estaing**, chairman of the European Convention (2002–03).
# Brexit
- Referendum **23 June 2016** (51.9% Leave) → David **Cameron** resigned; **Theresa May** (the Home Secretary) became Conservative leader and PM – the Conservatives stayed in power (the opposition did **not** come to power).
- Exit under **Article 50 TEU**; UK left **31 January 2020**.
- **Windsor Framework** (February 2023) – Northern Ireland, UK and EU.
# Other EU facts
- **Greek referendum (July 2015)** – whether to accept the **bailout conditions** of the EU and IMF (not on leaving the EU).
- **Ukraine and Moldova** granted candidate status – European Council, Brussels, **23 June 2022**.
- **Roberta Metsola** (Malta) – elected **President of the European Parliament**, January 2022.
- **Digital Single Market Strategy** – EU, adopted **6 May 2015**. **Stability and Growth Pact** – limits budget deficits.
- **India-EU FTA (2026):** EU commitments in **144 services subsectors**; social security agreements with all EU members within 5 years; post-study work framework for Indian students.
# Council of Europe
- Founded **1949**, HQ **Strasbourg**; upholds **human rights, democracy and rule of law** in Europe (European Convention on Human Rights) – **not** an EU body.
- **47 members** until **Russia was expelled (March 2022)** → now **46**.
> Exam angle: Maastricht created the EU; Croatia latest member; Brexit = Article 50; Euro not in circulation in 1999.

=== SAARC and BBIN
# Basics
- **Charter signed at Dhaka, 8 December 1985** (1st Summit) – SAARC founded **1985**, not 1984 or 1986.
- **Secretariat: Kathmandu** (Nepal) – not Dhaka (Charter venue) or New Delhi.
- **Seven founders:** India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Maldives, Bhutan. **Afghanistan** joined at the **14th Summit, April 2007** → **8 members** (not a founder).
- **Members (8):** Afghanistan, Bangladesh, **Bhutan**, India, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka.
- **Not members:** **Myanmar** (an observer), China (observer), Tibet (not a state), Malaysia, Iran. SAARC includes every land neighbour of India **except China and Myanmar**.
- **Observers (9):** EU, Australia, China, Iran, Japan, Republic of Korea, Mauritius, Myanmar, USA – China is **not** the only observer; **Russia is not** an observer.
# Secretariat and Secretary General
- The **Secretary General** heads the Secretariat; appointed by the **Council of Ministers** from member states in **alphabetical rotation** for a **three-year** term; assisted by **eight directors** on deputation from member states (CDS 2014: all three statements correct).
- **31st SAARC Council of Ministers** (February 2009) – **Colombo, Sri Lanka**.
# Summits
| Summit | Venue / date |
|---|---|
| 1st | Dhaka, December 1985 (Charter) |
| 12th | **Islamabad, January 2004** – **SAFTA** signed |
| 14th | New Delhi, April 2007 – Afghanistan admitted |
| 16th | **Thimphu, 28–29 April 2010** |
| 18th (last) | **Kathmandu, November 2014** |
| 19th | Islamabad, 2016 – **cancelled after the Uri attack** |
# Institutions and India's initiatives
- **SAPTA** 1995 → **SAFTA:** signed **2004**, in force **1 January 2006**; zero duty on eligible goods meeting Rules of Origin (DRI busted an areca-nut origin fraud, Aug 2026).
- **South Asian University** – **New Delhi** (2010). **SAARC Development Fund** – **Thimphu**. SAARC Disaster Management Centre – India.
- India's proposals: **South Asia Satellite (2017)**, **SAARC COVID-19 Emergency Fund (2020)**. SAARC Visa Exemption Scheme (1992) cancelled for Pakistanis after **Pahalgam (2025)**.
- Not all South Asian states are democracies (e.g., Taliban-ruled Afghanistan since 2021) – the wrong statement in CDS 2011.
- Jun 2026: SAARC and BIMSTEC both constrained by India's tensions with Islamabad and Dhaka.
# BBIN Motor Vehicles Agreement
- **Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Nepal**; signed at **Thimphu, 2015**; **Bhutan has not ratified** it.
> Exam angle: Charter **Dhaka** vs Secretariat **Kathmandu**; "not a member" → **Myanmar** (asked 5 times); 8 members.

=== ASEAN, East Asia Summit and RCEP
# ASEAN
- Founded by the **Bangkok Declaration, 8 August 1967** – five founders: Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand. **Secretariat: Jakarta**.
- **11 members** with **Timor-Leste (2025)**: Brunei, Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam, Timor-Leste.
- **Not members:** **China**, **India**, **South Korea**, Japan (dialogue partners). **Myanmar** joined ASEAN and BIMSTEC in **1997**; it is the **only ASEAN country with a land border with India**.
- **Timor-Leste (East Timor):** a **Portuguese** colony until 1975, then occupied by Indonesia (1975–99) and run by the UN (1999–2002); independent **20 May 2002**.
- Geostrategic importance of Southeast Asia: its **location between the Pacific and Indian Oceans** and its maritime character (UPSC 2011).
# India and ASEAN

|---|---|
| Sectoral dialogue partner | 1992 |
| Full dialogue partner | 1996 |
| Summit-level partner | 2002 |
| Comprehensive Strategic Partnership | 2022 |
- **ASEAN Regional Forum** – **1994**. **AIFTA (goods)** signed 2009, in force **2010**; India sought early completion of the **AITIGA review** (32nd ARF, Kuala Lumpur, July 2025).
# Summits and meetings
| Meeting | Venue |

# East Asia Summit
- **First EAS: 14 December 2005, Kuala Lumpur**; 16 countries (10 ASEAN + India, Japan, China, South Korea, Australia, NZ).
- Now **19**: 11 ASEAN + India, China, Japan, US, Russia, Australia, NZ, South Korea. India is a member of the **EAS**, but **not** of ASEAN or **APEC** (UPSC 2015). **20th EAS (Kuala Lumpur, 2025)** – Kuala Lumpur Declaration.
# RCEP
- **ASEAN-led** FTA: ten ASEAN + Australia, China, Japan, NZ, Korea; signed **15 November 2020**; about **30% of world population**; **India stayed out (November 2019)**.
- Seen as a counter to the US-led **Trans-Pacific Partnership**; often described as China-backed though it is ASEAN-centred.
# Mekong Ganga Cooperation
- Launched **10 November 2000, Vientiane**; **6 members**: India, Cambodia, Laos, Myanmar, Thailand, Vietnam; areas: **tourism, culture, education, transport and communications** (no defence).
> Exam angle: "not an ASEAN member" → China / South Korea / India (asked 3 times); RCEP belongs to ASEAN.

=== BIMSTEC
# Name, members, Secretariat
- **Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation** – set up by the **Bangkok Declaration, 1997** as **BIST-EC** (Bangladesh, India, Sri Lanka, Thailand); Myanmar joined 1997; **Nepal and Bhutan 2004**.
- **7 members:** Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand – **Pakistan is not** a member. **Secretariat: Dhaka**.
- A **bridge between South Asia (SAARC) and Southeast Asia (ASEAN)**. It was **never** called the "Bangkok Agreement" (that is the 1975 Asia-Pacific Trade Agreement, APTA) and was **not** renamed "BISTEC" in 2014.
- Sectors **cut from 14 to 7** (not expanded); each has a lead country. India leads **security** (counter-terrorism, disaster management, energy). Under the old 14 sectors **Myanmar** led **energy** (and agriculture).
# Summits
| Summit | Venue / date | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | **Bangkok, 31 July 2004** | – |
| 4th | **Kathmandu, 31 Aug 2018** | **Grid Interconnection MoU** signed |
| 5th | **30 March 2022, virtual, hosted by Sri Lanka** (Colombo); addressed by PM Modi | **BIMSTEC Charter** adopted; Sri Lanka chair (India did **not** chair it) |
| 6th | **Bangkok, 4 April 2025** (Thailand chair; PM Modi attended) | **Bangkok Vision 2030** ("Prosperous, Resilient and Open"); Maritime Transport Cooperation Agreement; India's 21-point Action Plan |
- Current chair: **Bangladesh** (from April 2025).
# Energy, security and India's initiatives
- **Grid Interconnection MoU:** Union Cabinet approved signing on **12 April 2017** (**power sector** – cross-border electricity trade); signed at the 4th Summit (2018); set up the **BIMSTEC Grid Interconnection Coordination Committee (BGICC)**.
- **First BIMSTEC Disaster Management Exercise (DMEx)** – **October 2017, hosted by India** (New Delhi/NCR).
- **MILEX 2018, Pune** – first BIMSTEC army exercise.
- **BIMSTEC leaders** were invited to PM Modi's **oath-taking, 30 May 2019**.
- **First BIMSTEC Conference on Combating Drug Trafficking** – **13–14 February 2020, New Delhi**, hosted by **India** (NCB).
- **1st BIMSTEC Youth Summit** – Gandhinagar (Feb 2025); **Cancer Care programme** at Tata Memorial (July 2025); **BODHI** training initiative (2025).
| BIMSTEC centre | Location |
|---|---|
| Cultural Industries Observatory | **Thimphu** |
| Energy Centre | **Bengaluru** (Host Country Agreement Nov 2024) |
| Centre for Weather and Climate | **Noida** |
| Technology Transfer Facility | **Colombo** |
- Weaknesses: few summits, weak Secretariat, slow FTA, Myanmar crisis.
> Exam angle: Secretariat **Dhaka**; 5th Summit host **Sri Lanka**; 6th Summit **Bangkok 2025**; centres → cities (UPSC 2026 match).

# Commonwealth
- **London Declaration 1949**; Secretariat London; **Secretary-General: Shirley Ayorkor Botchwey** (Ghana).

- Membership is **voluntary** – former British territories did not join automatically (e.g., Myanmar never joined).
- It had **no charter** until the **Commonwealth Charter** (adopted December 2012, signed **11 March 2013**).

=== SCO, GCC, IORA and African Union
# Shanghai Cooperation Organisation
- **Shanghai Five (1996)** → **SCO 2001**; Secretariat **Beijing**; **RATS** (Regional Anti-Terrorist Structure) at **Tashkent**.
- **10 members:** China, Russia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Uzbekistan, **India and Pakistan (2017, Astana)**, Iran (2023), **Belarus (2024 – newest)**.
| SCO summit | Venue |
|---|---|
| May 2003 | **Moscow** |
| 15–16 Sep 2022 | **Samarkand, Uzbekistan** |
| July 2024 | **Astana** – Belarus joined |
| 2025 (25th) | Tianjin, China |
# Gulf Cooperation Council
- **1981**, HQ **Riyadh**; **6 members:** Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE – **Iran is not** a member.
- **June 2017:** Saudi Arabia, UAE, Bahrain and Egypt (with others) cut ties with **Qatar**, citing its **support for terrorism and ties with Iran**; ended by the **Al-Ula Declaration (January 2021)**.
# IORA
- **Indian Ocean Rim Association** (1997, as IOR-ARC), Secretariat **Ebene, Mauritius**, **23** members; **India is a member**; **Nelson Mandela** was among the leaders behind its creation.
- Not "very recent" and not set up against piracy or oil spills; **not a maritime-security-only** alliance.
- **Priority areas:** maritime safety and security; trade and investment facilitation; fisheries management; disaster risk management; academic and scientific cooperation; tourism and cultural exchanges. Cross-cutting: blue economy, women's economic empowerment.
- **India is chair (2025-27)**, taking over from Sri Lanka in Nov 2025; **summit 2027**, first since **Jakarta 2017** (20th anniversary).
# African Union and AP angle
- **African Union** (2002) replaced the **OAU** (1963); HQ **Addis Ababa**; G20 permanent member since 2023.
- AP: **NACIN Palasamudram** (Rayalaseema) – a **World Customs Organization** regional training centre for officials of SAARC, ASEAN and African nations.
> Exam angle: "not a GCC member" → **Iran**; RATS → SCO; IORA Secretariat Ebene.
