=== Pravasi Bharatiya Divas and Diaspora Policy
- **L.M. Singhvi High Level Committee** on the Diaspora: set up **2000**, report **2002**; recommended PBD.
- **PBD** on **9 January** – **Mahatma Gandhi returned from South Africa on 9 January 1915**; declared by **Atal Bihari Vajpayee** on 8 January 2002; **first PBD 9 January 2003, New Delhi**; **biennial since 2015** (all four statements of the APPSC MVI question are correct).

|---|---|---|

| 13th | 2015 | **Gandhinagar** |
| 14th | 2017 | **Bengaluru** – Suriname Vice-President **Michael Ashwin Adhin** attended |
| **15th** | **21-23 January 2019** | **Varanasi** (not Prayagraj) |
| 16th | 2021 | Virtual |
| 17th | 2023 | **Indore** ("Diaspora: Reliable Partners for India's progress in Amrit Kaal") |

- **Pravasi Bharatiya Samman** – highest honour for overseas Indians, conferred by the **President**; **2015** list (presented at Gandhinagar): **Mala Mehta, Satya Nadella, Ashraf Palarkunnummal** and others – **not Ela Gandhi**.
- **Ministry of Overseas Indian Affairs:** created **May 2004** (first as Ministry of Non-Resident Indians' Affairs); merged into **MEA** in January 2016.
- **OCI** 2005; **PIO card merged into OCI in 2015**.
- Policy phases: Nehru – active dissociation; Rajiv Gandhi – outreach; **Vajpayee – PBD 2003**; Modi – leveraging the diaspora.
> Exam angle: PBD date (9 Jan), first year (2003) and venues (12th Delhi, 15th Varanasi) are asked again and again.

=== UN Peacekeeping
- **Three principles:** **consent** of the parties, **impartiality**, **no use of force except in self-defence and defence of the mandate**.
- **UNSC** creates and ends missions by resolution; **Department of Peace Operations** manages them; head = **Under-Secretary-General Jean-Pierre Lacroix** (since 2017, succeeded Hervé Ladsous; still in post 2026).
- **CUNPK, New Delhi** (Indian Army) trains Indian and foreign peacekeepers.
| Mission | Host | Period |
|---|---|---|
| **UNMIL** | **Liberia** | **2003-2018** |
| **MINURCAT** | CAR and Chad (not Haiti) | **2007-2010** |
| **MINUSTAH** | **Haiti** | **2004-2017** |
| **UNMISET** | **Timor-Leste** | **2002-2005** |
| UNMISS | South Sudan | 2011- |
| MONUSCO | DR Congo | 2010- |
| UNIFIL | Lebanon | 1978- |
- First contribution: **Korea, 1950**; largest contingent: **ONUC Congo (1960-64)**.
- India: **largest cumulative contributor** (2,90,000+ personnel since 1950); in monthly rankings India is now **fourth-largest** (2026). Around **2011** the top contributors were **Pakistan and Bangladesh**, with India third.
- **UNMISS:** India largest contributor (**1,775**). **All-women Formed Police Unit:** **Liberia, 2007**. **IPKF** (Sri Lanka) was not a UN force.

=== Diaspora Data and Mobility
- India has the **largest diaspora** (~35 million); major hosts **USA (~5.4 million)**, **UAE (~3.5 million – largest NRI population)**, Malaysia.
- India is the **top remittance recipient** – about **$129-135 billion (2024-25)**.
- Indians lead Fortune 500 firms – **Satya Nadella (Microsoft)**, Sundar Pichai (Google); India is the **largest supplier of foreign medical graduates** to the US and UK.
- Indian-born now Australia's largest immigrant community (Jul 2026).
- **Hong Kong** ended visa-free entry for Indians (pre-arrival registration from **23 January 2017**), citing rising **asylum claims / refugee problem**.
- **APNRTS:** set up by the AP Government in **May 2016**, chaired by the CM.
> Exam angle: largest host / remittances; visa curbs and their stated reasons.
