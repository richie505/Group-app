=== NOTES 5:92 | Climate Change – Greenhouse Effect, Greenhouse Gases, Radiative Forcing, Evidence and Impacts
## SUB 0 | The Greenhouse Effect
[92:0:0] - **Greenhouse effect** = natural warming caused by the **absorption and re-radiation of heat** by greenhouse gases; in exam words, a **rise in Earth's temperature due to concentration of gases**.
[92:0:1] - Concept proposed by **Joseph Fourier** in the **1820s**. Today the **WMO** is the main body reporting greenhouse gas levels.
[92:0:2] - Without it, Earth's average temperature would be about **−18°C** instead of about **+15°C**.
[92:0:3] # Mechanism – Step by Step
[92:0:4] - 1. Atmosphere is **transparent** to incoming **short-wave solar radiation**.
[92:0:5] - 2. Earth's surface re-emits **long-wave infrared** radiation (about 8,000-25,000 nm).
[92:0:6] - 3. Atmosphere is relatively **opaque** to this outgoing radiation; CO2 and water vapour absorb strongly at 14,000-25,000 nm.
[92:0:7] - 4. GHGs re-radiate heat **in all directions**, partly back to the surface.
[92:0:8] - **APPSC GS 2021**: Global warming = heat from sunlight, **re-radiated by the earth**, absorbed by CO2 and other gases.
[92:0:9] # Natural vs Enhanced Greenhouse Effect
[92:0:10] TABLE head: Feature | Natural | Enhanced (anthropogenic)
  [92:0:10.0] Cause | No human role | Human activities raise GHG levels
  [92:0:10.1] GHG levels | Stable for thousands of years | Rising: fossil fuels, deforestation, industry, agriculture, waste
  [92:0:10.2] Biggest contributor | Water vapour | CO2 (APPSC 2025 key)
  [92:0:10.3] Result | Keeps Earth habitable; essential for life | Global warming and climate change
[92:0:11] - **Primary cause of present global warming** = **increase in GHG concentrations** (APPSC 2025 key); **ozone depletion** is a separate problem.
[92:0:12] Transparent to short-wave coming IN; opaque to long-wave infrared going OUT. Not the other way round.
## SUB 1 | Heating of the Atmosphere
[92:1:0] - The atmosphere is heated mainly by **long-wave terrestrial radiation**, more than by incoming solar radiation.
[92:1:1] - CO2 and other GHGs **absorb long-wave radiation** well.
[92:1:2] TABLE head: Insolation | Share
  [92:1:2.0] Reflected (albedo) | about 35%
  [92:1:2.1] Absorbed by the atmosphere | about 14%
  [92:1:2.2] Absorbed by the surface | about 51%
[92:1:3] - Clouds and gases reflect about **one-fourth** of incoming solar radiation.
[92:1:4] - Earth's surface does **not** re-emit heat as **ultraviolet** (it emits infrared).
[92:1:5] - **Cloudy nights are warmer**: clouds stop heat escaping from the ground and send it back.
[92:1:6] - Heat trapped by the atmosphere maintains the average temperature; "without atmosphere it would be below freezing everywhere" was marked **wrong** (average would be about −18°C).
[92:1:7] - **Fresh snow** reflects the most sunlight: about **80-85%**.
## SUB 2 | Greenhouse Gases – Types, Order, NON-GHGs
[92:2:0] # The Gases
[92:2:1] TABLE head: Gas | Formula | Key fact
  [92:2:1.0] Water vapour | H2O | Most abundant and most important GHG; about half of Earth's greenhouse effect (NASA)
  [92:2:1.1] Carbon dioxide | CO2 | Main anthropogenic driver; about 66% of radiative forcing by long-lived GHGs (WMO); ~0.04% of air
  [92:2:1.2] Methane (marsh gas) | CH4 | Paddy fields, wetlands, cud-chewing animals; also in biogas
  [92:2:1.3] Nitrous oxide | N2O | Nitrogen fertilisers; both natural and man-made
  [92:2:1.4] Ozone | O3 | Stratospheric ozone = UV protection; tropospheric ozone = smog pollutant and GHG
  [92:2:1.5] CFCs | — | Both GHG and ozone-depleting
[92:2:2] - **Water vapour**: humans raise it only **indirectly**, by warming the air (APPSC GS 2023). **Warmer air holds more water vapour.**
[92:2:3] - **Primary GHGs**: water vapour, CO2, methane, N2O, CFCs, ozone. **Most important set**: CO2, methane, N2O, water vapour.
[92:2:4] - **Most responsible for global warming**: **CO2 and methane**.
[92:2:5] - **Oxygen** is not one of the four principal human-driven GHGs.
[92:2:6] - **Soot (black carbon), SF6 and water vapour** all warm the atmosphere; soot absorbs solar and infrared radiation.
[92:2:7] # Natural vs Synthetic
[92:2:8] TABLE head: Type | Gases
  [92:2:8.0] Natural | Water vapour, CO2, methane, N2O, ozone
  [92:2:8.1] Synthetic (entirely man-made) | CFCs, HFCs, PFCs, SF6
[92:2:9] # Contribution Order – Two Different Measures
[92:2:10] TABLE head: Measure | Order and shares
  [92:2:10.0] Share of the enhanced effect (textbook) | CO2 60% > CH4 20% > CFCs 15% > N2O 5%
  [92:2:10.1] Share of the whole greenhouse effect | Water vapour ~60%, CO2 ~26%, CH4 4-9%, O3 3-7%, N2O ~1.5%
  [92:2:10.2] Order among long-lived gases | CO2 > CH4 > N2O > CFCs
[92:2:11] Natural effect: water vapour biggest. Enhanced effect: CO2 biggest. Read which one the question asks.
[92:2:12] # What is NOT a Greenhouse Gas?
[92:2:13] TABLE head: Not a GHG | Why
  [92:2:13.0] Nitrogen (N2) – 78.08% of air | Does not absorb infrared
  [92:2:13.1] Oxygen (O2) – 20.95% | Does not absorb infrared
  [92:2:13.2] Argon (Ar) – 0.93% | Inert gas (APPSC 2025)
  [92:2:13.3] Hydrogen | Not a GHG
  [92:2:13.4] Sulphur dioxide (SO2) | Causes acid rain
  [92:2:13.5] Propane | Its use reduces GHG emissions
[92:2:14] - **Carbon monoxide**: only an **indirect** GHG; also the answer to "does **not** cause tropospheric ozone depletion".
[92:2:15] - **Oxides of sulphur and nitrogen** do not add to warming directly.
[92:2:16] - **Nitrogen oxide** = answer to "not a GHG **found naturally**"; naturally found: CO2, methane, ozone, water vapour.
[92:2:17] - CO2, N2O and methane: **all three are GHGs**.
[92:2:18] N2, O2, Ar make up the bulk of air but are NOT greenhouse gases.
## SUB 3 | Global Warming Potential, Lifetime and Source
[92:3:0] - **GWP** = heat a gas absorbs over a period (usually **100 years**) compared with the **same mass of CO2**.
[92:3:1] TABLE head: Gas | GWP (100 yr) | Lifetime | Main anthropogenic source
  [92:3:1.0] CO2 | 1 (reference) | 100-1,000 years | Fossil fuels, deforestation, cement
  [92:3:1.1] Methane | 28-36 | ~12 years | Paddies, livestock, landfills, wetlands, fossil fuels
  [92:3:1.2] N2O | 265-298 | ~114 years | Fertilisers, biomass burning
  [92:3:1.3] CFCs / HFCs | 1,000-23,000 | 50-500 years | Refrigerants, foams, aerosol sprays
  [92:3:1.4] SF6 | ~23,900 (highest) | Long-lived | Dielectric fluid in switchgear
  [92:3:1.5] Water vapour | Variable | ~9 days | Natural
[92:3:2] - Older IPCC values (CH4 21, N2O 310) are superseded.
[92:3:3] - **Perfluoromethane** comes from **aluminium** production.
[92:3:4] # Halocarbons (CFCs) – Ozone and Warming
[92:3:5] - CFCs contain **carbon, fluorine and chlorine**; **entirely man-made** (refrigerants, aerosols).
[92:3:6] - They are **greenhouse gases** and their **chlorine destroys ozone**: a double role.
## SUB 4 | Emission Sources – Sectors and Top Emitters
[92:4:0] # Sectors
[92:4:1] - **Largest source**: **fossil-fuel combustion** (coal, oil, gas) for energy, transport and industry.
[92:4:2] TABLE head: Sector (global GHG, 2016) | Share

  [92:4:2.1] Agriculture, forestry and land use | ~18%
  [92:4:2.2] Direct industrial processes (incl. cement) | ~5%
[92:4:3] - **Deforestation** destroys carbon sinks; burning or rotting trees release stored carbon.
[92:4:4] - Aerosols: **black and brown carbon warm**; **sulphate droplets cool**.
[92:4:5] # Methane and N2O from Farms
[92:4:6] - **Low-land (flooded) rice**: methane under **no-oxygen (anaerobic)** conditions; paddy fields emit about **50-100 million t/year**.
[92:4:7] - **Rice** = main human-made source of **both methane and N2O** (flooded paddies; burning straw and stubble).
[92:4:8] - India's irrigated paddy: **3.9 million t methane/yr**, a little more than Germany's total methane.
[92:4:9] - **Enteric fermentation** (cud-chewing livestock) → CH4; **nitrogen fertilisers** → N2O.
[92:4:10] TABLE head: Methane source | Type
  [92:4:10.0] Rice fields, coal mines, domestic animals | Human
  [92:4:10.1] Wetlands (largest natural), sea hydrates | Natural

[92:4:14] - **GHG Emission Intensity Target (Amendment) Rules 2025** under **EPA 1986**: **208 units** must cut emission intensity (GHG per unit of output, e.g. per tonne of cement).

## SUB 5 | Radiative Forcing, Sensitivity, Feedbacks and Tipping Points
[92:5:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [92:5:0.0] Radiative forcing | Change in Earth's energy balance (W/m²) caused by a factor such as a GHG
  [92:5:0.1] Climate sensitivity | Warming after a doubling of CO2 over pre-industrial levels
  [92:5:0.2] Albedo feedback | Ice melts → lower albedo → more heat absorbed → more melting (positive feedback)
  [92:5:0.3] Water vapour feedback | Warmer air holds more water vapour → traps more heat
  [92:5:0.4] Methane hydrates | Methane locked in ice lattices in permafrost and seabed; warming releases it = positive feedback
  [92:5:0.5] Tipping points | Thresholds where small changes cause self-feeding shifts, irreversible on human timescales; non-linear
[92:5:1] - **WMO GHG Bulletin (Oct 2025)**: CH4 gives about **16%** of radiative forcing by long-lived GHGs; **CO2 (not methane)** drove about **81%** of the **54% rise** in warming effect, 1990-2024.
[92:5:2] - Tipping example: beyond about **2°C** parts of the **Amazon** could turn into **savanna** (UNSG Scientific Advisory Board Brief #12). Beyond **3°C**, the land biosphere may turn from **carbon sink to source**.
[92:5:3] - India sought clarity at the **Bonn talks** on using tipping points for policy.
## SUB 6 | Short-lived Climate Pollutants (SLCPs)
[92:6:0] - Members: **black carbon, methane, HFCs, tropospheric ozone**; short life but strong warming. **Not water vapour.**
[92:6:1] - Tackled by the **Climate and Clean Air Coalition (CCAC)**.
[92:6:2] TABLE head: SLCP | Source | Lifetime
  [92:6:2.0] Black carbon | Incomplete combustion of biomass and diesel | Short
  [92:6:2.1] Methane | Rice, livestock, waste, fossil-fuel operations | ~12 years
  [92:6:2.2] Tropospheric ozone | Secondary: NOx + VOCs in sunlight | Short
[92:6:3] - Black carbon **darkens snow and ice** → lower albedo → faster glacier melt (APPSC-G2 2025: "short-lived pollutant produced by incomplete combustion").
[92:6:4] - Tropospheric (surface) ozone **cuts crop yields**; worst in summer.
[92:6:5] # Methane Initiatives – Dated
[92:6:6] - **Global Methane Pledge**: launched at **COP26, Glasgow (2021)**; cut methane **30% from 2020 levels by 2030**; **India has not signed** (Global Methane Status Report 2025, UNEP-CCAC).
[92:6:7] - **24 Jun 2026**: UN Secretary-General's **Call to Action on Methane** at **London Climate Action Week** (waste, agriculture, fossil fuels).

## SUB 8 | Observed Impacts
[92:8:0] TABLE head: Area | Observed change
  [92:8:0.0] Ocean heat | Oceans took over 90% of extra energy (1971-2010)
  [92:8:0.1] Ice sheets | Greenland and Antarctic both losing mass
  [92:8:0.2] Arctic sea ice | Down 3.5-4.1% per decade (1979-2012)

## SUB 9 | Carbon Accounting, Warming Limits and Projections
[92:9:0] # Carbon Terms

[92:9:4] - **UNFCCC** aim: stabilise GHG concentrations (emissions mitigation).
[92:9:5] - **EU CBAM**: fully enforced from **1 Jan 2026**. **Green Hydrogen Mission**: 5 MMT/year.
[92:9:6] # Warming Thresholds and Carbon Budget

=== NOTES 5:93 | Ocean Acidification – Causes, Effects on Marine Ecosystems and Coral Reefs
## SUB 0 | Ocean Acidification – Cause and Names
[93:0:0] - **Primary cause**: **dissolution of atmospheric CO2** in seawater (APPSC-GS 2024).
[93:0:1] - Called the **"evil twin of global warming"** and **"the other CO2 problem"**.
[93:0:2] - Oceans absorb about **one-third** of human CO2 (also cited 30-40%).
[93:0:3] - **Cement** sector: about **5-8%** of global anthropogenic CO2.
## SUB 1 | Carbonate Chemistry and pH
[93:1:0] - 1. CO2 + H2O → **carbonic acid** → H+ + bicarbonate.
[93:1:1] - 2. Extra **H+ binds carbonate ions**.
[93:1:2] - 3. **Less carbonate** left for shells and skeletons.
[93:1:3] - Surface pH **8.2 (pre-industrial) → ~8.1** = about **30% rise in acidity**; ocean **still alkaline**.
[93:1:4] - Worst in **cold/temperate and Arctic waters**: CO2 more soluble in cold water; melting sea ice exposes more water.
[93:1:5] - Eutrophic **bloom collapse** adds CO2 → worsens acidification.
[93:1:6] pH falls only 0.1, yet acidity rises ~30% (log scale). The ocean has not turned acidic.
## SUB 2 | Coral Reefs and Bleaching
[93:2:0] - Reef-building corals secrete **calcium carbonate** skeletons; symbiotic algae **zooxanthellae** give food and colour.
[93:2:1] - **Bleaching**: heat-stressed corals lose zooxanthellae → lose colour, food and skeleton-building.
[93:2:2] - **4th global bleaching event** (declared by NOAA-ICRI, Apr 2024): Jan 2023-Mar 2025 hit **84%** of world reefs in **82 countries/territories**. Earlier events: **1998, 2010, 2014-17**.

  [93:2:4.3] Andaman & Nicobar | Most diverse reefs in India
[93:2:5] - **Epithet and largest reef:** Coral reefs are called the **"rainforests of the sea"**: they support more species per unit area than any other marine habitat. The **Great Barrier Reef** (Queensland, **Australia**) is the **world's largest coral reef system**; UNESCO World Heritage Site since **1981**.
