## SUB 0 | Greenhouse Gases – Which Are and Which Are NOT
[0:0] # The greenhouse gases
[0:1] TABLE head: Gas | Formula | Key fact
  [0:1.0] Water vapour | H2O | Most abundant GHG and the biggest overall (natural) contributor to the greenhouse effect (about half of it)
  [0:1.1] Carbon dioxide | CO2 | Main anthropogenic GHG; product of fossil-fuel combustion; about 66% of radiative forcing by long-lived GHGs (WMO); ~0.04% of air
  [0:1.2] Methane (marsh gas) | CH4 | Paddy fields, wetlands, cud-chewing animals, coal mines, landfills; main component of natural gas and biogas
  [0:1.3] Nitrous oxide (laughing gas) | N2O | Nitrogen fertilisers, burning fuels and crop residue; also a weak anaesthetic (used in anaesthesia)
  [0:1.4] Ozone | O3 | Stratospheric ozone = UV shield; tropospheric ozone = smog pollutant and GHG
  [0:1.5] CFCs, HFCs, PFCs, SF6 | — | Synthetic (entirely man-made) fluorinated gases; CFCs are both GHG and ozone-depleting
[0:2] - **Natural GHGs:** water vapour, CO2, CH4, N2O, O3. **Synthetic:** CFCs, HFCs, PFCs, SF6 (do not occur naturally).
[0:3] - **Most important set:** **CO2, methane, nitrous oxide, water vapour**. **Most responsible pair for warming:** **CO2 and methane**. Four principal human-driven GHGs: CO2, CH4, N2O (+ fluorinated gases) – **never oxygen**.
[0:4] - **Statement sets:** "CO2, CFCs, CH4 and N2O are the main GHGs" – true; "marshes, paddy fields, cattle sheds and biogas plants add methane" – true; "CO2 and methane are commonly known GHGs" – true.
[0:5] - **Soot (black carbon), sulphur hexafluoride and water vapour** all warm the atmosphere (UPSC 2010: all three).
[0:6] - **Tropospheric ozone, methane, nitrous oxide** = a correct GHG group; aerosols, ammonia, lead and methanol are not GHGs.
[0:7] - **CFCs:** used as refrigerants, inactive (stable) in the lower atmosphere, destroy stratospheric ozone **and** are GHGs – "CFCs are not responsible for the greenhouse effect" is the **false** statement.
[0:8] - **Methane uses:** decomposition of methane gives **carbon black**, used in **printing ink**; Kigali Amendment (2016) phases down **HFCs** – 85% cut avoids about 70-72 billion t CO2e **by 2050**.
[0:9] # What is NOT a greenhouse gas
[0:10] TABLE head: Not a GHG | Why
  [0:10.0] Nitrogen (N2, di-nitrogen) – 78% of air | Does not absorb infrared
  [0:10.1] Oxygen (O2) – 21% of air | Does not absorb infrared
  [0:10.2] Argon (Ar) – 0.93% | Inert gas
  [0:10.3] Hydrogen | Not a GHG
  [0:10.4] Sulphur dioxide (SO2) | Causes acid rain (cools as sulphate aerosol)
  [0:10.5] Propane, sodium hydroxide, calcium carbonate | Fuel / chemical / solid mineral – not GHGs
[0:11] - **Carbon monoxide:** only an **indirect** GHG (TSPSC: "methane only" is a GHG, not CO); also the key to "which does **not** cause tropospheric ozone depletion" (UPPCS 2008).
[0:12] - **Oxides of sulphur and nitrogen** (SOx, NOx) do **not** warm directly; **nitrogen oxide (NO)** = "not a GHG found naturally" (UPPCS 2020). Strictly, the GHG is **nitrous oxide (N2O)**, not "nitrogen oxides".
[0:13] > Most-asked (×35): "which is NOT a GHG" – N2, O2, Ar, H2, SO2. Water vapour, CO2, CH4, N2O, O3 and CFCs are GHGs.

## SUB 1 | The Greenhouse Effect and Heating of the Atmosphere
[1:0] # Definition and mechanism
[1:1] - **Greenhouse effect** = natural warming of the **lower atmosphere** (surface and troposphere) by **absorption and re-radiation of heat** by GHGs – exam words: "**increase in atmospheric temperature due to concentration of GHGs**"; a **naturally occurring** phenomenon (not caused by biodegradable waste in water).
[1:2] - Concept first proposed by **Joseph Fourier** in the **1820s**.
[1:3] - Without it, Earth's average temperature would be about **−18°C** (Shankar: −19°C) instead of **+15°C**.
[1:4] - **Step 1:** atmosphere is **transparent to incoming short-wave** solar radiation.
[1:5] - **Step 2:** the surface re-emits **long-wave infrared** (8,000-25,000 nm) – **not ultraviolet**.
[1:6] - **Step 3:** CO2 and water vapour **strongly absorb infrared** (14,000-25,000 nm) – the atmosphere is **opaque to outgoing long-wave**.
[1:7] - **Step 4:** GHGs re-radiate heat in all directions, partly back to the surface: GHGs warm the air by **trapping heat** (not generating it).
[1:8] - **Glass analogy:** the glass of a greenhouse or of a **solar cooker** lets light in and stops long-wave heat going out – the same principle.
[1:9] - **Global warming** = gradual long-term rise in the average temperature of the atmosphere and oceans; mainly human (fossil fuels); GHGs **intensify** the natural effect (TSPSC: all four statements true).
[1:10] # Heating of the atmosphere – heat budget
[1:11] TABLE head: Insolation (100 units) | Share
  [1:11.0] Reflected (albedo): 27 from cloud tops, 2 from snow/ice, rest scattered | 35
  [1:11.1] Absorbed by the atmosphere | 14
  [1:11.2] Absorbed by the surface | 51
[1:12] - The atmosphere is heated **mainly by long-wave terrestrial radiation**, indirectly from the earth, not by incoming solar radiation (UPSC 2024: Statement I wrong, II right).
[1:13] - **Clouds and gases reflect about one-fourth** of insolation (27 units from cloud tops); the surface does **not** re-emit heat as UV (CGPSC 2023: only statement 2).
[1:14] - **Cloudy nights are warmer**: clouds stop heat radiating away from the ground and send it back.
[1:15] - **Fresh snow** has the highest albedo – reflects about **80-85%** of sunlight (more than sand desert, paddy or prairie).
[1:16] - UPSC 2025: heat trapped by the atmosphere maintains the average temperature, and CO2 is a good absorber (II, III true); "below freezing **everywhere** without an atmosphere" is too sweeping – the day side would be very hot, like the Moon.
[1:17] > The atmosphere is warmed from below: short-wave in, long-wave out. "Heated more by incoming solar radiation" is always the wrong statement.

## SUB 2 | Sources – Fossil Fuels, Deforestation, Paddy and Livestock
[2:0] - **Largest source of GHGs:** **burning fossil fuels** (coal, oil, gas) for electricity, transport and industry, plus **deforestation** – the human activities that contribute most to warming.
[2:1] - **Primary cause of present global warming** = **increase in GHG concentrations**, not ozone depletion, over-population or solar flares.
[2:2] - **Deforestation increases CO2** (stored carbon released, fewer sinks) and causes **loss of biodiversity**; "deforestation is decreasing CO2" is the **incorrect** statement. **Forest regeneration** mitigates the greenhouse effect.
[2:3] - **Primary causes of climate change (UPSC/UPPSC 2017):** excessive burning of fossil fuels, exploding numbers of oil-driven automobiles, heavy deforestation – **not increasing solar flares** (I, II and IV).
[2:4] - **AP Group-IV 2023:** coal and petroleum burning, deforestation, methane from animal waste – **all three** contribute.
[2:5] - Reducing warming: public transport, less refrigerant use, **afforestation** – all help.
[2:6] # Methane and nitrous oxide from farms
[2:7] TABLE head: Source | Gas | Why
  [2:7.0] Low-land (flooded) rice / paddy fields | Methane | Organic matter decays without oxygen (anaerobic); 50-100 million t/yr
  [2:7.1] Nitrogen fertilisers on rice and other crops | N2O | Soil microbes release N2O
  [2:7.2] Cud-chewing animals (enteric fermentation) | Methane | Rumen fermentation
  [2:7.3] Rice fields, coal mining, domestic animals | Methane (human) | —
  [2:7.4] Wetlands (largest natural), sea hydrates | Methane (natural) | —
[2:8] - **Rice** = the most important anthropogenic source of **both methane and nitrous oxide** (UPSC 2022); UPSC 2010: both statements on anaerobic methane and fertiliser N2O are correct.
[2:9] - UPSC 2008: **rice fields, coal mining, domestic animals and wetlands – all four** are methane sources.
[2:10] - Methane is **not** a fuel-exhaust gas of automobiles or industrial chimneys; among such options **wetland** is the source.
[2:11] - **Source matching (UPPSC 2017):** CO2 – thermal power stations; N2O – waterlogged paddy; SO2 – brick kilns; **CFC – automobile is the wrong pair** (CFCs come from refrigerants, foams, aerosols).
[2:12] > Paddy → methane (anaerobic); fertiliser → N2O; the TSPSC/UPSC rice questions ask for both.

## SUB 3 | Contribution Order and Global Warming Potential
[3:0] # Two different measures
[3:1] TABLE head: Measure | Order and shares
  [3:1.0] Whole (natural) greenhouse effect | Water vapour biggest (about half; most abundant)
  [3:1.1] Share of enhanced (human) warming – textbook (NCERT Biology XII; NextGen) | CO2 60% > CH4 20% > CFCs 14-15% > N2O 5-6%
  [3:1.2] Radiative forcing by long-lived GHGs (WMO) | CO2 ~66% > CH4 ~16% > N2O ~6% > CFCs
[3:2] - **Increasing order of contribution (TSPSC 2023):** **N2O < CH4 < CO2**.
[3:3] - **Highest contribution to total global warming / main component / largest concentration among CO2, CH4, N2O, CFCs** = **CO2**. **Second most abundant anthropogenic GHG** after CO2 = **methane**.
[3:4] - **Water vapour**: humans raise it only **indirectly**, by warming the air (warmer air holds more water vapour) – APPSC Degree Lecturers 2023.
[3:5] # Global Warming Potential (GWP)
[3:6] - **GWP** = heat a gas absorbs over a period (usually **100 years**) relative to the **same mass of CO2** (GWP 1, the reference).
[3:7] TABLE head: Gas | GWP (100 yr) | Lifetime | Main anthropogenic source
  [3:7.0] CO2 | 1 | 50-200 years (and longer) | Fossil fuels, deforestation, cement
  [3:7.1] Methane | 21 (older IPCC) / 28-36 (newer) | ~12 years | Paddies, livestock, landfills, wetlands
  [3:7.2] N2O | 310 (older) / 265-298 | ~114-120 years | Fertilisers and biomass burning
  [3:7.3] HFCs | 140-11,700 | 1-270 years | Refrigerants (CFC substitutes)
  [3:7.4] PFCs (perfluoromethane) | 6,500-9,200 | 800-50,000 years | Aluminium production
  [3:7.5] SF6 | 23,900 (highest) | 3,200 years | Dielectric fluid in switchgear
[3:8] - **Ascending GWP:** **CO2 < CH4 < N2O < HFC** (Degree Lecturers 2017).
[3:9] - CFCs contain **carbon, fluorine and chlorine**; entirely man-made.
[3:10] > "Largest contributor to the greenhouse effect" with water vapour offered → water vapour; without it → CO2.

[4:0] TABLE head: Area | Observed change
  [4:0.0] Glaciers | Retreat worldwide; Gangotri melting = global warming
  [4:0.1] Sea level | Rising (melting ice + thermal expansion) – changes coastlines
  [4:0.2] Weather | Extreme events, altered rainfall, early flowering (mango), crop-pattern change
  [4:0.3] Health | Spread of diseases, heat stress
  [4:0.4] Ocean heat | Oceans took over 90% of the extra energy (1971-2010)
  [4:0.5] Ice sheets | Greenland and Antarctic both losing mass
  [4:0.6] Arctic sea ice | Down 3.5-4.1% per decade (1979-2012)
  [4:0.7] Antarctic sea ice | Sharp decline since 2015 (not expanding)
[4:1] - Impacts of global warming: **melting glaciers, sea-level rise, weather changes, rise in temperature** – all four (UPSC 2009); "better global communication" is not an impact.
[4:2] - **Matching (APPSC):** global warming – rising sea levels; air pollution – respiratory illness; sub-regional temperature – **patches of cooling**; habitat loss – biodiversity extinction.
[4:3] - **Floods** = disasters caused by **indirect** human action (printed key); wars and pollution are direct.
[4:4] - **Anthropocene** = proposed name for the epoch of human impact (Holocene is the current official epoch).
[4:5] - **Indicators of past climate change:** floral (plant fossils), cryogenic (glaciation), tectonic (continental drift) – MPPSC 2015 key: "long-term change" is not an indicator.
[4:6] # Evidence
[4:7] - **Keeling curve:** CO2 record at **Mauna Loa, Hawaii**, since **1958** (Charles David Keeling). **Ice cores**: air bubbles give past CO2 and temperature.
[4:8] - **WMO** publishes the **Greenhouse Gas Bulletin**. Bulletin 2025 (data for 2024): CO2 **423.9 ppm**, highest in about **2 million years**; rise of **3.5 ppm** in 2024, largest since 1957; CO2 **152%**, CH4 **266%**, N2O 125% of pre-industrial (CO2 ~278-280 ppm then).
[4:9] - **2024** = first calendar year above **1.5°C**; each of the last three decades warmer than any since **1850**; long-term warming about **1.3°C** (UNSG brief, Jun 2026).

## SUB 5 | Radiative Forcing, Feedbacks, Tipping Points and Warming Limits
[5:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [5:0.0] Radiative forcing | Change in Earth's energy balance (W/m²) caused by a factor such as a GHG
  [5:0.1] Climate sensitivity | Warming after a doubling of CO2 over pre-industrial
  [5:0.2] Albedo feedback | Ice melts → lower albedo → more heat absorbed → more melting (positive feedback)
  [5:0.3] Water vapour feedback | Warmer air holds more water vapour → traps more heat
  [5:0.4] Methane hydrates | Methane locked in ice lattices in permafrost and seabed
  [5:0.5] Tipping points | Thresholds where small changes trigger self-perpetuating shifts, irreversible on human timescales
[5:1] - **WMO Bulletin (Oct 2025):** methane ≈ **16%** of forcing by long-lived GHGs; **CO2** drove about 81% of the **54% rise** in warming effect, 1990-2024.
[5:2] - Beyond about **2°C**, parts of the **Amazon** could turn to **savanna**.
[5:3] - **UPSC 2014 (beyond 3°C):** terrestrial biosphere tends to a **net carbon source** and **widespread coral mortality** – 1 and 2 only; "all wetlands disappear" and "no cereals anywhere" are extreme.
[5:4] - **Safe warming?** Paris uses **1.5°C and 2°C** as limits, but **no level is proven "safe"** (AP Asst. Director key: "No one knows").
[5:5] - Remaining **1.5°C carbon budget**: about **130 Gt CO2** from start of 2025. **UNEP Emissions Gap Report 2025**: updated NDCs → **2.3-2.5°C**; **Limiting Overshoot (Sep 2026)**: **2.6°C** on current policies.

## SUB 6 | Carbon Footprint, Carbon Pricing and Removal
[6:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [6:0.0] Carbon footprint | Total GHGs (CO2) emitted by the activities of a person, organisation or product, in t CO2 per year
  [6:0.1] Carbon credit | Tradable permit to emit one metric tonne of CO2 or equivalent
  [6:0.2] Social cost of carbon | Money value of the long-term damage from one tonne of CO2 emitted in a given year
  [6:0.3] Carbon sequestration | Storing carbon in sinks: soil, biochar, agroforestry, forests

## SUB 7 | Short-lived Climate Pollutants and Black Carbon
[7:0] - **SLCPs:** **black carbon, methane, HFCs, tropospheric ozone** – short life, strong warming; not water vapour, N2O or SF6.
[7:1] - **Black carbon** = **short-lived pollutant from incomplete combustion** (biomass and diesel); darkens snow and ice → lower albedo → faster melt; it is not methane, not from CFCs and does not deplete ozone.
[7:2] - **Climate and Clean Air Coalition (CCAC)**, 2012: tackles **methane, black carbon and HFCs**; launched by a few countries with UNEP – **not a G20 initiative** (UPSC 2017: statement 2 only).
[7:3] - **Tropospheric ozone**: a **secondary pollutant** (NOx + VOCs in sunlight); **cuts crop yields**, worst in summer.
[7:4] - Aerosols: **black and brown carbon warm; sulphate droplets cool**.
[7:5] - **Global Methane Pledge**: COP26 Glasgow (2021); cut methane **30% from 2020 levels by 2030**; **India has not signed**. UNSG **Call to Action on Methane**: **London Climate Action Week**, 24 Jun 2026.
