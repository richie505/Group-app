=== Greenhouse Gases – Which Are and Which Are NOT
# The greenhouse gases
| Gas | Formula | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| Water vapour | H2O | **Most abundant** GHG and the **biggest overall (natural) contributor** to the greenhouse effect (about half of it) |
| Carbon dioxide | CO2 | **Main anthropogenic GHG**; product of **fossil-fuel combustion**; about **66%** of radiative forcing by long-lived GHGs (WMO); ~0.04% of air |
| Methane (**marsh gas**) | CH4 | Paddy fields, wetlands, cud-chewing animals, coal mines, landfills; main component of natural gas and **biogas** |
| Nitrous oxide (**laughing gas**) | N2O | Nitrogen fertilisers, burning fuels and crop residue; also a weak **anaesthetic** (used in anaesthesia) |
| Ozone | O3 | Stratospheric ozone = UV shield; **tropospheric ozone** = smog pollutant and GHG |
| CFCs, HFCs, PFCs, SF6 | — | Synthetic (entirely man-made) fluorinated gases; CFCs are **both GHG and ozone-depleting** |
- **Natural GHGs:** water vapour, CO2, CH4, N2O, O3. **Synthetic:** CFCs, HFCs, PFCs, SF6 (do not occur naturally).
- **Most important set:** **CO2, methane, nitrous oxide, water vapour**. **Most responsible pair for warming:** **CO2 and methane**. Four principal human-driven GHGs: CO2, CH4, N2O (+ fluorinated gases) – **never oxygen**.
- **Statement sets:** "CO2, CFCs, CH4 and N2O are the main GHGs" – true; "marshes, paddy fields, cattle sheds and biogas plants add methane" – true; "CO2 and methane are commonly known GHGs" – true.
- **Soot (black carbon), sulphur hexafluoride and water vapour** all warm the atmosphere (UPSC 2010: all three).
- **Tropospheric ozone, methane, nitrous oxide** = a correct GHG group; aerosols, ammonia, lead and methanol are not GHGs.
- **CFCs:** used as refrigerants, inactive (stable) in the lower atmosphere, destroy stratospheric ozone **and** are GHGs – "CFCs are not responsible for the greenhouse effect" is the **false** statement.
- **Methane uses:** decomposition of methane gives **carbon black**, used in **printing ink**; Kigali Amendment (2016) phases down **HFCs** – 85% cut avoids about 70-72 billion t CO2e **by 2050**.
# What is NOT a greenhouse gas
| Not a GHG | Why |
|---|---|
| **Nitrogen (N2, di-nitrogen)** – 78% of air | Does not absorb infrared |
| **Oxygen (O2)** – 21% of air | Does not absorb infrared |
| **Argon (Ar)** – 0.93% | Inert gas |
| **Hydrogen** | Not a GHG |
| **Sulphur dioxide (SO2)** | Causes acid rain (cools as sulphate aerosol) |
| Propane, sodium hydroxide, calcium carbonate | Fuel / chemical / solid mineral – not GHGs |
- **Carbon monoxide:** only an **indirect** GHG (TSPSC: "methane only" is a GHG, not CO); also the key to "which does **not** cause tropospheric ozone depletion" (UPPCS 2008).
- **Oxides of sulphur and nitrogen** (SOx, NOx) do **not** warm directly; **nitrogen oxide (NO)** = "not a GHG found naturally" (UPPCS 2020). Strictly, the GHG is **nitrous oxide (N2O)**, not "nitrogen oxides".
> Most-asked (×35): "which is NOT a GHG" – N2, O2, Ar, H2, SO2. Water vapour, CO2, CH4, N2O, O3 and CFCs are GHGs.

=== The Greenhouse Effect and Heating of the Atmosphere
# Definition and mechanism
- **Greenhouse effect** = natural warming of the **lower atmosphere** (surface and troposphere) by **absorption and re-radiation of heat** by GHGs – exam words: "**increase in atmospheric temperature due to concentration of GHGs**"; a **naturally occurring** phenomenon (not caused by biodegradable waste in water).
- Concept first proposed by **Joseph Fourier** in the **1820s**.
- Without it, Earth's average temperature would be about **−18°C** (Shankar: −19°C) instead of **+15°C**.
- **Step 1:** atmosphere is **transparent to incoming short-wave** solar radiation.
- **Step 2:** the surface re-emits **long-wave infrared** (8,000-25,000 nm) – **not ultraviolet**.
- **Step 3:** CO2 and water vapour **strongly absorb infrared** (14,000-25,000 nm) – the atmosphere is **opaque to outgoing long-wave**.
- **Step 4:** GHGs re-radiate heat in all directions, partly back to the surface: GHGs warm the air by **trapping heat** (not generating it).
- **Glass analogy:** the glass of a greenhouse or of a **solar cooker** lets light in and stops long-wave heat going out – the same principle.
- **Global warming** = gradual long-term rise in the average temperature of the atmosphere and oceans; mainly human (fossil fuels); GHGs **intensify** the natural effect (TSPSC: all four statements true).
# Heating of the atmosphere – heat budget
| Insolation (100 units) | Share |
|---|---|
| Reflected (albedo): 27 from cloud tops, 2 from snow/ice, rest scattered | **35** |
| Absorbed by the atmosphere | **14** |
| Absorbed by the surface | **51** |
- The atmosphere is heated **mainly by long-wave terrestrial radiation**, indirectly from the earth, not by incoming solar radiation (UPSC 2024: Statement I wrong, II right).
- **Clouds and gases reflect about one-fourth** of insolation (27 units from cloud tops); the surface does **not** re-emit heat as UV (CGPSC 2023: only statement 2).
- **Cloudy nights are warmer**: clouds stop heat radiating away from the ground and send it back.
- **Fresh snow** has the highest albedo – reflects about **80-85%** of sunlight (more than sand desert, paddy or prairie).
- UPSC 2025: heat trapped by the atmosphere maintains the average temperature, and CO2 is a good absorber (II, III true); "below freezing **everywhere** without an atmosphere" is too sweeping – the day side would be very hot, like the Moon.
> The atmosphere is warmed from below: short-wave in, long-wave out. "Heated more by incoming solar radiation" is always the wrong statement.

=== Sources – Fossil Fuels, Deforestation, Paddy and Livestock
- **Largest source of GHGs:** **burning fossil fuels** (coal, oil, gas) for electricity, transport and industry, plus **deforestation** – the human activities that contribute most to warming.
- **Primary cause of present global warming** = **increase in GHG concentrations**, not ozone depletion, over-population or solar flares.
- **Deforestation increases CO2** (stored carbon released, fewer sinks) and causes **loss of biodiversity**; "deforestation is decreasing CO2" is the **incorrect** statement. **Forest regeneration** mitigates the greenhouse effect.
- **Primary causes of climate change (UPSC/UPPSC 2017):** excessive burning of fossil fuels, exploding numbers of oil-driven automobiles, heavy deforestation – **not increasing solar flares** (I, II and IV).
- **AP Group-IV 2023:** coal and petroleum burning, deforestation, methane from animal waste – **all three** contribute.
- Reducing warming: public transport, less refrigerant use, **afforestation** – all help.
# Methane and nitrous oxide from farms
| Source | Gas | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Low-land (flooded) rice / paddy fields** | **Methane** | Organic matter decays **without oxygen (anaerobic)**; 50-100 million t/yr |
| Nitrogen fertilisers on rice and other crops | **N2O** | Soil microbes release N2O |
| Cud-chewing animals (enteric fermentation) | Methane | Rumen fermentation |
| Rice fields, coal mining, domestic animals | Methane (human) | — |
| **Wetlands** (largest natural), sea hydrates | Methane (natural) | — |
- **Rice** = the most important anthropogenic source of **both methane and nitrous oxide** (UPSC 2022); UPSC 2010: both statements on anaerobic methane and fertiliser N2O are correct.
- UPSC 2008: **rice fields, coal mining, domestic animals and wetlands – all four** are methane sources.
- Methane is **not** a fuel-exhaust gas of automobiles or industrial chimneys; among such options **wetland** is the source.
- **Source matching (UPPSC 2017):** CO2 – thermal power stations; N2O – waterlogged paddy; SO2 – brick kilns; **CFC – automobile is the wrong pair** (CFCs come from refrigerants, foams, aerosols).
> Paddy → methane (anaerobic); fertiliser → N2O; the TSPSC/UPSC rice questions ask for both.

=== Contribution Order and Global Warming Potential
# Two different measures
| Measure | Order and shares |
|---|---|
| Whole (natural) greenhouse effect | **Water vapour** biggest (about half; most abundant) |
| Share of enhanced (human) warming – textbook (NCERT Biology XII; NextGen) | **CO2 60% > CH4 20% > CFCs 14-15% > N2O 5-6%** |
| Radiative forcing by long-lived GHGs (WMO) | CO2 ~66% > CH4 ~16% > N2O ~6% > CFCs |
- **Increasing order of contribution (TSPSC 2023):** **N2O < CH4 < CO2**.
- **Highest contribution to total global warming / main component / largest concentration among CO2, CH4, N2O, CFCs** = **CO2**. **Second most abundant anthropogenic GHG** after CO2 = **methane**.
- **Water vapour**: humans raise it only **indirectly**, by warming the air (warmer air holds more water vapour) – APPSC Degree Lecturers 2023.
# Global Warming Potential (GWP)
- **GWP** = heat a gas absorbs over a period (usually **100 years**) relative to the **same mass of CO2** (GWP 1, the reference).
| Gas | GWP (100 yr) | Lifetime | Main anthropogenic source |
|---|---|---|---|
| CO2 | 1 | 50-200 years (and longer) | Fossil fuels, deforestation, cement |
| Methane | 21 (older IPCC) / 28-36 (newer) | ~12 years | Paddies, livestock, landfills, wetlands |
| N2O | 310 (older) / 265-298 | ~114-120 years | **Fertilisers and biomass burning** |
| HFCs | 140-11,700 | 1-270 years | Refrigerants (CFC substitutes) |
| PFCs (perfluoromethane) | 6,500-9,200 | 800-50,000 years | **Aluminium production** |
| SF6 | **23,900 (highest)** | 3,200 years | **Dielectric fluid in switchgear** |
- **Ascending GWP:** **CO2 < CH4 < N2O < HFC** (Degree Lecturers 2017).
- CFCs contain **carbon, fluorine and chlorine**; entirely man-made.
> "Largest contributor to the greenhouse effect" with water vapour offered → water vapour; without it → CO2.

=== Observed Impacts and Evidence
| Area | Observed change |
|---|---|
| Glaciers | Retreat worldwide; **Gangotri** melting = **global warming** |
| Sea level | Rising (melting ice + thermal expansion) – changes coastlines |
| Weather | Extreme events, altered rainfall, early flowering (mango), crop-pattern change |
| Health | Spread of diseases, heat stress |
| Ocean heat | Oceans took **over 90%** of the extra energy (1971-2010) |
| Ice sheets | **Greenland and Antarctic** both losing mass |
| Arctic sea ice | Down **3.5-4.1% per decade** (1979-2012) |
| Antarctic sea ice | Sharp decline since 2015 (not expanding) |
- Impacts of global warming: **melting glaciers, sea-level rise, weather changes, rise in temperature** – all four (UPSC 2009); "better global communication" is not an impact.
- **Matching (APPSC):** global warming – rising sea levels; air pollution – respiratory illness; sub-regional temperature – **patches of cooling**; habitat loss – biodiversity extinction.
- **Floods** = disasters caused by **indirect** human action (printed key); wars and pollution are direct.
- **Anthropocene** = proposed name for the epoch of human impact (Holocene is the current official epoch).
- **Indicators of past climate change:** floral (plant fossils), cryogenic (glaciation), tectonic (continental drift) – MPPSC 2015 key: "long-term change" is not an indicator.
# Evidence
- **Keeling curve:** CO2 record at **Mauna Loa, Hawaii**, since **1958** (Charles David Keeling). **Ice cores**: air bubbles give past CO2 and temperature.
- **WMO** publishes the **Greenhouse Gas Bulletin**. Bulletin 2025 (data for 2024): CO2 **423.9 ppm**, highest in about **2 million years**; rise of **3.5 ppm** in 2024, largest since 1957; CO2 **152%**, CH4 **266%**, N2O 125% of pre-industrial (CO2 ~278-280 ppm then).
- **2024** = first calendar year above **1.5°C**; each of the last three decades warmer than any since **1850**; long-term warming about **1.3°C** (UNSG brief, Jun 2026).

=== Radiative Forcing, Feedbacks, Tipping Points and Warming Limits
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Radiative forcing | Change in Earth's energy balance (**W/m²**) caused by a factor such as a GHG |
| Climate sensitivity | Warming after a **doubling** of CO2 over pre-industrial |
| Albedo feedback | Ice melts → lower albedo → more heat absorbed → more melting (**positive feedback**) |
| Water vapour feedback | Warmer air holds more water vapour → traps more heat |
| Methane hydrates | Methane locked in ice lattices in permafrost and seabed |
| Tipping points | Thresholds where small changes trigger self-perpetuating shifts, irreversible on human timescales |
- **WMO Bulletin (Oct 2025):** methane ≈ **16%** of forcing by long-lived GHGs; **CO2** drove about 81% of the **54% rise** in warming effect, 1990-2024.
- Beyond about **2°C**, parts of the **Amazon** could turn to **savanna**.
- **UPSC 2014 (beyond 3°C):** terrestrial biosphere tends to a **net carbon source** and **widespread coral mortality** – 1 and 2 only; "all wetlands disappear" and "no cereals anywhere" are extreme.
- **Safe warming?** Paris uses **1.5°C and 2°C** as limits, but **no level is proven "safe"** (AP Asst. Director key: "No one knows").
- Remaining **1.5°C carbon budget**: about **130 Gt CO2** from start of 2025. **UNEP Emissions Gap Report 2025**: updated NDCs → **2.3-2.5°C**; **Limiting Overshoot (Sep 2026)**: **2.6°C** on current policies.

=== Carbon Footprint, Carbon Pricing and Removal
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Carbon footprint** | Total GHGs (CO2) emitted by the activities of a person, organisation or product, in **t CO2 per year** |
| Carbon credit | Tradable permit to emit **one metric tonne of CO2** or equivalent |
| **Social cost of carbon** | Money value of the **long-term damage from one tonne of CO2** emitted in a given year |
| Carbon sequestration | Storing carbon in sinks: soil, biochar, agroforestry, forests |
| CCUS | Captures CO2 from **point sources** (factory flue gases) |
| **Direct Air Capture (DAC)** | Removes CO2 from **ambient air** – sequestration; CO2 for plastics, food processing, and synthetic low-carbon aviation fuel (UPSC 2025: all three) |
| Carbon bomb | Fossil project able to emit **over 1 Gt CO2**; **LINGO** lists 425 (India: **Gevra, Rajmahal**) |
- **Singapore**: carbon tax **from 2019** (announced Feb 2018, under Paris commitments).
- India: CCUS road map covers **industry**; agriculture removal = soil carbon, biochar, agroforestry; country-level social cost of carbon **$86/t CO2**.
- **EU CBAM** fully enforced from **1 Jan 2026**.

=== Short-lived Climate Pollutants and Black Carbon
- **SLCPs:** **black carbon, methane, HFCs, tropospheric ozone** – short life, strong warming; not water vapour, N2O or SF6.
- **Black carbon** = **short-lived pollutant from incomplete combustion** (biomass and diesel); darkens snow and ice → lower albedo → faster melt; it is not methane, not from CFCs and does not deplete ozone.
- **Climate and Clean Air Coalition (CCAC)**, 2012: tackles **methane, black carbon and HFCs**; launched by a few countries with UNEP – **not a G20 initiative** (UPSC 2017: statement 2 only).
- **Tropospheric ozone**: a **secondary pollutant** (NOx + VOCs in sunlight); **cuts crop yields**, worst in summer.
- Aerosols: **black and brown carbon warm; sulphate droplets cool**.
- **Global Methane Pledge**: COP26 Glasgow (2021); cut methane **30% from 2020 levels by 2030**; **India has not signed**. UNSG **Call to Action on Methane**: **London Climate Action Week**, 24 Jun 2026.
