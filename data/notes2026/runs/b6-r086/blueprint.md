- **India's rank with edition:** "38th of 139" is both LPI 2023 and GII 2025; WPFI 138 (2018) → 159 → 151 → 157 (2026).
- **AP angle:** IJR 2025 (2nd), IJR Consumer Justice 2026 (1st), N-SIPI 2016 (4th), SEEI (5–15 Mtoe group), Export Preparedness (5th), PLFS literacy (lowest).
- **Statements / pairs / NOT:** Swachh Survekshan award pairs, ISR 2024 statements, CCPI statements, TDR 2017 statements.
- **Current linkage / stale values:** Doomsday Clock (2½ min in 2017 → 85 s in 2026); Henley topper (Japan → Singapore).
