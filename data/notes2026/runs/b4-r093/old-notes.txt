## SUB 2 | East vs West Coast – Physical Reasons
[93:2:0] - East coast has fewer ports: **shallow continental shelf** + **cyclones and storms** (APPSC 2023 key: statements 1 and 2).
[93:2:1] - **Deep water close to shore** is found on the **west coast**, not the east.
[93:2:2] - West = sheltered natural harbours; east = mostly artificial.

[93:7:2] - **UDAN** (Ude Desh ka Aam Nagrik): launched **2016**; works through **viability gap funding**.
[93:7:3] - Operational airports **74 (2014) → 164 (2025)**; India = **3rd largest domestic** aviation market.
[93:7:4] - **Cochin (CIAL)** = world's **first fully solar-powered airport**.
[93:7:5] - **Busiest airport:** **Delhi IGI** (~78.1 million passengers in 2025; 11th in world); Mumbai 2nd (~55.5 million).
[93:7:6] - **AAI:** **Airports Authority of India**, formed **1995**, manages most Indian airports.
[93:7:7] - **Highest airports:** **Leh** = India's highest commercial airport; world's highest = **Daocheng Yading, China** (~4,411 m); Lhasa Gonggar ~3,570 m.
