## SUB 5 | National Waterways and Inland Water Transport
[5:0] - **Navigable inland waterways: about 14,500 km** (rivers, canals, backwaters, creeks) – about **1%** of transport.
[5:1] - **IWAI:** IWAI Act **1985**, set up **1986**, HQ **Noida**. **CIWTC** (Central Inland Water Transport Corporation) HQ **Kolkata**.
[5:2] - **National Waterways Act 2016** declared **111 NWs** (5 existing + 106 new); 25 viable, 13 operational. Before late 2008 only **3 NWs** existed (CDS 2008: statement "three NWs" was then true).
[5:3] TABLE head: NW | Route | Length
  [5:3.0] NW-1 (1986) | Ganga-Bhagirathi-Hooghly, Allahabad (Prayagraj) – Haldia | 1,620 km
  [5:3.1] NW-2 | Brahmaputra, Sadiya-Dhubri (Assam) | 891 km
  [5:3.2] NW-3 | West Coast Canal, Kollam-Kottapuram (Kerala) | 205 km
  [5:3.3] NW-4 | Kakinada-Puducherry canals (incl. Buckingham Canal) + Godavari, Krishna (AP, TN) | 1,095 km (old); 2,890 km after 2016
  [5:3.4] NW-5 | Talcher-Dhamra, Brahmani-Mahanadi delta + East Coast Canal (Odisha, WB) | 588 km
[5:4] - **Second longest NW (old figures): NW-4 / Buckingham Canal** after NW-1; NW-2 Sadiya-Dhubri is **not** the longest.
[5:5] - NW-1 terminals include **Haldia, Kolkata, Varanasi, Prayagraj**; NW-2 terminals include **Dhubri, Jogighopa, Tezpur, Dibrugarh, Sadiya**.
[5:6] - **Coastal shipping** grew **118%** in a decade.
