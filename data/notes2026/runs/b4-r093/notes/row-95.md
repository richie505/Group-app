=== Ports of AP – Framework, Districts and Models
- **Coastline 1,053.07 km** = **third-longest** (after Gujarat, Tamil Nadu; 2023-24 remeasurement); older **974 km = second** (APPSC 2022 key).
- **1 major port** (Visakhapatnam, GoI) + **16-17 notified non-major ports** under the **AP Maritime Board (APMB)**, 3 captive.
- Major ports = **Union List Entry 27**; non-major (Other Than Major Ports) = **Concurrent List Entry 31**.
| Port | District | Model / operator | Feature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visakhapatnam | Visakhapatnam | Major; Visakhapatnam Port Authority (GoI) | 141.60 MTPA; record 91.17 MMT (2025-26, +10.34%) |
| **Gangavaram** | Visakhapatnam | PPP-BOOT (2003); ops 17 Apr 2009 | **Greenfield deep-water port; deepest draft**; 64 MMT capacity; "largest private-sector port" (UPSC 2009 key – as built/at opening in 2009, by depth and design capacity; by cargo handled today Krishnapatnam is bigger) |
| **Krishnapatnam** | **SPSR Nellore** | PPP-BOST (2004); ops 20 Mar 2009 | Largest private port in AP **by cargo handled** (2025-26: 41.09 MMT to Nov 2025 vs Gangavaram 24.39) – not a conflict with the 2009 Gangavaram key |
| Kakinada Deep Water | Kakinada | PPP-OMST; **Kakinada Seaports Ltd** (from 1999) | Highest Govt revenue share, 22% of gross income; farm exports, chemicals, fertilisers |
| Kakinada Anchorage | Kakinada | State-run by APMB (not PPP DBFOT) | Lighterage; rice exports; ~4 MMT |
| Rawa (Ravva) | Kakinada region (S. Yanam) | Captive non-major; Cairn SBM | Offshore oil and gas |
| Kakinada Gateway | Kakinada | DBFOT; Kakinada SEZ-linked | 16 MTPA |
- **Kakinada** = AP's **port city**: Anchorage Port, Deep Water Port (State-built 1997), Fishing Harbour, Ship-Breaking Unit.
- **Krishnapatnam** (Nellore) benefits AP most; its rail outlet is the Obulavaripalle-Venkatachalam line.
- Non-major cargo 2025-26 (to Nov 2025): **80.82 MMT**; Krishnapatnam 41.09, Gangavaram 24.39, Kakinada DWP 12.93; ₹252.21 crore to the State.
- **PYQ 2024 key:** Krishnapatnam-**Nellore**, Gangavaram-**Visakhapatnam**, Ramayapatnam-**Prakasam**, **Bhavanapadu-Srikakulam**.
- AP has 3 national industrial corridors + NH-16: an **east-coast gateway to South-East Asia**. **Blue economy target: US$ 20 bn by 2035.**

=== Visakhapatnam Port
- Opened to ocean traffic in **October 1933** with the arrival of the passenger ship **SS Jaladurga**.
- **Deepest land-locked and protected port** (NextGen; APPSC 2025 key); natural + artificial harbour parts; called the **"Brightest Jewel"**.
- Protected by **Dolphin's Nose** (Yarada hills, ~335 m) from waves and cyclones.
- **Outer harbour** for **iron ore exports**; crude oil berth; all-weather, deep-water port.
- Ranked **third** in port traffic (FY 2022 key). Helped transport of agricultural products (APPSC 2022 key; the port itself is a 1933, 20th-century work).
- **Top seafood-export port** (2025-26): **5.12 lakh tonnes**, over **26%** of India's 19.32 lakh tonnes.
- First direct **South-East India – North Europe (IEX)** service, also linking the Mediterranean.
- Deepest port = Visakhapatnam; deepest draft = Gangavaram.

# Bhogapuram
- **Alluri Sitarama Raju International Airport**, Bhogapuram, **Vizianagaram**; ~50 km from Visakhapatnam.
- Developer **GMR (GVIAL)**, **PPP-DBFOT** (not an NIIF corpus); ~₹5,640 crore; **6 Mppa**, expandable to **40 Mppa**.
- **Inaugurated by PM 1 Aug 2026**; commercial operations **17 Aug 2026** (code VTZ); replaced the INS Dega civil enclave.
- **AP's first greenfield airport since 2014.** Nodal SPV: **APADCL** (formerly BIACL), **100% State-owned**.
- **Proposed:** **Dagadarthi** (Nellore; no-frills PPP, cargo hub), **Kuppam, Srikakulam**, Ongole, Nagarjuna Sagar, Tuni-Annavaram, Tadepalligudem, **Amaravati (4,500 acres)**. Gannavaram is the existing Vijayawada airport.
- **Seaplane** aerodromes planned at ten sites incl. **Prakasam Barrage**.
- **Cochin (1999)** = first airport built by a **public limited company** under PPP.
- **History:** Tata's Karachi-Madras air service linked to **Hyderabad (Hakimpet)** in **1935**.

=== Railways – South Coast Railway, Lines and Tunnels
- **First railway in Andhra: Puttur-Renigunta, 1862** (Madras Railway; a 1864 "Renigunta-Reddipalli" key also uses Renigunta). AP network **100% electrified**; works worth ₹92,649 crore under way (3 Feb 2026).
- **South Coast Railway:** mandated by the **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014**; announced 2019; carved from **SCR and ECoR**; **India's 18th zone**, **HQ Visakhapatnam**, operational **1 Jun 2026**.
| SCoR division | Came from |
|---|---|
| Visakhapatnam | Truncated Waltair division of ECoR |
| Vijayawada | SCR |
| Guntur | SCR |
| Guntakal | SCR |
- **SCR keeps:** **Hyderabad, Secunderabad (HQ), Nanded**. Rest of Waltair → ECoR's new **Rayagada** division.
| New line | Key facts |
|---|---|
| Errupalem-Nambur (Amaravati line) | 57 km, ₹2,245 crore; NTR and Guntur districts (AP) + Khammam (Telangana); 3-km Krishna bridge; 4-year timeline |
| Nadikudi-Srikalahasti | 308 km; four backward districts Guntur, Prakasam, Nellore, Chittoor; cement and granite traffic |
| Kotipalli-Narasapur | Konaseema coast; in progress (Feb 2026) |
| 3rd/4th lines Rajahmundry-Visakhapatnam | ₹9,889 crore |
- APPSC G2 2025: the "**6-year timeline**" for the Amaravati line was the **WRONG** statement.
- **Cherlopalli-Rapuru tunnel:** **6.6 km**, SPSR Nellore; **India's longest electrified rail tunnel when opened (2019)**, built by **South Central Railway**; on the **Obulavaripalle-Venkatachalam** line linking **Rayalaseema to Krishnapatnam port**.
- **Port rail links:** Ramayapatnam next to **NH-16 and Thettu** station; Kakinada Gateway has its own line; last-mile rail to **Kopparthy, Orvakal, Krishnapatnam** nodes incomplete.

=== Logistics, Sagarmala and Metro
- **Nexus Integrated Logistics Park:** Chinna Eetivakam (Satyavedu, Tirupati); 63.01 acres, ₹255 crore, ~1,000 jobs; built through **APMB = AP Maritime Board** (not Medical Board).
- **APLINC** (AP Logistics Infrastructure Corporation) = holding company. **AP Logistics Fund** = AIF, State sponsor up to 20%.
- Bhogapuram aerotropolis: 25,000 t cargo capacity.
- **Sagarmala pillars:** port modernisation, connectivity, port-led industrialisation, coastal community development, coastal shipping/IWT (**DFCs not a pillar**).
- AP asks: Sagarmala roads to Gangavaram + AM/NS captive port; Sagarmala 2.0 funds for fishing harbours (Juvvaladinne 97.98%).
- **Visakhapatnam Metro:** **4 corridors, 76.70 km**; metro-cum-flyover on NH-16. State says Vizag + Vijayawada metros approved (27 Jan 2026); Centre still checking.
- **Vizhinjam** (Kerala, May 2025) = India's first deep-water transshipment and first semi-automated container port.
- Challenges: logistics cost, no dedicated freight rail to clusters, water-short inland nodes, CBI-stalled ports, land delays.
