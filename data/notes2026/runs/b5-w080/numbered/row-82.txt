## SUB 0 | Green Economy, Green Technology and Green Skills
[0:0] # Green economy
[0:1] - **Green economy:** reduces environmental risks and ecological scarcities; **decouples growth from resource use and pollution**; **central theme of Rio+20 (2012)**.
[0:2] - **Green jobs:** work in agriculture, industry, services or administration that **preserves or restores environmental quality**.
[0:3] - **UNEP Green Economy Initiative:** **2008**. **PAGE** (Partnership for Action on Green Economy): **2013**, a response to the **Rio+20** call ("The Future We Want"); UNEP, ILO, UNIDO, UNDP, UNITAR.
[0:4] # Green technology
[0:5] - **Green technology** pollutes less, uses resources sustainably and recycles more of its waste.
[0:6] - **NOT correct:** "green technology is not expensive for developing countries" – **high cost is its main barrier**.
[0:7] - **CII–Sohrabji Godrej Green Business Centre:** India's first platinum-rated green building – **Hyderabad**.
[0:8] - **ISO 14001** = **Environmental Management System** (9001 quality, 22000 food safety, 27001 information security).
[0:9] # Green chemistry and cleaner production
[0:10] - **12 Principles of Green Chemistry:** **1998**, **Paul Anastas and John Warner**; core idea – **waste prevention rather than cleanup**; **Green Pharmacy** = green chemistry for medicines.
[0:11] - **Green chemistry + cleaner production** reduce waste and pollution across the **whole product life cycle** – hence they serve **EIA** and **Life-Cycle Assessment (LCA)**.
[0:12] # Green Skill Development Programme
[0:13] - Launched **2017** by **MoEFCC** on the **ENVIS** network; trains youth in **biodiversity conservation, pollution monitoring, waste management, forestry and wildlife** (not semiconductors).

## SUB 1 | Circular Economy and the 3 Rs
[1:0] - **Circular economy:** **reuse, recycling and repair** of materials to reduce waste; waste becomes a resource; replaces the linear **take–make–dispose** ("use and dispose") model.
[1:1] - **3 Rs (environment-friendly):** **Reduce, Reuse, Recycle**.
[1:2] TABLE head: Linear model | Circular model
  [1:2.0] Take, make, dispose | Reduce, reuse, repair, recycle; keep materials in use
  [1:2.1] Waste = end point | Waste = resource; growth decoupled from resource use
[1:3] - **Why it cuts GHG emissions (UPSC 2025):** it **reduces raw-material inputs** and **reduces production waste** – both true and both explain the emission cut.
[1:4] - **Not** exclusively about carbon or fossil fuels; **not** linear growth.
[1:5] - **India:** NITI Aayog + MoEFCC committees drew up action plans for priority waste streams – **plastics, e-waste, batteries, tyres, metals**.
[1:6] ~~ Sources: PMF Environment p.34 (circular economy of reduce, reuse, recycle); app notes 5:82 sub 1
## SUB 2 | European Green Deal
[2:0] - **December 2019**: make the EU **climate-neutral (net-zero GHG) by 2050**; at least **55% cut by 2030 vs 1990** – package **"Fit for 55"**.
[2:1] - **Poland** did not commit to the 2050 climate-neutrality target at the December 2019 summit.
[2:2] TABLE head: Component | Content
  [2:2.0] Farm to Fork | Sustainable food system
  [2:2.1] Circular Economy Action Plan | Resource efficiency, recycling
  [2:2.2] Biodiversity Strategy | Protect and restore ecosystems (so the Deal is NOT only about carbon)
  [2:2.3] Just Transition Fund | Support for affected regions
  [2:2.4] Renovation Wave | Energy-efficient buildings
[2:3] > Order: GEI 2008 → Rio+20 2012 → PAGE 2013 → GSDP 2017 → Green Deal 2019 → Green Credit 2023.

## SUB 3 | Greenwashing, Green Finance and ESG
[3:0] # Greenwashing
[3:1] - **Greenwashing** ("green sheen"): **false or exaggerated claims / promises** that a product, firm or country is environment-friendly.
[3:2] - A **clear taxonomy** helps regulators curb **greenwashing and "transition washing"**.
[3:3] # Green finance
[3:4] TABLE head: Instrument | Fact
  [3:4.0] Green bond | Pioneer EIB 2007, World Bank 2008
  [3:4.1] Sovereign Green Bonds (India) | Announced Budget 2022-23; first issue 2023, Rs 8,000 crore
  [3:4.2] Blue bond | Ocean and water projects; first sovereign – Seychelles 2018
  [3:4.3] India's first blue bond | Sagarmala Finance Corporation (planned)
  [3:4.4] RBI Green Deposit Framework | 2023 – deposits earmarked for green lending
  [3:4.5] Social bond / Sustainability bond (ICMA) | Positive social outcomes / a mix of green and social projects
[3:5] - **Climate finance taxonomy:** Budget 2024-25; **no final all-sector taxonomy** yet; **Ministry of Steel – Green Steel Taxonomy**; **MSE-GIFT** – green finance for MSMEs.
[3:6] # ESG and BRSR
[3:7] - **ESG** = **Environmental, Social, Governance**.
[3:8] - **BRSR** set by **SEBI**: **top 1,000 listed companies** report audited ESG metrics yearly; **SEBI regulates ESG Rating Providers**.

## SUB 4 | Resource Efficiency, EPR and Green Credit
[4:0] - **Draft National Resource Efficiency Policy, 2019** (**MoEFCC**): secondary raw materials; a **National Resource Efficiency Authority**.
[4:1] - **EPR regimes:** **plastic packaging, e-waste, batteries, tyres**.
[4:2] - **Green Credit Programme:** notified by **MoEFCC, October 2023**; **market-based incentive** for voluntary actions (tree plantation, water conservation); tightened August 2025.
[4:3] - **National Green Hydrogen Mission:** Rs **19,744 crore**; **5 MMT a year** by 2030; linked to **net zero 2070**.
[4:4] - **AMRUT 2.0:** treated used water for **20% of city** and **40% of industrial** demand.
[4:5] ~~ Sources: PMF Environment p.40, p.88-89 (Green Credit Programme); app notes 5:82 sub 8
