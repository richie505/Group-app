## SUB 1 | AI – Meaning, Pioneers and Types
[1:0] - **AI** = machines copying the thinking functions of human intelligence (learning, reasoning, perception) – "**computer-created intelligence**"; it augments humans by **making machines intelligent and mimicking human actions**.
[1:1] - **Father of AI:** **John McCarthy** – coined "artificial intelligence" for the **Dartmouth workshop (1956)**; not Turing, Babbage, Lee de Forest or Eckert.
[1:2] TABLE head: Item | Fact
  [1:2.0] Dartmouth workshop | 1956; proposed in 1955 by McCarthy, Minsky, Rochester and Shannon
  [1:2.1] Turing test | Alan Turing, "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" (1950); first called "the imitation game"
  [1:2.2] Narrow / Weak AI (ANI) | One specific task, cannot transfer across domains (Siri, Alexa, recommendations) – all AI in use today
  [1:2.3] General / Strong AI (AGI) | Human-level reasoning across domains – not achieved
  [1:2.4] Artificial Superintelligence | Hypothetical; surpasses humans in every way
[1:3] ~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 5 p.131-132 (types); PMF S&T Part 1 p.30 (Turing); app notes 5:16 sub 0
## SUB 2 | Machine Learning, Key Concepts and Hardware
[2:0] - **Machine learning** = a **branch / subset of AI**; learns from data without explicit programming (not network design, cyber forensics, full-stack).
[2:1] TABLE head: Type | Key point
  [2:1.0] Supervised | Trained on labelled data
  [2:1.1] Unsupervised | Unlabelled data; finds hidden patterns
  [2:1.2] Reinforcement | Trial and error with rewards
[2:2] - **RAS 2024 statements:** ML subset of AI ✓; supervised = "without labelled data" ✗; reinforcement = trial and error with rewards ✓ → **A and C**.
[2:3] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [2:3.0] Deep learning | Subset of ML using many-layered neural networks
  [2:3.1] Random forest | Group of decision trees; reduces overfitting
  [2:3.2] NLP | Machines understand and generate human language (chatbots, translation)
  [2:3.3] Computer vision | Interprets images – facial recognition
  [2:3.4] Generative AI | Creates new text, images, music
  [2:3.5] LLM | General-purpose model trained on massive data for many language tasks
  [2:3.6] Recommender system | Suggests content from past behaviour (Netflix)
  [2:3.7] GPU | Massively parallel – workhorse of AI training
  [2:3.8] FPGA | Field Programmable Gate Array – IC reconfigurable after manufacture
  [2:3.9] TPU | Google's AI chip
[2:4] - **Cloud-based machine learning tools** are bringing AI to a far broader audience.
[2:5] - **Data scientists** need **all** of: statistics, machine learning, data visualisation.

[6:1] TABLE head: Concern | Point
  [6:1.0] Bias | Sources: data, algorithms, people (human interpretation) – "ability" is not a source
  [6:1.1] Health-AI bias | Trained on urban/Western data; may miss India's social, genetic, demographic diversity
