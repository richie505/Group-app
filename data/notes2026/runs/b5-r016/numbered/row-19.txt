## SUB 0 | Cryptocurrency and Bitcoin
[0:0] - **Cryptocurrency:** money transfers secured with **encryption (cryptography)**, peer-to-peer, **without a central bank** (not legal tender, RTGS, NEFT).
[0:1] - **Bitcoin** (**2009**): first cryptocurrency, by pseudonymous **Satoshi Nakamoto**; decentralised; coins created by **mining** (complex software / computation).
[0:2] - **UPSC 2016:** Bitcoins are **not tracked by central banks** ✗; anyone with an address can send/receive ✓; payments possible **without either side knowing the other's identity** ✓ → **2 and 3 only**.
[0:3] - **CDS 2017:** decentralised ✓, generated by complex software ✓, **RBI never recognised it as legal tender** ✗ → **I and II**.
[0:4] - **Japan:** recognised Bitcoin as a **legal payment method from 1 April 2017**.
[0:5] - **BuyUcoin:** first Indian platform for **wholesale crypto trading** (April 2019).
[0:6] - **Not a cryptocurrency:** the **Yen** (Japan's fiat currency).
[0:7] - India: crypto **not legal tender**; VDA providers under **PMLA (March 2023)**; **30%** tax on VDA gains (**Sec 115BBH**, Finance Act **2022**) + **1% TDS** (**Sec 194S**); losses cannot be set off.

## SUB 1 | Blockchain and Distributed Ledger
[1:0] - **Blockchain:** a **time-stamped series of immutable records** managed by a **cluster of computers not owned by any single entity**; first introduced in **Bitcoin** (Satoshi Nakamoto).
[1:1] - **UPSC 2020:** public ledger everyone can inspect, no single user controls ✓; data only about cryptocurrency ✗; apps can be built **without anybody's permission** ✓ → **1 and 3 only**.
[1:2] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [1:2.0] Distributed ledger | Shared ledger copied across nodes; single source of truth; no central validator
  [1:2.1] Block | Transactions + timestamp + hash of the previous block (not private keys)
  [1:2.2] Hash / node | Cryptographic fingerprint of a block / a computer holding a ledger copy
  [1:2.3] Immutability | Cannot be altered without consensus – no central authority changes data
  [1:2.4] Consensus | Rule by which nodes agree on valid transactions
[1:3] - Stakeholders **hold copies** and changes need **consensus** – so records are visible but not alterable.
[1:4] - **Uses:** land records, certificates, supply chain, **e-voting**, health records, e-Notary, farm insurance.
[1:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen S&T G2 Part 1 p.26; PMF S&T Part 7 p.107, p.111-112; app notes 5:19 sub 0
## SUB 2 | CBDC – the e-Rupee
[2:0] - **Digital rupee (e₹)** = India's **CBDC**, **sovereign currency issued by the RBI**, legal tender; a **direct liability on the RBI's balance sheet**; **freely convertible against commercial bank money and cash**; **not "insured against inflation"** → UPSC 2024: **1, 2 and 4**.
[2:1] TABLE head: Pilot | Start | Use
  [2:1.0] Wholesale (e₹-W) | 1 Nov 2022 | Settlement of secondary-market government securities
  [2:1.1] Retail (e₹-R) | 1 Dec 2022 | Closed user group – Mumbai, New Delhi, Bengaluru, Bhubaneswar (not Hyderabad)
[2:2] - RBI **concept note on CBDC: October 2022**. e-rupee is RBI money; UPI only moves bank-account money.
[2:3] ~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 5 p.105-106; app notes 5:19 sub 5; general knowledge, not checked (convertibility, RBI concept note)
