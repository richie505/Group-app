=== AI – Meaning, Pioneers and Types
- **AI** = machines copying the thinking functions of human intelligence (learning, reasoning, perception) – "**computer-created intelligence**"; it augments humans by **making machines intelligent and mimicking human actions**.
- **Father of AI:** **John McCarthy** – coined "artificial intelligence" for the **Dartmouth workshop (1956)**; not Turing, Babbage, Lee de Forest or Eckert.
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Dartmouth workshop | **1956**; proposed in 1955 by McCarthy, Minsky, Rochester and Shannon |
| Turing test | Alan Turing, "**Computing Machinery and Intelligence**" (**1950**); first called "**the imitation game**" |
| Narrow / Weak AI (ANI) | One specific task, cannot transfer across domains (**Siri, Alexa**, recommendations) – all AI in use today |
| General / Strong AI (AGI) | Human-level reasoning across domains – not achieved |
| Artificial Superintelligence | Hypothetical; surpasses humans in every way |

=== Machine Learning, Key Concepts and Hardware
- **Machine learning** = a **branch / subset of AI**; learns from data without explicit programming (not network design, cyber forensics, full-stack).
| Type | Key point |
|---|---|
| Supervised | Trained on **labelled** data |
| Unsupervised | Unlabelled data; finds hidden patterns |
| Reinforcement | **Trial and error** with **rewards** |
- **RAS 2024 statements:** ML subset of AI ✓; supervised = "without labelled data" ✗; reinforcement = trial and error with rewards ✓ → **A and C**.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Deep learning | Subset of ML using many-layered **neural networks** |
| Random forest | Group of **decision trees**; reduces overfitting |
| NLP | Machines understand and generate human language (chatbots, translation) |
| Computer vision | Interprets images – **facial recognition** |
| Generative AI | Creates new text, images, music |
| LLM | General-purpose model trained on massive data for many language tasks |
| Recommender system | Suggests content from past behaviour (Netflix) |
| **GPU** | Massively parallel – workhorse of AI training |
| **FPGA** | **Field Programmable Gate Array** – IC reconfigurable after manufacture |
| **TPU** | **Google**'s AI chip |
- **Cloud-based machine learning tools** are bringing AI to a far broader audience.
- **Data scientists** need **all** of: statistics, machine learning, data visualisation.

=== Ethical Concerns and Challenges
- **Hawking (2014):** "The development of full AI could spell the **end of the human race**" – **Stephen Hawking**.
| Concern | Point |
|---|---|
| **Bias** | Sources: **data**, **algorithms**, **people** (human interpretation) – "**ability**" is not a source |
| Health-AI bias | Trained on urban/Western data; may miss India's **social, genetic, demographic diversity** |
| Deepfakes | Made with **GANs** and diffusion models |
| Facial recognition | Identification without consent, mass surveillance, predictive policing |
| Job displacement | **Both manual and intellectual** work |
| Explainability | Deep models are **black boxes** – decisions hard to explain |
- India: GERD **~0.64–0.70% of GDP**; **Economic Survey 2025-26** – small, application-specific models + a state-built **AI-OS** modelled on UPI and Aadhaar.
- **14 Aug 2026:** Supreme Court sought the Centre's reply on no legal framework for high-risk AI in **welfare delivery**.
