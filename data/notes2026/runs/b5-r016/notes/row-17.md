=== Quantum Computing and Supercomputers
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Qubit** | Basic unit of quantum information – context of **quantum computing** (UPSC 2022) |
| **Superposition** | A quantum system can be in **multiple states at once until measured** (qubit = 0 and 1) |
| Entanglement | Links qubits' states regardless of distance |
| **Decoherence** | Loss of quantum state to the environment – main barrier |
| Shor's algorithm / Q-Day | Can break public-key cryptography; Q-Day = when it becomes able to |
- **Applications:** **cryptography**, **drug discovery**, **financial modelling**, logistics, materials – **not desktop publishing**.
| Machine | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Micius** (QUESS), China, **16 Aug 2016** | World's **first quantum communication satellite** |
| Google **Sycamore** (2019) | Quantum supremacy – a set task in ~200 s |
| Jiuzhang (China, 2020) | Photonic supremacy machine |
| **Fugaku** (Japan) | World's fastest supercomputer 2020-21 (exam key for "least time to calculate") |
| **Pratyush** (IITM Pune, 2018) | India's fastest supercomputer as of **March 2018** |
- **National Quantum Mission:** approved **19 Apr 2023**; **₹6,003.65 crore** (2023-24 to 2030-31); **50–1,000 physical qubits** in 8 years; **four thematic hubs** – computing, communication, sensing & metrology, materials & devices (not quantum biology).
- **2025** = UN **International Year of Quantum Science and Technology** (centenary of quantum mechanics); **World Quantum Day = 14 April**.
- 2026 items: Raman Research Institute – a timed local "quantum flip" delays **entanglement sudden death**; **Microsoft Majorana 2** – qubit stability 20–60 s.

=== Turing Test and AI Milestones
- **Turing test:** Alan Turing (1950, *Computing Machinery and Intelligence*) – checks whether a machine's replies can be told apart from a human's; original name **"the Imitation Game"** (not LISP or the Halting Problem).
- **Term "AI":** **John McCarthy**, Dartmouth **1956**. **Colossus** (1943–45): Tommy Flowers.
- **Robotics** = technology most closely associated with "artificial intelligence" (exam key).
| Emerging technology | Person |
|---|---|
| Artificial intelligence | **Alan Turing** |
| OpenAI | **Sam Altman** |
| Internet of Things | **Kevin Ashton** |
| Augmented reality | **Thomas P. Caudell** |

=== Advanced Materials and Nanotechnology

=== 3D Printing and Bio-printing
- **Additive manufacturing:** object built **layer by layer** from a **CAD** model; **4D printing** – printed object **changes shape over time**.
- **3D-printed cornea:** **LV Prasad Eye Institute + IIT Hyderabad + CCMB** (Hyderabad).
- **3D-printed human liver tissue:** **Pandorum Technologies**, Bengaluru – so "cornea and liver tissue respectively" = **LVPEI and Pandorum**.
- India's first 3D-printed post office: **Bengaluru (2023)**.
