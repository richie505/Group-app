## SUB 0 | The English in Andhra – Globe, Machilipatnam and Armagaon
[0:0] - **1611**: the ship **Globe** under **Captain (Anthony) Hippon** – not Hawkins – reached **Machilipatnam** (January 1611), with **Peter Floris** (a Dutchman in English service) as merchant.
[0:1] - Permission from the Golconda Sultan **Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah** → **first English factory in Andhra / South India / on the east coast = Machilipatnam (1611)** – not Kakinada, Visakhapatnam, Bheemunipatnam or Golconda.
[0:2] - A-R (APPSC G2 2025): "Globe under Hawkins" is **false**; "Quli Qutb Shah permitted the factory" is **true** → (A) false, (R) true.
[0:3] TABLE head: Year | English settlement | Note
  [0:3.0] 1611 | Machilipatnam | 1st
  [0:3.1] 1621 | Pulicat (Dutch settlement) | Abandoned – Dutch hostility
  [0:3.2] 1626 | Armagaon (Dugarajapatnam), Nellore region, now Tirupati district | Named after Karanam Armugam Mudali; 35 miles north of Pulicat; second English factory in the Andhra region
  [0:3.3] 1632 | Golden Farman → back to Machilipatnam | 
  [0:3.4] 1639 | Madras | Francis Day
  [0:3.5] 1682 | Visakhapatnam | 
[0:4] - English factory in **Nellore district** = **1626** (Armagaon); other English posts: Nizampatnam, Narsapur, **Madapollam**, Bheemunipatnam.
[0:5] - **Golden Farman** = **1632** (Spectrum, PMF; NextGen Qutb Shahi notes give 1636) from the Sultan of Golconda **Abdullah Qutb Shah**: free trade in **Golconda ports** for **500 pagodas a year** – not from the Mughal emperor.
[0:6] - English used **Dadni** advances through **dubashes**; they **paid weavers less** and demanded high quality, so weavers preferred the Dutch, Portuguese and French.

## SUB 1 | First Europeans in Andhra – Order of the Factories
[1:0] - **First Europeans to trade with India = Portuguese (1498)**; order of arrival: **Portuguese → Dutch → English → French**.
[1:1] - **First trade contacts with the Andhra region = Portuguese** (16th century, from San Thome and Pulicat) – but they opened a factory at Machilipatnam only in **1670**.
[1:2] - **First European factory in Andhra = Dutch, Machilipatnam, 1605** (NextGen; Spectrum) – "first factory / trade centre at Machilipatnam" = **Dutch**, not Portuguese.
[1:3] TABLE head: Order | Power | Machilipatnam factory | Permission
  [1:3.0] 1st | Dutch | 1605 | Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah
  [1:3.1] 2nd | English | 1611 | Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah
  [1:3.2] 3rd | French | 1669 | Abdullah Qutb Shah
  [1:3.3] 4th | Portuguese | 1670 | Abdullah Qutb Shah
[1:4] - Why the Europeans came: coastal Andhra's position on the **Bay of Bengal** for sea trade, its **chintz** and **diamonds**; Hyderabad founded **1591** by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah.
[1:5] - **Tavernier** (17th-century French jeweller) visited the Andhra (Golconda) mines for diamonds; **Marco Polo** visited **Motupalli**.
[1:6] - **Portuguese** contributions: crops such as **tobacco**, chilli, cashew (to check soil erosion in Goa); Arabian Sea monopoly until the **17th century**; **Blue Water Policy** of **Almeida**.

## SUB 3 | The French in Andhra – Machilipatnam to the Northern Circars
[3:0] - **French factory in Andhra = Machilipatnam, 1669** (by **Marcara**, permission of **Abdullah Qutb Shah**) – not Motupalli, Narsapur, Visakhapatnam, Yanam or Kakinada.
[3:1] - **Yanam** (1723) = the **only French territorial possession / settlement in Andhra**; not Rajahmundry, Hamsaladivi or Ongole.
[3:2] - **1753**: **Bussy** got the **Northern Circars** – Mustafanagar (**Kondapalli**), Eluru, Rajahmundry, Chicacole – from **Salabat Jang** to pay for French troops (Nellore was not among them).
[3:3] TABLE head: Battle | Year | Fact
  [3:3.0] Tumnapalem | 1756 | Bussy + Vijayaramaraju vs Jafar Ali
  [3:3.1] Bobbili | 24 Jan 1757 | Vijayaramaraju of Vizianagaram, with Bussy's French, destroyed Bobbili; Ranga Rao killed; queen Mallamma; Tandra Papayya then killed Vijayaramaraju
  [3:3.2] Chandurthi (Condore) | Dec 1758 | Col. Forde (sent from Bengal), aided by Ananda Gajapati of Vizianagaram, defeated the French under Conflans
  [3:3.3] Fall of Masulipatnam | 1759 | Forde took Machilipatnam; Salabat Jang gave the English Machilipatnam, Nizampatnam, Vakal Mannar, part of Kondapalli
  [3:3.4] Wandiwash | 1760 | Eyre Coote beat Count de Lally
[3:4] - **Chandurthi** lies in the old **East Godavari** district (now the **Kakinada** region) – not Vizianagaram, Nellore, Chittoor or Kurnool.
[3:5] - Chandurthi led to the **expulsion of the French** from the Northern Sarkars; Circars to the English 1765-68, **Guntur 1788**.
[3:6] - The **European** traders entered Indian politics when the Nizams of Hyderabad sought their help against rivals.
