## SUB 0 | First Carnatic War (1746-48) – Causes and Course
[0:0] - Carnatic Wars were fought between the **English and the French** (not English–Dutch, English–Marathas or Haider Ali–Marathas).
[0:1] - Background: an **extension of the War of Austrian Succession** in Europe and Anglo-French rivalry.
[0:2] - **Immediate cause**: the **English navy under Barnet seized French ships** → France retaliated (the Austrian war and the Carnatic throne were background, not the immediate cause).
[0:3] - **Sept 1746**: **La Bourdonnais** with the fleet from **Mauritius** captured **Madras**; **Dupleix** refused to ratify his ransom deal.
[0:4] - **Anwaruddin** (guardian of the minor Nawab sent by Nizam-ul-Mulk; Nawab of Arcot from 1744) took the English side – later killed at **Ambur (1749)**.
[0:5] - The **European** traders got their chance to interfere in Indian politics when Indian rulers such as the Nizams of Hyderabad enlisted their help against rivals.
[0:6] ~~ Sources: Spectrum Modern India p.76-77 (book p.44-45); NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 5 p.34; old app notes row 159
## SUB 1 | Battle of St. Thome and the Treaty of Aix-la-Chapelle
[1:0] - **Battle of St. Thome (Adyar), 1746**: a small French force (about **930**) under **Captain Paradis** defeated about **10,000** troops of Nawab Anwaruddin under **Mahfuz Khan**.
[1:1] - Lesson: a small disciplined European army could beat a large Indian army; **naval power** would decide the contest.
[1:2] - **Treaty of Aix-la-Chapelle (1748)** ended the War of Austrian Succession **and** the First Carnatic War; **Madras returned to the English** in exchange for **Louisbourg** (North America) → both statements correct (UPPCS 2025).
[1:3] - Political result **nil** – pre-war position restored.
