=== NOTES 5:114 | Health and Environment – Vector-borne and Water-borne Diseases, Air Pollution, Amr, One Health
## SUB 0 | Vector-borne Diseases
[114:0:0] - Vector-borne = spread by a **vector** (mosquito, flea, sandfly, mite).
[114:0:1] - **Only female mosquitoes suck blood** (bigger wings); males feed on plant juices. **Culex, Anopheles, Aedes** are found worldwide.
[114:0:2] # Vector → Disease
[114:0:3] TABLE head: Vector | Disease (pathogen)
  [114:0:3.0] Female Anopheles | Malaria (*Plasmodium*, protozoan)
  [114:0:3.1] Aedes aegypti | Dengue, chikungunya, Zika, yellow fever
  [114:0:3.2] Aedes albopictus (tiger / forest mosquito) | Dengue, chikungunya, yellow fever – NOT JE
  [114:0:3.3] Culex (*C. quinquefasciatus*) | Filariasis, Japanese encephalitis
  [114:0:3.4] Haemagogus | Forest yellow fever
  [114:0:3.5] Sandfly (*Phlebotomus argentipes*) | Kala-azar (*Leishmania donovani*)
  [114:0:3.6] Tsetse fly (*Glossina*) | Sleeping sickness (*Trypanosoma brucei*)
  [114:0:3.7] Chigger mite | Scrub typhus (*Orientia tsutsugamushi*)
  [114:0:3.8] Rat flea | Plague (*Yersinia pestis*)
  [114:0:3.9] Ticks (monkeys) | Kyasanur Forest Disease
  [114:0:3.10] Cyclops (water flea) | Carries Guinea worm larvae
  [114:0:3.11] Snail (intermediate host) | Schistosomiasis
  [114:0:3.12] Leafhopper / whitefly | Rice tungro / okra yellow vein mosaic
[114:0:4] Not spread by mosquitoes: kala-azar, sleeping sickness, scrub typhus, plague (APPSC G2 2025). Mosquito-borne: Zika, West Nile, chikungunya, dengue, malaria, JE, filariasis.
[114:0:5] # Disease Details
[114:0:6] TABLE head: Disease | Pathogen | Affects
  [114:0:6.0] Malaria | Protozoan *Plasmodium* | Liver first, then RBCs; enlarged spleen
  [114:0:6.1] Dengue ("breakbone fever") | Flavivirus | Platelets fall → internal bleeding (APPSC 2023)
  [114:0:6.2] Chikungunya | Alphavirus (viral, not bacterial) | Severe joint pain
  [114:0:6.3] Zika | Zika virus; also blood, sexual spread | Fever, rash, joint pain
  [114:0:6.4] JE | JEV (flavivirus) | Brain; pigs amplify, pigs + wild birds = reservoirs
  [114:0:6.5] Kala-azar | *Leishmania* | Liver, spleen, bone marrow
  [114:0:6.6] Sleeping sickness | *T. b. rhodesiense, T. b. gambiense* | Brain (late stage); tropical Africa
  [114:0:6.7] Filariasis | Nematode *Wuchereria bancrofti* | Lymph vessels → elephantiasis; fever, swelling of lower limbs
[114:0:7] - **Pigs away** from settlements curbs JE. Malaria vs dengue: female **Anopheles** vs female **Aedes aegypti**.
[114:0:8] # Plasmodium
[114:0:9] - Five human species: ***P. falciparum, vivax, malariae, ovale, knowlesi***.
[114:0:10] - ***P. falciparum*** = **most lethal, malignant/cerebral malaria**, no relapse.
[114:0:11] - ***P. vivax*** = **~65%** of India's cases; **relapsing** (liver **hypnozoites**).
[114:0:12] - Vaccine hard: species have **different antigens**.
[114:0:13] - **Arteether** from ***Artemisia annua***.

  [114:1:0.0] Amoebiasis | *Entamoeba histolytica* | Large intestine
  [114:1:0.1] Ascariasis | Nematode *Ascaris lumbricoides* | Swallowing eggs; small intestine

  [114:1:0.3] Taeniasis | *Taenia solium* (pork), *T. saginata* (beef) | Eggs → neurocysticercosis (brain cysts)
  [114:1:0.4] Guinea worm | Nematode *Dracunculus medinensis* (not annelid) | Drinking water with Cyclops
  [114:1:0.5] Schistosomiasis | Flatworm *Schistosoma* | Urinary tract, intestine; unsafe water
  [114:1:0.6] Athlete's foot | *Trichophyton, Epidermophyton, Microsporum* | Skin between toes; floors, towels
  [114:1:0.7] Mucormycosis | Mucormycetes | Nose, sinuses, eyes, brain; not zoonotic

[114:1:1] - **Tapeworm = hermaphrodite**; roundworm = separate sexes.
[114:1:2] - Ringworm – dermatophytes (**griseofulvin**); candidiasis – *Candida*; aspergillosis – *Aspergillus*; **Amanita** – alpha-amanitin.
[114:1:3] - **Epidemic dropsy**: mustard oil adulterated with ***Argemone mexicana*** (sanguinarine).
[114:1:4] - Fungal (per keys): baldness, seborrheic dermatitis; **colour blindness = genetic**.
[114:1:5] - Plant: *Puccinia* – rust of wheat; *Ustilago* – smut.

[114:2:6] - Remedies: **Jal Jeevan Mission, Swachh Bharat, AMRUT**.

## SUB 3 | Bacterial and Viral Diseases – Quick Match
[114:3:0] TABLE head: Bacterial | Fact
  [114:3:0.0] TB | *M. tuberculosis*; lungs; kills 3-5 lakh Indians/yr
  [114:3:0.1] Leprosy (Hansen's) | *M. leprae / M. lepromatosis*; skin, peripheral nerves
  [114:3:0.2] Tetanus ("lock jaw") | *C. tetani*; via wound
  [114:3:0.3] Diphtheria | *Corynebacterium*; false membrane in throat
  [114:3:0.4] Pertussis | Whooping cough
  [114:3:0.5] Gonorrhoea / Syphilis | *Neisseria* / *Treponema*; sexual (not droplets)
  [114:3:0.6] Botulism | *C. botulinum* toxin; nerves, paralysis
  [114:3:0.7] Trachoma | *Chlamydia*; eyelid scarring, blindness
  [114:3:0.8] Atypical pneumonia | *Mycoplasma pneumoniae*
  [114:3:0.9] Brucellosis, plague | Bacteria
[114:3:1] - **Triple antigen (DPT)**: diphtheria, whooping cough, tetanus.
[114:3:2] - **P-amino salicylic acid** treats **both TB and leprosy**.
[114:3:3] - **Smallpox** = only disease eradicated in India (**1977**).
[114:3:4] - **Rheumatic fever** follows *Streptococcus pyogenes* infection (not counted bacterial).
[114:3:5] TABLE head: Viral | Fact
  [114:3:5.0] AIDS | HIV, retrovirus; kills helper T cells; blood, sex, placenta – not mosquitoes; swollen nodes, night sweats, memory loss, weight loss
  [114:3:5.1] Measles | Viral, airborne; antibiotics cannot cure
  [114:3:5.2] Mumps | Parotid glands; saliva
  [114:3:5.3] COVID-19 | SARS-CoV-2 (RNA coronavirus); lungs
  [114:3:5.4] Common cold | Rhinovirus
  [114:3:5.5] Viral set | Mumps, rabies, herpes, chickenpox, polio
[114:3:6] - **Measles in infants:** maternal antibodies (natural **passive** immunity) protect under ~6 months; MR vaccine at 9-12 and 16-24 months.
[114:3:7] - **Meningitis**: meninges; *Haemophilus influenzae* in young children.
[114:3:8] - **Pneumonia**: lung air sacs; **emphysema** (COPD) pollution-linked; **pneumoconiosis**: coal miners.
[114:3:9] - No vaccine controls **diabetes**.
[114:3:10] - **Diphtheria and pertussis (whooping cough):** spread from person to person through the respiratory route (droplets from coughing and sneezing).

## SUB 5 | Vector Control and Elimination
[114:5:0] - **NVBDCP (now NCVBDC)**: six diseases – **malaria, JE, dengue, chikungunya, kala-azar, LF** (**not TB**).
[114:5:1] - **Elimination**: malaria, LF, kala-azar; dengue, chikungunya, JE only controlled.
[114:5:2] - **National Framework for Malaria Elimination (2016-2030)**: **malaria-free India by 2030**.
[114:5:3] - **MERA India** (Malaria Elimination Research Alliance): **ICMR**, **24 April 2019**; steered by **ICMR-NIMR, New Delhi**.
[114:5:4] - WHO malaria-free in South Asia: **Maldives (2015), Sri Lanka (2016)**.
[114:5:5] TABLE head: Bio-control | Mechanism
  [114:5:5.0] *Gambusia affinis* (mosquito fish), guppy | Larvivorous fish
  [114:5:5.1] Bti | Bacterial bio-larvicide
  [114:5:5.2] Wolbachia | Bacterium; cytoplasmic incompatibility + pathogen blocking; no GM, no pesticide
[114:5:6] - Wolbachia: in about **50%** of insect species; **naturally absent in Aedes aegypti and Anopheles**; blocks dengue, chikungunya, Zika.
[114:5:7] - Verily **'Debug'** (2 Jun 2026): Wolbachia male *Culex quinquefasciatus* released.

[114:6:3] - **DALY = YLL + YLD**.

## SUB 8 | Antibiotics and Antimicrobial Resistance
[114:8:0] # Antibiotics
[114:8:1] - **Fleming**: **penicillin, 1928** (*Penicillium notatum*); **Waksman** coined "**antibiotic**" (**1942**), found **streptomycin** (first anti-TB drug).
[114:8:2] - Mostly from **bacteria (Streptomyces)**; mostly natural or semi-synthetic; **sulfa drugs synthetic**.
[114:8:3] - **Chloramphenicol** = broad-spectrum (typhoid); Penicillin G narrow; Cibazole = short-acting sulpha.
[114:8:4] - **Prontosil** (Domagk, 1935): a **pro-drug** converted in the body to **sulfanilamide**; began the sulpha-drug era.
[114:8:5] - Analgesics – pain; antiseptics – stop microbes; aspirin, paracetamol – analgesic, antipyretic; chloroquine – antimalarial.
[114:8:6] - **Acetobacter** – vinegar; **Bacillus** – several antibiotics.
[114:8:7] - **Monoclonal antibodies**: man-made proteins binding specific antigens.
[114:8:8] # How AMR Builds
[114:8:9] - Causes: **incorrect dose**, **incomplete course**, **overuse**, **self-medication**, **livestock use**, unregulated veterinary drugs, **horizontal gene transfer**, mutation, poor sanitation, pharma effluents.
[114:8:10] - **DBT Scoping Report (Nov 2017)**: MDR from **incorrect doses**, **livestock use** – **not genetics**.
[114:8:11] - Severity: **MDR → XDR → TDR**.
[114:8:12] - **Biofilm**: microbes in a self-made **EPS matrix** on **implants, catheters, tissues, food surfaces**; blocks antibiotics → **chronic infection**, food spoilage.

[114:8:16] - **Incomplete course:** stopping antibiotics early kills only the weaker microbes; the resistant survivors are left to multiply.
