=== Mosquito-borne Diseases – Vectors and Pathogens
# Vector basics
- **Vector:** an organism (mosquito, flea, sandfly, tick, mite) that carries a pathogen between hosts – insects that transmit disease are called **vectors**.
- **Only female mosquitoes suck blood** (they need it for eggs) and have **bigger wings** than males; **males feed on plant juices/nectar**.
- **Culex, Anopheles and Aedes** – all three genera are blood-feeders found throughout the world.
# Mosquito → disease
| Mosquito | Disease (pathogen type) |
|---|---|
| **Female Anopheles** | **Malaria** (*Plasmodium*, protozoan) |
| **Aedes aegypti** | **Dengue, chikungunya, Zika, yellow fever** (all viral) |
| **Aedes albopictus** (Asian tiger / forest mosquito) | Dengue, chikungunya, Zika, yellow fever – **NOT Japanese encephalitis** (most books) |
| **Culex** (*C. quinquefasciatus*, *C. pipiens*) | **Lymphatic filariasis** (nematode), **Japanese encephalitis** (virus) |
| Haemagogus | Forest (jungle) yellow fever |
- **NOT mosquito-borne:** kala-azar (sandfly), sleeping sickness (tsetse fly), Chagas (kissing bug), plague (rat flea), scrub typhus (chigger mite), KFD (ticks).
- Wrong sets often asked: "Aedes – chikungunya – **bacteria**" is wrong (chikungunya is an **alphavirus**); "Tsetse – filariasis" and "bed bug – sleeping sickness" are wrong.
# Disease details
| Disease | Pathogen | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Dengue** ("breakbone fever") | Dengue virus (flavivirus), 4 serotypes | **Platelets fall sharply** → internal bleeding; virus suppresses platelet production in **bone marrow**, infects **endothelial cells** and **binds platelets** (CDS 2016: I, II, III) |
| **Chikungunya** | CHIKV, alphavirus | Severe joint pain |
| **Zika** | Zika virus (flavivirus) | Same mosquito as dengue (Aedes aegypti); also **sexual and blood** transmission; microcephaly risk |
| **Yellow fever** | Yellow fever virus | Spread by **mosquito** – not air, water or housefly |
| **Japanese encephalitis** | JEV (flavivirus), a **virus** | Infects the **brain**; **pigs amplify**, pigs + wild birds = reservoirs → keeping **pigs away** from settlements curbs JE; not water-borne |
| **Lymphatic filariasis** | Nematode ***Wuchereria bancrofti*** (thread-like roundworm) | **Lymph vessels/nodes** → fever early, then chronic swelling of **lower limbs** = **elephantiasis**; *Culex* is the vector, not the cause |
- Malaria vs dengue: common = fever, mosquito bite, human host; **different = mosquito species** (Anopheles vs Aedes) and pathogen (protozoan vs virus).
- **Dengue vaccine:** **Qdenga (TAK-003, Takeda)** – live-attenuated tetravalent, age 4–60; CDSCO approval **July 2026** = India's first approved dengue vaccine.
> Exam angle: vector of dengue/Zika (×8), what falls in dengue (platelets ×5), filariasis/elephantiasis organism and organ (×7), JE cause/organ/pigs (×4), "not mosquito-borne" (×5), only females suck blood (×2).

=== Malaria and Plasmodium
- **Pathogen:** protozoan ***Plasmodium***; **carrier/vector = female Anopheles mosquito** (not tick, fly or louse).
- Parasites multiply first in the **liver**, then destroy **red blood cells**; the **spleen** enlarges (organ affected by malaria).
| Species | Fact |
|---|---|
| ***P. falciparum*** | **Malignant (cerebral) malaria** – most severe |
| ***P. vivax*** | **Relapsing** malaria (dormant liver **hypnozoites**); old books: **~65%** of India's cases (now ~40%; falciparum ~60% in 2024) |
| *P. malariae*, *P. ovale* | Milder |
| *P. knowlesi* | Zoonotic (macaques) |
- **Five** Plasmodium species infect humans. Vaccine is hard because malaria is caused by **several species** (with changing antigens) – UPSC 2010.
- **Drugs:** **chloroquine** (prevention and treatment; *P. vivax*), quinine (from cinchona bark), **artemisinin/arteether** from the seeded plant ***Artemisia annua***; ACTs first-line for falciparum.
- **Vaccines:** RTS,S/AS01 (Mosquirix) and R21/Matrix-M (WHO-recommended); India's **AdFalciVax** (ICMR-RMRC Bhubaneswar) at preclinical stage (2026).
> Exam angle: carrier of Plasmodium (×3), malignant malaria (falciparum), 65% share (vivax), organ (spleen), drug (chloroquine, arteether), why vaccine is hard.

=== Water-borne and Faeco-oral Diseases
- **Faeco-oral (contaminated water/food) group:** **cholera, typhoid, hepatitis A and E, acute diarrhoea, dysentery, polio**; also amoebiasis, ascariasis.
| Disease | Pathogen | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Typhoid** (enteric **fever**) | *Salmonella Typhi* (bacterium) | **Widal test**; intestine |
| **Cholera** | *Vibrio cholerae* (bacterium) | Contaminated food/water → severe diarrhoea, dehydration; ORS |
| **Dysentery** | Bacillary = *Shigella* (**bacterial**); amoebic = *Entamoeba* (protozoan) | Blood in stool |
| **Hepatitis A, E** | Viruses | Inflammation of the **liver** → **jaundice**; spread by polluted **water** |
| **Polio** | Poliovirus | Enters through **polluted food and water**; spinal cord |
| Acute diarrhoea | Rotavirus, *E. coli*, *Shigella* | Leading child killer; **acute diarrhoeal diseases caused the most water-borne deaths in India** (2012-2017) |
| Food poisoning | ***Salmonella*** (not Anopheles, Culex, Aedes) | Contaminated food |
- **Unsafe water + poor sanitation** (developing countries): **malaria, acute diarrhoea, schistosomiasis**; ~**5,000 children die daily** worldwide. **Kwashiorkor** (protein deficiency) is NOT water-borne.
- **Floods** spread cholera, typhoid, hepatitis, **leptospirosis**.
- **Shallow hand-pump** water → cholera, typhoid, jaundice – **NOT fluorosis** (fluoride, deep groundwater).
- **GI diseases:** symptoms are diarrhoea, vomiting, abdominal pain – **kidney failure is not** a GI symptom. **Inflammatory bowel disease** = gastrointestinal tract.
# Look-alikes that are NOT water-borne
| Item | Route / fact |
|---|---|

| **Jaundice** | A **symptom** of liver disease (bilirubin), not a disease of one microbe – **not so contagious** |
| TB, pneumonia | Airborne bacterial |

=== Infection, Immunity and Vaccination
- **Disease vs symptom:** **cholera** is a disease; fever, sneezing, headache are symptoms. Typhoid is a type of **fever** (enteric fever).
- **Contagious:** **yawning** is the classic contagious reflex (not snoring, gnashing, vomiting). Infectious diseases (AIDS, chickenpox, mumps, herpes) pass from person to person; AIDS is **not spread by casual contact**.
- **Measles:** infants **below six months** are protected by **maternal antibodies** (natural passive immunity); MR vaccine at 9-12 and 16-24 months.
- **No vaccine** controls **diabetes**; polio, whooping cough, smallpox are vaccine-preventable. **Rickets** (vitamin D deficiency) cannot be prevented by any UIP vaccine.
- **Smallpox:** WHO declared it **eradicated in 1980** (8 May); India free in **1977** – the only human disease eradicated.
- **Rabies:** spread through the **saliva** of a rabid animal (bite) → nervous system, hydrophobia.
- **Community transmission (COVID-19):** an infected person in the community, unaware of infection, infects others – the source cannot be traced to travel or a known contact.
> Exam angle: contagious/infectious (×4), measles immunity, no vaccine (diabetes, rickets), smallpox 1980.

=== Vector Control, Insecticide Resistance and Elimination
- **DDT resistance** in mosquitoes = **natural selection**: resistant individuals survive and breed, so resistance spreads.
| Bio-control | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| *Gambusia affinis* (mosquito fish), guppy | Larvivorous fish – eat larvae |
| Bti | Bacterial bio-larvicide |
| **Wolbachia** | Bacterium; **controls viral diseases spread by mosquitoes** (dengue, chikungunya, Zika) through cytoplasmic incompatibility + pathogen blocking; no GM, no pesticide |
| Pitcher plant | Insectivorous plant – traps insects |
- Wolbachia: in **50-60%** of insect species; **naturally absent in Aedes aegypti and Anopheles**. Verily **'Debug'** (2 Jun 2026): Wolbachia male *Culex quinquefasciatus* released.
- **NCVBDC** (ex-NVBDCP): six diseases – **malaria, JE, dengue, chikungunya, kala-azar, lymphatic filariasis** (not TB). **Elimination:** malaria, LF, kala-azar; dengue, chikungunya, JE only controlled.
- **National Framework for Malaria Elimination (2016-2030):** zero indigenous cases by **2027**, malaria-free India by **2030**. **MERA India** – ICMR, 24 April 2019.
- WHO malaria-free in South Asia: **Maldives (2015), Sri Lanka (2016)**. Malaria and dengue are outbreak-prone and climate-sensitive.
> Exam angle: Wolbachia method (UPSC 2023), DDT – natural selection (APPSC G2 Mains 2017).

=== Air Pollution and Health
- Main target of air pollution: **respiratory system**; also heart disease, stroke, lung cancer.
- **PM2.5 raises heart disease** because these fine particles **penetrate deep into the lungs and enter the blood** (A and R true, R explains A).
- Air pollution comes from **natural sources too** (volcanoes, dust, forest fires) – "only human activities" is false; **planting trees reduces** it (only Statement II true).
- **WHO:** **4.2 million** premature deaths from **ambient** air pollution (**2019**). State of Global Air 2020: 116,000+ Indian infants died within a month of birth (2019).
- **IQAir 2025:** India **6th** most polluted country (PM2.5 **48.9 µg/m³**); only 13 countries met the WHO annual guideline of **5 µg/m³**.
- **DALY = YLL + YLD.** Noise: hearing loss, hypertension, anxiety, sleep disorders.
> Exam angle: PM2.5 assertion-reason, air-pollution statements.

=== Antibiotics and Antimicrobial Resistance
# Antibiotics
- **Fleming: penicillin, 1928**; **Waksman** coined "antibiotic", found **streptomycin** (first anti-TB drug).
- Most antibiotics are **natural or semi-synthetic**, mostly from bacteria (*Streptomyces*); sulfa drugs synthetic. **Prontosil** (Domagk, 1935) → sulfanilamide.
- **Griseofulvin** treats ringworm; **Acetobacter** → vinegar; chloroquine = antimalarial.
# How AMR builds
- Causes: incorrect dose, **incomplete course** (resistant survivors left to multiply), overuse, **self-medication**, livestock use, **mutation**, horizontal gene transfer.
- Severity: **MDR → XDR → TDR**.
- **Biofilm:** microbes in a self-made **EPS (extracellular polymeric substance)** matrix on **implants, catheters, tissues, food surfaces**; blocks antibiotics → chronic infection; food spoilage and contamination.
