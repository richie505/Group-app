## SUB 2 | Dairying and the White Revolution
[2:0] # Milk
[2:1] - India = **world's largest milk producer**: **247.87 MT** (2024-25), per capita **485 g/day**.
[2:2] - India also leads in **butter and ghee** (milk-based), not meat, pork or cheese.
[2:3] - Top milk States 2024-25: **Uttar Pradesh** (15.66%), Rajasthan (14.82%).
[2:4] # White Revolution
[2:5] - **Operation Flood** (**1970**, by **NDDB**) = milk production and distribution through cooperatives (Anand pattern).
[2:6] - **Dr Verghese Kurien** = **Father of the White Revolution** (not P.J. Kurien, Swaminathan).
[2:7] - **NDDB** – **Anand** (Gujarat); **NDRI** – **Karnal** (Haryana).
[2:8] # Schemes
[2:9] - **Rashtriya Gokul Mission** (Dec 2014): development and conservation of **indigenous bovine breeds**; set up **Gokul Grams** and **National Kamdhenu Breeding Centres** (see row 84).
[2:10] - Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Development Fund: milk- and meat-processing capacity.
[2:11] - **20th Livestock Census (2019)**: 535.78 million livestock, 851.81 million poultry; **21st** (2024-25) fully digital.
[2:12] - Livestock and fisheries grew faster than crops (fisheries about 8.8%, livestock about 7.1% a year, FY15-FY24).
[2:13] > Exam angle: Operation Flood → NDDB → Anand → Kurien; NDRI is at Karnal, not Anand.
[2:14] ~~ Sources: Rocket Indian Geography p.53 (White Revolution = milk) and p.68 (NDRI Karnal, NDDB Anand); app notes 4:83 (sub 4); PMF Human & Economic Geography 06 p.32 (Operation Flood cooperatives)

## SUB 8 | Plantation Crops
[8:0] - Main plantation crops: **tea, rubber, coconut, coffee** (not sunflower, soyabean, grapes).
[8:1] - **Ministry of Commerce**: tea, coffee, rubber (and spices); **Ministry of Agriculture**: coconut, arecanut, oil palm, cocoa, cashew.
[8:2] - **Rubber**: **Kerala about 90%** of India's output (APPSC 2003: "more than 90%"); then TN, Karnataka.
[8:3] - **Cashew**: Maharashtra is now the largest producer; older books and keys name **Kerala** (largest producer and processor in earlier decades).
[8:4] - **Coffee**: Karnataka largest; the edible coffee "bean" is keyed as the **perisperm** (APPSC 2010), though botanists describe it as mainly **endosperm**.
[8:5] - Assam – largest tea producer. Coconut – Kerala, Karnataka, TN, AP. **Arecanut** – plantation crop of **tropical humid** climate, Karnataka, Kerala. Cashew – a growth-engine crop of AP.
