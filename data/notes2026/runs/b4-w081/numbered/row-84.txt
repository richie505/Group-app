## SUB 1 | AP Fisheries – Rank, Production and Welfare
[1:0] # Rank and production
[1:1] - AP = **1st in India in fish and shrimp production**; about **30%** of India's fish and 31% of shrimp exports (2024-25); about 29% (2025-26).
[1:2] - **Highest inland fish landings** in India (**2019-20**).
[1:3] - AP plans to make the State the "**Aqua Hub**" of the world.
[1:4] - Fish production 2024-25: **55.39 LMT** (target 56.62 LMT).
[1:5] - Shrimp about **70%** of AP's export basket; **Vannamei** (white-leg) the main export; AP about 32% of India's seafood exports.
[1:6] - **IQF** = **Individually Quick Frozen** – each piece frozen separately and fast.
[1:7] - Coastline **1,053.07 km – 3rd** (revised 2025; old 974 km, 2nd).
[1:8] - **State fish**: **Murrel** (*Channa striata*, Korramenu) – state fish of undivided AP, kept by AP; also **Telangana's** state fish (July 2016).
