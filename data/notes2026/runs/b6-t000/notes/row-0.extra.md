=== New Current Affairs: 5–14 Nov 2026
# Cabinet decisions (10 Nov 2026)
- **State Cabinet, Tuesday 10 Nov 2026** (CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu**) cleared **30+ agenda items**: **AP AVGC-XR Policy 2026–31** (Animation, Visual Effects, Gaming, Comics and Extended Reality); **AP Robotics and Physical AI Policy 2026–2036**; draft amendments to the **AP Semiconductor and Display Fab Policy (4.0) 2024–29**.
- **Directorate of Traffic Management and Road Safety:** a new directorate at **Police HQ, Mangalagiri**, headed by an **IGP**-rank officer (Home Department).
- **APGENCO** allowed to explore **Small Modular Reactor (SMR)** nuclear technology as a future alternative to thermal units.
- **FLO Women Industrial Park:** **30 acres** at **Jayanthipuram, NTR district**, at concessional price, for women entrepreneurs recommended by the **FICCI Ladies Organisation (FLO)**.
# Appointments and statutory bodies
- **APERC Chairperson (4 Nov 2026):** retired IAS officer **Binoy Kumar**, succeeding **P. Venkata Rama Reddy**; term **5 years or till age 70**, whichever is earlier; appointed under the **AP Electricity Reform Act, 1998** (he was Principal Secretary to the CM of undivided AP, 2012–13). **APERC** = statutory body set up **31 March 1999**; chairperson + **two members**; **Ancha Pandu Ranga Rao** = Member (Legal), Oct 2026.
- **APHEFRMC (11 Nov 2026):** **P. Chandrasekhar** appointed **National Medical Commission (NMC)** representative on the **AP Higher Education Fee Regulatory and Monitoring Committee** for **3 years**; body set up **19 Aug 2019** under the **AP Higher Education Regulatory and Monitoring Commission Act, 2019** – regulates fees of **private unaided** higher education institutions, bars **capitation fees**.
- **Vidya Vahini portal (11 Nov 2026):** launched by **APSCHE** on **National Education Day (11 Nov, birth anniversary of Maulana Abul Kalam Azad)** under its **LMS–MOOCs** initiative; first **three** courses – Introduction to AI, Applications of AI, **Quantum Technology** (each **4 credits**, 40 modules). **APSCHE**: est. **20 May 1988** under the **APSCHE Act, 1988 (Act 16 of 1988)**, on the **NPE 1986** advice – **India's first State Council of Higher Education**.
- **Municipal office-bearers:** the **State Election Commission** fixed elections of **Deputy Mayors and Vice-Chairpersons** on **23 Nov 2026**. Under AP's amended municipal laws the **Mayor and Municipal Chairperson are directly elected** by voters, the **Deputy Mayor and Vice-Chairperson indirectly** by members; **Art. 243R(2)(b)** lets the State law decide how the chairperson is elected (**74th Amendment, 1992**; three types of municipalities under **Art. 243Q**).
- **12th Pay Revision Commission:** **AP JAC Amaravati** (employees' body formed **5 Feb 2017, Tirupati**; chairman **Bopparaju Venkateswarlu**) asked the 12th PRC to report within **six months**.
# e-Governance and services
- **Mana Mitra** (Centre lauded its use in **cyclone management**, Nov 2026): **WhatsApp governance** service launched **30 Jan 2025** by IT Minister **Nara Lokesh** at **Undavalli**; WhatsApp number **95523 00009**; **161** services at launch → **200** by March 2025. Other AP e-governance tools: **D-Krishi (2017)** – subsidised seeds with Aadhaar authentication; **eHospital (2022)**; **e-Cabinet (2024)** – paperless Cabinet meetings.
- **Ease of Vehicle Ownership Index (EVOI)** by **IndiaTech.org** with **Consumer VOICE**: **AP ranked 1st** for smooth used-vehicle ownership transfer, then **Kerala, Tamil Nadu**; Delhi 4th; **Uttar Pradesh last**. *(news 10 Nov 2026)*
- **PHC patient-satisfaction ranking (Oct 2026)** by the Health Department, through **IVRS** feedback on four parameters (doctors present, cleanliness, money demanded, medicines): **Palnadu 1st (81.6%)**, Polavaram 81.5%, Nellore 80.7%; **Kurnool last (69.5%)**.
# Disaster, drought and crop insurance
- **Drought mandals, Kharif 2026 (G.O. 5):** **24 mandals in 8 districts** – **12 severe, 12 moderate**; issued by **G. Sai Prasad**, Special Chief Secretary, Revenue (Disaster Management). Severe: Racherla (Markapuram); Aspari, Nandavaram, Adoni-2, Kosigi, Mantralayam, Kollur (**Kurnool**); Rayadurg, Peddapappur (Anantapur); Rolla, Agali, Penukonda (Sri Sathya Sai).
- **PMFBY, Kharif 2026:** after cyclones **Arnab** (crossed near **Narsapur**, 28 Oct) and **Murasu**, the CM said crop insurance must cover farmers **and tenants**; **TATA AIG General Insurance** selected for **nine districts** (Anakapalli, Bapatla, Chittoor, Konaseema, Krishna, Kurnool, NTR, Parvathipuram Manyam, SPSR Nellore); implemented by the **Agriculture and Cooperation Department**. PMFBY: launched **18 Feb 2016**; farmer premium **2% Kharif, 1.5% Rabi, 5%** commercial/horticultural crops.
- **Hepatitis B and C:** over **10 lakh** people (over 2% of AP) estimated affected; a **three-dose Hepatitis B vaccination** drive for about **3.69 lakh** high-risk people; more cases in NTR, East Godavari, Visakhapatnam, Kurnool, Nellore. *(news 4 Nov 2026)*
# Awards and rankings
- **Swachh Survekshan 2025–26** (**10th edition**, MoHUA; theme "**Swachhata Ki Nayi Pehel – Badhayein Haath, Karein Safai Saath**"; awards given by President **Droupadi Murmu**): **six AP ULBs** awarded – GVMC, Vijayawada, Guntur, Tirupati, Rajamahendravaram, Kuppam. **Vijayawada** among the **top three of the Super Swachh League** (with Navi Mumbai, Surat); **Tirupati** in the Super Swachh League for the **4th straight year** and, with **Kuppam** (mentee), **3rd** in the **Swachh Shehar Jodi** category; **Visakhapatnam** among the top three in the **million-plus** category (with Jabalpur, Lucknow). Swachh Survekshan began **2016** under **SBM-U**.
- **National SARAS Mela 2026** (**13–31 Oct 2026**, **Punnami Ghat, Vijayawada**) – the **first** National SARAS Mela held **outside Delhi-NCR**; by the **Ministry of Rural Development** with **SERP**; total sales **₹12.84 crore**; **AP 1st – ₹2.99 crore (~23%)**, Uttar Pradesh 2nd (₹1.22 crore). First SARAS Mela: **1999, Delhi**.
