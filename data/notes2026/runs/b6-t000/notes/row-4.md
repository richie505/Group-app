=== Health - Universal Health Policy, NTR Vaidya Seva and Gates Partnership
# Universal Health Policy (Cabinet, September 2025)
- Approved by the AP Cabinet chaired by CM N. Chandrababu Naidu.
- Free treatment **up to ₹25 lakh per family per year**, **irrespective of economic status**: AP is the **first State** to offer such high-value universal cover.
- Beneficiaries: **1.63 crore families** = **1.43 crore economically weaker + 20 lakh others**.
- Integrates **Ayushman Bharat + NTR Vaidya Seva**; network of **2,493 hospitals**; **3,257 procedures**.
- **Pre-authorisation within 6 hours**.
| Layer (hybrid model) | Cover | Paid by |
|---|---|---|
| Insurance | claims up to ₹2.5 lakh per family | insurance companies |
| Trust | claims ₹2.5 lakh – ₹25 lakh per family | NTR Vaidya Seva Trust |
- The **hybrid model** was announced in **January 2025**: insurance ₹2.5 lakh plus Trust-funded treatment up to **₹25 lakh per family** for the poor (**APPSC 2025 key: ₹25 lakh**).
- **Insurers:** the insurance layer uses **both public and private insurance companies**; the Trust bears ₹2.5–25 lakh (APPSC FRO 2025: both statements – ₹25 lakh per family a year and public + private insurers – correct).
- **APSES 2024–25** (earlier pattern): up to **₹5 lakh per family per year**; total up to ₹25 lakh; **no limit for cancer**; annual family income up to **₹5 lakh**; runs alongside **Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY (₹5 lakh per family)**.
> Insurance handles the first ₹2.5 lakh; the Trust handles ₹2.5–25 lakh, not the reverse.
> ₹2.5 lakh = insurance layer; ₹25 lakh = per family (Trust); ₹5 lakh = per family a year (PM-JAY / APSES).
# NTR Vaidya Seva - dues and rationalisation
- **ASHA (AP Speciality Hospitals Association)** resumed **cashless services from 8 April 2025** after the CM released **₹500 crore**.
- Suspension was in **over 730 private hospitals**; dues **₹3,500 crore** (**₹2,000 crore** to private hospitals); **package rates unchanged since 2018**.
- Scheme: free tertiary care for BPL patients through empanelled hospitals.
- **Rationalisation:** **155 treatments added** to government hospitals (hysterectomy, appendectomy, ENT) → total **324**; **197 rare services continue free** (e.g. thymus removal, duodenum excision; not merged); earlier list inflated to **3,257** services (dengue, typhoid, paratyphoid listed separately); **319 similar services → 133 categories**; remaining **2,736 → 2,550 categories** via insurance company.
# Project Sanjeevani
- Launched by the CM at the **Swasth Nari–Sashakt Parivar Abhiyan** programme, **Visakhapatnam**; upcoming initiative for **preventive healthcare**.
- Integrates **Ayushman Bharat + NTR health services + global practices**.

=== P4 - Zero Poverty Programme
# What P4 is
- **P4 = Public-Private-People Partnership** (also written **People–Private–Public Partnership for the Poor**).
- Launched on **Ugadi, 30 March 2025**; under the **Planning Department**; goal: **complete eradication of poverty** (**Zero Poverty** sutram); linked with **SDG 1 (No Poverty)**.
- **Swarna Andhra P4 Foundation**: poverty eradication, under the **Planning Department**.
- Claimed as a **first-of-its-kind convergence model** in India; government acts only as a **facilitator**; whole-of-society, **voluntary** model (2025-26).
# Who is who in P4
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Margadarshi / Margadarsi | well-off mentor who adopts a family; selected voluntarily (no compulsion) |
| Bangaru Kutumbam / Bangaru Kutumbalu | "Golden Families": the poor families who are adopted |
- Mentor role goes beyond money: **emotional reassurance, guidance, continuous support**. Mentors can be individuals, NGOs or corporates.
- Target: **5 lakh** Golden Families adopted **by 15 August**; Phase 1 scale about **21.8 lakh families**.
- Donor platforms: **Milaap, DonateKart, RangDe**.
- **Dokka Seethamma** (19th-century social reformer, known for selfless service to the poor) is remembered in P4 messaging.
- Update (6 Jul 2026): about **9,000 Kuppam families** adopted by **Adani, TVS Motor and MEIL**.
> Margadarsi = mentor (adopts); Bangaru Kutumbam = poor family (adopted). Margadarsis are volunteers, not nominated by government.

- **AIC–APGB MoU** (Guntur): **Agriculture Insurance Company of India** + **Andhra Pradesh Grameena Bank**; **bancassurance** (AIC products via APGB rural branches); covers shrimp losses from diseases, cyclones, poor water quality; livestock insurance planned. **APGB** = RRB sponsored by **Union Bank of India**.
- AP produces **35–40% of India's shrimp**.
- **APSADA Act, 2020 amendment**: licence validity **lifetime → 1 year** for aquaculture business (hatcheries, feed mills, seed units) and **5 years** for farming; aligns with **Coastal Aquaculture Authority Act, 2005**; old registrations endorsed on licence fee + **50% late fee** (about 20% farmers can regularise). **APSADA = Andhra Pradesh State Aquaculture Development Authority (Agency)**.

=== Education and APPSC
# Personalised Adaptive Learning (PAL)
- Under **Samagra Shiksha, AP**: **AP first State to embed PAL in the government school curriculum**.

=== Cleanliness, Environment and Urban Governance
# Swachha Andhra Awards–2025
- Launched **1 September 2025**: **AP first State in India to institutionalise annual cleanliness and sanitation awards** (**52 State-level** awards); **GO-RT-916** of MAUD; modelled on **Swachh Survekshan**.
- Categories: municipalities, gram panchayats, schools, hospitals, bus stations, Rythu Bazaars, hostels, industries, anganwadis, residential schools; NGOs and sanitation workers as **"Swachhata Warriors"**.
- **First ceremony: 2 October 2025 (Gandhi Jayanti)**.
- **Swarna Andhra–Swachha Andhra (SASA)**: **every third Saturday = Swachha Andhra Day**.
# AQLI 2025 - air pollution
- AP average PM2.5 **26 µg/m³** (about 5× WHO) → life expectancy loss **2.1 years** (lower than national **3.5 years**).
- **Visakhapatnam 40–50 µg/m³** (about 8× WHO); Vijayawada 30–40 µg/m³.
- Trend: **17.8 µg/m³ (1998) → above 30 µg/m³ (2021)**, rise over **70%**; 50% of AP exposed to >30 µg/m³.
- India average PM2.5 (2023) **41 µg/m³**; meeting WHO limit adds 3.5 years; northern plains could gain 5 years.
| Standard | PM2.5 annual | PM10 annual |
|---|---|---|
| WHO (2021) | 5 µg/m³ (from 10) | 15 µg/m³ (from 20) |
| India NAAQS | 40 µg/m³ | 60 µg/m³ |
- **NCAP (2019)**: 20–30% cut by 2024 (extended to **2026**); **132 non-attainment cities**.
# Other governance items
- **National Efficient Cooking Programme** in all Anganwadis: about **₹24 crore** saved a year; **28,327 tonnes CO₂** cut; AP delegation led by **A. Surya Kumari**; praised by WCD Secretary Anil Malik and **BEE**.
- **Aspirational Blocks Programme**: **Ibrahimpatnam and Penuganchiprolu** (NTR district) won **NITI Aayog gold** for 100% results in 6 indicators; dashboard tracks **49 indicators**; ABP launched **7 January 2023**, **500 blocks**.
- **Puramithra app** (Vijayawada Municipal Corporation): property and vacant land tax, grievances with photos; **5% discount** on advance tax 2025–26.
- **CAG** delegation (28 May 2025) at **TUDA** (Tirupati Urban Development Authority; statutory body under the AP Urban Development Authorities Act): recommended **digitising financial and accounting systems**; reviewed land acquisition, layouts, zoning, parks.
