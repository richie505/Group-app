=== P4 Zero Poverty, NTR Bharosa Pensions and TTD Insurance
# P4 – Public-Private-People Partnership (APPSC FSO PYQ)
- **P4 = Public-Private-People Partnership**; launched by CM Naidu and Dy CM Pawan Kalyan at **Velagapudi, Amaravati** on **Ugadi, 30 March 2025** (a Sunday) – not Independence Day, Republic Day or Diwali.
- Rolled out statewide from **19 August 2025**.
- **Model:** the **top 10%** of society adopt the **bottom 20%**; mentors/donors = **Margadarsis**, beneficiary families = **Bangaru Kutumbams** (golden families); voluntary help (aid, mentoring), not a cash-transfer scheme.
- About **19.16 lakh** Bangaru families identified.
- **First anniversary (30 Mar 2026, Tirupati):** **1.06 lakh Margadarsis** had adopted 10.3 lakh families; goal restated as a **poverty-free society by 2047** (earlier: zero poverty by 2029).
# NTR Bharosa and NSAP pensions
- **NTR Bharosa** = AP's social security pensions; about **₹76,000 crore** disbursed by October 2026.
- **October 2026:** **₹2,692.34 crore** released; door-to-door distribution from 1 October 2026.
- From **1 October 2026** the **Central share** of **NSAP** (**National Social Assistance Programme**) pensions goes straight to **Aadhaar-linked bank accounts** via **SNA-SPARSH** – **9.34 lakh** beneficiaries, ₹382.82 crore; the **State share is still paid in cash**.

=== Health and Food Safety Decisions
# AP Human Organ Transplantation Amendment Act 2026
- Approved by the Governor as **AP Act No. 25 of 2026**; covers tissues; **grandparents and grandchildren** added as near relatives; **swap transplantation** recognised.
- Parent law **THOTA** enacted **1994** (amended 2011); **Jeevandan** = AP's **deceased organ donation** programme.
# Trauma Registry (21 Oct 2026)
- For timely treatment in the **Golden Hour**; **Pre-Hospital Notification** by **108 and 104** ambulance staff.
- **Good Samaritan** protection: **Section 134A, Motor Vehicles Act, 1988**.
# Artificial paneer ban (29 Oct 2026)
- Gazette notification bans manufacture, storage, transport and sale of artificial paneer for **one year**.
- **FSSAI:** set up under the **Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006**; under the Union **Health** Ministry.

=== Fisheries and Skill Development
# Pulicat Lake – GPS fishing boundary
- **Pulicat** = **brackish-water** lake shared by **AP and Tamil Nadu**.
- Fishermen asked for **GPS transponders** on boats instead of **palmyrah trunk markers**; **NCCR (National Centre for Coastal Research)** to fix digital coordinates.
- **Rayadoruvu sea mouth project:** **₹133.11 crore** from the Union Fisheries Ministry; about **199** cases pending against TN fishermen.
