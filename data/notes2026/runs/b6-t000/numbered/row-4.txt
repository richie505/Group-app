[0:1] - Approved by the AP Cabinet chaired by CM N. Chandrababu Naidu.

## SUB 2 | P4 - Zero Poverty Programme
[2:0] # What P4 is
[2:1] - **P4 = Public-Private-People Partnership** (also written **People–Private–Public Partnership for the Poor**).
[2:2] - Launched on **Ugadi, 30 March 2025**; under the **Planning Department**; goal: **complete eradication of poverty** (**Zero Poverty** sutram); linked with **SDG 1 (No Poverty)**.
[2:3] - **Swarna Andhra P4 Foundation**: poverty eradication, under the **Planning Department**.
[2:4] - Claimed as a **first-of-its-kind convergence model** in India; government acts only as a **facilitator**; whole-of-society, **voluntary** model (2025-26).
[2:5] # Who is who in P4
[2:6] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [2:6.0] Margadarshi / Margadarsi | well-off mentor who adopts a family; selected voluntarily (no compulsion)
  [2:6.1] Bangaru Kutumbam / Bangaru Kutumbalu | "Golden Families": the poor families who are adopted
[2:7] - Mentor role goes beyond money: **emotional reassurance, guidance, continuous support**. Mentors can be individuals, NGOs or corporates.
[2:8] - Target: **5 lakh** Golden Families adopted **by 15 August**; Phase 1 scale about **21.8 lakh families**.
[2:9] - Donor platforms: **Milaap, DonateKart, RangDe**.
[2:10] - **Dokka Seethamma** (19th-century social reformer, known for selfless service to the poor) is remembered in P4 messaging.
[2:11] - Update (6 Jul 2026): about **9,000 Kuppam families** adopted by **Adani, TVS Motor and MEIL**.
[2:12] > Margadarsi = mentor (adopts); Bangaru Kutumbam = poor family (adopted). Margadarsis are volunteers, not nominated by government.
