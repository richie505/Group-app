=== AP Retail Trade Policy 2015-20
- Issued by G.O.Ms. No. 11, Industries & Commerce, **11 Jan 2016**; valid **5 years**; purpose: **development of the retail sector**.
- Objectives: make AP a **preferred retail destination**; hub for **retail logistics** (warehouses); accelerate investment to underdeveloped regions; skills and jobs; retail as a tool of socio-economic development; promote **e-commerce**.
- Targets: **Rs 5,000 crore** new investment and **20,000 additional jobs by 2020**; train **1 lakh youth** in 5 years.
- **Applies to** retail enterprises with **turnover Rs 1 crore or more** OR **10 or more full-time employees** – so a **family kirana shop with fewer than 10 people is outside it** (single-desk and simplification apply to all).
- Mega retail status: investment Rs 100 crore+ or 2,000 direct jobs.
> Exam angle: 5 PYQs (Panchayat Secretary, Group-1) – aim, job target, investment target, applicability.

=== Tourism – Economic Role, Policy and Bodies
- Tourism gives **direct employment**, **indirect employment much higher than direct**, and **foreign exchange** (APPSC G2).
- **AP Tourism Policy 2024-29**: linked to **Swarna Andhra @2047**; **industry status** to tourism and hospitality across all segments; tailor-made packages for **Mega and Ultra Mega** projects; targets: AP among India's **top three destinations**, tourism **above 20% of GVA** and 20% of workforce.
- **Seven anchor hubs** (hub-and-spoke): Visakhapatnam, Tirupati, Araku, Rajahmundry, Amaravati, Srisailam, Gandikota; **25+ circuits** (2 Buddhist, 10 temple, 5 beach, 4 river cruise, 3 eco, 2 sea cruise, seaplane).
- **Budget hotels**: promoted to **attract tourists**. **Swarna Andhra Tourism Vision 2047** launched at the **FAITH conclave**; AP won **Best Tourism Promotion State** at **GTM 2026**.
- **APTDC**: fully State-owned operational arm – **Haritha hotels**, boating, sound-and-light, **Srisailam ropeway**; revenue **Rs 106.80 crore** (FY 2024-25); in **2000-05** its revenue rose **more than six times** (APPSC G2 2011). **APTA** = nodal tourism authority.
- **Arrivals**: 36.42 crore (2025-26). Rank in domestic visits: **4th in 2024** (Ministry of Tourism: after UP, TN, Karnataka); SES 2024-25 calls AP the third most visited. **France** = most visited country (2024).
- **Incredible India 2.0** (2017): promotion of **tourism and employment**.

=== Tourism Places, Heritage and Circuits
- AP = birthplace of **Mahayana Buddhism**; about **40** Buddhist monuments. Buddhist sites: Amaravati, Thotlakonda, Bavikonda, Bojjannakonda, Sankaram, Salihundam, Ghantasala (**Srisailam not** a Buddhist site).
- **Rushikonda Beach** (Visakhapatnam): **Blue Flag**; 21 more beaches proposed.
- Vizag beach-shack policy modelled on **Goa**.
- **Visakhapatnam-Araku train:** glass-roofed **Vistadome** coach; Araku: coffee, **Borra caves**.
- **Gandikota Fort** (YSR Kadapa): anchor hub; a recently found **16th-century copper plate** inscription relates to Gandikota's history (APPSC Panchayat Secretary).
