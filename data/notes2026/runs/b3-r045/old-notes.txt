## SUB 5 | Cropping Seasons
[45:5:0] TABLE head: Season | Sowing | Harvest | Major crops
  [45:5:0.0] Kharif | June-July (SW monsoon) | Sep-Oct or Oct-Nov | Rice, maize, jowar, bajra, cotton, groundnut, soybean, jute, sugarcane, tur
  [45:5:0.1] Rabi | Oct-Dec (after monsoon) | March-April | Wheat, barley, gram, mustard, peas, linseed, potato
  [45:5:0.2] Zaid | March-June | June-July | Watermelon, muskmelon, cucumber, vegetables, fodder
[45:5:1] - Rabi depends on winter rain from **western disturbances** + irrigation.
[45:5:2] - **Zaid**: short, irrigation-dependent summer window **between rabi and kharif** (APPSC 2024).
[45:5:3] - Zaid sub-seasons: Zaid Kharif (Aug-Sep sowing); Zaid Rabi (Feb-Mar sowing).
[45:5:4] - Mustard: Rabi, sown Oct-Nov, harvested Feb-Mar.
[45:5:5] - **Sugarcane**: annual/long-duration cash crop placed on the **Kharif** side.
[45:5:6] **Jute is Kharif**, not Rabi. Cotton = Kharif; wheat = Rabi; watermelon = Zaid.
[45:5:7] # Crops Grown in Both Seasons
[45:5:8] - AP: **paddy, maize, jowar, bajra and pulses** grown in both kharif and rabi.
[45:5:9] - AP 2024-25 paddy: **14.04 lakh ha kharif + 6.93 lakh ha rabi**.
[45:5:10] - Pulses: **tur/arhar = kharif**; **gram/chana = rabi**.
[45:5:11] - Millets mainly kharif; some in southern rabi.

## SUB 7 | Cropping Pattern Trends – India
[45:7:0] - Long-run shift: **food crops → commercial and high-value crops** (drivers: irrigation, prices, technology).
[45:7:1] - **Rice: largest cropped area** in India (~51-52 Mha).
[45:7:2] - Pulses and oilseeds: stagnant yields.
[45:7:3] - Rice-wheat expansion (2015-2025): Punjab, Telangana, Madhya Pradesh – assured MSP procurement.
[45:7:4] - **Horticulture overtook foodgrains** (2024-25).
[45:7:5] - Policy: shift **~20% of rice area to pulses/legumes** → cut urea use, save groundwater.
[45:7:6] - Largest soyabean area: **Madhya Pradesh** (5.35 Mha, 2009-10), then Maharashtra (3.02 Mha).
[45:7:7] - **Commercialisation of agriculture:** when the area under non-food (cash) crops grows faster than the area under food crops, farming is becoming market-oriented – this is commercialisation of agriculture.

## SUB 10 | Agriculture Since 1991
[45:10:0] - After **1991 liberalisation**: **decline in public investment** in agriculture (APPSC 2023, correct statement).
[45:10:1] - **WTO Agreement on Agriculture (1995)**: market access, domestic support, export subsidies (**not land ceilings**).
[45:10:2] - **GM crops**: **Bt cotton (2002)** only major GM crop; GM mustard DMH-11 got GEAC environmental-release nod in 2022 (pending in Supreme Court); **Bt brinjal** under moratorium since 2010.
[45:10:3] - **Agricultural Renewal Action Plan** (Group-II 2017): soil health; irrigation/water augmentation and demand management; credit and insurance; **not "boosting capital formation"**.
