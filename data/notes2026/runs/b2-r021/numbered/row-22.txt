## SUB 1 | Federalism, Governor and the Floor Test
[1:0] - **S.R. Bommai (1994)**, **nine judges**: the judgment that **redefined Centre–State relations** – Art. 356 proclamations are **judicially reviewable**; majority to be tested **on the floor of the House**; **secularism and federalism** basic.
[1:1] - **Composite floor test**: ordered by the Supreme Court in **Kalyan Singh v. Jagdambika Pal (February 1998, U.P.)** – a single vote between the two rival claimants (Kalyan Singh won 225–196).
[1:2] - **Rameshwar Prasad (2006)**: dissolution of the **Bihar Assembly** void. **Nabam Rebia (2016)**: Governor acts on **advice**.
[1:3] - **State of Tamil Nadu v. Governor (April 2025)**: **deemed assent** for long-pending Bills; **Presidential reference opinion (November 2025)**: **no fixed timelines** and no deemed assent.
[1:4] - **Sarbananda Sonowal (2005)**: **IMDT Act** struck for breaching the Union's duty under **Art. 355**.

## SUB 4 | Reservation Judgments
[4:0] - **Indra Sawhney (1992)**: upheld **27% OBC** quota; **caste can be a factor** in identifying backwardness; **creamy layer** excluded; **50% ceiling**; no reservation in promotions (→ 77th Amendment).
[4:1] - **M. Nagaraj (2006)**: promotion quota needs **quantifiable data** on backwardness, inadequacy and efficiency (Art. 335).
[4:2] - **State of Punjab v. Davinder Singh (1 Aug 2024)**: seven judges, **6:1**; States may **sub-classify SCs**; overruled **E.V. Chinnaiah (2004)** (AP Act, 2000).
[4:3] - **Chebrolu Leela Prasad Rao (2020)**: **100% ST** teacher quota in AP Scheduled Areas **struck down**.
