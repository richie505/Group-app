## SUB 1 | Strait of Hormuz Crisis and UNCLOS
[1:0] # Iran's Selective Blockade and Toll System
[1:1] - Iran **closed** the strait for the **US, Israel and allies**; **open** to "friendly nations" **India, China, Russia, Iraq, Pakistan** (announced by FM Araghchi).
[1:2] - Traffic: **~130 ships/day → 3–4/day**; **20+ vessels attacked**; **~2,000 stranded**.
[1:3] - **Toll-booth system**: geopolitical vetting, clearance code, escort; **up to $2 million per ship**, some in **yuan** (Iran denies).
[1:4] - Indian ships through Hormuz: **Pine Gas, Jag Vasant, Shivalik, Nanda Devi, Jag Laadki** – India had **no formal agreement and made no payments**.
[1:5] - **GCC** called the tolls a clear violation of law.
[1:6] - Workarounds: US **Project Freedom** escorts; **Habshan–Fujairah pipeline** (UAE) **bypasses** the strait.

[1:11] # Geography of Hormuz
[1:12] - Between **Iran (north)** and **Oman (Musandam) & UAE (south)**; links the **Persian Gulf** with the **Gulf of Oman / Arabian Sea**.
[1:13] - Width **~50 km at entry**, **~33 km at narrowest**.
[1:14] - Carries **~20 mb/d** (**~20%** of global oil consumption / about one-fifth of petroleum), **20–30% of global LNG** (incl. **Qatar LNG**); trade **~$600 billion a year**.
[1:15] - Users: **Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE**; the Gulf produces **22%** of global oil.
[1:16] - Hormuz has **no direct link to the Red Sea**.

## SUB 8 | 1973 and 1979 Oil Crises – Comparison with 2026
[8:0] # 1973 Oil Crisis – Chain
[8:1] - **Yom Kippur War (1973)**.
[8:2] - **Arab members of OPEC (OAPEC)** imposed an **oil embargo** on countries **supporting Israel**.
[8:3] - Prices rose **~fourfold (~400%, 1973–74)** → **stagflation**.
[8:4] - Other factors: **end of the gold standard (1971)**; territories lost in the **1967 war**.
[8:5] - Outcomes: **petrodollar system**, **strategic petroleum reserves (SPRs)**, creation of the **International Energy Agency (IEA)**.
[8:6] - **First major use of oil as a geopolitical weapon**.
[8:7] # Comparison
[8:8] TABLE head: Crisis | Cause | India
  [8:8.0] 1973 | Political embargo (OAPEC) | Prices $3 → $5+ (~70% initial rise); oil import bill $500 million (1973) → $1.3 billion (1974); inflation ~20%; Fifth Five-Year Plan assumptions upset
  [8:8.1] 1979 | Iranian Revolution (not OPEC price-stabilising cuts); also stagflation | Prices $13 (1979) → ~$34 (1980); BoP crisis → India approached the IMF after 1979
  [8:8.2] 2026 | Physical disruption of supply routes – Strait of Hormuz | Selective passage; no payments
[8:9] > India went to the **IMF after 1979**, not after 1973. 1973 = political embargo; 2026 = physical disruption.
