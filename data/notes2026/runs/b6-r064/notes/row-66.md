=== Strait of Hormuz Crisis and UNCLOS
# Iran's Selective Blockade and Toll System
- Iran **closed** the strait for the **US, Israel and allies**; **open** to "friendly nations" **India, China, Russia, Iraq, Pakistan** (announced by FM Araghchi).
- Traffic: **~130 ships/day → 3–4/day**; **20+ vessels attacked**; **~2,000 stranded**.
- **Toll-booth system**: geopolitical vetting, clearance code, escort; **up to $2 million per ship**, some in **yuan** (Iran denies).
- Indian ships through Hormuz: **Pine Gas, Jag Vasant, Shivalik, Nanda Devi, Jag Laadki** – India had **no formal agreement and made no payments**.
- **GCC** called the tolls a clear violation of law.
- Workarounds: US **Project Freedom** escorts; **Habshan–Fujairah pipeline** (UAE) **bypasses** the strait.
- **Philippines** (President **Ferdinand Marcos Jr.**) declared a **National Energy Emergency** – imports **~98%** of its oil.
# UNCLOS – Straits Articles
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|

| 39 | Duties of ships during passage |
| 44 | Coastal States cannot suspend or hamper passage |
> **Natural straits – no tolls**; only **artificial canals (Suez, Panama)** charge fees.
# Geography of Hormuz
- Between **Iran (north)** and **Oman (Musandam) & UAE (south)**; links the **Persian Gulf** with the **Gulf of Oman / Arabian Sea**.
- Width **~50 km at entry**, **~33 km at narrowest**.
- Carries **~20 mb/d** (**~20%** of global oil consumption / about one-fifth of petroleum), **20–30% of global LNG** (incl. **Qatar LNG**); trade **~$600 billion a year**.
- Users: **Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE**; the Gulf produces **22%** of global oil.
- Hormuz has **no direct link to the Red Sea**.

=== 1973 and 1979 Oil Crises – Comparison with 2026
# 1973 Oil Crisis – Chain
- **Yom Kippur War (1973)**.
- **Arab members of OPEC (OAPEC)** imposed an **oil embargo** on countries **supporting Israel**.
- Prices rose **~fourfold (~400%, 1973–74)** → **stagflation**.
- Other factors: **end of the gold standard (1971)**; territories lost in the **1967 war**.
- Outcomes: **petrodollar system**, **strategic petroleum reserves (SPRs)**, creation of the **International Energy Agency (IEA)**.
- **First major use of oil as a geopolitical weapon**.
# Comparison
| Crisis | Cause | India |
|---|---|---|
| 1973 | Political embargo (OAPEC) | Prices $3 → $5+ (~70% initial rise); oil import bill $500 million (1973) → $1.3 billion (1974); inflation ~20%; Fifth Five-Year Plan assumptions upset |
| 1979 | Iranian Revolution (not OPEC price-stabilising cuts); also stagflation | Prices $13 (1979) → ~$34 (1980); BoP crisis → India approached the IMF after 1979 |
| 2026 | Physical disruption of supply routes – Strait of Hormuz | Selective passage; no payments |
> India went to the **IMF after 1979**, not after 1973. 1973 = political embargo; 2026 = physical disruption.
# India's Energy Basket Today
- **Coal ~48.4%**, **crude ~24.7%** of energy demand (IEA).
- Domestic crude meets **~13%**; import dependence **~88.5% (FY26)**.
- **Strategic Petroleum Reserves**: **~5.33 million tonnes** capacity.
