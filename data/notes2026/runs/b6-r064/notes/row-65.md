=== Strait of Hormuz and the US–Iran Conflict
# The 20% Transit Fee
- President **Donald Trump** proposed a **20% reimbursement fee** on cargo through the Strait of Hormuz ("**Guardian of the Hormuz Strait**"); **withdrawn one day later**, replaced by **trade and investment deals with Gulf countries**.
- **IMO opposed**: **no legal basis for tolls** on international strait transit; **Iran's FM** called 20% excessive.
> **Neither the US nor Iran has ratified UNCLOS**, but transit passage is **customary law**; tolls apply only to **artificial canals (Suez, Panama)**.
# US–Iran Context
