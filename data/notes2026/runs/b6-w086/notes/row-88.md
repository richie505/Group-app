=== World Press Freedom Index – India's Rank and Method
# WPFI 2026
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Publisher | Reporters Without Borders (RSF), est. 1985, HQ Paris |
| Countries | 180 |
| India 2026 | 157th (score 31.96) – fell 6 places from 151st (2025) |
| India's category | "Very serious" |
| Rank 1 / last | Norway / Eritrea |
- Global press freedom at a **25-year low**; about **100 countries declined**; for the first time **more than half** of countries are "Difficult" or "Very serious".
- Reasons for India's fall: violence against journalists, arrests and legal pressure, ownership concentration, political influence, online harassment (not newspaper circulation); the Union Government called the rankings **biased**.
# Five Indicators
- **Political context, legal framework, economic context, socio-cultural context, safety (of journalists)** – circulation or internet penetration is not an indicator.
- Categories: Good – Satisfactory – Problematic – Difficult – **Very serious**.
