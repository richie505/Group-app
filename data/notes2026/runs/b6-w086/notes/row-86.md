=== UNEP and Environment Reports
# Global Environment Outlook (GEO-7), 2025
- By **UNEP** (7th GEO), released at UNEP's 7th session, Nairobi.
| Indicator | Figure |
|---|---|
| Global temperature 2024 | 1.55°C – highest ever |
| Species facing extinction | 1 million of 8 million |
| Pollution deaths | 9 million premature deaths a year |
| Degraded land | 20–40%, affecting 3 billion+ |
- Projections: **1.5°C breached by the early 2030s**, 2°C by the 2040s; food transformation could lift **200 million** out of undernutrition and **100 million** out of extreme poverty.
- **UNEP**: founded **5 June 1972**, HQ **Nairobi, Kenya**; reports – **Emissions Gap Report, Frontiers, GEO**; campaigns – **Beat Pollution, World Environment Day**; **Common Carbon Metric** = carbon footprint of **building operations**.
# UNEP State of Finance for Nature 2026
- For every **USD 1** protecting nature, ~**USD 30** goes to nature-degrading activities (**30:1**); nature-negative flows **USD 7.3 trillion (2023)**; biodiversity spending **up 11%**.
- India's **Nature-based Solutions** initiatives cited: Green India Mission, **MISHTI**, Amrit Sarovar, **CAMPA funds**.
- **Rio Conventions**: 30x30 = **CBD**; restore 1.5 billion ha by 2030 = **UNCCD**.
- **New Zealand**: first country to propose **pricing agricultural (livestock) emissions**.
# IQAir World Air Quality Report 2025 (24 March 2026)
- **IQAir** (Swiss); **8th edition**; **9,446 cities** in **143 countries/territories**.
- Most polluted countries: **1. Pakistan**, **2. Bangladesh**, 3. Tajikistan, 4. Chad, 5. DR Congo; **India 6th** (5th in 2024), PM2.5 **48.9 µg/m³**.

- **New Delhi** – most polluted **capital** for the **8th year** running.
- Only **13** countries/territories and ~**14%** of cities met the WHO guideline (**5 µg/m³**).
