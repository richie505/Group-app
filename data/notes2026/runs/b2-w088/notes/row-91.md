=== Constitutional Provisions – Articles 309-323
| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| 309 | Recruitment and conditions of service |
| **310** | **Doctrine of Pleasure** – Union/All-India/defence services hold office during the pleasure of the President; State services of the Governor |
| **311** | **Safeguards to civil servants**: no dismissal by an authority subordinate to the appointing one; inquiry with a reasonable hearing |
| **312** | **All India Services** – Parliament may create new AIS if the **Rajya Sabha** passes a resolution by **two-thirds** of members present and voting |
| 315 / 316 / 317 | PSCs for Union and States / appointment and term / removal and suspension |
| 320 | Functions of PSCs |
- **UPSC** member: **6 years or 65 years**, whichever is earlier; **SPSC**: 6 years or **62**; removal for misbehaviour only after a **Supreme Court inquiry**.
> Angles: Article 312 (×3), 310 (pleasure), 311 (safeguards), UPSC age 65.

=== Civil Services Day
- **21 April**: on **21 April 1947** **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel** addressed the first batch of probationers at **Metcalfe House, Delhi**, calling them the **"steel frame of India"**.
- Organised annually by **DARPG** since **2006** (first function at Vigyan Bhawan, 21 Apr 2006); **18th Civil Services Day (21 Apr 2026)** theme "Viksit Bharat: Citizen-Centric Governance and Development at the Last Mile".
- **17th CSD (21 Apr 2025)**: **16** PM's Awards for Excellence in Public Administration.

=== Role, Structure, Neutrality and Capacity Building
- Roles of civil servants: **policy advisers, implementers, stabilisers**.
- **State Secretariat**: apex of State administration; the main force forming the principles of procedure for the State Government's work; **split system** = policy (Secretariat) vs implementation (field agencies); **tenure system** = fixed Central deputation then return to cadre.
- Cadre control: **IAS – DoPT**; **IPS – MHA**; **IFoS – MoEFCC**.
- **Weber**: legal-rational bureaucracy (no personal patronage); **Morstein Marx**: guardian, caste, patronage, merit.
- Neutrality: **AIS (Conduct) Rules 1968**, **CCS (Conduct) Rules 1964**; **T.S.R. Subramanian case** – fixed tenure, Civil Services Boards.
- **Mission Karmayogi (2020)**: platform **iGOT Karmayogi**; custodian **Capacity Building Commission**; lateral entry backed by the **2nd ARC and Hota**.
- Karmayogi **six pillars**: policy, institutional, competency, digital learning (iGOT), e-HRMS, monitoring-evaluation (lateral entry is not one); **GDCBA** launched by CBC on 20 Feb 2026.
- **AP (Apr 2026)**: section officer dismissed under **Rule 9, AP CCA Rules 1991** for campaigning in the 2024 elections; Chhattisgarh's 21 Apr 2026 circular barring party membership was withdrawn.
