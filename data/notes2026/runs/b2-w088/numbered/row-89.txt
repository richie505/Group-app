## SUB 0 | Social Audit – MGNREGA and AP's SSAAT
[0:0] - **MGNREGA 2005, Section 17**: the **Gram Sabha** conducts the **social audit** of all works – the process used to **enhance accountability** in MGNREGA (not financial, political or legal audit).
[0:1] - **AP SSAAT** (**Society for Social Audit, Accountability and Transparency**): set up **2009** as an independent society; looks after social audit of Panchayat Raj / rural development programmes in AP (not SERP, Streenidhi or DWACRA).
[0:2] - **CAG** found that the Social Audit Unit **lacks functional independence**.
[0:3] - **Village Social Auditors**: short training of about **two days** at the mandal level before the audit.
[0:4] - **AP pioneered State-wide MGNREGA social audits**; **Meghalaya (2017)** passed the first law making social audit of government programmes mandatory.
[0:5] - **Second ARC (12th Report)**: social audits to be **institutionalised in all major welfare schemes**.
[0:6] > Angles: MGNREGA accountability process (×2), who conducts it (Gram Sabha), SSAAT, CAG finding, training days.

## SUB 2 | Citizens' Charter and Ombudsman
[2:0] # Citizens' Charter
[2:1] - A **written commitment** on service standards; main purpose = **time-bound service delivery and grievance redress**.
[2:2] - Origin **UK, 1991** (John Major); India adopted **1997**; **DARPG** coordinates; charters are **non-justiciable**.
[2:3] - **Six principles**: quality, choice, standards, value, accountability, transparency (**participation is not** one).
[2:4] - **Basic elements (India)**: vision and mission; services provided and their standards; information on how to obtain them; **grievance redress**; **expectations from citizens** – **inspection of the agency's work is NOT** an element.
[2:5] - **Citizens Charter Bill, 2011** lapsed with the Lok Sabha in **2014**; **Second ARC** wanted statutory backing.
[2:6] # Ombudsman
[2:7] - Ombudsman = **Sweden, 1809**; banks, insurance and tax departments use an **Ombudsman** as the alternate grievance redress system for complaints (Banking/Insurance/Tax Ombudsman).
[2:8] - Classic ombudsman = **maladministration**; Lokpal/Lokayukta = **corruption** (Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act **2013**); terms coined by **L.M. Singhvi**; first Lokayukta **Maharashtra 1971**.
[2:9] - **First ARC** (1966-70, **Morarji Desai**): recommended a **Lokpal at the Union** and **Lokayuktas in the States**.

## SUB 3 | Right to Services Acts
[3:0] - Idea grew out of the **Citizens' Charter** in **Great Britain**.
[3:1] - First law: **Madhya Pradesh Lok Sewa Guarantee Act, 2010**; **Bihar** 2011; among Punjab, Assam, Gujarat, Maharashtra the earliest is **Punjab**.
[3:2] - Features: notified services in a **fixed time**, appeals, **penalties**; they do **not** abolish other grievance mechanisms.
[3:3] - **AP Public Services Delivery Guarantee Act, 2017** – in force **18 Oct 2017**.
[3:4] - **Project Saksham** (railways) = time-bound skill upgradation of railway employees (a CA item, not a Right to Services law).

## SUB 4 | Sevottam, CPGRAMS and the Second ARC 12th Report
[4:0] # Sevottam
[4:1] - **Seva + Uttam**; suggested in the **Second ARC's 12th Report**; promoted by **DARPG** (framework for improving service delivery); from **2009**.
[4:2] - **Three modules**: Citizens' Charter; Public Grievance Redress; Service Delivery Capability; certification **IS 15700:2005**.
[4:3] # CPGRAMS
[4:4] - **Centralised Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System**; launched **2007** by **DARPG** (Ministry of **Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions**, not Home Affairs); 24×7 via web, app and **UMANG**.
[4:5] - DARPG circular **23 Aug 2024**: maximum disposal time **30 → 21 days**; **GRAI**: 11 indicators, 4 dimensions (efficiency, feedback, domain, organisational commitment); pensioners: **CPENGRAMS**.
[4:6] # Second ARC 12th Report (2009)
[4:7] - **"Citizen Centric Administration – The Heart of Governance"**; Second ARC (2005), chairman **Veerappa Moily**; recommended Sevottam, statutory charters, social audits, right to public services laws.
[4:8] - **Punchhi Commission (2010)** volume on good governance argued for treating good governance / corruption-free delivery of services as a **right** of citizens.
