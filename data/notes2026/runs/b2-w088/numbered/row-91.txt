## SUB 3 | Role, Structure, Neutrality and Capacity Building
[3:0] - Roles of civil servants: **policy advisers, implementers, stabilisers**.
[3:1] - **State Secretariat**: apex of State administration; the main force forming the principles of procedure for the State Government's work; **split system** = policy (Secretariat) vs implementation (field agencies); **tenure system** = fixed Central deputation then return to cadre.
[3:2] - Cadre control: **IAS – DoPT**; **IPS – MHA**; **IFoS – MoEFCC**.
[3:3] - **Weber**: legal-rational bureaucracy (no personal patronage); **Morstein Marx**: guardian, caste, patronage, merit.
[3:4] - Neutrality: **AIS (Conduct) Rules 1968**, **CCS (Conduct) Rules 1964**; **T.S.R. Subramanian case** – fixed tenure, Civil Services Boards.
[3:5] - **Mission Karmayogi (2020)**: platform **iGOT Karmayogi**; custodian **Capacity Building Commission**; lateral entry backed by the **2nd ARC and Hota**.
[3:6] - Karmayogi **six pillars**: policy, institutional, competency, digital learning (iGOT), e-HRMS, monitoring-evaluation (lateral entry is not one); **GDCBA** launched by CBC on 20 Feb 2026.
[3:7] - **AP (Apr 2026)**: section officer dismissed under **Rule 9, AP CCA Rules 1991** for campaigning in the 2024 elections; Chhattisgarh's 21 Apr 2026 circular barring party membership was withdrawn.
