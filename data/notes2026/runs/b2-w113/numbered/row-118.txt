## SUB 2 | Consequences
[2:0] - **Partition:** 14-18 million crossed borders (largest forced migration); deaths 2-20 lakh, most historians ~10 lakh.
[2:1] - **Godhra:** 59 burnt on **Sabarmati Express**, mostly kar sevaks; riots killed **1,169**; SC-appointed **SIT** investigated.
[2:2] - **Ghettoisation** = moving into religiously segregated neighbourhoods; also **displacement**, loss of trust in police and courts, **polarisation**, weaker social capital.
[2:3] - **Veena Das** (*Critical Events*): impact of Partition and riots on **women**.
[2:4] ~~ Sources: NextGen Society G2 Part 1 p.87; app notes 2:118 sub 2
## SUB 3 | Remedies
[3:0] - **Prevention of Communal and Targeted Violence Bill, 2011**: drafted by the **National Advisory Council**; **never passed**.
[3:1] - **National Integration Council**: chaired by the **PM**; Union ministers, CMs, eminent persons; criticised as inactive.
[3:2] - **National Foundation for Communal Harmony**: helps **child victims**; inter-faith dialogue.
[3:3] - **Home Guards**: first raised **Dec 1946**; voluntary force auxiliary to police; raised by **State governments** under State Acts.
[3:4] - **Peace committees** and **MHA Guidelines for Communal Harmony** (prevention, containment, response).
[3:5] - **Home Guards – purpose:** first raised in December 1946 to help the police control civil disturbance and communal riots.
[3:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Society G2 Part 1 p.88; app notes 2:118 sub 3
## SUB 4 | Police and Administration
[4:0] - **Sachar Committee (2005)**: Muslims behind in education and jobs; under-represented in public jobs incl. **police**.
[4:1] - **Prakash Singh v. UoI (2006)**: police reforms – State Security Commission, Police Establishment Board, Police Complaints Authority.
[4:2] - **Tehseen Poonawalla (2018)**: **nodal officers** in every district against mob violence.
