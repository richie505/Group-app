=== New Current Affairs: 5–14 Nov 2026
# Climate Indices at COP31
- **[11 Nov 2026] Climate Change Performance Index (CCPI) 2027:** by **Germanwatch** and the **NewClimate Institute**, released at **COP31, Antalya**; covers **64 countries + the EU** (over **90%** of global GHG emissions); 4 categories: **GHG emissions, renewable energy, energy use, climate policy**.
- **CCPI 2027 – India 28th** (score **60.85**, rating **'Medium'**), **down 5** from **23rd (CCPI 2026)** and from **10th (CCPI 2025)** – a third straight fall; reasons: coal dependence, losses in **energy use** and **GHG emissions**.
- **CCPI 2027 ranks:** **1st–3rd left vacant** (no country good enough); **Denmark 4th** (best); **UK 7th** – the **only G20** member rated 'High'; Morocco 8th, Chile 9th; EU 19th; Pakistan 26th; **China 56th**; host **Türkiye 53rd**; bottom: Saudi Arabia 65th, Russia 66th, **USA 67th**, **Iran 68th (last)**.
- **[05 Nov 2026] Allianz Green Transition Tracker 2026:** by **Allianz Research**; **65 economies** (81% of emissions); **India 45th** (up **5** from 50th); top **Luxembourg, Denmark, Estonia**; USA 41st, Canada 42nd, China 43rd; India's GHG emissions **fell 0.2% in 2025** to **4,508 MtCO2e** despite about **7.6% growth** – **first fall outside an economic contraction in 25 years**; globally **renewables (33.5%) overtook coal (33.2%)** in electricity generation.
# World Bank International Debt Report 2026 (7 Nov 2026)
- External debt of **developing countries** rose **5.5%** to a **record $9.4 trillion in 2025** (fastest since 2021); in **2022–2025** they paid **$436 billion more** in debt service than they received in new financing.
- Bond issuance by public entities rose nearly **35% to $251 billion** (five-year high); debt of **IDA-eligible** countries rose **8.1% to $1.3 trillion**.
- **India's external debt: $763.5 billion (2025)** vs **$717.4 billion (2024)**, about **6.4%** growth.
# QS World University Rankings: Asia 2027 (4–5 Nov 2026)
- Published by **Quacquarelli Symonds (QS, UK)**; QS Asia rankings since **2009**; **no Indian university in the top 50** for the **second year running**.
- **IIT Delhi** = India's best at **66th** (59th last year); **IISc Bangalore 79th**, **IIT Bombay 83rd**, **IIT Madras 85th**, IIT Kharagpur 90th, IIT Kanpur 97th; India's institutions in the list rose to **325** (from 294).
- **AP angle:** **SRM University-AP (Amaravati)** ranked **first in Andhra Pradesh**, among the **top 100 in Southern Asia** and top 25 private universities in India.
# Swachh Survekshan 2025-26 Awards (11 Nov 2026)
- Conferred by **President Droupadi Murmu** at **Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi**, on **11 Nov 2026**; survey by **MoHUA** (Minister **Manohar Lal**); **10th edition**; about **4,700+ ULBs** assessed; theme "**Swachhata Ki Nayi Pehel – Badhayein Haath, Karein Safai Saath**".
- **Top cleanest cities (overall):** **1. Navi Mumbai, 2. Surat, 3. Vijayawada (AP)**; **Indore** – cleanest for **8 years** – now a **Mentor City** under **Swachh Shehar Jodi**.
- **10 lakh+ population:** **1. Greater Visakhapatnam (GVMC, AP)**, 2. Jabalpur, 3. Lucknow; **3–10 lakh:** 1. **Ujjain**, 2. Chandigarh, 3. **Guntur (AP)**; **50,000–3 lakh:** 1. Dewas, 2. NDMC, 3. Sagar; **20,000–50,000:** 1. Vita (Maharashtra).
- **Swachh Shehar Jodi (mentor–mentee, new):** 1. **Ahmedabad–Sanand**, 2. **Indore–Depalpur**, 3. **Tirupati–Kuppam (AP)**; **first-time categories:** Riverfront Town (**Rewa**), Himalayan City (**Rudrapur**), Coastal Town (**Puri**, then Alappuzha, Ratnagiri).
- **AP's six award-winning ULBs:** **Greater Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Guntur, Tirupati, Rajamahendravaram** Municipal Corporations and **Kuppam** Municipality; Swachh Survekshan began in **2016** under **SBM-Urban**.
# State Rankings with an AP Angle
- **[10 Nov 2026] Access (In)Equality Index (AEI) 2026:** by the **Centre for New Economic Studies (CNES), O.P. Jindal Global University** (index since **2021**); **5 dimensions** (basic infrastructure, healthcare, education, socio-economic security, access to justice), **60 indicators, 23 categories**, equal weights; **Goa 1st (0.68)**, **Andhra Pradesh 2nd (0.66)**, **Sikkim 3rd**; AP **1st in socio-economic security** (0.69 vs Goa 0.50); AP a "**Front Runner**" though **13th in per capita income**.
- **[10 Nov 2026] Ease of Vehicle Ownership Index (EVOI):** by **IndiaTech.org** with **Consumer VOICE** (used-vehicle ownership transfer, **six metrics**); **Andhra Pradesh 1st**, Kerala 2nd, Tamil Nadu 3rd, Delhi 4th; **Uttar Pradesh last** (behind Bihar and West Bengal); pre-owned vehicle market **$36.4 bn (2024-25) → $73.5 bn (2030)**.
- **[10 Nov 2026] AP Public Health Services Performance Assessment (PHCs/UPHCs, IVRS patient feedback):** **Palnadu 1st (81.6%)**, Polavaram 81.5%, Nellore 80.7%; **Kurnool last (69.5%)**.
# Labour and Gender Reports
- **[11 Nov 2026] ICRIER – "The Participation Paradox: Women's Work and Time-Use in India (2017-18 to 2025)"** (PLFS + Time Use Survey data): working women **104 → 201 million**; female **Worker Population Ratio 22% → 38%**; a rural woman now earns about **45%** of a rural man's earnings (53% earlier); about **60%** of women outside the labour force cite **childcare/household duties**; **TUS 2024:** women spend **3.7 hours more per day** than men on domestic work.
- **[02 Nov 2026] Decent work:** India's unemployment rate fell from **6.1% (2017-18)** to **3.2% (2023-24)**, but nearly **90%** of the workforce is **informal** (**India Employment Report 2024, ILO–IHD**); decent work = **SDG 8** ("Decent Work and Economic Growth").
