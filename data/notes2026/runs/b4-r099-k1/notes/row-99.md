=== Petroleum and Natural Gas – Oilfields, Refineries, Reserves
# Firsts and oilfields
- **First petroleum detection:** Margherita (Assam). **First oil well:** **Makum**, upper Assam, **26 March 1867** (118 ft) – not Digboi.
- **Digboi** (Tinsukia, Assam) = first commercially successful oil discovery, production from **1889**; **oldest oil field** and oldest oil-producing area (Assam = oldest oil State).
- **Naharkatiya** lies about **32 km south-west of Digboi**; Naharkatia-Noonmati-Barauni = India's **first** (Asia's first cross-country) oil pipeline.
- World: first commercial oil output recorded in **Romania (1857)**; USA (Drake well) 1859.
| Field / basin | State |
|---|---|
| **Digboi, Makum, Naharkatiya, Moran-Hugrijan** | **Assam** (Brahmaputra valley) |
| **Ankleshwar** (Bharuch), **Kalol**, Mehsana, **Navagam**, Kosamba, Lunej (Cambay basin) | **Gujarat** |
| **Mumbai High** (offshore, **about 160 km** off Mumbai; found by Indian-Russian survey from 1964-65, oil struck 1974) | Arabian Sea – largest oil and **largest natural-gas producer** |
| Barmer (Mangala, Bhagyam, Aishwariya) | Rajasthan – largest onshore producer |
| **KG basin** (KG-D6 = **gas**; Rawa field) | Andhra Pradesh – crude from Rawa exported via a single buoy mooring |
- Kalol is in Gujarat, **not Assam**. Exploration: **ONGC** and **Oil India Ltd** (HPCL and IOC refine and market).
- Shale gas: Cambay basin has the largest estimate; also Assam-Arakan, Gondwana (Damodar), KG, Cauvery.
- Oil trap (anticline under cap rock), bottom to top: **water → oil → gas/air** (by density).
- India imports about **88%** of its crude. Hormuz closure (March 2026): India fell back on coal and gas and bought LNG and LPG from other suppliers.

=== Fuels – Fossil, Renewable and Commercial
# Classification
| Class | Sources | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| Renewable (replenished in a short time) | **Solar, wind**, hydro (energy of water), **geothermal (energy of the Earth)**, tidal, biomass, **biogas, biofuel**, wood | Basis of the energy transition |
| Non-renewable (finite) | **Fossil fuels – coal, petroleum (crude oil), natural gas** + nuclear fuel (uranium, thorium) | Fossil fuels formed over millions of years from buried organisms |
- **Not fossil fuels:** **solar energy, wood, uranium** (non-renewable **nuclear** fuel), biofuel, biogas. Kerosene, diesel, petrol are petroleum products (fossil-derived).
- **Ultimate source** of fossil-fuel energy = **the Sun**.
- Fossil fuels **cannot be prepared in the laboratory** from dead organisms (statement trap: "coal can be made in the lab" is false).
- Earth's **source function**: supplies water, minerals, fish stocks, **fossil fuels**.
- **Petroleum products:** petrol (gasoline), diesel, kerosene, **LPG, naphtha, bitumen**, plastics; **coal and anthracite are not** petroleum products. Thermal power plants burn **fossil fuels**; solar plants use radiation, not coal.
- **Bio-asphalt:** made from **renewable/organic waste**, not fossil-based, eco-friendly road surfacing (Budget 2011-12 duty exemption).
- **Commercial** sources = **power, coal, oil, gas, hydro-electricity, uranium** (atomic); **non-commercial** = firewood, dung, agricultural waste, **biogas**.
- Coal, crude oil, natural gas together = **fossil fuels**. Coke and producer gas are manufactured fuels.
- **First oil crisis: 1973** (OPEC quadrupled prices; 1970s-80s crises). Fossil fuels give about **75% of global GHGs and 90% of CO2**.
- Energy-transition sources = **solar and wind**; National Solar Mission is under the **NAPCC**.
> Exam angle: "not a fossil fuel" = solar / wood / uranium; "not renewable" = natural gas / petroleum; commercial vs biogas.

# Gondwana vs Tertiary
| Type | Age | Where |
|---|---|---|

- **Tamil Nadu** = largest lignite reserves (about 80%) and output (48.65% in 2024-25; Gujarat, Rajasthan next); **Jharkhand has no lignite**. India's lignite 47.36 bn t (2023).

=== Coal – Reserves, Production, Mining and Institutions
# Reserves and production
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Proved (measured) reserves, 1.4.2023 | **Jharkhand 55.7 bn t > Odisha 52.0 > Chhattisgarh 37.2**, then WB |
| Total resources, 1.4.2023 | **Odisha 94.5 bn t > Jharkhand 87.8 > Chhattisgarh 80.8** (older books: Jharkhand largest) |
| Production | Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, MP lead (2020-21: Chhattisgarh 157.7 MT, Odisha 143, Jharkhand 131.7, MP 125.7); **Odisha** is the top producer among the usual options |
- Coal meets about **55%** of primary energy and over **70%** of electricity – **the largest source of energy in India**.
- World: **China** = largest producer and consumer; India **2nd producer and consumer**, **5th in reserves**. Reserves: USA, Russia, Australia (14%), China (13%), India (11%) – China led Australia until about 2018.
- Hard coal reserves: China far ahead of India; Nepal and New Zealand negligible.
# Mining
- **Opencast** = main method. **Rat-hole mining** (Meghalaya): narrow pits dug with simple tools, by **small family groups** ("mining by the family members" – APPSC key); **banned by NGT 2014**.
- **Coal India Ltd** (1975) = world's largest coal producer; **NLC India** = Neyveli lignite.
- **Coal Controller's Organisation** (1916): main source of **coal statistics**; monitors **captive coal/lignite blocks**; hears **objections to acquisition of coal-bearing areas** – does **not** police delivery times.
- Revised **SHAKTI** (May 2025): coal through two windows.
- Indian coal: high ash (30-45%), low sulphur.
> Exam angle: "largest reserves" – Jharkhand (proved) vs Odisha (total); Chhattisgarh/Odisha in production; rat-hole = family pits.

=== Fuel Gases – Composition and Fuel Properties
| Fuel | Composition |
|---|---|
| Natural gas / **CNG** | Mainly **methane (CH4)**, 70-90%, + ethane |
| **LPG** | **Propane + butane** |
| **Biogas / gobar gas** | **Methane** 50-75% + CO2 25-50%; anaerobic digestion; gives **power + manure** |
| **Water gas** (= **syngas**) | **CO + H2** (steam on coke or hydrocarbons) |
| **Producer gas** | **CO + N2** (+ H2) |
| **Coal gas** | **H2, CH4, CO** |
| Petrol / Kerosene / Diesel | C5-C10 / C10-C16 / C15-C18 (fractional distillation of crude) |
| Hydrogen | Electrolysis or steam reforming |
- Fractional distillation: lighter fractions boil off first – **gasoline (petrol)** at the lowest temperature, then kerosene, diesel, heavy oil.
- Calorific value (kJ/kg): hydrogen 1,50,000 > LPG 55,000 > **CNG/methane 50,000** > petrol, diesel 45,000 > biogas > coal (brown coal lower) > wood > dung.
- CO2 per kWh of heat: **anthracite** (about 0.37 kg) > house coal (0.35) > LPG (about 0.23); wood logs counted nearly carbon-neutral.
- **Kerosene floats** on water because its **density is less than water's** (and it does not mix).
- **Alessandro Volta** first isolated methane (marsh gas, 1776) and exploded it with an electric spark.
- **Green hydrogen** = RE-powered electrolysis; **blue hydrogen** = gas + CCS.
> Exam angle: match fuel gas with constituents (UPSC 1999, 2004); syngas = water gas = CO + H2; LPG = propane + butane.
