## SUB 5 | Meaning, Types and Theories
[5:0] - **Deviance:** behaviour that **violates the norms** of a group or society (crime = law-breaking deviance).
[5:1] - **Youth unrest:** collective frustration among young people when society's norms seem **ineffective or harmful**.
[5:2] TABLE head: Type | Example
  [5:2.0] Persuasive | Exam postponement
  [5:2.1] Resistance | Against attendance rules
  [5:2.2] Revolutionary | Overthrow the system (AASU, Bodo)
[5:3] - **Life cycle:** **Discontent** (first) → initiation → formalisation → public support → official action.
[5:4] - **Theories:** discontent; **personal maladjustment**; **relative deprivation (Stouffer)**; **resource mobilisation** (tactics and leadership matter more than grievances).
