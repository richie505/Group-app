## SUB 2 | Caste vs Casteism
[2:0] - **Casteism:** the evil form of caste – **prejudice towards one's own caste and animosity towards other castes**; power, money, prestige held by a few castes.
[2:1] - **Kaka Kalelkar:** casteism = "an **overriding, blind and supreme group loyalty**" ignoring justice and brotherhood.
[2:2] TABLE head: Caste (institution) | Casteism (attitude)
  [2:2.0] Hereditary, endogamous group, traditional occupation | Partisan loyalty to own caste
  [2:2.1] Social structure (Ghurye) | Caste used for political, economic gain
  [2:2.2] May exist without hostility | Discrimination, vote-bank politics
[2:3] - **Ghurye's** features include restricted occupation and **endogamy** (exception: **hypergamy/anuloma**).
[2:4] - **Evil faces of caste:** untouchability, slavery-like bondage, discrimination – **hygiene** is not one.
[2:5] ~~ Sources: Rocket Indian Society p.18 l.812; app notes 2:116 sub 0
