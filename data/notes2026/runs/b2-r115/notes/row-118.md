=== Consequences
- **Partition:** 14-18 million crossed borders (largest forced migration); deaths 2-20 lakh, most historians ~10 lakh.
- **Godhra:** 59 burnt on **Sabarmati Express**, mostly kar sevaks; riots killed **1,169**; SC-appointed **SIT** investigated.
- **Ghettoisation** = moving into religiously segregated neighbourhoods; also **displacement**, loss of trust in police and courts, **polarisation**, weaker social capital.
- **Veena Das** (*Critical Events*): impact of Partition and riots on **women**.

=== Remedies
- **Prevention of Communal and Targeted Violence Bill, 2011**: drafted by the **National Advisory Council**; **never passed**.
- **National Integration Council**: chaired by the **PM**; Union ministers, CMs, eminent persons; criticised as inactive.
- **National Foundation for Communal Harmony**: helps **child victims**; inter-faith dialogue.
- **Home Guards**: first raised **Dec 1946**; voluntary force auxiliary to police; raised by **State governments** under State Acts.
- **Peace committees** and **MHA Guidelines for Communal Harmony** (prevention, containment, response).
- **Home Guards – purpose:** first raised in December 1946 to help the police control civil disturbance and communal riots.

=== Police and Administration
- **Sachar Committee (2005)**: Muslims behind in education and jobs; under-represented in public jobs incl. **police**.
- **Prakash Singh v. UoI (2006)**: police reforms – State Security Commission, Police Establishment Board, Police Complaints Authority.
- **Tehseen Poonawalla (2018)**: **nodal officers** in every district against mob violence.

=== Hate Speech Law
| Offence | IPC | BNS 2023 |
|---|---|---|
| Promoting enmity between groups | 153A | 196 |
| Outraging religious feelings | 295A | 299 |
| Statements conducing to public mischief | 505 | 353 |
- **Hate speech** is **not specifically defined** in Indian law.
- **Pravasi Bhalai Sangathan (2014)**: SC asked Law Commission to define hate speech → **267th Report (2017)**.
- **Ramji Lal Modi v. UP** upheld Sec. 295A.
- **Karnataka Bill, 2025**: **first State law** to define hate speech; **2-10 years**; organisational liability.
- **⚠ Trap:** 20.2% = conviction under 153A (NCRB); 500% = hate-speech case rise 2014-2020
