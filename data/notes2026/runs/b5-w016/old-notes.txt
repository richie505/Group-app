## SUB 1 | Machine Learning, Key Concepts and Hardware
[16:1:0] - **Machine learning** = a **branch / subset of AI**; learns from data without explicit programming (not network design, cyber forensics, full-stack).
[16:1:1] TABLE head: Type | Key point
  [16:1:1.0] Supervised | Trained on labelled data
  [16:1:1.1] Unsupervised | Unlabelled data; finds hidden patterns
  [16:1:1.2] Reinforcement | Trial and error with rewards
[16:1:2] - **RAS 2024 statements:** ML subset of AI ✓; supervised = "without labelled data" ✗; reinforcement = trial and error with rewards ✓ → **A and C**.
[16:1:3] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [16:1:3.0] Deep learning | Subset of ML using many-layered neural networks
  [16:1:3.1] Random forest | Group of decision trees; reduces overfitting
  [16:1:3.2] NLP | Machines understand and generate human language (chatbots, translation)
  [16:1:3.3] Computer vision | Interprets images – facial recognition
  [16:1:3.4] Generative AI | Creates new text, images, music
  [16:1:3.5] LLM | General-purpose model trained on massive data for many language tasks
  [16:1:3.6] Recommender system | Suggests content from past behaviour (Netflix)
  [16:1:3.7] GPU | Massively parallel – workhorse of AI training
  [16:1:3.8] FPGA | Field Programmable Gate Array – IC reconfigurable after manufacture
  [16:1:3.9] TPU | Google's AI chip
[16:1:4] - **Cloud-based machine learning tools** are bringing AI to a far broader audience.
[16:1:5] - **Data scientists** need **all** of: statistics, machine learning, data visualisation.

## SUB 2 | AI – Meaning, Pioneers and Types
[16:2:0] - **AI** = machines copying the thinking functions of human intelligence (learning, reasoning, perception) – "**computer-created intelligence**"; it augments humans by **making machines intelligent and mimicking human actions**.
[16:2:1] - **Father of AI:** **John McCarthy** – coined "artificial intelligence" for the **Dartmouth workshop (1956)**; not Turing, Babbage, Lee de Forest or Eckert.
[16:2:2] TABLE head: Item | Fact
  [16:2:2.0] Dartmouth workshop | 1956; proposed in 1955 by McCarthy, Minsky, Rochester and Shannon
  [16:2:2.1] Turing test | Alan Turing, "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" (1950); first called "the imitation game"
  [16:2:2.2] Narrow / Weak AI (ANI) | One specific task, cannot transfer across domains (Siri, Alexa, recommendations) – all AI in use today
  [16:2:2.3] General / Strong AI (AGI) | Human-level reasoning across domains – not achieved
  [16:2:2.4] Artificial Superintelligence | Hypothetical; surpasses humans in every way
[16:2:3] ~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 5 p.131-132 (types); PMF S&T Part 1 p.30 (Turing); app notes 5:16 sub 0

## SUB 6 | Ethical Concerns and Challenges
[16:6:0] - **Hawking (2014):** "The development of full AI could spell the **end of the human race**" – **Stephen Hawking**.
[16:6:1] TABLE head: Concern | Point
  [16:6:1.0] Bias | Sources: data, algorithms, people (human interpretation) – "ability" is not a source
  [16:6:1.1] Health-AI bias | Trained on urban/Western data; may miss India's social, genetic, demographic diversity
  [16:6:1.2] Deepfakes | Made with GANs and diffusion models
  [16:6:1.3] Facial recognition | Identification without consent, mass surveillance, predictive policing
  [16:6:1.4] Job displacement | Both manual and intellectual work
  [16:6:1.5] Explainability | Deep models are black boxes – decisions hard to explain
[16:6:2] - India: GERD **~0.64–0.70% of GDP**; **Economic Survey 2025-26** – small, application-specific models + a state-built **AI-OS** modelled on UPI and Aadhaar.
[16:6:3] - **14 Aug 2026:** Supreme Court sought the Centre's reply on no legal framework for high-risk AI in **welfare delivery**.

=== NOTES 5:18 | Big Data, Cloud Computing, Edge Computing and Data Centres
## SUB 0 | Cloud Computing
[18:0:0] - **Cloud computing** = hosted computing services (storage, servers, databases, software) over the internet **on demand**, pay-as-you-go.
[18:0:1] - Features: on-demand self-service, broad network access, **resource pooling** (many users share resources), **elasticity** (resources grow/shrink with demand), measured service.
[18:0:2] TABLE head: Model | User gets | Example
  [18:0:2.0] IaaS | Servers, storage, network | Amazon EC2, AWS
  [18:0:2.1] PaaS (Platform as a Service) | Platform to develop, test, deploy, run apps | Google App Engine
  [18:0:2.2] SaaS | Ready software over the web; mobile access, customisable | Gmail, Outlook
[18:0:3] - **Not a separate layer:** "**Hardware as a Service**" (comes under IaaS); **allotment of electromagnetic spectrum** is not a cloud service.
[18:0:4] - Deployment models: public, private, hybrid, community ("platform cloud" is not one).
[18:0:5] TABLE head: Storage service | Company
  [18:0:5.0] SkyDrive (now OneDrive) | Microsoft
  [18:0:5.1] S3 | Amazon Web Services
  [18:0:5.2] iCloud / Google Drive / Dropbox | Apple / Google / Dropbox
[18:0:6] - **MeghRaj (GI Cloud):** Government of India cloud for e-services on **NIC** infrastructure.
[18:0:7] ~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 5 p.67, p.69, p.100; PMF S&T Part 7 p.122; app notes 5:18 sub 1
## SUB 1 | Big Data and Data Analytics
[18:1:0] - **Big data** = large volume of **high-velocity, complex and variable** data (APPSC key).
[18:1:1] - Classic **3 Vs: Volume, Velocity, Variety**; **Veracity** and **Value** added later.
[18:1:2] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [18:1:2.0] Data mining | Finding patterns in large datasets
  [18:1:2.1] Data warehouse | Cleaned, structured data for analysis
  [18:1:2.2] Data lake | Raw data in native format
  [18:1:2.3] Metadata | Data about data
  [18:1:2.4] R | Language used for statistical computing
[18:1:3] - **APPSC 2025:** AI, quantum computing and data science improve environmental management by **analysing large datasets** to predict pollution, optimise resources and simulate climate models.
[18:1:4] - **NDGF Policy (MeitY, 2022):** India Data Management Office + India Datasets Programme. **OGD Platform** (NDSAP **2012**). IndiaAI Datasets Platform = **AI-Kosh**.
[18:1:5] - India: member of the **UN Committee of Experts on Big Data and Data Science** and its Advisory Board. **ES 2025-26:** UDISE+, AISHE, ABDM surveys + AI for health hotspots.

## SUB 2 | Data Centres
[18:2:0] - **HyperVault AI Data Centre Ltd:** **TCS + TPG** joint venture (2025) for AI-focused, sovereign data centres.
[18:2:1] - Capacity: **375 MW (2020) → 1.57 GW (2026) → ~8 GW (2030)**; **Mumbai** largest share (~52%).
[18:2:2] - **PUE** = total facility energy ÷ IT energy; **closer to 1 = better** (1.1 best). **Uptime Tier IV**: fully fault-tolerant, **~99.995%**.
[18:2:3] - **Draft Data Centre Policy (Sep 2025):** **20-year** tax holiday (not 10). Data centres use ~**1%** of global electricity.
[18:2:4] - **Visakhapatnam:** projected biggest hub (~6 GW over 10 years); Vizag–Kapuluppada corridor in **Seismic Zone II**; submarine cables, sea-breeze cooling, nearness to **Southeast Asia** (not the EU); **Google $15 billion, 1 GW AI hub** (Oct 2025); **TCS – HyperVault**.

[18:3:0] TABLE head: Model | Where | Feature
  [18:3:0.0] Cloud | Distant central data centres | Heavy storage, analytics
  [18:3:0.1] Fog | Between edge and cloud | Local aggregation
  [18:3:0.2] Edge | On / near the device | Lowest latency; saves bandwidth
[18:3:1] - **Virtualisation:** one server runs many VMs, each with its own OS. **Containers** (Docker) are lighter – **share the host OS kernel**.
[18:3:2] - **Serverless:** provider runs and scales servers, bills per use (AWS Lambda).

## SUB 0 | Electromagnetic Spectrum – Order, Uses and Wave Basics
[20:0:0] # Order of the spectrum
[20:0:1] TABLE head: Wave | Wavelength | Frequency | Main uses
  [20:0:1.0] Radio | > 1 m (longest) | < 3 × 10⁸ Hz (lowest) | AM/FM radio, TV, mobile, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, MRI; satellite links (radio family)
  [20:0:1.1] Microwave | 1 mm – 1 m | 300 MHz – 300 GHz | Satellite communication, radar, 5G, point-to-point telecom links, ovens
  [20:0:1.2] Infrared | 700 nm – 1 mm |  | TV/AC remotes, night vision, physiotherapy (muscle pain, paralysis), heaters
  [20:0:1.3] Visible | ~400–700 nm (violet shortest, red longest) |  | Vision, Li-Fi/VLC, optical fibre (some)
  [20:0:1.4] Ultraviolet | 10–400 nm |  | Sterilisation (UV lamps, semen straws, water), forgery detection on currency, vitamin D; sunscreens block UV
  [20:0:1.5] X-rays | 0.01–10 nm |  | Radiography, CT, baggage scans, cracks in metals
  [20:0:1.6] Gamma | < 0.01 nm (shortest) | highest | Radiotherapy, sterilising instruments
[20:0:2] - **Energy and frequency rise** Radio < Microwave < IR < Visible < UV < **X-ray** < **Gamma**; photon energy E = hν, so shorter wavelength = more energy (200–280 nm UV beats 280–400 nm; **blue** beats green, yellow, red).
[20:0:3] - **Decreasing frequency:** **Gamma > X-rays > UV > Radio**. Longest wavelength: **radio**; smallest among X-ray/visible/IR/microwave: **X-rays**; among radio/visible/IR/UV: **UV**.
[20:0:4] - Visible light range: **400–700 nm** (also given as **390–780 nm**; VLC uses 375–780 nm).
[20:0:5] - **Visible > X-ray in wavelength; X-ray photons > UV photons > visible photons in energy.**
[20:0:6] - **X-rays** are uncharged EM waves: **not deflected** by electric or magnetic fields; high penetration; wavelength far below visible.
[20:0:7] - **Red** is used in danger/traffic signals: **longest wavelength**, least scattered, seen from far.
[20:0:8] - **TV remote:** common remotes use **infrared** (some use radio). **Telecommunication** links: **microwaves**; **radar**: **microwaves**.
[20:0:9] # Wave basics and sound
[20:0:10] - **Wavelength** = distance between **two successive crests or troughs**.
[20:0:11] - EM waves are transverse and need no medium; **sound** is mechanical and **longitudinal** (radio, X-ray, gamma are EM).
[20:0:12] - **Supersonic waves are not electromagnetic.** **Sonic boom** = shock-wave bang from an object faster than sound (**supersonic aircraft**).
[20:0:13] - Sound travels **faster in steel (~5,000–6,000 m/s) than in air (~343 m/s)** – a listener at the rail hears a blow twice.

## SUB 1 | Optical Fibre and Total Internal Reflection
[20:1:0] # Total internal reflection (TIR)
[20:1:1] - Conditions: light goes from **denser → rarer** medium **and** angle of incidence **> critical angle**; light is reflected **with no loss of intensity**.
[20:1:2] - Refractive index: **Diamond (~2.42) > sapphire (~1.77) > dense flint glass > glass (~1.5) > fused quartz > water > air** – so TIR is possible **diamond → glass**, **diamond → air**, never air → water/glass.
[20:1:3] - Higher refractive index = **slower light** (among glass, water, air, vacuum: slowest in **glass**).
[20:1:4] TABLE head: Phenomenon / device | Principle
  [20:1:4.0] Optical fibre | TIR (successive TIRs let light follow a bent, zig-zag path)
  [20:1:4.1] Endoscope | Bundle of optical fibres, TIR
  [20:1:4.2] Brilliance of diamond | TIR (critical angle ≈ 24.4°)
  [20:1:4.3] Mirage (desert) | TIR in hot, rarer air near the ground
  [20:1:4.4] Glass beads on traffic signs | TIR (A and R both true, R explains)
  [20:1:4.5] Crack in glass seen obliquely | Looks silvery (TIR)
  [20:1:4.6] Rainbow | Dispersion + (total) internal reflection + refraction

=== NOTES 5:21 | Emerging Ict Applications – Ai, Big Data, Cloud, Blockchain, Iot and 5G
## SUB 0 | Internet of Things – Concept, Layers and Devices
[21:0:0] - **IoT = Internet of Things:** physical devices with sensors, software and connectivity that **communicate without human intervention** – smart homes, connected appliances, smart mirror, fridge that reorders groceries, car that reroutes (UPSC 2018 scenario).
[21:0:1] - **Four pillars:** **device, data, analytics, connectivity**.
[21:0:2] # Three-layer architecture
[21:0:3] TABLE head: Layer | Role
  [21:0:3.0] Perception | Sensors collect data at the physical level
  [21:0:3.1] Network | Transmits data – the wireless connection (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular, RFID)
  [21:0:3.2] Application | User services
[21:0:4] - Order from the physical level up: **Perception → Network → Application**.
[21:0:5] TABLE head: Use | Examples
  [21:0:5.0] Smart city | Traffic and air-quality monitoring, waste collection
  [21:0:5.1] Smart agriculture | Precision farming, soil monitoring
  [21:0:5.2] Wearables | Fitness trackers, health monitors
  [21:0:5.3] IoMT | Internet of Medical Things: medical devices linked to health IT systems
  [21:0:5.4] IIoT | Industrial Internet of Things
[21:0:6] # IoT devices and RFID in news
[21:0:7] - **AmbiTag** (IIT **Ropar**, AWaDH hub with ScratchNest, 2021): **USB-shaped IoT** data logger recording **ambient temperature** of perishables, vaccines, blood, organs (−40 to +80 °C, 90 days) – **not** IISc.
[21:0:8] - **RFID** = **Radio Frequency Identification**: tags + readers over **radio waves**; **active** tags have their own battery and longer range, **passive** tags are powered by the **reader's signal**.
[21:0:9] - **RFID Access Control System:** **Paradip Port** was first among major ports (Nov 2016), with **enhanced maritime security** features, as per **Ministry of Shipping** directives – so "Mumbai Port was first" is false.

## SUB 3 | Cloud Computing, Big Data and Edge
[21:3:0] - **Cloud computing:** **storing and accessing data and programs over the internet** instead of the computer's hard drive.
[21:3:1] TABLE head: Service model | What is provided
  [21:3:1.0] IaaS | Infrastructure (computing, storage)
  [21:3:1.1] PaaS | Platform to build and run applications
  [21:3:1.2] SaaS | Ready-to-use software – e.g. Outlook, Hotmail, Yahoo! Mail; users can customise the interface and change data fields; access from mobile devices (UPSC 2022: all three)
[21:3:2] - **MeghRaj = GI Cloud** (MeitY); NIC national cloud **2014**; empanelment models: Public Cloud, Virtual Private Cloud, **Government Community Cloud**.
[21:3:3] - **Big data Vs:** original 3 (2001) **Volume, Velocity, Variety**; later **Veracity** (quality, trustworthiness) and **Value** (usefulness).
[21:3:4] - **Edge computing:** processing near the source → low latency; **fog** sits between edge and cloud. Reliance pledged a nationwide edge network (India AI Summit 2026).
[21:3:5] - **AP State Data Lake:** links **RCH health with POSHAN**, and **housing with land pattas** to plug leakages.

[21:4:0] - **Features:** decentralised **distributed ledger**, **immutable**, **transparent** (records are not controlled by one body), smart contracts.
[21:4:1] - **NFTs** = unique digital assets as tokens; real-world asset tokens (land, bonds).
[21:4:2] - **Governance uses:** **land records**, pharma and agri supply chains, **e-Notary (e-Sign)**, certificates, e-voting.

[21:5:0] - 5G: latency as low as **1 millisecond**; ITU/3GPP use cases **eMBB, URLLC, mMTC** (GPRS is not one).
[21:5:1] - **Network slicing** splits one physical network into virtual networks (SDN/NFV) – it does **not merge** them.
[21:5:2] - **Bharat Telecom Stack:** **C-DOT** core + **Tejas Networks** radio + **TCS** integration; BSNL; launched by PM **27 Sep 2025**.
