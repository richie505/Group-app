## SUB 0 | Internet of Things – Concept, Layers and Devices
[0:0] - **IoT = Internet of Things:** physical devices with sensors, software and connectivity that **communicate without human intervention** – smart homes, connected appliances, smart mirror, fridge that reorders groceries, car that reroutes (UPSC 2018 scenario).
[0:1] - **Four pillars:** **device, data, analytics, connectivity**.
[0:2] # Three-layer architecture
[0:3] TABLE head: Layer | Role
  [0:3.0] Perception | Sensors collect data at the physical level
  [0:3.1] Network | Transmits data – the wireless connection (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular, RFID)
  [0:3.2] Application | User services
[0:4] - Order from the physical level up: **Perception → Network → Application**.
[0:5] TABLE head: Use | Examples
  [0:5.0] Smart city | Traffic and air-quality monitoring, waste collection
  [0:5.1] Smart agriculture | Precision farming, soil monitoring
  [0:5.2] Wearables | Fitness trackers, health monitors
  [0:5.3] IoMT | Internet of Medical Things: medical devices linked to health IT systems
  [0:5.4] IIoT | Industrial Internet of Things
[0:6] # IoT devices and RFID in news
[0:7] - **AmbiTag** (IIT **Ropar**, AWaDH hub with ScratchNest, 2021): **USB-shaped IoT** data logger recording **ambient temperature** of perishables, vaccines, blood, organs (−40 to +80 °C, 90 days) – **not** IISc.
[0:8] - **RFID** = **Radio Frequency Identification**: tags + readers over **radio waves**; **active** tags have their own battery and longer range, **passive** tags are powered by the **reader's signal**.
[0:9] - **RFID Access Control System:** **Paradip Port** was first among major ports (Nov 2016), with **enhanced maritime security** features, as per **Ministry of Shipping** directives – so "Mumbai Port was first" is false.

## SUB 1 | Metaverse, AI Tools and Facial Recognition
[1:0] - **Metaverse:** a shared immersive **virtual world for people's interaction**; "an **interoperable network of 3D virtual worlds** accessed simultaneously by millions, with **property rights over virtual items**" (UPSC 2024); built on AR and VR.
[1:1] - **Kiyaverse** (Kiya.ai, June 2022): **India's first banking metaverse**.
[1:2] - **Facial recognition:** **NAFRS** (National Automated Facial Recognition System) is being built by **NCRB** (MHA), **not NIC**; main concern is **privacy/accuracy**, not "cannot identify in real time" → APPSC 2023: **neither** statement correct.
[1:3] - **ASTR** (AI and Facial Recognition powered Solution for Telecom SIM Subscriber Verification): developed by the **Department of Telecommunications** to catch SIMs taken on fake documents.
[1:4] - **Geofencing:** virtual geographic boundary that triggers an action on a device; credited to **Michael Dimino** (Getting, Gladys West, Easton = GPS pioneers).
[1:5] TABLE head: AI tool | Developer | Fact
  [1:5.0] ChatGPT | OpenAI | Launched 30 Nov 2022
  [1:5.1] Krutrim | Ola (Bhavish Aggarwal) | Indian-language chatbot
  [1:5.2] Hanooman | SML India (BharatGPT group led by IIT Bombay) | May 2024
[1:6] - **AI Sowing App** (2016, **Microsoft + ICRISAT + AP**): **groundnut**, Devanakonda mandal, Kurnool; ~**30%** higher yield.
[1:7] - **I4C's NCAP** uses AI and big data to analyse **crime trends**.

## SUB 3 | Cloud Computing, Big Data and Edge
[3:0] - **Cloud computing:** **storing and accessing data and programs over the internet** instead of the computer's hard drive.
[3:1] TABLE head: Service model | What is provided
  [3:1.0] IaaS | Infrastructure (computing, storage)
  [3:1.1] PaaS | Platform to build and run applications
  [3:1.2] SaaS | Ready-to-use software – e.g. Outlook, Hotmail, Yahoo! Mail; users can customise the interface and change data fields; access from mobile devices (UPSC 2022: all three)
[3:2] - **MeghRaj = GI Cloud** (MeitY); NIC national cloud **2014**; empanelment models: Public Cloud, Virtual Private Cloud, **Government Community Cloud**.
[3:3] - **Big data Vs:** original 3 (2001) **Volume, Velocity, Variety**; later **Veracity** (quality, trustworthiness) and **Value** (usefulness).
[3:4] - **Edge computing:** processing near the source → low latency; **fog** sits between edge and cloud. Reliance pledged a nationwide edge network (India AI Summit 2026).
[3:5] - **AP State Data Lake:** links **RCH health with POSHAN**, and **housing with land pattas** to plug leakages.
[3:6] ~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 5 p.67-68, p.100-101; app notes 5:21 sub 5-7
## SUB 4 | Blockchain
[4:0] - **Features:** decentralised **distributed ledger**, **immutable**, **transparent** (records are not controlled by one body), smart contracts.
[4:1] - **NFTs** = unique digital assets as tokens; real-world asset tokens (land, bonds).

## SUB 5 | 5G, 6G and the Bharat Telecom Stack
[5:0] - 5G: latency as low as **1 millisecond**; ITU/3GPP use cases **eMBB, URLLC, mMTC** (GPRS is not one).
[5:1] - **Network slicing** splits one physical network into virtual networks (SDN/NFV) – it does **not merge** them.
[5:2] - **Bharat Telecom Stack:** **C-DOT** core + **Tejas Networks** radio + **TCS** integration; BSNL; launched by PM **27 Sep 2025**.
[5:3] - **IIITDM Kurnool** topped DoT's 5G labs ranking (July 2026); Airtel 'Priority Postpaid' raised a **net neutrality** debate.
[5:4] - **Wi-Fi 7:** IEEE **802.11be**, Multi-Link Operation, up to **46 Gbps**. **6G:** early 2030s; Bharat 6G Alliance **2023**.
