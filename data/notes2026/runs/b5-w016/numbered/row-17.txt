## SUB 1 | Quantum Computing and Supercomputers
[1:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [1:0.0] Qubit | Basic unit of quantum information – context of quantum computing (UPSC 2022)
  [1:0.1] Superposition | A quantum system can be in multiple states at once until measured (qubit = 0 and 1)
  [1:0.2] Entanglement | Links qubits' states regardless of distance
  [1:0.3] Decoherence | Loss of quantum state to the environment – main barrier
  [1:0.4] Shor's algorithm / Q-Day | Can break public-key cryptography; Q-Day = when it becomes able to
[1:1] - **Applications:** **cryptography**, **drug discovery**, **financial modelling**, logistics, materials – **not desktop publishing**.
[1:2] TABLE head: Machine | Fact
  [1:2.0] Micius (QUESS), China, 16 Aug 2016 | World's first quantum communication satellite
  [1:2.1] Google Sycamore (2019) | Quantum supremacy – a set task in ~200 s
  [1:2.2] Jiuzhang (China, 2020) | Photonic supremacy machine
  [1:2.3] Fugaku (Japan) | World's fastest supercomputer 2020-21 (exam key for "least time to calculate")
  [1:2.4] Pratyush (IITM Pune, 2018) | India's fastest supercomputer as of March 2018
[1:3] - **National Quantum Mission:** approved **19 Apr 2023**; **₹6,003.65 crore** (2023-24 to 2030-31); **50–1,000 physical qubits** in 8 years; **four thematic hubs** – computing, communication, sensing & metrology, materials & devices (not quantum biology).
[1:4] - **2025** = UN **International Year of Quantum Science and Technology** (centenary of quantum mechanics); **World Quantum Day = 14 April**.
[1:5] - 2026 items: Raman Research Institute – a timed local "quantum flip" delays **entanglement sudden death**; **Microsoft Majorana 2** – qubit stability 20–60 s.

## SUB 2 | Turing Test and AI Milestones
[2:0] - **Turing test:** Alan Turing (1950, *Computing Machinery and Intelligence*) – checks whether a machine's replies can be told apart from a human's; original name **"the Imitation Game"** (not LISP or the Halting Problem).
[2:1] - **Term "AI":** **John McCarthy**, Dartmouth **1956**. **Colossus** (1943–45): Tommy Flowers.
[2:2] - **Robotics** = technology most closely associated with "artificial intelligence" (exam key).
