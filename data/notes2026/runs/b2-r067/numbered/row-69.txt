## SUB 1 | Removal and Suspension – Article 317
[1:0] # Grounds (President removes)
[1:1] - 1. Adjudged **insolvent**.
[1:2] - 2. **Paid employment** outside the duties of office during the term.
[1:3] - 3. **Infirmity of mind or body** – in the **President's opinion**.
[1:4] - 4. **Misbehaviour** – only after the President refers the matter to the **Supreme Court**, and the Court upholds it after an enquiry; its **advice is binding**.
[1:5] - **Misbehaviour (Art. 317(4))** includes being **interested in a government contract** or sharing in its profit.
[1:6] - **Parliament has no role** (unlike SC/HC judges and the CAG, who are removed on an address of both Houses).
[1:7] - **SPSC:** appointed by the Governor, **removed only by the President** after the **Supreme Court** enquiry (not the Governor, not a HC enquiry, not impeachment in the Vidhan Sabha); the **Governor** may suspend during the enquiry.
[1:8] - Being found guilty in a **civil case** is **not** a ground; "inquiry committee headed by the CJI" and "misconduct in the President's opinion" are loose versions of the real grounds.
[1:9] > Asked as: who removes a UPSC / SPSC member (7 PYQs); Article of removal (317); role of Parliament (none – UPSC 2020); grounds statements.

## SUB 2 | Constitutional Status – Part XIV, Articles 315-323
[2:0] - UPSC is an **independent constitutional body**, directly created by the **Constitution** (Art. 315) – not by an Act, ordinance or Presidential order; the **central recruiting agency** of India.
[2:1] - SPSCs are equally constitutional; the **UPSC has nothing to do with the SPSCs** (no supervision, no guidelines).
[2:2] - **Election Commission, Finance Commission and UPSC** all have **constitutional status**.
[2:3] - Provisions: **Part XIV, Articles 315-323** (not Part XIII, XVI or Arts 324-329).
[2:4] TABLE head: Article | Content
  [2:4.0] 315 | PSCs for the Union and the States (incl. JSPSC)
  [2:4.1] 316 | Appointment and term of members
  [2:4.2] 317 | Removal and suspension
  [2:4.3] 318 | Regulations on conditions of service
  [2:4.4] 319 | Bar on holding office after ceasing to be a member
  [2:4.5] 320 | Functions
  [2:4.6] 321 | Power to extend functions (by law)
  [2:4.7] 322 | Expenses – charged on the Consolidated Fund
  [2:4.8] 323 | Reports – UPSC to President, SPSC to Governor
[2:5] - **Sources of UPSC functions:** the **Constitution** (Art. 320), **Parliamentary laws** (Art. 321), **executive rules and orders** (e.g. Exemption from Consultation Regulations, 1958) and **conventions**.

## SUB 3 | Joint State PSC and UPSC Serving a State
[3:0] - **JSPSC (Art. 315(2)):** two or more States **agree**, their **Legislatures pass resolutions**, and **Parliament creates it by law** – a **statutory**, not constitutional, body. Example: **Punjab and Haryana** (once).
[3:1] - Chairman and members **appointed, suspended and removed by the President** (not the Governors, not a committee of Governors); resign to the **President**; term 6 years or **62**.
[3:2] - **Art. 315(4):** the **UPSC** may serve all or any needs of a State **on the request of the Governor** with the **approval of the President**.
[3:3] - On the request of two or more States the UPSC assists in **joint recruitment** schemes.
[3:4] > Asked as: who provides a JSPSC (Parliament – 3 PYQs); who appoints its Chairman (President – 3); UPSC serving a State (Governor's request + President's approval – 2).

## SUB 4 | Annual Report and Nature of Advice – Article 323
[4:0] TABLE head: Body | Report to | Laid before
  [4:0.0] UPSC | President (annually) | both Houses, with a memorandum on advice not accepted
  [4:0.1] SPSC (e.g. Rajasthan PSC) | Governor | State Legislature, with a similar memorandum
  [4:0.2] JSPSC | each concerned Governor | his State Legislature
[4:1] - **Non-acceptance** of UPSC advice: every case needs approval of the **Appointments Committee of the Cabinet**.
[4:2] - Advice is **advisory, not binding**; consultation is **directory, not mandatory** – action without consulting is **not invalid**; **selection confers no right to the post** (Supreme Court).
[4:3] - **Art. 321:** functions extended only by an **Act of Parliament** (State Legislature for an SPSC) – may cover **local bodies, corporate bodies, public institutions**.
[4:4] - **CVC** (1964; statutory 2003) is also consulted on disciplinary matters; the UPSC has the edge. SPSC = "**watchdog of the merit system**" in the State.
[4:5] > Asked as: UPSC annual report to (President – 4 PYQs); how often (annually); SPSC report to (Governor).

## SUB 5 | Functions and Limitations – Article 320
[5:0] # Consulted on
[5:1] - Conducts **examinations** for **All-India, Central and Group-A services** and services of Union territories (SPSC: State services).
[5:2] - **Methods of recruitment**, framing and amendment of **recruitment rules**.
[5:3] - Principles of **appointment, promotion and transfer from one service to another**; suitability of candidates.
[5:4] - **Disciplinary matters** affecting civil servants; claims for **legal expenses**; **pension** for injuries on duty; any matter **referred by the President**.
[5:5] - **Temporary appointments of more than one year**; extension of service / re-employment.
[5:6] # NOT consulted
[5:7] - **Reservations** of posts for backward classes (**manner of reservation**); claims of SCs/STs.
[5:8] - **Temporary posts of less than one year**; **Group C/D** posts; chairpersons of boards/tribunals; **heads of diplomatic missions**.
[5:9] - **Classification, pay and training** of services (DoPT).
[5:10] - Actual **transfers** of civil servants (only the *principles* of transfer are referred).
[5:11] - **Departmental examinations** are not an Art. 320 function.
[5:12] - President (Governor for an SPSC) may exclude posts by **regulations**, laid before Parliament/Legislature for **14 days**.
[5:13] > Asked as: which is NOT a function (manner of reservation; departmental exams; transfers – 3 PYQs).

## SUB 6 | Independence Safeguards and Post-Retirement Bar – Article 319
[6:0] - Removal only in the manner and on the grounds in the Constitution (**security of tenure**).
[6:1] - **Conditions of service** cannot be varied to the member's disadvantage after appointment.
[6:2] - **Expenses** charged on the **Consolidated Fund** (Art. 322) – not voted.
[6:3] - **No reappointment** to the same office (no second term).
[6:4] TABLE head: Office (on ceasing to hold it) | May later hold
  [6:4.0] UPSC Chairman | No further employment under GoI or any State (a Governor's post is not "employment" – A.R. Kidwai, 1979)
  [6:4.1] UPSC member | Chairman of the UPSC or of an SPSC only
  [6:4.2] SPSC Chairman | UPSC Chairman/member, or Chairman of another SPSC
  [6:4.3] SPSC member | UPSC Chairman/member, or Chairman of the same or another SPSC
[6:5] - A former SPSC member can get **no other government employment** (e.g. under the Government of India).

## SUB 8 | Andhra Pradesh Public Service Commission
[8:0] - **APPSC** came into existence on **1 November 1956** with the formation of Andhra Pradesh, merging the **Andhra PSC (1953)** and the **Hyderabad PSC**.
[8:1] - **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 (s.83):** the existing PSC becomes the **PSC of AP**; a separate PSC to be constituted for **Telangana** under Art. 315; until then the **UPSC**, with the President's approval, may serve Telangana; the sitting Chairman and members continue with **AP** – they were **not divided on population basis**.
[8:2] - HQ moved to **Vijayawada**; strength **Chairman + 9 Members** (enhanced 1994); appointed by the **Governor of AP**, removed by the **President**; term 6 years / **62**.
[8:3] - Also conducts **lower judicial service** exams (SPSC consulted under **Art. 234**).
[8:4] - **Chairperson:** retired IPS officer **A.R. Anuradha** (**October 2024**), after **Gautam Sawang** resigned.
[8:5] > Asked as: year APPSC established (1956 – 2 PYQs); APRA 2014 wrong statement.
