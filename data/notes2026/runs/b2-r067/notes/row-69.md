=== Appointment, Composition and Term – UPSC, SPSC, JSPSC
| Point | UPSC | SPSC | JSPSC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Appointed by | **President** | **Governor** (Art. 316(1)) | **President** |
| Strength and service conditions fixed by | **President** (usually **9-11** incl. Chairman) | **Governor** | **President** |
| Term | **6 years or age 65** | **6 years or age 62** | **6 years or age 62** |
| Resigns to | President | **Governor** | **President** |
| Removed by | President | **President (not Governor)** | President |
| Suspended during SC inquiry by | President | **Governor** | President |
| Annual report to | President | Governor | **each concerned Governor** |
- The Constitution does **not fix** the number of members; **at least half** must have held office under the GoI or a State Government for **10 years**.
- **Acting Chairman** (Art. 316(1A), 15th Amendment 1963): a member appointed by the **President** (Governor for an SPSC) when the Chairman's office is vacant – **not** automatically the senior-most member.
- **Conditions of service** of UPSC members are determined by the **President** (Art. 318) – not by Parliament or the CJI.
- SPSC age limit was **60**, raised to **62** by the **41st Amendment (1976)**.
- **MPPSC:** maximum **five** members (MPPSC 2021 key); the 1973 Regulations as amended read "not more than seven".
- Compare: **CJI and SC judges retire at 65**; **High Court judges at 62**.

=== Removal and Suspension – Article 317
# Grounds (President removes)
- 1. Adjudged **insolvent**.
- 2. **Paid employment** outside the duties of office during the term.
- 3. **Infirmity of mind or body** – in the **President's opinion**.
- 4. **Misbehaviour** – only after the President refers the matter to the **Supreme Court**, and the Court upholds it after an enquiry; its **advice is binding**.
- **Misbehaviour (Art. 317(4))** includes being **interested in a government contract** or sharing in its profit.
- **Parliament has no role** (unlike SC/HC judges and the CAG, who are removed on an address of both Houses).
- **SPSC:** appointed by the Governor, **removed only by the President** after the **Supreme Court** enquiry (not the Governor, not a HC enquiry, not impeachment in the Vidhan Sabha); the **Governor** may suspend during the enquiry.
- Being found guilty in a **civil case** is **not** a ground; "inquiry committee headed by the CJI" and "misconduct in the President's opinion" are loose versions of the real grounds.
> Asked as: who removes a UPSC / SPSC member (7 PYQs); Article of removal (317); role of Parliament (none – UPSC 2020); grounds statements.

=== Constitutional Status – Part XIV, Articles 315-323
- UPSC is an **independent constitutional body**, directly created by the **Constitution** (Art. 315) – not by an Act, ordinance or Presidential order; the **central recruiting agency** of India.
- SPSCs are equally constitutional; the **UPSC has nothing to do with the SPSCs** (no supervision, no guidelines).
- **Election Commission, Finance Commission and UPSC** all have **constitutional status**.
- Provisions: **Part XIV, Articles 315-323** (not Part XIII, XVI or Arts 324-329).
| Article | Content |
|---|---|
| **315** | PSCs for the Union and the States (incl. JSPSC) |
| 316 | Appointment and term of members |
| **317** | Removal and suspension |
| **318** | Regulations on conditions of service |
| **319** | Bar on holding office after ceasing to be a member |
| **320** | Functions |
| **321** | Power to extend functions (by law) |
| **322** | **Expenses** – charged on the Consolidated Fund |
| **323** | **Reports** – UPSC to President, SPSC to Governor |
- **Sources of UPSC functions:** the **Constitution** (Art. 320), **Parliamentary laws** (Art. 321), **executive rules and orders** (e.g. Exemption from Consultation Regulations, 1958) and **conventions**.

=== Joint State PSC and UPSC Serving a State
- **JSPSC (Art. 315(2)):** two or more States **agree**, their **Legislatures pass resolutions**, and **Parliament creates it by law** – a **statutory**, not constitutional, body. Example: **Punjab and Haryana** (once).
- Chairman and members **appointed, suspended and removed by the President** (not the Governors, not a committee of Governors); resign to the **President**; term 6 years or **62**.
- **Art. 315(4):** the **UPSC** may serve all or any needs of a State **on the request of the Governor** with the **approval of the President**.
- On the request of two or more States the UPSC assists in **joint recruitment** schemes.
> Asked as: who provides a JSPSC (Parliament – 3 PYQs); who appoints its Chairman (President – 3); UPSC serving a State (Governor's request + President's approval – 2).

=== Annual Report and Nature of Advice – Article 323
| Body | Report to | Laid before |
|---|---|---|
| **UPSC** | **President** (annually) | both Houses, with a **memorandum** on advice not accepted |
| **SPSC** (e.g. Rajasthan PSC) | **Governor** | State Legislature, with a similar memorandum |
| JSPSC | each concerned Governor | his State Legislature |
- **Non-acceptance** of UPSC advice: every case needs approval of the **Appointments Committee of the Cabinet**.
- Advice is **advisory, not binding**; consultation is **directory, not mandatory** – action without consulting is **not invalid**; **selection confers no right to the post** (Supreme Court).
- **Art. 321:** functions extended only by an **Act of Parliament** (State Legislature for an SPSC) – may cover **local bodies, corporate bodies, public institutions**.
- **CVC** (1964; statutory 2003) is also consulted on disciplinary matters; the UPSC has the edge. SPSC = "**watchdog of the merit system**" in the State.
> Asked as: UPSC annual report to (President – 4 PYQs); how often (annually); SPSC report to (Governor).

=== Functions and Limitations – Article 320
# Consulted on
- Conducts **examinations** for **All-India, Central and Group-A services** and services of Union territories (SPSC: State services).
- **Methods of recruitment**, framing and amendment of **recruitment rules**.
- Principles of **appointment, promotion and transfer from one service to another**; suitability of candidates.
- **Disciplinary matters** affecting civil servants; claims for **legal expenses**; **pension** for injuries on duty; any matter **referred by the President**.
- **Temporary appointments of more than one year**; extension of service / re-employment.
# NOT consulted
- **Reservations** of posts for backward classes (**manner of reservation**); claims of SCs/STs.
- **Temporary posts of less than one year**; **Group C/D** posts; chairpersons of boards/tribunals; **heads of diplomatic missions**.
- **Classification, pay and training** of services (DoPT).
- Actual **transfers** of civil servants (only the *principles* of transfer are referred).
- **Departmental examinations** are not an Art. 320 function.
- President (Governor for an SPSC) may exclude posts by **regulations**, laid before Parliament/Legislature for **14 days**.
> Asked as: which is NOT a function (manner of reservation; departmental exams; transfers – 3 PYQs).

=== Independence Safeguards and Post-Retirement Bar – Article 319
- Removal only in the manner and on the grounds in the Constitution (**security of tenure**).
- **Conditions of service** cannot be varied to the member's disadvantage after appointment.
- **Expenses** charged on the **Consolidated Fund** (Art. 322) – not voted.
- **No reappointment** to the same office (no second term).
| Office (on ceasing to hold it) | May later hold |
|---|---|
| UPSC Chairman | **No** further employment under GoI or any State (a Governor's post is not "employment" – A.R. Kidwai, 1979) |
| UPSC member | **Chairman of the UPSC or of an SPSC** only |
| SPSC Chairman | UPSC Chairman/member, or Chairman of another SPSC |
| SPSC member | UPSC Chairman/member, or Chairman of the same or another SPSC |
- A former SPSC member can get **no other government employment** (e.g. under the Government of India).
> Asked as: employment open to a retired UPSC member (UPSC Chairman).

=== Andhra Pradesh Public Service Commission
- **APPSC** came into existence on **1 November 1956** with the formation of Andhra Pradesh, merging the **Andhra PSC (1953)** and the **Hyderabad PSC**.
- **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 (s.83):** the existing PSC becomes the **PSC of AP**; a separate PSC to be constituted for **Telangana** under Art. 315; until then the **UPSC**, with the President's approval, may serve Telangana; the sitting Chairman and members continue with **AP** – they were **not divided on population basis**.
- HQ moved to **Vijayawada**; strength **Chairman + 9 Members** (enhanced 1994); appointed by the **Governor of AP**, removed by the **President**; term 6 years / **62**.
- Also conducts **lower judicial service** exams (SPSC consulted under **Art. 234**).
- **Chairperson:** retired IPS officer **A.R. Anuradha** (**October 2024**), after **Gautam Sawang** resigned.
> Asked as: year APPSC established (1956 – 2 PYQs); APRA 2014 wrong statement.
