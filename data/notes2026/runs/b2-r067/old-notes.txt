=== NOTES 2:67 | Regional Parties in India and in Andhra Pradesh – Rise, Role and Impact
## SUB 0 | Rise of Regional Parties
[67:0:0] # Causes
[67:0:1] - **Regional economic neglect** (not uniform development).
[67:0:2] - **National parties' failure** to accommodate State leaders.
[67:0:3] - **Identity**: language, caste, region – e.g. TDP's "**Telugu atma gauravam**".
[67:0:4] - After **1989**: coalition arithmetic raised the weight of regional parties.
[67:0:5] - Roots of regionalism: **historical, geographical, linguistic (22 scheduled languages), religious, ethnic**.
[67:0:6] - Strong regional parties grow from regional aspirations (true, but this does **not** explain why India has a written Constitution).
[67:0:7] # Andhra Social Base
[67:0:8] - In the **Justice Party** era, non-Brahmin agrarian castes – **Reddys, Kammas, Kapus, Velamas** – awakened politically; this became the base for Andhra regional parties.

## SUB 3 | Telugu Desam Party – Founding and Firsts
[67:3:0] # Founding and First Victory
[67:3:1] - Founded by **NTR in 1982** on **Telugu self-respect (Telugu atma gauravam)** against perceived slights from the Centre.
[67:3:2] - Won power within **nine months** – **January 1983** Assembly election – one of the fastest rises of any party.
[67:3:3] - Ideology: no clear ideology beyond **Telugu pride and welfare populism**.
[67:3:4] # End of Continuous Congress Rule in AP
[67:3:5] - The **1983** election ended Congress's **unbroken rule since the State's formation (1956)**; the winner was the **TDP**.
[67:3:6] - AP then became bipolar: **Congress vs TDP**; since **2014**: **TDP vs YSRCP**.
[67:3:7] # National Role
[67:3:8] - **8th Lok Sabha (1984)**: TDP with **30 seats** = **main opposition party** – the **first regional party** to reach that position.
[67:3:9] - **1988**: **NTR** = **chairman of the National Front**, which formed the **V.P. Singh** government in **1989**.
[67:3:10] - **30 April 2002**: TDP (backing NDA from outside) **abstained** on the opposition censure motion on the Gujarat riots in the Lok Sabha.
[67:3:11] - **2018**: TDP **left the NDA** over **Special Category Status** for residuary AP.
[67:3:12] - Now: TDP leads AP's ruling coalition with **BJP support**.
[67:3:13] 1982 founded → Jan 1983 power → 1984: 30 LS seats, main opposition → 1988 National Front chairman → 2018 left NDA.
