=== Babur's Battles – Panipat I, Khanwa, Chanderi, Ghaghra
| Battle | Year | Opponent | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Panipat I** | **21 April 1526** | **Ibrahim Lodi** (last Delhi Sultan) | Lodis shattered; **Delhi and Agra** + Lodi treasure |
| **Khanwa** | **16 March 1527** | **Rana Sanga** of Mewar + Mahmud Lodi + Hasan Khan Mewati | Rajput power broken; title **Ghazi** |
| **Chanderi** | **1528** | **Medini Rai** (Malwa, ally of Sanga) | Malwa secured; Jauhar |
| **Ghaghra** | **1529** | **Afghans** of Mahmud Lodi + **Nusrat Shah of Bengal** | Suzerainty over Bihar |
| Chausa | 1539 | Humayun vs Sher Shah | Sher Shah |
| Kannauj (Bilgram) | 1540 | Humayun vs Sher Shah | Sher Shah |
| Panipat II | 1556 | Akbar (Bairam Khan) vs **Hemu** | Akbar |
| Samugarh | 1658 | Aurangzeb vs Dara | Aurangzeb |
| Haldighati | 1576 | Akbar (Man Singh) vs **Maharana Pratap** | Akbar |
- **Chronology:** Panipat I → Khanwa → Chanderi → Ghaghra (**II, IV, III, I** – UKPCS 2024); Khanwa → Ghaghra → Chausa → Samugarh (**II, I, III, IV** – CGPSC 2008).
- **Wrong pair:** "Second Battle of Panipat – Tipu Sultan and Marathas" ✗ (it was **Akbar vs Hemu**, 1556; Third Panipat 1761 = Abdali vs Marathas).
- **Khanwa:** **Rana Sanga** was defeated (UPPCS 2004; Uttarakhand 2010; UDA 2010) – not Rana Pratap, Man Singh, Hemu or Uday Singh; Babur declared **jihad** here (UPPCS 2009), **renounced wine**, remitted the **tamgha** for Muslims, took the title **Ghazi**; trenches and barricades against Sanga's elephants.
- **Khanwa more decisive than Panipat (UPSC 2001 ×2):** A ✓, R ✓ and **R explains A** – Rana Sanga was a more formidable foe than Ibrahim Lodi; Khanwa "broke the back of Rajput power" and **set up Mughal rule on the path of expansion** – the "real beginning" of the empire (APPSC G1 2008 key: **Kanwah**) – Panipat gave only Delhi and Agra.
- **Why Babur won:** a **skilled combination of artillery and cavalry** (APPSC HWO) – **tulughma** (Uzbek flanking) + **araba / Rumi** carts shielding the guns; artillery under **Ustad Ali and Mustafa**; Babur's army ~**12,000** vs Ibrahim's ~1,00,000; **no war elephants** on Babur's side.
- **Panipat I** = first large-scale use of **field artillery** in North India; but **firearms were not new** – the **Bahmanis and Vijayanagara** had used them earlier.
- **Babur's arrival (UPSC 2015):** gunpowder ✗ (known before), arch and dome ✗ (Sultanate), **Timurid dynasty** ✓ → **3 only**; the State PSC variant with "introduction of **cannons** in warfare" keys **3 and 4**.
- Invited by **Daulat Khan Lodi** (governor of Punjab), **Alam Khan** (Ibrahim's uncle) and **Rana Sanga**; nickname **Qalandar** for sharing the Lodi treasure.

=== Babur – Origin, Name and Titles
- **Name:** **Zahir-ud-din Muhammad** (APPSC G1); "**Babur**" = **tiger** – he took the name Babur (CGPSC 2003); not Abdul Raheem, Umar Sheikh (his father) or Daulat Khan.
- **Born 14 Feb 1483** at **Farghana (Ferghana)**, Central Asia (CDS 2001) – not Khiva, Khorasan or Seistan; ruler of Farghana at 12 (1494).
- **Descent:** **Timur** (father's side) and **Chingiz Khan** (mother's side).
- **Mughals were Turks** – **Chagatai Turks**, named after **Chagatai, second son of Chingiz Khan** (APPSC G1 2002; UDA 2010) – not Mongols, Afghans, Persians or Arabs; they called themselves **Timurids**.
- **Title Padshah:** first adopted at **Kabul** (taken **1504**; title in 1507), dropping the ancestral "Mirza" (UPPCS 2015) – not Ferghana, Delhi or Samarkand (held only 8 months, 1501).
- **Founder of Mughal rule in India:** **Babur** (1526) (SSC 2017).
- **Contemporaries:** **Krishnadevaraya** of Vijayanagara (praised by Babur as the most powerful ruler in India), Sultan Quli of Golconda, Rana Sanga; the Mughals **did not interfere in the Deccan** in Babur's time.
~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval 04 p.80 (Zahiruddin Muhammad; Chagatai Turks), p.82 (Farghana 1494, Kabul 1504); NextGen Indian History - APPSC 04 p.20 (born in Farghana); app notes 1:127 (sub 0)

=== Babur's Empire, Coins and Buildings
- **Empire at his death:** **Kabul**, **Punjab** and modern **Uttar Pradesh** (Delhi, Agra, up to Bihar's border) – **not modern Rajasthan** or Bengal → **I, II and III** (UKPSC 2003 ×2); **Badakhshan** given to **Humayun**; ruled through **jagirs** and **paraganas**; no administrative system (Mansabdari = **Akbar**).
- **Death:** **Agra, December 1530**; first buried at Agra, later moved to **Bagh-e-Babur, Kabul** (SSC 2009) – Kabul is the trap.
- **Coin:** silver **Shahrukhi** (Timurid style) – Babur (UPRO 2017); also copper coins.
- **Babri Masjid, Ayodhya (1528)** – built by **Mir Baqi**, Babur's commander (UPPSC 1992) – not Babur himself.
- **Buildings and gardens:** **Kabuli Bagh mosque** (Panipat), Sambhal mosque; **charbagh** gardens – **Ram Bagh (Aram Bagh), Agra** = first Mughal garden.
~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval 04 p.84 (died in Agra 1530, Bagh-e Babur; charbagh; Kabuli Bagh), p.85 (Babri Masjid – Mir Baqi); app notes 1:127 (sub 5, 7)

=== Tuzuk-i-Baburi (Baburnama)
- Babur's memoirs, written in **Turki (Chagatai Turkish)** (State PSC) – not Persian, Arabic or Urdu.
- **A–R (IAS 1993):** "Babur wrote his memoirs in Turki" ✓; "Turki was the official language of the Mughal court" ✗ – the court language was **Persian** → **A true, R false**.
- **Translations:** Persian (1589) under Akbar by **Abdur Rahim Khan-i-Khanan**; English first by **Leyden and Erskine** (1826; "Leyden and Ruskin" in the UPRO 2017 options), later **Annette Beveridge** (from the Turki).
- **India in the Baburnama:** **five Muslim and two Hindu** powers – the Hindu ones **Vijayanagara and Mewar** (UPPCS 1995) – not Orissa, Gujarat or Kashmir.
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC 04 p.20 ("Tuzuk-i-Baburi" in Turkish), APPSC 05 p.14 (Baburnama in Turkish); PMF Ancient & Medieval 04 p.84-85; app notes 1:127 (sub 6)
