## SUB 1 | Classical Dances – Kuchipudi and the Eight Forms
[1:0] - **Kuchipudi** = classical dance of **Andhra Pradesh** (MPPCS 1995; UKPCS 2002) – named after **Kuchipudi (Kuchelapuram) village, Krishna district**; shaped by **Siddhendra Yogi**; **Abul Hasan Tana Shah** (last Qutb Shahi) gifted the village to the performers; popular shows Bhama Kalapam, Usha Parinayam.
[1:1] - **Kuchipudi vs Bharatanatyam (IAS 2012):** dancers **speak dialogue** in Kuchipudi ✓; **Tarangam** – dancing on the **rim of a brass plate** – is a **Kuchipudi** feature, not Bharatanatyam ✗ → **only 1**.
[1:2] - Kuchipudi = **dance-drama** tradition (Bhagavata Mela, male Brahmin troupes); Bharatanatyam = solo temple-dance tradition; **Lakumadevi**, court dancer of **Kumaragiri Reddy**, was famed for Bharatanatyam.
[1:3] - **Solo dance (IAS 1997):** **Mohiniyattam** (Kerala; women) – Kuchipudi, Bharatanatyam and Odissi are also staged by groups.

[1:6] - **Sonal Mansingh** – **Odissi** (also Bharatanatyam); **Padma Bhushan 1992**, Padma Vibhushan 2003 – not Birju Maharaj (Kathak), Mallika Sarabhai or Yamini Krishnamurthy.
[1:7] - **Uday Shankar** – blended Indian classical dance with **Western ballet / European theatre** – pioneer of modern Indian dance.

## SUB 5 | Festivals of Andhra Pradesh
[5:0] - **Ugadi** = **Telugu New Year** (Chaitra), in AP, Karnataka and Telangana (APPSC JL) – not Dasara, Sankranti or "Panduga".
[5:1] - **Sankranti** (January harvest): Bhogi → Sankranti → **Kanuma** (day of **thanksgiving to cattle and birds**, APPSC Endowment 2022); Gangireddulaata, **Kolla Pandalu** (cockfights), Pottella Pandalu (ram fights).
[5:2] - **Vinayaka Chavithi** – Vinayaka is worshipped with **21 kinds of leaves** (Ekavimsati Patra Puja) (APPSC Endowment 2022).
[5:3] - **Vasantotsavam** (spring festival) – **most popular festival under the Reddi kings** (APPSC G2 2019; AP History PYQ) – not Sankranti, Dussehra or Deepavali; **Kumaragiri Reddy** styled himself **Karpura Vasantarayalu**; merchant **Avachi Tippayashetti** headed the **Sugandhabhandagara** (perfume store) that supplied it.
[5:4] - **Atla Taddi** – women's festival of **Ashwayuja** (not Chaitra).
[5:5] - **Bathukamma, Bonalu** = Telangana identity festivals.
[5:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP History - APPSC 12 p.10-14 (Vasantotsavam, Karpura Vasanta Rayalu); NextGen AP History - APPSC 08 p.24; PMF Art & Culture 06 p.10, p.21 (Ugadi); app notes 1:125 (sub 4)

[7:1] - **Folk songs:** **Jola patalu** = lullabies; **Kolatam kirtanas** (for the stick dance) – **Annamacharya**; Sankranti songs and Gangireddulaata – January harvest.
