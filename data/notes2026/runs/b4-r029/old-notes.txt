[29:4:0] - India **2nd** fish producer after **China**, ~8–9% of world output; **inland >75%**; seafood exports **₹62,408 crore** (FY 2024-25), led by frozen shrimp.
[29:4:1] - **National fish: Indian Mackerel** (not formally notified; some cite **Golden Mahseer**; national aquatic animal = **Gangetic dolphin**). Blue Revolution = fisheries/aquaculture.
[29:4:2] TABLE head: Zone | Rule
