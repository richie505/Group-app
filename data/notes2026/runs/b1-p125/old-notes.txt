## SUB 1 | Classical Dances – Kuchipudi and the Eight Forms
[125:1:0] - **Kuchipudi** = classical dance of **Andhra Pradesh** (MPPCS 1995; UKPCS 2002) – named after **Kuchipudi (Kuchelapuram) village, Krishna district**; shaped by **Siddhendra Yogi**; **Abul Hasan Tana Shah** (last Qutb Shahi) gifted the village to the performers; popular shows Bhama Kalapam, Usha Parinayam.
[125:1:1] - **Kuchipudi vs Bharatanatyam (IAS 2012):** dancers **speak dialogue** in Kuchipudi ✓; **Tarangam** – dancing on the **rim of a brass plate** – is a **Kuchipudi** feature, not Bharatanatyam ✗ → **only 1**.
[125:1:2] - Kuchipudi = **dance-drama** tradition (Bhagavata Mela, male Brahmin troupes); Bharatanatyam = solo temple-dance tradition; **Lakumadevi**, court dancer of **Kumaragiri Reddy**, was famed for Bharatanatyam.
[125:1:3] - **Solo dance (IAS 1997):** **Mohiniyattam** (Kerala; women) – Kuchipudi, Bharatanatyam and Odissi are also staged by groups.

[125:1:6] - **Sonal Mansingh** – **Odissi** (also Bharatanatyam); **Padma Bhushan 1992**, Padma Vibhushan 2003 – not Birju Maharaj (Kathak), Mallika Sarabhai or Yamini Krishnamurthy.
[125:1:7] - **Uday Shankar** – blended Indian classical dance with **Western ballet / European theatre** – pioneer of modern Indian dance.
[125:1:8] - **Kuravanji** – a **group dance-drama by women** on a girl's love for her beloved (Tamil; Telugu **Koravanji** = earliest type of Yakshaganam); **Rukmini Devi Arundale** staged Kuravanji (Kutrala Kuravanji) at **Kalakshetra**. APPSC asked "a famous Kuravanji performer" twice: one key **Rukmini Arundale**, the G2 2019 key **Jakka Radhika** (no source names her).

[125:1:11] - **Abhinaya Darpanam** (Sanskrit treatise on gesture in dance): by **Nandikeshvara** – Natyashastra is Bharata's, Nritta Ratnavali Jayapa's.

## SUB 5 | Festivals of Andhra Pradesh
[125:5:0] - **Ugadi** = **Telugu New Year** (Chaitra), in AP, Karnataka and Telangana (APPSC JL) – not Dasara, Sankranti or "Panduga".
[125:5:1] - **Sankranti** (January harvest): Bhogi → Sankranti → **Kanuma** (day of **thanksgiving to cattle and birds**, APPSC Endowment 2022); Gangireddulaata, **Kolla Pandalu** (cockfights), Pottella Pandalu (ram fights).
[125:5:2] - **Vinayaka Chavithi** – Vinayaka is worshipped with **21 kinds of leaves** (Ekavimsati Patra Puja) (APPSC Endowment 2022).
[125:5:3] - **Vasantotsavam** (spring festival) – **most popular festival under the Reddi kings** (APPSC G2 2019; AP History PYQ) – not Sankranti, Dussehra or Deepavali; **Kumaragiri Reddy** styled himself **Karpura Vasantarayalu**; merchant **Avachi Tippayashetti** headed the **Sugandhabhandagara** (perfume store) that supplied it.
[125:5:4] - **Atla Taddi** – women's festival of **Ashwayuja** (not Chaitra).
[125:5:5] - **Bathukamma, Bonalu** = Telangana identity festivals.
[125:5:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP History - APPSC 12 p.10-14 (Vasantotsavam, Karpura Vasanta Rayalu); NextGen AP History - APPSC 08 p.24; PMF Art & Culture 06 p.10, p.21 (Ugadi); app notes 1:125 (sub 4)
[125:5:7] - **Chandanotsavam, Simhachalam:** the sandal paste of Sri Varaha Lakshmi Narasimha Swamy is removed for **Nijaroopa darshan** and fresh sandalwood paste applied on **Akshaya Tritiya**.

[125:7:1] - **Folk songs:** **Jola patalu** = lullabies; **Kolatam kirtanas** (for the stick dance) – **Annamacharya**; Sankranti songs and Gangireddulaata – January harvest.

## SUB 1 | Formation of Andhra Pradesh, 1 November 1956 – First Holders
[262:1:0] - **Andhra Pradesh formed on 1 November 1956** under the **States Reorganisation Act, 1956**, on a **linguistic basis** – the first state formed under the SR Act; **Andhra State (11 districts) + 9 Telugu districts of Hyderabad State**; Kannada areas → Mysore, Marathi → Bombay.
[262:1:1] - **Andhra State** (1 Oct 1953): capital **Kurnool**; first CM **Tanguturi Prakasam**, Deputy CM N. Sanjiva Reddy; Prakasam's ministry fell on **prohibition** (1954) → mid-term polls 1955 → **Bezawada Gopala Reddy** CM. Before it, the **Wanchoo Committee** (Dec 1952) identified the **financial and administrative problems** of forming Andhra State; the **Pataskar Award** fixed the Andhra–Madras border (**Tiruttani** to Tamil Nadu).
[262:1:2] - **Capital:** **Kurnool = capital of Andhra State until 1956** (the "capital before 1956" answer); **Hyderabad became the capital of AP in 1956** – reasons: the SRC's view and the Mahasabha demand. Exam keys often call Kurnool "the first capital of AP".
[262:1:3] - **Andhra Legislature met at Kurnool** and backed the merger **unanimously**; **inaugurated by Prime Minister Nehru on 1 Nov 1956**.
[262:1:4] - **Name:** draft Bill "**Andhra Telangana**"; Communists wanted "**Visalandhra**"; the **Joint Select Committee** changed it to "**Andhra Pradesh**" – so none of "Andhra Telangana / Telangana Andhra / Visalandhra".

## SUB 2 | Visalandhra Movement and the Visalandhra Mahasabha
[262:2:0] - **Visalandhra** ("**Greater Andhra**") = the movement for **one state for all Telugu people** – the Circars, the Ceded districts (Rayalaseema) and Telangana, then divided between Madras and the Nizam's Hyderabad (in **1950** southern and eastern Andhra were in Madras State, Telangana in Hyderabad State).
[262:2:1] - Roots: 1912 Unnava Lakshminarayana and Jonnavittula Gurunatham sketched a united Andhra; 1914 second Andhra Mahasabha – **Nyapati Subbarao** proposed one rule for all Telugu areas; **1936 Andhra Congress Swaraj Party manifesto**; 1937 the idea spread in the Circars; 1954 **Madapati Hanumantha Rao**: the idea was 50 years old.
[262:2:2] - **Started / championed by the Communist Party** – demanded integrated linguistic states from **1946**; **Puchalapalli Sundarayya** coined the word and wrote ***Visalandhralo Prajarajyam***; daily **Visalandhra** from **June 1952**; Sundarayya later first General Secretary of CPI(M) (1964).
[262:2:3] - The Communists **became a great force in Telangana politics** and propagated Visalandhra **even before Andhra State (1953)**; they **did not oppose** integrated linguistic states (the only incorrect statement, G2 2025).
[262:2:4] - Other Communist leaders: **Ravi Narayana Reddy, Baddam Ella Reddy, Arutla Ramachandra Reddy**; the Hyderabad State Communist Party was a branch of the Andhra party (HQ **Vijayawada**); its 1952 electoral front = **People's Democratic Front (PDF)**. The Congress was first cold because the idea came from the Communists.

## SUB 5 | SRC, Merger Debate and the Leaders' Stands
[262:5:0] - **SRC** (announced 22 Dec 1953; Fazl Ali, H.N. Kunzru, K.M. Panikkar – not Harishchandra Heda) **heard opinions in Hyderabad in June 1954**; report **30 September 1955**. On **8 July 1954** a Hyderabad PCC delegation asked it for a **self-sufficient Telangana of 9 districts with Bastar and parts of Chanda**.
[262:5:1] - SRC **acknowledged Telangana's special claims** (finance, education) – **para 386**: Telangana to stay a separate Hyderabad State, merging after the **1961** elections if **two-thirds** of its legislature agreed; Raichur, Gulbarga → Mysore, Marathwada detached.
[262:5:2] - SRC's case for Visalandhra: **unified control of Krishna–Godavari waters**, minerals and power, **Hyderabad as a ready capital**, lower administrative cost.
[262:5:3] - **Hyderabad Assembly**: **103 for, 29 against, 15 neutral**; **Andhra Assembly (1955)**: Bezawada Gopala Reddy's **unanimous** resolution.
[262:5:4] - **Burgula Ramakrishna Rao**, earlier for a separate Telangana, **changed his stand in 1955** and urged **immediate merger** with Andhra.
[262:5:5] - 1956: to settle differences the **Congress leadership called the leaders to Delhi** → Gentlemen's Agreement.
