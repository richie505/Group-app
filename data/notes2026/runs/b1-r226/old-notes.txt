  [227:1:0.1] Lucknow Pact 1916 | Congress accepts them + weightage + communal veto (three-fourths rule)
  [227:1:0.2] GoI Act 1919 | Extended to Sikhs, Indian Christians, Anglo-Indians, Europeans
  [227:1:0.3] Communal Award 1932 | Extended to depressed classes; Poona Pact replaced it with reserved seats in joint electorates
  [227:1:0.4] GoI Act 1935 | Further to SCs, women, labour; Muslims 33% of Federal Legislature seats
