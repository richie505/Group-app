## 3. Repeats and traps
- The **Lahore Resolution** (place, March 1940, Jinnah) is the single most repeated fact; the mover (**Fazlul Huq**, not Khaliquzzaman who seconded) is where printed keys go wrong.
- **Madras 1937 – Rajaji / Sambamurthy** is the APPSC favourite (5 of 17 APPSC PYQs).
- Numbers: 7 ministries (8 with Assam coalition), 5 absolute majorities, 14% franchise, 59-54-36 lists.
- Sources disagree on Lucknow vs Faizpur "decided to contest" (Lucknow first) and on "no majority" (Bombay and Assam both).
