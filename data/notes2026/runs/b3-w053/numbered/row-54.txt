  [0:1.0] Bhudhaar (Bhudaar) | Web portal (with Bhuseva) launched by CM N. Chandrababu Naidu on 20 Nov 2018; gives every land parcel an 11-digit unique number; people view land records online
  [0:1.1] Meebhoomi | Earlier portal (13 June 2015) to view Adangal / 1-B records
  [0:1.2] Webland | AP's digitised land records platform
  [0:1.3] Bhu Bharathi | Computerisation of land records, united AP, 1992; pilot Nizamabad
  [0:1.4] CARD | Computer-aided registration: anywhere registration, auto-mutation, at Grama Sachivalayams – a registration system, not a court
  [0:1.5] Mee Bhoomi-Blockchain | Pilot 24 July 2026, 7 mandals

## SUB 2 | DILRMP, ULPIN and NGDRS
[2:0] - Began as **NLRMP in 2008** (UPPSC 2016) = merger of **CLR** (Computerisation of Land Records) + **SRA&ULR** (Strengthening of Revenue Administration and Updating of Land Records); renamed **Digital India Land Records Modernisation Programme**.
[2:1] - **Central Sector, 100% central funding** from **1 April 2016** (earlier centrally sponsored); extended to **2025-26** with two new components – **consent-based Aadhaar seeding** and **computerisation of revenue courts**; mandatory **gender column** in the RoR.
[2:2] - Components: modern record rooms, survey/resurvey, **digitised cadastral maps**, computerised registration, State data centre (Property Cards for abadi are **SVAMITVA's**, not DILRMP's).
[2:3] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [2:3.0] ULPIN (Bhu-Aadhaar) | 14-digit alphanumeric parcel ID from the geo-coordinates of the parcel's vertices; 36.67 crore parcels
  [2:3.1] NGDRS | "One Nation One Registration Software" – common, configurable registration application for sub-registrars and citizens
  [2:3.2] Land Stack (National Digital Land Initiative) | Cloud GIS; a Bhu-Aadhaar for every parcel
  [2:3.3] Phase (DILRMP 3.0) | 1 (2008-16) digitisation, resurvey; 2 (2016-26) ULPIN, NGDRS, e-Court integration; 3 (2026-31) GIS Land Stack
[2:4] - Progress: **RoR digitised 99.8%**; cadastral maps 97.24%; SROs computerised **95.73%**.
[2:5] ~~ Sources: Ramesh Singh Indian Economy (10th ed.) part 04 p.146 (NLRMP 2008); Economic Survey 2025-26 Summary part 2 p.41; app notes 3:54 sub 0, 2
## SUB 3 | Land Titling – Presumptive vs Conclusive
[3:0] TABLE head: Presumptive (present) | Conclusive
  [3:0.0] Registration only records a transaction; title can be challenged | State guarantees the title; cannot be disputed once issued
  [3:0.1] Registration, survey and revenue records kept separately | Single title register
[3:1] - **Clear titles** → land usable as **collateral**, less litigation.
[3:2] - **NITI Aayog Model Conclusive Land Titling Act, 2020:** adoption is **each State's choice**.
[3:3] - **SC (1 January 2026):** **registration of a transfer deed is distinct from title**.
[3:4] - **AP Land Titling Act, 2022:** presumptive → **conclusive**, State as guarantor; criticised for limiting disputes to the **High Court**; **repealed 2024**.
