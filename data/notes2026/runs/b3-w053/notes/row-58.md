=== Price Terms, Economic Cost and Food Subsidy
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| MSP | Floor price announced before sowing (CACP → CCEA) |
| **Procurement price** | Price at which the government **buys grain for PDS and buffer stocks** (announced separately at harvest from 1966-67; since 1968-69 MSP itself is the effective procurement price) |
| **Central Issue Price (CIP)** | Price at which the **Centre supplies grain to States** for distribution through fair price / ration shops |
| FPS retail price | Fixed by **State Governments** (States' role in PDS is operational) |
| Ceiling price | Maximum price that may be charged – protects consumers |
| **Economic cost** | **MSP + bonus + procurement incidentals + distribution cost** (UPSC 2019) |
| **Food subsidy** | **Economic cost minus CIP** |
| Hidden subsidy | FCI carrying cost financed by borrowing (NSSF loans) instead of budget releases |
- Pooled grain cost (MSP + bonus) ≈ **two-thirds** of economic cost. Subsidy components: **FCI subsidy + subsidy to DCP States**; **NSSF** borrowing ended and subsidy brought on budget in **Budget 2021-22**.
- In the UPSC 2004 statements only **(c)** is right: wheat and rice are issued at **uniform CIPs**; procurement is **open-ended** (not target-based) and MSP is announced for pulses and oilseeds too, **not only cereals**.
- **PDS issue prices were raised** to **lessen the subsidy burden**.
| Year | Food subsidy (₹ crore) |
|---|---|
| 1991-92 | 2,850 |
| 1998-99 | **8,700** (CAG 2000: from ~2,800 in 1992-93 = **more than three times**) |
| **2000-01** | **12,010** total = buffer carrying cost **4,233** + consumer (PDS) subsidy **≈ 7,777** (BE 8,210) |
| 2024-25 | 1,99,500 (FCI 1,29,089 + DCP States 70,411) |
| **Union Budget 2026-27** | **₹2,27,629 crore** |
- **Flaws of the food security system:** subsidy burden, **inefficient FCI operations**, limited benefit to the poor – all of them.

=== Food Management and the FCI
- **Objectives of PDS:** supply a **minimum quantity of grain at affordable prices to low-income groups**, equitable distribution, **control of price fluctuations**.
- Procurement objectives: assure MSP, **free grain to the weak under PMGKAY**, market intervention. **Not objectives:** **controlling the production of foodgrains** (SSC 2008), **export of foodgrains**.
| FCI | Fact |
|---|---|
| Set up | **14 January 1965** |
| Act | **Food Corporations Act, 1964** |
| Recommended by | Food Grains Prices Committee (**L.K. Jha**, 1964) |
| HQ | **New Delhi** |
| Role | Nodal agency for price-support purchase of wheat and paddy, with State agencies |
- **FCI missions:** procurement at MSP; storage, movement and distribution; operational and buffer stocks; grain at reasonable prices to weaker sections through TPDS. **Not a mission: regulation of markets in agricultural products** – that is done under **State APMC Acts** (APPSC 2018).
- **OSM = Online Storage Management**, rolled out from **1 April 2022** for storage, movement and distribution under TPDS. **Anna Darpan** modernises **FCI's** supply chain (Dept of Food and PD, not Agriculture).
- **OMSS (Domestic):** FCI sells wheat and rice from the Central Pool in the **open market at predetermined prices** to moderate prices.

=== Procurement, Buffer Stocks and Storage
| System | Feature |
|---|---|
| Centralised | FCI (directly or via State agencies) buys for the Central Pool |
| **DCP (1997, with TPDS)** | Designated States **procure, store and distribute** grain for TPDS; Centre pays their economic cost minus CIP |
| Open-ended | Wheat and paddy – all produce meeting standards at MSP |
| Target-based | PSS pulses and oilseeds – cap **25%** of production |
- **Buffer stocks** (wheat and rice, bought from farmers at procurement prices) are released through PDS and open-market sales to **check price rise** and meet shortages.
- **Buffer norms** fixed by **CCEA** quarterly – as on **1 April, 1 July, 1 October, 1 January**; 1 April norm **210.40 LMT** (rice 135.80, wheat 74.60).
- **Strategic (food security) reserve:** **30 LMT wheat** (from 1 July 2008) and **20 LMT rice** (from 1 January 2009), included in the norms.
- **604.02 LMT** in the Central Pool on 1 April 2026 – nearly **three times** the norm → **higher carrying costs**.
- **CAP = Cover and Plinth** (grain on raised plinths under polythene covers); **steel silos under PPP**; **Grain Storage Plan** (cooperative sector): PACS chosen by **District Cooperative Development Committees**, implemented by **NCDC**.
- **IGMRI** (Indian Grain Storage Management and Research Institute): **Hapur (UP)**; trains SAARC officials; field stations Hyderabad and Ludhiana.

=== PDS Evolution – RPDS, TPDS, AAY, NFSA, PMGKAY
| Year | Step |
|---|---|
| 1940s | Rationing – **Bengal famine**; revived in the 1960s shortages |
| June 1992 | **RPDS** – backward, hilly, drought-prone areas |
| **June 1997** | **TPDS** – BPL and APL entitlements |
| **25 December 2000** | **Antyodaya Anna Yojana** – **35 kg** per family per month to the poorest |
| 2013 | **NFSA** – legal entitlement for about **800 million** (75% rural, 50% urban); **first promulgated as an ordinance (July 2013)**, Act in September 2013 |
| 1 Jan 2023 | Free grain under **PMGKAY** to 80 crore+; extended to **31 Dec 2028** |
- Sequence: **PDS → RPDS → TPDS → AAY**. PMGKAY motto: "**One Nation-One Price-One Ration**".
- **Fortified rice** (iron, **vitamin B12, folic acid**) through PDS to Dec 2028.
- Of rights-based laws, only **NFSA** began as an ordinance; RTE, MGNREGA and RTI were passed directly as Acts.

=== PDS Reforms – ONORC, Leakage and the Shanta Kumar Committee
| Reform | Period / content |
|---|---|
| End-to-End Computerisation of TPDS | **Oct 2012 – Mar 2020**: digitised cards, online allocation, ePoS |
| IM-PDS | From **April 2018**; **national portability** (ONORC) |
| SMART-PDS | Apr 2023 – Mar 2026; part of SARTHAK-PDS |
| **SARTHAK-PDS** | **2026-2031** (**CCEA**, 28 May 2026); end-to-end tracking from procurement to FPS, real-time stock monitoring |
- **ONORC:** draw ration **away from where the card was issued** via ePoS, Aadhaar/biometric authentication; family draws the balance at home. App: **Mera Ration**; rollout target **1 June 2020**; now **all 36 States/UTs**.
- **DBT in food:** Chandigarh, **Puducherry**, urban Dadra and Nagar Haveli (2015). **Delhi**: first **e-ration card** service (March 2015).
- Anti-leakage measures: e-PoS, ONORC, biometrics (not "ending procurement").
- **Coverage vs leakage:** States with **wider coverage had lower leakage** → **the lower the coverage, the higher the leakage**.
- **Shanta Kumar HLC (2014-15):** PDS leakage **46%** (NSS 2011-12); only ~6% farmers sell at MSP; expand DCP; hand procurement in surplus States (Punjab, Haryana, AP) to States so FCI focuses on **eastern States**; cut NFSA coverage **67% → 40%** (not 50%); cash transfers; private storage.
