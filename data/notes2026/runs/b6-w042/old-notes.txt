## SUB 0 | BIS – IS 19445:2025 Bomb Disposal Standard and SHINE
[42:0:0] # IS 19445:2025 (UPSC CSE Prelims 2026 PYQ)
[42:0:1] - **What:** India's **first national standard for bomb disposal systems** – "Bomb Disposal Systems – Performance Evaluation and Requirements"; released by Union Consumer Affairs Minister **Pralhad Joshi** around **National Consumer Day, December 2025**.
[42:0:2] - **Covers:** bomb blankets, baskets and inhibitors – tested against **blast loads and splinter effects**.
[42:0:3] - **Aim:** uniform testing and procurement → **better interoperability** of equipment across the Armed Forces, CAPFs and State Police.
[42:0:4] - **Developed by:** **BIS**, at the request of the **MHA** and **TBRL (DRDO)**; TBRL convened the expert panel (Arms and Ammunition for Civilian Use Sectional Committee) – **no foreign (Russian) collaborator**.
[42:0:5] - **Status:** a **voluntary** standard (mandatory only through a Quality Control Order under the BIS Act, 2016).
[42:0:6] > Statement trap: "developed with the 30th Central Scientific Research Institute, Russia" is false – answer **1 and 2 only**.

[42:4:0] # Kyasanur Forest Disease (monkey fever)
[42:4:1] - News: a **29-year-old man in Karnataka** died of KFD (**January 2026**).
[42:4:2] - **Tick-borne viral haemorrhagic** disease; first identified **1957**, Kyasanur Forest, Karnataka.
[42:4:3] - Virus: family Flaviviridae; vector – **hard tick Haemaphysalis spinigera**.
[42:4:4] - **No human-to-human transmission**; season Oct–Nov, peaks **January–April**.
[42:4:5] - Fatality **5–10%** (not 20–30%); **no antiviral**; **vaccine available**.
