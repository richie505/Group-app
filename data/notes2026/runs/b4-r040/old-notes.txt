[40:0:0] - Classification by **H.G. Champion and S.K. Seth (1968)**: **5 major groups, 16 type groups** – Moist Tropical, Dry Tropical, Montane Subtropical, Montane Temperate, Alpine.

  [40:0:1.2] Rank 2 | Tropical moist deciduous (~1,35,492 sq km)
  [40:0:1.3] Rank 3 | Wet evergreen – ~8% (51,249 sq km); semi-evergreen 4.1%

  [40:1:0.3] Dry deciduous | 70–100 cm | Central Gangetic plains to south India; peninsular interior; AP (Nallamala, Eastern Ghats, Seshachalam) | Teak, sandalwood (south), Terminalia, red sanders

[40:1:1] - **Evergreen**: dense, multi-layered; **maximum plant diversity and biomass**; leaf litter decomposes fastest; epiphytes and climbers reach the canopy; no dry season so leaves are not shed together. Grow near the equator (**10°N–10°S**); cover about 7% of the earth's land.
[40:1:2] - **Tropical rainforest in India**: Assam and Kerala.
[40:1:3] - **Moist deciduous**: shed leaves in the dry season; **teak** dominant. **Dry deciduous**: transition to thorn forest; major type of central India.
[40:1:4] - **Thorn**: open, stunted, long roots, thorns/spines; **western** (not eastern) AP.
[40:1:5] - **Desert vegetation** (< 25 cm): xerophytes of W Rajasthan; date palms in oases.
[40:1:6] - A&N: **mangrove + evergreen + deciduous** together; North Andamans wet evergreen, Middle Andamans moist deciduous.
[40:1:7] - **Cinchona** – evergreen tropical plant; does not grow in Chhattisgarh. **Mahogany** – native to the Americas, not Himalayan.

[40:3:6] - Himalayan soils lack humus; the Himalaya has the largest area under forest cover.

## SUB 6 | Forest Terms, Cover Classes and Silviculture
[40:6:0] - **Forest cover** = land **> 1 ha** with canopy density **≥ 10%**, irrespective of ownership.
[40:6:1] TABLE head: Class | Canopy density
  [40:6:1.0] Very Dense (VDF) | ≥ 70%
  [40:6:1.1] Moderately Dense (MDF) | 40–70%
  [40:6:1.2] Open (OF) | 10–40%
  [40:6:1.3] Scrub | < 10% – not forest cover
  [40:6:1.4] Silvicultural term | Meaning
  [40:6:1.5] Coppice | Regrowth from stool shoots after felling; short rotation (fuelwood)
  [40:6:1.6] High forest | Crop raised from seed; long rotation (timber)
  [40:6:1.7] Selection system | Mature trees removed singly or in groups; uneven-aged forest
[40:6:2] - **Afforestation** – planting on never-forested land; **reforestation** – re-establishing depleted forest; **social forestry** – trees for community needs; Joint Forest Management = community participation.
[40:6:3] - **TOFI** (Trees Outside Forests in India) – AP Forest Dept + USAID, Nov 2022.
