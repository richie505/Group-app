## SUB 2 | Concepts and the National Trend, 1901-2011
[2:0] # Definitions
[2:1] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [2:1.0] Sex ratio (India) | Females per 1,000 males – the social indicator of equality between males and females
  [2:1.1] Sex ratio (UN / international) | Males per 100 females
  [2:1.2] Child sex ratio (CSR) | Girls per 1,000 boys aged 0-6
  [2:1.3] Sex ratio at birth (SRB) | Girls born per 1,000 boys born
  [2:1.4] Biological norm | About 105 boys per 100 girls = roughly 950 girls per 1,000 boys
  [2:1.5] Missing women | Shortfall of women, esp. in Asia; coined by Amartya Sen (1990)
[2:2] - Sex ratio covers the **whole population**, not only adults; first Census **1872**, then every **10 years**; density is **lower**, not higher, in mountains.

## SUB 5 | Causes, Laws and Schemes
[5:0] # Causes
[5:1] - Accepted causes: **high maternal mortality, high female child mortality, female foeticide, more male births**. **Male** child mortality is naturally **higher** – so "lower male than female mortality" is not a cause.
[5:2] - Roots: **son preference, patriarchy**, neglect of girls' health, intra-household discrimination, male migration.
[5:3] - MMR: **254 (2004-06) → 88 (2021-23)**.
[5:4] - After curbs on prenatal sex selection, firstborn girls show **25% higher child mortality** than firstborn boys.
[5:5] - **Infant Mortality Rate:** deaths before the **1st birthday** per 1,000 live births; **child (under-5) mortality** = deaths before the **5th birthday**.
[5:6] - British India: literacy **below 16%**, **female** (not male) literacy about **7%**, IMR about **218**, life expectancy about **32 years** (older NCERT, the APPSC answer; the revised NCERT text in OnlyIAS gives **44 years**).
[5:7] - Census in British India: first (non-synchronous) Census **1872**; first synchronous Census **1881**.
[5:8] # Laws and schemes
[5:9] TABLE head: Measure | Year | Aim
  [5:9.0] Cradle Baby Scheme (Tamil Nadu) | 1992 | Against female infanticide
  [5:9.1] PCPNDT Act | 1994 | Bans sex selection; prenatal tests only for genetic/chromosomal abnormalities
  [5:9.2] Beti Bachao Beti Padhao | 22 Jan 2015, Panipat (Haryana) | Reverse falling CSR; multi-sectoral action in gender-critical districts
[5:10] - BBBP does **not** allow prenatal sex determination.
[5:11] - **Supreme Court (Jun 2026):** CSR shows signs of improvement but gender bias is **deep-seated** (son preference, patriarchy).
