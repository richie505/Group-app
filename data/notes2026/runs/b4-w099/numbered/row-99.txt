## SUB 1 | Fuels – Fossil, Renewable and Commercial
[1:0] # Classification
[1:1] TABLE head: Class | Sources | Key point
  [1:1.0] Renewable (replenished in a short time) | Solar, wind, hydro (energy of water), geothermal (energy of the Earth), tidal, biomass, biogas, biofuel, wood | Basis of the energy transition
  [1:1.1] Non-renewable (finite) | Fossil fuels – coal, petroleum (crude oil), natural gas + nuclear fuel (uranium, thorium) | Fossil fuels formed over millions of years from buried organisms
[1:2] - **Not fossil fuels:** **solar energy, wood, uranium** (non-renewable **nuclear** fuel), biofuel, biogas. Kerosene, diesel, petrol are petroleum products (fossil-derived).
[1:3] - **Ultimate source** of fossil-fuel energy = **the Sun**.
[1:4] - Fossil fuels **cannot be prepared in the laboratory** from dead organisms (statement trap: "coal can be made in the lab" is false).
[1:5] - Earth's **source function**: supplies water, minerals, fish stocks, **fossil fuels**.
[1:6] - **Petroleum products:** petrol (gasoline), diesel, kerosene, **LPG, naphtha, bitumen**, plastics; **coal and anthracite are not** petroleum products. Thermal power plants burn **fossil fuels**; solar plants use radiation, not coal.
[1:7] - **Bio-asphalt:** made from **renewable/organic waste**, not fossil-based, eco-friendly road surfacing (Budget 2011-12 duty exemption).
[1:8] - **Commercial** sources = **power, coal, oil, gas, hydro-electricity, uranium** (atomic); **non-commercial** = firewood, dung, agricultural waste, **biogas**.
[1:9] - Coal, crude oil, natural gas together = **fossil fuels**. Coke and producer gas are manufactured fuels.
[1:10] - **First oil crisis: 1973** (OPEC quadrupled prices; 1970s-80s crises). Fossil fuels give about **75% of global GHGs and 90% of CO2**.
[1:11] - Energy-transition sources = **solar and wind**; National Solar Mission is under the **NAPCC**.
[1:12] > Exam angle: "not a fossil fuel" = solar / wood / uranium; "not renewable" = natural gas / petroleum; commercial vs biogas.

## SUB 7 | Fuel Gases – Composition and Fuel Properties
[7:0] TABLE head: Fuel | Composition
  [7:0.0] Natural gas / CNG | Mainly methane (CH4), 70-90%, + ethane
  [7:0.1] LPG | Propane + butane
  [7:0.2] Biogas / gobar gas | Methane 50-75% + CO2 25-50%; anaerobic digestion; gives power + manure
  [7:0.3] Water gas (= syngas) | CO + H2 (steam on coke or hydrocarbons)
  [7:0.4] Producer gas | CO + N2 (+ H2)
  [7:0.5] Coal gas | H2, CH4, CO
  [7:0.6] Petrol / Kerosene / Diesel | C5-C10 / C10-C16 / C15-C18 (fractional distillation of crude)
  [7:0.7] Hydrogen | Electrolysis or steam reforming
[7:1] - Fractional distillation: lighter fractions boil off first – **gasoline (petrol)** at the lowest temperature, then kerosene, diesel, heavy oil.
[7:2] - Calorific value (kJ/kg): hydrogen 1,50,000 > LPG 55,000 > **CNG/methane 50,000** > petrol, diesel 45,000 > biogas > coal (brown coal lower) > wood > dung.
[7:3] - CO2 per kWh of heat: **anthracite** (about 0.37 kg) > house coal (0.35) > LPG (about 0.23); wood logs counted nearly carbon-neutral.
[7:4] - **Kerosene floats** on water because its **density is less than water's** (and it does not mix).
[7:5] - **Alessandro Volta** first isolated methane (marsh gas, 1776) and exploded it with an electric spark.
[7:6] - **Green hydrogen** = RE-powered electrolysis; **blue hydrogen** = gas + CCS.
[7:7] > Exam angle: match fuel gas with constituents (UPSC 1999, 2004); syngas = water gas = CO + H2; LPG = propane + butane.
