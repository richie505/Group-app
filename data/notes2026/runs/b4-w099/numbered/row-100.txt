## SUB 0 | Transcontinental and Famous Railways of the World
[0:0] # Trans-Siberian Railway (asked 6 times)
[0:1] - **Longest continuous / longest continental railway in the world** (SSC CGL 2015) – not the Canadian National, Canadian Pacific or a "Transatlantic Railway".
[0:2] - **Main line: Moscow (Yaroslavsky station) → Vladivostok**, **9,289 km**, crosses **8 time zones** (APPSC G1 Prelims 2024 → **Moscow to Vladivostok**).
[0:3]   - NCERT XII / PMF describe the route from **St. Petersburg (Leningrad)** in the west to **Vladivostok** (9,332 km) – the classic "Leningrad to Vladivostok" pair in match questions.
[0:4] - **Built mainly 1891–1916** (work began 1891; through route 1904–1916); opened Siberia to European markets; crosses the **Urals**, the **Ob and Yenisei**.
[0:5] - **Baikal–Amur Mainline (BAM):** runs **parallel** to the Trans-Siberian – the alternative strategic route.
[0:6] - **On the route (west → east):** St. Petersburg, Moscow, Yaroslavl, Kirov, Perm, Yekaterinburg, Tyumen, **Omsk**, Novosibirsk, Krasnoyarsk, Irkutsk (fur centre), Ulan-Ude, **Chita** (agro-centre), **Khabarovsk**, **Vladivostok**; **Kazan** lies on a connecting branch that joins near Yekaterinburg and is counted on the route in exam keys.
[0:7] - **NOT on it:** **Sochi** (Black Sea resort, UPPCS 2019), **Murmansk** (far north-west), **Georgia** (a separate country, APPSC G1 2003).
[0:8] - **Vladivostok:** Pacific terminus, HQ of Russia's Pacific Fleet; permanent venue of the **Eastern Economic Forum** (since 2015).

[5:0] - **Logistics cost:** **7.97% of GDP (2023-24)** official estimate – debated against the older **13–14%** figure.
