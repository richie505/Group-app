## SUB 0 | Inter-State Council – Article 263
[0:0] # Article 263 and who sets it up
[0:1] - **Article 263** = provision for an **Inter-State Council** to effect co-ordination **between the States** and **between the Centre and the States** (not Arts. 260, 261, 262, 264, 360, 365 or 368). Art. 262 is river-water disputes; Art. 261 is full faith and credit.
[0:2] - Set up by **the President** by order, when public interest would be served (not Parliament, the PM, the Council of Ministers, the Speaker, the NDC or a Zonal Council); the President also defines its duties, organisation and procedure.
[0:3] - Art. 263 duties: (a) **enquire into and advise upon disputes** between States; (b) **investigate and discuss subjects of common interest**; (c) make **recommendations for better co-ordination of policy and action**.
[0:4] - Other councils set up under Art. 263: **Central Council of Health** (and Family Welfare), **Central Council of Local Self-Government** (1954), **Transport Development Council**, Regional Councils for Sales Tax.
[0:5] # Establishment (1990)
[0:6] - Set up by **Presidential order of 28 May 1990** (V.P. Singh government) on the recommendation of the **Sarkaria Commission**, which made a strong case for a permanent council called the "**Inter-Governmental Council**".
[0:7] - Earlier backers: **First ARC (1966-69)** and the **Rajamannar Committee (1969)** – but neither got it set up.
[0:8] - Sarkaria also proposed constitutional status under **Art. 263** for the NDC renamed as the **National Economic and Development Council**.
[0:9] - **Nature:** a **constitutional, recommendatory** body for consultation – advice **not binding**, not a court (Art. 131 disputes go to the Supreme Court).
[0:10] - **Meetings:** at least **three a year** (on paper), held **in camera**; all questions decided by **consensus** (not by simple, two-thirds or three-fourths majority). Only **11 meetings** so far, the last on **16 July 2016**.
[0:11] # Composition
[0:12] TABLE head: Post | Holder
  [0:12.0] Chairman | Prime Minister (not the President)
  [0:12.1] Members | Chief Ministers of all States and of UTs with legislatures
  [0:12.2] Members | Administrators of UTs without assemblies
  [0:12.3] Members (only then) | Governors of States under President's Rule – not permanent members for all States
  [0:12.4] Members | Six Union Cabinet ministers (incl. Home Minister) nominated by the PM
  [0:12.5] Permanent invitees | Five Cabinet ministers / MoS (independent charge) nominated by the PM
  [0:12.6] Standing Committee (1996) | Chaired by the Union Home Minister; 5 Union ministers + 9 CMs – reconstituted November 2024 with Amit Shah as chairperson
  [0:12.7] Secretariat | Inter-State Council Secretariat, set up 1991, headed by a Secretary to GoI; since 2011 also the secretariat of the Zonal Councils

[0:14] > A–R: Art. 263 contemplates the Council (true). Rajamannar did urge it "immediately" (true) – but a 1969 committee cannot explain a 1950 Article, and the body actually came on **Sarkaria's** case.

## SUB 1 | Zonal Councils and the North Eastern Council
[1:0] # Zonal Councils
[1:1] - **Statutory, not constitutional** bodies – set up by **Parliament** under the **States Reorganisation Act, 1956** (Sections 15-22); idea of **PM Jawaharlal Nehru**. "Regional councils are constituted by **Parliamentary laws**."
[1:2] - States and UTs grouped with an advisory council each = **Zonal Council** (not State, Regional or Joint Council).
[1:3] - **Five** zonal councils: **Northern, Central, Eastern, Western, Southern**. **Chandigarh**, though not a State, is in the Northern council.
[1:4] - **Chairman:** the **Union Home Minister** – common chairman of all five (not the PM). **Vice-Chairman:** a **CM by annual rotation**.
[1:5] - Members: Home Minister, **CMs of the zone's States**, **two other ministers from each State**, **Administrators of UTs**; advisers (no vote): a NITI Aayog nominee, Chief Secretaries, Development Commissioners.
[1:6] - Only **deliberative and advisory** bodies – recommendations **not binding**; described as **sub-federal links** that promote **co-operation and integration** among States (both statements true).
[1:7] - Objectives: **emotional integration**, check **regionalism, linguism**, remove after-effects of reorganisation, co-operate on development projects, **political equilibrium** between regions.

[1:13] # North Eastern Council
[1:14] - Separate statute: **North Eastern Council Act, 1971** (came into existence 8 August 1972) – statutory, not under Art. 263.
[1:15] - **2002 amendment:** **Sikkim** added as the **8th** member → the North Eastern zone/council now has **8 States** (Assam, Arunachal, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Tripura, Sikkim); NEC became a **regional planning body**.
[1:16] - Composition after the 2002 Act: **Governors** and **Chief Ministers** of the 8 States + **three members nominated by the President** (the Home Minister is not a member under the Act; he chairs it since 2018 by Cabinet decision; DoNER Minister vice-chairman).

## SUB 3 | Full Faith and Credit (Art. 261) and Inter-State Trade (Arts. 301-307)
[3:0] # Article 261
[3:1] - **261(1):** **full faith and credit** throughout India to **public acts, records and judicial proceedings** of the **Union and every State** (not Arts. 133, 142, 256, 263, 266 or 324).
[3:2] - **261(2):** manner of proof and their effect – decided by **Parliament by law**.
[3:3] - **261(3):** **final judgments or orders of civil courts** can be **executed anywhere** in India (civil only, not criminal).
[3:4] - Modelled on the **US** Full Faith and Credit Clause.
[3:5] # Articles 301-307 (Part XIII)
[3:6] TABLE head: Article | Content
  [3:6.0] 301 | Trade, commerce and intercourse throughout India shall be free (inter- and intra-State) – not Arts. 19, 25, 299, 300, 308 or 309
  [3:6.1] 302 | Parliament may restrict it in public interest
  [3:6.2] 303 | No preference or discrimination between States (except scarcity)
  [3:6.3] 304(b) | State may impose reasonable restrictions – the bill needs the previous sanction of the President (not the Governor, Union government, PM, CM or ISC)
  [3:6.4] 305 | Saves existing laws and laws for State monopolies
  [3:6.5] 306 | Part B States – repealed
  [3:6.6] 307 | Parliament may appoint an authority (Inter-State Trade and Commerce Commission) – none appointed so far

## SUB 4 | River Water Disputes – Article 262 and Tribunals
[4:0] - **Art. 262(1):** Parliament may provide for **adjudication** of disputes on the **use, distribution or control of waters of inter-State rivers** (not Arts. 56, 105 or 352).
[4:1] - **Art. 262(2):** Parliament may bar the **Supreme Court and all other courts**; so **Art. 131** (SC original jurisdiction over Centre-State disputes) does not cover water disputes.
[4:2] - **Inter-State River Water Disputes Act, 1956:** Centre sets up an **ad hoc tribunal**; a State complains to the **Central Government** (Section 3); award final and binding.
[4:3] - **River Boards Act, 1956** (**not 1970**) – boards to advise States; **no board ever set up** under it. National Water Policy: 1987.
[4:4] - **Jurisdiction:** water is a **State** subject (Entry 17, List II) but **Parliament** regulates inter-State rivers when declared expedient in public interest (Entry 56, List I).
[4:5] - A downstream State **cannot** build a dam that **submerges an upstream State's territory** without its consent – the false statement.

## SUB 5 | Krishna and Godavari – AP Reorganisation Act, 2014
[5:0] - **KRMB** and **GRMB** set up under the **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014**, supervised by an **Apex Council** (Union Water Resources Minister + the two CMs).
[5:1] - **Apex Council functions (s.84):** supervise the boards; **plan and approve proposals for new projects**; **resolve disputes amicably**; **refer disputes not covered by KWDT** to a tribunal under the ISRWD Act, 1956. It does **not** take barrage matters **directly to the Supreme Court**.
[5:2] - **Section 89:** the **KWDT (KWDT-II)** term extended to make **project-wise allocation** and to prepare an **operational protocol for project-wise release of water in deficit years** – the Tribunal, not the boards or the States.
[5:3] - KWDT-II term: **two years from 1.8.2014**, then extended **one year at a time** (from **1.8.2016**, 1.8.2017, …).
[5:4] - KWDT-I allotted **811 tmcft** to undivided AP; ad hoc split from 2014-15 **512 (AP) : 299 (Telangana)** (66:34).
[5:5] - Centre set fresh terms of reference for KWDT-II on the AP-Telangana dispute in **October 2023**.
[5:6] - **Jan 2026 (CWC-chaired inter-State committee):** AP rejected Telangana's **50:50** Krishna demand as **contrary to the KWDT awards and Apex Council decisions**; objected to 968 tmcft of Godavari water claimed for Telangana projects.
