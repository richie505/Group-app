## SUB 1 | Recommendations on the Governor
[1:0] - **Fixed tenure of 5 years**; removal not at the sweet will of the Centre (Sarkaria first proposed a secure five-year term).
[1:1] - Follow **Sarkaria's guidelines**: eminent person, **from outside the State**, detached, not too active in politics.
[1:2] - Not in **active politics, even at the local level**, for **at least a couple of (two) years** before appointment.
[1:3] - **Article 156:** delete "**during the pleasure of the President**"; removal only by **impeachment by the State Legislature**, on the lines of the **President's impeachment**.
[1:4] - **Universities:** Governors should **not be Chancellors** or hold other **statutory positions** – role confined to constitutional provisions.
[1:5] - **Bills:** decide within **six months**; Art. 163 gives no general discretion.
[1:6] - **Hung assembly:** a **pre-poll alliance** is treated as **one political party**; then largest single party, post-poll coalitions.
[1:7] - Criticised **arbitrary dismissal** of Governors.
[1:8] - **Distractors** (not recommended): **periodic rotation** of Governors, **abolition** of the office, CM's **consent** (only consultation) for appointment.
[1:9] ~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.15 p.241-242; app notes 2:57
## SUB 2 | Articles 355 and 356 and Central Forces
[2:0] - **Amend Articles 355 and 356** – "no need to amend them" is the **wrong** statement.
[2:1] - **Art. 356** only as a **last resort**; incorporate **S.R. Bommai** guidelines.
[2:2] - **"Localised emergency":** the Centre takes over a **district or part of a district** for a **limited period** (under Art. 355), instead of a whole State.
[2:3] - **Central forces:** in **communal violence**, deploy **suo motu, without the State's consent, for a short period**; consent obtained afterwards.
[2:4] - **Article 357:** laws made by Parliament for a State under President's Rule **continue after the Proclamation ends** until changed by the State Legislature.
[2:5] - Origin of "either on their request or otherwise": the **First ARC**.
[2:6] ~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.15 p.242; app notes 2:57
