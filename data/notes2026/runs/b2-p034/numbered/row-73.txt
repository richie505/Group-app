## SUB 1 | Composition, Functions, Powers and Reports
[1:0] # Composition
[1:1] - NCSC, NCST and the constitutional NCBC each: **Chairperson + Vice-Chairperson + 3 other members**.
[1:2] - Appointed by the **President by warrant under his hand and seal**; tenure fixed by the President's Rules (2004) = **3 years**; not more than **two terms**.
[1:3] - The statutory NCBC (1993 Act, in force 2 Apr 1993) had **5 members** headed by a **retired judge of the Supreme Court or a High Court**.
[1:4] # Functions (Art. 338(5), 338A(5), 338B(5))
[1:5] - 1. **Investigate and monitor** the constitutional and legal safeguards and evaluate their working.
[1:6] - 2. **Inquire into specific complaints** of deprivation of rights and safeguards.
[1:7] - 3. **Participate and advise on the planning process** of socio-economic development and evaluate progress.
[1:8] - 4. **Present reports to the President** annually (and when it deems fit) – **not to the Prime Minister**; the President lays them before **Parliament** with an action-taken memorandum; State-related reports go to the **Governor** (NCBC: to the State government).
[1:9] - 5. Recommend measures for effective implementation of safeguards.
[1:10] - **Not** functions: giving scholarships, raising a protection force, implementing Union welfare schemes, punishing offenders.
[1:11] - **Consultation:** the Union and every State must consult the Commission on all major policy matters (Art. 338(9), 338A(9)); proposals for **de-reservation** of posts are examined by the NCSC/NCST/NCBC first (NCSC review of three years' proposals, Aug 2026).
[1:12] - **NCST extra (2005 Rules):** ownership of **minor forest produce**, rights over mineral and water resources, rehabilitation of displaced tribals, PESA implementation.
[1:13] # Powers – Art. 338(8)
[1:14] - While investigating or inquiring it has **all the powers of a civil court trying a suit**: **summon** and examine on oath; **discovery of documents**; **receive evidence on affidavits**; **requisition any public record** from any court or office; **issue summons** for examining witnesses and documents. It **cannot punish**; its advice is recommendatory.
[1:15] > Asked as: NCST tenure (3 years) and members besides Chairperson and Vice (3); a function of NCSC (advise on planning); NOT a function (report to the PM – APPSC Group-I); civil-court powers "all of the above" (APPSC Group-I 2023).

## SUB 3 | Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities – Art. 350B
[3:0] - **Art. 350B** inserted by the **7th Amendment, 1956** (with **Art. 350A**) on the **States Reorganisation Commission's** recommendation – not the 9th Amendment.
[3:1] - **One** Special Officer for the whole country, **appointed by the President** – there is **no** post "for every State".
[3:2] - Duty: **investigate all matters relating to the safeguards** for linguistic minorities and **report to the President**, who lays the reports before **each House of Parliament** and sends them to the States.
[3:3] - Office created **1957**; designated **Commissioner for Linguistic Minorities (CLM)**; under the **Ministry of Minority Affairs**; headquarters **New Delhi** (moved back from Allahabad, 2015); regional offices **Belgaum, Chennai, Kolkata**.
[3:4] - The Constitution does **not** fix his qualifications, tenure, salary or removal.
[3:5] - **Art. 350A**: States to provide **instruction in the mother tongue at the primary stage** to linguistic-minority children.
[3:6] > Asked as: Article for the Special Officer (350B – APPSC FRO 2022); statements "one officer for every State" and "added by the 9th Amendment" both false (UPPSC 2024).
