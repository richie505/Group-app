## SUB 0 | Sarkaria Commission at a Glance
[0:0] TABLE head: Item | Detail
  [0:0.0] Set up by | Central Government, 9 June 1983 (Indira Gandhi government)
  [0:0.1] Chairman | Justice Rajinder (Ranjit) Singh Sarkaria, retired Supreme Court judge
  [0:0.2] Members | B. Sivaraman and Dr S.R. Sen – 3 in all (not Rajamannar, Punchhi, V. Shankar, Hanumanthaiah)
  [0:0.3] Objective | Examine the relationship and balance of power between Centre and States; suggest changes within the existing constitutional framework
  [0:0.4] Report | January 1988 (worked 1983-87); 247 recommendations
[0:1] - Subject = **Centre-State relations** – not President-PM, legislature-executive, executive-judiciary, reservations, inter-State disputes or agricultural trade.
[0:2] - It **rejected** curtailing the Centre: a **strong Centre** is essential, but not centralisation ("blood pressure at the centre, anaemia at the periphery").
[0:3] - Report year: **1988** (a few keys, e.g. BPSC 2018, say 1987).

## SUB 1 | Recommendations on the Governor
[1:0] - Governor should be an **eminent person** in some walk of life.
[1:1] - **From outside the State**; a **detached figure**, not intimately connected with local politics; **not taken part in politics in the recent past** (UPSC 2019: Sarkaria, not ARC, Rajamannar or NCRWC).
[1:2] - **Chief Minister** to be consulted; procedure to be in the Constitution.
[1:3] - **Secure 5-year term**, not disturbed except for **extremely compelling reasons** – the first body to recommend this.
[1:4] - Governor cannot dismiss a ministry that commands a majority; decide on **Bills within a specified period**.
[1:5] - Convention in practice: Governor is a **person from outside the State** (no age bar, not appointed by the CM, no legal qualification).
[1:6] - **NOT** recommended: Governor "should have **travelled extensively** in the State", "must be an eminent **political leader**", "should have **served the Government** long", "other-State person shall not be appointed".
[1:7] ~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.15 p.239-241; NextGen Polity - APPSC Exams p.313
## SUB 2 | Inter-State Council and Article 356
[2:0] # Inter-Governmental Council
[2:1] - A **permanent Inter-State Council** called the **Inter-Governmental Council** under **Art. 263** – the main implemented recommendation; ISC set up **28 May 1990** (ISC Secretariat 1991).
[2:2] - "Establish an **Inter-Government Council in place of Inter-State Council**" = **Sarkaria** (not Rajamannar, Punchhi or the ARC).
[2:3] # Article 356
[2:4] - 1. Use **very sparingly**, in extreme cases, as a **last resort**, after a **warning** to the State.
[2:5] - 2. Doubt over majority → **floor test** in the Assembly, not the Governor's own assessment.
[2:6] - 3. **Hung Assembly** → invite parties in a **set order of preference**.
[2:7] - **A–R:** Sarkaria said use Art. 356 sparingly **because** parties in power at the Centre had **misused** it → both true, R explains A.
[2:8] - Courts relied on Sarkaria in **S.R. Bommai (1994)** and **Rameshwar Prasad (2006)**.
[2:9] ~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.15 p.239-240, ch.16 p.253; NextGen Polity - APPSC Exams p.313-314
## SUB 3 | Legislative, Administrative and Financial Recommendations
[3:0] TABLE head: Sphere | Recommendation
  [3:0.0] Legislative | Consult the States (institutionalised process) before a law on a Concurrent List subject
  [3:0.1] Legislative | Residuary taxation stays with Parliament; other residuary powers to the Concurrent List
  [3:0.2] Administrative | Strengthen All-India Services and create more (Rajamannar: abolish)
  [3:0.3] Administrative | Centre may deploy central armed forces even without the State's request (consultation desirable)
  [3:0.4] Administrative | Zonal Councils constituted afresh and reactivated
  [3:0.5] Financial | Cesses and surcharges minimal, time-bound, then merged into the shareable pool
  [3:0.6] Financial | Present Finance Commission – Planning Commission division is reasonable; continue
  [3:0.7] Financial | NDC renamed National Economic and Development Council
  [3:0.8] Financial | Net proceeds of corporation tax permissibly shareable
[3:1] - Sarkaria = first official body to make **cooperative federalism** the guiding principle ("federalism is a functional arrangement for co-operative action").
[3:2] ~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.15 p.239-240; NextGen Polity - APPSC Exams p.313-314
