=== Telugu Desam Party – Founding, Firsts and National Role
# Founding and first victory
- Founded by **NTR** on **29 March 1982** on **Telugu self-respect (Telugu atma gauravam)**; ideology: none specific beyond safeguarding the **political, economic, social and cultural foundations of Telugu-speaking people**.
- Won power within **nine months** – **January 1983** Assembly election – ending the **Congress's unbroken rule since 1956**.
- NTR was CM three times (1983-84, 1984-89, 1994-95) – **about seven years** in all.
- **Pragathi Patham:** TDP's **15-point programme** (NTR era) – welfare thrust: drinking water, SC/ST/BC programmes, farm inputs.
# National role
- **8th Lok Sabha (1984):** TDP with **30 seats** = **main opposition party** – the **first regional party** to reach that position.
- **1988:** NTR = **chairman of the National Front**, which formed the **V.P. Singh** government in **1989**.
- **Gujarat censure motion (debated 30 April, voted 1 May 2002):** the **TDP** (backing the NDA from outside) **walked out before the vote** – it did not take part; the **AIADMK** and National Conference abstained, the **BSP voted against**; the motion was defeated (276-182).
- **2018:** TDP **left the NDA** over **Special Category Status** for residuary AP; **2024:** back with the NDA – TDP leads AP's ruling coalition with **BJP** (and Jana Sena) support.
- AP politics: **Congress vs TDP** (1983-2014); **TDP vs YSRCP** since 2014.

=== Rise, Role and Impact of Regional Parties
# Features and classification (Laxmikanth)
- Operates within one State/region; articulates a cultural, linguistic, religious or ethnic identity; seeks power at State level and **greater State autonomy**.
| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Regional culture / ethnicity | Akali Dal, National Conference, DMK, **Telugu Desam**, Shiv Sena, AGP |
| All-India outlook, no national base | Samajwadi Party, NCP |
| Split from national parties | BJD, RJD, Trinamool, **YSR Congress** |
| Personalised (charismatic leader) | short-lived |
# Causes of rise
- Cultural/ethnic pluralism; **economic disparities and regional imbalance (neglect)**; linguistic reorganisation of States; **failure of national parties** to meet regional aspirations; charismatic leaders; Congress centralisation; caste and religion.
- Roots of regionalism: **historical, geographical, linguistic, religious, ethnic**. In Andhra, the **Justice Party** era awakened non-Brahmin agrarian castes – **Reddys, Kammas, Kapus, Velamas**.
- After **1989** coalition arithmetic raised their weight.
# Role and dysfunctions
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Coalitions | **Kingmakers** – supplied Speakers, Union Ministers, survival votes; PM became "first among equals" |
| Federalism | Challenged one-party dominance; made Centre-State bargaining real |
| Merits | Representation of backward regions, balanced development, wider voter choice |
| Demerits | **Parochialism**, instability, populism, inter-State disputes |
- **Special Category Status** for residuary AP – TDP left the NDA over it (**2018**).
- BJP defeated regional parties: **BJD (Odisha, 2024)**, **AAP (Delhi, 2025)**, **Trinamool (West Bengal, 2026)**.
