=== Sarkaria Commission at a Glance
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Set up by | **Central Government**, **9 June 1983** (Indira Gandhi government) |
| Chairman | Justice **Rajinder (Ranjit) Singh Sarkaria**, retired **Supreme Court** judge |
| Members | **B. Sivaraman** and **Dr S.R. Sen** – **3** in all (not Rajamannar, Punchhi, V. Shankar, Hanumanthaiah) |
| Objective | Examine the **relationship and balance of power between Centre and States**; suggest changes **within the existing constitutional framework** |
| Report | **January 1988** (worked 1983-87); **247** recommendations |
- Subject = **Centre-State relations** – not President-PM, legislature-executive, executive-judiciary, reservations, inter-State disputes or agricultural trade.
- It **rejected** curtailing the Centre: a **strong Centre** is essential, but not centralisation ("blood pressure at the centre, anaemia at the periphery").
- Report year: **1988** (a few keys, e.g. BPSC 2018, say 1987).
~~ Sources: NextGen Polity - APPSC Exams p.313-314; Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.15 p.238-240

=== Recommendations on the Governor
- Governor should be an **eminent person** in some walk of life.
- **From outside the State**; a **detached figure**, not intimately connected with local politics; **not taken part in politics in the recent past** (UPSC 2019: Sarkaria, not ARC, Rajamannar or NCRWC).
- **Chief Minister** to be consulted; procedure to be in the Constitution.
- **Secure 5-year term**, not disturbed except for **extremely compelling reasons** – the first body to recommend this.
- Governor cannot dismiss a ministry that commands a majority; decide on **Bills within a specified period**.
- Convention in practice: Governor is a **person from outside the State** (no age bar, not appointed by the CM, no legal qualification).
- **NOT** recommended: Governor "should have **travelled extensively** in the State", "must be an eminent **political leader**", "should have **served the Government** long", "other-State person shall not be appointed".
~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.15 p.239-241; NextGen Polity - APPSC Exams p.313

=== Inter-State Council and Article 356
# Inter-Governmental Council
- A **permanent Inter-State Council** called the **Inter-Governmental Council** under **Art. 263** – the main implemented recommendation; ISC set up **28 May 1990** (ISC Secretariat 1991).
- "Establish an **Inter-Government Council in place of Inter-State Council**" = **Sarkaria** (not Rajamannar, Punchhi or the ARC).
# Article 356
- 1. Use **very sparingly**, in extreme cases, as a **last resort**, after a **warning** to the State.
- 2. Doubt over majority → **floor test** in the Assembly, not the Governor's own assessment.
- 3. **Hung Assembly** → invite parties in a **set order of preference**.
- **A–R:** Sarkaria said use Art. 356 sparingly **because** parties in power at the Centre had **misused** it → both true, R explains A.
- Courts relied on Sarkaria in **S.R. Bommai (1994)** and **Rameshwar Prasad (2006)**.
~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.15 p.239-240, ch.16 p.253; NextGen Polity - APPSC Exams p.313-314

=== Legislative, Administrative and Financial Recommendations
| Sphere | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Legislative | **Consult the States** (institutionalised process) before a law on a **Concurrent List** subject |
| Legislative | **Residuary taxation** stays with **Parliament**; other residuary powers to the **Concurrent List** |
| Administrative | **Strengthen All-India Services** and create more (Rajamannar: abolish) |
| Administrative | Centre may deploy **central armed forces even without the State's request** (consultation desirable) |
| Administrative | **Zonal Councils** constituted afresh and reactivated |
| Financial | **Cesses and surcharges** minimal, **time-bound**, then merged into the shareable pool |
| Financial | Present **Finance Commission – Planning Commission** division is **reasonable**; continue |
| Financial | **NDC** renamed **National Economic and Development Council** |
| Financial | Net proceeds of **corporation tax** permissibly shareable |
- Sarkaria = first official body to make **cooperative federalism** the guiding principle ("federalism is a functional arrangement for co-operative action").
~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.15 p.239-240; NextGen Polity - APPSC Exams p.313-314
