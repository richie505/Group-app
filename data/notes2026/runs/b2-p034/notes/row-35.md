=== Financial Committees – Composition (Estimates, PAC, COPU)
| Committee | Strength | Rajya Sabha members | Chairman |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Estimates** | **30 (largest)** | **None – all 30 from the Lok Sabha** | Appointed by the Speaker; invariably from the **ruling party** |
| **Public Accounts (PAC)** | **22 = 15 LS + 7 RS** | Yes – 7 | Appointed by the Speaker; **Opposition** member since 1967 (convention) |
| **Public Undertakings (COPU)** | **22 = 15 LS + 7 RS** | Yes – 7 | Appointed by the Speaker from its LS members |
- **The three financial committees:** **PAC, Estimates, COPU** – the Business Advisory Committee, Petitions, Government Assurances and Library committees are **not** financial committees.
- **Only committee with no RS member:** the **Estimates Committee** (PAC, COPU, Government Assurances, Petitions all have RS members/counterparts). Committee on Empowerment of Women is joint (20 LS + 10 RS).
- **Election:** members elected **every year** by **proportional representation – single transferable vote**; **term one year**; **Ministers ineligible**.
- **PAC** is a **joint committee** of both Houses (LS members elected by the LS, 7 RS members associated) – not a select committee of the LS alone.
- **⚠ Trap:** "Estimates = 20 LS + 10 RS" is **false** (that is the SC/ST Welfare or Women's Empowerment committee).
- **Budget** = control **before** spending; financial committees = scrutiny **after** – they support, not replace, the budgetary process.
> Exam angle: "no RS member" (asked ×9) → Estimates; "largest" → Estimates; "not a financial committee" → Business Advisory.

=== Public Accounts Committee – Functions, Chairman, Reports
- **Oldest parliamentary committee:** PAC, first set up in **1921** under the **Government of India Act, 1919**.
- **Function:** examines the **Appropriation Accounts, Finance Accounts** and the **CAG's audit reports** – the financial accounts of the Government; checks that money was spent "**within the scope of the demand**"; looks at waste, loss and nugatory expenditure.
- **"Watchdog"** on departmental expenditure and irregularities; the **CAG** is its "guide, friend and philosopher" and assists it.
- **Limits:** a **post-mortem** check; not concerned with **policy**; recommendations **advisory**; cannot appoint officials or issue orders.
- **Chairman:** **appointed (nominated) by the Speaker** from its members; since **1967** an **Opposition** member by **convention** – not a rule of procedure, law or Presidential order.
- **Reports:** submitted to the **Speaker / Lok Sabha** (i.e. to Parliament; a copy laid in the RS) – not the President, PM, FM or CAG.

=== Estimates Committee and Committee on Public Undertakings
# Estimates Committee
- **Origin:** traces to the Standing Financial Committee (1921); first post-independence Estimates Committee **1950** on the recommendation of **John Mathai** (then FM); 25 members, raised to **30 in 1956**.
- **Function (Rule 310):** report what **economies, improvements in organisation, efficiency or administrative reform** consistent with the policy underlying the estimates may be effected; suggest **alternative policies** for efficiency and economy in administration; suggest the form of the estimates → a **"continuous economy committee"**.
- **⚠ Key:** "Estimates Committee – which reform to ensure efficiency?" → **administrative reform** (the words of the rule), not new legislation, defence, electoral or judicial reform.
- **Limits:** examines estimates only after they are voted; cannot question policy laid down by Parliament; no CAG assistance; advisory.
- **Chairmen asked:** **Ummareddy Venkateswarlu** (1999-2004, 13th LS); **Sanjay Jaiswal** (18th LS, as of 2026).
# Committee on Public Undertakings (COPU)
- Created **1964** on the recommendation of the **Krishna Menon Committee**; 15 (10 LS + 5 RS) raised to **22 (15 + 7) in 1974**.
- Examines reports and accounts of public undertakings and the CAG's reports on them; whether they run on sound business principles; not major policy or day-to-day administration.

=== Other Standing Committees – Petitions, Subordinate Legislation, Assurances, Offices of Profit
# Six groups of standing committees (Laxmikanth)
- **Financial** (PAC, Estimates, COPU); **24 DRSCs**; **to inquire** (Petitions, Privileges, Ethics); **to scrutinise and control** (Government Assurances, Subordinate Legislation, Papers Laid, **Welfare of SCs and STs**, Empowerment of Women, Offices of Profit); **day-to-day business** (Business Advisory, Private Members' Bills, Rules, Absence of Members); **house-keeping** (General Purposes, House, Library, Salaries of MPs).
- **Welfare of SCs and STs** is itself a standing committee (30 = 20 LS + 10 RS; **Ministers cannot serve**; examines the reports of the **National Commissions for SCs and STs** and welfare programmes) – it is the **only one of PAC / Estimates / COPU / SC-ST that is not a financial committee**.
- **No parliamentary committee on Welfare of Minorities** (there are committees on Public Undertakings, Government Assurances, Estimates; OBC welfare is an LS committee).
| Committee | Function | Strength / note |
|---|---|---|
| **Petitions** | Petitions on Bills and public-interest matters; representations of citizens – the **"Ombudsman"** of Parliament | LS 15, RS 10 |
| **Subordinate Legislation** | Whether **rules, regulations, sub-rules, bye-laws** made under powers **delegated by Parliament or conferred by the Constitution** are properly exercised by the Executive | 15 each House (1953) |
| Government Assurances | Tracks ministers' assurances till implemented | LS 15, RS 10 (1953) |
| **Joint Committee on Offices of Profit** | Which offices should disqualify MPs | **15 = 10 LS + 5 RS** |
| Privileges | Breach of privilege | LS 15, RS 10 |
| Ethics | Members' conduct | **RS 4 Mar 1997**, LS 16 May 2000 |
| Business Advisory | Allots time for Government and other business | LS 15, **Speaker chairs** |
- **Delegated legislation** = rule-making power **delegated by the legislature to the executive**; watched by the Committee on Subordinate Legislation.
- **Lok Sabha-only:** Committee on Private Members' Bills and Resolutions; Committee on Absence of Members.
- **Watchdog group** (NextGen): Subordinate Legislation, Government Assurances, Estimates, PAC, COPU, DRSCs – **not** the Business Advisory Committee.

=== Departmentally Related Standing Committees (DRSCs)
- **Set up 1993** (17) on the recommendation of the **Rules Committee** of the Lok Sabha; **24 since 2004** = **16 under the Lok Sabha + 8 under the Rajya Sabha**.
- **Each: 31 members = 21 LS + 10 RS** → standing committees draw LS and RS members in the ratio **2 : 1**; **quorum 11**; **Ministers ineligible**; term one year.
- **Functions:** examine **Demands for Grants** (between general discussion and voting), **Bills referred**, **annual reports** and long-term policy documents of their ministries; no cut-motion-type suggestions.
- **Regulators:** the **DRSCs** and **ad hoc committees** (e.g. the 2G JPC) may review independent regulators (telecom, insurance, electricity) – not the Finance Commission, FSLRC or NITI Aayog (UPSC 2019 → 1 and 2).
- **Chairs asked (APPSC G1 2004):** Railways – **Basudeb Acharia**, Defence – **Balasaheb Vikhe Patil** (14th LS) and External Affairs – Laxminarayan Pandey are given as correct; the Committee on **Food – A. Narendra** pairing is the **wrong** one (printed key; chair lists not independently verified).

=== Ad Hoc Committees, JPCs and the Role of Committees
- **Role:** Parliament exercises control over the **administration** mainly **through parliamentary committees** – detailed scrutiny the whole House has no time for (not via ministries' advisory committees or writs).
- **Ad hoc committees:** temporary, end with the report – (i) **Inquiry committees** (e.g. on the conduct of members during the President's address, Railway Convention Committee, MPLADS); (ii) **Advisory committees** – **Select / Joint Committees on Bills**, Committee on the **Draft Five Year Plan** → "all the above".
- A Bill goes to a Select Committee (one House) or Joint Committee (both Houses) by a **motion adopted in the House**.
