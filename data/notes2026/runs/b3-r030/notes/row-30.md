=== Poverty Committees – Chronology, Chairmen and Firsts
| Committee / study | Set up – report | Chair | Known for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Planning Commission Working Group | **1962** | – | First official line: **₹20 rural / ₹25 urban** per capita per month (1960-61 prices) |
| **Dandekar and Rath** – "Poverty in India" | **1971** | **V.M. Dandekar, N. Rath** | **First systematic assessment** of poverty; first to use **calories**: line = spending adequate for **2,250 kcal/day in both rural and urban areas** (1960-61 NSS data) |
| **Alagh Task Force** | Formed 1977 – report **1979** | **Y.K. Alagh** | Line based **exclusively on nutritional requirements**: **2,400 kcal rural / 2,100 kcal urban** |
| **Lakdawala Expert Group** | Set up **1989** – report **1993** | **D.T. Lakdawala** | Poverty estimation methodology; **State-specific** poverty lines |
| **Tendulkar Expert Group** | Set up **Dec 2005** – report **Dec 2009** | **Prof. Suresh D. Tendulkar** | "Fresh look" at poverty lines; revised methodology |
| **Rangarajan Expert Group** | Set up **May-June 2012** – report **June 2014** | **Dr C. Rangarajan** | Members **Mahendra Dev, K. Sundaram, Mahesh Vyas, K.L. Datta**; last Planning Commission group on poverty |
| NITI Aayog Task Force on Elimination of Poverty | 2015 | Arvind Panagariya | – |
- **Chronological order:** Dandekar-Rath (1971) → Alagh (1979) → Lakdawala (1989/1993) → Tendulkar (2005/2009) → Rangarajan (2012/2014).
- **Not poverty-line committees:** Vijay Kelkar (tax / fiscal), S.P. Gupta (employment), Raj Krishna ("Hindu rate of growth"), Amartya Sen (no committee); **Rangarajan also chaired the 12th Finance Commission** – the setting in which other PYQs use his name.
- Both the **Lakdawala and Tendulkar** groups were poverty-estimation committees; a question asking "which one" usually wants the latest offered (Tendulkar).
> Most asked angle (APPSC 2011-2025, UPPSC, RAS, JPSC): who chaired which group and when; "first" = Dandekar-Rath; "nutrition only" = Alagh; "2005 fresh look" = Tendulkar; "new committee to identify the poor (2012)" = Rangarajan.

=== Calorie Norms and the Method of Each Committee
| Committee | Method / basis |
|---|---|
| Dandekar-Rath | **2,250 kcal** per day, **same for rural and urban** |
| **Alagh** | Poverty line = per capita consumption expenditure meeting **2,400 kcal (rural)** and **2,100 kcal (urban)** – purely nutritional |
| **Lakdawala** | Kept the calorie anchor (₹49.09 rural / ₹56.64 urban per month at 1973-74 prices); **State-specific lines**; update by **CPI-AL (rural)** and **CPI-IW (urban)**; stopped scaling to National Accounts |
| **Tendulkar** | Moved **away from the calorie anchor**; basket covers spending on **food, education, health, electricity, clothing and footwear**; **MRP** data; **one urban poverty line basket (25.7% urban HCR) used for both rural and urban** |
| **Rangarajan** | Separate **food and non-food** baskets; **2,155 kcal rural / 2,090 kcal urban** plus protein and fat norms; **MMRP** data |
- **Why State poverty lines differ:** **price levels vary from State to State** (inter-state price differentials), not poverty rates, GSDP or PDS quality.
- "Planning Commission poverty line": **2,400 kcal rural, 2,100 kcal urban** – the rural figure is the higher one.
- The basis of urban poverty estimation (Lakdawala) = **CPI-IW**; rural = **CPI-AL**.
> UPSC 2019 (State lines differ because of prices) and the calorie pair 2,400/2,100 are the repeats; "single urban basket" belongs to Tendulkar, not Rangarajan.

=== Recall Periods, NSS Data and Price Indices
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **URP** (Uniform Reference/Recall Period) | **30-day recall for all items**; used up to 1993-94 |
| **MRP** (Mixed Reference Period) | **365-day recall** for five low-frequency items (clothing, footwear, durables, education, institutional medical care), 30 days for the rest; basis of Tendulkar |
| **MMRP** (Modified MRP) | **7-day recall for some food items**; first used 2009-10; used by Rangarajan |
| CPI-AL / CPI-IW | Update rural / urban lines (Lakdawala) |
- URP and MRP measure the **incidence of poverty** (not exports, urbanisation or demonetisation).
- The **61st NSS round (2004-05)** gave **both URP and MRP** distributions.
- Poverty is estimated from NSS **household consumer expenditure** surveys (MPCE), normally every five years; now **HCES 2022-23 and 2023-24**.

=== BPL Identification Committees
| Person / body | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **N.C. Saxena** (Ministry of Rural Development, report Aug 2009) | Method for the **rural BPL Census** – automatic exclusion / inclusion, ranking the rest on a 10-point scale |
| **S.R. Hashim** (Planning Commission expert group) | Identification of **urban BPL** families |
| Abhijit Sen (MoRD, 2012) | Use of SECC indicators to choose beneficiaries of rural programmes |
- **Automatic exclusion (Saxena):** income-tax payers, owners of motor vehicles / mechanised farm equipment, salaried above ₹10,000 a month, large landowners.
- **Automatic inclusion:** **PVTGs**, Maha Dalit groups, single-woman-headed and disabled-breadwinner households, destitute, homeless, bonded labourers.
- Saxena = rural BPL; **Hashim = urban BPL**; Tendulkar / Lakdawala / Rangarajan = poverty estimation.

=== NITI Aayog National Multidimensional Poverty Index
- **Body:** **NITI Aayog**, with technical inputs from **OPHI and UNDP**; **Alkire-Foster** method.
- **3 equally weighted dimensions** – health, education, standard of living; **12 indicators** (Global MPI 10) – the two added are **maternal health and bank account**.
- Standard-of-living indicators: cooking fuel, sanitation, drinking water, electricity, housing, assets, bank account (years of schooling is an **education** indicator).
- Data: NFHS-4 (2015-16), NFHS-5 (2019-21).
- **Discussion paper (Jan 2024):** headcount **29.17% (2013-14) → 11.28% (2022-23)**; **24.82 crore** people exited in 9 years. Earlier: 55.3% (2005-06) → 14.96% (2019-21).
- **AP:** MPI 0.053 → 0.025; headcount 6.06% (2019-21), 9th.
> New PYQ angle (AP High Court 2026): the 2022-23 MPI headcount, 11.28%.

=== Early Estimates and Poverty-Line Debates
- **Dadabhai Naoroji** ("Poverty and Un-British Rule in India"): subsistence line **₹16-35 per capita per year** at 1867-68 prices – the earliest estimate.
- **National Planning Committee (1938):** ₹15-20 per month; **Bombay Plan (1944):** ₹75 per year.
- The Planning Commission estimated poverty from NSS data **since the Sixth Plan**; the Ministry of Rural Development runs the **BPL Census**.
- **Debate:** a higher line raises fiscal cost, a lower one excludes the needy; Tendulkar line called "too low" → Rangarajan group (2012).
- **A-R (UPPSC 1997):** there is no clear picture of the extent of poverty (estimates differ by method) **and** poverty-eradication strategy has shifted – both true, but R does not explain A.
