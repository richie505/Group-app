[1:1] - Decline accelerated: 0.74 points a year (1993-94 to 2004-05) → **2.18 points a year (2004-05 to 2011-12)**.
[1:2] - **After the 1991 New Economic Policy:** the official quinquennial estimates show **both rural and urban poverty declining** (all-India 36.0% in 1993-94 → 26.1% in 1999-2000, Lakdawala); thin-round data showed a short rise in rural poverty in 1991-92.
[1:3] - **MDG:** India used the Tendulkar 2011-12 figure **21.9%** in its MDG report; target – halve poverty from 47.8% (1990) to **23.9% by 2015**; India **met the poverty-halving target (Goal 1)** but missed hunger, maternal health and sanitation.
[1:4] - **2011 to 2015:** more than **90 million** lifted out of poverty (World Bank).
[1:5] - **SDG 1:** **end poverty in all its forms everywhere by 2030** (no numerical ratio).
[1:6] - World Bank $3-a-day extreme poverty: 27.1% (2011-12) → **5.3% (2022-23)**; MPI 55.3% (2005-06) → 14.96% (2019-21) → 11.28% (2022-23).
