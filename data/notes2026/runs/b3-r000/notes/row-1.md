=== Sectoral Shares of GDP – Trend and Ranking
# Order and trend
- **Decreasing order of contribution to GDP now: services > industry > agriculture** (UPSC 2007, NDA 2014, UPPCS 2017) – the **tertiary (service) sector is the largest source of national income** in the 21st century.
- **At Independence agriculture** was the largest contributor (about half of GDP); over the plan period the composition **shifted in favour of the service sector**.
- **As the economy develops the tertiary share rises**; since 1980 it has shown an **increasing trend**; agriculture's contribution is **decreasing**.
| Year | Agriculture / primary | Industry | Services |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1950-51 | over half (SI 2017 key: **56.5%**) | about **13%** | **30.3%** |
| 1980-81 → 1990-91 | 38.1% → 34.0% | 25.9% → 24.9% | 37.65% → 42.55% |
| 1991 | about **29%** | – | – |
| 2001-03 | about **26%** (APPSC JL 2003 key) | – | 50.37% (2000-01) |
| 2006-07 (Economic Survey 2006-07) | 18.5% | **26.4%** (between 25 and 30) | 55.1% |
| 2012-13 | about **13.7%** (SI 2017 key 13.6%) | – | – |
| 2017-18 | about 17% | 29.2% | about **54-55%** (55.2% AE; APPSC key **54.4%**) |
| 2025-26 | **17.88%** (about 18%) | manufacturing stagnant | **54.3%** |
- **Agriculture around 2008-13: about 15%** of GDP (BPSC 2008, UPRO 2013 key) – now about 18% after the revisions.
# New GDP series (base 2022-23, Feb 2026)
- **2024-25, current prices: primary 21.4%, secondary 25.8%, tertiary 52.9%** – figures to quote now; the new series shows higher agriculture and industry shares, lower services.
- **The 21.4% primary share includes mining**, so it is not comparable with the old agriculture-forestry-fishing share.
- **Manufacturing 14.7%** in the new series (not 14.3%); built from **ASI** (factories) + **ASUSE** (unincorporated units).
# Sub-sectors
- **2000-01 (constant prices) order:** agriculture-forestry-fishing > trade-hotels-transport-communication > manufacturing > finance-insurance-real estate-business services (UPSC 2002: **1, 3, 2, 4**).
- **Highest services share in 2006:** trade, hotels, transport and communication; **fastest-growing service 2006-2010: communication**.
- **Mining and quarrying ≈ 3% of GVA** in 2016-17 at 2011-12 prices (about 2.4% at current prices) – small output share, key raw-material supplier.
- **1999-2008 (1999-2000 prices):** within the primary sector the share of **forestry** declined steeply (APPSC G2 2011 key).
- **Fishing and aquaculture** (old series): **0.8% (2011-12) → 1.3% (2022-23)**; manufacturing 17.4% → 14.3%.
- **Agriculture's importance** shows in its contribution to **national income and employment, industrial raw materials, trade and foodgrains – all**; its large contribution to GNP is in terms of **value of output**, not exports or revenue.
- **Highest growth in industrial production** (APPSC G1 2003): **manufacturing**.

=== Sector Classification – Primary, Secondary, Tertiary and Beyond
| Sector | Other name | Activities |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Agricultural sector; most dependent on natural resources | Agriculture, livestock, **dairy farm**, forestry, **fishing**, **mining and quarrying**, **mineral exploration** |
| Secondary | Industrial sector | **Manufacturing** (large, medium, small), textile mills, **weaving cloth**, processing, **construction**, electricity, gas, **water supply** |
| Tertiary | **Service sector** | Trade, **marketing**, **transport**, **storage** (incl. storage of farm produce), communication, **hotels**, banking, finance, **real estate**, public administration and defence, education, health, social work, personal services |
| Quaternary | **Knowledge sector** | R&D, information, software, consulting |
| Quinary | Top decision-making | Government policy, scientific research |
- **Water supply = secondary** (industrial) sector (APPSC G1), not tertiary.
- **Real estate** is a tertiary activity – **not part of manufacturing**.
- **Service sector** includes **transport and communication and hotels** – not mining and quarrying, forestry or fishing (UPPCS 2004: **II and III**).
- **UPSC 2024 pairs:** storage = secondary ✗ (tertiary); dairy farm = primary ✓; mineral exploration = tertiary ✗ (primary); weaving cloth = secondary ✓ → **only two**.
# Real sector vs financial sector
| Basis | Real sector | Financial sector |
|---|---|---|
| Involves | Production and flow of goods and services | **Money and financial assets** |
| Examples | **Farmers harvesting crops, textile mills making fabric** | **Bank lending**, rupee-denominated bonds overseas |
- UPSC 2022: real sector = **1 and 2 only**.
# GVA groups (national accounts)
| Group | Components |
|---|---|
| Agriculture and allied | Agriculture, **livestock**, forestry, fishing |
| Industry | **Mining and quarrying**, manufacturing, electricity-gas-water supply, construction |
| Services | Trade-hotels-transport-communication; finance-real estate-professional services; **public administration, defence**, other services |
- **Mining = primary** in the three-sector scheme but counted under **Industry** in GVA groups and the AP Socio-Economic Survey.
- **Compilers:** GDP and sectoral GVA – **CSO, now NSO (MoSPI)**; State GSDP and sector GVA – **Directorate of Economics and Statistics** of each State; ASI and ASUSE – **NSO** (not the State DES).

=== Output vs Employment – the Mismatch
- **The maximum number of people work in the primary sector** (agriculture) – the **largest employer**, though it gives the least output; the tertiary sector gives the most output (APPSC JL 2004, AD 2017).
- Agriculture employs **46.1%** of workers (PLFS 2023-24); earlier **more than 50%** of the working population depended on the primary sector (WBCS 2017).
| Sector (2021-22) | Share of GDP | Share of workforce |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | 21.82% | **43.96%** |
| Tertiary | 53.89% | **33.80%** |
- Services were **22.82%** of employment in 1993-94 (agriculture 64.75%).
- **Manufacturing** employs only **11.4%** of workers (2023-24); **MSMEs** are the second-largest employer after agriculture.
- **Organised-sector employment growth declined** in recent years mainly because the **manufacturing share of GDP did not increase** (APPSC AD 2017).

=== Structural Change and Services-led Growth
- **Structural change** = changing shares of the primary, secondary and tertiary sectors in national income; it occurred **because the process of economic growth was initiated** (APPSC G1: A and R true, **R explains A**).
- **Shifting of workers from agriculture to other sectors** = an indication of **economic growth** (APPSC G2 2011), not backwardness.
| Model | Author | Prediction |
|---|---|---|
| Three-sector | **Colin Clark and Allan Fisher** | As income rises, labour and output shift **primary → secondary → tertiary** |
| Dual-sector | **W. Arthur Lewis (1954)** | Surplus farm labour moves to modern industry until it runs out – the **Lewis turning point** |
- **Post-1991 growth was service-led** – not import-, export- or agriculture-led (APPSC 2018); in **1992-2001 services** grew fastest and contributed most to GDP.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Missing middle | Agriculture → services **without a manufacturing-led stage** |
| Premature deindustrialisation | Services **generally require high skills and may not absorb semi-skilled farm workers** → jobless growth |
| Premature leapfrogging | **Services absorb value while manufacturing fails to absorb surplus farm labour** |
| Nominal vs real share | Fall in manufacturing's nominal share = **relative-price effect** (Economic Survey 2025-26) |
| Share logic | A sector growing slower than GDP loses share even if its output rises |
- **EAC-PM Chairman** (Nov 2025): services-led growth cannot be sustained without a manufacturing base.
- India: **2.9%** of global manufacturing value added; **1.8%** of world merchandise exports; **44th** on the Economic Complexity Index.

=== Andhra Pradesh – GSVA Composition
| Year (source) | Agriculture & allied | Industry | Services |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2024-25 (AP Budget 2026-27) | **37%** | **21%** | **42%** |
| 2025-26 (AP SES 2025-26) | about 33% | 24% | **43%** |
- AP has **a farm share well above the national share** (India below 20%).
- **AP growth 2025-26 (constant prices):** fishing and aquaculture fastest within agriculture (17.69%); **construction** fastest within industry (14.18%); **public administration** lowest (0.05%).
- **Manufacturing only about 11% of GSDP** → **Industrial Development Policy 4.0 (2024-29)**.
- Telangana's services grew from 1.25 to 1.47 times AP's (2013-14 to 2024-25), led by **real estate, IT and trade-hotels**.
