=== Inflation Target – What, Who and How Much
- **Inflation targeting** = **public announcement of a medium-term numerical target** for inflation by the central bank (APPSC G2).
- **Target: 4% CPI-Combined, tolerance ±2 percentage points** (band **2–6%**).
- **Who fixes it:** the **Government of India, in consultation with the RBI** (Sec 45ZA) – not the RBI, MPC, NITI Aayog or Finance Commission (RAS 2018 ×2); **RBI/MPC must achieve it**.
- Set **once every five years**, notified in the **Official Gazette**.
| Notified | Period | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **August 2016** | to **31 March 2021** | **4% ±2%** (APPSC G2) |
| March 2021 | April 2021 – March 2026 | Retained |
| 25 March 2026 | 1 April 2026 – 31 March 2031 | Retained (second time) |
- **Anchor**: headline **CPI-C** compiled by **NSO (MoSPI)**; new series **base 2024** released **12 Feb 2026** (earlier base 2012); not WPI.
- **Failure (Sec 45ZN):** average CPI inflation **above 6% or below 2% for three consecutive quarters** (NextGen's "3 consecutive months" is wrong) → RBI reports reasons, remedial action and time to return to target; first invoked **2022**.
- Primary objective under FIT: **price stability while keeping growth in mind**; MPC sets the policy rate to achieve **the inflation target**.

=== Monetary Policy Committee – Composition and Powers
| Member | How |
|---|---|
| **RBI Governor** | **Chairperson**; **casting vote** on a tie (no veto) |
| Deputy Governor (monetary policy) | Ex officio |
| One RBI officer | Nominated by the **RBI Central Board** |
| **Three external members** | Appointed by the **Central Government**; **4-year term, no reappointment** |
- **6 members** (3 RBI + 3 external) – not 12, not reconstituted every year, **chaired by the RBI Governor, not the Finance Minister** (UPSC 2017: only I).
- MPC **fixes the benchmark policy (repo) rate** (APPSC G2; APPSC GS 2019) to **control inflation** – it does not dictate fiscal policy, trade or industry (APPSC ASO).
- Decisions by **majority**, **binding on the RBI**; **quorum 4**; meets **at least 4 times** a year by law (six in practice); first met **October 2016**; constituted under **Sec 45ZB**.
- RBI publishes a **Monetary Policy Report every six months**.
- **Governors:** Shaktikanta Das (Dec 2018 – Dec 2024; "present Governor" in the APPSC G4 2022 paper); **Sanjay Malhotra since 11 Dec 2024**.

=== Committees and the Legal Framework
| Committee | Year | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Sukhamoy Chakravarty** – reviewed the **working of the monetary system** | **1985** | Monetary targeting with feedback (M3) |
| N. Vaghul | 1987 | Money-market blueprint → Commercial Paper 1990 |
| S.S. Tarapore | 1997; 2006 | Capital account convertibility |
| **Urjit Patel** – Expert Committee to **Revise and Strengthen the Monetary Policy Framework** (set up by RBI) | **2014** | **Flexible inflation targeting**: CPI-C anchor, **4% ±2%**, an MPC to set rates; drop multiple indicators |
- Chronology: Chakravarty 1985 – Vaghul 1987 – Tarapore 1997 – Urjit Patel 2014.
| Instrument | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Monetary Policy Framework Agreement** | Signed by Government and RBI on **20 February 2015**; not a statute; CPI below 6% by January 2016, then 4% ±2% |
| **RBI (Amendment) Act, 2016** (May 2016) | Amended the **RBI Act, 1934** – inserted **Sections 45Z to 45ZO**; **statutory basis for FIT** and the MPC (SSC 2019; APPSC FRO 2022: **2016**) |
- Section map: **45ZA** = target; **45ZB** = MPC; **45ZN** = failure. Not the BR Act, DICGC Act or IFCI Act.

=== Evolution, Stance Record and Global Tightening
| Phase | Approach |
|---|---|
| 1985–1998 | Monetary targeting with feedback |
| 1998–2014 | **Multiple indicator approach** – criticised as **opaque** |
| 2014–2016 | Glide path: Urjit Patel Committee, 2015 Agreement |
| 2016 onwards | Flexible inflation targeting with the MPC |
- **"Seven Ages of India's Monetary Policy"**: Governor **Shaktikanta Das**, **24 January 2020**, St. Stephen's College – the multiple indicator approach "served fairly well from **1998-99 to 2008-09**": average growth **6.4%**, WPI inflation **5.4%** (APPSC FRO: both statements correct).
| Period | Repo | Stance |
|---|---|---|
| May 2022 – Feb 2023 | **4.00% → 6.50%** | Withdrawal of accommodation (tightening bias) |
| Apr 2025 | Cut to 6.0% | Neutral → **accommodative** |
| Jun 2025 | 5.50%; CRR cut 100 bps | Back to neutral |
| Dec 2025 | **5.25%** (125 bps since Feb 2025) | Neutral |
| Feb, Jun, Aug 2026 | Held 5.25% | Neutral |
| **7 Oct 2026** | **Hiked to 5.50%** | **Calibrated tightening** |
- **Post-pandemic:** many central banks **hiked rates**, assuming they **can counter rising consumer prices by monetary means** – both statements correct, II explains I (UPSC 2023).
- June 2026: as major central banks geared up to tighten, the MPC **held 5.25% and cut the FY27 growth forecast**; spillovers = capital outflows, rupee pressure, **imported inflation**.
- Debate: critics want a **core** target, since food and supply shocks lie outside MPC control. AP retail inflation **4.9%** vs national **3.9%** (May 2026).
- **CBDC:** in Aug 2021 Das said trials could start **by December 2021**; the e₹ pilots actually began in **Nov–Dec 2022** (general knowledge, not checked).
