## SUB 0 | Inflation Target – What, Who and How Much
[0:0] - **Inflation targeting** = **public announcement of a medium-term numerical target** for inflation by the central bank (APPSC G2).
[0:1] - **Target: 4% CPI-Combined, tolerance ±2 percentage points** (band **2–6%**).
[0:2] - **Who fixes it:** the **Government of India, in consultation with the RBI** (Sec 45ZA) – not the RBI, MPC, NITI Aayog or Finance Commission (RAS 2018 ×2); **RBI/MPC must achieve it**.
[0:3] - Set **once every five years**, notified in the **Official Gazette**.
[0:4] TABLE head: Notified | Period | Target
  [0:4.0] August 2016 | to 31 March 2021 | 4% ±2% (APPSC G2)
  [0:4.1] March 2021 | April 2021 – March 2026 | Retained
  [0:4.2] 25 March 2026 | 1 April 2026 – 31 March 2031 | Retained (second time)
[0:5] - **Anchor**: headline **CPI-C** compiled by **NSO (MoSPI)**; new series **base 2024** released **12 Feb 2026** (earlier base 2012); not WPI.
[0:6] - **Failure (Sec 45ZN):** average CPI inflation **above 6% or below 2% for three consecutive quarters** (NextGen's "3 consecutive months" is wrong) → RBI reports reasons, remedial action and time to return to target; first invoked **2022**.
[0:7] - Primary objective under FIT: **price stability while keeping growth in mind**; MPC sets the policy rate to achieve **the inflation target**.

## SUB 1 | Monetary Policy Committee – Composition and Powers
[1:0] TABLE head: Member | How
  [1:0.0] RBI Governor | Chairperson; casting vote on a tie (no veto)
  [1:0.1] Deputy Governor (monetary policy) | Ex officio
  [1:0.2] One RBI officer | Nominated by the RBI Central Board
  [1:0.3] Three external members | Appointed by the Central Government; 4-year term, no reappointment
[1:1] - **6 members** (3 RBI + 3 external) – not 12, not reconstituted every year, **chaired by the RBI Governor, not the Finance Minister** (UPSC 2017: only I).
[1:2] - MPC **fixes the benchmark policy (repo) rate** (APPSC G2; APPSC GS 2019) to **control inflation** – it does not dictate fiscal policy, trade or industry (APPSC ASO).
[1:3] - Decisions by **majority**, **binding on the RBI**; **quorum 4**; meets **at least 4 times** a year by law (six in practice); first met **October 2016**; constituted under **Sec 45ZB**.
[1:4] - RBI publishes a **Monetary Policy Report every six months**.
[1:5] - **Governors:** Shaktikanta Das (Dec 2018 – Dec 2024; "present Governor" in the APPSC G4 2022 paper); **Sanjay Malhotra since 11 Dec 2024**.
[1:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 07 p.3-4 (6 members, composition, 4-year term, casting vote, MPR); NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC 01 p.31 (MPC fixes repo, 3 + 3, majority); app notes 3:83 sub 4; RBI MPC minutes 5-7 Oct 2026 (rbi.org.in prid=63839)

## SUB 3 | Evolution, Stance Record and Global Tightening
[3:0] TABLE head: Phase | Approach
  [3:0.0] 1985–1998 | Monetary targeting with feedback
  [3:0.1] 1998–2014 | Multiple indicator approach – criticised as opaque
  [3:0.2] 2014–2016 | Glide path: Urjit Patel Committee, 2015 Agreement
  [3:0.3] 2016 onwards | Flexible inflation targeting with the MPC
[3:1] - **"Seven Ages of India's Monetary Policy"**: Governor **Shaktikanta Das**, **24 January 2020**, St. Stephen's College – the multiple indicator approach "served fairly well from **1998-99 to 2008-09**": average growth **6.4%**, WPI inflation **5.4%** (APPSC FRO: both statements correct).
[3:2] TABLE head: Period | Repo | Stance
  [3:2.0] May 2022 – Feb 2023 | 4.00% → 6.50% | Withdrawal of accommodation (tightening bias)
  [3:2.1] Apr 2025 | Cut to 6.0% | Neutral → accommodative
  [3:2.2] Jun 2025 | 5.50%; CRR cut 100 bps | Back to neutral
  [3:2.3] Dec 2025 | 5.25% (125 bps since Feb 2025) | Neutral
  [3:2.4] Feb, Jun, Aug 2026 | Held 5.25% | Neutral
  [3:2.5] 7 Oct 2026 | Hiked to 5.50% | Calibrated tightening
[3:3] - **Post-pandemic:** many central banks **hiked rates**, assuming they **can counter rising consumer prices by monetary means** – both statements correct, II explains I (UPSC 2023).
[3:4] - June 2026: as major central banks geared up to tighten, the MPC **held 5.25% and cut the FY27 growth forecast**; spillovers = capital outflows, rupee pressure, **imported inflation**.
[3:5] - Debate: critics want a **core** target, since food and supply shocks lie outside MPC control. AP retail inflation **4.9%** vs national **3.9%** (May 2026).
[3:6] - **CBDC:** in Aug 2021 Das said trials could start **by December 2021**; the e₹ pilots actually began in **Nov–Dec 2022** (general knowledge, not checked).
