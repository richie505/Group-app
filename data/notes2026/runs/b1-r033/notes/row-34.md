=== Chandragupta Maurya – Names, Rise and the First Empire
# Greek names
- **Sandrocottus / Sandrokottos:** Greek name of Chandragupta used by **Justin** (Uttarakhand PCS 2016) and Strabo; Arrian and Plutarch – **Androkottus**. Not Chandragupta I or II, or Samudragupta.
- **Sir William Jones (1793)** first identified Sandrocottus with Chandragupta – the sheet-anchor of Indian chronology.
- **Meeting with Alexander:** described by **Justin** (UP Lower 2008 ×2) – not Pliny, Strabo or Megasthenes. Justin also called his army a "**gang of dacoits**".
# Origin and rise
| Tradition | Origin |
|---|---|
| Brahmanical | "Maurya" from a Shudra woman **Mura** |
| Buddhist and Jain | Kshatriya of the **Moriya** clan |
| Mudrarakshasa commentary (Dhundiraja) | A scion of the Nanda house |
- **Rise:** studied at **Taxila** (in **Gandhara**), met Chanakya; with the Himalayan chief Parvataka overthrew **Dhana Nanda** and took the throne in **321 BCE**; reign **324/321–297 BCE**.
- **Army (Greek accounts):** **6,00,000 infantry, 30,000 cavalry**, thousands of elephants.
- **First Indian empire:** founded by **Chandragupta Maurya** (UP Lower 2012) – the **Mauryan** empire was the first to unite most of the subcontinent; not Kanishka, Harsha or Samudragupta.
- **Oldest dynasty** among Gupta, Maurya, Vardhana and Kushana: **Maurya** (321–185 BCE) (Uttarakhand Lower 2010).
- **Ashoka's dynasty:** **Maurya** (CDS 2006) – grandson of Chandragupta, son of Bindusara.
> Exam angle: "Sandrocottus" → **Chandragupta Maurya**; "met Alexander" → **Justin**; first empire → **Mauryan**.
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History APPSC Part 2 p.42-43; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.111; app notes 1:34 (sub 0, 1)

=== Conquests, Western India and the Sudarshana Lake
| Region | Conquest / evidence |
|---|---|
| North-west | Expelled the Greek satraps; won Seleucus's provinces up to Afghanistan |
| Central and western India | Region north of the Narmada; **Malwa, Gujarat (Saurashtra) and Maharashtra** – Junagadh inscription |
| South | Empire reached Karnataka (**Brahmagiri, Siddapura, Jatinga-Rameshwara**); Tamil kingdoms (Chola, **Pandya**, Satyaputra, Keralaputra) stayed outside |
- **First king to conquer Malwa, Gujarat and Maharashtra:** **Chandragupta Maurya** (UPPCS 1991) – not Harsha, Skandagupta or Vikramaditya.
- **Conqueror of the Deccan:** **Bindusara** – PMF: he "conquered the Deccan up to Mysore" (State PSC key).
  - **⚠ Disputed:** some books credit Chandragupta (Jain tradition of his end at Shravanabelagola); NextGen says Bindusara made no conquests.
- **Mamulanar**, a Tamil (not Greek) poet, refers to the Mauryas' southward push.
- **Provincial capitals:** Pataliputra (centre), Taxila (north), **Ujjain** (west), **Tosali** (Kalinga, east), **Suvarnagiri** (south).
- **Andhra:** Ashokan edicts at **Yerragudi** (major + minor) and **Rajula Mandagiri** (only minor), Kurnool; RE XIII names the Andhras among peoples following Dhamma within the domain; the **Satavahanas** began as Mauryan feudatories.
# Sudarshana lake (Girnar, Saurashtra)
| Person | Role |
|---|---|
| **Pushyagupta** | Chandragupta's governor; **built** it – the first lake in Girnar |
| **Tushaspha** | Ashoka's Yavana governor; **added canals** |
| **Rudradaman I** | **Repaired** it (c. 150 CE) without forced labour – Junagadh inscription |
| **Skandagupta** | Repaired it again |
- **First ruler to get a lake built at Girnar:** **Chandragupta Maurya** (UPPCS Mains 2002) – who **constructed the Sudarshana lake** (UKPSC 2024).
- **Resource management at Girnar some centuries BCE:** **Chandragupta Maurya and Ashoka** → codes **2, 3** (UPUDA 2006); Rudradaman was after the Christian era, Mahapadma Nanda not linked.
> Exam angle: built = **Pushyagupta / Chandragupta**; canals = **Tushaspha / Ashoka**; repaired = **Rudradaman, Skandagupta**.
~~ Sources: NextGen Rocket Indian History Part 1 p.6-7; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.111, p.116; NextGen Indian History APPSC Part 2 p.43, p.47; app notes 1:34 (sub 3, 11)

=== Seleucus Nicator and the Greek Envoys
- **Seleucus I Nicator**, Alexander's general and ruler of Sindh–Afghanistan, was **defeated by Chandragupta in 305 BCE** (MPPSC 2008).
| Treaty term | Detail |
|---|---|
| Ceded to Chandragupta | **Aria** (Herat), **Arachosia** (Kandahar), **Paropanisadai** (Kabul), **Gedrosia** (Balochistan) – not Bactria |
| Marriage | Seleucus's daughter (Helena) married Chandragupta – not the reverse |
| Chandragupta gave | **500 war elephants** |
| Envoy | **Megasthenes** sent to Pataliputra |
| Envoy | Sent by | Court |
|---|---|---|
| **Megasthenes** | **Seleucus I Nicator** | **Chandragupta Maurya** – wrote Indica |
| **Deimachus** | **Antiochus I** (Syria) | **Bindusara** – replaced Megasthenes (Strabo) |
| **Dionysius** | **Ptolemy II Philadelphus** (Egypt) | Bindusara |
- **Megasthenes:** ambassador of **Seleucus** (State PSC); came in the reign of **Chandragupta Maurya** (UPPCS Mains 2017; RAS 1997) – not Ashoka, Harsha or Kumaragupta; the **first Greek envoy** to an Indian court.
- **Deimachus:** came under **Bindusara** (UP Lower 2015); he **did not come with Alexander** – Nearchus, Onesicritus, Aristobulus did (UPPCS 2015).
- **Diplomatic ties with Syria and Egypt:** the **Mauryas** (UPPSC 2010) – not Cholas, Guptas or Pallavas.
- **Megasthenes on Palibothra:** a **30-member body in six boards**, **64 gates**; his seven classes had **no merchants**.
> Exam angle: Megasthenes → Chandragupta; Deimachus, Dionysius → Bindusara.
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History APPSC Part 2 p.43; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.111, p.116; app notes 1:34 (sub 4, 7)

=== Chanakya (Kautilya, Vishnugupta)
- **Other name / childhood name:** **Vishnugupta** (UPPCS 2006; IAS 1993 ×2) – not Bhattaswami, Rajasekhara or Vishakhadatta; one Arthashastra verse names its author Vishnugupta.
- **Office:** **prime minister** of **Chandragupta Maurya** (UPPCS 2002) – not Chandragupta Vikramaditya, Ashoka or Janaka.
- **Taranath and Jain tradition:** he stayed on as **Bindusara's** minister; Puranas call him "dvijarshabha".
- **Mudrarakshasa:** Chanakya against **Rakshasa**, minister of the **Nandas**.
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History APPSC Part 2 p.43, p.47; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.109; app notes 1:34 (sub 2)

=== Bindusara
- **Title:** **Amitraghata** ("**slayer of foes**", Mahabhashya) = Greek Amitrochates (UP Lower 2015) – not Chandragupta, Samudragupta or Ashoka.
- **Birth name:** **Simhasena**; mother **Durdhara**; reign **297–273 BCE**.
- **Antiochus I** was asked for sweet wine, dried figs and a **philosopher** – the philosopher could not be sent.
- **Religion:** **Ajivika**; council of 500 ministers under Khallataka.
- **Taxila revolt:** **Susima** failed, then **Ashoka** was sent (Divyavadana); a later Taxila revolt under Ashoka was handled by **Kunala**. Ashoka was viceroy at Taxila and **Ujjain**.
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History APPSC Part 2 p.43; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.116; app notes 1:34 (sub 6)

=== Chandragupta's Jain End and the Religion of the Mauryas
- **Went south with Chandragupta in the great famine:** **Bhadrabahu** (APPSC G2 2003) – not Sthulabhadra, Merutunga or Lakulisha.
- **12-year famine** → split into **Digambaras** (Bhadrabahu) and **Svetambaras** (followers of **Sthulabhadra**).
- **First Indian king to abdicate:** **Chandragupta Maurya** (APPSC G1) – gave up the throne for **Bindusara**, became a Jain ascetic and died by **Sallekhana** at **Shravanabelagola** (Karnataka); source **Parishishtaparvan**.
| Ruler | Religion |
|---|---|
| Chandragupta | Jainism (Bhadrabahu) |
| Bindusara | **Ajivika** |
| Ashoka | Buddhism; gave **Barabar** caves to the Ajivikas |
| Dasharatha | **Buddhist** (key), though he gave the **Nagarjuni** caves to the Ajivikas |
| **Samprati** | Jainism – "**Ashoka of Jainism**"; spread Jainism in Andhra |
- **Barabar caves** (Bihar): the **oldest surviving rock-cut caves**, carved in **granite** – not at Girnar; **Lomas Rishi** cave is in the Barabar hills.
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History APPSC Part 2 p.43, p.52; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.111; app notes 1:34 (sub 5, 10)
