[0:7] - **Megasthenes on Palibothra:** a **30-member body in six boards**, **64 gates**; his seven classes had **no merchants**.

## SUB 1 | Chandragupta Maurya – Names, Rise and the First Empire
[1:0] # Greek names
[1:1] - **Sandrocottus / Sandrokottos:** Greek name of Chandragupta used by **Justin** (Uttarakhand PCS 2016) and Strabo; Arrian and Plutarch – **Androkottus**. Not Chandragupta I or II, or Samudragupta.
[1:2] - **Sir William Jones (1793)** first identified Sandrocottus with Chandragupta – the sheet-anchor of Indian chronology.
[1:3] - **Meeting with Alexander:** described by **Justin** (UP Lower 2008 ×2) – not Pliny, Strabo or Megasthenes. Justin also called his army a "**gang of dacoits**".
[1:4] # Origin and rise
[1:5] TABLE head: Tradition | Origin
  [1:5.0] Brahmanical | "Maurya" from a Shudra woman Mura
  [1:5.1] Buddhist and Jain | Kshatriya of the Moriya clan
  [1:5.2] Mudrarakshasa commentary (Dhundiraja) | A scion of the Nanda house
[1:6] - **Rise:** studied at **Taxila** (in **Gandhara**), met Chanakya; with the Himalayan chief Parvataka overthrew **Dhana Nanda** and took the throne in **321 BCE**; reign **324/321–297 BCE**.
[1:7] - **Army (Greek accounts):** **6,00,000 infantry, 30,000 cavalry**, thousands of elephants.
[1:8] - **First Indian empire:** founded by **Chandragupta Maurya** (UP Lower 2012) – the **Mauryan** empire was the first to unite most of the subcontinent; not Kanishka, Harsha or Samudragupta.
[1:9] - **Oldest dynasty** among Gupta, Maurya, Vardhana and Kushana: **Maurya** (321–185 BCE) (Uttarakhand Lower 2010).
[1:10] - **Ashoka's dynasty:** **Maurya** (CDS 2006) – grandson of Chandragupta, son of Bindusara.
[1:11] > Exam angle: "Sandrocottus" → **Chandragupta Maurya**; "met Alexander" → **Justin**; first empire → **Mauryan**.
[1:12] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History APPSC Part 2 p.42-43; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.111; app notes 1:34 (sub 0, 1)
## SUB 2 | Conquests, Western India and the Sudarshana Lake
[2:0] TABLE head: Region | Conquest / evidence
  [2:0.0] North-west | Expelled the Greek satraps; won Seleucus's provinces up to Afghanistan
  [2:0.1] Central and western India | Region north of the Narmada; Malwa, Gujarat (Saurashtra) and Maharashtra – Junagadh inscription
  [2:0.2] South | Empire reached Karnataka (Brahmagiri, Siddapura, Jatinga-Rameshwara); Tamil kingdoms (Chola, Pandya, Satyaputra, Keralaputra) stayed outside
[2:1] - **First king to conquer Malwa, Gujarat and Maharashtra:** **Chandragupta Maurya** (UPPCS 1991) – not Harsha, Skandagupta or Vikramaditya.
[2:2] - **Conqueror of the Deccan:** **Bindusara** – PMF: he "conquered the Deccan up to Mysore" (State PSC key).
[2:3]   - **⚠ Disputed:** some books credit Chandragupta (Jain tradition of his end at Shravanabelagola); NextGen says Bindusara made no conquests.
[2:4] - **Mamulanar**, a Tamil (not Greek) poet, refers to the Mauryas' southward push.
[2:5] - **Provincial capitals:** Pataliputra (centre), Taxila (north), **Ujjain** (west), **Tosali** (Kalinga, east), **Suvarnagiri** (south).
[2:6] - **Andhra:** Ashokan edicts at **Yerragudi** (major + minor) and **Rajula Mandagiri** (only minor), Kurnool; RE XIII names the Andhras among peoples following Dhamma within the domain; the **Satavahanas** began as Mauryan feudatories.
[2:7] # Sudarshana lake (Girnar, Saurashtra)
[2:8] TABLE head: Person | Role
  [2:8.0] Pushyagupta | Chandragupta's governor; built it – the first lake in Girnar
  [2:8.1] Tushaspha | Ashoka's Yavana governor; added canals
  [2:8.2] Rudradaman I | Repaired it (c. 150 CE) without forced labour – Junagadh inscription
  [2:8.3] Skandagupta | Repaired it again
[2:9] - **First ruler to get a lake built at Girnar:** **Chandragupta Maurya** (UPPCS Mains 2002) – who **constructed the Sudarshana lake** (UKPSC 2024).
[2:10] - **Resource management at Girnar some centuries BCE:** **Chandragupta Maurya and Ashoka** → codes **2, 3** (UPUDA 2006); Rudradaman was after the Christian era, Mahapadma Nanda not linked.
[2:11] > Exam angle: built = **Pushyagupta / Chandragupta**; canals = **Tushaspha / Ashoka**; repaired = **Rudradaman, Skandagupta**.
[2:12] ~~ Sources: NextGen Rocket Indian History Part 1 p.6-7; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.111, p.116; NextGen Indian History APPSC Part 2 p.43, p.47; app notes 1:34 (sub 3, 11)
## SUB 3 | Chanakya (Kautilya, Vishnugupta)
[3:0] - **Other name / childhood name:** **Vishnugupta** (UPPCS 2006; IAS 1993 ×2) – not Bhattaswami, Rajasekhara or Vishakhadatta; one Arthashastra verse names its author Vishnugupta.
[3:1] - **Office:** **prime minister** of **Chandragupta Maurya** (UPPCS 2002) – not Chandragupta Vikramaditya, Ashoka or Janaka.
[3:2] - **Taranath and Jain tradition:** he stayed on as **Bindusara's** minister; Puranas call him "dvijarshabha".
[3:3] - **Mudrarakshasa:** Chanakya against **Rakshasa**, minister of the **Nandas**.
[3:4] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History APPSC Part 2 p.43, p.47; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.109; app notes 1:34 (sub 2)
## SUB 4 | Chandragupta's Jain End and the Religion of the Mauryas
[4:0] - **Went south with Chandragupta in the great famine:** **Bhadrabahu** (APPSC G2 2003) – not Sthulabhadra, Merutunga or Lakulisha.
[4:1] - **12-year famine** → split into **Digambaras** (Bhadrabahu) and **Svetambaras** (followers of **Sthulabhadra**).
[4:2] - **First Indian king to abdicate:** **Chandragupta Maurya** (APPSC G1) – gave up the throne for **Bindusara**, became a Jain ascetic and died by **Sallekhana** at **Shravanabelagola** (Karnataka); source **Parishishtaparvan**.
[4:3] TABLE head: Ruler | Religion
  [4:3.0] Chandragupta | Jainism (Bhadrabahu)
  [4:3.1] Bindusara | Ajivika
  [4:3.2] Ashoka | Buddhism; gave Barabar caves to the Ajivikas
  [4:3.3] Dasharatha | Buddhist (key), though he gave the Nagarjuni caves to the Ajivikas
  [4:3.4] Samprati | Jainism – "Ashoka of Jainism"; spread Jainism in Andhra
[4:4] - **Barabar caves** (Bihar): the **oldest surviving rock-cut caves**, carved in **granite** – not at Girnar; **Lomas Rishi** cave is in the Barabar hills.
[4:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History APPSC Part 2 p.43, p.52; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.111; app notes 1:34 (sub 5, 10)
## SUB 5 | Bindusara
[5:0] - **Title:** **Amitraghata** ("**slayer of foes**", Mahabhashya) = Greek Amitrochates (UP Lower 2015) – not Chandragupta, Samudragupta or Ashoka.
[5:1] - **Birth name:** **Simhasena**; mother **Durdhara**; reign **297–273 BCE**.
[5:2] - **Antiochus I** was asked for sweet wine, dried figs and a **philosopher** – the philosopher could not be sent.
[5:3] - **Religion:** **Ajivika**; council of 500 ministers under Khallataka.
[5:4] - **Taxila revolt:** **Susima** failed, then **Ashoka** was sent (Divyavadana); a later Taxila revolt under Ashoka was handled by **Kunala**. Ashoka was viceroy at Taxila and **Ujjain**.
[5:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History APPSC Part 2 p.43; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.116; app notes 1:34 (sub 6)
