## SUB 1 | Membership Changes – ICC Withdrawals and Recognition of Palestine
[62:1:0] # Sahel states leave the ICC
[62:1:1] - Military-led **Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger** (coups **2020–2023**) announced withdrawal from the **ICC** (2025), calling it a **"neo-colonial instrument"**.
[62:1:2] - Grouping: **Alliance of Sahel States (AES)** (not ECOWAS or G5 Sahel); moving closer to **Russia**, away from **France**.
[62:1:3] - **Rome Statute:** withdrawal takes effect **one year after notice to the UN Secretary-General** (not immediately, not 3 or 6 months).
[62:1:4] # ICC – background
[62:1:5] - Established **2002**; seat **The Hague, Netherlands** – the **first permanent international criminal court** (the ICJ is the UN's court for disputes between states).
[62:1:6] - Crimes: war crimes, crimes against humanity, genocide, aggression; works on the **principle of complementarity** (steps in only when national courts cannot or will not act).
[62:1:7] - **Vladimir Putin** under an ICC arrest warrant since **March 2023**.

[62:1:9] - Already recognised (2024): **Spain, Ireland, Norway**; **France** signalled readiness.

[62:1:11] - **UNGA, September 2025:** about **10** countries moving to recognise – **Britain, France, Canada, Australia, Belgium**.
[62:1:12] - **PLO** declared statehood in **1988**; Palestine is a UN **observer** state.
