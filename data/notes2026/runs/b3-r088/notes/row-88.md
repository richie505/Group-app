=== Financial Inclusion – Meaning, Objectives and Committees
# Meaning
- **Financial inclusion**: basic banking and financial services for **everybody, whatever their income or savings** – it can be encouraged by banking services and financial services for low-income people ("**all of the above**").
- **Rangarajan definition**: ensuring **access to financial services** and **timely and adequate credit** for **vulnerable groups** (weaker sections, low-income groups) at an **affordable cost**.
- **RBI view** covers consumer protection for newly included customers, easy and speedy grievance redressal and wider financial literacy – "**All of the above**".
| Objective | Fact |
|---|---|
| Extend financial services | **Poor population** |
| Expand banking infrastructure | **Underserved and rural areas** (not metros) |
| Credit access | **Weaker sections** – unlock their growth potential |
| Financial literacy | Awareness + informed use |
| Payments / remittances, insurance | Formal system; raises financial security |
- **Shrinking of banking infrastructure** is NOT an objective; "reducing financial security" is not an outcome.
- **Hindrances**: **low income, illiteracy, lack of bank branches** – "all of the above".
| Financial inclusion | Financial exclusion |
|---|---|
| Bank account, credit, insurance | No / limited banking or insurance access |
| Payments and remittances | Limited payment facilities |
| Financial literacy | Lack of awareness |
# Earlier inclusion measures
- **Lead Bank Scheme (LBS)** – "Lead Banking Scheme" in the UPPSC stem; a district-level financial inclusion measure (1969).
- **Swabhimaan (10 February 2011)**: financial inclusion campaign taking **banking to the doorsteps of the rural poor** → associated with **rural banking** (not women's rights or food security).

=== Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)
# Launch and objective
- **National Mission for Financial Inclusion**: announced **15 August 2014**, launched **28 August 2014** (not 26 Jan or 5 Sept); nodal: **Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance**.
- Aim: **financial inclusion** – universal access to banking, **a bank account for every household** (not housing loans, SHGs, credit to businessmen or bank income).
- Slogan: "**Mera Khata – Bhagya Vidhata**".
- Tenets: **banking the unbanked, securing the unsecured, funding the unfunded**.
- India's flagship / **world's largest financial inclusion programme**; entered the **Guinness World Records** for the most bank accounts opened in a week (23-29 August 2014) – not MUDRA, Aadhaar or MGNREGS.
- **SI 2016 statements**: launched 28 Aug 2014 ✔, accounts + insurance + debit card ✔, Ministry of Finance in charge ✔; it does **not** grant business loans → "**a, b & c**".
# Features
| Feature | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Account | **Zero-balance** BSBD account; earns interest |
| RuPay debit card | Accident cover **₹2 lakh** (₹1 lakh for accounts before 28 Aug 2018) |
| Overdraft | Up to **₹10,000** (after **6 months** of satisfactory conduct) |
| Life cover | **₹30,000** (accounts opened 15 Aug 2014 – 26 Jan 2015) |
| Others | DBT, financial literacy; linked to **PMJJBY, PMSBY and APY** |
# Coverage (as of August 2026)
| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Total accounts | **59.09 crore** (14.72 crore in 2015) |
| Rural / semi-urban | 45.95 crore (about three-fourths) |
| Women | 32.92 crore (**over half**) |
| Deposits | ₹3,16,514 crore |
- Concern: dormant Jan Dhan accounts; 90%+ of operative accounts Aadhaar-seeded.

=== MUDRA – Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana
- **PMMY** launched **8 April 2015** (not 2014/2017/2019); **Ministry of Finance (DFS)** – not Corporate Affairs or Rural Development.
- **MUDRA = Micro Units Development and Refinance Agency**; MUDRA Ltd set up **18 March 2015** as a **SIDBI subsidiary**; **refinances** banks, NBFCs, MFIs, RRBs (it does not lend directly).
- Aim: **collateral-free** loans to **non-corporate, non-farm micro and small enterprises** – brings **small entrepreneurs into the formal financial system** ("fund the unfunded"); not for large corporates, farm cultivation, pensions or NGOs.
- **Shishu, Kishore, Tarun** are the loan categories of **MUDRA** (not RRBs, SIDBI or IDBI schemes).
| Category | Loan size |
|---|---|
| **Shishu** | Up to **₹50,000** |
| **Kishore** | **More than ₹50,000 up to ₹5 lakh** |
| **Tarun** | ₹5 lakh – **₹10 lakh** |
| **Tarun Plus** | **₹10 – 20 lakh** (from **24 Oct 2024**, Budget 2024-25), for Tarun borrowers who repaid |
- Limit: **₹10 lakh** at launch (2015-2024); **now ₹20 lakh**. Guarantees via **CGFMU**; MUDRA Card (RuPay).
- Performance: 57 crore+ loans; about two-thirds to **women**.
- **Launch order**: PMJDY (28 Aug 2014) → Digital Gender Atlas (9 Mar 2015) → MUDRA (8 Apr 2015) → PMSBY (9 May 2015).

=== Microfinance and SHG-Bank Linkage
# Microfinance
- **Microfinance** = financial services for low-income people: **microcredit, savings, microinsurance, payments / fund transfer** → UPSC 2011 answer "**1, 2, 3 and 4**".
- Modern microfinance roots: **Grameen Bank, Bangladesh (1976)** – **Muhammad Yunus**, pioneer of microfinance (Nobel Peace Prize 2006).
- UN **International Year of Microcredit: 2005**.
- **AP microfinance crisis (2010)** began with **SKS Microfinance** (IPO, coercive recoveries, borrower suicides) → AP MFI (Regulation of Money Lending) **Ordinance, 15 October 2010**: MFIs had to **register with a state-appointed authority**; replaced by an Act (Dec 2010).
- The crisis **reshaped national regulation** → RBI **Y.H. Malegam** sub-committee (Oct 2010) → **NBFC-MFI** category created **2 December 2011**.
- **MFIN**: self-regulatory national body of RBI-regulated MFIs (2009).
# SHG-Bank Linkage
| Body / programme | Year | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| SEWA | 1970s | Genesis of SHGs in India |
| MYRADA | Mid-1980s | Pioneered linkage |
| **NABARD SHG-Bank Linkage pilot** | **1992** (1992-93) | Started by **NABARD** (not SBI); **world's largest microfinance project** |
| DAY-NRLM | 2011 | **Ministry of Rural Development**; rural livelihoods through SHGs |
- In an SHG **all members take responsibility** for a member's loan (joint liability); **RRBs, commercial and co-operative banks** lend to SHGs. UPSC 2023: only statements 2 and 3 correct → "**Only two**".
- **Implementers**: commercial banks, RRBs, co-operative banks; **NABARD** promotes and refinances – keyed as the body that does **not** implement (UPPSC 2015).
# Andhra Pradesh model
- **DWCRA** (1982-83, sub-scheme of IRDP; AP with UNICEF) – early women's group model; **SERP** runs the SHG model.
- **AP ranks 1st** in SHG-bank linkage: about **36% national share**, **99.66% recovery**.

=== NPCI, Cards, ATMs and RTGS-NEFT
# NPCI
- **National Payments Corporation of India**: incorporated **December 2008** by the **RBI and Indian Banks' Association (IBA)**; **not-for-profit**, **Section 25 company** (Companies Act 1956) = **Section 8** of the 2013 Act; works under the **Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007**.
- NPCI **promotes financial inclusion** (AePS, RuPay, UPI) and **launched RuPay** → UPSC 2017 "**Both I and II**".
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 2004 | **NFS** designed and launched by RBI's **IDRBT** |
| 2009 | NPCI took over NFS / authorised for retail payment systems |
| 2010 | **IMPS** and **AePS** |
| 2012 | **RuPay**; NACH |
| 2016 | **UPI** (April, 21 banks); **BHIM**; NETC FASTag |
# Products → purpose
| Product | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **NFS – National Financial Switch** | **Largest interoperable ATM network**; **links all ATMs** in India (not IBA, NSDL or RBI) |
| **RuPay** | India's **domestic card payment network** – the Indian **payment card** (not Jan Dhan, Digi Vyapar, Lucky Grahak) |
| AePS | Basic banking at micro-ATMs through **Aadhaar authentication** |
| NACH | **Bulk recurring payments** – salaries, dividends, bills |
| NETC | **FASTag** electronic toll collection |
| BHIM | NPCI's **UPI app** |
| Bharat BillPay | Interoperable bill payment |
| e-RUPI | **Person- and purpose-specific** prepaid voucher |
| UPI 123Pay | UPI for **feature phones** |
- **POS = Point of Sale** – card / QR terminal at a merchant.
- **Bharat QR** (20 Feb 2017; **NPCI, Mastercard and Visa**): **world's first interoperable QR code acceptance** – the customer scans the merchant's QR from the phone to pay instantly; needs a bank account.
# RTGS vs NEFT
| Basis | RTGS | NEFT |
|---|---|---|
| Settlement | Real-time, gross, instantaneous | Deferred net, half-hourly batches (takes some time) |
| Minimum | **₹2 lakh** | No minimum |
| Hours | **24x7x365** since 14 Dec 2020 | 24x7x365 since 16 Dec 2019 |
| Inward charge | None | None |
- UPSC 2025: only "RTGS instant, NEFT takes time" is correct – no inward charges in either; neither has restricted days now → "**I only**".

=== UPI – Features, New Products and UPI Abroad
- **UPI = Unified Payments Interface**; NPCI, launched **April 2016** with **21 banks**; instant account-to-account P2P / P2M via a VPA; runs on the **IMPS** framework (not NEFT).
- Consequence (UPSC 2017): **mobile wallets are not necessary** for online payments – money moves bank-to-bank.
- **BHIM** lets a user pay anyone with a UPI-enabled bank account; the "card has four factors, BHIM two" claim is wrong → "**Only I**".
# New features
| Feature | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **UPI LITE X** | **Offline** payments (remote / poor-network areas) |
| **Hello! UPI** | **Voice** payments – built by **NPCI with Bhashini / AI4Bharat (IIT Madras)**, launched at Global Fintech Fest, Sept 2023 – not a "consortium of private start-ups" |
| UPI123Pay | Feature phones |
| Credit Line on UPI | Pre-sanctioned credit via UPI |
- **APPSC Group 1 2024**: I (promotion of digital payments moved from **MeitY to DFS**) ✔ and IV (UPI LITE X offline) ✔; II wrong (e-RUPI is **person- and purpose-specific**); III wrong → "**I and IV**".

=== Digital Payments Policy, Schemes and RBI Indices
# Policy and schemes
- **Lucky Grahak Yojana** (consumers) and **Digi-Dhan Vyapar Yojana** (merchants) – NITI Aayog, 25 Dec 2016: cash prizes to promote digital payments; under Digi-Dhan **merchants get prizes** for digital transactions.
- **MDR (Merchant Discount Rate)**: the **charge a bank levies on a merchant** for accepting card / digital payments; shared by issuing bank, acquiring bank and network. **Zero MDR** on **RuPay debit and BHIM-UPI** since **1 Jan 2020** (Sec 10A, PSS Act) – cost burden on **payment system providers**.
- **RuPay-BHIM UPI incentive scheme**: **0.15%** on P2M payments up to **₹2,000** to small merchants; **NPCI** nodal agency.
- **Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026**: UPI stays free for consumers; MDR may apply to **high-value** payments.
- **Data localisation (RBI, April 2018)**: entire payment-system data must be **stored only in India** – no public-sector ownership or CAG audit condition → "**Only I**".
- **Kerala**: first state with **digital banking in all eligible accounts** (January 2023).
# RBI indices
| Index | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **RBI-DPI** | Base **March 2018 = 100** (not 2021); published from Jan 2021, **semi-annual**; **5 parameters** – payment enablers, payment infrastructure (demand side), payment infrastructure (supply side), payment performance, **consumer centricity**; risen every period since launch; 516.76 (Sep 2025) |

=== JAM, India Stack, ONDC and Differentiated Banks
- **JAM** = **Jan Dhan – Aadhaar – Mobile**; PMJDY is the first layer → made **DBT** possible.
- **India Stack**: Aadhaar, UPI, DigiLocker, Account Aggregator, ONDC (RuPay is not part of it).
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Account Aggregator | **Consent-based sharing of financial data**; RBI-regulated NBFC-AAs |
| **ONDC** | **Open, interoperable network** for digital commerce across platforms (**DPIIT**) – not government control, not replacing private platforms, no UPI mandate |
| OCEN | Common protocol linking lenders and marketplaces for small-ticket credit |
- **Payments banks** (Nachiket Mor): deposits up to **₹2 lakh** (₹1 lakh initially); **cannot lend** → **no credit cards**; ATM/debit cards, G-sec investment only.
- **Small finance banks**: **50% of loans up to ₹25 lakh**.

=== Cryptocurrency, CBDC and the Digital Rupee
| Basis | Cryptocurrency | Digital Rupee (e₹, CBDC) | UPI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nature | Private, decentralised; not legal tender | **Legal tender issued by RBI** | Payment system, not a currency |
| Liability | None | **RBI** | Commercial bank money – your bank |
| Rules | 30% tax on gains + 1% TDS (2022) | RBI Act amended by **Finance Act 2022** | NPCI, PSS Act 2007 |
- UPSC 2026 (not correct): "in both UPI and Digital Rupee the liability lies with users and banks" – the e₹ is an **RBI liability**.
- **CBDC** (UPSC 2023): payments possible **without the US dollar or SWIFT**, and money can be **programmed** (e.g. a time limit for spending) → "**Both**".
- **e-rupee pilots**: wholesale **1 Nov 2022**; retail **1 Dec 2022**. **Sand Dollar (Bahamas, 2020)**: first live retail CBDC.
- **Bitcoin**: **2009**, **Satoshi Nakamoto**; not tracked or issued by central banks; anyone with a Bitcoin address can send / receive; parties can stay anonymous → "**2 and 3 only**".
- Facebook's 2019 cryptocurrency: **Libra** (later Diem).
- **Supreme Court, 2020** struck down RBI's **2018** circular barring banks from crypto; the Finance Act 2022 did **not** make crypto legal tender.
