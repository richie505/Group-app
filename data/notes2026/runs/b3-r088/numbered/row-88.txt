## SUB 0 | Financial Inclusion – Meaning, Objectives and Committees
[0:0] # Meaning
[0:1] - **Financial inclusion**: basic banking and financial services for **everybody, whatever their income or savings** – it can be encouraged by banking services and financial services for low-income people ("**all of the above**").
[0:2] - **Rangarajan definition**: ensuring **access to financial services** and **timely and adequate credit** for **vulnerable groups** (weaker sections, low-income groups) at an **affordable cost**.
[0:3] - **RBI view** covers consumer protection for newly included customers, easy and speedy grievance redressal and wider financial literacy – "**All of the above**".
[0:4] TABLE head: Objective | Fact
  [0:4.0] Extend financial services | Poor population
  [0:4.1] Expand banking infrastructure | Underserved and rural areas (not metros)
  [0:4.2] Credit access | Weaker sections – unlock their growth potential
  [0:4.3] Financial literacy | Awareness + informed use
  [0:4.4] Payments / remittances, insurance | Formal system; raises financial security
[0:5] - **Shrinking of banking infrastructure** is NOT an objective; "reducing financial security" is not an outcome.
[0:6] - **Hindrances**: **low income, illiteracy, lack of bank branches** – "all of the above".

## SUB 1 | Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)
[1:0] # Launch and objective
[1:1] - **National Mission for Financial Inclusion**: announced **15 August 2014**, launched **28 August 2014** (not 26 Jan or 5 Sept); nodal: **Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance**.
[1:2] - Aim: **financial inclusion** – universal access to banking, **a bank account for every household** (not housing loans, SHGs, credit to businessmen or bank income).
[1:3] - Slogan: "**Mera Khata – Bhagya Vidhata**".
[1:4] - Tenets: **banking the unbanked, securing the unsecured, funding the unfunded**.

[1:6] - **SI 2016 statements**: launched 28 Aug 2014 ✔, accounts + insurance + debit card ✔, Ministry of Finance in charge ✔; it does **not** grant business loans → "**a, b & c**".

## SUB 3 | Microfinance and SHG-Bank Linkage
[3:0] # Microfinance
[3:1] - **Microfinance** = financial services for low-income people: **microcredit, savings, microinsurance, payments / fund transfer** → UPSC 2011 answer "**1, 2, 3 and 4**".
[3:2] - Modern microfinance roots: **Grameen Bank, Bangladesh (1976)** – **Muhammad Yunus**, pioneer of microfinance (Nobel Peace Prize 2006).
[3:3] - UN **International Year of Microcredit: 2005**.
[3:4] - **AP microfinance crisis (2010)** began with **SKS Microfinance** (IPO, coercive recoveries, borrower suicides) → AP MFI (Regulation of Money Lending) **Ordinance, 15 October 2010**: MFIs had to **register with a state-appointed authority**; replaced by an Act (Dec 2010).
[3:5] - The crisis **reshaped national regulation** → RBI **Y.H. Malegam** sub-committee (Oct 2010) → **NBFC-MFI** category created **2 December 2011**.
[3:6] - **MFIN**: self-regulatory national body of RBI-regulated MFIs (2009).
[3:7] # SHG-Bank Linkage
[3:8] TABLE head: Body / programme | Year | Fact
  [3:8.0] SEWA | 1970s | Genesis of SHGs in India
  [3:8.1] MYRADA | Mid-1980s | Pioneered linkage
  [3:8.2] NABARD SHG-Bank Linkage pilot | 1992 (1992-93) | Started by NABARD (not SBI); world's largest microfinance project
  [3:8.3] DAY-NRLM | 2011 | Ministry of Rural Development; rural livelihoods through SHGs
[3:9] - In an SHG **all members take responsibility** for a member's loan (joint liability); **RRBs, commercial and co-operative banks** lend to SHGs. UPSC 2023: only statements 2 and 3 correct → "**Only two**".
[3:10] - **Implementers**: commercial banks, RRBs, co-operative banks; **NABARD** promotes and refinances – keyed as the body that does **not** implement (UPPSC 2015).

[3:13] - **AP ranks 1st** in SHG-bank linkage: about **36% national share**, **99.66% recovery**.

## SUB 5 | UPI – Features, New Products and UPI Abroad
[5:0] - **UPI = Unified Payments Interface**; NPCI, launched **April 2016** with **21 banks**; instant account-to-account P2P / P2M via a VPA; runs on the **IMPS** framework (not NEFT).

## SUB 6 | Digital Payments Policy, Schemes and RBI Indices
[6:0] # Policy and schemes
[6:1] - **Lucky Grahak Yojana** (consumers) and **Digi-Dhan Vyapar Yojana** (merchants) – NITI Aayog, 25 Dec 2016: cash prizes to promote digital payments; under Digi-Dhan **merchants get prizes** for digital transactions.
[6:2] - **MDR (Merchant Discount Rate)**: the **charge a bank levies on a merchant** for accepting card / digital payments; shared by issuing bank, acquiring bank and network. **Zero MDR** on **RuPay debit and BHIM-UPI** since **1 Jan 2020** (Sec 10A, PSS Act) – cost burden on **payment system providers**.
[6:3] - **RuPay-BHIM UPI incentive scheme**: **0.15%** on P2M payments up to **₹2,000** to small merchants; **NPCI** nodal agency.
[6:4] - **Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026**: UPI stays free for consumers; MDR may apply to **high-value** payments.
[6:5] - **Data localisation (RBI, April 2018)**: entire payment-system data must be **stored only in India** – no public-sector ownership or CAG audit condition → "**Only I**".
[6:6] - **Kerala**: first state with **digital banking in all eligible accounts** (January 2023).
