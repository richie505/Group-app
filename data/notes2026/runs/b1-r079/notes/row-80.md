=== Chalukya Bhima I – Pancharamas and the Twin Bhimeswara Temples
# The builder
- **Chalukya Bhima I** (892-922): fought **360 battles** (Pithapuram inscription of Mallapadeva) and is said to have built **360 temples**; "the Vengi ruler who built many temples" (APPSC PYQ).
- **Pancharamas built by:** **Chalukya Bhima I**, i.e. under the **Vengi (Eastern) Chalukyas** (APPSC PYQ ×2) – not Kubja Vishnuvardhana, Pulakesin II, Rajaraja Narendra, Kakatiyas or Vijayanagara.
- **Draksharama founder:** **Chalukya Bhima I** built the **Bhimeswara** temple (G2 1993); an **Eastern Chalukya** monument, not Pallava or Satavahana.
- **Twin temples:** **Draksharama Bhimeswara** and **Kumararama Bhimeswara at Samalkot**, the latter in memory of his **son**.

| **Ksheerarama** | Palakollu | West Godavari | West Godavari |
| **Amararama** | **Amaravati** | Guntur | Palnadu |
- **Amaravati is a Pancharama** (G1 1991), not a Shakti Peetha.
- **Not a Pancharama:** **Simhachalam** (a Narasimha shrine) (MPDO 1997); Chalukya Bhimavaram is one.
- **Chalukya Bhimavaram = Samalkot (Samarlakota)** (Welfare Officers 2017) – not Pithapuram, Draksharama or Gudipudi.
- **All are two-storeyed except Ksheerarama**; four lie in the Godavari delta, **Amararama** on the **Krishna**.
- **Literary sources:** **Skanda Purana** and **Srinatha's Bhimeswara Puranam**.
# Draksharama vs Samalkot
| Feature | Draksharama | Samalkot |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Bhimeswara | Kumararama / Kumara Bhimeswara |
| Plan | Two-storeyed shrine, massive lingam | Two-storeyed, closely similar; **black-granite lingam** |
| Extras | Huge Nandi mandapa; one of the **Trilinga** kshetras | Towering gopuram added later |
| Later work | Renovated by the Reddis | Renovated by the Reddis |
- **Sapta Godavari:** the sacred tank (**pushkarini**) of the **Draksharama Bhimeswara** temple; legend says the Seven Sages brought its water, used for the abhisheka (Welfare Officers PYQ).
- **Jananathapura:** Draksharama was also the provincial capital and coronation seat of the Chalukya-Chola viceroys of Vengi.

=== Other Temple Builders – Vijayaditya II, Bikkavolu and Rajahmundry
# Vijayaditya II – 108 temples
- **Vijayaditya II (Narendra Mrigaraja):** fought **108 battles in 12 years** against the Rashtrakutas (**Govinda III, Amoghavarsha I**) and their Gangavadi allies.
- **108 Shiva temples** (**Narendreswara / Narendra Mrigeswara**) at the battle sites – recorded in the **Sataluru** inscription (G2 2008, 2012, 2019; statement a of G2 2025 ✓).
- **⚠ Trap:** the source is **Sataluru**, not Kopparam (a Pulakesin II record).
# Bikkavolu (Biccavolu) group
- **Old name:** **Birudankaprolu = Bikkavolu** (East Godavari), after Gunaga Vijayaditya's title **Birudankabhima** (Welfare Officers 2017; APPSC PYQ).
- **Builder:** **Gunaga Vijayaditya III** (848-892) – **Golingeswara, Rajarajeswara (Raja Raja), Chandrasekhara, Mukteswara, Vijayeswara**.
- **Golingeswara** = the only one still standing; **Vijayeswara** has carved **Ganga and Yamuna** figures.
- **⚠ Settled:** "**Rajaraja Narendra** built the Rajarajeswara temple at Biccavole" is the **incorrect** statement (G2 Mains 2025 key: "b only").
  - NextGen p.20 credits Rajaraja Narendra with a temple at Bikkavolu; the ASI attributes the whole group to Gunaga Vijayaditya III.

=== Vengi School and the Chalukya-Chola Style
- **Vengi School:** mainly **Dravida** temples – **Pallava (Kanchi) + Badami Chalukya** influences with **local Andhra** features; local laterite and limestone; concentrated in the **Krishna-Godavari delta**.
- **Vesara (Chalukya) style:** NextGen defines it as **Dravidian vimana + Buddhist** art; it belongs to the **Badami Chalukyas** and was brought into Andhra by the Vengi Chalukyas.
- **Navabrahma temples (nine), Alampur:** **Badami Chalukyas**, 7th–8th c. – not Eastern Chalukyas.
- **Chalukya-Chola model:** the **Draksharama Bhimeswara** temple blends **Pallava + early Chalukya** features into an independent style (G2 Mains 2025).
