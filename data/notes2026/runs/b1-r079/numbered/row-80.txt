## SUB 0 | Chalukya Bhima I – Pancharamas and the Twin Bhimeswara Temples
[0:0] # The builder
[0:1] - **Chalukya Bhima I** (892-922): fought **360 battles** (Pithapuram inscription of Mallapadeva) and is said to have built **360 temples**; "the Vengi ruler who built many temples" (APPSC PYQ).
[0:2] - **Pancharamas built by:** **Chalukya Bhima I**, i.e. under the **Vengi (Eastern) Chalukyas** (APPSC PYQ ×2) – not Kubja Vishnuvardhana, Pulakesin II, Rajaraja Narendra, Kakatiyas or Vijayanagara.
[0:3] - **Draksharama founder:** **Chalukya Bhima I** built the **Bhimeswara** temple (G2 1993); an **Eastern Chalukya** monument, not Pallava or Satavahana.
[0:4] - **Twin temples:** **Draksharama Bhimeswara** and **Kumararama Bhimeswara at Samalkot**, the latter in memory of his **son**.

[0:7] - **Amaravati is a Pancharama** (G1 1991), not a Shakti Peetha.
[0:8] - **Not a Pancharama:** **Simhachalam** (a Narasimha shrine) (MPDO 1997); Chalukya Bhimavaram is one.
[0:9] - **Chalukya Bhimavaram = Samalkot (Samarlakota)** (Welfare Officers 2017) – not Pithapuram, Draksharama or Gudipudi.
[0:10] - **All are two-storeyed except Ksheerarama**; four lie in the Godavari delta, **Amararama** on the **Krishna**.
[0:11] - **Literary sources:** **Skanda Purana** and **Srinatha's Bhimeswara Puranam**.

[0:15] - **Jananathapura:** Draksharama was also the provincial capital and coronation seat of the Chalukya-Chola viceroys of Vengi.

## SUB 1 | Other Temple Builders – Vijayaditya II, Bikkavolu and Rajahmundry
[1:0] # Vijayaditya II – 108 temples
[1:1] - **Vijayaditya II (Narendra Mrigaraja):** fought **108 battles in 12 years** against the Rashtrakutas (**Govinda III, Amoghavarsha I**) and their Gangavadi allies.
[1:2] - **108 Shiva temples** (**Narendreswara / Narendra Mrigeswara**) at the battle sites – recorded in the **Sataluru** inscription (G2 2008, 2012, 2019; statement a of G2 2025 ✓).
[1:3] - **⚠ Trap:** the source is **Sataluru**, not Kopparam (a Pulakesin II record).
[1:4] # Bikkavolu (Biccavolu) group
[1:5] - **Old name:** **Birudankaprolu = Bikkavolu** (East Godavari), after Gunaga Vijayaditya's title **Birudankabhima** (Welfare Officers 2017; APPSC PYQ).
[1:6] - **Builder:** **Gunaga Vijayaditya III** (848-892) – **Golingeswara, Rajarajeswara (Raja Raja), Chandrasekhara, Mukteswara, Vijayeswara**.
[1:7] - **Golingeswara** = the only one still standing; **Vijayeswara** has carved **Ganga and Yamuna** figures.
[1:8] - **⚠ Settled:** "**Rajaraja Narendra** built the Rajarajeswara temple at Biccavole" is the **incorrect** statement (G2 Mains 2025 key: "b only").
[1:9]   - NextGen p.20 credits Rajaraja Narendra with a temple at Bikkavolu; the ASI attributes the whole group to Gunaga Vijayaditya III.

## SUB 2 | Vengi School and the Chalukya-Chola Style
[2:0] - **Vengi School:** mainly **Dravida** temples – **Pallava (Kanchi) + Badami Chalukya** influences with **local Andhra** features; local laterite and limestone; concentrated in the **Krishna-Godavari delta**.
[2:1] - **Vesara (Chalukya) style:** NextGen defines it as **Dravidian vimana + Buddhist** art; it belongs to the **Badami Chalukyas** and was brought into Andhra by the Vengi Chalukyas.
[2:2] - **Navabrahma temples (nine), Alampur:** **Badami Chalukyas**, 7th–8th c. – not Eastern Chalukyas.
[2:3] - **Chalukya-Chola model:** the **Draksharama Bhimeswara** temple blends **Pallava + early Chalukya** features into an independent style (G2 Mains 2025).

  [2:4.1] Brihadeeswara, Thanjavur | Imperial Chola

  [2:4.3] Lepakshi | Vijayanagara
