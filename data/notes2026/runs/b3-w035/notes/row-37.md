=== IRDP and the Direct-Attack Programmes (1977-1999)
# Programme → full form, year, aim
| Programme | Full form / aim | Start |
|---|---|---|
| **Food for Work (FFW)** | Foodgrains as wages for rural works; **renamed / restructured as NREP** in 1980; revived as NFFWP (14 Nov 2004), merged into NREGA | **1977-78** |
| **IRDP** | **Integrated Rural Development Programme**: subsidy + bank credit (income-generating assets) so that **poor rural families come above the poverty line**; self-employment; **individual family** approach | Started in a small way **1978-79** (experimental); **all blocks 2 Oct 1980** (**6th Plan**, 1980-85) |
| **TRYSEM** | **Training of Rural Youth for Self-Employment**; supporting arm of IRDP; a **rural development** programme | **15 Aug 1979** |
| **NREP** | **National Rural Employment Programme**; wage employment; successor of FFW | **Oct 1980** (6th Plan) |
| **RLEGP** | **Rural Landless Employment Guarantee Programme** | **1983** (6th Plan) |
| **DWCRA** | **Development of Women and Children in Rural Areas**; sub-scheme of IRDP for women's groups; "thrift and credit society" in the group; income activities, **not natural-resource** SHGs | **1982-83** (50 districts) |
| **JRY** | **Jawahar Rozgar Yojana** = merger of **NREP + RLEGP**; **labour-oriented additional (wage) employment** | **April 1989** (7th Plan) – not April 1995 |
| **SITRA** | **Supply of Improved Toolkits to Rural Artisans** – sub-scheme of **IRDP** | 1992 |
| **Ganga Kalyan Yojana** | Bore-well / tube-well irrigation for small and marginal farmers | 1997 |
| **PMRY / REGP** | Prime Minister's Rozgar Yojana (self-employment) / Rural Employment Generation Programme | **1993 / 1995** |
- **IRDP main aim:** assistance to **poor rural families to come above the poverty line** on a lasting basis (assets + credit); the **Seventh Plan** strategy = **total household approach**.
- **IRDP, TRYSEM and NREP** = all for eradication of rural poverty; **NREP and RLEGP** both in the **6th Plan**; IRDP is the programme of the **Sixth Plan** (1980).
- **"Direct attack on poverty"** = IRDP, NREP, RLEGP, JRY (target-group approach); **Family Planning** is a population measure, NOT direct attack.
- **Odd-one-out (not anti-poverty):** **MRTP** (1969 Act on monopolies), **NSSF** (2011, ₹1,000 crore social-security fund for unorganised workers), **CRY** (an NGO); MGNREGA, NRLM, SJSRY, TRYSEM, JRY, IRDP are anti-poverty.
- **Five categories of poverty-alleviation programmes:** self-employment, wage employment, food security, social security, urban poverty alleviation.
- **Assertion-reason (Sachivalayam):** change in beneficiaries' socio-economic status is due to PAPs (A) and income/employment were created (R) – both true; official key: R not the correct explanation.
| Chronology | Order |
|---|---|
| IRDP, RLEGP, PMRY, REGP | **IRDP (1978-80) → RLEGP (1983) → PMRY (1993) → REGP (1995)** |
| IRDP, NREP, DWCRA, JRY | **IRDP → NREP (1980) → DWCRA (1982-83) → JRY (1989)** |
| Long chain | IRDP (1980) → SGSY (1999) → NRLM (2011) → DAY-NRLM (2016) |
> Year angle: "IRDP started" = **1978-79** when 1978-79 is offered; **1980** (nationwide, 2 Oct) when only single years are offered. Avoid "IRDP pilot 1976".

=== Restructuring Chain – SGSY to DAY-NRLM
| Old | New | Year |
|---|---|---|
| **IRDP + TRYSEM + DWCRA + SITRA + GKY + Million Wells Scheme (6)** | **SGSY** (Swarnajayanti Gram Swarozgar Yojana) – replaced IRDP | **1 April 1999** |
| SGSY | **NRLM "Aajeevika"** | Restructured 2010; launched **June 2011**; fully replaced SGSY **1 April 2013** |
| NRLM | **DAY-NRLM** (Deendayal **Antyodaya** Yojana – NRLM) | **29 March 2016** |
| SJSRY (1997) | NULM → DAY-NULM | 2013 |
| PMRY | PMEGP | 2008 |
- **SGSY = restructured IRDP and TRYSEM** (with four allied schemes); **group financing** – organising the rural poor into **SHGs**; emphasis on skills of the self-employed; bring assisted BPL families above the poverty line.
- **IRDP = individual family approach → SGSY = SHG/group approach.**
- **JRY is not part of SGSY.** SGSY → NRLM = rural chain; SJSRY → NULM = urban chain.
- "Aajeevika" was NRLM's brand name; there is no "Deendayal Aajeevika Yojana".

=== DAY-NRLM – Design, Panchasutra and Scale
- **Ministry of Rural Development**; Centrally Sponsored; funding (Aug 2026): general **60:40**; NE incl. Sikkim **90:10**; UTs **100%**.
- **Aim:** mobilise rural poor households (one woman per household) into **Self Help Groups and their federations**; the **institutions of the poor** plan and implement interventions for economic development and social justice.
- **Primary unit** of community institutional design = **Self Help Group**; tiers: **SHG → Village Organisation → Cluster-Level Federation**.
- **Borrowers:** **women SHGs** (not individual farmers, SMEs or cooperatives); SHG of **10-20 members**; poor identified by **Participatory Identification of Poor (PIP)**, vetted by the **Gram Sabha**; up to **30%** members may be slightly above the poverty line.
- **Panchasutra (5 conditions):** regular meetings, regular savings, regular internal lending, timely repayment, up-to-date books → SHGs practising it for **3-6 months** get the **Revolving Fund**.
- **Interest subvention (May 2013):** women SHGs get loans up to **₹3 lakh at 7%**.
- **Capitalisation support:** ₹5,919.71 crore to SHG women till Nov 2018 (APPSC Panchayat Secretary key; figure not traced in an official release).
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Coverage (3 Feb 2026) | ~10 crore households; 91 lakh SHGs; 5.35 lakh VOs; 33,558 CLFs |
| Lakhpati Didi (2023) | ₹1 lakh+ a year; 2 crore crossed (Feb 2026); target 6 crore by March 2029 (Aug 2026) |
| SVEP (2016) | Start-up Village Entrepreneurship Programme: non-farm enterprises; 4.32 lakh; women ≥60% (Jul 2026) |
| Mahila Kisan Sashaktikaran Pariyojana | Sub-component of DAY-NRLM for women farmers (not part of NSAP) |

=== SHG Concept and SHG-Bank Linkage
| Person / body | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Muhammad Yunus**, Grameen Bank (1976) | Idea of SHGs / group lending as a **tool for poverty alleviation**; collateral-free loans to poor women |
| MYRADA | Pioneered bank linkage, mid-1980s (not the national programme) |
| **NABARD** | **SHG-Bank Linkage pilot 1992** (about 500 SHGs, 1992-93); world's **largest microfinance programme**; **refinances** banks, trains and promotes SHGs |
- **SHG:** 10-20 members; **microfinance + thrift** for poor women; **loan decisions taken by the members** themselves; **all members are jointly responsible** for a member's loan.
- SHG programme first started by **NABARD, not SBI**; **RRBs and scheduled commercial banks** finance SHGs; RBI = regulator.
- **Savings-linked SHGs (NABARD):** ~**85-87 lakh** (2016-18) → **143.3 lakh** (31 March 2025).
> Year trap: SHG-BLP = **1992** – not 1990, 1995 or 1999.

=== SHG Movement in Andhra Pradesh – SERP, Stree Nidhi, Unnathi
| Programme | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| DWCRA in AP | 1982-83 | With UNICEF; rural BPL women's self-employment |
| SAPAP | 1995 | UNDP; Mahabubnagar, Kurnool, Anantapur |
| **Janmabhoomi** | **1997** | AP rural development programme |
| **SERP** | 2000 | Society for Elimination of Rural Poverty; also called **Velugu** |
| Velugu | 2000 | World Bank District Poverty Initiatives Project; federated SHGs into VOs, **Mandal Samakhyas** |
| IKP | 2004-05 | Velugu + DWCRA merged; by SERP |
| Pavala Vaddi | 2004 | Interest subsidy for SHGs |
- **Unnathi (PoP):** SERP programme to bring the **poorest of the poor** (SC/ST sub-plan funds) out of poverty; implemented by **Velugu (SERP)**.
- **SARAS** (Sales of Articles of Rural Artisans Society) – listed among SERP's NRLM non-farm initiatives.
- **Stree Nidhi** (Credit Cooperative Federation, 2011): livelihood loans to SHG women, rural and urban, through VOs/MS; loan credited **within 48 hours**; about half its credit earmarked for the **poorest of the poor** (APPSC key 50%); loans up to ₹1 lakh at 11%.
- **Abhaya Hastham** (Velugu Pension and Insurance): for **all women enrolled in SHGs**; member contributes **₹365 a year** (₹1 a day), Government matches.
- **Pasupu Kumkuma** (2019): one-time assistance to **SHG members**. **Vaddi Leni Runalu**: interest-free loans rewarding prompt repayment.
- **SERP study of SC SHG CBOs:** about **40-60% of SHGs defunct** (APPSC key).
- **SHG-Bank Linkage:** AP **1st**, 36% national share, **99.66% recovery**; ~₹32,190 crore rural disbursement (2024-25).
- AP's SERP/Velugu = template for NRLM; rural = SERP, urban = MEPMA. Challenges: uneven quality, weak market links, debt stress, political interference.

=== DAY-NULM and MEPMA (Urban)
- **Ministry of Housing and Urban Poverty Alleviation** (now Housing and Urban Affairs) – **not Labour**; replaced **SJSRY** (2013); skill development, credit and shelters for urban poor; NextGen gives funding 75:25 (90:10 NE/special category).
| Component | Meaning |
|---|---|
| SM&ID | Social Mobilisation & Institution Development |
| SUSV | Support to Urban Street Vendors – vending spaces, credit, skills |
| SUH | Shelter for Urban Homeless – permanent all-weather shelters |
| **PAISA** | **Portal for Affordable Credit and Interest Subvention Access** |
- **AP agency: MEPMA** – Mission for Elimination of Poverty in Municipal Areas (**1 Sep 2007**) = **poverty alleviation scheme for the urban poor**.
- **UNDP – DAY-NULM** partnership (**June 2023**): empowering **women** to make well-informed career choices in entrepreneurship.
- Urban poverty is rooted in rural areas: low rural literacy; migrants do low-quality work.
