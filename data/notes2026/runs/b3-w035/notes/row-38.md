=== Insurance Schemes – PMSBY, PMJJBY, AABY, RSBY and Others
# PMJJBY vs PMSBY (Jan Suraksha schemes, 9 May 2015, DFS)
| Feature | PMJJBY | PMSBY |
|---|---|---|
| Type | **Life – one-year renewable term** insurance (death from any cause); NOT health insurance | **Accidental** death and disability (personal accident cover) |
| Age | **18-50** (cover continues to 55) | **18-70** (not 18-50) |
| Premium | ₹330 → **₹436/yr** (1 June 2022) | **₹12/yr** (flat per member) → **₹20/yr** from 1 June 2022 |
| Cover | ₹2 lakh | ₹2 lakh death/total disability; ₹1 lakh partial |
| Enrolments | 27.33 crore | 57.92 crore |
- **Jan Suraksha schemes** = **PMSBY, PMJJBY** (+ APY); Sovereign Gold Bond and Kisan Vikas Patra are savings instruments, not Jan Suraksha.
- PMSBY has **no suicide cover**; 34.18 crore enrolled by Apr 2023.

| Scheme | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Aam Aadmi Bima Yojana** | Launched **2 Oct 2007**; LIC; life and disability cover for **rural landless households below the poverty line** (head or one earning member) |
| **RSBY** | Health insurance for **unorganised-sector (BPL) workers**; launched by the **Ministry of Labour** (not Health); enrolment from 1 Apr 2008; now in **Ayushman Bharat – PMJAY** |
| **Rajiv Gandhi Shilpi Swasthya Bima Yojana** | Health insurance for handicraft artisans – **Ministry of Textiles** |

| **Krishi Shramik Samajik Suraksha Yojana** (1 July 2001) | **Pension + insurance** for agricultural workers |
| **Ashraya Bima Yojana** (2001) | Social security to workers who **lose their jobs**; up to 30% of salary for a year |

=== Pension Schemes – APY, NPS, PM-SYM, PM-KMY, PMVVY
| Scheme | Target / entry age | Contribution | Benefit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Atal Pension Yojana** (9 May 2015) | **Unorganised workers 18-40** (citizens with a savings bank / post-office account); not taxpayers (from Oct 2022) | Age-linked; Govt co-contribution **50% or ₹1,000/yr for 5 yrs** (joined before 31 Dec 2015) – NOT 100% | **Guaranteed (defined-benefit) ₹1,000-5,000/month at 60**; same pension to **spouse for life**; any member of a family may join |
| **PM-SYM** (2019) | Unorganised workers **18-40** (not 21-40), income ≤ ₹15,000/month; not in NPS/ESIC/EPFO | Voluntary, **age-specific contribution** ₹55-200; Centre matches | **₹3,000/month at 60**; family pension (50%) to **spouse only** |
| **PM Kisan Maan-dhan** (**12 Sep 2019, Ranchi**) | Small and marginal farmers 18-40 | ₹55-200/month; Centre matches | ₹3,000/month at 60 |
| **NPS** | Citizens (resident/NRI) and **OCI** 18-70; minors via NPS Vatsalya; **compulsory for Central Govt civilian employees joining on/after 1 Jan 2004** (not Armed Forces); **State Govt employees from the date of each State's notification** | Voluntary, **defined contribution**, market-linked | Corpus + annuity |
| **PM Vaya Vandana Yojana** (LIC, 2017) | Senior citizens 60+ | Lump-sum purchase | Assured pension for 10 years – **social security in old age**, protects the elderly **against a future fall in interest rates** |
| Unified Pension Scheme (2025) | Central Govt employees under NPS | Govt-backed | 50% of average basic pay of last 12 months for 25 years' service |
- **PFRDA** regulates NPS (not SEBI/IRDAI) and administers APY.
- NPS: an account **cannot be opened or operated for a third person**.
- 9 May 2015 = launch day of **PMJJBY, PMSBY and APY** together.
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 18 p.5 (APY co-contribution); Ramesh Singh Indian Economy 10th ed. part 05 (NPS-Swavalamban, APY); app notes 3:38 sub 5

=== DBT, PAHAL and Ujjwala
- **DBT** piloted **1 Jan 2013** (7 schemes, 20 districts); scaled up in 2015 with JAM; FY 2024-25: **₹6.9 lakh crore, 327 schemes**; the **DBT Mission** sits in the **Cabinet Secretariat**.
- **State DBT nodal officer / coordinator:** a **Principal Secretary (Planning/IT/Finance)** or equivalent officer.
- **MGNREGS:** largest number of DBT transactions in 2018-19 (about 43 crore by Feb 2019); **PAHAL** = largest cash-transfer scheme by beneficiaries.
| Scheme | Fact |
|---|---|
| **PAHAL** (Pratyaksha Hastaantarit Laabh) | **DBT of LPG subsidy into the consumer's bank account**; 54 districts 15 Nov 2014, national **1 Jan 2015**; **first JAM application**; **Guinness record (2015): world's largest cash-transfer programme**; Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas |
| **PM Ujjwala Yojana** | **Free LPG connection to women of BPL households** – clean cooking fuel to safeguard the **health of women and children**; **1 May 2016, Ballia (UP)**; "Swachh Indhan Behtar Jeevan"; **₹8,000 crore** for 3 years; **5 crore** connections; support **₹1,600** per connection (not ₹2,800); target later raised to 8 crore |
- Schemes vs inequality: PMJDY, MGNREGA, Stand-Up India reduce inequality; **UDAN** (regional air connectivity) does not.
~~ Sources: Business Standard/IANS 10 May 2019 (top DBT schemes, MGNREGA 43.24 crore transactions; web search); app notes 3:38 sub 2

=== PM Jan Dhan Yojana, JAM and Aadhaar
- **PMJDY:** launched **28 Aug 2014**; **Department of Financial Services**; financial inclusion; **zero-balance** account, **RuPay** card; **Guinness World Record** – most bank accounts opened in one week (18 million, 23-29 Aug 2014).
| Feature | Old | Now |
|---|---|---|
| RuPay accident cover | ₹1 lakh | ₹2 lakh |
| Overdraft | ₹5,000 | ₹10,000 |
| Focus (after 14 Aug 2018) | Every household | Every unbanked adult |
- **Phase II (2015-18):** overdraft; Credit Guarantee Fund; unorganised-sector pensions (**Swavalamban**). Accounts (19 Aug 2026): **59.09 crore**; women 32.92 crore; rural 45.95 crore.
| JAM component | Role |
|---|---|
| Jan Dhan | Bank account – destination of funds |
| Aadhaar | Biometric identity – verifies beneficiary |
| Mobile | Communication channel |
- JAM first proposed in **Economic Survey 2014-15**. **Aadhaar** launched **29 Sep 2010** at **Tembhli** (Nandurbar, Maharashtra).
| India Stack layer | Enabler |
|---|---|
| Presence-less | Aadhaar authentication |
| Paperless | e-KYC, e-Sign, DigiLocker |
| Cashless | UPI, AePS |
| Consent | DEPA; Account Aggregator – consent-based data sharing (not ATM cash) |
- **APBS** = DBT routing to Aadhaar-seeded accounts; **AePS** = cash withdrawal at micro-ATMs.
- **Jeevan Pramaan** (2014): pensioners create a **digital life certificate** (Aadhaar biometrics) for the Pension Disbursing Agency.
- **Aadhaar Act, 2016, Sec 7:** Aadhaar may be required for subsidies from the Consolidated Fund of India.
| Case | Year | Bench | Ruling |
|---|---|---|---|
| K.S. Puttaswamy I | 2017 | 9 judges | Privacy a fundamental right under Art. 21 |
| K.S. Puttaswamy II | 2018 | 5 judges, 4:1 | Sec 7 upheld; Sec 57 (private use) struck down |
- Challenge: **biometric-failure** exclusion (elderly, labourers); data privacy.
~~ Sources: Ramesh Singh Indian Economy 10th ed. part 05 (PMJDY launched 28 Aug 2014); app notes 3:38 sub 0, 1, 3

=== NSAP and Social Security Concepts
- **NSAP:** from **15 Aug 1995**; **Ministry of Rural Development**; Core of the Core CSS; fulfils **Article 41**; BPL households; chief aim = **old-age pension to the very poor**.
| Component | Who | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| IGNOAPS | 60-79 / 80+ | ₹200 / ₹500 a month |
| IGNWPS | BPL widows | Monthly pension |
| IGNDPS | BPL, 18-79, 80%+ disability | ₹300 a month |
| NFBS | Family losing breadwinner | Lump sum |
| Annapurna | Needy elderly not on IGNOAPS | 10 kg free foodgrains/month |
- **NOT in NSAP / not social security:** **Mahila Kisan Sashaktikaran Pariyojana** (DAY-NRLM), **Skill Development Programme**, **SJSRY** (employment generation).
- ~3.09 crore beneficiaries (FY 2025-26); May 2026: old-age pension lost nearly **half its real value** to inflation.
- **Universal Basic Income** = an unconditional **minimum income support by the Government to every citizen** (Economic Survey 2016-17).
- **ILO** = the UN body for social security; about **80% of the world's population** lacked adequate **social security** (ILO).
~~ Sources: Ramesh Singh Indian Economy 10th ed. part 06 p.87 (UBI); app notes 3:38 sub 6; general knowledge, not checked (ILO 80%)

=== Social Security Laws and Bodies – ESI, EPF, NSSN
| Law / body | Year | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **ESI Act** | 1948 | Covers factories and **hotels, restaurants, motor (road) transport, newspapers, shops, private medical and educational institutions** (10+ persons); wage ceiling **₹21,000/month** (2017) |
| EPF & MP Act | 1952 | Establishments with 20 or more employees |
| **Unorganised Workers' Social Security Act** | **2008** | Notified 30 Dec 2008; merged into the Code on Social Security, 2020 |
| Code on Social Security | 2020 | One of 4 labour codes replacing 29 laws |
| **NHFDC** (now Divyangjan FDC) | Jan 1997 | Under the **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment** |
- **EPF interest 2016-17:** **8.65%**, recommended by EPFO's **Central Board of Trustees** (ratified by Finance Ministry).
- **National Social Security Number card (June 2004):** issued by the **Labour Ministry (EPFO)**, not Home Affairs; **same number continues on change of employment**; forerunner of the **UAN (2014)**.
- **eShram** (26 Aug 2021): National Database of Unorganised Workers with a **UAN**. Social protection coverage: **64.3%** (2025).
- **Ministry of Labour schemes:** PMRPY, PM Laghu Vyapari Maan-dhan, National Child Labour Project – **DAY-NULM is under Housing and Urban Affairs**.
~~ Sources: Ramesh Singh Indian Economy 10th ed. part 06 (unorganised workers, ESI); app notes 3:38 sub 7

=== AP Social Security – NTR Bharosa, Chandranna Bima
- **NTR Bharosa (2014):** a **livelihood pension scheme for the old and disabled** (secure and dignified life for the poor); ₹1,000 a month, **₹1,500 for persons with 80%+ disability** (2014-19).
- **YSR Pension Kanuka** (2019): doorstep delivery through Village/Ward Secretariat staff.
- **NTR Bharosa Pension** again (June 2024): old age, widows etc. **₹4,000**; disabled **₹6,000**; fully disabled **₹15,000**; chronic diseases ₹10,000 (from 1 July 2024); first distribution **65.31 lakh** beneficiaries, 28 categories.
- **Chandranna Bima** (2016): death and disability insurance for **unorganised workers**.
