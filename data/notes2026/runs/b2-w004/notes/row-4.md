=== 42nd Amendment, 1976 – Socialist, Secular, Integrity
# What the 42nd Amendment did to the Preamble
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Words added | **Socialist**, **Secular**, **Integrity** (three words) |
| Description of India | "Sovereign Democratic Republic" → "**Sovereign Socialist Secular Democratic Republic**" |
| Fraternity clause | "unity of the Nation" → "**unity and integrity of the Nation**" |
| Year / context | **1976**, during the **Emergency** (1975-77), **Indira Gandhi** government |
| Based on | **Swaran Singh Committee** |
| Nickname | "**Mini Constitution**" – it changed the Preamble, the Seventh Schedule and 53 Articles |
- **Only amendment of the Preamble so far:** the **42nd (1976)**; asked about 25 times (APPSC G2 2008, 2011, 2025 Mains; AEE 2008; Endowment 2008; JL 2003; Polytechnic 2019; Degree Lecturers 2020; Constable 2016; SSC, NDA 2018, UPPSC, MPPSC 2023).
- **⚠ Trap:** not the 1st, 2nd, 17th, 24th, 25th, 38th, 40th, 41st, 43rd, 44th, 45th, 46th, 52nd, 73rd or 86th Amendment ("1976 Second Amendment" is wrong).
- **'Socialist' and 'Secular' were inserted between 'Sovereign' and 'Democratic'.**
- **Integrity** was **not** in the Preamble in **1975**; it came in **1976** (SSC 2019; UPPSC 2006). The word **"unity"** was in the **original** text – only "and integrity" was added (a question saying "'Unity of the Nation' was added by the 42nd" means this change).
- **NOT added by the 42nd:** **Fraternity**, **Sovereign**, Democratic, Republic (all original); **Gram Swarajya**, Dignity of person, Privacy, Liberal are not 42nd words.
- **Assertion-reason:** "India is a sovereign socialist secular democratic republic" (A) is true and its reason – the 42nd Act **substituted** these words for "Sovereign Democratic Republic" (R) – correctly explains it.
# Other changes of the 42nd Amendment (for "not associated" items)
- Added **Fundamental Duties** (Part IVA) and new DPSPs; **curtailed** judicial review in some matters but did **not abolish** it entirely.
- **Minerva Mills (1980)** struck down its Art. 368 clauses **(4) and (5)** (unlimited amending power, exclusion of judicial review) and the clause giving **DPSPs full priority over all Fundamental Rights** – restoring the balance of Parts III and IV.
> Status today: in **Dr Balram Singh v. Union of India (25 Nov 2024)** the Supreme Court **upheld 'Socialist' and 'Secular'** and dismissed the challenges to their insertion (as of 2026; the words were again debated politically in Jul 2025).

=== Preamble Text – Order of Words, Original 1949 Text, Words Not in It
# The text (as amended in 1976)
- **WE, THE PEOPLE OF INDIA**, having solemnly resolved to constitute India into a **SOVEREIGN SOCIALIST SECULAR DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC** and to secure to all its citizens: **JUSTICE**, social, economic and political; **LIBERTY** of thought, expression, belief, faith and worship; **EQUALITY** of status and of opportunity; and to promote among them all **FRATERNITY** assuring the dignity of the individual and the unity and integrity of the Nation; IN OUR CONSTITUENT ASSEMBLY this twenty-sixth day of November, 1949, do HEREBY ADOPT, ENACT AND GIVE TO OURSELVES THIS CONSTITUTION.
- **Order:** **Sovereign → Socialist → Secular → Democratic → Republic** (code C, A, D, B for Socialist A, Democratic B, Sovereign C, Secular D); objectives in order **Justice → Liberty → Equality → Fraternity**.
- **India is declared secular by the Preamble** (Fundamental Rights, DPSP and 9th Schedule do not "describe" India as secular).
- **Equality of** → **status** (and of opportunity); not "of nationality", "religion" or "community".
# Original 1949 text vs now
| Item | 26 Nov 1949 / 26 Jan 1950 | Now (after 1976) |
|---|---|---|
| Description of India | "**Sovereign Democratic Republic**" | "Sovereign Socialist Secular Democratic Republic" |
| Fraternity clause | "unity of the Nation" | "unity and integrity of the Nation" |
| Words absent in 1949 | **Socialist, Secular, Integrity** | added |
- **Exact status on 26 January 1950:** a **Sovereign Democratic Republic** (UPSC 2021).
- **Not in the 1949 Preamble:** Socialism / Socialist, Secular, Integrity; "Reliable" and "Non-religious" were never in it; **Republic** was in the original (UPPSC 2009; Uttarakhand 2024 → 2, 3, 4).
# Words NOT in the Preamble at all
- **Federal / federation** (APPSC Deputy Surveyors 2012; UPPSC 2021) – "Quasi-federal" is not in it either.
- **Freedom** (it uses **Liberty**), **Right** (as a word), **Public Welfare** / **Welfare State**, **Adult franchise**, **Fundamental Duties**, "**People's**".
- **Religious Justice**, **Natural** or **Cultural** justice (justice is only **social, economic, political**).
- **Political Liberty** and **Economic Liberty** as categories; liberty "of action" (liberty is only of **thought, expression, belief, faith, worship**) (UPSC 2017; APPSC G2).
- Words that ARE in it: Fraternity, Equality, Justice, **Unity**, Integrity, Thought, Opportunity, **Dignity**, Republic, Sovereign.

=== We, the People – Source of Authority, Date and Adoption
- **Source of the Constitution / of political power:** the **people of India** – the Preamble opens "**We, the People of India**", showing **people's sovereignty**; the Constitution derives its authority from the people (APPSC G1 2002, G2, AEE 2012, CDPO 2013; HWO 2017).
- **⚠ Trap:** not the Constitution, Parliament, "Parliament and State Legislatures", the President or the Supreme Court.
- The Preamble **indicates the source** from which the Constitution comes (APPSC G2 2011).
- **"We, the People of India"** is used in the **Preamble**, not in the FRs, DPSPs or Citizenship part.
- **Enacting clause:** "We, the people of India … **in our Constituent Assembly** … do hereby **adopt, enact and give to ourselves** this Constitution" – the Constitution is declared adopted and enacted by the **people of India**, acting through the **Constituent Assembly** (when only the CA is offered, it is the answer for "adopted by").
# The date
- **Date in the Preamble:** **26 November 1949** = **adoption** of the Constitution (drafting completed; signed by the President of the CA) – not 26 January 1950 (commencement), 24 January 1950 or 13 December 1946.
- **Commencement:** **26 January 1950** (some provisions – citizenship, elections, provisional Parliament – took effect on 26 Nov 1949).
- **Constitution Day:** **26 November**, observed **since 2015** (Constitution Day 2019 = 26 Nov) (APPSC G2 Mains 2017; JL 2020).

=== Is the Preamble Part of the Constitution? – Cases and Legal Status
| Case | Year | Held on the Preamble |
|---|---|---|
| **Berubari Union** (President's reference) | **1960** | **Not a part** of the Constitution (the first such ruling); a "**key to the minds of the makers**"; aid to interpretation where an Article is ambiguous |
| Golaknath | 1967 | About amending Fundamental Rights; not the Preamble case (distractor) |
| **Kesavananda Bharati** | **1973** | **Is a part** of the Constitution (overruled Berubari); amendable under **Art. 368** but the **basic structure** cannot be destroyed; 13 judges |
| **S.R. Bommai** | **1994** | Relied on the Preamble – **secularism** a basic feature; Preamble treated as integral part |
| **LIC of India** | **1995** | Reaffirmed the Preamble is an **integral part** |
| Minerva Mills | 1980 | Struck down 42nd Amendment Art. 368 clauses – not a Preamble-status case |
- **"Preamble is a part" =** **Kesavananda Bharati** (APPSC TWO 2012, G2 2008, ZSWO 2025); with no Kesavananda option, **Bommai** (UPPSC 2012); A.K. Gopalan, Maneka Gandhi, Mohini Jain, Sajjan Singh are distractors.
- **"Not a part" =** **Berubari, 1960** (not 1968; Kesavananda was 1973, not 1970).
# Legal nature
- **Non-justiciable** – **not enforceable** in a court of law (UPPSC 2019).
- **Neither a source of power** to the legislature **nor a prohibition** on its powers; confers no power and creates no right by itself.
- **Part of the Constitution but has no legal effect independently of other parts** (UPSC 2020).
- **Cannot override** the specific provisions of the Constitution; where a provision is clear, the Preamble yields.
- **Aid to interpretation:** may be invoked to determine the **ambit of Fundamental Rights and Directive Principles** (APPSC G1).
- **Amendable** under Art. 368 (done once, 1976) – so "it cannot be amended" is false; "not a part of the Constitution" is false.
- **Enacted after** the rest of the Constitution, so that it conformed to the text – "enacted before the entire Constitution" is false (APPSC G1 2023 → I and III incorrect).
- Reflects the **philosophy, ideals and objectives** of the Constitution – the **mind of the makers** (UPSC 2017) and the **objective of the Constitution** (UPPSC 2013) are reflected in the Preamble, not in FRs, DPSP or FDs.
> Statement sets: "Objectives Resolution became the Preamble ✓; non-justiciable ✓; can't be amended ✗; can't override specific provisions ✓" → **I, II and IV** (UPPSC 2009, Panchayat Secretary). "Not a part ✗; amended only under Art. 368 ✓; three words added in 1976 ✓" → **B and C**.

=== Keywords and Objectives – Meaning
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sovereign** | Supreme authority; free internally and externally; **not subject to any external authority** |
| **Socialist** | **Democratic socialism** – a **mixed economy** (public and private sectors); a blend of Marxism and Gandhism, leaning towards **Gandhian socialism** |
| **Secular** | Equal respect for all religions; **no State religion**; before 1976 the word appeared only in **Art. 25(2)(a)**; the term is **not defined** in any Article |
| **Democratic** | Government by the people; **representative parliamentary democracy**; covers **political, social and economic** democracy; **universal adult franchise** and **rule of law** are its marks |
| **Republic** | **Elected head of State** (the **President**, indirectly elected for 5 years); **no hereditary element**; public offices open to all |
- **Republic ≠ PM:** India is a republic because its **head of State (President) is elected** – not because ministers are responsible to Parliament or hold office at the President's pleasure.
- **Philosophy of the Constitution:** welfare state, socialist state, political equality – but **not a Communist State** (UPPSC 2015).
- India is a **democratic polity, a sovereign State, a democratic society and a welfare state** – all four (UPPSC 2012); the **welfare-state** idea is found in the **Preamble and the DPSP** (UPPSC 1997).
- **Not valid (APPSC G1 2024):** "'democratic' is a mix of British Fabian Socialism and American Populism"; "Republic = elected Prime Minister"; "Liberty is neither absolute nor qualified"; "Fraternity originally denoted only dignity of the individual" → **none valid**.
# Objectives
| Objective | Of / kinds |
|---|---|
| **Justice** | **Social, economic and political** – **three** kinds |
| **Liberty** | **Thought, expression, belief, faith and worship** – five |
| **Equality** | **Status and opportunity** – civic, political, economic dimensions |
| **Fraternity** | Sense of **brotherhood**; assures **dignity of the individual** and **unity and integrity of the Nation**; promoted by **single citizenship** |
- **Goals secured to all:** justice (social, economic, political), liberty of thought and expression, equality of opportunity and **dignity of the individual** – **all** of them (UPPSC 1998).
- **Liberty** = **absence of restraints plus opportunity to develop one's personality**; UPSC 2019 "most appropriate definition" = **opportunity to develop oneself fully** (positive liberty); liberty is not licence to do whatever one likes.
- **Law and liberty (UPSC 2018):** "**If there are no laws, there is no liberty**" – law is the condition of liberty.
- **Political theory links:** popular/political **sovereignty** – **Rousseau** (general will); **distributive justice** – **Aristotle**.

=== Making and Sources of the Preamble's Ideals
- **Basis:** the **Objectives Resolution** of **Jawaharlal Nehru** – moved **13 December 1946**, adopted **22 January 1947**; its modified version became the Preamble (Nehru "proposed the Preamble"; not Ambedkar, B.N. Rau or Gandhi) (APPSC ASWO; Uttarakhand 2006; APPSC JA).
- The Constitution of India was **not** the first to begin with a Preamble (the **US** Constitution did); the Preamble has been amended **once**, not twice.
| Element | Source |
|---|---|
| Idea of a Preamble; "We, the people" | **USA** |
| Language of the Preamble | **Australia** |
| **Liberty, Equality, Fraternity** (the "trinity"); **Republic** | **French Revolution** (1789) / French Constitution |
| **Justice – social, economic and political** | **Russian Revolution** (1917), **USSR** |
| Fraternity (possible) | Art. 1 of the **UDHR** (1948) |
- **⚠ Trap:** Liberty-equality-fraternity is **not** from the Russian, American or Glorious Revolution or the UN Charter; justice is **not** from the French Revolution.
- **Third reading** of the Constitution: **17-26 November 1949**; Preamble enacted last.
