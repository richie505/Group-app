## SUB 0 | SDG India Index – Editions, Front Runners and Score Bands
[0:0] - Published by **NITI Aayog** (with UN support) – main tool to **localise and monitor the SDGs**; promotes **competitive federalism**.
[0:1] TABLE head: Edition | Release | India score | Key facts
  [0:1.0] 1st (Baseline 2018) | Dec 2018 | 57 | Kerala and Himachal Pradesh front runners (score 69); Tamil Nadu 3rd; AP 4th
  [0:1.1] 2nd (2019-20) | Dec 2019 | 60 | Top: Kerala (70), HP (69), AP, TN (67), Telangana; Gujarat not in top five
  [0:1.2] 3rd (2020-21) | Jun 2021 | 66 | Kerala (75); HP and TN (74); AP, Goa, Karnataka, Uttarakhand (72)
  [0:1.3] 4th (2023-24) | 12 Jul 2024 | 71 | Kerala and Uttarakhand (79); TN (78); bottom Bihar (57); AP 74, 9th
[0:2] - **Baseline 2018:** framed on 17 SDGs but **only 13 goals scored**; **4 left out** – SDG **12 (sustainable consumption and production)**, **13**, **14** (no State data) and **17** (international partnerships).
[0:3] - **2023-24 design:** **113 indicators** aligned to MoSPI's NIF (277 indicators); **16 goals** scored, **SDG 17 qualitative**; composite = average of the goal scores (goals not applicable to a State excluded); raw data are **normalised** to 0-100.
[0:4] TABLE head: Category | Score band
  [0:4.0] Achiever | 100
  [0:4.1] Front Runner | 65-99
  [0:4.2] Performer | 50-64
  [0:4.3] Aspirant | 0-49
[0:5] > Kerala topped every edition ("which State topped SDGs" = Kerala). Gujarat is the usual "NOT in top five" answer.

[6:0] - **AIM** set up by **NITI Aayog** in **2016** (16 Jan 2016) – flagship to promote **innovation and entrepreneurship** in schools, universities, research bodies and MSMEs (not MHRD, AICTE, UGC or Ministry of S&T).

  [6:1.0] Atal Tinkering Labs | Schools, grades 6-12
  [6:1.1] Atal Incubation Centres | Start-up incubators
  [6:1.2] Atal New India Challenges | Product innovation
  [6:1.3] Atal Community Innovation Centres | Underserved areas
  [6:1.4] Mentor India | Mentors for ATLs and AICs
[6:2] - **Women Transforming India Awards** (NITI, with UN): 2017 winners (12) included **Safeena Husain** (Educate Girls), Arunima Sinha, Laxmi Agarwal.
[6:3] - **Electric vehicles:** NITI's 2017 proposal – only **electric vehicles** to be sold **after 2030**.
[6:4] - Reports: Strategy for New India @75 (2018); national **MPI** 55.3% (2005-06) → **14.96%** (2019-21) → est. **11.28%** (2022-23); **135 million** left multidimensional poverty 2015-21.
