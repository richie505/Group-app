=== SDG India Index – Editions, Front Runners and Score Bands
- Published by **NITI Aayog** (with UN support) – main tool to **localise and monitor the SDGs**; promotes **competitive federalism**.
| Edition | Release | India score | Key facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st (Baseline 2018) | Dec 2018 | 57 | **Kerala and Himachal Pradesh** front runners (score **69**); **Tamil Nadu** 3rd; **AP 4th** |
| 2nd (2019-20) | Dec 2019 | 60 | Top: Kerala (70), HP (69), AP, TN (67), Telangana; **Gujarat not in top five** |
| 3rd (2020-21) | Jun 2021 | 66 | Kerala (75); HP and TN (74); **AP, Goa, Karnataka, Uttarakhand (72)** |
| 4th (2023-24) | 12 Jul 2024 | 71 | Kerala and Uttarakhand (79); TN (78); bottom Bihar (57); AP 74, 9th |
- **Baseline 2018:** framed on 17 SDGs but **only 13 goals scored**; **4 left out** – SDG **12 (sustainable consumption and production)**, **13**, **14** (no State data) and **17** (international partnerships).
- **2023-24 design:** **113 indicators** aligned to MoSPI's NIF (277 indicators); **16 goals** scored, **SDG 17 qualitative**; composite = average of the goal scores (goals not applicable to a State excluded); raw data are **normalised** to 0-100.
| Category | Score band |
|---|---|
| Achiever | **100** |
| Front Runner | **65-99** |
| Performer | **50-64** |
| Aspirant | **0-49** |
> Kerala topped every edition ("which State topped SDGs" = Kerala). Gujarat is the usual "NOT in top five" answer.
~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.309-316 (SDG India Index 2023-24, score bands); NITI Aayog SDG India Index Baseline Report 2018 and 2019-20, 2020-21 reports; app notes 3:13 sub 2

=== SDG Basics, Nodal Body and Partners
- **17 SDGs, 169 targets**; adopted **25 Sept 2015** under the UN **2030 Agenda**; succeeded the **MDGs (2000-15)**.
| SDG | Name |
|---|---|
| SDG 4 | Quality Education |
| SDG 7 | Affordable and Clean Energy |
| SDG 12 | Responsible Consumption and Production |
| SDG 13 | Climate Action |
| **SDG 14** | **Life Below Water** – conserve and sustainably use oceans, seas, marine resources |
| SDG 15 | Life on Land |
| SDG 17 | Partnerships for the Goals |
- **NITI Aayog** = India's **nodal institution** for SDG implementation (not the Planning, Disinvestment or Finance Commission); **MoSPI** frames the **National Indicator Framework** (277 indicators).
- **April 2022:** NITI Aayog and **UNICEF India** – Statement of Intent on SDGs, focus on **children**.
- **August 2023:** NITI Aayog **MoU with UNDP** on fast-tracking SDGs.
| Tool / report | Publisher |

| Sustainable Development Report / SDG Index | **SDSN** (not NITI Aayog) |

=== Aspirational Districts and Blocks
- **Aspirational Districts Programme:** launched **January 2018**; **112** districts; strategy **Convergence** (of Central and State schemes), **Collaboration**, **Competition**; monthly **delta ranking** on the **Champions of Change** dashboard; 49 indicators.
- **Convergence of schemes** = to **optimise planning and make implementation effective**.
| Theme | Weight |
|---|---|
| Health and Nutrition | 30% |
| Education | 30% |
| Agriculture and Water Resources | 20% |
| Financial Inclusion and Skill Development | 10% |
| Basic Infrastructure | 10% |
- AP districts: **Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam, YSR Kadapa** (after 2022: Alluri Sitharama Raju and Parvathipuram Manyam replaced the parent districts; YSR Kadapa continues).
- **Baseline ranking (March 2018):** **Vizianagaram 1st overall (48.13%)** and **1st in Agriculture and Water Resources**; first delta ranking (May 2018): Vizianagaram 4th, YSR Kadapa 5th.
- Feb 2025: YSR Kadapa among best-performing districts (Rs 3 crore); Jul 2026: ASR district 1st in basic infrastructure.
- **Aspirational Blocks Programme:** **7 January 2023**; **500 blocks**; **40** KPIs.
~~ Sources: Drishti IAS summary of NITI "Transformation of Aspirational Districts – Baseline Ranking" (Mar 2018); NITI First Delta Ranking May 2018; app notes 3:13 sub 6

=== Atal Innovation Mission and Other NITI Initiatives
- **AIM** set up by **NITI Aayog** in **2016** (16 Jan 2016) – flagship to promote **innovation and entrepreneurship** in schools, universities, research bodies and MSMEs (not MHRD, AICTE, UGC or Ministry of S&T).
| Component | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Atal Tinkering Labs | **Schools**, grades 6-12 |
| Atal Incubation Centres | **Start-up** incubators |
| Atal New India Challenges | Product innovation |
| Atal Community Innovation Centres | Underserved areas |
| Mentor India | Mentors for ATLs and AICs |
- **Women Transforming India Awards** (NITI, with UN): 2017 winners (12) included **Safeena Husain** (Educate Girls), Arunima Sinha, Laxmi Agarwal.
- **Electric vehicles:** NITI's 2017 proposal – only **electric vehicles** to be sold **after 2030**.
- Reports: Strategy for New India @75 (2018); national **MPI** 55.3% (2005-06) → **14.96%** (2019-21) → est. **11.28%** (2022-23); **135 million** left multidimensional poverty 2015-21.
~~ Sources: NITI Aayog AIM (aim.gov.in); NITI "Women Transforming India 2017 Awardees" (niti.gov.in); NITI MPI reports 2023-24; app notes 3:13 sub 5, 8
