=== Court Artists and Scholars – Chellavva and Ugradityacharya
# Chellavva – the most-asked person of this row
- **Who:** **Chellavva (Challavva / Chellava)** – court **singer and dancer** of **Chalukya Bhima I** (G2 2011 ×2; APPSC PYQ).
- **Titles:** **Gandharva-Vidya-Visarada** (G2 Mains 2019 ×2; APPSC PYQ ×2) and **Samasta Gandharva Vidya Kovida**.
- **Reward:** Bhima I gave her the village **Attili** (Attili inscription) and two kandikas of land.
- **Father:** **Mallappa**, a renowned musician, compared to **Tumburu** in the Attili inscription.
- **⚠ Trap:** patron = **Chalukya Bhima I**, not Gunaga Vijayaditya, Rajaraja Narendra or the Rashtrakuta Amoghavarsha.
- **⚠ Trap:** in the "who got the title" options, **Mallappa** (her father), **Chamekamba** (courtesan of Ammaraja II's time) and **Bhattadeva** (court poet) are distractors.

# Music and dance
- **Hallisaka = Kolatam:** the popular dance of the period; music and dance were held in courts and temples.
- **Bhairavakona** (Nallamala hills, **Prakasam**): **eight rock-cut Shiva cave temples** (7th–8th c.), a central seat of Shaivism; sculptures of musical instruments.
- **Panchadarla** (Dharapalem, **Anakapalli**): **Uma Dharmalingeswara** temple with Eastern Chalukya and 15th-century inscriptions; ASI recorded **17** in 1899 (in news Aug 2026).

=== Religion – Sects, Agamas and the Chalukya Mother Goddess
# Family goddess
- **Mother goddess of the early Chalukyas:** **Hariti** – the Chalukyas called themselves **Hariti-putras** of the **Manavya gotra**, nourished by the **Seven Mothers** (Saptamatrikas) (G2 2019).
  - **⚠ Settled:** the printed key "Cushmandini" is wrong – **Kushmandini** is a **Jain yakshi**; Rocket and the Chalukya prasastis give **Hariti**.
- **Same goddess, other dynasty:** Hariti was also the tutelary deity of the **Ikshvakus**; the metronymic "Hariti-putra" suggests a southern origin of the Eastern Chalukyas.
# Shaivism and Vaishnavism
- **Shaivism dominant:** sects **Pasupata** (founder **Lakulisha**, oldest, peaceful), **Kalamukha** (extremist) and **Kapalika** (Bhairava worship).
- **Trilinga desa:** Draksharama, Kaleshwaram and Srisailam; Srisailam was called a Kapalika kshetra by Bhavabhuti.
- **Vaishnava kings:** **Kubja Vishnuvardhana** (Kunti Madhava temple, Pithapuram) and **Danarnava** (Mandavya Narayana temple, Samalkot).
- **Jayasimha I** performed the **Ashvamedha**; royal prasastis boast of many Ashvamedhas.
- **Jayasimha II** brought the **Koya Kottalu** (Boya Kottas) into the Brahmanical fold.
# Agamas and saint traditions
| Tradition | Faith | Point |

| **Pancharatra** | Vaishnava | Common to **Ramanuja and Madhva** (APPSC Endowment 2022) |

# Buddhism
- **Declining:** Buddharamas became Shaiva temples (Dhanyakataka → Amareswara); Hiuen Tsang (639) found Buddhist sites decaying; survived at **Amaravati and Nagarjunakonda**.

=== Administration – Offices, Nadus and Taxes
# Structure
- **State theory:** the **Saptanga** idea (seven limbs); king autocratic, succession to the eldest son; official language **Sanskrit**.
- **Ashtadasa Tirthas** = **eighteen administrative offices**.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Tirthulu | Council of ministers; **Samaharta** = its chief |
| Vishaya / Mandala | Province under a **Vishayapati / Mandaleswara** |
| Kottam | Another territorial subdivision |
| Grameyaka / Gramani / Urgavunda | Village head |
| Rattodi (Rattadi) | Chief village officer |
| Vanneyus (Manneyus) | **Toll collectors** |
| Sthanadhipati | Officer managing temples and their endowments |
| Pradvivaka | King's adviser on **judicial** matters; verdicts = **jayapatras** with the royal seal |
| Talari | Village watchman; paid the value of goods if the thief was not caught |
# Nadus → region
| Nadu | Region |
|---|---|
| **Pakanadu** | **Nellore** (asked 2002) |
| Palnadu | Macharla, Gurajala |
| Kammanadu | Bapatla, Ongole, Narasaraopet |
| Venginadu | Krishna and Godavari |
- **Capitals:** Pithapuram → **Vengi** (Jayasimha I) → **Rajamahendravaram** (Ammaraja I).
# Taxes
- **Land tax:** **1/6** of the produce, called **Koru / Aripannu / Siddhaya**; tax-free land = **Sarva Kara Pariharam**.
- **Udranga:** in epigraphy the **fixed or principal land tax** (tax on permanent tenants), paired with **uparikara** (minor taxes) – D.C. Sircar.
  - **⚠ Settled:** "Udranga = tax on water" (old app key) has no source support; a PYQ offering only tolls / salt / water / shop tax has no correct option.

=== Society, Economy and Guilds
# Society
- **Varna order** with Brahmin privilege; Brahmins split into **Vaidikas** and **Niyogis** (administration and army).
- **Agraharas:** tax-free villages for Brahmins, who performed **Hiranyagarbha** and **Bahusuvarna** rituals.
- **Vaisyas = Komatis**; their guilds = **Nakarams**; Reddy, Kamma and Kapu communities grew strong.
- **Panchanamvaru:** five artisan groups (blacksmith, goldsmith, brazier, carpenter, sculptor) appear as an organised body.
- **Panduranga** (general of Gunaga Vijayaditya) **subdued the Boyas**, built Pandurangapuram on Pulicat lake and issued the Addanki verse inscription.
# Guilds and trade
- **Penugonda (West Godavari)** = **headquarters of the merchant guilds** of medieval Andhra and main seat of goddess **Vasavi** (APPSC PYQ).
  - Bejawada was a trade centre but not the guild headquarters.
- **Kakumanu inscription** (Chalukya Bhima I) names a **Setti Shreshthi** (chief merchant).
| Fact | Ruler |
|---|---|
| Gold coins found in **Arakan (Burma)** | **Saktivarma I** (Chalukya Chandra) |
| Trade with the eastern islands | Rajaraja Narendra |
| **72 merchants** sent to China | **Kulottunga I** |

=== Education – Ghatikas
- **Ghatikas** = advanced **Sanskrit schools** attached to **agraharas or temples**.
- **Heads:** **Ghatika Samanyulu**, e.g. **Mandasharma** of the **Asanapura Ghatika**, Kammanadu.
- **Subjects:** Vedas, Vedangas, Dharmashastras, Tarka, Vyakarana, Arthashastra, **Dhanurveda**, **Ayurveda**.
- **⚠ Trap:** **Hallisaka** (a dance) was **not** a Ghatika subject.
- **Jain pupils** began their studies with **"Om Namah Siddham"**.
~~ Sources: NextGen Eastern Chalukyas p.10, p.30; app notes 1:79 (sub 5)
