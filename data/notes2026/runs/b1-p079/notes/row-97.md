| Temple | Deity | Point |
|---|---|---|
| **Srikurmam** (Srikakulam) | **Kurma** (Vishnu's 2nd avatara) | Only Kurma temple; **only AP temple with two flagpoles (dhwajastambhas)** |
| **Arasavalli** (Srikakulam) | **Surya** | The **Sun temple** of Andhra |
| Simhachalam | Varaha Lakshmi Narasimha | Present temple of Eastern Ganga **Narasimha Deva I**; Kalinga style |
| Mukhalingam | **Madhukeswara** | Eastern Ganga capital Kalinganagari |

| **Lepakshi** (Sri Sathya Sai) | Veerabhadra | **Hanging pillar**; Vijayanagara paintings |
- **Two flagpoles:** **Srikurmam** (G2 2001, 2002) – not Tirumala, Simhachalam or Yadagirigutta.
- **Srikurmam district:** **Srikakulam** (Sachivalayam PYQ; the stem's "10th avatara" is wrong – Kurma is the 2nd).
- **Sun temple:** **Arasavalli** (AEE 2008; IAS 2017 – only I; Amarkantak and Omkareshwar are not Sun temples).
- **Maha Sivaratri in grand manner:** **Kotappakonda**.
- **Jyotirlingas:** **12** (CGPSC 2011, 2012); AP's = **Srisailam** (Mallikarjuna).

=== Temple Components and the Three Orders
- **Core:** **garbhagriha** + **mandapa**; **shikhara** (north) or **vimana** (south); axial **vahana with dhvaja pillar**.
- **Dwarapalakas:** door-guardian figures **left and right** of the entrance or gopuram (Endowment 2022) – dikpalakas guard the eight directions, kshetrapalakas the site.
- **Trikutalaya:** three shrines on one plan (Thousand Pillar temple).
| Order | Marker | Andhra example |
|---|---|---|
| Nagara | Curvilinear shikhara | Kalinga variant: **Mukhalingam**, Simhachalam |
| Dravida | Pyramidal vimana, gopurams | Eastern Chalukya delta temples; Vijayanagara |
| **Vesara** | Blend | **Kakatiya** temples |
- **Pattadakal:** **Papanatha** = Nagara; Virupaksha = Dravida.
- **Siddhavatam (Kadapa):** western gopuram in a **1233 CE** Tamil-Grantha inscription of **Chola Rajendra III**.

=== Styles by Dynasty
| Dynasty | Features | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Eastern Chalukya | Dravidian, from Pallava and Badami forms; **laterite and limestone** | **Vijayaditya II** – 108 temples; Biccavolu |
| Chalukya-Chola | Model for the Godavari region | **Draksharama** – **Chalukya Bhima I** |
| Kakatiya | Vesara; star platforms, sandbox, floating bricks – not wooden superstructures | Ramappa (1213), Thousand Pillar (Shiva, Vishnu, Surya – not Brahma) |
| Reddi | Renovations; forts | **Kondaveedu** (Guntur–Palnadu, near Chilakaluripet) |
| Vijayanagara | Chalukya and Chola roots (not Hoysala/Kalinga); **Sahasra Stambha (thousand-pillared kalyana) mandapa**, gopurams | Hampi; Lepakshi |
- **Eastern Chalukya ✗ trap:** "pure Vesara on Hoysala models" is wrong.

=== Materials, Sculpture and Painting
| Material | Dynasty | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Sandstone hill | **Vishnukundinas** | **Undavalli** caves |
| **Granite** monolith | Vishnukundinas | Reclining Ananta Padmanabha, Undavalli |
| Black basalt (ekasila) | Kakatiyas | Thousand Pillar Nandi |
| Floating bricks | Kakatiyas | Ramappa vimana |
- **Sandbox technology:** foundation pits of sand, stone chips and earth cushion temples (Ramappa) against earthquakes.
- **Ramappa madanikas** follow **Nritta Ratnavali** of **Jayapa Senani** (commander of **Ganapatideva**); Ramappa = UNESCO **2021**, built **1213**.
- **Perini** revived by **Nataraja Ramakrishna**.
- **Kakatiya painting (Machaladevi)** led to the **Lepakshi** style.
