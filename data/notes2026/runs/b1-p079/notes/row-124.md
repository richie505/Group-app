=== Folk Entertainment under Vijayanagara – Yakshaganam and Tolu Bommalata
| Form | Audience under Vijayanagara |
|---|---|
| **Yakshaganam** | Originated in the **royal courts**; entertained the court and elites |
| **Tolu Bommalata (leather shadow puppets)** | The chief entertainment of **rural Andhra** besides Yakshagana (APPSC G2 2019 ×2) – not Harikatha, dramas or Burrakatha |
| Vipra-Vinodins | Brahmin jugglers for Brahmin households |
- **Yakshaganam** was performed by the **Jakkula (Jakkula Varu)** community; **Jakkula Purandhri** is a character of the **Yakshaganam** (APPSC G1 2004) – not Perini, Oggu Katha or puppetry.
- **Koravanji** = precursor of the Telugu Yakshagana; **Sugreeva Vijayam** by **Kandukuri Rudrakavi** = first (published) Telugu Yakshaganam; performed in Telugu with rich costumes and Puranic stories.
- **Veedhi Natakam / Veedhi Bhagavatam** (street dance-drama) → forerunner of **Kuchipudi**.
- Under the **Qutb Shahis** Yakshaganam became folk theatre and Kuchipudi flourished; folk forms later carried Telugu consciousness to the villages in the **Andhra Movement**.
~~ Sources: Singhania Art & Culture 03 p.84 (Yakshagana – Vijayanagar courts, Jakkula Varu), p.100 (Tholu Bommalata); NextGen previous papers; app notes 1:124 (sub 0, 1), 1:123 (sub 9)

=== Burrakatha
- **Earlier name: Jangam Katha** (APPSC JL; Singhania) – not Kuchipudi, Bhamakalapam or Veeranatyam; in Rayalaseema called **Thandana Katha**.
- Folk narrative art of **Andhra Pradesh** (and Telangana); birthplace **Guntur**.
- Troupe of **three**: the main narrator (with the **burra / tambura**) and **two co-performers (Vantalu)**; instruments Gummata, Burra, Tambura, Ukki; chorus "Thandana Taana".
- **Made popular by Shaik Nazar** (APPSC ×2) = **Burrakatha Pitamaha** – born 5 Feb 1920, **Pannekallu, Guntur**; **Padma Shri 1986**; works Palnati Yuddham, **Bengal Karuvu** – not Mikkilineni or Mastan Saheb.
- Popularisers: Nazar, **Nittala Brothers**; used for news, reform and nationalist messages; **Praja Natya Mandali** used it for socialist ideals (**Mabhoomi** – Sunkara Satyanarayana and Vasireddy Bhaskara Rao); Sunkara's Burrakathas Kashtajeevi, Raithu Vijayam, Alluri Sitaramaraju.
~~ Sources: Singhania Art & Culture 03 p.58-59 ("Burrakatha or Jangam Katha ... from Andhra Pradesh"); PMF Art & Culture 02 p.102 (one leading performer and two co-performers); app notes 1:124 (sub 3, 7)

=== Harikatha
- **Father of Harikatha (Harikatha Pitamaha):** **Adibhatla Narayana Dasu** (1864-1945) (APPSC G2 2012 ×2) – not Kanakaiah Naidu, Shankaramanchi Ramalingaiah or Ballari Raghava.
- Born at **Ajjada**, Vizianagaram district; autobiography **Na Yeruka**; Harikatha is deeply rooted in **Vizianagaram**.
- A **single artist** sings, acts and dances (about 3 hours) with cymbals (**Chidatalu**) – Burrakatha is a trio.
~~ Sources: PMF Art & Culture 02 p.102 (Harikatha – story of Hari); app notes 1:124 (sub 2)

=== Kuchipudi – Andhra's Classical Dance
- **Kuchipudi** = the classical dance of **Andhra Pradesh** (APPSC ×3) – Kathakali and **Mohiniattam** are Kerala's, Kathak North India's, Bharatanatyam Tamil Nadu's, Odissi Odisha's.
- Named after **Kuchipudi village, Krishna district**; founded by **Siddhendra Yogi**; grew from the **Bhagavata Mela** of hereditary Brahmin families; village gifted by **Abul Hasan Tana Shah**.
- **Statements (APPSC FRO):** Kuchipudi from Krishna district ✓; Warangal Fort – **Kakatiyas** ✓; Telugu – classical Dravidian language ✓; "**Amaravati stupa – a centre of Jainism**" ✗ (it was **Buddhist**) → **a, c and d only**.
~~ Sources: PMF Art & Culture 02 p.73; NextGen AP History (Amaravati – Buddhist stupa); app notes 1:124 (sub 6)

=== Tolu Bommalata – the Craft, Nimmalakunta and Surabhi
- Thin, **translucent leather** figures **painted on both sides**, moved behind a **white screen** lit by a lamp; **heads of the puppets move** (unique feature); Ramayana, Mahabharata, Puranas.
- **Nimmalakunta** (Sri Sathya Sai / Anantapur) – leather puppetry **GI tag 2008**; **Dalavai Chalapathi Rao** – Padma Shri 2020.
- Mentioned in **Panditaradhya Charitra** (Palkuriki Somanatha), **Palnati Veeracharitra** (Srinatha), Bhaskara Satakam.
- **Surabhi theatre:** **Vanarasa Govinda Rao**, from a **leather-puppet family**, replaced puppets with live actors (**Keechaka Vadham**) at **Sorugu** near Jammalamadugu (Kadapa).
~~ Sources: Singhania Art & Culture 03 p.100; app notes 1:124 (sub 4)

=== Oggu Katha and Community Kathas
| Form | Community | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Oggu Katha** | **Golla / Kuruma (shepherds)** | Oggu drum; Telangana ballads |
| Asadi Katha | Asadi (Madiga sub-caste) | Story of **Ellamma** |
| Bhagavatulu | Mostly **Yadavas**, Rayalaseema | Dance round a pillar with Sita-Rama-Lakshmana idols |
| Pichukuntla Katha | Troupe of 3 | Sings Srinatha's **Palnati Veeracharitra** over 15 nights |
| Chindu Bhagavatam | Chindu (**Madiga** sub-group) | Dance-drama |
| **Veeranatyam** | **Veeramushti** | Dance of **Veerabhadra** in Shiva temples |
