=== Village Autonomy – the Chola Hallmark
- **Special feature of Chola polity:** **autonomy of village administration** / autonomous village panchayats (UPPSC 1995; CDS 2000) – not mandalam divisions, monarchy, ministers or tax methods.
- **Famous for:** **village assemblies** (RAS 1993); **excellent village administration** (UPPSC 1991); **elaborate local self-government** (State PSC) – the **Cholas**, not the Chalukyas, Cheras, Solankis or Parmars.
- **Early Panchayati Raj:** "Chola village administration was an early example of the Panchayati Raj system" – autonomous elected village bodies evolved by the **Dravidians (Cholas)** (RAS 1997).
- **Real powers:** the sabha, ur and nagaram were **elected bodies with real powers** over revenue, irrigation, temples and justice – not symbolic councils.
- **Nature of the gram sabhas (Degree Lecturers 2011):** fully **autonomous**, **helped the public** (tanks, roads, justice) and acted as **bankers and trustees** (raised loans, held endowments) → **all of the above**.
- **Historians:** K.A. **Nilakanta Sastri** – a remarkable "grassroots democracy"; **Burton Stein** – a **segmentary state**.
| Dynasty | Local government |
|---|---|
| **Cholas** | Culmination: elected assemblies, committees, lottery |
| Pallavas | Sabha and ur already appear – the base the Cholas built on |
| **Badami Chalukyas** | **Highly centralised** |
| Rashtrakutas | Village assemblies active in disputes |

=== Uttaramerur Inscriptions
- **What:** two inscriptions on the temple walls at **Uttaramerur**, a **brahmadeya** village in **Kanchipuram district**, Tamil Nadu.
- **Date and king:** **919 and 921 CE** (c. 920), reign of **Parantaka I** – not Rajaraja I.
- **Source of Chola local government:** Uttaramerur (APPSC G2 2000, 2003; APPSC PYQ ×3; IAS 1993; APPSC G1) – not Kudumiyanmalai, Gangaikondacholapuram, Kanchipuram, Thanjavur, Uraiyur, Silappadikaram, Tolkappiyam or Periya Puranam.
- **Contents:** constitution of the sabha, **qualifications**, **disqualifications**, mode of election (**kudavolai**), committees, functions and the **right to recall**.
- **⚠ Trap:** the **Thanjavur** inscription of Rajaraja I records military feats (Chera navy at Kandalur), not village government.
# Qualifications and disqualifications
| Qualifications | Disqualifications |
|---|---|
| Own at least **1/4 veli** of tax-paying land | **Failed to submit accounts** |
| Own a **house (residence)** on own site | Committed sins / crimes, alcoholism |
| Age **35–70** | Debt defaulters; close relatives of members |
| Knowledge of the **Vedas** or administration | Served on a committee in the **past three years** |
- **Aug 2025:** the PM cited the Uttaramerur **"ballot pot"** as proof of India's early democratic traditions.

=== Kudavolai, Terms and Variyams
# Kudavolai (pot-ballot)
- 1. Village divided into **30 wards (kudumbus)**; 2. names of eligible persons written on **palm leaves**; 3. put in a **pot (kudam)**; 4. a **young boy** drew the lots in public.
- **Term:** **360 days** (one year); removed at once for an offence; a person who served in the last three years was barred.
- **⚠ Sources differ:** NextGen says committee members "served three-year terms" – the inscription fixes a **one-year** term and a **three-year bar** on re-selection (PMF).

=== Assemblies – Ur, Sabha, Nagaram, Nattar
| Assembly | Where | Members |
|---|---|---|
| **Ur** | Non-brahmadeya (**vellanvagai**) villages | Landholders (urar); land sale, gifts, tax exemptions |
| **Sabha / Mahasabha** | **Brahmadeya** (agrahara) villages | **Brahmins** with property, family, learning (Vedic) and good conduct |
| **Nagaram** | **Merchant (market) towns** | Merchants (nagarattar) |
| **Nattar** | The **nadu** (group of villages) | Leading **vellala** landholders |
- **Nadu** = a group of villages; the **nattar** handled **revenue collection and disputes above the village** (a Chola body, not created by the 73rd Amendment).
- **Powers:** absolute proprietor of village lands; collected royal dues in **cash, kind or both**; levied taxes; **raised loans**; **auctioned defaulters' land**.
- **Temples:** Rajaraja I directed the **Cholamandalam sabhas** to serve the **Brihadeshvara** temple.
- **AP parallel:** village **mahajana** assemblies in **Kakatiya and Velanati** inscriptions – a weaker parallel.
~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval India p.407-408; NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams 3 p.204; app notes 1:86 (sub 1, 2, 7)

=== Chola Assembly vs 73rd Amendment
| Point | Chola village assembly | 73rd Amendment |
|---|---|---|
| Selection | Lottery among the qualified | Direct election |
| Eligibility | Property, age 35–70, Vedic learning | Adult franchise |
| Term | 360 days | **5 years (Art. 243E)** |
| Women | **No quota** (women, labourers, landless excluded) | At least **1/3 seats (Art. 243D)** |
- **Gram Sabha:** **Art. 243A**; the intermediate tier may be omitted in a State with population **not above 20 lakh** (Art. 243B).
