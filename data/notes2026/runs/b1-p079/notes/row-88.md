=== Merchant Guilds – Ayyavole, Manigramam, Nanadesi
| Body | Base / activity |
|---|---|
| **Ayyavole-500** (Ainnurruvar) | Originated at **Aihole (Ayyavolu), Karnataka**; pan-regional and overseas |
| **Manigramam** | **Corporation of merchants** in long-distance and overseas trade (IAS 1997) |
| **Nanadesi** | "Of **many countries**" – itinerant guild in **foreign trade** (UPPSC 2018) |
| **Ubhaya Nanadesi** | Both domestic and foreign trade |
| **Nagaram** (Nakaram) | Local merchant assembly of a market town |
| **Anjuvannam** | Guild of **West Asian foreign merchants** |
- **Guilds** (shrenis, pugas) had their own code (**bananju-dharma**), symbols and militias, and acted as bankers.
- **Independence:** as Chola power declined the guilds became **more independent**; they were not confined to local trade.
- **⚠ Distractors:** Chaturvedimangalam (Brahmin village), Parishad, Ashtadiggaja (Krishnadevaraya's poets), Sreni (general guild).
- **NDA 2018:** "Ayyavole originated at Aihole" ✓; "Manigramam was subordinated to the Anjuvannam in the 13th century" ✗ → **only I**.
# AP angle
- **Penugonda** (West Godavari) = central trade centre of medieval Andhra (**APPSC G2 2012**); Ayyavole and Manigramam had **18 branches** across Vengi.
- Eastern Chalukya **Nakaras** were headed by **Shreshthis**; **Tagara** = trade centre between Kalyan and Vengi.

=== Temple Economy and the Chudamani Vihara
- **Donations to temples:** **land, money and livestock** (cows, sheep for lamps), guild gifts and royal grants of paddy – **slaves** were not a donation category (CDS 2008).
- **Chudamani Vihara, Nagapattinam:** Buddhist vihara built (**1006**) by the Srivijaya king **Sri Mara Vijayottunga Varman**; Rajaraja I granted it Anaimangalam village.
~~ Sources: NCERT Class VII Our Pasts-II ch.2; app notes 1:88 (sub 5)

=== Overseas Trade – China, Srivijaya, Ports
- **China:** Chola embassies reached the **Song** court in **1015** and **1033**; **Kulottunga I** sent **72 merchants** in **1077**.
- **Srivijaya:** friendly at first (Chudamani Vihara) → hostile under **Rajendra I** over the China route (1025).
- **Guild records abroad:** **Barus (Lobu Tua)**, Sumatra – Ayyavole-500 Tamil inscription, **1088**; **Takua Pa**, Thailand – **Manigramam**; **Pagan**, Myanmar – Nanadesi Vishnu temple.
- **Significance:** guild networks carried Chola trade and Dravidian culture across the Bay of Bengal.
| Port | Note |
|---|---|
| **Nagapattinam** | Main Chola port; **Chudamani Vihara** |
| **Kaveripattinam (Puhar)** | Sangam Chola port at the **Kaveri** mouth |
| **Quilon (Kollam)** | **Kerala** port; Arab and Chinese trade |
- **⚠ Trap:** **Kaveripattinam** was the early (Sangam) port-capital; **Nagapattinam** was the port of the later Cholas, not an early capital.
- **West coast:** Arab merchants traded **horses and spices**; the **Anjuvannam** represented foreign merchants.
- **Terms:** **Nagarams** = market centres; **Erivirappattanas** = protected mercantile towns; **Kasu** = gold coin.
| Exports | Imports |
|---|---|
| Textiles, **pepper**, spices, precious stones | **Horses**, Chinese silk, porcelain, camphor |
