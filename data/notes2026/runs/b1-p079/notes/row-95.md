=== Shaiva Sects, Veerashaivism and the Aradhyas
# Shaiva sects
| Sect | Founder / trait |
|---|---|
| **Pasupata** | **Oldest** Shaiva sect; founded by **Lakulisa** (Nakulisa), 1st–2nd c. AD; popular into the Reddi period, declined under Vijayanagara |
| **Kalamukha** | Founded by **Kalasana** (Kalanana), disciple of Lakulisa; extremist; **temple managers**, gave **anna dana**; Simha Parishads |
| **Kapalika** | **Offshoot of Pasupata** (the "Vamacharyas" of the Kalamukhas); worship **Bhairava**; skull-bowl, Panchamudras |
| **Aradhya** | **Brahmin Shaivas** led by **Mallikarjuna Panditaradhya**; kept Vedic ritual and varna |
- **Pasupata founder:** **Lakulisa** (APPSC PYQ) – not Basava (Veerashaivism), Bijjala (Kalachuri king) or Kallata.
- **Kapalikas emerged from:** **Pasupata Shaivism** (APPSC G2 2019; APPSC PYQ).
  - ⚠ Sources differ: NextGen's PYQ list says "the Kapalikas emerged from the school of **Misra Saivam**" – the app keys Pasupata (most books) and shows the dispute.
- **Kakatiya patronage:** early kings (Beta, Durga) were disciples of **Kalamukha** acharyas of the Srisailam Silamatha; Kalamukha centres – **Srisailam, Alampur, Vemulawada, Kaleshwaram**, Tripurantakam.
# Aradhya acharyas – Siddhatraya
- **12 Aradhya acharyas** in **four triads**; **Siddhatraya** = **Revana, Marula, Ekorama** (G2 2012; APPSC PYQ).
- **Not a Siddhacharya:** **Udbhata** – a Sanskrit poetician (alankarika).
# Veerashaivism
- **Founder:** **Basaveshwara** (Basava), minister of **Kalachuri Bijjala** (Karnataka); spread into Andhra in the Kakatiya period through **Palkuriki Somanatha** (Basavapuranam, Panditaradhya Charitra).
- **Followers:** **Lingayats**; gurus **Jangamas**; in Andhra inscriptions called **Veeravratulu**; main Andhra centre **Srisailam**.
- **Teaching:** **rejected** the Vedas, caste, child marriage and sacrifice → drew artisans and lower groups (social mobility).
- **Reddi period:** the **popular religion was Veerashaivism** (G2 2011), alongside Pasupata patronised by the kings.
> Kakatiya age = the **"Shaiva era"**; dynastic deity **Svayambhu Shiva**.

=== Caste Associations (Samayas) and Non-Brahmin Rule
# Samayas
- **Samaya / Samayamu** = **caste association** (G2 PYQ; APPSC PYQ ×2) – not Sreni-bandham (guild), Sangha or Kutamu.
- **Bodies:** Brahmins – **Mahajanulu**; Vaishyas (Komatis) – **Nakaramu**; violators fined (**Samayadandam**), enforced by the **Samaya Senapati**.
| Group | Body / castes |
|---|---|
| Brahmins | **Mahajanas** (Vaidikas vs Niyogis) |
| Vaishyas | **Nakaram** |
| **Panchanamuvaru** | The **five artisan** (smith) groups |
| **Ashtadasha kulas** | **18 occupational groups** inside Warangal fort (Kridabhiramam) |
| Veerashaivism | New castes **Lingayats, Balijas, Jangamas** |
| Vaishnavism | **Satanis, Dasaris** |
# Non-Brahmanisation
- **Culmination:** the non-Brahmanisation of religion and politics **culminated under the Kakatiyas** (G2 PYQ; APPSC PYQ) – not the Vengi Chalukyas, Pallavas or Rashtrakutas.
- **Why:** the Kakatiyas were **Sudras** (of **Pulinda/Boya** origin) who rose through military service; officials came from warrior and peasant groups (Nayankara); **Brahmins lost political clout**.

=== From Jainism to Shaivism
- **Early Kakatiyas:** **Jains** (G2 2011) → **Shaiva** from **Prola II / Rudradeva**.
- **Decline of Jainism:** **Kalamukha and Veerashaiva hostility**; Jain–Saiva conflicts from Rudradeva's reign.
- **Jain centres:** **Bodhan, Vemulawada, Patancheru, Kolanupaka**, Hanumakonda (Anumakonda).
- **Padmakshi temple, Hanumakonda:** built by **Prola II**, **originally a Jain shrine**.
- **Kadalalaya Jain basadi, Anumakonda:** built by **Mailama, wife of minister Bethana (Chetana)** – a minister's family, not the king.
- **Tolerance:** Vaishnava shrines (Thousand Pillar Vasudeva, Prasanna Keshava) were also endowed.
~~ Sources: NextGen Kakatiyas p.29, p.37, p.43; NextGen AP History - APPSC 11 p.11; app notes 1:95 (sub 2)

=== Temples as Institutions – the Malkapuram Inscription
- **Temples:** **landed corporations** with lands and staff; attached **monasteries and feeding houses**; courtesans (**Kalavatis**) sustained dance and music.
- **Malkapuram inscription (1261, Rudramadevi's reign):** **Visvesvara Sambhu** of the **Golaki matha** (Kalamukha-**Pasupata** Saiva lineage), guru of Ganapatideva, set up a Saiva matha, a **college**, a **feeding house**, a **maternity home (prasuti sala)** and a hospital at **Mandadam**.
- **Pasupata Saiva sect record:** the **Malkapuram** inscription (Junior Asst. PYQ) – not Bayyaram (Mailamba's tank, genealogy), Hanumakonda or Kazipet Dargah (Beta's Betheswara temple).
~~ Sources: NextGen Kakatiyas p.3, p.71; Epigraphia Indica (Malkapuram pillar inscription); app notes 1:95 (sub 5)
