## SUB 0 | Motupalli Port and the Abhaya Sasanam
[0:0] - **Chief port:** **Motupalli** – the main Kakatiya seaport for external trade (APPSC G2 2000, 2011, 2012) – not Warangal (inland capital), Krishnapatnam, Pulicat, Nellore, Machilipatnam, Ghantasala, Dharanikota or Korangi.
[0:1] - **Other name:** **Desiyakonda (Deseeyakkonda / Desyakkonda) Pattanam** (APPSC G2 2019 ×2; G2 2012; APPSC PYQ) – not Nidadavolu, Vadarevu, Motukur, Mudigonda, Mukalapura or Malakapuram.
[0:2] # Abhaya Sasanam (Motupalli Charter)
[0:3] - **Issuer:** **Ganapatideva**, **1244** CE, a pillar inscription at Motupalli (APPSC G2 2019; Group-II-A 1984; Welfare Officers 2017 ×2; APPSC PYQ ×3) – not Rudramba, Ambadeva or Prataparudra.
[0:4] - **Content:** **protection and shelter to foreign (sea) merchants**; unfair taxes cancelled, only fixed duties; **compensation for shipwreck or theft**; no harassment by officials.
[0:5] - **Official posted:** **Siddayya Devudu**.
[0:6] - **Most important commercial document** of the period.
[0:7] - **Goods at Motupalli:** **sandal, camphor, rose-water, ivory**, pearls, corals, copper, silk, pepper.
[0:8] - **Marco Polo** visited Motupalli in **Rudramadevi's** reign.

[0:10] - **Kara Pattanam** = ports collecting import and export duties.

## SUB 1 | Revenue, Village Officers and Coins
[1:0] # Land revenue
[1:1] - **Ari (Aari):** the Kakatiya **land tax / land revenue** (Juvenile Welfare 2011; APPSC PYQ) – generally **one-sixth** of the produce; taxpayers = **Arigapulu**; collectors = **Kolakandru**.
[1:2] - **Land classes:** **Magani** = irrigated wet land (food crops); **Metta** = rain-fed dry land.
[1:3] - **Tax-free:** temple lands, Brahmin grants, Ayagar lands, **Dashabandhamu** (land for tank upkeep).
[1:4] TABLE head: Tax | Meaning
  [1:4.0] Koru | Cess on wet land
  [1:4.1] Putti Wahindi | Cess on dry land
  [1:4.2] Pullari | Tax on grass (grazing)
  [1:4.3] Dari Sunkam | Road toll
  [1:4.4] Angadi Sunkam | Weekly-market tax
  [1:4.5] Kanika | Customary gift to the king or officials
  [1:4.6] Pellipannu | Wedding tax
[1:5] - **Not Kakatiya:** **Meruka** = Reddi wet-land tax; **Moturpha** = British tax on trades and looms; Vijayanagara **Krishnadevaraya** withdrew the marriage tax.
[1:6] # Village officers
[1:7] - **Ayagars:** **twelve (12)** village servants (APPSC PYQ) – Karnam, Reddi, Talari, purohita, smiths; paid by **shares of produce and service lands**, held land **without any cess or tax**.
[1:8] - **Karanam** = village accountant; **Reddi** = headman; **Talari** = village watchman.
[1:9] # Coins

  [1:10.2] Ruka | Silver

## SUB 2 | Guilds, Samayas and Trade Routes
[2:0] # Samayas and guilds
[2:1] - **Samaya (Samayamu):** a **caste organisation** / caste-occupational association – Mahajanulu (Brahmins), Nakaramu (Vaishyas); each elected a head; meeting in temple mandapams.
[2:2] - **Chitrameli:** the samaya (shreni) of **agricultural farmers** (APPSC G2 Mains; APPSC PYQ) – not goldsmiths, painters or dancers.
[2:3] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [2:3.0] Nakaram | Domestic merchants
  [2:3.1] Nanadesi | Foreign (inter-regional) merchants
  [2:3.2] Ubhaya Nanadesi | Merchants of both the Desis, Paradesis and Nanadesis
  [2:3.3] Ayyavole 500 | Guild named after Aihole
  [2:3.4] Virabanajam | Licensed for Pakanadu, Vengi and Hanumakonda (Tripurantakam inscription, 1282)
[2:4] - **Guild head:** **Setti / Sreshthi**; guilds had seals, emblems and militia and acted as bankers – but **no royal monopoly** over trade.
[2:5] # Routes (APPSC G1 2023 ×2)
[2:6] - **Kridabhiramamu routes:** **Motupalli–Tripurantakam–Ballari** and **Palnadu–Nalgonda–Nekarikallu–Orugallu** → answer **III and IV**; **Orugallu** was the inland trade hub.
[2:7] - **Tripurantakam** linked Motupalli and the salt towns to **Ballari** via Kurnool.
[2:8] - **Ketana:** chief inland route Kanchi → Nellore → Warangal → **Ayodhya**.
[2:9] - Kakatiyas controlled routes between the **Deccan interior and the eastern coast**.
[2:10] - **Kridabhiramamu:** Telugu play by **Vinukonda Vallabharaya** describing life in **Orugallu (Warangal)**.
[2:11] - **Imports:** **Arabian war horses**, pearls, silk, perfumes; **exports:** fine textiles, spices, diamonds, gold.

## SUB 3 | Nayankara System and Administration
[3:0] # Nayankara system
[3:1] - **Introduced by:** **Prataparudra II** (Asst. Welfare Officers PYQ; APPSC 2025), who organised the kingdom into **77 Nayankaras** – ⚠ one book key and a NextGen Level-II line say Rudrama.
[3:2] - **How:** military commanders (**Nayakas**) were made administrators of **Sthalas / Mandalams** and given charge of **forts** – the Kakatiya empire was ruled through vassals called **Nayakas** (2002 paper).
[3:3] - **Land instead of salary:** villages called **Nayakasthalam**; Nayankaras held about **one-fourth** of the land; no monthly cash salary.
[3:4] - **Duties:** collect revenue → keep a fixed army for the king → pay a fixed annual sum.
[3:5] - **Sources:** **Ekamranatha's Pratapa Charitra**; Baddena's Niti Sastra Muktavali.
[3:6] - **Successor systems:** Vijayanagara **Amaranayaka** (land = **Amaram**) → **Poligars**.
[3:7] # Administration
[3:8] - **Nature of the state:** most scholars – **decentralised monarchy**; **Romila Thapar** – "metropolitan state"; **Burton Stein** – "segmentary state"; **Cynthia Talbot** – "Golden Age of the Shudras".
[3:9] - **Prime ministers:** **Malyala Hemadri Reddi** (Ganapatideva); **Muppidi Nayudu** (Prataparudra II).
[3:10] - **Units:** **Rashtra / Mandala** (largest) → Nadu → Sthala → Grama.
[3:11] - **72 Niyogas** (departments) under the **Bahattara Niyogadhipati**.
[3:12] - **Tirthulu / Ashtadasa Tirthas:** the **18 high officers (ministers) of state** (Sakala Neeti Sammatamu of **Madiki Singana**) – ministers, not water sources, temples or spies (APPSC PYQ).
[3:13] TABLE head: Officer | Role
  [3:13.0] Pradvivaka | Judicial adviser
  [3:13.1] Kayasthas | Scribes
  [3:13.2] Sunkadhikari | Collector of tolls and customs
[3:14] # Army
[3:15] - **Mula Balam** = king's own standing army; **Gaja Sahini** (elephants) = **Jayapa Senani** under Ganapatideva; **Ashwa Sahini** (cavalry) = **Gangaya Sahini**.
[3:16] - **Giridurga** = hill fort (also Jala, Vana, Sthala durgas); **Padmanayakas** = Recherla Velama chiefs, key commanders.
[3:17] - Warangal fort: inner stone wall with **45 bastions**.
