## SUB 0 | Prataparudra Yasobhushanam and Kakatiya Court Literature
[0:0] - **Prataparudra Yasobhushanam:** Sanskrit work on **alankara (poetics)** praising **Prataparudra II**, by his court poet **Vidyanatha** (APPSC G2 2000, 2011; Police Constable 2016; GS common paper; APPSC PYQ ×5) – not Prataparudra I or II themselves, Jayapa, Ambadeva, Sarangadhara, Rudradeva, Tikkana, Ketana, Baddena or Palkuriki Somanatha.
[0:1] - **Vidyanatha = Agastya** (G2 2012): historians identify the two; Agastya also wrote **Nalakirtikaumudi**, **Balabharata** and **Krishnacharita**.
[0:2] - **"What Bana was to Harsha, Vidyanatha was to Prataparudradeva."**
[0:3] - **Gangadevi**, author of **Madhura Vijayam** (Vijayanagara), hailed **Agastya** as her guru; Madhura Vijayam also describes the hardship after the conquest.
[0:4] # Other works of the age
[0:5] TABLE head: Work | Author
  [0:5.0] Nitisara (Neetisastram, Sanskrit polity) | Rudradeva (Kakati Rudra) (DAO 2011)
  [0:5.1] Sakala Niti Sammatamu (polity anthology) | Madiki Singana (APSLPRB 2023)
  [0:5.2] Niti Sara Muktavali, Sumati Satakam | Baddena
  [0:5.3] Nrittaratnavali (dance) | Jayapa Senani, Gajasahini of Ganapatideva
  [0:5.4] Prataparudra Charitramu | Ekamranatha – Kakatiya traditions (Jaitugi–Ganapati marriage), Rudrama's Yadava victory
  [0:5.5] Khazain-ul-Futuh | Amir Khusrau – Kafur's 1310 siege; Warangal fort double-walled with a moat; calls Prataparudra Ravaddar Dev
  [0:5.6] Tarikh-i-Firoz Shahi | Barani
[0:6] - **Sakalya Malla** was another court poet of Prataparudra II.

## SUB 1 | Rudrama Devi (1262–1289)
[1:0] # Family and accession
[1:1] - **Daughter of Ganapatideva** and **Kupama Devi** (APPSC PYQ); Ganapati had **no son**, so she was raised as a son (**putrika**).
[1:2] - **Kakatiya** queen (Uttarakhand PCS 2021) – not Eastern Ganga, Hoysala or Western Chalukya.
[1:3] - **Co-regent from 1258** under the male name **Rudradeva Maharaja**; ruled to **1289**.
[1:4] - **First woman ruler of the Andhra region** (Rudramba; APPSC PYQ); **second great woman ruler of medieval India after Razia Sultana**.
[1:5] - **4th sovereign Kakatiya** (Rudradeva → Mahadeva → Ganapatideva → Rudrama).
[1:6] - **Husband:** Chalukya prince **Veerabhadra** (Nidadavolu).
[1:7] TABLE head: Daughter | Married / son
  [1:7.0] Mummadamma | Mahadeva; mother of Prataparudra II
  [1:7.1] Rudramma | Yadava prince Ellanadeva
  [1:7.2] Ruyyamma | Induluri Annaya
[1:8] - **Children:** **three daughters**, no son (APPSC Drug Inspector 2011).
[1:9] # Wars and title
[1:10] - **Statements (UPRO 2023):** "4th independent ruler" ✓ and "defeated the Pandyas, Eastern Gangas of Odisha and Seunas of Devagiri" ✓ → **both**.
[1:11] - **Rayagajakesari:** title taken for her victory over the **rulers of Devagiri** (Yadava **Mahadeva**, chased back to Devagiri) (APPSC G2 PYQ ×3) – not the Pandyas, Kalinga rulers or Vaidumbas.
[1:12] - **Internal revolt:** half-brothers **Harihara and Murari**, crushed with Recherla Prasaditya and others.
[1:13] - **Vengi:** occupied by Ganga **Narasimhadeva** (1262); regained **1278** when **Poti Nayaka and Prolinayaka** beat Virabhanudeva.
[1:14] - **Death, 1289:** killed fighting the Kayastha rebel **Ambadeva** (made head of Kadapa in 1272) with general **Mallikarjuna Nayaka**.
[1:15] - **Chandupatla inscription** (**Nalgonda**), set up by her servant **Puvvula Mummadi**, records her death (APPSC Asst. Welfare Officers) – not Tripurantakam (Ambadeva's victories), Gurjala or Induluru.
[1:16] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP History - Kakatiyas p.15-17, p.51-53; NextGen previous papers p.14; app notes 1:93 (sub 0, 1, 2)
## SUB 2 | Marco Polo's Account
[2:0] - **Who:** **Marco Polo**, the **Venetian (Italian)** traveller; work **Il Milione / The Travels**.
[2:1] - **When:** visited in **Rudramadevi's** reign (DAO 2011; G2 2011 ×2; Group-II-A 1984) – reached the Coromandel coast in **1292**, towards the end of her reign.
[2:2] - **Where:** landed at the Kakatiya port **Motupalli ("Mutfili")** (Group-I 1990-91; G1 1994; APPSC PYQ) – not Ibn Battuta, Abdur Razzaq, Nicolo Conti, Domingo Paes, Jordanus, Hiuen Tsang or Columbus.
[2:3] TABLE head: Theme | Observation
  [2:3.0] Ruler | Praised the queen's strict and just rule and the country's prosperity
  [2:3.1] Trade | Flourishing sea trade; port duties at Motupalli; merchants not heavily taxed
  [2:3.2] Wealth | Diamond mines of the Golconda region (APPSC PYQ); fine textiles
  [2:3.3] Climate, food | Hot climate; rice the only local grain
[2:4] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP History - Kakatiyas p.15, p.25-27, p.53; app notes 1:93 (sub 3)
## SUB 3 | Fall of Warangal (1323) and the Captivity of Prataparudra
[3:0] - **Captor:** **Ulugh Khan** (Juna Khan, later **Muhammad bin Tughlaq**), under Sultan **Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq** (APPSC G2 2019; G2 PYQ; Group-II-A 1989) – not Malik Kafur, Alauddin Khalji or Malik Chajju.
[3:1] - **Year:** **1323** – the end of the Kakatiya kingdom (Group-II-B 1989); final siege about **five months**; **Bobbareddi's** withdrawal weakened the defence.
[3:2] - **Death:** Prataparudra **drowned himself in the Narmada** on the way to Delhi (**Vilasa** copper plate of Musunuri Prolaya Nayaka; one account says the Kaveri).
[3:3] - **Renaming:** Orugallu → **Sultanpur** (APPSC PYQ).
[3:4] - **After 1323:** Andhra under **Malik Burhanuddin**; **Malik Maqbul** (converted Brahmin Jagannatha Pandit) at Warangal; survivors **Prolaya Nayaka, Racherla Singama, Prolaya Vema** led the liberation → Musunuri and Reddi states.
[3:5] - **Causes:** southern disunity, weak cavalry, betrayal by chiefs, exhaustion from repeated raids – **not** a European sea blockade.
[3:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP History - Kakatiyas p.19, p.57-59; NextGen previous papers p.14, p.58; app notes 1:93 (sub 6)
## SUB 4 | Prataparudra II – Campaigns and the Delhi Invasions
[4:0] - **Prataparudra II (1289–1323):** grandson of Rudrama through **Mummadamma**; title **Kumara Rudradeva**; last Kakatiya.
[4:1] - **Campaigns:** commanders beat **Ambadeva** at **Tripurantakam** (1291); **Ranganatha** of Nellore (Pandya ally) replaced by **Manuma Gandapalu**; Gonavithala pushed to Devagiri.
[4:2] - **Nayankara:** kingdom divided into **77 Nayankaras** held by military officers (**Nayakas**); each held a **fort**, kept troops and paid a **fixed tribute**; about **one-fourth** of the land; continued as Vijayanagara **Amaranayakas** → later **Poligars**.
[4:3] TABLE head: No. | Year | Commander | Outcome
  [4:3.0] 1 | 1303 | Alauddin's Malik Fakhruddin Juna | Repulsed at Upparapalli by Pothuganti Aili and Recherla Venna – first attack on Orugallu
  [4:3.1] 2 | 1309–10 | Malik Kafur | Siege; tribute and Delhi overlordship
  [4:3.2] 3 | 1318 | Mubarak Khalji's Khusrau Khan | Tribute renewed
  [4:3.3] 4 | 1321–22 | Ulugh Khan | Rumour of the Sultan's death; army chased back
  [4:3.4] 5 | 1323 | Ulugh Khan | Warangal fell
[4:4] - **1311:** Kakatiya troops under **Muppidi Nayaka** joined Kafur's march on Madurai; **1316:** tribute stopped after Alauddin's death.
[4:5] - **⚠ Trap:** the 1323 conquest was by Ulugh Khan, not Khusrau Khan.
[4:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP History - Kakatiyas p.17-19, p.53-57; app notes 1:93 (sub 4, 5)
