## SUB 0 | Persian Chronicles and Their Authors
[0:0] - **Tawarikh** (singular **tarikh**): histories in **Persian**, the administrative language of the Sultans, written by secretaries, administrators, poets and courtiers.
[0:1] TABLE head: Work | Author | Subject / note
  [0:1.0] Taj-ul-Maasir | Hasan Nizami (UPPSC 1997) | First official chronicle of the Sultanate; patron Qutbuddin Aibak
  [0:1.1] Tabaqat-i-Nasiri | Minhaj-us-Siraj (State PSC) | Ghurids and early Sultans up to 1260; earliest account of Bakhtiyar Khalji's Bihar invasion (BPSC 2018); dedicated to Nasiruddin Mahmud
  [0:1.2] Khazain-ul-Futuh | Amir Khusrau | Alauddin's campaigns (Warangal 1310)
  [0:1.3] Futuh-us-Salatin | Abdul Malik Isami | Ghaznavids to Muhammad bin Tughlaq; written at the Bahmani court c. 1350
  [0:1.4] Tarikh-i-Firoz Shahi | Ziauddin Barani; another by Shams-i-Siraj Afif | Balban to early Firuz; main account of Alauddin's market rules
  [0:1.5] Fatawa-i-Jahandari | Ziauddin Barani (UKPSC 2024) | Political theory of kingship – advice to Muslim rulers
  [0:1.6] Futuhat-i-Firuz Shahi | Firuz Shah Tughlaq | The Sultan's own account and ordinances
  [0:1.7] Tarikh-i-Mubarak Shahi | Yahya bin Ahmad Sarhindi | Sayyid Sultan Mubarak Shah
  [0:1.8] Jawami-ul-Hikayat, Lubab-ul-Albab | Sadiduddin Muhammad Aufi | Earliest reference to the magnetic compass in Indian seas (JPSC 2016)
[0:2] - **Matches asked:** Futuh-us-Salatin – **Isami**; Futuhat-i-Firuz Shahi – **Firuz Shah**; Tarikh-i-Firuz Shahi – **Barani**; Khazain-ul-Futuh – **Khusrau** (UP RO/ARO 2023). Barani – Tarikh-i-Firuzshahi; Hasan Nizami – Taj-ul-Maasir; Minhaj – Tabaqat-i-Nasiri; Yahya – Tarikh-i-Mubarakshahi (UPPSC 1997).
[0:3] - **Periods covered, in order:** Taj-ul-Maasir → Tabaqat-i-Nasiri → Tarikh-i-Firoz Shahi → Tabaqat-i-Akbari.
[0:4] - **Tabaqat-i-Nasiri:** **23 volumes** (tabaqat); English translation by **H.G. Raverty**.
[0:5] TABLE head: Later work | Author / note
  [0:5.0] Tabaqat-i-Akbari | Nizamuddin Ahmad (not Badauni)
  [0:5.1] Muntakhab-ut-Tawarikh | Badauni
  [0:5.2] Fawaid-ul-Fawad | Amir Hasan Sijzi – conversations of Nizamuddin Auliya
  [0:5.3] Tarikh-i-Firishta (Gulshan-i-Ibrahimi) | Firishta; dedicated to Ibrahim Adil Shah of Bijapur; calls Telangana "Tilang"
  [0:5.4] Muntakhab-ul-Lubab | Khafi Khan – Aurangzeb's conquest of Golconda
  [0:5.5] Burhan-i-Maasir | Deccan history; Sadashivaraya's income 12 crore hons
[0:6] - **⚠ Not:** Kitab-ul-Hind ≠ Firdausi (Firdausi = Shahnama); Futuh-us-Salatin ≠ Khusrau.

## SUB 2 | Al-Biruni and Kitab-ul-Hind
[2:0] - **Came to India with Mahmud of Ghazni** (UKPSC 2010) – 11th century; native of **Khwarazm (Khiva)**, present **Uzbekistan**.
[2:1] - **Kitab-ul-Hind = Tahqiq-i-Hind** (Tahqiq ma li'l-Hind) – written in **Arabic**, c. **1030** (APPSC G2 2011).
[2:2] - **Learned Sanskrit**; studied Indian religion, philosophy, astronomy, caste – **first Muslim scholar to study the Puranas** (UP Lower 2002).
[2:3] - **Diwali** recorded on **Kartika Amavasya**.
[2:4] - Mahmud also patronised **Firdausi** (Shahnama) and **Utbi** (Kitab-ul-Yamini).
[2:5] ~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval 04 p.29; app notes 1:104 (sub 2); Britannica "al-Biruni"

## SUB 7 | Using the Sources – Bias and Cross-checking
[7:0] - **Foreign accounts supplement** Indian texts and must be **cross-verified** with inscriptions and archaeology.
[7:1] - **Court bias:** chroniclers wrote for rewards and glorified the patron; **Amir Khusrau** – court poet of Alauddin, went with **Malik Kafur's 1310** Warangal campaign.
[7:2] - **Barani:** orthodox **Sunni** view; theorised kingship.
[7:3] - Firishta's story of **Deva Raya kidnapping Nehal** – **rejected** by modern historians.
