## SUB 0 | Origins, Overlords and First Mentions
[0:0] # Overlords
[0:1] - **First overlords:** the **Rashtrakutas** (APPSC G2 2005 ×2; G2 Mains 2017 ×2) – the Magallu record calls Gundaya and Eriya "Rashtrakuta", and the early emblem was **Garuda**.
[0:2] - **Then:** feudatories of the **Kalyani (Western) Chalukyas** – emblem **Varaha** (changed by **Prola I**); title **Mahamandaleswara** (APPSC G2 2001), borne by Beta II.
[0:3] - **⚠ Trap:** the **Eastern Chalukyas** only issued the Magallu grant; they were never Kakatiya overlords.
[0:4] - **Phases:** samanta (feudatory) 956–1162 → **sovereign 1163–1323** – the Kakatiyas ruled Andhra in the **12th–13th centuries** (APPSC Social & Cultural History).
[0:5] - **UPSC 2023:** none of the Hoysalas (10th–11th c.), Gahadavalas (11th c.), Kakatiyas or Yadavas (12th c.) was founded in the early 8th century → **None**.
[0:6] # First mentions and progenitor
[0:7] TABLE head: Record | Fact
  [0:7.0] Magallu grant (c. 950–956) of Eastern Chalukya Danarnava | First mention of the Kakatiyas – Kakatiya Gundyana (Kakara Gundana) (G2 Mains 2017; APPSC PYQ)
  [0:7.1] Bayyaram tank inscription of Mailamba (Ganapati's sister) | Best genealogy; line begins with Venna (Venna Bhupati) (Group-II)
  [0:7.2] Kazipet (Dargah) inscription | Names Beta I as the first Kakatiya king (APPSC G1 2002; APPSC PYQ ×2); records the accession of Durga Nripathi
[0:8] - **Early base:** **Hanamkonda (Anumakonda) Vishaya**, received by Beta I through his aunt **Viriyala Kamasani**.
[0:9] # Religion and identity
[0:10] - **Jains first:** the Kakatiyas were initially **Jains** and later **Shaivas** – the clan whose first rulers were Jains (2002 paper).
[0:11] - **Garuda emblem** from Jain Tirthankara **Shantinatha**; clan deity **Kakatamma (Kakati Devi)** = the Jain **Kushmandini**; **"Kakati" = gourd**.
[0:12] - **Saiva gurus:** **Rameswara Pandita** (Mallikarjuna matha, **Srisailam**) → **Beta II**; **Dhruveshwara Pandita** → **Mahadeva**; **Visvesvara Sivacharya** (Golaki Matha) → **Ganapatideva** (raja guru, APPSC PYQ ×3).
[0:13] - **Unifiers of Andhra:** the Kakatiyas were the **second dynasty after the Satavahanas** to unite all Telugu lands; they "gave a comprehensive form to Andhraism" – the dynasty that gave the **ultimate design to Andhra culture** (DAO 2011).
[0:14] - **Loose PYQ:** "one of the major dynasties that ruled Andhra" – key **Satavahanas**, the first major Andhra dynasty (all four options ruled Andhra).
[0:15] - **Eastern Deccan:** the **Kakatiyas** ruled the eastern Deccan in the 12th–14th c.; the Seunas held the west.
[0:16] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP History - Kakatiyas p.35-37, p.41; PMF Ancient & Medieval India p.410; app notes 1:92 (sub 0, 9)
## SUB 1 | Capitals – Anumakonda to Orugallu (Warangal)
[1:0] # Who shifted the capital?
[1:1] TABLE head: Step | Ruler | Capital
  [1:1.0] 1 | Prola I | Made Anumakonda (Hanumakonda) the capital
  [1:1.1] 2 | Prola II | Developed Hanamkonda; Swayambhu temple in Orugallu
  [1:1.2] 3 | Rudradeva | Founded Orugallu and began the Warangal fort
  [1:1.3] 4 | Ganapatideva | Completed the fort and transferred the capital from Hanumakonda to Orugallu (Rocket: 1254); called it Andhranagari
  [1:1.4] 5 | After 1323 | Renamed Sultanpur
[1:2] - **Answer to "who shifted the capital?":** **Ganapatideva** (APPSC G2 2012; Architecture 2011; APPSC PYQ; G1 2024 match B-I).
[1:3] - **⚠ Disputed:** the Bekkallu inscription (1195) already calls Orugallu Rudradeva's capital, so some keys (G2 2019, Asst. Welfare Officers) gave **Rudradeva**; NextGen, Rocket and the G1 2024 official match credit **Ganapatideva**, who completed the move.
[1:4] - **Orugallu fort:** construction **begun by Rudradeva** (APSLPRB 2023), completed by Ganapatideva, strengthened by Rudramadevi.
[1:5] - **Kakatiya capital:** **Orugallu / Warangal** (APPSC 2019 ×2; Polytechnic Lecturers 2020) – not Vijayanagara, Amaravati, Vijayawada, Guntur or Visakhapatnam.
[1:6] - **Orugallu** = "single rock", the ancient name of Warangal; **Warangal** is the place that best reflects the Kakatiya contribution to culture (fort, Thousand Pillar temple, Ramappa nearby) (AEE 2008).
[1:7] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP History - Kakatiyas p.11, p.15, p.45; Rocket AP History p.47; app notes 1:92 (sub 3)

## SUB 3 | Ganapatideva (1199–1262)
[3:0] - **Reign:** **1199–1262** (about **63 years**, the longest; APPSC AE 2011); first inscription **Manthena, 26 Dec 1199**; title **Andhra Deshadhiswara**.
[3:1] - **Accession:** held captive by Yadava **Jaitugi**; **Recherla Rudra** crushed revolts and installed him.
[3:2] - **Unifier:** brought **the whole Telugu area under one rule** after the Satavahanas (1199 PYQ ×2), from Ganjam to Kanchi – the Kakatiya who ruled all Andhradesa as one sovereign (APPSC G1 2003).
[3:3] - **Kanchi:** the most powerful Kakatiya, who **extended the empire up to Kanchi** (Group-I 1990-91; G1 1994) – not Beta I, Prola I or Prola II.
[3:4] - **Gurus:** raja guru **Visvesvara Sivacharya** – not Vidyanatha, Narahari Tirtha, Rameswara Pandita or Dhruveshwara Pandita.
[3:5] # Campaigns
[3:6] - **Velanadu:** 1201 coastal campaign – general **Mutyalachaundaraya** took **Divi**; married **Narama and Perama**, daughters of **Pinnachodi**; made their brother **Jayapa** **Gajasahani**.
[3:7] - **1210:** with **Choda Tikka** of Nellore killed **Prithviswara** → Velanati kingdom ended (title **Prithviswara Shirakhandika Kreeda Vinoda**); **1257:** with Akkana and Bayyana killed **Mummadi Rajendra Chola** – final end of the Velanati Chodas.
[3:8] - **Pithapuram inscription** of Velanati **Prithvisvara** names **Kolleru Lake** (G2 2017).
[3:9] - **Nellore:** restored **Manumasiddhi II** after the poet **Tikkana** came as envoy; Tikkana dedicated **Nirvachanottara Ramayana** to **Manumasiddhi**, not Ganapati.
[3:10] - **Kalinga:** fought the **Eastern Gangas** for southern Kalinga and Vengi.
[3:11] - **Defeat, 1263:** **Jatavarman Sundara Pandya** beat the Kakatiyas at **Muttukur** (Nellore); **Nellore fell to the Pandyas** (G2 Mains 2017 ×2) – not the Velanati Chodas, Yadavas or Hoysalas.
[3:12] # Works and succession
[3:13] - **Ramappa temple** (Recherla Rudra, 1213), **Lakkavaram** and **Ghanapur** tanks, **Ganapavaram** villages, **Motupalli Abhaya Sasanam** (1244).
[3:14] - **Telugu replaced Sanskrit** in inscriptions from his reign.
[3:15] - **Family:** no son → daughter **Rudramadevi succeeded him** (APPSC PYQ), raised as a son (putrika), trained by her mother **Kupama Devi**; married **Veerabhadra** of the **Nidadavolu Eastern Chalukyas**; daughter **Ganapamba** married the **Kota** chief; sisters **Mailamba** and **Kundamamba** married the sons of **Natavadi Buddharaja**.
[3:16] - Rudrama defeated the Pandyas, Eastern Gangas and Seunas – **not the Western Chalukyas**; Marco Polo visited Motupalli ("Mutfili") in her reign.
[3:17] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP History - Kakatiyas p.11-15, p.47-51; app notes 1:92 (sub 4, 5)
