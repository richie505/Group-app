## SUB 1 | Ramananda – Hindi, Caste and Disciples
[1:0] - **Born at Prayag** (tradition, 1299/1300) in a **Kanyakubja Brahmin** family; educated and settled at **Varanasi** (NextGen gives Varanasi as his home).
[1:1] - Follower of **Ramanuja's** school; replaced Vishnu worship with **Rama (and Sita)**.
[1:2] - **First Bhakti saint to use Hindi** (vernacular) to preach (UPSC 2002) – not Kabir, Dadu or Tulsidas.
[1:3] - "**Let no man ask a man's sect or caste**" (jati-panti puchhe nahin koi) – **Ramananda** (UPPCS 2009); first medieval reformer to denounce caste.
[1:4] - **Kabir was his disciple** (one of 12) (UP Lower 2002); others: Raidas, Dhanna, Sena, Pipa, Padmavati, Sadhana.
[1:5] - Liberal ideas that echo Islamic monotheism and equality → among the options **Ramananda is the leader "influenced by Islam"** (APPSC G2 2011) – not Ramanuja, the Nayanars or Chaitanya.
[1:6] - **Born at Prayag** – Ramananda; Raidas – Varanasi; Tulsidas – Rajapur; Kumbhandas – Mathura (UPPCS 2010).
[1:7] > Ramananda: Prayag, Hindi, Rama, "no caste", guru of Kabir.
[1:8] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 5 p.1; PMF Art & Culture Part 5 p.121; old app notes row 143
## SUB 2 | Ramanuja – Teaching, Andhra and Recent Links
[2:0] - Born at **Sriperumbudur** (Tamil Nadu); centre **Srirangam**; called **Ilaya Azhwar**; incarnation of **Adisesha / Lakshmana**.
[2:1] - **First true exponent of Bhakti**; reaction against Shankara's Advaita; wrote the **Sri Bhashya**.
[2:2] - **Teaching:** **the best means of salvation is devotion** (UPSC 2022) – not Vedic authority, logic or meditation.
[2:3] - Made the **Pancharatra Agama** the basis of Sri Vaishnava temple worship (APPSC Endowment 2022).
[2:4] - Sri Vaishnava ascetics (**yatis**) carry the **tridanda** (triple staff) (APPSC Endowment 2022).
[2:5] - **1000th birth anniversary:** PM released a **stamp on Sri Ramanujacharya** (1 May 2017); **Statue of Equality**, Hyderabad – sitting statue, inaugurated **Feb 2022**.
[2:6] - **Andhra:** patrons **Velanati Cholas**, **Palnati Haihayas**; inspired **Palnati Brahmanaidu's Veera Vaishnava** movement; gave **Panchamas** temple entry; installed **Govindaraja** at Tirupati.
[2:7] - **Sri Vaishnava split:** **Vadakalai** (**Vedanta Desika**) and **Tengalai** – both Vishishtadvaita.
[2:8] > Ramanuja: Sriperumbudur → Srirangam; devotion; Pancharatra; 1017 → stamp 2017.
[2:9] ~~ Sources: PMF Art & Culture Part 5 p.121; NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 4 p.39; PIB, 1 May 2017 (stamp); old app notes row 143
## SUB 3 | Shankaracharya – Advaita, the Forerunner
[3:0] - Born at **Kaladi, Kerala** (788); died 820; **founder of Advaita** philosophy (APPSC Endowment 2022) – not Bhaskaracharya, Prabhakara or Madhusudana Saraswati.
[3:1] - **Guru = Govinda Bhagavatpada** (disciple of Gaudapada); **Padmapada** was Shankara's own disciple.
[3:2] - Wrote **Saundarya Lahari** in praise of the **Goddess**; Sivananda Lahari praises Shiva.
[3:3] - **Forerunner / pioneer of the Bhakti cult** (NextGen); four mathas – Sringeri, Puri, Dwarka, Jyotirmath.
[3:4] > Shankara: Kaladi – Govinda Bhagavatpada – Advaita – Saundarya Lahari.
[3:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 4 p.39; Britannica 'Shankara'; old app notes row 143

## SUB 5 | Vijayanagara Court and Dvaita in the Deccan
[5:0] - **Purandara Dasa** (1484-1564), Haridasa and "Pitamaha of Carnatic music", was a contemporary of **Sri Krishnadevaraya** (1509-29).
[5:1] - **Vallabha** at Krishnadevaraya's court defeated Dvaita scholar **Vyasaraya (Vyasa Tirtha)**; Vyasa Tirtha was **Rajaguru** of Saluva Narasimha and Krishnadevaraya; works Nyayamrita, Tarka Tandava.
[5:2] - **Narahari Tirtha** spread **Dvaita** in Andhra-Kalinga from **Srikurmam**.
[5:3] - **Amuktamalyada** (Krishnadevaraya; Mala Dasari Katha) – inclusion of lower groups.
