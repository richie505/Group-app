## 3. Repeats and trends
- **Most repeated:** Thousand Pillar = Rudradeva (9 incl. dynasty); Ramappa = Recherla Rudra, 1213 (8); Jalakaranda = Dharmasagar (4); Jayapa's Nritta Ratnavali (8); Visvesvara Sambhu (3); Samaya (3); Srikurmam two flagpoles (2+1).
- **Key traps found:** the G2 2019 bank key "Purushartha Saramu" for the women's-property tenet is wrong (NextGen: Vignanesvaram of Ketana); "Kapalikas from Pasupata" vs NextGen's "Misra Saivam"; Ramappa "Monolithic" = Ekasila; Ramappa's district has changed (Mulugu).
- The old notes were heavy on **sandbox/floating-brick techniques, Warangal Fort, Pancharamas, temple orders and royal women donors**. These are asked 0–1 times in PYQs and are kept short, only for the own MCQs. Earthquake-plan PYQs filed here are geography or disaster management, not temple engineering.
