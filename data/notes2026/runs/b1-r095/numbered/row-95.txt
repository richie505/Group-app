## SUB 0 | Shaiva Sects, Veerashaivism and the Aradhyas
[0:0] # Shaiva sects
[0:1] TABLE head: Sect | Founder / trait
  [0:1.0] Pasupata | Oldest Shaiva sect; founded by Lakulisa (Nakulisa), 1st–2nd c. AD; popular into the Reddi period, declined under Vijayanagara
  [0:1.1] Kalamukha | Founded by Kalasana (Kalanana), disciple of Lakulisa; extremist; temple managers, gave anna dana; Simha Parishads
  [0:1.2] Kapalika | Offshoot of Pasupata (the "Vamacharyas" of the Kalamukhas); worship Bhairava; skull-bowl, Panchamudras
  [0:1.3] Aradhya | Brahmin Shaivas led by Mallikarjuna Panditaradhya; kept Vedic ritual and varna
[0:2] - **Pasupata founder:** **Lakulisa** (APPSC PYQ) – not Basava (Veerashaivism), Bijjala (Kalachuri king) or Kallata.
[0:3] - **Kapalikas emerged from:** **Pasupata Shaivism** (APPSC G2 2019; APPSC PYQ).
[0:4]   - ⚠ Sources differ: NextGen's PYQ list says "the Kapalikas emerged from the school of **Misra Saivam**" – the app keys Pasupata (most books) and shows the dispute.
[0:5] - **Kakatiya patronage:** early kings (Beta, Durga) were disciples of **Kalamukha** acharyas of the Srisailam Silamatha; Kalamukha centres – **Srisailam, Alampur, Vemulawada, Kaleshwaram**, Tripurantakam.
[0:6] # Aradhya acharyas – Siddhatraya
[0:7] - **12 Aradhya acharyas** in **four triads**; **Siddhatraya** = **Revana, Marula, Ekorama** (G2 2012; APPSC PYQ).
[0:8] - **Not a Siddhacharya:** **Udbhata** – a Sanskrit poetician (alankarika).
[0:9] # Veerashaivism
[0:10] - **Founder:** **Basaveshwara** (Basava), minister of **Kalachuri Bijjala** (Karnataka); spread into Andhra in the Kakatiya period through **Palkuriki Somanatha** (Basavapuranam, Panditaradhya Charitra).
[0:11] - **Followers:** **Lingayats**; gurus **Jangamas**; in Andhra inscriptions called **Veeravratulu**; main Andhra centre **Srisailam**.
[0:12] - **Teaching:** **rejected** the Vedas, caste, child marriage and sacrifice → drew artisans and lower groups (social mobility).
[0:13] - **Reddi period:** the **popular religion was Veerashaivism** (G2 2011), alongside Pasupata patronised by the kings.
[0:14] > Kakatiya age = the **"Shaiva era"**; dynastic deity **Svayambhu Shiva**.

## SUB 1 | Caste Associations (Samayas) and Non-Brahmin Rule
[1:0] # Samayas
[1:1] - **Samaya / Samayamu** = **caste association** (G2 PYQ; APPSC PYQ ×2) – not Sreni-bandham (guild), Sangha or Kutamu.
[1:2] - **Bodies:** Brahmins – **Mahajanulu**; Vaishyas (Komatis) – **Nakaramu**; violators fined (**Samayadandam**), enforced by the **Samaya Senapati**.
[1:3] TABLE head: Group | Body / castes
  [1:3.0] Brahmins | Mahajanas (Vaidikas vs Niyogis)
  [1:3.1] Vaishyas | Nakaram
  [1:3.2] Panchanamuvaru | The five artisan (smith) groups
  [1:3.3] Ashtadasha kulas | 18 occupational groups inside Warangal fort (Kridabhiramam)
  [1:3.4] Veerashaivism | New castes Lingayats, Balijas, Jangamas
  [1:3.5] Vaishnavism | Satanis, Dasaris
[1:4] # Non-Brahmanisation
[1:5] - **Culmination:** the non-Brahmanisation of religion and politics **culminated under the Kakatiyas** (G2 PYQ; APPSC PYQ) – not the Vengi Chalukyas, Pallavas or Rashtrakutas.
[1:6] - **Why:** the Kakatiyas were **Sudras** (of **Pulinda/Boya** origin) who rose through military service; officials came from warrior and peasant groups (Nayankara); **Brahmins lost political clout**.
[1:7] ~~ Sources: NextGen Kakatiyas p.37, p.69; NextGen AP History - APPSC 07 p.1; app notes 1:95 (sub 0, 1)

## SUB 3 | From Jainism to Shaivism
[3:0] - **Early Kakatiyas:** **Jains** (G2 2011) → **Shaiva** from **Prola II / Rudradeva**.
[3:1] - **Decline of Jainism:** **Kalamukha and Veerashaiva hostility**; Jain–Saiva conflicts from Rudradeva's reign.
[3:2] - **Jain centres:** **Bodhan, Vemulawada, Patancheru, Kolanupaka**, Hanumakonda (Anumakonda).
[3:3] - **Padmakshi temple, Hanumakonda:** built by **Prola II**, **originally a Jain shrine**.
[3:4] - **Kadalalaya Jain basadi, Anumakonda:** built by **Mailama, wife of minister Bethana (Chetana)** – a minister's family, not the king.
[3:5] - **Tolerance:** Vaishnava shrines (Thousand Pillar Vasudeva, Prasanna Keshava) were also endowed.
[3:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Kakatiyas p.29, p.37, p.43; NextGen AP History - APPSC 11 p.11; app notes 1:95 (sub 2)
## SUB 4 | Temples as Institutions – the Malkapuram Inscription
[4:0] - **Temples:** **landed corporations** with lands and staff; attached **monasteries and feeding houses**; courtesans (**Kalavatis**) sustained dance and music.
[4:1] - **Malkapuram inscription (1261, Rudramadevi's reign):** **Visvesvara Sambhu** of the **Golaki matha** (Kalamukha-**Pasupata** Saiva lineage), guru of Ganapatideva, set up a Saiva matha, a **college**, a **feeding house**, a **maternity home (prasuti sala)** and a hospital at **Mandadam**.
[4:2] - **Pasupata Saiva sect record:** the **Malkapuram** inscription (Junior Asst. PYQ) – not Bayyaram (Mailamba's tank, genealogy), Hanumakonda or Kazipet Dargah (Beta's Betheswara temple).
[4:3] ~~ Sources: NextGen Kakatiyas p.3, p.71; Epigraphia Indica (Malkapuram pillar inscription); app notes 1:95 (sub 5)
