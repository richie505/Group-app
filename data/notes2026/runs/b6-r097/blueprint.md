- Women's ODI WC 2025: the 2005 and 2017 lost finals were India's, not South Africa's (South Africa reached its first final in 2025).
- Grand Slam entry rule: the 2026 Rulebook says "ranking of 500 or better", which is why the UPSC 2026 key is marked disputed.
