=== Patriotic Songs – Poet and Occasion
| Song / line | Poet | Occasion / collection |
|---|---|---|
| **"Bharata Khandambu chakkani paadi aavu"** | **Chilakamarti Lakshmi Narasimham** | Improvised while translating **Bipin Chandra Pal's Rajahmundry speeches (1907)**, Vande Mataram movement – India a milch cow milked by the British |
| **"Maakoddee (Makoddu ye) Tella Doratanamu"** | **Garimella Satyanarayana** | **Non-Cooperation** movement (first stage, 1921) |
| **"Veeragandhamu techinaramu, veerulevaro telpudi"** | **Tripuraneni Ramaswamy Chowdary** | **Civil Disobedience / Salt Satyagraha** (1930) – not NCM |
| "Kallu Manandoi" | Gollapudi Sitarama Sastri | Civil Disobedience (anti-liquor) |
| "Dandalu Dandalu Bharatamata" | Rajahmundry Kesari Samaj | Vijayawada AICC, 1921 |
| **"Desamunu preminchumanna"** – "Desamante matti kadoi, desamante manushuloi" | **Gurajada Apparao** | ***Mutyala Saralu*** (1910) |
| "Ye desamegina ... pogadara nee talli bhoomi Bharatini" (*Janmabhoomi*) | **Rayaprolu Subbarao** | Patriotic lyric |
| **"Maa Telugu Talliki mallepoodanda"** | **Sankarambadi Sundarachari** | Later the AP state song |
| "Kollayi gattithenemi maa Gandhi" | Basavaraju Apparao | Gandhi song |
- Chilakamarti edited *Desamata*; called the first Telugu national poet ("Andhra Milton").
- **Rayaprolu Subbarao** is credited (exam key) with first using **"Tenugu talli"** (Mother Telugu) in nationalist verse.
- Songs in spoken Telugu reached villagers whom pamphlets could not.
> Bharata Khandambu = Chilakamarti (1907); Makoddu = Garimella (NCM); Veeragandham = Tripuraneni (Salt Satyagraha).

=== Gurajada Apparao
- **Titles:** **"Abhyudaya Kavitha Pitamahudu"** (father of advanced / progressive poetry), **Kavisekhara**; NextGen: Navayuga Vaitalikudu, Bhava Kavita Vaitalikudu.
- **Works:** ***Kanyasulkam*** (play on bride-price; written 1892, first staged at **Vizianagaram**), ***Mutyala Saralu*** (1910 poems in his own colloquial metre), *Puttadibomma Purnamma*, *Diddubatu*, *Desabhakti Geyam*; English poem ***Sarangadhara***.
- Champion of **vyavaharika bhasha** (spoken Telugu) as a literary medium; *Mutyala Saralu* opened modern Telugu poetry.
- Born at Rayavaram (Visakhapatnam); in the court of Ananda Gajapati of Vizianagaram.
> "Desamante matti kadoi" is from *Mutyala Saralu* – not *Kanyasulkam*, *Manishi* or *Puttadi Bomma Purnamma*.
~~ Sources: NextGen Modern India L2 Part 3 p.9; NextGen AP History Part 7 p.26; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gurajada_Apparao; app notes 1:211 (sub 2)

=== Rayaprolu Subbarao and Bhava Kavitvam
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Titles | **"Abhinava Nannaya"** (Encyclopedia of Indian Literature); Father of Telugu Romanticism |
| Contribution | Brought Western (English lyric) romanticism – **Bhava Kavitvam** – into Telugu; left translations of Sanskrit epics for original, emotive verse |
| Works | *Trunakankanamu* (first independent poem), *Andhravali*, *Swatantrya Rathamu*, *Janmabhoomi*, ***Misra Manjari*** (Sahitya Akademi 1965) |
- **Bhava Kavitvam:** romantic, personal emotion, nature, love; poets Basavaraju Apparao, Duvvuri Ramireddy, Devulapalli Krishna Sastri (Andhra Shelley), Venkata Parvateeswara Kavulu (*Ekanta Seva*).
- Progressive writers such as **Sri Sri** called it **"escapist"**.
> Abhinava Nannaya = **Rayaprolu** (not Viswanatha, Boyi Bhimanna or Sri Sri); Kavi Samrat = Viswanatha.
~~ Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rayaprolu_Subba_Rao; NextGen previous papers p.41; app notes 1:211 (sub 3)

=== Garimella Satyanarayana
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Song | **"Maakoddee Tella Doratanamu"** – Non-Cooperation era; copied a thousand times; praised by Gandhi |
| Life | 1893-1952, born at Gonedapadu (Srikakulam); left studies at Gandhi's call |
| Jail | Arrested 9 Feb 1922; one year for the song |
| Legacy | First people's poet of the freedom struggle; also "Dandalu Dandalu Bharat Mata" |
- *Ramayana Kalpavrikshamu* is **not** his – it is Viswanatha's.
> "Makoddu ye tella doratanamu" = **Garimella** – not Gurajada, Unnava, Tripuraneni, Dasarathi or Sankarambadi.
