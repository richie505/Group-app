[1:8] - **Second National Space Day: 23 August 2025** – marks the **Chandrayaan-3** soft landing near the lunar south pole.
[1:9] - India has ratified the UN space treaties but has **no comprehensive national space law** (no Space Activities Act); **IN-SPACe works without statutory authority**.
[1:10] - Existing framework: Catalogue of Indian Standards for Space Industry (safety); **Indian Space Policy (2023)**; **IN-SPACe Norms, Guidelines and Procedures (NPG), 2023** – authorisation of commercial activities.

[1:12] - Countries with national space laws: **Japan, Luxembourg, US**.

[9:0] # From GM to Genome-Edited Crops
[9:1] - **Bt cotton** – **India's only commercial GM crop**.
[9:2] - **GM vs GE**: GM inserts **foreign genes** (transgenic); GE **edits the plant's own genes, no foreign DNA**.

  [9:3.3] Samba Mahsuri – Gn1a (cytokinin oxidase) edited | More spikelets, ~19% yield gain |

  [9:3.5] Mustard (GE) | Canola-quality, low pungency, disease/pest resistance (2nd-year trials, possible release 2026) | 
[9:4] - Technology: **CRISPR-Cas9**; claimed 17-30% higher yield, 20 days earlier maturity, drought/salinity tolerance; non-transgenic.
[9:5] - Criticism: **Coalition for a GM-Free India** analysed AICRIP (2023-24) data – Pusa DST Rice 1 equal/lower yield in 12 of 20 sites; Kamala underperformed in 8 of 19; Kamala flowering 101 vs 104 days.
[9:6] - Regulation: **March 2022 MoEFCC OM** – **transgene-free GE plants exempt** from GM biosafety rules; need **IBSC certification** (not GEAC). **DBT guidelines (2022)** – gene-edited crops without foreign DNA **not treated as GMOs**; oversight **RCGM and GEAC**.
[9:7] - Funding: National Agricultural Science Fund (~2018); ₹500 crore in Union Budget 2023-24. ICAR mapped ~178 genes in 24 field crops and 43 genes in horticultural crops.
[9:8] - Tools: Cas9, Cas12a; newer **GeoCas9 and CasLambda** supplied by the **Innovative Genomics Institute** (founded by Jennifer Doudna) – training at IARI (Feb 2025); indigenous **TnpB-based mini genome editor** – team of **Kutubuddin Ali Molla, Central Rice Research Institute (CRRI), Cuttack**; 9 scientists trained abroad, 12 more scheduled.
[9:9] > Transgene-free GE plants need only IBSC certification, not GEAC approval.
[9:10] # Asgard Archaea (IISc Study)
[9:11] - **IISc Bengaluru** (The EMBO Journal) – **Odinarchaeota** (an Asgard archaea) has **two FtsZ genes** plus a tubulin-like gene.
[9:12] - **OdinFtsZ1** – straight protofilaments, **anchors directly to membranes** (bacteria-like); **OdinFtsZ2** – **spiral ring-like structures**, **needs adaptor proteins**. Methods: phylogenetics, cryo-electron microscopy.
[9:13] - **Asgard archaea** – **named after Norse gods** (Odin-, Loki-, Thorarchaeota); deep-sea sediments; **closest living relatives of eukaryotes**.
[9:14] - **FtsZ** – bacterial division protein forming the **Z-ring**; **tubulin** is the **eukaryotic** hallmark (not unique to bacteria).
