[1:16] TABLE head: Technology | Key point
  [1:16.0] Compact fission reactor | Shipping-container size; 10-100 kW; NASA KRUSTY demo up to 10 kW
  [1:16.1] Nuclear Thermal Propulsion (NTP) | Reactor heats propellant (liquid hydrogen); high thrust; US DRACO test by 2026
  [1:16.2] Nuclear Electric Propulsion (NEP) | Reactor electricity powers ion/plasma thrusters; low continuous thrust
  [1:16.3] Space law | Key point
  [1:16.4] Outer Space Treaty 1967, Article IV | Bans nuclear weapons/WMDs in orbit and on celestial bodies – does not ban peaceful nuclear power
  [1:16.5] UN Principles on Nuclear Power Sources in Outer Space, 1992 | UNGA Resolution 47/68; non-binding; do not cover NTP/NEP propulsion
  [1:16.6] 1972 Liability Convention | Absolute liability for damage on Earth or to aircraft in flight; fault-based in outer space
  [1:16.7] COPUOS | UN Committee on the Peaceful Uses of Outer Space

## SUB 2 | Semiconductors, Processors and Supercomputing
[2:0] # DHRUV64
[2:1] - **India's first fully indigenous 1.0 GHz, 64-bit dual-core microprocessor**, by **C-DAC** under the **Microprocessor Development Programme (MDP)**.
[2:2] - Architecture: open-source **RISC-V** (no licence costs); the **third chip fabricated under the Digital India RISC-V (DIR-V) Programme**.
[2:3] - Uses: 5G infrastructure, automotive, consumer electronics, industrial automation, IoT. Next: **Dhanush and Dhanush+** (DHANUSH64, DHANUSH64+ SoC).
[2:4] - India uses about **20% of the world's microprocessors** and has 20% of the world's chip design engineers.
