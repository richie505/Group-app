## SUB 0 | Planning Commission and the National Development Council
[0:0] # Planning Commission
[0:1] - Set up **15 March 1950** by a **resolution of the Union Cabinet / Government of India** – **non-constitutional, non-statutory** (not by an amendment or ordinance); the Constitution provides for the Finance Commission (Art. 280), Election Commission (Art. 324) and UPSC (Art. 315), **not** the Planning Commission.
[0:2] - **First Chairman: Jawaharlal Nehru** (PM ex officio); first Deputy Chairman **Gulzarilal Nanda**; the Deputy Chairman had **Cabinet Minister** rank.
[0:3] - Early Five-Year Plans (from 1951, Mahalanobis model in the 2nd) gave the public sector the commanding heights and kept the **market economy at bay**.
[0:4] - Cabinet repealed the 1950 resolution on **13 August 2014** (Modi govt); **no longer in existence** – replaced by NITI Aayog on 1 January 2015.
[0:5] # National Development Council
[0:6] - Set up **6 August 1952** by an **executive order** – **non-constitutional and non-statutory** (calling it statutory is wrong).
[0:7] - Members: **PM (chairman)**, **Union Cabinet Ministers**, **Chief Ministers of all States**, CMs/administrators of UTs, members of the Planning Commission – **not** the Finance Commission Chairman or the CJI.
[0:8] - **Secretary: the Secretary of the Planning Commission**; meant to meet at least **twice a year**.
[0:9] - Role: **coordinator between the Planning Commission and the States**; **final approval of Five-Year Plans**; prescribes **guidelines** for formulating the Plan, **considers/discusses its progress**, and **suggests ways to achieve its targets** – it did not allocate plan money.
[0:10] - Restructured into NITI Aayog's **Governing Council**.
[0:11] > Angles: Planning Commission basis (Cabinet resolution ×3), first chairman, NDC date/members/secretary/functions (×8), "not in existence now".

## SUB 1 | NITI Aayog – Status, Structure and Hubs
[1:0] - Came into force on **1 January 2015** (Cabinet Secretariat notification) by a **Union Cabinet resolution** – **neither constitutional nor statutory** (not an ordinance or Act); a **think-tank**; based on **cooperative federalism**.
[1:1] - **Chairperson: the Prime Minister** (ex officio – no full-time chairman); **Vice-Chairperson** (Cabinet rank), appointed by the PM; full-time members (MoS rank); ex-officio Union Ministers; a **CEO**.
[1:2] - **First Vice-Chairman: Arvind Panagariya**; then Rajiv Kumar, Suman K. Bery; **Ashok Kumar Lahiri** from April 2026 (as of 2026).
[1:3] - Nodal agency for **poverty estimation**.
[1:4] TABLE head: Body / hub | Role
  [1:4.0] Governing Council | PM, all State CMs, CMs of UTs with legislatures, LGs of other UTs, VC, members (no Governors); 11th meeting 11 June 2026
  [1:4.1] Regional Councils | Regional issues; chair PM or his nominee
  [1:4.2] Team India Hub | Leads engagement of States with the Centre
  [1:4.3] Knowledge and Innovation Hub | Think-tank capacity
  [1:4.4] State Support Mission | SETUs → State Vision@2047
[1:5] # Planning horizon
[1:6] - Five-Year Plans replaced by a **15-year Vision**, **7-year Strategy** and **3-year Action Agenda** – **5 years is not** one of them.
[1:7] > Angles: date 1 Jan 2015 (×3), Cabinet resolution, no full-time chairman, first VC, Team India Hub, 3/7/15 years.

## SUB 2 | NITI Aayog vs the Planning Commission
[2:0] TABLE head: Feature | Planning Commission | NITI Aayog
  [2:0.0] Approach | Top-down | Bottom-up, cooperative federalism (Team India)
  [2:0.1] Funds | Allocated funds to States | No fund allocation (Finance Ministry / Finance Commission)
  [2:0.2] States | Limited role | Equal partners
  [2:0.3] Basis | Cabinet resolution 1950 | Cabinet resolution 2015
[2:1] - NITI envisages: **empowered States as equal partners**, a **think-tank** role (domain knowledge, strategy), a **catalyst** role – but **not allocation of resources to needy States**.
