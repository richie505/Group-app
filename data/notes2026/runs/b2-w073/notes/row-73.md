=== Articles and Amendments – 338, 338A, 338B, 350B
| Body | Article | Created / changed by |
|---|---|---|
| Special Officer (Commissioner) for SCs and STs | **338** (original) | Constitution, 1950 |
| Combined **National Commission for SCs and STs** | 338 | **65th Amendment, 1990** (in force 12 Mar 1992) |
| **NCSC** (separate) | **338** | **89th Amendment, 2003** (bifurcation); from 2004 |
| **NCST** | **338A** | **89th Amendment, 2003**; came into being **19 Feb 2004** |
| **NCBC** | **338B** | **102nd Amendment, 2018** (statutory from **1993**) |
| **Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities** | **350B** | **7th Amendment, 1956** |
- **Constitutional bodies** "in the sense that they are directly established by the Constitution": NCSC (338), NCST (338A), NCBC (338B, since 2018), Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities (350B). **NCW, NCM, NHRC, NCPCR are statutory.**
  - So NCST was created by a **constitutional amendment** – not a government order, Cabinet resolution or statute.
- **Distractors:** Art. **340** = Backward Classes Commission (Kalelkar, Mandal); **341/342** = lists of SCs/STs; **280** = Finance Commission; **332** = Assembly seats for SCs/STs; **350** = representation for grievances; **343/347** = official language.
- Evolution: 1978 non-statutory Commission for SCs and STs (resolution) → **1987** resolution renamed it the National Commission → **65th Amendment** replaced the single Special Officer with a multi-member constitutional body → **1999** Ministry of Tribal Affairs → **89th** split it.
- **102nd Amendment** also inserted **Art. 342A** (President notifies SEBCs) and relieved the **NCSC of backward-class work**; the **105th Amendment (2021)** restored States' power to keep their own SEBC lists.
- **⚠ Dated key:** before 2018 the NCBC was statutory, so old papers answer "NCBC is not a constitutional body"; today all four (NCSC, NCST, NCBC, Special Officer) are constitutional.
> Asked as: Article for NCSC (338 – 6 PYQs), NCST (338A – 4), Special Officer (350B); amendment that created NCST / split the commission (89th – 3); NCBC status (102nd – APPSC FRO 2022); odd one out "not a constitutional body".

=== Composition, Functions, Powers and Reports
# Composition
- NCSC, NCST and the constitutional NCBC each: **Chairperson + Vice-Chairperson + 3 other members**.
- Appointed by the **President by warrant under his hand and seal**; tenure fixed by the President's Rules (2004) = **3 years**; not more than **two terms**.
- The statutory NCBC (1993 Act, in force 2 Apr 1993) had **5 members** headed by a **retired judge of the Supreme Court or a High Court**.
# Functions (Art. 338(5), 338A(5), 338B(5))
- 1. **Investigate and monitor** the constitutional and legal safeguards and evaluate their working.
- 2. **Inquire into specific complaints** of deprivation of rights and safeguards.
- 3. **Participate and advise on the planning process** of socio-economic development and evaluate progress.
- 4. **Present reports to the President** annually (and when it deems fit) – **not to the Prime Minister**; the President lays them before **Parliament** with an action-taken memorandum; State-related reports go to the **Governor** (NCBC: to the State government).
- 5. Recommend measures for effective implementation of safeguards.
- **Not** functions: giving scholarships, raising a protection force, implementing Union welfare schemes, punishing offenders.
- **Consultation:** the Union and every State must consult the Commission on all major policy matters (Art. 338(9), 338A(9)); proposals for **de-reservation** of posts are examined by the NCSC/NCST/NCBC first (NCSC review of three years' proposals, Aug 2026).
- **NCST extra (2005 Rules):** ownership of **minor forest produce**, rights over mineral and water resources, rehabilitation of displaced tribals, PESA implementation.
# Powers – Art. 338(8)
- While investigating or inquiring it has **all the powers of a civil court trying a suit**: **summon** and examine on oath; **discovery of documents**; **receive evidence on affidavits**; **requisition any public record** from any court or office; **issue summons** for examining witnesses and documents. It **cannot punish**; its advice is recommendatory.
> Asked as: NCST tenure (3 years) and members besides Chairperson and Vice (3); a function of NCSC (advise on planning); NOT a function (report to the PM – APPSC Group-I); civil-court powers "all of the above" (APPSC Group-I 2023).

=== Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities – Art. 350B
- **Art. 350B** inserted by the **7th Amendment, 1956** (with **Art. 350A**) on the **States Reorganisation Commission's** recommendation – not the 9th Amendment.
- **One** Special Officer for the whole country, **appointed by the President** – there is **no** post "for every State".
- Duty: **investigate all matters relating to the safeguards** for linguistic minorities and **report to the President**, who lays the reports before **each House of Parliament** and sends them to the States.
- Office created **1957**; designated **Commissioner for Linguistic Minorities (CLM)**; under the **Ministry of Minority Affairs**; headquarters **New Delhi** (moved back from Allahabad, 2015); regional offices **Belgaum, Chennai, Kolkata**.
- The Constitution does **not** fix his qualifications, tenure, salary or removal.
- **Art. 350A**: States to provide **instruction in the mother tongue at the primary stage** to linguistic-minority children.
> Asked as: Article for the Special Officer (350B – APPSC FRO 2022); statements "one officer for every State" and "added by the 9th Amendment" both false (UPPSC 2024).

=== Backward Classes Commissions, Other Bodies and the AP Angle
# Backward Classes Commissions (Art. 340)
| Commission | Chairman | Year | Government |
|---|---|---|---|
| First Backward Classes Commission | **Kaka Kalelkar** | **29 Jan 1953** | Nehru |
| Second Backward Classes Commission | **B.P. Mandal** | **1 Jan 1979** | **Janata** (Morarji Desai) |
- **Mandal** first recommended **27%** OBC reservation; **Indra Sawhney v UoI (16 Nov 1992)** upheld 27%, the **creamy layer**, and ordered a permanent body → statutory **NCBC (1993 Act)**.
- First reservation policy: **Kolhapur (Shahu Maharaj), 1902**; Art. 15(4) added by the **1st Amendment, 1951**.
# Status of other bodies
- **NCW, NCM, NHRC** = statutory; **NITI Aayog** = executive (**Cabinet resolution**, 1 Jan 2015); **NCSK** = statutory under a **1993 Act** (lapsed 29 Feb 2004), now **non-statutory**, extended to **31 March 2028**.
# AP angle
- **AP State Commission for SCs and STs** created by a **2003** State Act; split later – **AP State Commission for Scheduled Tribes Act, 2020** (separate SC and ST commissions).
> Asked as: own MCQs only (Kalelkar 1953, Mandal 1979, Kolhapur, NITI resolution, AP commissions); no PYQ in this row.
