## 4. Keys that changed or need care
- **Navakhandavada** (G2 2008): printed key Narayana Bhatta → **Pavuluri Mallana** (Narayana Bhatta got Nandampudi).
- **Nannaya statements (G2 PYQ)**: printed I and III → **I and II** (Nandampudi went to Narayana Bhattu; the official key accepted III).
- **Who spread Veerashaivism in Andhra (G1 1987)**: printed Basava → **Panditaradhya** (NextGen; Basava founded it in Karnataka).
- **17th-18th c. textile centres**: printed Pochampally → **Madhavayapalem (Madapollam)**.
- Old app notes said the woman's-property rule was in Purushartha Saramu. NextGen previous papers and Rocket put it in **Ketana's Vignanesvaram**, so the notes now follow them.
