# Bhogapuram - Alluri Sitarama Raju International Airport
- At **Bhogapuram, Vizianagaram district** (not Visakhapatnam); **45 km** from Visakhapatnam, 25 km from Vizianagaram; land **2,203 acres**.
- **Dedicated by PM Narendra Modi on 1 August 2026**; commercial flights moved from Vizag from **17 August 2026**; code **VTZ**.
- First major **greenfield international airport** in AP after bifurcation (first since 2014); replaced Visakhapatnam's civil airport (**INS Dega** civil enclave).
- Developer: **GMR** via **GMR Visakhapatnam International Airport Ltd (GVIAL)**, not AAI; **PPP-DBFOT**; cost over **₹5,640 crore** (AIR/PIB figure; Praveen CA-360 gives construction cost ₹4,750 crore; Phase I estimate earlier ₹4,592 crore); a further about 500 acres were later handed over for allied facilities.
- **3,800 m runway**; withstands **275 kmph** winds; initial capacity **6 million (60 lakh) passengers/year** (Phase II 1.2 crore, III 1.8 crore); planned for 50 years of demand; 40 check-in and 10 immigration counters; GMR Aero City proposed.
- AI-enabled operations, biometric processing, Airport Predictive Operations Centre, Airside 4.0, BIM; **5 MW solar plant**; Green Building Standards; design inspired by **Chuttilu cottages** and **Araku Valley**.
- **Immigration Check Post (ICP)** by gazette under the **Immigration and Foreigners Act** - **39th airport in Category-1** list (direct international flights).
- **Thimsa (Dhimsa) dance Guinness record** at the event: about **13,000** girls and women (Rocket CA: **13,102**, 31 July 2026) in 260 groups of 50, over about 8 acres (Odia folk song). **Thimsa** = tribal folk dance of **North Andhra and Odisha**. Guinness World Records est. 1955, London.

=== Environment and Coast
# Human-elephant conflict - regional action plan
- AP gets **₹169.98 crore** over 5 years (**17%**); Kerala 20%; Karnataka 38% (largest). Four States (AP, Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu) total **₹999.87 crore** (incl. ₹500 crore Central).
- Assessment by **Project Elephant Division (MoEFCC)** and **Wildlife Institute of India**; released at **Visakhapatnam** on **World Elephant Day** (12 August, since 2012); **Gaj Gaurav Awards 2026** given at the same event.
- AP is a relatively **new elephant range** - elephants returned from **Hosur-Dharmapuri** forests in the **1980s**; AP elephants 65 (2017) → 142 (2023).
- Concentrated in **Chittoor, Tirupati, Annamayya**; small groups in Srikakulam, Parvathipuram Manyam. Critical habitat: **Koundinya Wildlife Sanctuary**.
- Threats: live-wire fencing, crude explosives, NH-716, Bengaluru-Chennai Expressway, railway doubling in Tirupati. Measures: Elephant Task Force; **Gaja Praja app**; 735 km elephant-proof trenches; solar fencing; **Kumki elephants from Odisha** for Parvathipuram-Manyam.
- Asian elephant **Elephas maximus**: Schedule I, CITES Appendix I, **National Heritage Animal**. **Project Elephant**: Centrally Sponsored, launched **February 1992** (financial year 1991-92).
