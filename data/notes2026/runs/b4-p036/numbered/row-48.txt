## SUB 1 | Basins, Rankings and Inter-state Rivers
[1:0] - **Count:** AP has **about 40 major, medium and minor rivers**, of which **15 are inter-state** (Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25); records also speak of **40 river basins**.
[1:1] - **Big three:** about **75%** of AP lies in the **Godavari, Krishna and Pennar** basins.
[1:2] - **Sharing:** **Krishna and Godavari** by **tribunal awards**; **Bahuda, Vamsadhara, Nagavali, Pennar, Palar, Ponniar** by **inter-state agreements**.

[1:4] - **Longest river flowing in AP = Godavari** (1,465 km; "longest and broadest river of South India", AP's lifeline); **longest course inside AP = Penna** (536 km).
[1:5] - **Largest basin in AP:** keys answer **Godavari**; strictly, by area inside present AP the **Pennar** is largest (**48,276 sq km** of its 55,213 sq km) – the Godavari's AP share is only ~4.5% of 312,812 sq km, most having gone to Telangana in 2014.
[1:6] - **Undivided AP's Godavari basin:** **73,201 sq km** (about 23% of the basin) – a pre-2014 figure.
[1:7] - **Godavari = longest river of Peninsular India** and, in books and keys, the **2nd-largest basin of India after the Ganga** (~9.5–10% of India); strictly the Indian part of the **Indus** basin (321,289 sq km) is a little larger.
[1:8] - **Peninsular order:** basin Godavari > Krishna > Mahanadi; length **Godavari 1,465 > Krishna ~1,400 > Narmada ~1,312 > Cauvery ~800**; Penna 597 > Vamsadhara 254.
[1:9] - **India's basins:** **20 = 12 major** (over 20,000 sq km) **+ 8 composite**; the **Kaveri** basin has the highest water utilisation (CWC), mostly for irrigation in Karnataka and Tamil Nadu.
[1:10] - **Irrigation lifeline:** the **Krishna** is the classic answer for "major irrigation source of Maharashtra, Telangana and AP" (the Godavari also crosses all three).
[1:11] - **Penna districts:** Anantapur → YSR Kadapa → SPSR Nellore (**not Chittoor**).

## SUB 2 | Polavaram, Lift Schemes, Canals and River Links
[2:0] # Polavaram
[2:1] - **Site:** multipurpose **earth-cum-rockfill** dam on the **Godavari** at **Polavaram** village (erstwhile **West Godavari**, now Eluru), **42 km upstream of the Cotton Barrage**; contemplated 1941, begun 2005 (Jalayagnam).
[2:2] - **Name:** first planned as **Ramapada Sagar** by **Dr. K.L. Rao**.
[2:3] - **Status:** **National Project** under **Section 90**, AP Reorganisation Act 2014; **seven Khammam mandals** transferred to AP to facilitate it.
[2:4] - **Design:** gross storage **194.6 TMC** (FRL 45.72 m); **live storage ~75.2 TMC ≈ 2,130 Mm³**; **7.20 lakh acres**; **960 MW**; **80 TMC Godavari → Krishna** (the Godavari–Krishna link of the ILR, not Krishna → Godavari); 23.44 TMC for Visakhapatnam; reservoir spreads into **Odisha and Chhattisgarh**.
[2:5] - **Left Main Canal** → **North Coastal Andhra and Visakhapatnam** (5 TMC to Kanithi, Sep 2026); **Right Main Canal** → Krishna at Vijayawada.
[2:6] - **Progress:** 53.47% overall, works 77% (Dec 2024, SES); 89% (2026); to be dedicated by **March 2027**, before the **Godavari Pushkaralu 2027**.

[4:5] # Krishna
