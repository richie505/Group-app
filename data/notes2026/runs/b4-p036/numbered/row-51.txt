## SUB 0 | Protected-Area Network and National Parks
[0:0] # At a glance (APSES 2024-25)
[0:1] - **3 national parks, 13 wildlife sanctuaries** (13 also in 2019, Jan 2020, Dec 2021), **4 deer parks**, **2 zoological parks**; **1 tiger reserve, 1 elephant reserve, 1 biosphere reserve**.
[0:2] - PA network (NPs + WLSs): **8,139.88 sq km** = 4.99% of AP, 21.87% of notified forest; notified forest ~22.96% of AP (2025-26 figure).
[0:3] - **Zoos (2):** **Indira Gandhi Zoological Park, Visakhapatnam** and **Sri Venkateswara Zoological Park, Tirupati** (managed by ZAAP); 1st ex-situ biodiversity park – Dolphin Conservation Society, Visakhapatnam.
[0:4] - **Deer parks:** **Kandaleru** (Rapur mandal, **SPS Nellore**, 25 ha), Chittoor reserve forest, Kotappakonda (Palnadu).
[0:5] # The three national parks
[0:6] TABLE head: National Park | District / place | Fact
  [0:6.0] Sri Venkateswara NP | Tirupati–Annamayya (erstwhile Chittoor and Kadapa); Seshachalam–Tirumala hills | First NP of AP, 1989; lies inside SV WLS; indicator species slender loris, golden gecko, red sanders, *Cycas beddomei*, giant squirrel; Talakona falls
  [0:6.1] Papikonda NP | near Rajamahendravaram; Godavari gorge (erstwhile East & West Godavari; now Eluru, ASR) | Largest NP (1,012.90 sq km); WLS 1978 → NP 2008; Godavari flows through it; moist deciduous; kanchu mekha dwarf goat; moth *Eucyclodes gavissima* recorded
  [0:6.2] Rajiv Gandhi NP (Rameswaram) | Kadapa, north bank of the Penna | Smallest NP (2.30 sq km); 2005
[0:7] - **Papikonda after 2014:** the Khammam (Telangana) part came to AP with the Polavaram mandals, so the park is now **wholly in AP** – a "three districts incl. Khammam" statement is outdated.
[0:8] - Tiger "Explorer" from **Tadoba** released into Papikonda (Feb 2026).
[0:9] - **NP vs WLS:** no rights and **no grazing** inside an NP; boundary changed only on **National Board for Wildlife** advice.
[0:10] - **Not AP:** Mrugavani NP, Kasu Brahmananda Reddy NP, Eturunagaram and **Manjira WLS** (Telangana); **Chinnar WLS** (Kerala); **Bhainsrorgarh WLS** (Rajasthan); Periyar (Kerala), Gir, Bandipur, Mudumalai.

## SUB 2 | Tiger, Elephant and Biosphere Reserves
[2:0] # Nagarjunasagar–Srisailam Tiger Reserve (NSTR)
[2:1] - Nallamala hills along the **Krishna**; AP + Telangana; declared **1983**; **3,727.50 sq km** – **India's largest tiger reserve** by area and by **core/critical tiger habitat** (Manas, Pakke, Periyar are smaller).
[2:2] - The **Nagarjunasagar–Srisailam WLS** was renamed **Rajiv Gandhi WLS**; it is the core. AP core: Nandyal, Prakasam, Palnadu.
[2:3] - Srisailam circle; NTCA core-buffer model; **tigers 63** (excl. 7 cubs, APSES 2024-25; AITE 2022: 63–67); Chenchus serve as protection watchers.
[2:4] - AP's only tiger reserve; NSTR is **not** a biosphere reserve.
[2:5] # Elephant reserve
[2:6] - **Koundinya WLS + Rayala Elephant Reserve** (Anantapuramu circle) – **Koundinya is AP's only sanctuary with Asian elephants**; nearest city **Chittoor**.
[2:7] - Elephants came into **Palamaner** in the **1980s** because of **drought in the Hosur–Dharmapuri reserve forests and Bannerghatta NP**.
[2:8] # Seshachalam Biosphere Reserve
[2:9] - **AP's first and only BR**, notified **2010**; **4,755.997 sq km**; Tirupati and Kurnool circles; Seshachalam (Tirumala) hills – the area of the **Sri Venkateswara** NP and WLS.
[2:10] - Endemic **red sanders**; fauna incl. **slender loris**, golden gecko; 1,756 flowering plants. Not "Sri Venkateswara BR".
[2:11] ~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.80; NextGen AP Geography 02 p.12; Rocket AP Geography p.32; app notes 4:51 (sub 4, 5, 6)
