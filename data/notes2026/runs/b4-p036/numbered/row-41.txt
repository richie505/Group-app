## SUB 7 | Protected-area Law, Zoos and Parks
[7:0] TABLE head: Category (WPA 1972) | Declared by / on | Rights
  [7:0.0] National Park | State (or Centre); boundaries fixed | Highest protection; no grazing, no private rights
  [7:0.1] Wildlife Sanctuary | State | Some rights and grazing regulated but allowed
  [7:0.2] Conservation Reserve (Sec 36A) | State, on government land adjoining/linking PAs, after consulting locals | Local rights unaffected
  [7:0.3] Community Reserve (Sec 36C) | State, on private/community land volunteered | Rights unaffected; not inside NP/WLS/CR
[7:1] - WPA got Presidential assent **9 September 1972**; reserves added by the 2002 amendment. Protection: **NP > WLS > Reserved > Protected forests**.
[7:2] - **ESZ:** ordinary buffer **1 km** around NPs/WLS (SC, June 2022); SC extended a **10-km mining buffer** to wetland conservation reserves.
[7:3] - **Zoos:** **Nandankanan** (Bhubaneswar, **Odisha**); Bannerghatta Biological Park (Bengaluru); Indira Gandhi Zoological Park (Visakhapatnam).
[7:4] - Other firsts: ISA – first treaty-based IGO in India (Secretariat **Gurugram**); Montreal Protocol in force **1989**.
