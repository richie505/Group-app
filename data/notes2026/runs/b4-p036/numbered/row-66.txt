[0:3] - **Native Americans** of North America = **Mongoloid** stock; their name = **Amerinds** (American Indians). **Mestizo** = European-Amerind mix; Mulatto = European-African; Zambo = Amerindian-African.
[0:4] - Humans share **over 99%** of genes; race is subjective – all belong to *Homo sapiens*.
[0:5] # Criteria of classification

## SUB 1 | Racial Stock of Indian Tribes
[1:0] - **Andaman tribes** (Onge, Jarawa, Great Andamanese, Sentinelese) = **Negrito**, a branch of the **Negroid** race (UPSC 1997; "aborigine tribes of Andaman" → Negroid); traces among Kadar, Irula.
[1:1] - **Nicobar Shompen = Mongoloid**, like the Nicobarese.
[1:2] - **North-East hills and forests** = **Mongoloid** (Naga = Indo-Mongoloid).
[1:3] - **Proto-Australoid** = central and south Indian tribes – **Santhal**, Munda, Oraon, Ho, Bhil, Chenchu, Yeruva (Khasi and Tharu are Mongoloid).
[1:4] - **AP**: **Chenchus (Nallamala) = Proto-Australoid**; AP plains = mainly **Mediterranean**, Dravidian-speaking.
[1:5] ~~ Sources: PMF Human & Economic Geography 01 p.28-29; NextGen Indian Geography 14 p.12-13 (Naga); Rocket World Geography #27 p.44; app notes 4:66 sub 4
## SUB 2 | Risley Classification – 7 Types (1901 Census)
[2:0] - **H.H. Risley**: Census Commissioner, **1901**, ran its ethnographic survey and divided Indians into **seven** racial types; **The People of India (1908)**.
[2:1] - Basis: **nasal index**; dictum: caste status varies inversely with nasal index – **racial theory of caste**, rejected.
[2:2] TABLE head: Type | Region
  [2:2.0] Turko-Iranian | Baluchistan, NWFP
  [2:2.1] Indo-Aryan | Punjab, Rajasthan, Kashmir
  [2:2.2] Scytho-Dravidian | Western India – Maharashtra, Saurashtra, Coorg
  [2:2.3] Aryo-Dravidian | Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, parts of Rajasthan
  [2:2.4] Mongolo-Dravidian | Lower Bengal, Odisha
  [2:2.5] Mongoloid | Himalaya, Assam, north-east
  [2:2.6] Dravidian | South India, Chota Nagpur, central India (Paniyan, Santals)
[2:3] - "Dravidian" properly names a **language family** (Indo-Aryan + Dravidian = main groups).
[2:4] ~~ Sources: OnlyIAS NCERT Indian Society 01 p.1 (1901 Census, Risley); app notes 4:66 sub 2
## SUB 3 | B.S. Guha Classification – 6 Groups (1931 Census)
[3:0] - **1931 Census** data: **6 groups, 9 sub-types**; most cited.
[3:1] TABLE head: Group | Region / peoples
  [3:1.0] Negrito | Andaman – Onge, Jarawa, Great Andamanese, Sentinelese; traces Kadar, Irula
  [3:1.1] Proto-Australoid | Central and south tribes – Santhal, Munda, Oraon, Ho, Bhil, Chenchu, Yeruva
  [3:1.2] Mongoloid | Palaeo-Mongoloid (Sema Nagas, Lepchas) + Tibeto-Mongoloid (Tibetans of Sikkim); Shompen
  [3:1.3] Mediterranean | Palaeo-Mediterranean (Dravidian south) + Mediterranean (Punjab, Ganga plain) + Oriental (Punjab, Rajasthan)
  [3:1.4] Western Brachycephals | Alpinoid, Dinaric, Armenoid (Parsis)
  [3:1.5] Nordic | North-West Frontier, Punjab, Rajasthan
[3:2] - Only Guha lists Negrito and Proto-Australoid separately.
[3:3] - **Western Brachycephals – regions:** Alpinoid and Dinaric types are found in Gujarat, Bengal, Odisha and Coorg; the Armenoid type in the Parsis.

## SUB 4 | Anthropological Survey of India and Modern Criticism
[4:0] - **AnSI**: founded **1945 at Varanasi**, moved to **Kolkata (HQ) 1948**; first Director **B.S. Guha**.
[4:1] - **People of India project (1985)**: **K.S. Singh**, about **4,635 communities**; the book *The People of India* (1908) = Risley.
[4:2] - AnSI classified **268** DNT communities for NITI Aayog.
[4:3] - **2009** genetics: Indians = mix of **Ancestral North Indians (ANI)** + **Ancestral South Indians (ASI)**; Rakhigarhi DNA – no **Steppe Pastoralist** gene.
[4:4] - **Criticism of old schemes:** Risley and Guha relied on visible physical traits and colonial ethnography; modern genetics shows rigid racial categories are not scientific.
[4:5] - India ratified **ICERD 1968**; UN CERD review first since **2007**.
[4:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Geography 14 p.1 (99% shared genes); app notes 4:66 sub 6
