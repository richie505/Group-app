=== Protected-Area Network and National Parks
# At a glance (APSES 2024-25)
- **3 national parks, 13 wildlife sanctuaries** (13 also in 2019, Jan 2020, Dec 2021), **4 deer parks**, **2 zoological parks**; **1 tiger reserve, 1 elephant reserve, 1 biosphere reserve**.
- PA network (NPs + WLSs): **8,139.88 sq km** = 4.99% of AP, 21.87% of notified forest; notified forest ~22.96% of AP (2025-26 figure).
- **Zoos (2):** **Indira Gandhi Zoological Park, Visakhapatnam** and **Sri Venkateswara Zoological Park, Tirupati** (managed by ZAAP); 1st ex-situ biodiversity park – Dolphin Conservation Society, Visakhapatnam.
- **Deer parks:** **Kandaleru** (Rapur mandal, **SPS Nellore**, 25 ha), Chittoor reserve forest, Kotappakonda (Palnadu).
# The three national parks
| National Park | District / place | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Sri Venkateswara NP** | **Tirupati–Annamayya** (erstwhile **Chittoor** and Kadapa); Seshachalam–Tirumala hills | **First NP of AP, 1989**; lies inside SV WLS; indicator species **slender loris, golden gecko, red sanders, *Cycas beddomei***, giant squirrel; Talakona falls |
| **Papikonda NP** | near **Rajamahendravaram**; Godavari gorge (erstwhile East & West Godavari; now Eluru, ASR) | **Largest NP (1,012.90 sq km)**; WLS **1978** → NP **2008**; **Godavari flows through it**; moist deciduous; **kanchu mekha** dwarf goat; moth *Eucyclodes gavissima* recorded |
| **Rajiv Gandhi NP (Rameswaram)** | **Kadapa**, north bank of the **Penna** | **Smallest NP (2.30 sq km)**; 2005 |
- **Papikonda after 2014:** the Khammam (Telangana) part came to AP with the Polavaram mandals, so the park is now **wholly in AP** – a "three districts incl. Khammam" statement is outdated.
- Tiger "Explorer" from **Tadoba** released into Papikonda (Feb 2026).
- **NP vs WLS:** no rights and **no grazing** inside an NP; boundary changed only on **National Board for Wildlife** advice.
- **Not AP:** Mrugavani NP, Kasu Brahmananda Reddy NP, Eturunagaram and **Manjira WLS** (Telangana); **Chinnar WLS** (Kerala); **Bhainsrorgarh WLS** (Rajasthan); Periyar (Kerala), Gir, Bandipur, Mudumalai.

=== Tiger, Elephant and Biosphere Reserves
# Nagarjunasagar–Srisailam Tiger Reserve (NSTR)
- Nallamala hills along the **Krishna**; AP + Telangana; declared **1983**; **3,727.50 sq km** – **India's largest tiger reserve** by area and by **core/critical tiger habitat** (Manas, Pakke, Periyar are smaller).
- The **Nagarjunasagar–Srisailam WLS** was renamed **Rajiv Gandhi WLS**; it is the core. AP core: Nandyal, Prakasam, Palnadu.
- Srisailam circle; NTCA core-buffer model; **tigers 63** (excl. 7 cubs, APSES 2024-25; AITE 2022: 63–67); Chenchus serve as protection watchers.
- AP's only tiger reserve; NSTR is **not** a biosphere reserve.
# Elephant reserve
- **Koundinya WLS + Rayala Elephant Reserve** (Anantapuramu circle) – **Koundinya is AP's only sanctuary with Asian elephants**; nearest city **Chittoor**.
- Elephants came into **Palamaner** in the **1980s** because of **drought in the Hosur–Dharmapuri reserve forests and Bannerghatta NP**.
# Seshachalam Biosphere Reserve
- **AP's first and only BR**, notified **2010**; **4,755.997 sq km**; Tirupati and Kurnool circles; Seshachalam (Tirumala) hills – the area of the **Sri Venkateswara** NP and WLS.
- Endemic **red sanders**; fauna incl. **slender loris**, golden gecko; 1,756 flowering plants. Not "Sri Venkateswara BR".

- **Wood stork** and **spot-billed pelican** are wetland birds – **not** indicator species of SV NP.
- "**Rosewood** mostly found in" in the APPSC 2017 paper means **red sanders** (ఎర్రచందనం) → **Seshachalam**.
- APSES biodiversity highlights: red sanders, tiger, Indian bison, GIB, golden gecko – not the one-horned rhino.

| Site | District | Feature |
|---|---|---|

- **Flamingo Festival**: annual festival at **Pulicat Lake and Nelapattu**.
- **Telineelapuram mass deaths:** **spot-billed pelican, *Pelecanus philippensis*** (not *P. onocrotalus*, white stork *Ciconia ciconia* or common crane *Grus grus*).
- **Kolleru = AP's only Ramsar site**; India has **103** Ramsar sites (Oct 2026: Sukhna Wetland, Sauj Jheel added) – none new in AP.
- Chilika is in Odisha; Bhavanam is not a major lake.
