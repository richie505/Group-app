=== One Nation One Election – Constitution (129th Amendment) Bill, 2024
> APPSC AEE asked the **aim of the 129th Amendment Bill, 2024**: **simultaneous elections to the Lok Sabha and State Assemblies** – not cooperatives (97th Amendment, 2011) or NCBC (102nd Amendment, 2018).
# The Bill
- **Constitution (129th Amendment) Bill, 2024** = **One Nation One Election**: **inserts Article 82A**; amends **Article 83** (duration of Parliament) and **Article 172** (duration of State Legislatures).
- **Appointed date** notified by the **President** (first sitting of the new Lok Sabha).
- After **mid-term (early) dissolution**, election only for the **unexpired term**.
- **Local bodies are excluded** from the Bill.
- Companion: **Union Territories Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2024** - **Delhi, Puducherry, J&K**.
- Introduced **17 December 2024**; **JPC chair P.P. Chaudhary**.
- **[13 Feb 2026]** Former CJIs told the JPC the Bill does **not violate the Basic Structure**.
- **[19 Mar 2026]** JPC term extended to the **last week of the Monsoon Session 2026**; Bill still pending.
> **129th** Amendment Bill = One Nation One Election (JPC P.P. Chaudhary); **131st** = Lok Sabha **850 seats** (defeated).
# History of simultaneous elections
- Held together in **1952, 1957, 1962, 1967**; cycle broken in **1968-69**; **4th Lok Sabha dissolved 1970**, elections **1971**.
| Body | Year | View |
|---|---|---|
| Election Commission | 1983 | Recommended return |
| Law Commission 170th Report (Justice B.P. Jeevan Reddy) | 1999 | Supported simultaneous elections |
| Parliamentary Standing Committee | 2015 | Burden of the MCC |
| NITI Aayog paper | 2017 | Phased model |
| Law Commission draft | 2018 | Constructive no-confidence |
| High-Level Committee (chair Ram Nath Kovind) | 2023-24 | Phased; local bodies within 100 days |
- Earlier electoral-reform committees: **Dinesh Goswami (1990)**, **Indrajit Gupta (1998)**.
# Numbers and concerns
- **CMS**: 2024 election cost about **₹1.35 lakh crore** (vs **₹60,000 crore** in 2019).
- **Kesavananda Bharati (1973)**: free and fair elections and parliamentary democracy = **basic structure**.
- **Constructive no-confidence motion** - borrowed from **Germany**.
- **73rd/74th Amendments**: local elections by **State Election Commissions**.

=== Disaster Framework and the 16th Finance Commission
- **[7 Aug 2026]** **MHA** added **heatwave and lightning** to the **Notified Natural Calamities** in the **SDRF/NDRF operational guidelines (2026-2031)**, on the advice of the **16th Finance Commission**; the list rises **12 to 14**.
- Full list (14): **cyclone, drought, earthquake, fire, flood, tsunami, hailstorm, landslide, avalanche, cloudburst, pest attack, cold wave, heatwave, lightning**.
- Effect: victims get relief under **SDRF/NDRF norms**.
- **Disaster Management Act, 2005**: nodal **MHA**.
- **SDRF**: main **State** fund for notified disasters; **Centre and States contribute** in set ratios. **NDRF** (Fund): **additional aid** for disasters of **severe nature**.
- **16th Finance Commission**: **constitutional** body (Art 280); chair **Arvind Panagariya**; award **2026-27 to 2030-31**; States' share of the divisible pool kept at **41%**.
> Notified calamities: 12 + heatwave + lightning = 14

=== Regulators and Health Laws – FSSAI, PCPNDT, ASF Vaccine
# FSSAI crackdown on misleading food claims
- **150+ notices** over misleading ads, false claims and labelling; scope: labels, ads, **e-commerce** listings, delivery platforms.
- **Mondelez India** withdrew health and nutrient-comparison claims on **Bournvita**.
- **May 2025**: FSSAI advised stopping the use of **"100%"** on labels.
# PCPNDT Act and portable ultrasound
- Enacted **1994** as the Pre-Natal Diagnostic Techniques (Regulation and Prevention of Misuse) Act; **2003 amendment** added **pre-conception** - renamed **PCPNDT (Prohibition of Sex Selection) Act**.
- Rules: **registration before buying** machines; machine must **stay at its registered location**; **no sex disclosure**.
- Debate: **high-frequency linear probes** (breast, thyroid) **cannot image foetal sex** - reform sought for community cancer screening with AI.
- **2025**: illegal sex-determination network busted in **Karnataka**.
# First indigenous African Swine Fever vaccine
- **"MA-104 Cell Line-Based Live Attenuated African Swine Fever Vaccine"** - **India's first indigenous ASF vaccine**.
- Developed by **ICAR-NIHSAD, Bhopal** (est. **1985**); dedicated by **Shivraj Singh Chouhan**.
- ASF: viral disease of **pigs**; **does not infect humans**.
