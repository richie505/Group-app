=== RTI, Courts and Equity Rules
# CIC: BCCI not a public authority
- **[18 May 2026]** **CIC**: **BCCI is not a "public authority"** under **Section 2(h), RTI Act 2005**.
- BCCI: **private society** under the **Tamil Nadu Societies Registration Act, 1975**; not substantially controlled or financed by government.
- **2018**: CIC (Info Commissioner **M. Sridhar Acharyulu**) had held BCCI a public authority - **set aside by the Madras HC**.
| Case / report | Holding |
|---|---|
| Zee Telefilms v. Union of India (2005) | BCCI not "State" under Art 12 |
| Thalappalam Service Coop. Bank (2013) | "Control" = substantial control |
| BCCI v. Cricket Association of Bihar (2016) | Lodha Committee reforms; BCCI subject to Art 226 review |
| Law Commission 275th Report (2018) | Recommended BCCI under RTI |
- **National Sports Governance Act, 2025**: govt-funded sports bodies under RTI **only to the extent of those funds**.
- **RTI Section 24 / Second Schedule**: security bodies exempt (e.g. **NSCS, Assam Rifles**) except **corruption and human rights**.
# SC: education in mother tongue
- Bench **Justice Vikram Nath and Justice Sandeep Mehta**; petition to include **Rajasthani** in a teacher recruitment exam syllabus.
- Thinking and expressing in one's own language = part of the **right to identity**.
- **Article 350A**: mother-tongue instruction at the **primary stage** for linguistic-minority children; **NEP 2020** favours the mother tongue.
# UGC Equity Regulations 2026 stayed

- Caste discrimination defined as against **SC, ST, OBC**; **no explicit penalty for false complaints**.
