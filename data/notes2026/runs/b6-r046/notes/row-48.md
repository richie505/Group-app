=== Security and Internal Governance – PRAHAAR, NATGRID, NIDMS
# PRAHAAR – India's First National Counter-Terrorism Policy
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Released by | MHA, 23 February 2026 |
| FIRST | India's first-ever national counter-terrorism policy |
| Drafting announced | November 2024 |
| Earlier policy | National Policy and Action Plan for LWE (2015) |
| Spur | Pahalgam attack (22 April 2025) – NIA–State coordination |
- **Seven pillars**: **Prevention; Response; Aggregating internal capacities; Human rights and rule-of-law processes; Countering radicalisation; Alignment with global efforts; Recovery and resilience** (whole-of-society).
- **Zero tolerance** – deny terrorists **funds, weapons, safe havens**.
- Threats on land, **air (drones)**, water; critical sectors – power, railways, aviation, ports, defence, space, atomic energy.
- **CBRNED** = **Chemical, Biological, Radiological, Nuclear, Explosive, Digital**.
- Recognises **cyber-attacks by nation-states, crypto funding, dark web**.
- Does **not link terrorism to any religion, ethnicity or civilisation**.
# NATGRID
- **National Intelligence Grid** expanded into population-scale surveillance; integrated with **National Population Register (NPR)**.
- Origin: response to **26/11 Mumbai attacks (2008)** – to integrate fragmented intelligence databases.
- Approved by **executive action** – **no statutory/parliamentary backing**.
- Right to Privacy: fundamental right – **K.S. Puttaswamy (2017)**.
# NIDMS
- **NIDMS** (Jan 2026) – India's **first national IED data platform**; launched by **Amit Shah** at **NSG garrison, Manesar**; built by the **National Bomb Data Centre (NSG)**.
> NATGRID is an **executive** creation with no statute; PRAHAAR is a **policy** (MHA), not an Act.

=== Census, Economic Census and Caste Surveys
# Census 2027
- **2026–27**: **16th decennial Census**, **two-phase**; **first fully digital** with **self-enumeration**; separate head for **transgender-headed households**.
- **Caste enumeration** for the first time since **1931**.
- Conducted by the **Registrar General and Census Commissioner (RGI)**, under **MHA**; ad-hoc organisation till 1951; gazette notified.

# Health
- **PMBJP**: generics **50–80%** cheaper; ~2,110 medicines, 315 surgical items; target **20,000 Janaushadhi Kendras by 31 March 2026**; launched 2008, revamped 2015; run by **PMBI** (est. 2010, Chemicals and Fertilizers ministry).

- **World Malaria Report 2025** (WHO, December): Asia-Pacific a bright spot – **10 of 17** endemic countries declined; cases **9.6 million (2023) → 8.9 million (2024)**; big drop in Pakistan; historic lows 2nd year in **Cambodia, Lao PDR, Vietnam**; Greater Mekong Subregion tackled drug resistance.
- India: zero indigenous malaria cases by **2027** (global 2030) – off track; five States + Northeast = ~80% of burden; artemisinin resistance not established; **oral artemisinin monotherapy banned**.
- Malaria: protozoan **Plasmodium**; vector female **Anopheles**; species P. falciparum (deadliest), P. vivax, P. malariae, P. ovale – India mainly **falciparum and vivax**; treatment ACT; vaccines **RTS,S and R21** (prioritised for Africa). TB is caused by Mycobacterium.
- **Cellulitis**: bacterial infection of skin and subcutaneous tissue (**Streptococcus, Staphylococcus**); legs, feet; not contagious; antibiotics.
- **Spina bifida**: types **Myelomeningocele** (most severe), Meningocele, Spina Bifida Occulta (mildest); prevented by **folic acid** in early pregnancy.
