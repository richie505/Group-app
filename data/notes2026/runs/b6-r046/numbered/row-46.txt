## SUB 0 | One Nation One Election – Constitution (129th Amendment) Bill, 2024
[0:0] > APPSC AEE asked the **aim of the 129th Amendment Bill, 2024**: **simultaneous elections to the Lok Sabha and State Assemblies** – not cooperatives (97th Amendment, 2011) or NCBC (102nd Amendment, 2018).
[0:1] # The Bill
[0:2] - **Constitution (129th Amendment) Bill, 2024** = **One Nation One Election**: **inserts Article 82A**; amends **Article 83** (duration of Parliament) and **Article 172** (duration of State Legislatures).
[0:3] - **Appointed date** notified by the **President** (first sitting of the new Lok Sabha).
[0:4] - After **mid-term (early) dissolution**, election only for the **unexpired term**.
[0:5] - **Local bodies are excluded** from the Bill.
[0:6] - Companion: **Union Territories Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2024** - **Delhi, Puducherry, J&K**.
[0:7] - Introduced **17 December 2024**; **JPC chair P.P. Chaudhary**.
[0:8] - **[13 Feb 2026]** Former CJIs told the JPC the Bill does **not violate the Basic Structure**.
[0:9] - **[19 Mar 2026]** JPC term extended to the **last week of the Monsoon Session 2026**; Bill still pending.
[0:10] > **129th** Amendment Bill = One Nation One Election (JPC P.P. Chaudhary); **131st** = Lok Sabha **850 seats** (defeated).
[0:11] # History of simultaneous elections
[0:12] - Held together in **1952, 1957, 1962, 1967**; cycle broken in **1968-69**; **4th Lok Sabha dissolved 1970**, elections **1971**.
[0:13] TABLE head: Body | Year | View
  [0:13.0] Election Commission | 1983 | Recommended return
  [0:13.1] Law Commission 170th Report (Justice B.P. Jeevan Reddy) | 1999 | Supported simultaneous elections
  [0:13.2] Parliamentary Standing Committee | 2015 | Burden of the MCC
  [0:13.3] NITI Aayog paper | 2017 | Phased model
  [0:13.4] Law Commission draft | 2018 | Constructive no-confidence
  [0:13.5] High-Level Committee (chair Ram Nath Kovind) | 2023-24 | Phased; local bodies within 100 days
[0:14] - Earlier electoral-reform committees: **Dinesh Goswami (1990)**, **Indrajit Gupta (1998)**.
[0:15] # Numbers and concerns
[0:16] - **CMS**: 2024 election cost about **₹1.35 lakh crore** (vs **₹60,000 crore** in 2019).
[0:17] - **Kesavananda Bharati (1973)**: free and fair elections and parliamentary democracy = **basic structure**.
[0:18] - **Constructive no-confidence motion** - borrowed from **Germany**.
[0:19] - **73rd/74th Amendments**: local elections by **State Election Commissions**.

## SUB 2 | Disaster Framework and the 16th Finance Commission
[2:0] - **[7 Aug 2026]** **MHA** added **heatwave and lightning** to the **Notified Natural Calamities** in the **SDRF/NDRF operational guidelines (2026-2031)**, on the advice of the **16th Finance Commission**; the list rises **12 to 14**.
