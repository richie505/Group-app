=== Jai Andhra 1972–73, Six-Point Formula and 32nd Amendment
# Jai Andhra
- **1972–73**, Coastal Andhra and Rayalaseema – **against the Mulki Rules**; demand: repeal or a **separate Andhra State**.
- **Main / immediate cause:** the **Supreme Court judgment of October 1972** upholding the Mulki Rules – not taxes, water or administration.
- Main issue: Andhras treated as "outsiders" in Hyderabad, a capital built with the whole State's revenue.
- Leaders: **Kakani Venkataratnam**, **B.V. Subba Reddy**, **Tenneti Viswanatham**; students the core.
- **CM: P.V. Narasimha Rao** (sworn in 30 Sep 1971); resigned **18 Jan 1973**.
- **Five-Point Formula – 27 Nov 1972** (PM Indira Gandhi): Mulki rules in twin cities till **1977**, Telangana till **1980**; **1 in 3** Secretariat posts to Telangana locals; first two gazetted posts localised.
- Separate Andhra **flag hoisted 10 Dec 1972**.
- **President's Rule: 18 Jan – 10 Dec 1973** (~11 months) – the **first** in Andhra Pradesh and the **longest**; imposed because of the Jai Andhra agitation.
- **Jalagam Vengala Rao** CM from **10 Dec 1973**.
- **Ended by the Six-Point Formula** (21 Sep 1973); agitation called off **1 Oct 1973** – not by repression, arrests or mere Mulki abolition.
# Six-Point Formula
| No. | Clause | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mulki Rules and Telangana Regional Committee abolished | TRC dissolved **1 Jan 1974** |
| 2 | Local preference in direct recruitment; tribunal for service disputes | Local cadres, 6 zones, AP Administrative Tribunal |
| 3 | Employees' grievances committee | — |
| 4 | Backward-area development | State Planning Commission with local sub-committees |
| 5 | Local preference in admissions | Central University, Hyderabad (Art 371E) |
| 6 | Constitutional amendment | 32nd Amendment, Presidential Order 1975 |
- Approved **18 Dec 1973**; Mulki Rules replaced by the **zonal system**.
# 32nd Amendment, 1973
- Gave the Six-Point Formula constitutional backing: inserted **Art 371D** and **Art 371E**; in force **1 July 1974**.
- It is the **32nd** Amendment **Act** – some NextGen pages say "33rd", which was the **Bill's** number.
- **371D**: equitable opportunities in jobs and education; local cadres by **Presidential Order**; **Administrative Tribunal**. **371E**: **Central University** in the State.
> Jai Andhra 1972 (SC Mulki verdict; CM PVNR) → PR 18 Jan–10 Dec 1973 → Six-Point Formula → 32nd Amendment (371D, 371E).

# AP capital
| Body | Chairman | Mandate |
|---|---|---|

- Hyderabad's joint-capital term ended **June 2024**.
- **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Act, 2026** amends Sec 5: **Amaravati sole, permanent capital** w.e.f. **2 June 2024**, after AP Assembly resolution of **28 Mar 2026**.
# Districts
- AP districts: **13 (2014) → 26 (4 Apr 2022) → 28 (31 Dec 2025)**, new **Polavaram** (HQ Rampachodavaram) and **Markapuram**.
- 2022: draft gazette notification for 13 new districts under **Sec 3(5) of the AP Districts (Formation) Act** on **25 January 2022**; districts started 4 April 2022.
- **AP Districts (Formation) Act, 1974:** legal basis for forming new districts and altering boundaries.
- Governor **S. Abdul Nazeer**, CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu** (as of 2026).
# Polavaram and rivers
- **Polavaram** (first proposed as **Ramapada Sagaram** by Sir Shonthi Venkataramamurthy) is a multipurpose project on the **Godavari**; national project (Sec 90).
- **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Ordinance, 2014** ("Polavaram ordinance", May 2014) amended **Section 3** to move **7 Khammam mandals** to AP.
- Villages moved: **APPSC key 327 revenue villages**; press reports give about **205 (ordinance) / 211 (Bill, July 2014)** – figure disputed.
- Most contentious rivers: **Krishna and Godavari**; **Apex Council** chaired by the Union Water Resources Minister with both CMs.
- **Polavaram–Banakacherla** link: Telangana says it violates APRA and the **GWDT Award (1980)** (Jul 2025).
- **Special Category Status**: PM's assurance in the **Rajya Sabha**, not in the Act.
- State symbols after bifurcation: flower **Jasmine**, tree neem, bird rose-ringed parakeet, animal blackbuck.
> Sivaramakrishnan = AP capital (Sec 6); Polavaram on the Godavari, ordinance amended Sec 3; districts 13 → 26 → 28.

=== New States After 1956 and Article 3
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| Article 2 | Admission/establishment of new states from outside India |
| **Article 3** | Parliament may by law **form a new state**, increase/diminish area, alter boundaries or name |
| Article 4 | Such a law is not an amendment under Article 368 |
- Bill only on the **President's recommendation**; President refers it to the **affected state legislature for its views** (time limit added by the **5th Amendment, 1955**); views **not binding** – no concurrence or two-thirds majority; no reference for a UT; passed by **simple majority**.
- APRA 2014 made under **Articles 3 and 4**.
| Year | State(s) | Law / basis |
|---|---|---|
| 1 May 1960 | Maharashtra, **Gujarat** | Bombay Reorganisation Act 1960 (Samyukta Maharashtra, Mahagujarat) |
| **1 Dec 1963** | **Nagaland** (16th state; Statehood Day 1 Dec) | State of Nagaland Act 1962; **first NE state carved out of Assam** |
| 1 Nov 1966 | Haryana (17th) | Punjab Reorganisation Act 1966 – **Hukam Singh** Parliamentary Committee (Punjabi Suba), Shah Commission (boundaries); Chandigarh UT and shared capital |
| 25 Jan 1971 | Himachal Pradesh (18th) | State of HP Act 1970 |
| 1972 | Manipur, Tripura, Meghalaya | North-Eastern Areas (Reorganisation) Act 1971 |
| 1975 | Sikkim (22nd) | **36th Amendment** |
| 1987 | Mizoram (23rd), Arunachal (24th), **Goa (25th)** | Goa separated from UT of Goa, Daman and Diu |
| Nov 2000 | Chhattisgarh (1 Nov), Uttarakhand (9 Nov), Jharkhand (15 Nov) | Tribal/regional backwardness |
| 2 June 2014 | Telangana – 29th | AP Reorganisation Act 2014 |
| 31 Oct 2019 | UTs J&K and Ladakh | J&K Reorganisation Act 2019 (after Art 370 order) |
- **Puducherry** is a **Union Territory** (since 1962, 14th Amendment), not a state.
- Present count: **28 States and 8 UTs** (after the Dadra & Nagar Haveli–Daman & Diu merger, **Jan 2020**); **Ladakh has no legislature**.
- Order: Haryana (1966) → Fourth general elections (1967) → Meghalaya, Tripura full states (1972) → Mysore renamed Karnataka (1973).
- Gujarat became a state in **1960**, not 1996; Sikkim by the 36th Amendment, not a "State of Sikkim Act, 1987".

=== Mulki Rules and Jai Telangana 1969
- **1888**, Hyderabad State: locals to get State posts; outsider only with the **Prime Minister's (Diwan's)** permission. **1919 Nizam's Farman**: outsider needed the **Nizam's personal permission**.
- **Mulki** = continuous residence of **15 years** (Nizam's rules); **12 years** (Gentlemen's Agreement, 1956).
| Year | Event | Stance |
|---|---|---|
| 1956 | Gentlemen's Agreement – safeguards | Both regions |
| 1957 | Public Employment (Requirement as to Residence) Act | Residence condition for Telangana posts |
| 1969 | Jai Telangana | Telangana – **for** Mulki rules |
| 1972 | Jai Andhra | Andhra – **against** Mulki rules |
| 1973 | Six-Point Formula; 32nd Amendment | Compromise |
| 1975 | Presidential Order – local cadres | Implementation |
| 1985 | GO 610 | Telangana grievance |
| 2001–04 | Girglani Commission | Telangana grievance |
- Chronology PYQ: Gentlemen's Agreement (1956) → Jai Telangana (1969) → Jai Andhra (1972) → 32nd Amendment (1973) → GO 610 (1985) → Girglani Report (2004).
# Causes of 1969
- Failure to implement the **Gentlemen's Agreement**; feeling that **Telangana surpluses** were diverted; **competition for jobs** from Andhras – non-locals recruited, local posts reclassified.
- Flashpoint **1968**: regional sub-committee extended five years.
- **Not a 1969 cause:** the AP High Court view that the Mulki Rules had lapsed came in **1970** (reversed by the SC in 1972).
# Course and TPS
- Began as a **student strike at Khammam, January 1969**; **first hunger strike** – student **Ravindranath**; police firings killed **over 360** – Telangana martyrs.
- **Prof. K. Jayashankar** (former VC, **Kakatiya University**) – ideologue.
- **Telangana Praja Samithi** – formed **February 1969**; convener **A. Madan Mohan**; president **Marri Chenna Reddy**; symbol spade.
- **1971 Lok Sabha election**: won **10 of 14** seats; **merged with Congress (Sept 1971)** after agreement with Indira Gandhi; opponents formed the **Complete TPS** under **B. Satyanarayana Reddy**.
- CM in 1969: **Kasu Brahmananda Reddy** (resigned 1971); **P.V. Narasimha Rao** sworn in **30 Sep 1971**.
# Eight-Point Formula and court verdicts
- **Eight-Point Formula** – **11 April 1969**, PM **Indira Gandhi**: committee under a Supreme Court judge, surplus funds, supervision of Telangana schemes, PM's **six-monthly review**.
| Committee | Task |
|---|---|
| Bhargava (Justice Vashist Bhargava) | Telangana's revenue surpluses |
| Lalit (Kumar Lalit) | Official accounting of surpluses |
| Wanchoo (Justice K.N. Wanchoo) | Legal opinion on job safeguards |
- **Court verdicts on the Mulki Rules:**
| Year | Case | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1969 | A.V.S. Narasimha Rao v. State of AP | SC struck down the Telangana residence clause of the 1957 Act – under **Art 16(3)** only **Parliament** sets residence conditions |
| Oct 1972 | Director of Industries v. V. Venkata Reddy | SC upheld the old Mulki Rules → triggered Jai Andhra |
> 8 – 5 – 6 = 1969 – 1972 – 1973 (Eight-, Five-, Six-Point formulas); 1969 Telangana for Mulki, 1972 Andhra against.

=== Presidential Order 1975, GO 610 and Girglani
- **AP Public Employment (Organisation of Local Cadres and Regulation of Direct Recruitment) Order, 1975**: **6 zones** – **I–III Coastal Andhra**, **IV Rayalaseema**, **V–VI Telangana** (**Hyderabad in Zone VI**).
| Zone | Districts (undivided AP) |
|---|---|
| II | East Godavari, West Godavari, Krishna |
| VI | Hyderabad, Ranga Reddy, Nizamabad, Mahbubnagar, Medak, Nalgonda |
- **GO 610** – **30 Dec 1985**, **NTR** government: repatriate non-locals appointed against the 1975 Order.
- **Girglani Commission** – one-man (**J.M. Girglani**), set up **2001** by **Chandrababu Naidu**; examined violations of **GO 610 and the 1975 Presidential Order**; report **2004**: large-scale violations, especially **Zone VI**.
- After 2014: AP kept **Zones I–IV** for its 13 districts. **AP Public Employment Order-2025**: **6 zones and 2 multi-zones** (Cabinet Apr 2026; mapping May 2026).
> GO 610 = NTR 1985; Girglani = Naidu 2001–04; Hyderabad in Zone VI.
