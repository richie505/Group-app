=== Anti-arrack (Anti-Sara) Movement
- Began in **Dubagunta** village, **Nellore** district – launched by **rural women in 1991** (spread statewide 1992–93); leader **Dubagunta (Vardhineni) Rosamma**.
- Name: **Anti-Sara** ("sara" = arrack) movement – not Jai Andhra, anti-dowry or Nava Samaj; not Renigunta, Domalamadugu or Rajupalem.
- Grew out of **"Aksharajyothi" adult-literacy classes** (story "Seethamma katha"): women discussed men's **arrack drinking, debt, domestic violence**.
- **Methods:** women **closed arrack shops** and **stopped arrack auctions** with broomsticks and chilli powder. Legislative elections were **NOT** a method.
- One of India's **most successful women-led movements**.
| Step | Date |
|---|---|
| Arrack prohibited in AP (CM Kotla Vijayabhaskara Reddy) | **1 October 1993** |
| Total prohibition (NTR) | 1995 |
| Prohibition lifted | 1997 |
- **1930s precursor:** **Kallur Subbarao** led **anti-liquor and Swadeshi movements** in the **Dattamandalas (Rayalaseema)**.
> Dubagunta (Nellore), 1991, Rosamma → arrack ban 1 Oct 1993.

=== Backward Class Commissions and Caste Data
| Commission | Set up | Chairman | Key point |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First Backward Classes Commission** | **1953** (29 Jan, Art. 340) | **Kaka Kalelkar** | Reported 1955; little practical effect |
| Second (**Mandal**) Commission | 1 January 1979 | B.P. Mandal | Report 31 Dec 1980; **27% OBC reservation in government jobs**; implemented 7 Aug 1990 (V.P. Singh) |
| Commission | Year | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| Anantharaman Commission | 1968–70 | First AP BC Commission; grouped BCs into A–D |
| Muralidhar Rao Commission | Took charge 27 Jan 1982 | Examined whether minorities were in the BC list; BC, ST and religious-group populations combined at 29.08% |
- **1921 Census (Andhra districts, as asked):** forward castes **29.9%**, backward castes **46.1%**, scheduled castes **17.0%**.
- Andhra had 86 BC castes, with a separate Telangana list (NextGen).
> First national = Kaka Kalelkar (1953); Mandal = 27% (1979–80); first AP = Anantharaman.

=== Dalit Movement – Adi-Andhra to Madiga Dandora
| Movement / body | Leader | Place | Year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adi-Andhra conference | Bhagya Reddy Varma | Machilipatnam | 1919 |
| Adi-Andhra Mahasabha | Kusuma Dharmanna | Vizianagaram | 1936 |
| AP Dalit Mahasabha | **Katti Padmarao**; **Bojja Tarakam** also a founder | – | 1985 |
| MRPS (Madiga Dandora) | **Manda Krishna Madiga** | Eedumudi, Prakasam | 7 July 1994 |
- Adi-Andhra leaders were inspired by **Phule's Satyashodhak Samaj (1873)** and **Periyar's Self-Respect Movement**.
| Event | Date | District |
|---|---|---|
| Karamchedu massacre | 17 July 1985 | Prakasam (now **Bapatla** district) |
| Chunduru massacre | 6 August 1991 | Guntur |
- After Karamchedu, Dalits **broke away from the Congress and Communist parties** and formed **independent Dalit Mahasabhas**.
- **MRPS demand:** **sub-categorisation of SC reservation**; Manda Krishna Madiga – **Padma Shri (2025)**.
- **S.R. Sankaran** (IAS) – welfare of Dalits and tribals; freed **bonded labourers (vetti)**, enforced the **Bonded Labour (Abolition) Act, 1976**; social welfare residential schools; Special Component Plan.
> Karamchedu 1985 (Prakasam) → Dalit Mahasabha 1985; Chunduru 1991 (Guntur); MRPS 1994.

=== Farmers' Movements and Bhu Poratam
| Person / event | Fact |
|---|---|
| N.G. Ranga | Indian Peasants' Institute, **Nidubrolu (1933)**; a founder of AIKS (1936) |
| All India Kisan Sabha | A mass organisation of the **CPI** (not the Congress) |
| Power tariff agitation (2000) | Left parties + farmers against electricity tariff hikes under power-sector reforms |
| Bashir Bagh firing | 28 August 2000, Hyderabad |
- **Bhu Poratam** ("land struggle"): launched in **Andhra Pradesh** by the **CPI(M)** in **2007** and joined by the **CPI** (not 1997) – to **occupy and distribute government and surplus land** to the landless; police firing at **Mudigonda (Khammam), 28 July 2007**.
> Bhu Poratam = AP, 2007, CPI + CPI(M).
