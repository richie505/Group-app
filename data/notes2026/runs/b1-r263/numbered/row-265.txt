## SUB 0 | Anti-arrack (Anti-Sara) Movement
[0:0] - Began in **Dubagunta** village, **Nellore** district – launched by **rural women in 1991** (spread statewide 1992–93); leader **Dubagunta (Vardhineni) Rosamma**.
[0:1] - Name: **Anti-Sara** ("sara" = arrack) movement – not Jai Andhra, anti-dowry or Nava Samaj; not Renigunta, Domalamadugu or Rajupalem.
[0:2] - Grew out of **"Aksharajyothi" adult-literacy classes** (story "Seethamma katha"): women discussed men's **arrack drinking, debt, domestic violence**.
[0:3] - **Methods:** women **closed arrack shops** and **stopped arrack auctions** with broomsticks and chilli powder. Legislative elections were **NOT** a method.
[0:4] - One of India's **most successful women-led movements**.
[0:5] TABLE head: Step | Date
  [0:5.0] Arrack prohibited in AP (CM Kotla Vijayabhaskara Reddy) | 1 October 1993
  [0:5.1] Total prohibition (NTR) | 1995
  [0:5.2] Prohibition lifted | 1997
[0:6] - **1930s precursor:** **Kallur Subbarao** led **anti-liquor and Swadeshi movements** in the **Dattamandalas (Rayalaseema)**.
[0:7] > Dubagunta (Nellore), 1991, Rosamma → arrack ban 1 Oct 1993.
