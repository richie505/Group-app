## SUB 1 | Naxalbari and Naxalite Groups
[1:0] - **Naxalbari peasant uprising**: **March–May 1967**, Siliguri sub-division, **Darjeeling** district, West Bengal; leaders **Charu Majumdar** and **Kanu Sanyal**; slogan **"Land to the tiller"**.
[1:1] - **"Ballot to Bullet"**: rejected the **CPI(M)'s parliamentary path**; causes – **failed land reforms**, exploitation by landlords and moneylenders.
[1:2] - **1967 Andhra extremist wing** split from CPI(M): **Tarimela Nagi Reddy, Chandra Pulla Reddy**, Kolla Venkaiah, Nagabhushana Patnaik.
[1:3] TABLE head: Group | Founder(s) | Year | Base
  [1:3.0] CPI(ML) | Charu Majumdar (with Kanu Sanyal) | 1969 | West Bengal
  [1:3.1] People's War Group (PWG) | Kondapalli Seetharamaiah | 1980 | Andhra Pradesh (North Telangana)
  [1:3.2] CPI (Maoist) | PWG + MCC merger | 2004 | Red Corridor
[1:4] - **CPI(ML)** saw the Indian state as **semi-feudal**; Chinese-model protracted guerrilla war.
[1:5] - **Kishanji** (Mallojula Koteswara Rao) – Maoist leader killed by security forces in **November 2011** – not Charu Majumdar (died 1972) or Kanu Sanyal (died 2010).
[1:6] > 1967 Naxalbari | 1969 CPI(ML) | 1980 PWG | 1992 PWG banned | 2004 CPI (Maoist) | 2011 Kishanji killed.

## SUB 2 | Government Responses – Greyhounds, Commissions, Talks
[2:0] TABLE head: Response | Year | Key fact
  [2:0.0] Bhargava Commission | 1977 | Justice Vashist Bhargava – inquired into Emergency "encounter" killings of Naxalites
  [2:0.1] Greyhounds | 1989 | Elite anti-Naxal special police force of the AP Police – not a dog squad
  [2:0.2] Ban | 1992 | PWG banned in AP
  [2:0.3] Surrender policy | 1998–2001 | Under DGP H.J. Dora, over 1,200 cadres surrendered
  [2:0.4] Peace talks | October 2004 | Government–Maoist talks in Hyderabad (YSR government); S.R. Sankaran chief negotiator
[2:1] - APRA 2014 **Sec 9**: Greyhound Training Centre at Hyderabad went to Telangana; Centre to help AP set up its own – **Anandapuram** (near Visakhapatnam); **Greyhound and OCTOPUS** forces divided.
[2:2] - Anandapuram base secures the LWE corridor of **Alluri Sitharama Raju (ASR)** and **Parvathipuram Manyam** districts (north coastal AP, Odisha border).
[2:3] > Greyhounds = police special force (1989); Bhargava = Emergency encounters (1977).
