## SUB 0 | Articles 2, 3, 4 – Constitutional Procedure
[263:0:0] TABLE head: Article | Provision
  [263:0:0.0] Article 2 | Admission/establishment of new states from outside India (e.g. Sikkim, 36th Amendment)
  [263:0:0.1] Article 3 | Parliament may by law form a new state, increase/diminish area, alter boundaries or name
  [263:0:0.2] Article 4 | Such a law is not an amendment under Article 368
[263:0:1] - Bill only on the **President's recommendation**.
[263:0:2] - President refers it to the **affected state legislature** for views within a set time (time limit added by the **5th Amendment, 1955**).
[263:0:3] - Views **not binding**; no fresh reference for each amendment to the Bill; no reference for a UT.
[263:0:4] - Passed by **simple majority**.
[263:0:5] - **APRA 2014** was made under **Articles 3 and 4** – not Article 2.
[263:0:6] - Present count: **28 States and 8 UTs** (after J&K reorganisation 2019 and the Dadra & Nagar Haveli–Daman & Diu merger, **January 2020**).
[263:0:7] - **Puducherry:** a **Union Territory** (not a state) with its own legislature under the **Government of Union Territories Act, 1963**.

## SUB 2 | Mulki Rules – Origin and Chronology
[263:2:0] # Origin and Meaning
[263:2:1] - **1888**, Hyderabad State: locals to get State posts; outsider only with the **Prime Minister's (Diwan's)** permission.
[263:2:2] - **1919 Nizam's Farman**: outsider needed the **Nizam's personal permission**.
[263:2:3] - **Mulki** = person with a fixed period of continuous residence: **15 years** (Nizam's rules); **12 years** (Gentlemen's Agreement, 1956).
[263:2:4] # Chronology
[263:2:5] TABLE head: Year | Event | Stance
  [263:2:5.0] 1956 | Gentlemen's Agreement – domicile safeguards | Both regions
  [263:2:5.1] 1957 | Public Employment (Requirement as to Residence) Act | Residence condition for Telangana posts
  [263:2:5.2] 1969 | Jai Telangana | Telangana – for Mulki rules
  [263:2:5.3] 1972 | Jai Andhra | Andhra – against Mulki rules
  [263:2:5.4] 1973 | Six-Point Formula; 32nd Amendment | Compromise
  [263:2:5.5] 1975 | Presidential Order – local cadres | Implementation
  [263:2:5.6] 1985 | GO 610 | Telangana grievance
  [263:2:5.7] 2001–04 | Girglani Commission | Telangana grievance
[263:2:6] PYQ order: Jai Telangana → Jai Andhra → 32nd Amendment → GO 610 → Girglani Report.

## SUB 5 | Jai Andhra 1972–73
[263:5:0] - **Year 1972** (into 1973), Coastal Andhra and Rayalaseema – **against Mulki Rules**.
[263:5:1] - **Immediate cause**: SC judgment of **October 1972** upholding the Mulki Rules.
[263:5:2] - **Main issue**: Andhras treated as "outsiders" in Hyderabad, a capital built with the whole State's revenue; demand – repeal or a **separate Andhra State**.
[263:5:3] - **Five-Point Formula – 27 Nov 1972**: Mulki rules in twin cities till **1977** and Telangana till **1980**; **1 in 3** Secretariat posts to Telangana locals; first two gazetted posts localised; Asst. Surgeons and Asst. Engineers brought under Mulki rules. Indira ruled out a separate Andhra.
[263:5:4] - Separate Andhra **flag hoisted 10 Dec 1972**.
[263:5:5] - **CM**: **P.V. Narasimha Rao**; resigned **18 Jan 1973**.
[263:5:6] - **President's Rule**: **18 Jan – 10 Dec 1973** (~11 months) – **first in AP**, due to breakdown of law and order.
[263:5:7] - **Jalagam Vengala Rao** CM from **10 Dec 1973**.
[263:5:8] - **Ended by the Six-Point Formula** (21 Sep 1973); agitation called off **1 Oct 1973**.
[263:5:9] TABLE head: Leader | Role
  [263:5:9.0] Kakani Venkataratnam | Congress leader who spearheaded it
  [263:5:9.1] B.V. Subba Reddy | Congress leader in it
  [263:5:9.2] Tenneti Viswanatham | Prominent Andhra leader of the movement
  [263:5:9.3] Students | Core of the movement
[263:5:10] Jai Andhra ended by the **Six-Point Formula**, not repression or mere Mulki abolition.

[263:6:2] # 32nd Amendment, 1973
[263:6:3] - Inserted **Art 371D** and **Art 371E**; in force **1 July 1974** (APHQ 2019).
[263:6:4] - **371D**: equitable opportunities in jobs and education for different parts; local cadres, local areas by **Presidential Order**; **Administrative Tribunal** (out of High Court jurisdiction).
[263:6:5] - **371E**: Parliament may set up a **Central University** in the State.
[263:6:6] 32nd, not 33rd, Amendment.

## SUB 7 | Anti-arrack Movement
[265:7:0] # Origin and Course
[265:7:1] - Began **1992**, **Dubagunta** village, **Nellore** district; led by **rural women**; leader **Vardhineni (Dubagunta) Rosamma**.
