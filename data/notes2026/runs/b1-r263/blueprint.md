## 4. Settled conflicts
- **32nd vs 33rd Amendment:** NextGen Part 17 / Part 1 and previous-papers p.66 say "33rd"; Laxmikanth p.349 and NextGen previous-papers p.16 say the 32nd Amendment Act, 1973 inserted 371D/371E. Notes say 32nd and explain that 33rd was the Bill's number.
- **Anti-arrack start year:** NextGen Part 6 and Rocket say 1991; the old notes said 1992. Settled as launched 1991 at Dubagunta, spread statewide 1992–93 (thenewsminute.com, 2016); ban 1 Oct 1993.
- **Section 108:** removal-of-difficulties orders are barred after **three** years (Gazette text). The old notes gave no figure.
- **AP districts:** 13 → 26 (4 Apr 2022) → **28 (31 Dec 2025)**. The old notes' "28 (2025)" is confirmed.
- **327 villages:** this could not be verified. It is kept as the APPSC key with a ⚠ dispute line.
