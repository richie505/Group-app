- **Factual recall / pick the region** dominates (~70%): which region is a hotspot, where an endemic lives.
- **NOT / INCORRECT:** NTRUHS ×2 (90% loss; Andaman in Sundaland), FRO (Thar), UPPCS 2016 (Vindhyas), MPPSC 2023 (Naro Hills), FSO 2025 ×2 (Lilium not AP).
- **Statements / how-many:** IAS 2009, 2010, 2011, 2015, 2023; TSPSC 2023.
- **Assertion–reason:** Parthenium (moved).
- **AP linkage:** AP endemics (Kalanchoe, Pimpinella, Boswellia, Cycas beddomei, Golden Gecko); Eastern Ghats not a hotspot.
- **Repeats:** "Western Ghats is a hotspot" (~10), "Eastern Himalaya + Western Ghats" (×4), criteria 1-3-5 (×2), BirdLife (×2), lion-tailed macaque (×2), nocturnal fauna (×2), Lilium (×2), hangul (×2), UPSC 1996 (×2).
- **Old two-hotspot framing:** many State PSC keys still expect "Eastern Himalayas and Western Ghats"; the notes keep both the old two and the current four.
- **Heavy in the old notes, never asked:** BD Act 2023 amendment details, BMC/PBR counts, BHS list, ABS money (Red Sanders, Kani model), KBA/Global 200. These are kept short for the own MCQs.
