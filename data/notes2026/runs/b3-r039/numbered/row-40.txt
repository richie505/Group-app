## SUB 0 | Community Development and Early Rural Programmes (1948-1980)
[0:0] # Early community development
[0:1] - **Etawah Pilot Project:** **September 1948**, Meheva village near Etawah (UP); by **Lt. Col. Albert Mayer** (American town planner); UP Govt + Point-4 programme; the **first rural-development pilot project**.
[0:2] - **Community Development Programme:** **2 October 1952**; the **first rural development programme**; aim – overall development and **improving the standard of living** of rural people through people's participation; it **set the stage for Panchayati Raj**.
[0:3] - **National Extension Service:** **1953**; the **first programme of community development** (NextGen); CDP could not cover all India as funds were short.
[0:4] - **S.K. Dey:** **architect of community development** in India; first Cooperation and Panchayati Raj Minister.
[0:5] - **Balwantrai Mehta Committee:** reviewed CDP and NES; report **November 1957**; **democratic decentralisation** → Panchayati Raj (Rajasthan, then **Andhra Pradesh, 1959**).
[0:6] - **Gandhiji's approach:** **Samagra Gramseva** (constructive programme for the whole village).

## SUB 1 | Bharat Nirman (2005-2009)
[1:0] - **Launched December 2005** (2005-06) – a **rural infrastructure** programme; targets to be met by the end of **2009**.
[1:1] - **Six components:** **irrigation, drinking water, electrification (RGGVY), roads (PMGSY), housing (IAY) and rural telephony**.
[1:2] - **Irrigation component:** Accelerated Irrigation Benefits Programme, **inter-linking of river projects**, **repair, renovation and restoration of water bodies**; rehabilitation of people displaced by irrigation projects was NOT included.
[1:3] - **Drinking water:** **National Rural Drinking Water Programme** – renamed from **ARWSP (1972-73)** on **1 April 2009**; now **Jal Jeevan Mission** (2019).
[1:4] - **NOT in Bharat Nirman:** **education / Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan**, **NRLM**, agro-based industries, **rural hospitals**.
[1:5] - **Rajiv Gandhi Grameen Vidyutikaran Yojana:** **April 2005** – electrify all villages; subsumed in DDUGJY (2015). **Saubhagya** (2017, PM Sahaj Bijli Har Ghar Yojana) – **Ministry of Power**, every household electrified.
[1:6] - **National Food for Work Programme:** **14 November 2004** in the **150 most backward districts**; merged into NREGA (2006).
[1:7] > NOT angle: SSA, NRLM, rural hospitals, agro-industries, education are outside the six.
[1:8] ~~ Sources: Ramesh Singh Indian Economy 10th ed. part 05 p.13, p.32 (Bharat Nirman rural infrastructure; roads one of its six components); app notes 3:40 sub 0, 1
## SUB 2 | PMGSY – Rural Roads
[2:0] - **Launched 25 December 2000** (Ministry of Rural Development) – the earliest of PMGSY, DDU-GKY (2014), SAGY (2014) and DAY (2014-16).
[2:1] - **Objective:** a **single all-weather road** connecting eligible unconnected habitations to the main road network – **develop the road network to villages** / community life in villages not well connected.
[2:2] - **Target year:** connect habitations of **1,000+ by 2003** and **500+ by 2007** (end of the Tenth Plan) – the "full connectivity" target year asked = **2007**.
[2:3] TABLE head: Area | Population norm
  [2:3.0] Plains | 500+
  [2:3.1] Hill States, NE, deserts, Schedule V/tribal areas | 250+
  [2:3.2] LWE-affected blocks | 100+ (not 250+)
  [2:3.3] Phase | Focus
  [2:3.4] PMGSY-I (2000) | New roads
  [2:3.5] PMGSY-II (2013) | Upgradation
  [2:3.6] PMGSY-III (2019) | Through routes and major rural links
[2:4] - **2016-17:** about **130 km of road built per day** (47,350 km in the year – a record).
[2:5] - **Meri Sadak** app (2015): citizens' complaints on PMGSY road quality.
[2:6] - **2014 initiative:** inclusion of **women SHGs in the transect walk** for road planning.
[2:7] - 99.6% of targeted eligible habitations connected (Aug 2026).

## SUB 3 | Rurbanisation – PURA and SPMRM
[3:0] # PURA
[3:1] - **PURA** = **Provision of Urban Amenities in Rural Areas** – **Dr A.P.J. Abdul Kalam** (explained on the eve of the **54th Republic Day, 2003**); elaborated in ***Target 3 Billion*** (co-author **Srijan Pal Singh**).
[3:2] - Aim: **economic upliftment of villages and poverty eradication**; **bridging the rural-urban divide**. Joint AP pilot: Miryalaguda (2004).
[3:3] # Shyama Prasad Mukherji Rurban Mission (SPMRM)
[3:4] - **Rurban** (not "Rural/Revival") Mission; launched **21 Feb 2016** (not 2015) at **Kurubhat, Rajnandgaon (Chhattisgarh)**; successor of PURA.
[3:5] - Aim: **300 Rurban clusters** – "a rural soul with urban amenities"; curb **distress migration**; make rural areas **socially, economically and physically sustainable** regions.
[3:6] - Objectives: **stimulate local economic development**, enhance basic services, create well-planned clusters (not literacy, sex ratio or free power).
[3:7] - Funding: **convergence** (MGNREGA, PMGSY, SBM, PMAY) + **Critical Gap Funding up to 30%**; Centre:State **60:40**, **90:10** for NE and Himalayan States.
[3:8] - Cluster: contiguous villages of **25,000-50,000** population (plain/coastal). **Rurban cluster** = rural areas with rising density, non-farm jobs, growing economic activity.
[3:9] - AP: **6 clusters** in the second phase (APPSC key); MoRD classified **359 AP panchayats** under Rurban clusters (Mar 2026).
[3:10] - SPMRM does NOT replace Gram Panchayats with urban local bodies.
[3:11] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP Economy STATIC-1 01 p.15 (PURA, Miryalaguda); OnlyIAS NCERT Economy 05 p.13 (SPMRM – MoRD, integrated infrastructure); app notes 3:40 sub 6

## SUB 5 | Rural Water and Sanitation – JJM and SBM-G
[5:0] # Sanitation
[5:1] TABLE head: Scheme | Fact
  [5:1.0] Nirmal Gram Puraskar (Oct 2003) | Award for open-defecation-free GPs, blocks, districts, States – boosted the Total Sanitation Campaign
  [5:1.1] Nirmal Bharat Abhiyan (2012) | Renewed Total Sanitation Campaign – ODF rural India, WASH approach (not slum toilets or housing)
  [5:1.2] SBM-G Phase II | Approved Feb 2020 for 2020-21 to 2024-25; ODF Plus – sustain ODF + solid and liquid waste management; ₹1,40,881 crore
  [5:1.3] 10-Year Rural Sanitation Strategy | 2019-2029; DDWS, Jal Shakti, 27 Sep 2019
[5:2] - **ODF sustainability convergence:** MGNREGS, NRDWP, **RuRBAN** and Atal Bhujal (not PMGSY or AMRUT).
[5:3] - **PMAY-G convergence:** SBM-G, NRDWP, DDUGJY, **Ujjwala** and MGNREGS – all.
[5:4] - Drinking Water and Sanitation: separate Ministry in **2011** → Department under **Ministry of Jal Shakti** since **June 2019**.
[5:5] # Jal Jeevan Mission
[5:6] - **Announced in Budget 2019-20**, launched **15 Aug 2019** (Jal Shakti): **Functional Household Tap Connection (FHTC)** to every rural household + schools, anganwadis, GP buildings, health centres – originally **by 2024**.
[5:7] - **Census 2011:** only about **31%** of rural households had tap water (30.8%).
[5:8] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [5:8.0] Service level | 55 litres per capita per day of prescribed quality
  [5:8.1] Har Ghar Jal village | Tap for every household and institution, certified by Gram Sabha resolution
  [5:8.2] Jal Arpan | Handover of schemes to GPs
  [5:8.3] Sujal Gaon ID | Digital ID for every rural piped scheme
[5:9] - Target **19.36 crore** households; **15.86 crore (81.94%)** connected (Aug 2026); about 18% still unconnected.
[5:10] - Deadline **2024** → **December 2028** as **JJM 2.0** (Cabinet, Mar 2026): from infrastructure creation to **service delivery** including O&M; outlay ₹8.69 lakh crore.

## SUB 6 | Housing – PMAY-G and PMAY-U
[6:0] - **Indira Awaas Yojana** (1985-86): began under RLEGP, a **sub-scheme of Jawahar Rozgar Yojana** from 1989, independent from **1 Jan 1996**; restructured as **PMAY-G (2016)**.
[6:1] - **PMAY-G:** unit assistance **₹1.20 lakh (plains)** / **₹1.30 lakh (hilly/difficult)**; beneficiaries from **SECC 2011** housing-deprivation data, verified by the **Gram Sabha**.
[6:2] - Target **2.95 crore** houses by March 2024; Cabinet (9 Aug 2024) approved **2 crore more** for **2024-25 to 2028-29**; 3.13 crore completed by mid-2026.
[6:3] - **PMAY-U:** in June 2019 the Housing Ministry **advanced** the 1-crore-house deadline **from 2022 to 2020**; later extended to Dec 2024 and PMAY-U 2.0.
