## SUB 0 | National Food Security Act, 2013 – Coverage, Entitlements, Grievances
[0:0] # Enactment and nature
[0:1] - **Dates:** first an **Ordinance (5 July 2013)**; Act notified **10 Sep 2013** with effect from **5 July 2013** – the Act is of **2013** (not 2014, 2019, 2020).
[0:2] - **Nature:** welfare → **rights-based** approach; legal right to subsidised grain. Ministry: **Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution** (DFPD).
[0:3] - **Objective (long title):** **food and nutritional security** by ensuring access to adequate quality food at **affordable prices** so people can **live a life with dignity**. Distractors: "PDS Act", "FCI Act", "NFSA 2014".
[0:4] - **Coverage:** up to **75% of the rural** and **50% of the urban** population = about **two-thirds** of India; about **80-81 crore** people (not "50 crore"). Coverage is NOT limited to BPL families (Priority Households + AAY).
[0:5] - **Women:** the **eldest woman aged 18 or above** is head of household for the ration card (else the eldest male).
[0:6] # Entitlements
[0:7] TABLE head: Category | Entitlement | Issue price
  [0:7.0] Priority Household | 5 kg per person per month | ₹3 rice / ₹2 wheat / ₹1 coarse grain
  [0:7.1] Antyodaya Anna Yojana | 35 kg per household per month | Same; now free
  [0:7.2] Pregnant women and lactating mothers | Free meal during pregnancy and 6 months after + maternity benefit of at least ₹6,000 | –
  [0:7.3] Children 6 months-6 years | Take-home ration or hot meal through ICDS/anganwadi | –
  [0:7.4] Children 6-14 years | Mid-day meal / PM POSHAN | –
[0:8] - **Special focus on nutritional support to women and children** is a stated feature of the Act.
[0:9] - **Calorie trap (UPSC 2018):** the "take-home ration of 1,600 calories" for pregnant women is not in the Act (Schedule II norm is 600 kcal) – so only the eldest-woman statement is right.
[0:10] - **Issue prices** ₹3/2/1 **not revised since July 2013** while MSP and subsidy keep rising.
[0:11] - **Draft NFS (Amendment) Bill 2026** (DFPD, 24 June 2026): AAY → **7 kg per person, capped at 35 kg**; amends the first proviso to Sec 3(1); ends small households getting more grain per head.
[0:12] # Grievance and transparency
[0:13] TABLE head: Section | Mechanism
  [0:13.0] Sec 15 | Internal mechanism + District Grievance Redressal Officer (one per district); officials ignoring DGRO relief can be penalised
  [0:13.1] Sec 16 | State Food Commission in every State (Chairperson, five Members, Member-Secretary) – monitors and hears appeals; an existing commission may be designated
[0:14] - **Transparency:** **vigilance committees** at **State, district, block and Fair Price Shop** levels, **social audits**, mandatory disclosure of PDS records.
[0:15] > Statement angle: 75/50 coverage, eldest woman 18+, nutrition focus = correct; "only BPL", "50 crore covered", "1,600 calories" = wrong.

## SUB 1 | Food Security – Concept, Dimensions and Entitlements
[1:0] # Definition
[1:1] - **World Food Summit 1996 / FAO:** food security exists when **all people, at all times**, have **physical and economic access** to **sufficient, safe and nutritious food** for an active and healthy life.
[1:2] - Food security does NOT mean access to "**the food of their choice**".
[1:3] # Dimensions
[1:4] TABLE head: Dimension | Meaning
  [1:4.0] Availability | Production + imports + previous years' stock in government granaries
  [1:4.1] Accessibility | Food within reach of every person
  [1:4.2] Affordability | Enough money to buy sufficient, safe, nutritious food
  [1:4.3] Absorption / utilisation | Body's ability to use the food
  [1:4.4] Stability | Access at all times
[1:5] - **NOT dimensions:** **Accountability**, price, **quantity of exports**.
[1:6] # National vs household
[1:7] TABLE head: Level | Meaning
  [1:7.0] National | Aggregate production + buffer stock (sufficient food stock)
  [1:7.1] Household | Each household can actually access and afford food
[1:8] - "**National but not household food security**" = **sufficient food stock, but not all households can get it** – hence PDS and NFSA.
[1:9] - Food security is needed **even in surplus years**: starvation comes from **unequal distribution and lack of access** (A-R: both true, R explains A).
[1:10] # Entitlement approach
[1:11] - **Amartya Sen**, *Poverty and Famines* (**1981**): famines come from a **collapse of entitlements** (command over food through income, exchange and transfers), not merely a fall in food availability; Sen added "**access**" to the 1970s "availability" idea.
[1:12] - **Per capita norms:** the APPSC 2011 key credits the **440 g/day foodgrain norm to FAO**; Indian nutrition bodies use about 400-420 g (ICMR/NIN) – treat 440 g as an exam figure only.
[1:13] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC 03 p.6-7 (availability, accessibility, affordability); OnlyIAS NCERT Economy 05 p.25 (World Food Summit, Sen's access); app notes 3:39 sub 0, 1
## SUB 2 | Public Distribution System – Aims, Evolution, AAY and Laws
[2:0] # Aims
[2:1] - **PDS** = distribution of essential commodities to a large number of people through a **network of Fair Price (ration) Shops at subsidised prices**; food procured by FCI goes to the poorer sections.
[2:2] - **Main aim:** **food security for the poor** – to **protect the low-income / vulnerable sections against high prices** by guaranteeing a minimum quantity at affordable prices; also checks **hoarding and black-marketing** and stabilises prices ("all of the above").
[2:3] - **Procurement at MSP** ensures **stable farm prices, remunerative prices to farmers and food stocks for the PDS** – all three.
[2:4] # Evolution
[2:5] TABLE head: Stage | Year | Fact
  [2:5.0] Rationing | 1940s | World War II / Bengal famine backdrop; revived in the 1960s food shortage
  [2:5.1] Buffer stock | Fourth Plan (1969-74) | Formally introduced
  [2:5.2] ICDS / Food-for-Work | 1975 / 1977-78 | Food intervention programmes after NSSO poverty data
  [2:5.3] RPDS | 1992 | Revamped PDS for remote and backward blocks
  [2:5.4] TPDS | June 1997 (not 1985) | BPL/APL differential prices
  [2:5.5] Antyodaya Anna Yojana | 25 Dec 2000 | 1 crore poorest BPL families; 25 kg at ₹2 wheat/₹3 rice → 35 kg from April 2002
  [2:5.6] Annapurna | 2000 | 10 kg free grain to indigent senior citizens
  [2:5.7] NFSA | 2013 | Rights-based
  [2:5.8] PMGKAY | 2020 | Extra free grain
[2:6] # State food schemes
[2:7] TABLE head: State | Scheme
  [2:7.0] Andhra Pradesh | NTR's ₹2 a kg rice scheme (1983): up to 25 kg per household per month
  [2:7.1] Chhattisgarh | Chhattisgarh Food Security Act, 2012 – the first State Right to Food law, before NFSA
  [2:7.2] Uttarakhand | Atal Khadyanna Yojana (2011): APL 10 kg wheat + 10 kg rice at ₹4 and ₹6/kg; BPL at ₹2 and ₹3/kg
  [2:7.3] Tamil Nadu | About 94% of fair price shops run by cooperatives
[2:8] # Laws and bodies
[2:9] TABLE head: Law / body | Year | Purpose
  [2:9.0] Agricultural Produce (Grading and Marking) Act | 1937 | AGMARK
  [2:9.1] Prevention of Food Adulteration Act | 1954 | Replaced by FSSA 2006
  [2:9.2] Essential Commodities Act | 1955 | Controls production, supply, price and stocks; base for PDS control orders
  [2:9.3] Food Corporations Act | 1964 | Creates FCI
  [2:9.4] NCCF | 1965 | Price stabilisation; Consumer Affairs Ministry
  [2:9.5] Food Safety and Standards Act | 2006 | Replaced PFA Act 1954; created FSSAI (under the Ministry of Health; its head is a Chairperson, not the DG Health Services)
  [2:9.6] Article 47 (DPSP) | – | Raise nutrition and standard of living
[2:10] - **Procurement prices** = prices at which government buys grain for PDS and buffer stock.
[2:11] > Match angle: ECA 1955 – Grading and Marking 1937 – PFA 1954 – FSSA 2006.

## SUB 4 | PMGKAY, One Nation One Ration Card, Buffer Stocks and Price Interventions
[4:0] # PMGKAY
[4:1] - **Announced 26 March 2020** (COVID relief, PM Garib Kalyan package): **5 kg free grain per person** per month on top of NFSA.
[4:2] - **Sept 2022:** extended by **three months** (Oct-Dec 2022) beyond 30 Sep 2022.
[4:3] - From **1 Jan 2023** merged with NFSA – the **entire NFSA allocation became free** (AAY 35 kg per family; PHH 5 kg per person).
[4:4] - Extended for five years **1 Jan 2024 – 31 Dec 2028**; covers about **81.35 crore** people; ~₹11.80 lakh crore by the Centre.
[4:5] # One Nation One Ration Card
[4:6] - Runs on **IM-PDS** (Integrated Management of PDS, 2018) – nationwide portability and de-duplication.
[4:7] - Started **Aug 2019** in 4 States (AP-Telangana, Gujarat-Maharashtra); **Assam** became the **36th and last** State/UT (**June 2022**); now in all 36 States/UTs covering nearly 80 crore NFSA beneficiaries.
[4:8] - **Mera Ration** app (DFPD with NIC) for migrants.
[4:9] # Buffer stocks
[4:10] - **CACP** advises MSP → **FCI** procures wheat and rice → **CCEA** fixes minimum norms **each quarter** (1 Apr, 1 Jul, 1 Oct, 1 Jan) → sold at issue price in deficit areas, or via OMSS.
[4:11] TABLE head: Quarter | Norm (rice + wheat)
  [4:11.0] 1 April | 210.4 lakh tonnes
  [4:11.1] 1 July | 411.2 lakh tonnes (highest)
  [4:11.2] 1 October | 307.7 lakh tonnes
  [4:11.3] 1 January | 214.1 lakh tonnes
[4:12] - **Strategic reserve:** **5 million tonnes** above operational stocks (**30 LT wheat + 20 LT rice**).
[4:13] - **OMSS:** **FCI** sells surplus central-pool grain at predetermined prices – raises supply, cools prices, clears stock; does not replace procurement.
[4:14] - **Anna Darpan** (FCI, 2024; Coforge as system integrator): microservices-based supply chain system **replacing the Depot Online System**, covering mandis, mills, depots and all office levels; an **FCI / Department of Food and Public Distribution** project, not the Agriculture Ministry.
[4:15] # Bharat brand
[4:16] - **Bharat Rice** (**Feb 2024**) at **₹29/kg**; Bharat Atta and dal – via **NAFED, NCCF, Kendriya Bhandar** outlets and mobile vans to curb inflation.

## SUB 5 | National Food Security Mission and Global Initiatives
[5:0] - **NFSM (2007-08):** Centrally Sponsored Scheme of the **Ministry of Agriculture**, launched in pursuance of the **National Development Council resolution (29 May 2007)** – the APPSC 2011 key calls it the "**National Food Security Resolution**"; targets for the end of the **Eleventh Plan**.
[5:1] - **Crops:** originally **rice, wheat and pulses**; **coarse cereals** added only from 2014-15 (12th Plan) – so "not covered" (2010) = coarse cereals.
[5:2] - **Global Food Security Platform** (2022, $6 billion): launched by the **International Finance Corporation (IFC)** of the World Bank Group to help the private sector respond to the food crisis.

## SUB 6 | Andhra Pradesh PDS and Hunger Indices
[6:0] # AP PDS
[6:1] - **Doorstep delivery (Door Delivery System):** from Sept 2019 (Srikakulam pilot) sortex rice delivered to homes, later by **Mobile Dispensing Units** (2021) with electronic weighing – to stop under-weighment, poor quality and leakages; MDUs ended **1 June 2025**, fair price shops restored; doorstep kept for **65+ and the disabled**.
[6:2] - **2024-25:** **29,796** Fair Price Shops; 1.48 crore cards; AAY 35 kg/card free; PHH 5 kg/unit free; **ragi** (all) and **jowar** (Rayalaseema) up to **3 kg per card** free in place of rice; wheat 1 kg at ₹16.
[6:3] - **Rice card (Feb 2020):** replaced white ration cards; only for PDS rice, **delinked from Aarogyasri** and fee reimbursement.
[6:4] - **Mee Mart** (Jul 2026): AP Civil Supplies + **NCCF**; first at **Lalupuram near Guntur**; to link with the **Digital Rupee (CBDC)**.
[6:5] # Hunger indices
[6:6] - **GHI 2025:** India **102nd of 123**, score **25.8** – "**serious**" (2024: 105th).
[6:7] - **GHI publisher:** **Welthungerhilfe** with partners; four indicators: **undernourishment, child stunting, child wasting, child mortality** (not adult literacy).
[6:8] - India's objections: **three of four indicators** concern children only; undernourishment from a **small opinion-poll-type sample**.
[6:9] - **SOFI** (*State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World*): led by **FAO** with IFAD, UNICEF, WFP, WHO. **SOFI 2026:** about 645 million hungry in 2025; **Africa (~309 million)** overtook Asia.
[6:10] ~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.44-45 (29,796 FPS, 1.48 crore beneficiaries, MDUs); app notes 3:39 sub 10
