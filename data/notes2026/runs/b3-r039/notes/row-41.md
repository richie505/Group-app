=== Rural Credit – History, Surveys and Sources
# History and surveys
- **1951:** **moneylenders ~70%** of rural credit; institutional (government, cooperatives, banks) only about **5-7%** – the "modern lending institutions" share asked in APPSC = **about 5%**.
- **All India Rural Credit Survey Committee** (RBI, 1951; report 1954), chair **A.D. Gorwala**: "**Rural credit has failed, but cooperation must succeed**" → **SBI (1 July 1955)** from the **Imperial Bank of India**.
- **1969:** social banking and multi-agency approach; **NABARD (1982)** coordinates rural finance – the **apex rural development bank**.
- **AIDIS 2019** (NSS **77th round**): rural debt **66% institutional**, 34% non-institutional.
- **NAFIS 2021-22** (NABARD): rural household income **₹12,698**/month; indebted households 47.4% → **52.0%**.
# Sources
| Institutional | Non-institutional |
|---|---|
| Commercial banks, RRBs, cooperatives/PACS, Small Finance Banks, SHG-bank linkage | Moneylenders, **traders**, landlords, relatives |
- **Kisan Credit Card:** valid **5 years**; open to **tenants, sharecroppers, SHGs, JLGs** (not only landowners).
- **Small Finance Banks:** lend to small businesses, small and marginal farmers, rural entrepreneurs (not to financial-sector regulators).

=== Informal Debt, Debt Trap and Microfinance
| Debt type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Temporary | Seasonal, repaid after harvest |
| Chronic | Long-standing |
| **Debt trap** | **Taking a new loan (debt) to repay an old one** – for a household or a country |
- Usurious interest → distress sale of land/gold, bonded-labour-like ties; often the trigger in farmer suicides.
- **Microfinance route (DAY-NRLM):** **SHGs, JLGs, BC Sakhis** (not moneylenders); Kerala's **Kudumbashree** (1995 thrift and credit society).

=== MGNREGA, Migration and Rural Distress
# MGNREGA and distress
- **NREGA** passed 2005, in force 2006, renamed **MGNREGA 2009**: **100 days** per household; wages within **15 days** (not 30); **one-third** women. Now replaced by **VB-G RAM G**.
- **Positive results:** **higher purchasing power of the rural poor** and **less distress migration** to cities; it **raised** (not reduced) labourers' bargaining power.
- Person-days **389.09 crore (2020-21) → 183.77 crore** (2025-26, to Dec 2025); urban wages **1.7-2 times** rural (ES 2025-26).
- AP: **125 days** wage work in distressed pockets under VB-G RAM G (Jun 2026).
# Migration
| Push (rural) | Pull (urban) |
|---|---|
| **Few jobs**, low wages, landlessness, crop failure, debt | **High labour demand**, better wages |
| Poor health and schools | Education, healthcare |
| **Unbalanced rural-urban development** | Industry and services |
- Causes of rural-urban migration: urban labour demand, unbalanced development, few rural jobs – **all of the above**.
- **Distress migration:** forced by debt, landlessness, crop loss. **Seasonal:** for a season, then return. **26.8%** of rural Indians were migrants (2020-21).
- **AP:** distress belts **Rayalaseema, Srikakulam**; Gulf migration from **YSR Kadapa, Godavari/Konaseema**; **93.2%** agricultural households indebted (highest, NSSO 77th round); ~**50%** of AP farmers depend on non-institutional credit (NABARD; not 25%).
# Rural connectivity gap
- **TRAI:** urban teledensity is about **three times** rural teledensity (APPSC key; e.g. urban ~155 vs rural ~57 in 2018).

=== Rural Non-farm Sector and Feminisation of Agriculture
# Non-farm sector
- Components: rural industries, Khadi & Village Industries, handloom/handicrafts, food processing, trade, transport, services (**not crop cultivation**).
| Body / scheme | Role |
|---|---|
| **KVIC** | Statutory, **KVI Act 1956**; Ministry of **MSME**; implements **PMEGP** |
| SVEP | Under DAY-NRLM; funds rural non-farm enterprises |
| MSMEs | 8.7 crore units, ~**31.1%** of GDP |
| DDU-GKY | Placement-linked skilling; parliamentary panel flags **low wages and drop-outs** |
# Feminisation of agriculture
- Rising share of women among cultivators, labourers and unpaid family workers as **men move to non-farm/urban work** (male out-migration); participation, not land ownership.
- **Claudia Goldin** (2023 Nobel): **U-shaped curve** – high in the 1980s, fell 2004-05 to 2012, rising after 2020.
- Rural women = **80%** of the rise in female workers (2017-18 to 2023-24).
