=== National Food Security Act, 2013 – Coverage, Entitlements, Grievances
# Enactment and nature
- **Dates:** first an **Ordinance (5 July 2013)**; Act notified **10 Sep 2013** with effect from **5 July 2013** – the Act is of **2013** (not 2014, 2019, 2020).
- **Nature:** welfare → **rights-based** approach; legal right to subsidised grain. Ministry: **Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution** (DFPD).
- **Objective (long title):** **food and nutritional security** by ensuring access to adequate quality food at **affordable prices** so people can **live a life with dignity**. Distractors: "PDS Act", "FCI Act", "NFSA 2014".
- **Coverage:** up to **75% of the rural** and **50% of the urban** population = about **two-thirds** of India; about **80-81 crore** people (not "50 crore"). Coverage is NOT limited to BPL families (Priority Households + AAY).
- **Women:** the **eldest woman aged 18 or above** is head of household for the ration card (else the eldest male).
# Entitlements
| Category | Entitlement | Issue price |
|---|---|---|
| **Priority Household** | **5 kg per person per month** | **₹3 rice / ₹2 wheat / ₹1 coarse grain** |
| **Antyodaya Anna Yojana** | **35 kg per household per month** | Same; now free |
| Pregnant women and lactating mothers | **Free meal** during pregnancy and 6 months after + **maternity benefit of at least ₹6,000** | – |
| Children 6 months-6 years | Take-home ration or hot meal through **ICDS/anganwadi** | – |
| Children 6-14 years | Mid-day meal / PM POSHAN | – |
- **Special focus on nutritional support to women and children** is a stated feature of the Act.
- **Calorie trap (UPSC 2018):** the "take-home ration of 1,600 calories" for pregnant women is not in the Act (Schedule II norm is 600 kcal) – so only the eldest-woman statement is right.
- **Issue prices** ₹3/2/1 **not revised since July 2013** while MSP and subsidy keep rising.
- **Draft NFS (Amendment) Bill 2026** (DFPD, 24 June 2026): AAY → **7 kg per person, capped at 35 kg**; amends the first proviso to Sec 3(1); ends small households getting more grain per head.
# Grievance and transparency
| Section | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Sec 15** | Internal mechanism + **District Grievance Redressal Officer** (one per district); officials ignoring DGRO relief can be penalised |
| **Sec 16** | **State Food Commission** in every State (Chairperson, five Members, Member-Secretary) – monitors and hears appeals; an existing commission may be designated |
- **Transparency:** **vigilance committees** at **State, district, block and Fair Price Shop** levels, **social audits**, mandatory disclosure of PDS records.
> Statement angle: 75/50 coverage, eldest woman 18+, nutrition focus = correct; "only BPL", "50 crore covered", "1,600 calories" = wrong.

=== Food Security – Concept, Dimensions and Entitlements
# Definition
- **World Food Summit 1996 / FAO:** food security exists when **all people, at all times**, have **physical and economic access** to **sufficient, safe and nutritious food** for an active and healthy life.
- Food security does NOT mean access to "**the food of their choice**".
# Dimensions
| Dimension | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Availability** | Production + imports + previous years' stock in government granaries |
| **Accessibility** | Food within reach of every person |
| **Affordability** | Enough money to buy sufficient, safe, nutritious food |
| Absorption / utilisation | Body's ability to use the food |
| Stability | Access at all times |
- **NOT dimensions:** **Accountability**, price, **quantity of exports**.
# National vs household
| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| National | Aggregate production + buffer stock (sufficient food stock) |
| Household | Each household can actually access and afford food |
- "**National but not household food security**" = **sufficient food stock, but not all households can get it** – hence PDS and NFSA.
- Food security is needed **even in surplus years**: starvation comes from **unequal distribution and lack of access** (A-R: both true, R explains A).
# Entitlement approach
- **Amartya Sen**, *Poverty and Famines* (**1981**): famines come from a **collapse of entitlements** (command over food through income, exchange and transfers), not merely a fall in food availability; Sen added "**access**" to the 1970s "availability" idea.
- **Per capita norms:** the APPSC 2011 key credits the **440 g/day foodgrain norm to FAO**; Indian nutrition bodies use about 400-420 g (ICMR/NIN) – treat 440 g as an exam figure only.

=== Public Distribution System – Aims, Evolution, AAY and Laws
# Aims
- **PDS** = distribution of essential commodities to a large number of people through a **network of Fair Price (ration) Shops at subsidised prices**; food procured by FCI goes to the poorer sections.
- **Main aim:** **food security for the poor** – to **protect the low-income / vulnerable sections against high prices** by guaranteeing a minimum quantity at affordable prices; also checks **hoarding and black-marketing** and stabilises prices ("all of the above").
- **Procurement at MSP** ensures **stable farm prices, remunerative prices to farmers and food stocks for the PDS** – all three.
# Evolution
| Stage | Year | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| Rationing | **1940s** | **World War II / Bengal famine** backdrop; revived in the 1960s food shortage |
| Buffer stock | Fourth Plan (1969-74) | Formally introduced |
| ICDS / Food-for-Work | 1975 / 1977-78 | Food intervention programmes after NSSO poverty data |
| RPDS | **1992** | Revamped PDS for remote and backward blocks |
| TPDS | **June 1997** (not 1985) | BPL/APL differential prices |
| **Antyodaya Anna Yojana** | **25 Dec 2000** | 1 crore poorest BPL families; **25 kg** at ₹2 wheat/₹3 rice → **35 kg from April 2002** |
| Annapurna | 2000 | 10 kg free grain to indigent senior citizens |
| NFSA | 2013 | Rights-based |
| PMGKAY | 2020 | Extra free grain |
# State food schemes
| State | Scheme |
|---|---|
| **Andhra Pradesh** | NTR's **₹2 a kg rice** scheme (1983): up to **25 kg per household** per month |
| **Chhattisgarh** | **Chhattisgarh Food Security Act, 2012** – the **first State Right to Food law**, before NFSA |
| **Uttarakhand** | **Atal Khadyanna Yojana** (2011): APL 10 kg wheat + 10 kg rice at ₹4 and ₹6/kg; BPL at ₹2 and ₹3/kg |
| Tamil Nadu | About 94% of fair price shops run by cooperatives |
# Laws and bodies
| Law / body | Year | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Agricultural Produce (**Grading and Marking**) Act | **1937** | **AGMARK** |
| Prevention of Food Adulteration Act | **1954** | Replaced by FSSA 2006 |
| **Essential Commodities Act** | **1955** | Controls production, supply, price and stocks; base for PDS control orders |
| Food Corporations Act | 1964 | Creates **FCI** |
| NCCF | 1965 | Price stabilisation; Consumer Affairs Ministry |
| **Food Safety and Standards Act** | **2006** | Replaced PFA Act 1954; created **FSSAI** (under the **Ministry of Health**; its head is a Chairperson, not the DG Health Services) |
| Article 47 (DPSP) | – | Raise nutrition and standard of living |
- **Procurement prices** = prices at which government buys grain for PDS and buffer stock.
> Match angle: ECA 1955 – Grading and Marking 1937 – PFA 1954 – FSSA 2006.

=== Food-based Safety Nets – Mid-Day Meal and ICDS
| Programme | Support | Year |
|---|---|---|
| PDS / NFSA | Grain through Fair Price Shops | – |
| **ICDS** | Supplementary nutrition at anganwadis (0-6, pregnant, lactating) | **1975** |
| Food-for-Work | **Grain as wages** | 1977-78 |
| **Mid-Day Meal / PM POSHAN** | Hot cooked meal in schools (Bal Vatika to upper primary); **Ministry of Education** | **1995** (15 Aug 1995) |
- **PDS, ICDS and Mid-Day Meal** all provide a **food-based safety net** ("all of the above").
- Mid-Day Meal: encourages attendance and retention and improves nutrition. Corporate partners in Rajasthan: **Adamya Chetna Trust, Havells, Hindustan Zinc, DSCL Kota** (Shriram).

=== PMGKAY, One Nation One Ration Card, Buffer Stocks and Price Interventions
# PMGKAY
- **Announced 26 March 2020** (COVID relief, PM Garib Kalyan package): **5 kg free grain per person** per month on top of NFSA.
- **Sept 2022:** extended by **three months** (Oct-Dec 2022) beyond 30 Sep 2022.
- From **1 Jan 2023** merged with NFSA – the **entire NFSA allocation became free** (AAY 35 kg per family; PHH 5 kg per person).
- Extended for five years **1 Jan 2024 – 31 Dec 2028**; covers about **81.35 crore** people; ~₹11.80 lakh crore by the Centre.
# One Nation One Ration Card
- Runs on **IM-PDS** (Integrated Management of PDS, 2018) – nationwide portability and de-duplication.
- Started **Aug 2019** in 4 States (AP-Telangana, Gujarat-Maharashtra); **Assam** became the **36th and last** State/UT (**June 2022**); now in all 36 States/UTs covering nearly 80 crore NFSA beneficiaries.
- **Mera Ration** app (DFPD with NIC) for migrants.
# Buffer stocks
- **CACP** advises MSP → **FCI** procures wheat and rice → **CCEA** fixes minimum norms **each quarter** (1 Apr, 1 Jul, 1 Oct, 1 Jan) → sold at issue price in deficit areas, or via OMSS.
| Quarter | Norm (rice + wheat) |
|---|---|
| 1 April | 210.4 lakh tonnes |
| **1 July** | **411.2 lakh tonnes (highest)** |
| 1 October | 307.7 lakh tonnes |
| 1 January | 214.1 lakh tonnes |
- **Strategic reserve:** **5 million tonnes** above operational stocks (**30 LT wheat + 20 LT rice**).
- **OMSS:** **FCI** sells surplus central-pool grain at predetermined prices – raises supply, cools prices, clears stock; does not replace procurement.
- **Anna Darpan** (FCI, 2024; Coforge as system integrator): microservices-based supply chain system **replacing the Depot Online System**, covering mandis, mills, depots and all office levels; an **FCI / Department of Food and Public Distribution** project, not the Agriculture Ministry.
# Bharat brand
- **Bharat Rice** (**Feb 2024**) at **₹29/kg**; Bharat Atta and dal – via **NAFED, NCCF, Kendriya Bhandar** outlets and mobile vans to curb inflation.

=== National Food Security Mission and Global Initiatives
- **NFSM (2007-08):** Centrally Sponsored Scheme of the **Ministry of Agriculture**, launched in pursuance of the **National Development Council resolution (29 May 2007)** – the APPSC 2011 key calls it the "**National Food Security Resolution**"; targets for the end of the **Eleventh Plan**.
- **Crops:** originally **rice, wheat and pulses**; **coarse cereals** added only from 2014-15 (12th Plan) – so "not covered" (2010) = coarse cereals.
- **Global Food Security Platform** (2022, $6 billion): launched by the **International Finance Corporation (IFC)** of the World Bank Group to help the private sector respond to the food crisis.

=== Andhra Pradesh PDS and Hunger Indices
# AP PDS
- **Doorstep delivery (Door Delivery System):** from Sept 2019 (Srikakulam pilot) sortex rice delivered to homes, later by **Mobile Dispensing Units** (2021) with electronic weighing – to stop under-weighment, poor quality and leakages; MDUs ended **1 June 2025**, fair price shops restored; doorstep kept for **65+ and the disabled**.
- **2024-25:** **29,796** Fair Price Shops; 1.48 crore cards; AAY 35 kg/card free; PHH 5 kg/unit free; **ragi** (all) and **jowar** (Rayalaseema) up to **3 kg per card** free in place of rice; wheat 1 kg at ₹16.
- **Rice card (Feb 2020):** replaced white ration cards; only for PDS rice, **delinked from Aarogyasri** and fee reimbursement.
- **Mee Mart** (Jul 2026): AP Civil Supplies + **NCCF**; first at **Lalupuram near Guntur**; to link with the **Digital Rupee (CBDC)**.
# Hunger indices
- **GHI 2025:** India **102nd of 123**, score **25.8** – "**serious**" (2024: 105th).
- **GHI publisher:** **Welthungerhilfe** with partners; four indicators: **undernourishment, child stunting, child wasting, child mortality** (not adult literacy).
- India's objections: **three of four indicators** concern children only; undernourishment from a **small opinion-poll-type sample**.
- **SOFI** (*State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World*): led by **FAO** with IFAD, UNICEF, WFP, WHO. **SOFI 2026:** about 645 million hungry in 2025; **Africa (~309 million)** overtook Asia.
