=== Soil Science Terms – Pedology, Eluviation, pH, Water and Soil Biology
# Branches and terms
- **Pedology** = scientific study of soils in their natural setting (formation, classification, mapping); **edaphology** = soil in relation to plants; **soil geography** = soil formation, types, fertility status, distribution and use.
- **Edaphic** = relating to the **soil** (its physical and chemical make-up) – as against climatic, biotic and topographic factors.
- **Eluviation** = downward washing out (translocation) of clay, iron, humus from an upper horizon (the **E** layer); their deposit lower down = **illuviation** (B horizon).
# Water, pH and salts
- **Capillary rise** is strongest in **clayey** soil (finest pores) and weakest in sandy soil.
- **Soil pH:** most common crops grow best near neutral, **pH 6–7**; pH 3–4 is strongly acidic, 9–10 strongly alkaline.
- **Halophytes** (e.g. mangroves) grow in **saline** soils; cryophytes – cold soils; lithophytes – rock; xerophytes – deserts.
# Soil biology
- **Legumes** – **pea, black gram (urd)**, gram, moong – fix atmospheric nitrogen in root nodules (*Rhizobium*) and **improve soil fertility**; wheat, rice, sugarcane, sorghum deplete it.
- **Smell of rain on parched soil (petrichor):** **geosmin** made by soil **Streptomyces** (actinomycetes).
