## SUB 0 | The Four Buddhist Councils – Place, Patron, President
[0:0] TABLE head: Council | Place / year | Patron | President | Outcome
  [0:0.0] First | Rajagriha (Rajgir), Saptaparni Cave; c. 483 BCE, soon after the Buddha's death | Ajatashatru (Haryanka) | Mahakassapa | Vinaya (recited by Upali) and Sutta (by Ananda) compiled
  [0:0.1] Second | Vaishali; 383 BCE, 100 years after the Buddha's death | Kalashoka (Shishunaga) | Sabakami (Sabbakami) | Dispute on monastic rules; split into Sthaviravadins and Mahasanghikas
  [0:0.2] Third | Pataliputra (Ashokarama monastery); c. 250 BCE | Ashoka (Maurya) | Moggaliputta Tissa | Abhidhamma Pitaka, Kathavatthu; heretics expelled; missions abroad
  [0:0.3] Fourth | Kundalavana, Kashmir; 1st c. CE | Kanishka (Kushana) | Vasumitra (Ashvaghosha deputy) | In Sanskrit; Mahavibhasa commentaries; division into Mahayana and Hinayana
[0:1] - **Order of venues:** **Rajagriha → Vaishali → Pataliputra → Kundalavana** = codes "2, 1, 4, 3" (UPPCS 2025; UP Lower 2002).
[0:2] - **Presidents (match):** 1st **Mahakassapa** – 2nd **Sabakami** – 3rd **Moggaliputta Tissa** – 4th **Vasumitra** → "1-(b), 2-(a), 3-(d), 4-(c)" (APPSC DAO 2020, FRO 2020).
[0:3] - **Patrons in order:** **Ajatashatru → Kalashoka → Ashoka → Kanishka**.
[0:4] - **First council:** presided by **Mahakassapa** (not Ananda, Upali, Ajatashatru or Nagasena); Ajatashatru was the patron.
[0:5] - **Third council:** at **Pataliputra**, in the **Ashokarama** monastery; recorded only in the Sri Lankan **Dipavamsa and Mahavamsa** – not in Ashoka's inscriptions or the Divyavadana (UPPCS 1999).
[0:6] - **Fourth council:** held on the advice of the monk **Parsva**; settled **Sarvastivada** disputes of Kashmir–Gandhara; after it Buddhism split into **Mahayana and Hinayana** (SSC 2018; IAS 1993).
[0:7] > Chronology of rulers: Ashoka (3rd c. BCE) → Milinda/Menander (2nd c. BCE) → Kanishka (1st–2nd c. CE).

## SUB 1 | Hinayana, Mahayana, Vajrayana and the Bodhisattvas
[1:0] TABLE head: Sect | Ideal / features | Buddha | Texts / spread
  [1:0.0] Hinayana / Theravada | Arhat – individual liberation; original teachings | Symbols, no idols; a great man, not a god | Pali canon; Sri Lanka, Myanmar, Thailand; Ashoka patronised it
  [1:0.1] Mahayana (after the 4th council, Kanishka) | Bodhisattva – delays own nirvana to help all beings; Sunyata | Image worship – Buddha as god | Sanskrit – Prajnaparamita, Lotus Sutra; China, Japan, Korea, Tibet; Kanishka, Harsha patrons
  [1:0.2] Vajrayana ("vehicle of the thunderbolt") | Salvation through magical power (vajra); mantras, mudras, mandalas | Taras, wrathful deities | Guhyasamaja, Arya Manjushrimulakalpa; Bengal–Bihar, Tibet, Nepal
[1:1] - **Fundamental difference:** Mahayana made **images of the Buddha and worshipped him as God**; Hinayana did not (UPPCS 1996). The Buddha was **elevated to God in Kanishka's period**.
[1:2] - **Vajrayana:** emerged in the **7th century CE** (APPSC AP History), flourished from the 8th c.; **most prominent in the Sultanate period** (JPSC 2013); centre **Vikramashila**; linked with **Padmasambhava**. In Andhra: **Salihundam**.
[1:3] - **Arya Manjushrimulakalpa** = **Vajrayana** (Mahayana tantra; MPPSC 2023 key also accepted Mahayana).
[1:4] - **Bodhisattva** – central to **Mahayana, not Hinayana**; a compassionate one on the way to enlightenment who **delays his own salvation** to help all beings (UPSC 2016: B and C only).
[1:5] - **Six paramitas** (perfections) of the Bodhisattva: **dana, sila, kshanti, virya, dhyana, prajna**.
[1:6] - **Avalokiteshvara** = **Padmapani** ("lotus-bearer") = Lokeshvara, Bodhisattva of **compassion** (IAS 1997). **Maitreya** = the **future Buddha** yet to come (UPSC 2018). Manjushri = wisdom; Vajrapani = power.
[1:7] - **Sthaviravadins** = Hinayana (not Mahayana); **Lokottaravadins** = offshoot of the **Mahasanghikas**; Mahasanghika **deification of the Buddha** fostered Mahayana (UPSC 2020: II and III).
[1:8] - **Satavahana-period Andhra:** **Madhyamika, Mahayana and Hinayana** (Chaityaka sects) – not Zen or Theravada (APPSC ATWO).
[1:9] - **Tibetan** Buddhism merges Mahayana and Vajrayana with Bon; Mahayana reached Tibet in the 7th c.; **Gelug** school dominant by the 15th c.; canon = **Kangyur and Tengyur**. **Karmapa Lama** = **Kagyu (Kangyupa)** sect, seat Rumtek (Sikkim) (UPPCS 2000).
[1:10] > Arhat = Hinayana; Bodhisattva = Mahayana; vajra, Taras, Guhyasamaja = Vajrayana.
[1:11] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 1 p.68-70; NextGen Part 2 p.21; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.54-59; PMF Art & Culture Part 5 p.79; Rocket Indian History Part 1 p.122; old app notes 1:29
## SUB 2 | Buddhist Scholars – Nagarjuna, Ashvaghosha, Buddhaghosa, Bhavaviveka
[2:0] TABLE head: Scholar | School / fact | Work
  [2:0.0] Nagarjuna | Founder of Madhyamika (Shunyavada) of Mahayana; doctrine of Shunyata (void); friend of a Satavahana king | Mulamadhyamakakarika, Suhrllekha, Ratnavali (Rajaparikatha)
  [2:0.1] Ashvaghosha | Kanishka's court poet; deputy at the 4th council | Buddhacharita (Sanskrit), Saundarananda
  [2:0.2] Buddhaghosa | Theravada, 5th c.; born near Guntur (Kota Nemapuri); worked at Anuradhapura | Visuddhimagga (Pali); Samantapasadika
  [2:0.3] Bhavaviveka | Madhyamika; acharya at the Dhanyakataka (Amaravati) vihara | Tarkajvala
  [2:0.4] Aryadeva | Nagarjuna's disciple; Madhyamika – a Buddhist, not Jaina scholar | Chatuhshataka
  [2:0.5] Dignaga (Dinnaga) | Buddhist logician; "Father of Indian logic"; Yogachara | Pramana Samuccaya
  [2:0.6] Dharmakirti (7th c.) | "Kant of India"; developed Dignaga's logic; taught at Nalanda | Pramana Vartika
  [2:0.7] Asanga, Vasubandhu | Brothers (4th c.); Yogachara | Vasubandhu – Abhidharmakosa
  [2:0.8] Nagasena | Dialogue with Menander | Milindapanho
  [2:0.9] Sanghabhuti | Monk in China, 381–384 CE | Commentary on the Sarvastivada Vinaya
[2:1] - **Nagarjuna – welfare state:** set out the theory of the welfare state (**Shreyorajya**) in the **Ratnavali** (Rajaparikatha-ratnavali), addressed to a Satavahana king (APPSC AP History).
[2:2] - **Nagarjuna** formulated the **basic ideas of Mahayana** (NDA 2014) and taught **Shunyata** (UPPCS 1998; CDS 2006) – not Nagasena, Ashvaghosha or Shankaracharya.
[2:3] - **Suhrllekha** – letter to a friend; **I-tsing** saw students memorising it. Title "**Einstein of India**" – idea of relativity.
[2:4] - **Sriparvata:** royal friend **Yajna Sri Satakarni** built the **1,500-room Maha Vihara (Parvata Vihara)** for Nagarjuna.
[2:5] - **Siddha Nagarjuna** (Vajrayana alchemist at Sriparvata, later period) is a **different person** from the philosopher.
[2:6] - **Bhavaviveka = Dhanyakataka** (NextGen; Hiuen Tsang) – a printed key of "Sriparvatam" (APPSC G2 2019) is wrong.
[2:7] - **Wrong pair:** "**Nagarjuna – Buddhacharita**" (Ashvaghosha wrote it); Hala – Gathasaptasati, Vatsyayana – Kamasutra, Gunadhya – Brihatkatha are right.
[2:8] - **UPSC 2022:** Aryadeva – Jaina ✗, Dignaga – Buddhist ✓, **Nathamuni – Vaishnava** ✓ → **only two pairs** correct.
[2:9] > Visuddhimagga = Buddhaghosa (not Nagarjuna, Vasubandhu, Padmasambhava, Dignaga).

## SUB 3 | Tripitaka – Vinaya, Sutta, Abhidhamma and the Nikayas
[3:0] TABLE head: Pitaka | Content | Books / compiler
  [3:0.0] Vinaya | Rules and discipline of the Sangha (monastic life) | Patimokkha (227 rules for monks), Vibhanga, Khandhaka; Upali
  [3:0.1] Sutta | Buddha's sermons on ethics and doctrine (Buddhavacana) | 5 Nikayas; Ananda
  [3:0.2] Abhidhamma | Philosophical analysis of the Dhamma | 7 books; added at the 3rd council (Moggaliputta Tissa)
[3:1] - **Tripitaka** = the collection of the Buddha's teachings, the **Buddhist** canon (not Jain, Sikh or Hindu); written in **Pali**.
[3:2] - **Patimokkha** = the **rules of the Sangha** (CDS 2009), recited every fortnight.
[3:3] - **Seven Abhidhamma books:** **Dhammasangani** (first), Vibhanga, Dhatukatha, Puggalapannatti, **Kathavatthu**, **Yamaka**, **Patthana** (seventh). Yamaka belongs to the **Abhidhamma** (JPSC 2016).
[3:4] TABLE head: Nikaya (Sutta Pitaka) | Content
  [3:4.0] Digha | Long discourses in Pali; Mahaparinibbana Sutta; Samannaphala Sutta
  [3:4.1] Majjhima | Middle-length discourses
  [3:4.2] Samyutta | Grouped discourses
  [3:4.3] Anguttara | Arranged by number; lists the 16 Mahajanapadas
  [3:4.4] Khuddaka | 15 books: 550 Jatakas, Dhammapada, Suttanipata, Buddhavamsa, Theragatha, Therigatha
[3:5] - **Branches of Buddhist literature:** **Pitakas and Jatakas**; the **Angas** are the **Jain** canon (CDS 2006).
[3:6] - **Early literature** is generally **canonical**, but schools do not class their canon as "only the Pitakas" (NDA 2014: only I).
[3:7] > 16 Mahajanapadas = Anguttara; Jatakas, Dhammapada = Khuddaka; Kathavatthu, Yamaka = Abhidhamma.
[3:8] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 2 p.6-8; NextGen Part 3 p.31; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.47-54; PMF Art & Culture Part 5 p.5; Rocket Indian History Part 1 p.131

## SUB 5 | Philosophical Schools – Sarvastivada to Yogachara
[5:0] TABLE head: School | Founder / teacher | Doctrine
  [5:0.0] Sthaviravada | Elders at the 2nd council | Orthodox, strict Vinaya → Theravada
  [5:0.1] Mahasanghika | Mahadeva's group | Relaxed rules; parent of Chaitika, Lokottaravada and Mahayana
  [5:0.2] Vaibhashika (Sarvastivada) | Based on the Mahavibhasa | Dharmas exist in past, present and future ("all exists")
  [5:0.3] Sautrantika | Kumaralata | Relies on the Suttas; Hinayana
  [5:0.4] Madhyamika / Shunyavada | Nagarjuna; Aryadeva, Buddhapalita, Bhavaviveka | Sunyata (emptiness); midway between realism and idealism
  [5:0.5] Yogachara / Vijnanavada | Maitreyanatha, Asanga, Vasubandhu | Only consciousness is real
[5:1] - **Nagarjuna's school = Madhyamika** (UK UDA 2007) – not Sautrantika, Vaibhashika or Yogachara.
[5:2] - **Aryadeva** = Madhyamika (not Yogachara); Yogachara and Vijnanavada are one school.
