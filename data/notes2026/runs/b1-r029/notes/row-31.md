=== Ajivikas – Makkhali Gosala and Niyati
- **Founder (exam answer):** **Makkhali Gosala** (Gosala Maskariputra) – its most important leader and populariser (UPPCS 1996; UKPSC 2024; NDA 2015); earlier teachers like Nanda Vaccha are also named. Not Ananda, Upali, Vasubandhu or Dignaga.
- **Niyati (fate):** "**Fate assigns everything, man is powerless**" = **Ajivikas** (UPPCS 2006; RAS 2013) – not Jains, Buddhists or Mimamsakas.
- All thoughts and actions are **fixed before birth**; **no free will**; karma cannot change destiny; the **order of rebirth cannot be changed**.
- **Practice:** belief in **determinism** and **severe austerities** (RAS 2024: 1 and 3); ascetics called **Sudra sannyasins**; **no caste discrimination**; held meetings in regular **sabhas** (State PSC: A, B and D).
- **Atomic theory** of matter – influenced the later **Vaisheshika**.
- Gosala was **Mahavira's companion for about six years**; Jain texts call him Mahavira's **disciple**; the **Bhagavati Sutra** records their quarrel; the **Majjhima Nikaya** records the Buddha's debates with Jains and Ajivikas.
| Ruler / record | Ajivika link |
|---|---|
| **Bindusara** | Popular under him; joined the sect |
| **Ashoka**, Dasharatha | Gifted the **Barabar** (and Nagarjuni) caves – among the **earliest rock-cut caves** in India |
| **7th Pillar Edict** (Delhi-Topra) | Names Ajivikas with Brahmanas, the Sangha and Nirgranthas |
- Barabar caves were given by **Ashoka, not Chandragupta**; Ashoka himself was Buddhist.
- **Decline:** survived about **2,000 years**, to the **14th c. CE** in **Karnataka and Tamil Nadu**; faded with the Puranic-Bhakti revival and loss of patronage.
> Niyativada = Gosala (Ajivika); materialism = Charvaka.

=== Charvaka / Lokayata Materialism
- **Other names:** **Lokayata** / **Lokayata Darshan** ("of the common people") (State PSC; CGPSC 2018); **Barhaspatya** after the legendary founder **Brihaspati**.
- **Complete materialism**; highest value = **Kama** (pleasure) – not Dharma, Artha or Moksha (CGPSC 2017).
- **Dehatmavada:** "**The conscious body is the soul**" (CGPSC 2024).
- **Only pramana = perception** (pratyaksha) (CGPSC 2018); rejects inference, Vedic testimony, ritualism and the afterlife. Nyaya accepts **four** pramanas.
- **Aphorism (IAS 1994):** "**Yavaj jivet sukham jivet, rinam kritva ghritam pibet**" – live well while you live, even on borrowing; once cremated there is no return → **Charvakas**.
- Original **Brihaspati Sutra** is lost; Lokayata is known through **rivals' criticism**, e.g. **Madhava's Sarvadarshanasangraha**.
| School | Karma | Soul |
|---|---|---|
| Jainism | Removable by penance | Eternal jiva in all things |
| Buddhism | Intention-based | Anatta |
| Ajivika | Cannot alter destiny | Accepted |
| Charvaka | Does not decide rebirth | Conscious body |

=== Six Teachers and the Age of Religious Ferment
- **6th century BCE:** an age of religious upheaval across the world, while the **Vedic religion had become complex** – both true (UKPSC 2012). Shramana movements arose amid **urbanisation and Magadhan patronage**.
| Teacher | Doctrine |
|---|---|
| **Makkhali Gosala** | Niyativada – fatalism (Ajivika) |
| **Purana Kassapa** | **Akriyavada** – actions bring neither merit nor demerit |
| **Ajita Kesakambalin** | **Materialist** – nothing survives death |
| **Pakudha Kaccayana** | **Seven** eternal, uncreated elements |
| **Sanjaya Belatthiputta** | **Sceptic** – "eel-wriggling" |
| **Nigantha Nataputta** | = **Mahavira** |
- Source: the **Samannaphala Sutta** of the **Digha Nikaya** – Ajatashatru reports the six teachers' views to the Buddha.
