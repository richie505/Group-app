=== The Four Buddhist Councils – Place, Patron, President
| Council | Place / year | Patron | President | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **First** | **Rajagriha (Rajgir)**, **Saptaparni Cave**; c. 483 BCE, soon after the Buddha's death | **Ajatashatru** (Haryanka) | **Mahakassapa** | **Vinaya** (recited by **Upali**) and **Sutta** (by **Ananda**) compiled |
| **Second** | **Vaishali**; 383 BCE, 100 years after the Buddha's death | **Kalashoka** (Shishunaga) | **Sabakami** (Sabbakami) | Dispute on monastic rules; split into **Sthaviravadins and Mahasanghikas** |
| **Third** | **Pataliputra** (Ashokarama monastery); c. 250 BCE | **Ashoka** (Maurya) | **Moggaliputta Tissa** | **Abhidhamma Pitaka**, **Kathavatthu**; heretics expelled; missions abroad |
| **Fourth** | **Kundalavana, Kashmir**; 1st c. CE | **Kanishka** (Kushana) | **Vasumitra** (**Ashvaghosha** deputy) | In **Sanskrit**; **Mahavibhasa** commentaries; division into **Mahayana and Hinayana** |
- **Order of venues:** **Rajagriha → Vaishali → Pataliputra → Kundalavana** = codes "2, 1, 4, 3" (UPPCS 2025; UP Lower 2002).
- **Presidents (match):** 1st **Mahakassapa** – 2nd **Sabakami** – 3rd **Moggaliputta Tissa** – 4th **Vasumitra** → "1-(b), 2-(a), 3-(d), 4-(c)" (APPSC DAO 2020, FRO 2020).
- **Patrons in order:** **Ajatashatru → Kalashoka → Ashoka → Kanishka**.
- **First council:** presided by **Mahakassapa** (not Ananda, Upali, Ajatashatru or Nagasena); Ajatashatru was the patron.
- **Third council:** at **Pataliputra**, in the **Ashokarama** monastery; recorded only in the Sri Lankan **Dipavamsa and Mahavamsa** – not in Ashoka's inscriptions or the Divyavadana (UPPCS 1999).
- **Fourth council:** held on the advice of the monk **Parsva**; settled **Sarvastivada** disputes of Kashmir–Gandhara; after it Buddhism split into **Mahayana and Hinayana** (SSC 2018; IAS 1993).
> Chronology of rulers: Ashoka (3rd c. BCE) → Milinda/Menander (2nd c. BCE) → Kanishka (1st–2nd c. CE).

=== Hinayana, Mahayana, Vajrayana and the Bodhisattvas
| Sect | Ideal / features | Buddha | Texts / spread |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hinayana / Theravada** | **Arhat** – individual liberation; original teachings | Symbols, **no idols**; a great man, not a god | **Pali** canon; **Sri Lanka, Myanmar**, Thailand; Ashoka patronised it |
| **Mahayana** (after the 4th council, Kanishka) | **Bodhisattva** – delays own nirvana to help all beings; Sunyata | **Image worship – Buddha as god** | **Sanskrit** – Prajnaparamita, Lotus Sutra; China, Japan, Korea, Tibet; Kanishka, Harsha patrons |
| **Vajrayana** ("vehicle of the thunderbolt") | Salvation through magical power (**vajra**); **mantras, mudras, mandalas** | **Taras**, wrathful deities | **Guhyasamaja**, **Arya Manjushrimulakalpa**; Bengal–Bihar, Tibet, Nepal |
- **Fundamental difference:** Mahayana made **images of the Buddha and worshipped him as God**; Hinayana did not (UPPCS 1996). The Buddha was **elevated to God in Kanishka's period**.
- **Vajrayana:** emerged in the **7th century CE** (APPSC AP History), flourished from the 8th c.; **most prominent in the Sultanate period** (JPSC 2013); centre **Vikramashila**; linked with **Padmasambhava**. In Andhra: **Salihundam**.
- **Arya Manjushrimulakalpa** = **Vajrayana** (Mahayana tantra; MPPSC 2023 key also accepted Mahayana).
- **Bodhisattva** – central to **Mahayana, not Hinayana**; a compassionate one on the way to enlightenment who **delays his own salvation** to help all beings (UPSC 2016: B and C only).
- **Six paramitas** (perfections) of the Bodhisattva: **dana, sila, kshanti, virya, dhyana, prajna**.
- **Avalokiteshvara** = **Padmapani** ("lotus-bearer") = Lokeshvara, Bodhisattva of **compassion** (IAS 1997). **Maitreya** = the **future Buddha** yet to come (UPSC 2018). Manjushri = wisdom; Vajrapani = power.
- **Sthaviravadins** = Hinayana (not Mahayana); **Lokottaravadins** = offshoot of the **Mahasanghikas**; Mahasanghika **deification of the Buddha** fostered Mahayana (UPSC 2020: II and III).
- **Satavahana-period Andhra:** **Madhyamika, Mahayana and Hinayana** (Chaityaka sects) – not Zen or Theravada (APPSC ATWO).
- **Tibetan** Buddhism merges Mahayana and Vajrayana with Bon; Mahayana reached Tibet in the 7th c.; **Gelug** school dominant by the 15th c.; canon = **Kangyur and Tengyur**. **Karmapa Lama** = **Kagyu (Kangyupa)** sect, seat Rumtek (Sikkim) (UPPCS 2000).
> Arhat = Hinayana; Bodhisattva = Mahayana; vajra, Taras, Guhyasamaja = Vajrayana.

=== Buddhist Scholars – Nagarjuna, Ashvaghosha, Buddhaghosa, Bhavaviveka
| Scholar | School / fact | Work |
|---|---|---|
| **Nagarjuna** | Founder of **Madhyamika (Shunyavada)** of Mahayana; doctrine of **Shunyata** (void); friend of a Satavahana king | **Mulamadhyamakakarika**, **Suhrllekha**, **Ratnavali (Rajaparikatha)** |
| **Ashvaghosha** | **Kanishka's** court poet; deputy at the 4th council | **Buddhacharita** (Sanskrit), **Saundarananda** |
| **Buddhaghosa** | Theravada, 5th c.; born near **Guntur** (Kota Nemapuri); worked at **Anuradhapura** | **Visuddhimagga** (Pali); Samantapasadika |
| **Bhavaviveka** | Madhyamika; acharya at the **Dhanyakataka** (Amaravati) vihara | Tarkajvala |
| **Aryadeva** | Nagarjuna's disciple; **Madhyamika** – a **Buddhist, not Jaina** scholar | Chatuhshataka |
| **Dignaga (Dinnaga)** | **Buddhist logician**; "Father of Indian logic"; Yogachara | Pramana Samuccaya |
| **Dharmakirti** (7th c.) | "**Kant of India**"; developed Dignaga's logic; taught at Nalanda | Pramana Vartika |
| **Asanga, Vasubandhu** | Brothers (4th c.); **Yogachara** | Vasubandhu – **Abhidharmakosa** |
| **Nagasena** | Dialogue with Menander | Milindapanho |
| **Sanghabhuti** | Monk in China, 381–384 CE | Commentary on the Sarvastivada Vinaya |
- **Nagarjuna – welfare state:** set out the theory of the welfare state (**Shreyorajya**) in the **Ratnavali** (Rajaparikatha-ratnavali), addressed to a Satavahana king (APPSC AP History).
- **Nagarjuna** formulated the **basic ideas of Mahayana** (NDA 2014) and taught **Shunyata** (UPPCS 1998; CDS 2006) – not Nagasena, Ashvaghosha or Shankaracharya.
- **Suhrllekha** – letter to a friend; **I-tsing** saw students memorising it. Title "**Einstein of India**" – idea of relativity.
- **Sriparvata:** royal friend **Yajna Sri Satakarni** built the **1,500-room Maha Vihara (Parvata Vihara)** for Nagarjuna.
- **Siddha Nagarjuna** (Vajrayana alchemist at Sriparvata, later period) is a **different person** from the philosopher.
- **Bhavaviveka = Dhanyakataka** (NextGen; Hiuen Tsang) – a printed key of "Sriparvatam" (APPSC G2 2019) is wrong.
- **Wrong pair:** "**Nagarjuna – Buddhacharita**" (Ashvaghosha wrote it); Hala – Gathasaptasati, Vatsyayana – Kamasutra, Gunadhya – Brihatkatha are right.
- **UPSC 2022:** Aryadeva – Jaina ✗, Dignaga – Buddhist ✓, **Nathamuni – Vaishnava** ✓ → **only two pairs** correct.
> Visuddhimagga = Buddhaghosa (not Nagarjuna, Vasubandhu, Padmasambhava, Dignaga).

=== Tripitaka – Vinaya, Sutta, Abhidhamma and the Nikayas
| Pitaka | Content | Books / compiler |
|---|---|---|
| **Vinaya** | **Rules and discipline of the Sangha** (monastic life) | **Patimokkha** (227 rules for monks), Vibhanga, Khandhaka; **Upali** |
| **Sutta** | **Buddha's sermons on ethics and doctrine** (Buddhavacana) | **5 Nikayas**; **Ananda** |
| **Abhidhamma** | Philosophical analysis of the Dhamma | **7 books**; added at the 3rd council (Moggaliputta Tissa) |
- **Tripitaka** = the collection of the Buddha's teachings, the **Buddhist** canon (not Jain, Sikh or Hindu); written in **Pali**.
- **Patimokkha** = the **rules of the Sangha** (CDS 2009), recited every fortnight.
- **Seven Abhidhamma books:** **Dhammasangani** (first), Vibhanga, Dhatukatha, Puggalapannatti, **Kathavatthu**, **Yamaka**, **Patthana** (seventh). Yamaka belongs to the **Abhidhamma** (JPSC 2016).
| Nikaya (Sutta Pitaka) | Content |
|---|---|
| **Digha** | **Long discourses** in Pali; **Mahaparinibbana Sutta**; Samannaphala Sutta |
| Majjhima | Middle-length discourses |
| Samyutta | Grouped discourses |
| **Anguttara** | Arranged by number; lists the **16 Mahajanapadas** |
| **Khuddaka** | 15 books: **550 Jatakas**, **Dhammapada**, Suttanipata, Buddhavamsa, Theragatha, Therigatha |
- **Branches of Buddhist literature:** **Pitakas and Jatakas**; the **Angas** are the **Jain** canon (CDS 2006).
- **Early literature** is generally **canonical**, but schools do not class their canon as "only the Pitakas" (NDA 2014: only I).
> 16 Mahajanapadas = Anguttara; Jatakas, Dhammapada = Khuddaka; Kathavatthu, Yamaka = Abhidhamma.

=== Non-canonical Texts – Language and Sect
| Text | Language | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Milindapanho** (Milinda-Panha) | **Pali** | Dialogue of monk **Nagasena** and Indo-Greek king **Milinda (Menander)** |
| **Dipavamsa, Mahavamsa** | **Pali** | Sri Lankan chronicles; **Ashoka**, councils; Mahavamsa by **Mahanama** |
| Nettipakarana, **Visuddhimagga** | Pali | Visuddhimagga by Buddhaghosa |
| **Divyavadana** | **Sanskrit** | Buddhist legends (Ashoka) |
| Avadanashataka | Sanskrit | Avadana tales |
| **Arya Manjushrimulakalpa** | **Sanskrit** | Mahayana–Vajrayana, historical data |
| **Buddhacharita** | Sanskrit | **Ashvaghosha** – the Buddha's **biography** |
| **Lalitavistara** | Buddhist hybrid Sanskrit | Buddha biography; source of Edwin Arnold's **The Light of Asia** |
| Mahavastu | Mixed Sanskrit–Prakrit | Biography of the Buddha (Lokottaravada) |
| **Dohakosa** | Apabhramsa | Songs of the Vajrayana siddhas – the **last Buddhist text produced in India** (SSC 2011) |
| Vinayavatthu | Pali | Mentions the "**Andhrapatha**" |
- **Language of propagation:** **Pali**, the people's language (SSC 2017); Sanskrit came with Mahayana.
- **Sanskrit group:** Divyavadana and Manjushrimulakalpa (RAS 2024: 1 and 4); **Pali group:** Dipavamsa, Mahavamsa, Milindapanho.
- **Account of Ashoka:** the **Mahavamsa** (CDS 2018), not the Pitakas.
> Divyavadana is Sanskrit, Milindapanho is Pali; Mahavamsa is by Mahanama, not Ashvaghosha.

=== Philosophical Schools – Sarvastivada to Yogachara
| School | Founder / teacher | Doctrine |
|---|---|---|
| Sthaviravada | Elders at the 2nd council | Orthodox, strict Vinaya → Theravada |
| Mahasanghika | **Mahadeva's** group | Relaxed rules; parent of Chaitika, Lokottaravada and Mahayana |
| **Vaibhashika (Sarvastivada)** | Based on the Mahavibhasa | Dharmas exist in past, present and future ("all exists") |
| **Sautrantika** | **Kumaralata** | Relies on the Suttas; Hinayana |
| **Madhyamika / Shunyavada** | **Nagarjuna**; Aryadeva, Buddhapalita, Bhavaviveka | **Sunyata** (emptiness); midway between realism and idealism |
| **Yogachara / Vijnanavada** | Maitreyanatha, **Asanga, Vasubandhu** | **Only consciousness is real** |
- **Nagarjuna's school = Madhyamika** (UK UDA 2007) – not Sautrantika, Vaibhashika or Yogachara.
- **Aryadeva** = Madhyamika (not Yogachara); Yogachara and Vijnanavada are one school.
