=== Public Health Spending and National Health Accounts
# Public spending – the most-asked numbers
| Year / source | Government (public) health spending | OOPE share of THE |
|---|---|---|
| NHA 2013-14 (first NHA) | **1.15% of GDP**; 28.6% of THE | 64.2% |
| NHA 2017-18 | 1.35% of GDP | – |
| **NHA 2018-19** | **1.28% of GDP** (a dip); 40.6% of THE | 48.2% |
| **NHA 2019-20** | 1.35% of GDP | **47.1% – less than 50%** (62.6% in 2014-15) |
| NHA 2021-22 (COVID peak) | **1.84% of GDP** (highest); about 48% of THE | **39.4%** (fell, did not rise) |
| NHA 2022-23 (10th edition) | **1.43% of GDP**; **43.7%** of THE; per capita ₹2,786 | **43.4%** |
- **Centre + States budgeted spending, 2019-20: about 1.3% of GDP** (Economic Survey 2021-22) – not 2.5 or 2.8.
- **NHA**: prepared by NHATS at **NHSRC** (MoHFW); NHA Guidelines 2016 based on **SHA 2011**; first estimates for **2013-14**.
- **National Health Policy 2017**: public health spending **2.5% of GDP by 2025** (Centre's share 40%) – not 3% or 3.5%; target missed (1.43% in 2022-23).
- **Union Budget 2022-23**: MoHFW about **₹86,200 crore**.
- **15th Finance Commission**: **₹70,051 crore** health grants **through local governments** (2021–26) – diagnostics for SCs/PHCs, urban HWCs, buildings for building-less SCs/PHCs/CHCs.
# Out-of-pocket expenditure (OOPE)
- **Outpatient care, diagnostics and medicines = over 50% of OOPE** – usually not insured.
- Private hospital stay costs about **7.6 times** a government one; private outpatient care about 5 times.
- **"Missing middle"**: over **40 crore** Indians without financial protection – term from **NITI Aayog**.
> Rocket CA 2026 p.24 prints the COVID-year values (OOPE 39.4%, GHE ~48% of THE) under "NHA 2022-23"; the 10th edition itself gives 1.43% of GDP and 43.7% of THE for 2022-23 – the 39.4% belongs to 2021-22.

=== PM-JAY and Health Insurance
- **PM-JAY** (launched **23 Sep 2018**): **health insurance/assurance for economically vulnerable families** – **₹5 lakh per family per year** for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation; **world's largest publicly funded health assurance scheme**.
- Coverage: about **12 crore families (55 crore people)** identified from **SECC 2011**; 26,901 hospitals empanelled (2023).
- **Oct 2024**: all citizens **aged 70+, irrespective of income** (Ayushman Vay Vandana card); 70+ members of covered families get an extra ₹5 lakh top-up.
- Funding: Centre:State **60:40**; **90:10** for North-Eastern and Himalayan States; up to 100% for UTs without legislature. West Bengal became the 36th State/UT to implement it (8 Jun 2026).
- Other schemes: **CGHS 1954, Delhi**; **ESI Act 1948**, scheme inaugurated at **Kanpur, 24 Feb 1952**.
- Trends: government-financed insurance up 2.5-fold (2017-18 to 2025); NFHS-6 households with an insured member 41.0% → 60.2%. Criticism: insurance-led models raise coverage without raising care.
- **Abroad: USA – "ObamaCare"** = Patient Protection and **Affordable Care Act 2010**, the landmark law for **affordable health care**.
~~ Sources: NextGen Environment APPSC Part 8.2 p.11 (PM-JAY); Rocket CA 2026 Part 02 p.65 (West Bengal); app notes; general knowledge, not checked (ObamaCare)

=== Andhra Pradesh Health Assurance
- **Dr. NTR Vaidya Seva** (formerly YSR Aarogyasri): cashless care for BPL families and families with income up to ₹5 lakh; **3,257 procedures**; cover up to **₹25 lakh per family per year**, no limit for cancer; 1.43 crore families benefited; in convergence with PM-JAY.
- **Employees Health Scheme (EHS)**: State Government **employees, pensioners and dependants**; 50:50 premium; 2,510 procedures; **₹2 lakh per episode of illness, no limit on the number of episodes**.
- Working Journalists Health Scheme: premium ₹2,500 a year, shared 50:50.
- AP Universal Health Policy (Sep 2025): up to ₹25 lakh per family; EHS employees excluded. AP Budget 2024-25: ₹4,000 crore to NTR Vaidya Seva Trust; State scheme + EHS + RSBY + ESIS + CGHS = 70% of households (2026).
~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.225-226; app notes
