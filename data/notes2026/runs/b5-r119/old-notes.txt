## SUB 1 | Targets and Finance Commission Grants
[119:1:0] - **NHP 2017**: GHE **2.5% of GDP by 2025**; Centre's share **40%** of public spending – **target missed** (Centre now ~0.29% of GDP).
[119:1:1] - **15th Finance Commission**: **₹70,051 crore** health grants **via local governments** (2021–26) – diagnostic infrastructure for SCs/PHCs, **urban HWCs**, **buildings for building-less SCs/PHCs/CHCs** (not only urban HWCs).
## SUB 2 | Out-of-pocket Expenditure
[119:2:0] - OOPE: **64.2% → 43.4%** of THE (2013-14 to 2022-23); still about **half of current health expenditure**.
[119:2:1] - **Outpatient care, diagnostics, medicines = over 50% of OOPE** – usually not insured.
[119:2:2] - Private care costlier: childbirth ₹37,630 vs ₹2,299.
[119:2:3] - **"Missing middle"**: over **40 crore** Indians without financial protection; term flagged by **NITI Aayog** (parliamentary panel, Sep 2026).
## SUB 3 | Pm-jay
[119:3:0] - Cover **₹5 lakh per family per year**.
[119:3:1] - **Beneficiaries:** poor and vulnerable families identified from **SECC 2011** deprivation data.
[119:3:2] - **Oct 2024**: extended to **all citizens aged 70+, irrespective of income** (**Ayushman Vay Vandana card**); 70+ members of already covered families get an extra **₹5 lakh top-up**.
[119:3:3] - **Scale:** PM-JAY, launched on **23 September 2018**, is the **world's largest health assurance scheme**; it aims to cover over **12 crore families** (nearly **55 crore people**).
[119:3:4] - **Funding:** Centre:State **60:40**; **90:10** for North-Eastern States and three Himalayan States/UTs (J&K, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand); up to **100%** central for UTs without legislature.
## SUB 4 | Insurance Landscape
[119:4:0] - Government-financed insurance up **2.5-fold** (2017-18 to 2025, NSS 80th round).
[119:4:1] - NFHS-6: households with an insured member **41.0% → 60.2%**.
[119:4:2] - Criticism: **insurance-led models raise coverage without increasing care**; fix = more spending on **primary and outpatient care**.
[119:4:3] - **CGHS:** started in **1954** in **Delhi** for Central Government employees and pensioners.
[119:4:4] - **ESI:** **ESI Act 1948**; scheme inaugurated at **Kanpur** on **24 February 1952** by PM Nehru (ESIC Day).
