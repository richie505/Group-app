## SUB 2 | PM-JAY and Health Insurance
[2:0] - **PM-JAY** (launched **23 Sep 2018**): **health insurance/assurance for economically vulnerable families** – **₹5 lakh per family per year** for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation; **world's largest publicly funded health assurance scheme**.
[2:1] - Coverage: about **12 crore families (55 crore people)** identified from **SECC 2011**; 26,901 hospitals empanelled (2023).
[2:2] - **Oct 2024**: all citizens **aged 70+, irrespective of income** (Ayushman Vay Vandana card); 70+ members of covered families get an extra ₹5 lakh top-up.
[2:3] - Funding: Centre:State **60:40**; **90:10** for North-Eastern and Himalayan States; up to 100% for UTs without legislature. West Bengal became the 36th State/UT to implement it (8 Jun 2026).
[2:4] - Other schemes: **CGHS 1954, Delhi**; **ESI Act 1948**, scheme inaugurated at **Kanpur, 24 Feb 1952**.
[2:5] - Trends: government-financed insurance up 2.5-fold (2017-18 to 2025); NFHS-6 households with an insured member 41.0% → 60.2%. Criticism: insurance-led models raise coverage without raising care.
[2:6] - **Abroad: USA – "ObamaCare"** = Patient Protection and **Affordable Care Act 2010**, the landmark law for **affordable health care**.
[2:7] ~~ Sources: NextGen Environment APPSC Part 8.2 p.11 (PM-JAY); Rocket CA 2026 Part 02 p.65 (West Bengal); app notes; general knowledge, not checked (ObamaCare)
## SUB 3 | Andhra Pradesh Health Assurance
[3:0] - **Dr. NTR Vaidya Seva** (formerly YSR Aarogyasri): cashless care for BPL families and families with income up to ₹5 lakh; **3,257 procedures**; cover up to **₹25 lakh per family per year**, no limit for cancer; 1.43 crore families benefited; in convergence with PM-JAY.
[3:1] - **Employees Health Scheme (EHS)**: State Government **employees, pensioners and dependants**; 50:50 premium; 2,510 procedures; **₹2 lakh per episode of illness, no limit on the number of episodes**.
[3:2] - Working Journalists Health Scheme: premium ₹2,500 a year, shared 50:50.
[3:3] - AP Universal Health Policy (Sep 2025): up to ₹25 lakh per family; EHS employees excluded. AP Budget 2024-25: ₹4,000 crore to NTR Vaidya Seva Trust; State scheme + EHS + RSBY + ESIS + CGHS = 70% of households (2026).
[3:4] ~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.225-226; app notes
