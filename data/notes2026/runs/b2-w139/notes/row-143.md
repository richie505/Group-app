=== State Measures and Reservation for Transgender Persons
| State | Measure |
|---|---|
| **Karnataka** | **First State with 1% reservation** for transgender persons in all government (civil) services by direct recruitment – notification **6 July 2021** (horizontal) |
| Tamil Nadu | First **Transgender Welfare Board (2008)**; Third Gender Tailoring Cooperative (2020); Thirunangaigal app (2021) |
| Kerala | **First State transgender policy** (November 2015); Sthree Suraksha scheme |
| Andhra Pradesh | State Transgender Policy **2025**; ~**3,125** certificates via the National Portal |
- **Horizontal reservation** (cutting across SC/ST/OBC/General) is the community's demand; NALSA had given a **vertical**, OBC-type quota.
- The horizontal-reservation demand returned in **June 2023**: **27 June 2023** Mumbai protest by **transgender** persons over **Maharashtra's** reply to the **Bombay High Court** that more reservation was "difficult".
> Trap: Karnataka = job quota; Tamil Nadu = welfare board; Kerala = policy.

=== NALSA v. Union of India (2014) and Related Judgements
- Delivered **15 April 2014** by Justices **K.S. Radhakrishnan** and **A.K. Sikri**; recognised transgender persons as the **"third gender"**.
- **Gender identity is self-determined** – not by biological examination.
- Directions: treat as **SEBC** for reservation (**vertical, OBC-type** quota); separate **public toilets**, health facilities, welfare schemes.
| Case | Year | Issue decided |
|---|---|---|
| K.S. Puttaswamy | 2017 | **Nine**-judge bench: privacy a fundamental right (Art. 21) |
| Navtej Singh Johar | 2018 | Decriminalised same-sex relations (Section 377 IPC) |
| Supriyo v. Union of India | 17 Oct 2023 | 5-judge bench, 3:2: no legal recognition of same-sex marriage; left to Parliament |

=== Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act, 2019 and 2026 Amendment
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 2014 | Rights of Transgender Persons Bill – private member's Bill by **Tiruchi Siva** (DMK) |
| April 2015 | **Rajya Sabha** passed it – first private member's Bill passed there in over four decades; not passed by Lok Sabha |
| 2019 | Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act; in force **10 January 2020** |
| 13 Mar 2026 | Amendment Bill introduced by **Virendra Kumar** in Lok Sabha |
- **Definition:** includes trans men/women, **intersex variations**, genderqueer and socio-cultural identities (kinner, hijra, aravani, jogta); administered by **MoSJE**.
- **Certificate of identity:** **District Magistrate**; revised certificate **after surgery**; application through the **National Portal for Transgender Persons** (Rules 2020).
- Offences (forced labour, abuse etc.) = **6 months to 2 years + fine**; criticism: lighter penalties, DM gatekeeping, **no reservation**.
- **2026 Amendment:** self-perceived identity **excluded** (Sec. 4(2) omitted); certificate by DM on a medical board's advice – criticised for **reversing NALSA's self-identification**; challenged by **Laxmi Narayan Tripathi** and **Zainab Javid Patel**.
- **National Council for Transgender Persons:** statutory (Sec. 16, 2019 Act), constituted **21 August 2020**; chair = **Union Minister for Social Justice and Empowerment**; non-official term **3 years**.
- **NHRC Advisory 2.0** (May 2026): separate **intersex, trans men, trans women** categories in Census 2027.

=== SMILE, Garima Greh and Data
- **SMILE** (Support for Marginalized Individuals for Livelihood and Enterprise): 2022, **MoSJE**; outlay **Rs 365 crore**; goal **"Bhiksha Vritti Mukt Bharat"**; skilling via **PM-DAKSH**.
- **Garima Greh** = **shelter homes** for transgender persons – **23 in 17** States/UTs (ES 2025-26).
- **Census 2011:** about **4.88 lakh** transgender persons; highest **Uttar Pradesh**.
- **NTR Bharosa Pension (AP):** **Rs 4,000/month** for elderly (60+), widows and transgender persons.

=== Provisions for the Elderly
- NSAP rests on **Articles 41 and 42**; **"invalidity and old age pensions"** = **Concurrent List Entry 24**.
- Senior Citizens Act 2007: **Maintenance Tribunal** (sub-division) and Appellate Tribunal (district); max **Rs 10,000/month**; earlier law **Himachal Pradesh, 2001**.
- **NPOP 1999** (older person = **60+**); **NPSC 2011** (elderly women, ageing in place).
- **Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana:** physical aids to BPL seniors; launched **1 April 2017, Nellore**; **AVYAY** (2021) = umbrella of **4**; **SACRED portal** = **re-employment**.
