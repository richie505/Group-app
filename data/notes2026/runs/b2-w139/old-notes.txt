## SUB 1 | Early Childhood Care and Education and Child Development
[139:1:0] # ECCE
[139:1:1] - **Article 45** (after the 86th Amendment, 2002): **early childhood care and education** for all children until they complete **six years**.
[139:1:2] - National ECCE Policy **2013**. **NEP 2020 foundational stage**: ages **3–8** = 3 years Anganwadi/pre-school + **Classes 1–2**.
[139:1:3] - **NCERT's National Curricular and Pedagogical Framework for ECCE** (children up to 8) is in **two parts** (0–3 and 3–8).
[139:1:4] - **NCF for the Foundational Stage (3–8)** – **Panchakosha**: **Sharirik** (physical), **Pranik** (life energy), **Manasik** (mental), **Bauddhik** (intellectual), **Chaitsik** (spiritual) vikas – **Dharmik vikas is not one**.
[139:1:5] - **NISHTHA 4.0** = online teacher training for **ECCE** (NISHTHA 1.0–3.0: elementary, secondary, FLN).
[139:1:6] - **Montessori** method: curriculum **child-centred**, based on the child's natural abilities and self-paced activity.

## SUB 2 | POSHAN Abhiyaan, Poshan 2.0 and Nutrition Campaigns
[139:2:0] # POSHAN Abhiyaan
[139:2:1] - **Prime Minister's Overarching Scheme for Holistic Nourishment** (earlier **National Nutrition Mission**) – the **2018 flagship** for holistic nutrition of children, adolescents, pregnant women and lactating mothers; launched **8 March 2018** at **Jhunjhunu, Rajasthan**. Goal: **Suposhit Bharat**, a **Jan Andolan**.
[139:2:2] - Targets: reduce **stunting**, undernutrition, **anaemia**, low birth weight; stunting to **25% by 2022** ("Mission 25 by 2022").
[139:2:3] - **Objectives (UPSC 2017)**: awareness of malnutrition among pregnant women and lactating mothers; reduce **anaemia** in young children, adolescent girls and women – **not** promoting millets/unpolished rice or poultry eggs.
[139:2:4] # Campaigns and tools
[139:2:5] TABLE head: Campaign / tool | Fact
  [139:2:5.0] Rashtriya Poshan Maah | September; 5th in 2022 (celebrated through Gram Panchayats as "Poshan Panchayats"), 6th in 2023, 9th in 2026 at Varanasi
  [139:2:5.1] Poshan Pakhwada | A fortnight, March–April; 8th edition April 2026 on brain development in the first six years
  [139:2:5.2] Poshan Tracker | Digital monitoring of Anganwadi services – not a panic button (that is 112 India, MHA – single-number police, medical, fire dispatch)
  [139:2:5.3] Poshan Bhi Padhai Bhi | ECCE in Anganwadis through play-based pedagogy
  [139:2:5.4] Poshan Vatika | Nutrition gardens at Anganwadi centres
  [139:2:5.5] Bhartiya Poshan Krishi Kosh | Announced November 2019 by the Ministry of Women and Child Development (with the Gates Foundation) – a repository of diverse crops by agro-climatic zone
  [139:2:5.6] Tikki Mausi | A mascot (Odisha WCD dept with UNICEF, 2019) for child and women's nutrition
[139:2:6] # Saksham Anganwadi and Poshan 2.0 (2021)
[139:2:7] - Introduced in **2021** (MWCD, CSS, 2021-22 to 2025-26); merged **Anganwadi Services**, **POSHAN Abhiyaan** and the **Scheme for Adolescent Girls** (girls **14–18** in Aspirational Districts and NER); **PM POSHAN is not merged**. One of MWCD's three umbrella missions.
[139:2:8] - Funding: **60:40**; **90:10** NE, Himalayan States, J&K; 100% UTs without legislature.
[139:2:9] - Three verticals: nutrition support; **ECCE (3–6 years)**; Anganwadi infrastructure (not school mid-day meals). **2 lakh** AWCs to become Saksham Anganwadis at **40,000 a year**.

## SUB 6 | Girl Child and Child Marriage
[139:6:0] # Child marriage law
[139:6:1] - **Prohibition of Child Marriage Act, 2006**: minimum age **18 (girls) / 21 (boys)** – the minimum marriageable age of girls under law is **18**. Adult male contracting it: RI up to **2 years** + fine up to **₹1 lakh**. Child marriage is **voidable**; petition for annulment within **two years of attaining majority**. Child Marriage Prohibition Officers appointed by **State Governments**.
[139:6:2] - **PCM (Amendment) Bill, 2021**: women's age to **21**; annulment window **two → five years** after majority; **overrides any other law, custom or practice**; amends the 2006 Act → statements A, C, D correct. Referred to a Standing Committee; **lapsed** with the 17th Lok Sabha. **Jaya Jaitly Task Force (2020)** recommended it.
[139:6:3] - **Allahabad HC (1 July 2026)**: PCMA age limits apply to all citizens **irrespective of religion**. **NFHS-5**: **23.3%** of women 20–24 married before 18; highest **West Bengal**.
[139:6:4] - **ALIVA**: data-driven model to end child marriage – **Nayagarh district, Odisha**.

## SUB 2 | Types of Disability – Learning Disability and Blindness
[140:2:0] - **Learning disability** = difficulty in a specific academic skill despite normal intelligence: **dyslexia** (reading), dysgraphia (writing), dyscalculia (maths).
[140:2:1] - **Dyslexia** is the example specific to learning disability; **autism spectrum disorder**, **ADHD** and **mental retardation (intellectual disability)** are separate developmental conditions, not specific learning disabilities.
[140:2:2] - RPwD Act 2016 lists **specific learning disabilities** among its 21 disabilities, separately from autism and intellectual disability.
[140:2:3] - **New definition of blindness (April 2017, MoHFW, WHO criteria):** a person is blind if unable to **count fingers from 3 metres** (the 1976 definition used **6 metres**); programme renamed National Programme for Control of Blindness and Visual Impairment.
[140:2:4] > Trap: dyslexia = learning disability; ADHD and autism are not "specific learning disabilities"; blindness now 3 m, not 6 m.

## SUB 0 | NSAP and Old-age Pensions
[141:0:0] # National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP)
[141:0:1] - Launched **15 August 1995** (first national social assistance programme); ministry **Rural Development (MoRD)**, not Social Justice; rests on **Articles 41 and 42**.
[141:0:2] - Cabinet approved continuation **FY 2026-27 to 2030-31** (outlay ₹53,428 crore); renamed **Pradhan Mantri Samajik Sahayata Yojana** (sub-schemes e.g. PM Vriddhavastha Pension Yojana, PM Divyangjan Pension Yojana) (2026).
[141:0:3] - Five components:
[141:0:4]   - 1. **IGNOAPS** – old-age pension (BPL, 60+): Rs 200/month (60-79), Rs 500 (80+); Centre's share frozen at Rs 200 since 2007; about 2.2 crore beneficiaries (June 2026).
[141:0:5]   - 2. **IGNWPS** – widow pension (BPL widows 40-79).
[141:0:6]   - 3. **IGNDPS** – disability pension (80% or more disability).
[141:0:7]   - 4. **NFBS** – lump sum on death of the primary breadwinner (Rs 20,000).
[141:0:8]   - 5. **Annapurna** – **10 kg free food grains** a month to seniors eligible for but not getting IGNOAPS.

## SUB 2 | Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007
[141:2:0] - Enacted in **2007**; nodal ministry **MoSJE**; brought into force **state by state** from **29 December 2007**.
[141:2:1] - Creates a **legal obligation** on adult **children (biological, adopted, step)** and **legal heirs** to maintain parents and senior citizens who cannot maintain themselves.
[141:2:2] - The Act **punishes children for neglecting old parents**: **abandonment** of a senior citizen – up to **3 months** jail and/or **Rs 5,000** fine.
[141:2:3] - **Old age homes:** at least **one per district**.
[141:2:4] TABLE head: Section | Provision
  [141:2:4.0] Section 20 | Medical care of senior citizens; basis of the NPHCE
  [141:2:4.1] Section 23 | Property transfer made on condition of care can be declared void by the Tribunal if the transferee neglects the parent
  [141:2:4.2] Feature | Detail
  [141:2:4.3] Maintenance Tribunal | Sub-division level; in AP headed by RDO / Sub-Collector
  [141:2:4.4] Appellate Tribunal | District level; in AP headed by the District Collector
  [141:2:4.5] Maximum maintenance | Rs 10,000 per month
  [141:2:4.6] Time limit | Case decided within 90 days
[141:2:5] - **2019 Amendment Bill:** proposed **removing the Rs 10,000 cap** and covering **in-laws and grandchildren**; it **lapsed** (not passed).
[141:2:6] - **First State law:** **Himachal Pradesh** Maintenance of Parents and Dependents Act, **2001**.

[141:2:8] - **Assam PRANAM Act, 2017:** first State law obliging **government employees to care for their parents** (10% of salary can be deducted for neglected parents).
