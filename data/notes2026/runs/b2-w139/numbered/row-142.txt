## SUB 1 | Population Theories and Demographic Transition
[1:0] - **T.R. Malthus**, *An Essay on the Principle of Population* (**1798**): population grows in **geometric progression**, food in **arithmetic progression**.
[1:1] TABLE head: Check | Meaning | Examples
  [1:1.0] Positive checks | Raise the death rate | Famine, disease, war
  [1:1.1] Preventive checks | Lower the birth rate | Moral restraint: late marriage, celibacy
  [1:1.2] Theory | Propounder | 
  [1:1.3] Demographic transition | Warren Thompson and Frank Notestein | 
  [1:1.4] Optimum population | Edwin Cannan | 
[1:2] - Stages of transition: **high birth and death rates** → **death rate falls** → **birth rate falls** → **both low**; India now: **youth-bulge** stage.
[1:3] - **Dependency ratio** = (0-14 + 65+) ÷ (15-64). **Demographic dividend** window lasts till about **2055**.

## SUB 2 | Fertility, Family Planning and Population Policy
[2:0] TABLE head: Source | India | Others
  [2:0.0] NFHS-5 (2019-21) | TFR 2.0 | AP 1.7; Bihar 3.0 (highest)
  [2:0.1] SRS 2024 | TFR 1.9 | Rural 2.1, urban 1.5; Delhi 1.2 (lowest)
[2:1] - India: **first national family planning programme** in the world (**1952**); early approach **clinic-based**, mainly in cities.
[2:2] - **ICPD, Cairo 1994**: shift from population control to individual well-being; **April 1996**: **target-free approach**.
[2:3] - **NPP 2000:** immediate – unmet need for contraception, health infrastructure; medium-term – TFR 2.1 by 2010; long-term – **stable population by 2045**.
[2:4] - **National Commission on Population** (May 2000): chaired by the **Prime Minister**.

## SUB 3 | Effects of Population Growth and Control Debates
[3:0] - Jobs needed for about **9.7 million** new working-age people a year (5 Aug 2026); land – **smaller land holdings**.
[3:1] - Southern States' concern: population has **15-25%** weight in **Finance Commission** formulas.
[3:2] - **Javed v. State of Haryana (2003)**: SC **upheld** the two-child norm for local body polls.
