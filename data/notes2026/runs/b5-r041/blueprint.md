## 3. Angles
- **Factual recall** dominates (~70%): launch year, vehicle, lander/rover names, first-to-X.
- **Match the following:** mission ↔ month (APPSC 2024 recruitment), launch ↔ date (NTRUHS), mission ↔ vehicle (TSPSC 2018), mission ↔ objective (Grama Sachivalayam ×2).
- **Statements / NOT:** Mangalyaan (UPSC 2016, UP RO 2023), Chandrayaan-2 false pair (DAO 2020), LUPEX (×2), XPoSat (×2), Chang'e-5, ExoMars, Shukrayaan (×2).
- **Assertion-reason:** Luna-25 / ILRS; shuttle radio blackout.
- **Current linkage:** every APPSC 2022-24 paper asked the latest launch (Ch-3, Aditya-L1, XPoSat, H3, SpaDeX). Expect Artemis II (Apr 2026), SpaDeX re-entry (Oct 2026), Chandrayaan-4 (2028), JAXA MMX (Oct 2026).
