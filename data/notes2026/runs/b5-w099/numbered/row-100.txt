## SUB 0 | Bharat Stage Timeline and Supreme Court Deadlines
[0:0] - **First vehicle emission norms:** **1991 (petrol), 1992 (diesel)** – before the Euro-based system.
[0:1] - **Mashelkar Committee → National Auto Fuel Policy (2003) → Euro norms** (roadmap for BS stages).
[0:2] - **Euro-based norms under the Bharat Stage name from 2000** (four-wheelers, APPSC Asst. Chemist); BS-I was also called **"India 2000"**.
[0:3] - **Euro I** came into force in Europe in **1992**; India generally lagged Europe by about **5 years**.
[0:4] - **Who notifies:** tailpipe standards notified under the **Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989** by **MoRTH**; CPCB/MoEFCC set the pollution side (Shankar credits CPCB under MoEFCC with implementation).
[0:5] TABLE head: Stage | Euro equivalent | First applied | Nationwide
  [0:5.0] BS-I ("India 2000") | Euro I | – | 2000
  [0:5.1] BS-II | Euro II | Delhi, 1 April 2000 | 2005
  [0:5.2] BS-III | Euro III | NCR + 10 cities incl. Hyderabad-Secunderabad, 1 April 2005 | 2010
  [0:5.3] BS-IV | Euro IV | 13 cities, 2010 | 1 April 2017
  [0:5.4] BS-V | Euro 5 | Skipped | –
  [0:5.5] BS-VI | Euro 6 | – | 1 April 2020
  [0:5.6] BS-VI Phase 2 | – | Real Driving Emissions (RDE) + OBD Stage II-A | 1 April 2023 (OBD II-B from 1 April 2025)
  [0:5.7] BS-VII | Euro 7 | Planned 2026-27 | –
[0:6] - **Supreme Court, 29 March 2017 (M.C. Mehta case):** **no sale or registration of BS-III (and below) vehicles from 1 April 2017**.
[0:7] - **Supreme Court, 2018:** **no sale or registration of BS-IV vehicles from 1 April 2020** (APPSC Polytechnic Lecturers).
[0:8] - **Leapfrog decision 2016:** BS-IV straight to BS-VI – urgent **Delhi-NCR** air pollution, close the gap with Europe in **one step**; BS-V is the only stage skipped.
[0:9] - **BS-VI statements (APPSC NTRUHS):** BS-IV and BS-VI are based on **Euro 4 and Euro 6**; **RDE introduced for the first time**; **On-Board Diagnostics (OBD) is mandatory** – "OBD not necessary" is the **INCORRECT** statement.

## SUB 1 | Fuel Sulphur, Pollutant Limits and Control Technologies
[1:0] TABLE head: Stage | Diesel sulphur | Petrol sulphur
  [1:0.0] BS-I | ~2,500 ppm | –
  [1:0.1] BS-II | 500 ppm | 500 ppm
  [1:0.2] BS-III | 350 ppm | 150 ppm
  [1:0.3] BS-IV | 50 ppm | 50 ppm
  [1:0.4] BS-VI | 10 ppm (−80% vs BS-IV) | 10 ppm
[1:1] - Euro II ultra-low sulphur diesel: **0.05%** or less = **500 ppm**.
[1:2] - Euro / BS norms limit **CO, hydrocarbons, NOx, PM**; Euro norms also cover **lead** ("all of the above" with CO, HC, lead).
[1:3] - **BS-VI limits:** NOx **60 mg/km petrol, 80 mg/km diesel**; diesel PM **4.5 mg/km**; NOx down ~70%, PM ~80%.
[1:4] - Refineries had to supply **10 ppm fuel nationwide**; sulphur cut 500 → 10 ppm in about **15 years**.
[1:5] TABLE head: Technology | Function
  [1:5.0] Catalytic converter (BS-I era) | CO → CO2
  [1:5.1] Multi-point fuel injection (BS-II) | Replaced carburettors
  [1:5.2] SCR | Diesel NOx → N2 + water using urea AdBlue
  [1:5.3] DPF | Traps soot, periodically oxidises it
  [1:5.4] OBD-II | Monitors emissions, alerts driver
  [1:5.5] Electronic Fuel Injection | Two-wheelers

## SUB 2 | Vehicular Pollutants and Health Effects
[2:0] TABLE head: Pollutant | Effect
  [2:0.0] CO | Forms carboxyhaemoglobin; cuts oxygen supply
  [2:0.1] NOx | Respiratory illness; forms ground-level ozone and acid rain
  [2:0.2] PM2.5 | Deep lungs/bloodstream; heart and lung disease
  [2:0.3] Benzene | Carcinogen – leukaemia
  [2:0.4] Lead | Neurotoxin; leaded petrol phased out by 2000
[2:1] - **Photochemical smog** needs NOx + hydrocarbons + **sunlight** (cannot form without sunlight).
[2:2] - **Chernobyl** = nuclear (radioactive) pollution. **Nanoparticles** pollute water, soil and the food chain.
[2:3] - **Bioremediation** uses **microorganisms**.
[2:4] ~~ Sources: PMF Environment Part 04 p.5, p.43; app notes 5:100 sub 4
## SUB 3 | Allied Measures – PUC, E20, CAFE, Scrappage
[3:0] - **PUC** certificate mandatory under the **CMV Rules, 1989** (generally 6 months; 12 months for BS-IV/BS-VI vehicles).
[3:1] - **E20:** notified **17 Feb 2026**; all States/UTs from **1 April 2026**, minimum **RON 95** (not 91); replaced the 2021 E10 notification; concerns – mileage, older vehicles.
[3:2] - **CAFE III** third draft (16 July 2026): cars from **April 2027**; credits for ethanol, CBG, biofuels; targets about **21% relaxed** (not stricter) vs the Sept 2025 proposal.
[3:3] - **Vehicle Scrappage Policy (2021):** Automated Testing Stations + **Registered Vehicle Scrapping Facilities**.
