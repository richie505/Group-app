=== Air Pollution in Numbers – Studies, Reports and Firsts
- **WHO urban air-quality database (May 2016):** **Gwalior** = most polluted Indian city (2nd in the world after Zabol, Iran), followed by **Allahabad**, Patna and Raipur.
- **WHO (2022 database update):** **99%** of the world's population in **2019** lived where WHO air-quality guideline levels were not met.
- **University of Maryland study (2017, *Scientific Reports*, NASA OMI satellite data):** India's **SO2** emissions rose by about **50% since 2007**, while China's fell by about 75%.
- **MoEF reply on air-pollution deaths:** acute respiratory infection deaths in **2006–2015** were **35,616**; **West Bengal** had the most, then **Andhra Pradesh**.
- **IIT Bombay study:** the economic cost of air pollution in **Delhi and Mumbai in 2015** was about **₹70,000 crore**.
- **Odd-even road rationing:** **Delhi** was the first to use it, **1–15 January 2016**, against winter smog.
- **Delhi average AQI 2021 (CPCB, printed key):** **144**. Caution: CAQM/CPCB year-round daily averages for 2021 are usually quoted at about 209, so the 144 figure may cover only part of the year.
