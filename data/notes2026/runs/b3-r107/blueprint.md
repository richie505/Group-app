## 4. Corrections to the old notes
- ES 2016-17 does not say women without toilets "travel long distances" – it lists safety threats and reduced food and water intake (kept as the APPSC key with a dispute line).
- Gender budgeting: NextGen 2005-06 vs Ramesh Singh 2006-07 – most books 2005-06.
- Centre's non-tax revenue among mines/forests/opium/roads = roads (Receipt Budget 2012-13), not mines.
- NextGen calls it "Acworth Committee (1924)"; the committee reported in 1921 and separation came in 1924 (Laxmikanth).
