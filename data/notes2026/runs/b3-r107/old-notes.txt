=== NOTES 3:107 | Union Budget - Structure, Process, Key Documents; Highlights of the Latest Union Budget
## SUB 0 | Budget - Meaning and Constitutional Basis
[107:0:0] - Word from French **bougette** = "little bag". The Constitution does **not** use the word "Budget".
[107:0:1] - **Article 112**: **Annual Financial Statement (AFS)** - estimated receipts and expenditure of the Government of India.
[107:0:2] - The Budget is the main instrument of **fiscal policy**; functions: **resource allocation, income redistribution, stabilisation**.
[107:0:3] - Prepared and presented by the **Budget Division, Department of Economic Affairs (DEA), Ministry of Finance**, with NITI Aayog and spending ministries. DEA also prepares budgets of States under President's Rule and UTs.
[107:0:4] - **Revenue (tax) estimates**: **Department of Revenue** (CBDT and CBIC) in the **Ministry of Finance**.
[107:0:5] **Banking Division** is NOT a Department of the Ministry of Finance.
[107:0:6] # Articles
[107:0:7] TABLE head: Article | Provision
  [107:0:7.0] 110(1)(a) | Finance Bill (tax provisions) as Money Bill
  [107:0:7.1] 112 | Annual Financial Statement
  [107:0:7.2] 112(3) | List of charged expenditure
  [107:0:7.3] 113 | Estimates / Demands for Grants - only Lok Sabha votes
  [107:0:7.4] 114 / 114(3) | Appropriation Bill / no withdrawal from CFI without an Appropriation Act
  [107:0:7.5] 115 | Supplementary, additional or excess grants
  [107:0:7.6] 116 | Vote on account, vote of credit, exceptional grants
  [107:0:7.7] 266 / 266(2) | Consolidated Fund / Public Account
  [107:0:7.8] 267 | Contingency Fund - corpus ₹30,000 crore from FY 2021-22
[107:0:8] - **Public Account**: provident funds, small savings, deposits - government acts as **banker**; withdrawals need **no appropriation**.

## SUB 2 | Budget Types
[107:2:0] TABLE head: Type | Meaning
  [107:2:0.0] Balanced | Receipts = expenditure
  [107:2:0.1] Surplus | Receipts > expenditure; checks inflation, may slow growth
  [107:2:0.2] Deficit | Expenditure > receipts (India's normal case); counter-cyclical, risks inflation and debt
  [107:2:0.3] Zero-based | Each manager justifies the entire budget from the beginning (zero) every period
  [107:2:0.4] Performance | Links spending to activities/work done
  [107:2:0.5] Outcome | Links outlays to outputs and outcomes (Output-Outcome Framework)
  [107:2:0.6] Gender | Allocations for women: Part A (100%), Part B (30-99%), Part C (below 30%)
  [107:2:0.7] Line-item | Traditional; control over individual items
[107:2:1] - **Zero-based**: developed by **Peter Pyhrr** (1970s); first adopted in the **USA**; first used in government by **Jimmy Carter**.
[107:2:2] - **ZBB in India:** adopted across Union ministries from **1986-87**; contrasts with **incremental** budgeting.
[107:2:3] - **Performance**: from **USA**, first **Hoover Commission (1949)**; India from the **late 1960s** on **ARC** advice.
[107:2:4] - **Gender Budget Statement = Statement 13**, since 2005-06; 2026-27: **₹5.01 lakh crore (9.37%)** - highest ever.
[107:2:5] - 16th Finance Commission ties Centrally Sponsored Schemes to measurable outcomes.
## SUB 3 | Receipts Classification
[107:3:0] # The Test
[107:3:1] TABLE head: Basis | Revenue receipts | Capital receipts
  [107:3:1.0] Liability | Create no liability | Create a liability
  [107:3:1.1] Assets | Do not reduce assets | Or reduce assets
[107:3:2] - **Correct order**: **Revenue Receipts → Tax Revenue → Non-tax Revenue → Capital Receipts**.
[107:3:3] # Items
[107:3:4] TABLE head: Receipt | Examples
  [107:3:4.0] Tax revenue | Income tax, corporation tax (direct); GST, customs, excise (indirect)
  [107:3:4.1] Non-tax revenue | Interest receipts, dividends and profits (RBI surplus, PSU dividends), spectrum, fees, fines
  [107:3:4.2] Debt capital | Market borrowings, T-Bills (short-term domestic borrowing), external loans (World Bank), small savings/PF, RBI borrowing
  [107:3:4.3] Non-debt capital | Recovery of loans, disinvestment, other receipts
[107:3:5] - **Interest received on loans** does NOT create a liability - revenue receipt.
[107:3:6] - **Small savings** are NOT in the revenue account (debt receipts, capital account).
[107:3:7] - **Disinvestment / PSU stake sale** = capital receipt that **reduces assets** - NOT a revenue receipt, NOT a liability.
[107:3:8] - **Paper taxes**: wealth tax, gift tax, estate duty - never large, now abolished; GST is not one.
[107:3:9] # Revenue and Capital Accounts
[107:3:10] - **Revenue account** = current-year transactions (taxes, interest, profits, dividends, revenue expenditure).
[107:3:11] - **Capital account** = assets and liabilities (borrowings, disinvestment, loan recovery, asset creation).
[107:3:12] - **Capital Budget** = capital receipts (market borrowings, T-Bills, foreign loans) + capital payments (land, buildings, machinery, investment in shares, loans to States/UTs).
[107:3:13] - Rule: **borrow only to invest**. Mnemonic: Revenue = Running the government; Capital = Creating assets.
## SUB 4 | Expenditure Classification
[107:4:0] TABLE head: Pair | Distinction
  [107:4:0.0] Revenue vs capital | Revenue creates no asset (salaries, allowances, pensions, interest, subsidies, grants to States); capital creates assets or reduces liabilities (capital works, loans given, debt repayment)
  [107:4:0.1] Plan vs non-plan | Plan = Central Plans + central assistance for State/UT Plans; non-plan = interest, salaries, pensions, defence, subsidies, statutory transfers, maintenance
  [107:4:0.2] Developmental vs non-developmental | Economic and social services vs interest, defence, administration, pensions
[107:4:1] - **Interest payments** = classic **non-plan** item; **largest non-plan item** (2015-16); **most important revenue-account expenditure**.
[107:4:2] - Plan/non-plan replaced by **revenue/capital** from **2017-18** (not 2010-11).
[107:4:3] - **Defence** = non-development, "committed" spending. **Revenue expenditure** = largest in amount.
[107:4:4] - **Capital receipts are less than revenue receipts** (2010-11 and now).
## SUB 5 | Charged vs Voted Expenditure
[107:5:0] - **Charged (Art. 112(3))**: comes straight from the Consolidated Fund; **discussable but not voted** (Art. 113(1)); shown separately in Demands.
[107:5:1] # Charged Items - Full List
[107:5:2] - 1. Emoluments and office expenses of the **President**.
[107:5:3] - 2. Salaries of **Chairman/Deputy Chairman (RS)** and **Speaker/Deputy Speaker (LS)**.
[107:5:4] - 3. **Debt charges** - interest on public debt, redemption.
[107:5:5] - 4. Salaries, allowances, pensions of **Supreme Court judges**; **pensions of High Court judges**.
[107:5:6] - 5. Salary and office expenses of the **CAG**.
[107:5:7] - 6. **UPSC** chairman, members and office expenses.
[107:5:8] - 7. Sums for **court decrees, judgments, awards**.
[107:5:9] SC judges = salaries + pensions charged; **HC judges = only pensions** charged.
## SUB 6 | Budget Process in Parliament
[107:6:0] - **Budget circular** issued in **September**; **Halwa ceremony** marks printing at **North Block**.
[107:6:1] - Presented in **Lok Sabha** on the first working day of February - in practice **1 February even on a Sunday** (2026). Economic Survey a day or more earlier.
[107:6:2] # Six Stages
[107:6:3] TABLE head: Stage | Key facts
  [107:6:3.0] 1. Presentation | FM in Lok Sabha
  [107:6:3.1] 2. General discussion | Both Houses, 3-4 days; no cut motions or voting
  [107:6:3.2] 3. Committee scrutiny | Departmental standing committees examine demands
  [107:6:3.3] 4. Voting on demands | Only Lok Sabha; President's recommendation needed; RS cannot vote; cut motions here
  [107:6:3.4] 5. Appropriation Bill | Art. 114
  [107:6:3.5] 6. Finance Bill | Money Bill; tax proposals
[107:6:4] # Control Terms
[107:6:5] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [107:6:5.0] Policy cut | Demand reduced to Re 1
  [107:6:5.1] Economy cut | Reduced by a stated amount
  [107:6:5.2] Token cut | Reduced by ₹100
  [107:6:5.3] Guillotine | Last allotted day: all outstanding demands voted without discussion
  [107:6:5.4] Vote on account (Art. 116) | Part-year spending, about two months (one-sixth); 3-5 months in election years
  [107:6:5.5] Interim budget | Full statement of receipts and expenditure by an outgoing government in an election year
[107:6:6] - **Appropriation Bill**: no amendment may vary a voted grant, change its purpose or vary charged expenditure.
[107:6:7] - **Finance Bill**: passed within **75 days** (**Provisional Collection of Taxes Act, 1931**).
[107:6:8] - Vote on account still used in election years - not ineffectual after the 1 February shift.
[107:6:9] - **Public Accounts Committee** = Parliament's audit control (examines **CAG** reports).
[107:6:10] - Supplementary demands 2025-26 (second batch): **Economic Stabilisation Fund ₹1 lakh crore**.

## SUB 10 | Deficits - Be vs Re
[107:10:0] TABLE head: Year | Fiscal deficit (% GDP)
  [107:10:0.0] 2024-25 RE | 4.8
  [107:10:0.1] 2025-26 BE / RE | 4.4
  [107:10:0.2] 2026-27 BE | 4.3
[107:10:1] - **FD RE 2024-25 (4.8%) > BE 2025-26 (4.4%)**.
[107:10:2] - Formulas: **FD** = total expenditure - receipts excluding borrowings; **PD** = FD - interest; **RD** = revenue expenditure - revenue receipts.
[107:10:3] - Worked: FD ₹80,000 crore - interest ₹25,000 crore = **PD ₹55,000 crore**.
[107:10:4] - FY26: revenue expenditure up only 1.8%; FD at 62.3% of BE by Nov 2025. FY27: FD at **26.8%** of target by July (CGA, 1 Sep 2026); tax devolution Apr-Jul 2026 ₹3,72,354 crore, ₹56,190 crore lower than a year earlier.
[107:10:5] - Criticism: interest takes **40% of revenue receipts**; underspending in JJM and PMAY in 2025-26.
[107:10:6] - States' combined FD FY25 **3.2%** of GDP; **18 States** exceeded the 3% limit in FY24.
[107:10:7] - **AP Budget 2026-27**: expenditure **₹3,10,058 crore** (+12%); capital outlay ₹48,698 crore. CAG flagged AP's revenue deficit three years running; revenue receipts only 48% of BE by Nov 2025.
