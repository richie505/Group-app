## SUB 0 | Sources of Air Pollution
[99:0:0] # Natural vs Anthropogenic
[99:0:1] TABLE head: Basis | Examples
  [99:0:1.0] Natural | Forest fires (APPSC 2025), volcanic eruptions, dust storms; volcanoes = natural SOx; lightning = natural NOx
  [99:0:1.1] Anthropogenic | Vehicles, power plants, industry, biomass burning, agriculture (ammonia, methane), waste burning
[99:0:2] - Volcanic eruptions emit all four: pyroclastic debris, ash and dust, nitrogen compounds, sulphur compounds.
[99:0:3] - Main source in **Indian cities: automobile emissions** (APPSC 2025). Main pollutants in metros: **CO and SO2**.
[99:0:4] - NCR: **transport** = largest primary source, **23% of winter load** (CAQM synthesis report, Jan 2026).
[99:0:5] - Vehicle/fuel combustion products: CO, NOx, VOCs, PM, unburnt hydrocarbons, lead.
[99:0:6] - **Steel industry** emits SOx, NOx, CO and CO2 (all four).
[99:0:7] - **Crop residue burning** releases CO, methane, ozone, SO2 (also NOx, CO2, black carbon, VOCs).
[99:0:8] - Tree for preventing dust pollution: **Sita Ashok**. Sleeping under a tree at night is harmful – it releases **CO2**.
[99:0:9] # Fuel → Emissions
[99:0:10] TABLE head: Fuel | Emissions
  [99:0:10.0] Coal | High CO2, SO2, NOx, PM, ash – most polluting
  [99:0:10.1] Petrol / diesel | CO2, CO, NOx, unburnt HC, PM; diesel gives more PM than petrol
  [99:0:10.2] LPG / CNG | Less CO, HC, PM; still emit CO2; LPG burns without smoke
  [99:0:10.3] Biogas | Low; mainly CO2, traces of H2S when untreated
  [99:0:10.4] Hydrogen | Cleanest fuel; only water – no carbon, CO, SO2 or particles
  [99:0:10.5] Wood, cow-dung | Smoke, CO, fine particles – indoor pollution
## SUB 1 | Primary vs Secondary Pollutants and Smog
[99:1:0] TABLE head: Type | Examples
  [99:1:0.0] Primary (emitted directly) | CO, SO2, NOx, PM, lead from vehicles
  [99:1:0.1] Secondary (formed in air) | O3, PAN, photochemical smog, ammonium sulphate, ammonium nitrate
  [99:1:0.2] Smog | Formed from | Conditions
  [99:1:0.3] Classical / London | SO2, smoke, particulates + fog | Cold, humid
  [99:1:0.4] Photochemical / Los Angeles (summer smog) | NOx + VOCs + sunlight → ozone, PAN (peroxyacetyl nitrate) | Warm, sunny, traffic-heavy
[99:1:1] - Photochemical smog **always contains ozone**; nitrogen oxides are the key input. Smog is a **secondary** pollutant.
[99:1:2] - **Asian Brown Cloud** (2002): soot, **black carbon**, sulphate aerosols over South Asia and Indian Ocean, dry season (**January–March**); from biomass and fossil-fuel burning.
[99:1:3] London smog = cold + SO2; Los Angeles smog = warm + sunlight. Ground-level ozone is not emitted directly.
## SUB 2 | Pollutants → Source and Health Effect
[99:2:0] TABLE head: Pollutant | Source | Effect
  [99:2:0.0] CO | Incomplete combustion; vehicle exhaust (main gas), cigarettes | Forms carboxyhaemoglobin; affinity for haemoglobin 200–250 times oxygen → hypoxia; "most dangerous"
  [99:2:0.1] SO2 | Fossil fuels, coal power, smelters, diesel | Eye/airway irritation; acid rain; secondary PM2.5
  [99:2:0.2] NOx / NO2 | Vehicles, power plants, boilers | Respiratory illness; acid rain, smog
  [99:2:0.3] PM2.5 / PM10 | Combustion, construction, road dust, biomass | Heart/lung disease; PM2.5 crosses placenta – pre-term birth, low birth weight (AIIMS, 3 June 2026)
  [99:2:0.4] Ground O3 | Photochemical (secondary) | Asthma, crop damage
  [99:2:0.5] Lead | Leaded petrol, smelters | Brain damage; high blood pressure, kidney damage
  [99:2:0.6] H2S | Decaying matter (main), sewage, refineries, landfills, volcanoes | Rotten-egg smell; toxic
  [99:2:0.7] Ammonia | Fertilisers + livestock (77–80%) | Secondary particle formation
  [99:2:0.8] Magnetite particles | Combustion, brake wear | Found in brain tissue; possible link to Alzheimer's
  [99:2:0.9] Fly ash | Coal thermal plants | –
  [99:2:0.10] Cadmium / PAHs | Metal smelting / incomplete burning | –
[99:2:1] - Cigarette smoke: **CO and benzene** (also HCN, arsenic, formaldehyde). Inorganic gaseous pollutants: HCN, H2S.
[99:2:2] - Gases: CO2, methane = greenhouse; **stratospheric ozone** = UV shield, depleted by **CFCs**.
[99:2:3] - Secondary PM2.5: **ammonium sulphate** ≈ one-third of India's PM2.5; ammonium nitrate up to 50%.
[99:2:4] - IIT Delhi: curbing power-plant SO2 could prevent ~**1.24 lakh deaths** a year.
[99:2:5] - **RSPM** = particles **< 5 µm** (generally taken as PM2.5).
[99:2:6] "Vehicle gas that cuts the blood's oxygen-carrying capacity" = carbon monoxide.
[99:2:7] - **Ammonium sulphate formation:** SO2 is oxidised in the air to sulphate, which reacts with ammonia (NH3) to form ammonium sulphate particles.

## SUB 9 | Acid Rain – Causes and Chemistry
[99:9:0] - Precipitation with **pH below 5.6**; typical **4.2–4.4**; each pH step = tenfold acidity. Normal rain ~5.6 (carbonic acid).
[99:9:1] - Term: **Robert Angus Smith, 1872** (Manchester coal burning).
[99:9:2] - Causes: **SO2 and NOx** (APPSC 2018: SO2 and NO2); main single factor **SO2**; main source industries (fossil fuels). **Not** CO, O3, CH4, CFCs or hydrogen.
[99:9:3] TABLE head: Gas | Anthropogenic | Natural
  [99:9:3.0] SOx | Coal power plants, sulphide-ore smelting, H2SO4 plants, refineries | Volcanoes, oceans, decay
  [99:9:3.1] NOx | Vehicles, power plants, biomass burning | Lightning, forest fires
[99:9:4] - Reaction: SO2 + NOx + O2 + water + photo-oxidants → **H2SO4 (~two-thirds, maximum)** + **HNO3 (~one-third)**.
[99:9:5] - **Wet deposition** (rain, snow, fog) vs **dry deposition** (gases/particles settle) – about **half** returns dry.
[99:9:6] A-R item: CO2, SO2, NO2 dissolve in rain and gases pollute air – both true, but not cause-and-effect. Coal plants emit carbon oxides too, but acid rain is due to SO2 and NOx.
[99:9:7] - Gold dissolves in **aqua regia** ('royal water', 3 conc HCl : 1 HNO3), not in nitric acid alone – asked with acid rain.

[99:10:3] - **M.C. Mehta v. Union of India (1996)**: SC barred coal/coke in TTZ → natural gas or relocation.
[99:10:4] - Regions: **Scandinavia** (Norway, Sweden – most acid rain among options) from UK/central Europe – transboundary; industrial China; **India = largest SO2 emitter (2022), over 20%**.
[99:10:5] - Control: **FGD scrubbers**, low-NOx burners, low-sulphur fuels (IMO ship caps), catalytic converters; **liming** = remedial, not preventive; US Clean Air Act cut SO2 >90%.
