=== Federal Doctrines – Pith and Substance, Repugnancy and Others
| Doctrine | Meaning | Field |
|---|---|---|
| **Pith and substance** | Judge a law by its **true nature**; incidental trespass on the other List is fine – the doctrine used to decide **encroachment of the Union List on the State List** | Union–State Lists |
| **Colourable legislation** | What cannot be done directly cannot be done indirectly; "fraud on the Constitution" | Tests **competence, not motive** |
| **Territorial nexus** | State law may operate outside the State if there is a sufficient nexus | Extra-territorial operation |
| **Harmonious construction** | Read conflicting provisions so that **both operate** | Provision vs provision |
| **Incidental and ancillary** | An entry covers matters incidental to it; widest meaning | Entries |
| **Repugnancy / federal paramountcy** | Conflict on the same Concurrent subject – Union law prevails (Art. 254) | Concurrent List |
| **Parens patriae** | The **State as legal protector of citizens unable to protect themselves** (minors, persons of unsound mind, victims) | Welfare |
- Not to be confused: **eclipse** (pre-Constitution law vs FRs), **eminent domain** (State's power to acquire property), lex loci.
- True character is read from the **whole Act, its object and effect**, not its title (A.S. Krishna, 1957).

=== Repugnancy – Article 254
- **Concurrent List**: Parliament's law **prevails** over a repugnant State law (254(1)) – but not "without any exception".
- **Art. 254(2)**: a State law on a **Concurrent List** matter, **reserved for and assented to by the President**, **prevails in that State** – so "if the State law is within the Concurrent List it will prevail, subject to the assent of the President" (APPSC G2 2011).
- Parliament **may later override** such a law; the assent gives **no permanent immunity**.
- **State List**: State law prevails in its own field; 254(2) applies **only to Concurrent List** matters.

=== Fundamental Rights Doctrines (Article 13)
| FR doctrine | Meaning | Case |
|---|---|---|
| **Eclipse** | Pre-Constitution law conflicting with FRs is **overshadowed, not void** | Bhikaji Narain (1955); Ambica Mills (1974) |
| **Severability** | Only the invalid part goes if the rest is complete | R.M.D.C. (1957) |
| **Waiver** | FRs **cannot be waived** | Basheshar Nath (1959) |
- Federal (not FR) doctrines: pith and substance, colourable legislation, territorial nexus, incidental and ancillary, repugnancy – **eclipse is not a federal doctrine**.

=== Constitutional Morality and Transformative Constitutionalism
- **Constitutional morality** = fidelity to **constitutional values over popular morality**; phrase used by **Ambedkar, after Grote**.
| Case (2018) | Doctrine used |
|---|---|
| Navtej Johar | Both |
| Sabarimala | Both |
| NCT Delhi | Constitutional morality only |
| Joseph Shine | Transformative constitutionalism only |
- Criticism: **subjective**; a route to **judicial overreach**.
- **Secularism** as a constitutional value = **equal respect for all religions and no discrimination on grounds of religion** (sarva dharma sambhava).
