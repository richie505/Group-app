=== Rights and Liberty – Article 21 and the Early Cases
| Case | Held |
|---|---|
| **A.K. Gopalan (1950)** | Narrow Art. 21 – court only checks that the procedure in the law was followed (preventive detention) |
| **Champakam Dorairajan (1951)** | FRs over DPSPs (communal G.O.) → **1st Amendment**, **Art. 15(4)** |
| **Berubari (1960)** | Advisory: ceding territory needs a constitutional amendment; Preamble **not** part of the Constitution (reversed in Kesavananda) |
| **Golaknath (1967)** | FRs cannot be amended – they let a man "chalk out his life" |
| **ADM Jabalpur (1976)** | FRs including Art. 21 unenforceable during Emergency; overruled in Puttaswamy |
| **Maneka Gandhi (1978)** | Travel abroad; procedure must be **just, fair and reasonable**; **golden triangle 14, 19, 21**; life with dignity |
| **Bachan Singh (1980)** | Death penalty only in the "**rarest of rare**" cases |
| **Randhir Singh (1982)** | **Equal pay for equal work** read from **Arts. 14 and 16** with **39(d)** and the word "**Socialist**" in the Preamble |
| **Shreya Singhal (2015)** | **Sec. 66A IT Act** struck – Art. 19(1)(a) |
| **K.S. Puttaswamy (2017)** | **Privacy** a FR under Art. 21 / Part III; nine judges, CJI **J.S. Khehar**; applied to DNA paternity tests |

- Later order: **Bommai (1994) → Vishaka (1997) → Shreya Singhal (2015) → Puttaswamy (2017)**.

=== Federalism, Governor and the Floor Test
- **S.R. Bommai (1994)**, **nine judges**: the judgment that **redefined Centre–State relations** – Art. 356 proclamations are **judicially reviewable**; majority to be tested **on the floor of the House**; **secularism and federalism** basic.
- **Composite floor test**: ordered by the Supreme Court in **Kalyan Singh v. Jagdambika Pal (February 1998, U.P.)** – a single vote between the two rival claimants (Kalyan Singh won 225–196).
- **Rameshwar Prasad (2006)**: dissolution of the **Bihar Assembly** void. **Nabam Rebia (2016)**: Governor acts on **advice**.
- **State of Tamil Nadu v. Governor (April 2025)**: **deemed assent** for long-pending Bills; **Presidential reference opinion (November 2025)**: **no fixed timelines** and no deemed assent.
- **Sarbananda Sonowal (2005)**: **IMDT Act** struck for breaching the Union's duty under **Art. 355**.

=== Reservation Judgments
- **Indra Sawhney (1992)**: upheld **27% OBC** quota; **caste can be a factor** in identifying backwardness; **creamy layer** excluded; **50% ceiling**; no reservation in promotions (→ 77th Amendment).
- **M. Nagaraj (2006)**: promotion quota needs **quantifiable data** on backwardness, inadequacy and efficiency (Art. 335).
- **State of Punjab v. Davinder Singh (1 Aug 2024)**: seven judges, **6:1**; States may **sub-classify SCs**; overruled **E.V. Chinnaiah (2004)** (AP Act, 2000).
- **Chebrolu Leela Prasad Rao (2020)**: **100% ST** teacher quota in AP Scheduled Areas **struck down**.

=== Cases on Amendments and the Tenth Schedule
- **Indira Gandhi v. Raj Narain (1975)**: **39th Amendment** clause (Art. 329A(4)) struck; free and fair elections basic; the **44th** later undid the 39th's changes.
- **Minerva Mills (1980)**: linked to the **42nd** Amendment; FRs and DPSPs complementary.
- **P. Sambamurthy (1987)**: **32nd Amendment** (AP, Art. 371D(5)) struck – not the 52nd.
- **Kihoto Hollohan (1992)**: upheld the **Tenth Schedule** (52nd Amendment); Speaker's decision reviewable.

=== Aadhaar Judgment (2018)
- Upheld **4:1**; authentication records kept at most **6 months**; **Sec. 57** (private entities) struck.
- Mandatory for benefits from the **Consolidated Fund** and **PAN/ITR**; **not** for bank accounts, SIM cards, school admissions. **Sec. 9**: Aadhaar is not proof of **citizenship, domicile or date of birth**.

=== Recent Judgments and Andhra Pradesh
- **Ayodhya mediation (March 2019)**: panel headed by Justice **F.M.I. Kalifulla**, with **Sri Sri Ravi Shankar** and **Sriram Panchu** as members; the Court decided the case in **Nov 2019**.
- **Article 370 (11 Dec 2023)**: five judges under CJI **D.Y. Chandrachud**, unanimous; Art. 370 a **temporary provision**; elections by **30 September 2024**.
- **Demonetisation (Jan 2023)**: upheld **4:1** under **Sec. 26(2) RBI Act**.
- CJIs: Puttaswamy **Khehar**; Shayara Bano, Navtej, Aadhaar **Dipak Misra**; Art. 370, Bonds **Chandrachud**.
- **Amaravati** sole capital of AP from **2 June 2024**; AP High Court ruled against the three-capitals plan in **2022**.
- **Andrabi v. NIA (May 2026)**: bail is the rule even under the UAPA.
