## SUB 0 | Federal Doctrines – Pith and Substance, Repugnancy and Others
[0:0] TABLE head: Doctrine | Meaning | Field
  [0:0.0] Pith and substance | Judge a law by its true nature; incidental trespass on the other List is fine – the doctrine used to decide encroachment of the Union List on the State List | Union–State Lists
  [0:0.1] Colourable legislation | What cannot be done directly cannot be done indirectly; "fraud on the Constitution" | Tests competence, not motive
  [0:0.2] Territorial nexus | State law may operate outside the State if there is a sufficient nexus | Extra-territorial operation
  [0:0.3] Harmonious construction | Read conflicting provisions so that both operate | Provision vs provision
  [0:0.4] Incidental and ancillary | An entry covers matters incidental to it; widest meaning | Entries
  [0:0.5] Repugnancy / federal paramountcy | Conflict on the same Concurrent subject – Union law prevails (Art. 254) | Concurrent List
  [0:0.6] Parens patriae | The State as legal protector of citizens unable to protect themselves (minors, persons of unsound mind, victims) | Welfare
[0:1] - Not to be confused: **eclipse** (pre-Constitution law vs FRs), **eminent domain** (State's power to acquire property), lex loci.
[0:2] - True character is read from the **whole Act, its object and effect**, not its title (A.S. Krishna, 1957).

## SUB 1 | Repugnancy – Article 254
[1:0] - **Concurrent List**: Parliament's law **prevails** over a repugnant State law (254(1)) – but not "without any exception".
[1:1] - **Art. 254(2)**: a State law on a **Concurrent List** matter, **reserved for and assented to by the President**, **prevails in that State** – so "if the State law is within the Concurrent List it will prevail, subject to the assent of the President" (APPSC G2 2011).
[1:2] - Parliament **may later override** such a law; the assent gives **no permanent immunity**.
[1:3] - **State List**: State law prevails in its own field; 254(2) applies **only to Concurrent List** matters.
[1:4] ~~ Sources: NextGen Polity - APPSC Exams p.309-310 (src/polity/nextgen-polity-full.txt); Constitution of India Art. 254

[3:0] TABLE head: FR doctrine | Meaning | Case
  [3:0.0] Eclipse | Pre-Constitution law conflicting with FRs is overshadowed, not void | Bhikaji Narain (1955); Ambica Mills (1974)
