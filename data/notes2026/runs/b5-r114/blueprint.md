## Trends
- **Recall of "vector / pathogen / organ" dominates.** Dengue–Aedes–platelets and filariasis–Wuchereria–lymph come up again and again across all commissions.
- **"NOT" questions** (not water-borne, not mosquito-borne, not infectious, no vaccine) make up about a fifth of the PYQs. Hepatitis B vs A/E and jaundice-as-symptom are the usual traps.
- **APPSC adds veterinary items** (Animal Husbandry and Forest papers: HI test, CMT, brucellosis tests, mastitis, fever types) and plain human-biology items, which were moved out.
- **Disputed or changed keys:** Chagas (no printed vector is right → option added), gnashing → yawning, dysentery (bacterial vs protozoan), baldness as "fungal", AIDS as "not infectious", the Hepatitis A pairing in the transmission match. One fact is outdated: P. vivax is no longer ~65% of India's cases.
- **AMR, One Health, climate and AP figures** have no PYQs. They are kept compact for the own MCQs.
