## SUB 0 | Round Table Conferences, Communal Award and Poona Pact
[0:0] TABLE head: RTC | Dates | Congress | Ambedkar
  [0:0.0] First | Nov 1930 – Jan 1931 | Did not take part (Gandhi, Sarojini Naidu, Malaviya absent; Jinnah attended) | Represented Depressed Classes (with R. Srinivasan); demanded separate electorates
  [0:0.1] Second | 7 Sept – 1 Dec 1931 | Gandhi alone – the only RTC Congress attended | Again demanded separate electorates; Gandhi rejected it
  [0:0.2] Third | 17 Nov – 24 Dec 1932 | Did not take part | Attended
[0:1] - **Ambedkar attended all three RTCs**; Gandhi only the second; the Congress missed the **First and Third**.
[0:2] - **Communal Award** (16 August 1932, **Ramsay MacDonald**) – a **unilateral British award** expanding separate electorates: Muslims, Europeans, Sikhs, Indian Christians, Anglo-Indians, **depressed classes** (declared a minority; separate electorate + double vote, for **20 years** – not 10), women, Marathas.
[0:3] - **Gandhi's fast** at **Yerwada Jail** from **20 September 1932** – he feared a permanent split in Hindu society.
[0:4] - **Poona Pact, 24 September 1932** ("Gandhi–Ambedkar Pact"): signed by **Ambedkar** (depressed classes) and **Madan Mohan Malaviya** (caste Hindus), also by Rajagopalachari and others – **Gandhi did not sign**.
[0:5]   - **Joint electorate** with reserved seats: **148** in provincial legislatures (vs 71), **18%** of the Central Legislature; fair share in **civil services** and **local bodies**. Purpose: **representation of the untouchables**.
[0:6] - **Aftermath:** Gandhi made **Harijan upliftment** part of his programme – All India Anti-Untouchability League (Sept 1932) → **Harijan Sevak Sangh**; weekly **Harijan** (1933); temple entry at **Siddantham** (Krishna district, AP).

  [0:7.1] Gandhi–Irwin ("Delhi Pact") | 5 March 1931 (not 1932) | Gandhi, Irwin
