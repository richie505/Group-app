## SUB 0 | P4 - Zero Poverty Programme
[4:0:0] # What P4 is
[4:0:1] - **P4 = Public-Private-People Partnership** (also written **People–Private–Public Partnership for the Poor**).
[4:0:2] - Launched on **Ugadi, 30 March 2025**; under the **Planning Department**; goal: **complete eradication of poverty** (**Zero Poverty** sutram); linked with **SDG 1 (No Poverty)**.
[4:0:3] - **Swarna Andhra P4 Foundation**: poverty eradication, under the **Planning Department**.
[4:0:4] - Claimed as a **first-of-its-kind convergence model** in India; government acts only as a **facilitator**; whole-of-society, **voluntary** model (2025-26).
[4:0:5] # Who is who in P4
[4:0:6] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [4:0:6.0] Margadarshi / Margadarsi | well-off mentor who adopts a family; selected voluntarily (no compulsion)
  [4:0:6.1] Bangaru Kutumbam / Bangaru Kutumbalu | "Golden Families": the poor families who are adopted
[4:0:7] - Mentor role goes beyond money: **emotional reassurance, guidance, continuous support**. Mentors can be individuals, NGOs or corporates.
[4:0:8] - Target: **5 lakh** Golden Families adopted **by 15 August**; Phase 1 scale about **21.8 lakh families**.
[4:0:9] - Donor platforms: **Milaap, DonateKart, RangDe**.
[4:0:10] - **Dokka Seethamma** (19th-century social reformer, known for selfless service to the poor) is remembered in P4 messaging.
[4:0:11] - Update (6 Jul 2026): about **9,000 Kuppam families** adopted by **Adani, TVS Motor and MEIL**.
[4:0:12] Margadarsi = mentor (adopts); Bangaru Kutumbam = poor family (adopted). Margadarsis are volunteers, not nominated by government.

## SUB 1 | Health - Universal Health Policy and Ntr Vaidya Seva
[4:1:0] # Universal Health Policy (Cabinet, September 2025)
[4:1:1] - Approved by the AP Cabinet chaired by CM N. Chandrababu Naidu.
[4:1:2] - Free treatment **up to ₹25 lakh per family per year**, **irrespective of economic status**: AP is the **first State** to offer such high-value universal cover.
[4:1:3] - Beneficiaries: **1.63 crore families** = **1.43 crore economically weaker + 20 lakh others**.
[4:1:4] - Integrates **Ayushman Bharat + NTR Vaidya Seva**; network of **2,493 hospitals**; **3,257 procedures**.
[4:1:5] - **Pre-authorisation within 6 hours**.
[4:1:6] TABLE head: Layer (hybrid model) | Cover | Paid by
  [4:1:6.0] Insurance | claims up to ₹2.5 lakh per family | insurance companies
  [4:1:6.1] Trust | claims ₹2.5 lakh – ₹25 lakh per family | NTR Vaidya Seva Trust
[4:1:7] - The **hybrid model** was announced in **January 2025**: insurance ₹2.5 lakh plus Trust-funded treatment up to **₹25 lakh per family** for the poor (**APPSC 2025 key: ₹25 lakh**).
[4:1:8] - **APSES 2024–25** (earlier pattern): up to **₹5 lakh per family per year**; total up to ₹25 lakh; **no limit for cancer**; annual family income up to **₹5 lakh**; runs alongside **Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY (₹5 lakh per family)**.
[4:1:9] Insurance handles the first ₹2.5 lakh; the Trust handles ₹2.5–25 lakh, not the reverse.
[4:1:10] ₹2.5 lakh = insurance layer; ₹25 lakh = per family (Trust); ₹5 lakh = per family a year (PM-JAY / APSES).
