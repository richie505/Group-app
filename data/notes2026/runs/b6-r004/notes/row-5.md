=== Employees, Acts and State Institutions (2017-2022 Decisions)
# Government employees
- **Retirement age:** raised **60 → 62** for State government employees – announced **7 January 2022** with the 11th PRC (**23% fitment**); ordinance of 31 Jan 2022, effective **1 January 2022** (earlier 58 → 60 in 2014).
- **APSRTC merger:** the **AP State Road Transport Corporation (Absorption of Employees into Government Service) Act, 2019** (Act 36 of 2019; Bill passed 16 Dec 2019) absorbed about **51,500 RTC staff** into a new **Public Transport Department** from **1 January 2020**, on par with government employees.
# State emblem (order of 14 November 2018)
| Version | Authorised users |
|---|---|
| Multi-coloured | CM, Ministers, Chief Secretary, **Secretaries to Government**, **Advocate General**, all **Heads of Departments**, District Collectors and Magistrates |
| Blue | **District HoDs** and mid-level Secretariat officers |
- **Not authorised:** **heads of autonomous bodies** (APPSC Divisional Accounts Officer key).
# Endowments Act (Act 30 of 1987)
- **Executive Officer, Sec. 29(3):** custody of **records, accounts, jewels**, valuables and funds; **collection of income** and incurring expenditure; sues and is sued; deposits money in bank/treasury; emergency works for the **safety of pilgrims**.
- **Not a duty:** '**publication of institutions**' (APPSC Endowment Prelims 2022).
# Institutions and policies
- **APHRDI** (AP Human Resource Development Institute), the State's apex training institute for officials: at **Bapatla** (the Dr MCR HRD Institute stayed in Hyderabad).
- **Sand policy (September 2019):** **APMDC** mines sand and sells it at government **stockyards** (about ₹370 a tonne) from 5 Sept 2019, ending free sand.
- **Backward districts:** under the **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 (Sec. 46, 94)** the Centre gives a special development package to the backward districts of **Rayalaseema and north coastal AP**; the Centre's decision to continue it concerns **Andhra Pradesh**.
> Retirement age 62 (not 65 or 60); APSRTC Act = 'Absorption of Employees into Government Service'; APHRDI = Bapatla.

=== NTR Bharosa Social Security Pensions
# Category → monthly pension
- **NTR Bharosa** (renamed from **YSR Pension Kanuka**) is run through **SERP**.
| Category | Monthly pension |
|---|---|
| Old age (60+), widows (18+), weavers, toddy tappers, fishermen, single women, transgender persons, artists, others | ₹4,000 (raised from ₹3,000, April 2024) |
| Disabled (40%+ disability), leprosy | ₹6,000 |
