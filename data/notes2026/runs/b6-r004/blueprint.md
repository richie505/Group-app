## 3. Trends and repeats
- **Repeats:** Prana Raksha (same question twice); retirement age 62 (two papers, 2022); Kutumba (15) / Samaja (10) Vikasam as a pair in one paper.
- **2025 papers** test the newest flagship of each sector: P4, Stree Shakti, Universal Health Policy / NTR Vaidya Seva hybrid, SC sub-classification, NTR district CCTV, SHRESTH, Akshara Andhra, Gates MoU.
- **Older papers** test "who holds the post now" – these go out of date quickly, so the notes carry an "earlier holder → now" table and the keys carry ⚠ Outdated lines.
- **Comparison traps:** other States' schemes put in AP's place (Delhi's Saheli card; Gujarat's Conquer Exam app).

## 4. Heavy in the old notes but never asked
- District/revenue-division reorganisation (Dec 2025 – Jan 2026), MEPMA initiatives, BPS 2025 / zoning, Madvi Hidma, AQLI, APPSC reform proposals, 2026 Bills and universities: 0 PYQs, but 170+ own MCQs; kept, placed after the PYQ-weighted sub-sections.
- The old "APPSC Previous Year Questions" tables (rows 4 and 5) were dropped as meta; old "Traps" lists were folded into the last sub-section of each row.
