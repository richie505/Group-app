## SUB 1 | Battle of Ten Kings and the Tribes
[1:0] # Dasarajna (Battle of Ten Kings)
[1:1] - **Where:** banks of the **Parushni (Ravi)** (UPPCS 2008) – not the Vitasta, Vipasa or Asikni.
[1:2] - **Victor:** **Sudas**, Bharata king of the **Tritsu** family, with the priest **Vasishtha**.
[1:3] - **Opponents:** a confederacy of ten tribes – five Aryan (**Puru, Yadu, Turvasa, Anu, Druhyu**) and five non-Aryan – whose priest was **Vishvamitra**; rivalry of Vasishtha and Vishvamitra led to the war.
[1:4] - **Source:** **Mandala VII** of the Rigveda.
[1:5] - **After:** the defeated **Purus** joined the Bharatas → **Kurus**; the Kurus and Panchalas ruled the upper Ganga basin.
[1:6] - **NDA 2012 "not true":** "Purus sided with the Bharatas" ✗ – they were in the confederacy. The Rig Veda does mention Dasarajna and Sudas; it has about 300 non-Indo-European words.
[1:7] # Tribes
[1:8] - **Panchajana:** **Anu, Druhyu, Yadu, Turvasa, Puru** – **Kikata** is not one (UPPCS 2009, twice); the Kikatas lived in **south Bihar (Magadha)**.
[1:9] - **Other tribes:** Bharatas, Tritsus, Krivis, Srinjayas; **Bharatavarsha** is named after the **Bharatas**.
[1:10] - **Divodasa** (Bharata) defeated **Sambara**.
[1:11] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History APPSC Part 1 p.26-27; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 1 p.124-125; app notes 1:15 (sub 4, 5)
## SUB 2 | Sapta Sindhu, Chronology and Geography
[2:0] - **Period:** Early Vedic c. **1500–1000 BCE**; oldest core = **family books, Mandalas II–VII**.
[2:1] - **Sapta Sindhu:** land of seven rivers – Indus, its five Punjab tributaries and the Sarasvati; spread over **eastern Afghanistan, NWFP (Khyber Pakhtunkhwa), Punjab and Haryana**; the Later Vedic core shifted east to the Ganga–Yamuna Doab.
[2:2] - **Ganga is not one of the Sapta Sindhu**; Vipas is.
[2:3] - **Gandhara Grave culture (Swat):** horse remains – linked to the early Indo-Aryans.
[2:4] - **Terms:** **Himavant** = Himalayas; **Mujavant** = soma mountain in the **Hindu Kush**; **Dhanva** = desert land; **samudra** = a collection of water (the **Satapatha Brahmana** names the eastern and western seas).
[2:5] - **Later regions:** **Brahmarshi Desh** = Ganga–Yamuna Doab and adjoining area; **Madhya Desh** = between the Himalayas and the **Vindhyas**.
[2:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History APPSC Part 1 p.18, p.20, p.22, p.29; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 1 p.123; app notes 1:15 (sub 0, 1, 3)
