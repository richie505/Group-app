## 5. Settled conflicts
- **"The Saga of Hyderabad Struggle…" (APPSC G1 2002):** old notes and one printed key gave Swami Ramananda Tirtha; the release report (Gulf News) names **V.H. Desai and C. Sudarshan** as authors, published by the Ramananda Tirtha Memorial Trust → both PYQs keyed to Desai and Sudarshan.
- **Hyderabad State Congress founding day:** sources give 29 Jan, 18 June and 29 July 1938; 29 January 1938 (launch announced) keyed with a dispute line.
- **Aravamudu Iyengar committee:** NextGen and the stems say 1932; the committee was appointed in 1937 and reported in 1938 (Wikipedia; GIPE report record). The answer (political reforms) does not change.
- **Year Hyderabad joined India:** keys say 1948; Spectrum says "acceded November 1949" (the Nizam's firman of 24 Nov 1949) → 1948 keyed with a dispute line.
- **M.K. Vellodi:** CM from 1 December 1949, not January 1950 (old explanation and old notes).
- **Interim Government of 2 Sept 1946:** both Azad (Jan 1947) and Liaquat (Oct 1946) were absent; key Azad with a dispute line.
- **Parallel movement in the states:** the State People's Movement and the Praja Mandal movement are the same thing → "More than one of the above".
