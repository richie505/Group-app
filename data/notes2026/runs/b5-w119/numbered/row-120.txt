## SUB 0 | National Health Mission – ASHA and Mother-Child Schemes
[0:0] - **NRHM launched 2005** (accessible, affordable, quality care for rural India); **NUHM 2013**; **NHM** (NRHM + NUHM) approved 1 May 2013.
[0:1] - **ASHA = Accredited Social Health Activist** – village-level community health worker who, with the ANM, links households to the health system (not "Association" or "Assistant"). AP has 42,060 ASHAs (2026).
[0:2] - Funding: Centre:State **60:40**; North-Eastern and Himalayan States **90:10**.
[0:3] TABLE head: Component | What to remember
  [0:3.0] JSY (Janani Suraksha Yojana) | Cash for institutional delivery; 100% centrally sponsored
  [0:3.1] JSSK (Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram) | Free ("zero expense") delivery incl. C-section, drugs, diagnostics, blood, diet and transport for pregnant women and sick newborns at public facilities
  [0:3.2] RKSK | Adolescents 10–19 years
  [0:3.3] Anaemia Mukt Bharat (2018) | 6x6x6 strategy; iron deficiency anaemia is India's major nutritional public health problem
  [0:3.4] Kilkari / Mobile Academy | 72 free weekly audio messages to pregnant women and mothers (2nd trimester till child is one); Mobile Academy = audio course for ASHAs
[0:4] - **"Public" in public health** = community-wide, municipal, unrestricted (not private or exclusive).
[0:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy APPSC 18 p.4 (NHM 2013); NextGen Environment APPSC Part 8.2 p.10 (ASHAs); Rocket Biology p.35 (iron → anaemia); app notes
## SUB 1 | Immunisation – UIP, Mission Indradhanush and eVIN
[1:0] - **Mission Indradhanush (Dec 2014)**: catch-up **immunisation of children** (and pregnant women) under UIP; reaches **zero-dose and partly immunised** children; target **90% full immunisation**.
[1:1] - **eVIN** (Electronic Vaccine Intelligence Network, MoHFW + UNDP): indigenous system that **digitises vaccine stocks and monitors cold-chain temperature through a smartphone app**. **U-WIN**: digital registry of routine immunisation.
[1:2] TABLE head: Age (UIP) | Vaccines
  [1:2.0] Birth | BCG, OPV-0, Hepatitis B birth dose
  [1:2.1] 6, 10, 14 weeks | OPV 1–3, Pentavalent 1–3, Rotavirus, fIPV; PCV (6 and 14 weeks)
  [1:2.2] 9–12 months | MR-1, JE-1, PCV booster, Vitamin A
  [1:2.3] 16–24 months | MR-2, DPT booster-1, OPV booster, JE-2
  [1:2.4] 5–6 years | DPT booster-2
  [1:2.5] 10 and 16 years | Td
[1:3] - Childhood vaccines vs disease: **BCG – TB; OPV – polio; DPT – diphtheria, pertussis, tetanus; MMR – measles, mumps, rubella**; hepatitis B; TT booster. There is **no "cancer vaccine"** in the child schedule.
[1:4] > Wrong pairs in PYQs: Td at 5–6 years; OPV-1 at 16–24 months; DPT booster-2 at 6 weeks.
[1:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen Rocket Biology p.32 (BCG, OPV, DPT, toxoid); PMF Science & Technology Part 3 p.46-47 (UIP, Mission Indradhanush); app notes
## SUB 2 | Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission and Digital Health
[2:0] - **ABDM**: launched **September 2021**; implemented by the **National Health Authority (NHA)**; consent-based, interoperable digital health ecosystem.
[2:1] TABLE head: Type | Components
  [2:1.0] Registries | ABHA (Health ID, 14 digits), Healthcare Professionals Registry, Health Facility Registry, Drug Registry
  [2:1.1] Gateways | Health Information Consent Manager, UHI, National Health Claim Exchange (NHCX)
  [2:1.2] Testing | Sandbox
[2:2] - **Arogya Mitra is NOT an ABDM component** – it is the PM-JAY hospital facilitator (the app's notes record an APPSC 2025 key of "Sand Box", which is wrong: Sandbox is ABDM's testing environment).
[2:3] - **Digital Health Incentive Scheme** (NHA, Dec 2022): in effect from **1 January 2023**.
[2:4] - **First ABDM Hackathon** (Round 1 – Unified Health Interface): **Pune**, 14–17 July 2022.
[2:5] - **Andhra Pradesh = first State to link the electronic health records of one crore people** to the ABDM database.
[2:6] - ABHA accounts: **over 90 crore** (30 May 2026); nearly half held by women. NMC (Mar 2026): medical colleges to link ABHA IDs for all OPD, IPD and emergency patients.
[2:7] - Benefit of digital records: **better sharing and use of patient information** (not less privacy or more paperwork). eSanjeevani = telemedicine.
[2:8] TABLE head: Public health technology – chronology | Year
  [2:8.0] Vaccines (Jenner, smallpox) | 1796
  [2:8.1] X-ray (Röntgen) | 1895
  [2:8.2] Electron microscope | 1931
  [2:8.3] Electronic health records | 1960s onward
[2:9] ~~ Sources: Rocket CA 2026 Part 02 p.171, p.180 (ABHA 90 crore); NextGen Rocket Biology p.3 (Jenner 1796); digitalhealthnews.com / indianpharmapost.com on the Pune hackathon (search results, July 2022); app notes; general knowledge, not checked (X-ray, electron microscope, EHR, DHIS date)
## SUB 3 | Ayushman Bharat – Pillars, PM-JAY, NHA and PM-ABHIM
[3:0] - **Ayushman Bharat** = the **health** sector flagship (2018) with four pillars:
[3:1] TABLE head: Pillar | Role
  [3:1.0] Ayushman Arogya Mandir (earlier AB-HWCs) | Comprehensive primary care; target 1,50,000; meets 80–90% of health needs
  [3:1.1] PM-JAY | Hospitalisation cover ₹5 lakh per family per year
  [3:1.2] ABDM | Digital backbone
  [3:1.3] PM-ABHIM | Health infrastructure – launched by PM Narendra Modi at Varanasi, 25 Oct 2021; ₹64,180 crore (2021-22 to 2025-26)
[3:2] - **National Health Authority** (runs PM-JAY and ABDM): Governing Board **chaired by the Union Health Minister**.
[3:3] - AAM services: NCD screening (hypertension, diabetes, oral/breast/cervical cancer), mental health, ENT, eye, oral, geriatric, palliative and trauma care.
[3:4] - PM-ABHIM: critical care blocks, Integrated Public Health Labs, BSL-3 labs; in AP 10 Critical Care Blocks and 12 IPHLs inaugurated from **Vijayawada** (Sep 2026).
[3:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen Environment APPSC Part 8.2 p.10-11 (PM-ABHIM, AAM, PM-JAY); app notes

## SUB 5 | ICMR, AYUSH and Health Research Bodies
[5:0] - **ICMR**: began **1911** as IRFA, renamed **1949**. **Strategic Plan / Vision 2030** (Oct 2017): 5 pillars – capacity building, data management, **leveraging traditional medicine**, evidence to policy, research for programme implementation; **aligned with NHP 2017** – so all three statements of the APPSC 2023 PYQ are correct.
[5:1] - ICMR initiatives: MERA India, India TB Research Consortium, India Hypertension Control Initiative (not "India Food Security Consortium").
[5:2] - **ICMR-NARFBR, Hyderabad** (National Animal Resource Facility for Biomedical Research): inaugurated by **Health Minister Mansukh Mandaviya, 17 Dec 2022**.
[5:3] - **Ministry of AYUSH**: formed **9 Nov 2014** (earlier Department of Indian Systems of Medicine and **Homoeopathy**, 1995 – renamed AYUSH 2003); a separate ministry, not under Home Affairs; **Sowa-Rigpa** (Tibetan medicine) added – AYUSH → AYUSH(S).
[5:4] ~~ Sources: app notes (ICMR); general knowledge, not checked (AYUSH history, NARFBR)
