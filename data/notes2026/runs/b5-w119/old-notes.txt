## SUB 2 | PM-JAY and Health Insurance
[119:2:0] - **PM-JAY** (launched **23 Sep 2018**): **health insurance/assurance for economically vulnerable families** – **₹5 lakh per family per year** for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation; **world's largest publicly funded health assurance scheme**.
[119:2:1] - Coverage: about **12 crore families (55 crore people)** identified from **SECC 2011**; 26,901 hospitals empanelled (2023).
[119:2:2] - **Oct 2024**: all citizens **aged 70+, irrespective of income** (Ayushman Vay Vandana card); 70+ members of covered families get an extra ₹5 lakh top-up.
[119:2:3] - Funding: Centre:State **60:40**; **90:10** for North-Eastern and Himalayan States; up to 100% for UTs without legislature. West Bengal became the 36th State/UT to implement it (8 Jun 2026).
[119:2:4] - Other schemes: **CGHS 1954, Delhi**; **ESI Act 1948**, scheme inaugurated at **Kanpur, 24 Feb 1952**.
[119:2:5] - Trends: government-financed insurance up 2.5-fold (2017-18 to 2025); NFHS-6 households with an insured member 41.0% → 60.2%. Criticism: insurance-led models raise coverage without raising care.
[119:2:6] - **Abroad: USA – "ObamaCare"** = Patient Protection and **Affordable Care Act 2010**, the landmark law for **affordable health care**.
[119:2:7] ~~ Sources: NextGen Environment APPSC Part 8.2 p.11 (PM-JAY); Rocket CA 2026 Part 02 p.65 (West Bengal); app notes; general knowledge, not checked (ObamaCare)
## SUB 3 | Andhra Pradesh Health Assurance
[119:3:0] - **Dr. NTR Vaidya Seva** (formerly YSR Aarogyasri): cashless care for BPL families and families with income up to ₹5 lakh; **3,257 procedures**; cover up to **₹25 lakh per family per year**, no limit for cancer; 1.43 crore families benefited; in convergence with PM-JAY.
[119:3:1] - **Employees Health Scheme (EHS)**: State Government **employees, pensioners and dependants**; 50:50 premium; 2,510 procedures; **₹2 lakh per episode of illness, no limit on the number of episodes**.
[119:3:2] - Working Journalists Health Scheme: premium ₹2,500 a year, shared 50:50.
[119:3:3] - AP Universal Health Policy (Sep 2025): up to ₹25 lakh per family; EHS employees excluded. AP Budget 2024-25: ₹4,000 crore to NTR Vaidya Seva Trust; State scheme + EHS + RSBY + ESIS + CGHS = 70% of households (2026).
[119:3:4] ~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.225-226; app notes

=== NOTES 5:120 | Recent Public Health Initiatives – Ayushman Bharat, National Health Mission, Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission
## SUB 0 | National Health Mission – ASHA and Mother-Child Schemes
[120:0:0] - **NRHM launched 2005** (accessible, affordable, quality care for rural India); **NUHM 2013**; **NHM** (NRHM + NUHM) approved 1 May 2013.
[120:0:1] - **ASHA = Accredited Social Health Activist** – village-level community health worker who, with the ANM, links households to the health system (not "Association" or "Assistant"). AP has 42,060 ASHAs (2026).
[120:0:2] - Funding: Centre:State **60:40**; North-Eastern and Himalayan States **90:10**.
[120:0:3] TABLE head: Component | What to remember
  [120:0:3.0] JSY (Janani Suraksha Yojana) | Cash for institutional delivery; 100% centrally sponsored
  [120:0:3.1] JSSK (Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram) | Free ("zero expense") delivery incl. C-section, drugs, diagnostics, blood, diet and transport for pregnant women and sick newborns at public facilities
  [120:0:3.2] RKSK | Adolescents 10–19 years
  [120:0:3.3] Anaemia Mukt Bharat (2018) | 6x6x6 strategy; iron deficiency anaemia is India's major nutritional public health problem
  [120:0:3.4] Kilkari / Mobile Academy | 72 free weekly audio messages to pregnant women and mothers (2nd trimester till child is one); Mobile Academy = audio course for ASHAs
[120:0:4] - **"Public" in public health** = community-wide, municipal, unrestricted (not private or exclusive).

## SUB 0 | HIV/AIDS – Facts, Tests, Drugs and Programmes
[121:0:0] - **HIV causes AIDS**; first recognised **1981**; dominant strain in India **HIV-1 subtype C**.
[121:0:1] - **Transmission**: sexual contact, **blood transfusion**/needles, **mother to child across the placenta** (and breastfeeding) – **not by mosquitoes or other blood-sucking insects**.
[121:0:2] - **Symptoms of AIDS**: swollen lymph nodes, night sweats, weight loss, and neurological problems incl. **memory loss** – all four of the classic PYQ list.
[121:0:3] - **ELISA** = first widely used HIV screening test (detects antibodies); **Zidovudine (Azidothymidine, AZT)** = the most used anti-retroviral. No licensed HIV vaccine.

  [121:0:4.3] Project Sunrise (2016) | HIV/AIDS among people who inject drugs in the North-Eastern States
  [121:0:4.4] HIV/AIDS Act 2017 | Bars discrimination against people living with HIV
  [121:0:4.5] Red Ribbon Express / AVAHAN | Awareness train / Gates Foundation prevention initiative
