## SUB 0 | Prisons, Custody and Parole – Law and Administration
[127:0:0] TABLE head: Law / rule | Provision
  [127:0:0.0] Seventh Schedule | 'Prisons / persons detained therein' = State List, Entry 4 – States manage prisons with their own rules
  [127:0:0.1] Prisons Act 1894 | Colonial law that kept prisons with the Provincial (now State) Governments (power to make rules); with State jail manuals; BPR&D advisory
  [127:0:0.2] Model Prisons Act 2023 | MHA guide for States to replace the 1894 Act; not binding
  [127:0:0.3] BNSS s.479 | Undertrial freed after half the maximum term (one-third for first-timers)
[127:0:1] - **UPSC 2023:** Statement I (States manage prisons) and Statement II (1894 Act kept prisons with Provincial Governments) are **both correct, and II explains I** (widely given key).
[127:0:2] - **Judicial custody:** the accused is in the **magistrate's custody** and lodged in **jail** (not the police station); police **cannot interrogate without the court's approval**. Police custody = lock-up at the police station.
[127:0:3] - **Parole/furlough:** **not an absolute right** – even a sufficient case does not make parole a matter of right; **each State has its own Prisoners Release on Parole Rules** (UPSC 2021).
[127:0:4] - **FIR on websites:** **Youth Bar Association of India v UoI (2016)** – copies of FIRs (except sensitive cases) to be **uploaded to the police/State website within 24 hours** of registration (48 hours in remote areas).
[127:0:5] - **Aug 2025:** SC ordered release of prisoners held beyond their sentence.

## SUB 0 | Sexual Harassment at Workplace – Vishaka and POSH Act 2013
[129:0:0] - **Vishaka v. State of Rajasthan (1997):** SC guidelines against sexual harassment of **working women at the workplace** (Arts 14, 19, 21) → **POSH Act, 2013** evolved from the **Vishaka guidelines** (asked ×4).
[129:0:1] - Full name: Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (**Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal**) Act, 2013; **in force 9 December 2013**.
[129:0:2] TABLE head: Committee | Where | Composition
  [129:0:2.0] Internal Committee (IC) | Every workplace with 10 or more employees | Senior woman employee as Presiding Officer; 2+ employees; 1 NGO member; half women
  [129:0:2.1] Local Committee (LC) | District; constituted by the District Officer | Workplaces with fewer than 10, or complaint against the employer
[129:0:3] - **Time limits:**
[129:0:4] TABLE head: Step | Time limit
  [129:0:4.0] Complaint | Within 3 months (extendable by 3)
  [129:0:4.1] Inquiry | Within 90 days
  [129:0:4.2] Employer action | Within 60 days
[129:0:5] - Harassment includes **showing pornography**, unwelcome contact, demands for sexual favours.
[129:0:6] - **SHe-Box:** online complaint system for workplace sexual harassment, **single window for every woman** whatever her work status – organised or unorganised, private or public; **SHe-Box 2.0** (Mission Shakti) also tracks ICs/LCs.

## SUB 1 | Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961
[129:1:0] - **Enacted 1961; in force 1 July 1961** (not 1955, 1976, 20 May or 1 June). The **1st joint sitting** of Parliament was on the Dowry Prohibition Bill.
[129:1:1] - **Section 2 – Dowry:** **any property or valuable security** given or agreed to be given, **directly or indirectly**, before, at or any time after marriage, in connection with the marriage.
[129:1:2] - **Not dowry:** **dower/mahr** (Muslim personal law); **presents given without demand and entered in a list** under the rules (s.3(2)).
[129:1:3] TABLE head: Section | Provision
  [129:1:3.0] s.3 | Giving/taking/abetting: not less than 5 years + fine ≥ ₹15,000 or dowry value, whichever is more
  [129:1:3.1] s.4 | Demanding dowry: 6 months to 2 years + fine up to ₹10,000
  [129:1:3.2] s.8A | Burden of proof on the accused
  [129:1:3.3] s.8B | Dowry Prohibition Officers appointed by the State Government
[129:1:4] - Offences are **cognisable, non-bailable and non-compoundable**.
[129:1:5] - **1986 amendment** brought **dowry death** (IPC 304B, now BNS 80) and its presumption (Evidence Act 113B, now BSA 118); also amended 1984.
[129:1:6] - **⚠ Trap:** 5 years is the **minimum**; any payment **on demand** (to the groom's father, sister …) is dowry.
[129:1:7] - Weak enforcement: **poor enforcement, faulty FIRs**, deaths shown as accidents/suicides; about **20 dowry deaths a day**; **UP, Bihar, MP, Rajasthan** lead.
