## SUB 0 | Andhra Pradesh – Disha and Women-Safety Initiatives
[0:0] # AP Disha Bill (December 2019)
[0:1] - **Passed:** the AP Legislative Assembly passed the **AP Disha Bill** (AP Criminal Law (Amendment) Act, 2019) on **13 December 2019** – response to the **2019 Hyderabad veterinarian** rape-murder.
[0:2] - **Death** for rape and gang rape; verdict in **21 days** = **7 days investigation + 14 days trial** (vs CLA 2018: 2-month probe).
[0:3] - **Special court in every district** (not only the capital); forensic evidence; cyber-stalking penalties.
[0:4] - Needs **President's assent** (criminal law is in the **Concurrent List**); **never got assent – not in force**.
[0:5] # Initiatives → Purpose
[0:6] TABLE head: Initiative | Purpose
  [0:6.0] Disha App | SOS app; alerts police and shares location (APPSC G1 2022)
  [0:6.1] Disha police stations | 18 women's police stations
  [0:6.2] Abhayam | Chittoor police awareness campaign for women and children (Dec 2019, SP S. Senthil Kumar)
  [0:6.3] Abhay | Prakasam (Ongole) pilot, 4 Dec 2019: free pick-up and drop for stranded women, 9 pm-5 am
  [0:6.4] Shakti Teams | AP Police teams for prevention of crimes against women and children (164 teams); patrolling, awareness
  [0:6.5] Mahila Samrakshana Karyadarsi | Women-protection functionaries in village/ward secretariats
  [0:6.6] Trishul committees | State/district: zero drugs, zero tolerance to atrocities against women, zero road accidents (Sep 2026)
[0:7] > **Abhayam** = Chittoor campaign; **Abhay** = Ongole pick-up. **SOS** = Disha SOS app and also **Sexual Offender Sheets**.
[0:8] # AP data
[0:9] - Cases: **25,503** (2022) → **22,418** (2023, **11th** in India) → **19,952** (2024); further **4.4%** fall credited to **Shakti Teams**, Women's Safety Week rallies, **Sexual Offender Sheets**.
[0:10] - **Conviction rate** (crimes against women, 2021): AP only **5.6%** (pendency 86.7%) vs India 26.5%.
[0:11] - **District-wise** (2022): **Visakhapatnam** most rape cases (69), then NTR district (51).

## SUB 1 | Nirbhaya Case, Verma Committee and CLA 2013
[1:0] # Nirbhaya case (Delhi, December 2012) → outcomes
[1:1] - 1. **Justice J.S. Verma Committee** (report **January 2013**)
[1:2] - 2. **Criminal Law (Amendment) Act 2013** = popularly the **"Nirbhaya Act"** (amended IPC, CrPC, Evidence Act)
[1:3] - 3. **Nirbhaya Fund** (2013) – non-lapsable corpus for **safety and security of women**
[1:4] - 4. **JJ Act 2015** – 16-18-year-olds may be tried as adults for **heinous crimes**
[1:5] - Not an outcome: the BNS. **Nirbhay Raho**: Panchayati Raj + MWCD + **NLSIU** under the Nirbhaya Fund.
[1:6] # Dates of the Nirbhaya Act

[1:8] # Verma Committee

[1:10] - Recommended: **criminalise marital rape**, **no death penalty for rape**, police reforms, **age of consent kept at 16** → Parliament chose **18**.

## SUB 3 | CLA 2018, BNS 2023 and Victim Identity
[3:0] # CLA 2018
[3:1] - Context: **Kathua and Unnao** cases (2018); led to the FTSC scheme.
[3:2] - Minimum for rape: **7 → 10 years**.
[3:3] TABLE head: Victim | Punishment
  [3:3.0] Girl under 16 | Minimum 20 years
  [3:3.1] Girl under 12 | 20 years to life or death
  [3:3.2] Gang rape, girl under 12 | Life or death
[3:4] - Investigation **2 months** + trial 2 months + appeals 6 months; **no anticipatory bail** for rape of a girl under 16.
[3:5] # BNS 2023 (in force 1 July 2024)
[3:6] - **Gang-rape age raised 16 → 18**; **s.69**: intercourse by **deceitful means / false promise of marriage or job**; **s.103**: honour killing prosecuted as **murder**.
[3:7] - **Cheating** and dishonestly inducing delivery of property: **IPC 420** → now **BNS 318(4)**.
[3:8] # Identity of rape victims
[3:9] - **IPC 228A (now BNS 72):** victim's name cannot be published except by or under the written order of the **officer in charge of the police station / investigating police officer**, or with the victim's (next of kin's) written authorisation to a recognised welfare organisation.
