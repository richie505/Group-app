[91:0:0] - AP (through **APIIC**) develops three corridors under the **National Industrial Corridor Development Programme**.

## SUB 1 | Vcic – Features and Adb Funding
[91:1:0] - **India's first coastal corridor**; first phase of the **East Coast Economic Corridor** (Kolkata-Kanyakumari).
[91:1:1] - Links India to **ASEAN and East Asia**: **Act East + Make in India**; aligned with Golden Quadrilateral (NH-16).
[91:1:2] - **North:** metallurgy, pharma, petrochemicals, IT. **South:** food processing, leather, automobiles.
[91:1:3] TABLE head: ADB programme (US$ mn) | Policy-based loan | Project loan | Total
  [91:1:3.0] ADB | 125 (100%) | 500 (70%) | 625
  [91:1:3.1] Govt of AP | 0 | 215 (30%) | 215
  [91:1:3.2] Total | 125 | 715 (70:30) | 840
[91:1:4] - **Policy-based loan:** reforms, institutions, skills; **project loan:** roads, power, water, parks.
[91:1:5] - **Executing agency:** Department of Industries and Commerce; **PMU** under Commissioner of Industries.
[91:1:6] PYQ G1 2022: VCIC is **partly funded by an ADB loan**, not JICA.

## SUB 6 | Apiic, SEZs, Pcpir and Ports
[91:6:0] - **APIIC (1973):** nodal agency for industrial land, parks and SEZs; **PPP** model; after **1991** moved from direct manufacturer to **facilitator**.
[91:6:1] - Managed ~571 parks earlier; Survey 2025-26: **635 clusters** (331 parks, 304 estates).
[91:6:2] - **SEZs (2023):** 47 or more notified; **10 by APIIC**.
[91:6:3] - **APSEZ:** multi-product SEZ at **Atchutapuram-Rambilli** (Anakapalli); notified **12 Apr 2007**; ~3,213 acres; part of PCPIR.
[91:6:4] - **Budget 2026:** SEZ units can sell in India at concessional tax.
[91:6:5] - **Four PCPIRs:** **Visakhapatnam-Kakinada**, Dahej, Paradip, Cuddalore-Nagapattinam. AP PCPIR approved 2009, ~640 sq km; includes APSEZ and Kakinada SEZ (petrochemicals).
[91:6:6] - **PCPIR Policy 2020-35:** minimum area **250 → 50 sq km**; up to **20% VGF**; target ₹10 lakh crore (₹20 lakh crore by 2035).
[91:6:7] - PPP ports **Krishnapatnam + Gangavaram** drew heavy industry coastward.
