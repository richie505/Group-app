## SUB 0 | Founding of the INC, 1885 – Who, When, Where
[0:0] # The core facts
[0:1] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [0:1.0] Year | 1885 (not 1883, 1886 or 1888)
  [0:1.1] Founder | A.O. Hume, a retired ICS officer (civil servant) – "Father/founder of the INC"
  [0:1.2] First President | W.C. (Womesh Chandra) Bonnerjee – not Hume, not S.N. Banerjea
  [0:1.3] General Secretary | A.O. Hume (1885-1906)
  [0:1.4] Dates | 28-31 December 1885
  [0:1.5] Venue | Gokuldas Tejpal Sanskrit College, Bombay
  [0:1.6] Delegates | 72 (only 2 Muslims); mainly lawyers, journalists and teachers
  [0:1.7] Viceroy | Lord Dufferin (1884-88) – not Ripon (left 1884), Lytton or Mayo
[0:2] - **Earlier name:** Hume's **Indian National Union** (1884) became the INC; the name **"Indian National Congress"** was suggested by **Dadabhai Naoroji** (not W.C. Bonnerjee).
[0:3] - **Prelude:** after the **Theosophical Convention at Madras (Dec 1884)** a private meeting of **17** people planned an all-India body.
[0:4] - **Venue shift:** planned for **Poona**, moved to Bombay at the last minute because of **cholera** in Poona (not plague).
[0:5] - **Absent:** **Surendranath Banerjea** and other Bengal leaders – busy with the **2nd Indian National Conference** at Calcutta (Dec 1885).
[0:6] - **Government officials** (e.g. some Indian officials) attended the first session as observers – NDA statement "some officials were present" ✓.
[0:7] - **Merger:** the **Indian National Conference** of the **Indian Association** (S.N. Banerjea, A.M. Bose; conferences 1883, 1885) **merged with the INC in 1886**.
[0:8] - **Dufferin's attitude:** Hume convinced him **not to obstruct** the Congress; he may have suggested an annual forum but did not found it; soon hostile – "**microscopic minority**" (1888), "factory of sedition".
[0:9] > Statement angle (NDA 2006): Dufferin's tenure ✓, Bonnerjee first president ✓, first session in Calcutta ✗ (Bombay), officials present ✓ → "I, II and IV".

## SUB 5 | A.O. Hume and the Safety-valve Debate
[5:0] # A.O. Hume
[5:1] - **Retired ICS officer** (retired **1882**); demoted in **1879** after a clash with **Lytton**; settled at **Shimla**.
[5:2] - Drew the idea of local self-government from **Ripon**; closer to the **Bombay-Poona** leaders than to Calcutta.
[5:3] - Founded the **Indian National Union**; **first General Secretary** of the INC.
[5:4] - Title: **"Father of Indian Ornithology"**.

## SUB 6 | Aims, Demands and Early Character
[6:0] - **Aims stated by W.C. Bonnerjee (1885):** (a) personal intimacy and **friendship among workers** in the national cause; (b) removing **race, creed and provincial prejudices** – **national unity**; (c) recording the opinions of the educated classes on **pressing (social) questions**; (d) laying down the **lines of political work** for the next 12 months.
[6:1] - **Exam answer:** the Congress kept **social issues off its platform** (Naoroji's rule at the 1886 session: only national questions) – so "to express views on social issues" is the option books treat as NOT an objective.
[6:2] - **First-session demands:** **simultaneous ICS examinations** in England and India; **elected** (not nominated) councils; **opposition to the annexation of Upper Burma**; **cut in military expenditure** – NOT complete provincial autonomy.
[6:3] - **Early demand:** **separation of the Executive and the Judiciary**.
[6:4] - **Character:** the **first all-India political platform**; urban, English-educated middle class – not a mass peasant body.
