## SUB 0 | Deficits, Debt and the FRBM Act
[0:0] # FRBM Act – PYQ
[0:1] - **Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management Act, 2003** (Union): enacted **26 August 2003**, in force **5 July 2004**; caps fiscal deficit (3% of GDP goal) and debt.
[0:2] - The FRBM **Bill** was introduced in Parliament in **2000**; **AP's own FRBM Act is of 2005** – both are traps for "the FRBM Act year" (answer **2003**).
[0:3] # Definitions
[0:4] - **Revenue deficit** = revenue expenditure − revenue receipts.
[0:5] - **Fiscal deficit** = total expenditure − total receipts excluding borrowings.
[0:6] # AP deficit ratios

  [0:7.0] Revenue deficit | ₹22,002 crore = 1.11% of GSDP | ₹41,118 crore = 2.33%

[0:8] - FD **3.84%** is still **above the 3% norm**.
[0:9] - Outstanding debt about **36% of GSDP** (about ₹7.11 lakh crore) in 2026-27.
