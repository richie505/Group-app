## SUB 3 | Agriculture – Pulses and Mango Exports
[3:0] # Pulses 2025-26
[3:1] - **AP 1st in pulse production growth: +57.73%**; **Uttar Pradesh 2nd (+17.7%)**.
[3:2] - Production **9.96 → 15.71 lakh tonnes** (+5.75 lakh t); area 28.45 → 34.70 lakh acres.
[3:3] - **Green gram** contributes the **largest share**.
[3:4] - Reasons: self-sufficiency drive, quality seeds, pest management, value addition, **higher MSP**.
[3:5] # Mango exports 2025-26
[3:6] - **Top importer: UAE** – ₹144.41 crore; shipments +11,622 MT; India's mangoes went to **52 countries** (DGCIS, APEDA data).
[3:7] - AP varieties in demand: **Banganapalli, Benishan, small Rasalu**; AP ≈ **8–10%** of India's mango exports.
[3:8] - Dominant Indian varieties abroad: **Alphonso, Kesar, Khader**.
[3:9] - **APEDA:** statutory body, **APEDA Act 1985**, Ministry of Commerce and Industry; HQ **New Delhi** (not Mumbai).
[3:10] ~~ Sources: Praveen CA-360 Jun 2026 p.86 (pulses); CARE APPSC CA Jul 2026 p.21-22 (mango exports); Praveen CA-360 Feb 2026 p.19 and Jan 2026 p.69 (APEDA, New Delhi)
## SUB 4 | MSMEs (EESL, RAMP) and the Amaravati Chitrakala Veedhi
[4:0] # EESL MSME sustainability plan
[4:1] - **EESL** (Energy Efficiency Services Limited): PSU under the **Union Ministry of Power**; known for **LED lighting, e-mobility, smart meters**.
[4:2] - Aim: **energy efficiency and water efficiency** in AP MSMEs – energy audits of 452, water audits of 300 MSMEs; cluster studies in 15 clusters.
[4:3] - Under the Centre's **RAMP** and **AP MSME & Entrepreneur Development Policy 4.0**; work order at the **AP MSME Growth Summit**.
[4:4] - **RAMP** = **Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance**: **World Bank-supported** Central Sector Scheme (2022), Ministry of MSME, 2022-23 to 2026-27.
[4:5] # Amaravati Chitrakala Veedhi
[4:6] - Held at **Guntur, 8–9 August 2026**; inspired by Bengaluru's **Chitra Santhe**; AP Creativity and Culture Commission; ~700 artists from 10 States.
[4:7] ~~ Sources: CARE APPSC CA Jun 2026 p.4-6 (EESL, RAMP); CivicCentre IAS APPSC daily 11 Nov 2026 p.5 (EESL under Ministry of Power); Praveen CA-360 Aug 2026 p.36 (Chitrakala Veedhi)
