## SUB 0 | Vedic Assemblies – Sabha, Samiti, Vidatha, Gana
[0:0] TABLE head: Assembly | Nature | Key facts
  [0:0.0] Sabha | Small, select council of elders/nobles; advised the Rajan and had judicial functions | Mentioned 8 times in the Rigveda; women members Sabhavati; "narishta" in the Atharvaveda
  [0:0.1] Samiti | General tribal assembly of the people | Tribal business, legislation, choice of the chief
  [0:0.2] Vidatha | Oldest assembly – religious, military and distributive | Most mentioned (122 times); first to disappear in Later Vedic times
  [0:0.3] Gana | Tribal group / troop | Also a military unit – not a judicial body or a priest
[0:1] - **Daughters of Prajapati:** the **Atharvaveda** calls the **Sabha and Samiti** the two daughters of Prajapati – where they appear as separate institutions (UPPCS 2009; MPPSC 2024).
[0:2] - **Judicial assembly:** **Sabha** (JPSC 2016).
[0:3] - **RAS 1994:** the Sabha = a select council (closest: "**council of ministers**"); the "assembly of all the people" is the **Samiti**.
[0:4] - **Women:** attended the **Sabha and Vidatha**; the Atharvaveda mentions women in the **Samiti**; in the Later Vedic age women were excluded and the Vidatha vanished, while the Sabha and Samiti came under chiefs and nobles.
[0:5] - **Democratic element:** traces of election of the chief by the Samiti.

## SUB 1 | Nature of the Polity – Rajan and Tribal Chiefdom
[1:0] - **Tribal polity:** kinship-based **tribal chiefdom** (APPSC SQT 2018) – no formal bureaucracy, **no fixed territory**, no standing army.
[1:1] - **Hierarchy:** **Jana** (tribe; 275 mentions) → **Vis** (clan, under **Vispati**) → **Grama** (under **Gramani**) → **Kula** (family, under **Kulapa**); **janapada** is absent from the Rigveda.
[1:2] - **Rajan:** called **Gopati** (lord of cattle) / gopajanasya; protector of the tribe and cattle, **not ruler of a territory**; queen = **Mahisi**.
[1:3] - **Succession:** **monarchy**; the post tended to be **hereditary** (with traces of choice by the Samiti) → "**descended (hereditary) monarchy**" (RAS 1993).
[1:4] - **Divinity:** kingship not divine – only the Puru king **Trasadasyu** is called **ardhadeva** (demi-god).
[1:5] - **Later Vedic contrast:** territorial kingship, **Rajasuya**, **ratnins** and regular taxes.
[1:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History APPSC Part 1 p.26, p.28; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 1 p.127-128; app notes 1:16 (sub 0, 2)

  [2:0.4] Kulapa | Head of the family
  [2:0.5] Spasa | Spy
  [2:0.6] Duta | Messenger
  [2:0.7] Jivagribha, Ugra | Police officials
  [2:0.8] Madhyamasi | Mediator
[2:1] - **Army:** temporary tribal militia (**Sena**) in units **Sardha, Vrata and Gana**; foot soldiers (patti) and chariot-warriors (rathins); **bronze** weapons, **no iron**.
[2:2] - **Gavishti** = "search for cows" – the word for war.
[2:3] - **Bali:** a **voluntary offering** to the Rajan, not a regular tax.
