=== Meaning of 'Arya' – A Speech Group, Not a Race
- **'Aryan' = a speech (linguistic) group:** speakers of Indo-Aryan, a branch of the **Indo-European** family – not an ethnic group, nomadic people or "superior race" (UPSC 1999, asked 3 times).
- **'Arya' in classical Sanskrit:** a **noble man**; originally associated with "**high birth**" (UP Lower Sub 1998).
- **Max Muller:** popularised the term in modern scholarship and was the first European to speak of an "**Aryan race**" (SSC 2006), though he later said it meant only a language group.
- **William Jones** (18th c.) found the Sanskrit–Latin–Greek kinship that led to the idea of the Indo-European family.
- **Languages in India:** Indo-European (Aryan) about **73%** of speakers (NextGen); **Dardic** branch – Jammu and Kashmir.
> Exam angle: "The term Aryan denotes" → **a speech group**.

=== Avesta and Boghazkoi – Linguistic and Epigraphic Evidence
- **Avesta:** the **oldest Iranian** (Zoroastrian) text, in **Avestan, an old Iranian language**; closely similar to the Rig Veda → relates to **Iran** (UP Lower Sub 2004; APPSC FBO).
| Boghazkoi inscription | Fact |
|---|---|
| Place | **Asia Minor (Turkey)** – Hittite capital |
| Date | c. **1380 BCE** (14th century BCE) |
| Content | Treaty between a **Hittite** king and a **Mitanni** king |
| Gods named | **Indra, Mitra, Varuna, Nasatyas** – **not Agni** |
| Significance | Vedic-speaking people or gods in **West Asia**; taken as evidence of Aryan movement via Iran |
- **⚠ Trap:** Mansehra and Shahbazgarhi are Ashokan rock edicts; Junagarh is Rudradaman's – none names Vedic gods.
- **Other early Indo-European records:** Kassite (Iraq, c. 1600 BCE), Mitanni (Syria, 14th c. BCE).
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History APPSC Part 1 p.19, p.22; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 1 p.121; app notes 1:14 (sub 1)

=== Rigvedic Aryans vs Harappans
| Feature | Rigvedic Aryans | Harappans |
|---|---|---|
| Horse | **Domesticated**; horse-drawn chariots | No clear evidence |
| Warfare | **Coat of mail (varman)**, helmet, bronze weapons | No such evidence |
| Metals | Gold, silver, copper (ayas) | **Gold, silver, copper – not iron** |
| Wheels | Spoked | **Solid** |
| Settlement | Rural, mud settlements | Planned **urban** cities |
| Transmission | **Oral hymns** | Seals and script |
| Dead | Cremation | Mostly burial |
- **UPSC 2017:** I (coat of mail) ✓, II ("Indus people knew copper and iron") ✗, III (horse) ✓ → **I and III only**.
- **Early Vedic period:** c. **1500–1000 BCE**.

=== Migration Debate – Archaeology and Genetics
- **Mainstream:** Indo-Aryans came from Central Asia in waves, the Rig Vedic wave c. **1500 BCE**; migration, not invasion (supporters: **Asko Parpola**, Romila Thapar, Upinder Singh – migration with interaction and assimilation).
- **Route:** **Andronovo** (steppe, horses, chariots) → **BMAC** (Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, north Afghanistan – contact zone) → **Gandhara Grave culture** (Swat, horse remains, new burials): **3-1-2**.
- **Indigenous / Out of India:** **S.R. Rao, B.B. Lal, Shrikant Talageri**; B.B. Lal linked **Painted Grey Ware** with the Vedic tradition (PGW–Later Vedic link still open).
- **Genetics:** haplogroup **R1a**; 2021 *Science* paper – steppe migration 2000–1500 BCE.
- **Rakhigarhi** (Hisar, Haryana; largest known Harappan site, on the seasonal **Drishadvati**): DNA of a woman of about 4,600 years ago with **no steppe ancestry**; **Mound 7** burial plot – **56** skeletons.

=== Dasas, Dasyus and Panis
- **Dasas:** a branch of the early Aryans (mentioned in Iranian literature); the Aryan chief was soft towards them.
- **Dasyus:** the original inhabitants; possibly phallus-worshippers who kept no cattle for dairy; **Trasadasyu** = soft to Dasas, hostile to Dasyus.
- **Divodasa** of the Bharata clan defeated **Sambara**.
- **Panis:** non-Aryan cattle-holders and traders, condemned for greed.
