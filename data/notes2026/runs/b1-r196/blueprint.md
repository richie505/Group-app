## 5. Key corrections the owner should know
- **Tatvabodhini was not "the first Telugu magazine from Bellary".** The old notes copied the G2 2011 key. NextGen's PYQ book and the Rocket sheets both say Satya Doota was the first Telugu journal and was published from Bellary.
- **The Madras Mahajana Sabha president (16 May 1884) was P. Rangaiah Naidu** (NextGen L3 p.73). None of the printed options is his name, so the original question may have been garbled in transcription.
- **UPPCS 2017 "not a pre-Congress association":** all four options were pre-Congress bodies, so the printed key (Indian Association) cannot be right.
