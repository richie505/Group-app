## 3. Repeats and traps
- Repeats: Mediterranean–Jordan/Iraq (UPSC 2015 and 2017, five copies), UPSC 2019 sea pairs ×2, UPSC 2024 North Sea ×2 and Congo peatland ×2, UPSC 2020 flows-into ×2, UPSC 2000 Lake Victoria ×3, Victoria Falls ×3, Mekong ×3, Baikal ×7, Finland ×4, Seine ×3, Michigan ×2, Balearic ×2, Malta ×2, Baltic volcanoes ×2, Mediterranean continents ×2.
- Traps settled: Siam (Khorat) Plateau lies between the Menam and the Mekong – the old notes' "APPSC key: Irrawaddy and Sittang" was wrong and is dropped; the two UP RO/ARO Rhine questions (2021, 2023) carried opposite keys for the same statements – both now "both true"; Dead Sea 1,385 ft kept as the closest option but tagged outdated (now ~435–440 m); Lake Onega is in Karelia, not the "Moscow basin" (kept as the books' answer with a dispute line); "largest freshwater lake" = Superior by area, Baikal by volume; Lake Victoria is not in the Rift Valley; Red Sea key "Red Sea highest salinity" in the old notes dropped (the Dead Sea/Lake Assal are saltier).
