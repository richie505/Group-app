## SUB 1 | Rivers of Africa and the Americas
[32:1:0] # Africa
[32:1:1] - **Nile**: White Nile from **Lake Victoria**, Blue Nile from **Lake Tana** (Ethiopia; upper course **Abbay**); meet at **Khartoum**; into the Mediterranean. Egypt = "**Gift of the Nile**".
[32:1:2] - Basin (11): Burundi, Rwanda, DR Congo, Ethiopia, Sudan, South Sudan, Tanzania, Kenya, Eritrea, Uganda, Egypt – **NOT Chad**.
[32:1:3] - **Exotic river:** rises in a humid region and crosses a desert without local tributaries – **Nile**, Indus, Colorado.
[32:1:4] - **Congo**: Atlantic; its basin = second-largest tropical rainforest and **largest tropical peatland** (~29 billion t carbon; UNEP 2023).
[32:1:5] - **Zambezi**: Victoria Falls, **Kariba Dam** (Zambia–Zimbabwe); into the Indian Ocean.
[32:1:6] - **Niger**: rises in **Fouta Djallon**; "**Oil River**"; **inland delta in Mali**; mouth Gulf of Guinea (Atlantic).
[32:1:7] - South Sudan: landlocked; White Nile; capital **Juba**.
[32:1:8] # Americas
[32:1:9] - **Amazon**: rises in the Andes of **Peru**; Peru, Colombia, Brazil → Atlantic; navigable ~1,600 km.
[32:1:10] - **Mississippi**: rises at glacial **Lake Itasca** (Minnesota); tributaries Ohio, Arkansas, Red; into the **Gulf of Mexico** near New Orleans.
[32:1:11] - **St Lawrence** links the Great Lakes to the Atlantic.
[32:1:12] - **Colorado**: Grand Canyon, **Hoover Dam**.
## SUB 2 | Rivers of Asia and Europe
[32:2:0] # Asia
[32:2:1] - **Yangtze**: Tibetan Plateau → **East China Sea**; **Three Gorges Dam** (largest hydel project).
[32:2:2] - **Huang He** (Yellow River): "**Sorrow of China**"; Loess Plateau silt raises its bed → floods, course changes.
[32:2:3] - **Mekong**: "**Danube of the East**"; Tibetan Plateau → Yunnan (China), Myanmar, Laos, Thailand, Cambodia, Vietnam → **South China Sea** – NOT Malaysia or Bangladesh.
[32:2:4] - **Amur** (2,897 km): Russia–China (–Mongolia) border; into the **Sea of Okhotsk**.
[32:2:5] - **Ob, Yenisei, Lena**: north to the Arctic; spring floods (frozen mouths).
[32:2:6] - **Amu Darya, Syr Darya** → **Aral Sea**. **Tigris + Euphrates** → **Shatt al-Arab** → Persian Gulf.
[32:2:7] - **Mo Chhu** joins **Pho Chhu** at **Punakha** (Bhutan) → **Sankosh** (APPSC 2019).
[32:2:8] - Lop Nor: saline, China. APPSC key: **Siam (Khorat) plateau** – between the Irrawaddy and Sittang.
