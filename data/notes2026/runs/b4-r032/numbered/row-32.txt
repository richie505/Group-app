## SUB 4 | Rivers and Waterfalls of Africa and the Americas
[4:0] # Nile
[4:1] - **White Nile** from **Lake Victoria**; **Blue Nile** from **Lake Tana** (Ethiopia); they meet at **Khartoum**; mouth Mediterranean (arcuate delta).
[4:2] - **Basin (11):** Burundi, Rwanda, DR Congo, Tanzania, Kenya, Uganda, Ethiopia, Eritrea, South Sudan, Sudan, Egypt – **NOT Chad**.
[4:3] - **Exotic river:** rises in a humid region and crosses a desert without local tributaries – the **Nile** (also Indus, Colorado).
[4:4] - **Egypt = "Gift of the Nile"** (Herodotus): the Nile valley is a long **oasis** in the Sahara.
[4:5] - **South Sudan:** **landlocked**, main river the **White Nile**, capital **Juba** (not Akobo), mainly Christian.
[4:6] # Other African Rivers
[4:7] - **Congo:** Atlantic; equatorial basin = home of the **Pygmies** (Mbuti, Baka); basin holds the **world's largest tropical peatland** (Cuvette Centrale, ~29 billion t carbon ≈ 3 years of global fossil emissions; UNEP 2023).
[4:8] - **Zambezi:** **Victoria Falls** ("Mosi-oa-Tunya", the Smoke that Thunders; **Zambia–Zimbabwe** border, not Mozambique), **Kariba Dam**; into the **Indian Ocean**.
[4:9] - **Niger:** rises in Fouta Djallon; inland delta in Mali; "Oil River"; Gulf of Guinea. **Limpopo:** crosses the Tropic of Capricorn twice; Indian Ocean.
[4:10] # Americas
[4:11] - **Mississippi:** rises at glacial **Lake Itasca** (Minnesota); into the **Gulf of Mexico** near New Orleans (bird's-foot delta).
[4:12] - **Rio Grande:** forms the **USA (Texas)–Mexico** border (Rio Bravo del Norte in Mexico).
[4:13] - **Amazon:** rises in the Peruvian Andes → Atlantic; **St Lawrence** links the Great Lakes to the Atlantic; **Colorado:** Grand Canyon, Hoover Dam.
