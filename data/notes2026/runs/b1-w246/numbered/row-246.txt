## SUB 0 | Bardoli Satyagraha and Patel's Peasant Campaigns
[0:0] TABLE head: Campaign | Year | Patel's role / issue | Outcome
  [0:0.0] Kheda | 1918 | Gandhi the spiritual head; Patel, Indulal Yagnik, with Mohanlal Pandya and Narahari Parikh, led the mobilisation | Revenue collected only from those who could pay
  [0:0.1] Sarabandi (no-tax) campaign | 1922 | Led by Vallabhbhai Patel (UPSC 1996; State PSC) – not C.R. Das, Rajendra Prasad or Lajpat Rai | –
  [0:0.2] Borsad | 1923 | Patel against a punitive tax | Tax withdrawn
  [0:0.3] Bardoli | 1928 | Led by Vallabhbhai Patel | Hike suspended; fresh inquiry
[0:1] - **Order:** Kheda 1918 → Sarabandi 1922 → Borsad 1923 → Bardoli 1928.
[0:2] # Bardoli Satyagraha, 1928
[0:3] - **Leader:** **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel** (UPSC 2003; UKPSC 2005; WBCS 2020; AP High Court) – **not Gandhi**, Vithalbhai Patel or Mahadev Desai; year **1928** – not 1918, 1922 or 1936.
[0:4] - **Patel's important role** among the movements offered: **Bardoli** (UPPCS 2002) – Bijolia was Vijay Singh Pathik's, Dandi and the Ahmedabad mill strike Gandhi's.
[0:5] - **Place:** **Bardoli taluk, Surat district**, Gujarat; begun locally by the **Mehta brothers (Kalyanji, Kunwarji)** and **Dayalji Desai**.
[0:6] - **Issue:** land revenue raised by about **30%** despite famine and falling cotton prices.
[0:7] - **Method:** no-revenue campaign, 13 workers' camps (chhavanis), the **Bardoli Satyagraha Patrika**; women such as **Mithuben Petit, Bhaktiba, Maniben Patel**.
[0:8] - **Outcome:** the **Maxwell-Broomfield inquiry** cut the increase to about **6%**.
[0:9] - **Title "Sardar":** the **women of Bardoli** gave Patel the title "Sardar" during this satyagraha (some keys credit Gandhi) – for Bardoli, **not Kheda**.
[0:10] > Exam angle: Bardoli = Patel (×6); Sarabandi 1922 = Patel (×2); "Sardar" came from Bardoli.

## SUB 1 | Commemoration – National Unity Day and the Statue of Unity
[1:0] - **Rashtriya Ekta Diwas (National Unity Day):** **31 October**, Patel's **birth anniversary** (MPPSC 2016; APPSC G2 Mains 2019; AEE; Civil Assistant Surgeon) – not 14 Nov (Children's Day), 2 Oct or 14 April; announced by the Centre in **2014**.
[1:1] - **31 October 2024:** two-year commemoration of the **150th birth anniversary** launched.
[1:2] - **Statue of Unity:** dedicated to **Patel**; **182 m**, the world's tallest statue; inaugurated **31 October 2018** (Patel's 143rd birthday) (APPSC 2019 ×2); **Sadhu Bet island, Ekta Nagar (Kevadia), Narmada district, Gujarat**, between the **Vindhya and Satpura** ranges, facing the **Sardar Sarovar Dam**; sculptor **Ram V. Sutar**; bronze cladding by the Chinese foundry **Jiangxi Tongqing** (Toqine) – all three APPSC statements correct; among the SCO's "eight wonders" (2022). Not in Hyderabad.
[1:3] - **Sardar Sarovar Dam** (Narmada): **dedicated to the nation by PM Modi on 17 September 2017** (APPSC ASO).
[1:4] - **Sardar Patel National Unity Award (2019):** a **medal and citation, no cash**; at most **three a year**; open to any citizen or institution, not only the armed forces.
[1:5] > Exam angle: 31 October (×5); Statue of Unity – 2018, 182 m, Narmada district.
