=== Round Table Conferences, Communal Award and Poona Pact
| RTC | Dates | Congress | Ambedkar |
|---|---|---|---|
| First | Nov 1930 – Jan 1931 | **Did not take part** (Gandhi, Sarojini Naidu, Malaviya absent; Jinnah attended) | Represented Depressed Classes (with R. Srinivasan); **demanded separate electorates** |
| Second | 7 Sept – 1 Dec 1931 | **Gandhi alone** – the only RTC Congress attended | Again demanded separate electorates; Gandhi rejected it |
| Third | 17 Nov – 24 Dec 1932 | **Did not take part** | Attended |
- **Ambedkar attended all three RTCs**; Gandhi only the second; the Congress missed the **First and Third**.
- **Communal Award** (16 August 1932, **Ramsay MacDonald**) – a **unilateral British award** expanding separate electorates: Muslims, Europeans, Sikhs, Indian Christians, Anglo-Indians, **depressed classes** (declared a minority; separate electorate + double vote, for **20 years** – not 10), women, Marathas.
- **Gandhi's fast** at **Yerwada Jail** from **20 September 1932** – he feared a permanent split in Hindu society.
- **Poona Pact, 24 September 1932** ("Gandhi–Ambedkar Pact"): signed by **Ambedkar** (depressed classes) and **Madan Mohan Malaviya** (caste Hindus), also by Rajagopalachari and others – **Gandhi did not sign**.
  - **Joint electorate** with reserved seats: **148** in provincial legislatures (vs 71), **18%** of the Central Legislature; fair share in **civil services** and **local bodies**. Purpose: **representation of the untouchables**.
- **Aftermath:** Gandhi made **Harijan upliftment** part of his programme – All India Anti-Untouchability League (Sept 1932) → **Harijan Sevak Sangh**; weekly **Harijan** (1933); temple entry at **Siddantham** (Krishna district, AP).
| Pact | Year | Parties |
|---|---|---|
| Lucknow | **1916** | Congress–League; accepted separate electorates for Muslims |
| Gandhi–Irwin ("Delhi Pact") | 5 March **1931** (not 1932) | Gandhi, Irwin |
| Poona | 1932 | Ambedkar, Malaviya (Gandhi–Ambedkar) |
| Desai–Liaquat | January **1945** | Bhulabhai Desai, Liaquat Ali Khan |
| Nehru–Liaquat ("Delhi Pact") | April 1950 | Minorities |
