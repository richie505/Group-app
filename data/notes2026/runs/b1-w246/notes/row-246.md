# Bardoli Satyagraha, 1928
- **Leader:** **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel** (UPSC 2003; UKPSC 2005; WBCS 2020; AP High Court) – **not Gandhi**, Vithalbhai Patel or Mahadev Desai; year **1928** – not 1918, 1922 or 1936.
- **Patel's important role** among the movements offered: **Bardoli** (UPPCS 2002) – Bijolia was Vijay Singh Pathik's, Dandi and the Ahmedabad mill strike Gandhi's.
- **Place:** **Bardoli taluk, Surat district**, Gujarat; begun locally by the **Mehta brothers (Kalyanji, Kunwarji)** and **Dayalji Desai**.
- **Issue:** land revenue raised by about **30%** despite famine and falling cotton prices.
- **Method:** no-revenue campaign, 13 workers' camps (chhavanis), the **Bardoli Satyagraha Patrika**; women such as **Mithuben Petit, Bhaktiba, Maniben Patel**.
- **Outcome:** the **Maxwell-Broomfield inquiry** cut the increase to about **6%**.
- **Title "Sardar":** the **women of Bardoli** gave Patel the title "Sardar" during this satyagraha (some keys credit Gandhi) – for Bardoli, **not Kheda**.
> Exam angle: Bardoli = Patel (×6); Sarabandi 1922 = Patel (×2); "Sardar" came from Bardoli.

=== Commemoration – National Unity Day and the Statue of Unity
- **Rashtriya Ekta Diwas (National Unity Day):** **31 October**, Patel's **birth anniversary** (MPPSC 2016; APPSC G2 Mains 2019; AEE; Civil Assistant Surgeon) – not 14 Nov (Children's Day), 2 Oct or 14 April; announced by the Centre in **2014**.
- **31 October 2024:** two-year commemoration of the **150th birth anniversary** launched.
- **Statue of Unity:** dedicated to **Patel**; **182 m**, the world's tallest statue; inaugurated **31 October 2018** (Patel's 143rd birthday) (APPSC 2019 ×2); **Sadhu Bet island, Ekta Nagar (Kevadia), Narmada district, Gujarat**, between the **Vindhya and Satpura** ranges, facing the **Sardar Sarovar Dam**; sculptor **Ram V. Sutar**; bronze cladding by the Chinese foundry **Jiangxi Tongqing** (Toqine) – all three APPSC statements correct; among the SCO's "eight wonders" (2022). Not in Hyderabad.
- **Sardar Sarovar Dam** (Narmada): **dedicated to the nation by PM Modi on 17 September 2017** (APPSC ASO).
- **Sardar Patel National Unity Award (2019):** a **medal and citation, no cash**; at most **three a year**; open to any citizen or institution, not only the armed forces.
> Exam angle: 31 October (×5); Statue of Unity – 2018, 182 m, Narmada district.
