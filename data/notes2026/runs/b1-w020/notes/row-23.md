=== Gana-sanghas – Vajji, Malla, Shakya
| Aspect | Gana-sangha | Monarchy |
|---|---|---|
| Rule | Many rajas in an **assembly of clan heads** | Single hereditary king |
| Hall | **Santhagara** | Royal court |
| Location | NW and Himalayan foothills | Ganga plain |
| Examples | **Vajji, Malla** | Kosala, Magadha, **Vatsa** |
- **Vajji confederacy:** 8 clans – **Lichchhavis** (most powerful), Videhas, Jnatrikas, Vajjis; capital **Vaishali** = seat of the **world's first republic**; led by **Chetaka**, maternal uncle of **Mahavira**.
- **Malla:** confederacy of 9 clans; capitals **Kushinara** (Buddha's **Mahaparinirvana**) and **Pava** (Mahavira died). A-R (UPPSC 2004): Kushinagar = Malla capital and parinirvana site, both true, R not the explanation.
- **Buddha-period republics:** Shakya (Kapilavastu), Bhagga (Sumsumaragiri), Buli (Alakappa), Kalama (Kesaputta), Koliya (Ramagama), Malla, Lichchhavi. **Yaudheya** is NOT one – it is a post-Mauryan republic of Punjab–Haryana.
- **Kamboja:** a monarchy in the 6th century BCE but a **sangha** in the Arthashastra.

=== Sources Listing the Mahajanapadas
| Text | Tradition | List |
|---|---|---|
| **Anguttara Nikaya** (Sutta Pitaka) | Buddhist | **16 Mahajanapadas** |
| **Bhagavati Sutra** | **Jain** | Also 16, some different names; adds Vanga, Malava, Ladha |
| Digha Nikaya | Buddhist | Only 12 (omits Ashmaka, Avanti, Gandhara, Kamboja) |

- **Panini:** born at **Pushkalavati** (Gandhara); Ashtadhyayi = ~4,000 sutras in **8 chapters**.
- Anguttara order: Anga, Magadha, Kashi, Kosala, Vajji, Malla, Chedi, Vatsa, Kuru, Panchala, Matsya, Shurasena, Ashmaka, Avanti, Gandhara, Kamboja.

=== Rivers and the South – Assaka on the Godavari
- **Assaka (Ashmaka):** the **only Mahajanapada south of the Vindhyas**, on the **Godavari** (between the Godavari and Manjira); on the **Dakshinapatha**.
- Capital **Potana / Potali = Bodhan, Nizamabad (Telangana)**; some place it at **Paithan (ancient Pratishthana)**.
- **Suttanipata:** sage **Bavari's** ashram on the Godavari in Assaka – earliest Andhra-region link in Buddhist texts.

=== Kingdoms and Rulers of the Buddha's Time
| Ruler | Kingdom |
|---|---|
| Bimbisara, Ajatashatru | Magadha |
| **Prasenajit** (Pasenadi) | **Kosala** |
| **Udayana** | **Vatsa** |
| **Chanda Pradyota** | **Avanti** (a monarchy) |
| Pukkusati | Gandhara |
- **Four great powers:** **Magadha, Kosala, Vatsa, Avanti**; Gandhara was not one. After Magadha, **Kosala** (which absorbed Kashi and the Shakyas) was the strongest – "only Koshala and Magadha emerged as powerful".
- **Kosala** annexed **Kashi**; Magadha later got it as dowry and by war. **Pradyota** rivalled **Bimbisara and Udayana**.
- **Udayana:** married **Vasavadatta**, daughter of Pradyota (**Bhasa's Svapnavasavadatta**); the Buddha visited **Kaushambi** in his reign; turned Buddhist under **Pindola Bharadvaja**; donated the **Ghoshitarama** vihara.
- **Legend links:** **Dasharatha** ruled **Kosala** (Ayodhya); in the Puranas **King Anga** was the father of **Vena** (father of Prithu).
