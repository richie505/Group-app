=== Robotics and Automation
# Basics
- **Robotics:** interdisciplinary – mechanical, electrical/electronics, computer engineering; **Mechatronics** = mechanical engineering + electronics + robotics/computing (product engineering).
- **CAM (Computer-Aided Manufacturing):** computers assist in **all operations of a manufacturing plant** (planning, control); paired with CAD.
| Type | Use |
|---|---|
| Industrial | Welding, painting, assembly, packaging |
| Service | Hospitality, retail, reception; domestic (vacuum cleaners) |
| Surgical / medical | Robotic surgery, rehabilitation |
| Humanoid | Human-like form (Manav, Vyommitra, Optimus) |
| Cobots | Work safely beside humans (Industry 4.0) |
- **Advantages:** help people with **disabilities**, work in **dangerous environments**, **never tire**. **Disadvantage:** **job displacement** ("can replace jobs" is NOT an advantage).
- **ISO 10218** = safety norms for **industrial robots**.

=== Semiconductors and Indigenous Chips
# Basics
- **Silicon** = the **semiconductor** used for computer (IC) chips (exam key for "newest substance in chips"; strictly **gallium arsenide** is the newer compound semiconductor).
- **Not a semiconductor:** **Krypton** (noble gas); silicon, germanium, selenium are.
- **p-type:** trivalent doping → **holes** = majority carriers; n-type → electrons.
- **Diode:** current one way only → **AC to DC** (rectification); a **semiconductor diode is non-ohmic** (copper coil, heater coil, rheostat are ohmic).
- **Silicon carbide** (carborundum): very hard → abrasive for **cutting very hard substances**; also a power semiconductor.
- **Piezoelectric MEMS** (May 2023): **IIT Madras + DRDO** (NPOL) – PZT thin-film **underwater** acoustic sensor for Navy SONAR.

=== Extended Reality, Metaverse and Haptics
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Virtual Reality (VR)** | Fully computer-generated, **immersive** world that **shuts out** the real one; the user is still a **real person** |
| **Augmented Reality (AR)** | Digital content **overlaid on the real world** via a phone/PC camera (Pokemon Go); does not block the physical world |
| Mixed Reality (MR) | Real and virtual objects interact in real time |
| Extended Reality (XR) | Umbrella for VR + AR + MR |
| **Metaverse** | **Interoperable network of 3D virtual worlds**, accessed by millions at once, with **property rights over virtual items** (UPSC 2024) |
| **Haptic technology** | **Tactile feedback** – recreates touch by **forces, vibrations or motions** |
- **UPSC 2019:** "AR shuts out the physical world" ✗ (that is VR); "VR projects images onto real objects" ✗ (that is AR) → **3 and 4 only**.
- Thomas Caudell (Boeing) coined "augmented reality" (1990); **VARCoE** at IIT Bhubaneswar; **IMAGE CoE** at Hyderabad.

=== 3D Printing
- **Additive manufacturing:** objects built **layer by layer** from a **CAD** model; opposite of subtractive machining; **4D printing** adds materials that **change shape over time**.
- **Applications (UPSC 2018 key: all five):** **confectionery**, **bionic ears**, **automotive industry**, **reconstructive surgery**, data-processing technologies; also aerospace, construction, implants.
- **Surgery:** 3D printing gives the surgeon a **physical model of the patient's anatomy** (surgical planning).
- **India's first 3D-printed post office:** **Bengaluru** (Cambridge Layout, near Ulsoor), **2023**; 1,021 sq ft, built in **43 days**.
> ⚠ Disputed: PMF IAS (book p.772) prints (a) 1, 3 and 4 only for the 2018 question; the UPSC key and most books give (d) all five.

=== Internet of Things, RFID and Wearables
# IoT
- **IoT:** sensor-equipped devices sharing data over a network **without human intervention**; term by **Kevin Ashton** (1999).
- **Agriculture (APPSC FRO 2022 A-R):** traditional farming is labour-intensive (A true); IoT sensors offer an alternative (R true) – **R does not explain A**.
| Layer | Job |
|---|---|
| Perception | Senses (sensors, RFID) |
| Network | Transmits |
| Application | Smart services |
- Four-stage version adds a **transport** layer.
| Variant | Concerns |
|---|---|
| IIoT | Industrial automation |
| IoNT | Nano-devices |
| Internet of Mobile Things | Mobile objects (not assembly lines) |
| Mission-critical IoT | Military applications |
- **IoT vs AI:** smart thermostat, fitness wearable = **IoT**; chatbot, image recognition = **AI** (analyses and decides).
- Uses: **smart farming, homes, grids**, cities, healthcare; **NDCP 2018** covers 5G, IoT, M2M; IoT CoEs (MeitY-NASSCOM): Bengaluru, Gurugram, Gandhinagar, Visakhapatnam.
# RFID
| Tag | Power | Reply |
|---|---|---|
| **Passive** | **No battery** – powered by the reader's field | Backscatters the reader's signal |
| **Semi-passive** | Battery runs the chip only | Uses the reader's radio waves to reply |
| **Active** | **Own battery** and transmitter | Broadcasts itself, long range |
- RFID needs **no line of sight** within range → APPSC G1: **II and IV only** correct.
# Wearables
- Smartwatches, fitness bands: **location**, **sleep monitoring**, heart rate; **hearing aids** for the hearing impaired → UPSC 2019: **I, II and III**.

=== Drones, UAVs and eVTOL
- **UAV types:** multirotor (quadcopter), **fixed-wing**, single-rotor, hybrid **VTOL**; **not all** land vertically, hover automatically or run on battery only → UPSC 2025: **none** correct.
- **eVTOL** = electric Vertical Take-Off and Landing aircraft (air taxis) → **aviation**.
| Drone Rules, 2021 class | Weight |
|---|---|
| Nano | up to 250 g |
| Micro | 250 g – 2 kg |
| Small | 2–25 kg |
| Medium | 25–150 kg |
| Large | above 150 kg |
- **Swarms:** many drones coordinated by software (surveillance, light shows, strikes).
- **Drone hub by 2030:** Drone Rules 2021, **DigitalSky**, **PLI ₹120 crore** (2021), **Drone Shakti**, DGCA-approved training institutes.
- **Kisan drones:** spraying and land mapping; **100** flagged off Feb 2022. **Namo Drone Didi:** drones to **women SHGs**; ₹1,261 crore (2023-24 to 2025-26) for **15,000** SHGs.
- **AP Drone City:** **Orvakal, Kurnool**.

=== Industry 4.0 and Automation Terms
| Stage | Defining technology |
|---|---|
| Industry 1.0 | Mechanisation, **steam** (steam looms) |
| Industry 2.0 | **Electricity, mass production, assembly line** |
| Industry 3.0 | Automation, **computers and electronics** |
| Industry 4.0 | **Cyber-physical systems**, IoT, AI, big data; smart factories |
| Industry 5.0 | **Human-machine collaboration** |
- **'Industry 4.0'** coined in **Germany** (Hannover Fair, 2011); **'Fourth Industrial Revolution'** – **Klaus Schwab** (WEF), 2016.
- **SAMARTH Udyog Bharat 4.0** – **Ministry of Heavy Industries**; **NM-ICPS** – **25 Technology Innovation Hubs**.
- **AP:** WEF 4IR Centre would be India's **third** (after Mumbai and Telangana).
- **Criticism:** automation and AI replace **intellectual as well as manual** work.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| PLC | Programmable Logic Controller |
| SCADA | Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition – remote monitoring of plants and grids |
| Digital twin | Virtual replica for simulation and monitoring |
| Cyber-physical system | Computing joined with physical processes |
| Cobot | Collaborative robot |
| mMTC | 5G slice for dense IoT sensors |
