=== Digital Media, Multimedia and Media Terms
# Multimedia and digital content
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Multimedia** | **Text, graphics, audio, video, animation, voiceovers** combined in one presentation (numeric data alone is not multimedia) |
| **Podcast** | A **radio programme shared via the Internet** (digital audio to stream or download); internet radio / digital broadcast = live |
| **e-journals** | Published on the **Internet** |
| **FCP** | **Final Cut Pro** – Apple's non-linear **video editing** (production) software; Adobe Premiere Pro also edits video |
| **Heliodisplay** | **Air-based (mid-air) display**: images projected onto a thin layer of **mist/fog**, no screen |
| **Ticker** | Strip of scrolling text (headlines, share prices) along the bottom of a **television** screen |
| **Referral service** | Directing the user to suitable **sources of information available elsewhere** (library science) |
| **Specimen** | A **real object taken out of its natural setting** (teaching aid); a model is a replica |
- **Kazaa download:** **Supari** (2003) was the first Bollywood feature sold as a download ($2.99) to Kazaa users (APPSC G1 2004).
# Media terms (APPSC G2 Mains 2019 set)
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Infomercial** | **Paid programming** – long TV advertisement looking like a programme |
| **Innuendo** | Defamation by implication; spoken = **slander**, written = libel |
| **Semiotics** | Study of **signs and sign systems** (Saussure, Peirce) |
| **Technological determinism** | Technology **'causes'** behaviour / drives social change (**Marshall McLuhan**) |
| **'Knowledgeable sources'** | **Not official**, but with **access to official information** |
# Broadcasting and media organisations
- **BARC** (Broadcast Audience Research Council, **2015**): measures **TV audiences**, replacing **TAM**; IRS / NRS are readership surveys.
- **The Wall Street Journal:** business daily from **New York, USA** (Dow Jones, News Corp); **CNN** = **Cable News Network** (US, 1980).
- **Disney** bought 21st Century Fox's studio and TV assets (deal completed **2019**).
- **ANI** (1971): first Indian news agency with **syndicated video news**.
- **DTH** takes signals from **geostationary satellites** straight to home dishes; **DD Free Dish** (Prasar Bharati) – India's **first free DTH**, launched **December 2004** as DD Direct+, renamed 2013.

=== Mobile Telephony – Firsts, Standards and Terms
# Firsts
- **First handheld mobile call:** **Martin Cooper** of **Motorola**, **3 April 1973** (to Joel Engel of Bell Labs); so Cooper is credited with inventing the **mobile phone**.
- **First mobile service in India:** exam key **Hutchison** (Max Touch, Mumbai); India's **first mobile call** was on **Modi Telstra**, Kolkata, **31 July 1995** (Jyoti Basu to Sukh Ram).
- **Motorola** (March 2003) showed a phone displaying the user's location; **Tata Teleservices PTT** (push-to-talk, May 2004) – then only in USA, Japan, Malaysia (**not Australia**).
- **QR code:** **Quick Response**, read quickly by a phone camera; designed **1994** (not 1996) by **Denso Wave**, Japan, for the **car industry**.
- **Mobile Number Portability:** pilot **Haryana**, 25 Nov 2010; pan-India **20 Jan 2011**; full nationwide porting 3 Jul 2015.
- India = world's **second-largest** telecom market (May 2025).
# Standards and full forms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **GSM** | **Global System for Mobile Communications** – 2G digital standard (India 1995) |
| **CDMA** | **Code Division Multiple Access** |
| **GPRS** | **General Packet Radio Service** – 2G packet data: Internet on the phone without modem or computer ('GRPS' in the 2003 paper = GPRS) |
| **LTE** | **Long-Term Evolution** – raises capacity and speed; 4G (uses **OFDM and MIMO**); WiMAX lost out |
| **SIM** | **Subscriber Identity Module** |
| **OTG** | **On-The-Go** (USB OTG: phone acts as USB host) |
- **LTE and IMT-Advanced:** original LTE did **not** meet the ITU **IMT-Advanced** 4G requirements; only **LTE-Advanced** did (UPSC 2010: only statement I correct).
- Generations: 1G analog → 2G GSM/CDMA → 3G WCDMA → 4G LTE → 5G NR; **6G expected early 2030s**.
- **Android 16** released for Pixel phones in June 2025; **Pixel Buds** (2017): Google Assistant with real-time translation of **40 languages**.
# NFC (Near Field Communication)
- **Contactless** communication using **electromagnetic radio fields** (**not light waves**); range **a few centimetres** (~4 cm, not a metre); can **encrypt**; built into **smartphones**.
- Speed **106–424 kbps**; easier pairing and **lower power than Bluetooth**; RBI allows **offline UPI Lite** payments over NFC; RuPay tap-and-pay.
> UPSC 2015: "a metre apart" ✗ → answer **a and c only**; APPSC: "uses light waves" = the untrue statement.

=== E-mail and Video Conferencing
# E-mail
- **E-mail** = **Electronic Mail**; address = **user name @ domain name** – in mark.sttol@ITdesk.info the **domain is ITdesk.info**.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| SMTP | Sends mail |
| POP3 / IMAP | Receive mail |
| CC | Sends copies (all see each other) |
| **BCC** | **Blind carbon copy** – hides recipients' addresses from one another |
| **Video mail** | Pre-recorded video by e-mail – can carry **graphics, video clips, video messages** (all) |
| **Finger** | Protocol/command to **find a user's e-mail address** and login details (Archie = FTP file search) |
| **Listserv** | Mailing list linking people with **similar interests** worldwide |
| **Instant messaging** | Real-time messages between two or more people |
- **Free e-mail providers:** **Hotmail, Rediffmail, Yahoo** (and Gmail) – all free.
- **Rajasthan:** first state to give residents a **free e-mail address in Hindi** (2016).
# Video conferencing
- **Live two-way video and audio** between two or more places using telecom technology (with data).
- **Essentials:** **video camera, display, microphone**, speaker, broadband, codec software; a **telephone is not essential** (nor an RFID reader).
> Match (FSO): e-mail = instant delivery with attachments; listserv = similar interests; video conferencing = video + audio + data; instant messaging = real-time exchange.

=== Laws on Digital Platforms
| Act / rules | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules | 2020 | Under the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019**; grievance officer; display **country of origin** |
| IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules | 2021 | Under **Section 87** of the IT Act; replaced 2011 rules; social media, OTT, digital news; **three-tier** grievance redressal |
| IT Amendment Rules | 2023 | Ignoring orders on false content → loss of **safe harbour (Section 79)** |
| Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act | 2025 | Bans **real-money gaming**; **Online Gaming Authority of India** – attached office of **MeitY**; rules effective **1 May 2026** |
| Code on Social Security | 2020 | **Gig and platform workers** defined first; in force with all four labour codes from **21 Nov 2025** |
| DPDP Act | 2023 | **Child = below 18**; verifiable parental consent (**Section 9**) |
- Proposed law on social-media access: **graded, age-based limits**, not a blanket ban.
