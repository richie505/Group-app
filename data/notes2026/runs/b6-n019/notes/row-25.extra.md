=== New Current Affairs: 5–14 Nov 2026
# Supreme Court rulings
- **[6 Nov 2026] Article 22(2) - 24-hour rule**: the **24-hour deadline** for producing an arrested person before a **magistrate** runs from the **time of detention**, **not** from when the police **formally record the arrest**.
  - Art 22 (ordinary law): grounds of arrest; right to a lawyer; produce before a magistrate **within 24 hours** (journey time excluded); no further custody without the magistrate's authority.
  - Art 22 (preventive detention, citizens and aliens): detention beyond **3 months** needs an **Advisory Board** of **High Court judges**.
  - **Section 58, BNSS 2023**: arrest without warrant - no custody beyond **24 hours** without a magistrate's special order.
- **[6 Nov 2026] Children's internet access** (as reported by CivicCentre, 2026; ⚠ the case title CivicCentre gives, *Just Rights for Children Alliance v. S. Harish*, is the 2024 CSEAM ruling — verify): **internet access** recognised as a **fundamental right of children** under **Articles 21 and 19(1)(a)**; Court **refused a blanket ban** on children's social media use; case on **CSEAM** under the **POCSO Act 2012** and **IT Act 2000**.
  - Related: **Unni Krishnan J.P. v. State of Andhra Pradesh (1993)** - **right to education** flows from **Article 21** (five-judge bench).
- **[7 Nov 2026] OBC creamy layer**: status **cannot be decided only by parents' salary/income**; the **social status and category of post** held by the parents must also count. The **DoPT Office Memorandum of 1993** had **excluded income from salary and agricultural land** from the income/wealth test.
- **[5 Nov 2026] Two-child norm laws**: SC said laws **penalising more than two children** have **lost relevance** as fertility has fallen.
  - **SRS Statistical Report 2024** (**ORGI**): India's **TFR 1.9** (rural **2.1**, urban **1.5**); **Bihar highest (2.9)**, then UP (2.6); **Delhi lowest (1.2)**; Tamil Nadu, Kerala, West Bengal **1.3**.
- **[7 Nov 2026]** SC: **"Cybercriminals have no place in society."** **I4C** (Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre) - **MHA**, dedicated to the nation **10 January 2020**.
> ⚠ Trap: the 24-hour clock starts at **detention**, not at the recorded arrest; replacement-TFR is 2.1 - India's 1.9 is **below replacement**.
# Cabinet, schemes and regulators
- **[5 Nov 2026] Fast Track Special Courts (FTSCs)**: **Centrally Sponsored Scheme** extended **5 years** - **1 April 2026 to 31 March 2031**.
  - Started **October 2019**; **Ministry of Law and Justice**; time-bound trial of **rape** and **POCSO Act 2012** cases.
  - Outlay **₹3,788.04 crore** (Centre **₹2,353.15 crore**, States/UTs **₹1,434.89 crore**); Central share from the **Nirbhaya Fund** (**2013**, **non-lapsable**, Ministry of Finance).
- **[5 Nov 2026] Agnipath change**: the **remaining 75% of Agniveers** get **2 more years** and a chance to join the regular forces (earlier only **25%** retained after **4 years**). Seva Nidhi in the first 4 years **50:50** (Agniveer:Government); Government pays **70%** of the corpus for those leaving after the extra 2 years. Scheme of **2022** (MoD).
- **[5 Nov 2026] Vayo Samman Rashtriya Abhiyan**: a **National Intergenerational Bonding Campaign** launched by **Dr Virendra Kumar** (**Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment**) under the **Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY, 2021)**; **"5C" model** - **Connect, Converse, Co-create, Contribute, Continue**. **Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana** (1 Apr 2017) gives assisted-living devices (Central Sector).
- **[5 Nov 2026] CERT-In AI security guidelines**: AI-related security incidents to be reported **within 6 hours**; AI systems in **four risk categories** - low, moderate, high, critical. **CERT-In**: **2004**, **MeitY**, national incident-response agency under **Section 70B, IT Act 2000**.
- **[9 Nov 2026] NPPA** capped trade margins on the MRP of **137 anti-cancer drugs** (follow-up of the Oct 2026 **30% trade-margin cap**). NPPA: **1997**, attached office of the **Department of Pharmaceuticals**; enforces **DPCO**; **Pharma Sahi Daam** app.
- **[10 Nov 2026] NMC** warned students against **fake medical colleges**. NMC: statutory, **NMC Act 2019**, set up **2020**, **Ministry of Health**; **33 members** (Chairperson + **10 ex officio** + **22 part-time**) under **Section 4(1)**.
> ⚠ Trap: FTSCs are for **rape and POCSO** cases and run to **31 March 2031**; NMC has **33** members, not 25.
# Constitutional bodies in news
- **[7 Nov 2026] Kerala PSC**: a delegation asked the **President** to act on alleged irregularities in the **Kerala Public Service Commission**.
  - **SPSC**: Part **XIV (Arts 315-323)**; Chairman and members **appointed by the Governor** but **removed only by the President**; term **6 years or age 62** (UPSC: **65**); strength left to the **Governor**.
  - Removal for **misbehaviour** needs a **Supreme Court inquiry** whose advice **binds** the President; other grounds: insolvency, paid outside employment, infirmity of mind or body.
- **[2 Nov 2026] States' Formation Day (1 November)**: **AP, Karnataka (as Mysore), Kerala, MP, Tamil Nadu (as Madras)** - **1956**; **Punjab and Haryana** - **1966**; **Chhattisgarh** - **2000**. **Article 2**: admit/establish new States (territories outside India); **Article 3**: form new States, alter areas, boundaries, names of existing States.
- **[3 Nov 2026] Kannada flag**: Karnataka is considering official recognition of the **red-and-yellow Kannada flag**; a 2018 State-flag proposal was **rejected by MHA**. **Flag Code of India, 2002**; national flag ratio **3:2**, adopted **22 July 1947**.
> ⚠ Trap: an SPSC member is appointed by the **Governor** but can be removed only by the **President** - never by the Governor.
