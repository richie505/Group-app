[2:4] - **Rock Garden, Chandigarh:** created by **Nek Chand** from industrial and urban waste.
[2:5] - **Mylacherla stepwell:** a horseshoe-shaped granite stepwell (c. 300-350 years old) credited to the **Gandi brothers**, cattle herders, in the Nallamala forest – hamlet near Vatlabailu, **Chandrasekharapuram** mandal, **Prakasam** district (Forest Beat Officer 2019).

## SUB 4 | Nagarjunakonda and Buddhist Sites Dug after 1956
[4:0] # Nagarjunakonda – salvage archaeology
[4:1] - Found **1926** by **A. Ranganatha Saraswati**; dug by **Longhurst (1927)**, later Ramachandran, **R. Subrahmanyam**.
[4:2] - Finds: acoustic building, **amphitheatre**, **Santamula's Ashvamedha altar**, **Sati sculpture**, **30+ stupas**.
[4:3] - **Nagarjunasagar Dam:** site submerged **after 1960**; ASI rebuilt monuments on higher ground at **Anupu** and on the island = **"Transplanted Monuments"**.
[4:4] - Island museum on the hilltop opened **1966** (ASI).
[4:5] # Buddhist sites
[4:6] TABLE head: Site | District | Feature
  [4:6.0] Bavikonda | Visakhapatnam | Hilltop monastery, among the oldest (Hinayana phase)
  [4:6.1] Thotlakonda | Visakhapatnam | Stupa, apsidal chaityas, rainwater harvesting; spotted by the Indian Navy
  [4:6.2] Salihundam | Srikakulam | Port town; Tara bronzes, Buddha footprint; found by Gidugu Venkata Ramamurthy
  [4:6.3] Bojjannakonda | Anakapalli | Monolithic Buddha in Dhyana mudra; Mahayana-Vajrayana
  [4:6.4] Gudiwada Dibba | – | Mauryan to Satavahana remains
