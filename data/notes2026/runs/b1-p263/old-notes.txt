## SUB 2 | Jai Andhra 1972–73, Six-Point Formula and 32nd Amendment
[263:2:0] # Jai Andhra
[263:2:1] - **1972–73**, Coastal Andhra and Rayalaseema – **against the Mulki Rules**; demand: repeal or a **separate Andhra State**.
[263:2:2] - **Main / immediate cause:** the **Supreme Court judgment of October 1972** upholding the Mulki Rules – not taxes, water or administration.
[263:2:3] - Main issue: Andhras treated as "outsiders" in Hyderabad, a capital built with the whole State's revenue.
[263:2:4] - Leaders: **Kakani Venkataratnam**, **B.V. Subba Reddy**, **Tenneti Viswanatham**; students the core.
[263:2:5] - **CM: P.V. Narasimha Rao** (sworn in 30 Sep 1971); resigned **18 Jan 1973**.
[263:2:6] - **Five-Point Formula – 27 Nov 1972** (PM Indira Gandhi): Mulki rules in twin cities till **1977**, Telangana till **1980**; **1 in 3** Secretariat posts to Telangana locals; first two gazetted posts localised.
[263:2:7] - Separate Andhra **flag hoisted 10 Dec 1972**.
[263:2:8] - **President's Rule: 18 Jan – 10 Dec 1973** (~11 months) – the **first** in Andhra Pradesh and the **longest**; imposed because of the Jai Andhra agitation.
[263:2:9] - **Jalagam Vengala Rao** CM from **10 Dec 1973**.
[263:2:10] - **Ended by the Six-Point Formula** (21 Sep 1973); agitation called off **1 Oct 1973** – not by repression, arrests or mere Mulki abolition.
[263:2:11] # Six-Point Formula
[263:2:12] TABLE head: No. | Clause | Outcome
  [263:2:12.0] 1 | Mulki Rules and Telangana Regional Committee abolished | TRC dissolved 1 Jan 1974
  [263:2:12.1] 2 | Local preference in direct recruitment; tribunal for service disputes | Local cadres, 6 zones, AP Administrative Tribunal
  [263:2:12.2] 3 | Employees' grievances committee | —
  [263:2:12.3] 4 | Backward-area development | State Planning Commission with local sub-committees
  [263:2:12.4] 5 | Local preference in admissions | Central University, Hyderabad (Art 371E)
  [263:2:12.5] 6 | Constitutional amendment | 32nd Amendment, Presidential Order 1975
[263:2:13] - Approved **18 Dec 1973**; Mulki Rules replaced by the **zonal system**.
[263:2:14] # 32nd Amendment, 1973
[263:2:15] - Gave the Six-Point Formula constitutional backing: inserted **Art 371D** and **Art 371E**; in force **1 July 1974**.
[263:2:16] - It is the **32nd** Amendment **Act** – some NextGen pages say "33rd", which was the **Bill's** number.
[263:2:17] - **371D**: equitable opportunities in jobs and education; local cadres by **Presidential Order**; **Administrative Tribunal**. **371E**: **Central University** in the State.
[263:2:18] > Jai Andhra 1972 (SC Mulki verdict; CM PVNR) → PR 18 Jan–10 Dec 1973 → Six-Point Formula → 32nd Amendment (371D, 371E).

## SUB 6 | Mulki Rules and Jai Telangana 1969
[263:6:0] - **1888**, Hyderabad State: locals to get State posts; outsider only with the **Prime Minister's (Diwan's)** permission. **1919 Nizam's Farman**: outsider needed the **Nizam's personal permission**.
[263:6:1] - **Mulki** = continuous residence of **15 years** (Nizam's rules); **12 years** (Gentlemen's Agreement, 1956).
[263:6:2] TABLE head: Year | Event | Stance
  [263:6:2.0] 1956 | Gentlemen's Agreement – safeguards | Both regions
  [263:6:2.1] 1957 | Public Employment (Requirement as to Residence) Act | Residence condition for Telangana posts
  [263:6:2.2] 1969 | Jai Telangana | Telangana – for Mulki rules
  [263:6:2.3] 1972 | Jai Andhra | Andhra – against Mulki rules
  [263:6:2.4] 1973 | Six-Point Formula; 32nd Amendment | Compromise
  [263:6:2.5] 1975 | Presidential Order – local cadres | Implementation
  [263:6:2.6] 1985 | GO 610 | Telangana grievance
  [263:6:2.7] 2001–04 | Girglani Commission | Telangana grievance
[263:6:3] - Chronology PYQ: Gentlemen's Agreement (1956) → Jai Telangana (1969) → Jai Andhra (1972) → 32nd Amendment (1973) → GO 610 (1985) → Girglani Report (2004).
[263:6:4] # Causes of 1969
[263:6:5] - Failure to implement the **Gentlemen's Agreement**; feeling that **Telangana surpluses** were diverted; **competition for jobs** from Andhras – non-locals recruited, local posts reclassified.
[263:6:6] - Flashpoint **1968**: regional sub-committee extended five years.
[263:6:7] - **Not a 1969 cause:** the AP High Court view that the Mulki Rules had lapsed came in **1970** (reversed by the SC in 1972).
[263:6:8] # Course and TPS
[263:6:9] - Began as a **student strike at Khammam, January 1969**; **first hunger strike** – student **Ravindranath**; police firings killed **over 360** – Telangana martyrs.
[263:6:10] - **Prof. K. Jayashankar** (former VC, **Kakatiya University**) – ideologue.
[263:6:11] - **Telangana Praja Samithi** – formed **February 1969**; convener **A. Madan Mohan**; president **Marri Chenna Reddy**; symbol spade.
[263:6:12] - **1971 Lok Sabha election**: won **10 of 14** seats; **merged with Congress (Sept 1971)** after agreement with Indira Gandhi; opponents formed the **Complete TPS** under **B. Satyanarayana Reddy**.
[263:6:13] - CM in 1969: **Kasu Brahmananda Reddy** (resigned 1971); **P.V. Narasimha Rao** sworn in **30 Sep 1971**.
[263:6:14] # Eight-Point Formula and court verdicts
[263:6:15] - **Eight-Point Formula** – **11 April 1969**, PM **Indira Gandhi**: committee under a Supreme Court judge, surplus funds, supervision of Telangana schemes, PM's **six-monthly review**.

## SUB 3 | Tribe – Definition, Law and Social Organisation
[272:3:0] - **Marshall Sahlins:** tribe = **"segmental organisation"** of equal multi-family groups.
[272:3:1] - **Article 342:** **President** notifies STs after consulting the **Governor**; only **Parliament** amends the list (**342(2)**).
[272:3:2] - **Census 2011:** ST = **8.6%** of India; **Bhil** largest tribe, then Gond; **Madhya Pradesh** has the largest tribal population (not Odisha).
[272:3:3] # The Chenchus
[272:3:4] - **Endogamous tribe**, **exogamous clans** (kulam/gothram); no clan hierarchy; **patriarchal, patrilineal**.
