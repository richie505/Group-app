=== NOTES 2:30 | Parliament – Composition of Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha, Qualifications, Terms, Special Powers of Rajya Sabha
## SUB 0 | Parliament – Article 79 and the President's Place
[30:0:0] - **Article 79**: Parliament = **President + Council of States (Rajya Sabha) + House of the People (Lok Sabha)**. Parliament is in **Part V, Articles 79–122**.
[30:0:1] - President is a **part of Parliament** because **no Bill becomes law without his assent** (Art 111) – like the **Crown-in-Parliament** (UK), unlike the US President, who is separate from Congress.
[30:0:2] - But **Article 59(1)**: the President is **not a member** of either House or a State Legislature; an MP/MLA elected President gives up his seat. He does not sit or take part in discussions.
[30:0:3] - President's legislative powers: summons and prorogues Parliament, dissolves the LS; **Art 86** – may address either or both Houses; **Art 87** – **special address** at the first session after each general election and the first session of each year; assent – **absolute, suspensive (return once, not a Money Bill), pocket veto**; nominates **12** to RS; **ordinances (Art 123)** when Parliament is not in session.
[30:0:4] Assertion-Reason: "Parliament consists of the President and two Houses" – true; the President's opening address is a supporting reason.

## SUB 5 | Terms of the Houses (article 83)
[30:5:0] # Lok Sabha – Art 83(2)
[30:5:1] - **5 years from its first meeting**; may be **dissolved earlier** by the President on the PM's advice – cannot be challenged in court.
[30:5:2] - **National Emergency**: extended by law **1 year at a time**, not beyond **6 months after** the Emergency ends.
[30:5:3] - **5th LS** (15 Mar 1971 – 18 Jan 1977, about 6 years) – extended during the 1975–77 Emergency.
[30:5:4] - Dissolved early: **4th (1970), 6th (1979), 9th (1991), 11th (1997), 12th (1999), 13th (2004)**.
[30:5:5] - **9th LS**: 2 Dec 1989 – 13 Mar 1991. **12th LS**: elections Feb–Mar 1998, constituted 10 Mar 1998, dissolved 26 Apr 1999. **13th LS**: 10 Oct 1999 – 6 Feb 2004.
[30:5:6] # Rajya Sabha – Art 83(1)
[30:5:7] - **Permanent House**, never dissolved; about **one-third retire every second year**.
[30:5:8] - Member's **6-year term** fixed by the **RPA, 1951**, not the Constitution; casual vacancy filled for the **rest of the term**.
[30:5:9] - First RS (1952): members to retire first chosen by **lottery**.
[30:5:10] - **First Lok Sabha:** duly constituted on **17 April 1952** after the first general elections (25 Oct 1951 – 21 Feb 1952); its first sitting was on **13 May 1952**.
## SUB 6 | Qualifications and Minimum Ages (article 84)
[30:6:0] - **Article 84**: citizen; oath before an **EC-authorised person** (Third Schedule form); **25 years (LS)**, **30 years (RS)**; other conditions by Parliament.
[30:6:1] - **RPA, 1951**: must be an **elector in a parliamentary constituency**; SC/ST to contest a reserved seat.
[30:6:2] - **No educational qualification**.
[30:6:3] - RS **domicile** (voter in the same State) required only before **6 Apr 2003**; removal upheld in **Kuldip Nayar v. UoI (2006)** with **open ballot**.

## SUB 7 | Disqualification and Vacation of Seats
[30:7:0] # Article 102 and Article 103
[30:7:1] - Grounds: **office of profit** (Union/State, exempted posts apart), **unsound mind** (court-declared), **undischarged insolvent**, **loss of citizenship**, a **law of Parliament** (RPA 1951) – **not lack of a degree**.
[30:7:2] - **Article 103**: **President** decides, on the **Election Commission's opinion** (binding); decision final.
[30:7:3] - **Art 101(3)(a):** seat becomes vacant **immediately** on incurring an Art 102 disqualification – no House decision needed.
[30:7:4] - "Office of profit" is **not defined** in the Constitution or RPA; exemptions by **Parliament (Prevention of Disqualification) Act, 1959** – amended **five times** (1993, 1999, 2000, 2006, 2013).
[30:7:5] # Tenth Schedule (defection)
[30:7:6] - Decided by the **Speaker (LS) / Chairman (RS)**, not the President; **subject to judicial review** – **Kihoto Hollohan (1992)**.
[30:7:7] - Grounds: giving up party membership; voting/abstaining against the whip unless **condoned within 15 days**; independent joining a party; nominated member joining after **6 months**. The term "**political party**" is in the Tenth Schedule.
[30:7:8] # Vacation – Article 101

[30:7:10] - Resignation in writing to Speaker / Chairman; **33rd Amendment, 1974** (Art 101(3)(b)) – may be rejected if **not voluntary or genuine**.

## SUB 9 | Sessions and Procedure
[30:9:0] - **Article 85**: President summons each House; **maximum six months** between sessions → LS meets **at least twice a year**; no minimum sitting days. Usually three sessions: **Budget, Monsoon, Winter** (no fixed calendar).
[30:9:1] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [30:9:1.0] Sitting | Daily meeting during a session
  [30:9:1.1] Adjournment | Ends a sitting for a set time – Speaker/Chairman
  [30:9:1.2] Adjournment sine die | Ends a sitting without a date – Speaker/Chairman
  [30:9:1.3] Prorogation (85(2)) | President ends the session, not the House
  [30:9:1.4] Recess | Gap between prorogation and new session – ordinances possible
  [30:9:1.5] Dissolution | Ends the House – Lok Sabha only
[30:9:2] - Sequence: adjournment sine die → prorogation → recess → new session.
[30:9:3] - **Quorum (Art 100(3))**: **one-tenth** of total membership – an APPSC key gave 15%.
[30:9:4] - **Art 118**: each House makes its rules. **Art 120(1)**: business in **Hindi or English**; mother tongue with presiding officer's permission.
[30:9:5] - Secretariats independent: LS under the **Speaker**, RS under the **Chairman**.
[30:9:6] - **2026**: Monsoon Session 20 Jul – sine die 13 Aug; productivity **LS 19%, RS 39%**; FCRA (Amendment) Bill, 2026 sent to a JPC. **11 Mar 2026**: resolution under **Art 94(c)** to remove Speaker **Om Birla** rejected by voice vote.
