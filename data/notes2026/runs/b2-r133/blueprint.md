## 5. Key corrections
- Unkeyed → keyed: 331 is the non-SC/ST provision; reservation in proportion to population; "untouchability" is defined nowhere ("None").
- Added options: Art. 243D panchayats is also correct ("1, 2, 3 and 4 only"); Parliament's power to change the SC list is **Art. 341(2)**, not 341(1).
- PoA private-defence plea: s.6 applies IPC Chapter IV, so all pleas are open → "None of the above" (was "private defence").
- Brahmo Samaj A-R: both true, R does not explain A (was "R explains A").
