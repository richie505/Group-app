## SUB 0 | Make in India – Launch, Pillars and Sectors
[0:0] # Launch
[0:1] - **Launched:** **25 September 2014** by PM Modi (MPPSC 2020; CDS 2019 – not 2018, not 2012/2016); nodal **Ministry of Commerce and Industry (DPIIT)**, not Heavy Industries or S&T (SSC 2019).
[0:2] - **Aim:** encourage multinational and domestic companies to manufacture in India; make India a **global manufacturing hub** and **foster innovation**; focus on job creation and skills in **25 key sectors**.
[0:3] - **Logo:** a **lion** made of cogwheels (UPPSC RO 2014). Links: "Vocal for Local", "Make in India for the World".
[0:4] - **Central approach:** **attracting FDI and global firms** to manufacture in India; **streamlining regulatory processes** to attract foreign investment and foster innovation – not higher tariffs, subsidies to old sectors or centralised planning (APPSC ASO).
[0:5] - **Targets (via NMP 2011):** manufacturing **25% of GDP** and **100 million additional jobs** – by 2022, later restated **by 2025** (APPSC JA: target for 2025 = **25%**).
[0:6] # Four Pillars
[0:7] TABLE head: Pillar | Covers
  [0:7.0] New Processes | Ease of doing business, single window, e-Biz, faster IPR registration
  [0:7.1] New Infrastructure | Industrial corridors and smart cities, world-class infrastructure
  [0:7.2] New Sectors | FDI opened in defence, insurance, medical devices, construction, railway infrastructure
  [0:7.3] New Mindset | Government as facilitator, not regulator
[0:8] - The initiative "built on four pillars – New Processes, New Infrastructure, New Sectors, New Mindset" and integrating India into global supply chains = **Make in India** (not Start-up, Digital or Skill India).
[0:9] - **Strategic components:** skill development, world-class infrastructure, manufacturing hub for domestic and foreign firms – **liberal finance to rural women for new enterprises is NOT one** (APPSC JA).
[0:10] - **Not part of Make in India (Degree Lecturers 2017 key):** "facilitating easy exit for investors" – EoDB, 100% FDI in new sectors such as defence, and corridors/smart cities are parts. (Faster exit norms belong to the Start-up India action plan.)
[0:11] # Sectors
[0:12] TABLE head: Phase | Sectors
  [0:12.0] Original (2014) | 25 sectors (APPSC AEE 2016)
  [0:12.1] Make in India 2.0 – Economic Survey 2017-18 | 10 champion sectors for renewed focus (APPSC AMVI flashcard: 10)
  [0:12.2] Champion services sectors (Cabinet, Feb 2018) | 12
  [0:12.3] Make in India 2.0 (now) | 27 = 15 manufacturing (DPIIT) + 12 services (Dept of Commerce)
[0:13] - Champion sectors include capital goods, auto, defence and aerospace, pharma, ESDM, leather, textiles, food processing, renewable energy, shipping, railways.
[0:14] - **Public Procurement (Preference to Make in India) Order:** preference to local suppliers in government purchase.
[0:15] > Most-asked: launch date (Sept 2014), 25 sectors, four pillars, lion logo, nodal ministry. Trap: "10" champion sectors (ES 2017-18) vs 12 champion services vs 27 sectors now.

## SUB 1 | National Manufacturing Policy 2011 and NIMZs
[1:0] # NMP 2011
[1:1] TABLE head: Item | Fact
  [1:1.0] Released | 4 November 2011 (approved by Cabinet 25 Oct 2011) – "introduced in 2011"
  [1:1.1] Share of manufacturing in GDP | 16% → 25% within a decade, i.e. by 2022
  [1:1.2] Growth target | 12-14% per annum over the medium term
  [1:1.3] Jobs | 100 million additional jobs (by 2022)
  [1:1.4] Competitiveness | enhance global competitiveness, domestic value addition and technological depth
  [1:1.5] Infrastructure | on PPP basis; skilling of rural youth; sector-, location- and technology-neutral
[1:2] - **Objectives (RAS 2013):** 12-14% growth, 100 million jobs, global competitiveness = **1, 2 and 3**; "manufacturing exports to rise 25% a year" is NOT an objective – 25% is the GDP-share target.
[1:3] - **Present share:** manufacturing still about **16.3-17.5%** of GVA → **25% target missed**.
[1:4] - **National Manufacturing Mission (Budget 2025-26):** 25% share and **143 million jobs by 2035**.
[1:5] # NIMZ – National Investment and Manufacturing Zones
[1:6] - **NIMZ** = large **integrated industrial township** – zoned land use, clean and energy-efficient technology, social infrastructure, skill facilities; the main instrument of NMP 2011.
[1:7] - NIMZs differ from SEZs in **size, infrastructure planning, governance, exit policy and fiscal incentives**.
[1:8] - **India's first NIMZ was proposed in Andhra Pradesh** (UPSC 2016). In-principle approval for **Chittoor and Prakasam** (14 Aug 2013); **Prakasam** got final approval (2016).
[1:9] # National Capital Goods Policy 2016
[1:10] - Aims to **raise domestic capital goods production**, deepen technology across sub-sectors, a **technology development fund** – **increasing imports of capital goods is NOT a feature** (OPSC 2018).
[1:11] > NMP year asked 4 times (2011; 4 Nov 2011); target year 2022; NIMZ = AP (Chittoor and Prakasam).
[1:12] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 22 p.8 (NIMZ); NextGen Rocket Indian Economy p.98-99 (NIMZ = instrument of NMP 2011); Ramesh Singh part 05 p.25 (25% of GDP, 100 million jobs by 2022); app notes 3:66 sub 0-1

## SUB 3 | Ease of Doing Business – World Bank Report and Reforms
[3:0] - **Doing Business report:** **World Bank Group** (conceptualised with **IFC**); mainly **guides foreign investors**; **10 parameters** – starting a business, construction permits, getting electricity, registering property, getting credit, protecting minority investors, paying taxes, trading across borders, enforcing contracts, resolving insolvency.
[3:1] TABLE head: Edition | India's rank
  [3:1.0] DB 2015 | 142
  [3:1.1] DB 2018 | 100 – jump of 30
  [3:1.2] DB 2020 | 63 (last edition)
[3:2] - **Discontinued 2021** (data irregularities). Successor **B-READY** – 3 pillars: regulatory framework, public services, operational efficiency.
[3:3] - Weakest indicators: **enforcing contracts, registering property**.
[3:4] TABLE head: Reform | EoDB area
  [3:4.0] GST (2017), faceless assessment, e-filing | Paying taxes, unified market
  [3:4.1] SPICe+ (2020), MCA21 V3 | Starting a business
  [3:4.2] IBC 2016 | Resolving insolvency
  [3:4.3] Online building-plan approvals; land records (DILRMP) | Construction permits; registering property
[3:5] - **GST** is the reform credited with improving EoDB (APPSC G2) – "removal of all foreign ownership restrictions", nationalisation of banks and PSU expansion are NOT EoDB reforms.
[3:6] - **Platforms:** **e-Biz** (Mission Mode Project under NeGP); **National Single Window System** (FIF portal merged Aug 2022); **Invest India** (2009, not-for-profit JV of DIPP, FICCI, CII, NASSCOM and States).
[3:7] - **Jan Vishwas 2.0 Bill:** 16 Acts, 355 provisions, **288 decriminalised**. Four **labour codes** → formalisation and consolidation, yet **80%+** workers informal. IMD Competitiveness rank **41** (2025).
[3:8] - **Industrial-development initiatives:** Make in India, Ease of Doing Business, Start-up India – **Digital India is NOT one** (digital infrastructure and services).
[3:9] > Statement PYQ (APPSC ASO): Make in India = manufacturing hub ✓; Skill India = employability ✓; "success depends solely on the Centre" ✗ → 1 and 3.

[5:0] - **PLI (2020):** **14 sectors**, **₹1.97 lakh crore**; incentive on **incremental sales** of goods made in India; sectors include white goods, solar PV modules, specialty steel, textiles, drones – **NOT coal mining equipment**.
[5:1] - Results (to Sept 2025): investment >₹2.0 lakh crore; **12.60 lakh jobs**.
[5:2] - **PLI-Auto:** **₹25,938 crore**; **≥50% domestic value addition**.

## SUB 7 | SEZ Basics for this Row
[7:0] - **SEZ Act, 2005** with SEZ Rules in force **10 February 2006**; set up by **Central Govt, State Govt or any person**.
[7:1] - **Sec 10AA:** **100%** tax exemption on export income for 5 years, **50%** next 5, 50% of ploughed-back profit for 5 more; **sunset** – units operational by **30 June 2020**.
[7:2] - **DESH** = Development of Enterprise and Service Hubs (proposed successor framework).
