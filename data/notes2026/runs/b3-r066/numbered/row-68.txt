## SUB 0 | EPZs and Free Trade Zones – Kandla to Surat
[0:0] - **SEZ = Special Economic Zone** (not Special Export / Exemption / Environment Zone) – asked 3 times.
[0:1] - **Kandla** (Gulf of Kutch, Gujarat), **1965**: **India's and Asia's first Export Processing Zone**; also India's first Free Trade Zone; later Kandla SEZ.
[0:2] - **Surat**, **1997**: **first private-sector EPZ**; converted into an SEZ on **1 November 2000**.
[0:3] - **Free trade zone:** area where goods are landed, stored, processed and re-exported **without customs duty**; set up for **promotion of export industries** (not backward areas, SSI or IT).
[0:4] TABLE head: EPZ | Place
  [0:4.0] Kandla | Gujarat (1965)
  [0:4.1] SEEPZ | Santacruz, Mumbai
  [0:4.2] MEPZ | Chennai
  [0:4.3] Falta | West Bengal
  [0:4.4] Others | Noida, Cochin, Visakhapatnam
[0:5] - **Thiruvananthapuram had no FTZ** (Kerala's zone was at Cochin) – UPPSC 1999.
[0:6] - **"City without an SEZ" (UPPSC 2001 key): Kandla** – listed as an EPZ, not an SEZ, then; Chennai, Kochi, Surat had SEZs. From 2006 EPZs and EOUs could convert into SEZs.
[0:7] - **Area-based incentive:** in 2001 PM Vajpayee announced a **5-year excise duty holiday** for industries in **earthquake-ravaged Kutch** (UPSC 2002).
[0:8] > Kandla is asked 7 times (Asia's first EPZ, 1965, Gulf of Kutch). Trap: Santacruz = SEEPZ, not first; Surat = first private EPZ.

## SUB 2 | SEZ Act 2005 – Objectives and Features
[2:0] # Objectives
[2:1] - 1. Generation of **additional economic activity**.
[2:2] - 2. Promotion of **exports of goods and services**.
[2:3] - 3. Promotion of **investment from domestic and foreign sources**.
[2:4] - 4. Creation of **employment** opportunities.
[2:5] - 5. Development of **infrastructure** facilities.
[2:6] - So "all the above" (economic activity, employment, exports); infrastructure + foreign investment = **1 and 2 only** – "exports of **services only**" is NOT an objective (UPSC 2010); "**discouraging** foreign investment" is NOT an objective.
[2:7] # Features
[2:8] - A **duty-free enclave** for exports and FDI; developer: **Central Government, State Government or any person** (public, private, joint sector).
[2:9] - Purpose: manufacturing, **services** or Free Trade Warehousing Zone; proposal via **State Government → Board of Approval**; **single-window clearance**.
[2:10] - **100% FDI** under the automatic route for most manufacturing in SEZs.
[2:11] - Min. area (Rules 2006): multi-product **1,000 ha**; sector-specific reduced to **50 ha** (2019).
[2:12] - **Not true:** "SEZ is set up **primarily** to generate employment" – SEZs are **export-oriented** (APPSC G2 2017).
[2:13] - **A-R (Constable 2016 key):** SEZs attract foreign companies and give more local employment – both true, (R) taken as the explanation.
[2:14] - **s.18:** one IFSC per SEZ. **s.10AA:** **100%** exemption 5 years, 50% next 5, 50% of reinvested profit 5 more; holidays **phased out 2017-2020** (sunset clause).
[2:15] - **Japan** came forward to develop SEZs / Japanese industrial townships in India (with DMIC).
[2:16] ~~ Sources: NextGen Rocket Indian Economy p.26 (objectives: exports, investment, employment, infrastructure, economic activity); app notes 3:68 sub 2
## SUB 3 | SEZ Performance and Criticism
[3:0] - Total investment in SEZs by **31 Dec 2011**: **₹2,49,630 crore** (Economic Survey 2011-12).
[3:1] - **Highest export growth over the previous year (2004-05 to 2007-08): 2007-08** (₹66,638 crore, about +92%).
[3:2] - FY 2024-25 SEZ exports **₹14.64 lakh crore**, about **21%** of India's exports; SEZs got **less than 3% of FDI**.
[3:3] TABLE head: State with most SEZs | Fact
  [3:3.0] Andhra Pradesh (combined) | most approved and operational SEZs (June 2012)
  [3:3.1] Tamil Nadu | most operational SEZs now
  [3:3.2] Telangana | most approved SEZs now
[3:4] - **Nandigram** (Purba Medinipur, West Bengal), **2007**: **Salim Group** chemical-hub SEZ; violent protests against **land acquisition**.
[3:5] - Criticism: revenue loss, idle land, **WTO-inconsistent export subsidies**; employment gains not guaranteed.
[3:6] - **Baba Kalyani Committee (2018):** rename SEZs as **Employment and Economic Enclaves** (3Es).
[3:7] - **DESH Bill** (Development of Enterprise and Services Hubs, **Budget 2022-23**): removes positive NFE condition; still pending.
[3:8] - **BHAVYA – Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojana (2026):** ₹33,660 crore; **100** plug-and-play parks (not 50).
