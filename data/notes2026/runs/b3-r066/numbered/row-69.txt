## SUB 2 | National Monetisation Pipeline (NMP)
[2:0] - Announced in **Union Budget 2021-22**; monetises **brownfield core infrastructure assets**; **ownership retained by Government**.
[2:1] - **Strategic objective:** lets public asset owners **use new financial structures with private-sector capital** – unlocking value of public (not private) assets; leases are for a fixed period.
[2:2] - Instruments: PPP concessions, InvITs, TOT, securitisation.
[2:3] TABLE head: Item | NMP 1.0 | NMP 2.0
  [2:3.0] Period | FY22-FY25 | FY26-FY30
  [2:3.1] Value | ₹6 lakh crore | ₹16.72 lakh crore (prepared by NITI Aayog)
  [2:3.2] Largest sector | Roads (APPSC 2021) | Highways, MMLPs, ropeways 26%
  [2:3.3] Result | ₹5.3 lakh crore; coal gave most proceeds | launched 23 Feb 2026

## SUB 3 | Bharatmala, Sagarmala, Railways and Logistics
[3:0] TABLE head: Feature | Bharatmala Pariyojana | Sagarmala
  [3:0.0] Sector | Roads | Port-led development (ports)
  [3:0.1] Launch | 2017 | National Perspective Plan 2016; Sagarmala 2.0 (2025)
  [3:0.2] Scale | Phase-I 34,800 km, 580+ districts | 800+ projects
[3:1] - **Sagarmala pillars:** port modernisation, port connectivity, port-led industrialisation, coastal community development, coastal shipping and IWT – not naval stationing, not agricultural insurance.
[3:2] - **Railway electrification:** **50.91%** of routes by **March 2019**; **99.1%** by Oct 2025. Targets: **100% electrification by 2023** and **net-zero carbon by 2030** – both statements correct.
[3:3] - **National Rail Plan 2030:** freight share 28% → **45%**. **Eastern DFC** (1,337 km) – **World Bank**; **Western DFC** – **JICA**.
[3:4] - **Mumbai-Ahmedabad bullet train:** JICA loan of about ₹88,000 crore at **0.1%** a year, 50 years, 15-year moratorium.
[3:5] - **National Logistics Policy:** logistics cost to single digits by **2030**; **7.97%** of GDP (FY24); **ULIP** = Unified Logistics Interface Platform; **LEADS** (DPIIT, 2018) modelled on the World Bank's **Logistics Performance Index**; AP = **Achiever** (LEADS 2023); Exemplars 2025 include **Tamil Nadu**.
[3:6] - **Z-Morh tunnel** (6.5 km, J&K): concession agreement Jan 2020, about **₹2,379 crore**.

## SUB 4 | Infrastructure Financing and PPP
[4:0] - **PPP = Public Private Partnership**.
[4:1] - **IIFCL** (2006; NBFC-IFC since 2013) → SEBI **InvIT** regulations (2014) → **NIIF** (2015; Category II AIFs) → **NaBFID Act 2021** (paid-up ₹20,000 crore).
[4:2] - **Deepak Parekh Committee:** infrastructure financing (May 2007). **Vijay Kelkar Committee (2015):** revisiting and revitalising PPP; proposed an **Infrastructure PPP Adjudication Tribunal**.
[4:3] - **UK-India Infrastructure Financing Bridge:** launched at the **12th India-UK Economic and Financial Dialogue** (11 Sept 2023).
[4:4] - **Cochin airport (CIAL):** first PPP airport; world's first fully solar-powered airport (2015). **Vizhinjam (Kerala):** first deep-water transhipment port. Navi Mumbai airport: operations **25 Dec 2025**.

[5:0] - AP coastline **~1,053 km** (1,053.07 km) – **3rd** longest; **AP Maritime Board** handles ports.
