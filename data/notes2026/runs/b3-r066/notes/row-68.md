=== EPZs and Free Trade Zones – Kandla to Surat
- **SEZ = Special Economic Zone** (not Special Export / Exemption / Environment Zone) – asked 3 times.
- **Kandla** (Gulf of Kutch, Gujarat), **1965**: **India's and Asia's first Export Processing Zone**; also India's first Free Trade Zone; later Kandla SEZ.
- **Surat**, **1997**: **first private-sector EPZ**; converted into an SEZ on **1 November 2000**.
- **Free trade zone:** area where goods are landed, stored, processed and re-exported **without customs duty**; set up for **promotion of export industries** (not backward areas, SSI or IT).

| EPZ | Place |
|---|---|
| Kandla | Gujarat (1965) |
| SEEPZ | Santacruz, **Mumbai** |
| MEPZ | Chennai |
| Falta | **West Bengal** |
| Others | Noida, **Cochin**, **Visakhapatnam** |
- **Thiruvananthapuram had no FTZ** (Kerala's zone was at Cochin) – UPPSC 1999.
- **"City without an SEZ" (UPPSC 2001 key): Kandla** – listed as an EPZ, not an SEZ, then; Chennai, Kochi, Surat had SEZs. From 2006 EPZs and EOUs could convert into SEZs.
- **Area-based incentive:** in 2001 PM Vajpayee announced a **5-year excise duty holiday** for industries in **earthquake-ravaged Kutch** (UPSC 2002).
> Kandla is asked 7 times (Asia's first EPZ, 1965, Gulf of Kutch). Trap: Santacruz = SEEPZ, not first; Surat = first private EPZ.

=== SEZ Act 2005 – Objectives and Features
# Objectives
- 1. Generation of **additional economic activity**.
- 2. Promotion of **exports of goods and services**.
- 3. Promotion of **investment from domestic and foreign sources**.
- 4. Creation of **employment** opportunities.
- 5. Development of **infrastructure** facilities.
- So "all the above" (economic activity, employment, exports); infrastructure + foreign investment = **1 and 2 only** – "exports of **services only**" is NOT an objective (UPSC 2010); "**discouraging** foreign investment" is NOT an objective.
# Features
- A **duty-free enclave** for exports and FDI; developer: **Central Government, State Government or any person** (public, private, joint sector).
- Purpose: manufacturing, **services** or Free Trade Warehousing Zone; proposal via **State Government → Board of Approval**; **single-window clearance**.
- **100% FDI** under the automatic route for most manufacturing in SEZs.
- Min. area (Rules 2006): multi-product **1,000 ha**; sector-specific reduced to **50 ha** (2019).
- **Not true:** "SEZ is set up **primarily** to generate employment" – SEZs are **export-oriented** (APPSC G2 2017).
- **A-R (Constable 2016 key):** SEZs attract foreign companies and give more local employment – both true, (R) taken as the explanation.
- **s.18:** one IFSC per SEZ. **s.10AA:** **100%** exemption 5 years, 50% next 5, 50% of reinvested profit 5 more; holidays **phased out 2017-2020** (sunset clause).
- **Japan** came forward to develop SEZs / Japanese industrial townships in India (with DMIC).

=== SEZ Performance and Criticism
- Total investment in SEZs by **31 Dec 2011**: **₹2,49,630 crore** (Economic Survey 2011-12).
- **Highest export growth over the previous year (2004-05 to 2007-08): 2007-08** (₹66,638 crore, about +92%).
- FY 2024-25 SEZ exports **₹14.64 lakh crore**, about **21%** of India's exports; SEZs got **less than 3% of FDI**.

| State with most SEZs | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Andhra Pradesh (combined)** | most approved and operational SEZs (**June 2012**) |
| Tamil Nadu | most **operational** SEZs now |
| Telangana | most **approved** SEZs now |
- **Nandigram** (Purba Medinipur, West Bengal), **2007**: **Salim Group** chemical-hub SEZ; violent protests against **land acquisition**.
- Criticism: revenue loss, idle land, **WTO-inconsistent export subsidies**; employment gains not guaranteed.
- **Baba Kalyani Committee (2018):** rename SEZs as **Employment and Economic Enclaves** (3Es).
- **DESH Bill** (Development of Enterprise and Services Hubs, **Budget 2022-23**): removes positive NFE condition; still pending.
- **BHAVYA – Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojana (2026):** ₹33,660 crore; **100** plug-and-play parks (not 50).
