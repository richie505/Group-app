=== Start-up India – Launch, Bodies and Programmes
# Launch
- Launched **16 January 2016** (SSC CGL 2019) – to support entrepreneurs, build a start-up ecosystem and make India a country of **job creators instead of job seekers**; **16 January = National Startup Day**.
- Nodal: **DPIIT**, Ministry of Commerce and Industry – also the **nodal agency for start-up recognition**. (Udyam portal = MSME registration.)
- **Start-up and Stand-up India** (slogan of Jan 2016) aim to promote innovation and entrepreneurship among **enterprising youth** – Stand-Up targets SC/ST and women.
- **Action plan (ES 2015-16):** self-certification, **Startup India Hub**, mobile app and portal, relaxed public procurement, fast-track patents, **faster exit norms**, ₹10,000 crore fund of funds, credit guarantee, 3-year tax exemption.

=== Stand-Up India Scheme (2016)
| Feature | Fact |
|---|---|
| Launch | **5 April 2016** |
| Ministry | **Ministry of Finance**; portal **Standupmitra** |
| Target groups | **SC/ST and women** entrepreneurs aged 18+ |
| Loan | **₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore** (composite: term loan + working capital) |
| Enterprise | **Greenfield** (first-time) only – manufacturing, services, trading, agri-allied; **NOT brownfield** |
| Branch norm | at least **one SC/ST and one woman** borrower per bank branch |
| Refinance | **SIDBI** ₹10,000 crore |
- **Non-individual enterprises** eligible if at least **51% shareholding and controlling stake** is with an SC/ST or woman entrepreneur.
- Loan up to **85%** of project cost; repayment up to **7 years**, moratorium up to **18 months**.
- **Working capital up to ₹10 lakh** = **overdraft** (RuPay debit card); above ₹10 lakh = cash credit limit.
- **Security:** primary security plus collateral or guarantee of the **Credit Guarantee Fund Scheme for Stand-Up India Loans (CGFSIL)**, as decided by banks.
- **PM MUDRA:** micro loans up to **₹20 lakh** (Tarun Plus).
> APPSC G1 2024 statements: brownfield ✗; non-individual ✓; working capital ≤₹10 lakh as cash credit ✗ (it is overdraft); CGFSIL ✓ → **II and IV**.

=== Funds and Financing Sources for Start-ups
| Fund | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)** | ₹945 crore (2021); up to **₹20 lakh** grant for **proof of concept**, prototypes, trials + ₹50 lakh investment; given **through incubators**, not banks |
| **Fund of Funds for Startups (FFS)** | **₹10,000 crore**, managed by **SIDBI**; contributes to **SEBI-registered AIFs** (VC funds), not directly |
| FFS 2.0 | another ₹10,000 crore for **deep tech, space, AI** |
| **Credit Guarantee Scheme for Start-ups** | Oct 2022; via **NCGTC**; up to **₹20 crore** per borrower; replaced the Credit Guarantee Fund for Startups |

| Source | Feature |
|---|---|
| Bootstrapping | founder's own savings and revenue |
| **Angel investors** | individuals investing own money at idea/seed stage |
| **Venture capital** | early-stage equity with active guidance for high-growth firms |
| **Crowdfunding** | small sums from many people, usually online |
| Incubators, bank loans, government schemes | MUDRA, Stand-Up India, CGSS |
- Start-ups' needs can be met through angel investors, venture capital and crowdfunding – **all the above** (UPPSC 2017).
- **Big deal:** **Walmart** bought a majority stake in **Flipkart** (2018). **Microsoft** launched the **100X100X100** programme for B2B SaaS start-ups.

=== Start-up Definition and Benefits
| Criterion | Start-up | Deep-tech |
|---|---|---|
| Age | up to **10 years** | up to **20 years** |
| Turnover | ≤ **₹200 crore** | ≤ **₹300 crore** |
- Eligible forms: private limited company, **registered partnership firm**, LLP; split or reconstructed businesses are NOT start-ups.
- Tax holiday **3 consecutive years out of 10** (Sec **80-IAC**); **self-certification** under labour and environment laws; fast-track patents; **angel tax abolished** (Budget **2024-25**).

# PM Vishwakarma
- Launched **17 September 2023**; **5 years, FY 2023-24 to FY 2027-28**; strengthens the **Guru-Shishya parampara** of family-based traditional skills.
- Covers **18 traditional trades** (not 21); collateral-free credit **₹1 lakh (1st tranche) + ₹2 lakh (2nd)** = up to **₹3 lakh** at **5%** concessional interest.
# Theory and State schemes
- **McClelland's achievement-motivation (n-Ach) theory** – set out in *The Achieving Society* (**1961**) at Harvard (The Achievement Motive, 1953).
- **Yashaswini** – **Goa** (2021): interest-free loans to women SHGs for enterprise (Karnataka's Yeshasvini is a health-insurance scheme).
- **Global Innovation Index** (WIPO): **India 38th (2025)**; Switzerland 1st.

=== Ecosystem Data and Unicorns
- India = **3rd-largest start-up ecosystem**; DPIIT-recognised start-ups **2.47 lakh+** (Aug 2026).
- **Unicorn** = privately held start-up valued at **US$1 billion+**.
- **Hurun Global Unicorn Index 2026:** India **4th with 61**; **Bengaluru** leads Indian cities.
