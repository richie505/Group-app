## SUB 1 | National Manufacturing Policy 2011 and NIMZs
[66:1:0] - Infrastructure on **PPP basis**; **skilling of rural youth**; sector-, location- and technology-neutral.
[66:1:1] - **NIMZ** (National Investment and Manufacturing Zone): large **integrated industrial township** – zoned land use, clean technology, social infrastructure; instrument of NMP 2011.
[66:1:2] - NIMZs differ from SEZs in size, infrastructure planning, governance, exit policy and incentives.
[66:1:3] - **India's first NIMZ was proposed in Andhra Pradesh.** In-principle approval for **Chittoor and Prakasam** (14 Aug 2013); **Prakasam** got final approval (2016); TEFS report submitted (2025-26).
[66:1:4] - Industrial corridors: DMIC, CBIC, BMEC, AKIC.

## SUB 4 | Pli and Sectoral Schemes
[66:4:0] # PLI Scheme (2020)
[66:4:1] - **14 sectors, ₹1.97 lakh crore**; incentive on **incremental sales** of goods made in India (electronics **4-6%**); backs sunrise industries.
[66:4:2] - Sectors include **white goods, solar PV modules, specialty steel**, textiles (MMF, technical), drones – NOT coal mining equipment.
[66:4:3] - Results (to Sept 2025): investment >₹2.0 lakh crore; incremental production >₹18.70 lakh crore; **12.60 lakh jobs**.
[66:4:4] - **PLI-Auto:** ₹25,938 crore; **≥50% domestic value addition**.
[66:4:5] # PM E-DRIVE and Electronics Schemes
[66:4:6] - **PM E-DRIVE:** **Ministry of Heavy Industries**, **29 Sept 2024**, **₹10,900 crore**; replaced **FAME-II** (₹11,500 crore, 2019-24); subsumed EMPS-2024; e-2W/3W support till 31 Mar 2026, others till 31 Mar 2028.
[66:4:7] - **Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme (FY27-FY31):** ₹62,500 crore; replaces PLI-LSEM; **2.25-5%** on eligible sales; up to **1.5%** extra for domestic sourcing; **3%** extra for design and R&D by Indian brands.
[66:4:8] - **ECMS:** outlay ₹22,919 crore → ₹40,000 crore (Budget 2026-27).
## SUB 5 | Electronics and Semiconductors

[66:5:2] - Mobile output ₹18,900 cr (2014-15) → **₹5.45 lakh cr (2024-25)**; electronics ₹1.90 → ₹11.32 lakh cr; **99.2%** of phones used are made in India; **smartphones = largest export category (2025)**.

## SUB 6 | SEZs and Zones
[66:6:0] - **SEZ Act, 2005** with SEZ Rules – in force **10 February 2006**; SEZ Policy 1 April 2000.
[66:6:1] - Who can set up: **Central Govt, State Govt, or any person** (private, joint or foreign developer).
[66:6:2] - **Sec 10AA:** **100%** tax exemption on export income for **5 years**, **50%** next 5, 50% of ploughed-back profit for a further 5.
[66:6:3] - **Sunset clause:** only units operational by **30 June 2020** get 10AA.
[66:6:4] - **DESH** (Development of Enterprise and Service Hubs, Jun 2026): no compulsory positive NFE; **DTA sales allowed**; WTO-compliant.
## SUB 7 | Atmanirbhar Bharat and Logistics
[66:7:0] - **Five pillars:** **Economy, Infrastructure, System, Vibrant Demography, Demand** (NOT privatisation).
[66:7:1] - **May 2020 package:** about ₹20 lakh crore (~10% of GDP).
[66:7:2] - **PM GatiShakti (Oct 2021)**, **National Logistics Policy (Sept 2022)** – cut logistics cost (historically 13-14% of GDP).
[66:7:3] - **"China+1":** India as an added manufacturing base in global supply chains.
## SUB 8 | World Bank Doing Business and B-ready
[66:8:0] - Published by the **World Bank Group** (conceptualised with **IFC**); mainly **guides foreign investors** (APPSC 2019).
[66:8:1] - **10 parameters:** starting a business, construction permits, getting electricity, registering property, getting credit, protecting minority investors, paying taxes, trading across borders, enforcing contracts, resolving insolvency.
[66:8:2] TABLE head: Edition | India's rank
  [66:8:2.0] DB 2015 (2014) | 142
  [66:8:2.1] DB 2018 | 100 – jump of 30
  [66:8:2.2] DB 2020 | 63 (last edition)
[66:8:3] - **Discontinued 2021** – data irregularities, ethics concerns.
[66:8:4] - Weakest indicators: **enforcing contracts, registering property**.
[66:8:5] - **B-READY** (Business Ready) – successor; **3 pillars: regulatory framework, public services, operational efficiency**; India in the third report (around Sept 2026).
[66:8:6] Doing Business = 10 parameters | B-READY = 3 pillars | NITI Investment Friendliness Index = 8 pillars

## SUB 0 | Who Is A Start-up?
[67:0:0] # Definition and Criteria (DPIIT)
[67:0:1] - **DPIIT** (Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade) is the **nodal agency for start-up recognition**.
[67:0:2] - Eligible forms: **private limited company, registered partnership firm, LLP, cooperative**.
[67:0:3] TABLE head: Criterion | Start-up | Deep-tech start-up
  [67:0:3.0] Age (from incorporation) | Up to 10 years | Up to 20 years
  [67:0:3.1] Annual turnover | Not above ₹200 crore | Not above ₹300 crore
[67:0:4] - The older limit of ₹100 crore turnover has been revised to **₹200 crore**.
[67:0:5] An entity formed by **splitting or reconstructing** an existing business is NOT a start-up.
[67:0:6] # Benefits of Recognition
[67:0:7] - Income-tax deduction for **3 consecutive years out of 10** (Section **80-IAC**; for start-ups incorporated before 1 April 2030).
[67:0:8] - **Self-certification** under labour and environment laws; **fast-track patents**.
[67:0:9] - **Angel tax abolished for all investors** (Budget **2024-25**).
[67:0:10] There is NO permanent tax exemption - only a 3-of-10-years tax holiday.
## SUB 1 | Start-up India
[67:1:0] # Launch and Bodies
[67:1:1] - Launched **16 January 2016**; **16 January = National Startup Day**. The decade of Startup India was marked on 16 January 2026.
[67:1:2] - Nodal: **DPIIT**, Ministry of Commerce and Industry. (Udyam portal = MSME registration, a different thing.)
[67:1:3] - **National Startup Advisory Council**: notified **22 January 2020**.
[67:1:4] - **Startup India Yatra**: hunt for entrepreneurial talent in **Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities**.
[67:1:5] - **Startup India Hub**: single online platform for all stakeholders of the ecosystem.
[67:1:6] - **MAARG** (Mentorship, Advisory, Assistance, Resilience and Growth): national **mentorship** platform of Startup India (DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce and Industry); start-up onboarding from Nov 2022.
[67:1:7] # States' Start-up Ranking Framework (DPIIT)
[67:1:8] - Promotes **competitive federalism** by judging states' start-up policies and their implementation.
[67:1:9] - Categories: **Best Performers, Top Performers, Leaders, Aspiring Leaders, Emerging Start-up Ecosystems**.
[67:1:10] - **Andhra Pradesh = No. 1 (Best Performer) in States' Startup Ranking 2022**.
## SUB 2 | Stand-up India, 2016
[67:2:0] # Key Features
[67:2:1] TABLE head: Feature | Fact
  [67:2:1.0] Launch | 5 April 2016
  [67:2:1.1] Ministry | Ministry of Finance
  [67:2:1.2] Channel | Scheduled commercial bank branches
  [67:2:1.3] Target groups | SC/ST and women entrepreneurs aged 18+
  [67:2:1.4] Loan range | ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore (composite: term loan + working capital)

[67:2:3] # Loan Terms
[67:2:4] - Non-individual enterprises (company, firm): at least **51% shareholding and controlling stake** must be with an SC/ST or woman entrepreneur.
[67:2:5] - Loan covers up to **85% of project cost**; borrower contribution at least **10%**.
[67:2:6] - Working capital up to ₹10 lakh as an **overdraft** with a **RuPay debit card**; above that as a **cash credit limit**.
[67:2:7] - Security: primary security of assets; collateral or guarantee of the **Credit Guarantee Fund for Stand-Up India Loans (CGFSIL)**.
[67:2:8] - Repayment up to **7 years** with a moratorium of up to **18 months**.
[67:2:9] - Status: extended for the 15th Finance Commission period, operational till **31 March 2025**; **2.75 lakh loans worth ₹62,790 crore** sanctioned (to 31 March 2025).

=== NOTES 3:68 | SEZs - Policy, Performance and Criticism; Industrial Corridors (dmic, Cbic, Akic) and Nicdc
## SUB 0 | EPZs and Free Trade Zones
[68:0:0] - **Kandla** (Gulf of Kutch, Gujarat), **1965**: **India's and Asia's first EPZ**; also India's first Free Trade Zone; later Kandla SEZ.
[68:0:1] - **Surat**, **1997**: **first private-sector EPZ**; turned into an SEZ on **1 November 2000**.
[68:0:2] - **Free trade zone**: area where goods are landed, stored, processed and re-exported **without customs duty**; set up to **promote export industries**.
[68:0:3] TABLE head: EPZ | Place
  [68:0:3.0] Kandla | Gujarat
  [68:0:3.1] Falta | West Bengal (UPPSC 2023)
  [68:0:3.2] SEEPZ | Santacruz, Mumbai
  [68:0:3.3] MEPZ | Chennai
  [68:0:3.4] Others | Noida, Cochin, Visakhapatnam
[68:0:4] Kandla was keyed as the city "without an SEZ" (UPPCS 2001) because it was known for its EPZ. From 2006, old EPZs and EOUs could convert into SEZs (e.g. Kandla).
## SUB 1 | Sez Policy Evolution
[68:1:0] TABLE head: Date | Milestone
  [68:1:0.0] 1 April 2000 | SEZ policy announced in EXIM Policy 2000-01
  [68:1:0.1] May 2005 | SEZ Act passed by Parliament
  [68:1:0.2] 23 June 2005 | President's assent
  [68:1:0.3] 10 February 2006 | SEZ Act and SEZ Rules 2006 in force
  [68:1:0.4] 2025 | SEZ (Amendment) Rules: semiconductor/electronic-component SEZ land cut 50 ha → 10 ha
[68:1:1] Policy 2000 → Act 2005 → in force 2006. "SEZ policy announced in" = **both 2000 and 2006** (APPSC 2018 key); Act in force = **2006**.
## SUB 2 | Sez Act 2005 - Objectives and Features
[68:2:0] # Objectives (full list)
[68:2:1] - 1. Generation of **additional economic activity**.
[68:2:2] - 2. Promotion of **exports of goods and services**.
[68:2:3] - 3. Promotion of **investment from domestic and foreign sources**.
[68:2:4] - 4. Creation of **employment** opportunities.
[68:2:5] - 5. Development of **infrastructure** facilities.
[68:2:6] "Exports of services only" is NOT an objective. "SEZ is set up primarily to generate employment" is FALSE - SEZs are **export-oriented** enclaves (APPSC-G2 2017).
[68:2:7] # Features
[68:2:8] - A **duty-free enclave** for exports.
[68:2:9] - Developer: **Central Government, State Government or any person** (public, private, joint sector), together or alone. SEZs under the Act are mainly private.
[68:2:10] - Purpose: manufacturing, services or **Free Trade Warehousing Zone**.
[68:2:11] - Route: proposal through the **State Government → Board of Approval**.
[68:2:12] - **Single-window clearance** for Central and State matters.
[68:2:13] - **100% FDI** under automatic route for most manufacturing.
[68:2:14] - Minimum area (Rules 2006): multi-product **1,000 ha**, sector-specific **100 ha**, IT/ITES 10 ha; reduced in 2019 (sector-specific to **50 ha**).
[68:2:15] TABLE head: Section | Provision
  [68:2:15.0] s.18 | IFSC in an SEZ - only one IFSC per SEZ
  [68:2:15.1] s.10AA (Income Tax Act) | 100% tax exemption 5 years; 50% next 5; 50% of reinvested profit for 5 more
[68:2:16] - Tax holidays **phased out 2017-2020** (sunset clause), not 2010-2013.
[68:2:17] - **SEZ India mobile app**: launched **15 January 2017** by Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
## SUB 3 | Sez Performance
[68:3:0] - FY 2024-25 exports **₹14,63,669 crore (~US$173 bn)**, +8%, about **21%** of India's exports.
[68:3:1] - Export growth below 4% in FY25; **466 units shut** in five years across seven SEZs (100 in FY25).
[68:3:2] - SEZs got **less than 3% of FDI**; DTA sales capped.
[68:3:3] - Highest growth in SEZ exports over the previous year (among options asked): **2007-08**.
