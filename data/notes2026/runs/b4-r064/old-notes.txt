## SUB 4 | Culture, Economy and Language
[64:4:0] - **Vidyarthi and Rai:** hunters Birhor, Chenchu; agriculturists Santhal, Munda; pastoralists Toda; artisans Irula.
[64:4:1] - **Transhumance** (herds lowlands ↔ highlands): Bhotiya, Bakarwal (J&K), Gaddi; Maasai = Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda.
[64:4:2] - Longpi pottery - **Tangkhul Naga**; last head-hunters - **Konyak Naga**.
[64:4:3] - **Sarhul** (new year, tree worship) - Oraon, Munda, Santhal, Ho; Oraon dormitory **Ghumkaria**, chief Mahato; Oraon speak **Kudukh (Dravidian)**.
[64:4:4] - Ho: 'Andi' (negotiation), 'Oportipi' (capture). **Agaria** iron-makers, worship Lohasur. **Kamar** bamboo baskets. **Khairwar** catechu. **Bilama** dance - Baiga. Gond and Korku - month-long fruit-tree festival.
[64:4:5] - Hornbill - Nagaland; Bastar Dussehra - Chhattisgarh. Sahariya ('Sehara' = wilderness) - starvation deaths.

[64:4:9] - **Tribal dictionaries:** In **November 2018**, **Odisha** released **bilingual dictionaries of 21 tribal languages** (with Odia), made by its Academy of Tribal Language and Culture and SCSTRTI, for multilingual education in tribal areas.

## SUB 7 | Pvtgs
[64:7:0] - Criteria (4): **pre-agricultural technology, low literacy, economic backwardness, declining/stagnant population** (not Sixth Schedule residence).
[64:7:1] - Timeline: Dhebar → **52 PTGs (1975)** → +23 (1993) = **75** → renamed **PVTGs 2006** → Feb 2024: 7 variants added (Odisha 4; AP 3: Bondo Porja, Khond Porja, Konda Savara).
[64:7:2] - Count: **Odisha 13 > AP + Telangana 12** > Bihar incl. Jharkhand 9.
[64:7:3] - Largest PVTG **Sahariya**; smallest **Sentinelese, Great Andamanese**. Baiga - MP; Chenchu - AP; Toda, Kota, Irula - TN.
[64:7:4] - **Habitat rights (FRA Sec 3(1)(e))**: **Baiga** first in India (Dindori, MP, 2015-16), then Bharia and Kamar. "Critical Wildlife Habitat" is defined in **FRA 2006**.
