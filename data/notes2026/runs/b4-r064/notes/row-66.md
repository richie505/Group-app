=== World Races and Physical Traits
# Three (four) major races
| Race | Main distribution | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Caucasoid** | Europe, North Africa, Middle East, parts of Central and South Asia | Europeans, Arabs, Persians, many North Indians; Nordic, Alpine, Mediterranean sub-types |
| **Mongoloid** | **China, Japan, Korea, Mongolia**, SE Asia, Arctic (**Inuit/Eskimo**), **Native Americans** | North-East India (hill and forest tribes) |
| **Negroid** | Sub-Saharan Africa; **Pygmies** (Congo Basin), Bushmen (Kalahari), Bantu, Zulu, Maasai | **Negrito** sub-race in the Andamans |
| Australoid | Australian Aborigines, Melanesians | Proto-Australoid tribes of central and south India |
- **Largest group:** exam keys say **Caucasoid** (Europe + West and South Asia counted together); NextGen notes call the **Mongoloid** the most populous – the count depends on how South Asians are classed.
- **Native Americans** of North America = **Mongoloid** stock; their name = **Amerinds** (American Indians). **Mestizo** = European-Amerind mix; Mulatto = European-African; Zambo = Amerindian-African.
- Humans share **over 99%** of genes; race is subjective – all belong to *Homo sapiens*.
# Criteria of classification
- Criteria: **skin, eye and hair** (colour, form), **face, nose**, stature, head form – **cephalic, nasal, stature indices**; blood groups – **not the ear**.
- **Cephalic index** = maximum breadth ÷ maximum length × 100.
| Index | Value | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Cephalic | 82+ / 77–81.99 / 76.99 and below | Brachy (round) / Meso / Dolicho (long) |
| Nasal | 85+ / 70–84.9 / below 70 | Platyrrhine (broad) / Mesorrhine / Leptorrhine |
| Stature (Topinard) | 170 cm+ / under 160 cm | Tall / small |
| Hair | leio / cymo / ulotrichi | Straight (Mongoloid) / wavy (Caucasoid) / woolly (Negroid) |
| Type | Diagnostic traits |
|---|---|
| Mongoloid | Yellowish complexion, oblique eyes (epicanthic fold), high cheek bones, sparse hair, flat face |
| Negrito | Dark skin, woolly/frizzy hair, short stature, broad flat nose |
| Proto-Australoid | Dark skin, wavy hair, long head, depressed nasal root |
| Dravidian (Risley) | Long head, broad nose, dark skin |
| Caucasoid / Negroid | High bridge, narrow nose / low bridge, broad nose |

=== Racial Stock of Indian Tribes
- **Andaman tribes** (Onge, Jarawa, Great Andamanese, Sentinelese) = **Negrito**, a branch of the **Negroid** race (UPSC 1997; "aborigine tribes of Andaman" → Negroid); traces among Kadar, Irula.
- **Nicobar Shompen = Mongoloid**, like the Nicobarese.
- **North-East hills and forests** = **Mongoloid** (Naga = Indo-Mongoloid).
- **Proto-Australoid** = central and south Indian tribes – **Santhal**, Munda, Oraon, Ho, Bhil, Chenchu, Yeruva (Khasi and Tharu are Mongoloid).
- **AP**: **Chenchus (Nallamala) = Proto-Australoid**; AP plains = mainly **Mediterranean**, Dravidian-speaking.

=== Risley Classification – 7 Types (1901 Census)
- **H.H. Risley**: Census Commissioner, **1901**, ran its ethnographic survey; **The People of India (1908)**.
- Basis: **nasal index**; dictum: caste status varies inversely with nasal index – **racial theory of caste**, rejected.
| Type | Region |
|---|---|
| Turko-Iranian | Baluchistan, NWFP |
| Indo-Aryan | Punjab, Rajasthan, Kashmir |
| Scytho-Dravidian | Western India – Maharashtra, Saurashtra, Coorg |
| Aryo-Dravidian | Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, parts of Rajasthan |
| Mongolo-Dravidian | Lower Bengal, Odisha |
| Mongoloid | Himalaya, Assam, north-east |
| Dravidian | South India, Chota Nagpur, central India (Paniyan, Santals) |
- "Dravidian" properly names a **language family** (Indo-Aryan + Dravidian = main groups).
~~ Sources: OnlyIAS NCERT Indian Society 01 p.1 (1901 Census, Risley); app notes 4:66 sub 2

=== B.S. Guha Classification – 6 Groups (1931 Census)
- **1931 Census** data: **6 groups, 9 sub-types**; most cited.
| Group | Region / peoples |
|---|---|
| Negrito | Andaman – Onge, Jarawa, Great Andamanese, Sentinelese; traces Kadar, Irula |
| Proto-Australoid | Central and south tribes – Santhal, Munda, Oraon, Ho, Bhil, Chenchu, Yeruva |
| Mongoloid | Palaeo-Mongoloid (Sema Nagas, Lepchas) + Tibeto-Mongoloid (Tibetans of Sikkim); Shompen |
| Mediterranean | Palaeo-Mediterranean (Dravidian south) + Mediterranean (Punjab, Ganga plain) + Oriental (Punjab, Rajasthan) |
| Western Brachycephals | Alpinoid, Dinaric, Armenoid (Parsis) |
| Nordic | North-West Frontier, Punjab, Rajasthan |
- Only Guha lists Negrito and Proto-Australoid separately.
- **Western Brachycephals – regions:** Alpinoid and Dinaric types are found in Gujarat, Bengal, Odisha and Coorg; the Armenoid type in the Parsis.

=== Anthropological Survey of India and Modern Criticism
- **AnSI**: founded **1945 at Varanasi**, moved to **Kolkata (HQ) 1948**; first Director **B.S. Guha**.
- **People of India project (1985)**: **K.S. Singh**, about **4,635 communities**; the book *The People of India* (1908) = Risley.
- AnSI classified **268** DNT communities for NITI Aayog.
- **2009** genetics: Indians = mix of **Ancestral North Indians (ANI)** + **Ancestral South Indians (ASI)**; Rakhigarhi DNA – no **Steppe Pastoralist** gene.
- **Criticism of old schemes:** Risley and Guha relied on visible physical traits and colonial ethnography; modern genetics shows rigid racial categories are not scientific.
- India ratified **ICERD 1968**; UN CERD review first since **2007**.
