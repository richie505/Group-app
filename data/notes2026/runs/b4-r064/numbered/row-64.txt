[1:2] # Marriage and family
[1:3] - **Polyandry** (one wife, several husbands): **Toda** (classic), **Khasa**, **Jaunsari** (Jaunsar-Bawar), Kota – "Jaunsaris and Todas" = **III and IV** (UPPSC 2009).
[1:4] - **Polygamy** = plural marriage, of two kinds: **polyandry** (Toda, Khasa) and **polygyny** – one husband, several wives (Bhil, Gond, Naga, Muria). Exam keys for "which tribe practises polygamy" answer **Toda**; strictly the polygynous tribes are polygamous too.
[1:5] - **Dudh-lautawa ('Duvautana')** – cross-cousin marriage (to the mother's brother's child, like Telugu *menarikam*): **Gonds of MP**.
[1:6] - **Ho:** **'Andi'** = marriage by negotiation, **'Oportipi'** = marriage by capture.
[1:7] - **Santhal:** commonest form = **Bapla** (Kirin bahu bapla, arranged); Itut (forced sindur), Nir-bolok (intrusion), Sanga (widow remarriage) are minor forms.
[1:8] - **Khasi, Garo, Jaintia:** matrilineal – the youngest daughter (Khasi) inherits.
[1:9] - **Oraon:** youth dormitory **Ghumkaria**, village chief Mahato; Gond dormitory **ghotul**.
[1:10] # Economy and crafts
[1:11] - **Vidyarthi and Rai:** hunters **Birhor, Chenchu**; agriculturists Santhal, Munda; pastoralists Toda; artisans Irula.
[1:12] - **Hunting / food-gathering tribes:** Chenchu (AP), Kamar (MP/Chhattisgarh), Irula (TN, Kerala), Birhor – the **Meena** of Rajasthan are settled agriculturists (**not a hunting tribe**).
[1:13] - **Transhumance** = **seasonal movement of people with their herds** between lowland and highland pastures: Bhotiya, Bakarwal (J&K), Gaddi; Maasai = Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda.
[1:14] - **Kamar** – livelihood on **bamboo** (baskets); **Khairwar** – make **catechu** (khair); **Agaria** – iron smelting; **Bhunjia** – sacred kitchen **'Lal Bangla'** no outsider may enter.
[1:15] - Longpi pottery – **Tangkhul Naga**; last head-hunters – **Konyak Naga**.
[1:16] - Sahariya ('Sehara' = wilderness) – starvation deaths in news.
[1:17] - **Living root bridges** – **Meghalaya** (Khasi and Jaintia hills).
[1:18] - **Traditional dresses:** Bokhu – Sikkim; Mekhla – Assam; Pheran – Kashmir; **Mundu – Kerala** (not Chhattisgarh).

[2:6] # Largest tribes
[2:7] - Order: **Bhil (1,70,71,049) > Gond (1,32,56,928) > Santhal > Mina** (then Naikda, Oraon). Bhil ≈ 1.7 crore = about **16%** of STs (the "38%" in some books is the Bhil share of **MP's** STs, 37.7%).
[2:8] - **Census 2001 too: Bhil 1.27 crore > Gond 1.09 crore > Santhal 58 lakh > Mina 38 lakh** – so Bhil is the answer for any year; answer **Gond only when Bhil is not an option** (MPPSC 2013).

[2:11] # Who is a tribe
[2:12] - **Basis of determination:** **cultural specification and distinct habitation** – Lokur criteria: primitive traits, distinctive culture, geographical isolation, shyness of contact, backwardness.
[2:13] - **No definition in the Constitution:** Art. 366(25) defines STs only by reference to **Art. 342** notification.

[3:5] # PVTGs
[3:6] - Criteria (4): **pre-agricultural technology, low literacy, economic backwardness, declining/stagnant population** (not Sixth Schedule residence).
[3:7] - Timeline: Dhebar → **52 PTGs (1975)** → +23 (1993) = **75** → renamed **PVTGs 2006** → Feb 2024: 7 variants added (Odisha 4; AP 3: Bondo Porja, Khond Porja, Konda Savara).
[3:8] - Count: **Odisha 13 > AP + Telangana 12** > Bihar incl. Jharkhand 9.
[3:9] - Largest PVTG **Sahariya**; smallest **Sentinelese, Great Andamanese**. Baiga – MP; Chenchu – AP; Toda, Kota, Irula – TN.
[3:10] - **Jharkhand PVTGs:** Asur, Birhor, Birjia, Hill Kharia, **Korwa**, Mal Paharia, Parhaiya, Sauria Paharia, Savar – Kawar, Kora, Karmali are not PVTGs.
[3:11] - **Chhattisgarh PVTGs:** Abujhmaria, **Baiga (largest)**, Birhor, Pahadi Korwa, Kamar (+ Bharia).
[3:12] - **Habitat rights (FRA Sec 3(1)(e))**: **Baiga** first in India (Dindori, MP, 2015-16), then Bharia and Kamar; Kamar first in Chhattisgarh (2023 Baiga second there). "Critical Wildlife Habitat" is defined in **FRA 2006**; habitat rights are recognised under FRA by the gram sabha and district committees, with the **Ministry of Tribal Affairs** as nodal ministry – not declared by MoEFCC (UPSC 2018 → "1 and 2 only").
