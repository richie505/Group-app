=== Telugu Bhakti Composers – Bhakta Ramadasu, Tyagaraja, Kshetrayya and Others
# Bhakta Ramadasu (Kancherla Gopanna)
- **Kancherla Gopanna = Bhakta Ramadasu**: Golconda revenue official (tahsildar of **Bhadrachalam**); used state revenue to rebuild the **Bhadrachalam Rama temple**.
- Jailed **12 years in Golconda fort** by **Abul Hasan Tana Shah**; in prison he composed the **Dasarathi Satakam** (104 poems to Rama) and the Ramadasu kirtanas.
- **Dasarathi Satakam = Gopanna** (APPSC G1 2002, asked three times) – NOT **Sumati Satakam** (Baddena), Bhaskara Satakam (Maravi Venkayya) or Vemana Satakam.
- English translation of Dasarathi Satakam credited (APPSC G2 2001 key) to **Dr. Bhanumathi Ramakrishna**.
# Other composers
| Figure | Deity / place | Work / fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Tyagaraja** (1767–1847) | **Rama**; born Tiruvarur, lived and attained samadhi at **Tiruvaiyaru** (**Thanjavur** district) | Thousands of kritis, mostly in **Telugu**, mostly in praise of **Rama**; **Pancharatna kritis** (five gems, in Ghana ragas); created **new ragas**; musical dramas **Prahlada Bhakti Vijayam** and **Nauka Charitram**; Carnatic Trinity with **Muthuswami Dikshitar** and **Syama Sastri** |
| **Kshetrayya** (Varadayya of **Movva**, Krishna dt.) | **Muvva Gopala** (Krishna) | 1,000+ Shringara **padams**; sang at **Abdullah Qutb Shah**'s court |
| Tarigonda Vengamamba | Venkateswara, Tirumala | Venkatachala Mahatyamu (18th c.) |
| Dhurjati | Srikalahasti | Srikalahasteeswara Satakam |
- **Thyagaraja Aradhana** (yearly festival) = **Tiruvaiyaru, Thanjavur** district (UPPCS 2000) – not Adyar, Mamallapuram or Udipi.
- **Nauka Charitram = Tyagaraja** (APPSC G1 1994), not Annamacharya, Kshetrayya or Ramadasu.
- **Foremost composer of Carnatic music = Tyagaraja** (APPSC G2 2019); **pioneer of the kriti / sankirtana form = Annamacharya** (APPSC G2) – the same four options, two different stems.
- **UPSC 2018:** Tyagaraja created new ragas (2 ✔); Annamacharya's kirtanas praise **Venkateswara** (4 ✔); most Tyagaraja kritis praise **Rama, not Krishna** (1 ✘); Annamacharya (1408–1503) and Tyagaraja (1767–1847) were **not contemporaries** (3 ✘) → **2 and 4 only**.
- Carnatic Trinity = Tyagaraja, Dikshitar, Syama Sastri – **Annamacharya is not one of them**.
- Telugu Bhakti poets fed Carnatic music through **padams, keertanas, satakas and dvipadas**.
> Exam angle: book → author (Dasarathi Satakam, Nauka Charitram, Pancharatna) and statement sets mixing Annamacharya with Tyagaraja.

=== Tallapaka Annamacharya and the Tallapaka Family
# Annamacharya (1408–1503)
- Born at **Tallapaka** village (Rajampet mandal) – historically **Cuddapah (Kadapa) district** (APPSC 2012 key). The area went to the new **Annamayya district** (named after him) in April 2022 and, with the **Rajampet division, back to YSR Kadapa district** from 1 Jan 2026 (as of 2026).
- Contemporary of **Saluva Narasimha Raya** (APPSC G2 2011) – not Harihara, Deva Raya I or III; also a contemporary of **Purandara Dasa**.
- About **32,000 sankirtanas** to **Lord Venkateswara of Tirumala**; about **12,000** survive on **copper plates** found at Tirumala (1922); recovered and edited by **Veturi Prabhakara Sastry**. Compositions are Adhyatma (spiritual) and Sringara.
- Titles: **Pada Kavita Pitamaha**, Sankirtanacharya; earliest composer of sankirtanas → **pioneer of the kriti form**; the kirtana structure shaped later Carnatic music. Opposed untouchability (**Brahmam Okkate**).
- Other works: **Sri Venkateswara Satakam** (first Telugu Shringara Satakam), Sringara Manjari, Sankirtana Lakshanam (Sanskrit).
- **Arudra** called him "Incarnated Telugu".

| Peda Tirumalacharya | Son | Vairagya Vachanamalika, sankirtanas |

- **Paramayogi Vilasamu = Tallapaka Tiruvengalanatha** (APPSC Degree Lecturers 2011) – not Annamayya himself, Dhurjati or Ramaraja Bhushana.

=== Virashaivism and Saiva Bhakti in Andhra
# Basava and the Virashaivas (Lingayats)
- Founder: **Basavanna (Basaveshwara)**, 12th c., a **Brahmana** and **minister of the Kalachuri ruler Bijjala II** at **Kalyana**; Anubhava Mantapa ("religious parliament"); **Akka Mahadevi** and Allama Prabhu were Vachana saints (12th-c. Karnataka).
- Lingayats worship **Shiva** in the form of the **Ishtalinga** worn on the body; theology **Shakti Vishishtadvaita**; **Kayaka** = honest labour, **Dasoha** = sharing.
- Social message: rejected caste, **untouchability** and Brahmanical ritual, **condemned child marriage**, **questioned the theory of rebirth**, and encouraged practices the **Dharmashastras disapproved** – **post-puberty marriage and widow remarriage**.
- **NDA 2019:** I (Basavanna, a Brahmana, minister of a Kalachuri ruler) ✔; II says the practices were "approved by the Dharmashastras" ✘ → **only I**.
- **UPSC 2016:** Siddhas (Sittars) of Tamil Nadu were **monotheistic and condemned idolatry** ✔; Lingayats **questioned rebirth and rejected caste hierarchy** ✔ → **both**.
# Spread into Andhra
| Person | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Mallikarjuna Panditaradhya** | **Spread Virashaivism in Andhra** (Kakatiya age); founder of **Aradhya Saivism** (moderate, kept Vedic learning and varna); **Sivatattvasaram**; originator of the Sataka form in Telugu; one of the Panchacharyas |
| **Palkuriki Somanatha** | **Basava Puranam** (Telugu **dwipada**) and **Panditaradhya Charitra**; Vrushadhipa Satakam (first full-featured satakam) |
- **Virashaivism spread in Andhra by Panditaradhya** (APPSC G1 1983-84) – Basava founded it in Karnataka; Somanatha was its later Telugu poet.
- **Basava Purana = Palkuriki Somanatha** – not Mallikarjuna Panditaradhya (Sivatattvasaram).
- Panchacharyas: Revanaradhya, Marularadhya, Ekoramaradhya, **Panditaradhya**, Vishwaradhya.
> Exam angle: founder and Andhra propagators; statement questions on Lingayat social ideas (NCERT wording).

=== Bammera Potana
- Native of **Bammera** (Jangaon dt., Telangana); one tradition places him at **Vontimitta (Ontimitta), Kadapa** (APPSC MPDO 1997).
- Titles: **Sahaja Panditudu**, Nigarva Chudamani; contemporary of **Srinatha** and Pedakomati Vema Reddy's age.
| Work | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Veerabhadra Vijayam** | **His first work**, at the request of his guru **Ivaturi Somanaradhya** |
| **Andhra Maha Bhagavatamu** | Telugu Bhagavata Purana, dedicated to **Lord Rama**; Potana wrote **8 of 12 skandhas** (5th Bopparaju Gangana, 6th Erchuri Singana, 11-12th Veligandala Narayana); printed by C.P. Brown, **1840** |
| Bhogini Dandakam | **First Telugu Dandakam** |
| Narayana Satakam | Satakam |
- **Veerabhadra Vijayam = Potana** (APPSC G2 2011, asked three times) – not Srinatha, Tikkana or Errapragada.
- **Refused to dedicate** his work to a king (**Sarvajna Singabhupala of Rachakonda**).

=== Vemana, Veerabrahmam and the Social Message of Andhra Bhakti
| Feature | Vemana | Potuluri Veerabrahmendra Swamy |
|---|---|---|
| Work | Vemana padyalu (Vemana Satakam), colloquial Telugu | **Kalagnanam** – prophecies of the evils of Kali Yuga |
| Form / theme | **Ataveladi** metre; refrain "Vishwadabhirama Vinura Vema"; satire on caste, ritual, idol worship, superstition | Condemned caste; disciples of all castes and faiths – **Siddaiah (a Muslim, Dudekula)**, Kakkaiah (Madiga) |
- **Kalagnanam = Veerabrahmam** (Dy. EO 2008) – not Vemana Satakam.
- **C.P. Brown** collected and translated **Verses of Vemana (1829)**.
- **Social impact:** Bhakti did not uniformly uphold Brahmanical dominance – Basava, Vemana, Veerabrahmam attacked caste; **satakam and dwipada** carried Bhakti to common people; Telugu replaced Sanskrit as its vehicle.
