## SUB 0 | Telugu Bhakti Composers – Bhakta Ramadasu, Tyagaraja, Kshetrayya and Others
[0:0] # Bhakta Ramadasu (Kancherla Gopanna)
[0:1] - **Kancherla Gopanna = Bhakta Ramadasu**: Golconda revenue official (tahsildar of **Bhadrachalam**); used state revenue to rebuild the **Bhadrachalam Rama temple**.
[0:2] - Jailed **12 years in Golconda fort** by **Abul Hasan Tana Shah**; in prison he composed the **Dasarathi Satakam** (104 poems to Rama) and the Ramadasu kirtanas.
[0:3] - **Dasarathi Satakam = Gopanna** (APPSC G1 2002, asked three times) – NOT **Sumati Satakam** (Baddena), Bhaskara Satakam (Maravi Venkayya) or Vemana Satakam.
[0:4] - English translation of Dasarathi Satakam credited (APPSC G2 2001 key) to **Dr. Bhanumathi Ramakrishna**.
[0:5] # Other composers
[0:6] TABLE head: Figure | Deity / place | Work / fact
  [0:6.0] Tyagaraja (1767–1847) | Rama; born Tiruvarur, lived and attained samadhi at Tiruvaiyaru (Thanjavur district) | Thousands of kritis, mostly in Telugu, mostly in praise of Rama; Pancharatna kritis (five gems, in Ghana ragas); created new ragas; musical dramas Prahlada Bhakti Vijayam and Nauka Charitram; Carnatic Trinity with Muthuswami Dikshitar and Syama Sastri
  [0:6.1] Kshetrayya (Varadayya of Movva, Krishna dt.) | Muvva Gopala (Krishna) | 1,000+ Shringara padams; sang at Abdullah Qutb Shah's court
  [0:6.2] Tarigonda Vengamamba | Venkateswara, Tirumala | Venkatachala Mahatyamu (18th c.)
  [0:6.3] Dhurjati | Srikalahasti | Srikalahasteeswara Satakam
[0:7] - **Thyagaraja Aradhana** (yearly festival) = **Tiruvaiyaru, Thanjavur** district (UPPCS 2000) – not Adyar, Mamallapuram or Udipi.
[0:8] - **Nauka Charitram = Tyagaraja** (APPSC G1 1994), not Annamacharya, Kshetrayya or Ramadasu.
[0:9] - **Foremost composer of Carnatic music = Tyagaraja** (APPSC G2 2019); **pioneer of the kriti / sankirtana form = Annamacharya** (APPSC G2) – the same four options, two different stems.
[0:10] - **UPSC 2018:** Tyagaraja created new ragas (2 ✔); Annamacharya's kirtanas praise **Venkateswara** (4 ✔); most Tyagaraja kritis praise **Rama, not Krishna** (1 ✘); Annamacharya (1408–1503) and Tyagaraja (1767–1847) were **not contemporaries** (3 ✘) → **2 and 4 only**.
[0:11] - Carnatic Trinity = Tyagaraja, Dikshitar, Syama Sastri – **Annamacharya is not one of them**.
[0:12] - Telugu Bhakti poets fed Carnatic music through **padams, keertanas, satakas and dvipadas**.
[0:13] > Exam angle: book → author (Dasarathi Satakam, Nauka Charitram, Pancharatna) and statement sets mixing Annamacharya with Tyagaraja.

## SUB 1 | Tallapaka Annamacharya and the Tallapaka Family
[1:0] # Annamacharya (1408–1503)
[1:1] - Born at **Tallapaka** village (Rajampet mandal) – historically **Cuddapah (Kadapa) district** (APPSC 2012 key). The area went to the new **Annamayya district** (named after him) in April 2022 and, with the **Rajampet division, back to YSR Kadapa district** from 1 Jan 2026 (as of 2026).
[1:2] - Contemporary of **Saluva Narasimha Raya** (APPSC G2 2011) – not Harihara, Deva Raya I or III; also a contemporary of **Purandara Dasa**.
[1:3] - About **32,000 sankirtanas** to **Lord Venkateswara of Tirumala**; about **12,000** survive on **copper plates** found at Tirumala (1922); recovered and edited by **Veturi Prabhakara Sastry**. Compositions are Adhyatma (spiritual) and Sringara.
[1:4] - Titles: **Pada Kavita Pitamaha**, Sankirtanacharya; earliest composer of sankirtanas → **pioneer of the kriti form**; the kirtana structure shaped later Carnatic music. Opposed untouchability (**Brahmam Okkate**).
[1:5] - Other works: **Sri Venkateswara Satakam** (first Telugu Shringara Satakam), Sringara Manjari, Sankirtana Lakshanam (Sanskrit).
[1:6] - **Arudra** called him "Incarnated Telugu".
[1:7] # The Tallapaka poets
[1:8] TABLE head: Member | Relation | Works / title
  [1:8.0] Tallapaka Timmakka (Tirumalamma) | Annamacharya's wife | Subhadra Kalyanam (= Subhadra Parinayam), also Rukmini Kalyanam; first woman poet of Telugu
  [1:8.1] Peda Tirumalacharya | Son | Vairagya Vachanamalika, sankirtanas
  [1:8.2] China Tirumalacharya | Grandson | Title Ashtabhasha Kavi Chakravarti
  [1:8.3] Chinnanna (Tiruvengalanatha) | Grandson | Paramayogi Vilasam, Annamacharya Charitra (dvipada biography), Usha Kalyanam, Ashtamahishi Kalyanam
[1:9] - **Subhadra Kalyanam / Parinayam = Tallapaka Timmakka** (APPSC G2; G2 2012) – not Molla, Rangajamma, Madhuravani or Gangadevi.
[1:10] - **Paramayogi Vilasamu = Tallapaka Tiruvengalanatha** (APPSC Degree Lecturers 2011) – not Annamayya himself, Dhurjati or Ramaraja Bhushana.
[1:11] > Exam angle: contemporary ruler, birthplace district, and "who wrote" within the family.

## SUB 3 | Virashaivism and Saiva Bhakti in Andhra
[3:0] # Basava and the Virashaivas (Lingayats)
[3:1] - Founder: **Basavanna (Basaveshwara)**, 12th c., a **Brahmana** and **minister of the Kalachuri ruler Bijjala II** at **Kalyana**; Anubhava Mantapa ("religious parliament"); **Akka Mahadevi** and Allama Prabhu were Vachana saints (12th-c. Karnataka).
[3:2] - Lingayats worship **Shiva** in the form of the **Ishtalinga** worn on the body; theology **Shakti Vishishtadvaita**; **Kayaka** = honest labour, **Dasoha** = sharing.
[3:3] - Social message: rejected caste and Brahmanical ritual, **questioned the theory of rebirth**, and encouraged practices the **Dharmashastras disapproved** – **post-puberty marriage and widow remarriage**.
[3:4] - **NDA 2019:** I (Basavanna, a Brahmana, minister of a Kalachuri ruler) ✔; II says the practices were "approved by the Dharmashastras" ✘ → **only I**.
[3:5] - **UPSC 2016:** Siddhas (Sittars) of Tamil Nadu were **monotheistic and condemned idolatry** ✔; Lingayats **questioned rebirth and rejected caste hierarchy** ✔ → **both**.
[3:6] # Spread into Andhra
[3:7] TABLE head: Person | Contribution
  [3:7.0] Mallikarjuna Panditaradhya | Spread Virashaivism in Andhra (Kakatiya age); founder of Aradhya Saivism (moderate, kept Vedic learning and varna); Sivatattvasaram; originator of the Sataka form in Telugu; one of the Panchacharyas
  [3:7.1] Palkuriki Somanatha | Basava Puranam (Telugu dwipada) and Panditaradhya Charitra; Vrushadhipa Satakam (first full-featured satakam)
[3:8] - **Virashaivism spread in Andhra by Panditaradhya** (APPSC G1 1983-84) – Basava founded it in Karnataka; Somanatha was its later Telugu poet.
[3:9] - **Basava Purana = Palkuriki Somanatha** – not Mallikarjuna Panditaradhya (Sivatattvasaram).
[3:10] - Panchacharyas: Revanaradhya, Marularadhya, Ekoramaradhya, **Panditaradhya**, Vishwaradhya.
[3:11] > Exam angle: founder and Andhra propagators; statement questions on Lingayat social ideas (NCERT wording).

## SUB 4 | Bammera Potana
[4:0] - Native of **Bammera** (Jangaon dt., Telangana); one tradition places him at **Vontimitta (Ontimitta), Kadapa** (APPSC MPDO 1997).
[4:1] - Titles: **Sahaja Panditudu**, Nigarva Chudamani; contemporary of **Srinatha** and Pedakomati Vema Reddy's age.
[4:2] TABLE head: Work | Fact
  [4:2.0] Veerabhadra Vijayam | His first work, at the request of his guru Ivaturi Somanaradhya
  [4:2.1] Andhra Maha Bhagavatamu | Telugu Bhagavata Purana, dedicated to Lord Rama; Potana wrote 8 of 12 skandhas (5th Bopparaju Gangana, 6th Erchuri Singana, 11-12th Veligandala Narayana); printed by C.P. Brown, 1840
  [4:2.2] Bhogini Dandakam | First Telugu Dandakam
  [4:2.3] Narayana Satakam | Satakam
[4:3] - **Veerabhadra Vijayam = Potana** (APPSC G2 2011, asked three times) – not Srinatha, Tikkana or Errapragada.
[4:4] - **Refused to dedicate** his work to a king (**Sarvajna Singabhupala of Rachakonda**).
[4:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP previous papers (src/ap-history/nextgen-previous-papers.txt): "Potana was the author of Virabhadra Vijayam", "Narayana Satakam, Andhra Bhagavatham"; Rocket AP History (src/rocket-ap-history.txt); old app notes 1:145 sub 5

## SUB 6 | Vemana, Veerabrahmam and the Social Message of Andhra Bhakti
[6:0] TABLE head: Feature | Vemana | Potuluri Veerabrahmendra Swamy
  [6:0.0] Work | Vemana padyalu (Vemana Satakam), colloquial Telugu | Kalagnanam – prophecies of the evils of Kali Yuga
  [6:0.1] Form / theme | Ataveladi metre; refrain "Vishwadabhirama Vinura Vema"; satire on caste, ritual, idol worship, superstition | Condemned caste; disciples of all castes and faiths – Siddaiah (a Muslim, Dudekula), Kakkaiah (Madiga)
[6:1] - **Kalagnanam = Veerabrahmam** (Dy. EO 2008) – not Vemana Satakam.
[6:2] - **C.P. Brown** collected and translated **Verses of Vemana (1829)**.
[6:3] - **Social impact:** Bhakti did not uniformly uphold Brahmanical dominance – Basava, Vemana, Veerabrahmam attacked caste; **satakam and dwipada** carried Bhakti to common people; Telugu replaced Sanskrit as its vehicle.
