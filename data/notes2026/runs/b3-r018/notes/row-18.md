=== Ease of Doing Business – India's Rank, Sub-indices and State Rankings
| Doing Business report (released) | India's rank |
|---|---|
| DB 2015 (Oct 2014) | 142 |
| **DB 2017** (Oct 2016) | **130** of 190 |
| **DB 2018** (**31 Oct 2017**) | **100** of 190 (first time in the top 100) |

| **DB 2020** (Oct 2019) | **63** of 190 (14 places up) – the last report |
- **New Zealand** topped DB 2019 and DB 2020. India's worst area in DB 2020: **enforcing contracts (163/190)**; India lagged in **paying taxes**.
- **10 sub-indices:** starting a business, dealing with **construction permits**, getting electricity, **registering property**, getting credit, protecting minority investors, **paying taxes**, trading across borders, enforcing contracts, resolving insolvency. **NOT** a sub-index: **maintenance of law and order**. Counts the Single Window System; not per capita income, inflation or literacy.
- Purpose: a **ready reckoner for foreign investors**.
- **Discontinued in September 2021** after data irregularities → successor **B-READY** (World Bank, first report Oct 2024); DPIIT is India's nodal agency.

=== Hunger – Global Hunger Index and Related Data
- **GHI** = 4 indicators: **undernourishment**, **child stunting**, **child wasting**, **child mortality** (under-5) – no maternal mortality. Scale 0-100, **higher = worse**. (UPSC 2016: undernourishment, stunting and mortality – all three are used.)
| GHI edition | India |
|---|---|
| **GHI 2008** | **66th** of 88 |
| **GHI 2010** (Oct 2010, IFPRI) | **67th** of 84 |
| **GHI 2011** | **67th of 81** |
| GHI 2012 | 65th of 79 |
| **GHI 2017** | **100th of 119** |
| **GHI 2018** | **103rd** of 119 – **shared with Nigeria** (score 31.1) |
| **GHI 2019** | **102nd** of 117 |
| GHI 2024 → **GHI 2025** | 105th → **102nd of 123**, score **25.8**, "**serious**" |
| **GHI 2026** (Oct 2026) | **100th of 122**, score **24.1**, serious; Pakistan 106th |
- India's objection: 3 of 4 indicators cover only children; undernourishment rests on an opinion poll of about 3,000 people.
- **FAO SOFI 2003**: India had about **214 million** of the world's **842 million** undernourished – about **one-fourth (25%)**.
- **WDR 2006**: **Bangladesh** poorest of Bangladesh, India, Pakistan and Sri Lanka (Nepal, not offered, was poorer still).

=== Gender Gap and Human Capital Indices
- **Global Gender Gap Index** (WEF, first 2006): 4 sub-indices – **economic** participation, **education**, **health** and survival, **political** empowerment – **not leisure**.
| GGGR | India | Top |
|---|---|---|
| **2018** (Dec 2018) | **108th of 149** | Iceland |
| **2020** | **112th** of 153 | **Iceland** (most gender-neutral) |
| 2022 | 135th | Iceland |
| 2025 | 131st of 148 | Iceland |
- **Human Capital Index** (World Bank, launched **2018**): **survival** (under-5 mortality), expected **quality-adjusted years of schooling**, **health** (adult survival, stunting). **HCI 2020** topped by **Singapore**.
- HDI 2019 (HDR Dec 2019): **Norway** 1st; India's HDI 2022 (HDR 2021/22) rank **132nd**.
