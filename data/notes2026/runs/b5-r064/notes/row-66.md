=== Turbines, Generators and Dynamos
- **Wind turbine chain:** **kinetic energy** of wind → **mechanical** (rotation) → **electrical** energy in the generator.
- **Generator / dynamo:** converts **mechanical energy into electrical energy** (asked ×5); a dynamo gives DC through a commutator.
- Turbines plus dynamos convert **mechanical** energy, not chemical, solar or magnetic energy, into electricity.
- Reverse device: **electric motor** = electrical → mechanical; **transformer** only steps voltage up or down.
- Rotating generators give **AC**; stationary solar panels give **DC**.
- Power varies with the **cube of wind speed**; **Betz limit 16/27 (~59.3%)**.
| Feature | HAWT | VAWT |
|---|---|---|
| Axis | **Parallel to the wind** | Vertical |
| Designs | 3-blade, yaw control | **Darrieus, Savonius** |
| Efficiency | Higher – standard utility turbine | Lower; takes wind from any direction |
- **CUF** = actual output ÷ output at full rated power; wind **36-40%**, solar ~21%.
- **IEC classes:** Class I high-wind, **Class III low-wind** sites.
~~ Sources: PMF Environment Part 7 p.73 (rotating → AC); app notes 5:66 sub 0

=== Onshore vs Offshore Wind
| Point | Onshore | Offshore |
|---|---|---|
| Winds | Variable | **Stronger, steadier** → higher capacity factor |
| Cost | Cheaper, easy upkeep | **2-3 times** costlier; long blades, port needs |
| Issues | Land | **Rusting, subsea cables**, foundations, navigation, coastal security |
- **Floating turbines** for deep water; offshore does **not** remove all environmental and navigation risks.
- Wind complements solar: blows in evenings and in the **SW monsoon (June-Oct)**.
~~ Sources: PMF Environment Part 7 p.82-83; app notes 5:66 sub 1

=== Wind Farms and Hybrid Policy
| Farm | Place | Capacity |
|---|---|---|
| **Muppandal** | Kanyakumari, TN | **1,500 MW** – largest onshore farm (PMF) |
| **Jaisalmer Wind Park** | Rajasthan | **1,064 MW** – the answer many keys give for "largest" |
| Khavda hybrid park | Kutch, Gujarat | 30 GW solar-wind |
- **National Wind-Solar Hybrid Policy, 2018:** wind turbines and solar PV at the **same grid-connection point**; one resource ≥25% of the other.
- **India's first wind-solar hybrid plant:** **Adani Hybrid Energy**, **Jaisalmer**, Rajasthan (390 MW, May 2022).
- AP: **Ramagiri** (Ananthapuramu) hybrid solar-wind park.
- **Round-the-clock (RTC) tenders:** first by **SECI, 2020**; ≥**51%** of energy from RE. **ISTS charges waived** for projects commissioned by **30 June 2025**.
~~ Sources: PMF Environment Part 7 p.80-82, p.85 (Muppandal largest); app notes 5:66 sub 7

=== Offshore Wind Policy
- **National Offshore Wind Energy Policy, 2015:** farms in India's **EEZ**; nodal ministry **MNRE**, nodal agency **NIWE**.
- Potential ~140 GW by 2050: ~36 GW off **Gujarat**, ~35 GW off **Tamil Nadu** (Gulf of Mannar).
- **VGF scheme (2024): ₹7,453 crore** for the first **1 GW** – 500 MW each off **Gujarat and Tamil Nadu**.
- Partners: **UK** Offshore Wind Taskforce (Feb 2026); Denmark.
~~ Sources: PMF Environment Part 7 p.83; Shankar Environment 17 (OCR) p.5; app notes 5:66 sub 6

=== Repowering and Other Support
- **Repowering** = replacing ageing turbines with **larger, modern turbines at the same site**.
- **National Repowering Policy, 2016** → **Repowering and Life Extension Policy, 2023**.
- Supports: accelerated depreciation, **GBI**, competitive bidding, **Wind RPO trajectory to 2030**.
> Order: Offshore 2015 → Repowering 2016 → Hybrid 2018 → Repowering and Life Extension 2023 → Offshore VGF 2024.
~~ Sources: NextGen S&T Group 2 Part 5 p.43; app notes 5:66 sub 8
