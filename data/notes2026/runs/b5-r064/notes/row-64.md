=== Renewable vs Non-renewable, Conventional vs Non-conventional
# Classification
| Basis | Sources |
|---|---|
| **Renewable** | Solar, wind, **hydro**, tidal, **geothermal**, **biomass**, biogas |
| **Non-renewable** | **Coal**, **petroleum**, **natural gas**, **nuclear (uranium)**; minerals such as **iron ore** |
| Conventional | Coal, petroleum, natural gas, hydel, **firewood**, cattle-dung cake |
| **Non-conventional** | Solar, wind, tidal, geothermal, biomass – by definition **renewable** |
- **Most-asked NOT:** **nuclear is NOT renewable** (asked ×4) and NOT non-conventional – uranium is a finite, non-fossil fuel (Rocket Environment).
- **Fossil fuels:** coal, petroleum, natural gas – formed from buried organisms; ultimate source the **Sun**; all non-renewable.
- **Natural gas is NOT green energy**; sunlight, wind and algae (biofuel) are.
- **Petroleum** is the non-renewable raw material of both **plastics** (e.g. toothbrushes) and **gasoline**.
- **Hydel** is conventional yet renewable; **hydroelectricity** is the electricity source suited to **sustainable development** (Rocket Environment).
- **Gobar gas** = mainly **methane**; **gasohol** = **petrol + alcohol** (ethanol), not benzene + alcohol.
# Not derived from the Sun
- Wind, hydro, biomass and fossil fuels = stored solar energy.
- NOT from the Sun: **nuclear** (fission), **geothermal** (Earth's internal heat), **tidal** (Moon's pull).
> Statement angle: "fossil fuels = coal, oil, gas" ✓; "gasohol = benzene + alcohol" ✗; "geothermal is non-conventional" ✓; "gobar gas mainly methane" ✓ → 1, 3 and 4.

=== Solar – India's Most Important Alternative Source
- **Most important non-conventional source** for India and **greatest renewable potential** (APPSC G2 Mains 2017; UPPSC 2011).
- **Largest storehouse of alternative energy** = solar; used most in **organic form** (photosynthesis).
- **No atmospheric pollution** in converting sunlight to electricity; a **solar cell** is the most **eco-friendly** option among petroleum, forest products and nuclear fission.
- **Photovoltaic cells** relate to **solar energy** (RAS 2016).
- **A/R angle:** "wind and solar are cleaner, greener" and "India depends heavily on thermal and hydro, which harm the environment" – both true, but R does not explain A.

=== Green Energy Corridors and RPO
- **Green Energy Corridor (GEC):** purpose = **transmission to evacuate renewable power** – mainly **intra-state transmission systems** for RE projects, plus inter-state lines.
- **Intra-State GEC-I:** **8 states** – AP, Gujarat, HP, Karnataka, MP, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu (**not Telangana, not Odisha**); funding **MNRE 40% : KfW (Germany) loan 40% : state utility 20%**.
- **GEC-II** (2022): **7 states**, ~20 GW RE. **Inter-State GEC** lines built by **PGCIL**.
- **RPO:** DISCOMs and large/designated consumers must buy a share of renewable power; **solar RPO 8% by March 2022**.
- **Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022:** RPO recast as a duty to **consume** renewable energy.
~~ Sources: NextGen S&T Group 2 Part 5 p.43 (RPO, solar RPO 8%); app notes 5:64 sub 5-6

=== IREDA and the Clean Energy Fund
- **IREDA:** set up **1987** as a **Non-Banking Financial** institution; **Public Limited Government Company** under the **administrative control of MNRE**; motto "Energy for Ever".
- **Objective:** **financial support** to schemes generating electricity from **new and renewable sources** and **conserving energy through energy efficiency**.
- **Status:** **Navratna** from **April 2024** (it was a Mini Ratna when APPSC G1 asked in Jan 2023); listed on NSE and BSE **29 Nov 2023**.
- Functions: project finance, appraisal, consultancy – **not setting tariffs**.
- **National Clean Energy Fund (NCEF):** **Budget 2010-11**; **Clean Energy Cess on coal, lignite and peat** (₹50/t, later ₹400/t); funded **research and innovative clean-energy projects**; cess subsumed in **GST (1 July 2017)**.
> G1 2023 angle: "Navratna" ✗ then (✓ since April 2024); "finances RE and energy efficiency" ✓; "under MNRE" ✓.
