=== Photovoltaic vs Solar Thermal; Solar Cells
| Point | Photovoltaic (PV) | Solar thermal / CSP |
|---|---|---|
| Principle | **Photovoltaic effect**: light → electricity **directly** (semiconductor) | Sun's rays → **heat** → steam turbine → electricity |
| Output | **Direct current (DC)**; an **inverter** changes it to AC | Turbine-generator gives AC |
| Heat storage | No | Possible |
| Examples | Solar panels, satellites | Solar cooker, water heater, parabolic troughs |
- **Photovoltaic cells = solar cells**; they convert solar energy into electrical energy – not Leclanche, dry or voltaic (chemical) cells.
- **Material:** **silicon** (semiconductor) – crystalline silicon dominant; thin-film (CdTe, CIGS, a-Si) cheaper, bendable, less efficient; IIT Bombay perovskite-silicon tandem **30.2%**.
- **Artificial satellites** get electricity from **solar cells**.
- **Solar water pumps** can run **surface and submersible** pumps, **centrifugal and piston** pumps – so both "not for" statements are wrong (UPSC 2018).
- **Solar constant ≈ 1.4 kW/m²** (1.361) – solar energy per unit area at one astronomical unit.
- **Space-based solar power (SBSP):** collected in space, ~99% uninterrupted supply – suggested as a national goal (UPPSC 2010 A/R: both true, R explains A).
- **CUF:** solar about **21%**, wind 36-40%, coal about 85%.
> UPSC 2014: "PV direct, thermal via heat" ✓; "PV gives AC, thermal DC" ✗ (reverse); "India makes thermal but not PV" ✗ → **1 only**.

=== Solar Potential of India
- **250-300 clear sunny days** a year; most parts get **4-7 kWh/m²/day**.
- About **5,000 trillion kWh** a year incident over India's land area → tremendous potential (APPSC G1: both statements true, II explains I).
- Very high radiation: Rajasthan, northern Gujarat, Ladakh, AP, Maharashtra, MP.
- **NISE estimate: 748 GW** ground-based (using **3% of wasteland**); ~4.5% used.
- RE-potential share: **Rajasthan 23.70%**; **AP 9.10%**.
- **NISE (National Institute of Solar Energy), Gurugram** – autonomous body of MNRE; hosts the ISA headquarters.

=== Jawaharlal Nehru National Solar Mission
- Launched **January 2010** ("Solar India"); one of the **8 missions of NAPCC (2008)**.
- Aim: make India a **global leader in solar energy**.
| Item | Target |
|---|---|
| Original | **20 GW** grid-connected by 2022 |
| Revised (June 2015) | **100 GW by 2022** = **40 GW rooftop** + 60 GW large grid-connected (achieved early 2025) |
| Phases (off-grid PV) | I 2010-13 (200 MW); II 2014-17; **III 2017-22** |
- **Grid-connected rooftop is part of the mission** – a statement that it "excludes rooftop" is false.
- Successor goal: 500 GW non-fossil by 2030.

=== Floating Solar
- Potential **102.18 GW** on reservoirs; **Maharashtra** leads (~16 GW); first assessment by **NISE**.
- **Omkareshwar** (Narmada, **Khandwa, MP**): 278 MW → 600 MW, **India's largest floating solar**.
- AP's floating plant: **NTPC Simhadri** (Visakhapatnam).
- Pluses: saves land, cuts evaporation, cooler panels; minus **~25% higher upfront cost**.

=== Solar Manufacturing – DCR, ALMM, PLI, Duties
- **Domestic Content Requirement (DCR):** introduced in the **National Solar Mission (2010)** – Indian-made crystalline PV cells and modules for mission projects (30% domestic content for solar thermal) → term seen with **developing solar power production** (UPSC 2017).
- The **US challenged India's DCR at the WTO**, and the WTO ruled for the US.
- Now: domestic cells/modules compulsory in **CPSU, PM-KUSUM and rooftop-subsidy** projects; domestic-cell modules mandated from **June 2026**.
| ALMM list | Covers | From |
|---|---|---|
| List-I | Modules | **2019** |
| **List-II** | **Cells** | 1 June 2026 |
| List-III | Ingots, wafers | **June 2028** |
- Capacity: modules ~**190 GW** vs cells ~25 GW. **BCD (Apr 2022):** **modules 40%, cells 25%**. **GST cut 12% → 5%** (22 Sep 2025). **PLI** for modules **2021**.
- Gap: **no solar-panel recycling norms** yet (E-Waste Rules 2022 targets do not apply to PV waste).
~~ Sources: PMF Environment Part 6 p.119-120 (DCR, WTO); PMF Environment Part 7 p.78-79; app notes 5:65 sub 7

=== International Solar Alliance (ISA)
| Event | Fact |
|---|---|
| Launch | India + France, **30 Nov 2015**, **COP21, Paris** |
| HQ foundation stone | **25 January 2016**, NISE, **Gwal Pahari, Gurugram** – Modi and Hollande |

- Goal: **$1 trillion** solar investment by 2030 ("Towards 1000").
| Initiative | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **OSOWOG** | Transnational grid sharing solar power across time zones (Modi, 1st Assembly 2018) |
| **World Solar Bank** | Proposed solar financing vehicle (~$15 bn) |
| Global Solar Facility | De-risk investment in underserved regions; MIGA guarantee programme from Sub-Saharan Africa (Sept 2024) |
| **ISTARC / STAR-C** | Training and technology resource centres |
| Common procurement platform | Pool demand to cut costs |
- **Partners:** **ADB** (March 2018 pact – solar in Asia and the Pacific), World Bank, IRENA; training through India's **ITEC**.
- **PM-KUSUM is a domestic MNRE scheme, NOT an ISA initiative**.
