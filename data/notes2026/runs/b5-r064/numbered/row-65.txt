## SUB 0 | Photovoltaic vs Solar Thermal; Solar Cells
[0:0] TABLE head: Point | Photovoltaic (PV) | Solar thermal / CSP
  [0:0.0] Principle | Photovoltaic effect: light → electricity directly (semiconductor) | Sun's rays → heat → steam turbine → electricity
  [0:0.1] Output | Direct current (DC); an inverter changes it to AC | Turbine-generator gives AC
  [0:0.2] Heat storage | No | Possible
  [0:0.3] Examples | Solar panels, satellites | Solar cooker, water heater, parabolic troughs
[0:1] - **Photovoltaic cells = solar cells**; they convert solar energy into electrical energy – not Leclanche, dry or voltaic (chemical) cells.
[0:2] - **Material:** **silicon** (semiconductor) – crystalline silicon dominant; thin-film (CdTe, CIGS, a-Si) cheaper, bendable, less efficient; IIT Bombay perovskite-silicon tandem **30.2%**.
[0:3] - **Artificial satellites** get electricity from **solar cells**.
[0:4] - **Solar water pumps** can run **surface and submersible** pumps, **centrifugal and piston** pumps – so both "not for" statements are wrong (UPSC 2018).
[0:5] - **Solar constant ≈ 1.4 kW/m²** (1.361) – solar energy per unit area at one astronomical unit.
[0:6] - **Space-based solar power (SBSP):** collected in space, ~99% uninterrupted supply – suggested as a national goal (UPPSC 2010 A/R: both true, R explains A).
[0:7] - **CUF:** solar about **21%**, wind 36-40%, coal about 85%.
[0:8] > UPSC 2014: "PV direct, thermal via heat" ✓; "PV gives AC, thermal DC" ✗ (reverse); "India makes thermal but not PV" ✗ → **1 only**.
[0:9] ~~ Sources: PMF Environment Part 7 p.72-73; Shankar Environment ch.22 p.2 (inverters, thermal); app notes 5:65 sub 0

## SUB 7 | Solar Manufacturing – DCR, ALMM, PLI, Duties
[7:0] - **Domestic Content Requirement (DCR):** introduced in the **National Solar Mission (2010)** – Indian-made crystalline PV cells and modules for mission projects (30% domestic content for solar thermal) → term seen with **developing solar power production** (UPSC 2017).
[7:1] - The **US challenged India's DCR at the WTO**, and the WTO ruled for the US.
[7:2] - Now: domestic cells/modules compulsory in **CPSU, PM-KUSUM and rooftop-subsidy** projects; domestic-cell modules mandated from **June 2026**.
[7:3] TABLE head: ALMM list | Covers | From
  [7:3.0] List-I | Modules | 2019
  [7:3.1] List-II | Cells | 1 June 2026
  [7:3.2] List-III | Ingots, wafers | June 2028
[7:4] - Capacity: modules ~**190 GW** vs cells ~25 GW. **BCD (Apr 2022):** **modules 40%, cells 25%**. **GST cut 12% → 5%** (22 Sep 2025). **PLI** for modules **2021**.
[7:5] - Gap: **no solar-panel recycling norms** yet (E-Waste Rules 2022 targets do not apply to PV waste).
[7:6] ~~ Sources: PMF Environment Part 6 p.119-120 (DCR, WTO); PMF Environment Part 7 p.78-79; app notes 5:65 sub 7
