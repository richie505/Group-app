## SUB 0 | Turbines, Generators and Dynamos
[0:0] - **Wind turbine chain:** **kinetic energy** of wind → **mechanical** (rotation) → **electrical** energy in the generator.
[0:1] - **Generator / dynamo:** converts **mechanical energy into electrical energy** (asked ×5); a dynamo gives DC through a commutator.
[0:2] - Turbines plus dynamos convert **mechanical** energy, not chemical, solar or magnetic energy, into electricity.
[0:3] - Reverse device: **electric motor** = electrical → mechanical; **transformer** only steps voltage up or down.
[0:4] - Rotating generators give **AC**; stationary solar panels give **DC**.
[0:5] - Power varies with the **cube of wind speed**; **Betz limit 16/27 (~59.3%)**.
[0:6] TABLE head: Feature | HAWT | VAWT
  [0:6.0] Axis | Parallel to the wind | Vertical
  [0:6.1] Designs | 3-blade, yaw control | Darrieus, Savonius
  [0:6.2] Efficiency | Higher – standard utility turbine | Lower; takes wind from any direction
[0:7] - **CUF** = actual output ÷ output at full rated power; wind **36-40%**, solar ~21%.
[0:8] - **IEC classes:** Class I high-wind, **Class III low-wind** sites.
[0:9] ~~ Sources: PMF Environment Part 7 p.73 (rotating → AC); app notes 5:66 sub 0

## SUB 7 | Offshore Wind Policy
[7:0] - **National Offshore Wind Energy Policy, 2015:** farms in India's **EEZ**; nodal ministry **MNRE**, nodal agency **NIWE**.
[7:1] - Potential ~140 GW by 2050: ~36 GW off **Gujarat**, ~35 GW off **Tamil Nadu** (Gulf of Mannar).
[7:2] - **VGF scheme (2024): ₹7,453 crore** for the first **1 GW** – 500 MW each off **Gujarat and Tamil Nadu**.
[7:3] - Partners: **UK** Offshore Wind Taskforce (Feb 2026); Denmark.
[7:4] ~~ Sources: PMF Environment Part 7 p.83; Shankar Environment ch.22 p.5; app notes 5:66 sub 6
