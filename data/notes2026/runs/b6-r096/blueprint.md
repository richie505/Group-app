## 3. Angles
- **Awardee → field** (the dominant APPSC angle): Padma Shri/Bhushan 2017 list (Meenakshi Gurukkal – Kalaripayattu, Basanti Bisht – folk music, Niranjanananda – yoga, Udwadia – medicine, T.H. Chowdary – civil service, Mallesham – Asu machine).
- **Winner of year X** (Oscar Best Picture, NFA Best Feature, Jnanpith edition, Nobel Peace, Magsaysay, Miss World/Universe) – the "recent" ones go stale (Jnanpith, Best Actor Oscar tagged outdated).
- **Firsts** (first woman / foreigner / singer Bharat Ratna; first Best Actor/Actress Oscar; first Indian Oscar; first Dronacharya trio).
- **Match the following**: Arjuna 2022/2023 sportsperson ↔ discipline; Dronacharya categories 2022; Outstanding Parliamentarian ↔ year.
- **Statements / NOT-correct**: Param Vir Chakra, Khel Ratna vs Arjuna 2024, Golden Globes 2023, Abhinandan's Ahimsa Puraskar, SNA 2023 pairs.
- **Award → body/purpose**: Global Goalkeeper (Gates), Crystal (WEF), Tyler (environment), Sabin (amphibians), Rajiv Gandhi Environment (clean technology), Murti Devi (Bharatiya Jnanpith).
- **Repeats**: Palme d'Or 2002, Meryl Streep 2003, Dronacharya 1985, Rajiv Gandhi Environment Award, Outstanding Parliamentarian pair, Nobel Peace 2016/2019, Village Rockstars, IFFI 50th, Miss World 2016, Heart Lamp (four times).
- **AP angle**: Telugu Padma awardees (Venkatasubbaiah, Koteswaramma, Daripalli Ramaiah, Yadagiri Rao), Jyothi Yarraji, Gopichand, Lok Nayak Foundation, Nandi Awards.
