=== New Current Affairs: 5–14 Nov 2026
# 31st CII Partnership Summit – Visakhapatnam (12–13 Nov 2026)
- **31st CII Partnership Summit**, **12–13 Nov 2026** (Thu–Fri), **Andhra University Engineering College grounds, Visakhapatnam** (the **30th** edition, Nov 2025, was also in Visakhapatnam); organised by **CII** with **DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce and Industry**, and the AP Government; held annually since **1995**.
- **Theme:** "**Navigating New World Order: Trade, Technology, Trust**"; six themes incl. trade and global value chains, clean energy, talent and skills; delegates from about **58 countries** (16 foreign ministers). Union Home Minister **Amit Shah** – chief guest at the inaugural session; Vice-President **C.P. Radhakrishnan** – valedictory (13 Nov).
- **Before the summit:** five **regional investment conferences** (Visakhapatnam, Kakinada, Amaravati, Ananthapuramu, Tirupati) – **194 MoUs, ₹86,522 crore**.
- **Day 1 (12 Nov):** **73 MoUs + 15 G.O.s = ₹5,84,355 crore**, about **1.08 lakh jobs**; biggest – **Reliance Industries ₹2.73 lakh crore (East Godavari)**; Greenko ₹45,000 crore, CleanMax ₹41,000 crore, **MEIL ₹36,000 crore (Krishna)** (renewables); **Kia India ₹18,878 crore** (Penukonda, **Sri Sathya Sai**; 12,000 jobs); Rashmi Metallurgical ₹18,500 crore (Srikakulam); JSW ₹15,000 crore (Kadapa); **GAIL ₹13,500 crore (Srikakulam)**; **fabrIQ** (Analog–MEIL JV, semiconductors) ₹4,300 crore.
- **Two-day total (PTI, 13 Nov):** **109 MoUs worth ₹9.02 lakh crore**, over **2 lakh jobs**; space firms **Agnikul Cosmos (₹510 crore)** and **Hathor Rockets (₹250 crore)**; IOCL ₹6,600 crore; Naidu: **₹32.5 lakh crore** of MoUs in the last two CII summits (₹22.5 lakh crore in 2025), **74% conversion** so far; **5,000 acres near Tirupati** for the space sector.
> ⚠ Trap: the 2026 summit (31st) was in **Visakhapatnam**, like the 30th; Reliance's ₹2.73 lakh crore project is in **East Godavari**, Kia's expansion in **Sri Sathya Sai** (Penukonda).

# MoUs and foreign investment
- **South Korean auto-component firms – ₹1,614 crore:** **Hwasin Automotive India** ₹850 crore, **Sri Sathya Sai** district (parts for **Kia**); **Seoyon E-Hwa Summit Automotive** ₹514.17 crore (918 jobs); **Iyamant India** ₹250 crore, **Kurnool** (400 jobs). *(news 3 Nov 2026)*
- **Tripartite MoU – INCOIS, Vignan's University and Ananth Technologies (ATPL, Hyderabad):** ocean sciences, satellite oceanography, blue economy; **Potential Fishing Zone (PFZ)** advisories. **INCOIS** = est. **1999**, **Ministry of Earth Sciences**, HQ **Hyderabad**.
- **Essar Renewables Ltd** (arm of Essar Power): about **₹4,500 crore** for a **300 MW** solar + wind + **BESS** project in AP; **PPA with NHPC**; site not disclosed. Related AP RE projects: **Pinnapuram IRESP – Greenko, 1,680 MW, Kurnool**; **Upper Sileru PSP – APGENCO, 1,350 MW (ASR)**; Gandikota PSP 1,000 MW and Chitravathi PSP 500 MW – Adani Renewable Energy.
- **UAE and Japan (12 Nov 2026 news):** AP invited UAE agrifood firm **Silal** to the **₹1 lakh crore Rayalaseema horticulture hub**; Dubai-based **Floormaster's** project in **Chittoor**. Japan: a **second Japanese Industrial Cluster** in AP (Marubeni, Sumitomo, Mitsui interested) and a **Global Capability Centre in Visakhapatnam**; **JERA** (Japan) – preliminary **5-year** deal with **AM Green** for **70,000 t/year of green ammonia from Kakinada**. AP's **first Japanese Industrial Township = Sri City**.
- **WIPO–Andhra Pradesh Academy** proposed by CM Naidu to WIPO DG **Daren Tang** (Visakhapatnam) – IPR training, help for **GI tags** of AP handlooms and handicrafts. **WIPO**: Convention signed **Stockholm, 14 July 1967**, in force 1970, UN specialised agency **1974**, HQ **Geneva**, **194** members; India joined **1975**.
# Irrigation infrastructure
- **789 defunct lift irrigation schemes** to be restored at **₹632.07 crore** before the next Kharif; **Jalaharathi Corporation** to monitor and maintain them. LIS sources: HNSS – Krishna; **Chintalapudi, Pushkara, Tadipudi – Godavari**; **Guru Raghavendra – Tungabhadra (Kurnool)**; **Taraka Rama Teertha Sagaram – Champavathi (Vizianagaram)**. *(news 12 Nov 2026)*
- **Handri–Neeva Sujala Sravanthi (HNSS):** lift scheme taking **Krishna** water from **Srisailam** at **Malyala (Kurnool)** to **Rayalaseema**; canal about **569 km**; about **6 lakh acres** in erstwhile Anantapur, Kadapa, Kurnool, Chittoor; water reached **Madanapalle** (Nov 2026).
- **Polavaram Right Main Canal:** cement lining damaged near **Repallewada (Polavaram mandal)** by **Cyclone Murasu** rains. Project facts: on the **Godavari** near **Ramayyapeta**, **National Project under Section 90 of the AP Reorganisation Act, 2014**; **Polavaram Project Authority** under the **Ministry of Jal Shakti**; live storage **75.20 TMC** at FRL **+45.72 m (150 ft)**; diverts **80 TMC** of Godavari water to the **Krishna** basin.
> ⚠ Trap: Guru Raghavendra LIS lifts from the **Tungabhadra**, HNSS from the **Krishna (Srisailam)**; Polavaram is a National Project under **Section 90** (not Sec. 84/85, which deal with the Apex Council and river boards).
