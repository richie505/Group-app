## SUB 5 | Direct Tax and Compliance (budget 2026-27)
[37:5:0] - **New Income Tax Act**: in force from **April 2026**; simplified rules and forms to be notified, redesigned for common citizens.
[37:5:1] - **Motor Accident Claims Tribunal (MACT)**: interest awarded to a **natural person exempt**; **TDS on it removed**.
[37:5:2] - **Return revision deadline**: extended from **31 December to 31 March** (nominal fee).
[37:5:3] - **Foreign Asset Disclosure Scheme**: **one-time, 6-month**, for small taxpayers.
[37:5:4] - **Updated returns**: allowed **even after reassessment begins** – **additional 10% tax**.
[37:5:5] - **Prosecution immunity**: retrospective from **1 October 2024** for undisclosed non-immovable foreign assets **below ₹20 lakh**.
[37:5:6] - **Form 15G/15H**: **single-window filing through depositories**.
[37:5:7] - **Lower/nil TDS certificate**: **rules-based automated process** for small taxpayers.
[37:5:8] - **Decriminalised**: non-submission of books/documents; TDS where payment is in kind.
