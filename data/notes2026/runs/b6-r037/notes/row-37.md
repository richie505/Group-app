=== Budget History, Traditions and Landmark Economic Decisions
# Landmark events – chronology (asked: APPSC AEE 2025)
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 26 Nov 1947 | First Union Budget of independent India – R.K. Shanmukham Chetty |
| June 1966 | Indira Gandhi government devalued the rupee by about **57%** against the US dollar (₹4.76 → ₹7.50 per $) |
| 19 July 1969 | Indira Gandhi nationalised **14** major private banks (deposits over ₹50 crore) |
| 1985-86 | V.P. Singh's Budget – direct-tax reform: lower rates, higher income-tax exemption limit |
| July 1991 | Narasimha Rao–Manmohan Singh reforms – **Licence Raj dismantled** (Industrial Policy, 24 July 1991) |
| 1997-98 | "Dream Budget" – P. Chidambaram cut income-tax and corporate-tax rates; VDIS |
> Order asked: first Budget (1947) → devaluation (1966) → bank nationalisation (1969) → tax-cutting Budget (1985-86) → Licence Raj dismantled (1991). Devaluation came **before** nationalisation.

| Fact | Answer |
|---|---|

- **1947 Budget:** covered **4 months** to 31 March 1948; **Liaquat Ali Khan** had earlier presented the 1947-48 Budget of the Interim Government.
- **Other counts:** P. Chidambaram 9, Pranab Mukherjee 8, Yashwant Sinha 8, Manmohan Singh 6.
- **Morarji Desai** presented on his birthday, **29 February**, in **1964 and 1968**.
- **PMs who presented a Budget:** Jawaharlal Nehru, Indira Gandhi, Rajiv Gandhi.
- **1973-74:** "Black Budget".
# Changes over time
| Year | Change |
|---|---|
| 1924 | Separate Railway Budget began (Acworth Committee) |
| 1950 | Budget leak → printing moved from Rashtrapati Bhavan to Minto Road |
| 1980 | Printing in North Block basement press |
| 1999 | Yashwant Sinha moved time from 5 PM to 11 AM |

- **Halwa ceremony:** North Block; marks the start of printing about **10 days** before the Budget.

=== Direct Tax, TCS and STT (Budget 2026-27)
- **New Income Tax Act:** in force from **April 2026**.
- **MACT interest** to a natural person: **exempt**; **TDS removed**.
- **Revised return deadline:** **31 December → 31 March** (nominal fee).
- **Foreign Asset Disclosure Scheme:** **one-time, 6-month**, small taxpayers.
- **Updated returns:** allowed **even after reassessment begins** – **additional 10%** tax.
- **Form 15G/15H:** **single-window filing through depositories**.
- **Lower/nil TDS certificate:** rules-based automated process.
| Item | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| TCS – foreign tour packages | 2%–20% | 2% |
| TCS – LRS for education and medical | 5% | 2% |
| STT – futures | 0.02% | 0.05% |
| STT – options premium and exercise | 0.1% / 0.125% | 0.15% |
- **Buybacks:** taxed as **capital gains for all shareholders** (effective 22% corporate / 30% non-corporate promoters).
- **MAT credit:** old credit set-off **only for companies moving to the new regime**, capped at **one-fourth**.
> STT on futures was **raised** (0.02% → 0.05%), not cut.

=== IT Services, Data Centres, Non-residents and Cooperatives
- **IT services safe harbour:** common margin **15.5%**; threshold **₹300 crore → ₹2,000 crore**.
- **Unilateral APAs (IT):** within **2 years**, extendable by **6 months**.
| Item | Provision |
|---|---|
| Data centres | Tax holiday up to 2047 for foreign companies giving cloud services to global customers via Indian data centres |
| Related-entity data centre services | 15% safe harbour on costs |
| Component warehousing (bonded) | Safe harbour 2% of invoice value |
| Toll manufacturing in bonded zones | 5-year exemption to non-residents supplying capital goods |
| Non-resident experts | Global income exempt for 5 years under notified schemes |
- **Cooperatives:** deduction extended to societies supplying **cattle feed and cotton seed**; 3-year dividend exemption for notified National Cooperative Federations.
