## SUB 4 | Direct Tax, TCS and STT (Budget 2026-27)
[4:0] - **New Income Tax Act:** in force from **April 2026**.
[4:1] - **MACT interest** to a natural person: **exempt**; **TDS removed**.
[4:2] - **Revised return deadline:** **31 December → 31 March** (nominal fee).
[4:3] - **Foreign Asset Disclosure Scheme:** **one-time, 6-month**, small taxpayers.
[4:4] - **Updated returns:** allowed **even after reassessment begins** – **additional 10%** tax.
[4:5] - **Form 15G/15H:** **single-window filing through depositories**.
[4:6] - **Lower/nil TDS certificate:** rules-based automated process.
