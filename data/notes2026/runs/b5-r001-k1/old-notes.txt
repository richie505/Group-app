## SUB 1 | Food Technology - Additives, Preservation, Fortification
[1:1:0] # Additives vs Adulterants
[1:1:1] - **Food additives** = added **intentionally** to improve food (preservatives, colours, emulsifiers, sweeteners, flavour enhancers). **Adulteration** = adding inferior/harmful substances; causes poisoning, allergies, nutritional deficiency.
[1:1:2] - Sweeteners: aspartame, stevia; fructose = natural sugar. **MSG (Ajinomoto)** = flavour enhancer, **umami**.
[1:1:3] # Preservatives
[1:1:4] TABLE head: Preservative | Used in
  [1:1:4.0] Sodium benzoate (E211) | Soft drinks, pickles, sauces (acidic foods)
  [1:1:4.1] Sorbic acid / potassium sorbate | Cheese, pickles; against yeasts, moulds
  [1:1:4.2] Sodium metabisulphite | Jams, sauces
  [1:1:4.3] SO2 | Dried fruits, winemaking
  [1:1:4.4] Propionates | Stored grains, cheese
  [1:1:4.5] Vinegar (acetic acid) | Pickles; made by fermenting sugarcane juice
  [1:1:4.6] Boric acid | Old, no longer used (toxic)
[1:1:5] # Irradiation, Fortification, Cold Chain
[1:1:6] - **Irradiation**: ionising (gamma) radiation kills organisms without much nutrient loss; potato/onion shelf life **8 months** without cold storage. **DAE: 25 irradiators in PPP mode**.
[1:1:7] - **PM Kisan Sampada Yojana** (MoFPI) Cold Chain scheme: 16 irradiation units approved, 9 operational (Aug 2025).
[1:1:8] - **Fortification**: iodised salt; rice with iron, folic acid, B12; oil/milk with A and D; FSSAI logo **"+F"**.
[1:1:9] - **Jul 2026**: food-safety reforms - Food Safety on Wheels, blockchain batch tracing, "One Nation, One Regulator" ends dual **BIS/AGMARK** certification.
[1:1:10] - Kitchen: colas contain **phosphoric acid** and **caffeine**; lemon = **citric acid**; ant bite = **formic acid** (stronger than acetic acid).
[1:1:11] - **Formic acid** (methanoic acid) also acts as an **antibacterial preservative**; aspartame and stevia are sweeteners and fructose is a natural sugar, not preservatives.

[1:2:2] - Scientists: **Casimir Funk** coined "vitamine"; **James Lind** - citrus cures scurvy; Hopkins - accessory food factors; McCollum and Davis - vit A (1913); Suzuki - rice bran extract.
[1:2:3] - Nutrients: carbohydrate = main energy; **fat = 9.3 kcal/g**; protein ~4 kcal/g. Highest protein grain **soybean**; groundnut rich (~25% protein); highest biological value **egg**; iodine **seaweed**; alpha-linolenic acid **linseed**; carbohydrate **rice**.
[1:2:4] - Proteins: **casein** (milk), **gluten** (wheat), **keratin** (hair, nails), collagen, fibroin (silk); oxytocin is NOT a protein. **Kwashiorkor** (oedema) and **marasmus** = protein deficiency. Nursing mother 65 g/day.
[1:2:5] - Amino acids: 20 (10 essential); not stored. Protein digestion starts in stomach (pepsin), then trypsin.
[1:2:6] - **Pyridoxine = vitamin B6:** amino-acid metabolism and **antibody production**; deficiency → anaemia, dermatitis.
[1:2:7] - **Vitamin K sources:** green leafy vegetables, also **nuts, eggs and animal liver**; needed for **blood clotting**.
[1:2:8] - **Parboiling** (soak + steam) **raises rice's nutritive value**: B vitamins move from bran into grain; polished raw rice → beri-beri.
[1:2:9] - **Stomach HCl:** from **parietal (oxyntic) cells**; pH ~1.5-3.5; activates **pepsinogen → pepsin**, kills microbes.
[1:2:10] - **More vitamins:** B7 = **biotin** (vitamin H); B-group vitamins (B1, B9 etc.) and vitamin C are **water-soluble**, while A, D, E and K are fat-soluble. **McCollum**, co-discoverer of vitamin A, also identified **vitamin D** (1922) as the anti-rickets factor in cod-liver oil.
## SUB 3 | Human Body - Glands and Kidney
[1:3:0] TABLE head: Gland | Hormone / fact
  [1:3:0.0] Pituitary (master) | GH, TSH, ACTH, ADH; oxytocin released here, made in hypothalamus
  [1:3:0.1] Pineal | Melatonin - biological clock
  [1:3:0.2] Thyroid (largest endocrine) | T3, T4 (iodine), calcitonin; deficiency: cretinism
  [1:3:0.3] Parathyroid | PTH - blood calcium
  [1:3:0.4] Adrenal (on kidney) | Adrenaline, cortisol
  [1:3:0.5] Pancreas | Endocrine + exocrine; insulin from Islets of Langerhans
  [1:3:0.6] Kidney / placenta | Erythropoietin / chorionic gonadotropin
[1:3:1] - **Urea made in the liver**, filtered by kidney; urine ~1.5 L/day, pH ~6.0; albumin in urine = kidney failure (Bright's disease). Normal BP **120/80 mmHg**.
[1:3:2] - **Dialysis** when kidneys fail: haemodialysis (machine filter) vs **peritoneal** (peritoneum). Lithotripsy breaks stones with ultrasound.
[1:3:3] - **Liver:** **largest gland** (bile, glycogen, detox); **regrows** after partial removal → living-donor transplants.
[1:3:4] - **Insulin** (beta cells) lowers blood sugar, promotes glucose uptake and fat storage; **glucagon** (alpha) raises it. Type 1 diabetes = **low insulin**; type 2 = **insulin resistance**.
[1:3:5] - **Blood groups:** Landsteiner (1901); **AB = universal recipient**, **O negative = universal donor**.
[1:3:6] - **Pacemaker:** natural = **SA node** (right atrium); artificial pacemaker sends electric pulses to regulate heartbeat.
[1:3:7] - **Gland locations:** **pituitary** - at the base of the brain; **thymus** - behind the sternum, trains **T cells**; thyroid - front of the neck; adrenals - above the kidneys (adrenaline, cortisol, aldosterone); parathyroid - back (dorsal side) of the thyroid; **pineal** - in the brain (epithalamus), not on the kidney; **testes** (in the scrotum) - sperm and **testosterone**.
[1:3:8] - **Blood cells:** **neutrophils** are the most abundant granulocytes (white blood cells) in human blood.

[1:6:1] - Soap: alkaline, poor lather in **hard water**; detergent lathers well. Antiseptics on living tissue (H2O2, iodoform); disinfectants on objects (phenol).
[1:6:2] - Polymers: PVC - vinyl chloride; Teflon - tetrafluoroethene; neoprene - chloroprene; natural rubber - isoprene; **Bakelite** - phenol + formaldehyde; PHBV biodegradable.
[1:6:3] - **Hard water:** Ca/Mg salts form scum with soap; bicarbonates = **temporary** (boiling, Clark's lime); chlorides/sulphates = **permanent** (washing soda, ion exchange, Calgon).
[1:6:4] - **Alloys:** brass = Cu + **Zn**; bronze = Cu + **Sn**; German silver Cu-Zn-Ni; solder Sn-Pb; **Dow metal = Mg + Al**; boiler superheater tubes = **Cr-Mo** steel.
[1:6:5] - **Metals:** **tungsten** bulb filament (highest melting point); nichrome heater coil; **aluminium** most used in pure form (foil, cables), iron mostly as steel; sodium kept in **kerosene**.
[1:6:6] - **Gold:** 24 carat pure; 22 carat = **91.6%** (916 hallmark, ~8.4% Cu/Ag); 18 carat = 75%; dissolves in **aqua regia** (3 HCl : 1 HNO3).
[1:6:7] - **Materials:** **PVC** blood bags and dialysis tubes; **glass wool** = fine glass fibres (heat/sound insulation); **flint (lead) glass** lenses, prisms; Pyrex labware.
[1:6:8] - **Others:** **CO2** extinguisher suits electrical fires; ruby = Al2O3 + **chromium**; Statue of Liberty green = copper **patina**; alkanes burn to CO2 + water + heat (⚠ APPSC key 2017 gave carbon).
[1:6:9] - **Colloids:** blood and milk are colloids; milk = emulsion of **fat in water** (butter = water in fat); ⚠ APPSC key (2002) gave water dispersed in fat.
[1:6:10] - **Carbon forms and uses:** diamond - jewellery, cutting, drilling and grinding tools, glass cutters; graphite - pencil 'lead', dry lubricant, electrodes, reactor moderator, Li-ion battery anodes; **coke** - fuel and **reducing agent in iron extraction**; **carbon black** (lampblack) - pigment in **paints**, inks, tyres; **activated charcoal** - adsorbs gases (**gas masks**), purification; fullerenes and **carbon nanotubes** - **electronics, nanotechnology**, drug delivery, lubricants, catalysts; graphene - electronics, composites, sensors, flexible devices.
[1:6:11] - **More polymers:** **Nylon-2-Nylon-6** = **glycine + amino caproic acid** (biodegradable); **PHBV** = **3-hydroxybutanoic acid + 3-hydroxypentanoic acid** (biodegradable); nylon-6,6 = hexamethylenediamine + adipic acid; terylene = ethylene glycol + terephthalic acid; **Buna-S** = **1,3-butadiene + styrene**; Buna-N = 1,3-butadiene + acrylonitrile.
[1:6:12] - **Electromagnetic induction** is used in generators, transformers, induction cooktops, **wireless charging** and the **dynamic microphone** (turns sound vibrations into electrical signals).
[1:6:13] - **Refrigerator cycle:** the refrigerant **absorbs heat while evaporating** inside the refrigerator and releases it at the **condenser** outside.
[1:6:14] - **Osmosis:** water (solvent) moves through a semi-permeable membrane from low to high solute concentration (plant roots absorb water this way); in **reverse osmosis** pressure is applied on the **concentrated side**, leaving salts and bacteria behind (desalination, water purification).
[1:6:15] - **Bleaching powder** releases chlorine, so it is used for **water purification and bleaching**. **Disinfectants** (phenol, bleaching powder) are **toxic to living tissue**, so they are used only on objects; antiseptics (boric acid, iodoform, H2O2) are safe on living tissue.
[1:6:16] - **Soap vs detergent:** soap is **biodegradable** but its alkaline solution harms silk and wool; detergents suit delicate clothes; many **older detergents** had branched chains that bacteria could not break down, so they were **non-biodegradable** and caused foam in rivers.

[1:10:4] # Everyday Physics and Chemistry Basics
[1:10:5] - **Units:** henry = inductance; farad = capacitance; pascal = pressure; decibel = sound level; angstrom = 10^-10 m; light year = distance; picosecond = 10^-12 s.
[1:10:6] - **Instruments:** **lactometer** = milk purity (specific gravity); **hygrometer** = humidity; barometer = pressure; anemometer = wind speed; **atomic (caesium) clock** = most exact time.
[1:10:7] - **Optics:** silvery crack in glass, diamond sparkle = **total internal reflection**; rainbow = refraction + dispersion + TIR; sky **black** in space (no scattering); convex mirror magnification <1.
[1:10:8] - **Radiation:** **infrared** heat therapy for muscular pain; **X-rays** strongly darken photographic plates; fan under AC lamp seems to reverse (**stroboscopic** effect).
[1:10:9] - **Mechanics:** mass measures **inertia**; friction opposes motion; doubling momentum makes KE x4 (**+300%**); no external force = centre-of-mass velocity **constant**; egg floats in denser salt water.
[1:10:10] - **Electricity:** transformer works only on **AC**; bird on a live wire is safe (no **potential difference**); unmagnetised iron is attracted by **either** pole.
[1:10:11] - **Heat:** tea cools fastest in a **metal** cup (good conductor); **free expansion** = into vacuum, no work, ideal-gas temperature unchanged.
[1:10:12] - **Matter:** **plasma** (ionised gas) = 4th state, BEC = 5th; **liquid crystals** lie between solid and liquid, used in **LCD**.
[1:10:13] - **Air:** inhaled ~**21% O2**, 78% N2, 0.04% CO2; exhaled ~16% O2, 4% CO2.
[1:10:14] - **Chemistry basics:** **OIL RIG** (oxidation = electron loss); hydrogen = 'inflammable air' (**Cavendish 1766**), atomic number 1; **silicon** = 2nd most abundant crustal element after oxygen.
[1:10:15] - **Molecules:** chiral = Greek 'hand'; **alkaloids** contain nitrogen; excess Li makes LiCl **pink** (F-centres); energy-absorbing reactions = **endergonic**, energy-releasing = exergonic.
[1:10:16] - **Physics principles:** **equivalence principle** - basis of **general relativity**, used in GPS time corrections; **Doppler** principle - **red shift** of receding galaxies, Doppler ultrasound; **Huygens'** principle - every point on a wavefront is a source, explains wave propagation, reflection and refraction; **Pascal's** - hydraulic lift, syringe; Archimedes' - buoyancy; Bernoulli's - wing lift; **conservation of momentum** - **gun recoil, rocket propulsion**; **conservation of angular momentum** - an ice-skater spins faster on pulling in the arms, planetary orbits.
[1:10:17] - **Electrical units:** resistance - ohm; **resistivity** - **ohm-metre** (ρ = RA/l); conductance (G = 1/R) - siemens, older unit **mho**; **conductivity** (1/ρ) - **siemens per metre (S/m)**; elementary charge e = **1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C** (1 C = charge of 6.25 × 10¹⁸ electrons); **conventional current** flows from **positive to negative**, opposite to electron flow.
[1:10:18] - **Convex lens** (converging): object **within F** gives a virtual, erect, magnified image (**magnifying glass**); it forms real images in a **camera, compound microscope and telescope**; a **burning glass** is a convex lens that **converges** sun rays to a point.
[1:10:19] - **Myopia** (short-sightedness): the image of a distant object forms **in front of the retina**; corrected by a **concave lens** of **negative power**.
