## 5. Corrections against the old notes
- Old notes said Forward Bloc "April 1939": Spectrum and PMF say **May 1939** (April was the resignation).
- Old notes put **C. Rajagopalachari** on the INA defence team: Spectrum and PMF list Desai, Sapru, Katju, Nehru and Asaf Ali only.
- "Desh Nayak, 1938": Tagore's Deshnayak address and letter are from **1939**.
- The printed key for the Tripuri opponent (Nehru) was wrong: it was **Pattabhi Sitaramayya**.
