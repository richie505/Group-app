## SUB 0 | INA (Red Fort) Trials, 1945-46
[0:0] # Venue, accused, verdict
[0:1] - **Venue:** **Red Fort, Delhi**; first trial opened **5 November 1945**; about ten court-martials, **November 1945 – May 1946**.
[0:2] - **First (joint) trial – the accused:** **Prem Kumar Sahgal** (Hindu), **Shah Nawaz Khan** (Muslim), **Gurbaksh Singh Dhillon** (Sikh) – a symbol of unity (UPSC 2021: they are "officers of the INA").
[0:3] - **Not in the dock:** **Mohan Singh**, Rash Behari Bose (died January 1945), Subhas Bose; Bhulabhai Desai was **counsel**, not an accused.
[0:4] - **Verdict:** guilty of waging war against the King-Emperor → transportation for life, **remitted by C-in-C Claude Auchinleck (January 1946)**; all three released.
[0:5] - **Rashid Ali:** INA officer given a **seven-year** sentence → Calcutta upsurge of **11 February 1946**.
[0:6] - Later lives: Shah Nawaz Khan became a **Union minister**; P.K. Sahgal married **Capt. Lakshmi**.
[0:7] # INA Defence Committee
[0:8] - Set up by the **Indian National Congress** (AICC Bombay, September 1945).
[0:9] - **Lead counsel: Bhulabhai Desai** – his last great contribution; with **Tej Bahadur Sapru, Asaf Ali, K.N. Katju, Jawaharlal Nehru** (also Sarat Chandra Bose).
[0:10] - **Not** in the defence: **Sardar Patel**, **Jinnah**, C. Rajagopalachari; **C.R. Das** and **Motilal Nehru** were dead by then.
[0:11] > Exam angle: venue → **Red Fort, 1945**; accused trio; lead counsel → **Bhulabhai Desai**; 7 years → **Rashid Ali**.

[1:1] - Loyalists, government employees and armed-forces men also sympathised – "an edge of a volcano".

## SUB 2 | Royal Indian Navy Mutiny, February 1946
[2:0] - **Start:** **18 February 1946**, ratings of **HMIS Talwar**, **Bombay**; on 17 Feb they refused a meal of watery, stone-filled food.
[2:1] - **Immediate cause:** **bad food and the racial arrogance / abuse of British officers**.
[2:2] - **Demands:** better food and **living conditions**, civilised treatment, **equal pay** with white sailors, **release of INA prisoners** and political prisoners, withdrawal of Indian troops from Indonesia (Java) and China.
[2:3] - **Spread:** Karachi, Calcutta, Madras, **Visakhapatnam**, Cochin …; about **20,000** ratings, **78 ships**, **20 shore establishments**; RIAF sympathy strikes.
[2:4] - **Flags:** Congress tricolour, League green and Communist red hoisted together.
[2:5] - **Naval Central Strike Committee** – president **M.S. Khan**; **B.C. Dutt** was a Talwar rating (not INA).
[2:6] - **Support:** Communists and **Aruna Asaf Ali**; Congress and League opposed it; **Patel and Jinnah** persuaded the ratings to **surrender on 23 February 1946**.
[2:7] - RIN set up **1934**; ratings got freedom-fighter pensions in **1973**; 80th anniversary **18 February 2026**.
[2:8] > Exam angle: ship → **HMIS Talwar**; reason / immediate demand → **food and treatment**; surrender → **Patel and Jinnah**.

## SUB 3 | Impact on the British Decision; Chronology
[3:0] - INA trials + RIN mutiny showed the British could **no longer rely on the loyalty of the Indian armed forces** (the 2.5-million Indian Army).
[3:1] - **Attlee** (to Justice P.V. Chakraborty): the INA and the RIN mutiny, not Quit India, made the British leave; Gandhi's role "minimal".
[3:2] - RIN mutiny = "last nail in the coffin"; **Cabinet Mission announced 19 February 1946** (a day after), arrived **24 March 1946**.
[3:3] - **20 February 1947:** Attlee – British to leave by **June 1948**.

  [3:4.2] INA Red Fort trials begin | November 1945

[3:5] - So 2nd RTC → Quit India → RIN mutiny = **3-2-1**.
