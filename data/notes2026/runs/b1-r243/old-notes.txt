## SUB 2 | Tripuri Crisis, 1939
[243:2:0] - Contest: moderates vs radicals for the presidency.
[243:2:1] - Bose **defeated Gandhi-backed Dr. Pattabhi Sitaramayya** (**1,580 to 1,377**) - APPSC 2018.
[243:2:2] - Gandhi: **"Pattabhi's defeat is my defeat."**
[243:2:3] - **Dispute:** over the **formation of the Congress Working Committee**; old guard refused to cooperate.
[243:2:4] - **Pant Resolution:** Bose to name the Working Committee **according to Gandhi's wishes**.
[243:2:5] - **Outcome:** Working Committee deadlock → Bose **resigned (April 1939)** → **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** became President.
[243:2:6] - Bose was removed from party positions in **August 1939**.
## SUB 3 | Forward Bloc
[243:3:0] - Founded by **Subhas Chandra Bose**, **1939** (April), after resigning the presidency.
[243:3:1] - **Aim:** a bloc **within Congress** to unite **radical, left-wing and anti-imperialist** forces.
[243:3:2] - **Sheel Bhadra Yajee** (Bihar) joined Bose in founding the All India Forward Bloc; also linked to the INA.
[243:3:3] # Andhra Forward Bloc - Madduri Annapurnaiah
[243:3:4] - **Madduri Annapurnaiah** - **first Andhra leader to join**; led it in Andhra with **Peta Bapayya** (APPSC 2018, G2 2019).
[243:3:5] - Title: **"Andhra Netaji"**; born **1899**, **Pithapuram** taluk.
[243:3:6] - Jailed **18 months** for the article **"Chicharapidugu"**; **4 years** in jail as a Forward Bloc member in WWII.
[243:3:7] - Later edited **Jai Bharat, Navashakti, Velugu**.
## SUB 4 | Bose Abroad - Escape, Radio, Legion
[243:4:0] # Escape Route
[243:4:1] - **January 1941**: Calcutta → **Peshawar → Kabul → Moscow → Berlin**.
[243:4:2] - Saw the war as a godsend: **"England's need is India's opportunity"** (like "Need of England is an opportunity for Ireland").
[243:4:3] # In Germany
[243:4:4] TABLE head: Organisation | Fact
  [243:4:4.0] Free India Centre | Berlin, with Hitler's approval
  [243:4:4.1] Free Indian Legion | Raised by Bose from Indian POWs captured by Rommel in North Africa
  [243:4:4.2] Azad Hind Radio | Started in Germany, broadcast from Berlin (APPSC 2018)
[243:4:5] - Bose reached Germany on **3 April 1941**.
[243:4:6] - **Early 1943:** German submarine → Japanese submarine → **Singapore, July 1943**; reached **Tokyo June 1943**.
[243:4:7] Azad Hind Radio = **Germany**, not Japan.
## SUB 5 | Indian National Army (azad Hind Fauj)
[243:5:0] # First INA, 1942
[243:5:1] - Idea of **Giani Pritam Singh Dhillon** with **Capt. Mohan Singh** and **Major Iwaichi Fujiwara** (head of **F-Kikan**).
[243:5:2] - **Capt. Mohan Singh**: **first commander**; INA formed from **Indian POWs after the fall of Singapore** (February 1942) with Japanese help; formally raised **1 September 1942** (~40,000 volunteers; first division **16,300** men).
[243:5:3] - Formed in **Singapore** (then part of British **Malaya**), South-East Asia.
[243:5:4] - **IIL:** **Rash Behari Bose** (Japan) - **Tokyo conference 28-30 March 1942**; **Bangkok Convention (June 1942)** met the Japanese to set up the INA; IIL = political wing of INA.
[243:5:5] - **Dissolved December 1942**.
[243:5:6] **Rash Behari Bose** (IIL, Japan) is NOT **Rash Behari Ghosh** (Moderate).
[243:5:7] # Second Phase under Subhas Bose, 1943
[243:5:8] - **4 July 1943:** Rash Behari Bose handed IIL/INA charge to Subhas Bose.
[243:5:9] - **25 August 1943:** Bose became **Supreme Commander** of the INA.
[243:5:10] - **HQ:** Singapore initially, later **Rangoon**.
[243:5:11] - **Rani of Jhansi Regiment** (1943): **all-women combat unit**, under **Capt. Lakshmi Sahgal**; named after Rani Lakshmi Bai.
[243:5:12] - **Lakshmi Sahgal:** contested the **2002 presidential election**, lost to **A.P.J. Abdul Kalam**; mother **Ammu Swaminathan** was a Constituent Assembly member
[243:5:13] - Rash Behari Bose: "**Avenge the blood of Indian Martyrs**... Stand up against the entire Anglo-American enemy".
[243:5:14] # Campaigns and Ethos
[243:5:15] - **Imphal-Kohima campaign (1944):** INA flag hoisted on Indian soil at **Moirang, Manipur, 14 April 1944**; retreat after Japanese defeats and supply shortages.
[243:5:16] - **INA motto:** **"Ittefaq, Itmad, Qurbani"** (Unity, Faith, Sacrifice).
[243:5:17] - **INA anthem:** Hindustani **Jana Gana Mana** by **Abid Hasan 'Safrani'**.
[243:5:18] - **Order:** first INA (1942) → Bose takes command (July 1943) → Azad Hind Government (21 Oct 1943) → Red Fort trials (Nov 1945).
[243:5:19] - **Other name:** The **Indian National Army (INA)** was also called the **Azad Hind Fauj**; it was the army of the Azad Hind Government, which was a separate body.
## SUB 6 | Azad Hind Government
[243:6:0] - **Provisional Government of Free India** (**Arzi Hukumat-e-Azad Hind**), proclaimed **21 October 1943** at **Singapore** (not Rangoon).
[243:6:1] - **Recognised by 9 countries** - Axis powers **Japan, Germany, Italy** and allies (**Burma, Thailand, Philippines, Manchukuo**).
[243:6:2] - **Declared war on Britain and the USA**.
[243:6:3] - A separate government; INA was its army.

  [243:6:4.2] Swaraj Dweep | New name of Nicobar
  [243:6:4.3] Port Blair, 30 Dec 1943 | Bose hoisted the tricolour
[243:6:5] - **Jan 2026:** PM marked the **82nd anniversary** of the Port Blair tricolour.
[243:6:6] Andaman | Shaheed | Martyr
Nicobar | Swaraj | Self-rule
[243:6:7] - **Proclamation:** The Azad Hind proclamation **guaranteed religious liberty** and **equal rights and equal opportunities** to all citizens.
## SUB 7 | Red Fort Trials and Rin Mutiny
[243:7:0] # INA Trials, November 1945
[243:7:1] - Venue: **Red Fort, Delhi**; joint trial 1945-46.
[243:7:2] - Accused: **Shah Nawaz Khan, Prem Kumar Sehgal, Gurbaksh Singh Dhillon**.
[243:7:3] - **Defence led by Bhulabhai Desai** with **Tej Bahadur Sapru, Nehru, Asaf Ali, K.N. Katju**; also C. Rajagopalachari.
[243:7:4] - NOT in the defence: **Sardar Patel**. Did NOT face the trials: **Mohan Singh**.
[243:7:5] - **Rashid Ali** sentenced to seven years.
[243:7:6] - Linked to INA: Shah Nawaz Khan, P.K. Sahgal, **Shaukat Ali Malik**; NOT linked: **Major Kartar Singh, B.C. Dutta**.
[243:7:7] - Shah Nawaz Khan later became a **Union minister**; Prem Kumar Sehgal married **Captain Lakshmi**.
[243:7:8] # Royal Indian Navy Mutiny, 1946
[243:7:9] - Began **18 February 1946** when ratings of **HMIS Talwar**, Bombay, refused duty (food, mistreatment).
[243:7:10] - **Naval Central Strike Committee** led by rating **M.S. Khan**; spread to Karachi and other ports.
[243:7:11] - Bombay ships surrendered **23 February 1946**.
[243:7:12] INA trials + RIN Mutiny showed the British could **no longer rely on the loyalty of Indian armed forces**.

[243:9:6] - **Wardha CWC 14 July**; **AICC at Gowalia Tank Maidan, Bombay, 8 August 1942** (not 6 August); arrests **9 August**.
[243:9:7] - Gandhi at **Aga Khan Palace, Pune**; CWC at **Ahmednagar Fort**.
[243:9:8] - Movement became leaderless and violent (railways, telegraphs, police stations).
