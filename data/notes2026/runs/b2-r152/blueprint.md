## 4. Trends and gaps
- The UN is the heavyweight (more than half of the PYQs): basics repeat every few years – P5, 15 members, six languages, UN Day, ICJ 15/9 years, present SG.
- Exercises with partner names are the second pattern (Yudh Abhyas, Tiger Triumph, Garuda, IMBEX, Dustlik) – often with the venue.
- Many APPSC "relations" PYQs are really current-affairs one-liners of their year (2017-2020).
- Heavy in the old notes but never asked: 2025-26 tariffs, Mission 500, iCET/TRUST, IPOI pillars, island projects, UNSC reform groups beyond G4, 2026 summit numbers (kept for the own MCQs only, in short form).
- Key corrections: the old notes put Yudh Abhyas 2009 in Madhya Pradesh – Babina is in Jhansi district, Uttar Pradesh; Russia's "Special and Privileged" partnership dates from 2010, not 2000.
