## SUB 2 | Status and US Doctrines
[2:0] - Present status: **Comprehensive Global Strategic Partnership**.
[2:1] - The **2008 civil nuclear deal** = turning point in India-US ties.
[2:2] TABLE head: Doctrine / law | Year | Purpose
  [2:2.0] Pressler Amendment | 1985 | Stopped US aid to Pakistan unless the President certified it had no nuclear device; applied in 1990
  [2:2.1] Pivot to Asia / Rebalance | 2011 (Obama era) | USA's foreign-policy shift of focus to the Asia-Pacific
  [2:2.2] CAATSA | 2017 | Sanctions on buyers of Russian arms; India sought a waiver over the S-400
[2:3] - **Larry Pressler** = US Senator; linked aid to non-proliferation; **criticised the proposed US military help to Pakistan** (Clinton era) over its nuclear programme.
[2:4] > Exam angle: Pressler targeted Pakistan, not India; 'Pivot to Asia' is a US plan.

## SUB 3 | Diaspora and Visas
[3:0] - US has the world's **largest PIO community** (Malaysia second) (Feb 2026).
[3:1] - 2020 Census: about **4.4 million** "Indian-alone" – largest Asian-alone group.
[3:2] - **Remittances**: US is the largest source – **27.7%** (RBI survey, 2023-24), ahead of the UAE.
[3:3] - **Telugu** = fastest-growing foreign language in the US (2010-17).
[3:4] - **Dalip Singh Saund:** first **Indian American** (and first Asian American) elected to the US Congress – House of Representatives, California, **1956**.
[3:5] - **Pramila Jayapal** (Washington, 2016): **first Indian-American woman elected to the US House of Representatives** (Kamala Harris – Senate, then Vice President; Ami Bera – a man).
[3:6] - **H-1B**: **June 2023** (PM Modi's state visit) – the **US** announced **in-country renewal** of H-1B visas (pilot); **$100,000 fee** on new petitions from **21 Sept 2025**; Indians get about **71%** of approved H-1Bs.
