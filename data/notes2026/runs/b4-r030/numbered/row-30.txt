## SUB 4 | Watershed Management and Water Schemes
[4:0] - **Watershed development** = soil and moisture conservation of a whole catchment: **earthworks, soil conservation and tree planting** to conserve moisture and raise groundwater recharge – its hallmark in semi-arid India (UPSC 1994).
[4:1] - **IWMP** (2009-10): run by the **Department of Land Resources** (MoRD); **merged DPAP, DDP and IWDP**; key objective – **agricultural productivity and water conservation**.
[4:2] - IWMP/IWDP benefits: **prevents soil run-off, rainwater harvesting, groundwater recharge, regenerates vegetation** – NOT linking perennial and seasonal rivers.
[4:3] - **DPAP and DDP** = Department of Land Resources (MoRD), not Agriculture or Environment; **NWDPRA** = **Ministry of Agriculture**, not Rural Development (so none of the UPSC 2014 pairs is correct).
[4:4] - IWMP merged into **PMKSY (2015)** as the **Watershed Development Component**; CAD&WM is a PMKSY component.
[4:5] - **Hermann Bacher** (Swiss priest, WOTR) – **Father of community-led watershed development** in India.
[4:6] - **Neeranchal** – World Bank-assisted watershed project; **Watershed Development Fund** – NABARD.
[4:7] TABLE head: Scheme | Key facts
  [4:7.0] Hariyali (27 Jan 2003) | Vajpayee; Centrally sponsored watershed and wasteland development through Panchayati Raj Institutions – for the rural population, not urban

## SUB 5 | Forest Resources and Forest Movements
[5:0] - **Forests are renewable**; that they improve the environment is true but does not explain why they are renewable.

[5:2] - **FAO (Global Forest Resources Assessment 2010):** net forest loss fell from **8.3 million ha/yr (1990s)** to **5.2 million ha/yr (2000–2010)**.
[5:3] - Highest share of area under forest among China, India, Indonesia, Japan: **Japan** (about two-thirds).

[7:5] - Fallow ladder: current fallow ≤ 1 yr; other fallow 1–5 yrs; culturable wasteland > 5 yrs. Highest net-sown share: Punjab and Haryana.
[7:6] - **India's land use (approx.):** net sown area **~46%**, forests **~23%**, other areas ~31% (2022-23: 45.9 / 23.5 / 30.6).
[7:7] - **Trends (NCERT):** forest, non-agricultural use, current fallow and net sown area have **increased**; **barren and wasteland, pastures and tree crops**, culturable waste and other fallow have **declined**; non-agricultural use has risen fastest. Forest area has **not** declined.
[7:8] - AP (undivided, 2006-07): current fallow about **6.02 lakh ha** (printed key; figure not verified).
[7:9] - **Wasteland:** degraded land restorable with reasonable effort; mapped by satellite (IRS) National Wastelands Monitoring.
[7:10] - Land degradation: erosion, waterlogging, salinisation, nutrient loss, falling water tables; UNCCD (**1994**) – desertification in arid, semi-arid and dry sub-humid areas.
[7:11] - Milestones: Club of Rome (Apr 1968), Limits to Growth (1972), Brundtland "Our Common Future" (1987).

[8:1] - **DMF:** non-profit trust in every mining district; purpose – **protect the interests and benefit of persons and areas affected by mining** (it neither promotes exploration nor issues licences); funded by miners (30% of royalty for old leases, 10% for auctioned leases), not the Consolidated Fund; runs PMKKKY.
