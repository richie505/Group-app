# Project Facts
- **Sardar Sarovar:** part of the Narmada Valley Project; **MP gets the largest share of power (57%)**, Gujarat most of the irrigation; **Medha Patkar** founded the **Narmada Bachao Andolan**, which opposed raising its height.
- **Chambal Project:** **Rajasthan and MP** (Gandhi Sagar, Rana Pratap Sagar, Jawahar Sagar, Kota barrage); Chambal is the main tributary of the Yamuna, so the Chambal Canal project belongs to the **Yamuna basin**.
- **Beas Project:** Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan; **Pong** dam keeps water flowing to the **Indira Gandhi Canal**. **Mahi Bajaj Sagar:** Rajasthan and Gujarat.
- A dam on the Sutlej in Himachal Pradesh above Bhakra was built to **check silt** flowing into the Bhakra reservoir.
- **DVC (1948):** statutory body run jointly by the **Centre and the governments of West Bengal and Jharkhand**.
- **States by number of large dams (NRLD):** **Maharashtra (most) > Madhya Pradesh > Gujarat**; Rajasthan comes before Telangana.
- **After bifurcation (2014):** AP looks after Srisailam, Pulichintala and Prakasam barrage; **Telangana claims Nagarjuna Sagar and Jurala**.

=== Watershed Management and Water Schemes
- **Watershed development** = soil and moisture conservation of a whole catchment: **earthworks, soil conservation and tree planting** to conserve moisture and raise groundwater recharge – its hallmark in semi-arid India (UPSC 1994).
- **IWMP** (2009-10): run by the **Department of Land Resources** (MoRD); **merged DPAP, DDP and IWDP**; key objective – **agricultural productivity and water conservation**.
- IWMP/IWDP benefits: **prevents soil run-off, rainwater harvesting, groundwater recharge, regenerates vegetation** – NOT linking perennial and seasonal rivers.
- **DPAP and DDP** = Department of Land Resources (MoRD), not Agriculture or Environment; **NWDPRA** = **Ministry of Agriculture**, not Rural Development (so none of the UPSC 2014 pairs is correct).
- IWMP merged into **PMKSY (2015)** as the **Watershed Development Component**; CAD&WM is a PMKSY component.
- **Hermann Bacher** (Swiss priest, WOTR) – **Father of community-led watershed development** in India.
- **Neeranchal** – World Bank-assisted watershed project; **Watershed Development Fund** – NABARD.
| Scheme | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Hariyali (27 Jan 2003)** | Vajpayee; Centrally sponsored **watershed** and **wasteland development** through **Panchayati Raj Institutions** – for the **rural** population, not urban |
| PMKSY (2015-16) | AIBP = faster completion of major/medium projects; Har Khet Ko Pani = CAD&WM, minor irrigation, RRR of water bodies |
| **Jal Shakti Abhiyan (2019)** | Water conservation and water security campaign; **Phase 1: 1 Jul–15 Sep 2019**; **Phase 2: 1 Oct–30 Nov 2019** for states of the NE (retreating) monsoon – AP, Karnataka, Puducherry, TN |
| Atal Bhujal Yojana | First community-led CSS on demand-side groundwater management |
| Namami Gange (2014); NRCP (1995) | Ganga main stem; other rivers |

- AP (Aug 2026): Pattiseema lift water fed the **Krishna delta** in the El Niño deficit; **Gandikota** reservoir on the **Pennar** serves Rayalaseema.

=== Forest Resources and Forest Movements
- **Forests are renewable**; that they improve the environment is true but does not explain why they are renewable.
- **Forest dieback:** a stand of trees losing health and dying **without an obvious cause**.
- **FAO (Global Forest Resources Assessment 2010):** net forest loss fell from **8.3 million ha/yr (1990s)** to **5.2 million ha/yr (2000–2010)**.
- Highest share of area under forest among China, India, Indonesia, Japan: **Japan** (about two-thirds).
| Movement | Facts |
|---|---|
| Chipko | Himalayas (Garhwal, 1973) |
| **Appiko** | **Karnataka** – Salkani forest, Sirsi, Uttara Kannada, 8 Sep 1983; modelled on Chipko; aims – **save the forests of the Western Ghats**, restore greenery to denuded areas, rational use of forest resources (NOT cleaning rivers) |
- **NFP 1988:** 33% of the country under forest, **two-thirds in hills**; formally introduced JFM (Centre's guidelines 1990; forerunner **Arabari, West Bengal, 1972**).
- **Forest Rights Act 2006:** Gram Sabhas manage community forest resources. **National Agroforestry Policy 2014** – India's first.
- Terms: silviculture (raising and tending trees), agroforestry (trees with crops or livestock), jhum (NE shifting cultivation; shorter fallow → degradation and erosion).
- **ISFR 2023:** largest forest area – **Madhya Pradesh** (77,073 sq km).
- World: **Amazon** – largest continuous rainforest (Congo second); **taiga** – largest terrestrial biome; Bhutan – Constitution keeps ≥ 60% under forest; Canada – largest softwood and pulp exporter; dense forests lie near the equator.

# Land Use
- **Nine-fold classification:** forests; non-agricultural uses; barren and unculturable; permanent pastures; miscellaneous tree crops; culturable wasteland; current fallow; other fallow; net sown area ("seasonal plantation reserve" is not one).
- Fallow ladder: current fallow ≤ 1 yr; other fallow 1–5 yrs; culturable wasteland > 5 yrs. Highest net-sown share: Punjab and Haryana.
- **India's land use (approx.):** net sown area **~46%**, forests **~23%**, other areas ~31% (2022-23: 45.9 / 23.5 / 30.6).
- **Trends (NCERT):** forest, non-agricultural use, current fallow and net sown area have **increased**; **barren and wasteland, pastures and tree crops**, culturable waste and other fallow have **declined**; non-agricultural use has risen fastest. Forest area has **not** declined.
- AP (undivided, 2006-07): current fallow about **6.02 lakh ha** (printed key; figure not verified).
- **Wasteland:** degraded land restorable with reasonable effort; mapped by satellite (IRS) National Wastelands Monitoring.
- Land degradation: erosion, waterlogging, salinisation, nutrient loss, falling water tables; UNCCD (**1994**) – desertification in arid, semi-arid and dry sub-humid areas.
- Milestones: Club of Rome (Apr 1968), Limits to Growth (1972), Brundtland "Our Common Future" (1987).

=== Mineral Policy – MMDR Act, DMF and Deep-sea Minerals
| Act / amendment | Provision |
|---|---|
| **MMDR Act 1957** | Parent law of mining (Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, Act 67 of 1957); it replaced the **1948** Mines and Minerals (Regulation and Development) Act |
| 2015 | Auction mandatory; **District Mineral Foundation** (Sec 9B); NMET |
| 2023 | Centre auctions critical and strategic minerals (lithium); exploration licence |
| 2026 (14 Aug) | States may tax mineral rights / lands only within limits set by the Centre |
- **DMF:** non-profit trust in every mining district; purpose – **protect the interests and benefit of persons and areas affected by mining** (it neither promotes exploration nor issues licences); funded by miners (30% of royalty for old leases, 10% for auctioned leases), not the Consolidated Fund; runs PMKKKY.
- **MADA v. SAIL (2024, nine judges):** royalty is not a tax; States tax mineral rights (Entry 50) and mineral land (Entry 49).
- India: diamonds – Panna (MP); chromite – Sukinda (Odisha); gold – Jonnagiri (Kurnool, AP); Peninsular plateau = mineral heartland.
- **Deep sea:** India first to sign an **ISA** contract for **polymetallic sulphides** on the **Carlsberg Ridge** (2025); sulphides – Cu, Zn, Pb, Fe, Ag, Au; nodules – Mn, Co, Ni, Cu. ISA – Jamaica; ITLOS – Hamburg.
