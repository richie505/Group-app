=== New Current Affairs: 5–14 Nov 2026
# 31st CII Partnership Summit – Visakhapatnam (AP)
- **31st CII Partnership Summit**: **Visakhapatnam**, **12–13 November 2026** (Andhra University Engineering College grounds); organised by **CII with DPIIT** (Ministry of Commerce and Industry) and the **Government of Andhra Pradesh**; held annually since **1995**; the **30th edition (2025)** was also in Visakhapatnam.
- Theme (CivicCentre / South First): "**Navigating New World Order: Trade, Technology, Trust**"; delegates from about **58 countries**; partner nations Singapore, UAE, Japan, Russia, Slovakia, Guatemala; **Amit Shah** scheduled for the inaugural session, **VP C. P. Radhakrishnan** for the valedictory.
- Outcome: **109 MoUs worth ₹9.02 lakh crore**, potential **over 2 lakh jobs**; pre-summit regional conferences had signed **194 MoUs (₹86,522 crore)**; AP earmarked **5,000 acres near Tirupati for the space sector** (Agnikul Cosmos ₹510 crore, Hathor Rockets ₹250 crore).
- On the sidelines: CM Naidu proposed a **WIPO–Andhra Pradesh Academy** to WIPO DG **Daren Tang** (IPR training; WIPO help for **GI tags** of AP handlooms and handicrafts).
- **WIPO**: Convention signed at **Stockholm, 14 July 1967**, in force **26 April 1970**, UN specialised agency **1974**; HQ **Geneva**; **194 members**; **India joined 1975**.
- Japan–AP: a **second Japanese Industrial Cluster** in AP (first Japanese Industrial Township = **Sri City**); **JERA**–AM Green deal for **70,000 tonnes a year of green ammonia from Kakinada**.
# COP31 – Antalya, Türkiye
- **COP31** of UNFCCC: **Antalya, Türkiye**, **9–20 November 2026**; Türkiye hosts a COP **for the first time**; **COP31 President: Murat Kurum** (Türkiye's Environment Minister); **Australia's Chris Bowen = "President of Negotiations"** – the compromise reached at **COP30, Belém (Brazil), 2025**.
- **"35-by-35" Global Electrification Pledge**: flagship of the **Turkish COP31 presidency**, introduced at the **Bonn conference, June 2026**; raise electricity's share of global final energy use from ~20–23% to **35% by 2035**; **voluntary, non-binding**; **India had not decided** to join (Nov 2026).
- Earlier pledge: **Global Renewables and Energy Efficiency Pledge (COP28, Dubai)** – **triple renewable capacity to 11,000 GW by 2030** and **double** the efficiency-gain rate (about 2% to over 4% a year).
- **UNFCCC**: adopted **9 May 1992** (New York), opened at the **Rio Earth Summit**; in force **21 March 1994**; **198 Parties**; India ratified **1 November 1993**.
# Other summits and conferences
- **G7 Foreign Ministers**: **Paris, 10–11 Nov 2026** (France = 2026 G7 presidency; **52nd summit 2026 France**, **53rd 2027 USA**); G7 has **no legal existence or permanent secretariat**.
- **ICAPP** (International Conference of Asian Political Parties): **13th General Assembly, Seoul**, unanimously adopted the **Seoul Declaration** (sovereignty, territorial integrity, UN Charter); ICAPP launched in **Manila, September 2000**; General Assembly **every two years**; BJP and INC take part.
- **BOB-NOR** (**Bay of Bengal Network of Ocean Researchers for Fisheries**): launched by **India, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh and the Maldives** at a meeting hosted by **ICAR-CMFRI**; these four are the members of **BOBP-IGO (2003)** – Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar, Thailand are cooperating non-contracting parties.
- **First-ever BIMSTEC Maritime Security Exercise**: hosted by India at **Sri Vijaya Puram (Andaman and Nicobar)**, **30 Oct – 3 Nov 2026**; theme "**Shared Seas, Shared Security and Shared Prosperity**"; all **7 members** took part.
- **BRICS Sustainable Lifestyle Week**: **1–7 November 2026**, observed in **Andhra Pradesh** under **India's BRICS Presidency** and **Mission LiFE** (launched by PM Modi at **COP26, Glasgow, 2021**); one **Mission LiFE theme per day** (Day 1 healthy lifestyles … Day 5 single-use plastic, Day 6 e-waste, Day 7 general waste).
- **FATF plenary** (Oct–Nov 2026) agreed to step up action on new financial-crime threats; FATF set up by the **G7 in 1989**; **40 members** (38 jurisdictions + **European Commission** and **GCC**); **India joined 2010**; Secretariat at **OECD HQ, Paris**; **black list: Iran, North Korea, Myanmar**.
# Agreements and calendar
- **Horizon Europe**: India and the EU **concluded negotiations** (Nov 2026) for India's **association** with the EU's research and innovation programme (**2021–2027**; **23 associated countries**; successor 2028–2034 proposed at €175 billion).
- **India–Saudi Arabia Haj Agreement 2027**: India's quota **1,75,025** pilgrims.
- **Coalition of the Willing** (for Ukraine; set up **March 2025** by the **UK and France**; 35 nations): first exercise **"Koalicja"** in **Poland** (about 2,800 soldiers from 11 countries) for its **Multinational Force–Ukraine** (Jan 2026, HQ Paris; **not a NATO force**).
- **Ganga Waters Treaty** (India–Bangladesh, **1996**, **H. D. Deve Gowda – Sheikh Hasina**): sharing at **Farakka**, expires **12 December 2026**; each side guaranteed **35,000 cusecs** in alternate 10-day periods (11 March – 10 May).
- Next (scheduled): **49th ASEAN Summit** and Related Summits, **Manila** (Philippines chair), mid-November 2026 – the **ASEAN DEFA** (first binding region-wide digital-economy deal) due; **33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting**, **Shenzhen, China, 18–19 Nov 2026**, theme "**Building an Asia-Pacific Community to Prosper Together**".
> ⚠ Trap: COP31 is **hosted and presided by Türkiye**, but **Australia** runs the negotiations; COP30 was Belém; the "35-by-35" pledge is about **electrification**, not renewables tripling (that is COP28).
