## SUB 0 | Sex Ratio – National Trend, Child Sex Ratio and Causes
[0:0] # Definition and Census 2011 figures
[0:1] - **Sex ratio:** number of **females per 1,000 males**; "male : female" questions write it as **1000 : 943**.
[0:2] - **Census 2011:** final **943** (provisional **940**) – rural **949**, urban **929**; 2001: **933** (1000 : 933).
[0:3] - **Child sex ratio (0-6):** 2011 final **919** (provisional **914**); 2001: 927.
[0:4] > 940 vs 943 and 914 vs 919 are the provisional vs final traps: a "provisional figures" stem wants 940 / 914; when only 940 is offered, key 940.
[0:5] # Trend 1901-2011
[0:6] TABLE head: Census | Sex ratio | Change
  [0:6.0] 1901 | 972 | –
  [0:6.1] 1941 | 945 | −5
  [0:6.2] 1951 | 946 | +1
  [0:6.3] 1961 → 1971 | 941 → 930 | −11 (sharpest decline)
  [0:6.4] 1981 | 934 | +4
  [0:6.5] 1991 | 927 (all-time low) | −7
  [0:6.6] 2001 | 933 | +6
  [0:6.7] 2011 | 943 | +10
[0:7] - Long run **declining with ups and downs** (rises in 1951, 1981, 2001, 2011) – keys call the trend **"fluctuating"**; the 1991 figure itself was a fall (934 → 927).
[0:8] - **Child sex ratio** has **fallen continuously** since 1961: **976 (1961), 964 (1971), 962 (1981), 945 (1991), 927 (2001), 919 (2011)**.
[0:9] # Causes of the low sex ratio
[0:10] - **Female foeticide / sex-determination tests** (the fear for the world sex ratio), **high maternal mortality**, **neglect and higher death rate of girl children**, and the natural **excess of male births** (about 105 boys per 100 girls) – all four are counted.
[0:11] - **More boys than girls are born** each year in India (so "more female children are born" is false); boys have the higher biological (neonatal) death risk.
[0:12] - **NFHS-5 (2019-21):** overall sex ratio **1,020** (NFHS-4: 991) – first time more women than men; **sex ratio at birth 929** (up from 919).
[0:13] # International
[0:14] - 2011 decreasing order: **Bangladesh > Pakistan > India > China**; most favourable among Sweden, Switzerland, Netherlands, Finland: **Finland**.

## SUB 4 | Population Growth – Phases, Decadal Record and the Great Divide
[4:0] # Phases
[4:1] TABLE head: Phase | Feature
  [4:1.0] 1901-21 | Stagnant (stationary); population fell in 1911-21
  [4:1.1] 1921 | "Year of the Great Divide" / "Demographic Divide"
  [4:1.2] 1921-51 | Steady growth
  [4:1.3] 1951-81 | Rapid growth / population explosion (1961-71 the peak decade)

## SUB 5 | Census – History, Organisation and India at a Glance 2011
[5:0] - **Census:** official enumeration of population, every **10 years**; **first attempted 1872** (non-synchronous, operations 1867-72; often loosely "1871-72"); **first complete / synchronous 1881**, regular since.
[5:1] - **Census Organisation:** under the **Ministry of Home Affairs** – **Registrar General and Census Commissioner of India**, on a permanent footing since **1961**; Census Act 1948.
[5:2] - **Census 2011:** **15th** national Census (7th after Independence); motto **"Our Census, Our Future"**; first with a **"No religion"** category.
