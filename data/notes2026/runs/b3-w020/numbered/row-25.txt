## SUB 0 | Growth Record – Real GDP Growth Year by Year
[0:0] # The long run
[0:1] - **First Plan (1951-56):** achieved growth **3.6%** against a 2.1% target – the figure APPSC 2019 keys as "GDP growth 1950-51" (strictly it is the First Plan average, not a single-year rate).
[0:2] - **1950-80:** about **3.5%** a year (NextGen: 3.52%) = **"Hindu rate of growth"**; 1980s: **5.13%** a year.
[0:3] - Trend is **not a steady rise**: contractions in **1979-80** (drought + oil shock) and **2020-21** (COVID-19), then a **sharp rebound in 2021-22**.
[0:4] - **Since 1991:** the economy grew **"almost 12 times"** – the APPSC key (2018, 2020); in US $ terms it is about **10 times** ($270 bn in 1991 → $2.7-2.9 trillion by 2019-20).
[0:5] - **IAS 2010:** GDP did **not** rise four-fold in 2000-10 (about three-fold at current prices); the **public sector's share of GDP declined** after liberalisation.

## SUB 5 | Per Capita Income – Meaning, Level and Slow Growth
[5:0] - **PCI = National income ÷ Population** (NextGen).
[5:1] - At **current prices** PCI rises faster than at **constant prices** (constant prices remove inflation).
[5:2] - **Per capita NNI 2025-26** (FAE, Jan 2026, current prices): **₹2,47,487**, up ₹16,025; the 2026 new series **lowered** nominal GDP and PCI.
[5:3] - **2016-17:** per capita NNI ₹1,03,219 (+9.7%); **2017-18:** PCI at current prices up about **8.6%** (CSO provisional).
[5:4] - **2006-07 (UPSC 2008):** per capita GDP at current prices about **₹37,000**; wheat about **75 mt**; rice about **93 mt** (not 180) → A and C.
[5:5] - **Reasons for slow growth of real PCI (UPPCS 2001):** rapid **population growth**, **high price rise**, **slow growth of agriculture and industry**, and (before 1991) **scarcity of foreign exchange**; low farm productivity and a large unorganised sector – a **rising services share is not a cause**.
[5:6] - **Size 2025-26:** about **₹345 lakh crore ≈ US $3.9 trillion** (new series); earlier estimate ₹357 lakh crore.
[5:7] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 11 p.17 (PCI = NI ÷ population); NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 12 p.8; MoSPI PE 31 May 2017 (PCI ₹1,03,219, via Tribune); app notes 3:25 sub 3
## SUB 6 | Growth Concepts – Hindu Rate, Miracle Economies, Middle-income Trap
[6:0] - **Hindu rate of growth:** about **3.5%** a year in **1950-80**; term coined by **Raj Krishna**.
[6:1] - **Miracle economies:** GDP growth of **7%** for a **decade or more** (NextGen) – not 8, 8.5 or 7.5%.
[6:2] - **Middle-income trap:** countries that rose from low to middle income but stagnated there; causes = **poor infrastructure, weak institutions, unfavourable growth conditions** – **climate change is not** one of the listed causes.
[6:3] - **GDP does not measure income distribution**; dollar rankings move with exchange rates and base revisions.
[6:4] - **World Bank income groups:** low, lower-middle, upper-middle, high; WDR 1994 low income = per capita GNP **US $725 or less**.
[6:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC 02 p.33 (miracle economies 7%); NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 12 p.11; NextGen Rocket Sheets Economy p.98 (Hindu rate – Raj Krishna); app notes 3:25 sub 0
