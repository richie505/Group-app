=== Who Compiles National Income – CSO to NSO (MoSPI)
- National income was computed by the **Central Statistical Organisation (CSO)** – the key in every older PYQ; not the NSSO (surveys), ISI, NCAER, Indian Standards Institution, RBI, Finance Ministry or Planning Commission.
- **2019**: CSO + NSSO merged into the **National Statistical Office (NSO)** under **MoSPI**, headed by the **Chief Statistician of India** (also Secretary, MoSPI) – NSO now releases GDP, GNI, NNI and per capita income (UP RO/ARO 2023).
- **CSO** grew out of the Central Statistical Unit (1949) and was set up on **2 May 1951**; National Accounts Division = GDP, consumption, capital formation; **Economic Statistics Division (ESD) compiled the IIP**; Industrial Statistics Wing, Kolkata conducts the **ASI**.
- **P.C. Mahalanobis** = **architect of independent India's statistical system** – first Statistical Adviser to the Cabinet (Jan 1949), founder of the **ISI (1931)** and the **NSS (1950)**; P.V. Sukhatme built agricultural statistics.
| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| NSC (2006) | Part-time Chairperson + 4 part-time members; CEO of NITI Aayog ex officio |
| ISI | Autonomous, ISI Act 1959 |
| State DES | State Domestic Product |
| NSO | Follows IMF **SDDS**; nodal agency for the **ICP**; not monetary policy |
- **Economic Census:** the **6th Economic Census** fieldwork ran **January 2013 – April 2014** (MoSPI).

=== Base Year – Meaning and Revisions
- **Base year** = year whose prices value real GDP (constant prices); index base = 100; revised every 5-10 years.
- Sequence: **1948-49, 1960-61, 1970-71, 1980-81, 1993-94, 1999-2000, 2004-05, 2011-12, 2022-23** – nine base years; **2009-10 was never a base year**.
| Series | When released | Base it replaced |
|---|---|---|
| 1993-94 | **Feb 1999** (UPSC 2000) | 1980-81 |
| 1999-2000 | 2006 | 1993-94 (still "present" base at UPPCS 2009) |
| 2004-05 | Jan 2010 | 1999-2000 |
| **2011-12** | **Jan 2015** | **2004-05** (APPSC 2012-2023 and UPPCS 2015-16 asked this 5 times) |
| **2022-23** | **27 Feb 2026** (current) | 2011-12 |
- **2015 change = both base year and method** (RAS 2013 re-exam): GDP at factor cost dropped; headline = **GVA at basic prices**; GDP at market prices; **MCA-21** corporate database partly replaced ASI.
- **AP GSDP base**: 1999-2000 at the 2008 exam; AP still reports at **2011-12** (APSES 2024-25), moving to 2022-23.
- Other new bases (Feb 2026): IIP **2022-23**; CPI **2024** (from calendar 2012); first new CPI print **2.75%** (Jan 2026).
- **Real income** = current income × 100 / price index; e.g. Rs 30,000 crore × 100 / 150 = **Rs 20,000 crore**.
~~ Sources: NextGen Rocket Sheets Economy p.39 (base year removes price changes); MoSPI press releases Jan 2015 and 27 Feb 2026 (new series); RAS 2013 explanation (base year and method changed); app notes 3:24 sub 3, 4

=== 2011-12 and 2022-23 Series – Changes and Data Debates
- **2011-12 series criticism:** manufacturing growth not matching physical indicators; private corporate sector overestimated via MCA-21. **Arvind Subramanian** (former CEA, 2019): select indicators and GDP growth **"flipped" post-2011**; 2011-17 growth about **4.5%**, not ~7%.
- **2022-23 series** (NSO, 27 Feb 2026): follows **UN SNA 2025**; 2022-23 chosen as a "normal" year (not 2017-18 GST, 2019-21 COVID, 2021-22 base effects).
- Changes: **double deflation**; **Supply and Use Tables**; GST, e-Vahan, PFMS data; PFCE benchmarked on **HCES 2022-23**; four institutional sectors – government, public corporations, private corporations, households (**not exports**).
| Item | Revision |
|---|---|
| 2023-24 growth | 9.2% → **7.2%** |
| 2024-25 growth | 6.5% → **7.1%** |
| Size | GDP cut 3-4%; 2022-23 nominal Rs 269 → **Rs 261 lakh crore** |
~~ Sources: Arvind Subramanian, "India's GDP Mis-estimation", CID Harvard Working Paper 354 (June 2019); MoSPI press release 27 Feb 2026; app notes 3:24 sub 4, 5

=== Release Calendar and Quarterly GDP
- 1. **First Advance Estimate (FAE)** – early **January**; FY26 7.4%.
- 2. **Second Advance Estimate (SAE)** – end-**February**; FY26 **7.6%** (new series).
- 3. **Provisional Estimate (PE)** – moved from the last working day of May to **7 June from 2026**.
- 4. First, Second and Third Revised Estimates.
- Advance estimates use the **benchmark-indicator method**.
- **Quarterly GDP**: about **two months** after the quarter ends (Q1 2026-27 end-August), from high-frequency indicators and GST data; Q3 FY26 7.8%.

=== Savings and Capital Formation
- **MoSPI/NSO** estimates saving and capital formation by institutional sector; **K.N. Raj Committee (1981)** on saving and capital formation estimates.
- **Household sector = largest contributor** to gross domestic savings (WBCS 2017); descending order: **household > private corporate > public corporations/enterprises > government** (UP Lower Sub. 1998).
- Gross domestic saving rate: about **18%** of GDP in the early 1980s (G1 1983-84); 2001-10 peak in **2007-08** (about 37% of GDP, UPPCS 2009).
- Household saving **18.6% → 18.1%** of GDP (2022-23 → 2023-24, NAS 2025); private corporate investment **11.2%** of GDP (FY24).
~~ Sources: Economic Survey 2025-26 (gross domestic savings by sector); RBI Handbook of Statistics on the Indian Economy (GDS rate series); NAS 2025; app notes 3:24 sub 8
