=== Blood and Immunity Terms
- **ABO blood groups** discovered by **Karl Landsteiner** (published **1901**).
- Antigens sit on red blood cells; antibodies are in the plasma.
| Blood group | Antigen on RBC | Role |
|---|---|---|
| O negative | No A, no B, no Rh | Universal donor (red cells) |
| AB positive | A and B, Rh | Universal recipient |
- **Rh factor**: present = **Rh positive (+)**; absent = **Rh negative (-)**.
- For plasma the universal donor is AB, not O.
