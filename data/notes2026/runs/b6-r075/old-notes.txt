## SUB 0 | Indian and Joint Space Missions
[75:0:0] # NISAR - NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar
[75:0:1] - **NISAR** = the **first ISRO-NASA joint satellite** and the **world's first dual-frequency radar satellite**.
[75:0:2] - Launched on **30 Jul 2025** by **GSLV-F16** from **Sriharikota**.
[75:0:3] - Partners: **ISRO + NASA/JPL**.
[75:0:4] - Dual radar: **L-band SAR by NASA** + **S-band SAR by ISRO**.
[75:0:5] - Cost **$1.5 billion**: the **costliest Earth-observation satellite**.
[75:0:6] - Scans nearly all of Earth **every 12 days**.
[75:0:7] - Objectives: **land deformation** (earthquakes, landslides, **land subsidence**), **ice sheets and glaciers**, ecosystems and **biomass**, disasters.
[75:0:8] NISAR flew on GSLV-F16, not PSLV. L-band = NASA; S-band = ISRO (do not swap).
