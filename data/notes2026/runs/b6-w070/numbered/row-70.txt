[8:6] # Nuclear Power in Space
[8:7] TABLE head: System | How it works | Examples / use
  [8:7.0] RTG (Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generator) | Decay heat of plutonium-238 → electricity; no moving parts | Voyager 1 & 2, Curiosity, Perseverance
  [8:7.1] RHU (Radioisotope Heater Unit) | Heat only – no electricity | Keeps instruments from freezing
  [8:7.2] Fission reactor | Controlled chain reaction → much higher power | Lunar/Martian habitats
  [8:7.3] NTP (Nuclear Thermal Propulsion) | Reactor heats liquid hydrogen | ~twice efficiency of chemical rockets
  [8:7.4] NEP (Nuclear Electric Propulsion) | Electricity accelerates ionised propellant | Low thrust, very high efficiency
[8:8] - Radioisotope systems used since **1961**; Voyager launched **1977** (5-year mission), now >20 billion km away.
