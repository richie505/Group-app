## SUB 0 | Balban – Kingship, Court and Army
[0:0] - **Name:** born **Bahauddin** (c. 1205); regnal title **Ghiyasuddin Balban** (CGPSC 2004; Chhattisgarh 2004).
[0:1] - **Rise:** slave bought by Iltutmish → **water-carrier** → member of **the Forty** → **Amir-i-Shikar** (under Razia) → **naib** (principal minister) of **Nasiruddin Mahmud**, who gave him the title **Ulugh Khan** (IAS 2006).
[0:2] - **Imaduddin Raihan:** leader of the **Indian Muslim** group who briefly displaced Balban in Nasiruddin Mahmud's reign (MPPSC 2002).
[0:3] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [0:3.0] Zil-i-Ilahi | Sultan = shadow of God
  [0:3.1] Sijda | Prostration before the Sultan
  [0:3.2] Paibos | Kissing the Sultan's feet
  [0:3.3] Nauroz (Nawruz) | Persian festival introduced at court by Balban (IAS 1993; SSC 2006) – not Alauddin, Iltutmish or Firuz
[0:4] - **Court** copied the **Iranian kings**; strict etiquette, aloofness.
[0:5] - **Blood and iron:** broke **the Forty** by promoting **junior Turks**; efficient spies; harsh justice – amir **Malik Baqbaq** (governor of Badaun) **publicly flogged** for killing a servant (UPPSC 2011).
[0:6] - **Army:** created the military department **Diwan-i-Arz**, separating it from **Diwan-i-Wizarat** (finance) (RAS 2018); **outposts and forts** against the Mongols.
[0:7] - **⚠ Iltutmish created the Forty; Balban broke it.**
[0:8] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams 03 p.56-57; PMF Ancient & Medieval 04 p.33-34; app notes 1:105 (sub 4, 5)
## SUB 1 | Iltutmish – Capital, Coins and the Mongol Threat
[1:0] - **Capital:** moved from **Lahore to Delhi** (UPPSC 2016) – first Sultan to make **Delhi** the capital and issue **regular coins** (UPPSC Mains 2014).
[1:1] - **Coins:** purely **Arabic** silver **tanka** and copper **jital** – basis of the later rupee.
[1:2] - **Chengiz (Genghis) Khan** – original name **Temujin (Temuchin)** (UPPSC 2015) – reached the **Indus (1221)** chasing **Jalaluddin Mangbarni** of Khwarazm; Iltutmish **refused him shelter**, so the Mongols turned back. Mongols seen on the Indus **for the first time** in Iltutmish's reign (UPPSC 1993; CGPSC 2013).
[1:3] - **Rivals:** **Tajuddin Yildiz** (beaten at **Tarain, 1216**) and **Nasiruddin Qabacha** (Multan, Uch; fought for Lahore).
[1:4] - **Bihar:** after defeating Iwaz Khalji of Bengal, Iltutmish made **Malik (Alauddin) Jani** the **first governor of Bihar** (BPSC 2008).
[1:5] - **Investiture ("mansur")** from the **Abbasid Caliph** of Baghdad – **1229**.
[1:6] TABLE head: Institution | Meaning
  [1:6.0] Turkan-i-Chahalgani | "The Forty" – Turkish military elite (not Persian revenue clerks)
  [1:6.1] Iqta | Revenue assignment to officers (muqtis) instead of cash salary
[1:7] - **Buildings:** completed the **Qutb Minar** (begun by Aibak; dedicated to **Khwaja Qutbuddin Bakhtiyar Kaki**); **Sultan Ghari** tomb → "**Father of tomb building**".
[1:8] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams 03 p.54-56; PMF Ancient & Medieval 04 p.31-32
## SUB 2 | Qutbuddin Aibak (1206-1210)
[2:0] - **Origin:** born in **Turkestan**; sold to the **Qazi of Nishapur**, then slave of **Muhammad Ghori**; **Amir-i-Akhur** (Master of Stables) → general; Ghori's representative after **Tarain II (1192)**.
[2:1] - **Titles:** **Lakh Bakhsh** ("giver of lakhs"), and **Quran Khwan / "Quran Khan"** (reciter of the Quran) (JPSC 2003) – **Zil-i-Ilahi** was Balban's.
[2:2] - **Capital:** **Lahore**.
[2:3] - **Buildings:** began the **Qutb Minar**; **Quwwat-ul-Islam** mosque (first mosque in Delhi); **Adhai Din ka Jhonpra** (Ajmer). Patron of **Hasan Nizami**.
[2:4] - **Death (1210):** at **Lahore**, **fell from his horse while playing chaugan (polo)** – not stabbed, not killed by Yildiz, not at Kalinjar (UPSC 2003); buried near **Anarkali Bazaar**.
[2:5] - **Polo** was brought to India by the **Turks** (UPPSC 2001).
[2:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams 03 p.53-54; PMF Ancient & Medieval 04 p.31
## SUB 3 | Razia Sultan (1236-1240)
[3:0] - **First and only woman ruler** of the Delhi Sultanate – the "first woman ruler of (medieval) India" in exam keys (UPPSC 2004; SSC 2017) – before Chand Bibi, Durgavati, Nur Jahan.
[3:1] - **Ways:** discarded **purdah**, wore **male attire** (kuba, kulah), hunted, led the army.
[3:2] - **Opposition:** the **Turkish nobles** (the Forty) resented her favour to **Yaqut**, an **Abyssinian** (Amir-i-Akhur) → her fall (UP Lower 2004).
[3:3] - **1240:** **Altuniya**, governor of **Bhatinda (Tabarhinda)**, revolted → Yaqut killed, Razia imprisoned; she **married Altuniya**, marched on Delhi and was **killed** (1240).
[3:4] - She made **Balban Amir-i-Shikar** (not Diwan-i-Arz).
[3:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams 03 p.56; PMF Ancient & Medieval 04 p.32-33
## SUB 4 | Mamluk Lines, Rulers and the Turkish Nobility
[4:0] - **Mamluk** = Arabic "**owned**" – an owned **military slave**; Slave dynasty ruled **1206-1290**.
[4:1] - **Ruling class:** most amirs and Sultans of the Sultanate were **Turks** (Turko-Afghan) (UPPSC 1991).
[4:2] TABLE head: Line | Period | Founder
  [4:2.0] Qutbi | 1206-1211 | Qutbuddin Aibak
  [4:2.1] First Ilbari | 1211-1266 | Iltutmish
  [4:2.2] Second Ilbari | 1266-1290 | Balban
[4:3] - **Order:** Aibak → Aram Shah → **Iltutmish** → **Ruknuddin Firuz** → **Razia** → Bahram Shah → Masud → **Nasiruddin Mahmud** (1246-66; Tabaqat-i-Nasiri named after him) → **Balban** → **Kaiqubad** → infant **Kaimurs** (last).
[4:4] - **End:** **Jalaluddin Firoz Khalji** crowned himself at **Kilokhari (1290)** → **Khalji** dynasty.
[4:5] TABLE head: Dynasty | Period
  [4:5.0] Mamluk | 1206-1290
  [4:5.1] Khalji | 1290-1320
  [4:5.2] Tughlaq | 1320-1414
  [4:5.3] Sayyid | 1414-1451
  [4:5.4] Lodi | 1451-1526
[4:6] - **⚠ Odd one out:** Ruknuddin, Masud, Nasiruddin Mahmud **are** Mamluks; **Jalaluddin Khalji is not**.
