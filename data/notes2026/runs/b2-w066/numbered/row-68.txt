## SUB 0 | National Party Criteria – Paragraph 6B of the Symbols Order
[0:0] - A party is a **national party** if it fulfils **any one** of three conditions (**Para 6B**, Election Symbols (Reservation and Allotment) Order, 1968):
[0:1] TABLE head: # | Condition
  [0:1.0] 1 | 6% of valid votes in four or more States at a Lok Sabha or Assembly general election AND at least 4 Lok Sabha seats from any State(s) – both mandatory
  [0:1.1] 2 | 2% of Lok Sabha seats (11 of 543) with members elected from at least three States
  [0:1.2] 3 | Recognised as a State party in four or more States
[0:2] - Memory hook: **6%-4 States + 4 seats / 2%-3 States / 4 States**.
[0:3] - Distractors (all wrong): "recognised in **2 or more** States", "**5%** of seats", "**3** States' recognition", "**5%** of votes in four States", "4% in one State", "10 Lok Sabha seats", "contests all constituencies", "power in four States", "10 Rajya Sabha seats".
[0:4] - APPSC G2: all three listed conditions valid → "**None of the above**" is the answer to "which is NOT a condition".
[0:5] > Asked as: which is a condition (5 PYQs); which is not (None of the above); State-party status in how many States (four); 6% in 4 States; statements with 2 States / 5% seats (neither).

## SUB 2 | Registration and Recognition – Role of the ECI
[2:0] TABLE head: Step | Law | Authority
  [2:0.0] Registration | Sec. 29A, RPA 1951 | ECI
  [2:0.1] Recognition (national/State) and symbols | Election Symbols (Reservation and Allotment) Order, 1968 | ECI
  [2:0.2] Split disputes | Para 15; Sadiq Ali (1971) majority test | ECI
  [2:0.3] Suspend/withdraw recognition | Para 16A – defying the Model Code of Conduct | ECI
[2:1] - Three statuses: **unregistered**; **registered but unrecognised (RUPP)**; **recognised** (national or State).
[2:2] - Apply for registration within **30 days** of formation; the party constitution must pledge allegiance to the **Constitution, socialism, secularism, democracy** and the unity and integrity of India.
[2:3] - The ECI **recognises** parties (not the President, Law Ministry or Speaker) on the basis of **poll performance**, and acts as a **court** in recognition and symbol disputes – it **does** settle disputes between splinter groups of recognised parties.
[2:4] - NCERT: the ECI requires parties to hold **organisational elections** and file income-tax returns.
[2:5] - **Nature of the 1968 Order:** a **statutory order of the ECI** under the RPA 1951 and **Art. 324** – not an Act of Parliament.
[2:6] - **Deregistration:** only for **fraud** in obtaining registration, ceasing allegiance to the Constitution, or being declared unlawful (**INC v. Institute of Social Welfare, 2002**); Law Commission **255th** Report seeks a limited power.
[2:7] - RUPPs: over **2,800**; about **750** contested in 2024.
[2:8] > Asked as: who recognises parties (ECI); wrong statement on the ECI and parties (splinter-group disputes); RPA 1951 registration statements.

## SUB 3 | State Party Criteria
[3:0] - A party is a **State party** in a State if it fulfils **any one** of five conditions:
[3:1] TABLE head: Route | Condition
  [3:1.0] 1 | 6% valid votes in the Assembly election + 2 Assembly seats
  [3:1.1] 2 | 6% valid votes in the Lok Sabha election from the State + 1 Lok Sabha seat
  [3:1.2] 3 | 3% of Assembly seats or 3 seats, whichever is more
  [3:1.3] 4 | 1 Lok Sabha seat for every 25 allotted to the State
  [3:1.4] 5 | 8% of valid votes (Lok Sabha or Assembly) – no seat needed
[3:2] - The **8%** vote-only route is the one candidates forget (not 10%).
[3:3] ~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. Ch79 (src/polity/laxmikanth-indian-polity-02.txt) p.806-807; app notes 2:68 sub 3
## SUB 4 | Symbols – Reserved and Free
[4:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [4:0.0] Reserved symbol | Only for recognised parties (Lotus – BJP, Hand – INC); national party: whole country; State party: its State(s); two recognised parties in different States may hold the same symbol
  [4:0.1] Free symbol | For RUPPs and independents from a notified list; latest May 2025 – 184 symbols
  [4:0.2] Common symbol for RUPPs | If they contest at least 2 Lok Sabha seats or 5% of Assembly seats
[4:1] - **CPI** – **ears of corn and sickle** since **1952**: the party that has contested **every** Lok Sabha election on the **same symbol** (Congress moved from bullocks → cow and calf → **hand**; the DMK first contested in 1957).
[4:2] - **Sadiq Ali (1971):** split disputes over name/symbol decided by **majority support** of the factions (Para 15). 2026: Trinamool rebel MPs chose a merger over a Para 15 claim.
[4:3] > Asked as: party with the same symbol in all 17 Lok Sabha elections (CPI).
