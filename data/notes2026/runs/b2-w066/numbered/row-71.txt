## SUB 0 | CAG – Status, Appointment, Term and Removal
[0:0] # Status
[0:1] - Official name: **Comptroller and Auditor-General of India**; **Part V, Chapter V**, Articles **148–151** – an **independent constitutional authority created by the Constitution** (Art. 148), not by an Act, a Cabinet resolution or under the Finance Ministry.
[0:2] TABLE head: Article | Content
  [0:2.0] 148 | Appointment, oath, term, removal; 148(4) post-retirement bar; 148(6) expenses charged
  [0:2.1] 149 | Duties and powers (Parliament prescribes them – CAG Act, 1971)
  [0:2.2] 150 | Form of accounts – prescribed by the President on the CAG's advice
  [0:2.3] 151 | Audit reports: Union → President (151(1)); State → Governor (151(2))
[0:3] - Head of the **Indian Audit and Accounts Department**; "**Guardian of the Public Purse**" / of public finance.
[0:4] - **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar**: the CAG is "the **most important officer** under the Constitution"; one of the bulwarks of democracy with the **Supreme Court, Election Commission and UPSC**; auditing both Union and State accounts is a **unitary** feature.
[0:5] - The CAG is **not part of the Union Executive** (President, VP, PM, Council of Ministers, Attorney General), nor is the **Speaker** (part of Parliament).
[0:6] - For the President's election, **CAG** is an **office of profit**; the posts of VP, Governor and Minister are not (Art. 58 explanation).
[0:7] - **Table of Precedence:** CAG is at **9A** with the **Chairperson UPSC** and the **Chief Election Commissioner**; Chief Justices of High Courts are at 14.
[0:8] # Appointment, term and removal
[0:9] - Appointed by the **President** by **warrant under his hand and seal** (Art. 148(1)) – not the PM, FM, UPSC, Parliament or a PM–LoP committee; Attorney General (Art. 76) and Governor (Art. 155) are also appointed by the President.
[0:10] - **Oath** before the **President** (or a person he appoints); form in the **Third Schedule**.
[0:11] - **Term: 6 years or age 65, whichever is earlier** (CAG Act, 1971) – the longest among President, VP and Lok Sabha member (5 years). Same as **CEC/ECs and UPSC members**; SPSC members 6 years or **62**; **SC judge retires at 65**, **HC judge at 62** (15th Amendment, 1963: 60 → 62).
[0:12] - **Removal** in like manner and on like grounds as a **Supreme Court judge** (Art. 148(1) with Art. 124(4)): by the President on an **address by both Houses** with **special majority** for **proved misbehaviour or incapacity** – he does **not** hold office at the President's pleasure; resigns to the President.
[0:13] - **Constitution prescribes no qualifications** for the CAG; it is a **single-member** office.

## SUB 1 | Audit Reports, PAC and Parliamentary Control
[1:0] # Report to action
[1:1] - **CAG report → President (Union) / Governor (State)** under **Art. 151** → laid before **Parliament / State Legislature** → examined by the **Public Accounts Committee** (PSUs: **Committee on Public Undertakings**) → **Action Taken Notes**.
[1:2] - Reports go first to the **President**, not to the PAC, the Speaker, the PM, the CJI or the Vice-President; State reports to the **Governor** (Art. **151(2)**).
[1:3] - Three Union reports: **Appropriation Accounts, Finance Accounts, Public Undertakings** (not Estimates).
[1:4] - **Ultimate action** on CAG reports rests with **Parliament** (not the President, SC or NDC).
[1:5] - **Art. 281/151/338:** the President must lay before Parliament the **FC recommendations**, the **CAG report** and the **NCSC report** – the **PAC report** is not laid by him (it goes to the Speaker).
[1:6] # Public Accounts Committee
[1:7] - Set up **1921** (GoI Act, 1919); **22 members (15 LS + 7 RS)**, elected yearly by **single transferable vote**; **no Minister**.
[1:8] - Chairman appointed by the **Speaker**, from the **Opposition since 1967**; 2026-27 chair **K.C. Venugopal**; PAC reports to the **Speaker** / Parliament.
[1:9] - Main function: **examine the CAG's audit reports** (Appropriation and Finance Accounts) – not day-to-day interference or orders.
[1:10] - CAG = PAC's "**friend, philosopher and guide**" – he **attends its meetings** (not the AG or Solicitor General); not of the Estimates Committee or COPU.
[1:11] - PAC recommendations are **not binding**; backlog of Action Taken Notes (May 2026).
[1:12] # CAG and Parliament
[1:13] - **No Minister represents the CAG** in Parliament (not the Finance Minister).
[1:14] - The CAG has **no right to speak, take part or vote** in either House or a joint sitting – these Art. 88 rights (speak and take part, **no vote**) belong to **Ministers and the Attorney General**.

## SUB 2 | Duties and Powers – Article 149, 1971 Act, Types of Audit
[2:0] # Duties
[2:1] - **CAG (Duties, Powers and Conditions of Service) Act, 1971**; **1976 amendment** (in force **1 March 1976**): **accounts separated from audit** (departmentalisation) – Union accounts now compiled by the **Controller General of Accounts** (Finance Ministry), who gives the FM a **monthly** analytical review; the CAG still keeps most **State accounts**.
[2:2] - Audits: expenditure from the **Consolidated Funds** of India, States and UTs with legislature; **Contingency Funds** and **Public Accounts**; trading, manufacturing, profit-and-loss accounts; **receipts** payable into the CFI; **stores and stock**.
[2:3] - Also **government companies** and bodies **substantially financed** by government; **local bodies** only on request of the President/Governor.
[2:4] - **Certifies net proceeds** of any tax or duty (incl. income tax, Art. 279) – his certificate is final; advises the President on the **form of accounts** (Art. 150).
[2:5] - Duty: **uphold the Constitution and laws of Parliament** in financial administration – checks not only **legality** but also **propriety** of expenditure.
[2:6] # Types of audit
[2:7] TABLE head: Type | Nature | Tests
  [2:7.0] Legal and regulatory (compliance) | Obligatory | Money legally available, spent as rules require
  [2:7.1] Financial attest | Obligatory | Accounts true
  [2:7.2] Propriety | Discretionary | Wisdom, faithfulness, economy; flags extravagance
  [2:7.3] Performance | Discretionary (2006 OM) | 3 Es: economy, efficiency, effectiveness
[2:8] - **Social audit**: CAG laid down the **Auditing Standards for Social Audit** of **MGNREGA** (2016).
[2:9] # Limits
[2:10] - Only an **Auditor General**, not a Comptroller: **no control over the issue of money** from the Consolidated Fund or the receipt/lodging of revenue (Britain's C&AG has it); audit is **ex-post**.
[2:11] - He does **not control the entire financial system of the Union only** – he audits both **Centre and States**; cannot disallow expenditure, enforce recovery, prosecute or **suo motu investigate corruption** – he only reports.
[2:12] - **Secret service expenditure**: accepts a **certificate** of the competent authority.
[2:13] TABLE head: Category | Example
  [2:13.0] Audited directly by CAG | DVC, ONGC
  [2:13.1] Private auditors on CAG's advice + CAG supplementary audit | Central Warehousing Corp., IFCI
  [2:13.2] Wholly private audit (no CAG role) | LIC, RBI
[2:14] - **Paul Appleby** criticised the CAG's role and suggested relieving it of audit.

## SUB 3 | Independence Safeguards
[3:0] - **Security of tenure** – removal only by the constitutional procedure.
[3:1] - **Salary = Supreme Court judge**, determined by Parliament – **Second Schedule (Part E)**; salary, leave, pension and retirement age **cannot be varied to his disadvantage**.
[3:2] - **Leave** granted or refused by the **President**.
[3:3] - His **salary** (Art. 112(3)) and the office's expenses (salaries, allowances, pensions of staff) are **charged on the Consolidated Fund of India** – non-votable (Art. 148(6)); the Attorney General's and Service Chiefs' salaries are not charged.
[3:4] - **Complete bar** on further office under GoI or any State after retirement (Art. 148(4)) – the UPSC Chairman is also barred; retired judges often head commissions.
[3:5] - Service conditions of IA&AD staff and his administrative powers are prescribed by the President **after consulting the CAG**.
[3:6] - **Not** a safeguard: holding office during the President's pleasure.
