=== UNESCO World Heritage Sites – India and the World
# India's count
- **India: 45 WHS** (July 2026) – **37 cultural, 7 natural, 1 mixed**; **6th in the world**. **Andhra Pradesh has none**.
- **44 in July 2025** (Maratha Military Landscapes, 47th session, Paris) → **45th = Sarnath** (UP's 4th), 48th session, **Busan**, July 2026.
- Maratha Military Landscapes: **12 forts – 11 in Maharashtra + Gingee (Tamil Nadu)**.
- Earliest (1983): **Ajanta, Ellora (Maharashtra)**, Agra Fort, Taj Mahal. India ratified the Convention in 1977.
- Only **mixed** site: **Khangchendzonga NP, Sikkim** (2016). Only Harappan WHS: **Dholavira** (2021).

# World Heritage City
- **Ahmedabad = India's first World Heritage City (2017)**; Jaipur second (2019). Varanasi, Hyderabad, Delhi not inscribed.
- **Tentative List:** Delhi, Ahmedabad and Jaipur city nominations were on it (Ahmedabad and Jaipur since inscribed); **Tirupati never was**.
- AP on the Tentative List: **Sri Veerabhadra Temple and Monolithic Nandi, Lepakshi** (Sri Sathya Sai district), added **2022**.
# World sites asked
- **Plitvice Lakes NP, Croatia** – WHS 1979; sixteen lakes linked by waterfalls.
- **Prosecco Hills** of Conegliano and Valdobbiadene, **Italy** – inscribed 2019.
- ICOMOS (1965, Paris) evaluates cultural nominations; India on the World Heritage Committee 2021-25.
