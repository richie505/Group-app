[0:11] # The 2026 Hormuz shock
[0:12] - **Strait of Hormuz** (between **Iran and Oman**) effectively closed by Iran, early March 2026; it carries ~15 million bbl/day (~**34%**, about **one-third**, of world crude trade) and a fifth of global LNG.
[0:13] - India's crude basket **$82.04/bbl** (July 2026) vs $70.95 a year earlier; LPG output raised **35 → 54 TMT/day** under the LPG control order.
[0:14] - Response: proposed **41 MT strategic fuel system** (Aug 2026); more domestic coal and gas; LNG/LPG from the US, Oman, Nigeria, Angola.
[0:15] # Import bill
[0:16] - Crude import bill **FY 2024-25: $137.0 billion**; July 2026: $13.7 billion, up 41% (PPAC).
[0:17] - Higher oil prices → wider **trade deficit** and higher **inflation**.

## SUB 1 | Import-substitution Fuels – Methanol, Ethanol, CBG
[1:0] # Methanol Economy (NITI Aayog)
[1:1] - Driven by **NITI Aayog**. Aims (**APPSC G1: all three correct**): (i) cut the **oil import bill**, (ii) cut **GHG emissions**, (iii) convert **coal reserves and municipal solid waste** into methanol. Increasing LPG imports is not an aim.
[1:2] TABLE head: Term | Meaning / use
  [1:2.0] Methanol | Low-carbon, hydrogen-carrier fuel from biomass, high-ash coal, agri-residue, CO₂, natural gas; partly replaces LPG and kerosene in cooking
  [1:2.1] DME (Di-Methyl Ether) | Two methanol molecules minus one water molecule; replaces propane in LPG and diesel in slow-RPM engines
  [1:2.2] M15 | 15% methanol in petrol: at least 15% cut in petrol/crude imports
  [1:2.3] 20% DME in LPG | Saves about ₹6,000 crore a year

## SUB 2 | Hydrocarbon Vision 2025 and Upstream Policy
[2:0] - **Hydrocarbon Vision 2025:** long-term hydrocarbon plan **adopted in 2000**; exam keys link it with **storage of petroleum products** (UPPCS 2000) – it calls for adequate strategic storage of crude and products, within a wider aim of energy security.
[2:1]   - Focus: **self-reliance** through **indigenous production** and **equity oil abroad**; appraisal of all sedimentary basins; **free market and competition** – it does **not** ban private participation.
[2:2] - **HELP:** a **single licence for all hydrocarbons**; **OALP** = **Open Acreage Licensing Policy** – companies choose and bid for blocks.
[2:3] - **ONGC Videsh (OVL):** overseas arm of ONGC; acquires **equity oil and gas abroad** (not LPG retail).
[2:4] - **One Nation One Gas Grid**; refiners + Shipping Corporation of India JV (2026).
[2:5] - **TAPI** (Turkmenistan–Afghanistan–Pakistan–India) stalled; **IPI** (Iran–Pakistan–India) dropped by India 2007-08.
