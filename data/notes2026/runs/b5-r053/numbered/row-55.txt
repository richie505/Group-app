## SUB 1 | DERs, Net Metering, RPO and Electricity Planning
[1:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [1:0.0] Distributed energy resources | Small, decentralised units near the point of use: battery storage, biomass generators, fuel cells, rooftop solar PV – all four (UPSC 2024)
  [1:0.1] Net metering | Billing mechanism that credits rooftop-solar owners for power fed to the grid
  [1:0.2] Prosumer | Consumer who also produces/stores power and sells the surplus
  [1:0.3] RPO | DISCOMs and open-access/captive users must buy a share of RE; fixed by SERCs (Electricity Act 2003)
[1:1] - **Draft National Electricity Plan (CEA, Dec 2016):** **no coal-based capacity addition needed in 2017-2022** beyond ~50 GW under construction (final plan 2018: 6,440 MW).
[1:2] - **ISTS charge waiver:** 100% for solar/wind commissioned by **30 June 2025**; then graded.
[1:3] - **Tariff Policy** 2006, revised **2016**. Draft National Electricity Policy 2026: **100 GW pumped storage by 2047**, Time-of-Day tariffs.
[1:4] - EC Act 2001: emission-intensity targets for **208** industrial units feed the **domestic carbon market** (Jun 2026).

[2:1] - 1. **500 GW** non-fossil capacity by 2030
[2:2] - 2. **50%** of energy requirement from renewables by 2030 (not 60%)
[2:3] - 3. Cut projected cumulative emissions by **1 billion tonnes** by 2030

[3:1] # Integrated Energy Policy
[3:2] - Meet energy needs of all at **least cost** till **2031-32**; **market-determined / trade-parity pricing**; **subsidies targeted to the poor**.
[3:3] - Pricing reforms: petrol deregulated **June 2010**, diesel **October 2014**; LPG subsidy by DBT (**PAHAL**, 2015).
[3:4] # IESS 2047 and the National Energy Policy
[3:5] - IESS: open-access scenario tool; **Business-As-Usual** and **Ambitious** scenarios.
[3:6] - **National Energy Policy aim** (APPSC FSO 2025): **energy access for all while ensuring energy security, affordability and sustainability**. Four objectives: **access at affordable prices; improved security and independence; greater sustainability; economic growth** (not greater import dependence).
[3:7] - Fossil fuels = nearly **90%** of commercial primary energy supply.
[3:8] - NITI "Scenarios Towards Viksit Bharat and Net Zero" (Feb 2026): nuclear ~100 GW by 2047, **200–300 GW by 2070**.

## SUB 5 | Energy Finance – NCEF, IREDA, Green Bonds
[5:0] - **National Clean Energy Fund:** financed by a **clean energy cess on coal** (Budget 2010-11, ₹50/tonne → **₹400/tonne** by 2016-17); after GST (2017) the cess became part of the **GST Compensation Cess**.
[5:1] - **IREDA:** set up **1987** under **MNRE** (not Ministry of Power); listed Nov 2023; **Navratna April 2024**.
[5:2] - **Sovereign green bonds:** first issued **January 2023**. Net-zero finance need: **over $20 trillion** by 2070.
[5:3] - Stranded gas-based capacity: about **25 GW**.
