## SUB 2 | Demand, Mix and Per Capita
[53:2:0] - India = **third-largest energy consumer**, **second-largest coal consumer**; demand to **triple by 2047**.
[53:2:1] - **Coal**: ~55% of energy mix, **74%+ of generation**. Second source after fossil fuels: **solar** (UPPSC 2021).
[53:2:2] - **Non-fossil capacity**: 32% (2014) → **51% (2025)**; 50% target crossed five years early.
[53:2:3] - **Per-capita electricity**: **957 kWh (FY14) → 1,460 kWh (FY25)**; ~1/5 of China's, 1/9 of USA's.
[53:2:4] - Per-capita energy ~**one-third of world average**; large urban-rural gap.
[53:2:5] - **HDI link** (**APPSC 2016**): a minimum per-capita consumption is needed for basic well-being.
## SUB 3 | Sectors and Drivers
[53:3:0] - **Industry** uses ~**50%** of electricity and stays largest by 2040; **cooking** demand falls.
[53:3:1] - Household electrification ~99%; **Saubhagya** ~26 million households.
[53:3:2] - Drivers: **EVs, farm pumps, appliances, cooling, data centres, telecom**; peak **183 GW (Dec 2020) → 250+ GW (Apr 2026)**, ~37%.
## SUB 4 | Peak Deficit and Regional Gaps
[53:4:0] - Shortage: **10–15%** earlier → **4.2% (FY14)** → **0.03% (2025)** → **zero by Nov 2025**.
[53:4:1] - Record peak **270.8 GW (May 2026)** (+90 GW vs 2019).
[53:4:2] - 25 Apr 2026: no solar-hour shortage; **2% non-solar deficit** → **evening peak** (6–11 p.m.) → **storage** push; Delhi: **ToD tariffs, smart meters**.
[53:4:3] - **Regional imbalances:** poor **storage** and slow **transmission** can strand renewable power in some regions; heatwaves add stress on grids.
