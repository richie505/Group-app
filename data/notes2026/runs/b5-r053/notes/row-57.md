=== Coal Types, Carbon Content and Fuel Quality
| Type | Carbon (NextGen/PMF) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Anthracite** | **80–95%** | **Highest carbon, best quality**; hardest, little smoke, highest calorific value; J&K (Kalakot), small quantity |
| Bituminous | 40–80% | **Most widely used**; includes the **coking** grade; Jharkhand, Odisha, WB, Chhattisgarh, MP |
| Sub-bituminous | – | Between bituminous and lignite; used in **power plants** |
| Lignite (brown coal) | 40–55% | High moisture; **Neyveli (TN), Palana (Rajasthan), Lakhimpur (Assam)** |
| Peat | below 40% | **First stage** of wood turning into coal |
- Order of carbon: **anthracite > bituminous > sub-bituminous > lignite > peat**.
- International (ASTM-style) tables give lignite **25–35%**, sub-bituminous 35–45%, bituminous 45–86%, anthracite 86–97% – use these when the options are on that scale.
- **Calorific value** depends mainly on the **carbon content** (moisture and ash lower it).
- A good fuel has a **high** calorific value, low moisture, harmless combustion products and controllable burning – "low calorific value" is not a property of a good fuel.
- **Coal is a natural (fossil) fuel**; petrol and diesel are refined, charcoal is made from wood. **Lignite** contains carbon (tin, silver, iron are metals).
# Gondwana vs Tertiary coal
- **Gondwana** (~250 million years): **98% of reserves**, 99% of production; coking and bituminous coal; Damodar, Mahanadi, Son, Godavari, Wardha valleys – hence Gondwana rocks are India's most important rock system.
- **Damuda series** (Lower Gondwana): India's **metallurgical (coking) coal** and the most productive fields.
- **Tertiary** (15–60 million years): younger, **low carbon, high moisture and sulphur**; lignite (chief producer **Tamil Nadu**); **North-East India and J&K** (extra-peninsular), about 2% – not "mainly Peninsular". Bituminous coal is a Gondwana coal.
- Some coal lies buried under the **Ganga alluvium and the Deccan Trap**.

=== Indian Coal Quality, Imports, Washing and Health
- **Indian coal:** **high ash (35–45%)**, **low calorific value**, **low sulphur (~0.5%)** and a **high ash-fusion temperature (~1500°C)** (UPSC 2013: "1 and 2 only").
- **Pollution (UPSC 2020, all correct):** coal ash contains **arsenic, lead and mercury**; coal plants emit **SO₂ and NOx**; Indian coal has high ash.
- **Imports:** **coking coal** mainly from **Australia** (the steel industry imports it, UPSC 2015); non-coking from **Indonesia, South Africa**.
- **Coal washing (APPSC G1 2024 A–R):** washing raises generation efficiency and cuts costs, but washed-coal use stays limited because it raises the cost of power and hurts plants in **merit-order** dispatch – **A and R true, R explains A**.
- **Problems:** poor quality, movement bottlenecks, **low washery use**, rising coking-coal imports – not "excess high-quality coking coal".
- **Pneumoconiosis** (black lung): dust disease of **coal-mine** workers.

=== Commercial Mining and Laws
- Supreme Court (2014) cancelled **204** coal blocks → **Coal Mines (Special Provisions) Act, 2015** for their auction.
- **MMDR** amendment March 2021: **sale of coal from captive mines**; another amendment declared **coking coal a Critical and Strategic Mineral**.
- **SHAKTI (2017):** replaced the **New Coal Distribution Policy 2007**; linkages by **auction or tariff-based bidding**.
- **Revenue-sharing commercial auctions** began **2020**; 13th round opened **August 2025**; 14th-round agreements first with **underground coal gasification** clauses.

=== Gasification, CBM and UCG
- **Gasification:** coal + oxygen + steam → **syngas (CO + H₂ + CH₄ + CO₂)**; runs **IGCC** plants.
- Products: SNG, methanol, **ethanol**, **urea**/ammonia, hydrogen, DME – **not nitroglycerine** (UPSC 2025).
- **CBM** = "**sweet gas**"; free gas in joints, **adsorbed** in micropores.
- **UCG:** in-situ; the MoEFCC panel **refused to waive the 300 m** minimum depth.
- **National Coal Gasification Mission:** **100 MT by 2030**.
- **Surface Coal/Lignite Gasification scheme** (13 May 2026): **₹37,500 crore**; up to **20%** of plant cost, cap ₹5,000 crore; no bidders yet – bid windows kept open, not withdrawn.
- Commercial blocks: **50% revenue-share rebate** if at least **10%** output is gasified.
- **JSPL Angul** (Odisha): only commercial-scale plant; **Talcher**: first gasification fertiliser plant.
