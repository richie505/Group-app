=== Import Dependence, Suppliers and Oil Challenges
# Import share by fuel
| Fuel | Import share (newest) |
|---|---|
| Crude oil | **88.5%** (PPAC provisional, July 2026) |
| Natural gas / LNG | ~47–51% (2026); **Qatar** = key LNG supplier |
| Coal | ~**26%**, although India is the second-largest producer |
| Lithium | ~**90%** |
| LPG | Depends mainly on **OMC stocks**; semi-strategic underground storage only ~0.14 MT |
- India = **3rd-largest energy consumer** and **3rd-largest oil consumer** (2026).
# Crude suppliers (2025)
| Supplier | Share |
|---|---|
| **Russia** (largest in recent years) | ~**35%** |
| Iraq | ~20% |
| Saudi Arabia | ~15% |
| USA | ~8% |
- Others: UAE; West Asia + Africa ~65% of supply (Dec 2025).
- **OPEC** produces ~**40%** of global oil; its share of India's crude imports fell **below 50%** (Aug 2025).
- **Saudi Arabia:** world's **number one oil exporter** and the usual exam answer for "largest reserves" (strictly, Venezuela has the largest proved reserves; Saudi Arabia second).
# Core challenge (APPSC G2 2025)
- Managing oil consumption = **supply-chain disruptions and fluctuating global oil prices** (not low demand, steady prices or abundant reserves).
| Angle | Issue |
|---|---|
| Price | Global volatility feeds domestic **inflation** |
| External | **Crude imports** = biggest driver of trade deficit / CAD |
| Route | Chokepoints like the **Strait of Hormuz** |
# The 2026 Hormuz shock
- **Strait of Hormuz** (between **Iran and Oman**) effectively closed by Iran, early March 2026; it carries ~15 million bbl/day (~**34%**, about **one-third**, of world crude trade) and a fifth of global LNG.
- India's crude basket **$82.04/bbl** (July 2026) vs $70.95 a year earlier; LPG output raised **35 → 54 TMT/day** under the LPG control order.
- Response: proposed **41 MT strategic fuel system** (Aug 2026); more domestic coal and gas; LNG/LPG from the US, Oman, Nigeria, Angola.
# Import bill
- Crude import bill **FY 2024-25: $137.0 billion**; July 2026: $13.7 billion, up 41% (PPAC).
- Higher oil prices → wider **trade deficit** and higher **inflation**.

=== Import-substitution Fuels – Methanol, Ethanol, CBG
# Methanol Economy (NITI Aayog)
- Driven by **NITI Aayog**. Aims (**APPSC G1: all three correct**): (i) cut the **oil import bill**, (ii) cut **GHG emissions**, (iii) convert **coal reserves and municipal solid waste** into methanol. Increasing LPG imports is not an aim.
| Term | Meaning / use |
|---|---|
| Methanol | Low-carbon, hydrogen-carrier fuel from biomass, high-ash coal, agri-residue, CO₂, natural gas; partly replaces **LPG and kerosene** in cooking |
| DME (Di-Methyl Ether) | Two methanol molecules **minus one water molecule**; replaces **propane in LPG** and diesel in slow-RPM engines |
| M15 | 15% methanol in **petrol**: at least **15%** cut in petrol/crude imports |
| 20% DME in LPG | Saves about **₹6,000 crore** a year |
# Ethanol Blended Petrol (EBP)
| Year | Step |
|---|---|
| **January 2003** | 5% EBP in 9 sugarcane States + 4 UTs (incl. AP) |
| ESY 2025-26 | **E20** achieved (target advanced from 2030) |
| 1 Apr 2026 | E20 with minimum 95 RON mandated |
# Compressed Biogas Obligation (CBO) in City Gas Distribution
- Share of CNG/PNG: **1% / 3% / 4%** (FY 2025-26 to 2027-28), **5% from 2028-29**; aim: replace **LNG** imports.

=== Hydrocarbon Vision 2025 and Upstream Policy
- **Hydrocarbon Vision 2025:** long-term hydrocarbon plan **adopted in 2000**; exam keys link it with **storage of petroleum products** (UPPCS 2000) – it calls for adequate strategic storage of crude and products, within a wider aim of energy security.
  - Focus: **self-reliance** through **indigenous production** and **equity oil abroad**; appraisal of all sedimentary basins; **free market and competition** – it does **not** ban private participation.
- **HELP:** a **single licence for all hydrocarbons**; **OALP** = **Open Acreage Licensing Policy** – companies choose and bid for blocks.
- **ONGC Videsh (OVL):** overseas arm of ONGC; acquires **equity oil and gas abroad** (not LPG retail).
- **One Nation One Gas Grid**; refiners + Shipping Corporation of India JV (2026).
- **TAPI** (Turkmenistan–Afghanistan–Pakistan–India) stalled; **IPI** (Iran–Pakistan–India) dropped by India 2007-08.

=== Energy Resources and Domestic Reserves
# Classification
| Basis | Types |
|---|---|
| Renewal | Renewable (solar, wind, hydro, biomass, tidal, geothermal) vs non-renewable / **fossil fuels (coal, oil, natural gas)** and nuclear fuels |
| Use history | Conventional (fossil fuels, hydro, biomass) vs non-conventional (solar, tidal, geothermal, nuclear, ocean thermal) |
| Not from the Sun | **Geothermal** (Earth's heat), tidal, nuclear |
- "Conventional" ≠ non-renewable: hydro and biomass are **both conventional and renewable**. Alcohol, ether and water gas are not fossil fuels; **natural gas** is.
# Reserves – number game
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Share of world | ~17% of population vs **0.4% oil**, 0.6% gas, 7% coal reserves |
| Coal | World's **fifth**-largest reserves; **second**-largest producer |
| Sedimentary basins | 26, only **7** producing |
| Thorium (monazite) | 11.93 Mt monazite; **Andhra Pradesh largest (3.72 Mt)**, then Tamil Nadu, Odisha, Kerala, West Bengal |
| Uranium | Low-grade; large deposit at **Tummalapalle (YSR Kadapa, AP)** |

=== Strategic Petroleum Reserves
| Phase I site | State | Capacity |
|---|---|---|
| **Visakhapatnam** | Andhra Pradesh | **1.33 MMT** |
| Mangaluru | Karnataka | 1.5 MMT |

=== Partnerships, Demand-side Measures and Critical Minerals
# Partnerships
- **IEA:** India's **association** status activated in **March 2017** (APPSC: "association status announced in 2017" = IEA, not OECD/IAEA/MTCR); sought full membership (Oct 2023).
- **Global Biofuels Alliance:** launched 9 Sep 2023 at the **G20 New Delhi** summit; core founders **India, USA, Brazil**; secretariat **New Delhi**.
- **International Energy Forum:** secretariat **Riyadh**; India hosted the **16th IEF Ministerial** in **New Delhi** (April 2018).
# Demand side
- **PM E-DRIVE:** from **1 Oct 2024**, outlay **₹10,900 crore**, **replaced FAME-II**; extended to March 2028. NITI's **India Electric Mobility Index** (Aug 2025).
- **500 GW non-fossil by 2030** and the **Green Hydrogen Mission** = import-substitution tools.
# Critical minerals
- **National Critical Mineral Mission:** approved 29 Jan 2025; **₹34,300 crore** over seven years.
- **KABIL** (2019): JV of **NALCO, HCL and MECL** (Ministry of Mines); five lithium blocks in **Catamarca, Argentina** (Jan 2024).
