## SUB 4 | Court Rulings and Judicial Initiatives
[4:0] # SC: grounds of arrest in writing
[4:1] - Grounds of arrest must be given **in writing** and in a **language the accused understands** - under **Article 22(1)**, for **all offences incl. PMLA and UAPA**.
[4:2] - Written grounds at least **2 hours before production for remand**, else arrest illegal.
[4:3] - **Section 47, BNSS** - inform the arrested person of grounds and bail rights.
[4:4] - Exceptional cases: oral first, written within reasonable time; failure → arrest and remand illegal.
[4:5] - Re-arrest: written grounds + permission of a **Judicial Magistrate**; probe to another officer; disciplinary action on the erring officer.
[4:6] # Article 142 - NEET-UG protest FIRs quashed
[4:7] - SC used **Article 142** ("complete justice") to quash FIRs against students protesting the **NEET-UG paper leak**.
[4:8] - **142(1)**: decrees/orders for complete justice, enforceable across India as Parliament (or, till then, the President) prescribes; **142(2)**: attendance, documents, contempt.
[4:9] - Power is broad but **not unlimited** - cannot override fundamental constitutional limits.
[4:10] - Cases: **Prem Chand Garg (1963)**, Union Carbide, Delhi Judicial Service Association, Supreme Court Bar Association.
