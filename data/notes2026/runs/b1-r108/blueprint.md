## 3. Repeats and trends
- **Most repeated:** Agra – Sikandar Lodi (6); Diwan-i-Amir-i-Kohi – MBT (5); Public Works Dept – Firoz (4); Ibn Battuta under MBT (4); token currency (4); Firoz's canals / haqq-i-sharb (5); Badauni's remark (2); Babur's victory reason (3).
- **Pattern:** almost every Firoz question is "which Sultan set up X" – distractors are always Alauddin, MBT, Iltutmish, Balban. The UP papers repeat the same item year after year.
- **Key traps found:** the RPSC khalisa key "Kankut" (Ghiyasuddin dropped measurement for sharing); two copies of UPPSC 1990 carried different keys (skilled commandant / artillery); "state canal irrigation" (books Firoz, strictly first canals Ghiyasuddin); Adha/Bikh are Firoz's coins, not MBT's.
- The old notes were heavy on **southern revolts (Madurai, Musunuri, Vilasa plate), Firoz's revenue/jizya/iqta, Sayyid trivia (Mubarak Shah's tomb, Khokhars) and Babur's later battles** – asked 0–1 times as PYQs here; kept short for the own MCQs. Babur's later battles go to row 127.
