## SUB 3 | Census Definitions – Urban Area, Towns and Size Classes
[3:0] TABLE head: Type | Criteria
  [3:0.0] Statutory town | Any place with a municipal corporation, municipality, nagar panchayat, cantonment board or notified town area – notified by the State by law
  [3:0.1] Census town (= urban area test, Census 2001/2011) | All 3: population ≥5,000; ≥75% of male main workers in non-agricultural work; density ≥400 per sq km
[3:1] - **No minimum area** and **no electricity criterion** (e.g. "60% households electrified" is not a test; "60% males" is wrong – it is 75%).

[3:3] - **Census 2027** keeps the 1981 census-town criteria; RGI retained them despite **MoHUA's** objection before the Parliamentary Standing Committee on Housing and Urban Affairs (chair **Magunta Sreenivasulu Reddy**).

## SUB 5 | Causes and Social Effects of Urbanisation
[5:0] - Main component: **migration** (natural increase similar in rural and urban areas).
[5:1] - **Push**: monsoon failure, rural unemployment, **lack of rural jobs**, non-growing farm land, **rural-urban imbalance**.
[5:2] - **Pull**: **magnetic features of cities** – jobs, public facilities (health, education), transport, industrial growth.
[5:3] - **Philip M. Hauser** – "**Population Implosion**" = rural-urban concentration of people. "**New Population Bomb**" = rapidly growing **Third World urban population**.
[5:4] - **Crime rises with urbanisation and industrialisation** – industrial-urban life causes family and cultural disorganisation (UPPSC A-R: R explains A).
[5:5] > Statement sets mixing push and pull factors are usually **all correct**.
[5:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Society - Group 2 Prelims 01 p.93 (factors behind urbanisation) and 02 p.1 (urban crime); app notes 3:42 sub 5
## SUB 6 | Urban Challenges – Slums, Housing, Heat, Governance
[6:0] # Slums
[6:1] - **Mumbai**: slums **41.3% of urban households** – highest share among metros (Census 2011); Kolkata 29.6%, Chennai 28.5%, Delhi 14.6%.
[6:2] - Among **million-plus cities**: **Greater Visakhapatnam ~44%** of population in slums; Greater Mumbai and Vijayawada ~41%.
[6:3] - Slum population 2001: Greater Mumbai 6.5 m, **Delhi 1.9 m** (more than Chennai 0.8 m, Bangalore, Surat).
[6:4] - 2011: 6.55 crore slum dwellers = 17.4% of urban population; World Bank estimate ~**177 million**.
[6:5] # Housing, waste, heat, governance
[6:6] - Urban housing shortage ~**18.78 million units** (2012 Technical Group); costs exceed middle-class incomes → cramped, unserviced housing (NextGen).
[6:7] - Sewerage, solid waste and public-transport gaps; private vehicles cause congestion.
[6:8] - **Urban heat island** = a city much **warmer than nearby rural areas** (3-5°C).
[6:9] - Governance: **fragmented authority**, little **fiscal autonomy** (ES 2025-26 ch.15); urbanising areas left under panchayats.
[6:10] - **Urban design** = the arrangement and design of buildings, **streets and public spaces**; urban (city) planning = wider land-use regulation (master plans, **zoning**).
[6:11] - **India Urban Observatory** – **New Delhi** (MoHUA office); data hub for evidence-based planning.
[6:12] - **MoHUA (2017)** = merger of Ministry of Urban Development + **Ministry of Housing and Urban Poverty Alleviation** (MoHUPA – earlier nodal ministry for identifying urban BPL families).
[6:13] - **e-lala** – e-commerce portal for small traders (CAIT); Ministry of Urban Development, Nov 2015.
[6:14] - Social Forestry is NOT under the Minimum Needs Programme.
[6:15] ~~ Sources: NextGen Society - Group 2 Prelims 01 p.93 and 02 p.1-2 (housing, transport, sewerage, master plans, zoning); en.wikipedia.org "Urban design" (via web search); app notes 3:42 sub 6
