=== Sources of Irrigation – India and States
| Source of net irrigated area (India, 2021-22) | Share |
|---|---|
| **Tubewells** | 47.3% (2022-23: 49.34%) |
| Canals | 24.7% (2022-23: 22.85%) |
| Other wells | 13.2% |
| Other sources | 12% |
| Tanks | 2.8% |
- **Dominant source = wells/tubewells (groundwater)**, not canals; groundwater meets about **64%** of irrigation needs; India ≈ **25% of global extraction** and has the **largest groundwater-irrigated area in the world**.
- **Uttar Pradesh**: largest **tubewell-irrigated area** and largest irrigated area overall.
| State | Irrigation fact |
|---|---|
| **Punjab** | Highest **percentage** of cropped area irrigated (~98%) |
| **Haryana** | Highest % among Haryana, UP, MP, Maharashtra (2011: 83.9%) |
| UP, Haryana, Rajasthan, AP | Four States with the **largest canal-irrigated area** |
- **Canals in North India:** fed by **perennial (snow-fed) rivers** – Ganga, Sutlej, Beas – so they flow all year.
- **Tanks** matter in **AP, Telangana, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu** – undulating **hard-rock**, impervious terrain with poor groundwater, where wells fail; hence tank and well maps are **negatively related**.
- **Bamboo drip irrigation:** 200-year-old tribal system of **Meghalaya (North-East India)**.

=== PMKSY and Watershed Development
# PMKSY
- Launched **1 July 2015**; umbrella irrigation scheme; motto: **"Har Khet Ko Pani"** and **"More crop per drop"** – raise **water-use efficiency**.
- Components: **AIBP**, **Har Khet Ko Pani** (assured irrigation; CAD and water management), **Per Drop More Crop** (micro-irrigation; now under PM-RKVY), **Watershed Development**. **National Water Grid is NOT** a component.
- Objectives: **convergence of investment** in irrigation at field level, **expand cultivable area under irrigation**, **improve on-farm water-use efficiency**; works: renovate water bodies, rainwater-harvesting structures, **recharge of aquifers**. **Not** an objective: protecting farmers against crop failure (that is crop insurance – PMFBY); "construction of wells" is the item keyed as incorrect.
- **PMKSY planning:** District Irrigation Plans → State Irrigation Plans; **State Level Sanctioning Committee chaired by the Chief Secretary**.
- **Micro Irrigation Fund**: corpus **Rs 5,000 crore** with **NABARD**.
# Watershed
- **Hariyali** (launched January 2003; guidelines from **1 April 2003**): watershed / rainwater-harvesting scheme = **water management**.
- Hariyali, **IWDP** (Integrated Wasteland Development Programme, 1989-90 nationally; 100% central assistance) and NWDPRA merged into **IWMP** (2008); IWMP subsumed in PMKSY as **WDC-PMKSY** (2015); funding **90:10 → 60:40**; **WDC 2.0** adds springsheds.
- **Neeranchal National Watershed Project**: **World Bank**; **nine States incl. AP and Telangana**.
- **Watershed Development Fund:** NABARD, 1999-2000, corpus Rs 200 crore.
- **Tarun Bharat Sangh**: **Rajasthan** NGO of **Dr Rajendra Singh** ("Waterman", johads).
- **Four Waters Concept** (T. Hanumantha Rao): **rain water, surface water, ground water and soil moisture** – "river water" is not one of the four.

=== Groundwater – CGWB, Aquifers and Atal Bhujal
- **Aquifers:** clay holds water but its pores are tiny and poorly connected → **poor aquifer**; sand and gravel are good aquifers.
- **CGWA** was constituted under **Section 3(3) of the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986**.
- CGWB categories (stage of extraction): **Safe, Semi-critical, Critical, Over-exploited**, + Saline.
- India stage: **60.63% (2025)**; high utilisation in **Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu**, Delhi; low in high-rainfall Odisha.
- **Uttar Pradesh** has the largest **replenishable groundwater** resource (Ganga alluvium).
- **GRACE** satellite studies: severe depletion in **north-west India** – Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan and **Delhi** (epicentre named in the APPSC 2019 key).
- **Atal Bhujal Yojana**: **December 2019**; **World Bank**-aided **Central Sector**; **Ministry of Jal Shakti**; 7 States – Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, MP, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, UP; **AP NOT included**; **demand-side** community management via GP Water Security Plans.
- **Piezometers** (digital water-level recorders with telemetry) give real-time groundwater levels (AP: 1,810 piezometers).

=== PM-KUSUM and Micro-irrigation
- **KUSUM = Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan** (2019, **MNRE**).
| PM-KUSUM | Content |
|---|---|
| A | 10,000 MW decentralised grid-connected solar plants (0.5-2 MW) |
| B | 14 lakh standalone solar pumps (up to 7.5 HP) |
| C | Solarisation of grid-connected pumps, incl. feeder-level |
- Objective: **remove farmers' dependence on diesel and kerosene** – an **agricultural solar pump** scheme; concern: may encourage **over-drawing of groundwater**.
- **Drip irrigation:** water straight to the root zone; fertiliser through the same lines (fertigation).
- **APMIP** (since **2003**): AP ranks **1st** in micro-irrigation; **Rayalaseema 64%** of area; AP has **converged MGNREGA with the AP Micro Irrigation Project**.

=== Andhra Pradesh – Irrigation, Groundwater and APWALTA
| Project | River | Facts |
|---|---|---|
| Polavaram | Godavari | National Project (APRA 2014); 960 MW; diverts 80 TMC to Krishna; **Left Main Canal → East Godavari and Visakhapatnam** (Yeleru, Vizag water); 89% complete; dedication by March 2027 |
| Galeru-Nagari | Krishna | 38 TMC; 2.60 lakh acres |
| Veligonda (Poola Subbaiah) | Krishna | 43.5 TMC; 4.47 lakh acres |
- AP 2024-25: GIA 36.77 lakh ha; groundwater **41% of GIA** (~19 lakh borewells).
| AP net irrigated area by source (lakh ha) | Canals | Tanks | Wells | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2016-17 | 12.25 | 2.36 | 11.62 | 27.19 |
| 2023-24 | 10.08 | 2.55 | **12.42** | 26.03 |
- After bifurcation **groundwater (wells)** has become the major source, overtaking canals.
- **38,862 tanks**; groundwater stage **31.5% (Safe)**; **12 of 679** mandals over-exploited; **Anantapur** district has had the most over-exploited mandals.
- **APWALTA 2002** (AP Water, Land and Trees Act): against indiscriminate **borewell** digging; controls groundwater; promotes tree planting; **rainwater harvesting compulsory on plots of 200 sq m and above**; drinking-water norm in the exam key: one tube well per **250 population** (not verified in the library); over-exploited villages notified (300 villages, GEC 2023-24).
- **HNSS:** lift canal taking Krishna flood water from **Srisailam** to Rayalaseema; **Neeru-Chettu** (2015): drought-free AP via tank desilting.
