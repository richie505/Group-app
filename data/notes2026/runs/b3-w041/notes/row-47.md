=== Water Resources, Water Stress and State Firsts
| Per capita water per year | Status |
|---|---|
| Below 1,700 m³ | Water-stressed |
| Below 1,000 m³ | Water-scarce |
- India has about **4% of the world's water resources** (and ~18% of its population); already **below 1,700 m³** (water-stressed).
- **Cauvery** is the most exploited (most fully utilised) river in terms of abstraction of available water.
- **Water** is the **universal solvent** (it dissolves more substances than any other liquid).
- **Meghalaya**: first State to approve a **draft State Water Policy** (2019).
- **Tamil Nadu**: first to make **rooftop rainwater harvesting compulsory** for all buildings; AP's APWALTA 2002 covers plots 200 sq m+.
- Water = **Entry 17, State List**; inter-State rivers = **Entry 56, Union List**.

=== Water-use Efficiency and Conservation
| Method | Efficiency |
|---|---|
| Drip | ~90% (highest) |
| Sprinkler | ~70-80% |
| Flood/basin/canal surface | 30-50% (lowest) |
- **PMKSY** aims at enhancing **water-use efficiency** ("More crop per drop"), not organic, hybrid or GM seeds.
- **Micro-irrigation**: water straight to the root zone; cuts evaporation and runoff. **DSR** and **SRI**: save up to 50% water.
- **Zero / minimum tillage**: lower evaporation, better soil moisture. **Crop-residue mulch** keeps soil moisture.
- **Conservation Agriculture** (FAO): minimum soil disturbance, **permanent soil cover (at least 30%)**, crop diversification.
- **Bench terracing**: most widely adopted mechanical soil and water conservation measure on **hill slopes**.
- **Virtual water** (Tony Allan): water **consumed in producing a product**; exporting rice exports virtual water.
- **Flow duration curve:** plots discharge against the **percentage of time it is equalled or exceeded**.

=== National River Linking Project
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| National Perspective Plan | 1980 |
| **NWDA** | Set up **July 1982** as a society (not 1970, 1980 or 1992); carries out NPP |
| Links | 16 Peninsular + 14 Himalayan = 30 |
| Progress | PFRs all 30; FRs 26; DPRs 14 |
- **Arthur Cotton**: first proposed (19th century); **K.L. Rao**: Ganga-Cauvery link; **Suresh Prabhu**: 2002 Task Force.
- **Ken-Betwa**: **first NPP link implemented**; MP-UP; MoA March 2021; **Daudhan Dam inside Panna Tiger Reserve**.
| Godavari-Cauvery link | From | To |
|---|---|---|
| Godavari-Krishna | Inchampalli | Nagarjunasagar |
| Krishna-Pennar | Nagarjunasagar | Somasila |
| Pennar-Cauvery | Somasila | Grand Anicut |
- **AP drought-proofing:** **Godavari** surplus water is moved to the Krishna basin – **Pattiseema lift** (Polavaram → Prakasam barrage) and Polavaram's 80 TMC diversion feeding **HNSS and GNSS**.
- Criticism: "surplus" basins unreliable under **climate change**; displacement; ecological loss.

=== Command Area Development
- **CAD Programme 1974-75**: narrow the gap between irrigation potential **created and used**; **AP CADA** August 1974.
- Components: **field channels, drains, land levelling, Water Users' Associations**.
- **CADWM** under **PMKSY-Har Khet Ko Pani**; **no new approvals after 2021-22**.
- **M-CADWM** from 2025-26: Rs 1,600 crore; underground piped irrigation, SCADA/IoT.
