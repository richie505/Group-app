=== Fuel Gases – LPG, Natural Gas, CNG, LNG and Their Chemistry
# What each gas is made of
| Fuel | Main constituents | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Natural gas** | Mostly **methane (CH₄)**, with some ethane, propane, CO₂, N₂ | **Cleanest fossil fuel**; burnt directly or piped |
| **CNG** – **Compressed Natural Gas** | **Methane** (80–90%) | Natural gas compressed to **200–250 kg/cm²**; vehicles |
| **PNG** – Piped Natural Gas | 85–95% methane | Supplied to homes through city gas networks |
| **LNG** – Liquefied Natural Gas | Methane | Cooled to about **−160°C**; volume shrinks about **600 times**; carried in special cryogenic tankers; **lighter than air** |
| **LPG** – Liquefied Petroleum Gas | **Propane + butane** (roughly 65% butane, 35% propane; a little ethane) | Refinery / gas by-product; liquefied under pressure; **heavier than air** |
- **LPG is NOT mainly methane** (IAS 2005: A false, R true). When only one gas is asked, **butane** is the chief constituent; the 3-gas option is **ethane, propane and butane**.
- **LPG is odourless** in its natural state; **ethyl mercaptan (ethanethiol)** is added so that a **leak can be smelt** (the "rotten" stench).
- **Butane (C₄H₁₀)** is the gas in **cigarette lighters**; it is a **Natural Gas Liquid (NGL)**.
- **Propane** (an LPG gas) is a clean-burning fuel; coke, petrol and wax are not "clean energy" (NDA 2019).
- **Methane** is a **greenhouse gas** (ethane, propane, butane are not counted as GHGs); in the air it oxidises to CO₂ in about a decade.
- **Methane + air** (firedamp, 5–15% methane) causes most **explosions in coal mines**.
# Other fuel facts asked
| Fuel / term | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Water gas** | **CO + H₂** (producer gas = CO + H₂ + N₂) |
| **Acetylene (ethyne)** | **Gas welding** (oxy-acetylene flame) |
| **Kerosene** | Calorific value about the **same as petrol** (~45,000 kJ/kg); LPG ~55,000, methane/CNG ~50,000 |
| Hydrocarbon order by molecular weight | **Methane (16) < ethane (30) < propane (44) < butane (58)** |
| **Petroleum** | A mixture of many hydrocarbons of different densities; its **grade** depends on their relative proportion (NDA 2016: both true, II does not explain I) |
- **LNG statements (IAS 2005):** only "LNG is liquefied under extreme cold for storage and transport" is right; India's first LNG terminal is **Dahej** (Gujarat, Petronet LNG, 2004), not Hassan; NGLs (ethane, propane, butane, natural gasoline) are separated from **natural gas**, not from LPG.
> Exam angle: "main constituent of LPG" is asked a dozen times – butane (with propane); methane is natural gas / CNG.

=== Petroleum Geology, Basins and Oil Fields
# Geology
- **Coal, petroleum, natural gas** = conventional, **non-renewable** fossil fuels.
- Oil is found in **Tertiary** sedimentary rocks; petroleum = 90–95% hydrocarbons.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Source rock | Organic-rich shale where oil forms |
| Reservoir rock | **Porous, permeable sandstone or fissured limestone** |
| Cap rock | **Impervious bed that seals the trap** |
| Traps | Anticlines, faults, salt domes; commercial oil at the **crest of an anticline** |
- Layers by density from the top: **gas, oil, water**.
- **Wet gas** = associated with oil; **dry gas** = non-associated; **sour gas** = rich in **H₂S**.
- Conventional gas: permeable sandstone. Unconventional: impermeable sandstone, shale, coal seams – costly technology.

=== Shale Gas, Coal Bed Methane and Gas Hydrates
| Resource | Where held | India |
|---|---|---|
| **Shale gas / oil** | Fine-grained, low-permeability shale below 1,500 m; **horizontal drilling + hydraulic fracturing (fracking)** | **Cambay basin largest**; also KG, Cauvery, Gondwana (Damodar), Assam-Arakan, Indo-Gangetic; ONGC estimate 187.5 TCF in five basins |
| **Coal bed methane (CBM)** | Adsorbed in coal seams; released by **pumping water out** | Damodar valley – **Raniganj** (first commercial output), Jharia, Bokaro |
| **Gas (methane) hydrates** | Methane-water ice crystals under the **seafloor** and in **Arctic tundra / permafrost** | KG, Mahanadi, Andaman offshore (not Damodar) |
- **Hydraulic fracturing** is the technology that stimulates the flow of oil or gas and raises the recoverable volume.
- **Guar gum** from the seeds of the **cluster bean** thickens fracking fluid and carries sand into the cracks – hence guar's new importance (UPSC 2014).
- **Methane hydrate (UPSC 2019):** warming may release the methane; deposits lie under the seafloor and in Arctic tundra; atmospheric methane oxidises to CO₂ in a decade or two – **all three true**.

=== Exploration Policies – NELP, HELP, OALP
| Policy | Year | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| NELP | 1997-99 | Blocks bid out; **production-sharing contracts** (profit split after cost recovery) |
| Discovered Small Fields | 2015 | Auction of small fields of **ONGC and OIL** |
| **HELP** | 2016 | One licence for all hydrocarbons; pricing freedom; **revenue-sharing contract** – firms bid the share of revenue for the Government |
| OALP | 2017 | Firms choose blocks and bid year-round using the **National Data Repository** |
| Samudra Manthan | 3 Aug 2026 | ₹84,084 crore to 2030-31; 50% drilling support (cap ₹675 crore/well); open to all OALP block holders |
- **NELP blocks, JV blocks, nomination blocks and CBM blocks** are all **petroleum exploration** blocks (RAS 2010).
> Exam angle: APPSC G1 2017 – under HELP the Government earns through a **biddable revenue-sharing contract**, not production-sharing.

=== PNGRB and City Gas Distribution
- **PNGRB** (Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board): statutory, **PNGRB Act 2006**, under the **Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas**.
- Regulates **refining, processing, storage, transportation, distribution, marketing and sale** – **excludes production of crude oil and natural gas** (UPSC 2025: only two of the four).
- Authorises entities for **city gas distribution (CGD)** in Geographical Areas and fixes pipeline tariffs.
- **AP CGD:** **10 GAs**; target **83.61 lakh** PNG connections by **March 2032**.
