=== Electricity Basics – Units, Frequency, Transformers
- Discoms bill consumption in **kWh ("units")**; India's grid runs at **50 Hz** (not 60 Hz) – MPPSC 2008: A true, R false.
- **Step-up transformer** raises **voltage** (and lowers current) for long-distance transmission; it does **not** raise power.
- A **transformer works only on AC** (mutual induction); a **capacitor stores electric charge/energy**; a **generator/dynamo** converts mechanical to electrical energy.
| Transformer / generator fact | Answer |
|---|---|
| Protection not provided on small distribution transformers | **Buchholz relay** (used on oil-filled transformers of about 500 kVA and above) |
| Power factor of a transformer | **Depends on the power factor of the load** |
| Maximum efficiency of a distribution transformer | Designed for about **50% of full load** (it runs lightly loaded most of the day) |
| Low power factor of a DG set | Needs **higher excitation current** |
- The **integrated national (all-India) power grid** evens out supply-demand mismatches between regions.

=== Transmission – One Nation One Grid
- **One Nation-One Grid-One Frequency** = world's **largest synchronous grid**; over 5 lakh ckm.
- **Five regional grids** (N, E, W, NE, S); **Southern** synchronised last, **December 2013**.
- **POWERGRID (PGCIL)** = Central Transmission Utility.
- **Green Energy Corridor:** began with a **PGCIL study (2012)**; built from 2015.
- Record peak **270.82 GW** (21 May 2026); peak shortage **4.2% (2014) → 0.03% (2025)**.
- **BESS** helps meet the **evening peak** when solar fades.

=== Discom Finances – Why the SEBs Went Sick
- Causes: **below-cost sale** to farm and domestic users, **poor cost recovery**, high T&D losses, **rising commercial losses**, **delays** in commissioning new projects.
- **Not a cause:** the **autonomy of States** over generation and distribution (APPSC G2 Mains 2017).
| Metric | Meaning | Trend |
|---|---|---|
| AT&C loss | **Technical + commercial** loss | 22.62% (FY14) → **15.04%** (FY25) |
| ACS-ARR gap | **Average cost of supply minus average revenue realised** per unit | ₹0.78 → **₹0.06** |
- DISCOMs: loss ₹67,962 crore (2013-14) → **profit ₹2,701 crore** (FY25). States spend about **₹2.4 lakh crore/year** on power subsidies.
- **PFC Integrated Rating** grades DISCOMs every year.
~~ Sources: app notes 5:59 sub 6; APPSC G2 Mains 2017 key

=== UDAY, RDSS and Smart Meters
- **UDAY** (Ujwal DISCOM Assurance Yojana, Ministry of Power, **Nov 2015**): financial turnaround – States took over **75%** of DISCOM debt.
| Operational / supply side | Demand side |
|---|---|
| Feeder and DT metering, smart meters, transformer upgrades, **coal swapping**, cutting **transmission loss**, cheaper funds | **Energy-efficient LED bulbs (UJALA)**, efficient pumps, fans, ACs |
- **RDSS (2021):** **₹3.03 lakh crore**; results-linked; to FY 2027-28; nodal **REC and PFC**; smart meters on **TOTEX (PPP)**; **20.33 crore** smart meters sanctioned.
- **EESL** signed an MoU with **PNGRB** for smart PNG meters.
> Exam angle: the UDAY "demand-side intervention" is **LED bulbs**; coal swapping and lower transmission loss are supply-side.
~~ Sources: app notes 5:59 sub 7; APPSC G1 key (UDAY)
