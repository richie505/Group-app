=== NOTES 5:58 | Oil and Natural Gas – Basins, Refineries, Spr, Gas Grid, City Gas Distribution
## SUB 0 | Energy Classification and Petroleum Geology
[58:0:0] - **Coal, petroleum, natural gas** = conventional, **non-renewable** fossil fuels. **Natural gas = cleanest fossil fuel**; coal most polluting.
[58:0:1] - Oil formed in the **Tertiary** period; petroleum = 90–95% hydrocarbons.
[58:0:2] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [58:0:2.0] Source rock | Organic-rich shale where oil forms
  [58:0:2.1] Reservoir rock | Porous, permeable sandstone or fissured limestone
  [58:0:2.2] Cap rock | Impervious bed sealing the trap
  [58:0:2.3] Traps | Anticlines, fault traps, salt domes; most reserves in anticlines + fault traps; commercial oil at crests of anticlines
[58:0:3] - Layers by density – from top: **gas, oil, water** (from bottom: water, oil, gas).
[58:0:4] - **Wet gas** = associated; **dry gas** = non-associated; **sour gas** = H₂S-rich.
[58:0:5] - Conventional gas: permeable sandstone. Unconventional: impermeable sandstone, shale, coal seams – costly technology.

=== NOTES 5:60 | Hydropower and Pumped Storage; Large Dams and Their Trade-offs
## SUB 0 | Energy Conversion in A Hydro Plant
[60:0:0] - Sequence: stored water (**potential**) → falling water (**kinetic**) → turbine (**mechanical**) → generator (**electrical**, by **electromagnetic induction**).
[60:0:1] - Net: **potential → electrical** (APPSC 2023).
[60:0:2] - Mechanical energy = **kinetic + potential**. **Thermal energy** = internal energy of moving molecules, not potential; electric, chemical, nuclear are stored (potential) forms.
[60:0:3] TABLE head: Device / process | Conversion
  [60:0:3.0] Dynamo / generator | Mechanical → electrical (DC via commutator)
  [60:0:3.1] Electric motor | Electrical → mechanical
  [60:0:3.2] Battery / dry cell | Chemical → electrical
  [60:0:3.3] Photoelectric / solar cell | Light → electrical
  [60:0:3.4] Wind turbine | Kinetic → mechanical/electrical
  [60:0:3.5] Free fall, pendulum, roller coaster | Potential → kinetic
  [60:0:3.6] Friction | Kinetic → heat
[60:0:4] - Dynamo and motor work on **electromagnetic induction (Faraday's law)**. Wind energy is only **kinetic**. **Photoelectric effect**: **Heinrich Hertz, 1887**. Lightning burning a tree = huge **electric** energy. PYQ key: electrical energy converted into motion – no heat loss.
[60:0:5] - **Chemical energy:** energy stored in the **bonds of atoms and molecules** (food, fuels, dry cells); a stored (potential) form.
## SUB 1 | Types and Classification
