# States' and AP's share by Finance Commission
| FC | States' share | AP's share |
|---|---|---|
| 13th | 32% | – |
| 14th (2015-20) | **42%** | **4.305%** |
| 15th (2021-26) | **41%** (J&K became a UT) | **4.05%** |
| 16th (2026-31), **Arvind Panagariya** | **41%** | **4.217%** |
- **Special Category:** began **1969**, credited to the **5th Finance Commission** (Mahavir Tyagi) in most books – strictly conferred by the **NDC** under the **Gadgil formula**; the **14th FC (2015)** stopped distinguishing special and general States.
- **16th FC criteria:** income distance **42.5%**, population (2011) **17.5%**, demographic performance 10%, area 10%, forest 10%, **contribution to GDP 10% (new)**; tax effort dropped. 18 States asked for 50%; all five southern States gained.
- **Grants:** post-devolution **revenue deficit grants** under the 14th and 15th FCs (Art. 275); **16th FC ended RD grants**. 16th FC award to AP (2026-31): rural local bodies ₹16,627 cr, urban ₹12,158 cr, disaster management ₹6,125 cr.
- **SDRF/SDMF corpus ₹2,04,401 cr**, **75:25** (general), **90:10** (NE/Himalayan); heatwaves and lightning added. **14th FC SDRF to AP:** ~**₹2,492 cr** (2015-20).
- Divisible pool ≈ 81% of gross tax revenue (cesses excluded). Tax devolution to AP: ₹50,475 cr (2024-25 BE), ₹52,080 cr (RE), **₹64,362 cr (2026-27 BE)**.

=== Central Sector vs Centrally Sponsored Schemes
| Feature | Central Sector (CS) | Centrally Sponsored (CSS) |
|---|---|---|
| Subjects | Union List | State/Concurrent |
| Funding | **100% Centre**, run by Central Ministries | **60:40** general; **90:10** special category/NE/Himalayan |
| Examples | PM-KISAN, Ayushman Bharat, MPLADS | NHM, ICDS, PMAY, PMKSY |
| Union spending 2026-27 | 17% | 8% |
- **Rationalisation (2016):** on the CMs' sub-group report, CSS were merged into **28 umbrella schemes** (core-of-the-core + core + optional).
- **Bharatmala Pariyojana** (2017): umbrella programme for **holistic highway development**.
- **PFMS** (Public Financial Management System, CGA): online system through which the Centre **tracks fund flow to States** under schemes. **SNA-SPARSH:** **just-in-time** release of CSS funds via **State IFMIS, RBI e-Kuber and PFMS**; 28 States + 3 UTs, 66 CSS.
- **AP ranked first** among States in **28 CSS and central sector schemes** (up to Nov 2018).
- After the **Plan/non-Plan** merger, the **Tribal Sub-Plan** continues as the **Scheduled Tribe Component** – **commensurate allocation** within schemes.
- **VB-G RAM G Act** replaced MGNREGA (Centre paid 100% of unskilled wages) with **60:40**. New 5-year CS/CSS appraisal cycle from 1 Apr 2026; **16th FC wants outcome-linked CSS**. CSS matching shares (**rigid co-financing**) squeeze States' fiscal space; **CSS ≈ 40%** of central transfers.
- Central funds sent **directly to State implementing agencies** in 2016-17: **₹1,033.19 cr** (APPSC key).

=== Package in Lieu of SCS and Polavaram
- SCS not given; **Special Assistance Measure ₹24,350 cr**, incl. **₹3,979 cr RD grant**.
- Centre pays **90%** of **externally aided project** cost; EAP inflow ₹16,252 cr (2024-25 RE), ₹21,585 cr (2025-26 RE). Amaravati: ₹15,000 cr special assistance (Jan 2026).
- **Polavaram Irrigation Project** = **national project** under **Section 90, APRA 2014**; the **Centre executes and funds** it (irrigation component); older estimate **₹55,548 cr**; Phase I revised cost ₹30,436.95 cr (Aug 2024). State lines ₹6,705 cr (2025-26), ₹6,105 cr (2026-27).
- **APRA Eleventh Schedule para 8:** **ongoing and new projects on the Krishna and Godavari** are executed by the **State in which the project is located**.

=== Plan-era Financing and the 2017-18 Reforms
- State Plans = **own resources + Normal Central Assistance** via the **Gadgil (Gadgil-Mukherjee) formula**; NCA loans:grants = **70:30** general, **10:90** special category.
- **Budget 2017-18:** Budget advanced to **1 February**, **Railway Budget merged**, **Plan/non-Plan** distinction ended (Outcome Budget came earlier, 2005); it did **not** increase the number of CSS.
- Trap: Gadgil **70:30 = loans:grants**; CSS **60:40 = cost sharing**.
