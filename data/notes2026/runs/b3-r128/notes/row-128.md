=== Local Candidates Act and Employment Guarantee
| Law / scheme | Year | Key provision |
|---|---|---|
| **AP Employment of Local Candidates in Industries/Factories Act** | **July 2019** | **75% of private-sector jobs** in industries/factories reserved for **locals** – AP the **first State** to do so |
| **MGNREGS** (NREGA 2005) | launched **2 Feb 2006** from **Bandlapalli, Anantapur district** (AP) | 100 days of wage work; Centre paid 100% of unskilled wages |
| **VB-G RAM G** (125-day Act, replaces MGNREGA) | began **1 Jul 2026**; national launch at **Mukkavaripalli (Tirupati district)** | **125 days**; pause up to **60 days** in peak farm season; **60:40** Centre:State |
- Local-jobs Act criticised nationally as a **deterrent to investors**.
- **MGNREGS 2025-26:** AP **3rd** nationally – **1,635.10 lakh person-days** (labour budget 1,800 lakh); **women 60.54%**; 1.06 lakh households got 100 days; wage rate **₹307/day** (from ₹300).
- **GP buildings in MGNREGS convergence:** funding **90:10**, unit value **₹19 lakh in tribal areas** (APPSC key; not verified).
- **PLFS 2023-24:** AP unemployment **4.1%** (all ages); **youth unemployment ~17.5%**, far **above** the national level.
> Exam angle: "75% / 2019 / first State" is asked again and again; "125 days, not 100; 60:40".

=== APSSDC and AP Skill Programmes
- **APSSDC** (AP State Skill Development Corporation): set up **2014** as a not-for-profit company on the lines of **NSDC**; **nodal agency for placement-linked training** via **Skill Hubs, Skill Spokes**, colleges and **Sector Skill Councils**; 50 skill hubs in rural colleges (Jan 2026).
- **70% placement** is the minimum for all State skill schemes.
- **Siemens Centres of Excellence:** APSSDC with **Siemens** in engineering colleges (e.g. **JNTU Anantapur**).
- **Merger (Apr 2026):** all skill departments into one **Commissionerate of Skill, Employment and Training** – motto "**One District – One Skill**".
- **FY 2025-26:** 1,11,159 trained in Employability Skill Centres; **PM Internship Scheme 72,481** enrolments.
| Programme | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Rajiv Udyogasri** (2007) | skill training that placed **rural youth in private-sector jobs** |
| **Naipunyam** | AI portal: **skilling, certification and job matching**; goal **20 lakh by 2029** |
| **Skill International** | **German (A1–B2)** training for overseas jobs, mainly nurses; LoI (6 May 2026) with **Japan's FVCC** (not German) |
| **Skill Census 2024** | household survey of the **skills, qualifications and jobs of 100% of youth** |
| **Nirudyoga Bruthi** (Super Six) | **₹3,000/month** to unemployed youth |
| **Yuva Nestham** | allowance for educated youth, **2014–19 TDP** government |
- **Super Six** employment target: **20 lakh jobs in 5 years**.

=== Livelihood Missions, Recruitment and Overseas Jobs
| Mission | Implementing society in AP |
|---|---|
| **DAY-NRLM** (rural) | **SERP** – AP **1st in India in SHG-bank linkage** |
| **DAY-NULM** (urban) | **MEPMA** – **2.74 lakh SHGs** |
| **DDU-GKY** (rural youth placement) | **SEEDAP** |
- **Streenidhi:** loans to SHG members up to **₹1 lakh at 11%**; **20.31 lakh Lakhpati Didis**.
- **Mega DSC-2025:** **15,941** teachers; upper age limit for govt jobs **44 years till 2027**.
- Village/ward secretariats renamed **Swarna Gramam and Swarna Ward** department; staff as Village Skill Officers.
- AP **top State on iGOT Karmayogi** (1 crore+ enrolments).
- **OMCAP** (**Overseas Manpower Company AP Ltd**): agency under the **Emigration Act, 1983**; Gulf migration heavy among women from **Konaseema**; housemaids must go via OMCAP – **over 90%** of abuse complaints come from those who did not.
