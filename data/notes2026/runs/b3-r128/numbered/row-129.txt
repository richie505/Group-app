[0:5] - **Portals:** **Jnanabhumi** – scholarships and fee reimbursement (Aadhaar-linked); **Pedarikampai Gelupu** – welfare corporations' bank-linked loans (2014-19); **OBMMS** = **Online Beneficiary Monitoring and Management System** of the welfare corporations.
[0:6] - **APSWREIS** residential schools: results **higher** than the State average.

[0:9] - Vehicle scheme (four-wheelers for SC youth): about **35% subsidy**.
[0:10] - **Inter-caste marriage incentive:** **₹50,000** a couple.

[0:18] - **Nai Roshni** (Ministry of Minority Affairs): **leadership development of minority women**.
