[1:0] - **Revenue receipts** = income the government **need not pay back** = **own tax + own non-tax + share in central taxes + grants-in-aid from the Centre**. Centre's gross tax order (2009 question): customs > corporation tax > income tax > Union excise.

## SUB 2 | Own Non-tax Revenue and Minerals
[2:0] - Non-tax = receipts not raised by taxing the public: **interest receipts, dividends and profits of PSUs, fees, fines, user charges, royalties** (GST is a tax, not non-tax). AP's 2023-24 BE non-tax: **₹15,400 cr**. **User charges** = a fiscal consolidation tool; State PSU profits/dividends are in "Others" (₹2,680 cr, 2024-25 RE).

[2:5] - **Minor minerals:** **sand** is a minor mineral; **States** frame the rules for granting minor-mineral concessions (MMDR s.15) and to **prevent illegal mining** (s.23C).
[2:6] - **MADA v. MMDR amended 2023; India in **Minerals Security Partnership**; not rich in all **30 critical minerals**.
[2:7] - **Free Sand Policy (8 July 2024):** buyers pay only operation cost, seigniorage and taxes.
