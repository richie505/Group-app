  [122:0:1.0] Egoistic | Too little integration
  [122:0:1.1] Altruistic | Too much integration

=== NOTES 2:123 | Poverty, Unemployment and Social Exclusion As Social Problems
## SUB 0 | Poverty – Concepts and Social Indicators
[123:0:0] # Absolute vs Relative Poverty
[123:0:1] TABLE head: Concept | Meaning | Measured by
  [123:0:1.0] Absolute poverty | Income or spending below the minimum needed for basic needs | A fixed poverty line (MPCE cut-off)
  [123:0:1.1] Relative poverty | Being poor compared with others in the same society | Inequality / share of income
  [123:0:1.2] Multidimensional poverty | Deprivation in health, education and living standards together | National MPI (NITI Aayog)
[123:0:2] - **MPCE** (Monthly Per Capita Consumption Expenditure) sets the poverty line in India.
[123:0:3] - India's official poverty line is a **consumption-based** (not income-based) line.
[123:0:4] # Social Indicators of Poverty
[123:0:5] - **Illiteracy**, **malnutrition**, **poor health care**, lack of **safe drinking water** and **sanitation**.
[123:0:6] **Size of the house** is NOT a social indicator of poverty (UPPSC 2025 key: "Only 4" was the odd one).
[123:0:7] # Culture of Poverty
[123:0:8] - Concept given by **Oscar Lewis (1959)**.
[123:0:9] - Meaning: **fatalism**, low aspiration and poverty are **passed down generations**; the poor adapt to poverty and it becomes a way of life.
[123:0:10] "Culture of poverty" = Oscar Lewis (1959). "Social exclusion" = René Lenoir (France, 1974).

## SUB 4 | Unemployment – Types
[123:4:0] TABLE head: Type | Meaning | Predominant sector
  [123:4:0.0] Disguised (hidden) | More workers than needed; marginal productivity of extra workers near zero | Agriculture (family farms), informal sector
  [123:4:0.1] Seasonal | Work only in some months of the year | Agriculture; also construction, tourism
  [123:4:0.2] Structural | Mismatch between skills and the jobs available; long-term | Industry / whole economy
  [123:4:0.3] Cyclical | Fall in labour demand during an economic slowdown | Industry and services (small in India)
  [123:4:0.4] Frictional | Temporary, while changing or searching for jobs | Urban labour market
  [123:4:0.5] Educated | Joblessness among the educated; weak skill-industry link | Urban areas
[123:4:1] Disguised and seasonal unemployment = rural/agriculture. Educated and frictional = urban. Cyclical = trade cycle (slowdown).
