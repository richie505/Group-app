## SUB 2 | Human Trafficking
[2:0] - **Meaning:** trade in humans for sexual slavery, forced labour or commercial sexual exploitation – a form of **modern slavery**.
[2:1] - **Article 23 (Fundamental Right – Right against Exploitation)** prohibits **traffic in human beings, begar and forced labour** – it is **NOT a Directive Principle** (UPSC 2008).
[2:2] - **Immoral Traffic (Prevention) Act, 1956** (SITA 1956, renamed ITPA in 1986) is the main law on sex trafficking; **BNS Sec. 143** defines trafficking; also POCSO 2012, Bonded Labour (Abolition) Act 1976; India signed the **Palermo Protocol**.
[2:3] - **Most cases – West Bengal:** NCRB *Crime in India 2016*: **West Bengal 3,579 cases (~44%)**, then Rajasthan (1,422).
[2:4] - **Anti Human Trafficking Units (AHTUs):** **696** across States/UTs (NCRB *Crime in India 2020*); ~2,183 trafficking cases in 2023 (NCRB).
[2:5] - UNODC: India is both a **high-origin and high-destination** country.
[2:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Society G2 Part 3 p.37; PMF Lens Apr 2026 p.9; Laxmikanth 8th ed. (Art. 23); web: fighttrafficking.org (NCRB 2016); app notes 2:122 sub 5
## SUB 3 | Beggary
[3:0] - **West Bengal** tops the Social Justice Ministry's Census 2011 list of beggars and vagrants (**81,224**), then **UP** (65,835), **AP** (30,218), **Bihar** (29,723); India total **4,13,670** (BPSC 2017).
[3:1] - **Bombay Prevention of Begging Act, 1959**: punitive model copied by many States.
[3:2] - **Delhi HC (2018, Harsh Mander)**: **decriminalised begging** in Delhi.
[3:3] - **SC (2021)**: refused to ban begging; a **socio-economic problem**.
[3:4] - **SMILE (MoSJE, 2022)**: sub-scheme for **comprehensive rehabilitation of persons engaged in begging** ("Bhiksha Vritti Mukt Bharat"); rights-based, not punitive; 216 cities, target 295 by 2030-31.
[3:5] - **Indore**: **first officially beggar-free city (2025)**.
[3:6] ~~ Sources: PMF Lens Jul 2026 p.63-64 (SMILE, Indore); web: gktoday.in (Lok Sabha reply, beggars by State); app notes 2:122 sub 2
## SUB 4 | Alcoholism
[4:0] - **Art. 47 (DPSP)**: State to prohibit **intoxicating drinks** and drugs injurious to health **except for medicinal purposes**.
[4:1] - **Health effect:** chronic alcohol and drug use → **liver cirrhosis** and **nervous-system damage** (not leukaemia/lymphoma).
[4:2] - **Anti-Arrack Movement**: began **1992**, **Dubagunta** village, **Nellore** district (not Nizamabad); neo-literate women of the literacy campaign; led by **Dubagunta Rosamma**.
[4:3] - Precedent: **R. Venkataratnam Naidu's Social Purity Association (1891)**, a temperance campaign.
[4:4] ~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. (DPSP, Art. 47); PMF General Science 05 p.5 (cirrhosis); app notes 2:122 sub 3

## SUB 6 | Jogini (Devadasi) System
[6:0] - **Jogini/Devadasi/Basavi:** dedication of girls (mostly Dalit) to a village deity, leading to sexual exploitation – a form of trafficking and social evil.
[6:1] - **Law:** **AP Devadasis (Prohibition of Dedication) Act, 1988/89**; AP rehabilitates Jogin women through APSCCFC economic-support schemes.
[6:2] - **Most prevalent (Telangana):** old **Mahbubnagar** district (about **2,000 Joginis**); also Nizamabad, Medak, Adilabad; only marginal in Karimnagar, Warangal, Khammam.
[6:3] ~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 (Jogin rehabilitation, Devadasis Act); web: thehansindia.com 21 Mar 2017 (Mahbubnagar); web: deccanchronicle.com (Adilabad)
