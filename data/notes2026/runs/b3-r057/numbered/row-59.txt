## SUB 0 | Malnutrition – Terms, Indicators and Burden
[0:0] TABLE head: Indicator | Measure | Type
  [0:0.0] Stunting | Low height-for-age | Chronic
  [0:0.1] Wasting | Low weight-for-height | Acute
  [0:0.2] Underweight | Low weight-for-age | Composite
[0:1] - **Undernutrition** = **protein-energy malnutrition + micronutrient deficiency + stunting + wasting** (i, ii, iii, v); overweight and **obesity** are **overnutrition**. Both together in one population = **double burden of malnutrition**.
[0:2] - Malnutrition results from **all** of: inadequate diet, disease/infection, poverty and food insecurity. Biggest factor: **poor dietary habits and food insecurity**; **open defecation and unsafe water** add through **disease-linked loss of nutrients** – it is multi-causal, not diet alone.
[0:3] - **Both rich and poor are malnourished** – the rich eat a wrong diet, the poor a poor diet (A and R true, R explains A).
[0:4] - **FAO estimate (c. 2012): about 245 million undernourished Indians** (SOFI 2022 revised series: 247.8 million in 2004-06 → **224.3 million in 2019-21**; SOFI 2025: 12.0% of the population).

[0:6] - **Anaemia (NFHS-5):** children under 5 about **67%**, women about **57%**. **AP (NFHS-5):** stunting **31.2%**, wasting 16.1%, underweight 29.6%.

## SUB 2 | PM POSHAN (Mid-Day Meal)
[2:0] - **NP-NSPE** (National Programme of Nutritional Support to Primary Education) launched **15 August 1995** as a Centrally Sponsored Scheme in 2,408 blocks (started in UP the same year); renamed **PM POSHAN in 2021**.
[2:1] - Covers **Bal Vatika and classes I-VIII** in government and aided schools; financed and managed by the **Ministry of HRD (now Education)**; Centre-State **60:40** (non-NER States/UTs).
[2:2] - Cooking cost for Bal Vatika and primary raised to **₹6.78** per child per day.
[2:3] - Organised by **school management, PRIs and SHGs**; a **contractor** does not initiate or organise it. Partner NGO: **Akshay Patra Foundation** (2000, Bengaluru) – mid-day meals for school students.
[2:4] ~~ Sources: app notes 3:59 sub 7; Ramesh Singh Indian Economy part 04 p.165 (MDMS)
## SUB 3 | POSHAN Abhiyaan and Anaemia Mukt Bharat
[3:0] - **POSHAN Abhiyaan (2018):** goal **Suposhit Bharat**; pillars **convergence, technology, Jan Andolan** (people's movement); targets stunting, underweight, anaemia and low birth weight – **not adult obesity**.
[3:1] - **Rashtriya Poshan Maah = September**; **Poshan Pakhwada** (April, two weeks; 2025 edition) – to **improve nutrition**, especially of women and children.
[3:2] - **Saksham Anganwadi and Poshan 2.0** (2021-22 to 2025-26): **2 lakh** Saksham AWCs; **POSHAN Tracker** = real-time monitoring of Anganwadi services.
[3:3] - **Anaemia Mukt Bharat:** **IFA tablets, deworming**, outreach for **6 target groups**; **WIFS** for adolescents **10-19**; **delayed cord clamping** campaign; **periodic deworming**; addresses **non-nutritional causes** (malaria, haemoglobinopathies, fluorosis). It does **not** give prophylactic **calcium** → **only three** of the four UPSC 2023 statements are correct.
