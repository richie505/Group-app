=== NOTES 3:57 | Minimum Support Price – Mechanism, Cacp, Crops, Procurement, Criticism; Pm-aasha
## SUB 0 | Msp – Concept and Authority
[57:0:0] - **MSP** = government-announced **minimum (floor) price** for selected crops, declared **before** each kharif/rabi sowing.
[57:0:1] - Purpose: protect farmers against a **sharp fall** in prices and **distress sales**; if market price < MSP, agencies buy the stock offered; builds **PDS** buffer stocks (food security).
[57:0:2] - **CACP recommends**; **Government of India – CCEA approves/fixes**.
[57:0:3] - Legal status: **administrative decision**, not a legal right; no statute.
[57:0:4] - Marketing policy tools: **PDS, MSP, buffer stock**.
[57:0:5] An old APPSC G2 2017 key says CACP "sets" MSP; in fact CACP only recommends and CCEA decides.
## SUB 1 | Cacp
[57:1:0] - **Agricultural Prices Commission (APC)**: **January 1965** → renamed **CACP in 1985**.
[57:1:1] - **Attached office** of the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare; **not statutory**.
[57:1:2] - Composition: Chairman + Member Secretary + 1 official + 2 non-official members.
[57:1:3] - Main objective: **incentivise farmers** → modern technology → productivity → production. Also stable prices, fair farm income, consumer interest (not maximum prices).
[57:1:4] - **Factors**: cost of production, demand and supply, domestic and **international prices**, **inter-crop price parity**, terms of trade agriculture vs non-agriculture, minimum 50% margin over cost, effect on consumers.
[57:1:5] - Also recommends **FRP for sugarcane**.
## SUB 2 | Cost Concepts
[57:2:0] TABLE head: Concept | Meaning
  [57:2:0.0] A2 | Actual paid-out costs (seeds, fertilisers, hired labour, fuel, irrigation)
  [57:2:0.1] FL | Imputed family labour
  [57:2:0.2] A2+FL | Paid-out cost + family labour (leaves out own-land rent, interest on fixed capital)
  [57:2:0.3] C2 | A2 + FL + land rent + interest on fixed capital (fullest cost)
[57:2:1] - Present policy: MSP at least **1.5 × (A2+FL)** = 50% margin over A2+FL, **not C2**.
[57:2:2] - **Swaminathan Commission (NCF, 2004-06)**: **C2 + 50%**; SKM says paddy at C2+50% should be **₹3,243/q** (2026-27) vs ₹2,441 fixed.
## SUB 3 | Crops Covered
[57:3:0] - **22 mandated crops** + **sugarcane FRP** = 23. Split: **14 kharif + 6 rabi + 2 commercial** (jute, copra).

  [57:3:1.0] Cereals | 7 | Paddy, wheat, barley, jowar, maize, ragi, bajra
  [57:3:1.1] Pulses | 5 | Gram, tur, moong, urad, lentil

  [57:3:1.3] Commercial | 3 | Copra, cotton, raw jute
[57:3:2] - **Not covered**: fruits, vegetables, onion, potato, most spices, **areca nut, coffee, turmeric** (under MIS instead).
[57:3:3] - **Niger** (Guizotia abyssinica): MSP, **kharif**, tribal cooking oil – all correct (UPSC 2023); oil content 37-47%.
[57:3:4] - **Cotton**: MSP for medium and long staple; nodal agency **CCI** (Ministry of Textiles).
[57:3:5] - Tobacco, cotton, sugarcane, oilseeds = **cash crops**; rice, millets, pulses = food crops.

## SUB 7 | Pm-aasha and Price Support Beyond Msp
[57:7:0] - **Pradhan Mantri Annadata Aay SanraksHan Abhiyan**, **September 2018**, Ministry of Agriculture; aim: MSP reaches farmers incl. small and marginal (farmer welfare).
[57:7:1] - Outlay **₹35,000 crore** to 2025-26 (APPSC 2023).
[57:7:2] TABLE head: Component | Nature
  [57:7:2.0] PSS | Physical procurement of pulses, oilseeds, copra by NAFED, NCCF; registered farmers; 25% cap
  [57:7:2.1] PDPS | Pays MSP minus market price; no buying; mainly oilseeds
  [57:7:2.2] PPSS | 2018 pilot; private stockists bought oilseeds at MSP
  [57:7:2.3] MIS | Perishables not under MSP; trigger ≥10% price fall or production rise; loss 50:50 (75:25 NE)
[57:7:3] - 2026 components: PSS, PDPS, MIS. PSS 2024-25: 61.87 LMT, >₹39,000 crore, 26.56 lakh farmers.
[57:7:4] - **Pulses mission**: 100% MSP buying of **tur, urad, masoor** for **4 years**.
[57:7:5] - **Price Stabilisation Fund (2014-15)**: buffers of **onion, potato, pulses**.
[57:7:6] - **Kerala**: first State with floor prices for **16 vegetables (Nov 2020)**.
[57:7:7] - AP maize sold at ₹1,400-1,600 vs ₹2,400 MSP (May 2026).
## SUB 8 | Phases of Agricultural Development and Policy Since 1991
[57:8:0] TABLE head: Phase | Hallmark
  [57:8:0.0] Pre-Green Revolution (1950-65) | Land reforms, community development, irrigation
  [57:8:0.1] Green Revolution (mid-1960s) | HYV wheat, rice; APC and FCI 1965; "Wheat Revolution" stamp July 1968
  [57:8:0.2] Price-support phase | MSP, FCI buffer stocks, PDS at issue price
  [57:8:0.3] Post-1991 | Liberalisation, WTO 1995, e-NAM, FPOs, PM-KISAN
[57:8:1] - **WTO Agreement on Agriculture** (1995): pillars – market access, domestic support, export subsidies. **Amber box** (trade-distorting, e.g. MSP price support) capped by **de minimis 10%** of production value for developing countries (5% developed); **green box** (research, infrastructure) unlimited; **blue box** production-limiting payments.
[57:8:2] - **Peace clause (Bali, 2013)** protects public stockholding for food security.
[57:8:3] - **Bt cotton** approved **2002** (GM crop).
## SUB 9 | Criticism and the Legal-guarantee Debate

## SUB 0 | Food Management – Objectives
[58:0:0] - Department of Food and Public Distribution works through **procurement, storage and movement, public distribution, buffer stocks**.
[58:0:1] TABLE head: Arm | Objective
  [58:0:1.0] PDS | Minimum grain at affordable prices for low-income groups; equitable distribution; control of price swings
  [58:0:1.1] Procurement | Assure MSP; free grain for the weak under PMGKAY; market intervention to check prices
[58:0:2] - Buying at support prices gives price stability, remunerative prices and stocks for PDS.
[58:0:3] **Export of foodgrains** is NOT an objective of food management (BPSC 2020).
## SUB 1 | Food Corporation of India
[58:1:0] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [58:1:0.0] Set up | 14 January 1965 (same year as APC)

  [58:1:0.4] Role | Nodal agency buying wheat and paddy under price support, with State agencies
[58:1:1] - **Missions**: (1) procurement at MSP; (2) storage, movement and distribution; (3) operational and **buffer stocks**; (4) grain at reasonable prices to weaker sections through **TPDS**.
[58:1:2] - NOT a mission: **regulating markets in agricultural products** (State APMC laws) (APPSC 2018).
[58:1:3] - **Digital systems**: **OSM (Online Storage Management)** from **1 April 2022** – storage, movement, distribution under TPDS (APPSC 2021); **Anna Darpan** (2024) – FCI supply-chain system of the Dept of Food and PD, **not Agriculture Ministry**.
