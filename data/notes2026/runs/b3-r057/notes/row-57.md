=== MSP – Meaning, CACP and Who Fixes It
- **Minimum Support Price (MSP):** a form of **market intervention** by the Government of India to insure farmers against a **sharp fall in farm prices** – a guarantee (floor) price announced **at the beginning of the sowing season**, before the crop reaches the market.
- **Purpose:** save farmers from **distress sales** and **procure foodgrains for public distribution** (PDS buffer stocks → food security); it **ensures a minimum fixed price** and supports food security, but it is **not** a promise of "immense profit", and farmers sell to the government only when the **market price falls below MSP**.
- **Mechanism:** if the market price falls below MSP (bumper crop, glut), **government agencies purchase the stock offered** by farmers at MSP.
- **Who:** **CACP recommends → Government of India (Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs, CCEA) approves/fixes**. Exam answer for "MSP is determined/decided/recommended by": **CACP** – not ICAR, IARI, NABARD, Planning Commission, RBI, President, Finance Commission, State Governments or FCI.
- **Legal status:** an **administrative decision**, not backed by a statute; not a legal right.
- Government's tools to improve agricultural marketing: **PDS, MSP and buffer stocks** – all three ("more than one of the above").
| CACP fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Origin | **Agricultural Prices Commission (APC), January 1965** (same year as FCI) |
| Renamed | **CACP in 1985** |
| Status | **Attached office** of the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare; a decentralised agency, **not statutory** |
| Composition | Chairman + **Member Secretary + 1 official member + 2 non-official members** |
| Main objective | Recommend MSPs to **incentivise farmers to adopt modern technology**, raise productivity and output |
| Mandated objectives (UPSC 1995) | **Stabilise agricultural prices; ensure meaningful real income to farmers; protect consumers via PDS** – **not** "maximum price for the farmer" |
- **Ashok Gulati** (CACP Chairman 2011-14): long-term food security needs agricultural growth of **more than 4% a year**.
> Exam angle: "Who determines MSP" – more than a dozen PYQs; the key is always CACP (strictly it recommends, CCEA decides).

=== CACP Factors, Cost Concepts and the MSP Formula
- **Factors CACP considers:** **cost of production**, demand and supply, **price trends in domestic and international markets**, **inter-crop price parity**, terms of trade between agriculture and non-agriculture, a minimum 50% margin over cost, and the likely **effect on consumers / the economy**.
- **Not a factor:** a **cost of living index** as such (CDS 2019) – CACP only weighs the *effect* of MSP on cost of living.
| Cost concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A2** | **Actual paid-out costs** (seed, fertiliser, hired labour, fuel, irrigation) |
| FL | Imputed value of **family labour** |
| **A2+FL** | Paid-out cost + family labour (leaves out rent on owned land and interest on fixed capital) |
| **C2** | **A2 + FL + land rent + interest on fixed capital** – the fullest cost |
- **Present formula (since 2018-19):** MSP at least **1.5 × (A2+FL)** – a 50% margin over A2+FL, **not over C2**; in short, the support price is **"cost plus"** a margin (cost plus benefit).
- **Swaminathan Commission (National Commission on Farmers, 2004-06):** MSP = **C2 + 50%**; farmer unions (SKM) say paddy at C2+50% should be ₹3,243/q for 2026-27 vs ₹2,441 fixed.
- **Farm running (working) capital** = seed, fertiliser, irrigation water, hired labour; **land revenue** is a fixed charge, **not** running capital.

=== Sugarcane – FRP, SMP and SAP
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **FRP** (Fair and Remunerative Price) | Minimum price sugar mills must pay; **approved by the CCEA** on **CACP's recommendation**; linked to the **sugar recovery rate** |
| **SMP** (Statutory Minimum Price) | Replaced by FRP from **2009-10** (Sugarcane (Control) Order amended **22 Oct 2009**) |
| **SAP** (State Advised Price) | Announced by **some State Governments** (UP, Punjab, Haryana), usually above FRP; not by CACP |
- **Legal basis:** **Sugarcane (Control) Order, 1966** under the **Essential Commodities Act, 1955**; sugar and sugarcane are essential commodities.
- "FRP" relates to **sugarcane** only; CACP **recommends**, it does **not approve**.

=== Procurement at MSP and Its Price Effects
| Crop | Procurement |
|---|---|
| **Wheat, paddy** | FCI + State agencies – **effectively open-ended** |
| Coarse grains | **State agencies with prior GoI approval** (not exclusively FCI) |
| Pulses, oilseeds, copra | Price Support Scheme – capped at **25% of production** |
| Cotton | CCI |
- **Neither** statement of UPSC 2020 holds: procurement is **not unlimited** for all cereals, pulses and oilseeds, and MSP is **not** set at a level the market price "will never rise" above.
- **Rice prices** in the recent past were affected by **all four**: MSP, government trading, government stockpiling and consumer subsidies (UPSC 2020).
- **Decentralised Procurement (DCP), 1997:** States procure, store and distribute; Centre pays economic cost minus CIP.
- States **expedite kharif procurement** so farmers get **timely payment and protection from market fluctuations**.
- **AP:** State agencies procure paddy for the Central Pool; **DCMS** societies buy at MSP; APMARKFED apex cooperative; payment by DBT within 48 hours.
- Wheat procurement RMS 2026-27 above **35 MT**.

=== PM-AASHA, PSF, MIS and Other Price Support
- **PM-AASHA** = **Pradhan Mantri Annadata Aay SanraksHan Abhiyan**, approved by the Union Cabinet **September 2018**, Ministry of Agriculture; beneficiaries: **farmers** – aims that MSP benefits reach farmers, including small and marginal ones.
- **Outlay ₹35,000 crore** for the 15th Finance Commission cycle **up to 2025-26** (APPSC 2023).
| Component | Nature |
|---|---|
| **PSS** | **Physical procurement** of pulses, oilseeds, copra by **NAFED, NCCF**; cap 25% |
| **PDPS** | Pays **MSP minus market price** without buying; mainly oilseeds |
| **PPSS** | 2018 pilot: **private stockists** procured oilseeds at MSP |
| **MIS** | **Perishables not under MSP**; trigger ≥ **10%** price fall or output rise; loss shared **50:50** (Centre : NE States **75:25**) |
- PSS 2024-25: 61.87 LMT bought, over ₹39,000 crore, 26.56 lakh farmers. **Pulses mission:** 100% MSP buying of **tur, urad, masoor** for **4 years**.
| Price Stabilisation Fund | Fact |
|---|---|
| **PSF 2003** (Ministry of Commerce) | For growers of **tea, coffee, natural rubber and tobacco** (plantation crops); 2003-2013 – the PSF of the **UPPCS 2006** question |
| PSF 2015 (agriculture, then Consumer Affairs) | Buffers of **onion, potato**, later **pulses** |
- **Kerala:** first State with **floor prices (MSP) for 16 vegetables/fruits, November 2020**.
- **Kisan Kranti Yatra** (Bharatiya Kisan Union, October 2018): demands included **MSP, a blanket loan waiver and the Swaminathan Committee report** – all three.
- AP maize sold at ₹1,400-1,600 against ₹2,400 MSP (May 2026).

=== Price Floors, Ceilings, Phases and WTO
| Term | Example |
|---|---|
| **Price floor** (minimum) | **MSP** (e.g. for jowar, cane), minimum wages |
| **Price ceiling** (maximum) | **MRP printed on a biscuit packet** |
- A ration-shop price is a subsidised issue price and a fertiliser subsidy is a subsidy – neither is a price floor.
| Phase | Hallmark |
|---|---|
| Pre-Green Revolution (1950-65) | Land reforms, community development, irrigation |
| Green Revolution (mid-1960s) | HYV wheat, rice; APC and FCI 1965 |
| **Third phase: price-support** | **Price policy based on MSP and procurement**, FCI buffer stocks, PDS |
| Post-1991 | Liberalisation, WTO 1995, **e-NAM, FPOs**, PM-KISAN |
- **WTO Agreement on Agriculture (1995):** pillars – **market access, domestic support, export subsidies** (not IPR). **Aggregate Measure of Support (AMS)** = **quantum of (trade-distorting) subsidy to agriculture**.
- **Amber box** (MSP price support, input subsidies) capped by **de minimis 10%** of production value for developing countries (5% developed); **green box** (research, infrastructure) unlimited; **blue box** = production-limiting payments.
- **Peace clause (Bali, 2013)** protects public stockholding for food security. **Bt cotton** approved **2002**.

=== Criticism and the Legal-Guarantee Debate
| Issue | Effect |
|---|---|
| Crop bias | Procurement mostly **paddy and wheat** |
| Regional bias | Punjab, Haryana, Telangana, AP, Chhattisgarh gain; **eastern and North-Eastern** farmers gain little |
| **Shanta Kumar HLC (2015)** | Only about **6%** of farmers sell at MSP |
- **Ineffective MSP** is one cause of agrarian distress (with unviable farming, indebtedness).
- **Legal MSP:** farmer unions want a law **binding agencies to buy eligible produce at MSP**; economists fear fiscal cost, trade distortion, monoculture and WTO problems and prefer **PDPS-type deficiency payments**.
- SC notice on plea for MSP ≥ weighted average cost (14 Apr 2026). **Economic Survey 2025-26:** use procurement savings for **voluntary crop diversification** away from water-guzzling rice and wheat.
