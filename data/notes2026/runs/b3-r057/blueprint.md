## 3. Key settlements
- **PSF (UPPCS 2006):** the 2003 Price Stabilisation Fund was for **tea, coffee, rubber, tobacco** growers; the onion-potato PSF is from 2015 (Arthapedia).
- **2000-01 PDS subsidy:** total food subsidy ₹12,010 crore (not offered); consumer part ≈ ₹7,777 crore, buffer ₹4,233 crore (ES 2001-02 Table 5.19) → keyed ₹8,100 crore with a dispute line; the old notes' "about ₹12,000 crore ⚠ ₹4,000" trap is replaced.
- **Strategic reserve of rice:** 20 LMT (30 LMT is wheat) → option added.
- **Buffer stocks:** aim to check **price rise**, not black marketing.
