=== Islands, Seas, Straits and Channels; India–Sri Lanka
# Channels and straits around India
| Channel / strait | Separates or links |
|---|---|
| **Ten Degree Channel** (along **10° N**, ~150 km) | **Andaman group – Nicobar group** |
| **Nine Degree Channel** | Minicoy – main Lakshadweep group |
| **Eight Degree Channel** | **Minicoy (Lakshadweep) – Maldives** |
| **Palk Strait** | **Tamil Nadu – Sri Lanka (Jaffna)**; links Palk Bay (Bay of Bengal) with the Gulf of Mannar; named after Robert Palk, Governor of Madras (1763–67) |
| Duncan Passage | South Andaman – Little Andaman |
- **India and Sri Lanka** are separated by the **Palk Strait and the Gulf of Mannar**; one-word answer = **Palk Strait**. The **Gulf of Mannar** lies **south-east of Tamil Nadu** (between SE India and western Sri Lanka), south of Adam's Bridge.
- From **Nagercoil/Kanyakumari** (south of Adam's Bridge) the sea route to Sri Lanka crosses the **Gulf of Mannar**; the Palk Strait lies farther north (Rameswaram–Point Calimere to Jaffna).
- **Palk Bay** lies between the **Gulf of Mannar and the Bay of Bengal** (bounded in the south by Adam's Bridge).
- **Adam's Bridge (Rama Setu):** a chain of **limestone shoals** between **Dhanushkodi (Pamban Island) and Talaimannar** (~32 km).
- **Sethusamudram Project:** a proposed 167 km shipping channel linking the **Gulf of Mannar with Palk Bay**.

=== Tropic of Cancer and the Standard Meridian
- **Tropic of Cancer (23°30′ N)** passes through **8 States**, west to east: **Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, Mizoram**; it roughly halves India. **Andhra Pradesh lies wholly south** of it; it does not touch Odisha or Bihar.
- **Indian Standard Meridian: 82°30′ E** (a multiple of 7°30′), reference place **Mirzapur (UP)**; **IST = GMT/UTC + 5:30**.
- It passes through **5 States:** **Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, Andhra Pradesh** (not Telangana). **Chhattisgarh** lies on both the Tropic and the meridian; **Odisha** only on the meridian.
- **Time spread:** ~30° of longitude = **~2 hours** (1° = 4 min); the sun rises in the North-East about 2 hours before Jaisalmer.
- **Separate time zone demand:** **Arunachal Pradesh** CM Pema Khandu (2017) asked for a North-East time zone; India keeps a single zone.
