[0:1] - **SERP** = Society for Elimination of Rural Poverty (rural); **MEPMA** = Mission for Elimination of Poverty in Municipal Areas (**urban** SHGs).
[0:2] - **SHG registration:** **no registration is mandatory** (informal groups of 10-20).
[0:3] - **NRLM "Institutions of the Poor":** **SHGs and their federations** (VOs, MS) – not PACS or RRBs.
[0:4] - **AP leads India** in SHG-bank linkage: **36%** national share, **99.66%** recovery (2024-25). Members lack individual **CIBIL** histories.
[0:5] - **MEPMA limits:** SHG bank linkage up to **Rs 20 lakh**; individual loans **Rs 50,000-2 lakh**.
[0:6] # Stree Nidhi
[0:7] - **Streenidhi Credit Cooperative Federation Ltd** (2011): **banking institution promoted by SHG women**; a **community-based micro-finance** body of the Mandal Samakhyas with GoAP; registered under the AP Cooperative Societies Act, 1964.
[0:8] - **Purpose:** **timely and affordable credit** to SHG members through VOs/SLFs and MS/TLFs; also deposits, loan insurance, Business Correspondent.
[0:9] - **Rate:** **13%** at launch (2011); **11%** now (livelihood loans up to Rs 1 lakh, APSES 2024-25). Loans Rs 21,123.71 crore (2011-2024).
[0:10] - Stree Nidhi describes itself as a "**historic necessity**" – SHG women needed their own credit body.
[0:11] - **PS-SBG** (Pattabhi Sitaramayya-Self Business Group) for rural women entrepreneurs: launched by **Andhra Bank**.
[0:12] > Traps: MEPMA urban, SERP rural; IKP merged Velugu and DWCRA (not Indira Prabha – that is land development); SHGs need no registration.

## SUB 1 | NABARD – Establishment and Functions
[1:0] TABLE head: Item | Fact
  [1:0.0] Recommended by | CRAFICARD – Committee to Review Arrangements for Institutional Credit for Agriculture and Rural Development, chaired by B. Sivaraman
  [1:0.1] Act / set up | NABARD Act 1981; set up 12 July 1982
  [1:0.2] Took over | RBI's ACD and RPCC and the whole of ARDC
  [1:0.3] Capital / HQ | Rs 100 crore; Mumbai; fully Government-owned since 2019
[1:1] - **Role:** apex **refinancing** institution for rural credit – **term loans/refinance to State Cooperative Banks**, RRBs and commercial banks; **long-term loans to State Governments** to contribute to the **share capital** of cooperative credit societies.
[1:2] - **Does NOT lend short-term credit directly** to farmers.
[1:3] - **Supervision:** inspects **RRBs, State and District Central Cooperative Banks** – **not PACS** (inspected by the Registrar/DCCB).
[1:4] - **SHG-Bank Linkage** (1992-93); **KCC** model scheme (**1998**, R.V. Gupta Committee). Same year as NABARD: **EXIM Bank** (1982).
[1:5] - Order: RRBs (1975) → NABARD (1982) → SHG linkage (1992-93) → KCC (1998).
[1:6] > Trap: NABARD does not inspect primary cooperative societies.
[1:7] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 07 p.10; Rocket Indian Economy p.44 (NABARD 12 July 1982, Sivaraman); NextGen AP Economy Static-1 01 p.14 (KCC 1998); app notes 3:123 sub 0

## SUB 3 | Sources of Agricultural Credit and RRBs in AP
[3:0] - **Commercial banks** = **major source** of institutional farm credit in AP (as in India: SCBs over 70% of formal farm credit).
[3:1] - **2007-08:** **cooperative credit societies** gave about **one-fifth** of AP's crop loans; commercial banks most of the rest.
[3:2] - Institutional: cooperatives, commercial banks, RRBs, Government. **Non-institutional:** moneylenders.
[3:3] - Exclusion: **tenant, tribal, women and oral-lessee** farmers – no **land records**.
[3:4] TABLE head: Loan term | Period
  [3:4.0] Short-term | Up to 1 year, maximum 15 months
  [3:4.1] Medium-term | 15 months to 3 (or 5) years
  [3:4.2] Long-term | Above 3 (or 5) years
[3:5] # RRBs
[3:6] - Set up **2 October 1975** on the **Narasimham Working Group** (1975) recommendations; first = **Prathama Bank** (Moradabad); RRB Act 1976; shares Centre 50 : State 15 : sponsor bank 35.
[3:7] - **One State, One RRB:** AP's four RRBs merged into **Andhra Pradesh Grameena Bank** from **1 May 2025**; sponsor **Union Bank of India**; RRBs in India 43 → **28**.
[3:8] - **Andhra Bank:** founded **1923**, Machilipatnam, by **Bhogaraju Pattabhi Sitaramayya**; merged into **Union Bank of India** (1 April 2020).
[3:9] - **Narasimham Committee I (1991):** began banking reforms (different from the 1975 Working Group).
