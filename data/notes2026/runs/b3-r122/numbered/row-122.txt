## SUB 0 | Cooperative Credit Structure – Three Tiers in AP
[0:0] TABLE head: Tier | Institution in AP | Number / fact
  [0:0.0] State (apex) | APCOB – AP State Cooperative Bank | 18 branches
  [0:0.1] District | DCCBs – District Cooperative Central Banks | 13 DCCBs; 442 branches (2024-25), 436 (2025-26)
  [0:0.2] Village | PACS – Primary Agricultural Cooperative Credit Societies | 2,042 (APSES 2024-25); 2,024 (2025-26)
[0:1] - **Short-term cooperative credit is three-tiered**: PACS (village) → DCCB (district) → State Cooperative Bank (apex).
[0:2] - **DCCB = federation of the PACS of a district**: it works as their **integrated unit**, financing and supervising them.
[0:3] - **DCCB funds:** **entry fee, share capital, deposits and loans** (borrowings from APCOB/NABARD) – all of these.
[0:4] - **Long-term credit:** nationally by **ARDBs** (Land Development / Cooperative and Rural Development Banks).
[0:5] - **AP's integration:** the **Single Window** system (**1987**, on the Mohan Kanda recommendations) put short- and long-term loans under one cooperative umbrella; AP integrated the two from **1986**; primary agricultural development banks abolished in **1987**.
[0:6] - **APCCADB merged into APCOB** from **30 April 1994** (Single Window Act, 1994); **APCOB** now refinances DCCBs for long-term credit.
[0:7] > Traps: DCCB (not APCOB, not an Agricultural Development Bank) is the district integrated unit of PACS; three tiers for short-term credit.

## SUB 2 | PACS – Functions and Computerisation
[2:0] - **Credit:** crop and short-term loans; **inputs** (fertilisers, pesticides, seeds, cattle feed).
[2:1] - **Non-credit:** paddy and maize procurement, seed processing, PDS, insurance, drinking water, warehousing, petrol bunks, Jan Aushadhi Kendras, CSCs.
[2:2] - **Model bye-laws:** **25+** non-credit activities; **NABARD** supports PACS as **Multi-Service Centres**.
[2:3] - **Computerisation (CSS, Ministry of Cooperation):** **2,037** AP PACS; PACS software integrated with **B@ncs24** of DCCBs (6,000+ touch points); 1,553 PACS completed pre-migration; ERP nationally about 80,000 PACS (Jul 2026).
[2:4] - **Who runs it:** **NABARD** = national-level implementing agency; in AP the project runs through the Cooperation Department and **APCOB** (State nodal / facilitating agency) with the DCCBs.
[2:5] - **1,246 of 1,490** PACS (assigned to fertilizer companies) became **PM Kisan Samruddhi Kendras**.
[2:6] - **PACS as FPOs:** PACS in **207 blocks** eligible for **NCDC** equity grants; **APCOB = CBBO**.
[2:7] - **AIF through PACS:** NABARD loan Rs 736.03 crore; 695 godowns, 3.98 lakh MT; farm-gate infrastructure at RSKs; PACS as Multi-Purpose Facility Centres.
[2:8] ~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.63-65; indiancooperative.com (APCOB facilitates PACS computerisation in AP; web search); app notes 3:122 sub 3
