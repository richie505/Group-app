[122:3:0] - Credit: crop and short-term loans; inputs (fertilisers, pesticides, seeds, cattle feed).
[122:3:1] - Non-credit: paddy and maize procurement, PDS, rice mills, warehousing, petrol bunks, RO water.
[122:3:2] - **Model bye-laws**: **25+** non-credit activities; **NABARD** backs PACS as **Multi-Service Centres**.
[122:3:3] - AP computerised (CSS): **2,037** PACS; 1,553 completed pre-migration; DCCB platform B@ncs24. ERP nationally: about **80,000** PACS (Jul 2026).

[122:3:5] - PACS in **207 blocks** eligible for **NCDC** equity grants as FPOs; **APCOB = CBBO**.

=== NOTES 3:124 | Agricultural Marketing in Andhra Pradesh – Market Yards, Rythu Bazaars and E-marketing
## SUB 0 | Why Regulated Marketing
[124:0:0] - Farmers selling alone face a **bargaining imbalance** with traders: forced sales, false weights, unrecorded prices, middlemen margins.
[124:0:1] - AP answers this with three layers: **regulated market yards** (by law), **Rythu Bazars** (direct sale scheme) and **e-marketing** (e-NAM), backed by storage, pledge loans and price intervention.
## SUB 1 | Ap Agricultural Produce and Livestock Markets Act
[124:1:0] # Acts and rules
[124:1:1] TABLE head: Act / rule | Year | Provision
  [124:1:1.0] AP (Agricultural Produce and Livestock) Markets Act | 1966 | Act No. 16 of 1966; covers crops, livestock and livestock products
  [124:1:1.1] AP (AP&L) Markets Rules | 1969 | Made under Section 33 of the Act; govern licensing (issue, fees, turnover, period)
  [124:1:1.2] Three central farm laws | 2020 | Repealed November 2021 → 1966 AMC framework remains operative
  [124:1:1.3] Model APMC Act | — | Single market fee, registration in place of licensing, farmers' and consumers' markets
[124:1:2] # Mechanisms of the 1966 Act
[124:1:3] - **Open auction**, **standard weights**, **recorded prices**, **licensed buyers**.
[124:1:4] - The Act **allows** licensed buyers; it does not end them.
[124:1:5] Act **1966** (No. 16); Rules **1969** under **Section 33**. Not 1969 Act.
## SUB 2 | Agricultural Market Committees and Market Yards
[124:2:0] # How a regulated market is set up
[124:2:1] - 1. Government declares a **notified area**.
[124:2:2] - 2. A **Market Committee** is constituted.
[124:2:3] - 3. The Market Committee establishes the **notified market** (market yard).
[124:2:4] - Supervision at district level: **District Agricultural Trade and Marketing Officer (DATMO)**.
[124:2:5] - **Adoni** market yard (Kurnool district) = **second largest** market yard in AP.
[124:2:6] - **Guntur Mirchi Yard:** **Asia's largest chilli** market; Agri Export Zone for **red chillies** proposed in Guntur.
[124:2:7] # Market fee
[124:2:8] - Market fee in regulated yards: **1%**.
[124:2:9] TABLE head: Market fee collected | Amount
  [124:2:9.0] 2024-25 (to Dec 2024) | Rs 433.39 crore
  [124:2:9.1] 2025-26 (to Dec 2025) | Rs 429.77 crore
[124:2:10] - **Central Market Fund:** Market Committees pay **25%** of their income (earlier **10%**, raised from **October 2015**) into the **Central Market Fund** (CAG audit, 2017).
## SUB 3 | Rythu Bazaars
[124:3:0] # Key facts
[124:3:1] - Concept: **farmer sells directly to the consumer**, no middlemen.
[124:3:2] - Started by AP government in **1999**; functioning from **January 1999**.
[124:3:3] - Run day-to-day by an **Estate Officer**; under the **Revenue Department**.
[124:3:4] - **Outside the purview** of Agricultural Market Committees.
[124:3:5] - District coordinating officer: **Joint Collector** (not the District Collector).
[124:3:6] - Pricing: prices fixed at about **25%** relative to open-market prices – about 25% **above wholesale** and 25% **below local retail**.
[124:3:7] - Number: **124** functioning (2024-25); **129** functioning (2025-26).
[124:3:8] - Role in price intervention: the State's **quickest market-intervention channel**; government procures **perishables** through them to steady prices.
[124:3:9] - **Ryot Bazar** scheme first came in **Punjab (Apni Mandi)**; AP was the **second** State.
[124:3:10] APPSC 2025 key: "District Collector is the coordinating officer" = **wrong**; the Joint Collector coordinates, the Estate Officer manages.
