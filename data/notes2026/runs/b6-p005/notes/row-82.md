# Symbols and Snakes
- **National fish:** books and exam keys give the **Indian Mackerel**; no national fish has been formally notified. National **aquatic animal** = **Ganges river dolphin** (2009). Hilsa = national fish of Bangladesh.
- **Viper bite:** **haemotoxic** venom (tissue and blood damage) – local necrosis, ecchymosis, blistering, painful swelling, compartment syndrome; **Russell's viper** causes more snakebite deaths than any other Indian snake. Sea snakes are mainly neurotoxic/myotoxic; the rat snake is non-venomous.
- **Snow leopard:** declared **Kyrgyzstan's national symbol** (Dec 2023); Kyrgyzstan hosts the **GSLEP**; IUCN **Vulnerable**.
# Big Mammals and Turtles
- **Kaziranga (rhino):** the SC's **Central Empowered Committee (CEC)** pulled up **Assam** over **mining near the park** (Aug 2026).
- **Olive Ridley:** **arribada** at **Gahirmatha and Rushikulya (Odisha)**; threat = **coastal erosion** of nesting beaches (Aug 2026).
