=== Judgments, Ordinances and the Judiciary
# Presidential Reference on Governors' assent (20 Nov 2025)
- **Bench:** five-judge Constitution Bench led by **CJI B.R. Gavai**, on a Reference under **Article 143**.
- **Held:** courts **cannot fix timelines** for the Governor (Art 200) or President (Art 201); **no "deemed assent"**.
- **Nature:** an Art 143 opinion is **advisory** – not "law declared" under **Article 141**.
# Supreme Court judges – Ordinance to Act
| Step | Fact |
|---|---|
| Ordinance | Promulgated **16 May 2026** under Article 123 |
| Change | **33 → 37 judges excluding the CJI** (34 → 38 with the CJI) |
| Act | Assent **11 August 2026** (Act No. 14 of 2026) |
# Judicial infrastructure
- **eCourts Phase III:** Cabinet **13 Sep 2023**; Central Sector Scheme; **₹7,210 crore**; **1,150 virtual courts**; **4,400 eSewa Kendras**.
- **Fast Track Special Courts (rape, POCSO):** **Centrally Sponsored Scheme** (2023–26), ₹1,952.23 crore; Central share from the **Nirbhaya Fund**.
# Legal services (NALSA)
- **Justice Vikram Nath** – Executive Chairman of NALSA from **24 Nov 2025**; **Justice J.K. Maheshwari** – Chairman, SC Legal Services Committee.
- **NALSA Veer Parivar Sahayata Yojana, 2025:** legal aid for **families of soldiers**; launched at **Srinagar**.
