## SUB 0 | Planning Commission – Set-up, Status, Chairman
[0:0] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [0:0.0] Set up | 15 March 1950 (began work 28 March 1950), with the Budget of 1950-51
  [0:0.1] How | Cabinet (executive) resolution of the Government of India – "a resolution of the Government of India"
  [0:0.2] Recommended by | Advisory Planning Board (1946), chaired by K.C. Neogy – not the Congress National Planning Committee
  [0:0.3] Basis | In line with Article 39 (DPSP) objectives
  [0:0.4] Status | Non-constitutional, non-statutory, advisory body (Rocket: also "quasi-political")
  [0:0.5] Chairman | Prime Minister, ex officio; first – Jawaharlal Nehru (as first PM)
  [0:0.6] Model | Broadly the Soviet Gosplan
  [0:0.7] Replaced by | NITI Aayog, 1 January 2015 (also a Cabinet resolution); last Plan = Twelfth (2012-17)
[0:1] - Body set up after Independence to prepare a **common plan** for the country = **Planning Commission**.
[0:2] - **Five-Year Plan idea in India: Jawaharlal Nehru** (as PM and first Chairman); planned development began with the **First Plan from 1 April 1951**.
[0:3] - **Functions:** assess material, capital and human resources; **formulate** plans; fix stages and priorities; determine resources; **appraise progress**; recommend on policy and administration; **perspective planning**.
[0:4] - **Execution is NOT its function** – plans were executed by ministries and States.
[0:5] - No mention in the Constitution; **no fixed tenure or qualifications** for the Deputy Chairman and members.
[0:6] - Was the **nodal agency for poverty estimation** at national and state level (now NITI Aayog, its successor).
[0:7] - **UIDAI** (Unique Identification Authority, 2009) began as an **attached office of the Planning Commission**; statutory since the Aadhaar Act 2016, under **MeitY**.
[0:8] - **National Planning Council (1965):** expert advisory body attached to the Commission.
[0:9] > 1950 = Planning Commission; 1952 = NDC; 2015 = NITI Aayog. "Advisory body" is the keyed status.
[0:10] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 01 p.4-5; NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC 04 p.28; Rocket Indian Economy #23 p.33; app notes 3:7 sub 1, 3:8 sub 1
## SUB 1 | National Development Council (NDC)
[1:0] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [1:0.0] Set up | 6 August 1952 – executive resolution, on the recommendation of the draft outline of the First Plan
  [1:0.1] Status | Neither constitutional nor statutory; extra-constitutional; advice not binding; to meet at least twice a year
  [1:0.2] Chairman | Prime Minister (presides over meetings)
  [1:0.3] Members | PM, all Union Cabinet Ministers (since 1967), Chief Ministers of all States, UT administrators, members of the Planning Commission
  [1:0.4] Secretary | Secretary of the Planning Commission
  [1:0.5] NOT members | President, Vice-President, Governors, Chairman of the Finance Commission, Secretary of the Ministry of Planning and Programme Implementation
[1:1] - **Objectives:** **chief – secure cooperation of the States in executing the plan** (NextGen); strengthen and mobilise the nation's efforts and resources for the plan; promote common economic policies; ensure **balanced and rapid development** of all regions.
[1:2] - **Functions:** prescribe **guidelines for the national plan, including assessment of resources**; consider and **approve the draft Five-Year Plan** – the **apex / final authority** that approves plans (can modify the draft).
[1:3] - **Sarkaria Commission:** constitutional status under **Article 263**, rename it "**National Economic and Development Council**".
[1:4] - Now inactive; its role passed to **NITI Aayog's Governing Council** (formal abolition pending).
[1:5] - **Chronology (UPPCS 2003):** India joins IMF (**Dec 1945**) → first devaluation (**Sept 1949**) → First Plan submitted to Parliament (**draft outline July 1951**) → NDC (**Aug 1952**).
[1:6] > NDC is NOT 1950 and NOT statutory; "guidelines incl. assessment of resources" = NDC, "assess resources and draft" = Planning Commission.
[1:7] ~~ Sources: NextGen Polity - APPSC 20 (NDC: composition, objectives); NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC 04 p.28, p.53 (NDC finally approves plans); app notes 3:7 sub 2
## SUB 2 | Origins – Pre-independence Plans
[2:0] TABLE head: Year | Effort | Person / idea
  [2:0.0] 1931 | Karachi Resolution (INC) | Economic freedom; state ownership / control of key industries
  [2:0.1] 1934 | Planned Economy for India | M. Visvesvaraya – first blueprint; ten-year plan to double national income; "Father of Indian Planning"; Bharat Ratna 1955
  [2:0.2] 1934 | FICCI | Called for national planning (Depression, New Deal, Soviet experiment)
  [2:0.3] 1938 | National Planning Committee (Congress) | Initiated by Subhas Chandra Bose (Haripura); chaired by Jawaharlal Nehru; final report 1949
  [2:0.4] 1944 | Bombay Plan | Eight industrialists (J.R.D. Tata, G.D. Birla, Kasturbhai Lalbhai, Purshottamdas Thakurdas, Ardeshir Dalal, John Mathai) – state-led industrialisation with protection
  [2:0.5] 1944 | Gandhian Plan | Shriman Narayan Agarwal (not Kripalani) – decentralised, self-contained villages
  [2:0.6] 1944 | Planning and Development Department | Ardeshir Dalal; abolished 1946
  [2:0.7] 1945 | People's Plan | M.N. Roy (Indian Federation of Labour) – Marxist; priority to agriculture
  [2:0.8] 1946 | Advisory Planning Board | K.C. Neogy – recommended the Planning Commission
  [2:0.9] 1947 | Economic Programme Committee | Jawaharlal Nehru
  [2:0.10] 1950 | Sarvodaya Plan | Jayaprakash Narayan (inspired by Gandhi and Vinoba)
[2:1] - **First Five-Year Plan in the world: USSR (1928, Stalin)**; India from 1951.
[2:2] - Indian planning drew on **Soviet planning** and the **Fabian socialism of Sidney and Beatrice Webb** (Nehru) – not Lenin's NEP.
[2:3] - **Mahatma Gandhi opposed centralised planned development** – self-reliance meant simple living, local resources and local production.
[2:4] - Supporters of planning: industrialists (Bombay Plan) and socialists; Gandhian and Sarvodaya plans = **decentralised alternative**.
[2:5] > Bose initiated, Nehru chaired the NPC; M.N. Roy = People's Plan, not a Bombay Plan signatory.
[2:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 01 p.1-4; NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC 04 p.28 (Visvesvaraya first attempt; NPC 1938 Nehru); app notes 3:7 sub 0, 3:8 sub 0
## SUB 3 | Which Body Did What – PC, NDC, Finance Commission, Finance Ministry
[3:0] TABLE head: Task | Body
  [3:0.0] Assess resources; prepare the draft plan | Planning Commission
  [3:0.1] Guidelines incl. assessment of resources; final approval of the plan | NDC
  [3:0.2] Plan becomes operative | Union Cabinet, then Parliament
  [3:0.3] Share of States in taxes; principles of distribution of net tax proceeds; grants | Finance Commission (Article 280, constitutional)
  [3:0.4] Suggestions on transfer and allocation of financial resources to Union and States | Finance Commission
  [3:0.5] Control of government expenditure; budgetary allocation | Finance Ministry
  [3:0.6] Allocation of central funds to States now (after the Planning Commission) | Union Finance Ministry (devolution on Finance Commission advice)
[3:1] - **Flow:** Commission drafts → **NDC approves** → Cabinet → Parliament. So the bodies "associated with planning" = **NDC and Parliament** (UPSC 2014), not the Finance Commission or the line ministries.
[3:2] TABLE head: Basis | Planning Commission | Finance Commission
  [3:2.0] Nature | Non-constitutional, permanent | Constitutional (Art. 280), periodic
  [3:2.1] Dealt with | Plan resources, planning | Tax devolution, grants (old non-Plan)
[3:3] - Finance Commission decides the **method and formula** for sharing tax proceeds between Centre and States.
[3:4] - **State plans:** State Planning Board (**CM as Chairman**, Vice-Chairman, 6-9 members) drafts; the **Chief Minister** sends the State draft plan to the Planning Commission.

## SUB 4 | Planning, the Constitution and Decentralised Planning
[4:0] - "**Economic and social planning**" = **Concurrent List (List III), Entry 20**, Seventh Schedule.
[4:1] - **Article 39** (DPSP) – source of plan objectives; **Article 40** – village panchayats, the village as the primary unit.
[4:2] - **Not mentioned in the Constitution:** **NDC, Planning Commission, Zonal Councils** (all three; Zonal Councils are **statutory** – States Reorganisation Act 1956); NITI Governing Council is not statutory.
[4:3] - **Bottom-up planning** is a goal not yet achieved; the village is accepted as a suitable unit (UPPSC 1998 key: A and R true, R explains A).
[4:4] TABLE head: Body | Article | Rule
  [4:4.0] District Planning Committee | 243ZD | Four-fifths elected from elected members of district panchayat and municipalities; chairperson sends the draft district plan to the State
  [4:4.1] Metropolitan Planning Committee | 243ZE | Metro areas of 10 lakh+; two-thirds elected
[4:5] - **74th Amendment (1992)** inserted **Part IXA** (municipalities), which holds the DPC and MPC.
[4:6] - **Balwantrai Mehta (1957):** planning to PRIs (accepted Jan 1958); **JP Narayan Committee (1961):** flagged centralisation; **Thungon (1988):** **Zila Parishad** the pivot of planning.
[4:7] - **SFDA, DPAP, ITDP, IADP** ran **outside the panchayats**; **73rd and 74th Amendments** restored local bodies' role.
[4:8] - Levels: Centre (Planning Commission → NITI Aayog) → State Planning Boards → DPC / MPC → Mandal plans, **GPDP**.
[4:9] - **AP:** State Planning Commission in the **Fifth Plan**; first district-level planning in the **Sixth Plan – Rs 1 crore to each of 23 districts**; Economic Regions – **Visakhapatnam 10 districts** (NITI-assisted, launched 13 Nov 2025), **Amaravati 9**, Tirupati 10.
[4:10] - **Kerala People's Plan Campaign:** 17 Aug 1996; 35-40% of plan outlay to local bodies.
[4:11] - **NITI State Support Mission** (2026) helps States set up **State Institutions for Transformation**.
[4:12] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 01 p.4 (JP Committee, 73rd/74th); Constitution of India (List III Entry 20; Arts 40, 243ZD, 243ZE); app notes 3:7 sub 4, 6, 7, 3:8 sub 9

## SUB 6 | Criticism and the Shift to NITI Aayog
[6:0] - Epithets: "**super cabinet**", "economic cabinet", "parallel cabinet", "**fifth wheel of the coach**" – for **centralising decisions** and eroding **State autonomy**.
[6:1] - **Nehru (1958)** admitted the Commission had become a kind of "super-cabinet"; the Advisory Planning Board's "**common plan**" idea bred lasting Centre-State tension.
[6:2] - **NITI Aayog:** think tank; **bottom-up**, **cooperative (and competitive) federalism**; **no financial powers** – not a fund allocator.
[6:3] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 01 p.5; Rocket Indian Economy #24 p.34; app notes 3:7 sub 5
