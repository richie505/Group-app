=== Planning Commission – Set-up, Status, Chairman
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Set up | **15 March 1950** (began work 28 March 1950), with the Budget of 1950-51 |
| How | **Cabinet (executive) resolution** of the Government of India – "a resolution of the Government of India" |
| Recommended by | **Advisory Planning Board (1946)**, chaired by **K.C. Neogy** – not the Congress National Planning Committee |
| Basis | In line with **Article 39** (DPSP) objectives |
| Status | **Non-constitutional, non-statutory, advisory body** (Rocket: also "quasi-political") |
| Chairman | **Prime Minister, ex officio**; first – **Jawaharlal Nehru** (as first PM) |
| Model | Broadly the **Soviet Gosplan** |
| Replaced by | **NITI Aayog, 1 January 2015** (also a Cabinet resolution) |
- **Five-Year Plan idea in India: Jawaharlal Nehru** (as PM and first Chairman); planned development began with the **First Plan from 1 April 1951**.
- **Functions:** assess material, capital and human resources; **formulate** plans; fix stages and priorities; determine resources; **appraise progress**; recommend on policy and administration; **perspective planning**.
- **Execution is NOT its function** – plans were executed by ministries and States.
- No mention in the Constitution; **no fixed tenure or qualifications** for the Deputy Chairman and members.
- Was the **nodal agency for poverty estimation** at national and state level (now NITI Aayog, its successor).
- **UIDAI** (Unique Identification Authority, 2009) began as an **attached office of the Planning Commission**; statutory since the Aadhaar Act 2016, under **MeitY**.
- **National Planning Council (1965):** expert advisory body attached to the Commission.
> 1950 = Planning Commission; 1952 = NDC; 2015 = NITI Aayog. "Advisory body" is the keyed status.

=== National Development Council (NDC)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Set up | **6 August 1952** – executive resolution, on the recommendation of the **draft outline of the First Plan** |
| Status | **Neither constitutional nor statutory**; extra-constitutional; advice **not binding**; to meet at least **twice a year** |
| Chairman | **Prime Minister** (presides over meetings) |
| Members | PM, **all Union Cabinet Ministers** (since 1967), **Chief Ministers of all States**, **UT administrators**, **members of the Planning Commission** |
| Secretary | **Secretary of the Planning Commission** |
| NOT members | President, Vice-President, Governors, **Chairman of the Finance Commission**, Secretary of the Ministry of Planning and Programme Implementation |
- **Objectives:** **chief – secure cooperation of the States in executing the plan** (NextGen); strengthen and mobilise the nation's efforts and resources for the plan; promote common economic policies; ensure **balanced and rapid development** of all regions.
- **Functions:** prescribe **guidelines for the national plan, including assessment of resources**; consider and **approve the draft Five-Year Plan** – the **apex / final authority** that approves plans (can modify the draft).
- **Sarkaria Commission:** constitutional status under **Article 263**, rename it "**National Economic and Development Council**".
- Now inactive; its role passed to **NITI Aayog's Governing Council** (formal abolition pending).
- **Chronology (UPPCS 2003):** India joins IMF (**Dec 1945**) → first devaluation (**Sept 1949**) → First Plan submitted to Parliament (**draft outline July 1951**) → NDC (**Aug 1952**).
> NDC is NOT 1950 and NOT statutory; "guidelines incl. assessment of resources" = NDC, "assess resources and draft" = Planning Commission.

=== Origins – Pre-independence Plans
| Year | Effort | Person / idea |
|---|---|---|
| 1931 | Karachi Resolution (INC) | Economic freedom; **state ownership / control of key industries** |
| 1934 | **Planned Economy for India** | **M. Visvesvaraya** – first blueprint; ten-year plan to **double national income**; "**Father of Indian Planning**"; Bharat Ratna 1955 |
| 1934 | FICCI | Called for national planning (Depression, New Deal, Soviet experiment) |
| 1938 | **National Planning Committee** (Congress) | Initiated by **Subhas Chandra Bose** (Haripura); **chaired by Jawaharlal Nehru**; final report 1949 |
| 1944 | **Bombay Plan** | **Eight industrialists** (J.R.D. Tata, G.D. Birla, Kasturbhai Lalbhai, Purshottamdas Thakurdas, Ardeshir Dalal, John Mathai) – state-led industrialisation with protection |
| 1944 | **Gandhian Plan** | **Shriman Narayan Agarwal** (not Kripalani) – decentralised, self-contained villages |
| 1944 | Planning and Development Department | **Ardeshir Dalal**; abolished 1946 |
| 1945 | **People's Plan** | **M.N. Roy** (Indian Federation of Labour) – Marxist; priority to agriculture |
| 1946 | **Advisory Planning Board** | **K.C. Neogy** – recommended the Planning Commission |
| 1947 | Economic Programme Committee | **Jawaharlal Nehru** |
| 1950 | **Sarvodaya Plan** | **Jayaprakash Narayan** (inspired by Gandhi and Vinoba) |
- **First Five-Year Plan in the world: USSR (1928, Stalin)**; India from 1951.
- Indian planning drew on **Soviet planning** and the **Fabian socialism of Sidney and Beatrice Webb** (Nehru) – not Lenin's NEP.
- **Mahatma Gandhi opposed centralised planned development** – self-reliance meant simple living, local resources and local production.
- Supporters of planning: industrialists (Bombay Plan) and socialists; Gandhian and Sarvodaya plans = **decentralised alternative**.
> Bose initiated, Nehru chaired the NPC; M.N. Roy = People's Plan, not a Bombay Plan signatory.

=== Which Body Did What – PC, NDC, Finance Commission, Finance Ministry
| Task | Body |
|---|---|
| Assess resources; prepare the draft plan | **Planning Commission** |
| Guidelines incl. assessment of resources; **final approval** of the plan | **NDC** |
| Plan becomes operative | Union **Cabinet**, then **Parliament** |
| Share of States in taxes; principles of distribution of net tax proceeds; grants | **Finance Commission** (Article **280**, constitutional) |
| Suggestions on transfer and allocation of financial resources to Union and States | **Finance Commission** |
| Control of government expenditure; budgetary allocation | **Finance Ministry** |
| Allocation of central funds to States **now** (after the Planning Commission) | **Union Finance Ministry** (devolution on Finance Commission advice) |
- **Flow:** Commission drafts → **NDC approves** → Cabinet → Parliament. So the bodies "associated with planning" = **NDC and Parliament** (UPSC 2014), not the Finance Commission or the line ministries.
| Basis | Planning Commission | Finance Commission |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Non-constitutional, permanent | Constitutional (Art. 280), periodic |
| Dealt with | Plan resources, planning | Tax devolution, grants (old non-Plan) |
- Finance Commission decides the **method and formula** for sharing tax proceeds between Centre and States.
- **State plans:** State Planning Board (**CM as Chairman**, Vice-Chairman, 6-9 members) drafts; the **Chief Minister** sends the State draft plan to the Planning Commission.

=== Planning, the Constitution and Decentralised Planning
- "**Economic and social planning**" = **Concurrent List (List III), Entry 20**, Seventh Schedule.
- **Article 39** (DPSP) – source of plan objectives; **Article 40** – village panchayats, the village as the primary unit.
- **Not mentioned in the Constitution:** **NDC, Planning Commission, Zonal Councils** (all three; Zonal Councils are **statutory** – States Reorganisation Act 1956); NITI Governing Council is not statutory.
- **Bottom-up planning** is a goal not yet achieved; the village is accepted as a suitable unit (UPPSC 1998 key: A and R true, R explains A).
| Body | Article | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| District Planning Committee | **243ZD** | **Four-fifths** elected from elected members of district panchayat and municipalities; chairperson sends the draft district plan to the State |
| Metropolitan Planning Committee | **243ZE** | Metro areas of **10 lakh+**; **two-thirds** elected |
- **74th Amendment (1992)** inserted **Part IXA** (municipalities), which holds the DPC and MPC.
- **Balwantrai Mehta (1957):** planning to PRIs (accepted Jan 1958); **JP Narayan Committee (1961):** flagged centralisation; **Thungon (1988):** **Zila Parishad** the pivot of planning.
- **SFDA, DPAP, ITDP, IADP** ran **outside the panchayats**; **73rd and 74th Amendments** restored local bodies' role.
- Levels: Centre (Planning Commission → NITI Aayog) → State Planning Boards → DPC / MPC → Mandal plans, **GPDP**.
- **AP:** State Planning Commission in the **Fifth Plan**; first district-level planning in the **Sixth Plan – Rs 1 crore to each of 23 districts**; Economic Regions – **Visakhapatnam 10 districts** (NITI-assisted, launched 13 Nov 2025), **Amaravati 9**, Tirupati 10.
- **Kerala People's Plan Campaign:** 17 Aug 1996; 35-40% of plan outlay to local bodies.
- **NITI State Support Mission** (2026) helps States set up **State Institutions for Transformation**.

=== Criticism and the Shift to NITI Aayog
- Epithets: "**super cabinet**", "economic cabinet", "parallel cabinet", "**fifth wheel of the coach**" – for **centralising decisions** and eroding **State autonomy**.
- **Nehru (1958)** admitted the Commission had become a kind of "super-cabinet"; the Advisory Planning Board's "**common plan**" idea bred lasting Centre-State tension.
- **NITI Aayog:** think tank; **bottom-up**, **cooperative (and competitive) federalism**; **no financial powers** – not a fund allocator.
