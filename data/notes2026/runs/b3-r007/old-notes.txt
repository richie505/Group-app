=== NOTES 3:7 | Economic Planning in India – Origins, Planning Commission, National Development Council
## SUB 0 | Pre-independence Planning Efforts
[7:0:0] # Year → Effort → Person
[7:0:1] TABLE head: Year | Effort | Key person / idea
  [7:0:1.0] 1931 | Karachi Resolution (INC) | Economic freedom; state ownership of key industries
  [7:0:1.1] 1934 | Planned Economy for India | M. Visvesvaraya – ten-year plan to double national income; shift labour to industry
  [7:0:1.2] 1938 | National Planning Committee (Congress) | Initiated by Subhas Chandra Bose (Haripura); chaired by Jawaharlal Nehru; final report 1949
  [7:0:1.3] 1944 | Bombay Plan | Eight industrialists; state-led industrialisation with protection
  [7:0:1.4] 1944 | Gandhian Plan | Shriman Narayan Agarwal – decentralised, self-contained villages
  [7:0:1.5] 1944 | Planning and Development Department | Under Ardeshir Dalal (abolished 1946)
  [7:0:1.6] 1947 | Economic Programme Committee | Under Nehru
  [7:0:1.7] — | People's Plan | M.N. Roy (not a Bombay Plan signatory)
[7:0:2] - **Visvesvaraya** was the first to support planning – "**Father of Indian Planning**".
[7:0:3] - **Bombay Plan signatories:** J.R.D. Tata, G.D. Birla, Kasturbhai Lalbhai, Purshottamdas Thakurdas, Ardeshir Dalal, John Mathai.
[7:0:4] - Bombay Plan demanded protection and state intervention → the licence raj was partly a capitalist demand.
[7:0:5] - **Supporters of planning:** industrialists (Bombay Plan) and socialists. Gandhian and Sarvodaya plans offered a **decentralised alternative**.
[7:0:6] NPC 1938: **Bose initiated, Nehru chaired** – not the other way round.
## SUB 1 | Planning Commission
[7:1:0] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [7:1:0.0] Set up | 15 March 1950, by a Cabinet (executive) resolution; began work 28 March 1950
  [7:1:0.1] Status | Non-constitutional, non-statutory, advisory
  [7:1:0.2] Basis | In line with Article 39 (DPSP)
  [7:1:0.3] Chairman | Prime Minister (ex-officio); first – Jawaharlal Nehru
  [7:1:0.4] First Deputy Chairman | Gulzarilal Nanda
  [7:1:0.5] Last Deputy Chairman | M.S. Ahluwalia
  [7:1:0.6] Last Plan | Twelfth (2012-17)
  [7:1:0.7] Replaced by | NITI Aayog (2015), also by Cabinet resolution
[7:1:1] - Planning Commission was the **nodal agency for poverty estimation** (national and state).
[7:1:2] - M.S. Ahluwalia also headed the **Sugar De-control Committee**.
[7:1:3] - **National Planning Council (1965):** expert advisory body attached to the Commission.
## SUB 2 | National Development Council (ndc)
[7:2:0] # Set-up and Status
[7:2:1] - Set up **August 1952** by an **executive resolution**, on the recommendation of the First Five-Year Plan (draft outline).
[7:2:2] - **Neither constitutional nor statutory**; advisory; advice **not binding**; to meet **at least twice a year**.
[7:2:3] - **Sarkaria Commission:** give NDC constitutional status under **Article 263** and rename it "**National Economic and Development Council**".
[7:2:4] - Now inactive; role passed to **NITI Aayog's Governing Council**; formal abolition pending.
[7:2:5] Chronology: first post-Independence devaluation → First Five Year Plan submitted to Parliament → NDC set up.
[7:2:6] # Composition – Who Is and Is NOT a Member
[7:2:7] TABLE head: Members | NOT members
  [7:2:7.0] Prime Minister (Chairman) | President
  [7:2:7.1] Union Cabinet Ministers | Vice-President
  [7:2:7.2] Chief Ministers of all states | Governors
  [7:2:7.3] UT administrators | Chairman of the Finance Commission
  [7:2:7.4] Members of the Planning Commission | Secretary, Ministry of Planning and Programme Implementation
[7:2:8] - **Secretary:** Secretary of the Planning Commission.
[7:2:9] The Deputy Chairman of the Planning Commission was **not** the NDC chairman; the PM chaired both.
[7:2:10] # Functions
[7:2:11] - Secure states' cooperation; mobilise resources; promote common economic policies; balanced development of all regions.
[7:2:12] - **Prescribe guidelines for the national plan, including assessment of resources**; **approve the draft Five-Year Plan** (final authority to approve; can modify).
## SUB 3 | Which Body Did What
[7:3:0] TABLE head: Task | Body
  [7:3:0.0] Assess resources; prepare the draft plan | Planning Commission
  [7:3:0.1] Guidelines incl. assessment of resources; approve the draft | NDC (apex approving body)
  [7:3:0.2] Operative document | Union Cabinet, then Parliament
  [7:3:0.3] Budgetary allocation for plan spending | Finance Ministry
  [7:3:0.4] Allocation of central funds to states now | Union Finance Ministry (on Finance Commission advice)
[7:3:1] - **Flow:** Commission drafts → NDC approves → Cabinet → Parliament.
[7:3:2] # Planning Commission vs Finance Commission
[7:3:3] TABLE head: Basis | Planning Commission | Finance Commission
  [7:3:3.0] Nature | Non-constitutional | Constitutional – Article 280
  [7:3:3.1] Dealt with | Planning, resource allocation | Tax devolution, grants
  [7:3:3.2] Old framework | Plan resources | Non-Plan resources
[7:3:4] - Finance Commission decides the **method and formula** for sharing tax proceeds between Centre and States.
## SUB 4 | Planning and the Constitution
[7:4:0] - "**Economic and social planning**" = **Entry 20, List III (Concurrent List)**, Seventh Schedule.
[7:4:1] - **Article 39** (DPSP) – source of plan objectives; **Article 40** – village panchayats, village as primary unit.
[7:4:2] - **NDC, Planning Commission, Zonal Councils** – none mentioned in the Constitution; **Zonal Councils are statutory**, the other two are not; NITI Governing Council is not statutory.
[7:4:3] - **Planning machinery:** Planning Commission (1950), NDC (1952), State Planning Boards, District and Metropolitan Planning Committees.
## SUB 5 | Epithets and Criticism
[7:5:0] - Epithets: "**super cabinet**", "**economic cabinet**", "**parallel cabinet**", "**fifth wheel of the coach**".
[7:5:1] - **Nehru (1958)** admitted a feeling that the Commission had become a kind of "super-cabinet".
[7:5:2] - Criticism: **centralised decisions**, weakened **state autonomy**; the "common plan" idea of the Advisory Planning Board bred lasting Centre-state tension.
[7:5:3] - **NITI Aayog** = think tank; **bottom-up** planning and **cooperative federalism**; not mainly a fund allocator.
## SUB 6 | State and Ap Planning Bodies
[7:6:0] - **State Planning Board:** **CM as Chairman** + Vice-Chairman + **6-9 members**; drafts the state plan; CM sends the state draft to the Planning Commission.
[7:6:1] - **AP State Planning Commission:** set up during the **Fifth Plan (1974-79)**.
[7:6:2] - **AP FIRST:** district-level decentralised planning in the **Sixth Plan (1980-85)** – **Rs 1 crore each to 23 districts**; District Planning Councils set up.
## SUB 7 | Decentralised Planning
[7:7:0] TABLE head: Body | Article | Rule
  [7:7:0.0] District Planning Committee | 243ZD | Mandatory; four-fifths elected by and from elected members of district panchayat and municipalities (rural-urban ratio)
  [7:7:0.1] Metropolitan Planning Committee | 243ZE | Metro areas of 10 lakh+; two-thirds elected
[7:7:1] - DPC chairperson sends the draft district plan (from panchayat and municipal plans) to the **State**.
[7:7:2] - **74th Amendment, 1992** inserted **Part IXA** (municipalities), which holds DPC and MPC.
[7:7:3] - **Balwantrai Mehta (1957):** entrust planning to PRIs; accepted January 1958. **Thungon sub-committee (1988):** **Zila Parishad** the pivot of planning (not Gram Sabha).
[7:7:4] - Bottom-up planning is a goal not yet reached; the village is a suitable unit.
[7:7:5] DPCs are in **Part IXA (74th Amendment)**, not "Article 243J".

## SUB 4 | Growth Models and the Mahalanobis Strategy
[8:4:0] # Model → Plan
[8:4:1] TABLE head: Model | Plan | Logic
  [8:4:1.0] Harrod-Domar | First Plan | Growth depends on savings rate and capital-output ratio
  [8:4:1.1] Feldman-Mahalanobis | Second Plan | Build capital-goods capacity first, then consumption rises
  [8:4:1.2] Wage goods model (C.N. Vakil and P.R. Brahmananda) | Alternative to Mahalanobis | Expand wage goods (food, consumer goods) first
[8:4:2] - Feldman (Soviet, **1928**) and Mahalanobis (**1953**) built the model independently.
[8:4:3] # Mahalanobis Strategy – Components
[8:4:4] - **Included:** state-led **heavy and capital-goods industries**; large **public sector**; rapid industrialisation (Bhilai, Durgapur, Rourkela steel plants); inward-looking, limited foreign trade (import substitution).
[8:4:5] - **Not given priority:** mechanisation of agriculture, wage goods, export promotion.
[8:4:6] - Foreign-exchange crisis forced cuts in **1958**; model replaced by the Third Plan (1961).
[8:4:7] Harrod-Domar = **First** Plan; Mahalanobis = **Second** Plan.
