## 4. Heavy in the old notes but never asked
- Gupta temple stages (Tigawa, Nachna, Bhumara, Sanchi 17) were in row 66 but belong to row 65. They moved there, together with the one Bhitargaon PYQ.
- The old notes had a number game, an "APPSC PYQ" table and a "traps" list (several "traps" came from unverified keys, for example "Hiuen Tsang never mentions Jainism"). All three were dropped. Their facts now sit in the topic sub-sections, and the Jainism claim is corrected.
- Zero/place-value epigraphy (Mankani, Gwalior) and the Navaratna list beyond Kshapanaka are tested only by own MCQs, so they are kept short.
- Harsha's officers (Avanti, Simhanada, Kuntala) and Hiuen Tsang in Andhra are tested by own MCQs only. Both are kept as short last sub-sections.
