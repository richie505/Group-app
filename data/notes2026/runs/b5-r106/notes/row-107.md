=== Geospatial and Digital Tools in Epidemics
| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| **Geospatial technology** | Collection/processing of **location-linked data**; involves **GPS, GIS and RS** |
| Geospatial in public health | **Contact tracing** of exposed people; hotspot and containment-zone mapping – it **cannot administer medicines or perform surgery** |
| **GIS real-time spatial mapping** | Efficient **distribution of vaccines** in biological disasters (COVID-19) |
| **GIS network analysis tool** | Finds locations and the **fastest routes** to hospitals, fire stations in disasters like earthquakes |
| COVID-19 GIS **war room** | **Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP)** |
- AP **Covid Pharma app** (25 April 2020, Medical & Health Dept): tracked people buying over-the-counter cough, cold and fever medicines.
> APPSC 2021-2024 papers ask the "which GIS tool" pair: network analysis = routes; real-time spatial mapping = vaccines.

=== Nodal Ministries and Legal Basis of Epidemic Powers
- **Nodal ministry for all disaster management: Ministry of Home Affairs**; **for epidemics / biological disasters: Ministry of Health & Family Welfare** (an APPSC 2018 key gave MHA – wrong).
- **COVID lockdown** (March 2020): legal basis = **DM Act 2005** (orders of NDMA/NEC and MHA) **and the Epidemic Diseases Act 1897** – not the Mental Health Care Act 2017 or Enemy Property Act 1968.
- **Epidemic Diseases Act 1897**: States notify diseases; **2020 amendment** (ordinance April, Act September 2020) – violence against health workers **cognizable, non-bailable**; **3 months–5 years** jail, **Rs 50,000–2 lakh** fine (grievous hurt 6 months–7 years). Criticised as colonial, no definition of "dangerous epidemic disease".
- Draft Public Health Bill 2017 – never enacted. Notifiable diseases: urgent within **24 hours**; 20 States made human rabies notifiable (Jan 2026).

|---|---|
| MoHFW (DGHS technical arm; DM Cell Dec 2020) | Nodal for epidemics |
| **NCDC** (formerly NICD) | Runs IDSP and National Public Health EOC |
| NDMA | Biological Disaster Management guidelines **2008** |

=== Epidemic Management in the DM Cycle
| Measure | Phase |
|---|---|
| **Vaccine distribution before an outbreak**, sanitation, vector control | **Mitigation** (some texts: preparedness) |
| Surveillance, stockpiles, **mock drills** (test preparedness), school drills | **Preparedness** |
| Isolation, quarantine, contact tracing, lockdown | Response |
| Restoring services, rehabilitation | Recovery |
- **Vaccinating animals**: primary purpose = **prevention of infectious diseases**.
- **NDMP 2016** aligned with **Sendai, SDGs and Paris Agreement**.
> Phase-matching is the tested angle; schools: conducting mock drills.

=== PM-ABHIM and International Health Regulations
- **PM-ABHIM**: **744** Integrated Public Health Labs (one per district); **631** Critical Care Blocks (districts > **5 lakh**); **15** Health EOCs; **50 BHISHM Cubes** (used in Operation Sindoor). AP: 10 CCBs + 12 IPHLs opened at **Vijayawada** (13 Sep 2026).
- **IHR 2005**: legally binding, in force **2007**; report possible PHEIC within **24 hours**; National IHR Focal Point; **2024 amendments** add **"pandemic emergency"**.
- **WHO Pandemic Agreement**: adopted **20 May 2025**. BRICS New Delhi Declaration (Sep 2026): sub-working group on integrated early warning for infectious diseases.
