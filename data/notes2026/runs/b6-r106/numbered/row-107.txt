## SUB 4 | December 2025 – Days and Anniversaries
[4:0] # World AIDS Day – 1 December
[4:1] - **2025 theme**: "**Overcoming disruption, transforming the AIDS response**".
[4:2] - **HIV identified 1983** by **Françoise Barré-Sinoussi and Luc Montagnier** (Institut Pasteur); attacks **CD4+ T-cells**; AIDS = advanced stage of untreated HIV.
[4:3] - India prevalence: **0.33% (2010) → 0.20% (2024)**; India supplies **70% of global ART medicines**.
[4:4] - **Advanced HIV Disease** (WHO): CD4 < 200 cells/mm³ or WHO Stage 3/4; all children under 5 with HIV are AHD.
[4:5] - Window period: antibodies in about **28 days**; infants need virological testing at birth or 6 weeks.
[4:6] - **PEP** within **72 hours** of exposure.
[4:7] - WHO PrEP options: oral TDF-based PrEP, Dapivirine vaginal ring, long-acting injectable cabotegravir, **long-acting injectable lenacapavir**.
[4:8] - WHO strategy: Global Health Sector Strategies 2022–2030; **"End AIDS by 2030"** (SDG 3.3).
[4:9] - Why incurable: retrovirus joins host DNA through **reverse transcription**; **latent reservoirs**; high mutation rate; destroys immune cells.
