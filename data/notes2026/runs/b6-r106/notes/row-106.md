=== Somnath Swabhiman Parv and Amrit Mahotsav
# Why
- Marks **1,000 years** since the first recorded attack (**January 1026**) and **75 years** since reopening (**May 1951**); PM Modi visited on **11 May 2026**.
- Special pujas for the next **1,000 days**; theme "**Vikas Bhi, Virasat Bhi**".
# Temple facts
- At **Prabhas Patan, near Veraval, Saurashtra coast, Gujarat**; **first of the 12 Jyotirlingas** (Dwadasha Jyotirlinga Stotram).
- Present temple consecrated by **Dr. Rajendra Prasad, 11 May 1951**; style **Kailash Mahameru Prasad**; reconstruction began 1950 under **Sardar Patel** (resolved 1947).
- Restorers: **Kumarapala** (12th c.), King of Junagarh (13th c.), **Ahilyabai Holkar** (18th c.), Sardar Patel.
- Described by **Al-Biruni** in **Kitab-ul-Hind**.
