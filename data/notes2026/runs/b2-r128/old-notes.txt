## SUB 0 | Status of Women Through History
[128:0:0] - **Vedic/early:** honourable place, **'Ardhangini'** (better half); role in sacred rites.
[128:0:1] - **Later Vedic and medieval:** decline; confined to home, **purdah** (not early Vedic), social evils.
[128:0:2] TABLE head: Custom | Meaning
  [128:0:2.0] Niyoga | Childless widow/wife has a son by the husband's brother or kinsman (not a ban on widow remarriage)
  [128:0:2.1] Sati | Widow immolation; Bengal Sati Regulation 1829; Commission of Sati (Prevention) Act 1987
  [128:0:2.2] Devadasi | Girls dedicated to temples; Madras Act 1947, AP Devadasis (Prohibition of Dedication) Act 1988
[128:0:3] - Colonial laws: **Female Infanticide Prevention Act 1870** (APPSC 2018) → Sati 1829 → **Widow Remarriage Act 1856** → **Sarda Act 1929** → Hindu Marriage Act 1955.

## SUB 4 | Gender Concepts and Indices
[128:4:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [128:4:0.0] Patriarchy | Men hold main power and status in family and society
  [128:4:0.1] Gender discrimination | Unequal treatment of men and women by society (APPSC 2024)
  [128:4:0.2] Gender mainstreaming | Gender perspective in all policies, laws, institutions; adopted at Beijing 1995
  [128:4:0.3] Gender audit | Systematic check of policies, programmes, institutions and budgets (not budgets alone): gender budgeting, equal opportunity, workplace safety, decision-making
  [128:4:0.4] Index | Measures
  [128:4:0.5] GDI | Female-to-male ratio: health, education, command over resources
  [128:4:0.6] GII | 0 (equality) to 1 (worst)
  [128:4:0.7] Global Gender Gap Index (WEF) | Economic participation, education, health and survival, political empowerment (not military spending)
[128:4:1] - **Global Gender Gap Index 2025:** India **131st of 148** (129th in 2024); Iceland first.
[128:4:2] - Women, Peace and Security Index 2023: India **131/177**.
[128:4:3] # Sex Ratio and Work
[128:4:4] - NFHS-4 **991** → NFHS-5 **1020**. **SRB 904 (2017-19) → 917 (2021-23)** (Women and Men in India 2025).
[128:4:5] - Most male-skewed: **Haryana, Punjab**; best: **Kerala, North-East**.
[128:4:6] - Causes: **son preference, female foeticide/infanticide, dowry**, neglect of girls (not equal inheritance or schooling).
[128:4:7] - Women's LFPR **37% (2022-23)** vs 23.3% (2017-18). Crimes against women 2024: **4,41,534**; spousal violence 32% (NFHS-5).
[128:4:8] The 0-1 scale belongs to **GII**, not GDI.
[128:4:9] - **Aims of gender mainstreaming:** reduce gender discrimination, ensure equitable development, promote inclusive governance and women's empowerment.
