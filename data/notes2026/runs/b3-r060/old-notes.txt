## SUB 2 | Horticulture
[60:2:0] - Output **367.72 MT (2024-25)** – more than **foodgrains (357.73 MT)**.
[60:2:1] - Top fruit: **banana** (~39,512 thousand MT, 2024-25), ahead of mango, apple, grapes. Leading fruit State: **Andhra Pradesh**.
[60:2:2] - **MIDH**: Centrally Sponsored, **since 2014-15** (APPSC 2023); subsumed **National Horticulture Mission (2005-06)** and the NE/Himalayan horticulture mission.
[60:2:3] - Export mangoes: **Alphonso** (most exported; Maharashtra), Kesar, **Banganapalli and Totapuri (AP)**. **Banganapalle GI: May 2017**.
[60:2:4] - **Turmeric**: India **largest producer and exporter**; 30+ varieties; **National Turmeric Board – Nizamabad**; Maharashtra, Telangana, Karnataka, TN grow it.
[60:2:5] - **Indo-Israel Centre of Excellence** in AP: **Vegetables and Flowers, Kuppam** (MIDH + MASHAV).
[60:2:6] - **FPO (Food Products Order) mark**: mandatory on processed fruit products (jams, squashes, pickles).

## SUB 6 | Fisheries – Blue Revolution
[60:6:0] - Output: 95.79 lakh t (2013-14) → **197.75 lakh t (2024-25)**; India **2nd** in world (~8%); **China** largest (SOFIA 2024: 36% in 2022).
[60:6:1] - **Inland fisheries** > **75%** of output; most important = natural inland fisheries.
[60:6:2] - **PMMSY**: launched **2020**, **₹20,050 crore**; FY 2020-21 to 2024-25, extended to **2025-26**; Department of Fisheries.
[60:6:3] TABLE head: PMMSY component | Funding
  [60:6:3.0] Central Sector | 100% Centre: training, quarantine, harbour modernisation, data systems
  [60:6:3.1] Centrally Sponsored | 60:40 general; 90:10 NE/Himalayan; 100% UTs
[60:6:4] - **PM-MKSSY** (Matsya Kisan Samridhi Sah-Yojana): **Central Sector sub-scheme** under PMMSY; formalises fisheries, supports micro-enterprises (APPSC 2024 – all statements correct).
[60:6:5] - **FIDF**: fishing harbours and landing centres.
[60:6:6] - **NFDB**: autonomous, **2006**, **HQ Hyderabad**; training and capacity building under PMMSY (not MPEDA).
[60:6:7] - **MPEDA**: Act of Parliament **24 August 1972**; **HQ Kochi** (not Kolkata); under **Ministry of Commerce and Industry**; apex body for marine exports; absorbed Marine Products Export Promotion Council (1961).
