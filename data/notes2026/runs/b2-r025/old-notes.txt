## SUB 1 | Electoral College (arts. 54, 55)
[25:1:0] # Who votes and who does not
[25:1:1] TABLE head: Votes | Does NOT vote
  [25:1:1.0] Elected MPs of both Houses (LS + RS) | Nominated MPs and nominated MLAs
  [25:1:1.1] Elected MLAs of States | All members of Legislative Councils (elected and nominated)
  [25:1:1.2] Elected MLAs of Delhi and Puducherry (added by 70th Amendment, 1992) | UTs without Assemblies
[25:1:2] - A **CM who sits in the Legislative Council cannot vote** (APPSC GS 2025).
[25:1:3] - APPSC G2 2023 key: elected MPs, elected MLAs of States, Delhi/Puducherry MLAs.
[25:1:4] - Members of Legislative Councils vote in **neither** the Presidential nor the Vice-Presidential election.
[25:1:5] - Elected MLAs vote for Lok Sabha (as citizens), Rajya Sabha and the President.
[25:1:6] # Method and value of vote
[25:1:7] - **Indirect** election (college of elected MPs and MLAs); **proportional representation by single transferable vote**, **secret ballot**; taken from **Ireland** (Art. 55(3)).
[25:1:8] - PR gives representation of minorities. PR-STV is used for President, VP, Rajya Sabha and Legislative Council.
[25:1:9] - **MLA vote = (State population / elected MLAs) × 1/1000** – varies from State to State → **uniformity among States**.
[25:1:10] - **MP vote = total value of MLAs' votes / total elected MPs** – uniform → **parity between Union and States** (Art. 55(1)).
[25:1:11] - Population base: **1971 Census**, frozen by the **84th Amendment** till the first Census after 2026.
[25:1:12] - Nomination: **50 proposers + 50 seconders** (since 1997; earlier 10 + 10). Vice-President: 20 + 20.
[25:1:13] # Election disputes (Art. 71)
[25:1:14] - Decided **only by the Supreme Court**; its decision is final.
[25:1:15] - **Acts done** before the election is declared void **remain valid**.
[25:1:16] - **Art. 71(4)**: election cannot be challenged or delayed because of **vacancies in the electoral college**; a dissolved Assembly cannot take part.
## SUB 2 | Qualifications, Term, Oath, Emoluments
[25:2:0] - **Art. 58**: citizen of India (not necessarily by birth); **35 years**; qualified for **Lok Sabha**; no **office of profit** under Union, State or any local/other authority.
[25:2:1] - **Education is not a qualification.**
[25:2:2] - **Art. 56**: **5 years** from entering office; outgoing President continues until the successor enters office.
[25:2:3] - **Art. 57**: re-election **any number of times**; no term limit.
[25:2:4] - **Art. 60**: oath by the **CJI** (in absence, senior-most SC judge): "**preserve, protect and defend** the Constitution and the law". Not by the VP.
[25:2:5] - **Resignation** in writing to the **Vice-President**, who informs the **Speaker**.
[25:2:6] - **Art. 59**: not a member of any legislature; **rent-free** residence.
[25:2:7] - Emoluments fixed by **Parliament by law**, listed in the **Second Schedule**, charged on the Consolidated Fund; **cannot be diminished** during the term.
[25:2:8] - Salary **₹5 lakh per month** (Finance Act, 2018, from 1 Jan 2016).
## SUB 3 | Impeachment (art. 61)
[25:3:0] - 1. Ground: **"violation of the Constitution"** only (APPSC GS 2022: Art. 61).
[25:3:1] - 2. Charge in **either House**: resolution signed by **one-fourth** of members after **14 days' notice**.
[25:3:2] - 3. Passed by **two-thirds of total membership** of that House (simple majority not enough).
[25:3:3] - 4. Other House **investigates** (President may appear or be represented); passes by **two-thirds** → impeached.
[25:3:4] - **Quasi-judicial** procedure; borrowed from the **USA**.
[25:3:5] - **Nominated MPs take part**; **elected MLAs** (States, Delhi, Puducherry) **do not**.
[25:3:6] - **No President has ever been impeached.**
[25:3:7] - "Impeachment" is used only for the President; judges and the VP are "**removed**".
[25:3:8] Election | elected MLAs vote | nominated MPs do not
Impeachment | nominated MPs take part | MLAs do not
## SUB 4 | President vs Vice-president
[25:4:0] TABLE head: Basis | President | Vice-President
  [25:4:0.0] Electors | Elected MPs + elected MLAs (States, Delhi, Puducherry) | All MPs, elected and nominated; no MLAs
  [25:4:0.1] Qualification linked to | Lok Sabha | Rajya Sabha
  [25:4:0.2] Removal | Impeachment | RS resolution agreed to by LS
  [25:4:0.3] Resigns to | VP | President
  [25:4:0.4] Part of Parliament | Yes (Art. 79) | No
  [25:4:0.5] Vacancy | Election within 6 months | No 6-month deadline (Art. 68)
[25:4:1] - **11th Amendment (1961)** ended the joint-meeting method for electing the VP.
[25:4:2] - Electoral college for the **16th VP election (2022)**: **788** members.
[25:4:3] - RS Chairman (VP) is not a member; Deputy Chairman is a member.
## SUB 5 | Vacancy and Acting President (arts. 62, 65)
[25:5:0] - Causes: expiry, death, resignation, impeachment, disqualification, election declared void.
[25:5:1] - Expiry: election completed **before** the term ends (Art. 62(1)).
[25:5:2] - Other vacancy: election **within six months** (Art. 62(2)); new President gets a **full 5-year term**.
[25:5:3] - **VP acts** (Art. 65(1)); while acting, he stops functioning as RS Chairman – **Deputy Chairman presides**.
[25:5:4] - Both offices vacant: **CJI**, then senior-most SC judge – **President (Discharge of Functions) Act, 1969**; not the PM.
[25:5:5] - **M. Hidayatullah** – only CJI to act as President (**20 July–24 August 1969**).
[25:5:6] - **July 2025**: VP **Jagdeep Dhankhar resigned**; **C.P. Radhakrishnan** elected – Arts. 65, 68 tested; third mid-term VP vacancy.
## SUB 6 | Powers of the President
[25:6:0] # Executive and appointments
[25:6:1] - Appoints **PM** (majority leader – a **convention**) and Ministers, **Governors (155)**, SC/HC judges, **CAG (148)**, **AG (76)**, UPSC, **Election Commissioners (324)**, Finance Commission, Ambassadors and High Commissioners (on ministerial advice, not own discretion).
[25:6:2] - **Art. 78**: seeks information from the PM.
[25:6:3] - **Art. 240**: **regulations** for A&N Islands, Lakshadweep, DNH and Daman & Diu, and **Puducherry** when its Assembly is suspended or dissolved.
[25:6:4] TABLE head: Appointment | Basis
  [25:6:4.0] CJI, SC/HC judges | Arts. 124(2), 217(1): consultation with CJI; collegium (CJI + 4, Third Judges Case 1998)
  [25:6:4.1] CAG, Governors, AG | Advice of PM / Council (Art. 74)
[25:6:5] - ⚠ APPSC key (2025) gave **only the CJI** as appointed on the PM's recommendation; in fact **AG and Governors** are appointed on the PM/Council's advice (Art. 74), judges through the **collegium**.
[25:6:6] # Legislative
[25:6:7] - **Part of Parliament (Art. 79)**; no Bill becomes law without assent.
[25:6:8] - Summons/prorogues Houses; **dissolves Lok Sabha**; summons **joint sitting** (chaired by **Speaker**).
[25:6:9] - Addresses under **Arts. 86, 87** (first session after general election and first session of each year); sends messages.
[25:6:10] - **Nominates 12** to Rajya Sabha.
[25:6:11] - Prior recommendation for **Money Bills** and **Art. 3** Bills.
[25:6:12] # Financial
[25:6:13] - Budget laid in his name; Money Bills and demands for grants on his recommendation; advances from the **Contingency Fund**.
[25:6:14] # Judicial, emergency, military, diplomatic
[25:6:15] - **Art. 72** pardon (a **judicial power**); **Art. 143** advisory opinion – **not binding**.
[25:6:16] - Emergencies: **352** National, **356** State, **360** Financial.
[25:6:17] - **Supreme Commander**; declares war and makes peace on Council's advice; treaties in his name.
## SUB 7 | Ordinance (art. 123)
[25:7:0] - Only when Parliament (either House) is **not in session**; has the force of an Act.
[25:7:1] - Must be **laid** before Parliament; lapses **six weeks after reassembly**; may be **withdrawn** any time.
[25:7:2] - Subject to **judicial review**.
[25:7:3] TABLE head: Case | Holding
  [25:7:3.0] D.C. Wadhwa (1987) | Repeated re-promulgation in Bihar = fraud on the Constitution
  [25:7:3.1] Krishna Kumar Singh (2017) | Seven judges (5:2); laying is mandatory; satisfaction reviewable
[25:7:4] - Latest: **Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Ordinance, 2026** (President Droupadi Murmu; gazette **16 May 2026**): SC judges **33 → 37**, excluding the CJI.
[25:7:5] - Governor's parallel power: **Art. 213**.
## SUB 8 | Veto Power (art. 111)

  [25:8:0.1] Suspensive | Returns once; must assent if re-passed – Ordinary Bills only | Yes – not Money Bills

## SUB 9 | Pardoning Power (art. 72 vs 161)
[25:9:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [25:9:0.0] Pardon | Removes both sentence and conviction
  [25:9:0.1] Commutation | Lighter form (death → rigorous life)
  [25:9:0.2] Remission | Shorter period, same character (2 years → 1 year)
  [25:9:0.3] Respite | Lesser sentence for special reasons (pregnancy, disability)
  [25:9:0.4] Reprieve | Temporary stay of execution
[25:9:1] - Art. 72 covers: (a) **court-martial**, (b) offence against **Union law**, (c) **death sentence**. Amnesty is outside Art. 72.

## SUB 10 | Position of the President (art. 74)
[25:10:0] - **Nominal / de jure** executive; PM = **real / de facto**.
[25:10:1] - **42nd Amendment (1976)**: advice **binding**.
[25:10:2] - **44th Amendment (1978)**: may **return once** for reconsideration, then must act.
[25:10:3] - **Art. 74(2)**: advice **cannot be inquired into** by any court.
[25:10:4] - Limited discretion: PM in a **hung Parliament**; dismissing a government that lost majority; dissolving LS; **pocket veto**.
[25:10:5] - Ambedkar's model; no term limit criticised. Compare: **USA** two terms (**22nd Amendment**); **South Korea** single 5-year term.
