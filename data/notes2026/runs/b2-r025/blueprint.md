## 4. Key decisions
- **y89c39e9d3dd** (UPPCS Mains 2012): "All the members of the two Houses" includes nominated MPs, so statement 1 is false → **2 and 3 only** (printed key 1, 2 and 3).
- **y3b8ad1613b9** (UPPCS 2004): the rule since 1997 is **50 proposers + 50 seconders**; none of 10/20/5/15 is right → add_option "Fifty electors".
- **yf0b56bb6f98**: Hidayatullah performed the President's functions **twice** (1969 as CJI; 6–31 October 1982 as VP) → key 2, with a dispute line (many keys: 1).
- **y1aa94b477eb** (CGPSC 2016): the forms not in Art. 72 are detention and continuation (vi, vii); no printed code says so → add_option "vi vii".
- **y4ffb9c74065**: "youngest elected MP" was Chandrani Murmu (2019) → outdated tag, now the 2024 group of 25-year-olds.
- **y6ef94c5d408** (rationale for MLAs not removing the President): no source states it; printed key ("free from narrow regional considerations") kept as generated.
- **y8088a344969**: NextGen p.383 says courts cannot interfere with pardon; the settled law (Epuru Sudhakar 2006; PMF; UPSC 2025 key) is limited judicial review – key "not subject to judicial review" = the untrue statement.
