=== WTO, IMF, Banks and Regional Bodies
# WTO
- **WTO MC14 (Yaoundé, Cameroon, March 2026)** ended without consensus; the **e-commerce moratorium** (no customs duties on electronic transmissions, in place since **1998**) **lapsed** (**30 March 2026**), for the first time in 26 years.
- No multilateral extension followed. A group of co-sponsors has applied the moratorium **only among themselves since 8 May 2026**; on **1 September 2026** the co-sponsors (**25 entries**, incl. **US, EU, UK, Japan, Australia, Singapore**; India **not** among them) circulated a revised proposal for a **permanent plurilateral moratorium**, open to other members.
> As of Sep–Oct 2026 the WTO-wide e-commerce moratorium stays lapsed; only a plurilateral group keeps it among themselves.
| WTO term (Agreement on Agriculture) | Subject |
|---|---|
| **Amber Box** | **Trade-distorting** domestic support; subject to **reduction commitments** |
| **De minimis** (within Amber) | Exempt up to **5%** of production value (**developed**), **10%** (**developing**, e.g. India) |
| **Blue Box** | Direct payments under **production-limiting** programmes (fixed area/yield or livestock numbers); exempt |
| **Green Box** | **Minimally or non-distorting** support (government services); **no limit**; incl. **public stockholding for food security** and domestic food aid |
| **Article 6.2** (development measures) | Certain support in **developing countries** exempt from reduction |
| **TRIMs** | **Trade-Related Investment Measures** - goods only (not services); bars measures against **GATT Art. III (national treatment) and Art. XI (quantitative restrictions)** |
| **TRIPS** | **Trade-Related Intellectual Property Rights** |
| **Dunkel Draft** | Draft Final Act of the **Uruguay Round**, **20 December 1991**, by GATT Director-General **Arthur Dunkel**; became the basis of the **WTO** |
> PYQ (APPSC Group 2): TRIPS of WTO relates to **intellectual property**.

=== UN Bodies, the 81st UNGA and UN Missions
# UNSC and Visegrád Group
- **UNSC = 15 members**: **5 permanent**, **10 non-permanent** (**2-year terms**).
- At the **first India-Visegrád Group Foreign Ministers' Meeting**, all **4 members** backed India's **permanent UNSC membership**.
- **Visegrád Group**: formed **1991** at **Visegrád, Hungary**; members **Czech Republic, Hungary, Poland, Slovakia** (4 after Czechoslovakia split in **1993**).
# UN Convention against Cybercrime
- EAM **S. Jaishankar** signed it for India in **New York on 25 September 2026**, during the **81st UNGA**.
- **First comprehensive global treaty on cybercrime**. Earlier (Feb 2026) India had held back over privacy and data sovereignty, as with the IFD, to protect policy space.
- Adopted by **UNGA Resolution 79/243 (Dec 2024)**; opened for signature at **Hanoi, Oct 2025** (**72 signed**); binding **90 days after 40 ratifications**; secretariat **UNODC**.
# UNGA declaration on sea-level rise
- **81st UNGA unanimously** adopted a declaration protecting **continuity of statehood and maritime rights** of low-lying states and **SIDS** despite submergence.
- Process under **UNGA Decision 78/558**; co-facilitators **Australia and Cabo Verde**; calls for **adaptation and loss-and-damage finance**.
# UN mission in Somalia ends
- **UNTMIS** (UN Transitional Assistance Mission in Somalia) **ended operations on 31 Oct 2026**, closing **over three decades** of UN political missions in Somalia; a **Special Political Mission** under **UN DPPA**; tasks passed to the **Federal Government of Somalia** and the **UN Country Team**.
- UNTMIS created **2024** by **UNSC Resolution 2753**, replacing **UNSOM** (**2013, Resolution 2102**). Somalia: capital **Mogadishu**, **Horn of Africa**. UN SG **António Guterres**.

=== US PERM Suspension, H-1B and Green Card
# News
- US suspended firms from the **Permanent Labor Certification (PERM)** programme: **Cognizant, Infosys, TCS, Wipro, HCL Technologies, Capgemini, Microsoft, Adobe**.
- Reasons cited: **fraudulent filings, wage undercutting, displacement of US workers** ("America First").
- US claim: the firms had **>230,000 H-1B approvals** and **>100,000 labour certifications** since **2009**.
# Comparison
| Basis | H-1B | PERM | Green Card |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nature | **Temporary work status** | **Labour certification** | **Permanent residence** |
| Authority | USCIS (+ LCA to DOL) | **US Department of Labor** | USCIS / State Dept |
| Duration | **Up to 6 years** | Certification **valid 180 days** | Permanent |
- PERM is **employer-filed** on **Form ETA-9089**; then **Form I-140** with USCIS. Filing date = **priority date**.
- H-1B extension beyond 6 years if PERM/I-140 pending **365 days**; up to **3 years at a time**.
