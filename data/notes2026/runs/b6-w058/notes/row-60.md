# WTO subsidy boxes and agreements
| Term | Subject |
|---|---|
| **Amber Box** | **Trade-distorting** support; reduction commitments; **de minimis 5% (developed), 10% (developing)** |
| **Blue Box** | Payments under **production-limiting** programmes (fixed area/yield or herd) |
| **Green Box** | **Minimally distorting** support, **no ceiling**; incl. **public stockholding for food security** |
| **TRIMs** | **Trade-Related Investment Measures** - goods only; bars breaches of **GATT Art. III and XI** |
| **TRIPS** | **Trade-Related Intellectual Property Rights** |
| **Dunkel Draft** | Uruguay Round Draft Final Act (**20 Dec 1991**), GATT DG **Arthur Dunkel**; basis of the WTO |
> Public stockholding for food security falls in the **Green Box** criteria; trade-distorting price support is **Amber**.

=== Ecocide, International Law and Conventions
# News

- Demands to add ecocide as a crime under the **Rome Statute**.

# Key Facts
- Term **"ecocide"** coined in **1970** by biologist **Arthur W. Galston** (Vietnam War, **Agent Orange**).
- **Rome Statute Article 8:** environmental damage is a war crime only if **intentional, widespread, long-term and severe**.
- Countries with ecocide laws: **Vietnam, Russia, Ukraine, France, Belgium, Chile**.
- **ENMOD Convention:** bans **hostile environmental modification** techniques.

=== Exits and Withdrawals from Organisations
# UAE Exits OPEC and OPEC+
- **UAE** announced its exit from **OPEC and OPEC+** with effect from **1 May 2026**.
- Reasons: dissatisfaction with **production limits** and **strained ties with Saudi Arabia**.
- UAE had been an **OPEC member since 1967**.
- **OPEC+ = 12 OPEC + 10 non-OPEC** countries; together about **59% of global crude**.
- Output fall under the cuts: **UAE 3.60 → 2.34 mb/d**; **Saudi Arabia 10.40 → 7.80 mb/d**.
- **OPEC:** founded **1960**; HQ **Vienna (Austria)**.
- OPEC members (12, before the UAE exit): **Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Iran, UAE, Kuwait, Venezuela, Nigeria, Algeria, Libya, Gabon, Congo, Equatorial Guinea**.
- UAE: capital **Abu Dhabi** (not Dubai); currency **Dirham**; President **Mohammed bin Zayed Al Nahyan**.
> OPEC HQ is **Vienna**, not Riyadh or Abu Dhabi; the UAE capital is **Abu Dhabi**, not Dubai.
# Argentina and USA Leave the WHO

- Argentina: capital **Buenos Aires**; currency **Argentine Peso**.

=== India's Chairships - Kimberley Process and CCDB
# Kimberley Process (KP) 2026
- India chairs the **Kimberley Process** from **1 January 2026** – its **3rd** time (earlier **2008** and **2019**).
- India is a **founding member**; member since **2003**.
- KP launched **2000**; **KPCS** (certification scheme) established **2003**; **voluntary** scheme.
- **60 participants** (**59 + EU** counted as one); covers **99%+** of rough diamond trade.
- Conflict diamonds of the **1990s**: **Angola, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Rwanda**.
- Reforms India seeks: **wider definition of conflict diamonds** (human rights, child labour, environment); **digital certification and traceability**; capacity building.
- India: **largest cutting and polishing hub**; **largest exporter of cut and polished diamonds** – about **$13.3 billion (2024–25)**.

- India's certification authorities: **MeitY** and the **STQC** (Standardisation Testing and Quality Certification) Directorate.

=== India-New Zealand Free Trade Agreement
# Key Terms
- Signed on **27 April 2026** in **New Delhi** by **Piyush Goyal** and NZ Trade Minister **Todd McClay**.
- **New Zealand:** eliminates tariffs on **100%** of goods from India (zero tariff).
- **India:** removes/reduces tariffs on about **95%** of imports from NZ.
- NZ to facilitate about **$20 billion investment** in India over **15 years**; mobility of **professionals and students**.
- Talks began **March 2025**, concluded **December 2025** – among India's **fastest FTAs**.
- Needs **ratification by the NZ Parliament**.
- NZ PM **Christopher Luxon**; capital **Wellington**.
# FTA vs Customs Union
| Feature | Free Trade Agreement | Customs Union |
|---|---|---|
| Internal tariffs | Removed/reduced | Removed |
| External tariff | Each member keeps its **own** external trade policy | **Common external tariff** |
| Integration | Lower | Higher |
- **Rules of Origin** prevent **trade deflection** under an FTA.
- **SPS** (sanitary and phytosanitary) measures and **TBT** (technical barriers to trade) are **non-tariff barriers**.
> A **common external tariff** is a feature of a **Customs Union**, not of an FTA.
