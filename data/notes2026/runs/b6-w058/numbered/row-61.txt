## SUB 0 | India-EU Free Trade Agreement
[0:0] # News
[0:1] - India and the EU **concluded negotiations** on a comprehensive FTA in **January 2026**; called the **"Mother of All Deals"**.
[0:2] - The **EU is India's 22nd FTA partner**; India's FTA count: **15 FTAs covering 27 countries**.
[0:3] - Runs alongside an **Investment Protection Agreement** and a **GI Agreement**.
[0:4] - **90.7%** of India's exports become **duty-free at once**.
[0:5] - Next steps: **legal scrubbing**, translation, then **ratification by the 27 member states and the European Parliament**.
[0:6] - Talks began **2007**; revived after **2022**.
[0:7] # Commitments
[0:8] TABLE head: Item | EU | India
  [0:8.0] Tariff lines liberalised | 97% | 92.1%
  [0:8.1] Services subsectors | 144 | 102
[0:9] - **Labour-intensive gainers** (about **USD 33 billion** of exports duty-free; current tariffs **4–26%** removed): **textiles, apparel, leather, footwear, marine products, gems and jewellery, toys, sports goods**.
[0:10] - **India protected:** **dairy, cereals, poultry, soymeal**; gradual opening for **automobiles, wines and spirits**.
[0:11] - Car tariffs: **70–110% → 40% → 10%** in phases; wine **20–30%**, spirits **40%**, beer **50%**; **olive oil** duty eliminated.
[0:12] - **Rules of origin:** self-certification via **Statements of Origin**; flexibility for **shrimps** and **downstream aluminium**.
[0:13] - Combined: about **25% of global GDP** and **one-third of global trade** (India **4th**, EU **2nd** largest economy).
[0:14] # Concerns
[0:15] - **CBAM** (steel, aluminium, cement, chemicals): Indian steel may face a **20–35% cost rise** from **2026**.
[0:16] - **EUDR** – deforestation after **2020**; **CSDDD** – applies from **2027**; EU objections to India's **QCOs**.
[0:17] - Average tariff: **India 10–12%** vs **EU 3–4%**.
[0:18] # India–EU Trade and Background
[0:19] - EU = India's **largest goods partner** – **USD 135 billion (FY24)**; services **USD 53 billion**.
[0:20] - Bilateral trade about **USD 190 billion (2024–25)**; EU about **17%** of India's exports; EU FDI **USD 117.4 billion (2020–24)**.
[0:21] - EU evolution: **ECSC 1951 → Treaties of Rome 1957 → Maastricht 1992 → Brexit 2020** (now **27 members**).
[0:22] - **Schengen** includes **4 non-EU states**; **Bulgaria and Romania** full Schengen members from **1 January 2025**.
[0:23] - **EU Stability and Growth Pact** – limits budget deficits.
[0:24] - Other links: **Operation Atalanta** (EUNAVFOR); EU joined **IPOI (2023)**; **CECP (2016)**; **IMEC**; ISRO launched ESA's **Proba-3 (2024)**; India an **associate member of CERN**.
[0:25] # India–Euratom Cooperation
[0:26] - India and the EU to strengthen **peaceful (civilian only)** nuclear cooperation under the **India–Euratom Agreement**.
[0:27] - **Euratom:** est. **1957** by the **Treaty of Rome**; HQ **Brussels**.
