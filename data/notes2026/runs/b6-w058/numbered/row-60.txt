## SUB 6 | India-New Zealand Free Trade Agreement
[6:0] # Key Terms
[6:1] - Signed on **27 April 2026** in **New Delhi** by **Piyush Goyal** and NZ Trade Minister **Todd McClay**.
[6:2] - **New Zealand:** eliminates tariffs on **100%** of goods from India (zero tariff).
[6:3] - **India:** removes/reduces tariffs on about **95%** of imports from NZ.
[6:4] - NZ to facilitate about **$20 billion investment** in India over **15 years**; mobility of **professionals and students**.
[6:5] - Talks began **March 2025**, concluded **December 2025** – among India's **fastest FTAs**.
[6:6] - Needs **ratification by the NZ Parliament**.
[6:7] - NZ PM **Christopher Luxon**; capital **Wellington**.
[6:8] # FTA vs Customs Union
[6:9] TABLE head: Feature | Free Trade Agreement | Customs Union
  [6:9.0] Internal tariffs | Removed/reduced | Removed
  [6:9.1] External tariff | Each member keeps its own external trade policy | Common external tariff
  [6:9.2] Integration | Lower | Higher
[6:10] - **Rules of Origin** prevent **trade deflection** under an FTA.
[6:11] - **SPS** (sanitary and phytosanitary) measures and **TBT** (technical barriers to trade) are **non-tariff barriers**.
[6:12] > A **common external tariff** is a feature of a **Customs Union**, not of an FTA.
