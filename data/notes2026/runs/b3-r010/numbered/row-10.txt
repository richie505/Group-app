## SUB 0 | Plans I to V and the Plan Holiday – Objectives and Firsts
[0:0] TABLE head: Plan | Period | Model | Main objective / tagline
  [0:0.0] First | 1951-56 (launched 1 April 1951) | Harrod-Domar | Agriculture and irrigation (with power, transport); food, refugee rehabilitation, price stability
  [0:0.1] Second | 1956-61 ("Mahalanobis Plan") | Nehru-Mahalanobis | Rapid industrialisation – heavy and basic industries; socialistic pattern of society
  [0:0.2] Third | 1961-66 ("Gadgil Yojana") | Sandy-Chakravarty | Self-reliant and self-generating economy ("make the economy independent"); self-sustaining growth; agriculture priority
  [0:0.3] Plan Holiday | 1 April 1966 – 31 March 1969 | – | Three Annual Plans; food problem, Green Revolution (1966-67); agriculture and industry equal weight
  [0:0.4] Fourth | 1969-74 (finally launched 1969) | Manne-Rudra (Gadgil strategy) | Twin objectives: growth with stability and progressive achievement of self-reliance; 14 banks nationalised 1969
  [0:0.5] Fifth | 1974-79 (ended 1978) | Dhar (final draft by D.P. Dhar) | Twin objectives: removal of poverty (Garibi Hatao) and self-reliance; control of inflation; zero net foreign aid
[0:1] # First Plan (1951-56)
[0:2] - **Draft outline** presented by the Planning Commission in **July 1951**; final plan December 1952.
[0:3] - Prepared to face **Partition (refugees)**, **inflation** and **food shortage**; priority **agriculture and irrigation** – about **one-third** of outlay (highest agriculture share of any plan); **Bhakra Nangal, Hirakud**.
[0:4] - **Land reform:** the First Plan first proposed **co-operative village management** (village land managed co-operatively) – not the Second Plan.
[0:5] - Succeeded mainly due to **good harvests** in its last two years.
[0:6] # Second Plan (1956-61)
[0:7] - **Mahalanobis** two- and four-sector model; **heavy industry-led development**; **maximum emphasis on the industrial sector**; the **first major industrial plan**; primary responsibility of development shifted to the **public sector** ("public sector plan").
[0:8] - From the Second Plan a **determined thrust towards substitution (import substitution) of basic and capital goods** industries.
[0:9] - Goal of **socialistic pattern of society** (with IPR 1956).
[0:10] - **Targets pruned** because of an **acute shortage of foreign exchange** (1957-58 crisis) and rising prices.
[0:11] # Third, Fourth and Fifth Plans
[0:12] - **Third:** self-reliant, self-generating economy – self-reliance / self-sustaining growth first made the objective; hit by **two wars (1962, 1965)** and the 1965-66 drought.
[0:13] - **Fourth:** "growth with stability and progressive achievement of self-reliance"; **correcting the earlier trend of increased concentration of wealth and economic power**; **exports to grow about 7 per cent a year** for balance on foreign account; **Growth Centre approach** for regional disparities; target-group programmes.
[0:14] - **Fifth:** **removal of poverty for the first time** the basic objective; "**Garibi Hatao**" – Indira Gandhi's slogan of the **1971 elections**; **anti-poverty programmes** and serious concern for **unemployment** start here; high priority to **bring inflation under control** and achieve stability.
[0:15] - **Financial sector** was **not** first made an integral part of the plan in the Fifth (false statement, UPSC 2019).
[0:16] > Twin objectives (APPSC): Fourth = growth with stability + progressive self-reliance; Fifth = poverty removal + self-reliance. Garibi Hatao = Fifth, not Fourth or Sixth.

## SUB 2 | Plans VI to XII – Objectives and Firsts
[2:0] TABLE head: Plan | Period | Main objective / tagline
  [2:0.0] Sixth | 1980-85 | Poverty eradication and technological self-reliance; power significant
  [2:0.1] Seventh | 1985-90 | "Food, Work and Productivity"; employment thrust

[2:1] # Sixth to Ninth
[2:2] - **Sixth:** first plan to focus on **gender issues** and inter/intra-State inequalities.
[2:3] - **Eighth:** shift from heavy industry to **infrastructure** (UPSC 2010); **generation of productive employment** = central element of its strategy; growth "on two legs" with a safety net.
[2:4] - **Ninth:** agriculture growth **decelerated**; seven Basic Minimum Services; Women's Component Plan.
[2:5] # Tenth
[2:6] - **Monitorable targets** for 11 indicators, set for the Centre **and for each State** in consultation with the States (state-wise targets – unique feature).
[2:7] - Agriculture the prime moving force; Governance as a factor of development.
[2:8] # Eleventh and Twelfth
[2:9] - **Eleventh:** top priority to **agriculture** (NDC Approach Paper); social sector first, education on top.
[2:10] - **Twelfth:** growth strategy depends crucially on **productivity gains**; three scenarios – "Strong Inclusive Growth", "Insufficient Action", "**Policy Logjam**" (Scenario 3); households with electricity **56% (2001) → 67% (2011)**.
[2:11] - **Twelfth targets:** Approach Paper **9%** GDP → NDC (Dec 2012) **8%**; agriculture **4%**; **manufacturing 10%**; **50 million** non-farm jobs; core aims – health, education, skills, PPP in infrastructure, fiscal correction (a "gene revolution" is **not** among them).
[2:12] > Eleventh = faster and **more inclusive**; Twelfth adds **sustainable**; Ninth = growth with social justice; Eighth = human development.

## SUB 5 | Plans Cut Short, Plan Holiday and the Rolling Plan
[5:0] TABLE head: Plan | Cause | Effect
  [5:0.0] Third | 1962 and 1965 wars + 1965-66 drought | Plan Holiday (1966-69) declared after the Third Plan
  [5:0.1] Fifth | Emergency, new elections, Janata Party elected; Janata government did not want to continue it | Terminated a year early (1978); Rolling Plan (1978-80)
  [5:0.2] Eighth | 1990-91 political instability + BoP crisis | Postponed two years; Annual Plans 1990-92
[5:1] - **Rolling Plan** = the Janata government's **Sixth Plan (1978-83)**, advocated by Gunnar Myrdal; emphasis on **employment**; ended 1980; Congress launched a new Sixth Plan. Not introduced in any of the Third, Fourth or Fifth Plans.
[5:2] - Rolling Plan structure: a plan for **one year**, a plan for **3, 4 or 5 years** renewed every year, and a **perspective plan** for 10, 15 or 20 years.
[5:3] - **Two wars in one plan:** the Third (1962 China, 1965 Pakistan).
[5:4] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 01 p.10-17; NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC 04 p.37, p.52; app notes 3:10 sub 2
