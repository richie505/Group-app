=== Rajamannar Committee – Appointment and Purpose
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Formal name | **Centre-State Relations Inquiry Committee** |
| Appointed by | **Tamil Nadu** – DMK government of **M. Karunanidhi** (not the Centre) |
| Year | **1969** (22 Sept); report **1971** |
| Chairman | Dr **P.V. Rajamannar**, former **Chief Justice of the Madras High Court** |
| Members | **Three** in all – Rajamannar, **A. Lakshmanaswami Mudaliar**, **P. Chandra Reddy** |
- **First** committee on Centre-State relations appointed by a **State**.
- Purpose: to **study the Centre-State relations** as a whole and suggest amendments to secure **utmost autonomy to the States** (not Tamil Nadu's relations with the Centre or with other southern States, nor UT-State relations).
- Took forward **C.N. Annadurai's 1967** view that the Union need not control **education and health**; it was to reduce, not strengthen, the Centre's control over them.
- P.V. Rajamannar also chaired the **4th Finance Commission**.
~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.15 p.237-238; app notes 2:55

=== Rajamannar Recommendations
| Subject | Rajamannar recommended |
|---|---|
| **Inter-State Council** | Set up **immediately** under **Art. 263**; no decision affecting States without consulting it; every Bill affecting States to be referred to it first |
| **Article 356** | **Complete deletion** (with 357 and 365); if kept, only for a complete breakdown |
| **All-India Services** | **Abolish IAS, IPS (and IFoS)** – to reduce central administrative control |
| Planning Commission | **Abolish** – extra-constitutional; **alone decided grants** to States; replace with a statutory body |
| Finance Commission | Make **permanent**; revitalise with **wider revenue devolution** |
| Governor | Delete "pleasure of the Governor" clause for the State ministry – **not** strengthen the Governor system |
- Ending IAS and IPS = **Rajamannar** (UPSC 1993) – not the Dhebar, Kalelkar or Kher commissions, nor Sarkaria.
- Passage clue: "made a call for **immediate constitution of the Inter-State Council**, every Bill affecting States referred to it first, Art. 356 only in rare cases" = **Rajamannar**.
- Passage clue: "greater autonomy in legislative and fiscal matters, critical analysis of the '**strong Centre**' concept, States' competence to amend Central laws, mandatory consultation" = **Rajamannar** (Sarkaria **defended** a strong Centre).
# Findings
- Despite the federal form, States had become "**administrative units of the Centre**"; causes – special central powers, **one-party rule**, weak State finances, **central planning**.
- Overreach Articles: **256, 257, 365** (not 280, 282).
- The Centre's control over States in the **planning process** has been termed "**municipal relations**" (UPSC 1994) – not legislative, administrative or financial control.
- Relations depend on constitutional provisions, conventions and practices, judicial interpretation and mechanisms for dialogue.
# Seventh Schedule and residuary powers
- Transfer subjects (e.g. **stock exchanges, oil fields, trade unions, industrial disputes**, inland waterways) from Union/Concurrent Lists to the **State List** – **not foreign affairs**.
- **Residuary powers → States** (Sarkaria: residuary taxation with Parliament, other residuary to the Concurrent List).

=== Aftermath and Influence
- **Centre ignored** the report; no action.
- **April 1974:** TN Assembly adopted a **State autonomy resolution** based on it.
- DMK slogan: "**autonomy for the States, federalism at the Centre**".
- Base for **Anandpur Sahib (1973)** and the **West Bengal memorandum (1977)**.
- The ISC (1990) came through **Sarkaria**, not Rajamannar.
- **Kurian Joseph Committee** – appointed by Tamil Nadu in **2025** (Part I, 16 Feb 2026): State (1969) → Centre (1983, 2007) → State (2025).
