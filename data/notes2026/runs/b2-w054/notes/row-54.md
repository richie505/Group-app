=== Federal Tensions and Unifying Institutions
- Shaping factors: **constitutional provisions**, **planning**, **party politics**, **judicial interpretation**.
| Phase | Features |
|---|---|
| 1950s – early 1960s | Congress at Centre and States; calm; States relied on grants |
| Mid-1960s onwards | Congress lost many States; autonomy demands grew |
| Since **1990s** | **Coalition era**; greater say for States → cooperative federalism |
- Institutions that promote **"unity among diversity"** in Indian federalism: **Inter-State Council and NDC**, **Finance Commission and Regional (Zonal) Councils**, **unitary (single integrated) judiciary and All India Services** → **all of the above**.
- **Centre-State financial distribution** follows the recommendations of the **Finance Commission** (Art. 280) – not the ISC, Planning Commission or Parliament.
- **S.R. Bommai (1994):** Art. 356 action is **subject to judicial review**. **2015:** Planning Commission replaced by **NITI Aayog**.

=== All India Services and State Autonomy
- **Art. 312:** AIS = **IAS, IPS, Indian Forest Service**; recruited by **UPSC**; **ultimate control with the Centre, immediate control with the States**.
- New AIS: **Rajya Sabha** resolution by **two-thirds present and voting** – a power **exclusive to the Rajya Sabha** (unlike creating States or changing boundaries, done by Parliament as a whole).
- **A–R:** AIS are said to **violate the federal principle and State autonomy** because officers are governed by **Central rules** and States lack full control → both true, R explains A.
- For AIS: high standards, uniformity, **Centre-State coordination**.
- Abolition of **IAS and IPS** = **Rajamannar**; Sarkaria wanted AIS **strengthened**; Punchhi wanted **new AIS** (health, education, engineering, judiciary).
~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.15 p.238-243 and ch.75; Constitution Art. 249, 312

=== Special Category Status and Article 371
- SCS: **not in the Constitution**; introduced **1969** (Gadgil formula); **granted by the National Development Council** on Planning Commission advice (not the Union Cabinet or Finance Commission).
- Criteria (**Gadgil formula**): hilly terrain, low density or tribal share, border location, backwardness, weak finances.
- Benefit: **90%** Central funding of plan assistance; **11 States** (e.g. Sikkim, Himachal Pradesh, J&K, NE States, Uttarakhand). **Bihar never granted** SCS.
- **14th FC:** devolution raised to **42%**; no special/general distinction – Centre's main reason to deny SCS.
- **AP:** PM's assurance **20 Feb 2014**; got a **special assistance package (2016)** instead.
| Article | State |
|---|---|
| 371 | Maharashtra, Gujarat |
| **371A** | **Nagaland** |
| 371B / 371C | **Assam** / **Manipur** |
| **371D** | **AP** – local cadres, education, jobs |
| **371E** | **Central University in AP** |
| 371F-J | Sikkim, Mizoram, Arunachal, Goa, Karnataka |
- Ladakh offered **Art. 371** safeguards instead of the **Sixth Schedule** (2026); **FNTA** gives eastern Nagaland autonomy over **46** subjects (2026).
- **131st Amendment Bill 2026** (Lok Sabha expansion) defeated – sharpened the **North–South** debate (May 2026).

=== GST Council and Fiscal Centralisation
- **Art. 279A**; vote weight: **Centre one-third**, **States two-thirds**; decision needs **three-fourths** of weighted votes.
- **Mohit Minerals (May 2022):** GST Council recommendations **not binding**, only persuasive.
- GST **subsumed State taxes**; cesses and surcharges stay **outside the divisible pool** (~81% of gross tax, 2025-26).
- **16th FC keeps 41%** (2026-31); **State Finance Commissions** strengthen local devolution.
~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.47 (GST Council); app notes 2:54

=== Governor–State Conflicts and the Inter-State Council
- **State of Tamil Nadu v Governor (April 2025):** timelines and "deemed assent".
- **Presidential Reference opinion, 20 Nov 2025:** **no court-imposed timelines** for Governor or President; **deemed assent** alien to the Constitution.

- **Inter-State Council:** set up **1990** under Art. 263; **PM chairs**; **6 Union Cabinet ministers**; **11th and last meeting 16 July 2016**.
