[72:6:8] # Quantum and AI Terms
[72:6:9] - **Quantum entanglement**: shared quantum state - **measuring one particle's property (momentum, spin) instantly fixes its partner's, at any distance**. [72:6:10] - **Anthropic's Mythos** - AI model that can autonomously find and exploit software vulnerabilities; not public; shared selectively with critical-infrastructure organisations under **Project Glasswing**.
[72:6:11] - **Zero-day vulnerability** - flaw **unknown to developers, no patch** (not already patched).
[72:6:12] - **LLMs** use the **transformer** architecture; **temperature** controls randomness; **tokenization** splits text into words/subwords/characters (it does **not** decide the architecture); loss function measures prediction error.
[72:6:13] - Other terms: adversarial attacks, data poisoning, **XAI = Explainable AI**, EDR systems, deepfakes.
[72:6:14] - **Neuro-Symbolic AI** - hybrid of neural networks (patterns) + symbolic reasoning (rules, knowledge graphs) -> explainable answers, fewer hallucinations. [72:6:15] - **Anthropic study - "Observed Exposure"**: **actual workplace use** of AI (not theoretical capability); data from ~800 US occupations and Claude usage. **Most exposed**: computer programmers, financial analysts, customer service, data analysts, **legal**, office administration. **Least exposed**: **construction, agriculture, protective services, personal care**.
