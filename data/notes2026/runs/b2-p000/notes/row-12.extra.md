=== AP Endowments Act, 1987 – Authorities, Temple Property and the Tribunal
# The Act and its officers
- **Act:** **AP Charitable and Hindu Religious Institutions and Endowments Act, 1987 (Act 30 of 1987)** – regulates Hindu temples, mutts and charitable endowments in AP (State power under Art. 25(2)(a): secular activities of religion).
- **Hierarchy:** Commissioner → Regional Joint Commissioner (RJC) → Deputy Commissioner (DC) → Assistant Commissioner (AC) → **Executive Officers**; EOs outside the RJC, DC and AC cadres are in **three grades** (Grade I, II, III).
- **Executive Officer:** manages the institution and **represents the temple in legal proceedings** before courts.
- **Commissioner:** may **group institutions** and appoint a **single Executive Officer** for each group.
- **Budgets:** the **Assistant Commissioner** sanctions budgets of temples published under **Sec. 6(c)** (the lowest-income category).
# Temple property
- **Purchase** of immovable property for a temple: only with the **prior sanction of the Commissioner** (Sec. 79).
- **Sale** of temple land by **public auction:** needs the **Commissioner's** permission; objections are first called and disposed of and permission obtained **before** the auction – an E.O. **cannot auction first and seek approval later**.
- **Sec. 81 – unauthorised sale void:** a sale of endowment immovable property made **without the permission of the Government / Commissioner**, whether **before or after** the commencement of Act 30/1987, is **void**.
- **Sec. 82 – tenants:** a **small or marginal farmer** in possession of temple land at the commencement of the Act who cannot buy it may **continue as tenant** on paying at least **two-thirds of the market rent** (upheld by the SC in *State of AP v. Nallamilli Rami Reddi*, 2001).
# Encroachment and eviction
- **Group encroachment** on endowment lands: action initiated by the **Deputy Commissioner**.
- **Eviction petition** against an encroacher before the **Endowments Tribunal**: filed by the **Assistant Commissioner**, who also **implements the Tribunal's order**.
# Income of temples
- Sources: **hundi collections, devotees' offerings and kanukas, rents and income from the temple's own lands and properties** – **government grants are not** a regular source (Art. 27 bars taxes spent to promote a religion).
