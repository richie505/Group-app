## SUB 1 | MUDRA and Enterprise Credit
[167:1:0] - **MUDRA** = Micro Units Development and Refinance Agency; **PM MUDRA Yojana** launched **April 2015** – finances **micro enterprises**.
[167:1:1] - Aim: **"fund the unfunded"** – bring **small entrepreneurs into the formal financial system**; loans to **non-corporate, non-farm micro enterprises** (not farmers' crop loans, not pensions, not NGOs).
[167:1:2] - Nodal ministry: **Finance** (not MSME); refinance through **SIDBI** (not NABARD).
[167:1:3] TABLE head: Category | Loan size
  [167:1:3.0] Shishu | Up to ₹50,000
  [167:1:3.1] Kishor | ₹50,000 – ₹5 lakh
  [167:1:3.2] Tarun | ₹5 lakh – ₹10 lakh
  [167:1:3.3] Tarun Plus (24 Oct 2024) | ₹10 lakh – ₹20 lakh, for those who repaid a Tarun loan
[167:1:4] - MUDRA loans are **collateral-free** up to **₹10 lakh**; CGFMU cover extends to ₹20 lakh since Oct 2024.
[167:1:5] ~~ Sources: app notes 2:167 sub 1; Economic Survey 2025-26 summary (src/economy/economic-survey-2025-26-summary-01.txt) p.28 (PMMY finances micro and small enterprises)
