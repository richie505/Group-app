=== Infrastructure and Connectivity Programmes
| Programme | Objective |
|---|---|
| **Golden Quadrilateral** (NHDP Phase I) | Four-lane highways joining Delhi–Mumbai–Chennai–Kolkata; launched under PM **Atal Bihari Vajpayee** (1999-2001) |
| **UDAN** (RCS; scheme Oct 2016, first flight **April 2017**) | **Cost-effective regional air connectivity** for common people ("Ude Desh ka Aam Nagrik"); capped fares, viability gap funding; Ministry of **Civil Aviation** |
| **National Air Sports Policy (NASP 2022)** | Ministry of Civil Aviation; make India a **top air-sports nation by 2030** |
| PM Gati Shakti (Oct 2021) | National Master Plan for multimodal connectivity; GIS-based integrated planning |
| Bharatmala (2017) | Highways: economic corridors, border and coastal roads; NHAI/MoRTH |
| Sagarmala (2015) | Port-led development; Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways |
| National Infrastructure Pipeline | **₹111 lakh crore**, 2020-25 |
| Dedicated Freight Corridors | Freight-only rail by **DFCCIL**: Eastern Ludhiana–Sonnagar/Dankuni; Western **Dadri–JNPT** |
- UDAN is an air-connectivity scheme – **not free travel**, not a freight railway; Gati Shakti is not an air scheme.
- PM Gramin Sadak Yojana (2000) was also a Vajpayee-era rural-roads programme.

=== MUDRA and Enterprise Credit
- **MUDRA** = Micro Units Development and Refinance Agency; **PM MUDRA Yojana** launched **April 2015** – finances **micro enterprises**.
- Aim: **"fund the unfunded"** – bring **small entrepreneurs into the formal financial system**; loans to **non-corporate, non-farm micro enterprises** (not farmers' crop loans, not pensions, not NGOs).
- Nodal ministry: **Finance** (not MSME); refinance through **SIDBI** (not NABARD).
| Category | Loan size |
|---|---|
| Shishu | Up to ₹50,000 |
| Kishor | ₹50,000 – ₹5 lakh |
| Tarun | ₹5 lakh – ₹10 lakh |
| Tarun Plus (24 Oct 2024) | ₹10 lakh – ₹20 lakh, for those who repaid a Tarun loan |
- MUDRA loans are **collateral-free** up to **₹10 lakh**; CGFMU cover extends to ₹20 lakh since Oct 2024.

=== Industrial Corridors in Andhra Pradesh
- Of India's **11 industrial corridors**, **three** pass through **Andhra Pradesh** – the only State with nodes on three; nodal body **NICDC** (DPIIT).
| Corridor | AP node |
|---|---|
| **Visakhapatnam–Chennai (VCIC)** – with **ADB** | Kopparthy (YSR Kadapa) |
| **Chennai–Bengaluru (CBIC)** | Krishnapatnam (Nellore) |
| **Hyderabad–Bengaluru (HBIC)** | Orvakal (Kurnool) |
- Not AP corridors: Guntur–Bangalore, Nellore–Bangalore, Tirupati–Bangalore; Haldia is a West Bengal port.

=== Region-specific Missions – PM-DevINE
- **PM-DevINE**: announced in **Union Budget 2022-23** to close **development gaps in the North East**; **DoNER**; **100% central funding**.
- It is **additional** to existing Central and State schemes (not a substitute); funds **infrastructure convergently in the spirit of PM Gati Shakti**; supports **social development** and **livelihood activities for youth and women** → all four APPSC statements correct.
