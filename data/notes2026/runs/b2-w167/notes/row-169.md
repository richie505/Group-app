=== E-governance and Real Time Governance
# Real Time Governance (RTGS)
- **RTGS**: e-governance tools for **maximum governance with minimum government**, with **real-time performance indicators**; command centre at the **Secretariat** watching services and complaints live – the initiative **first launched in Andhra Pradesh**.
- Motto: **"People First"** (People First 1100 call centre takes citizens' feedback).
- Uses the **Hitachi Visualization Suite** – disaster plans and other real-time data on a **single pane of glass** for timely decisions.
- **CORE dashboard** = **CM Office Real-time Executive** dashboard tracking departments' indicators.
# Other Platforms
| Initiative | Purpose |
|---|---|
| e-Seva (2001) | India's first integrated citizen service centres |
| MeeSeva | Single-window delivery of certificates and services |
| BhuSeva | Integrated land-records platform; **Bhudhaar** number per land parcel |
| AP FiberNet (2017) | Low-cost broadband, TV and telephone to homes and offices |
| **Jnanabhumi** (2017) | Common paperless platform for **all scholarships and education-related schemes** |
| **Pedarikampai Gelupu (PPG)** | Common online platform of the **welfare (finance) corporations**, linked to federations and bank branches – not scholarships |
> Exam angle: RTGS "first in AP", "People First", Hitachi; scholarships = Jnanabhumi vs welfare corporations = PPG.

=== Agriculture and Groundwater Programmes
# Programmes
| Programme | Year | Purpose / benefit |
|---|---|---|
| **Krishi Cabinet** | June 2015 | Cabinet sub-committee steering the **Primary Sector Mission** |
| APCNF | Since 2016 | Natural farming run by **Rythu Sadhikara Samstha (RySS)** |
| Rythu Bharosa Kendras (RBKs) | 2020 | **Village one-stop centres** for seeds, fertilisers, advisory, procurement |
| **YSR Jala Kala** | 2020 | **Free borewells** for small and marginal farmers – single or group farmers with **2.5 acres**; a policy against irrigation water shortage |
| YSR Zero Interest Crop Loans | 20 April 2021 | Crop loans up to **Rs 1 lakh** repaid on time become interest-free |
| e-Crop | – | **Digital booking of the crop** on every field each season |
- **CCRC** (AP Crop Cultivators Rights Act, 2019): tenants get access to **loans, insurance and input subsidy**; **no ownership title**.
- **PMFBY:** farmer premium **2% kharif, 1.5% rabi**, 5% commercial; **voluntary for all since Kharif 2020**.
# APFAMGS (Farmer Managed Groundwater Systems)
- **FAO**-supported (2004-09), 7 drought-prone districts: farmers measure and manage groundwater **demand** themselves – **demand-side** management.
- Principle: **rules are set by the users** (farmer groups), not by outsiders.
- **AP Integrated Irrigation and Agriculture Transformation Project** – **World Bank** (USD 172.2 million, 2018).
> Exam angle: Jala Kala = free borewells (asked twice); APFAMGS demand side, user-made rules.

=== Swarna Andhra @2047 and Zero Poverty – P4
- Vision unveiled **December 2024**; frame for the **2024-29 policies** and industrial corridors.
- 2047 targets: GSDP **USD 2.4 trillion**; per capita income **about USD 42,000**; **net zero by 2047**.
- **Ten principles (Sutralu)** – the first is **zero poverty**.
- **Zero Poverty – P4 Policy** (**P**ublic-**P**rivate-**P**eople **P**artnership): launched **30 March 2025 (Ugadi)**, the cornerstone of Swarna Andhra @2047 – aims to **eradicate poverty** (zero poverty), not investment or tourism.

=== Energy, Green and Aqua Policies
- **Tribal mini hydel plants:** APTRIPCO built **1.6 MW** plants at Pinjarikonda, Metlapalem and **Vetamamidi**; **Vetamamidi** (Addateegala mandal) began production in **2011**.
- **AP Integrated Clean Energy Policy, 2024**: solar, wind, hybrid, **pumped storage**, **green hydrogen/ammonia**; target **160 GW**; **net zero by 2047** (not 2070).
- **Pinnapuram (Kurnool)**: Greenko integrated solar-wind-**pumped storage** project.
- **NTPC Green Hydrogen Hub, Pudimadaka** (Anakapalli): JV of NTPC Green Energy and **NREDCAP**.
- **AP State Aquaculture Development Authority Act, 2020**: regulates **aqua farms, seed and feed**.
- Fishing harbours Phase I: **Juvvaladinne, Nizampatnam, Machilipatnam, Uppada** (Pudimadaka is Phase II).
