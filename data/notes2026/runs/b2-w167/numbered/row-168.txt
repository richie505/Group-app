## SUB 0 | Farmer and Occupational Support Schemes
[0:0] # YSR Rythu Bharosa (2019-24)
[0:1] - **Launched:** **October 2019** (15 Oct, Nellore) – the farmer pillar of **Navaratnalu**; Navaratnalu itself is the whole package, not a farmer scheme.
[0:2] - **Amount:** **Rs 13,500 a year per farmer family = Rs 7,500 State + Rs 6,000 PM-KISAN** (three instalments) – SES 2021-22: paid to **land-owning** families and this **includes** the PM-KISAN Rs 6,000 (both statements true).
[0:3] - **Promise vs payment:** the 2019 manifesto/Budget promised **Rs 12,500**; raised to **Rs 13,500** at launch – Rs 10,500/14,500/20,500 are distractors.
[0:4] - **Tenants:** the State paid the full Rs 13,500 from its own funds to **SC, ST, BC and minority tenant farmers** and **ROFR (forest land) holders**.
[0:5] - **Budget 2019-20:** **Rs 8,750 crore** (not 8,570) for **64.06 lakh farmers incl. 15.36 lakh tenant farmers**.
[0:6] - **Successor:** **Annadata Sukhibhava** (2024-): **Rs 20,000 = Rs 14,000 State + Rs 6,000 PM-KISAN**, tenants included.
[0:7] - **Telangana's Rythu Bharosa** = Rs 12,000 an acre (successor of **Rythu Bandhu**) – do not mix with AP.
[0:8] # Rythu Runa Mafi (TDP, 2014)
[0:9] - Crop-loan waiver up to **Rs 1.5 lakh per farm family** – the maximum relief.

## SUB 2 | Education Welfare
[2:0] # Amma Vodi → Talliki Vandanam
[2:1] - **Jagananna Amma Vodi** (Jan 2020): **Rs 15,000 a year** to the **mother/recognised guardian** of a poor child who **enrols and attends** school – the massive **school-enrolment support** programme (from 2021-22 Rs 2,000 kept for school/toilet maintenance, Rs 13,000 credited).
[2:2] - **Talliki Vandanam** (Super Six) replaced it: **Rs 15,000 per child** a year, children in **government or private** schools; aims to raise the **Gross Enrolment Ratio**.
