## SUB 0 | Arcuate Style – Features and Architectural Firsts
[0:0] - **Arcuate style:** **arch, dome and vault** brought by the Turks; the **true arch** is held by a **keystone**. Indian **trabeate** style = **lintel, pillar and corbel**; the two merged.
[0:1] - **Materials:** rubble masonry in **surkhi** (brick dust in lime mortar), chunam plaster, red sandstone and marble; decoration moved away from **human and animal figures** to **arabesque**, geometry and **Quranic calligraphy**.
[0:2] - **Terms:** **mihrab** = prayer niche; minbar = pulpit; **jali** = pierced stone screen.
[0:3] TABLE head: First | Building
  [0:3.0] Earliest Indo-Islamic building; first Sultanate mosque | Quwwat-ul-Islam, Delhi (Aibak, completed 1198)
  [0:3.1] First Islamic tomb (strict) | Sultan Ghari (1231), built by Iltutmish for his son Nasiruddin Mahmud
  [0:3.2] First tomb in a purely Indo-Islamic style; first true arch | Balban's tomb, Mehrauli
  [0:3.3] First true dome; first building fully on Islamic principles; horse-shoe arch introduced | Alai Darwaza (1311, Alauddin Khalji)
  [0:3.4] First octagonal tomb | Khan-i-Jahan Tilangani's tomb (Firoz Tughlaq's reign)
  [0:3.5] First double dome | Sikandar Lodi's tomb (Lodis pioneered it; Humayun's Tomb brought it to the Mughals)
[0:4] - **⚠ Disputed (UPPSC 2004 "first Indo-Islamic tomb"):** most keys say **Balban's tomb** (true arch); strictly **Sultan Ghari** is older.
[0:5] # Dynasty → style
[0:6] TABLE head: Dynasty | Features | Example
  [0:6.0] Khalji | Horse-shoe arch with lotus-bud fringe; true dome; red sandstone with marble (Seljuq influence) | Alai Darwaza
  [0:6.1] Tughlaq | Battered (sloping) walls, arch-beam combination, plain fort-like mass, octagonal plan emerges, encaustic tiles; Syrian/Byzantine-type false arches | Tughlaqabad; Ghiyasuddin's tomb (sloping walls, marble dome)
  [0:6.2] Sayyid and Lodi | Octagonal tombs with verandah, double dome, Hindu-style gateways, plaster ornament, drip-stones | Sikandar Lodi's tomb (octagonal), Bara Gumbad
  [0:6.3] Sharqi (Jaunpur) | Massive propylon gateway with inclined walls, no minarets | Atala Masjid, Lal Darwaza Mosque
  [0:6.4] Vijayanagara | Bodegai (bracket capital) on pillars | Hampi
[0:7] - **RAS 2013 match:** lotus-bud fringe – **Khaljis**; octagonal tomb – **Tughluqs**; bodegai – **Vijayanagara**; massive inclined-wall gate – **Sharqis** → **3 4 2 1**.
[0:8] > Alai Darwaza = horse-shoe arch + first true dome; Balban's tomb = first true arch. The commonest confusion in this topic.

## SUB 2 | Qutb Minar and the Qutb Complex
[2:0] TABLE head: Storey | Builder
  [2:0.0] 1st | Qutbuddin Aibak – began it (c. 1199)
  [2:0.1] 2nd–4th | Iltutmish – completed it
  [2:0.2] Top: 5th storey | Firoz Shah Tughlaq – replaced the lightning-damaged top storey and added the fifth
  [2:0.3] Repairs | Sikandar Lodi
[2:1] - **Name:** commonly believed to honour Sufi saint **Khwaja Qutbuddin Bakhtiyar Kaki** (⚠ debated: some say Aibak).
[2:2] - **Height:** about **72.5 m (238 ft)**, **five** tapering storeys – "180 ft" is false.
[2:3] - **UNESCO:** "**Qutb Minar and its Monuments, Delhi**" – **1993**: Minar, Quwwat-ul-Islam, Alai Darwaza, Alai Minar, Iron Pillar, Iltutmish's tomb, **Imam Zamin's tomb**. **Tughlaqabad is NOT** part of it.
[2:4] - **Iron Pillar:** **Gupta** period; king "Chandra" = **Chandragupta II**; rust-free.
