## SUB 1 | Community Development Programme and Nes
[80:1:0] # Grow More Food Inquiry Committee (1952)
[80:1:1] - Chairman: **V.T. Krishnamachari** (APPSC G2 2008); distractors – Balwant Rai Mehta, Ashok Mehta, G.V.K. Rao.
[80:1:2] - Found the campaign too narrow → **integrated village development**; suggested a **nationwide extension organisation** → CDP and NES.
[80:1:3] # CDP (1952)
[80:1:4] - Launched **2 October 1952** (Gandhi Jayanti) by **Nehru**, in the **First Five Year Plan** (1951-56); began with **55 community projects** (pilot).
[80:1:5] - **First major rural development programme** after Independence; called "**mother of social development programmes**".
[80:1:6] - **Aim**: **development of villages through public participation** (APPSC 2018); a step towards **Article 40**.
[80:1:7] - Components: agriculture, irrigation, communications, health, housing, **social education** (adult literacy, community centres, youth and women's clubs).
[80:1:8] - AP: Andhra State Plan (1953, capital **Kurnool**) carried CDP under the First Plan.
[80:1:9] APPSC 2019: **Social education** was a component of the **CDP** – NOT of NAEP, IRDP or the National Literacy Mission.
[80:1:10] # National Extension Service (1953) and Organisation
[80:1:11] - **NES (1953)**: lighter, extension-staff version that spread the CDP pattern nationwide; **NES followed CDP** (BPSC 2022).

[80:1:13] - Failure: seen as **government-imposed**, run top-down; **poor people's participation** → Balwant Rai Mehta review.

## SUB 2 | Balwant Rai Mehta Committee (1957)
[80:2:0] # Appointment and Report

  [80:2:1.3] Report | 24 November 1957
  [80:2:1.4] Accepted | NDC, January 1958 – States free to vary the pattern; basic principles common
[80:2:2] - Finding: **public participation is indispensable**; needs full cooperation of the **local public**.
[80:2:3] - Remedy: **democratic decentralisation** – term **first used** by this committee (APPSC 2019); "later came to be known as Panchayati Raj".

  [80:2:6.2] Zila Parishad | District | Indirect | Advisory, coordinating, supervisory
[80:2:7] - Tiers "**organically linked**" through indirect elections.
[80:2:8] - **District Collector = chairman** of the Zila Parishad.
[80:2:9] - **Genuine transfer of power** and **adequate resources**; all planning and development work entrusted to these bodies.
[80:2:10] APPSC 2019: BRM did NOT recommend a **two-tier** system, **direct election to the Zila Parishad**, or the ZP as **executive**. **Nyaya Panchayat is not a tier** (UPPSC 2024).
[80:2:11] - Under the **73rd Amendment**, Panchayat Samiti members are **directly elected** (UP: **Kshetra Panchayat**) – unlike BRM's indirect model.

## SUB 5 | Ashok Mehta Committee (1977-78)
[80:5:0] # Appointment
[80:5:1] - Appointed **December 1977** by the **Janata government** of **PM Morarji Desai** (APPSC G2 2008); report **August 1978**, **132 recommendations**.
[80:5:2] # Recommendations
[80:5:3] - **Two-tier**: **Zila Parishad** (district, **executive**, district planning) + **Mandal Panchayat** (group of villages, **15,000-20,000** people).
[80:5:4] - **Term: 4 years** (standard; APPSC G2 2023 key) – ⚠ APPSC key (2025) gave **two tiers with a five-year term**.
[80:5:5] - **District = first point of decentralisation** below the State.
[80:5:6] - **Official participation of political parties**; elections by the State's Chief Electoral Officer with the CEC; superseded body → **elections within 6 months**.
[80:5:7] - **Compulsory taxation powers**; **social audit** by a district agency and a committee of legislators.
[80:5:8] - **Constitutional recognition**; **Minister for Panchayati Raj** in the State cabinet; SC/ST reservation by population; separate **Nyaya Panchayats**.
[80:5:9] # Fate and Legacy
[80:5:10] - Janata government fell before finishing its term → **no national action**; two-tier model **never adopted nationally**.
[80:5:11] - **Sixth Plan (Janata, 1978-83)** gave PRIs new life; dropped after 1980, never an official Plan (APPSC G2 2017).
[80:5:12] - Post-1977 panchayats = "**Second Generation Panchayats**"; **West Bengal** (Panchayat Act 1973; polls June 1978), **Karnataka** and **AP** adopted its ideas.
[80:5:13] # Comparison
[80:5:14] TABLE head: Point | Balwant Rai Mehta (1957) | Ashok Mehta (1978)
  [80:5:14.0] Tiers | 3 | 2
  [80:5:14.1] Unit | Block (Samiti executive) | District (ZP executive)
  [80:5:14.2] Elections | Direct at village, indirect above | Party participation at all levels
  [80:5:14.3] Collector | ZP chairman | Reduced role
  [80:5:14.4] Constitutional status | Not proposed | Recommended
[80:5:15] UPPSC 2023: "three-tier replaced by two-tier" is correct; the block statement is false – Ashok Mehta made the **district** the unit.

## SUB 9 | Road to Constitutional Status (1989-1993)
[80:9:0] TABLE head: Step | Fact
  [80:9:0.0] 64th Amendment Bill | Rajiv Gandhi government, Lok Sabha, July 1989; first attempt at constitutional status; based on Singhvi, Thungon, Gadgil
  [80:9:0.1] Fate | Passed Lok Sabha, defeated in Rajya Sabha (October 1989) for lack of special majority
  [80:9:0.2] 65th Amendment Bill | Nagarpalika Bill for municipalities; also defeated in the Rajya Sabha (1989)
  [80:9:0.3] National Front (V.P. Singh) bill, 1990 | Lapsed – Lok Sabha dissolved
  [80:9:0.4] Narasimha Rao bills | Modified bills, September 1991 → Joint Parliamentary Committee → passed December 1992
  [80:9:0.5] 73rd Amendment | In force 24 April 1993 (not 15 August 1993); Part IX (Art. 243-243O), Eleventh Schedule (29 subjects); 74th = Municipalities
[80:9:1] - Opposition objection: **Local Government is a State List** subject – Centre would deal with local bodies over States' heads and **centralise power**.
[80:9:2] - 64th Bill = legislative origin of the 73rd Amendment (BPSC 2020).
[80:9:3] - Key Articles: **243B(2)** – middle tier may be skipped below **20 lakh** population; **243D(3)** – at least **one-third** seats for **women**; **243G** – powers of Panchayats; **243J** – audit; **243K** – **State Election Commission**. Regular remuneration to members is **not** a provision.
[80:9:4] - PRIs made **justiciable**; constitutional obligation on States; women now **over 49.7%** of elected representatives, **19 States/UTs** reserve 50% (23 Apr 2026).
[80:9:5] - **National Panchayati Raj Day: 24 April** – 2025 (32nd anniversary) main event at **Lohna Uttar GP, Madhubani**; 17th edition held 24 April 2026.
