## SUB 0 | Gram Panchayat at Work – Sarpanch, Panchayat Secretary, Meetings
[0:0] # Who does what in a Gram Panchayat (AP Panchayat Raj Act, 1994)
[0:1] TABLE head: Function | Answer
  [0:1.0] Convenes Gram Panchayat meetings | Panchayat Secretary – with the approval of, or on the direction of, the Sarpanch (s.31); the Sarpanch directs that the meeting be held
  [0:1.1] Presides over GP meetings | Sarpanch; in his absence the Upa-Sarpanch
  [0:1.2] Implements resolutions of the GP and its committees | Panchayat Secretary (the executive authority)
  [0:1.3] Collects taxes; calculates house tax of a new building | Panchayat Secretary
  [0:1.4] Written permission for any advertisement in the village (s.64) | Panchayat Secretary (executive authority)
  [0:1.5] Expels a convicted bye-law offender from a public market (s.107) | Panchayat Secretary (executive authority)
  [0:1.6] Marriage Officer of the gram panchayat (appointed by the Governor) | Panchayat Secretary
  [0:1.7] Bans sewage on public roads, sanitation orders; cremation/burial of destitute bodies | Gram Panchayat (the body)
  [0:1.8] Scrutinises the records of the GP | Sarpanch
  [0:1.9] Forms Joint Action Committees for joint works and settles disputes between panchayats | Sarpanch
  [0:1.10] Reports epidemics, atrocities on SCs, prepares the draft BPL list | Panchayat Secretary
  [0:1.11] Reports births/bodies found in hospitals, jails, choultries, open places | the officer in charge / the person who first sees it
[0:2] - **Sarpanch:** **executive head** and political head of the GP; **directly elected**; **controls** the Panchayat Secretary.
[0:3] - **Panchayat Secretary:** the **Government's representative** (functionary) in each panchayat; **executive authority**; works under the **administrative control of the Gram Panchayat** and is its **immediate subordinate** (all officers and employees are **subordinate to the Gram Panchayat**, s.36(5)).
[0:4] - **Not** a Secretary's function: **school supervision** (his work is village administration, community welfare and community development).
[0:5] - **Appeal:** an order of the Panchayat Secretary granting, refusing, suspending or revoking a **licence or permission** → appeal to the **Gram Panchayat** (s.128).
[0:6] - **Obstruction:** wrongfully restraining the Secretary or his delegate from entering any place to exercise lawful powers is **an offence under the Indian Penal Code** (now the **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023**).
[0:7] - **Annual report:** the Secretary prepares an **Administration Report** on the panchayat's governance each year, submitted by **May**.
[0:8] - **Other Acts:** the competent authority under the **Minimum Wages Act, 1948** can notify the Panchayat Secretary as an **Inspector**.
[0:9] - **Dissolution:** if a GP abuses its powers, the **State Government** (through the administration) can dissolve it.
[0:10] - **Body corporate:** every Gram Panchayat (and every Mandal Praja Parishad) is a **body corporate** with perpetual succession and a common seal.
[0:11] - **Disqualification:** contractors for the panchayat, its employees and honorary magistrates are disqualified; a person who served the last term as a **ward member can contest**.
[0:12] # Obligatory vs discretionary functions
[0:13] TABLE head: Type | Examples
  [0:13.0] Obligatory | Sanitation, drinking water, drains, street lighting, village roads, burial grounds
  [0:13.1] Discretionary | Tree planting, libraries, markets, mother-child welfare homes, modern farming practices
[0:14] - **Municipal Corporation:** **water supply** is an **obligatory** function; relief to destitutes, fairs and exhibitions and roadside tree planting are **discretionary**.
[0:15] - GP **property protection:** the 10% open-space land of layouts and parks is a **Category A** property to be protected; district **GP Properties Protection Wing** committees are supervised at the Commissionerate by the **Vigilance (Encroachment) Wing**.
[0:16] > Exam angle: "convenes / implements / collects / permits" = **Secretary**; "presides / directs / scrutinises / controls" = **Sarpanch**; "the body that orders" = **Gram Panchayat**.

## SUB 1 | Gram Panchayat Finances – Taxes, Accounts and Funds
[1:0] # Taxes (s.60)
[1:1] - **Compulsory taxes:** **House tax** (main own revenue, levied every year) and **Kolagaram / Katarusum** – tax on village produce sold in the village **by weight, measurement or number** (i.e. by weight and by volume).
[1:2] - **Optional taxes:** a **vehicle tax** and a **tax on agricultural land for a specific purpose**.
[1:3] - **Land cess:** 2 naya paise in the rupee on annual rental value; a fee for a specific purpose over and above taxes on land is a **cess**.
[1:4] - **Surcharge on land:** with the prescribed authority's prior approval, up to **25 naya paise in the rupee** (s.60(5)).
[1:5] - **Betterment charges:** **Rs 50 per sq m** in GPs under a municipal master plan, at mandal headquarters, or with 10,000+ population.
[1:6] - **Gated Community:** minimum site area **1000 sq m** (AP key).
[1:7] - **Not** a source of GP funds: **fees on services to SHGs** (taxes, Central grants and loans are).
[1:8] # Accounts
[1:9] - **Model Accounting System (MAS)** – the framework prepared by the **Central Government** (MoPR with the **C&AG**, 2009); PRIs keep accounts on a **cash basis**.
[1:10] - MAS classifies transactions on a **three-tier structure**: **Function (Major Head), Programme (Minor Head), Object (Object Head)**; "weekly upgradation of accounts" is **not** a MAS feature.
[1:11] - Book-keeping: **double entry** (PRIASoft); some keys say "both single and double entry".
[1:12] - **PRIASoft** – MoPR's web-based **accounting software for PRIs**, for accountability and transparency; the most popular accounting application in panchayats.
[1:13] - Fiscal year ends **31 March**; salaries paid through the **Acquittance Register**; durable goods first entered in the **Stock Register**; approved bills stamped **"Paid and cancelled"**.
[1:14] - Working balance: at least **5%** of estimated receipts; surplus money invested for at most **three years**; contractor's **EMD 2.5%** of the estimate; **10%** of GP funds for welfare activities.
[1:15] - **Surcharge certificate** – enforceable like a **civil court decree**.
[1:16] - **Zilla Parishad markets:** income shared **35 : 65** among PRIs.
[1:17] - **Internal audit** of Zilla Praja Parishads – inspected by the **Commissioner, Panchayat Raj**.
[1:18] - **m-FMS pilot** (Government of India) in AP – **East Godavari** district.
[1:19] # Devolution and State Finance Commissions
[1:20] - **SFC** (243I/243Y) – formed by the **Governor** (not the SEC) every five years; Part VI of the 1994 Act. **4th SFC** report Oct 2019; **5th SFC** constituted 20 March 2023 for **2025-30**.
[1:21] - **3rd SFC** per-capita grant **ZP : MP : GP = Rs 8 : 16 : 8**.
[1:22] - **14th and 15th FC** grants favoured **drinking water and sanitation**. **16th FC (2026-31):** AP rural local bodies **Rs 16,627 crore** (AP Budget 2026-27); only **duly constituted** (elected) bodies get grants.
[1:23] - **Main own revenue of a GP – property (house) tax**; intermediate and district tiers rely on grants.
[1:24] - **Atmanirbhar Panchayat Program:** GP own revenue **≥ Rs 50 lakh** (Block ≥ Rs 1 crore).
[1:25] - **3Fs – Funds, Functions, Functionaries** – still not fully devolved.

  [1:26.0] Transfer of Finances | 50
  [1:26.1] Transfer of Functionaries | 15
  [1:26.2] Autonomy of PRIs | 15
  [1:26.3] Transfer of Functions | 10

## SUB 2 | Evolution of Panchayati Raj in AP – Acts and Committees
[2:0] # Order among States
[2:1] - **Balwant Rai Mehta Committee (1957)** – accepted by the **NDC, January 1958**; three tiers – **Gram Panchayat, Panchayat Samiti, Zilla Parishad** (no Mandal Parishad); **Panchayat Samiti** the executive body; the **Zilla Parishad approves the Samiti's budget**.
[2:2] - **Rajasthan** first – **Nehru** inaugurated it on **2 October 1959** at **Nagaur**; **Andhra Pradesh** second – **1 November 1959**.
[2:3] - **Kasu Brahmananda Reddy** – Minister for Local Bodies in N. Sanjiva Reddy's cabinet.
[2:4] # AP laws
[2:5] TABLE head: Law / reform | Year
  [2:5.0] AP Panchayat Samithis and Zilla Parishads Act (first AP law) | 1959 (amended 1961: standing committees 5 → 7)
  [2:5.1] AP Gram Panchayats Act – integrated Andhra and Telangana panchayats; provided for the Gram Sabha | 1964
  [2:5.2] Mandal system (NTR Government, Act 31 of 1986): Panchayat Samithis abolished → Mandal Praja Parishads; ZPs → Zilla Praja Parishads | 1986
  [2:5.3] AP Panchayat Raj Act (Act 13 of 1994) – incorporates the 73rd Amendment | 21 April 1994
  [2:5.4] AP Panchayat Raj (Amendment) Act for Scheduled Areas (PESA) | 1998
[2:6] - **1104 mandals** were formed in united AP after the Samithis were abolished.
[2:7] - 1994 Act: **278 sections**; Part II Gram Panchayat; III Mandal Parishads; IV Zilla Parishads; V elections; VI Finance Commission; **s.40** – functional committees at GP level (agriculture, public health, water supply, sanitation, education …).
[2:8] - **Revenue village** (s.3): a local area recognised as a village in the revenue accounts, after excluding municipal, cantonment and similar areas.
[2:9] - The 1986 Mandal system drew on **Ashok Mehta**'s "Mandal Panchayat".

[3:1] - AP's intermediate tier = **Mandal Parishad**, not Panchayat Samiti; **13 Zilla Praja Parishads → 28** after district reorganisation.
[3:2] - **Article 243C:** village chairperson's mode left to the State; intermediate and district chairpersons indirect.
[3:3] - **Members:** a GP of population **up to 300** has **5 members including the Sarpanch**. **Mandal Parishad permanent invitees – five** (Collector, Sarpanches, ZPTC member, ZP Chairperson, Agricultural Market Committee President; no vote); one minority member co-opted.
[3:4] - **Zilla Praja Parishad:** **7 standing committees** (1994 Act).
[3:5] - **Non-party polls:** **Gram Panchayat** (Sarpanch and ward) polls are **without party symbols** – no party competition at GP level; MPTC/ZPTC polls are on party symbols.
[3:6] - **Two-child norm:** s.19(3) – a person with **more than two children** was disqualified; removed by the AP Panchayat Raj and Municipal Laws (Amendment) Acts, **Nov 2024**.

## SUB 4 | Gram Sabha, MGNREGA and Community Organisations
[4:0] - **Gram Sabha** (Art. 243A; defined in **Art. 243(b)**) – all persons registered in the electoral rolls of the panchayat area; convened and presided over by the Sarpanch (or Upa-Sarpanch).
[4:1] - **Article 243G** – powers, authority and responsibilities of Panchayats: plans for **economic development and social justice** – the main objective of PRIs.
[4:2] - **Requisition:** the Sarpanch must call a Gram Sabha on a written request of **10% of members or 50 members**.
[4:3] - **MGNREGA works** are identified by the **Gram Panchayat on the recommendation of the Gram Sabha and Ward Sabhas**.
[4:4] - **MGNREGA social audit** in AP culminates in an **open meeting (public hearing) at mandal level on the 7th day**.
[4:5] - **Community-based organisations** for PR purposes: **SHGs, Village Organisations, Mandal Samakhyas** – **not political parties**; SHGs must **encourage members to attend Gram Sabhas**.
[4:6] - **Gram Samvaad** – app giving single-window access to rural-development programme information at GP level.
[4:7] - **Jamabandi** – annual check (audit) of village revenue accounts.
