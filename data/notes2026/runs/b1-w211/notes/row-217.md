=== Prakasam Committee and the 1948 Abolition Act
- **1937:** Rajaji's Madras ministry appointed a **zamindari committee** chaired by **Revenue Minister T. Prakasam**; report **7 Nov 1938**: **the land belongs to the tiller; the zamindar is only a tax collector**.
| Step | Date |
|---|---|
| Bill introduced by Revenue Minister **Kala Venkata Rao** | **11 Nov 1947** |
| Passed – **Madras Estates (Abolition and Conversion into Ryotwari) Act** | **1948** |
| Assent of Governor-General **C. Rajagopalachari** | **2 April 1949** (called the first law of independent India with the GG's assent) |
| In force | 7 Sept 1949 |
- Abolished zamindari and inam estates; tenants became **direct ryots of the State**; zamindars compensated.
- Shielded by the **1st (1951)** and **4th (1955)** Amendments; one of the **first abolition laws** of independent India.
- **Madras Estates Land Act 1908:** **regulated** landholder–ryot relations, **did not abolish** estates; 3rd Amendment (1936) – occupancy rights for those in possession on **30 June 1934**.

=== Causes and Exactions
- About **40%** of Andhra's land was still under zamindars at Independence; **70%** of the Presidency's zamindari land lay in Andhra districts.
- **Pattabhi** first called for an anti-zamindari struggle in **1920**.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|

| **Vetti** | Forced free labour |

| **Bedalli** | **Eviction** for non-payment |
| **Gothi** | Hereditary **bonded labour** in the Agency |
