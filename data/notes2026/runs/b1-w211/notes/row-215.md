=== Tanguturi Prakasam – Andhra Kesari
# Simon boycott, 1928
- Simon Commission appointed **Nov 1927**, arrived **3 Feb 1928**.
- In the **Simon Go Back** protest at **Madras (1928)** Prakasam **bared his chest before police guns** ("shoot me") → title **"Andhra Kesari"** – not Home Rule, Vande Mataram or Quit India.
# Papers and career
| Year | Fact |
|---|---|
| 1872 | Born **23 Aug 1872**, **Vinodarayunipalem** (NextGen prints 1892, Valluru – wrong) |
| 1903 | Chairman, **Rajahmundry Municipality**; acted main roles in the Hindu Nataka Samajam |
| **1921** | Gave up law practice for the NCM (not 1925); started the **English daily *Swarajya*** (26 Oct 1921); also weekly *Grama Swarajya* |
| 1937 | **Revenue Minister** in **Rajaji's** ministry – pro-peasant zamindari enquiry, opposed inside his party |
| 1946 | **Premier of composite Madras**; **Firka Development Scheme** |
| 1951 | Left Congress with N.G. Ranga → **Kisan Mazdoor Praja Party** |
| 1953 | **First Chief Minister of Andhra State** (Bezawada Gopal Reddy 2nd and last) |
- President of the Andhra Congress Committee **18 times**; **Prakasam district** is named after him.
- Books: autobiography ***Naa Jeevita Yatra*** (4th part by **Tenneti Viswanatham**); ***Law Times***.
- **Prakasam district irrigation:** **Gundlakamma Reservoir** (also Veligonda).
> Trap: Swarajya = English daily; "Kesari" is only his title.

=== Pattabhi Sitaramayya
- **Andhra Bank (1923, Machilipatnam)**; also Krishna District Co-operative Bank, **Bharata Lakshmi Bank**, Andhra Insurance – the patriot who was also a great **businessman**.
- Books: ***History of the Indian National Congress* (1935)** – the **official history** of the Congress; ***Feathers and Stones*** (Ahmednagar Fort diary); *Gandhi and Gandhism*.
- **Tripuri (1939):** Gandhi's candidate, **defeated by Subhas Chandra Bose** – Gandhi called it "my own defeat".
- Presided the **Jaipur session (1948)**; member of the **JVP Committee** (Nehru, Patel, Pattabhi) – it first **rejected** linguistic states (report **April 1949**).
- Wrote in *The Hindu* on the need for **linguistic states**; APCC President (1937).
- **Governor of Madhya Pradesh (1952-57)**.
- First Telugu INC president: exam keys say **Pattabhi**; strictly **P. Ananda Charlu** presided in 1891.
~~ Sources: NextGen AP History Part 7 p.9, Part 19 p.24; app notes 1:215 (sub 5)

=== Bulusu Sambamurthy and the Sribagh Pact
- **Bulusu Sambamurthy ("Maharshi"):** backed the 1920 election boycott; attended the Vijayawada AICC (1921); **Reception Committee chairman, Kakinada session (1923)**; **toured all Andhra** for the Simon boycott (1928).
- **Speaker of the Madras Assembly** in the **first Rajaji ministry (1937)**; declared Andhra state must be formed before the Constitution came into force.
- **Potti Sriramulu's fast** began **19 Oct 1952** at **Sambamurthy's house in Mylapore, Madras**; Sriramulu died 15 Dec 1952.
- **Sribagh Pact, 16 Nov 1937:** Rayalaseema–Circar agreement at **Kasinathuni Nageswara Rao's house "Sribagh"**, Madras; coastal signatories **Prakasam and Sambamurthy**.
- Tirupati question (1953): the keyed answer credits **C. Rajagopalachari** with keeping Tirupati in Andhra against the Tamil claim (unverified).
~~ Sources: NextGen AP History Part 6 p.13, Part 7 p.17, p.23, Part 23 p.14; app notes 1:215 (sub 6)

=== Pingali Venkayya
- Born **2 Aug 1876**, **Bhatlapenumarru** (near Machilipatnam).
- **Soldier in the British Army in South Africa (Anglo-Boer War)**.
- Attended the **Calcutta INC session (1906)**; 1916 book ***Bharatha Desaniki Oka Jatiya Patakam*** (about 30 flag designs).
- Showed his flag to Gandhi at the **Vijayawada AICC (31 Mar – 1 Apr 1921)**.
- Titles: **Patti** (cotton varieties), **Japan**, **Diamond** Venkayya.
