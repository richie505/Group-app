=== Viplava Parivartanamu and Plays Banned by the British
# The banned NCM drama
- **Viplava Parivartanamu:** published and **banned during the Non-Cooperation Movement**; it pushed **Swadeshi goods** and khaddar.
- **Author: Kodali Anjaneyulu** (NextGen; APPSC 2018 and G2 2019) – not **Gollapudi Sitarama Sastri** (Swami Sitaram), the usual trap.
  - Gollapudi Sitarama Sastri wrote the NCM song **"Kallu Monadoi"** and supported the **NCM boycott of elections (1920)**.
- **Why troupes were watched:** a drama company could put a political idea before **thousands of illiterate people in one evening**.
- Drama companies were the **main mass entertainment** of the time, so they carried **social-reform** themes first and **nationalist** ones later.
- **Jan 2022:** the AP government banned staging of ***Chintamani*** (over the character Subbi Setty); the ban was challenged in the High Court.
> Trap: Viplava Parivartanamu = Kodali Anjaneyulu, NCM; Gollapudi Sitarama Sastri = "Kallu Monadoi".

=== Playwrights and Plays
| Play | Playwright | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| **Vyavahara Dharmabodhini** | **Kandukuri Veeresalingam** | Reformist social drama |
| **Kanyasulkam** | **Gurajada Apparao** | Against **bride-price**; spoken Telugu |
| **Kuliraju** | **Boyi Bhimanna** | Social drama |
| **Prahlada Charitamu** | **Chilakamarti Lakshmi Narasimham** | Mythological |
| Gayopakhyanam (Prachanda Yadavam) | Chilakamarti Lakshmi Narasimham | Mythological padya natakam |
| **Varavikrayam** | **Kallakuri Narayana Rao** | Against **dowry** |
| **Chintamani** | **Kallakuri Narayana Rao** | Social drama on a courtesan |
| Prataparudriyam (play) | **Vedam Venkataraya Sastri** | Historical play on Prataparudra |
- **Match (APPSC):** 1-d, 2-c, 3-b, 4-a = Veeresalingam, Gurajada, Boyi Bhimanna, Chilakamarti.
- **Padya natakam:** mythological or epic content, dialogue mainly in **sung verse (padyam)** – Keechaka Vadham, Gayopakhyanam.
- **Social drama:** contemporary evils in **spoken Telugu prose** – Kanyasulkam, Vyavahara Dharmabodhini, Varavikrayam.
> Trap: the Sanskrit **Prataparudriyam** on poetics is Vidyanatha's (Kakatiya age), not this play.

- **Trigger:** the **Marathi Darwada Nataka Samajam** toured **Rajamahendravaram (1880-81)**; it inspired Guntur's Hindu Nataka Samajam and Nellore's Lakshmi Vilasa Nataka Sabha.
- **Order of the Samajams:** Guntur (1880-81) → Kakinada (1885) → Rajahmundry (1889).
- **Surabhi:** a **Tholu Bommalata** (leather-puppet) family turned to **live actors** – still a family theatre; first play **Keechaka Vadham**; **not** founded at Bellary.
- **Rajahmundry Samajam** staged plays of **Chilakamarti Lakshmi Narasimham** and **Toleti Venkata Subbarao**.

=== Theatre beyond Andhra – Bengal
- **Binodini Dasi:** pioneering Bengali actress; a prime mover of the **Star Theatre, Calcutta (1883)**; serialised her autobiography ***Amar Katha*** (1910-13) – both statements true.
- Bengal's **Nil Darpan (1860)** shows how a play could rouse opinion (see Peasant Movements).
