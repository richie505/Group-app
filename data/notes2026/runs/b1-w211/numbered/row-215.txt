## SUB 0 | Tanguturi Prakasam – Andhra Kesari
[0:0] # Simon boycott, 1928
[0:1] - Simon Commission appointed **Nov 1927**, arrived **3 Feb 1928**.
[0:2] - In the **Simon Go Back** protest at **Madras (1928)** Prakasam **bared his chest before police guns** ("shoot me") → title **"Andhra Kesari"** – not Home Rule, Vande Mataram or Quit India.
[0:3] # Papers and career
[0:4] TABLE head: Year | Fact
  [0:4.0] 1872 | Born 23 Aug 1872, Vinodarayunipalem (NextGen prints 1892, Valluru – wrong)
  [0:4.1] 1903 | Chairman, Rajahmundry Municipality; acted main roles in the Hindu Nataka Samajam
  [0:4.2] 1921 | Gave up law practice for the NCM (not 1925); started the English daily *Swarajya* (26 Oct 1921); also weekly *Grama Swarajya*
  [0:4.3] 1937 | Revenue Minister in Rajaji's ministry – pro-peasant zamindari enquiry, opposed inside his party
  [0:4.4] 1946 | Premier of composite Madras; Firka Development Scheme
  [0:4.5] 1951 | Left Congress with N.G. Ranga → Kisan Mazdoor Praja Party
  [0:4.6] 1953 | First Chief Minister of Andhra State (Bezawada Gopal Reddy 2nd and last)
[0:5] - President of the Andhra Congress Committee **18 times**; **Prakasam district** is named after him.
[0:6] - Books: autobiography ***Naa Jeevita Yatra*** (4th part by **Tenneti Viswanatham**); ***Law Times***.
[0:7] - **Prakasam district irrigation:** **Gundlakamma Reservoir** (also Veligonda).

## SUB 2 | Pattabhi Sitaramayya
[2:0] - **Andhra Bank (1923, Machilipatnam)**; also Krishna District Co-operative Bank, **Bharata Lakshmi Bank**, Andhra Insurance – the patriot who was also a great **businessman**.
[2:1] - Books: ***History of the Indian National Congress* (1935)** – the **official history** of the Congress; ***Feathers and Stones*** (Ahmednagar Fort diary); *Gandhi and Gandhism*.
[2:2] - **Tripuri (1939):** Gandhi's candidate, **defeated by Subhas Chandra Bose** – Gandhi called it "my own defeat".
[2:3] - Presided the **Jaipur session (1948)**; member of the **JVP Committee** (Nehru, Patel, Pattabhi) – it first **rejected** linguistic states (report **April 1949**).
[2:4] - Wrote in *The Hindu* on the need for **linguistic states**; APCC President (1937).
[2:5] - **Governor of Madhya Pradesh (1952-57)**.
[2:6] - First Telugu INC president: exam keys say **Pattabhi**; strictly **P. Ananda Charlu** presided in 1891.
[2:7] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP History Part 7 p.9, Part 19 p.24; app notes 1:215 (sub 5)

## SUB 4 | Bulusu Sambamurthy and the Sribagh Pact
[4:0] - **Bulusu Sambamurthy ("Maharshi"):** backed the 1920 election boycott; attended the Vijayawada AICC (1921); **Reception Committee chairman, Kakinada session (1923)**; **toured all Andhra** for the Simon boycott (1928).
[4:1] - **Speaker of the Madras Assembly** in the **first Rajaji ministry (1937)**; declared Andhra state must be formed before the Constitution came into force.
[4:2] - **Potti Sriramulu's fast** began **19 Oct 1952** at **Sambamurthy's house in Mylapore, Madras**; Sriramulu died 15 Dec 1952.
[4:3] - **Sribagh Pact, 16 Nov 1937:** Rayalaseema–Circar agreement at **Kasinathuni Nageswara Rao's house "Sribagh"**, Madras; coastal signatories **Prakasam and Sambamurthy**.
[4:4] - Tirupati question (1953): the keyed answer credits **C. Rajagopalachari** with keeping Tirupati in Andhra against the Tamil claim (unverified).
[4:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP History Part 6 p.13, Part 7 p.17, p.23, Part 23 p.14; app notes 1:215 (sub 6)
