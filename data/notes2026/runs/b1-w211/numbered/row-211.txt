## SUB 1 | Gurajada Apparao
[1:0] - **Titles:** **"Abhyudaya Kavitha Pitamahudu"** (father of advanced / progressive poetry), **Kavisekhara**; NextGen: Navayuga Vaitalikudu, Bhava Kavita Vaitalikudu.
[1:1] - **Works:** ***Kanyasulkam*** (play on bride-price; written 1892, first staged at **Vizianagaram**), ***Mutyala Saralu*** (1910 poems in his own colloquial metre), *Puttadibomma Purnamma*, *Diddubatu*, *Desabhakti Geyam*; English poem ***Sarangadhara***.
[1:2] - Champion of **vyavaharika bhasha** (spoken Telugu) as a literary medium; *Mutyala Saralu* opened modern Telugu poetry.
[1:3] - Born at Rayavaram (Visakhapatnam); in the court of Ananda Gajapati of Vizianagaram.
[1:4] > "Desamante matti kadoi" is from *Mutyala Saralu* – not *Kanyasulkam*, *Manishi* or *Puttadi Bomma Purnamma*.
[1:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen Modern India L2 Part 3 p.9; NextGen AP History Part 7 p.26; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gurajada_Apparao; app notes 1:211 (sub 2)
## SUB 2 | Rayaprolu Subbarao and Bhava Kavitvam
[2:0] TABLE head: Angle | Fact
  [2:0.0] Titles | "Abhinava Nannaya" (Encyclopedia of Indian Literature); Father of Telugu Romanticism
  [2:0.1] Contribution | Brought Western (English lyric – Shelley and Keats) romanticism – Bhava Kavitvam – into Telugu; left translations of Sanskrit epics for original, emotive verse
  [2:0.2] Works | *Trunakankanamu* (first independent poem), *Andhravali*, *Swatantrya Rathamu*, *Janmabhoomi*, *Misra Manjari* (Sahitya Akademi 1965)
[2:1] - **Bhava Kavitvam:** romantic, personal emotion, nature, love; poets Basavaraju Apparao, Duvvuri Ramireddy (*Matru Desa Sankeertanam*), Devulapalli Krishna Sastri (Andhra Shelley), Venkata Parvateeswara Kavulu (*Ekanta Seva*).
[2:2] - Progressive writers such as **Sri Sri** called it **"escapist"**.
[2:3] > Abhinava Nannaya = **Rayaprolu** (not Viswanatha, Boyi Bhimanna or Sri Sri); Kavi Samrat = Viswanatha.
[2:4] ~~ Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rayaprolu_Subba_Rao; NextGen previous papers p.41; app notes 1:211 (sub 3)
## SUB 3 | Garimella Satyanarayana
[3:0] TABLE head: Angle | Fact
  [3:0.0] Song | "Maakoddee Tella Doratanamu" – Non-Cooperation era; copied a thousand times; praised by Gandhi
  [3:0.1] Life | 1893-1952, born at Gonedapadu (Srikakulam); left studies at Gandhi's call
  [3:0.2] Jail | Arrested 9 Feb 1922; one year for the song

[3:1] - *Ramayana Kalpavrikshamu* is **not** his – it is Viswanatha's.
[3:2] > "Makoddu ye tella doratanamu" = **Garimella** – not Gurajada, Unnava, Tripuraneni, Dasarathi or Sankarambadi.
