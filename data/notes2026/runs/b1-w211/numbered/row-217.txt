## SUB 2 | Prakasam Committee and the 1948 Abolition Act
[2:0] - **1937:** Rajaji's Madras ministry appointed a **zamindari committee** chaired by **Revenue Minister T. Prakasam**; report **7 Nov 1938**: **the land belongs to the tiller; the zamindar is only a tax collector**.
[2:1] TABLE head: Step | Date
  [2:1.0] Bill introduced by Revenue Minister Kala Venkata Rao | 11 Nov 1947
  [2:1.1] Passed – Madras Estates (Abolition and Conversion into Ryotwari) Act | 1948
  [2:1.2] Assent of Governor-General C. Rajagopalachari | 2 April 1949 (called the first law of independent India with the GG's assent)
  [2:1.3] In force | 7 Sept 1949
[2:2] - Abolished zamindari and inam estates; tenants became **direct ryots of the State**; zamindars compensated.
[2:3] - Shielded by the **1st (1951)** and **4th (1955)** Amendments; one of the **first abolition laws** of independent India.
[2:4] - **Madras Estates Land Act 1908:** **regulated** landholder–ryot relations, **did not abolish** estates; 3rd Amendment (1936) – occupancy rights for those in possession on **30 June 1934**.
