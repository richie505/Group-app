## SUB 1 | Gorky's Mother and Leftist Literature in Andhra
[1:0] - **Maxim Gorky's *Mother*** was translated into Telugu as ***Amma*** by **Krovvidi (K.) Lingaraju** in **1934** – the work most responsible for **sowing socialism among Andhra youth** (with Sri Sri's *Mahaprasthanam*); not M.S. Ram, Gopichand or C. Rajeswara Rao.
[1:1] - Background: failure of the CDM and Nehru's socialist writings turned Andhra youth to the left (Communist Party, Congress Socialist Party, M.N. Roy's Radical Humanists).
[1:2] - **Andhra Socialist Party:** June 1934, Vijayawada – N.G. Ranga president, Madduri Annapurnayya secretary; members included K. Lingaraju, P. Sundarayya, C. Rajeswara Rao.
[1:3] - Leftist journals: *Udayini* (Kompella Janardhana Rao), *Pratibha* (Siva Sankara Sastri); Rayalaseema – *Navya Sahityamala*.
[1:4] - Publishers: **Adarsha Grantha Mandali – Gadde Lingayya**; **Viswa Sahityamala – Mahidhara brothers**.
[1:5] - **Tenali** radicals (M.N. Roy): **T. Gopichand** – ***Asamarthuni Jeevitha Yatra*** (1947), first psychological novel in Telugu (not N.G. Ranga, Sri Sri or K. Lingaraju); *Pandita Parameswara Sastri Veelunama* – Sahitya Akademi; first Director of Information, Andhra State (1953).
[1:6] > Amma (1934) = Krovvidi Lingaraju; *Parivarthana*, *Mundadugu*, *Maa Bhoomi* are distractors.
[1:7] ~~ Sources: NextGen Modern India L3 Part 7 p.12; NextGen Modern India L2 Part 8 p.5; NextGen previous papers p.11, p.23, p.30; app notes 1:212 (sub 4, 6)
## SUB 2 | Arasam, AIPWA and Virasam
[2:0] TABLE head: Body | Year / place | Note
  [2:0.0] AIPWA (All India Progressive Writers' Association) | 1936, Lucknow | Founding session presided by Premchand; president in 2011: Namvar Singh (exam key)
  [2:0.1] Arasam (Abhyudaya Rachayitala Sangham) | 1943; first conference Tenali | Telugu counterpart of the AIPWA; first conference presided by Tapi Dharma Rao
  [2:0.2] Virasam (Viplava Rachayitala Sangham) | July 1970, Hyderabad | Revolutionary Writers' Association; founding President Sri Sri; Vice-President Kodavatiganti Kutumba Rao
[2:1] - Path: **nationalist poetry → Abhyudaya (progressive, Arasam) → Viplava (revolutionary, Virasam, after Srikakulam)**.
[2:2] - Virasam's spark: **Sri Sri's Shashtipurti, Vizag, Feb 1970**; its publications ***Le*** and ***March*** were banned.
[2:3] - Virasam is **not** the "Progressive Writers' Association" – that is Arasam.
[2:4] > Arasam 1943 = progressive; Virasam 1970 = revolutionary.
[2:5] ~~ Sources: app notes 1:212 (sub 0, 1)

## SUB 4 | Praja Natya Mandali and Progressive Theatre
[4:0] - **Praja Natya Mandali (1943):** communist cultural front, **Andhra wing of IPTA**; spread ideas through drama, songs and folk forms – main form **Burrakatha**; workshops by Garikapati Rajarao and Koduri Atchaiah; suspended by 1948.
[4:1] - Play ***Maa Bhoomi*** – **Sunkara Satyanarayana and Vasireddy Bhaskara Rao**.
[4:2] - **Jana Natya Mandali (1971):** grew from the Art Lovers (1970) of **Gaddar and B. Narsinga Rao** – Viplava phase.
[4:3] ~~ Sources: NextGen Modern India L2 Part 8 p.5; app notes 1:212 (sub 7)
