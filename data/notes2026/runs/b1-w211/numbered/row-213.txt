## SUB 0 | Viplava Parivartanamu and Plays Banned by the British
[0:0] # The banned NCM drama
[0:1] - **Viplava Parivartanamu:** published and **banned during the Non-Cooperation Movement**; it pushed **Swadeshi goods** and khaddar.
[0:2] - **Author: Kodali Anjaneyulu** (NextGen; APPSC 2018 and G2 2019) – not **Gollapudi Sitarama Sastri** (Swami Sitaram), the usual trap.
[0:3]   - Gollapudi Sitarama Sastri wrote the NCM song **"Kallu Monadoi"** and supported the **NCM boycott of elections (1920)**.
[0:4] - **Why troupes were watched:** a drama company could put a political idea before **thousands of illiterate people in one evening**.
[0:5] - Drama companies were the **main mass entertainment** of the time, so they carried **social-reform** themes first and **nationalist** ones later.
[0:6] - **Jan 2022:** the AP government banned staging of ***Chintamani*** (over the character Subbi Setty); the ban was challenged in the High Court.
[0:7] > Trap: Viplava Parivartanamu = Kodali Anjaneyulu, NCM; Gollapudi Sitarama Sastri = "Kallu Monadoi".
[0:8] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP History Part 7 p.2, Part 18 p.28, Part 19 p.4; app notes 1:213 (sub 4)
