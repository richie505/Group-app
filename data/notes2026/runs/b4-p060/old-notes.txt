## SUB 2 | Concepts and the National Trend, 1901-2011
[60:2:0] # Definitions
[60:2:1] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [60:2:1.0] Sex ratio (India) | Females per 1,000 males – the social indicator of equality between males and females
  [60:2:1.1] Sex ratio (UN / international) | Males per 100 females
  [60:2:1.2] Child sex ratio (CSR) | Girls per 1,000 boys aged 0-6
  [60:2:1.3] Sex ratio at birth (SRB) | Girls born per 1,000 boys born
  [60:2:1.4] Biological norm | About 105 boys per 100 girls = roughly 950 girls per 1,000 boys
  [60:2:1.5] Missing women | Shortfall of women, esp. in Asia; coined by Amartya Sen (1990)
[60:2:2] - Sex ratio covers the **whole population**, not only adults; first Census **1872**, then every **10 years**; density is **lower**, not higher, in mountains.

## SUB 3 | Causes, Laws and Schemes
[60:3:0] # Causes
[60:3:1] - Accepted causes: **high maternal mortality, high female child mortality, female foeticide, more male births**. **Male** child mortality is naturally **higher** – so "lower male than female mortality" is not a cause.
[60:3:2] - Roots: **son preference, patriarchy**, neglect of girls' health, intra-household discrimination, male migration.
[60:3:3] - MMR: **254 (2004-06) → 88 (2021-23)**.
[60:3:4] - After curbs on prenatal sex selection, firstborn girls show **25% higher child mortality** than firstborn boys.
[60:3:5] - **Infant Mortality Rate:** deaths before the **1st birthday** per 1,000 live births; **child (under-5) mortality** = deaths before the **5th birthday**.
[60:3:6] - British India: literacy **below 16%**, **female** (not male) literacy about **7%**, IMR about **218**, life expectancy about **32 years** (older NCERT, the APPSC answer; the revised NCERT text in OnlyIAS gives **44 years**).
[60:3:7] - Census in British India: first (non-synchronous) Census **1872**; first synchronous Census **1881**.
[60:3:8] # Laws and schemes
[60:3:9] TABLE head: Measure | Year | Aim
  [60:3:9.0] Cradle Baby Scheme (Tamil Nadu) | 1992 | Against female infanticide
  [60:3:9.1] PCPNDT Act | 1994 | Bans sex selection; prenatal tests only for genetic/chromosomal abnormalities
  [60:3:9.2] Beti Bachao Beti Padhao | 22 Jan 2015, Panipat (Haryana) | Reverse falling CSR; multi-sectoral action in gender-critical districts
[60:3:10] - BBBP does **not** allow prenatal sex determination.
[60:3:11] - **Supreme Court (Jun 2026):** CSR shows signs of improvement but gender bias is **deep-seated** (son preference, patriarchy).
[60:3:12] ~~ Sources: OnlyIAS NCERT Economy 04 p.42-43 (literacy below 16%, female 7%, IMR 218, life expectancy 44; census from 1881); app notes 4:60 (sub 6, 7)

=== NOTES 4:61 | India - Literacy: Levels, Gender and Regional Gaps, Trends
## SUB 0 | Education Policy and School Schemes
[61:0:0] # Policies
[61:0:1] - **NPE 1986** launched **Operation Blackboard (1987)** – at least **two rooms and two teachers**, teaching aids, for primary schools; also stressed ST education. **Ramamurti Committee (1990)** reviewed NPE; NPE **revised 1992** (Programme of Action) gave more weight to tribal education.
[61:0:2] - **NEP 2020:** replaced NPE 1986 after **34 years**; **5+3+3+4** – Foundational **3-8**, Preparatory 8-11, Middle 11-14, Secondary 14-18.
[61:0:3] - NEP targets: **100% school GER by 2030**; **50% higher-education GER by 2035**; **100% youth and adult literacy by 2030**.
[61:0:4] - **NIPUN Bharat:** foundational literacy by **Grade 3**; deadline moved to **2026-27**.
[61:0:5] # Schemes

  [61:0:6.0] Samagra Shiksha (2018) | Subsumes SSA, RMSA, Teacher Education
  [61:0:6.1] Unnat Bharat Abhiyan | Higher education – institutions adopt villages
  [61:0:6.2] SEMIS | Secondary Education MIS – nodal agency NUEPA, New Delhi
  [61:0:6.3] Mana Badi Nadu-Nedu (AP) | 14 Nov 2019; English labs (not Operation Blackboard)
[61:0:7] # Learning and enrolment data
[61:0:8] - **FLN Index** (Institute for Competitiveness for **EAC-PM**): 2nd edition – **Punjab** highest (64.19) among **small States**; Jharkhand was **not** the lowest small State in the web summaries. 2021 edition – West Bengal top among large States.
[61:0:9] - **AISHE 2023-24:** GER about **30**, female GER **31.2**, GPI **1.08**; female GER 28.5 → **30.2** (2021-23), above male; gender parity at all school levels.
[61:0:10] - **UDISE+ 2024-25** dropout: preparatory **2.3%**, middle **3.5%**, secondary **8.2%** (from 3.7, 5.2, **10.9**).
