=== NOTES 1:255 | Andhra Movement, Potti Sriramulu's Fast and the Formation of Andhra State, 1953
## SUB 0 | Causes of the Andhra Movement
[255:0:0] # Grievances in Madras Presidency
[255:0:1] - Telugus about **40%** (2 crore) of Madras Presidency (c. 1910), lumped as "Madrasis"; official language of the Presidency = **Tamil**.
[255:0:2] TABLE head: Post | Telugu | Non-Telugu
  [255:0:2.0] Deputy Collectors | 21 | 39
  [255:0:2.1] District Munsifs | 30 | 93
  [255:0:2.2] High State jobs | 8 | 56
  [255:0:2.3] District Judges | 0 | 19
[255:0:3] - **Education (1915)**: 8 of 31 colleges, 163 of 583 secondary schools; **no engineering, medical or law college** in Andhra.
[255:0:4] - **Revenue grievance**: surplus revenue of the Andhra **deltas** allegedly spent on Tamil areas.
[255:0:5] - Early demands: **province**, **university**, agricultural and medical colleges, **port at Visakhapatnam**.
[255:0:6] # Regional enthusiasm
[255:0:7] TABLE head: Region | Stand
  [255:0:7.0] Krishna, Guntur (Circars) | Keenest supporters
  [255:0:7.1] Rayalaseema, Nellore, Ganjam, Visakhapatnam | Lukewarm; saw a Circars/"Brahmin" movement
[255:0:8] # Bengal and Bihar precedent
[255:0:9] - **Partition of Bengal 1905**, annulled 1911; **Bihar** separation announced at the **Delhi Durbar (12 Dec 1911)**; **Bihar and Orissa** province from **1 April 1912** → provinces could be redrawn on language lines.

## SUB 8 | Commissions and Committees After 1947
[255:8:0] - **27 Nov 1947**: Nehru backed Andhra in the Assembly; dropped from the **Draft Constitution, 21 Feb 1948** (Tamil, Kannada objections); Centre feared for unity (Partition, Kashmir, princely states).
[255:8:1] TABLE head: Body | Appointed by / date | Members | Finding
  [255:8:1.0] Dhar (Dar) Commission (Linguistic Provinces Commission) | President of the Constituent Assembly, 17 June 1948 | Justice S.K. Dhar; Dr. Panna Lal, Jagat Narayan Lal | Report 10 Dec 1948: administrative convenience, not language; linguistic states a threat to national unity
  [255:8:1.1] JVP Committee | Congress, Jaipur session, Dec 1948 | Nehru, Patel, Pattabhi Sitaramayya | Report April 1949: postpone; Andhra if Madras claim given up; "if public opinion is insistent and overwhelming, we as democrats have to submit"
  [255:8:1.2] Partition Committee | Madras govt, Dec 1949 | Chair Kumaraswamy Raja; Andhra: Prakasam, Kala Venkatrao, B. Gopala Reddy, N. Sanjiva Reddy | Divide assets and liabilities; report 4 Jan 1950; state by 26 Jan 1950 – failed over Madras

[255:8:2] - Nehru promised a committee on his **1948 Visakhapatnam** visit.
[255:8:3] - **CWC**: Andhra without Madras city.

## SUB 9 | Fasts for Andhra State
[255:9:0] # Swami Sitaram, 1951
[255:9:1] - **Gollapudi Sitarama Sastri**, a Gandhian; fast at Guntur, **15 Aug-20 Sep 1951, 35 days**.
[255:9:2] - Demands: Andhra State, Madras city's status, interim arrangement (not Telangana merger).
[255:9:3] - **Ended on the appeal of Vinoba Bhave**; no gain.
[255:9:4] - April 1951 APCC poll: **Sanjiva Reddy beat N.G. Ranga by 5 votes** → Prakasam, Ranga formed the **Kisan Mazdoor Praja Party**.
[255:9:5] # Potti Sriramulu – life
[255:9:6] - Born **16 March 1901, Madras**; Arya Vysya; ancestral village **Gajula Dinne, Nellore**.
[255:9:7] - About **3 years at Sabarmati Ashram**; Salt Satyagraha, Quit India; khadi, prohibition, **Harijan uplift**.
[255:9:8] TABLE head: Earlier fast | Outcome
  [255:9:8.0] 1946, Nellore Venugopala Swamy temple | Harijan entry
  [255:9:8.1] 1948, Wardha, 29 days | Harijan Day, ₹1 crore; Madras Harijan Welfare Day on the 30th of every month
  [255:9:8.2] 1949, 28 days | Gandhi Smarak Din
[255:9:9] - Fasted three times (1946-48) in Nellore for Harijan temple entry; **1952 = only fast for statehood**.
[255:9:10] # The death fast, 1952

## SUB 10 | Andhra State, 1 October 1953
[255:10:0] # Act and formation
[255:10:1] - Nehru, **25 Mar 1953**: "no question" of Madras as capital. Bill introduced **10 Aug 1953**; assent **14 Sept 1953**.
[255:10:2] - **1 October 1953** – **first linguistic state**, carved from Madras State; capital **Kurnool**, **inaugurated by Nehru**.
[255:10:3] - **11 districts**: Coastal (7) Srikakulam, Visakhapatnam, East and West Godavari, Krishna, Guntur, Nellore; Rayalaseema (4) Chittoor, Kadapa, Anantapur, Kurnool. (No Vizianagaram, Prakasam districts.)
[255:10:4] - **Bellary** (Justice L.S. Misra): Bellary taluk → Mysore; **Rayadurg → Anantapur**; **Adoni, Alur → Kurnool**.
