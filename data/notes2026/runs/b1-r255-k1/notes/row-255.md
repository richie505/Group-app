=== The First Andhra Conference (Andhra Mahasabha), Bapatla 1913
# Before Bapatla
| Meeting | Place, year | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| Youngmen's Literary Association | **Guntur, 1903** | **Cradle of the Andhra movement** |
| Joint conference of Godavari, Krishna, Guntur districts | **Nidadavolu, May 1912** | Andhra province issue first discussed in the Subjects Committee (21st Krishna-Guntur conference); **first formal separate-province resolution, moved by Jonnavittula Gurunatham** (ruled out of order); committee under **Konda Venkatappayya** (secretary) to organise an all-Andhra conference |
| Andhra Mahasabha Committee | Guntur, 1912-13 | President **Vinjamuri Bhavanacharyulu** |
| Rayalaseema meeting | **Mahanandi, April 1913** | **Guthi Kesava Pillai** – separation impractical, a "**Brahmin movement**" |
| Vizag district meeting | **June 1913** | **Manda Suryanarayana** (municipal chairman of **Vizianagaram**); started the weekly **Andhra Advocate** |
- **First to oppose:** in 1912 **Nyapati Subba Rao** opposed a separate province (too small, would get only a Chief Commissioner); Pattabhi Sitaramayya favoured even a small province.
- **Growth of opinion 1907-1913:** from Vandemataram (1907) to the first Andhra Conference (1913) – from problems of the people to a province for the people, from district conferences to an Andhra conference.
# Bapatla, 26-27 May 1913 – the facts
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Date, place | **26-27 May 1913, Bapatla** (Guntur district) – not Kakinada, Guntur, Nidadavolu, Madras, Gudivada or Tadepalligudem |
| President | **B.N. Sarma (Bayya / Baiya Narasimheswara Sarma)**, member of the **Madras Legislative Council** – not Nyapati Subba Rao, Pattabhi or Konda Venkatappayya |
| Secretary | **Konda Venkatappayya** |
| Attendance | About 800 delegates, 2,000 visitors; delegates also from Hyderabad, Warangal, Nagpur |
| Province resolution | Moved by **Vemavarapu Ramadasu Pantulu**; opposed by **Nyapati Subba Rao**, M. Adinarayanaiah, **Mocherla Ramachandra Rao** → amended to "take it up at the next conference" (deferred); a committee under **Pattabhi** to collect opinion |
| University | A **separate university for Andhras**, with Telugu as medium, **first demanded** here |
| Other agenda | An English daily; **lifting the ban on Telugus in the Army**; fund for technical training abroad |
- **B.N. Sarma's caution:** the demand was premature and "might do **more harm than good**"; "**First we are Indians, then we are Andhras**" – so he presided but **opposed** a separate state (APPSC G2 2017).
- **Result:** the awakening got a **respectable forum**; Krishna and Guntur delegates were disappointed; Rayalaseema, Ganjam and Visakhapatnam were lukewarm.
- After Bapatla, the **Nellore** district meeting called the demand "**not yet ripe**".
- **Aims of the Andhra movement** (propaganda phase 1913-20): Andhra province, Andhra University, development of Telugu, cultural revival – love of education, culture and high ideals, interest in sanitation, health and physical culture.
> Trap: President **B.N. Sarma**, secretary **Konda Venkatappayya**, mover **Ramadasu Pantulu**, chief opponent **Nyapati Subba Rao**; the resolution was **passed only at Vijayawada 1914**.

=== Causes, Press and Early Writings
# Grievances in the Madras Presidency
- Telugus about **40%** of the Presidency's people (58% of its area), lumped as "Madrasis"; dominant language **Tamil**; Tamils taunted them for lacking figures like Bhasyam Aiyengar.
| Post (c. 1911) | Telugu | Non-Telugu |
|---|---|---|
| High posts (64) | 8 | 56 |
| Deputy Collectors | 21 | 39 |
| District Munsifs | 30 | 93 |
| District Judges | **0** | 19 |
- **Main cause:** **unequal opportunities in education, employment and administration**; higher, technical and professional education out of reach (1915: 8 of 31 colleges, **no engineering, medical or law college**); Anglo-Indian officers knowing little Telugu.
- Revenue grievance: surplus of the Andhra **deltas** spent on Tamil areas; early demands – province, university, **port at Visakhapatnam**.
- **Keenest:** **Krishna and Guntur**; lukewarm: Rayalaseema, Nellore, Ganjam, Visakhapatnam.
- **Precedent:** Bengal partition 1905, annulled 1911; **Bihar** separation announced at the **Delhi Durbar, 12 Dec 1911** → Bihar and Orissa province 1 April 1912 → provinces could be redrawn on linguistic lines.
- **Tamil press:** opposed and **raised doubts about the Andhras' ability** to run a province; an Andhra Patrika writer replied that separation would **not harm national unity**.

=== Sri Bagh Pact, 1937
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Date | **16 November 1937** |
| Place | **'Sri Bagh', Madras** – residence of **Kasinathuni Nageswara Rao** (Andhra Patrika) |
| Between | **Coastal Andhra (Circars) and Rayalaseema** leaders – not Telangana |
| Why | Rayalaseema fears of Circars domination (Rajaji's 1937 cabinet left Rayalaseema out; 1935 Kadapa opposition) |
| Coastal signatories | **Prakasam, Konda Venkatappayya**, Bulusu Sambamurthy, Bezawada Gopala Reddy, K. Nageswara Rao, Pattabhi |
| Rayalaseema signatories | **K. Koti Reddy, Kalluri Subba Rao**, H.M. Sitaram Reddy, Gadicherla |
# Clauses
| Clause | Provision |
|---|---|
| University | **Two** centres of Andhra University – **Waltair and Anantapur** |
| Irrigation | Priority to **Rayalaseema and Nellore** in **Tungabhadra, Krishna, Pennar** waters – exclusive attention to major projects for **10 years** |
| Legislature | General seats shared on an **equal district-wise** basis |
| Capital / High Court | In different regions – **Rayalaseema chooses first** |
- **Implemented 1953:** capital **Kurnool**, High Court **Guntur**; it served as a model for the **Gentlemen's Agreement (1956, 60:40 cabinet)**; still cited in Rayalaseema capital, High Court and irrigation debates.
> Trap: 60:40 and Telangana belong to the **1956** agreement, not Sri Bagh.

=== The Fasts – Swami Sitaram and Potti Sriramulu
# Swami Sitaram, 1951
- **Gollapudi Sitarama Sastri**; fast at **Guntur, 15 Aug - 20 Sept 1951, 35 days**; demands – Andhra by **2 Oct 1951**, Madras city's status settled in three years (no Telangana merger demand).
- Ended on **Vinoba Bhave's** appeal, no gain. April 1951 APCC poll: **Sanjiva Reddy beat N.G. Ranga by 5 votes** → Prakasam and Ranga formed the **Kisan Mazdoor Praja Party**.
# Potti Sriramulu
- Born **16 March 1901, Madras**; **Arya Vysya**; ancestral village **Gajula Dinne, Nellore**; about **3 years at Sabarmati**; khadi, prohibition, **Harijan uplift**.

|---|---|
