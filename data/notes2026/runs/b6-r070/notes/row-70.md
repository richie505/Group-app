=== ISRO Missions, Launches and Engines
# EOS-05 (GISAT-1A) on GSLV-F17
- Launched **4 September 2026** by **GSLV-F17** from the **Second Launch Pad, SDSC-SHAR, Sriharikota**.
- **India's first imaging satellite from geosynchronous orbit**; images the subcontinent **every 30 minutes** (priority areas ~5 minutes) from ~36,000 km.
- Mass ~**2,367 kg**; placed in **Sub-GTO** (perigee 170 km, apogee 28,934 km, inclination 19.28°, azimuth 104°) – **not directly in GEO**.
- Uses: agriculture, land resources, environmental and weather monitoring, disaster management.
- Unlike LEO / Sun-synchronous EO satellites, it watches the **same large area with very high temporal frequency** (not once in 24 hours).
| GSLV-F17 item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Flight number | 19th GSLV flight |
| Height / lift-off mass | 51.7 m / 420.5 t |
| Stages | 3; 4 m ogive fairing |
| GS1 | S139 solid core + 4 L40H liquid strap-ons |
| GS2 | GL40HT liquid stage |
| GS3 | CUS15 cryogenic (LH2 + LOX) |
- **GSLV Mk II** capacity: ~**2,250 kg to GTO, 6,000 kg to LEO**; first flight **18 April 2001**; indigenous cryogenic stage from **GSLV-D5 (January 2014)**.
- **SDSC-SHAR**: Sriharikota, **Andhra Pradesh** – ISRO's principal launch centre; former name Sriharikota Range; named after **Prof. Satish Dhawan**.
# Orbits – Comparison
| Orbit | Key facts |
|---|---|
| LEO | 160–2,000 km; one orbit 90–120 min; ~15,562 active payloads (4 Sep 2026); India has 22 operational LEO satellites (Apr 2026 ISRO report) |
| MEO | Galileo (Europe): 30 satellites |
| Geosynchronous | Period ~23 h 56 min; can be oval (elliptical) or tilted (inclined) |
| Geostationary | Circular, equatorial, zero inclination, ~35,786 km; period ~24 h; appears stationary – communications, weather, continuous regional monitoring; India has 31 GEO satellites |
- Every geostationary orbit is geosynchronous, but a geosynchronous orbit need not be geostationary.
# Semi-Cryogenic Power Head Test Article (PHTA)
- Hot test at **100% rated level = 200 tonnes thrust** (47% level = 94 t) at **ISRO Propulsion Research Complex (IPRC), Mahendragiri, Tamil Nadu**.
- **9th hot test** in the series; **first at full rated level**; total **35 seconds**, but **only 5 seconds at full level**.
- Validated switch from low-pressure start tank to medium-pressure run tank.
- PHTA includes **all engine systems except the thrust chamber**.
- **SE2000** – 2,000 kN-class engine powering the **SC120** semi-cryogenic stage; SC120 to replace the **L110 core stage of LVM3**; with uprated **C32** cryogenic upper stage it raises LVM3 payload.
| Type | Fuel | Engines/stages |
|---|---|---|
| Semi-cryogenic | Refined hydrocarbon + LOX | SE2000 / SC120 |
| Cryogenic | LH2 + LOX | CUS15, C32 |
# Oceansat-3 (EOS-06) Tracks El Niño
- **EOS-06 / Oceansat-3** saw **weaker easterly winds (westerly reversals)** and **lower chlorophyll-a** in **June 2026** – a developing **El Niño** in the tropical Pacific.
- Analysis by **NRSC (ISRO)** of April–June data, 2023–2026; combined **OCM-3 chlorophyll + scatterometer winds**; productive zone **contracted westward**.
- Launched **26 November 2022**, **PSLV-C54**, Sriharikota; polar Sun-synchronous orbit; 3rd-generation Oceansat.
- Payloads: **Ocean Colour Monitor-3, Ku-band Scatterometer (SCAT-3), Sea Surface Temperature Monitor, ARGOS**.
- El Niño weakens upwelling → lower phytoplankton.

# Nuclear Power in Space
| System | How it works | Examples / use |
|---|---|---|
| RTG (Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generator) | Decay heat of plutonium-238 → electricity; no moving parts | Voyager 1 & 2, Curiosity, Perseverance |
| RHU (Radioisotope Heater Unit) | Heat only – no electricity | Keeps instruments from freezing |
| Fission reactor | Controlled chain reaction → much higher power | Lunar/Martian habitats |
| NTP (Nuclear Thermal Propulsion) | Reactor heats liquid hydrogen | ~twice efficiency of chemical rockets |
| NEP (Nuclear Electric Propulsion) | Electricity accelerates ionised propellant | Low thrust, very high efficiency |
