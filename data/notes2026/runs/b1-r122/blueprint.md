## 3. Repeats and trends
- **Most repeated:** Abdur Razzaq – Persian envoy – Devaraya II (9), Lepakshi paintings (9), 300 ports (6), Vipravinodins (6), Conti the Italian (6), Virupaksha at Hampi (5), ruins at Hampi (4).
- **Sati** is asked in three PYQs with three different printed keys (Conti, Abdur Razzaq, Nuniz); the APPSC G2 2017 / NextGen answer is **Nuniz** – keyed Nuniz in all three with a ⚠ Disputed line, since Conti and Barbosa also describe sati.
- **"Temple built by Krishnadevaraya"**: NextGen and Rocket give Hazara Rama (and the Vithala); MPPSC 2014 and APPSC both expect Krishnadevaraya – kept consistent with b1-r119 (Hazara Rama = Krishnadevaraya, disputed).
- APPSC asks mostly Andhra angles of the art (Lepakshi, Tadipatri) and hardly any Hampi structural detail beyond Virupaksha / Vittala / Hazara Rama; UPSC asks Hampi musical pillars, Virupaksha, kalyana mandapas and Nuniz on women.
- Folk culture (row 124) has only 12 real PYQs: Tolu Bommalata as rural entertainment (×2), Harikatha Pitamaha (×2), Burrakatha – Nazar / Jangam Katha / AP (×4), Kuchipudi (×4).
