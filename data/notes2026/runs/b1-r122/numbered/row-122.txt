## SUB 2 | Society – Castes and Social Groups
[2:0] - **Vipravinodins (Vipravinodulu):** **Brahmin jugglers** who gave **indoor entertainment to Brahmins only** (APPSC G2 2017, 2019; ×6) – a sect of **Brahmins**, not Shudras, weavers, Kshatriyas or Vaisyas.
[2:1] - **Balanjalu (Balijas, Veerabalijas):** **traders / business** – like the Ayyavalu; not weavers, herders or barbers.
[2:2] - **Four varnas** and about **18 castes**.

## SUB 3 | Women, Marriage and Social Customs
[3:0] - **Sati, polygamy and dowry** were prevalent and **child marriages were common** (NextGen) – "child marriages were unknown" is the wrong statement.
[3:1] - **Most popular form of marriage:** **Brahma vivaha** (the father gives his daughter to a suitable groom) – not Daiva, Gandharva or Prajapatya.
[3:2] - **No strict purdah**; women served as bodyguards, wrestlers, accountants, dancers; learned women **Gangadevi** and **Tirumalamba**.
[3:3] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [3:3.0] Kanyashulkam | Bride price
  [3:3.1] Varashulkam | Dowry
  [3:3.2] Benabaga | Slavery
  [3:3.3] Devadasis | Temple dancers trained in music and dance; often land-owning and respected
[3:4] - Prostitution was state-recognised and **taxed** (Abdur Razzaq).

## SUB 4 | Religion and Festivals
[4:0] - **Tutelary deity:** **Virupaksha** (a form of Shiva, Pampapati of Hampi) – the kings ruled on his behalf; family deity of the Sangamas.
[4:1] - **Mahanavami (Dasara):** the festival celebrated on the grandest scale every year – on the **Mahanavami Dibba** built by Krishnadevaraya; nayakas brought tribute; the financial year began with it; described by **Paes and Abdur Razzaq**; Burton Stein compared it to an **Ashvamedha**.
[4:2] - Vasantotsava (spring festival) – in Krishnadevaraya's **Jambavati Parinayam**.
[4:3] TABLE head: Ruler / dynasty | Faith / act
  [4:3.0] Sangamas | Shaiva; guru Kalamukha Kriya Shakti
  [4:3.1] Saluva, Tuluva, Aravidu | Vaishnava; Tirupati rose under Saluva Narasimha
  [4:3.2] Devaraya II | Enlisted Muslims in the army; built a mosque
  [4:3.3] Venkatapati Raya II (Aravidu) | Venkateswara replaced Virupaksha as main deity; allowed a church at Chandragiri (Jesuits)
[4:4] - Centres: **Srisailam, Tripurantakam, Srikalahasti** (Shaiva); Tirupati, Mangalagiri (Vaishnava); **Ahobilam, Kadiri** (Narasimha); Narahari Tirtha spread Dvaita from **Srikurmam**.
[4:5] - **Penugonda (West Godavari)** = seat of **Vasavi Kanyaka Parameswari** (Arya Vysyas) – not Penukonda (Anantapur).

## SUB 5 | Agriculture and Irrigation – Porumamilla and the Tanks
[5:0] - **Porumamilla inscription (Kadapa):** lays down the **12 rules (Dwadasa Sutras)** for building a **tank (tataka)** (APPSC G2 2012, 2019; ×3) – issued by Prince **Bhaskara (Bhaskara Bhavadura / Bhaskara Mantri)**, son of Bukka I, for the **Anantasagaram** tank (**1369**) – not Tummalagudem, Korlagallu, Bitragunta, Palampet, Pakhala or Bodhan.
[5:1] - **Narasambudhi tatakam** = **Vijayanagara** period (Saluva Narasimha, Anantapur) – not Kakatiya, Yadava, Hoysala, Velama or Reddi.
[5:2] - Kings promoted agriculture by building **tanks (ponds), dams and wells** – tank-building was one of the **Saptasantanas** (seven meritorious acts).
[5:3] TABLE head: Ruler | Work
  [5:3.0] Bukka I | Siruveru tank near Penukonda
  [5:3.1] Saluva Narasimha | Narasambudhi tank
  [5:3.2] Krishnadevaraya | Nagalapuram tank (Portuguese engineers), Toragallu dam, Kambham tank
[5:4] - Temple economy: **Devadaya** = temple villages / endowments; **Sri Bhandara** = temple treasury kept by the Karanam; **Samayas** (merchant guilds) met in **temple mandapas**; main state revenue = **land tax**.
