## SUB 0 | Folk Entertainment under Vijayanagara – Yakshaganam and Tolu Bommalata
[0:0] TABLE head: Form | Audience under Vijayanagara
  [0:0.0] Yakshaganam | Originated in the royal courts; entertained the court and elites
  [0:0.1] Tolu Bommalata (leather shadow puppets) | The chief entertainment of rural Andhra besides Yakshagana (APPSC G2 2019 ×2) – not Harikatha, dramas or Burrakatha
  [0:0.2] Vipra-Vinodins | Brahmin jugglers for Brahmin households
[0:1] - **Yakshaganam** was performed by the **Jakkula (Jakkula Varu)** community; **Jakkula Purandhri** is a character of the **Yakshaganam** (APPSC G1 2004) – not Perini, Oggu Katha or puppetry.
[0:2] - **Koravanji** = precursor of the Telugu Yakshagana; **Sugreeva Vijayam** by **Kandukuri Rudrakavi** = first (published) Telugu Yakshaganam; performed in Telugu with rich costumes and Puranic stories.
[0:3] - **Veedhi Natakam / Veedhi Bhagavatam** (street dance-drama) → forerunner of **Kuchipudi**.
[0:4] - Under the **Qutb Shahis** Yakshaganam became folk theatre and Kuchipudi flourished; folk forms later carried Telugu consciousness to the villages in the **Andhra Movement**.

## SUB 1 | Burrakatha
[1:0] - **Earlier name: Jangam Katha** (APPSC JL; Singhania) – not Kuchipudi, Bhamakalapam or Veeranatyam; in Rayalaseema called **Thandana Katha**.
[1:1] - Folk narrative art of **Andhra Pradesh** (and Telangana); birthplace **Guntur**.
[1:2] - Troupe of **three**: the main narrator (with the **burra / tambura**) and **two co-performers (Vantalu)**; instruments Gummata, Burra, Tambura, Ukki; chorus "Thandana Taana".
[1:3] - **Made popular by Shaik Nazar** (APPSC ×2) = **Burrakatha Pitamaha** – born 5 Feb 1920, **Pannekallu, Guntur**; **Padma Shri 1986**; works Palnati Yuddham, **Bengal Karuvu** – not Mikkilineni or Mastan Saheb.
[1:4] - Popularisers: Nazar, **Nittala Brothers**; used for news, reform and nationalist messages; **Praja Natya Mandali** used it for socialist ideals (**Mabhoomi** – Sunkara Satyanarayana and Vasireddy Bhaskara Rao); Sunkara's Burrakathas Kashtajeevi, Raithu Vijayam, Alluri Sitaramaraju.
[1:5] ~~ Sources: Singhania Art & Culture 03 p.58-59 ("Burrakatha or Jangam Katha ... from Andhra Pradesh"); PMF Art & Culture 02 p.102 (one leading performer and two co-performers); app notes 1:124 (sub 3, 7)
## SUB 2 | Harikatha
[2:0] - **Father of Harikatha (Harikatha Pitamaha):** **Adibhatla Narayana Dasu** (1864-1945) (APPSC G2 2012 ×2) – not Kanakaiah Naidu, Shankaramanchi Ramalingaiah or Ballari Raghava.
[2:1] - Born at **Ajjada**, Vizianagaram district; autobiography **Na Yeruka**; Harikatha is deeply rooted in **Vizianagaram**.
[2:2] - A **single artist** sings, acts and dances (about 3 hours) with cymbals (**Chidatalu**) – Burrakatha is a trio.
[2:3] ~~ Sources: PMF Art & Culture 02 p.102 (Harikatha – story of Hari); app notes 1:124 (sub 2)
## SUB 3 | Kuchipudi – Andhra's Classical Dance
[3:0] - **Kuchipudi** = the classical dance of **Andhra Pradesh** (APPSC ×3) – Kathakali and **Mohiniattam** are Kerala's, Kathak North India's, Bharatanatyam Tamil Nadu's, Odissi Odisha's.
[3:1] - Named after **Kuchipudi village, Krishna district**; founded by **Siddhendra Yogi**; grew from the **Bhagavata Mela** of hereditary Brahmin families; village gifted by **Abul Hasan Tana Shah**.
[3:2] - **Statements (APPSC FRO):** Kuchipudi from Krishna district ✓; Warangal Fort – **Kakatiyas** ✓; Telugu – classical Dravidian language ✓; "**Amaravati stupa – a centre of Jainism**" ✗ (it was **Buddhist**) → **a, c and d only**.
[3:3] ~~ Sources: PMF Art & Culture 02 p.73; NextGen AP History (Amaravati – Buddhist stupa); app notes 1:124 (sub 6)
## SUB 4 | Tolu Bommalata – the Craft, Nimmalakunta and Surabhi
[4:0] - Thin, **translucent leather** figures **painted on both sides**, moved behind a **white screen** lit by a lamp; **heads of the puppets move** (unique feature); Ramayana, Mahabharata, Puranas.
[4:1] - **Nimmalakunta** (Sri Sathya Sai / Anantapur) – leather puppetry **GI tag 2008**; **Dalavai Chalapathi Rao** – Padma Shri 2020.
[4:2] - Mentioned in **Panditaradhya Charitra** (Palkuriki Somanatha), **Palnati Veeracharitra** (Srinatha), Bhaskara Satakam.
[4:3] - **Surabhi theatre:** **Vanarasa Govinda Rao**, from a **leather-puppet family**, replaced puppets with live actors (**Keechaka Vadham**) at **Sorugu** near Jammalamadugu (Kadapa).
[4:4] ~~ Sources: Singhania Art & Culture 03 p.100; app notes 1:124 (sub 4)
