- **Persian envoy to Devaraya II** = **Abdur Razzaq** – not Ferishta (historian), Amir Khusrau or Siraj.
- **Under Krishnadevaraya:** **Barbosa and Domingo Paes** (APPSC G2 1989) – not Conti, Razzaq or Bernier (Mughal India).
- **Nuniz:** Portuguese (APPSC; NextGen PYQ book wrongly prints "Italy") – reign of **Achyuta Deva Raya**, not Krishnadevaraya, Sadasiva or Venkatapati II.
- **Wrong pairs asked:** "Nikitin – Achyutaraya / came as a pilgrim under Devaraya" ✗ (APPSC G1 2023); "Caesar Frederick – Venkatapati Raya II" ✗ (he came in 1567); "Hiuen Tsang wrote on Vijayanagara" ✗ (he came in Harsha's time, 7th c.).

=== Society – Castes and Social Groups
- **Vipravinodins (Vipravinodulu):** **Brahmin jugglers** who gave **indoor entertainment to Brahmins only** (APPSC G2 2017, 2019; ×6) – a sect of **Brahmins**, not Shudras, weavers, Kshatriyas or Vaisyas.
- **Balanjalu (Balijas, Veerabalijas):** **traders / business** – like the Ayyavalu; not weavers, herders or barbers.
- **Four varnas** and about **18 castes**.
| Group | Occupation |
|---|---|
| Vaidikas / Niyogis | Brahmin priests / **Brahmin officials** |
| **Settis, Komatis, Balijas** | Traders |
| Sudras | Farmers – backbone of the kingdom |
| **Vira Panchalas** | **Artisans** (gold, iron, brass, wood, stone) |
| **Kaikkolars**, Padmasalis, Jandra, Kuruba | **Weavers** |
- **Valangai (right-hand)** = mainly agricultural and trading groups; **Idangai (left-hand)** = mainly **artisans** and some traders; the division began in **Chola** times.
~~ Sources: NextGen previous papers p.10, p.15; NextGen Vijayanagara Kingdom p.30 (weavers); app notes 1:122 (sub 1, 2)

=== Women, Marriage and Social Customs
- **Sati, polygamy and dowry** were prevalent and **child marriages were common** (NextGen) – "child marriages were unknown" is the wrong statement.
- **Most popular form of marriage:** **Brahma vivaha** (the father gives his daughter to a suitable groom) – not Daiva, Gandharva or Prajapatya.
- **No strict purdah**; women served as bodyguards, wrestlers, accountants, dancers; learned women **Gangadevi** and **Tirumalamba**.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Kanyashulkam | Bride price |
| **Varashulkam** | **Dowry** |
| **Benabaga** | **Slavery** |
| Devadasis | Temple dancers trained in music and dance; often land-owning and respected |
- Prostitution was state-recognised and **taxed** (Abdur Razzaq).
~~ Sources: NextGen Vijayanagara Kingdom p.6 ("Sati, child marriages, and dowry systems were prevalent"); app notes 1:122 (sub 3)

=== Religion and Festivals
- **Tutelary deity:** **Virupaksha** (a form of Shiva, Pampapati of Hampi) – the kings ruled on his behalf; family deity of the Sangamas.
- **Mahanavami (Dasara):** the festival celebrated on the grandest scale every year – on the **Mahanavami Dibba** built by Krishnadevaraya; nayakas brought tribute; the financial year began with it; described by **Paes and Abdur Razzaq**; Burton Stein compared it to an **Ashvamedha**.
- Vasantotsava (spring festival) – in Krishnadevaraya's **Jambavati Parinayam**.
| Ruler / dynasty | Faith / act |
|---|---|
| Sangamas | Shaiva; guru **Kalamukha Kriya Shakti** |
| Saluva, Tuluva, Aravidu | Vaishnava; **Tirupati** rose under **Saluva Narasimha** |
| **Devaraya II** | Enlisted Muslims in the army; built a mosque |
| **Venkatapati Raya II** (Aravidu) | **Venkateswara** replaced Virupaksha as main deity; allowed a **church at Chandragiri** (Jesuits) |
- Centres: **Srisailam, Tripurantakam, Srikalahasti** (Shaiva); Tirupati, Mangalagiri (Vaishnava); **Ahobilam, Kadiri** (Narasimha); Narahari Tirtha spread Dvaita from **Srikurmam**.
- **Penugonda (West Godavari)** = seat of **Vasavi Kanyaka Parameswari** (Arya Vysyas) – not Penukonda (Anantapur).
~~ Sources: NextGen Vijayanagara Kingdom p.31; NextGen previous papers p.13 (Vasavi at Penugonda); app notes 1:122 (sub 4, 5)

=== Agriculture and Irrigation – Porumamilla and the Tanks
- **Porumamilla inscription (Kadapa):** lays down the **12 rules (Dwadasa Sutras)** for building a **tank (tataka)** (APPSC G2 2012, 2019; ×3) – issued by Prince **Bhaskara (Bhaskara Bhavadura / Bhaskara Mantri)**, son of Bukka I, for the **Anantasagaram** tank (**1369**) – not Tummalagudem, Korlagallu, Bitragunta, Palampet, Pakhala or Bodhan.
- **Narasambudhi tatakam** = **Vijayanagara** period (Saluva Narasimha, Anantapur) – not Kakatiya, Yadava, Hoysala, Velama or Reddi.
- Kings promoted agriculture by building **tanks (ponds), dams and wells** – tank-building was one of the **Saptasantanas** (seven meritorious acts).
| Ruler | Work |
|---|---|
| Bukka I | **Siruveru** tank near Penukonda |
| Saluva Narasimha | Narasambudhi tank |
| Krishnadevaraya | **Nagalapuram** tank (Portuguese engineers), Toragallu dam, Kambham tank |
- Temple economy: **Devadaya** = temple villages / endowments; **Sri Bhandara** = temple treasury kept by the Karanam; **Samayas** (merchant guilds) met in **temple mandapas**; main state revenue = **land tax**.
~~ Sources: NextGen previous papers p.15 ("Porumamilla inscription describes the 12 rules ... tank construction"); Rocket AP History (inscriptions table: Porumamilla – Prince Bhaskara Bhavadura); NextGen Vijayanagara Kingdom p.30; app notes 1:122 (sub 7)

=== Trade, Industries and Ports
- **Chief import = horses** (from Arabia, Persia, Central Asia) (APPSC; WBCS 2020) – not pearls, precious stones, silk, pottery, wine or gold; **chief export = Kalamkari** cloth.
- **Kudirai Chettis** = local **horse merchants** of the **Vijayanagara** empire (NCERT Themes II) – not Chola, Delhi Sultanate or Mughal.
- **Vajrakarur** (Anantapur) = **diamonds** – diamonds mined in Kurnool, Gutti, Anantapur.

|---|---|
| **Tadipatri, Adoni, Gutti** | **Textiles**, Kalamkari |
| **Gutti, Rayadurg** | Silk |
| Venkatagiri | Zari |
| **Pulicat, Mylapore, Motupalli**, Machilipatnam | East-coast ports |
| Honnavar, **Bhatkal**, Goa, Mangalore, Calicut | West-coast ports |
| Motupalli | Taken by **Devaraya I**; Motupalli Dharma Sasanam (1416) |
