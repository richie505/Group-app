=== Hampi – the Capital, its Ruins and its Plan
- **Ruins of Vijayanagara = Hampi** (SI 2011; UPPCS 2008; ×5) – not Bijapur, Golconda, Ahmadnagar or Baroda (sultanate / other capitals).
- On the **southern bank of the Tungabhadra** (Jharkhand PCS 2023) – not Narmada, Godavari or Bhima; in **Vijayanagara district, Karnataka** (SSC 2019).
- "**Group of Monuments at Hampi**" = UNESCO World Heritage Site, **1986**; the **Vittala stone chariot** is on the reverse of the **₹50 note**.
- **"Athens of Vijayanagara"** = the **valley area around the Virupaksha temple** (app notes; APPSC) – not Nellore-Chittoor, Bangalore-Kolar-Chittoor or the Pandya country.
- **Fields inside the fortifications:** so the city would have **food supply even if invaders blockaded it for months** (NCERT; Paes) – not irrigation, revenue accounts or royal granaries.
- **Why so many temples:** to **legitimise royal authority** by association with the deities – the kings ruled on behalf of **Virupaksha** (NCERT).
- **Raw material:** the **Deccan plateau** gave easy local access to **stone (granite)** – that made temples like Lepakshi possible.

=== Hampi Temples – Virupaksha, Vitthala, Hazara Rama
| Temple | Popular name / deity | Builder and facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Virupaksha** | **Pampapati**; Shiva – the kingdom's deity | First and largest temple of Hampi; 7th-century core; enlarged by **Lakkana Dandesha** under Devaraya II; Krishnadevaraya added the central hall and eastern gopuram (**1510**); ceiling paintings incl. **Vidyaranya in procession** |
| **Vitthala (Vithalalayam)** | Saptaswara Devalayam; Vishnu (Vithoba) | Begun under Devaraya II, expanded by **Krishnadevaraya**, continued under Achyutaraya; **56 musical ("SaReGaMa") pillars**; stone chariot |
| **Hazara Rama** | **Ramachandra temple** (APPSC G2 2008) – royal chapel | **Krishnadevaraya** (NextGen; Rocket; MPPSC 2014) – many art histories date its core to Devaraya I/II; Ramayana friezes |
| Krishna | Bala Krishna | Krishnadevaraya – idol brought from **Udayagiri** |
| Achyutaraya | Tiruvengalanatha | 1534, set up by Hiriya Tirumalaraja |
- **Musical pillars:** **Vittala temple, Hampi** (UPSC 2007) – not Belur, Bhadrachalam or Srirangam.
- **Virupaksha is at Hampi** (UPSC 2009) – "Virupaksha – Thiruvananthapuram" is the **wrong pair** (APPSC G1 2019 ×2); Kandariya Mahadeva – Khajuraho, Lingaraja – Bhubaneswar, Mahabodhi – Bodh Gaya, Dilwara – Mount Abu, Brihadeswara – Thanjavur are right.
- **Kings ruled on behalf of Virupaksha** (RPSC 2012) – not Nataraja, Murugan, Venkatesa, Pampadevi or Vishnu.
- **Vidyaranya's portrait:** painted on the ceiling of the **Ranga mandapa of the Virupaksha temple, Hampi** (Vidyaranya in a palanquin procession).
- Krishnadevaraya's other Hampi works: **Ugra Narasimha**, Hanuman statue, monolithic Ganesha.
- Distractor temples: **Kailasa, Ellora – Rashtrakuta Krishna I**; Hoysaleswara, Halebidu; Chennakesava, Belur – Vishnuvardhana.

=== The Vijayanagara Style – Raya Gopurams, Kalyana Mandapas, Secular Buildings
- **Raya gopurams:** towering gateways that dwarf the central shrine – built by **Krishnadevaraya** at Hampi, Tiruvannamalai, Chidambaram, Srirangam, Tirupati (SSC 2001) – not Vidyaranya, Harihara or Rajaraja; he also built the southern Rajagopuram of the **Ekambaranatha temple, Kanchipuram (1509)**.
- **Kalyana mandapas** (pillared hall for the divine marriage) = a notable feature of **Vijayanagara** temples (UPSC 2019) – not Chalukya, Chandela or Rashtrakuta.
| Feature | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Amman shrine | Separate goddess shrine |
| Sahasra Stambha Mandapa | Thousand-pillared hall with composite pillars |
| Ekapaadam | Hoysala-type single pillar in 3-4 sections |
- **Percy Brown:** the "**matured state of the Dravidian tradition**"; new: **secular buildings in temple premises**, pillared corridors added to older temples.
- **Indo-Saracenic / Indo-Islamic building:** **Lotus Mahal** (Chitrangi / Padma Mahal), built by Krishnadevaraya "in Indo-Arabic style" (NextGen) – not the Vitthala or Hazara Rama temples.
- Queen's Bath near the Lotus Mahal; **Mahanavami Dibba** (Krishnadevaraya); **Huzur Daftar** – Indo-Persian council chamber with **40 pillars**.
- **Music:** **Sangita Suryodayam** by **Bandaru Lakshminarayana** (court musician of Krishnadevaraya) – not Krishnadevaraya, Rama Raya or Ramabhadramba; the **veena** gained prominence.
~~ Sources: NextGen Vijayanagara Kingdom p.5 (Lotus Mahal in Indo-Arabic style), p.33 (Sahasra Stambha Mandapas); Wikipedia "Ekambareswarar Temple" (southern gopuram, Krishnadevaraya 1509, inscriptions); app notes 1:123 (sub 0, 3, 9)

=== Lepakshi – Veerabhadra Temple, Nandi, Hanging Pillar, Paintings
- **Veerabhadra (Papanaseswara) temple** on **Kurma Shailam**, near **Hindupur**, **Sri Sathya Sai district** (formerly Anantapur) – kingdom: **Vijayanagara**.
- Built **c. 1530-40** by the Penukonda governors **Virupanna and Veeranna**, officers of **Achyuta Deva Raya** – "Penugonda Virupanna's Veerabhadreswara temple" = **Lepakshi** (not Tadipatri, Hampi or Kadiri).
- **Three shrines:** **Shiva, Vishnu and Veerabhadra** (APPSC ASO) – not Jagannath, Kedarnath or Somnath; **Virabhadra = the fierce form of Shiva**.
- **Hanging pillar** = Lepakshi (APPSC 2019).
- **Huge stone bull (monolithic Nandi)** of the Vijayanagara period = **Lepakshi** – largest monolithic Nandi in India; not Tadipatri, Kadiri or Penugonda.
- **Naga linga:** a linga under a **seven-hooded** serpent from a single boulder.
- **Paintings:** the finest **Vijayanagara-school** murals in Andhra are at **Lepakshi** (×9) – not Srisailam, Tirupati, Tadipatri, Simhachalam, Ahobilam, Pushpagiri, Draksharama, Vontimitta, Kondapalli or Kondavidu.
  - Ceilings: **Ramayana and Mahabharata**; walls: **Parvati Parinayamu**, **Tripurasura Samharam**; "pictorial inscriptions".
  - Hampi Virupaksha murals: **Dashavatara**, **Girijakalyana**; style – mineral colours on lime plaster, bold outlines, **elongated, energetic figures**; survey – **Stella Kramrisch**.
- **Match (APPSC JA):** largest Nandi – Lepakshi; Lord of the Seven Hills – Tirumala; saviour of Prahlada – Ahobilam; Jyotirlinga – Srisailam.
- Latest: 8 Jun 2026 – state launch of **Yogandhra-2026** at Lepakshi.

=== Tadipatri and Other Andhra Temples
- **Tadipatri (Anantapur):** art critics rate its **gopurams above those of Hampi's Vitthala temple** (APPSC G2 2019 ×3) – not Ahobilam, Srisailam, Lepakshi or Chandragiri.
- **Chintala Venkataramana** (Vishnu) and **Bugga Ramalingeswara** (Shiva) temples = **Tadipatri** (APPSC G1 1990-91, 1994) – patrons the **Pemmasani Nayakas** (Pemmasani Timmanayudu); high-relief sculpture (**Ubattu Shilpalu**) and the painting of **Vatapatra Shayi**.
| Temple | Place / note |
|---|---|
| **Kodandarama, Ontimitta (Vontimitta)** | **Kadapa**; AP's official **Sri Rama Navami** temple after Bhadrachalam went to Telangana (2014) |
| **Yaganti (Uma Maheswara)** | Kurnool – now **Nandyal** district; Agastya, Venkateswara and Veera Brahmam caves; the "growing" Nandi |
| Ahobilam / Kadiri | Narasimha |
| Srikalahasti | Vayu linga; gali gopuram collapsed 2010 |
| Srisailam | Mallikarjuna; four gateways; **mukha mandapam – Harihara II**; sculptured prakaram – Shanti Lingayya |
| Tirumala | Kalyana mandapam and bronze statues of the king with his queens – **Krishnadevaraya**; steps and Govindaraja temple – Anantaraju |
- **Ekambaranatha, Kanchipuram:** Pallava origin, but the great southern **Rajagopuram (1509)** is **Krishnadevaraya's** – the builder the APPSC question expects.
- Latest (5 Mar 2026): Achyutaraya's **1534** inscriptions on a 50-ft pillar of the **Somapalayam Chennakesava** temple, Annamayya district.
~~ Sources: NextGen previous papers p.15, p.43 ("Chintala Venkata Ramana temple is located at Thadipatri"); Rocket AP History sheet #24; nandyal.ap.gov.in (district formed 4 Apr 2022); app notes 1:123 (sub 6, 7)

=== Forts, Cities and Later Capitals
- **Gooty (Gutti) fort:** wall inscriptions from the **7th century** (APPSC FRO 2023 ×2); a **Bukka I** record calls it the foremost of forts; Aliya Rama Raya's base.
- **Penukonda fort** = **Vijayanagara** kingdom; first **Aravidu capital (1570)** → **Chandragiri** (Venkatapati Raya II; Raja Mahal) → **Vellore (1600)**.
- **Nagalapura (Nagalapuram)** = city founded by **Krishnadevaraya** for his mother Nagala Devi (UPPCS 2016) – not Warangal, Udayagiri or Chandragiri.
- Udayagiri (Nellore) – Panchaloha cannon; Gandikota – idols looted by **Mir Jumla** (1657).
