## Trends
- **Noise dominates (18 of 34).** Almost all are one-line recall: the decibel and the green muffler repeat across APPSC and UPPSC papers; the Noise Rules zone table and timings are the next layer.
- **APPSC Group I 2023 used current-affairs statement questions** (GloLitter, dark sky reserve) where every printed statement is a near-miss – the key turns on who runs the body and where the first site is.
- **Radioactive items carry the weak keys:** the building-material question (thorium as keyed vs radon strictly) and the UPPSC 2009 effects code (printed I, III, IV; Rocket gives I and IV).
- Recent APPSC (G2 Mains 2025, Factories Inspector) tests bioremediation applications and NOT-a-method pickers.
