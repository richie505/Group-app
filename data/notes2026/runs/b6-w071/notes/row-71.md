=== Digital India, Quantum Mission and Semiconductors
# National Quantum Mission (NQM)
- Sanctioned **April 2023**; **₹6,003.65 crore** (2023–31); nodal **DST** (IndiaAI Mission and ISM = MeitY).
- Covers **computing, communication, sensing and materials** (not only computing); **four** thematic hubs (T-Hubs); **17 start-ups**.
- Targets: qubits 50–1,000 in 8 years; satellite QKD over 2,000 km; a **1,000-km** quantum communication network (not 10,000 km).
| T-Hub | Domain |
|---|---|
| IISc Bengaluru | Computing |
| IIT Madras + C-DOT | Communication |
| **IIT Bombay** | **Sensing** |
| IIT Delhi | Materials |
- India's first **Quantum Valley** foundation – **Amaravati** (February 2026).
- **QpiAI, Bengaluru** – **India's first QPU fabrication facility** (superconducting quantum processors).
# Quantum basics
- **Qubit** – 0, 1 or both (**superposition**); also **entanglement** and **interference**.
- **Quantum Error Correction** – many **physical qubits** combined to form **one logical qubit**.
| Qubit type | Basis |
|---|---|
| Superconducting | Josephson junctions (Google Willow) |
| **Trapped-ion** | **Charged atoms held in electromagnetic fields** |
| Quantum-dot | Semiconductors |
| Photonic | Photons |
- **QKD** – uses **photons**, the **observer effect** and the **no-cloning rule**.
- **IBM** aims at a fault-tolerant quantum computer by **2029**.
- **Physics Nobel 2025** – **John Clarke, Michel H. Devoret, John M. Martinis** (macroscopic quantum tunnelling).
# Semiconductors and electronics
- **Semicon 2.0** – outlay **₹1,27,500 crore**; **six** pillars (design; machinery and raw materials; fabs; ATMP/OSAT; R&D; talent).
- **ATMP** = Assembly, Testing, Marking, Packaging; **OSAT** = Outsourced Semiconductor Assembly and Test.
- **Micron ATMP, Sanand, Gujarat** – commercial production; cited as a step towards digital sovereignty.
- **NIDAR 2.0** – **MeitY + Drone Federation of India** (July 2026); drones with **VEGA**, the indigenous **RISC-V** processor.
- **MPMP-S** (Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme) – ₹62,500 crore, FY 2026–27 to 2030–31; **extra 1.5%** incentive for Indian-made key components.
- **NIPU-2026** (urea investment policy) – gas-based plants; **fixed RoE 12–16%**.
# Digital India (launched 2015; 11 years on 1 July 2026)
- **DPI:** UPI, RuPay, Aadhaar, DigiLocker, ONDC.
- **Digital Rupee = RBI liability**; **UPI money = bank deposit liability** (not RBI).
- **BharatNet ~97%** of targeted GPs (2.15 of 2.22 lakh).
- **Aadhaar 144 crore+**; **DBT ₹51 lakh crore+**; PDS authentication **98%+** (not 100%); Jan Dhan **57.78 crore** accounts, deposits **₹2.94 lakh crore**.
- **MeghRaj** (2014) – government cloud; **342 → 2,323 departments**.
- **DIKSHA** built on **Sunbird**.
- **India Stack Global** and **Global DPI Repository** – launched during India's **G20 Presidency (2023)**, not in 2026.
- **Global Innovation Index 2025:** India **38th** (81st in 2015). R&D spend ~**0.74% of GDP** (2000–2020 average, app notes; PMF: GERD still below 1%) vs world ~2.07%.
- Digital-sovereignty trigger: **EseeCloud** (**China**) – Indian CCTV networks compromised (April 2026).

- Launched **18 July 2026** from **SDSC Sriharikota** – India's first private orbital launch from Indian soil; **Mission Aagaman**.

=== Energy and Technology Firsts – Hydrogen Train, Geothermal, Helium and EVs
# India's first hydrogen train
- Flagged off at **Jind, Haryana**, **17 July 2026**; route **Jind–Sonipat, 89 km**; under **"Hydrogen for Heritage"**.
- **PEM (Proton Exchange Membrane) fuel cell**; power **2,400 kW** (1,200 kW per power car).
- **Jind** – India's largest railway hydrogen **storage and refuelling** centre.
- World's first hydrogen passenger train = **Alstom Coradia iLint (Germany, 2018)**.
- **National Green Hydrogen Mission** – Jan 2023, **₹19,744 crore**, **MNRE** (not DST/DPIIT).
# Geothermal – Puga Valley
- **India's first and deepest geothermal wells** – **Puga Valley, Ladakh** (July 2026); **1 MW** demonstration; by **ONGC** Energy Centre (not NTPC); Carbon-Neutral Ladakh.
- **National Policy on Geothermal Energy, 2025** (MNRE) – supports **Net Zero 2070**; potential ~**10.6 GW**.
- Sites: Puga (Ladakh), Manikaran (HP), Tapoban (Uttarakhand), **Yumesamdong (Sikkim)**. Plant types: **dry steam, flash, binary** (not solar PV).
# Helium
- **US ~43%** of world helium; China only ~1.6% (China's export ban, July 2026).
- Helium: atomic number 2; **Group 18** noble gas; boiling point ~**–269°C**; **cannot solidify at normal pressure** (~25 atm at ~1 K).
- Uses: **quantum computers**, MRI magnets, semiconductor wafers, rocket tank pressurisation; in **scuba tanks** to avoid **nitrogen narcosis**.
# Electric vehicles and batteries
- EV sales ~**23 lakh (2025)**; EV adoption **0.08% (FY16) → 8.26% (FY26)**.
- **PM E-DRIVE** (2024) – **Ministry of Heavy Industries**, ₹10,900 crore. **e-Amrit** – EV information and charging-station locator portal.
- **PLI-ACC Battery** (2021) – **₹18,100 crore, 50 GWh**. **GST on EVs 5%**.
- **India Electric Mobility Index** – **NITI Aayog** (not Ministry of Heavy Industries), August 2025; target **30% EV by 2030**; transport ~9% of emissions.
| EV type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| BEV | Battery only |
| HEV | Engine + motor |
| **PHEV** | **Hybrid with plug-in battery** |
| FCEV | Fuel cell, usually hydrogen |
