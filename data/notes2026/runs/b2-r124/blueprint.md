## 5. Key corrections a reader should know
- JPSC 2016 "Right to Privacy is not a UN-listed human right" – wrong: UDHR Art. 12 protects privacy (option added: None of the above).
- APPSC Group 1 2024 (Human Rights Commissions statements) – printed "None" is wrong: statement III (SHRC member removed by the President after an SC inquiry) is true (option added: Only III).
- MPPSC/UPSC "only a retired CJI can chair NHRC", "tenure 5 years" – correct when asked, outdated since the 2019 amendment.
