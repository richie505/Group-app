=== NOTES 2:124 | Human Rights – Concept, Generations, Udhr, Iccpr, Icescr and Un Machinery
## SUB 0 | Concept of Human Rights
[124:0:0] - **Inherent, inalienable** rights held simply because one is human.
[124:0:1] - Rest on **human dignity** (UDHR Preamble: dignity and justice); **universal** across cultures.
[124:0:2] - **Indivisible and interdependent**: civil-political and socio-economic rights stand together (**Vienna Declaration, 1993**).
[124:0:3] - Every **Fundamental Right** is a human right, but not every human right is a Fundamental Right.
[124:0:4] - **Right to vote in India** = only a **statutory right** (**Section 62, RP Act**), not an FR or DPSP.
[124:0:5] - First document of the human rights campaign: **Magna Carta (1215)** → 1688 → **1776** (US Declaration of Independence) → **1789** (French Revolution).
## SUB 1 | Generations of Human Rights
[124:1:0] - Proponent: **Karel Vasak (1977)**; linked to **liberty, equality, fraternity**.
[124:1:1] TABLE head: Generation | Ideal | Rights
  [124:1:1.0] First | Liberty | Civil and political: life, speech, fair trial, vote, participation in political life
  [124:1:1.1] Second | Equality | Economic, social, cultural: work, education, health, eradication of social discrimination
  [124:1:1.2] Third | Fraternity / solidarity | Collective: healthy environment, development, peace
[124:1:2] - Generations overlap: education is both a second- and a third-generation right.
[124:1:3] Eradication of social discrimination and Right to Education = **second** generation (JPSC 2016).
## SUB 2 | Udhr, 1948
[124:2:0] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [124:2:0.0] Adopted by | UN General Assembly, 10 December 1948
  [124:2:0.1] Human Rights Day | 10 December (UNGA resolution 423(V), 1950)
  [124:2:0.2] Drafting chair | Eleanor Roosevelt
  [124:2:0.3] Structure | Preamble + 30 articles
  [124:2:0.4] Legal status | Not a treaty, not legally binding
[124:2:1] - Covers civil, political, economic, social and cultural rights; guides the **SDGs Agenda**.
[124:2:2] - **Included:** dignity, liberty, equality, life, adequate standard of living, education; right to work (economic-social); free speech, religion, equality before law (civil-political).
[124:2:3] - **NOT included:** right to **universal basic income** (JPSC 2023).
[124:2:4] - **Privacy:** UDHR **Art. 12**, ICCPR **Art. 17**; but the JPSC 2016 key treated privacy as not a UN-listed human right.
[124:2:5] - Context: UN came into being **24 Oct 1945**; India's Constitution adopted **26 Nov 1949**.
[124:2:6] ICCPR **Art. 19** = expression, not privacy (privacy = Art. 17).
[124:2:7] - **Human Rights Day themes:** **2016** "Stand up for someone's rights today"; **2024** "Our Rights, Our Future, Right Now"; **2025** "**Human Rights, Our Everyday Essentials**".
[124:2:8] - **Social security in the UDHR:** the right to social security (Art. 22) is an economic and social right, like the rights to work, education and health.
## SUB 3 | International Bill of Human Rights
[124:3:0] - Components: **UDHR + ICCPR + ICESCR + optional protocols**.
[124:3:1] TABLE head: Instrument | Adopted | In force | Key content
  [124:3:1.0] ICESCR | 16 Dec 1966 | 3 Jan 1976 | Work, social security, health, education; realised progressively
  [124:3:1.1] ICCPR | 16 Dec 1966 | 23 Mar 1976 | Life (Art. 6), no torture (Art. 7), fair trial (Art. 14), privacy (Art. 17), expression (Art. 19), child rights (Art. 24)
  [124:3:1.2] ICCPR First Optional Protocol | 1966 | – | Individual complaints
  [124:3:1.3] ICESCR Optional Protocol | 2008 | – | Complaints
[124:3:2] - **India:** party to ICCPR since **1979**; has **not signed the First Optional Protocol**, so Indians cannot take complaints to the UN (6 May 2026).
[124:3:3] Child rights: ICCPR **Article 24**; also **Article 24** of the Indian Constitution (child labour).

## SUB 6 | Un Human Rights Machinery
[124:6:0] # Human Rights Council
[124:6:1] - Set up **2006**, replacing the **UN Commission on Human Rights**; based in **Geneva**.
[124:6:2] - **47 members** elected by the UNGA; **3-year terms**, at most **two consecutive** terms.
[124:6:3] - **Two-thirds** UNGA vote can suspend a member for gross violations.
[124:6:4] TABLE head: Region | Seats
  [124:6:4.0] Africa | 13
  [124:6:4.1] Asia-Pacific | 13
  [124:6:4.2] Latin America and Caribbean | 8
  [124:6:4.3] Western Europe and Others | 7
  [124:6:4.4] Eastern Europe | 6
[124:6:5] - **Universal Periodic Review (UPR):** peer review of every UN member's record; cycles of about **4.5 years** since **2008**.
[124:6:6] - India was a member for **2019-21** and **2022-24**.
[124:6:7] # Other Bodies
[124:6:8] TABLE head: Body | Role
  [124:6:8.0] OHCHR | Set up 1993, after the Vienna Conference
  [124:6:8.1] Treaty bodies | Expert committees monitoring each core treaty
  [124:6:8.2] Special rapporteurs | HRC "special procedures": unpaid independent experts on a theme or country
[124:6:9] - **Jun 2026:** OHCHR convened the **Global Alliance for Human Rights** (Imagine-Dialogue-Act model) ahead of the UDHR's **80th anniversary (Dec 2028)**.
## SUB 7 | Paris Principles and NHRIs
[124:7:0] - Adopted by the **UNGA in 1993**.
[124:7:1] - Content: **independence and autonomy** from government, **pluralist** composition, adequate resources, **powers to investigate** (not reliance on the executive).
[124:7:2] - **GANHRI** accredits NHRIs via its **Sub-Committee on Accreditation**: **"A" = fully**, **"B" = partly** compliant.
[124:7:3] - **India's NHRC:** set up **12 Oct 1993** under the Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993; **statutory**, not constitutional; 2019 amendment: three human-rights members, including a **woman**.
[124:7:4] 1993 = Vienna Declaration + OHCHR + Paris Principles + NHRC.
