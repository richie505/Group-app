=== Concept of Human Rights – Meaning, Thinkers and Milestones
# Meaning
- **Human rights:** rights given to **all human beings by nature**, simply because they are human – **inherent, inalienable, universal**; essential conditions of social life without which no one can lead a normal life.
- **Core value:** **dignity of man as a human being** (UDHR Preamble: inherent dignity and equal rights); **indivisible and interdependent** – civil-political and socio-economic rights stand together (**Vienna Declaration, 1993**).
- **Rights vs the State:** rights are **claims of the citizens against the State** – limits on State power, not claims of the State on citizens or privileges of a few.
- Every **Fundamental Right** is a human right, but **not every human right is a Fundamental Right**.
- **Kinds of rights:** **economic rights** give a **reasonable and legal source of livelihood** (work, wages, leisure); **political rights** = vote, contest, hold office (the **right to vote is a political right**; in India only a **statutory right** – **Sec. 62, RP Act 1951**, not an FR); civil rights = life, liberty, religion.
- **Values:** "respect for human rights and responsibilities" is a **constitutional value**; in a **democracy** equality is accepted as a **legal norm**, which makes it easier to guarantee women's freedom and dignity (it does not ensure equal representation).
- **Violations (NDA 2010):** detaining a person on the way to vote and killing a civilian in a combing operation are **both** human-rights violations.

=== UDHR 1948 and Human Rights Day
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Adopted by | **UN General Assembly, 10 December 1948** (Paris) |
| Human Rights Day | **10 December** every year (UNGA resolution 423(V), **1950**) |
| Drafting chair | **Eleanor Roosevelt** |
| Structure | Preamble + **30 articles** |
| Legal status | A declaration – **not a treaty, not legally binding** |
| Core value | **Human dignity** |
- **Covers** civil, political, economic, social and cultural rights; **guides the SDGs Agenda** (not the Paris Agreement, Magna Carta or NAFTA).
- **Included:** dignity, liberty, equality (Art. 1); **life, liberty, security (Art. 3)**; equality before law (Art. 7); **privacy (Art. 12)**; social security (Art. 22); **work (Art. 23)**; **adequate standard of living (Art. 25)**; **education (Art. 26)**; **duties to the community (Art. 29)**.
- **Art. 25(1):** security in the event of **unemployment, sickness, disability, widowhood, old age** – linked to an adequate standard of living (food, clothing, housing, medical care).
- **NOT included:** **right to universal basic income** (JPSC 2023).
- **⚠ Privacy IS a UDHR right (Art. 12)** and an ICCPR right (Art. 17); the JPSC 2016 key that called privacy "not a UN-listed human right" is wrong.
- **Context dates:** UN Day **24 Oct 1945**; India's Constitution adopted 26 Nov 1949.

=== Generations of Human Rights
- **Karel Vasak (1977)** classified rights into three generations, linked to **liberty, equality, fraternity**.
| Generation | Ideal | Rights |
|---|---|---|
| First | Liberty | **Civil and political**: life, speech, fair trial, vote, participation in political life |
| Second | Equality | **Economic, social, cultural**: **work, education**, health, social security, **eradication of social discrimination** |
| Third | Fraternity / solidarity | **Collective**: healthy environment, development, peace |
- **Right to Education** and **Right to Work** are both **second-generation** (ICESCR) rights; Liberty and Equality before law are first-generation.

=== International Bill of Human Rights and Core Treaties
- **International Bill of Human Rights = UDHR + ICCPR + ICESCR + optional protocols.**
| Instrument | Adopted | In force | Key content |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICESCR | 16 Dec 1966 | **3 Jan 1976** | Work, social security, health, education; realised progressively |
| ICCPR | 16 Dec 1966 | **23 Mar 1976** | Life (Art. 6), no torture (Art. 7), fair trial (Art. 14), **privacy (Art. 17)**, **expression (Art. 19)**, **child rights (Art. 24)** |
| ICCPR First Optional Protocol | 1966 | – | Individual complaints |
| ICESCR Optional Protocol | **2008** | – | Complaints |
- **ICESCR entered into force first** (Jan 1976), ICCPR in March 1976.
- **India** acceded to both Covenants on **10 April 1979**; has **not signed the ICCPR First Optional Protocol**.
- **Child rights:** **ICCPR Art. 24** (also Art. 24 of the Indian Constitution – child labour).

=== UN Machinery – Human Rights Council, OHCHR, Paris Principles
# Human Rights Council (UNHRC)
- Set up **2006**, replacing the **UN Commission on Human Rights**; based in **Geneva**.
- **47 members** elected by the UNGA; **3-year terms**, at most **two consecutive** terms; **two-thirds** UNGA vote can suspend a member.
| Region | Seats |
|---|---|
| Africa | 13 |
| **Asia-Pacific** | **13** |
| Latin America and Caribbean | 8 |
| Western Europe and Others | 7 |
| Eastern Europe | 6 |
- **Universal Periodic Review (UPR):** peer review of every UN member's record; since **2008**, cycles of about **4.5 years**.
- **Special rapporteurs** ("special procedures") = **unpaid independent experts**; **treaty bodies** monitor each core treaty.
- **OHCHR** set up **1993**, after the **Vienna Conference**.
# Paris Principles and NHRIs
- Adopted by the **UNGA in 1993** (drafted in Paris, Oct 1991): **independence and autonomy**, **pluralist** composition, adequate resources, **powers to investigate** (not dependence on the executive).
- **GANHRI** accredits NHRIs through its **Sub-Committee on Accreditation**: **"A" = fully**, "B" = partly compliant.
- India's **NHRC** (1993, under the PHRA 1993) is a **statutory** NHRI.
