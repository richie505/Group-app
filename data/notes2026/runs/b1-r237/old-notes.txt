## SUB 0 | Run-up: Simon Commission, Nehru Report, Calcutta Ultimatum
[237:0:0] - **Simon Commission** appointed **Nov 1927**; arrived **3 February 1928**.
[237:0:1] - **Nehru Report (1928):** All Parties Conference committee; **chairman Motilal Nehru**, **secretary Jawaharlal Nehru**; constitutional framework asking for **Dominion Status**.
[237:0:2] - **Calcutta session (1928):** accepted the Nehru Report with a **one-year ultimatum** – if not accepted within the year, a mass movement would follow.
[237:0:3] - **Viceroy Lord Irwin (1926-31):** Simon Commission visit, Lahore Purna Swaraj, Dandi March all fall in his time.
[237:0:4] Purna Swaraj was adopted only at Lahore (1929); crediting Calcutta 1928 is WRONG.
## SUB 1 | Lahore Session, 1929 – Purna Swaraj
[237:1:0] # Session Facts
[237:1:1] - Venue and date: **Lahore, December 1929**; resolution passed **31 December 1929**.
[237:1:2] - **President: Jawaharlal Nehru** – he moved the Purna Swaraj resolution and declared the aim; Viceroy **Lord Irwin**.
[237:1:3] - Nehru **hoisted the tricolour at midnight, 31 December 1929, on the banks of the Ravi**.
[237:1:4] # Resolutions
[237:1:5] - 1. **Nehru Report / Dominion Status rejected** (Calcutta deadline had lapsed).
[237:1:6] - 2. Goal = **Purna Swaraj (Complete Independence)**.
[237:1:7] - 3. No benefit from the Round Table Conference – to be boycotted.
[237:1:8] - 4. **26 January** to be observed every year as **Independence Day**.
[237:1:9] - 5. **Congress Working Committee authorised to launch Civil Disobedience**.
[237:1:10] - **Abolition of untouchability was NOT among the Lahore resolutions.**
[237:1:11] # Purna Swaraj Day
[237:1:12] - **First Independence Day: 26 January 1930**.
[237:1:13] - **26 January 1950** was chosen for the Constitution because Congress had celebrated 26 January 1930 as the first Independence Day.
[237:1:14] - **AP angle:** **Bulusu Sambamurthy (East Godavari)** got a **Purna Swaraj resolution passed at district level first**, before Lahore.
[237:1:15] Lahore 1929 = Jawaharlal Nehru = Purna Swaraj = flag on the Ravi = 26 January 1930.
## SUB 2 | Dandi March, 1930
[237:2:0] # Prelude
[237:2:1] - Gandhi's **11-point ultimatum/letter to Viceroy Irwin** (30 Jan 1930; letter of 2 March 1930); main demand **abolition of the salt tax**; Irwin refused.
[237:2:2] - **Why salt:** a **universal necessity** of rich and poor; the salt tax and monopoly were the most visible sign of colonial exploitation.
[237:2:3] - Ultimate goal of the Salt Satyagraha: **Purna Swaraj**.

  [237:2:5.4] Destination | Dandi, Navsari district, Gujarat coast
  [237:2:5.5] Reached | 5 April 1930
  [237:2:5.6] Salt law broken | 6 April 1930 – CDM begins
  [237:2:5.7] Viceroy | Lord Irwin
[237:2:6] - **Only Telugu marcher:** **Yerneni (Erneni) Subrahmanyam** of **Krishna district**; later founded an ashram at **Komaravolu**.
[237:2:7] - Gandhi arrested **5 May 1930** (Yerwada Jail); successors: **1st Abbas Tyabji, 2nd Sarojini Naidu**.

## SUB 9 | Gandhi-irwin Pact and Karachi Session
[237:9:0] # The Pact
[237:9:1] - Signed **5 March 1931 (Delhi Pact)** – not 1930; CWC authorised Gandhi.
[237:9:2] - **Mediators: Tej Bahadur Sapru and M.R. Jayakar** (also V.S. Srinivasa Sastri).
[237:9:3] - Sarojini Naidu called Gandhi and Irwin the **"Two Mahatmas"**; Irwin's biographer **Alan Campbell Johnson** called Gandhi's gains **"Consolation Prizes"**.
[237:9:4] TABLE head: Irwin agreed | Irwin refused | Gandhi agreed
  [237:9:4.0] Release non-violent political prisoners | Public inquiry into police excesses | Suspend the CDM
  [237:9:4.1] Coastal villagers may make salt for own use | Commuting death sentences of Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Rajguru | Attend the 2nd RTC
  [237:9:4.2] Withdraw ordinances; remit fines; return unsold lands | – | –
[237:9:5] # Karachi Session (29-31 March 1931)
[237:9:6] - President **Vallabhbhai Patel**; **endorsed** (did not oppose) the Pact; chose Gandhi for the 2nd RTC.
[237:9:7] - Passed the **Fundamental Rights** and **National Economic Programme** resolutions – drafted by **Jawaharlal Nehru** (with M.N. Roy's help).
[237:9:8] - Criticism: no commutation for Bhagat Singh (executed **23 March 1931**); no police inquiry; youth disillusioned.

[237:10:6] - **Poona Pact: 24 September 1932**, Yerwada Jail – signed by **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar** (depressed classes) and **Madan Mohan Malaviya** (caste Hindus); separate electorates dropped; **148 reserved seats** in provincial legislatures instead of **71** under the Award, within joint electorates.
[237:10:7] - **Harijan Sevak Sangh** (first named Anti-Untouchability League) founded by Gandhi, **30 September 1932**; first president **G.D. Birla**, secretary **Thakkar Bapa**.
[237:10:8] - Gandhi: **"Service to Harijans"** his top priority among constructive programmes.
