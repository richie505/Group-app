## 3. Repeats and traps
- **Lahore 1929 – Nehru – Purna Swaraj – 31 Dec, Ravi – 26 January** is asked again and again; the "19 December 1929" date in keys is wrong.
- **Dandi:** 12 March (not 15), 78 followers (not 100), 240 miles (not km), salt law broken 6 April; "started CDM from" – keys split between Dandi and Sabarmati.
- **Webb Miller** = Dharasana, United Press. **Konda Venkatappayya** = Andhra Dictator, not Prakasam.
- Andhra centre matching (Bulusu / Kasi Viswanadham / Dandu Narayana Raju) is where sources disagree.
- The old notes were heavy on the Gandhi-Irwin Pact, RTCs, Poona Pact and Harijan Sevak Sangh (row 239 matter), on number-game and "APPSC PYQ" tables – removed here; Pedaganjam/Uppada craft tables moved to their own rows.
