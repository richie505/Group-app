## SUB 0 | The Dictator and the Organisation in Andhra
[0:0] - **Konda Venkatappayya** (had met Gandhi with an Andhra team; "Father of the Andhra State Movement") was appointed **Dictator for Andhra** by the **Andhra Congress Committee** to conduct the **Salt Satyagraha / CDM** – the first leader of the CDM in Andhra (APPSC G2 2000, 2008, 2017) – not Prakasam, Pattabhi, Gadicherla or N.G. Ranga.
[0:1] - **Each district had its own dictator, a war council and a military-style camp (sibiram)** – the camps served as salt satyagraha headquarters (idea credited to **Pattabhi Sitaramayya**).
[0:2] TABLE head: Camp | Place
  [0:2.0] Guntur camp | Guntur
  [0:2.1] Pallepadu Ashram | Nellore
  [0:2.2] Bellary camp | Rayalaseema
  [0:2.3] Vedavanam (T. Prakasam's house) | Madras – the centre of Salt Satyagraha activity in Madras (not Krishna district)
  [0:2.4] Eluru National School | West Godavari – volunteers joined (May 1930)
[0:3] - **Programme:** towns – **picketing foreign-cloth shops**; villages – **making salt, cutting toddy palms, picketing toddy and arrack shops, raiding salt depots**; main target the **salt tax**. Later echo: women's anti-arrack agitation at **Dubagunta (Nellore), 1991**.
[0:4] - Spectrum: district salt marches in East and West Godavari, Krishna and Guntur; merchants funded the Congress; but mass support of the NCM level was missing.

## SUB 3 | Kakinada Bomb Case, Repression and End
[3:0] - **Kakinada Bomb Case:** circle inspector **Mustafa Ali** brutally attacked the satyagrahis of the **Seethanagaram (Sita) Ashram**; in revenge **Prativadi Bhayankarachari**, founder of the revolutionary "**Ujjivana Bharata Samithi / Sammelan**" in the **Godavari district**, threw a bomb at him at Kakinada (failed); he was sentenced (14 years) and sent to the **Andaman Cellular Jail**; he wrote "**Andaman Jeevitam**". NextGen heads the case "1930" (the year of the ashram raid); the app's MCQ dates the bomb case **1933**.
[3:1] - Repression: **Venneti Satyanarayana** lathi-charged at **Rajahmundry**; **15 January 1932** police firing at **Berhampore** (Willingdon's repression).
[3:2] - **Viceroys:** **Irwin** – Dandi March 1930, Pact 1931; **Willingdon** – repression of 1932-34.
