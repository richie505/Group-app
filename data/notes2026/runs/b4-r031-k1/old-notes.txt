## SUB 5 | Plateaus
[31:5:0] TABLE head: Type | Nature | Examples
  [31:5:0.0] Intermontane | Enclosed by mountains | Tibetan, Pamir, Bolivian (Altiplano), Mongolian, Peru, Colombia
  [31:5:0.1] Piedmont | At mountain foot | Piedmont (USA), Patagonia (foot of the Andes)

  [31:5:0.3] Volcanic / lava | Lava accumulation | Deccan (one of the oldest), Columbia (NW USA)

[31:5:1] - **Tibetan**: highest and largest plateau; average **4,500 m**; 2.5 million sq km.
[31:5:2] - **Pamir**: "**Roof of the World**"; southern border of Central Asia, meeting point of many ranges.
[31:5:3] - **Meseta**: Iberian Peninsula (Spain). **Anatolian**: Turkiye, between Pontic and Taurus ranges.
[31:5:4] - **Colorado**: USA, cut by the Grand Canyon; largest of Colorado (150,580 sq miles), Columbia (105,633), Coconino (5,000), Aquarius (900).
[31:5:5] - **Patagonia**: "storehouse of minerals" of South America. **East African Plateau**: Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda.
[31:5:6] - **Tsaidam Basin**: between Kunlun and Altyn-Tagh, Qinghai (China); rich in minerals and salt.
## SUB 6 | Plains and Basins
[31:6:0] - **Great Plains**: east of the Rockies (USA, Canada). **North European Plain**.
[31:6:1] - **West Siberian Plain**: between the **Urals** (west) and **Yenisei** (east).
[31:6:2] - **Indo-Gangetic Plain**: **6,52,000 sq km**; over 2,400 km long, 70–500 km wide; aggradational; **youngest** physiographic division of India.
[31:6:3] - **Great Basin** (USA): between the Rockies and the Sierra Nevada; Columbia Plateau north, Mojave and Sonoran south.
[31:6:4] - APPSC key: upland between the **Brooks Range and Alaska Range** = "Great Basin" (Yukon-Tanana Upland area).
[31:6:5] - **Great Artesian Basin** (Australia): one of the largest underground water reservoirs (~1.7 million sq km), near the Great Dividing Range.
