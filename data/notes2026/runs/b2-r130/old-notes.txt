## SUB 3 | Nirbhaya Case, Verma Committee and Cla 2013
[130:3:0] # Nirbhaya Case (Delhi, December 2012) → Outcomes
[130:3:1] - 1. **Justice J.S. Verma Committee** (report Jan 2013)
[130:3:2] - 2. **Criminal Law (Amendment) Act 2013**
[130:3:3] - 3. **Nirbhaya Fund** (2013) – non-lapsable corpus
[130:3:4] - 4. **JJ Act 2015** – 16-18-year-olds may be tried as adults for heinous crimes
[130:3:5] - Not an outcome: the BNS. **Nirbhay Raho** (recent): Panchayati Raj + MWCD + NLSIU under Nirbhaya Fund.
[130:3:6] # Verma Committee
[130:3:7] - Members: **J.S. Verma, Leila Seth, Gopal Subramanium**.
[130:3:8] - Recommended (not adopted in that form): **criminalise marital rape**, **no death penalty for rape**, police reforms, **age of consent kept at 16** → Parliament chose **18**.

## SUB 4 | Cla 2018 and Bns 2023
[130:4:0] # CLA 2018
[130:4:1] - Context: **Kathua and Unnao** cases (2018); led to the **FTSC scheme**.
[130:4:2] - Minimum for rape: **7 → 10 years**.
[130:4:3] TABLE head: Victim | Punishment
  [130:4:3.0] Girl under 16 | Minimum 20 years
  [130:4:3.1] Girl under 12 | 20 years to life or death
  [130:4:3.2] Gang rape, girl under 12 | Life or death
[130:4:4] - Investigation **2 months** + trial 2 months + appeals 6 months; **no anticipatory bail** for rape of girl under 16.
[130:4:5] # BNS 2023
[130:4:6] - Keeps IPC provisions on rape, stalking, voyeurism.
[130:4:7] - **Gang-rape age raised 16 → 18**.
[130:4:8] - New **s.69**: intercourse by **deceitful means / false promise of marriage or job**, identity fraud.
[130:4:9] - **s.103**: honour killings prosecuted as **murder**.

## SUB 9 | Andhra Pradesh

[130:9:1] - Response to the **2019 Hyderabad veterinarian** rape-murder.

[130:9:4] - Needs **President's assent** (criminal law in **Concurrent List**); **not in force** as passed.

## SUB 10 | Labour and Welfare Laws – Key Authorities
[130:10:0] TABLE head: Term (Factories Act 1948) | Meaning
  [130:10:0.0] Occupier | Person with ultimate control over the factory's affairs
  [130:10:0.1] Manager | If none is named, the occupier is deemed manager
  [130:10:0.2] Inspector | Appointed by the State Government; every District Magistrate is an Inspector for his district
[130:10:1] - Factories Act was the first post-1947 law fixing **14 years** as minimum age for factory work.
[130:10:2] - **Four Labour Codes:** **Code on Wages 2019**, **Industrial Relations Code 2020**, **Code on Social Security 2020** and **OSH Code 2020** came into force on **21 November 2025**; together they consolidate **29** central labour laws.
[130:10:3] - **OSH Code 2020:** Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code replaces **13** laws on safety, health and working conditions, including the **Factories Act 1948**.
[130:10:4] - **Code on Wages 2019:** subsumes **4** laws – Payment of Wages Act 1936, **Minimum Wages Act 1948**, Payment of Bonus Act 1965, Equal Remuneration Act 1976; the **appropriate Government** fixes minimum wages for all employments; the Inspector becomes an **Inspector-cum-Facilitator** (s.51).

## SUB 1 | 73RD/74TH Amendments – Women and Sc/st in Local Bodies
[131:1:0] # Article 243D
[131:1:1] - **243D(2):** not less than **one-third of SC/ST seats** for SC/ST women (not 50%).
[131:1:2] - **243D(3):** not less than **one-third of total seats** in every Panchayat.
[131:1:3] - **243D(4):** not less than one-third of **Chairperson** offices at all three levels.
[131:1:4] - **243T** gives the same for municipalities; ST seats in municipalities = **243T(1)**.
[131:1:5] - SC/ST seats in proportion to population; reserved seats and chairs **rotate**. **BC reservation** is left to States (optional).
[131:1:6] - **Arunachal Pradesh** – no SC reservation (no SC population).
[131:1:7] - Minimum age to contest: **21** (**243F, 243V**). Panchayat tenure **5 years** from first meeting (**243E(1)**).
[131:1:8] - **73rd in force 24 April 1993** (National Panchayati Raj Day).
[131:1:9] - **Sunset:** SC/ST reservation tied to Art. 334 (to **2030**, 104th Amendment; 243D(5)); **women's reservation has no sunset**.
[131:1:10] **243D(3)** = women's seats; **243D(4)** = women chairpersons.
[131:1:11] # States at 50%
[131:1:12] - One-third is a **floor, not a ceiling**. **Bihar** first with **50%** (2006); then MP, Uttarakhand, HP. **19 States/UTs** now give 50%.
[131:1:13] - **Andhra Pradesh:** **50%** in panchayats and municipalities (APPSC 2019: "1/2 of the seats"). AP 2026: 34% BC (rural) and 33.33% (urban) approved.
[131:1:14] - Women ~**46%** of PRI representatives (2026); over 49.7% of elected panchayat members nationally.
[131:1:15] - UP Panchayat Laws (Amendment) Act 1994: one-third for women (in force 22 April 1994).
