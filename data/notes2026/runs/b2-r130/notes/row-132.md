=== Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana
- **Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas**; launched **1 May 2016**, **Ballia (UP)**.
- **Free LPG connections in women's names** for **BPL households** – **clean cooking fuel**, ends indoor smoke, improves health (APPSC 2017, 2025). Meant for **women**, not students or BCs.
- **Ujjwala 2.0 (August 2021, Mahoba):** **free first refill + hotplate**; **migrants self-declare address**. Reach: over **10.5 crore** households (2026).
- **Abroad:** **Ghana** (National Petroleum Authority) signed an MoU with **Indian Oil** (Jan 2020) to replicate the Ujjwala model.
- **AP's own LPG scheme:** **Deepam** (1999) – LPG connections to women of BPL/DWCRA families; Deepam-2 (2024) – three free cylinders a year.
> **Ujjawala (2007)** = anti-trafficking (now Shakti Sadan); **Ujjwala (2016)** = LPG – not an anti-trafficking scheme.

=== Beti Bachao Beti Padhao and Gender Mainstreaming
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Launch | **22 January 2015**, **Panipat** (Haryana) |
| Nodal ministry | **Women and Child Development** |
| Joint ministries | **Health & Family Welfare + Education (then HRD)** |
| Not involved | **Social Justice & Empowerment** (APPSC 2023) |
| Funding | **100% central** |
| Coverage | **100** gender-critical districts (**low CSR**) → now all districts |
| Vertical | **Sambal** of Mission Shakti |
- **Objectives:** arrest the decline in **Child Sex Ratio**; prevent gender-biased sex-selective elimination (**female infanticide/foeticide**); ensure girls' survival, protection and **education** (female illiteracy); women's empowerment, gender equality – **not legal aid**, not child marriage or honour killing.
- BBBP is a **PM flagship** with **Make in India** and **Digital India**; **Antyodaya Yojana is not**.
# Gender mainstreaming
- **Gender budgeting** = allocating resources to gender-sensitive programmes; Ministry of Finance mandated **Gender Budgeting Cells** in all Ministries in **2004-05** (Dec 2004); GBC Charter 2007; Gender Budget Statement since 2005-06.
- **UN – five components of women's empowerment:** sense of self-worth; right to have and determine choices; right to **access** opportunities and resources; right to control their own lives; ability to influence social change (**"restrictions on access"** is not one).

=== Mission Shakti and MWCD Umbrella Missions
- From **1 April 2022**, MWCD schemes sit under **three** missions: **Mission Shakti** (women), **Mission Vatsalya** (child protection; subsumed **ICPS**; aligned to **JJ Act 2015**; Childline **1098 integrated with 112**), **Saksham Anganwadi & Poshan 2.0** (0-6 years; Anganwadi Services, Poshan Abhiyaan, **Scheme for Adolescent Girls**; **Poshan Bhi Padhai Bhi** = **ECCE**, launched May 2023 by the **WCD Minister**).
| Sub-scheme | Component | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Sambal (safety) | **One Stop Centre (Sakhi)** | Police help, medical, legal, counselling, shelter under one roof; **MWCD**, Nirbhaya Fund |
| Sambal | Women Helpline **181** | 24x7 |
| Sambal | BBBP; **Nari Adalat** | Girl child; alternative redressal of petty cases |
| Samarthya (empowerment) | **Shakti Sadan** | Merger of **Swadhar Greh + Ujjawala** |
| Samarthya | **Sakhi Niwas** / **Palna** | Working-women hostels / crèches |
| Samarthya | PMMVY; SANKALP-HEW | Maternity benefit; hub for women's empowerment |
- **Swadhar Greh** beneficiaries: women in difficult circumstances – **deserted women** without support, **women prisoners released** without family support, survivors of disasters, trafficking, violence.
- **Stree Mano Raksha (March 2022):** MWCD + **NIMHANS, Bengaluru** – women's mental health.
- **Discontinued:** Mahila Shakti Kendra, Mahila Police Volunteers.
