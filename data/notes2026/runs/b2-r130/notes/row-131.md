=== 73rd/74th Amendments – Women in Local Bodies
# Article 243D (panchayats) / 243T (municipalities)
- **243D(2):** not less than **one-third of SC/ST seats** for SC/ST women (not 1/4, not 50%).
- **243D(3):** not less than **one-third of total seats** in every Panchayat; rotated.
- **243D(4):** not less than one-third of **Chairperson** offices (Pradhan, Pramukh, Adhyaksha).
- **243D(5) sunset:** SC/ST reservation tied to **Art. 334** (to 2030); **women's reservation has no sunset**.
- **243T** gives the same for municipalities; ST seats in municipalities = **243T(1)**. **BC reservation** is left to States (optional).
- Tenure **5 years** from first meeting (**243E**); minimum age to contest **21** (243F, 243V). **73rd in force 24 April 1993**.
# States at 50%
- One-third is a **floor, not a ceiling**. **Bihar** first with **50% (2006)**; MP, Uttarakhand, HP followed.
- **MoPR (April 2026): 21 States and 2 UTs** have 50% for women (about 20 when APPSC asked); **Haryana** moved to 50% in **2020**. Women are **49.75%** of 25 lakh+ elected panchayat representatives.
- Earlier one-third-only States (APPSC flashcard): **UP, Haryana (till 2020), Goa, J&K**. **UP Panchayat Laws (Amendment) Act 1994:** **one-third** for women.
- **Andhra Pradesh:** **50%** in panchayats and municipalities. AP 2026: 34% BC (rural) and 33.33% (urban) approved.
- **Nagaland (2017):** violent protests against **33% reservation for women in urban local bodies** (claimed to violate Art. 371A).

=== 106th Amendment and the Women's Reservation Bill
- **Constitution (106th Amendment) Act 2023 = Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam:** **one-third** of Lok Sabha, State Assembly and Delhi Assembly seats for women, incl. one-third of SC/ST seats; **no OBC sub-quota**; **not** Rajya Sabha or Councils.
- Before it, Arts 330-334 had **no provision for women**, so a **constitutional amendment** was needed (party ticket quotas need none).
| Article | Content |
|---|---|
| 330A / 332A / 239AA(2)(ba) | Lok Sabha / State Assemblies / Delhi Assembly |
| 334A | Starts after the **first Census after the Act + delimitation** (2027 Census); **15 years**, extendable; seats **rotate** after each delimitation |
- **Assent 28 September 2023**; brought into force **16 April 2026**; stated in force for **J&K and Puducherry** Assemblies from 17 April 2026 (app notes).
# Bill history
| Year | Bill | Fate |
|---|---|---|
| 1996 | 81st Amendment Bill (**Deve Gowda**) | Lapsed |
| 2008 | 108th Bill (Rajya Sabha) | Referred to the Law and Justice Standing Committee (chair **E.M. Sudarsana Natchiappan**; report Dec 2009 signed by Jayanti Natarajan); passed RS **9 March 2010**; lapsed 2014 |
| 2023 | **128th Bill** | Passed 21 Sept 2023 → **106th Amendment** |
| 2026 | **131st Bill** (Lok Sabha **550 → 850**, delimitation on 2011 Census) | Defeated 17 April 2026: **298-230**, short of **352** (Art. 368) |
- Women's groups demand removing the **Census-delimitation condition**.
- **Arts 325-326** = equal franchise; **Arts 84/173** = women can contest.

=== Grassroots Initiatives, Studies and Proxy Representation
| Initiative | Body / place | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Balika Panchayat** | **Kutch, Gujarat** (June 2022) | India's first girls' panchayat |
| Mahila Sabha | e.g. Maharashtra | Women-only meeting before the Gram Sabha |
| Sashakt Panchayat Netri Abhiyan | MoPR | Trains **women** representatives |
| Nirbhay Raho – Nirbhay Chetna | MoPR | Sensitises **male** representatives |
| Model Women-Friendly GPs | MoPR | At least one per **district** |
- **Bokkasam Palem** (Srikalahasti, Tirupati): **1st** among women-friendly panchayats (2026).
- **NCW** set up **1992** under the **NCW Act 1990**. Reserving seats in **local bodies came first** among political empowerment measures.
- **Reservation of chairperson posts brought a distinct change** in working – **Chattopadhyay-Duflo (2004)**, **West Bengal and Rajasthan**: women leaders invested more in **drinking water, roads**. It came from the 73rd Amendment, not a long rural demand.
- **Proxy ("sarpanch-pati"):** male relative runs the office informally; does not legally replace her.
- Dalit/tribal representatives: **SC/ST (PoA) Act 1989** and **PCR Act 1955**; **Therku Thittai** (Cuddalore, TN, 2020): Dalit woman president made to **sit on the floor**.
