## SUB 0 | Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana
[0:0] - **Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas**; launched **1 May 2016**, **Ballia (UP)**.
[0:1] - **Free LPG connections in women's names** for **BPL households** – **clean cooking fuel**, ends indoor smoke, improves health (APPSC 2017, 2025). Meant for **women**, not students or BCs.
[0:2] - **Ujjwala 2.0 (August 2021, Mahoba):** **free first refill + hotplate**; **migrants self-declare address**. Reach: over **10.5 crore** households (2026).
[0:3] - **Abroad:** **Ghana** (National Petroleum Authority) signed an MoU with **Indian Oil** (Jan 2020) to replicate the Ujjwala model.
[0:4] - **AP's own LPG scheme:** **Deepam** (1999) – LPG connections to women of BPL/DWCRA families; Deepam-2 (2024) – three free cylinders a year.
[0:5] > **Ujjawala (2007)** = anti-trafficking (now Shakti Sadan); **Ujjwala (2016)** = LPG – not an anti-trafficking scheme.

## SUB 1 | Beti Bachao Beti Padhao and Gender Mainstreaming
[1:0] TABLE head: Item | Fact
  [1:0.0] Launch | 22 January 2015, Panipat (Haryana)
  [1:0.1] Nodal ministry | Women and Child Development
  [1:0.2] Joint ministries | Health & Family Welfare + Education (then HRD)
  [1:0.3] Not involved | Social Justice & Empowerment (APPSC 2023)
  [1:0.4] Funding | 100% central
  [1:0.5] Coverage | 100 gender-critical districts (low CSR) → now all districts
  [1:0.6] Vertical | Sambal of Mission Shakti
[1:1] - **Objectives:** arrest the decline in **Child Sex Ratio**; prevent gender-biased sex-selective elimination (**female infanticide/foeticide**); ensure girls' survival, protection and **education** (female illiteracy); women's empowerment, gender equality – **not legal aid**, not child marriage or honour killing.
[1:2] - BBBP is a **PM flagship** with **Make in India** and **Digital India**; **Antyodaya Yojana is not**.
[1:3] # Gender mainstreaming
[1:4] - **Gender budgeting** = allocating resources to gender-sensitive programmes; Ministry of Finance mandated **Gender Budgeting Cells** in all Ministries in **2004-05** (Dec 2004); GBC Charter 2007; Gender Budget Statement since 2005-06.
[1:5] - **UN – five components of women's empowerment:** sense of self-worth; right to have and determine choices; right to **access** opportunities and resources; right to control their own lives; ability to influence social change (**"restrictions on access"** is not one).

  [3:1.2] Sambal | BBBP; Nari Adalat | Girl child; alternative redressal of petty cases
