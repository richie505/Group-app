## SUB 0 | Dietary Fats, Cholesterol and Heart Health
[0:0] # Good and bad fats
[0:1] - **Trans fat:** raises **LDL** and **lowers HDL** ("good cholesterol"), so it is bad for health (UPPSC 2012); excess trans fat raises cardiovascular risk.
[0:2] - **Bakery products** made with **hydrogenated fats** contain trans fats, and excess trans fats raise the risk of NCDs – **both statements correct** (APPSC Fisheries 2023).
[0:3] - **WHO REPLACE plan:** eliminate industrially produced trans fats from the global food supply **by 2023** (target date; many countries still pending). **FSSAI** caps trans fat at **2%** in fats and oils.
[0:4] - **Saturated fat** excess raises blood lipids and cholesterol; **HDL** removes bad cholesterol – high HDL lowers heart-disease risk.
[0:5] - **Triglyceride** = ester of glycerol + three fatty acids = a **fat**; the main constituent of body fat.
[0:6] - **Most coronary heart disease patients** have elevated **cholesterol** (LDL).
[0:7] # Oils and fatty acids
[0:8] - Cook in **oil rather than vanaspati ghee**: oils hold **unsaturated fats** (mono- and polyunsaturated) that lower cholesterol (Uttarakhand 2006).
[0:9] - **Sunflower oil:** suitable for **heart patients**; good-quality edible oil **because** it is rich in unsaturated fatty acids (A and R both true, R explains A); more vitamin E than other vegetable oils.
[0:10] - **Highest PUFA among common oils:** **safflower oil** (about 75% linoleic acid) > sunflower > soybean; **coconut oil** is mostly saturated.
[0:11] - **Fish** is healthier than other meat because it contains **polyunsaturated fatty acids** (omega-3) that cut LDL and heart risk.
[0:12] - **Eskimos (Inuit):** little heart disease despite a high meat/fat diet – food rich in **omega-3** from fish and marine mammals.
[0:13] - **Linseed = flaxseed:** richest source of **alpha-linolenic acid (18-carbon omega-3)**; not oats, moth or mung bean.
[0:14] - **Essential fatty acids** cannot be made by the body and must be in the diet; their absence causes deficiency disease – but body cells **can** make most other fatty acids.
[0:15] TABLE head: Fatty acid | Source
  [0:15.0] α-Linolenic (omega-3) | Linseed oil
  [0:15.1] Linoleic | Peanut oil, chicken fat, olive oil
  [0:15.2] Arachidonic | Liver, brain, glandular organs
  [0:15.3] Oleic | Olive oil, canola oil
  [0:15.4] Lauric | Coconut / palm-kernel oil, breast milk
  [0:15.5] Palmitic | Butter, palm oil, meat
  [0:15.6] Stearic | Meat, cocoa butter
  [0:15.7] Butyric | Butter
[0:16] - **Obesity:** excess energy intake + low physical activity → obesity and heart disease; NFHS-5 overweight/obese: **24% women, 23% men**.
[0:17] > 14 PYQs: the trans-fat/HDL pair, oil vs vanaspati, sunflower/safflower, omega-3 (linseed, fish, Eskimos) are the repeats.

## SUB 1 | Major NCDs – Heart, Diabetes, Cancer, Lungs and Brain
[1:0] # Heart and blood vessels
[1:1] - **Coronary artery disease** = result of **plaque buildup** in the arteries supplying the heart (APPSC G2 2018; not "coronary veins disease").
[1:2] - **Atherosclerosis** (hardening and narrowing of arteries by plaque) is a disease of the **circulatory system**.
[1:3] - **Blood pressure generally increases with age.**
[1:4] - Leading cause of death in **India = cardiovascular diseases**; world's single biggest killer (WHO) = **ischaemic heart disease**.
[1:5] # Diabetes
[1:6] - **Normal fasting blood sugar** (non-diabetic): about **70–100 mg/dL** – nearest option **60–100 mg/dL**.
[1:7] - **HbA1c (glycosylated haemoglobin):** average blood glucose over 2–3 months → used to manage **diabetes**.
[1:8] - **BGR-34:** CSIR's Ayurvedic anti-**diabetes** drug (NBRI + CIMAP, 2016).
[1:9] - **First State diabetes registry:** **Goa** (June 2011).
[1:10] TABLE head: ICMR-INDIAB (Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology, June 2023) | Prevalence | People
  [1:10.0] Diabetes | 11.4% | ~101 million
  [1:10.1] Prediabetes | 15.3% | ~136 million
  [1:10.2] Hypertension | 35.5% | ~315 million
[1:11] # Cancer
[1:12] - **Cancer** = **abnormal (uncontrolled) growth of cells**; non-contagious; can spread (metastasis).
[1:13] - **Oncogenes** (mutated growth genes) are associated with **cancer**; first oncogene (SRC) found 1970.
[1:14] - **Blood cancer = leukaemia** (abnormal WBC production in bone marrow); haemophilia is a clotting disorder.
[1:15] - Cases **1.41 million (2022)** → **2.46 million (2045)**; cancer is **not nationally notifiable**, **17 States** have notified it, **Telangana** latest (Jul 2026).
[1:16] - **Free HPV vaccination** campaign for **14-year-old girls** (cervical cancer) launched **28 Feb 2026** from Ajmer.
[1:17] # Lungs and brain
[1:18] - **COPD = Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease**; World COPD Day in November.
[1:19] - **Alzheimer's disease:** degenerative disorder of the **brain**, the leading cause of **dementia** (memory loss, confusion).
[1:20] - **PSE = Photosensitive Epileptic Seizures** – triggered by flashing lights or regular patterns.
[1:21] - **"Big four" NCDs:** cardiovascular diseases, cancers, chronic respiratory diseases, diabetes.

## SUB 2 | Dual Burden and Classification of Diseases
[2:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [2:0.0] Dual burden of disease | Rising rates of both infectious and non-communicable diseases (TB, malaria, dengue alongside diabetes, CVD)
  [2:0.1] Triple burden | Adds injuries (road accidents) to CDs + NCDs
  [2:0.2] Dual burden of malnutrition | Undernutrition + obesity
  [2:0.3] DALY | YLL (years of life lost) + YLD (years lived with disability) – not years gained by treatment
[2:1] - **Genetic disorders** (haemophilia, sickle-cell, Down syndrome) are **non-communicable**; **club foot** is a **birth defect**.
[2:2] - **Malaria** is communicable (Plasmodium, Anopheles) – **NOT a lifestyle disease**; typhoid and chikungunya are also communicable.
[2:3] - **Lifestyle risk factors:** unhealthy diet, physical inactivity, **tobacco**, alcohol.
[2:4] TABLE head: Disease | Vector / spread
  [2:4.0] Dengue, chikungunya, Zika | Aedes
  [2:4.1] Japanese encephalitis | Culex
  [2:4.2] Meningococcal meningitis | Person to person by respiratory droplets
[2:5] - **India State-Level Disease Burden Initiative** = **ICMR + PHFI + IHME**; part of the **Global Burden of Disease (GBD)** study.
[2:6] - Emerging/re-emerging: TB, dengue, Nipah, avian influenza; ICMR **MERA** (malaria elimination); **wastewater surveillance 5 → 50 cities** (2026), tracks **10 viruses**; vaccination platform **U-WIN**.

## SUB 3 | Road Injuries and Mental Health
[3:0] - **AP Road Safety Policy** was declared in **2015** (target: 15% annual cut in fatalities), not 2017; AP is **8th** in road-accident deaths; 2024 (Jan–Nov): 7,477 deaths in 16,898 accidents.
[3:1] - **Rural areas** had **53.8%** of India's road accidents in 2015 (MoRTH).
[3:2] - **MoRTH 2022:** 4,61,312 accidents, **1,68,491 deaths**; **two-wheeler users** the largest group of victims. **NCRB ADSI 2024:** road deaths **1,75,142** (~37.4% of accidental deaths).
[3:3] - **SDG 3:** **halve road-traffic deaths**.
[3:4] - **Mental disorders (GBD 2017):** **197.3 million** Indians – about **1 in 7**; most common **depressive (45.7 mn)** then anxiety disorders.
[3:5] - **Tele-MANAS:** 24x7 tele-mental-health support in all 36 States/UTs.

## SUB 4 | Epidemiological Transition and Key Data
[4:0] - **Omran's stages:** 1. **Age of Pestilence and Famine** (first) → 2. Age of Receding Pandemics → 3. **Age of Degenerative and Man-Made Diseases** (last).
[4:1] - **"Nations within a nation"** (Lancet 2017; data 1990–2016): **4 ETL groups**; ETL = ratio of communicable DALYs to NCD + injury DALYs; **low ratio = high ETL** (more advanced).
[4:2] - **High ETL:** **Kerala**, Goa, Tamil Nadu, Punjab; **Karnataka** and Delhi higher-middle; NCD + injury DALYs overtook communicable DALYs for India in **2003**.
[4:3] TABLE head: Indicator | Fact
  [4:3.0] NCD share of deaths | 57% (Economic Survey 2025-26)
  [4:3.1] MMR since 1990 | Fallen 86%
  [4:3.2] Under-five mortality since 1990 | Fallen 78%
[4:4] - India's probability of dying from an NCD **rose** in 2001–2019 – **women over 40, men over 55** (Lancet).
