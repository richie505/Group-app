## SUB 5 | Sarnath – India's 45th World Heritage Site
[5:0] # Inscription
[5:1] - **Ancient Buddhist Site of Sarnath** (near Varanasi, UP) inscribed at the **48th World Heritage Committee, Busan, South Korea**.
[5:2] - India now **45 WHS** – **6th globally, 2nd in Asia-Pacific**; on tentative list since **1998** (about 28 years).
[5:3] - Previous inscription: **Maratha Military Landscapes of India** – 47th session, **Paris**.
[5:4] - India hosted the **46th World Heritage Committee** – **New Delhi, 2024**.
[5:5] # Significance
[5:6] - Buddha's **first sermon** (after enlightenment at Bodh Gaya) – **Dharmachakra Pravartan** (turning of the wheel of Dharma), 6th century BCE.
[5:7] - Other names: **Rishipatana, Isipatana, Mrigadava, Deer Park** (Dhamek Stupa is a monument, not a name).
[5:8] - **Dhamek Stupa** marks the spot of the first sermon (Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta); also **Chaukhandi Stupa**; Sarnath **Ashoka Pillar** – 3rd century BCE.
[5:9] - Finds: **Mauryan Lion Capital** (National Emblem); **Dharmachakrapravartana Buddha** (Gupta art).
[5:10] - Lion Capital: shaft (stambha-yashti), circular carved abacus (elephant, bull, horse, lion with Dharmachakras), inverted lotus petals.
[5:11] - **Sarnath Museum (1904)** – India's **earliest site museum**.
[5:12] - Culture Minister: **Gajendra Singh Shekhawat**.
[5:13] # World Heritage background
[5:14] - **World Heritage Convention 1972**; 196 States; India's tentative list **69 sites**; one nomination per year; nodal **ASI**.
[5:15] - India's sites: **36 cultural** (incl. Sarnath), **7 natural**, **1 mixed** (**Khangchendzonga NP, Sikkim**).
[5:16] - First Indian inscriptions (**1983**): **Agra Fort, Ajanta, Ellora, Taj Mahal**; **Konark 1984**.
