=== Share in GVA, Growth and Jobs – the Mismatch
# Share of output
- **Services = more than 50% of GVA** (Economic Survey 2025-26) – the **highest** contributor to GDP (WBCS 2017: "more than **50%**").
| Sector (2021-22) | Share of GDP | Share of workforce |
|---|---|---|
| Secondary | 24.29% | 22.24% |
| Tertiary | **53.89%** | **33.80%** |
- **Employment-output paradox** (**NITI Aayog**): services give over half of output but only about **one-third** of jobs; high-end services (IT, finance) need high skills, so most new jobs are in low-value trade, hospitality, transport.
- **Regular wages: 51.5%** of service workers vs about 1% in agriculture.
# Share of growth
- **Contribution of services to GVA growth, 2011-12 to 2015-16: about 69%** (CGPSC 2017 key); in 2015-16 alone **66.1%** (Economic Survey 2015-16); 2017-18 about 72.5%.
- Growth: **9.1%** in FY2025-26 (FAE; new series 8.9%); **10%** in Q1 FY2026-27, led by finance, real estate, IT and professional services (**12.1%**).
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 14 p.4-5; Economic Survey 2015-16 Vol.2 ch.7 (services 66.1% of GVA growth); Economic Survey 2025-26; app notes 3:2 sub 1

=== What Is (and Is Not) a Service
| Tertiary (services) | Not services |
|---|---|
| Trade, hotels, restaurants | Mining & quarrying (primary) |
| Transport, storage, communication | Forestry, fishing (primary) |
| Finance, **insurance**, real estate, business services | Manufacturing, **construction**, utilities (secondary) |
| Public administration, defence, other services | Agriculture (primary) |
- **Construction is secondary**, not a service (CDS 2015); transport, hotels and restaurants and insurance are tertiary.
- **NIC** lists **15** types of services; **WTO** uses the UN's **UNCPC** (Central Product Classification); AP Survey uses **8** service sub-sectors.
| Constitution list | Services |
|---|---|
| Union | Telecom, postal, **broadcasting**, financial services, insurance |
| State | **Health, real estate** |
| Concurrent | Education, accounting, engineering, **electricity** |
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC 04 p.36 (NIC 15 types; three Lists); NCERT Class 10 Economics ch.2; app notes 3:2 sub 0

=== Services Exports, FDI and Digital Services
# Exports
- India's share of world services trade: **2% (2005) → 4.3% (2024)**; older target: **4.2% of world services exports by 2022**.
- **Digitally delivered services exports:** India's share of the world total about **6% in 2023** (Economic Survey 2023-24).
- Rank: **8th**-largest services exporter (WTO, April 2025 data); also 8th in 2014.
- 2024-25: exports **USD 387.5 bn** vs imports USD 198.7 bn → surplus; **software and business services = 77.8%** of services exports.
- Target: services exports **USD 1 trillion** within the USD 2 trillion export goal by **2030**.
# FDI
- **1997-2000:** largest FDI share – **services sector** in most books' keys (UPSC 2001; some keys say engineering).
- **Last decade (to 2006):** services sector attracted the highest FDI among chemicals, food processing and telecom; April 2000-December 2025 cumulative: **services 16.38%**, computer software and hardware 15.63%, trading, telecom, automobiles.
- FY2024-25: **services sector** again the **top** FDI equity recipient (~19%, about $9.35 bn), ahead of computer software and hardware (16%).
- Large foreign investment came into **life insurance, banking and automobiles** (UPPCS 2009).
~~ Sources: Economic Survey 2023-24 ch.9 (digitally delivered services 6%); DPIIT FDI Factsheet Dec 2025; app notes 3:2 sub 3

=== Andhra Pradesh and the States
- **AP:** services = **AP's largest sector** – about **43%** of GVA (current prices) (APPSC DL 2020).
- Yet AP is **basically an agricultural economy** (APPSC Panchayat Secretary): agriculture is its main employer, so the success of its Five Year Plans depended mainly on **agriculture** (APPSC G2 2011).
- AP 2025-26: services **Rs 3,59,931 crore (+10.67%)**; **communications** fastest (**15.13%**).
- **Villaments** (villa + apartment) = new urban-housing trend in AP real estate (APPSC G2 Mains 2019).
- Kerala **64.3%**, **Bihar 58.7%** of GSVA from services; Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Telangana = about **40%** of India's services output.
- Largest services share in State GDP, 2008-09: **Delhi** > Chandigarh > Kerala > Maharashtra; 2023-24 (NITI Aayog, Oct 2025): Chandigarh 88.8%, Delhi 84.1%.
~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 (GVA by sector); NITI Aayog "India's Services Sector" (Oct 2025); app notes 3:2 sub 6

=== Service-led Growth and the 1991 Reforms
- Boom began in the **mid-1980s**; sped up after the **1990s reforms** – services gained most from globalisation.
- The 1991 reforms **did not bring much benefit to agriculture** (public investment in agriculture fell; APPSC ASO 2017) – unlike services, stock markets and power.
- **Software** is a major export, but India **lacks a strong hardware base** (UPSC 1999: A true, R false).
- **EAC-PM Chairman** (Nov 2025): service-led growth cannot last without a **manufacturing base**; manufacturing creates demand for logistics, IT, finance and steadies GDP cycles.
- **Paradox of thrift** – popularised by **Keynes**.
~~ Sources: NCERT Class 11 Indian Economic Development ch.3 (reforms and agriculture); NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 14 p.9; app notes 3:2 sub 2

=== Indices and Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| PMI | Survey of purchasing managers; indicator of the health of the **manufacturing** (and services) sector; above 50 = expansion, below 50 = contraction |
| Index of Services Production (ISP) | MoSPI's first trial (for **April 2026**) – services counterpart of the **IIP**; monthly, within **60 days**; base **2024-25**; Laspeyres index |
| GCC | Offshore unit of an MNC doing its **own** core work (not a third-party vendor); **over 1,800** in India |
