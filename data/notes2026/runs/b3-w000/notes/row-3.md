=== Revenue and Export Growth
- 1990s boom: software exports grew **over 50% a year after 1995-96** because Indian firms were **cost-effective with international quality** (UPSC 2001: A and R true, R explains A); IX Plan (1997-2002) CAGR **52%** (exports 57%).
- Question-period data (UPSC 2003): exports CAGR about **60%** over five years ✓; software and services growth about **28%** in rupee terms in 2001-02 ✓ → **both**.
- Software services exports **2022-23: US $185.5 bn** (RBI, +18.4%); computer services 67.3%, ITES/BPO 32.7%.
- **FY2025 (NASSCOM):** revenue **USD 282.6 bn** (+5.1%); exports **USD 224.4 bn** (~**79%**); domestic USD 58.2 bn.
- Software/computer services = India's **largest services export** and **largest services forex earner** → offsets the **merchandise trade deficit**.
~~ Sources: Economic Survey 2002-03 (software exports); RBI Bulletin Oct 2023; NASSCOM Strategic Review 2025; app notes 3:3 sub 1

=== Origins – Policies and Institutions
| Policy / institution | Year | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Department of Electronics | 1970 | Foresaw India's export potential in **software** as early as the mid-1970s |
| Software Export Scheme | **1972** | Computers imported against an export obligation |
| Computer Policy / Software Export Policy | 1984 / **1986** | Liberalised imports and data links |
| NASSCOM | **1988** | Industry body; annual Strategic Review = data source |
| STPI | **1991** | Under the Department of Electronics; satellite data links + single-window clearances; India's **first export-park model** for software |
| NPIT 2012 | 2012 | Targets for 2020 |
| MeitY | separate ministry **2016** | Earlier **DeitY** |
- **NPIT 2012** targets (2020): revenue USD 100 bn → 300 bn; exports USD 69 bn → **200 bn**; 10 million more skilled workers; **one e-literate person in every household** (not two).

=== Employment
- **~58 lakh (5.8 million)** direct jobs (FY2025); mostly **organised** jobs with contracts and social security; high share of women.
- **Quarterly Employment Survey (QES)** – **Labour Bureau**; **9 non-farm sectors**; in 2021 rounds **manufacturing** had the largest share (~39-41%); IT/BPO ~7% but fastest growth (152%).
~~ Sources: NASSCOM Strategic Review 2025; Labour Bureau QES 2021; app notes 3:3 sub 2

=== Challenges and Role in Development
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| External dependence | **Visa rules**, protectionism, **client spending cycles** |
| Automation | **Routine ITES/BPM** most exposed to generative AI |
| Jobs paradox | Fast GVA growth, few jobs – **skill-intensive and automated** |
| Other risks | Urban concentration, Bengaluru water stress, Chennai floods 2015, digital divide |
- IT **earns forex and formal jobs** but serves a **skilled few in a few cities** → cannot deliver broad development alone; a forex shortage is **not** one of its problems.
- Skill gap: employers want AI-ready skills – cloud, GenAI, data analytics (May 2026).
~~ Sources: app notes 3:3 sub 4

=== Digital India and Data Protection
- **Digital India**: launched **1 July 2015** (MeitY); **nine pillars**, first = **Broadband Highways**.
- **BharatNet**: renamed from **NOFN** (2015); optical fibre to about 2.5 lakh Gram Panchayats.
- **IndiaAI Mission**: Cabinet approval March 2024, outlay **Rs 10,372 crore**.
- **DPDP Act 2023**: assent **11 August 2023**; **Data Protection Board of India** imposes penalties up to **Rs 250 crore**; **DPDP Rules 2025** notified November 2025.
