## SUB 1 | Insurance Terms and Principles
[1:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [1:0.0] Insurance penetration | Total premium as % of GDP – not "insured persons per 100 people" (APPSC G2 2017 → none of these)
  [1:0.1] Insurance density | Premium per capita (USD 97 in FY25)
  [1:0.2] Principle of indemnity | Restores the insured to the pre-loss position, no profit; applies to fire, marine, motor, health – not life insurance
  [1:0.3] Actuary | Insurance expert who judges risk and estimates the premium
  [1:0.4] Cyber insurance | Data breach, hacking, ransomware, identity theft
  [1:0.5] Aviation hull | Physical damage to the aircraft itself
  [1:0.6] P&I | Shipowners' third-party liabilities; first sovereign-backed cover: Bharat Maritime Insurance Pool (Jul 2026)
[1:1] ~~ Sources: Economic Survey 2025-26 Summary - Part 1 p.33-34 (penetration, density); NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC 01 p.33; app notes 3:4 sub 1

## SUB 5 | Para-banking
[5:0] - Legal basis: **Section 6, Banking Regulation Act, 1949**, with **RBI** approval; run **departmentally or through subsidiaries** (subsidiary route: **Section 19(1)**).
[5:1] - Para-banking = **credit cards, insurance** (as **corporate agent** or **joint venture**), **factoring**, equipment leasing, mutual funds – **not** "credit to paramilitary personnel" or issuing currency.
[5:2] - Bank credit-deposit ratio: 62-year high of 82.6% in Q1 FY27 (Jul 2026).
