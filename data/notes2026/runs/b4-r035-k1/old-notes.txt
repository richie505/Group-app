## SUB 1 | Physiographic Divisions
[35:1:0] - **Six divisions** (relief and structure): **Himalayas, Northern Plains, Peninsular Plateau, Indian Desert, Coastal Plains, Islands**.
[35:1:1] TABLE head: Division | Key features
  [35:1:1.0] Himalayas | Young fold mountains; separate the subcontinent from the rest of Asia; world's highest range
  [35:1:1.1] Northern Plains | Alluvial; Bhabar, Terai, Bhangar, Khadar; youngest division
  [35:1:1.2] Peninsular Plateau | Ancient stable crystalline landmass; largest and oldest (from Gondwana Land); rich in metallic minerals
  [35:1:1.3] Indian Desert | West of the Aravallis; Thar, dunes, Rann
  [35:1:1.4] Coastal Plains | Narrow in west, broad in east
  [35:1:1.5] Islands | A&N (tectonic/volcanic), Lakshadweep (coral)
[35:1:2] - Types of plains: **alluvial** (Indo-Gangetic), **flood**, **deltaic** (Ganga-Brahmaputra Delta – world's major delta), **coastal**, **loess** (wind-blown silt, China), **outwash** (glacial meltwater).
[35:1:3] - **Chambal**: ravines and badlands (gully erosion). **Sahara** is not in the subcontinent.
[35:1:4] - **Paleomagnetic** results show the Indian landmass moved **northward**.
## SUB 2 | Northern Plains
[35:2:0] # Belts from Hills to Plains
[35:2:1] TABLE head: Belt | Feature
  [35:2:1.0] Bhabar | Coarse gravel and pebbles at the foot of the Shiwaliks; streams sink underground
  [35:2:1.1] Terai | Marshy, waterlogged, forested belt where Bhabar streams re-emerge
  [35:2:1.2] Bhangar | Older alluvium above flood level; kankar nodules
  [35:2:1.3] Khadar | Newer alluvium of floodplains, renewed yearly
[35:2:2] # Regions
[35:2:3] - Area **6,52,000 sq km**; 2,400 km long (W–E); 70–500 km wide; northern edge = **Himalayan Frontal Fault**.
[35:2:4] - Gradient: land drops only ~**200 m** over **1,600 km** from Delhi to the Bay.
[35:2:5] - **Punjab Plain**: formed by **Indus tributaries**; has **doabs**.
[35:2:6] - **Ganga Plain**: ~3,75,000 sq km; alluvium 2,000–3,000 m thick. Divisions: **Ganga–Yamuna Doab** (upper >200 m, middle 100–200 m, lower <100 m), **Rohilkhand, Avadh, Bihar, Bengal** plains – **not Malwa** (a plateau).
[35:2:7] - **Brahmaputra Plain**: Assam valley; river enters Bangladesh.
[35:2:8] - **Kosi Plain** (north Bihar): huge alluvial fan / **megafan** with shifting channels; Kosi = "**Sorrow of Bihar**".
[35:2:9] - **Molassis Basin**: **Mizoram** (Purvanchal); soft loose deposits; common language Lushai.
## SUB 3 | Indian Desert and the Rann of Kutch
[35:3:0] - **Thar**: west of the **Aravallis**; sandy rolling plain; rainfall **below 150 mm**.
[35:3:1] - **Marusthali** = true desert (west); **Bagar** = semi-arid strip (east) along the Aravallis.
[35:3:2] - Features: **barchan and seif** dunes, salt lakes (**Sambhar** saline), desertification spreading north.
[35:3:3] - Order of features (APPSC 2025): arid climate → **sand dunes** → **thorny bushes** → **seasonal streams** only with rain.
[35:3:4] - Inland drainage: Luni, Machhu, Rupen, Saraswati, Banas, Ghaggar – end in salt lakes or are lost in sand.
[35:3:5] - **Luni**: only significant river; rises in the **Aravalli** (south-west of Ajmer); **511 km**; seasonal; lost in the **Rann of Kutch** – does not reach the sea.
[35:3:6] # Rann of Kutch
[35:3:7] - **Seasonal salt marsh** flooded in the monsoon; a lowland; **Great Rann** (north) and **Little Rann** (south); Kachchh (Gujarat) and Sindh (Pakistan).
[35:3:8] - Formation: once a **shallow arm of the Arabian Sea** / Kutch an island amid seas and lagoons; repeated falls in sea level and aridity made it a salt plain – best example of such marshland.
[35:3:9] - **Indian Wild Ass Sanctuary**; rich in solar energy. **Banni grasslands** (edge of the salt marsh) – Gujarat.
[35:3:10] - **Agaria / Agariya**: traditional salt-pan workers of the Little Rann.
## SUB 4 | Peninsular Plateau
[35:4:0] # Structure
[35:4:1] - **Gondwana shield** (Precambrian); triangular block of igneous and metamorphic rocks; about **3,600 million years** old.
[35:4:2] - **Tilts east** → most rivers flow east.
[35:4:3] - Coal formed in the **Carboniferous** (Damodar, Son, Mahanadi, Godavari basins).
[35:4:4] - **S.P. Chatterjee**: Peninsula in **8** parts – North Central Highlands, South Central Highlands, Eastern Plateau, Meghalaya Plateau, North Deccan (has Mahanadi Basin), South Deccan, Western Ghats, Eastern Ghats.
[35:4:5] - **Horst**: Satpura (between Narmada and Tapi); Aravalli = Archaean horst.
[35:4:6] # Sub-divisions

  [35:4:7.1] Malwa Plateau | Western MP, SE Rajasthan; Deccan Trap basalt, black soils | Between Aravalli (north) and Vindhya (south); Bundelkhand on its east; ~1,50,000 sq km

  [35:4:7.8] Coimbatore | Tamil Nadu | Southern plateau

[35:4:9] - **Damodar does not rise at Amarkantak**.
[35:4:10] - Dandakaranya keys: Chhattisgarh and Odisha, and Andhra Pradesh.
[35:4:11] # Deccan Traps
[35:4:12] - **Fissure eruptions** of basaltic lava, end-**Cretaceous** to early Eocene; 'Trap' = **Swedish for step**.
[35:4:13] - Area once ~**10 lakh sq km**, now ~**5 lakh sq km** (Kuchchh, Saurashtra, Maharashtra, Malwa, N. Karnataka).
[35:4:14] - Thickness **3,000 m in the west → 150 m in the east**.
[35:4:15] - Weathers into **regur** (black cotton soil).
[35:4:16] # Vindhya, Satpura and Central Ranges
[35:4:17] - **Vindhya**: north of the Narmada; **Mukandara Hills** = extension in SE Rajasthan.
[35:4:18] - **Satpura** ("Seven Folds"): between **Narmada and Tapi**; southern boundary of the Narmada valley; eastern Gujarat through Maharashtra–MP border to Chhattisgarh; **Tapti lies south** of it. Highest **Dhupgarh (1,350 m)**, Pachmarhi, **Mahadeo** hills.
[35:4:19] - **Narmada Valley**: between Vindhya and Satpura.
[35:4:20] - Vindhya and Satpura converge at **Amarkantak** (Maikal range) – not Yercaud (Shevaroy) or Sahyadri.
[35:4:21] - **Maikal Range**: eastern end of the Satpura; MP–Chhattisgarh; Ramgiri hills part of it.
[35:4:22] - West → east: **Satpura, Mahadeo, Maikal, Chotanagpur**; Barwani, Mahadeo, Maikal; Mahadeo, Maikal, Chotanagpur, Khasi.
[35:4:23] - North → south: **Mahadeo Hills, Satpura Range, Sahyadri**.
[35:4:24] - **Kaimur**: MP, UP, Bihar. **Dalma Hills**: Jharkhand. **Ajanta Range**: only Maharashtra (Aravalli, Sahyadri, Satpura span several States).
[35:4:25] - **Dandakaranya:** a highland of the **central-eastern** part of the Peninsula (Chhattisgarh–Odisha–Andhra Pradesh region).
