=== Soilless and Protected Cultivation – Hydroponics, Aeroponics, Phytotron
| Technique | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Hydroponics** | Growing plants **without soil**, roots in **mineral nutrient solution** (sand, gravel or water may support them); still needs water, light, nutrients; ~90% less water |
| **Aeroponics** | Roots **suspended in air** and **misted** – no soil medium |
| Aquaponics | Fish culture + hydroponics; fish waste feeds plants |
| Vertical farming | Stacked indoor layers, year-round output where land is scarce |
| Greenhouse / polyhouse | Controlled microclimate; protection from weather, pests and diseases |
| **Phytotron** | Enclosed research greenhouse to **grow plants under controlled conditions** |
- **Aeroponics limits disease transmission** (less plant-to-plant contact, oxygen-rich roots) – so "disease transmission is its major disadvantage" is **incorrect**; aeroponic plants have **high dry-weight biomass** and need less nutrient input than hydroponics.
- "Hydrophonics" in old papers = hydroponics, i.e. growth **without soil**, not without water.
> Exam angle: hydroponics definition (×5), aeroponics INCORRECT statement, phytotron.

=== Micro-irrigation and Fertigation
- **Localised (micro) irrigation:** water piped under low pressure and given in small discharges at each plant – **drip, micro-sprinkler, bubbler**; **conserves water** and **reduces water consumption** (vs flood, canal or open-field watering).
| Method | Efficiency | How water is applied |
|---|---|---|
| **Drip** | ~90% (highest) | To the root zone through emitters |
| Sprinkler | 70-80% | Through nozzles like rain; loses more to wind and evaporation |
| Flood / furrow | 30-50% | Surface methods |
- Drip limit = **higher initial cost**; Evergreen Revolution ("more output at lower ecological cost") – **M.S. Swaminathan**.
# Fertigation
- **Fertigation = applying fertiliser through irrigation water** (through the drip line); not chemigation (pesticides) or "microgation".
- Advantages (**UPSC 2020: I, III and IV**): **controls alkalinity** of irrigation water, **more nutrients available**, **less leaching**. Efficient application of **rock phosphate** is **not** possible (it is insoluble).
> Exam angle: micro-irrigation conserves water (×2), fertigation definition and advantages.

=== Resource-conserving Technologies and Crop Practices
| Technology | Benefit |
|---|---|
| **Zero tillage** | New crop sown into previous residue; **soil carbon sequestration**, more earthworms, less runoff |
| Zero/reduced tillage + **crop residue on field** | **Conserves water** (UPSC 2017: 1 and 3); **gypsum** before irrigation does **not** |
| Contour bunding | Soil conservation on slopes |
| Relay cropping | Next crop sown before the standing crop is harvested |
| Laser land levelling | Uniform field; saves 20-25% irrigation water |
| SRI (alternate wetting and drying) | Less seed, water and methane |
| Direct-seeded rice | No nursery or transplanting; promoted with zero-till wheat (NICRA) |
| Sustainable Sugarcane Initiative | **Single-bud chips / bud-chip settlings** |
- **Carbon sequestration (UPSC 2012):** of contour bunding, relay cropping and zero tillage, **only zero tillage** (most keys).
- **Sugarcane (UPSC 2020: 1 and 4):** **bud-chip settlings** raised in a nursery save seed material; **tissue-culture settlings** can be used. Single-budded setts germinate worse and survive worse than multi-bud setts in bad weather.
- **Onion** is grown by **transplanting** nursery seedlings (maize, sorghum, soyabean are sown directly).
- **Bio-drainage:** fast-transpiring trees such as **Eucalyptus** pump out excess water from waterlogged land.
> Exam angle: UPSC statement questions on zero tillage, water conservation, sugarcane; onion transplanting; bio-drainage plant.

=== Irrigation Schemes – PMKSY, Per Drop More Crop and APMIP
- **PMKSY** (Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana): launched **1 July 2015 (2015-16)**; motto **"Har Khet Ko Pani"** + **"Per Drop More Crop"**; aims to **enhance physical access of water on farm**, **expand assured irrigation**, **reduce wastage and raise water-use efficiency**, and achieve **convergence of investment in irrigation at field level**; creates protected irrigation by **harnessing rainwater at micro level** (watershed component).
| Component | Fact |
|---|---|
| AIBP, Har Khet Ko Pani | Ministry of Jal Shakti (DoWR) |
| **Per Drop More Crop (PDMC)** | Agriculture Ministry; drip and sprinkler; under PMKSY 2015-16 to 2021-22, under **RKVY from 2022-23** |
| Watershed Development (WDC-PMKSY 2.0) | Department of Land Resources (Rural Development) |
| Micro Irrigation Fund | Rs 10,000 crore corpus with **NABARD**; operational 2019-20 |
- PDMC: **102 lakh ha** under micro-irrigation (2015-16 to 2025-26); priority to rainfed, water-stressed and critical groundwater blocks; NMSA subsidy up to 55%, capped at 5 ha.
- Gross irrigated area **41.7% (2001-02) → 55.8%** of GCA (2022-23); Soil Health Cards issued: **25.55 crore** (Nov 2025).
# Andhra Pradesh Micro Irrigation Project (APMIP)
- AP Micro Irrigation programme introduced **2003**; APMIP now implemented State-wide under **PMKSY**; **AP ranked 1st** in micro-irrigation (2024-25), led by **Ananthapuramu**.
| Farmer category (drip) | Subsidy |
|---|---|
| SC/ST small and marginal | 100% |
| Other small and marginal | 90% |
| 5-10 acres | 70% |
| Above 10 acres | 50% |
- **7 Jan 2026**: automation-based micro-irrigation at **Governorpet, Vijayawada** – Minister **K. Atchannaidu**; APSES 2024-25 focus: precision farming, drones, IoT.

=== Precision Agriculture, Remote Sensing and Crop Monitoring
- **Precision farming:** data-driven use of **GIS, GPS, remote sensing**, sensors and drones to apply water, fertiliser and pesticide where and when needed; tools include fertigation, greenhouse, mulching, micropropagation, high-density planting, drip.
| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| **Mahalanobis National Crop Forecast Centre** (2012, Agriculture Ministry) | Institutionalises **ISRO's** crop forecasting (FASAL) and drought/disaster methodology |
| KISAN project (Oct 2015) | Space tech for crop-cutting experiments under PMFBY |
| GIS | Soil and Land Use Survey of India maps soils |
| Variable-rate technology | Inputs at varying rates within a field by sensor/GIS maps |
| GPS, drones, AI, sensors | Second Green Revolution / sustainable intensification |
- **Crop logging** = **plant (tissue) analysis** to assess the crop's nutrient requirement during growth (developed for sugarcane), not soil testing.
> Exam angle: MNCFC (ISRO methodology), crop logging definition.

=== Drones, Digital Agriculture Mission, Solar Pumps and AI
- **Kisan drones**: crop assessment, soil-health analysis, spraying; regulated by **DGCA** under the **Drone Rules, 2021**; spraying SOPs released by **Narendra Singh Tomar** (21 Dec 2021).
- **Drone Shakti**: manufacturing and **Remote Pilot Certificate** training.
- **Namo Drone Didi**: drones to **women SHGs**, target **15,000**, outlay **Rs 1,261 crore**, **80%** central assistance (max Rs 8 lakh).
- **Drone Corporation of AP**: State-owned, **drone-as-a-service**; spraying in all 26 districts (Jan 2026).
| Digital Agriculture Mission | Fact |
|---|---|

| Outlay | **Rs 2,817 crore** |
