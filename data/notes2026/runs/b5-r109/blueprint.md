## Trends
- **Row 109 is mostly one-line recall of chemistry and biology facts** that UPPSC, CDS and SSC repeat (urea, DAP, BGA, Azolla, Bt, repellents). APPSC prefers the statement and "sustainable practice" style, and asks the same I-IV statement question across three papers (2022-2025).
- **AP angle:** APCNF (full form, Year of Natural Farming 2018, Gulbenkian Prize 2024) and PKVY. APCNF also won the **Food Planet Prize 2026**, which is the likely next question.
- **UPSC sets** (biochar 2020, mycorrhiza 2013, NPOP 2018, ZBNF 2018, neem 2014, fertigation 2020, zero tillage 2012/2017, sugarcane 2020, SSI 2014) are reused verbatim by State PSCs.
- **Weak keys found:** the UPSC 2014 neem question was printed with two different keys (all three; 1 and 3); the official key is 1 and 3. APPSC Group I 2010's "mosquito repellents contain mainly" gives only fertiliser salts as options (the stem looks garbled from a superphosphate question), so an option was added. Aluminium phosphide is keyed "insecticide" though it is used mainly as a grain fumigant. The 2012 carbon-sequestration question is split between "3 only" and "all three".
