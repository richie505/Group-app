=== Army and Navy
- **Army:** **elephants, cavalry, infantry** (the three limbs) **and a navy** – **chariots were not part of it** (Police Constable 2016).
- **Naval power:** the **Cholas** were the South Indian state famous for naval power (UPPSC 2004 ×2); **Rajaraja I** built the navy.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Kaikkolar** | Royal infantry regiments |
| **Velaikkarar** | Bodyguards sworn to die with the king |
| **Kadagams** | Military cantonments |
- Rajaraja I's navy destroyed the Chera fleet at **Trivandrum (Kandalur)**; Rajendra I's 1025 fleet took **Kadaram = Kedah**; the Bay of Bengal became a **"Chola Lake"**.

=== Kingship and Chola Records
- **Successor named in the king's lifetime:** the **Cholas** – the yuvaraja was made **co-regent** (UPPSC 2001); Rajendra I co-regent **1012**, king 1014.
- **⚠ Trap:** "the successor was named only after the king's death" is false.
- **King's image:** titles *ko*, *perumal*, *rajarajadhiraja*; praised as warrior, patron of arts and protector of **varnashrama dharma**; Rajaraja I = **Ulakalanda Perumal** – measured the earth like **Vishnu's three strides**.
- **Royal orders:** a **Sanskrit preamble** (genealogy, conquests) + a **Tamil operative part**.
- **Temple-wall records:** Rajaraja I and Rajendra I had their victories inscribed on **temple walls** – the main reason we know far more about the Cholas than their predecessors.
> A-R (UPPSC 2020): A "more information about the Cholas" ✓; R "inscriptions on temple walls narrating victories" ✓ and **explains A**.
| Record | Point |
|---|---|
| Anbil plates | **Sundara Chola**; Sanskrit genealogy |
| Tiruvalangadu plates | **Rajendra I** – genealogy and conquests |
| Larger Leiden (Anaimangalam) | Rajaraja I's grant to the **Chulamanivarma-vihara** of the **Srivijaya** king at Nagapattinam |
| Smaller Leiden | **3 sheets**; Kulottunga I's extension of the grant after an appeal by two envoys |

=== Land Classes, Taniyur and Revenue
# Land classes
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Eripatti** | Land whose revenue maintained the **village tank** |
| **Brahmadeya** | Rent-free land gifted to **Brahmins** (sabha villages) |
| **Vellanvagai** | Land of non-Brahmin peasant proprietors (ur villages) |
| **Devadana** / Tirunamattukkani | Land to **temples** |
| **Shalabhoga** | Land for a **school** |
| **Pallichchandam** | Land to **Jaina** institutions |
| **Ghatikas** | **Colleges attached to temples** |
- **Taniyur:** a **very large village** (an important brahmadeya or nagaram) **administered as a single, separate unit**, outside the nadu (APPSC G1) – not kurram, kottam or nadu.
- **UPSC 2016 pairs:** Eripatti ✓, Ghatikas ✓; "Taniyurs = villages donated to a single Brahmin or group of Brahmins" ✗ → **A and C (1 and 3)**.
- **Land rights (kani):** **karanmai** = right to cultivate; **mitachi** = superior possessory right; **kaniyudaiyar** = land-owning farmers; **ulukudi** = tenants.
# Revenue
- **Rate:** land revenue normally **1/6 of the produce** (like the Pallavas and Kakatiyas); collected by village assemblies in cash, kind or both; **Rajaraja I's land survey**.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Kadamai** | Land revenue **in kind** |
| **Antarayam** | Rural tax **in cash** |
| **Eccoru** | Duty to **feed visiting officials** |
| **Nettal** | Compulsory labour |
| **Vetti, Muttaiyal, Kudimai** | Forced labour services |
| Irai / Ayam | Land tax / royal dues |
- **Remission:** exemptions granted through the **ur**; **Kulottunga I = Sungam Tavirtta Chola** (abolished tolls).
- **Modern parallel:** **DILRMP** first phase ran **2008–2016**.

=== Officials and Provinces
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Perundaram / **Sirudaram** | Highest / **lower** officials |
| **Tirumandira-olai** | Wrote the king's oral orders on palm leaf |
| **Olai-nayagam** | Checked and countersigned orders |
| **Adhikari** | Royal officer supervising local bodies |
| **Nattar** | Members of the nadu assembly |
- **Order (small to large):** **Ur → Nadu (kurram) → Kottam / Valanadu → Mandalam** – a kottam is above the nadu, not below it.
- **Eight mandalams**, each under a viceroy, often a **prince**; officials paid in **revenue-bearing land**.
- **Samanta:** a defeated king restored after accepting overlordship, tribute and military duty; tribute-paying lands = **samanta (vassal) territories**.
| Mandalam | Region |
|---|---|
| Cholamandalam | Core Kaveri country |
| **Jayankondacholamandalam** | Former Pallava **Tondaimandalam** |
| **Mummudicholamandalam** | **Northern Sri Lanka** |
- **Vengi:** Kulottunga I's sons governed as **Rajapratinidhi**.

=== Irrigation and the Temple
- **Eri variyam:** managed village tanks and water distribution; repairs paid from **eripatti** land.
- **Kallanai (Grand Anicut)** on the Kaveri = **Karikala** (Sangam); **tank irrigation** was a major feature of the Chola economy (also Vijayanagara, Kakatiyas).
- **Araghatta:** a water wheel with earthen pots tied to its rim, used for irrigation.
- **Temple:** biggest landholder, employer and lender, and the **meeting place of the village assembly**; Rajaraja I made the Cholamandalam sabhas serve the **Brihadeeswara, Thanjavur**.
