# Style
- **Vesara** in exam keys (CGPSC 2023: Hoysaleshwara = Vesara) – hybrid, neither fully Nagara nor Dravida; Kandariya Mahadeva is Nagara.
- **Plan:** **stellate (star-shaped)** on a raised **jagati** (~1 m); the plan began under the **Chalukyas**; best preserved at **Keshava, Somanathapura**.
- **Stone:** soft **chlorite schist (soapstone)**; **lathe-turned, polished** pillars (not rough); **Madanika** bracket figures at **Belur**.
- **Sculptors:** **Jakanachari** – Belur; **Mallitamma** – Somanathapura.

=== Rulers – Vishnuvardhana, Ramanuja and the Ballalas
- **Region:** most of **Karnataka**, parts of Tamil Nadu; feudatories of the **Western Chalukyas** (early chief **Nripa Kama II**).
- **Rise:** independent by the **close of the 12th century**; ended at the **beginning of the 14th** – none of Hoysala, Gahadavala, Kakatiya, Yadava arose in the early 8th century (UPSC 2023: **none**).
- **Vishnuvardhana** (original name **Bittideva / Bittiga**, from 1108): fought Vikramaditya VI and the Cholas; took **Talakad** from the Cholas → **Talakadugonda**; built Belur (1117).
- **Ramanuja:** fled **Chola** persecution; Vishnuvardhana converted from **Jainism to Vaishnavism** under him; queen **Shantala Devi** stayed Jain.
- **Aug 2026:** a **Veeragallu** (hero stone) at **Neeragunda** (Arsikere) may settle the date of Vishnuvardhana's death.

=== Malik Kafur and the End of the Hoysalas
- **Malik Kafur's victims:** Yadava **Ramachandra** of Devagiri (**1307**, taken to Delhi), Kakatiya Prataparudra (1309–10), Hoysala **Vira Ballala III** (Dwarasamudra, **1310–11**), then the Pandyas.
- **G1 1990-91 key:** "defeated by Malik Kafur" = **Yadava Ramachandra**; Vira Ballala III also submitted, Rudramadevi had died in 1289.
- **Wars:** constant Hoysala–Yadava wars – **Singhana** defeated the Hoysalas.
- **End:** Ballala III was killed by **Ghiyas-ud-din**, Sultan of Madurai, at **Kannanur** (1342–43); Hoysala land passed to **Vijayanagara** (by 1346).
