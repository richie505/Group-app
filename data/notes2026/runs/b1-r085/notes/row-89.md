=== Chola Bronzes – Nataraja
- **Technique:** **lost-wax (cire perdue)** casting; bronze images were already cast in the **Pallava** period (8th–9th c.), but the **Chola** period (10th–12th c.) produced the finest; queen **Sembiyan Mahadevi** was a key patron.
- **Nataraja period:** the bronze Nataraja developed under the **Imperial Cholas** (APPSC G1 2002; APPSC G1 PYQ; CGPSC 2013 "Chola art") – not the Pandyas, Cheras, Pallavas, Gandhara, Mathura or Mauryan art.
- **Greatest creation** of South Indian sthapatis: **Nataraja** (IAS 1993) – not Mahishasuramardini, Rama or Somaskanda.
- **APPSC "cire perdue Nataraja" PYQ:** options lacked the Cholas; of those given, the **Pallavas** (earliest South Indian bronzes) is the defensible answer (Ikshvakus, Vishnukundins, Eastern Chalukyas did not make them).
| Element | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Four hands** (Ananda Tandava) | Standard form (UPSC 1995; UPRO 2021) |
| Damaru (upper right) | **Creation** – first sound |
| Agni (upper left) | **Destruction** |
| Abhaya mudra (lower right) | **Protection** |
| Lower left hand | Points to the raised foot – liberation |
| Dwarf **Apasmara** under the right foot | **Ignorance** |
| Man's earring right, woman's left | Fusion of male and female principles |
> Statements (UPRO 2021): "four hands" ✓; "man's earring right, woman's left" ✓ → **both**.

=== Temple Life – Dance, Devadasis, Colleges
- **Dance and music:** **Rajaraja I** appointed about **400 dancing girls** (with musicians and staff, paid by grants) at the Thanjavur temple; hymns of the Alvars and Nayanars sung in temples.
- **Devadasis** ("female servants of God"): unmarried women dedicated to temple service, music and dance.
- **Devadasis in the news (UPSC 1996 ×2):** the **Jagannath temple, Puri**, whose Devadasis (**Maharis**) served its rituals – not Pashupatinath, Kandariya Mahadev or Chausath Yogini.
- **Vedic colleges:** Chola inscriptions record temple colleges at **Ennayiram** and **Tribhuvani** (near Pondicherry, South Arcot) (APPSC G2 2000; APPSC PYQ) – Chola, not Pallava or Pandya.
- **Uttaramerur (Parantaka I):** important for the **kudavolai** election system.

=== Chola Temples and the Dravida Style
- **Dravida style** = South India (APPSC G1 2008); reached its **peak under the Cholas**; continued under the Kalyani Chalukyas and Hoysalas.
- "The **Pallavas laid the foundation**, the **Cholas** developed it on a monumental scale"; early Chola temples (**Vijayalaya's Narthamalai**, Pudukottai region) are of stone.
- **Gopurams:** the mighty **gateways** of South Indian temples (State PSC) – not shikharas or mandapams.
| Temple | Builder | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| Koranganatha, **Srinivasanallur** | **Parantaka I** | Early Chola |
| **Brihadeeswara (Rajarajeswara, Dakshina Meru), Thanjavur** | **Rajaraja I**, 1010 | **Shiva**; granite; vimana **216 ft**; capstone ~**80 tonnes**; largest and tallest in Tamil Nadu |
| Gangaikondacholapuram | **Rajendra I** | Vimana **180 ft**, concave curvilinear profile |
| Airavatesvara, **Darasuram** | **Rajaraja II** | Horse-drawn stone chariot; Musical Steps |
| Kampaharesvara, **Tribhuvanam** | **Kulottunga III** | Late Chola |
| Sun temple, Kumbakonam | Kulottunga I | First of its kind in the south |
- **⚠ Trap (CDS 2009):** Brihadeeswara is a **Shiva** temple (huge linga; Tripurantaka) – "dedicated to Vishnu" is the wrong statement.
- **Chidambaram** Nataraja temple: enlarged by the Cholas; **Chit Sabha** with **108 dance poses**; **not** in the UNESCO group.
- **UNESCO "Great Living Chola Temples":** Thanjavur (**1987**) + Gangaikondacholapuram and **Darasuram** (2004).
- **Order:** Pallava Shore temple → Brihadeeswara → **Gangaikondacholapuram** (latest of the three).
- **Frescoes:** Brihadeeswara ambulatory – Shiva as **Tripurantaka**, **Rajaraja I with guru Karuvur Thevar**; Narthamalai paintings – Shiva and Parvati.

=== Temple Styles and Andhra Temples
| Style | Tower | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Nagara** (north, from c. 5th c.) | Curvilinear **shikhara** | Khajuraho (Kandariya Mahadeva), Lingaraj |
| **Dravida** | Pyramidal **vimana**, gopurams, prakara | Kailasanatha, Brihadeeswara |
| **Vesara** | Hybrid; started by the **Badami Chalukyas** | Hoysala, Rashtrakuta temples |
# Temple terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Garbhagriha** | Womb-house with the principal icon |
| **Vimana** | Pyramidal tower over the garbhagriha |
| **Shikhara** | Dome-like crown of the vimana |
| **Antarala** | Vestibule between sanctum and mandapa |
| **Mandapa** | Pillared hall |
| **Prakara** | Enclosure walls |
- **Mixed:** **Teli temple, Gwalior** – Dravida pinnacle with Nagara ornament.
- **Pairs:** Kailasanathar, Kanchi = **Rajasimha** style; Virupaksha = **Hampi**; **Dilwara** = Mount Abu (not Morena); **Dashavatara, Devgarh** = **Gupta**; Jagannath, Puri founded by **Anantavarman Chodaganga**.
# Andhra
- **Kapoteswara temple:** **Chejarla** (Palnadu district) – a Buddhist apsidal shrine, later Shaiva (APPSC Welfare 2017 ×2; APPSC PYQ) – not Nallajerla, Velpur, Srikakulam or Alampur.
- **Draksharama Bhimeswara** = **Chalukya-Chola** style; **Padmakshi, Hanamkonda** = Kakatiya (not Pallava).
