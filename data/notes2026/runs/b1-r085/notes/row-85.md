=== Naval Expeditions – Sri Lanka, Maldives, Srivijaya and the "Chola Lake"
# Who conquered what overseas
| Ruler | Naval / overseas conquest |
|---|---|
| **Rajaraja I** (985–1014) | Built the Chola **navy**; destroyed the **Chera navy at Kandalur Salai** (Trivandrum); **northern Sri Lanka** (Anuradhapura) after defeating **Mahinda V**; the **Maldives** |
| **Rajendra I** (1012–1044) | **Whole of Sri Lanka** – captured Mahinda V and his royal insignia; **Srivijaya** expedition (**1025**) – took **Kadaram (Kedah)** and places in Malaya and Sumatra |
| Kulottunga I | **Kalinga** wars (land) |
- **Navy founder:** **Rajaraja I** was the first Chola king to create a naval army (CGPSC 2014; UPPSC 2004).
- **Northern Sri Lanka:** **Rajaraja I** – made it the province **Mummudicholamandalam** (UPPSC 2014; "province of his empire" PYQ).
- **"Conquered Ceylon":** **Rajendra I** completed the conquest of the whole island (IAS 2001 key); Rajaraja I had taken only the north.
  - Sri Lanka stayed Chola for about half a century until **Vijayabahu I** drove the Cholas out (c. 1070).
# The Srivijaya expedition (1025 CE)
- **Leader:** **Rajendra I** (UPSC 2025; APPSC G2 2000, 2003; APSLPRB SI 2019) – not Rajaraja I, Rajendra II, Rajadhiraja, Kulottunga II or Rajaraja III.
- **Target:** **Srivijaya**, ruled by the **Sailendra** dynasty over the **Malay Peninsula, Sumatra, Java** and nearby islands; Srivijaya was forced to accept Chola power.
- **Cause:** Srivijaya controlled the **Strait of Malacca**, harassed Tamil traders and blocked the **China route** – the campaign was **trade-driven**, a punitive raid, not colonisation (Srivijaya was not annexed).
- **Earlier ties:** friendly under Rajaraja I – the Srivijaya king built the **Chudamani Vihara at Nagapattinam**.
> Statements (UPSC 2003, asked thrice): "Cholas defeated Pandya and Chera rulers and dominated peninsular India" ✓; "sent an expedition against the Sailendra empire and conquered some areas" ✓ → **both**.
# Significance
- **"Chola Lake":** **K.A. Nilakanta Sastri** – the Chola navy turned the **Bay of Bengal** into a Chola lake, after **Rajendra I**'s expeditions (UPPSC 2008).
- **Naval power of the south:** the **Cholas** (UPPSC 2004 ×2) – not the Chalukyas, Hoysalas, Pandyas, Cheras, Pallavas or Rashtrakutas.
- **Maritime trade:** the Cholas = the South Indian dynasty of **maritime trade with South-East Asia** and China (APPSC ASO).
- **Firsts:** the Imperial Cholas were the **first Indian power to carry out overseas naval campaigns**; their contacts spread **Dravidian art** to South-East Asia.

=== Founder, Succession and Extent
# Founder
- **Founder:** **Vijayalaya**, a **Pallava feudatory at Uraiyur**, captured **Thanjavur** from the **Muttaraiyars** in **850 CE** and founded the imperial line (9th century) (RAS 2016; APPSC G1).
- **⚠ Trap:** "founded by **Parantaka I**" or "by Rajendra I" is false (APPSC G1 statement; APPSC 2023 Deputy).
- **Successor:** **Aditya I** (871–907) – defeated the last Pallava **Aparajita** and ended Pallava rule.
# Kings in order
| Order | Ruler | Exam fact |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Vijayalaya** (850) | Founder; took Thanjavur |
| 2 | **Aditya I** (871–907) | Ended the Pallavas |
| 3 | **Parantaka I** (907–953) | **Maduraikonda**; Uttaramerur inscriptions; lost **Takkolam (949)** to Rashtrakuta **Krishna III** |
| 4 | Sundara Chola (Parantaka II) | Anbil plates |
| 5 | **Rajaraja I** (Arumolivarman, 985–1014) | Accession began the **golden age**; Brihadeeswara; navy |
| 6 | **Rajendra I** (co-regent 1012; 1014–1044) | Ganga (1022) and Srivijaya (1025) expeditions |
| 7 | **Rajadhiraja I** | Killed at **Koppam (1053)** on an elephant |
| 8 | **Rajendra II** | Crowned on the battlefield of Koppam; victory pillar at **Kolhapur** |
| 9 | Virarajendra → Adhirajendra | Won **Kudalasangamam**; line ended 1070 |
| 10 | **Kulottunga I** (1070–1122) | First **Chalukya-Chola** king |
| Last | **Rajendra III** (–1279) | Defeated by Pandya **Maravarman Kulasekara**; Chola rule ended **1279** |
- **Order often asked:** **Vijayalaya → Aditya I → Rajaraja I → Rajendra I**.
- **Dominant power under Rajaraja I (985–1014):** the **Cholas** (Social & Cultural History of AP).
- **Successor in the king's lifetime:** Rajaraja I named Rajendra I heir in **1012**; they ruled together for two years.
# Extent and period
- **Period:** c. **850–1279 CE** (not the 4th–6th century) – from the **Kaveri delta**.
- **Extent:** the **Coromandel Coast** and parts of the Deccan (Tungabhadra–Krishna) (UPPSC 1991).
- **Coromandel** comes from **Cholamandalam**; Tondaimandalam was the Pallava country.
- **Capital:** **Tanjore (Thanjavur)** from Vijayalaya; later **Gangaikondacholapuram** (Rajendra I). **Uraiyur** = earliest (Sangam) Chola capital; Kaveripattinam (Puhar) = Sangam port (U.P. Lower 2009).

=== Chola–Eastern Chalukya Marriages and the Chalukya-Chola Line
# Marriages
| Chola princess | Father | Husband (Eastern Chalukya) |
|---|---|---|
| **Kundavai** | **Rajaraja I** | **Vimaladitya** – not Saktivarman |
| **Ammanga Devi** | **Rajendra I** | **Rajaraja Narendra** (1019–1061), married 16 Aug 1022 |
| **Madhurantaki** | **Rajendra II** | **Rajendra Chalukya = Kulottunga I** |
- **Vimaladitya's father-in-law:** **Rajaraja I** (APSLPRB SI 2019 ×2).
- **Rajaraja Narendra's wife:** **Ammanga Devi** (APPSC PYQ); their son = **Rajendra Chalukya (Kulottunga I)**.
- **Statements PYQ (G2):** "Saktivarma married Kundavai" ✗; the other two ✓ → **only I incorrect**.
- **Saktivarman I:** Vimaladitya's elder brother; ruled Vengi with Rajaraja I's help after **Jatachoda Bhima** was ousted (1002).
# Kulottunga I – the first Chalukya-Chola
- **First Chalukya-Chola king:** **Kulottunga I** (G2 2011 ×2; G2 2012; APPSC PYQ) – not Rajaraja Narendra, Rajendra I, Rajadhiraja or Vijayalaya.
- **Born:** **Rajendra Chalukya** (counted as **Rajendra II** of Vengi), son of Rajaraja Narendra and Ammanga Devi; took the Chola throne in **1070** as Kulottunga (CDS 2001).
- **1075:** after **Vijayaditya VII** (last Eastern Chalukya) died, he **merged Vengi** with the Chola state – uniting Tamil and Andhra kingdoms.
- **Vengi viceroys:** his sons, titled **Rajapratinidhi** – Rajaraja (Mummadi Chola; commander **Gonka I**; installed **Anantavarman Chodaganga** in Kalinga), Vira Chola, Rajaraja Chola Ganga (crowned at **Jananathapura = Draksharama**; **Tekkali** inscription).
- **Last Chalukya-Chola ruler in Vengi:** **Parantaka Chola**; then the **Mandalika Yugam** (**1076 to c. 1210**).
# Kulottunga I's reign
- **China:** sent **72 merchants** to China in **1077** (UPPSC 1992).
- **Sri Lanka:** **Vijayabahu I** made the island independent; Kulottunga accepted it and married his daughter to the Sinhala prince **Virapperumal** (UPPSC 2012).
- **Kalinga war:** sung in **Jayamkondar's Kalingattuparani**; abolished tolls (**Sungam Tavirtta**).
- **Decline after him:** weakening central bureaucracy, growth of **local chieftains**, rise of the Pandyas.
~~ Sources: NextGen AP History 06 (Eastern Chalukyas) l.927-1121; PMF Ancient & Medieval India p.405; app notes 1:85 (sub 7, 8, 9)

=== Battles and the Struggle for Vengi
| Battle | Sides | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Sripurambiyam** (c. 885) | Pallava **Aparajita** (+ Ganga Prithvipati I, Chola Aditya I) vs Pandya **Varaguna II** | **Aparajita won** |
| **Takkolam** (949) | Rashtrakuta **Krishna III** vs Parantaka I (prince Rajaditya killed) | **Rashtrakutas won** |
| Kalidindi (near **Eluru**) | Rajendra I's army vs Vijayaditya VII + Kalyani Chalukyas | Cholas; **Rajaraja Narendra** built three Shiva temples for the fallen Chola commanders |
| Dharanikota | **Rajadhiraja I** vs Vijayaditya VII and the Kalyani army | Cholas; then burnt **Kollipaka** |
| **Koppam** (1053) | Rajadhiraja I / Rajendra II vs **Someshvara I** | Rajadhiraja I killed; Rajendra II crowned on the field |
| **Kudalasangamam** (c. 1062) | **Virarajendra** vs Someshvara I | Cholas |
- **Sripurambiyam:** the Pallava Aparajitavarman defeated the Pandyas there (APPSC G2 2000; APPSC PYQ) – not Peruvalanallur or Kanchipuram.
- **⚠ Trap:** Takkolam was a **Chola defeat**.
- **Cause of Chola–Chalukya wars:** control of **Vengi** (each side backed a claimant) and supremacy in the Deccan.
- **Western Chalukyas expelled from Vengi:** they held it c. **1120–1137** (Vikramaditya VI); the Cholas recovered it **with the help of local chiefs** – the **Velanati Chodas** (Gonka II) (Social & Cultural History of AP).
~~ Sources: Rocket Indian History #17; Yashoda Devi, History of Andhra Country 1000-1500 (wisdomlib); app notes 1:85 (sub 6)
