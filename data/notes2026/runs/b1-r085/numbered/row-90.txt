## SUB 2 | Rulers – Vishnuvardhana, Ramanuja and the Ballalas
[2:0] - **Region:** most of **Karnataka**, parts of Tamil Nadu; feudatories of the **Western Chalukyas** (early chief **Nripa Kama II**).
[2:1] - **Rise:** independent by the **close of the 12th century**; ended at the **beginning of the 14th** – none of Hoysala, Gahadavala, Kakatiya, Yadava arose in the early 8th century (UPSC 2023: **none**).
[2:2] - **Vishnuvardhana** (original name **Bittideva / Bittiga**, from 1108): fought Vikramaditya VI and the Cholas; took **Talakad** from the Cholas → **Talakadugonda**; built Belur (1117).
[2:3] - **Ramanuja:** fled **Chola** persecution; Vishnuvardhana converted from **Jainism to Vaishnavism** under him; queen **Shantala Devi** stayed Jain.
[2:4] - **Aug 2026:** a **Veeragallu** (hero stone) at **Neeragunda** (Arsikere) may settle the date of Vishnuvardhana's death.

  [2:5.2] Ballala III (1292–1343) | Beat Yadava Ramachandra's forces (1305); lost to Kakatiya commander Pedarudru after 1316; surrendered to Malik Kafur

## SUB 3 | Malik Kafur and the End of the Hoysalas
[3:0] - **Malik Kafur's victims:** Yadava **Ramachandra** of Devagiri (**1307**, taken to Delhi), Kakatiya Prataparudra (1309–10), Hoysala **Vira Ballala III** (Dwarasamudra, **1310–11**), then the Pandyas.
[3:1] - **G1 1990-91 key:** "defeated by Malik Kafur" = **Yadava Ramachandra**; Vira Ballala III also submitted, Rudramadevi had died in 1289.
[3:2] - **Wars:** constant Hoysala–Yadava wars – **Singhana** defeated the Hoysalas.
[3:3] - **End:** Ballala III was killed by **Ghiyas-ud-din**, Sultan of Madurai, at **Kannanur** (1342–43); Hoysala land passed to **Vijayanagara** (by 1346).
[3:4] ~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval India p.412, p.479; app notes 1:90 (sub 6)
