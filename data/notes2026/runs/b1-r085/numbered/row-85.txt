## SUB 0 | Naval Expeditions – Sri Lanka, Maldives, Srivijaya and the "Chola Lake"
[0:0] # Who conquered what overseas
[0:1] TABLE head: Ruler | Naval / overseas conquest
  [0:1.0] Rajaraja I (985–1014) | Built the Chola navy; destroyed the Chera navy at Kandalur Salai (Trivandrum); northern Sri Lanka (Anuradhapura) after defeating Mahinda V; the Maldives
  [0:1.1] Rajendra I (1012–1044) | Whole of Sri Lanka – captured Mahinda V and his royal insignia; Srivijaya expedition (1025) – took Kadaram (Kedah) and places in Malaya and Sumatra
  [0:1.2] Kulottunga I | Kalinga wars (land)
[0:2] - **Navy founder:** **Rajaraja I** was the first Chola king to create a naval army (CGPSC 2014; UPPSC 2004).
[0:3] - **Northern Sri Lanka:** **Rajaraja I** – made it the province **Mummudicholamandalam** (UPPSC 2014; "province of his empire" PYQ).
[0:4] - **"Conquered Ceylon":** **Rajendra I** completed the conquest of the whole island (IAS 2001 key); Rajaraja I had taken only the north.
[0:5]   - Sri Lanka stayed Chola for about half a century until **Vijayabahu I** drove the Cholas out (c. 1070).
[0:6] # The Srivijaya expedition (1025 CE)
[0:7] - **Leader:** **Rajendra I** (UPSC 2025; APPSC G2 2000, 2003; APSLPRB SI 2019) – not Rajaraja I, Rajendra II, Rajadhiraja, Kulottunga II or Rajaraja III.
[0:8] - **Target:** **Srivijaya**, ruled by the **Sailendra** dynasty over the **Malay Peninsula, Sumatra, Java** and nearby islands; Srivijaya was forced to accept Chola power.
[0:9] - **Cause:** Srivijaya controlled the **Strait of Malacca**, harassed Tamil traders and blocked the **China route** – the campaign was **trade-driven**, a punitive raid, not colonisation (Srivijaya was not annexed).
[0:10] - **Earlier ties:** friendly under Rajaraja I – the Srivijaya king built the **Chudamani Vihara at Nagapattinam**.
[0:11] > Statements (UPSC 2003, asked thrice): "Cholas defeated Pandya and Chera rulers and dominated peninsular India" ✓; "sent an expedition against the Sailendra empire and conquered some areas" ✓ → **both**.
[0:12] # Significance
[0:13] - **"Chola Lake":** **K.A. Nilakanta Sastri** – the Chola navy turned the **Bay of Bengal** into a Chola lake, after **Rajendra I**'s expeditions (UPPSC 2008).
[0:14] - **Naval power of the south:** the **Cholas** (UPPSC 2004 ×2) – not the Chalukyas, Hoysalas, Pandyas, Cheras, Pallavas or Rashtrakutas.
[0:15] - **Maritime trade:** the Cholas = the South Indian dynasty of **maritime trade with South-East Asia** and China (APPSC ASO).
[0:16] - **Firsts:** the Imperial Cholas were the **first Indian power to carry out overseas naval campaigns**; their contacts spread **Dravidian art** to South-East Asia.
[0:17] ~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval India p.403-405; Rocket Indian History #1, #11; app notes 1:85 (sub 3, 4)
## SUB 1 | Chola–Eastern Chalukya Marriages and the Chalukya-Chola Line
[1:0] # Marriages
[1:1] TABLE head: Chola princess | Father | Husband (Eastern Chalukya)
  [1:1.0] Kundavai | Rajaraja I | Vimaladitya – not Saktivarman
  [1:1.1] Ammanga Devi | Rajendra I | Rajaraja Narendra (1019–1061), married 16 Aug 1022
  [1:1.2] Madhurantaki | Rajendra II | Rajendra Chalukya = Kulottunga I
[1:2] - **Vimaladitya's father-in-law:** **Rajaraja I** (APSLPRB SI 2019 ×2).
[1:3] - **Rajaraja Narendra's wife:** **Ammanga Devi** (APPSC PYQ); their son = **Rajendra Chalukya (Kulottunga I)**.
[1:4] - **Statements PYQ (G2):** "Saktivarma married Kundavai" ✗; the other two ✓ → **only I incorrect**.
[1:5] - **Saktivarman I:** Vimaladitya's elder brother; ruled Vengi with Rajaraja I's help after **Jatachoda Bhima** was ousted (1002).
[1:6] # Kulottunga I – the first Chalukya-Chola
[1:7] - **First Chalukya-Chola king:** **Kulottunga I** (G2 2011 ×2; G2 2012; APPSC PYQ) – not Rajaraja Narendra, Rajendra I, Rajadhiraja or Vijayalaya.
[1:8] - **Born:** **Rajendra Chalukya** (counted as **Rajendra II** of Vengi), son of Rajaraja Narendra and Ammanga Devi; took the Chola throne in **1070** as Kulottunga (CDS 2001).
[1:9] - **1075:** after **Vijayaditya VII** (last Eastern Chalukya) died, he **merged Vengi** with the Chola state – uniting Tamil and Andhra kingdoms.
[1:10] - **Vengi viceroys:** his sons, titled **Rajapratinidhi** – Rajaraja (Mummadi Chola; commander **Gonka I**; installed **Anantavarman Chodaganga** in Kalinga), Vira Chola, Rajaraja Chola Ganga (crowned at **Jananathapura = Draksharama**; **Tekkali** inscription).
[1:11] - **Last Chalukya-Chola ruler in Vengi:** **Parantaka Chola**; then the **Mandalika Yugam** (**1076 to c. 1210**).
[1:12] # Kulottunga I's reign
[1:13] - **China:** sent **72 merchants** to China in **1077** (UPPSC 1992).
[1:14] - **Sri Lanka:** **Vijayabahu I** made the island independent; Kulottunga accepted it and married his daughter to the Sinhala prince **Virapperumal** (UPPSC 2012).
[1:15] - **Kalinga war:** sung in **Jayamkondar's Kalingattuparani**; abolished tolls (**Sungam Tavirtta**).
[1:16] - **Decline after him:** weakening central bureaucracy, growth of **local chieftains**, rise of the Pandyas.
[1:17] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP History 06 (Eastern Chalukyas) l.927-1121; PMF Ancient & Medieval India p.405; app notes 1:85 (sub 7, 8, 9)
## SUB 2 | Founder, Succession and Extent
[2:0] # Founder
[2:1] - **Founder:** **Vijayalaya**, a **Pallava feudatory at Uraiyur**, captured **Thanjavur** from the **Muttaraiyars** in **850 CE** and founded the imperial line (9th century) (RAS 2016; APPSC G1).
[2:2] - **⚠ Trap:** "founded by **Parantaka I**" or "by Rajendra I" is false (APPSC G1 statement; APPSC 2023 Deputy).
[2:3] - **Successor:** **Aditya I** (871–907) – defeated the last Pallava **Aparajita** and ended Pallava rule.
[2:4] # Kings in order
[2:5] TABLE head: Order | Ruler | Exam fact
  [2:5.0] 1 | Vijayalaya (850) | Founder; took Thanjavur
  [2:5.1] 2 | Aditya I (871–907) | Ended the Pallavas
  [2:5.2] 3 | Parantaka I (907–953) | Maduraikonda; Uttaramerur inscriptions; lost Takkolam (949) to Rashtrakuta Krishna III
  [2:5.3] 4 | Sundara Chola (Parantaka II) | Anbil plates
  [2:5.4] 5 | Rajaraja I (Arumolivarman, 985–1014) | Accession began the golden age; Brihadeeswara; navy
  [2:5.5] 6 | Rajendra I (co-regent 1012; 1014–1044) | Ganga (1022) and Srivijaya (1025) expeditions
  [2:5.6] 7 | Rajadhiraja I | Killed at Koppam (1053) on an elephant
  [2:5.7] 8 | Rajendra II | Crowned on the battlefield of Koppam; victory pillar at Kolhapur
  [2:5.8] 9 | Virarajendra → Adhirajendra | Won Kudalasangamam; line ended 1070
  [2:5.9] 10 | Kulottunga I (1070–1122) | First Chalukya-Chola king
  [2:5.10] Last | Rajendra III (–1279) | Defeated by Pandya Maravarman Kulasekara; Chola rule ended 1279
[2:6] - **Order often asked:** **Vijayalaya → Aditya I → Rajaraja I → Rajendra I**.
[2:7] - **Dominant power under Rajaraja I (985–1014):** the **Cholas** (Social & Cultural History of AP).
[2:8] - **Successor in the king's lifetime:** Rajaraja I named Rajendra I heir in **1012**; they ruled together for two years.
[2:9] # Extent and period
[2:10] - **Period:** c. **850–1279 CE** (not the 4th–6th century) – from the **Kaveri delta**.
[2:11] - **Extent:** the **Coromandel Coast** and parts of the Deccan (Tungabhadra–Krishna) (UPPSC 1991).
[2:12] - **Coromandel** comes from **Cholamandalam**; Tondaimandalam was the Pallava country.
[2:13] - **Capital:** **Tanjore (Thanjavur)** from Vijayalaya; later **Gangaikondacholapuram** (Rajendra I). **Uraiyur** = earliest (Sangam) Chola capital; Kaveripattinam (Puhar) = Sangam port (U.P. Lower 2009).
[2:14] - **Granary of South India:** **Thanjavur** in the Kaveri delta (WBCS 2020).
[2:15] ~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval India p.403-405; Rocket Indian History #4, #11, #18; app notes 1:85 (sub 0, 1)

## SUB 5 | Battles and the Struggle for Vengi
[5:0] TABLE head: Battle | Sides | Result
  [5:0.0] Sripurambiyam (c. 885) | Pallava Aparajita (+ Ganga Prithvipati I, Chola Aditya I) vs Pandya Varaguna II | Aparajita won
  [5:0.1] Takkolam (949) | Rashtrakuta Krishna III vs Parantaka I (prince Rajaditya killed) | Rashtrakutas won
  [5:0.2] Kalidindi (near Eluru) | Rajendra I's army vs Vijayaditya VII + Kalyani Chalukyas | Cholas; Rajaraja Narendra built three Shiva temples for the fallen Chola commanders
  [5:0.3] Dharanikota | Rajadhiraja I vs Vijayaditya VII and the Kalyani army | Cholas; then burnt Kollipaka
  [5:0.4] Koppam (1053) | Rajadhiraja I / Rajendra II vs Someshvara I | Rajadhiraja I killed; Rajendra II crowned on the field
  [5:0.5] Kudalasangamam (c. 1062) | Virarajendra vs Someshvara I | Cholas
[5:1] - **Sripurambiyam:** the Pallava Aparajitavarman defeated the Pandyas there (APPSC G2 2000; APPSC PYQ) – not Peruvalanallur or Kanchipuram.
[5:2] - **⚠ Trap:** Takkolam was a **Chola defeat**.
[5:3] - **Cause of Chola–Chalukya wars:** control of **Vengi** (each side backed a claimant) and supremacy in the Deccan.
[5:4] - **Western Chalukyas expelled from Vengi:** they held it c. **1120–1137** (Vikramaditya VI); the Cholas recovered it **with the help of local chiefs** – the **Velanati Chodas** (Gonka II) (Social & Cultural History of AP).
