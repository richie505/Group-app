=== GSDP, Per Capita Income and District Data 2025-26
- **GSDP** 2025-26 (advance estimates, current prices): **₹17.62 lakh crore** (₹17,62,357 crore), up from ₹15.91 lakh crore = nominal growth **10.75%** (not 10.43%, which is the per capita income growth).
- **Per capita income** 2025-26 (AE): **₹2,94,507**, up from ₹2,66,699 (about **10.43%**).
- **Agriculture's share of GSDP: 33.20%** (₹5,39,454 crore of ₹17,62,357 crore) – about one-third.
- **Districts:** **Visakhapatnam** = highest per capita income (₹6,58,091) and GDDP; **Krishna** is next in per capita income (₹4,82,078); NTR is 2nd in GDDP.
- Target for 2026-27 (district strategies under Swarna Andhra): GSDP ₹20.36 lakh crore, growth 15.53%; the Budget itself assumes ₹19,75,073 crore (12%).
> PYQ angle (book 3 rows 109-110, 132): growth rate, agriculture's share and the top district are the facts APPSC asks from each Survey.

=== Deficits, Debt and Fiscal Rules
| Item | 2024-25 actuals | 2025-26 RE | 2026-27 BE |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue deficit | ₹60,285 cr = **3.79%** | ₹41,118 cr = 2.33% | **₹22,003 cr = 1.1%** of GSDP |
| Fiscal deficit | ₹81,362 cr = **5.11%** | ₹80,567 cr = 4.57% (BE 4.4%) | **₹75,868 cr = 3.8%** of GSDP |
- 2024-25 figures are from the AG's finance accounts: RD **3.79%**, FD **5.11%** (not the other way round).
- GSDP projected for 2026-27: **₹19,75,073 crore**, growth **12%**.
- **Centre's** fiscal-deficit target 2026-27 = **4.3% of GDP**: AP's 3.8% of GSDP is **lower** than it. 16th FC limit for States: **3% of GSDP** (2026-31), so AP's 3.8% is above that.
- AP FRBM Act **2005** sets the deficit and debt targets (Union FRBM Act 2003).
# Debt and guarantees
- **Outstanding debt:** **₹6,35,177 crore = 36.04% of GSDP** (2025-26 RE); expected **₹7,11,094 crore** (about ₹7.11 lakh crore) by end of 2026-27; steady at about **36%** of GSDP (33% in 2021-22).
- The **₹11,000 crore** of Centre's **50-year interest-free capex loans** (0.6% of GSDP) are kept outside the borrowing ceiling / debt figure.
- **Guarantees:** total guarantees availed about **₹3,11,182 crore** (app notes) – cited as evidence of growing **off-budget borrowing**; outstanding guarantees **₹1,77,328 crore** (31 Dec 2025, 10% of GSDP, PRS).
- CAG: off-budget borrowings **₹1.28 lakh crore** (March 2023, 9.7% of GSDP).
> PYQ angle: AP's debt–GSDP ratio is the single most repeated AP-budget PYQ (book 3 row 130: 9 PYQs on debt, 5 on deficits).

- Own tax revenue **₹1,25,846 crore** (6.4% of GSDP).

=== Swarna Andhra @2047 Vision
- Vision document unveiled by CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu** on **13 December 2024** at Vijayawada; theme **"10 Principles – One Vision"** (not "One State – One Vision").
- **10 guiding principles = Padi Sutralu** (not 7, 12 or 15): zero poverty, employment, skilling, water security, agri-tech, energy, logistics, deep tech and others.
- Aim: a **Wealthy, Healthy and Happy** (WHH) Andhra Pradesh.
- Target: a **$2.4 trillion economy by 2047**, growth of **15%** a year or more.
- **P4** = **Public–Private–People Partnership**, linked to zero poverty (the SES 2024-25 heading writes it "Public-People Philanthropic Partnership").
- **AP State Development Planning Society (APSDPS)** is the main execution body for the roadmap (app notes).
~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.14 ($2.4 trillion, $42,000), p.302 (P4), p.306 (Ten Sutras), p.310 (Wealthy, Healthy, Happy); ThePrint 13 Dec 2024 ("10 Principles-One Vision") via web search; CARE APPSC Feb 2026 p.14 (P4)
