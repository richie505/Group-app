## SUB 8 | Vision Documents and Externally Aided Projects
[9:8:0] # Swarna Andhra @2047
[9:8:1] - Vision unveiled by CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu** in December 2024, aligned with **Viksit Bharat @2047**.
[9:8:2] - Targets by **2047**: **$2.4 trillion** economy; per capita income about **$42,000** (about ₹55 lakh); annual growth **15%** or more.
