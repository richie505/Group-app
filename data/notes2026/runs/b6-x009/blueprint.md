## 2. Angles (from those PYQs)
- **Factual recall / number** dominates: a ratio (debt/GSDP, interest/revenue receipts, FD/GSDP), a ₹ figure (budget size, an allocation), a growth rate, a count (10 Sutralu, districts).
- **"Largest / first / highest"**: highest allocation, largest tax source (now SGST), top district by GDDP or per capita income.
- **Match / wrong pair**: project → funding agency; scheme → amount.
- **Statements / NOT-correct**: budget statements (2016-17, 2019-20, 2023-24 budgets), Group-I 2023 EAP set.
- **Trap options** are always near numbers from the same budget (revenue vs fiscal deficit, 10.75% GSDP vs 10.43% PCI growth, Polavaram ₹6,105 vs Amaravati ₹6,000 crore, 15th vs 14th vs 16th FC share).
- **Current linkage:** each new Budget and Survey replaces the numbers; APPSC asks the edition current at the exam (2023-24 budget figures were asked in 2023 papers).
