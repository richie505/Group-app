  [1:0.0] EPCG | Export Promotion Capital Goods: zero-duty import of capital goods against an export obligation

  [1:0.2] Advance Authorisation | Duty-free import of inputs for export production

  [1:0.4] SEIS | Services exports reward (FTP 2015-20)
  [1:0.5] RoDTEP | Replaced the WTO-challenged MEIS; refunds unrebated central, state and local taxes; run online by CBIC
  [1:0.6] RoSCTL | Replaced RoSL; apparel and made-ups

  [1:0.8] Gold Card Scheme (RBI, EXIM Policy 2003-04) | Quicker, cheaper credit for creditworthy exporters
  [1:0.9] Niryat Bandhu | DGFT training/outreach – mentoring new and first-generation entrepreneurs in export-import

## SUB 4 | Trade Policy Instruments and Economic Integration
[4:0] - **Import substitution industrialisation (ISI):** inward-looking; produce substitutes at home to **reduce import dependence** (India's policy for about four decades) – not open trade, FDI or liberalisation.
[4:1] - **Import quota** = quantitative restriction; tariffs, countervailing and anti-dumping duties = price-based.
[4:2] TABLE head: Stage | Feature
  [4:2.0] FTA | Free internal trade; members keep own external tariffs
  [4:2.1] Customs union | Free internal trade + common external tariff
  [4:2.2] Common market | Customs union + free movement of factors
  [4:2.3] Economic union | Common market + coordinated economic policies
[4:3] ~~ Sources: Rocket Indian Economy p.80; app notes 3:74 sub 0

## SUB 6 | EOUs, EHTPs, STPs and SEZs
[6:0] TABLE head: Unit | Covers / nature
  [6:0.0] SEZ | Duty-free enclave treated as foreign territory; policy 1 April 2000, Act 2005, Act + Rules in force 10 Feb 2006; concept from EXIM Policy 1997-2002; 100% FDI automatic for most manufacturing
  [6:0.1] EOU | Goods generally
  [6:0.2] EHTP | Electronics hardware
  [6:0.3] STP | Software
[6:1] - EOU/EHTP/STP: **export all output**, can be **located anywhere** (need not be in an SEZ), duty-free inputs.
[6:2] - SEZ aims: exports, investment, employment, infrastructure; internationally competitive, hassle-free, **simplified business-friendly** environment.
[6:3] - First EPZ: **Kandla, 1965** (Asia's first).
