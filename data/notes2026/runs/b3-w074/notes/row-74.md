=== Trade Promotion and Export Credit Bodies
| Body | Year / role |
|---|---|
| **ECGC** (Export Credit Guarantee Corporation, now ECGC Ltd) | **1957**, wholly government-owned; **export credit-risk insurance** – the body that insures exporters |
| **EXIM Bank** | Act 1981, set up **1982**, **Mumbai**; finance for **foreign trade** – lines of credit, buyer's/seller's credit, project exports; **not an insurer** |
| **MMTC** | **1963**; minerals, metals, precious metals; **largest international trading organisation / trading government company**; was the **largest non-oil importer** |
| **STC** (State Trading Corporation) | **1956**; canalising agency for bulk goods; foreign trade, not insurance |
| **IBEF** (India Brand Equity Fund/Foundation) | **1996**, trust of the Department of Commerce; promotes the "Made in India" brand abroad |
| PEC (Project and Equipment Corporation) | Under the **Ministry of Commerce**, not Industry |
| DGFT | Attached office of the Department of Commerce, **Vanijya Bhavan**, New Delhi; issues FTP authorisations |
- **Insurance of export risk = ECGC only** (not EXIM Bank, GIC, STC or RBI); "export financing and insurance" = ECGC.
- **Pre-shipment inspection / quality control** = **Export Inspection Council**, NOT an ECGC objective (UPSC 2002: only statement 1 – MMTC – correct).
- **NIRVIK** (Niryat Rin Vikas Yojana, **Budget 2020-21**): ECGC scheme – higher insurance cover, lower premium for **exporters**.
- Channelising (canalising) exports and imports – UPPSC 1999 key: **MMTC, EXIM Bank, STC and FCI (all four)**. Promote foreign trade: **ECGC, MMTC, STC (all)**.
- **Neelachal Ispat Nigam Ltd (NINL)** was set up by **MMTC jointly with the Government of Odisha** (Odisha PSUs); sold to Tata Steel in 2022.
- Agri goods exporters: **NAFED, STC, MMTC**; **IFFCO** (fertilisers) is NOT one.
- Status holders: earlier **Export House → Trading House → Star Trading House → Super Star Trading House** (high, sustained export performance = Star Trading Houses); now **One-Star to Five-Star Export Houses**.

=== Export Incentive Schemes and Towns of Export Excellence
| Scheme | Fact |
|---|---|
| **EPCG** | **Export Promotion Capital Goods**: zero-duty import of capital goods against an export obligation |
| **Duty drawback** | **Refund of import (customs) and excise duties** on inputs used in exports – not income tax, not export duty |
| Advance Authorisation | Duty-free import of inputs for export production |
| **MEIS** (FTP 2015-20) | Goods exports reward; **replaced five schemes – Focus Product, Focus Market, Market Linked Focus Product, VKGUY, Agri Infrastructure Incentive Scrip** |
| SEIS | Services exports reward (FTP 2015-20) |
| **RoDTEP** | Replaced the WTO-challenged MEIS; refunds unrebated central, state and local taxes; run online by **CBIC** |
| RoSCTL | Replaced RoSL; apparel and made-ups |
| **Vishesh Krishi (and Gram) Upaj Yojana (2004)** | Promoted exports of **fruits, vegetables, flowers**, minor forest produce and their value-added products |
| **Gold Card Scheme** (RBI, EXIM Policy 2003-04) | Quicker, cheaper credit for **creditworthy exporters** |
| **Niryat Bandhu** | DGFT training/outreach – **mentoring new and first-generation entrepreneurs** in export-import |
- **Towns of Export Excellence (TEE):** **Tirupur** (hosiery), **Ludhiana** (woollen knitwear), **Panipat** (woollen blankets) – 2003; **Madurai** (handlooms) is also a TEE – so "Madurai was not a TEE" is wrong; Visakhapatnam and Bhimavaram (seafood) are AP's TEEs.
- **Export Promotion Mission:** ₹25,060 crore, FY 2025-26 to 2030-31, run by **DGFT**; merges **Interest Equalisation + Market Access Initiative**; for **MSMEs and first-time exporters**.
- **RELIEF Scheme 2026:** ₹497 crore via **ECGC** for **war-risk and logistics costs in West Asia**.

=== Foreign Trade Policy – History and Targets
| Policy | Fact |
|---|---|
| First EXIM Policy | 1985-88 |
| **EXIM Policy 1992** | **Five years (1992-97)** |
| EXIM Policy 2000 | Announced 31 March 2000 (1997-2002 framework); SEZ policy 1 April 2000 |
| **FTP 2004-09** (Aug 2004) | First "Foreign Trade Policy"; **double India's share of world merchandise trade** to **1.5% by 2009**; stressed growth **and employment** |
| **FTP 2015-20** (1 April 2015) | Raise share of world exports from **2% to 3.5%**; MEIS/SEIS; extended to **31 March 2023** |
| **FTP 2023** | From **1 April 2023**, **no end date**; **$2 trillion exports by 2030** |
- FTP 2023: incentives → **remission and entitlement**; **Districts as Export Hubs**; rupee trade and **merchanting trade**; one-time **amnesty** for EPCG and Advance Authorisation defaults.
- FTP services target: **4.2%** share of world services exports by 2022.
- Administered by the **Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industry**, through **DGFT**.
- 1992 partial convertibility: **60% market rate : 40% official rate**.

=== Export-Import Procedure, Documents and Rules of Origin
| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Indent** | Importer's order to buy goods – **the import process begins with the indent** |
| **Letter of credit** | Produced by the **importer** (through its issuing bank), promising payment to the exporter (beneficiary) |
| Shipping bill | Customs document for clearing export goods |
| Mate's receipt | Ship's mate acknowledges goods loaded on board |
| Bill of lading | Shipping company's receipt and **document of title** |
- **Rules of origin:** fix a product's **national source**; tell customs which **duties, regulations and fees** apply; used for **trade statistics**; matter because duties and restrictions depend on the **source of imports** – **all four statements correct**.
~~ Sources: Rocket Indian Economy p.80; app notes 3:74 sub 8

=== Trade Policy Instruments and Economic Integration
- **Import substitution industrialisation (ISI):** inward-looking; produce substitutes at home to **reduce import dependence** (India's policy for about four decades) – not open trade, FDI or liberalisation.
- **Import quota** = quantitative restriction; tariffs, countervailing and anti-dumping duties = price-based.
| Stage | Feature |
|---|---|
| FTA | Free internal trade; members keep own external tariffs |
| **Customs union** | Free internal trade + **common external tariff** |
| Common market | Customs union + **free movement of factors** |
| Economic union | Common market + coordinated economic policies |

=== Agri-Export Promotion and Trade Facilitation

=== EOUs, EHTPs, STPs and SEZs
| Unit | Covers / nature |
|---|---|
| **SEZ** | Duty-free enclave treated as **foreign territory**; policy **1 April 2000**, Act **2005**, Act + Rules in force **10 Feb 2006**; concept from EXIM Policy 1997-2002; 100% FDI automatic for most manufacturing |
| EOU | Goods generally |
| EHTP | Electronics hardware |
| STP | Software |
- EOU/EHTP/STP: **export all output**, can be **located anywhere** (need not be in an SEZ), duty-free inputs.
- SEZ aims: exports, investment, employment, infrastructure; internationally competitive, hassle-free, **simplified business-friendly** environment.
- First EPZ: **Kandla, 1965** (Asia's first).
~~ Sources: Rocket Indian Economy p.26, p.79; app notes 3:74 sub 6
