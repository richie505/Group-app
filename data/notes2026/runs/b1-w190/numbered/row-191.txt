## SUB 1 | Ramakrishna Mission, 1897 – Founder, Year, Work
[1:0] - **Founded 1 May 1897 by Swami Vivekananda**, at Balaram Bose's house, **Baghbazar, Calcutta**, in his guru's name – not by Ramakrishna (died 1886), Vidyasagar, Dayananda, Rammohan Roy, Gokhale, Shyamji Krishna Varma, M.N. Das Gupta or Ranganathananda.
[1:1] - **Year = 1897** – not 1861, 1879, 1886, 1891, 1892, 1893, 1898 or 1899. "Founded the Mission in **1879**" = the INCORRECT statement.
[1:2] - **HQ: Belur Math** on the Hooghly, Howrah (land bought **1898**); Math and Mission are twin bodies, legally and financially separate.
[1:3] - Motto: **"Atmano Mokshartham Jagad Hitaya Cha"** – for one's own liberation and the welfare of the world.
[1:4] - **Two wings:** (1) a **monastic order** of renunciation and practical spirituality; (2) **lay disciples** – preaching, philanthropy, humanitarian work.
[1:5] - **Work:** schools, hospitals, orphanages, **famine and disaster relief**; social and spiritual – **non-political, non-sectarian, not proselytising**; unlike the Arya Samaj it accepts image worship.
[1:6] - **Current linkage (1 Feb 2026):** RKM's **Vivekananda Human Excellence Centre, Vijayawada**, inaugurated by RKM President Swami Gautamananda.
[1:7] > RK Mission = Vivekananda, 1 May 1897, Calcutta; HQ Belur Math.
[1:8] ~~ Sources: Spectrum Modern India (Ahir 2019) file p.249, p.251-252; PMF Modern History Part 3 p.175, p.200; Rocket Indian History Part 2 p.44, p.99, p.104; old app notes row 191 (Vijayawada centre, 2026)

## SUB 3 | Theosophical Society, 1875 – Founders, Adyar, Beliefs
[3:0] TABLE head: Point | Fact
  [3:0.0] Founded | New York, November 1875 – not London, Paris or Adyar (same year as the Arya Samaj)
  [3:0.1] Founders | Madame H.P. Blavatsky (Russian) and Col. H.S. Olcott (American), with William Q. Judge – not Annie Besant, Hume, Rammohan, Gandhi, Vivekananda
  [3:0.2] Came to India | 1879
  [3:0.3] HQ | Adyar, near Madras, from 1882 – not Madanapalle, Pondicherry, Kanchipuram
  [3:0.4] Success in India | Mainly due to Annie Besant (President 1907-33)

[3:2] - A Western-led **revivalist** movement allied with the Hindu renaissance; gave Indians self-respect – and led straight into the **Home Rule** movement.
[3:3] - **A-R trap:** HQ at Adyar ✔, but it was **not** founded at Madras ✘ → A true, R false.
