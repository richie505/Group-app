## SUB 2 | Slums and Problems of Large Cities
[2:0] TABLE head: Measure | Place
  [2:0.0] Slum population (2011) | 6.54 crore = 17.4% of urban
  [2:0.1] Largest number (State) | Maharashtra
  [2:0.2] Highest share (State) | Undivided AP (~36%)
  [2:0.3] Largest number (city) | Greater Mumbai; Dharavi (Mumbai) – India's best-known largest slum
  [2:0.4] Highest share (million-plus city) | Greater Visakhapatnam (~44%) vs Mumbai (~41%)
[2:1] - World Bank: **177 million** slum dwellers (broader definition).
[2:2] - **UN World Habitat Award 2023: Odisha** – **JAGA Mission** (land titling and slum upgrading).
[2:3] - **Air pollution:** IQAir World Air Quality Report 2019 – **6 of the world's 10** most polluted cities were Indian; **IQAir 2025 report (24 Mar 2026): 5 of the top 10**, **Loni** (Ghaziabad) most polluted (112.5 µg/m³), India 6th most polluted country. **Delhi** – most polluted capital; repeatedly hits "**severe plus**" AQI in winter.
[2:4] - **Waste**: ~**62 million tonnes** MSW a year; **70% collected, 30% processed**. **Noida** was billed as India's first **zero-discharge city** (sewage reuse).
[2:5] - **Congestion**: 1.5-2 hours a day lost; **one planner per 1 lakh** (developed countries 1:5,000).
[2:6] - **Urban flooding**: building over lakes, wetlands, floodplains; blocked drains – building **basements** is NOT a flood-control measure. **Urban heat island**: dense construction, asphalt, lost tree cover.
