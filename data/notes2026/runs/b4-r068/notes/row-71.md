=== Metropolitan Cities, Megacities and Urban Terms
| Term | Meaning | Coiner / source |
|---|---|---|
| **Metropolitan city** | **10 lakh (1 million) to 1 crore (10 million)** | Census usage; NCERT XII: 1-5 million |
| **Megacity** | **1 crore (10 million)+** | UN / Census usage; NCERT XII: over 5 million |
| Metropolitan area (Constitution) | **10 lakh or more** – **Art. 243P** (74th Amendment) | – |
| **Megalopolis** | Chain of merged metropolitan areas (US north-east seaboard, Boston-Washington) | **Jean Gottmann (1961)** |
| Umland | Area surrounding a town, linked to it | German term |
| Conurbation | Continuous urban area of merged towns | Patrick Geddes |
| Primate city | Several times larger than the next city | Mark Jefferson |
- **Rakesh Mohan**: three processes of Indian urbanisation.
- **Mumbai** is the large city built on an **island** (Salsette and the seven islands of Bombay).
> Exam angle: 1-10 million = metropolitan city; above 10 million = megacity; Class I = only 1 lakh+.

=== National Capital Region and Delhi
- **NCR = NCT Delhi + parts of Haryana, UP, Rajasthan**; **Punjab and Madhya Pradesh are NOT included** (UPPSC 2008, 2017).
| State | NCR towns |
|---|---|
| Haryana | **Rohtak, Karnal**, Panipat, Sonipat, Gurugram, Faridabad |
| UP | **Khurja** (Bulandshahr), Meerut, Ghaziabad, **Noida** |
| Rajasthan | Alwar |
- **Ambala** is NOT in the NCR (UPPSC 2011).
- **NCR Planning Board** – **NCRPB Act, 1985**; approved four greenfield **Namo Cities** (Jun 2026).
- **Delhi facts:** **Khari Baoli** (Old Delhi) – Asia's largest wholesale spice market; **Mandi House** – a cultural-hub roundabout (named after the Raja of Mandi's house), not a market, film or agency; **Thal Sena Bhawan** (new Army HQ) foundation stone laid in **Delhi**, Feb 2020.
- **V.V. Giri National Labour Institute**: **Noida** (UP, NCR).
- UN treats **Delhi-Gurugram-Noida-Ghaziabad-Faridabad** as one agglomeration; the Census counts them separately.

=== Slums and Problems of Large Cities
| Measure | Place |
|---|---|
| Slum population (2011) | **6.54 crore** = **17.4%** of urban |
| Largest number (State) | Maharashtra |
| Highest share (State) | Undivided AP (~36%) |
| Largest number (city) | Greater Mumbai; **Dharavi** (Mumbai) – India's best-known largest slum |
| Highest share (million-plus city) | Greater Visakhapatnam (~44%) vs Mumbai (~41%) |
- World Bank: **177 million** slum dwellers (broader definition).
- **UN World Habitat Award 2023: Odisha** – **JAGA Mission** (land titling and slum upgrading).
- **Air pollution:** IQAir World Air Quality Report 2019 – **6 of the world's 10** most polluted cities were Indian; **IQAir 2025 report (24 Mar 2026): 5 of the top 10**, **Loni** (Ghaziabad) most polluted (112.5 µg/m³), India 6th most polluted country. **Delhi** – most polluted capital; repeatedly hits "**severe plus**" AQI in winter.
- **Waste**: ~**62 million tonnes** MSW a year; **70% collected, 30% processed**. **Noida** was billed as India's first **zero-discharge city** (sewage reuse).
- **Congestion**: 1.5-2 hours a day lost; **one planner per 1 lakh** (developed countries 1:5,000).
- **Urban flooding**: building over lakes, wetlands, floodplains; blocked drains – building **basements** is NOT a flood-control measure. **Urban heat island**: dense construction, asphalt, lost tree cover.
