## SUB 3 | Socialists and Communists – Joining and Split
[3:0] - CPI banned **23 July 1934** → Sundarayya, Chandra Rajeswara Rao, Krovvidi Lingaraju, Gadde Lingayya joined Ranga's party, advised to **work inside the Congress**.
[3:1] - **Andhra Communist Party:** **Kakinada, January 1936** (first meeting 29 Jan 1936); Secretary **P. Sundarayya** (not Chandra Rajeswara Rao).
[3:2] - CSP **expelled the communists in 1940**; the **"People's War" (1941)** line and opposition to Quit India widened the break.
[3:3] - First Labour Day in India: Madras, 1923, Singaravelu's **Labour Kisan Party** (not the Forward Bloc).
[3:4] ~~ Sources: NextGen Modern India L2 Part 8 p.4; app notes 1:221
## SUB 4 | KMPP, PSP, KLP and After, 1951-1977
[4:0] - **KMPP:** **J.B. Kripalani**, **June 1951** (not July 1952); **P.C. Ghosh** and **T. Prakasam** were its leaders.
[4:1] - **PSP:** KMPP + Socialist Party, **September 1952** – Kripalani chairman, Ashoka Mehta general secretary; **Tanguturi Prakasam** was its active Andhra leader.
[4:2] - **Tenneti Viswanatham** left the Congress in 1951 → Kisan Mazdoor Party.
[4:3] - **1952 Madras** (375 seats): United Front chose Prakasam, but Governor **Sri Prakasa** invited **Rajaji**.
[4:4] - **Capital, 1953:** the **PSP wanted Tirupati**; **Kurnool** was chosen.
[4:5] - **1955 Andhra mid-term poll:** Congress front with the **Krishikar Lok Party** (leader **N.G. Ranga**); Congress **119**, KLP 22, CPI 15, PSP 13, Praja Party 5.
[4:6] - **1977:** the Socialist Party and the PSP stream merged into the **Janata Party**.
[4:7] ~~ Sources: Spectrum p.672; NextGen Modern India L2 Part 8 p.3; app notes 1:221
