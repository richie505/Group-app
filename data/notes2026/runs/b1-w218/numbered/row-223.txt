## SUB 0 | Communal Award, 1932
[0:0] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [0:0.0] Date | 16 August 1932
  [0:0.1] By | British PM Ramsay MacDonald
  [0:0.2] Context | 2nd Round Table Conference failed over minority representation
  [0:0.3] Separate electorates | Muslims, Sikhs, Europeans, Indian Christians, Anglo-Indians, depressed classes (and Marathas for some Bombay seats) – not Buddhists
  [0:0.4] Depressed classes | Declared a minority with a separate electorate for the first time; double vote (separate + general); 71 provincial seats
  [0:0.5] Women | 3% seats in all provinces except the NWFP
[0:1] - Indians were divided into **11 groups**; the Award gave each minority seats elected by **separate electorate**.
[0:2] - Muslims had separate electorates from 1909; Europeans, Anglo-Indians, Indian Christians from 1919.

## SUB 1 | Gandhi's Fast and the Poona Pact
[1:0] - **Gandhi's fast unto death:** from **20 September 1932**, **Yerwada Jail, Poona**, against the separate electorate for the depressed classes – his **first fast unto death** (not RTC failure, Congress-League rift, or riots).
[1:1] - Gandhi: separate electorates would **permanently divide Hindu society**; **Ambedkar** supported the separate electorate.
[1:2] - **Poona Pact, 24 September 1932:** **Ambedkar** for the depressed classes, **Madan Mohan Malaviya** for caste Hindus; the government accepted it as an **amendment** to the Award (not withdrawn); Gandhi ended his fast.
[1:3] TABLE head: Feature | Communal Award | Poona Pact
  [1:3.0] Electorate | Separate (+ double vote) | Joint electorate with reserved seats
  [1:3.1] Provincial reserved seats | 71 | 147 (Spectrum) / 148 (most books) – increased
  [1:3.2] Centre | – | 18% of general seats
[1:4] - The Pact's joint electorate with reserved seats became the **model for SC reservation** (it concerned SCs, not STs).
[1:5] - Forerunner: **Rajah-Moonje Pact** (Feb 1932) – **M.C. Rajah** and **B.S. Moonje**.
[1:6] - Order: Award (Aug) → fast (20 Sept) → Pact (24 Sept 1932).
[1:7] - **Bose on Ambedkar:** "leadership thrust upon him by a benign British Government" – attributed to **Subhas Chandra Bose**.
[1:8] ~~ Sources: Spectrum p.423-424; PMF Modern History Part 4 p.205-207; app notes 1:223
## SUB 2 | Anti-untouchability and Depressed Classes Bodies

  [2:0.0] Depressed Classes Mission | 1906 (18 Oct), Bombay | V.R. Shinde
  [2:0.1] Bahishkrit Hitakarini Sabha | 1924 | Ambedkar – "Educate, Agitate, Organise"
  [2:0.2] All India Depressed Classes Association | 1920s | M.C. Rajah (president)
  [2:0.3] All India Anti-Untouchability League | 30 Sept 1932 | Founded by Gandhi; first president G.D. Birla, secretary Amritlal Thakkar; renamed Harijan Sevak Sangh

[2:1] - **Bhagya Reddy Varma:** at the **1917 Vijayawada** conference the depressed classes were to be called **Adi-Hindu**; the conference took the name **Adi-Andhra Mahajana Sabha**; worked with **Arige Ramaswamy** among the Malas; title "Varma" from the **Arya Samaj**.
[2:2] - **Journals:** *Mooknayak* (31 Jan 1920) and *Bahishkrit Bharat* – Ambedkar; *Harijan* (1933) – Gandhi.
[2:3] - **"Scheduled Castes":** first used by the **Simon Commission**, fixed by the **GoI Act 1935**, first list **1936**; SCs are also called **Dalits**.
