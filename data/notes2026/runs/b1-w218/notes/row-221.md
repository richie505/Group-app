=== Andhra Socialist Party, 1934 – Founding and Leaders
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Founded | **June 1934 (23 June), Vijayawada** – the Andhra unit of the Congress Socialist Party (not 1930-32 or 1935) |
| **First President** | **N.G. Ranga** ("Acharya Ranga") – not Annapurnayya, Sundarayya or Rajeswara Rao |
| **First Secretary** | **Madduri Annapurnayya** – not Ranga, Lingaraju or Alluri Satyanarayana Raju |
| Members | Alluri Satyanarayana Raju, Krovvidi Lingaraju, **P. Sundarayya** (the left-wing / communist leader from its start), Chandra Rajeswara Rao |
| First session | **1935, Guntur**, presided by **Tenneti Viswanatham** |
| Later conference | 24-26 February 1948 under Nannapaneni Venkata Rao – keyed to **Gudivada** |
- National leaders JP, Narendra Dev and Yusuf Meherally were not Andhra leaders; the Andhra leader was **Ranga**.
- **Madduri Annapurnayya:** b. 1899, Komaragiri (Pithapuram taluk); **"Andhra Netaji"**; jailed in the Salt Satyagraha, then joined the CSP; jailed for the playlet **"Chichula Pidugu"**; **first Andhra leader in Bose's Forward Bloc** and led its Andhra branch.
- **Andhra Swaraj Party** – 1934, **Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao**.
> Ranga = President, Annapurnayya = Secretary, Vijayawada, 1934.

=== Ranga's Peasant Programme and Political Schools
- **N.G. Ranga** led the **peasant and agrarian movements in Andhra** (with Pullela Syamasundar Rao and Gouthu Latchanna, against zamindars) – not Prakasam, Konda Venkatappayya or Gadicherla.
- **Parties founded by Ranga:** Andhra Socialist Party (**1934**), **Krishikar Lok Party (1951)**, **Swatantra Party (1959**, with Rajaji); **Raithu Rakshana Sangham** (1934).

=== Socialists and Communists – Joining and Split
- CPI banned **23 July 1934** → Sundarayya, Chandra Rajeswara Rao, Krovvidi Lingaraju, Gadde Lingayya joined Ranga's party, advised to **work inside the Congress**.
- **Andhra Communist Party:** **Kakinada, January 1936** (first meeting 29 Jan 1936); Secretary **P. Sundarayya** (not Chandra Rajeswara Rao).
- CSP **expelled the communists in 1940**; the **"People's War" (1941)** line and opposition to Quit India widened the break.
- First Labour Day in India: Madras, 1923, Singaravelu's **Labour Kisan Party** (not the Forward Bloc).

=== KMPP, PSP, KLP and After, 1951-1977
- **KMPP:** **J.B. Kripalani**, **June 1951** (not July 1952); **P.C. Ghosh** and **T. Prakasam** were its leaders.
- **PSP:** KMPP + Socialist Party, **September 1952** – Kripalani chairman, Ashoka Mehta general secretary; **Tanguturi Prakasam** was its active Andhra leader.
- **Tenneti Viswanatham** left the Congress in 1951 → Kisan Mazdoor Party.
- **1952 Madras** (375 seats): United Front chose Prakasam, but Governor **Sri Prakasa** invited **Rajaji**.
- **Capital, 1953:** the **PSP wanted Tirupati**; **Kurnool** was chosen.
- **1955 Andhra mid-term poll:** Congress front with the **Krishikar Lok Party** (leader **N.G. Ranga**); Congress **119**, KLP 22, CPI 15, PSP 13, Praja Party 5.
- **1977:** the Socialist Party and the PSP stream merged into the **Janata Party**.
