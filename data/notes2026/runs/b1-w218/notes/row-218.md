=== All India Kisan Sabha (1936)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Founded | **April 1936, Lucknow** (during the INC session); first named **All India Kisan Congress** |
| First President | **Swami Sahajanand Saraswati** |
| General Secretary | **N.G. Ranga** |
| Addressed by | **Jawaharlal Nehru** (Congress president at Lucknow) |
| Kisan manifesto | **August 1936** – radical demands (zamindari abolition, debt cancellation); a periodical under **Indulal Yagnik** |
| Link with Congress | Peasant wing of the Congress; **joint sessions at Faizpur (1936)**; Congress 1937 manifesto shaped by the AIKS agenda |
| Palasa session | **March 1940**, presided by Sahajanand; Ranga, confined to Nidubrolu, defied the order and was jailed |
| WWII split | Communist-led AIKS took the pro-war "People's War" line; **Sahajanand, Yagnik and Ranga left** |
| 1943 | Ranga formed the **Andhra Rashtra Kisan Congress** |
- AIKS = **1936**, not 1940 (Palasa) or 1926/1946.
- **Sahajanand:** founded the **Bihar Provincial Kisan Sabha (1929)**; papers **Lok Sangraha** and the Hindi weekly **Hunkar (Patna)**; before his death (**1950**) the **All India United Kisan Sabha** – demand: **nationalisation of land and waterways**.
- **Oudh Kisan Sabha (1920)** = Nehru and Baba Ramchandra.

=== N.G. Ranga – Founder of the Andhra Kisan Movement
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Name | **Gogineni Ranganayakulu** – "Acharya" **N.G. Ranga** |
| Born | 7 Nov 1900, **Nidubrolu (Guntur)** |
| Titles | **Rythu Bandhu / Rythu Bandhava**; "**Father of the Indian Peasant Movement**"; founder of the **Kisan movement in Andhra** (G2 2011, 2019) |
| Joined freedom struggle | **1930**, on **Gandhi's** call (not Bose) – APPSC A-R: A true, R false |
| 1933 | Led the historic **Ryot Agitation of Andhra (1933)**; farmers' movement in Venkatagiri 1933-36 |
| Guntur | Organised the **first farmers' (ryots') association in Guntur** |
| Institutions | **Indian Peasants Institute / first Kisan School, Nidubrolu (1933-34)**; **Rajakeeya Pathashala ("School of Politics"), Kankipadu, 1939** |
| Other | 1929 memorandum to the **Whitley Commission** – **minimum wages**; Andhra Socialist Party (1934); Krishikar Lok Party (1951); Swatantra Party (1959) |
| Honour | **Padma Vibhushan (1991)**, not Bharat Ratna |
| Journals | **Praja Bandhu, Navayuga** (also Chitragupta) |
| Books | **Credo of World Peasantry, Revolutionary Peasantry, Fight for Freedom**, Bapu Blesses |
- Other papers: **Zamin Ryot = Nellore**; **Prajavani = Vizag** (Garimella Krishna Murthy).
- **Kisan movement demands:** **MSP**, **minimum wages**, **zamindari abolition**.
> Old-notes trap fixed: the **1933 Ryot agitation = N.G. Ranga**, not Parvataneni Veeraiah Chowdary (Parvataneni = **Pedanandipadu no-tax, 1921-22**, "Andhra Sivaji").

=== Ryots' Associations and the 1937 Rythu Rakshana Yatra
- **Krishna District Rythu Sangham (1915)**; Guntur and Godavari sanghams (1923).
- **Andhra Provincial Ryots Association / Andhra Rashtra Rythu Sangham – 1928**, started by **N.G. Ranga** (with **Narasimha Devara Satyanarayana, Maganti Bapineedu**); first General Secretary **B.V. Ratnam** (not a founder); **merged into the AIKS in 1936**.
- Nellore: Nellore Zilla Zamindari Rytu Sangam (Dodla Ramireddy, 1929); Zamin Rytu Sangam (Vennalkanti Raghavaiah, 1930) – paper '**Zamin Rytu**'.
- Visakhapatnam Peasant Mahasabha (**1929**) – first in North Coastal Andhra; paper **Prajavani**.
# Rythu Rakshana Yatra (1937)
- **Ichchapuram → Madras**; **launched by Ranga on 3 July 1937**, **led on the ground by Kommareddi Satyanarayana**.
- Compared to the **Dandi March**; ended with a memorandum to **Speaker Bulusu Sambamurthy** (Prakasam was Revenue Minister).
- NextGen also dates a Ranga cycle rally Itchapuram → Madras to **1938-39** (against the Madras Land Settlement Act).

=== Munagala Paragana Struggle (1930s)
- **Location:** **Nandigama taluk, Krishna district** then; now **Suryapet (earlier Nalgonda)**, Telangana.
- **Zamindar:** **Nayani Venkata Ranga Rao** (Keesaravari family) – literary patron but a harsh landlord.
- Bodies: Munagala Peasant Association (Batchala Jagannadh Das); Zamin Peasant Association (**Kodati Venkata Narasimha Rao**).
- **Kalukova Satyagraha (1938)** – **Nanduri Prasada Rao**, over a paddy wage of 80 seers per acre.
- **Brahmaiah Award (15 Jan 1939)** – by APCC Secretary **Gottipati Brahmaiah**; settlement covering **142 villages**.
- **Munagala Day: 11 June 1939**; agitation **withdrawn 19 June 1939** after Prakasam and Ranga promised action.
