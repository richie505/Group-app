## 3. Angles
- **Factual recall of a number:** "how many SDGs" is asked about 15 times, and 169 targets, 2030, 15 years, 193 states and MDG 2015 follow. This is the largest share of the row.
- **Goal number ↔ title:** SDG 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 15, 17. These are asked both directions, with stem keywords such as "lifelong learning" and "sustainable management of water".
- **NOT / odd one out:** outer space, life in sky, stop migration, internet for all, end racism, sports tournaments, and "reducing poverty".
- **Match:** goal ↔ description; problem ↔ goal; SDG ↔ Indian scheme.
- **Statements:** NIF by MoSPI plus 193 states; SDG Index 2023-24 (Group-II Mains 2025); circular economy (UPSC 2025); EU Green Deal.
- **Current linkage:** NITI SDG India Index editions (2019-20, 2020-21, 2023-24), the UN SDG Report 2022 and the SDSN index. The index scores and ranks are dated facts.

## 4. Repeats
- The AP SDG 7 rank in 2020-21 was asked 3 times. The "A Million Voices" report and the UN DESD were each asked twice, and one DESD stem misprints the resolution as 57/264. "SDG 1" (2025 ×2, 2021) and "how many SDGs" (2025 ×2) also repeat.
