=== Green Economy, Green Technology and Green Skills
# Green economy
- **Green economy:** reduces environmental risks and ecological scarcities; **decouples growth from resource use and pollution**; **central theme of Rio+20 (2012)**.
- **Green jobs:** work in agriculture, industry, services or administration that **preserves or restores environmental quality**.
- **UNEP Green Economy Initiative:** **2008**. **PAGE** (Partnership for Action on Green Economy): **2013**, a response to the **Rio+20** call ("The Future We Want"); UNEP, ILO, UNIDO, UNDP, UNITAR.
# Green technology
- **Green technology** pollutes less, uses resources sustainably and recycles more of its waste.
- **NOT correct:** "green technology is not expensive for developing countries" – **high cost is its main barrier**.
- **CII–Sohrabji Godrej Green Business Centre:** India's first platinum-rated green building – **Hyderabad**.
- **ISO 14001** = **Environmental Management System** (9001 quality, 22000 food safety, 27001 information security).
# Green chemistry and cleaner production
- **12 Principles of Green Chemistry:** **1998**, **Paul Anastas and John Warner**; core idea – **waste prevention rather than cleanup**; **Green Pharmacy** = green chemistry for medicines.
- **Green chemistry + cleaner production** reduce waste and pollution across the **whole product life cycle** – hence they serve **EIA** and **Life-Cycle Assessment (LCA)**.
# Green Skill Development Programme
- Launched **2017** by **MoEFCC** on the **ENVIS** network; trains youth in **biodiversity conservation, pollution monitoring, waste management, forestry and wildlife** (not semiconductors).

=== Circular Economy and the 3 Rs
- **Circular economy:** **reuse, recycling and repair** of materials to reduce waste; waste becomes a resource; replaces the linear **take–make–dispose** ("use and dispose") model.
- **3 Rs (environment-friendly):** **Reduce, Reuse, Recycle**.
| Linear model | Circular model |
|---|---|
| Take, make, dispose | Reduce, reuse, repair, recycle; keep materials in use |
| Waste = end point | Waste = resource; growth decoupled from resource use |
- **Why it cuts GHG emissions (UPSC 2025):** it **reduces raw-material inputs** and **reduces production waste** – both true and both explain the emission cut.
- **Not** exclusively about carbon or fossil fuels; **not** linear growth.
- **India:** NITI Aayog + MoEFCC committees drew up action plans for priority waste streams – **plastics, e-waste, batteries, tyres, metals**.

=== European Green Deal
- **December 2019**: make the EU **climate-neutral (net-zero GHG) by 2050**; at least **55% cut by 2030 vs 1990** – package **"Fit for 55"**.
- **Poland** did not commit to the 2050 climate-neutrality target at the December 2019 summit.
| Component | Content |
|---|---|
| **Farm to Fork** | Sustainable food system |
| **Circular Economy Action Plan** | Resource efficiency, recycling |
| **Biodiversity Strategy** | Protect and restore ecosystems (so the Deal is NOT only about carbon) |
| Just Transition Fund | Support for affected regions |
| Renovation Wave | Energy-efficient buildings |
> Order: GEI 2008 → Rio+20 2012 → PAGE 2013 → GSDP 2017 → Green Deal 2019 → Green Credit 2023.
~~ Sources: app notes 5:82 sub 2; Poland 2019: general knowledge

=== Greenwashing, Green Finance and ESG
# Greenwashing
- **Greenwashing** ("green sheen"): **false or exaggerated claims / promises** that a product, firm or country is environment-friendly.
- A **clear taxonomy** helps regulators curb **greenwashing and "transition washing"**.
# Green finance
| Instrument | Fact |
|---|---|
| Green bond | Pioneer **EIB 2007**, World Bank 2008 |
| Sovereign Green Bonds (India) | Announced **Budget 2022-23**; first issue **2023, Rs 8,000 crore** |
| Blue bond | **Ocean and water** projects; first sovereign – **Seychelles 2018** |
| India's first blue bond | **Sagarmala Finance Corporation** (planned) |
| RBI Green Deposit Framework | 2023 – deposits **earmarked for green lending** |
| Social bond / Sustainability bond (ICMA) | **Positive social outcomes** / a **mix of green and social** projects |
- **Climate finance taxonomy:** Budget 2024-25; **no final all-sector taxonomy** yet; **Ministry of Steel – Green Steel Taxonomy**; **MSE-GIFT** – green finance for MSMEs.
# ESG and BRSR
- **ESG** = **Environmental, Social, Governance**.
- **BRSR** set by **SEBI**: **top 1,000 listed companies** report audited ESG metrics yearly; **SEBI regulates ESG Rating Providers**.

=== Resource Efficiency, EPR and Green Credit
- **Draft National Resource Efficiency Policy, 2019** (**MoEFCC**): secondary raw materials; a **National Resource Efficiency Authority**.
- **EPR regimes:** **plastic packaging, e-waste, batteries, tyres**.
- **Green Credit Programme:** notified by **MoEFCC, October 2023**; **market-based incentive** for voluntary actions (tree plantation, water conservation); tightened August 2025.
- **National Green Hydrogen Mission:** Rs **19,744 crore**; **5 MMT a year** by 2030; linked to **net zero 2070**.
- **AMRUT 2.0:** treated used water for **20% of city** and **40% of industrial** demand.
