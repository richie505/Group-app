=== NOTES 5:81 | Goals of Sustainable Development – the 17 SDGs, Targets and India's Sdg Index Performance
## SUB 0 | Agenda 2030 – Origin and Adoption
[81:0:0] # Origin at Rio+20
[81:0:1] - SDGs were **conceptualised** at the **UN Conference on Sustainable Development (Rio+20)**, Rio de Janeiro, **2012**.
[81:0:2] - Rio+20 outcome **"The Future We Want"** called for global goals to **follow the MDGs**.
[81:0:3] - **Colombia and Guatemala** first proposed the SDG idea there.
[81:0:4] # Adoption
[81:0:5] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [81:0:5.0] Framework title | "Transforming our World: the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development" (not 2035)
  [81:0:5.1] Adopted by | All UN Member States, UNGA 70th session, September 2015
  [81:0:5.2] Goals / targets | 17 goals, 169 targets (not 196)
  [81:0:5.3] Period | 2015–2030 – a 15-year plan
  [81:0:5.4] In force | 1 January 2016 (from 2016)
  [81:0:5.5] Motto | "Leave no one behind"
[81:0:6] - A **universal call to action** to end poverty and protect the planet.
[81:0:7] Conceived at **Rio+20 (2012)**; adopted at **UNGA 70th session (2015)**; in force **2016**.
## SUB 1 | Nature of Agenda 2030
[81:1:0] - **Universal**: applies to **all nations** – developed, developing and least developed (MDGs were mainly for developing countries).
[81:1:1] - **Human-rights basis**: guided by the **Universal Declaration of Human Rights (1948)** and international human-rights treaties – **not** the Paris Agreement or Magna Carta.
[81:1:2] - Integrated: three dimensions – **economic, social, environmental**.
[81:1:3] - **USDG vision**: universal access to **clean water, sanitation and healthcare** is essential for **human dignity**.
[81:1:4] - In India: **NITI Aayog** coordinates and monitors SDGs (incl. the SDG India Index); **MoSPI** builds the **National Indicator Framework**.

[81:2:8] "Life in sky" and "Green Economy" are not SDGs; the two "life" goals are **Life Below Water (14)** and **Life on Land (15)**.

## SUB 3 | Goal-specific Targets
[81:3:0] # Goal 4 – Quality Education
[81:3:1] - **Free, equitable primary and secondary** education.
[81:3:2] - **Early-childhood** care.
[81:3:3] - **Affordable technical, vocational and tertiary** education.
[81:3:4] - End **gender disparity** in education; **youth and adult literacy**.
[81:3:5] - **Target 4.7**: by 2030 all learners acquire knowledge and skills to promote **sustainable development**, including **global citizenship**, a culture of **peace and non-violence** and appreciation of **cultural diversity**.
[81:3:6] # Goal 5 – Gender Equality
[81:3:7] - **End all discrimination and violence against women and girls** (PYQ answer).
[81:3:8] - End **child marriage** and **female genital mutilation**.
[81:3:9] - **Recognise unpaid care work**; women's **full participation in leadership**.
[81:3:10] # Goal 10 – Reduced Inequalities
[81:3:11] - Reforms needed: **better regulation of global financial markets**; **greater voice for developing countries** in global institutions; **safe and orderly migration**; **remittance costs below 3%**.
[81:3:12] Goal 10 target = remittance costs **below 3%**.

## SUB 5 | MDGs vs SDGs
[81:5:0] TABLE head: Feature | MDGs | SDGs
  [81:5:0.0] Goals / targets | 8 / 21 | 17 / 169
  [81:5:0.1] Period | 2000–2015 | 2015/16–2030
  [81:5:0.2] Source | Millennium Declaration, 2000 | 2030 Agenda, 2015
  [81:5:0.3] Scope | Mainly developing countries | Universal
  [81:5:0.4] Focus | Poverty, basic well-being | Social, economic, environmental; "leave no one behind"
[81:5:1] - **MDG results**: under-5 mortality **90 → 43** per 1,000 live births; maternal mortality ratio down **45%**.
[81:5:2] - India **achieved the MDG target** on eradicating extreme poverty and hunger (**halving extreme poverty**) before 2015.
[81:5:3] # Why SDGs Replaced MDGs
[81:5:4] - MDGs focused on **poverty and basic well-being** in poor countries; they had no strong environmental pillar.
[81:5:5] - SDGs add **sustainability** (Goals 11–17), **inequality** (Goal 10) and **institutions** (Goal 16), and they bind **all countries**, rich and poor.
[81:5:6] - SDGs build on MDG results: Goals 1–7 continue the unfinished MDG work on poverty, hunger, health, education, gender, water and energy.

=== NOTES 5:82 | Sustainable Development in Practice – Green Economy, Circular Economy and Esg
## SUB 0 | Green Economy and Green Technology
[82:0:0] - **Green economy**: reduces environmental risks and ecological scarcity; **decouples growth from resource use and pollution**; **central theme of Rio+20 (2012)**.
[82:0:1] - **Green jobs**: work in farming, industry, services or administration that **preserves or restores environmental quality**.
[82:0:2] - **Green technology**: pollutes less, uses resources sustainably, recycles more of its waste.
[82:0:3] Incorrect statement: "green technology is not expensive for developing countries". **High cost is its main barrier** – green technology is expensive for developing countries.
## SUB 1 | Circular Economy
[82:1:0] - Economic system built on **reuse, recycling and waste minimisation**; waste becomes a resource.
[82:1:1] - PYQ answer: "promotes the **reuse, recycling and repair** of materials to reduce waste".
[82:1:2] TABLE head: Linear model | Circular model
  [82:1:2.0] Take, make, dispose | Reduce, reuse, repair, recycle; keep materials in use
  [82:1:2.1] Waste = end point | Waste = resource; growth decoupled from resource use
[82:1:3] - How it cuts GHG emissions: it **reduces raw-material use** and **reduces production waste** (both correct).
[82:1:4] - India's action plans: **NITI Aayog + MoEFCC** committees → plans for priority waste streams (**plastics, e-waste, batteries, tyres, metals**).
[82:1:5] Circular economy does **not** focus exclusively on carbon or fossil fuels.
## SUB 2 | International Green Economy Initiatives
[82:2:0] - **UNEP Green Economy Initiative**: launched **2008**.
[82:2:1] - **PAGE** (Partnership for Action on Green Economy): launched **2013** in response to the **Rio+20** call; brings together **UNEP, ILO, UNIDO, UNDP, UNITAR**.
[82:2:2] # European Green Deal (December 2019)
[82:2:3] - **Climate-neutral EU by 2050**; at least **55% GHG cut by 2030 vs 1990**; legislative package = **"Fit for 55"**.
[82:2:4] TABLE head: Component | Content
  [82:2:4.0] Farm to Fork | Sustainable food system
  [82:2:4.1] Circular Economy Action Plan | Resource efficiency, recycling
  [82:2:4.2] Biodiversity Strategy | Protect and restore ecosystems
  [82:2:4.3] Just Transition Fund | Support for affected regions
  [82:2:4.4] Renovation Wave | Energy-efficient buildings
[82:2:5] Order: GEI 2008 → Rio+20 2012 → PAGE 2013 → GSDP 2017 → Green Deal 2019 → Green Credit 2023
## SUB 3 | Green Skill Development Programme
[82:3:0] - Launched **2017** by **MoEFCC**, building on the **ENVIS** network.
[82:3:1] - Trains youth for green jobs: **biodiversity conservation, pollution monitoring, waste management, forestry and wildlife**, allied environment sectors.
## SUB 4 | Green Chemistry and Cleaner Production
[82:4:0] - **12 Principles of Green Chemistry**: **1998**, **Paul Anastas and John Warner**; core idea – **waste prevention rather than cleanup**.
[82:4:1] - **Green chemistry + cleaner production** reduce waste and pollution across the **whole product life cycle** → serve the goals of **EIA** and **Life-Cycle Assessment (LCA)** (PYQ key).
[82:4:2] - **Green Pharmacy** = green chemistry applied to design, use and disposal of **medicines**.
## SUB 5 | Greenwashing
[82:5:0] - **Greenwashing** = **false or exaggerated claims** that a product, firm or investment is environment-friendly.
[82:5:1] - Remedy: a **clear taxonomy**, so regulators can curb **greenwashing and "transition washing"**.
## SUB 6 | Green Finance
[82:6:0] TABLE head: Instrument | Use of proceeds / fact
  [82:6:0.0] Green bond | Renewables, green transport, climate; pioneers EIB 2007, World Bank 2008
  [82:6:0.1] Sovereign Green Bonds | Announced Budget 2022–23; framework 2022; first issue 2023, Rs 8,000 crore
  [82:6:0.2] Blue bond | Ocean and water projects; world's first sovereign – Seychelles 2018
  [82:6:0.3] India's first blue bond | Sagarmala Finance Corporation, Rs 1,000 crore, planned FY 2026–27
  [82:6:0.4] RBI Green Deposit Framework | 2023; banks raise deposits earmarked for green lending
[82:6:1] - **Climate finance taxonomy**: announced **Budget 2024–25** (**Ministry of Finance**); **Ministry of Steel** has a **Green Steel Taxonomy**.
[82:6:2] - Gaps: **no final all-sector green taxonomy**; Rs 35 crore per-borrower PSL cap.
[82:6:3] - Municipal green bonds are rare (**Ghaziabad 2021, Indore 2023**); most ULBs lack credit ratings.
[82:6:4] - **MSE-GIFT**: green finance for MSMEs (2023–26).
[82:6:5] - **Social and sustainability bonds (ICMA):** a **social bond** funds projects with **positive social outcomes**; a **sustainability bond** funds a **mix of green and social** projects.
## SUB 7 | Esg and Brsr
[82:7:0] - **ESG** = **Environmental, Social, Governance** (three pillars).
[82:7:1] - **BRSR** (Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting): set by **SEBI**; **top 1,000 listed companies** publish **audited ESG metrics every year**.
[82:7:2] - **ESG ratings**: **SEBI regulates ESG Rating Providers**.
[82:7:3] - Public financial institutions publish Green Bond Frameworks + ESG financing policies; carry ESG impact ratings.
## SUB 8 | Resource Efficiency, Epr and Green Credit
[82:8:0] - **Draft National Resource Efficiency Policy, 2019** (**MoEFCC**): promotes **secondary raw materials** + a **National Resource Efficiency Authority**.
[82:8:1] - **EPR regimes**: **plastic packaging, e-waste, batteries, tyres**. 2025–26: **308 lakh tonnes** recycled; **296.53 lakh tonnes** of EPR certificates.
[82:8:2] - **Green Credit Programme**: notified by **MoEFCC, October 2023**; **market-based incentive** for voluntary actions (tree plantation, water conservation).
[82:8:3] - **Green Hydrogen Mission**: outlay **Rs 19,744 crore**; **5 MMT a year**; linked to **net zero 2070**.
[82:8:4] - **AMRUT 2.0**: reuse treated water for **20% of city demand + 40% of industrial demand**.
