=== Arcuate Style – Features and Architectural Firsts
- **Arcuate style:** **arch, dome and vault** brought by the Turks; the **true arch** is held by a **keystone**. Indian **trabeate** style = **lintel, pillar and corbel**; the two merged.
- **Materials:** rubble masonry in **surkhi** (brick dust in lime mortar), chunam plaster, red sandstone and marble; decoration moved away from **human and animal figures** to **arabesque**, geometry and **Quranic calligraphy**.
- **Terms:** **mihrab** = prayer niche; minbar = pulpit; **jali** = pierced stone screen.
| First | Building |
|---|---|
| Earliest Indo-Islamic building; first Sultanate mosque | **Quwwat-ul-Islam**, Delhi (Aibak, completed 1198) |
| First Islamic tomb (strict) | **Sultan Ghari** (**1231**), built by Iltutmish for his son **Nasiruddin Mahmud** |
| First tomb in a purely Indo-Islamic style; **first true arch** | **Balban's tomb**, Mehrauli |
| **First true dome**; first building fully on Islamic principles; **horse-shoe arch** introduced | **Alai Darwaza** (**1311**, Alauddin Khalji) |
| First **octagonal** tomb | **Khan-i-Jahan Tilangani's** tomb (Firoz Tughlaq's reign) |
| First **double dome** | **Sikandar Lodi's tomb** (Lodis pioneered it; Humayun's Tomb brought it to the Mughals) |
- **⚠ Disputed (UPPSC 2004 "first Indo-Islamic tomb"):** most keys say **Balban's tomb** (true arch); strictly **Sultan Ghari** is older.
# Dynasty → style
| Dynasty | Features | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Khalji** | **Horse-shoe arch with lotus-bud fringe**; true dome; red sandstone with marble (Seljuq influence) | Alai Darwaza |
| **Tughlaq** | **Battered (sloping) walls**, arch-beam combination, plain fort-like mass, **octagonal plan emerges**, encaustic tiles; Syrian/Byzantine-type false arches | Tughlaqabad; Ghiyasuddin's tomb (**sloping walls, marble dome**) |
| **Sayyid and Lodi** | **Octagonal tombs** with verandah, **double dome**, Hindu-style gateways, plaster ornament, drip-stones | **Sikandar Lodi's tomb** (octagonal), Bara Gumbad |
| **Sharqi** (Jaunpur) | **Massive propylon gateway with inclined walls**, no minarets | Atala Masjid, Lal Darwaza Mosque |
| **Vijayanagara** | **Bodegai** (bracket capital) on pillars | Hampi |
- **RAS 2013 match:** lotus-bud fringe – **Khaljis**; octagonal tomb – **Tughluqs**; bodegai – **Vijayanagara**; massive inclined-wall gate – **Sharqis** → **3 4 2 1**.
> Alai Darwaza = horse-shoe arch + first true dome; Balban's tomb = first true arch. The commonest confusion in this topic.

=== Qutb Minar and the Qutb Complex
| Storey | Builder |
|---|---|
| 1st | **Qutbuddin Aibak** – began it (c. 1199) |
| 2nd–4th | **Iltutmish** – completed it |
| Top: **5th storey** | **Firoz Shah Tughlaq** – replaced the lightning-damaged top storey and **added the fifth** |
| Repairs | **Sikandar Lodi** |
- **Name:** commonly believed to honour Sufi saint **Khwaja Qutbuddin Bakhtiyar Kaki** (⚠ debated: some say Aibak).
- **Height:** about **72.5 m (238 ft)**, **five** tapering storeys – "180 ft" is false.
- **UNESCO:** "**Qutb Minar and its Monuments, Delhi**" – **1993**: Minar, Quwwat-ul-Islam, Alai Darwaza, Alai Minar, Iron Pillar, Iltutmish's tomb, **Imam Zamin's tomb**. **Tughlaqabad is NOT** part of it.
- **Iron Pillar:** **Gupta** period; king "Chandra" = **Chandragupta II**; rust-free.

=== Latest: Rajon ki Baoli (2025)
- **Rajon ki Baoli** ("stepwell of the masons"), **Mehrauli Archaeological Park** – four-tiered, built **c. 1506** by **Daulat Khan Lodi** under **Sikandar Lodi**.
- **May 2025:** conserved by the **ASI** with **World Monuments Fund India** (TCS Foundation funding) and reopened to the public (as of 2026).
