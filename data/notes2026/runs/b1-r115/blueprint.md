## 4. Key traps settled
- **True arch (Balban's tomb) vs horse-shoe arch and first true dome (Alai Darwaza) vs first tomb (Sultan Ghari 1231)**: the "first Indo-Islamic tomb" key (Balban) is kept with a ⚠ Disputed line.
- **Tarikh-i-Firoz Shahi** has two authors (Barani, Afif); the printed key "Isami" for MPPSC 2019 was wrong.
- **Jahaz Mahal** = Ghiyasuddin Khalji (Malwa); MPPSC 2017's "Jantar Mahal" key "Alauddin Khilji" was wrong → Mahmud Shah Khalji (the only Malwa Khalji offered).
- **Diu**: Bahadur Shah gave it to the Portuguese (1535) and was killed there (1537), not Mahmud Begarha.
