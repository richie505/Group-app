## 3. Repeats and trends
- **Most repeated:** Khajuraho = Chandelas (7); Tarain 1191/1192 (8); Al-Biruni (6); first Muslim invaders = Arabs (4); Dilwara at Mount Abu (3); Ghori's first defeat = Mularaja II (3); Chandawar (3).
- **Key traps found:** the Ghaznavid founder is Sabuktigin (PMF answers this PYQ), not Alptigin; the second copy of the UPPCS 1991 Vidyadhara question was keyed "Jaishakti"; Odisha's tallest temple is the Jagannath, not Lingaraj; "Kirti Stambha" of Chittor = Kumbha only in books (the Jain tower is Jijaji's); Somnath has two options for the same king (Bhima I).
- The old notes were heavy on **Nagara sub-types, the Bhojshala and Somnath current-affairs lines, R.S. Sharma's feudalism and the number-game / trap lists**; these are asked 0–1 times in PYQs and are kept short for the own MCQs. Rajasthan-specific questions (Kumbha, Raso literature, Kachwahas) come from RPSC/UPPSC papers and were absent from the old notes; they are now covered.
