## SUB 6 | Microgravity and the Human Body
[42:6:0] - **Weightlessness** = free fall: satellite and astronaut fall with the same **g**, so the **normal force is zero**; mass stays the same.
[42:6:1] - No candle flame in space; astronauts cannot stand erect; an apple released moves along with the ship.
[42:6:2] - Moon weight = **1/6** of Earth's; at **Earth's centre**, true g is zero.
[42:6:3] TABLE head: Condition | Effect
  [42:6:3.0] Bone loss | 1–1.5% bone mass a month
  [42:6:3.1] Muscle atrophy | Postural muscles weaken
  [42:6:3.2] Fluid shift | "Puffy face, bird legs"
  [42:6:3.3] Space Adaptation Syndrome | Space motion sickness
  [42:6:3.4] SANS | Optic disc swelling, vision changes
[42:6:4] - **Charlie Brown effect:** fluid-shift **puffy face and thin legs** in microgravity (also 'puffy face-bird leg' syndrome).

=== NOTES 5:45 | Applications of Space Technology – Agriculture, Disaster Management, Telemedicine, Tele-education, Navigation
## SUB 0 | Remote Sensing Basics
[45:0:0] - **Remote sensing:** information about Earth's surface **without physical contact**, from reflected or emitted radiation.
[45:0:1] TABLE head: Type | Energy | Example
  [45:0:1.0] Active | Sends its own signal | RISAT radar
  [45:0:1.1] Passive | Natural radiation (sunlight) | Resourcesat, Oceansat
[45:0:2] - **Microwave / SAR** sees **through cloud and darkness** → flood mapping of cloud-covered areas (Assam's planned flood satellites may carry SAR, Mar 2026).
[45:0:3] - **InSAR (Interferometric SAR):** compares the **phase of two or more radar images** of the same area → **ground deformation** – earthquakes, **land subsidence**, landslides, volcanoes, glaciers.
[45:0:4] - **NISAR:** radar (**L-band and S-band**); centimetre-scale ground movement.
[45:0:5] - **Soil moisture:** best measured by **microwave/SAR** sensors.
[45:0:6] # What remote sensing can measure
[45:0:7] - Land cover, vegetation, **chlorophyll**, forest cover, **surface and groundwater prospects**, snow and glaciers, soil, wetlands, **mineral and hydrocarbon zones**, crop area and **crop yield** (YES-TECH).
[45:0:8] Assessing **soil nutrient levels through chemical testing** is NOT a remote-sensing application.
[45:0:9] APPSC 2024: InSAR = mapping ground deformation.
## SUB 1 | Spectral Indices and Gis
[45:1:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [45:1:0.0] NDVI | Normalized Difference Vegetation Index = (NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red); healthy vegetation reflects strongly in near-infrared; range −1 to +1; most widely used index for operational drought assessment
  [45:1:0.1] VCI (Vegetation Condition Index) | Based on NDVI; used for drought declaration (mandal-level in AP)
  [45:1:0.2] NH Green Cover Index 2025-26 | NHAI + NRSC; India's first satellite-based green-cover assessment of national highways; chlorophyll detection over ~30,000 km in 24 States
[45:1:1] # GIS components
[45:1:2] TABLE head: Component | Examples
  [45:1:2.0] Hardware | Computer, digitizer (tablet/table converting analog data to digital), scanner, GPS receiver, plotter
  [45:1:2.1] Software | QGIS – free, open-source, cross-platform desktop GIS to analyse/edit spatial data and compose maps
  [45:1:2.2] Data | Spatial (vector: points, lines, polygons; raster: satellite images) + attribute tables
[45:1:3] - GIS = **storing and manipulation of data**; each theme is a **layer**; **overlay analysis** combines layers.
[45:1:4] - Situational awareness: real-time layers for disaster response, flood alerts, shoreline mapping.
[45:1:5] - **Decision Support System:** data systems for planning that help organisations decide.
[45:1:6] **News channels** are NOT a source of real-time data for GIS in disaster response.

## SUB 5 | Disaster Management
[45:5:0] # Can vs cannot be monitored
[45:5:1] TABLE head: Can be monitored from space | Cannot
  [45:5:1.0] Floods, cyclones, drought, forest fires, tsunami inundation, landslides, post-quake ground deformation | Spread of a disease among humans (APPSC 2022), heart attack (APPSC 2025); earthquake prediction
[45:5:2] "Which CANNOT be monitored" – the answer is the human-health event.
[45:5:3] # Satellites and fire hotspots
[45:5:4] TABLE head: Satellite | Use
  [45:5:4.0] RISAT / SAR | Flood mapping through clouds
  [45:5:4.1] INSAT-3DS | Cyclone tracks
  [45:5:4.2] Cartosat | Damage mapping
  [45:5:4.3] Oceansat | Ocean-state data
[45:5:5] - **Bhuvan** combines RISAT/SAR and INSAT-3DS data for flood-inundation maps and cyclone warnings.
[45:5:6] - Fire hotspots: thermal-infrared **MODIS** (NASA Terra/Aqua) and **VIIRS** (Suomi-NPP); received at **Shadnagar** → **Forest Survey of India** alert system (not IMD).
[45:5:7] - **Ganga-Brahmaputra basin** = most flood-prone area in India (NRSC).
[45:5:8] # Disaster support institutions
[45:5:9] - **Disaster Management Support Programme (DMSP)** of ISRO: INSAT and IRS for **imaging and communication** support (floods, cyclones, droughts, fires, landslides, earthquakes).
[45:5:10] - **Decision Support Centre** at **NRSC** – single window for satellite inputs; **States must request data from NRSC**.
[45:5:11] - **NDEM = National Database for Emergency Management** – GIS repository for the full disaster cycle, at NRSC.
[45:5:12] - **NDMA** and SDMAs use GIS for planning, decision support, guidelines.
[45:5:13] - **INSAT Disaster Warning System:** Cyclone Warning Centre **Chennai → C-band uplink → S-band downlink** → coastal receivers.
[45:5:14] - **ISRO** led the **International Charter Space and Major Disasters** for **6 months from April 2025**.
[45:5:15] Earthquake prediction is NOT one of NRSC's disaster-support activities.

  [45:6:1.0] Nabhmitra | ISRO SAC, Ahmedabad | Fishermen safety device; two-way messaging via GSAT; tested at Neendakara (Kerala), Aug 2023 (APPSC 2023)
