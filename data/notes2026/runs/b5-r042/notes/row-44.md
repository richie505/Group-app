=== NSIL and Demand-driven Missions
- **Demand-driven model:** NSIL **builds, owns and operates** satellites against **assured customer demand** (Antrix was supply-driven).
| Mission | Fact |
|---|---|
| **GSAT-24** (June 2022) | First demand-driven satellite after the reforms; **24 Ku-band** satellite **built by ISRO**; launched by **Ariane-5 from Kourou, French Guiana** (not Balasore); entire capacity leased to **Tata Play** (DTH); inaugurated at Tata Play's broadcast centre, New Delhi (Aug 2023) |
| **GSAT-N2 (GSAT-20)** | **First demand-driven satellite fully funded and owned by NSIL**; **Ka-band** broadband, in-flight connectivity; **SpaceX Falcon 9, 19 Nov 2024** (not Q2 2025) – Indian rockets lacked capacity for it |
| LVM3 (2022) | First dedicated commercial mission – **36 OneWeb** satellites |
- 10 in-orbit satellites transferred to NSIL in 2022; NSIL launches: **141 satellites (138 customer)** as of July 2026.

=== Indian Space Policy 2023 and Space Legislation
- **Indian Space Policy 2023:** **approved by the Union Cabinet 6 April 2023**, **released by DoS 20 April 2023**; boosts the role of the Department of Space and private sector.
| Entity | Role under the policy |
|---|---|
| ISRO | Advanced R&D, deep space, **human spaceflight**; moves out of operational and commercial production |
| IN-SPACe | Independent regulator – promotes, authorises, supervises NGEs |
| NSIL | Commercialises mature ISRO technology |
| DoS | Policy, **space diplomacy, treaty compliance** |
| NGEs | End-to-end space activity |
- Sept 2026: ISRO "**will not be privatised or reduced**" to a regulatory body.
| Instrument | Year | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Draft Space Activities Bill | **2017** | Licensing of private space activity |
| **Space Activities Bill** | Finalised **May 2025** | Private participation; **statutory powers to IN-SPACe** |
| Liability Convention | 1972 | Liability on the **launching State** |
- India still has **no Space Activities Act**; no statutory liability framework yet.

=== Space Reforms 2020, FDI and Space Economy
- **June 2020 reforms** opened the sector to **Non-Governmental Entities (NGEs)**.
| Segment | Automatic-route FDI |
|---|---|
| Satellite components and subsystems | **100%** |
| Satellite manufacturing/operation, data | **74%** |
| Launch vehicles and spaceports | **49%** |
- Overall FDI up to **100%** in non-sensitive segments; beyond limits = Government route.
| Item | Figure |
|---|---|
| Current size | About US$8.4 billion (~2% global) |
| Target 2033 | **US$44 billion** (~8% global) |
| Other goals | **50 launches a year; 2 lakh jobs** |
| Venture Capital Fund | ₹1,000 crore via **SIDBI** |
| Space start-ups | **440+** (Aug 2026) |
