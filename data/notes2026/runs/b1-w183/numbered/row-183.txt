## SUB 3 | Bahadur Shah Zafar – Symbolic Leader
[3:0] - **Last Mughal emperor**; proclaimed **Shahenshah-e-Hindustan** (Emperor of India) by the rebels at Delhi.
[3:1] - Unwilling at first, he **vacillated but agreed** to assume the title; wrote to rulers to form a confederacy.
[3:2] - Proclamations stressed **Hindu-Muslim unity** and the return of the **Badshahi**.
[3:3] - Pension: **Rs 1 lakh per month** (Rs 99,000 at Delhi + Rs 1,000 at Lucknow) – "less than one lakh" is wrong. An **emperor without an empire**; poets **Zauq and Ghalib** at his court; **Hasan Askari** his spiritual guide.
[3:4] - **Fate:** captured after Delhi fell (Sep 1857), tried, **exiled to Rangoon (Yangon)** with Zeenat Mahal (Oct 1858); **died there in 1862** – died **in exile**.
[3:5] - Hindu-Muslim unity of 1857 was later cited against the **two-nation theory**.

## SUB 5 | Affected, Unaffected and Loyal Areas
[5:0] - **Main zone:** the **Doab, Awadh, Rohilkhand, Bihar, Bundelkhand and Central India** – **North and Central India**.
[5:1] - **Largely unaffected:** **Bengal, Punjab, eastern, western and southern India**; **Chittor** unaffected; **Jaipur** not a centre (Ajmer, Neemuch, Auwa were).
[5:2] - **Bihar:** Danapur, Patna and Arrah were storm centres; **Munger** is keyed as unaffected (BPSC 1995).
[5:3] TABLE head: Place | Feature
  [5:3.0] Nasirabad (May) and Neemuch (June 1857) | Sepoy risings in Rajputana
  [5:3.1] Tonk and Bhopal | Troops rebelled, rulers stayed loyal
  [5:3.2] Kota | Troops rose against their own ruler
  [5:3.3] Gwalior | Scindia loyal (most loyal dynasty); troops joined the Rani; he fled to Agra
[5:4] - **Did not join:** **Scindia of Gwalior, Nizam of Hyderabad** (Afzal-ud-Daula), Holkar, Mysore – the Nizam did **not** support the revolt.
