=== NOTES 2:40 | Chief Minister, State Council of Ministers and Advocate General
## SUB 0 | Position of the Chief Minister
[40:0:0] - CM = **real executive head** of the State and head of the Government; the Governor is the nominal head.
[40:0:1] - Leader of the majority party or coalition in the **Legislative Assembly**; leader of the House; chief spokesperson of the State Government.
[40:0:2] - **Art. 163(1)**: Council of Ministers headed by the CM aids and advises the Governor, **except where he must act in his discretion** – so not all functions are on advice.
[40:0:3] - CM = State counterpart of the **PM (Arts 74-75)**; the State has **no counterpart of the Vice-President**.
[40:0:4] - **Secretariat**: top wing of State administration; main force shaping the procedure rules of State Government.
[40:0:5] Executive is subordinate to the Legislature: the Council of Ministers is collectively answerable to the popular House.
## SUB 1 | Article 164 – Clause by Clause
[40:1:0] TABLE head: Clause | Provision
  [40:1:0.0] 164(1) | Governor appoints the CM (not the President); other Ministers on CM's advice; hold office during Governor's pleasure
  [40:1:0.1] 164(1A) | Size limit – 15%, minimum 12 (91st Amendment)
  [40:1:0.2] 164(2) | Collectively responsible to the Legislative Assembly alone
  [40:1:0.3] 164(3) | Oath administered by the Governor
  [40:1:0.4] 164(4) | Non-member can be Minister for six consecutive months, then ceases
  [40:1:0.5] 164(5) | Salaries fixed by the State Legislature by law; until then, Second Schedule
[40:1:1] - Ministers hold office at the Governor's pleasure but are **not collectively answerable to him**; not answerable to the **Legislative Council**.
[40:1:2] - CM must be a member of the Assembly or Council – need not be one when appointed, but must become one within **6 months**.
[40:1:3] - Resignation addressed to the **Governor**. CM's **term is not fixed** by the Constitution.
[40:1:4] - **Delhi and Puducherry**: CM appointed by the **President** (Art. 239AA Delhi; Art. 239A / GUT Act 1963 Puducherry).
[40:1:5] APPSC-GS 2021: CM is appointed under Art. 164(1) by the Governor. Clause order: (1) appointment, (2) responsibility, (3) oath, (4) six months, (5) salaries.
## SUB 2 | Appointment in A Hung Assembly and Floor Test
[40:2:0] - Constitution gives **no detailed criteria**; the Governor uses **discretion**.
[40:2:1] - **Sarkaria (1988) / Punchhi (2010)** order of invitation:
[40:2:2] - 1. Pre-poll alliance
[40:2:3] - 2. Single largest party with support
[40:2:4] - 3. Post-poll coalition
[40:2:5] - 4. Post-poll alliance with outside support
[40:2:6] - Followed by an **early floor test** (applied in Tamil Nadu, May 2026).
[40:2:7] # Tenure and Confidence
[40:2:8] - CM stays only while he has the **Assembly's confidence**; on losing it – **resign or be dismissed** by the Governor.
[40:2:9] - The Constituent Assembly cautioned against a literal reading of "pleasure".

## SUB 5 | Powers of the Cm and Duties Towards the Governor
[40:5:0] # Powers
[40:5:1] - Selects Ministers, **allocates portfolios**, can recommend removal of any Minister.
[40:5:2] - Advises the Governor on **summoning and prorogation** of the Legislature and **dissolution** of the Assembly.
[40:5:3] # Article 167 – Duties of the CM
[40:5:4] - 1. **Communicates** to the Governor all Council decisions on administration and **proposals for legislation**.
[40:5:5] - 2. **Furnishes information** the Governor calls for.
[40:5:6] - 3. If the Governor requires, **submits for the Council's consideration** any matter decided by a single Minister.
[40:5:7] - **Art. 167** defines the CM's duties; CM is the **chief link** between Governor and Council.
[40:5:8] - Governor asking for information under Art. 167 = a **constitutional discretion** of the Governor.

=== NOTES 2:41 | State Legislature – Assembly, Legislative Council and Article 169

## SUB 1 | Legislative Assembly – Strength, Term, Quorum
[41:1:0] # Strength – Article 170
[41:1:1] - **Not more than 500, not less than 60**, by **direct election** (FPTP).
[41:1:2] TABLE head: State / UT | Seats
  [41:1:2.0] Uttar Pradesh (largest) | 403
  [41:1:2.1] Andhra Pradesh | 175
  [41:1:2.2] Telangana | 119
  [41:1:2.3] Goa, Mizoram | 40 each
  [41:1:2.4] Puducherry (smallest UT Assembly) | 33 (30 elected + 3 nominated)
  [41:1:2.5] Sikkim (smallest; Art. 371F) | 32 – exception to the 60 minimum
[41:1:3] - **Arunachal Pradesh**: minimum 30 under Art. 371H, but actually has **60**.
[41:1:4] # Term and Reservation
[41:1:5] - **5 years** (Art. 172); can be **dissolved**, cannot be abolished.
[41:1:6] - **National Emergency**: Parliament may extend it by law **one year at a time** (Art. 172(1)), not beyond **six months** after the Emergency ends.
[41:1:7] - **Art. 332**: SC/ST seats in proportion to population. No caste reservation in the Rajya Sabha and Council.
[41:1:8] - **104th Amendment, 2019 (in force Jan 2020)**: ended **Anglo-Indian** nomination (**Art. 333**).
[41:1:9] - **106th Amendment, 2023**: about **one-third** seats for **women** (after post-census delimitation).
[41:1:10] # Quorum and Seats
[41:1:11] - **Quorum (Art. 189(3))**: **10 members or one-tenth**, whichever is **greater** (70-member House → 10).
[41:1:12] - **Art. 190**: no one can be a member of **both Houses** or of the legislatures of **two States**.
[41:1:13] - **Art. 190(3)(b)**: resignation takes effect only on the **Speaker's / Chairman's acceptance**.
[41:1:14] - **Art. 190(4)**: seat **vacated** after **60 days' absence** without permission – vacation, not Art. 191 disqualification.

## SUB 4 | Money Bills and Ordinary Bills
[41:4:0] # Money Bills – Articles 198, 199, 207
[41:4:1] - Introduced **only in the Assembly**, by a minister, on the **Governor's recommendation** (**Art. 207(1)**; APPSC-GS 2018 key).
[41:4:2] - **Art. 199(3)**: **Speaker certifies** a Money Bill – sole and final.
[41:4:3] - **Art. 198(2)**: Council returns within **14 days**, else deemed passed; cannot reject or amend; Assembly may accept or reject its recommendations.
[41:4:4] # Ordinary Bills – Article 197
[41:4:5] - Council holds a Bill **not more than 3 months** on first passage; after re-passage, **1 month** – maximum **4 months**; then deemed passed.
[41:4:6] - Governor may reserve a State Bill for the President (**Art. 200**); President considers it (**Art. 201**).
[41:4:7] Council delay: 14 days (Money Bill) vs 3 + 1 months (ordinary Bill). There is NO joint sitting in States (unlike Art. 108).
