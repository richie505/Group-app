=== National Green Tribunal – Set-up, Purpose and Structure
# Set-up (most asked)
| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| NGT Act passed | **2 June 2010** |
| NGT established / Act in force | **18 October 2010** (falls in the **11th Five Year Plan, 2007-12**) |
| Started functioning | **July 2011** |
- **Full form:** **National Green Tribunal**; created by the **NGT Act 2010** – a **statutory (not constitutional)** body; constitutional anchor **Article 21** (right to a healthy environment), not Article 28.
- **Purpose:** **effective and expeditious disposal of cases relating to environmental protection and conservation of forests and other natural resources**, enforcing environmental legal rights and giving **relief and compensation** – i.e. it **handles environmental and pollution-control cases**; monitoring pollution and setting emission/water standards are **CPCB/SPCB** jobs.
- India is the **third country** with a specialised environmental tribunal, after **Australia and New Zealand**; NGT reduces the litigation burden on higher courts.
- **Procedure:** **not bound by the CPC 1908** (or the Evidence Act); follows **natural justice**; powers of a civil court; applies sustainable development, precautionary and **polluter-pays** principles and **absolute liability** for accidents.
- **Time limits:** disposal within **6 months**; appeal to the **Supreme Court within 90 days**.
# Composition and Chairpersons
- **Members:** Chairperson + **not less than 10 and up to 20** full-time **judicial** members and as many **expert** members (not "5 to 10").
- **Chairperson:** must be **a judge of the Supreme Court or a Chief Justice of a High Court** (serving or retired) – an ordinary HC judge does not qualify.

# Benches – five places of sitting
| Bench | Location |
|---|---|
| Principal | **New Delhi** (headquarters) |
| Zonal | **Pune** (west), **Bhopal** (central), **Chennai** (south), **Kolkata** (east) |
| Circuit | Shimla, Shillong, Jodhpur |
- **Five places of sitting** (New Delhi + 4 zonal) – not six; zonal bench is at **Pune, not Mumbai**; Jodhpur and Shillong are only **circuit** benches.

=== NGT – Jurisdiction, Predecessors and Landmark Orders
# Acts under the NGT (Schedule I – 7 Acts)
| Under NGT | Outside NGT |
|---|---|
| Water Act 1974; Water Cess Act 1977; Forest (Conservation) Act 1980; Air Act 1981; Environment (Protection) Act 1986; **Public Liability Insurance Act 1991**; Biological Diversity Act 2002 | **Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972**; Indian Forest Act 1927; Forest Rights Act 2006 |
# NGT vs CPCB (UPSC 2018)
| NGT | CPCB |
|---|---|
| Judicial tribunal set up by the **NGT Act 2010** | **Statutory** regulator under the **Water Act 1974** – **not** an executive order |
| Environmental justice; **reduces litigation burden on higher courts**; awards compensation | Promotes **cleanliness of streams and wells**; improves **air quality**; sets standards, monitors |
- The CPCB cannot direct the NGT; the NGT can direct the CPCB.
# Predecessors
| Law | Purpose | Fate |
|---|---|---|
| **National Environment Tribunal Act, 1995** | **Strict liability** for **hazardous-substance accidents** | **Never brought into force**; repealed by NGT Act 2010 |
| **National Environment Appellate Authority Act, 1997** | **Appeals against environmental clearances** | NEAA dissolved; role taken over by NGT |
- Both repealed by **Section 38** of the NGT Act; NGT **combines original jurisdiction** over disputes with NEAA's **appellate role**.

=== EIA – Meaning, Origin and Related Tools
# What EIA is
- **EIA:** systematic evaluation of the likely environmental impacts of a **proposed project before it is implemented** (studying the environmental effects of a project – not a census, pollution check or plantation); linked with **environmental clearance (EC)**; governed by the **EP Act 1986**.
- **Global origin:** **NEPA, USA, 1969**.
- **India:** **1976-77** – Planning Commission asked the **DST** to examine **river-valley projects** (first sector); **1978** – EIA first used; until 1994 EC was only an administrative decision.
- **R2 Code of Practices is NOT EIA** – it is for **responsible electronics recycling**.
# EIA vs related tools
| Tool | Focus |
|---|---|
| **EIA** | A specific **project, before it starts** |
| **LCA** (Life-Cycle Assessment) | A **product or process** across its whole life – **cradle to grave** |
| **SEA** (Strategic Environmental Assessment) | **Policies, plans, programmes** – upstream of projects (e.g. a transport policy) |
| **Cumulative Impact Assessment** | **Combined effect of several projects** in one area |
| **Environmental audit** | **Compliance of a running unit**, after the fact |
- **Cleaner production:** cuts waste and pollution **at the source** (process change, input substitution) – preventive, not end-of-pipe.
- **Green chemistry:** **12 principles – Paul Anastas and John Warner, 1998**.
- **Sector-wide EIAs** strengthen **strategic and cumulative** assessment.
> Exam angle: EIA = project before start; LCA = product life cycle (APPSC 2025).

=== EIA Cases and Andhra Pradesh Projects
# Post-facto clearance cases
| Case | Holding |
|---|---|
| Common Cause (2017) | Odisha mining beyond EC – pay **100% of the value** of illegally mined mineral |
| Alembic v. Rohit Prajapati (2020) | Ex post facto EC contrary to law; **compensation, not closure** of every project; 2002 circular quashed |
| **Noble M. Paikada (21 Mar 2024)** | Struck down item 6 of the **28 Mar 2020 notification** exempting **extraction of ordinary earth for linear projects (roads, pipelines)** from EC – a blanket exemption violating **Article 14** |
| Vanashakti (May 2025) | Retrospective EC a "gross illegality" |
| Recall (Nov 2025) | **2:1** majority recalled it (not unanimous) |
| Final (**29 July 2026**) | **CJI Surya Kant** bench: **2021 Office Memorandum** nullified **prospectively**; ex post facto EC must meet **Articles 14 and 21** (proportionality) |

- **Amaravati:** NGT (17 Nov 2017) upheld the EC; held it **Category B**.

=== EIA Process, Notifications, Public Hearing and PARIVESH
# Stages
| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Screening** (first) | Is EIA needed; which category |
| **Scoping** | Key issues; **Terms of Reference** |
| Baseline data | Existing environment surveyed |
| **Impact prediction** | **Alternatives** compared; mitigation, EMP |
| Public consultation | Hearing + written responses |
| **Appraisal** | **Expert committee evaluates the EIA report** |
| Decision | EC with conditions or rejection |
| **Monitoring** | **EMP implementation checked** |
# Notifications
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| **27 Jan 1994** | First EIA Notification under the EP Act; **Schedule I** projects needed MoEF clearance |
| **1997** | Amendment made **public hearing mandatory** |
| **14 Sep 2006** | EIA Notification 2006: basis shifted **from investment size to impact potential**; EC compulsory for **39 categories** |
| **2018** | **PARIVESH** portal |
| 2020 | **Draft EIA** – **not finalised**; criticised for **post-facto EC**, hearing notice **30 → 20 days**, violations reportable **only by the violator or government** |
- **Categories under EIA 2006** (Category B is appraised by the **state** SEAC, not the central EAC):
| Category | Appraisal | Clearance | EIA |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | EAC (central) | **MoEFCC** | Full EIA, no screening |
| **B1** | **SEAC** | SEIAA | **EIA needed** |
| **B2** | SEAC | SEIAA | **No EIA** |
# Public hearing
- Conducted by the **SPCB**, **District Collector** presiding; notice at least **30 days**; completed within **45 days**.
- Exempt: defence/strategic projects (status decided by the **Central Government**), **B2** projects, notified industrial estates – **Category A is not exempt**.
- APPCB hearing (May 2026) on a Water Aerodrome at **Prakasam Barrage** – draft EIA called error-ridden.
# PARIVESH and appraisal bodies
- **PARIVESH (2018):** **single-window online portal** for environment, forest, wildlife and CRZ clearances.
- **July 2026 amendment:** SEAC delay beyond **120 days** escalates to the new **Standing Committee on EIA** on PARIVESH; **SAEIA** stands in for SEIAA; the Centre may **remove EAC/SEAC/SEIAA members mid-term** for persistent delays.
- Members serve 3-4 years; **age limit 70**.
- **Criticism:** EIAs prepared by **consultants paid by the project proponents**; fix – an **independent EIA authority**.
