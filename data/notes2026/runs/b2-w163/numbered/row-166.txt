[3:1] - **TRYSEM** = Training of Rural Youth for Self-Employment → **employment (self-employment) to rural youths**.
[3:2] # MGNREGA
[3:3] - **100 days** of **unskilled manual work** per household per year; **one-third** women; unemployment allowance after **15 days**.
[3:4] - **Gram Panchayat** plans, executes and implements works on the **Gram Sabha's** recommendation; **Gram Sabha** conducts the **social audit**; AP's **SSAAT** pioneered social audits.
[3:5] - **Barefoot technicians**: local youth trained (by NIRDPR) to measure and supervise MGNREGA works.
[3:6] - **Geo-MGNREGA (2016):** assets geotagged with ISRO's **Bhuvan**; linked to **SDG 1 and SDG 8**.

  [3:7.2] Funding | Centre paid wages | CSS 60:40; NE/Himalayan 90:10
  [3:7.3] Allowance | States | Allowance and delay compensation fully by States
  [3:7.4] Status | Repealed | Assent 20 Dec 2025; in force 1 July 2026
