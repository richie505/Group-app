## SUB 2 | Chabahar, INSTC and Connectivity Corridors
[163:2:0] - **Chabahar:** **Sistan-Baluchestan**, Gulf of Oman; access to **Afghanistan and Central Asia bypassing Pakistan**; MoU 2015; **trilateral India-Iran-Afghanistan agreement, May 2016** (PM Modi's visit); **Shahid Beheshti** terminal via **IPGL**; **10-year** contract **May 2024**.
[163:2:1] - **Sanctions:** India follows **UN** sanctions, not unilateral Western ones; the US Chabahar waiver was revoked **Sept 2025**, a 6-month waiver followed (Oct 2025 – Apr 2026), and ended in 2026. The US strikes on Iran (July 2026) did **not** damage the Chabahar terminal.

## SUB 3 | Energy Security and Pipelines
[163:3:0] - Imports ~**85-88%** of crude, ~**50%** of gas; ~50% of crude via **Hormuz**.
[163:3:1] - **Third-largest** energy consumer; four A's: availability, accessibility, affordability, **acceptability** (not autonomy).
[163:3:2] - **TAPI** = Turkmenistan-Afghanistan-Pakistan-India; **IPI** (Iran-Pakistan-India) = **"Peace Pipeline"** (~2,775 km).
[163:3:3] - **SPR:** Visakhapatnam, Mangaluru, **Padur** (**5.33 MMT**); **9.5 days**; **UAE** the only country storing oil in India's SPR.
