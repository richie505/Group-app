[3:1] - **2001-11:** urban **+31.8%** decadal growth; rural/urban **68.84% / 31.16%**; absolute increase urban **91.0 m > rural 90.4 m** – first time since Independence.
[3:2] - **Census towns 1,362 → 3,894**; statutory towns 3,799 → 4,041.
[3:3] - **Main reason for the higher urban increase 2001-11: reclassification** of erstwhile rural areas as urban (new census towns), not migration (**APPSC G1 2017**).
[3:4] - **Three components**: **natural increase**, **net rural-urban migration**, **reclassification / new towns**.
[3:5] - **Causes (APPSC 2008 – "all of the above")**: growth of **industry and trade** in towns; **population pressure on rural land**; **low farm productivity**; services and transport.
[3:6] - Hidden urbanisation: World Bank (2010) **55.3%** "urban-like"; EAC-PM ~**63%** (2015, **satellite data**); UN "Degree of Urbanisation" would count peri-urban areas.

## SUB 5 | 74th Amendment, Urban Schemes and Urban Problems
[5:0] - **74th Amendment, 1992**: in force **1 June 1993**; **Part IXA** (Art. 243P-243ZG) + **Twelfth Schedule** (18 functions). **Art. 243ZE**: Metropolitan Planning Committee; two-thirds elected by municipal members and panchayat chairpersons.
[5:1] - ULBs: Nagar Panchayat – area in transition; Municipal Council – smaller urban area; Municipal Corporation – larger urban area.
[5:2] - **16th Finance Commission (2026-31)**: **₹3.6 lakh crore** for ULBs; urban share **36% → 45%**. ES 2025-26: cities "**administered, not governed**" (too many agencies, low own revenue).
[5:3] TABLE head: Scheme | Year | Key facts
  [5:3.0] Smart Cities Mission | 2015 | 100 cities, 4 rounds; includes Visakhapatnam
  [5:3.1] AMRUT | 25 Jun 2015 | 500 cities, now 485; AMRUT 2.0 (2021) ₹2,99,000 crore
  [5:3.2] HRIDAY | 2015 | 12 heritage cities, incl. Amaravati
  [5:3.3] PMAY-U 2.0 | 2024 | 1 crore families, ₹2.30 lakh crore, to 2028-29
  [5:3.4] SPM Rurban Mission | 2016 | Rural clusters – Ministry of Rural Development (not urban rejuvenation)
  [5:3.5] SBM-U 2.0 | 1 Oct 2021 | "Garbage Free" cities
  [5:3.6] Urban Challenge Fund | Budget 2025-26 | ₹1 lakh crore, competition-based
  [5:3.7] City Economic Regions | Budget 2026-27 | ₹5,000 crore per CER
[5:4] - **Slums: 6.54 crore** (2011); cities **~3% of land, ~60% of GDP** – ES 2025-26: "**critical economic infrastructure**"; urban population **~60 crore by 2031**.
[5:5] - **Peri-urban areas**: **recharge zones** for cities. **Right to the City** – **Henri Lefebvre** (1968).
[5:6] - **UN-Habitat** (1978, **Nairobi**): **World Cities Report 2026** at WUF **Baku** – 3.4 billion face housing shortage, **1.1 billion** in slums; MoU for a Centre of Excellence for Urban Affairs, **Amaravati** (Jul 2026).
[5:7] - **APMCHUD**: set up **2006**, first meeting **New Delhi**, secretariat hosted by **India**; 6th edition **New Delhi, 2016**.
[5:8] - **World Urbanization Prospects 2025 (UN DESA)**: **33 megacities** in 2025, 37 by 2050.
