## SUB 2 | Natural Farming – ZBNF to APCNF
[2:0] # Origin and Agency
[2:1] - **ZBNF** developed **mid-1990s** by **Subhash Palekar** (Padma Shri); "zero budget" = **no purchased fertilisers or pesticides**.
[2:2] - AP launched ZBNF in **2016**; renamed **APCNF = Andhra Pradesh Community-managed Natural Farming**. **2018** = AP's Year of Natural Farming.
[2:3] - **RySS = Rythu Sadhikara Samstha**: **not-for-profit company** of the **AP Department of Agriculture**; incorporated **October 2014**; implements APCNF.
[2:4] - **Target (2018)**: make AP **India's first 100% natural farming (ZBNF) State by 2024** (6 million farmers); now "100% natural farming" by 2047.
[2:5] - **ZBNF won 3rd place** (top 10 of 120 entries) at the **Paris Peace Forum, Nov 2018**.
[2:6] # Practices
[2:7] TABLE head: Practice | Meaning
  [2:7.0] Beejamrutham | Seed treatment
  [2:7.1] Jeevamrutham | Bio-fertiliser: cow dung, urine, jaggery, pulse flour, soil
  [2:7.2] Ghanajeevamrutham | Solid form of Jeevamrutham
  [2:7.3] Mulching (Acchadana) | Crop residue cover: saves moisture, checks weeds
  [2:7.4] PMDS | Pre-Monsoon Dry Sowing on residual moisture
  [2:7.5] 365-day green cover | Soil covered all year by cover crops
[2:8] - Trap crops: marigold, castor. PMDS on 92,000 acres in Kurnool (Jul 2026).
[2:9] # Coverage and Targets
[2:10] - Now: **10.20 lakh farmers, 4.89 lakh ha**, 4,116 GPs. Targets: all GPs by 2026-27; 41 lakh farmers on 19 lakh ha by 2028-29.
[2:11] - Unit = **cluster of about 5 Gram Panchayats/villages** (~2,000 farmers; 1,500-2,500 acres).
[2:12] - **Pesticide use** falling: 2,650 MT (2014-15) → 1,990 MT (2023-24); **266.166 MT a.i. reduction in 2023-24** and 193.248 MT in Kharif 2024-25, credited to **awareness** by the Agriculture Department.
[2:13] # Funding, Awards and Replication
[2:14] - **KfW** (German bank) loan; **Azim Premji Foundation** grants.
[2:15] TABLE head: Award | Detail
  [2:15.0] M.S. Swaminathan Award for Environment Protection | To RySS (2022-23)
  [2:15.1] Gulbenkian Prize for Humanity (shared) | 2024; Lisbon, 11 July 2024
  [2:15.2] Food Planet Prize | Sweden (Curt Bergfors Foundation); launched with US$1 million prizes; 2026 prize US$1.5 million, Bastad, June 2026; APCNF first Indian finalist
[2:16] - APCNF = "**world's largest agro-ecological transition**"; copied in **Zambia and Sri Lanka**.
[2:17] - **NMNF** = natural farming (no external inputs); **PKVY** = **organic**, clusters of **50 farmers / 50 acres**, PGS-India certified.
[2:18] - NMNF action plans: **7.78 lakh ha, 19.75 lakh farmers**; PKVY: 200 tribal clusters in Visakhapatnam. RySS earlier ran the **Debt Redemption Scheme 2014**; ZBNF features: minimum tillage, livestock integration, crop diversity, no chemicals. Kenyan and Gujarat delegations visited Guntur (2026).
[2:19] - **ISHA Foundation awards (28 July 2024)**: four APCNF paddy farmers from **Guntur, Krishna, West Godavari and Srikakulam** honoured at Sripuram (Vellore), Save Soil campaign.
