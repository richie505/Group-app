[0:0] - Residual AP has **six agro-climatic zones** (SES 2024-25 Table 5.2): regions defined by climate, soil, topography – they decide the best cropping systems.
[0:1] TABLE head: # | Zone | Districts (13 old districts)

  [0:1.1] 2 | Godavari | East Godavari, West Godavari
  [0:1.2] 3 | Krishna | Krishna, Guntur, Prakasam
  [0:1.3] 4 | Southern | Chittoor, YSR Kadapa, SPS Nellore
  [0:1.4] 5 | Scarce Rainfall | Kurnool, Anantapuramu

[0:2] - **No "Eastern Zone"** and **no "Low Altitude" zone**: the sixth is **High** Altitude and Tribal Areas.
[0:3] - **Chittoor** is Southern, not North Coastal or Scarce Rainfall; **Nellore** is Southern, not Krishna or Scarce Rainfall; Kurnool is Scarce Rainfall, not Southern.
[0:4] - Undivided AP (NARP): **7 zones** (incl. North and Southern Telangana; Krishna-Godavari as one zone).
[0:5] - Not the six **Article 371-D** zones (administrative zones for public employment).

## SUB 1 | Rainfall, Drought and Cloud Seeding
[1:0] TABLE head: Zone | Annual rainfall
  [1:0.0] North Coastal | 1,000-1,100 mm
  [1:0.1] Krishna-Godavari | 800-1,100 mm
  [1:0.2] Southern | 700-1,100 mm
  [1:0.3] Scarce Rainfall | 500-750 mm (lowest)
  [1:0.4] High Altitude and Tribal | above 1,400 mm (highest)
[1:1] - **Average rainfall** of (undivided) AP: about **890-940 mm** (Group-I 2003 key: 890 mm). June-Dec 2024: **960.3 mm** vs normal **860.0 mm** (+11.7%).
[1:2] - **Drought-prone**: Scarce Rainfall Zone (Kurnool, Anantapuramu). Highest rainfall: North Coastal + tribal highlands.
[1:3] - **Cloud seeding:** first artificial rain experiment in AP at **Atmakur (Anantapur)**; the 2004 programme used **silver iodide** and **sodium chloride**.
[1:4] - **Kharif 2026**: all 28 districts in deficit; worst hit Annamayya, Prakasam, Chittoor, Markapuram, Visakhapatnam (Sep 2026).
[1:5] ~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.8; app notes 3:121; general knowledge for the 2003 average (not checked)

[2:2] - AP lies in the **Southern cotton zone** with Telangana and Karnataka.
[2:3] - District agricultural contingency plans: **ICAR-CRIDA**; **PMDS** under natural farming uses leftover soil moisture in drought-prone areas.
