## SUB 6 | Natural Farming – Zbnf to Apcnf
[120:6:0] # Origin and Agency
[120:6:1] - **ZBNF** developed **mid-1990s** by **Subhash Palekar** (Maharashtrian agriculturist, **Padma Shri**).
[120:6:2] - "**Zero budget**" = **no purchased fertilisers or pesticides** (not zero cost for all inputs or labour).
[120:6:3] - Features: **minimum tillage, livestock integration, crop diversity, no chemicals**.
[120:6:4] - AP launched ZBNF in **2016**; renamed **APCNF = Andhra Pradesh Community-managed Natural Farming**: the Agriculture Department's natural-farming initiative.
[120:6:5] - **2018** declared AP's **Year of Natural Farming**.
[120:6:6] - **RySS (Rythu Sadhikara Samstha)**: **not-for-profit company** of the **AP Department of Agriculture**; incorporated **October 2014**; implements APCNF; earlier ran the Debt Redemption Scheme 2014.
[120:6:7] # Practices
[120:6:8] TABLE head: Practice | Meaning
  [120:6:8.0] Beejamrutham | Seed treatment
  [120:6:8.1] Jeevamrutham | Bio-fertiliser: cow dung, urine, jaggery, pulse flour, soil
  [120:6:8.2] Ghanajeevamrutham | Solid form of Jeevamrutham
  [120:6:8.3] Mulching (Acchadana) | Crop residue/straw cover: saves soil moisture, checks weeds
  [120:6:8.4] PMDS | Pre-Monsoon Dry Sowing on residual moisture
  [120:6:8.5] 365-day green cover | Soil covered all year by cover crops
[120:6:9] - Trap crops: marigold, castor. PMDS on 92,000 acres in Kurnool (Jul 2026).
[120:6:10] # Coverage and Targets
[120:6:11] - Now: **10.20 lakh farmers, 4.89 lakh ha**, 4,116 GPs.
[120:6:12] - Targets: **all GPs by 2026-27**; **41 lakh farmers on 19 lakh ha by 2028-29**; "100% natural farming state" by 2047.
[120:6:13] - Unit = **cluster of 5 villages (1,500-2,500 acres)**.
[120:6:14] # Funding Partners
[120:6:15] - **KfW** (German development bank): loan for expansion; **Azim Premji Foundation**: technical support grants.
