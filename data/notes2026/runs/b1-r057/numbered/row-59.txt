## SUB 0 | The Three Crowned Kingdoms – Capitals, Ports, Emblems, Rivers
[0:0] # Muvendar
[0:1] - **Muvendar** = the three crowned kings: **Chera, Chola, Pandya** – the only ruling dynasties of the Sangam age.
[0:2] TABLE head: Kingdom | Capital | Port(s) | Emblem | River | Territory
  [0:2.0] Chera | Vanji = Karur (Karuvur) | Muziris (Mushiri, Muchiri), Tondi | Bow | Periyar | Central/north Kerala + Kongu
  [0:2.1] Chola | Uraiyur → Puhar | Puhar (Kaveripattinam) | Tiger | Kaveri | Kaveri delta, Tiruchi up to southern Andhra
  [0:2.2] Pandya | Madurai | Korkai (pearl fishery) | Fish | Vaigai | Madurai, Tirunelveli, Ramnad
[0:3] - **Chera capital** = Karur/Vanji, not Kanchipuram or Madurai (State PSC).
[0:4] - **Pandya lifeline** = **Vaigai** (printed "Vengi" in one PYQ) – not Godavari, Krishna or Tungabhadra.
[0:5] - **Ma'bar** = Arab writers' name for the **Pandya** kingdom (Coromandel coast) (G2 2000, APPSC PYQ).
[0:6] - **Odd one out:** **Pallavas** (rose from the **4th c. CE**) and **Kadambas** (4th c., Banavasi) are **not** Sangam dynasties (UPPSC 2010; State PSC).
[0:7] # Ports by coast
[0:8] TABLE head: Coast | Ports
  [0:8.0] West (mainly Chera) | Muziris (Mushiri), Tondi, Naura, Nelisanda
  [0:8.1] East | Korkai (Pandya, Gulf of Mannar), Puhar (Chola), Arikamedu (near Puducherry)
[0:9] - **Chera kingdom parts:** **Musiri and Tondi** (IAS 1996); Korkai is Pandya and Puhar Chola.
[0:10] - **Korkai, Poompuhar and Muchiri** were all **ports** (UPSC 2023).
[0:11] - **Uraiyur** (inland Chola capital) was a **cotton** centre, named in the Periplus.
[0:12] # Why small kingdoms?
[0:13] - South India grew **small regional kingdoms** rather than large empires because it lacked **vast continuous fertile plains** – its fertile land lies in separate river deltas (IAS 1999).

## SUB 1 | Rulers – Karikala, Udiyanjeral, Senguttuvan, Nedunjeliyan
[1:0] TABLE head: Ruler | Kingdom | Achievement / title
  [1:0.0] Karikala (Karikalan) | Chola | Won Venni (Cheras, Pandyas + 11 chieftains) and Vahaipparandalai; built the Kallanai (Grand Anicut) on the Kaveri; subject of Pattinappalai
  [1:0.1] Udiyanjeral (Perum Sorru Udhiyan Cheralathan) | Chera | First historical king of the Sangam age; legend: fed the armies of the Mahabharata war
  [1:0.2] Imayavaramban Nedunjeral Adan | Chera | Claimed his boundary up to the Himalayas
  [1:0.3] Senguttuvan ("Red Chera") | Chera | Founded the Pattini cult (Kannagi as the ideal wife); idol stone from the Himalayas; Sri Lankan king Gajabahu attended (not Augustus)
  [1:0.4] Nedunjeliyan | Pandya | Won Talaiyalanganam over the Chera, Chola and Velir chiefs → Pandya control of the Tamil country; "Arya Padai Kadantha" Nedunjeliyan executed Kovalan
[1:1] - **Karikala** = Chola (G2 2000); **Udiyanjeral** = **Chera** (Uttarakhand 2021); "Udiyanjeral – Chola" is the wrong pair (UPSC 2026).
[1:2] - **Renati, Pottapi and Nellore Chodas** (Telugu) claimed descent from **Karikala**.
[1:3] - **15 Aug 2026:** remains of a Sangam-period water structure found in the **Kollidam** river.
[1:4] ~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval India Part 3 p.31-34; app notes 1:59 (sub 3, 4, 5)
## SUB 2 | Sangam Kingdoms and Rome
[2:0] - **Cheras** = the power most associated with **trade with Rome**: Romans kept a settlement at **Muziris** and built a **temple of Augustus** there (State PSC).
[2:1] - **Pandya embassy to Augustus:** a Pandya king sent an ambassador to Rome (c. **26–20 BCE**) (MPPSC 2005) – not Chola or Chera.
[2:2] - Pliny called Muziris the "first emporium of India".
[2:3] ~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval India Part 3 p.33-34; Rocket Indian History Part 1 p.89; app notes 1:59 (sub 1)

[4:1] - **18 May 2026:** the **Leiden copper plates** (Chola grants to the Chulamanivarma vihara, Nagapattinam) – seal: Chola **tiger** with the Pandya **two fish** and the Chera **bow**.
