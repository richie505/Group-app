## SUB 1 | Gathasaptasati – the Chief Source of Satavahana Society
[1:0] # Hala's anthology
[1:1] - **Author:** Satavahana king **Hala**; about **700 verses (gathas)** in **Maharashtri Prakrit** on love, village and family life.
[1:2] - **Chief literary source** of Satavahana social and cultural history (G2 Prelims 2019; APPSC PYQ) – not the Aitareya Brahmana, Dharmasutras or Puranas.
[1:3] - **Opening:** begins with a prayer to **Shiva (Pasupati)** and ends with a prayer to **Gauri** (APPSC PYQ); Saiva names Pashupati, Rudra, Gauri, Ganapati appear.
[1:4] - **Women poets in it:** **Madhavi, Revati, Anupamalabdha, Anulakshmi**.
[1:5] - **Telugu words in it:** atta, addam, potta, pilla, padi.
[1:6] > Exam angle: "most important source of Satavahana society" = **Gathasaptasati of Hala**; opening prayer = **Shiva**.
[1:7] ~~ Sources: NextGen Satavahanas p.1-2, p.14, p.23-26; app notes 1:57 (sub 5)
## SUB 2 | Dress, Cosmetics and Amusements
[2:0] # Dancers and dress
[2:1] - **Foundation cream of Satavahana dancers:** **Aradalam** – in Telugu "**maipoota / Maiputa**" (body coating) (G2 Mains 2019 and G2 2019).
[2:2]   - **⚠ Settled:** "Maiputa = Kusuma Lepanam" (old app key) is wrong – Maiputa is the Telugu word for the foundation cream, which NextGen and Rocket name **Aradalam**; Kusuma Lepanam, Mukhateji and Meni-kanti are distractors.
[2:3] - **Reliefs (Amaravati, Nagarjunakonda):** **men** – lower cloth and **turban**; **women** – heavy **anklets, bangles, girdles, necklaces**.
[2:4] - **Amphitheatre at Nagarjunakonda (Vijayapuri):** for **performances and games**, not Vedic sacrifices.

## SUB 3 | Varna and Caste
[3:0] # Varna in early Andhra
[3:1] - **Caste of the Satavahanas:** **Brahmins** – Gautamiputra Satakarni is called **Ekabrahmana** (Eka Brahmin) and **Agama Nilaya** in the Nasik prasasti; kings upheld **varna-dharma**.
[3:2] - **Nanaghat inscription:** Brahmins trained in Vedic learning and ritual; Brahmins got **tax-free villages**.
[3:3] - **Kshatriya status by assimilation:** **Greeks, Sakas, Pahlavas, Kushanas** took Indian names and faiths – e.g. Nahapana's daughter **Dakshamitra**, son-in-law Rishabhadatta.
[3:4] - **Vishnukundins:** followed varnasramadharma; **farmers = "the backbone"**, got land grants.

[3:6] - **Samayamu** = caste associations of the **Kakatiya** period (later term).

## SUB 4 | Position of Women and Marriage
[4:0] # Women
[4:1] - **Patriarchal joint family**, yet women had **respect and freedom** – joined men in festivals and gatherings and **donated generously** to viharas and chaityas.
[4:2] - **Matronymics:** kings bore their mothers' names – **Gautamiputra** Satakarni, **Vasishthiputra** Pulumavi.
[4:3] - **Titles from husbands:** **Mahasenapatni, Mahabhojaki, Maharathi**.
[4:4] - **Sati:** a **Nagarjunakonda sculpture** and the Greek writer **Strabo**.
[4:5] - **Polygamy** was practised in the **upper classes**; widows observed **celibacy**; marriage was **sacred**.
[4:6]   - **⚠ Settled:** the PYQ "what inscriptions tell us about women" has three true options (prominence, lavish donations, matronymics) and no "all of these" – left unkeyed; "no polygamy among princes" is false.

## SUB 5 | Education and Popular Religion
[5:0] # Education
[5:1] TABLE head: Centre | Nature
  [5:1.0] Viharas | Buddhist monastic learning (Nagarjunakonda a leading centre)
  [5:1.1] Ghatikasthanas | Vedic colleges (Vishnukundins)
  [5:1.2] Agraharas | Land grants to Vedic Brahmins
[5:2] - **Indrabhattaraka Varma** (Vishnukundin) – "**Ghatika Vapta punyasancharya**"; credited with founding ghatikas.
[5:3] # Religion and folk cults
[5:4] - **Amaravati reliefs:** worship of **nagas, yakshas and sacred trees** beside Buddhist symbols.
[5:5] - **Ikshvaku kings** performed **Vedic sacrifices** (**Vasishthiputra Chamtamula**: Asvamedha, Vajapeya); **queens** patronised Buddhism; Brahmanical shrines such as **Pushpabhadraswami** stood at Nagarjunakonda.
[5:6] - **Vishnukundins:** family deity **Sriparvataswamy**; titles **Paramamaheswara, Parama Brahmanya**.
