## 3. Repeats and traps
- **Repeated (same fact, 3+ times):** Yavanapriya = pepper (×3); Arikamedu as the Roman station (×6); Bodhisiri (×3); Allahabad Pillar = Samudragupta (×5); Gupta era = Chandragupta I (×3); Napoleon of India (×3); Silappadikaram author (×2), Kural = Laghuveda (×2), Tolkappiyam = grammar (×3).
- **Keys settled against the printed or old key:**
  - **Maiputa:** this is the Telugu wording of the G2 2019 "foundation cream" question. The answer is **Aradalam**, not Kusuma Lepanam. The old notes had copied that wrong key as a fact.
  - **Devarashtra** = Yelamanchili (Visakhapatnam). The old note said S. Kota (Vizianagaram).
  - **Kaurala** = Kolleru (Kielhorn's identification; it is disputed).
- **Source conflicts found:**
  - One NextGen Indian History page (Part 3 p.2) says Chandragupta I defeated the Sakas and minted the first silver coins. That is wrong: both belong to Chandragupta II (PMF; NextGen p.4, p.7).
  - Param Bhagavata: Rocket gives Samudragupta (Nalanda and Gaya plates); the official UPPSC key gave Chandragupta II.
  - UPSC 2000 A–R: PMF keys (a); some keys give (b).
