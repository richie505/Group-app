=== Indo-Roman Trade – Arikamedu, Muziris, Yavanapriya
# Arikamedu – the east-coast Roman station
- **Arikamedu** (near **Puducherry**) = **Poduke** of the Periplus; the **foremost Indo-Roman trading station of eastern India** (State PSC; APPSC ASO 2011; APPSC PYQ).
- **Finds:** Roman **amphorae**, red-glazed **Arretine ware**, rouletted ware, beads and gems, an **intaglio of Augustus**, Tamil-Brahmi potsherds – proof of brisk India–Rome trade in the early Christian era (UPPCS 2003; UP Lower 2009).
- **Excavated** by **Sir Mortimer Wheeler** in **1945**.
- **Amphora** = a **tall double-handled jar** for **wine and oil**; found at Arikamedu, Kodumanal, Uraiyur, Alagankulam (UPPSC 2014) – not painted grey or black-and-red ware.
# Muziris – the chief centre
- **Muziris (Mushiri, Muchiri)**, the Chera pepper port, = the **chief centre of Indo-Roman trade** on the Indian side; Pliny's "first emporium of India" (APPSC G1). Alexandria was only the Roman-side entrepot.
- Identified with **Pattanam** (**Ernakulam**, Kerala) by KCHR excavations (Jun 2026: still continuing).
# Goods
| Exports | Imports |
|---|---|
| **Pepper** and spices, cotton and muslin, **pearls**, ivory, **precious stones** | **Gold**, horses, **sweet wine**, Roman pottery; Roman coins |
- **Yavanapriya** ("dear to the Yavanas") = **black pepper** (UPSC 1995 ×3) – not muslin, ivory or dancers.
- **Sangam economy (statements):** pearls and precious stones were main exports; the Tamils traded with **Egypt and Arabia**; **decline of trade** helped the decay of the Tamil kingdoms; **Uraiyur**, not Madurai, was famous for **cotton**.
# Evidence and peak
| Source | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Periplus of the Erythraean Sea** | c. **60 CE**, anonymous Greek merchant – chief account |
| **Pliny the Elder** | Rome lost **55 million sesterces** a year to India |
| Coin hoards | Gold and silver of **Augustus, Tiberius and Nero**; hoards at **Akkanpalle, Kondapur** |
- **Peak:** **Satavahana–Sangam age** (1st c. BCE–2nd c. CE); trade grew after Rome took **Egypt (30 BCE)** and the monsoon route was used; it did **not** flourish after the Satavahanas (G2 2025).
- **Indian embassies to Rome:** best known – the **Pandya ruler to Augustus**.
- Tamil ports traded with **Berenike** and **Myos Hormos** on the **Egyptian Red Sea coast**.

=== Social Groups and Customs – Vellalar, Vattakirutal
# Tolkappiyam's four castes
| Order | Caste | Role |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Arasar** | Rulers |
| 2 | **Anthanar** | Priests (Brahmins) |
| 3 | **Vanigar** | Traders |
| 4 | **Vellalar** | **Rich peasants / landowners** – the **fourth** caste (APPSC G1) |
- Other groups: **Uzhavar** = ordinary farmers (ploughmen); **Kadaisiyar, Adimai** = landless labourers and slaves; **Panar** = wandering bards; **Parayar** = drummers who worked with skins; **Viraliyar** = women dancers at courts.
# Society of the Sangam age (IAS 1998)
- **No complex caste system** – a rigid varna system had not developed; kings married into Vellala families.
- **Gradual Aryanisation** – Brahmins gained influence; Pandya kings performed **Vedic sacrifices**. Only this statement fits.
# Customs and terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Vattakirutal** | A king **defeated in battle** commits **ritual suicide by starving** himself to death (UPSC 2023 ×2) |
| **Kalavu** | Pre-marital (secret) love |
| **Karpu** | Married life / wedded chastity |
| **Pukal** | **Glory** – the hero's goal in puram poems |
| **Nadukal** | **Hero stone** for a warrior fallen in battle |

=== Religion, Women and Festivals
# Gods
- Chief deity: **Seyon / Murugan**; his six abodes = **Arupadai Veedu**.
- **Mayon** = Vishnu; **Vendan** = Indra; **Varunan**; **Korravai** = goddess of **war and victory**.
- **Hero stones** and megalithic burials were practised.
- **Onam:** Kerala harvest festival of the return of king **Mahabali**, already mentioned in the Sangam idyll **Maduraikanchi** (UPPSC 2011) – not Rama's victory or Durga's killing of Mahishasura.
# Women
- Women poets: **Avvaiyar**, **Nachchellaiyar, Kakkaipadiniyar**.
- **Sati** was prevalent mainly in the **higher strata**; widows' life was hard.

=== Tinai – the Five-fold Division of Land
- The five-fold division of land (**tinai**) is given in the **Tolkappiyam**.
| Tinai | Landscape | Occupation | Deity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kurinji** | Hilly | Hunting, honey | **Murugan** |
| **Mullai** | Pastoral / forest | Cattle rearing | **Mayon** (Vishnu) |
| **Marutam** | Agricultural plains | Agriculture | **Vendan** (Indra) |
| **Neidal** | Coastal | **Fishing, salt making** | **Varunan** |
| **Palai** | Desert / dry | Robbery | **Korravai** – the only goddess |
