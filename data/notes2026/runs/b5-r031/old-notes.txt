=== NOTES 5:32 | Data Protection and Privacy - Puttaswamy, Dpdp Act, Data Localisation
## SUB 0 | Right to Privacy Cases
[32:0:0] - **K.S. Puttaswamy v UoI** (**24 Aug 2017**): **9-judge**, **unanimous**; privacy intrinsic to life and liberty under **Article 21** and **Part III**.
[32:0:1] - Overruled **M.P. Sharma (1954)** and **Kharak Singh (1962)** insofar as they denied privacy.
[32:0:2] - Tests: **legality** (a law needed; public aim alone not enough), **legitimate aim**, **proportionality**.
[32:0:3] - **Gobind v State of MP (1975)**: privacy part of personal liberty. **PUCL v UoI (1997)**: interception safeguards.
## SUB 1 | Aadhaar Judgment (26 Sep 2018)
[32:1:0] - **5 judges, 4:1**; dissent **Justice D.Y. Chandrachud**.
[32:1:1] TABLE head: Upheld | Struck down
  [32:1:1.0] Aadhaar Act; welfare use | Mandatory bank account and mobile SIM linking
  [32:1:1.1] PAN/ITR linking (Sec. 139AA, IT Act) | Sec. 57 private-entity use
[32:1:2] Puttaswamy = 9 judges, unanimous; Aadhaar = 5 judges, 4:1.

  [32:2:0.0] 2012 | Justice A.P. Shah Group of Experts on Privacy

[32:2:1] - **Kris Gopalakrishnan** (2020) = **non-personal data**; **A.K. Bhargava** (DoT, 2015) = **net neutrality**.
## SUB 3 | Dpdp Act 2023 - Scope and Terms
[32:3:0] - Only **digital** personal data; excludes **non-personal** data; covers foreign entities offering goods/services in India.
[32:3:1] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [32:3:1.0] Data Principal | Individual whose data is processed; for a child (under 18), parent/guardian
  [32:3:1.1] Data Fiduciary | Decides purpose and means (GDPR: Data Controller)
  [32:3:1.2] Significant Data Fiduciary | DPO in India, independent auditor, impact assessment (no consent exemption)
  [32:3:1.3] Consent Manager | Registered entity to give, manage, withdraw consent
## SUB 4 | Rights, Duties and Children
[32:4:0] - Rights: information/access, correction, **erasure (Sec. 12)**, grievance redress, withdrawal of consent, **nomination**.
[32:4:1] - Duties: no false information, impersonation or frivolous complaints.
[32:4:2] - Children: **verifiable parental consent** below 18; **no tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted ads**.
## SUB 5 | Exemptions and Rti
[32:5:0] - **Sec. 7**: legitimate uses (State processing without consent). **Sec. 17**: Centre may exempt notified **State agencies** (sovereignty, security, public order); research, archiving, statistics; possibly startups; **journalism - no exemption**.
[32:5:1] - **Sec. 44(3)** amended **RTI Sec. 8(1)(j)**: all personal information may be refused; **public-interest override removed**.
[32:5:2] - Challenge referred to a **Constitution Bench** (Aug 2026).

[32:7:0] - Draft **3 Jan 2025**; final notified **14 Nov 2025**: Board provisions immediately; **Consent Manager Nov 2026**; full compliance **May 2027** (18 months).

[32:8:0] - **GDPR** (**EU**): adopted **April 2016**, applied **25 May 2018**; replaced 1995 Directive. Penalties financial + criminal; DPDP financial only.

[32:8:2] - **Data portability:** an explicit right under the GDPR (Art. 20); the DPDP Act, 2023 gives no statutory right to data portability.
