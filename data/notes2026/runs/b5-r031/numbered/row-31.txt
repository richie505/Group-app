## SUB 0 | CERT-In, NCSP 2013 and the Nodal Agencies
[0:0] # CERT-In (Indian Computer Emergency Response Team)
[0:1] - **Status:** national nodal agency for **cyber security** and cyber incident response; operational since **January 2004**, under **MeitY**; statutory under **Sec. 70B, IT Act** (2008 amendment).
[0:2] - **Functions:** identify cyber incidents; **issue alerts and advisories on the latest cyber threats and countermeasures** (daily), vulnerability notes, guidelines and white papers; emergency measures; coordinate incident response.
[0:3] - **Not its job:** cyber treaties (MEA), private companies' security budgets, legal support in cybercrime cases (police / I4C). RAW, ISRO and NITI Aayog are not cyber-security agencies.
[0:4] - **CERT-In Directions 2022:** report specified incidents within **6 hours**; logs kept in India.
[0:5] # National Cyber Security Policy (NCSP) 2013
[0:6] - Released **2013** by DeitY, then under the Ministry of Communications and IT (now **MeitY**); India's first comprehensive cyber-security policy.
[0:7] - **Vision:** a **secure and resilient cyberspace** for citizens, businesses and government.
[0:8] - **Target:** **5 lakh** cyber security professionals in **5 years**.
[0:9] - **Aims:** create an **Information Security Assurance Framework**; protect critical information infrastructure; indigenous security technology, R&D and PPP; trained workforce.
[0:10] - Every ministry, department and organisation names a **Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)**.
[0:11] TABLE head: Function | Nodal agency
  [0:11.0] Incident response, crisis management | CERT-In
  [0:11.1] Critical information infrastructure | NCIIPC
  [0:11.2] Situational awareness, timely information-sharing | NCCC (National Cyber Coordination Centre)
[0:12] > APPSC asks CERT-In twice: "agency for cyber security" and "issues alerts and advisories".

## SUB 1 | Cyber Security Basics, C-DAC Tools and Threats in News
[1:0] # Cyber security
[1:1] - **Cyber security:** protecting computer-based information from unauthorised access, transfer, modification or destruction; threats = hacking, malware, cybercrime, cyber terrorism, data theft.
[1:2] - **Features:** **compliance**, **defence against internal threats**, **threat prevention** – so "all of the above".
[1:3] - Legal base for cybercrime: **IT Act 2000**, amended **2008** (sexually explicit material, video voyeurism, data leakage by intermediaries, e-commerce fraud).
[1:4] # C-DAC security software
[1:5] - **NAYAN:** **C-DAC** software against **cyber attacks and data theft** (UPPCS key; AVARODH and TRIKAL are distractors).
[1:6] - **USB Pratirodh:** C-DAC tool that controls unauthorised **USB storage devices**; with **AppSamvid** (application whitelisting) released free via **Cyber Swachhta Kendra** (Feb 2017).
[1:7] - Exam key: **NAYAN, USB Pratirodh and Malware Resist** are all C-DAC tools (IAS key: I, II and III).
[1:8] # Threats in news
[1:9] - **StrandHogg** (Dec 2019): **Android** vulnerability flagged by MHA's cyber wing (I4C Threat Analytical Unit) – lets malicious apps pose as genuine apps and steal data; found by Promon (Norway). Trojan Horse, Morris Worm (1988), Timofonica (first phone virus, 2000) are older malware.
[1:10] # Forensic sciences
[1:11] - **Gujarat Forensic Sciences University (GFSU), Gandhinagar:** first university dedicated to **forensic and allied sciences**; set up by the **Gujarat Government** under a State Act (**Act 17 of 2008, published 30 Sep 2008**), **not PPP**; Director General **Dr J.M. Vyas**; working from **February 2009** (not July 2010). Upgraded to **National Forensic Sciences University** (INI, Oct 2020).
[1:12] > TSLPRB 2018: incorrect statements = **B (PPP) and E (July 2010)**.

## SUB 2 | Policy Evolution – Draft Strategy and CCMP
[2:0] - **Draft National Cyber Security Strategy:** prepared under the **National Cyber Security Coordinator** in the **NSCS**; still a **draft, not notified** – **NCSP 2013 remains operative**.
[2:1] - There is **no "NCSP 2020" in force**: 2020 = the draft strategy.
[2:2] - **Cyber Crisis Management Plan (CCMP):** **MeitY** framework against cyber attacks and **cyber terrorism**; followed by **all Central and State Governments**; 205 workshops till July 2025.
