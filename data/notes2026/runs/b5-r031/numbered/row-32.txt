## SUB 0 | Right to Privacy – Article 21 and Puttaswamy
[0:0] # Justice K.S. Puttaswamy (Retd) v Union of India
[0:1] - **Decided 24 Aug 2017** by a **9-judge** bench, **unanimous**: right to privacy is **intrinsic to life and personal liberty under Article 21** and part of the freedoms of **Part III** – a **Fundamental Right**.
[0:2] - So **Article 21** secures **life, personal liberty and (read in) privacy**; not Art. 14, 15, 17, 19, 20, 22, 23, 24, 29, and not the DPSPs or any amendment.
[0:3] - Petition filed by **Justice K.S. Puttaswamy (Retd)**, a former Karnataka High Court judge, against Aadhaar.
[0:4] - Overruled **M.P. Sharma (1954)** and **Kharak Singh (1962)** insofar as they denied privacy.
[0:5] - **Three-fold test:** **legality** (a law is needed; a public aim alone is not enough), **legitimate aim**, **proportionality**.
[0:6] TABLE head: Case | Point
  [0:6.0] Gobind v State of MP (1975) | Privacy part of personal liberty
  [0:6.1] PUCL v UoI (1997) | Safeguards on telephone interception
  [0:6.2] Puttaswamy (2017) | Privacy = Fundamental Right (Art. 21 + Part III)
[0:7] > Asked 7 times (APPSC AEE 2023, Welfare Officers, Group-2; UPSC 2018, 2021, 2024): the answer is always **Article 21 / Part III**.

## SUB 1 | GDPR and the Right to Be Forgotten
[1:0] - **GDPR** (General Data Protection Regulation) – **European Union**: adopted **April 2016**, applied from **25 May 2018**; replaced the **1995** Data Protection Directive; explicit consent before processing.
[1:1] - GDPR: administrative fines, member states may add criminal penalties; DPDP Act: **financial penalties only**.
[1:2] - **Right to Be Forgotten:** began in **Google Spain v AEPD (2014)**; = **Right to Erasure, GDPR Art. 17** (DPDP: erasure Sec. 12). In India it limits **name-based searches** and **does not erase judicial records**.
[1:3] - **Data portability:** explicit GDPR right (Art. 20); no statutory right under the DPDP Act.
[1:4] ~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 6 p.46; app notes 5:32 sub 8

## SUB 4 | DPDP Act 2023 – Scope and Terms
[4:0] - Covers only **digital** personal data; excludes **non-personal** data; also applies to **foreign entities** offering goods/services in India.
[4:1] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [4:1.0] Data Principal | Individual whose data is processed; for a child (under 18), the parent/guardian
  [4:1.1] Data Fiduciary | Decides purpose and means (GDPR: Data Controller)
  [4:1.2] Significant Data Fiduciary | DPO in India, independent auditor, impact assessment (no consent exemption)
  [4:1.3] Consent Manager | Registered entity through which consent is given, managed, withdrawn
[4:2] ~~ Sources: app notes 5:32 sub 3
## SUB 5 | Rights, Duties and Children
[5:0] - **Rights:** information/access, correction, **erasure (Sec. 12)**, grievance redress, withdrawal of consent, **nomination** (no right to impose criminal penalties).
[5:1] - **Duties:** no false information, impersonation or **frivolous complaints**.
[5:2] - **Children:** **verifiable parental consent below 18**; **no tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted ads**.
[5:3] ~~ Sources: app notes 5:32 sub 4
## SUB 6 | Exemptions and RTI
[6:0] - **Sec. 7:** **legitimate uses** (State processing without consent).
[6:1] - **Sec. 17:** Centre may exempt notified **State agencies** (sovereignty, security, public order); research, archiving, statistics; **journalism – no exemption**.
[6:2] - **Sec. 44(3)** amended **RTI Sec. 8(1)(j)**: personal information may be refused; **public-interest override removed**; challenge referred to a **Constitution Bench** (Aug 2026).

## SUB 8 | DPDP Rules 2025, Localisation and Surveillance
[8:0] - **DPDP Rules:** draft **3 Jan 2025**; final notified **14 Nov 2025** – Board provisions at once; **Consent Manager Nov 2026**; full compliance **May 2027** (18 months).
[8:1] - Rules require clear, specific, informed consent; plain-language notices; purpose limitation; data minimisation; retention controls.
[8:2] - **Localisation:** **RBI (April 2018)** – payment-system data stored **only in India**. DPDP cross-border transfer = **blacklist** (allowed unless the Centre restricts); GDPR = **whitelist / adequacy**.
[8:3] - First national standards for **cloud, data centres, ethical AI** focus on bias, data sovereignty, privacy.
[8:4] - **Pegasus:** SC (27 Oct 2021, CJI N.V. Ramana) set up a technical committee overseen by **Justice R.V. Raveendran** (retd SC judge); malware on **5 of 29** phones, not clearly Pegasus.
[8:5] - **AP:** AP Police **AI4AP** pilot (Chittoor, Guntur, Annamayya) raises DPDP issues.
