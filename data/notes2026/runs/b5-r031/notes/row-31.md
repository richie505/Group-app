=== CERT-In, NCSP 2013 and the Nodal Agencies
# CERT-In (Indian Computer Emergency Response Team)
- **Status:** national nodal agency for **cyber security** and cyber incident response; operational since **January 2004**, under **MeitY**; statutory under **Sec. 70B, IT Act** (2008 amendment).
- **Functions:** identify cyber incidents; **issue alerts and advisories on the latest cyber threats and countermeasures** (daily), vulnerability notes, guidelines and white papers; emergency measures; coordinate incident response.
- **Not its job:** cyber treaties (MEA), private companies' security budgets, legal support in cybercrime cases (police / I4C). RAW, ISRO and NITI Aayog are not cyber-security agencies.
- **CERT-In Directions 2022:** report specified incidents within **6 hours**; logs kept in India.
# National Cyber Security Policy (NCSP) 2013
- Released **2013** by DeitY, then under the Ministry of Communications and IT (now **MeitY**); India's first comprehensive cyber-security policy.
- **Vision:** a **secure and resilient cyberspace** for citizens, businesses and government.
- **Target:** **5 lakh** cyber security professionals in **5 years**.
- **Aims:** create an **Information Security Assurance Framework**; protect critical information infrastructure; indigenous security technology, R&D and PPP; trained workforce.
- Every ministry, department and organisation names a **Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)**.
| Function | Nodal agency |
|---|---|
| Incident response, crisis management | **CERT-In** |
| Critical information infrastructure | **NCIIPC** |
| Situational awareness, timely information-sharing | **NCCC** (National Cyber Coordination Centre) |
> APPSC asks CERT-In twice: "agency for cyber security" and "issues alerts and advisories".

=== Cyber Security Basics, C-DAC Tools and Threats in News
# Cyber security
- **Cyber security:** protecting computer-based information from unauthorised access, transfer, modification or destruction; threats = hacking, malware, cybercrime, cyber terrorism, data theft.
- **Features:** **compliance**, **defence against internal threats**, **threat prevention** – so "all of the above".
- Legal base for cybercrime: **IT Act 2000**, amended **2008** (sexually explicit material, video voyeurism, data leakage by intermediaries, e-commerce fraud).
# C-DAC security software
- **NAYAN:** **C-DAC** software against **cyber attacks and data theft** (UPPCS key; AVARODH and TRIKAL are distractors).
- **USB Pratirodh:** C-DAC tool that controls unauthorised **USB storage devices**; with **AppSamvid** (application whitelisting) released free via **Cyber Swachhta Kendra** (Feb 2017).
- Exam key: **NAYAN, USB Pratirodh and Malware Resist** are all C-DAC tools (IAS key: I, II and III).
# Threats in news
- **StrandHogg** (Dec 2019): **Android** vulnerability flagged by MHA's cyber wing (I4C Threat Analytical Unit) – lets malicious apps pose as genuine apps and steal data; found by Promon (Norway). Trojan Horse, Morris Worm (1988), Timofonica (first phone virus, 2000) are older malware.
# Forensic sciences
- **Gujarat Forensic Sciences University (GFSU), Gandhinagar:** first university dedicated to **forensic and allied sciences**; set up by the **Gujarat Government** under a State Act (**Act 17 of 2008, published 30 Sep 2008**), **not PPP**; Director General **Dr J.M. Vyas**; working from **February 2009** (not July 2010). Upgraded to **National Forensic Sciences University** (INI, Oct 2020).
> TSLPRB 2018: incorrect statements = **B (PPP) and E (July 2010)**.

=== Policy Evolution – Draft Strategy and CCMP
- **Draft National Cyber Security Strategy:** prepared under the **National Cyber Security Coordinator** in the **NSCS**; still a **draft, not notified** – **NCSP 2013 remains operative**.
- There is **no "NCSP 2020" in force**: 2020 = the draft strategy.
- **Cyber Crisis Management Plan (CCMP):** **MeitY** framework against cyber attacks and **cyber terrorism**; followed by **all Central and State Governments**; 205 workshops till July 2025.
