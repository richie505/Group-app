=== MSME Definition – Evolution and Current Limits
| Year | Small-scale / MSME limit |
|---|---|
| **1950** | Small unit = **gross fixed capital (investment) up to ₹5 lakh** |
| 1966 → 1975 → 1980 | Plant and machinery ₹7.5 lakh → ₹10 lakh → ₹20 lakh |
| **1991** | Industrial Policy: small unit **₹35 lakh → ₹60 lakh**; ancillary ₹45 lakh → ₹75 lakh |
| 1995-96 | **Foreign / other-industry equity in SSI up to 24%** |
| 1997 → 1999 | ₹3 crore → **₹1 crore (₹100 lakh)** – the limit in **2004** |
| **2 Oct 2006** | **MSMED Act** – separate manufacturing / services limits |
| **1 June 2020** | **Composite investment + turnover** criterion notified (effective 1 July 2020) – latest classification at the 2021 exam |
| **1 April 2025** | Limits raised (Budget 2025-26) |
- **Original MSMED Act limits (investment only):**
| MSMED Act 2006 | Manufacturing (plant and machinery) | Services (equipment) |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | up to ₹25 lakh | up to ₹10 lakh |
| **Small** | **above ₹25 lakh – ₹5 crore** | up to ₹2 crore |
| Medium | above ₹5 crore – ₹10 crore | up to ₹5 crore |
- **2020 composite criterion (Atmanirbhar package):**
| Category | 2020 (investment / turnover) | From 1 April 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| **Micro** | **₹1 cr / ₹5 cr** | **₹2.5 cr / ₹10 cr** |
| Small | ₹10 cr / ₹50 cr | ₹25 cr / ₹100 cr |
| **Medium** | **₹50 cr / ₹250 cr** (not ₹15-25 cr) | ₹125 cr / ₹500 cr |
- Basis now: **investment in plant and machinery/equipment AND annual turnover**; manufacturing/services distinction removed. Example: ₹8 cr investment, ₹40 cr turnover = **small** (2020).
- Under the MSMED Act the categories are **micro, small and medium** – "small-scale industry" is the old (pre-2006) label.
- **Nature and size of an industry** (UPSC 2001 key): **capital investment, labour force, power consumption** (turnover also relevant).
- The micro **turnover ceiling was never ₹15 crore** (2020: ₹5 crore; 2025: ₹10 crore).

=== Role, Contribution and Problems
# Why small and cottage industries
- **Labour-intensive, low capital** → **generate large employment** (the primary reason to encourage them); disperse industry; reduce **concentration of economic power**; ancillaries to large industry; priority since the **First Plan**.
- Small-scale sector = about **40% of manufacturing gross turnover** (since 1992).
- **Industrial clusters** help MSMEs (inclusiveness, technology, efficiency), exist in handloom, handicrafts and modern SMEs, are run by several ministries – they **do not promote monopoly**.
| Indicator (Rajya Sabha, Aug 2026) | Figure |
|---|---|
| Share in GDP | **31%** |
| Share in manufacturing output | **36%** |
| Share in exports | **41%** |
| Outstanding MSME credit | **₹38.35 lakh crore** |
- Udyam: **6.63 crore** registered (1 July 2020 – 31 July 2025); **micro = 95.88%**.
# Problems
| Problem | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Finance / capital** | Shortage of **working capital** = main SSI problem |
| **Marketing** | Weak marketing and exports |
| **Raw materials** | Scarce, costly |
| Delayed payments | **MSME Samadhaan (2017)** |
| Others | No latest technology, power, poor managerial skills, sickness |
- **IPR 1948** listed handicaps: **raw materials, finance, marketing, technology** → "all of the above".
- **Strikes and lockouts** = problem of **large** industries, NOT SSIs; excess cheap credit is not a problem.
- **Globalisation and WTO** hit **small-scale industrialists and businessmen** hardest (cheap imports, dereservation).

=== MSMED Act, Procurement and Payments
- **Chapter V (delayed payments):** pay an MSE within **45 days**; else **compound interest at 3 times the RBI bank rate**; disputes → **MSE Facilitation Council**.
- **MSMED (Amendment) Bill 2026:** every **CPSE to settle MSME invoices on TReDS**.
- **Public Procurement Policy for MSEs Order, 2012:** **25%** of annual procurement from MSEs (from **20% in 2018**); **4% SC/ST**, **3% women**; achieved FY26 (to 1 Dec 2025) **44.76%**.

=== SSI Reservation and Committees
- Reservation began **1967 with 47 items** → peak about **873 (1984)** → **125 dereserved on 13 March 2007, leaving 114** (the number "until 2007") → **79 more in Feb 2008, leaving 35** → last **20 dereserved in 2015**.

|---|---|---|

| 2019 | U.K. Sinha (RBI) | MSME finance |

=== Entrepreneurship Schemes
| Scheme | Key fact |
|---|---|
| PMEGP (2008) | = PMRY + REGP; margin-money subsidy; run by **KVIC**; women up to 35% |
| **Stand-Up India** (April 2016) | Bank loans ₹10 lakh – ₹1 crore for **greenfield** units of **SC/ST and women**; non-individual units need **51%** with them |

| Udyam Registration (2020) | Free, paperless, self-declaration; linked to GeM, income tax, GST, TReDS |

- **State schemes:** **Udyam Kranti Yojana** (Madhya Pradesh) = **collateral-free credit for youth self-employment**; **SEMFEX** (ex-servicemen) loans in Rajasthan through the **Rajasthan Financial Corporation (RFC)**.
