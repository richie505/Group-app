[61:0:6] First cotton mill = Bombay 1854 (Indian-run); first jute mill = Rishra 1855 (foreign-run). Fort Gloster 1818 failed.
[61:0:7] - **Ownership:** early **cotton mills** were mostly **Indian-owned** (Bombay, Ahmedabad); **jute mills** in Bengal were mostly **foreign (British)-owned**.

## SUB 2 | Industrial Structure Since 1991
[61:2:0] # Sectoral Shares
[61:2:1] - **Services, not industry,** gained the largest GDP share after 1991.
[61:2:2] - Industry = about **25.3% of GVA**; real industry GVA grows about 7%.
[61:2:3] - **Manufacturing** real share steady at **17-18%**; nominal share fell only due to relative prices.
[61:2:4] - Public sector about **13-14% of GDP** → **private sector dominates** industrial output.
[61:2:5] - Post-LPG growth favoured **capital- and skill-intensive** sectors.
[61:2:6] "Industry's share rose significantly compared to services" is FALSE (APPSC-GS 2023: only II and III correct).
[61:2:7] - Medium- and high-tech manufacturing = **46.3%** (ES 2025-26).

## SUB 4 | Index of Industrial Production (iip)
[61:4:0] # Meaning and Compilation
[61:4:1] - Measures **short-term changes in volume** of industrial production against a base period.
[61:4:2] - Compiled **monthly** by **NSO (MoSPI)**, which replaced the CSO.
[61:4:3] - **Base year 2011-12 → 2022-23**, effective **1 June 2026**.
[61:4:4] - New basket **1,042 products** (old: 407 items).
[61:4:5] # Coverage and Weights
[61:4:6] - Sectors: **mining, manufacturing, electricity**. NOT included: **agriculture, services, construction**.
[61:4:7] - New additions: gas, water supply, sewerage, waste management, minor and rare-earth minerals; renewable electricity shown separately.
[61:4:8] - Weights based on each sector's **GVA share in 2022-23**: manufacturing **76.1%**, mining **11.1%**, electricity and gas **10.9%**, water supply 2.02%.
[61:4:9] - **Use-based groups:** primary, capital, intermediate, infrastructure/construction, consumer durables, consumer non-durables. 2011-12 series: **primary goods highest (~34%)**, **capital goods lowest (8.22%)**.
[61:4:10] - **Index of Services Production (ISP):** base 2024-25; first release **14 July 2026**; 60-day lag.
[61:4:11] # Growth Trend
[61:4:12] - **FY 2023:** capital goods and infrastructure/construction goods grew fastest.
[61:4:13] - FY 2024-25: IIP **4.0%**, ICI 4.5%; FY 2025-26: IIP 4.1%.
[61:4:14] - **Jun 2026:** IIP **7.3%** (23-month high); new ICI 5% (later 6%), led by iron ore (+43.9%). Jul 2026: ICI 5.4%.

[61:9:9] - **PESB** selects **board-level executives** of CPSEs.
