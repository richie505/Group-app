=== Sector Classification – Activity to Sector
# Five sectors
| Sector | Nature | Activities |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | Direct use of nature (land, water, vegetation, minerals) | Hunting, gathering, pastoralism, **fishing / fisheries**, forestry, **agriculture**, **dairy**, **bee-keeping**, mining and quarrying |
| **Secondary (Industry)** | Processing / manufacturing | Manufacturing, cement, construction; in national accounts also **electricity, gas and water supply** |
| **Tertiary (Services)** | Services | **Transport, trade, banking**, call-centre work, tuition |
| Quaternary | Knowledge | Research and development, IT, education, consulting |
| Quinary | Top decisions | Top decision-making: government, top executives ("gold collar") |
- **Agriculture = primary; services = tertiary** (Astt. Comm. 2017).
- **Dairy** (animal husbandry) and **bee-keeping** are **primary** (genetic) activities – not services (SSC 2017, 2018).
- APPSC match: **Trade – Service**, **Water supply – Industrial**, **Fisheries – Primary**, **GDP – Central Statistical Organisation** (now NSO, MoSPI).
- **Mining** is primary by nature, but GVA reporting groups it under the broad **Industry** sector.
> Exam angle: transport, trade, banking = tertiary; water supply is grouped with industry; GDP is compiled by the CSO (now NSO).
~~ Sources: OnlyIAS NCERT Human Geography 04 p.11 (book p.35: four sectors; primary = hunting, pastoralism, fishing, forestry, agriculture, mining); app notes 4:79 (sub 0); CSO/water supply: general knowledge, not checked

=== Types of Primary Activity and Climate
| Type | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Oldest | Depend directly on nature | Hunting and gathering |
| Extractive | Take what nature provides | Mining, capture fishing, hunting, lumbering |
| Genetic | Breed plants and animals | Agriculture, **animal husbandry**, fish farming, dairy |
- **Stock farming = breeding of animals** (keeping livestock).
- **Animal husbandry** = the branch of agricultural science dealing with the **management and care of farm animals** – feeding, breeding, disease control.
- Animal husbandry is a **genetic** industry, not extractive.
- **Farming** is the human activity **most affected by climate** (rainfall, temperature, seasons); fishing and mining less, manufacturing least.

=== Sectoral Shares – India and AP
| Economy | Primary | Secondary | Tertiary |
|---|---|---|---|
| India GVA 2024-25 (base 2022-23) | 21.4% | 25.8% | 52.9% |
| AP GVA 2025-26 (current prices) | 33% | 24% | 43% |
| AP employment (PLFS 2023-24) | 45.6% | 22.8% | 31.6% |
- **Services lead in GVA; agriculture leads in jobs** (India and AP). The **primary sector employs the largest share** of India's workers – about **46.1%** (Aug 2026); NCERT: "around 50 percent of persons are engaged in agricultural activity".
- AP more agrarian than India: agriculture ~33% vs ~21%, led by **livestock and fisheries**.
- **New GDP series (Feb 2026)**: base year **2022-23**; GDP levels **3-4% lower**; manufacturing **14.7%**; FY26 growth **7.6%**.
- **Revised IIP (June 2026)** added a **water supply and waste management** sector.
> "Majority of the working population" in exam keys = primary sector (the largest share, now just under half).

=== Structural Transformation
- **Fisher-Clark thesis** – **Allan Fisher (1935)** and **Colin Clark (1940)**: as income rises, labour and output move **primary → secondary → tertiary**.
- **Premature deindustrialisation**: India skipped the mass factory-job stage before services expanded; only **2.9%** of global manufacturing value added.
- AP: services-led; manufacturing only ~**11% of GSDP** – the "**missing middle**" targeted by **Industrial Policy 4.0 (2024-29)**.
- Counterpoint: organised manufacturing jobs rose 6% in FY24 (about 10 lakh).

=== Organised vs Unorganised
| Organised | Unorganised |
|---|---|
| Registered; regular terms; labour laws (PF, minimum wages, leave) | Small, scattered units; irregular pay; no social security |
- **Public vs private** is a separate split, by **ownership**; organised vs unorganised is by **regulation**.
- **e-Shram**: 31 crore+ unorganised workers, **54% women** (Jan 2026).
- AP informal employment: **62.3% rural** vs **45.6% urban**.
