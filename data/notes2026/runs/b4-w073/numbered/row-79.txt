## SUB 0 | Sector Classification – Activity to Sector
[0:0] # Five sectors
[0:1] TABLE head: Sector | Nature | Activities
  [0:1.0] Primary | Direct use of nature (land, water, vegetation, minerals) | Hunting, gathering, pastoralism, fishing / fisheries, forestry, agriculture, dairy, bee-keeping, mining and quarrying
  [0:1.1] Secondary (Industry) | Processing / manufacturing | Manufacturing, cement, construction; in national accounts also electricity, gas and water supply
  [0:1.2] Tertiary (Services) | Services | Transport, trade, banking, call-centre work, tuition
  [0:1.3] Quaternary | Knowledge | Research and development, IT, education, consulting
  [0:1.4] Quinary | Top decisions | Top decision-making: government, top executives ("gold collar")
[0:2] - **Agriculture = primary; services = tertiary** (Astt. Comm. 2017).
[0:3] - **Dairy** (animal husbandry) and **bee-keeping** are **primary** (genetic) activities – not services (SSC 2017, 2018).
[0:4] - APPSC match: **Trade – Service**, **Water supply – Industrial**, **Fisheries – Primary**, **GDP – Central Statistical Organisation** (now NSO, MoSPI).
[0:5] - **Mining** is primary by nature, but GVA reporting groups it under the broad **Industry** sector.
[0:6] > Exam angle: transport, trade, banking = tertiary; water supply is grouped with industry; GDP is compiled by the CSO (now NSO).

## SUB 1 | Types of Primary Activity and Climate
[1:0] TABLE head: Type | Meaning | Examples
  [1:0.0] Oldest | Depend directly on nature | Hunting and gathering
  [1:0.1] Extractive | Take what nature provides | Mining, capture fishing, hunting, lumbering
  [1:0.2] Genetic | Breed plants and animals | Agriculture, animal husbandry, fish farming, dairy
[1:1] - **Stock farming = breeding of animals** (keeping livestock).
[1:2] - **Animal husbandry** = the branch of agricultural science dealing with the **management and care of farm animals** – feeding, breeding, disease control.
[1:3] - Animal husbandry is a **genetic** industry, not extractive.
[1:4] - **Farming** is the human activity **most affected by climate** (rainfall, temperature, seasons); fishing and mining less, manufacturing least.
[1:5] ~~ Sources: OnlyIAS NCERT Human Geography 04 p.11-13 (book p.35-37: hunting and gathering oldest; pastoralism; commercial livestock rearing); app notes 4:79 (sub 1)
## SUB 2 | Sectoral Shares – India and AP
[2:0] TABLE head: Economy | Primary | Secondary | Tertiary
  [2:0.0] India GVA 2024-25 (base 2022-23) | 21.4% | 25.8% | 52.9%

[2:4] - **Revised IIP (June 2026)** added a **water supply and waste management** sector.
[2:5] > "Majority of the working population" in exam keys = primary sector (the largest share, now just under half).

## SUB 4 | Structural Transformation
[4:0] - **Fisher-Clark thesis** – **Allan Fisher (1935)** and **Colin Clark (1940)**: as income rises, labour and output move **primary → secondary → tertiary**.
[4:1] - **Premature deindustrialisation**: India skipped the mass factory-job stage before services expanded; only **2.9%** of global manufacturing value added.
[4:2] - AP: services-led; manufacturing only ~**11% of GSDP** – the "**missing middle**" targeted by **Industrial Policy 4.0 (2024-29)**.
[4:3] - Counterpoint: organised manufacturing jobs rose 6% in FY24 (about 10 lakh).
[4:4] ~~ Sources: app notes 4:79 (sub 4)
