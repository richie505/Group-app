[78:1:5] # HDI method
[78:1:6] - Published by **UNDP**; first Human Development Report **1990**; conceived by **Mahbub ul Haq** with **Amartya Sen**.
[78:1:7] - Three dimensions: **health** (life expectancy), **education** (mean + expected years of schooling), **income** (**GNI per capita, PPP**, used as a logarithm).
[78:1:8] - Since **2010**: dimension indices combined by a **geometric mean**.
[78:1:9] - HDI hides inequality within a State → **IHDI**, district indices.
[78:1:10] - **Averaging indicators:** adding a constant to every score raises the **mean by that constant** (+5 → mean +5); the spread (range, SD) is unchanged.

## SUB 0 | Sector Classification – Activity to Sector
[79:0:0] # Five sectors
[79:0:1] TABLE head: Sector | Nature | Activities
  [79:0:1.0] Primary | Direct use of nature (land, water, vegetation, minerals) | Hunting, gathering, pastoralism, fishing / fisheries, forestry, agriculture, dairy, bee-keeping, mining and quarrying
  [79:0:1.1] Secondary (Industry) | Processing / manufacturing | Manufacturing, cement, construction; in national accounts also electricity, gas and water supply
  [79:0:1.2] Tertiary (Services) | Services | Transport, trade, banking, call-centre work, tuition
  [79:0:1.3] Quaternary | Knowledge | Research and development, IT, education, consulting
  [79:0:1.4] Quinary | Top decisions | Top decision-making: government, top executives ("gold collar")
[79:0:2] - **Agriculture = primary; services = tertiary** (Astt. Comm. 2017).
[79:0:3] - **Dairy** (animal husbandry) and **bee-keeping** are **primary** (genetic) activities – not services (SSC 2017, 2018).
[79:0:4] - APPSC match: **Trade – Service**, **Water supply – Industrial**, **Fisheries – Primary**, **GDP – Central Statistical Organisation** (now NSO, MoSPI).
[79:0:5] - **Mining** is primary by nature, but GVA reporting groups it under the broad **Industry** sector.
[79:0:6] > Exam angle: transport, trade, banking = tertiary; water supply is grouped with industry; GDP is compiled by the CSO (now NSO).

## SUB 1 | Types of Primary Activity and Climate
[79:1:0] TABLE head: Type | Meaning | Examples
  [79:1:0.0] Oldest | Depend directly on nature | Hunting and gathering
  [79:1:0.1] Extractive | Take what nature provides | Mining, capture fishing, hunting, lumbering
  [79:1:0.2] Genetic | Breed plants and animals | Agriculture, animal husbandry, fish farming, dairy
[79:1:1] - **Stock farming = breeding of animals** (keeping livestock).
[79:1:2] - **Animal husbandry** = the branch of agricultural science dealing with the **management and care of farm animals** – feeding, breeding, disease control.
[79:1:3] - Animal husbandry is a **genetic** industry, not extractive.
[79:1:4] - **Farming** is the human activity **most affected by climate** (rainfall, temperature, seasons); fishing and mining less, manufacturing least.

## SUB 4 | Structural Transformation
[79:4:0] - **Fisher-Clark thesis** – **Allan Fisher (1935)** and **Colin Clark (1940)**: as income rises, labour and output move **primary → secondary → tertiary**.
[79:4:1] - **Premature deindustrialisation**: India skipped the mass factory-job stage before services expanded; only **2.9%** of global manufacturing value added.
[79:4:2] - AP: services-led; manufacturing only ~**11% of GSDP** – the "**missing middle**" targeted by **Industrial Policy 4.0 (2024-29)**.
[79:4:3] - Counterpoint: organised manufacturing jobs rose 6% in FY24 (about 10 lakh).
