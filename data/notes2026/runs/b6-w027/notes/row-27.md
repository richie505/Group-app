=== Landmark Supreme Court Judgments (Mar–May 2026)
# Harish Rana – passive euthanasia (11 March 2026)
- **Held:** SC allowed **withdrawal of life support** (life-sustaining treatment) for Harish Rana – right to die with dignity is part of **Art 21**.
- **First:** the **first time** the SC actually **implemented passive euthanasia** in a specific case; **active euthanasia remains illegal**.
- **CANH:** Clinically Assisted Nutrition and Hydration = **medical treatment** that may be **withdrawn in the patient's best interest** (family + medical boards) – not "basic care", not active euthanasia.
| Case | Holding |
|---|---|
| P. Rathinam (1994) | Recognised a right to die – **overruled by Gian Kaur** |
| **Gian Kaur v. Punjab (1996)** | Right to live with dignity; rejected a right to die |
| **Aruna Shanbaug (2011)** | **Passive euthanasia** in exceptional cases |
| **Common Cause (2018)** | Right to die with dignity; refuse treatment; **living wills** (guidelines simplified **2023**) |
| **Chenna Jagadeeswar v. State of A.P.** (AP HC) | Right to life does **not** include the right to die |
# Conversion ends Scheduled Caste status
- **Held:** SC status only for **Hindus, Sikhs, Buddhists**; conversion to another religion → immediate and complete loss of SC status.
- **AP case:** SC/ST (PoA) complaint by **Pastor Chintada Anand Paul**; AP HC quashed charges (30 April 2025); SC Bench **Prashant Kumar Mishra and Manmohan** dismissed the SLP.
- **AP's 1977 GO:** gives only **non-statutory concessions** – it does **not** confer SC status after conversion.
- **Reconversion:** claimant must prove **return, renunciation and acceptance by the original caste**.
- **Constitution (SC) Order, 1950** under **Art 341(1)**; **Clause 3** – Hindus (1950) → **Sikhs 1956** → **Buddhists 1990**; Art 341(2) – only Parliament amends.
- **Basis:** SC (Art 341) = **untouchability**; ST (Art 342) = tribal characteristics, isolation.
# Sabarimala review – nine-judge Bench
- **Case:** **Kantaru Rajeevaru v Indian Young Lawyers Association**; Bench chaired by **CJI Surya Kant**; hearing from **7 April 2026**; judgment reserved after 16 days (May 2026).
- **Centre's stand:** rigid definitions of "religious denomination" and "essential religious practices" **distort the plural nature of Hinduism**.
- **Temple:** in **Periyar Tiger Reserve**, Kerala; deity Ayyappa; **41-day penance**; women aged **10–50** barred; run by **Travancore Devaswom Board**.
| Year | Development |
|---|---|
| 2018 | Five-judge Bench, **4:1** – exclusion unconstitutional; lone dissent **Justice Indu Malhotra** |
| 2019 | Referred to a larger Bench |
| 2026 | Nine-judge Bench hears the reference |
- **Doctrine:** **Shirur Mutt (1954)** – essential religious practices; **Art 145(3)** – at least **five judges** for a substantial question of constitutional interpretation; Art 26 denominational rights (subject to public order, morality, health).
# Meaning of "industry" – nine-judge Bench
- **Bench:** nine judges led by **CJI Surya Kant** (from 17 March 2026) re-examining **Bangalore Water Supply v. A. Rajappa (1978)** (seven judges; majority by Justice V.R. Krishna Iyer).
- **Triple Test:** systematic activity; employer–employee cooperation; production/distribution of goods or services – **profit motive and capital irrelevant**.
- **Dominant Nature Test:** the **primary function** decides whether it is an "industry".
- **Industrial Disputes (Amendment) Act, 1982:** **never notified**; reference arose via State of UP v. Jai Bir Singh (2005).
# Other SC rulings
- **Hamsanandini Nanduri v. UoI (March 2026):** struck down Sec 60(4), Code on Social Security 2020 – **all adoptive mothers get 12 weeks** of maternity leave **irrespective of the child's age** (earlier only child below 3 months); Maternity Benefit Act 1961 gives **26 weeks** since 2017.
- **Highways (suo motu):** after the **Nov 2025 accidents in Rajasthan and Telangana** – **safe travel on highways** is part of **Art 21**.
- **Stray dogs (19 May 2026):** rabid/incurably ill dogs euthanised **only after examination by a veterinarian**, per the **ABC Rules 2023**.

# Delimitation Commission
- **Constituted four times: 1952, 1963, 1973, 2002**; last exercise completed **2008** (on the 2001 Census).
- **Appointed by the President**; chair – retired SC judge; members – CEC + State Election Commissioners; temporary body; decides **SC/ST reserved constituencies**.
- **Orders:** force of law; laid before Parliament but **cannot be modified – no approval needed**; courts barred by **Article 329(a)** (no judicial review).
# Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill, 2026
- **Lok Sabha cap 550 → 850** (815 States + 35 UTs) – amends **Art 81**, Art 82, Art 170 and **Art 334A**; the earlier maximum was **550**.
- **Delimitation on the 2011 Census** (to implement the 2023 Act before the 2029 polls).
- **"Schedule" mechanism:** a law lists State-wise seats – each State's **percentage share unchanged**.
- **Package of three Bills:** 131st Amendment Bill; **Delimitation Bill, 2026**; **Union Territories Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026**.
- **Needed:** **special majority** (two-thirds of members present and voting + majority of total membership, **Art 368**) **+ ratification by half of the States**.
- **Art 75(1A):** Council of Ministers ≤ **15%** of Lok Sabha; **Rajya Sabha strength (Art 80) does not change automatically**.
# Defeat in the Lok Sabha – April 2026
- **528 voted – 298 in favour, 230 against**; special majority needed **326** → **defeated**; Delimitation Bill 2026 then withdrawn.
- **106th Amendment Act brought into force from 16 April 2026** (passed Sept 2023; valid 15 years).
# The ~816-seat proposal
- **Aim:** women's reservation **without waiting for post-Census delimitation**, from the **2029 Lok Sabha polls**.
- **Seats 543 → ~816**; about **273** seats (one-third) for women; **States' proportions unchanged**.
- **Southern seats 129 → 195**; share stays 23.8%.
- **Temporary mechanism:** ~273 women's seats allotted to parties **by their vote share in the last general election, from pre-declared lists**.
- **Lok Sabha expansion does not automatically trigger delimitation.**
# Parliament static facts
- **Art 80:** Rajya Sabha maximum **250 (238 elected + 12 nominated)**, current 245; **one-third retire every 2 years**; elected by **elected MLAs only (not MLCs)**.
- **Rajya Sabha:** first constituted **3 April 1952** (first sitting 13 May 1952).
- **Art 170:** Assembly maximum **500**, minimum **60**; **Art 169:** Council created/abolished by **ordinary law** after an Assembly special-majority resolution.

=== Census 2027 and Naxalism
# Census 2027
- **16th Census** (8th since Independence); **first fully digital**; **first full caste enumeration since 1931**.
- **Phase 1 (House Listing)** from **1 April 2026**; reference date **midnight of 1 March 2027** (snowbound areas 1 October 2026).
- **Digital tools by C-DAC** (HLBC, HL Mobile App, Self-Enumeration Portal, CMMS).
- **Mascots "Pragati" and "Vikas"**; brand ambassador **Sudarsan Pattnaik** (not the RGI – RGI is Mrityunjay Kumar Narayan).
- **Census Act, 1948**; first Census **1872**, **first complete (synchronous) 1881**.
# Naxalism
- **MHA declared complete elimination of Naxalism** – no affected districts; new categories **"Legacy and Thrust" (37 districts)** and "Districts of Concern".
- **AP district in the new list:** **Alluri Sitharama Raju**.
