=== NOTES 1:262 | Formation of Andhra Pradesh 1956 – Visalandhra, Gentlemen's Agreement
## SUB 0 | Visalandhra – Meaning, Roots and Firsts
[262:0:0] # Meaning
[262:0:1] - **Visalandhra** ("**Greater Andhra**") = one state for all Telugu people divided between **Madras** and the **Nizam's Hyderabad** (APPSC GS 2024: the united-Telugu-state movement = Visalandhra Movement).
[262:0:2] - In **1950** southern and eastern Andhra were in Madras State; Telangana was part of Hyderabad State.
[262:0:3] - Prakasam's slogan: "**Visalandhra nirmanam is the mother's right of all Andhras.**"
[262:0:4] # Roots – Year → Event
[262:0:5] TABLE head: Year | Event
  [262:0:5.0] 1912 | Unnava Lakshminarayana and Jonnavittula Gurunatham sketched a united Andhra (Telangana + Circars + Ceded districts)
  [262:0:5.1] 1914 | Second Andhra Mahasabha – Nyapati Subbarao proposed one rule for all Telugu areas
  [262:0:5.2] 1921 | Andhra Jana Sangham founded; the Nizam Andhra Mahasabha grew out of it
  [262:0:5.3] 1936 | Andhra Congress Swaraj Party manifesto
  [262:0:5.4] 1937 | Concept spreads in the Circar districts
  [262:0:5.5] 1946 | Communist Party first demands it; Puchalapalli Sundarayya writes *Visalandhralo Prajarajyam* ("People's Rule in Greater Andhra") and coins the term
  [262:0:5.6] 1954 | Madapati Hanumantha Rao: idea 50 years old, as old as the Andhra Movement
[262:0:6] First to use the word "Visalandhra" = Sundarayya; first party demand = Communist Party (1946). The movement started from the (Nizam) Andhra Mahasabha milieu and was championed by the Communists.
[262:0:7] # Chronology Asked in Papers
[262:0:8] - **Dhar Commission (1948) → Visalandhra Mahasabha (1949) → Swami Sitaram's fast (1951) → Andhra State / SRC (1953)**.
## SUB 1 | Telangana Background – Mahasabha, Hyderabad State, Mulki Rules
[262:1:0] # Nizam Andhra Mahasabha
[262:1:1] - Grew out of the **Andhra Jana Sangham (1921)**.
[262:1:2] - First Andhra Mahasabha: **Jogipet, 1930**, presided by **Suravaram Pratapa Reddy**.
[262:1:3] - Vehicle of Telugu cultural and political awakening; later split into moderate and Communist wings.
[262:1:4] - **Chanda Railway Scheme agitation (1883)** = beginning of public political consciousness in Hyderabad State.
[262:1:5] # Hyderabad State
[262:1:6] - **Join Indian Union Movement** launched **7 Aug 1947** under **Swami Ramananda Tirtha** (founder of the **Hyderabad State Congress**); opposed the Nizam declaring independence. Hyderabad integrated **17 Sep 1948** (**Operation Polo**).
[262:1:7] - **Part B State** from **1950 to 31 Oct 1956**.
[262:1:8] - **Burgula Ramakrishna Rao** headed the **first elected ministry (1952–56)** – also the **last** elected CM of Hyderabad State (APPSC G2 2002, 2011). Speaker at merger: **Kashinatha Rao**.
[262:1:9] # Mulki Rules and the 1952 Agitation
[262:1:10] - Mulki rules from **1888**; made stricter by the **Nizam's farman of 1919**; only residents of about **15 years** got local jobs.
[262:1:11] - **Mulki agitation, 1952** under the Burgula ministry; **police firing near City College, Hyderabad** (3–4 September 1952) killed students.
[262:1:12] - Inquiry commission under Justice **Pingali Jaganmohan Reddy** (report 28 Dec 1952) found lack of coordination; no major change in Mulki rules followed.
[262:1:13] - Outcome: domicile protection became the central Telangana demand → Mulki clause of the Gentlemen's Agreement.
[262:1:14] Mulki: 1888 → tightened 1919 → agitation 1952 → Agreement domicile 12 years (1956)

## SUB 5 | Arguments for and Against
[262:5:0] TABLE head: For Visalandhra | Against / Telangana apprehensions
  [262:5:0.0] River waters: integrated Krishna–Godavari planning | Employment: weakening of Mulki rules
  [262:5:0.1] Economic: Andhra's agriculture and coast + Telangana's minerals | Education/services: advanced Coastal Andhra would dominate
  [262:5:0.2] Capital: Hyderabad solves Andhra's capital problem | Revenue: Telangana's surplus spent elsewhere
  [262:5:0.3] Development: Telangana backward under the Nizam | Disparities would widen
  [262:5:0.4] Agrarian (Communists): push land reforms, weaken feudalism | Telangana had higher per capita income and revenue → viable alone
[262:5:1] Not a pro-Visalandhra argument: permanent protection of separate Telangana political institutions.
## SUB 6 | Wanchoo Committee, Andhra State and Src
[262:6:0] - **Wanchoo Committee** – appointed **Dec 1952** after Potti Sriramulu's death; Justice **K.N. Wanchoo**, CJ of Rajasthan HC; identified the **financial and administrative problems** of forming Andhra State (APPSC G2 2012); **7 Feb 1953** report; Centre's decisions 25 Mar 1953.
[262:6:1] - **Andhra State** – **1 Oct 1953**, capital **Kurnool**; first CM **Prakasam**, Deputy CM **N. Sanjiva Reddy**. Prakasam lost a no-confidence vote (**Nov 1954**, prohibition) → mid-term polls **1955** → **Bezawada Gopala Reddy** CM.
[262:6:2] - **Pataskar Award** – Andhra–Madras border; **Tiruttani** to Tamil Nadu.
[262:6:3] - **SRC** – announced **22 Dec 1953**, constituted **29 Dec 1953**; **Fazl Ali, H.N. Kunzru, K.M. Panikkar** (not Harishchandra Heda); report **1955**.
[262:6:4] - **Para 386**: Telangana initially a separate **Hyderabad State**; merger after the **1961** elections by **two-thirds** of its legislature. Raichur, Gulbarga → Mysore; Marathwada separated.
[262:6:5] - **Andhra Assembly (1955)**: Bezawada Gopala Reddy's **unanimous** resolution – Telangana priority + population-based shares.
[262:6:6] - **Hyderabad Assembly**: **103 for**, **29 against**, **15 neutral**.
[262:6:7] - **22 Oct 1955**: CMs agreed to merge at Hyderabad House, New Delhi.

## SUB 8 | Regional Committee, Deputy Cm and Later Law
[262:8:0] - **Telangana Regional Committee**: provided by the Agreement (GS 2019); **Art 371(1)** (7th Amendment, 1956) + **AP Regional Committee Order, 1958**.
[262:8:1] - Members: Telangana MLAs/MPs elected by Telangana legislators; Telangana ministers (except CM) ex officio. Powers: development, irrigation, agriculture, industry, education, health, recruitment, prohibition.
[262:8:2] - **Extended five years in 1968**; wound up after the **32nd Amendment (1973)**.
[262:8:3] - **Deputy CM**: **Sanjiva Reddy appointed none**, calling it a "**sixth finger**".
[262:8:4] - **Violations** (employment, domicile, revenue surplus) → **Jai Telangana agitation, 1969** (CM **Kasu Brahmananda Reddy**). Root flaw: political assurances, not constitutional mandates.
[262:8:5] TABLE head: Law | Year | Provision
  [262:8:5.0] Art 371D | 32nd Amendment, 1973 (Six-Point Formula) | Local cadres, equal opportunity in jobs and education, AP Administrative Tribunal
  [262:8:5.1] Presidential Order | 1975 | AP Public Employment (Organisation of Local Cadres and Regulation of Direct Recruitment) Order
  [262:8:5.2] APRA 2014, Section 97 | 2014 | Art 371D extended to AP or Telangana
[262:8:6] 371D came by the **32nd**, not 33rd, Amendment.
[262:8:7] - **First Chairman, Telangana Regional Committee:** **K. Achyuta Reddy**, Congress MLA from **Kodangal**, elected in **1958**.
## SUB 9 | Formation of Andhra Pradesh, 1 November 1956
[262:9:0] - Under the **States Reorganisation Act, 1956**, on a **linguistic basis** (APPSC GS 2023).
[262:9:1] - **11 Andhra districts + 9 Telangana districts**; Kannada areas → Mysore, Marathi → Bombay.
[262:9:2] - Andhra Legislature at Kurnool backed the merger unanimously; both ministries resigned.
[262:9:3] - Name: draft Bill "**Andhra Telangana**"; Communists "Visalandhra"; Joint Select Committee changed it to "**Andhra Pradesh**" (7th Amendment Bill, 10 Aug 1956).
[262:9:4] - **Capital Hyderabad**, replacing **Kurnool** (1953–56); reasons – SRC view and Mahasabha demand.
[262:9:5] - **Inaugurated by Nehru**, 1 Nov 1956.
