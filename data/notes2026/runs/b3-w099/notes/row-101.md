=== Union Finance Commission Grants to Local Bodies
| FC | Local-body grants | Key features |
|---|---|---|
| 10th | ₹4,380 crore PRIs + ₹1,000 crore ULBs | First (ad hoc) local-body grants, before Parts IX and IX-A took effect |
| 11th | ₹8,000 crore rural + ₹2,000 crore urban | Weights: **population (1991) 40%**, index of decentralisation 20%, distance from highest per capita income 20%, revenue effort 10%, area 10% |
| 12th | ₹25,000 crore (80:20 rural:urban) | Population **40% (Census 2001)**, area 10%, income distance 20%, deprivation 10%, revenue effort 20% |
| 13th | ₹87,519 crore | Share of divisible pool; **basic + performance** grants |
| 14th | **₹2,87,436 crore** | Gram panchayats **₹2,00,292.2 crore**, municipalities ₹87,143.8 crore; **90% population, 10% area** |
| 15th | **₹4,36,361 crore** | Rural **₹2,36,805 crore**; all three PRI tiers; 60% tied |
| 16th | **₹7,91,493 crore** | Rural 4.35, urban 3.56 lakh crore; urban share **36% → 45%** |
- **14th FC:** grants only to **gram panchayats** (not block/district tiers); **basic : performance = 90:10** for gram panchayats and **80:20** for **municipalities**; rural-urban split by population, so the **same per capita** amount (about ₹488 per head a year) for both.
- **14th FC performance grant:** from **2016-17**, released in a later year against **audited annual accounts** and a rise in **own revenue**.
- **14th FC on local taxes:** States should empower local bodies to levy **advertisement tax**; property-tax reform, municipal bonds, professional tax up to ₹12,000.
- **15th FC conditions:** rural grants **60% tied** (drinking water, rainwater harvesting; sanitation/ODF) and **40% untied** (not for **salaries**); provisional and **audited accounts online** by at least **25%** of rural bodies (all from 2023-24); ULBs also notify **floor (baseline) property-tax rates**; SFC compliance required.
| 16th FC component | Share | Tied / untied |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | **80%** | Half tied (water, sanitation), half untied |
| Performance | **20%** | **Fully untied** |
| Special infrastructure (ULBs) | **₹56,100 crore** | Wastewater, cities of 10-40 lakh |
| Urbanisation Premium | **₹10,000 crore** | One-time; merging peri-urban villages, Rural-to-Urban Transition Policy |
- **16th FC entry conditions:** elected bodies, **audited accounts online**, timely SFCs – **municipal bonds are not** a condition.
- Union FCs have **not in practice based** local-body grants on SFC reports (irregular, unsynchronised), using their own criteria instead.
> Exam angle: APPSC Panchayat Secretary papers ask amounts (₹2,00,292.20 crore), ratios (80:20, 90:10) and weights (40% population).

=== Constitutional Provisions and State Finance Commissions
| Article | Body | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| 243H | Panchayats | State Legislature **may** authorise taxes, tolls, fees; assign State taxes; give grants |
| 243-I | Panchayats | **Governor** constitutes an SFC every **five** years |
| 243X | Municipalities | Same enabling power |
| 243Y | Municipalities | The same SFC (243-I) reviews municipal finances – **no separate SFC** |
| 280(3)(bb), (c) | Both | Central FC suggests measures to augment the State fund **on SFC recommendations** |
- Clause 280(3)(bb)/(c) was added by the **73rd and 74th Amendments (1992)** – not the 80th (2000), which changed tax sharing.
- **SFC functions:** review the **financial position** of panchayats and municipalities; recommend the **distribution of State revenues between the State and local bodies**, and among rural and urban bodies; grants-in-aid. It **determines its own procedure**.
- **"May" = enabling** – the State is not forced to impose taxes.
- **Eleventh Schedule** (Art. 243G): **29** subjects; **Twelfth Schedule** (Art. 243W): **18** subjects → **funds, functions, functionaries (3Fs)** rarely move together.

=== Panchayat Revenue
- **Property (house) tax** = major own revenue of panchayats; they levy only **State-authorised** taxes: **local fairs**, house tax, fees, tolls.
- Sources: **FC grants**, **Central Cooperative Banks**, **CSS** allocations, **SFC** allocations, **loans from the State**. **NABARD** is not a direct panchayat source; **sales tax, land revenue** are State levies.
- **Grant dependence:** limited taxing power → panchayats depend substantially on **grants** (FC, SFC, CSS funds).
- **Matching grant:** the panchayat raises about **10%** as its matching share.
- **BRGF:** States had to pass Central funds to PRIs within **15 days**.
- Gram sabhas spend only **4%** of meeting time on revenue (13% on local issues).

=== Urban Local Body Revenue
| Type | Sources |
|---|---|
| Tax | **Property tax (most important)**, professional tax (ceiling **₹2,500**), advertisement, entertainment, water, market taxes; octroi (traditional) |
| Non-tax | **Rents**, fees, fines, **user charges** (water supply, sewerage) |
| Other | Grants, SFC devolution, loans; municipal bonds in a few large cities |
- Payment for municipal water supply = **user charge** (non-tax). Property tax is a **local**, not Central, tax.
- Weak municipalities = **under-assessment and under-collection** of property tax.
- **ES 2016-17:** **satellite imagery** estimated the property-tax gap in **Bengaluru and Jaipur**.
- **SVAMITVA** drone maps → panchayat property-tax baseline; **AMRUT 2.0** rewards property-tax reform and bonds.

=== Challenges of Local Finance
- **IIHS** (80 ULBs, 2012-17): transfers ≈ **40%** of ULB revenue.
- India's intergovernmental transfers to local bodies ≈ **0.5% of GDP** vs global **2-5%**.
- No **accrual accounting**, weak balance sheets → no bond market access.
- SFCs **not constituted regularly**; State governments accept and release only part of SFC awards.
