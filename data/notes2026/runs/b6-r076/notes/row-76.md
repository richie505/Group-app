=== Disasters, Cyclones and Natural Phenomena
# Nepal Disaster, 26 Aug 2026
- **Event:** a **rock-ice collapse** near the **Nepal-China border**; material plunged about **1 km**; first read as a **4.4-magnitude quake**, but **USGS** said the signal came from the collapse (energy ~**magnitude 5.2**).
- **Type:** a **rock-ice collapse-triggered debris flood**, **not a conventional GLOF**; an **ice-rock avalanche** from the northern slopes of **Mt Langtang Lirung** choked the **Lhende River (Lhende Khola)**, which feeds the **Bhotekoshi**; flash floods on the **Bhotekoshi-Trishuli** rivers.
- **Toll (NDRRMA, 8 Sep 2026):** **1,357 dead**, **5,326** unaccounted for (it rose from a few hundred in the first days); **93,000+** need assistance; Kailash Mansarovar pilgrims missing.
- **Damage:** Nepal lost about **475 MW** hydropower (**13 hydropower projects** damaged); China's **Gyirong Port (Tibet)** buried (upgraded at **$175.8 million**).
- **India:** rivers **Gandak, Kosi, Ganga**; **Bihar and UP** on alert; **Valmikinagar Barrage** watched (not Farakka); Maharajganj, Kushinagar; India sent **47.5 tonnes** of relief.
# India's Himalayan Hazard Measures
- **National GLOF Risk Mitigation Programme:** **₹150 crore**; **Arunachal Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Sikkim, Uttarakhand**.
- **NDMA Guidelines on GLOFs: 2020**; **Dam Safety Act: 2021**; **National Landslide Risk Mitigation Project:** **₹1,000 crore**, **15 States**.

- **CWC glacial lake monitoring:** the **Central Water Commission** monitors **2,843 glacial lakes and water bodies** of the Himalayan region by satellite; its 2011-2025 study found many lakes expanding.

=== Short-lived Climate Pollutants and the Ozone Treaties
# SLCPs
- SLCPs cause up to **45% of present warming**; IPCC: rapid cuts can avoid up to **0.6°C by 2050**; also called **super pollutants** / **Short-Lived Climate Forcers**.
- Major warming SLCPs: **black carbon, methane, tropospheric ozone, HFCs**; **sulphate aerosols cool**.
- **Black carbon is not a greenhouse gas** (a particle that absorbs radiation); **tropospheric ozone is not directly emitted** (formed from methane, NOx, VOCs in sunlight).
- **Methane:** about **80 times** CO2's warming over **20 years**; second-largest contributor after CO2.
- Stratospheric ozone **~10-50 km**; tropospheric ozone **surface to 10 km**.
- Ozone causes loss of about **110 million tonnes** of staple crops a year; air pollution about **7 million** premature deaths a year globally.
- India: air pollution caused **1.67 million deaths in 2019** (**17.8%**); loss **US$36.8 billion** (**1.36% of GDP**).
- India's methane (2016, **Third BUR**): **Agriculture 73.96%**, Waste 14.46%, Energy 10.62%, Industry 0.96%.
- India's measures: **PMUY** (over **10.5 crore** LPG connections), **GOBARdhan**, **SRI**, **DSR**, **alternate wetting and drying**.
- India has **NOT joined** the Global Methane Pledge; it calls farm methane "**survival emissions**".

=== Snow Leopard, GIB and New Protected Areas
# Project Snow Leopard (PYQ)
- **Launched January 2009**; covers the high-altitude areas of **J&K, Ladakh, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Sikkim, Arunachal Pradesh** (NextGen: five Himalayan States); landscape-level participatory approach.
- **Three large landscapes:** **Hemis-Spiti** (Ladakh + Himachal), **Nanda Devi-Gangotri** (Uttarakhand), **Khangchendzonga-Tawang** (Sikkim + Arunachal), so they are **not confined to Uttarakhand, Himachal and Ladakh**.
- **CMS (2003)** listed the snow leopard as a **Concerted Action Species** under **Appendix I**; CITES (2003) extended its Tiger Enforcement Task Force to all Asian big cats.
# Snow Leopard Safari, Leh
- **India's first high-altitude Snow Leopard Safari:** launched **7 October 2026**, **Leh, Ladakh**, during **Wildlife Week**; **LG Vinai Kumar Saxena** flagged off **6 open-back vehicles**.
- Implemented by **SHAN Society** (Snow Leopard and High-Altitude Nature Conservation) + **Ladakh Forest Department**.
- **SPAI 2024:** India **718** snow leopards; **Ladakh 477** (highest).
- *Panthera uncia*; **IUCN Vulnerable**; **WPA Schedule I**; **CITES Appendix I**; **State animal of Himachal Pradesh**; **UT animal of Ladakh**.
- **International Snow Leopard Day: 23 October**; Ladakh UT since **31 October 2019**.
# Great Indian Bustard
- Union Environment Minister released **two captive-bred male GIBs** at **Sam, Desert National Park**, near **Jaisalmer**.
- **Desert NP:** **Thar Desert**; over **3,160 sq km**; rainfall **under 100 mm**; **Jurassic fossils**; extinct **salt-lake beds**.
- **GIB:** *Ardeotis nigriceps*; **IUCN Critically Endangered**; **Rajasthan State Bird**; main threat **power lines**.

=== Climate Negotiations and Climate Reports
- **[19 Oct 2026] COP31:** 31st Conference of the Parties, **9-20 Nov 2026**, **Expo Center, Antalya, Turkiye**; **Turkiye** host country, **Australia** to lead negotiations.
- **UNFCCC:** adopted **1992** (Rio Earth Summit), in force **21 Mar 1994**; secretariat **Bonn, Germany**.
- **Paris Agreement:** adopted at **COP21 (2015)**, in force **4 Nov 2016**; aims **well below 2°C** while pursuing **1.5°C**; **NDCs** = national climate commitments under it.
- **[25 Oct 2026] UNEP 17th Emissions Gap Report 2026 "The Point of Return":** global GHG emissions **58.4 Gt CO2e in 2025**, down only **0.1 Gt** from 2024.
- **EGR findings:** a **temporary overshoot of 1.5°C** is unavoidable without rapid cuts; low-overshoot pathway **1.7-1.8°C**, with about **50% chance** of returning to 1.5°C by 2100; needs cuts of **18% by 2030, 45% by 2035, 69% by 2040** (vs 2025); new NDCs (about **two-thirds** of countries) cut only **5-8% by 2030** and **11-15% by 2035**.
- **[29 Oct 2026] UNFCCC NDC report:** even with full NDC implementation, emissions fall only **9% by 2035** from **2019** levels; commitments insufficient for 1.5°C; strong cuts could limit peak warming to about **1.8°C**.
