## SUB 6 | Housing
[165:6:0] # PMAY-Gramin (2016)
[165:6:1] - Restructured from **Indira Awaas Yojana** (began **1985**, independent **1996**).
[165:6:2] - Unit: at least **25 sq m**; **₹1.20 lakh (plains) / ₹1.30 lakh (hilly)**.
[165:6:3] - Target: 2.95 crore houses (2016-17 to 2023-24) + **2 crore more** (2024-25 to 2028-29); **extended to 2028-29**. Completed 10 years in 2026.
[165:6:4] # PMAY-Urban (2015)
[165:6:5] - Pucca house for **EWS/LIG and MIG** categories, including slum dwellers; original **Housing for All by 2022** target.
[165:6:6] - **Four components**: in-situ slum redevelopment; credit-linked subsidy; affordable housing in partnership; beneficiary-led construction (rural tap water is NOT one).
[165:6:7] - **PMAY-U 2.0 (2024)**: **1 crore** urban houses. Budget 2026-27 cut from ₹19,794 crore to ₹18,625 crore.
[165:6:8] - **Rajiv Awas Yojana**: aimed at a **slum-free India**; **subsumed into PMAY-U in 2015**.
[165:6:9] - Houses registered in women's names make PMAY a **gender-budget** scheme (26 Jan 2026).
[165:6:10] - **Launch years:** **PMAY-Gramin – 2016** (from 1 April 2016, restructured from IAY); **PMAY-Urban – 2015**.

## SUB 8 | Pension Schemes
[165:8:0] # NPS and UPS
[165:8:1] - **NPS**: from **1 January 2004** for Central Government employees (**armed forces excluded**); open to **all citizens from 1 May 2009**; applies to State employees joining after that State's notification.
[165:8:2] - Regulator **PFRDA** (Act passed 19 Sep 2013, notified 1 Feb 2014); **defined-contribution**; PRAN; **Tier I mandatory, Tier II voluntary**; extra **₹50,000 under 80CCD(1B)**.
[165:8:3] - **OCIs may join**; an account **cannot be opened or run for a third person**.
[165:8:4] - 31 Mar 2026: **2.17 crore+** subscribers; AUM about ₹15.95 lakh crore.
[165:8:5] - **UPS (2025)**: after **25 years**, **50%** of last 12 months' average basic pay; minimum **₹10,000**. **NPS Vatsalya (2025)**: for minors; minimum ₹250.
