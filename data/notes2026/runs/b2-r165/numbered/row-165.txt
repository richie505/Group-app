## SUB 1 | Housing – PMAY-Gramin and PMAY-Urban
[1:0] # PMAY-Gramin
[1:1] - **Indira Awaas Yojana** (began **1985**, independent **1996**) was **restructured into PMAY-G with effect from 1 April 2016** – to **address the gaps** in IAY (poor targeting, small units, weak monitoring), not because of its name.
[1:2] - First aim: assist construction of **1 crore houses in 3 years** (2016-17 to 2018-19); overall target 2.95 crore by 2023-24 + **2 crore more** (2024-25 to **2028-29**).
[1:3] TABLE head: Feature | PMAY-G
  [1:3.0] Ministry | Rural Development
  [1:3.1] Minimum house size | 25 sq m (with a hygienic cooking space)
  [1:3.2] Unit assistance | ₹1.20 lakh (plains) / ₹1.30 lakh (hilly, NE)
  [1:3.3] Centre : State share | 60 : 40 (plains); 90 : 10 NE and Himalayan States; 100% UTs without legislature
  [1:3.4] Who builds | The beneficiary (with technical help; no contractor)
  [1:3.5] Monitoring | AwaasApp – geo-tagged, time-stamped photos for real-time, evidence-based progress
  [1:3.6] Convergence | MGNREGS (90/95 person-days of unskilled work), SBM-G toilet
[1:4] # PMAY-Urban
[1:5] - Launched **25 June 2015**: pucca house for **EWS/LIG and MIG** categories, including slum dwellers; original **Housing for All by 2022** target.
[1:6] - **Four components**: in-situ slum redevelopment; credit-linked subsidy; affordable housing in partnership; beneficiary-led construction (rural tap water is NOT one).
[1:7] - **PMAY-U 2.0 (2024)**: **1 crore** urban houses. Budget 2026-27 cut from ₹19,794 crore to ₹18,625 crore.
[1:8] - **Rajiv Awas Yojana**: aimed at a **slum-free India**; **subsumed into PMAY-U in 2015**.
[1:9] - Houses registered in women's names make PMAY a **gender-budget** scheme (26 Jan 2026).
[1:10] > Not offered in the options does not change the fact: PMAY-G share is **60:40** (not 75:25 or 90:10 for general States).
[1:11] ~~ Sources: app notes 2:165 sub 6; PMF LENS Apr 2026 (src/current-affairs/pmf-lens-2026-04-09.txt) p.11 (minimum unit size 25 sq m); static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/sep/doc2025924644801.pdf (60:40, 90:10, ₹1.20/1.30 lakh)

## SUB 3 | Pensions, Insurance and Social Assistance
[3:0] # Government and armed-forces pensions
[3:1] - **OROP = One Rank One Pension**: same pension for armed-forces personnel retiring in the **same rank with the same length of service**.
[3:2] - **NPS**: from **1 January 2004** for new Central Government employees (**armed forces excluded**); **State employees** join from the date their State notifies it; open to **all citizens 18-70 (incl. NRIs, OCIs) from 1 May 2009** – so "resident citizens only" or "21-55 only" are wrong.
[3:3] - Regulator **PFRDA** (Act 2013); defined-contribution; PRAN; **Tier I mandatory, Tier II voluntary**; no account for a third person.
[3:4] - **UPS (2025)**: after **25 years**, **50%** of last 12 months' average basic pay; minimum ₹10,000. **NPS Vatsalya (2025)**: for **minors**.
