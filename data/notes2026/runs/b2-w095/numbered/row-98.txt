## SUB 0 | Constitutional vs Non-constitutional – Odd One Out
[0:0] # How to tell them apart
[0:1] TABLE head: Type | Basis | Examples
  [0:1.0] Constitutional | An Article of the Constitution | FC, CAG, ECI, UPSC/SPSC, NCBC, ISC, SFC, SEC
  [0:1.1] Statutory | Act of Parliament; changed by amending that Act | NHRC, NCM, Zonal Councils, UGC, Press Council
  [0:1.2] Executive / extra-constitutional | Resolution / executive order; advisory | Planning Commission, NITI Aayog, NDC, Law Commission
  [0:1.3] Quasi-judicial | Statute; can summon, sanction; not a court | NHRC, SEBI, CIC, Press Council
[0:2] - **Extra-constitutional power/right:** power exercised though it has **no provision in the Constitution** ("not in accordance with" its text) – e.g. the Planning Commission, set up by Cabinet resolution, still exercised power over the Centre and the States.
[0:3] - **Most-asked fact:** the **Planning Commission** (resolution, **15 March 1950**) is **not** a constitutional body – the odd one out against **Finance Commission (Art. 280), Election Commission (Art. 324), UPSC / State PSCs (Art. 315), Inter-State Council (Art. 263)** (asked 8 times: APPSC G2 2003, 2008; NTR Univ.; UPPCS 2004, 2005, 2013, 2014).
[0:4] - **NITI Aayog** (Cabinet resolution, 1 Jan 2015) = **extra-constitutional**; the odd one among FC, ECI, ISC (UP Lower 2015) and among NCBC, CAG, FC (APPSC 2025).
[0:5] - **NITI Aayog, NDC and National Integration Council** = non-constitutional (executive); **Inter-State Council** = constitutional (Art. 263) → answer "A, B & C" (APSLPRB SI 2016).
[0:6] - **NDC, Planning Commission, Zonal Councils:** **none** is mentioned in the Constitution (UPSC 2013); Zonal Councils are statutory (States Reorganisation Act 1956, Sec. 15).
[0:7] - **Count question (APPSC G2 Mains 2025):** NITI Aayog ✗ (executive), NCM ✗ (statutory 1992), **Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities ✓ (Art. 350B)**, NHRC ✗ (statutory 1993) → **only 1** constitutional.
[0:8] - **ECI (Art. 324)** = the commission set up under a definite Article; **UGC, NHRC, CVC** are statutory (UPSC 2006).
[0:9] - **Constitution provides for** the Finance Commission, Election Commission **and** UPSC → "All the above".
[0:10] - **Inter-State Council:** source = the **Constitution** (Art. 263); set up by Presidential order on **28 May 1990** on Sarkaria advice – not a parliamentary law or Planning Commission recommendation.
[0:11] - **Inter-State Commerce Commission:** Art. 307 lets Parliament appoint an authority for Arts. 301-304; treated as constitutionally provided in exam keys.
[0:12] TABLE head: Body | Status | Basis
  [0:12.0] Inter-State Council | Constitutional | Art. 263; set up by President, 1990, on Sarkaria advice
  [0:12.1] River water tribunals | Statutory | Inter-State River Water Disputes Act 1956 (Art. 262 enables)
  [0:12.2] Zonal Councils | Statutory, non-constitutional | States Reorganisation Act 1956 (Sec. 15)
  [0:12.3] North Eastern Council | Statutory | NEC Act 1971; Governors + CMs; 8 NE States
  [0:12.4] National Security Council | Extra-constitutional | Executive order, 1998
[0:13] - Of ISC, NSC, Zonal Councils → **only ISC** is created by the Constitution.
[0:14] - **NDC and Governors' Conference:** extra-constitutional, extra-legal devices.
[0:15] - "Unity in diversity": ISC, NDC, FC, Zonal Councils, unitary judiciary, AIS.
[0:16] > Pattern: in every "not a constitutional body" PYQ the answer is the **Planning Commission** or **NITI Aayog**; NHRC is the odd one out when the other three are constitutional commissions.

## SUB 1 | Law Commission of India
[1:0] - **Nature:** a **non-statutory, non-constitutional advisory body**, set up by **executive order** of the Central Government for a **3-year** term; **not quasi-judicial**; recommendations not binding.
[1:1] - **Functions:** identify obsolete laws; examine laws in the light of DPSP; **convey to the Government** its views on law and judicial administration referred to it (not to the President); revise Central Acts.
[1:2] - **Working:** takes up research on references from the **Central Government or suo motu**; final report → **Ministry of Law and Justice** → considered and **submitted to Parliament** from time to time.
[1:3] - **APPSC G1 2024:** statements I (quasi-judicial), II (opinion to the President), III (reports not submitted to Parliament), IV (no suo motu power) are **all incorrect**.

[4:1] - **Press Council of India** = **statutory quasi-judicial** body (UP Lower 2004).
[4:2] - **NHRC** = statutory, so it has a status different from the Backward Class Commission (Art. 340), Finance Commission and Election Commission (UPPCS Mains 2004).
[4:3] - **Minorities:** "minority" is **not defined** in the Constitution, and the **Minority Commission is not a constitutional body** – both true, but the second does not explain the first (UPPCS 1991).
[4:4] - **NHRC** after 2019: term **3 years or 70** (earlier 5).
[4:5] - **NCM:** chair + vice-chair + **5**; **five of seven** from notified minorities; 3-year term; chair **Harjit Singh Grewal** (Jul 2026).
[4:6] - **CBI:** from **Santhanam Committee (1962-63)**; Director chosen by PM, CJI (or nominee), LoP; **Sec. 6 DSPE**: needs State consent.
[4:7] - **MPPSC:** formed by President under Sec. 118(3), States Reorganisation Act 1956.
[4:8] # Quasi-judicial bodies
[4:9] - Not a court; can **interpret and apply law**, summon, sanction. Examples: **NHRC, SEBI, CIC**, Mental Health Review Commission.
[4:10] - **Civil court powers** (summon, requisition records): **NCBC** (esp. since 2018), **NCSC, NCST, CIC**.
[4:11] - Not quasi-judicial: **NITI Aayog, Law Commission** (advisory).
