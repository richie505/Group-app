=== Centrally Sponsored Schemes (2015) and Central Control Loosened
- **1991 reforms loosened the Centre's control over the States** in **industrial licensing, tariffs and foreign investment**: central licensing largely ended, tariffs fell and FDI was opened, so States began competing for investment (APPSC Polytechnic Lecturers 2011). Planning-era control over State plans was not the target.
- **CSS rationalised** by the **NITI Aayog Sub-Group of Chief Ministers (2015)**.
| Group | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Core of the core** | **Social protection and social inclusion** schemes – MGNREGA, NSAP, umbrella schemes for SCs, STs, minorities; first charge on funds |
| Core | Mostly 60:40; 90:10 for NE and Himalayan states |
| Optional | States' choice; 50:50 (80:20 for NE and Himalayan states) |
- **PMMVY, ICDS** now **60:40**. CSS grew **26.5% a year** since 2016-17; state plan allocations fell nearly **30%**.
> APPSC G1 2017: "core of the core" = social protection and social inclusion schemes (not infrastructure, Digital India or "National Agenda" schemes in general).

=== Competitive vs Cooperative Federalism
- Post-1991 **delicensing** → states compete for private and foreign investment.
| Type | Symbol |
|---|---|
| Competitive | NITI Aayog (replaced Planning Commission's allocation role) |
| Cooperative | GST Council (Centre and States decide jointly) |
| Competitive and accountable | 16th FC's new GDP-contribution criterion |
- **BRAP** state ranking by **DPIIT** (first 2015): **AP first** in BRAP 2019 (Sept 2020), UP second, Telangana third.
# Regional disparities
- **Divergence view:** richer, industrialised states attracted most post-1991 private investment → gaps widened.
- Transfers try to correct imbalances; horizontal criteria spark disputes.

=== Fiscal Rules, Borrowing and GST
- **FRBM Act 2003** (amended 2018): fiscal deficit **≤3%** of GDP; general government debt **60%**, Central debt **40%** of GDP by 2025.
- Four statements in Parliament: Medium-term Fiscal Policy, Fiscal Policy Strategy, Macro-economic Framework, Medium-term Expenditure Framework.
- **Art. 293(3):** a state indebted to the Centre needs Union consent to borrow.
- **State of Kerala v. Union (2024):** **Art. 131** suit against the **Net Borrowing Ceiling** (counts off-budget borrowings).
- **16th FC:** state deficit cap **3% of GSDP**; end off-budget borrowing; rationalise subsidies.
- **Economic Survey 2025-26:** general government debt down **7.1 points** since 2020; Centre steers state capex through **SASCI**.
# GST and States' fiscal autonomy
- **GST Council (Art. 279A, 101st CAA 2016):** Centre **one-third** vote, all States **two-thirds**; decisions by **three-fourths** weighted majority.
- **Compensation cess:** 5-year compensation ended **June 2022**; extended to repay COVID-era loans; discontinued for most goods from **22 Sept 2025**.
