=== Contractualisation and Outsourcing
- **Outsourcing:** hiring outside sources (legal, IT, advertising, security) for regular work once done in-house; an outcome of globalisation, spread by IT – the method that "became popular in the recent past" for staffing jobs in both public and private sectors (APPSC G1).
  - Distractors: contract appointments (a form of it, narrower), emergency recruitment, on-line recruitment (a channel, not a method of employing).
- **Contract labour:** **20% (1999) → 40.7%** of manufacturing workforce (2022-23, ASI); such firms **31% lower productivity** (Aug 2025).
- Weaker direct employer relation → weaker unions; **Industrial Relations Code 2020**: 10% or 100 workers to form a union.
# AP angle
- **APCOS** (2020): recruits outsourced staff and **pays directly**, cutting contractor middlemen.
- **APSRTC:** about 8,000 outsourced staff paid via contractors; wage, PF, ESI irregularities (28 Jul 2026).
- **Civil Services Board:** curbs political interference in postings.
- Upper age limit for non-uniform direct recruitment raised **42 → 44** till 30 Sep 2027 (G.O. Ms. No. 187).

=== Downsizing the State and Pay Commissions
- **Downsizing, disinvestment, privatisation** shrink the State; **FDI promotion** is a growth/external-sector measure, **not** a cut in State size.
- Keyed set: downsizing, disinvestment, privatising higher education (UPSC 2010).
- **Disinvestment:** sale of part of PSU equity to the public; need not transfer ownership or management. **Privatisation:** withdrawal or outright sale.
- **Economic Survey 2025-26:** redefine "Government Company" so the State may hold **less than 51%**.

=== New Public Management
- Private-sector techniques in government (Phase 4 of Indian public administration after 1991); term popularised by **Christopher Hood (1991)**.
- **Hood's doctrines:** hands-on professional management, explicit standards and performance measures, **output controls**, competition.
- **Features:** performance auditing, **contracting out non-core services**, citizen as customer; it does **not** reject performance measures.
- **Pioneers:** **UK (Thatcher), New Zealand, Australia, USA**.

=== Reinventing Government (Osborne and Gaebler, 1992)
- "**Steering, not rowing**." Ten principles:
| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Catalytic | Steer rather than row |
| Community-owned | Empower rather than serve |
| Competitive | Inject competition |
| Mission-driven | Rules → mission |
| Results-oriented | Fund outcomes |
| Customer-driven | Meet customer needs |
| Enterprising | Earn rather than spend |
| Anticipatory | Prevent rather than cure |
| Decentralised | Hierarchy → participation and teamwork |
| Market-oriented | Change through the market |

=== Critiques of NPM and Citizen as Customer
| Thinker | Work | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| Denhardt & Denhardt | New Public Service (2000) | "Serve, not steer"; citizenship, public interest |
| Mark Moore | Creating Public Value (1995) | Value includes legitimacy and trust, not only efficiency |
- Both reject citizen → customer and State → service seller.
- **Citizen-as-customer instruments:** Citizens' Charters, service standards, grievance redressal.
- **Criticism:** citizen has **rights, not just purchasing power**; equity and accountability suffer.

=== Lateral Entry and Performance Management
- **Normal route:** JS/Director posts by tenure deputation (**Central Staffing Scheme, DoPT**).
- **Second ARC:** lateral entry at **Joint Secretary** level; performance-based appraisal and promotion; performance incentives.
- **NITI Aayog Action Agenda:** wider entry at middle/senior levels; longer, stable tenures.
- First lateral JS joined **2019**; **Aug 2024**: UPSC told to cancel advertisement for **45** posts.
- **Debate:** domain expertise vs no reservation in single posts, conflict of interest, demotivated career officers.
# Performance tools
- **RFD (2009)**, PMES, Cabinet Secretariat: objectives, success indicators, targets = **Key Result Areas**.
- **Referral Board** reviews adverse ACR remarks; **360-degree** multi-source feedback.
- **Good Governance Index:** uniform framework for States/UTs. **NeSDA** (2019; 2025 portal June 2026). **GRAI:** **11 indicators, 4 dimensions** on CPGRAMS.
- India **97th** in UN EGDI 2024.
