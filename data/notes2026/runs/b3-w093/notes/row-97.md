=== Rate Structure and GST 2.0
- 2017 slabs **5, 12, 18, 28%** + exempt + cess – meant as **transitional**.
- **GST 2.0** (**56th meeting**, effective **22 Sep 2025**): **0, 5% (merit), 18% (standard), 40% (demerit)**; ~**99%** of old 12% items → 5%; ~90% of old 28% items → 18%.
- **40% slab**: pan masala, tobacco, aerated sugary drinks, large luxury cars, yachts and **private aircraft**, online money gaming. Special: rough diamonds **0.25%**; gold, silver, jewellery **3%**.
- Drawback of multiple rates: **classification disputes** and litigation.
~~ Sources: Economic Survey 2025-26 Summary Part 1 p.15; app notes 3:97

=== Impact on Commerce and MSMEs
- **Common national market** through tax harmonisation; **ITC** at every stage ends cascading.
- **E-way bill** (above ₹50,000; 1 day per 200 km) → inter-State **check-posts removed** → less transit time and logistics cost.
- Formalisation: taxpayers **66.5 lakh (2017) → 1.51 crore (2025)**.
- **Composition scheme**: up to ₹1.5 crore (goods, **1%**), ₹50 lakh (services, **6%**); **no ITC**. Reform agenda: pre-filled returns, automated registration and refunds.
~~ Sources: app notes 3:97

=== Key Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Inverted Duty Structure | **Input tax > output tax** → ITC piles up, working capital blocked |
| ITC fraud | Bogus entities issue **invoices without supply** |
| Destination principle | Favours **consuming** States |
| IGST | Levied by the Centre and apportioned; State share goes to the **destination** State |
~~ Sources: app notes 3:97

=== Impact on States and Compensation
- States gave up VAT, entry, luxury, entertainment tax, octroi → more **dependence on transfers**.
- **Producing States** (Maharashtra, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka) feared loss → **compensation July 2017 – June 2022** at **14%** growth over **2015-16**.
- Cess continued to repay **COVID-19 loans**; scrapped 22 Sep 2025 except **tobacco**; unused balance **50% Centre, 50% States** by **SGST revenue**.
- **VAT** was the main State tax subsumed.
~~ Sources: Ramesh Singh Indian Economy 10th ed. Part 5 p.207; app notes 3:97

=== Unresolved GST Issues
- **Petroleum** (crude, petrol, HSD, natural gas, ATF) and **electricity** outside; petroleum date set by the Council under **Art. 279A(5)**.
- **Council voting**: Centre 1/3 can block a **3/4** decision → **effective veto**.
- **Mohit Minerals (2022)**: recommendations **not binding**; **simultaneous** power under Art. 246A; "**flexible federation**".
- **GSTAT** (Finance Act 2024): Principal Bench **New Delhi**; launched Sep 2025, hearings Dec 2025; mid-2025 no functional benches in many States.
- **Cesses outside the divisible pool** → concern over State fiscal autonomy.
~~ Sources: app notes 3:97

=== AP Angle
- AP is a **consuming State** – gains via **IGST settlement**; **Net GST = SGST + IGST settlement share**.
- Own tax revenue 2024-25 RE: **GST 38%** (largest); **State Excise 22%** (second).
- **AP High Court (May 2026)**: in overlapping Central/State GST probes, authorities decide **inter se** who proceeds.
