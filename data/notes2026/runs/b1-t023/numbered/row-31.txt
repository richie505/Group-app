## SUB 0 | Ajivikas – Makkhali Gosala and Niyati
[0:0] - **Founder (exam answer):** **Makkhali Gosala** (Gosala Maskariputra) – its most important leader and populariser (UPPCS 1996; UKPSC 2024; NDA 2015); earlier teachers like Nanda Vaccha are also named. Not Ananda, Upali, Vasubandhu or Dignaga.
[0:1] - **Niyati (fate):** "**Fate assigns everything, man is powerless**" = **Ajivikas** (UPPCS 2006; RAS 2013) – not Jains, Buddhists or Mimamsakas.
[0:2] - All thoughts and actions are **fixed before birth**; **no free will**; karma cannot change destiny; the **order of rebirth cannot be changed**.
[0:3] - **Practice:** belief in **determinism** and **severe austerities** (RAS 2024: 1 and 3); ascetics called **Sudra sannyasins**; **no caste discrimination**; held meetings in regular **sabhas** (State PSC: A, B and D).
[0:4] - **Atomic theory** of matter – influenced the later **Vaisheshika**.
[0:5] - Gosala was **Mahavira's companion for about six years**; Jain texts call him Mahavira's **disciple**; the **Bhagavati Sutra** records their quarrel; the **Majjhima Nikaya** records the Buddha's debates with Jains and Ajivikas.
[0:6] TABLE head: Ruler / record | Ajivika link
  [0:6.0] Bindusara | Popular under him; joined the sect
  [0:6.1] Ashoka, Dasharatha | Gifted the Barabar (and Nagarjuni) caves – among the earliest rock-cut caves in India
  [0:6.2] 7th Pillar Edict (Delhi-Topra) | Names Ajivikas with Brahmanas, the Sangha and Nirgranthas
[0:7] - Barabar caves were given by **Ashoka, not Chandragupta**; Ashoka himself was Buddhist.
[0:8] - **Decline:** survived about **2,000 years**, to the **14th c. CE** in **Karnataka and Tamil Nadu**; faded with the Puranic-Bhakti revival and loss of patronage.
[0:9] > Niyativada = Gosala (Ajivika); materialism = Charvaka.

## SUB 1 | Charvaka / Lokayata Materialism
[1:0] - **Other names:** **Lokayata** / **Lokayata Darshan** ("of the common people") (State PSC; CGPSC 2018); **Barhaspatya** after the legendary founder **Brihaspati**.
[1:1] - **Complete materialism**; highest value = **Kama** (pleasure) – not Dharma, Artha or Moksha (CGPSC 2017).
[1:2] - **Dehatmavada:** "**The conscious body is the soul**" (CGPSC 2024).
[1:3] - **Only pramana = perception** (pratyaksha) (CGPSC 2018); rejects inference, Vedic testimony, ritualism and the afterlife. Nyaya accepts **four** pramanas.
[1:4] - **Aphorism (IAS 1994):** "**Yavaj jivet sukham jivet, rinam kritva ghritam pibet**" – live well while you live, even on borrowing; once cremated there is no return → **Charvakas**.
[1:5] - Original **Brihaspati Sutra** is lost; Lokayata is known through **rivals' criticism**, e.g. **Madhava's Sarvadarshanasangraha**.
[1:6] TABLE head: School | Karma | Soul
  [1:6.0] Jainism | Removable by penance | Eternal jiva in all things
  [1:6.1] Buddhism | Intention-based | Anatta
  [1:6.2] Ajivika | Cannot alter destiny | Accepted
  [1:6.3] Charvaka | Does not decide rebirth | Conscious body

## SUB 2 | Six Teachers and the Age of Religious Ferment
[2:0] - **6th century BCE:** an age of religious upheaval across the world, while the **Vedic religion had become complex** – both true (UKPSC 2012). Shramana movements arose amid **urbanisation and Magadhan patronage**.
[2:1] TABLE head: Teacher | Doctrine
  [2:1.0] Makkhali Gosala | Niyativada – fatalism (Ajivika)
  [2:1.1] Purana Kassapa | Akriyavada – actions bring neither merit nor demerit
  [2:1.2] Ajita Kesakambalin | Materialist – nothing survives death
  [2:1.3] Pakudha Kaccayana | Seven eternal, uncreated elements
  [2:1.4] Sanjaya Belatthiputta | Sceptic – "eel-wriggling"
  [2:1.5] Nigantha Nataputta | = Mahavira
[2:2] - Source: the **Samannaphala Sutta** of the **Digha Nikaya** – Ajatashatru reports the six teachers' views to the Buddha.
