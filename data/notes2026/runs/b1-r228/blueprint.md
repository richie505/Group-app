## 5. Settled conflicts
- **Last step of satyagraha:** the printed key and the old notes said "first step fasting, last step strike". Gandhi himself (Harijan, 18 Aug 1946, mkgandhi.org) wrote that "fasting unto death is the last and the most potent weapon in the armoury of Satyagraha". Key → fast; the notes and own MCQ ffb86c390048f are fixed.
- **A-R (UP RO/ARO 2023):** the printed key (c) read "militancy" as violence. Spectrum (p.315) and PMF (p.116) both say Gandhi found in South Africa the masses' immense capacity to struggle and sacrifice, so R is true and explains A → (a).
- **Age on return:** PMF says "at the age of 46" and it is keyed. Strictly 45 → a dispute line.
- **Tagore as painter:** keyed 70, from the 1930 exhibitions. He began painting at about 60 (Wikipedia), and no option offers 60.
