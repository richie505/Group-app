=== Harijan Sevak Sangh and the Harijan Campaign (1932-34)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Founder | **M.K. Gandhi**, **30 September 1932**, while in (Yerawada) prison |
| Context | Set up **after the Poona Pact (Sept 1932)** – from then Gandhi made **Harijan upliftment** part of his political and social programme (UPSC 2025) |
| Former name | **All India Anti-Untouchability League** |
| First president | **G.D. (Ghanshyam Das) Birla** |
| Secretary | **Amritlal Thakkar (Thakkar Bapa)** |
- Work: access to **wells, schools, temples**; schools, hostels, cooperatives; **inter-dining, inter-caste marriage** – **separate electorates was NOT its activity**.
- **Harijan** weekly: first issue **11 February 1933, Poona**.
- **Harijan tour: November 1933 – July 1934**, about **20,000 km**, starting from **Wardha**; funds for the Sangh; orthodox groups disrupted his meetings.
- Untouchability = "**a blot on Hinduism**"; removal **from within Hindu society** by moral persuasion, temple entry, funds, schools, hostels and constructive work ("Hinduism dies if untouchability lives").
- **"Harijan"** ("children of God") – term from Gujarati saint **Narsi Mehta**; **Ambedkar** and Dalit leaders **rejected the term**.
- Post-1937 Congress ministries: **free education** and other upliftment for Harijans.
> Founder = Gandhi (not Ambedkar, Gokhale, Birla); president = Birla, secretary = Thakkar Bapa – do not swap.

=== Nai Talim – Basic Education (Wardha Scheme, 1937)
- **Basic education** propounded by **Mahatma Gandhi** (articles in Harijan, 1937); **craft-centred**, vocational; dignity of manual labour; education should be **self-supporting**.
- **Wardha (All-India National Education) Conference, October 1937** → scheme drawn up by the **Zakir Hussain Committee**; adopted at **Haripura (Feb 1938)**.
- Principles: **free and compulsory**, **7 years (age 7-14)**; **mother tongue** medium; learning through a **productive craft**; self-sufficient villages.
- Ultimate aim of education (Gandhi): **self-realisation** – growing into a **divine human being** by realising godliness, not acquisition or mere employment.
- **Socially Useful Productive Work (SUPW)** in the curriculum grew from **Gandhi's** idea of education through productive work.

=== Women and Religion
- Opposed **purdah, child marriage, dowry**; **supported remarriage of child widows** – but widow remarriage is not one of "Gandhi's concepts" (those are satyagraha, sarvodaya, trusteeship, Nai Talim).
- Women's role in satyagraha: **picketing liquor shops**, salt-making.
- **Sarva dharma samabhava** – equal respect for all religions; **ashram prayers** drew on many faiths.
- **Hindu-Muslim unity** = bedrock of nationalism; backed the **Khilafat**.

=== Gandhi in Andhra
- **Khadi tour (April 1929):** visited **Bezawada**; laid the foundation stone of the **Saraswata Niketanam** library building, **Vetapalem**.
- **Harijan tour (December 1933):** to collect funds and campaign against untouchability; spoke at **Mogalrajpuram, Vijayawada (16 Dec 1933)**.
- **Desasevikalu** = women's wing; women gave jewellery for the Tilak Fund; **Durgabai** met Gandhi at **Kakinada (1921)** and raised **Rs 5,000**.
- **Kolla Tayaramma (Tenali)** and **Alladi Annapurna Devi (Nellore)** – Harijan schools and hostels.
