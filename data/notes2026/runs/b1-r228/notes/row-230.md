=== Sarvodaya and the Bhoodan Movement
- **Sarvodaya** = welfare / uplift of all, reaching the last person; the **term was used by M.K. Gandhi** (title of his 1908 Gujarati paraphrase of Ruskin's Unto This Last).
| Person | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Gandhi** | Coined / used "Sarvodaya"; charkha and khadi |
| **Vinoba Bhave** | Made Sarvodaya the cornerstone; organised the **Sarvodaya Samaj**; **started the Bhoodan (land-gift) movement – 18 April 1951, Pochampalli** (Nalgonda, now Telangana); landlords asked to gift at least **one-sixth** of their land |
| **Jayaprakash Narayan** | Drafted the **Sarvodaya Plan (1950)** |
| **Shriman Narayan Aggarwal** | **Gandhian Plan (1944)**; Gandhian Constitution for Free India (1946) |
| Tummala Sitaramamurthy | Wrote **Sarvodaya Ganam**; translated Gandhi's autobiography into Telugu |
| Ambedkar | Dissent: the village = seat of **caste domination**, "a sink of localism, a den of ignorance" |
- **Sarvodaya Day: 30 January**.
> Bhoodan call = **Vinoba Bhave**, not Gandhi, JP or Balwantrai Mehta; first gift at **Pochampalli**, not Udaygiri or Venkatagiri.

=== Gandhian Economy and Trusteeship
- **Non-violent economy**: **centralisation leads to exploitation and inequality** → decentralise; **production for need (consumption), not profit**; self-reliance.
- Priority to **agriculture, village and cottage industries**; against mechanisation that displaces labour in labour-surplus India.
- A country's economy should suit its **climate, land and the nature of its citizens** → Gandhi **did not oppose mechanisation in the USA** ("did not favour mechanisation in the USA" = the **incorrect** statement).
- Not against modern industry as such: close to industrialists **G.D. (Ghanshyam Das) Birla** (who favoured healthy capitalism working with Gandhi for a common object) and Ambalal Sarabhai.
- **Gandhian Innovation** = **produce more from less input for more people** – coined by **C.K. Prahalad and R.A. Mashelkar**.
- **Ram Rajya** = sovereignty of the people based on **pure moral authority** (Harijan, 1937) – not Hindu Raj.
# Trusteeship
- The rich hold wealth **in trust for society**; **class harmony**, not class struggle; capitalists as trustees of workers.
- Idea linked to **South Africa, 1903** (law office at Johannesburg); roots in the Gita's non-possession and the English law of trusts.
- Applied to **capitalists and zamindars** (e.g. Ahmedabad mill owners); **Ahmedabad Mill Strike 1918 → 35% wage rise**; **Textile Labour Association (1920)** – arbitration.
- **Practical Trusteeship Formula**: drafted by **Narhari Parikh and Kishorelal Mashruwala** (Aga Khan Palace), refined by M.L. Dantwala – **minimum living wage and a ceiling on income**.

=== Books Behind the Ideas; Bread Labour
| Book | Author | Gandhi's link |
|---|---|---|
| **Unto This Last** | **Ruskin** | Gujarati retelling "**Sarvodaya**" (**1908**); dignity of labour; a source of bread labour |
| **The Kingdom of God is Within You** | **Tolstoy** | Love and non-violence |
| **Civil Disobedience** | **Thoreau** | Right to break unjust laws – not bread labour |
| Constructive Programme | Gandhi | Booklet, **1941** |
- Non-violence and civil disobedience were influenced by **Ruskin, Tolstoy and Thoreau** (not Churchill, Truman, Cripps).
- **Bread labour** (everyone must earn bread by physical labour): from **Tolstoy** (who took the term from **T.M. Bondaref**) + Ruskin; linked to the Gita's **yajna**.

=== Swadeshi, Khadi and Rural Reconstruction
- **Swadeshi + khadi**: rural employment, **self-reliance**; bridge between towns and villages.
| Body | Year |
|---|---|
| **All India Spinners' Association** | **1925** |
| **All India Village Industries Association** | **1934** |
- **Ponduru (Srikakulam)** – finest khadi; Prakasam's Khadi Centre at **Guravareddypalem** got Rs 10,000 from the AISA.
- **Constructive programme**: khadi, prohibition, removal of untouchability, national schools, Hindu-Muslim unity, village panchayats – **not heavy industrialisation**.
- Gandhi's rural development approach = **Samagra Gramseva** (comprehensive village service) – APPSC key.
| Article (DPSP) | Gandhian principle |
|---|---|
| **40** | Organise **village panchayats** |
| **43** | **Cottage industries** in rural areas (individual / co-operative) |
| **47** | Prohibition of intoxicating drinks |
