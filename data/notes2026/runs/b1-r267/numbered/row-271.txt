  [0:0.4] APSALT Regulation | 1/1959, amended 1/1970 | Bars transfer of Scheduled Area land to non-tribals
[0:1] - **1973 Act enforced in:** **1975** (Group-IV Mains 2023) – enacted 1973, in force from 1 January 1975.
[0:2] - **1973 Act followed the suggestions of:** the **Central Land Reforms Committee** (G2 2011), whose 1972 guidelines lowered ceilings and made the family the unit.
[0:3] - **Maximum retainable land:** **54 acres of 'K' grade (Class K dry) land** – the Act's table gives Class K a standard holding of 21.85 ha = 54 acres (G2 2008) – not "54 acres of the best land" and not hectares.
[0:4] - **Endowment lands:** exempted from the Tenancy Acts, but **not** from the **AP (Andhra Area) Inams (Abolition and Conversion into Ryotwari) Act, 1956** (Endowment Prelims 2022).
[0:5] - **AP New Tenancy Act, 2024** (planned): tenant entitlement cards, crop loans; rights on the **crop, not the land**.

[0:8] - Report **2006**, released May 2007; **104 recommendations** on surplus land, tenancy, assigned land and **tribal land** restoration.

## SUB 1 | Land Reforms – Phases, Types and Aims
[1:0] # Background and phases
[1:1] - **Prakasam Committee (1938):** advised **abolition of intermediaries**.
[1:2] - **Kumarappa Committee (1949):** set Congress's land reform agenda.
[1:3] - **Phase I (1947-70)** → **Phase II (1970 onwards)**.
[1:4] - **First Constitutional Amendment** shielded zamindari abolition from legal challenge.
[1:5] - **Raj Krishna's** classification: **liberative, distributive, organisational, developmental**.
[1:6] # The four measures
[1:7] TABLE head: Measure | Key fact
  [1:7.0] Abolition of intermediaries | Most successful; about 2 crore tenants in direct contact with the State; ~40% of cultivated area
  [1:7.1] Tenancy reform | Aim: security and ownership rights for the tiller tenant; rent capped at 1/4 to 1/6 of produce; defeated by "voluntary surrenders"; exception Operation Barga (1977), West Bengal
  [1:7.2] Ceiling | Two rounds (1950s, early 1970s); family made the unit from 1972; benami transfers
  [1:7.3] Consolidation | Succeeded in Punjab, Haryana, western UP
[1:8] - **Aim of post-independence tenancy laws:** **transfer of ownership rights to tiller tenants** (Asst. Director of Fisheries) – not protecting owners or only fixing returns.
[1:9] - Failure on ceilings and tenancy helped cause the **Naxalbari revolt (1967)**; abolition only changed the revenue hierarchy.
[1:10] - **National Council for Land Reforms:** set up **9 January 2008**, **chaired by the Prime Minister**.
[1:11] - First State to abolish zamindari = **UP (1950)**, though Madras's 1948 Act was earlier.

[1:14] - **Jagir and Sarfekhas** lands became **Diwani (ryotwari)** lands.

## SUB 4 | Tribal Policy – Nehru and Verrier Elwin
[4:0] - **Nehru:** **integration** respecting tribal identity; rejected isolation ("museum") and forced assimilation.
[4:1] - **Tribal Panchsheel** (credited to **Verrier Elwin** under Nehru's influence): develop on their own genius; rights in land and forests; tribal languages; own administration; **avoid over-administration** (not "maximum administrative control").
[4:2] - **Elwin's books:** **A Philosophy for NEFA** (cultural autonomy, limits on land alienation); **The Baiga** (1939; suggested a "National Park" for the tribe) (MPPCS 1999) – not S.C. Roy, D.N. Majumdar or H. Risley.
[4:3] - **Fifth Schedule:** Scheduled Areas outside the North-East; **PESA 1996** applies only there (not Sixth Schedule areas).
