## SUB 0 | Firsts of Indian and Telugu Cinema
[0:0] # Indian cinema
[0:1] TABLE head: Film | Milestone | Year | Maker
  [0:1.0] Raja Harishchandra | First full-length feature film – silent | 1913 | Dadasaheb Phalke
  [0:1.1] Alam Ara | First Indian sound film (talkie); premiered Majestic Cinema, Bombay, 14 March 1931 | 1931 | Ardeshir Irani
[0:2] - **First Indian talkie:** **Alam Ara** (UPSC 1999 ×2; G2 PYQ; APPSC 2018 Mains) – Raja Harishchandra was silent; Kalidas (1931) was the first Tamil/Telugu talkie; Kagaz ke Phool and Mughal-e-Azam are later.
[0:3] - **First Indian silent / feature film:** **Raja Harishchandra**, made by Phalke in **1913** (Extension Officer; SSC CGL 2015).
[0:4] - **Golden Age of Indian cinema:** **1940-1960** – Guru Dutt, Bimal Roy, Raj Kapoor, Mehboob Khan, Satyajit Ray (G2 Mains 2019) – not 1930-40, 1970-80 or 1990-present.
[0:5] - **Devika Rani** ("first lady of Indian screen", first Phalke awardee) married Russian painter **Svetoslav Roerich** (1945) (MPPSC 1993).
[0:6] - **Satyajit Ray** created the detective **Feluda** (G1 Prelims 2010).
[0:7] - **Tollywood:** the **Telugu** film industry (also used for Bengali cinema).

## SUB 1 | Film Awards – Phalke, Nandi and National Film Awards
[1:0] # Dadasaheb Phalke Award (Centre, 1969)
[1:1] - Started **1969**; first awardee **Devika Rani**; **Swarna Kamal** + **Rs 15 lakh** (raised from Rs 10 lakh in 2024).
[1:2] TABLE head: Telugu awardee | Year

  [1:2.1] L.V. Prasad | 1982

[1:3] - **Phalke Award for 2016:** **K. Viswanath** (Degree Lecturers 2017; Polytechnic Lecturers 2018) – not Kamal Haasan, S.P. Balasubrahmanyam, Manoj Kumar (2015) or S.S. Rajamouli.
[1:4] - **51st Phalke Award (for 2019):** **Rajinikanth** (Group-IV 2022).
[1:5] - Latest: **Mohanlal** (for 2023, 71st NFA); **Anant Nag** (for 2024, 72nd; second Kannada actor after **Rajkumar**, Sep 2026).
[1:6] # Nandi Awards (AP Government, 1964)
[1:7] - Instituted **1964** by the **Government of AP**; for **films, television and drama** – Telugu cinematic excellence, not science or sports (Sainik Welfare).
[1:8] - **Raghupathi Venkaiah Award:** the Nandi lifetime-achievement award for the Telugu film industry (Deputy Inspector of Survey) – not Kala Ratna or the NTR National Award (national-level contribution).
[1:9] - **Nandi best actress 2013:** **Anjali Patil** (Na Bangaaru Talli) (Asst. Director of Fisheries).
[1:10] # National Film Awards and IFFI
[1:11] - **Presented by** the **President of India**; run by the Directorate of Film Festivals/NFDC.
[1:12] - **66th NFA (2019) – best Telugu film:** **Mahanati**.
[1:13] - **Chiranjeevi:** Indian Film Personality of the Year **2022** (**53rd IFFI**); Padma Bhushan **2006**, Padma Vibhushan **2024**.
[1:14] - **S.P. Balasubrahmanyam:** **Centenary Award for Indian Film Personality of the Year** at the **47th IFFI (2016)**.
[1:15] > Exam angle: Nandi = AP, 1964; Phalke = Centre, 1969; last Telugu Phalke = K. Viswanath.
