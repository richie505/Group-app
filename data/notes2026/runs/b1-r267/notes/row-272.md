=== Major Tribes of AP – Region, PVTGs and the Lambadas
| Tribe | Region / districts |
|---|---|
| Koya, Konda Reddi | **Godavari region** |
| **Savara**, Jatapu, Gadaba | **Srikakulam, Vizianagaram** (north agency) |
| Bagata, Kondadora, Poraja | **Araku / Paderu agency** |
| Chenchu | **Nallamala** – Kurnool, Prakasam, Guntur |
| Yanadi | **Nellore**, Chittoor, Prakasam |
| **Lambada** (Sugali/Banjara) | Telangana, AP, Karnataka, Maharashtra – **not MP** |
- **Wrong pair (UPSC 1999):** **Madhya Pradesh – Lambada** – Assam–Miri, Nagaland–Konyak, Arunachal–Apatani are correct.
- **Sevalal (Sant Sevalal Maharaj, 1739-1806):** guru worshipped by the **Lambadis / Banjaras**; every thanda has his temple with goddess Jagadamba; samadhi at **Poharadevi** (Washim, Maharashtra); Sevalal Jayanti in mid-February (TSPSC 2016) – not Gonds, Chenchus or Koyas.
# PVTGs
- Undivided AP had **12 PVTGs**, second after **Odisha (13)**; India **75**.
- List: **Bodo Gadaba, Gutob Gadaba, Bondo Poroja, Khond Poroja, Parengi Poroja, Chenchu, Dongria Khond, Kutia Khond, Kolam, Konda Reddi, Konda Savara, Thoti** – the Koya are not a PVTG.

=== Dances and Festivals
# Dances
| Dance | Tribe |
|---|---|
| **Dhimsa** | **Bagata, Gadaba, Kondadora, Poraja, Valmiki** of Araku; also Savara, Jatapu, Khond, Kotia |
| **Kommu** | **Koya** – **bison-horn** headgear |
| Lambadi | Lambada/Sugali women |
| **Gussadi** | **Gond** (Telangana side) – not Lambada |
- **Dance of the Araku Valley tribes:** **Dhimsa (Thimsa)** (AEE 2008) – "Tappeta dance" and "Araku dance" are distractors.
- Dhimsa: **15-20 women** in circles, hands on shoulders; dappu, tudumu, mori, kidgi, gilka, jodukommulu; ASR and Parvathipuram Manyam; origin **Koraput**.
- **Guinness record** (Jul 2026): largest Dhimsa, **13,000+** women at **Bhogapuram** airport (Vizianagaram); certificate to AP Tribal Welfare Dept.

=== Tribal Languages – Families and Extinction
| Family | Languages |
|---|---|
| Dravidian | Gondi, Koya, Kolami, Yerukula, Konda (Kubi) |
| **Munda** | **Savara**, **Gadaba** |
- **Savara** is the only AP tribal language with its own **script**.
- **Language reported to have totally disappeared:** **Reli** (a 2013 Bhasha Research and Publication Centre study named it among ~20 languages lost in AP in 50 years) (Social & Cultural History of AP) – **Gutob Gadaba** was listed only as "on the verge of extinction"; Yerukula survives.
  - ⚠ Census 2011 still records about 13,000 Reli speakers, so "extinct" is the exam's answer, not a settled fact.

=== Tribe – Definition, Law and Social Organisation
- **Marshall Sahlins:** tribe = **"segmental organisation"** of equal multi-family groups.
- **Article 342:** **President** notifies STs after consulting the **Governor**; only **Parliament** amends the list (**342(2)**).
- **Census 2011:** ST = **8.6%** of India; **Bhil** largest tribe, then Gond; **Madhya Pradesh** has the largest tribal population (not Odisha).
# The Chenchus
- **Endogamous tribe**, **exogamous clans** (kulam/gothram); no clan hierarchy; **patriarchal, patrilineal**.
| Village office | Clan |
|---|---|
| **Raju** | **Bhoomani** |
| Mantri | Kumakula |
| **Kolagadu** | Dasari / Uttaluri / Mandla |
- These three officers, from different clans, **officiate marriages**.
- Youth dormitory **Ghotul** = **Muria** of Chhattisgarh (not an AP tribe).
