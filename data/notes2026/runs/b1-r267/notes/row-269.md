=== Firsts of Indian and Telugu Cinema
# Indian cinema
| Film | Milestone | Year | Maker |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Raja Harishchandra** | First full-length **feature film** – **silent** | **1913** | **Dadasaheb Phalke** |
| **Alam Ara** | First Indian **sound film (talkie)**; premiered Majestic Cinema, Bombay, **14 March 1931** | **1931** | **Ardeshir Irani** |
- **First Indian talkie:** **Alam Ara** (UPSC 1999 ×2; G2 PYQ; APPSC 2018 Mains) – Raja Harishchandra was silent; Kalidas (1931) was the first Tamil/Telugu talkie; Kagaz ke Phool and Mughal-e-Azam are later.
- **First Indian silent / feature film:** **Raja Harishchandra**, made by Phalke in **1913** (Extension Officer; SSC CGL 2015).
- **Golden Age of Indian cinema:** **1940-1960** – Guru Dutt, Bimal Roy, Raj Kapoor, Mehboob Khan, Satyajit Ray (G2 Mains 2019) – not 1930-40, 1970-80 or 1990-present.
- **Devika Rani** ("first lady of Indian screen", first Phalke awardee) married Russian painter **Svetoslav Roerich** (1945) (MPPSC 1993).
- **Satyajit Ray** created the detective **Feluda** (G1 Prelims 2010).
- **Tollywood:** the **Telugu** film industry (also used for Bengali cinema).
# Telugu cinema
| Film | Milestone | Year | Director |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bhishma Pratigna | First Telugu silent film | 1921 | **Raghupathi Venkaiah Naidu, R.S. Prakash** |
| Bhakta Prahlada | First Telugu talkie | 1931 | **H.M. Reddy** (not C. Pullaiah) |
| Lava Kusa | First full-length Telugu colour film | 1963 | C. Pullaiah, C.S. Rao |
> Exam angle: silent 1913 (Phalke) → talkie 1931 (Alam Ara); Telugu: silent 1921 → talkie 1931 → colour 1963.

=== Film Awards – Phalke, Nandi and National Film Awards
# Dadasaheb Phalke Award (Centre, 1969)
- Started **1969**; first awardee **Devika Rani**; **Swarna Kamal** + **Rs 15 lakh** (raised from Rs 10 lakh in 2024).
| Telugu awardee | Year |
|---|---|
| **B.N. Reddy** (first South Indian) | 1974 |
| L.V. Prasad | 1982 |
| B. Nagi Reddy | 1986 |
| **Akkineni Nageswara Rao** | 1990 |
| **D. Ramanaidu** | 2009 |
| **K. Viswanath** | **2016** (latest Telugu awardee) |
- **Phalke Award for 2016:** **K. Viswanath** (Degree Lecturers 2017; Polytechnic Lecturers 2018) – not Kamal Haasan, S.P. Balasubrahmanyam, Manoj Kumar (2015) or S.S. Rajamouli.
- **51st Phalke Award (for 2019):** **Rajinikanth** (Group-IV 2022).
- Latest: **Mohanlal** (for 2023, 71st NFA); **Anant Nag** (for 2024, 72nd; second Kannada actor after **Rajkumar**, Sep 2026).
# Nandi Awards (AP Government, 1964)
- Instituted **1964** by the **Government of AP**; for **films, television and drama** – Telugu cinematic excellence, not science or sports (Sainik Welfare).
- **Raghupathi Venkaiah Award:** the Nandi lifetime-achievement award for the Telugu film industry (Deputy Inspector of Survey) – not Kala Ratna or the NTR National Award (national-level contribution).
- **Nandi best actress 2013:** **Anjali Patil** (Na Bangaaru Talli) (Asst. Director of Fisheries).
# National Film Awards and IFFI
- **Presented by** the **President of India**; run by the Directorate of Film Festivals/NFDC.
- **66th NFA (2019) – best Telugu film:** **Mahanati**.
- **Chiranjeevi:** Indian Film Personality of the Year **2022** (**53rd IFFI**); Padma Bhushan **2006**, Padma Vibhushan **2024**.
- **S.P. Balasubrahmanyam:** **Centenary Award for Indian Film Personality of the Year** at the **47th IFFI (2016)**.
> Exam angle: Nandi = AP, 1964; Phalke = Centre, 1969; last Telugu Phalke = K. Viswanath.

=== Studios, Television and Mass Media
# Studios
| Studio | Founder | Place | Year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vijaya Vauhini | B. Nagi Reddy and Aluri Chakrapani | **Madras** | 1948 |
| **Annapurna** | **Akkineni Nageswara Rao** | Banjara Hills, Hyderabad | **1976** |
| Ramanaidu | D. Ramanaidu | Hyderabad | – |
| **Ramoji Film City** | **Ramoji Rao** | Hyderabad (1,666 acres) | **1996** |
- **Ramoji Film City:** set up **1996** (Polytechnic Lecturers) – not 1990, 1994 or 1997; **Guinness** – world's **largest film studio complex**.
- The industry's move from Madras to Hyderabad after 1956 built the Hyderabad studio belt.
# Television, radio and print
- **Doordarshan Kendra Hyderabad:** **1977**.
- **Gemini TV** and **ETV**: both **1995**; ETV by **Ramoji Rao** (not NTR).
- **Prasar Bharati** = Akashvani + Doordarshan; **NFDC** (runs National Film Awards) and **CBFC** are separate.
- **TV serial Ramayan** (Doordarshan, 1987-88): produced and directed by **Ramanand Sagar** (MPPSC 1998) – B.R. Chopra made Mahabharat.
- **Sangham Radio**, Machnoor (**Medak district**): one of India's first community radios (**2008**), run by **Dalit women** of the Deccan Development Society (Asst. Telugu Translator).
- **Chandamama:** children's magazine started **July 1947**, Madras, by **B. Nagi Reddy** and **Chakrapani**.
