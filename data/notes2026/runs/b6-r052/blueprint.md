## 2. Angles
- **Factual recall** dominates: chair/host (BRICS 17th), theme (BIMSTEC 6th), year (IWT 1960), country (snow leopard, PNG, Nigeria), committee number (1267).
- **Count-type statement** (UPSC 2025 NATO): "how many of the above" – needs exact membership lists.
- **NOT / odd-one-out** (CDP components). The app MCQs add many INCORRECTLY-matched pairs (summit–venue, HQ–body) and NOT-correct statement items.
- **Current linkage:** PYQs ask the summit of the year before the exam (Rio 2025, Bangkok 2025), and older facts revived by news (IWT abeyance 2025; Masood Azhar via 1267 listings; Sweden/Finland joining NATO).
