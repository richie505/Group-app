=== UN 1267 Committee, Gaza Plan and Pakistan's UN Roles
# UN 1267 Committee – PYQ
- The **UNSC 1267 (ISIL/Da'esh and Al-Qaida) Sanctions Committee** designates global terrorists: **asset freeze, travel ban, arms embargo**; **Masood Azhar** (Jaish-e-Mohammad) listed under it (May 2019) after years of Chinese "holds".
- **All 15 UNSC members**; decides by **consensus**; delisting via the **Ombudsperson**.
- **1540 Committee** = WMD non-proliferation; **1373 / CTC** = general counter-terror obligations of states.
- **Sept 2025**: Pakistan and China's bid to list the **BLA** and **Majeed Brigade** was **blocked by the US, UK and France**; the US had itself designated both **Foreign Terrorist Organizations**.
> Exam angle: APPSC Group-1 asked which committee listed Masood Azhar – **1267**.
# Pakistan's UN counter-terrorism roles
- **June 2025**: **Chair of the UNSC Taliban Sanctions Committee** and **Vice-Chair of the Counter-Terrorism Committee**.
- CTC works with UNOCT and **FATF** – the **IMF is not** a formal CTC partner.
- Pakistan **removed** from the FATF grey list in **October 2022** (not added).
# Trump–Netanyahu 20-point Gaza plan
- Hamas **disarmed**, **no role in governance**; international security force (with Egypt) **trains Palestinian police**.
- **Board of Peace** headed by **Donald Trump and Tony Blair**; Palestinian statehood only **vague**.
# Doha emergency summit (September 2025)
- After Israel's strike on Hamas leaders in Doha; **40+** Arab and Islamic nations.
- **Pakistan** pushed a NATO-style "Arab-Islamic task force"; **Turkey** preferred **economic sanctions**; Pakistan – only nuclear-armed Muslim nation, ~**170** warheads.
