=== 18th BRICS Summit, New Delhi – India's 2026 Chairship
# Summit facts
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Edition and dates | **18th BRICS Summit**, **12–13 September 2026** |
| Venue | **Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi** |
| Chairship | India's **4th** (from **1 January 2026**, taken over from **Brazil**); earlier 2012, 2016, 2021 |
| Theme | "**Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability**" |
| Logo | **Lotus** (national flower) with a "**Namaste**" motif |
| Next chair | **China (2027)** – India handed over on 13 Sep 2026 |
- **Members (11):** Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa + Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, UAE (2024), Saudi Arabia, **Indonesia (January 2025)**. Rocket counts **11 full members and 10 partners**; **Saudi Arabia** has not formally confirmed full membership and attended as an **invited country** (some counts give 10 full members).
- **Partners (10):** Belarus, Bolivia, Cuba, Kazakhstan, Malaysia, Nigeria, Thailand, Uganda, Uzbekistan, **Vietnam (10th partner, 2025)**.
- BRICS share: **49.5% of world population**, ~40% of GDP, 26% of trade.
- **NDB:** HQ **Shanghai**; President **Dilma Rousseff**; agreement signed at **Fortaleza, 2014** (operational 2015).
# New Delhi Leaders' Declaration
- Adopted **unanimously** on 12 Sep 2026; promotes **local currencies**; opposes **unilateral wars**, unilateral sanctions and carbon tariffs (CBAM); no common BRICS currency.
- China and Russia backed India's and Brazil's greater UN/UNSC role; condemned the **Pahalgam** attack (22 April 2025).
- **First BRICS declaration since 2022 with no reference to Ukraine**.
- **NIMHANS** to coordinate BRICS mental-health centres; welcomed the **BRICS Photovoltaic Industry Action Plan** (adopted at **Jaipur**, August 2026).
- Modi proposed a **BRICS Continuity and Implementation Mechanism**.

=== SCO 2026 – Bishkek Summit and Dushanbe Heads of Government
# 26th SCO Council of Heads of State
| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Venue and dates | **Bishkek, Kyrgyz Republic**, **31 Aug–1 Sep 2026** |
| Chair | President **Sadyr Japarov** |
| Occasion | SCO's **25th anniversary** (founded **15 June 2001**) |
| Theme | "**25 Years of the SCO: Together Towards Sustainable Peace, Development and Prosperity**" |
| Next chair | **Pakistan** (2026–27; summit in 2027) |
- **28 documents** headed by the **Bishkek Declaration**; an **SCO–CSTO–CIS roadmap** approved; Universal Centre for Countering Challenges and Threats and an SCO Anti-Drug Centre.

- Summits: **2024 Astana**, **2025 Tianjin**, **2026 Bishkek**.
# 25th SCO Council of Heads of Government
- **Dushanbe, Tajikistan**, **19–20 Oct 2026**; chaired by Tajik PM **Kohir Rasulzoda**.
- India represented by **Rajnath Singh** (not PM Modi); adopted Action Plan 2026–2030 for PMTEC and SCO Budget 2027.
# SCO background
- **1996: Shanghai Five** – China, Russia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan (**not Uzbekistan**).
- **2001**: SCO founded in **Shanghai** with Uzbekistan; **2017: India and Pakistan**; **2023: Iran**; **July 2024: Belarus (10th member)** – **10 full members**.
- **Secretariat: Beijing**; **RATS: Tashkent**.

- **7 members**: Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand (**not Maldives**).

- 6th Summit Bangkok, April 2025 – theme "**Prosperous, Resilient and Open**"; next summit host **Bangladesh**.

=== Bilateral Talks and Disputes, Sep–Oct 2026
# Indus Waters Treaty and the PCA
- India **rejected** the **Permanent Court of Arbitration** (est. 1899, **The Hague**) ruling as an "illegally constituted arbitral tribunal".
- Treaty **in abeyance** since the **Pahalgam terror attack (22 April 2025)**.
- **IWT**: signed **19 September 1960, Karachi**, by **Jawaharlal Nehru and Ayub Khan**; brokered by the **World Bank**; **Eastern rivers (Ravi, Beas, Sutlej) – India**; Western (Indus, Jhelum, Chenab) – Pakistan.
# India–China
- **6 Sep 2026**: first Corps Commander talks in Arunachal at the **Wacha–Damai BPM point, Kibithu sector** (Anjaw).
- Followed tensions at **Taksing, Upper Subansiri**; Doval–Wang Yi talks, 25 Aug 2026.
- **McMahon Line** – **Simla Convention, 1914**.
# Other items
- **India–Czech Republic** (13 Oct 2026): defence industrial cooperation via the **India–Czech Joint Defence Committee**.
- **India–Germany Joint Declaration of Intent** on conservation and '**wise use**' of Ramsar wetlands. **Ramsar Convention**: 2 Feb **1971**, Ramsar, **Iran**; **Montreux Record** = sites with adverse ecological change.
- **IMEC**: Eastern corridor India–Arabian Gulf; Northern corridor Arabian Gulf–Europe; rail, shipping, electricity, digital, clean hydrogen.
- **EU "Associate Member" status for Canada**: proposed by **Ursula von der Leyen**; EU 27 members, **21 use the euro**.
- **Marshall Islands**: capital **Majuro**; free association with the US under the **Compact of Free Association**.
