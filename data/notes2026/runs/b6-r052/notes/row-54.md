=== WHO – Ebola PHEIC and the 79th World Health Assembly
# Ebola declared a PHEIC (May 2026)
- **WHO** declared the Ebola outbreak in **DR Congo and Uganda** a **Public Health Emergency of International Concern** – highest alert under the IHR, **not a pandemic emergency**.
- Strain **Bundibugyo** – limited vaccine options; **17th** Ebola outbreak in Congo since **1976**.
- Led to the **postponement of IAFS-IV**.
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Largest outbreak | **West Africa 2014–16** – **Guinea, Liberia, Sierra Leone** |
| Incubation / contact tracing | 2–21 days / **21 days** |
| Treatments | mAb114 (Ansuvimab), REGN-EB3 (Inmazeb) |
| Vaccine | **Ervebo**; Zabdeno + Mvabea |

# Leiden (Anaimangalam) Copper Plates returned
- 21 large + 3 small plates + bronze ring, from the **University of Leiden**.
- Grant of Anaimangalam village to the **Chulamanivarma Vihara**, built by Srivijaya ruler **Sri Mara Vijayotunga Varman**; **promised by Raja Raja Chola I**, **executed by Rajendra Chola I**.
- Genealogy in **Sanskrit (Grantha)**; boundaries in **Tamil**; seal – **Chola Tiger, Pandya Fish, Chera Bow**.
- **Karandai Copper Plates** = record 57-plate charter of Rajendra I; **Sogaura** plates = Mauryan famine relief in **Prakrit, Brahmi**.
