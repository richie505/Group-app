## SUB 1 | Gravitational Waves, LIGO-India and Mega-Science Projects
[5:1:0] # Gravitational waves
[5:1:1] - Predicted by **Einstein (1916)**; ripples in space-time from **merging black holes**, neutron-star collisions.
[5:1:2] - **LIGO** = **Laser Interferometer Gravitational-Wave Observatory** (USA); first detection **14 September 2015** (announced 11 Feb 2016); **Nobel 2017**: Weiss, Barish, Thorne.
[5:1:3] - **Second detection** announced **June 2016**: two **black holes** merging ~**1.4 billion light-years** away (not white dwarfs).
[5:1:4] - **eLISA** (evolved Laser Interferometer Space Antenna) = space mission to **detect gravitational waves** (not neutrinos).
[5:1:5] - Black-hole merger observations (UPSC 2019) = **gravitational waves detected**, not Higgs bosons.
[5:1:6] # LIGO-India
[5:1:7] - At **Aundh, Hingoli**, Maharashtra; **DAE + DST** with US **NSF**.
[5:1:8] - Lead institutions: **IPR Gandhinagar, IUCAA Pune, RRCAT Indore** (not IIST or "Kepler's Observatory").
[5:1:9] - Consortium = **IndIGO** (Indian Initiative in Gravitational-wave Observations) – the APPSC G2 2017 key said "IndLIGO", which is not the name.
[5:1:10] # Other projects and telescopes

  [5:1:11.0] ITER | Cadarache, France | ITER-India, IPR Gandhinagar (leads both LIGO-India and ITER-India)
  [5:1:11.1] Thirty Meter Telescope | Mauna Kea, Hawaii | DST + DAE; IIA Bengaluru
  [5:1:11.2] Square Kilometre Array | South Africa, Australia | NCRA-TIFR, Pune
  [5:1:11.3] INO (neutrinos) | Bodi West Hills, Theni, Tamil Nadu | TIFR

  [5:1:11.5] FAIR | Darmstadt, Germany | Bose Institute; DST:DAE 50:50
[5:1:12] - **Devasthal Optical Telescope** (3.6 m, ARIES, Nainital, 2016) = **India's largest optical telescope**.
[5:1:13] - **Sampurnanand Telescope** (104 cm) at **ARIES, Nainital** (Uttarakhand – **not Ladakh**): **50 years** (1972-2022); comets, star-forming regions, clusters.
[5:1:14] - **IIA Bengaluru**: VELC payload of Aditya-L1, Himalayan Chandra Telescope (2 m, Hanle), Kodaikanal Solar Observatory.

## SUB 2 | ICAR – Origin, Structure, Heads and News
[5:2:0] - **ICAR** = **Indian Council of Agricultural Research**; set up **16 July 1929** (ICAR Day = **16 July**) as a **society** (Societies Registration Act 1860) on the **Royal Commission on Agriculture** report; HQ New Delhi.
[5:2:1] - Under **DARE** (Dec 1973), Ministry of Agriculture; **113 institutes** (72 research institutes).
[5:2:2] - **Director General** = Secretary DARE: **Dr S. Ayyappan** (2010-16, the answer in 2015) → **Dr Mangi Lal Jat** (as of 2026).
[5:2:3] - **KVKs**: district level, **100% centrally funded**, under **11 ATARIs**; first KVK **Puducherry, 1974** (Mohan Singh Mehta committee, 1973). Sep 2026: skill centres in all KVKs by Dec 2028.
[5:2:4] # In the news
[5:2:5] - **13 Dec 2019**: ICAR–**NABARD** MoU (sustainable agriculture, climate-resilient farming).
[5:2:6] - **Sardar Patel Outstanding ICAR Institution Award 2021** (large institute): **ICAR-NAARM, Hyderabad**.
[5:2:7] - **562** high-yielding field-crop varieties released 2019-2021 (APPSC AE 2022 key).
[5:2:8] - **Lumpi-ProVacInd**: lumpy skin disease vaccine developed by **two ICAR institutes**.

## SUB 4 | DAE, AEC and the Department of Space
[5:4:0] # Atomic energy
[5:4:1] - **Atomic Energy Commission: 1948** (Atomic Energy Act 1948), chaired by Secretary DAE; **DAE: 1954** by Presidential Order; both DAE and DoS are under the **Prime Minister**.
[5:4:2] - **AERB** (1983) = **nuclear and radiation safety regulator** (BARC = research, NPCIL = power plants, TIFR = research).
[5:4:3] TABLE head: Unit (all under DAE) | Function
  [5:4:3.0] BARC, Trombay | Began 1954 as AEET, renamed 1967 after Homi Bhabha
  [5:4:3.1] IGCAR, Kalpakkam | Fast breeder reactor R&D
  [5:4:3.2] NPCIL | PSU (1987); builds and runs nuclear plants (23 reactors, 7,480 MW)
  [5:4:3.3] TIFR (1945, Bombay) | Start of organised nuclear research; NCRA-TIFR runs GMRT near Pune
  [5:4:3.4] Heavy Water Board (Mumbai) | Heavy water – not AMD
  [5:4:3.5] IREL (Mumbai) | Beach-sand minerals, rare earths, zircon
  [5:4:3.6] AMD (Hyderabad) | Atomic minerals exploration
  [5:4:3.7] UCIL | Uranium mining
  [5:4:3.8] HBNI | DAE deemed university (NIRF 2025: 7th research, 20th overall)
[5:4:4] - Research reactors: **APSARA** Asia's first (1956), **Dhruva** largest, **KAMINI** only U-233-fuelled.
[5:4:5] - DAE products: Bhabhatron (cobalt therapy), Nisargruna (biogas), AKRUTI (rural incubator), TAG groundnut varieties.
[5:4:6] # Space and defence
[5:4:7] - **ISRO** 15 Aug 1969 (INCOSPAR 1962, Vikram Sarabhai); **Space Commission + DoS 1972**; **ISRO HQ and DoS secretariat: Antariksh Bhavan, Bengaluru**; Chairman V. Narayanan (2026).
[5:4:8] - Arms: Antrix (Sep 1992), **NSIL (2019)**, **IN-SPACe (2020, Ahmedabad)**; NRSC Hyderabad. LVM3-M6 (24 Dec 2025): BlueBird Block-2, heaviest payload from India.
[5:4:9] - **DRDO** (1958), DRDO Bhawan, New Delhi, under **DDR&D**; Chairman Samir V. Kamat.
[5:4:10] - Mandates: ISRO – space for development; DRDO – defence systems; DAE – nuclear power, radioisotopes; ICAR – agriculture; ICMR – biomedical research. ICMR under DHR, CSIR under DSIR.
[5:4:11] - Other HQs: Indian Bureau of Mines Nagpur; CIMFR Dhanbad; NATMO Kolkata; **MIDHANI Hyderabad**.

## SUB 0 | Indian Supercomputers and Computing Firsts
[8:0:0] - India's supercomputer family is **PARAM**, designed and assembled by **C-DAC, Pune** (C-DAC set up 1988) – not Shubham, SX-2, Tejas or EKA.
[8:0:1] - **PARAM 8000 (1991)** = India's **first** (first indigenously developed) supercomputer; C-DAC under **Vijay Bhatkar**, the **father of Indian supercomputing**.
[8:0:2] TABLE head: Machine | Developer / site
  [8:0:2.0] PARAM 8000 | C-DAC (1991)
  [8:0:2.1] Anupam | BARC – from 1991, parallel processing
  [8:0:2.2] Flosolver | NAL Bengaluru
  [8:0:2.3] PARAM Padma | C-DAC (2003) – first Indian system on the TOP500 list (June 2003, rank 171)
  [8:0:2.4] SAGA-220 | ISRO (2011) – about 220 TFLOPS
  [8:0:2.5] SahasraT (Cray XC40) | SERC, IISc – India's fastest in 2015-16 (ahead of Aaditya, IITM Pune)
  [8:0:2.6] PARAM Siddhi-AI | C-DAC (2020) – ranked 62nd/63rd on TOP500 (Nov 2020), India's highest then
  [8:0:2.7] Pratyush / Mihir (weather) | IITM Pune / NCMRWF Noida
[8:0:3] - **Computing firsts:** first analog computer in India designed by **Samarendra Kumar Mitra** (ISI Kolkata, 1953); **TIFRAC** (1960) = first indigenous digital computer; exam keys name **"Siddharth"** as "the first computer made in India".

## SUB 1 | C.V. Raman and National Science Day
[9:1:0] - Born **7 November 1888**; worked at **IACS, Kolkata**; **Raman Effect** discovered **28 February 1928** → **National Science Day, 28 February** (marks the discovery, not his birthday or a satellite launch).
[9:1:1] - **Raman Effect:** when light passes through a **transparent** material (any transparent medium – solid, liquid or gas), a small fraction is **scattered with a change in wavelength**.
[9:1:2] - **Raman spectroscopy** identifies molecular structure – the robust tool to **characterise graphene**.
[9:1:3] - **Sky is blue** because of **scattering** (Rayleigh scattering of shorter wavelengths) – not absorption, reflection or dispersion.
[9:1:4] - Discovered the Effect at **IACS, Kolkata**; first Indian director of **IISc**; first to study the harmonic nature of the **tabla and mridangam**. He did **not** found the Saha Institute.
[9:1:5] - **Honours:** knighted **1929**, **Nobel Physics 1930** (first Asian science Nobel), **Bharat Ratna 1954**; "Father of Indian Physics".
[9:1:6] - Founded the **Indian Academy of Sciences (1934)** and the **Raman Research Institute, Bengaluru (1948)** – now an autonomous institute under **DST**.
