=== INSPIRE – DST Flagship (2008)
# Basics
- **INSPIRE** = **Innovation in Science Pursuit for Inspired Research**; flagship scheme of the **Department of Science and Technology (DST)**, launched **2008**, to draw young talent into science.
- **Not** a Ministry of Education scheme and **not** a residential programme for Classes VI–X (the residential part is the INSPIRE science camp; MANAK is an award, not a course).
# Components
| Component | Full form | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **SEATS** | **Scheme for Early Attraction of Talents for Science** | School students (MANAK awards, INSPIRE camps) |
| MANAK | Million Minds Augmenting National Aspiration and Knowledge | Classes VI–X, aged 10–15; **₹10,000** each |
| **SHE** | **Scholarship for Higher Education** | Top-ranked UG/PG students in natural and basic sciences |
| **AORC** | **Assured Opportunity for Research Careers** – INSPIRE Fellowship (PhD) + INSPIRE Faculty | Doctoral and post-doctoral researchers |
- **2025 data:** MANAK **8.54 lakh** ideas, **46,926** students given ₹10,000; INSPIRE Fellowship **653** doctoral fellowships, **65% women**.
> ⚠ Trap: SHE = **Scholarship for Higher Education**, NOT "Support for Her Education" – it is not a women's scheme (APPSC FRO 2022). Statement angle: "separate components SEATS, SHE, AORC" ✓ and "DST flagship" ✓; "residential for VI–X" ✗ and "launched by Ministry of Education" ✗ → **B & C only** (TREI-RB 2023).

=== Women in STEM – KIRAN, WIDUSHI, WEST
| Scheme | Body | Target group |
|---|---|---|
| **WISE-KIRAN** (Knowledge Involvement in Research Advancement through Nurturing; WISE = Women in Science and Engineering) | DST | **Women scientists**, esp. after a career break; WISE-PhD, WISE-PDF, WIDUSHI |
| **WIDUSHI** | DST (under WISE-KIRAN) | **Senior women scientists**, **interdisciplinary** S&T |
| Vigyan Jyoti | DST, with Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti | Meritorious girls, Classes IX–XII |
| CURIE | DST | 22 women PG colleges – research facilities |
| GATI (Gender Advancement for Transforming Institutions) | DST | **Institutions** – gender-equity charter, modelled on UK's **Athena SWAN** |
| SERB-POWER | SERB (now ANRF) | Women researchers in academic/R&D institutions |
| BioCARe | DBT | Women biotech scientists after a career break |
| SEED | DST | 52 STI Hubs for SC/ST communities |
- **KIRAN** is the GoI initiative for innovation and research **especially among women** – not Jan Dhan, Digital India or Make in India (TSPSC 2023).
- **WEST (Women in Engineering, Science & Technology):** launched **September 2022** by the **Office of the Principal Scientific Adviser** as a new initiative **of I-STEM**; helps women with STEM backgrounds, including after a career break.
- **I-STEM** (Indian Science, Technology and Engineering facilities Map) = a **national** web portal for sharing R&D facilities (PM-STIAC mission) – **not international**.
> ⚠ Trap: senior women + interdisciplinary = **WIDUSHI**; GATI targets institutions, not individuals; SERB-POWER is SERB, not DST.

=== Fellowships and Brain-gain Schemes
| Scheme | Awarding body | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **VAJRA** (Visiting Advanced Joint Research Faculty) | SERB (now ANRF) | Overseas scientists incl. NRIs/OCIs, short spells in India |
| **Ramanujan Fellowship** | SERB, now ANRF | Indian scientists abroad who return |
| Ramalingaswami Re-entry | **DBT** (not DST) | Life-science/biotech returnees only |
| J.C. Bose Fellowship (now JC Bose Grant) | SERB/ANRF | Active senior scientists |
| Swarnajayanti | DST, 1997 (50 years of Independence) | Young scientists with a proven record |
| PMRF | Ministry of Education (2018) | PhD scholars in IITs, IISc, IISERs |
- **Brain drain → brain gain:** **VAJRA, Ramanujan Fellowship and INSPIRE** (Group 1); the ASEAN-India Research Training Fellowship is for ASEAN researchers coming to India – not a brain-gain scheme.
- ANRF schemes: PMECRG (PM Early Career Research Grant), Prime Minister Professorship, National Science Chair, N-PDF.
> ⚠ Trap: PMRF is Ministry of Education and not a career-break scheme; Ramalingaswami and BioCARe are the only DBT schemes here.

=== Atal Innovation Mission (AIM)
- **AIM** = flagship of **NITI Aayog**, set up **2016** (announced Budget 2015) to promote innovation and entrepreneurship across the country – not DST, Labour or Skill Development (UPSC 2019; APPSC Polytechnic Lecturers 2018).
| Component | Focus |
|---|---|
| Atal Tinkering Labs (ATLs) | Schools, **grades 6–12**: IoT, 3D printing, robotics; **10,000 ATLs** in 700+ districts |
| Atal Incubation Centres (AICs) | **Start-ups** in universities/institutions |
| Atal Community Innovation Centres (ACICs) | **Rural and under-served** areas |
| ARISE | **MSMEs** |
| Atal New India Challenges | Product innovation for national challenges |
- **AIM 2.0 (2024):** **₹2,750 crore** till **March 2028**; **LIPI** – vernacular innovation centres in the 22 scheduled languages.
- **Budget 2026-27:** ATL funding **₹500 crore → ₹3,200 crore**. **Jun 2026:** **Tripura** first State Innovation Mission under AIM 2.0; **India-France ATL Bridge** launched at **Nice**.
> ⚠ Trap: "Sardar Patel / Mahatma Innovation Mission" do not exist; Kaushal Navin Lakshay is a distractor.
~~ Sources: PMF S&T Part 1 p.41 (NITI Aayog – AIM); app notes 5:7 sub 7

=== DST Facility and Start-up Programmes – SATHI, FIST, NIDHI
| Scheme | Full form | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **SATHI** | **Sophisticated Analytical & Technical Help Institutes** | Shared, professionally run instrument centres – tackle **accessibility, maintenance, redundancy and duplication** of costly equipment and foster **collaboration across institutions and disciplines** |
| FIST | Fund for Improvement of S&T Infrastructure | Upgrade S&T infrastructure in universities |
| PURSE | Promotion of University Research and Scientific Excellence | University-only research support (2009) |
| SAIF | Sophisticated Analytical Instrument Facilities | Access to costly analytical instruments |
- **SATHI statement-conclusion (FRO 2023):** both conclusions (access/duplication; collaboration) follow → **both i and ii**.
- **NIDHI** (**National Initiative for Developing and Harnessing Innovations**, DST, 2016): covers the chain from scouting to scaling – **PRAYAS**, Entrepreneur-in-Residence, Seed Support System, Technology Business Incubators, Accelerators, Centres of Excellence; a NIDHI **research park** at **IIT Gandhinagar** (FRO 2022).
| Other start-up support | Body | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| TIDE 2.0 | MeitY, 2019 | ICT start-ups via incubators |
| Fund of Funds for Startups | SIDBI | ₹10,000 crore |
| Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups | NCGTC | NOT a NIDHI component |
- **RDI Scheme** (Cabinet 1 July 2025): **₹1 lakh crore over six years** for private-sector research, development and innovation.

=== Andhra Pradesh Innovation Ecosystem
- **Andhra Pradesh Innovation Society (APIS):** set up **2015**; nodal agency for start-ups under the **ITE&C Department**, headed by a CEO; began at **Sunrise Towers, Innovation Valley, Rushikonda, Visakhapatnam** (exam answer for "headquarters"); its office is now at **Mangalagiri** (Amaravati region).
- AP was the **first State** with an **Innovation and Startup Policy (2014-2020)**; AP has **590 ATLs** (2024-25).
- **Ratan Tata Innovation Hub (RTIH):** set up by APIS as a Section-8 company; inaugurated **20 Aug 2025**; central hub at **Mangalagiri** with spokes at Visakhapatnam, Rajahmundry, Vijayawada-Guntur, Tirupati, Anantapur.
- **IIDT (International Institute of Digital Technologies):** **Tirupati**.
> ⚠ Outdated angle: "APIS HQ = Visakhapatnam" is the old answer; current office Mangalagiri.
