=== Gravitational Waves, LIGO-India and Mega-Science Projects
# Gravitational waves
- Predicted by **Einstein (1916)**; ripples in space-time from **merging black holes**, neutron-star collisions.
- **LIGO** = **Laser Interferometer Gravitational-Wave Observatory** (USA); first detection **14 September 2015** (announced 11 Feb 2016); **Nobel 2017**: Weiss, Barish, Thorne.
- **Second detection** announced **June 2016**: two **black holes** merging ~**1.4 billion light-years** away (not white dwarfs).
- **eLISA** (evolved Laser Interferometer Space Antenna) = space mission to **detect gravitational waves** (not neutrinos).
- Black-hole merger observations (UPSC 2019) = **gravitational waves detected**, not Higgs bosons.
# LIGO-India
- At **Aundh, Hingoli**, Maharashtra; **DAE + DST** with US **NSF**.
- Lead institutions: **IPR Gandhinagar, IUCAA Pune, RRCAT Indore** (not IIST or "Kepler's Observatory").
- Consortium = **IndIGO** (Indian Initiative in Gravitational-wave Observations) – the APPSC G2 2017 key said "IndLIGO", which is not the name.

=== ICAR – Origin, Structure, Heads and News
- **ICAR** = **Indian Council of Agricultural Research**; set up **16 July 1929** (ICAR Day = **16 July**) as a **society** (Societies Registration Act 1860) on the **Royal Commission on Agriculture** report; HQ New Delhi.
- Under **DARE** (Dec 1973), Ministry of Agriculture; **113 institutes** (72 research institutes).
- **Director General** = Secretary DARE: **Dr S. Ayyappan** (2010-16, the answer in 2015) → **Dr Mangi Lal Jat** (as of 2026).
- **KVKs**: district level, **100% centrally funded**, under **11 ATARIs**; first KVK **Puducherry, 1974** (Mohan Singh Mehta committee, 1973). Sep 2026: skill centres in all KVKs by Dec 2028.
# In the news
- **13 Dec 2019**: ICAR–**NABARD** MoU (sustainable agriculture, climate-resilient farming).
- **Sardar Patel Outstanding ICAR Institution Award 2021** (large institute): **ICAR-NAARM, Hyderabad**.
- **562** high-yielding field-crop varieties released 2019-2021 (APPSC AE 2022 key).
- **Lumpi-ProVacInd**: lumpy skin disease vaccine developed by **two ICAR institutes**.

=== DAE, AEC and the Department of Space
# Atomic energy
- **Atomic Energy Commission: 1948** (Atomic Energy Act 1948), chaired by Secretary DAE; **DAE: 1954** by Presidential Order; both DAE and DoS are under the **Prime Minister**.
- **AERB** (1983) = **nuclear and radiation safety regulator** (BARC = research, NPCIL = power plants, TIFR = research).
| Unit (all under DAE) | Function |
|---|---|
| BARC, Trombay | Began 1954 as AEET, renamed 1967 after Homi Bhabha |
| **IGCAR, Kalpakkam** | **Fast breeder reactor** R&D |
| NPCIL | PSU (1987); builds and runs nuclear plants (23 reactors, 7,480 MW) |
| TIFR (1945, Bombay) | Start of organised nuclear research; NCRA-TIFR runs GMRT near Pune |
| **Heavy Water Board** (Mumbai) | **Heavy water** – not AMD |
| **IREL** (Mumbai) | Beach-sand minerals, rare earths, **zircon** |
| **AMD** (Hyderabad) | Atomic minerals **exploration** |
| UCIL | Uranium mining |
| HBNI | DAE deemed university (NIRF 2025: 7th research, 20th overall) |
- Research reactors: **APSARA** Asia's first (1956), **Dhruva** largest, **KAMINI** only U-233-fuelled.
- DAE products: Bhabhatron (cobalt therapy), Nisargruna (biogas), AKRUTI (rural incubator), TAG groundnut varieties.
# Space and defence
- **ISRO** 15 Aug 1969 (INCOSPAR 1962, Vikram Sarabhai); **Space Commission + DoS 1972**; **ISRO HQ and DoS secretariat: Antariksh Bhavan, Bengaluru**; Chairman V. Narayanan (2026).
- Arms: Antrix (Sep 1992), **NSIL (2019)**, **IN-SPACe (2020, Ahmedabad)**; NRSC Hyderabad. LVM3-M6 (24 Dec 2025): BlueBird Block-2, heaviest payload from India.
- **DRDO** (1958), DRDO Bhawan, New Delhi, under **DDR&D**; Chairman Samir V. Kamat.
- Mandates: ISRO – space for development; DRDO – defence systems; DAE – nuclear power, radioisotopes; ICAR – agriculture; ICMR – biomedical research. ICMR under DHR, CSIR under DSIR.
- Other HQs: Indian Bureau of Mines Nagpur; CIMFR Dhanbad; NATMO Kolkata; **MIDHANI Hyderabad**.
