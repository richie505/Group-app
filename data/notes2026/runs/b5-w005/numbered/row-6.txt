## SUB 2 | Vigyan Prasar, Outreach Bodies and the National Innovation Foundation
[2:0] # Science communication
[2:1] - **Vigyan Prasar**: autonomous body of **DST**, **1989**, New Delhi (not HRD).
[2:2] - **DD Science** (one-hour slot on DD National) + **India Science** (Vigyan Prasar's internet/OTT channel, not NCSM) – launched together **15 January 2019**.
[2:3] - **Vigyan Dhara**: DST umbrella scheme merging 3 schemes – capacity building; R&D; innovation and technology deployment (Aug 2024).

[2:8] - India's **first dinosaurium**: **B.M. Birla Science Centre, Hyderabad**.
[2:9] # National Innovation Foundation
[2:10] - Grew from the **Honey Bee Network** (Prof. **Anil K. Gupta**, SRISTI, IIM Ahmedabad); **NIF-India, March 2000**: **autonomous body of DST**, **Gandhinagar**.
[2:11] - Mandate: scout, support, spread **grassroots innovations** and traditional knowledge – **not** advanced research with foreign institutes (UPSC 2015: "a only").
[2:12] - IGNITE, Biennial Grassroots Innovation Awards, **Festival of Innovation (FOIN)** at **Rashtrapati Bhavan**.
