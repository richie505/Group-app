## SUB 0 | Vikram Sarabhai and the Space Pioneers
[0:0] # Vikram Sarabhai (1919-1971)
[0:1] - **Title:** **Father of the Indian Space Programme** (Jharkhand PCS 2021; IAS 2007 match).
[0:2] - **Institutions:** **PRL Ahmedabad (1947)**; **INCOSPAR (1962)** → **ISRO (1969)**; **TERLS, Thumba (1962)**; **Community Science Centre (1965)**.
[0:3] - **Posts:** **first ISRO Chairman** till his death (**1971**); **AEC Chairman 1966** (after Bhabha).
[0:4] - **Awards:** Bhatnagar 1962, Padma Bhushan 1966, **Padma Vibhushan (posthumous) 1972**.
[0:5] - **Named after him:** **Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre (VSSC)** = TERLS renamed, **Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala** (asked ×2); Chandrayaan-2 lander **"Vikram"**; **COSPAR Vikram Sarabhai Medal** (1990, COSPAR + ISRO, space scientists of **developing countries**).
[0:6] - **2026:** **Annapurni Subramaniam** won the COSPAR Sarabhai Medal at the 46th COSPAR Assembly, Florence – **first Indian woman**, fourth Indian (she had the RVP Vigyan Shri 2024).

## SUB 1 | A.P.J. Abdul Kalam
[1:0] - **Title:** **"Missile Man of India"**; **11th President (2002-07)**.
[1:1] - **Space:** project director of **SLV-3**, which put **Rohini** in orbit (**1980**).
[1:2] - **Missiles:** chief of **IGMDP (1983)** – Agni, Prithvi, Akash, Nag, Trishul.
[1:3] - **Nuclear:** **Pokhran-II (1998)** with Anil Kakodkar. **Bharat Ratna 1997**.
[1:4] - **Fighter flight:** **Sukhoi-30 MKI**, **8 June 2006**, Pune (co-pilot Wg Cdr Ajay Rathore) – first Indian Head of State in a fighter. **Pratibha Patil** flew a Sukhoi-30 on 25 Nov 2009 – first Indian woman in a fighter.
[1:5] - **Ideas and books:** **PURA** – Providing Urban Amenities in Rural Areas (APPSC G2 Mains 2017); **Wings of Fire (1999**, with Arun Tiwari), **Ignited Minds**, **Indomitable Spirit**.
