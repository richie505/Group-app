## SUB 0 | Devolution Index – Measuring Devolution to Panchayats
[0:0] # The report
[0:1] - **Devolution Index / Devolution Report** ("**Status of Devolution to Panchayats in States**"): **commissioned by the Ministry of Panchayati Raj (Central Government)** to assess the States' **policy and legislative framework and its outcomes** for Panchayati Raj – not a "PR Dashboard" or a progress report.
[0:2] - It measures the **actual devolution of functions, funds and functionaries** (devolution **in policy and in practice**), not merely expected devolution or funds alone.
[0:3] - **Who prepared it:** **IIPA** (earlier editions and the **2024** edition), but the **2014-15 and 2015-16 reports were entrusted to the Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS)**, Mumbai – not NIRD or NCAER.
[0:4] # TISS 2015-16 index design (score out of 100)
[0:5] TABLE head: Dimension | Weight | Components
  [0:5.0] Operational core of decentralisation | 90% | Functions 10, Functionaries 15, Finances 50, Autonomy 15
  [0:5.1] Support systems for devolution / decentralisation | 10% | Capacity building 2, constitutional mechanisms (SFC, DPC) 5, accountability and transparency 3
[0:6] # Awards
[0:7] - **Incremental Devolution Index award 2015-16:** **Andhra Pradesh, Sikkim and Odisha** were the top performers (short-term progress 2012-15) – so "**all of the given options**".
[0:8] - Presented by the **Prime Minister** on **National Panchayati Raj Day, 24 April 2016** (Jamshedpur) – AP's award in **April 2016** was the **Incremental Devolution Index Award**, not a Best/Swachh/ODF award.
[0:9] # 2024 Index (released Feb 2025, MoPR with IIPA, 6 dimensions)
[0:10] - **Six dimensions:** Framework, Functions, Finances, Functionaries, **Capacity Enhancement**, **Accountability**.
[0:11] TABLE head: 2024 Index | Top / score
  [0:11.0] Overall | Karnataka 1st, then Kerala, Tamil Nadu
  [0:11.1] Functions / Finances / Functionaries | Tamil Nadu / Karnataka / Gujarat
  [0:11.2] AP | 54.43, medium band (Telangana 55.1)

## SUB 2 | The 3Fs – Functions, Funds, Functionaries
[2:0] TABLE head: F | Meaning | Reality
  [2:0.0] Functions | Eleventh Schedule 29 (Panchayats), Twelfth Schedule 18 (Municipalities) | Devolved on paper
  [2:0.1] Funds | Untied money matching functions | Tied to schemes
  [2:0.2] Functionaries | Staff answerable to the local body | Report to State line departments
[2:1] - **Arts 243G / 243W say "may"** – the Schedules transfer nothing automatically; devolution is discretionary.
[2:2] - **Roadmap for Panchayati Raj / Laxmikanth:** weak 3Fs, **excessive bureaucratic control**, **tied funds**, **dependence on grants**, **reluctance to tax**, parallel bodies → PRIs are **implementing agencies, not planning units**.
[2:3] ~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. Ch39 (src/polity/laxmikanth-indian-polity-02.txt) p.112-113; app notes 2:61 sub 0
## SUB 3 | Funds Gap and the 16th FC Conditions
[3:0] - **Property tax** = main ULB own revenue, **under-assessed and under-collected**.
[3:1] - ULB own revenue ≈ **0.3% of GDP**; cities ≈ **2/3 of GDP**.
[3:2] - **16th FC:** urban share **36% → 45%**; grants **80% basic, 20% performance**.
[3:3] - **Conditions:** **properly constituted local bodies**, **published audited accounts**, **timely SFCs** – so delayed polls put grants at risk; **SFC reports are often ignored** by States.
[3:4] ~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. Ch39 (src/polity/laxmikanth-indian-polity-02.txt) p.112; PMF Polity Part 3 (src/polity/pmf-polity-03.txt) p.40; app notes 2:61 sub 2
