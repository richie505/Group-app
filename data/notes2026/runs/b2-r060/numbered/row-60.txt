## SUB 0 | State Finance Commission – Article 243I
[0:0] # The body
[0:1] - **Art. 243I (Panchayats) / 243Y (Municipalities):** the **Governor** constitutes a **Finance Commission** within one year of the 73rd Amendment (1992) and then **every five years** to **review the financial position of the Panchayats** – not the President, Parliament, the Finance Ministry or the Chief Minister.
[0:2] - It is a **constitutional body** (Part IX, 73rd Amendment) – not informal, administrative or a "finance committee"/"advisory commission".
[0:3] - **243Y:** the **same SFC** also reviews the finances of **Municipalities**.
[0:4] # What it recommends to the Governor
[0:5] - **Principles governing** (a) the **distribution between the State and the Panchayats** of the net proceeds of State taxes, duties, tolls and fees, and their **allocation among Panchayats at all levels**; (b) the **determination of taxes, duties, tolls and fees that may be assigned to or appropriated by the Panchayats**; (c) **grants-in-aid** from the **Consolidated Fund of the State**.
[0:6] - Also **measures to improve the finances** of the Panchayats and any matter the **Governor** refers.
[0:7] - The **State Legislature** fixes its composition and members' qualifications; the **Governor lays the report, with an explanatory memorandum (action taken), before the State Legislature**.
[0:8] - So the SFC – not the Governor, the CM, the District Planning Committee or the State PR Ministry – is the body for **revenue distribution between the State and its local governments**.

  [0:9.0] 243G / 243W | Powers and functions – Eleventh Schedule (29) / Twelfth (18) – State "may" devolve

## SUB 1 | Fourteenth Finance Commission (2015-20) – Grants to Local Bodies
[1:0] # The design
[1:1] - **14th FC (Y.V. Reddy):** total local-body grant **₹2,87,436 crore** for 2015-20, of which **Panchayats ₹2,00,292 crore**, the rest to municipalities.
[1:2] - Grants only to **duly constituted** bodies and, among Panchayats, **only to Gram Panchayats** – the grant goes **directly to the GPs** (no share for block and district Panchayats; States do not decide a share for other tiers).
[1:3] - **Inter-State distribution of the grant:** **2011 population 90% : area 10%**.
[1:4] - **Within a State:** among GPs by the **latest SFC formula**; if none, **population 90 : area 10**.
[1:5] - **Basic : performance grant** – **Gram Panchayats 90 : 10**; **Municipalities 80 : 20**. Performance grant from **2016-17**, needs **audited accounts** and **increase in own revenue**.
[1:6] - **Undisbursed performance grant:** distributed on an **equitable basis among all the eligible Gram Panchayats** (not rolled over).
[1:7] - **Release:** States must pass the grant to the local bodies **within 15 days** of its credit to the State's account; for any delay the **State pays interest from its own funds** (the 15th FC later set **10 working days**).
[1:8] - **Attitude:** the Commission held that local bodies **need to be trusted and respected** as institutions of self-government, with minimal conditions.
[1:9] # Basic/performance grants and excluded areas – which FC
[1:10] - The **13th FC (2010-15)** first split local-body grants into a **general basic grant** and a **general performance grant**, and gave a **special area grant** to areas **outside Parts IX/IXA** (Fifth and **Sixth Schedule** areas).
[1:11] - The **14th FC gave no grant to Sixth Schedule / excluded areas** (Part IX does not apply; the Centre gave a one-time special assistance instead); the **15th FC** covered **all tiers** including **Fifth and Sixth Schedule areas and traditional bodies** – so the coverage **varies from Commission to Commission**.

## SUB 2 | Union Finance Commission and Local Bodies – Article 280(3)(bb) and (c)
[2:0] - **Art. 280(3)(bb)** (Panchayats, 73rd) and **(c)** (Municipalities, 74th), added in **1992**: the Union FC recommends **measures to augment the Consolidated Fund of a State** to supplement the resources of local bodies **on the basis of the SFC's recommendations**.
[2:1] - So the Union FC **does have a role** in financial help to local bodies – "A: the Union FC has no role" and "R: it cannot recommend assistance to Panchayats" are **false**.
[2:2] - **Local government** remains a **State subject** – **Entry 5, State List** – despite the 73rd/74th Amendments (true).
[2:3] - Union FC grants to Panchayats rest on **Art. 280** and are **routed through the States**; **Art. 270** = divisible pool of Union taxes.
[2:4] > Asked as: assertion-reason (UPPCS 2011: A false, R true; UPPCS 2015: SFC constitutional – A true, R false).

## SUB 3 | Panchayat Revenue – Own, Assigned and Shared (Andhra Pradesh)
[3:0] TABLE head: Source | Basis
  [3:0.0] Own taxes, duties, tolls, fees | Only if the State Legislature authorises (Art. 243H, "may")
  [3:0.1] Assigned taxes | State-levied/collected taxes handed over to Panchayats, on SFC advice
  [3:0.2] Shared taxes | Share of State taxes as per SFC
  [3:0.3] Grants-in-aid | State Consolidated Fund (243H / 243I)
  [3:0.4] Union FC grants | Art. 280, routed through the States
[3:1] - Panchayats have **no independent constitutional taxing power**.
[3:2] - **Property (house) tax** is a **local tax** and the **major own-source revenue** of Panchayats (APPSC GS 2018), as of ULBs.
[3:3] - **AP Panchayat Raj Act, 1994:** a GP may levy a **surcharge on the seigniorage fee** collected by the Government on materials (other than minerals and minor minerals) **quarried in the village**; **seigniorage fees** go to the **Gram Panchayat Fund**.
[3:4] - **Transfer duty** – a **surcharge on stamp duty** on sale/transfer of immovable property in the GP area (**1.5%** in AP) – is collected by the State and credited to the GP **quarterly**.
[3:5] - **Land cess and registration (stamp-duty) surcharge** are **collected by the State Government and the share is transferred to the Gram Panchayat**.
[3:6] > Asked as: seigniorage surcharge (flashcard); surcharge on stamp duty on property transactions; land cess and registration surcharge; property tax a local tax.

## SUB 5 | Audit of Local Bodies
[5:0] - **Art. 243J / 243Z:** State law provides for the **maintenance and audit of accounts** of Panchayats/Municipalities.
[5:1] - **AP State Audit Act, 1989:** the **Local Fund Audit Department** was renamed the **State Audit Department**; the **Director of State Audit** (Finance Dept) is the **primary (statutory) auditor** of PRIs and can levy a **surcharge** for losses (s.10).
[5:2] - **CAG** gives only **Technical Guidance and Support (TGS)** over local-body audit – recommended by the **11th Finance Commission**.
[5:3] > Asked as: primary auditor of PRIs (Director, State Audit – not the CAG).
