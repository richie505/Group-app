=== Devolution Index – Measuring Devolution to Panchayats
# The report
- **Devolution Index / Devolution Report** ("**Status of Devolution to Panchayats in States**"): **commissioned by the Ministry of Panchayati Raj (Central Government)** to assess the States' **policy and legislative framework and its outcomes** for Panchayati Raj – not a "PR Dashboard" or a progress report.
- It measures the **actual devolution of functions, funds and functionaries** (devolution **in policy and in practice**), not merely expected devolution or funds alone.
- **Who prepared it:** **IIPA** (earlier editions and the **2024** edition), but the **2014-15 and 2015-16 reports were entrusted to the Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS)**, Mumbai – not NIRD or NCAER.
# TISS 2015-16 index design (score out of 100)
| Dimension | Weight | Components |
|---|---|---|
| **Operational core of decentralisation** | **90%** | Functions 10, Functionaries 15, **Finances 50**, Autonomy 15 |
| **Support systems for devolution / decentralisation** | **10%** | Capacity building 2, constitutional mechanisms (SFC, DPC) 5, accountability and transparency 3 |
# Awards
- **Incremental Devolution Index award 2015-16:** **Andhra Pradesh, Sikkim and Odisha** were the top performers (short-term progress 2012-15) – so "**all of the given options**".
- Presented by the **Prime Minister** on **National Panchayati Raj Day, 24 April 2016** (Jamshedpur) – AP's award in **April 2016** was the **Incremental Devolution Index Award**, not a Best/Swachh/ODF award.

=== The 3Fs – Functions, Funds, Functionaries
| F | Meaning | Reality |
|---|---|---|
| Functions | **Eleventh Schedule 29** (Panchayats), **Twelfth Schedule 18** (Municipalities) | Devolved on paper |
| Funds | Untied money matching functions | **Tied to schemes** |
| Functionaries | Staff answerable to the local body | Report to **State line departments** |
- **Arts 243G / 243W say "may"** – the Schedules transfer nothing automatically; devolution is discretionary.
- **Roadmap for Panchayati Raj / Laxmikanth:** weak 3Fs, **excessive bureaucratic control**, **tied funds**, **dependence on grants**, **reluctance to tax**, parallel bodies → PRIs are **implementing agencies, not planning units**.
~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. Ch39 (src/polity/laxmikanth-indian-polity-02.txt) p.112-113; app notes 2:61 sub 0

=== Funds Gap and the 16th FC Conditions
- **Property tax** = main ULB own revenue, **under-assessed and under-collected**.
- ULB own revenue ≈ **0.3% of GDP**; cities ≈ **2/3 of GDP**.
- **16th FC:** urban share **36% → 45%**; grants **80% basic, 20% performance**.
- **Conditions:** **properly constituted local bodies**, **published audited accounts**, **timely SFCs** – so delayed polls put grants at risk; **SFC reports are often ignored** by States.
~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. Ch39 (src/polity/laxmikanth-indian-polity-02.txt) p.112; PMF Polity Part 3 (src/polity/pmf-polity-03.txt) p.40; app notes 2:61 sub 2

=== Parallel Bodies and Bureaucratic Control
| Body / control | Effect |
|---|---|
| DRDAs, user groups, SHGs, **MPLADS** / MLA LADS | Run schemes outside PRIs; legislators pick works |
| **Janmabhoomi committees (2014-19)**; **village/ward secretariats and volunteers (2019)** – AP | Parallel to GPs and wards |
| **Special Officers** (AP) | Run lapsed local bodies |
- **MPLADS:** MP recommends → **Implementing District Authority** executes; RTI applies; **eSAKSHI** portal.
- **Balwantrai Mehta Committee:** the **Collector chairs the Zilla Parishad**.
- DPC (243ZD) and MPC (243ZE) are **constitutional** planning bodies, not parallel bodies.
~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. Ch39 (src/polity/laxmikanth-indian-polity-02.txt) p.113; app notes 2:61 sub 3

=== Women Representatives – the Proxy Problem
- **"Sarpanch pati":** a male relative rules in her name; proxy behaviour **falls markedly in her second term**.
- **MoPR advisory committee (Sushil Kumar, 2025):** **exemplary penalties** in proven proxy cases.
- **Sashakt Panchayat-Netri Abhiyan** and **Model Women-Friendly GPs:** strengthen the **participation and leadership of elected women representatives**.
