=== NOTES 2:60 | Devolution of Powers and Finances to Local Levels – Finance Commission and State Finance Commission Transfers
## SUB 0 | Decentralisation – Key Terms
[60:0:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [60:0:0.0] Democratic decentralisation (Balwant Rai Mehta's term) | Power moves to elected local bodies
  [60:0:0.1] Deconcentration / administrative delegation | Work moves to field officers inside the bureaucracy
  [60:0:0.2] Vertical devolution | States' share of the divisible pool (Art. 270 taxes) – between Union and States
  [60:0:0.3] Horizontal devolution | Split of that share among States by formula
  [60:0:0.4] Vertical fiscal imbalance | Union has buoyant taxes; States spend more (health, education, agriculture, police)
[60:0:1] - Depth: **deconcentration → delegation → devolution** (most authority).
[60:0:2] - **Cesses and surcharges are not in the divisible pool**; pool ≈ **81% of gross tax revenue** (2025-26).
[60:0:3] # What the 73rd Amendment Made Mandatory
[60:0:4] - Three tiers, direct elections, 5-year term, **reservation**, **SEC (243K)**, **SFC (243I)**.
[60:0:5] The **29 Eleventh Schedule subjects are not mandatory** – Art. 243G says "**may**".
## SUB 1 | Constitutional Articles on Local Body Finance
[60:1:0] TABLE head: Article | Provision
  [60:1:0.0] 243G | Eleventh Schedule (29 subjects) – only a menu
  [60:1:0.1] 243H / 243X | Own taxes, duties, tolls, fees only if the State Legislature authorises; grants from State Consolidated Fund
  [60:1:0.2] 243I | SFC, formed by the Governor; reviews panchayat finances
  [60:1:0.3] 243Y | Same SFC reviews municipal finances
  [60:1:0.4] 243J / 243Z | State law provides for maintenance and audit of accounts
  [60:1:0.5] 243K | State Election Commission
  [60:1:0.6] 270 | Taxes forming the divisible pool
  [60:1:0.7] 280 | Union Finance Commission, formed by the President; FC grants routed through States
[60:1:1] - **Article 280(3)(bb) and (c)** (added in **1992** by the 73rd and 74th Amendments): Union FC suggests how to **augment the Consolidated Fund of a State** for **panchayats (bb)** and **municipalities (c)**, **based on SFC recommendations**.
## SUB 2 | State Finance Commission
[60:2:0] - Constituted by the **Governor** **every five years** (Art. 243I); reviews **finances** of panchayats and (243Y) municipalities.
[60:2:1] - Advises the **Governor** on sharing and **assignment** of State taxes and **grants-in-aid**; Governor lays it, with an **explanatory memorandum**, before the **State Legislature**.
[60:2:2] # AP State Finance Commissions

## SUB 3 | Sources of Panchayat Revenue
[60:3:0] TABLE head: Source | Basis
  [60:3:0.0] Own taxes, duties, tolls, fees | Only if State Legislature authorises (Art. 243H, "may")
  [60:3:0.1] Assigned taxes | State-levied taxes handed over to panchayats, on SFC advice
  [60:3:0.2] Shared taxes | Share of State taxes as per SFC
  [60:3:0.3] Grants-in-aid | State Consolidated Fund (243H / 243I)
  [60:3:0.4] Union FC grants | Art. 280, routed through States
  [60:3:0.5] Scheme funds | Centrally Sponsored Schemes
[60:3:1] - Panchayats have **no independent constitutional taxing power**.
[60:3:2] - **Major own-source revenue: property (house) tax** (APPSC GS 2018); same for ULBs.
[60:3:3] # AP – Assigned Revenues
[60:3:4] - **AP Panchayat Raj Act, 1994**: GP may add a **surcharge on seigniorage** on village-quarried materials (s. 60(4)); **seigniorage fees** go to the GP Fund.
[60:3:5] - **Transfer duty** (surcharge on stamp duty, s. 69) goes to the **local body**.

## SUB 6 | Horizontal Devolution – Criteria (%)
[60:6:0] TABLE head: Criterion | 13th | 14th | 15th | 16th
  [60:6:0.0] Income distance (13th: fiscal capacity distance) | 47.5 | 50 | 45 | 42.5
  [60:6:0.1] Population 1971 | 25 | 17.5 | – | –
  [60:6:0.2] Population 2011 | – | 10 | 15 | 17.5
  [60:6:0.3] Area | 10 | 15 | 15 | 10
  [60:6:0.4] Forest cover / forest and ecology | – | 7.5 | 10 | 10
  [60:6:0.5] Fiscal discipline | 17.5 | – | – | –
  [60:6:0.6] Demographic performance | – | – | 12.5 | 10
  [60:6:0.7] Tax and fiscal effort | – | – | 2.5 | –
  [60:6:0.8] Contribution to GDP (new) | – | – | – | 10
[60:6:1] - **Highest weight always: income distance.**
[60:6:2] - 15th FC's other three criteria: **demographic performance, forest and ecology, tax and fiscal effort**.
[60:6:3] # 16th FC Changes and AP
[60:6:4] - Income distance from the **average of the three largest States**.
[60:6:5] - Demographic performance = **population growth 1971-2011** (15th FC used change in TFR).
[60:6:6] - Forest includes **open forests**.
[60:6:7] - **Contribution to GDP** (square root of GSDP) **replaced tax effort** (not a 15th FC criterion); helped AP.
[60:6:8] - AP's **"prosperity paradox"**: above-average per capita GSDP hurts it under income distance.
[60:6:9] - **AP share 4.217%** (from 4.05%); all five southern States gained.
