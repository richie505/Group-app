## SUB 1 | Forms of Marriage – Monogamy, Polygamy, Levirate, Sororate
[1:0] # Forms by number of spouses
[1:1] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [1:1.0] Monogamy | one man – one woman (the most common form)
  [1:1.1] Polygamy | more than one spouse at a time: polygyny (one man, many wives) or polyandry (one woman, many husbands)
  [1:1.2] Sororal polygyny | a man marries several sisters
  [1:1.3] Fraternal (adelphic) polyandry | a woman's husbands are brothers (Toda, Khasa)
  [1:1.4] Group marriage | several men with several women
  [1:1.5] Serial monogamy | remarriage after death/divorce, one spouse at a time
[1:2] # Preferential widow/widower marriage
[1:3] TABLE head: Term | Telugu term in APPSC papers | Meaning
  [1:3.0] Levirate | Devara Nyayam ("divine justice") | a widow marries her deceased husband's (younger) brother (Gond, Munda)
  [1:3.1] Sororate | Bharya Bhagini Nyayam ("justice for spouses / wife's sister") | a widower marries his deceased wife's sister
[1:4] - **Levirate = husband's brother; Sororate = wife's sister** – both after the spouse's death.
[1:5] - **Toda (Nilgiris):** fraternal **polyandry → polygyny (polyandry with several wives) → monogamy** – the order asked by UPSC 1997.
[1:6] - Polyandry tends to arise where one man cannot support a family (extreme poverty).
[1:7] - **Legal position today:** polygamy and polygyny are prohibited for Hindus (HMA Sec. 11 – bigamous marriage void); monogamy prevails.

## SUB 2 | Endogamy, Exogamy, Hypergamy and Incest Taboo
[2:0] # Rules of mate selection
[2:1] TABLE head: Rule | Meaning
  [2:1.0] Endogamy (Telugu *antar-vivaham*, "intermarriage" in translated papers) | marriage within one's own caste, tribe, religion – keeps caste boundaries
  [2:1.1] Exogamy (*bahir-vivaham*, "extramarriage") | marriage outside a group: sapinda exogamy, sagotra exogamy, village exogamy
  [2:1.2] Hypergamy = Anuloma | higher-caste man + lower-caste woman (woman marries up) – tolerated
  [2:1.3] Hypogamy = Pratiloma | lower-caste man + higher-caste woman (woman marries down) – condemned
[2:2] - **Ghurye**: endogamy is the **defining feature of caste**.
[2:3] - **North India**: caste endogamy + gotra exogamy + **village exogamy**; daughters marry into distant villages.
[2:4] - **Incest taboo**: a social prohibition that **forbids sexual contact between certain relatives** (parents–children, siblings) – the basis of exogamy.
[2:5] - **Irawati Karve** analysed sapinda and gotra.
[2:6] # Sapinda and prohibited degrees (HMA)
[2:7] - **Sec. 3(f)** sapinda: **3 generations** on the mother's side, **5** on the father's.
[2:8] - **Sec. 5(iv)** bars prohibited degrees; **Sec. 5(v)** bars **sapinda** marriage – both unless **custom** allows.
[2:9] - **Delhi High Court (Jan 2024)** upheld **Sec. 5(v)** (sapinda bar).
[2:10] - **⚠ Trap:** anuloma ≠ pratiloma – in both terms the direction is the **woman's** marriage relative to her own caste.

## SUB 3 | Hindu Marriage – Aims, Manu's Eight Forms, Ancient Customs
[3:0] # Definitions and aims
[3:1] - **Malinowski**: marriage is a **contract for the production and maintenance of children** ("a covenant to bear and nurture children").
[3:2] - **Lowie**: a relatively permanent bond between permissible mates; **Leach**: a **bundle of rights** (legitimating offspring, access to the spouse, affinal ties, domestic rights).
[3:3] - Hindu marriage = a **sacrament** (homa, panigrahana, saptapadi); Muslim marriage = a **contract**.
[3:4] - **Aims (K.M. Kapadia)** in descending order: **Dharma** (religious duty) → **Praja** (progeny) → **Rati** (pleasure).
[3:5] # Manu Smriti – 8 forms (4 approved, 4 disapproved)
[3:6] TABLE head: Form | Meaning | Status
  [3:6.0] Brahma | daughter given to a learned, virtuous groom; no dowry or bride price | approved; highest
  [3:6.1] Daiva | daughter given to a priest officiating at a sacrifice | approved
  [3:6.2] Arsha | after symbolic gifts (cow and bull) from the groom | approved
  [3:6.3] Prajapatya | duty, family welfare, procreation | approved
  [3:6.4] Gandharva | love, mutual consent (Shakuntala–Dushyanta) | disapproved but conditionally accepted
  [3:6.5] Asura | purchase of the bride – groom pays bride price | disapproved
  [3:6.6] Rakshasa | forcible abduction; linked with Kshatriyas, seen in the epics | disapproved
  [3:6.7] Paisacha | deceit, intoxication, no consent | most condemned
[3:7] # Other customary unions
[3:8] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [3:8.0] Niyoga | a wife cohabits with a designated man to beget a child when her husband is impotent or dead
  [3:8.1] Sambandham | consensual, informal union of Namboothiris and Nairs under Kerala's matrilineal system
[3:9] - **Later Vedic** changes: child marriage began, **Swayamvara** declined, widow remarriage discouraged, same-gotra marriage banned.
[3:10] - **Satyashodhak marriage** (started by **Savitribai Phule**): no dowry, no Brahmin priest.

## SUB 4 | Divorce and Marriage Offences
[4:0] # Divorce
[4:1] - **Divorce** = the **legal dissolution of a marriage by a court**; **W.P. Scott**: "an institutionalised arrangement for terminating the marriage relationship and allowing each partner the right to remarry". Living apart is only separation.
[4:2] - **Desertion** (husband leaving the family to fend for itself) = **"the poor man's divorce"**.
[4:3] - First divorce laws: **Parsi Marriage and Divorce Act 1865**, **Indian Divorce Act 1869** (Christians) – not "Parsi Act 1869".
[4:4] - **HMA Sec. 13** grounds: adultery, cruelty, desertion, conversion, unsoundness of mind, renunciation, not heard of for **7 years**; **13B** mutual consent; **Sec. 11** bigamous marriage void; **Sec. 12** voidable marriages.
[4:5] - **Irretrievable breakdown** is not an HMA ground; **May 2023 (Shilpa Sailesh)**: the SC can dissolve such a marriage under **Art. 142(1)** even if one party opposes.
[4:6] - **Muslim law:** 1939 Act grounds – no maintenance for **2 years**, imprisonment of 7+ years, cruelty; 2019 Act gives subsistence allowance and custody of minor children.
[4:7] # Offences (IPC → BNS)
[4:8] - **IPC Chapter XX (Sec. 493–498)** = **offences relating to marriage** = **BNS 81–84**; bigamy IPC **494** = **BNS 82**.
[4:9] - **Assam Prohibition of Polygamy Bill 2025** (app notes): cognizable, non-bailable; up to **7 years'** RI.

## SUB 6 | Child Marriage – Causes, Data, Campaigns
[6:0] - **Causes:** **poverty**, **low education of girls**, **social customs and traditions**, dowry, safety of girls, the old belief in pre-puberty marriage – "status symbol" is not a cause.
[6:1] - More prevalent in **lower castes, poorer classes** and **joint families**.
[6:2] - **International Day of the Girl Child**: 11 October; first observed **2012**, theme **"Ending Child Marriage"**.
[6:3] TABLE head: Data | Fact
  [6:3.0] NCRB 2021 (PCMA cases) | Karnataka 273, Tamil Nadu 169, Assam 155 – the top three
  [6:3.1] NCRB 2023 | 6,038 cases (about six times 1,002 of 2022); Assam nearly 90%
  [6:3.2] NFHS-6 | 20.1% of women aged 20–24 married before 18 (app notes)
[6:4] - **PCM (Amendment) Bill 2025** (app notes): women's age to **21**; **Bal Vivah Mukt Bharat**: cut child marriage **10% by 2026**, eliminate by **2030**; AP adolescent-education programme in **9,675 schools**.
