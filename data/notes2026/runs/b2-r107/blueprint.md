## 4. Repeats and traps
- Levirate / Devara Nyayam asked 7 times; anuloma/pratiloma 5; consanguineal vs affinal 8; dominant caste = Srinivas 3; first synchronous census 1881 twice.
- Translated Telugu terms decide many items: "intermarriage" = endogamy (*antar-vivaham*), "extramarriage" = exogamy, "justice for spouses" = sororate, "patriarchal relationship" = amitate, "native of matula" = avunculocal.
- Printed keys found wrong: same "first inter-caste law" item keyed HMA 1955 once and SMA 1872 once (SMA 1872 right); "complementary filiation" item keyed consanguinity once; tribal exchange keyed jajmani twice (barter); Lakshadweep keyed matrilocal (duolocal); Rudolph keyed "professional mobilisation" (vertical, horizontal, differential); couvade keyed without the Khasi; Sarda Act keyed with the false "15 years" statement.
