## SUB 2 | Port-based Trade of Andhra Pradesh
[2:0] - **Visakhapatnam** = only major port of AP; non-major: **Krishnapatnam, Kakinada, Gangavaram**.
[2:1] - **Iron ore** from Visakhapatnam (outer harbour) is exported mainly to **Japan** (Australia is itself an iron-ore exporter).
[2:2] - Visakhapatnam Port handled a record **91.17 MMT in 2025-26** (82.62 MMT in 2024-25) – **4th** among major ports, **2nd on the east coast** (not 1st overall).
[2:3] - **AP coastline 1,053.07 km** (3rd after Gujarat and Tamil Nadu; old 974 km).
[2:4] - First English factory in coastal Andhra: **Machilipatnam, 1611**.
