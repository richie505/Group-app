=== Port-based Trade of Andhra Pradesh
- **Visakhapatnam** = only major port of AP; non-major: **Krishnapatnam, Kakinada, Gangavaram**.
- **Iron ore** from Visakhapatnam (outer harbour) is exported mainly to **Japan** (Australia is itself an iron-ore exporter).
- Visakhapatnam Port handled a record **91.17 MMT in 2025-26** (82.62 MMT in 2024-25) – **4th** among major ports, **2nd on the east coast** (not 1st overall).
- **AP coastline 1,053.07 km** (3rd after Gujarat and Tamil Nadu; old 974 km).
- First English factory in coastal Andhra: **Machilipatnam, 1611**.

=== SEZ Act 2005 and EOUs
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| SEZ policy | **2000**, under the EXIM Policy |
| Act | **2005** |
| Who sets up | **Centre, a State or any person**, jointly or alone; also FTWZ |
| Approval | State → **Board of Approval** |
| Status | Treated as **foreign territory** for trade and tariffs; single window |
| Baba Kalyani Committee (2018) | Rename SEZs "3Es" – **Employment and Economic Enclaves** |
- **Objectives:** economic activity, exports of goods and services, **promotion of domestic and foreign investment**, employment, infrastructure.
- SEZ exports ≈ **21%** of India's exports; **Kandla** = Asia's first EPZ (1965).
| SEZ | EOU |
|---|---|
| Demarcated enclave under an Act | **Single unit that may be located anywhere** under the FTP scheme (**1981**) |
| Developer + units | Unit only |

=== AP Trade Policies, Districts as Export Hubs, EPI and LEADS
| Policy | Target |
|---|---|
| Retail Trade Policy 2015-20 | **20,000** additional retail jobs |
| **APEX 2022-27** (Export Promotion Policy) | Leading export hub; **integrated export ecosystems from districts to ports** |
| Industrial Development Policy 4.0 (2024-29) | **Rs 30 lakh crore** investment, **US$ 40 bn exports**, **175+ industrial parks** |
| Maritime Policy 2024 | **NSHIP-AP Ltd** builds the Dugarajapatnam shipbuilding cluster – **25%** of national capacity by 2047 |
- **DEH** launched **2019** under the FTP; nodal agency **DGFT**; ODOP merged.
| DEH Phase-I district | Products |
|---|---|
| **Visakhapatnam** | Marine products, engineering, health services |
| **East Godavari** | Coir, cashew, nursery plants |
| **Guntur** | Chillies, turmeric, cotton yarn |
- **EPI** – NITI Aayog with the **Institute for Competitiveness** (first 2020). AP **9th in EPI 2021**, **5th among large States in EPI 2024** (topper **Maharashtra**); EPI 2024 weights: **Business Ecosystem 40%**, others 20% each.
- **LEADS:** AP **Fast Mover (2024)**; **Accelerator** among coastal States (2025).

=== Export Infrastructure – Corridors and Dry Port
- **VCIC** = India's **first coastal industrial corridor**, part of ECEC; nodes **Visakhapatnam, Srikalahasti-Yerpedu, Kopparthy**. **Orvakal** = node of **HBIC** (not VCIC/ECEC). CBIC: Krishnapatnam node.
- **Sri City dry port** – under **Inter-Ministerial Committee** review; CBIC norm: no new dry port within **200 km of a seaport** (100 km of an ICD).
