## Trends
- **APPSC asks this row as basic recall plus match-the-following.** Group 2 Mains 2025 had three matches (waste → method, method → function, method → pros/cons), and Forest Beat/Range papers repeat the landfill and 3R items.
- **UPSC items recur across papers:** pyrolysis/plasma gasification (2019), SWM Rules 2016 (2019), e-waste metals (2013), BPA (2021) and microbeads (2019) each appear two or three times through UPPCS and book reprints.
- **Swachh Survekshan rankings are a steady current-affairs angle:** Indore by year, the SS 2023 theme, and AP cities (SS 2020, SS 2022, SS 2025-26 awards Nov 2026).
- **Outdated or disputed items:** the SWM Rules 2016 have been replaced by the SWM Rules 2026 (four streams, from 1 Apr 2026), so three PYQs carry outdated lines. BMW "17 lakh t" is officially keyed but the MoEF figure is 0.17 MT (1.7 lakh t). EPR guidelines are keyed 1 July 2022, although they took effect on 16 Feb 2022. For "cleanest city 2024-25" Indore is keyed (Super Swachh League), although Ahmedabad was the cleanest big city. MFC (UPSC 2011) is re-keyed to (c).
