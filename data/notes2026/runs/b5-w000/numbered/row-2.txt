  [1:1.0] Scientific Policy Resolution (SPR) – first | 1958 | Nehru | "Foster, promote and sustain" science – pure, applied, educational; state-led institution building (national labs, IITs)

## SUB 2 | Objectives of Each Policy
[2:0] # STP 2003
[2:1] - **Launched:** **3 January 2003** (Indian Science Congress); research, innovation, technology transfer, more R&D investment.
[2:2] - **Objectives include all four** asked by UPPSC 2008: **optimal utilisation of existing physical and knowledge resources**, **development of innovative technologies**, **systems and technologies for mitigation and management of natural hazards**, and **management of intellectual property** → "All the four".
[2:3] - **GERD target:** **2% of GDP by the end of the Tenth Plan**.
[2:4] # STI Policy 2013
[2:5] - India among the **top five** scientific powers by 2020; publications share **3.5% → over 7%**; about **10 high-impact sectors** for direct STI intervention.
[2:6] - **"Small idea-small money"**, **Risky Idea Fund**, **Fund for Innovations for Social Inclusion**; GERD to **2%** with private spending matching public.
[2:7] # Draft STIP 2020
[2:8] - Vision: among the **top three** scientific superpowers in the coming decade; **double FTE researchers, GERD and private GERD share every 5 years**.
[2:9] - Proposals: **One Nation, One Subscription**, **STI Development Bank** (corpus for long-term strategic areas), **RIEF**, National STI Observatory, open science (INDSTA archive), National Research Foundation with private/CSR (hybrid) funding.
[2:10] - **⚠ Trap:** **Atal Innovation Mission** is NOT a STIP 2020 outcome – set up by **NITI Aayog in 2016**. Top five by 2020 = STI 2013 (not STP 2003).
[2:11] - **ONOS** approved Nov 2024, about **Rs 6,000 crore** (not Rs 60,000 crore).
[2:12] ~~ Sources: NextGen S&T Group 2 Part 1 p.3-6; PMF S&T Part 1 p.42-43; app notes 5:2 (sub 1)
## SUB 3 | Innovation and Research Concepts
[3:0] - **Adopters of an innovation (Rogers' diffusion of innovations):** **Innovators → Early Adopters → Early Majority → Late Majority → Laggards** (APPSC G2).
[3:1] - **Validity of a study:** it measures what it claims to measure; **external validity** = the results can be **generalised** beyond the sample (APPSC G2 answer: "results can be generalised").
[3:2] - **Decade of Innovation: 2010-2020**; vehicle = **National Innovation Council** under **Sam Pitroda**; inclusive innovation.

## SUB 6 | GERD Targets and Advisory Bodies
[6:0] TABLE head: Policy | GERD target
