## SUB 0 | Price Terms, Economic Cost and Food Subsidy
[0:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [0:0.0] MSP | Floor price announced before sowing (CACP → CCEA)
  [0:0.1] Procurement price | Price at which the government buys grain for PDS and buffer stocks (announced separately at harvest from 1966-67; since 1968-69 MSP itself is the effective procurement price)
  [0:0.2] Central Issue Price (CIP) | Price at which the Centre supplies grain to States for distribution through fair price / ration shops
  [0:0.3] FPS retail price | Fixed by State Governments (States' role in PDS is operational)
  [0:0.4] Ceiling price | Maximum price that may be charged – protects consumers
  [0:0.5] Economic cost | MSP + bonus + procurement incidentals + distribution cost (UPSC 2019)
  [0:0.6] Food subsidy | Economic cost minus CIP
  [0:0.7] Hidden subsidy | FCI carrying cost financed by borrowing (NSSF loans) instead of budget releases
[0:1] - Pooled grain cost (MSP + bonus) ≈ **two-thirds** of economic cost. Subsidy components: **FCI subsidy + subsidy to DCP States**; **NSSF** borrowing ended and subsidy brought on budget in **Budget 2021-22**.
[0:2] - In the UPSC 2004 statements only **(c)** is right: wheat and rice are issued at **uniform CIPs**; procurement is **open-ended** (not target-based) and MSP is announced for pulses and oilseeds too, **not only cereals**.
[0:3] - **PDS issue prices were raised** to **lessen the subsidy burden**.

## SUB 1 | Food Management and the FCI
[1:0] - **Objectives of PDS:** supply a **minimum quantity of grain at affordable prices to low-income groups**, equitable distribution, **control of price fluctuations**.
[1:1] - Procurement objectives: assure MSP, **free grain to the weak under PMGKAY**, market intervention. **Not objectives:** **controlling the production of foodgrains** (SSC 2008), **export of foodgrains**.
[1:2] TABLE head: FCI | Fact
  [1:2.0] Set up | 14 January 1965
  [1:2.1] Act | Food Corporations Act, 1964
  [1:2.2] Recommended by | Food Grains Prices Committee (L.K. Jha, 1964)
  [1:2.3] HQ | New Delhi
  [1:2.4] Role | Nodal agency for price-support purchase of wheat and paddy, with State agencies
[1:3] - **FCI missions:** procurement at MSP; storage, movement and distribution; operational and buffer stocks; grain at reasonable prices to weaker sections through TPDS. **Not a mission: regulation of markets in agricultural products** – that is done under **State APMC Acts** (APPSC 2018).
[1:4] - **OSM = Online Storage Management**, rolled out from **1 April 2022** for storage, movement and distribution under TPDS. **Anna Darpan** modernises **FCI's** supply chain (Dept of Food and PD, not Agriculture).
[1:5] - **OMSS (Domestic):** FCI sells wheat and rice from the Central Pool in the **open market at predetermined prices** to moderate prices.
[1:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 20 p.9; NextGen Indian Economy P1 - APPSC 01 p.37, 04 p.45, p.48; app notes 3:58 sub 0-2
## SUB 2 | Procurement, Buffer Stocks and Storage
[2:0] TABLE head: System | Feature
  [2:0.0] Centralised | FCI (directly or via State agencies) buys for the Central Pool
  [2:0.1] DCP (1997, with TPDS) | Designated States procure, store and distribute grain for TPDS; Centre pays their economic cost minus CIP
  [2:0.2] Open-ended | Wheat and paddy – all produce meeting standards at MSP
  [2:0.3] Target-based | PSS pulses and oilseeds – cap 25% of production
[2:1] - **Buffer stocks** (wheat and rice, bought from farmers at procurement prices) are released through PDS and open-market sales to **check price rise** and meet shortages.
[2:2] - **Buffer norms** fixed by **CCEA** quarterly – as on **1 April, 1 July, 1 October, 1 January**; 1 April norm **210.40 LMT** (rice 135.80, wheat 74.60).
[2:3] - **Strategic (food security) reserve:** **30 LMT wheat** (from 1 July 2008) and **20 LMT rice** (from 1 January 2009), included in the norms.
[2:4] - **604.02 LMT** in the Central Pool on 1 April 2026 – nearly **three times** the norm → **higher carrying costs**.
[2:5] - **CAP = Cover and Plinth** (grain on raised plinths under polythene covers); **steel silos under PPP**; **Grain Storage Plan** (cooperative sector): PACS chosen by **District Cooperative Development Committees**, implemented by **NCDC**.
[2:6] - **IGMRI** (Indian Grain Storage Management and Research Institute): **Hapur (UP)**; trains SAARC officials; field stations Hyderabad and Ludhiana.

## SUB 3 | PDS Evolution – RPDS, TPDS, AAY, NFSA, PMGKAY
[3:0] TABLE head: Year | Step
  [3:0.0] 1940s | Rationing – Bengal famine; revived in the 1960s shortages
  [3:0.1] June 1992 | RPDS – backward, hilly, drought-prone areas
  [3:0.2] June 1997 | TPDS – BPL and APL entitlements
  [3:0.3] 25 December 2000 | Antyodaya Anna Yojana – 35 kg per family per month to the poorest
  [3:0.4] 2013 | NFSA – legal entitlement for about 800 million (75% rural, 50% urban); first promulgated as an ordinance (July 2013), Act in September 2013
  [3:0.5] 1 Jan 2023 | Free grain under PMGKAY to 80 crore+; extended to 31 Dec 2028
[3:1] - Sequence: **PDS → RPDS → TPDS → AAY**. PMGKAY motto: "**One Nation-One Price-One Ration**".
[3:2] - **Fortified rice** (iron, **vitamin B12, folic acid**) through PDS to Dec 2028.
[3:3] - Of rights-based laws, only **NFSA** began as an ordinance; RTE, MGNREGA and RTI were passed directly as Acts.
[3:4] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy P1 - APPSC 03 p.9; Ramesh Singh Indian Economy part 04 p.190; app notes 3:58 sub 6
## SUB 4 | PDS Reforms – ONORC, Leakage and the Shanta Kumar Committee
[4:0] TABLE head: Reform | Period / content
  [4:0.0] End-to-End Computerisation of TPDS | Oct 2012 – Mar 2020: digitised cards, online allocation, ePoS
  [4:0.1] IM-PDS | From April 2018; national portability (ONORC)
  [4:0.2] SMART-PDS | Apr 2023 – Mar 2026; part of SARTHAK-PDS
  [4:0.3] SARTHAK-PDS | 2026-2031 (CCEA, 28 May 2026); end-to-end tracking from procurement to FPS, real-time stock monitoring
[4:1] - **ONORC:** draw ration **away from where the card was issued** via ePoS, Aadhaar/biometric authentication; family draws the balance at home. App: **Mera Ration**; rollout target **1 June 2020**; now **all 36 States/UTs**.
[4:2] - **DBT in food:** Chandigarh, **Puducherry**, urban Dadra and Nagar Haveli (2015). **Delhi**: first **e-ration card** service (March 2015).
[4:3] - Anti-leakage measures: e-PoS, ONORC, biometrics (not "ending procurement").
[4:4] - **Coverage vs leakage:** States with **wider coverage had lower leakage** → **the lower the coverage, the higher the leakage**.
[4:5] - **Shanta Kumar HLC (2014-15):** PDS leakage **46%** (NSS 2011-12); only ~6% farmers sell at MSP; expand DCP; hand procurement in surplus States (Punjab, Haryana, AP) to States so FCI focuses on **eastern States**; cut NFSA coverage **67% → 40%** (not 50%); cash transfers; private storage.
