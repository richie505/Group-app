## SUB 2 | PMKSY and Watershed Development
[2:0] # PMKSY
[2:1] - Launched **1 July 2015**; umbrella irrigation scheme; motto: **"Har Khet Ko Pani"** and **"More crop per drop"** – raise **water-use efficiency**.
[2:2] - Components: **AIBP**, **Har Khet Ko Pani** (assured irrigation; CAD and water management), **Per Drop More Crop** (micro-irrigation; now under PM-RKVY), **Watershed Development**. **National Water Grid is NOT** a component.
[2:3] - Objectives: **convergence of investment** in irrigation at field level, **expand cultivable area under irrigation**, **improve on-farm water-use efficiency**; works: renovate water bodies, rainwater-harvesting structures, **recharge of aquifers**. **Not** an objective: protecting farmers against crop failure (that is crop insurance – PMFBY); "construction of wells" is the item keyed as incorrect.
[2:4] - **PMKSY planning:** District Irrigation Plans → State Irrigation Plans; **State Level Sanctioning Committee chaired by the Chief Secretary**.
[2:5] - **Micro Irrigation Fund**: corpus **Rs 5,000 crore** with **NABARD**.
[2:6] # Watershed
[2:7] - **Hariyali** (launched January 2003; guidelines from **1 April 2003**): watershed / rainwater-harvesting scheme = **water management**.
[2:8] - Hariyali, **IWDP** (Integrated Wasteland Development Programme, 1989-90 nationally; 100% central assistance) and NWDPRA merged into **IWMP** (2008); IWMP subsumed in PMKSY as **WDC-PMKSY** (2015); funding **90:10 → 60:40**; **WDC 2.0** adds springsheds.
[2:9] - **Neeranchal National Watershed Project**: **World Bank**; **nine States incl. AP and Telangana**.
[2:10] - **Watershed Development Fund:** NABARD, 1999-2000, corpus Rs 200 crore.
[2:11] - **Tarun Bharat Sangh**: **Rajasthan** NGO of **Dr Rajendra Singh** ("Waterman", johads).
[2:12] - **Four Waters Concept** (T. Hanumantha Rao): **rain water, surface water, ground water and soil moisture** – "river water" is not one of the four.
[2:13] ~~ Sources: PMF Environment 07 p.51 (Har Khet Ko Pani, Per Drop More Crop); AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.128; app notes 3:46 sub 3; general knowledge (IWDP, four waters)
## SUB 3 | Groundwater – CGWB, Aquifers and Atal Bhujal
[3:0] - **Aquifers:** clay holds water but its pores are tiny and poorly connected → **poor aquifer**; sand and gravel are good aquifers.
[3:1] - **CGWA** was constituted under **Section 3(3) of the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986**.
[3:2] - CGWB categories (stage of extraction): **Safe, Semi-critical, Critical, Over-exploited**, + Saline.
[3:3] - India stage: **60.63% (2025)**; high utilisation in **Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu**, Delhi; low in high-rainfall Odisha.
[3:4] - **Uttar Pradesh** has the largest **replenishable groundwater** resource (Ganga alluvium).
[3:5] - **GRACE** satellite studies: severe depletion in **north-west India** – Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan and **Delhi** (epicentre named in the APPSC 2019 key).
[3:6] - **Atal Bhujal Yojana**: **December 2019**; **World Bank**-aided **Central Sector**; **Ministry of Jal Shakti**; 7 States – Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, MP, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, UP; **AP NOT included**; **demand-side** community management via GP Water Security Plans.
[3:7] - **Piezometers** (digital water-level recorders with telemetry) give real-time groundwater levels (AP: 1,810 piezometers).
[3:8] ~~ Sources: PMF Environment 07 p.54 (CGWB mandate); AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.132 (piezometers); app notes 3:46 sub 4
## SUB 4 | PM-KUSUM and Micro-irrigation
[4:0] - **KUSUM = Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan** (2019, **MNRE**).
[4:1] TABLE head: PM-KUSUM | Content
  [4:1.0] A | 10,000 MW decentralised grid-connected solar plants (0.5-2 MW)
  [4:1.1] B | 14 lakh standalone solar pumps (up to 7.5 HP)
  [4:1.2] C | Solarisation of grid-connected pumps, incl. feeder-level
[4:2] - Objective: **remove farmers' dependence on diesel and kerosene** – an **agricultural solar pump** scheme; concern: may encourage **over-drawing of groundwater**.
[4:3] - **Drip irrigation:** water straight to the root zone; fertiliser through the same lines (fertigation).
[4:4] - **APMIP** (since **2003**): AP ranks **1st** in micro-irrigation; **Rayalaseema 64%** of area; AP has **converged MGNREGA with the AP Micro Irrigation Project**.
