## SUB 0 | Disinvestment – Meaning, Methods and Strategic Sales
[0:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [0:0.0] Disinvestment | Sale of government equity in PSEs – reduces equity; control may stay
  [0:0.1] Privatisation | Ownership + management control to the private sector
  [0:0.2] Strategic sale | A block of shares (usually 50% or more) with transfer of management control to a strategic partner
  [0:0.3] Minority disinvestment | Govt keeps >51% and control
  [0:0.4] Liberalisation | Removing licences, quotas, controls (no sale)
  [0:0.5] Complete privatisation | 100% sold – most comprehensive mode
[0:1] - **Methods:** cross-holding, **warehousing**, minority sale, **OFS**, IPO/FPO, **CPSE ETF**, Bharat 22, buyback, **strategic sale** – methods of **disinvestment** (SLR, CRR, repo are NOT).
[0:2] - **Policy recast (1999-2000):** shift from token minority sales to **strategic sale** – **majority holding (51%+) sold to a strategic private partner with change in management**.
[0:3] - **Rationale:** poor PSU returns, **fiscal deficit**, efficiency, listing; disinvestment is an **integral part of liberalisation**; it reduces – never raises – government equity.
[0:4] - **Why disinvest (UPSC 2011):** proceeds are **not** mainly for repaying external debt, and government does **not** give up control in every CPSE – so neither statement holds.
[0:5] - **A-R (UPPCS 2000):** disinvestment is true; "PSUs have not generated enough employment" is **false** as a reason.
[0:6] - **Criticism:** **"selling the family silver"** – proceeds bridge the **fiscal deficit** instead of building assets; **undervaluation**; lost dividends.

## SUB 1 | Competition Act, 2002 and the CCI
[1:0] - **Competition Act, 2002:** recommended by the **S.V.S. Raghavan Committee (2000)**; replaced the MRTP Act; **commenced 31 March 2003**.
[1:1] - **CCI** established **14 October 2003**; Chairperson + **2-6 members**; **Ministry of Corporate Affairs**.
[1:2] - Fully operational: anti-competitive provisions from **20 May 2009**; merger (combination) control from **June 2011**.
[1:3] TABLE head: Chairperson | Term
  [1:3.0] Dhanendra Kumar (first) | 2009 – June 2011
  [1:3.1] Ashok Chawla | Oct 2011 – Jan 2016
  [1:3.2] Ravneet Kaur | from May 2023
[1:4] - **Key sections of the Act:**
[1:5] TABLE head: Section | Covers
  [1:5.0] 3 | Anti-competitive agreements – cartels, bid-rigging, price-fixing
  [1:5.1] 4 | Abuse of dominance
  [1:5.2] 5-6 | Combinations – M&A above thresholds; deemed approved if CCI misses the deadline
[1:6] - **NCLAT** hears appeals against CCI orders (since 26 May 2017).
[1:7] - Cases: **Jet Airways, IndiGo, SpiceJet** fined (Nov 2015) for cartelisation in the cargo **fuel surcharge**; **Google ₹1,337 crore (2022)**; **Meta/WhatsApp ₹213.14 crore (2024)**.
[1:8] - **2024:** CCI engaged **Management Development Institute Society** for a market study on **AI and competition**.
[1:9] - **M&A (UPPCS 2004 A-R):** the M&A wave after 1991 was **explained by dilution of MRTP provisions**.

[3:0] TABLE head: Structure | Feature

  [3:0.1] Monopolistic competition | Many sellers, differentiated but close-substitute products
  [3:0.2] Oligopoly | A few large firms

## SUB 6 | New PSE Policy 2021, Monetisation and Restructuring
[6:0] - **New PSE Policy for Atmanirbhar Bharat (2021):** **minimise the Government's presence** in PSEs; **4 strategic sectors** (incl. banking, insurance, financial services) with **1-4 CPSEs** each; non-strategic → privatise, merge or close.
[6:1] - ES 2025-26: stake in listed CPSEs may fall to **26%**.
[6:2] - **Asset monetisation:** **leases** brownfield assets for a fixed period, ownership stays (disinvestment **sells** equity); objective – **unlock value of existing assets through new financial structures**.
[6:3] - **NMP 1.0:** Aug 2021, **₹6 lakh crore** (FY22-25); **NMP 2.0:** **23 Feb 2026**, **₹16.72 lakh crore** (FY26-30).
[6:4] - **NLMC** (2022, under **DPE**): monetises **surplus land of CPSEs**, incl. loss-making units.
[6:5] - AP PPP ports: **Krishnapatnam, Gangavaram**.
[6:6] - **NRF (1992):** VRS and a safety net for workers.
[6:7] - **IBC** enacted **28 May 2016** (T.K. Viswanathan BLRC); BIFR dissolved **1 Dec 2016**; **NCLT** under **s.408** Companies Act 2013.
