[0:0] - **29** central laws → **4 Codes**; sections 1,232 → 480. Labour = **Concurrent List**.
[0:1] - In force **21 November 2025**; draft rules **Dec 2025**; Central Rules May 2026.

  [0:2.0] Wages, 2019 | 4 | Minimum Wages 1948, Payment of Wages 1936, Payment of Bonus 1965, Equal Remuneration 1976; universal minimum wage; Centre's national floor wage; no gender discrimination; 8-hour day
  [0:2.1] Industrial Relations, 2020 | 3 | Trade Unions 1926, Standing Orders 1946, Industrial Disputes 1947; lay-off permission 100 → 300
  [0:2.2] Social Security, 2020 | 9 | EPF, ESI, Employees' Compensation; 26-week maternity; gig workers

[0:5] - **Occupier (Factories Act 1948, Sec 2(n)):** person with **ultimate control** over a factory; in a company a **director** must be named
[0:6] - **2018 Standing Orders Rules**: **fixed-term employment, all sectors**; no termination notice for temporary workmen.
[0:7] - Gains (ES 2025-26): formalisation 60.4% → 75.5%; unions: anti-worker.

## SUB 2 | GST as Structural Reform
[2:0] - **101st Amendment Act, 2016** (122nd Bill); in force **1 July 2017**; **Kelkar** Committee; **Canadian dual model**; **Assam** ratified first.
[2:1] TABLE head: Article | Subject
  [2:1.0] 246A | Power to levy GST
  [2:1.1] 269A | IGST on inter-State supply
  [2:1.2] 279A | GST Council
  [2:1.3] 366(12A) | Definition
  [2:1.4] 286 | No State tax on supplies outside State / import-export
[2:2] - Destination-based; input tax credit ends cascading; subsumed **State VAT**, Central excise, service tax, CST.
[2:3] - **GST 2.0** (Aug 2025): rate rationalisation, **40% slab**: sin/luxury goods.
[2:4] - **EoDB gain:** one tax replaced many Central and State indirect taxes; ended cascading and **checkpost delays** → better **Ease of Doing Business**.
