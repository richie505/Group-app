## SUB 1 | Rate Structure and GST 2.0
[1:0] - 2017 slabs **5, 12, 18, 28%** + exempt + cess – meant as **transitional**.
[1:1] - **GST 2.0** (**56th meeting**, effective **22 Sep 2025**): **0, 5% (merit), 18% (standard), 40% (demerit)**; ~**99%** of old 12% items → 5%; ~90% of old 28% items → 18%.
[1:2] - **40% slab**: pan masala, tobacco, aerated sugary drinks, large luxury cars, yachts and **private aircraft**, online money gaming. Special: rough diamonds **0.25%**; gold, silver, jewellery **3%**.
[1:3] - Drawback of multiple rates: **classification disputes** and litigation.
[1:4] ~~ Sources: Economic Survey 2025-26 Summary Part 1 p.15; app notes 3:97
## SUB 2 | Impact on Commerce and MSMEs
[2:0] - **Common national market** through tax harmonisation; **ITC** at every stage ends cascading.
[2:1] - **E-way bill** (above ₹50,000; 1 day per 200 km) → inter-State **check-posts removed** → less transit time and logistics cost.
[2:2] - Formalisation: taxpayers **66.5 lakh (2017) → 1.51 crore (2025)**.
[2:3] - **Composition scheme**: up to ₹1.5 crore (goods, **1%**), ₹50 lakh (services, **6%**); **no ITC**. Reform agenda: pre-filled returns, automated registration and refunds.

## SUB 4 | Impact on States and Compensation
[4:0] - States gave up VAT, entry, luxury, entertainment tax, octroi → more **dependence on transfers**.
[4:1] - **Producing States** (Maharashtra, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka) feared loss → **compensation July 2017 – June 2022** at **14%** growth over **2015-16**.
[4:2] - Cess continued to repay **COVID-19 loans**; scrapped 22 Sep 2025 except **tobacco**; unused balance **50% Centre, 50% States** by **SGST revenue**.
[4:3] - **VAT** was the main State tax subsumed.
[4:4] ~~ Sources: Ramesh Singh Indian Economy 10th ed. Part 5 p.207; app notes 3:97
