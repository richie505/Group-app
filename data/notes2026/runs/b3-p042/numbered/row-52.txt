## SUB 0 | Crop Insurance – CCIS to PMFBY and AP Schemes
[0:0] TABLE head: Scheme | Year | Fact
  [0:0.0] CCIS (Comprehensive Crop Insurance Scheme) | 1 April 1985 (kharif) | first national crop insurance scheme
  [0:0.1] NAIS | Rabi 1999-2000 | replaced CCIS
  [0:0.2] Varsha Bima (rainfall insurance) | SW monsoon 2004 | run by Agriculture Insurance Company of India (AICIL); piloted in 20 areas of AP, UP, Karnataka, Rajasthan – not 2007
  [0:0.3] WBCIS | Kharif 2007, first in Karnataka | extended to 10 States from Kharif 2008
  [0:0.4] MNAIS | 2010-11 | –
  [0:0.5] PMFBY | 2016 | replaced NAIS and MNAIS (not WBCIS); premium 2% kharif, 1.5% rabi, 5% commercial/horticulture – not a uniform 2%; covers post-harvest losses from cyclones and unseasonal rain; voluntary from 2020; YES-TECH (yield), WINDS (weather), DigiClaim
  [0:0.6] RWBCIS | 2016 | protects insured farmers (all categories) against loss from adverse weather; pays when weather deviates from triggers
[0:1] - **Not a claim ground:** low yield from **poor-quality seed**. Cover is for natural calamities (drought, flood), pests and diseases.
[0:2] - **AP:** PMFBY, WBCIS/RWBCIS and the Unified Package Insurance Scheme have run in AP; there is **no** special scheme for GM crops. **Dr YSR Free Crop Insurance (2019-24):** State paid the whole premium; covered **22 notified crops** (2022); voluntary from **Rabi 2024-25** (farmer pays a small fixed share, State and Centre share the rest); Kharif 2026 – every crop insured, Collectors accountable.
[0:3] - **PMFBY slogans:** "One Nation – One Scheme"; "One Nation, One Crop, One Premium".
[0:4] > Most-asked angle (13 PYQs): scheme years – CCIS 1985, NAIS 1999, Varsha Bima 2004, WBCIS 2007 Karnataka; PMFBY premium statements (UPSC 2016).

## SUB 1 | Causes of Agrarian Crisis and Farmer Suicides
[1:0] TABLE head: Cause | Data point
  [1:0.0] Small holdings | 86% small and marginal
  [1:0.1] Rain-fed | about 50% of NSA; groundwater = 64% of irrigation; 17% blocks over-exploited
  [1:0.2] Post-harvest | cold-chain deficit 30% → up to 40% fruit/vegetable loss
  [1:0.3] Soil | NPK 10.9:4.1:1 vs ideal 4:2:1
[1:1] - **Indian agriculture is still ailing due to** under-investment, low productivity **and** post-harvest losses – "all the above".
[1:2] - **Rural poverty** is the effective reason for slow agricultural development (no capital for inputs and technology).
[1:3] - **Debt cycle:** high input cost → borrowing → crop failure or low price → inability to repay → fresh borrowing → debt trap.
[1:4] - **Suicides:** main reasons – **crop failure** and **bankruptcy/indebtedness** (NCRB ADSI 2015); concentrated in **rain-fed** belts. ADSI 2023: farm sector 10,786; Maharashtra 38.5%, Karnataka 22.5%, AP 8.6%.
[1:5] - **Swaminathan Commission** = National Commission on Farmers, constituted **2004** (18 Nov), after farmer suicides; MSP = **C2 + 50%** (C2 = A2 + imputed family labour + rent and interest on owned land and capital); National Agricultural Risk Fund.
[1:6] - **Farm protests:** Sharad Joshi (Shetkari Sangathan) backed globalisation; Nanjundaswamy (KRRS), Tikait (BKU) opposed; 2020-21 farm laws repealed 2021.
[1:7] ~~ Sources: app notes 3:52 sub 0, 3, 4, 8; NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC 01 p.59 (NCF on 18 Nov 2004, farmer suicides)

## SUB 3 | Loan Waivers and Debt Redemption
[3:0] TABLE head: Waiver | Facts
  [3:0.0] ADWDRS 2008 | small/marginal: full waiver; others: 25% OTS relief on paying 75%
  [3:0.1] AP Agricultural Debt Redemption Scheme 2014 (Runa Mafi / Runa Upasamana) | implemented by Rythu Sadhikara Samstha; ceiling ₹1.5 lakh/family; horticulture ₹10,000/acre; balance paid in annual instalments – 2nd instalment released from 22 June 2016 (Runa Upasamana Arhatha Patralu)
[3:1] - **Effect on aggregate demand (all four channels):** private consumption (net wealth up), public-sector spending/taxes, crowding-out via higher State borrowing, crowding-in as bank NPAs fall.
[3:2] - **RBI critique:** moral hazard; hurts credit culture.

## SUB 4 | Income Support and AP Tenant Farmers
[4:0] TABLE head: Scheme | Fact
  [4:0.0] PM-KISAN | ₹6,000/yr in 3 instalments of ₹2,000; at launch only small and marginal families with up to 2 hectares – extended to all landholding families from June 2019
  [4:0.1] Annadata Sukhibhava (AP) | ₹20,000; tenants covered

[4:3] - **2011 crop holiday (Konaseema paddy farmers):** special committee chaired by **Mohan Kanda** (not Jayati Ghosh, not Koneru Ranga Rao).
[4:4] - Tenants often lack passbook, KCC and insurance; those missing from **e-crop** data miss insurance and relief.
