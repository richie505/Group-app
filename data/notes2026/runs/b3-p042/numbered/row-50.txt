## SUB 4 | Yield Gaps, Inter-State Variation and Pathways
[4:0] # Yields
[4:1] TABLE head: Crop | India | Benchmark / leader
  [4:1.0] Rice | about 2,929 kg/ha | China about 7,100 – India less than half; Punjab highest
  [4:1.1] Wheat | – | Punjab highest (about 50 q/ha)
  [4:1.2] Cotton | about 440 kg/ha | world about 770; Kapas Kanti target 755
  [4:1.3] Oilseeds | 1,398 kg/ha | groundnut is the exception (above world average)
[4:2] - **Rice yield above national average:** AP, Telangana, TN; **below:** Odisha, UP, Bihar.
[4:3] - **Pulses yield:** highest in **MP and Gujarat**.
[4:4] - **Cotton yield (UPPSC 2008):** among **West Bengal**, Bihar, Assam and AP, **West Bengal** is highest (about 510 kg/ha in 2005-06 vs AP 347, Assam 73); Gujarat and Haryana are higher still. **Black (regur) soil** is best for cotton.
[4:5] # Concepts and causes
[4:6] - **Intensive cultivation:** more **labour and capital** per hectare to raise output from the same land; extensive = more land under the plough.
[4:7] - **Cereal yield:** kg per hectare of **harvested** land under cereals. **TFP:** output growth not explained by input growth.
[4:8] - **Causes of low productivity:** small holdings, monsoon dependence and inadequate irrigation (rice ~67% irrigated vs pulses ~26%), skewed NPK, low seed replacement, ~30% cold-chain losses, climate (~8% yield fall per 1°C).
[4:9] - **Pathways:** irrigation, quality seed (SRR/VRR, genome editing), balanced fertiliser (Soil Health Card), plant protection, mechanisation (Custom Hiring Centres).
[4:10] - Growth has come **more from area than yield**; now led by livestock and fisheries.
[4:11] - **TFP drivers:** agricultural research and new varieties, irrigation, rural roads and markets, extension, education.
[4:12] - **El Niño and pulses:** acreage −2 to −9%, yield −5 to −25%, output −6 to −30%. Universal cold-chain access is NOT a cause of the yield gap – its lack is.
[4:13] - **Area trends:** cotton area down 18 lakh ha, output **290.91 lakh bales (2025-26)** from about 350 – India became a **net importer of raw cotton**; soybean area in MP fell about **10%** (2023 to 2024); Telangana's cultivated area 1.31 crore acres (2014) → **2.2 crore acres** (FY23).
[4:14] > A "which one is the pathway" stem with irrigation, seed, pesticides and fertilisers as options has no single answer – all four raise productivity.
