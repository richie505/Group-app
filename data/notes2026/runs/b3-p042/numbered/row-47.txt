## SUB 2 | Water-use Efficiency and Conservation
[2:0] TABLE head: Method | Efficiency
  [2:0.0] Drip | ~90% (highest)
  [2:0.1] Sprinkler | ~70-80%
  [2:0.2] Flood/basin/canal surface | 30-50% (lowest)
[2:1] - **PMKSY** aims at enhancing **water-use efficiency** ("More crop per drop"), not organic, hybrid or GM seeds.
[2:2] - **Micro-irrigation**: water straight to the root zone; cuts evaporation and runoff. **DSR** and **SRI**: save up to 50% water.
[2:3] - **Zero / minimum tillage**: lower evaporation, better soil moisture. **Crop-residue mulch** keeps soil moisture.
[2:4] - **Conservation Agriculture** (FAO): minimum soil disturbance, **permanent soil cover (at least 30%)**, crop diversification.
[2:5] - **Bench terracing**: most widely adopted mechanical soil and water conservation measure on **hill slopes**.
[2:6] - **Virtual water** (Tony Allan): water **consumed in producing a product**; exporting rice exports virtual water.
[2:7] - **Flow duration curve:** plots discharge against the **percentage of time it is equalled or exceeded**.
[2:8] ~~ Sources: app notes 3:47 sub 1; general knowledge (bench terracing, flow duration curve)
