=== GST – Nature and Objectives
- **GST (Goods and Services Tax)**: an **indirect tax**; **single, comprehensive, multi-stage, destination-based** tax levied on **every value addition** – a **value-added consumption tax**, **not a gross value tax**.
- **Taxable event: supply** of goods and services (not manufacture, not sale).
- **Destination principle**: tax accrues to the State where goods/services are **consumed**, **not where produced**.
- Constitutional definition: **Article 366(12A)** – a tax on supply of goods or services except **alcoholic liquor for human consumption**.
- In force **1 July 2017**; **101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016**; biggest indirect-tax reform since Independence – a unified framework for many indirect taxes.
- Objectives: **"one nation, one tax"**; **end cascading ("tax on tax")** through input tax credit; **one national market** (replaces multiple taxes of multiple authorities); transparency in **indirect** taxes; designed to be **non-inflationary** (A-R: avoids tax-on-tax → correct explanation).
- Not an advantage: GST does not "drastically cut the current account deficit" or make India "overtake China" (UPSC 2017: only I).
- GST involves **both Centre and States** (dual GST) – "only at the central level" is false.
> Exam angle: statements on nature – destination-based (4 PYQs), replaces indirect taxes, not direct taxes, not gross value.

=== Origin, Timeline and Ratification
| Step | Fact |
|---|---|
| L.K. Jha Committee (1976) → MODVAT (1986) → CENVAT (2000) | Path to VAT on manufacture |
| State VAT | **Haryana first (April 2003)**; 20 States/UTs in **April 2005**; rest by 2008-09; **Maharashtra** had earlier taken **IMF** advice on VAT design |
| First proposed | Under the **Vajpayee** government; Empowered Committee of State FMs (**2000**, first chair **Asim Dasgupta**) |
| First recommended national GST | **Vijay Kelkar Task Force** (FRBM implementation, **2004**) |
| EC roadmap / First Discussion Paper | "A Model and Roadmap for GST" **2008**; First Discussion Paper **Nov 2009** |
| Target dates announced | **1 April 2010** (FM, July 2009) → **April 2011** (FM's speech, Feb 2010) → **1 April 2017** (target in 2016) → actual **1 July 2017** |
| 115th Amendment Bill | March 2011 (lapsed) |
| **122nd Amendment Bill** | Tabled **Dec 2014**; passed by Rajya Sabha **Aug 2016** |
| **101st Amendment Act** | Presidential assent **8 September 2016** |
| First State to ratify | **Assam** (12 Aug 2016), then Bihar |
| GST Council formed | By the **President**, **September 2016** |
| Rollout | Midnight 30 June / **1 July 2017** |
| Last State to adopt | **Jammu and Kashmir** (8 July 2017) – the only one not in on 1 July |
| GST 2.0 | In effect 22 Sep 2025 |
- Concept first used in **France** (1954); India follows the **Canadian dual** model; brand ambassador **Amitabh Bachchan**.
- **Revenue Neutral Rate**: rate that keeps GST revenue equal to pre-GST revenue – **Arvind Subramanian** Committee (Dec 2015): RNR **15-15.5%**, standard rate **17-18%**.
- Threshold in the original design: **₹20 lakh** turnover (₹10 lakh in special category States); from April 2019 **₹40 lakh** for suppliers of goods (States may opt), ₹20 lakh for services.
> Exam angle: "Bill 122nd vs Act 101st"; Assam first to ratify; dates of rollout (1 July 2017) and proposed dates.

=== Constitutional Basis – 101st Amendment
| Article | Content |
|---|---|
| **246A** | Parliament and State legislatures both make GST laws (simultaneous power); **Parliament alone** for inter-State supply |
| **269A** | **IGST** on inter-State trade – levied and collected by the Union, apportioned between Centre and States on Council advice |
| **279A** | **GST Council**, constituted by the **President** |
| **366(12A)** | Definition of GST |
| 268A | Service tax – **omitted** |
- **Ratification** by at least **half the State legislatures** (federal provisions touched).
- **VAT** before GST was a **State subject** (Entry 54, State List).
> Exam angle: 122nd Bill = 101st Act; 279A Council vs 269A IGST.
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 09 p.4-5; NextGen Rocket Sheets Indian Economy p.20; app notes 3:96

=== GST Council
- **Constitutional body** under **Article 279A**; **never statutory** (APPSC G1 2024: "statutory, later constitutional" is false).
- **Chairperson**: **Union Finance Minister** (first: Arun Jaitley). **Members**: Union **MoS Revenue/Finance** (only a member); Finance/Taxation Minister of each State and UT with legislature; **Vice-Chairperson** chosen by State members.
| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Quorum | One-half of total members |
| Centre's weight | 1/3 of votes cast |
| All States together | 2/3 of votes cast – each State has an **equal** vote |
| Decision | **3/4** majority of weighted votes of members present and voting |
- Recommends **tax rates, exempted goods, threshold limits**, model laws, special rates, date for petroleum – the **Council recommends**, the Centre and States **notify** rates (rates are not fixed by Parliament, EC or the Finance Commission).
- **Disputes**: the Council itself sets up a dispute mechanism (not the PAC); proceedings are **not invalid** due to a vacancy or defect in appointment.
- **Mohit Minerals (2022)**: recommendations **persuasive, not binding**; simultaneous power under 246A; "flexible federation".
> Exam angle: chair = Union FM (5 PYQs); statements on status, voting and functions.
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 09 p.5; NextGen Rocket Sheets Indian Economy p.19-21; app notes 3:96

=== Taxes Subsumed vs Not Subsumed
| Central taxes subsumed | State taxes subsumed |
|---|---|
| Central Excise Duty, Additional Excise Duties, excise on medicinal and toilet preparations | **State VAT** / Sales Tax, Central Sales Tax |
| **Service Tax** | **Purchase Tax**, Luxury Tax, Entry Tax (all forms), **Octroi** |
| **CVD** and SAD on imports | Entertainment Tax (except by local bodies) |
| Central cesses and surcharges on supply | Taxes on advertisements, lotteries, betting, gambling |
- GST replaced **indirect** taxes only – conventionally **8 Central + 9 State** taxes (subsumed **Central as well as State** taxes) – **not** income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax or property tax.
- **Not subsumed**: **Basic Customs Duty**, **stamp duty**, electricity duty, motor vehicle tax, property tax, **State excise on alcoholic liquor**.
> Exam angle: "Which tax was subsumed / abolished?" → VAT, excise, service tax, octroi, purchase tax ("all of these"); not customs.
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 09 p.5-6; Ramesh Singh Indian Economy 10th ed. Part 5 p.207; app notes 3:96

=== Items Outside GST and Exemptions
| Item | Position |
|---|---|
| **Alcoholic liquor for human consumption** | Excluded **by the Constitution** (Art. 366(12A)) |
| Five petroleum products: **crude, petrol, high-speed diesel, natural gas, ATF** | Within GST constitutionally; levied only from a date the Council recommends (Art. 279A(5)); also kept out in the 2011 Bill |
| **Electricity** | Outside |
| Ghee | **Under** GST |
- Lubricating oil, kerosene, naphtha are **not** among the five petroleum products.
- APPSC G2 2025: no GST on petrol/diesel, liquor, electricity supply to consumers, services of charitable entities → **"All of the above"**.
- **Exempt** (UPSC 2018): cereal grains hulled, chicken eggs cooked, newspapers with advertising; **not** processed canned fish.
| Term | Meaning | ITC |
|---|---|---|
| Nil-rated / Exempt | 0% / notified out of tax | No ITC |
| Zero-rated | Exports, SEZ supplies | Full ITC refund |
- **GST 2.0**: exempts **individual life and health insurance premiums**; rare-disease medicines fully exempt (Jun 2026).
~~ Sources: Ramesh Singh Indian Economy 10th ed. Part 5 p.207; app notes 3:96

=== Dual GST Components and IGST Settlement
| Component | Levied / collected by | Supply |
|---|---|---|
| CGST | Centre | Intra-State |
| SGST | States | Intra-State (equal halves with CGST) |
| **IGST** | Centre (Art. 269A) | **Inter-State and imports** |
| UTGST | Centre, for UTs without legislature | Intra-UT |
| Compensation cess | Centre | Luxury / sin goods |
- **IGST revenue settles to the destination (consuming) State**; Centre administers **CGST and IGST**; States SGST.
- **AP angle**: **SGST** is AP's **largest own-tax source** (₹36,296 crore, 38%, 2024-25 RE).
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 09 p.4-5; NextGen Rocket Sheets Indian Economy p.21; app notes 3:96

=== Rates and Rate-change Procedure
- 2017: **four slabs 5, 12, 18, 28%** + exempt + compensation cess; **GST 2.0** (56th Council meeting, effective **22 Sep 2025**): **5% (merit)**, **18% (standard)**, **40% (sin / ultra-luxury)**.
- Rates are changed by **government notification on GST Council advice**, within **statutory ceilings** (**CGST 20%**, IGST 40%) – not by amending the Acts.
- Item rates in PYQs: **sugar 5%**; **lottery** uniform **28%** (from March 2020; 40% under GST 2.0); **music books** 12% → **nil** (31st meeting, Dec 2018).

=== GST Mechanisms – ITC, Anti-profiteering, Composition
| Mechanism | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Input Tax Credit (ITC)** | Credit of tax paid on inputs by a registered person, set off against output tax; **avoids double taxation/cascading**; **not** available for exempt or non-taxable supplies, or under composition |
| Reverse Charge (RCM) | Liability moves from supplier to recipient |
| **Anti-profiteering (Sec 171, CGST Act)** | Rate-cut / ITC benefit must reach consumers (NAA) |
| Composition scheme | Goods turnover up to **₹1.5 crore** (1%), services **₹50 lakh** (6%); no ITC |
| Inverted duty structure | ITC refund allowed |
- **Anti-profiteering chain**: **NAA → CCI (Dec 2022) → GSTAT Principal Bench (from 1 Oct 2024)**.
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 09 p.6; NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC 02 p.29; app notes 3:96

=== GSTN, Saksham, E-way Bill and E-invoicing
- **GSTN**: IT backbone for GST; set up (2013) as a **not-for-profit, non-government company** (Centre 24.5%, States 24.5%, private institutions **51%**); made **fully government-owned** (decision **2018**). **GSTIN** = **15** digits.
- **Project Saksham**: CBEC/CBIC IT project for the **new indirect tax (GST) network** (approved Sept 2016).
- **E-way bill**: goods **above ₹50,000**; valid **1 day per 200 km**. **E-invoicing** curbs fake invoices, checks ITC.
- Budget 2026-27: automated registration within **3 working days** for low-risk applicants.
