=== Cropping Seasons – Kharif, Rabi and Zaid
# Three crop seasons
| Season | Sowing | Harvest | Major crops |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kharif** | June-July (SW monsoon onset) | Sep-Oct / Oct-Nov | **Rice (paddy)**, maize, jowar, bajra, **cotton**, **groundnut**, soybean, **jute**, sugarcane, **tur/arhar** |
| **Rabi** | Oct-Dec (after monsoon) | March-April | **Wheat** (the most important rabi crop), barley, **gram**, **mustard**, peas, **linseed**, potato |
| **Zaid** | March-June (summer) | June-July | **Watermelon**, muskmelon, cucumber, vegetables, fodder |
- **Crop seasons:** Kharif, Rabi, Zaid-Kharif and Zaid-Rabi together denote the **crop seasons** of India; besides Rabi and Kharif the **third season is Zaid** ("Track", Barsati, Sharad, Jhum are not seasons).
- **Rabi** depends on winter rain from **western disturbances** + irrigation; mustard is sown Oct-Nov and harvested Feb-Mar.
- **Zaid:** short, irrigation-dependent summer window **between rabi and kharif**; products are mainly **vegetables and fruits** (watermelon). Sub-seasons: Zaid Kharif (Aug-Sep sowing), Zaid Rabi (Feb-Mar sowing).
- **Sugarcane:** annual/long-duration crop, placed on the **Kharif** side in exam keys (barley, gram, wheat are Rabi).
- **Jowar** is mainly a kharif crop (also some rabi jowar in the Deccan) – the usual "NOT a major rabi crop" answer against linseed, gram, wheat.
- **Pulses:** **tur/arhar = kharif**; **gram/chana = rabi**. Millets mainly kharif.
- **Kharif set (UPSC 2004):** cotton, groundnut, maize = kharif; mustard = rabi.
- **Monsoon season:** the SW monsoon season runs from about **mid-June to mid-September** (kharif sowing window).
# Crops grown in both seasons (AP)
- AP: **paddy, maize, jowar, bajra and pulses** are grown in both kharif and rabi; AP 2024-25 paddy: 14.04 lakh ha kharif + 6.93 lakh ha rabi.
> Match pattern: Rabi – wheat; Kharif – paddy; Zaid – watermelon; sunshine = a natural resource. Jute and cotton are Kharif, never Rabi.

=== Crop Classification – Food, Cash and Mixed Farming
| Group | Crops |
|---|---|
| Foodgrains = cereals + pulses | Rice, wheat, coarse cereals/millets (Shree Anna), pulses |
| Oilseeds | Groundnut, mustard, soybean, sunflower, sesame |
| Cash / commercial | **Cotton**, jute, sugarcane, tobacco, rubber, plantation crops, groundnut |
- **Cash crop:** grown for sale, not self-consumption – **cotton** (textiles), jute, groundnut, sugarcane, tobacco, rubber. **Jowar** is a food crop, not a cash crop; in the UPPSC key **soyabean** is also treated as "not a cash crop" (oilseed/pulse-type food crop).
- Rice, maize, wheat = **food crops**.
- AP traditional food crops: paddy, jowar, bajra, ragi, pulses; traditional commercial: **groundnut, cotton, tobacco, sugarcane**.
- **Commercialisation of agriculture:** producing crops **for sale** (market) rather than for **own consumption**; shown when non-food crop area grows faster than food-crop area.
- **Mixed farming:** **cultivation of crops together with rearing of animals** (milch cattle); raising several crops together is mixed/multiple cropping, not mixed farming.
- **GM mustard** (DMH-11) was cleared by GEAC as safe (2017 recommendation; environmental release nod 2022, matter in the Supreme Court); **Bt cotton (2002)** is the only commercial GM crop; Bt brinjal under moratorium since 2010.

=== Operational Holdings – Agriculture Census
# Definition and size classes
- **Operational holding**: all land used for farm production and operated as **one technical unit** by one person, alone or with others, **regardless of title, size or location**.
- Size of an operational holding = **owned land + leased-in land – leased-out land**.
- Agriculture Census uses **five size classes**:
| Class | Size |
|---|---|
| **Marginal** | **Below 1 ha** |
| **Small** | **1-2 ha** |
| Semi-medium | 2-4 ha |
| Medium | 4-10 ha |
| Large | 10 ha and above |
- "Small and marginal farmers" = holdings up to 2 ha.
# All-India data

|---|---|---|
| Small + marginal share of holdings | ~85% | **86.2%** |
| Small + marginal share of area | ~44% | 47.3% |
| **Women-operated holdings** | **12.8%** | 13.9% |
| Average holding | 1.15 ha | 1.08 ha |
- **2010-11 average holding:** all groups **1.15 ha**; **SCs 0.80 ha**; **STs 1.52 ha**.
- 10th Census: **146 million** holdings; average down from **2.28 ha (1971)** → fragmentation. **Largest average holding: Nagaland** (2015-16).

=== Land Use – Terms, Nine-fold Classification and AP Data
# Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| NSA (Net Sown Area) | Counts a plot once |
| GCA (Gross Cropped Area) | Counts a plot once per crop |
| Cropping intensity | GCA ÷ NSA × 100 |
- "Area sown more than once" included = **GCA**, so GCA > NSA.
# India, 2021-22
| Item | Figure |
|---|---|
| Geographical area | 328.7 Mha |
| Agricultural land | ~54.8% |
| Net sown area | 141 Mha (42.8%) |
| Gross cropped area | 219.16 Mha |
| Cropping intensity | 219.16 ÷ 141 × 100 ≈ 155.4% |
| Forest cover (ISFR 2021) | 21.71% of geographical area |
# Nine-fold classification
- 1 Forests; 2 Area under non-agricultural uses; 3 Barren and unculturable land; 4 Permanent pastures and grazing land; 5 Land under miscellaneous tree crops; 6 Culturable waste land; 7 Current fallows; 8 Fallows other than current fallows; 9 Net sown area. **Net irrigated area is NOT** a category.
- **Land use pattern** (deforestation, hill-slope cultivation) is a man-made factor behind landslides; steep slope is natural.

=== Importance of Agriculture and Growth
| Indicator | Figure |
|---|---|
| Share in GVA | **~18%** (ES 2025-26) – Ministry of Agriculture 2021: **between 15% and 25%** (~20%) |
| Employment | **46.1%** of workforce (PLFS 2023-24); 54.6% in Census 2011 |
| Growth | Average 4.4% (FY16-FY25); volatility of agricultural growth has **declined** |
| Agri exports | Record $51.91 billion = 11.86% of merchandise exports |
| Horticulture | ~One-third of agri GVA; 2024-25 output 367.72 MT passed foodgrains 357.73 MT |
| Foodgrains 2016-17 (3rd AE) | **273.38 million tonnes** |
- Roles: GVA and employment, exports, **raw material** for industry, **food security**.
- Output rose mainly through **improved methods and technology** (HYV seeds, irrigation, fertiliser – Green Revolution), not area expansion.
- **Rain-fed farming:** sustainability depends most on the **frequency and adequacy of rainfall**.
- After **1991**: **decline in public investment** in agriculture; **WTO AoA (1995)**: market access, domestic support, export subsidies.
- **Agricultural Renewal Action Plan** (AP, Group-II 2017): soil health; irrigation/water augmentation and demand management; credit and insurance – **not "boosting capital formation"**.
- 2009-10 (drought year): kharif area fell, but **rabi rice area was higher** than the previous year.
> Contrast: GVA share ~18% vs employment 46.1% – Statement "agriculture still has a large employment share" true; "no decline in volatility" false.
~~ Sources: Economic Survey 2025-26 (app notes 3:45 sub 0); general knowledge (foodgrains 2016-17, 2009-10 rabi)

=== Cropping Pattern – India and Andhra Pradesh
# India
- Long-run shift: **food crops → commercial and high-value crops**.
- **Factors of cropping pattern:** natural (soil, rainfall), historical, social, economic and **government policy** (MSP, procurement, subsidies).
- **Rice: largest cropped area** in India (~51-52 Mha); pulses and oilseeds: stagnant yields.
- Rice-wheat expansion (2015-2025): Punjab, Telangana, MP – assured **MSP procurement**; policy to shift **~20% of rice area to pulses** to cut urea use and save groundwater.
- **Soyabean:** largest area **Madhya Pradesh** (5.35 Mha, 2009-10), then Maharashtra.
# Andhra Pradesh
- **Rice** = largest crop (about **one-third of GCA** in 2004-05); 2024-25: **paddy 20.97 of 37.51 lakh ha** of foodgrains.
- **West Godavari** = largest area under food crops (rice bowl, Godavari delta).
| AP crop | Main region |
|---|---|
| Rice | **Krishna-Godavari delta** |
| Maize | **Guntur** (Godavari-Krishna belt) |
| Sorghum (jowar) | **Kurnool** |
| Groundnut | **Anantapuramu** |
- **Cotton = "white gold"** of AP, expanding; groundnut shrinking (11.36 → 3.46 lakh ha, 2011-12 → 2024-25); oilseeds 15.46 → 5.35 lakh ha.
- Expanding: maize (poultry feed), horticulture, **oil palm (East Godavari, Eluru, West Godavari)**, aquaculture; shrinking: ragi, jowar.
| AP foodgrains area (lakh ha) | 2021-22 | 2023-24 | 2024-25 | 2025-26 (2nd AE) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Area | 41.44 | 33.24 | 37.51 | 42.74 |
- 2024-25: pulses 11.66 lakh ha, maize 3.20 lakh ha; production 161.86 lakh t. 2025-26 (2nd AE): paddy 24.68 lakh ha.
- **Kharif 2026** (El Niño): AP rainfall **51% below normal** (1 June-25 Aug); sown 20.35 vs 25.63 lakh ha; shift to pulses and drought-resilient crops (as of 2026).
- AP problems: monsoon dependence (~50% NSA rainfed), holdings below 1 ha, soil-fertility loss.

=== Irrigated Area
- India 2021-22: **net irrigated area 77.9 Mha**; **gross irrigated area 120.3 Mha**.
| Source of net irrigated area | Share |
|---|---|
| **Tubewells** | 47.3% |
| Canals | 24.7% |
| Other wells | 13.2% |
| Other sources | 12% |
| Tanks | 2.8% |
- **Wells (tubewells + other wells) irrigate the largest area** – about 60% of NIA.
- **Largest irrigated area (absolute): Uttar Pradesh**.
- Irrigation covers ~67% of rice area but only ~26% of pulses; ~51-52% of NSA is **rainfed**.
- **AP:** gross irrigated area ≈ **52% of GCA** (AP Economy in Brief 2020); GIA 32.71 lakh ha, NIA 26.03 lakh ha (2023-24).
- **Canals in the north:** Himalayan rivers are perennial (snow-fed) and can feed canals all year; peninsular areas rely more on tanks.

=== Agro-climatic Zones
- **Agro-climatic zones:** regions delineated by climate, soil, topography and other factors that decide suitable cropping; soil formation itself depends on climate, altitude and bedrock – both statements true but unrelated.
- **AP: six zones** (APSES 2024-25, on 13 pre-2022 districts); older material and 2017 Group-II key: seven; undivided AP: nine.
| Zone (pre-2022) | Districts |

| Godavari ("Rice Bowl") | East and West Godavari |
| Krishna | Krishna, Guntur, Prakasam |
| Southern | Chittoor, YSR Kadapa, SPS Nellore |
| Scarce Rainfall | Kurnool, Anantapuramu |
| High Altitude and Tribal | Agency areas |

|---|---|

| ICAR – NARP | **127** zones |

- AP has **22 soil categories**; **10** of them cover over **88%** of the State.
