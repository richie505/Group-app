=== PM GatiShakti National Master Plan
- Launched **13 October 2021** (APPSC GS: **2021**); **whole-of-government, GIS-based** planning platform for **multimodal connectivity** infrastructure to economic zones.
- Brings **16 ministries** (incl. Railways, Roads, Ports) on one digital platform – so both PYQ statements (multimodal connectivity; 16 ministries) are correct.
- Portal by **BISAG-N, Gandhinagar**; **Empowered Group of Secretaries** headed by the **Cabinet Secretary** → Network Planning Group.
- **Seven engines:** Railways, Roads, Ports, Waterways, Airports, Mass Transport, Logistics Infrastructure – **7** (APPSC 2024); **power transmission is NOT an engine**.
- **National Logistics Policy (Sept 2022)** complements it – came after GatiShakti.

=== National Infrastructure Pipeline (NIP) and Infrastructure Types
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Period | FY2019-20 to FY2024-25 |
| Investment | about **₹111 lakh crore** |
| Coverage | **both economic and social** infrastructure; **greenfield and brownfield** projects |
| Funding | Centre **39%**, States 40%, private 21% |
| Sector shares | **Energy ~24% (largest)**, roads ~18%, urban ~17%, railways ~12% |
- **India Investment Grid (IIG)** – the **Invest India** platform that showcases NIP projects to investors (it has not "ceased").
- APPSC G1 2024 statements: only economic ✗; greenfield only ✗; offers investment opportunities and attracts investment ✓; IIG ceased ✗ → **only III**.
- **Economic infrastructure:** energy, roads, railways, communication; **social infrastructure:** schools, health, sanitation. UPPSC 2004 key: "not an example of economic infrastructure" = **coal mines** (a production activity).
- Infrastructure multiplier **2.5-3.5**; Centre's capex **₹12.2 lakh crore** (Budget 2026-27).

=== National Monetisation Pipeline (NMP)
- Announced in **Union Budget 2021-22**; monetises **brownfield core infrastructure assets**; **ownership retained by Government**.
- **Strategic objective:** lets public asset owners **use new financial structures with private-sector capital** – unlocking value of public (not private) assets; leases are for a fixed period.
- Instruments: PPP concessions, InvITs, TOT, securitisation.

| Item | NMP 1.0 | NMP 2.0 |
|---|---|---|
| Period | FY22-FY25 | FY26-FY30 |
| Value | **₹6 lakh crore** | **₹16.72 lakh crore** (prepared by NITI Aayog) |
| Largest sector | **Roads** (APPSC 2021) | Highways, MMLPs, ropeways 26% |
| Result | ₹5.3 lakh crore; **coal** gave most proceeds | launched 23 Feb 2026 |

=== Bharatmala, Sagarmala, Railways and Logistics
| Feature | Bharatmala Pariyojana | Sagarmala |
|---|---|---|
| Sector | **Roads** | **Port-led development** (ports) |
| Launch | **2017** | National Perspective Plan **2016**; Sagarmala 2.0 (2025) |
| Scale | Phase-I **34,800 km**, 580+ districts | 800+ projects |
- **Sagarmala pillars:** port modernisation, port connectivity, port-led industrialisation, coastal community development, coastal shipping and IWT – not naval stationing, not agricultural insurance.
- **Railway electrification:** **50.91%** of routes by **March 2019**; **99.1%** by Oct 2025. Targets: **100% electrification by 2023** and **net-zero carbon by 2030** – both statements correct.
- **National Rail Plan 2030:** freight share 28% → **45%**. **Eastern DFC** (1,337 km) – **World Bank**; **Western DFC** – **JICA**.
- **Mumbai-Ahmedabad bullet train:** JICA loan of about ₹88,000 crore at **0.1%** a year, 50 years, 15-year moratorium.
- **National Logistics Policy:** logistics cost to single digits by **2030**; **7.97%** of GDP (FY24); **ULIP** = Unified Logistics Interface Platform; **LEADS** (DPIIT, 2018) modelled on the World Bank's **Logistics Performance Index**; AP = **Achiever** (LEADS 2023); Exemplars 2025 include **Tamil Nadu**.
- **Z-Morh tunnel** (6.5 km, J&K): concession agreement Jan 2020, about **₹2,379 crore**.

=== Infrastructure Financing and PPP
- **PPP = Public Private Partnership**.
- **IIFCL** (2006; NBFC-IFC since 2013) → SEBI **InvIT** regulations (2014) → **NIIF** (2015; Category II AIFs) → **NaBFID Act 2021** (paid-up ₹20,000 crore).
- **Deepak Parekh Committee:** infrastructure financing (May 2007). **Vijay Kelkar Committee (2015):** revisiting and revitalising PPP; proposed an **Infrastructure PPP Adjudication Tribunal**.
- **UK-India Infrastructure Financing Bridge:** launched at the **12th India-UK Economic and Financial Dialogue** (11 Sept 2023).
- **Cochin airport (CIAL):** first PPP airport; world's first fully solar-powered airport (2015). **Vizhinjam (Kerala):** first deep-water transhipment port. Navi Mumbai airport: operations **25 Dec 2025**.

=== Andhra Pradesh Infrastructure
- AP coastline **~1,053 km** (1,053.07 km) – **3rd** longest; **AP Maritime Board** handles ports.
- Ports: **Ramayapatnam** (Nellore), **Mulapeta** (Srikakulam), **Machilipatnam** (Krishna; **MEIL**); **Krishnapatnam** (BOST, 2004; **largest private port**); Gangavaram (BOOT).
- **Bhogapuram airport:** GMR, **PPP-DBFOT**, **3,800 m** runway. International airports: Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Tirupati (not Kadapa). **Orvakal** = first State-built airport in South India (2021).
- Targets: Blue Economy **US$20 bn by 2035**; **4 greenfield ports + 11 fishing harbours** by 2030.
