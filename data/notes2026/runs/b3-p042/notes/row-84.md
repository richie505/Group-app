=== State Bank of India – Origin, Size and Milestones
# Origin
| Stage | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Bank of Calcutta** | **1806** – first Presidency bank (first bank of the East India Company); renamed **Bank of Bengal** – the bank SBI was "originally founded as" |
| Bank of Bombay, Bank of Madras | **1840**, **1843** – the other two Presidency banks |
| **Imperial Bank of India** | **1921** – amalgamation of the **three Presidency banks**; SBI was "previously known as" Imperial Bank |
| **State Bank of India** | **1 July 1955**, SBI Act 1955 – Imperial Bank taken over ("partially nationalised"); **first public sector bank**; the RBI bought about **92%** of the shares (NextGen, Ramesh Singh; many quiz sources say 60%), diluted after the 1993 SBI share issue |
| SBI (Subsidiary Banks) Act | **1959** – **eight** princely-state banks became SBI associates |
| RBI stake to Government | **June/July 2007** – Government acquired the RBI's **59.7%** stake in SBI |
- **SBI "nationalised" in 1955** (exam wording) – earlier than LIC (1956) and the 14 banks (1969), later than the RBI (1949).
# Size and status
- **Largest commercial bank** and **largest public sector bank** of India – not the RBI (central bank), NABARD, IFCI, IDBI or Exim Bank (development/apex institutions), not PNB or Bank of Baroda.
- **Headquarters: Mumbai**; Fortune 500; the **only Indian bank in the global top 100** (S&P: 55th April 2020, **47th April 2024**).
- SBI is a **commercial bank, not an apex institution** (NABARD, SIDBI, State Co-operative Bank are apex bodies); it is the **odd one out** among UTI, Canbank Financial Services and UP State Financial Corporation (non-bank financial institutions).
- **Tagline: "With You All The Way"** (also "The Banker to Every Indian").
- Formed **Farmers' Clubs** (with NABARD) to reach farmers.
# Mergers into SBI
- **State Bank of Saurashtra – 2008**; **State Bank of Indore – 2010** (August).
- **1 April 2017**: the five associates (**SBBJ, SBH, SBM, SBP, SBT**) and **Bharatiya Mahila Bank** merged into SBI – **MUDRA Bank was not** (it is a refinancing NBFC under SIDBI).
# Abroad
- **First Indian bank branch in China** – **SBI (Shanghai, 2006)**; first Indian **private** bank in China – **Axis Bank** (Shanghai, January 2014).
- **Full (Qualifying Full Bank) status in Singapore – SBI (March 2008)**, the first Indian bank; ICICI followed (2010).
- **First overseas/offshore banking unit (OBU)** of SBI – **Mumbai** (SEEPZ SEZ).

|---|---|
| April 2011 – Sept 2013 | **Pratip Chaudhuri** |
| Oct 2013 – Oct 2017 | **Arundhati Bhattacharya** (first woman chair) |
| Oct 2017 – Oct 2020 | **Rajnish Kumar** |
| Oct 2020 – Aug 2024 | Dinesh Kumar Khara |
| **Aug 2024 – (as of 2026)** | **Challa Sreenivasulu Setty** |
- Distractors: **Shikha Sharma** (Axis Bank), **Chanda Kochhar** (ICICI Bank).

=== Bank Nationalisation – 1969 and 1980
# Two rounds
| Basis | 1969 | 1980 |
|---|---|---|
| Date | **19 July 1969** | **15 April 1980** |
| Banks | **14** | **6** |
| Deposit size | **₹50 crore and above** | **₹200 crore and above** |
| Law | Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1969 | Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1980 |
| PM | **Indira Gandhi** | **Indira Gandhi** |
- **Total nationalised: 14 + 6 = 20** private banks ("how many banks nationalised till now" = 20).
- **New Bank of India merged into PNB (September 1993)** → nationalised banks came down to **19**.
  - After the 2019-20 mergers there are **12 public sector banks** (SBI + 11 nationalised banks) (as of 2026).
- The 14 banks of 1969 held about **85% of bank deposits**; Indira Gandhi's note "**Stray Thoughts on Bank Nationalisation**"; 50th anniversary 19 July 2019.
# Which banks
- **1969 – 14**: Allahabad (now Indian Bank), Bank of Baroda, Bank of India, Bank of Maharashtra, Central Bank, Canara, **Dena (now BoB)**, Indian Bank, IOB, PNB, **Syndicate (now Canara)**, UCO, Union, United Bank of India (now PNB).
- **1980 – 6**: **Andhra Bank**, **Corporation Bank** (both now Union Bank), **Oriental Bank of Commerce** (now PNB), **Punjab & Sind Bank**, **Vijaya Bank** (now BoB), **New Bank of India** (into PNB 1993).
- **Andhra Bank = 1980**; **Syndicate Bank = 1969**; **ICICI Bank was never nationalised** (formed 1955 as a DFI by the World Bank, GoI and Indian industry); Corporation, Dena and Vijaya were nationalised (1980/1969/1980).
# Reasons and results
| Factor | Point |
|---|---|
| Reasons | Banks controlled by industrial houses; agriculture, small industry and small business neglected |
| Purpose | **Social control of credit – improving credit facilities** for neglected sectors (agriculture, small industry) |
| Results | **Branch expansion**, growth of **priority-sector lending**, more deposit mobilisation |
- Allied steps of the social-banking era: **Lead Bank Scheme (1969)**, **RRBs (1975)**, **village/district adoption by bank branches** – all features of the Indian banking system (UPSC 2010).

=== Banking Operations, Instruments and Customer Rules
# Assets and liabilities of a commercial bank
| Liabilities | Assets |
|---|---|
| Capital and reserves | Cash in hand, balances with RBI |
| **Deposits** (current, savings, term) – owed to customers | **Investments** (SLR securities) |
| **Borrowings from RBI** and others | **Loans and advances** (e.g. **credit to farmers**), **money at call and short notice**, fixed assets |
- **Deposits are NOT an asset** of a bank (UPSC 2019); credit to farmers is an asset.
# Instruments and rules
| Rule | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Banker's cheque** | = **Pay Order** (payable locally; a demand draft is payable at another centre) |
| **Cheque validity** | **3 months** from date of issue (since 1 April 2012; earlier 6 months) |
| Savings-bank interest | **Deregulated (Oct 2011)** – each bank fixes it; never fixed by the Finance Minister, Finance Commission or IBA |
| Para-banking | Credit cards, insurance, factoring, mutual fund distribution – **not** "credit to paramilitary personnel" |
| Aadhaar | KYC only with the customer's consent |
| ATM cash-out penalty | From 1 Oct 2021: **₹10,000** per ATM out of cash > 10 hours in a month |
# Banking Ombudsman
- Started **14 June 1995** under **Section 35A, BR Act 1949**; quasi-judicial; **appointed by the RBI**; free; NRIs with accounts in India may complain.
- Its awards are **not final**: an **appeal** lies with the RBI (Appellate Authority) – so "orders final and binding" is the wrong statement (now Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021).
# Seasons, green banking, apex refinance
- **Busy season: November–April; slack season: May–October** (RBI's traditional credit-policy seasons; Ramesh Singh gives slack May–September, busy October–April).
- **Green banking**: banking that considers **environmental factors in internal processes and in products/services** (not merely paperless banking).
- **NABARD** is an **apex refinancing** body – **not a source of direct finance**; RRBs, SBI and other commercial banks lend directly.
- **RIDF** (Rural Infrastructure Development Fund) – set up **1995-96** in **NABARD** (nodal agency).
- **Scheduled banks in 2013**: 161 (exam figure, not verified).

=== Lead Bank Scheme and Service Area Approach
# Origin
- Idea first mooted by the **D. R. Gadgil Study Group** (report **October 1969**: "area approach", district as the unit).
- **Nariman Committee** (F. K. F. Nariman, bankers' committee appointed by RBI, report **November 1969**) – each public sector bank to act as **Lead Bank** for allotted districts.
- **Introduced by the RBI in December 1969** – the same year as the 14-bank nationalisation; an initiative of the **bank-nationalisation** era (not NITI Aayog, XII Plan or demonetisation).
- **Priority-sector** idea: traced to RBI's 1967-68 slack-season credit policy; the sector was formally defined in 1972.
# Working
- **Basic aim**: **individual banks adopt particular districts for intensive development** (not mere office-opening, competition or deposit drives).
- **District credit planning** is done **under the Lead Bank**; lead-bank functions are performed by **a bank designated for the purpose** (not automatically SBI or the RBI).
- **Service Area Approach (April 1989)** – implemented **under the Lead Bank Scheme**; each rural/semi-urban branch serves a designated group of **15–25 villages**.
| Forum | Level |
|---|---|
| Lead District Manager / District Consultative Committee | District |
| **State Level Bankers' Committee (SLBC)** | State |
- **AP**: lead bank / **SLBC convener = Union Bank of India**; 236th SLBC (16 Sep 2026): CM Naidu asked banks to lend more to MSMEs, tenant farmers, PM Surya Ghar.
- RBI draft revision of the scheme (Feb 2026) to strengthen SLBCs and LDM offices.

=== Small Finance Banks and Payments Banks
# Origin
- **Nachiket Mor Committee (2013)** → **differentiated (niche) banks**: SFBs and payments banks; licences: universal and differentiated; SFB licences **on tap since 2019**.
- Payments banks are licensed under **Section 22, BR Act 1949**; both are public limited companies (Companies Act, 2013).
# Comparison
| Feature | Small finance bank | Payments bank |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Savings vehicles + credit to **small business units, small and marginal farmers**, micro and small industries, unorganised sector | Small savings accounts, **payments/remittances** for migrant labour and low-income households |
| Deposits | All types | **Demand deposits only**; max **₹2 lakh** per customer (initially ₹1 lakh) |
| Lending | Yes: **75% of ANBC to PSL**; **50% of loans ≤ ₹25 lakh** | **No lending; no credit cards** (debit/ATM cards allowed) |
| Minimum capital | **₹100 crore** (2014 guidelines) | ₹100 crore |
| Other | 25% branches in unbanked rural areas; can sell MFs and insurance | Funds in G-Secs/T-bills up to 1 year (75% of demand deposits in SLR) |
| Promoters | Experienced individuals, NBFCs, MFIs, local area banks | **Mobile companies, supermarket chains (resident-owned)**, NBFCs, PPI issuers, corporates, PSUs |
- UPSC 2017: SFB purpose = **credit to small business units and small/marginal farmers** ("encourage young entrepreneurs in rural areas" is not a stated aim).
- UPSC 2016: payments banks – resident-owned **telecoms and supermarket chains can promote** ✓; **cannot issue credit cards** ✗; **cannot lend** ✓ → **1 and 3**.
- **SFB → universal bank** (RBI, April 2024): voluntary; **5 years** satisfactory record; net worth **₹1,000 crore**; no change of promoters; **AU SFB** approved (2025).
- RBI **cancelled Paytm Payments Bank's** licence (harmful to depositors and public).
- **75%** = SFB PSL share = RRB PSL share = payments bank share of demand deposits in SLR securities.

=== Priority Sector Lending
- **Priority sector** = sectors that may not get timely, adequate credit but are vital: **agriculture**, **micro and small enterprises**, **weaker sections**, export credit, education, housing, social infrastructure, renewable energy – so "**all of the above**".
| Target (domestic banks) | Share of ANBC |
|---|---|
| **Total PSL** | **40%** |
| Agriculture | **18%** (small and marginal farmers 10%) |
| Weaker sections | 12% |
| Micro enterprises | **7.5%** |
| SFBs / RRBs | 75% |
- **PSLCs** (2016): banks with surplus sell certificates to banks with shortfalls; traded on RBI's **e-Kuber**.
- **Collateral-free MSE loans** up to **₹20 lakh** (and all PMEGP units); banks may raise to ₹25 lakh (Feb 2026).
- DRI scheme (April 1972): 1% of lending to the poorest at 4%.

=== Banking Structure and Development Finance Institutions
| Sector | Who |
|---|---|
| Organised | RBI; commercial banks (public, private, foreign, RRBs, SFBs, payments banks, local area banks); co-operative banks; development banks (NABARD, SIDBI, Exim, NHB, MUDRA) |
| **Unorganised** | Indigenous bankers, **moneylenders** |
- **Scheduled banks**: in the **Second Schedule, RBI Act 1934**; paid-up capital and reserves **≥ ₹5 lakh**; can borrow from the RBI. Five groups: SBI, nationalised banks, RRBs, foreign banks, private banks.
- **Co-operative banks**: State Co-operative Societies Acts + RBI under BR Act; **one member, one vote**; no-profit-no-loss.
- **NBFCs** cannot take demand deposits or issue cheques; deposits not DICGC-insured.
- **DICGC (1978)**: merger of Deposit Insurance Corporation (DIC, 1962) and Credit Guarantee Corporation of India (CGCI).
- **Foreign banks**: branches or **wholly owned subsidiaries** with near national treatment (RBI, Nov 2013); WOS minimum capital **₹500 crore**.
- **Syndicated loan**: one loan to a **single borrower** funded by a **group of lenders**, arranged by a lead bank.

| **IFCI** | **1948** | First DFI; industrial finance |
| ICICI | 1955 | Industrial credit (World Bank initiative) |
| **IDBI** | **1964** | Apex industrial finance |
| EXIM Bank | 1982 | Foreign trade |
| **SIDBI** | **1990** | MSMEs |
| **NaBFID** | 2021 | **Infrastructure** |
