=== External Debt
- **Size:** end-Dec 2025 **US$ 765.5 bn = 20.4% of GDP**. History: US$ 98.2 bn (Mar 2000) → 100.2 bn (Mar 2001, rise from **commercial borrowings and NRI deposits**); about **US$ 300 bn** around end-2010 (297.5 bn Dec 2010, 305.9 bn Mar 2011); ratio ~**21%** of GDP (Mar 2002), **23.7%** (Mar 2016).
- **Who owes it:** mostly **non-sovereign** (companies, banks); government external debt ~2.6% of GDP.
- **Largest component:** **commercial borrowings** (about 38% till 2017; still largest end-Dec 2025). **ADB** = largest multilateral lender (32.8%).
- **Currency (Dec 2025):** **US dollar 54.8%**, rupee 30.1%, yen 6.3% – not all in dollars.
- **ECBs** are regulated by the **RBI** (FEMA).
- **World Bank:** India a **less indebted** country. **GDF 2010** most indebted: **Russia, China, Turkey, Brazil, India**; around 2000 **Brazil** was the top developing-country debtor; India ranked about **10th** of the top fifteen (GDF, ES 2001-02).
- **Grants 2005-06:** the largest source was the **United Kingdom** (DFID).
- "India's External Debt: A Status Report" – **DEA, Ministry of Finance**; RBI compiles quarterly data.
> Exam angle: UPSC 2019 – "most debt is governmental" and "all in dollars" are both false.

=== Components of Public Debt and Government Funds
- **Art. 292:** the Union borrows on the security of the **Consolidated Fund of India**. Internal debt = **96.7%** of public debt; **G-Secs** over 90% of Central debt. Internal vs external is by **source** of borrowing.
| Head | Items |
|---|---|
| Internal – marketable | **Market loans (dated G-Secs)**, **Treasury Bills** (91/182/364-day), Cash Management Bills |
| Internal – non-marketable | **Special securities to NSSF** and RBI, 14-day intermediate T-Bills, securities to international institutions |
| Other liabilities (Public Account) | **Small savings** (NSC, post-office deposits), **provident funds**, reserve funds |
| External | Loans from foreign governments and multilaterals |
- **Consolidated Fund (Art. 266)** = main account: **all revenues, loans raised and loan repayments**. **Public Account** = money held **in trust** (State Provident Fund credited here). **Contingency Fund** = urgent needs.
- **Provident fund = contractual savings.** **Life insurance policies** are insurers' liabilities – **not** national (government) debt; NSCs and PF are.
- **T-Bills** = short-term debt instruments; dated securities at market-related rates in auctions = the largest part of internal debt.
- **Non-financial(-sector) debt** covers households (**housing loans, credit-card dues**), non-financial firms and government (**Treasury Bills**).

=== Debt Sustainability and Debt Trap
- **Debt trap:** a country has to **borrow to pay interest** on its outstanding loans.
- **Zero primary deficit** = fresh borrowing only for interest payments.
- **r − g:** if g > r the debt-GDP ratio falls; if **r > g a primary surplus** is needed.
- Interest ≈ **40% of revenue receipts** → high interest **squeezes other spending**; a **rising debt-GDP ratio hampers** good fiscal administration.

=== Market Borrowings and Savings
- **Market borrowings = largest source of capital receipts**; borrowings gave the maximum resources for plan expenditure.
- 2026-27: gross **₹17.20 lakh crore**, net ₹11.73 lakh crore.
- Household savings reach government via bank deposits (**SLR** holdings), small savings/NSSF, insurance and PF.
- **Per capita (Union) debt:** ₹53,796 at end-March 2016 (₹49,270 a year earlier).

=== State Debt
- **Art. 293:** States borrow **only within India** on the security of their Consolidated Fund, via **SDLs**; a State **owing the Centre** needs its **consent** to borrow; the Centre may lend to States (Art. 293(2)). External loans to States are **routed through the Centre** since **2005-06**.
- **Net borrowing ceiling:** **4% of GSDP in 2021-22** (+0.5% for power reforms); **16th FC: 3% of GSDP** (2026-31). Key ratios: debt-GSDP, interest/revenue receipts.
| State (2023-24) | Debt % GSDP |
|---|---|
| **Punjab** | 42.9 |
| West Bengal | 38.3 |
| Rajasthan | 37.9 |
| Andhra Pradesh | 34.6 |
- **AP** 2025-26 RE: **₹6,35,177 crore = 36.04% of GSDP**; **market borrowing** (₹4,83,093 crore) largest; crosses ₹7 lakh crore (BE 2026-27).

=== Off-budget Borrowing and Debt Management
- Off-budget: PSU/SPV loans **serviced from the budget but kept outside the deficit** → hides true debt (makes it look lower), hurts ratings.
- **FCI borrowing from NSSF** for food subsidy – ended in **Budget 2021-22**. FRBM: new Central guarantees ≤ **0.5% of GDP** a year.
- **AP** off-budget **₹1.28 lakh crore** (9.7% of GSDP, March 2023). **16th FC:** stop it and bring existing debt **on-budget**.
- **RBI** manages government debt (RBI Act 1934). **PDMC** (2016) is in the **DEA**, not the RBI. A statutory **PDMA** was proposed (2015-16) but **not created**.
