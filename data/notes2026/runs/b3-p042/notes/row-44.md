=== Human Capital Formation – Meaning, Means and Indicators
- **Human capital** = the **stock of skill and productive knowledge** (and health) embodied in people; population becomes human capital when there is investment in **education, training and medical care**.
- **Human capital formation** = a process of **increasing the knowledge, skill levels and capacities** of the people – **intangible wealth** (UPSC 2018 key: "2 and 4"); it is not accumulation of tangible wealth or of more (physical) capital.
- **Means:** investment in **education, health, on-the-job training, migration and information**. **Skill development** programmes enhance **human capital** (not physical, working or fixed capital). **Purchase of machinery** = physical capital; **irrigation** has no direct impact on human capital.
- **Returns:** investment in human capital **yields returns in the future** because **education and health make people more productive** (UPPSC 2025 A-R: both true, R explains A). India's IT revolution shows human capital ranking above plant and machinery.
- **Kerala:** low **infant mortality rate** because of **adequate provision of basic health and educational facilities** (APPSC 2025).
- **Economic Survey 2025-26** (ch. 11): shift from **access to quality**, learning outcomes and **preventive care**; new concerns obesity and digital addiction.
- Indicators cited: **MMR 88** per lakh live births; **stunting 29.3%**, wasting 19%.
> Human capital = people (education, health, skills); physical capital = machines, buildings. Statement items test "intangible" and "process of raising knowledge and skills".

=== Health Policy and Financing – NHP and National Health Accounts
- **First National Health Policy:** **1983**. Later NHP **2002** and **NHP 2017**.
- **NHP 2017:** public health spending **2.5% of GDP by 2025**; shift from curative to **preventive and primary care**; **universal health coverage** (not restricting services to tertiary hospitals).
- **Seven priority areas** (NHP 2017, environment for health): **Swachh Bharat Abhiyan**, balanced healthy diets and exercise, tobacco/alcohol/substance abuse, **Yatri Suraksha**, **Nirbhaya Nari**, reduced stress and safety at the workplace, reducing indoor and outdoor air pollution. **Annapurna Scheme is NOT** among them.
- **National Health Accounts 2019-20:** released **25 April 2023** by **Dr V.K. Paul, Member (Health), NITI Aayog** (prepared by NHSRC); OOPE share fell from **62.6% (2014-15) to 47.1% (2019-20)**; government health expenditure 1.35% of GDP.
| Indicator (176th Report, Standing Committee on Health) | Figure |
|---|---|
| Government health expenditure | **1.43% of GDP** (target 2.5%) |
| Household OOPE | **43.4%** of total health expenditure (2022-23) |
| Health share of government spending | 6.12% (2021-22) → **4.89%** (2022-23) |
| Medicines | ~21% of current health expenditure |
- **Missing middle:** **40 crore+** Indians outside full subsidy cover who cannot afford private care.
> Targets: health **2.5%** of GDP (NHP 2017) vs education **6%** (NEP 2020).

=== Educational Thinkers, Philosophies and Teaching Methods
| Thinker / school | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Rousseau** | Naturalism; learning by doing, heuristic discovery, observation – the **project method** (Kilpatrick, 1918) is not his |
| **John Dewey** | **Pragmatism** – "**learning by doing**"; father of the progressive education movement (Laboratory School, 1896) |
| **A.S. Neill** | **Summerhill** (1921) – keyed as the initiator of **progressive schools** |
| **Froebel** | Kindergarten – **school as a play garden** for primary learners ("gifts and occupations") |
| **Maria Montessori** | **Didactic** (self-correcting) apparatus |
| **Ivan Illich** | **Deschooling** ("Deschooling Society", 1971) |
| **J. Krishnamurti** | Founder of "**The School**" (KFI, Chennai); "a school is a **sacred place** where all are learning about the complexity of life and its simplicity" |
| **Robert Glaser** (1962) | Basic teaching model – **4** components: instructional objectives, entering behaviour, instructional procedures, performance assessment |
| **Derek Unwin** | Educational technology = "**application of modern skills and techniques to the requirements of education and training**" |
- **Methods:** **laboratory method** uses the **maximum senses**; **role play** best lets one **re-examine one's own and others' views, feelings and behaviour**; **learner-controlled instruction** = learner decides when, where, with what to study and when to be tested.
- **Functions of education:** adjustment function = **social adjustment**.
- **Guidance services:** **follow-up service** is on the weakest footing for vocational courses.
- **Group dynamics** (Kurt Lewin) = **adjustment consequences in small groups**.
- **Obstacles to educational change:** cultural lag, traditionalism, vested interests and lack of resources are usually listed as obstacles.
- **Mobile schools** concept – printed key **McDonald** (not verified).
> Most items come from the APPSC Hostel Welfare Officer paper (education and psychology portion).

=== Educational Psychology – Intelligence, Needs, Learning, Development, Personality
| Theory / term | Given by / meaning |
|---|---|
| **Two-factor** theory of intelligence (g and s) | **Charles Spearman** |
| **Hierarchical** theory of intelligence | **Cyril Burt and Philip Vernon** |
| **Multifactor** theory | **Thorndike** – level (difficulty), **range** (number of tasks at a level), area, speed |
| **Hierarchy of needs** | **Abraham Maslow** – **self-actualisation** is the highest need |
| Word association test | **Jung** (1910, Clark University lectures) |
| **Projective test** | **Unstructured stimuli** – ink-blots (Rorschach), pictures (TAT) |
| **Law of exercise** (Thorndike) | Repetition forms habits and **strengthens the S-R bond** |
| Gestalt **law of Prägnanz** | Keyed as **closure** (strictly the master law of "good figure") |
- **Learning:** a response is strengthened through **reward** (reinforcement).
- **Piaget:** **sensorimotor 0-2 years**; **conservation** appears at about **6 years** (concrete operational).
- **Conflicts (Lewin):** two positive goals = **approach-approach**; two motives not in accord = **conflict**.
- **Defence mechanism:** raising self-worth by identifying with illustrious persons or institutions = **identification**.
- **Percept** = organisation of sensory experiences about an object; **giftedness** = early language, abstract thinking, strong memory, intellectual curiosity.
- **Parten's play:** **parallel play** – each child plays alone but watches what others do.
- **Statistics basics:** **tally marks** give the **class frequency**; separate vertical/horizontal rectangles = **bar graph** (a histogram has adjoining rectangles).
