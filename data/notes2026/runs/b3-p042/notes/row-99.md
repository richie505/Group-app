=== Article 280 – Mandate, Composition and Nature
- **Article 280:** the **President** constitutes a Finance Commission **every fifth year or earlier**; **Chairman + 4 members**; members are eligible for re-appointment.
- **Qualifications:** fixed by **Parliament** – **Finance Commission (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1951**: Chairman = experience in **public affairs**; members from: a High Court judge (or one qualified), finance and accounts of government, financial matters and administration, economics.
| Clause | Duty (recommendation to the President) |
|---|---|
| 280(3)(a) | **Distribution of net proceeds of taxes** between the Union and the States (vertical) and **among the States** (horizontal) |
| 280(3)(b) | **Principles of grants-in-aid** to States out of the **Consolidated Fund of India** (Art. 275) |
| 280(3)(bb), (c) | Measures to **augment a State's Consolidated Fund** for **panchayats** and **municipalities**, on **State Finance Commission** advice – added **1992** (73rd/74th Amendments) |
| 280(3)(d) | Any other matter referred by the President in the interests of **sound finance** |
- **Art. 281:** the President lays the report and an **explanatory memorandum** on action taken before **each House**.
- **Nature:** **quasi-judicial**; recommendations **advisory**, not binding (Rajamannar: should not be turned down without compelling reasons); **"balancing wheel of fiscal federalism"**.
- **Not permanent:** it is constituted afresh each time and its **tenure is fixed by the President's order** (it reports in about two years) – not a five-year or permanent body.
- **Not its job:** preparing the Budget, assessing income tax, controlling expenditure, **Art. 282 discretionary grants**, GST sharing (GST Council), devolving State taxes such as trade/sales tax.
> Exam angle: "primary function = distribute revenue between Centre and States"; "constituted by the President under Art. 280".
- Also not an FC job: **maintaining the income-expenditure accounts** of the Union and the States.

=== Thirteenth and Fourteenth Finance Commissions
| Item | 13th FC (Kelkar, 2010-15) | 14th FC (Y.V. Reddy, 2015-20) |
|---|---|---|
| States' share | **32%** (from 30.5%) | **42%** (+10 points, largest-ever rise) |
| Highest-weight criterion | **Fiscal capacity distance 47.5%** | **Income distance 50%** |
| Other criteria | Population 1971 25%, area 10%, fiscal discipline 17.5% | Population 1971 17.5%, population 2011 10%, area 15%, **forest cover 7.5% (first time)** |
- **14th FC approach:** **tax devolution, not grants,** as the primary route ("compositional shift"); took a State's **entire revenue expenditure** – **ended the Plan/non-Plan distinction**; **no distinction** between general and **Special Category** States; **no sector-specific grants**; States to share more of CSS costs.
- **14th FC fiscal roadmap:** Centre's fiscal deficit **3% of GDP** (2016-17 to 2019-20); States anchored at **3% of GSDP**; States **excluded from NSSF** loans from **1 April 2015**.
- **14th FC local bodies:** **₹2,87,436 crore** (2015-20), of which gram panchayats **₹2,00,292 crore**; distributed **90% population (2011), 10% area**.
- Government accepted the 14th FC report on **24 Feb 2015**.

=== Fifteenth Finance Commission
- Constituted **27 Nov 2017**, chairman **N.K. Singh**; two reports – **2020-21** (interim) and **2021-22 to 2025-26**.
- **States' share 41%** (one point less than 42%, because **J&K became a Union Territory**).
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Income distance | **45%** |
| Population (2011) | 15% |
| Area | 15% |
| Forest and ecology | 10% |
| Demographic performance | 12.5% |
| Tax effort | **2.5%** (reintroduced to reward fiscal performance) |
- Not a criterion: **income tax collected in a State**.
- **Grants:** revenue-deficit, **sector-specific**, **State-specific**, local-body and disaster grants (about **₹10.1 lakh crore**); **₹4,800 crore** (₹1,200 crore a year, **2022-23 to 2025-26**) to improve **educational outcomes**; **₹45,000 crore** performance incentive for **agricultural reforms**; rural local bodies **₹2.36 lakh crore**.
- **Terms of reference rows:** mandated to use **Census 2011** population → **southern States** objected; ToR also covered disaster financing and a **non-lapsable fund for defence** (and internal security).
> Exam angle: UPSC 2025 – ₹4,800 crore education, ₹45,000 crore agriculture, tax effort true; "45% share" false (41%).

=== Sixteenth Finance Commission (2026-31)
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Constituted | **31 December 2023** (not 2024) |
| Chairman | **Arvind Panagariya** |
| Report submitted | **17 November 2025** |
| Tabled in Parliament | **1 February 2026** (with Budget 2026-27, Art. 281) |
| In force | **1 April 2026**; award **2026-27 to 2030-31** |
| States' share | **41% retained** (States' demand for 50% rejected) |
- **Horizontal formula:** income distance **42.5%**, population (2011) **17.5%**, demographic performance 10%, area **10%** (cut from 15%), forest 10%, **contribution to GDP 10% (new)**; **tax effort dropped**. It is the **first FC to use contribution to GDP**.
- All **five southern States'** shares rose (AP **4.217%**).
- **Grants:** total about **₹9.47 lakh crore**; **local bodies ₹7.91 lakh crore** (rural 4.35, urban 3.56); disaster corpus (SDRF + SDMF) **₹2,04,401 crore**; **no revenue-deficit, sector-specific or State-specific grants**.
- **Disaster cost-share:** **90:10** for North-Eastern and Himalayan States, **75:25** for others; **heatwave and lightning** to be notified disasters.
- **Fiscal path:** Centre's fiscal deficit **3.5% of GDP by 2030-31**; States **3% of GSDP**; combined debt projected to fall from 77.3% to 73.1% of GDP; off-budget borrowing to stop.
- Budget 2026-27 gave States **₹1.4 lakh crore** as FC grants for 2026-27.
- **16th FC terms of reference** (notified **31 Dec 2023**): the Art. 280(3) items – tax sharing, principles of grants-in-aid, augmenting States' funds for local bodies – plus clause (iv): it **may review the present arrangements on financing disaster management** (DM Act 2005 funds).

=== Earlier Commissions (10th to 12th) and Disaster Funds
- **States' shares:** 10th **29%** (Alternative Scheme of Devolution, gross Central taxes); 11th **29.5%**; 12th **30.5%**.
- **Disaster funds:** 9th FC – Calamity Relief Fund; 10th FC – National Fund for Calamity Relief; **11th FC – National Calamity Contingency Fund (NCCF)**, ₹500 crore corpus (renamed **National Disaster Response Fund** in 2010). Papers call it a "National Disaster Reserve Fund" – the intended answer is the **11th FC**.

=== Andhra Pradesh and the Finance Commissions
- **Sec 46(1), AP Reorganisation Act, 2014:** the **13th FC** award to undivided AP is apportioned between AP and Telangana on **population ratio and other parameters**; the **14th FC** was asked to make **separate awards**.
- AP relied on the **14th FC** revenue-deficit grant (about **₹22,113 crore**, 2015-20) and **15th FC** revenue-deficit grants and disaster funds; a special package was given **in lieu of Special Category Status**.
- Revenue deficit 2014-15 to 2018-19: about **₹16,000 crore**; projected **₹48,000 crore** (2025-26).
- **16th FC:** AP share **4.047% → 4.217%**; loss of up to ₹38,000 crore from dropped grants.
