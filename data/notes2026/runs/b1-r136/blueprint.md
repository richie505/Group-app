## 3. Repeats and trends
- **Most repeated:** Panch Mahal (4), Buland Darwaza (4 + Akbar's list), Red Fort – Shah Jahan (3 incl. "not Akbar's"), Delhi Jama Masjid (2), Itimad-ud-Daula – Nur Jahan (2), Haji Begum (2), Lucknow madrasa (2, same UPPSC 2016 question), IAS 1998 chronology (2 copies), V.A. Smith on the Taj (2: direct + wrong-pair).
- **Key traps checked:** the Taj's chief architect – books key "Ustad Isa", modern scholarship (and PMF) Ustad Ahmad Lahori; the Peacock Throne normally stood in the Diwan-i-Khas, but the SSC key "Diwan-i-Am" matches Tavernier; Bibi ka Maqbara is keyed "Aurangzeb" though Azam Shah built it in his reign; "Mughal emperor with no mausoleum in India" – Babur, though Jahangir's Lahore tomb is now in Pakistan.
- Row 136 (society) is lightly tested; the old notes' nobility, peasantry and travellers tables are asked only by the own MCQs (kept short). The old "Number game", "APPSC PYQ table" and "traps" lists were dropped; the UNESCO / other-dynasty capitals table is never asked by a PYQ (kept for 15 own MCQs).
