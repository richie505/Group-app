=== Talliki Vandanam and School / Higher Education Decisions
# Talliki Vandanam (Thalliki Vandanam) 2026
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Earlier name | **Amma Vodi** |
| Department | **School Education Department** |
| Benefit | **₹15,000 per child per year** to the mother/guardian; **₹2,000** deducted for school maintenance, sanitation, hygiene |
| 2026 DBT | From **22 July 2026** – **₹13,000** credited (second year of the scheme) |
| Coverage | **Class 1 to 12** (incl. Intermediate) – government, aided, private, residential schools |
| Type | **Conditional cash transfer** – **75% attendance** |
| Aim | Reduce schooling cost → **lower dropout, higher Gross Enrolment Ratio (GER)** (APPSC Endowment PYQ) – not GDP, literacy or admissions in government schools only |
- Benefit goes to **every eligible child** (not one per family); extended to orphaned children; account must be Aadhaar-seeded and NPCI-mapped. **DBT** was launched nationally in **2013**.
| Criterion | Rule |
|---|---|
| Income | Rural below ₹10,000/month; **urban below ₹12,000/month** |
| Land | Wet up to 3 acres / dry up to 10 acres |
| Electricity | **Below 300 units per month** |
| Other | Rice Card mandatory; house below 1,000 sq ft |
- **Excluded:** **ITI and Polytechnic** students.
# Prashast 2.0, PM SHRI, KGBVs, Canva
- **Prashast 2.0** app: mandatory screening for **21 types of disabilities** in **all schools** (incl. private), 2026–27. **Part-I – class teachers, by September 2026**; Part-II – special educators and Bhavita Centre staff, by October 2026.
- **AP ranked 1st** in utilisation of **PM SHRI** funds (reviewed by Nara Lokesh); PM SHRI = Prime Minister's **Schools for Rising India** – model **government** schools under NEP 2020 (not private).
- **KGBVs** in AP: **352**; seats 75% SC/ST/OBC/minority, **25% BPL**.
- **Canva MoU:** free Canva for Education in **four phases**.
# Higher education and new schools in Amaravati
- **AP Higher Education Fee Regulatory and Monitoring Committee:** Chairman **Dr. Justice V.R.K. Krupasagar** (former AP HC judge).
- **Four higher-education Bills (Aug 2026):** the Regulatory Commission turned into a **fee-regulation committee**; less land for private universities.
- **Bedford School (UK):** founded **1552 by King Edward VI**; Amaravati = first foreign educational institution in Amaravati and the school's **third international campus**; its first India campus is at **Mohali**.
- **Abburaajupalem academy:** built by a farmers' group (DAV Amaravati Rythu Vidyalaya Vedantam Group, Thullur) – **not** under the AP School Education Corporation.

| Particular | Fact |

=== Population, Health and Child Care
# Pillale Sampada
- Launched in **Vijayawada** on **World Population Day, 11 July 2026** – to tackle **declining birth rate and ageing**.
- **₹30,000** for the **third child**; **₹40,000** for the **fourth**.
- World Population Day set up in **1989** by **UNDP**.
# Sanjeevani digital health and ABDM
- AI-based digital health project **piloted in Kuppam**, to go statewide.
- **ABDM** launched **2021** (not 2022); national **e-Sanjeevani** telemedicine **2019**.
# Biofoundry Task Force (cancer)
- State biobank with **CSIR–IGIB and CSIR–CCMB**; **HPV vaccination for 3.45 lakh girls** – HPV is the major cause of cervical cancer (A and R both true, R explains A).
# Shishu Maapan and urban crèches
- **Shishu Maapan:** contactless AI app with **Wadhwani AI** – estimates **newborn measurements from smartphone video** without contact.
- Urban crèches: implementing agency **MEPMA** (**Mission for Elimination of Poverty in Municipal Areas**).
