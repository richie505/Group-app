[0:1:7] # P4 – Zero Poverty Programme (status as of 30 March 2026)
[0:1:8] - **P4** = **Public-Private-People Partnership**; launched on **Ugadi, 30 March 2025**; rolled out statewide from **19 August 2025**.
[0:1:9] - Goal: **zero poverty** in AP, first targeted for **2029**; at the first anniversary (**30 Mar 2026, Tirupati**) restated as a **poverty-free society by 2047**.
[0:1:10] - Model: the **top 10%** of society adopt the **bottom 20%**; mentors/donors = **Margadarsis**, beneficiary families = **Bangaru Kutumbams** (golden families); help is financial aid, mentoring and resources (voluntary, not a cash-transfer scheme).
[0:1:11] - About **19.16 lakh** (19,15,771) Bangaru families identified.
[0:1:12] - [30 Mar 2026, first anniversary] **1.06 lakh Margadarsis** had adopted **10.3 lakh families**.
[0:1:13] P4 = Public-**Private**-**People** Partnership (4 Ps: Public, Private, People, Partnership); Margadarsi = mentor, Bangaru Kutumbam = beneficiary family.
