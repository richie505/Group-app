## SUB 1 | The 74TH Amendment Act, 1992 – Dates and Provisions
[82:1:0] # Enactment
[82:1:1] - The **Nagarpalika Act**; passed by both Houses **December 1992**; Presidential assent **April 1993**; in force **1 June 1993**.
[82:1:2] - Inserted **Part IXA "The Municipalities"** (Articles **243P to 243ZG**, 18 Articles) and the **Twelfth Schedule** (18 items, Art. 243W).
[82:1:3] - Gave **constitutional status to urban local governments**; uniform framework obligatory for States; State laws to conform within **one year** (243ZF).
[82:1:4] - 'Local Government' stays in the **State List**.
[82:1:5] 24 April 1993 = 73rd (Panchayats, National Panchayati Raj Day); **1 June 1993 = 74th** (Municipalities).
[82:1:6] # 73rd vs 74th
[82:1:7] TABLE head: Basis | 73rd | 74th
  [82:1:7.0] Concerns | Rural – Panchayats | Urban – Municipalities
  [82:1:7.1] Part | IX (243–243O) | IXA (243P–243ZG)
  [82:1:7.2] Schedule | Eleventh – 29 | Twelfth – 18
  [82:1:7.3] Institutions | Gram Panchayat, Panchayat Samiti, Zila Parishad | Nagar Panchayat, Municipal Council, Municipal Corporation
[82:1:8] - Both are linked to district planning bodies (**DPC 243ZD**, **MPC 243ZE**) – both added by the **74th** (Part IXA).
[82:1:9] - The 73rd recognised **Panchayats** (not urban bodies) as self-government; constitutional base of urban government = **Part IX-A only**.
[82:1:10] - 73rd/74th created **SEC, DPC and SFC** – not a "State Property Tax Valuation Board". (A keyed answer also lists DPC and SEC under the 73rd.)

## SUB 4 | Composition, Membership, Term and Elections
[82:4:0] # Composition (243R)
[82:4:1] - Members **directly elected** from wards. State law may add: persons with **municipal-administration experience (no vote)**; Lok Sabha/Assembly members for the area; Rajya Sabha/Council members registered as electors; chairpersons of committees.
[82:4:2] - Chairperson's election – left to the State.
[82:4:3] # Qualifications (243V)
[82:4:4] - Minimum age **21** (not 25); disqualifications as under the law for **State Legislature** elections or a State law.
[82:4:5] - A councillor who, as a lawyer, acts against the Municipal Council – removed by the **Collector** (keyed answer).
[82:4:6] # Term and elections (243U, 243ZA, 243Y)
[82:4:7] - **Five years** from the date appointed for its first meeting; **reasonable opportunity of being heard** before dissolution.
[82:4:8] - Election before expiry, or **within six months** of dissolution (not one year); not needed if less than six months remain.
[82:4:9] - Electoral rolls and polls – **State Election Commission** (same as 243K). Finances – the **243I SFC** also reviews municipalities (243Y).
## SUB 5 | Types of Urban Local Bodies
[82:5:0] # Article 243Q
[82:5:1] TABLE head: Type | Area
  [82:5:1.0] Nagar Panchayat | Transitional area (rural to urban)
  [82:5:1.1] Municipal Council | Smaller urban area
  [82:5:1.2] Municipal Corporation | Larger urban area – big cities
[82:5:2] - Specified by the **Governor** by public notification, on population, density, revenue, share of non-agricultural employment, economic importance.
[82:5:3] - **Industrial township** (243Q proviso): where an **industrial establishment** provides municipal services, no municipality need be constituted.
[82:5:4] - Corporation's three authorities: **Council (Mayor)**, **Standing Committees**, **Commissioner**.
[82:5:5] # Eight types of ULBs
[82:5:6] - **Municipal Corporation, Municipality, Notified Area Committee, Town Area Committee, Cantonment Board, Township, Port Trust, Special Purpose Agency** – NOT Zonal Development Authority.
[82:5:7] - **Cantonment Board** – not in 243Q; under **Ministry of Defence**. MoHUA – main ministry for ULBs; Home Affairs – ULBs in UTs.
[82:5:8] - **Cantonments Act, 1924:** governed cantonment boards; replaced by the **Cantonments Act, 2006**.
[82:5:9] - Panchayat vs Cantonment Board dispute – decided by **State Government with Central agreement** (s. 79(2), Chhattisgarh Municipalities Act, 1961).
## SUB 6 | Twelfth Schedule – 18 Functions
[82:6:0] - Urban/town planning; **regulation of land use and building construction**; planning for economic and social development.
[82:6:1] - Roads and bridges; **water supply**; **fire services**.
[82:6:2] - Public health, sanitation, conservancy, solid waste management; **urban forestry**, environment and ecology.
[82:6:3] - Weaker sections incl. disabled; **slum improvement and upgradation**; **urban poverty alleviation**.
[82:6:4] - Public amenities (street lighting, parking, bus stops, conveniences).
[82:6:5] - **Cattle pounds**, prevention of cruelty to animals; **vital statistics**; **slaughter houses and tanneries**; burial grounds.
[82:6:6] TABLE head: Only Eleventh (Panchayats) | Only Twelfth (Municipalities)
  [82:6:6.0] Non-conventional energy | Cattle pounds
  [82:6:6.1] Small-scale industries | Fire services
  [82:6:6.2] Technical and vocational education | Slum improvement
  [82:6:6.3] Libraries; markets and fairs; PDS | Slaughter houses and tanneries; vital statistics
[82:6:7] APPSC G2 2025: municipal Twelfth Schedule function = **cattle pounds**.
[82:6:8] - The Schedule is not only physical infrastructure – social, environmental, cultural, regulatory too.
[82:6:9] - **Public order** is NOT a local self-government concern.
[82:6:10] - Also in the Twelfth Schedule: **parks, gardens and playgrounds**; **promotion of cultural, educational and aesthetic aspects**.

## SUB 8 | Dpc and Mpc – Articles 243ZD and 243ZE
[82:8:0] TABLE head: Feature | DPC | MPC
  [82:8:0.0] Article | 243ZD | 243ZE
  [82:8:0.1] Area | Every district | Every metropolitan area – 10 lakh+ (may span districts)

## SUB 9 | Functionaries and Andhra Pradesh Ulbs
[82:9:0] # Functionary → institution

[82:9:4] # AP data and laws
[82:9:5] - **AP SES 2025-26:** **123 ULBs = 17 Municipal Corporations + 76 Municipalities + 30 Nagar Panchayats**. Older keys: 71 municipalities (APPSC 2018); 17 corporations (G1 2019).
[82:9:6] - Department: **MA&UD**; **DIGIT** e-Governance platform.
[82:9:7] - **AP Municipalities Act, 1965** (municipalities, Nagar Panchayats); **AP Municipal Corporations Act, 1994** (corporations), which applies the **Hyderabad Municipal Corporations Act, 1955** as its parent law.
[82:9:8] - State Government controls ULBs in legislative, financial and personnel matters.
[82:9:9] - **2026 developments:**
[82:9:10]   - **18 Aug 2026:** Cabinet – **33.33% BC quota** in ULBs (34% in rural bodies); **direct election of Mayors** and chairpersons.
[82:9:11]   - **19 Aug 2026:** **G.O. Rt. No. 1065** – BC reservation; polls due in **100 of 123 ULBs**; merger of 23 municipalities deferred.
[82:9:12]   - SEC **Anil Chandra Punetha** – ward-wise photo rolls for **41 ULBs** (12 corporations incl. GVMC, Vijayawada, Guntur, Tirupati, Kurnool + 29); qualifying date 1 Jan 2026; rolls due 22 Sep 2026.
[82:9:13]   - ULBs may lose **₹12,150 crore** if polls slip; 16th FC grants only to duly constituted ULBs.
[82:9:14] # Metropolitan planning in AP
[82:9:15] - **VMRDA** – formed 2018 (G.O.Ms. 302, 5 Sep 2018) dissolving **VUDA** (1978); covers Visakhapatnam, Vizianagaram, Anakapalli.
[82:9:16] - **APCRDA** – set up December 2014 under the **APCRDA Act (11 of 2014)**; plans and develops the capital region and **Amaravati**.
[82:9:17] - ULB finances: spend **< 1% of GDP**, employ ~10% of public workers – "**stepchild**" of federalism; **property tax** main own revenue, under-collected; cities house ~36% of population.
