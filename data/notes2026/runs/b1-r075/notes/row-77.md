=== Mahabalipuram – Rathas, Reliefs and the Shore Temple
# Pancha (Pandava) Rathas
- **Builder:** the **Pallavas** under **Narasimhavarman I (Mamalla, 630–668)** – "Seven Ratha" / Pancha Ratha temples (APPSC GS 2025; UPPSC 1994, 2014, 2015; SSC 2008, CGL 2020) – not Mahendravarman, Paramesvaravarman, Nandivarman, Simhavarman, Pulakesin II or Mayuravarman; not Cholas, Cheras, Chedis, Chalukyas, Rashtrakutas or Satavahanas.
- **Monolithic:** each carved from **one granite rock**, copying wooden and structural forms (not brick and timber).
- **Five:** **Dharmaraja, Bhima, Arjuna, Nakula-Sahadeva, Draupadi**.
| Ratha | Special fact |
|---|---|
| **Dharmaraja** | **Largest**; forerunner of the Dravida vimana |
| **Draupadi** | **Smallest** (UPPSC Mains 2011); hut-shaped single cell, no ornament |
| Nakula-Sahadeva | Apsidal |
# Reliefs and mandapas
| Monument | Theme |
|---|---|
| **Arjuna's Penance / Descent of the Ganga** | Bhagiratha's penance; **largest open-air rock relief** in India – the "descent of the Ganga" relief is at **Mahabalipuram** (Asst. Comm. 2018), not Ellora, Sanchi or Amaravati |
| Varaha Mandapa | **Varaha rescuing Bhudevi** |
| Mahishasuramardini Cave | Durga slaying Mahishasura |
| Krishna Mandapa | Krishna lifting **Govardhana** |
- Pancha Rathas, Varaha Mandapa and Mahishasuramardini cave = **Narasimhavarman I** phase.
# Shore Temple
- **Structural** temple of **Narasimhavarman II (Rajasimha)**; **two Shiva shrines + one Vishnu shrine**; sole survivor of the "**Seven Pagodas**".
# Port and UNESCO
- **Mahabalipuram = the famous Pallava port** (APSLPRB SI 2019) – not Krishnapatnam, Motupalli (Kakatiya) or Achalapuram.
| Point | Mahabalipuram | Ajanta |
|---|---|---|
| UNESCO | **1984** | **1983** |
| Patron | Hindu; **Pallavas** | Buddhist; Satavahana and Vakataka phases |
| Form | Rathas, reliefs, Shore Temple | **25 viharas + 4 chaityas**; found **1819** by **John Smith** |

=== Phases of Pallava Architecture, Mandagapattu and Painting
# Four phases
| Phase | Ruler | Type | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mahendra | Mahendravarman I (600–630) | Rock-cut pillared mandapas | **Mandagapattu**, Mahendravadi, Dalavanur |
| Mamalla | Narasimhavarman I (630–668) | Monolithic rathas and mandapas | Pancha Rathas |
| **Rajasimha** | **Narasimhavarman II** | **First structural temples** | **Shore Temple; Kailasanatha, Kanchi** |
| Nandivarman / Aparajita | Nandivarman II and later | Smaller structural temples | Vaikuntha Perumal |
- Evolution: **rock-cut → monolithic → structural**.
# Mandagapattu inscription
- Issued by **Mahendravarman I**: a temple for **Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva** made **without brick, timber, metal or mortar** (stone is not in the list).
- = **first rock-cut temple in South India**; first **dvarapalakas** at a temple entrance.
# Painting
- **Sittannavasal** (**Pudukkottai**, TN): **Jain** cave with **lotus-pond** murals; renovated by the **Pandyas** (9th c.).
- **Panamalai** painting of **Parvati** = **Rajasimha** period.
# Builder match
- **Shore Temple – Narasimhavarman II; Brihadisvara – Rajaraja Chola I; Gommateshwara – Chamundaraya; Barabar caves – Ashoka**.

=== Kanchi Temples – Kailasanatha and Vaikuntha Perumal
- **Kailasanatha (Rajasimheshvara), Kanchi: built by Narasimhavarman II (Rajasimha)** (G2 2000 ×2) – not Mahendravarman I, Narasimhavarman I or Nandivarman Pallavamalla; **completed by Mahendravarman III**.
  - **Sandstone**; the **oldest surviving Pallava structural temple** at Kanchi; carries **Vikramaditya II's Kannada inscription**; model for the **Virupaksha, Pattadakal**.
- **Vaikuntha Perumal, Kanchi:** **Nandivarman II**; **Vishnu**; storeyed sanctum and panels on Pallava history.

=== Pallava-style Rock-cut Caves in Andhra – Bhairavakona and Undavalli
- **Bhairavakona = a centre of Shaivism** (G2 2008; APPSC PYQ) – not Buddhism, Jainism or Vaishnavism; **eight** rock-cut Shiva caves resembling early Pallava caves.
  - Location: Nallamala hills, **Prakasam district** on the Nellore border (old books and keys: "Nellore district").
| Cave | Location | Attribution |
|---|---|---|
| **Undavalli** | Guntur, Krishna bank | **Vishnukundins** – not Cholas; reclining Anantasayana Vishnu |
| **Mogalrajapuram** | **Vijayawada** | Vishnukundins; Shiva and Vishnu shrines |
| Bhairavakona | Prakasam | Vishnukundin / early Pallava idiom |
- The **Vishnukundins** started Hindu cave temples in Telugu lands, imitating the **Pallava** cave style.
