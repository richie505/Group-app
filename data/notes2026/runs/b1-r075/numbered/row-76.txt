## SUB 0 | Mahendravarman I (600–630) – Titles and the Mattavilasa Prahasana
[0:0] # Titles
[0:1] - **Mahendravarman I**, son of **Simhavishnu**: titles **Mattavilasa, Vichitrachitta, Gunabhara, Chitrakarapuli, Lalitankura** (UPSC 2025 ×2; APPSC G1 PYQ "Vichitrachitta") – not Simhavishnu, Narasimhavarman I or Simhavarman.
[0:2]   - **⚠ Trap:** **Vichitrachitta** is the father's title; **Narasimhavarman I** is **Mamalla / Vatapikonda**.
[0:3] - **Not his title:** **Vatapikonda** (Narasimhavarman I).
[0:4] # Mattavilasa Prahasana
[0:5] - **Author: Mahendravarman I** (G1 Prelims 2012; UP 2010) – not Ramanujacharya, Narasimhavarman, Dandin, Gautamiputra Satakarni, Rudradaman or Pulakesin II.
[0:6] - A **Sanskrit** one-act **farce (prahasana)** mocking **Kapalikas and Buddhist monks**; he also wrote **Bhagavadajjuka**.
[0:7] # Faith and building
[0:8] - Began as a **Jain** → turned **Shaiva** under **Appar (Tirunavukkarasar)**, not Sambandar.
[0:9] - **Rock-cut temples of South India begin with him** – **Mandagapattu** (no brick, timber, metal or mortar); **Kudumiamalai** music inscription credited to his patronage.
[0:10] - Wars with **Pulakesin II** began under him; he lost the Krishna-Godavari lands (Maruturu, Kopparam records).

## SUB 1 | Origin, Territory, Seal and the Andhra Links
[1:0] # Territory and emblem
[1:1] - **Tondaimandalam** = Pallava territory (APPSC 2023), capital **Kanchipuram** (between the Pennar and Vellar).
[1:2] - **Royal seal / crest: the bull (Nandi)** (G1 1983-84) – tiger = Chola, boar = Chalukya, fish = Pandya.
[1:3] - **Origin theories:** Parthian (Pahlava), **Andhra** (early Prakrit charters from the Krishna-Guntur region; replaced the Ikshvakus) and local Tamil – **not Scythian**; originally **feudatories of the Satavahanas**.
[1:4] # Charters and the end of the Ikshvakus
[1:5] TABLE head: Charter | Ruler | Point
  [1:5.0] Mayidavolu (Maidavolu) plates | Sivaskandavarman (as yuvaraja) | Prakrit; among the earliest Pallava records
  [1:5.1] Hirahadagalli plates | Sivaskandavarman | Agnishtoma, Vajapeya, Ashvamedha
  [1:5.2] Manchikallu inscription | Simhavarman | Pallava conquest of Vijayapuri after defeating the Ikshvakus
[1:6] - **Manchikallu and Maidavolu = Pallava** inscriptions (G2 Mains 2025) – not Chola, Chalukya or Vijayanagara.
[1:7] - **Manchikallu inscription of Simhavarman** proved that the **Pallavas replaced the Ikshvakus of Vijayapuri** (G2 PYQ) – not the Nasik record, a Nagarjunakonda inscription or the Nandampudi grant.
[1:8] - **Downfall of the Ikshvakus = the Pallavas** (APPSC PYQ): Simhavarman defeated the **last Ikshvaku Rudrapurushadatta**.
[1:9] - **Renati Chodas** = early Pallava feudatories.

## SUB 2 | Pallava Rulers – Order, Events and Nandivarman II
[2:0] # Ruler → event
[2:1] TABLE head: Ruler | Reign (CE) | Event
  [2:1.0] Sivaskandavarman | Early | Kanchi came under the Pallavas
  [2:1.1] Vishnugopa | Early | Faced Samudragupta's invasion
  [2:1.2] Simhavishnu | c. 575–600 | Founder of the imperial line; defeated the Kalabhras
  [2:1.3] Mahendravarman I | 600–630 | Wars with Pulakesin II
  [2:1.4] Narasimhavarman I | 630–668 | Defeated Pulakesin II, destroyed Vatapi (642)
  [2:1.5] Paramesvaravarman I | c. 670–700 | –
  [2:1.6] Nandivarman II | 731–795 | Defeated by Vikramaditya II; Kanchi captured for a time
  [2:1.7] Aparajita | c. 880–897 | Killed by Aditya I Chola (c. 897; some say 903) – end
[2:2] - **Match (G1):** Sivaskandavarman – Kanchi; Vishnugopa – Samudragupta; Narasimhavarman I – Vatapi; Nandivarman II – Vikramaditya II = **A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III**.
[2:3] - **Order:** Simhavishnu → **Mahendravarman I → Narasimhavarman I → Paramesvaravarman I** → Narasimhavarman II (Rajasimha) → **Nandivarman II** (UP BEO 2019: 4, 2, 1, 3).
[2:4] - **Cross-dynasty order (UPSC 2020):** **Mahendravarman I (c. 600) → Gopala (Pala, c. 750) → Bhoja (Pratihara, 836) → Parantaka I (Chola, 907)** = 2-4-1-3.
[2:5] - **Later rulers:** Dantivarman, Nandivarman III, Nripatunga, **Aparajita**.
[2:6] # Nandivarman II Pallavamalla
[2:7] - **Chosen king:** a **boy of 12** from a **collateral line**, **chosen by ministers, nobles and people** after Paramesvaravarman II died (c. 731).
[2:8] - **"Elected" kings (State PSC):** **Gopala** (Pala – Khalimpur inscription) and **Nandivarman Pallavamalla** – not Harsha or Yashovarman.
[2:9] - Ardent **Vaishnava**; built **Vaikuntha Perumal, Kanchi**; contemporary of **Tirumangai Alvar**; title **Pallavamalla**.

## SUB 3 | Kanchi as a Centre of Learning – Ghatikas, Dandin, Bharavi
[3:0] # Ghatika
[3:1] - **Ghatika = a Brahminical (Vedic) college / settlement of Brahmin scholars** (G1 2012; G1 PYQ), i.e. **Hindu colleges** (G1 Prelims 2008) – not merchants, Jain monks, artisans or Buddhist centres; usually **attached to temples**; **Sanskrit** medium.
[3:2] - Andhra link: **Vishnukundins** and **Eastern Chalukya Jayasimha I** (Asanapura ghatika) also set up ghatikas.
[3:3] - **Mayurasharman**, a Brahmin student at the **Kanchi Ghatika**, was insulted by a **Pallava horseman** → founded the **Kadamba** kingdom (**Talagunda inscription**).
[3:4] # Book → author
[3:5] TABLE head: Book | Author | Note
  [3:5.0] Mattavilasa Prahasana | Mahendravarman I | Sanskrit farce
  [3:5.1] Kiratarjuniya | Bharavi | Simhavishnu's court
  [3:5.2] Dashakumaracharita, Kavyadarsha | Dandin | Pallava court (Rajasimha); called Vengi "Andhra Nagaram"
[3:6] - **Dashakumaracharita = Dandin** (UP 2008) – not Bharavi, Bilhana or Somadeva.
[3:7] - **Hiuen Tsang** visited Kanchi **c. 640 CE**, under Narasimhavarman I.
[3:8] - **Grantha** script of the Pallavas = parent of Southeast Asian scripts; the Pallavas pioneered **Dravida** temple art.

## SUB 4 | Narasimhavarman I Mamalla (630–668)
[4:0] - **Titles:** **Mamalla / Mahamalla** ("great wrestler") and **Vatapikonda**; **founded the port city of Mamallapuram (Mahabalipuram)** (Sachivalayam) – not Simhavishnu, Narasimhavarman II or Paramesvaravarman I.
[4:1] - **Wars:** killed **Pulakesin II** at **Manimangala (642)**; general **Paranjoti** sacked **Vatapi**.
[4:2] - **Navy:** **two naval expeditions to Ceylon** (c. 642 onwards) to restore his ally **Manavarma** (UP RO 2016) – not Rajaraja, Kirtivarman I or Jayasimha I.
[4:3] - **Art:** the **monolithic rathas** at Mahabalipuram; a firm Shaiva; Hiuen Tsang visited Kanchi in his reign.
[4:4] ~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval (src/indian-history/pmf-ancient-medieval-03.pdf) p.115; app notes 1:76 (sub 4)
