## SUB 3 | 52nd G7 Summit – Évian-les-Bains, June 2026
[3:0] # The Summit
[3:1] - **52nd G7 Summit: Évian-les-Bains, France** (not Paris), **15–17 June 2026**, hosted by President **Macron**.
[3:2] - Theme: "**Working Together to Address Major International Challenges**".
[3:3] - **India invited for the 13th time**, **7th consecutive year**; other invitees China, South Korea, Kenya, Brazil.
[3:4] - **Outcomes:** G7 Leaders' **Declaration on Securing Supply Chains for Critical Minerals** (through **PGII**); Call on the Fight Against Cancer; Call on a Safer Digital Space for Minors; statement on growth that also sought free transit through the **Strait of Hormuz**; debt-relief support for middle-income countries outside the G20 Common Framework.
[3:5] - **53rd G7: USA, 2027.**
[3:6] # India at the outreach (17 June 2026)
[3:7] - Modi proposed **IMPACT** – **International Mobilisation Partnership for Accelerating Connectivity and Trade**: **G7 capital + India's talent + Global South ownership** (Africa, Latin America, Pacific).
[3:8] - Also proposed a **Global Skills Partnership**; warned of fuel, fertiliser and food shocks from the West Asia crisis (**Strait of Hormuz** disruption – LPG, LNG, diesel, fertilisers).
[3:9] - On the sidelines, Modi met **António Costa and Ursula von der Leyen**: they welcomed the **concluded India–EU FTA** (negotiations concluded at the 16th India–EU Summit, **27 Jan 2026**) and committed to **sign it by end-2026**. India and Canada also aimed at a CEPA by year-end.
[3:10] # G7 and G20 background
[3:11] - **G7**: informal; **no treaty, no permanent secretariat**; **EU** takes part as a **non-enumerated member**.
[3:12] - **G20 Common Framework** (with the **Paris Club**): debt treatment for **Low Income Countries**; G20 members include **Argentina, Mexico, South Africa, Turkey** (not Nigeria).
