=== 18th BRICS Summit – New Delhi, September 2026
# The Summit
- **18th BRICS Summit: Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, 12–13 September 2026** (not 17–19 September); India's **fourth** presidency (earlier 2012, 2016, 2021).
- **Theme:** "**Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability**" – the initials spell **BRICS**.

=== 52nd G7 Summit – Évian-les-Bains, June 2026
# The Summit
- **52nd G7 Summit: Évian-les-Bains, France** (not Paris), **15–17 June 2026**, hosted by President **Macron**.
- Theme: "**Working Together to Address Major International Challenges**".
- **India invited for the 13th time**, **7th consecutive year**; other invitees China, South Korea, Kenya, Brazil.
- **Outcomes:** G7 Leaders' **Declaration on Securing Supply Chains for Critical Minerals** (through **PGII**); Call on the Fight Against Cancer; Call on a Safer Digital Space for Minors; statement on growth that also sought free transit through the **Strait of Hormuz**; debt-relief support for middle-income countries outside the G20 Common Framework.
- **53rd G7: USA, 2027.**
# India at the outreach (17 June 2026)
- Modi proposed **IMPACT** – **International Mobilisation Partnership for Accelerating Connectivity and Trade**: **G7 capital + India's talent + Global South ownership** (Africa, Latin America, Pacific).
- Also proposed a **Global Skills Partnership**; warned of fuel, fertiliser and food shocks from the West Asia crisis (**Strait of Hormuz** disruption – LPG, LNG, diesel, fertilisers).
- On the sidelines, Modi met **António Costa and Ursula von der Leyen**: they welcomed the **concluded India–EU FTA** (negotiations concluded at the 16th India–EU Summit, **27 Jan 2026**) and committed to **sign it by end-2026**. India and Canada also aimed at a CEPA by year-end.
# G7 and G20 background
- **G7**: informal; **no treaty, no permanent secretariat**; **EU** takes part as a **non-enumerated member**.
- **G20 Common Framework** (with the **Paris Club**): debt treatment for **Low Income Countries**; G20 members include **Argentina, Mexico, South Africa, Turkey** (not Nigeria).
