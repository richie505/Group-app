=== India AI Impact Summit, Pax Silica and Tech Alliances
# India AI Impact Summit 2026
- **Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi**, **16–21 February 2026** (Bharat Mandapam also hosted the **2023 G20 Summit**); closed with the **New Delhi Declaration on AI Impact**.
- Attended by **Macron**; India joined **Pax Silica** there.
# India joins Pax Silica (20 February 2026)
- India signed the **Pax Silica Declaration** on **20 February 2026** at the AI Impact Summit, with Union Minister **Ashwini Vaishnaw**; a **US-led, capability-based** alliance.
- Launched in **December 2025** at the inaugural **Pax Silica Summit, Washington D.C.**
- **India is NOT a founding member** – it was invited later by the US.
- India = **first major developing country** and **first non-US-ally strategic partner** to join.
- Name: **"Pax" = peace**; **"Silica" = chip-making compound**.
- Aim: secure supply chains for **critical minerals, semiconductors, AI infrastructure, logistics**; counter **China's rare-earth** dominance.
- **Core (founding) members**: **US, Japan, South Korea, Australia**, UK, Israel, Singapore, UAE, Qatar, Greece.
- **Inaugural observers / guests**: **Canada, EU, OECD, Taiwan**.
- Signatories: about a dozen when India joined; **35 countries (India + 34)** signed the related **Joint Declaration on AI Opportunities** at the 2nd summit (June 2026).
- Related: **Supply Chain Resilience Initiative** (**India–Japan–Australia, 2021**); **MeitY** est. **2016**.
# Trusted Tech Alliance – Munich Security Conference
- **15 global tech companies** (including **Jio Platforms**) formed the **Trusted Tech Alliance (TTA)** at the **Munich Security Conference, 13–15 February 2026**.
- Aim: joint **verifiable standards** for transparency, security, governance, data protection; **five principles**.

=== Alliances and Membership Changes – ISA, IPOI, Arab League
# US exits 66 organisations – impact on ISA
- **January 2026**: the **US withdrew from 66 international organisations** (35 non-UN, 31 UN), including the **International Solar Alliance (ISA)**.
- The US joined ISA late (**2021**); gave ~**$2.1 million** (~1% of ISA funds) – small financial impact; the risk is for **Africa** and small developing economies.
- **ISA**: launched **2015** at **COP21 Paris** by **India and France**; HQ **Gurugram**; first for tropical countries, opened to **all UN members** by a **2020 amendment** (but not all UN members have joined).
- Target: mobilise **$1 trillion** solar investment by **2030**; ISA **does not build plants** – it facilitates finance, de-risking and capacity building.
- India's solar manufacturing (Nov 2025, ALMM list): modules ~**144 GW**, cells ~**25 GW**.
# Spain joins the Indo-Pacific Oceans Initiative (IPOI)
- **Spain** presented its **Accession Declaration** to EAM **S. Jaishankar**.
- IPOI: launched by India at the **East Asia Summit, Bangkok, 2019**; **non-treaty, voluntary**; **seven pillars**.
# Arab League
- India's EAM met foreign ministers of **five Arab League** states.
- Founded **22 March 1945**, **Cairo**; founders **Egypt, Iraq, Transjordan, Lebanon, Saudi Arabia, Syria**; **Yemen** joined **5 May 1945**.
