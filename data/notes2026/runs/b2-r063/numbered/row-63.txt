## SUB 0 | Part XV and the Right to Vote – Articles 324-329, Adult Suffrage
[0:0] # Part XV – Elections (Articles 324-329)
[0:1] TABLE head: Article | Content
  [0:1.0] 324 | Election Commission: superintendence, direction and control of electoral rolls and of all elections to Parliament, State Legislatures, President and Vice-President; 324(2): CEC + other ECs as the President fixes
  [0:1.1] 325 | One general electoral roll for every territorial constituency; no one excluded on grounds only of religion, race, caste or sex
  [0:1.2] 326 | Elections to the Lok Sabha and State Assemblies on the basis of adult suffrage (universal franchise)
  [0:1.3] 327 / 328 | Parliament / State Legislature may make laws on elections
  [0:1.4] 329 | Bar on courts: delimitation laws cannot be questioned; an election can be challenged only by an election petition
[0:2] - Elections are in **Part XV** (not XIV, XVI or XVII). Correct pair: **ECI – Art. 324** (Supreme Court = 124, UPSC = 315, Attorney General = 76); Art. 280 = Finance Commission; **Art. 171** = Legislative Councils, not elections.
[0:3] - ECI set up on **25 January 1950** – permanent constitutional body; its institute **IIIDEM** trains election managers. **Mohinder Singh Gill v CEC (1978)**: Art. 324 is a "**reservoir of power**" where Parliament has not legislated.
[0:4] - **ECI** conducts Lok Sabha, Assembly, Rajya Sabha, Council, **President and Vice-President** polls and prepares **electoral rolls / registers voters**; **State Election Commissions** (Arts. **243K, 243ZA**) run panchayat and municipal polls.
[0:5] - Qualifications/disqualifications: **Art. 84 / 102** (Parliament), **173 / 191** (State Legislatures). Simultaneous polls would need amending **Arts. 83** (duration of Houses), **85, 172, 174, 356**.
[0:6] # Adult suffrage
[0:7] - **Art. 326**: every citizen aged **18+** may vote unless disqualified by law for **non-residence, unsoundness of mind, crime, corrupt or illegal practice** – universal franchise "subject to limitations by statute".
[0:8] - Voting age **21 → 18**: **61st Amendment Act, 1988** (in force **28 March 1989**); 18-year-olds first voted in the **1989** Lok Sabha election (not 59th, 60th, 63rd, 69th or 73rd).
[0:9] - Principles for Lok Sabha/Assembly elections (UPPSC 2011): adult suffrage, **one roll per territorial constituency**, no exclusion on religion, race, sex (Arts. 325-326); the UPPSC key also accepted "parties free to have their own norms" (All four).
[0:10] - Democracy needs **political parties, an electorate, an independent judiciary and universal adult suffrage** (UPSC 1994: all four).
[0:11] - Right to vote and to contest = **statutory rights** (**Kuldip Nayar v UoI, 2006**), not Fundamental Rights; with the right to petition they are **political rights** – the **right to life** (Art. 21) is a civil right.
[0:12] - **Who cannot vote:** a person **confined in prison** (Sec. 62(5) RPA 1951); **preventive detenus** vote by **postal ballot**. **Overseas electors** (Sec. **20A** RPA 1950, 2010): Indian **citizen**, enrolled at the **passport address**, must vote **in person** in India.

## SUB 3 | Delimitation – Commission, Freeze and Amendments
[3:0] # Meaning and basis
[3:1] - **Delimitation** = fixing the **number and boundaries** of Lok Sabha and Assembly constituencies, and the **SC/ST reserved seats**, on the latest **census** – "**one person, one vote, one value**".
[3:2] - The body that decides the number and size of constituencies = **Delimitation Commission** (not the ECI, NITI Aayog or MHA); purpose = revising **constituency** boundaries, **not State boundaries** (Parliament, Art. 3).
[3:3] - Basis: **Art. 82** (Lok Sabha), **Art. 170** (Assemblies) – readjustment after each census; seats go to States on **population, not area**.
[3:4] # Delimitation Commission
[3:5] - Set up by the **Central Government** under an **Act of Parliament**; can be set up for **select States/UTs** (2020: J&K, Assam, Manipur, Arunachal, Nagaland). Nodal ministry: **Ministry of Law and Justice** (Legislative Department).
[3:6] - Members (2002 Act): a retired **Supreme Court judge** (chair), the **CEC** or an EC he nominates, and the **State Election Commissioner** – ex officio.
[3:7] - Orders have the **force of law**: **cannot be questioned in any court** and **cannot be modified** when laid before the Lok Sabha/Assembly (UPSC 2012: both correct).
[3:8] TABLE head: Act | Commission | Chair
  [3:8.0] 1952 | 1952 (first) | Justice N. Chandrasekhara Aiyar
  [3:8.1] 1962 | 1963 | Justice J.L. Kapur
  [3:8.2] 1972 | 1973 | Justice J.L. Kapur
  [3:8.3] 2002 | 2002 | Justice Kuldip Singh
  [3:8.4] J&K | 2020 (orders 2022) | Justice Ranjana Desai
[3:9] - **Four** Delimitation Commissions (1952, 1963, 1973, 2002) till December 2023 (UPSC 2024).
[3:10] # Freeze
[3:11] TABLE head: Amendment | Effect
  [3:11.0] 42nd (1976) | Froze State seat shares on the 1971 Census till 2000
  [3:11.1] 84th (2001) | Freeze extended till the first census after 2026; lifted the freeze for redrawing boundaries (on 1991 Census)
  [3:11.2] 87th (2003) | Boundaries redrawn on the 2001 Census, total seats unchanged
[3:12] - Present constituencies (2001 Census) continue till the **first census after 2026**. Lok Sabha: **543** elected; ceiling **550 (530 States + 20 UTs)**.
[3:13] - AP Reorganisation Act, 2014, **Sec. 26**: AP Assembly **175 → 225**, Telangana 119 → 153, subject to Art. 170 – waits for the **first census after 2026**. The **RPA 1950** deals with **allocation of seats and electoral rolls**.
[3:14] - 2026: **Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill** – Lok Sabha ceiling 550 → **850**; **defeated** in the Lok Sabha for want of a special majority. Southern States fear seats shifting to **UP, Bihar, Rajasthan**; Tamil Nadu Assembly resolution (Aug 2026) sought a permanent freeze at 543 and women's quota from 2029 not tied to delimitation.

## SUB 4 | Electoral Systems and Types of Elections
[4:0] # Who is elected how
[4:1] TABLE head: Office / House | Electorate | Method
  [4:1.0] Lok Sabha / Legislative Assembly | Citizens directly (adult suffrage) | First past the post, single-member territorial constituencies
  [4:1.1] Rajya Sabha | Elected MLAs; seats allotted to States by population | PR – single transferable vote
  [4:1.2] President | Elected MPs + elected MLAs (incl. Delhi, Puducherry – 70th Amendment) | PR-STV
  [4:1.3] Vice-President (Art. 66) | All MPs – elected and nominated; State Legislatures have no role | PR-STV
  [4:1.4] Legislative Council | MLAs, local bodies, graduates, teachers + Governor's nominees | PR-STV
[4:2] - India uses **direct election on adult suffrage** and **PR by single transferable vote**; the **list system** and **cumulative vote** are not used.
[4:3] # First past the post vs proportional representation
[4:4] - **FPTP** (plurality): the candidate with **more votes than any other** wins – no 50% needed. Vote-seat distortion, e.g. **Karnataka 1994**: Janata Dal 116 seats on 36% votes.
[4:5] - **PR** gives seats in proportion to votes → **representation of minorities**; not needed where a **two-party system** has developed (UK). Rejected for the Lok Sabha: complex, fragmentation, **unstable governments**, no by-elections.
[4:6] # Types of elections and special constituencies
[4:7] TABLE head: Type | Meaning
  [4:7.0] General | All seats, at normal end of term
  [4:7.1] Mid-term | After the House is dissolved before its term ends
  [4:7.2] By-election | A single vacancy; within 6 months (Sec. 151A) – not if the remaining term is under one year
[4:8] - **Floating constituency**: no fixed geographical boundary – **Sangha (Sikkim)**: only registered **Buddhist monks** vote and contest (both statements correct).
[4:9] - **Security deposit** is forfeited if a candidate does not get **more than one-sixth** of the valid votes (Sec. 158).

## SUB 5 | EVM-VVPAT, NOTA and Indelible Ink
[5:0] # EVM and VVPAT
[5:1] - EVM = electronic vote-recording and counting device (not an electoral system). **First used: Parur (Kerala), 1982**; older EVMs recorded up to **3,840** votes – current ECI EVMs up to **2,000**.
[5:2] - **VVPAT first used: Noksen Assembly by-poll, Nagaland, 4 September 2013** (not LS 2014, Goa 2017).
[5:3] - **N. Chandrababu Naidu v UoI (2019)**: VVPAT slips tallied in **5 polling stations per Assembly segment**. **ADR v ECI (2024)**: SC rejected **100% VVPAT** counting; 2nd/3rd-placed candidates may seek checks of 5% of EVMs.
[5:4] # NOTA
[5:5] - **PUCL v Union of India, 27 September 2013** – SC ordered a NOTA button on all EVMs (secret negative vote, **Art. 19(1)(a)**). NOTA introduced in **2013** – first used in the **Nov-Dec 2013 Assembly polls**; first Lok Sabha use **2014 = 16th Lok Sabha** (1.1% votes). The 12th-15th Lok Sabha polls had no NOTA, and it was not used in 2009.
[5:6] - Symbol: ballot paper with a **black cross**, designed by **NID Ahmedabad**. NOTA votes are **not counted as valid votes** for result/deposit; even if NOTA tops, the leading candidate **wins** – no fresh poll; using NOTA is **voting, not abstention**.
[5:7] - **Not** for **Rajya Sabha / Legislative Councils** (SC struck it down, **2018**) – only direct elections.
[5:8] - Old **Rule 49-O**: voter refused to vote before the Presiding Officer, noted in **Form 17-A** – broke secrecy.
[5:9] # Indelible ink

  [5:10.2] Chemical / finger | Silver nitrate / left forefinger

## SUB 6 | Model Code of Conduct, Polls and Voter Facilitation
[6:0] # Model Code of Conduct
[6:1] - **ECI guidelines** evolved by **consensus of parties** – **not enacted by Parliament**, not in the Constitution, IPC or RPA (voluntary compliance); authority: **Art. 324**.
[6:2] - In force from the **announcement of the election schedule** (**Harbans Singh Jalal v UoI, 1997**) till results (A true, "enacted by Parliament" false).
[6:3] TABLE head: Year | Milestone
  [6:3.0] 1960 | First drafted and used in the Kerala Assembly election
  [6:3.1] 1968 | Formalised by the ECI
  [6:3.2] 1979 | Part VII ("party in power") added
  [6:3.3] 1991 | Strictly enforced by CEC T.N. Seshan
[6:4] - Part VII: ministers may **not combine official visits with campaigning**; ordinary official visits are allowed.
[6:5] - On violation the ECI can **warn / censure** a candidate or party (it cannot disqualify, cancel results or fine); recognition can be suspended under **Para 16A, Election Symbols Order, 1968**.
[6:6] # Exit polls and opinion polls
[6:7] - **Exit poll** = **post-election survey** of voters just after leaving the booth, by private agencies; **Sec. 126A**: barred from the first phase till **half an hour after the last phase**. **Opinion poll** = pre-poll survey; **Sec. 126**: 48-hour silence. They are not the same.

## SUB 7 | Candidates, RPA Acts, Machinery and Election Petitions
[7:0] # Candidates and the RPA
[7:1] - Lok Sabha nomination: **any citizen of India whose name is on the electoral roll of any constituency**, aged **25+** (residence in that constituency not needed).
[7:2] - **Sec. 8 RPA 1951** (made by **Parliament**): conviction with **2+ years** → disqualified during the sentence + **6 years** after release (not permanently). A non-member minister must be **qualified for membership**, not merely a voter.
[7:3] TABLE head: Basis | RPA 1950 | RPA 1951
  [7:3.0] Deals with | Allocation of seats, delimitation framework, voter qualifications, electoral rolls | Conduct of elections, disqualifications, party registration (Sec. 29A), corrupt practices, election petitions
  [7:3.1] Linked Articles | 81, 82, 170, 171, 325, 326 | 84, 102, 173, 191, 324, 329
[7:4] - **Sec. 14 / 15**: **President** (Lok Sabha) / **Governor** (Assembly) issue the formal notification on ECI's recommendation; the ECI notifies the schedule, scrutinises nominations, allots symbols. **Validity of an election** is settled only by the **courts**.
[7:5] - **Sec. 77-78**: expense accounts to the **DEO** within **30 days**.
[7:6] # Rolls, machinery and parties
[7:7] - Rolls: four **qualifying dates** – 1 Jan, 1 Apr, 1 Jul, 1 Oct (not 1 December); **Special Intensive Revision** rebuilds the roll (**Bihar** SIR removed about 70 lakh names).
[7:8] - Officers: **CEO** (State), **DEO** = usually the **District Collector**, **Returning Officer** conducts the poll and declares the result; observers (Sec. 20B) – general, police, **expenditure**. The ECI controls officers on poll duty.
[7:9] - Parties: **registered** by the **ECI** under **Sec. 29A RPA 1951**; **recognised** by the ECI under the **Election Symbols (Reservation and Allotment) Order, 1968**. National party: State party in **4** States, or 6% votes in 4 States + 4 LS seats, or 2% LS seats from 3 States; State party: 3% of Assembly seats or **3** seats. **Six** national parties (2025): BJP, INC, BSP, CPI(M), AAP, NPP (not TMC). ECI delisted **808** registered unrecognised parties.
[7:10] # Election petitions
[7:11] TABLE head: Section (RPA 1951) | Content
  [7:11.0] 80 | No election can be questioned except by an election petition (Art. 329(b))
  [7:11.1] 80A | Tried by the High Court (original jurisdiction); to try to decide in 6 months
  [7:11.2] 81 | Filed within 45 days of the result
  [7:11.3] 100 | Grounds for declaring an election void (disqualification, corrupt practice, improper rejection of nomination – not a small margin)
[7:12] - Appeal: **Supreme Court**. **President / Vice-President** election disputes: **only the Supreme Court** (Art. 71) – not Parliament or the ECI.
[7:13] - Electoral offences (bribery, undue influence, personation): from **1 July 2024 BNS Chapter IX (Secs. 169-177)**.
