## SUB 0 | Branches of Science and the "-ologies"
[0:0:0] # Kinds of science
[0:0:1] TABLE head: Type | Fields
  [0:0:1.0] Natural | Physics, chemistry, biology, earth science, space science
  [0:0:1.1] Formal | Mathematics, logic, statistics
  [0:0:1.2] Applied | Engineering, medicine, agriculture
[0:0:2] - **Interdisciplinary:** **biotechnology** = biology + engineering; **geoinformatics** (geospatial) = geography + **information technology** (GIS, remote sensing, GPS); **data science** = statistics + computing; **cyber-physical systems** = computing + physical systems via sensors.
[0:0:3] - **Genetic engineering** (rDNA technology) is a branch of **biology** (biotechnology), not of electronics or civil/mechanical engineering (APRO 2008).
[0:0:4] - **ESSO** (Earth System Science Organisation, **2007**) works under the **MoES**: ocean, atmospheric and climate, geoscience, polar and cryosphere science.

  [0:0:6.2] Entomology | Insects (UK UDA/LDA 2003 ×2)
  [0:0:6.3] Ichthyology / Malacology / Parasitology | Fish / molluscs / parasites
  [0:0:6.4] Rheology | Flow and deformation of matter – soft solids and flowing matter

## SUB 1 | Scientific Method and Research Methods
[0:1:0] # Steps of the scientific method
[0:1:1] - **Order:** **observation → question → hypothesis → experiment → analysis → conclusion**, then replication / peer review.
[0:1:2] - **Modern method of acquiring knowledge = the scientific method**; authority, expert opinion and personal experience are traditional sources (HWO).
[0:1:3] - **Theory** = well-tested explanation of **why**; **law** = what always happens, often mathematical (Newton's laws).
[0:1:4] - **⚠ Trap:** a theory does **not** "become" a law.
[0:1:5] # Research designs
[0:1:6] TABLE head: Design | Meaning
  [0:1:6.0] Pilot study | Small study preceding the main study to check its viability
  [0:1:6.1] Action research | Practitioners (teachers) study their own classroom problems to improve practice – Stephen Corey (1953)
  [0:1:6.2] Field experiment | Hypothesis tested in a natural setting
  [0:1:6.3] Clinical / case study | In-depth study of one individual
[0:1:7] > ⚠ Trap: "studying a classroom problem" = **action research**, not field experiment.
[0:1:8] ~~ Sources: app notes 5:0 sub 2; structural-learning.com (action research, Corey 1953); general knowledge (pilot study)

[0:2:2] - **Subjective:** **introspection**, interview, dream analysis (depend on the person's own report).
[0:2:3] - **Simplest form of cognition: sensation** → perception → concept.

## SUB 5 | Science vs Technology; Characteristics of Science
[0:5:0] # Science vs technology
[0:5:1] TABLE head: Basis | Science | Technology
  [0:5:1.0] Nature | Know-why | Know-how
  [0:5:1.1] Output | Laws, theories | Products, processes
[0:5:2] # Research and innovation
[0:5:3] TABLE head: Type | Meaning
  [0:5:3.0] Basic research | Curiosity-driven
  [0:5:3.1] Applied research | Solves a set problem
  [0:5:3.2] Translational | Takes lab results to use
  [0:5:3.3] Invention | First creation of a product or process
  [0:5:3.4] Innovation | Makes the invention useful in market or society
[0:5:4] - **Science:** **empirical, testable (falsifiable), reproducible, systematic, self-correcting**.
[0:5:5] - **Pseudoscience:** claims scientific status but **cannot be tested**.
[0:5:6] - **J.V. Narlikar:** **astrology** is not a science (UPPSC 2018); **astronomy** (celestial bodies), **cosmology** and **nanotechnology (1-100 nm)** are.
