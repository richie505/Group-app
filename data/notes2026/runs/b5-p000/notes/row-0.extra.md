=== Pedagogy – Curriculum, Instruction, Teaching Aids and Measurement
# Models and methods of instruction
- **Carroll's Model of School Learning** (John B. Carroll, 1963): degree of learning = f(time actually spent ÷ time needed); six elements – aptitude, ability to understand instruction, quality of instruction, opportunity to learn, perseverance and the degree of learning; HWO key: **1 output, 1 input, 4 intermediate variables**.
- **Personalized System of Instruction (PSI, "Keller Plan")**: **Fred S. Keller** (1968) – self-paced units, mastery before moving on, proctors; not Froebel (kindergarten) or Pestalozzi.
- **Criticism of the subject-centred traditional curriculum:** **A.N. Whitehead** (The Aims of Education, 1929) attacked "inert ideas" and the fatal disconnection of subjects (HWO key); John Dewey (The Child and the Curriculum, 1902) is the other name books link with child-centred, activity curriculum.
# Classroom interaction and teaching aids
- **Flanders' Interaction Analysis Categories (FIAC):** **10 categories** – 7 teacher talk (4 indirect, 3 direct), 2 pupil talk, 1 silence/confusion.
- **Epidiascope:** projects **both opaque and transparent** objects (episcope = opaque only; diascope / slide projector = transparencies only).
# Measurement scales (S.S. Stevens, 1946)
| Scale | Feature | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Nominal | Names / categories only | Gender, roll number |
| Ordinal | Rank order | Class rank |
| Interval | Equal intervals, no true zero | Temperature °C, IQ |
| **Ratio** | Equal intervals **and a true zero** | **Height, weight, age** |
