## SUB 1 | Scientific Method and Research Methods
[1:0] # Steps of the scientific method
[1:1] - **Order:** **observation → question → hypothesis → experiment → analysis → conclusion**, then replication / peer review.
[1:2] - **Modern method of acquiring knowledge = the scientific method**; authority, expert opinion and personal experience are traditional sources (HWO).
[1:3] - **Theory** = well-tested explanation of **why**; **law** = what always happens, often mathematical (Newton's laws).
[1:4] - **⚠ Trap:** a theory does **not** "become" a law.
[1:5] # Research designs
[1:6] TABLE head: Design | Meaning
  [1:6.0] Pilot study | Small study preceding the main study to check its viability
  [1:6.1] Action research | Practitioners (teachers) study their own classroom problems to improve practice – Stephen Corey (1953)
  [1:6.2] Field experiment | Hypothesis tested in a natural setting
  [1:6.3] Clinical / case study | In-depth study of one individual
[1:7] > ⚠ Trap: "studying a classroom problem" = **action research**, not field experiment.
[1:8] ~~ Sources: app notes 5:0 sub 2; structural-learning.com (action research, Corey 1953); general knowledge (pilot study)
## SUB 2 | Psychology – Methods and Theories of Behaviour
[2:0] # Methods of studying behaviour
[2:1] - **Objective:** **observation**, experiment, tests.
[2:2] - **Subjective:** **introspection**, interview, dream analysis (depend on the person's own report).
[2:3] - **Simplest form of cognition: sensation** → perception → concept.

## SUB 5 | Science vs Technology; Characteristics of Science
[5:0] # Science vs technology
[5:1] TABLE head: Basis | Science | Technology
  [5:1.0] Nature | Know-why | Know-how
  [5:1.1] Output | Laws, theories | Products, processes
[5:2] # Research and innovation
[5:3] TABLE head: Type | Meaning
  [5:3.0] Basic research | Curiosity-driven
  [5:3.1] Applied research | Solves a set problem
  [5:3.2] Translational | Takes lab results to use
  [5:3.3] Invention | First creation of a product or process
  [5:3.4] Innovation | Makes the invention useful in market or society
[5:4] - **Science:** **empirical, testable (falsifiable), reproducible, systematic, self-correcting**.
[5:5] - **Pseudoscience:** claims scientific status but **cannot be tested**.
[5:6] - **J.V. Narlikar:** **astrology** is not a science (UPPSC 2018); **astronomy** (celestial bodies), **cosmology** and **nanotechnology (1-100 nm)** are.
