## SUB 0 | Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code 2016
[0:0] - Enacted **28 May 2016** (in force **2016**; CIRP from 1 Dec 2016) on the **T.K. Viswanathan Committee (2015)**; **debtor-in-possession → creditor-in-control**; replaced SICA 1985 / BIFR.
[0:1] - **Insolvency** = a **financial state** of not paying debts when due; **bankruptcy** = the **legal declaration / process** (assets liquidated or reorganised) that may follow – so insolvency is the state, bankruptcy the conclusion.
[0:2] - **IBBI** (1 Oct 2016) is the **regulator** of insolvency professionals, agencies, information utilities and valuers.
[0:3] - **CIRP:** default **₹1 crore**; **Committee of Creditors** votes on the plan; **180 + 90**, outer **330 days**. **Sec 29A** bars wilful defaulters / NPA promoters. **PPIRP for MSMEs (2021): 120 days**.
[0:4] - Waterfall: resolution costs, **workmen**, secured creditors – all above government dues. **Lalit Kumar Jain (2021)**: personal guarantors stay liable.
[0:5] - **IBC (Amendment) Act 2026:** admit/reject in **14 days**; liquidation **180 + 90**; **CIIRP 150 days**; "may" → "shall" in Sec 7(5)(a) (overturns **Vidarbha Industries**).
[0:6] - **Concerns:** deep haircuts, protracted litigation, **NCLT capacity constraints**; deterrent effect = many cases **settled before admission**; largest recovery channel.
[0:7] > Exam angle: State PSC statement item – only "IBBI is the regulator" is correct; the definitions of insolvency and bankruptcy are swapped in the trap statements.
[0:8] ~~ Sources: Ramesh Singh Part 5 p.93-94; app notes 3:87 sub 4

## SUB 2 | Stressed-Asset Schemes – CDR to S4A, Sashakt and the ICA
[2:0] TABLE head: Scheme | Year | Feature
  [2:0.0] CDR | 2001 | Out-of-court multi-bank restructuring
  [2:0.1] 5/25 | 2014 | Infra loans amortised over 25 years, refinanced every 5
  [2:0.2] SDR | 2015 | Debt → 51%+ equity
  [2:0.3] S4A | June 2016 | RBI scheme to rework the financial structure of large corporates in genuine difficulty: split debt into sustainable and unsustainable parts; ended Feb 2018
  [2:0.4] Project Sashakt (Sunil Mehta) | 2018 | Inter-Creditor Agreement (July 2018, 33 lenders): faster resolution of stressed assets of ₹50 crore+ under consortium lending; binds all once 66% approve
  [2:0.5] Prudential Framework | 7 June 2019 | 30-day review; ICA mandatory; resolve in 180 days
[2:1] - S4A is **not** an ecological costing, a disinvestment plan or an IBC provision; the ICA is **not** a regulator or a fiscal-deficit measure.

## SUB 3 | Prompt Corrective Action
[3:0] - **PCA = Prompt Corrective Action**: an **RBI** framework (2002; revised Nov 2017 and **Nov 2021**) that puts **weak commercial banks with high NPAs / low capital** under watch – not co-operatives, State borrowing or the fiscal deficit.
[3:1] - Triggers (2021): **capital** (CRAR / CET1), **asset quality** (net NPA), **leverage** (Tier 1 leverage ratio). The earlier framework (still in NextGen notes) also used **profitability** (ROA), dropped in 2021.
[3:2] - Curbs: lending, branches, **dividends**, management pay; RBI may order capital infusion, management change, merger.
[3:3] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC 02 p.25-26; app notes 3:87 sub 7

## SUB 5 | SARFAESI Act 2002 and ARCs
[5:0] - Based on the **Narasimham Committee**; lets **secured lenders** enforce security **without court** intervention.
[5:1] - **Sec 13(2):** lenders with **60%** of dues (75% before the **2016** amendment) issue a **60-day** notice; **Sec 13(4):** take **possession**, take over management or **sell**; **Sec 13(8):** redeem only by paying the **full dues**; **Sec 17:** appeal to the **DRT within 45 days**.
[5:2] - **ARCs** (created under SARFAESI) buy NPAs at a discount against security receipts; registered with and regulated by **RBI (not SEBI)**.
[5:3] ~~ Sources: Ramesh Singh Part 5 p.94; Ramesh Singh Part 6 p.100; app notes 3:87 sub 2
## SUB 6 | NARCL and IDRCL
[6:0] - **NARCL** ("bad bank", **Budget 2021-22**) aggregates loans **above ₹500 crore** (~₹2 lakh crore); **PSBs hold majority**.
[6:1] - Pays **15% cash + 85% security receipts**; Govt guarantee **₹30,600 crore for 5 years**. **IDRCL** (majority private) resolves.
[6:2] ~~ Sources: app notes 3:87 sub 6
