=== NPA Definition and Asset Classification
- **NPA:** a loan or advance whose **instalment of principal or interest** stays **overdue for more than 90 days** – it has stopped producing income for the bank (not idle deposits, unused capital or low-interest loans).
- **Farm loans:** short-duration crops = overdue **2 crop seasons**; long-duration crops = **1 crop season**.
| Class | Criterion |
|---|---|
| Standard | Regular; no overdue beyond 90 days |
| SMA-0 / SMA-1 / SMA-2 | Overdue 1-30 / **31-60** / 61-90 days: early warning, **still standard** |
| Sub-standard | NPA for **12 months or less** |
| Doubtful | NPA for **more than 12 months** |
| Loss | Loss identified by the bank, auditors or RBI inspection, but not wholly written off |
- Sequence: Standard → SMA-0/1/2 → NPA (sub-standard → doubtful → loss).
- **Pannir Selvam Committee (1998)** gave a detailed report on the **NPAs of public sector banks** (recovery by arbitration/conciliation).
> Exam angle: 90 days (not 30/60/180); SMA-2 is not an NPA.
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 08 p.5; testbook.com Pannir Selvam question page

=== Stress Indicators and Key Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Gross NPA | All NPAs |
| Net NPA | Gross NPA − provisions |
| Stressed assets | NPAs + **restructured loans** + **written-off assets** |
| Interest coverage ratio (ICR) | **EBIT ÷ interest due**; below 1 = cannot cover interest from earnings; shows the **present and emerging risk** of a borrower a bank is lending to – a **higher** ICR means a **better** ability to service debt |
| Evergreening | Fresh loans to repay old ones → default stays hidden |
- **Balance sheet:** a statement of a firm's **assets and liabilities** on a date – it shows their **size and composition**, not profitability (that is the profit and loss account) or market share.
> Exam angle: UPSC 2020 ICR – statements A and B true; "higher ICR = worse" is false.
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 08 p.5; app notes 3:86 sub 1

=== Recognition and Provisioning
- **IRACP** = **Income Recognition, Asset Classification, Provisioning** (prudential norms from 1992).
- **Income recognition:** interest on an NPA is booked as income **only when actually received**.
- **Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR)** = provisions held ÷ gross NPAs; higher PCR → more resilient bank.
- **From 1 April 2027:** **borrower-level tagging** – one NPA loan → all that borrower's loans at the bank become NPAs; **upgrade only after all arrears** on every facility are paid; 90-day test stays; automated identification.
- **ECL model:** RBI moves from the **incurred-loss** model to a forward-looking **3-stage Expected Credit Loss** model.
| ECL stage | Provision |
|---|---|
| Stage 1 (low risk) | 12-month expected loss |
| Stage 2 (risk rises) | Lifetime expected loss |
| Stage 3 (over 90 days) | Full lifetime provision |
~~ Sources: app notes 3:86 sub 2

=== Twin Balance Sheet Problem
- Meaning: **over-leveraged corporates + capital-starved, NPA-laden banks** at the same time.
- Framed in **Economic Survey 2016-17** (CEA **Arvind Subramanian**), which proposed a public sector asset rehabilitation agency (**PARA**).
- Loan losses eat bank capital → banks stop new loans → firms cannot invest → **private investment falls**.
~~ Sources: Ramesh Singh Part 6 p.77-78; Ramesh Singh Part 5 p.92

=== Causes of NPAs
- **Aggressive lending (2010-15)** to **infrastructure, steel, power** without strict appraisal → stalled projects.
- Global shocks: 2008 crisis + weak exports. **Evergreening** hid defaults.
- Vicious cycle: lending + shocks → NPAs → **provisioning and capital erosion** → weak credit growth.
~~ Sources: app notes 3:86 sub 4

=== Asset Quality Review, 4R and the GNPA Trend
- **AQR by RBI in 2015**: forced recognition; disclosed NPAs jumped, **peaking in March 2018**; exposed the twin balance sheet problem.
- **4R:** **Recognise, Resolve, Recapitalise, Reform**, with the **IBC**, recapitalisation and **NARCL**.
| Date | SCB GNPA |
|---|---|
| Mar 2015 | 4.28% (before AQR) |
| Mar 2018 | ~11.2% peak |
| Sep 2025 | 2.15% |
| Mar 2026 | **1.93%**; NNPA **0.39%** (decadal low) |
- PSBs: 4.97% → 2.58% (Mar 2015 → Mar 2025). Fall due to 4R, higher provisioning, IBC, NARCL.
- **AP angle:** **Andhra Pradesh Grameena Bank** FY26 – GNPA **0.91%**, NNPA **nil**.
