=== Cultural Institutions of AP after 1956 – Ravindra Bharathi, Sahitya Akademi, Telugu Institutes
# Institutions and years
| Institution | Year | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| **AP Sahitya Akademi** | **1957** | Telugu language, literature, translation, awards (G2 Mains 2017) |
| **Ravindra Bharathi**, Hyderabad | **11 May 1961** | Built for **Tagore's** birth centenary (not Gandhi's); inaugurated by Vice-President **Dr S. Radhakrishnan** |
| Nataka Vidyalayam, Vijayawada (Andhra Nataka Kala Parishad) | 1 August 1964 | Drama training school |
| **International Telugu Institute** | **1975** | Set up by the AP Government on a resolution of the first World Telugu Conference |

- **Ravindra Bharathi – inaugurator:** **Dr S. Radhakrishnan**, then Vice-President (G2 Mains 2017 ×2; AP History PYQ) – not Nehru, Indira Gandhi or K.L. Shrimali; opened on **11 May 1961** (Asst. BC Welfare Officers 2017; AP History PYQ) – not 1962, 1963 or 1964.

- **International Telugu Institute:** **1975**, the year of the first World Telugu Conference (Asst. Telugu Translator).
- **1966:** Telugu made the official language of the State (see row 271).

# World Telugu Federation – biennial conferences (a separate, private series)
- Venues: Chennai 1994, Hyderabad 1996, Delhi 1998, Visakhapatnam 2000, **Singapore 2002 (5th)**, **Bangalore 2004 (6th)**, Dubai 2006 (7th); president **V.L. Indira Dutt**.
- **5th conference, December 2002:** **Singapore** (G1 Prelims 2002) – not Kuala Lumpur, Bangkok or Manila.
- **6th conference, 24-26 December 2004:** **Bangalore** (G1 PYQ) – not Hyderabad, Singapore or Mauritius.
# Other literary events in Hyderabad
- **ACLALS Triennial** (Association for Commonwealth Literature and Language Studies), Hyderabad, from **4 August 2004**: chaired by literary critic **Meenakshi Mukherjee** (G1 Prelims 2004).
> Exam angle: do not mix the two series – Kuala Lumpur/Mauritius = World Telugu Conferences; Singapore/Bangalore = World Telugu Federation.

=== National Akademis – the Background
| Akademi | Year / first |
|---|---|
| **Sangeet Natak Akademi** | First national arts academy (**music, dance, drama**); inaugurated by **Rajendra Prasad, 28 January 1953** |
| Sahitya Akademi | Set up 1952, inaugurated **12 March 1954**; a registered society, not statutory |
| Lalit Kala Akademi | 1954; a national academy, NOT an AP award |
- **Body for dance, drama and music:** the **Sangeet Natak Akademi** (UP UDA/LDA 2002) – not the Lalit Kala Akademi (visual arts), Sahitya Akademi (letters) or National School of Drama.
- **SNA golden jubilee (28 January 2003):** chairman then = **Bhupen Hazarika** (chairman c. 1998-2003) (G1 Prelims 2003) – not Kapila Vatsyayan or Ammannur Madhava Chakyar.

=== Theatre and Cultural Awards – Revived and Restructured
# Theatre
- **Surabhi:** family theatre company founded **1885** in **Surabhi village, Kadapa** by **Vanarasa Govinda Rao** and his brother; puppets gradually replaced by live actors.
- **Nandi Natakotsavams:** State drama festivals; revival "on a grand scale" announced (**27 July 2026**).
- **National Theatre Festival** (Sangeet Natak Akademi, **15 July 2026**): **150 locations**, on **150 years of Vande Mataram**; two AP plays selected.
# Bodies abolished, restructured or revived
- **1985:** the **NTR government merged the State akademis** into the new **Telugu University**.
- The previous (**YSRCP**) government let the **Nandi Awards** and **Kandukuri Veeresalingam Awards** lapse.
- The **coalition government** revived the Kandukuri Awards and announced revival of the Nandi Awards and Nandi Natakotsavams (**March 2026**).
- **Telugu Mahotsavam** to be held **annually**: announced at **Undavalli Caves**, **July 2026**, Minister **Kandula Durgesh**.
- **AP cultural awards (July 2026):** **Ugadi Kala Ratna, Kandukuri, Telugu Language, Gurram Jashuva** awards – the Lalit Kala Akademi Award is national, not an AP award.
