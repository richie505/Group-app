## SUB 5 | Theatre and Cultural Awards – Revived and Restructured
[5:0] # Theatre
[5:1] - **Surabhi:** family theatre company founded **1885** in **Surabhi village, Kadapa** by **Vanarasa Govinda Rao** and his brother; puppets gradually replaced by live actors.
[5:2] - **Nandi Natakotsavams:** State drama festivals; revival "on a grand scale" announced (**27 July 2026**).
[5:3] - **National Theatre Festival** (Sangeet Natak Akademi, **15 July 2026**): **150 locations**, on **150 years of Vande Mataram**; two AP plays selected.
[5:4] # Bodies abolished, restructured or revived
[5:5] - **1985:** the **NTR government merged the State akademis** into the new **Telugu University**.
[5:6] - The previous (**YSRCP**) government let the **Nandi Awards** and **Kandukuri Veeresalingam Awards** lapse.
[5:7] - The **coalition government** revived the Kandukuri Awards and announced revival of the Nandi Awards and Nandi Natakotsavams (**March 2026**).
[5:8] - **Telugu Mahotsavam** to be held **annually**: announced at **Undavalli Caves**, **July 2026**, Minister **Kandula Durgesh**.
[5:9] - **AP cultural awards (July 2026):** **Ugadi Kala Ratna, Kandukuri, Telugu Language, Gurram Jashuva** awards – the Lalit Kala Akademi Award is national, not an AP award.
