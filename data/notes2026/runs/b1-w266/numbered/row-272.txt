## SUB 3 | Tribe – Definition, Law and Social Organisation
[3:0] - **Marshall Sahlins:** tribe = **"segmental organisation"** of equal multi-family groups.
[3:1] - **Article 342:** **President** notifies STs after consulting the **Governor**; only **Parliament** amends the list (**342(2)**).
[3:2] - **Census 2011:** ST = **8.6%** of India; **Bhil** largest tribe, then Gond; **Madhya Pradesh** has the largest tribal population (not Odisha).
[3:3] # The Chenchus
[3:4] - **Endogamous tribe**, **exogamous clans** (kulam/gothram); no clan hierarchy; **patriarchal, patrilineal**.

  [3:5.0] Raju | Bhoomani
  [3:5.1] Mantri | Kumakula
  [3:5.2] Kolagadu | Dasari / Uttaluri / Mandla
[3:6] - These three officers, from different clans, **officiate marriages**.
[3:7] - Youth dormitory **Ghotul** = **Muria** of Chhattisgarh (not an AP tribe).
