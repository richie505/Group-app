## SUB 0 | Employees, Acts and State Institutions (2017-2022 Decisions)
[0:0] # Government employees
[0:1] - **Retirement age:** raised **60 → 62** for State government employees – announced **7 January 2022** with the 11th PRC (**23% fitment**); ordinance of 31 Jan 2022, effective **1 January 2022** (earlier 58 → 60 in 2014).
[0:2] - **APSRTC merger:** the **AP State Road Transport Corporation (Absorption of Employees into Government Service) Act, 2019** (Act 36 of 2019; Bill passed 16 Dec 2019) absorbed about **51,500 RTC staff** into a new **Public Transport Department** from **1 January 2020**, on par with government employees.

[0:7] - **Executive Officer, Sec. 29(3):** custody of **records, accounts, jewels**, valuables and funds; **collection of income** and incurring expenditure; sues and is sued; deposits money in bank/treasury; emergency works for the **safety of pilgrims**.
[0:8] - **Not a duty:** '**publication of institutions**' (APPSC Endowment Prelims 2022).
[0:9] # Institutions and policies
[0:10] - **APHRDI** (AP Human Resource Development Institute), the State's apex training institute for officials: at **Bapatla** (the Dr MCR HRD Institute stayed in Hyderabad).
[0:11] - **Sand policy (September 2019):** **APMDC** mines sand and sells it at government **stockyards** (about ₹370 a tonne) from 5 Sept 2019, ending free sand.
[0:12] - **Backward districts:** under the **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 (Sec. 46, 94)** the Centre gives a special development package to the backward districts of **Rayalaseema and north coastal AP**; the Centre's decision to continue it concerns **Andhra Pradesh**.
