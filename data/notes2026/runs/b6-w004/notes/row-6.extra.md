=== New Current Affairs: 5–14 Nov 2026
# GST revenue – October 2026 (State-wise data, Government of India)
| Item | Oct 2025 | Oct 2026 | Growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gross GST** | ₹3,463 crore | **₹4,307 crore** | **24%** (India **16%**) |
| SGST | ₹1,247 crore | ₹1,504 crore | 21% (+₹257 crore) |
| **Net GST** | ₹3,021 crore | **₹3,582 crore** | **19%** (India **13%**) |
| Total Commercial Taxes | ₹4,458 crore | ₹5,329 crore | 20% (+₹871 crore) |
- **AP = highest Gross GST growth among southern States (24%)**, then **Kerala 23%**, Karnataka 18%, Telangana 14%, Odisha 10%, **Tamil Nadu 3%**.
- Net GST growth: AP 19% vs Telangana 17%, Karnataka 16%, Tamil Nadu 8%, Odisha –3%.
- **April–October 2026** cumulative Net GST: **₹23,217 crore** (₹19,781 crore a year earlier) = **+17.4%**; pre-settlement SGST +21% (India 14%).
> ⚠ Trap: 24% is **Gross** GST growth, 19% **Net**, 21% SGST; Kerala (23%), not Karnataka, is second in the south.
# Access (In)Equality Index 2026
- **Access (In)Equality Index (AEI)** – by the **Centre for New Economic Studies (CNES), O.P. Jindal Global University**; first in **2021**; **five dimensions** (basic infrastructure, healthcare, education, socio-economic security, access to justice), **equal weight**; **60 indicators in 23 categories**.
- **2026:** **Goa 1st (0.68)**, **Andhra Pradesh 2nd (0.66)**, **Sikkim 3rd**; AP a **"Front Runner"** State and **1st in socio-economic security (0.69**, Goa 0.50).
- AP is **13th in per capita income** but 2nd in access to services and opportunities.
# Socio-Economic Survey 2025-26 – more data (Nov 2026 news)
- **Fish production 2025-26 (up to Dec 2025):** **47,28,067 t** against an annual target of **66.62 lakh t**. Top 5 districts: **Eluru** (10,39,792 t), **Krishna** (9,95,145 t), West Godavari, Bapatla, SPSR Nellore; bottom: **Chittoor** (1,314 t), Annamayya, YSR Kadapa, Sri Sathya Sai, Ananthapuramu. (News peg: cyclone losses to **shrimp farmers around Kolleru Lake**.)
- **MSME units 2025-26 – top 5 districts:** **Visakhapatnam** (12,334 units; 48,447 jobs), **Palnadu** (11,472), **Ananthapuramu** (10,155), SPSR Nellore (6,936), Guntur (6,542). Net-Zero roadmap covers MSMEs with HT connections above **100 kVA** contract demand.
# Kharif 2026 drought – crop losses in Rayalaseema
- **Kurnool**: about **1.85 lakh farmers**, damage on **1.68 lakh ha**; **Nandyal** 1.64 lakh ha; **YSR Kadapa** 1.49 lakh ha; Anantapur + Sri Sathya Sai about **4 lakh ha**, loss over **₹550 crore**. *(news 10 Nov 2026)*
- **Meteorological drought** = rainfall **25% or more below normal**; agricultural drought = soil moisture too low for crops; hydrological = falling river, reservoir and groundwater levels.
