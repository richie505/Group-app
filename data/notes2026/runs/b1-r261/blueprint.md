## 4. Traps found
- "Andhra Pradesh formed in 1953 following the SRC" – the printed key 1953 is Andhra State; AP followed the SRC on 1 Nov 1956.
- "April 1953" – Andhra State was formed on 1 October 1953 (no printed option was right).
- "Kurnool as capital solves the permanent-capital problem" – the SRC (para 371) said the twin cities of Hyderabad and Secunderabad.
