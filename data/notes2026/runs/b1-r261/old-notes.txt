## SUB 2 | Andhra State 1953
[261:2:0] - **Potti Sriramulu** (Gandhian) fasted from **19 Oct 1952** in Madras; died **15 Dec 1952** after **58 days** → unrest → Nehru announced Andhra State on **19 Dec 1952**.
[261:2:1] - **Andhra State** formed **1 October 1953** from Madras State = **first linguistic state** of independent India.
[261:2:2] - Capital **Kurnool** (Assembly vote 80 to 79 over Vijayawada); first CM **Tanguturi Prakasam**.
[261:2:3] - **Oct 1953**: Nehru called the Visalandhra demand "**Andhra imperialism**".

  [261:2:4.1] Capital | Kurnool | Hyderabad
  [261:2:4.2] First CM | Tanguturi Prakasam | N. Sanjiva Reddy
[261:2:5] - **For** linguistic states: government in the people's language, cultural identity, easier administration (APPSC 2024). **Against**: fear for unity after Partition, regionalism, border and minority disputes.
## SUB 3 | Src – Appointment and Terms
[261:3:0] - **Trigger**: creation of Andhra (1953) set off similar demands everywhere.
[261:3:1] - **Announced 22 December 1953** by Nehru in the Lok Sabha (G2 2017 key); constituted by Home Ministry Resolution **29 December 1953**.

[261:3:4] # Principles
[261:3:5] - Rejected "**one language, one state**"; language **important but not the only factor**.
[261:3:6] - Other factors: **national unity and security**, **administrative efficiency**, **economic and financial viability**.
[261:3:7] - Could split same-language areas into more than one state.

## SUB 5 | Src on Hyderabad / Telangana
[261:5:0] - **Chapter 5** = Hyderabad; **Chapter 6** = Andhra; **paras 369–374** = case for Visalandhra.
[261:5:1] - **Raichur, Gulbarga → Mysore**; **Marathwada → Bombay**.
[261:5:2] - Rejected keeping Hyderabad undivided for the 200-year "**Deccani culture**"; **para 368**: 45.4% Urdu speakers, but **no Union Territory** for the twin cities.
[261:5:3] - **Para 386**: Telangana to function initially as a separate state, **Hyderabad State**, capital Hyderabad.
[261:5:4] - Review: merger **after the 1961 general elections** if **two-thirds** of elected Telangana legislators agree.
[261:5:5] TABLE head: For Visalandhra (paras 369–374) | Against immediate merger
  [261:5:5.0] Large state (~3.2 crore), rich in minerals | Advanced Coastal Andhra would dominate services
  [261:5:5.1] Hyderabad solves Andhra's capital problem | Mulki rules and local jobs at risk
  [261:5:5.2] Unified Krishna–Godavari waters and power | Telangana's revenue surplus diverted
  [261:5:5.3] Telangana's food grains; lower costs | Regional disparities widen
## SUB 6 | Reactions and the Centre's Decision
[261:6:0] - **Andhra**: CM **Bezawada Gopala Reddy's** resolution (Telangana priority, population-based shares) passed **unanimously** (1955).
[261:6:1] - **Communists** consistently backed integrated linguistic states and Visalandhra.
[261:6:2] - **Telangana**: Hyderabad Assembly vote – **103 for**, **29 against**, **15 neutral**; CM **Burgula Ramakrishna Rao** worked to allay fears on Congress High Command's direction.
[261:6:3] - **22 Oct 1955**: Andhra and Hyderabad CMs agreed to merge (Hyderabad House, Delhi).
[261:6:4] - **Nov 1955**: Congress sub-committee – **Nehru, G.B. Pant, Maulana Azad** (Azad kept opposing).
[261:6:5] - **20 Feb 1956**: **Gentlemen's Agreement**, Hyderabad House, New Delhi – **Telangana Regional Council**, surplus revenue spent in Telangana, **Mulki rules** retained.
[261:6:6] - Centre overrode the wait-till-1961 formula by political agreement.
[261:6:7] - **Nehru (1956)**: merger a "**matrimonial alliance**" with a provision for **divorce** – later cited by separate-Telangana advocates.
[261:6:8] "Communists opposed linguistic states" = incorrect (PYQ 2025).
## SUB 7 | States Reorganisation Act 1956
[261:7:0] - In force **1 November 1956**: **14 states + 6 UTs**.
[261:7:1] - Telangana + Andhra State = **Andhra Pradesh** – **first state formed under the SR Act** (not Mysore).
[261:7:2] - Also bilingual Bombay, enlarged Madras, Mysore, Kerala.
[261:7:3] - **APRA 2014** amended **Section 15** of the SR Act to read "Andhra Pradesh and Telangana".
[261:7:4] # Zonal Councils
[261:7:5] - **Five** – Northern, Central, Eastern, Western, Southern; statutory (Part III, Sections 15–22).
[261:7:6] - Chairman: **Union Home Minister** (common to all); Vice-Chairman: a member-state CM by annual rotation.
[261:7:7] - Members: CM + two ministers of each state; two members from UTs. Purpose: inter-state cooperation.
## SUB 8 | Seventh Constitutional Amendment 1956
[261:8:0] - Bill introduced in Lok Sabha **10 Aug 1956**.
[261:8:1] - Abolished **Part A/B/C/D** → **states and Union Territories**; abolished **Rajpramukh**.
[261:8:2] - Inserted **Art 350A** (mother-tongue instruction at primary stage) and **Art 350B** (Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities).
[261:8:3] SRC 1955 | 16 states | 3 UTs
SR Act 1956 | 14 states | 6 UTs
## SUB 9 | Later Bifurcations and Debates
[261:9:0] - **1 May 1960**: bilingual Bombay split into **Gujarat** and **Maharashtra**.
[261:9:1] - **Punjab Reorganisation Act, 1966** (after Shah Commission): **Haryana** from **1 Nov 1966**; hill areas to Himachal Pradesh.
[261:9:2] - **Belgaum** dispute (Maharashtra–Karnataka) continues.
[261:9:3] - Departure from para 386 = core grievance of Telangana movements (**1969, 2001–14**).
