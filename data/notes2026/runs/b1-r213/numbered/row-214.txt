## SUB 0 | Durgabai Deshmukh
[0:0] # Early role (NCM)
[0:1] - **April 1921, Kakinada:** the young Durgabai **translated Gandhi's speech into Telugu**, collected **Rs 5,000** and arranged his meeting with **Devadasis and Muslim women**.
[0:2] - As a volunteer she **stopped Jawaharlal Nehru at the gate** for having no pass.
[0:3] - **CDM:** broke the salt law in **Madras city** with T. Prakasam and K. Nageswara Rao.

## SUB 1 | Maganti Annapurnamma and Gandhi's 1921 Tour
[1:0] - **Gandhi's 1921 coastal tour** (Kakinada, Rajahmundry, Eluru, Vijayawada, Chirala, Nellore) **drew women into the struggle**; he collected the **Tilak Swaraj Fund**.
[1:1] - **Maganti Annapurnamma** (Eluru, wife of **Maganti Bapineedu**): **first Telugu woman to give all her jewellery to the Tilak Swaraj Fund** and **burn foreign goods**; first to **give up foreign cloth and wear khaddar** during Gandhi's visit.
[1:2] - **Yamini Purna Tilakam:** a **dancer (devadasi)** who gave her **entire property** to the fund and followed Gandhi – "as Amrapali followed the Buddha".
[1:3] - **Women leaders of the NCM:** Duvvuri Subbamma, Ponaka Kanakamma, Unnava Lakshmibayamma – orators and organisers.
[1:4] > Repeat (×7): first jewels / first khaddar = **Maganti Annapurnamma**, not Durgabai or Ponaka Kanakamma.
[1:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP History Part 7 p.14, Part 18 p.28, Part 22 p.21; app notes 1:214 (sub 0, 5)
## SUB 2 | Unnava Couple and Sarada Niketanam
[2:0] - **Sri Sarada Niketanam, Guntur:** founded by **Unnava Lakshminarayana** with his wife **Unnava Lakshmibayamma** (G2 2019 key: Lakshminarayana).
[2:1]   - **1902:** the couple ran a **widow asylum**; she held its first widow remarriage. **1922:** Women's Gurukulam, into which the asylum merged.
[2:2]   - She ran the monthly **Sri Sarada Niketanam**.
[2:3] - **Unnava Lakshminarayana** wrote the novel ***Malapalli***; he went to **Palnadu (July 1921)** to study the forest (pullari) satyagraha and was arrested.
[2:4] - **Guntur hartal:** after his arrest, Lakshmibayamma led the Guntur hartals with **Yamini Purna Tilakam**.
[2:5] - **Salt Satyagraha, 9 April 1930:** Lakshmibayamma and Rukmini Lakshmipathi led the women of **Krishna and Guntur**.
[2:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP History Part 7 p.26, Part 19 p.27; app notes 1:214 (sub 3)
## SUB 3 | Duvvuri Subbamma and the Salt Satyagraha Women
[3:0] TABLE head: Woman | Distinctive fact
  [3:0.0] Duvvuri Subbamma | Title "Desabandhavi"; first Andhra woman jailed (first in India: Parvati Devi of Punjab); national school Sanatana Vidyalayam, Rajahmundry; made salt at Chollangi (6 April 1930) → Nellore jail; lathi charge at Peddapuram (QIM 1942)
  [3:0.1] Achanta Rukmini Lakshmipathi | First woman arrested in the Salt Satyagraha (Vedaranyam, 1930); APPSC key: first woman graduate of Andhra
  [3:0.2] Khammampati Manikyamba | Jailed in the Salt Satyagraha with her six-month-old baby
  [3:0.3] Durgabai Deshmukh | Salt Satyagraha in Madras city; Constituent Assembly
  [3:0.4] Dr. Muthulakshmi Reddy | Presided the first Andhra Rashtra Mahila Mahasabha (APPSC match)
  [3:0.5] Ravuri Alivelu Mangatayaramma | One of 12 women jailed at Chirala-Perala (1921)
  [3:0.6] Chitturi Annapurna Devi | Arrested at 17 for distributing pamphlets, QIM (Guntur)
[3:1] - Other CDM women jailed: **Bharati Devi Ranga**, Vallabhaneni Sitamahalakshmamma, Digumarti Janakamma.
