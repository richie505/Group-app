=== Durgabai Deshmukh
# Early role (NCM)
- **April 1921, Kakinada:** the young Durgabai **translated Gandhi's speech into Telugu**, collected **Rs 5,000** and arranged his meeting with **Devadasis and Muslim women**.
- As a volunteer she **stopped Jawaharlal Nehru at the gate** for having no pass.
- **CDM:** broke the salt law in **Madras city** with T. Prakasam and K. Nageswara Rao.

=== Maganti Annapurnamma and Gandhi's 1921 Tour
- **Gandhi's 1921 coastal tour** (Kakinada, Rajahmundry, Eluru, Vijayawada, Chirala, Nellore) **drew women into the struggle**; he collected the **Tilak Swaraj Fund**.
- **Maganti Annapurnamma** (Eluru, wife of **Maganti Bapineedu**): **first Telugu woman to give all her jewellery to the Tilak Swaraj Fund** and **burn foreign goods**; first to **give up foreign cloth and wear khaddar** during Gandhi's visit.
- **Yamini Purna Tilakam:** a **dancer (devadasi)** who gave her **entire property** to the fund and followed Gandhi – "as Amrapali followed the Buddha".
- **Women leaders of the NCM:** Duvvuri Subbamma, Ponaka Kanakamma, Unnava Lakshmibayamma – orators and organisers.
> Repeat (×7): first jewels / first khaddar = **Maganti Annapurnamma**, not Durgabai or Ponaka Kanakamma.

=== Unnava Couple and Sarada Niketanam
- **Sri Sarada Niketanam, Guntur:** founded by **Unnava Lakshminarayana** with his wife **Unnava Lakshmibayamma** (G2 2019 key: Lakshminarayana).
  - **1902:** the couple ran a **widow asylum**; she held its first widow remarriage. **1922:** Women's Gurukulam, into which the asylum merged.
  - She ran the monthly **Sri Sarada Niketanam**.
- **Unnava Lakshminarayana** wrote the novel ***Malapalli***; he went to **Palnadu (July 1921)** to study the forest (pullari) satyagraha and was arrested.
- **Guntur hartal:** after his arrest, Lakshmibayamma led the Guntur hartals with **Yamini Purna Tilakam**.
- **Salt Satyagraha, 9 April 1930:** Lakshmibayamma and Rukmini Lakshmipathi led the women of **Krishna and Guntur**.

=== Duvvuri Subbamma and the Salt Satyagraha Women
| Woman | Distinctive fact |
|---|---|
| **Duvvuri Subbamma** | Title **"Desabandhavi"**; **first Andhra woman jailed** (first in India: **Parvati Devi** of Punjab); national school **Sanatana Vidyalayam**, Rajahmundry; made salt at **Chollangi (6 April 1930)** → Nellore jail; **lathi charge at Peddapuram (QIM 1942)** |
| **Achanta Rukmini Lakshmipathi** | **First woman arrested in the Salt Satyagraha** (Vedaranyam, 1930); APPSC key: **first woman graduate of Andhra** |
| **Khammampati Manikyamba** | Jailed in the Salt Satyagraha **with her six-month-old baby** |
| **Durgabai Deshmukh** | Salt Satyagraha in **Madras city**; Constituent Assembly |
| **Dr. Muthulakshmi Reddy** | Presided the **first Andhra Rashtra Mahila Mahasabha** (APPSC match) |
| Ravuri Alivelu Mangatayaramma | One of **12 women jailed** at **Chirala-Perala (1921)** |
| Chitturi Annapurna Devi | Arrested at **17** for **distributing pamphlets**, QIM (Guntur) |
- Other CDM women jailed: **Bharati Devi Ranga**, Vallabhaneni Sitamahalakshmamma, Digumarti Janakamma.
> Match (APPSC): Muthulakshmi – Mahila Mahasabha; Durgabai – Constituent Assembly; Rukmini – Vedaranyam; Subbamma – Desabandhavi.
