## SUB 0 | Nehru – Profile, Books and Ideas
[0:0] - PM from **15 Aug 1947** till his death on **27 May 1964**; Lok Sabha seat **Phulpur**.
[0:1] TABLE head: Book | Note
  [0:1.0] Glimpses of World History | Letters to Indira from prison (1934)
  [0:1.1] The Discovery of India | Written in Ahmednagar Fort jail (1944)
  [0:1.2] A Bunch of Old Letters | 1958 – edited collection of letters written to and by Nehru
  [0:1.3] Letters from a Father to His Daughter; An Autobiography | –
[0:2] - **Influences:** **Fabian socialism**, **British liberalism**, **Gandhi**, Soviet planning – **not German nationalism** (UKPSC 2006).
[0:3] - **Nehru vs Gandhi:** both stood for truth, non-violence and against untouchability; Nehru alone favoured **heavy industrialisation**, science and central planning – Gandhi favoured a decentralised village and cottage-industry economy (UPPCS 2006).
[0:4] - **Quotes:** "**Success** often **comes to those who dare** and act" – Nehru (AP SI 2018); "Everything else can wait, but not agriculture" – Nehru.
[0:5] - Prison link table: Ahmednagar Fort – Nehru; Yerwada – Gandhi; Mandalay – Tilak; Cellular Jail – Savarkar.
[0:6] > Not German nationalism; heavy industry = Nehru, not Gandhi; A Bunch of Old Letters = 1958.

## SUB 6 | Secularism, the Hindu Code and Language
[6:0] - Indian secularism: **no State religion**; the State is neutral but **may intervene to reform social practices** (unlike strict Western separation).
[6:1] - **Art. 30** – minority educational institutions; **Art. 343** – Hindi in Devanagari, English to continue for **15 years**.
[6:2] - **Ambedkar** – first **Law Minister** (1947); resigned **27 Sep 1951** over the stalled **Hindu Code Bill**.
[6:3] - **Hindu Marriage Act 1955**; Succession, Minority and Guardianship, Adoptions and Maintenance Acts **1956**.
