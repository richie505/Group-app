[1:2] - **NHDR 2001:** AP's HDI (**0.416**) was **less than India's (0.472)**; AP's rank **slipped from 9 to 10** between 1991 and 2001 – "practically no improvement in HDI ranking 1981-2001" despite growth.
[1:3] - **First AP Human Development Report (2007):** prepared by **CESS, Hyderabad** (not the Planning Commission); sponsored by the **Planning Commission and UNDP**; released by CM **Y.S. Rajasekhara Reddy** in **June 2008**.
[1:4] - **Sub-national HDI 2023 (as in the app notes):** India **0.732**; Goa and Kerala above 0.86 (Kerala **2nd**).
[1:5] # HDI method
[1:6] - Published by **UNDP**; first Human Development Report **1990**; conceived by **Mahbub ul Haq** with **Amartya Sen**.
[1:7] - Three dimensions: **health** (life expectancy), **education** (mean + expected years of schooling), **income** (**GNI per capita, PPP**, used as a logarithm).
[1:8] - Since **2010**: dimension indices combined by a **geometric mean**.
[1:9] - HDI hides inequality within a State → **IHDI**, district indices.
[1:10] - **Averaging indicators:** adding a constant to every score raises the **mean by that constant** (+5 → mean +5); the spread (range, SD) is unchanged.
