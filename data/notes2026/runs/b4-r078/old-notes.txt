=== NOTES 4:78 | Human Development in Andhra Pradesh – Indicators and District-level Variation
## SUB 0 | Hdi Basics and AP's Hdi
[78:0:0] - Published by **UNDP**; first Human Development Report **1990**; conceived by **Mahbub ul Haq** with **Amartya Sen**.
[78:0:1] - Three dimensions: **health** (life expectancy), **education** (mean + expected years of schooling), **income** (**GNI per capita, PPP**, used as a logarithm).
[78:0:2] - Since **2010**: dimension indices combined by a **geometric mean**.
[78:0:3] - HDI hides inequality within a State → **IHDI**, district indices.
