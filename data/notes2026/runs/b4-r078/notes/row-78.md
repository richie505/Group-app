# Mission Antyodaya
- **What:** single umbrella **convergence framework** (launched **2017-18**, Ministry of **Rural Development** with Panchayati Raj) to reach **1 crore households in 5,000 rural clusters / 50,000 gram panchayats in 1,000 days**; target "poverty-free" GPs by **2019**, later shifted to **2022** (Poverty Free India by 2022).
- **Annual survey** ranks gram panchayats; **AP's State average score: 61**.
- **Not a healthcare mission**; Rurban = Shyama Prasad Mukherji Rurban Mission (clusters of villages, infrastructure), not the poverty umbrella.

=== AP's HDI – NHDR 2001, AP HDR 2007 and the HDI Method
# National Human Development Report 2001 (Planning Commission)
| Year | AP HDI | AP rank (major States) | India HDI |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1981 | 0.298 | 9th | 0.302 |
| 1991 | 0.377 | **9th** | 0.381 |
| 2001 | **0.416** | **10th** | **0.472** |
- **NHDR 2001:** AP's HDI (**0.416**) was **less than India's (0.472)**; AP's rank **slipped from 9 to 10** between 1991 and 2001 – "practically no improvement in HDI ranking 1981-2001" despite growth.
- **First AP Human Development Report (2007):** prepared by **CESS, Hyderabad** (not the Planning Commission); sponsored by the **Planning Commission and UNDP**; released by CM **Y.S. Rajasekhara Reddy** in **June 2008**.
- **Sub-national HDI 2023 (as in the app notes):** India **0.732**; Goa and Kerala above 0.86 (Kerala **2nd**).
# HDI method
- Published by **UNDP**; first Human Development Report **1990**; conceived by **Mahbub ul Haq** with **Amartya Sen**.
- Three dimensions: **health** (life expectancy), **education** (mean + expected years of schooling), **income** (**GNI per capita, PPP**, used as a logarithm).
- Since **2010**: dimension indices combined by a **geometric mean**.
- HDI hides inequality within a State → **IHDI**, district indices.
- **Averaging indicators:** adding a constant to every score raises the **mean by that constant** (+5 → mean +5); the spread (range, SD) is unchanged.

=== Social Composition, Health, Welfare and Vision Targets
# Social composition
- APSES 2024-25: **SCs 17.08%**, **STs 5.53%** of AP population.
- Census 2011: AP SC population **84.69 lakh** = **4.21%** of India's SCs; highest SC share **Prakasam (23.19%)**, also the largest SC number.
# Health and welfare
- **MMR 30, IMR 19**: AP better on health than literacy. Life expectancy (SRS 2016-20): AP **70.6** years vs India **70.0** (Kerala 75).
| Scheme | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Talliki Vandanam** (successor to Amma Vodi) | **₹15,000 per child per year** to the mother (₹13,000 credited after a ₹2,000 deduction, July 2026); Classes 1-12; **government, aided and private** schools; 75% attendance |
| **Dr. NTR Vaidya Seva** (formerly Aarogyasri) | Cashless care for **1.43 crore families**, 3,257 procedures |
# Vision targets
- **Sunrise AP Vision 2029:** HDI of over **0.9** by **2029**.
- **Swarna Andhra @2047:** GSDP **USD 2.4 trillion**, per capita income over **USD 43,000**, **100% literacy**.
