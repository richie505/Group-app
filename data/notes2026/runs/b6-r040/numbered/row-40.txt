[1:1] - Notified by the Union government; **replaces EPF Scheme, 1952**; in force **29 June 2026** under the **Code on Social Security, 2020**; administered by **EPFO**, Ministry of Labour and Employment.
[1:2] - **Partial withdrawals:** **13 categories to 3 groups** - essential needs (illness, education, marriage), housing needs, special circumstances.
[1:3] - At least **25%** of total balance must remain (max ~75% withdrawable); most withdrawals after 12 months of membership.
[1:4] TABLE head: Purpose | Limit

  [1:4.2] Housing | max 5; up to 75% of balance
