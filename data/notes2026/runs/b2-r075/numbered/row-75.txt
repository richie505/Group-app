[0:5] # Under the 2013 Act
[0:6] - **Section 63**: every State to set up a **Lokayukta** by its **own State law** within **one year (365 days)** of the Act's commencement – each State has **a Lokayukta**, not a Lokpal; the States decide its contours.
[0:7] # Structure, appointment, tenure, report
[0:8] TABLE head: Structure | States
  [0:8.0] Lokayukta + Upa-Lokayukta | Rajasthan, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra
  [0:8.1] Lokayukta only | Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh
  [0:8.2] Officials designated "Lokpal" | Punjab, Odisha
[0:9] - **Appointed by the Governor**, generally after consulting the **Chief Justice of the High Court** and the **Leader of the Opposition** (not the President, CM or CJI).
[0:10] - Term in most States: **5 years or 65 years of age**; **Rajasthan cut the tenure from 8 to 5 years** (2019); the **Narpat Mal Lodha committee** (2014, Rajasthan Advocate General) reviewed the Rajasthan Lokayukta Act.
[0:11] - **Annual consolidated report to the Governor**, who lays it with an explanatory memorandum before the State Legislature (also true of UP) – not to the CM, HC Chief Justice or Speaker.
[0:12] - Recommendations **advisory** in most States, **binding** in some.
[0:13] - Weakness: no uniform law; many lack their own investigating wing and depend on the **State police / ACB**.
[0:14] # Jurisdiction
[0:15] - Looks into **administrative actions** of public servants (corruption, maladministration) – **not judicial or legislative** acts.
[0:16] - **Chief Minister:** included in some States (**Madhya Pradesh, Odisha, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh**), excluded in others (**Maharashtra**, UP, Rajasthan, Bihar, **Andhra Pradesh**).
[0:17] - Ministers and higher civil servants: covered almost everywhere; **MLAs** in some States; local bodies, corporations, societies in most.
[0:18] - **Justice N. Santosh Hegde** (Karnataka Lokayukta): **Bellary illegal iron-ore mining report (2011)** → arrest of former minister **G. Janardhana Reddy**.
[0:19] > Angles: first State (Maharashtra 1971, ×6), appointing/reporting authority (Governor ×4), Uttarakhand 2011 Bill, Rajasthan 8→5, Lodha committee, CM within ambit (MP & Orissa, UPSC 1995).

## SUB 2 | Ombudsman Idea and Recommending Bodies
[2:0] # Origin
[2:1] - **Ombudsman** first created in **Sweden in 1809** (Scandinavia); spread to Finland 1919, Denmark 1955, Norway 1962; **New Zealand 1962** (first Commonwealth); UK Parliamentary Commissioner 1967.
[2:2] - **Indian versions: Lokpal** (Union) and **Lokayukta** (State) – not Lekhpal, Tehsildar or Governor.
[2:3] - Other models: French **Administrative Courts**; **Procurator** system in the USSR/China.
[2:4] - Term **"Lokpal"** coined by **Dr. L.M. Singhvi** in **1963**.
[2:5] - Sweden 1809 = Ombudsman; Sweden **1766** = first right-to-information law.
[2:6] # Committees and commissions
[2:7] TABLE head: Body | Chair | Recommendation
  [2:7.0] Santhanam Committee on Prevention of Corruption (1962-64) | K. Santhanam | CVC (1964) and CBI – not the Lokpal
  [2:7.1] First ARC (1966-70) | Morarji Desai (later K. Hanumanthaiya) | Lokpal + Lokayukta, interim report "Problems of Redressal of Citizens' Grievances" (1966) – first recommended State Lokayuktas
  [2:7.2] Second ARC (2007) | – | Reiterated the Lokpal
[2:8] - ARC: Lokpal to be appointed by the President after consulting the CJI, Speaker and RS Chairman; judiciary kept outside (as in New Zealand).
[2:9] - **⚠ Trap:** the Rajasthan State Administrative Reforms Committee and the Second ARC are distractors for "first recommended Lokayuktas".
[2:10] > Angles: origin country (Sweden/Scandinavia ×3), Indian model = Lokpal, First ARC first recommended Lokayuktas (×3), ARC chair Morarji Desai.
[2:11] ~~ Sources: Laxmikanth 8th ed. ch.66 p.721-724 (laxmikanth-indian-polity-02 p.269-272); Rocket Polity Part 1 p.5; app notes 2:75 sub 0
## SUB 3 | Andhra Pradesh Lokayukta
[3:0] - Law: **AP Lokayukta and Upa-Lokayukta Act, 1983** (amended 2007, **2024**); **Lokayukta** for higher functionaries, **Upa-Lokayukta** for the rest.
[3:1] - **FIRST Lokayukta of integrated AP: Justice A. Sambasiva Rao**.
[3:2] - **Chief Minister is outside its purview:** the Act defines "Minister" as a member **other than the Chief Minister** of the Council of Ministers; it covers **Ministers (other than the CM), MLAs** and State public servants.
[3:3] - **Time bar (Section 8(2))**: no complaint can be investigated after **6 years** from the date of the action complained against (Lokpal: 7 years).
[3:4] - **Vacancy:** when the Lokayukta's office is vacant, the **Upa-Lokayukta** performs its functions as the **Governor** directs (P. Rajani held charge in 2025).
[3:5] - Appointment by the **Governor** on the selection committee's recommendation; **5-year term**.
[3:6] - **Aug 2026**: Justice (Retd) **T. Mallikarjuna Rao** appointed by Governor **S. Abdul Nazeer** under **Section 3(1)**; **5-year term**.
[3:7] - **Seat**: worked from Hyderabad after bifurcation; shifted to **Kurnool** in **2021** (principal seat).
[3:8] - Features: **informal procedure, easy access, almost free and prompt service, independence from executive control** – it is **not an interpreter of the Constitution** (that is the judiciary).
[3:9] TABLE head: Body | Role in AP
  [3:9.0] Lokayukta / Upa-Lokayukta | Statutory ombudsman (1983 Act); inquires, recommends; reports to the Governor
  [3:9.1] State Vigilance Commission | From 1964, on the CVC pattern; vigilance and disciplinary advice
  [3:9.2] Anti-Corruption Bureau | Police agency for PC Act offences (trap and disproportionate-assets cases)
[3:10] > Angles: who is outside (CM), first AP Lokayukta, 6-year bar, features (not interpreter of Constitution), vacancy rule.

## SUB 4 | Lokpal – Composition, Selection, Jurisdiction and People
[4:0] # Composition
[4:1] - **Chairperson + not more than 8 members**; **50% judicial**; at least **50%** from **SC, ST, OBC, minorities and women**; minimum age **45**.
[4:2] - Chairperson: former **CJI**, SC judge, or an eminent person with **25 years'** experience.
[4:3] - Term: **5 years or age 70**; **no reappointment**; cannot be an **MP or MLA**; appointed by the **President**.
[4:4] # Selection Committee (5)
[4:5] TABLE head: Member | Role
  [4:5.0] Prime Minister | Chair
  [4:5.1] Speaker of the Lok Sabha | Member
  [4:5.2] Leader of the Opposition in the Lok Sabha | Member
  [4:5.3] CJI or an SC judge nominated by him | Member
  [4:5.4] Eminent jurist (nominated by the President on the others' recommendation) | Member
[4:6] - **Chairman of the Rajya Sabha is NOT a member**; **Search Committee** of **at least 7 persons** (half from SC/ST/OBC/minorities/women).
[4:7] # Jurisdiction, powers, time limits
[4:8] - Covers the **PM** (except international relations, security, public order, atomic energy, space; inquiry only if **two-thirds** of the full bench approves, in camera), **Ministers, MPs** (not for anything said or votes in Parliament), **Group A–D** central officials (**C and D → CVC**, which reports back), NGOs with foreign contributions **above ₹10 lakh**, public servants **posted abroad**.
[4:9] - **Inquiry Wing (Section 11)** and Prosecution Wing; **superintendence over the CBI only in cases referred by the Lokpal**; attach property **even while prosecution is pending**.
[4:10] - Time limits: preliminary inquiry **90 days** (+90); Inquiry Wing report **60 days**; investigation **6 months + 6 months**; trial **1 year, up to 2**; complaint bar **7 years**.
[4:11] - **Section 17A** belongs to the **PC Act** (prior approval before probe): **22 Jan 2026** SC split verdict – **Justice B.V. Nagarathna** (unconstitutional) vs **Justice K.V. Viswanathan**.
