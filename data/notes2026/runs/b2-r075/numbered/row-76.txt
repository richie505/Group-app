## SUB 0 | CBI – Legal Basis, Director and Units
[0:0] # Legal basis
[0:1] - **Special Police Establishment (1941)** → **Delhi Special Police Establishment (DSPE) Act, 1946** (it constitutes the special police force = today's **CBI**) → **CBI set up 1 April 1963** by a **Ministry of Home Affairs resolution** → **CVC (1964)**.
[0:2] - Set up on the recommendation of the **Santhanam Committee on Prevention of Corruption (1962-64)**.
[0:3] - **Neither constitutional nor statutory**: its powers come from the DSPE Act, 1946; attached office under the **Ministry of Personnel (DoPT)**.
[0:4] - **Founder Director: D.P. Kohli** (1963-68). Motto: **Industry, Impartiality, Integrity**; mission – to preserve values in public life and ensure the health of the national economy.
[0:5] - CBI = "**caged parrot**" (SC, coal allocation case 2013); **Second ARC (2007)**: new law to replace the DSPE Act.
[0:6] # Director
[0:7] - Appointed by the **Central Government** on the recommendation of a **3-member committee**: **PM, LoP in Lok Sabha (or leader of the largest opposition party), CJI or an SC judge nominated by him** – brought by the **Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act, 2013**.
[0:8] - Minimum tenure **2 years**, extendable **one year at a time up to 5 years** (**2021**; same for the **ED Director**).
[0:9] - **Director of Prosecution**: appointed on **CVC recommendation**, **2-year** term; officers of SP rank and above chosen by a **CVC-led** committee.
[0:10] TABLE head: Director | Period
  [0:10.0] D.P. Kohli | First (1963-68)
  [0:10.1] Ranjit Sinha | 2012-14
  [0:10.2] Alok Verma | Feb 2017 – Jan 2019 (exam key of 2017-18)
  [0:10.3] Praveen Sood | From 25 May 2023; extended to May 2025, May 2026 and again to May 2027 (as of 2026)
[0:11] # Vineet Narain (1997) and State consent
[0:12] - **Vineet Narain v Union of India (1997)**: quashed the "**Single Directive**"; gave the **CVC superintendence** over the CBI; **2-year tenure** for the Director; **CBI alone decides** whether to investigate.
[0:13] - **Section 6, DSPE Act**: CBI needs the State's **consent**, as **police** is **State List Entry 2**; **AP withdrew general consent Nov 2018**, restored June 2019.
[0:14] - **Lokpal**: superintendence over the CBI only in cases it refers.
[0:15] # Mandate, units and platforms
[0:16] - **Corruption, economic and special crimes**, mainly **Central Government and UT employees** and their PSUs; there is **no constitutional bar** on its probing economic offences.
[0:17] TABLE head: Unit / platform | Purpose
  [0:17.0] OCSAE Prevention/Investigation Unit (2019) | Online child sexual abuse and exploitation – set up by the CBI (not NIA, NCPCR or CVC)
  [0:17.1] BHARATPOL | Developed by the CBI; launched 7 January 2025 by Home Minister Amit Shah; real-time Interpol requests (not RAW, IB or ED)
  [0:17.2] Interpol | CBI is India's National Central Bureau
[0:18] > Angles: year 1963, founder Kohli, DSPE Act → CBI, present Director (Alok Verma then; now Praveen Sood), OCSAE, BHARATPOL, UPSC 2026 row-match (CBI–DoPT correct).

## SUB 2 | Information Commissions Under the RTI Act
[2:0] - **CIC** constituted **2005** under **Section 12**: CIC + up to **10** ICs; only body that can declare an organisation a "**public authority**".
[2:1] - **No appeal lies against the CIC's order**: its decision on a second appeal is **binding** (Sec 19(7)); it can be challenged only by **writ** (Arts 226/32) – not an appeal to the CAT, HC or SC.
[2:2] - **RTI (Amendment) Act, 2019**: the **Centre fixes tenure and salaries**; **2019 Rules**: term **3 years**. Earlier the State CIC's pay = **Election Commissioner's**.
[2:3] - **Removal of State CIC/IC (Section 17)**: by the **Governor** after a **Supreme Court** inquiry finds proved misbehaviour or incapacity.
[2:4] TABLE head: Section | Content
  [2:4.0] 5 | PIOs designated by each public authority
  [2:4.1] 7 | Reply in 30 days; life or liberty – 48 hours; free for BPL
  [2:4.2] 8(1)(j) | Personal information – DPDP Act 2023 made it an outright exemption
  [2:4.3] 12 | Constitution of the CIC
  [2:4.4] 18 | Complaints
  [2:4.5] 19 | First appeal; second appeal to CIC/SIC within 90 days
  [2:4.6] 20 | Penalty on the PIO: ₹250 per day, maximum ₹25,000
[2:5] - **Economic Survey 2025-26**: called for RTI Act "**re-examination**", a "**ministerial veto**".
