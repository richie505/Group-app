=== State Lokayuktas – Firsts, Appointment, Reports and Jurisdiction
# Firsts and chronology
| State | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Maharashtra** | **FIRST to set up a Lokayukta** – Lokayukta and Upa-Lokayuktas Act, **1971**; came into force **25 October 1972** |
| **Odisha** | **FIRST to pass a law (1970)**, but the institution started only in **1983** – not the first Lokayukta |
| Bihar | 1973 |
| Rajasthan | 1973 |
| Uttar Pradesh | 1975 |
| Madhya Pradesh | 1982 |
| Andhra Pradesh | **1983** |
| Karnataka | **1984** (Karnataka Lokayukta Act; own **Lokayukta Police** wing) |
| Assam | 1986 |
| **Uttarakhand** | First State to pass a Lokayukta Bill in **2011** (1 November 2011) |
- **⚠ Trap:** a question asking which State "constituted the Lokayukta in 1971" = **Maharashtra**; Odisha = first **Act** (1970). Some keys print Orissa – wrong.
- Chronology: Sweden (1809) → first Lokpal Bill (1968) → Maharashtra Lokayukta (1971) → first Lokpal (2019).
- By 2013, **21 States and 1 UT (Delhi)** had Lokayuktas.
# Under the 2013 Act
- **Section 63**: every State to set up a **Lokayukta** by its **own State law** within **one year (365 days)** of the Act's commencement – each State has **a Lokayukta**, not a Lokpal; the States decide its contours.
# Structure, appointment, tenure, report
| Structure | States |
|---|---|
| Lokayukta + Upa-Lokayukta | Rajasthan, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra |
| Lokayukta only | Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh |
| Officials designated "Lokpal" | Punjab, Odisha |
- **Appointed by the Governor**, generally after consulting the **Chief Justice of the High Court** and the **Leader of the Opposition** (not the President, CM or CJI).
- Term in most States: **5 years or 65 years of age**; **Rajasthan cut the tenure from 8 to 5 years** (2019); the **Narpat Mal Lodha committee** (2014, Rajasthan Advocate General) reviewed the Rajasthan Lokayukta Act.
- **Annual consolidated report to the Governor**, who lays it with an explanatory memorandum before the State Legislature (also true of UP) – not to the CM, HC Chief Justice or Speaker.
- Recommendations **advisory** in most States, **binding** in some.
- Weakness: no uniform law; many lack their own investigating wing and depend on the **State police / ACB**.
# Jurisdiction
- Looks into **administrative actions** of public servants (corruption, maladministration) – **not judicial or legislative** acts.
- **Chief Minister:** included in some States (**Madhya Pradesh, Odisha, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh**), excluded in others (**Maharashtra**, UP, Rajasthan, Bihar, **Andhra Pradesh**).
- Ministers and higher civil servants: covered almost everywhere; **MLAs** in some States; local bodies, corporations, societies in most.
- **Justice N. Santosh Hegde** (Karnataka Lokayukta): **Bellary illegal iron-ore mining report (2011)** → arrest of former minister **G. Janardhana Reddy**.
> Angles: first State (Maharashtra 1971, ×6), appointing/reporting authority (Governor ×4), Uttarakhand 2011 Bill, Rajasthan 8→5, Lodha committee, CM within ambit (MP & Orissa, UPSC 1995).

=== Lokpal Bill History and the 2013 Act – Dates
# Bills
- **First Lokpal Bill: May 1968** (introduced **9 May 1968**, 4th Lok Sabha, Indira Gandhi govt); **passed by the Lok Sabha in 1969**, **lapsed** when the Lok Sabha was dissolved before the Rajya Sabha passed it.
- **⚠ Trap: 1968 = introduced; 1969 = passed by the Lok Sabha.** "First introduced" = **1968**.
- Re-introduced in **1971, 1977, 1985, 1989, 1996, 1998, 2001, Aug 2011, Dec 2011** (ten attempts in all – Laxmikanth); many books also list **2005 and 2008** ("1968 … 2001, 2005, 2008" – Indian Express via Wikipedia); the tenth (Lokpal and Lokayuktas Bill, 2011) became the 2013 Act.
| Stage (Lokpal Bill / Lokpal and Lokayuktas Bill, 2011) | Date |
|---|---|
| Lokpal Bill introduced in the Lok Sabha by **V. Narayanasamy**, Minister of State for Parliamentary Affairs (not P. Chidambaram) | **4 August 2011** |
| Referred to the Standing Committee on Personnel, Public Grievances, Law and Justice (chair **Abhishek Manu Singhvi**); civil society called it **"weak"** | Aug–Dec 2011 |
| Lokpal and Lokayuktas Bill passed by the **Lok Sabha**; Rajya Sabha sent it to a Select Committee | **27 December 2011** |
# The Act – dates
| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Passed by the **Rajya Sabha** (with amendments) – first House in 2013 | **17 December 2013** |
| Lok Sabha passed the amended Bill | **18 December 2013** |
| **President's assent** | **1 January 2014** |
| **Came into force** | **16 January 2014** |
| Lokpal began functioning | **27 March 2019** |
- **⚠ Trap:** 1 January 2014 = assent; **16 January 2014 = in force** (G2 Mains 2025).
- **Statutory** body (Act of Parliament), not constitutional.
- The Act also amended the **DSPE Act, 1946**: PM-led committee for the **CBI Director**; **Directorate of Prosecution** for the CBI.
- Original Act: public servant declares assets and liabilities within **30 days** of entering office; **2016 amendment**: form and manner prescribed by the **Central Government**.
> Angles: year/date items dominate (first Bill 1968 ×4, LS passage Dec 2011 ×3, in force 16 Jan 2014 ×3); statement item on the 2011 Bill (introduced 4 Aug 2011, Standing Committee, "weak" → 1, 3, 4).

=== Ombudsman Idea and Recommending Bodies
# Origin
- **Ombudsman** first created in **Sweden in 1809** (Scandinavia); spread to Finland 1919, Denmark 1955, Norway 1962; **New Zealand 1962** (first Commonwealth); UK Parliamentary Commissioner 1967.
- **Indian versions: Lokpal** (Union) and **Lokayukta** (State) – not Lekhpal, Tehsildar or Governor.
- Other models: French **Administrative Courts**; **Procurator** system in the USSR/China.
- Term **"Lokpal"** coined by **Dr. L.M. Singhvi** in **1963**.
- Sweden 1809 = Ombudsman; Sweden **1766** = first right-to-information law.
# Committees and commissions
| Body | Chair | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Santhanam Committee on Prevention of Corruption** (1962-64) | **K. Santhanam** | **CVC (1964)** and **CBI** – not the Lokpal |
| **First ARC** (1966-70) | **Morarji Desai** (later K. Hanumanthaiya) | **Lokpal + Lokayukta**, interim report "**Problems of Redressal of Citizens' Grievances**" (**1966**) – **first recommended State Lokayuktas** |
| Second ARC (2007) | – | Reiterated the Lokpal |
- ARC: Lokpal to be appointed by the President after consulting the CJI, Speaker and RS Chairman; judiciary kept outside (as in New Zealand).
- **⚠ Trap:** the Rajasthan State Administrative Reforms Committee and the Second ARC are distractors for "first recommended Lokayuktas".
> Angles: origin country (Sweden/Scandinavia ×3), Indian model = Lokpal, First ARC first recommended Lokayuktas (×3), ARC chair Morarji Desai.

=== Andhra Pradesh Lokayukta
- Law: **AP Lokayukta and Upa-Lokayukta Act, 1983** (amended 2007, **2024**); **Lokayukta** for higher functionaries, **Upa-Lokayukta** for the rest.
- **FIRST Lokayukta of integrated AP: Justice A. Sambasiva Rao**.
- **Chief Minister is outside its purview:** the Act defines "Minister" as a member **other than the Chief Minister** of the Council of Ministers; it covers **Ministers (other than the CM), MLAs** and State public servants.
- **Time bar (Section 8(2))**: no complaint can be investigated after **6 years** from the date of the action complained against (Lokpal: 7 years).
- **Vacancy:** when the Lokayukta's office is vacant, the **Upa-Lokayukta** performs its functions as the **Governor** directs (P. Rajani held charge in 2025).
- Appointment by the **Governor** on the selection committee's recommendation; **5-year term**.
- **Aug 2026**: Justice (Retd) **T. Mallikarjuna Rao** appointed by Governor **S. Abdul Nazeer** under **Section 3(1)**; **5-year term**.
- **Seat**: worked from Hyderabad after bifurcation; shifted to **Kurnool** in **2021** (principal seat).
- Features: **informal procedure, easy access, almost free and prompt service, independence from executive control** – it is **not an interpreter of the Constitution** (that is the judiciary).
| Body | Role in AP |
|---|---|
| Lokayukta / Upa-Lokayukta | Statutory ombudsman (1983 Act); inquires, recommends; reports to the Governor |
| State Vigilance Commission | From **1964**, on the CVC pattern; vigilance and disciplinary advice |
| Anti-Corruption Bureau | Police agency for **PC Act** offences (trap and disproportionate-assets cases) |
> Angles: who is outside (CM), first AP Lokayukta, 6-year bar, features (not interpreter of Constitution), vacancy rule.

=== Lokpal – Composition, Selection, Jurisdiction and People
# Composition
- **Chairperson + not more than 8 members**; **50% judicial**; at least **50%** from **SC, ST, OBC, minorities and women**; minimum age **45**.
- Chairperson: former **CJI**, SC judge, or an eminent person with **25 years'** experience.
- Term: **5 years or age 70**; **no reappointment**; cannot be an **MP or MLA**; appointed by the **President**.
# Selection Committee (5)
| Member | Role |
|---|---|
| **Prime Minister** | Chair |
| Speaker of the Lok Sabha | Member |
| Leader of the Opposition in the Lok Sabha | Member |
| CJI or an SC judge nominated by him | Member |
| Eminent jurist (nominated by the President on the others' recommendation) | Member |
- **Chairman of the Rajya Sabha is NOT a member**; **Search Committee** of **at least 7 persons** (half from SC/ST/OBC/minorities/women).
# Jurisdiction, powers, time limits
- Covers the **PM** (except international relations, security, public order, atomic energy, space; inquiry only if **two-thirds** of the full bench approves, in camera), **Ministers, MPs** (not for anything said or votes in Parliament), **Group A–D** central officials (**C and D → CVC**, which reports back), NGOs with foreign contributions **above ₹10 lakh**, public servants **posted abroad**.
- **Inquiry Wing (Section 11**; preliminary inquiry into **Prevention of Corruption Act** offences) and Prosecution Wing; **superintendence over the CBI only in cases referred by the Lokpal**; attach property **even while prosecution is pending**.
- Time limits: preliminary inquiry **90 days** (+90); Inquiry Wing report **60 days**; investigation **6 months + 6 months**; trial **1 year, up to 2**; complaint bar **7 years**.
- **Section 17A** belongs to the **PC Act** (prior approval before probe): **22 Jan 2026** SC split verdict – **Justice B.V. Nagarathna** (unconstitutional) vs **Justice K.V. Viswanathan**.

=== Central Vigilance Commission and Whistle Blowers Act
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Origin | **Santhanam Committee on Prevention of Corruption** (chair **K. Santhanam**, MPs + officers); set up **February 1964** by executive resolution |
| Purpose | Apex body to **prevent corruption** in the Central Government; advises and guides central agencies on vigilance |
| Statutory status | **Vineet Narain case (1997)** directed it; **CVC Act, 2003** – at first neither constitutional nor statutory |
| Nature | **Statutory**, multi-member; not constitutional |
| Composition | Central Vigilance Commissioner + not more than **2** Vigilance Commissioners |
| Term | **4 years or age 65** |
| Selection (Sec 4) | **PM** (chair), **Union Home Minister**, **LoP in Lok Sabha**; appointment by the **President** |
| Over CBI | Superintendence over the CBI in **PC Act** cases |
- **CVCs:** K.V. Chowdary (2015-19); **Praveen Kumar Srivastava** (to 2 Aug 2026); **A.S. Rajeev acting CVC from 3 August 2026** (as of 2026).
- **Whistle Blowers Protection Act, 2014** (assent **9 May 2014**): **public interest disclosure** on corruption; **competent authority** for central employees = **CVC**; anonymous complaints not taken up; 7-year bar.
- **⚠ Trap:** Santhanam → CVC/CBI; Kripalani, Gorwala, Kelkar, Swaran Singh, Bakshi Tek Chand → distractors.
> Angles: recommended by Santhanam (×4), year 1964, statutory body, objective = prevent corruption, present CVC.
