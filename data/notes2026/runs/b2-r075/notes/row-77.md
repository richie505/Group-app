=== Planning Commission and the National Development Council
# Planning Commission
- Set up **15 March 1950** by a **resolution of the Union Cabinet / Government of India** – **non-constitutional, non-statutory** (not by an amendment or ordinance); the Constitution provides for the Finance Commission (Art. 280), Election Commission (Art. 324) and UPSC (Art. 315), **not** the Planning Commission.
- **First Chairman: Jawaharlal Nehru** (PM ex officio); first Deputy Chairman **Gulzarilal Nanda**; the Deputy Chairman had **Cabinet Minister** rank.
- Early Five-Year Plans (from 1951, Mahalanobis model in the 2nd) gave the public sector the commanding heights and kept the **market economy at bay**.
- Cabinet repealed the 1950 resolution on **13 August 2014** (Modi govt); **no longer in existence** – replaced by NITI Aayog on 1 January 2015.
# National Development Council
- Set up **6 August 1952** by an **executive order** – **non-constitutional and non-statutory** (calling it statutory is wrong).
- Members: **PM (chairman)**, **Union Cabinet Ministers**, **Chief Ministers of all States**, CMs/administrators of UTs, members of the Planning Commission – **not** the Finance Commission Chairman or the CJI.
- **Secretary: the Secretary of the Planning Commission**; meant to meet at least **twice a year**.
- Role: **coordinator between the Planning Commission and the States**; **final approval of Five-Year Plans**; prescribes **guidelines** for formulating the Plan, **considers/discusses its progress**, and **suggests ways to achieve its targets** – it did not allocate plan money.
- Restructured into NITI Aayog's **Governing Council**.
> Angles: Planning Commission basis (Cabinet resolution ×3), first chairman, NDC date/members/secretary/functions (×8), "not in existence now".

=== NITI Aayog – Status, Structure and Hubs
- Came into force on **1 January 2015** (Cabinet Secretariat notification) by a **Union Cabinet resolution** – **neither constitutional nor statutory** (not an ordinance or Act); a **think-tank**; based on **cooperative federalism**.
- **Chairperson: the Prime Minister** (ex officio – no full-time chairman); **Vice-Chairperson** (Cabinet rank), appointed by the PM; full-time members (MoS rank); ex-officio Union Ministers; a **CEO**.
- **First Vice-Chairman: Arvind Panagariya**; then Rajiv Kumar, Suman K. Bery; **Ashok Kumar Lahiri** from April 2026 (as of 2026).
- Nodal agency for **poverty estimation**.
| Body / hub | Role |
|---|---|
| **Governing Council** | PM, all State CMs, CMs of UTs with legislatures, **LGs of other UTs**, VC, members (no Governors); 11th meeting 11 June 2026 |
| Regional Councils | Regional issues; chair **PM or his nominee** |
| **Team India Hub** | Leads **engagement of States with the Centre** |
| **Knowledge and Innovation Hub** | Think-tank capacity |
| State Support Mission | **SETUs** → State Vision@2047 |
# Planning horizon
- Five-Year Plans replaced by a **15-year Vision**, **7-year Strategy** and **3-year Action Agenda** – **5 years is not** one of them.
> Angles: date 1 Jan 2015 (×3), Cabinet resolution, no full-time chairman, first VC, Team India Hub, 3/7/15 years.

=== NITI Aayog vs the Planning Commission
| Feature | Planning Commission | NITI Aayog |
|---|---|---|
| Approach | **Top-down** | **Bottom-up**, cooperative federalism (Team India) |
| Funds | **Allocated funds** to States | **No fund allocation** (Finance Ministry / Finance Commission) |
| States | Limited role | **Equal partners** |
| Basis | Cabinet resolution 1950 | Cabinet resolution 2015 |
- NITI envisages: **empowered States as equal partners**, a **think-tank** role (domain knowledge, strategy), a **catalyst** role – but **not allocation of resources to needy States**.

=== NITI Aayog Initiatives – Aspirational Districts and Others
| Initiative | Launch | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| **Atal Innovation Mission** | **2016** | Innovation and entrepreneurship |
| Women Entrepreneurship Platform | **2018** | Women entrepreneurs |
| **Aspirational Districts (ADP)** | **January 2018** | **112** districts, **49** indicators, **5** themes |
| Aspirational Blocks (ABP) | Jan 2023 | **513 blocks in 329 districts** (27 States, 4 UTs) |
- ADP themes: **Health and Nutrition 30%**, Education 30%, Agriculture and Water Resources 20%, **Financial Inclusion and Skill Development 10%**, Basic Infrastructure 10% – **Urban Transport and Housing is NOT a theme**; strategy = **Convergence, Collaboration, Competition**.
- **AP**: original – **Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam, YSR Kadapa**; now **Alluri Sitarama Raju, Parvathipuram Manyam, YSR Kadapa** (Kurnool, Anantapur are NOT); AP Reorganisation Act 2014 package for 7 backward districts (**4 Rayalaseema** + 3 North Coastal).
