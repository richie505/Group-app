=== CBI – Legal Basis, Director and Units
# Legal basis
- **Special Police Establishment (1941)** → **Delhi Special Police Establishment (DSPE) Act, 1946** (it constitutes the special police force = today's **CBI**) → **CBI set up 1 April 1963** by a **Ministry of Home Affairs resolution** → **CVC (1964)**.
- Set up on the recommendation of the **Santhanam Committee on Prevention of Corruption (1962-64)**.
- **Neither constitutional nor statutory**: its powers come from the DSPE Act, 1946; attached office under the **Ministry of Personnel (DoPT)**.
- **Founder Director: D.P. Kohli** (1963-68). Motto: **Industry, Impartiality, Integrity**; mission – to preserve values in public life and ensure the health of the national economy.
- CBI = "**caged parrot**" (SC, coal allocation case 2013); **Second ARC (2007)**: new law to replace the DSPE Act.
# Director
- Appointed by the **Central Government** on the recommendation of a **3-member committee**: **PM, LoP in Lok Sabha (or leader of the largest opposition party), CJI or an SC judge nominated by him** – brought by the **Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act, 2013**.
- Minimum tenure **2 years**, extendable **one year at a time up to 5 years** (**2021**; same for the **ED Director**).
- **Director of Prosecution**: appointed on **CVC recommendation**, **2-year** term; officers of SP rank and above chosen by a **CVC-led** committee.

# Vineet Narain (1997) and State consent
- **Vineet Narain v Union of India (1997)**: quashed the "**Single Directive**"; gave the **CVC superintendence** over the CBI; **2-year tenure** for the Director; **CBI alone decides** whether to investigate.
- **Section 6, DSPE Act**: CBI needs the State's **consent**, as **police** is **State List Entry 2**; **AP withdrew general consent Nov 2018**, restored June 2019.
- **Lokpal**: superintendence over the CBI only in cases it refers.
# Mandate, units and platforms
- **Corruption, economic and special crimes**, mainly **Central Government and UT employees** and their PSUs; there is **no constitutional bar** on its probing economic offences.
| Unit / platform | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **OCSAE Prevention/Investigation Unit (2019)** | Online child sexual abuse and exploitation – set up by the **CBI** (not NIA, NCPCR or CVC) |
| **BHARATPOL** | Developed by the **CBI**; launched **7 January 2025** by Home Minister **Amit Shah**; real-time Interpol requests (not RAW, IB or ED) |
| Interpol | CBI is India's **National Central Bureau** |
> Angles: year 1963, founder Kohli, DSPE Act → CBI, present Director (Alok Verma then; now Praveen Sood), OCSAE, BHARATPOL, UPSC 2026 row-match (CBI–DoPT correct).

=== Status of the Bodies and Who Appoints Whom
# Status
| Body | Set up | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| CBI | 1 April 1963, MHA resolution | Non-statutory; DSPE Act 1946 |
| CVC | 1964, executive resolution | Statutory from CVC Act, 2003 |
| CIC / SICs | 12 Oct 2005, RTI Act s.12 / s.15 | Statutory |
| NITI Aayog | 1 Jan 2015, Cabinet resolution | Non-constitutional, non-statutory |
| SHRC | Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993 | Statutory |
- **NITI Aayog, SHRC, CVC and CIC are all NOT mentioned in the Constitution** (answer: all four).
- None of the CBI, CVC or NIA prefers impeachment charges – that is done by **either House of Parliament**.
# Appointing authorities
| Post | Appointed by | Committee |
|---|---|---|
| **Chief Information Commissioner / ICs** | **President** | PM, LoP in Lok Sabha, Union Cabinet Minister nominated by PM |
| **State CIC / State ICs** | **Governor** | CM, LoP in Assembly, Cabinet Minister nominated by CM (no Chief Justice) |
| Central Vigilance Commissioner | President | PM, Union Home Minister, LoP in Lok Sabha |
| CBI Director | Central Government | PM, LoP, CJI or his nominee |

- **Public Information Officers** are designated by **each public authority** (Section 5) – not by the Supreme Court; first appellate authority is a senior officer of the public authority – not elected by Parliament.

=== Information Commissions Under the RTI Act
- **CIC** constituted **2005** under **Section 12**: CIC + up to **10** ICs; only body that can declare an organisation a "**public authority**".
- **No appeal lies against the CIC's order**: its decision on a second appeal is **binding** (Sec 19(7)); it can be challenged only by **writ** (Arts 226/32) – not an appeal to the CAT, HC or SC.
- **RTI (Amendment) Act, 2019**: the **Centre fixes tenure and salaries**; **2019 Rules**: term **3 years**. Earlier the State CIC's pay = **Election Commissioner's**.
- **Removal of State CIC/IC (Section 17)**: by the **Governor** after a **Supreme Court** inquiry finds proved misbehaviour or incapacity.
| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| 5 | PIOs designated by each public authority |
| 7 | Reply in **30 days**; life or liberty – **48 hours**; free for **BPL** |
| 8(1)(j) | Personal information – **DPDP Act 2023** made it an outright exemption |
| 12 | Constitution of the CIC |
| 18 | **Complaints** |
| 19 | First appeal; second appeal to CIC/SIC within **90 days** |
| 20 | Penalty on the PIO: ₹250 per day, maximum **₹25,000** |
- **Economic Survey 2025-26**: called for RTI Act "**re-examination**", a "**ministerial veto**".
