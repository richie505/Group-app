## SUB 5 | E-panta and Urea Use
[8:5:0] - Rabi 2025-26: cropped area **+36%**, urea availability only **+3%**.
[8:5:1] - Urea per hectare **513 kg → 338 kg (~34% cut)**.
[8:5:2] - Driver: **e-Panta** digital crop mapping - farmers **17.56 lakh → 35.51 lakh**; area 46.07 lakh → 73.55 lakh acres (~60%).
[8:5:3] # Fertilizer Facts
[8:5:4] - **Urea: 46% nitrogen**; only 30-40% absorbed by crops.
[8:5:5] - Ideal **N:P:K = 4:2:1**.
[8:5:6] - DAP = N + P; **MOP (Muriate of Potash) = potassic** (not phosphatic); SSP = phosphatic.
[8:5:7] - **Chlorophyll - nitrogen and magnesium** (not phosphorus); phosphorus - roots and flowering.
[8:5:8] - **Rhizobium** - legumes; **zinc sulphate** is a chemical micronutrient fertilizer, not a biofertilizer.
