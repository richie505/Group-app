## 2. Angles
- **Factual recall / number:** all four PYQs (count of Sutralu, year of an Act, a percentage).
- **Trap options:** near numbers (5/7/9/10 Sutralu; 2000/2001/2003/2005 for FRBM – 2000 = Bill, 2005 = AP Act).
- **Current linkage:** Swarna Andhra @2047 is the framework of the AP Budgets 2025-26 and 2026-27 and of the Socio-Economic Survey; expect more state-vision and budget-figure questions.

## 3. Weight and what the notes do
- Real PYQ weight is tiny (2 on-topic PYQs); the rows are driven by the app's 219 own MCQs on budget figures, deficits, district indicators, rankings and schemes.
- Sub-sections are ordered so that the PYQ-tested items come first in their rows: **Swarna Andhra Sutralu** (row 10, sub 0) and **Deficits/FRBM** (row 11, sub 0).
- Dropped from the old notes as never tested and not needed by any MCQ: empty "APPSC Previous Year Questions" tables, duplicate "Number game" and "MCQ traps" lists (traps now sit beside the fact), rubber climate and area detail, egg/drip sub-figures, ITR slab tables, crime-against-children counts, HSRP features, KPI mid/low department lists.
- Two different 2026-27 growth targets exist in the sources: **17.1%** (Sept 2025 Q1 review) and **15.53%** (Socio-Economic Survey 2025-26, GSDP ₹20.36 lakh crore). Both are kept, each with its date.
