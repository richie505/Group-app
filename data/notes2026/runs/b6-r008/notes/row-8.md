=== Urea, e-Panta and Fertilizer Facts
- **Rabi 2025-26:** cropped area **+36%**, urea availability only +3%.
- **Urea per hectare:** **513 kg → 338 kg** (~34% cut).
- **Driver:** **e-Panta** digital crop mapping – farmers 17.56 lakh → 35.51 lakh.
# Fertilizer basics
- **Urea: 46% nitrogen**; ideal **N:P:K = 4:2:1**.
- DAP = N + P; SSP = phosphatic; **MOP (Muriate of Potash) = potassic, not phosphatic**.
- **Chlorophyll:** **nitrogen and magnesium** (not phosphorus).
- **Rhizobium:** biofertilizer of **legumes**.

=== Rabi Paddy Procurement and Rubber in Polavaram
# Rabi paddy procurement 2026
- Target **23 lakh MT**, from 4 April 2026; **1,917** centres; payment within **24 hours**.
- **Rabi:** sown October–December, harvested **March–April** (Kharif: June–July / September–October).
# Rubber for tribals
- **Ministry of Tribal Affairs** proposed rubber over **3,000 acres** in **Polavaram district** for the **Konda Reddi** tribe; support from Rubber Board + ITDA.
- **Rubber Board:** **Rubber Act, 1947**; **Ministry of Commerce and Industry**; HQ **Kottayam**, Kerala.
- **Vulcanisation:** heating rubber with **sulphur** (not carbon) – hardens it and improves elasticity and strength.
| Scheme | Key point |
|---|---|
| Rubber Production Incentive Scheme (RPIS) | Minimum price **₹150/kg** |
| **Rubber Plantation Development Scheme** | Traditional + non-traditional areas |
| Proposed PLI | **Rubber goods manufacturing** |
