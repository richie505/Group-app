=== The Survey and Its Headline Numbers
# The document
- Prepared by the **Planning Department**, Government of AP; by convention placed in the Legislature **before / alongside the State Budget**.
- **SES 2024-25:** last chapter "**Progress Monitoring – KPIs and SDGs**". **SES 2025-26:** **11 chapters**, last = "**Data-Driven Governance**"; growth = **First Advance Estimates (FAE)**.
- **Swarna Andhra @2047** (13 Dec 2024): GSDP target **₹308.11 lakh crore by 2047-48**.

- **2024-25 FRE:** GSDP **₹15.91 / ₹8.67 lakh cr** (current / constant); largest current GVA = **Services ₹6.11 lakh cr**; AP **8th** by GSDP.
- **Sub-sectors 2025-26:** fastest agriculture = **Fishing and aquaculture (17.69%)**, slowest **Forestry**; fastest services = **Banking**, slowest **Public Administration**. APSES 2024-25 GVA shares: **Manufacturing and Horticulture 10% each**.
- **Deficits:** 2025-26 RE revenue deficit **₹41,118 cr (2.33% of GSDP)**, fiscal deficit 4.57%; RD near ₹48,000 cr once the **16th FC ended revenue-deficit grants**. The fiscal deficit is always larger than the revenue deficit (2024-25 RE: RD ₹48,311 cr, FD ₹73,362 cr).
