=== Structure of the Act – Parts, Sections, Schedules
# The Act at a glance
- **Size:** **12 Parts, 108 Sections, 13 Schedules** – Act **No. 6 of 2014** (Gazette of India Extraordinary **No. 6**, 1 March 2014).
- **Popular name:** the "**Telangana Act**" (bifurcation Act); made under **Articles 3 and 4**.
- **Character:** passed by Parliament although the AP Legislature had rejected the Bill; widely criticised as hurriedly drafted ("arbitrary, does not clearly address many issues").
| Part | Title | Sections |
|---|---|---|
| I | Preliminary (definitions: appointed day, **population ratio 58.32 : 41.68**) | 1–2 |
| **II** | **Reorganisation of the State of Andhra Pradesh** | 3–11 |
| III | Representation in the Legislatures | 12–29 |
| IV | High Court | 30–43 |
| **V** | **Authorisation of Expenditure and Distribution of Revenues** | 44–46 |
| VI | Apportionment of Assets and Liabilities | 47–67 |
| VII | Provisions as to Certain Corporations | 68–75 |
| **VIII** | **Provisions as to Services** (not water) | 76–83 |
| **IX** | **Management and Development of Water Resources** | 84–91 |
| X | Infrastructure and Special Economic Measures | 92–94 |
| XI | Access to Higher Education | 95 |
| XII | Legal and Miscellaneous Provisions | 96–108 |
- **⚠ Trap:** Part VIII = Services; water resources = **Part IX**; Part V = expenditure and revenues (not Part IV, which is the High Court).
> 12 Parts – 108 Sections – 13 Schedules; II reorganisation, V revenues, IX water.

=== Enactment Timeline and the Appointed Day
| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Bill introduced in Lok Sabha (Sushilkumar Shinde) | **13 February 2014** |
| **Passed by Lok Sabha** | **18 February 2014** |
| **Passed by Rajya Sabha** (Upper House) | **20 February 2014** |
| **President's assent** (Pranab Mukherjee) and Gazette publication (Act 6, Gazette No. 6) | **1 March 2014** |
| Notification fixing the appointed day | 4 March 2014 |
| **Appointed day** – Telangana formed as the **29th State** | **2 June 2014** |
- **Parliament passed it in February 2014; the President signed it in March 2014.**
- **"Appointed day" (Sec 2(a)):** the day the **Central Government appoints by notification in the Official Gazette** – fixed as 2 June 2014 (the notification itself was issued on 4 March 2014).
- The State was bifurcated in **June 2014**; residuary AP continues the State of 1956 – only Telangana was new.
- **Sec 10:** First Schedule of the Constitution amended – new entry "**29. Telangana**" (India now has 28 States after J&K became a UT in 2019).
> LS 18 Feb – RS 20 Feb – assent 1 Mar – appointed day 2 Jun 2014.

=== Part II – Telangana, Common Capital, Governor and Police
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| **3** | Formation of **Telangana** – **10 districts** incl. Hyderabad, Medak, Karimnagar, Khammam (minus Polavaram villages); amended by the 2014 Polavaram Ordinance/Act to move **7 Khammam mandals** to AP |
| 4 | Residuary AP – the remaining territories (**13 districts**, e.g. Srikakulam) |
| **5** | **Hyderabad common capital** for a period **not exceeding ten years**; then capital of Telangana, AP to get a **new capital**; common capital = area notified as **GHMC** |
| **6** | Central **expert committee** on AP's new capital – report within **six months** of enactment |
| **7** | Governor of existing AP to be **common Governor** for a period **determined by the President** |
| **8** | Governor's **special responsibility for security of life, liberty and property** of residents of the common capital (law and order, internal security, vital installations, Government buildings) |
| **9** | **Central Government** to assist both States to raise additional police; Greyhound Training Centre |
| 10 | First Schedule of the Constitution – Telangana as entry 29 |
- **Sec 8(3):** Governor acts in his **individual judgment after consulting the Telangana Council of Ministers**; his decision in his discretion is **final**.
- **Sec 8(4):** assisted by **two advisors** appointed by the **Central Government**.
- **Sec 9(2):** Centre to maintain the **Greyhound Training Centre, Hyderabad** as a common centre for **three years**, then it goes to Telangana; **9(5):** **Greyhound and OCTOPUS** forces divided **on options from personnel**.
- **Hyderabad's status:** it lay in Telangana's territory (not common territory); it was AP's **de jure** capital till 2 June 2024 while AP worked from Amaravati; tax revenue of Hyderabad is **not shared** with AP.
- **⚠ Trap:** the common-capital limit is "not exceeding 10 years"; the common Governor's period is set by the President, not by the Act.
> Sec 5 capital ≤10 yrs (GHMC); 6 committee 6 months; 7 common Governor; 8 Governor + 2 advisers; 9 police, Greyhounds 3 yrs.
~~ Sources: AP Reorganisation Act 2014 Gazette text pp.2-4, ss.3-10; job b1-r263 notes row-263 (Polavaram ordinance)

=== Part III – Seats in Parliament and the Legislatures
| House | AP | Telangana | Section |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rajya Sabha | **11** | **7** | 12 (Fourth Schedule of Constitution), 13 + First Schedule of the Act |
| Lok Sabha | **25** | **17** | **14** |
| Legislative Assembly | **175** | **119** | 17 |
| Legislative Council | **50** (not more than) | **40** | 22, 23 |
| Assembly after delimitation | **225** | **153** | **26** |
- **Sec 15:** Delimitation of Parliamentary and Assembly Constituencies Order, **2008** amended (Second Schedule).
- **Sec 18:** Governor may nominate **one Anglo-Indian** MLA in each State (Art 333) – ended by the **104th Amendment** (2020).
- **Sec 22:** Legislative Councils (AP not more than 50, Telangana 40); existing members allotted by the **Fourth Schedule**.
- **Sec 26:** seats to rise to **225 / 153** subject to **Art 170**; under **26(2)(a)** all constituencies are **single-member** (not Sec 22).
- **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Act, 2015:** AP Council raised from **50 to 58** (Art 171: one-third of 175).
- AP's 175 seats: **29 SC, 7 ST**; most seats East Godavari, fewest Vizianagaram (9) in 2014.
- Constitution Schedules amended by the Act: **First** (States) and **Fourth** (Rajya Sabha seats).
> 25 + 17 Lok Sabha; 175/119 Assembly → 225/153; Council 50 (58 from 2015)/40; Sec 18 Anglo-Indian.
~~ Sources: AP Reorganisation Act 2014 Gazette text pp.4-7, ss.12-26; prsindia.org billtrack AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Bill 2015; old app notes 3:133

=== Part IV – High Court
- **Sec 30:** the **High Court of Judicature at Hyderabad** = **common High Court** for both States till AP's High Court is constituted (named "High Court of Judicature at Hyderabad for the State of Telangana and the State of Andhra Pradesh"); judges' salaries shared by population ratio.
- **Sec 31:** a **separate High Court for AP**; the Hyderabad court becomes Telangana's; **31(2): principal seat at such place as the President notifies** – the Act names **no place**.
- **Presidential order of 26 December 2018** (Art 214) → **High Court of Andhra Pradesh at Amaravati** functioning from **1 January 2019**.
> Sec 30 common HC; Sec 31 separate AP HC; Amaravati, 1 Jan 2019.
~~ Sources: AP Reorganisation Act 2014 Gazette text pp.8-9, ss.30-31; job b1-r263 notes row-263; old app notes 3:133

=== The Thirteen Schedules
| Schedule | Content |
|---|---|
| **First** (s.13) | Allocation of **sitting members of the Council of States** (Rajya Sabha) |
| **Second** (s.15) | Amendments to the **Delimitation of Parliamentary and Assembly Constituencies Order, 2008** |
| Third (s.24) | Modification of the Delimitation of **Council** Constituencies Order, 2006 |
| **Fourth** (s.22) | **List of members of the Legislative Councils** of the two States |
| **Fifth** (s.28) | SC Order 1950 amended – item 9 **Beda (Budga) Jangam** deleted from AP's list (it stays in Telangana's list) |
| Sixth (s.29) | ST Order 1950 amended |
| **Seventh** (s.52) | **List of Funds** – A. **Provident, Pension and Insurance Funds** (incl. **Central Government Employees Group Insurance Scheme**); B. Sinking, reserve funds – "Industrial Development Funds" incl. **Sericulture Development Fund**; C. other funds incl. **C.M. Relief Fund** |
| **Eighth** (s.59) | Apportionment of **pension** liability |
| **Ninth** (ss.68, 71) | Government **companies and corporations** (e.g. SCCL, **Sports Authority of AP**) |
| **Tenth** (s.75) | **Training institutions/centres** – continuance of facilities; **107** institutions, mostly in Hyderabad (e.g. Telugu and Hindi Academies) |
| Eleventh (s.85) | Principles for the **River Management Boards** (drinking water first) |
| **Twelfth** (s.92) | **Coal, Oil and Gas, Power** – SCCL equity **51% Telangana, 49% GoI**; APERC joint 6 months; SLDC shared 2 years |
| **Thirteenth** (s.93) | Central commitments – **education** institutions and **infrastructure** (within 10 years) |
- **Tenth Schedule (s.75):** terms between the two States to be agreed within **one year**; the **State Information Commission** is not listed, APPSC is dealt with by s.83.
> I RS members, II delimitation, IV councils, VII funds, IX corporations, X institutions (107), XII coal-oil-power, XIII commitments.

=== Capital Question After 2014
- **Sivaramakrishnan Committee** (Sec 6) on the new capital; AP chose **Amaravati** (2015).
- Common capital ended on **2 June 2024**; Hyderabad is now Telangana's capital only.
- AP Assembly resolution of **28 March 2026** asked to insert "**at Amaravati**" in Sec 5(2); the **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Act, 2026** named **Amaravati** the sole capital with effect from **2 June 2024** – the first law in which Parliament named a State's capital. AP High Court capital order: 3 March 2022.
