[133:0:0] - Full name: **Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014** (Act **No. 6 of 2014**).
[133:0:1] - Divided the old State of Andhra Pradesh into **2 States**: **Telangana** and residual **Andhra Pradesh**.
[133:0:2] - Structure: **12 Parts**, **108 sections** and **13 Schedules**.
[133:0:3] - **Part V:** authorisation of **expenditure and distribution of revenues**; Part VI assets and liabilities; Part VIII services.

=== NOTES 3:134 | Special Assistance and the A.p. Reorganisation Act Commitments
## SUB 0 | Background – the Act and Its Promises
[134:0:0] - **AP Reorganisation Act (APRA), 2014**: President's assent **1 March 2014**; appointed day (bifurcation) **2 June 2014**.
[134:0:1] - Residuary AP got about **46% of estimated revenue** at bifurcation but carried a **larger share of debt**.
[134:0:2] - **Census 2011 population ratio**: AP **58.32** : Telangana **41.68** – the basis of most liability splits.
[134:0:3] - Promises are in the **sections** (finance, Polavaram, capital, Governor) and in the **Thirteenth Schedule** (education and infrastructure).
[134:0:4] - **3 March 2022**: AP High Court order on the capital.
[134:0:5] 58.32 : 41.68 = population ratio; 46% = AP's share of revenue; 58% population got 46% of power.

## SUB 1 | Special Category Status (scs)
[134:1:0] # Origin and Benefits
[134:1:1] - SCS began in **1969** (**Fifth Finance Commission** era); first given to **Jammu and Kashmir, Assam and Nagaland**.
[134:1:2] - Benefits: Centrally Sponsored Schemes funded **90:10** (Centre : State), against **60:40** for other States; **concessional loans**; **tax breaks**.
[134:1:3] # 14th Finance Commission Position
[134:1:4] - The **Fourteenth Finance Commission (2015)** removed the **special/general category distinction** in tax devolution.
[134:1:5] - AP was given **special packages instead of SCS**.
[134:1:6] - **Demand history**: AP, **Bihar** and **Odisha** still demand SCS (position as of January 2026).
[134:1:7] SCS is NOT granted by the APRA 2014. The Act has no SCS clause; the demand rests on assurances outside the Act.
## SUB 2 | Special Assistance Measure (package in Lieu of Scs)
[134:2:0] # Components
[134:2:1] - Approved by the **Union Cabinet on 15 March 2017**.
[134:2:2] - **Component 1 – Externally Aided Projects (EAPs)**: Centre repays **loans and interest** of EAPs signed in **2015-16 to 2019-20**; Centre bears **90%** (not 60%) of EAP cost.
[134:2:3] - **Component 2 – Polavaram**: Centre funds **100% of the remaining cost of the irrigation component** for the period from **1 April 2014** (cost as on that date; ⚠ APPSC key (2017) gave 'as the cost is incurred').
[134:2:4] # SCS vs Special Assistance

  [134:2:5.3] Status | Ended by 14th FC (2015) | Cabinet decision (2017)
