[1:0] TABLE head: Scientist | Associated with
  [1:0.0] Edward Jenner (1749–1823) | Father of immunology; smallpox vaccine 1796 – the first vaccine and first attempt at vaccination; proposed the immunisation concept
  [1:0.1] Jonas Salk | First successful polio vaccine (1952); Albert Sabin – oral polio vaccine
  [1:0.2] Louis Pasteur | Rabies vaccine, germ theory; live attenuated fowl cholera vaccine (1879-80)
  [1:0.3] Robert Koch | Bacteriology, TB bacterium – NOT father of immunology
  [1:0.4] Elie Metchnikoff | Phagocytosis, cellular immunity
[1:1] - **DPT** = diphtheria + pertussis + tetanus – a **combined vaccine**; MMR = measles, mumps, rubella; **BCG** – TB, just after birth.

[1:3] - **Tetanus** – bacterium *Clostridium tetani* (contaminated wounds), not *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* (TB).
[1:4] - **Biojector:** a **painless needle-free injector** (used for ZyCoV-D).
[1:5] - **World Immunization Week:** **24–30 April** (last week of April).

## SUB 3 | SARS-CoV-2 and COVID-19 Basics
[3:0] - Virus = **SARS-CoV-2**; disease = **COVID-19 ("Coronavirus Disease 2019")** – the WHO name for the disease caused by the **2019 novel coronavirus (2019-nCoV)**; it caused the COVID-19 global pause (not SARS-CoV-1 or MERS).
[3:1] - First identified: **Wuhan, Hubei, China, December 2019**; MoHFW screened flights for **novel coronavirus (nCoV)** illness in January 2020.
[3:2] - **SARS** is caused by a **coronavirus**; **MERS, SARS and COVID-19** are all coronavirus diseases.
[3:3] - Enters human cells via the **ACE2 receptor**; reservoir bats, **pangolin** suspected intermediate host.
[3:4] - **Viruses** are acellular – no cells and no metabolic enzymes of their own (unlike lichen, bacteria, algae).
