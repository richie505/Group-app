## SUB 0 | HIV/AIDS – Facts, Tests, Drugs and Programmes
[0:0] - **HIV causes AIDS**; first recognised **1981**; dominant strain in India **HIV-1 subtype C**.
[0:1] - **Transmission**: sexual contact, **blood transfusion**/needles, **mother to child across the placenta** (and breastfeeding) – **not by mosquitoes or other blood-sucking insects**.
[0:2] - **Symptoms of AIDS**: swollen lymph nodes, night sweats, weight loss, and neurological problems incl. **memory loss** – all four of the classic PYQ list.
[0:3] - **ELISA** = first widely used HIV screening test (detects antibodies); **Zidovudine (Azidothymidine, AZT)** = the most used anti-retroviral. No licensed HIV vaccine.
[0:4] TABLE head: Programme / term | Fact
  [0:4.0] NACP (NACO, MoHFW) | Launched 1992; Phase V 2021–26 (80% cut in new infections and deaths)
  [0:4.1] VCTC | Voluntary Counselling and Testing Centre (now ICTC – Integrated Counselling and Testing Centre)
  [0:4.2] 'Test and Treat' (2017) | Every HIV-positive person gets free ART regardless of CD4 count
  [0:4.3] Project Sunrise (2016) | HIV/AIDS among people who inject drugs in the North-Eastern States
  [0:4.4] HIV/AIDS Act 2017 | Bars discrimination against people living with HIV
  [0:4.5] Red Ribbon Express / AVAHAN | Awareness train / Gates Foundation prevention initiative
[0:5] - **Sub-Saharan Africa** = region with the most HIV/AIDS cases (UNAIDS 2003 and since); UNAIDS set up 1996; 95-95-95 target.
[0:6] - **15th International AIDS Conference**, Bangkok, July 2004 – slogan **"Access for All"**.
[0:7] - India: highest burden Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka. World AIDS Day 1 December.

## SUB 2 | Tuberculosis – NTEP, Ni-kshay and TB Reports
[2:0] - India topped the world in new TB cases (WHO, **2016** and since); TB is the **leading single infectious killer** (WHO).
[2:1] - National target: TB elimination **2025** (SDG 2030). **BCG** protects against TB.
[2:2] TABLE head: Scheme / term | Feature
  [2:2.0] Ni-kshay | Central TB Division's web-enabled patient management system (notification, tracking)
  [2:2.1] Ni-kshay Poshan Yojana (2018) | Nutritional DBT for the whole duration of anti-TB treatment – ₹500/month, raised to ₹1,000 from Oct 2024
  [2:2.2] PM TB Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan (Sep 2022) | Ni-kshay Mitras adopt patients
  [2:2.3] India TB Report 2022 | Notifications in 2021 up 19% over 2020
  [2:2.4] India TB Report 2024 | Estimated incidence 2023 ≈ 27–28 lakh (between 25 and 30 lakh); 25.52 lakh notified
  [2:2.5] WHO Global TB Report 2025 | India incidence 237 → 187 per lakh (-21%), 2015–2024; mortality 28 → 21 per lakh (-25%); treatment coverage 53% → 92%; 26.18 lakh diagnosed of ~27 lakh
[2:3] - World TB Day 24 March. AP notified over 78,000 cases in 2024.

## SUB 3 | Malaria – Elimination, Quinine and Vaccines
[3:0] - **National Framework for Malaria Elimination 2016–2030**: zero indigenous cases by **2027**, elimination by **2030**. **MERA India** (ICMR) supports the 2030 goal.
[3:1] - **Quinine** comes from the **bark of Cinchona**.
[3:2] - Vaccines: **RTS,S/AS01 (Mosquirix, GSK)** – first; **R21/Matrix-M** – **Oxford Jenner Institute + Serum Institute of India** (Novavax adjuvant); AdFalciVax – ICMR-RMRC Bhubaneswar candidate.
[3:3] - NVBDCP tools: IRS, LLINs, larvivorous fish, source reduction (not antibiotic prophylaxis). WHO malaria-free: Maldives (first in SEAR), Sri Lanka (2016), China, Timor-Leste.
[3:4] ~~ Sources: NextGen Rocket Biology p.32 (malaria vaccines), p.33 (quinine), p.75 (Cinchona bark); Rocket CA 2026 Part 02 p.111-112 (targets 2027/2030); app notes
## SUB 4 | Vaccines in News – HPV/Cervavac, Hib and Serum Institute
[4:0] - **Cervavac** (Sep 2022): India's **first indigenous quadrivalent HPV vaccine** against **cervical cancer** – **Serum Institute of India** with **DBT-BIRAC** (not Indian Immunologicals).
[4:1] - India carries about **one-fifth of the global cervical-cancer burden** (~1.23 lakh cases, ~67,000 deaths a year).
[4:2] - Nationwide free **HPV vaccination** for 14-year-old girls: launched **28 Feb 2026, Ajmer** (single dose).
[4:3] - **Serum Institute of India** is in **Pune** (Hib, MR, Covishield, R21).

## SUB 5 | Sickle Cell, Leprosy and Other Elimination Missions
[5:0] - **Sickle cell anaemia**: a **congenital, inherited** single-gene disorder (haemoglobin) – misshapen red cells cannot carry oxygen properly. **National Sickle Cell Anaemia Elimination Mission**: launched **1 July 2023, Shahdol (MP)**; eliminate by **2047**; screen **7 crore** at-risk (tribal) people.
[5:1] - **Leprosy**: NLEP gives free MDT; **World Leprosy Day = last Sunday of January**; Anti-Leprosy Day (India) **30 January**.
[5:2] - Lymphatic filariasis 2027 (IDA = Ivermectin, DEC, Albendazole); measles-rubella 2026; kala-azar target met 2023.
[5:3] - **Elimination targets**: TB 2025, measles-rubella 2026, lymphatic filariasis 2027, malaria 2030, AIDS as a public health threat 2030, sickle cell 2047. **IDSP** (2004, under NHM) = outbreak surveillance, not an NCD target.
[5:4] ~~ Sources: NextGen Rocket Biology p.34 (sickle cell – inherited); app notes
