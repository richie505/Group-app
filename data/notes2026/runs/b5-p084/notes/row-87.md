=== Biosphere Reserves and the MAB Programme
# Concept (most asked)
- **Biosphere Reserve (BR):** large terrestrial/coastal/marine area recognised under **UNESCO's Man and the Biosphere (MAB) Programme** (launched **1971**; not IUCN); UNESCO calls BRs "learning places for sustainable development".
- **Purpose:** conserve the **genetic diversity** of the whole landscape **together with the traditional life of local/tribal people** – so the "most important strategy for conservation of biodiversity together with traditional human life" is the **biosphere reserve**, not an NP, sanctuary or botanical garden.
- Designation **does not change legal status or ownership**; a BR may contain one or more NPs/sanctuaries. **IN-MAB Committee (MoEFCC)** recommends sites; State prepares the management plan; funding **60:40** (**90:10** for NE and three Himalayan States). **Seville Strategy (1995)** added social, cultural and economic needs.
- Not a protected-area category under the WPA 1972 (those are NP, sanctuary, conservation reserve, community reserve).
| Zone | What is allowed |
|---|---|
| Core | Strictly protected (usually NP/WLS); **no human activity** |
| Buffer | Limited activity: research, education, training, limited recreation |
| Transition | **Settlements**, farming, forestry, tourism |

=== In-situ and Ex-situ Conservation
| Basis | In-situ (on-site) | Ex-situ (off-site) |
|---|---|---|
| Where | Within the **natural habitat** | Outside it, in a controlled setting |
| Examples | **National park, wildlife sanctuary, biosphere reserve**, reserved/protected forest, nature reserve, **sacred groves** | **Botanical garden**, zoo, safari park, **seed/gene bank**, **cryobank (cryopreservation)**, tissue culture, captive breeding |
| Saves | Whole ecosystem and its genetic diversity | The species or its genes |
- "Not an in-situ site" = **Botanical Garden** (UPSC 2011, UPPCS 2018). Genetic diversity in **National Parks** is maintained by **in-situ conservation**.
- **In-situ is generally most effective:** species keep interacting with their environment; ex-situ helps education, research and reintroduction but needs space and money and cannot save the ecosystem.
- **Captive breeding** for species whose habitat is lost, e.g. Gangetic gharials bred and released; 10 captive-bred **Asian Giant Tortoises** released in **Ntangki NP (Nagaland)**.
- **Meristem culture** = virus-free propagation. "Biological diversity" coined by **Raymond F. Dasmann**.
> Exam angle: "which is NOT in-situ / which is ex-situ" – botanical garden, zoo, seed bank and cryopreservation are ex-situ.

=== Protected-Area Categories, Eco-sensitive Zones and Global Coverage
| Category (WPA 1972) | Key feature |
|---|---|
| **National Park** | Declared by State (Centre also may); **no rights, no grazing, no collection of biomass/forest produce**; boundary altered only on NBWL recommendation |
| **Wildlife Sanctuary** | Some rights allowed; grazing and firewood collection regulated by the **Chief Wildlife Warden** |
| Conservation Reserve | 2002/2003 amendment; **government land**, usually adjoining/linking PAs |
| **Community Reserve** | 2002/2003 amendment; **private or community land** a community volunteers; declared by the **State Government**; managed by a **Community Reserve Management Committee** (not the Chief Wildlife Warden); sanctuary restrictions (no hunting) apply; residents' rights not affected |
- Local people may **not collect biomass in National Parks** (they may in BR transition zones, Ramsar wetlands and, regulated, in sanctuaries).
- **Network (2026):** **1,134 PAs** (981 in 2020): 109 NPs, 573 WLS, 127 conservation, 75 community reserves; ~**5.32%** of India's area; 58 tiger reserves. First community reserve: **Keshopur-Chhamb (Punjab)**. Biosphere reserve is NOT a PA category.
# Eco-sensitive Zones (ESZ)
- **Purpose:** **"shock absorbers"** / transition zones around NPs and sanctuaries (MoEFCC guidelines), to minimise forest depletion and human–animal conflict.
- Notified under the **Environment (Protection) Act, 1986** – **not the WPA 1972**; they **do not ban all activity except agriculture** (only mining, polluting industry, major hydro etc. are prohibited; hotels, tree felling regulated; agriculture, organic farming permitted) → UPSC 2014: **neither statement correct**.
- **Wildlife Action Plan 2002-16** / MoEF 2011 guidelines: up to **10 km**; **Supreme Court (June 2022):** minimum **1 km** ESZ, no mining inside PAs or within 1 km.
# Global coverage
- **2014:** protected areas covered **15.2% of land** and **8.4% of coastal-marine areas** (marine areas under national jurisdiction).
- **30x30** = Target 3 of the **Kunming-Montreal GBF** (2022): 30% of land and oceans by 2030; **OECMs** (e.g. Aravalli Biodiversity Park, Gurugram, India's first, 2022) count towards it. **WDPA** is run by **UNEP-WCMC** with IUCN.
> Exam angle: NP = no biomass collection; ESZ = shock absorber under EP Act; community reserve statements; 2014 coverage figure.

# Marine conservation
- **First marine sanctuary and first Marine National Park:** **Gulf of Kachchh** (Jamnagar, Gujarat; corals, molluscs, dolphins, turtles, sea birds) – "the Marine/Oceanic National Park" of India = **Kachchh**.
- **Gulf of Mannar Marine NP** (Tamil Nadu) is a marine NP; Bhitarkanika, Gahirmatha and Sundarbans are mangrove/turtle areas, not marine NPs.
- **National Centre for Marine Biodiversity (NCMB):** India's first, at **Jamnagar**, Gujarat.
- India has identified **106 Important Coastal and Marine Biodiversity Areas (ICMBAs)**; **Naupada** ICMBA (swamp) is in **Srikakulam**, AP.
- **ICMAM** (Integrated Coastal and Marine Area Management) Project Directorate: **Chennai** (MoES; launched 1998).
- Palk Bay (448 km²) = first **Dugong Conservation Reserve**; A&N and Lakshadweep = India's first **Hope Spots** (2013, Mission Blue).
> Exam angle: which NP is in which State/zone; first marine NP = Gulf of Kachchh; NCMB Jamnagar; 106 ICMBAs.

=== Gene Banks, Seed Vaults and Cryopreservation
- **Cryopreservation:** in **liquid nitrogen at −196 °C**; the gas used in cryo-banks is **nitrogen**; metabolism stops. Stores gametes, embryos, seeds, pollen, tissues, DNA – **semen straws / cattle sperm for artificial insemination** are kept in liquid nitrogen.
- Seeds keep longest in **cool and dry** conditions (low moisture and temperature slow respiration and fungi).
| Facility | Location / fact |
|---|---|
| **Svalbard Global Seed Vault** | **Spitsbergen, Norway**; world's largest secure seed vault, in permafrost (~−18 °C) |
| **Chang La seed vault** | High-altitude pass in **Ladakh**; joint venture of **NBPGR** and **DIHAR (DRDO)** |
| National Gene Bank | **ICAR-NBPGR, New Delhi** |
| **International Rice Genebank** | **IRRI, Los Baños, Philippines** |
| Field gene bank | NBRI's **Banthara** (Lucknow) – preserves endangered plants, identifies useful wild plants, checks biopiracy |
| Millennium Seed Bank | Kew (Wakehurst), UK |
- APPSC G1: all three seed-vault statements (Svalbard, Chang La, NBPGR+DIHAR) are **correct** → "none incorrect".
- ICAR bureaux: NBAGR **Karnal** (livestock), NBFGR **Lucknow** (fish), NBAIM **Mau** (microbes); 2nd National Gene Bank (Budget 2025-26): 10 lakh lines. **LaCONES** (CCMB Hyderabad) = India's frozen zoo.
> Exam angle: −196 °C / liquid nitrogen (asked four times), Svalbard = Norway, rice gene bank = Philippines.

=== Community Conservation – Sacred Groves and the Bishnois
- **Sacred groves:** forest patches preserved by communities on **religious belief** (trees and wildlife venerated) – a **traditional method of conserving tree cover**; an **in-situ** practice, comparable to today's **biosphere reserves** (community-protected nature).
| Local name | State |
|---|---|
| Devrai / Devrahati | Maharashtra |
| Kavu | Kerala |
| Orans | Rajasthan |
| Law Kyntang / Law Lyngdoh | Meghalaya |
| Devarakadu | Karnataka |
| Sarna | Jharkhand |
- **Bishnois, Khejarli (Jodhpur), 1730:** **Amrita Devi** and **363** Bishnois hugged khejri trees and were killed by the king's men.
- **Amrita Devi Bishnoi Wildlife Protection Award** (GoI): individuals/communities from **rural areas** showing courage in protecting wildlife; **Rs 1 lakh** cash.
> Exam angle: sacred groves = traditional/in-situ conservation; Bishnois tree-hugging; award description.
