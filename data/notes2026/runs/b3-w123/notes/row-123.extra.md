=== SHG–Bank Linkage and SERP – Figures Asked (2016-17)
# SERP SHGs and bank credit
- **SHGs organised by SERP:** about **7 lakh** (AP Budget 2017-18).
- **Exclusive women SHGs:** about **90%** of the SHGs credit-linked with banks (31-3-2017).
- **Bank loans availed by SERP-guided SHGs, 2016-17:** about **₹12,000 crore** from commercial banks.
- **SHG loans outstanding to banks (31-3-2017):** about **₹61,600 crore**.
- **AP's rank** in the share of SHGs credit-linked to banks out of all SHGs: **second** among States (2017 key).
# Ratings, Stree Nidhi and interest support
- **'A' grade rating:** an SHG must follow the **Pragatiki Padi Sutralu** (ten principles for progress).
- **Stree Nidhi:** began **commercial operations in October 2011** (6 October 2011).
- **Stree Nidhi recovery rate:** **94.4%** of loans (as on 31-10-2016).
- **YSR Sunna Vaddi** (zero-interest loans to SHGs, 2019-20): about **6,32,254 rural SHGs** to benefit.
