=== SHG Movement – SERP, Velugu/IKP, Stree Nidhi, MEPMA
| Year | Programme | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| Sep 1982 | **DWCRA** | Development of Women and Children in Rural Areas; Centre:State 75:25 |
| 2000 | **Velugu** | Women's groups against poverty; World Bank-aided; **SERP** registered 2000 |
| 2004 | **Pavala Vaddi** | Interest subsidy for **SHGs** (3% effective) |
| 2004-05 | **Indira Kranthi Patham (IKP)** | Velugu + DWCRA **merged** – all anti-poverty programmes in one integrated scheme; **flagship of SERP** |
| 2011 | **Stree Nidhi** | Credit cooperative federation promoted by **Mandal Samakhyas + GoAP** |
- **SERP** = Society for Elimination of Rural Poverty (rural); **MEPMA** = Mission for Elimination of Poverty in Municipal Areas (**urban** SHGs).
- **SHG registration:** **no registration is mandatory** (informal groups of 10-20).
- **NRLM "Institutions of the Poor":** **SHGs and their federations** (VOs, MS) – not PACS or RRBs.
- **AP leads India** in SHG-bank linkage: **36%** national share, **99.66%** recovery (2024-25). Members lack individual **CIBIL** histories.
- **MEPMA limits:** SHG bank linkage up to **Rs 20 lakh**; individual loans **Rs 50,000-2 lakh**.
# Stree Nidhi
- **Streenidhi Credit Cooperative Federation Ltd** (2011): **banking institution promoted by SHG women**; a **community-based micro-finance** body of the Mandal Samakhyas with GoAP; registered under the AP Cooperative Societies Act, 1964.
- **Purpose:** **timely and affordable credit** to SHG members through VOs/SLFs and MS/TLFs; also deposits, loan insurance, Business Correspondent.
- **Rate:** **13%** at launch (2011); **11%** now (livelihood loans up to Rs 1 lakh, APSES 2024-25). Loans Rs 21,123.71 crore (2011-2024).
- Stree Nidhi describes itself as a "**historic necessity**" – SHG women needed their own credit body.
- **PS-SBG** (Pattabhi Sitaramayya-Self Business Group) for rural women entrepreneurs: launched by **Andhra Bank**.
> Traps: MEPMA urban, SERP rural; IKP merged Velugu and DWCRA (not Indira Prabha – that is land development); SHGs need no registration.

=== NABARD – Establishment and Functions
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Recommended by | **CRAFICARD** – Committee to Review Arrangements for Institutional Credit for Agriculture and Rural Development, chaired by **B. Sivaraman** |
| Act / set up | NABARD **Act 1981**; set up **12 July 1982** |
| Took over | RBI's **ACD** and **RPCC** and the whole of **ARDC** |
| Capital / HQ | Rs 100 crore; **Mumbai**; fully **Government-owned since 2019** |
- **Role:** apex **refinancing** institution for rural credit – **term loans/refinance to State Cooperative Banks**, RRBs and commercial banks; **long-term loans to State Governments** to contribute to the **share capital** of cooperative credit societies.
- **Does NOT lend short-term credit directly** to farmers.
- **Supervision:** inspects **RRBs, State and District Central Cooperative Banks** – **not PACS** (inspected by the Registrar/DCCB).
- **SHG-Bank Linkage** (1992-93); **KCC** model scheme (**1998**, R.V. Gupta Committee). Same year as NABARD: **EXIM Bank** (1982).
- Order: RRBs (1975) → NABARD (1982) → SHG linkage (1992-93) → KCC (1998).
> Trap: NABARD does not inspect primary cooperative societies.
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 07 p.10; Rocket Indian Economy p.44 (NABARD 12 July 1982, Sivaraman); NextGen AP Economy Static-1 01 p.14 (KCC 1998); app notes 3:123 sub 0

=== RIDF, Indira Prabha and Other NABARD Programmes
- **RIDF:** created by GoI **in NABARD** in **1995-96**; corpus **Rs 2,000 crore** (not Rs 20,000 crore); **NABARD** is the nodal agency.
| Activity | RIDF category |
|---|---|
| Rural drinking water | Social sector |
| Rural roads | Rural connectivity |
| Minor irrigation | Agriculture and related |
- Loans to States: **irrigation** largest (30%), roads 26%, social 21%; RIDF XXVIII (2023-24) Rs 40,475 crore.
- AP: **68 lift irrigation schemes** modernised at Rs 74.22 crore to stabilise **1.03 lakh acres** (2024-25).
- **Indira Prabha** (2004, Sirigandayapalem, Guntur): State programme with **NABARD** support for **land development** of **assigned land distributed to the poor**. (Indira Kranthi = SHG programme; Bhubharati = land records; Janmabhoomi = people's participation.)
| Fund | Purpose |
|---|---|
| LTIF | State share of PMKSY irrigation projects |
| Micro Irrigation Fund | AP among 8 States funded |
| Warehouse Infrastructure Fund (2013-14) | Scientific storage, APMC facilities |
- Share-sale (disinvestment) money goes to **NIF**, not RIDF.
~~ Sources: NextGen AP Economy Static-1 01 p.15 (Indira Prabha 2004); app notes 3:123 sub 4

=== Sources of Agricultural Credit and RRBs in AP
- **Commercial banks** = **major source** of institutional farm credit in AP (as in India: SCBs over 70% of formal farm credit).
- **2007-08:** **cooperative credit societies** gave about **one-fifth** of AP's crop loans; commercial banks most of the rest.
- Institutional: cooperatives, commercial banks, RRBs, Government. **Non-institutional:** moneylenders.
- Exclusion: **tenant, tribal, women and oral-lessee** farmers – no **land records**.
| Loan term | Period |
|---|---|
| Short-term | Up to 1 year, maximum **15 months** |
| Medium-term | 15 months to 3 (or 5) years |
| Long-term | Above 3 (or 5) years |
# RRBs
- Set up **2 October 1975** on the **Narasimham Working Group** (1975) recommendations; first = **Prathama Bank** (Moradabad); RRB Act 1976; shares Centre 50 : State 15 : sponsor bank 35.
- **One State, One RRB:** AP's four RRBs merged into **Andhra Pradesh Grameena Bank** from **1 May 2025**; sponsor **Union Bank of India**; RRBs in India 43 → **28**.
- **Andhra Bank:** founded **1923**, Machilipatnam, by **Bhogaraju Pattabhi Sitaramayya**; merged into **Union Bank of India** (1 April 2020).
- **Narasimham Committee I (1991):** began banking reforms (different from the 1975 Working Group).

=== Crop Loan Schemes – Zero Interest, Pavala Vaddi, KCC
| Scheme | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Vaddi Leni Runalu / YSR Zero Interest Crop Loan** (payout 20 Apr 2021) | Crop loans up to **Rs 1 lakh** repaid on time; **State pays interest** |
| **Pavala Vaddi** (farmers) | Crop loans **above Rs 1 lakh and up to Rs 3 lakh** at 3% ("quarter-rupee" interest) for prompt repayers |
| Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (GoI) | Up to **Rs 3 lakh at 4%** for prompt repayers |
| KCC (RBI Directions, 19 Jun 2026) | Loans from 1 Jan 2027; max tenure 6 years |
- India farm credit 2024-25: Rs 28.69 lakh crore vs target Rs 27.50 lakh crore = **104%**.
