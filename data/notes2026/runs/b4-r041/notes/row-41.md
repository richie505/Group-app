=== Firsts, Oldest and Counts
- **First / oldest NP of India (and mainland Asia):** **Jim Corbett**, 1936 as **Hailey NP**; Project Tiger launched there 1973.
- Chronology: **Corbett 1936 → Kanha 1955 → Kaziranga 1974 → Silent Valley 1984**.
- **Most national parks:** **Madhya Pradesh (11)**; then A&N (6 or 9, depending on whether the three Button Island NPs are counted), **Assam (8 since 2025)**, Kerala/Maharashtra/Uttarakhand/WB 6.
- **Most wildlife sanctuaries:** **A&N Islands (~97)**; among States **Maharashtra (~48)** > Karnataka (~35) > Tamil Nadu (~33) > MP (~24).
| Category | Count (2026) |
|---|---|
| National Parks | 109 (~1.35% of India) |
| Wildlife Sanctuaries | 573 |
| Conservation / Community Reserves | 127 / 75 |
| Tiger / Elephant / Biosphere Reserves | 58 (~2.6% of India, 29 Jul 2026) / 33 / 18 |
- PA network ≈ **5.3%** of India's area.
- **Bhutan** – more than 30% of its area under national parks.
- **Largest high-altitude PA:** Tsarap Chu conservation reserve (Spiti, HP).
- **First bird sanctuary:** Vedanthangal (TN); **first wolf sanctuary:** Mahuadanr (Latehar, Jharkhand); **first butterfly park:** **Bannerghatta Biological Park**, Bengaluru (2006).
- World's first NP: **Yellowstone (1872)**; Canada's first: Banff (1885).

=== Protected-area Law, Zoos and Parks
| Category (WPA 1972) | Declared by / on | Rights |
|---|---|---|
| National Park | State (or Centre); boundaries fixed | **Highest protection**; no grazing, no private rights |
| Wildlife Sanctuary | State | Some rights and grazing regulated but allowed |
| Conservation Reserve (Sec 36A) | State, on **government land** adjoining/linking PAs, after consulting locals | Local rights unaffected |
| Community Reserve (Sec 36C) | State, on **private/community land** volunteered | Rights unaffected; not inside NP/WLS/CR |
- WPA got Presidential assent **9 September 1972**; reserves added by the 2002 amendment. Protection: **NP > WLS > Reserved > Protected forests**.
- **ESZ:** ordinary buffer **1 km** around NPs/WLS (SC, June 2022); SC extended a **10-km mining buffer** to wetland conservation reserves.
- **Zoos:** **Nandankanan** (Bhubaneswar, **Odisha**); Bannerghatta Biological Park (Bengaluru); Indira Gandhi Zoological Park (Visakhapatnam).
- Other firsts: ISA – first treaty-based IGO in India (Secretariat **Gurugram**); Montreal Protocol in force **1989**.
