=== NOTES 4:41 | National Parks, Wildlife Sanctuaries, Biosphere Reserves and Conservation Reserves
## SUB 0 | Wildlife Protection Act 1972 – Protected Area Categories
[41:0:0] - First comprehensive wildlife law of India; **Presidential assent 9 September 1972**.
[41:0:1] - PAs under it: **National Parks, Wildlife Sanctuaries, Conservation Reserves, Community Reserves**.
[41:0:2] TABLE head: Category | Declared by / on | Rights and use
  [41:0:2.0] National Park | State (or Centre) | Highest protection; no grazing or private rights
  [41:0:2.1] Wildlife Sanctuary | State | Some activities/rights regulated but allowed
  [41:0:2.2] Conservation Reserve (Sec 36A) | State, on government-owned land beside or linking PAs, after consulting locals | Local rights unaffected; committees with panchayats
  [41:0:2.3] Community Reserve | State, on private/community land freely offered | Rights unaffected; land cannot be inside an NP, WLS or CR
[41:0:3] - Conservation and community reserves were added by the **WPA (Amendment) 2002**.
[41:0:4] - **Supreme Court**: **10-km mining buffer** extended to all **wetland conservation reserves**; ordinary eco-sensitive buffer around NPs/WLS = **1 km**. Final ESZ notifications: **353**.
