[81:0:0] # The Convention
[81:0:1] - **Ramsar Convention** – signed/adopted in **1971** at **Ramsar, Iran**.
[81:0:2] - Subject: **wetlands of international importance**.
[81:0:3] - India became a party on **1 February 1982**.
[81:0:4] India joined in **1982**, not 1971; 1971 is the year the Convention was signed at Ramsar, Iran.

[81:0:11] - Four components of Amrit Dharohar: **Species and Habitat Conservation**, **Nature Tourism**, **Wetlands Livelihoods**, **Wetlands Carbon Assessment**.
[81:0:12] - Other support: **National Plan for Conservation of Aquatic Ecosystems (NPCA)** (centrally sponsored) and the **Wetlands (Conservation and Management) Rules, 2017**.

[81:0:16]   - **Zimbabwe** took over the Convention presidency for **2025–2028**, after **China**.
