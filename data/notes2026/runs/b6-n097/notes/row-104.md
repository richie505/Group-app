# Key Notes on September Days
- **International Day of Charity** (5 Sept) falls on the **same day as Teachers' Day**.
- **Hindi Diwas** (14 Sept): the **Constituent Assembly** adopted **Hindi in Devanagari** as the Union's official language in **1949** under the **Munshi-Ayyangar formula**; observed **since 1953**.
- **Clean Air for Blue Skies** (7 Sept): set by the **UNGA in 2019**; **first observed in 2020**; India marks it as **Swachh Vayu Diwas** – **Swachh Vayu Sarvekshan** awards are given.
- **International Day of Sign Languages**: proclaimed by UNGA in **2017**; **2025 theme** "**No Human Rights Without Sign Language Rights**".
- **Punjab** – first State to **telecast Assembly proceedings in sign language** (2025).
- **Ayurveda Day**: **fixed on 23 September from 2025** (**autumnal equinox**); earlier held on **Dhanvantari Jayanti (Dhanteras)**; declared by the **Ministry of Ayush**; **2025 theme** "**Ayurveda for People and Planet**" (PYQ: 23 Sept).
- **World Digestive Health Day** – **29 May**.
> **Yoga Day = summer solstice (21 June)**; **Ayurveda Day = autumnal equinox (23 September)**.
> Hindi Diwas marks the **1949** decision; it has been **observed since 1953**. Clean Air Day: set **2019**, first observed **2020** (not 2019).

=== UNESCO – Sarnath and India's Tentative List
# Sarnath – India's 45th World Heritage Site
- **Ancient Buddhist Site of Sarnath** inscribed at the **48th World Heritage Committee session, Busan (South Korea), July 2026**.
- **India's 45th** WHS; **UP's 4th** (others: **Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Fatehpur Sikri**).
- Components: **Chaukhandi Stupa** + archaeological remains; criteria **(iii) and (vi)**.
- **Buddha's first sermon** (**Dharmachakra**); ancient name **Rishipattan**.
- On the Tentative List since **3 July 1998** (**28 years**).
# Tentative List – Additions in September 2026
- **4 September 2026** – four sites added, total **73**: **Rangpur–Sivasagar Historic Ensemble** (Assam – Ahom: **Rang Ghar, Talatal Ghar**, historic large water tanks), **Colonial Penal Settlements of the Andaman Islands** (incl. **Cellular Jail**), **Shekhawati Painted Haveli Towns** (Rajasthan – merchants' havelis with murals) and a **Nicobarese** heritage site.
- Five more added – total now **78**:

# Background and Process
- **World Heritage Convention 1972**; **196 States Parties** (2024); **India ratified in 1977** (not 1972).
- **10 criteria**: (i)–(vi) cultural, (vii)–(x) natural, merged in **2004**.
- **ICOMOS** evaluates cultural, **IUCN** natural sites; **21-member** World Heritage Committee.
- A site must first be on the **Tentative List** before nomination; tentative listing **does not** give WHS status.
- In India, **ASI and INCCU** propose Tentative List sites.
- Danger-list examples: **Bamiyan Valley**, **Zabid (Yemen)**, **Bam (Iran)**.
