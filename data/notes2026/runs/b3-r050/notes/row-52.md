=== Crop Insurance – CCIS to PMFBY and AP Schemes
| Scheme | Year | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **CCIS** (Comprehensive Crop Insurance Scheme) | **1 April 1985** (kharif) | first national crop insurance scheme |
| **NAIS** | **Rabi 1999-2000** | replaced CCIS |
| **Varsha Bima** (rainfall insurance) | **SW monsoon 2004** | run by **Agriculture Insurance Company of India (AICIL)**; piloted in 20 areas of AP, UP, Karnataka, Rajasthan – **not 2007** |
| **WBCIS** | **Kharif 2007**, first in **Karnataka** | extended to 10 States from Kharif 2008 |
| MNAIS | 2010-11 | – |
| **PMFBY** | 2016 | replaced NAIS and MNAIS (not WBCIS); premium **2% kharif, 1.5% rabi, 5% commercial/horticulture** – not a uniform 2%; covers **post-harvest losses from cyclones and unseasonal rain**; voluntary from 2020; YES-TECH (yield), WINDS (weather), DigiClaim |
| **RWBCIS** | 2016 | protects **insured** farmers (all categories) against loss from adverse weather; pays when weather deviates from triggers |
- **Not a claim ground:** low yield from **poor-quality seed**. Cover is for natural calamities (drought, flood), pests and diseases.
- **AP:** PMFBY, WBCIS/RWBCIS and the Unified Package Insurance Scheme have run in AP; there is **no** special scheme for GM crops. **Dr YSR Free Crop Insurance (2019-24):** State paid the whole premium; covered **22 notified crops** (2022); voluntary from **Rabi 2024-25** (farmer pays a small fixed share, State and Centre share the rest); Kharif 2026 – every crop insured, Collectors accountable.
> Most-asked angle (13 PYQs): scheme years – CCIS 1985, NAIS 1999, Varsha Bima 2004, WBCIS 2007 Karnataka; PMFBY premium statements (UPSC 2016).
~~ Sources: app notes 3:52 sub 6; NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC 04 p.54 (NAIS 1999 replacing CCIS); Ramesh Singh Indian Economy 04 p.181 (PMFBY replaced NAIS of 1999 and MNAIS 2010-11)

=== Causes of Agrarian Crisis and Farmer Suicides
| Cause | Data point |
|---|---|
| Small holdings | 86% small and marginal |
| Rain-fed | about 50% of NSA; groundwater = 64% of irrigation; 17% blocks over-exploited |
| Post-harvest | cold-chain deficit 30% → up to 40% fruit/vegetable loss |
| Soil | NPK 10.9:4.1:1 vs ideal 4:2:1 |
- **Indian agriculture is still ailing due to** under-investment, low productivity **and** post-harvest losses – "all the above".
- **Rural poverty** is the effective reason for slow agricultural development (no capital for inputs and technology).
- **Debt cycle:** high input cost → borrowing → crop failure or low price → inability to repay → fresh borrowing → debt trap.
- **Suicides:** main reasons – **crop failure** and **bankruptcy/indebtedness** (NCRB ADSI 2015); concentrated in **rain-fed** belts. ADSI 2023: farm sector 10,786; Maharashtra 38.5%, Karnataka 22.5%, AP 8.6%.
- **Swaminathan Commission** = National Commission on Farmers, constituted **2004** (18 Nov), after farmer suicides; MSP = **C2 + 50%** (C2 = A2 + imputed family labour + rent and interest on owned land and capital); National Agricultural Risk Fund.
- **Farm protests:** Sharad Joshi (Shetkari Sangathan) backed globalisation; Nanjundaswamy (KRRS), Tikait (BKU) opposed; 2020-21 farm laws repealed 2021.
~~ Sources: app notes 3:52 sub 0, 3, 4, 8; NextGen Indian Economy Part 1 - APPSC 01 p.59 (NCF on 18 Nov 2004, farmer suicides)

=== Small Holdings and Agricultural Households
| Agriculture Census | Small + marginal holdings | Their area |
|---|---|---|
| **2010-11** | about **85%** (marginal 67.10 + small 17.91) | **44.58%** (22.50 + 22.08) |
| **2015-16** | **86.2%** | **47.3%** |
- **2015-16 States:** by number of holdings **UP 1st, Bihar 2nd, Maharashtra 3rd**; by operated area **Rajasthan 1st**. Semi-medium + medium: 13.2% of holdings, 43.6% of area.
- **NSSO 70th Round (2013) SAS:** **Rajasthan** – highest share of agricultural households among rural households (about 78%); **Kerala** – over 60% earned most income from non-farm sources; OBCs are about **45%** of agricultural households (not "over 60%").
- **NSS 77th Round (2019):** about half indebted; debt ₹74,121; income wages → crops → livestock; most indebted **AP 93.2%**, Telangana 91.7%.
- **Bullock capitalists:** **Lloyd and Susanne Rudolph** ("In Pursuit of Lakshmi", 1987) – self-employed small-to-medium farmers with **some resources but not rich**.
- Remedies: 10,000 FPOs (2020, ₹6,865 crore), Custom Hiring Centres, cluster and joint farming. Other: Guntur study (tenants' debt ₹2,07,444; informal 51.5%); R. Radhakrishna Expert Group on indebtedness (2007); AP Microfinance Act 2011 (3 lenders, ₹2 lakh cap); 7.72 crore KCCs vs 146 million holdings.

=== Loan Waivers and Debt Redemption
| Waiver | Facts |
|---|---|
| ADWDRS 2008 | small/marginal: full waiver; others: 25% OTS relief on paying 75% |
| **AP Agricultural Debt Redemption Scheme 2014** (Runa Mafi / Runa Upasamana) | implemented by **Rythu Sadhikara Samstha**; ceiling ₹1.5 lakh/family; horticulture ₹10,000/acre; balance paid in annual instalments – 2nd instalment released from 22 June 2016 (Runa Upasamana Arhatha Patralu) |
- **Effect on aggregate demand (all four channels):** private consumption (net wealth up), public-sector spending/taxes, crowding-out via higher State borrowing, crowding-in as bank NPAs fall.
- **RBI critique:** moral hazard; hurts credit culture.
~~ Sources: app notes 3:52 sub 5; apagrisnet.gov.in GO dated 22-6-2016 (2nd instalment drive, via web search)

=== Income Support and AP Tenant Farmers
| Scheme | Fact |
|---|---|
| **PM-KISAN** | ₹6,000/yr in **3 instalments** of ₹2,000; at launch only small and marginal families with up to **2 hectares** – extended to all landholding families from June 2019 |
| Annadata Sukhibhava (AP) | ₹20,000; tenants covered |
| KALIA / Rythu Bandhu | Odisha / Telangana (₹12,000 per acre) |
| PM Kisan Maandhan | pension for small farmers |
- **First State to give loans to tenant farmers:** **Andhra Pradesh** – AP Land Licensed Cultivators Act, **2011** (Loan Eligibility Cards).
- **CCRC = Crop Cultivator Rights Card** (AP Crop Cultivator Rights Act, **2019**): for lease-holders/tenants, valid 11 months; no ownership transfer; hurdle – landowner's signature. AP pioneered RMGs and JLGs.
- **2011 crop holiday (Konaseema paddy farmers):** special committee chaired by **Mohan Kanda** (not Jayati Ghosh, not Koneru Ranga Rao).
- Tenants often lack passbook, KCC and insurance; those missing from **e-crop** data miss insurance and relief.
