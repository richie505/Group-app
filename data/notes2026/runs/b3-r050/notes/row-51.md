=== National Agriculture Policy 2000 and Earlier Commissions
- **NAP 2000** (July 2000): growth target **over 4% per annum**.
- **Thrusts:** private participation via **contract farming** and **land leasing**; price protection; national agricultural insurance; **Rainbow Revolution** across all farm sub-sectors.
- **Land reform in NAP 2000:** consolidation of holdings all over the country, redistribution of ceiling-surplus and waste land, tenancy reform, updating land records; APPSC key: **consolidation of holdings**.

| Golden | Horticulture / honey |
| Silver | Eggs |
| Grey | Fertilisers |
- **ICAR Drishti (Vision) 2025:** increase in **food production**.
- **National Commission on Agriculture (NCA):** 1970-76, **Nathu Ram Mirdha**; coined "social forestry".
- **National Commission on Farmers (NCF):** constituted **18 Nov 2004**, **M.S. Swaminathan**; MSP at **C2 + 50%** (government pays **1.5 × A2+FL**, 22 mandated crops); Agricultural Renewal Action Plan – 2006-07 the Year of Agricultural Renewal.
- **Agricultural Renewal Action Plan packages:** soil health enhancement, water harvesting and efficient use, credit and insurance reform, technology dissemination, market reform – phasing out MSP is not one of them.

=== Policy Since 1991 and the WTO Boxes
| Change after 1991 | Fact |
|---|---|
| Public investment | **Declined** with liberalisation |
| Technology | new technologies and **contract farming** helped productivity |
| MSP | **not abolished** – still in force |
| WTO (1995) | import competition rose; subsidies fell as a share of farm income |
| GM crops | Bt cotton (2002) – only GM crop approved for commercial cultivation |

| Box | Nature | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Green** | minimal or no trade distortion – **permitted** without limit | **decoupled direct income payments**, research, training, extension, pest control, infrastructure, food security stocks |
| **Amber** | trade-distorting – **reduction commitments** expressed as **Total AMS** (product-specific + non-product-specific support in one figure); de minimis 5% developed / 10% developing | MSP, input subsidies |
| **Blue** | Amber support that **requires farmers to limit production** – placed in the Blue Box | direct payments tied to production limits |
- **Peace Clause (Bali 2013):** shields developing countries' public stockholding from challenge **even if** they breach the 10% ceiling – it does not tell them "not to exceed" it.
- **Agriculture Orientation Index (SDG 2.a.1):** agriculture's share of government spending ÷ agriculture's share of GDP; tracks **SDG 2**; compiled by **FAO**, not IFPRI.
- Dunkel Draft: **Sharad Joshi** backed it; **Tikait (BKU)** and **Nanjundaswamy (KRRS)** opposed. Budget 2020-21: 16 action points for farmers.
> APPSC G1 2024 WTO statements: only the Total AMS statement is right.

=== Sustainable Agriculture and Rainfed Areas
- **Meaning:** use of land so that its quality remains intact – meeting present needs without harming future generations.
- **Ultimate goal:** conservation of natural resources, environmental protection **and** human health and safety – "**all of these**"; pillars = environmental health, economic profitability, social and economic equity.
- **Rainfed farming:** 50-60% of net sown area, about 40% of food output.
- **National Rainfed Area Authority (NRAA, 2006):** the **single platform** for holistic, sustainable development of rainfed areas; knowledge inputs and watershed approach.

=== Doubling Farmers' Income
- **Ashok Dalwai Committee (2016-18):** 14 volumes; base year **2015-16**, target **2022**.
- Shift from production to **income**: value addition, e-NAM/APMC reform, post-harvest infrastructure, land-leasing laws.
- **Seven sources:** crop productivity, livestock productivity, resource-use efficiency, cropping intensity, diversification to high-value crops, better price realisation, shift to non-farm jobs. Dependence only on traditional low-value crops is NOT a source.

=== Phases of Agricultural Development
| Phase | Period | Defining feature |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-Green Revolution | 1951-68 | land reforms, irrigation, Community Development; **PL-480** imports; droughts **1965-67** |
| Early Green Revolution | 1968-81 | **New Agricultural Strategy** – HYV seeds (**wheat first**), fertiliser, irrigation |
| Later Green Revolution | 1987-92 | spread to **rice** and other regions |
| Income phase | 2016 onwards | Doubling Farmers' Income – from production to income |
- Foodgrains: 72.3 MT (1965-66) → record 357.73 MT (2024-25).
