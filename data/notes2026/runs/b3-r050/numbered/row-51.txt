## SUB 1 | National Agriculture Policy 2000 and Earlier Commissions
[1:0] - **NAP 2000** (July 2000): growth target **over 4% per annum**.
[1:1] - **Thrusts:** private participation via **contract farming** and **land leasing**; price protection; national agricultural insurance; **Rainbow Revolution** across all farm sub-sectors.
[1:2] - **Land reform in NAP 2000:** consolidation of holdings all over the country, redistribution of ceiling-surplus and waste land, tenancy reform, updating land records; APPSC key: **consolidation of holdings**.
[1:3] TABLE head: Colour | Sector
  [1:3.0] Yellow | Oilseeds
  [1:3.1] Blue | Fisheries
  [1:3.2] Golden | Horticulture / honey
  [1:3.3] Silver | Eggs
  [1:3.4] Grey | Fertilisers
[1:4] - **ICAR Drishti (Vision) 2025:** increase in **food production**.
[1:5] - **National Commission on Agriculture (NCA):** 1970-76, **Nathu Ram Mirdha**; coined "social forestry".
[1:6] - **National Commission on Farmers (NCF):** constituted **18 Nov 2004**, **M.S. Swaminathan**; MSP at **C2 + 50%** (government pays **1.5 × A2+FL**, 22 mandated crops); Agricultural Renewal Action Plan – 2006-07 the Year of Agricultural Renewal.
[1:7] - **Agricultural Renewal Action Plan packages:** soil health enhancement, water harvesting and efficient use, credit and insurance reform, technology dissemination, market reform – phasing out MSP is not one of them.

[2:1] TABLE head: Box | Nature | Examples

## SUB 3 | Sustainable Agriculture and Rainfed Areas
[3:0] - **Meaning:** use of land so that its quality remains intact – meeting present needs without harming future generations.
[3:1] - **Ultimate goal:** conservation of natural resources, environmental protection **and** human health and safety – "**all of these**"; pillars = environmental health, economic profitability, social and economic equity.
[3:2] - **Rainfed farming:** 50-60% of net sown area, about 40% of food output.
[3:3] - **National Rainfed Area Authority (NRAA, 2006):** the **single platform** for holistic, sustainable development of rainfed areas; knowledge inputs and watershed approach.
[3:4] ~~ Sources: app notes 3:51 sub 5
## SUB 4 | Doubling Farmers' Income
[4:0] - **Ashok Dalwai Committee (2016-18):** 14 volumes; base year **2015-16**, target **2022**.
[4:1] - Shift from production to **income**: value addition, e-NAM/APMC reform, post-harvest infrastructure, land-leasing laws.
[4:2] - **Seven sources:** crop productivity, livestock productivity, resource-use efficiency, cropping intensity, diversification to high-value crops, better price realisation, shift to non-farm jobs. Dependence only on traditional low-value crops is NOT a source.
