## SUB 1 | Formation of Andhra Pradesh, 1 November 1956 – First Holders
[262:1:0] - **Andhra Pradesh formed on 1 November 1956** under the **States Reorganisation Act, 1956**, on a **linguistic basis** – the first state formed under the SR Act; **Andhra State (11 districts) + 9 Telugu districts of Hyderabad State**; Kannada areas → Mysore, Marathi → Bombay.
[262:1:1] - **Andhra State** (1 Oct 1953): capital **Kurnool**; first CM **Tanguturi Prakasam**, Deputy CM N. Sanjiva Reddy; Prakasam's ministry fell on **prohibition** (1954) → mid-term polls 1955 → **Bezawada Gopala Reddy** CM. Before it, the **Wanchoo Committee** (Dec 1952) identified the **financial and administrative problems** of forming Andhra State; the **Pataskar Award** fixed the Andhra–Madras border (**Tiruttani** to Tamil Nadu).
[262:1:2] - **Capital:** **Kurnool = capital of Andhra State until 1956** (the "capital before 1956" answer); **Hyderabad became the capital of AP in 1956** – reasons: the SRC's view and the Mahasabha demand. Exam keys often call Kurnool "the first capital of AP".
[262:1:3] - **Andhra Legislature met at Kurnool** and backed the merger **unanimously**; **inaugurated by Prime Minister Nehru on 1 Nov 1956**.
[262:1:4] - **Name:** draft Bill "**Andhra Telangana**"; Communists wanted "**Visalandhra**"; the **Joint Select Committee** changed it to "**Andhra Pradesh**" – so none of "Andhra Telangana / Telangana Andhra / Visalandhra".

## SUB 3 | Visalandhra Movement and the Visalandhra Mahasabha
[262:3:0] - **Visalandhra** ("**Greater Andhra**") = the movement for **one state for all Telugu people** – the Circars, the Ceded districts (Rayalaseema) and Telangana, then divided between Madras and the Nizam's Hyderabad (in **1950** southern and eastern Andhra were in Madras State, Telangana in Hyderabad State).
[262:3:1] - Roots: 1912 Unnava Lakshminarayana and Jonnavittula Gurunatham sketched a united Andhra; 1914 second Andhra Mahasabha – **Nyapati Subbarao** proposed one rule for all Telugu areas; **1936 Andhra Congress Swaraj Party manifesto**; 1937 the idea spread in the Circars; 1954 **Madapati Hanumantha Rao**: the idea was 50 years old.
[262:3:2] - **Started / championed by the Communist Party** – demanded integrated linguistic states from **1946**; **Puchalapalli Sundarayya** coined the word and wrote ***Visalandhralo Prajarajyam***; daily **Visalandhra** from **June 1952**; Sundarayya later first General Secretary of CPI(M) (1964).
[262:3:3] - The Communists **became a great force in Telangana politics** and propagated Visalandhra **even before Andhra State (1953)**; they **did not oppose** integrated linguistic states (the only incorrect statement, G2 2025).
[262:3:4] - Other Communist leaders: **Ravi Narayana Reddy, Baddam Ella Reddy, Arutla Ramachandra Reddy**; the Hyderabad State Communist Party was a branch of the Andhra party (HQ **Vijayawada**); its 1952 electoral front = **People's Democratic Front (PDF)**. The Congress was first cold because the idea came from the Communists.

## SUB 4 | SRC, Merger Debate and the Leaders' Stands
[262:4:0] - **SRC** (announced 22 Dec 1953; Fazl Ali, H.N. Kunzru, K.M. Panikkar – not Harishchandra Heda) **heard opinions in Hyderabad in June 1954**; report **30 September 1955**. On **8 July 1954** a Hyderabad PCC delegation asked it for a **self-sufficient Telangana of 9 districts with Bastar and parts of Chanda**.
[262:4:1] - SRC **acknowledged Telangana's special claims** (finance, education) – **para 386**: Telangana to stay a separate Hyderabad State, merging after the **1961** elections if **two-thirds** of its legislature agreed; Raichur, Gulbarga → Mysore, Marathwada detached.
[262:4:2] - SRC's case for Visalandhra: **unified control of Krishna–Godavari waters**, minerals and power, **Hyderabad as a ready capital**, lower administrative cost.
[262:4:3] - **Hyderabad Assembly**: **103 for, 29 against, 15 neutral**; **Andhra Assembly (1955)**: Bezawada Gopala Reddy's **unanimous** resolution.
[262:4:4] - **Burgula Ramakrishna Rao**, earlier for a separate Telangana, **changed his stand in 1955** and urged **immediate merger** with Andhra.
[262:4:5] - 1956: to settle differences the **Congress leadership called the leaders to Delhi** → Gentlemen's Agreement.

## SUB 5 | Hyderabad State Background – Mahasabha, Congress, Burgula
[262:5:0] - **Chanda Railway Scheme agitation (1883)** = the **beginning of public (political) consciousness** in Hyderabad State.
[262:5:1] - **Andhra Jana Sangham** (1921) – its **first meeting, Hyderabad, 14 Feb 1922, presided by K.V. Ranga Reddy**; it grew into the **Nizam Andhra Mahasabha** – first session **Jogipet, 1930**, **Suravaram Pratapa Reddy**.
[262:5:2] - **Hyderabad State Congress** (1938) – led and founded by **Swami Ramananda Tirtha**, who also led the **"Join Indian Union" movement (7 Aug 1947)** against the Nizam's claim to independence; integration by **Operation Polo, 17 Sep 1948**.
[262:5:3] - **Mulki rules** from **1888**, tightened by the **1919 farman** (about 15 years' residence); **Mulki agitation 1952** under Burgula – **firing near City College, Hyderabad**; inquiry under **Justice Pingali Jaganmohan Reddy**.
[262:5:4] - Hyderabad = **Part B State, 1950 – 31 Oct 1956**; Speaker at the merger **Kashinatha Rao**.
[262:5:5] - **Burgula Ramakrishna Rao** = **first elected (1952) and last CM of Hyderabad State** – Chief Minister of **Hyderabad State**, not Andhra State or AP.
[262:5:6] > Chanda Railway 1883 = public consciousness; Jana Sangham 1922 – K.V. Ranga Reddy; Join Indian Union – Ramananda Tirtha; Burgula = first elected CM of Hyderabad.

=== NOTES 1:265 | Social Movements in Andhra Pradesh – Dalit, Backward Class, Farmers' and Anti-arrack Movements
## SUB 0 | Anti-arrack (Anti-Sara) Movement
[265:0:0] - Began in **Dubagunta** village, **Nellore** district – launched by **rural women in 1991** (spread statewide 1992–93); leader **Dubagunta (Vardhineni) Rosamma**.
[265:0:1] - Name: **Anti-Sara** ("sara" = arrack) movement – not Jai Andhra, anti-dowry or Nava Samaj; not Renigunta, Domalamadugu or Rajupalem.
[265:0:2] - Grew out of **"Aksharajyothi" adult-literacy classes** (story "Seethamma katha"): women discussed men's **arrack drinking, debt, domestic violence**.
[265:0:3] - **Methods:** women **closed arrack shops** and **stopped arrack auctions** with broomsticks and chilli powder. Legislative elections were **NOT** a method.
[265:0:4] - One of India's **most successful women-led movements**.
[265:0:5] TABLE head: Step | Date
  [265:0:5.0] Arrack prohibited in AP (CM Kotla Vijayabhaskara Reddy) | 1 October 1993
  [265:0:5.1] Total prohibition (NTR) | 1995
  [265:0:5.2] Prohibition lifted | 1997
[265:0:6] - **1930s precursor:** **Kallur Subbarao** led **anti-liquor and Swadeshi movements** in the **Dattamandalas (Rayalaseema)**.
[265:0:7] > Dubagunta (Nellore), 1991, Rosamma → arrack ban 1 Oct 1993.
