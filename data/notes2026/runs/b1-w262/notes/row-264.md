=== Srikakulam Girijan Armed Struggle
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Place | Srikakulam Agency (tribal hills) |
| **Period** | **1968–70** (began 1967; Naxalbari was 1967) |
| Tribes | **Savaras (Soras) and Jatapus** |
| **Leaders** | **Vempatapu Satyanarayana (Satyam)** and **Adibhatla Kailasam** (school teachers) |
| Spread by | Kanu Sanyal, Tarimela Nagi Reddy; Nagabhushana Patnaik |
| **Party** | Began under the **CPI(M)** (Girijan Sangham); shifted to the **CPI(ML)** in 1969 |
| Nature | Tribal-peasant uprising against landlords, moneylenders and forest officials |
- Trigger: killing of two tribal activists (Koraana and Manganna) on **31 October 1967** at **Levidi**, on the way to a Girijan Sangham meeting.
- **Women:** **Panchadi Nirmala** (wife of activist Panchadi Krishnamurthy) was a leader; killed in a police encounter, Dec 1969.
- Vempatapu Satyam and Adibhatla Kailasam were killed in **July 1970**; the struggle broke up by the end of 1970; suppressed under **Jalagam Vengala Rao** (Home Minister, later CM).
- Outcome: **suppressed**, but it made AP a core base of Indian Maoism and seeded revolutionary literature.
| Point | Naxalbari | Srikakulam |
|---|---|---|
| State | West Bengal | Andhra Pradesh |
| Period | March–May 1967 | 1968–70 |
| Leaders | Charu Majumdar, Kanu Sanyal | Vempatapu Satyam, Adibhatla Kailasam |
| Nature | Peasant | Tribal (Savaras, Jatapus) |
> Srikakulam = 1968–70, CPI(M) → CPI(ML), Vempatapu Satyam + Adibhatla Kailasam, Panchadi Nirmala.

=== Naxalbari and Naxalite Groups
- **Naxalbari peasant uprising**: **March–May 1967**, Siliguri sub-division, **Darjeeling** district, West Bengal; leaders **Charu Majumdar** and **Kanu Sanyal**; slogan **"Land to the tiller"**.
- **"Ballot to Bullet"**: rejected the **CPI(M)'s parliamentary path**; causes – **failed land reforms**, exploitation by landlords and moneylenders.
- **1967 Andhra extremist wing** split from CPI(M): **Tarimela Nagi Reddy, Chandra Pulla Reddy**, Kolla Venkaiah, Nagabhushana Patnaik.
| Group | Founder(s) | Year | Base |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPI(ML) | Charu Majumdar (with Kanu Sanyal) | 1969 | West Bengal |
| People's War Group (PWG) | **Kondapalli Seetharamaiah** | 1980 | Andhra Pradesh (North Telangana) |
| **CPI (Maoist)** | **PWG + MCC** merger | **2004** | Red Corridor |
- **CPI(ML)** saw the Indian state as **semi-feudal**; Chinese-model protracted guerrilla war.
- **Kishanji** (Mallojula Koteswara Rao) – Maoist leader killed by security forces in **November 2011** – not Charu Majumdar (died 1972) or Kanu Sanyal (died 2010).
> 1967 Naxalbari | 1969 CPI(ML) | 1980 PWG | 1992 PWG banned | 2004 CPI (Maoist) | 2011 Kishanji killed.

=== Government Responses – Greyhounds, Commissions, Talks
| Response | Year | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| Bhargava Commission | 1977 | Justice **Vashist Bhargava** – inquired into **Emergency "encounter" killings** of Naxalites |
| **Greyhounds** | 1989 | Elite **anti-Naxal special police force** of the AP Police – **not a dog squad** |
| Ban | 1992 | PWG banned in AP |
| Surrender policy | 1998–2001 | Under DGP **H.J. Dora**, over 1,200 cadres surrendered |
| Peace talks | October 2004 | Government–Maoist talks in Hyderabad (**YSR** government); **S.R. Sankaran** chief negotiator |
- APRA 2014 **Sec 9**: Greyhound Training Centre at Hyderabad went to Telangana; Centre to help AP set up its own – **Anandapuram** (near Visakhapatnam); **Greyhound and OCTOPUS** forces divided.
- Anandapuram base secures the LWE corridor of **Alluri Sitharama Raju (ASR)** and **Parvathipuram Manyam** districts (north coastal AP, Odisha border).
> Greyhounds = police special force (1989); Bhargava = Emergency encounters (1977).
~~ Sources: AP Reorganisation Act 2014 Gazette text s.9; en.wikipedia.org S. R. Sankaran (2004 talks); old app notes row 264

=== Impact on Tribal Areas
| Measure | Year / place | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| Regulation 1 of 1970 (AP Scheduled Areas Land Transfer) | 1970 | Barred transfer of Scheduled Area land from tribals to non-tribals |
| ITDAs (Integrated Tribal Development Agencies) | Fifth Plan (1974–75), Tribal Sub-Plan | Single-line tribal development (S.R. Sankaran) |
| Girijan Cooperative Corporation (GCC) | HQ Visakhapatnam; since 1956 | Buys **minor forest produce**, supplies essentials; frees tribals from moneylenders |
- GCC (1956) is older than the movement – not a response created after Srikakulam.
