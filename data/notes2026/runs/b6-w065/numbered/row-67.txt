## SUB 0 | India–US Trade Deal (February 2026)
[0:0] # Tariff Cut
[0:1] - **Reciprocal tariff 25% → 18%**; the **additional 25% punitive duty** (imposed **August 2025** over **Russian oil** purchases) **removed**.
[0:2] - Effective burden **~50% → 18%** – lower than **Vietnam/Bangladesh (~20%)** and **China (30–35%)**.
[0:3] - India's expected commitments: cut Russian crude, buy from the US (and possibly **Venezuela**); lower tariffs / NTBs; purchases **up to USD 500 billion** of US energy, agriculture, coal, technology.
[0:4] - **Donald Trump** claimed India "agreed to **stop buying Russian oil**".
[0:5] - India has **not regained GSP** (withdrawn in **2019**).
[0:6] > The deal did **not** restore GSP; US tariff is now **18%**, not zero.

[0:9] # Bilateral Trade Agreement – Numbers
[0:10] - What India gets: **18%** rate on **USD 900 bn** of US imports; **zero duty on USD 150 bn**; no additional duty on **USD 720 bn**; continued exemption on **USD 350 bn**; preferential treatment on **Section 232** tariffs.

[0:12] - **Section 232** (national-security tariffs): zero reciprocal duty for **USD 28.30 bn** of exports.

[0:17] - **Fully protected**: **dairy, poultry, meat, GM foods, millets, maize, cereals, pulses, oilseeds, honey, ethanol, tobacco**.
[0:18] - Tools: phased elimination up to **10 years**; **Tariff Rate Quotas** (quantity-limited, not unlimited).
