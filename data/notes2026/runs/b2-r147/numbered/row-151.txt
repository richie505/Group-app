## SUB 0 | Nepal
[0:0] - **Treaty of Peace and Friendship, 1950**: open border, security cooperation; Nepal seeks revision.
[0:1] - **First Constituent Assembly** abolished the monarchy – **28 May 2008**; new Constitution **20 September 2015**.
[0:2] - **Dr. Ram Baran Yadav** – **first (elected) President**, July 2008 (not G.P. Koirala or Paramananda Jha, the first Vice-President).
[0:3] - **Pushpa Kamal Dahal 'Prachanda'** (UCPN-Maoist) – **first PM** of the Federal Democratic Republic, August 2008.
[0:4] - **K.P. Sharma Oli** – PM for the **third time** in 2021 (APPSC Endowment 2022); fourth term July 2024 – September 2025 (fell in the Gen-Z protests; **Sushila Karki** interim PM).
[0:5] - **March 2026**: **Balen Shah** (Rastriya Swatantra Party) became Nepal's **youngest PM (35)**; youth-led government pushes to revise the 1950 Treaty.
[0:6] - Water: **Kosi (1954)**, **Gandak (1959)**, **Mahakali Treaty 1996** (Sarada, Tanakpur, **Pancheshwar**) – Nepal, not Bangladesh; Arun III; hydro potential **83,000 MW**.
[0:7] - Dispute: **Kalapani–Limpiyadhura–Lipulekh** (Sugauli Treaty 1816; Nepal's new map 2020).
[0:8] - **Operation Maitri** – India's **earthquake relief, 2015**.
[0:9] - **First trilateral power deal**: Nepal supplies Bangladesh through the **Indian grid**.

## SUB 1 | Bangladesh
[1:0] # Land boundary
[1:1] - **Longest land border**: Bangladesh **4,096.7 km** (then China 3,488, Pakistan 3,323, Nepal 1,751, Myanmar 1,643, Bhutan 699, Afghanistan ~106 – shortest).
[1:2] - **Land Boundary Agreement**: signed **1974** (Indira–Mujib); ratified by the **100th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2015**: **111 Indian enclaves → Bangladesh**, **51 Bangladeshi enclaves → India** (162 in all); **6.1 km** undemarcated boundary settled; territory of **four States** (Assam, West Bengal, Meghalaya, Tripura) changed.
[1:3] - **Tin Bigha**: corridor **leased** to Bangladesh in **1992** for access to **Dahagram-Angarpota** enclaves (UPSC 1999: Kachchativu and Tin Bigha were handed to Sri Lanka and Bangladesh respectively).
[1:4] - **Dawki (Meghalaya)–Tamabil (Sylhet)** – India–**Bangladesh** border crossing (not China).
[1:5] - **Border haat** at **Bholaganj** (Companiganj, Sylhet) inaugurated **May 2023**.
[1:6] # Ties and connectivity
[1:7] - **Maitri Diwas – 6 December**: India **recognised Bangladesh on 6 December 1971**.
[1:8] - **Maitri Express** Kolkata–Dhaka: launched **April 2008**.
[1:9] - **July 2020**: first container ship **Kolkata → Agartala** via **Chattogram (Chittagong) port**.
[1:10] - Water: **Farakka Accord 1977**; **Ganga Water Treaty 1996** (Farakka, **30 years**, Gujral Doctrine); **Teesta** still unsettled.
[1:11] - **Akhaura–Agartala** rail link; **Friendship Pipeline** (diesel); **Rooppur** (nuclear, Russia-built) and **Maitree** (thermal, Rampal).
[1:12] - Politics: **two-party system** – Awami League vs BNP (UPSC 1996 key); after the 2024 upheaval, **Tarique Rahman (BNP)** government sworn in **February 2026** (Speaker Om Birla represented India). Rohingya refugee issue (from Myanmar).

## SUB 2 | Sri Lanka
[2:0] - **Indo-Sri Lanka Accord, 1987**: **Rajiv Gandhi–J.R. Jayewardene**; merger of **Northern and Eastern** provinces; **13th Amendment** (provincial councils); **IPKF (Operation Pawan)** – ended up fighting the LTTE; withdrawn on **V.P. Singh**'s order (last troops **24 March 1990**).
[2:1] - **LTTE** killed **Rajiv Gandhi (1991)** → India banned it **1992** (not part of the 1987 Accord). Banned also by **Sri Lanka and the USA** (UPPSC 1998: India, Sri Lanka, USA). **2009**: Sri Lankan Army crushed the LTTE; chief **V. Prabhakaran** killed.
[2:2] - **Katchatheevu** ceded to Sri Lanka in **1974**; fishermen issue.
[2:3] - Leaders: **Chandrika Kumaratunga** – first woman **President** (1994); Sirimavo Bandaranaike – world's first woman **PM** (1960). **Mahinda Rajapaksa** became PM in **November 2019** (brother Gotabaya – President).
[2:4] - In the news: banned **drones and unmanned aircraft** after the **Easter Sunday bombings (April 2019)**; waived tourist-visa fee for **48 countries** (2019); **Colombo** – largest city; Knuckles range is in Sri Lanka.
[2:5] - India's aid: economic crisis support **~$4 billion (2022)**; **KKS (Kankesanthurai)** port; **Dickoya district hospital**; DTAA protocol (2020). **Hambantota** = Chinese, not Pakistani, encirclement.

## SUB 3 | Myanmar, Bhutan, Maldives and Afghanistan
[3:0] # Myanmar
[3:1] - **Kaladan Multi-Modal Transit Transport Project** (conceived 2008): **Kolkata → Sittwe port (Myanmar) → Kaladan river and road → Mizoram** – with **Myanmar** (APPSC 2018).
[3:2] - **India–Myanmar–Thailand Trilateral Highway** (~1,360 km): **Moreh (Manipur)–Bagan–Mae Sot (Thailand)**.
[3:3] - **Military coup, 1 February 2021** – Senior General **Min Aung Hlaing**; India engages the junta for Act East and north-east security.
[3:4] - Burma joined the UN in 1948 and stayed neutral – refused to name China the aggressor in the Korean War (NDA 2019). **Kyaiktiyo (Golden Rock) pagoda** is in Myanmar.
[3:5] # Bhutan
[3:6] - **Treaty of Friendship 1949**, revised **2007** – removed the clause that Bhutan be "**guided**" by India in **foreign affairs**.
[3:7] - India **imports hydropower from Bhutan** (APPSC G2 2017): Chukha, Tala, **Mangdechhu (720 MW, inaugurated 2019)**, Punatsangchhu I and II.
[3:8] - **Doklam (2017)** – India-Bhutan-China tri-junction.
[3:9] # Maldives
[3:10] - Smallest neighbour (≈300 sq km). **Operation Cactus (1988)** – India foiled a coup.
[3:11] - **Sheikh Imran Abdulla** – Home Minister, visited India February 2020. "**India Out**" campaign → ties repaired; PM visited 2025; **Favara** linked with **UPI** (Aug 2026); **Greater Male Connectivity** = bridge-causeway (not a tunnel); India built the **Institute of Security and Law Enforcement Studies**.
[3:12] # Afghanistan
[3:13] - **Salma Dam** (Afghan-India Friendship Dam, 42 MW), **Hari Rud, Herat** – inaugurated by Modi and Ghani, **June 2016**.
[3:14] - **Zaranj–Delaram highway** (218 km, **BRO**); **Afghan Parliament building** (Modi, 2015); **Stor Palace** restored with the **Aga Khan Trust for Culture** (2016); India-funded **public library** (Kabul, in news Jan 2019).
[3:15] - Nehru visited Kabul (Sept 1959). After the **Taliban takeover (Aug 2021)**: wheat, medicines, vaccines; **technical team in Kabul (2022)**.
