## SUB 2 | Panchsheel 1954
[147:2:0] - Preamble of the **India–Tibet Region of China Agreement on Trade and Intercourse**, **29 April 1954** (Nehru–**Zhou Enlai**).
[147:2:1] - Five principles: respect for **territorial integrity and sovereignty**; **non-aggression**; **non-interference**; **equality and mutual benefit**; **peaceful co-existence**.
[147:2:2] **Non-alignment is NOT** a Panchsheel principle (UPSC 1997).
[147:2:3] - Burma adopted it later in **1954**; Bandung Ten Principles **1955**; **UNGA 1957**: principle of international law; Belgrade **1961**: core NAM principle.
[147:2:4] - Don't confuse: **Tribal Panchsheel (1952)** — Nehru with **Verrier Elwin**, tribal policy.
[147:2:5] - **Sep 2025:** Modi–Xi meeting: China stressed Panchsheel.
## SUB 3 | Non-aligned Movement
[147:3:0] - **Bandung Conference, April 1955, Indonesia**: 29 Afro-Asian countries; **Ten Principles** (included Panchsheel).
[147:3:1] - Founded at **1st Belgrade Summit, 1961 (Yugoslavia)**. Initiated by **Nehru** (UPPSC 2014).
[147:3:2] - Non-alignment **≠ neutrality**: judging each issue on merits.

## SUB 5 | Core Principles
[147:5:0] - **Anti-colonialism, anti-apartheid**.
[147:5:1] - **Disarmament**; opposed the **NPT (1968)** as discriminatory.
[147:5:2] - **Peaceful settlement**; UNSC reform through **"reformed multilateralism"**.
[147:5:3] - **Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam** ("world is one family"): G20 2023 theme **"One Earth, One Family, One Future"**.
## SUB 6 | Institutions and Nuclear Doctrine
[147:6:0] - **CCS**: PM + Finance, External Affairs, Home, **Defence**; NSA, Cabinet Secretary, service chiefs **invitees**.
[147:6:1] - **NSC (1998)**: NSA is its secretary; first NSA **Brajesh Mishra**.
[147:6:2] - **Standing Committee on External Affairs**: scrutinises MEA.
[147:6:3] - **ICWA**: 1943, **Tej Bahadur Sapru**, Sapru House; **Vice-President** ex officio President; **ICWA Act 2001**.
[147:6:4] - **Nuclear doctrine (4 January 2003, CCS)**: **credible minimum deterrence**, **No First Use**, retaliation only. **Nuclear Command Authority** = **Political Council** (chaired by **PM**; sole body to authorise use) + **Executive Council** (chaired by **NSA**).
[147:6:5] - **Committee on External Affairs:** one of the **24 Departmentally Related Standing Committees**; serviced by the **Lok Sabha**; its Chairman is appointed by the **Speaker**; term **one year**. **Shashi Tharoor** was reappointed Chairman in the **2026** reconstitution.

=== NOTES 2:148 | Phases of Indian Foreign Policy
## SUB 0 | Six Phases (jaishankar)
[148:0:0] TABLE head: Phase | Label | Features
  [148:0:0.0] 1946–62 | Optimistic non-alignment | Idealism, Panchsheel, NAM, Commonwealth, anti-imperialism
  [148:0:0.1] 1962–71 | Realism and recovery | 1965 war, Tashkent (1966); no NPT (1968)
  [148:0:0.2] 1971–91 | Regional assertion | Leading power in South Asia
  [148:0:0.3] 1991–98 | Safeguarding strategic autonomy | USSR collapse, 1991 crisis
  [148:0:0.4] 1998–2013 | Balancing power | Pokhran-II, NFU, Indo-US deal (NSG waiver 2008)
  [148:0:0.5] 2014 onwards | Energetic engagement | Multi-alignment
[148:0:1] - Idealism → realism; non-alignment → multi-alignment; the constant is **strategic autonomy**.
