=== Ashtadiggajas, Bhuvana Vijayam and the Golden Age of Telugu
# The Ashtadiggajas
- **Who they were:** **eight Telugu poets** ("eight elephants" holding up the eight directions) – not ministers, warriors or advisers (UP UDA 2001).
- **Patron:** **Krishnadevaraya** (APPSC G2 2019; AP History PYQ; RAS 2010) – not Rama Raya, Achyutaraya, Tirumala Raya, Devaraya II, Shivaji or Rajendra I.
- **Golden age:** his reign (1509–29) = **golden age of Telugu literature**, the **Prabandha** era (UP UDA 2006) – not Konkani, Malayalam or Tamil.
- **Why we remember him:** for his **literary patronage** (AP History PYQ) – the Ashtadiggajas in the **Bhuvana Vijayam**.
# Poet → work → title
| Poet | Main work | Title / note |
|---|---|---|
| **Allasani Peddana** | ***Manucharitramu*** (Swarochisha Manu Sambhavam) | **Andhra Kavita Pitamaha**; greatest of the eight; poet of **Telugu and Sanskrit** |
| **Nandi Timmana** | *Parijatapaharanamu* | **Mukku Timmana**; the king's favourite |
| **Dhurjati** | *Sri Kalahasteeswara Mahatyam* and Satakam | Shaiva poet of Kalahasti |
| **Madayyagari Mallana** | *Rajasekhara Charitramu* | Early social narrative |
| **Ayyalaraju Ramabhadrudu** | *Ramabhyudayamu*, Sakalakathasara Sangrahamu | Ramayana in Telugu |
| **Pingali Surana** | *Raghava Pandaviyamu*, *Kalapurnodayamu*, Prabhavati Pradyumnamu | **Dvyarthi kavya** (double meaning) |
| **Ramarajabhushana (Bhattumurti)** | *Vasucharitramu*, Narasabhupaliyamu | Vasucharitra dedicated to **Tirumala Raya** |
| **Tenali Ramakrishna** | ***Panduranga Mahatmyamu*** (also Ghatikachala Mahatmyam, Udbhataradhya Charitra) | Court wit |
- **Manucharitramu:** by **Allasani Peddana** (APPSC G2 2019; IAS 2007) – not Kalapurnodayam or Raghava Pandaviyam (Pingali Surana), or Haravilasam (Srinatha).
- **Andhra Kavita Pitamaha:** **Allasani Peddana** (Raj. PCS 2023; WBCS 2007) – not Bhattumurti, Tenali Ramakrishna, Pingali Surana, Srinatha, Sayana or Madhava. **Andhra Bhoja** is the king's own title.
- **Panduranga Mahatyam:** **Tenali Ramakrishna** (APPSC Jr Asst) – not Peddana, Dhurjati or the king.
- **RAS 2021 statements:** Krishnadevaraya wrote Amuktamalyada ✓; he is called Andhra Bhoja ✓; Peddana "well versed in **Sanskrit and Tamil**" ✗ (he was a **Telugu and Sanskrit** poet) → **1 and 2 only**.
- **Gandapenderam:** the king himself fixed this anklet on **Peddana's** foot.
- **"Prabandha Parameshwara"** is **Errana's** title, not Nandi Timmana's.
# Bhuvana Vijayam and the Tippalur grant
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Bhuvana Vijayam** | "Conquest of the world"; Krishnadevaraya's court hall of poets; **House of Victory** |
| **Ashtadiggajas** | Eight elephants of the eight directions → eight pillar-poets |
| **Kavula Samstha** | Institution of poets – the **Tippalur inscription's** name for them |
- **Tippalur inscription:** gift of **Tippalur agrahara** (Kadapa) to the poets by **Krishnadevaraya**, not Achyutaraya.
# Languages patronised
- Vijayanagara patronised **Telugu, Kannada, Sanskrit and Tamil** – all four (Grama Sachivalayam) – so "**Tamil, Telugu and Sanskrit**" is the SSC 2007 answer (Hindi, Marathi, Malayalam, Urdu are wrong).
- **Kannada:** **Mallanarya** of Krishnadevaraya's court wrote *Bhava Chinta Ratna* and *Veerasaivamrita* in **Kannada** (Raj. PCS 2024).
- **Correct combination (Grama Sachivalayam):** **Krishnadevaraya – Amuktamalyada – Sahiti Samarangana Sarvabhauma** ✓. Wrong: Hala's title is not Devanampriya (Ashoka's); Peddana wrote **Manucharitra**, not Vasucharitra; Srinatha's title is **Kavisarvabhauma**.

=== Amuktamalyada and Krishnadevaraya's Own Works
# Amuktamalyada
- **Author:** **Krishnadevaraya** himself (APPSC AEE; Tribal Welfare Officer; AP History PYQ) – not Peddana, Tenali Ramakrishna, Nannaya, Nanne Choda, Palkuriki Somanatha, Gona Budda Reddy, Gangamba or Praudha Devaraya.
- **Language:** **Telugu** – his only Telugu work; a work on **statecraft** (rajaniti) as well as devotion. NDA 2017 "NOT correct": "**wrote Amuktamalyada in Sanskrit**".
- **Other name:** **Vishnuchittiyam**; meaning "**one who offers the garland she has worn**".
- **Legend:** **Andhra Mahavishnu of Srikakulam** (Krishna district) told the king in a **dream** to write it, while he was **resting at Srikakulam** during the **Kalinga campaign**; dedicated to **Lord Venkateswara**.
- **Central theme:** the **wedding of Goda Devi (Andal) with Sri Ranganatha** (Endowment 2022) – not his victories, the Ashtadiggajas or his literary achievements; sub-stories **Yamunacharya Charitra** and **Maladasari Katha**.
- **Political maxims:** kingship, administration and statecraft; social, religious and economic life.
- Preface: "**Desa Bhashalandu Telugu Lessa**" (Telugu is the best of the regional languages).
- One of the **Telugu Pancha Kavyas**; the **first Telugu work named after its heroine**.
# What Amuktamalyada does NOT contain (G2 2003; AP History PYQ)
- Its preface **describes the king's Kalinga campaign** (the march to Vijayawada and the halt at Srikakulam), and the work **discusses political matters**.
- It does **not** reveal a **genealogy from Bukkaraya to Krishnadevaraya** – the Tuluvas were not descendants of Bukka → this is the answer.
- ⚠ Some keys of the G2 2003 paper print "describes the invasions"; that is wrong, since the preface narrates the Kalinga campaign.

| Work | Language |

| **Jambavati Kalyanam / Parinayam** (drama; Krishna weds Jambavati; mentions Virupaksha, Vasantotsavams) | **Sanskrit** (APPSC G2 2019; G2 Mains; AP History PYQ) |
| Usha Parinayam | Sanskrit |
| Madalasa Charita | Sanskrit |
| Rasamanjari | Sanskrit |
| Jnana Chintamani | Sanskrit |
| Satyavadhu Parinayam | Sanskrit |
- **Court musician:** **Lakshminatha** wrote *Sangita Suryodaya*.

=== The Saluva Dynasty and the Two Usurpations
# Decline of the Sangamas
- **Nuniz** called **Virupaksha II** a drunkard and **Praudha Raya** worthless; Gajapati and Bahmani invasions ate into the kingdom.
# The two usurpations
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **1485** | **First usurpation:** **Saluva Narasimha** of Chandragiri expels **Praudha Raya**; ends the **Sangama** dynasty, begins the **Saluva** dynasty |
| **1505** | **Second usurpation:** **Vira Narasimha** (son of Tuluva Narasa Nayaka) kills **Immadi Narasimha**, the last Saluva, at **Penugonda**; begins the **Tuluva** dynasty |
| 1509 | **Krishnadevaraya** crowned on **8 August 1509** |
| 1529 | **Achyutaraya** crowned at Tirupati |
| 1543 | Sadasiva made a figurehead under Aliya Rama Raya |
| Jan 1565 | **Talikota** (Rakshasi-Tangadi / Bannihatti): Rama Raya beheaded; flight to Penukonda |
- **Dynasty after the first usurpation:** **Saluva** (AP History PYQ). **Came to power:** **1485** (G2 2012).
- **Founder of the Saluva dynasty:** **Saluva Narasimha (Narasimharaya)** (SSC 2005; AP History PYQ) – not Immadi Narasimha (last Saluva), Vira Narasimha (Tuluva founder), Praudha Devaraya or Rama Raya.
- **UPSC 2003 A–R:** A "Saluva Narasimha ended the old dynasty and took the royal title" ✓; R "to save the kingdom from further degeneration and disintegration" ✓ and **explains A**.
- **IAS 2004 (×3): all four correct** – Saluva Narasimha ended the Sangamas; Vira Narasimha deposed the last Saluva; Vira Narasimha → younger brother **Krishnadevaraya**; Krishnadevaraya → half-brother **Achyuta Raya**.
- **Chronology (APSLPRB 2023):** Deva Raya II (Sangama) → Saluva Narasimha → Achyuta Deva Raya (Tuluva) → Venkatapati II (Aravidu) = **ii, iv, i, iii**.
# Saluva Narasimha (1485–91)
| Angle | Fact |
|---|---|
| Origin | **Telugu** clan from Kalyana; governor of **Chandragiri** |
| Capital | Kalyanapuram → **Chandragiri** |
| Title | **Raya Maharasu**; his general **Tuluva Narasa Nayaka** took the throne for him |
| Army | **Arab horses from Ormuz**, Arab cavalry; introduced **Samugaris** (gunmen) |
| Lost | **Udayagiri** to Gajapati **Purushottama** |
| Court poet | **Rajanatha Dindima** – *Saluvabhyudayam* |
| Contemporary | **Annamacharya** (Tallapaka, 1408–1503): **32,000 kirtanas** on Venkateswara; wife Timmakka wrote Subhadra Kalyanam |
- **Annamayya was a contemporary of:** **Saluva Narasimha Raya** (APPSC G2 2011; AP History PYQ) – not Harihara, Devaraya I, II or III.

=== Tuluva Succession, Court and Contemporaries
# Tuluva origin
- From **Tuluvanadu** near Mysore; progenitor **Timmaraju**; mother tongue **Kannada**, but **Telugu** favoured at court.
- **Tuluva Narasa Nayaka:** Saluva general and real ruler under Immadi Narasimha; died 1503. **Founder of the Tuluva dynasty = Vira Narasimha** (1505), not Narasa Nayaka.
- **Krishnadevaraya belonged to the Tuluva dynasty** (AMVI 2023) – not Pandya, Kakatiya or Qutb Shahi.
# Succession
| Ruler | Immediate successor |
|---|---|
| Praudha Deva Raya | Saluva Narasimha |
| Immadi Narasimha | Vira Narasimha |
| **Vira Narasimha** (1505–09) | **Krishnadevaraya** (his younger half-brother) |
| **Krishnadevaraya** (1509–29) | **Achyuta Deva Raya** (his half-brother) |
- **King before Krishnadevaraya:** **Vira Narasimha** (G2-A 1989). **After him:** **Achyuta Devaraya** (Asst. Archaeologist 2011) – not Rama Raya or Sadasiva.
- **Aravidu rule began with:** **Tirumala Deva Raya** (Penukonda).
- **Death of Krishnadevaraya:** **1529** (NextGen; reign often written 1509–1529/30).
# Court
- **Prime Minister:** **Saluva Timmarusu (Appaji)** – minister of **Krishnadevaraya** (AP History PYQ), not Saluva Narasimha, Rajaraja Narendra or Kyatya Varma; later blinded on suspicion.
- **Rajaguru:** **Vyasatirtha (Vyasaraya)**, Madhva saint (AP History PYQ) – not Vidyaranya (Sangama founders' guru). His disciple **Purandaradasa** (father of Carnatic music) was a contemporary.
# Contemporaries
- **Babur** (1526–30): Krishnadevaraya was his contemporary (FRO 2009; SI 2011); Babur praised him in the **Tuzuk-i-Baburi** as the most powerful ruler of India. Not Akbar, Aurangzeb, Firoz Tughlaq or Alauddin Khalji.

=== Krishnadevaraya's Campaigns – Raichur, Golconda and Kalinga
# Campaigns
| Campaign | Year | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Diwani / Kovilkonda** | 1509–10 | Bahmani "jihad" crushed; **Yusuf Adil Khan** killed |
| Raichur Doab and Bidar | 1510–12 | Raichur taken; freed **Sultan Mahmud Shah** from Barid → title **Yavanarajya Sthapanacharya** |
| **Ummattur / Sivasamudram** | 1512–13 | **Gangaraja** defeated; new province at **Srirangapatna** |
| **Kalinga** | **1513–1519** | Udayagiri, Kondaveedu, Kondapalli, Simhachalam, Cuttack (1518) |
| **Battle of Raichur** | **1520** | **Ismail Adil Shah** of Bijapur routed with Portuguese help; **Gulbarga** taken **1523** |
- **Bijapur Sultan at Raichur (1520):** **Ismail Adil Khan / Shah** (G2 2000; AP History PYQ) – not Ahmed Barid, Muhammad Shah I or Mujahid Shah.
- **"Great battle of 1520":** the battle of Raichur, which Telugu texts name **Gobburu** (the field near Raichur) – G2-A 1989; **Mudgal** was Devaraya I vs Firoz Shah Bahmani.
- **Battle of Golconda:** fought against **Quli Qutb Shah** of Golconda (BPSC 1999), who had taken forts while the king fought the Gajapatis – not Qutbuddin Aibak, Ismail Adil Shah or the Gajapati.
- **Yusuf Adil Khan** (Kovilkonda, 1509–10) ≠ **Ismail Adil Shah** (Raichur, 1520).
# The Kalinga campaign against Prataparudra Gajapati
| Fort / place | What happened |
|---|---|
| **Udayagiri** (1513–14) | Fell after an **18-month** siege; **Balakrishna** idol taken to Hampi (Krishna temple built for it) |
| **Kondaveedu** | Taken with **Saluva Timmarusu's** help (split Veerabhadra Gajapati's army); king performed **Talapurusha Danam at Amaravati** |
| Kondapalli and Bejawada | Gajapati forces defeated |
| Vinukonda, Addanki, Kandukuru | Also taken |
| **Cuttack** | Reached in **1518**; Gajapati sued for peace |
- **Idol taken from Udayagiri:** **Balakrishna** (G2-A 1989) – not Ramachandra, Ugranarasimha or Virupaksha.
- **Victory pillar (Jayastambha) for the conquest of coastal Andhra:** **Potnuru** (Padmanabham mandal, near Visakhapatnam; Bezawada inscription 1516: "Simhadri-Potnuru") – AP History PYQ; G2 PYQ. Not Kondaveedu, Kondapalli, Udayagiri, Rajahmundry or Nagarjunakonda.
- ⚠ The old note "APPSC key (2025) gave Kondaveedu" is unverified; Kondaveedu was a fort taken, not the pillar site.
- **Temple grants:** **Simhachalam** – diamond necklace to Varaha Narasimha; **Mangalagiri** – land to **Lakshmi Narasimha**.
- **Peace terms:** Prataparudra's daughter (**Tukka / Bhadradevi**) married Krishnadevaraya; lands **north of the Krishna** went back to the Gajapati.
- **Salt:** **Pedaganjam and Chinaganjam** were famous for salt; the salt pans at **Pedaganjam** were taxed.

=== Achyutaraya, Sadasiva and Aliya Rama Raya
- **Achyutaraya** (1529–42): named heir by Krishnadevaraya; crowned at **Tirupati** and **Srikalahasti** (the only king crowned at Tirumala-Tirupati).
- **Assembly hall:** Achyutaraya's sabha = **Venkatavilasa Mantapam** (AP History PYQ); Krishnadevaraya's = **Bhuvana Vijayam**. (NextGen also has Venkatapati II's literary meets at "Venkatapati Vilasam".)
- **Aliya Rama Raya:** Krishnadevaraya's **son-in-law**, of the **Aravidu** family; killed **Salakam Tirumala** (1543); made **Sadasiva** a figurehead.
- **Talikota** (January **1565**), also **Rakshasi-Tangadi** / Bannihatti: Rama Raya beheaded; Sadasiva and Tirumala fled to **Penukonda**. **Last Tuluva ruler = Sadasiva**.
- **Latest – Sadasivakona inscriptions** (ASI, **June 2026**): three **trilingual** (Telugu, Tamil, Kannada) inscriptions of **1554 CE** in the **Seshachalam forest, Tirupati district**; record Sadasiva Raya's holy bath and transfer of **Kaanika** taxes from Gudimallam temple lands.
