## SUB 2 | Tuluva Dynasty – Origin and Succession
[120:2:0] # Origin
[120:2:1] - Progenitor: **Timmaraju**; mother tongue **Kannada**, but **Telugu** favoured at court.
[120:2:2] - **Tuluva Narasa Nayaka**: Saluva general and real ruler under Immadi Narasimha; died 1503.
[120:2:3] - **Founder = Vira Narasimha** (APPSC G2 2002).
[120:2:4] Tuluva Narasa Nayaka helped both usurpations (1485 as general; 1505 as father of the usurper), but the founder of the Tuluva dynasty is **Vira Narasimha**.
[120:2:5] # Succession and Immediate Successors
[120:2:6] - Vira Narasimha (1505-09) → **Krishnadevaraya** (1509-29, his half-brother) → **Achyutaraya** (1529-42) → Venkata I (1542) → **Sadasiva** (1543-70/76).
[120:2:7] TABLE head: Ruler | Immediate successor
  [120:2:7.0] Praudha Deva Raya | Narasimha Saluva
  [120:2:7.1] Immadi Narasimha | Vira Narasimha
  [120:2:7.2] Vira Narasimha | Krishnadevaraya
  [120:2:7.3] Krishnadevaraya | Achyutadeva Raya
[120:2:8] - Other transitions: **Tirumala Deva Raya** began **Aravidu** rule.
[120:2:9] - Prime Minister of Krishnadevaraya: **Saluva Timmarusu (Appaji)**.
[120:2:10] - **Rajaguru:** **Vyasatirtha (Vyasaraya)**, Madhva saint; his disciple **Purandaradasa** (father of Carnatic music) was a contemporary

## SUB 7 | Krishnadevaraya's Works
[120:7:0] # Amuktamalyada
[120:7:1] - **Telugu**; also called **Vishnuchittiyam**; meaning "one who offers the garland she has worn".
[120:7:2] - **Legend**: **Andhra Mahavishnu of Srikakulam** (Krishna district) told the king in a **dream** to write it; written during the **Kalinga campaign**; dedicated to **Lord Venkateswara**.
[120:7:3] - **Theme**: **Goda Devi (Andal)** and her marriage to **Sri Ranganatha**; sub-stories **Yamunacharya Charitra** and **Maladasari Katha**.
[120:7:4] - **Political maxims**: statecraft (**rajaniti**), kingship and administration; social, religious and economic life; references to his **campaigns** (but **no** genealogy from Bukka).
[120:7:5] - Preface: "**Desa Bhashalandu Telugu Lessa**" (Telugu is the best of the regional languages).
[120:7:6] - One of the **Telugu Pancha Kavyas**; **first Telugu work named after its heroine**.
[120:7:7] APPSC G2 2003: Amuktamalyada does NOT give a genealogy from **Bukka** to Krishnadevaraya; it does mention his campaigns.

## SUB 11 | Achyutaraya, Sadasiva and Rama Raya's Regency
[120:11:0] - **Achyutaraya** (1529-42): named heir; the **only king crowned at Tirupati** (and Srikalahasti).
[120:11:1] - **Aliya Rama Raya**: Krishnadevaraya's **son-in-law**, of the **Aravidu** family; killed Salakam Tirumala (1543); made **Sadasiva** a figurehead.
[120:11:2] - Talikota (January 1565): Rama Raya beheaded; Sadasiva and Tirumala fled to **Penukonda**. **Last Tuluva ruler = Sadasiva**.
[120:11:3] - **Sadasivakona inscriptions** (found by ASI, June 2026): three **trilingual** (Telugu, Tamil, Kannada) inscriptions of **1554 CE**, Seshachalam forest, Tirupati district; record Sadasiva Raya's holy bath and transfer of **Kaanika** taxes from Gudimallam temple lands.
[120:11:4] - AP economy: **Pedaganjam and Chinaganjam** were famous for **salt**; salt pans at Pedaganjam were taxed (PYQ 2019).
