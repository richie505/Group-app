## 4. Keys that changed or need care
- **Amuktamalyada – what does NOT belong (G2 2003):** printed "describes the invasions" → **genealogy from Bukkaraya**. The preface narrates the Kalinga campaign (march to Vijayawada, halt at Srikakulam); the Tuluvas did not descend from Bukka. The AP History PYQ copy already had this key.
- **Marriage tax totally withdrawn (G1 PYQ):** printed Sadasivaraya → **Krishnadevaraya** (Rocket p.18; NextGen previous papers p.45; G1 2010 key). ⚠ dispute line kept; Sadasiva/Rama Raya inscriptions record the **barbers'** tax remission. Own MCQ f55a0b88b3da9 changed the same way; old notes "abolished under Sadasiva" dropped.
- **Battle of 1520 = "Gobburu":** key kept; no source gives the name Gobburu, but it is the only option for the Raichur battle (Mudgal = Devaraya I).
- **Potnuru victory pillar:** kept; old note "APPSC key 2025 gave Kondaveedu" not confirmed – inscriptions and the pillar site support Potnuru.
- **Imperial council president:** Prime Minister kept; ⚠ Sabhanayaka is the same office.
- **Village headman:** Gowda kept; ⚠ Reddi is the Andhra name.
- **Venkatavilasa Mantapam (Achyutaraya):** kept from the app notes only; NextGen has "Venkatapati Vilasam" under Venkatapati II – owner may want to verify.
