=== NOTES 3:75 | Balance of Payments - Structure, Current and Capital Account, Convertibility, Forex Reserves
## SUB 0 | Meaning of Balance of Payments
[75:0:0] - **Balance of Payments (BoP)**: a **systematic and complete record of all economic transactions** between the residents of a country and the rest of the world during a period, **normally one year**.
[75:0:1] - BoP has a **current account and a capital account** (PYQ 2025). The term relates to exports and imports.
[75:0:2] - **IMF BPM6** uses **three accounts** (current, capital, financial); **RBI combines** capital and financial as "**Capital Account**".
[75:0:3] - BoP includes **visible trade, invisible trade and borrowings**.
[75:0:4] # Balance of Trade vs Balance of Payments
[75:0:5] TABLE head: Basis | Balance of Trade | Balance of Payments
  [75:0:5.0] Coverage | Goods only (visible) | All transactions: goods, services, transfers, capital
  [75:0:5.1] Formula | Goods exports − goods imports | Whole external account, double-entry
  [75:0:5.2] Place | Part of the current account | Current + capital/financial account
  [75:0:5.3] Picture | Not the complete record | Better, complete picture
[75:0:6] - **BoT surplus**: goods exports > goods imports; **BoT deficit**: goods imports > goods exports.
[75:0:7] BoT = goods only; current account balance = goods and services + transfers (+ income).
## SUB 1 | Structure - Current Account vs Capital Account
[75:1:0] TABLE head: Current account | Capital (and financial) account
  [75:1:0.0] Merchandise exports and imports (visible trade; trade balance) | FDI, FPI/FII, ADRs/GDRs
  [75:1:0.1] Invisibles - services: software, travel, transport, banking, insurance | ECB, external assistance
  [75:1:0.2] Invisibles - primary income: interest, dividends, profits, wages | NRI deposits (e.g. FCNR(B)), banking capital
  [75:1:0.3] Secondary income: remittances, gifts, grants | Short-term trade credit; change in forex reserves
[75:1:1] # Current account
[75:1:2] - Includes **goods, services, income and transfers**; it includes the Balance of Trade.
[75:1:3] - **Invisibles** = services + primary income + transfers. Services = **non-factor** (shipping, banking, tourism, software) + factor income.
[75:1:4] - **Private remittances** = current account → invisibles → private transfers.
[75:1:5] - A **gift to foreigners** = **debit** in the current account.
[75:1:6] # Capital account
[75:1:7] - Cross-border capital movements: **foreign loans, FDI, portfolio investment**, purchase and sale of assets. **FDI is NOT current account.**
[75:1:8] - Residents **buying foreign assets = debit**; **sale of Indian assets to foreigners = credit**.
[75:1:9] TABLE head: Item | BoP placement
  [75:1:9.0] Software services exports | Current account (services)
  [75:1:9.1] Foreign loans | Capital account
  [75:1:9.2] Remittances | Current transfers
  [75:1:9.3] Crude oil imports | Merchandise trade
## SUB 2 | Accounting Identity - What Always Balances
[75:2:0] - **Current Account + Capital Account + Financial Account + Net Errors & Omissions = 0**.
[75:2:1] - BoP **always balances** in accounting terms: a surplus in one account = deficit in another. BoT need not balance.
[75:2:2] - **Current account balance** = (exports − imports of goods and services) + net primary income + net secondary income (APPSC 2019: goods and services + net unilateral transfers).
[75:2:3] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [75:2:3.0] Autonomous transactions | Happen for their own sake
  [75:2:3.1] Accommodating transactions | Fill the gap: reserve changes, official borrowing
  [75:2:3.2] Financing a CAD | Capital account surplus (net inflow) or drawing down reserves; monetary authority = ultimate financier
  [75:2:3.3] Overall BoP | Positive → adds to reserves; negative → draws them down; reserves run short → BoP crisis
## SUB 3 | Current Account Deficit - Trend
[75:3:0] - **CAD**: payments exceed receipts from goods, services, transfers and net income (excess of total imports over total exports).

  [75:3:2.2] FY13 | Peak 4.8% of GDP (taper tantrum)

  [75:3:2.4] Q4 2023-24 | Surplus - surge in services exports (PYQ 2025)

[75:3:3] Peak CAD 4.8% (FY13) is higher than the 1990-91 crisis level of 3.1-3.2%.
[75:3:4] - **FY24 external recovery**: moderation in merchandise export growth plus a **sharp fall in import growth** (G1 Prelims 2024). Services exports cushioned the deficit (up 4.85% to **USD 341.06 bn**, FY 2023-24).
[75:3:5] - Goods trade: **14.2% of GDP (1990-91)** → almost three times by 2011-12; **30.4% (2023)**, 29.7% (2024).

  [75:3:7.1] Cyclical | Commodity shocks, temporary global slowdown, rupee volatility
## SUB 4 | Invisibles and Remittances - the Cushions
[75:4:0] - **India = world's largest remittance recipient** (also in 2015): **$135.4 bn (3.5% of GDP) in FY25**; **USA** top source (**27.7%**).
[75:4:1] - Workers' remittances (narrower measure) **$110.47 bn in FY26** (from $87.55 bn); Q4 FY26 $31.07 bn, a 13-year high.
[75:4:2] - Remittances exceed gross FDI inflows; **counter-cyclical**; create **no debt**, nothing to repay.

## SUB 6 | Capital Flight and External Vulnerability
[75:6:0] # Weak capital flows
[75:6:1] - FY26: **FPIs pulled out $16.4 bn**; **net FDI only $6.9 bn** (down from $44.0 bn in 2020-21).
[75:6:2] - Since mid-2024 capital inflows shrank, breaking the post-1991 pattern of a capital surplus covering the CAD.
[75:6:3] - BoP position described as weak as the 2013 "Fragile Five" phase (12 May 2026).
[75:6:4] # External shocks
[75:6:5] - **US Fed rate hikes + strong dollar** → higher US yields → **hot money leaves** emerging markets → **rupee weakens**.
[75:6:6] - **2013 taper tantrum**: **Morgan Stanley** named India one of the "**Fragile Five**".
[75:6:7] - Morgan Stanley 1998 emerging markets: India **3rd** (Brazil 1st, Mexico 2nd).
[75:6:8] # External debt (end-June 2026)
[75:6:9] TABLE head: Item | Fact
  [75:6:9.0] Total | $778.2 bn (up $15.4 bn from March)
  [75:6:9.1] Debt-to-GDP | 20.8% (from 20.9%)
  [75:6:9.2] Short-term debt | 19.7% of total; 23.0% of reserves
  [75:6:9.3] Currency | US dollar 54.8%, rupee 29.8%, yen 6.9%, SDR 4.1%, euro 3.5%
  [75:6:9.4] Debt service ratio | 5.6% of current receipts
[75:6:10] # Safeguards
[75:6:11] - **Adequate reserves** (import cover), **low short-term debt**, less reliance on volatile **FPI** and debt flows. Relying on hot money raises risk.
[75:6:12] - FCNR(B) and ECB inflows are **temporary**; **FDI** supports a self-sustaining capital surplus.
## SUB 7 | Correcting BoP Disequilibrium
[75:7:0] TABLE head: Expenditure-switching | Expenditure-reducing / others
  [75:7:0.0] Devaluation/depreciation | Tight fiscal policy: cut government spending, raise taxes
  [75:7:0.1] Import tariffs, quotas, import substitution | Tight monetary policy: higher interest rates
  [75:7:0.2] Export incentives, export/tourist promotion | Foreign loans, IMF assistance, FDI; exchange control
  [75:7:0.3] Higher gold import duty | 
[75:7:1] - **Devaluation** = classic expenditure-switching (home goods cheaper relative to foreign goods); used to fix the balance of trade.
[75:7:2] - **NOT a correction measure**: **increasing government spending on domestic projects** - raises demand and imports (PYQ 2025).
[75:7:3] - **Encouraging FDI** finances a BoP deficit; it is not a classical correction tool.
[75:7:4] - **May 2026**: PM's appeal to cut import-linked consumption (gold, fuel) as the Iran conflict raised fuel and fertiliser prices + gold import duty hike = expenditure-switching steps.
[75:7:5] - **Rekhi Committee (1992)** = indirect taxes, not export-import simplification.
## SUB 8 | Imf Role and Sdr
[75:8:0] # IMF functions
[75:8:1] - Core function: **surveillance** of global and member economies; main purpose: help solve **BoP problems**.
[75:8:2] - Lends to members with BoP difficulties; **lender of last resort** when reserves cannot cover a deficit; loans meet **temporary BoP deficits**. India drew on the IMF in **1991**.
[75:8:3] - Provides technical assistance.
[75:8:4] # Facilities
[75:8:5] TABLE head: Facility | Meaning
  [75:8:5.0] Reserve tranche | Member's unconditional drawing right from its quota; old name gold tranche
  [75:8:5.1] Rapid Financing Instrument (RFI) | Rapid help for any member with an urgent BoP need
  [75:8:5.2] Rapid Credit Facility (RCF) | Concessional, for low-income countries
  [75:8:5.3] Stand-By Arrangement / Extended Fund Facility | Regular lending windows
[75:8:6] RBI swaps and Special Rupee Vostro Accounts are NOT IMF instruments.
[75:8:7] # SDR
