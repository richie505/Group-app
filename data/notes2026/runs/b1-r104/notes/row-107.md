=== Market Control – Aim, Markets and Officials
- **Chain:** Mongol invasions → **large standing army paid in cash** → **low fixed salaries** → **fixed low prices** of daily goods → **soldiers live comfortably on low pay** (APPSC G2 2017; RRB 2002).
- **Introduced by Alauddin Khalji** (UPPCS 1992) – the first state price-control and rationing system, called the first "**Public Distribution System**" (UPPCS Mains 2010): state **granaries** released grain in shortage.
- **⚠ Not** to pay lower wages to palace artisans (UPPCS Mains 2005: A true, R false); not for farmers' returns or direct purchase.
- **Four-point plan:** **fixed prices**, sure **supply**, controlled **distribution**, **end of monopolies** – enforced by a separate administration.
| Market | Goods |
|---|---|
| **Mandi** | **Food grains** |
| **Sarai Adl** | **Cloth**, sugar, dry fruits, butter, oil |
| Third market | **Horses, slaves, cattle** |
| Fourth market | Miscellaneous goods |
# Officials and Departments
| Official / department | Role |
|---|---|
| **Shahna-i-Mandi** | Superintendent of each market (e.g. **Malik Kabool**) |
| **Diwan-i-Riyasat** | Ran the whole market system, under the **Naib-i-Riyasat** |
| **Barids** | Intelligence officers – daily reports |
| **Munhiyan** | **Secret spies** – daily reports |
| Diwan-i-Mustakhraj | Recovery of **revenue arrears** from officials |
- **Slave boys** made test purchases; cheating merchants punished; banjaras registered.

=== Revenue Reforms
- **First Sultan to measure land** – **masahat** (HPPSC 2012) – unit **biswa**; revenue in **cash**.
- **Statements (UPPCS 1994):** he **measured cultivated land** – true; he **curbed the provincial governors' (muqtis') independent powers** – true in the key; measurement **covered the whole empire – false** (only **Delhi and the Doab**).
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Kharaj** | Land tax = **one-half (50%)** of produce (NextGen's "one-fifth" is a slip; PMF: 50%) |
| **Ghari** | **House** tax |
| **Charai** | **Grazing** tax on milch cattle |
| Khuts, muqaddams, chaudharis | Intermediaries – **privileges abolished**, taxed like others |
| **Kismat-i-khuti** | Headman's cess – **abolished** |
| **Milk, waqf, inam** | Grants **reclaimed** → **khalisa** (crown land) **expanded on a large scale** (State PSC) |
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams 03 p.61; PMF Ancient & Medieval 04 p.40; app notes 1:107 (sub 2)

=== Ordinances Against the Nobles
| Cause of revolt (Barani) | Ordinance |
|---|---|
| Nobles' wealth | Confiscated property, religious grants and free lands |
| Weak spy system | Reorganised spies (**barids, munhiyan**) |
| Drinking | **Prohibited liquor**, intoxicants and **gambling** |
| Nobles' intermarriage and gatherings | **Banquets, festivities and marriage alliances only with the Sultan's permission** (SSC 2013) |
- **Severe punishments**; prohibition + market control + severe punishment = **Alauddin** (APPSC G1 2018).

=== Military Reforms
- **First Sultan with a large permanent standing army**, recruited by the Ariz and **paid in cash** from the treasury.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Dagh** | **Branding of horses** |
| **Huliya / Chehra** | **Descriptive roll** of each soldier |
- **Purpose:** stop fraud and false musters; strict periodic reviews.
- **Ariz-i-Mamalik:** **army** administration.
- **Later copy:** **Mahmud Gawan** (Bahmani) brought in dagh, chehra and land measurement.
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams 03 p.62; PMF Ancient & Medieval 04 p.40; app notes 1:107 (sub 3)

=== Effects, Sources and End of the Reforms
- **Verdict in keys:** market control was **highly successful** (UPPCS Mains 2017) – prices stayed fixed even in famine; Alauddin called a "great political economist".
- **Other side:** espionage and repression bred discontent; merchants and peasants hurt; controls largely confined to Delhi.
- **Main source:** **Ziauddin Barani**, **Tarikh-i-Firoz Shahi**; also **Khazain-ul-Futuh** (Amir Khusrau).
- **Reign = "imperial period"** of the Sultanate – conquests, centralisation, imperial ambition.
- **End:** **Qutbuddin Mubarak Shah (1316-20)** abolished price control and restored confiscated lands.
| Dynasty | Key contribution |
|---|---|
| Khaljis | Market control, taxation reforms |
| **Tughlaqs** | Token currency, capital transfer, canals |
| **Lodis** | First **Afghan** dynasty; **Agra** founded |
