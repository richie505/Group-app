=== Iltutmish – Capital, Coins and the Mongol Threat
- **Capital:** moved from **Lahore to Delhi** (UPPSC 2016) – first Sultan to make **Delhi** the capital and issue **regular coins** (UPPSC Mains 2014).
- **Coins:** purely **Arabic** silver **tanka** and copper **jital** – basis of the later rupee.
- **Chengiz (Genghis) Khan** – original name **Temujin (Temuchin)** (UPPSC 2015) – reached the **Indus (1221)** chasing **Jalaluddin Mangbarni** of Khwarazm; Iltutmish **refused him shelter**, so the Mongols turned back. Mongols seen on the Indus **for the first time** in Iltutmish's reign (UPPSC 1993; CGPSC 2013).
- **Rivals:** **Tajuddin Yildiz** (beaten at **Tarain, 1216**) and **Nasiruddin Qabacha** (Multan, Uch; fought for Lahore).
- **Bihar:** after defeating Iwaz Khalji of Bengal, Iltutmish made **Malik (Alauddin) Jani** the **first governor of Bihar** (BPSC 2008).
- **Investiture ("mansur")** from the **Abbasid Caliph** of Baghdad – **1229**.
| Institution | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Turkan-i-Chahalgani** | "The Forty" – **Turkish military** elite (not Persian revenue clerks) |
| **Iqta** | Revenue assignment to officers (**muqtis**) instead of cash salary |
- **Buildings:** completed the **Qutb Minar** (begun by Aibak; dedicated to **Khwaja Qutbuddin Bakhtiyar Kaki**); **Sultan Ghari** tomb → "**Father of tomb building**".
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams 03 p.54-56; PMF Ancient & Medieval 04 p.31-32

=== Balban – Kingship, Court and Army
- **Name:** born **Bahauddin** (c. 1205); regnal title **Ghiyasuddin Balban** (CGPSC 2004; Chhattisgarh 2004).
- **Rise:** slave bought by Iltutmish → **water-carrier** → member of **the Forty** → **Amir-i-Shikar** (under Razia) → **naib** (principal minister) of **Nasiruddin Mahmud**, who gave him the title **Ulugh Khan** (IAS 2006).
- **Imaduddin Raihan:** leader of the **Indian Muslim** group who briefly displaced Balban in Nasiruddin Mahmud's reign (MPPSC 2002).
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Zil-i-Ilahi** | Sultan = **shadow of God** |
| **Sijda** | **Prostration** before the Sultan |
| **Paibos** | **Kissing the Sultan's feet** |
| **Nauroz (Nawruz)** | **Persian festival** introduced at court by **Balban** (IAS 1993; SSC 2006) – not Alauddin, Iltutmish or Firuz |
- **Court** copied the **Iranian kings**; strict etiquette, aloofness.
- **Blood and iron:** broke **the Forty** by promoting **junior Turks**; efficient spies; harsh justice – amir **Malik Baqbaq** (governor of Badaun) **publicly flogged** for killing a servant (UPPSC 2011).
- **Army:** created the military department **Diwan-i-Arz**, separating it from **Diwan-i-Wizarat** (finance) (RAS 2018); **outposts and forts** against the Mongols.
- **⚠ Iltutmish created the Forty; Balban broke it.**
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams 03 p.56-57; PMF Ancient & Medieval 04 p.33-34; app notes 1:105 (sub 4, 5)

=== Qutbuddin Aibak (1206-1210)
- **Origin:** born in **Turkestan**; sold to the **Qazi of Nishapur**, then slave of **Muhammad Ghori**; **Amir-i-Akhur** (Master of Stables) → general; Ghori's representative after **Tarain II (1192)**.
- **Titles:** **Lakh Bakhsh** ("giver of lakhs"), and **Quran Khwan / "Quran Khan"** (reciter of the Quran) (JPSC 2003) – **Zil-i-Ilahi** was Balban's.
- **Capital:** **Lahore**.
- **Buildings:** began the **Qutb Minar**; **Quwwat-ul-Islam** mosque (first mosque in Delhi); **Adhai Din ka Jhonpra** (Ajmer). Patron of **Hasan Nizami**.
- **Death (1210):** at **Lahore**, **fell from his horse while playing chaugan (polo)** – not stabbed, not killed by Yildiz, not at Kalinjar (UPSC 2003); buried near **Anarkali Bazaar**.
- **Polo** was brought to India by the **Turks** (UPPSC 2001).
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams 03 p.53-54; PMF Ancient & Medieval 04 p.31

=== Razia Sultan (1236-1240)
- **First and only woman ruler** of the Delhi Sultanate – the "first woman ruler of (medieval) India" in exam keys (UPPSC 2004; SSC 2017) – before Chand Bibi, Durgavati, Nur Jahan.
- **Ways:** discarded **purdah**, wore **male attire** (kuba, kulah), hunted, led the army.
- **Opposition:** the **Turkish nobles** (the Forty) resented her favour to **Yaqut**, an **Abyssinian** (Amir-i-Akhur) → her fall (UP Lower 2004).
- **1240:** **Altuniya**, governor of **Bhatinda (Tabarhinda)**, revolted → Yaqut killed, Razia imprisoned; she **married Altuniya**, marched on Delhi and was **killed** (1240).
- She made **Balban Amir-i-Shikar** (not Diwan-i-Arz).
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams 03 p.56; PMF Ancient & Medieval 04 p.32-33

=== Mamluk Lines, Rulers and the Turkish Nobility
- **Mamluk** = Arabic "**owned**" – an owned **military slave**; Slave dynasty ruled **1206-1290**.
- **Ruling class:** most amirs and Sultans of the Sultanate were **Turks** (Turko-Afghan) (UPPSC 1991).
| Line | Period | Founder |
|---|---|---|
| Qutbi | 1206-1211 | **Qutbuddin Aibak** |
| First Ilbari | 1211-1266 | **Iltutmish** |
| Second Ilbari | 1266-1290 | **Balban** |
- **Order:** Aibak → Aram Shah → **Iltutmish** → **Ruknuddin Firuz** → **Razia** → Bahram Shah → Masud → **Nasiruddin Mahmud** (1246-66; Tabaqat-i-Nasiri named after him) → **Balban** → **Kaiqubad** → infant **Kaimurs** (last).
- **End:** **Jalaluddin Firoz Khalji** crowned himself at **Kilokhari (1290)** → **Khalji** dynasty.
| Dynasty | Period |
|---|---|
| Mamluk | 1206-1290 |
| **Khalji** | 1290-1320 |
| **Tughlaq** | 1320-1414 |
| **Sayyid** | 1414-1451 |
| **Lodi** | 1451-1526 |
- **⚠ Odd one out:** Ruknuddin, Masud, Nasiruddin Mahmud **are** Mamluks; **Jalaluddin Khalji is not**.
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams 03 p.52, p.58; PMF Ancient & Medieval 04 p.28-29
