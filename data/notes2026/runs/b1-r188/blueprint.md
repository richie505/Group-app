## 3. Repeats and traps
- **Founder questions dominate:** Arya Samaj founder asked 10 times, Brahmo founder 4, Lokahitawadi 6, Andhra Prarthana Samaj (Veeresalingam 1878) 3 (moved to row 195).
- **Brahmo Samaj year:** two exams printed no correct year (APPSC G1 2008: 1826/1829/1834/1836; MPPSC 1992: 1827/1829/1831/1843) – the old keys gave 1829 and 1843. Correct 1828, option added.
- **Brahmunical Magazine (APPSC G1):** the old key and old explanation said Debendranath; it was Rammohan Roy's own journal (1821).
- **"Father of Indian Nationalism" / "Prophet of Nationalism":** books split (Roy vs Naoroji; Roy vs Aurobindo) – keyed on the options, disputes flagged.
- **Hindu College founder:** UPSC 2009 key Rammohan Roy; strictly David Hare (Roy supported it), and Duff came in 1830.
