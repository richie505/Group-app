=== Arya Samaj, 1875 – Founder and Founding
- **Founder:** **Swami Dayananda Saraswati** (Mool Shankar, 1824-1883), born in a **Brahmin** family in **Morvi state (Tankara), Gujarat**; guru **Swami Virajananda** of Mathura; died at Ajmer, 1883.
- **First Arya Samaj:** **Bombay, 1875** (10 April); headquarters later at **Lahore** (1877); strongest base Punjab.
- Founder = **Dayananda** – not Vivekananda, Gokhale, Ramakrishna, Ranade, V.R. Shinde, Chandavarkar or Keshab.
- **Wrong date trap:** "Arya Samaj founded in 1835" ✘ (IAS 2001).
- **Current linkage:** **International Arya Mahasammelan 2025**, New Delhi, **30 Oct-2 Nov 2025** – 150 years of the Arya Samaj and Dayananda's **200th birth anniversary**.
> Arya Samaj = Dayananda, Bombay 1875, HQ Lahore.

=== Doctrines, Satyarth Prakash and Titles
- **'Back to the Vedas':** Vedas = **infallible**, "India's Rock of Ages"; a revival of Vedic learning and purity, not of Vedic times; rejected the **Puranas**.
- **Attacked:** idolatry, polytheism, caste rigidity and untouchability, child marriage, priestly ritual; caste by **merit/occupation, not birth** (CDS 2009: all three statements ✔).
- **Supported:** widow remarriage, inter-caste marriage, women's education; minimum marriage age 25 (boys) and 16 (girls); **ten principles**.
- **Satyarth Prakash** ('The Light of Truth', 1875, **Hindi**, revised 1882) – Dayananda's main work and the **Arya Samaj's** sacred text.
- **Revivalist** in form (cf. reformist Brahmo/Prarthana Samaj).

=== Shuddhi, DAV Schools, the 1893 Split and Social Base
- **Shuddhi** (purification): started by the **Arya Samaj** (Dayananda) to **reconvert** to Hinduism those who had become Muslims or Christians; also tried to raise untouchables; aggressive campaign of the 1920s (Shraddhanand) sharpened communalism.
- **DAV:** **Dayananda Anglo-Vedic School/College, Lahore, 1886** (after Dayananda's death) – **Lala Hansraj** first headmaster/founder; Western-style education.
- **1893 split:** **College Party** – Lala Hansraj, Lala Lal Chand, **Lala Lajpat Rai** (kept DAV); **Mahatma / Gurukul Party** – Guru Datta Vidyarthi and **Munshi Ram (Swami Shraddhanand)**, who set up the **Gurukul** (Gujranwala 1900, **Kangri, Haridwar 1902**).
- **Swami Shraddhanand** spoke on Hindu-Muslim unity from the pulpit of the **Jama Masjid, Delhi, 4 April 1919** (Rowlatt satyagraha).
- **Impact (A-R):** gave Hindus **self-respect and self-confidence**, which **undermined the myth of white superiority** – R explains A.
- **Social base:** **Punjab and western U.P.**, mainly the **trading castes**; wider than the Brahmo Samaj, whose influence hardly went beyond Bengal; famous for social service in calamities.
- **Lala Lajpat Rai:** Arya Samaj leader, ran the paper **The People** (not "unconnected"); **Al Hilal** (1912) = **Maulana Abul Kalam Azad**.
- **Hyderabad, 1938-39:** Arya Samaj satyagraha against the **Nizam's curbs on religious and civil rights**, alongside the State Congress satyagraha.
> Shuddhi = Arya Samaj; DAV Lahore 1886 = Hansraj; Gurukul Kangri 1902 = Shraddhanand; split 1893.
