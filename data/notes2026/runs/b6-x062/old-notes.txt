## SUB 0 | Withdrawals from the International Criminal Court
[62:0:0] # Sahel States Leave the ICC
[62:0:1] - Military-led **Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger** (coups **2020–2023**) withdrew from the **ICC**, calling it a **"neo-colonial instrument"**.
[62:0:2] - Grouping: **Alliance of Sahel States (AES)**; moving closer to **Russia**, away from **France**.
[62:0:3] - Under the **Rome Statute**, withdrawal takes effect **one year after notice to the UN Secretary-General**.
[62:0:4] # ICC – Background
[62:0:5] - Established **2002**; seat **The Hague, Netherlands** – the **first permanent international criminal court**.
[62:0:6] - Crimes: **war crimes, crimes against humanity, genocide, aggression**; works on the **principle of complementarity**.
[62:0:7] - **Vladimir Putin** under an ICC arrest warrant since **March 2023**.
[62:0:8] ICC withdrawal is effective **one year** after notice to the **UN Secretary-General**, not immediately.
