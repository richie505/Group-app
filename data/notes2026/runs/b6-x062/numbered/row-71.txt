## SUB 11 | Energy and Technology Firsts – Hydrogen Train, Geothermal, Helium and EVs
[11:0] # India's first hydrogen train
[11:1] - Flagged off at **Jind, Haryana**, **17 July 2026**; route **Jind–Sonipat, 89 km**; under **"Hydrogen for Heritage"**.
[11:2] - **PEM (Proton Exchange Membrane) fuel cell**; power **2,400 kW** (1,200 kW per power car).
[11:3] - **Jind** – India's largest railway hydrogen **storage and refuelling** centre.
[11:4] - World's first hydrogen passenger train = **Alstom Coradia iLint (Germany, 2018)**.
[11:5] - **National Green Hydrogen Mission** – Jan 2023, **₹19,744 crore**, **MNRE** (not DST/DPIIT).
[11:6] # Geothermal – Puga Valley
[11:7] - **India's first and deepest geothermal wells** – **Puga Valley, Ladakh** (July 2026); **1 MW** demonstration; by **ONGC** Energy Centre (not NTPC); Carbon-Neutral Ladakh.
[11:8] - **National Policy on Geothermal Energy, 2025** (MNRE) – supports **Net Zero 2070**; potential ~**10.6 GW**.
[11:9] - Sites: Puga (Ladakh), Manikaran (HP), Tapoban (Uttarakhand), **Yumesamdong (Sikkim)**. Plant types: **dry steam, flash, binary** (not solar PV).
[11:10] # Helium
[11:11] - **US ~43%** of world helium; China only ~1.6% (China's export ban, July 2026).
[11:12] - Helium: atomic number 2; **Group 18** noble gas; boiling point ~**–269°C**; **cannot solidify at normal pressure** (~25 atm at ~1 K).
[11:13] - Uses: **quantum computers**, MRI magnets, semiconductor wafers, rocket tank pressurisation; in **scuba tanks** to avoid **nitrogen narcosis**.
