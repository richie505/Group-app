=== WTO, IMF and World Bank – Heads, Members, Venues and Loans
# WTO Ministerial Conferences
| Conference | Venue | Key result |
|---|---|---|
| MC12 (2022) | **Geneva** | First fisheries subsidies deal – bans subsidies to illegal, unreported and unregulated (IUU) fishing |
| MC13 (26 Feb–2 Mar 2024) | **Abu Dhabi (UAE)** | E-commerce moratorium extended two years; no second-wave fisheries deal |
| MC14 (26–29 March 2026) | **Yaoundé (Cameroon)** | Ended in deadlock; e-commerce moratorium (in place since **1998**) lapsed on **30 March 2026** (WTO; MC13 decision ran "until MC14 or 31 March 2026, whichever is earlier" – PMF LENS gives 31 March) – first time since 1998 |
- **Agreement on Fisheries Subsidies:** in force **15 September 2025**, after acceptances by **two-thirds (111)** of WTO members; the WTO's **first agreement with environmental sustainability at its core** (bans subsidies to IUU fishing and overfished stocks).
- After MC14, **66** members (about **70%** of world trade) agreed interim implementation of a **plurilateral E-Commerce Agreement**.
> Venue match: MC12 – Geneva, MC13 – Abu Dhabi, MC14 – Yaoundé (not Geneva or Bali).
# WTO subsidy boxes (Agreement on Agriculture)
| Box | Meaning | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Amber | Trade-distorting support – price support (e.g. MSP), input subsidies | **Capped**; de minimis **10%** of production value for developing, **5%** for developed countries |
| Blue | Amber support with production-limiting conditions | **Exempt** from reduction |
| Green | Minimal trade distortion – research, infrastructure, decoupled income support | **Unlimited** |
- **Bali (2013) peace clause:** developing countries not challenged for breaching de minimis limits on **public stockholding for food security**.

=== Membership Changes – ICC Withdrawals and Recognition of Palestine
# Sahel states leave the ICC
- Military-led **Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger** (coups **2020–2023**) announced withdrawal from the **ICC** (2025), calling it a **"neo-colonial instrument"**.
- Grouping: **Alliance of Sahel States (AES)** (not ECOWAS or G5 Sahel); moving closer to **Russia**, away from **France**.
- **Rome Statute:** withdrawal takes effect **one year after notice to the UN Secretary-General** (not immediately, not 3 or 6 months).
# ICC – background
- Established **2002**; seat **The Hague, Netherlands** – the **first permanent international criminal court** (the ICJ is the UN's court for disputes between states).
- Crimes: war crimes, crimes against humanity, genocide, aggression; works on the **principle of complementarity** (steps in only when national courts cannot or will not act).
- **Vladimir Putin** under an ICC arrest warrant since **March 2023**.
# Recognition of Palestine
- Already recognised (2024): **Spain, Ireland, Norway**; **France** signalled readiness.
- Reluctant: **Germany and Austria** (Holocaust legacy). EU **CFSP** needs **unanimity**, so recognition is by individual states.
- **UNGA, September 2025:** about **10** countries moving to recognise – **Britain, France, Canada, Australia, Belgium**.
- **PLO** declared statehood in **1988**; Palestine is a UN **observer** state.
- **147 of 193** UN members recognised Palestine (latest before Sep 2025: **Mexico, 2025** – Current Care: January 2025).
- **Norway** hosted the **Oslo Accords (1993)**.
- Governance: **Palestinian Authority** – parts of the West Bank; **Hamas** has run **Gaza since 2007** (not Fatah/PA).
- **ICJ:** **South Africa's genocide case** against Israel.
> Trap: Spain, Ireland and Norway had already recognised; Britain, France, Canada, Australia and Belgium were the September 2025 group.

=== India's Bilateral Treaties and Disputes – Indus Waters, LBA 2015 and Corn
# Indus Waters Treaty and Sawalkote
- India **suspended the Indus Waters Treaty (signed 1960)** after the **Pahalgam attack (April 2025)**; this gave momentum to **Sawalkote** and the **Wullar Barrage** (both part of the push).
- **Sawalkote:** **1.8 GW**, on the **Chenab** (J&K), by **NHPC**; reservoir >50,000 crore litres – termed **run-of-river** but closer to a **storage** project.
- Forest diversion 847 ha; about 1,500 families displaced.
- Other Chenab projects: **Dulhasti, Baglihar, Salal**.

# India–US corn trade dispute
- India's corn imports 2024–25: 0.97 mt – **Myanmar 0.53 mt (largest)**, Ukraine 0.39 mt, **US only 1,100 t**.
- **Tariff-rate quota:** 0.5 mt at 15% duty; beyond that **50%**.

=== WHO – Rubella Elimination and the Pandemic Agreement
# Nepal eliminates rubella
- **18 August 2025:** WHO declared **Nepal** has **eliminated rubella as a public health problem** (not measles, not all vaccine-preventable diseases).
- Nepal was **first in the WHO South-East Asia Region** to adopt a **laboratory algorithm** for rubella surveillance.
- **Rubella = German measles**; can cause **Congenital Rubella Syndrome (CRS)** in infants (~1,00,000 a year globally).
- **MMR vaccine: two doses** (not three).
# WHO Pandemic Agreement (May 2025)
- Adopted by the **World Health Assembly** on **20 May 2025** (Geneva); committee vote the day before **124–0–11**.
- Opens for signature **only after the PABS (Pathogen Access and Benefit-Sharing) Annex** is adopted (not on adoption day); **60** ratifications needed for entry into force.
> Pattern: WHO "elimination / certification" items (country, date, disease) are a recurring PYQ angle in this topic.

=== Visas, Migration, the ILO and India's Diaspora
# US H-1B fee and China's K Visa
- **September 2025:** **$100,000** fee for **new H-1B applicants** (~six times the earlier fee).
- H-1B quota **85,000** a year since **2004** (incl. 20,000 for US advanced degrees); Indians ~**71%** of recipients.
| Visa (China) | Target | Sponsorship |
|---|---|---|
| R Visa | High-level foreign experts | Employer sponsorship mandatory |
| **K Visa** | Young foreign **STEM** talent | **Not required** |
- K Visa announced **weeks before** the US raised the H-1B fee. Xi Jinping's slogan **"New Quality Productive Forces"**; **Thousand Talents Programme**.
- **Skilled migration** since the 1990s (e.g. US H-1B); **GCC** countries host mainly **temporary labour** migrants.
# India–ILO MoU on IRCO
- India and the **ILO** to develop the **International Reference Classification of Occupations (IRCO)**; signed in **Geneva** by Ambassador **Arindam Bagchi** and ILO DG **Gilbert F. Houngbo**; rooted in **G20 (India 2023)** skills-based migration pathways; does **not** mandate migration.
- **PM Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana:** **35 million** (3.5 crore) formal jobs in **2 years**.

- **Citizenship (Amendment) Act, 2003 – Sections 7A–7D** created **OCI**; **PIO–OCI merger 2015**; **India does not permit dual citizenship**.
