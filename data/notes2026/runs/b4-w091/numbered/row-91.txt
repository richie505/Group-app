## SUB 2 | VCIC – Features and ADB Funding
[2:0] - **India's first coastal corridor**; first phase of the **East Coast Economic Corridor** (Kolkata-Kanyakumari).
[2:1] - Links India to **ASEAN and East Asia** (**Act East + Make in India**); aligned with the Golden Quadrilateral (**NH-16**).
[2:2] - **North:** metallurgy, pharma, petrochemicals, IT. **South:** food processing, leather, automobiles (not confined to one region).

  [2:3.1] Govt of AP | 0 | 215 (30%) | 215
  [2:3.2] Total | 125 | 715 (70:30) | 840
[2:4] - **Policy-based loan:** reforms, institutions, skills; **project loan:** roads, power, water, parks.
[2:5] - **Executing agency:** Department of **Industries and Commerce**; PMU under the Commissioner of Industries.
[2:6] - VCIC is **partly funded by an ADB loan**, not JICA.

## SUB 5 | APIIC, SEZs and PCPIR
[5:0] - **APIIC (1973):** the nodal agency that provides **industrial infrastructure** – land, **industrial parks and SEZs**; **PPP** model; after **1991** moved from direct manufacturer to **facilitator**.
[5:1] - APSEZ is one SEZ (Atchutapuram-Rambilli) and APGIC a gas JV – neither is the State-wide infrastructure agency.
[5:2] - Survey 2025-26: APIIC has **635 clusters** (331 parks, 304 estates).
[5:3] - **SEZs (2023):** 47 or more notified; **10 by APIIC**.
[5:4] - **APSEZ:** multi-product SEZ at Atchutapuram-Rambilli (Anakapalli); notified **2007**; part of the PCPIR.
[5:5] - **Budget 2026:** SEZ units may **sell in the domestic market at a concessional tax**.
[5:6] - **Four PCPIRs:** **Visakhapatnam-Kakinada**, Dahej, Paradip, Cuddalore-Nagapattinam. AP PCPIR approved 2009, ~**640 sq km**.
[5:7] - **PCPIR Policy 2020-35:** minimum area **50 sq km** (was 250).
[5:8] - PPP ports **Krishnapatnam + Gangavaram** drew heavy industry to the coast.
