## SUB 3 | Railways – History, Network, Konkan and Units
[3:0] - **First train: 16 April 1853**, **Bori Bunder (Bombay) – Thane, 34 km**; by **GIPR**, under **Lord Dalhousie**.
[3:1] - **Broad gauge = 1.676 m (5½ ft)**; metre 1 m; narrow 0.762 / 0.610 m. BG >95% of the network.
[3:2] - Network **70,000+ route km**; **4th largest** in the world (after USA, Russia, China); freight **1,617 MT (2024-25)** = world's **2nd-largest freight railway**.
[3:3] - **Coal** is the largest single commodity carried (about half of freight).
[3:4] - Railway Budget 2012-13: about **13.6 lakh employees** (13,61,519).
[3:5] - **Konkan Railway:** **760 km, Roha (Maharashtra) – Mangalore (Thokur, Karnataka)**, opened **1998**; partners **Maharashtra, Goa, Karnataka** (Kerala benefits most as a gateway); runs through Ratnagiri, Madgaon, Udupi – **not Belgaum** and **not Kerala**; KRCL HQ **Navi Mumbai**; a **PSU, not a zone**.
[3:6] - First rail track on **PPP**: **Gujarat** (Pipavav port line).

## SUB 6 | Roads – Network, Traffic, BRO and Expressways
[6:0] - Road network **63.45 lakh km**, **2nd largest** in the world; NHs **1.46 lakh km**.
[6:1] - NHs = about **2%** of road length but carry about **40%** of road traffic; roads carry ~85% of passengers and 70% of freight.
[6:2] - **Road density:** highest in **Kerala**.
[6:3] - **Surface roads per 100 km² (UPSC 1998):** Punjab and Tamil Nadu far ahead of Maharashtra and Haryana (books differ on which of the two leads).
[6:4] - Road transport got the post-1947 impetus because rail is capital-heavy and indivisible and cannot reach scattered settlements – but **rail is cheaper per tonne-km**.
[6:5] - **BRO:** set up **1960** (Ministry of Defence); builds border roads.
[6:6] - **Mumbai–Pune Expressway** (94.5 km) = India's **first six-lane concrete expressway**; **Agra–Lucknow** six-lane expressway opened Nov 2016 (UP).
[6:7] - **Bharatmala Pariyojana (2017):** ~**34,800 km** in Phase I; highest built length **Rajasthan** (2,241 km to Oct 2024).
[6:8] - **Madhya Pradesh** first to issue a unified vehicle registration card.
[6:9] - World: **Autobahn** = Germany's highways (Autoroute = France); **Asian Highway** promoted by **UNESCAP**; the Silk Route was a land-and-sea network, not one modern highway.
