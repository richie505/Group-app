# Historic silk industry of Andhra (APPSC statements)
- Important silk-weaving centres: **Berhampur, Peddapuram, Dharmavaram**.
- Silks were **articles of luxury**, in great demand among the rich; weavers came from **Devanga, Padmasali**, Satani, Mala and Muslim communities.
- In the **19th century** the industry **declined** under competition from **Chinese and Japanese silk**.
- It was **NOT mainly a rural industry** – it was town-based and served rich buyers.
> Exam angle: "the silk industry was a rural industry supported by villagers" is the FALSE statement; Kondapalli and Etikoppaka = wooden toys; Chirala = textiles; Eluru = carpets.

=== Industrial Corridors and Nodes in AP
- AP (through **APIIC**) develops hubs on three corridors under the **National Industrial Corridor Development Programme**, with **ADB** and **NICDIT** funds.
- AP is the **only State with nodes on all three corridors**: **VCIC, CBIC, HBIC**.
| Corridor | AP node(s) and district | Key data |
|---|---|---|

=== VCIC – Features and ADB Funding
- **India's first coastal corridor**; first phase of the **East Coast Economic Corridor** (Kolkata-Kanyakumari).
- Links India to **ASEAN and East Asia** (**Act East + Make in India**); aligned with the Golden Quadrilateral (**NH-16**).
- **North:** metallurgy, pharma, petrochemicals, IT. **South:** food processing, leather, automobiles (not confined to one region).
| ADB programme (US$ mn) | Policy-based loan | Project loan | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| ADB | 125 (100%) | 500 (70%) | **625** |
| Govt of AP | 0 | 215 (30%) | 215 |
| Total | 125 | 715 (**70:30**) | 840 |
- **Policy-based loan:** reforms, institutions, skills; **project loan:** roads, power, water, parks.
- **Executing agency:** Department of **Industries and Commerce**; PMU under the Commissioner of Industries.
- VCIC is **partly funded by an ADB loan**, not JICA.

=== APIIC, SEZs and PCPIR
- **APIIC (1973):** the nodal agency that provides **industrial infrastructure** – land, **industrial parks and SEZs**; **PPP** model; after **1991** moved from direct manufacturer to **facilitator**.
- APSEZ is one SEZ (Atchutapuram-Rambilli) and APGIC a gas JV – neither is the State-wide infrastructure agency.
- Survey 2025-26: APIIC has **635 clusters** (331 parks, 304 estates).
- **SEZs (2023):** 47 or more notified; **10 by APIIC**.
- **APSEZ:** multi-product SEZ at Atchutapuram-Rambilli (Anakapalli); notified **2007**; part of the PCPIR.
- **Budget 2026:** SEZ units may **sell in the domestic market at a concessional tax**.
- **Four PCPIRs:** **Visakhapatnam-Kakinada**, Dahej, Paradip, Cuddalore-Nagapattinam. AP PCPIR approved 2009, ~**640 sq km**.
- **PCPIR Policy 2020-35:** minimum area **50 sq km** (was 250).
- PPP ports **Krishnapatnam + Gangavaram** drew heavy industry to the coast.

=== Industrial Policy 4.0, MSMEs and Exports
- **AP Industrial Development Policy 4.0 (2024-29)** replaced the **Industrial Development Policy 2023-27**; notified **26 Oct 2024** by **G.O. 68**; **MSME Policy 4.0** by **G.O. 69**.
- Companion policies: Food Processing, Private Industrial Parks, Aerospace and Defence (not a highway policy).
| Category | FCI (excluding land) |
|---|---|
| Large | ₹501-1,000 crore |
| **Mega** | **₹1,001-5,000 crore** |
| Ultra Mega | above ₹5,000 crore |
- Incentives: capital subsidy 12-15%; **net SGST reimbursed 100% for 5 years** (not ten).
- **Targets:** **₹30 lakh crore** investment; electronics by 2029 **US$ 50 bn** and **5 lakh jobs**.
- **MSME Policy 4.0:** "**One Family, One Entrepreneur**" by **2030**; capital subsidy **25%** general, **45%** special categories.
- **Udyam** registration since **1 Jul 2020**: about **36.8 lakh** MSMEs by 15 Dec 2025; **Anantapur** highest (~2.37 lakh).
- **AP Cluster Development Programme** (G.O. 17, Jan 2026): **45** clusters/CFCs, aid up to **80%**.
- **ODOP (DPIIT, 2023):** Kakinada – Uppada Jamdani; Krishna – Machilipatnam Kalamkari; Srikakulam – Budithi brass; **West Godavari – Narsapur crochet laces**.
- **BRAP 2020** (released **Jun 2022**): AP among the **7 top achievers** – categories, not numerical ranks.
- AP = **6th** largest exporting State (Survey 2025-26). **CII Partnership Summit:** **Visakhapatnam**, 14-15 Nov 2025.
