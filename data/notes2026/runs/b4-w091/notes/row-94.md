=== Postal Network – PIN, Speed Post and India Post
- **PIN code:** **6 digits**, from **15 Aug 1972**. 1st = zone; 2nd = sub-zone; 3rd = sorting district; last 3 = delivery office.
- **9 PIN zones:** **8 regional** (1-8) + **1 functional** zone **9 = Army Postal Service**.
| First digit | Region (exam examples) |
|---|---|
| 1 | Delhi, Haryana, Punjab, HP, J&K |
| **2** | **Uttar Pradesh** (Lucknow), Uttarakhand |
| **3** | Rajasthan, **Gujarat** (Gandhinagar) |
| **4** | **Maharashtra** (Nagpur), MP, Chhattisgarh, Goa |
| **5** | **Andhra Pradesh** (Amaravati), Telangana, Karnataka |
| 6 | Tamil Nadu, Kerala |
| 7 | West Bengal, Odisha, North-East |
| 8 | Bihar, Jharkhand |
- **Digi PIN:** **10-digit alphanumeric**, on the **APT** platform built by **CEPT**; rollout in 23 circles done **4 Aug 2025** (1.64 lakh offices).
- **Speed Post: Aug 1986** (to compete with couriers), refund if late. **IPPB: 1 Sep 2018**, Department of Posts.
- **Project Arrow (2008):** India Post's programme to upgrade post offices and postal services.
- India has the **largest number of post offices** in the world: **1,64,999** (31 Mar 2025). **AP: 10,660**.
> Match angle (SI 2018): Lucknow 2, Gandhinagar 3, Nagpur 4, Amaravati 5.

=== Mass Media – Radio, Television and Development Communication
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1927 | Indian Broadcasting Company (private) |
| **1930** | **Indian State Broadcasting Service** (the "Indian Broadcasting Service" of exam options) |
| **1936** | Renamed **All India Radio** |
| 1957 | AIR named **Akashvani** |
| **15 Sep 1959** | **Doordarshan** begins in **Delhi** |
| 1991 / 1992 / 1995 | Private TV: **Star** (1991) → **Zee** (1992) → **Sony** (1995) |
- **Traditional TV broadcasting = terrestrial** (over-the-air) transmission; cable, DTH and IPTV came later.
- **Prasar Bharati** (Act 1990) runs AIR and Doordarshan; **Yojana** magazine = Ministry of **Information & Broadcasting** (Publications Division).
- First community radio: **Anna FM** (Anna University, **2004**); licensed by MIB. Community radio = participatory tool of development communication.
- **Edgar Dale's Cone of Experience (1946):** direct experiences → … → **radio, recordings, still pictures** → visual symbols → **verbal symbols**; **television** was added only in **1954**.

=== Telecom – Regulators, Milestones and Market

| TDSAT | 2000 amendment of TRAI Act | Disputes; appeals against TRAI |

=== Digital Connectivity – BharatNet, Digital India, PM-WANI
- **Digital India: 1 Jul 2015**, **9 pillars**, first = broadband highways.
- **BharatNet:** ex-**NOFN (2011)** (not NKN) – optic fibre broadband to all **Gram Panchayats**; funded by **USOF (Digital Bharat Nidhi)**; nodal **BBNL** (merged with BSNL); **2.18 lakh GPs**, **6.92 lakh km** fibre; Phase 3 = last mile + private players. Amended BharatNet: **₹1.39 lakh crore**.
- **PM-WANI: Dec 2020** (DoT); public Wi-Fi via **PDOs** and **PDOAs**. **National Broadband Mission: Dec 2019**.
- **CSCs: 5.69 lakh**. **Visakhapatnam Declaration** extended NeSDA to Ladakh and NE. SC: digital access part of **Article 21**.
