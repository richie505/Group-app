## SUB 4 | APIIC, SEZs and PCPIR
[91:4:0] - **APIIC (1973):** the nodal agency that provides **industrial infrastructure** – land, **industrial parks and SEZs**; **PPP** model; after **1991** moved from direct manufacturer to **facilitator**.
[91:4:1] - APSEZ is one SEZ (Atchutapuram-Rambilli) and APGIC a gas JV – neither is the State-wide infrastructure agency.
[91:4:2] - Survey 2025-26: APIIC has **635 clusters** (331 parks, 304 estates).
[91:4:3] - **SEZs (2023):** 47 or more notified; **10 by APIIC**.
[91:4:4] - **APSEZ:** multi-product SEZ at Atchutapuram-Rambilli (Anakapalli); notified **2007**; part of the PCPIR.
[91:4:5] - **Budget 2026:** SEZ units may **sell in the domestic market at a concessional tax**.
[91:4:6] - **Four PCPIRs:** **Visakhapatnam-Kakinada**, Dahej, Paradip, Cuddalore-Nagapattinam. AP PCPIR approved 2009, ~**640 sq km**.
[91:4:7] - **PCPIR Policy 2020-35:** minimum area **50 sq km** (was 250).
[91:4:8] - PPP ports **Krishnapatnam + Gangavaram** drew heavy industry to the coast.
[91:4:9] ~~ Sources: app notes 4:91 (sub 6); Praveen CA360 Sep 2026 magazine p.40 (APIIC with NICDIT)
## SUB 5 | VCIC – Features and ADB Funding
[91:5:0] - **India's first coastal corridor**; first phase of the **East Coast Economic Corridor** (Kolkata-Kanyakumari).
[91:5:1] - Links India to **ASEAN and East Asia** (**Act East + Make in India**); aligned with the Golden Quadrilateral (**NH-16**).
[91:5:2] - **North:** metallurgy, pharma, petrochemicals, IT. **South:** food processing, leather, automobiles (not confined to one region).
[91:5:3] TABLE head: ADB programme (US$ mn) | Policy-based loan | Project loan | Total
  [91:5:3.0] ADB | 125 (100%) | 500 (70%) | 625
  [91:5:3.1] Govt of AP | 0 | 215 (30%) | 215
  [91:5:3.2] Total | 125 | 715 (70:30) | 840
[91:5:4] - **Policy-based loan:** reforms, institutions, skills; **project loan:** roads, power, water, parks.
[91:5:5] - **Executing agency:** Department of **Industries and Commerce**; PMU under the Commissioner of Industries.
[91:5:6] - VCIC is **partly funded by an ADB loan**, not JICA.

## SUB 6 | Industrial Policy 4.0, MSMEs and Exports
[91:6:0] - **AP Industrial Development Policy 4.0 (2024-29)** replaced the **Industrial Development Policy 2023-27**; notified **26 Oct 2024** by **G.O. 68**; **MSME Policy 4.0** by **G.O. 69**.
[91:6:1] - Companion policies: Food Processing, Private Industrial Parks, Aerospace and Defence (not a highway policy).
[91:6:2] TABLE head: Category | FCI (excluding land)
  [91:6:2.0] Large | ₹501-1,000 crore
  [91:6:2.1] Mega | ₹1,001-5,000 crore
  [91:6:2.2] Ultra Mega | above ₹5,000 crore
[91:6:3] - Incentives: capital subsidy 12-15%; **net SGST reimbursed 100% for 5 years** (not ten).
[91:6:4] - **Targets:** **₹30 lakh crore** investment; electronics by 2029 **US$ 50 bn** and **5 lakh jobs**.
[91:6:5] - **MSME Policy 4.0:** "**One Family, One Entrepreneur**" by **2030**; capital subsidy **25%** general, **45%** special categories.
[91:6:6] - **Udyam** registration since **1 Jul 2020**: about **36.8 lakh** MSMEs by 15 Dec 2025; **Anantapur** highest (~2.37 lakh).
[91:6:7] - **AP Cluster Development Programme** (G.O. 17, Jan 2026): **45** clusters/CFCs, aid up to **80%**.
[91:6:8] - **ODOP (DPIIT, 2023):** Kakinada – Uppada Jamdani; Krishna – Machilipatnam Kalamkari; Srikakulam – Budithi brass; **West Godavari – Narsapur crochet laces**.
[91:6:9] - **BRAP 2020** (released **Jun 2022**): AP among the **7 top achievers** – categories, not numerical ranks.
[91:6:10] - AP = **6th** largest exporting State (Survey 2025-26). **CII Partnership Summit:** **Visakhapatnam**, 14-15 Nov 2025.
