=== NOTES 4:82 | India – Agriculture: Commercial Crops (cotton, Sugarcane, Oilseeds, Jute, Tea, Coffee, Rubber, Spices) and Their Regions
## SUB 0 | Cash vs Food Crops
[82:0:0] - **Cash crops**: cotton, sugarcane, jute, tobacco, oilseeds, groundnut, bananas – grown for sale.
[82:0:1] - **Food crops**: wheat, rice, jowar, bajra, millets – NOT cash crops.
[82:0:2] - **Plantation crops** (tea, coffee, rubber, coconut): high-value, **labour- and capital-intensive**; colonial plantations (tea, coffee, rubber, indigo) European-dominated, export-oriented.
[82:0:3] - **Fibre crops**: cotton, jute, sunhemp. **Green Gold** = tea.
[82:0:4] - **Tea** earns the most foreign exchange among cash crops (2024-25 exports 257.88 million kg).

  [82:1:0.2] Tea | 20-30°C | 150-300 cm | Deep, well-drained, acidic hill slopes, high humus; no waterlogging
  [82:1:0.3] Coffee | 15-28°C, shade | 150-250 cm | Well-drained, humus-rich; Western Ghats
  [82:1:0.4] Rubber | 25-35°C, hot humid, equatorial | Over 200 cm (200-300) | Laterite, Western Ghats; NOT delta flood plains
  [82:1:0.5] Sugarcane | Hot humid; 21-27°C; 10-15 months | 75-100 cm | Alluvial / black

  [82:1:0.7] Soybean | Warm growing season | – | Kharif oilseed
[82:1:1] - **Black soils** (clay over 62%) → **cotton, chillies, tobacco**; **red soils** → groundnut, tobacco, ragi.
[82:1:2] - **Sugarcane** = **least water-efficient** (1,400-2,300 mm subtropics, 2,000-3,500 mm tropics). Water-efficient: **millets, pulses, oilseeds**; paddy in Punjab and sugarcane in Maharashtra = subsidy distortion (Jun 2025).
[82:1:3] Rubber needs over **200 cm**, not 100 cm. Soybean is an oilseed AND needs a warm season – but being an oilseed does not explain the warm season.
[82:1:4] - **Red chilli:** a kharif crop that needs a warm climate; Andhra Pradesh (Guntur) is the leading producer.

## SUB 4 | Sugarcane – Tropical vs Sub-tropical
[82:4:0] TABLE head: Point | Tropical (south) | Sub-tropical (north)
  [82:4:0.0] States | Maharashtra, Karnataka, TN, AP | UP, Bihar
  [82:4:0.1] Yield | Higher | Lower
  [82:4:0.2] Area | Smaller | Larger
  [82:4:0.3] Crushing | Oct to May/June (longer) | Nov to Feb
  [82:4:0.4] Reason | Cool nights with clear skies, latitude, long sunshine (APPSC G1 2023: I, II, III) | Alluvium rich in lime and potash, level land, dense market, compact blocks
[82:4:1] - **Western UP** = main sugar-mill belt; UP = "**Sugar Bowl of India**" (2nd largest sugar producer, Dec 2025).
[82:4:2] - Mills near fields: cane is **weight-losing** (loses weight and sugar after cutting). Disease: **red rot**; variety **Co. 1148**.
[82:4:3] - UP output more than Maharashtra, productivity less; most Maharashtra mills cooperative (true, not explanation).
[82:4:4] - Pricing: **FRP**, not MSP.
