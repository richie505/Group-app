## SUB 3 | Cash, Fibre, Food and Plantation Crops
[3:0] - **Cash crops** (grown for sale): **cotton, sugarcane, jute, tobacco, oilseeds (groundnut/peanut), tea, rubber, bananas**.
[3:1] - **Food crops:** wheat, rice, maize, **jowar, millets**, gram, peas – the usual "not a cash crop" answer.
[3:2] - **Soybean** is a commercial oilseed too; in the tobacco–cotton–soybean–rubber set it is keyed as "not a cash crop".
[3:3] - **Fibre crops:** **cotton, jute, sunhemp** (also flax/mesta); jute–wheat–cotton mixes a food crop.
[3:4] - **Plantation crops** (tea, coffee, rubber, coconut): high-value, **labour- and capital-intensive**; colonial plantations (tea, coffee, rubber, indigo) were **European-dominated and export-oriented**.
[3:5] - **Medicinal crop:** **aloe vera** (not sugarcane, cotton or mahua).
[3:6] - **Tea** earns the most foreign exchange among tea, tobacco, flax and wheat.
[3:7] - Tea and coffee are **substitutes**: if tea gets cheaper, **coffee consumption decreases**.

[5:0] TABLE head: Point | Tropical (south) | Sub-tropical (north)

  [5:0.1] Yield and sucrose | Higher | Lower
  [5:0.2] Area | Smaller | Larger
  [5:0.3] Crushing season | October to May/June (longer) | November to February

[5:1] - **Sugar industry is shifting south**: higher yield and juice per hectare explain it (A and R true, R explains A).
[5:2] - **UP** produces more cane and sugar than Maharashtra but has **lower productivity**; most Maharashtra mills are **cooperatives** (true, but not the explanation).
[5:3] - **Frost** at ripening **lowers sucrose**; excess rain lowers sugar content.
[5:4] - **Adsali** cane (Maharashtra): planted July-August, ripens in about **18 months** (normal crop 10-12).
[5:5] - **Sustainable Sugarcane Initiative (SSI):** fewer seed setts (**low seed cost**), suits **drip irrigation**, more **intercropping**; it does **not** ban chemical fertilisers.
[5:6] - Pest **Pyrilla** (leafhopper); disease **red rot**; variety **Co. 1148**.
[5:7] - **UP = "Sugar Bowl of India"**; western UP is the mill belt; mills sit near fields because cane is **weight-losing**.
[5:8] - **Pricing: FRP**, not MSP.

[6:7] - **Import dependence:** farmers prefer foodgrains with support prices; oilseeds are mostly **rain-fed**; tree-borne oilseeds and rice bran remain unexploited.
[6:8] - **India** = largest producer and exporter of **castor** (Gujarat about 86%).
[6:9] - **MSP:** **23** commodities – 7 cereals, 5 pulses, **7 oilseeds** (groundnut, rapeseed-mustard, soybean, sesamum, sunflower, safflower, nigerseed) + commercial crops; coffee, areca nut, turmeric are not MSP crops.
[6:10] - **Oilseeds 2025-26:** area, production and productivity all rose; **NMEO-Oilseeds** + **NMEO-Oil Palm**; **AP leads oil palm** (area, production, productivity).
