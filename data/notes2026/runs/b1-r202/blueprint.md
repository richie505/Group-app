## 3. Repeats and trends
- **Kakinada Riot Case = first criminal case** is the single most repeated Andhra fact (6 PYQs + 2 pair items); **annulment in 1911** the most repeated Bengal fact (6).
- **Immediate cause = partition** recurs in UPSC 2001/2010 and CDS 2011; **Indian Society of Oriental Art = Abanindranath** 5 times across UPPCS, CDS and APPSC.
- **The same AP History items appear with conflicting printed keys** (Pal's host at Machilipatnam, the Rajahmundry body, the National College president). All three were settled from NextGen and tagged as disputes.
- APPSC recently uses **pairs and match items** (Sample Taker 2023, G2 Mains 2025) that combine the Kakinada, Kotappakonda, Tenali and Chirala-Perala cases with their years.
