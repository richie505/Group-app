## SUB 0 | Vandemataram Movement in Andhra – Name, Phase, Beginnings
[0:0] - **Name:** in Andhra the Swadeshi Movement was called the **Vandemataram Movement** (a **sequel to the partition of Bengal**); Andhra phase **1906-1911**, ended with the **1911 annulment**.
[0:1] - **Real political awakening** in Andhra began with it; **students** of Andhra took an active part in politics **from the Vandemataram movement onwards**.
[0:2] - **Madras, Sept 1905:** Madras Beach students' meeting under **G. Subramania Iyer** condemned the partition; Kela Sri Ramamurthy sang Vandemataram; Subramania Bharati sang Tamil songs.
[0:3] - **Madras, 1906:** an **Industrial Association** and a **Swadeshi League**; the League sent **Nyapati Subbarao and K. Venkataramana Rao** to tour Andhra and popularise Swadeshi – **before Pal's visit**.
[0:4] - **S.N. Banerjea's arrest (1906)** → protests at Rajahmundry, Kakinada, Vizianagaram, Gooty; slogan "**Vandemataram – Manade Rajyam**".
[0:5] - **Bala Bharati Samiti:** founded at **Rajahmundry, February 1907** by local youths (Chilukuri Veerabhadra Rao, Ganti Lakshmanna and others; members incl. T. Prakasam) – Andhra's response to the **partition of Bengal**; backed by Karanam Guneswara Rao.
[0:6] - **Legacy:** the movement roused interest in Andhra's language and history (Andhrula Charitramu, 1910) and **moulded the Andhra Movement** for a separate province.

## SUB 1 | Bipin Chandra Pal's Tour of Andhra, April 1907
[1:0] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [1:0.0] When | April 1907 (about 16-27 April) – not 1908, not May
  [1:0.1] Why | to spread the Vandemataram movement – Pal was the Extremist leader who promoted it in Andhra
  [1:0.2] Organiser | Mutnuri Krishna Rao, editor of Krishna Patrika (he called for an Extremist leader)
  [1:0.3] Route | Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam → Kakinada (17 April, two lectures) → Rajahmundry (19-24 April, five lectures) → Vijayawada → Machilipatnam → Madras
  [1:0.4] Rajahmundry | the Bala Bharati Samiti organised the meetings; Pal opened the Godavari Swadeshi Stores; Guneswara Rao gave Rs 1,000 for a national school
  [1:0.5] Machilipatnam | Pal was the guest of Ramadasu Naidu; Young Men's Swarajya Samiti and a national school committee → Andhra Jateeya Kalasala
  [1:0.6] Madras | T. Prakasam presided over Pal's Madras meetings
[1:1] - **Pal's message:** **Swaraj the goal, boycott the weapon**; Swadeshi, national education.
[1:2] - **Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao** – the Andhra student leader who welcomed Pal and **went with the Vandemataram campaign to many places**.
[1:3] - **Translator:** **Chilakamarti Lakshmi Narasimham** rendered Pal's Rajahmundry speech into Telugu.
[1:4] - **Hosts and chairs (old notes):** Kakinada lectures chaired by **Kattiventi Perraju**; at Rajahmundry he stayed at **Madella Sourayya's** house; Vijayawada hosts **Munagala Raja and Nayani Venkata Rangarao**; last Machilipatnam speech "**Matru Murthi**"; the tour **began at Vizianagaram**, not Rajahmundry.
[1:5] - Pal (Brahmo, weekly **New India**, 1901) was later **arrested**.
[1:6] > Keys disputed in old banks: Rajahmundry body = **Bala Bharati Samiti** (not Young Men's Association); Machilipatnam host = **Ramadasu Naidu** (not Kopalle/Kompalle Hanumantha Rao).

## SUB 2 | Rajahmundry College Incident, 1907 – Student Participation
[2:0] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [2:0.0] College | Government Arts and Teachers' Training College, Rajahmundry
  [2:0.1] Principal | Mark Hunter – not Pichard, Tyler/Taylor or Reid
  [2:0.2] Order | March-April 1907: banned Vandemataram badges and the slogan
  [2:0.3] Defiance | student Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao (teacher trainee) presented an address to Pal; with J. Ramachandra Rao organised the protest
  [2:0.4] Punishment | 138 students debarred from exams for two years; Gadicherla expelled and blacklisted from government jobs
  [2:0.5] Significance | beginning of student participation from Andhra in the freedom struggle – the "Rajahmundry College incident"
[2:1] - Not Chittoor, Kurnool or Hyderabad college; the student leader was **Gadicherla**, not Gunneswara Rao, Chidambaram Pillai, Pattabhi or Nyapati Subbarao.

## SUB 3 | National College, Machilipatnam and Swadeshi Institutions
[3:0] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [3:0.0] Name | Andhra Jateeya Kalasala ("National College", Bandar National College), Machilipatnam
  [3:0.1] Founded | 1910 – not 1905-1908 or 1912-1915 (Andhra Jateeya Vidya Parishad 1907 preceded it)
  [3:0.2] Founder / first principal | Kopalle Hanumantha Rao
  [3:0.3] President | Konda Venkatappaiah
  [3:0.4] Aim | to train young men in techniques of modern production (crafts, weaving) – national education free of government control
  [3:0.5] Origin | Pal's Machilipatnam visit (1907) → national school committee
[3:1] - Other Swadeshi institutions: **Godavari Swadeshi Stores** (Rajahmundry, opened by Pal); national school at Ramachandrapuram (Kattiventi Perraju's land, Rs 40,000); Munagala Raja's textile unit, Vijayawada (1906).
[3:2] - **Machilipatnam chain (old notes):** **Andhra Jateeya Vidya Parishad** set up by **Kopalle Hanumantha Rao** on **17 Nov 1907**; the Kalasala was **inaugurated by Konda Venkatappayya (20 Feb 1910)**; Kopalle made **weaving** a subject.
[3:3] - **Others (old notes):** **Godavari Swadeshi Store** set up by **Karanam Guneswara Rao**; the Ramachandrapuram school was **inaugurated by Nyapathi Subbarao**; **Malladi Venkata Subbarao** – Swadeshi **pencils**; Munagala Raja's unit (June 1906) was the **first such unit in the Madras Presidency**; **Raghupati Venkataratnam Naidu** was principal of **Mahaboob College**; Nizam College, Hyderabad – 1887.
[3:4] - **Regional leaders:** Visakhapatnam/Vizianagaram – C.Y. Chintamani; Rajahmundry – Chilakamarti; Vijayawada – Ayyadevara Kaleswara Rao; Guntur – Konda Venkatappaiah; Bandar – Gadicherla.
[3:5] > President is keyed as **Konda Venkatappaiah** (NextGen); some keys give Nyapati Subbarao.

## SUB 4 | Riots and Cases, 1907-1909 – Kakinada, Kotappakonda, Tenali
[4:0] TABLE head: Event | Date / place | Key facts
  [4:0.0] Kakinada Riot Case | 1907 (APPSC 2025 key: July 1907; some books 31 May 1907) | District Medical Officer Captain (Dr) Kemp clashed with the boy Kompella (Kopalle) Krishna Rao over "Vande Mataram"; troops called from Rajahmundry; defended by Nyapati Subbarao – the first criminal case in Andhra in the movement
  [4:0.1] Kotappakonda riots | February 1909, Shivaratri, Guntur (near Narasaraopet) | 14 died; (Gade) Chinnapa Reddy sentenced to death – the only death sentence in India in the Vandemataram movement
  [4:0.2] Tenali Bomb Case | April 1909, Tenali | Chukkapalli Ramaiah, Lakkaraju Basavaiah, K. Venkatarayudu accused of a bomb against the Howrah passenger; T. Prakasam got them released – a case of the Vandemataram movement
[4:1] - **Match (APPSC G2 2025):** Kakinada riots – **July 1907**; Pal's tour – **April 1907**; Kotappakonda – **February 1909**; Tenali bomb – **April 1909**.
[4:2] - **Pairs:** "Kakinada Riot Case – 1917" ✗ (1907); Kotappakonda – Chinnapa Reddy ✓; **Chirala-Perala – Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya** ✓ (1921, NCM).
[4:3] - **AICC at Bezawada** met in **1921** (not 1919).
[4:4] - **Kakinada details (old notes):** **Dr Kemp** beat the boy **Kompelli Krishna Rao** for shouting Vande Mataram → the **European Club** was attacked; reserve police came from Rajahmundry.
[4:5] - **Curbs on "Vande Mataram"** caused the **Rajahmundry** (badges) and **Kakinada** (greeting) incidents; later echo – **Osmania University 1938**, students incl. **P.V. Narasimha Rao** expelled for singing Vande Mataram.
