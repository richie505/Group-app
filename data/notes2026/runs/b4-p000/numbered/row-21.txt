## SUB 0 | Evidence of Climate Change – Past Climates and Recent Warming
[0:0] # Past climates (asked 7 times)
[0:1] - **Tree rings (dendroclimatology)**: ring width is a fairly accurate index of a year's **precipitation** (water available in the growing season); old tree rings are the best record of a place's **past local climate over centuries** (weather logs cover only recent decades).
[0:2] - Other evidence: glacial landforms (advances and retreats), sediments of glacial lakes, archaeological finds (Rajasthan wet and cool about 8,000 BC).
[0:3] - **Great Ice Age = Pleistocene** (cooling began in the **Pliocene**) – APPSC G1 2002 key: "Pleistocene and Pliocene". Last glacial peak about 18,000 years ago; present interglacial began about 10,000 years ago.
[0:4] - **Glaciation of peninsular India** = the **Permo-Carboniferous (Carboniferous) ice age** of Gondwana, recorded in the **Talchir tillite** at the base of the Gondwana rocks; Pleistocene glaciers reached only the Himalaya.
[0:5] - **Little Ice Age**: about **1550–1850** (NCERT; others 1300–1850, glaciers greatest **1500–1850**); much colder winters in Europe and N America; among exam options **1650–1870** is the period given. 950–1250 AD = Medieval Warm Period.
[0:6] # Recent warming
[0:7] TABLE head: Indicator | Record
  [0:7.0] 2024 | Hottest year on record, about 1.55°C above pre-industrial
  [0:7.1] 2025 | 2nd/3rd warmest (~1.43°C); warmest La Nina year
  [0:7.2] 2015–2025 | The 11 warmest years (WMO State of the Global Climate 2025)
  [0:7.3] India | Warmed about 0.7°C since 1900 (less than global land, ~1.59°C); IMD: 2024 warmest year since 1901
  [0:7.4] Gangotri Glacier | Retreated about 1,700 m since 1935
  [0:7.5] Sea level | Up about 11 cm since 1993
[0:8] - **Global warming** = rise in global surface temperature, caused by rising greenhouse-gas concentrations (CDS 2017 A-R: both true, II explains I).
[0:9] - **Sea-level rise** = thermal expansion + melting ice; higher snow albedo is not a cause.

## SUB 1 | Causes – Natural and Anthropogenic
[1:0] # Natural causes (asked 5 times)
[1:1] TABLE head: Cause | Meaning / effect
  [1:1.0] Eccentricity | Shape of Earth's orbit
  [1:1.1] Obliquity | Axial tilt
  [1:1.2] Precession (of the equinoxes) | Wobble of the rotational axis
  [1:1.3] Solar output (sunspots) | Satellites since 1978 show no net increase: the Sun does not explain recent warming
  [1:1.4] Volcanoes | SO2 + ash form aerosols that reflect sunlight: short-term cooling
[1:2] - **Milankovitch** (Serbian, **1920s**): astronomical theory – orbital forcing by eccentricity, tilt (shifting of the axis) and precession. **Solar irradiance is NOT a Milankovitch factor** (NCERT lists sunspots separately as a solar cause).
[1:3] # Anthropogenic causes
[1:4] - **Chief contributor** to warming in the last and present century = **anthropogenic impact** (IPCC AR6), not plate tectonics, volcanoes or Earth's rotation.
[1:5] - Fossil fuels (CO2), **land-use change** (deforestation removes sinks), **agriculture** (methane, N2O). CO2 causes about **three-quarters** of warming.
[1:6] - Rising GHGs → global warming, **CO2 fertilisation** of plants, stratospheric cooling linked to **ozone depletion**; there is **no "oxygen fertilisation effect"** (CDS 2007).
[1:7] - **Water vapour** = most abundant greenhouse gas (feedback).
[1:8] - **China** = largest CO2 emitter, and largest in the developing world; India 3rd (after China and USA).
[1:9] TABLE head: Gas | GWP (100 yr, AR6) | Main sources
  [1:9.0] CO2 | 1 | Fossil fuels, deforestation, cement
  [1:9.1] Methane | ~27–30 | Rice paddies, livestock (enteric fermentation), landfills
  [1:9.2] Nitrous oxide | ~273 | Fertilisers (largest farm source), manure
  [1:9.3] CFCs / HFCs | Very high | Refrigeration, air conditioning (synthetic)
  [1:9.4] SF6 | ~25,200 (highest) | Electrical switchgear (entirely synthetic)
[1:10] # Short-lived climate pollutants (SLCPs)
[1:11] - Stay in the air **days to a few decades** – far **shorter** than CO2.
[1:12] - **Black carbon** = **soot**; **tropospheric ozone** is not emitted directly (sunlight on methane, CO, VOCs, NOx); methane; HFCs.
[1:13] - **Climate and Clean Air Coalition**: UNEP + **six countries**, 16 Feb 2012.
[1:14] ~~ Sources: NCERT Fundamentals ch.11 (OnlyIAS Physical Geography 08 p.8); IPCC AR6 WG I; Global Carbon Project; app notes 4:21 sub 1–3
## SUB 2 | Impacts of Global Warming
[2:0] - IPCC: warming raises the frequency and severity of **cyclones, storms and hurricanes – all of these**, plus floods, droughts, glacier melt (UPPCS 2018).
[2:1] - **Melting polar ice and sea-level rise** = the most cited effect; Uttarakhand 2002 PYQ keyed statements 1, 3 and 4 (coral islands submerged, Fiji by 2044) – these were alarmist forecasts, not facts (as of 2026 Fiji is not submerged).
[2:2] TABLE head: Area | Effect
  [2:2.0] Sea level | Uninhabited Kiribati islets Tebua Tarawa, Abanuea submerged 1999; Tuvalu at risk
  [2:2.1] Coral reefs | 1.2°C warm-water reef tipping point already crossed (UN brief, Jun 2026)
  [2:2.2] Amazon | Beyond ~2°C parts may turn savanna and a carbon source
  [2:2.3] Oceans, species | Acidity up 30% since the Industrial Revolution; up to 25% of mountain species face extinction (AR6)
  [2:2.4] Disease | Warmer, wetter conditions spread vector-borne malaria, dengue
[2:3] - **AP**: heatwaves in Palnadu, Kurnool, Anantapur; **coastal erosion at Uppada (Kakinada)**.
[2:4] - **COP31**: **Türkiye (Antalya), November 2026**.
[2:5] ~~ Sources: IPCC AR6; Shankar Environment ch.17, 20; WMO; app notes 4:21 sub 6
## SUB 3 | Geoengineering – Reducing Global Warming
[3:0] TABLE head: Proposal | Meaning | Note
  [3:0.0] Stratospheric sulphate aerosol injection | SO2 forms reflective aerosols; copies volcanic cooling | Termination shock if stopped suddenly
  [3:0.1] Cirrus cloud thinning | Seeding high cirrus so they thin and more outgoing heat escapes | –
  [3:0.2] Marine cloud brightening | Sea-salt spray as cloud condensation nuclei | –
[3:1] - UPSC 2019: cirrus thinning and sulphate injection are proposed for **reducing global warming** – not artificial rain, cyclones or solar wind.
