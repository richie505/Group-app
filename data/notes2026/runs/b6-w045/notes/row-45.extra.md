=== New Current Affairs: 5–14 Nov 2026
# Andhra Pradesh – commissions and their heads
- **APERC Chairperson**: retired IAS officer **Binoy Kumar**, appointed **4 November 2026**, succeeding **P. Venkata Rama Reddy**; term **5 years or till age 70**, whichever is earlier; he was Principal Secretary to the CM of undivided AP (2012–13).
- **APERC**: **independent statutory body**, set up **31 March 1999** under the **AP Electricity Reform Act, 1998**; **Chairperson + two Members**; fixes tariffs for generation, transmission, distribution and supply; **Ancha Pandu Ranga Rao** – Member (Legal), Oct 2026.
- **AP Higher Education Fee Regulatory and Monitoring Committee (APHEFRMC)**: **P. Chandrasekhar** appointed **NMC representative** for **3 years** (Nov 2026); body est. **19 August 2019** under the **AP Higher Education Regulatory and Monitoring Commission Act, 2019**; regulates fees of **private unaided** higher education institutions.
- **National Medical Commission**: statutory, **NMC Act, 2019**, functioning from **2020**, Ministry of Health; **33 members** = Chairperson + **10 ex officio** + **22 part-time** (Section 4(1)); Nov 2026 warning against fake medical colleges.
- **12th Pay Revision Commission (AP)**: **AP JAC Amaravati** (formed **5 February 2017, Tirupati**; Chairman **Bopparaju Venkateswarlu**) urged it to report **within six months**.
# Andhra Pradesh in national rankings and assessments
- **Access (In)Equality Index 2026** (by **CNES, O.P. Jindal Global University**; since **2021**): **Goa 1st (0.68)**, **Andhra Pradesh 2nd (0.66)**, **Sikkim 3rd**; AP a **"Front Runner"**.
- AEI method: **5 dimensions** (basic infrastructure, healthcare, education, socio-economic security, access to justice), **60 indicators in 23 categories**, equal weights; **AP 1st in socio-economic security (0.69)** though **13th in per capita income**.
- **Ease of Vehicle Ownership Index** (by **IndiaTech.org** with **Consumer VOICE**): **AP 1st** for used-vehicle transfer, then **Kerala, Tamil Nadu**; Delhi 4th; **UP last**; pre-owned vehicle market $36.4 bn (2024-25) → $73.5 bn (2030).
- **Swachh Survekshan 2025-26** (**10th edition**; **MoHUA**, launched **2016** under SBM-U): **60 national awards** given by **President Droupadi Murmu**, **Vigyan Bhawan, 11 Nov 2026**; theme "**Swachhata Ki Nayi Pehel – Badhayein Haath, Karein Safai Saath**".
- Super Swachh League top three: **Navi Mumbai, Surat, Vijayawada**; million-plus top three: **GVMC Visakhapatnam, Jabalpur, Lucknow**; Swachh Shehar Jodi top three: Ahmedabad–Sanand, Indore–Depalpur, **Tirupati (mentor)–Kuppam (mentee)**; new categories: **Clean Himalayan City, River Front Town, Coastal Town**.
- **AP PHC patient-satisfaction assessment** (IVRS feedback, Health Department, Jul–Nov 2026): **Palnadu 1st (81.6%)**, Polavaram 2nd (81.5%), Nellore 3rd; **Kurnool last (69.5%)**.
# National and global reports
- **QS World University Rankings: Asia 2027**: no Indian institution in the **top 50** for the **2nd year running**; **IIT Delhi** best Indian at **66th** (59th last year); IISc 79th, IIT Bombay 83rd; Indian entries up **294 → 325**.
- **SRS Statistical Report 2024** (**Registrar General & Census Commissioner, ORGI**): India's **TFR 1.9** (rural 2.1, urban 1.5); highest **Bihar 2.9**, then **UP 2.6**; cited by the **Supreme Court** (Nov 2026), which said laws penalising **more than two children** have lost relevance.
- **ICRIER report "The Participation Paradox"** (PLFS + Time Use Survey, 2017-18 to 2025): working women **104 million → 201 million**; **female WPR 22% → 38%**; a rural woman now earns about **45%** of a rural man's pay (53% earlier); rural women's regular salaried share fell 10.5% → 9.3%.
- Same report: about **60%** of women outside the labour force cite **childcare and household work**; **Time Use Survey 2024** – women do **3.7 hours more** domestic work a day than men.
- **World Bank International Debt Report 2026**: developing countries' external debt up **5.5%** to a record **$9.4 trillion (2025)**; they paid **$436 billion more** in debt service than they got in new finance (2022–25); debt of **IDA-eligible** countries up **8.1% to $1.3 trillion**.
- **WMO State of the Climate 2026** (released **9 Nov 2026**, opening day of **COP31, Antalya**): **Jan–Sep 2026 = 1.47°C** above 1850–1900; 2026 among the **four warmest years**; **CO2 426.8 ppm (2025)**, 53% above 278 ppm of 1750; a strengthening **El Niño**.
