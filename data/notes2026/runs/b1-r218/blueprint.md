## 5. Settled conflicts
- **1933 Ryot agitation:** old notes said Parvataneni Veeraiah Chowdary ("GS 2024 key"); Wikipedia (N.G. Ranga) and the printed APPSC FRO key say **N.G. Ranga**; Parvataneni belongs to Pedanandipadu 1921-22 (NextGen Part 19 p.1). Notes and own MCQ fixed.
- **Challapalli temple land:** NextGen (three copies) 6,000 acres vs old notes 600 – NextGen followed; owner to confirm.
- **Komaram Bheem:** NextGen Society gives the Gond movement 1938-44; the AP match PYQ gives his martyrdom as 1940 – both kept.
- **Santhal Pargana tenancy law:** PMF says "Santhal Pargana Tenancy Act 1876"; old notes and own MCQ use the 1949 SPT (Supplementary Provisions) Act – kept 1949 (general knowledge).
