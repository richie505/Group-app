## SUB 3 | Ryots' Associations and the 1937 Rythu Rakshana Yatra
[3:0] - **Krishna District Rythu Sangham (1915)**; Guntur and Godavari sanghams (1923).

[3:2] - Nellore: Nellore Zilla Zamindari Rytu Sangam (Dodla Ramireddy, 1929); Zamin Rytu Sangam (Vennalkanti Raghavaiah, 1930) – paper '**Zamin Rytu**'.

[3:4] # Rythu Rakshana Yatra (1937)

[3:7] - NextGen also dates a Ranga cycle rally Itchapuram → Madras to **1938-39** (against the Madras Land Settlement Act).

## SUB 4 | Munagala Paragana Struggle (1930s)
[4:0] - **Location:** **Nandigama taluk, Krishna district** then; now **Suryapet (earlier Nalgonda)**, Telangana.
[4:1] - **Zamindar:** **Nayani Venkata Ranga Rao** (Keesaravari family) – literary patron but a harsh landlord.
[4:2] - Bodies: Munagala Peasant Association (Batchala Jagannadh Das); Zamin Peasant Association (**Kodati Venkata Narasimha Rao**).
[4:3] - **Kalukova Satyagraha (1938)** – **Nanduri Prasada Rao**, over a paddy wage of 80 seers per acre.
[4:4] - **Brahmaiah Award (15 Jan 1939)** – by APCC Secretary **Gottipati Brahmaiah**; settlement covering **142 villages**.
[4:5] - **Munagala Day: 11 June 1939**; agitation **withdrawn 19 June 1939** after Prakasam and Ranga promised action.
[4:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP History Part 19 p.11-12; old app notes (142 villages, Munagala Day, withdrawal)
