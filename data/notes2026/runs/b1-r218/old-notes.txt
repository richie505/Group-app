=== NOTES 1:218 | Kisan Movements in Andhra – Munagala, Challapalli and Other Struggles
## SUB 0 | Kisan Movement in Andhra – Overview
[218:0:0] - Demands: **MSP**, **minimum wages** and **zamindari abolition**.
[218:0:1] - Ranga's **1929 memorandum to the Whitley Commission** asked for **minimum wages**.
[218:0:2] - Early bodies: **Krishna District Rythu Sangham (1915)**; Guntur and Godavari sanghams (1923).
[218:0:3] - **Guntur:** the first farmers' association there was founded by **N.G. Ranga**.
[218:0:4] - **PM-KISAN extended (2026):** On 31 July 2026 the Union Cabinet extended PM-KISAN to **2030-31** (period 2026-27 to 2030-31) with an outlay of about **Rs 3.15 lakh crore**; the Rs 6,000 a year support to farmers stays the same.
## SUB 1 | Pioneers and Leaders – Titles
[218:1:0] TABLE head: Leader | Title / role
  [218:1:0.0] N.G. Ranga (Gogineni Ranganayakulu) | Rythu Bandhu / Rythu Bandhava, "Father of the Indian Peasant Movement", "Acharya"; founder of the Andhra Kisan movement (G2 2019, 2011 keys); Padma Vibhushan 1991
  [218:1:0.1] Gottipati Brahmaiah | "Rythu Pedda" (leader of farmers); pioneer of the Zamindari Ryot Movement; APCC Secretary; author of the Munagala Award; led the 1931 Mopidevi no-tax resolution
  [218:1:0.2] Kommareddi Satyanarayana (Murthy) | Formed village peasant associations (1935); led the 1937 Rythu Yatra
  [218:1:0.3] Parvataneni Veeriah Chowdary | Led the 1933 Ryot agitation of Andhra (GS 2024 key)
  [218:1:0.4] Pulleti Shyamasundara Rao, Gouthu Latchanna | Srikakulam kisan leaders; Pulleti died in jail after the Mandasa protest
  [218:1:0.5] Swami Sahajanand Saraswati | First AIKS President; founded the Bihar Provincial Kisan Sabha (1929)
[218:1:1] - Brahmaiah: boycotted the Simon Commission; later **Padma Bhushan (1982)** and Chairman, AP Legislative Council (1964-68).
[218:1:2] - **Ranga** joined the freedom struggle around **1930**, inspired by **Gandhi** (not Bose).
[218:1:3] Ranga got the **Padma Vibhushan (1991)**, not the Bharat Ratna. The Munagala Award is Brahmaiah's, not a book of Ranga.

## SUB 7 | Munagala Paragana Struggle (1930-39)
[218:7:0] - Location: **Nandigama taluk, Krishna district** then; now **Suryapet/Nalgonda** (back to Nalgonda 1959).
[218:7:1] - Zamindar: **Nayani Venkata Ranga Rao** – patron of writers but a harsh landlord.
[218:7:2] - **Kalukova Satyagraha (1938)** – **Nanduri Prasada Rao**; over a paddy wage of **80 seers per acre**; the **Sub-Collector** made the zamindar pay.
[218:7:3] - **Brahmaiah Award (15 Jan 1939)** – settlement covering **142 villages**.
[218:7:4] - **Munagala Day: 11 June 1939**; agitation **withdrawn 19 June 1939** after Prakasam and Ranga promised action.
