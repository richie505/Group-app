## 4. Key corrections worth knowing
- Largest Central tax: **Union excise until 2005-06** (old notes and two printed keys said corporation tax for 2005-06 / 2002-03), corporation tax 2006-07 to about 2019-20, income tax now (Receipts Budget figures).
- Two largest in the 1990s: **excise and customs**, not "customs and income tax" (printed key and old notes).
- Kelkar's final report **scaled down** the housing-loan proposal (₹50,000 rebate kept) – so UPSC 2005 statement B is false; GTB went to OBC, not PNB.
- "Shared / not shared / accrues wholly to the Centre" questions follow the **pre-2000** scheme; since the 80th Amendment all Union taxes except cess and surcharge are shareable (dispute lines).
