=== NOTES 4:72 | Human Development – Concept, Hdi Components and India's Position
## SUB 0 | Concept – Human Development vs Economic Growth
[72:0:0] - Human development = **enlargement of human choices**, not just a rise in national income.
[72:0:1] - **Economic growth** = quantitative (income-linked); **development** = quantitative + qualitative.
[72:0:2] - Two countries with the same GNI per capita can reach different human outcomes.
[72:0:3] - **Amartya Sen** – capability approach: capabilities = **substantive freedoms** to lead lives people value; he avoided a fixed universal list. Book: "**Development as Freedom**" (1999); **Nobel in Economics 1998**.
