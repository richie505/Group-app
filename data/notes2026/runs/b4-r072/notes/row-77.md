=== Amaravati – the Capital Question
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 2 Jun 2014 | Bifurcation: **Hyderabad** common capital for up to 10 years; AP Secretariat first worked from Hyderabad |
| **30 Dec 2014** | **APCRDA** (**Andhra Pradesh Capital Region Development Authority**) under the **APCRDA Act, 2014** |
| **22 Oct 2015** | Foundation stone of **Amaravati** ("People's Capital") by PM **Narendra Modi** at **Uddandarayunipalem** (Guntur) |
| 2016 | Interim **Secretariat at Velagapudi, Amaravati** – the answer keys give "Amaravati" for the post-bifurcation Secretariat |
| **1 Jan 2019** | **High Court of AP** at the **Amaravati capital region** |
| Dec 2019 | Three-capitals plan: **Visakhapatnam** (executive), **Amaravati** (legislative), **Kurnool** (judicial) – **Vijayawada not** one of them |
| Jan 2023 | CM declared **Visakhapatnam** the (executive) capital |
| 2024 → 2026 | New government: **Amaravati the sole capital**; Lok Sabha passed the **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Bill, 2026** formally declaring Amaravati the capital |
- Amaravati lies on the **southern bank of the Krishna**; capital cluster **Vijayawada–Guntur–Tenali–Mangalagiri**. Physical progress only **6.84%** (Survey 2025-26).
- **Jul 2026:** MoU with **UN-Habitat** → Centre of Excellence for Urban Affairs at Amaravati.
> Exam angle: "new capital after Hyderabad" = Amaravati; "executive capital Jan 2023" = Visakhapatnam (now outdated).

=== Urbanisation – Share and Trend
- **Census town:** **5,000+** people, **75%+** of male workers outside agriculture, density **400+ per sq km**.
| Census | AP urban share |
|---|---|
| 1971 | **18.35%** |
| 1981 / 1991 | 22.14% / 24.77% |
| **2001** | **24.13%** (residual AP); **27.3%** for undivided AP – the "**27 percent**" of APPSC 2003 |
| **2011** | **29.47%** vs India **31.16%** |
- AP is the **least urbanised southern State** (Tamil Nadu about 48%, Kerala 47.7%, Karnataka 38.6%).
- 2001–11: urban **109.56 → 146.10 lakh ≈ 33%**; rural only about 1.5%. AP has **3.87%** of India's urban population.
- **16th Finance Commission "Urbanisation Premium"** rewards merging peri-urban villages into ULBs.

=== Migration and Urban Problems
- About **39.55 lakh** people of AP live away from their native places; about half seasonal.
- **Gulf migration:** **East Godavari (Konaseema)**, West Godavari, Kadapa; supported by **APNRTS**. Rayalaseema migrants also go to Bengaluru and Hyderabad; **Uttarandhra** is an out-migration region.
- **Slums:** undivided AP about **1.02 crore** slum dwellers (2011), **second after Maharashtra**.
- **Urban flooding:** Vijayawada – **Budameru** rivulet; flash floods from **lost wetlands, blocked drains**. AI flood forecasting: RTGS + APSDMA + **IIT Gandhinagar**.
