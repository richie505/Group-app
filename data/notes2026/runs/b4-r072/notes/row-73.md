=== Density and Growth – AP vs India
- Density: **304 per sq km** (2011) vs India **382**; 145 (1961) → 208 (1981) → 252 (1991) → **279 (2001)** → 304 (2011). Undivided AP 2011: **308**.
| Decade | AP | India |
|---|---|---|
| 1961–71 | 18.88% | 24.80% |
| **1981–91** | **21.13% (highest)** | 23.87% |
| 1991–2001 | 12.32% | **21.5%** |
| 2001–11 | **9.21%** | 17.7% |
- 2001–11 AP grew at about **half** the national rate. Reasons: falling fertility, literacy and **female education**, urbanisation, family planning – **not** a rise in fertility.
> Exam angle: 304 (residual) vs 308 (undivided); 279 = 2001.
~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.1 and Annexure 8.2-8.3 (p.438-439); app notes 4:73 (sub 1, 2)

=== Fertility, Age Structure and Population Policy
| Indicator | AP | India |
|---|---|---|
| TFR (SRS) | **1.5** (NFHS-5: about **1.7**) | **1.9** (replacement 2.1) |
| CBR (SRS) | **15.0** | 18.4 |
| CDR (SRS) | **6.9** (higher) | 6.4 |

- **2007-08 (DLHS-3 era, undivided AP):** mean age at marriage of boys about **23.8 years**; population below 15 years about **27.7%** (APPSC G-2 2011 keys).
- **12 Feb 2026:** two-child cap for local body elections removed.
- **5 Mar 2026:** Draft Population Management Policy – raise TFR **1.5 → 2.1**; prepare for ageing by 2047.
- **1 Apr 2026:** Swarna Andhra Population Management Day started (every fourth Saturday).
- **16 May 2026:** CM – **Rs 30,000** for a 3rd child, **Rs 40,000** for a 4th; "**Pillale Sampada**" (children are wealth).
- Census 2027 data will feed the post-2026 **delimitation** debate.
