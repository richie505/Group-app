# Godavari valley
- **Koya** – Godavari basin (Chintoor, Polavaram, Eluru, ASR); call themselves **Koyi Toor**.
- **Konda Reddi** – Godavari hills, **Rampa** (East and West Godavari); **PVTG**; podu.
# Southern forests and plains (plains tribes)
| Tribe | Area |
|---|---|
| **Chenchu** | **Nallamala** forests around Srisailam, Ahobilam – the "**aboriginal tribe**" of AP; name = "those who live under trees"; Ahobilam **Narasimha** married **Chenchita** |
| **Yanadi** | Nellore plains, Chittoor; original home near **Pulicat Lake** |
| **Yerukula** | Almost every plains district (**Korava** in TN) |
| **Sugali** (Lambadi, Banjara) | Rayalaseema and central Andhra; settlements = **thandas** |
- **Boya** is a BC community in AP, **not** a plains ST.
- **Not AP tribes:** **Munda, Santhal, Bhil, Asur** (Jharkhand/central India).

=== PVTGs of Andhra Pradesh
- **12 PVTGs** (list for AP and Telangana together): Bodo Gadaba, Gutob Gadaba, Bondo Poraja, Khond Poraja, Parengi Poraja, **Chenchu**, Dongria Khond, Kutia Khond, **Kolam**, **Konda Reddi**, Konda Savara, **Thoti**. About 7 lie in residual AP.
- Criteria: **pre-agricultural technology**, **extremely low literacy**, economic backwardness, **stagnant or declining population** – not caste or sex ratio.
- **Dhebar Commission 1973** → PTG; renamed **PVTG in 2006**. India **75** PVTGs (18 States + 1 UT); **Odisha 13** (most).
- Konda Reddis and Kolams are the "tribes on the verge of extinction" of the TSPSC 2017 question; **Chenchus** work as forest watchers in the **Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam Tiger Reserve**.
- **Yerukula is NOT a PVTG**.
| Cluster | PVTGs | Habitat |
|---|---|---|
| Gadaba / Poraja | Bodo, Gutob Gadaba; Bondo, Khond, Parengi Poraja | Araku Valley / Visakhapatnam agency |
| Khond | Dongria, Kutia Khond | North coastal Khond belt |
| Hill / forest | Chenchu, Konda Reddi, Konda Savara | Nallamala; Godavari hills; Srikakulam hills |
- 2024 ST Order amendment added 7 PVTG communities to ST lists (4 Odisha, 3 AP); total stays 75.

=== SC and ST Population – Census 2011
| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| ST population | **27.39 lakh = 5.53%** of residual AP (4.39% in 1971; undivided AP 7.0%) |
| SC population | 84.69 lakh = **17.08%** |
| Highest ST population | **Visakhapatnam** (6.18 lakh; now mostly ASR) |
| Highest SC share | **Prakasam (23.19%)**, then SPS Nellore (22.49%) |
| Lowest ST share, 26-district map | **West Godavari** (HQ Bhimavaram) – agency mandals went to Eluru |
- SC literacy, undivided AP 2001: **53.5%**.
- India 2011: **698** STs counted; largest tribe **Bhil**; **Odisha** most notified tribes (62).
- **Art. 342:** **President** notifies STs after consulting the Governor; only **Parliament** amends the list. **Art. 366(25)** defines STs.
- **1976 SC and ST Orders (Amendment) Act:** **33 STs** in undivided AP; 5 area-restricted (**Goudu, Mali, Nayak, Thoti, Valmiki**); **Goudu, Nayak, Valmiki** only in the **Agency tracts**. Present AP: **34** ST communities.
- Feb 2024: Porja and Savara sub-groups added (Bondo Porja, Khond Porja, Konda Savara).
- **Polavaram district** (HQ **Rampachodavaram**) carved from ASR on **31 Dec 2025**.

=== Livelihoods, GCC and Araku Coffee
| Tribe | Occupation |
|---|---|
| Chenchu | Hunting, food gathering; semi-nomadic |
| Konda Reddi, Koya, Porja, Savara | **Podu** and hill agriculture |
| Yanadi | Fishing, rice, herbal medicine |
| Yerukula | **Basket and rope making, fortune-telling**, pig-rearing |
| Lambada | Once traders/transporters; now farming |
- **Podu** = slash-and-burn; erosion and forest fires; in **ASR and Parvathipuram Manyam** (not Nallamala).
- **GCC** (Girijan Cooperative Corporation, **1956**): buys **MFP**, runs PDS depots; trademark "**GCC Araku Valley Coffee**" – the coffee brand of Visakhapatnam's tribals.
- Coffee grows in the **Araku Valley** (Paderu ITDA); the ITDA Paderu coffee project targeted **expansion by 1,00,000 acres** – Araku coffee now about **1 lakh acres**.
- Van Dhan Vikas Kendras 415 sanctioned (258 working); annatto at Maredumilli.

=== Laws, Institutions and Movements
| Year | Law / event |
|---|---|
| 1956 | GCC |
| 1959 / **1970** | **1/70 Regulation** – no land transfer from tribals to non-tribals or between non-tribals in Scheduled Areas |
| 1997 | **Samatha** – no leasing of Scheduled Area land to non-tribals or private miners |
| 2019 | Central Universities (Amendment) Act – **CTUAP** (13th Schedule of AP Reorganisation Act), campus **Kondakarakam, Vizianagaram** |
| 2020 | SC struck down **G.O. Ms. 3** (100% ST teacher quota) |
- **Tribal Freedom Fighters Museum** at **Tajangi, Chintapalli** (near Lambasingi); the earlier "Alluri Sitarama Raju memorial tribal museum" was planned at **Visakhapatnam** (Timmapuram).
- **Bauxite:** Araku, Paderu, Chintapalli agency. **Polavaram:** about **276 villages** submerged; opposed by **Koya and Kondh**. Projects clash with **1/70 Regulation, PESA, FRA**.
