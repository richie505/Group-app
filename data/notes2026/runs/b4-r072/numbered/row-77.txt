## SUB 5 | Migration and Urban Problems
[5:0] - About **39.55 lakh** people of AP live away from their native places; about half seasonal.
[5:1] - **Gulf migration:** **East Godavari (Konaseema)**, West Godavari, Kadapa; supported by **APNRTS**. Rayalaseema migrants also go to Bengaluru and Hyderabad; **Uttarandhra** is an out-migration region.
[5:2] - **Slums:** undivided AP about **1.02 crore** slum dwellers (2011), **second after Maharashtra**.
[5:3] - **Urban flooding:** Vijayawada – **Budameru** rivulet; flash floods from **lost wetlands, blocked drains**. AI flood forecasting: RTGS + APSDMA + **IIT Gandhinagar**.
