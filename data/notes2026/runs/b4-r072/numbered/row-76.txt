## SUB 0 | Official Languages of AP and Urdu
[0:0] - First official language: **Telugu** (**AP Official Languages Act, 1966**).
[0:1] - **Urdu** = **second official language**: in joint AP it applied to **15 districts** – six now in AP (**Anantapur, Kadapa, Chittoor, Guntur, Nellore, Kurnool**) and nine in Telangana.
[0:2] - **AP Official Languages (Amendment) Bill, 2022:** tabled and passed in the Assembly on **23–24 March 2022**; extends Urdu to **all districts**; the government order bringing it into force came in **June 2022** – so "in force on 25 April 2022" is wrong.
[0:3] - So the official languages of AP = **Telugu and Urdu**; **Urdu** is also the **second most widely spoken** language of AP (about 3.7% in 2011).
[0:4] - **Dr. Abdul Haq Urdu University:** **Kurnool**; AP State Act of **2016**.
[0:5] > Exam angle: Urdu is second official language in **all** districts since 2022, not only Kurnool or Guntur.
[0:6] ~~ Sources: web search summaries (Hans India, Deccan Chronicle 24 Mar 2022 and 17 Jun 2022; legitquest text of the 1966 Act proviso; Siasat on the 15 districts); app notes 4:76 (sub 1)
