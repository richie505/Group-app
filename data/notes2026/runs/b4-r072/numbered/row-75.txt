## SUB 1 | PVTGs of Andhra Pradesh
[1:0] - **12 PVTGs** (list for AP and Telangana together): Bodo Gadaba, Gutob Gadaba, Bondo Poraja, Khond Poraja, Parengi Poraja, **Chenchu**, Dongria Khond, Kutia Khond, **Kolam**, **Konda Reddi**, Konda Savara, **Thoti**. About 7 lie in residual AP.
[1:1] - Criteria: **pre-agricultural technology**, **extremely low literacy**, economic backwardness, **stagnant or declining population** – not caste or sex ratio.
[1:2] - **Dhebar Commission 1973** → PTG; renamed **PVTG in 2006**. India **75** PVTGs (18 States + 1 UT); **Odisha 13** (most).
[1:3] - Konda Reddis and Kolams are the "tribes on the verge of extinction" of the TSPSC 2017 question; **Chenchus** work as forest watchers in the **Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam Tiger Reserve**.
[1:4] - **Yerukula is NOT a PVTG**.

  [1:5.2] Hill / forest | Chenchu, Konda Reddi, Konda Savara | Nallamala; Godavari hills; Srikakulam hills
[1:6] - 2024 ST Order amendment added 7 PVTG communities to ST lists (4 Odisha, 3 AP); total stays 75.
