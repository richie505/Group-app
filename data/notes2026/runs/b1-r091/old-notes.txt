=== NOTES 1:91 | Yadavas of Devagiri – Bhillama, Singhana, Hemadri and the Hemadpanti Style
## SUB 0 | The Dynasty and Capital
[91:0:0] - Also called the **Seuna Yadavas**; first **feudatories of the Western (Kalyani) Chalukyas**.
[91:0:1] - Ruled western India and the Deccan, about **1187–1317 AD**.
[91:0:2] - Mother tongue **Marathi**; Sanskrit = language of scholarship; the Yadavas **encouraged Marathi**.
[91:0:3] - Capital **Devagiri**, founded by **Bhillama V** (c. **1187**) – not by Shankaradeva.
[91:0:4] - Later name: **Daulatabad**, given by **Muhammad bin Tughlaq** (1327) when he shifted his capital there.
## SUB 1 | Rulers and Achievements
[91:1:0] TABLE head: Ruler | Achievement
  [91:1:0.0] Bhillama V | Threw off Kalyani Chalukya rule; founded Devagiri; beaten by Hoysala Veera Ballala II
  [91:1:0.1] Jaitugi (Jaitrapala) | Killed Kakatiya Rudradeva; held Ganapatideva captive, released him (1199), married his daughter Somaladevi to him
  [91:1:0.2] Singhana II (c. 1210–1246) | Greatest Yadava; title Teluguraya Sthapanacharya (installer of the Telugu king)
  [91:1:0.3] Krishna (1246–1261) | Fought the Hoysalas
  [91:1:0.4] Mahadeva (1261–1271) | Defeated by the Hoysalas; attack on Kakatiyas repulsed by Rudramadevi
  [91:1:0.5] Ramachandra (1271–1311) | Ruler at Alauddin Khalji's invasion; became a feudatory
  [91:1:0.6] Shankaradeva (1311–1313) | Died in Malik Kafur's invasion
  [91:1:0.7] Harapaladeva (1313–1317) | Last ruler; killed in Mubarak Khalji's invasion
[91:1:1] Bhillama V = founder; Singhana II = greatest; Harapaladeva = last.
## SUB 2 | Singhana II and the Kakatiyas (ap Angle)
[91:2:0] - Defeated the **Hoysalas**.
[91:2:1] - Released Ganapatideva as a counterweight to Veera Ballala's push into the **Krishna–Tungabhadra** region.
[91:2:2] - His son **Sarangapanideva** later served at **Rudramadevi's** court.
[91:2:3] - Court scholars: **Sarngadeva** (music) and astronomer **Changadeva**.
[91:2:4] - **Paithan copper plate** of Ramachandra confirms Jaitugi killed Rudradeva and held Ganapatideva.
[91:2:5] - **Bidar fort inscription** records Rudramadevi's victory over Mahadeva.
## SUB 3 | Scholars and Works
[91:3:0] TABLE head: Person | Role | Work
  [91:3:0.0] Sarngadeva | Court musician of Singhana II | Sangita Ratnakara – base text of both Hindustani and Carnatic music
  [91:3:0.1] Changadeva | Grandson of Bhaskaracharya | School at Patan to teach Bhaskara's works
  [91:3:0.2] Hemadri | Minister and scholar | Chaturvarga Chintamani (Sanskrit encyclopaedia)
  [91:3:0.3] Dnyaneshwar | Bhakti saint | Dnyaneshwari – Marathi commentary on the Gita, 1290, Nevasa, Ramachandra's reign
  [91:3:0.4] Chakradhar Swami | Founder | Mahanubhava sect
  [91:3:0.5] Mhaimbhat | Mahanubhava author | Lilacharitra = first Marathi prose
[91:3:1] - Later Bhakti saints Namdev, Tukaram, Ramdas carried Upanishad and Gita ideas into Marathi.
[91:3:2] Mahanubhava sect = Chakradhar Swami, not Dnyaneshwar.
## SUB 4 | Hemadri and the Hemadpanti Style
[91:4:0] - Office: **Shrikaranadhipa** (head of secretariat/records) under **Mahadeva and Ramachandra**.
[91:4:1] - Chaturvarga Chintamani covers dharma, rituals, charity, pilgrimage, vratas (APPSC G2 2000).
[91:4:2] - Claimed Jaitugi captured and sacrificed Kakatiya Rudra.
[91:4:3] - **Hemadpanti style**: local **black basalt**; dressed stones **interlocked without mortar** (**dry masonry**).
## SUB 5 | Fall to Delhi
[91:5:0] TABLE head: Year | Event
  [91:5:0.0] 1296 | First Muslim invasion of the South: Alauddin Khalji defeats Ramachandra; Yadavas become vassals; promised Ellichpur revenue yearly but did not send it
  [91:5:0.1] 1307–08 | Malik Kafur: Ramachandra submits, taken to Delhi
  [91:5:0.2] 1313 | Shankaradeva dies in Kafur's invasion
  [91:5:0.3] 1317–18 | Harapaladeva killed by Mubarak Khalji; kingdom ends
  [91:5:0.4] After 1323 | Malik Burhanuddin of Devagiri given Andhra; Devagiri = Sultanate's Deccan base
[91:5:1] - Devagiri = **first southern kingdom to fall** to Delhi; opened the road to Warangal, Dwarasamudra, Madurai.
[91:5:2] - **1296 raid:** Alauddin Khalji led it while he was governor of Kara under his uncle, Sultan **Jalaluddin Khalji**, before he became Sultan himself.

=== NOTES 1:92 | Kakatiyas – Origins, Beta I, Rudradeva, Ganapatideva; Kakatiya–yadava–hoysala Relations
## SUB 0 | Origin, Overlords and Religion
[92:0:0] - **Second dynasty after the Satavahanas** to unify all the Telugu-speaking regions.
[92:0:1] - Ruled the **eastern Deccan** (12th–14th c.) from **Warangal**; Seunas held the western Deccan, Eastern Gangas held Odisha.
[92:0:2] - **First mention:** **Magallu grant (956)** of Eastern Chalukya **Danarnava**, through **Kakartya Gundana (Gundyana)**.
[92:0:3] # Progenitor, Overlords and Phases
[92:0:4] TABLE head: Point | Fact
  [92:0:4.0] Progenitor | Kakara Gundana (Magallu grant) or Venna Bhupati (Bayyaram inscription, which traces the line from Durjaya)
  [92:0:4.1] Overlords | Rashtrakutas first (APPSC G2 2005) → then Kalyani Chalukyas
  [92:0:4.2] Phases | Samanta (feudatory) 956–1162 → sovereign 1163–1323
  [92:0:4.3] Early base | Hanamkonda Vishaya (modern Karimnagar, Warangal, Khammam)
  [92:0:4.4] Feudatory title | Mahamandaleswara (under the Kalyani Chalukyas)
  [92:0:4.5] Emblem | Garuda (Rashtrakuta phase) → Varaha (Kalyani Chalukya phase), changed by Prola I
[92:0:5] Overlord question: answer Rashtrakutas. If Rashtrakutas is not an option, choose Eastern Chalukyas (the Magallu grant is an Eastern Chalukya record).
[92:0:6] # Religion and Clan Deity
[92:0:7] - Early Kakatiyas = **Jains** (APPSC G2 2011) → later **Shaivas**.
[92:0:8] - **Garuda emblem:** taken from Jain **Tirthankara Shantinatha** (Garuda = his attendant yaksha); later **Varaha**; not Neminatha or Parshvanatha.
[92:0:9] - Clan deity **Kakatamma (Kakati Devi)** = the Jain **Kushmandini** absorbed into Hinduism; **"Kakati" = gourd**.
[92:0:10] - **Beta II** was initiated into Saivism by **Rameswara Pandita** of the **Mallikarjuna matha, Srisailam** (APPSC 2025); gave him **Vaijanapalli**.
[92:0:11] - **Mahadeva's** guru: **Dhruveshwara Pandita**.
[92:0:12] - **Ganapatideva's** Raja Guru: **Visvesvara Sivacharya**, head of the **Golaki Matha**.
[92:0:13] Saiva gurus: Rameswara Pandita → Beta II; Dhruveshwara Pandita → Mahadeva; Visvesvara Sivacharya → Ganapatideva.

[93:0:0] - **Kakatiya** dynasty; daughter of **Ganapatideva** and **Kupama Devi**; Ganapati had **no sons**.

[93:4:3] # Nayankara System
[93:4:4] - Kingdom divided into **77 Nayankaras** held by military officers (**Nayakas**).
[93:4:5] - Each Nayaka held a **fort**, kept **troops** and paid a **fixed tribute**; about **one-fourth** of the land was under Nayakas.
[93:4:6] - Continued as Vijayanagara's **Amara Nayankara** → later **Poligars**.

## SUB 6 | Fall of Warangal (1323)
[93:6:0] - Captor: **Ulugh Khan** (Juna Khan, later **Muhammad bin Tughlaq**) (APPSC G2 2019) – not Kafur, not Alauddin.
[93:6:1] - Prataparudra **drowned himself in the Narmada** on the way to Delhi (**Vilasa copper plate** of **Musunuri Prolaya Nayaka**; one account says the Kaveri).
[93:6:2] - Orugallu renamed **Sultanpur**; Andhra placed under **Malik Burhanuddin** (vizier of Devagiri); **Malik Maqbul** (converted Brahmin Jagannatha Pandit) at Warangal.
[93:6:3] - **Causes:** southern disunity, weak cavalry, betrayal by chiefs, exhaustion from raids – **not** a European sea blockade.
[93:6:4] - Survivors **Prolaya Nayaka, Racherla Singama, Prolaya Vema** led the liberation → **Musunuri** and **Reddi** states.

=== NOTES 1:94 | Kakatiya Administration – Nayankara System, Tank Irrigation, Trade, Motupalli Abhaya Sasanam
## SUB 0 | Nature of the Kakatiya State
[94:0:0] - Most scholars: a **"decentralised monarchy"**.
[94:0:1] - **Romila Thapar**: **"metropolitan state"**; **Burton Stein**: compared it to a **"segmentary state"**.
[94:0:2] - **Cynthia Talbot**: the Kakatiya age = **"Golden Age of the Shudras"**.
[94:0:3] - Rulers were **non-Brahmins (Shudras)**; religion and politics became **non-Brahmin** (APPSC 2019); officials came from warrior and farmer groups.
[94:0:4] - Prime ministers: **Malyala Hemadri Reddi** (Ganapatideva); **Muppidi Nayudu** (Prataparudra).

## SUB 2 | Ayagar System and Society
[94:2:0] - **Ayagars (Ayagarlu)** = **twelve (12)** village functionaries (APPSC G2 2012).
[94:2:1] - Paid by **shares of produce + service lands**; held land **without any cess or tax**.
[94:2:2] - **Samayas** = caste/occupational groups → **caste mobility**; the Kakatiyas themselves were Shudras.
[94:2:3] - **Women donors:** **Mailamba** (Bayyaram tank) and **Kundamamba**; woman painter **Machaladevi**; **Surambika** (Santana Sagaram, Reddi period).
[94:2:4] 12 Ayagars (village) vs 72 Niyogas (centre) vs 77 Nayankaras.
## SUB 3 | Nayankara System
[94:3:0] - Military commanders (**Nayakas**) made administrators of **Sthalas/Mandalams**; each protected a **fort**.
[94:3:1] - **Introduced/reorganised by Prataparudra II** (APPSC 2025); some say Rudrama began it in Ganapatideva's time and Prataparudra strengthened it.
[94:3:2] - Number: **77 Nayankaras**.
[94:3:3] - **Land instead of salary:** villages called **Nayakasthalam**; Nayankaras controlled **one-fourth** of the land.
[94:3:4] - Obligations: collect revenue → **keep a fixed army for the king** → **pay a fixed annual sum**, remit the rest.
[94:3:5] - Sources: **Baddena's Neeti Sastra Muktavali**; **Ekamranatha's Pratapa Charitra**.
[94:3:6] - **Sakala Neeti Sammatamu:** polity anthology compiled by **Madiki Singana** (15th c.); a source on Kakatiya administration.
[94:3:7] TABLE head: State | Arrangement
  [94:3:7.0] Kakatiya | Nayankara
  [94:3:7.1] Vijayanagara | Amara Nayankara – land Amaram, tribute Kappam / Kist
  [94:3:7.2] Mughal | Jagir
[94:3:8] - Break-up of the Amara-Nayankara system produced the **Poligars**.
## SUB 4 | Military Organisation
[94:4:0] - **Chaturanga bala** = four arms.
[94:4:1] - **Mula Balam** = king's own standing army; **Nayankara army** = Nayakas' contingents.
[94:4:2] - Prataparudra's army: **100 elephants, 20,000 cavalry, 900,000 infantry**.
[94:4:3] TABLE head: Post | Holder
  [94:4:3.0] Gaja Sahini (elephants) | Jayapa Senani (Ganapatideva); Bendapudi Annayya, Induluri Annayya (Prataparudra II)
  [94:4:3.1] Ashwa Sahini (cavalry) | Gangaya Sahini (Ganapatideva); Maraya (Prataparudra II)
  [94:4:3.2] Commanders | Padmanayakas – Recherla Velama chiefs; later founded the Velama kingdom
[94:4:4] - Forts: **Giridurga** (hill), **Jaladurga** (water), **Vanadurga** (forest), **Sthaladurga** (plain).
[94:4:5] - **Warangal fort**: **concentric walls and a moat**; the inner stone wall has **45 bastions**.
[94:4:6] - Of **75 generals** in 1323 only 3 survived: **Prolaya Nayaka, Racherla Singama, Prolaya Vemareddy**.
## SUB 5 | Land Revenue and Taxes
[94:5:0] - **Ari / bhaga** (Bhumi sistu) = land revenue, generally **one-sixth** of produce.
[94:5:1] - **Kolakandru** = revenue **collectors**; **Arigapulu** = taxpayers.
[94:5:2] - Land classes: **Magani** = irrigated **wet** land (food crops); **Metta / Velipolam** = rain-fed **dry** land.
[94:5:3] - **Exempt:** temple lands, Brahmin grants, Ayagar lands, **Dashabandhamu** (tank upkeep) lands.

## SUB 6 | Agriculture
[94:6:0] - Crops: **rice** (fragrant variety near Orugallu), wheat, sorghum, **sugarcane**, oilseeds, sesame, **cotton**, ginger, horse gram, **betel**.

[94:6:2] - Forest land cleared with **tax concessions**; new villages Ganapavaram, Ghanpur, Mahadevapur, Rudravaram, Bayyaram, Muppavaram.
