=== Motupalli Port and the Abhaya Sasanam
- **Chief port:** **Motupalli** – the main Kakatiya seaport for external trade (APPSC G2 2000, 2011, 2012) – not Warangal (inland capital), Krishnapatnam, Pulicat, Nellore, Machilipatnam, Ghantasala, Dharanikota or Korangi.
- **Other name:** **Desiyakonda (Deseeyakkonda / Desyakkonda) Pattanam** (APPSC G2 2019 ×2; G2 2012; APPSC PYQ) – not Nidadavolu, Vadarevu, Motukur, Mudigonda, Mukalapura or Malakapuram.
# Abhaya Sasanam (Motupalli Charter)
- **Issuer:** **Ganapatideva**, **1244** CE, a pillar inscription at Motupalli (APPSC G2 2019; Group-II-A 1984; Welfare Officers 2017 ×2; APPSC PYQ ×3) – not Rudramba, Ambadeva or Prataparudra.
- **Content:** **protection and shelter to foreign (sea) merchants**; unfair taxes cancelled, only fixed duties; **compensation for shipwreck or theft**; no harassment by officials.
- **Official posted:** **Siddayya Devudu**.
- **Most important commercial document** of the period.
- **Goods at Motupalli:** **sandal, camphor, rose-water, ivory**, pearls, corals, copper, silk, pepper.
- **Marco Polo** visited Motupalli in **Rudramadevi's** reign.
| Port | Note |
|---|---|
| **Motupalli** | Chief port; Desiyakonda Pattanam |
| Krishnapatnam | Nellore coast |
| **Hamsaladeevi** | Krishna delta |
- **Kara Pattanam** = ports collecting import and export duties.
- **⚠ Trap:** the **Motupalli Dharma Sasanam (1416)** is of Vijayanagara **Deva Raya I**, not Ganapatideva.

=== Revenue, Village Officers and Coins
# Land revenue
- **Ari (Aari):** the Kakatiya **land tax / land revenue** (Juvenile Welfare 2011; APPSC PYQ) – generally **one-sixth** of the produce; taxpayers = **Arigapulu**; collectors = **Kolakandru**.
- **Land classes:** **Magani** = irrigated wet land (food crops); **Metta** = rain-fed dry land.
- **Tax-free:** temple lands, Brahmin grants, Ayagar lands, **Dashabandhamu** (land for tank upkeep).
| Tax | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Koru | Cess on wet land |
| **Putti Wahindi** | Cess on dry land |
| **Pullari** | Tax on grass (grazing) |
| **Dari Sunkam** | Road toll |
| Angadi Sunkam | Weekly-market tax |
| Kanika | Customary gift to the king or officials |
| **Pellipannu** | Wedding tax |
- **Not Kakatiya:** **Meruka** = Reddi wet-land tax; **Moturpha** = British tax on trades and looms; Vijayanagara **Krishnadevaraya** withdrew the marriage tax.
# Village officers
- **Ayagars:** **twelve (12)** village servants (APPSC PYQ) – Karnam, Reddi, Talari, purohita, smiths; paid by **shares of produce and service lands**, held land **without any cess or tax**.
- **Karanam** = village accountant; **Reddi** = headman; **Talari** = village watchman.

| Nishka | Gold |
| **Ruka** | **Silver** |

=== Guilds, Samayas and Trade Routes
# Samayas and guilds
- **Samaya (Samayamu):** a **caste organisation** / caste-occupational association – Mahajanulu (Brahmins), Nakaramu (Vaishyas); each elected a head; meeting in temple mandapams.
- **Chitrameli:** the samaya (shreni) of **agricultural farmers** (APPSC G2 Mains; APPSC PYQ) – not goldsmiths, painters or dancers.
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Nakaram | Domestic merchants |
| **Nanadesi** | Foreign (inter-regional) merchants |
| **Ubhaya Nanadesi** | Merchants of both the Desis, Paradesis and Nanadesis |
| **Ayyavole 500** | Guild named after **Aihole** |
| **Virabanajam** | Licensed for **Pakanadu, Vengi and Hanumakonda** (Tripurantakam inscription, 1282) |
- **Guild head:** **Setti / Sreshthi**; guilds had seals, emblems and militia and acted as bankers – but **no royal monopoly** over trade.
# Routes (APPSC G1 2023 ×2)
- **Kridabhiramamu routes:** **Motupalli–Tripurantakam–Ballari** and **Palnadu–Nalgonda–Nekarikallu–Orugallu** → answer **III and IV**; **Orugallu** was the inland trade hub.
- **Tripurantakam** linked Motupalli and the salt towns to **Ballari** via Kurnool; also: **Pedaganjam and Chinaganjam** (Prakasam coast) were known for **salt** production (APPSC G2 Mains 2019, 2025; APPSC PYQ ×5) – not textiles, indigo or sugar.
- **Ketana:** chief inland route Kanchi → Nellore → Warangal → **Ayodhya**.
- Kakatiyas controlled routes between the **Deccan interior and the eastern coast**.
- **Kridabhiramamu:** Telugu play by **Vinukonda Vallabharaya** describing life in **Orugallu (Warangal)**.
- **Imports:** **Arabian war horses**, pearls, silk, perfumes; **exports:** fine textiles, spices, diamonds, gold.

=== Nayankara System and Administration
# Nayankara system
- **Introduced by:** **Prataparudra II** (Asst. Welfare Officers PYQ; APPSC 2025), who organised the kingdom into **77 Nayankaras** – ⚠ one book key and a NextGen Level-II line say Rudrama.
- **How:** military commanders (**Nayakas**) were made administrators of **Sthalas / Mandalams** and given charge of **forts** – the Kakatiya empire was ruled through vassals called **Nayakas** (2002 paper).
- **Land instead of salary:** villages called **Nayakasthalam**; Nayankaras held about **one-fourth** of the land; no monthly cash salary.
- **Duties:** collect revenue → keep a fixed army for the king → pay a fixed annual sum.
- **Sources:** **Ekamranatha's Pratapa Charitra**; Baddena's Niti Sastra Muktavali.
- **Successor systems:** Vijayanagara **Amaranayaka** (land = **Amaram**) → **Poligars**.
# Administration
- **Nature of the state:** most scholars – **decentralised monarchy**; **Romila Thapar** – "metropolitan state"; **Burton Stein** – "segmentary state"; **Cynthia Talbot** – "Golden Age of the Shudras".
- **Prime ministers:** **Malyala Hemadri Reddi** (Ganapatideva); **Muppidi Nayudu** (Prataparudra II).
- **Units:** **Rashtra / Mandala** (largest) → Nadu → Sthala → Grama.
- **72 Niyogas** (departments) under the **Bahattara Niyogadhipati**.
- **Tirthulu / Ashtadasa Tirthas:** the **18 high officers (ministers) of state** (Sakala Neeti Sammatamu of **Madiki Singana**) – ministers, not water sources, temples or spies (APPSC PYQ).
| Officer | Role |
|---|---|
| **Pradvivaka** | Judicial adviser |
| **Kayasthas** | Scribes |
| **Sunkadhikari** | Collector of tolls and customs |
# Army
- **Mula Balam** = king's own standing army; **Gaja Sahini** (elephants) = **Jayapa Senani** under Ganapatideva; **Ashwa Sahini** (cavalry) = **Gangaya Sahini**.
- **Giridurga** = hill fort (also Jala, Vana, Sthala durgas); **Padmanayakas** = Recherla Velama chiefs, key commanders.
- Warangal fort: inner stone wall with **45 bastions**.

=== Tanks, Agriculture and Crafts
| Tank | Builder |
|---|---|
| **Pakhala** | **Mummadi Nayaka** (not Mailamba) |
| **Laknavaram, Ghanapuram** | **Ganapatideva** |
| Bayyaram | Mailamba |
| Santana Sagaram / **Govardhana Samudram** | **Surambika** (Reddi period) |
- **Golusukattu cheruvulu** = linked **chain tanks**; **Dashabandhamu** = tax-free land for tank upkeep.
- **Sapta Santanalu:** tank-building was one of the seven **meritorious acts**.
- **Porumamilla inscription (1369):** **Bhaskara, son of Bukka I** (Vijayanagara) – 12 requisites of a good tank.
- **Crops:** fragrant rice near Orugallu, sugarcane, cotton; **maize was not grown**.
| Product | Centre |
|---|---|
| **Ratna kambalu** (diamond blankets), carpets | **Warangal** |
| **Knives** | **Nirmal** |
| **Iron** | **Guthikonda and Palnadu** |
- **Palnati Veeracharitra:** about **1,500 painters' houses** in Orugallu; **Machaladevi** was a woman painter.
