[36:1:14] - **18% slab** gives about **70%** of GST revenue – kept unchanged.
[36:1:15] - More affected: manufacturing/service States (**Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu**; the 2018 rate cut caused a **3–4% monthly dip**). Less affected: agrarian States (**Bihar, UP, North-East**).
[36:1:16] - Old compensation: **5 years from July 2017** through the compensation cess – **ended June 2022**.
[36:1:17] - Maximum Indian exports (2018) went to the **European Union**.
