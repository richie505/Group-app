=== Karst Landforms – Groundwater Work
- **Karst**: landscape made by **groundwater dissolving limestone/dolomite** (carbonation); named after the Karst region of **Slovenia**. Sinkholes form in **limestone topography**, not sandstone, polar or desert areas.
| Landform | Key feature |
|---|---|
| Lapiaz / karren | Grooved, ridged limestone surface |
| **Sinkhole (doline)** | Closed funnel depression – the **commonest karst landform** |
| Uvala | Joined (coalesced) dolines |
| Polje | Large enclosed karst depression |
| Swallow hole | Point where a surface stream disappears underground |
| **Blind valley** | Valley that **ends abruptly** where its stream sinks (steephead valley) – a **karst** feature |
| **Stalactite** | Calcium-carbonate deposit **hanging from the cave roof** |
| **Stalagmite** | Deposit **rising from the cave floor** |
| Pillar (column) | Stalactite and stalagmite joined |
- Rising size: **sinkhole → doline → uvala → polje**.
- Stalactites, stalagmites and pillars are **depositional landforms of underground (ground) water** – water dissolves CaCO₃ and re-deposits it as it drips; not wind, glacier or river features.
- Pot hole = river bed; blow hole = coast; playa = desert.
- **Springs** are common in **well-jointed rocks**, **karst (limestone) regions** (resurgences / vauclusian springs) and **tilted strata** where permeable and impermeable beds alternate – **not** in arid areas (deep water table).

=== Caves of Andhra Pradesh and India
| Cave | Place | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Belum** | Nandyal district (earlier Kurnool) | **Karst**, black limestone, stalactites and stalagmites; **2nd-longest natural cave in India** (~3.2 km); found by **Robert Bruce Foote (1884)**; GSI Geo-Heritage Site (Jun 2025) |
| **Borra** | **Ananthagiri hills**, Araku (Alluri Sitharama Raju district) | **Limestone karst**; formed by the **Gosthani** river flowing through it; found 1807 |
| Undavalli | Near Vijayawada (Guntur side of Krishna) | Rock-cut sandstone cave temple, Vishnukundins; reclining Vishnu – **not karst** |
| **Bojjannakonda** | Sankaram, Anakapalli | **Buddhist rock-cut caves, stupas and monastic remains** – not karst |
| Erravaram | Left bank of the **Yeleru**, near Rajahmundry | Early historic Buddhist site – not karst |
| **Arwah** | Near **Cherrapunji (Sohra), Meghalaya** | Limestone formations and **fossils** |
| Krem Liat Prah | Meghalaya | **India's longest** natural cave; world's longest = Mammoth Cave (USA) |
- **APPSC G1:** karst topography in AP = **Belum** (not Undavalli, Bojjannakonda, Erravaram).
- **Billa Surgam**: Betamcherla, **Kurnool/Nandyal** – **not Anantapuramu**; Old Stone Age cave with animal remains. Kurnool caves and **Muchchatla Chintamanu Gavi** gave **Upper Palaeolithic bone tools** (APPSC G2 Mains 2025: only statement a is incorrect).
- **Kethavaram** rock paintings (Kurnool): deer the most painted animal. **Gandikota** (YSR Kadapa): Penna gorge.

=== Coastal Landforms
- **Main agent of coastal erosion: waves** (also tides, currents, tsunamis, humans). Processes: hydraulic action, abrasion, attrition, solution (**saltation is wind**).
| Type | Landform | Formation |
|---|---|---|
| Erosional | Sea cliff, **notch** | Wave erosion; notch cut at the cliff base |
| Erosional | Wave-cut platform | Cliff retreat |
| Erosional | Sea cave → **arch** → **stack** → stump | Weak zones eroded; cave cuts a headland; **arch collapses → stack** |
| Depositional | Beach | Sand/shingle between tides |
| Depositional | **Spit** / hook | Ridge joined to land at one end (longshore drift); a hooked spit = hook |
| Depositional | **Bar** | Ridge across a bay; closes it into a lagoon |
| Depositional | **Tombolo** | Bar joining an island to the mainland |
| Depositional | Lagoon | Shallow water cut off by a bar (Chilika, Pulicat) |
- **NDA 2018:** stack is **not** depositional; notch, arch, cliff are erosional, hook is depositional.
- Defences: soft = beach nourishment, dunes, **mangroves**; hard = seawalls, **groynes** (across longshore drift), breakwaters. Dams, harbours and sand mining cut sediment and worsen erosion.
- East coast erodes more: soft low alluvium, no rocky cliffs. AP: severe at **Uppada (Kakinada)** and the Krishna coast (Jul 2026). Pulicat = saltwater; Kolleru = freshwater.
| Coast | Formed by | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Fiord | Drowned glacial valley | Norway |
| Ria | Drowned river valley; ranges **at right angles** to the coast | NW Spain |
| Dalmatian | Ranges **parallel** to the coast; long islands | Adriatic (Croatia) |
- **Emergent coast**: **east coast** (Coromandel) – broad plain, few natural ports. West: **Konkan** submerged by faulting; Kerala emergent.
- Geo-heritage: **Erra Matti Dibbalu** (red dunes, Visakhapatnam) and **Tirumala Silathoranam** (quartzite arch) on the **UNESCO Tentative List (Sep 2025)**.

=== Glacial Landforms
| Landform | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Cirque (corrie)** | Bowl/armchair hollow at the valley head |
| Arête | Knife-edged ridge between two cirques |
| Horn | Sharp pyramidal peak (Matterhorn) |
| U-shaped valley | Glacier deepens and widens a river valley |
| **Hanging valley** | Tributary glacier valley left high above the deepened main valley; joins it by a **waterfall** – agent = **glacier** (Yosemite) |
| Fjord | Drowned glacial U-valley (Norway, Greenland, New Zealand) |
| **Moraine / till / boulder clay** | **Unsorted glacial debris** deposited by ice: lateral, terminal, medial, ground |
| Drumlin | Egg-shaped till hill; a field = "basket of eggs" |
| Esker | Winding ridge of **stratified** meltwater sediment from a sub-glacial tunnel |
- **APPSC G1 2017:** till, moraine and boulder clay = depositional landforms of **glacial activity**.
- **Hanging glaciers** are stranded on steep slopes as the main glacier **thins**; collapse → ice-rock avalanches, GLOFs (Nepal, 1 Sep 2026, app CA). From 2025 **CWC** monitors **2,843** glacial lakes/water bodies (up from 902).

=== Arid (Aeolian) Landforms
- Desert landforms are made **mainly by wind** (deflation, abrasion, deposition) with help from sheet floods. Wind transport: deflation, **saltation**, surface creep.
| Landform | Process | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| **Barchan** | Deposition | **Crescent** dune, horns downwind – e.g. **Thar (Jaisalmer)** |
| Seif | Deposition | Long narrow (longitudinal) dunes where sand is scarce |
| Sand dune | Deposition | Accumulation of wind-blown sand |
| **Yardang** | **Erosion** | Streamlined ridge parallel to the wind |
| Zeugen | Erosion | Table-like ridge, hard cap over soft rock |
| Mushroom rock | Erosion (abrasion) | Base abraded more than top |
| **Ventifact** | Erosion | Wind-faceted pebble |
| Deflation hollow | Erosion | Wind removal |
| **Inselberg** | Erosion (residual) | Isolated residual hill (Uluru) |
| Playa | Evaporation | Dry salt-lake bed |
| Loess | Deposition | Fine wind-blown silt (Loess Plateau, China) |
| Mesa / butte | Erosion | Flat-topped table hill / smaller remnant |
- **Barchans are depositional**, so they are "not an erosional landform of wind"; yardang, inselberg and ventifact are erosional.

=== Landform → Agent (Overview)
| Agent | Landforms |
|---|---|
| Running water | **Delta**, V-valley, gorge, canyon, meander, alluvial fan, levee, peneplain |
| Glaciers | **Cirque**, arête, horn, U-valley, hanging valley, moraine, till, esker; fjord (with drowning) |
| Wind | **Sand dunes**, yardangs, mushroom rocks, loess |
| Groundwater (karst) | Sinkholes, doline, stalactites, stalagmites, blind valley |
| Waves and currents | **Cliffs**, sea caves, arches, stacks; bars and spits |
| Tectonic faulting | Rift valleys (Death Valley, Narmada), horsts, grabens |
- **APPSC G2 2024 match:** Delta – running water; Cirque – glaciers; Cliffs – waves and currents; Sand dunes – winds (a-III, b-IV, c-I, d-II).
- Mountains: folding, faulting, volcanism, erosion; young fold mountains = **Alps, Andes, Rockies, Himalayas**; old = Appalachians, Urals, Pennines, Aravallis. Valleys: river, glacier, tectonic, karst.
