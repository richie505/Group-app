=== Deltas and Estuaries
| Basis | Delta | Estuary |
|---|---|---|
| Process | **Deposition** dominates | Erosion / **tidal flushing** dominates |
| Mouth | River splits into many distributaries | Single **funnel-shaped** (drowned) mouth with free connection to the sea |
| Example | Ganga-Brahmaputra, Godavari, Krishna, Mahanadi | **Narmada, Tapi (Tapti)**, Western Ghats rivers; world's largest = St. Lawrence |
- **Conditions for a delta:** heavy sediment load, slow flow, shallow sea, **weak tides and currents** (strong tides remove sediment).
- **West-flowing Deccan rivers** form **estuaries, not deltas**: **steep gradient** (fast flow), hard rock, short courses, little silt; the Narmada and Tapi also flow through rift valleys.
- An estuary is an **ecotone** (river–sea transition), among the most productive habitats.
# Largest delta
- **World's largest (greatest) delta:** **Ganga-Brahmaputra(-Meghna) = Sundarbans / Bengal delta** (~1 lakh sq km), **arcuate**; about **two-thirds in Bangladesh**; the **world's largest mangrove forest**; also the largest delta of the Indian subcontinent.
- In Bangladesh the **Ganga = Padma**; the **Brahmaputra = Jamuna** (so "Ganga is called Jamuna in Bangladesh" is false).
- **Moribund delta:** the old western part of the **Bengal delta** (Murshidabad–Nadia) whose distributaries are dead/inactive; the mature and active deltas lie further south-east.
| Delta type | Shape / condition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Arcuate** (fan) | Arc-shaped; most common; sediment denser than sea water | **Nile**, Ganga |
| **Bird's foot** | Long branching distributaries; sediment lighter, carried far | **Mississippi** (Gulf of Mexico) |
| Cuspate | Tooth-shaped; densities equal | Tiber (Italy) |
| Estuarine | Drowned funnel mouth | Narmada, Tapi |
- **Sinking deltas (Apr 2026):** 7 deltas (Ganga-Brahmaputra, Nile, Mekong, Yangtze, Amazon, Irrawaddy, Mississippi) ≈ **57%** of sinking delta area; causes **groundwater extraction + less silt** (app CA).
# AP coast
- Coastline ~974 km; built by the **Godavari** (delta = **Konaseema**) and **Krishna** deltas; minor deltas: **Vamsadhara** (Srikakulam), **Penna** (Nellore).
- **Kolleru**: freshwater lake between the Godavari and Krishna deltas; WLS **308.55 sq km**. **Pulicat**: saltwater lagoon on the **AP–Tamil Nadu border**.
> Asked again and again: largest delta = Ganga-Brahmaputra (Sundarbans); bird's foot = Mississippi; Tapti forms an estuary; west-flowing rivers – steep gradient.

=== River Course, Erosional Landforms, Gorge and Canyon
| Course (stage) | Dominant work | Typical features |
|---|---|---|
| **Upper (youth)** | **Vertical erosion**, steep gradient | V-shaped valleys, **gorges, canyons**, **waterfalls**, rapids, potholes |
| Middle (maturity) | **Lateral erosion + transport** | Wider valleys, **meanders**, floodplains begin |
| Lower (old age) | Deposition, gentle gradient | Broad floodplains, levees, **oxbow lakes**, braided channels, **deltas**/estuaries |
- **Order downstream (CDS 2014):** falls → meanders → oxbow lake → delta (**II, I, IV, III**). Middle course is not vertical erosion alone.
| Landform | Formation |
|---|---|
| V-shaped valley | Rills → gullies → valleys by vertical erosion |
| Waterfall | Drop over a resistant rock band or fault step |
| Potholes | Eddy-whirled pebbles drill holes; large one below a waterfall = **plunge pool** |
| Incised (entrenched) meanders | Uplift rejuvenates the river; meanders cut deep into hard rock |
| River terraces | Stream cuts down into its own floodplain (erosional); paired = same height both banks; unpaired = one bank |
- River-profile terms:
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Graded profile | Smooth concave long profile; erosion and deposition roughly balanced |
| Base level | Lowest level a river can erode to; **sea level = ultimate**; lakes and dams = local |
| Stream capture (river piracy) | Headward erosion breaches a divide and takes a neighbour's upper waters |
| **Peneplain** | Near-featureless gentle plain worn by **rivers** almost to base level (W.M. Davis) |
- Gorge vs canyon:
| Basis | Gorge | Canyon |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Deep, narrow, steep-sided valley | **Very deeply / widely eroded gorge** – a variant of a gorge |
| Width | Almost same at top and bottom | Wider at the top, step-like sides |
| Rock | Hard rocks | Horizontally bedded sedimentary rocks |
| Example | Chisapani (Karnali, Nepal) | **Grand Canyon** (Colorado River, Arizona) |
- Both gorges and canyons are **V-shaped valleys** (true); "gorges are extended forms of canyons" is **wrong** (APPSC G1).
- **Grand Canyon**: a gorge carved by the **Colorado** River in **Arizona, USA**, ~446 km long, over 1.8 km deep.
- **Gandikota canyon**: **Pennar (Penna) River**, YSR Kadapa, AP – "**Grand Canyon of India**" (UPSC 2022).
- **Dihang gorge**: Brahmaputra enters India (Arunachal); Yarlung Tsangpo Grand Canyon near **Namcha Barwa**.

=== Drainage Patterns
| Pattern | Control | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Dendritic** | Uniform rock, no structural control; **tree-like**, tributaries join at acute angles | Ganga plains, **Assam Valley**, Indus, Mahanadi, Godavari – most rivers of **South India** |
| **Trellis** | Folded rocks; primary tributaries parallel, secondary ones join **at right angles** | Appalachians, Singhbhum, upper Himalayan rivers |
| **Radial** | Streams flow **outward from a central high** | **Sri Lanka** central highlands; **Amarkantak** (Narmada, Son, Mahanadi) |
| **Centripetal** | Streams flow **into a central lake/depression** | **Loktak Lake**, Manipur |
| **Rectangular** | Joints and faults – **main stream bends at right angles** and tributaries join at right angles | Joint-controlled plateaus |
| Annular | Rings on an eroded dome | Structural domes |
- "Branches of a tree" = **dendritic**, not radial.
- **Antecedent drainage:** river older than the uplift, keeps its course by cutting gorges as the land rises – **most Himalayan rivers** (Indus, Sutlej, Brahmaputra).
- Genetic stream types: consequent (follows initial slope), subsequent (along weak strata), obsequent (opposite the dip), **insequent** – **no apparent control**, no clear reason for its course (dendritic on uniform rock).

=== Plains and River-plain Terms – Doab, Prayag, Bhabar, Terai, Lacustrine Plains
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Doab** | Land **between two confluent rivers** (Persian do-ab = **two waters**); Ganga-Yamuna doab, **Bist Doab (Beas–Sutlej)** |
| Prayag | A sacred river confluence |
| **Bhabar** | Porous belt of pebbles and boulders, **8–16 km** wide, along the **Shiwalik foothills** at the break of slope – a **piedmont** deposit; streams sink underground |
| Terai | Wet, marshy, forested belt south of the Bhabar (15–30 km) where streams re-emerge |
| Bhangar | Older alluvium above flood level; contains **kankar** |
| Khadar | Newer floodplain alluvium, renewed every flood; more fertile |
- **Panch Prayag** on the Alaknanda (downstream): **Vishnuprayag** (Dhauliganga), **Nandprayag** (Nandakini), **Karnaprayag** (Pindar), **Rudraprayag** (Mandakini), **Devprayag** (**Bhagirathi + Alaknanda → Ganga**).
| Plain type | Formed by | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Alluvial plain | River deposition | **Indo-Gangetic plain** (Indus, Ganga, Brahmaputra) |
| Flood plain | Fine silt dropped when the river overflows | Along river valleys |
| **Lacustrine plain** | Lake filled with sediment, then drained | **Kashmir Valley** (Karewas), **Imphal Basin** (Manipur) |
| Peneplain | Long river erosion to near base level | Peninsular plateau (rejuvenated) |
- **Karewas** of Kashmir = **lacustrine (lake) deposits** (fluvio-glacial clays, sands) – saffron land.
- **Ganga Plain** = a **geosyncline** (fore-deep trough filled with sediment) in the classical description. **Patland** (Chotanagpur, central India) = **lava-capped mesas**.

=== Depositional Landforms, Ravines and Badlands
| Landform | How it forms |
|---|---|
| **Alluvial fan** | Gently sloping, fan-shaped **coarse alluvium** dropped by (often braided) streams at the **break of slope** (hill foot) |
| Natural levee | Raised sandy banks along the channel |
| Floodplain | Fine alluvium dropped when the river overflows |
| Braided channel | Sand bars split the channel into many strands with riverine islands |
| Oxbow lake | Meander loop cut off; stagnant water left |
- Meander: **outer bend concave, erosional** (cliff); **inner slip-off side convex, depositional**.
- **APPSC G1 2023:** depositional work of Himalayan rivers = **braided channels, alluvial fans, natural levees**; **river terraces** are products of vertical **erosion** (NCERT) – so II, III and IV only.
- Bajada = coalesced alluvial fans of deserts; diara = riverine sand islands of Bihar.
- **Gully erosion → ravines**: rills deepen into gullies; dense gully network = **badland topography** – **Chambal ravines** (central India); **Chos** of Hoshiarpur (Punjab).
