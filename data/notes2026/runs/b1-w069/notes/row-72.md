=== Kailasa Temple – Ellora Cave 16
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Cave number | **Cave 16** of Ellora (Kailash leni) |
| Carved under | **Krishna I** (r. 756–773 CE), **Rashtrakuta** |
| Technique | **Monolithic**, carved top-down from a single **basalt** cliff |
| Deity | **Shiva** as lord of Mount Kailasa |
| Tower | Three-tiered **Dravida** vimana, although in Maharashtra (Nagara territory) |
- **Dynasty that built it = Rashtrakutas** (UPPSC 2005; UP Lower 2008; CDS 2007; UDA/LDA 2010) – not Chalukyas of Vatapi, Cholas, Pallavas, Kadambas, Gangas, Satavahanas or Mauryas.
- **Ruler = Krishna I** (Forest Beat Officer) – not Rajendra, Rajaraja or Narasimha I.
- **Rock-cut architecture (UPPSC 2007, 2012):** Kailasa = the **largest monolithic rock excavation in the world**; the Shore Temple, Brihadeeswara (Thanjavur), Lingaraja, Kandariya Mahadeva and Puri Jagannatha are **structural** temples.
- **Biggest cave temple in India** = Kailasa (match: biggest corridor – **Rameswaram**; delta – **Sundarbans**; dome – **Gol Gumbaz**).
- **Location:** **Ellora caves** (MPPSC 2024) – not Ajanta, Elephanta or Kanheri.
- Early Rashtrakuta rulers were staunch **Shaivas**; Rashtrakutas shaped the **Vesara** synthesis (**Nagara + Dravida**).
- **⚠ Trap:** Kailasa = **Krishna I**, NOT Dantidurga, Amoghavarsha I or **Krishna III** (the wrong pair in APPSC G1 2024).
~~ Sources: NextGen Rocket Indian History Part 2 p.124; PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 3 p.135-136; app notes 1:72 (sub 0)

=== Ellora Caves – Groups, Numbers and Chronology
# Setting
- Local name **Verul Leni**; in the **Charanandri hills**, near the **Elaganga** river, about **30 km** from **Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar (Aurangabad)**, **Maharashtra** – Ajanta and Ellora are both in **Aurangabad** district (MPPSC 1990; UPPSC 2009).
- On the old Ujjain–Deccan trade route; carved **c. 6th–10th century CE**; UNESCO site (1983).
# Number and groups
| Group | Caves | Count | Period / patron |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Buddhist** | **1–12** | 12 | **Earliest**, c. 600–730 CE (before the Rashtrakutas) |
| **Hindu** | **13–29** | 17 | Early ones Kalachuri/Chalukya; mainly **Rashtrakuta** |
| **Jain** | **30–34** | 5 | **Latest**, 9th–10th century (Digambara) |
| Total | 1–34 | **34** | – |
- **34 caves** (APPSC AEE: answer 34); **30–34 = Jain** (APPSC Welfare 2017; APPSC PYQ).
- **Buddhist, Hindu and Jain caves coexist** at **Ellora** (UPPSC 2002; UP Lower 2002: all three) – not Ajanta, Elephanta or Karle.
- **Built by:** most of the famous caves under the **Rashtrakutas** (UPPSC 1999); the Buddhist caves and Cave 21 are earlier.
# Cave number → name
| Cave | Group | Name / feature |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Buddhist | **Only chaitya** at Ellora (Vishwakarma / carpenter's cave) |
| 14 | Hindu | **Ravan ki Khai** |
| 15 | Hindu | **Dashavatara** – **Dantidurga's** inscription |
| 16 | Hindu | **Kailasa** (monolithic) |
| 21 | Hindu | Rameshwar Lena |
| 29 | Hindu | **Dhumar (Dumar) Lena** – among the earliest (6th c.) |
| 32 | Jain (**Digambara**) | **Indra Sabha** |
| 33 | Jain | Jagannath Sabha |
# Chronology
- **Order (G1 2023):** **Dumar Lena (29) → Ravan ki Khai (14) → Dashavatara (15) → Kailasa (16)** = IV, I, III, II.
  - **⚠ Settled:** none of the printed options gives this order; the printed key "III, I, IV, II" puts the Dashavatara before Ravan ki Khai and Dumar Lena third – both wrong (Caves 14 and 15 were begun before 16; Cave 29 is Kalachuri-period).
- **Groups:** Buddhist → Hindu → Jain; Kailasa is the latest of the four named temples.
- **2026:** India–Jordan **twinning** agreement between **Petra** and **Ellora** (UNESCO sites).

=== Elephanta Caves
- **Island** cave complex near **Mumbai** (Gharapuri): **7 caves – 5 Hindu, 2 Buddhist**; mainly **Shaiva** (UDA/LDA 2003).
- **Masterpiece:** **Trimurti / Maheshamurti (Sadashiva)** – three-headed Shiva, 7 m, Cave 1 (UPPSC 2011) – not Ellora, Ajanta or Bhaja.
- **Pashupata sect:** the Great Cave is linked with **Pashupata Shaivism** (Lakulisha image) (CDS 2018 ✓). Wrong options: named by the British (it was the **Portuguese**, after a stone elephant); "one large cave" (there are 7); a Vishnu carving (it is Shiva).
- **Patron:** books and keys credit the **Rashtrakutas** (APPSC G1; MPPSC 2021: Ellora and Elephanta = Rashtrakuta period).
  - **⚠ Disputed:** many historians date the main cave to the **6th-century Kalachuris**.
- Trimurti (Elephanta) + Kailasa (Ellora) = zenith of Rashtrakuta-era art; Elephanta Cave 1 resembles Ellora's Dumar Lena (Cave 29).
~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 3 p.135-136; NextGen Rocket Indian History Part 2 p.124; PMF Art & Culture Part 6 p.65; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elephanta_Caves; app notes 1:72 (sub 2)

=== Religion – Patronage
| Faith | Patronage |
|---|---|
| **Shaivism** | Early rulers firm **Shaivas**; Kailasa temple |
| **Jainism** | Peak under **Amoghavarsha I** (814–878), disciple of Jinasena; one-third of the Deccan Jain |
| Vaishnavism, Buddhism | Royal patronage, **no persecution**; Buddhist settlements at Kanheri, Sholapur, Dharwar |
| Islam | Arab merchants allowed to trade, worship and build mosques in port towns |
- **Religion patronised by the Rashtrakutas (CDS 2007): Jainism** – the keyed answer, as in UDA/LDA 2010 ("Jain").
  - **⚠ Settled:** the old key "Saivism" was changed: Saivism was the kings' own early faith, but the question's answer is Jainism (special patronage).

=== Arab Travellers and the Title Balhara
| Traveller | Time / reign | Account |
|---|---|---|
| **Sulaiman** | c. 851, **Amoghavarsha I** | Ranked the king among the **four great kings** of the world (with the Caliph, Chinese emperor, Byzantine emperor) |
| Ibn Khurdadbeh | 9th century | Balhara = greatest king of India |
| **Al-Masudi** | c. **915–16**, **Indra III** | "Balhara" = greatest king of India; many Arab subjects |
- **Balhara** = Arabic form of the Rashtrakuta title **Vallabha** (Vallabharaja).
- **Chronology of travellers (SI 2023):** **Sulaiman → Al-Masudi → Marco Polo → Fernao Nuniz** (a, b, c, d).

=== Administration, Trade and Decline
# Units and officers
| Unit | Level | Head |
|---|---|---|
| **Rashtra** | Province | **Rashtrapati** |
| **Vishaya** | District | **Vishayapati** |
| **Bhukti** | Smaller unit | **Bhogapati** |
| **Grama** | Village | **Gramakuta** / gavunda |
- **Nad-gavundas:** **hereditary** officers of the **nadu**; helped collect **revenue**.
# Trade
- Western ports: **Cambay (Khambhat), Thana, Sopara**.
- **Horses imported** by sea from **Arabia**; **textiles exported**.
- **Arab merchants** lived peacefully in the western ports (not barred).
# AP angle and decline
- **Krishna III** helped **Danarnava** kill Eastern Chalukya **Ammaraja II** (970) and take Vengi.
- The Rashtrakuta era raised **Kannada** to a classical medium.
- **Decline** by the late 10th century under **Chalukya and Chola** pressure.
