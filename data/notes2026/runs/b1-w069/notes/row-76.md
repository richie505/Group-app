=== Mahendravarman I (600–630) – Titles and the Mattavilasa Prahasana
# Titles
- **Mahendravarman I**, son of **Simhavishnu**: titles **Mattavilasa, Vichitrachitta, Gunabhara, Chitrakarapuli, Lalitankura** (UPSC 2025 ×2; APPSC G1 PYQ "Vichitrachitta") – not Simhavishnu, Narasimhavarman I or Simhavarman.
  - **⚠ Trap:** **Vichitrachitta** is the father's title; **Narasimhavarman I** is **Mamalla / Vatapikonda**.
- **Not his title:** **Vatapikonda** (Narasimhavarman I).
# Mattavilasa Prahasana
- **Author: Mahendravarman I** (G1 Prelims 2012; UP 2010) – not Ramanujacharya, Narasimhavarman, Dandin, Gautamiputra Satakarni, Rudradaman or Pulakesin II.
- A **Sanskrit** one-act **farce (prahasana)** mocking **Kapalikas and Buddhist monks**; he also wrote **Bhagavadajjuka**.
# Faith and building
- Began as a **Jain** → turned **Shaiva** under **Appar (Tirunavukkarasar)**, not Sambandar.
- **Rock-cut temples of South India begin with him** – **Mandagapattu** (no brick, timber, metal or mortar); **Kudumiamalai** music inscription credited to his patronage.
- Wars with **Pulakesin II** began under him; he lost the Krishna-Godavari lands (Maruturu, Kopparam records).

=== Origin, Territory, Seal and the Andhra Links
# Territory and emblem
- **Tondaimandalam** = Pallava territory (APPSC 2023), capital **Kanchipuram** (between the Pennar and Vellar).
- **Royal seal / crest: the bull (Nandi)** (G1 1983-84) – tiger = Chola, boar = Chalukya, fish = Pandya.
- **Origin theories:** Parthian (Pahlava), **Andhra** (early Prakrit charters from the Krishna-Guntur region; replaced the Ikshvakus) and local Tamil – **not Scythian**; originally **feudatories of the Satavahanas**.
# Charters and the end of the Ikshvakus
| Charter | Ruler | Point |
|---|---|---|
| **Mayidavolu (Maidavolu)** plates | **Sivaskandavarman** (as yuvaraja) | Prakrit; among the earliest Pallava records |
| Hirahadagalli plates | Sivaskandavarman | Agnishtoma, Vajapeya, Ashvamedha |
| **Manchikallu** inscription | **Simhavarman** | Pallava conquest of **Vijayapuri** after defeating the Ikshvakus |
- **Manchikallu and Maidavolu = Pallava** inscriptions (G2 Mains 2025) – not Chola, Chalukya or Vijayanagara.
- **Manchikallu inscription of Simhavarman** proved that the **Pallavas replaced the Ikshvakus of Vijayapuri** (G2 PYQ) – not the Nasik record, a Nagarjunakonda inscription or the Nandampudi grant.
- **Downfall of the Ikshvakus = the Pallavas** (APPSC PYQ): Simhavarman defeated the **last Ikshvaku Rudrapurushadatta**.
- **Renati Chodas** = early Pallava feudatories.

=== Pallava Rulers – Order, Events and Nandivarman II
# Ruler → event
| Ruler | Reign (CE) | Event |
|---|---|---|
| **Sivaskandavarman** | Early | **Kanchi came under the Pallavas** |
| **Vishnugopa** | Early | Faced **Samudragupta's** invasion |
| **Simhavishnu** | c. 575–600 | Founder of the imperial line; **defeated the Kalabhras** |
| Mahendravarman I | 600–630 | Wars with Pulakesin II |
| **Narasimhavarman I** | 630–668 | **Defeated Pulakesin II, destroyed Vatapi (642)** |
| Paramesvaravarman I | c. 670–700 | – |
| **Nandivarman II** | 731–795 | **Defeated by Vikramaditya II**; Kanchi captured for a time |
| Aparajita | c. 880–897 | Killed by **Aditya I Chola** (c. 897; some say 903) – end |
- **Match (G1):** Sivaskandavarman – Kanchi; Vishnugopa – Samudragupta; Narasimhavarman I – Vatapi; Nandivarman II – Vikramaditya II = **A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III**.
- **Order:** Simhavishnu → **Mahendravarman I → Narasimhavarman I → Paramesvaravarman I** → Narasimhavarman II (Rajasimha) → **Nandivarman II** (UP BEO 2019: 4, 2, 1, 3).
- **Cross-dynasty order (UPSC 2020):** **Mahendravarman I (c. 600) → Gopala (Pala, c. 750) → Bhoja (Pratihara, 836) → Parantaka I (Chola, 907)** = 2-4-1-3.
- **Later rulers:** Dantivarman, Nandivarman III, Nripatunga, **Aparajita**.
# Nandivarman II Pallavamalla
- **Chosen king:** a **boy of 12** from a **collateral line**, **chosen by ministers, nobles and people** after Paramesvaravarman II died (c. 731).
- **"Elected" kings (State PSC):** **Gopala** (Pala – Khalimpur inscription) and **Nandivarman Pallavamalla** – not Harsha or Yashovarman.
- Ardent **Vaishnava**; built **Vaikuntha Perumal, Kanchi**; contemporary of **Tirumangai Alvar**; title **Pallavamalla**.

=== Kanchi as a Centre of Learning – Ghatikas, Dandin, Bharavi
# Ghatika
- **Ghatika = a Brahminical (Vedic) college / settlement of Brahmin scholars** (G1 2012; G1 PYQ), i.e. **Hindu colleges** (G1 Prelims 2008) – not merchants, Jain monks, artisans or Buddhist centres; usually **attached to temples**; **Sanskrit** medium.
- Andhra link: **Vishnukundins** and **Eastern Chalukya Jayasimha I** (Asanapura ghatika) also set up ghatikas.
- **Mayurasharman**, a Brahmin student at the **Kanchi Ghatika**, was insulted by a **Pallava horseman** → founded the **Kadamba** kingdom (**Talagunda inscription**).
# Book → author
| Book | Author | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Mattavilasa Prahasana | Mahendravarman I | Sanskrit farce |
| Kiratarjuniya | **Bharavi** | Simhavishnu's court |
| **Dashakumaracharita**, Kavyadarsha | **Dandin** | Pallava court (Rajasimha); called Vengi "**Andhra Nagaram**" |
- **Dashakumaracharita = Dandin** (UP 2008) – not Bharavi, Bilhana or Somadeva.
- **Hiuen Tsang** visited Kanchi **c. 640 CE**, under Narasimhavarman I.
- **Grantha** script of the Pallavas = parent of Southeast Asian scripts; the Pallavas pioneered **Dravida** temple art.

=== Narasimhavarman I Mamalla (630–668)
- **Titles:** **Mamalla / Mahamalla** ("great wrestler") and **Vatapikonda**; **founded the port city of Mamallapuram (Mahabalipuram)** (Sachivalayam) – not Simhavishnu, Narasimhavarman II or Paramesvaravarman I.
- **Wars:** killed **Pulakesin II** at **Manimangala (642)**; general **Paranjoti** sacked **Vatapi**.
- **Navy:** **two naval expeditions to Ceylon** (c. 642 onwards) to restore his ally **Manavarma** (UP RO 2016) – not Rajaraja, Kirtivarman I or Jayasimha I.
- **Art:** the **monolithic rathas** at Mahabalipuram; a firm Shaiva; Hiuen Tsang visited Kanchi in his reign.
