## SUB 0 | Mahabalipuram – Rathas, Reliefs and the Shore Temple
[0:0] # Pancha (Pandava) Rathas
[0:1] - **Builder:** the **Pallavas** under **Narasimhavarman I (Mamalla, 630–668)** – "Seven Ratha" / Pancha Ratha temples (APPSC GS 2025; UPPSC 1994, 2014, 2015; SSC 2008, CGL 2020) – not Mahendravarman, Paramesvaravarman, Nandivarman, Simhavarman, Pulakesin II or Mayuravarman; not Cholas, Cheras, Chedis, Chalukyas, Rashtrakutas or Satavahanas.
[0:2] - **Monolithic:** each carved from **one granite rock**, copying wooden and structural forms (not brick and timber).
[0:3] - **Five:** **Dharmaraja, Bhima, Arjuna, Nakula-Sahadeva, Draupadi**.
[0:4] TABLE head: Ratha | Special fact
  [0:4.0] Dharmaraja | Largest; forerunner of the Dravida vimana
  [0:4.1] Draupadi | Smallest (UPPSC Mains 2011); hut-shaped single cell, no ornament
  [0:4.2] Nakula-Sahadeva | Apsidal
[0:5] # Reliefs and mandapas
[0:6] TABLE head: Monument | Theme
  [0:6.0] Arjuna's Penance / Descent of the Ganga | Bhagiratha's penance; largest open-air rock relief in India – the "descent of the Ganga" relief is at Mahabalipuram (Asst. Comm. 2018), not Ellora, Sanchi or Amaravati
  [0:6.1] Varaha Mandapa | Varaha rescuing Bhudevi
  [0:6.2] Mahishasuramardini Cave | Durga slaying Mahishasura
  [0:6.3] Krishna Mandapa | Krishna lifting Govardhana
[0:7] - Pancha Rathas, Varaha Mandapa and Mahishasuramardini cave = **Narasimhavarman I** phase.
[0:8] # Shore Temple
[0:9] - **Structural** temple of **Narasimhavarman II (Rajasimha)**; **two Shiva shrines + one Vishnu shrine**; sole survivor of the "**Seven Pagodas**".
[0:10] # Port and UNESCO
[0:11] - **Mahabalipuram = the famous Pallava port** (APSLPRB SI 2019) – not Krishnapatnam, Motupalli (Kakatiya) or Achalapuram.
[0:12] TABLE head: Point | Mahabalipuram | Ajanta
  [0:12.0] UNESCO | 1984 | 1983
  [0:12.1] Patron | Hindu; Pallavas | Buddhist; Satavahana and Vakataka phases
  [0:12.2] Form | Rathas, reliefs, Shore Temple | 25 viharas + 4 chaityas; found 1819 by John Smith

## SUB 1 | Phases of Pallava Architecture, Mandagapattu and Painting
[1:0] # Four phases
[1:1] TABLE head: Phase | Ruler | Type | Examples
  [1:1.0] Mahendra | Mahendravarman I (600–630) | Rock-cut pillared mandapas | Mandagapattu, Mahendravadi, Dalavanur
  [1:1.1] Mamalla | Narasimhavarman I (630–668) | Monolithic rathas and mandapas | Pancha Rathas
  [1:1.2] Rajasimha | Narasimhavarman II | First structural temples | Shore Temple; Kailasanatha, Kanchi
  [1:1.3] Nandivarman / Aparajita | Nandivarman II and later | Smaller structural temples | Vaikuntha Perumal
[1:2] - Evolution: **rock-cut → monolithic → structural**.
[1:3] # Mandagapattu inscription
[1:4] - Issued by **Mahendravarman I**: a temple for **Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva** made **without brick, timber, metal or mortar** (stone is not in the list).
[1:5] - = **first rock-cut temple in South India**; first **dvarapalakas** at a temple entrance.
[1:6] # Painting
[1:7] - **Sittannavasal** (**Pudukkottai**, TN): **Jain** cave with **lotus-pond** murals; renovated by the **Pandyas** (9th c.).
[1:8] - **Panamalai** painting of **Parvati** = **Rajasimha** period.
[1:9] # Builder match
[1:10] - **Shore Temple – Narasimhavarman II; Brihadisvara – Rajaraja Chola I; Gommateshwara – Chamundaraya; Barabar caves – Ashoka**.
[1:11] ~~ Sources: PMF Art & Culture (src/indian-history/pmf-art-culture-01.pdf) Pallava architecture; app notes 1:77 (sub 0, 1, 5, 7)
## SUB 2 | Kanchi Temples – Kailasanatha and Vaikuntha Perumal
[2:0] - **Kailasanatha (Rajasimheshvara), Kanchi: built by Narasimhavarman II (Rajasimha)** (G2 2000 ×2) – not Mahendravarman I, Narasimhavarman I or Nandivarman Pallavamalla; **completed by Mahendravarman III**.
[2:1]   - **Sandstone**; the **oldest surviving Pallava structural temple** at Kanchi; carries **Vikramaditya II's Kannada inscription**; model for the **Virupaksha, Pattadakal**.
[2:2] - **Vaikuntha Perumal, Kanchi:** **Nandivarman II**; **Vishnu**; storeyed sanctum and panels on Pallava history.
[2:3] ~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval (src/indian-history/pmf-ancient-medieval-03.pdf) p.115; PMF Art & Culture (pmf-art-culture-01.pdf) Pallava section; app notes 1:77 (sub 3)
## SUB 3 | Pallava-style Rock-cut Caves in Andhra – Bhairavakona and Undavalli
[3:0] - **Bhairavakona = a centre of Shaivism** (G2 2008; APPSC PYQ) – not Buddhism, Jainism or Vaishnavism; **eight** rock-cut Shiva caves resembling early Pallava caves.
[3:1]   - Location: Nallamala hills, **Prakasam district** on the Nellore border (old books and keys: "Nellore district").
[3:2] TABLE head: Cave | Location | Attribution
  [3:2.0] Undavalli | Guntur, Krishna bank | Vishnukundins – not Cholas; reclining Anantasayana Vishnu
  [3:2.1] Mogalrajapuram | Vijayawada | Vishnukundins; Shiva and Vishnu shrines
  [3:2.2] Bhairavakona | Prakasam | Vishnukundin / early Pallava idiom
[3:3] - The **Vishnukundins** started Hindu cave temples in Telugu lands, imitating the **Pallava** cave style.
