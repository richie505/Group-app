## SUB 0 | Kailasa Temple – Ellora Cave 16
[0:0] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [0:0.0] Cave number | Cave 16 of Ellora (Kailash leni)
  [0:0.1] Carved under | Krishna I (r. 756–773 CE), Rashtrakuta
  [0:0.2] Technique | Monolithic, carved top-down from a single basalt cliff
  [0:0.3] Deity | Shiva as lord of Mount Kailasa
  [0:0.4] Tower | Three-tiered Dravida vimana, although in Maharashtra (Nagara territory)
[0:1] - **Dynasty that built it = Rashtrakutas** (UPPSC 2005; UP Lower 2008; CDS 2007; UDA/LDA 2010) – not Chalukyas of Vatapi, Cholas, Pallavas, Kadambas, Gangas, Satavahanas or Mauryas.
[0:2] - **Ruler = Krishna I** (Forest Beat Officer) – not Rajendra, Rajaraja or Narasimha I.
[0:3] - **Rock-cut architecture (UPPSC 2007, 2012):** Kailasa = the **largest monolithic rock excavation in the world**; the Shore Temple, Brihadeeswara (Thanjavur), Lingaraja, Kandariya Mahadeva and Puri Jagannatha are **structural** temples.
[0:4] - **Biggest cave temple in India** = Kailasa (match: biggest corridor – **Rameswaram**; delta – **Sundarbans**; dome – **Gol Gumbaz**).
[0:5] - **Location:** **Ellora caves** (MPPSC 2024) – not Ajanta, Elephanta or Kanheri.
[0:6] - Early Rashtrakuta rulers were staunch **Shaivas**; Rashtrakutas shaped the **Vesara** synthesis (**Nagara + Dravida**).
[0:7] - **⚠ Trap:** Kailasa = **Krishna I**, NOT Dantidurga, Amoghavarsha I or **Krishna III** (the wrong pair in APPSC G1 2024).

## SUB 1 | Ellora Caves – Groups, Numbers and Chronology
[1:0] # Setting
[1:1] - Local name **Verul Leni**; in the **Charanandri hills**, near the **Elaganga** river, about **30 km** from **Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar (Aurangabad)**, **Maharashtra** – Ajanta and Ellora are both in **Aurangabad** district (MPPSC 1990; UPPSC 2009).
[1:2] - On the old Ujjain–Deccan trade route; carved **c. 6th–10th century CE**; UNESCO site (1983).
[1:3] # Number and groups

  [1:4.2] Jain | 30–34 | 5 | Latest, 9th–10th century (Digambara)
  [1:4.3] Total | 1–34 | 34 | –

[1:7] - **Built by:** most of the famous caves under the **Rashtrakutas** (UPPSC 1999); the Buddhist caves and Cave 21 are earlier.

## SUB 2 | Elephanta Caves
[2:0] - **Island** cave complex near **Mumbai** (Gharapuri): **7 caves – 5 Hindu, 2 Buddhist**; mainly **Shaiva** (UDA/LDA 2003).
[2:1] - **Masterpiece:** **Trimurti / Maheshamurti (Sadashiva)** – three-headed Shiva, 7 m, Cave 1 (UPPSC 2011) – not Ellora, Ajanta or Bhaja.
[2:2] - **Pashupata sect:** the Great Cave is linked with **Pashupata Shaivism** (Lakulisha image) (CDS 2018 ✓). Wrong options: named by the British (it was the **Portuguese**, after a stone elephant); "one large cave" (there are 7); a Vishnu carving (it is Shiva).
[2:3] - **Patron:** books and keys credit the **Rashtrakutas** (APPSC G1; MPPSC 2021: Ellora and Elephanta = Rashtrakuta period).
[2:4]   - **⚠ Disputed:** many historians date the main cave to the **6th-century Kalachuris**.
[2:5] - Trimurti (Elephanta) + Kailasa (Ellora) = zenith of Rashtrakuta-era art; Elephanta Cave 1 resembles Ellora's Dumar Lena (Cave 29).
[2:6] ~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 3 p.135-136; NextGen Rocket Indian History Part 2 p.124; PMF Art & Culture Part 6 p.65; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elephanta_Caves; app notes 1:72 (sub 2)
