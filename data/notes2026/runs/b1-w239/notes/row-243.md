=== Indian National Army – Origin, First Phase and Second Phase
# First INA (1942) – Mohan Singh
- **Idea and founder:** the idea of an army of Indian POWs was **Captain Mohan Singh's** (Spectrum); he is the **founder and first commander** of the INA (Azad Hind Fauj) – not Subhas Bose, who took over only in 1943.
- **Brainchild debate:** many books credit the idea to **Giani Pritam Singh Dhillon** (IIL, Bangkok) and **Major Iwaichi Fujiwara** (head of the Japanese **F-Kikan**), who brought the POWs to Mohan Singh – UPPSC 2001 key "I and IV".
- **Where and when:** formed in **Singapore** (then British Malaya) in **February 1942**, after the **fall of Singapore**; ~40,000 volunteers by end-1942; first division of **16,300** men formally raised **1 September 1942**.
- **Dissolved December 1942:** Mohan Singh quarrelled with the Japanese (they wanted a token force of 2,000) and was taken into custody.
# Indian Independence League – Rash Behari Bose
- **Rash Behari Bose** fled to Japan in **1915** (after the Hardinge bomb case), became a Japanese citizen, founded the Indian Club of Tokyo and the **Indian Independence League (IIL), Tokyo, 1942** (Tokyo conference 28-30 March 1942).
- **Bangkok Conference (June 1942):** INA placed under the IIL; Rash Behari Bose president; Mohan Singh commander; Subhas Bose invited.
- **Rash Behari Bose ≠ Rash Behari Ghosh** (Moderate). Rash Behari Bose helped Subhas set up the second INA and died in Japan on **21 January 1945** – so he was never in the Red Fort dock.
- **Quote (CGPSC 2019 key):** "Avenge the blood of Indian Martyrs… Stand up against the entire Anglo-American enemy" – attributed to **Rash Behari Bose**.
# Second phase under Subhas Bose (1943)
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| June 1943 | Bose (as "Abid Hussain") reached **Tokyo** by German and Japanese submarines, met PM Tojo |
| 2 July 1943 | Reached **Singapore** |
| 4 July 1943 | Rash Behari Bose handed IIL and INA to Subhas |
| 25 August 1943 | Bose became **Supreme Commander** of the INA |
| 21 October 1943 | Provisional Government of Free India, Singapore |
| January 1944 | INA **headquarters shifted to Rangoon** (Burma) |
- **"INA set up / started in 1943"** questions → **Singapore** (where Bose reorganised it); "head office" → **Rangoon**.
- **Other name:** the INA = **Azad Hind Fauj**, the army of the separate Azad Hind Government.
> Exam angle: first commander / founder / idea → **Mohan Singh**; IIL → **Rash Behari Bose**; formed → **Singapore, 1942**; HQ → **Rangoon**.

=== INA Regiments, Campaigns and Officers
# Regiments
- **Brigades:** Subhash, Gandhi, Azad and Nehru regiments.
- **Rani Jhansi (Rani Laxmibai) Regiment** (1943): an **all-women combat unit** established by **Subhas Chandra Bose**, led by **Capt. Lakshmi Sahgal (Swaminathan)**; named after Rani Lakshmi Bai.
  - Lakshmi Sahgal headed the Women's Department of the Azad Hind Government, contested the **2002 presidential election** (lost to **A.P.J. Abdul Kalam**); mother **Ammu Swaminathan** was a Constituent Assembly member; married **Prem Kumar Sahgal**.
# Imphal-Kohima campaign (1944)
- INA reached the Arakan front 4 Feb 1944; crossed the Burma border and stood on Indian soil **18 March 1944**; advanced up to **Kohima and Imphal**.
- **First INA flag on the Indian mainland:** **Moirang, Manipur, 14 April 1944**, by **Col. Shaukat Ali Malik** (Bahadur Group/Subhash Regiment).
- A battalion under **Shah Nawaz Khan** fought on the Indo-Burma (Imphal-Kohima) front; many quiz books say he hoisted the flag "at Kohima, March 1944".
- Retreat from **18 July 1944** (rains, no supplies or air cover); INA surrendered with Japan, **15 August 1945**.
# Ethos
- **Motto:** **"Ittefaq, Itmad, Qurbani"** (Unity, Faith, Sacrifice); **anthem:** Hindustani **Jana Gana Mana** ("Shubh Sukh Chain") by **Abid Hasan 'Safrani'**.
- **Linked to the INA:** Shah Nawaz Khan, P.K. Sahgal, G.S. Dhillon, Shaukat Ali Malik, Lakshmi Sahgal, Rashid Ali. **Not** linked: Major Kartar Singh, B.C. Dutt (an RIN rating of 1946).
- **Order:** first INA (1942) → Bose takes command (July 1943) → Azad Hind Government (21 Oct 1943) → Imphal campaign (1944) → Red Fort trials (Nov 1945).
> Exam angle: women's regiment → **Rani Jhansi**, under **Lakshmi Sahgal**, set up by **Subhas Bose**; first flag → **Moirang**.

=== Forward Bloc, 1939 and the Andhra Link
- **Founded by Subhas Chandra Bose** in **May 1939** (at Makur, Unnao), after resigning the Congress presidency (April 1939) – a **party within the Congress** to unite the **radical, left-wing and anti-imperialist** forces (UPSC 2005: not "Indian Freedom Party", "Azad Hind Fauj" or "Revolutionary Front").
- **Disciplinary action:** after his 9 July protest call, Bose was removed as Bengal PCC president and **debarred from Congress office for 3 years (August 1939)**.
- **Ramgarh, March 1940:** Anti-Compromise Conference of the Forward Bloc and the Kisan Sabha.
- **Sheel Bhadra Yajee** (Bihar): joined Bose in founding the **All India Forward Bloc**; also active in the INA movement.
- **Mian Akbar Khan:** Forward Bloc head of the NWFP who arranged Bose's 1941 escape.
# Andhra – Madduri Annapurnaiah
- **Madduri Annapurnaiah** – the Andhra leader who **joined the Forward Bloc** of Subhas Bose (APPSC; Group-II 2019); led it in Andhra with **Peta Bapayya**; called **"Andhra Netaji"**.
- Jailed **18 months** for the playlet/article **"Chicharapidugu"**; 4 years in jail as a Forward Bloc member in WWII; edited **Congress**, Jai Bharat, Navashakti.
- **Not** Forward Bloc: Puchalapalli Sundarayya (Communist), Gottipati Brahmaiah, N. Sanjiva Reddy (Congress).
> Exam angle: "party founded by Bose in 1939" → **Forward Bloc**; Andhra member → **Madduri Annapurnaiah**.

=== Haripura Session 1938 and the National Planning Committee
| Session | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Haripura** (Gujarat) | Feb 19-21, **1938** | **51st** session; **Bose unanimously elected President** (UPPCS, MPPSC) |
| **Tripuri** (near Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh) | March **1939** | Bose re-elected, then resigned |
- **Presidential address:** state-owned industrialisation and a **planning commission** to advise the national government → Bose is credited with the **idea of a Planning Commission in the British period**; he also advocated the **Roman script for Hindustani/Hindi**.
- **National Planning Committee (1938):** set up by the Congress **under Bose's presidency**; **Chairman: Jawaharlal Nehru**; Secretary: **K.T. Shah** – the genesis of the Planning Commission (1950) with the Bombay Plan (1944).
- **NDA 2010:** Haripura is a milestone because of **the idea of planning** – it did not declare war or accept the 1935 Act.
- Haripura also gave **moral support** to agitations in the princely states.
> Exam angle: "Congress President who set up the NPC" → **Bose**; "NPC chairman" → **Nehru**; year → **1938**.

=== Azad Hind Government, 1943 and the Islands
- **Provisional Government of Free India** (Arzi Hukumat-e-Azad Hind) proclaimed by Bose on **21 October 1943** at **Singapore** (not Rangoon, Tokyo or Berlin).
- Bose was **head of state, PM, war and foreign minister** and Supreme Commander; Lakshmi Swaminathan – women; H.C. Chatterjee – finance.
- **Recognised by 9 countries** (Axis powers incl. Japan, Germany, Italy, and Burma, Thailand, Philippines, Manchukuo …); **declared war on Britain and the USA**.
- **Andaman & Nicobar** handed over by **Japan** (6 November 1943); renamed **Shaheed Dweep** (Andaman) and **Swaraj Dweep** (Nicobar); Bose hoisted the tricolour at **Port Blair, 30 December 1943**.
- **⚠ Trap:** "Azad Hind Fauj founded on 21 October 1943" – that is the **Government**; the Fauj dates from 1942.
> Exam angle: date → **21 Oct 1943**; place → **Singapore**; names → **Shaheed and Swaraj**; recognition → **9**.

=== Bose Abroad – Escape, Germany, Legion and Radio
- **War as a godsend:** Bose saw a war between Germany and Britain as the opportunity to exploit Britain's difficulty ("England's need is India's opportunity") – not Rajaji, Jinnah or Nehru.
- **Escape:** house arrest at Elgin Road, Calcutta (Dec 1940) → escaped **January 1941** as Maulavi **Muhammad Ziauddin** → Peshawar (helped by Bhagat Ram, Mian Akbar Khan) → Kabul → Moscow → **Berlin** (Italian passport, "Orlando Mazzotta"), reached April 1941.
- **In Germany:** met Hitler; set up the **Free India Centre (Berlin, 1941)**; raised the **Free India(n) Legion** ("Mukti Sena", HQ Dresden) from Indian POWs captured by Germany and Italy (Rommel's North African campaign) – UPSC 2008: **Subhas Bose**, not Rash Behari Bose or Har Dayal.
- **Azad Hind Radio:** started in **Germany**, broadcasting from Berlin from January 1942 (later from South-East Asia) – not Japan.
- **February 1943:** left Germany by submarine for Japan.
> Exam angle: Free Indian Legion and Azad Hind Radio → **Germany**.
~~ Sources: PMF Modern Indian History Part 5 p.67-68; Spectrum Brief History of Modern India p.488-489 (book p.456-457); app notes 1:243 (sub 4)

=== Tripuri Crisis, 1939
- **Election (Jan 1939):** Bose **defeated Gandhi-backed Dr. Pattabhi Sitaramayya** by **1,580 to 1,377** – not Nehru, Patel or Rajendra Prasad (APPSC 2018).
- Gandhi: **"Pattabhi's defeat is my defeat."**
- **Tripuri session (March 1939):** Bose (ill) called for a six-month ultimatum to Britain; **Pant Resolution** asked him to nominate the Working Committee **"in accordance with the wishes of Gandhiji"**.
- **Core dispute:** the **formation of the Congress Working Committee** (RAS 2008).
- **Outcome:** Bose **resigned (April 1939)** → **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** became President → Forward Bloc (May 1939).
> Exam angle: defeated → **Pattabhi Sitaramayya**; successor → **Rajendra Prasad**; dispute → **Working Committee**.
~~ Sources: Spectrum Brief History of Modern India p.450-452 (book p.418-420); PMF Modern Indian History Part 5 p.66; app notes 1:243 (sub 2)

=== Death Controversy and Commemoration
- **Death:** reported in an air crash at **Taihoku (Taipei), 18 August 1945**.
| Inquiry | Year | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Shah Nawaz Committee | 1956 | Died in the crash |
| Khosla Commission | 1970 | Died in the crash |
| Mukherjee Commission | 1999 | Did **not** die in the crash (report rejected, 2006) |
- **Subhash Chandra Bose Aapda Prabandhan Puraskar:** national award for disaster rescue and relief (police and others), announced by PM Modi in **October 2018** (75th anniversary of the Azad Hind Government).
- **Andaman links:** Ross, Neil and Havelock islands renamed Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose Dweep, Shaheed Dweep and Swaraj Dweep (30 Dec 2018).
