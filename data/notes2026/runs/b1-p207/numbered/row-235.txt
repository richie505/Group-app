## SUB 0 | AICC at Vijayawada (Bezawada), 1921, and Gandhi's Andhra Tours
[0:0] - **AICC met at Vijayawada on 31 March – 1 April 1921** (venue "Gandhinagar"); leaders **accepted Gandhi's leadership** of the national struggle (APPSC 2023). Earlier, **January 1921**, the **APCC** met at Vijayawada and set up committees at **district, taluk and village** level.
[0:1] - Present: Gandhi, Motilal Nehru, C.R. Das, the Ali brothers.
[0:2] - **Gandhi's speech translated into Telugu by Ayyadevara Kaleswara Rao.**
[0:3] - **Pingali Venkayya** (born **Bhatlapenumarru**) presented the **tricolour flag** design (with the **charkha**), accepted by Gandhi; PM paid tribute on his **150th birth anniversary (August 2026)**.
[0:4] TABLE head: Target (by 30 June 1921) | Number
  [0:4.0] Tilak Swaraj Fund | Rs 1 crore
  [0:4.1] Congress members | 1 crore
  [0:4.2] Charkhas | 20 lakh
[0:5] - Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya formed the volunteer force **Ramadandu**; Duggirala was the **first Andhra leader to be AICC secretary**.
[0:6] # Gandhi's tours
[0:7] TABLE head: Tour | Towns
  [0:7.0] April 1921 (coastal) | Kakinada, Rajahmundry, Eluru, Machilipatnam, Chirala, Nellore – not Kurnool
  [0:7.1] October 1921 | Kurnool, Kadapa, Tirupati, Tadipatri
[0:8] - At Kakinada **Durgabai** translated his speech and gave Rs 5,000; women leaders **Duvvuri Subbamma, Ponaka Kanakamma, Unnava Lakshmibayamma** emerged.
[0:9] - Andhra boycotted the **Nov 1920 elections** (Justice Party won **63 of 98**; first ministry **A. Subbarayalu Reddiar**, 17 Dec 1920) and the **Prince of Wales (17 Nov 1921)** – hartal at Vijayawada complete. **Delhi AICC, 5 Nov 1921** allowed provinces civil disobedience incl. no-tax.
[0:10] - National institutions: **National College – Eluru**; Andhra Vidyapeetha Goshthi – Chirala; Pallepadu Ashram – Nellore; Seethanagaram Ashram near Rajahmundry (now Kasturba Ashram); **Ponduru (Srikakulam) khadi**.

## SUB 1 | NCM Struggles in Guntur – Chirala-Perala, Pedanandipadu, Palnadu
[1:0] TABLE head: Struggle | Place | Leader / facts
  [1:0.0] Chirala-Perala (1921) | Bapatla taluka, Guntur | Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya; against the formation of Chirala municipality (tax Rs 4,000 → 40,000); no-tax from March 1921; Gandhi visited 6 April 1921; 25 April 1921 13,572 people left for the camp Ramnagar; volunteer corps Ramadandu; Duggirala arrested Sept 1921
  [1:0.1] Pedanandipadu no-tax (1921-22) | Guntur | Parvataneni Veerayya Chowdary – "Andhra Shivaji"; Shanti Sena; village officers resigned so that no land revenue could be collected
  [1:0.2] Palnadu forest (Pullari) satyagraha (1921-22) | Palnadu, Guntur | Kanneganti Hanumanthu (born Guntur district); first forest satyagraha in Andhra; refusal of the pullari – tax on grazing cattle in the forests
[1:1] - Palnadu: failed rains 1921; pullari **12 annas a cow** (Forest Act 1878); **social boycott at Macherla**; **300 cattle impounded at Minchalapadu**; **Hanumanthu shot dead in the cattle-rescue at Minchalapadu, 22 Feb 1922** (NextGen: 26 Feb); **Unnava Lakshminarayana** (wrote **Nayakuralu** in jail) and Madabhushi Vedantam Narasimhachari backed it; **Konda Venkatappayya** tried to moderate it.
[1:2] - **Guntur no-tax campaign** suspended **11 Feb 1922** after Gandhi's message to **Konda Venkatappayya**.
[1:3] - All three were in the old **Guntur** district. **Chirala** is famous for **handloom textiles**.
[1:4] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP History Part 19 p.1; NextGen AP History Part 22 p.21-25; NextGen Rocket AP History p.35; NextGen Previous Papers (Chirala municipality); old app notes (Minchalapadu, 12 annas, Nayakuralu)
## SUB 2 | Home Rule Movement in Andhra
[2:0] - **Annie Besant** founded the Home Rule League (Sept 1916; Tilak's 28 April 1916); first woman INC President (**1917**); **Sarojini Naidu** first Indian woman president (1925); Nellie Sengupta 1933.
[2:1] - **Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao** – organised the movement and its **branches** in Andhra, **secretary** of the Andhra Home Rule League; **translated Sarojini Naidu's "Awake Mother"** as **"Nutana Haindava Matrugeetamu"**; pamphlet **Swarajya Paramoddesham**.
[2:2] - Besant toured **Kakinada, Rajahmundry, Eluru** after Lucknow 1916; 52 branches by 1917; Home Rule flag hoisted at **Coimbatore**; students of Machilipatnam National College wore "Besant lockets" after her arrest (June 1917).
[2:3] - **Andhra Congress Circle (APCC), 22 Jan 1918** – president **Nyapati Subba Rao**, secretary Konda Venkatappayya; Nyapati led the deputation to **Montagu (20 Dec 1917)** for a separate Andhra province.
[2:4] - **APCC, Vijayawada, 8 Dec 1918** recommended **Tilak** as delegate to the (Paris) **Peace Conference**.
[2:5] - Krishna Patrika – Mutnuri Krishna Rao; Desamata – Chilakamarti.
[2:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP History Part 18 p.26; NextGen AP History Part 22 p.11-16; NextGen AP History Part 7 p.9, p.27; old app notes (lockets, Coimbatore flag, pamphlet)
