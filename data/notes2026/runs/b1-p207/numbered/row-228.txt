## SUB 1 | Return to India (1915) and First Steps
[1:0] - Sailed from Cape Town **19 July 1914**; landed at **Bombay (Apollo Bunder) on 9 January 1915** → observed as **Pravasi Bharatiya Divas (NRI Day)**.
[1:1] - Year of return = **1915** (not 1914/1916/1917).
[1:2] - Age on return: born 2 Oct 1869 → strictly **45**; PMF and many books say "**at the age of 46**".
[1:3] - **Gopal Krishna Gokhale**, his **political guru**, advised him to spend **one year touring and observing India** before entering politics.
[1:4] - Received the **Kaisar-i-Hind gold medal (26 June 1915, Lord Hardinge)** for ambulance work in South Africa.
[1:5] - **May 1915:** Satyagraha Ashram at **Kochrab**, Ahmedabad.
[1:6] # BHU speech (February 1916) – first major public appearance
[1:7] - Charged the **Indian elite with lack of concern for the labouring poor**.
[1:8] - Said India's **salvation lies with the farmers**, not lawyers, doctors or landlords.
[1:9] - Did **not** take up untouchables or the Ahmedabad mill owners in that speech → statements I and II only (CDS 2017).
[1:10] # First satyagrahas in India
[1:11] TABLE head: Satyagraha | Issue | "First"
  [1:11.0] Champaran 1917 | Indigo (tinkathia); invited by Raj Kumar Shukla | First civil disobedience
  [1:11.1] Ahmedabad 1918 | Mill wages (plague bonus) | First hunger strike
  [1:11.2] Kheda 1918 | Revenue remission | First non-cooperation
[1:12] ~~ Sources: PMF Modern History Part 4 p.115-117, p.121; Spectrum (Ahir) printed p.316-318; NextGen Rocket Sheet #100 p.54

## SUB 3 | Early Life and the South African Phase (1893-1914)
[3:0] - Born **2 October 1869, Porbandar**; father **Karamchand** (diwan); mother **Putlibai**.
[3:1] - Early education at **Rajkot**; married **Kasturba at 13**; **Samaldas College, Bhavnagar**; law at the **Inner Temple, London (1888-91)**; practice at **Rajkot and Bombay** failed.
[3:2] - **1893:** went to South Africa for **Dada Abdullah's** case; stayed **1893-1914 (about 21 years)**.
[3:3] TABLE head: Year | Event
  [3:3.0] 7 June 1893 | Thrown out of a first-class compartment at Pietermaritzburg (on the way to Pretoria)
  [3:3.1] 1894 | Natal Indian Congress
  [3:3.2] 1896-1902 | Seva Dharma: compounder in Rustomji's charity hospital; Ambulance Corps in the Boer War
  [3:3.3] 1903 | Indian Opinion; law office at Johannesburg
  [3:3.4] 11 Sept 1906 | First satyagraha – against the Asiatic Registration Ordinance ("Black Act"), Transvaal
  [3:3.5] 1908 | First jail term (January); burning of registration certificates
  [3:3.6] 1913 | Great March / Volkrust satyagraha – against the £3 poll tax and invalidation of Indian marriages
  [3:3.7] 1914 | Indian Relief Act – poll tax abolished, Indian marriages recognised
[3:4] - South Africa = the **'laboratory'** where Gandhi forged satyagraha (not England or India); "the making of the Mahatma".
[3:5] - Lessons (Spectrum, PMF): he found the **masses had immense capacity to participate in and sacrifice for a cause** → the struggles **prepared him to lead the national movement** (UP RO/ARO 2023 A-R: A and R true, R explains A).
[3:6] > First satyagraha = **1906 Transvaal**; first in India = **Champaran 1917**.
[3:7] ~~ Sources: Spectrum (Ahir) printed p.312-316; PMF Modern History Part 4 p.111-116; NextGen Rocket Sheet #100 p.54; old app notes (Samaldas, Rustomji)
## SUB 4 | Hind Swaraj (1909)
[4:0] - Written by **Gandhi** in **1909**, in **Gujarati**, aboard the **Kildonan Castle** (London → South Africa).
[4:1] - **Banned** in 1910 (Bombay Government); **English version by Gandhi himself**.
[4:2] - Swaraj = **self-rule**; against modern Western civilisation and industrialisation (railways, lawyers, doctors).
[4:3] - Called the British Parliament **"sterile" and a "prostitute"**.
[4:4] - Argued that the British did not take India – **Indians gave it to them**: British rule was established with **Indians' cooperation**.
[4:5] > Not by Tilak, Gokhale, Ranade or Vinoba; not written in Hindi or English.
[4:6] ~~ Sources: PMF Modern History Part 4 p.115, p.124; PMF Part 5 p.208; NextGen Rocket Sheet p.76; old app notes (Kildonan Castle, "sterile and prostitute")
