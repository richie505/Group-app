## SUB 0 | Capital, High Court and the Bellary Taluks
[0:0] # Capital of Andhra State (1953)
[0:1] - **Capital:** **Kurnool** – temporary capital of Andhra State from its formation on **1 October 1953** (APPSC JA 2023: "Kurnool became the capital in **1953**").
[0:2] - **How it was chosen:** Rayalaseema legislators wanted the capital in their region as promised by the **Sribagh Pact**; the communists favoured **Vijayawada**; in the vote of the legislators **Kurnool won by majority**, but only with the help of **five non-Andhra members** of the residual Madras Assembly (C. Rajagopalachari had suggested they should not vote).
[0:3]   - Counted without the five non-Andhra votes, Vijayawada-Guntur would have had the edge – hence some keys answer "None" to "capital chosen with the support of the majority".
[0:4] - **Nehru's condition (25 Mar 1953):** capital to be **inside Andhra**, not Madras; temporary capital to be decided **before 1 July 1953**; some offices could stay in Madras for a while.
[0:5] # High Court
[0:6] - **Andhra High Court:** set up at **Guntur** in **1954** (first Chief Justice **Koka Subba Rao**) – the compromise for coastal Andhra, while the capital went to Kurnool; not Kurnool, Vijayawada or Anantapur.
[0:7] - **Interim:** the **Madras High Court** kept jurisdiction until the Guntur court was set up.
[0:8] # Bellary district split
[0:9] TABLE head: Bellary area | Went to
  [0:9.0] Alur (Aalur), Adoni | Andhra – Kurnool district
  [0:9.1] Rayadurg | Andhra – Anantapur district
  [0:9.2] Bellary taluk, Siruguppa, Hospet, Sandur, Harpanahalli, Hadagali | Mysore
[0:10] - **Justice L.S. Mishra** (Chief Justice, **Hyderabad High Court**): Bellary inquiry (hearings 1-15 May 1953); on his report **Bellary taluk went to Mysore**.
[0:11] - **Legislature:** members elected to the **Madras Assembly in 1952** formed the Andhra Assembly; **unicameral – no Legislative Council**.
[0:12] > Exam angle: Kurnool capital / Guntur High Court (×4); Alur-Adoni → Kurnool, Rayadurg → Anantapur.

## SUB 1 | Wanchoo Committee and the Andhra State Act, 1953
[1:0] # Wanchoo Committee
[1:1] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [1:1.0] Appointed | December 1952, after Nehru's statement of 19 December 1952 (following Potti Sriramulu's death)
  [1:1.1] Member | Justice Kailas Nath (K.N.) Wanchoo, Chief Justice of the Rajasthan High Court – a single-member committee
  [1:1.2] Task | To report on the financial and other implications of forming Andhra State – identifying the problems of forming the state (boundaries, finances, division of assets, administration); not the capital of India, not the Nizam or peasants
  [1:1.3] Report | 7 February 1953
  [1:1.4] Centre's decisions on it | Announced by Nehru on 25 March 1953
[1:2] - **NextGen's dates differ:** NextGen gives "appointed 7 Feb 1953, report 25 Mar 1953"; the Lok Sabha record has the report presented on 7 Feb 1953 and the decisions announced on 25 Mar 1953.
[1:3] - **On Madras:** Wanchoo suggested the Andhra Government work temporarily from **Madras** (not Kurnool) for **3-5 years**; Nehru rejected it – "no question" of Madras as capital.
[1:4] - **Earlier:** **Partition Committee (1949-50)** under **Kumaraswamy Raja** on asset split; **JVP Committee (April 1949)** – Andhra only if Telugus gave up Madras.
[1:5] - **Special Officer:** **C.M. Trivedi**, later the first Governor.
[1:6] # Finances, assets and services
[1:7] - Assets and administration divided between Andhra and **residual Madras**; the Wanchoo apportionment of **assets and liabilities** was largely reproduced in the Act's schedule.
[1:8] - The 25 Mar 1953 statement left **services** and **financial implications** to be considered separately later.
[1:9] - New state "**administratively and economically orphaned**": no developed capital, no commercial centre.
[1:10] # Andhra State Act, 1953
[1:11] - **Act 30 of 1953**; **assent of the President: 14 September 1953**; in force **1 October 1953** – not May or June 1953.
[1:12] - **2014 parallels:** **Kamalanathan** committee – employees; **Sheela Bhide** committee – Schedule IX institutions; **Section 6** expert committee – new capital.
[1:13] > Exam angle: Wanchoo = financial and other implications (×2); Bill assent 14 Sept 1953.
