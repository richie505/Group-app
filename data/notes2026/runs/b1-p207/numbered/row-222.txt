## SUB 1 | Andhra Jana Sangham and the Nizam's Andhra Mahasabha
[1:0] - **Andhra Jana Sangham:** formed 1921 (Madapati Hanumantha Rao, **"Andhra Pitamaha"**); **first meeting at Hyderabad, 14 February 1922**; grew into the (Nizam Rashtra) **Andhra Mahasabha** – keyed as the "National Andhra Mahasabha" of **Madapati**.

  [1:1.3] – | 1943 | Hyderabad | K.V. Ranga Reddy – first contested presidency

## SUB 2 | Communist Takeover of the Mahasabha; Comrades Association
[2:0] - **End of 1943 - 1944:** the Mahasabha split on **ideological** lines – communists vs moderates.
[2:1] - **Bhuvanagiri (1944):** **Ravi Narayana Reddy** captured the leadership; a rival **Nationalist Andhra Mahasabha** broke away.
[2:2] - NextGen / Rocket: **"Jatiya Andhra Mahasabha" founded by Ravi Narayana Reddy**.
[2:3] - **Moderates:** Burgula Ramakrishna Rao, K.V. Ranga Reddy, M. Narsing Rao, Madapati – **Ravi Narayana Reddy was not a moderate**.
[2:4] - **Hyderabad State Comrades Association:** **1939** (13 Dec 1939) – Makhdoom Mohiuddin, Raj Bahadur Gour, Syed Ibrahim; **Kashinath Rao Vaidya** presided the inaugural meeting; Telangana's first communist unit. (⚠ NextGen/Rocket and the APPSC 2012 key give 1940.)
[2:5] - It mobilised peasants for the **Telangana armed struggle**.
[2:6] ~~ Sources: NextGen Modern India L2 Part 10 p.2; NextGen AP History Part 7 p.21; NextGen Modern India L3 Part 1 p.131; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comrades_Association

## SUB 5 | Visalandhra and Electoral Politics
[5:0] - Communists **won 41 of 145** Andhra-region seats in **1952**.
[5:1] - Communists were the **first to demand Visalandhra (1946)** and started the movement; they **supported** linguistic states.
[5:2] - The People's War line cost the CPI in the **1945-46 elections**.
[5:3] - Prakasam's Andhra ministry fell on **15 Nov 1954**; CPI won only **15 of 196** seats in **1955**.
[5:4] ~~ Sources: NextGen AP History Part 7 p.12; app notes 1:222
