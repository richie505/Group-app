## SUB 0 | Indigo Revolt, Nil Darpan, Pabna and the Deccan Riots
[0:0] # Indigo Revolt (1859-60), Bengal
[0:1] - European planters forced ryots to grow **indigo instead of paying crops like rice**, through **advances and fraudulent contracts**, at prices that did not pay; intimidation, flogging, seizure of cattle.
[0:2] - Leaders **Digambar and Bishnu Biswas** of **Nadia**; ryots refused to sow, went on rent strike and used the courts.
[0:3] - **Supported by the Bengali intelligentsia** – press (**Harish Chandra Mukherjee's *Hindoo Patriot***), meetings, legal aid; missionaries too.
[0:4] - **Indigo Commission (March 1860):** found planters coercive; ryots must **fulfil existing contracts** but **could refuse future ones**; Nov 1860 notification – no compulsion. It did **not** find indigo profitable to ryots.
[0:5] - Came **immediately after 1857** (Sanyasi 1763-1800 and Santhal 1855-56 earlier; Pabna 1873-76 later).
[0:6] # Nil Darpan
[0:7] TABLE head: Role | Person
  [0:7.0] Written (Bengali play, 1860) | Dinabandhu Mitra
  [0:7.1] English translation | Michael Madhusudan Dutt
  [0:7.2] Published | Rev. James Long
[0:8] - Nil Darpan (1860) is earlier than Satyendranath Tagore's ICS (1863), Arya Samaj (1875) and Anandamath (1882).
[0:9] - **Tinkathia** (indigo on **3/20** of land) belongs to **Champaran (Bihar, 1917)**, ended by the Champaran Agrarian Act 1918.
[0:10] # Pabna and Deccan
[0:11] - **Pabna (1873-76):** agrarian league of **Ishan Chandra Roy and Shambhu Pal** against **rent hikes** by zamindars; legal, peaceful → **Bengal Tenancy Act 1885**.
[0:12] - **Deccan Riots (1875):** **Poona and Ahmednagar**; ryotwari peasants vs **Marwari and Gujarati moneylenders**; debt bonds burnt; cause incl. the **Limitation Law of 1859** → **Deccan Agriculturists' Relief Act 1879** (protection from **moneylenders**).

## SUB 4 | Telangana Armed Struggle (1946-51)
[4:0] - Against the **Nizam's feudal order** – deshmukhs, jagirdars, **vetti** (forced labour); led by the **CPI** through the **Andhra Mahasabha** – the biggest peasant guerrilla war (3,000 villages).
[4:1] - Land redistributed; **village soviets** set up; **called off October 1951**.
[4:2] - **Doddi Komaraiah:** **first martyr** – shot at **Kadavendi** (Jangaon taluka, Nalgonda) on **4 July 1946** by the **Visnur deshmukh's** men while **defending a poor washerwoman's land** (**Chakali Ailamma**).
[4:3] - Leaders: **P. Sundarayya, Chandra Rajeswara Rao**; woman fighter **Mallu Swarajyam**; Andhra Mahasabha president 1941-46 **M. Suryanarayana**; **Ravi Narayana Reddy** presided Chilkur (1941); 11th session **Bhongir (1944)** communist-dominated.
[4:4] TABLE head: Book | Author
  [4:4.0] *Veera Telangana – Naa Anubhavalu, Gnapakalu* (Heroic Telangana) | Ravi Narayana Reddy – not Mallu Swarajyam
  [4:4.1] *History of the People's Armed Struggle of Telangana (1946-51)* | Devulapalli Venkateswara Rao (NextGen/APPSC line: "Devulapalli Ramanuja Rao")
  [4:4.2] *Telangana People's Struggle and its Lessons* | P. Sundarayya
[4:5] - **Vetti vs bonded labour:** vetti = caste-bound forced service of village servant castes; bonded labour = debt-based, regardless of caste (the statements swap them).
[4:6] ~~ Sources: Spectrum p.615-616; NextGen AP History Part 7 p.4, p.8, Part 19 p.16; Wikipedia D._V._Rao; app notes 1:216 (sub 6, 9)
## SUB 5 | Moplah Revolt (1921) and Bardoli (1928)
[5:0] - **Moplah (Mappila):** **Muslim tenants of Malabar** against **Hindu landlords (jenmis)** and the British – no security of tenure, high rents, renewal fees; merged with the **Khilafat** agitation; arrest of **Ali Musliyar** (Aug 1921); leader **Variyankunnath Kunjahammad Haji**.
[5:1] - **Wagon Tragedy, November 1921:** Moplah prisoners suffocated in a closed railway wagon.
[5:2] - **Bardoli (Surat, Gujarat):** against a **30% land-revenue hike**; **Vallabhbhai Patel** – women gave him the title **"Sardar"**; inquiry cut the hike to 6.03%.
[5:3] ~~ Sources: Spectrum p.611-612; app notes 1:216 (sub 4)
## SUB 6 | Tebhaga Movement (1946-47)
[6:0] - Call of the **Bengal Provincial Kisan Sabha (Sept 1946)** to implement the **Floud Commission's** tebhaga: **two-thirds of the crop to the bargadar** instead of one-half – the landlord's share cut **from one-half to one-third**.
[6:1] - **Bargadars / adhiars** (sharecroppers) against **jotedars**; slogan "take the paddy to our own threshing floor".
[6:2] - Outcome: **Bargadari Bill / Act (1950)**.
[6:3] ~~ Sources: Spectrum p.615; app notes 1:216 (sub 5)
