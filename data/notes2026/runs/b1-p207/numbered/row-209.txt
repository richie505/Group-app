## SUB 0 | Lahore Conspiracy Case (1929-31), the Executions and Jatin Das
[0:0] # The case
[0:1] - **Trial:** Bhagat Singh, **Sukhdev**, **Rajguru** and others were tried for the **Saunders murder** (and bomb-making) in the **Lahore Conspiracy Case** before a **special tribunal set up by the Governor-General**; the **death sentence came in October 1930**.
[0:2] - Bhagat Singh had already got life in the **Assembly Bomb Case**; the sentence waited for the verdict in the Saunders (Lahore) case.
[0:3] - The accused turned the **court into a forum of propaganda**; the **defence was organised by Congress leaders**.
[0:4] - **"Second" Lahore case:** the **first Lahore Conspiracy Case (1915)** was the **Ghadar** case – the first trial under the **Defence of India Act, 1915** (passed mainly to deal with the Ghadarites).
[0:5] TABLE head: Sentence | Revolutionaries
  [0:5.0] Hanged, 23 March 1931, Lahore jail | Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Rajguru
  [0:5.1] Transportation for life | Kishori Lal, Mahabir Singh, Bejoy Kumar Sinha, Shiv Verma, Gaya Prasad, Jaidev Kapur, Kamalnath Tewari
  [0:5.2] Life (Assembly Bomb Case), not hanged | Batukeshwar Dutt
[0:6] > NOT hanged in the Lahore case: **B.K. Dutt** (life), **Bismil, Roshan Singh** (Kakori, 1927), **Azad** (died at Alfred Park, Feb 1931).
[0:7] - Cremated at **Hussainiwala**, banks of the **Sutlej**, **Ferozpur district** – today the **Shaheed Bhagat Singh memorial** (B.K. Dutt was also cremated there, 1965).
[0:8] # Lahore jail hunger strike (1929)
[0:9] - Demand: **honourable treatment as political prisoners**; Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, B.K. Dutt, **Jatin Das** and others fasted.
[0:10] - **Jatin (Jatindra Nath) Das** – earlier in **Non-Cooperation (1921)**; made bombs for the HSRA; arrested **14 June 1929** in the (supplementary) **Lahore Conspiracy Case**; **died after 63 days** of fasting (Spectrum: "on the 64th day"), **September 1929** – the freedom fighter who **died in jail on hunger strike**.
[0:11] # Cases in order
[0:12] TABLE head: Case | Year
  [0:12.0] First Lahore (Ghadar) | 1915
  [0:12.1] Kanpur (Bolshevik) conspiracy | 1924
  [0:12.2] Kakori | 1925
  [0:12.3] Meerut (31 communists) | 1929
  [0:12.4] Second Lahore (Bhagat Singh) | 1929-31

## SUB 1 | HRA (1924) and HSRA (1928)
[1:0] TABLE head: Feature | HRA – Hindustan Republican Association/Army | HSRA – Hindustan Socialist Republican Association/Army
  [1:0.0] Founded | October 1924, Kanpur | 9-10 September 1928, Ferozshah Kotla, Delhi
  [1:0.1] Founders / leader | Sachindranath Sanyal, Ram Prasad Bismil, Jogesh Chandra Chatterjee | Reorganised and renamed under Chandra Shekhar Azad
  [1:0.2] Members | Ashfaqullah Khan, Roshan Singh, Rajendra Lahiri, Manmathnath Gupta, Azad | Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Bhagwati Charan Vohra (Punjab); Bejoy Kumar Sinha, Shiv Verma, Jaidev Kapur (UP); Rajguru, B.K. Dutt
  [1:0.3] Goal | Armed revolution → Federal Republic of the United States of India, adult franchise | Same republic + socialism as the official goal; collective leadership
  [1:0.4] Main action | Kakori (1925) | Saunders (1928), Assembly bomb (1929)
[1:1] - **Azad** (not Bhagat Singh) **renamed** the HRA as the **HSRA**; Bhagat Singh is credited as a **founder (leader) of the HSRA**, never of the HRA.
[1:2] - **HRA founders – dispute:** Spectrum, PMF and Wikipedia name **Sanyal, Bismil, Chatterjee** only; **NextGen and the APPSC G1 2024 key also list Azad**, who joined as a member.
[1:3] - **Shiv Verma** came in with the **HSRA** (1928), not the HRA.
[1:4] - HSRA-era methods: **mass propaganda** – courtroom statements, leaflets, **hunger strikes**.
[1:5] - **AP link:** a "Hindustan Socialist Republican Army" in **Madras (10 March 1933)** – **Kanuru Ramanatha Choudary**.

## SUB 2 | Kakori Train Action (9 August 1925)
[2:0] - **HRA** men held up the **8-Down train at Kakori near Lucknow** (Lucknow-Saharanpur section) and **looted official railway cash (government treasury)** to **finance the organisation**; one passenger was killed by accident.
[2:1] - **43 arrested**; public prosecutor (government advocate): **Jagat Narain Mulla**; defence committee chaired by **Govind Ballabh Pant** (with Chandra Bhanu Gupta, Mohan Lal Saxena).
[2:2] - **Chandra Shekhar Azad** – the **only** Kakori revolutionary who **escaped and was never caught**.
[2:3] TABLE head: Hanged (December 1927) | Jail
  [2:3.0] Ram Prasad Bismil (19 Dec) | Gorakhpur
  [2:3.1] Ashfaqullah Khan (19 Dec) | Faizabad
  [2:3.2] Roshan Singh (19 Dec) | Naini, Allahabad
  [2:3.3] Rajendra Lahiri (17 Dec – two days early) | Gonda
[2:4] - **Sachindranath Sanyal** – life (second term in the Cellular Jail); **Jogesh Chatterjee** – life.
[2:5] - **Bismil** refused milk before his execution: "Now I shall take my **mother's milk** only".
[2:6] > NOT in Kakori: **Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Rajguru, Surya Sen**.
[2:7] ~~ Sources: Spectrum p.381-382; PMF Part 4 p.162, p.169-171; NextGen APPSC 06 p.78; en.wikipedia.org Kakori conspiracy (Mulla; Pant in the defence); old app notes (43 arrested, jails, milk)
## SUB 3 | Saunders, the Assembly Bomb and Other Assassinations
[3:0] # Saunders (Lahore, 17 December 1928)
[3:1] - **Lala Lajpat Rai** died of lathi blows on the **anti-Simon** procession at Lahore (October 1928) → **Bhagat Singh, Azad and Rajguru** shot **ASP J.P. Saunders**, mistaken for **SP James A. Scott**, who ordered the lathi-charge.
[3:2] - **Rajguru fired first**, then Bhagat Singh; Azad covered the retreat.
[3:3] # Central Legislative Assembly bomb (8 April 1929)
[3:4] TABLE head: Particular | Fact
  [3:4.0] Who | Bhagat Singh and Batukeshwar Dutt
  [3:4.1] Protest against | Public Safety Bill and Trade Disputes Bill
  [3:4.2] Bombs | Deliberately harmless – not to kill but "to make the deaf hear" (leaflet)
  [3:4.3] Then | Courted arrest to use the court as a forum for propaganda
  [3:4.4] Bhagat Singh's age | a little over 21 (born 28 September 1907)
[3:5] - **"Inquilab Zindabad" (Long Live Revolution):** coined by **Maulana Hasrat Mohani** (1921); **Bhagat Singh** first made it the revolutionaries' cry and popularised it – keys give "**Bhagat Singh**" for "who gave / first raised".
[3:6] # Other assassinations
[3:7] TABLE head: When | Who | Target
  [3:7.0] Calcutta, 1924 | Gopinath Saha (hanged) | Charles Tegart, Police Commissioner – shot Ernest Day by mistake
  [3:7.1] London, Caxton Hall, 13 March 1940 | Udham Singh | Michael O'Dwyer, Lt. Governor of Punjab at the time of Jallianwala Bagh

## SUB 4 | Chittagong Armoury Raid (18 April 1930) and Bengal
[4:0] - **Surya Sen ("Masterda")** – teacher in a national school, **Secretary of the Chittagong District Congress Committee** (1929); planned and led the raid with **Anant (Ananta) Singh, Ganesh Ghosh, Lokenath Bal, Ambika Chakravarty**.
[4:1] - Raid of **April 1930** by 65 activists under the banner of the **Indian Republican Army – Chittagong Branch**: seized the armouries, **cut telephone and telegraph lines**, cut the railway link; Sen proclaimed a **Provisional Revolutionary Government**; battle at **Jalalabad Hills**, then guerrilla war.
[4:2] - Surya Sen arrested **16 February 1933**, **hanged 12 January 1934**.
[4:3] TABLE head: Woman | Action
  [4:3.0] Kalpana Dutt(a) | Took part in the raid along with Surya Sen; tried with him – life (not hanged)
  [4:3.1] Pritilata Waddedar | Led the attack on the Pahartali European Club (Sept 1932); took cyanide – "Bengal's first woman martyr"
  [4:3.2] Santi Ghosh & Suniti Choudhury | Shot the District Magistrate of Comilla (December 1931)
  [4:3.3] Bina Das | Fired at Bengal Governor Stanley Jackson while receiving her degree at the convocation (February 1932)
[4:4] - New features: **group action** against the colonial state, **women fought with guns**, religious oaths dropped – **Muslims** joined Sen's group.
[4:5] ~~ Sources: Spectrum p.384-385; PMF Part 4 p.167-168; NextGen APPSC 06 p.78
## SUB 5 | Writings, Ideology and the Naujawan Bharat Sabha
[5:0] TABLE head: Work | Author
  [5:0.0] Why I am an Atheist (1930, in jail) | Bhagat Singh
  [5:0.1] Introduction to Dreamland (book by Lala Ram Saran Das) | Bhagat Singh
  [5:0.2] The Cult of the Bomb (*Young India*, 1930) | Mahatma Gandhi – after the bomb under Irwin's train (23 Dec 1929)
  [5:0.3] The Philosophy of the Bomb (reply) | Bhagwati Charan Vohra (with Azad and Yashpal)
  [5:0.4] Bandi Jivan | Sachindranath Sanyal
  [5:0.5] The Indian Struggle | Subhas Chandra Bose
  [5:0.6] The Indian Sociologist (journal, London) | Shyamji Krishna Varma – not Bhagat Singh
[5:1] - **"Sarfaroshi ki Tamanna ab hamare dil mein hai"** – written by **Bismil Azimabadi**, made famous by **Ram Prasad Bismil** on the way to the gallows (keys: Ram Prasad Bismil).
[5:2] - Bhagat Singh: "to be a revolutionary ... one required **criticism and independent thinking**"; revolution "is not the cult of bomb and pistol"; "**by the masses, for the masses**".
[5:3] - **Naujawan Bharat Sabha:** founded by **Bhagat Singh** at **Lahore, March 1926**, with **Sukhdev, Bhagwati Charan Vohra**, Yashpal, Chhabil Das – open wing to rouse **youth, peasants and workers**; **opposed communalism**. **Ambika Chakravarti** (Chittagong) was **not** a member.
[5:4] - Bhagat Singh's turn to revolution: **Jallianwala Bagh (1919)**, **Nankana Sahib (1921)**, **Chauri Chaura (1922)**.
[5:5] ~~ Sources: Spectrum p.381, p.386-387; PMF Part 4 p.162, p.165-166, p.170; old app notes (Atheist, Dreamland, Nankana Sahib)
