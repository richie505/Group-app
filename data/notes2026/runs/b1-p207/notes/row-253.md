=== National Symbols – Flag, Anthem, Song and Emblem
| Symbol | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **National Flag** | Adopted by the Constituent Assembly on **22 July 1947**; ratio **3:2**; **Ashoka Chakra** (navy blue, **24 spokes**) replaced the charkha |
| **National Anthem** – Jana Gana Mana | **Tagore**; Bengali, first of five stanzas of a **Brahmo hymn**; first sung at the **Calcutta INC session, 27 Dec 1911**; adopted **24 Jan 1950** |
| **National Song** – Vande Mataram | **Bankim Chandra Chatterji**; composed **1875**, in **Anandamath (1882)**; first sung by **Tagore** at the **1896 (Calcutta) INC session**; adopted **24 Jan 1950** |
| **State Emblem** | Lion Capital of Ashoka, **Sarnath**; abacus shows **bull, horse, elephant, wheel** – **no camel**; motto **Satyameva Jayate** from the **Mundaka Upanishad** |
| National bird / animal | **Peacock** / **Tiger** (Royal Bengal Tiger) – the lion is on the emblem but is not the national animal |
- **Flag designer = Pingali Venkayya** (born **1876, Bhatlapenumarru**, "**Jhanda Venkayya**") – not Gandhi or Tagore; inspired at the **1906 Calcutta session (Dadabhai Naoroji)**; showed his **1921** tricolour design to **Gandhi at the Bezwada (Vijayawada) AICC session**, and Gandhi added the **charkha**.
- **Vande Mataram first sung at the 1896 session** – by **Tagore**, not Bankim or Sarojini Naidu; Jana Gana Mana first sung **1911** (Calcutta).
- The Constituent Assembly adopted the flag on **22 July 1947** – not 14 Aug 1947 or 22 Jan 1947.
- **Current linkage:** 150 years of Vande Mataram (2025-26); PM's tributes to Pingali Venkayya on his **150th birth anniversary** (PMF Lens, 2 Aug 2026).
> Flag 22 July 1947 (3:2, 24 spokes, Venkayya); anthem + song 24 Jan 1950; Vande Mataram 1896 (Tagore), Jana Gana Mana 1911.

=== Origin of the Idea – Demand for a Constituent Assembly
| Year | Development |
|---|---|
| 1895 | **Swaraj Bill** (Constitution of India Bill) – early idea of a constitution; some sources credit Tilak |
| **1934** | **M.N. Roy** first put forward the idea; the **Swaraj Party** (Ranchi, May 1934) made the first formal demand |
| **1935** | Became an **official INC demand** |
| 1936 | Restated at the Lucknow and **Faizpur** sessions |
| 1938 | **Nehru**: constitution by an Assembly **elected on adult franchise** |
| **1940** | Accepted in principle – **August Offer** |
| 1942 | Cripps proposals conceded an Indian-made constitution |
| **1946** | Set up under the **Cabinet Mission Plan** |
- **First to put forward the idea = M.N. Roy (1934)** (UPSC 2017; APPSC Group-1) – not Nehru, Ambedkar, Tilak or Annie Besant.
- **"First mooted" among parties = the Swaraj Party** (UP UDA 2001: option "Swaraj Party in 1935"; strictly May 1934) – not the Congress (1936), the League or the 1946 conference.
> Roy 1934 → INC 1935 → Nehru 1938 → August Offer 1940 → Cabinet Mission 1946.

=== Composition, Members and Criticism
- Total strength **389** (296 British India + 93 princely states); fell to **299** after the League members from Pakistan areas withdrew.
- Provincial seats divided among **General, Muslim, Sikh**; elected by each community in the provincial assembly by **proportional representation – single transferable vote**; princely states' members **nominated by the rulers**.
- July–Aug 1946 elections: **Congress 208**, **League 73**, others 15.
- **15 women** members (Durgabai Deshmukh, Sarojini Naidu, Hansa Mehta …).
- **Not members: Gandhi and Jinnah**; also **Jayaprakash Narayan** was not a member (CDS 2009) – Patel, Kripalani and K.M. Munshi were.
- **Socialist Party** National Executive (1946; **JP, Lohia**) demanded its **dissolution and re-election by universal adult suffrage** (RPSC 2024).
- **Criticism:** indirectly elected on the **1935 Act's limited franchise** (tax, property, education); "Congress Assembly"; lawyer and Hindu dominance; "Drifting Committee" (Naziruddin Ahmed).
- **Patel on separate electorates:** "Can you show me one free country where there are separate electorates? … The British element is gone, but they have left **mischief** behind" (UPPCS 2021).
# Indian Independence Act, 1947
- Made it a **fully sovereign body**; also the **Dominion Legislature** – chaired by **Rajendra Prasad** as constitution-maker and by **G.V. Mavalankar** as legislature; continued as the **Provisional Parliament till 1952**.
> Not members: Gandhi, Jinnah, JP. Dissolution demand: Socialist Party. "Mischief" quote: Patel.

=== Adoption, Sessions and Commencement
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Adopted by** | the **Constituent Assembly** on **26 November 1949** |
| Came into force | **26 January 1950** (26 Jan = **Purna Swaraj Day**, Lahore Congress 1929) |
| Time taken | **2 years, 11 months, 18 days** |
| **Sessions** | **11** (9 Dec 1946 – 26 Nov 1949) + a final meeting on **24 Jan 1950** to sign |
| Signed | **24 January 1950** – 284 members; Prasad elected first President the same day |
| As adopted | Preamble, 395 Articles, 8 Schedules |
- **Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas) = 26 November** – adoption day, notified **2015** (earlier National Law Day) (APPSC Group-2) – not 26 Jan (Republic Day).
- **Sessions = 11** (Laxmikanth; NextGen gives the UPPSC Mains 2005 answer as 11). Some books count the 24 Jan 1950 meeting and say 12.
> Adopted 26 Nov 1949, in force 26 Jan 1950; 11 sessions; 2 y 11 m 18 d.

=== First Session and the Objectives Resolution
- **First sitting 9 December 1946** (Constitution Hall); **Muslim League boycotted**; **211** members attended (NextGen; Laxmikanth 8th ed. gives 207).
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Moved by | **Jawaharlal Nehru** |
| Moved on | **13 December 1946** |
| Adopted | **22 January 1947** (unanimously) |
| Link | Its modified version is the **Preamble** |
- **Objectives Resolution moved on 13 Dec 1946** (APPSC FSO) – not 26 Nov 1946 or 22 Jan 1947 (adoption).
> 9 Dec first sitting → 11 Dec Prasad → 13 Dec Objectives Resolution → 22 Jan 1947 adopted.
