=== INA (Red Fort) Trials, 1945-46
# Venue, accused, verdict
- **Venue:** **Red Fort, Delhi**; first trial opened **5 November 1945**; about ten court-martials, **November 1945 – May 1946**.
- **First (joint) trial – the accused:** **Prem Kumar Sahgal** (Hindu), **Shah Nawaz Khan** (Muslim), **Gurbaksh Singh Dhillon** (Sikh) – a symbol of unity (UPSC 2021: they are "officers of the INA").
- **Not in the dock:** **Mohan Singh**, Rash Behari Bose (died January 1945), Subhas Bose; Bhulabhai Desai was **counsel**, not an accused.
- **Verdict:** guilty of waging war against the King-Emperor → transportation for life, **remitted by C-in-C Claude Auchinleck (January 1946)**; all three released.
- **Rashid Ali:** INA officer given a **seven-year** sentence → Calcutta upsurge of **11 February 1946**.
- Later lives: Shah Nawaz Khan became a **Union minister**; P.K. Sahgal married **Capt. Lakshmi**.
# INA Defence Committee
- Set up by the **Indian National Congress** (AICC Bombay, September 1945).
- **Lead counsel: Bhulabhai Desai** – his last great contribution; with **Tej Bahadur Sapru, Asaf Ali, K.N. Katju, Jawaharlal Nehru** (also Sarat Chandra Bose).
- **Not** in the defence: **Sardar Patel**, **Jinnah**, C. Rajagopalachari; **C.R. Das** and **Motilal Nehru** were dead by then.
> Exam angle: venue → **Red Fort, 1945**; accused trio; lead counsel → **Bhulabhai Desai**; 7 years → **Rashid Ali**.

=== INA Campaign and the Upsurges of 1945-46
- **Unprecedented campaign:** INA Week **5-11 November 1945**; **INA Day 12 November 1945**; press, meetings, funds; supported by Congress, Muslim League, Communists, Akalis, Justice Party, RSS, Hindu Mahasabha …
- Loyalists, government employees and armed-forces men also sympathised – "an edge of a volcano".

=== Royal Indian Navy Mutiny, February 1946
- **Start:** **18 February 1946**, ratings of **HMIS Talwar**, **Bombay**; on 17 Feb they refused a meal of watery, stone-filled food.
- **Immediate cause:** **bad food and the racial arrogance / abuse of British officers**.
- **Demands:** better food and **living conditions**, civilised treatment, **equal pay** with white sailors, **release of INA prisoners** and political prisoners, withdrawal of Indian troops from Indonesia (Java) and China.
- **Spread:** Karachi, Calcutta, Madras, **Visakhapatnam**, Cochin …; about **20,000** ratings, **78 ships**, **20 shore establishments**; RIAF sympathy strikes.
- **Flags:** Congress tricolour, League green and Communist red hoisted together.
- **Naval Central Strike Committee** – president **M.S. Khan**; **B.C. Dutt** was a Talwar rating (not INA).
- **Support:** Communists and **Aruna Asaf Ali**; Congress and League opposed it; **Patel and Jinnah** persuaded the ratings to **surrender on 23 February 1946**.
- RIN set up **1934**; ratings got freedom-fighter pensions in **1973**; 80th anniversary **18 February 2026**.
> Exam angle: ship → **HMIS Talwar**; reason / immediate demand → **food and treatment**; surrender → **Patel and Jinnah**.

=== Impact on the British Decision; Chronology
- INA trials + RIN mutiny showed the British could **no longer rely on the loyalty of the Indian armed forces** (the 2.5-million Indian Army).
- **Attlee** (to Justice P.V. Chakraborty): the INA and the RIN mutiny, not Quit India, made the British leave; Gandhi's role "minimal".
- RIN mutiny = "last nail in the coffin"; **Cabinet Mission announced 19 February 1946** (a day after), arrived **24 March 1946**.
- **20 February 1947:** Attlee – British to leave by **June 1948**.
| Order (UPSC 2017) | Date |

| Quit India Resolution | 8 August 1942 |
