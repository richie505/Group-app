=== Lahore Session, 1929 – Purna Swaraj
# Run-up
- **Calcutta session (Dec 1928, president Motilal Nehru):** accepted the Nehru Report (Dominion Status) with a **one-year ultimatum** – if Dominion Status was not granted by **31 December 1929**, Congress would adopt **complete independence** and launch civil disobedience.
- **Irwin's (Deepavali) Declaration, 31 Oct 1929:** Dominion Status as the goal, no timeline; promised a Round Table Conference. The **Delhi Manifesto** (2 Nov 1929) demands were rejected in December 1929.
# Session facts
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Session | **44th session, Lahore, 29 Dec 1929 – 1 Jan 1930** |
| President | **Jawaharlal Nehru** – his **first** Congress presidency (backed by Gandhi though most PCCs opposed him); succeeded his father Motilal (1928) |
| Viceroy | **Lord Irwin (1926-31)** |
| Resolution passed | **Purna Swaraj** – at **midnight, 31 December 1929** (not Calcutta 1928, not Karachi 1931) |
| Flag | Nehru **hoisted the newly adopted tricolour at midnight, 31 Dec 1929, on the banks of the Ravi** |
- Nehru declared in his presidential address: "We have now an open conspiracy to free this country from foreign rule"; he **declared the aim** and is credited in most keys with **moving** the Purna Swaraj resolution (some accounts say Gandhi moved it).
# Decisions of the Lahore session
- **Purna Swaraj (complete independence)** declared the goal; the **Nehru Report / Dominion Status** scheme declared lapsed.
- **Round Table Conference to be boycotted.**
- **Congress Working Committee authorised to launch civil disobedience** (including non-payment of taxes); members of legislatures asked to resign.
- **26 January 1930** fixed as the **first Independence (Purna Swaraj) Day**, to be observed every year.
- **Not** a Lahore resolution: **abolition of untouchability**; not the session that launched the NCM (1920) or decided to attend the RTC.
# 26 January 1930
- **First Independence Day:** flag hoisted and the **Independence Pledge** read out all over India ("the inalienable right of the Indian people ... to have freedom"). Drafting: PMF – **Nehru** (issued by the Working Committee); Spectrum – "supposed to have been drafted by **Gandhi**".
- **26 January 1950** was chosen for the Constitution because Congress had celebrated **26 January 1930** as the first Independence Day.
- **Early demand:** **Hasrat Mohani** was the first to demand complete independence from a Congress forum (Ahmedabad, **1921**).
- **AP angle:** **Bulusu Sambamurthy (East Godavari)** got a Purna Swaraj resolution passed at the district level first.
> Lahore 1929 = Jawaharlal Nehru = Purna Swaraj = 31 Dec 1929, flag on the Ravi = 26 January 1930. The "19 December 1929" date seen in some keys is wrong – the session met only from 29 December.

=== Dandi March and the Salt Satyagraha, 1930
# Prelude
- **31 Jan 1930:** Gandhi's **eleven demands** to Viceroy **Irwin** (ultimatum to 11 March); the peasant demand **abolition of the salt tax and the government's salt monopoly**. **2 March 1930:** letter informing Irwin of the Salt March. Irwin refused.
- **Feb 1930:** CWC at Sabarmati gave Gandhi full powers to launch the movement.
# Why salt
- Salt = a **daily necessity of rich and poor alike**; the tax (at times 14 times the value) and the **state monopoly** showed the most oppressive face of British rule.
- Gandhi: the government even **destroyed naturally formed salt** to stop untaxed use – it had no right to destroy what nature produced.
- **Immediate issue:** salt tax / monopoly; **ultimate goal:** **Purna Swaraj**.
# The march
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Start | **12 March 1930** (not 15 March) from **Sabarmati Ashram**, Ahmedabad |
| Marchers | **78 followers** (79 with Gandhi; often rounded to "80"); the largest group (about half) from **Gujarat** |
| Only Telugu marcher | **Yerneni (Erneni) Subrahmanyam** |
| Distance / time | **About 240 miles (~385-390 km)** – not 240 km – in **24 days**, entirely on foot |
| Destination | **Dandi**, a coastal village in **Navsari district**, Gujarat |
| Reached Dandi | **5 April 1930** – "**I want world sympathy in this battle of Right against Might**" (message to the press, 5.4.1930) |
| Salt law broken | **6 April 1930** – Gandhi picked up a lump of natural salt on the beach; the **CDM formally began** |
| Viceroy | **Lord Irwin** |
- **"Sedition has become my religion":** said during the Salt March (**Borsad speech, 18 March 1930** – "I have made sedition my dharma"; UPSC 2025 key: at the time of violating the Salt Law).
- **Subhas Bose** likened the march to **Napoleon's march from Elba to Paris**; the **US press (Time)** followed the march – Time named Gandhi Man of the Year (1931).
- **Arrest:** Gandhi arrested on the night of **4-5 May 1930** at Karadi and lodged in **Yerwada Jail**; successors **Abbas Tyabji** (1st), then **Sarojini Naidu** (2nd).
> The CDM "began with" the Dandi March; Gandhi set out from Sabarmati but **launched** the movement at **Dandi** on 6 April (some keys give Sabarmati for "started the CDM from").

=== Spread of the Movement – Regions and Leaders
| Region / place | Leader / form |
|---|---|
| **Dharasana** (Gujarat) | Abbas Tyabji began it (7 May); **Sarojini Naidu**, Imam Sahib and Manilal led the raid on **21 May 1930** – unarmed beatings reported by US journalist **Webb Miller** (United Press) |
| **Vedaranyam** (Tanjore coast, TN) | **C. Rajagopalachari** – march from **Trichinopoly**, April 1930; **K. Kamaraj** first jailed here |
| Malabar | **K. Kelappan** – Kozhikode to Payyannur; P. Krishna Pillai defended the flag |
| Orissa | **Gopabandhu Choudhury** (Balasore, Cuttack, Puri; Inchudi) |
| Bengal | Satish Chandra Dasgupta (Mahishbathan); Midnapur – salt and chaukidari tax; **Surya Sen** – Chittagong armoury raid, provisional government |
| **Bihar** | Salt-making a mere gesture (land-locked) → **no-chaukidari tax** agitation (e.g. **Begusarai**); **Bihpur (Bhagalpur)** – **Mahadev Lal Sarraf** |
| UP | **No-revenue, no-rent** campaign |
| Assam | Students' protest against the **Cunningham Circular** |
| Karnataka | **Sanikatta** salt works; **Ambabai** picketed at **Udipi** |
| Maharashtra | **Sholapur** textile workers' rising – parallel government, martial law; forest satyagraha (also CP, Karnataka) |
| Manipur / Naga hills | **Rani Gaidinliu** – arrested 1932 |
- After Gandhi's arrest the CWC sanctioned **non-payment of revenue** (ryotwari areas), **no-chaukidari tax** (zamindari areas) and **forest-law violation** (CP).
- **Regional PYQs:** **Charan Paduka massacre** (Chhatarpur, 14 Jan 1931) = "Jallianwala Bagh of Madhya Pradesh"; Chhattisgarh 1930 order: Poorna Swaraj week → Bilaspur flag attempt → Raipur imprisonments → Ravi Shankar Shukla elected Raipur District Council chairman from jail.

=== Khudai Khidmatgars, Peshawar and the Garhwal Rifles
- **Khudai Khidmatgars ("Servants of God") = "Red Shirts"**, founded by **Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan** – "**Frontier Gandhi**" / Badshah Khan – among the **Pathans of the NWFP**; pledged to **non-violence** and the freedom struggle; called for **Pathan regional unity and a struggle against colonialism** (not union with Afghanistan, terror or communism).
- **23 April 1930:** his arrest led to mass demonstrations in **Peshawar** (Qissa Khwani firing); the city was in the crowds' hands for over a week.
- **Peshawar incident:** two platoons of the **Garhwal Rifles** under **Chandra Singh Garhwali** refused to fire on the unarmed crowd – during the **CDM**.

=== Women, Martyrs and Notable Participants
- CDM = **first nationalist movement with women in large numbers** (picketing liquor and foreign-cloth shops); "maximum women's participation" in keys = **Salt Satyagraha**. **Kamaladevi Chattopadhyay** persuaded Gandhi to admit women.
| Person | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Kamaladevi Chattopadhyay** | Led women into the Salt Satyagraha (Bombay Chowpatty) |
| **Kasturba Gandhi** | Protests; imprisoned |
| **Sarojini Naidu** | Led the Dharasana raid; **first Indian woman INC president (Kanpur 1925)** |
| **Aruna Asaf Ali** | Hoisted the flag at Gowalia Tank – **Quit India 1942**, not CDM |
| **Ambabai** | Karnataka (Mysore) – picketing at Udipi |
| **Prabhavati Devi** | Bihar – **Patna** |
| **Rama Devi** | Rajasthan – Bijolia peasant movement; jailed in the salt satyagraha (1930) and the 1932 CDM |
| **Dr. Radhabai** | Arrested **13 June 1932** leading a procession (second CDM) |
| **Duvvuri Subbamma** | Andhra (East Godavari) woman leader jailed in the CDM |
| **Paturi Balasaraswatamma** | First woman arrested in **Nellore** |
- **Babu Genu** – CDM martyr: crushed by a lorry carrying foreign cloth, **Bombay, 12 Dec 1930**. **Khudiram Bose, Prafulla Chaki** (Muzaffarpur, 1908, target Kingsford) and the **Chapekars** (Rand, Pune, 1897) belong to the earlier revolutionary phase.
- **Vinoba Bhave** – first arrested in **1923 (Nagpur Flag Satyagraha)** in his own words; many keys wrongly say the CDM.
- Andhra: **"Kattera Udyamam"** raised women's participation.

=== Chronology and Phases of the CDM
| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Chauri Chaura; NCM withdrawn | Feb 1922 |
| Surat split | 1907 |
| Simon Commission appointed / arrives | Nov 1927 / **3 Feb 1928** |
| Nehru Report; All Parties Convention; Calcutta ultimatum | 1928 |
| Purna Swaraj, Lahore | **31 Dec 1929** |
| First Independence Day | **26 Jan 1930** |
| Dandi March | **12 Mar – 5 Apr 1930**; salt law broken **6 Apr** |
| First RTC (Congress boycotted) | Nov 1930 – Jan 1931 |
| Chandra Shekhar Azad's martyrdom | 27 Feb 1931 |
| Gandhi-Irwin (Delhi) Pact – CDM suspended | **5 Mar 1931** |
| Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Rajguru executed / Karachi session | 23 Mar / 29-31 Mar 1931 |
| Second RTC | Sept – Dec 1931 |
| CDM resumed | 4 Jan 1932 (Willingdon's repression) |
| Communal Award / Poona Pact | 16 Aug / 24 Sept 1932 |
| CDM suspended / withdrawn | 14 July 1933 / **1934** |
| Cripps Mission / Quit India | 1942 |
- **Phases:** first **1930 – March 1931** (ended by the Gandhi-Irwin Pact); second **1932-34**, crushed by **repression**; withdrawn 1934.
- **Significance:** first movement to **break laws openly on a mass scale**; drew women, peasants, merchants and tribals; brought the **NWFP** in; revolutionary activity remained separate.
- NCM began **1920**, CDM **1930**, Quit India **1942**.
