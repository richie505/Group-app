=== Return to India (1915) and First Steps
- Sailed from Cape Town **19 July 1914**; landed at **Bombay (Apollo Bunder) on 9 January 1915** → observed as **Pravasi Bharatiya Divas (NRI Day)**.
- Year of return = **1915** (not 1914/1916/1917).
- Age on return: born 2 Oct 1869 → strictly **45**; PMF and many books say "**at the age of 46**".
- **Gopal Krishna Gokhale**, his **political guru**, advised him to spend **one year touring and observing India** before entering politics.
- Received the **Kaisar-i-Hind gold medal (26 June 1915, Lord Hardinge)** for ambulance work in South Africa.
- **May 1915:** Satyagraha Ashram at **Kochrab**, Ahmedabad.
# BHU speech (February 1916) – first major public appearance
- Charged the **Indian elite with lack of concern for the labouring poor**.
- Said India's **salvation lies with the farmers**, not lawyers, doctors or landlords.
- Did **not** take up untouchables or the Ahmedabad mill owners in that speech → statements I and II only (CDS 2017).

=== Early Life and the South African Phase (1893-1914)
- Born **2 October 1869, Porbandar**; father **Karamchand** (diwan); mother **Putlibai**.
- Early education at **Rajkot**; married **Kasturba at 13**; **Samaldas College, Bhavnagar**; law at the **Inner Temple, London (1888-91)**; practice at **Rajkot and Bombay** failed.
- **1893:** went to South Africa for **Dada Abdullah's** case; stayed **1893-1914 (about 21 years)**.
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **7 June 1893** | Thrown out of a first-class compartment at **Pietermaritzburg** (on the way to Pretoria) |
| 1894 | **Natal Indian Congress** |
| 1896-1902 | **Seva Dharma**: compounder in **Rustomji's charity hospital**; Ambulance Corps in the Boer War |
| 1903 | Indian Opinion; law office at Johannesburg |
| **11 Sept 1906** | **First satyagraha** – against the **Asiatic Registration Ordinance ("Black Act")**, Transvaal |
| 1908 | **First jail term** (January); burning of registration certificates |
| 1913 | **Great March / Volkrust satyagraha** – against the **£3 poll tax** and invalidation of Indian marriages |
| 1914 | **Indian Relief Act** – poll tax abolished, Indian marriages recognised |
- South Africa = the **'laboratory'** where Gandhi forged satyagraha (not England or India); "the making of the Mahatma".
- Lessons (Spectrum, PMF): he found the **masses had immense capacity to participate in and sacrifice for a cause** → the struggles **prepared him to lead the national movement** (UP RO/ARO 2023 A-R: A and R true, R explains A).
> First satyagraha = **1906 Transvaal**; first in India = **Champaran 1917**.
~~ Sources: Spectrum (Ahir) printed p.312-316; PMF Modern History Part 4 p.111-116; NextGen Rocket Sheet #100 p.54; old app notes (Samaldas, Rustomji)

=== Hind Swaraj (1909)
- Written by **Gandhi** in **1909**, in **Gujarati**, aboard the **Kildonan Castle** (London → South Africa).
- **Banned** in 1910 (Bombay Government); **English version by Gandhi himself**.
- Swaraj = **self-rule**; against modern Western civilisation and industrialisation (railways, lawyers, doctors).
- Called the British Parliament **"sterile" and a "prostitute"**.
- Argued that the British did not take India – **Indians gave it to them**: British rule was established with **Indians' cooperation**.
> Not by Tilak, Gokhale, Ranade or Vinoba; not written in Hindi or English.

=== Influences – Book and Author
| Book / source | Author | What Gandhi took |
|---|---|---|
| **Unto This Last** | **John Ruskin** | "**The good of the individual is contained in the good of all**"; dignity of labour; Gujarati paraphrase titled **'Sarvodaya'** |
| **Civil Disobedience** | **H.D. Thoreau** | Right to break unjust laws |
| **The Kingdom of God is Within You** | **Leo Tolstoy** | Love and non-violent resistance |
| Bhagavad Gita | — | **Nishkama karma** |
| Jainism (Raychandbhai) | — | Five vows: **ahimsa, satya, asteya, aparigraha, brahmacharya** |
- Copy of Unto This Last given by **Henry Polak (1904)**; it influenced him most – "transformed his life".
- "Three moderns": **Raychandbhai** (living contact), **Tolstoy**, **Ruskin** → Gandhian thought influenced by **Ruskin, Thoreau and Tolstoy – all**.
- Jain vows: first four from **Parshvanatha**; **brahmacharya added by Mahavira**.
~~ Sources: PMF Modern History Part 4 p.122-123; Spectrum (Ahir) printed p.314; old app notes (Gita, Jain vows)
