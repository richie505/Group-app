=== The Andhra (Kurnool) Circular
| Particular | Fact |
|---|---|
| Issued by | **Andhra Provincial Congress Committee** (state Congress leaders) |
| Date | **29 July 1942** – before the Bombay AICC of 8 August |
| Drafted by | **Kala Venkata Rao** (APPSC G2 2019; G2 Mains) – sent to the CWC through **Pattabhi Sitaramayya** |
| Name | Police seized a copy in a raid on the **Kurnool** Congress office → **"Kurnool Circular"** |
| Movement | **Quit India Movement** – not Vandemataram, Salt Satyagraha or Non-Cooperation |
- **⚠ Trap:** author = **Kala Venkata Rao** – not Pattabhi Sitaramayya, Ayyadevara Kaleswara Rao or Tanguturi Prakasam.
- **Aim:** "to paralyse all means of communications and machinery of administration".
- **Programme:** defy prohibitory orders; lawyers give up practice, students leave colleges; **picketing** salt, foreign trade and industry; cutting toddy trees; **travelling without tickets**, pulling chains; blowing up bridges, cutting telegraph and telephone wires; no taxes except municipal; national flags on government buildings.
  - **NOT in it:** **raids on British military camps** (APPSC Royalty Inspector key).
- **APCC meeting at Machilipatnam** under **Pattabhi Sitaramayya**.

=== Places and Incidents in Andhra
| Place | District | Event |
|---|---|---|
| **Tenali** | Guntur | **12 Aug 1942**: hartal; crowd set fire to the **railway station** (booking office, North Cabin) and the **Madras-Puri passenger train**; police firing – about **9 dead** (NextGen; local accounts: 3 at the station, 7 martyrs in all) |
| **Guntur** | Guntur | Firing **13 Aug** on students near Hindu College |
| **Bhimavaram** | West Godavari | Firing **17 Aug**; Collectorate attacked |
| **Bapatla** | Guntur (now Bapatla) | Railway station attacked and burnt |
| **Chirala** | then Guntur | **12 Aug**: 500 students marched on the Sub-Magistrate's court; signal wires cut |
| Tadepalligudem | West Godavari | Post office burnt |
| Kakinada | East Godavari | Lawyers demanded Gandhi's release |
- **Firing order:** **Tenali 12 → Guntur 13 → Bhimavaram 17 August 1942**.
- **Railway station burnt in 1942:** keys give **Tenali** (APPSC G2-A 1984); NextGen's previous-papers list gives **Bapatla** – both stations were attacked.
- **⚠ Trap:** the **Tenali Bomb Case (April 1909)** belongs to the **Vandemataram movement**, not Quit India.
- Gandhi was held at the **Aga Khan Palace** (not Chowmahalla or Falaknuma) for **21 months** (Aug 1942 – May 1944).

=== Background and the Communists
# Before 1942
- **Congress ministries resigned** in **October-November 1939** over the war → limited **Individual Satyagraha** (17 Oct 1940): **Vinoba Bhave first**, **Nehru second**; organiser in Andhra **Vavilala Gopalakrishnaiah**.
- **Kasturba** and **Mahadev Desai** died in detention; their samadhis are at the Aga Khan Palace.
# Communists vs Congress Socialists
- **CPI**: ban lifted **23 July 1942** → backed the British war effort ("People's War") and **opposed Quit India**; lost public support.
- **Third Communist Conference, Vijayawada, 1943** – the first open Communist conference in Andhra; secretary **Chandra Rajeswara Rao**.
- Price paid: heavy defeats in **1945-46** – only **Pillalamarri Venkateswara Rao** won from Andhra in 1945.
- **Congress Socialists** led the national underground: **JP, Lohia, Aruna Asaf Ali, Usha Mehta**.
