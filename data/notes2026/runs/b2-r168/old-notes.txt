## SUB 4 | Women's Safety and Support
[168:4:0] - **Disha app** (8 Feb 2020, Rajamahendravaram): **SOS** button alerts the police control room; first **Disha police station** opened the same day at **Rajamahendravaram** (18 planned). In 2025 the app was replaced by **Suraksha**.
[168:4:1] - **AP Disha Bill, 2019**: **investigation 7 days + trial 14 days = 21 days** for sexual offences against women; amended IPC and CrPC; **withdrawn** as **BNS and BNSS** replaced IPC/CrPC from **1 July 2024** (not 2023).
[168:4:2] - **One Stop Centres**: police, medical, legal, counselling and shelter help under **one roof**; part of MWCD's **Mission Shakti** (with Shakti Sadan); backed by **Women Helpline 181** (universalised **2019**, not 2024).
