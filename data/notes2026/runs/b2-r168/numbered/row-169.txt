## SUB 0 | E-governance and Real Time Governance
[0:0] # Real Time Governance (RTGS)
[0:1] - **RTGS**: e-governance tools for **maximum governance with minimum government**, with **real-time performance indicators**; command centre at the **Secretariat** watching services and complaints live – the initiative **first launched in Andhra Pradesh**.
[0:2] - Motto: **"People First"** (People First 1100 call centre takes citizens' feedback).
[0:3] - Uses the **Hitachi Visualization Suite** – disaster plans and other real-time data on a **single pane of glass** for timely decisions.
[0:4] - **CORE dashboard** = **CM Office Real-time Executive** dashboard tracking departments' indicators.

## SUB 1 | Agriculture and Groundwater Programmes
[1:0] # Programmes
[1:1] TABLE head: Programme | Year | Purpose / benefit
  [1:1.0] Krishi Cabinet | June 2015 | Cabinet sub-committee steering the Primary Sector Mission
  [1:1.1] APCNF | Since 2016 | Natural farming run by Rythu Sadhikara Samstha (RySS)
  [1:1.2] Rythu Bharosa Kendras (RBKs) | 2020 | Village one-stop centres for seeds, fertilisers, advisory, procurement
  [1:1.3] YSR Jala Kala | 2020 | Free borewells for small and marginal farmers – single or group farmers with 2.5 acres; a policy against irrigation water shortage
  [1:1.4] YSR Zero Interest Crop Loans | 20 April 2021 | Crop loans up to Rs 1 lakh repaid on time become interest-free
  [1:1.5] e-Crop | – | Digital booking of the crop on every field each season
[1:2] - **CCRC** (AP Crop Cultivators Rights Act, 2019): tenants get access to **loans, insurance and input subsidy**; **no ownership title**.
[1:3] - **PMFBY:** farmer premium **2% kharif, 1.5% rabi**, 5% commercial; **voluntary for all since Kharif 2020**.
[1:4] # APFAMGS (Farmer Managed Groundwater Systems)
[1:5] - **FAO**-supported (2004-09), 7 drought-prone districts: farmers measure and manage groundwater **demand** themselves – **demand-side** management.
[1:6] - Principle: **rules are set by the users** (farmer groups), not by outsiders.
[1:7] - **AP Integrated Irrigation and Agriculture Transformation Project** – **World Bank** (USD 172.2 million, 2018).
[1:8] > Exam angle: Jala Kala = free borewells (asked twice); APFAMGS demand side, user-made rules.
