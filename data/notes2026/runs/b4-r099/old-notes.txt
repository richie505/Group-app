## SUB 0 | Classification of Energy Sources
[99:0:0] TABLE head: Class | Sources | Key point
  [99:0:0.0] Renewable (replenished naturally, short period) | Solar, wind, hydro, geothermal, tidal, wave, biomass, biogas | Basis of the energy transition
  [99:0:0.1] Non-renewable (finite) | Fossil fuels (coal, petroleum, natural gas) + nuclear fuel (uranium, thorium) | Fossil fuels formed over millions of years; burning gives CO2
[99:0:1] - **Uranium** = non-renewable nuclear fuel, **NOT a fossil fuel**. **Wood** is not a fossil fuel. Natural gas is **not renewable**.
[99:0:2] - First source of fossil-fuel energy = **the Sun** (energy stored in buried organisms).
[99:0:3] - **Petroleum**: non-renewable, from ancient marine organisms; gives **plastic toothbrushes and gasoline**.
[99:0:4] - **Petroleum products**: petrol, diesel, kerosene, **LPG, naphtha, bitumen**, petrochemical feedstock. **Anthracite** is coal, not a petroleum product.
[99:0:5] - **Commercial** sources: power, coal, oil, gas, hydro-electricity, uranium. **Biogas = non-commercial**.
[99:0:6] - Non-conventional energy = renewable energy; biomass = renewable organic material.
[99:0:7] - APPSC 2025 keys: solar = not a fossil fuel; wind = renewable; **solar + wind = energy-transition sources**; thermal plants burn fossil fuels; solar plants use radiation, not coal.
[99:0:8] - Fossil fuels: ~**75% of global GHGs, ~90% of CO2**; subsidies $1.8 trillion (2024). Largest SO2 source: fossil-fuel power plants (73%).
[99:0:9] - **First oil crisis: 1973** (OPEC raised oil price four times). Photovoltaic = solar to electricity; National Solar Mission under **NAPCC**.
[99:0:10] - Earth's resources include water, minerals, fish stocks, fossil fuels.
