=== NOTES 4:48 | Andhra Pradesh Drainage – Godavari, Krishna, Penna, Vamsadhara, Nagavali; Reservoirs and Canals
## SUB 0 | Ap Drainage at A Glance
[48:0:0] - AP has about **40 rivers** (major, medium and minor); **15 are inter-state** (2024-25).
[48:0:1] - About **75%** of AP's area lies in the **Godavari, Krishna and Pennar** basins.
[48:0:2] - **Largest basin area in AP = Godavari**; Godavari is also the **2nd-largest basin of India** (~10% of India) after the **Ganga**.
[48:0:3] - Sharing framework: **Krishna and Godavari** waters by **tribunal awards**; **Bahuda, Vamsadhara, Nagavali, Pennar, Palar, Ponniar** by **inter-state agreements**.

[48:0:6] Read the stem: "longest river flowing in AP" = Godavari; "longest course within AP" = Penna.

[48:0:10] - **Narmada and Cauvery** basins are about the same size.
[48:0:11] - Length order: **Godavari 1,465 km > Krishna ~1,400 km > Penna 597 km > Vamsadhara 254 km**.

## SUB 2 | Godavari System
[48:2:0] - **Left-bank** tributaries: **Purna, Pranhita, Indravati, Sabari**; **right-bank**: **Pravara, Manjira, Manair**.
[48:2:1] - **Pranhita** = largest tributary by drainage area.
[48:2:2] - Joining in AP: **Sabari** (with its tributary **Sileru**), **Sileru**, and upstream Pranhita and Indravati (Telangana–Chhattisgarh reach).
[48:2:3] - 2025: Godavari stretch near **Rajamahendravaram** named among India's **most polluted** river stretches (untreated factory and sewage waste).
[48:2:4] - **Godavari floods, Jul–Aug 2026**: crossed the **3rd warning level at Bhadrachalam**; ~24,000 Koya and Konda Reddi people hit in Polavaram district; 150 relief camps; **5.2 lakh cusecs** at Dowleswaram; ~**521 tmc ft** went to sea by 10 Aug 2026.
## SUB 3 | Krishna System
[48:3:0] - Basin States: **Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana, AP**.
[48:3:1] - **Right bank**: Koyna, Ghataprabha, Malaprabha, **Tungabhadra** (largest tributary).
[48:3:2] - **Left bank**: **Bhima** (longest tributary), Dindi, **Musi**, Paleru, **Munneru**.
[48:3:3] - **Tungabhadra**: from **Varaha hills**; joins Krishna at **Sangameswaram (Kurnool)**; forms the **Karnataka–AP boundary for 58 km**.
[48:3:4] - **Hagari** = biggest tributary of the Tungabhadra; **Vedavathi** (Veda + Avathi) is in this system.
[48:3:5] - **Raichur Doab** = between **Krishna and Tungabhadra**.
[48:3:6] - **Vijayawada** is on the Krishna. **Basava Sagar Dam** is on the Krishna.
[48:3:7] Tungabhadra joins Krishna at Sangameswaram, not Antarvedi (Godavari mouth).

[48:4:1] - **Gandikota Fort** stands on the banks of the Pennar; Gandikota is one of 7 tourism anchor hubs (2025-26).

## SUB 5 | North-coastal and Minor Rivers
[48:5:0] - **Vamsadhara**: **biggest river born in the Eastern Ghats**; enters AP at **Pathapatnam (Srikakulam)**; flows between the **Rushikulya and Godavari** basins; tributary **Mahendratanaya** (Mahendragiri hills; shared Odisha–AP).

[48:5:6] - **Tammileru**: boundary of Krishna and West Godavari; drains into **Kolleru Lake**; Tammileru Dam.
[48:5:7] - **Kolleru feeders:** **Budameru** and **Tammileru** drain into it; the **Krishna** does not feed it directly.
[48:5:8] - **Mahendra Tanaya and Bahuda** are shared by AP and Odisha.

## SUB 8 | Polavaram
[48:8:0] - Multipurpose **earth-cum-rockfill** dam on the **Godavari**; **National Project** (central funding) under **Section 90, AP Reorganisation Act 2014**.
[48:8:1] - Design: **194.6 TMC** storage (FRL 45.72 m), **7.20 lakh acres**, **960 MW**, **80 TMC** diverted to the Krishna; drinking water.
[48:8:2] - **Seven Khammam mandals** transferred to AP, so the submerged area lies in AP.
[48:8:3] - First planned as **Ramapada Sagar** by **Dr. K.L. Rao**; early cost estimate **Rs 6,400 crore**; faced environmental-clearance and resettlement problems.
[48:8:4] - Left Main Canal → **North Coastal Andhra and Visakhapatnam**; 27 TMC for Visakhapatnam incl. 5 TMC to Kanithi (Sep 2026); Godavari water reached North Coastal Andhra by pumps (2 Sep 2026).
[48:8:5] - Progress: **78.64% (Dec 2024)** → **89% (2026)**; to be dedicated by **March 2027**, before the **Godavari Pushkaralu 2027**.
[48:8:6] National Project status is Section **90**, not Section 89.
