=== Basins, Rankings and Inter-state Rivers
- **Count:** AP has **about 40 major, medium and minor rivers**, of which **15 are inter-state** (Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25); records also speak of **40 river basins**.
- **Big three:** about **75%** of AP lies in the **Godavari, Krishna and Pennar** basins.
- **Sharing:** **Krishna and Godavari** by **tribunal awards**; **Bahuda, Vamsadhara, Nagavali, Pennar, Palar, Ponniar** by **inter-state agreements**.

- **Longest river flowing in AP = Godavari** (1,465 km; "longest and broadest river of South India", AP's lifeline); **longest course inside AP = Penna** (536 km).
- **Largest basin in AP:** keys answer **Godavari**; strictly, by area inside present AP the **Pennar** is largest (**48,276 sq km** of its 55,213 sq km) – the Godavari's AP share is only ~4.5% of 312,812 sq km, most having gone to Telangana in 2014.
- **Undivided AP's Godavari basin:** **73,201 sq km** (about 23% of the basin) – a pre-2014 figure.
- **Godavari = longest river of Peninsular India** and, in books and keys, the **2nd-largest basin of India after the Ganga** (~9.5–10% of India); strictly the Indian part of the **Indus** basin (321,289 sq km) is a little larger.
- **Peninsular order:** basin Godavari > Krishna > Mahanadi; length **Godavari 1,465 > Krishna ~1,400 > Narmada ~1,312 > Cauvery ~800**; Penna 597 > Vamsadhara 254.
- **India's basins:** **20 = 12 major** (over 20,000 sq km) **+ 8 composite**; the **Kaveri** basin has the highest water utilisation (CWC), mostly for irrigation in Karnataka and Tamil Nadu.
- **Irrigation lifeline:** the **Krishna** is the classic answer for "major irrigation source of Maharashtra, Telangana and AP" (the Godavari also crosses all three).
- **Penna districts:** Anantapur → YSR Kadapa → SPSR Nellore (**not Chittoor**).
> Read the stem: "longest river flowing in AP" = Godavari; "longest course within AP" = Penna; "largest basin" = Godavari in keys.

=== Polavaram, Lift Schemes, Canals and River Links
# Polavaram
- **Site:** multipurpose **earth-cum-rockfill** dam on the **Godavari** at **Polavaram** village (erstwhile **West Godavari**, now Eluru), **42 km upstream of the Cotton Barrage**; contemplated 1941, begun 2005 (Jalayagnam).
- **Name:** first planned as **Ramapada Sagar** by **Dr. K.L. Rao**.
- **Status:** **National Project** under **Section 90**, AP Reorganisation Act 2014; **seven Khammam mandals** transferred to AP to facilitate it.
- **Design:** gross storage **194.6 TMC** (FRL 45.72 m); **live storage ~75.2 TMC ≈ 2,130 Mm³**; **7.20 lakh acres**; **960 MW**; **80 TMC Godavari → Krishna** (the Godavari–Krishna link of the ILR, not Krishna → Godavari); 23.44 TMC for Visakhapatnam; reservoir spreads into **Odisha and Chhattisgarh**.
- **Left Main Canal** → **North Coastal Andhra and Visakhapatnam** (5 TMC to Kanithi, Sep 2026); **Right Main Canal** → Krishna at Vijayawada.
- **Progress:** 53.47% overall, works 77% (Dec 2024, SES); 89% (2026); to be dedicated by **March 2027**, before the **Godavari Pushkaralu 2027**.

# Telugu Ganga
- **Route:** Krishna water from **Srisailam** → **Pothireddypadu head regulator** at the start of the **Srisailam Right Main Canal** → Somasila (Penna) → **Kandaleru** → **Poondi (TN)**: a **Krishna–Pennar** link.
- **Purpose:** **drinking water to Chennai (Madras)**, plus irrigation in **Kurnool, Kadapa, Nellore and Chittoor** (Rayalaseema and Nellore, not Nellore alone); 10 tmc ft for Chennai (2026).
- **Partners:** conceived by **N.T. Rama Rao**; agreement of **Tamil Nadu** with the Krishna States **AP, Maharashtra, Karnataka** (**5 tmc each = 15 tmc**) – an example of **AP–Tamil Nadu** cooperation.

=== Tributaries of AP's Rivers
# Godavari
- **Left bank:** Purna, **Pranhita** (largest tributary = Wainganga + Wardha + Penganga; Maharashtra–Telangana boundary, joins at Kaleshwaram in Telangana – **not an AP river**), **Indravati**, **Sabari**.
- **Right bank:** Pravara, **Manjira**, Manair; **Kinnerasani** joins in the Bhadrachalam reach.
- **Sabari** (Kolab), from the Sinkaram hills, Odisha, is the main tributary inside AP; its tributary **Sileru** (upper reach = Machkund) joins at the AP–Chhattisgarh–Odisha tri-junction.
> UPSC 2015: of Vamsadhara, Indravati, Pranhita, Pennar, only **Indravati and Pranhita** are Godavari tributaries.
# Krishna
- **Right bank:** Koyna, **Panchganga**, **Ghataprabha, Malaprabha**, **Tungabhadra** – the **largest by drainage area** ("biggest and most important").
- **Left bank:** **Bhima** (longest), Dindi, **Musi**, **Paleru, Munneru** – Paleru and Munneru are the Krishna tributaries inside AP.
- **Tungabhadra:** joins the Krishna at **Sangameswaram (Kurnool)**; Karnataka–AP boundary for **58 km**; biggest tributary **Hagari = Vedavathi** (joins near Siruguppa).
- Not Krishna tributaries: Wardha (Godavari), Chitravathi and Jayamangali (Penna).
