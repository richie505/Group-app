## 4. Key decisions
- **y31ea4047d58** (CSE 2003, behaviour within the rights and duties of a status): the answer is **role**, which is not among the options. Added "Role" as an option; the printed "Designation" is wrong. Moved to row 114.
- **y77e08edbd73** (UPPCS 2004, second largest bench): keyed Golaknath and added a dispute line, because R.C. Cooper (1970) and T.M.A. Pai (2002) also sat 11 judges.
- **y5626c9fcacb** (Panchayat Secretary 2017, no key): keyed "2 only". The duties are non-justiciable, so "mandatory on all citizens" is false.
- **y85056d75487** (Swaran Singh, casteism): keyed "Only I". No source lists a casteism duty, and none is in Art. 51A.
- **y47f5b590ae1** (primacy of DPSPs): kept the 42nd Amendment. The explanation adds that the 25th gave only Art. 39(b)-(c) laws limited primacy.
- **y6090d64daa1** (ATWO, cultural diversity): composite culture 51A(f) over brotherhood 51A(e) (ambiguous stem).
