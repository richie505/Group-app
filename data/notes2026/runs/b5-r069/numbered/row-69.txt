## SUB 0 | Cells and Batteries – Basics and Redox
[0:0] # How a cell works
[0:1] - **Cell = chemical energy → electrical energy** (spontaneous **redox** reaction; a galvanic cell); not a motor, generator, heater or fan.
[0:2] - **Battery:** **two or more cells** joined by opposite terminals (series); everyday word for any cell pack.
[0:3] - **Oxidation = loss of electrons** (at the **anode**); **reduction = gain of electrons** (at the **cathode**) during discharge.
[0:4] - **Electrolyte** lets **ions** flow between electrodes and keeps them apart.
[0:5] - A dry cell works on a **spontaneous oxidation–reduction** reaction; **electrolysis** is the reverse (needs outside energy).
[0:6] - **Charging needs DC:** AC is **not preferable to charge a storage battery** (a rectifier converts AC → DC); AC is fine for motors, toasters and transmission.
[0:7] - **Battery acid** (dilute H2SO4) has a pH **near 0–1** – the most acidic of pure water, saline water, milk.
[0:8] > Recall angle (RAS 2013, RRB 2018, SSC 2017 ×2, APPSC 2018): device converting chemical → electrical = **cell / battery**; oxidation = **loss of electrons**.
[0:9] ~~ Sources: Rocket Physics p.33, p.40; Rocket Chemistry p.21; PMF S&T Part 8 p.61; app notes 5:69 sub 0
## SUB 1 | Common Batteries – Dry Cell, Lead-acid, Ni-Cd
[1:0] TABLE head: Battery | Electrodes / electrolyte | Rechargeable? | Uses
  [1:0.0] Dry (Leclanché, zinc-carbon) cell | Zinc case (anode), carbon rod + MnO2 (cathode); paste of NH4Cl + ZnCl2 | No – reaction irreversible | Torches, clocks, remotes
  [1:0.1] Lead-acid (car battery) | Lead and PbO2 (lead peroxide); sulphuric acid | Yes | Car starting, UPS; 12 V, capacity in ampere-hour

[1:1] - **Dry cell A–R (UP 2004):** "cannot be charged" ✓; "has a reversible reaction" ✗ → A true, R false.
[1:2] - **Car battery (IAS 1998):** 12 V ✓, ampere-hour ✓; hydrochloric acid ✗, lead and copper electrodes ✗ → **I and IV**.
[1:3] - **Traditional battery chemical (SSC CGL 2019):** **sulphuric acid** – not ethylene glycol, sodium bicarbonate, ethanol.
[1:4] > Dry cell electrolytes asked twice (IAS 2009, Jharkhand 2010): **ammonium chloride + zinc chloride**. Ni-Cd electrodes asked twice (IAS 1995, UK 2016).

## SUB 2 | Lithium-ion and New Battery Chemistries
[2:0] - **Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2019:** **development of lithium-ion batteries** (Goodenough, Whittingham, Yoshino) – APPSC JL 2020.
[2:1] - **Li-ion:** Li⁺ ions move **anode → cathode on discharge**; **anode = graphite**; cathode = lithium metal oxide (**LiCoO2, LiFePO4/LFP, NMC**); lithium salt in organic solvent; efficiency ~90–95%.
[2:2] - **Mobile phone batteries:** **lithium** (CDS 2015) – not copper, zinc, nickel.
[2:3] - **EV cathodes (UPSC 2025 ×2):** **cobalt, lithium, nickel** = cathode; **graphite = anode** → **only three**.
[2:4] - **ISRO Li-ion technology:** ISRO developed space-grade **lithium-ion cells** and offered the technology for transfer to industry (APPSC 2017 question).
[2:5] TABLE head: Chemistry | Key point
  [2:5.0] LFP (Li-ion) | Safe, long life
  [2:5.1] Sodium-ion | Abundant, cheaper, no cobalt/nickel, safer; lower energy density than Li-ion/LFP; grid storage
  [2:5.2] Solid-state | Solid electrolyte; higher density, safer; emerging EVs
  [2:5.3] Flow (vanadium redox) | Liquid electrolytes in external tanks; energy and power scale separately; grid-scale, long duration
[2:6] - Sodium-ion is a safer, **mineral-light** alternative, not a replacement for pumped storage.

## SUB 7 | Grid Storage – BESS, Pumped, Mechanical and Thermal
[7:0] - Storage is the **bottleneck** for RE: balances variable output, supports **24x7 RE**.
[7:1] - Need **336 GWh by 2029-30** (CEA) vs ~**500 MWh** BESS installed (Sept 2025).
[7:2] - **Energy Storage Obligation** (**Ministry of Power**, 2022): **1% (2023-24) → 4% (2029-30)**.
[7:3] - Framework: ESS a power-system element; **ISTS waiver** for co-located storage; consumers may own/lease – all are BESS measures.
[7:4] - **Draft National Electricity Policy 2026** (BESS, smart grids, ToD tariffs) replaces NEP **2005**.
[7:5] - **Khavda, Gujarat** (Adani Green): world's largest single-location BESS outside China (May 2026).
[7:6] TABLE head: Method | Principle
  [7:6.0] Pumped storage | Pump water uphill at low demand; release at peak (70–80% efficient)
  [7:6.1] CAES | Air compressed into caverns, expanded through turbines
  [7:6.2] Flywheel | Rotational kinetic energy
  [7:6.3] Molten salt | Thermal storage in CSP; power after sunset
[7:7] - **Tehri PSP** (Uttarakhand): India's first **variable-speed** PSP, 4 × 250 MW.
