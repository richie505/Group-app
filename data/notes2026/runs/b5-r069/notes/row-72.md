=== Solar Parks – Concepts and the Largest Parks
- **Solar Park Scheme** (MNRE, **12 December 2014**): at least 25 parks / ultra mega projects; CFA **₹25 lakh per park** for DPR + up to **₹20 lakh/MW or 30%** of cost.
- **Ultra Mega Solar Park = 500 MW or more** at one location – not 1,000 MW.
- **Largest solar park of India (2023):** **Bhadla Solar Park, Rajasthan** (~2,245 MW) – ahead of Pavagada (Karnataka, ~2,050 MW), Kurnool and Ananthapuram (APPSC DEO 2023; APPSC FBO 2025).
- **Kurnool Ultra Mega Solar Park (1,000 MW):** once the **world's largest single-location solar park** (2017).
- **Floating solar:** India ~0.7 GW vs **102 GW** potential; largest = **Omkareshwar** (278 MW, Narmada, **Madhya Pradesh**).
> Flashcard (APPSC FBO): "Ultra mega = 1,000 MW" ✗; "Kurnool = world's largest single-location park" ✓ → **only b**.

=== Installed Capacity, Potential and Ranking
- AP RE capacity **10,639.75 MW** = **solar 5,614.52 MW (~53%) + wind 4,397.67 MW (~41%)**; added **3,865.63 MW** in 2025-26.
- **State ranking (31 Mar 2026):** AP total RE incl. large hydro **15,980 MW** (~**5.8%** of India's 274.7 GW) = **6th**; **Gujarat** leads total RE and wind, **Rajasthan** leads solar.
- AP is also 6th in solar (7,494.8 MW) and wind (4,415.8 MW).
- **Potential:** solar **38.44 GWp** (**NISE**); wind **74.9 GW at 120 m**, **123.33 GW at 150 m** (**NIWE**).
- After **bifurcation AP lost its coal mines** → long coal haulage, poor coal quality.
