=== Cells and Batteries – Basics and Redox
# How a cell works
- **Cell = chemical energy → electrical energy** (spontaneous **redox** reaction; a galvanic cell); not a motor, generator, heater or fan.
- **Battery:** **two or more cells** joined by opposite terminals (series); everyday word for any cell pack.
- **Oxidation = loss of electrons** (at the **anode**); **reduction = gain of electrons** (at the **cathode**) during discharge.
- **Electrolyte** lets **ions** flow between electrodes and keeps them apart.
- A dry cell works on a **spontaneous oxidation–reduction** reaction; **electrolysis** is the reverse (needs outside energy).
- **Charging needs DC:** AC is **not preferable to charge a storage battery** (a rectifier converts AC → DC); AC is fine for motors, toasters and transmission.
- **Battery acid** (dilute H2SO4) has a pH **near 0–1** – the most acidic of pure water, saline water, milk.
> Recall angle (RAS 2013, RRB 2018, SSC 2017 ×2, APPSC 2018): device converting chemical → electrical = **cell / battery**; oxidation = **loss of electrons**.

=== Common Batteries – Dry Cell, Lead-acid, Ni-Cd
| Battery | Electrodes / electrolyte | Rechargeable? | Uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dry (Leclanché, zinc-carbon) cell** | **Zinc** case (anode), **carbon rod** + MnO2 (cathode); paste of **NH4Cl + ZnCl2** | **No** – reaction **irreversible** | Torches, clocks, remotes |
| **Lead-acid (car battery)** | **Lead and PbO2** (lead peroxide); **sulphuric acid** | Yes | Car starting, UPS; **12 V**, capacity in **ampere-hour** |
| **Nickel-cadmium (Ni-Cd)** | **Nickel** oxide-hydroxide cathode, **cadmium** anode; **KOH** electrolyte | Yes | Calculators, **cordless appliances**, torchlights, **shavers**, transistors; declining (toxic cadmium) |
| **NiMH** | Metal hydride replaces cadmium | Yes | Hybrid vehicles |
- **Dry cell A–R (UP 2004):** "cannot be charged" ✓; "has a reversible reaction" ✗ → A true, R false.
- **Car battery (IAS 1998):** 12 V ✓, ampere-hour ✓; hydrochloric acid ✗, lead and copper electrodes ✗ → **I and IV**.
- **Traditional battery chemical (SSC CGL 2019):** **sulphuric acid** – not ethylene glycol, sodium bicarbonate, ethanol.
> Dry cell electrolytes asked twice (IAS 2009, Jharkhand 2010): **ammonium chloride + zinc chloride**. Ni-Cd electrodes asked twice (IAS 1995, UK 2016).

=== Lithium-ion and New Battery Chemistries
- **Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2019:** **development of lithium-ion batteries** (Goodenough, Whittingham, Yoshino) – APPSC JL 2020.
- **Li-ion:** Li⁺ ions move **anode → cathode on discharge**; **anode = graphite**; cathode = lithium metal oxide (**LiCoO2, LiFePO4/LFP, NMC**); lithium salt in organic solvent; efficiency ~90–95%.
- **Mobile phone batteries:** **lithium** (CDS 2015) – not copper, zinc, nickel.
- **EV cathodes (UPSC 2025 ×2):** **cobalt, lithium, nickel** = cathode; **graphite = anode** → **only three**.
- **ISRO Li-ion technology:** ISRO developed space-grade **lithium-ion cells** and offered the technology for transfer to industry (APPSC 2017 question).
| Chemistry | Key point |
|---|---|
| LFP (Li-ion) | Safe, long life |
| Sodium-ion | Abundant, cheaper, no cobalt/nickel, safer; **lower energy density** than Li-ion/LFP; grid storage |
| Solid-state | **Solid electrolyte**; higher density, safer; emerging EVs |
| Flow (vanadium redox) | **Liquid electrolytes in external tanks**; energy and power scale separately; grid-scale, long duration |
- Sodium-ion is a safer, **mineral-light** alternative, not a replacement for pumped storage.

=== EVs, Fuel Cells and Alternative Powertrains
| Type | Power source |
|---|---|
| BEV | Battery only |
| HEV / PHEV | Engine + battery-motor; **only PHEV plugs in** |
| FCEV (Fuel Cell Electric Vehicle) | **Hydrogen + oxygen → electricity**; exhaust = **water vapour** |
| Fuel cell-electric hybrid | Fuel cell + battery |
- **Alternative powertrains (UPSC 2025 ×2):** BEV, hydrogen FCEV and fuel cell-electric hybrid → **all three**.
- **FCEV exhaust (UPSC 2024):** **water vapour** – not hydrogen peroxide, hydronium or oxygen.
- **Battery swapping policy:** Budget **2022-23**. **PM E-DRIVE** (2024) replaced **FAME-II**.
- FY 2025-26: >25 lakh EVs, ~**8.5%** share of sales; goal **30% by 2030**.

=== Andhra Pradesh – Storage Firsts
- **World's first thermal battery plant:** inaugurated in **India – Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh, 6 August 2018** by CM N. Chandrababu Naidu; **Bharat Energy Storage Technology (BEST)** (APSLPRB 2019).
- Its **High Energy Density Storage (HEDS)** device = the **world's first indigenously made high-energy storage device**, unveiled at **Amaravati** (APPSC 2018).
- **Pinnapuram** (Kurnool, Greenko): solar + wind + **pumped storage**.
- CEA plan for AP (Aug 2026): **3,931 MW battery** + 7,370 MW pumped storage.

=== Grid Storage – BESS, Pumped, Mechanical and Thermal
- Storage is the **bottleneck** for RE: balances variable output, supports **24x7 RE**.
- Need **336 GWh by 2029-30** (CEA) vs ~**500 MWh** BESS installed (Sept 2025).
- **Energy Storage Obligation** (**Ministry of Power**, 2022): **1% (2023-24) → 4% (2029-30)**.
- Framework: ESS a power-system element; **ISTS waiver** for co-located storage; consumers may own/lease – all are BESS measures.
- **Draft National Electricity Policy 2026** (BESS, smart grids, ToD tariffs) replaces NEP **2005**.
- **Khavda, Gujarat** (Adani Green): world's largest single-location BESS outside China (May 2026).
| Method | Principle |
|---|---|
| Pumped storage | Pump water uphill at low demand; release at peak (70–80% efficient) |
| CAES | Air compressed into **caverns**, expanded through turbines |
| Flywheel | Rotational kinetic energy |
| Molten salt | Thermal storage in CSP; power **after sunset** |
- **Tehri PSP** (Uttarakhand): India's first **variable-speed** PSP, 4 × 250 MW.

=== Battery Waste Management Rules 2022
- **MoEFCC**; replaced Batteries (Management and Handling) Rules, **2001**.
- **EPR:** **producers/importers** collect and recycle; **landfill and incineration banned**.
- **90%** material recovery by **2026-27**.
- **BPAN** (Battery Pack Aadhaar): only EV and industrial batteries **>2 kWh**, not every portable battery.
