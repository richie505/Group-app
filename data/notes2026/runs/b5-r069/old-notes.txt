=== NOTES 5:69 | Energy Storage – Battery Technologies, Pli for Acc, Grid Storage
## SUB 0 | Cell Basics
[69:0:0] - **Anode = oxidation** (loss of electrons); **cathode = reduction** (gain) during discharge.
[69:0:1] - **Electrolyte** lets ions flow and keeps electrodes apart.
[69:0:2] - Storage is the **bottleneck** for RE: balances variable output, supports **24x7 RE**.
## SUB 1 | Battery Chemistries
[69:1:0] TABLE head: Battery | Electrodes / feature | Uses
  [69:1:0.0] Lead-acid | Lead + PbO2, sulphuric acid | Car starting, UPS
  [69:1:0.1] Ni-Cd | Cathode nickel (oxide) hydroxide, anode cadmium, KOH electrolyte; rechargeable | Calculators, shavers, torchlights, power tools; declining (toxic cadmium)
  [69:1:0.2] NiMH | Metal hydride replaces cadmium | Hybrid vehicles
  [69:1:0.3] Zinc-carbon dry cell | Zinc pot, MnO2, carbon; NH4Cl + ZnCl2 paste, no free liquid; not rechargeable (reaction irreversible) | Torches, clocks
  [69:1:0.4] Li-ion | Graphite anode; NMC (nickel-manganese-cobalt) or LFP (safe, long life) cathode | Phones, EVs, grid
  [69:1:0.5] Sodium-ion | No cobalt/nickel; safer; density below LFP | Storage, low-cost EVs
  [69:1:0.6] Solid-state | Solid electrolyte | Emerging EVs
  [69:1:0.7] Flow (vanadium) | Liquid electrolytes in tanks; energy and power scaled separately | Grid-scale
[69:1:1] Sodium-ion is a safer, mineral-light alternative, not a replacement for pumped storage.
