## SUB 0 | Electricity Basics – Units, Frequency, Transformers
[0:0] - Discoms bill consumption in **kWh ("units")**; India's grid runs at **50 Hz** (not 60 Hz) – MPPSC 2008: A true, R false.
[0:1] - **Step-up transformer** raises **voltage** (and lowers current) for long-distance transmission; it does **not** raise power.
[0:2] - A **transformer works only on AC** (mutual induction); a **capacitor stores electric charge/energy**; a **generator/dynamo** converts mechanical to electrical energy.
[0:3] TABLE head: Transformer / generator fact | Answer
  [0:3.0] Protection not provided on small distribution transformers | Buchholz relay (used on oil-filled transformers of about 500 kVA and above)
  [0:3.1] Power factor of a transformer | Depends on the power factor of the load
  [0:3.2] Maximum efficiency of a distribution transformer | Designed for about 50% of full load (it runs lightly loaded most of the day)
  [0:3.3] Low power factor of a DG set | Needs higher excitation current
[0:4] - The **integrated national (all-India) power grid** evens out supply-demand mismatches between regions.

## SUB 2 | Transmission – One Nation One Grid
[2:0] - **One Nation-One Grid-One Frequency** = world's **largest synchronous grid**; over 5 lakh ckm.
[2:1] - **Five regional grids** (N, E, W, NE, S); **Southern** synchronised last, **December 2013**.
[2:2] - **POWERGRID (PGCIL)** = Central Transmission Utility.
[2:3] - **Green Energy Corridor:** began with a **PGCIL study (2012)**; built from 2015.
[2:4] - Record peak **270.82 GW** (21 May 2026); peak shortage **4.2% (2014) → 0.03% (2025)**.
[2:5] - **BESS** helps meet the **evening peak** when solar fades.

## SUB 3 | Discom Finances – Why the SEBs Went Sick
[3:0] - Causes: **below-cost sale** to farm and domestic users, **poor cost recovery**, high T&D losses, **rising commercial losses**, **delays** in commissioning new projects.
[3:1] - **Not a cause:** the **autonomy of States** over generation and distribution (APPSC G2 Mains 2017).

[3:3] - DISCOMs: loss ₹67,962 crore (2013-14) → **profit ₹2,701 crore** (FY25). States spend about **₹2.4 lakh crore/year** on power subsidies.
