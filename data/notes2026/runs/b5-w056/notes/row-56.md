=== MNRE – History, Institutions and IREDA
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1981 | Commission for Additional Sources of Energy (**CASE**) |
| 1982 | **DNES** in the Ministry of **Energy** (not S&T) |
| 1992 | Ministry of Non-Conventional Energy Sources (**MNES**) |
| 2006 | Renamed **MNRE** |
- **India = first country in the world** to set up a ministry for **non-conventional energy sources** (APPSC AMVI 2023).
| Institution | Location / role |
|---|---|
| NISE | **Gurugram** – solar |
| NIWE | **Chennai** – wind |
| NIBE | **Kapurthala** – bio-energy |
| SECI | PSU; implements the **National Solar Mission** |
| IREDA | PSU + NBFC, **1987**; RE loans |
- **DHRE, IIT Roorkee:** small-hydro support but **not under MNRE**.
- **IREDA MoU (APPSC G1):** with **IIT Bhubaneswar** for **innovation and research in renewable energy** and start-up support (100 Cube Start-up Conclave, Feb 2024).
- **IIT Tirupati + UNSW** (University of New South Wales): AP solar Centre of Excellence (31 Jul 2026).

# Capacity and ranks
- Installed capacity (15 Aug 2026): **about 552 GW**; non-fossil over 54%.
- Non-fossil share **51.93% (Dec 2025)** – the 50%-by-2030 goal met early.
- Global (Aug 2026): **3rd** in total RE capacity; **4th** in wind; overtook **Japan** as 3rd-largest solar producer.
- Wind targets: **100 GW by 2030**, **155 GW by 2035**.

=== National Clean Energy Fund (NCEF)
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Created | **2010-11** (public account, Finance Bill 2010-11) |
| Source | **Clean energy cess on coal, lignite and peat**, domestic and imported |
| Objective | Fund **research and innovative projects in clean energy technologies** – promote RE, reduce carbon emissions |
| Status | Cess merged into the **GST Compensation Cess (July 2017)** |
~~ Sources: Shankar Environment Part 21 p.11; app notes 5:56 sub 3
