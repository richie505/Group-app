## 3. Repeats
- **Kumarila Bhatta = Purva Mimamsa** – 5 APPSC PYQs (G2 2012, 2019 and three copies) – the single most repeated APPSC fact here.
- **Founders of the darshanas** (Kapila, Gautama, Jaimini, Patanjali, Badarayana/Vyasa) – 15+ PYQs; Uttara Mimamsa = Vyasa asked twice by APPSC.
- **Indra = most hymns / war-god** – 7 PYQs; **9th Mandala = Soma** ×2.
- **Lokayata–Kapalika not among six** (UPSC 2014) and **astika test = Vedas** (UPPCS 2005) appear twice each.
- **Vaishali = first republic** ×3; **UPSC 2025 river pairs** ×2; **Panini's janapadas** ×2.

## 4. Heavy in the old notes but never asked
- Row 23 old notes spent five sub-sections on **Magadha dynasties, rise of Magadha, Persian/Macedonian invasions and heterodox sects** – those PYQs and own MCQs belong to rows 25, 31 and 32 and were moved there.
- Row 24 (**NBPW, Rajghat, Mayiladumparai iron, towns, trade routes**) – no PYQ at all; kept lean for the own MCQs. Only guilds (UPSC 2012), Sarthavaha and nishka are tested.
- Row 22 **rituals, sanskaras, priests, Iranian parallels** – no PYQ; kept as one short sub-section. The AP angle (Satavahana/Ikshvaku sacrifices, Gudimallam) was moved to the AP rows.

## 5. Corrections to the old notes and keys
- "Area south of the Ganga = **Anarta**" (old notes, printed key) is **wrong**: it is **Magadha** (NCERT Class 6); Anarta is north Gujarat.
- "Bhavartha Dipika" is **Jnaneshwar's** (the Jnaneshwari), not Ramanujacharya's.
- Kashi Tamil Sangamam 2023: statement A (inaugurated by the PM in December at Varanasi, 17 Dec 2023) is true, so all statements are correct.
- GAIN director: Rita Baranwal left in 2019; Christine King now heads GAIN.
