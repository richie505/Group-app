## SUB 1 | Gana-sanghas – Vajji, Malla, Shakya
[1:0] TABLE head: Aspect | Gana-sangha | Monarchy
  [1:0.0] Rule | Many rajas in an assembly of clan heads | Single hereditary king
  [1:0.1] Hall | Santhagara | Royal court
  [1:0.2] Location | NW and Himalayan foothills | Ganga plain
  [1:0.3] Examples | Vajji, Malla | Kosala, Magadha, Vatsa
[1:1] - **Vajji confederacy:** 8 clans – **Lichchhavis** (most powerful), Videhas, Jnatrikas, Vajjis; capital **Vaishali** = seat of the **world's first republic**; led by **Chetaka**, maternal uncle of **Mahavira**.
[1:2] - **Malla:** confederacy of 9 clans; capitals **Kushinara** (Buddha's **Mahaparinirvana**) and **Pava** (Mahavira died). A-R (UPPSC 2004): Kushinagar = Malla capital and parinirvana site, both true, R not the explanation.
[1:3] - **Buddha-period republics:** Shakya (Kapilavastu), Bhagga (Sumsumaragiri), Buli (Alakappa), Kalama (Kesaputta), Koliya (Ramagama), Malla, Lichchhavi. **Yaudheya** is NOT one – it is a post-Mauryan republic of Punjab–Haryana.
[1:4] - **Kamboja:** a monarchy in the 6th century BCE but a **sangha** in the Arthashastra.

## SUB 3 | Rivers and the South – Assaka on the Godavari
[3:0] - **Assaka (Ashmaka):** the **only Mahajanapada south of the Vindhyas**, on the **Godavari** (between the Godavari and Manjira); on the **Dakshinapatha**.
[3:1] - Capital **Potana / Potali = Bodhan, Nizamabad (Telangana)**; some place it at **Paithan (ancient Pratishthana)**.
[3:2] - **Suttanipata:** sage **Bavari's** ashram on the Godavari in Assaka – earliest Andhra-region link in Buddhist texts.

## SUB 4 | Kingdoms and Rulers of the Buddha's Time
[4:0] TABLE head: Ruler | Kingdom
  [4:0.0] Bimbisara, Ajatashatru | Magadha
  [4:0.1] Prasenajit (Pasenadi) | Kosala
  [4:0.2] Udayana | Vatsa
  [4:0.3] Chanda Pradyota | Avanti (a monarchy)
  [4:0.4] Pukkusati | Gandhara
[4:1] - **Four great powers:** **Magadha, Kosala, Vatsa, Avanti**; Gandhara was not one. After Magadha, **Kosala** (which absorbed Kashi and the Shakyas) was the strongest – "only Koshala and Magadha emerged as powerful".
[4:2] - **Kosala** annexed **Kashi**; Magadha later got it as dowry and by war. **Pradyota** rivalled **Bimbisara and Udayana**.
[4:3] - **Udayana:** married **Vasavadatta**, daughter of Pradyota (**Bhasa's Svapnavasavadatta**); the Buddha visited **Kaushambi** in his reign; turned Buddhist under **Pindola Bharadvaja**; donated the **Ghoshitarama** vihara.
[4:4] - **Legend links:** **Dasharatha** ruled **Kosala** (Ayodhya); in the Puranas **King Anga** was the father of **Vena** (father of Prithu).
[4:5] ~~ Sources: PMF Ancient & Medieval Part 2 p.21-27; Vishnu Purana (Anga–Vena); old app notes row 23
