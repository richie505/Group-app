=== Six Orthodox Schools – Founders, Texts and Pairs
| School (darshana) | Founder | Key text | Core idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Samkhya** (oldest) | **Kapila** | Samkhya Sutra; Samkhya Karika (Ishvarakrishna) | Dualism of Purusha and Prakriti; Satkaryavada |
| **Yoga** | **Patanjali** | Yoga Sutra | Ashtanga (eight-limbed) discipline; Samkhya metaphysics |
| **Nyaya** | **Gautama (Akshapada)** | Nyaya Sutra | Logic; four pramanas; 16 padarthas |
| **Vaisheshika** | **Kanada** | Vaisheshika Sutra | **Atomism** (paramanu); 9 dravyas |
| **Purva Mimamsa** | **Jaimini** | Mimamsa Sutra | Vedic ritual and duty (karma) |
| **Uttara Mimamsa (Vedanta)** | **Badarayana (Vyasa)** | Brahma Sutra | Brahman the only reality; knowledge of Brahman |
- **Three pairs:** **Samkhya–Yoga, Nyaya–Vaisheshika, Mimamsa–Vedanta**.
- **Oldest darshana = Samkhya** (APPSC 2018 flashcard). Kapila is its **earliest exponent**.
- **Uttara Mimamsa = Badarayana = Vyasa** (APPSC G1 2002) – not Kapila, Patanjali or Gautama; an old APPSC key giving Kapila is wrong.
- **Not among the six:** **Smriti** (a class of texts), **Lokayata** (Charvaka materialism), **Kapalika** (a Shaiva sect) (UPSC 2014), Charvaka, Ajivika.
- **Fully articulated / perfected in the Gupta age** (SSC CPO 2003); their sutras are older.
- **Navya-Nyaya** ("new logic") was founded by **Gangesha Upadhyaya** of Mithila (Tattvachintamani, 14th c.); Raghunatha Shiromani came later.
- **Kapila and the sons of Sagara:** in the Ramayana and Puranas the sage **Kapila** burnt the 60,000 sons of King Sagara to ashes; Bhagiratha brought the Ganga down to redeem them.
> Founder matches: Kapila–Samkhya, Patanjali–Yoga, Gautama–Nyaya, Kanada–Vaisheshika, Jaimini–Purva Mimamsa, Badarayana–Uttara Mimamsa.

=== Mimamsa – Jaimini, Kumarila Bhatta, Karma and Apurva
- **Founder:** **Jaimini** (Mimamsa Sutra). Other exponents: **Kumarila Bhatta** and **Prabhakara**.
- **Kumarila Bhatta** = **Purva Mimamsa** (APPSC G2 2012, 2019; AP History PYQs) – not Uttara Mimamsa, Advaita, Dvaita, Yogachara or Jnana marga. Works: **Slokavartika**, Tantravartika. He **defended Vedic ritual (karma-kanda)** and attacked Buddhist doctrines.
- Also called **Karma-Mimamsa**: it centres on ritual action → the **theory of karma** belongs to Mimamsa (UPPSC 1997).
- **Liberation through karma** – correct performance of Vedic rites and duties (UPSC 2005).
- The **Vedas are eternal** and contain eternal truth (CGPSC 2013).
- **Apurva** = the **unseen force** that links a ritual act (karma) with its later result.
- **Purva Mimamsa = Karmakanda** (ritual part of the Vedas); **Uttara Mimamsa = Jnanakanda** (knowledge part).

=== Rigvedic Gods – Indra, Agni, Varuna, Soma
| God | Domain | Exam fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Indra** | Lightning, storm, **war** | **~250 hymns (most)**; ranked first; **Purandara** (fort-breaker); most popular god; wife **Shachi (Indrani)** |
| **Agni** | Fire | **~200 hymns (second)**; messenger between gods and men; first hymn of the Rigveda (1.1) |
| **Varuna** | Water, **cosmic order** | Ranked third; upholder of **Rita**; ethically the highest |
| **Soma** | Plant and ritual drink | **Whole 9th Mandala**; god of plants; Haoma of the Avesta |
| **Surya** | Sun | Aspects: **Savitri** (Gayatri), Mitra, **Pushan**, Vishnu |
| **Pushan** | Paths, herdsmen, cattle | Solar aspect; god of the Shudras in later Vedic times |
| **Brihaspati** | Prayer | **Priest (purohita) and preceptor of the gods** |
| Dyaus | Sky | Celestial father, like Zeus |
| Ushas | Dawn | Female |
| **Aditi** | Mother of the gods | Female; **not Prithvi** (Prithvi = fertility goddess) |
| Aranyani | Forest | Female |
| Rudra | Disease | Archer god of the mountains; later merged with Shiva |
| Yama | Death | – |
| Vishnu | Aspect of Surya | Only 3 hymns; rose later |
- **War-god** = Indra (UPPSC 2011, Uttarakhand 2016). When Indra is not offered, the most hymns = **Agni** (UPPSC 2010).
- **Priest of the gods** = **Brihaspati** in most keys; Agni is also hailed as purohita in Rigveda 1.1.
- **Later Vedic:** **Prajapati** became supreme creator; **Vishnu** rose as preserver; **Rudra** grew; **Indra and Agni lost importance**.
> Indra 250 > Agni 200 > Varuna third; 9th Mandala = Soma; Aditi (not Prithvi) = mother of gods.

=== Vedanta Schools – Shankara, Ramanuja, Madhva
| School | Teacher | Meaning | Path to mukti |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Advaita** | **Adi Shankaracharya** (8th c., Kaladi, Kerala) | **Non-dualism**: Brahman alone is real, Atman = Brahman, world = Maya | **Jnana** (knowledge) |
| **Vishishtadvaita** | **Ramanujacharya** (11th c., Sriperumbudur; Srirangam) | **Qualified non-dualism**: souls and world real, inseparable from Vishnu | **Bhakti** and divine grace |
| **Dvaita** | **Madhvacharya** | Dualism | Bhakti |
| Dvaitadvaita | Nimbarka | Dualistic non-dualism | – |
| Shuddhadvaita | Vallabhacharya | Pure non-dualism | – |
- **Shankara:** founded **four mathas** – **Sringeri** (south), **Puri** (east), **Dwarka** (west), **Badrinath / Jyotirmath (Joshimath)** (north); not Prayag, Kanchi or Varanasi. Called a "**crypto-Buddhist**" (Buddhist in disguise). Also Dashanami order and Panchayatana worship.
- **Ramanuja:** first great promoter of the **Bhakti** movement; Sri Vaishnava.
- **Bhagavad Gita:** gives **equal importance to Jnana, Karma and Bhakti** as means of liberation (UPPSC 2005).
- **Swami Vivekananda's Neo-Vedanta** reworked Advaita.
- Other teachers: **Lakulisha** – Pashupata Shaivism; **Nagarjuna** – Madhyamika (Shunyavada).
> Advaita = Shankara = non-dualism = jnana; Vishishtadvaita = Ramanuja = bhakti; Dvaita = Madhva.
~~ Sources: PMF Art & Culture Part 5 p.98, p.105-110; Britannica 'Bhagavadgita'; old app notes row 22

=== Doctrines of the Schools – Samkhya, Nyaya, Vaisheshika
| Doctrine | School |
|---|---|
| **Satkaryavada** (effect pre-exists in the cause) | **Samkhya** (MPPSC 2025) |
| World formed by the blending of nature (Prakriti) | **Samkhya** |
| **Atom** as the basis of the world | **Vaisheshika** (Kanada) |
| Pramanas – perception, inference, comparison, testimony | **Nyaya** |
| Apurva; theory of karma | **Mimamsa** |
| Niyati (predetermined fate) | **Ajivika** (Makkhali Gosala) |
| Materialism | **Lokayata / Charvaka** |
| Syadvada | **Jaina** |
| Shunyavada / Madhyamika | Nagarjuna |
- **Samkhya:** **accepts rebirth**; liberation by **self-knowledge** (discriminating Purusha from Prakriti), not by an outside agent → UPSC 2013 "only 2". Kapila taught that man's life is shaped by the **forces of nature, not a divine agency** (CGPSC 2020: both true).
- **Nyaya:** "nyaya" = logic or decision; **16 padarthas**. **Vaisheshika:** 9 dravyas; beginning of physics in India.
- **APPSC G1 2023 match:** Samkhya – blending of nature; Vaisheshika – atom; Lokayata – materialism; Ajivika – predetermined fate.

=== Astika vs Nastika; Charvaka (Lokayata)
- **Test:** **belief in the authority of the Vedas** – astika accept it, nastika reject it (UPPSC 2005); not belief in God, rebirth or heaven.
- **Astika:** the six darshanas. **Nastika:** **Charvaka, Buddhism (Bauddha), Jainism, Ajivika**.
- **Charvaka = Lokayata** ("of the common people"); credited to **Brihaspati**; **materialism**; **perception** the only valid knowledge; no soul, rebirth or afterlife.
- **Ajita Kesakambali** – early materialist of the Buddha's time.
> Odd-one-out lists slip a nastika school (Charvaka, Bauddha, Ajivika) among the six.
~~ Sources: PMF Art & Culture Part 5 p.97, p.105; old app notes rows 22-23

=== Upanishadic Philosophy
| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Brahman | Ultimate reality (cosmic principle) |
| Atman | The self – not body or mind |
| Karma | Links action with consequence |
| Samsara | Rebirth – transmigration first in the **Brihadaranyaka** |
| **Moksha** | Liberation – **not a Vedic Samhita term; first discussed in the Upanishads** |
- **Main theme = philosophy** (UPPSC 2004, UP Lower 1998); anti-ritualistic; **Atman is Brahman**.
- Criticised **mechanical sacrifice**; stressed meditation and the guru-disciple relationship; **ignorance** = cause of sorrow.
- **Katha (Katha) Upanishad:** **Nachiketa** questions **Yama** on death and the Atman (UPSC 1997).
- Basic approach is universal – it does not reject other schools or scriptures.

| No. | Limb | Group |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Yama | Bahiranga (outer) |
| 2 | Niyama | Bahiranga |
| 3 | Asana | Bahiranga |
| 4 | **Pranayama** | Bahiranga |
| 5 | **Pratyahara** | Bahiranga (the bridge to the inner limbs) |
| 6 | **Dharana** | **Antaranga (inner)** |
| 7 | **Dhyana** | Antaranga |
| 8 | **Samadhi** | Antaranga |
- **Antaranga sadhana = Dharana, Dhyana, Samadhi** (MPPSC 2024 → Dharana). **Anusmriti** is NOT a limb (CGPSC 2015).
- **Bahiranga = 5** (Yama to Pratyahara); **antaranga = 3**; **Pranayama** is the 4th limb.

=== Nature of Vedic Religion – Rita, Dharma, Sacrifice
- **Nature worship**; male gods dominate; worship for material gains; **sacrifice (yajna) central**, in the open air – **no temples, no idols** (UPPSC 1993: "sacrifice and rituals").
- **Henotheism** (term of **Max Müller**): each god praised in turn as supreme.
- **Rita** = cosmic and moral order, upheld by **Varuna**; **Dharma** = obligation and duty to oneself and others, grown from Rita → UPSC 2011 "both".
- Iranian parallels: **Mitra–Mithra, Rita–Asha, Soma–Haoma**.
| Ritual / term | Associated with |
|---|---|
| Hotri (Rig), **Adhvaryu (Yajur)**, Udgatri (Sama) | Priests |
| Ceremonial chanting | Samaveda |
| **Agnistoma** | Regular Soma sacrifice |
| Rajasuya, Vajapeya, Ashvamedha | Royal sacrifices |
| **Grihya Sutras** | Domestic rites |
- **Sanskaras: 16** = 3 before birth, 12 during life, **1 after death**.
- **Later Vedic:** elaborate, costly sacrifices; Prajapati supreme; Indra and Agni decline; priests grow powerful.
