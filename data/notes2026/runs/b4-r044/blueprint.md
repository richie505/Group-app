## 3. Repeats and traps
- Repeats: nickel/copper/tungsten "90%" pairs (UPSC 2010, three copies), German silver (×3), ilmenite–rutile (×2), Bailadila (×6), biggest mica producer (×2), value shares (×2 + 2 singles), bauxite "two leading producers" (×2 with different printed keys – now both Odisha–Gujarat).
- Printed keys to watch: "does not produce mica" keyed Rajasthan (wrong – MP); manganese leader keyed Odisha (production leader is MP); asbestos producer keyed Rajasthan (only AP ever produced, none since 2014-15); apatite "sole producer" (output nil since 2016-17).
- Old-question facts now outdated: AP biggest mica producer (Rajasthan narrowly ahead, 2020-21), India world mica leader (now China), Bihar richest in iron/minerals (now Jharkhand/Odisha).
