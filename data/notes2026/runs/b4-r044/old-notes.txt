## SUB 2 | Deposit Types and Occurrence
[44:2:0] TABLE head: Type | Formation | Minerals
  [44:2:0.0] Placer | Heavy minerals in alluvial sands | Gold (APPSC 2024), tin, platinum
  [44:2:0.1] Veins / lodes | Cracks in igneous, metamorphic rocks | Tin, copper, zinc, lead
  [44:2:0.2] Sedimentary beds | Layers | Coal, gypsum, salt, potash
  [44:2:0.3] Residual weathering | Decomposed rock (laterite) | Bauxite
[44:2:1] - **Native state**: Au, Ag, Pt (and Cu). **Never free**: K, Na, Ca, Mg, **Al** (top of reactivity series).
[44:2:2] - **Beach sands** (seven heavy minerals): **rutile, zircon, sillimanite, garnet, ilmenite, monazite, leucoxene** (brown ilmenite). Kerala sands: ilmenite, zircon, sillimanite (not tungsten); world-class between **Neendakara and Kayamkulam**. AP sands: Srikakulam, Visakhapatnam.
[44:2:3] - **Monazite**: thorium, lanthanum, uranium, cerium; **not** in all Indian coastal sands; Kerala largest **thorium** producer (**IREL, Udyogamandal, Aluva**). India self-sufficient in thorium; **uranium** = most important nuclear fuel.

## SUB 9 | Mining Law and Policy
[44:9:0] - **MMDR Act 1957**: Ministry of Mines (with GSI, IBM); all minerals except petroleum, gas, atomic. Major minerals – Centre (chromite, kyanite, sillimanite; not bentonite); **minor minerals – States** (**Section 15**).
[44:9:1] TABLE head: Amendment | Change
  [44:9:1.0] 2015 | Auction-only; DMF and NMET created
  [44:9:1.1] 2021 | NMET autonomous; captive mines may sell output
  [44:9:1.2] 2025 | New minerals added to leases; States approve in 30 days; captive-sale limit removed
  [44:9:1.3] 2026 Bill (Aug) | Limits States' power to tax mineral rights – fiscal federalism debate
[44:9:2] - **DMF** protects mining-affected persons (not exploration); **PMKKKY** (2015) with yearly audit.
[44:9:3] - **MADA v. SAIL (2024)**: royalty is **not a tax**. SC (Jul 2026) upheld royalty on Average Sale Price including royalty, DMF, NMET.
[44:9:4] - **Art. 297**: offshore minerals vest in Union; OAMDR Amendment 2023 – auction-only leases. **National Mineral Policy 2019** replaced 2008.
[44:9:5] - Meghalaya Assembly opposed uranium mining (27 Aug 2026).
