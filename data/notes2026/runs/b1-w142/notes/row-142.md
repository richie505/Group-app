=== Alvars and Nayanars – the Tamil Beginning
| Feature | Alvars | Nayanars |
|---|---|---|
| Devotees of | **Vishnu** (**Vaishnava**) | **Shiva** (**Shaiva**) |
| Number | **12** | **63** (3 women) |
| Hymns / canon | **Nalayira Divya Prabandham** (**4,000** hymns, "Tamil Veda"; called **pasurams**), compiled by **Nathamuni** (10th c.) | **Tirumurai** (12 books, "Fifth Veda"), compiled by **Nambi Andar Nambi**; **Tevaram** = hymns of Appar, Sambandar, Sundarar |
| Women | **Andal** – only woman Alvar | **Karaikkal Ammaiyar** |
| Notable | **Poigai, Bhoothath, Pey** = first three Alvars; **Nammalvar** = foremost (Tiruvaymoli); Periyalvar | **Appar, Sambandar, Sundarar, Manikkavachakar** = the Nalvar / Samayacharyas; Tiruvasagam = Manikkavachakar |
- **Bhakti movement was started by the Alvar (and Nayanar) saints** of the Tamil South, **6th-9th c.** – not by the Sufis, Surdas or Tulsidas.
- **First Vaishnava devotees of South India = Alvars** (APPSC G2 2000, AP History PYQ; HPSC 2012) – not Nayanars, Bhagavatas or Virashaivas.
- **Pasurams (pasuras)** = Tamil hymns of the Alvars **in praise of Vishnu** (AP Police 2016); Nayanar hymns on Shiva are the Tevaram.
- **Composers of the Nalayira Divya Prabandham = the Alvars** (CDS 2018); Appar and Sambandar were Nayanars.
- **Nayanars = Shaivites** (UP UDA 2006, UPRO 2014).
- Both came from **all castes**, even "untouchables" (Panars, Pulaiyars), and were critical of Buddhism and Jainism.
- **Periya Puranam** (lives of the 63 Nayanars) = **Sekkizhar** under **Kulottunga II** (12th Tirumurai).
> Alvars = Vishnu, 12, Divya Prabandham (pasurams); Nayanars = Shiva, 63, Tevaram/Tirumurai.

| **Jayadeva** | Bengal / Odisha, 12th c. (Lakshmana Sena's court) | **Gita Govinda** (Sanskrit) |

| **Waris Shah** | **Punjabi** | Heer Ranjha |

=== Origins, Roots and Common Features
- **Bhakti** from the root **"bhaj"** – to share / participate; devotion to a personal God.
- **Roots:** **Upanishads, Bhagavad Gita, Bhagavata Purana** – not the Arthashastra.
- **Bhagavad Gita gives equal importance to Jnana, Karma and Bhakti** as means of liberation (UPPSC 2005); **Chapter 12 = Bhakti Yoga**. Advaita privileges jnana.
- **Bhagavata Purana** (Tamil land, 9th-10th c.) put bhakti at the centre; **Navadha (ninefold, 9) bhakti** – shravana, kirtana, smarana, padasevana, archana, vandana, dasya, sakhya, atmanivedana (UP UDA 2010).
- **Spread:** Tamil South 6th-9th c. → north from the 12th-13th c.; **reborn / zenith in the 15th-16th c.** with Kabir, Nanak, Sur, Tulsi, Mira (UPPCS 1993).
- **Why it arose:** reaction against ritual, priestly dominance and caste; **jnana and karma margas too hard** for ordinary people.
- **Common feature of all Bhakti saints:** they **composed in the language of the people** (vernacular) (State PSC) – not all rejected priests or idols (Saguna saints kept idols).
- **Ramanuja's movement in Andhra** (Vishnu devotion + social equality) = the **Bhakti movement** (APPSC 2022) – not Arya Samaj or Lingayatism.
- Core beliefs: **monotheism**, **guru** essential, **prapatti** (self-surrender); devotion above birth and status.
- Modern scholars trace its roots to the **Alvars and Nayanars**, not to Islam; Islam shaped later egalitarian ideas.
> Gita = jnana + karma + bhakti; Bhagavata = 9 forms; vernacular = the common feature.

=== Nirguna and Saguna Streams
| Stream | Idea of God | Saints |
|---|---|---|
| **Nirguna** | Formless, without attributes; rejects idols | **Kabir, Guru Nanak, Dadu Dayal, Ravidas** |
| **Saguna – Ramamargi** | Rama | **Tulsidas** |
| **Saguna – Krishnamargi** | Krishna | **Surdas, Mirabai, Chaitanya, Vallabha** |
- Both streams drew on Upanishadic **Advaita**; both monotheistic.
- **Dadu Panth** – founded by **Dadu Dayal** in Rajasthan, in Kabir's tradition.
- **Raskhan** was a devotee of **Krishna**, not Rama.
> Nirguna = Kabir, Nanak, Dadu, Ravidas; Saguna = Tulsi (Rama), Sur, Mira, Chaitanya (Krishna).

=== Virashaiva Movement and the Vachanas
- **Basavanna (Basaveshwara)** (12th c., born **Bagewadi**) led the **Virashaiva / Lingayat** movement in **Karnataka**; treasurer at **Kalyana** under Kalachuri **Bijjala**.
- **Anubhava Mantapa** – assembly of sharanas, presided by **Allama Prabhu**; **Akkamahadevi** a leading woman sharana.
- **Vachanas** – short **Kannada** sayings, signed **Kudalasangamadeva**.
- Rejected caste, temple ritual and idol worship.
- **Andhra:** Veerashaivism came through **Mallikarjuna Panditaradhya** and **Palkuriki Somanatha** (**Basava Puranam**, Telugu).
> Basavanna – Kalyana – Bijjala – Kannada vachanas – Kudalasangama.

=== Nathpanthis, Siddhas and Yogis
- Popularised devotional religion in **Northern India**; led by **Matsyendranath** and **Gorakhnath**.
- Practised **hatha yoga**, meditation and **renunciation**; rejected ritual and caste; open to "low" castes.
- **Charyapada** – songs of the Buddhist **Siddhacharyas** (earliest Bangla poems); manuscript found by **Haraprasad Shastri** in the Nepal royal library (1907).
