=== Chronology and Contemporaries
| Saint | Period | Contemporary ruler |
|---|---|---|
| **Namdev** | **1270-1350** (earliest) | Tughlaq age |
| **Kabir** | 15th c. (trad. 1398-1518) | **Sikandar Lodi** |
| **Guru Nanak** | **1469-1539** | Sikandar Lodi, Ibrahim Lodi, **Babur** |
| **Chaitanya** | **1486-1533/34** | Prataparudra Gajapati, Husain Shah |
| **Mirabai** | **1498-1546** | Rana Sanga's Mewar |
| **Tulsidas** | **1532-1623** | **Akbar and Jahangir** |
| **Tukaram** | **1608-1649/50** | **Jahangir**, Shah Jahan; Shivaji |
| **Ramdas** | 1608-1681 | Guru of **Shivaji** |
- **Order:** **Namdev → Kabir → Nanak → Chaitanya → Mirabai** (UPPCS 2005, 2014, 2025).
- **Mirabai** overlapped with **Guru Nanak, Chaitanya and the young Tulsidas**; "Mirabai was a contemporary of Guru Nanak" is true (CDS 2005).
- **Ramdas was a contemporary (and guru) of Shivaji** (CDS 2005).
- **Sikh religion founded in the time of Sikandar Lodi** (1489-1517) (UPPCS 2008).
- **Tukaram – Jahangir** (UPSC 2006) – not Babur, Akbar or Aurangzeb.
- **Nimbarka (12th c.) was not Akbar's contemporary**; **Kabir could not be influenced by Shaikh Ahmad Sirhindi** (1564-1624) (UPSC 2019).
> Namdev 1270 → Kabir → Nanak 1469 → Chaitanya 1486 → Mira 1498 → Tulsi 1532 → Tukaram 1608.

=== Kabir
- Born (tradition) to a **Brahmin widow**, raised by Muslim weavers **Niru and Nima**; disciple of **Ramananda**; **Nirguna Bhakti** (APPSC FRO).
- One God of many names – Ram, Rahim, Allah, Hari; **condemned pilgrimage, idol worship** and ritual of Hindus and Muslims; stood for Hindu-Muslim unity, universal love, and **householder life** → "he was not against idol worship" is the **wrong** statement (CDS 2011).
- **Bijak** = his dohas and sakhis, holy book of the **Kabirpanthis** (MPPSC 2000, 2002); also **Sakhi, Sabad, Ramaini**.
- **Amarmul (Anurag Sagar)** = dialogues of **Kabir and Dharamdas** (UPPCS 2003).
- "**Pothi padh padh jag mua … dhai akhar prem ka, padhe so pandit hoy**" = **Kabir** (MPPSC 2022).
- Language **Sadhukkadi** (panchmel khichri); hymns in the Adi Granth.
> Kabir: Ramananda's disciple, Nirguna, Bijak, Amarmul (with Dharamdas).
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 5 p.2; PMF Art & Culture Part 5 p.122; old app notes row 144

=== Maharashtra Saints and the Varkari Sect
- **Varkari sect** worships **Vithoba (Vitthala)** at **Pandharpur** (Chandrabhaga / Bhima, Solapur) – its **seat** (RAS 2008, 2018) – not Sringeri, Nadia or Varanasi.
- **Jnaneshwar (1275-96)** – **Jnaneshwari = Bhavartha Dipika**, Marathi commentary on the Gita (APPSC G1 1990-91) – not Ramanuja; Amritanubhava.
- **Namdev** – **Varkari saint** (UPPCS 2004) – not Nimbarka, Chakradhara (Mahanubhava) or Ramdas (Samarth); tailor family; teacher **Visoba Khechar**; hymns in the Guru Granth Sahib; his nirguna bhakti **showed Islamic (Sufi) influence** (UP UDA 2010).
- **Eknath** (1533-99) – Eknathi Bhagavata; **Tukaram** – abhangas; **Ramdas** – Dasbodh, guru of Shivaji.
- Namdev was **not** Shivaji's contemporary; Tukaram and Ramdas were.
> Varkari = Pandharpur Vithoba; Jnaneshwari = Bhavartha Dipika; Namdev = Islam-influenced Varkari saint.
~~ Sources: PMF Art & Culture Part 5 p.115, p.121-124; NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 4 p.39; old app notes row 144

=== Chaitanya, Mirabai and Puri
- **Chaitanya** (1486, **Nabadwip**, Nadia; childhood name **Nimai**) – **Vaishnava**, Krishna devotee; founded **Gaudiya Vaishnavism**; **Sankirtan**; lived at **Puri** under Prataparudra Gajapati.
- Preached Krishna love to all, even chandalas; had **disciples from both Hindus and Muslims** (e.g. Haridas Thakur, born Muslim) (WBPSC 2007).
- **Ratha Yatra** = festival of **Jagannatha at Puri** (APPSC Endowment 2022).
- **Mirabai** – Rathore princess of Merta (born Kudki); married **Bhojraj**, son of **Rana Sanga** of Mewar (RAS 1997); widowed young; Krishna devotee.
> Chaitanya = Nabadwip → Puri, Gaudiya; Mira's husband = Bhojraj (Sanga was her father-in-law).
~~ Sources: PMF Art & Culture Part 5 p.122, p.124; NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 5 p.2; Britannica 'Mira Bai', 'Caitanya'; old app notes row 144

=== Guru Nanak
- **Founder of Sikhism**; born **1469, Talwandi (Nankana Sahib)**, Khatri family; companion **Bhai Mardana**; **Guru ka Langar** (began at Kartarpur).
- "**God knows man's virtues and inquires not his caste; in the next world there is no caste**" = **Guru Nanak** (RAS 2010).
- Against idol worship; one formless God; householder life; **Babur Vani**.
- **Baba Guru Nanak International University** – foundation stone laid at **Nankana Sahib, Pakistan** (Oct 2019, 550th birth anniversary).
> Nanak: Talwandi 1469, Langar, "no caste in the next world".
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 5 p.3; PMF Art & Culture Part 5 p.122-123; news reports Oct 2019 (Dawn, The Hindu); old app notes row 144
