  [0:0.0] Chishti | Begun at Chisht (near Herat, Afghanistan) by Abu Ishaq Shami with his disciple Khwaja Abu Ahmad Abdal; in India Moinuddin Chishti (came with Muhammad Ghori, 1192; Ajmer) | Bakhtiyar Kaki, Baba Farid, Nizamuddin Auliya, Nasiruddin Chiragh, Salim Chishti, Burhanuddin Gharib, Gesudaraz, Hamiduddin of Nagaur | Delhi–Ajmer, Doab; later Deccan | Liberal; aloof from the court; popularised sama

  [0:0.3] Qadiri | Abdul Qadir Jilani (Baghdad; Pir Dastagir) → introduced in India by Shah Niamatullah and Makhdum Muhammad Jilani (15th c.) | Miyan Mir, Mulla Shah, Dara Shikoh | Punjab, Sindh, Lahore | Wahdat-ul-Wujud
  [0:0.4] Naqshbandi | Bahauddin Naqshband (Central Asia, 14th c.; never came to India) → Khwaja Baqi Billah (late 16th c.) | Ahmad Sirhindi, Shah Waliullah, Khwaja Mir Dard | North India, Kashmir | Most orthodox; strict Sharia; opposed sama, tomb visits, Akbar's Sulh-i-kul
  [0:0.5] Shattari | Shah Abdullah Shattar (15th c.) | Shah Muhammad Ghaus of Gwalior (yogic text Bahr-ul-Hayat; Jawahir-e-Khamsa; guide of Humayun, Tansen) | Malwa, Gwalior, Jaunpur, Bengal | Liberal, yogic
[0:1] - **Chishti name comes from Chisht in Afghanistan, not a village in Ajmer** → UPSC 1997 A-R: **A true, R false**.
[0:2] - **Founder of the Chishti branch** (UPPSC 2014; State PSC): keys give **Khwaja Abu Abdal** (Abu Ahmad Abdal), the disciple who carried on Abu Ishaq Shami's line at Chisht.
[0:3] - **Chishtia order in India established by Khwaja Muinuddin** (UPPCS 2002).
[0:4] - **Incorrect pair: "Auliya – Madhya Pradesh"** – Auliya is a title, not an order (JPSC 2011); Chishti–Delhi and Doab, Suhrawardi–Sindh, Firdausi–Bihar are correct.
[0:5] - **Not Chishti:** **Abdul Qadir Jilani** (Qadiri; RAS 2016); **Bahauddin Zakariya** (Suhrawardi; NDA 2017). **Chishti:** **Gesudaraz** (APPSC G1 2019) – not Bahauddin Naqshband, Bahauddin Zakariya or Shah Waliullah.
[0:6] - **Bahauddin Zakariya = Suhrawardi** (BPSC 2018 ×2). **Bihar = Firdausi** (BPSC 2003).
[0:7] - **Most orthodox = Naqshbandi** (JPSC 2016; official key: Suhrawardi). **Against music = Naqshbandi** (UPPSC 2013 ×2).
[0:8] - **Dastgir Sahib** shrine (Srinagar, fire of 2012) = **Abdul Qadir Jilani** (Sayyad Abdul Jilani).
[0:9] - **Shah Muhammad Ghaus** (Shattari) counted **Krishna among the Auliya**; orthodox Shah Waliullah and Shah Abdul Aziz did not.
[0:10] - **Farr-i-Izadi** (divine light of kingship, used by Abul Fazl for Mughal kingship) was first developed by the Iranian Sufi **Shihabuddin Suhrawardi (d. 1191, the Illuminationist "Maqtul")** – a namesake, not the order's founder.
[0:11] - **SI 2019 match:** Bahauddin Zakariya – Suhrawardi; Fariduddin Masud – Chishti; Shah Niamatullah – Qadiri; Khwaja Baqi Billah – Naqshbandi.

## SUB 1 | Sufi Terms, Doctrines, Stages and Practices
[1:0] TABLE head: Term | Meaning
  [1:0.0] Pir / Shaikh / Murshid | Teacher and spiritual guide of the Sufis
  [1:0.1] Murid | Disciple (student); bond = Piri-Muridi
  [1:0.2] Khalifa | Successor nominated by the pir to carry on the order
  [1:0.3] Silsila | Order = chain of spiritual genealogy (master → disciple)
  [1:0.4] Khanqah | Hospice where disciples lived and trained; fed travellers and the poor
  [1:0.5] Sama | Musical assembly to produce ecstasy; raqs = dance
  [1:0.6] Zikr / Chilla | Remembrance of God / 40-day retreat
  [1:0.7] Wali / Auliya | Friend(s) of God – a title, not an order
  [1:0.8] Urs | Death anniversary = union with God
  [1:0.9] Fana / Baqa | Annihilation of self in God / subsistence in God
  [1:0.10] Malfuzat / Maktubat / Tazkira | Conversations / letters / biographies of saints
  [1:0.11] Ulema | Scholars of Islamic law – not a Sufi term
[1:1] - **Not related to Sufism = Ulema** (UPPSC 2014; State PSC) – Khanqah, Shaikh and Sama are Sufi terms.
[1:2] - **UPPSC 2009 match:** Pir – teacher (2), Murid – student (1), Khalifa – follower/successor (4), Khanqah – hospice (3) → **2 1 4 3**.
[1:3] - "Sufi" from **suf** = coarse wool (also safa, sophia). Sufism took shape in **Persia (Iran)** and Iraq and came to India before Turkish rule; it absorbed Neo-Platonism, Christianity, Buddhism, Vedanta and Yoga.
[1:4] - **First prominent Sufi in India: Ali Hujwiri (Abul Hasan, Data Ganj Bakhsh)**, author of **Kashf-ul-Mahjub** – not Moinuddin, Nizamuddin or Amir Khusrau.
[1:5] - **Sufi Kalam** devotional music = **Kashmir**.
[1:6] - Significance: Sufism gave Islam a **tolerant, vernacular face**; khanqahs became welfare centres open to all.
[1:7] # Doctrines
[1:8] - **Wahdat-ul-Wujud** (Unity of Being; Haq and Khalq are one) – **Ibn Arabi**; Chishtis, Qadiris.
[1:9] - **Wahdat-ush-Shuhud** (Unity of Appearance) – **Ahmad Sirhindi** (Naqshbandi).
[1:10] - **10 maqamat (stages):** **Tauba** (repentance), Wara, Zuhd, **Faqr** (poverty), **Sabr** (patience), Shukr, Khauf, Raja, Tawakkul, **Riza** (last). **Namaz – daily prayer is not one of the 10 stages** → the incorrect pair.
[1:11] - Ba-shara (within Sharia: Chishti, Suhrawardi, Qadiri, Naqshbandi, Firdausi) vs **Be-shara** (Qalandars, Madaris).
[1:12] # Practices and yoga
[1:13] - **UPSC 2012:** Sufis practised **meditation and breath control**, **severe ascetic exercises in a lonely place**, and **holy songs (sama) to arouse ecstasy** → **1, 2 and 3**.
[1:14] - Hatha-yogic **Amritakund** translated into Arabic and Persian (13th c.); **Chishti** practice took yogic postures and breath control, vital in every order **except the Naqshbandis**. **Sufi order that adopted yogic practice = Chishti** (UPPSC 2010; the Shattaris also did).
[1:15] - **Alberuni** compared Sufi ideas with **Patanjali's Yoga Sutra** and the **Gita** (Kitab-ul-Hind).
[1:16] - **Devotional music as a way to God = Muinuddin Chishti** (UPSC 1996 ×2) – Chishtis made sama central.
[1:17] > Exam angle: term ↔ meaning (pir, murid, khalifa, silsila, khanqah, ulema) and the UPSC practice statements.
