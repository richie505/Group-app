## SUB 2 | The Chishti Line – Masters, Disciples and Rulers
[2:0] - Chain: **Usman Harooni → Moinuddin Chishti → Qutbuddin Bakhtiyar Kaki → Baba Farid → Nizamuddin Auliya → Nasiruddin Chiragh-i-Delhi**; Amir Khusrau = disciple of Nizamuddin; Baba Farid's other main disciple = Alauddin Sabir Kaliyari.
[2:1] TABLE head: Saint | Dates | Ruler link
  [2:1.0] Moinuddin Chishti | 1143–1236 | Came with Muhammad Ghori's army (1192), in the age of Prithviraj Chauhan; first Chishti in India, earliest Sufi settled at Ajmer
  [2:1.1] Qutbuddin Bakhtiyar Kaki | 1173–1235 | Iltutmish (also contemporary of Suhrawardi Bahauddin Zakariya)
  [2:1.2] Baba Farid | 1178–1271 | Born Kothewal (Multan dt.)
  [2:1.3] Nizamuddin Auliya | 1238–1325 | Saw seven Sultans but never visited their court; "Hunuz Dilli dur ast" of Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq
  [2:1.4] Nasiruddin Chiragh | 1274–1356 | Muhammad bin Tughlaq, Firuz Tughlaq
[2:2] - **Moinuddin was the disciple of Khwaja Usman Harooni** (UPPSC 2014). **Nizamuddin was the disciple of Baba Farid** (UPPCS 2006).
[2:3] - **Most reputed disciple of Shaikh Farid who saw seven Sultans = Nizamuddin Auliya** (UPPCS 2014 ×2).
[2:4] - **UPSC 2002:** Sirhindi was a contemporary of **Akbar–Jahangir**, not Ibrahim Lodi (I ✘); Nasiruddin was Nizamuddin's disciple (II ✔); Salim Chishti was **Akbar's** contemporary, not Aurangzeb's (III ✘); **Qadiri order introduced in India by Shah Niamatullah and Makhdum Muhammad Jilani** (IV ✔) → **II and IV**.
[2:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 5 p.10; Singhania App.2 p.178-180; Rocket IH Part 2 p.1; old app notes 1:147 subs 0, 3
## SUB 3 | Baba Farid, Malfuzat and Shaikh Ahmad Sirhindi
[3:0] - **Baba Farid (Fariduddin Ganj-i-Shakar):** his Punjabi verses are in the **Adi Granth / Guru Granth Sahib** (UPPSC 1994 ×2) – not Moinuddin, Kaki or Nizamuddin.
[3:1] TABLE head: Text | Author / compiler

[3:2] # Shaikh Ahmad Sirhindi
[3:3] - **Naqshbandi**, disciple of **Khwaja Baqi Billah**; **Mujaddid Alf-i-Sani**; doctrine **Wahdat-ush-Shuhud** against Wahdat-ul-Wujud.
[3:4] - Opposed Akbar's **Sulh-i-kul**, Din-i-Ilahi, Shia influence and sama; **imprisoned by Jahangir in Gwalior Fort** (UPSC 1998).
