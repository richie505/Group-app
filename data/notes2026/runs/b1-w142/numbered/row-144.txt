## SUB 2 | Kabir
[2:0] - Born (tradition) to a **Brahmin widow**, raised by Muslim weavers **Niru and Nima**; disciple of **Ramananda**; **Nirguna Bhakti** (APPSC FRO).
[2:1] - One God of many names – Ram, Rahim, Allah, Hari; **condemned pilgrimage, idol worship** and ritual of Hindus and Muslims; stood for Hindu-Muslim unity, universal love, and **householder life** → "he was not against idol worship" is the **wrong** statement (CDS 2011).
[2:2] - **Bijak** = his dohas and sakhis, holy book of the **Kabirpanthis** (MPPSC 2000, 2002); also **Sakhi, Sabad, Ramaini**.
[2:3] - **Amarmul (Anurag Sagar)** = dialogues of **Kabir and Dharamdas** (UPPCS 2003).
[2:4] - "**Pothi padh padh jag mua … dhai akhar prem ka, padhe so pandit hoy**" = **Kabir** (MPPSC 2022).
[2:5] - Language **Sadhukkadi** (panchmel khichri); hymns in the Adi Granth.
[2:6] > Kabir: Ramananda's disciple, Nirguna, Bijak, Amarmul (with Dharamdas).
[2:7] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 5 p.2; PMF Art & Culture Part 5 p.122; old app notes row 144
## SUB 3 | Maharashtra Saints and the Varkari Sect
[3:0] - **Varkari sect** worships **Vithoba (Vitthala)** at **Pandharpur** (Chandrabhaga / Bhima, Solapur) – its **seat** (RAS 2008, 2018) – not Sringeri, Nadia or Varanasi.
[3:1] - **Jnaneshwar (1275-96)** – **Jnaneshwari = Bhavartha Dipika**, Marathi commentary on the Gita (APPSC G1 1990-91) – not Ramanuja; Amritanubhava.
[3:2] - **Namdev** – **Varkari saint** (UPPCS 2004) – not Nimbarka, Chakradhara (Mahanubhava) or Ramdas (Samarth); tailor family; teacher **Visoba Khechar**; hymns in the Guru Granth Sahib; his nirguna bhakti **showed Islamic (Sufi) influence** (UP UDA 2010).
[3:3] - **Eknath** (1533-99) – Eknathi Bhagavata; **Tukaram** – abhangas; **Ramdas** – Dasbodh, guru of Shivaji.
[3:4] - Namdev was **not** Shivaji's contemporary; Tukaram and Ramdas were.
[3:5] > Varkari = Pandharpur Vithoba; Jnaneshwari = Bhavartha Dipika; Namdev = Islam-influenced Varkari saint.
[3:6] ~~ Sources: PMF Art & Culture Part 5 p.115, p.121-124; NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 4 p.39; old app notes row 144
## SUB 4 | Chaitanya, Mirabai and Puri
[4:0] - **Chaitanya** (1486, **Nabadwip**, Nadia; childhood name **Nimai**) – **Vaishnava**, Krishna devotee; founded **Gaudiya Vaishnavism**; **Sankirtan**; lived at **Puri** under Prataparudra Gajapati.
[4:1] - Preached Krishna love to all, even chandalas; had **disciples from both Hindus and Muslims** (e.g. Haridas Thakur, born Muslim) (WBPSC 2007).
[4:2] - **Ratha Yatra** = festival of **Jagannatha at Puri** (APPSC Endowment 2022).
[4:3] - **Mirabai** – Rathore princess of Merta (born Kudki); married **Bhojraj**, son of **Rana Sanga** of Mewar (RAS 1997); widowed young; Krishna devotee.
[4:4] > Chaitanya = Nabadwip → Puri, Gaudiya; Mira's husband = Bhojraj (Sanga was her father-in-law).
[4:5] ~~ Sources: PMF Art & Culture Part 5 p.122, p.124; NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 5 p.2; Britannica 'Mira Bai', 'Caitanya'; old app notes row 144
## SUB 5 | Guru Nanak
[5:0] - **Founder of Sikhism**; born **1469, Talwandi (Nankana Sahib)**, Khatri family; companion **Bhai Mardana**; **Guru ka Langar** (began at Kartarpur).
[5:1] - "**God knows man's virtues and inquires not his caste; in the next world there is no caste**" = **Guru Nanak** (RAS 2010).
[5:2] - Against idol worship; one formless God; householder life; **Babur Vani**.
[5:3] - **Baba Guru Nanak International University** – foundation stone laid at **Nankana Sahib, Pakistan** (Oct 2019, 550th birth anniversary).
[5:4] > Nanak: Talwandi 1469, Langar, "no caste in the next world".
[5:5] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian History - APPSC Exams Part 5 p.3; PMF Art & Culture Part 5 p.122-123; news reports Oct 2019 (Dawn, The Hindu); old app notes row 144
