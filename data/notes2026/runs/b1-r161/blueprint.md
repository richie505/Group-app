## 4. Source conflicts settled
- **Pollilur:** Spectrum p.128-129 dates Baillie's defeat to 1781 and Porto Novo to November 1781; Rocket Indian History and PMF give Pollilur 10 Sept 1780 and Porto Novo July 1781 – the notes follow Rocket/PMF.
- **Marathas in 1799:** NextGen, PMF and Spectrum p.130 say they helped the British; Spectrum p.132 says they refused their share. Own MCQ f78bbfcf9787b keeps "no active help", now with a dispute line.
- **Bankim's last novel:** Spectrum p.42 calls Rajasimha his finale; most references give Sitaram (1887). Both statements of the UP RO/ARO 2023 PYQ stay correct.
- **Last Mughal-appointed Subahdar of Bengal:** most books and keys say Murshid Quli Khan, but Alivardi (Spectrum p.119) and Siraj (NextGen Part 6 p.13) also got imperial sanction – keyed with a dispute line.
