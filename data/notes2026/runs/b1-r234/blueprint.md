## 3. Repeats and traps
- **Belgaum 1924** (Gandhi's only presidency) is asked 8 times; one APPSC paper prints it as "Belgaum 1925".
- **Hasrat Mohani** (complete independence, Ahmedabad 1921) ×5; stems mix up 1920/1921.
- **Chauri Chaura date**: books split 4 vs 5 Feb 1922 – keys give 4 Feb.
- **Calcutta (Sept 1920) vs Nagpur (Dec 1920)**: "special session / first adopted" = Calcutta; "passed/adopted by the Congress" in keys = Nagpur.
- **Andhra Shivaji = Parvataneni Veerayya Chowdary**, not Duggirala (Andhra Ratna) – one APPSC key was wrong.
- The old notes linked Aitchison's "play off the Mohammedans" remark to the Khilafat; it is about 1857 (PMF Part 3 p.153).
