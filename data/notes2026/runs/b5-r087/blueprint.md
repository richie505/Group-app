## Trends
- **Row 87 dominates (66 of 86).** BR facts (State, WNBR status, counts) and marine "firsts" (Gulf of Kachchh, Jamnagar) repeat almost word for word across UPSC, UPPCS and APPSC.
- **Ex-situ is asked as fact recall:** −196 °C/liquid nitrogen comes up four times, and the "botanical garden is not in-situ" pattern appears three times.
- **APPSC adds an AP layer:** Seshachalam, NSTR, Rayala/Koundinya, Telineelapuram, Kambalakonda, Atapaka, Naupada, endemic Cycas/Boswellia, and the SV NP statement question.
- **Outdated or disputed items:** Cold Desert BR joined the WNBR in 2025 (APPSC G1 key now outdated). NSTR districts: books say five, while the AP part now spans three. The community-reserve question has an official key of "two" but coaching answers of one or three. Alipore zoo founder is also disputed. Ramsar count is now 103 (an own MCQ is fixed with an added option).
