=== NOTES 5:87 | Conservation – In-situ (protected Areas, Biosphere Reserves) and Ex-situ (zoos, Gene Banks, Seed Banks)
## SUB 0 | In-situ vs Ex-situ
[87:0:0] TABLE head: Basis | In-situ | Ex-situ
  [87:0:0.0] Where | Within natural habitat | Outside, in a controlled environment
  [87:0:0.1] Examples | NP, sanctuary, biosphere reserve, reserved/protected forest, nature reserve, sacred grove, tiger reserve, community reserve, gene sanctuary, OECM | Botanical garden, zoo, safari park, seed/gene bank, cryobank, pollen bank, field gene bank, tissue culture, captive breeding
  [87:0:0.2] Saves | Whole ecosystem | Species or its genes only
[87:0:1] - **In-situ is the most effective**: species keep interacting with their environment.
[87:0:2] - Ex-situ values: education, research, reintroduction; limits: needs space and money, cannot save the ecosystem.
[87:0:3] - **Captive breeding** for species whose habitat is permanently lost, e.g. **Gangetic gharials** bred and released; 10 captive-bred **Asian Giant Tortoises** released in **Ntangki NP (Nagaland)**, Dec 2022.
[87:0:4] - **Meristem culture** = virus-free propagation.
[87:0:5] PYQ: "Not an in-situ site" = **Botanical Garden**.
[87:0:6] - "Biological diversity" first used by **Raymond F. Dasmann** (1968).
## SUB 1 | Protected Area Categories (wpa 1972)
[87:1:0] TABLE head: Category | Legal basis / feature
  [87:1:0.0] National Park | WPA 1972; no rights, no grazing, no biomass collection; boundary altered only by State Legislature resolution on NBWL recommendation
  [87:1:0.1] Wildlife Sanctuary | WPA 1972, notified by State (not UNESCO); some activity; grazing regulated by Chief Wildlife Warden
  [87:1:0.2] Conservation Reserve | 2002 amendment; government land, usually linking PAs
  [87:1:0.3] Community Reserve | 2002 amendment, Sec 36C; private/community land when a community volunteers; declared by State Government for fauna, flora, traditional and cultural values
[87:1:1] - Four PA categories only; a **Biosphere Reserve is NOT one**. Tiger reserves are notified under the NTCA chapter.
[87:1:2] - Land already an NP, sanctuary or conservation reserve cannot become a community reserve. First community reserve: **Keshopur-Chhamb (Punjab**, not Rajasthan). WB plans the first community reserve for the **Gangetic dolphin**.
[87:1:3] Amchang and Bhagwan Mahavir are notified as wildlife sanctuaries, not community reserves.
[87:1:4] - **Network (2026):** **1,134 PAs** (981 in 2020): 109 NPs, 573 WLS, 127 conservation reserves, 75 community reserves; 58 TRs; only **5.32%** of India's area (Aug 2026).
[87:1:5] # BR vs NP vs Sanctuary
[87:1:6] TABLE head: Feature | Biosphere reserve | National park | Sanctuary
  [87:1:6.0] Legal basis | UNESCO MAB recognition; no change in land ownership | WPA 1972 | WPA 1972
  [87:1:6.1] Human use | Settlements in transition zone | None | Some rights
  [87:1:6.2] Boundaries | Core-buffer-transition | Clearly demarcated | Not strictly specified
[87:1:7] - Projects affecting wildlife need clearance from the **Standing Committee of NBWL**.
## SUB 2 | Eco-sensitive Zones
[87:2:0] - **"Shock absorber"** around an NP or sanctuary: transition from high protection to regulated development.
[87:2:1] - Notified by **MoEF&CC** under the **Environment (Protection) Act, 1986** (Sec 3 with Rule 5), **NOT WPA 1972** (APPSC 2019 trap).
[87:2:2] - **MoEF 2011 guidelines:** up to **10 km** around a PA. **Supreme Court:** no mining within **1 km** of any NP/WLS (larger notified ESZ prevails); mining banned inside PAs; mining within 10 km of wetland conservation reserves needs wildlife clearance (Aug 2026).
[87:2:3] TABLE head: Prohibited | Regulated | Permitted
  [87:2:3.0] Commercial mining, quarrying, polluting industries, major hydro projects | Tree felling, hotels and resorts, road widening | Ongoing agriculture, organic farming, rainwater harvesting
## SUB 3 | Global Coverage, 30x30 and OECMs
[87:3:0] - **2014:** PAs covered **15.2% of land** and **8.4% of coastal-marine** areas (APPSC 2018).
[87:3:1] - **Apr 2026:** nearly **10%** of ocean protected; only ~3% fully/highly protected; **1.66%** of high seas.
[87:3:2] - **WDPA:** run by **UNEP-WCMC (Cambridge)** with IUCN; tracks 30x30.
[87:3:3] - **30x30 = Target 3** of **Kunming-Montreal GBF** (CBD COP-15, 2022): protect 30% of land and oceans by 2030.
[87:3:4] APPSC G1 2024: 30x30 is NOT a renewable-energy target.
[87:3:5] - **OECM:** area outside the PA network giving long-term in-situ conservation; recognised by **outcomes**; no change in ownership or legal authority (sacred groves, greenbelts, wetlands). India's **first OECM: Aravalli Biodiversity Park, Gurugram (2022)**, a restored mining site. APSBB plans OECMs to meet 30x30 (Jun 2026).
## SUB 4 | Biosphere Reserves and Mab
[87:4:0] - **UNESCO MAB Programme, 1971** (not IUCN). **IN-MAB Committee (MoEF&CC)** recommends sites; States prepare management action plans.
[87:4:1] - Criteria: protected core with viable populations of all trophic levels; management involving local communities (no land transfer to UNESCO). **Seville Strategy (1995)** added social, cultural, economic needs.
[87:4:2] - **Funding (CSS):** **60:40**; **90:10** for North-Eastern and three Himalayan States.

## SUB 8 | Gene Banks, Seed Vaults and Cryopreservation
[87:8:0] - **Gene bank:** cold store of germplasm; each sample = an **accession**.
[87:8:1] TABLE head: ICAR Bureau | Resource | Location
  [87:8:1.0] NBPGR (National Gene Bank) | Plants | New Delhi
  [87:8:1.1] NBAGR | Livestock | Karnal
  [87:8:1.2] NBFGR | Fish | Lucknow
  [87:8:1.3] NBAIM | Microorganisms | Mau
[87:8:2] - **National Gene Bank** (1996): ~**4.7 lakh** accessions. **2nd National Gene Bank** (Budget 2025-26): **10 lakh** lines; safety duplicate.
[87:8:3] - **Field gene banks:** living collections of vegetatively propagated/recalcitrant crops (mango, coconut, banana); NBRI's **Banthara** station holds one.
[87:8:4] - **International Rice Genebank:** **IRRI, Los Banos, Philippines** (largest rice collection).
[87:8:5] # Seed Vaults
[87:8:6] TABLE head: Vault | Location | Features
  [87:8:6.0] Svalbard Global Seed Vault | Spitsbergen, Norway (APPSC G2 2017) | Inside a mountain in permafrost; global safety duplicate, holds India's seeds; capacity 4.5 million samples (capacity, not holdings)
  [87:8:6.1] Millennium Seed Bank | Kew, Wakehurst, UK | Largest store of wild plant seeds
  [87:8:6.2] Chang La seed vault | Ladakh (~17,300 ft) | DRDO (DIHAR) with NBPGR; natural cold store
[87:8:7] - Seeds keep longest in **cool and dry** conditions (low moisture and temperature slow respiration and fungi); Svalbard stores at about **-18°C**.
[87:8:8] # Cryopreservation
[87:8:9] - Medium: **liquid nitrogen at -196°C** (APPSC 2021); gas = **nitrogen**; halts metabolism.
[87:8:10] - Stores gametes, embryos, seeds, pollen, meristems, tissues, DNA; bull semen kept in liquid nitrogen.
[87:8:11] - **LaCONES** (CSIR-CCMB, Hyderabad): India's **frozen zoo**; assisted reproduction and DNA banking; bred the **Indian mouse deer** with Nehru Zoo for reintroduction.
[87:8:12] - **National Wildlife Genetic Resource Bank:** Nehru Zoological Park; DNA, tissues, gametes, embryos. Seychelles gifted two giant tortoises there (2018).
