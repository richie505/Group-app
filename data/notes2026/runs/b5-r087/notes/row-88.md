# NSTR
- **India's largest tiger reserve** by area (~3,296 km² as notified; ~3,728 km² landscape) and the **largest Critical Tiger Habitat/core (~2,596 km²)** – ahead of Manas and Amrabad; Corbett, Ranthambore and Sundarbans are smaller (UPSC 2020).
- In the **Nallamala** hills; the **Krishna** cuts through it (Srisailam and Nagarjunasagar reservoirs); notified 1983; Chenchus. Its Telangana part is now **Amrabad TR** (India's 2nd largest).
- **Districts:** books give **five districts** (undivided AP: Kurnool, Prakasam, Guntur, Mahabubnagar, Nalgonda). **Now (2026)** the AP part (Rajiv Gandhi WLS + Gundla Brahmeswaram WLS core) lies in **three districts – Nandyal, Prakasam, Palnadu**.
- 2026: AP set up a State-Level Steering Committee for NSTR (CM chair, Sec 38U WPA); India has **58 tiger reserves** (Sep 2026); tiger count 3,682 (2022).
> Exam angle: largest TR / largest core area, districts, AP's "only one" of each category.

=== Mangroves
- **Sundarbans** (Ganga–Brahmaputra delta, West Bengal–Bangladesh) = **world's largest mangrove forest**.
- **State order of mangrove cover (ISFR 2023):** **West Bengal (largest)** > Gujarat > A&N Islands > **Andhra Pradesh** (least of these four); India ~4,992 km².
- AP mangroves: **Coringa** (Godavari) and **Krishna WLS** (Krishna) – main vegetation **mangroves** (Avicennia, Rhizophora); tidal forests in AP are called "Koringa forests".
- **Katchal Island** (Nicobar): NASA (Aug 2022) highlighted its loss of mangrove cover.
> Exam angle: largest mangrove forest, State with most/least mangroves, AP mangrove sanctuaries.

=== Elephant Reserve of AP
- **Rayala Elephant Reserve** = AP's only elephant reserve under **Project Elephant** (centrally sponsored; Centre–State shared); includes **Koundinya WLS** (Chittoor) – correct pair "**Koundinya Sanctuary and Rayala Elephant Reserve**".
- Other pairs belong elsewhere: Similipal–Mayurbhanj ER (Odisha), Dalma–Singhbhum ER (Jharkhand), Mudumalai (TN); Nilambur, Anamudi (Kerala), Anamalai (TN).
- **India: 33 elephant reserves**; elephant count **22,446** (SAIEE 2021-25).
- **Mysore Elephant Reserve** (Karnataka): Rs 4.74 crore IISc-led project (MoU July 2025) on landscape-level management of human–elephant conflict.
> Exam angle: which reserve/sanctuary pair is in AP.

=== Kolleru and the Ramsar Sites
- **Ramsar sites** = internationally important **wetlands** (Ramsar, Iran, 1971). India: **103** (Oct 2026, after Sukhna Wetland and Sauj Jheel); first: Chilika and Keoladeo (1981).
- **Kolleru:** large **freshwater** lake **between the Godavari and Krishna deltas** (Eluru); Ramsar site **2002**; sanctuary 308.55 km²; pelicans. Pulicat is the brackish lagoon; Chilika (Odisha) and Loktak (Manipur) are elsewhere.
- Operation Kolleru (2006) removed illegal fish tanks up to the +5 ft contour.
> Exam angle: wetlands = Ramsar sites; lake between Godavari and Krishna deltas = Kolleru.

=== Seshachalam, Sri Venkateswara NP, Endemic Plants and State Symbols
- **Seshachalam Hills BR:** AP's first and only BR, notified **2010** (20 Sep); ~4,756 km²; Eastern Ghats around Tirupati–Tirumala; red sanders, golden gecko.
- **Sri Venkateswara WLS and NP:** total **525.97 km²** (WLS 1985; NP 353.62 km², 1989 – not 1956); abode of species **endemic to the Seshachalam hills**; Talakona waterfall.
| Endemic plant | Where |
|---|---|
| **Cycas beddomei** ("living fossil") | **Sri Venkateswara NP / Seshachalam** |
| **Boswellia ovalifoliolata** | AP (Seshachalam–Tirumala) |
| **Red sanders** (Pterocarpus santalinus) | AP only (Seshachalam, Veligonda, Nallamala) |
| Syzygium stocksii, Atuna travancorica, Ilex gardneriana | Western Ghats – not AP |
- **AP State animal:** **Blackbuck (Krishna Jinka)**; State tree: Neem.
> Exam angle: Cycas beddomei → SV NP; AP endemic; SV NP statements; State animal.
