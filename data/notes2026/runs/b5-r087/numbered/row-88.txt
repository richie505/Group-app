## SUB 2 | Mangroves
[2:0] - **Sundarbans** (Ganga–Brahmaputra delta, West Bengal–Bangladesh) = **world's largest mangrove forest**.
[2:1] - **State order of mangrove cover (ISFR 2023):** **West Bengal (largest)** > Gujarat > A&N Islands > **Andhra Pradesh** (least of these four); India ~4,992 km².
[2:2] - AP mangroves: **Coringa** (Godavari) and **Krishna WLS** (Krishna) – main vegetation **mangroves** (Avicennia, Rhizophora); tidal forests in AP are called "Koringa forests".
[2:3] - **Katchal Island** (Nicobar): NASA (Aug 2022) highlighted its loss of mangrove cover.
[2:4] > Exam angle: largest mangrove forest, State with most/least mangroves, AP mangrove sanctuaries.

## SUB 3 | Elephant Reserve of AP
[3:0] - **Rayala Elephant Reserve** = AP's only elephant reserve under **Project Elephant** (centrally sponsored; Centre–State shared); includes **Koundinya WLS** (Chittoor) – correct pair "**Koundinya Sanctuary and Rayala Elephant Reserve**".
[3:1] - Other pairs belong elsewhere: Similipal–Mayurbhanj ER (Odisha), Dalma–Singhbhum ER (Jharkhand), Mudumalai (TN); Nilambur, Anamudi (Kerala), Anamalai (TN).

[3:3] - **Mysore Elephant Reserve** (Karnataka): Rs 4.74 crore IISc-led project (MoU July 2025) on landscape-level management of human–elephant conflict.

## SUB 4 | Kolleru and the Ramsar Sites
[4:0] - **Ramsar sites** = internationally important **wetlands** (Ramsar, Iran, 1971). India: **103** (Oct 2026, after Sukhna Wetland and Sauj Jheel); first: Chilika and Keoladeo (1981).
[4:1] - **Kolleru:** large **freshwater** lake **between the Godavari and Krishna deltas** (Eluru); Ramsar site **2002**; sanctuary 308.55 km²; pelicans. Pulicat is the brackish lagoon; Chilika (Odisha) and Loktak (Manipur) are elsewhere.
[4:2] - Operation Kolleru (2006) removed illegal fish tanks up to the +5 ft contour.
[4:3] > Exam angle: wetlands = Ramsar sites; lake between Godavari and Krishna deltas = Kolleru.
[4:4] ~~ Sources: PMF Species & PAs Part 2 p.52 (Kolleru between Godavari and Krishna); Rocket CA 2026 Part 02 p.84 (Ramsar count 103), p.137; Rocket CA 2026 Part 01 p.5 (first Ramsar sites); app notes 5:88 sub 5
