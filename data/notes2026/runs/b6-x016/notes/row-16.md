=== Ports, South Coast Railway and Roads
# Four major ports under construction in AP
- Informed to Parliament by the **Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways** (February 2026).
| Port | District |
|---|---|
| Mulapeta (formerly **Bhavanapadu**) | Srikakulam |
| Machilipatnam | **Krishna** |
| Ramayapatnam | **SPSR Nellore** |
| Kakinada SEZ Port | Kakinada |
- **Sagarmala – five pillars:** port modernisation; port connectivity; port-led industrialisation; coastal community development; coastal shipping and inland water transport (**railway connectivity is not a pillar**).
- Related schemes: **Harit Sagar Guidelines** (green ports), **Green Tug Transition Programme (GTTP)**, **Sagar Setu 2.0** (Maritime Single Window), **Cruise Bharat Mission**.
> Exam angle: "which district" – Mulapeta = Srikakulam (old name Bhavanapadu); Ramayapatnam = SPSR Nellore, not Prakasam.
# South Coast Railway (SCoR) zone
- HQ **Visakhapatnam**; GM **Sandeep Mathur**; divisions **Vijayawada, Guntur, Guntakal** plus parts of the East Coast Railway (Visakhapatnam division).
- Promised under the **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014**; **Union Cabinet approval February 2025**; DPR sent to the Railway Board.
- In Dec 2025–Feb 2026 it was planned to start on 1 April 2026; the Gazette notification later fixed operations from **1 June 2026**.
- **South Central Railway** (est. **1966**, HQ **Secunderabad**) keeps the **Secunderabad, Hyderabad and Nanded** divisions.
- **East Coast Railway**: est. **2003**, HQ **Bhubaneswar**.
> Exam angle: a PYQ (SCoR statements) and an NH foundation-stone PYQ make this the most-asked block of the topic.

=== Green Ammonia and Green Hydrogen
# Kakinada green ammonia project (AM Green)
- Billed as **India's first commercial green ammonia plant** and the world's largest green ammonia project (Praveen: AP's largest green hydrogen-based green ammonia project).
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Foundation | **17 January 2026**, Kakinada – CM N. Chandrababu Naidu and Deputy CM Pawan Kalyan |
| Developer | **AM Green (Greenko Group)** |
| Investment | **₹18,000 crore** (~USD 2 billion) plant + USD 8 billion for renewables and storage |
| Area | **495 acres** |
| Capacity | **1.5 MTPA** green ammonia in phases, full by **2030**; electrolyser 1,950 MW |
| Brownfield | Conversion of the **Nagarjuna Fertilisers** plant |
| Policy | **Integrated Clean Energy (ICE) Policy, 2024** |
- Main buyer **Uniper (Germany)** – **first green energy export from India to Europe**; **5 lakh tonnes a year from 2028**.
- Funders: **ADIA (UAE)**, **Petronas (Malaysia)**, **GIC (Singapore)**.
- AM Green target **5 million tonnes by 2032** – about **20%** of the National Green Hydrogen Mission target; part of the **WEF Transitioning Industrial Clusters** initiative.
- Japan's **JERA**: 5-year deal to buy 70,000 t a year of green ammonia from Kakinada; **Krishnapatnam** and **Moolapeta** are among JERA's proposed AP green ammonia sites.
# India's first green urea plant, Poodimadaka
- **India's first large-scale indigenous green urea plant** – MoU between **NTPC Green Energy** and **Asago Industries** (February 2026).
- At the **Poodimadaka Green Hydrogen Hub** near Visakhapatnam (Anakapalli); **1,000 tonnes per day**.
- Hub products: green ammonia, **green methanol**, **sustainable aviation fuel**, green urea; aim – cut urea imports by **8–10 million tonnes**.
> Exam angle: Kakinada = green ammonia (AM Green); Poodimadaka = green hydrogen hub and green urea; Rambilli = Li-ion gigafactory.

=== Irrigation and River Waters
# Polavaram project
- **National Multipurpose Irrigation Project** on the **Godavari** – irrigation, drinking water, hydropower, flood mitigation.
- Legal basis: **Section 90, AP Reorganisation Act 2014**; on **NITI Aayog** advice the **State builds it and the Centre funds it**.
- CM **Naidu** (this period) ordered completion by **July 2027**, ahead of the **Godavari Pushkaralu 2027**; later target **March 2027**; **Budget 2026–27: ₹6,105 crore**.
- Headworks **80%** complete; inter-State coordination with **Telangana and Chhattisgarh**.
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Initial height | 41.11 m – 124.2 TMC |
| Final FRL | **45.72 m – 194.6 TMC** |
| Left Main Canal | **East Godavari, Visakhapatnam** – 4 lakh acres |
| Right Main Canal | West Godavari, Krishna – 3.2 lakh acres |
- New command area **7.2 lakh acres**; stabilisation 13.5 lakh acres.

# Mangroves and the Great Green Wall
- Deputy CM and Forest Minister **K. Pawan Kalyan** ordered green cover fast-tracked and unclassified mangroves protected – about **10,000 acres** found by satellite, to be notified as protected forests.
