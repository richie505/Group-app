## SUB 2 | Green Ammonia and Green Hydrogen
[2:0] # Kakinada green ammonia project (AM Green)
[2:1] - Billed as **India's first commercial green ammonia plant** and the world's largest green ammonia project (Praveen: AP's largest green hydrogen-based green ammonia project).
[2:2] TABLE head: Item | Detail
  [2:2.0] Foundation | 17 January 2026, Kakinada – CM N. Chandrababu Naidu and Deputy CM Pawan Kalyan
  [2:2.1] Developer | AM Green (Greenko Group)
  [2:2.2] Investment | ₹18,000 crore (~USD 2 billion) plant + USD 8 billion for renewables and storage
  [2:2.3] Area | 495 acres
  [2:2.4] Capacity | 1.5 MTPA green ammonia in phases, full by 2030; electrolyser 1,950 MW
  [2:2.5] Brownfield | Conversion of the Nagarjuna Fertilisers plant
  [2:2.6] Policy | Integrated Clean Energy (ICE) Policy, 2024
[2:3] - Main buyer **Uniper (Germany)** – **first green energy export from India to Europe**; **5 lakh tonnes a year from 2028**.
[2:4] - Funders: **ADIA (UAE)**, **Petronas (Malaysia)**, **GIC (Singapore)**.
[2:5] - AM Green target **5 million tonnes by 2032** – about **20%** of the National Green Hydrogen Mission target; part of the **WEF Transitioning Industrial Clusters** initiative.
[2:6] - Japan's **JERA**: 5-year deal to buy 70,000 t a year of green ammonia from Kakinada; **Krishnapatnam** and **Moolapeta** are among JERA's proposed AP green ammonia sites.
[2:7] # India's first green urea plant, Poodimadaka
[2:8] - **India's first large-scale indigenous green urea plant** – MoU between **NTPC Green Energy** and **Asago Industries** (February 2026).
[2:9] - At the **Poodimadaka Green Hydrogen Hub** near Visakhapatnam (Anakapalli); **1,000 tonnes per day**.
[2:10] - Hub products: green ammonia, **green methanol**, **sustainable aviation fuel**, green urea; aim – cut urea imports by **8–10 million tonnes**.
