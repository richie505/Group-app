## SUB 2 | Growth Record by Period
[25:2:0] # Long-run Record
[25:2:1] - **1950-51**: GDP growth **3.6%** (APPSC-GS 2021).
[25:2:2] - Trend is **not a steady rise**; contractions in **1979-80** (drought + oil shock) and **2020-21** (COVID-19), followed by a **sharp rebound in 2021-22**.
[25:2:3] - **Since 1991**: nominal GDP at market prices grew **almost 12 times** (APPSC 2018).
[25:2:4] - Economic Survey 2020-21 (IMF estimate): real growth **11.5%** for 2021-22 and **6.8%** for 2022-23.
[25:2:5] # Real GDP Growth – Recent Years
[25:2:6] TABLE head: Year | Real GDP growth
  [25:2:6.0] 2022-23 | 7.2% (older series)
  [25:2:6.1] 2023-24 | First printed 8.2% (2024, APPSC-G2 2025 key); later 9.2% (old series); 7.2% (new series)
  [25:2:6.2] 2024-25 | 7.1% (new series; old 6.5%)
  [25:2:6.3] 2025-26 | FAE 7.4% → SAE 7.6% (Feb 2026); Q3 7.8%; Provisional Estimates (5 Jun 2026) 7.7%
  [25:2:6.4] Q1 2026-27 | Real GDP 7.8% (RBI projected 7.0%); real GVA 8.2%; nominal GDP 10.3%; manufacturing 9.2%; tertiary GVA 10.0%
[25:2:7] - **2014-15:** real GDP growth about **7.3%** – first estimate on the new **2011-12 series** (2015).
[25:2:8] # New GDP Series (2026)
[25:2:9] - **Reduced nominal GDP and per capita income**; raised FY26 growth to **7.6%**.
[25:2:10] - Cut economy size by a cumulative **₹43 lakh crore** over four years since 2022-23; Opposition questioned the deflator.
[25:2:11] - Raised agriculture GVA by 3.8-5.9%; cut trade by 36%.
[25:2:12] - Sector growth FY 2025-26: agriculture **3.1%** (4.6% the year before).
## SUB 3 | Size of the Economy and Per Capita Income
[25:3:0] # Size
[25:3:1] - 2025-26: about **₹345 lakh crore ≈ USD 3.9 trillion** (new series); earlier estimate ₹357 lakh crore (about USD 4 trillion).
[25:3:2] # Per Capita Income
[25:3:3] - **Per capita income = National income ÷ Population.**
[25:3:4] - Per capita national income 2025-26 (FAE, Jan 2026, current prices): **₹2,47,487**, up **₹16,025** over the previous year.
[25:3:5] - At **current prices** PCI rises faster than at **constant prices** (constant removes inflation).
[25:3:6] - Reasons for slow PCI growth: population growth, low productivity in agriculture, large unorganised sector.
[25:3:7] - **PCI 2017-18:** per capita income at **current prices** rose about **8.6%** over 2016-17 (CSO provisional estimates).
[25:3:8] - The 2026 new series **lowered PCI**.
