=== Growth Record – Real GDP Growth Year by Year
# The long run
- **First Plan (1951-56):** achieved growth **3.6%** against a 2.1% target – the figure APPSC 2019 keys as "GDP growth 1950-51" (strictly it is the First Plan average, not a single-year rate).
- **1950-80:** about **3.5%** a year (NextGen: 3.52%) = **"Hindu rate of growth"**; 1980s: **5.13%** a year.
- Trend is **not a steady rise**: contractions in **1979-80** (drought + oil shock) and **2020-21** (COVID-19), then a **sharp rebound in 2021-22**.
- **Since 1991:** the economy grew **"almost 12 times"** – the APPSC key (2018, 2020); in US $ terms it is about **10 times** ($270 bn in 1991 → $2.7-2.9 trillion by 2019-20).
- **IAS 2010:** GDP did **not** rise four-fold in 2000-10 (about three-fold at current prices); the **public sector's share of GDP declined** after liberalisation.

=== Sectoral Shares and Structural Shift
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Largest contributor at Independence | **Agriculture** (56.5% of national income in 1950-51) |
| Industry share, 1950-51 | About **13%** |
| Agriculture share | 29% (1990s) → **17.9% (2014)** → about **17% (2017-18)** |
| Services share | **48.5% in 2000-01** → **over 50%** now |
| Order now | **Services > Industry > Agriculture** |
| Services' share of GVA growth | About 69% (2011-12 to 2015-16); 72.5% (2017-18) |
- **"Industry poised to take over as the major contributor to GDP"** (AEE 2008) = **Services**.
- Agriculture's share fell much faster than its **share of workforce** (72% in 1951, still the largest employer).

=== Per Capita Income – Meaning, Level and Slow Growth
- **PCI = National income ÷ Population** (NextGen).
- At **current prices** PCI rises faster than at **constant prices** (constant prices remove inflation).
- **Per capita NNI 2025-26** (FAE, Jan 2026, current prices): **₹2,47,487**, up ₹16,025; the 2026 new series **lowered** nominal GDP and PCI.
- **2016-17:** per capita NNI ₹1,03,219 (+9.7%); **2017-18:** PCI at current prices up about **8.6%** (CSO provisional).
- **2006-07 (UPSC 2008):** per capita GDP at current prices about **₹37,000**; wheat about **75 mt**; rice about **93 mt** (not 180) → A and C.
- **Reasons for slow growth of real PCI (UPPCS 2001):** rapid **population growth**, **high price rise**, **slow growth of agriculture and industry**, and (before 1991) **scarcity of foreign exchange**; low farm productivity and a large unorganised sector – a **rising services share is not a cause**.
- **Size 2025-26:** about **₹345 lakh crore ≈ US $3.9 trillion** (new series); earlier estimate ₹357 lakh crore.
~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 11 p.17 (PCI = NI ÷ population); NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC 12 p.8; MoSPI PE 31 May 2017 (PCI ₹1,03,219, via Tribune); app notes 3:25 sub 3

=== Growth Concepts – Hindu Rate, Miracle Economies, Middle-income Trap
- **Hindu rate of growth:** about **3.5%** a year in **1950-80**; term coined by **Raj Krishna**.
- **Miracle economies:** GDP growth of **7%** for a **decade or more** (NextGen) – not 8, 8.5 or 7.5%.
- **Middle-income trap:** countries that rose from low to middle income but stagnated there; causes = **poor infrastructure, weak institutions, unfavourable growth conditions** – **climate change is not** one of the listed causes.
- **GDP does not measure income distribution**; dollar rankings move with exchange rates and base revisions.
- **World Bank income groups:** low, lower-middle, upper-middle, high; WDR 1994 low income = per capita GNP **US $725 or less**.
