## SUB 2 | Energy Policies
[117:2:0] # AP Integrated Clean Energy Policy 2024
[117:2:1] - Launched **June 2024**; absorbed the **AP Green Hydrogen and Green Ammonia Policy, 2023** (asked G1 Prelims 2024).
[117:2:2] - **Green Hydrogen & Ammonia Policy 2023:** **0.5 MTPA** green hydrogen or **2 MTPA** green ammonia in five years; nodal **NREDCAP**.
[117:2:3] - Targets: **160 GW+** clean energy, **Rs 10 lakh crore**, **7.5 lakh jobs**, **Net Zero by 2047** (not 2030).
[117:2:4] - Progress: approvals Rs 2.88 lakh crore; Partnership Summit, Visakhapatnam (14-15 Nov 2025) Rs 4.54 lakh crore.
[117:2:5] # Green Hydrogen
[117:2:6] - **Green Hydrogen Valley Declaration**, Amaravati, **19 July 2025**: AP as "Green Hydrogen Capital"; cut cost Rs 460/kg to Rs 160-170/kg; nodal **SRM University**.
[117:2:7] - National Green Hydrogen Mission hubs **outside AP**: Paradip (Odisha), Kandla (Gujarat), Tuticorin (Tamil Nadu).
