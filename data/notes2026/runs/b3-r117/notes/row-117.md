=== Power Reforms, DISCOMs and Energy Efficiency
| DISCOM | Full form | HQ | Areas (old districts) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **APEPDCL** | AP **Eastern** Power Distribution **Company** Ltd | Visakhapatnam | Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam, East and West Godavari |
| **APCPDCL** | AP **Central** Power Distribution **Corporation** Ltd | Vijayawada | Krishna, Guntur, Prakasam |
| **APSPDCL** | AP **Southern** Power Distribution **Company** Ltd | **Tirupati** | Nellore, Chittoor, Kadapa, Anantapur, Kurnool |
- **AP Electricity Reform Act 1998**: APSEB unbundled; World Bank loan (APL I, 1999) – AP went ahead alone with power reform (APPSC G1 1999).
- **2000 unbundling**: four DISCOMs – EPDCL (Visakhapatnam), CPDCL (**Hyderabad**), NPDCL (Warangal), SPDCL (Tirupati).
- **After 2014 (APRA)**: **Anantapur and Kurnool** moved from APCPDCL to **APSPDCL**.
- Present Vijayawada APCPDCL **carved out of APSPDCL in 2020**, not one of the 2000 four.
- **UDAY** (approved **Nov 2015**) → replaced by **RDSS (2021)**: reforms-based, results-linked; smart metering and loss-reduction works; AP's RDSS aim (PYQ): save about **₹2,000 crore** of T&D losses.
- **World Bank** ranked AP **No. 1 in Energy Efficiency Implementation Readiness** (Nov 2016; score 42.01, then Rajasthan, Karnataka).
- **ChandraKanthi**: AP programme to **distribute LED lamps** (AP led the DELP/UJALA LED drive).
- **APSECM** (2012): India's first standalone State Designated Agency of BEE; first prize NECA-2024; SEEI 2024 score 87.25.
- Smart meters and feeder segregation cut AT&C losses; APEPDCL and APSPDCL among top performers (Jan 2026).
> Exam angle: APCPDCL = "Corporation", the other two = "Company"; Anantapur–Kurnool shift; World Bank EE ranking.
~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.132, p.135; deccanchronicle.com 5 Nov 2016 "World Bank declares AP No 1 in energy efficiency" (web search result); app notes 3:117

- **AP Maritime Policy 2024-29**: make AP India's leading **maritime gateway**; +145 MTPA by 2030; **300 MTPA by 2047**; one port in world's top 20; **20% of all-India cargo by 2047**; 25% of shipbuilding target. **AP Maritime Board (APMB)** = nodal body for **non-major ports**.
> Exam angle: statements on port models (Swiss Challenge, BOOT, minor-port status); Krishnapatnam is NOT a major port.

=== Solar, Pumped Storage and Clean Energy Policy
- **AP Integrated Clean Energy Policy 2024** (June 2024): absorbed the **AP Green Hydrogen and Green Ammonia Policy 2023**; targets **160 GW+**, **Rs 10 lakh crore**, **7.5 lakh jobs**, **Net Zero by 2047** (not 2030).
- **Green Hydrogen & Ammonia Policy 2023:** **0.5 MTPA** green hydrogen or **2 MTPA** green ammonia in five years; nodal **NREDCAP**.
- **Green Hydrogen Valley Declaration**, Amaravati, **19 July 2025**: "Green Hydrogen Capital"; nodal **SRM University**. National hydrogen hubs **outside AP**: Paradip (**Odisha**), Kandla, Tuticorin.
- Progress: approvals Rs 2.88 lakh crore; Partnership Summit, **Visakhapatnam** (14-15 Nov 2025).
