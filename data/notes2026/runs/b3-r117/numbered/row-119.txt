## SUB 1 | E-pragati, RTGS and Grievance Platforms
[1:0] - **e-Pragati**: **whole-of-government** State Enterprise Architecture; developed under **CM N. Chandrababu Naidu** (2014-19); **14 packages / 72 projects / 33 departments**; core hubs **People, Land, Entity, GIS, IoT** (**no Finance Hub**); goal **faceless, paperless, cashless**.
[1:1] - Campaign aims: AP Fibre Grid to every gram panchayat; State Data Centre hosting all data and apps; **People Hub** database of citizens for welfare delivery. **Computerising land registration is NOT an e-Pragati aim** (that was CARD, 1998).
[1:2] - **RTGS** (Real Time Governance Society, **2017**): real-time monitoring, analytics; Command and Control Centre; **AWARE 2.0** disaster early warning; **AP DigiVerify** blockchain certificates; AI4PGRS.
[1:3] - **State Data Lake**: single source of truth for **40+ departments**; concerns **privacy, algorithmic bias**. **Mana Mitra**: **865 services** (SES 2025-26).
[1:4] - **Grievance platforms:** **Prajavani** → **Meekosam** (online forum, 2014-19) → **Spandana** (2019-24) → **PGRS**.

## SUB 2 | Connectivity – Fiber Grid, Digital Village, APSFL
[2:0] - **AP Fiber Grid Phase-I**: **24,000 km** **IP-MPLS ring** (not GPON), all **13 districts**; **NOC at Visakhapatnam**.
[2:1] - **Mori** (Sakhinetipalli mandal, **East Godavari**): pilot fibre-grid smart village – AP's **first digital village** (Fibernet launched there **29 Dec 2016**).
[2:2] - **APSFL**: incorporated Oct 2015, launched 27 Dec 2017; **India's first State-owned ISP**.
[2:3] - **BharatNet Phase-II** (APSFL): **54,502 km, 11,254 GPs**. APSWAN (2010) → APSDWAN (1 May 2021).

## SUB 3 | IT Policies and Definitions
[3:0] - **AP IT Policy 2021-24**: approved 30 June 2021; in force **1 April 2021 – 31 March 2024**; **55,000 jobs** aim.
[3:1]   - **Local procurement:** IT needs **up to Rs 1 crore** procured **exclusively** from IT companies/start-ups **operating in AP**, provided quality is comparable.
[3:2]   - **Entry-level IT job**: CTC **up to Rs 5 lakh**; high-end above Rs 10 lakh; an **MNC** has facilities in at least one country other than home.
[3:3] - **IT & GCC Policy 4.0 (2024-29)**: in force **12 Dec 2024**; GCC = a multinational's **own offshore unit**; co-working spaces in Tirupati, Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada.
[3:4] - **LIFT Policy 4.0** = **Land Incentive for Tech Hubs** (land for GCCs and IT infra developers).
[3:5] - **Innovation and Startup Policy 4.0**: by **ITE&C Department**; **20,000 startups**, **1 lakh jobs**; implemented by **AP Innovation Society**.
[3:6] - **Data Center Policy 4.0**: up to **1 GW**. **Drone Policy 4.0**: 100 companies, 25,000 pilots, **15,000 direct** jobs.
[3:7] - Legal: IT Act 2000 (MeitY; last amended **2008**); **AP IT (Electronic Service Delivery) Rules 2011** (electronic delivery mandatory); AP Cyber Security Policy 2017 → APCSOC (2018).
[3:8] > Exam angle: policy clauses (local procurement, CTC definitions) – asked in Polytechnic/NTRUHS papers.
