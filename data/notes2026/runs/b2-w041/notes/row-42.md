=== Strength in Numbers – Assembly and Council
# Legislative Assembly
| Item | Number |
|---|---|
| AP Assembly in 1956 (unicameral) | **301** |
| Undivided AP Assembly (from 1978 to 2014) | **294** elected |
| **AP Assembly now** (Sec. 17, AP Reorganisation Act, 2014) | **175** elected – **29 SC + 7 ST** reserved |
| **Telangana Assembly** | **119** |
| Lok Sabha seats from AP | **25** (Telangana 17) |
- **After bifurcation** the residuary AP has **175 Assembly constituencies** (not 180, 185, 195 or 150); this was also the strength "as on May 2020" (elected members).
- **Anglo-Indian nominee:** Art. 333 let the Governor nominate **one** Anglo-Indian (Sec. 18 of the 2014 Act kept one for each State) – ended by the **104th Amendment, 2019** (from **25 January 2020**); **now none**.
# Legislative Council
| Stage | Strength |
|---|---|
| Revived Council of undivided AP (2007) | **90** |
| Residuary AP under the 2014 Act (Sec. 22) | **50** (Telangana **40**) |
| **AP now** – AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Act, **2015** | **58** |
- **Present strength: Assembly 175, Council 58** (Grama/Ward Sachivalayam: "175 and 58").
- **APPSC Group 2 Prelims 2019** printed 52 / 54 / **56** / 50 and keyed **56** – wrong: the Council has had **58** seats since the 2015 amendment (Laxmikanth; PMF).
> Asked as: "How many Assembly seats after bifurcation?" (175 – five times), "Council after reorganisation?" (50, the 2014 figure), "Strength of the Council?" (90 in 2011, 58 now), Telangana 119.

=== AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 – Legislature Provisions
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| 13 + **First Schedule** | Sitting Rajya Sabha members of AP allotted to Telangana's share **by draw of lots** by the **Chairman of the Rajya Sabha** (done 30 May 2014: 7 to Telangana, 11 to AP) |
| 17 | Assembly seats: AP **175**, Telangana **119** |
| 18 | One Anglo-Indian nominee each (Art. 333) – lapsed (104th Amendment) |
| 19 | Sitting MLAs continue for their allotted constituencies |
| 20 | Five-year term (Art. 172(1)) **counted from the date the Assembly of the existing (united) State commenced** |
| 21 | AP Speaker continues; **Deputy Speaker** goes to Telangana |
| 22 | Councils: AP **50** (now 58), Telangana **40**; existing MLCs allotted by the **Fourth Schedule** (Sec. 22(2)) |
| 24 | Council constituencies: AP Order (2006) amended; Telangana Order 2014 |
| 25 | Council **Chairman** continues in AP; **Deputy Chairman** goes to Telangana |
| 26 | Assembly seats to be raised to **225 (AP)** and **153 (Telangana)**, "subject to **Art. 170**" |
- **Sec. 26 status (2026):** not done – **Art. 170(3)** freezes seat numbers until the **first Census after 2026**, so an earlier increase needs a **constitutional amendment of Art. 170** (a Central notification or Assembly resolutions cannot do it).
- **Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill, 2026:** fresh delimitation, Lok Sabha to **850**; **negatived** in the Lok Sabha (Apr 2026) – the **84th Amendment** freeze stays.
> Asked as: RS members – draw of lots; Assembly term counted from the united Assembly's commencement; Sec. 26 – 225; how delimitation can be effected (amend Art. 170).

=== Legislative Council – 1958, 1985, 2007, 2015
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **1958** | Council constituted on **1 July 1958** (Legislative Councils Act, 1957 – Laxmikanth says "created in 1957") – **AP became bicameral**; inaugurated by Rajendra Prasad, 8 July 1958 |
| 1956 | AP Legislature was **unicameral** (Assembly only) |
| **1985** | Abolished (NTR government; AP Legislative Council (Abolition) Act, 1985) |
| **2007** | Revived under the **AP Legislative Council Act, 2005** |
| 2014 | Sec. 22: AP 50, Telangana 40, "in accordance with Art. 169" |
| **2015** | AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Act: **50 → 58** |
| Jan 2020 | Assembly resolution to abolish the Council again |
| Nov 2021 | Resolution **withdrawn**; the Council continues |
- Act of **2005**, revival in **2007** – do not mix the two years.

=== Committees, Bills and Sessions
| Committee | Note |
|---|---|
| Estimates | Financial committee – checks that grants are properly used |
| Public Accounts (PAC) | Financial committee; chair goes to the **Opposition** by convention |
| Public Undertakings | Exists **in addition to** (not instead of) Estimates and PAC |
- Members are **elected from the House**; chairpersons are named by the **Speaker** (not the Governor); they work like parliamentary committees.
| Bill / Act | Subject |
|---|---|
| AP Official Language Act, 1966 | **Telugu** the official language |
| Draft AP Reorganisation Bill, Jan 2014 | United Assembly **rejected** it by resolution (voice vote); Parliament's law prevailed |
- **Budget Session 2026:** began 11 Feb 2026; MLAs' **biometric attendance** mandatory from this session.
- **Monsoon Session 2026:** summoned by Governor **S. Abdul Nazeer** from **17 Aug 2026** under **Art. 174(1)**.
