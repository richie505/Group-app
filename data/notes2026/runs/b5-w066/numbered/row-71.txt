## SUB 0 | PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana and Net Metering
[0:0] - **Launched Feb 2024; MNRE**; Central Sector scheme; outlay **₹75,021 crore**; goal = **rooftop solar on 1 crore residential houses** by **March 2027** – rural and urban (not urban-only).
[0:1] - **Up to 300 units/month free**; households become **prosumers**.
[0:2] - **CFA: 60% for the first 2 kW; 40% for extra capacity up to 3 kW** (not a flat 30%); collateral-free loans at about **7%** (repo-linked, not 4%); **100% online** national portal.
[0:3] - **Capacity building:** MNRE trains people at the grassroots in installation, operation, maintenance and repair; **more than 3 lakh skilled manpower** through fresh skilling and upskilling.
[0:4] - 30 GW rooftop; **one Model Solar Village per district**; **world's largest domestic rooftop solar scheme**; subsumed Rooftop Solar Phase II.
[0:5] - Progress (2025): 14.43 lakh systems, 18.14 lakh households.
[0:6] - **Net metering (UPSC 2016 ×2):** promotes **production and use of solar energy by households/consumers** – surplus fed to the grid; household pays only the difference.
[0:7] > Statements angle: UPSC/IAS 2025 – all three statements correct; RAS 2024 – only "1 crore houses / 300 units" and "100% online" correct.

## SUB 1 | UDAY and RDSS – Discom Finances
[1:0] - **UDAY (Ujwal DISCOM Assurance Yojana), Nov 2015, Ministry of Power:** **financial turnaround and revival of DISCOMs** (UPSC 2016).
[1:1] - States took over **75%** of discom debt (**50% in 2015-16 + 25% in 2016-17**) via **UDAY bonds**; discoms bonded the other 25%.
[1:2] - UDAY states account for **97%** of total outstanding discom debt (Power Ministry; APPSC G2); bonds actually issued covered ~86% of debt to be taken over (2017).
[1:3] - **Rajasthan** had the **largest discom debt exposure** under UDAY (~₹70,000+ crore bonded).

## SUB 2 | Saubhagya, DDUGJY and IPDS – Electrification
[2:0] - **Saubhagya** (Pradhan Mantri Sahaj Bijli Har Ghar Yojana), **Sept/Oct 2017:** **universal household electrification** – last-mile connections to all un-electrified households in **rural and urban** areas (CDS 2020; APPSC FSO).
[2:1]   - Not clean cooking fuel (that is **PMUY**), not LPG subsidy.
[2:2]   - **2.86 crore** willing households electrified (2.82 crore = older figure).
[2:3] - **DDUGJY (Dec 2014):** rural distribution; **feeder separation** (agri vs non-agri); subsumed **RGGVY**.
[2:4] - **IPDS (Dec 2014):** **urban** sub-transmission and distribution, metering, IT; subsumed **R-APDRP**.
[2:5] - Last village electrified: **Leisang (Manipur), 28 April 2018**.

## SUB 4 | Andhra Pradesh – Free Power and Rooftop Solar
[4:0] - **Free power for SC/ST households:** the Social Welfare Department paid bills up to **75 units** (APPSC question) → **100 units** (Dec 2018, Jagjeevan Jyothi) → **200 units** for BPL SC/ST since 2019 (now **20.26 lakh** households; ₹10,449.92 crore subsidy, FY 2024-25).
[4:1] - **SC/ST:** free rooftop solar via **Virtual Net Metering**.
[4:2] - PM Surya Ghar target in AP: **6 lakh** (6,000 per constituency).
[4:3] - PM-KUSUM in AP: **2.93 lakh** pumps solarised; RDSS ₹15,083.14 crore.
