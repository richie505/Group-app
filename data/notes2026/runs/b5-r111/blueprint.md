## Trends
- **Recall of the tool or organism dominates.** Bt / Bacillus thuringiensis, molecular scissors and golden rice beta-carotene repeat across APPSC, UPSC and other PSCs, often word for word (the UPSC 1999/2000/2001 Bt items reappear as "I.A.S." copies).
- **UPSC sets the statement items:** the 2011 gene-map uses, 2012 Bt brinjal objections, 2013 rDNA across species, 2018 GM mustard, 2021 adenovirus/retrovirus, 2024 apples and GM food, 2026 genetic medicine. Each turns on one overstated word ("all human diseases", "soil fungus", "terminator", "entire DNA").
- **Recent APPSC:** G2 Mains 2025 asked about the environmental concern (cross-pollination), FSO Mains 2023 asked about CRISPR and biotech applications, and G2 PYQs asked about GEAC and Maharashtra-Haryana defiance. Regulation (GEAC) is rising.
- **Weak keys:** PCR "using living cells" (no correct printed option, so add_option), UPSC 2018 GM mustard (the right "B only" option is missing, so add_option), UPSC 2012 GE traits (PMF gives all four, most keys give 1, 2 and 4), and terminator "all of the above".
