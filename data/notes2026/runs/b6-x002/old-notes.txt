## SUB 0 | Amaravati – the Capital Story
[2:0:0] # Timeline
[2:0:1] TABLE head: Year / Date | Event
  [2:0:1.0] 1956 | Andhra Pradesh formed
  [2:0:1.1] 2014 | AP Reorganisation Act (made under Article 3; created Telangana); Section 5: Hyderabad = common capital for not more than 10 years from 2 June 2014 (till 2024); it named no capital – only said "there shall be a new capital"
  [2:0:1.2] 2019 | Then government proposed three capitals: Visakhapatnam (executive), Amaravati (legislative), Kurnool (judicial)
  [2:0:1.3] 2020 | AP Decentralisation and Inclusive Development of All Regions Act, 2020 – repealed in 2021; judicial capital never operationalised
  [2:0:1.4] 2022 | AP High Court: State lacked legislative competence to shift the capital; legitimate expectation of land-pooling farmers; rule of law; judicial review under Articles 32 & 226
  [2:0:1.5] 28 Mar 2026 | AP Legislative Assembly resolution asking for the amendment – reflects cooperative federalism
  [2:0:1.6] 1–2 Apr 2026 | Parliament passes the AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Bill, 2026 (Lok Sabha 1 April, Rajya Sabha 2 April)
  [2:0:1.7] Assent | President Droupadi Murmu (Article 111) → AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Act, 2026; in force after Gazette notification by the Ministry of Law
  [2:0:1.8] Effect | Retrospective from 2 June 2024
[2:0:2] # What the 2026 Amendment Does
[2:0:3] - Amends **Section 5 (5(2))** of the 2014 Act: inserts the words **"at Amaravati"** – "there shall be a new capital" → **"Amaravati shall be the new capital"**.
[2:0:4] - Declares Amaravati the **sole and permanent capital**; **future changes to the capital area need Parliament's approval**.
[2:0:5] - **Amaravati** = the area notified under the **APCRDA Act, 2014**.
[2:0:6] - Broad support in Parliament, except **YSR Congress Party** (had pushed the three-capital model).
[2:0:7] # Amaravati Capital Project
[2:0:8] - Planned area ~**217 sq km**; developed through the **Land Pooling Scheme** (farmers get developed plots + compensation).
[2:0:9] - **APCRDA Act, 2014**: special planning body – **Town Planning Schemes**, **Betterment Charges**; overriding powers over Gram Panchayats in the capital region.
[2:0:10] - APCRDA hired a consultant (**Mar–Apr 2026**) for an Amaravati **governance framework** in line with the **73rd and 74th Amendments** (local self-government).
[2:0:11] - 2014 Act: the Governor of the common capital (Hyderabad) had **special responsibility for law and order and internal security**; **institutions of national importance stay with the Union**.
[2:0:12] The 2014 Act did **not** name Amaravati or any city; the **Constitution does not specify** how a State capital is chosen – statutory law fills the gap. The three-capital plan was a **later** proposal.
[2:0:13] # National Judicial Academy and India's First Justice City
[2:0:14] - CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu** urged the **Supreme Court** to set up the **National Judicial Academy (NJA)** in Amaravati, at a state-level **mediation conference** of the **AP State Legal Services Authority**; chief guest **CJI Justice Surya Kant**.
[2:0:15] - State offer: land, full administrative support; Amaravati as a **Model Judicial City** – a futuristic **blue-green field capital** aligned with **Viksit Bharat–2047**.
[2:0:16] - AP is set to set up **India's first Justice City** (capital region): an integrated legal hub – **High Court, NJA, Legal Services Authority, Mediation Centre, International Law University, Bar Council of India**, law colleges.
[2:0:17] - CJI: **mediation is a pillar of justice**, not merely an alternative mechanism; called for rigorous mediator training and transparent selection.
[2:0:18] - **Cabinet of 26 March 2026:** chaired by CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu**; approved the resolution to name Amaravati in **Section 5** of the 2014 Act, and **96 new courts** with **1,730 posts** (first phase, to cut the pendency of cases).
