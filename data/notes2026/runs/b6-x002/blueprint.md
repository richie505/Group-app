## 3. Keys of the app MCQs
- All 333 MCQ keys were checked against the library (CARE APPSC Feb–Jun 2026, Praveen CA-360 Jan–May 2026, PMF LENS 2026, Rocket CA 2026 Part 01, AP Budget 2026-27 speech, CivicCentre / daily CA files) and, for Dec 2025 items missing from the library, against the web. **No key was changed.**
- **3 keys carry a "Disputed" line** (keys.jsonl): NARI 2025 "safest city" (Visakhapatnam is among the top cities; Kohima is usually listed first); the Chief Secretary's date (order w.e.f. 1 March 2026, charge assumed 28 Feb 2026); NTR Bharosa 2026-27 outlay (₹33,485.10 crore State figure vs ₹27,719 crore Budget allocation).
- Spelling settled: CIC = **Vajji** Srinivasa Rao (Praveen CA-360 Jan 2026 p.53), not "Vajja".
- Old-note errors fixed: Section 22-A "pending" list had "freehold lands". The source names conditional patta, service inam and dotted lands. The police health-insurance scheme belongs to the **Visakhapatnam City Police** (The Hans India, 5 Dec 2025).
