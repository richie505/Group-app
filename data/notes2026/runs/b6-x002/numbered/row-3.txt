## SUB 2 | Policing: AI4AP, Enforcement Drives and Women's Safety
[2:0] # AI4AP Police project
[2:1] - **Pilot in Chittoor, Guntur and Annamayya** (February 2026); launched by DGP **Harish Kumar Gupta** at **Mangalagiri Police HQ**; integrated with **CCTNS**; AI **assists, not replaces** investigators.
