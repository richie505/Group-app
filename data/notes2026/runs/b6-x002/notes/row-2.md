=== Welfare Schemes, Renamed Schemes and P4 (Mar–May 2026)
# Divyang Shakti – free bus travel
- Inaugurated **18 March 2026** at **Mangalagiri (Guntur district)** by CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu**: **free travel on RTC buses** for persons with disabilities.
- Eligibility: **more than 40% disability**; **21 categories**; **helpers may accompany** beneficiaries.
- Buses: **City Ordinary, Metro Express, Palle Velugu, Ultra Palle Velugu, Express** – urban and rural (not AC/luxury services).
- About **12.76 lakh** beneficiaries; about **₹207 crore** a year.
# Disability framework and Akshaya Kshetram
- **Akshaya Kshetram** (near Tirumala, Tirupati): residential home for persons with **intellectual disabilities**; founded **2 October 1996** as a **daycare centre**; completed **30 years** in 2026.

# P4 Margadarsi–Bangaru Kutumbam – first anniversary
- **P4 = Public-Private-People Partnership**; launched on **Ugadi, 30 March 2025** at Amaravati.
- Model: the **top 10%** (**Margadarsi = mentor / donor**) support the **bottom 20%** (**Bangaru Kutumbam = beneficiary "golden family"**) – voluntary mentoring and aid, **not a government cash-transfer scheme**.
- **First-anniversary event, 30 March 2026:** Sri Padmavati Mahila Viswa Vidyalayam, **Tirupati**; **1.06 lakh Margadarsis** adopted about **10.30 lakh families**; companies adopted **8,904 families in Kuppam** under CSR.
- Goal restated: a **poverty-free society by 2047** (zero poverty was first targeted for 2029).

=== Amaravati – the Capital Act 2026
| Year / date | Event |
|---|---|
| 2014 | **AP Reorganisation Act** (under **Article 3**); **Section 5**: Hyderabad = common capital for **not more than 10 years** from **2 June 2014** (till **June 2024**); it **named no capital** – only "there shall be a new capital" |
| 2019 | Three capitals proposed: **Visakhapatnam (executive)**, Amaravati (legislative), **Kurnool (judicial)** |

| 26 Mar 2026 | Cabinet (CM Naidu) approved the resolution and **96 new courts** (1,730 posts) |
| 28 Mar 2026 | AP Assembly resolution asking Parliament to amend Section 5 |
| 1–2 Apr 2026 | Parliament passed the AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Bill, 2026 (LS 1 April, RS 2 April) |
| Assent | President **Droupadi Murmu** under **Article 111**; in force after Gazette notification |
| Effect | **Retrospective from 2 June 2024** |
- The 2026 Act amends **Section 5(2)**: inserts the words **"at Amaravati"** (not "at Visakhapatnam") → Amaravati is the **sole and permanent capital**; **future changes to the capital area need Parliament's approval**.
- **Amaravati** = the area notified under the **APCRDA Act, 2014**.
- The **Constitution does not specify** how a State capital is chosen – statute fills the gap.
