=== Cabinet Decisions, Collectors' Conference and Welfare Delivery (Dec 2025 – Feb 2026)
# Cabinet of 11 February 2026 (20 proposals)
- Approved **"AP AI Living Labs"**: the **AI Living Labs Foundation** – a **Section-8 non-profit company**, Amaravati; AI training to **at least one person per family**; provides **GPUs and datasets** (it does not work without them); free AI teaching kits.
- **State Data Centre** at the **IIIT Tirupati campus** (with disaster recovery), on the **BOOT** model.
- **House regularisation on government land** extended to **30 June 2026**; **7,189 water management works**, ₹344 crore.
- Draft bills: **Assigned Lands Amendment Bill–2026** and **Gram/Ward Secretariat Bill–2026**.
# Digital / biometric attendance for MLAs
- Decided by Assembly **Speaker Chintakayala Ayyanna Patrudu**: **biometric attendance for MLAs introduced in February 2026** (from the Budget Session), after YSRCP MLAs (11) stayed away.
- **Article 190**: a seat may be declared vacant if a member is absent **60 days without permission**.
# Collectors' Conference (December 2025)
- **AP Population Management Policy** to be introduced **by Ugadi 2026**; aim: an **ageing population** (not over-population); promote normal deliveries.
- **Family Cards**: **digitally integrate details of all welfare beneficiaries**.
- Jobs: **20 lakh promised (Yuva Galam)**; 4.5 lakh created.
- **Groundwater targets**: raise to **3 metres** below ground at the end of the monsoon; not to fall below **8 metres** before the next monsoon (8 m is the floor, not the monsoon-end target).
- Swarnandhra-2047 has **10** guiding principles (Padi Sutralu), not 5; aim to **double GSDP and per capita income every five years** (not ten).
# Zero excise on raw tobacco
- Excise on **unbranded/raw tobacco** (HS code **2401**) cut to **0%** from **1 February 2026**; notified by the **Finance Ministry (CBIC)**, not the Agriculture Ministry.
- Aim: **remove classification ambiguity and curb evasion**; CM Naidu **welcomed it** as a benefit to AP tobacco farmers.

=== Policing: AI4AP, Enforcement Drives and Women's Safety
# AI4AP Police project
- **Pilot in Chittoor, Guntur and Annamayya** (February 2026); launched by DGP **Harish Kumar Gupta** at **Mangalagiri Police HQ**; integrated with **CCTNS**; AI **assists, not replaces** investigators.
- Origin: **AI4 Andhra Police Hackathon (June 2025)** – India's first police-specific AI hackathon; **knowledge partner 4sightAI**, technology partner Sparity.
- **Eight modules**: complaint reception, digital evidence, social media monitoring, petition review, telecom/banking notices, investigation co-pilot, OCR for paper records, news tracking – **no facial-recognition training module**.
# Enforcement drives
| Drive | Key facts |
|---|---|

- **Visakhapatnam** projected by AP as the **"Safest City for Women"** after the **NARI 2025** report (NCW, Aug 2025) put it among the top safest cities (with Kohima, Bhubaneswar); police reach time **15 minutes urban**, 10 minutes rural.

=== Census 2027, Districts and Urban Administration
- AP's statewide **IEC campaign** for **Census 2027** = the **16th Census**, first in **16 years** (last 2011); India's first **digital** census, budget **₹11,718 crore**.
- **Phase 1 (House Listing and Housing Census): 1 April – 30 September 2026**; **District Collectors = Principal Census Officers**.
- **Delimitation will follow Census 2027**, not precede it.
- **District reorganisation**: objections invited for **30 days** (not 15); placed before a **cabinet subcommittee**; final decision by the **CM** (not the Governor).
- **Divisional Development Officer (DDO) offices** (4 December) to strengthen **Panchayat Raj**.
# SOP for de-siltation of urban drains
- Covers the **entire lifecycle – design, mapping, maintenance and monitoring** (not only pre-monsoon cleaning); GIS inventory, mechanised cleaning; **Municipal Commissioners personally responsible**.
