[0:2] # Look East
[0:3] - Articulated under **P.V. Narasimha Rao** (not Deve Gowda); aimed at **South-East Asia** (not South Asia); basis = **economic cooperation** (not counter-terrorism).
[0:4] - Drivers: **countering China**, new markets, instability in West and Central Asia.
[0:5] # Act East
[0:6] - Conceived as an **economic** initiative, later extended to **political, strategic and cultural** dimensions (true statement, APPSC G1 2024).
[0:7] - **Objectives (MEA):** promote **economic cooperation, cultural ties and strategic relationship with the Asia-Pacific**; **enhanced connectivity to the North-East**; it places emphasis on **India-ASEAN cooperation in India's domestic agenda** (infrastructure, manufacturing, smart cities, Make in India). "Peace and amity with neighbours of Asia" is **not** one of its listed objectives.
[0:8] - **3Cs**: **Commerce, Culture, Connectivity**; 4th C = **Capacity building** (not Climate action).
[0:9] - **North-East** = gateway to ASEAN.
[0:10] - ASEAN-linked projects: **Kaladan Multi-Modal** (India–Myanmar) and **India-Myanmar-Thailand Trilateral Highway**. **Akhaura–Agartala rail** is with **Bangladesh**, not ASEAN.
[0:11] TABLE head: Country | Upgrade | Year

  [0:11.2] Philippines | Strategic partner | Aug 2025

[0:12] # East Asia Summit (EAS)
[0:13] - **ASEAN-centred** forum; **chaired only by an ASEAN member**; **18 members** = 10 ASEAN + India, China, Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand, USA, Russia (so "only the ten ASEAN states" is wrong).
[0:14] - **13th EAS: Singapore, November 2018**; 14th EAS: Bangkok, November 2019 (IPOI).

[1:5] # Chabahar (Iran)
[1:6] - On the **Gulf of Oman**; gives India access to **Afghanistan and Central Asia without depending on Pakistan**; linked to **INSTC**.
[1:7] - **Oct 2017**: India's first **wheat consignment to Afghanistan** went **through Chabahar**, bypassing Pakistan.
[1:8] - In **2026** the USA **ended** (not extended) India's sanctions waiver for Chabahar.
[1:9] # Link West / Think West
[1:10] - Outreach to **West Asia (Gulf, Iran, Israel)** – not to the Western industrialised nations.
[1:11] - **GCC**: India's largest socio-economic partner in West Asia – over **$160 billion** trade, **10 million** diaspora.
[1:12] - PM Modi: 15 visits to GCC states and two to Israel (Mar 2026).
[1:13] - **I2U2 ("West Asia Quad")**: **India, Israel, UAE, USA**; first leaders' summit **virtual, 2022**; handled by MEA's **Economic Diplomacy Division**. Think West has widened into I2U2.

## SUB 2 | SAGAR, MAHASAGAR, SHANTI and Maritime Security
[2:0] TABLE head: Vision | Year / place | Full form
  [2:0.0] SAGAR | 2015, Mauritius | Security and Growth for All in the Region
  [2:0.1] MAHASAGAR | 2025, Mauritius | Mutual and Holistic Advancement for Security and Growth Across Regions
  [2:0.2] SHANTI | 13 July 2026, EAM Jaishankar | Securing Holistic Advancement through Norms, Trust and Integrity
[2:1] - SAGAR casts India as **net security provider** in the IOR; partly an answer to China's String of Pearls.
[2:2] - SHANTI: rules framework for shared sea spaces; first in the **Bay of Bengal**; launched with India's UNSC 2028–29 campaign.
[2:3] - Debate: US sinking of Iran's IRIS Dena (Mar 2026) challenges the "net security provider" claim.
[2:4] - **MAITRI** (Marine and Allied Interdisciplinary Training and Research Initiative): scientific arm of **DRDO's Sagar Maitri** (NPOL ship INS Sagardhwani), for collaboration with **IOR countries** (Oman, Maldives, Sri Lanka, Myanmar, Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia) – not an East Asia programme.
[2:5] # Maritime security partnerships
[2:6] - **IFC-IOR** (Information Fusion Centre – Indian Ocean Region): inaugurated **22 December 2018** at **IMAC, Gurugram**; aim = **maritime security / domain awareness** through information sharing (not a satellite mission); hosted by the **Indian Navy**, IMAC being **jointly administered by the Indian Navy and Indian Coast Guard**.
[2:7] - **Coastal surveillance radar chain**: **Sri Lanka, Maldives, Mauritius, Seychelles**; to be strengthened along with IORA (Mar 2026).
[2:8] - **Colombo Security Conclave**: began as a **2011 trilateral** of India, Sri Lanka, **Maldives**; now also Mauritius, Bangladesh and Seychelles.
[2:9] - AP angle: **MILAN 2026** at **Visakhapatnam** ties AP to Act East and SAGAR.
[2:10] - Flagships of the SAGAR doctrine: radar chain, IFC-IOR, island projects.
[2:11] > Exam angle: SAGAR and MAHASAGAR both announced in Mauritius; SHANTI by the EAM, not the PM.

## SUB 3 | Indo-Pacific Vision and IPOI
[3:0] - **Shangri-La Dialogue keynote, June 2018**: free, open, inclusive Indo-Pacific with **ASEAN centrality**. Dialogue held in **Singapore since 2002**, run by London-based **IISS**.
[3:1] - **IPOI** (Indo-Pacific Oceans Initiative): proposed at the **14th East Asia Summit, Bangkok, November 2019**; open, non-treaty, builds on SAGAR.
[3:2] # Seven IPOI pillars
[3:3] - 1. Maritime Security; 2. Maritime Ecology; 3. Maritime Resources; 4. Capacity Building and Resource Sharing; 5. Disaster Risk Reduction and Management; 6. Science, Technology and Academic Cooperation; 7. Trade Connectivity and Maritime Transport.
[3:4] - **Nuclear Disarmament Verification** is NOT an IPOI pillar.
[3:5] ~~ Sources: app notes 2:152 sub 6
