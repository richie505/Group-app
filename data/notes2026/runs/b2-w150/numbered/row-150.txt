## SUB 0 | Belt and Road Initiative and Encirclement
[0:0] - **Belt and Road Initiative (BRI)** = **One Belt One Road (OBOR)**, "21st Century Silk Road": proposed by **China's Xi Jinping, 2013** (UPSC 2016, 2018; asked 5 times) – land "Belt" + Maritime Silk "Road".
[0:1] - **India's objection**: **CPEC** passes through **PoJK** → India boycotted the **Belt and Road Forum (May 2017)** on **territorial sovereignty** grounds (not finance); India (June 2018): connectivity projects must respect **sovereignty and territorial integrity**.
[0:2] - **String of Pearls**: China's network of **military and commercial facilities** (ports) around India in the Indian Ocean – not Japanese or Pakistani.
[0:3] TABLE head: Port | Country
  [0:3.0] Hambantota | Sri Lanka
  [0:3.1] Gwadar | Pakistan
  [0:3.2] Chittagong | Bangladesh
  [0:3.3] Kyaukphyu | Myanmar
[0:4] - Military ties: **Hand-in-Hand** – India-China anti-terror army exercise, first **2007, Kunming**. China-Pakistan exercises: **Warrior** (army), **Shaheen** (air force), **Sea Guardian** (navy; first January 2020, Arabian Sea).

## SUB 1 | Boundary, LAC and Arunachal
[1:0] - India recognised the **PRC** on **30 December 1949**; ties from **1 April 1950** – **first non-socialist-bloc** country to do so.
[1:1] TABLE head: Line | Origin | Position
  [1:1.0] Johnson Line | 1865, W.H. Johnson | Aksai Chin in Ladakh – India's claim
  [1:1.1] Macartney-MacDonald Line | 1899 | Aksai Chin under China – China's preferred
  [1:1.2] McMahon Line | Simla Convention 1914, Henry McMahon | Eastern Tibet-India boundary; China rejects
[1:2] - China occupies ~**38,000 sq km** in Aksai Chin; claims ~**90,000 sq km** in the north-east (most of **Arunachal**, "South Tibet").
[1:3] - **Shaksgam Valley** (Trans-Karakoram Tract): ceded by Pakistan to China, **1963 Sino-Pakistan Boundary Agreement** (after the war, not a cause); fresh dispute **January 2026**.
[1:4] - **Arunachal**: China denied visas to Arunachal members of India's delegation to the **Fourth World Conference on Women, Beijing 1995**; the **USA** reaffirmed Arunachal as part of India.
[1:5] - **Diphu Pass** (Arunachal): the **India–China–Myanmar tri-junction**.
