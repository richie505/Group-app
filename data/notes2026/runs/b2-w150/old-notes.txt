[150:0:0] - **Belt and Road Initiative (BRI)** = **One Belt One Road (OBOR)**, "21st Century Silk Road": proposed by **China's Xi Jinping, 2013** (UPSC 2016, 2018; asked 5 times) – land "Belt" + Maritime Silk "Road".

=== NOTES 2:155 | United Nations – Principal Organs, Unga, Unsc and Reform; India's Candidature
## SUB 0 | Security Council
[155:0:0] - **15 members**: **5 permanent (P5) – China, France, Russia, UK, USA** + **10 non-permanent**.
[155:0:1] - Non-permanent members: elected by the **UNGA**, two-thirds secret ballot, for **2 years**; **no immediate re-election (Art. 23)**.
[155:0:2] - **Veto (Art. 27)**: only the P5; a P5 negative vote on a **substantive** matter blocks a decision; **procedural matters need 9 affirmative votes and no veto applies**. The Charter never uses the word "veto" (Art. 27(3) speaks of the "concurring votes of the permanent members").
[155:0:3] - **Germany, Japan, India, Canada are NOT** permanent members; India has been an elected (non-permanent) member, never a permanent one.
[155:0:4] - Only organ whose decisions **bind** all members (Ch. VII: Art. 41 sanctions, Art. 42 military action).
[155:0:5] - **P5 + 1** (Iran nuclear talks, JCPOA 2015): the **+1 = Germany**.
[155:0:6] - Resolutions: **2328 (Dec 2016)** – UN monitors for evacuations from **Aleppo** (Syria); **2428 (July 2018)** – arms embargo on **South Sudan**.
[155:0:7] - **April 2024**: full UN membership for **Palestine** – **vetoed by the USA**; UK and Switzerland abstained.
