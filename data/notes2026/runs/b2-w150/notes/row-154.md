=== India-Japan
- Status: **Special Strategic and Global Partnership**.
- **First India-Japan 2+2 Ministerial Dialogue: New Delhi, 30 November 2019**.
- **Mumbai-Ahmedabad High Speed Rail** – Japan's **Shinkansen** technology, Japanese **ODA**.
- Act East Forum; **ACSA** (Acquisition and Cross-Servicing Agreement) – **2020**.
- **15th Summit** (2025): **10 trillion yen** (~$67 bn) over ten years; 16th Summit 2026.
- Trade **$27.47 bn** (2025-26); Japan = **5th-largest** FDI source (not the largest).
- Culture: **Shintoism** = indigenous religion of **Japan**; **Ikebana** = Japanese art of **flower arrangement** (origami = paper folding, bonsai = miniature trees).

=== India-Gulf and Iran
- **UAE**: Comprehensive Strategic Partnership (**2017**); **3rd-largest** trading partner (over $100 bn); **first foreign investor** in India's **SPR at Mangaluru**.
- **Republic Day 2017**: chief guest Abu Dhabi Crown Prince; a **UAE armed forces contingent led the parade** – first foreign contingent after France (2016).
- **Saudi Arabia**: Strategic Partnership Council (**2019**); PM **Modi** received Saudi Arabia's highest civilian award, the **Order of King Abdulaziz (April 2016)**.
- **IMEC** (G20 New Delhi, **Sept 2023**): east (India-Gulf) + northern (Gulf-Europe) corridors.
- **Oman CEPA**: signed Dec 2025, in force **1 June 2026**; 99% duty-free; India's **2nd** GCC CEPA after UAE. India-GCC FTA talks on (2026).
- **Remittances:** **UAE** = India's **second-largest** source – 19.2%, after the US (27.7%) (RBI survey, 2023-24).
- **Iran** = predominantly **Shia** (Twelver Shia state religion, about 90% of Muslims); Iraq also Shia-majority but less so; Pakistan, Afghanistan Sunni-majority.
> Exam angle: UAE is the 3rd-largest (not 2nd-largest) trading partner but the 2nd-largest remittance source.

=== India-Israel and the Arab-Israeli Question
- Full diplomatic relations: **1992**, **P.V. Narasimha Rao** government.
- **Narendra Modi** = first Indian PM to visit Israel (**2017**); visited twice by March 2026.
- Status: **Special Strategic Partnership**; **defence (incl. joint R&D)** = main pillar.
- **Indo-Israel Centres of Excellence**: AP centre at **Kuppam** – vegetables and flowers.
- Bilateral Investment Agreement – Sept 2025; FTA Terms of Reference – Nov 2025; trade $3.75 bn (2024-25).
- **I2U2** = India, Israel, **UAE**, USA.
- **Abraham Accords (2020)**: Israel normalised ties with Arab States – **UAE**, **Bahrain**, Morocco, Sudan – so "Israel has diplomatic relations with some Arab States" is true (Egypt 1979, Jordan 1994 earlier).
- **Arab Peace Initiative (2002)**: a **Saudi proposal endorsed by the Arab League** (Beirut summit) – **never signed by Israel**.
- **Yom Kippur War (October 1973)**: **Israel vs Arab states led by Egypt and Syria**.
- **Yair Lapid** became Israel's **14th Prime Minister** on 1 July 2022 (caretaker, after Naftali Bennett).

=== India-EU, Russia and Trade Agreements
# India-EU
- **BTIA** (**Broad-based Trade and Investment Agreement**) – India-**EU** FTA talks since **2007** (UPSC 2017).
- **Trade and Technology Council** – **2022**; first Strategic Dialogue – 2025.
- **16th India-EU Summit** (India, 2026): FTA concluded → EU = India's **22nd FTA partner**; Security and Defence Partnership signed the same day.
# India-Russia
- **Declaration on Strategic Partnership – 2000**; elevated to **Special and Privileged Strategic Partnership – 2010**.
- Anchors: **1971 Treaty** of Peace, Friendship and Cooperation; **Bhilai**, **Bokaro** plants (Soviet Union).
- **S-400** deal **2018**; **BrahMos** JV (**Brahmaputra + Moskva**); **Kudankulam** nuclear plant – **Tamil Nadu** (Kovvada – AP).
- ~**96%** of trade in national currencies.
- **INSTC**: sea from Mumbai to **Bandar Abbas** (Iran), then road/rail through Iran and the Caspian region to Russia; Chabahar being linked.
- **23rd Annual Summit** (New Delhi): 25th anniversary; $100 bn by 2030; "Programme 2030"; Russia joined the **International Big Cat Alliance** (not I2U2).
