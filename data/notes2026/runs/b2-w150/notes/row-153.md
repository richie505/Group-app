=== Status and US Doctrines
- Present status: **Comprehensive Global Strategic Partnership**.
- The **2008 civil nuclear deal** = turning point in India-US ties.
| Doctrine / law | Year | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Pressler Amendment** | 1985 | Stopped US aid to **Pakistan** unless the President certified it had no nuclear device; applied in 1990 |
| **Pivot to Asia / Rebalance** | **2011** (Obama era) | **USA**'s foreign-policy shift of focus to the Asia-Pacific |
| CAATSA | 2017 | Sanctions on buyers of **Russian arms**; India sought a waiver over the **S-400** |
- **Larry Pressler** = US Senator; linked aid to non-proliferation; **criticised the proposed US military help to Pakistan** (Clinton era) over its nuclear programme.
> Exam angle: Pressler targeted Pakistan, not India; 'Pivot to Asia' is a US plan.
~~ Sources: app notes 2:153 sub 0; general knowledge (Pressler, not checked)

=== Diaspora and Visas
- US has the world's **largest PIO community** (Malaysia second) (Feb 2026).
- 2020 Census: about **4.4 million** "Indian-alone" – largest Asian-alone group.
- **Remittances**: US is the largest source – **27.7%** (RBI survey, 2023-24), ahead of the UAE.
- **Telugu** = fastest-growing foreign language in the US (2010-17).
- **Dalip Singh Saund:** first **Indian American** (and first Asian American) elected to the US Congress – House of Representatives, California, **1956**.
- **Pramila Jayapal** (Washington, 2016): **first Indian-American woman elected to the US House of Representatives** (Kamala Harris – Senate, then Vice President; Ami Bera – a man).
- **H-1B**: **June 2023** (PM Modi's state visit) – the **US** announced **in-country renewal** of H-1B visas (pilot); **$100,000 fee** on new petitions from **21 Sept 2025**; Indians get about **71%** of approved H-1Bs.

=== Civil Nuclear Cooperation
- Order: **Hyde Act 2006** (signed **18 December 2006**; made the deal possible under US law) → **123 Agreement 2008** → **NSG clean waiver, Sept 2008** (allowed civil nuclear trade).
- Under iCET: talks on **Westinghouse AP1000** reactors and SMRs/AMRs.
- AP angle: **Kovvada (Srikakulam district)** = proposed site for the Westinghouse AP1000 reactors.
