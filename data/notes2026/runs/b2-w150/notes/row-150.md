=== Belt and Road Initiative and Encirclement
- **Belt and Road Initiative (BRI)** = **One Belt One Road (OBOR)**, "21st Century Silk Road": proposed by **China's Xi Jinping, 2013** (UPSC 2016, 2018; asked 5 times) – land "Belt" + Maritime Silk "Road".
- **India's objection**: **CPEC** passes through **PoJK** → India boycotted the **Belt and Road Forum (May 2017)** on **territorial sovereignty** grounds (not finance); India (June 2018): connectivity projects must respect **sovereignty and territorial integrity**.
- **String of Pearls**: China's network of **military and commercial facilities** (ports) around India in the Indian Ocean – not Japanese or Pakistani.
| Port | Country |
|---|---|
| Hambantota | Sri Lanka |
| Gwadar | Pakistan |
| Chittagong | Bangladesh |
| Kyaukphyu | Myanmar |
- Military ties: **Hand-in-Hand** – India-China anti-terror army exercise, first **2007, Kunming**. China-Pakistan exercises: **Warrior** (army), **Shaheen** (air force), **Sea Guardian** (navy; first January 2020, Arabian Sea).

=== Boundary, LAC and Arunachal
- India recognised the **PRC** on **30 December 1949**; ties from **1 April 1950** – **first non-socialist-bloc** country to do so.
| Line | Origin | Position |
|---|---|---|
| Johnson Line | 1865, W.H. Johnson | Aksai Chin in Ladakh – India's claim |
| Macartney-MacDonald Line | 1899 | Aksai Chin under China – China's preferred |
| McMahon Line | Simla Convention 1914, Henry McMahon | Eastern Tibet-India boundary; China rejects |
- China occupies ~**38,000 sq km** in Aksai Chin; claims ~**90,000 sq km** in the north-east (most of **Arunachal**, "South Tibet").
- **Shaksgam Valley** (Trans-Karakoram Tract): ceded by Pakistan to China, **1963 Sino-Pakistan Boundary Agreement** (after the war, not a cause); fresh dispute **January 2026**.
- **Arunachal**: China denied visas to Arunachal members of India's delegation to the **Fourth World Conference on Women, Beijing 1995**; the **USA** reaffirmed Arunachal as part of India.
- **Diphu Pass** (Arunachal): the **India–China–Myanmar tri-junction**.

- Disengagement: **Pangong Tso** (2021); **Gogra-Hot Springs** (2022); Galwan; **Depsang and Demchok** – **October 2024** understanding, backed at the **Modi–Xi Kazan** meeting.
- 2025–26: PLA deployment fell but **10 combined arms brigades** remain; **Mudh-Nyoma** airbase (~13,000 ft, BRO) in **eastern Ladakh** operational.
# 1962 war
- **20 October – 21 November 1962**; ended in China's **unilateral ceasefire**.
- Causes: unsettled boundary, **Tibet** and Dalai Lama's asylum (**1959**), India's **Forward Policy**. Sectors: Aksai Chin and **NEFA**.
- **Henderson Brooks-Bhagat Report (1963)**: India's military failure. Blow to NAM idealism; India sought Western military aid.

=== Trade, Water and Forums
- Trade deficit about **$100 billion** (Jul 2026); dependence: machinery, **APIs**, electronics, **solar**.
- **Strategic Economic Dialogue**: 2010; six rounds (2011–2019).
- **App bans (2020)**: **Section 69A, IT Act**. **Press Note 3 (2020)**: FDI from land-border countries needs **government approval** (not a ban).
- **Yarlung Tsangpo** (Brahmaputra): Chinese **mega dam**; India raised concern Aug 2025.
- **Expert Level Mechanism (2006)**; flood-season data MoUs for **Brahmaputra and Sutlej**.
- **Kailash Mansarovar Yatra** via **Lipulekh** and **Nathu La** (border trade reopened **2006**).
- **AIIB**: HQ **Beijing**; India a **founding member**. **18th BRICS Summit**: New Delhi, Sep 2026.
