=== Look East to Act East, the East Asia Summit and Myanmar
# Policies at a glance
| Policy | Launched | PM | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Look East** | **1991–92** | **P.V. Narasimha Rao** | Economic: trade and investment with **South-East and East Asia** (ASEAN), AIFTA |
| **Act East** | **2014**, **East Asia Summit, Myanmar** (not a SAARC Summit) | **Narendra Modi** | + political, strategic, security, cultural; Indo-Pacific |
| Neighbourhood First | May 2014 | Modi | Physical, digital, people-to-people connectivity in South Asia |
| Connect Central Asia | 2012, Bishkek | — | Central Asia |
| **Link West / Think West** | — | — | **Gulf, Iran, Israel** (West Asia): energy, trade, diaspora, counter-terrorism |
# Look East
- Articulated under **P.V. Narasimha Rao** (not Deve Gowda); aimed at **South-East Asia** (not South Asia); basis = **economic cooperation** (not counter-terrorism).
- Drivers: **countering China**, new markets, instability in West and Central Asia.
# Act East
- Conceived as an **economic** initiative, later extended to **political, strategic and cultural** dimensions (true statement, APPSC G1 2024).
- **Objectives (MEA):** promote **economic cooperation, cultural ties and strategic relationship with the Asia-Pacific**; **enhanced connectivity to the North-East**; it places emphasis on **India-ASEAN cooperation in India's domestic agenda** (infrastructure, manufacturing, smart cities, Make in India). "Peace and amity with neighbours of Asia" is **not** one of its listed objectives.
- **3Cs**: **Commerce, Culture, Connectivity**; 4th C = **Capacity building** (not Climate action).
- **North-East** = gateway to ASEAN.
- ASEAN-linked projects: **Kaladan Multi-Modal** (India–Myanmar) and **India-Myanmar-Thailand Trilateral Highway**. **Akhaura–Agartala rail** is with **Bangladesh**, not ASEAN.

# East Asia Summit (EAS)
- **ASEAN-centred** forum; **chaired only by an ASEAN member**; **18 members** = 10 ASEAN + India, China, Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand, USA, Russia (so "only the ten ASEAN states" is wrong).
- **13th EAS: Singapore, November 2018**; 14th EAS: Bangkok, November 2019 (IPOI).

=== Connect Central Asia, Chabahar and Link West
# Connect Central Asia
| Event | Year / place | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Connect Central Asia policy | **2012, Bishkek** | — |
| 1st India-Central Asia Dialogue (foreign ministers) | **Samarkand, Uzbekistan, 2019** | **Afghanistan** invited |
| 1st **India-Central Asia Summit** (leaders) | **January 2022, virtual** | Institutionalised the Connect Central Asia policy |
- 2025: all five – Kazakhstan, Kyrgyz Republic, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan – sent delegations to India.
- **India–Uzbekistan CSP (Aug 2026)**: **$5 billion** trade target by 2030; uranium-supply framework.
- Exercises: **DUSTLIK** = India–**Uzbekistan** (first edition, Nov 2019, Tashkent); Khanjar = Kyrgyzstan.
# Chabahar (Iran)
- On the **Gulf of Oman**; gives India access to **Afghanistan and Central Asia without depending on Pakistan**; linked to **INSTC**.
- **Oct 2017**: India's first **wheat consignment to Afghanistan** went **through Chabahar**, bypassing Pakistan.
- In **2026** the USA **ended** (not extended) India's sanctions waiver for Chabahar.
# Link West / Think West
- Outreach to **West Asia (Gulf, Iran, Israel)** – not to the Western industrialised nations.
- **GCC**: India's largest socio-economic partner in West Asia – over **$160 billion** trade, **10 million** diaspora.
- PM Modi: 15 visits to GCC states and two to Israel (Mar 2026).
- **I2U2 ("West Asia Quad")**: **India, Israel, UAE, USA**; first leaders' summit **virtual, 2022**; handled by MEA's **Economic Diplomacy Division**. Think West has widened into I2U2.

=== SAGAR, MAHASAGAR, SHANTI and Maritime Security
| Vision | Year / place | Full form |
|---|---|---|
| **SAGAR** | **2015, Mauritius** | **Security and Growth for All in the Region** |
| **MAHASAGAR** | **2025**, Mauritius | Mutual and Holistic Advancement for Security and Growth Across Regions |
| SHANTI | 13 July 2026, **EAM Jaishankar** | Securing Holistic Advancement through Norms, Trust and Integrity |
- SAGAR casts India as **net security provider** in the IOR; partly an answer to China's String of Pearls.
- SHANTI: rules framework for shared sea spaces; first in the **Bay of Bengal**; launched with India's UNSC 2028–29 campaign.
- Debate: US sinking of Iran's IRIS Dena (Mar 2026) challenges the "net security provider" claim.
- **MAITRI** (Marine and Allied Interdisciplinary Training and Research Initiative): scientific arm of **DRDO's Sagar Maitri** (NPOL ship INS Sagardhwani), for collaboration with **IOR countries** (Oman, Maldives, Sri Lanka, Myanmar, Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia) – not an East Asia programme.
# Maritime security partnerships
- **IFC-IOR** (Information Fusion Centre – Indian Ocean Region): inaugurated **22 December 2018** at **IMAC, Gurugram**; aim = **maritime security / domain awareness** through information sharing (not a satellite mission); hosted by the **Indian Navy**, IMAC being **jointly administered by the Indian Navy and Indian Coast Guard**.
- **Coastal surveillance radar chain**: **Sri Lanka, Maldives, Mauritius, Seychelles**; to be strengthened along with IORA (Mar 2026).
- **Colombo Security Conclave**: began as a **2011 trilateral** of India, Sri Lanka, **Maldives**; now also Mauritius, Bangladesh and Seychelles.
- AP angle: **MILAN 2026** at **Visakhapatnam** ties AP to Act East and SAGAR.
- Flagships of the SAGAR doctrine: radar chain, IFC-IOR, island projects.
> Exam angle: SAGAR and MAHASAGAR both announced in Mauritius; SHANTI by the EAM, not the PM.

=== Indo-Pacific Vision and IPOI
- **Shangri-La Dialogue keynote, June 2018**: free, open, inclusive Indo-Pacific with **ASEAN centrality**. Dialogue held in **Singapore since 2002**, run by London-based **IISS**.
- **IPOI** (Indo-Pacific Oceans Initiative): proposed at the **14th East Asia Summit, Bangkok, November 2019**; open, non-treaty, builds on SAGAR.
# Seven IPOI pillars
- 1. Maritime Security; 2. Maritime Ecology; 3. Maritime Resources; 4. Capacity Building and Resource Sharing; 5. Disaster Risk Reduction and Management; 6. Science, Technology and Academic Cooperation; 7. Trade Connectivity and Maritime Transport.
- **Nuclear Disarmament Verification** is NOT an IPOI pillar.

=== Neighbourhood First and Island Projects
- Launch: **SAARC leaders invited to the PM's swearing-in (May 2014)**; **BIMSTEC** leaders in **2019**.
- Roots: **Gujral Doctrine** — **non-reciprocity**, non-interference, no use of territory against a neighbour.
- Examples: COVID vaccines to neighbours, Bhutan hydropower, Kaladan, Trilateral Highway, Maldives High Impact Community Development Projects, Sri Lanka rooftop solar.
