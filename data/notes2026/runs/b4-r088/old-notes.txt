## SUB 1 | Cotton Textiles
[88:1:0] - Began near **black-soil cotton tracts** of Maharashtra and Gujarat; then **dispersed** because cotton is **not weight-losing** (pure material) and markets, power and rail spread.
[88:1:1] - **Coimbatore** (TN) spinning: **hydel power, cheap labour, local cotton**.
[88:1:2] - Centres: Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Surat, Sholapur, Nagpur, Indore, Coimbatore, Madurai, Kanpur. **Ludhiana** = hosiery (industrial capital of Punjab).
[88:1:3] - Woollen: Kanpur, Dhariwal, Ludhiana, Jamnagar, Srinagar. **Kota** = synthetic silk.

## SUB 2 | Jute
[88:2:0] - About **90%** of capacity in a **100 km x 3 km belt** along the **Hooghly** (most mills in the Hugli basin); **Bhatpara** = jute centre.
[88:2:1] - Reasons: raw jute of the Ganga-Brahmaputra delta, **soft water for retting**, Hooghly waterway, **Kolkata port**, cheap labour from WB, Bihar, UP, gunny-bag market (NOT iron ore).
[88:2:2] - **97 mills** (2026): West Bengal **71**, **AP 12** (2nd); WB over 80% of output. AP mills: **Chittivalasa, Nellimarla** (since the 1920s).
[88:2:3] - Largest jute area: West Bengal (0.43 million ha, 2024-25). Largest exporter: **Bangladesh** (~75%).
[88:2:4] TABLE head: Act / scheme | Provision
  [88:2:4.0] Jute Packaging Materials Act, 1987 | 100% foodgrains, 20% sugar in jute bags
  [88:2:4.1] National Jute Policy | 2005
  [88:2:4.2] JUTE-ICARE | Certified seed; microbial retting with SONA
  [88:2:4.3] JCI MSP | Raw jute ₹5,650/quintal
[88:2:5] - Raw-jute scarcity crisis in WB mills (2 Jan 2026).
## SUB 3 | Sugar
[88:3:0] # Location and Data
[88:3:1] - **2nd largest agro-based industry** after cotton textiles.
[88:3:2] - Mills within about **20 km** of cane: cane is **perishable**, yields only 9–12% sugar.
[88:3:3] - India = **2nd largest producer after Brazil** (~19%) and **largest consumer**; 2nd in sugarcane (FAO 2024).
[88:3:4] - 534 factories (2024-25), about equal private and cooperative.
[88:3:5] TABLE head: State | Long-run share
  [88:3:5.0] Uttar Pradesh | 35%
  [88:3:5.1] Maharashtra | 22%
  [88:3:5.2] Karnataka | 10%
  [88:3:5.3] Tamil Nadu | 8%
[88:3:6] - **Latest season (2026)**: **Maharashtra led** (~9.9 MT), UP 2nd (~8.9 MT), Karnataka 3rd (ISMA, 31 Dec 2025: Maharashtra top).
[88:3:7] - Belts: western UP (Meerut-Saharanpur-Muzaffarnagar), eastern UP (Gorakhpur-Deoria-Basti); AP: West and East Godavari, Visakhapatnam, Chittoor (not Nandyal).
[88:3:8] - **Shakkar Nagar** (Bodhan, Nizamabad; now Telangana) grew around the Nizam Sugar Factory.
[88:3:9] - Cuba = "Sugar Bowl of the world"; Brazil now largest exporter; **Ukraine** makes sugar almost entirely from **beet**.

  [88:3:11.2] Older private mills (Bihar mostly non-cooperative) | Cooperative mills – Nasik, Pune, Satara, Sangli, Kolhapur, Sholapur

## SUB 4 | Cement
[88:4:0] - Inputs: **limestone** (main), silica, alumina, **gypsum**, coal; plants sit in limestone areas.
[88:4:1] - India = **2nd** producer (over 8%; 426.29 MT in 2023-24); China 1st; per capita ~290 kg vs world 540 kg.
[88:4:2] - **Largest state (2026): Rajasthan**, then AP, Telangana, Gujarat, Chhattisgarh, TN. South India over 30% of capacity.
[88:4:3] - Clusters: **Satna, Katni** (MP), **Chittoor** (AP), **Nalgonda** (Telangana), Khilari, Lakheri, Chunar, Dalla, Churk.
[88:4:4] - AP: **Kadapa-Kurnool limestone** makes Rayalaseema a cement hub – **Yerraguntla** (Kadapa), **Jaggayyapeta** (NTR), **Nandyal**.
[88:4:5] - Green cements: **PPC** (fly ash from thermal plants) and **PSC** (slag from steel plants).

## SUB 6 | Fertilisers and Petrochemicals
[88:6:0] # Feedstock
[88:6:1] - **Urea** = ammonia (**Haber process**) + CO2; **natural gas (methane)** is ammonia feedstock (HBJ pipeline).
[88:6:2] - **Superphosphate** = **rock phosphate + sulphuric acid**; sulphur mainly from **oil refineries**.
[88:6:3] - Inputs: naphtha, natural gas, coal, rock phosphate, sulphur, gypsum; steel slag and coke. **Caustic soda is NOT** a fertiliser input.
[88:6:4] # Plants and Regions

  [88:6:5.2] North-West | Nangal (NFL), Bhatinda, Panipat

  [88:6:5.4] AP | Kakinada (Nagarjuna, Coromandel)
[88:6:6] - Coal-based FCI units: Talcher, Ramagundam, **Korba**; Ramagundam revived on gas (2021).
[88:6:7] - **Phulpur** (Prayagraj) = biggest cooperative fertiliser factory. Haldia, Sri Ganganagar = fertilisers.
[88:6:8] - **Gujarat** = largest fertiliser producer; **UP** = largest urea producer; India 2nd consumer. Urea record 314.07 LMT (2023-24).
