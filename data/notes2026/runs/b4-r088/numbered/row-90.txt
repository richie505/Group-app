## SUB 2 | Paper, Pulp and Timber – World Leaders
[2:0] - **Pulp and paper "most developed":** **USA and Canada** (softwood, hydel power, water); **Canada** exports wood pulp to the USA, Europe, Asia. The APPSC 2002 question was keyed both ways (Canada; **Mexico**).
[2:1] - **Soft timber and wood pulp (when asked):** **Canada** largest producer and exporter. **Now (FAO 2022):** pulp – producer **USA (23.5%)**, exporter **Brazil (29%)**; sawn wood exporter **Russia (20%)**, Canada 2nd.
[2:2] - **Pulp woods:** **poplar** (woody), also eucalyptus, bamboo; bagasse and rice straw are non-woody. Recycled fibre 71-75% of raw material.
[2:3] - Paper is **capital-intensive**; top paper exporters Finland, Sweden, Canada; China = largest furniture exporter; rayon led by China, India, Indonesia. India per capita paper use ~16 kg (world 57).
[2:4] ~~ Sources: PMF Human & Economic Geography Part 5 p.86, p.91; app notes 4:90 sub 7
