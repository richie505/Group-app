=== IT and Software
- IT is **footloose**: pulled by engineering talent and a tech ecosystem, not raw materials. **STPI** set up **1991**.
- **Bengaluru = "Silicon Valley of India"** and the city with the greatest IT capacity; **Karnataka = "Silicon State"** and the largest software exporter; **Hyderabad = "Cyberabad"**.

=== Electronics, Semiconductors and Pharmaceuticals
- **India Semiconductor Mission:** 2021. **Dholera** = fab; **Sanand** = Micron ATMP; **Jagiroad** (Assam) = Tata ₹27,000-crore plant (Mar 2024).
- Electronics PLI grew out of **National Policy on Electronics 2019**. AP electronics target (2024-29): **US$50 bn**.
- **Pharmacy of the World:** **3rd by volume, 11th by value**. Hubs: **Hyderabad (bulk drugs)**, Ahmedabad-Vadodara, **Parawada Pharma City (Visakhapatnam)**. **Bulk Drug Parks:** AP, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh.
