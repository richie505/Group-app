=== Classification by Raw Material – Agro, Mineral, Forest, Animal, Marine
| Class | Features | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Agro-based | Farm produce; near raw material (perishable, weight-losing) | Cotton textile, jute, sugar, **vanaspati, cigarettes**, tea, **paper** (bagasse, bamboo) |
| Mineral-based | Capital-intensive; near mines | Iron and steel, aluminium, **cement**, copper smelting |
| Forest-based | Timber + minor forest produce | **Pulp and paper (largest)**, furniture, plywood, matchsticks, **lac**, **rayon** |
| Animal-based | Animal produce | **Dairy**, leather, wool |
| Marine-based | Sea produce | Seafood and coastal aquaculture processing |
- **Cement is NOT agro-based** (limestone = mineral) (APPSC G-I 2008).
- **Rayon = "artificial silk"**, made from **wood pulp** (cellulose) (APPSC G-I 2002).
- **Pollutant–industry:** **bleaching liquor – paper and pulp** ✓; cellulose fibres are not a pharma pollutant, drugs/antibiotics not a mining pollutant (SSC 2017: only A).
- **Leather:** UP State Leather Development and Marketing Corporation – **Agra** (printed key). AP: Nellore – leather, auto parts.
~~ Sources: OnlyIAS NCERT Human Geography Part 7 p.13; app notes 4:90 sub 0, 2

=== Fuel-based and Manpower-based Industries; Water and Coal Use
| Class | Basis | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Fuel-based (energy-based) | Coal, petroleum, gas, nuclear | Thermal power, oil refining, petrochemicals, gas-based fertilisers |
| Manpower-based (labour-intensive) | High human labour | Handloom, handicrafts, leather, garments |
- **Maximum industrial water consumer = thermal power** (~**88%**); engineering ~5%, paper ~2.3%, textiles ~2% (UPSC 2013).
- **Maximum coal use = energy (power) production**, not steel, railways or ships.
- AP: Mallavalli (Krishna) Mega Food Park; Prakasam, Kurnool – mineral-based.

=== Paper, Pulp and Timber – World Leaders
- **Pulp and paper "most developed":** **USA and Canada** (softwood, hydel power, water); **Canada** exports wood pulp to the USA, Europe, Asia. The APPSC 2002 question was keyed both ways (Canada; **Mexico**).
- **Soft timber and wood pulp (when asked):** **Canada** largest producer and exporter. **Now (FAO 2022):** pulp – producer **USA (23.5%)**, exporter **Brazil (29%)**; sawn wood exporter **Russia (20%)**, Canada 2nd.
- **Pulp woods:** **poplar** (woody), also eucalyptus, bamboo; bagasse and rice straw are non-woody. Recycled fibre 71-75% of raw material.
- Paper is **capital-intensive**; top paper exporters Finland, Sweden, Canada; China = largest furniture exporter; rayon led by China, India, Indonesia. India per capita paper use ~16 kg (world 57).

=== MSMEs and Use-based Classes
- **Ministry of MSME (2007)** = merger of Ministry of Small Scale Industries + Ministry of Agro and Rural Industries. **2020 definition:** composite **investment + turnover**, no manufacturing/services difference.
| Class (from 1 Apr 2025) | Investment up to | Turnover up to |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | ₹2.5 crore | ₹10 crore |
| Small | ₹25 crore | ₹100 crore |
| Medium | ₹125 crore | ₹500 crore |
- **IIP use-based classes (six):** primary, capital, intermediate, infrastructure/construction, consumer durables (appliances), consumer non-durables – **no "export goods"**.
| Term | Meaning / examples |
|---|---|
| Basic / primary goods | Steel, cement, coal |
| Heavy | Bulky inputs and outputs, capital-intensive: iron and steel, HEC Ranchi |
| Light | Electronics assembly, garments |
| Footloose | Diamond polishing, software |
| Sunrise / sunset | AI, green hydrogen, EVs, semiconductors / legacy print |
- **Quinary** sector = top decision-making, **"gold collar"**.
