## SUB 0 | Plans I to V and the Plan Holiday (1951-79)
[10:0:0] TABLE head: Plan | Period | Model | Main objective / tagline
  [10:0:0.0] 1st | 1951-56 (launched 1 April 1951) | Harrod-Domar | Agriculture, irrigation, power (Bhakra Nangal, Hirakud); food, Partition rehabilitation, price stability
  [10:0:0.1] 2nd | 1956-61 (began 1956, year of IPR) | Nehru-Mahalanobis | Rapid industrialisation, heavy and basic industries; socialistic pattern of society
  [10:0:0.2] 3rd | 1961-66 ("Gadgil Yojana") | Sandy-Chakravarty | Self-reliant, self-generating economy ("make the economy independent"); self-reliance with priority to agriculture
  [10:0:0.3] Plan Holiday | 1 April 1966 – 31 March 1969 | — | Three Annual Plans; Green Revolution (1966-67); agriculture and industry equal weight
  [10:0:0.4] 4th | 1969-74 (finally launched 1969) | Manne-Rudra | Growth with stability and progressive achievement of self-reliance; 14 banks nationalised 1969
  [10:0:0.5] 5th | 1974-79 (ended 1978) | Dhar (final draft by D.P. Dhar) | Removal of poverty (Garibi Hatao) and self-reliance; control of inflation; zero net foreign aid
[10:0:1] - "**Garibi Hatao**" – Indira Gandhi's slogan from the **1971 elections**; became the **Fifth Plan's** basic objective.
[10:0:2] - **Second Plan** – the "Mahalanobis Plan" (two- and four-sector model); real planning began here; first proposed **land reforms through cooperative land management**.
[10:0:3] - **Fourth Plan** – self-sustaining development first adopted; **Growth Centre Approach** for regional disparities.
[10:0:4] - **Fifth Plan** – serious concern for **unemployment** begins; anti-poverty programmes first started.

[10:5:6] - Eighth Plan: target growth 5.6%, industrial growth **7.5%**; per capita output grew 3.9%; agriculture 3.6% (target 3.5%); industry 8.5%; maximum cumulative real GNP growth among plans asked; growth said to touch ~9% at peak.
[10:5:7] - Tenth Plan: **investment rate 32.10% > savings rate 26.84%**.
[10:5:8] - Ninth Plan: ICOR ~**4.3** envisaged.
[10:5:9] - Higher average growth with rising saving ratio: Sixth 5.7%, Seventh 6.0%, Eighth 6.8%.
[10:5:10] # GDP Record – Highest and Lowest Years
[10:5:11] - **1950-51:** ~3.6%.
[10:5:12] - **Lowest year since 1961 (excluding Covid):** **1979, −5.2%** (drought, second oil shock); 2020-21 **−5.8%**; other negative years 1965-66, 1972-73.
[10:5:13] - 1990-91: **1.1%**; 1991-92 below 1%; 1992-94 5%; 1994-95 6.3%.
[10:5:14] - 2000-02: about half the Tenth Plan target; 2002-03 ~4% (poor monsoon); 2003-04 **8.2%**.
[10:5:15] - **Highest average period:** **2004-05 to 2012-13** (boom peak ~**8.5%** in 2003-08); 1980s ~5.1-5.2%.
[10:5:16] - "**Decade of jobless growth**" – **1991-2000**; organised-sector jobs grew **0.36%** vs **2.6%** target.
## SUB 6 | Second Plan – Outlay and Criticism
[10:6:0] - Planned public outlay **Rs 4,800 crore** (private Rs 3,100 crore); actual **Rs 4,600 crore** (Rs 3,650 crore investment + Rs 950 crore current).
[10:6:1] - **Why pruned:** food and capital shortages, **inflation**, closed-economy assumption, **1957-58 foreign-exchange crisis** (not falling inflation).
[10:6:2] TABLE head: Factor | Criticism of Nehru-Mahalanobis strategy
  [10:6:2.0] Agriculture | Neglected → food shortages
  [10:6:2.1] Assumption | Closed economy
  [10:6:2.2] Employment | Capital-intensive industry → few jobs
  [10:6:2.3] Prices | High inflation by late 1950s
## SUB 7 | Eleventh and Twelfth Plans
[10:7:0] TABLE head: Feature | Eleventh (2007-12) | Twelfth (2012-17)
  [10:7:0.0] Theme | "Towards Faster and More Inclusive Growth" | "Faster, Sustainable and More Inclusive Growth"
  [10:7:0.1] GDP target | 9% (rising to 10% by end); ⚠ APPSC key (2010) gave 9.5% | 9% → 8%
  [10:7:0.2] Agriculture | 4% | 4% (agriculture, forestry, fisheries)
  [10:7:0.3] Health | IMR 28; MMR 1 per 1,000 live births; TFR 2.1 | IMR 25
  [10:7:0.4] Targets | 27 national monitorable; 13 State-level | 25 monitorable
  [10:7:0.5] Outlay | Rs 36,44,718 crore | Rs 76,69,807 crore
[10:7:1] - **Eleventh inclusive-growth strategy:** **continued reform and a competitive private sector** (not withdrawal of the private sector); more emphasis on agriculture; health, education, skills; infrastructure; investment **37% → 39%** of GDP.
[10:7:2] - Eleventh: cut poverty head-count ratio by **10 points**; **7 crore** new jobs; largest job growth expected in **construction (8.27%)**; education theme = compulsory elementary education; social services highest allocation, then energy.
[10:7:3] - **Twelfth:** reduce poverty and regional inequality; health, education, skills up by **1.2 points of GDP**; fewer CSS with a **flexi-fund**; social-sector PPPs; plan/non-plan distinction reviewed by the **Rangarajan Committee**.
[10:7:4] - 11th Plan 9% target hit by infrastructure fund shortfall; 12th Plan IMR 25 target missed for lack of funds.
[10:7:5] **27** monitorable targets (XI) vs **25** (XII); inclusive growth does **not** mean withdrawing the private sector.
## SUB 8 | Achievements of Planning
[10:8:0] - Average growth (I–XI) **~4.5%** vs **~1%** before Independence; agriculture 2-3% (vs 0.3%); industry 6-8%.
[10:8:1] - **Industrial base:** **Bhilai, Durgapur, Rourkela** steel plants; **Chittaranjan** (locomotives), **Perambur** (coaches); **IITs**; Atomic Energy Commission.
[10:8:2] - **Food self-sufficiency:** Green Revolution (1966-67), **FCI (1965)**, National Seeds Corporation.
[10:8:3] - **Institutions:** **SBI** (from Imperial Bank, First Plan period), NABARD (1982).
[10:8:4] - First Plan succeeded mainly due to **good harvests** in its last two years.
[10:8:5] - Poverty fell from **48.3% (1977-78)** to **37.4% (1983-84)**.
## SUB 9 | Failures of Planning
[10:9:0] TABLE head: Failure | Detail
  [10:9:0.0] Poverty | Persistent poverty and inequality; Eighth Plan – "the poor became poorer"
  [10:9:0.1] Unemployment | Job growth lagged (Eighth: 2% vs 2.6%)
  [10:9:0.2] Regional imbalance | Across and within States
  [10:9:0.3] Industry | Below target in several plans
  [10:9:0.4] Centralisation | Diluted people's participation
  [10:9:0.5] Implementation | Eighth Plan public-sector share 34.3% vs 45.2% target; overlapping rural electrification schemes

[10:10:11] # After the Plans
[10:10:12] - **NITI Aayog** replaced the Planning Commission on **1 Jan 2015**; plans ended **2017**.
[10:10:13] - **Swarna Andhra Vision 2047** launched **13 Dec 2024**; aligned with Viksit Bharat 2047; economic regions **Visakhapatnam, Amaravati, Tirupati** (not Kurnool).
[10:10:14] - NABARD AP State Focus Paper 2026-27: credit plan **Rs 5.11 lakh crore** (up ~20%); Rs 2.55 lakh crore agriculture; Rs 1.64 lakh crore MSMEs (6 May 2026).
[10:10:15] - **Prakasam Barrage:** built **1954-57** across the **Krishna** at **Vijayawada**, replacing the old anicut (1852-55); named after **Tanguturi Prakasam**, first Chief Minister of Andhra State.
