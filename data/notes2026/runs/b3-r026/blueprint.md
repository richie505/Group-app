## 4. Traps settled
- **First Census:** 1872 (attempted), 1881 (first synchronous); "1871" (UPPSC 2023 key) and "1881" (printed key) both wrong for "first" → option 1872 added, ⚠ Disputed.
- **UPSC 2004:** in 2001 Sikkim (875) was below Punjab (876), so "two lowest = Haryana and Punjab" is false → only c; ⚠ Disputed (printed key a and c).
- **Female literacy 2011** is 65.46 P / 64.6 final – the old notes' "MPPCS 1991 key 63.0" trap removed; key 65.5.
- **Sex ratio trend "fluctuating"** (IAS 1997) and **male child death rate** (IAS 1999) kept with ⚠ Disputed lines.
- **UP's most literate district:** Gautam Buddha Nagar (final) vs Ghaziabad (provisional) – ⚠ Disputed.
