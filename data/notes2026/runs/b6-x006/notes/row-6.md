=== Swarna Andhra @2047 – Targets and Ten Sutralu
# The vision
- **Released:** **13 December 2024** at Vijayawada by CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu** (NITI Aayog Vice-Chairman Suman Bery present); title "10 Principles – One Vision".
- **Guiding principles:** **10 Padi Sutralu** (Ten Sutras) – first is Zero Poverty (P4); others: population management, employment and skilling, water security, agri-tech, logistics, energy cost, product perfection, Swachh Andhra, deep tech (APPSC PYQ: "10").
- **Targets by 2047:** economy **USD 2.4 trillion**; per capita income **USD 42,000**; needs about **15% annual growth**.
> Trap: USD 2.4 trillion is the economy, USD 42,000 the per capita income; the vision drew on NITI Aayog, not the Finance Commission.
~~ Sources: AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 p.14 (1.10 Swarna Andhra Vision 2047, 10 principles, $2.4 trillion, $42,000), p.98 (6.1.6, 15% growth), p.306 (10.7 Ten Sutras); web: Deccan Chronicle / ThePrint reports of the release, 13 Dec 2024 (search results)

- **₹53,752.12 crore** for agriculture and allied sectors, presented in the Assembly by Agriculture Minister **K. Atchannaidu** (in the Council by Anagani Satya Prasad).

- **Annadata Sukhibhava–PM Kisan:** **₹6,600 crore** proposed for 2026-27 (₹6,660 crore was released in 2025-26).
- It is a regrouping **within** the ₹3.32 lakh crore budget, not an addition to it (Agriculture & Allied Departments alone get ₹13,598 crore in the main budget).

=== Debt, Borrowings and Revenue 2026-27
- **Public debt:** to rise to **₹7.11 lakh crore** in 2026-27 (₹6.35 lakh crore in 2025-26); outstanding liabilities about **36% of GSDP**.
- **Gross borrowings (public debt receipts) BE 2026-27:** **₹97,315.41 crore**; net public debt ₹75,168 crore.
- **Own tax revenue (BE 2026-27):** **₹1,25,845.78 crore** (6.4% of GSDP).
- **Share in central taxes (devolution, BE 2026-27):** **₹64,362.16 crore**.
> Trap: ₹97,315 crore is one year's fresh borrowing; ₹7.11 lakh crore is the outstanding debt stock; 3.8% is the fiscal deficit, not the debt ratio.
~~ Sources: AP Budget 2026-27 Explanatory Memorandum p.9 (Total E Public Debt 97315,41.30), p.50 (net public debt ₹75,168 crore); Praveen CA-360 Feb 2026 p.122 (₹64,362.16 crore central taxes); PRS AP Budget Analysis 2026-27 (own tax ₹1,25,846 crore, 36% of GSDP); Telangana Today, AP Budget 2026-27 (₹7.11 vs ₹6.35 lakh crore)

> Trap: not grants under Art. 275, not 30-year loans; not Jal Shakti.
