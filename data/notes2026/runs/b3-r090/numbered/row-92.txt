## SUB 0 | Global Financial Crisis 2007-09 – Sub-prime, Lehman, Teaser Loans
[0:0] - **Origin:** the **US sub-prime mortgage** market (surfaced July 2007) – loans to borrowers with poor credit records, caused by the **US housing (real-estate) bust**, **not by oil prices**.
[0:1] - **Chain:** 1. loans to **weak borrowers** → 2. sold on and **securitised** into **mortgage-backed securities** sold worldwide → 3. rates rise, **home-loan defaults** → 4. **failure of US banks** → 5. FPI outflows and a **crash in the Indian stock market**.
[0:2] - UPPSC 2010 statements: caused by oil prices ✗; home-loan defaults ✓; failure of US banks ✓; crash in Indian market ✓ → **2, 3 and 4 only**.
[0:3] - **Lehman Brothers** (US investment bank) filed for **Chapter 11 bankruptcy on 15 Sep 2008**; sub-prime lending was a main cause of the crisis → A and R both true, **R explains A**.
[0:4] - **Teaser loans:** **low fixed rate in the first years, higher later** (e.g. SBI home loans, 2008-09) – an aspect of **sub-prime lending**; banks risk **future defaults**. They were home loans, **not** loans to inexperienced entrepreneurs for factories or exports → **A only** (UPSC 2011).
[0:5] - **Credit Default Swap:** insurance-like derivative on bonds; seller pays on default; **counterparty risk**.
[0:6] - **Response in India:** FSDC set up **Dec 2010** (G-20 initiative after the crisis); shadow banking came under scrutiny worldwide.

## SUB 1 | East Asian Crisis 1997 and the Taper Tantrum 2013
[1:0] - **East Asian crisis (1997):** hit **Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia, South Korea**; began with the **fall of the Thai baht** (devalued 2 July 1997). Cause (UPSC 1999 key): the **prolonged over-valuation of local currencies vis-à-vis the US dollar** (pegs), with heavy short-term foreign debt and weak banks – not a Western recession.
[1:1] - **India** suffered only limited harm – **capital controls and low short-term debt**.
[1:2] - **Taper tantrum (2013):** in **May 2013** the **US Fed** signalled it would **taper** its asset purchases; money left emerging markets and the **rupee fell about 17%** against the dollar by 3 Sep 2013.
[1:3] ~~ Sources: Federal Reserve History, "Asian Financial Crisis" (federalreservehistory.org); RBA Research Discussion Paper 9805 (dollar pegs, overvaluation); app notes 3:92 sub 0

## SUB 3 | Shadow Banking, NBFCs and the 2018 Shadow-Lender Crisis
[3:0] - **Shadow banking** = credit creation by **financial institutions outside the regular banking regulatory framework** (hedge funds, NBFCs; also unregulated activities of regulated entities) – they escape CRR/SLR and capital rules.
[3:1] - **Shadow-lender crisis (2018-19):** began with **IL&FS** defaults; it hit **NBFCs** (not SFCs, IFC or LIC).
[3:2] - **Asset-liability mismatch:** NBFCs **borrow short** (CPs, liquid mutual funds) and **lend long** (infrastructure, housing).
[3:3] - **NBFCs:** registered under the **Companies Act**; **cannot take demand deposits, issue cheques or join the payment system**; deposits **not DICGC-covered**.
[3:4] - **Scale-Based Regulation (Oct 2022):** **Base, Middle, Upper, Top** layers.
[3:5] ~~ Sources: Ramesh Singh, Indian Economy 06 p.141 (shadow banking); NextGen Indian Economy - APPSC Exams 08 p.1-2 (NBFCs, demand deposits, liquid funds); app notes 3:92 sub 2
