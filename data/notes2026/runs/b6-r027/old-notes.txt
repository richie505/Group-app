[27:0:5] # Delimitation Commission
[27:0:6] - Constituted **four times: 1952, 1963, 1973, 2002**; last exercise completed **2008** (on 2001 Census).
[27:0:7] - Statutory body under the Delimitation Act; **appointed by the President**; chair – retired SC judge; members – CEC + State Election Commissioners.
[27:0:8] - **Temporary body** – dissolves after its work; decides **SC/ST reserved constituencies**.
[27:0:9] - Orders have the **force of law**; laid before Parliament **for information only** (no approval needed).
[27:0:10] Delimitation orders are NOT subject to courts – **Article 329(a)** bars judicial review.
[27:0:11] # Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill, 2026 – the Package
[27:0:12] - Lok Sabha maximum **550 → 850** (**815 States + 35 UTs**) – amends **Art 81**; Art 82 (removes post-2026 freeze), Art 170 and **Art 334A** (early women's reservation).
[27:0:13] - Delimitation on the **2011 Census**; one-third women's reservation.
[27:0:14] - **"Schedule" mechanism**: a law lists State-wise seats – Lok Sabha up ~50% while each State's percentage share stays the same.
[27:0:15] - Package of **three Bills**: 131st Amendment Bill; **Delimitation Bill, 2026** (new Commission replacing the Delimitation Act, 2002 framework; would fix SC, ST and women's seats); **Union Territories Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026** (Delhi, J&K, Puducherry).
[27:0:16] - Needed **special majority + ratification by half of the States**.
[27:0:17] - **Art 75(1A)**: Council of Ministers ≤ **15%** of Lok Sabha → up to ~**122 ministers** at 850.
[27:0:18] - **Art 108** joint sitting – a larger Lok Sabha dominates more; Rajya Sabha strength (Art 80) does **not** change automatically.
[27:0:19] - Gainers: **UP, Bihar, Rajasthan, MP** (Hindi belt); relative losers: **Kerala, Tamil Nadu**.
