## SUB 0 | Postal Network – PIN, Speed Post and India Post
[0:0] - **PIN code:** **6 digits**, from **15 Aug 1972**. 1st = zone; 2nd = sub-zone; 3rd = sorting district; last 3 = delivery office.
[0:1] - **9 PIN zones:** **8 regional** (1-8) + **1 functional** zone **9 = Army Postal Service**.
[0:2] TABLE head: First digit | Region (exam examples)
  [0:2.0] 1 | Delhi, Haryana, Punjab, HP, J&K
  [0:2.1] 2 | Uttar Pradesh (Lucknow), Uttarakhand
  [0:2.2] 3 | Rajasthan, Gujarat (Gandhinagar)
  [0:2.3] 4 | Maharashtra (Nagpur), MP, Chhattisgarh, Goa
  [0:2.4] 5 | Andhra Pradesh (Amaravati), Telangana, Karnataka
  [0:2.5] 6 | Tamil Nadu, Kerala
  [0:2.6] 7 | West Bengal, Odisha, North-East
  [0:2.7] 8 | Bihar, Jharkhand
[0:3] - **Digi PIN:** **10-digit alphanumeric**, on the **APT** platform built by **CEPT**; rollout in 23 circles done **4 Aug 2025** (1.64 lakh offices).
[0:4] - **Speed Post: Aug 1986** (to compete with couriers), refund if late. **IPPB: 1 Sep 2018**, Department of Posts.
[0:5] - **Project Arrow (2008):** India Post's programme to upgrade post offices and postal services.
[0:6] - India has the **largest number of post offices** in the world: **1,64,999** (31 Mar 2025). **AP: 10,660**.

## SUB 4 | Digital Connectivity – BharatNet, Digital India, PM-WANI
[4:0] - **Digital India: 1 Jul 2015**, **9 pillars**, first = broadband highways.
[4:1] - **BharatNet:** ex-**NOFN (2011)** (not NKN) – optic fibre broadband to all **Gram Panchayats**; funded by **USOF (Digital Bharat Nidhi)**; nodal **BBNL** (merged with BSNL); **2.18 lakh GPs**, **6.92 lakh km** fibre; Phase 3 = last mile + private players. Amended BharatNet: **₹1.39 lakh crore**.
[4:2] - **PM-WANI: Dec 2020** (DoT); public Wi-Fi via **PDOs** and **PDOAs**. **National Broadband Mission: Dec 2019**.
[4:3] - **CSCs: 5.69 lakh**. **Visakhapatnam Declaration** extended NeSDA to Ladakh and NE. SC: digital access part of **Article 21**.
