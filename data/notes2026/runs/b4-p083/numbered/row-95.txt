## SUB 1 | Visakhapatnam Port
[1:0] - Opened to ocean traffic in **October 1933** with the arrival of the passenger ship **SS Jaladurga**.
[1:1] - **Deepest land-locked and protected port** (NextGen; APPSC 2025 key); natural + artificial harbour parts; called the **"Brightest Jewel"**.
[1:2] - Protected by **Dolphin's Nose** (Yarada hills, ~335 m) from waves and cyclones.
[1:3] - **Outer harbour** for **iron ore exports**; crude oil berth; all-weather, deep-water port.
[1:4] - Ranked **third** in port traffic (FY 2022 key). Helped transport of agricultural products (APPSC 2022 key; the port itself is a 1933, 20th-century work).
[1:5] - **Top seafood-export port** (2025-26): **5.12 lakh tonnes**, over **26%** of India's 19.32 lakh tonnes.
[1:6] - First direct **South-East India – North Europe (IEX)** service, also linking the Mediterranean.
[1:7] - Deepest port = Visakhapatnam; deepest draft = Gangavaram.
[1:8] ~~ Sources: NextGen Indian Geography 11 p6-7; NextGen Rocket Sheets AP Geography p50; app notes 4:95 (sub 7); SS Jaladurga, IEX: general knowledge, not checked
