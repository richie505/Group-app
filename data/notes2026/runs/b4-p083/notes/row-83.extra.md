=== Veterinary Practice – Blood Sampling, Pregnancy Diagnosis, Breeds
# Blood collection sites
| Animal | Vein used |
|---|---|
| Cow / cattle, buffalo | **Jugular vein** |
| Dog (canine) | **Cephalic vein** |
| Pig | **Anterior vena cava** |
| Chicken / poultry | **Wing (brachial) vein** |
- Match asked: **Cow – jugular, Canine – cephalic, Pig – anterior vena cava, Chicken – wing vein**.
# Pregnancy diagnosis and animal facts
- **Fremitus** (the "buzzing" of the enlarged middle uterine artery felt per rectum) is a sign of pregnancy in cattle from about **120 days** (exam key).
- **Nellore sheep** (AP, meat breed): average **weaning weight of lambs about 12 kg** (exam key).
- **Camel** = classic example of **water management** (conserves body water, tolerates heavy dehydration).
- **Alsatian** (German Shepherd) dog – origin **Germany**.
> Exam angle: jugular (cattle) vs cephalic (dog); fremitus about 120 days; Nellore lamb 12 kg.
