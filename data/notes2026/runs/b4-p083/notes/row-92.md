=== Golden Quadrilateral and the N-S / E-W Corridors
| Corridor | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Golden Quadrilateral (GQ)** | **National Highway project** linking **Delhi–Mumbai–Chennai–Kolkata**; ~**5,846 km**, 4/6 lanes; NHDP Phase I (NHDP launched **1998**) |
| **North-South** | **Srinagar–Kanyakumari** (with Kochi–Salem spur), ~4,076 km; NHDP Phase I-II |
| **East-West** | **Silchar (Assam)–Porbandar (Gujarat)**, ~3,640 km; 7 States |
- GQ = **highways**, not rail lines, air routes or power grids. **Hyderabad and Bengaluru are not GQ nodes.**
- **Longest GQ arm: Kolkata–Chennai** (~1,649 km, NH-16). **Longest GQ stretch in one State: AP, 1,014 km.**
- N-S and E-W corridors cross at **Jhansi** (UP) – not Nagpur, Gwalior or Bhopal.
- N-S corridor north to south: **Srinagar – Agra – Gwalior – Jhansi – Nagpur – Hyderabad – Bengaluru – Krishnagiri – Kanyakumari** (NH-44, so it **passes through Hyderabad**).
- Not on the corridors: Lucknow, Kanpur, Kota (Agra is on the N-S corridor).
- UPSC 2023: E-W is not Dibrugarh–Surat; the Trilateral Highway is **Moreh–Mae Sot** (not Chiang Mai); BCIM is **Kunming–Kolkata** (not Varanasi) – **none** correct.
- GQ 2.0 high-speed expressway network planned (Oct 2025).

=== Railways – History, Network, Konkan and Units
- **First train: 16 April 1853**, **Bori Bunder (Bombay) – Thane, 34 km**; by **GIPR**, under **Lord Dalhousie**.
- **Broad gauge = 1.676 m (5½ ft)**; metre 1 m; narrow 0.762 / 0.610 m. BG >95% of the network.
- Network **70,000+ route km**; **4th largest** in the world (after USA, Russia, China); freight **1,617 MT (2024-25)** = world's **2nd-largest freight railway**.
- **Coal** is the largest single commodity carried (about half of freight).
- Railway Budget 2012-13: about **13.6 lakh employees** (13,61,519).
- **Konkan Railway:** **760 km, Roha (Maharashtra) – Mangalore (Thokur, Karnataka)**, opened **1998**; partners **Maharashtra, Goa, Karnataka** (Kerala benefits most as a gateway); runs through Ratnagiri, Madgaon, Udupi – **not Belgaum** and **not Kerala**; KRCL HQ **Navi Mumbai**; a **PSU, not a zone**.
- First rail track on **PPP**: **Gujarat** (Pipavav port line).

=== Roads – Network, Traffic, BRO and Expressways
- Road network **63.45 lakh km**, **2nd largest** in the world; NHs **1.46 lakh km**.
- NHs = about **2%** of road length but carry about **40%** of road traffic; roads carry ~85% of passengers and 70% of freight.
- **Road density:** highest in **Kerala**.
- **Surface roads per 100 km² (UPSC 1998):** Punjab and Tamil Nadu far ahead of Maharashtra and Haryana (books differ on which of the two leads).
- Road transport got the post-1947 impetus because rail is capital-heavy and indivisible and cannot reach scattered settlements – but **rail is cheaper per tonne-km**.
- **BRO:** set up **1960** (Ministry of Defence); builds border roads.
- **Mumbai–Pune Expressway** (94.5 km) = India's **first six-lane concrete expressway**; **Agra–Lucknow** six-lane expressway opened Nov 2016 (UP).
- **Bharatmala Pariyojana (2017):** ~**34,800 km** in Phase I; highest built length **Rajasthan** (2,241 km to Oct 2024).
- **Madhya Pradesh** first to issue a unified vehicle registration card.
- World: **Autobahn** = Germany's highways (Autoroute = France); **Asian Highway** promoted by **UNESCAP**; the Silk Route was a land-and-sea network, not one modern highway.

=== Airways
- **First airmail:** **18 Feb 1911**, **Allahabad – Naini**.
- **RCS-UDAN** under the **National Civil Aviation Policy 2016**; NCAP 2016 offered **"Open Skies"** – signed by Sri Lanka, Spain, Jamaica and others, **not Nepal**. Modified UDAN: 120 destinations, **4 crore** passengers in 10 years.
- **DigiYatra** began **1 Dec 2022** at **Delhi, Bengaluru, Varanasi** (not Mumbai).
- **Pakyong Airport = Sikkim** (first airport of the State).

- **Delhi** = first carbon-neutral airport in Asia-Pacific.
